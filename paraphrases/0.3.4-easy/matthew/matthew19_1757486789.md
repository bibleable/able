# Matthew 19 — Marriage, children, and riches

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers marriage questions, welcomes children, and challenges a wealthy young man.

## Matthew 19:1

After teaching, Jesus left Galilee for Judea beyond Jordan. 📖

## Matthew 19:2

Large crowds followed, and he healed them. 📖

## Matthew 19:3

Pharisees tested him: “Can a man lawfully divorce his wife for any reason?” 📖

## Matthew 19:4

He answered, “Have you not read that the Creator made them male and female from the beginning? 📖

## Matthew 19:5

‘A man leaves father and mother, joins his wife, and the two become one flesh.’ 📖

## Matthew 19:6

They are one flesh, not two; nobody should separate what God joined.” 📖

## Matthew 19:7

They asked why Moses ordered divorce certificates when sending wives away. 📖

## Matthew 19:8

Jesus replied, “Moses permitted divorce because of your hard hearts; it was not so originally. 📖

## Matthew 19:9

Divorcing a wife except for sexual immorality and marrying another woman is adultery.” 📖

**Help:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

The exception's interpretation requires further editorial review.

## Matthew 19:10

Disciples said, “If marriage is like that, better not marry.” 📖

## Matthew 19:11

He replied, “Only those given this word can accept it, not everyone. 📖

## Matthew 19:12

Some eunuchs were born that way, some made so by people, and some live that way for heaven's kingdom. 📖

Whoever can accept it should.”

**Help:** Eunuch can describe someone unable to have sexual relations or someone whose testicles were removed.

The third group describes choosing to live without sexual relations for the kingdom.

The draft does not assign these groups to modern identities.

## Matthew 19:13

People brought children for Jesus' hands and prayers, but disciples rebuked those bringing them. 📖

## Matthew 19:14

He said, “Let children come unhindered; heaven's kingdom belongs to such as these.” 📖

## Matthew 19:15

He placed hands on them and left. 📖

## Matthew 19:16

A man asked, “Teacher, what good must I do for eternal life?” 📖

## Matthew 19:17

Jesus said, “Why ask about good; only One is good. 📖

If you want to enter life, keep the commands.”

## Matthew 19:18

He asked which; Jesus said, “Do not murder, commit adultery, steal, or testify falsely. 📖

## Matthew 19:19

Honour parents and love your neighbour as yourself.” 📖

## Matthew 19:20

The young man said, “I kept all that; what is missing?” 📖

## Matthew 19:21

Jesus said, “If you want to be perfect, sell your possessions and give to poor people. 📖

You will have treasure in heaven.

Then come and follow me.”

## Matthew 19:22

He left sorrowful, because he was very wealthy. 📖

## Matthew 19:23

Jesus said, “It is hard for the rich to enter heaven's kingdom. 📖

## Matthew 19:24

A camel passes a needle's eye more easily than a rich person enters God's kingdom.” 📖

## Matthew 19:25

Astonished, disciples asked who could be saved. 📖

## Matthew 19:26

Jesus looked at them: “Impossible with humans, but everything is possible with God.” 📖

## Matthew 19:27

Peter asked, “We left everything for you; what will we receive?” 📖

## Matthew 19:28

Jesus said, “When everything is renewed, the Son of Man will sit on his glorious throne. 📖

You who followed me will sit on twelve thrones, judging Israel's twelve tribes.

## Matthew 19:29

Anyone leaving houses, brothers, sisters, father, mother, wife, children, or fields for my name will receive a hundredfold. 📖

They will inherit eternal life.

## Matthew 19:30

Many first will be last, and last first. 📖
