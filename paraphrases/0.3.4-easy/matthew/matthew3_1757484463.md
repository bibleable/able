# Matthew 3 — John preaches and baptizes Jesus

**About this chapter**

John calls people to repent, and Jesus comes for baptism.

## Matthew 3:1

John the Baptist preached in Judea's wilderness. 📖

## Matthew 3:2

“Repent, because heaven's kingdom is near.” 📖

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

## Matthew 3:3

Isaiah spoke about him: “A wilderness voice calls, ‘Prepare the Lord's way and straighten his paths.’” 📖

## Matthew 3:4

John wore camel-hair clothing and a leather belt, eating locusts and wild honey. 📖

**Help:** Locusts are insects.

## Matthew 3:5

People came from Jerusalem, all Judea, and the whole Jordan region. 📖

## Matthew 3:6

They confessed their sins, and John baptized them in the Jordan River. 📖

## Matthew 3:7

Seeing many Pharisees and Sadducees approaching for baptism, John said, “Vipers' offspring, who warned you to flee the coming wrath? 📖

**Help:** These were Jewish religious groups; vipers are snakes.

The snake image is John's severe accusation directed at these hearers.

Wrath means strong anger against evil, expressed in judgment.

## Matthew 3:8

Produce fruit matching repentance. 📖

**Help:** Fruit pictures actions showing a changed life.

## Matthew 3:9

Do not rely on saying, ‘Abraham is our father.’ 📖

God can raise children for Abraham from these stones.

## Matthew 3:10

The axe is ready at the trees' roots. 📖

Every tree without good fruit will be cut down and thrown into fire.

## Matthew 3:11

I baptize with water for repentance, but someone stronger follows, whose sandals I am unworthy to carry. 📖

He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.

## Matthew 3:12

He holds his winnowing fork to clear the threshing floor and gather wheat into the barn. 📖

He will burn the chaff with fire that cannot be put out.”

**Help:** The fork separates useful grain from chaff, its unwanted outer covering.

The floor is where harvested grain is processed.

John uses this harvest image for judgment.

## Matthew 3:13

Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan for John to baptize him. 📖

## Matthew 3:14

John tried stopping him: “I need your baptism, yet you come to me?” 📖

## Matthew 3:15

Jesus replied, “Allow it now; this is how we should fulfil all righteousness.” 📖

John agreed.

**Help:** Righteousness concerns being right before God and doing what he requires.

## Matthew 3:16

Jesus immediately came out of the water after baptism; heaven opened, and he saw God's Spirit descending like a dove. 📖

The Spirit rested on him.

## Matthew 3:17

A heavenly voice said, “This is my dearly loved Son, with whom I am well pleased!” 📖
