# Matthew 15 — Tradition, faith, and feeding a crowd

**About this chapter**

Jesus challenges traditions, answers a Canaanite woman, heals many people, and feeds another crowd.

## Matthew 15:1

Jerusalem's Pharisees and law teachers came to Jesus. 📖

## Matthew 15:2

They asked, “Why do your disciples break elders' tradition by eating without washing hands?” 📖

## Matthew 15:3

Jesus replied, “Why do you break God's command for your tradition? 📖

## Matthew 15:4

God said, ‘Honour father and mother,’ and ‘Anyone cursing father or mother must be put to death.’ 📖

## Matthew 15:5

But you allow someone to tell parents, ‘What you might receive from me is a gift devoted to God.’ 📖

## Matthew 15:6

Then they need not honour their parents with it, nullifying God's word for your tradition. 📖

## Matthew 15:7

Hypocrites, Isaiah rightly prophesied about you. 📖

## Matthew 15:8

‘Their lips honour me, but their hearts are far away. 📖

## Matthew 15:9

Their worship is empty, teaching human rules as doctrine.’” 📖

## Matthew 15:10

He called the crowd, saying, “Listen and understand. 📖

## Matthew 15:11

What enters the mouth does not defile someone; what comes out does.” 📖

**Help:** Defile means make impure in a religious or moral sense.

The dispute concerns religious purity, rather than hygiene advice.

## Matthew 15:12

Disciples asked, “Do you know your words offended the Pharisees?” 📖

## Matthew 15:13

He replied, “Every plant my heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted. 📖

## Matthew 15:14

Leave them; they are blind guides. 📖

If a blind person guides another blind person, both will fall into a pit.”

**Help:** The blindness image describes failed guidance, rather than moral blame for physical disability.

## Matthew 15:15

Peter asked him to explain the parable. 📖

## Matthew 15:16

Jesus asked, “Do you still not understand? 📖

## Matthew 15:17

Food goes from mouth to stomach and is passed out. 📖

## Matthew 15:18

What comes from the mouth comes from the heart and defiles someone. 📖

## Matthew 15:19

The heart produces evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false testimony, and slander. 📖

## Matthew 15:20

These defile people; eating with unwashed hands does not.” 📖

## Matthew 15:21

Jesus withdrew to Tyre and Sidon's district. 📖

## Matthew 15:22

A local Canaanite woman cried, “Lord, David's Son, have mercy; my daughter suffers terribly from a demon.” 📖

## Matthew 15:23

Jesus said nothing, and disciples urged, “Send her away; she keeps calling after us.” 📖

## Matthew 15:24

He answered, “I was sent only to Israel's lost sheep.” 📖

## Matthew 15:25

She knelt before him, saying, “Lord, help me!” 📖

## Matthew 15:26

He replied, “Taking children's bread and throwing it to dogs is not right.” 📖

## Matthew 15:27

She answered, “Yes, Lord, even dogs eat crumbs falling from their master's table.” 📖

**About this exchange:** The draft retains the difficult bread-and-dogs image and the woman's response without adding labels for modern groups.

## Matthew 15:28

Jesus said, “Your faith is great; let your desire be granted,” and her daughter was healed that very hour. 📖

## Matthew 15:29

He went along Galilee's sea, then up a mountain, and sat down. 📖

## Matthew 15:30

Large crowds brought people unable to walk, blind people, people with disabled limbs, people unable to speak, and many others. 📖

They laid them at his feet, and he healed them.

## Matthew 15:31

They marvelled at speech restored, disabled limbs restored, walking and sight restored, and glorified Israel's God. 📖

## Matthew 15:32

Jesus said, “I pity this crowd; three days with me, and they have no food. 📖

I will not send them hungry, lest they faint travelling.”

## Matthew 15:33

Disciples asked where enough bread could be found for so many in that desolate place. 📖

## Matthew 15:34

Jesus asked how many loaves they had; they answered, “Seven, and a few small fish.” 📖

## Matthew 15:35

He told the crowd to sit on the ground. 📖

## Matthew 15:36

Taking seven loaves and fish, he thanked God, broke them, and gave them to disciples to distribute. 📖

## Matthew 15:37

Everyone ate enough, and disciples gathered seven baskets of leftover pieces. 📖

## Matthew 15:38

Four thousand men ate, besides women and children. 📖

## Matthew 15:39

After dismissing the crowd, he sailed to Magadan's region. 📖
