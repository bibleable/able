# Matthew 4 — Temptation and the first followers

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers the devil with Scripture, begins preaching in Galilee, and calls followers.

## Matthew 4:1

The Spirit led Jesus into the wilderness for the devil to tempt him. 📖

## Matthew 4:2

After forty days and nights fasting, he was hungry. 📖

**Help:** Fasting means going without food for a time.

## Matthew 4:3

The tempter said, “If you are God's Son, command these stones to become bread.” 📖

## Matthew 4:4

Jesus replied, “Scripture says, ‘People live by every word from God's mouth, rather than bread alone.’” 📖

## Matthew 4:5

The devil took him to the holy city and set him on the temple's highest point. 📖

## Matthew 4:6

“If you are God's Son, jump down; Scripture says he will command angels to lift you in their hands. 📖

You will not strike your foot on a stone.”

**About the speaker:** The devil is using Scripture to tempt Jesus.

## Matthew 4:7

Jesus answered, “Scripture also says, ‘Do not test the Lord your God.’” 📖

## Matthew 4:8

The devil took him to a very high mountain, showing all the world's kingdoms and their glory. 📖

## Matthew 4:9

He said, “I will give all this if you fall down and worship me.” 📖

## Matthew 4:10

Jesus said, “Away, Satan; Scripture says, ‘Worship the Lord your God and serve only him.’” 📖

## Matthew 4:11

The devil left, and angels came to serve Jesus. 📖

## Matthew 4:12

Hearing John was imprisoned, Jesus withdrew to Galilee. 📖

## Matthew 4:13

He left Nazareth to live in seaside Capernaum, in Zebulun and Naphtali's region. 📖

## Matthew 4:14

This fulfilled Isaiah's prophecy. 📖

## Matthew 4:15

“Zebulun and Naphtali's lands, the Way of the Sea, beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles. 📖

## Matthew 4:16

The people in darkness saw great light; light dawned on those living in death's shadow.” 📖

## Matthew 4:17

Jesus began preaching, “Repent, because heaven's kingdom is near.” 📖

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

## Matthew 4:18

Walking beside Galilee's sea, he saw brothers Simon called Peter and Andrew casting a fishing net. 📖

They were fishermen.

## Matthew 4:19

Jesus said, “Follow me; I will make you fishers of people.” 📖

**Help:** This pictures bringing people to Jesus rather than catching fish.

## Matthew 4:20

They immediately left their nets and followed. 📖

## Matthew 4:21

He saw brothers James and John repairing nets in a boat with their father Zebedee, and called them. 📖

## Matthew 4:22

Immediately they left boat and father to follow him. 📖

## Matthew 4:23

Throughout Galilee, Jesus taught in synagogues, preached the kingdom's good news, and healed every disease and sickness. 📖

## Matthew 4:24

News spread throughout Syria, and people brought all those with diseases, severe pain, demons, seizures, and paralysis; he healed them. 📖

**Help:** Paralysis prevents movement of parts of the body; seizures are episodes affecting bodily awareness or control.

The list distinguishes these conditions rather than saying every illness or disability is caused by demons.

## Matthew 4:25

Large crowds followed from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond Jordan. 📖

**Help:** Decapolis names a region of ten cities.
