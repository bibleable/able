# Matthew 26 — Supper, prayer, and arrest

**About this chapter**

Jesus shares a meal with his disciples, prays, is arrested, and faces the religious council.

## Matthew 26:1

When Jesus finished all this teaching, he spoke to his disciples. 📖

## Matthew 26:2

“You know Passover is two days away, and the Son of Man will be handed over to be crucified.” 📖

**Help:** Passover remembers God's rescue of Israel from Egypt; Son of Man is Jesus' title for himself.

Crucified means killed on a cross.

## Matthew 26:3

The chief priests and the people's elders gathered in the courtyard of Caiaphas, the high priest. 📖

## Matthew 26:4

They plotted to arrest Jesus secretly and kill him. 📖

## Matthew 26:5

They decided against doing this during the feast, fearing a riot among the people. 📖

## Matthew 26:6

Jesus was in Bethany, at the home of Simon the Leper. 📖

**Help:** Leper was a name used for someone with a serious skin disease; it does not mean morally bad.

## Matthew 26:7

A woman brought an alabaster jar of costly perfume and poured it on his head as he reclined at table. 📖

**Help:** Alabaster is a stone used to make jars; people sometimes ate leaning on couches.

## Matthew 26:8

The disciples saw this and angrily asked why she wasted it. 📖

## Matthew 26:9

They said the perfume could have sold for a high price, with the money given to poor people. 📖

## Matthew 26:10

Jesus knew this and asked, “Why trouble her? 📖

She has done a beautiful thing for me.

## Matthew 26:11

You will always have poor people with you, but you will not always have me. 📖

## Matthew 26:12

By pouring this perfume on me, she prepared my body for burial. 📖

## Matthew 26:13

I tell you truly, wherever this good news is preached worldwide, her deed will be told to remember her.” 📖

## Matthew 26:14

Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, went to the chief priests. 📖

## Matthew 26:15

He asked what they would give him to hand Jesus over. 📖

They set out thirty pieces of silver for him.

## Matthew 26:16

From then on, Judas looked for a chance to betray Jesus. 📖

## Matthew 26:17

On the first day of the Unleavened Bread feast, the disciples asked Jesus where to prepare his Passover meal. 📖

**Help:** Unleavened bread is made without yeast, which makes bread rise.

## Matthew 26:18

Jesus told them to find a certain man in the city.

They must give him the Teacher's message: 📖

“My time is near.

I will keep Passover with my disciples at your house.”

## Matthew 26:19

The disciples followed Jesus' instructions and prepared the Passover. 📖

## Matthew 26:20

That evening, Jesus reclined at the meal with the twelve disciples. 📖

## Matthew 26:21

While they ate, he said, “I tell you truly, one of you will betray me.” 📖

## Matthew 26:22

Deeply saddened, each asked in turn, “Surely it is not me, Lord?” 📖

## Matthew 26:23

Jesus answered, “The one who dipped his hand in the bowl with me will betray me. 📖

## Matthew 26:24

The Son of Man will go as Scripture says about him. 📖

But woe to his betrayer!

It would be better for that man never to have been born.”

## Matthew 26:25

Judas, his future betrayer, asked, “Surely it is not me, Rabbi?” 📖

Jesus answered, “You have said it yourself.”

**Help:** Rabbi means teacher.

## Matthew 26:26

While they ate, Jesus took bread, blessed it, broke it, and gave it to the disciples. 📖

He said, “Take and eat; this is my body.”

## Matthew 26:27

He took the cup, gave thanks, and gave it to them. 📖

He said, “All of you, drink from it.

## Matthew 26:28

This is my blood of the covenant, poured out for many so that sins may be forgiven. 📖

**Help:** A covenant is a binding agreement; sin is wrongdoing against God.

## Matthew 26:29

I tell you, from now on I will not drink this fruit of the vine.

I will drink it anew with you only on that day in my Father's kingdom.” 📖

**Help:** Fruit of the vine means a drink made from grapes.

## Matthew 26:30

They sang a hymn and went to the Mount of Olives. 📖

## Matthew 26:31

Jesus said, “You will all turn away because of me this very night. 📖

Scripture says, ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the flock's sheep will scatter.’

## Matthew 26:32

But after I rise, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.” 📖

## Matthew 26:33

Peter said, “Even if everyone else turns away because of you, I never will.” 📖

## Matthew 26:34

Jesus answered, “I tell you truly, tonight, before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.” 📖

## Matthew 26:35

Peter said, “Even if I must die with you, I will never deny you.” 📖

All the other disciples said the same.

## Matthew 26:36

Jesus went with them to Gethsemane and told them to sit while he went farther to pray. 📖

## Matthew 26:37

He took Peter and Zebedee's two sons, and began feeling deeply sad and distressed. 📖

## Matthew 26:38

He said, “My soul is so full of sorrow that I could die. 📖

Stay here and keep watch with me.”

## Matthew 26:39

He went a little farther, fell facedown, and prayed, “My Father, if possible, let this cup pass from me. 📖

But let what you want happen, rather than what I want.”

**Help:** The cup pictures the suffering Jesus faces.

## Matthew 26:40

Returning, Jesus found the disciples asleep and asked Peter, “Could you not keep watch with me for one hour? 📖

## Matthew 26:41

Watch and pray so you will not enter temptation. 📖

The spirit is willing, but the body is weak.”

## Matthew 26:42

He went away a second time and prayed. 📖

“My Father, if this cup cannot pass unless I drink it, let what you want happen.”

## Matthew 26:43

He returned and again found them sleeping, because their eyes were heavy. 📖

## Matthew 26:44

He left again and prayed a third time, saying the same thing. 📖

## Matthew 26:45

He returned and asked, “Are you still sleeping and resting? 📖

Look, the hour is near, and the Son of Man is betrayed into sinners' hands.

## Matthew 26:46

Get up, let us go! 📖

My betrayer is approaching!”

## Matthew 26:47

While Jesus spoke, Judas, one of the Twelve, arrived with a large crowd carrying swords and clubs. 📖

The chief priests and the people's elders had sent them.

## Matthew 26:48

The betrayer had arranged this signal: “Arrest the man I kiss.” 📖

## Matthew 26:49

Judas went straight to Jesus, greeted him as Rabbi, and kissed him. 📖

## Matthew 26:50

Jesus answered, “Friend, do what you came for.” 📖

The men stepped forward, seized Jesus, and arrested him.

## Matthew 26:51

A companion of Jesus drew his sword and struck the high priest's servant, cutting off his ear. 📖

## Matthew 26:52

Jesus told him, “Put your sword away. 📖

All who draw the sword will die by the sword.

## Matthew 26:53

Do you not know I can ask my Father for help? 📖

He would immediately give me more than twelve legions of angels.

**Help:** A legion is a large group of soldiers; here Jesus speaks of groups of angels.

## Matthew 26:54

But then how would Scripture be fulfilled, saying this must happen this way?” 📖

## Matthew 26:55

Jesus asked the crowd, “Have you brought swords and clubs to arrest me as an outlaw? 📖

I sat teaching in the temple courts every day, and you never arrested me.

## Matthew 26:56

All this has happened to fulfil the prophets' writings.” 📖

Then all his disciples deserted him and fled.

## Matthew 26:57

They took Jesus to Caiaphas, the high priest, where the law teachers and elders had gathered. 📖

## Matthew 26:58

Peter followed at a distance to the high priest's courtyard. 📖

He entered and sat with the guards to see the outcome.

## Matthew 26:59

The chief priests and whole Sanhedrin sought false evidence against Jesus to put him to death. 📖

**Help:** The Sanhedrin was a religious council.

## Matthew 26:60

They found none, although many false witnesses came forward. 📖

Finally, two came forward.

## Matthew 26:61

They claimed Jesus had said he could destroy God's temple and rebuild it in three days. 📖

## Matthew 26:62

The high priest stood and asked Jesus why he did not answer the men's evidence against him. 📖

## Matthew 26:63

Jesus stayed silent. 📖

The high priest put him under oath by the living God to say whether he was the Christ, God's Son.

## Matthew 26:64

Jesus answered, “You have said it yourself. 📖

But from now, you will all see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power.

You will see him coming on heaven's clouds.”

**Help:** Power refers to God; sitting at God's right hand pictures honour and authority.

## Matthew 26:65

The high priest tore his clothes and accused Jesus of blasphemy. 📖

He said, “Why need more witnesses, now you have heard the blasphemy?

**Help:** Blasphemy means speaking against God; this accusation is the priest's statement.

## Matthew 26:66

What do you think?” 📖

They answered, “He deserves death.”

## Matthew 26:67

They spat in his face and hit him; others slapped him. 📖

## Matthew 26:68

They said, “Prophesy to us, Christ: who hit you?” 📖

## Matthew 26:69

Peter sat outside in the courtyard when a servant girl approached. 📖

She said he too had been with Jesus the Galilean.

## Matthew 26:70

Before everyone, he denied it, saying he did not know what she meant. 📖

## Matthew 26:71

At the gateway, another servant girl saw Peter and told those nearby he had been with Jesus of Nazareth. 📖

## Matthew 26:72

He denied it again, swearing that he did not know the man. 📖

## Matthew 26:73

A little later, people nearby told Peter his accent showed he was one of them. 📖

## Matthew 26:74

He began cursing and swearing that he did not know the man. 📖

Immediately, a rooster crowed.

## Matthew 26:75

Peter remembered Jesus' words about denying him three times before the rooster crowed. 📖

He went outside and cried bitterly.
