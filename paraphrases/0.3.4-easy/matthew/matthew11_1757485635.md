# Matthew 11 — John, judgment, and rest

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers John's question, speaks about the generation's response, and calls burdened people to himself.

## Matthew 11:1

Finishing instructions to the Twelve, Jesus went to teach and preach in their cities. 📖

## Matthew 11:2

In prison, John heard Christ's deeds and sent disciples. 📖

## Matthew 11:3

They asked, “Are you the coming one, or should we expect someone else?” 📖

## Matthew 11:4

Jesus replied, “Report what you hear and see to John. 📖

## Matthew 11:5

Blind people receive sight, and people unable to walk can walk. 📖

Lepers are cleansed, deaf people hear, and dead people are raised.

Poor people hear good news.

## Matthew 11:6

Blessed is whoever does not fall away because of me.” 📖

## Matthew 11:7

As they left, Jesus addressed the crowds. 📖

“Did you go into the wilderness to see a reed swaying in the wind?

## Matthew 11:8

Or did you seek someone in fine clothes? 📖

People wearing fine clothes are in kings' palaces.

## Matthew 11:9

Did you seek a prophet? 📖

Yes, and more than a prophet.

## Matthew 11:10

Scripture speaks about him: ‘I send my messenger ahead of you to prepare your way.’ 📖

## Matthew 11:11

No one born of women is greater than John the Baptist. 📖

Yet the least in heaven's kingdom is greater than him.

## Matthew 11:12

Since John's days until now, heaven's kingdom suffers violence, and violent people lay claim to it. 📖

**About this verse:** The draft retains the BSB wording's violence rather than resolving a disputed interpretation.

## Matthew 11:13

All the Prophets and Law prophesied until John. 📖

## Matthew 11:14

If you accept it, he is the Elijah who was to come. 📖

## Matthew 11:15

Anyone with ears should hear. 📖

## Matthew 11:16

What is this generation like? 📖

Children in marketplaces call to others,

## Matthew 11:17

‘We played the flute, but you did not dance; we sang mourning songs, but you did not mourn.’ 📖

## Matthew 11:18

John came neither eating nor drinking, and they claim he has a demon. 📖

## Matthew 11:19

The Son of Man eats and drinks, and they call him glutton, drunkard, and friend of tax collectors and sinners. 📖

But wisdom is proved right by her actions.”

**Help:** A glutton eats excessively; the claim is the critics' accusation.

## Matthew 11:20

Jesus denounced the cities where he performed most miracles, because they did not repent. 📖

## Matthew 11:21

“Woe to you, Chorazin! 📖

Woe to you, Bethsaida!

Had Tyre and Sidon seen your miracles, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.

**Help:** Woe announces terrible judgment; sackcloth and ashes express mourning and repentance.

## Matthew 11:22

Their judgment day will be more bearable than yours. 📖

## Matthew 11:23

Capernaum, will you rise to heaven? 📖

No, down to Hades!

Had Sodom seen your miracles, it would remain today.

**Help:** Hades refers to the realm of the dead.

## Matthew 11:24

Sodom's judgment day will be more bearable than yours.” 📖

## Matthew 11:25

Jesus said, “Father, Lord of heaven and earth, I praise you for hiding this from the wise and learned. 📖

You revealed it to little children.

## Matthew 11:26

Yes, Father, this pleased you. 📖

## Matthew 11:27

My Father entrusted everything to me. 📖

Only the Father knows the Son; only the Son and those he chooses to reveal him to know the Father.

## Matthew 11:28

Come, all weary and burdened people; I will give you rest. 📖

## Matthew 11:29

Take my yoke and learn from me, gentle and humble in heart, and find rest for your souls. 📖

## Matthew 11:30

My yoke is easy, and my burden light.” 📖

**Help:** A yoke joins working animals; Jesus uses it to picture coming under his guidance.
