# Matthew 18 — Little ones, reconciliation, and forgiveness

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches about humility, care for little ones, confronting sin, and forgiving from the heart.

## Matthew 18:1

Disciples asked who is greatest in heaven's kingdom. 📖

## Matthew 18:2

Jesus placed a little child among them. 📖

## Matthew 18:3

He said, “Unless you change and become like little children, you will never enter heaven's kingdom. 📖

## Matthew 18:4

Whoever humbles themselves like this child is greatest there. 📖

## Matthew 18:5

Welcoming such a child in my name welcomes me. 📖

## Matthew 18:6

If anyone leads one of these little ones believing in me into sin, this would be better for them. 📖

It would be better to have a large millstone round their neck and be drowned deep in the sea.

**Help:** A millstone is a heavy stone for grinding grain; stumbling here pictures being led into sin.

This is a severe warning about causing harm, rather than an instruction to harm someone.

## Matthew 18:7

Woe to the world for causes of sin; they must come, but woe to whoever brings them. 📖

## Matthew 18:8

If your hand or foot causes sin, cut it off and throw it away. 📖

Entering life with disabled limbs is better than keeping both hands and feet and being thrown into eternal fire.

## Matthew 18:9

Remove and discard an eye causing sin. 📖

Entering life with one eye is better than keeping two eyes and being thrown into hell's fire.

**Help with this image:** As in chapter 5, the shocking exaggeration stresses rejecting sin, rather than directing self-injury.

## Matthew 18:10

Do not despise these little ones; their heavenly angels always see my heavenly Father's face. 📖

## Matthew 18:11

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Matthew 18:12

Would a shepherd with a hundred sheep not leave ninety-nine on hills to seek one wandering sheep? 📖

## Matthew 18:13

If he finds it, he rejoices more than over the ninety-nine that did not wander. 📖

## Matthew 18:14

Likewise, your heavenly Father does not want any little one to perish. 📖

## Matthew 18:15

If your brother sins against you, confront him privately; if he listens, you have won him back. 📖

## Matthew 18:16

Otherwise take one or two others, so two or three witnesses establish every matter. 📖

## Matthew 18:17

If he refuses them, tell the church; if he refuses even the church, regard him as pagan or tax collector. 📖

## Matthew 18:18

Whatever you bind or loose on earth will be bound or loosed in heaven. 📖

## Matthew 18:19

If two of you on earth agree in asking anything, my heavenly Father will do it. 📖

## Matthew 18:20

Where two or three gather in my name, I am among them.” 📖

## Matthew 18:21

Peter asked, “Lord, should I forgive my brother's sins against me seven times?” 📖

## Matthew 18:22

Jesus answered, “Not seven, but seventy-seven times. 📖

## Matthew 18:23

Heaven's kingdom is like a king settling accounts with servants. 📖

## Matthew 18:24

One owed ten thousand talents. 📖

**Help:** A talent was a large unit of money.

## Matthew 18:25

Unable to pay, he was ordered by the master to be sold with his wife, children, and possessions. 📖

This was to pay his debt.

## Matthew 18:26

He knelt and begged, ‘Be patient; I will repay everything.’ 📖

## Matthew 18:27

The compassionate master cancelled his debt and released him. 📖

## Matthew 18:28

He found another servant owing a hundred denarii, grabbed and choked him, demanding repayment. 📖

**Help:** A denarius was a coin.

## Matthew 18:29

The other fell and begged, ‘Be patient; I will repay.’ 📖

## Matthew 18:30

He refused and imprisoned him until payment. 📖

## Matthew 18:31

Distressed, fellow servants told their master everything. 📖

## Matthew 18:32

The master summoned him: ‘Wicked servant, I cancelled everything because you begged. 📖

## Matthew 18:33

Should you not have shown mercy as I did?’ 📖

## Matthew 18:34

The angry master gave him to jailers for torture until he repaid everything. 📖

## Matthew 18:35

My heavenly Father will treat you likewise unless you forgive your brother from your heart.” 📖
