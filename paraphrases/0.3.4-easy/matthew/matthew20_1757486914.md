# Matthew 20 — Workers, service, and mercy

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells a vineyard parable, predicts his death, teaches service, and heals two blind men.

## Matthew 20:1

Heaven's kingdom is like a landowner hiring vineyard workers early in the morning. 📖

## Matthew 20:2

He agreed on one denarius for the day and sent them to work. 📖

**Help:** A vineyard grows grapes; a denarius was a coin.

## Matthew 20:3

About the third hour, he saw idle people in the marketplace. 📖

## Matthew 20:4

He promised fair pay if they too went to his vineyard. 📖

## Matthew 20:5

They went; he did likewise about the sixth and ninth hours. 📖

## Matthew 20:6

About the eleventh hour, he asked others why they stood idle all day. 📖

## Matthew 20:7

“Nobody hired us,” they replied, and he sent them into his vineyard too. 📖

## Matthew 20:8

That evening, he told the foreman to pay everyone, starting with the last hired and ending with the first. 📖

## Matthew 20:9

The eleventh-hour workers each received one denarius. 📖

## Matthew 20:10

The first expected more, but also received one denarius each. 📖

## Matthew 20:11

They grumbled at the landowner. 📖

## Matthew 20:12

“You treated their one hour like our day's burden and scorching heat!” 📖

## Matthew 20:13

He answered, “Friend, I am not unfair; you agreed on a denarius. 📖

## Matthew 20:14

Take your pay and go; I choose to give the last worker the same. 📖

## Matthew 20:15

Am I not free to use my own things as I choose? 📖

Are you envious because I am generous?”

## Matthew 20:16

So last will be first, and first last.” 📖

## Matthew 20:17

Travelling to Jerusalem, Jesus took the Twelve aside. 📖

## Matthew 20:18

“We are going to Jerusalem. 📖

There the Son of Man will be handed to chief priests and law teachers, who will condemn him to death.

## Matthew 20:19

They will hand him to Gentiles for mocking, whipping, and crucifixion; he will rise on the third day.” 📖

## Matthew 20:20

Zebedee's sons and their mother came, and she knelt with a request. 📖

## Matthew 20:21

Jesus asked what she wanted; she requested her sons sit at his right and left in his kingdom. 📖

## Matthew 20:22

He said, “You do not know your request; can you drink my coming cup?” 📖

They said yes.

## Matthew 20:23

He replied, “You will drink my cup, but those seats belong to whoever my Father prepared them for. 📖

They are not mine to grant.”

**Help:** Drinking the cup pictures sharing Jesus' suffering.

## Matthew 20:24

The other ten became angry with the brothers. 📖

## Matthew 20:25

Jesus called them aside and said, “Gentile rulers dominate their people, and superiors exercise authority. 📖

## Matthew 20:26

You must be different; whoever wants greatness must serve you. 📖

## Matthew 20:27

Whoever wants first place must be your slave. 📖

## Matthew 20:28

The Son of Man came to serve, not be served, and give his life as a ransom for many.” 📖

**Help:** A ransom is a price paid to release people.

## Matthew 20:29

Leaving Jericho, a large crowd followed. 📖

## Matthew 20:30

Two blind men sitting beside the road heard Jesus passing and cried, “Lord, David's Son, have mercy on us!” 📖

## Matthew 20:31

The crowd told them to be quiet, but they cried louder, “Lord, David's Son, have mercy on us!” 📖

## Matthew 20:32

Jesus stopped, called them, and asked what they wanted. 📖

## Matthew 20:33

They answered, “Lord, open our eyes.” 📖

## Matthew 20:34

Compassionately, he touched their eyes; they immediately saw and followed him. 📖
