# Matthew 13 — Parables of the kingdom

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells parables, explains several to his disciples, and returns to his hometown.

## Matthew 13:1

That day, Jesus left the house and sat beside the sea. 📖

## Matthew 13:2

Crowds grew so large he sat in a boat while they stood ashore. 📖

## Matthew 13:3

He taught many parables: “A farmer went to sow seed. 📖

**Help:** Parables are stories and comparisons used to teach.

## Matthew 13:4

Some fell along the path, and birds ate it. 📖

## Matthew 13:5

Some fell on shallow rocky soil and grew quickly because the soil was thin. 📖

## Matthew 13:6

Sun scorched it, and it withered without roots. 📖

## Matthew 13:7

Some fell among thorns, which grew and choked it. 📖

## Matthew 13:8

Some fell on good soil and yielded a hundred, sixty, or thirty times as much. 📖

## Matthew 13:9

Anyone with ears should hear.” 📖

## Matthew 13:10

Disciples asked, “Why teach the people in parables?” 📖

## Matthew 13:11

He answered, “You received knowledge of heaven's kingdom's mysteries, but they did not. 📖

## Matthew 13:12

Whoever has will receive more in abundance; whoever lacks will lose even what they have. 📖

## Matthew 13:13

I use parables because, ‘Though seeing, they do not see; though hearing, they neither hear nor understand.’ 📖

## Matthew 13:14

They fulfil Isaiah's prophecy: ‘You will keep hearing without understanding and seeing without perceiving. 📖

## Matthew 13:15

Their hearts grew hard, their ears hardly hear, and they have closed their eyes. 📖

Otherwise, they might see, hear, understand in their hearts, turn, and I would heal them.’

**Help:** The images describe resistance to the message, rather than moral blame for physical disabilities.

## Matthew 13:16

Your eyes are blessed because they see, and your ears because they hear. 📖

## Matthew 13:17

Many prophets and righteous people longed to see and hear what you do, but did not. 📖

## Matthew 13:18

Hear the sower's parable explained. 📖

## Matthew 13:19

Path seed pictures hearing the kingdom's message without understanding; the evil one steals what was sown in the heart. 📖

## Matthew 13:20

Rocky seed pictures someone hearing and immediately welcoming the word joyfully. 📖

## Matthew 13:21

Rootless, they last briefly, then fall away quickly under trouble or persecution because of the word. 📖

## Matthew 13:22

Thorn seed pictures hearing, but life's worries and wealth's deceit choke the word, making it unfruitful. 📖

## Matthew 13:23

Good soil pictures hearing and understanding, producing a hundred, sixty, or thirty times as much fruit.” 📖

## Matthew 13:24

Jesus told another parable: “Heaven's kingdom is like a man sowing good seed in his field. 📖

## Matthew 13:25

While everyone slept, his enemy sowed weeds among wheat and left. 📖

## Matthew 13:26

As wheat grew and bore grain, weeds appeared too. 📖

## Matthew 13:27

Servants asked, ‘Did you not sow good seed; where did the weeds come from?’ 📖

## Matthew 13:28

He replied, ‘An enemy did it’; they asked whether to pull them up. 📖

## Matthew 13:29

He said, ‘No; pulling weeds now might uproot wheat. 📖

## Matthew 13:30

Let both grow until harvest, when I will tell the harvesters to bundle and burn weeds first. 📖

Then gather wheat into my barn.’”

## Matthew 13:31

He said, “Heaven's kingdom is like a mustard seed planted in a man's field. 📖

## Matthew 13:32

Smallest of all seeds, it grows greatest among garden plants, becoming a tree where birds nest.” 📖

## Matthew 13:33

“Heaven's kingdom is like yeast a woman mixed into three measures of flour until it spread through all.” 📖

**Help:** Yeast makes dough rise; the three measures state the amount in the story.

## Matthew 13:34

Jesus told the crowds all this in parables, saying nothing without one. 📖

## Matthew 13:35

This fulfilled the prophet: “I will speak parables and reveal things hidden since the world's foundation.” 📖

## Matthew 13:36

He dismissed the crowds and entered the house; disciples asked him to explain the field's weeds. 📖

## Matthew 13:37

He said, “The Son of Man sows good seed. 📖

## Matthew 13:38

The field is the world, good seed the kingdom's sons, and weeds the evil one's sons. 📖

## Matthew 13:39

The sowing enemy is the devil, harvest the age's end, and harvesters angels. 📖

## Matthew 13:40

As weeds are collected and burned, so it will be at the age's end. 📖

## Matthew 13:41

The Son of Man will send his angels. 📖

They will remove every cause of sin and everyone practising lawlessness from his kingdom.

## Matthew 13:42

They will throw them into the fiery furnace, with weeping and grinding teeth. 📖

## Matthew 13:43

The righteous will shine like the sun in their Father's kingdom; anyone with ears should hear. 📖

## Matthew 13:44

Heaven's kingdom is like hidden treasure found in a field. 📖

The finder hides it again, joyfully sells everything, and buys the field.

## Matthew 13:45

Heaven's kingdom is also like a merchant seeking fine pearls. 📖

## Matthew 13:46

Finding one precious pearl, he sells everything to buy it. 📖

## Matthew 13:47

Heaven's kingdom is like a sea net catching every kind of fish. 📖

## Matthew 13:48

When full, it is pulled ashore; men sit, put good fish into containers, and discard bad ones. 📖

## Matthew 13:49

At the age's end, angels will separate wicked people from righteous people. 📖

## Matthew 13:50

They will throw them into the fiery furnace, with weeping and grinding teeth. 📖

## Matthew 13:51

Have you understood everything?” 📖

They answered yes.

## Matthew 13:52

He said, “Every law teacher trained in heaven's kingdom is like a householder bringing new and old treasures from storage.” 📖

## Matthew 13:53

Finishing the parables, Jesus left. 📖

## Matthew 13:54

Teaching in his hometown synagogue, he astonished them; they asked where his wisdom and miraculous powers came from. 📖

## Matthew 13:55

“Is he not the carpenter's son, Mary's son, and brother of James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas? 📖

## Matthew 13:56

Are his sisters not here; where did he get all this?” 📖

## Matthew 13:57

Offended, they rejected him; Jesus said, “A prophet lacks honour only in his hometown and household.” 📖

## Matthew 13:58

He did few miracles there because they did not believe. 📖
