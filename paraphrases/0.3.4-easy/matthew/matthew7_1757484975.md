# Matthew 7 — Judgment, fruit, and foundations

**About this chapter**

Jesus finishes his mountain teaching with warnings, promises, and the call to act on his words.

## Matthew 7:1

“Do not judge, or you will be judged. 📖

## Matthew 7:2

Your judgment will be used to judge you; your measure will be used for you. 📖

## Matthew 7:3

Why notice a speck in your brother's eye but ignore a beam in yours? 📖

## Matthew 7:4

How can you offer to remove his speck with a beam still in your own eye? 📖

## Matthew 7:5

Hypocrite, remove your beam first; then you can see clearly to remove his speck. 📖

**Help:** A beam is a large piece of wood; a speck is tiny.

The exaggerated picture challenges failing to address your own wrongdoing before correcting others.

## Matthew 7:6

Do not give dogs holy things or throw pearls before pigs. 📖

They may trample them, then turn and tear you apart.

**About this image:** Its application needs editorial review; the draft does not assign these animal labels to a modern group.

## Matthew 7:7

Ask, and you will receive; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will open. 📖

## Matthew 7:8

Everyone asking receives, everyone seeking finds, and the door opens to whoever knocks. 📖

## Matthew 7:9

Would you give your son a stone when he asks for bread? 📖

## Matthew 7:10

Or a snake when he asks for fish? 📖

## Matthew 7:11

If you, though evil, know to give your children good gifts, how much more will your heavenly Father! 📖

He will give good things to those asking him.

## Matthew 7:12

In everything, treat others as you want them to treat you; this sums up the Law and Prophets. 📖

## Matthew 7:13

Enter the narrow gate; the wide gate and broad road lead to destruction, and many enter there. 📖

## Matthew 7:14

The gate is small and the road narrow leading to life, and few find it. 📖

## Matthew 7:15

Beware false prophets, who come dressed as sheep but are ravenous wolves inside. 📖

**Help:** The image warns about harmful people whose appearance hides their true nature.

## Matthew 7:16

You will recognise them by their fruit; do grapes come from thornbushes or figs from thistles? 📖

## Matthew 7:17

Good trees bear good fruit, and bad trees bear bad fruit. 📖

## Matthew 7:18

A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, nor a bad tree good fruit. 📖

## Matthew 7:19

Every tree without good fruit is cut down and thrown into fire. 📖

## Matthew 7:20

So you will recognise them by their fruit. 📖

**Help:** Fruit pictures the results of people's lives and teaching.

## Matthew 7:21

Not everyone calling me ‘Lord, Lord’ enters heaven's kingdom, only those doing my heavenly Father's will. 📖

## Matthew 7:22

Many will say that day, ‘Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy, expel demons, and work many miracles in your name?’ 📖

## Matthew 7:23

I will tell them plainly, ‘I never knew you; leave me, you workers of lawlessness!’ 📖

## Matthew 7:24

Anyone hearing and doing my words is like a wise man building his house on rock. 📖

## Matthew 7:25

Rain fell, floods raged, and winds battered it, but it stood because its foundation was rock. 📖

## Matthew 7:26

Anyone hearing my words without doing them is like a foolish man building on sand. 📖

## Matthew 7:27

Rain fell, floods raged, and winds battered it; it fell with a terrible collapse!” 📖

## Matthew 7:28

The crowds were amazed at Jesus' teaching when he finished. 📖

## Matthew 7:29

He taught with authority, unlike their law teachers. 📖
