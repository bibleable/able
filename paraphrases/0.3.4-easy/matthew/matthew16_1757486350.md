# Matthew 16 — Signs, Peter's answer, and the cross

**About this chapter**

Jesus warns against false teaching, receives Peter's confession, and explains his suffering and the cost of following him.

## Matthew 16:1

Pharisees and Sadducees tested Jesus by asking for a heavenly sign. 📖

## Matthew 16:2

He replied, “At evening you predict fine weather from a red sky. 📖

## Matthew 16:3

At morning, red, overcast skies predict storms; you read the sky but not the times' signs. 📖

## Matthew 16:4

Only Jonah's sign will answer this wicked, adulterous generation's demand.” 📖

He left them.

## Matthew 16:5

Crossing the water, disciples forgot bread. 📖

## Matthew 16:6

Jesus warned, “Beware the Pharisees' and Sadducees' yeast.” 📖

## Matthew 16:7

They decided he meant their forgotten bread. 📖

## Matthew 16:8

Knowing their discussion, he said, “Why debate bread, you of little faith? 📖

## Matthew 16:9

Do you still not understand or remember five loaves, five thousand, and the baskets collected? 📖

## Matthew 16:10

Or seven loaves, four thousand, and those baskets? 📖

## Matthew 16:11

I did not mean bread, but beware the Pharisees' and Sadducees' yeast.” 📖

## Matthew 16:12

They understood he warned of their teaching rather than bread's yeast. 📖

## Matthew 16:13

Near Caesarea Philippi, Jesus asked, “Who do people say the Son of Man is?” 📖

## Matthew 16:14

They answered, “John the Baptist, Elijah, Jeremiah, or another prophet.” 📖

## Matthew 16:15

He asked, “Who do you say I am?” 📖

## Matthew 16:16

Simon Peter answered, “The Christ, the living God's Son.” 📖

## Matthew 16:17

Jesus replied, “Blessed Simon son of Jonah, my heavenly Father revealed this, rather than flesh and blood. 📖

## Matthew 16:18

You are Peter; on this rock I will build my church, and Hades's gates will not defeat it. 📖

**About these words:** Hades refers to the realm of the dead.

The rock, keys, and binding language needs independent interpretation review.

## Matthew 16:19

I will give you the keys to the kingdom of heaven. 📖

What you bind on earth will be bound in heaven; what you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven.”

## Matthew 16:20

He warned disciples to tell nobody he was the Christ. 📖

## Matthew 16:21

He began explaining he must go to Jerusalem and suffer from elders, chief priests, and law teachers. 📖

He must be killed and raised on the third day.

## Matthew 16:22

Peter rebuked him privately, “Lord, this must never happen!” 📖

## Matthew 16:23

Jesus turned, saying, “Get behind me, Satan; you are a stumbling block to me. 📖

You think about human things rather than God's things.”

## Matthew 16:24

He told disciples, “Anyone wishing to follow me must deny themselves, take up their cross, and follow me. 📖

## Matthew 16:25

Whoever seeks to save life loses it; whoever loses life for me finds it. 📖

## Matthew 16:26

What profit is gaining the whole world if you lose your soul? 📖

What can you give in exchange for your soul?

## Matthew 16:27

The Son of Man will come with angels in his Father's glory and repay each according to their deeds. 📖

## Matthew 16:28

Truly, some here will not die before seeing the Son of Man coming in his kingdom.” 📖
