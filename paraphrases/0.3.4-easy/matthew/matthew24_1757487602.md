# Matthew 24 — Be ready

**About this chapter**

Jesus speaks about the temple, suffering, his coming, and being ready.

## Matthew 24:1

As Jesus left the temple, his disciples came to point out its buildings. 📖

## Matthew 24:2

He answered, “Do you see all these things? 📖

I tell you truly, not one stone here will remain on another.

Every stone will be thrown down.”

## Matthew 24:3

Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives, and the disciples came privately. 📖

They asked, “When will this happen?

What sign will show your coming and the end of the age?”

## Matthew 24:4

Jesus answered, “Be careful that nobody deceives you. 📖

## Matthew 24:5

Many will come in my name, claiming to be the Christ, and will deceive many people. 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Matthew 24:6

You will hear about wars and reports of wars, but do not be alarmed. 📖

These things must happen, but the end is still to come.

## Matthew 24:7

Nations and kingdoms will fight each other. 📖

Food shortages and earthquakes will happen in different places.

## Matthew 24:8

These are only the start of birth pains. 📖

**Help:** Jesus compares these troubles to pains at the start of childbirth.

## Matthew 24:9

People will hand you over to be mistreated and killed. 📖

All nations will hate you because of my name.

## Matthew 24:10

Then many will turn away and betray and hate each other. 📖

## Matthew 24:11

Many false prophets will appear and deceive many people. 📖

## Matthew 24:12

As wickedness increases, most people's love will grow cold. 📖

## Matthew 24:13

But whoever keeps going to the end will be saved. 📖

## Matthew 24:14

This good news of the kingdom will be preached worldwide as a witness to every nation. 📖

Then the end will come.

## Matthew 24:15

When you see the ‘abomination of desolation’ standing in the holy place, follow this warning.

Daniel the prophet spoke about it. 📖

Let the reader understand.

**Help:** This difficult phrase describes something deeply offensive that brings ruin; its interpretation needs further assessment.

## Matthew 24:16

When you see this, people in Judea must flee to the mountains. 📖

## Matthew 24:17

Anyone on a roof must not come down to collect anything from the house. 📖

## Matthew 24:18

Anyone in a field must not return for a cloak. 📖

## Matthew 24:19

How terrible those days will be for pregnant mothers and mothers feeding babies! 📖

## Matthew 24:20

Pray that you will not need to flee in winter or on the Sabbath. 📖

**Help:** The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish day of rest.

## Matthew 24:21

There will be great suffering then, worse than anything since the world's beginning. 📖

Nothing like it will ever happen again.

## Matthew 24:22

Unless those days were shortened, nobody would be saved. 📖

But those days will be shortened for the sake of God's chosen people.

## Matthew 24:23

Then if anyone points here or there and says the Christ is there, do not believe it. 📖

## Matthew 24:24

False Christs and false prophets will appear, performing great signs and wonders. 📖

They will try to deceive even God's chosen people, if that were possible.

## Matthew 24:25

See, I have told you beforehand. 📖

## Matthew 24:26

If they say he is in the wilderness, do not go. 📖

If they say he is in the inner rooms, do not believe them.

## Matthew 24:27

The Son of Man's coming will be like lightning flashing from east to west. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Matthew 24:28

Wherever a dead body lies, vultures gather. 📖

**Help:** Vultures are birds that eat dead animals.

## Matthew 24:29

Immediately after those days of suffering, the sun will go dark and the moon will give no light. 📖

Stars will fall from the sky, and the powers of heaven will shake.

## Matthew 24:30

Then the Son of Man's sign will appear in heaven, and all the earth's tribes will mourn. 📖

They will see the Son of Man coming on heaven's clouds with power and great glory.

## Matthew 24:31

He will send his angels with a loud trumpet call. 📖

They will gather his chosen people from the four winds, from one end of the heavens to the other.

**Help:** Four winds pictures every direction.

## Matthew 24:32

Learn from the fig tree: tender branches and new leaves tell you summer is near. 📖

## Matthew 24:33

Likewise, when you see all these things, you will know he is near, right at the door. 📖

## Matthew 24:34

I tell you truly, this generation will not pass away until all these things happen. 📖

## Matthew 24:35

Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away. 📖

## Matthew 24:36

Only the Father knows that day or hour. 📖

Nobody else knows, not even heaven's angels or the Son.

## Matthew 24:37

The Son of Man's coming will be like Noah's days. 📖

## Matthew 24:38

Before the flood, people ate, drank, married, and were given in marriage until Noah entered the ark. 📖

**Help:** The ark was the large boat in the account of Noah and the flood.

## Matthew 24:39

They did not realize what was happening until the flood swept them all away. 📖

The Son of Man's coming will be like that.

## Matthew 24:40

Two men will be in a field; one will be taken and the other left. 📖

## Matthew 24:41

Two women will be grinding grain at a mill; one will be taken and the other left. 📖

## Matthew 24:42

So keep watch, because you do not know which day your Lord will come. 📖

## Matthew 24:43

Understand this: if a householder knew which part of the night a thief would come, he would keep watch. 📖

He would not let anyone break into his house.

## Matthew 24:44

You too must be ready, because the Son of Man will come at an hour you do not expect. 📖

## Matthew 24:45

Who is the faithful, wise servant whom the master puts over his household to feed others at the right time? 📖

## Matthew 24:46

That servant is blessed if his master returns and finds him doing this. 📖

## Matthew 24:47

I tell you truly, the master will put him in charge of everything he owns. 📖

## Matthew 24:48

But suppose the servant is wicked and thinks his master will stay away a long time. 📖

## Matthew 24:49

He starts beating fellow servants and eating and drinking with drunk people. 📖

## Matthew 24:50

His master will come on an unexpected day, at an unexpected hour. 📖

## Matthew 24:51

The master will cut him into pieces and give him a place with hypocrites. 📖

There will be crying and grinding of teeth.”

**Help:** Hypocrites pretend to be different from what they really are; Jesus gives a severe picture of judgment.
