# Matthew 9 — Forgiveness, calling, and healing

**About this chapter**

Jesus forgives sins, calls Matthew, answers questions, and heals people.

He urges prayer for workers among the crowds.

## Matthew 9:1

Jesus crossed by boat to his own town. 📖

## Matthew 9:2

Men brought a paralysed man on a mat; seeing their faith, Jesus said, “Take courage, son; your sins are forgiven.” 📖

## Matthew 9:3

Some law teachers thought, “He is blaspheming!” 📖

**Help:** Blaspheming means insulting God or claiming what belongs to God.

## Matthew 9:4

Knowing their thoughts, Jesus asked, “Why think evil in your hearts? 📖

## Matthew 9:5

Which is easier: saying ‘Your sins are forgiven’ or ‘Get up and walk’? 📖

## Matthew 9:6

But this is so you may know the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins.” 📖

He told the paralysed man, “Get up, take your mat, and go home.”

## Matthew 9:7

The man got up and went home. 📖

## Matthew 9:8

The crowds were filled with awe and glorified God, who gave such authority to people. 📖

## Matthew 9:9

Jesus saw Matthew at the tax booth and said, “Follow me”; Matthew rose and followed. 📖

## Matthew 9:10

At Matthew's house, many tax collectors and sinners ate with Jesus and his disciples. 📖

## Matthew 9:11

Pharisees asked the disciples, “Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?” 📖

## Matthew 9:12

Jesus heard and said, “Sick people need a doctor, rather than healthy people. 📖

## Matthew 9:13

Learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice’; I came to call sinners, rather than the righteous.” 📖

**Help:** Mercy means kindness towards those in need, including forgiveness.

## Matthew 9:14

John's disciples asked, “Why do we and the Pharisees fast often, but your disciples do not?” 📖

## Matthew 9:15

Jesus replied, “Can the bridegroom's guests mourn with him present? 📖

When he is taken away, they will fast.

## Matthew 9:16

Nobody patches old clothing with unshrunk cloth; the patch pulls away and worsens the tear. 📖

## Matthew 9:17

New wine in old wineskins bursts the skins, spills the wine, and ruins the containers. 📖

New wine goes in new skins, preserving both.”

**Help:** Wineskins are leather containers; these pictures explain why the old and new cannot simply be combined.

The detailed interpretation needs further review.

## Matthew 9:18

While Jesus spoke, a synagogue leader knelt, saying, “My daughter just died. 📖

But lay your hand on her, and she will live.”

## Matthew 9:19

Jesus and his disciples followed him. 📖

## Matthew 9:20

A woman bleeding for twelve years approached behind and touched his cloak's fringe. 📖

## Matthew 9:21

She thought, “Just touching his cloak will heal me.” 📖

## Matthew 9:22

Jesus turned, saw her, and said, “Take courage, daughter; your faith healed you.” 📖

She was healed that very hour.

## Matthew 9:23

At the leader's house, Jesus saw flute players and a noisy crowd. 📖

## Matthew 9:24

He said, “Leave; she is asleep, not dead,” and they laughed at him. 📖

## Matthew 9:25

Once the crowd left, Jesus went in, took the girl's hand, and she rose. 📖

## Matthew 9:26

News spread throughout the region. 📖

## Matthew 9:27

Two blind men followed Jesus, crying, “Son of David, have mercy!” 📖

## Matthew 9:28

They came into the house, and Jesus asked, “Do you believe I can do this?” 📖

They answered, “Yes, Lord.”

## Matthew 9:29

He touched their eyes, saying, “Let it happen according to your faith.” 📖

## Matthew 9:30

Their eyes opened, and Jesus sternly warned, “Let no one learn of this!” 📖

## Matthew 9:31

They went and spread news about him throughout the land. 📖

## Matthew 9:32

A possessed man unable to speak was brought as they left. 📖

## Matthew 9:33

After the demon left, he spoke, and crowds marvelled, “Nothing like this has happened in Israel!” 📖

## Matthew 9:34

Pharisees said, “He expels demons by their prince.” 📖

**About these words:** This is their accusation, rather than the narrator's explanation of Jesus' power.

## Matthew 9:35

Jesus visited every town and village, taught in synagogues, preached the kingdom's good news, and healed every disease and sickness. 📖

## Matthew 9:36

He felt compassion for the harassed, helpless crowds, like sheep without a shepherd. 📖

## Matthew 9:37

He said, “The harvest is plentiful, but workers are few. 📖

## Matthew 9:38

Ask the harvest's Lord to send workers into his harvest.” 📖

**Help:** Harvest pictures the people who need the message and care of Jesus' workers.
