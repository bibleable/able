# Matthew 21 — Jesus enters Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jesus enters Jerusalem, acts in the temple, and tells stories about responding to God.

## Matthew 21:1

Near Jerusalem, they reached Bethphage on the Mount of Olives, and Jesus sent two disciples ahead. 📖

## Matthew 21:2

He said, “Go into the village ahead, where you will immediately find a tied donkey with her colt. 📖

Untie both and bring them to me.

**Help:** A colt is a young donkey.

## Matthew 21:3

If anyone asks, say the Lord needs them, and he will send them at once.” 📖

## Matthew 21:4

This fulfilled the message spoken through the prophet: 📖

## Matthew 21:5

“Tell the Daughter of Zion: Your King comes gently, riding a donkey, a colt born to a donkey.” 📖

**Help:** Daughter of Zion is a name for Jerusalem and its people.

## Matthew 21:6

The disciples went and did as Jesus instructed. 📖

## Matthew 21:7

They brought both animals and put their cloaks on them, and Jesus sat on the cloaks. 📖

## Matthew 21:8

A huge crowd spread cloaks on the road; others cut tree branches and spread them there. 📖

## Matthew 21:9

The crowds ahead and behind shouted, “Hosanna to David's Son! 📖

Blessed is the one coming in the Lord's name!

Hosanna in the highest!”

**Help:** Hosanna is a cry asking for rescue and expressing praise.

## Matthew 21:10

His entry stirred the whole city of Jerusalem, and people asked who he was. 📖

## Matthew 21:11

The crowds answered, “This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.” 📖

## Matthew 21:12

Jesus entered the temple courts and drove out everyone buying and selling there. 📖

He overturned the money changers' tables and the seats of people selling doves.

## Matthew 21:13

He said, “Scripture calls my house a house of prayer, but you make it a robbers' den.” 📖

## Matthew 21:14

Blind people and people who could not walk properly came to Jesus in the temple, and he healed them. 📖

## Matthew 21:15

The chief priests and law teachers saw his wonderful deeds and were angry. 📖

Children were shouting in the temple courts, “Hosanna to David's Son!”

## Matthew 21:16

They asked Jesus whether he heard the children. 📖

He answered, “Yes, have you never read that God arranged praise from the mouths of children and babies?”

## Matthew 21:17

He left them, went outside the city to Bethany, and spent the night there. 📖

## Matthew 21:18

Returning to the city in the morning, Jesus was hungry. 📖

## Matthew 21:19

He approached a roadside fig tree, but found only leaves. 📖

He said, “May you never grow fruit again!”

The tree dried up immediately.

## Matthew 21:20

The amazed disciples asked how the fig tree had dried up so quickly. 📖

## Matthew 21:21

Jesus answered, “I tell you truly, if you have faith without doubting, you will do what happened to this tree. 📖

Even if you tell this mountain to rise and fall into the sea, it will happen.

## Matthew 21:22

If you believe, you will receive whatever you ask for in prayer.” 📖

## Matthew 21:23

Jesus was teaching in the temple courts when the chief priests and the people's elders approached him. 📖

They asked what authority he had for these actions, and who gave it to him.

## Matthew 21:24

Jesus said, “I too will ask one question. 📖

If you answer, I will tell you my authority for these actions.

## Matthew 21:25

Did John's baptism come from heaven or from people?” 📖

They discussed this: “If we say heaven, he will ask why we did not believe John.

## Matthew 21:26

If we say people, we fear the crowd, because everyone considers John a prophet.” 📖

## Matthew 21:27

They answered, “We do not know.” 📖

Jesus replied, “Then I will not tell you my authority for these actions.

## Matthew 21:28

What do you think about this? 📖

A father had two sons and asked the first to work in the vineyard that day.

## Matthew 21:29

The son refused, but later changed his mind and went. 📖

## Matthew 21:30

The father asked the second son the same thing. 📖

He said, ‘I will, sir,’ but did not go.

## Matthew 21:31

Which son did what his father wanted?” 📖

They answered, “The first.”

Jesus said, “I tell you truly, tax collectors and prostitutes are entering God's kingdom before you.

**Help:** Prostitutes receive money for sex.

## Matthew 21:32

John came showing the right way to live, but you did not believe him. 📖

The tax collectors and prostitutes believed him.

Even after seeing this, you did not turn back and believe him.

## Matthew 21:33

Listen to another story: A landowner planted a vineyard, built a surrounding wall, dug a winepress, and built a tower. 📖

He rented it to tenants and went away on a journey.

**Help:** A winepress crushes grapes for juice; tenants rent property.

## Matthew 21:34

Near harvest, he sent servants to collect his share of the fruit from the tenants. 📖

## Matthew 21:35

They seized the servants, beat one, killed another, and threw stones at a third. 📖

## Matthew 21:36

He sent more servants than before, but the tenants treated them the same way. 📖

## Matthew 21:37

Finally, he sent his son, saying they would respect him. 📖

## Matthew 21:38

Seeing the son, the tenants said, ‘He will inherit this property. 📖

Let us kill him and take his inheritance.’

## Matthew 21:39

They seized him, threw him outside the vineyard, and killed him. 📖

## Matthew 21:40

When the owner returns, what will he do to those tenants?” 📖

## Matthew 21:41

They answered, “He will give those wicked people a terrible death. 📖

He will rent the vineyard to others who give his share of fruit at harvest.”

## Matthew 21:42

Jesus asked, “Have you never read this Scripture? 📖

The stone the builders rejected became the cornerstone.

The Lord did this, and it is wonderful in our eyes.

**Help:** A cornerstone is a main stone in a building; this picture describes a rejected person receiving importance.

## Matthew 21:43

So I tell you, God's kingdom will be taken from you and given to a people producing its fruit. 📖

## Matthew 21:44

Whoever falls on this stone will break into pieces; anyone it falls on will be crushed.” 📖

## Matthew 21:45

The chief priests and Pharisees heard these stories and knew Jesus meant them. 📖

## Matthew 21:46

They wanted to arrest him, but feared the crowds, who considered him a prophet. 📖
