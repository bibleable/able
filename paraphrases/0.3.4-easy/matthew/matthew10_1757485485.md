# Matthew 10 — Jesus sends the Twelve

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives the Twelve authority and instructions, warning them about opposition and calling for faithfulness.

## Matthew 10:1

Jesus called his twelve disciples and gave authority to expel unclean spirits and heal every disease and sickness. 📖

**Help:** Unclean spirits means evil spirits, rather than physical dirt or disability.

## Matthew 10:2

The twelve apostles' names follow. 📖

First were Simon called Peter and his brother Andrew.

There were James son of Zebedee and his brother John.

## Matthew 10:3

There were Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew the tax collector, James son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus. 📖

## Matthew 10:4

There were Simon the Zealot and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus. 📖

## Matthew 10:5

Jesus sent these twelve, saying, “Do not take the Gentiles' road or enter a Samaritan town. 📖

## Matthew 10:6

Go instead to Israel's lost sheep. 📖

**Help:** Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

Lost sheep pictures people needing God's care and direction.

## Matthew 10:7

Preach as you go, ‘Heaven's kingdom is near.’ 📖

## Matthew 10:8

Heal sick people, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, and expel demons; give freely because you received freely. 📖

**Help:** Lepers describes people with serious skin conditions.

Cleansing here concerns healing and religious standing, rather than moral blame for illness.

## Matthew 10:9

Take no gold, silver, or copper in your belts. 📖

## Matthew 10:10

Take no travel bag, second tunic, sandals, or staff; a worker deserves provisions. 📖

## Matthew 10:11

In any town or village, find a worthy person. 📖

Stay at their house until you move on from the town or village.

## Matthew 10:12

Greet the people when you enter their home. 📖

## Matthew 10:13

Let peace rest on a worthy home; otherwise let it return to you. 📖

## Matthew 10:14

If anyone rejects you or your words, shake your feet's dust off when leaving that home or town. 📖

## Matthew 10:15

Truly, Sodom and Gomorrah will find judgment day more bearable than that town. 📖

## Matthew 10:16

I send you like sheep among wolves; be shrewd as snakes and innocent as doves. 📖

**Help:** Shrewd means alert and wise about danger.

## Matthew 10:17

Beware of people who will hand you to councils and whip you in synagogues. 📖

## Matthew 10:18

Because of me, you will face governors and kings as witnesses to them and Gentiles. 📖

## Matthew 10:19

When handed over, do not worry about your reply; you will be given words that hour. 📖

## Matthew 10:20

Your Father's Spirit will speak through you, rather than you yourselves. 📖

## Matthew 10:21

Brothers will betray brothers to death, fathers their children, and children will have their parents killed. 📖

## Matthew 10:22

Everyone will hate you for my name, but whoever endures to the end will be saved. 📖

## Matthew 10:23

Flee persecution in one town to the next. 📖

Truly, you will not reach all Israel's towns before the Son of Man comes.

**About this promise:** Its timing and interpretation need further editorial review; the draft retains the stated limit.

## Matthew 10:24

A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a servant above the master. 📖

## Matthew 10:25

It is enough for disciples to be like teachers and servants like masters. 📖

If the household's head is called Beelzebul, how much more its members!

**Help:** Beelzebul is a name used for the ruler of demons.

## Matthew 10:26

Do not fear them; everything concealed will be revealed, and everything hidden made known. 📖

## Matthew 10:27

Speak in daylight what I tell you in darkness; proclaim from rooftops what is whispered. 📖

## Matthew 10:28

Do not fear those killing bodies who cannot kill the soul. 📖

Fear the one who can destroy both soul and body in hell.

## Matthew 10:29

Two sparrows sell for a penny, yet neither falls apart from your Father's will. 📖

## Matthew 10:30

Even every hair on your head is numbered. 📖

## Matthew 10:31

Do not fear; you are worth more than many sparrows. 📖

## Matthew 10:32

Whoever acknowledges me before people, I will acknowledge before my heavenly Father. 📖

## Matthew 10:33

Whoever denies me before people, I will deny before my heavenly Father. 📖

## Matthew 10:34

Do not think I came to bring earth peace; I brought a sword instead. 📖

## Matthew 10:35

I came to turn ‘son against father, daughter against mother, and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law. 📖

## Matthew 10:36

A person's enemies will be their household members.’ 📖

## Matthew 10:37

Anyone loving father or mother more than me is unworthy of me. 📖

Anyone loving son or daughter more than me is unworthy of me.

## Matthew 10:38

Anyone refusing to take their cross and follow me is unworthy of me. 📖

**Help:** The cross was an instrument of execution; carrying it pictures costly faithfulness to Jesus.

## Matthew 10:39

Whoever finds life will lose it; whoever loses life for my sake will find it. 📖

## Matthew 10:40

Whoever welcomes you welcomes me, and whoever welcomes me welcomes the one sending me. 📖

## Matthew 10:41

Welcoming a prophet because they are a prophet brings a prophet's reward. 📖

Welcoming a righteous person because they are righteous brings a righteous person's reward.

## Matthew 10:42

Suppose someone gives one of these little ones even a cup of cold water. 📖

Truly, if they do this because that person is my disciple, they will never lose their reward.”
