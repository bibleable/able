# Matthew 14 — John's death, food, and the sea

**About this chapter**

John is killed, Jesus feeds a crowd, and the disciples see him walking on water.

## Matthew 14:1

Herod the tetrarch heard reports about Jesus. 📖

**Help:** A tetrarch was a regional ruler.

## Matthew 14:2

He told his servants, “This is John the Baptist, risen from death; that explains his miraculous powers.” 📖

**About these words:** This is Herod's claim about Jesus.

## Matthew 14:3

Herod had bound and imprisoned John because of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife. 📖

## Matthew 14:4

John repeatedly told him, “You cannot lawfully have her.” 📖

## Matthew 14:5

Herod wanted John dead but feared the people, who considered John a prophet. 📖

## Matthew 14:6

On Herod's birthday, Herodias's daughter danced and pleased him. 📖

## Matthew 14:7

He swore to give whatever she requested. 📖

## Matthew 14:8

Prompted by her mother, she asked, “Give me John the Baptist's head here on a platter.” 📖

## Matthew 14:9

Grieved, the king nevertheless ordered it because of his oaths and guests. 📖

## Matthew 14:10

He sent orders to behead John in prison. 📖

## Matthew 14:11

The head came on a platter to the girl, who carried it to her mother. 📖

## Matthew 14:12

John's disciples buried his body and told Jesus. 📖

## Matthew 14:13

Hearing this, Jesus went privately by boat to a lonely place, but crowds followed on foot from towns. 📖

## Matthew 14:14

Landing, he saw the large crowd, felt compassion, and healed their sick. 📖

## Matthew 14:15

At evening, disciples said, “It is late and desolate; send them to villages to buy food.” 📖

## Matthew 14:16

Jesus replied, “They need not leave; you feed them.” 📖

## Matthew 14:17

They answered, “We have only five loaves and two fish.” 📖

## Matthew 14:18

He said, “Bring them here.” 📖

## Matthew 14:19

He told people to sit on grass and took the five loaves and two fish. 📖

He looked up to heaven and spoke a blessing.

He broke bread and gave it to disciples, who gave it to the people.

## Matthew 14:20

Everyone ate enough, and disciples gathered twelve baskets full of leftover pieces. 📖

## Matthew 14:21

About five thousand men ate, besides women and children. 📖

## Matthew 14:22

He immediately sent disciples ahead across the water by boat while dismissing crowds. 📖

## Matthew 14:23

After dismissing them, he went alone up the mountain to pray; evening found him alone. 📖

## Matthew 14:24

The boat was far from land, battered by waves against the wind. 📖

## Matthew 14:25

During the night's fourth watch, Jesus walked to them on the sea. 📖

**Help:** A watch was a period during the night.

## Matthew 14:26

Terrified, they cried, “A ghost!” 📖

They saw him walking on the sea.

## Matthew 14:27

He immediately said, “Take courage; it is I; do not fear.” 📖

## Matthew 14:28

Peter replied, “Lord, if it is you, command me to come on the water.” 📖

## Matthew 14:29

Jesus said, “Come”; Peter left the boat and walked on the water towards him. 📖

## Matthew 14:30

Seeing the strong wind, Peter feared and began sinking, crying, “Lord, save me!” 📖

## Matthew 14:31

Jesus immediately reached out his hand and grasped Peter, saying, “You of little faith, why doubt?” 📖

## Matthew 14:32

They entered the boat, and the wind stopped. 📖

## Matthew 14:33

Those aboard worshipped him, saying, “Truly you are God's Son!” 📖

## Matthew 14:34

They landed at Gennesaret. 📖

## Matthew 14:35

Recognising Jesus, the local men alerted the whole region, and people brought all sick people. 📖

## Matthew 14:36

They begged to touch just his cloak's fringe, and everyone touching him was healed. 📖
