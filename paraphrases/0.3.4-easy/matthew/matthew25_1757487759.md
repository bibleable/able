# Matthew 25 — Readiness, trust, and judgment

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells two stories and describes the Son of Man judging the nations.

## Matthew 25:1

“At that time, heaven's kingdom will be like ten virgins taking lamps to meet a bridegroom. 📖

**Help:** Virgins are people who have not had sex; here they are young women attending a wedding.

A bridegroom is a man getting married.

## Matthew 25:2

Five were foolish, and five were wise. 📖

## Matthew 25:3

The foolish ones took lamps without extra oil. 📖

## Matthew 25:4

The wise ones took flasks of oil with their lamps. 📖

## Matthew 25:5

The bridegroom was late, and they all grew sleepy and fell asleep. 📖

## Matthew 25:6

At midnight, a shout announced the bridegroom and told them to come and meet him. 📖

## Matthew 25:7

All the virgins woke and trimmed their lamps. 📖

## Matthew 25:8

The foolish ones asked the wise for oil because their lamps were going out. 📖

## Matthew 25:9

The wise refused, saying there might not be enough for everyone. 📖

They told the others to buy their own from the oil sellers.

## Matthew 25:10

While they went to buy oil, the bridegroom arrived. 📖

Those ready went inside with him to the wedding feast, and the door was shut.

## Matthew 25:11

The other virgins arrived later and cried, ‘Lord, lord, open the door for us!’ 📖

## Matthew 25:12

He answered, ‘I tell you truly, I do not know you.’ 📖

## Matthew 25:13

So keep watch, because you do not know the day or hour. 📖

## Matthew 25:14

It is also like a man going on a journey, calling his servants and trusting them with his possessions. 📖

## Matthew 25:15

He gave one five talents, another two talents, and another one talent, each according to ability. 📖

Then he left on his journey.

**Help:** Here a talent is a large amount of money, not a skill.

## Matthew 25:16

The servant with five talents immediately used them to earn five more. 📖

## Matthew 25:17

Likewise, the servant with two talents earned two more. 📖

## Matthew 25:18

The servant with one talent dug a hole and hid his master's money in the ground. 📖

## Matthew 25:19

After a long time, the master returned and checked their accounts. 📖

## Matthew 25:20

The servant with five talents brought five more. 📖

He said, ‘Master, you trusted me with five talents, and I have gained five more.’

## Matthew 25:21

His master said, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant! 📖

You were faithful with a few things, so I will put you over many things.

Share your master's joy!’

## Matthew 25:22

The servant with two talents came and said, ‘Master, you trusted me with two talents. 📖

I have gained two more.’

## Matthew 25:23

His master said, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant! 📖

You were faithful with a few things, so I will put you over many things.

Share your master's joy!’

## Matthew 25:24

The servant with one talent said, ‘Master, I knew you were a hard man. 📖

You harvest where you never planted and gather where you never scattered seed.

## Matthew 25:25

I was afraid and hid your talent in the ground. 📖

Here is what belongs to you.’

## Matthew 25:26

His master replied, ‘You wicked, lazy servant! 📖

You knew I harvest where I never planted and gather where I never scattered seed.

## Matthew 25:27

You should therefore have put my money with the bankers. 📖

Then I would have received it back with interest when I returned.

**Help:** Interest is extra money paid for keeping or lending money.

## Matthew 25:28

Take his talent and give it to the servant with ten talents. 📖

## Matthew 25:29

Everyone who has will receive more and have plenty. 📖

Whoever does not have will lose even what he has.

## Matthew 25:30

Throw this worthless servant into the darkness outside, where there will be crying and grinding of teeth.’ 📖

## Matthew 25:31

When the Son of Man comes in glory with all his angels, he will sit on his glorious throne. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Matthew 25:32

All nations will gather before him. 📖

He will separate people, like a shepherd separating sheep from goats.

## Matthew 25:33

He will put the sheep on his right and the goats on his left. 📖

## Matthew 25:34

The King will tell those on his right, ‘Come, you whom my Father has blessed. 📖

Inherit the kingdom prepared for you since the world's beginning.

## Matthew 25:35

I was hungry and you fed me, thirsty and you gave me a drink, a stranger and you welcomed me. 📖

## Matthew 25:36

I was naked and you clothed me, sick and you cared for me, in prison and you visited me.’ 📖

## Matthew 25:37

The righteous will ask, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry and feed you? 📖

When did we see you thirsty and give you a drink?

## Matthew 25:38

When did we see you as a stranger and welcome you, or naked and clothe you? 📖

## Matthew 25:39

When did we see you sick or in prison and visit you?’ 📖

## Matthew 25:40

The King will reply with this truth. 📖

‘Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of mine, you did for me.’

## Matthew 25:41

He will tell those on his left, ‘You cursed people, leave me for the eternal fire. 📖

It was prepared for the devil and his angels.

## Matthew 25:42

I was hungry and you gave me no food, thirsty and you gave me no drink. 📖

## Matthew 25:43

I was a stranger and you did not welcome me, naked and you did not clothe me. 📖

I was sick and in prison and you did not care for me.’

## Matthew 25:44

They too will ask, ‘Lord, when did we fail to serve you? 📖

When did we see you hungry, thirsty, a stranger, naked, sick, or in prison?’

## Matthew 25:45

The King will reply with this truth. 📖

‘Whatever you failed to do for one of these least ones, you failed to do for me.’

## Matthew 25:46

They will go into eternal punishment, but the righteous will go into eternal life.” 📖

**Help:** Righteous describes people who are right before God.
