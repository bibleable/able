# Matthew 5 — Jesus teaches how to live

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches his followers on a mountain.

His words include blessings, warnings, and commands.

## Matthew 5:1

Jesus saw the crowds and went up a mountain. 📖

He sat down, and his followers came to him.

## Matthew 5:2

Jesus began to teach them. 📖

## Matthew 5:3

“God blesses people who know they are poor in spirit. 📖

The kingdom of heaven belongs to them.”

**Help with words:** Poor in spirit describes people who know they need God.

## Matthew 5:4

“God blesses people who mourn. 📖

They will be comforted.”

**Help with a word:** To mourn is to feel deep sadness over loss.

## Matthew 5:5

“God blesses gentle, humble people. 📖

They will receive the earth.”

## Matthew 5:6

“God blesses people who hunger and thirst for what is right. 📖

They will be satisfied.”

**Help with this picture:** Hunger and thirst describe a very strong desire.

## Matthew 5:7

“God blesses people who show mercy. 📖

They will receive mercy.”

**Help with a word:** Mercy means kindness and forgiveness towards people in need.

## Matthew 5:8

“God blesses people whose hearts are pure. 📖

They will see God.”

**Help with words:** Pure hearts are inner lives free from what is evil and false.

## Matthew 5:9

“God blesses people who make peace. 📖

They will be called God's children.”

## Matthew 5:10

“God blesses people attacked because they do right. 📖

The kingdom of heaven belongs to them.”

## Matthew 5:11

“God blesses you when people insult and attack you because of me. 📖

They may tell all kinds of evil lies about you.”

## Matthew 5:12

“Be glad! 📖

Your reward in heaven is great.

People attacked God's prophets in the same way before you.”

**Help with a word:** Prophets speak God's messages.

## Matthew 5:13

“You are the salt of the earth. 📖

If salt loses its taste, how can it become salty again?

It is useless.

People throw it out and walk on it.”

**Help with this picture:** Salt pictures the valuable effect Jesus' followers should have.

## Matthew 5:14

“You are the light of the world. 📖

A city on a hill cannot be hidden.”

## Matthew 5:15

“People put a lamp on a stand, not under a basket. 📖

Then it gives light to everyone in the house.”

## Matthew 5:16

“Let your light shine before others. 📖

Let them see your good actions and praise your Father in heaven.”

## Matthew 5:17

“I did not come to destroy the Law or the Prophets. 📖

I came to fulfil them.”

**Help with words:** The Law and the Prophets are parts of the Jewish Scriptures.

Fulfil means bring their purpose to completion.

## Matthew 5:18

“I tell you the truth. 📖

Not even the smallest written mark will disappear from the Law until everything is done.

This remains true while heaven and earth remain.”

## Matthew 5:19

“Anyone who breaks even a small command and teaches others to do so will be least in heaven's kingdom. 📖

Anyone who obeys and teaches these commands will be called great there.”

## Matthew 5:20

“You must do right more fully than the law teachers and Pharisees. 📖

Otherwise, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven.”

**Help with a word:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## Matthew 5:21

“Long ago, people were told not to murder. 📖

Anyone who murders must face judgment.”

## Matthew 5:22

“But anyone angry with a brother must also face judgment. 📖

Anyone calling a brother ‘Raca’ must face the council.

Anyone saying ‘You fool!’ is in danger of hell's fire.”

**Help with a word:** Raca is an insult meaning someone is worthless.

## Matthew 5:23

“Suppose you bring a gift to God's altar. 📖

There you remember that your brother has something against you.”

**Help with a word:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

## Matthew 5:24

“Leave your gift there. 📖

First go and make peace with your brother.

Then come back and offer your gift.”

## Matthew 5:25

“Settle quickly with someone taking you to court. 📖

Do it while you are on the way.

Otherwise, the judge may hand you to an officer and send you to prison.”

## Matthew 5:26

“I tell you the truth. 📖

You will stay there until you pay the very last coin.”

## Matthew 5:27

“You have heard the command not to commit adultery.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Adultery means having sex with someone who is married to another person.

## Matthew 5:28

“But looking at a woman with the desire to have sex with her is already adultery in your heart.” 📖

## Matthew 5:29

“If your right eye causes you to sin, pull it out and throw it away. 📖

Losing one body part is better than your whole body being thrown into hell.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus uses shocking exaggeration to show how serious sin is.

This is not a direction to injure yourself.

## Matthew 5:30

“If your right hand causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it away. 📖

Losing one body part is better than your whole body going to hell.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus continues the exaggeration about rejecting sin, not injuring yourself.

## Matthew 5:31

“People were also told to give a wife a written divorce document when sending her away.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Divorce legally ends a marriage.

## Matthew 5:32

“But divorcing a wife makes her involved in adultery, except when she has been sexually unfaithful. 📖

Anyone who marries a divorced woman also commits adultery.”

## Matthew 5:33

“Long ago, people were told not to break sworn promises. 📖

They were told to keep their promises to the Lord.”

## Matthew 5:34

“But I tell you not to swear such promises at all. 📖

Do not swear by heaven, because it is God's throne.”

## Matthew 5:35

“Do not swear by earth, because it is God's footstool. 📖

Do not swear by Jerusalem, because it is the great King's city.”

**Help with this picture:** Throne and footstool picture God's rule over everything.

## Matthew 5:36

“Do not swear by your own head. 📖

You cannot make even one hair white or black.”

## Matthew 5:37

“Simply say ‘Yes’ or ‘No’ and mean it. 📖

Anything more comes from the evil one.”

## Matthew 5:38

“You have heard the rule: an eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.” 📖

**Help with this rule:** It describes matching punishment to an injury.

## Matthew 5:39

“But do not fight back against an evil person. 📖

If someone slaps your right cheek, turn the other cheek too.”

## Matthew 5:40

“If someone takes you to court for your shirt, give your coat too.” 📖

## Matthew 5:41

“If someone forces you to go 1 mile, go 2 miles.” 📖

## Matthew 5:42

“Give to someone who asks. 📖

Do not turn away someone who wants to borrow.”

## Matthew 5:43

“You have heard people say to love your neighbour and hate your enemy.” 📖

## Matthew 5:44

“But I tell you to love your enemies. 📖

Pray for those who attack you.”

## Matthew 5:45

“Then you will be children of your Father in heaven. 📖

He gives sunlight to evil and good people.

He gives rain to people who do right and people who do wrong.”

## Matthew 5:46

“What reward is there in loving only those who love you? 📖

Even tax collectors do that.”

## Matthew 5:47

“What is special about greeting only your own people? 📖

Even people outside the Jewish faith do that.”

## Matthew 5:48

“Be perfect, as your Father in heaven is perfect.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Perfect here describes being complete in goodness.
