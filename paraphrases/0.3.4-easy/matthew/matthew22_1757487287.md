# Matthew 22 — Love God and love your neighbour

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells a story and answers questions from religious leaders.

## Matthew 22:1

Jesus spoke to them again using stories that teach. 📖

## Matthew 22:2

“The kingdom of heaven is like a king preparing a wedding meal for his son.” 📖

## Matthew 22:3

“He sent servants to call the invited guests. 📖

But they refused to come.”

## Matthew 22:4

“He sent more servants with a message. 📖

The meal is ready.

The oxen and fattened cattle have been killed.

Everything is ready, so come to the wedding meal.”

## Matthew 22:5

“The guests ignored the message. 📖

One went to his field and another to his business.”

## Matthew 22:6

“Others grabbed the servants, hurt them, and killed them.” 📖

## Matthew 22:7

“The king was furious. 📖

He sent soldiers to destroy the murderers and burn their city.”

## Matthew 22:8

“The king told his servants that the wedding meal was ready. 📖

But the invited guests were not worthy to come.”

## Matthew 22:9

“He told them to go to the roads where people meet. 📖

They must invite everyone they could find.”

## Matthew 22:10

“The servants gathered everyone they found, both good and evil. 📖

The wedding hall filled with guests.”

## Matthew 22:11

“The king came to see the guests. 📖

He saw a man who was not wearing wedding clothes.”

## Matthew 22:12

“The king asked, “Friend, how did you enter without wedding clothes?” 📖

The man had no answer.”

## Matthew 22:13

“The king ordered his servants to tie the man's hands and feet. 📖

The king told them to throw him outside into darkness.

There people would cry and grind their teeth in pain.”

## Matthew 22:14

Jesus said, “Many are called, but few are chosen.” 📖

## Matthew 22:15

The Pharisees planned to trap Jesus with a question. 📖

**Help with a word:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## Matthew 22:16

They sent their followers with supporters of Herod. 📖

They said, “Teacher, we know you are honest and teach God's way truthfully.

You seek nobody's favour and pay no attention to outward appearance.”

## Matthew 22:17

“Tell us what you think: is paying taxes to Caesar lawful or not?” 📖

**Help with a name:** Caesar was the Roman emperor.

## Matthew 22:18

Jesus knew their evil plan. 📖

He said, “You pretenders!

Why are you testing me?”

## Matthew 22:19

“Show me the tax coin.” 📖

They brought him a denarius.

**Help with a word:** A denarius was a Roman silver coin.

## Matthew 22:20

Jesus asked, “Whose picture and name are on it?” 📖

## Matthew 22:21

They said, “Caesar's.” 📖

Jesus answered, “Give Caesar what belongs to Caesar.

Give God what belongs to God.”

## Matthew 22:22

They were amazed by his answer. 📖

They left him and went away.

## Matthew 22:23

That day, Sadducees came with a question. 📖

They said that the dead would not rise again.

**Help with a word:** Sadducees were a Jewish religious group.

## Matthew 22:24

They said, “Teacher, Moses said that if a man dies childless, his brother must marry the widow. 📖

The brother must raise children for the dead man.”

## Matthew 22:25

“There were 7 brothers among us. 📖

The first married and died without children.

His brother then married the widow.”

## Matthew 22:26

“The same happened to the second and third brothers, and then to all 7.” 📖

## Matthew 22:27

“Finally the woman died too.” 📖

## Matthew 22:28

“When the dead rise, whose wife will she be? 📖

All 7 brothers married her.”

## Matthew 22:29

Jesus said, “You are mistaken. 📖

You do not understand the Scriptures or God's power.”

**Help with a word:** Scriptures are the sacred writings of the Bible.

## Matthew 22:30

“When the dead rise, people will neither marry nor be given in marriage. 📖

They will be like the angels in heaven.”

## Matthew 22:31

“About the dead rising, have you not read what God told you?” 📖

## Matthew 22:32

“God said, ‘I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’ 📖

He is God of the living, not the dead.”

## Matthew 22:33

The crowds were amazed at Jesus' teaching. 📖

## Matthew 22:34

The Pharisees heard that Jesus had silenced the Sadducees. 📖

They gathered together.

## Matthew 22:35

An expert in the law tested Jesus with a question. 📖

## Matthew 22:36

“Teacher, which command in the Law is greatest?” 📖

## Matthew 22:37

Jesus said, “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and mind.” 📖

**Help with these words:** Love God with your whole inner life and all your thinking.

## Matthew 22:38

“This is the first and greatest command.” 📖

## Matthew 22:39

“The second is like it. 📖

Love your neighbour as yourself.”

## Matthew 22:40

“All the Law and the Prophets depend on these 2 commands.” 📖

**Help with words:** The Law and the Prophets are parts of the Jewish Scriptures.

## Matthew 22:41

While the Pharisees were together, Jesus asked them a question. 📖

## Matthew 22:42

“What do you think about the Christ? 📖

Whose son is he?”

They answered, “David's.”

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

They meant he would come from King David's family.

## Matthew 22:43

Jesus said, “Then why does David, speaking by the Spirit, call him Lord?” 📖

## Matthew 22:44

“David says, ‘The Lord told my Lord: Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies under your feet.’” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Sitting at the right hand shows honour and power.

Enemies under someone's feet pictures defeat.

## Matthew 22:45

“If David calls him Lord, how can he be David's son?” 📖

## Matthew 22:46

No one could answer. 📖

From that day, no one dared to question Jesus further.
