# Matthew 17 — Glory, healing, and the tax

**About this chapter**

Three disciples see Jesus transformed; he later heals a boy and instructs Peter about a tax.

## Matthew 17:1

Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and James's brother John alone up a high mountain. 📖

## Matthew 17:2

He was transformed before them; his face shone like the sun and clothes became white as light. 📖

## Matthew 17:3

Moses and Elijah appeared, talking with him. 📖

## Matthew 17:4

Peter said, “Lord, it is good here; if you wish, I will build three shelters for you, Moses, and Elijah.” 📖

## Matthew 17:5

While he spoke, a bright cloud surrounded them. 📖

Its voice said, “This is my beloved Son, who pleases me; listen to him!”

## Matthew 17:6

Terrified, the disciples fell facedown. 📖

## Matthew 17:7

Jesus touched them, saying, “Get up; do not fear.” 📖

## Matthew 17:8

Looking up, they saw only Jesus. 📖

## Matthew 17:9

Descending, he commanded silence about the vision until the Son of Man rose from death. 📖

## Matthew 17:10

They asked why law teachers said Elijah must come first. 📖

## Matthew 17:11

Jesus replied, “Elijah indeed comes to restore everything. 📖

## Matthew 17:12

But he already came; unrecognised, he suffered whatever they wanted, as the Son of Man will suffer from them.” 📖

## Matthew 17:13

They understood he meant John the Baptist. 📖

## Matthew 17:14

At the crowd, a man knelt before Jesus. 📖

## Matthew 17:15

“Lord, have mercy on my son, suffering terrible seizures and often falling into fire or water. 📖

## Matthew 17:16

I brought him to your disciples, but they could not heal him.” 📖

## Matthew 17:17

Jesus said, “Unbelieving, twisted generation, how long must I stay and bear with you? 📖

Bring him here.”

## Matthew 17:18

He rebuked the demon; it left, and the boy was healed immediately. 📖

**About this healing:** The account describes this boy's situation without saying all seizures result from demons.

## Matthew 17:19

Privately, the disciples asked why they could not expel it. 📖

## Matthew 17:20

He said, “Your faith is too small. 📖

Truly, if your faith is a mustard seed's size, you can tell this mountain, ‘Move from here to there.’

It will move, and nothing will be impossible for you.”

## Matthew 17:21

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Matthew 17:22

In Galilee, Jesus said, “The Son of Man will soon be handed to people. 📖

## Matthew 17:23

They will kill him, and he will rise on the third day.”

The disciples grieved deeply. 📖

## Matthew 17:24

In Capernaum, collectors asked Peter, “Does your teacher pay the two-drachma tax?” 📖

**Help:** A drachma was a coin.

## Matthew 17:25

Peter answered yes, but Jesus spoke first when he entered the house. 📖

“Simon, do kings collect taxes and customs from their sons or from others?”

## Matthew 17:26

Peter said, “Others”; Jesus replied, “Then sons are exempt. 📖

## Matthew 17:27

To avoid offence, fish in the sea with a hook; take the first fish. 📖

In its mouth, find a four-drachma coin and pay both our taxes with it.”
