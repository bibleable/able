# Matthew 1 — Jesus' family line and birth

**About this chapter**

Matthew records Jesus' family line and describes his birth through the Holy Spirit.

## Matthew 1:1

This is the family record of Jesus Christ, son of David and son of Abraham. 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed, chosen king.

Son of here describes descent through a family line.

## Matthew 1:2

Abraham was Isaac's father. 📖

Isaac was Jacob's father.

Jacob was the father of Judah and his brothers.

## Matthew 1:3

Judah and Tamar were the parents of Perez and Zerah. 📖

Perez was Hezron's father.

Hezron was Ram's father.

## Matthew 1:4

Ram was Amminadab's father. 📖

Amminadab was Nahshon's father.

Nahshon was Salmon's father.

## Matthew 1:5

Salmon and Rahab were Boaz's parents. 📖

Boaz and Ruth were Obed's parents.

Obed was Jesse's father.

## Matthew 1:6

Jesse was King David's father. 📖

David and Uriah's wife were Solomon's parents.

## Matthew 1:7

Solomon was Rehoboam's father. 📖

Rehoboam was Abijah's father.

Abijah was Asa's father.

## Matthew 1:8

Asa was Jehoshaphat's father. 📖

Jehoshaphat was Joram's father.

Joram was Uzziah's father.

## Matthew 1:9

Uzziah was Jotham's father. 📖

Jotham was Ahaz's father.

Ahaz was Hezekiah's father.

## Matthew 1:10

Hezekiah was Manasseh's father. 📖

Manasseh was Amon's father.

Amon was Josiah's father.

## Matthew 1:11

Josiah was the father of Jeconiah and his brothers around the exile to Babylon. 📖

**Help:** Exile means being forced away from one's homeland.

## Matthew 1:12

After the Babylonian exile, Jeconiah was Shealtiel's father. 📖

Shealtiel was Zerubbabel's father.

## Matthew 1:13

Zerubbabel was Abiud's father. 📖

Abiud was Eliakim's father.

Eliakim was Azor's father.

## Matthew 1:14

Azor was Zadok's father. 📖

Zadok was Achim's father.

Achim was Eliud's father.

## Matthew 1:15

Eliud was Eleazar's father. 📖

Eleazar was Matthan's father.

Matthan was Jacob's father.

## Matthew 1:16

Jacob was Joseph's father. 📖

Joseph was Mary's husband.

Jesus, called Christ, was born of Mary.

## Matthew 1:17

There were fourteen generations from Abraham to David, fourteen from David to Babylon's exile, and fourteen from exile to Christ. 📖

**Help:** Generations are stages in a family line, such as parents, children, and grandchildren.

## Matthew 1:18

Jesus Christ's birth happened this way: Mary was pledged to marry Joseph. 📖

Before they came together, she was found pregnant through the Holy Spirit.

## Matthew 1:19

Joseph, her righteous husband, planned a quiet divorce because he did not want to disgrace her publicly. 📖

**Help:** Their marriage pledge was binding, so ending it required divorce.

Righteous means living rightly before God.

## Matthew 1:20

As he considered this, the Lord's angel appeared in a dream. 📖

“Joseph, David's son, do not fear taking Mary as wife.

Her child is conceived through the Holy Spirit.

## Matthew 1:21

She will bear a son; name him Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins.” 📖

## Matthew 1:22

This fulfilled the Lord's words through the prophet. 📖

## Matthew 1:23

“The virgin will conceive and bear a son, and they will call him Immanuel,” meaning “God with us.” 📖

**Help:** A virgin is someone who has not had sexual relations.

## Matthew 1:24

Joseph awoke, obeyed the Lord's angel, and took Mary as his wife. 📖

## Matthew 1:25

He had no sexual relations with her until she bore a son, whom he named Jesus. 📖
