# Matthew 2 — Visitors, danger, and return

**About this chapter**

Magi worship Jesus; his family escapes Herod and later settles in Nazareth.

## Matthew 2:1

Jesus was born in Bethlehem, Judea, during King Herod's reign; Magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem. 📖

**Help:** Magi were learned men who studied signs such as stars.

The text does not state how many came.

## Matthew 2:2

They asked, “Where is the newborn King of the Jews? 📖

We saw his star in the east and came to worship him.”

## Matthew 2:3

Herod was disturbed, and all Jerusalem with him. 📖

## Matthew 2:4

He gathered all chief priests and law experts, asking where the Christ would be born. 📖

## Matthew 2:5

They answered, “In Bethlehem, Judea, as the prophet wrote. 📖

## Matthew 2:6

‘Bethlehem in Judah, you are far from least among Judah's rulers. 📖

A ruler will come from you to shepherd my people Israel.’”

## Matthew 2:7

Herod secretly questioned the Magi about exactly when the star appeared. 📖

## Matthew 2:8

He sent them to Bethlehem, saying, “Search carefully and report when you find the child, so I may worship too.” 📖

## Matthew 2:9

They left; the star seen in the east led them and stopped above the child's location. 📖

## Matthew 2:10

Seeing the star, they rejoiced with great delight. 📖

## Matthew 2:11

Entering the house, they saw the child and Mary, fell down, and worshipped him. 📖

They opened their treasures, offering gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

**Help:** Frankincense and myrrh are fragrant substances.

## Matthew 2:12

Warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they went home another way. 📖

## Matthew 2:13

After they left, the Lord's angel told Joseph in a dream, “Take the child and his mother; flee to Egypt. 📖

Stay until I tell you, because Herod will seek the child to kill him.”

## Matthew 2:14

Joseph rose, took them by night, and fled to Egypt. 📖

## Matthew 2:15

He stayed until Herod died, fulfilling the Lord's prophetic words, “I called my son out of Egypt.” 📖

## Matthew 2:16

Furious at being outwitted, Herod had all boys aged two and under in Bethlehem and nearby areas killed. 📖

He used the time learned from the Magi.

## Matthew 2:17

Jeremiah's prophecy was fulfilled. 📖

## Matthew 2:18

“A voice in Ramah weeps and mourns deeply; Rachel weeps for her children, refusing comfort because they are dead.” 📖

## Matthew 2:19

After Herod died, the Lord's angel appeared to Joseph in a dream while he was in Egypt. 📖

## Matthew 2:20

He said, “Get up; take the child and his mother to Israel, because those seeking his life are dead.” 📖

## Matthew 2:21

Joseph rose, took them, and entered Israel. 📖

## Matthew 2:22

Learning Archelaus ruled Judea instead of his father Herod, he feared going there. 📖

Warned in a dream, he withdrew to Galilee.

## Matthew 2:23

He settled in Nazareth, fulfilling the prophets' words, “He will be called a Nazarene.” 📖
