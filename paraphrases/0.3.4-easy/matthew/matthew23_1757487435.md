# Matthew 23 — Warnings to religious leaders

**About this chapter**

Jesus warns the crowds and disciples about religious leaders and grieves over Jerusalem.

## Matthew 23:1

Jesus spoke to the crowds and his disciples: 📖

## Matthew 23:2

“The law teachers and Pharisees sit in Moses' seat. 📖

**Help:** This picture describes their position as teachers of Moses' law.

## Matthew 23:3

So obey everything they tell you, but do not copy their actions. 📖

They do not practise what they teach.

## Matthew 23:4

They tie up heavy loads for people's shoulders, but will not lift a finger to help move them. 📖

## Matthew 23:5

Everything they do is for people to see. 📖

They make their phylacteries wider and their tassels longer.

**Help:** Phylacteries are small boxes containing Scripture, worn for prayer; tassels are threads hanging from clothing.

## Matthew 23:6

They love places of honour at feasts and the main seats in synagogues. 📖

**Help:** Synagogues are Jewish places of worship and teaching.

## Matthew 23:7

They love greetings in marketplaces and being called Rabbi. 📖

**Help:** Rabbi is a title for a religious teacher.

## Matthew 23:8

But do not be called Rabbi, because you have one Teacher, and you are all brothers. 📖

## Matthew 23:9

Do not call anyone on earth your father, because your one Father is in heaven. 📖

## Matthew 23:10

Do not be called instructors, because your one Instructor is the Christ. 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Matthew 23:11

Your greatest person will be your servant. 📖

## Matthew 23:12

Whoever raises himself high will be brought low; whoever makes himself low will be raised high. 📖

## Matthew 23:13

Woe to you, law teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! 📖

You shut heaven's kingdom in people's faces.

You neither enter yourselves nor allow in those wanting to enter.

**Help:** Woe warns of grief and judgment; hypocrites pretend to be different from what they really are.

## Matthew 23:14

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Matthew 23:15

Woe to you, law teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! 📖

You cross land and sea to win one follower of your religion.

Then you make him twice as much a son of hell as yourselves.

**Help:** Son of hell is a severe picture of belonging to hell, not a description of someone's parents.

## Matthew 23:16

Woe to you, blind guides! 📖

You say promising by the temple means nothing, but promising by its gold makes the promise binding.

**Help:** Blind guides describes people failing to see the truth; it does not blame people with sight loss.

## Matthew 23:17

You blind fools, which is greater: the gold, or the temple making the gold holy? 📖

## Matthew 23:18

You say promising by the altar means nothing, but promising by the gift on it makes the promise binding. 📖

**Help:** An altar is where offerings are made to God.

## Matthew 23:19

You blind men, which is greater: the gift, or the altar making the gift holy? 📖

## Matthew 23:20

Anyone promising by the altar promises by it and everything on it. 📖

## Matthew 23:21

Anyone promising by the temple promises by it and the one living there. 📖

## Matthew 23:22

Anyone promising by heaven promises by God's throne and the one sitting on it. 📖

## Matthew 23:23

Woe to you, law teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! 📖

You give a tenth of mint, dill, and cumin, but neglect the law's more important matters: justice, mercy, and faithfulness.

You should practise these without neglecting the others.

**Help:** Mint, dill, and cumin are herbs used in food; giving a tenth is called tithing.

## Matthew 23:24

You blind guides, you strain out a gnat but swallow a camel! 📖

**Help:** A gnat is a tiny insect; this picture contrasts attention to small matters with neglect of large ones.

## Matthew 23:25

Woe to you, law teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! 📖

You clean a cup and dish outside, but inside they are full of greed and pleasing yourselves.

## Matthew 23:26

Blind Pharisee, clean the cup and dish inside first, so the outside can become clean too. 📖

## Matthew 23:27

Woe to you, law teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! 📖

You resemble tombs painted white, beautiful outside but full of dead people's bones and every kind of uncleanness inside.

## Matthew 23:28

Likewise, you look righteous outside, but inside are full of hypocrisy and wickedness. 📖

**Help:** Righteous means right before God; Jesus is speaking about hidden wrongdoing, not physical dirt or illness.

## Matthew 23:29

Woe to you, law teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! 📖

You build prophets' tombs and decorate the monuments of righteous people.

## Matthew 23:30

You say you would not have joined your ancestors in killing the prophets if you had lived then. 📖

## Matthew 23:31

You therefore testify against yourselves that you are children of those who murdered the prophets. 📖

## Matthew 23:32

Go on, then, and fill up the measure of your ancestors' sin! 📖

## Matthew 23:33

You snakes, you family of vipers, how will you escape being sentenced to hell? 📖

**Help:** Vipers are poisonous snakes; Jesus uses this image in a severe warning.

## Matthew 23:34

Because of this, I send you prophets, wise men, and teachers. 📖

You will kill and crucify some, and whip others in your synagogues and persecute them from town to town.

**Help:** Crucify means kill on a cross; persecute means mistreat people because of their beliefs.

## Matthew 23:35

So you will answer for all the righteous blood shed on earth, from righteous Abel to Zechariah, son of Berechiah. 📖

You murdered Zechariah between the temple and the altar.

## Matthew 23:36

I tell you truly, all this will come upon this generation. 📖

## Matthew 23:37

Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you kill the prophets and stone those sent to you! 📖

How often I wanted to gather your children like a hen gathering chicks under her wings!

But you were unwilling.

## Matthew 23:38

Look, your house is left empty and ruined. 📖

## Matthew 23:39

I tell you: you will not see me again until you say these words. 📖

‘Blessed is the one coming in the Lord's name.’”
