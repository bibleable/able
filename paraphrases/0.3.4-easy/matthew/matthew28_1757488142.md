# Matthew 28 — Jesus is alive

**About this chapter**

After Jesus dies, his followers learn that he has risen from death.

## Matthew 28:1

After the Sabbath, dawn came on the first day of the week. 📖

Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to the tomb.

**Help with a word:** A tomb is a place where a dead body is laid.

## Matthew 28:2

A strong earthquake happened. 📖

An angel of the Lord came down from heaven.

He rolled away the stone and sat on it.

## Matthew 28:3

The angel shone like lightning. 📖

His clothes were white like snow.

## Matthew 28:4

The guards shook with fear. 📖

They became like dead men.

## Matthew 28:5

The angel told the women, “Do not be afraid. 📖

You are looking for Jesus, who was killed on a cross.”

## Matthew 28:6

“He is not here. 📖

He has risen, just as he said!

Come and see where his body lay.”

## Matthew 28:7

“Go quickly and tell his followers. 📖

He has risen from death and is going ahead to Galilee.

You will see him there.

Remember what I have told you.”

## Matthew 28:8

The women hurried away, afraid and full of joy. 📖

They ran to tell Jesus' followers.

## Matthew 28:9

Jesus met them and greeted them. 📖

They held his feet and worshipped him.

## Matthew 28:10

Jesus said, “Do not be afraid. 📖

Tell my brothers to go to Galilee.

They will see me there.”

## Matthew 28:11

Some guards went into the city while the women were going. 📖

They told the chief priests everything that happened.

## Matthew 28:12

The chief priests met the elders and made a plan. 📖

They gave the soldiers a large amount of money.

## Matthew 28:13

They said, “Tell people his followers came at night. 📖

Say they stole his body while you were sleeping.”

## Matthew 28:14

“If the governor hears this, we will persuade him and keep you out of trouble.” 📖

## Matthew 28:15

The guards took the money and followed the instructions. 📖

At the time this was written, this story was still being spread among Jewish people.

## Matthew 28:16

The 11 followers went to the mountain in Galilee where Jesus had told them to go. 📖

## Matthew 28:17

They saw Jesus and worshipped him. 📖

But some doubted.

## Matthew 28:18

Jesus said, “I have been given all authority in heaven and on earth.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Authority means the right and power to rule.

## Matthew 28:19

“Go and make followers from all nations. 📖

Baptize them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.”

**Help with a word:** Baptism uses water to mark belonging to God.

## Matthew 28:20

“Teach them to obey everything I have commanded. 📖

I am with you always, until the end of the age.”
