# Matthew 12 — Sabbath, accusations, and responses

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers challenges about the Sabbath, his power, and signs, and describes his family.

## Matthew 12:1

On the Sabbath, Jesus crossed grainfields, and his hungry disciples picked and ate grain heads. 📖

## Matthew 12:2

Pharisees said, “Your disciples do what the Sabbath forbids.” 📖

## Matthew 12:3

Jesus replied, “Have you not read what hungry David and his companions did? 📖

## Matthew 12:4

David entered God's house, and they ate sacred bread, lawful only for priests, not them. 📖

## Matthew 12:5

Or that temple priests break the Sabbath yet remain innocent, according to the Law? 📖

## Matthew 12:6

Someone greater than the temple is here. 📖

## Matthew 12:7

Knowing ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice’ would have stopped you condemning innocent people. 📖

## Matthew 12:8

The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.” 📖

## Matthew 12:9

Jesus went into their synagogue. 📖

## Matthew 12:10

A man with a withered hand was there; seeking an accusation, they asked, “Is Sabbath healing lawful?” 📖

**Help:** Withered describes a hand that had lost strength and normal use.

## Matthew 12:11

Jesus replied, “Would you not lift your sheep from a pit on the Sabbath? 📖

## Matthew 12:12

A person is worth much more than a sheep, so doing good on the Sabbath is lawful.” 📖

## Matthew 12:13

He told the man, “Stretch out your hand”; he obeyed, and it became healthy like the other. 📖

## Matthew 12:14

The Pharisees left and plotted Jesus' death. 📖

## Matthew 12:15

Knowing this, Jesus withdrew; large crowds followed, and he healed them all. 📖

## Matthew 12:16

He warned them not to make him known. 📖

## Matthew 12:17

This fulfilled Isaiah's words. 📖

## Matthew 12:18

“Here is my chosen servant, dearly loved, who delights my soul. 📖

I will put my Spirit upon him, and he will proclaim justice to nations.

## Matthew 12:19

He will not quarrel or shout; nobody will hear his voice in the streets. 📖

## Matthew 12:20

He will not break a bruised reed or put out a smoking wick until he brings justice to victory. 📖

**Help:** A reed is a plant stem; a wick is a lamp's burning thread.

These images show gentleness towards what is fragile.

## Matthew 12:21

Nations will hope in his name.” 📖

## Matthew 12:22

A possessed man unable to see or speak was brought; Jesus healed him to speak and see. 📖

## Matthew 12:23

The crowds marvelled, asking, “Could this be David's Son?” 📖

## Matthew 12:24

Pharisees said, “He expels demons only by Beelzebul, their prince.” 📖

## Matthew 12:25

Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said, “A divided kingdom falls, and a divided city or household cannot stand. 📖

## Matthew 12:26

If Satan expels Satan, he is divided; how can his kingdom stand? 📖

## Matthew 12:27

If Beelzebul empowers me, who empowers your sons to expel demons? 📖

They will be your judges.

## Matthew 12:28

If I expel demons by God's Spirit, his kingdom has come upon you. 📖

## Matthew 12:29

To rob a strong man's house, must you not first bind him? 📖

Then you can plunder it.

## Matthew 12:30

Whoever is not with me is against me; whoever does not gather with me scatters. 📖

## Matthew 12:31

People will be forgiven every sin and blasphemy, except blasphemy against the Spirit. 📖

That will not be forgiven.

**Help:** Blasphemy means speech insulting God or treating what is holy with contempt.

The specific warning's interpretation needs independent review alongside the accusation and reply.

## Matthew 12:32

Speaking against the Son of Man will be forgiven. 📖

Speaking against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either in this age or the next.

## Matthew 12:33

Make the tree good with good fruit, or bad with bad fruit; fruit identifies the tree. 📖

## Matthew 12:34

Vipers' offspring, how can evil people like you speak good things? 📖

The mouth speaks what overflows from the heart.

## Matthew 12:35

A good person brings good from good stored treasure, and an evil person evil from evil treasure. 📖

## Matthew 12:36

On judgment day, people must account for every careless word. 📖

## Matthew 12:37

Your words will bring acquittal or condemnation.” 📖

**Help:** Acquittal means being cleared of guilt; condemnation means being judged guilty.

## Matthew 12:38

Some law teachers and Pharisees asked, “Teacher, show us a sign.” 📖

## Matthew 12:39

Jesus replied, “A wicked, adulterous generation seeks signs, but only Jonah the prophet's sign will be given. 📖

## Matthew 12:40

Jonah spent three days and three nights in the great fish's belly. 📖

The Son of Man will spend three days and three nights in earth's heart.

## Matthew 12:41

Nineveh's men will stand with this generation at judgment and condemn it. 📖

They repented at Jonah's preaching, and someone greater than Jonah is here.

## Matthew 12:42

The Queen of the South will rise with this generation at judgment and condemn it. 📖

She came from earth's ends for Solomon's wisdom, and someone greater than Solomon is here.

## Matthew 12:43

An unclean spirit leaves a person and searches dry places for rest without finding it. 📖

## Matthew 12:44

It says, ‘I will return to my former house.’ 📖

It returns and finds the house empty, swept, and orderly.

## Matthew 12:45

It brings seven worse spirits to live there, leaving the person worse than before; this wicked generation will be likewise.” 📖

## Matthew 12:46

While he spoke, his mother and brothers waited outside to speak with him. 📖

## Matthew 12:47

Someone told him, “Your mother and brothers are outside wanting to speak.” 📖

## Matthew 12:48

He replied, “Who are my mother and brothers?” 📖

## Matthew 12:49

Pointing to disciples, he said, “Here are my mother and brothers. 📖

## Matthew 12:50

Whoever does my heavenly Father's will is my brother, sister, and mother.” 📖
