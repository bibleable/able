# Matthew 8 — Healing and authority

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals, teaches about following him, calms a storm, and drives out demons.

## Matthew 8:1

Large crowds followed Jesus down the mountain. 📖

## Matthew 8:2

A man with leprosy knelt, saying, “Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean.” 📖

**Help:** Leprosy names a serious skin condition here.

Clean concerns healing and religious standing, rather than implying illness makes a person morally bad.

## Matthew 8:3

Jesus touched him, saying, “I am willing; be clean,” and his leprosy cleared immediately. 📖

## Matthew 8:4

Jesus said, “Tell nobody; show yourself to the priest and offer Moses' required gift as a testimony to them.” 📖

## Matthew 8:5

In Capernaum, a centurion approached, pleading. 📖

**Help:** A centurion was a Roman army officer.

## Matthew 8:6

“Lord, my servant lies paralysed at home, in terrible pain.” 📖

## Matthew 8:7

Jesus replied, “I will come and heal him.” 📖

## Matthew 8:8

He answered, “Lord, I am unworthy to have you under my roof; just speak, and my servant will be healed. 📖

## Matthew 8:9

I too am under authority and have soldiers under me. 📖

They obey when I say go or come; my servant does what I command.”

## Matthew 8:10

Jesus marvelled and told his followers, “Truly, I have found no one in Israel with such faith. 📖

## Matthew 8:11

Many will come from east and west to feast with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in heaven's kingdom. 📖

## Matthew 8:12

But the kingdom's sons will be thrown into outer darkness, with weeping and grinding teeth.” 📖

**About these words:** The warning contrasts expected membership with those coming from afar.

Its full interpretation needs further review.

## Matthew 8:13

Jesus told him, “Go; it will happen as you believed,” and his servant was healed that hour. 📖

## Matthew 8:14

At Peter's house, Jesus saw his mother-in-law bedridden with fever. 📖

## Matthew 8:15

He touched her hand; the fever left, and she rose to serve him. 📖

## Matthew 8:16

That evening, people brought many possessed people; Jesus expelled spirits with a word and healed all sick people. 📖

## Matthew 8:17

This fulfilled Isaiah's words, “He took our weaknesses and carried our diseases.” 📖

## Matthew 8:18

Seeing the crowd, Jesus ordered a crossing to the other shore. 📖

## Matthew 8:19

A law teacher said, “Teacher, I will follow wherever you go.” 📖

## Matthew 8:20

Jesus answered, “Foxes have dens and birds nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head.” 📖

## Matthew 8:21

Another disciple asked, “Lord, first let me bury my father.” 📖

## Matthew 8:22

Jesus replied, “Follow me, and let the dead bury their dead.” 📖

**About this saying:** The draft retains the difficult image; its full interpretation needs independent review.

## Matthew 8:23

Jesus entered the boat, and his disciples followed. 📖

## Matthew 8:24

A violent storm engulfed the boat with waves, but Jesus slept. 📖

## Matthew 8:25

They woke him, crying, “Lord, save us; we are dying!” 📖

## Matthew 8:26

He said, “Why so afraid, you of little faith?” 📖

He rose and rebuked winds and sea, and perfect calm followed.

## Matthew 8:27

They marvelled, asking, “What kind of man is this, whom winds and sea obey?” 📖

## Matthew 8:28

In the Gadarenes' region, two possessed men met him from the tombs, so violent nobody could pass. 📖

## Matthew 8:29

They shouted, “What do you want, God's Son; have you come to torment us before the appointed time?” 📖

## Matthew 8:30

A large pig herd fed in the distance. 📖

## Matthew 8:31

The demons begged, “If you expel us, send us into the pigs.” 📖

## Matthew 8:32

He said, “Go,” and they entered the pigs; the whole herd rushed down the steep bank and drowned. 📖

## Matthew 8:33

The herders fled to town, reporting everything, including the possessed men's story. 📖

## Matthew 8:34

The whole town met Jesus and begged him to leave their region. 📖
