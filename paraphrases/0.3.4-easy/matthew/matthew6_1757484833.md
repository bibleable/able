# Matthew 6 — Pray and trust your Father

**About this chapter**

Jesus continues teaching about giving, prayer, and trust in God.

## Matthew 6:1

“Do not do good just so people will notice you. 📖

If you do, your Father in heaven will not reward you.”

## Matthew 6:2

“When you give to people in need, do not announce it with trumpets. 📖

Pretenders do this in meeting places and streets so people will praise them.

They already have their full reward.”

## Matthew 6:3

“When giving to people in need, do not let your left hand know what your right hand does.” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Give quietly, without showing off.

## Matthew 6:4

“Keep your giving secret. 📖

Your Father sees what is done in secret and will reward you.”

## Matthew 6:5

“When praying, do not be like pretenders. 📖

They love standing in meeting places and street corners so people will see them.

They already have their full reward.”

## Matthew 6:6

“Go into your room and shut the door. 📖

Pray to your unseen Father.

He sees what is done in secret and will reward you.”

## Matthew 6:7

“Do not keep saying empty words like people who worship other gods. 📖

They think they will be heard because they use many words.”

## Matthew 6:8

“Do not be like them. 📖

Your Father knows what you need before you ask.”

## Matthew 6:9

“Pray like this. 📖

Our Father in heaven, may your name be honoured as holy.”

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart and worthy of special honour.

## Matthew 6:10

“May your kingdom come. 📖

May what you want be done on earth, as it is in heaven.”

**Help with words:** God's kingdom means God's rule.

## Matthew 6:11

“Give us the food we need today.” 📖

## Matthew 6:12

“Forgive our debts, as we have forgiven those who owe us.” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Debts picture wrongs that need forgiveness.

## Matthew 6:13

“Do not lead us into temptation. 📖

Rescue us from the evil one.”

**Help with words:** Temptation is pressure to do wrong.

The evil one means the devil.

## Matthew 6:14

“If you forgive others for their wrongs, your Father in heaven will forgive you too.” 📖

## Matthew 6:15

“If you refuse to forgive others, your Father will not forgive your wrongs.” 📖

## Matthew 6:16

“When fasting, do not look miserable like pretenders. 📖

They make their faces look bad so people notice their fasting.

They already have their full reward.”

**Help with a word:** Fasting means choosing not to eat for a time, often to focus on God.

## Matthew 6:17

“When you fast, put oil on your head and wash your face.” 📖

## Matthew 6:18

“Then people will not notice your fasting. 📖

Only your unseen Father will know.

He sees what is done in secret and will reward you.”

## Matthew 6:19

“Do not store up treasures on earth. 📖

Moths and rust destroy them.

Thieves break in and steal them.”

## Matthew 6:20

“Store up treasures in heaven. 📖

Moths and rust cannot destroy them.

Thieves cannot steal them.”

## Matthew 6:21

“Your heart will be where your treasure is.” 📖

**Help with this picture:** What you treasure most shapes your thoughts and desires.

## Matthew 6:22

“The eye is like a lamp for the body. 📖

If your eyes are good, your whole body is full of light.”

## Matthew 6:23

“If your eyes are bad, your whole body is full of darkness. 📖

If your inner light is darkness, that darkness is very great!”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus uses sight to picture a person's inner life.

## Matthew 6:24

“No one can serve 2 masters. 📖

You will either hate one and love the other.

Or you will be devoted to one and despise the other.

You cannot serve both God and money.”

## Matthew 6:25

“Do not worry about food, drink, or clothes. 📖

Life is more than food.

Your body is more than clothes.”

## Matthew 6:26

“Look at the birds. 📖

They do not plant crops, harvest them, or store food in barns.

Your Father in heaven feeds them.

You are worth much more than birds.”

## Matthew 6:27

“Can worrying add even 1 hour to your life?” 📖

## Matthew 6:28

“Why worry about clothes? 📖

Look at the field's lilies.

They grow without working or making cloth.”

## Matthew 6:29

“Even King Solomon, in all his splendour, was not dressed as beautifully as one of these flowers.” 📖

## Matthew 6:30

“God clothes field grass that lives today and is thrown into the furnace tomorrow. 📖

If he does that, will he not much more clothe you?

Your faith is so small!”

## Matthew 6:31

“Do not worry about what to eat, drink, or wear.” 📖

## Matthew 6:32

“Gentiles chase after all these things. 📖

Your Father in heaven knows you need them.”

**Help with a word:** Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## Matthew 6:33

“Seek God's kingdom and his righteousness first. 📖

Then these other things will be given to you too.”

**Help with a word:** Righteousness concerns being right before God and doing what he requires.

## Matthew 6:34

“Do not worry about tomorrow. 📖

Tomorrow will have its own worries.

Today has enough trouble of its own.”
