# Matthew 27 — Jesus is crucified and buried

**About this chapter**

Jesus faces Pilate, is killed on a cross, and is buried in a guarded tomb.

## Matthew 27:1

In the morning, all the chief priests and the people's elders plotted to put Jesus to death. 📖

## Matthew 27:2

They tied him, led him away, and handed him over to Pilate the governor. 📖

## Matthew 27:3

Judas, his betrayer, saw Jesus was condemned and felt deep regret. 📖

He returned the thirty silver pieces to the chief priests and elders.

## Matthew 27:4

He said, “I sinned by betraying innocent blood.” 📖

They replied, “What is that to us?

It is your responsibility.”

## Matthew 27:5

Judas threw the silver into the temple and left. 📖

He went away and hanged himself.

## Matthew 27:6

The chief priests collected the silver but said the law forbade putting blood money in the treasury. 📖

**Help:** Blood money here means money paid for someone's death; the treasury stored the temple's money.

## Matthew 27:7

After discussing it, they bought the potter's field with the money to bury foreigners there. 📖

**Help:** A potter makes clay pots.

## Matthew 27:8

That is why it is still called the Field of Blood. 📖

**About the time:** Still refers to when this account was written.

## Matthew 27:9

This fulfilled Jeremiah the prophet's words: “They took the thirty silver pieces, the price Israel's people set on him. 📖

## Matthew 27:10

They paid them for the potter's field, as the Lord commanded me.” 📖

## Matthew 27:11

Jesus stood before the governor, who asked, “Are you King of the Jews?” 📖

Jesus answered, “You have said so.”

## Matthew 27:12

He gave no answer to the chief priests' and elders' accusations. 📖

## Matthew 27:13

Pilate asked whether he heard how many accusations they were making. 📖

## Matthew 27:14

Jesus answered none of the accusations, greatly amazing the governor. 📖

## Matthew 27:15

At the feast, the governor usually released one prisoner chosen by the crowd. 📖

## Matthew 27:16

They then held a prisoner called Barabbas, known for wrongdoing. 📖

## Matthew 27:17

Pilate asked the gathered crowd whether to release Barabbas or Jesus, who was called Christ. 📖

## Matthew 27:18

He knew they had handed Jesus over because of envy. 📖

## Matthew 27:19

While Pilate sat on the judgment seat, his wife sent a message: “Have nothing to do with that innocent man. 📖

Today I suffered terribly in a dream because of him.”

## Matthew 27:20

The chief priests and elders persuaded the crowds to demand Barabbas' release and Jesus' death. 📖

## Matthew 27:21

The governor asked which of the two to release, and they answered, “Barabbas.” 📖

## Matthew 27:22

Pilate asked what to do with Jesus, called Christ, and everyone answered, “Crucify him!” 📖

**Help:** Crucify means kill on a cross.

## Matthew 27:23

Pilate asked what evil Jesus had done, but they shouted even louder for him to be crucified. 📖

## Matthew 27:24

Pilate saw he achieved nothing and a riot was starting, so he washed his hands before the crowd. 📖

He said, “I am innocent of this man's blood; you are responsible.”

## Matthew 27:25

All the people answered, “Let his blood be on us and our children!” 📖

**Help:** This records the crowd's words accepting responsibility; it does not declare all Jewish people guilty.

## Matthew 27:26

Pilate released Barabbas, but had Jesus whipped and handed over to be crucified. 📖

## Matthew 27:27

The governor's soldiers took Jesus into the Praetorium and gathered the whole company around him. 📖

**Help:** The Praetorium was the governor's headquarters; a company was a group of soldiers.

## Matthew 27:28

They stripped him and dressed him in a scarlet robe. 📖

**Help:** Scarlet is a bright red colour.

## Matthew 27:29

They twisted thorns into a crown, put it on his head, and placed a staff in his right hand. 📖

They knelt and mocked him: “Greetings, King of the Jews!”

## Matthew 27:30

They spat on him and repeatedly hit his head with the staff. 📖

## Matthew 27:31

After mocking him, they removed the robe and put his own clothes back on him. 📖

They led him away to crucify him.

## Matthew 27:32

On the way, they found Simon from Cyrene and forced him to carry Jesus' cross. 📖

## Matthew 27:33

They reached Golgotha, meaning the Place of the Skull. 📖

## Matthew 27:34

They offered Jesus wine mixed with gall, but after tasting it, he refused to drink. 📖

**Help:** Gall is a bitter substance.

## Matthew 27:35

They crucified him and divided his clothes by casting lots. 📖

**Help:** Casting lots is a way of choosing by chance.

## Matthew 27:36

They sat there guarding him. 📖

## Matthew 27:37

Above his head, they put the written accusation: THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS. 📖

## Matthew 27:38

They crucified two robbers with him, one on each side. 📖

## Matthew 27:39

People passing by insulted him and shook their heads. 📖

## Matthew 27:40

They said, “You would destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days, so save yourself! 📖

If you are God's Son, come down from the cross!”

## Matthew 27:41

The chief priests, law teachers, and elders likewise mocked him. 📖

## Matthew 27:42

They said, “He saved others but cannot save himself! 📖

He is Israel's King; let him come down from the cross now, and we will believe him.

## Matthew 27:43

He trusts God, so let God rescue him now if God wants him. 📖

He said he is God's Son.”

## Matthew 27:44

Even the robbers crucified with him insulted him in the same way. 📖

## Matthew 27:45

From the sixth until the ninth hour, darkness covered all the land. 📖

**Help:** This is about midday until three in the afternoon.

## Matthew 27:46

About the ninth hour, Jesus cried loudly, “Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?” 📖

This means, “My God, my God, why have you abandoned me?”

## Matthew 27:47

Some people there heard and said he was calling Elijah. 📖

## Matthew 27:48

One person quickly ran for a sponge, filled it with sour wine, and put it on a reed. 📖

He held it up for Jesus to drink.

## Matthew 27:49

The others said to leave him alone and see whether Elijah came to save him. 📖

## Matthew 27:50

Jesus cried loudly again and gave up his spirit. 📖

**Help:** Jesus died.

## Matthew 27:51

At that moment, the temple curtain tore in two from top to bottom. 📖

The earth shook and rocks split.

## Matthew 27:52

Tombs broke open, and many dead holy people's bodies were raised. 📖

## Matthew 27:53

After Jesus rose, they came out of the tombs, entered the holy city, and appeared to many people. 📖

**Help:** The holy city is Jerusalem.

## Matthew 27:54

The centurion and others guarding Jesus saw the earthquake and everything else and were terrified. 📖

They said, “Truly, this was God's Son.”

**Help:** A centurion was a Roman army officer.

## Matthew 27:55

Many women watched from a distance; they had followed Jesus from Galilee to care for him. 📖

## Matthew 27:56

They included Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of Zebedee's sons. 📖

## Matthew 27:57

That evening, Joseph, a rich man from Arimathea and a disciple of Jesus, arrived. 📖

## Matthew 27:58

He asked Pilate for Jesus' body, and Pilate ordered that he receive it. 📖

## Matthew 27:59

Joseph took the body and wrapped it in clean linen cloth. 📖

## Matthew 27:60

He laid it in his own new tomb, cut into rock. 📖

He rolled a large stone across the entrance and left.

## Matthew 27:61

Mary Magdalene and the other Mary sat opposite the tomb. 📖

## Matthew 27:62

The next day, after Preparation Day, the chief priests and Pharisees gathered before Pilate. 📖

## Matthew 27:63

They said, “Sir, we remember that deceiver saying while alive that he would rise after three days. 📖

## Matthew 27:64

Order the tomb guarded until the third day, or his disciples might steal him and claim he has risen. 📖

That last deception would be worse than the first.”

## Matthew 27:65

Pilate said, “You have a guard; go and secure the tomb as well as you can.” 📖

## Matthew 27:66

They secured the tomb, sealing the stone and posting the guard. 📖
