# Philemon 1 — Welcome him as a brother

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to Philemon and makes an appeal for Onesimus.

He asks for a welcome freely given, rather than forced.

## Philemon 1:1

This letter is from Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother. 📖

It is for Philemon, our dear fellow worker.

## Philemon 1:2

It is also for Apphia our sister, Archippus our fellow soldier, and the church meeting in your house. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Fellow soldier describes serving and struggling together.

## Philemon 1:3

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace. 📖

## Philemon 1:4

I always thank my God as I remember you in my prayers. 📖

## Philemon 1:5

I hear about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all God's people. 📖

## Philemon 1:6

I pray that your partnership in faith will become effective as you recognise every good thing we have in Christ. 📖

## Philemon 1:7

Your love brings me great joy and encouragement. 📖

Brother, you have refreshed the hearts of God's people.

## Philemon 1:8

In Christ, I have enough boldness to order you to do what is proper. 📖

## Philemon 1:9

But I prefer to appeal to you through love. 📖

I, Paul, am now old and also a prisoner of Christ Jesus.

## Philemon 1:10

I appeal for my child Onesimus, whose father I became while I was in chains. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes their close relationship in faith with the picture of a father and child.

## Philemon 1:11

Before, he was useless to you. 📖

Now he is useful to both you and me.

**Help with the setting:** Paul is talking about the help Onesimus gives.

## Philemon 1:12

I am sending him back to you, sending my very heart. 📖

**Help with this picture:** My very heart expresses how deeply Paul loves Onesimus.

## Philemon 1:13

I wanted to keep him with me so he could serve me on your behalf. 📖

I am in chains for the good news.

## Philemon 1:14

But I wanted to do nothing without your agreement. 📖

Your goodness should come freely, rather than from being forced.

## Philemon 1:15

Perhaps he was separated from you briefly so you might have him back for good. 📖

## Philemon 1:16

You might have him back no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, as a dear brother. 📖

He is especially dear to me, but even more to you, both in person and in the Lord.

## Philemon 1:17

So, if you consider me a partner, welcome him as you would welcome me. 📖

## Philemon 1:18

If he wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to me. 📖

## Philemon 1:19

I, Paul, write this with my own hand. 📖

I will repay it.

I need not mention that you owe me your very self.

## Philemon 1:20

Yes, brother, let me receive some benefit from you in the Lord. 📖

Refresh my heart in Christ.

## Philemon 1:21

I write with confidence in your obedience, knowing you will do even more than I ask. 📖

## Philemon 1:22

Meanwhile, prepare a guest room for me. 📖

I hope your prayers will bring me back to you.

## Philemon 1:23

Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends greetings. 📖

## Philemon 1:24

So do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers. 📖

## Philemon 1:25

May the kindness of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. 📖
