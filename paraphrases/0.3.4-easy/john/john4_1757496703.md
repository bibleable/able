# John 4 — Living water and belief

**About this chapter**

Jesus speaks with a Samaritan woman and later heals an official's son.

## John 4:1

Jesus learned the Pharisees knew he was gaining and baptizing more disciples than John. 📖

## John 4:2

His disciples, rather than Jesus himself, did the baptizing. 📖

## John 4:3

He left Judea and returned to Galilee. 📖

## John 4:4

He had to pass through Samaria. 📖

## John 4:5

He reached Sychar in Samaria, near the land Jacob gave his son Joseph. 📖

## John 4:6

Jacob's well was there, and Jesus sat beside it, tired from travelling. 📖

It was about the sixth hour.

**Help:** A well is a deep place for drawing water; the sixth hour is about midday when counted from sunrise.

## John 4:7

A Samaritan woman came for water, and Jesus asked her for a drink. 📖

## John 4:8

His disciples had gone to town to buy food. 📖

## John 4:9

She asked how a Jewish man could ask her, a Samaritan woman, for a drink. 📖

Jews did not associate with Samaritans.

## John 4:10

Jesus answered, “If you knew God's gift and who asks for a drink, you would ask him. 📖

He would give you living water.

## John 4:11

She said, “Sir, the well is deep and you have nothing to draw water with. 📖

Where will you get living water?

## John 4:12

Are you greater than our ancestor Jacob, who gave us this well? 📖

He, his sons, and his livestock drank from it.”

## John 4:13

Jesus said, “Anyone drinking this water will thirst again. 📖

## John 4:14

Anyone drinking my water will never thirst. 📖

It will become a spring inside him, flowing into eternal life.”

**Help:** A spring is a source of water; eternal life is life without end.

## John 4:15

She asked for this water so she would not thirst or need to keep coming to draw water. 📖

## John 4:16

Jesus told her to call her husband and come back. 📖

## John 4:17

She said she had no husband, and he said her answer was correct. 📖

## John 4:18

“You had five husbands, and the man you have now is not your husband. 📖

You spoke truthfully.”

## John 4:19

She said, “Sir, I see you are a prophet. 📖

## John 4:20

Our ancestors worshipped on this mountain, but you Jews say Jerusalem is where people must worship.” 📖

## John 4:21

Jesus answered, “Believe me, woman, a time is coming to worship the Father neither here nor in Jerusalem. 📖

## John 4:22

You worship what you do not know; we worship what we know, because salvation comes from the Jews. 📖

**Help:** Salvation means rescue.

## John 4:23

A time is coming, and is now here, for true worshippers to worship the Father in spirit and truth. 📖

The Father seeks such people to worship him.

## John 4:24

God is Spirit, and people worshipping him must worship in spirit and truth.” 📖

## John 4:25

The woman said Messiah, called Christ, was coming and would explain everything. 📖

**Help:** Messiah and Christ are titles for God's anointed one.

## John 4:26

Jesus said, “I, the one speaking to you, am he.” 📖

## John 4:27

The disciples returned, surprised he spoke with a woman. 📖

Nobody asked what he wanted from her or why he talked to her.

## John 4:28

She left her water jar, returned to town, and spoke to the people. 📖

## John 4:29

“Come and see a man who told me everything I did; could he be the Christ?” 📖

## John 4:30

They left town and headed for Jesus. 📖

## John 4:31

The disciples urged him, “Rabbi, eat something.” 📖

**Help:** Rabbi means teacher.

## John 4:32

He said, “I have food you know nothing about.” 📖

## John 4:33

They wondered whether someone brought him food. 📖

## John 4:34

Jesus said, “My food is doing the will of the one sending me and finishing his work. 📖

## John 4:35

Do you not say harvest is four months away? 📖

Look up at the fields, because they are ready for harvest.

## John 4:36

The reaper already receives wages and gathers a crop for eternal life, so sower and reaper rejoice together. 📖

**Help:** A sower plants seed; a reaper gathers crops; Jesus uses farming as a picture of his work.

## John 4:37

Here, the saying is true: one sows and another reaps. 📖

## John 4:38

I sent you to reap what you did not work for. 📖

Others did the hard work, and you have entered their labour.”

## John 4:39

Many Samaritans believed because the woman said he told her everything she did. 📖

## John 4:40

They came and asked him to stay, and he stayed two days. 📖

## John 4:41

Many more believed because of his message. 📖

## John 4:42

They told her, “We now believe not only because of your words. 📖

We heard him ourselves and know he truly is the world's Saviour.”

## John 4:43

After two days, Jesus left for Galilee. 📖

## John 4:44

He himself had said a prophet lacks honour in his hometown. 📖

## John 4:45

When he arrived, the Galileans welcomed him. 📖

They too attended Jerusalem's feast and saw all the great things he did there.

## John 4:46

He returned to Cana in Galilee, where he turned water into wine. 📖

A royal official's son was sick in Capernaum.

## John 4:47

The official heard Jesus came from Judea to Galilee. 📖

He went and begged Jesus to come down and heal the boy, who was about to die.

## John 4:48

Jesus said, “Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will never believe.” 📖

## John 4:49

The official said, “Sir, come down before my child dies.” 📖

## John 4:50

Jesus said, “Go, your son will live.” 📖

He believed Jesus' words and left.

## John 4:51

On the way, his servants met him and said his son lived. 📖

## John 4:52

He asked when his son recovered, and they said the fever left yesterday at the seventh hour. 📖

## John 4:53

He realized it was the very hour Jesus said his son would live. 📖

He and his entire household believed.

## John 4:54

This was Jesus' second sign after coming from Judea to Galilee. 📖
