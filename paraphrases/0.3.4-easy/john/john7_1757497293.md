# John 7 — Teaching at the feast

**About this chapter**

Jesus goes to the Feast of Tabernacles, teaches, and faces divided reactions.

## John 7:1

Jesus travelled through Galilee, avoiding Judea because the Jews there sought to kill him. 📖

## John 7:2

The Jewish Feast of Tabernacles was near. 📖

**Help:** Tabernacles means shelters; this was a Jewish feast.

## John 7:3

His brothers urged him to go to Judea so disciples there could see his works. 📖

## John 7:4

They said anyone wanting public notice would not act secretly, so he should show himself to the world. 📖

## John 7:5

Even his own brothers did not believe in him. 📖

## John 7:6

Jesus answered, “You can go at any time, but my time has not come yet. 📖

## John 7:7

The world cannot hate you, but hates me because I testify its deeds are evil. 📖

## John 7:8

Go to the feast yourselves; I am not going to this feast, because my time has not come yet.” 📖

## John 7:9

He stayed in Galilee after saying this. 📖

## John 7:10

After his brothers left, he too went to the feast secretly, rather than publicly. 📖

## John 7:11

The Jews looked for him there, asking where he was. 📖

## John 7:12

Many whispered about him, some calling him good and others saying he deceived people. 📖

## John 7:13

Nobody spoke publicly about him because they feared the Jews. 📖

## John 7:14

About halfway through the feast, Jesus entered the temple courts and taught. 📖

## John 7:15

The Jews were amazed at his learning, asking how he gained it without studying. 📖

## John 7:16

Jesus answered, “My teaching comes from the one who sent me, rather than myself. 📖

## John 7:17

Anyone wanting to do his will will know whether my teaching comes from God or myself. 📖

## John 7:18

Someone speaking on his own authority seeks his own glory. 📖

Someone seeking the glory of the one who sent him is truthful, without falsehood.

## John 7:19

Did Moses not give you the law, yet none of you keeps it? 📖

Why try to kill me?”

## John 7:20

The crowd accused him of having a demon and asked who was trying to kill him. 📖

## John 7:21

Jesus answered, “I did one miracle, and all of you are amazed. 📖

## John 7:22

Moses gave you circumcision, although it came from the patriarchs rather than Moses. 📖

You circumcise boys on the Sabbath.

**Help:** Circumcision removes skin from the end of a male's penis; patriarchs were Israel's early ancestors.

The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish day of rest.

## John 7:23

If a boy can receive circumcision on the Sabbath to keep Moses' law, why be angry with me? 📖

I made a whole man well on the Sabbath.

## John 7:24

Stop judging by appearances, and judge justly.” 📖

## John 7:25

Some Jerusalem people asked whether he was the man others sought to kill. 📖

## John 7:26

“He speaks publicly and they say nothing; have the rulers really recognized him as the Christ? 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## John 7:27

We know his origin, but nobody will know where the Christ comes from.” 📖

## John 7:28

Teaching in the temple courts, Jesus cried, “You know me and my origin. 📖

I did not come on my own, but my sender is true, and you do not know him.

## John 7:29

I know him because I come from him and he sent me.” 📖

## John 7:30

They tried to seize him, but nobody touched him because his hour had not come. 📖

## John 7:31

Many believed, asking whether the Christ would do more signs than this man. 📖

## John 7:32

The Pharisees heard the crowd whispering this and joined the chief priests in sending officers to arrest Jesus. 📖

## John 7:33

He said, “I remain with you a little longer, then go to the one who sent me. 📖

## John 7:34

You will seek me without finding me, and cannot come where I am.” 📖

## John 7:35

The Jews wondered where he would go beyond their finding. 📖

Would he go to Jews scattered among Greeks and teach Greeks?

## John 7:36

What did he mean about seeking without finding, and being unable to come where he was? 📖

## John 7:37

On the feast's last and greatest day, Jesus stood and cried loudly, “Anyone thirsty should come to me and drink. 📖

## John 7:38

Scripture says streams of living water will flow from within anyone believing in me.” 📖

## John 7:39

He meant the Spirit believers would later receive. 📖

The Spirit had not been given yet, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.

## John 7:40

Some hearing this said he truly was the Prophet. 📖

## John 7:41

Others called him the Christ, but others asked how the Christ could come from Galilee. 📖

## John 7:42

They asked whether Scripture said the Christ would come from David's family and Bethlehem, where David lived. 📖

## John 7:43

Jesus caused division among the crowd. 📖

## John 7:44

Some wanted to seize him, but nobody touched him. 📖

## John 7:45

The officers returned, and the chief priests and Pharisees asked why they had not brought him. 📖

## John 7:46

They answered, “Nobody ever spoke like him!” 📖

## John 7:47

The Pharisees asked whether they too had been deceived. 📖

## John 7:48

“Have any rulers or Pharisees believed in him? 📖

## John 7:49

This crowd does not know the law and is under a curse.” 📖

**Help:** This is the Pharisees' accusation against the crowd, not the narrator's declaration.

## John 7:50

Nicodemus, one of them who visited Jesus earlier, asked a question. 📖

## John 7:51

“Does our law convict someone before hearing him and finding out what he did?” 📖

## John 7:52

They asked whether he too came from Galilee. 📖

They told him to investigate, claiming no prophet came from Galilee.

## John 7:53

Everyone went home. 📖
