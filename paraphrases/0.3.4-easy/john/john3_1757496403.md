# John 3 — God gives new life

**About this chapter**

Jesus speaks with Nicodemus.

Later, John the Baptist speaks about Jesus.

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## John 3:1

Nicodemus was a Pharisee and a Jewish leader. 📖

**Help with a word:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## John 3:2

Nicodemus came to Jesus at night. 📖

He said, “Teacher, we know God sent you.

No one could do your wonderful signs unless God was with him.”

## John 3:3

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. 📖

A person must be born again to see God's kingdom.”

**Help with words:** God's kingdom is God's rule over his people.

## John 3:4

Nicodemus asked, “How can an old person be born again? 📖

Can he go back inside his mother and be born a second time?”

## John 3:5

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. 📖

A person must be born of water and God's Spirit to enter God's kingdom.”

## John 3:6

“A human parent gives birth to human life. 📖

God's Spirit gives birth to spiritual life.”

## John 3:7

“Do not be surprised that I said you must be born again.” 📖

## John 3:8

“The wind blows where it wants. 📖

You hear it, but cannot tell where it comes from or goes.

It is like this with everyone born of God's Spirit.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus compares the work of God's Spirit to the unseen wind.

## John 3:9

Nicodemus asked, “How can this happen?” 📖

## John 3:10

Jesus said, “You teach the people of Israel. 📖

Do you still not understand these things?”

## John 3:11

“I tell you the truth. 📖

We speak about what we know and have seen.

But you people do not accept what we tell you.”

## John 3:12

“You do not believe when I speak about things on earth. 📖

How will you believe when I speak about things in heaven?”

## John 3:13

“Only the Son of Man has gone up into heaven. 📖

He is the one who came down from heaven.”

**Help with a title:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## John 3:14

“Moses lifted up a snake in the desert. 📖

In the same way, the Son of Man must be lifted up.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus is speaking about his coming death on a cross.

## John 3:15

“Then everyone who believes in him can have life that lasts forever.” 📖

## John 3:16

God loved the world so much that he gave his one and only Son. 📖

Everyone who believes in his Son will not perish.

They will have life that lasts forever.

**Help with a word:** Perish here means being lost under God's judgment.

## John 3:17

God sent his Son to save the world through him. 📖

God did not send him to declare the world guilty.

## John 3:18

Anyone who believes in God's Son is not declared guilty. 📖

Anyone who refuses to believe is already declared guilty.

That person has refused to believe in God's one and only Son.

## John 3:19

This is God's judgment. 📖

The Light came into the world.

But people loved darkness more than light because they did evil.

**Help with this picture:** The Light points to Jesus and the truth he brings.

## John 3:20

People who do evil hate the light. 📖

They stay away because they fear their evil actions will be seen.

## John 3:21

People who live by the truth come into the light. 📖

Then everyone can see that they have acted with God's help.

## John 3:22

Jesus and his followers went into the countryside of Judea. 📖

He stayed there with them and baptized people.

**Help with a word:** Baptism uses water as a sign of turning to God.

## John 3:23

John was also baptizing people at Aenon, near Salim. 📖

There was plenty of water there.

People kept coming to be baptized.

## John 3:24

John had not yet been put in prison. 📖

## John 3:25

John's followers and a Jewish man began arguing about religious washing. 📖

**Help with words:** Religious washing was washing done as part of worship.

## John 3:26

John's followers said, “Teacher, remember the man you spoke about across the Jordan River? 📖

He is baptizing people, and everyone is going to him!”

## John 3:27

John answered, “People can receive only what God gives them from heaven.” 📖

## John 3:28

“You heard me say that I am not the Christ. 📖

God sent me ahead of him.”

## John 3:29

“The bride belongs to the man she is marrying. 📖

His friend waits and listens for him.

The friend is very happy to hear his voice.

That is how I feel, and my joy is now complete.”

**Help with this picture:** John compares himself to a happy friend at Jesus' wedding.

## John 3:30

“Jesus must become greater. 📖

I must become less important.”

## John 3:31

The one who comes from above is greater than everyone. 📖

A person from earth belongs to earth and speaks about earthly things.

The one from heaven is above everyone.

## John 3:32

He tells what he has seen and heard. 📖

Yet people do not accept what he says.

## John 3:33

Anyone who accepts what he says agrees that God tells the truth. 📖

## John 3:34

The one God sent speaks God's words. 📖

God gives him the Spirit without limit.

## John 3:35

The Father loves the Son. 📖

The Father has put everything under the Son's power.

## John 3:36

Anyone who believes in the Son has life that lasts forever. 📖

Anyone who rejects the Son will not have this life.

God's anger remains on that person.
