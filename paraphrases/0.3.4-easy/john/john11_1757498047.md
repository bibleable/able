# John 11 — Jesus raises Lazarus from death

**About this chapter**

Jesus goes to Bethany, where his friend Lazarus has died.

## John 11:1

A man named Lazarus was ill. 📖

He lived in Bethany, where Mary and her sister Martha lived.

## John 11:2

This Mary would later pour perfume on the Lord's feet and wipe them with her hair. 📖

Her brother Lazarus was ill.

## John 11:3

The sisters sent Jesus a message. 📖

“Lord, the one you love is ill.”

## John 11:4

Jesus said, “This illness will not end in death. 📖

It will show God's greatness.

Through it, the Son of God will receive honour.”

## John 11:5

Jesus loved Martha, her sister, and Lazarus. 📖

## John 11:6

When Jesus heard that Lazarus was ill, he stayed where he was for 2 more days. 📖

## John 11:7

Then Jesus told his followers, “Let us go back to Judea.” 📖

## John 11:8

They said, “Teacher, people there just tried to kill you with stones. 📖

Are you going back?”

## John 11:9

Jesus answered, “There are 12 hours of daylight. 📖

People walking in daylight do not trip.

They can see by this world's light.”

## John 11:10

“People walking at night trip because they have no light.” 📖

## John 11:11

Jesus said, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep. 📖

I am going to wake him.”

## John 11:12

The followers said, “Lord, if he is sleeping, he will get better.” 📖

## John 11:13

Jesus meant that Lazarus had died. 📖

But they thought he meant ordinary sleep.

## John 11:14

Jesus told them clearly, “Lazarus is dead.” 📖

## John 11:15

“For your sake, I am glad I was not there. 📖

This will help you believe.

Let us go to him.”

## John 11:16

Thomas, also called Didymus, told the other followers, “Let us go too, so we may die with him.” 📖

**Help with a name:** Didymus means twin.

## John 11:17

When Jesus arrived, Lazarus had been in the tomb for 4 days. 📖

**Help with a word:** A tomb is a place where a dead body is laid.

## John 11:18

Bethany was less than 2 miles from Jerusalem. 📖

## John 11:19

Many Jewish people had come to comfort Martha and Mary because their brother had died. 📖

## John 11:20

Martha heard Jesus was coming and went to meet him. 📖

Mary stayed at home.

## John 11:21

Martha said, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.” 📖

## John 11:22

“Even now, I know God will give you whatever you ask.” 📖

## John 11:23

Jesus said, “Your brother will rise again.” 📖

## John 11:24

Martha said, “I know he will rise when the dead are raised on the last day.” 📖

## John 11:25

Jesus said, “I am the resurrection and the life. 📖

Anyone who believes in me will live, even if they die.”

**Help with a word:** Resurrection means rising from death to life.

## John 11:26

“Everyone who lives and believes in me will never die. 📖

Do you believe this?”

**Help with this promise:** Jesus is speaking about life with him that death cannot end.

## John 11:27

Martha said, “Yes, Lord. 📖

I believe you are the Christ, the Son of God who was to come into the world.”

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## John 11:28

Martha went back and spoke privately to Mary. 📖

“The Teacher is here and wants to see you.”

## John 11:29

Mary quickly got up and went to Jesus. 📖

## John 11:30

Jesus had not entered the village. 📖

He was still where Martha had met him.

## John 11:31

The people comforting Mary saw her leave quickly and followed her. 📖

They thought she was going to cry at the tomb.

## John 11:32

Mary came to Jesus and fell at his feet. 📖

She said, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.”

## John 11:33

Jesus saw Mary crying and the people with her crying. 📖

He felt deeply moved and troubled.

## John 11:34

Jesus asked, “Where have you laid him?” 📖

They said, “Lord, come and see.”

## John 11:35

Jesus cried. 📖

## John 11:36

The people said, “See how much he loved Lazarus!” 📖

## John 11:37

Some asked, “He gave sight to a blind man. 📖

Could he not have kept Lazarus from dying?”

## John 11:38

Jesus was deeply moved again. 📖

He came to the tomb.

It was a cave with a stone over the entrance.

## John 11:39

Jesus said, “Take away the stone.” 📖

Martha said, “Lord, the body will smell by now.

He has been dead for 4 days.”

## John 11:40

Jesus said, “I told you that if you believed, you would see God's greatness.” 📖

## John 11:41

They removed the stone. 📖

Jesus looked up and said, “Father, thank you for hearing me.”

## John 11:42

“I know you always hear me. 📖

I say this for the people here.

I want them to believe that you sent me.”

## John 11:43

Jesus called out loudly, “Lazarus, come out!” 📖

## John 11:44

The dead man came out. 📖

Cloth strips still wrapped his hands and feet.

A cloth covered his face.

Jesus said, “Take off the cloths and let him go.”

## John 11:45

Many people visiting Mary saw what Jesus did. 📖

They believed in him.

## John 11:46

Others went to the Pharisees and reported what Jesus had done. 📖

**Help with a word:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## John 11:47

The chief priests and Pharisees called a meeting of the Jewish council. 📖

They said, “What shall we do?

This man is doing many wonderful signs.”

## John 11:48

“If we let him continue, everyone will believe in him. 📖

Then the Romans will take away our place and our nation.”

## John 11:49

Caiaphas was high priest that year. 📖

He told them, “You know nothing!”

**Help with a title:** The high priest was the leading priest.

## John 11:50

“It is better for one man to die for the people than for the whole nation to be destroyed.” 📖

## John 11:51

Caiaphas did not say this only from his own thoughts. 📖

As high priest that year, he spoke a message about Jesus dying for the nation.

## John 11:52

Jesus would also die to gather God's scattered children into one people. 📖

## John 11:53

From that day, they planned to kill Jesus. 📖

## John 11:54

Jesus stopped moving about openly among them. 📖

He went with his followers to Ephraim, a town near the desert.

He stayed there with them.

## John 11:55

The Jewish Passover festival was near. 📖

Many people went from the countryside to Jerusalem.

They went through religious washing before the festival.

**Help with a name:** Passover remembers God freeing Israel from Egypt.

## John 11:56

People looked for Jesus in the temple courts. 📖

They asked each other, “Do you think he will come to the festival?”

## John 11:57

The chief priests and Pharisees had given an order. 📖

Anyone who knew where Jesus was had to report it.

They wanted to arrest him.
