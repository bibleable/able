# John 9 — The man born blind

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives sight to a man born blind, who then faces questioning and rejection.

## John 9:1

Passing by, Jesus saw a man blind from birth. 📖

## John 9:2

The disciples asked, “Rabbi, did he or his parents sin to cause him to be born blind?” 📖

**Help:** Rabbi means teacher; sin means wrongdoing against God.

## John 9:3

Jesus answered, “Neither he nor his parents sinned to cause this. 📖

It happened so God's works would be shown in him.

## John 9:4

We must do my sender's works while it is day; night comes when nobody can work. 📖

## John 9:5

While in the world, I am its light.” 📖

## John 9:6

He spat on the ground, made mud, and put it on the man's eyes. 📖

## John 9:7

He told him to wash in Siloam's pool, whose name means Sent. 📖

He went, washed, and returned seeing.

## John 9:8

Neighbours and people who saw him begging asked whether this was the man who used to sit begging. 📖

## John 9:9

Some said yes; others said he only looked like him, but he kept saying he was the one. 📖

## John 9:10

They asked how his eyes opened. 📖

## John 9:11

He said, “The man called Jesus made mud and put it on my eyes. 📖

He told me to wash in Siloam, so I went, washed, and gained sight.”

## John 9:12

They asked where Jesus was, but he did not know. 📖

## John 9:13

They brought him to the Pharisees. 📖

## John 9:14

Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes on the Sabbath. 📖

**Help:** The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish day of rest.

## John 9:15

The Pharisees also asked how he gained sight. 📖

He answered, “He put mud on my eyes; I washed, and now I can see.”

## John 9:16

Some Pharisees said Jesus could not be from God because he did not keep the Sabbath. 📖

Others asked how a sinful man could do such signs, and they were divided.

## John 9:17

They again asked what he thought about the one opening his eyes, and he called him a prophet. 📖

## John 9:18

The Jews did not believe he had been blind and gained sight until they called his parents. 📖

## John 9:19

They asked if he was their son, born blind as they claimed, and how he could see now. 📖

## John 9:20

They answered, “We know he is our son and was born blind. 📖

## John 9:21

We do not know how he sees or who opened his eyes. 📖

Ask him, because he is old enough to speak for himself.”

## John 9:22

They feared the Jews, who already agreed to expel from the synagogue anyone confessing Jesus as Christ. 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one; a synagogue is a Jewish place of worship and teaching.

## John 9:23

That is why they said he was old enough and should be asked. 📖

## John 9:24

They called him a second time, telling him to glorify God and claiming Jesus was a sinner. 📖

## John 9:25

He answered, “I do not know if he is a sinner; I know I was blind and now see!” 📖

## John 9:26

They asked what Jesus did and how he opened his eyes. 📖

## John 9:27

He said, “I told you already, but you did not listen. 📖

Why ask again; do you too want to become his disciples?”

## John 9:28

They insulted him, calling him Jesus' disciple while they followed Moses. 📖

## John 9:29

“We know God spoke to Moses, but do not know this man's origin.” 📖

## John 9:30

He said, “How remarkable that you do not know his origin, although he opened my eyes! 📖

## John 9:31

We know God does not hear sinners, but hears anyone worshipping him and doing his will. 📖

**Help:** This is the healed man's argument during the questioning.

## John 9:32

Nobody ever heard of opening the eyes of someone born blind. 📖

## John 9:33

He could do nothing like this unless he came from God.” 📖

## John 9:34

They said, “You were born entirely in sin, and you are teaching us?” 📖

They threw him out.

## John 9:35

Jesus heard he had been expelled, found him, and asked whether he believed in the Son of Man. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## John 9:36

He asked, “Who is he, Sir? 📖

Tell me so I can believe in him.”

## John 9:37

Jesus answered, “You have seen him; he is speaking with you.” 📖

## John 9:38

He said, “Lord, I believe,” and worshipped Jesus. 📖

## John 9:39

Jesus said, “I came into the world for judgment, so blind people may see and seeing people become blind.” 📖

**Help:** Jesus uses seeing and blindness as a picture of recognizing truth; physical blindness is not a moral fault.

## John 9:40

Some Pharisees with him heard and asked whether they too were blind. 📖

## John 9:41

Jesus said, “If you were blind, you would not be guilty of sin. 📖

Since you claim to see, your guilt remains.”
