# John 6 — The bread of life

**About this chapter**

Jesus feeds a crowd, walks on the sea, and gives teaching that many followers find difficult.

## John 6:1

Jesus crossed the Sea of Galilee, also called the Sea of Tiberias. 📖

## John 6:2

A large crowd followed because they saw his signs healing sick people. 📖

## John 6:3

He went up the mountain and sat with his disciples. 📖

## John 6:4

The Jewish Passover feast was near. 📖

**Help:** Passover remembers God's rescue of Israel from Egypt.

## John 6:5

Seeing a large crowd approach, Jesus asked Philip where they could buy bread to feed them. 📖

## John 6:6

He asked to test Philip, already knowing what he would do. 📖

## John 6:7

Philip said two hundred denarii would not buy enough for each person to have even a small piece. 📖

**Help:** Denarii were coins; denarius is the name for one coin.

## John 6:8

Andrew, a disciple and Simon Peter's brother, spoke. 📖

## John 6:9

“A boy here has five barley loaves and two small fish, but how can they feed so many?” 📖

**Help:** Barley is a grain used to make bread.

## John 6:10

Jesus told them to seat the people; there was plenty of grass. 📖

About five thousand men sat down.

## John 6:11

Jesus took the bread and fish, gave thanks, and gave those seated as much as they wanted. 📖

## John 6:12

When everyone was full, he told the disciples to collect leftovers so nothing would be wasted. 📖

## John 6:13

They filled twelve baskets with leftover pieces of the five barley loaves. 📖

## John 6:14

Seeing the sign, people said he truly was the Prophet coming into the world. 📖

## John 6:15

Jesus knew they would force him to be king, so he withdrew alone to the mountain again. 📖

## John 6:16

At evening, the disciples went down to the sea. 📖

## John 6:17

They sailed towards Capernaum; it was dark, and Jesus had not joined them. 📖

## John 6:18

A strong wind blew, making the sea rough. 📖

## John 6:19

After rowing about three or four miles, they saw Jesus walking on the sea towards the boat and were terrified. 📖

## John 6:20

He said, “It is me; do not fear.” 📖

## John 6:21

They were willing to take him into the boat. 📖

At once it reached the shore they were heading for.

## John 6:22

Next day, the crowd remaining across the sea knew only one boat had been there. 📖

Jesus had not boarded with his disciples; they left alone.

## John 6:23

Boats from Tiberias arrived near where people ate bread after the Lord gave thanks. 📖

## John 6:24

Seeing Jesus and his disciples absent, the crowd boarded boats and went to Capernaum seeking him. 📖

## John 6:25

Finding him across the sea, they asked, “Rabbi, when did you arrive?” 📖

**Help:** Rabbi means teacher.

## John 6:26

Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, you seek me because you ate and were filled, rather than because you saw signs. 📖

## John 6:27

Do not work for food that perishes, but for food lasting into eternal life. 📖

The Son of Man will give you this food.

This is because God the Father placed his seal of approval on him.

**Help:** Son of Man is Jesus' title for himself; a seal marks approval; eternal life is life without end.

## John 6:28

They asked what they must do to carry out God's works. 📖

## John 6:29

Jesus answered, “God's work is to believe in the one he sent.” 📖

## John 6:30

They asked, “What sign will you do so we can see and believe you? 📖

What will you do?

## John 6:31

Our ancestors ate manna in the wilderness; Scripture says he gave them bread from heaven to eat.” 📖

**Help:** Manna was food in Israel's wilderness account.

## John 6:32

Jesus said, “Truly, truly, Moses did not give you the bread from heaven. 📖

My Father gives you the true bread from heaven.

## John 6:33

God's bread is the one coming from heaven and giving life to the world.” 📖

## John 6:34

They asked him, “Sir, give us this bread always.” 📖

## John 6:35

Jesus answered, “I am the bread of life. 📖

Anyone coming to me will never hunger, and anyone believing in me will never thirst.

## John 6:36

As I told you, you saw me yet do not believe. 📖

## John 6:37

Everyone the Father gives me will come, and I will never drive away anyone coming to me. 📖

## John 6:38

I came down from heaven to do the will of the one who sent me, rather than my own. 📖

## John 6:39

This is the will of the one who sent me. 📖

I will lose none of those he gave me, but raise them on the last day.

## John 6:40

Everyone who looks to the Son and believes in him shall have eternal life. 📖

This is my Father's will.

I will raise him on the last day.”

## John 6:41

The Jews grumbled because he said he was bread from heaven. 📖

## John 6:42

“Is he not Jesus, Joseph's son, whose parents we know? 📖

How can he say he came from heaven?”

## John 6:43

Jesus told them to stop grumbling among themselves. 📖

## John 6:44

“Nobody can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him. 📖

I will raise him on the last day.

## John 6:45

The Prophets say they will all be taught by God. 📖

Anyone hearing and learning from the Father comes to me.

## John 6:46

Nobody has seen the Father except the one from God; only he has seen the Father. 📖

## John 6:47

Truly, truly, anyone believing has eternal life. 📖

## John 6:48

I am the bread of life. 📖

## John 6:49

Your ancestors ate manna in the wilderness and died. 📖

## John 6:50

This bread comes from heaven so anyone may eat and not die. 📖

## John 6:51

I am living bread from heaven; anyone eating it will live forever. 📖

The bread I will give for the world's life is my flesh.”

## John 6:52

The Jews argued among themselves about how he could give his flesh to eat. 📖

## John 6:53

Jesus said, “Truly, truly, I tell you this. 📖

Unless you eat the Son of Man's flesh and drink his blood, you have no life in you.

**Help:** This difficult teaching about flesh and blood needs further interpretation; its wording is retained here.

## John 6:54

Whoever eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him on the last day. 📖

## John 6:55

My flesh is real food, and my blood real drink. 📖

## John 6:56

Whoever eats my flesh and drinks my blood remains in me, and I in him. 📖

## John 6:57

The living Father sent me, and I live because of him. 📖

Likewise, anyone feeding on me will live because of me.

## John 6:58

This is bread from heaven; whoever eats it will live forever, unlike your ancestors who ate manna and died.” 📖

## John 6:59

Jesus taught this in Capernaum's synagogue. 📖

**Help:** A synagogue is a Jewish place of worship and teaching.

## John 6:60

Many disciples said it was difficult teaching and asked who could accept it. 📖

## John 6:61

Knowing their grumbling, Jesus asked whether this offended them. 📖

## John 6:62

“What if you see the Son of Man ascending to where he was before? 📖

## John 6:63

The Spirit gives life; the flesh brings no benefit. 📖

My words are spirit and life.

## John 6:64

But some of you do not believe.” 📖

Jesus knew from the beginning who did not believe and who would betray him.

## John 6:65

Jesus said, “This is why I told you: nobody can come to me unless the Father grants it.” 📖

## John 6:66

Many disciples then turned back and stopped walking with him. 📖

## John 6:67

Jesus asked the Twelve whether they wanted to leave too. 📖

## John 6:68

Simon Peter asked, “Lord, who else can we go to? 📖

You have the words of eternal life.

## John 6:69

We believe and know you are God's Holy One.” 📖

## John 6:70

Jesus answered, “Did I not choose you, the Twelve? 📖

Yet one of you is a devil!”

## John 6:71

He meant Judas, son of Simon Iscariot, one of the Twelve who would later betray him. 📖
