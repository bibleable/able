# John 14 — Jesus promises help and peace

**About this chapter**

Jesus speaks to his followers before his death.

He calls God his Father.

## John 14:1

Jesus said, “Do not be troubled. 📖

Trust God, and trust me too.”

## John 14:2

“My Father's house has many rooms. 📖

I am going to prepare a place for you.

I would not tell you this if it were untrue.”

## John 14:3

“When I have prepared your place, I will come back and welcome you. 📖

Then you can be where I am.”

## John 14:4

“You know the way to where I am going.” 📖

## John 14:5

Thomas said, “Lord, we do not know where you are going. 📖

How can we know the way?”

## John 14:6

Jesus said, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. 📖

No one comes to the Father except through me.”

## John 14:7

“If you knew me, you would know my Father too. 📖

From now on, you know him and have seen him.”

## John 14:8

Philip said, “Lord, show us the Father. 📖

That will be enough for us.”

## John 14:9

Jesus said, “Philip, I have been with you so long. 📖

Do you still not know me?

Anyone who has seen me has seen the Father.

Why ask me to show you the Father?”

## John 14:10

“Do you believe I am in the Father and the Father is in me? 📖

My words do not come from me alone.

The Father living in me does his work.”

## John 14:11

“Believe that I am in the Father and he is in me. 📖

Or believe because of the works themselves.”

## John 14:12

“I tell you the truth. 📖

Anyone who believes in me will do the works I do.

They will do even greater things because I am going to the Father.”

## John 14:13

“I will do whatever you ask in my name. 📖

Then the Son will bring honour to the Father.”

**Help with these words:** Asking in Jesus' name means asking as someone who belongs to him.

## John 14:14

“If you ask me for anything in my name, I will do it.” 📖

## John 14:15

“If you love me, you will obey my commands.” 📖

## John 14:16

“I will ask the Father to give you another Helper. 📖

This Helper will stay with you forever.”

## John 14:17

“He is the Spirit of truth. 📖

The world cannot receive him because it does not see or know him.

But you know him.

He lives with you and will be in you.”

## John 14:18

“I will not leave you alone like children without parents. 📖

I will come to you.”

## John 14:19

“Soon the world will no longer see me. 📖

But you will see me.

Because I live, you will live too.”

## John 14:20

“Then you will know that I am in my Father. 📖

You are in me, and I am in you.”

## John 14:21

“The person who receives and obeys my commands loves me. 📖

My Father will love that person.

I will love that person and make myself known to them.”

## John 14:22

Judas, not Judas Iscariot, asked Jesus a question. 📖

“Lord, why will you show yourself to us, but not to the world?”

## John 14:23

Jesus said, “Anyone who loves me will obey my teaching. 📖

My Father will love that person.

We will come and make our home with that person.”

## John 14:24

“Anyone who does not love me does not obey my words. 📖

My message comes from the Father who sent me.”

## John 14:25

“I have told you these things while I am still with you.” 📖

## John 14:26

“The Father will send the Helper, the Holy Spirit, in my name. 📖

He will teach you everything.

He will remind you of all I have told you.”

## John 14:27

“I leave you peace. 📖

I give you my peace, differently from the way the world gives.

Do not be troubled or afraid.”

## John 14:28

“I said I am going away and coming back to you. 📖

If you loved me, you would be glad I am going to the Father.

The Father is greater than I.”

## John 14:29

“I have told you before it happens. 📖

When it happens, you can believe.”

## John 14:30

“I will not speak with you much longer. 📖

The ruler of this world is coming.

He has no power over me.”

**Help with this title:** The ruler here means the devil.

## John 14:31

“I do exactly what the Father commands. 📖

This shows the world that I love the Father.

Get up.

Let us leave.”
