# John 17 — Jesus prays to the Father

**About this chapter**

Jesus prays for his disciples and for those who will believe through their message.

## John 17:1

After speaking, Jesus looked up to heaven and said, “Father, the hour has come. 📖

Glorify your Son, so that your Son may glorify you.

**Help:** Glorify means to show someone's glory and honor.

## John 17:2

You gave him authority over all people. 📖

You did this so he may give eternal life to everyone you have given him.

**Help:** Authority is the right to act or rule; eternal life is life that lasts forever.

## John 17:3

This is eternal life: that they may know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom you sent. 📖

## John 17:4

I have glorified you on earth by finishing the work you gave me to do. 📖

## John 17:5

Now, Father, glorify me in your presence with the glory I had with you before the world existed. 📖

## John 17:6

I have made your name known to those you gave me out of the world. 📖

They were yours, and you gave them to me.

They have kept your word.

## John 17:7

Now they know that everything you gave me comes from you. 📖

## John 17:8

I gave them the words you gave me, and they received them. 📖

They knew for certain that I came from you, and they believed you sent me.

## John 17:9

I ask for them. 📖

I am not asking for the world, but for those you gave me, because they are yours.

## John 17:10

All I have is yours, and all you have is mine. 📖

I have been glorified in them.

## John 17:11

I will no longer be in the world, but they are in the world. 📖

I am coming to you.

Holy Father, protect them by your name, the name you gave me.

Do this so they may be one, as we are one.

**Help:** Holy means set apart and pure; being one here concerns being united.

## John 17:12

While I was with them, I protected and kept them safe by your name, the name you gave me. 📖

None has been lost except the son of destruction, so that Scripture would be fulfilled.

**Help:** Son of destruction describes Judas, who betrays Jesus; destruction means ruin.

Scripture means the sacred writings; fulfilled means that what they said came about.

## John 17:13

Now I am coming to you. 📖

I say these things while I am in the world so they may have my joy fully within them.

## John 17:14

I have given them your word, and the world has hated them. 📖

They do not belong to the world, just as I do not belong to the world.

## John 17:15

I am not asking you to take them out of the world. 📖

I ask you to keep them from the evil one.

**Help:** The evil one here means the devil.

## John 17:16

They do not belong to the world, just as I do not belong to the world. 📖

## John 17:17

Sanctify them by the truth; your word is truth. 📖

**Help:** Sanctify means to make holy, or set apart for God.

## John 17:18

As you sent me into the world, I have also sent them into the world. 📖

## John 17:19

For them, I sanctify myself, so they too may be sanctified by the truth. 📖

## John 17:20

I ask for more than these people alone. 📖

I also ask for those who will believe in me through their message.

## John 17:21

May they all be one, as you, Father, are in me and I am in you. 📖

May they also be in us, so that the world may believe you sent me.

## John 17:22

I have given them the glory you gave me, so they may be one as we are one. 📖

## John 17:23

I am in them, and you are in me, so they may be perfectly united. 📖

May this happen so the world may know that you sent me.

And may it know you loved them just as you loved me.

## John 17:24

Father, I want those you gave me to be with me where I am. 📖

Then they may see the glory you gave me because you loved me before the world's foundation.

**Help:** Before the world's foundation means before the world was made.

## John 17:25

Righteous Father, the world has not known you, but I know you. 📖

And they know that you sent me.

**Help:** Righteous means right and just.

## John 17:26

I have made your name known to them and will continue to make it known. 📖

I do this so that your love for me may be in them, and I may be in them.”
