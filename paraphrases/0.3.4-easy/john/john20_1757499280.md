# John 20 — Jesus is alive

**About this chapter**

Mary Magdalene finds the empty tomb.

Jesus appears to his followers and to Thomas.

## John 20:1

Early on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene went to the tomb. 📖

It was still dark.

She saw that the stone had been moved away from the entrance.

## John 20:2

Mary ran to Simon Peter and the other disciple, whom Jesus loved. 📖

Mary said, “They have taken the Lord out of the tomb!

We do not know where they have put him!”

**Help with disciple:** A disciple is a follower and learner.

## John 20:3

Peter and the other disciple set out for the tomb. 📖

## John 20:4

They ran together. 📖

The other disciple ran faster than Peter and reached the tomb first.

## John 20:5

The other disciple bent down and looked inside. 📖

He saw the linen cloths lying there, but did not go in.

**Help with linen:** Linen is a kind of cloth.

## John 20:6

Simon Peter arrived just after the other disciple. 📖

Peter went into the tomb and saw the linen cloths lying there.

**Help with linen:** Linen is a kind of cloth.

## John 20:7

The cloth that had been around Jesus’ head was rolled up. 📖

It lay apart from the other cloths.

## John 20:8

Then the other disciple, who had reached the tomb first, went inside too. 📖

He saw and believed.

## John 20:9

They still did not understand from Scripture that Jesus had to rise from the dead. 📖

**Help with Scripture:** Scripture means the holy writings in the Bible.

## John 20:10

Then the disciples went back to their homes. 📖

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 20:11

Mary stood outside the tomb crying. 📖

As she cried, she bent down to look inside.

## John 20:12

Mary saw 2 angels dressed in white. 📖

They sat where Jesus’ body had been.

One sat at the head, and the other at the feet.

**Help with angels:** Angels are messengers from God.

## John 20:13

The angels asked Mary, “Woman, why are you crying?” 📖

Mary said, “They have taken my Lord away.

I do not know where they have put him.”

**Help with angels:** Angels are messengers from God.

## John 20:14

After saying this, Mary turned around and saw Jesus standing there. 📖

She did not know that it was Jesus.

## John 20:15

Jesus asked, “Woman, why are you crying? 📖

Who are you looking for?”

Mary thought he was the gardener.

She said, “Sir, if you carried him away, tell me where you put him.

I will get him.”

## John 20:16

Jesus said, “Mary.” 📖

She turned and said to him in Hebrew, “Rabboni!”

This means “Teacher.”

## John 20:17

Jesus said, “Do not hold on to me. 📖

I have not yet gone up to the Father.

Go to my brothers and tell them this.

I am going up to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.”

**Help with my brothers:** Jesus uses this family word for his followers.

## John 20:18

Mary Magdalene went to the disciples and announced, “I have seen the Lord!” 📖

She told them what Jesus had said to her.

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 20:19

That same evening, on the first day of the week, the disciples were together. 📖

They had locked the doors because they feared the Jews.

Jesus came and stood among them.

He said, “Peace be with you!”

**Help with the people here:** The fear concerns Jewish opponents in this account.

Jesus and his first followers were Jewish too.

## John 20:20

After saying this, Jesus showed them his hands and his side. 📖

His followers were full of joy when they saw the Lord.

## John 20:21

Jesus said again, “Peace be with you. 📖

As the Father sent me, I am sending you.”

## John 20:22

After saying this, Jesus breathed on them. 📖

He said, “Receive the Holy Spirit.

**Help with the Holy Spirit:** The Holy Spirit is God’s Spirit.

## John 20:23

If you forgive anyone’s sins, those sins are forgiven. 📖

If you hold back forgiveness from anyone, forgiveness is held back.”

**Help with sins:** Sins are wrong things people do against God.

**About this teaching:** Jesus speaks about forgiveness and responsibility.

The wording is kept for further editorial discussion about its meaning.

## John 20:24

Thomas, also called Didymus, was one of the Twelve. 📖

Thomas was not with the disciples when Jesus came.

**Help with the Twelve:** This is the name for Jesus’ chosen group of 12 apostles.

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 20:25

The other disciples told Thomas, “We have seen the Lord!” 📖

Thomas answered, “Unless I see the nail marks in his hands, I will never believe.

I must put my finger where the nails were.

I must put my hand into his side.”

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 20:26

8 days later, the disciples were inside again, with Thomas there too. 📖

The doors were locked.

Jesus came and stood among them.

He said, “Peace be with you.”

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 20:27

Jesus told Thomas, “Put your finger here and look at my hands. 📖

Reach out your hand and put it into my side.

Stop doubting and believe.”

## John 20:28

Thomas answered Jesus, “My Lord and my God!” 📖

## John 20:29

Jesus told Thomas, “You believe because you have seen me. 📖

Those who have not seen me and yet believe are blessed.”

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed means receiving God’s favour.

## John 20:30

Jesus did many other signs in front of his disciples. 📖

Those signs are not written in this book.

**Help with signs:** These are acts that show who Jesus is.

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 20:31

These signs are written so that you may believe Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God. 📖

By believing, you may have life in his name.

**Help with Christ:** Christ means God’s chosen King, also called the Messiah.

**Help with in his name:** This links the life to Jesus himself and who he is.
