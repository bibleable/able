# John 2 — Wine at a wedding and the temple

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives his first sign at Cana, then acts in the temple at Jerusalem.

## John 2:1

On the third day, a wedding took place at Cana in Galilee, and Jesus' mother was there. 📖

## John 2:2

Jesus and his disciples had also been invited. 📖

## John 2:3

The wine ran out, and his mother told him they had none left. 📖

## John 2:4

Jesus answered, “Woman, what is that to you and me? 📖

My hour has not come yet.”

## John 2:5

His mother told the servants to do whatever he said. 📖

## John 2:6

Six stone water jars stood there for Jewish purification rites, each holding twenty to thirty gallons. 📖

**Help:** Purification rites were religious washing customs; gallons measure liquid.

## John 2:7

Jesus told the servants to fill the jars with water, and they filled them to the top. 📖

## John 2:8

He told them to draw some out and take it to the feast's master, and they did. 📖

## John 2:9

The feast's master tasted the water turned into wine without knowing its source, although the servants knew. 📖

He called the bridegroom aside.

**Help:** Bridegroom means the man getting married.

## John 2:10

He said, “People serve good wine first, then cheap wine once guests are drunk. 📖

You kept the good wine until now!”

## John 2:11

This was Jesus' first sign, at Cana in Galilee, revealing his glory. 📖

His disciples believed in him.

**Help:** A sign points to who Jesus is; glory means greatness and honour made known.

## John 2:12

He went down to Capernaum with his mother, brothers, and disciples, staying a few days. 📖

## John 2:13

Near the Jewish Passover, Jesus went up to Jerusalem. 📖

**Help:** Passover remembers God's rescue of Israel from Egypt.

## John 2:14

In the temple courts, people sold cattle, sheep, and doves, and money changers sat at tables. 📖

## John 2:15

He made a whip from cords and drove all out of the courts, both sheep and cattle. 📖

He poured out the money changers' coins and overturned their tables.

## John 2:16

He told the dove sellers, “Take these away! 📖

How dare you make my Father's house a marketplace!”

## John 2:17

The disciples remembered Scripture saying, “Zeal for your house will consume me.” 📖

**Help:** Zeal means intense devotion; consume pictures its powerful effect.

## John 2:18

The Jews asked what sign he could show to prove his authority for these actions. 📖

## John 2:19

He answered, “Destroy this temple, and I will raise it again in three days.” 📖

## John 2:20

They replied, “It took forty-six years to build, and you will raise it in three days?” 📖

## John 2:21

But he meant the temple of his body. 📖

## John 2:22

After he was raised from death, the disciples remembered this and believed Scripture and his words. 📖

## John 2:23

Many saw his signs in Jerusalem at Passover and believed in his name. 📖

## John 2:24

But Jesus did not entrust himself to them, because he knew them all. 📖

**Help:** Entrust yourself means put yourself in someone else's care or power.

## John 2:25

He needed nobody's testimony about people, because he knew what was in a person. 📖
