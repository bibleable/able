# John 19 — Jesus dies and is buried

**About this chapter**

Pilate hands Jesus over to be crucified; Jesus dies and is buried in a new tomb.

## John 19:1

Pilate took Jesus and had him whipped. 📖

**Help:** Flogged means whipped.

## John 19:2

The soldiers twisted thorns into a crown and put it on Jesus' head. 📖

They dressed him in a purple robe.

## John 19:3

They kept coming up to him, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!” 📖

They slapped his face.

**Help:** Hail is a greeting; the soldiers were mocking Jesus as king.

## John 19:4

Pilate came out again and told the Jews, “Look, I am bringing him out to you. 📖

I want you to know I find no basis for a charge against him.”

## John 19:5

Jesus came out wearing the crown of thorns and purple robe. 📖

Pilate told them, “Here is the man!”

## John 19:6

When the chief priests and officers saw Jesus, they shouted, “Crucify him! 📖

Crucify him!”

Pilate replied, “You take him and crucify him, because I find no basis for a charge against him.”

**Help:** Crucify means to kill someone by fastening them to a cross.

## John 19:7

The Jews answered, “We have a law, and by that law he must die. 📖

He declared himself to be the Son of God.”

## John 19:8

When Pilate heard this, he was even more afraid. 📖

## John 19:9

He went back into the Praetorium and asked Jesus, “Where are you from?” 📖

Jesus gave no answer.

**Help:** The Praetorium was the Roman governor's headquarters.

## John 19:10

Pilate asked, “Do you refuse to speak to me? 📖

Do you not know I have authority to release you and authority to crucify you?”

**Help:** Authority is the right or power to act.

## John 19:11

Jesus answered, “You would have no authority over me unless it were given to you from above. 📖

So the one who handed me over to you is guilty of greater sin.”

**Help:** From above points to God; sin is wrong against God.

## John 19:12

From then on, Pilate tried to release Jesus. 📖

But the Jews kept shouting, “If you release this man, you are no friend of Caesar.

Anyone who declares himself a king is defying Caesar.”

**Help:** Caesar was the Roman emperor; defying means opposing someone's authority.

## John 19:13

Hearing these words, Pilate brought Jesus out and sat on the judgment seat. 📖

The place was called the Stone Pavement, or Gabbatha in Hebrew.

## John 19:14

It was the day of Preparation for Passover, about the sixth hour. 📖

Pilate told the Jews, “Here is your King!”

**Help:** Preparation was the day for getting ready for the festival.

The source gives the sixth hour; this draft keeps that time wording.

## John 19:15

They shouted, “Away with him! 📖

Away with him!

Crucify him!”

Pilate asked, “Shall I crucify your King?”

The chief priests replied, “We have no king but Caesar.”

## John 19:16

Pilate handed Jesus over to be crucified, and the soldiers took him away. 📖

## John 19:17

Carrying his own cross, Jesus went out to The Place of the Skull. 📖

In Hebrew, this place is called Golgotha.

## John 19:18

They crucified him there with two others, one on each side, and Jesus in the middle. 📖

## John 19:19

Pilate had a notice put on the cross. 📖

It read, “JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS.”

## John 19:20

Many Jews read the sign because the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city. 📖

The sign was written in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek.

## John 19:21

The Jewish chief priests told Pilate, “Do not write, ‘The King of the Jews.’ 📖

Only write that he said, ‘I am the King of the Jews.’”

## John 19:22

Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.” 📖

## John 19:23

After crucifying Jesus, the soldiers divided his clothes into four parts, one for each soldier. 📖

His tunic remained; it had no seams and was woven in one piece from top to bottom.

**Help:** A tunic was a garment worn next to the body; seams join pieces of cloth.

## John 19:24

They said to each other, “Let us not tear it. 📖

Let us cast lots to decide who gets it.”

This fulfilled Scripture: “They divided my clothes among them and cast lots for my clothing.”

That is what the soldiers did.

**Help:** Casting lots was a way to choose by chance; Scripture means the sacred writings.

Fulfilled means that what Scripture said came about.

## John 19:25

Jesus' mother and her sister stood near his cross. 📖

Mary, the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene were also there.

## John 19:26

Jesus saw his mother and the disciple he loved standing nearby. 📖

He told his mother, “Woman, here is your son.”

## John 19:27

Then Jesus told the disciple, “Here is your mother.” 📖

From that hour, this disciple took her into his home.

## John 19:28

Jesus knew everything had now been completed. 📖

To fulfill Scripture, he said, “I am thirsty.”

## John 19:29

A jar of sour wine stood there. 📖

They soaked a sponge in the wine, put it on a hyssop stalk, and raised it to his mouth.

**Help:** Hyssop is a plant.

## John 19:30

After receiving the sour wine, Jesus said, “It is finished.” 📖

He bowed his head and gave up his spirit.

**Help:** Giving up his spirit here describes Jesus dying.

## John 19:31

It was Preparation day, and the next day was a High Sabbath. 📖

The Jews did not want the bodies to remain on the crosses during the Sabbath.

They asked Pilate to have the men's legs broken and their bodies removed.

**Help:** The Sabbath was a day of rest; this one was especially important because of the festival.

## John 19:32

The soldiers broke the legs of the first man crucified with Jesus, and then the other man's legs. 📖

## John 19:33

But when they came to Jesus, they saw he was already dead and did not break his legs. 📖

## John 19:34

Instead, one soldier pierced Jesus' side with a spear, and blood and water flowed out immediately. 📖

## John 19:35

The person who saw this has testified about it, and his testimony is true. 📖

He knows he tells the truth, so that you also may believe.

**Help:** Testimony is a witness's account of what happened.

## John 19:36

These things happened to fulfill Scripture: “Not one of his bones will be broken.” 📖

## John 19:37

Another Scripture says, “They will look at the one they have pierced.” 📖

## John 19:38

Joseph of Arimathea was Jesus' disciple, but secretly, because he feared the Jews. 📖

Afterward, he asked Pilate to let him remove Jesus' body.

Pilate allowed it, so Joseph came and removed the body.

## John 19:39

Nicodemus, who had earlier visited Jesus at night, also brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes. 📖

It weighed about 75 pounds.

**Help:** Myrrh and aloes are fragrant materials used here in preparing the body for burial.

## John 19:40

They took Jesus' body and wrapped it in linen cloths with the spices, following the Jewish burial custom. 📖

## John 19:41

There was a garden where Jesus was crucified. 📖

In it was a new tomb where no one had yet been laid.

## John 19:42

Because it was the Jewish day of Preparation and the tomb was nearby, they placed Jesus there. 📖
