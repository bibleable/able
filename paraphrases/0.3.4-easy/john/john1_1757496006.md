# John 1 — The Word becomes human

**About this chapter**

This chapter introduces Jesus and his first followers.

The Word and the Light are names used for Jesus.

John the Baptist tells people about him.

## John 1:1

In the beginning, the Word already existed. 📖

The Word was with God.

The Word was God.

## John 1:2

He was with God in the beginning. 📖

## John 1:3

God made everything through him. 📖

Nothing was made without him.

## John 1:4

Life was in him. 📖

That life was the light for all people.

**Help with this picture:** Light pictures the life and truth Jesus brings.

## John 1:5

The Light shines in the darkness. 📖

The darkness has not defeated it.

## John 1:6

God sent a man named John. 📖

## John 1:7

John came to tell people about the Light. 📖

Through his message, everyone could believe.

## John 1:8

John was not the Light. 📖

He came to tell people about the Light.

## John 1:9

The true Light was coming into the world. 📖

He gives light to everyone.

## John 1:10

He was in the world. 📖

God had made the world through him.

But the world did not recognise him.

## John 1:11

He came to his own people. 📖

But his own people did not welcome him.

## John 1:12

Some people welcomed him and believed in his name. 📖

He gave them the right to become God's children.

## John 1:13

They became God's children through birth from God. 📖

This birth did not come from human family, desire, or choice.

## John 1:14

The Word became human and lived among us. 📖

We saw his glory as the Father's one and only Son.

He was full of God's kindness and truth.

**Help with a word:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## John 1:15

John told people about him. 📖

John called out, “This is the one I spoke about.

He comes after me, but is greater than me, because he existed before me.”

## John 1:16

From all that he has, we have received kindness upon kindness. 📖

## John 1:17

God gave the law through Moses. 📖

God's kindness and truth came through Jesus Christ.

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## John 1:18

No one has ever seen God. 📖

But the one and only Son has made God known.

The Son is himself God and is close to the Father.

## John 1:19

Jewish leaders in Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to John. 📖

They asked, “Who are you?”

**Help with words:** Priests and Levites served in Jewish worship.

## John 1:20

John answered openly. 📖

He said, “I am not the Christ.”

## John 1:21

They asked, “Are you Elijah?” 📖

John said, “No.”

They asked, “Are you the Prophet?”

John said, “No.”

**Help with these names:** They were asking about people God had promised would come.

## John 1:22

They said, “Who are you? 📖

We need an answer for those who sent us.

What do you say about yourself?”

## John 1:23

John answered with words from the prophet Isaiah. 📖

“I am a voice calling in the desert.

Make a straight path for the Lord.”

**Help with this picture:** A straight path pictures getting ready for the Lord.

## John 1:24

The people sent to John included Pharisees. 📖

**Help with a word:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## John 1:25

They asked, “Why do you baptize if you are not the Christ, Elijah, or the Prophet?” 📖

**Help with a word:** Baptism uses water as a sign of turning to God.

## John 1:26

John answered, “I baptize with water. 📖

But someone you do not know stands among you.”

## John 1:27

“He is the one who comes after me. 📖

I am not worthy even to untie his sandal straps.”

## John 1:28

This happened at Bethany, across the Jordan River. 📖

John was baptizing there.

## John 1:29

The next day, John saw Jesus coming. 📖

John said, “Look!

Here is God's Lamb, who takes away the world's sin.”

**Help with this picture:** Lambs were offered in worship.

John uses this picture for Jesus, who takes away wrongdoing against God.

## John 1:30

“This is the man I spoke about. 📖

He comes after me, but is greater than me, because he existed before me.”

## John 1:31

“I did not know who he was. 📖

I came baptizing with water so that Israel could recognise him.”

## John 1:32

John said, “I saw the Spirit come down from heaven like a dove. 📖

The Spirit stayed on him.”

## John 1:33

“I did not know him. 📖

But God sent me to baptize with water and told me what to watch for.

The Spirit would come down and stay on a man.

That man would baptize with the Holy Spirit.”

## John 1:34

“I have seen this. 📖

I tell you that this is the Son of God.”

## John 1:35

The next day, John was there again with 2 of his followers. 📖

## John 1:36

John saw Jesus walking past. 📖

John said, “Look!

Here is God's Lamb!”

**Help with this title:** John uses the picture of a lamb offered to God for Jesus.

## John 1:37

The 2 followers heard John and followed Jesus. 📖

## John 1:38

Jesus turned and saw them. 📖

He asked, “What do you want?”

They said, “Rabbi, where are you staying?”

**Help with a word:** Rabbi means teacher.

## John 1:39

Jesus said, “Come and see.” 📖

They went and saw where he stayed.

They spent that day with him.

It was about the tenth hour.

**Help with time:** This is about 4 in the afternoon when counting hours from sunrise.

## John 1:40

One of the 2 was Andrew, Simon Peter's brother. 📖

He had heard John and followed Jesus.

## John 1:41

Andrew first found his brother Simon. 📖

He said, “We have found the Messiah!”

**Help with a title:** Messiah means Christ, the King God promised to send.

## John 1:42

Andrew brought Simon to Jesus. 📖

Jesus said, “You are Simon, son of John.

You will be called Cephas.”

**Help with a name:** Cephas is translated as Peter, a name meaning rock.

## John 1:43

The next day, Jesus decided to go to Galilee. 📖

He found Philip and said, “Follow me.”

## John 1:44

Philip came from Bethsaida. 📖

Andrew and Peter came from that town too.

## John 1:45

Philip found Nathanael. 📖

He said, “We found the one Moses and the prophets wrote about.

He is Jesus from Nazareth, Joseph's son.”

## John 1:46

Nathanael asked, “Can anything good come from Nazareth?” 📖

Philip said, “Come and see.”

## John 1:47

Jesus saw Nathanael coming. 📖

Jesus said, “Here is a true Israelite.

There is nothing false in him.”

## John 1:48

Nathanael asked, “How do you know me?” 📖

Jesus answered, “Before Philip called you, I saw you under the fig tree.”

## John 1:49

Nathanael said, “Teacher, you are the Son of God! 📖

You are the King of Israel!”

## John 1:50

Jesus said, “Do you believe because I saw you under the fig tree? 📖

You will see greater things than this.”

## John 1:51

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 📖

You will see heaven open.

God's angels will go up and come down on the Son of Man.”

**Help with a title:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.
