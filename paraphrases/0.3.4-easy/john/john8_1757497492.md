# John 8 — Light, truth, and freedom

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers the woman's accusers and speaks about his identity, truth, and freedom.

## John 8:1

Jesus went to the Mount of Olives. 📖

## John 8:2

Early next morning, he returned to the temple courts, and all the people came. 📖

He sat and taught them.

## John 8:3

The law teachers and Pharisees brought a woman caught in adultery and stood her before them. 📖

**Help:** Adultery breaks faithfulness in marriage through a sexual relationship with someone else.

## John 8:4

They said, “Teacher, she was caught in the act of adultery. 📖

## John 8:5

Moses' Law says to stone such a woman; what do you say?” 📖

**Help:** Stone means attack by throwing stones; this is the accusers' appeal to the Law.

## John 8:6

They tested him to find grounds for accusation, but he bent and wrote on the ground with his finger. 📖

## John 8:7

When they kept questioning, he straightened and said the sinless one among them should throw the first stone. 📖

## John 8:8

He bent again and wrote on the ground. 📖

## John 8:9

They heard and left one by one, the older ones first, until Jesus remained with the woman standing there. 📖

## John 8:10

He straightened and asked, “Woman, where are your accusers? 📖

Did nobody condemn you?”

## John 8:11

She answered, “Nobody, Lord.” 📖

He said, “Neither do I condemn you; go and sin no more.”

**Help:** Condemn means pronounce someone guilty and deserving punishment; sin is wrongdoing against God.

## John 8:12

Jesus said, “I am the world's light. 📖

Anyone following me will never walk in darkness, but have life's light.”

## John 8:13

The Pharisees said his testimony about himself was not valid. 📖

**Help:** Testimony means evidence someone gives.

## John 8:14

He answered, “Even if I testify about myself, my testimony is valid. 📖

This is because I know where I came from and where I am going.

You do not know either.

## John 8:15

You judge according to the flesh; I judge nobody. 📖

**Help:** According to the flesh concerns judging on human grounds; its meaning needs further assessment.

## John 8:16

Even if I judge, it is true judgment, because I am with the Father who sent me, not alone. 📖

## John 8:17

Your own Law says two men's testimony is valid. 📖

## John 8:18

I testify about myself, and the Father who sent me testifies too.” 📖

## John 8:19

They asked where his Father was. 📖

Jesus answered, “You know neither me nor my Father.

If you knew me, you would know my Father too.”

## John 8:20

He taught this near the treasury in the temple courts. 📖

Nobody seized him because his hour had not come yet.

**Help:** The treasury held temple offerings.

## John 8:21

He said, “I am leaving; you will seek me but die in your sin. 📖

You cannot come where I go.”

## John 8:22

The Jews wondered whether he would kill himself because he said they could not come. 📖

## John 8:23

He said, “You are from below and of this world; I am from above and not of this world. 📖

## John 8:24

You will die in your sins unless you believe I am he. 📖

That is why I told you this.

## John 8:25

They asked who he was, and he said he had told them from the beginning. 📖

## John 8:26

“I have much to say and judge about you, but my sender is truthful. 📖

I tell the world what I heard from him.”

## John 8:27

They did not understand he spoke about the Father. 📖

## John 8:28

Jesus said, “When you lift up the Son of Man, you will know I am he. 📖

You will know I do nothing on my own, but speak exactly what the Father taught me.

**Help:** Son of Man is Jesus' title for himself; lifted up points to his coming crucifixion.

## John 8:29

My sender is with me and never left me alone, because I always please him.” 📖

## John 8:30

Many believed in him as he spoke. 📖

## John 8:31

He told the Jews who believed, “If you continue in my word, you truly are my disciples. 📖

## John 8:32

You will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.” 📖

## John 8:33

They said they descended from Abraham and had never been anyone's slaves. 📖

How could he say they would be freed?

## John 8:34

Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, everyone sinning is sin's slave. 📖

## John 8:35

A slave does not stay in the house forever, but a son does. 📖

## John 8:36

If the Son frees you, you will be truly free. 📖

## John 8:37

I know you descend from Abraham, but seek to kill me because my word has no place within you. 📖

## John 8:38

I speak what I saw with the Father, and you do what you heard from your father.” 📖

## John 8:39

They called Abraham their father. 📖

Jesus said, “If you were Abraham's children, you would do his works.

## John 8:40

Instead, you try to kill me, although I told you truth heard from God. 📖

Abraham never did that.

## John 8:41

You do your father's works.” 📖

They replied, “We are not illegitimate children; our only Father is God.”

**Help:** Illegitimate refers to children born outside marriage; here the speakers use it in their argument about parentage.

It is not a measure of any child's worth.

## John 8:42

Jesus said, “If God were your Father, you would love me, because I came from him. 📖

I did not come on my own; he sent me.

## John 8:43

You fail to understand because you cannot accept my message. 📖

## John 8:44

You belong to your father, the devil, and want to carry out his desires. 📖

He murdered from the beginning and refused truth, having no truth in him.

He speaks his native language when lying, because he is a liar and the father of lies.

**Help:** This continues Jesus' exchange with these speakers; it is not a statement about all Jewish people.

## John 8:45

Because I speak truth, you do not believe me. 📖

## John 8:46

Who can prove me guilty of sin? 📖

If I speak truth, why not believe me?

## John 8:47

Anyone belonging to God hears God's words; you do not hear because you do not belong to him.” 📖

## John 8:48

The Jews asked if they were right to say he was a Samaritan and had a demon. 📖

## John 8:49

Jesus answered, “I have no demon, but honour my Father, and you dishonour me. 📖

## John 8:50

I do not seek my glory; someone seeks it and is the Judge. 📖

## John 8:51

Truly, truly, anyone keeping my word will never see death.” 📖

## John 8:52

The Jews claimed this proved he had a demon. 📖

Abraham and the prophets died, yet he said anyone keeping his word would never taste death.

## John 8:53

They asked whether he was greater than Abraham and the prophets, who died, and who he claimed to be. 📖

## John 8:54

Jesus answered, “My glory means nothing if I glorify myself. 📖

My Father glorifies me, and you call him your God.

## John 8:55

You do not know him, but I do. 📖

If I said I did not know him, I would be a liar like you.

I do know him and keep his word.

## John 8:56

Your father Abraham rejoiced that he would see my day. 📖

He saw it and was glad.”

## John 8:57

The Jews asked how he saw Abraham when not yet fifty years old. 📖

## John 8:58

Jesus said, “Truly, truly, before Abraham was born, I am!” 📖

## John 8:59

They took stones to throw, but Jesus was hidden and left the temple area. 📖
