# John 10 — The good shepherd

**About this chapter**

Jesus speaks about sheep, his life, and his relationship with the Father.

## John 10:1

Jesus said, “Truly, truly, I tell you this. 📖

Anyone entering the sheepfold by climbing elsewhere instead of using the gate is a thief and robber.

**Help:** A sheepfold is an enclosed place for keeping sheep.

## John 10:2

The one entering by the gate is the sheep's shepherd. 📖

## John 10:3

The gatekeeper opens for him, and the sheep listen for his voice. 📖

He calls his sheep by name and leads them out.

## John 10:4

After bringing out all his own sheep, he goes ahead, and they follow because they know his voice. 📖

## John 10:5

They never follow a stranger, but run away because they do not recognize his voice.” 📖

## John 10:6

Jesus used this picture, but they did not understand what he meant. 📖

## John 10:7

He said again, “Truly, truly, I am the sheep's gate. 📖

## John 10:8

All who came before me were thieves and robbers, but the sheep did not listen. 📖

## John 10:9

I am the gate; anyone entering through me will be saved. 📖

He will come in, go out, and find pasture.

**Help:** Pasture is land where sheep find food; Jesus uses a shepherd's care as a picture.

## John 10:10

The thief only comes to steal, kill, and destroy. 📖

I came so they may have life in all its fullness.

## John 10:11

I am the good shepherd, who gives his life for the sheep. 📖

## John 10:12

The hired worker is not the shepherd and does not own the sheep. 📖

Seeing a wolf, he leaves them and runs away; the wolf attacks and scatters them.

## John 10:13

He runs because he works for wages and does not care about the sheep. 📖

## John 10:14

I am the good shepherd; I know my sheep, and they know me. 📖

## John 10:15

This is like the Father knowing me and me knowing the Father. 📖

I give my life for the sheep.

## John 10:16

I have other sheep not of this fold. 📖

I must bring them too, and they will listen to my voice.

Then there will be one flock and one shepherd.

## John 10:17

The Father loves me because I give my life in order to take it up again. 📖

## John 10:18

Nobody takes it from me; I give it willingly. 📖

I have authority to give it and authority to take it up again.

I received this command from my Father.”

**Help:** Authority means the right and power to act.

## John 10:19

His words again divided the Jews. 📖

## John 10:20

Many claimed he had a demon and was out of his mind, asking why anyone listened. 📖

**Help:** These are accusations spoken by some in the group.

## John 10:21

Others said these were not a demon-possessed person's words and asked if a demon could give blind people sight. 📖

## John 10:22

It was winter, and Jerusalem held the Feast of Dedication. 📖

## John 10:23

Jesus walked in Solomon's Colonnade in the temple courts. 📖

**Help:** A colonnade is a walkway with columns supporting a roof.

## John 10:24

The Jews surrounded him, asking how long he would keep them wondering. 📖

“If you are the Christ, tell us plainly.”

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## John 10:25

Jesus answered, “I told you, but you did not believe. 📖

My works in my Father's name testify for me.

## John 10:26

You refuse to believe because you are not my sheep. 📖

## John 10:27

My sheep listen to my voice. 📖

I know them, and they follow me.

## John 10:28

I give them eternal life, and they will never perish. 📖

Nobody can snatch them from my hand.

**Help:** Eternal life is life without end; perish means die or be destroyed.

## John 10:29

My Father gave them to me and is greater than all. 📖

Nobody can snatch them from my Father's hand.

## John 10:30

I and the Father are one.” 📖

## John 10:31

The Jews again picked up stones to stone him. 📖

**Help:** Stone means attack by throwing stones.

## John 10:32

Jesus asked, “I have shown you many good works from the Father. 📖

For which are you stoning me?”

## John 10:33

They said, “For blasphemy, not good work, because you are a man making yourself God.” 📖

**Help:** Blasphemy means speaking against God; this is their accusation.

## John 10:34

Jesus answered, “Does your Law not say, ‘I said you are gods’? 📖

## John 10:35

Suppose he called those receiving God's word ‘gods.’ 📖

Scripture cannot be broken.

## John 10:36

What about the one whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world? 📖

How can you accuse me of blasphemy for saying I am God's Son?

**Help:** Sanctified means set apart as holy for God; this argument needs further interpretation.

## John 10:37

If I do not do my Father's works, do not believe me. 📖

## John 10:38

If I do them, believe the works, even if you do not believe me. 📖

Then you may know and understand the Father is in me, and I in him.”

## John 10:39

They again tried to seize him, but he escaped their grasp. 📖

## John 10:40

He returned across the Jordan to John's first baptizing place and stayed there. 📖

## John 10:41

Many came, saying John performed no sign, but everything he said about Jesus was true. 📖

## John 10:42

Many there believed in Jesus. 📖
