# John 13 — Jesus washes his disciples' feet

**About this chapter**

Jesus serves his disciples, identifies his betrayer, commands love, and predicts Peter's denial.

## John 13:1

Before Passover, Jesus knew his hour had come to leave the world and return to the Father. 📖

He loved his own in the world to the very end.

## John 13:2

During supper, the devil had already put the plan to betray Jesus into Judas’s heart.

Judas was the son of Simon Iscariot. 📖

## John 13:3

Jesus knew the Father had put everything into his hands. 📖

He knew he had come from God and was returning to God.

## John 13:4

He rose from supper, removed his outer clothing, and wrapped a towel round his waist. 📖

## John 13:5

He poured water into a basin and began washing the disciples' feet. 📖

He dried them with the towel round his waist.

## John 13:6

Jesus came to Simon Peter. 📖

Peter asked, “Lord, will you wash my feet?”

## John 13:7

Jesus answered, “You do not understand what I am doing now. 📖

But you will understand later.”

## John 13:8

Peter refused ever to let him wash his feet. 📖

Jesus said, “Unless I wash you, you have no part with me.”

## John 13:9

Simon Peter said, “Lord, wash my hands and head too, rather than only my feet!” 📖

## John 13:10

Jesus said, “Anyone already bathed needs only his feet washed to be completely clean. 📖

You are clean, but not all of you.”

## John 13:11

He knew his betrayer, which is why he said not all were clean. 📖

## John 13:12

After washing their feet, Jesus put on his outer clothing and lay back at the meal again. 📖

“Do you understand what I did for you?

## John 13:13

You rightly call me Teacher and Lord, because that is what I am. 📖

## John 13:14

If I, your Lord and Teacher, washed your feet, you should wash each other's feet. 📖

## John 13:15

I gave you an example to follow, doing what I did for you. 📖

## John 13:16

Truly, truly, a servant is not greater than his master, nor a messenger than the one sending him. 📖

## John 13:17

If you know these things, you will be blessed if you do them. 📖

## John 13:18

I do not mean all of you; I know those I chose. 📖

Scripture must be fulfilled: ‘The one sharing my bread lifted his heel against me.’

**Help:** A raised heel pictures turning against someone.

## John 13:19

I tell you before it happens so that afterwards you will believe I am he. 📖

## John 13:20

Truly, truly, anyone receiving the one I send receives me. 📖

Anyone receiving me receives the one who sent me.”

## John 13:21

Jesus became troubled in spirit and testified, “Truly, truly, one of you will betray me.” 📖

## John 13:22

The disciples looked at each other, puzzled about whom Jesus meant. 📖

## John 13:23

The disciple Jesus loved reclined at his side. 📖

**Help:** People sometimes ate leaning on couches; this disciple is not named here.

## John 13:24

Simon Peter motioned for him to ask whom Jesus meant. 📖

## John 13:25

Leaning against Jesus, he asked, “Lord, who is it?” 📖

## John 13:26

Jesus answered, “It is the one I give this piece of food to, after I dip it.” 📖

Jesus dipped the piece and gave it to Judas, son of Simon Iscariot.

**Help:** A morsel is a small piece of food.

## John 13:27

After Judas took it, Satan entered him. 📖

Jesus told him to do quickly what he was about to do.

## John 13:28

Nobody at table knew why Jesus said that. 📖

## John 13:29

Some thought he told Judas to buy feast supplies or give to poor people, because Judas kept the money bag. 📖

## John 13:30

As soon as Judas received the piece of food, he went out into the night. 📖

## John 13:31

After Judas left, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man is glorified, and God is glorified in him. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is Jesus' title for himself; glorified means shown in glory and honour.

## John 13:32

If God is glorified in him, God will glorify the Son in himself, and do so at once. 📖

## John 13:33

Little children, I am with you only a little longer. 📖

You will seek me; as I told the Jews, you cannot come where I am going.

**Help:** Jesus affectionately addresses his followers as little children.

## John 13:34

I give you a new command: love one another as I loved you. 📖

You too must love one another.

## John 13:35

Everyone will know you are my disciples if you love one another.” 📖

## John 13:36

Simon Peter asked Jesus, “Lord, where are you going?” 📖

Jesus answered, “You cannot follow now, but will follow later.”

## John 13:37

Peter said, “Lord, why can I not follow now? 📖

I will give my life for you.”

## John 13:38

Jesus answered, “Will you give your life for me? 📖

Truly, truly, before the rooster crows, you will deny me 3 times.”
