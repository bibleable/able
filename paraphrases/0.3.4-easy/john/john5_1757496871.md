# John 5 — Healing and the Son's authority

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals a man on the Sabbath and speaks about the Father, life, judgment, and witnesses.

## John 5:1

Later, Jesus went up to Jerusalem for a Jewish feast. 📖

## John 5:2

Near Jerusalem's Sheep Gate is a pool called Bethesda in Hebrew, with five covered colonnades. 📖

**Help:** Colonnades are walkways with rows of columns supporting a roof.

## John 5:3

Many sick people lay there, including blind people, people unable to walk properly, and paralyzed people. 📖

**Help:** Paralyzed means unable to move parts of the body.

## John 5:4

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## John 5:5

One man had been disabled for thirty-eight years. 📖

## John 5:6

Jesus saw him lying there, knew he had suffered long, and asked whether he wanted to get well. 📖

## John 5:7

He answered, “Sir, nobody helps me into the pool when the water stirs. 📖

Someone else enters before I reach it.”

## John 5:8

Jesus told him to rise, pick up his mat, and walk. 📖

## John 5:9

He immediately became well, picked up his mat, and walked. 📖

It was the Sabbath.

**Help:** The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish rest day.

## John 5:10

The Jews told the healed man carrying his mat was unlawful on the Sabbath. 📖

## John 5:11

He said the man healing him had told him to pick it up and walk. 📖

## John 5:12

They asked who told him to do this. 📖

## John 5:13

He did not know, because Jesus slipped away while a crowd was there. 📖

## John 5:14

Jesus later found him in the temple and said, “You are well; stop sinning or something worse may happen.” 📖

**Help:** Sin means wrongdoing against God; this warning does not say all illness or disability comes from personal sin.

## John 5:15

He went and told the Jews that Jesus healed him. 📖

## John 5:16

They persecuted Jesus because he did these things on the Sabbath. 📖

**Help:** Persecute means mistreat someone because of their beliefs or actions.

## John 5:17

Jesus answered, “My Father works to this very day, and I am working too.” 📖

## John 5:18

They tried even harder to kill him, because he broke the Sabbath and called God his own Father. 📖

He was making himself equal with God.

## John 5:19

Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, the Son can do nothing himself unless he sees the Father doing it. 📖

Whatever the Father does, the Son does too.

## John 5:20

The Father loves the Son and shows him everything he does. 📖

He will show even greater works, amazing you.

## John 5:21

As the Father raises dead people and gives life, the Son gives life to anyone he chooses. 📖

## John 5:22

The Father judges nobody, having assigned all judgment to the Son. 📖

## John 5:23

Everyone should honour the Son as they honour the Father. 📖

Anyone not honouring the Son does not honour the Father who sent him.

## John 5:24

Truly, truly, anyone hearing my word and believing the one sending me has eternal life. 📖

He will not come under judgment, having crossed from death to life.

**Help:** Eternal life is life without end.

## John 5:25

Truly, truly, the hour is coming and is now here when dead people hear the voice of God's Son. 📖

Those hearing will live.

## John 5:26

The Father has life in himself. 📖

He also granted the Son to have life in himself.

## John 5:27

He gave him authority to carry out judgment because he is the Son of Man. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## John 5:28

Do not be amazed; an hour is coming when everyone in their graves will hear his voice. 📖

## John 5:29

They will come out, those doing good to resurrection of life, and those doing evil to resurrection of judgment. 📖

**Help:** Resurrection means rising from death.

## John 5:30

I can do nothing alone, and I judge only as I hear. 📖

My judgment is just, because I seek the sender's will rather than my own.

## John 5:31

If I testify about myself, my testimony is not valid. 📖

**Help:** Testify means give evidence; testimony is the evidence given.

## John 5:32

Another testifies about me, and I know his testimony is valid. 📖

## John 5:33

You sent to John, and he testified to the truth. 📖

## John 5:34

I do not accept human testimony, but I say this so you may be saved. 📖

## John 5:35

John was a burning, shining lamp, and for a while you gladly enjoyed his light. 📖

## John 5:36

My evidence is greater than John's: the very works the Father gave me to finish, which I do. 📖

These testify that the Father sent me.

## John 5:37

The Father who sent me has himself testified about me. 📖

You never heard his voice or saw his form.

## John 5:38

His word does not remain in you, because you do not believe the one he sent. 📖

## John 5:39

You study Scripture closely, thinking that through it you have eternal life. 📖

These very words testify about me.

## John 5:40

Yet you refuse to come to me for life. 📖

## John 5:41

I do not accept glory from people. 📖

## John 5:42

I know you have no love of God within you. 📖

## John 5:43

I came in my Father's name, and you did not receive me. 📖

But if someone comes in his own name, you will receive him.

## John 5:44

How can you believe while accepting glory from each other but not seeking glory from the only God? 📖

## John 5:45

Do not think I will accuse you before the Father. 📖

Moses accuses you, although you put your hope in him.

## John 5:46

If you believed Moses, you would believe me, because he wrote about me. 📖

## John 5:47

Since you do not believe his writing, how will you believe my words?” 📖
