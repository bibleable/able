# John 15 — Stay close to Jesus

**About this chapter**

Jesus compares himself to a vine and his followers to its branches.

A vine is a plant that grows fruit such as grapes.

## John 15:1

Jesus said, “I am the true vine. 📖

My Father is the gardener.”

## John 15:2

“He cuts off each branch in me that grows no fruit. 📖

He trims each branch that grows fruit, so it can grow more.”

**Help with this picture:** Trimming removes parts of a plant to help it grow well.

## John 15:3

“You are already clean because of the message I have spoken to you.” 📖

## John 15:4

“Stay in me, and I will stay in you. 📖

A branch cannot grow fruit unless it stays on the vine.

You cannot grow fruit unless you stay in me.”

## John 15:5

“I am the vine. 📖

You are the branches.

Anyone who stays in me, with me in them, will grow much fruit.

Apart from me you can do nothing.”

**Help with this picture:** Fruit pictures the results of life with Jesus.

## John 15:6

“Anyone who does not stay in me is like a branch thrown away. 📖

It dries up.

People gather such branches and burn them.”

## John 15:7

“If you stay in me and keep my words in you, ask what you wish. 📖

It will be done for you.”

## John 15:8

“Growing much fruit brings honour to my Father. 📖

It shows that you are my followers.”

## John 15:9

“I have loved you as the Father has loved me. 📖

Stay in my love.”

## John 15:10

“If you obey my commands, you will stay in my love. 📖

I obey my Father's commands and stay in his love.”

## John 15:11

“I have told you this so my joy may be in you. 📖

Then your joy will be complete.”

## John 15:12

“This is my command. 📖

Love each other as I have loved you.”

## John 15:13

“The greatest love is to give up your life for your friends.” 📖

## John 15:14

“You are my friends if you do what I command.” 📖

## John 15:15

“I no longer call you servants. 📖

Servants do not know what their master is doing.

I call you friends.

I have told you everything I learned from my Father.”

## John 15:16

“You did not choose me. 📖

I chose you to go and grow fruit that lasts.

Then the Father will give you whatever you ask in my name.”

**Help with these words:** Asking in Jesus' name means asking as someone who belongs to him.

## John 15:17

“This is my command to you. 📖

Love each other.”

## John 15:18

“If the world hates you, remember that it hated me first.” 📖

## John 15:19

“If you belonged to the world, it would love you as its own. 📖

But I chose you out of the world.

You do not belong to it, so it hates you.”

## John 15:20

“Remember that no servant is greater than the master. 📖

If they attacked me, they will attack you too.

If they obeyed my teaching, they will obey yours too.”

## John 15:21

“They will do these things because you belong to me. 📖

They do not know the one who sent me.”

## John 15:22

“If I had not come and spoken, they would not be guilty of this sin. 📖

Now they have no excuse for their sin.”

## John 15:23

“Anyone who hates me also hates my Father.” 📖

## John 15:24

“I did works among them that no one else did. 📖

Without those works, they would not be guilty of this sin.

Now they have seen and hated both me and my Father.”

## John 15:25

“This fulfils the words in their Law. 📖

They hated me without a reason.”

## John 15:26

“I will send the Helper from the Father. 📖

He is the Spirit of truth who comes from the Father.

He will tell people about me.”

## John 15:27

“You must also tell people about me. 📖

You have been with me from the beginning.”
