# John 21 — Jesus and his followers by the sea

**About this chapter**

Jesus meets his followers after a night of fishing.

Jesus speaks with Peter about love, care, and following him.

## John 21:1

Later, Jesus showed himself to the disciples again, by the Sea of Tiberias. 📖

This is how it happened.

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 21:2

Simon Peter and Thomas, also called Didymus, were together. 📖

Nathanael from Cana in Galilee was there too.

The sons of Zebedee and 2 other disciples were also there.

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 21:3

Simon Peter said, “I am going fishing.” 📖

The others said, “We will go with you.”

They went out and got into the boat.

That night, they caught nothing.

## John 21:4

Early in the morning, Jesus stood on the shore. 📖

His followers did not know that it was Jesus.

## John 21:5

Jesus called to them, “Children, do you have any fish?” 📖

They answered, “No.”

## John 21:6

Jesus said, “Throw the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.” 📖

They threw the net there.

There were so many fish that they could not pull the net in.

## John 21:7

The disciple whom Jesus loved told Peter, “It is the Lord!” 📖

Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord.

He put on his outer clothing, which he had taken off.

Then he jumped into the sea.

**Help with disciple:** A disciple is a follower and learner.

## John 21:8

The other disciples came to shore in the boat, dragging the net full of fish. 📖

They were only about 100 yards from land.

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

**Help with yards:** A yard measures distance.

100 yards is about 91 metres.

## John 21:9

When they landed, they saw a charcoal fire with fish on it, and some bread. 📖

**Help with charcoal:** Charcoal is fuel made from wood.

## John 21:10

Jesus told them, “Bring some of the fish you just caught.” 📖

## John 21:11

Simon Peter went aboard and dragged the net to shore. 📖

The net held 153 large fish.

Even with so many fish, the net had not torn.

## John 21:12

Jesus said, “Come and have breakfast.” 📖

None of the disciples dared to ask, “Who are you?”

They knew it was the Lord.

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 21:13

Jesus came, took the bread, and gave it to them. 📖

He did the same with the fish.

## John 21:14

This was the 3rd time Jesus appeared to his disciples after he was raised from the dead. 📖

**Help with disciples:** Disciples are followers and learners.

## John 21:15

After they ate, Jesus asked, “Simon son of John, do you love me more than these?” 📖

Peter answered, “Yes, Lord.

You know I love you.”

Jesus said, “Feed my lambs.”

**Help with lambs:** Lambs are young sheep.

Jesus uses a picture of caring for sheep to describe caring for his people.

**About these:** The comparison is retained without choosing between possible meanings of “these.”

## John 21:16

Jesus asked a 2nd time, “Simon son of John, do you love me?” 📖

Peter answered, “Yes, Lord.

You know I love you.”

Jesus said, “Care for my sheep.”

**Help with sheep:** Jesus uses sheep as a picture of his people.

## John 21:17

Jesus asked a 3rd time, “Simon son of John, do you love me?” 📖

Peter felt deeply hurt because Jesus asked him a 3rd time, “Do you love me?”

Peter said, “Lord, you know everything.

You know I love you.”

Jesus said, “Feed my sheep.

**Help with sheep:** Jesus uses feeding sheep as a picture of caring for his people.

## John 21:18

I tell you the truth, truly. 📖

When you were young, you dressed yourself and walked where you wanted.

When you are old, you will stretch out your hands.

Someone else will dress you and lead you where you do not want to go.”

## John 21:19

Jesus said this to show the kind of death by which Peter would bring glory to God. 📖

Then Jesus told Peter, “Follow me.”

**Help with glory:** Bringing glory to God means showing God’s greatness and honour.

## John 21:20

Peter turned around and saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following them. 📖

At supper, this disciple had leaned back against Jesus.

He had asked, “Lord, who is going to betray you?”

**Help with disciple:** A disciple is a follower and learner.

**Help with betray:** Betray means turning against someone who trusts you.

## John 21:21

When Peter saw this disciple, he asked Jesus, “Lord, what about him?” 📖

**Help with disciple:** A disciple is a follower and learner.

## John 21:22

Jesus answered, “If I want him to stay until I return, what is that to you? 📖

You follow me!”

## John 21:23

A report spread among the brothers that this disciple would not die. 📖

But Jesus did not say the disciple would not die.

Jesus only said, “If I want him to stay until I return, what is that to you?”

**Help with brothers:** Here, brothers refers to the community of believers.

**Help with disciple:** A disciple is a follower and learner.

## John 21:24

This is the disciple who tells about these things and has written them down. 📖

We know that what he says is true.

**Help with disciple:** A disciple is a follower and learner.

## John 21:25

Jesus did many more things. 📖

Suppose every one of them was written down.

I think even the world itself would not have room for all those books.
