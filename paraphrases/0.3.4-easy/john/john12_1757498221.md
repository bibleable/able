# John 12 — Jesus enters Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Mary anoints Jesus' feet; he enters Jerusalem and speaks about his death and people's belief.

## John 12:1

Six days before Passover, Jesus reached Bethany, where Lazarus lived, whom he raised from death. 📖

**Help:** Passover remembers God's rescue of Israel from Egypt.

## John 12:2

They gave him dinner; Martha served, and Lazarus reclined at table with him. 📖

**Help:** People sometimes ate leaning on couches.

## John 12:3

Mary took about a pint of costly perfume made from pure nard and poured it on Jesus' feet. 📖

She wiped his feet with her hair, and the scent filled the house.

**Help:** Nard is a plant used for perfume; a pint measures liquid.

## John 12:4

Judas Iscariot, a disciple who would betray Jesus, asked a question. 📖

## John 12:5

“Why not sell this perfume for three hundred denarii and give the money to poor people?” 📖

**Help:** Denarii were coins; denarius is the name for one coin.

## John 12:6

He did not care about poor people; he was a thief keeping the money bag and taking from it. 📖

## John 12:7

Jesus said, “Leave her alone; she kept this perfume for my burial day. 📖

## John 12:8

Poor people will always be with you. 📖

But you will not always have me.”

## John 12:9

A large crowd of Jews came, hearing Jesus was there and wanting to see the raised Lazarus too. 📖

## John 12:10

The chief priests planned to kill Lazarus too. 📖

## John 12:11

Because of Lazarus, many Jews were leaving them and believing in Jesus. 📖

## John 12:12

Next day, the large crowd visiting the feast heard Jesus was coming to Jerusalem. 📖

## John 12:13

They took palm branches to meet him, shouting, “Hosanna! 📖

Blessed is the one coming in the Lord's name!

Blessed is Israel's King!”

**Help:** Hosanna is a cry asking for rescue and expressing praise.

## John 12:14

Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it, as Scripture says: 📖

## John 12:15

“Do not fear, Daughter of Zion; your King comes seated on a donkey's colt.” 📖

**Help:** Daughter of Zion names Jerusalem and its people; a colt is a young animal.

## John 12:16

The disciples did not understand at first. 📖

After Jesus was glorified, they remembered what was done to him.

They realized these very things were written about him.

## John 12:17

Many people who saw Jesus call Lazarus from the tomb and raise him continued testifying. 📖

## John 12:18

The crowd met him because they heard about this sign. 📖

## John 12:19

The Pharisees told each other, “This does no good; the whole world has gone after him!” 📖

## John 12:20

Some Greeks were among those who went up to worship at the feast. 📖

## John 12:21

They asked Philip from Bethsaida in Galilee, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.” 📖

## John 12:22

Philip told Andrew, and both went to tell Jesus. 📖

## John 12:23

Jesus answered, “The Son of Man's hour to be glorified has come. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is Jesus' title for himself; glorified means shown in glory.

## John 12:24

Truly, truly, a wheat grain remains one seed unless it falls into the ground and dies. 📖

If it dies, it bears much fruit.

## John 12:25

Whoever loves his life will lose it. 📖

Whoever hates his life in this world will keep it for eternal life.

**Help:** Eternal life is life without end; this severe contrast needs further interpretation.

## John 12:26

Anyone serving me must follow me, and my servant will be where I am. 📖

The Father will honour anyone serving me.

## John 12:27

My soul is troubled; what should I say? 📖

Should I say, ‘Father, save me from this hour’?

No, this is why I came to this hour.

## John 12:28

Father, glorify your name!” 📖

A voice from heaven said, “I glorified it and will do so again.”

## John 12:29

The crowd standing there heard it and said it thundered. 📖

Others said an angel spoke to him.

## John 12:30

Jesus said, “The voice was for your benefit, rather than mine. 📖

## John 12:31

Now this world faces judgment, and its prince will be cast out. 📖

## John 12:32

When lifted from the earth, I will draw everyone to myself.” 📖

## John 12:33

He said this to show what kind of death he would die. 📖

## John 12:34

The crowd said, “We heard from the Law that the Christ remains forever. 📖

How can you say the Son of Man must be lifted up?

Who is this Son of Man?”

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## John 12:35

Jesus said, “The Light remains with you a little longer. 📖

Walk while you have it, so darkness will not overtake you.

Anyone walking in darkness does not know where he goes.

## John 12:36

Believe in the Light while you have it, so you may become sons of light.” 📖

He left and was hidden from them.

## John 12:37

Despite his many signs before them, they did not believe. 📖

## John 12:38

This fulfilled Isaiah the prophet's words: “Lord, who believed our message? 📖

To whom was the Lord's arm revealed?”

**Help:** The Lord's arm pictures his power.

## John 12:39

They could not believe, because Isaiah also said: 📖

## John 12:40

“He has blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts. 📖

So they cannot see with their eyes or understand with their hearts.

Otherwise, they could turn and I would heal them.”

## John 12:41

Isaiah spoke this way because he saw Jesus' glory and spoke about him. 📖

## John 12:42

Many leaders believed but feared the Pharisees and synagogue expulsion, so they did not confess him. 📖

**Help:** A synagogue is a Jewish place of worship and teaching.

## John 12:43

They loved praise from people more than praise from God. 📖

## John 12:44

Jesus cried, “Anyone believing in me believes also in the one who sent me. 📖

## John 12:45

Anyone seeing me sees the one who sent me. 📖

## John 12:46

I came as light into the world so nobody believing in me should remain in darkness. 📖

## John 12:47

I do not judge anyone hearing my words without keeping them. 📖

I came to save the world, rather than judge it.

## John 12:48

Anyone rejecting me and my words has a judge: my spoken word will judge him on the last day. 📖

## John 12:49

I did not speak on my own. 📖

The Father who sent me commanded what to say and how to say it.

## John 12:50

I know his command leads to eternal life. 📖

So I speak exactly what the Father told me to say.”
