# John 18 — Jesus is arrested and questioned

**About this chapter**

Jesus is arrested; Peter denies being his disciple; Pilate questions Jesus.

## John 18:1

After speaking, Jesus went out with his disciples across the Kidron Valley. 📖

They entered a garden there.

## John 18:2

Judas, who betrayed Jesus, also knew this place. 📖

Jesus had often met there with his disciples.

## John 18:3

Judas brought a group of soldiers and officers from the chief priests and Pharisees. 📖

They arrived at the garden with lanterns, torches, and weapons.

**Help:** Chief priests were leading priests; the Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## John 18:4

Jesus knew everything that was coming upon him. 📖

He stepped forward and asked, “Who are you looking for?”

## John 18:5

They answered, “Jesus of Nazareth.” 📖

Jesus said, “I am he.”

Judas, his betrayer, stood there with them.

## John 18:6

When Jesus said, “I am he,” they drew back and fell to the ground. 📖

## John 18:7

He asked again, “Who are you looking for?” 📖

They answered, “Jesus of Nazareth.”

## John 18:8

Jesus replied, “I told you I am he. 📖

So if you are looking for me, let these men go.”

## John 18:9

This fulfilled Jesus' words: “I have not lost one of those you have given me.” 📖

**Help:** Jesus' earlier words were addressed to the Father; fulfilled means what he said came about.

## John 18:10

Simon Peter drew his sword and struck the high priest's servant, cutting off his right ear. 📖

The servant's name was Malchus.

## John 18:11

Jesus told Peter, “Put your sword back in its sheath! 📖

Shall I not drink the cup the Father has given me?”

**Help:** A sheath is a sword's cover; the cup pictures the suffering Jesus faces.

## John 18:12

The soldiers, their commander, and the Jewish officers arrested Jesus and tied him up. 📖

## John 18:13

They brought him first to Annas, the father-in-law of Caiaphas, who was high priest that year. 📖

**Help:** Father-in-law means a wife's father; the high priest was the leading priest.

## John 18:14

Caiaphas had advised the Jews that it would be better if one man died for the people. 📖

## John 18:15

Simon Peter and another disciple followed Jesus. 📖

The high priest knew this other disciple, who went with Jesus into the high priest's courtyard.

## John 18:16

Peter stood outside at the door. 📖

The disciple known to the high priest went out, spoke to the doorkeeper, and brought Peter in.

## John 18:17

The servant girl watching the door asked Peter, “Aren't you also one of this man's disciples?” 📖

He answered, “I am not.”

## John 18:18

It was cold, so the servants and officers had made a charcoal fire. 📖

They stood around it to keep warm, and Peter also stood with them, warming himself.

## John 18:19

Meanwhile, the high priest questioned Jesus about his disciples and his teaching. 📖

## John 18:20

Jesus answered, “I have spoken openly to the world. 📖

I always taught in synagogues and at the temple, where all the Jews gather.

I said nothing in secret.

## John 18:21

Why are you asking me? 📖

Ask those who heard my message; surely they know what I said.”

## John 18:22

An officer standing nearby slapped Jesus' face. 📖

He asked, “Is this how you answer the high priest?”

## John 18:23

Jesus replied, “If I said something wrong, give evidence of what was wrong. 📖

But if I spoke correctly, why did you hit me?”

## John 18:24

Annas sent Jesus, still tied up, to Caiaphas the high priest. 📖

## John 18:25

Simon Peter was still standing and warming himself. 📖

They asked, “Aren't you also one of his disciples?”

Peter denied it, saying, “I am not.”

## John 18:26

One of the high priest's servants was related to the man whose ear Peter cut off. 📖

He asked Peter, “Didn't I see you with him in the garden?”

## John 18:27

Peter denied it again, and immediately a rooster crowed. 📖

## John 18:28

They led Jesus from Caiaphas to the Praetorium; it was early morning. 📖

The Jews stayed outside to avoid being defiled and unable to eat the Passover meal.

**Help:** The Praetorium was the Roman governor's headquarters.

Defiled here concerns religious rules about being clean; it does not mean being physically dirty.

## John 18:29

Pilate went out to them and asked, “What charge are you bringing against this man?” 📖

## John 18:30

They replied, “If he were not a criminal, we would not have handed him over to you.” 📖

## John 18:31

Pilate told them, “You take him and judge him by your own law.” 📖

The Jews replied, “We are not allowed to execute anyone.”

**Help:** Execute means to put someone to death as punishment.

## John 18:32

This fulfilled Jesus' words showing the kind of death he was going to die. 📖

## John 18:33

Pilate went back inside the Praetorium and called Jesus to him. 📖

He asked Jesus, “Are you the King of the Jews?”

## John 18:34

Jesus asked, “Is this your own question, or did others tell you about me?” 📖

## John 18:35

Pilate replied, “Am I a Jew? 📖

Your own people and chief priests handed you over to me.

What have you done?”

## John 18:36

Jesus answered, “My kingdom is not of this world. 📖

If it were, my servants would fight to prevent my arrest by the Jews.

But my kingdom is not of this realm.”

**Help:** A realm is a kingdom or area of rule.

## John 18:37

Pilate said, “Then you are a king!” 📖

Jesus answered, “You say that I am a king.

I was born and came into the world to testify to the truth.

Everyone who belongs to the truth listens to my voice.”

**Help:** Testify means to speak as a witness about what is true.

## John 18:38

Pilate asked, “What is truth?” 📖

He went out again to the Jews and said, “I find no basis for a charge against him.

## John 18:39

Your custom is for me to release one prisoner to you at Passover. 📖

Do you want me to release the King of the Jews?”

## John 18:40

They shouted, “Not this man, but Barabbas!” 📖

Barabbas was a rebel who took part in an uprising.
