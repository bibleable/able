# John 16 — Sorrow will turn to joy

**About this chapter**

Jesus prepares his disciples for trouble and tells them about the Spirit of truth.

## John 16:1

Jesus continued, “I have told you these things so that you will not fall away. 📖

**Help:** Falling away here means turning away from following Jesus.

## John 16:2

They will put you out of the synagogues. 📖

A time is coming when anyone who kills you will think he is serving God.

**Help:** Synagogues are places where Jewish people gather for worship and teaching.

## John 16:3

They will do these things because they have not known the Father or me. 📖

## John 16:4

I have told you about these things so you will remember my warning when their hour comes. 📖

I did not tell you from the beginning because I was with you.

## John 16:5

But now I am going to the one who sent me. 📖

Yet none of you asks me, ‘Where are you going?’

## John 16:6

Instead, your hearts are full of sorrow because I have told you these things. 📖

## John 16:7

I tell you the truth: my going away is for your good. 📖

Unless I go away, the Advocate will not come to you.

But if I go, I will send him to you.

**Help:** An advocate supports someone and speaks for them.

## John 16:8

When he comes, he will convict the world about sin, righteousness, and judgment. 📖

**Help:** Convict means to show that someone is wrong or guilty.

Sin is wrong against God; righteousness concerns what is right before God; judgment is a decision about guilt.

## John 16:9

He will convict them about sin because they do not believe in me. 📖

## John 16:10

He will convict them about righteousness because I am going to the Father. 📖

And you will no longer see me.

## John 16:11

He will convict them about judgment because the prince of this world has been condemned. 📖

**Help:** The prince here is the devil; condemned means judged guilty.

## John 16:12

I still have much to tell you, but you cannot yet bear to hear it. 📖

## John 16:13

When the Spirit of truth comes, he will guide you into all truth. 📖

He will not speak on his own.

He will speak what he hears and tell you what is to come.

## John 16:14

He will glorify me by taking what is mine and making it known to you. 📖

**Help:** Glorify means to show someone's glory and honor.

## John 16:15

Everything belonging to the Father is mine. 📖

That is why I said the Spirit will take what is mine and make it known to you.

## John 16:16

In a little while you will no longer see me. 📖

Then, after a little while, you will see me.”

## John 16:17

Some disciples asked each other about Jesus' words. 📖

“Why does he say, ‘In a little while you will not see me’?

And why does he say, ‘After a little while you will see me’?

Why does he say, ‘Because I am going to the Father’?”

## John 16:18

They kept asking, “Why does he say ‘a little while’? 📖

We do not understand what he means.”

## John 16:19

Jesus knew they wanted to question him. 📖

He asked, “Are you asking each other why I said, ‘In a little while you will not see me’?

And why I said, ‘After a little while you will see me’?

## John 16:20

Truly, truly, I tell you, you will weep and cry loudly while the world rejoices. 📖

You will grieve, but your grief will turn to joy.

## John 16:21

A woman has pain in childbirth because her time has come. 📖

But after giving birth, she forgets her suffering because of her joy that a child was born into the world.

## John 16:22

You also have sorrow now, but I will see you again. 📖

Your hearts will rejoice, and no one will take your joy away.

## John 16:23

On that day you will no longer ask me anything. 📖

Truly, truly, I tell you, the Father will give you whatever you ask him in my name.

## John 16:24

Until now, you have asked for nothing in my name. 📖

Ask and you will receive, so that your joy may be complete.

## John 16:25

I have spoken about these things using pictures in words. 📖

An hour is coming when I will stop speaking to you this way.

I will tell you plainly about the Father.

## John 16:26

On that day you will ask in my name. 📖

I am not saying that I will ask the Father for you.

## John 16:27

The Father himself loves you because you have loved me and believed I came from God. 📖

## John 16:28

I came from the Father and entered the world. 📖

Now I will leave the world and go to the Father.”

## John 16:29

His disciples said, “Now you are speaking plainly, without pictures in words. 📖

## John 16:30

Now we understand that you know everything. 📖

You have no need for anyone to question you.

Because of this, we believe you came from God.”

## John 16:31

Jesus replied, “Do you finally believe? 📖

## John 16:32

Look, an hour is coming and has already come when you will be scattered, each to his own home. 📖

You will leave me all alone.

Yet I am not alone because the Father is with me.

## John 16:33

I have told you these things so that you may have peace in me. 📖

In the world you will have trouble and suffering.

But take courage: I have overcome the world!”
