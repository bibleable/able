# Acts 16 — Macedonia and the Philippian jail

**About this chapter**

Timothy joins Paul, and the group travels to Macedonia.

Lydia and a jailer believe, while Paul and Silas endure imprisonment.

## Acts 16:1

Paul reached Derbe, then Lystra, where Timothy was a disciple with a believing Jewish mother and Greek father. 📖

## Acts 16:2

The believers in Lystra and Iconium spoke well of him. 📖

## Acts 16:3

Paul wanted Timothy with him and circumcised him because the local Jews all knew his father was Greek. 📖

**Help:** Circumcision removes the foreskin, a piece of skin on the penis, as a Jewish covenant sign.

The account states Paul's reason here without changing the previous chapter's council decision.

## Acts 16:4

Town by town, they delivered Jerusalem's apostles' and elders' decisions for people to obey. 📖

## Acts 16:5

The churches grew stronger in faith and increased daily. 📖

## Acts 16:6

The Holy Spirit stopped them speaking the word in Asia, so they travelled through Phrygia and Galatia. 📖

**Help:** Asia here was a Roman province.

## Acts 16:7

Near Mysia, they tried entering Bithynia, but Jesus' Spirit did not allow them. 📖

## Acts 16:8

They passed by Mysia and went to Troas. 📖

## Acts 16:9

At night, Paul saw a Macedonian man in a vision, pleading, “Come to Macedonia and help us.” 📖

## Acts 16:10

We immediately prepared to leave, concluding God called us to preach the good news there. 📖

**About the narrator:** The account begins using we here, including the writer among the travellers.

## Acts 16:11

We sailed straight from Troas to Samothrace, then reached Neapolis next day. 📖

## Acts 16:12

We went to Philippi, a Roman colony and leading city of that Macedonian district, and stayed several days. 📖

## Acts 16:13

On the Sabbath, we went outside the gate to the river, where people customarily prayed. 📖

We sat and spoke to the women gathered there.

## Acts 16:14

Lydia, a God-worshipping purple-cloth dealer from Thyatira, listened, and the Lord opened her heart to respond to Paul. 📖

## Acts 16:15

After baptism with her household, she urged, “If you consider me a believer in the Lord, stay at my house.” 📖

She persuaded us.

## Acts 16:16

Going to prayer one day, we met a slave girl with a spirit of divination. 📖

Her fortune-telling earned her owners much money.

**Help:** Divination means seeking hidden knowledge, such as the future, through spiritual powers.

## Acts 16:17

She followed Paul and the rest of us, shouting. 📖

“These men serve the Most High God and proclaim the way of salvation to you!”

## Acts 16:18

After many days, Paul became greatly annoyed and told the spirit, “In Jesus Christ's name, come out of her!” 📖

It left immediately.

## Acts 16:19

Seeing their profit gone, her owners seized Paul and Silas and dragged them before the marketplace authorities. 📖

## Acts 16:20

They told the magistrates, “These Jews are upsetting our city. 📖

## Acts 16:21

They promote customs we Romans cannot lawfully accept or practise.” 📖

**About these words:** The account reports the owners' accusation.

## Acts 16:22

The crowd joined the attack; the magistrates ordered Paul and Silas stripped and beaten with rods. 📖

## Acts 16:23

After many blows, they imprisoned them and ordered the jailer to guard them securely. 📖

## Acts 16:24

He placed them in the inner cell, fastening their feet in stocks. 📖

**Help:** Stocks hold prisoners' feet in place.

## Acts 16:25

About midnight, they prayed and sang hymns to God while the prisoners listened. 📖

## Acts 16:26

A strong earthquake suddenly shook the prison's foundations; every door opened, and everyone's chains loosened. 📖

## Acts 16:27

The jailer awoke, saw open doors, and drew his sword to kill himself, thinking the prisoners escaped. 📖

## Acts 16:28

Paul shouted, “Do not harm yourself; we are all here!” 📖

## Acts 16:29

The jailer called for lights, rushed in, and fell trembling before Paul and Silas. 📖

## Acts 16:30

He brought them out, asking, “Sirs, what must I do to be saved?” 📖

## Acts 16:31

They replied, “Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved, you and your household.” 📖

## Acts 16:32

They spoke the Lord's word to him and everyone in his house. 📖

## Acts 16:33

That same night, he washed their wounds, and he and his whole household were baptized without delay. 📖

## Acts 16:34

He took them home and served food; his whole household rejoiced at having believed in God. 📖

## Acts 16:35

At dawn, the magistrates sent officers with orders to release them. 📖

## Acts 16:36

The jailer told Paul, “The magistrates ordered your release; go in peace.” 📖

## Acts 16:37

Paul replied, “They publicly beat us Roman citizens without trial and jailed us. 📖

Will they send us away secretly now?

No!

They must come and escort us out themselves!”

## Acts 16:38

The officers reported this, and the magistrates feared when they heard the men were Roman citizens. 📖

## Acts 16:39

They came to appease them, led them out, and asked them to leave the city. 📖

## Acts 16:40

Paul and Silas visited Lydia's house, encouraged the believers, and left the city. 📖
