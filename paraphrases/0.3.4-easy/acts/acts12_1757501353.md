# Acts 12 — Peter freed; Herod dies

**About this chapter**

Herod kills James and imprisons Peter, but an angel frees Peter.

Herod later dies, while God's word keeps spreading.

## Acts 12:1

King Herod began harming some church members. 📖

## Acts 12:2

He had James, John's brother, killed with a sword. 📖

## Acts 12:3

Seeing this pleased the Jews, he also arrested Peter during the Feast of Unleavened Bread. 📖

**Help:** This was the Jewish feast associated with Passover, using bread without yeast.

## Acts 12:4

He imprisoned Peter under four squads of four soldiers each, intending to present him to the people after Passover. 📖

## Acts 12:5

While Peter remained imprisoned, the church prayed earnestly to God for him. 📖

## Acts 12:6

The night before his trial, Peter slept between two soldiers, bound with two chains, with guards at the prison entrance. 📖

## Acts 12:7

Suddenly the Lord's angel appeared, and light filled the cell. 📖

He tapped Peter's side, woke him, and said, “Get up quickly.”

The chains fell from his wrists.

## Acts 12:8

The angel said, “Dress and put on your sandals.” 📖

Peter obeyed, and the angel added, “Wrap your cloak round you and follow me.”

## Acts 12:9

Peter followed outside, thinking he saw a vision rather than a real event. 📖

## Acts 12:10

They passed the first and second guards and reached the iron city gate, which opened by itself. 📖

They went out and walked one block, and suddenly the angel left.

## Acts 12:11

Peter realised, “Now I know the Lord sent his angel to rescue me from Herod and everything the Jews expected.” 📖

## Acts 12:12

He went to the house of Mary, mother of John called Mark, where many gathered to pray. 📖

## Acts 12:13

He knocked at the outer gate, and a servant girl called Rhoda came to answer. 📖

## Acts 12:14

Recognising Peter's voice, she was so joyful she forgot to open, but ran inside announcing, “Peter is at the gate!” 📖

## Acts 12:15

They said, “You are out of your mind!” 📖

When she insisted, they said, “It must be his angel.”

**About these words:** The account reports their responses without confirming their explanation about Peter's angel.

## Acts 12:16

Peter kept knocking; they opened, saw him, and were amazed. 📖

## Acts 12:17

He signalled for quiet and explained how the Lord brought him out. 📖

He said, “Tell James and the brothers,” then left for another place.

**About this name:** This James is distinct from the James killed in verse 2.

## Acts 12:18

At dawn, the soldiers were greatly disturbed about what happened to Peter. 📖

## Acts 12:19

Herod searched without success, questioned the guards, and ordered their execution, then left Judea for a stay in Caesarea. 📖

## Acts 12:20

Herod angrily disputed with Tyre and Sidon's people, who came together before him. 📖

They gained support from Blastus, the king's chamberlain.

They sought peace because their region depended on the king's country for food.

**Help:** A chamberlain was an official managing the king's household.

## Acts 12:21

On the appointed day, Herod wore royal robes, sat on his throne, and addressed the people. 📖

## Acts 12:22

They shouted, “A god's voice, not a man's!” 📖

## Acts 12:23

Immediately the Lord's angel struck him because he did not give God glory; worms ate him, and he died. 📖

## Acts 12:24

But God's word spread and multiplied. 📖

## Acts 12:25

After finishing their mission to Jerusalem, Barnabas and Saul returned, bringing John called Mark. 📖
