# Acts 8 — Samaria and the Ethiopian official

**About this chapter**

Persecution scatters believers, who carry the message with them.

Philip preaches in Samaria and meets an Ethiopian official reading Isaiah.

## Acts 8:1

Saul approved of Stephen's death. 📖

That day, severe persecution struck Jerusalem's church, scattering everyone except the apostles throughout Judea and Samaria.

**Help:** Persecution means cruel treatment because of faith.

## Acts 8:2

God-fearing men buried Stephen and mourned deeply for him. 📖

## Acts 8:3

Saul began destroying the church, entering houses and dragging men and women into prison. 📖

## Acts 8:4

The scattered believers preached the word wherever they went. 📖

## Acts 8:5

Philip went down to a Samaritan city and proclaimed the Christ. 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's chosen, anointed king.

## Acts 8:6

The crowds together paid close attention to Philip's message and the signs they saw him perform. 📖

## Acts 8:7

Unclean spirits left many possessed people with loud shrieks, and many paralysed people and people unable to walk were healed. 📖

**Help:** Unclean spirits means evil spirits, rather than a claim about dirt or disability.

Paralysed means unable to move parts of the body.

## Acts 8:8

Great joy filled that city. 📖

## Acts 8:9

Previously, Simon practised sorcery there, amazing Samaria's people and claiming greatness. 📖

**Help:** Sorcery means using magic or spiritual powers in ways opposed to God.

## Acts 8:10

Everyone, least and greatest, listened, saying, “This man is the divine power called the Great Power.” 📖

**About these words:** This is the people's claim about Simon.

## Acts 8:11

They listened closely because his sorcery had amazed them for a long time. 📖

## Acts 8:12

But they believed Philip's good news about God's kingdom and Jesus Christ's name, and men and women were baptized. 📖

## Acts 8:13

Simon also believed, was baptized, and followed Philip closely, amazed at the great signs and miracles. 📖

## Acts 8:14

Hearing Samaria had received God's word, Jerusalem's apostles sent Peter and John. 📖

## Acts 8:15

They arrived and prayed for the believers to receive the Holy Spirit. 📖

## Acts 8:16

The Spirit had not yet come upon any of them; they had only been baptized into the Lord Jesus' name. 📖

## Acts 8:17

Peter and John laid hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit. 📖

## Acts 8:18

Seeing the Spirit given through the apostles laying hands, Simon offered money. 📖

## Acts 8:19

He said, “Give me this power, so everyone I lay hands on may receive the Holy Spirit.” 📖

## Acts 8:20

Peter replied, “May your silver perish with you, because you thought money could buy God's gift! 📖

## Acts 8:21

You have no share in our service, because your heart is not right before God. 📖

## Acts 8:22

Repent of this wickedness and pray to the Lord; perhaps he will forgive your heart's intention. 📖

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

## Acts 8:23

I see you poisoned with bitterness and held captive by wrongdoing.” 📖

## Acts 8:24

Simon answered, “Pray to the Lord for me, so none of this happens to me.” 📖

## Acts 8:25

Peter and John testified, spoke the Lord's word, and returned to Jerusalem, preaching in many Samaritan villages. 📖

## Acts 8:26

The Lord's angel told Philip, “Go south to the desert road from Jerusalem to Gaza.” 📖

## Acts 8:27

He set out and met an Ethiopian eunuch, an official managing the entire treasury of Candace, the Ethiopians' queen. 📖

The man had visited Jerusalem to worship.

**Help:** Eunuch describes a male royal servant and can refer to a man whose testicles were removed.

The account gives no moral judgment about his bodily condition.

## Acts 8:28

Returning home, he sat in his chariot reading the prophet Isaiah. 📖

**Help:** A chariot is a wheeled vehicle pulled by horses.

## Acts 8:29

The Spirit told Philip, “Go to that chariot and stay beside it.” 📖

## Acts 8:30

Philip ran up, heard him reading Isaiah, and asked, “Do you understand what you read?” 📖

## Acts 8:31

He replied, “How can I unless someone guides me?” 📖

He invited Philip to climb up and sit beside him.

## Acts 8:32

He was reading, “Like a sheep, he was led to slaughter. 📖

Like a lamb silent before its shearer, he did not open his mouth.

**Help:** A shearer cuts wool from sheep.

## Acts 8:33

In humiliation, he was denied justice. 📖

Who can recount his descendants?

His life was taken from earth.”

## Acts 8:34

The eunuch asked, “Is the prophet speaking about himself or someone else?” 📖

## Acts 8:35

Starting with that Scripture, Philip told him the good news about Jesus. 📖

## Acts 8:36

They reached water, and the eunuch said, “Look, water! 📖

What prevents me from being baptized?”

## Acts 8:37

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Acts 8:38

He ordered the chariot stopped, and both went into the water, where Philip baptized him. 📖

## Acts 8:39

When they left the water, the Lord's Spirit carried Philip away, and the eunuch saw him no more. 📖

He continued his journey rejoicing.

## Acts 8:40

Philip appeared at Azotus and preached through all the towns until reaching Caesarea. 📖
