# Acts 24 — Paul before Felix

**About this chapter**

Paul answers charges before Felix, who delays judgment and keeps him imprisoned.

## Acts 24:1

Five days later, high priest Ananias, elders, and lawyer Tertullus presented their case against Paul to the governor. 📖

## Acts 24:2

Tertullus began, “You brought lasting peace and national improvements through your foresight. 📖

## Acts 24:3

Most excellent Felix, everywhere and in every way we gratefully acknowledge this. 📖

## Acts 24:4

To avoid delay, please kindly hear us briefly. 📖

## Acts 24:5

This man is a plague, stirring disputes among Jews worldwide and leading the Nazarenes' sect. 📖

**About these words:** These are the prosecutor's accusations; Nazarenes names Jesus' followers here.

## Acts 24:6

He even tried to desecrate the temple, so we seized him. 📖

## Acts 24:7

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Acts 24:8

Examine him yourself to discover the truth of our charges.” 📖

## Acts 24:9

The Jews agreed, declaring the charges true. 📖

## Acts 24:10

Signalled to speak, Paul said, “You have judged this nation many years, so I gladly defend myself. 📖

## Acts 24:11

You can confirm I went to Jerusalem to worship no more than twelve days ago. 📖

## Acts 24:12

They found me neither debating in the temple nor stirring crowds in synagogues or the city. 📖

## Acts 24:13

They cannot prove any accusation. 📖

## Acts 24:14

I admit I worship our ancestors' God according to the Way, which they call a sect. 📖

I believe everything in the Law and Prophets.

## Acts 24:15

Like them, I hope in God for the resurrection of righteous and wicked people alike. 📖

## Acts 24:16

So I always strive for a clear conscience before God and people. 📖

## Acts 24:17

After several years, I returned with gifts for the poor among my people and offerings. 📖

## Acts 24:18

They found me ceremonially clean in the temple, with no crowd or disturbance. 📖

Some Jews from Asia were there.

## Acts 24:19

They should appear before you and accuse me, if they have anything against me. 📖

## Acts 24:20

Otherwise, these men should say what crime they found when I faced the Sanhedrin. 📖

## Acts 24:21

Perhaps it was only my cry, ‘Today I stand trial before you for the resurrection of the dead.’” 📖

## Acts 24:22

Felix knew the Way well and postponed, saying, “I will decide when commander Lysias comes.” 📖

## Acts 24:23

He ordered Paul guarded but allowed some freedom and friends' help with his needs. 📖

## Acts 24:24

Later, Felix came with his Jewish wife Drusilla and heard Paul speak of faith in Christ Jesus. 📖

## Acts 24:25

Paul discussed righteousness, self-control, and coming judgment; Felix feared, saying, “Go now; I will call when convenient.” 📖

## Acts 24:26

Hoping for a bribe, Felix often sent for Paul and talked. 📖

## Acts 24:27

Two years later, Porcius Festus replaced Felix, who left Paul imprisoned to please the Jews. 📖
