# Acts 9 — Saul meets Jesus; Peter visits believers

**About this chapter**

Jesus confronts Saul, who becomes a witness for him.

Peter visits believers and ministers to Aeneas and Tabitha.

## Acts 9:1

Saul still threatened the Lord's disciples with murder and went to the high priest. 📖

## Acts 9:2

He requested letters to Damascus's synagogues, authorising him to bring men or women of the Way as prisoners to Jerusalem. 📖

**Help:** The Way was a name for Jesus' followers.

A synagogue is a Jewish gathering and place of worship.

## Acts 9:3

Near Damascus, a light from heaven suddenly flashed round Saul. 📖

## Acts 9:4

He fell and heard, “Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?” 📖

## Acts 9:5

Saul asked, “Who are you, Lord?” 📖

He replied, “I am Jesus, whom you persecute.

## Acts 9:6

Get up and enter the city; you will be told what to do.” 📖

## Acts 9:7

Saul's companions stood speechless, hearing the voice but seeing no one. 📖

## Acts 9:8

Saul stood, but could see nothing when he opened his eyes, so they led him by hand into Damascus. 📖

## Acts 9:9

For three days, he could not see and ate and drank nothing. 📖

## Acts 9:10

The Lord called to Ananias, a Damascus disciple, in a vision. 📖

“Ananias!”

He answered, “Here I am, Lord.”

## Acts 9:11

The Lord said, “Get up and go to Judas's house on Straight Street. 📖

Ask for Saul from Tarsus; he is praying.

## Acts 9:12

He saw in a vision a man called Ananias laying hands on him to restore his sight.” 📖

## Acts 9:13

Ananias answered, “Lord, many told me how much harm this man did to your holy people in Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 9:14

The chief priests authorised him to arrest everyone here calling on your name.” 📖

## Acts 9:15

The Lord said, “Go! 📖

He is my chosen instrument to carry my name to Gentiles, their kings, and Israel's people.

**Help:** Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 9:16

I will show him how much he must suffer for my name.” 📖

## Acts 9:17

Ananias entered the house and laid hands on Saul. 📖

“Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus, who appeared on your journey, sent me.

You are to see again and be filled with the Holy Spirit.”

## Acts 9:18

Immediately, something like scales fell from Saul's eyes, and his sight returned. 📖

He got up and was baptized.

## Acts 9:19

Food restored his strength, and he spent several days with Damascus's disciples. 📖

## Acts 9:20

He immediately preached Jesus in the synagogues, saying, “He is God's Son.” 📖

## Acts 9:21

Everyone was amazed: “Is this not the man who devastated Jerusalem's people calling on this name? 📖

Did he not come here to take them prisoner to the chief priests?”

## Acts 9:22

Saul grew stronger and confounded Damascus's Jews by proving Jesus is the Christ. 📖

**Help:** Confounded means left unable to answer his argument.

## Acts 9:23

After many days, the Jews plotted to kill him. 📖

## Acts 9:24

Saul learned their plan; they watched the gates day and night to kill him. 📖

## Acts 9:25

One night, his disciples lowered him in a basket through a window in the wall. 📖

## Acts 9:26

In Jerusalem, he tried joining the disciples, but all feared him, not believing he was a disciple. 📖

## Acts 9:27

Barnabas took him to the apostles, describing how Saul saw the Lord on the Damascus road. 📖

The Lord had spoken to him.

He also told how Saul spoke boldly in Jesus' name in Damascus.

## Acts 9:28

Saul stayed with them, moving freely in Jerusalem and speaking boldly in the Lord's name. 📖

## Acts 9:29

He debated the Greek-speaking Jews, but they tried to kill him. 📖

## Acts 9:30

The brothers learned this, took him to Caesarea, and sent him to Tarsus. 📖

## Acts 9:31

The church throughout Judea, Galilee, and Samaria enjoyed peace, growing stronger and more numerous. 📖

It lived in fear of the Lord and the Holy Spirit's encouragement.

## Acts 9:32

Travelling through the region, Peter visited God's holy people in Lydda. 📖

## Acts 9:33

He found Aeneas, who had been paralysed and confined to bed for eight years. 📖

## Acts 9:34

Peter said, “Aeneas, Jesus Christ heals you; get up and put away your mat.” 📖

He immediately stood up.

## Acts 9:35

Everyone living in Lydda and Sharon saw him and turned to the Lord. 📖

## Acts 9:36

Joppa had a disciple named Tabitha, translated Dorcas, always doing kindness and giving to others. 📖

## Acts 9:37

She became sick and died; they washed her body and put it in an upstairs room. 📖

## Acts 9:38

Since Lydda was nearby, disciples hearing Peter was there sent two men, urging, “Come without delay.” 📖

## Acts 9:39

Peter went with them, and they brought him upstairs. 📖

All the widows wept and showed him the tunics and other clothes Dorcas had made while alive.

**Help:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died; a tunic is a garment worn on the body.

## Acts 9:40

Peter sent everyone out, knelt, and prayed. 📖

Turning to her body, he said, “Tabitha, get up!”

She opened her eyes, saw Peter, and sat up.

## Acts 9:41

He took her hand and helped her stand, then called the believers and widows to present her alive. 📖

## Acts 9:42

All Joppa heard about this, and many believed in the Lord. 📖

## Acts 9:43

Peter stayed several days in Joppa with Simon, a tanner. 📖

**Help:** A tanner turns animal skins into leather.
