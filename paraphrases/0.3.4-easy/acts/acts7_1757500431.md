# Acts 7 — Stephen's speech and death

**About this chapter**

Stephen answers the high priest by recounting Israel's history and accusing his hearers of resisting God.

The council kills him, and he prays for his attackers.

## Acts 7:1

The high priest asked Stephen, “Are these accusations true?” 📖

## Acts 7:2

Stephen said, “Brothers and fathers, listen! 📖

The glorious God appeared to our ancestor Abraham in Mesopotamia, before he lived in Haran.

## Acts 7:3

God said, ‘Leave your country and relatives, and go to the land I will show you.’ 📖

## Acts 7:4

Abraham left the Chaldeans' land and settled in Haran. 📖

After his father died, God brought him here, to the land where you now live.

## Acts 7:5

God gave him no inheritance here, not even a foot of ground. 📖

Yet he promised this land to Abraham and his descendants, although Abraham had no child yet.

## Acts 7:6

God said his descendants would live as foreigners in another land, enslaved and mistreated for four hundred years. 📖

## Acts 7:7

God said, ‘I will punish the nation enslaving them; afterwards they will leave and worship me here.’ 📖

## Acts 7:8

God gave Abraham the covenant of circumcision. 📖

Abraham fathered Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day.

Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob fathered the twelve patriarchs.

**Help:** A covenant is a binding agreement or relationship established by God.

Circumcision removes the foreskin, a piece of skin on the penis, as a covenant sign.

Patriarchs are founding fathers of Israel's tribes.

## Acts 7:9

Jealous of Joseph, the patriarchs sold him as a slave into Egypt, but God was with him. 📖

## Acts 7:10

God rescued him from every trouble and gave him favour and wisdom before Pharaoh, Egypt's king. 📖

Pharaoh made Joseph ruler of Egypt and his whole household.

## Acts 7:11

Famine and great suffering came over Egypt and Canaan, and our ancestors could find no food. 📖

**Help:** Famine is a severe lack of food.

## Acts 7:12

Hearing Egypt had grain, Jacob sent our ancestors there for their first visit. 📖

## Acts 7:13

On their second visit, Joseph told his brothers who he was, and Pharaoh learned about his family. 📖

## Acts 7:14

Joseph sent for his father Jacob and all his relatives, seventy-five people altogether. 📖

## Acts 7:15

Jacob went to Egypt, where he and our ancestors died. 📖

## Acts 7:16

Their bones were taken to Shechem and placed in the tomb Abraham bought with silver from Hamor's sons there. 📖

## Acts 7:17

As the time for God's promise to Abraham approached, our people in Egypt greatly increased. 📖

## Acts 7:18

Then a different king, who knew nothing of Joseph, ruled Egypt. 📖

## Acts 7:19

He exploited our people and oppressed our ancestors, forcing them to abandon their babies so they would die. 📖

## Acts 7:20

Moses was born then, beautiful in God's sight, and cared for in his father's house for three months. 📖

## Acts 7:21

When he was put outside, Pharaoh's daughter took him and raised him as her son. 📖

## Acts 7:22

Moses learned all Egyptian wisdom and was powerful in speech and action. 📖

## Acts 7:23

At forty, he decided to visit his brothers, the Israelites. 📖

## Acts 7:24

Seeing an Israelite mistreated, he defended and avenged him by killing the Egyptian oppressor. 📖

## Acts 7:25

He thought his brothers would understand God was rescuing them through him, but they did not. 📖

## Acts 7:26

Next day, he found two Israelites fighting and tried to bring peace. 📖

‘Men, you are brothers; why mistreat each other?’

## Acts 7:27

The man mistreating his neighbour pushed Moses aside, saying, ‘Who made you our ruler and judge? 📖

## Acts 7:28

Do you want to kill me as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’ 📖

## Acts 7:29

Hearing this, Moses fled to Midian, lived there as a foreigner, and had two sons. 📖

## Acts 7:30

Forty years later, an angel appeared in a burning bush's flames in the desert near Mount Sinai. 📖

## Acts 7:31

Moses marvelled and came closer to look, and the Lord's voice spoke to him. 📖

## Acts 7:32

‘I am your ancestors' God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’ 📖

Moses trembled with fear and dared not look.

## Acts 7:33

The Lord said, ‘Remove your sandals, because you stand on holy ground. 📖

## Acts 7:34

I have seen my people's oppression in Egypt and heard their groaning. 📖

I have come down to rescue them.

Come now; I will send you back to Egypt.’

## Acts 7:35

They rejected Moses, asking, ‘Who made you ruler and judge?’ 📖

Yet God sent this very man as ruler and redeemer through the angel appearing in the bush.

**Help:** A redeemer sets people free.

## Acts 7:36

He led them out, performing wonders and signs in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and for forty wilderness years. 📖

## Acts 7:37

This Moses told Israel, ‘God will raise up a prophet like me from among your brothers.’ 📖

## Acts 7:38

Moses was with the wilderness assembly, our ancestors, and the angel speaking to him on Mount Sinai. 📖

He received living words to pass to us.

## Acts 7:39

Our ancestors refused to obey him, rejected him, and turned back to Egypt in their hearts. 📖

## Acts 7:40

They told Aaron, ‘Make gods to go before us! 📖

We do not know what happened to Moses, who brought us out of Egypt.’

## Acts 7:41

They made a calf, sacrificed to the idol, and rejoiced in what their hands made. 📖

**Help:** An idol is something worshipped as a god.

## Acts 7:42

God turned away and handed them over to worship heaven's host. 📖

The prophets' book says, ‘Israel, did you bring me sacrifices and offerings during forty years in the wilderness?

**Help:** Heaven's host here refers to heavenly bodies worshipped as gods.

## Acts 7:43

You carried Molech's tabernacle and your god Rephan's star, idols you made to worship. 📖

So I will send you into exile beyond Babylon.’

**Help:** A tabernacle is a tent or dwelling place; exile means being sent away from one's homeland.

## Acts 7:44

Our ancestors had the tabernacle of the Testimony in the wilderness. 📖

It was made exactly as God directed Moses, following the pattern he saw.

## Acts 7:45

They inherited it and brought it with Joshua when they took the land from nations God drove out. 📖

It remained until David's time.

## Acts 7:46

David found favour with God and asked to provide a dwelling for Jacob's God. 📖

## Acts 7:47

But Solomon built the house for him. 📖

## Acts 7:48

The Most High does not live in houses built by human hands. 📖

The prophet says,

## Acts 7:49

‘Heaven is my throne, and earth my footstool. 📖

What house will you build me, says the Lord, or where will I rest?

## Acts 7:50

Did my hand not make all these things?’ 📖

## Acts 7:51

You stubborn people with uncircumcised hearts and ears! 📖

You always resist the Holy Spirit, just like your ancestors.

**Help:** Uncircumcised hearts and ears pictures refusing to respond to God.

Stephen directs this accusation to his hearers; it should not become a claim about every Jewish person.

## Acts 7:52

Which prophet did your ancestors not persecute? 📖

They killed those foretelling the Righteous One, whom you now betrayed and murdered.

## Acts 7:53

You received the law given through angels but did not keep it!” 📖

## Acts 7:54

The Sanhedrin's members became furious and ground their teeth at him. 📖

## Acts 7:55

Full of the Holy Spirit, Stephen looked into heaven and saw God's glory and Jesus standing at God's right hand. 📖

## Acts 7:56

He said, “Look, heaven is open, and the Son of Man stands at God's right hand!” 📖

## Acts 7:57

They covered their ears, shouted loudly, and rushed together at him. 📖

## Acts 7:58

They dragged him outside the city and stoned him. 📖

The witnesses laid their clothes at a young man Saul's feet.

**Help:** Stoning means attacking with thrown stones to kill.

## Acts 7:59

As they stoned him, Stephen prayed, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.” 📖

## Acts 7:60

He knelt and cried loudly, “Lord, do not hold this sin against them.” 📖

After saying this, he fell asleep.

**Help:** Fell asleep here means died.
