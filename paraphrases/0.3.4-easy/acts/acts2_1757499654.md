# Acts 2 — Pentecost and Peter's message

**About this chapter**

The Holy Spirit fills Jesus' followers.

Peter speaks to the crowd, and many believe and join the believers' shared life.

## Acts 2:1

On Pentecost day, they were all together in one place. 📖

**Help:** Pentecost was a Jewish feast.

## Acts 2:2

Suddenly, a sound like a mighty rushing wind came from heaven and filled the whole house where they sat. 📖

## Acts 2:3

They saw tongues like flames of fire, separating and resting on each person. 📖

## Acts 2:4

They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke in other tongues as the Spirit enabled them. 📖

**Help:** Tongues here refers to languages the crowd recognised in the following verses.

## Acts 2:5

God-fearing Jews from every nation under heaven were living in Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 2:6

When the sound came, a confused crowd gathered because everyone heard the speakers in their own language. 📖

## Acts 2:7

Amazed and astonished, they asked, “Are all these speakers not Galileans? 📖

## Acts 2:8

How do we each hear them in our own native language? 📖

## Acts 2:9

We are Parthians, Medes, Elamites, and residents of Mesopotamia, Judea, Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia. 📖

## Acts 2:10

Others are from Phrygia, Pamphylia, Egypt, Libya's parts near Cyrene, or visiting from Rome. 📖

## Acts 2:11

We include Jews and converts to Judaism, Cretans and Arabs. 📖

We hear God's wonders declared in our own languages!”

**Help:** Converts to Judaism are people who joined the Jewish faith.

## Acts 2:12

Amazed and puzzled, they asked each other, “What does this mean?” 📖

## Acts 2:13

Others mocked them, saying, “They are drunk on new wine!” 📖

## Acts 2:14

Peter stood with the Eleven and spoke loudly to the crowd. 📖

“People of Judea and everyone living in Jerusalem, understand this and listen carefully.

## Acts 2:15

These men are not drunk as you suppose; it is only the day's third hour! 📖

**Help:** The third hour was about nine in the morning.

## Acts 2:16

This is what the prophet Joel said: 📖

## Acts 2:17

‘In the last days, God says, I will pour out my Spirit on all people. 📖

Your sons and daughters will prophesy, your young men will see visions, and your old men will dream dreams.

**Help:** Prophesy means speak a message from God.

## Acts 2:18

I will pour out my Spirit even on my male and female servants in those days, and they will prophesy. 📖

## Acts 2:19

I will show wonders in heaven above and signs on earth below: blood, fire, and clouds of smoke. 📖

## Acts 2:20

The sun will become dark and the moon blood before the Lord's great and glorious day comes. 📖

## Acts 2:21

Everyone calling on the Lord's name will be saved.’ 📖

## Acts 2:22

People of Israel, listen: God confirmed Jesus of Nazareth to you through miracles, wonders, and signs. 📖

God did these through him among you, as you know.

## Acts 2:23

He was handed over according to God's fixed plan and knowledge beforehand. 📖

You used lawless people's hands to nail him to a cross and kill him.

## Acts 2:24

God raised him from death, freeing him from death's agony, because death could not hold him. 📖

## Acts 2:25

David says about him, ‘I always saw the Lord before me. 📖

Because he is at my right hand, I will not be shaken.

## Acts 2:26

My heart is glad and my tongue rejoices; my body also lives in hope. 📖

## Acts 2:27

This is because you will not abandon my soul to Hades or let your Holy One decay. 📖

**Help:** Hades refers to the realm of the dead.

## Acts 2:28

You showed me life's paths and will fill me with joy in your presence.’ 📖

## Acts 2:29

Brothers, I can confidently say our ancestor David died and was buried; his tomb remains with us today. 📖

## Acts 2:30

He was a prophet who knew God had sworn to place one of his descendants on his throne. 📖

## Acts 2:31

Seeing this ahead, David spoke about the Christ's resurrection. 📖

He was not abandoned to Hades, and his body did not decay.

**Help:** Christ means God's chosen, anointed king; resurrection means being raised from death.

## Acts 2:32

God raised this Jesus to life, and we are all witnesses. 📖

## Acts 2:33

Raised to God's right hand, Jesus received the promised Holy Spirit from the Father. 📖

He poured out what you now see and hear.

## Acts 2:34

David did not rise into heaven. 📖

He said, ‘The Lord told my Lord, “Sit at my right hand.

## Acts 2:35

Stay until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.”’ 📖

## Acts 2:36

All Israel must know for certain: God made this Jesus, whom you crucified, both Lord and Christ!” 📖

## Acts 2:37

Deeply pierced in their hearts, the people asked Peter and the other apostles, “Brothers, what should we do?” 📖

## Acts 2:38

Peter replied, “Each of you must repent and be baptized in Jesus Christ's name for your sins' forgiveness. 📖

You will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

Baptism uses water as a sign of belonging to Christ.

## Acts 2:39

The promise is for you, your children, and everyone far away, all whom our Lord God calls to himself.” 📖

## Acts 2:40

He testified with many more words, urging them, “Be saved from this corrupt generation.” 📖

## Acts 2:41

Those accepting his message were baptized, and about three thousand joined the believers that day. 📖

## Acts 2:42

They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching, shared fellowship, breaking bread, and prayer. 📖

**Help:** Fellowship means shared life together; breaking bread describes sharing a meal.

## Acts 2:43

Awe came over everyone, and many wonders and signs happened through the apostles. 📖

## Acts 2:44

All believers were together and shared everything. 📖

## Acts 2:45

They sold possessions and goods and shared with anyone in need. 📖

## Acts 2:46

United, they met daily in the temple courts and broke bread from house to house. 📖

They shared meals gladly and with sincere hearts,

## Acts 2:47

praising God and enjoying everyone's favour. 📖

The Lord added those being saved to their number daily.
