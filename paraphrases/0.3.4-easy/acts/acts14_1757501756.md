# Acts 14 — Preaching, opposition, and returning

**About this chapter**

Paul and Barnabas preach through opposition, reject worship directed at them, and strengthen the churches.

## Acts 14:1

In Iconium, they entered the Jewish synagogue as usual, speaking so effectively that many Jews and Greeks believed. 📖

## Acts 14:2

Unbelieving Jews stirred the Gentiles against the believers and poisoned their minds. 📖

## Acts 14:3

Paul and Barnabas stayed a long time, speaking boldly for the Lord. 📖

He confirmed the message of his kindness by enabling them to perform signs and wonders.

## Acts 14:4

The city was divided, some supporting the Jews and others the apostles. 📖

## Acts 14:5

Gentiles, Jews, and their rulers planned to mistreat and stone them. 📖

## Acts 14:6

They discovered this and fled to Lystra and Derbe, cities of Lycaonia, and the surrounding region. 📖

## Acts 14:7

There they continued preaching the good news. 📖

## Acts 14:8

In Lystra sat a man with disabled feet, unable to walk since birth, who had never walked. 📖

## Acts 14:9

He listened to Paul, who looked closely and saw his faith to be healed. 📖

## Acts 14:10

Paul called loudly, “Stand on your feet!” 📖

The man jumped up and walked.

## Acts 14:11

Seeing this, crowds shouted in Lycaonian, “The gods came down to us in human form!” 📖

## Acts 14:12

They called Barnabas Zeus and Paul Hermes, because Paul was the main speaker. 📖

**Help:** Zeus and Hermes were gods these people worshipped.

## Acts 14:13

Zeus's priest, whose temple stood outside the city, brought bulls and wreaths to the gates for sacrifice with the crowds. 📖

**Help:** Wreaths are rings of leaves or flowers.

## Acts 14:14

Hearing this, the apostles Barnabas and Paul tore their clothes and rushed into the crowd, shouting. 📖

**Help:** Tearing clothes expressed deep distress.

## Acts 14:15

“Why do this? 📖

We are humans just like you.

Our good news tells you to turn from worthless things to the living God, maker of heaven, earth, and sea.

He made everything in them.

## Acts 14:16

In past generations, he let all nations follow their own ways. 📖

## Acts 14:17

Yet he showed his goodness through heavenly rain and fruitful seasons, filling your hearts with food and gladness.” 📖

## Acts 14:18

Even saying this, they could hardly stop the crowd sacrificing to them. 📖

## Acts 14:19

Jews from Antioch and Iconium won over the crowds, stoned Paul, and dragged him outside, thinking him dead. 📖

## Acts 14:20

The disciples gathered round him, and he got up and entered the city. 📖

Next day, he left with Barnabas for Derbe.

## Acts 14:21

They preached there and made many disciples, then returned to Lystra, Iconium, and Antioch. 📖

## Acts 14:22

They strengthened the disciples' souls and encouraged them to remain faithful. 📖

They said, “We must endure many hardships to enter God's kingdom.”

## Acts 14:23

They appointed elders in every church and, with prayer and fasting, entrusted them to the Lord they believed in. 📖

## Acts 14:24

They passed through Pisidia and came to Pamphylia. 📖

## Acts 14:25

After speaking the word in Perga, they went to Attalia. 📖

## Acts 14:26

They sailed to Antioch, where they had been entrusted to God's kindness for the work now finished. 📖

## Acts 14:27

They gathered the church and reported everything God did through them, opening faith's door to Gentiles. 📖

**Help:** Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

Opening faith's door pictures giving them an opportunity to believe.

## Acts 14:28

They stayed a long time with the disciples. 📖
