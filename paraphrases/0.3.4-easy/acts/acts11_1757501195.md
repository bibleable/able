# Acts 11 — Peter explains; the church grows in Antioch

**About this chapter**

Peter explains why he visited Gentiles.

Believers in Antioch receive teaching and send help to Judea.

## Acts 11:1

The apostles and believers throughout Judea soon heard that Gentiles also received God's word. 📖

**Help:** Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 11:2

When Peter went to Jerusalem, circumcised believers criticised him. 📖

## Acts 11:3

They said, “You visited uncircumcised men and ate with them.” 📖

**Help:** These terms distinguish Jewish believers, marked by circumcision, from the Gentiles Peter visited.

## Acts 11:4

Peter explained everything in order. 📖

## Acts 11:5

“While praying in Joppa, I fell into a trance and saw a vision. 📖

Something like a large sheet came from heaven by its four corners, right down to me.

## Acts 11:6

Looking closely, I saw four-footed animals, wild beasts, reptiles, and birds. 📖

## Acts 11:7

A voice said, ‘Get up, Peter; kill and eat.’ 📖

## Acts 11:8

I replied, ‘No, Lord; nothing impure or unclean has ever entered my mouth.’ 📖

## Acts 11:9

The heavenly voice replied a second time, ‘Do not call impure what God made clean.’ 📖

**Help:** Impure and unclean concern religious food rules here, rather than dirt or disability.

## Acts 11:10

This happened three times, and everything was pulled back into heaven. 📖

## Acts 11:11

Just then, three men sent from Caesarea stopped at the house where I stayed. 📖

## Acts 11:12

The Spirit told me to go without hesitation. 📖

These six brothers went too, and we entered the man's home.

## Acts 11:13

He told us an angel stood in his house, saying, ‘Send to Joppa for Simon called Peter. 📖

## Acts 11:14

He will tell you a message through which you and your whole household will be saved.’ 📖

## Acts 11:15

As I began speaking, the Holy Spirit came upon them, just as upon us at the beginning. 📖

## Acts 11:16

I remembered the Lord saying, ‘John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.’ 📖

## Acts 11:17

God gave them the same gift he gave us when we believed in the Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

If so, who was I to hinder God's work?”

## Acts 11:18

They stopped objecting and glorified God, saying, “God has given even Gentiles repentance leading to life.” 📖

**Help:** Repentance means turning from sin towards God.

## Acts 11:19

Those scattered by the persecution beginning with Stephen reached Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch, speaking only to Jews. 📖

## Acts 11:20

Some, from Cyprus and Cyrene, went to Antioch and spoke to Greeks too, announcing the Lord Jesus' good news. 📖

## Acts 11:21

The Lord's hand was with them, and many believed and turned to him. 📖

## Acts 11:22

Jerusalem's church heard the news and sent Barnabas to Antioch. 📖

## Acts 11:23

Seeing God's kindness there, he rejoiced and urged everyone to remain faithful to the Lord with their whole hearts. 📖

## Acts 11:24

Barnabas was good, full of the Holy Spirit and faith, and many people were brought to the Lord. 📖

## Acts 11:25

He went to Tarsus to find Saul. 📖

## Acts 11:26

Finding him, he brought him to Antioch, where they met with the church and taught many for a full year. 📖

The disciples were first called Christians in Antioch.

## Acts 11:27

Some prophets came from Jerusalem to Antioch then. 📖

## Acts 11:28

Agabus stood and predicted through the Spirit a great famine throughout the world, which happened under Claudius. 📖

**Help:** A famine is a severe lack of food.

## Acts 11:29

Each disciple decided to send help according to their ability to the believers in Judea. 📖

## Acts 11:30

They sent their gifts to the elders through Barnabas and Saul. 📖
