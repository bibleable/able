# Acts 22 — Paul's defence and citizenship

**About this chapter**

Paul recounts his encounter with Jesus and his commission.

The commander learns Paul is a Roman citizen.

## Acts 22:1

“Brothers and fathers, listen to my defence.” 📖

## Acts 22:2

Hearing Hebrew, they quieted further, and Paul spoke. 📖

## Acts 22:3

“I am Jewish, born in Tarsus of Cilicia but raised here. 📖

Gamaliel taught me strictly according to our ancestors' law, and I was zealous for God as you are today.

## Acts 22:4

I persecuted the Way to death, arresting men and women and imprisoning them. 📖

## Acts 22:5

The high priest and whole council can testify; they gave me letters to their brothers in Damascus. 📖

I travelled to seize these people and bring them to Jerusalem for punishment.

## Acts 22:6

Near Damascus about noon, bright heavenly light suddenly flashed around me. 📖

## Acts 22:7

I fell and heard, ‘Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?’ 📖

## Acts 22:8

I asked, ‘Who are you, Lord?’ 📖

He replied, ‘Jesus of Nazareth, whom you persecute.’

## Acts 22:9

My companions saw the light but did not understand the speaker's voice. 📖

## Acts 22:10

I asked what to do, and the Lord said, ‘Enter Damascus; there you will learn everything appointed for you.’ 📖

## Acts 22:11

The brilliant light blinded me, so my companions led me by hand into Damascus. 📖

## Acts 22:12

Ananias lived there, devoutly keeping the law and respected by all the local Jews. 📖

## Acts 22:13

He stood beside me, saying, ‘Brother Saul, receive your sight,’ and immediately I could see him. 📖

## Acts 22:14

He said, ‘Our ancestors' God appointed you to know his will, see the Righteous One, and hear his voice. 📖

## Acts 22:15

You will be his witness to everyone of what you saw and heard. 📖

## Acts 22:16

Why wait? 📖

Get up, be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling on his name.’

## Acts 22:17

Back in Jerusalem, I was praying at the temple and fell into a trance. 📖

## Acts 22:18

I saw the Lord saying, ‘Leave Jerusalem quickly; they will not accept your testimony about me.’ 📖

## Acts 22:19

I replied, ‘Lord, they know I imprisoned and beat your believers in synagogue after synagogue. 📖

## Acts 22:20

When your witness Stephen's blood was shed, I approved and guarded his killers' clothes.’ 📖

## Acts 22:21

He said, ‘Go; I will send you far away to Gentiles.’” 📖

## Acts 22:22

They listened until this, then shouted, “Remove him from earth; he is unfit to live!” 📖

## Acts 22:23

They shouted, threw off their cloaks, and tossed dust into the air. 📖

## Acts 22:24

The commander ordered Paul into the barracks for whipping and questioning, to learn why they shouted against him. 📖

## Acts 22:25

As they stretched him out for strapping, Paul asked the centurion, “Can you lawfully whip a Roman citizen without trial?” 📖

## Acts 22:26

The centurion reported, “What will you do? 📖

He is a Roman citizen.”

## Acts 22:27

The commander asked Paul whether he was a Roman citizen, and Paul answered yes. 📖

## Acts 22:28

The commander said he paid much for citizenship; Paul replied, “I was born a citizen.” 📖

## Acts 22:29

The interrogators immediately withdrew, and the commander feared because he had chained a Roman citizen. 📖

## Acts 22:30

Next day, he released Paul, summoning the chief priests and whole Sanhedrin to discover the Jews' real accusation. 📖

He brought Paul before them.
