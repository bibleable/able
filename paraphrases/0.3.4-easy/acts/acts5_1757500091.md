# Acts 5 — Deceit, prison, and faithful witness

**About this chapter**

Ananias and Sapphira lie about a sale and die.

The apostles continue their witness despite imprisonment and punishment.

## Acts 5:1

Ananias and his wife Sapphira also sold some property. 📖

## Acts 5:2

With his wife's full knowledge, he kept some money but laid part at the apostles' feet. 📖

## Acts 5:3

Peter said, “Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart to lie to the Holy Spirit? 📖

Why keep back some money from the land?

## Acts 5:4

Was the land not yours before selling, and the money yours to use afterwards? 📖

How could you plan this in your heart?

You lied to God, rather than humans!”

## Acts 5:5

Hearing this, Ananias fell down dead, and great fear came over everyone hearing what happened. 📖

## Acts 5:6

Young men came forward, wrapped his body, carried him out, and buried him. 📖

## Acts 5:7

About three hours later, his wife entered without knowing what happened. 📖

## Acts 5:8

Peter asked, “Is this what you and your husband received for the land?” 📖

She replied, “Yes, that is the price.”

## Acts 5:9

Peter said, “Why did you agree to test the Lord's Spirit? 📖

The men who buried your husband are at the door, and they will carry you out too.”

## Acts 5:10

Immediately she fell dead at his feet. 📖

The young men entered, found her dead, and carried her out to bury beside her husband.

## Acts 5:11

Great fear came over the whole church and everyone hearing about these events. 📖

## Acts 5:12

The apostles performed many signs and wonders among the people. 📖

The believers gathered together in unity at Solomon's Colonnade.

**Help:** A colonnade is a covered walkway supported by columns.

## Acts 5:13

Although people thought highly of them, no one else dared join them. 📖

## Acts 5:14

Yet more and more believers joined the Lord, large numbers of men and women. 📖

## Acts 5:15

People brought sick people into the streets on beds and mats. 📖

They hoped at least Peter's shadow might fall on some as he passed.

## Acts 5:16

Crowds from Jerusalem's surrounding towns brought sick people and those tormented by unclean spirits, and all were healed. 📖

**Help:** Unclean spirits means evil spirits, rather than a claim about physical dirt or disability.

## Acts 5:17

The high priest and his associates, all belonging to the Sadducees' party, became jealous and went out. 📖

## Acts 5:18

They arrested the apostles and put them in the public jail. 📖

## Acts 5:19

During the night, the Lord's angel opened the jail doors and brought them out. 📖

## Acts 5:20

He said, “Go, stand in the temple courts, and tell the people this new life's full message.” 📖

## Acts 5:21

At dawn, they obeyed, entered the temple courts, and taught. 📖

The high priest and his associates gathered the Sanhedrin, the full assembly of Israel's elders.

They sent to the jail for the apostles.

## Acts 5:22

The officers found no apostles at the jail and returned to report. 📖

## Acts 5:23

“The jail was securely locked, with guards at the doors, but when we opened it, no one was inside.” 📖

## Acts 5:24

Hearing this, the temple guard's captain and chief priests were puzzled about what was happening. 📖

## Acts 5:25

Someone announced, “The men you jailed are in the temple courts, teaching the people!” 📖

## Acts 5:26

The captain and officers brought them without force, fearing the people would stone them. 📖

**Help:** Stone means attack with thrown stones.

## Acts 5:27

They brought them before the Sanhedrin, and the high priest questioned them. 📖

## Acts 5:28

“We strictly ordered you not to teach in this name. 📖

You have filled Jerusalem with your teaching and want to make us responsible for this man's blood.”

## Acts 5:29

Peter and the apostles replied, “We must obey God rather than humans. 📖

## Acts 5:30

Our ancestors' God raised Jesus, whom you killed by hanging on a tree. 📖

**Help:** Tree here refers to the wooden cross.

## Acts 5:31

God raised him to his right hand as Prince and Saviour, granting Israel repentance and forgiveness of sins. 📖

## Acts 5:32

We witness these things, as does the Holy Spirit, whom God gives to those obeying him.” 📖

## Acts 5:33

The council became furious and decided to kill them. 📖

## Acts 5:34

Gamaliel, a Pharisee and law teacher honoured by everyone, stood in the Sanhedrin and ordered the men outside briefly. 📖

**Help:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

The Sanhedrin was the ruling council.

## Acts 5:35

He said, “People of Israel, consider carefully what you will do to these men. 📖

## Acts 5:36

Theudas once arose, claiming importance, and about four hundred men joined him. 📖

He was killed, his followers scattered, and it came to nothing.

## Acts 5:37

Afterwards, Judas the Galilean drew followers during the census; he too died, and his followers scattered. 📖

**Help:** A census counts the population.

## Acts 5:38

Now I advise you to leave these men alone and let them go. 📖

If their plan or work comes from humans, it will fail.

## Acts 5:39

If it comes from God, you cannot stop them and may find yourselves fighting God.” 📖

## Acts 5:40

They accepted Gamaliel's advice, called in the apostles, and had them whipped. 📖

They ordered them not to speak in Jesus' name and released them.

## Acts 5:41

The apostles left rejoicing that they were counted worthy to suffer disgrace for the Name. 📖

**Help:** The Name here means Jesus' name.

## Acts 5:42

Daily, in the temple courts and from house to house, they kept teaching and announcing Jesus as the Christ. 📖
