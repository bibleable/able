# Acts 21 — Journey to Jerusalem and arrest

**About this chapter**

Paul travels despite warnings, meets the elders, and is seized at the temple.

## Acts 21:1

After painfully parting, we sailed straight to Cos, then next day to Rhodes, and on to Patara. 📖

## Acts 21:2

We boarded a ship crossing to Phoenicia. 📖

## Acts 21:3

We sighted Cyprus, passed south, and sailed to Syria, landing at Tyre to unload the ship's cargo. 📖

## Acts 21:4

We found disciples and stayed seven days; through the Spirit they repeatedly told Paul not to go to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 21:5

When we left, all the disciples, with wives and children, accompanied us outside the city. 📖

Together we knelt on the beach and prayed.

## Acts 21:6

After farewells, we boarded, and they went home. 📖

## Acts 21:7

Sailing from Tyre to Ptolemais, we greeted believers and stayed one day. 📖

## Acts 21:8

Next day, we reached Caesarea and stayed with Philip the evangelist, one of the Seven. 📖

**Help:** An evangelist announces the good news; the Seven were appointed in Acts 6.

## Acts 21:9

He had four unmarried daughters who prophesied. 📖

## Acts 21:10

After several days, Agabus, a prophet, came from Judea. 📖

## Acts 21:11

He bound his hands and feet with Paul's belt. 📖

He said, “The Holy Spirit says Jerusalem's Jews will bind its owner like this and hand him to Gentiles.”

## Acts 21:12

We and the local people begged Paul not to go to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 21:13

He answered, “Why weep and break my heart? 📖

I am ready for chains and even death in Jerusalem for the Lord Jesus' name.”

## Acts 21:14

Unable to persuade him, we stopped, saying, “Let the Lord's will be done.” 📖

## Acts 21:15

We packed and went to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 21:16

Caesarean disciples accompanied us to stay with Mnason, an early disciple from Cyprus. 📖

## Acts 21:17

Jerusalem's believers welcomed us joyfully. 📖

## Acts 21:18

Next day, Paul and we visited James, with all the elders present. 📖

## Acts 21:19

Paul greeted them and described, one by one, God's deeds among Gentiles through his service. 📖

## Acts 21:20

They glorified God, then said, “Brother, thousands of Jews believe and eagerly uphold the law. 📖

## Acts 21:21

They hear you teach all Jews living among Gentiles to abandon Moses, not circumcise children, or follow our customs. 📖

## Acts 21:22

What should we do, since they will hear you arrived? 📖

## Acts 21:23

Follow our advice; four men here have taken a vow. 📖

## Acts 21:24

Join their purification and pay their expenses so their heads can be shaved. 📖

Everyone will know the rumours are false and you too keep the law.

**Help:** Purification is a religious cleansing ceremony; a vow is a solemn promise.

## Acts 21:25

We wrote Gentile believers to avoid idol sacrifices as food, blood, strangled animals' meat, and sexual wrongdoing.” 📖

## Acts 21:26

Next day, Paul purified himself with the men and entered the temple. 📖

He announced when their purification would end and offerings would be made for each.

## Acts 21:27

Near the seven days' end, Jews from Asia saw him in the temple, stirred the crowd, and seized him. 📖

## Acts 21:28

They cried, “Israelites, help! 📖

He teaches everyone everywhere against our people, law, and this place.

He even brought Greeks into the temple, defiling this holy place.”

## Acts 21:29

They had seen Trophimus the Ephesian with Paul in town and assumed Paul brought him into the temple. 📖

**About the accusation:** The narrator identifies this as their assumption, rather than confirming that Paul did it.

## Acts 21:30

The city was stirred; people rushed together, dragged Paul from the temple, and immediately shut the gates. 📖

## Acts 21:31

As they tried killing him, the Roman regiment's commander heard Jerusalem was in turmoil. 📖

## Acts 21:32

He immediately ran down with soldiers and centurions; seeing them, the crowd stopped beating Paul. 📖

## Acts 21:33

He arrested Paul, ordered two chains, and asked who he was and what he did. 📖

## Acts 21:34

Conflicting shouts prevented him discovering the truth, so he ordered Paul into the barracks. 📖

**Help:** Barracks house soldiers.

## Acts 21:35

At the steps, soldiers carried Paul because the mob was so violent. 📖

## Acts 21:36

The following crowd shouted, “Away with him!” 📖

## Acts 21:37

Paul asked to speak to the commander, who replied, “Do you speak Greek? 📖

## Acts 21:38

Are you the Egyptian who began a rebellion and led four thousand Assassins into the wilderness?” 📖

## Acts 21:39

Paul replied, “I am a Jew from Tarsus in Cilicia, a citizen of an important city. 📖

Please let me speak to the people.”

## Acts 21:40

Given permission, Paul stood on the steps and signalled; the crowd fell silent, and he addressed them in Hebrew. 📖
