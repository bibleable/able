# Acts 4 — Witnesses before the council

**About this chapter**

Peter and John face questioning and threats, then join the believers in prayer.

The believers share their possessions to meet needs.

## Acts 4:1

While Peter and John spoke, priests, the temple guard's captain, and Sadducees approached. 📖

**Help:** Sadducees were a Jewish religious group.

## Acts 4:2

They were deeply disturbed by the apostles teaching people and proclaiming resurrection from death in Jesus. 📖

## Acts 4:3

They arrested Peter and John and kept them until the next day, because evening had come. 📖

## Acts 4:4

Many who heard believed, and the number of men grew to about five thousand. 📖

## Acts 4:5

The next day, rulers, elders, and experts in the written law gathered in Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 4:6

Annas the high priest, Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and many of the high priest's family joined them. 📖

## Acts 4:7

They brought Peter and John in and asked, “By what power or name did you do this?” 📖

## Acts 4:8

Filled with the Holy Spirit, Peter said, “Rulers and elders of the people! 📖

## Acts 4:9

Suppose we are questioned today about a kindness to a man unable to walk, asking how he was healed. 📖

## Acts 4:10

You and all Israel must know: Jesus Christ of Nazareth's name made this man stand healed before you. 📖

You crucified Jesus, but God raised him from death.

## Acts 4:11

Jesus is ‘the stone you builders rejected, which became the cornerstone.’ 📖

**Help:** A cornerstone is an important stone in a building's foundation.

Peter uses this image to describe Jesus' importance despite his rejection.

## Acts 4:12

Salvation is in no one else. 📖

No other name under heaven is given to humanity by which we must be saved.”

## Acts 4:13

Seeing Peter and John's boldness and knowing they were ordinary, unschooled men, the leaders marvelled. 📖

They recognised that these men had been with Jesus.

## Acts 4:14

The healed man stood with them, so the leaders had nothing to say in reply. 📖

## Acts 4:15

They ordered them out of the Sanhedrin and discussed together. 📖

**Help:** The Sanhedrin was the Jewish ruling council.

## Acts 4:16

They asked, “What should we do?” 📖

“Everyone in Jerusalem knows a remarkable miracle happened through them, and we cannot deny it.

## Acts 4:17

To stop this spreading further, we must warn them not to speak to anyone in this name.” 📖

## Acts 4:18

They called them back and commanded them not to speak or teach at all in Jesus' name. 📖

## Acts 4:19

Peter and John replied, “Judge whether listening to you rather than God is right in God's sight. 📖

## Acts 4:20

We cannot stop speaking about what we saw and heard.” 📖

## Acts 4:21

After more threats, they released them, finding no way to punish them because everyone glorified God for what happened. 📖

## Acts 4:22

The man miraculously healed was over forty years old. 📖

## Acts 4:23

Peter and John returned to their own people, reporting everything the chief priests and elders said. 📖

## Acts 4:24

United, the believers raised their voices to God. 📖

“Sovereign Lord, you made heaven, earth, sea, and everything in them.

## Acts 4:25

Through the Holy Spirit, you spoke through your servant, our ancestor David. 📖

‘Why do nations rage and peoples plot for nothing?

## Acts 4:26

Earth's kings take their stand, and rulers gather against the Lord and his Anointed One.’ 📖

**Help:** Anointed One means God's chosen king, the Christ.

## Acts 4:27

In this city, Herod and Pontius Pilate conspired with Gentiles and Israel's people against your holy servant Jesus. 📖

You anointed him.

## Acts 4:28

They did what your power and will had decided beforehand would happen. 📖

## Acts 4:29

Lord, consider their threats and enable your servants to speak your word with complete boldness. 📖

## Acts 4:30

Stretch out your hand to heal and perform signs and wonders through your holy servant Jesus' name.” 📖

## Acts 4:31

After prayer, their meeting place shook, and all were filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke God's word boldly. 📖

## Acts 4:32

The believers were united in heart and soul. 📖

No one claimed possessions as their own; they shared everything.

## Acts 4:33

The apostles powerfully continued witnessing to the Lord Jesus' resurrection, and abundant kindness rested on them all. 📖

## Acts 4:34

No one among them was needy, because owners of land or houses sold them and brought the money. 📖

## Acts 4:35

They laid it at the apostles' feet to distribute according to each person's need. 📖

## Acts 4:36

Joseph was a Levite from Cyprus, whom the apostles called Barnabas, meaning Son of Encouragement. 📖

**Help:** A Levite belonged to Israel's tribe of Levi.

## Acts 4:37

He sold his field, brought the money, and laid it at the apostles' feet. 📖
