# Acts 6 — Seven chosen and Stephen accused

**About this chapter**

The believers appoint seven men to oversee a neglected need.

Stephen's opponents bring false witnesses against him.

## Acts 6:1

As the disciples increased, Greek-speaking Jews complained against Hebrew-speaking Jews. 📖

Their widows were being overlooked in the daily food distribution.

**Help:** Both groups were Jewish believers using different languages.

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## Acts 6:2

The Twelve gathered all the disciples. 📖

“We should not neglect God's word to serve tables.

## Acts 6:3

Choose seven men among you known to be full of the Spirit and wisdom. 📖

We will give them this responsibility.

## Acts 6:4

We will devote ourselves to prayer and serving the word.” 📖

## Acts 6:5

Everyone approved and chose Stephen, full of faith and the Holy Spirit. 📖

They also chose Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolas, a convert to Judaism from Antioch.

## Acts 6:6

They presented the seven to the apostles, who prayed and laid hands on them. 📖

## Acts 6:7

God's word spread, Jerusalem's disciples increased rapidly, and many priests obeyed the faith. 📖

## Acts 6:8

Stephen, full of kindness and power, performed great wonders and signs among the people. 📖

## Acts 6:9

Opponents from the Synagogue of the Freedmen argued with him. 📖

They included Cyrenians, Alexandrians, and men from Cilicia and Asia's provinces.

**Help:** A synagogue is a Jewish gathering and place of worship.

Freedmen means people released from slavery.

## Acts 6:10

They could not resist his wisdom or the Spirit through whom he spoke. 📖

## Acts 6:11

They prompted men to say, “We heard Stephen speak blasphemy against Moses and God.” 📖

**Help:** Blasphemy means speech insulting God or treating what is holy with contempt.

These are the opponents' accusations, rather than the narrator's judgment of Stephen.

## Acts 6:12

They stirred up the people, elders, and law experts. 📖

They confronted and seized Stephen, bringing him before the Sanhedrin.

## Acts 6:13

They presented false witnesses who said, “He never stops speaking against this holy place and the law. 📖

## Acts 6:14

We heard him say Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place and change the customs Moses handed to us.” 📖

## Acts 6:15

Everyone sitting in the Sanhedrin stared at Stephen and saw his face was like an angel's face. 📖
