# Acts 15 — The Jerusalem decision

**About this chapter**

The apostles, elders, and church address demands placed on Gentile believers.

Later, Paul and Barnabas disagree about Mark and travel separately.

## Acts 15:1

Men from Judea taught, “Unless you are circumcised according to Moses' custom, you cannot be saved.” 📖

**About these words:** The chapter records this disputed teaching and the council's response.

Circumcision removes the foreskin, a piece of skin on the penis, as a covenant sign.

## Acts 15:2

Paul and Barnabas debated sharply with them. 📖

They and other believers were appointed to take the question to Jerusalem's apostles and elders.

## Acts 15:3

Sent by the church, they travelled through Phoenicia and Samaria, reporting Gentile conversions and bringing the believers great joy. 📖

## Acts 15:4

Jerusalem's church, apostles, and elders welcomed them and heard everything God did through them. 📖

## Acts 15:5

Believing Pharisees said, “Gentiles must be circumcised and commanded to keep Moses' law.” 📖

## Acts 15:6

The apostles and elders gathered to consider this. 📖

## Acts 15:7

After much discussion, Peter said, “Brothers, God chose among you long ago that Gentiles would hear good news through me. 📖

They would hear and believe.

## Acts 15:8

God knows hearts and showed approval by giving them the Holy Spirit, just as to us. 📖

## Acts 15:9

He made no distinction, cleansing their hearts by faith. 📖

## Acts 15:10

Why test God by putting a yoke on disciples' necks which neither we nor our ancestors could bear? 📖

**Help:** A yoke joins working animals together, here picturing a burden placed on people.

## Acts 15:11

We believe we are saved through the Lord Jesus' kindness, just as they are.” 📖

## Acts 15:12

Everyone listened silently as Barnabas and Paul described God's signs and wonders among Gentiles through them. 📖

## Acts 15:13

After they finished, James said, “Brothers, listen! 📖

## Acts 15:14

Simon described God first visiting Gentiles to take a people for himself. 📖

**Help:** Simon here is Peter.

## Acts 15:15

The prophets agree, as written: 📖

## Acts 15:16

‘After this I will return and rebuild David's fallen tent, rebuild its ruins, and restore it. 📖

## Acts 15:17

Then humanity's remnant and all Gentiles called by my name may seek the Lord. 📖

The Lord says this, the one doing these things,

## Acts 15:18

which have been known for ages.’ 📖

## Acts 15:19

My judgment is that we should not trouble Gentiles turning to God. 📖

## Acts 15:20

We should write and tell them to avoid idol-polluted food, sexual wrongdoing, strangled animals' meat, and blood. 📖

**Help:** An idol is something worshipped as a god.

Strangling kills an animal by stopping its breathing.

## Acts 15:21

Moses has been proclaimed in every city since ancient times and is read in synagogues every Sabbath.” 📖

## Acts 15:22

The apostles, elders, and whole church chose leaders Judas called Barsabbas and Silas to accompany Paul and Barnabas to Antioch. 📖

## Acts 15:23

They sent this letter: “From the apostles and elders, your brothers, to Gentile believers in Antioch, Syria, and Cilicia. 📖

Greetings.

## Acts 15:24

Some left us without permission and disturbed you, troubling your minds with their words. 📖

## Acts 15:25

We agreed unanimously to send chosen men with our beloved Barnabas and Paul. 📖

## Acts 15:26

These men risked their lives for our Lord Jesus Christ's name. 📖

## Acts 15:27

Judas and Silas will tell you personally the same things we write. 📖

## Acts 15:28

The Holy Spirit and we thought it good to place no burden beyond these essential requirements. 📖

## Acts 15:29

Avoid idol sacrifices as food, blood, strangled animals' meat, and sexual wrongdoing. 📖

You will do well to avoid these things.

Farewell.”

## Acts 15:30

The men went to Antioch, gathered the congregation, and delivered the letter. 📖

## Acts 15:31

The people read it and rejoiced at its encouragement. 📖

## Acts 15:32

Judas and Silas, also prophets, spoke at length, encouraging and strengthening the believers. 📖

## Acts 15:33

After some time, the believers sent them in peace back to those who sent them. 📖

## Acts 15:34

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Acts 15:35

Paul and Barnabas remained with many others in Antioch, teaching and preaching the Lord's word. 📖

## Acts 15:36

Later Paul said, “Let us revisit the believers in every town where we preached, to see how they are.” 📖

## Acts 15:37

Barnabas wanted John called Mark to come. 📖

## Acts 15:38

Paul thought they should not take him, because he deserted them in Pamphylia and did not continue the work. 📖

## Acts 15:39

Their disagreement became so sharp they separated, and Barnabas took Mark to Cyprus by sea. 📖

## Acts 15:40

Paul chose Silas and left, entrusted by the believers to the Lord's kindness. 📖

## Acts 15:41

He travelled through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches. 📖
