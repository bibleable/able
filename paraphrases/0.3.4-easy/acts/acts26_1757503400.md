# Acts 26 — Paul addresses Agrippa

**About this chapter**

Paul recounts his commission and his hope in resurrection.

The hearers discuss his innocence and appeal.

## Acts 26:1

Agrippa allowed Paul to defend himself, and Paul stretched out his hand. 📖

## Acts 26:2

“King Agrippa, I am fortunate to defend myself before you today against all the Jews' accusations. 📖

## Acts 26:3

This is especially so because you know all Jewish customs and disputes. 📖

Please listen patiently.

## Acts 26:4

All Jews know my life from childhood among my people and in Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 26:5

They have long known I followed our religion's strictest sect as a Pharisee, and can testify if willing. 📖

## Acts 26:6

I stand trial for hope in God's promise to our ancestors. 📖

## Acts 26:7

Our twelve tribes earnestly serve God night and day, hoping for its fulfilment; this hope brings the Jews' accusation. 📖

## Acts 26:8

Why think it unbelievable that God raises the dead? 📖

## Acts 26:9

I too thought I must do everything to oppose Jesus of Nazareth's name. 📖

## Acts 26:10

With chief priests' authority, I imprisoned many holy people in Jerusalem and voted against them when sentenced to death. 📖

## Acts 26:11

I often punished them in synagogues and tried forcing blasphemy; raging, I pursued them even to foreign cities. 📖

## Acts 26:12

On such a mission, I travelled to Damascus with the chief priests' authority. 📖

## Acts 26:13

At noon, King, heavenly light brighter than sunlight shone round me and my companions. 📖

## Acts 26:14

We all fell, and a Hebrew voice said, ‘Saul, Saul, why persecute me? 📖

It is hard for you to kick against the goads.’

**Help:** Goads are sharp sticks used to guide animals; kicking against them pictures resisting guidance and causing oneself pain.

## Acts 26:15

I asked who spoke, and the Lord replied, ‘I am Jesus, whom you persecute. 📖

## Acts 26:16

Get up and stand; I appeared to appoint you my servant and witness. 📖

You will witness what you saw from me and what I will show you.

## Acts 26:17

I will rescue you from your people and Gentiles, to whom I send you. 📖

## Acts 26:18

Open their eyes so they turn from darkness to light and Satan's power to God. 📖

Then they may receive forgiveness of sins and an inheritance among those made holy by faith in me.’

**Help:** Open eyes and light picture understanding God's message, rather than making a moral judgment about physical blindness.

## Acts 26:19

King Agrippa, I obeyed that heavenly vision. 📖

## Acts 26:20

First in Damascus and Jerusalem, then throughout Judea and among Gentiles, I called people to repent and turn to God. 📖

Their deeds should show their repentance.

## Acts 26:21

That is why Jews seized me in the temple courts and tried killing me. 📖

## Acts 26:22

God helped me until today; I testify to small and great, saying only what Moses and the prophets foretold. 📖

## Acts 26:23

The Christ would suffer and, first to rise from death, proclaim light to our people and Gentiles.” 📖

## Acts 26:24

Festus shouted, “You are insane, Paul; your great learning drives you mad!” 📖

## Acts 26:25

Paul answered, “I am not insane, excellent Festus; my words are true and sensible. 📖

## Acts 26:26

The king knows, and I speak freely, sure none escaped his notice; it was not done in a corner. 📖

## Acts 26:27

Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? 📖

I know you do.”

## Acts 26:28

Agrippa asked, “Can you persuade me to become a Christian so quickly?” 📖

## Acts 26:29

Paul replied, “Quickly or slowly, I pray you and everyone hearing me become like me, except these chains.” 📖

## Acts 26:30

The king, governor, Bernice, and the others rose. 📖

## Acts 26:31

Leaving, they agreed, “He deserves neither death nor prison.” 📖

## Acts 26:32

Agrippa told Festus, “He could have been freed if he had not appealed to Caesar.” 📖
