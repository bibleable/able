# Acts 10 — Peter and Cornelius

**About this chapter**

God brings Peter to Cornelius's household.

Gentiles hear about Jesus, receive the Holy Spirit, and are baptized.

## Acts 10:1

In Caesarea, Cornelius was a centurion in the Italian Regiment. 📖

**Help:** A centurion was a Roman army officer; a regiment was a military unit.

## Acts 10:2

He and his whole household were devout and God-fearing; he gave generously and prayed regularly. 📖

## Acts 10:3

About the ninth hour one day, he clearly saw God's angel in a vision, calling, “Cornelius!” 📖

**Help:** The ninth hour was about three in the afternoon.

## Acts 10:4

Afraid, Cornelius stared and asked, “What is it, Lord?” 📖

The angel replied, “Your prayers and gifts to the poor have risen before God as a memorial offering.

## Acts 10:5

Send men to Joppa for Simon, called Peter. 📖

## Acts 10:6

He stays with Simon the tanner, whose house is beside the sea.” 📖

**Help:** A tanner makes leather from animal skins.

## Acts 10:7

After the angel left, Cornelius called two servants and a devout soldier among his attendants. 📖

## Acts 10:8

He explained everything and sent them to Joppa. 📖

## Acts 10:9

Next day, as they approached the city, Peter went to the roof to pray about the sixth hour. 📖

**Help:** The sixth hour was about noon.

## Acts 10:10

Hungry and wanting food, he fell into a trance while the meal was prepared. 📖

**Help:** A trance here is the state in which Peter receives the vision.

## Acts 10:11

He saw heaven open and something like a large sheet lowered to earth by its four corners. 📖

## Acts 10:12

It contained all kinds of four-footed animals, reptiles, and birds. 📖

## Acts 10:13

A voice said, “Get up, Peter; kill and eat!” 📖

## Acts 10:14

Peter replied, “No, Lord; I have never eaten anything impure or unclean.” 📖

**Help:** Impure and unclean concern religious food rules here, rather than dirt or disability.

## Acts 10:15

The voice spoke again: “Do not call impure what God made clean.” 📖

## Acts 10:16

This happened three times, and suddenly the sheet returned to heaven. 📖

## Acts 10:17

While Peter puzzled over the vision, Cornelius's men found Simon's house and approached the gate. 📖

## Acts 10:18

They called, asking whether Simon called Peter stayed there. 📖

## Acts 10:19

While Peter considered the vision, the Spirit said, “Three men are looking for you. 📖

## Acts 10:20

Get up, go downstairs, and accompany them without hesitation, because I sent them.” 📖

## Acts 10:21

Peter went down and said, “I am the one you seek; why have you come?” 📖

## Acts 10:22

They replied, “Cornelius the centurion sent us; he is righteous and God-fearing, respected throughout the Jewish nation. 📖

A holy angel told him to invite you home to hear your message.”

## Acts 10:23

Peter invited them in as guests and left with them next day, accompanied by some brothers from Joppa. 📖

## Acts 10:24

He reached Caesarea the following day, where Cornelius waited with his relatives and close friends. 📖

## Acts 10:25

As Peter entered, Cornelius met him and fell at his feet to worship. 📖

## Acts 10:26

Peter helped him up, saying, “Stand up; I am only a man too.” 📖

## Acts 10:27

Talking with him, Peter entered and found many gathered. 📖

## Acts 10:28

He said, “You know it is unlawful for a Jew to associate with or visit a foreigner. 📖

God showed me not to call any person impure or unclean.

**About these words:** Peter describes the boundary he had observed, then explains what God showed him about people.

## Acts 10:29

So I came without objection when invited; why did you send for me?” 📖

## Acts 10:30

Cornelius replied, “Four days ago, I prayed here at this same ninth hour, when a man in shining clothes appeared. 📖

## Acts 10:31

He said, ‘Cornelius, your prayer was heard, and God remembered your gifts to the poor. 📖

## Acts 10:32

Send to Joppa for Simon called Peter, staying with Simon the tanner beside the sea.’ 📖

## Acts 10:33

I sent immediately, and you kindly came. 📖

We are all before God, ready to hear everything the Lord instructed you to tell us.”

## Acts 10:34

Peter said, “I truly understand now that God shows no favouritism. 📖

## Acts 10:35

He welcomes those in every nation who fear him and do right. 📖

## Acts 10:36

He sent Israel the good news of peace through Jesus Christ, who is Lord of all. 📖

## Acts 10:37

You know what happened throughout Judea, beginning in Galilee with the baptism John preached. 📖

## Acts 10:38

God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and power. 📖

Jesus went around doing good and healing everyone oppressed by the devil, because God was with him.

**Help:** Anointing marks someone as chosen and set apart for a task.

## Acts 10:39

We witness everything he did in the Jews' land and Jerusalem. 📖

They killed him by hanging him on a tree.

## Acts 10:40

God raised him on the third day and caused him to be seen. 📖

## Acts 10:41

Not everyone saw him; we were witnesses God chose beforehand, who ate and drank with him after his resurrection. 📖

## Acts 10:42

He commanded us to preach and testify that God appointed him judge of living and dead. 📖

## Acts 10:43

All prophets testify that everyone believing in him receives forgiveness of sins through his name.” 📖

## Acts 10:44

While Peter spoke, the Holy Spirit came upon everyone hearing the message. 📖

## Acts 10:45

Peter's circumcised companions were amazed that the gift of the Holy Spirit was poured out even on Gentiles. 📖

**Help:** These companions were Jewish believers; Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 10:46

They heard them speaking in tongues and praising God's greatness. 📖

## Acts 10:47

Peter said, “Can anyone refuse water to baptize those who received the Holy Spirit just like us?” 📖

## Acts 10:48

He ordered baptism in Jesus Christ's name, and they asked him to stay a few days. 📖
