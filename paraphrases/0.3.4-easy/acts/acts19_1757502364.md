# Acts 19 — God's word and the Ephesian riot

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches in Ephesus, and God's word spreads.

A dispute over Artemis's shrines grows into a citywide disturbance.

## Acts 19:1

While Apollos was in Corinth, Paul travelled inland to Ephesus and found disciples. 📖

## Acts 19:2

He asked, “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?” 📖

They replied, “We have not even heard there is a Holy Spirit.”

## Acts 19:3

Paul asked, “What baptism did you receive?” 📖

They replied, “John's baptism.”

## Acts 19:4

Paul said, “John baptized for repentance, telling people to believe in the one coming after him, Jesus.” 📖

**Help:** Repentance means turning from sin towards God.

## Acts 19:5

Hearing this, they were baptized into the Lord Jesus' name. 📖

## Acts 19:6

Paul laid hands on them, and the Holy Spirit came; they spoke in tongues and prophesied. 📖

**Help:** Prophesied means spoke messages from God.

Tongues refers to Spirit-given speech or languages.

## Acts 19:7

There were about twelve men altogether. 📖

## Acts 19:8

Paul spoke boldly in the synagogue for three months, arguing persuasively about God's kingdom. 📖

## Acts 19:9

Some stubbornly refused belief and publicly attacked the Way, so Paul left with his disciples. 📖

He held daily discussions in Tyrannus's lecture hall.

**Help:** The Way names the faith and community following Jesus.

## Acts 19:10

For two years this continued, until all Asia's residents, Jews and Greeks, heard the Lord's word. 📖

**Help:** Asia here was a Roman province.

## Acts 19:11

God performed extraordinary miracles through Paul's hands. 📖

## Acts 19:12

Even handkerchiefs and aprons touching him were taken to sick people, whose diseases and evil spirits left them. 📖

## Acts 19:13

Travelling Jewish exorcists tried using the Lord Jesus' name over those with evil spirits. 📖

They said, “I command you by Jesus, whom Paul preaches.”

**Help:** Exorcists try to drive out evil spirits.

## Acts 19:14

Seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, did this. 📖

## Acts 19:15

The evil spirit replied, “I know Jesus and know about Paul, but who are you?” 📖

## Acts 19:16

The possessed man jumped on them and violently overpowered them all; they fled the house naked and wounded. 📖

## Acts 19:17

All Ephesus's Jews and Greeks heard, became afraid, and highly honoured the Lord Jesus' name. 📖

## Acts 19:18

Many believers openly confessed and disclosed their deeds. 📖

## Acts 19:19

Many practising magic brought their books and burned them publicly; the books were valued at fifty thousand drachmas. 📖

**Help:** Drachmas were coins.

## Acts 19:20

The Lord's word spread powerfully and prevailed. 📖

## Acts 19:21

Paul resolved in the Spirit to travel through Macedonia and Achaia to Jerusalem. 📖

He said, “After that, I must see Rome too.”

## Acts 19:22

He sent Timothy and Erastus, two helpers, to Macedonia, while remaining in Asia awhile. 📖

## Acts 19:23

A great disturbance arose about the Way. 📖

## Acts 19:24

Demetrius, a silversmith making Artemis's silver shrines, brought much business to the craftsmen. 📖

**Help:** A silversmith works with silver; shrines are objects or places used in worship.

Artemis was a goddess worshipped in Ephesus.

## Acts 19:25

He gathered them and related workers, saying, “This business makes us prosperous. 📖

## Acts 19:26

Paul has persuaded many in Ephesus and nearly all Asia to turn away, saying human-made gods are not gods. 📖

You see and hear this.

## Acts 19:27

Our business could lose its good name, and great Artemis's temple could lose honour. 📖

Her majesty could be brought down, though all Asia and the world worship her.”

## Acts 19:28

Furious, they shouted, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” 📖

## Acts 19:29

The city fell into disorder, and they rushed into the theatre with Gaius and Aristarchus, Paul's Macedonian travelling companions. 📖

## Acts 19:30

Paul wanted to enter the assembly, but disciples stopped him. 📖

## Acts 19:31

Even his friends among Asia's officials begged him not to enter the theatre. 📖

## Acts 19:32

People shouted different things in confusion; most did not know why they gathered. 📖

## Acts 19:33

The Jews pushed Alexander forward; he signalled for quiet to defend himself before the people. 📖

## Acts 19:34

Recognising he was Jewish, they shouted together for about two hours, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” 📖

## Acts 19:35

The city clerk quieted them, saying, “Everyone knows Ephesus guards great Artemis's temple and her image, which fell from heaven. 📖

**About these words:** The clerk states the city's religious claim; the narrator does not confirm its truth.

## Acts 19:36

These facts cannot be denied, so be calm and do nothing rash. 📖

## Acts 19:37

These men neither robbed our temple nor blasphemed our goddess. 📖

## Acts 19:38

If Demetrius and his craftsmen have complaints, courts and proconsuls are available; bring charges there. 📖

**Help:** Proconsuls were Roman governors.

## Acts 19:39

Anything beyond this must be settled in a lawful assembly. 📖

## Acts 19:40

We risk a rioting charge today, without any reason to justify this disturbance.” 📖

## Acts 19:41

He dismissed the assembly. 📖
