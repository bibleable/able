# Acts 28 — Malta and Rome

**About this chapter**

The travellers receive kindness on Malta and reach Rome.

Paul speaks to Jewish leaders and welcomes all who come to hear him.

## Acts 28:1

Safely ashore, we learned the island was Malta. 📖

## Acts 28:2

The islanders showed extraordinary kindness, lighting a fire and welcoming us all in the rain and cold. 📖

## Acts 28:3

Paul gathered sticks; as he put them on the fire, a viper driven out by heat fastened onto his hand. 📖

**Help:** A viper is a snake.

## Acts 28:4

Seeing it hanging, they said, “This man must be a murderer. 📖

Though he escaped the sea, Justice will not let him live.”

**About these words:** The islanders interpret the event; the narrator does not confirm their accusation.

## Acts 28:5

Paul shook it into the fire and suffered no harm. 📖

## Acts 28:6

They expected him to swell up or suddenly die. 📖

After a long wait without anything unusual, they changed their minds and called him a god.

## Acts 28:7

Publius, the island's chief official, owned a nearby estate and welcomed us generously for three days. 📖

## Acts 28:8

His father lay ill with fever and dysentery; Paul visited, prayed, laid hands on him, and healed him. 📖

**Help:** Dysentery is an illness causing severe diarrhoea.

## Acts 28:9

The island's other sick people came and were healed too. 📖

## Acts 28:10

They honoured us in many ways and supplied what we needed to sail. 📖

## Acts 28:11

Three months later, we sailed on an Alexandrian ship wintering there, with the Twin Brothers as its figurehead. 📖

**Help:** A figurehead is a carved image on a ship's front.

## Acts 28:12

We stayed three days at Syracuse. 📖

## Acts 28:13

We sailed to Rhegium; a south wind rose after one day, and on the second we reached Puteoli. 📖

## Acts 28:14

Believers invited us to stay a week; then we came to Rome. 📖

## Acts 28:15

Hearing about us, believers came to meet us at the Forum of Appius and the Three Taverns. 📖

Paul thanked God and took courage when he saw them.

## Acts 28:16

In Rome, Paul could stay separately with a soldier guarding him. 📖

## Acts 28:17

After three days, he gathered Jewish leaders, saying, “I wronged neither our people nor our ancestors' customs. 📖

Yet I was arrested in Jerusalem and handed to Romans.

## Acts 28:18

They examined me and wanted to release me, finding no reason for death. 📖

## Acts 28:19

Jewish objections forced my appeal to Caesar, though I have no accusation against my nation. 📖

## Acts 28:20

I called you to speak because Israel's hope is why I wear this chain.” 📖

## Acts 28:21

They replied, “We received no letters from Judea about you, nor any bad reports from visiting brothers. 📖

## Acts 28:22

We want to hear your views, because people everywhere speak against this sect.” 📖

## Acts 28:23

They set a day, and many came to his lodging. 📖

From morning until evening, he testified about God's kingdom and argued for Jesus from Moses' Law and the Prophets.

## Acts 28:24

Some were persuaded, while others refused belief. 📖

## Acts 28:25

Disagreeing, they left after Paul's final words: “The Holy Spirit spoke rightly through Isaiah to your ancestors. 📖

## Acts 28:26

‘Tell these people: you will keep hearing without understanding and seeing without perceiving. 📖

## Acts 28:27

Their hearts grew hard, their ears barely hear, and they closed their eyes. 📖

Otherwise, they might see, hear, understand in their hearts, turn, and I would heal them.’

**Help:** These images describe resistance to God's message, rather than condemning physical disabilities.

## Acts 28:28

Know that God sent salvation to Gentiles, and they will listen!” 📖

## Acts 28:29

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Acts 28:30

For two full years, Paul rented his own house and welcomed everyone who came. 📖

## Acts 28:31

He boldly and freely proclaimed God's kingdom and taught about the Lord Jesus Christ. 📖
