# Acts 25 — Paul appeals to Caesar

**About this chapter**

Festus hears the case, and Paul appeals to Caesar.

Agrippa arrives to hear Paul.

## Acts 25:1

Three days after arriving, Festus travelled from Caesarea to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 25:2

The chief priests and Jewish leaders presented their case and urged him to help them. 📖

## Acts 25:3

They wanted Paul summoned to Jerusalem, planning to ambush and kill him on the way. 📖

## Acts 25:4

Festus replied, “Paul stays in Caesarea, where I am going soon. 📖

## Acts 25:5

Let your leaders accompany me and accuse him there if he did wrong.” 📖

## Acts 25:6

After no more than eight or ten days, Festus returned to Caesarea and summoned Paul to court next day. 📖

## Acts 25:7

Jerusalem's Jews surrounded Paul, making many serious accusations they could not prove. 📖

## Acts 25:8

Paul said, “I offended neither Jewish law, the temple, nor Caesar.” 📖

## Acts 25:9

Wanting to please the Jews, Festus asked, “Will you stand trial before me in Jerusalem?” 📖

## Acts 25:10

Paul replied, “Caesar's judgment seat is where I should be tried. 📖

You know well I wronged no Jews.

## Acts 25:11

If I deserve death, I do not refuse it. 📖

If their accusations are false, no one may hand me over.

I appeal to Caesar!”

## Acts 25:12

After consulting his council, Festus replied, “To Caesar you appealed; to Caesar you will go.” 📖

## Acts 25:13

Days later, King Agrippa and Bernice visited Caesarea to pay respects to Festus. 📖

## Acts 25:14

During their stay, Festus explained, “Felix left a man imprisoned. 📖

## Acts 25:15

In Jerusalem, the Jewish chief priests and elders sought judgment against him. 📖

## Acts 25:16

I explained Romans do not hand someone over before he faces accusers and can defend himself. 📖

## Acts 25:17

When they came here, I acted without delay, summoning him to court next day. 📖

## Acts 25:18

The accusers brought none of the crimes I expected. 📖

## Acts 25:19

They disputed their religion and Jesus, dead but whom Paul claims alive. 📖

## Acts 25:20

Unsure how to investigate, I asked whether he would face trial in Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 25:21

He appealed for the Emperor's decision, so I ordered him held until sending him to Caesar.” 📖

## Acts 25:22

Agrippa wanted to hear him, and Festus replied, “Tomorrow you will.” 📖

## Acts 25:23

Next day, Agrippa and Bernice entered the auditorium with great ceremony, commanders, and leading citizens; Festus summoned Paul. 📖

## Acts 25:24

He said, “King Agrippa and everyone here, look at this man. 📖

The whole Jewish community here and in Jerusalem petitioned for his death.

## Acts 25:25

I found nothing deserving death, but decided to send him because he appealed to the Emperor. 📖

## Acts 25:26

I have nothing definite to write our sovereign, so I brought him before you, especially King Agrippa. 📖

After examining him, I may have something to write.

**Help:** Sovereign here means the emperor.

## Acts 25:27

Sending a prisoner without stating charges seems unreasonable.” 📖
