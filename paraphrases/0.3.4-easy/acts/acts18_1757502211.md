# Acts 18 — Corinth, travel, and Apollos

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches in Corinth, faces accusations, and travels again.

Priscilla and Aquila help Apollos understand God's way more accurately.

## Acts 18:1

Paul left Athens for Corinth. 📖

## Acts 18:2

He met Aquila, a Jew born in Pontus, recently arrived from Italy with his wife Priscilla. 📖

They left because Claudius ordered all Jews out of Rome, and Paul visited them.

## Acts 18:3

He stayed and worked with them because they, like him, made tents. 📖

## Acts 18:4

Each Sabbath, he reasoned in the synagogue, trying to persuade Jews and Greeks. 📖

## Acts 18:5

When Silas and Timothy arrived from Macedonia, Paul devoted himself fully to the word, testifying Jesus is the Christ. 📖

## Acts 18:6

When the Jews opposed and insulted him, he shook his clothes and said, “Your blood is on your heads! 📖

I am innocent; now I go to the Gentiles.”

**Help:** The blood expression concerns responsibility, rather than a command to harm anyone.

Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 18:7

He left the synagogue for the house next door, belonging to Titus Justus, a worshipper of God. 📖

## Acts 18:8

Crispus, the synagogue leader, and his whole household believed in the Lord. 📖

Many Corinthians hearing the message believed and were baptized.

## Acts 18:9

One night, the Lord said in a vision, “Do not fear; keep speaking, and do not be silent. 📖

## Acts 18:10

I am with you; no one will lay a hand on you, because many in this city are mine.” 📖

## Acts 18:11

Paul stayed a year and a half, teaching God's word. 📖

## Acts 18:12

Under Gallio, Achaia's proconsul, the Jews jointly attacked Paul and brought him before the judgment seat. 📖

**Help:** A proconsul was a Roman governor.

## Acts 18:13

They said, “He persuades people to worship God against the law.” 📖

## Acts 18:14

Before Paul spoke, Gallio said, “Jews, if this concerned wrongdoing or serious crime, I could reasonably hear you. 📖

## Acts 18:15

It concerns words, names, and your law; settle it yourselves, because I refuse to judge this.” 📖

## Acts 18:16

He drove them from the judgment seat. 📖

## Acts 18:17

The crowd seized Sosthenes, the synagogue leader, and beat him there, but Gallio was unconcerned. 📖

## Acts 18:18

Paul stayed longer, then said goodbye to the believers. 📖

He shaved his head in Cenchrea because of a vow, then sailed for Syria with Priscilla and Aquila.

**Help:** A vow is a solemn promise.

## Acts 18:19

At Ephesus, he left them and reasoned with the Jews in the synagogue. 📖

## Acts 18:20

They asked him to stay longer, but he declined. 📖

## Acts 18:21

He said, “I will return if God wills,” and sailed from Ephesus. 📖

## Acts 18:22

Landing at Caesarea, he went up to greet Jerusalem's church, then down to Antioch. 📖

## Acts 18:23

After staying there, he travelled throughout Galatia and Phrygia, strengthening all disciples. 📖

## Acts 18:24

Apollos, an eloquent Jew from Alexandria, came to Ephesus, knowing Scripture well. 📖

## Acts 18:25

Instructed in the Lord's way and fervent in spirit, he taught accurately about Jesus, knowing only John's baptism. 📖

**Help:** Eloquent means able to speak clearly and persuasively; fervent means deeply enthusiastic and committed.

## Acts 18:26

He spoke boldly in the synagogue; Priscilla and Aquila heard, took him aside, and explained God's way more accurately. 📖

## Acts 18:27

When he wanted to cross to Achaia, believers encouraged him and wrote asking the disciples there to welcome him. 📖

He greatly helped those who believed by God's kindness.

## Acts 18:28

He powerfully refuted the Jews publicly, proving from Scripture that Jesus is the Christ. 📖

**Help:** Refuted means showed their argument to be wrong.
