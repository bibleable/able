# Acts 20 — Journeys and farewell to the elders

**About this chapter**

Paul travels, speaks in Troas, and meets the Ephesian elders at Miletus.

He warns them to care for the church and remain alert.

## Acts 20:1

After the uproar, Paul called the disciples, encouraged them, said goodbye, and left for Macedonia. 📖

## Acts 20:2

He travelled through the region, giving much encouragement, and reached Greece. 📖

## Acts 20:3

He stayed three months; a Jewish plot before his voyage to Syria made him return through Macedonia. 📖

## Acts 20:4

His companions included Sopater, Pyrrhus's son from Berea, and Aristarchus and Secundus from Thessalonica. 📖

Gaius from Derbe, Timothy, Tychicus, and Trophimus also accompanied him.

Tychicus and Trophimus came from Asia.

## Acts 20:5

These men went ahead and waited for us in Troas. 📖

## Acts 20:6

After the Feast of Unleavened Bread, we sailed from Philippi and reached Troas in five days, staying seven days. 📖

## Acts 20:7

On the week's first day, we gathered to break bread. 📖

Paul spoke until midnight because he planned to leave next day.

## Acts 20:8

Many lamps lit our upstairs meeting room. 📖

## Acts 20:9

Eutychus, a young man at the window, fell deeply asleep during Paul's long speech. 📖

He fell from the third storey and was picked up dead.

## Acts 20:10

Paul went down, threw himself on him, and embraced him, saying, “Do not fear; he is still alive!” 📖

## Acts 20:11

Paul went upstairs, broke bread and ate, talked until dawn, and left. 📖

## Acts 20:12

They took the young man home alive and were greatly relieved. 📖

## Acts 20:13

We sailed ahead to Assos to collect Paul, as he arranged, because he travelled there on foot. 📖

## Acts 20:14

Meeting him there, we took him aboard and sailed to Mitylene. 📖

## Acts 20:15

Next day, we sailed opposite Chios; the day after to Samos, then the following day to Miletus. 📖

## Acts 20:16

Paul sailed past Ephesus to avoid delay in Asia, hoping to reach Jerusalem by Pentecost if possible. 📖

## Acts 20:17

From Miletus, he summoned Ephesus's church elders. 📖

## Acts 20:18

He told them, “You know how I lived among you from my first day in Asia. 📖

## Acts 20:19

I served the Lord with great humility and tears, especially during trials from Jewish plots. 📖

## Acts 20:20

I held nothing helpful back, teaching publicly and from house to house. 📖

## Acts 20:21

I testified to Jews and Greeks about repentance towards God and faith in our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## Acts 20:22

Compelled by the Spirit, I go to Jerusalem without knowing what awaits me. 📖

## Acts 20:23

I know only that the Holy Spirit warns in every town of chains and suffering ahead. 📖

## Acts 20:24

My life means nothing to me if I can finish my course and the service received from the Lord Jesus. 📖

That service is witnessing to the good news of God's kindness.

## Acts 20:25

None of you among whom I preached the kingdom will see my face again. 📖

## Acts 20:26

Today I testify that I am innocent of everyone's blood. 📖

## Acts 20:27

I held back none of God's whole will from you. 📖

## Acts 20:28

Watch yourselves and the whole flock the Holy Spirit made you oversee. 📖

Shepherd God's church, which he bought with his own blood.

**Help:** Flock and shepherd picture the believers and their leaders' responsibility to care for them.

## Acts 20:29

After I leave, savage wolves will come among you and not spare the flock. 📖

**Help:** Wolves picture people who harm the believers.

## Acts 20:30

Even some of you will twist truth to draw disciples after yourselves. 📖

## Acts 20:31

Stay alert; for three years I never stopped warning each of you night and day with tears. 📖

## Acts 20:32

I entrust you to God and the word of his kindness. 📖

It can build you up and give an inheritance among everyone made holy.

## Acts 20:33

I coveted no one's silver, gold, or clothes. 📖

**Help:** Coveted means strongly desired something belonging to someone else.

## Acts 20:34

You know these hands worked to supply my needs and my companions' needs. 📖

## Acts 20:35

In everything, I showed you we must help the weak by working hard like this. 📖

Remember the Lord Jesus' own words: ‘Giving is more blessed than receiving.’”

## Acts 20:36

Paul knelt with them all and prayed. 📖

## Acts 20:37

They wept openly, embraced him, and kissed him. 📖

## Acts 20:38

They especially grieved that they would not see him again, then accompanied him to the ship. 📖
