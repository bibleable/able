# Acts 13 — Sent out and preaching in Antioch

**About this chapter**

The Spirit sends Barnabas and Saul out from Antioch.

They travel through Cyprus and preach in Pisidian Antioch.

## Acts 13:1

Antioch's church had prophets and teachers: Barnabas, Simeon called Niger, Lucius of Cyrene, Manaen, and Saul. 📖

Manaen had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch.

**Help:** A tetrarch was a regional ruler.

## Acts 13:2

While they worshipped and fasted, the Holy Spirit spoke. 📖

“Set Barnabas and Saul apart for me, for the work I called them to.”

**Help:** Fasting means going without food for a time, here in worship.

## Acts 13:3

After fasting and prayer, they laid hands on them and sent them off. 📖

## Acts 13:4

Sent by the Holy Spirit, Barnabas and Saul went to Seleucia and sailed to Cyprus. 📖

## Acts 13:5

At Salamis, they proclaimed God's word in Jewish synagogues, with John helping them. 📖

## Acts 13:6

They travelled across the whole island to Paphos and met Bar-Jesus, a Jewish sorcerer and false prophet. 📖

## Acts 13:7

He attended the proconsul Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man who called for Barnabas and Saul to hear God's word. 📖

**Help:** A proconsul was a Roman governor.

## Acts 13:8

Elymas the sorcerer, as his name means, opposed them and tried turning the proconsul from the faith. 📖

**Help:** Sorcery means using magic or spiritual powers in ways opposed to God.

Elymas and Bar-Jesus name the same man.

## Acts 13:9

Saul, also called Paul, was filled with the Holy Spirit and looked straight at Elymas. 📖

## Acts 13:10

He said, “Devil's child and enemy of all righteousness! 📖

You are full of every kind of deceit and trickery.

Will you never stop twisting the Lord's straight ways?

## Acts 13:11

The Lord's hand is against you; for a time you will be blind, unable to see sunlight.” 📖

Immediately mist and darkness covered him, and he searched for someone to lead him by hand.

## Acts 13:12

Seeing this, the proconsul believed, amazed at the teaching about the Lord. 📖

## Acts 13:13

Paul's group sailed from Paphos to Perga in Pamphylia, where John left them and returned to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 13:14

They travelled inland to Pisidian Antioch and sat in the synagogue on the Sabbath. 📖

## Acts 13:15

After the Law and Prophets were read, the leaders said, “Brothers, speak if you have encouragement for the people.” 📖

## Acts 13:16

Paul stood, signalled with his hand, and said, “Israelites and God-fearing Gentiles, listen! 📖

## Acts 13:17

Israel's God chose our ancestors and made them great during their stay in Egypt. 📖

With a raised arm, he led them out.

## Acts 13:18

He endured their conduct in the wilderness for about forty years. 📖

## Acts 13:19

He defeated seven nations in Canaan and gave their land to his people as an inheritance. 📖

## Acts 13:20

All this took about 450 years; afterwards he gave judges until Samuel the prophet. 📖

## Acts 13:21

They requested a king, so God gave Saul, Kish's son from Benjamin's tribe, who ruled forty years. 📖

## Acts 13:22

God removed Saul and made David king. 📖

He testified, ‘I found David, Jesse's son, a man after my own heart.

He will carry out my whole will.’

## Acts 13:23

As promised, God brought Israel the Saviour Jesus from this man's descendants. 📖

## Acts 13:24

Before Jesus arrived, John preached baptism of repentance to all Israel. 📖

**Help:** Repentance means turning from sin towards God.

## Acts 13:25

Finishing his work, John said, ‘Who do you think I am? 📖

I am not that One.

Someone comes after me whose sandals I am unworthy to untie.’

## Acts 13:26

Brothers, Abraham's children, and God-fearing Gentiles, this salvation message was sent to us. 📖

## Acts 13:27

Jerusalem's people and rulers did not recognise Jesus, yet condemning him fulfilled the prophets read each Sabbath. 📖

## Acts 13:28

They found no reason for a death sentence, but asked Pilate to execute him. 📖

## Acts 13:29

After fulfilling everything written about him, they took him from the tree and placed him in a tomb. 📖

## Acts 13:30

But God raised him from death. 📖

## Acts 13:31

For many days, those who travelled with him from Galilee to Jerusalem saw him; they now witness to our people. 📖

## Acts 13:32

We announce the good news of God's promise to our ancestors. 📖

## Acts 13:33

He fulfilled it for us, their children, by raising Jesus. 📖

The second Psalm says, ‘You are my Son; today I have become your Father.’

## Acts 13:34

God raised him from death, never to decay. 📖

He said, ‘I will give you the holy, sure blessings promised to David.’

## Acts 13:35

Another Psalm says, ‘You will not let your Holy One decay.’ 📖

## Acts 13:36

David served God's purpose in his generation, died, was buried with his ancestors, and decayed. 📖

## Acts 13:37

But the one God raised did not decay. 📖

## Acts 13:38

Brothers, know that forgiveness of sins is announced to you through Jesus. 📖

## Acts 13:39

Through him, every believer is justified from everything the law of Moses could not justify you from. 📖

**Help:** Justified means declared right before God.

## Acts 13:40

Beware that the prophets' words do not happen to you. 📖

## Acts 13:41

‘Look, you scoffers; wonder and perish! 📖

I do work in your days you would never believe, even if someone told you.’”

**Help:** Scoffers mock or scorn a message.

## Acts 13:42

As they left, people urged Paul and Barnabas to continue the message next Sabbath. 📖

## Acts 13:43

After the meeting, many Jews and devout converts to Judaism followed Paul and Barnabas. 📖

They spoke with them, urging them to continue in God's kindness.

## Acts 13:44

Next Sabbath, nearly the whole city gathered to hear the Lord's word. 📖

## Acts 13:45

Seeing the crowds, the Jews became jealous and blasphemously contradicted Paul. 📖

## Acts 13:46

Paul and Barnabas boldly replied, “God's word had to be spoken to you first. 📖

You reject it and judge yourselves unworthy of eternal life, so we turn to Gentiles.

## Acts 13:47

The Lord commanded, ‘I made you a light for Gentiles to bring salvation to earth's ends.’” 📖

## Acts 13:48

The Gentiles rejoiced and glorified the Lord's word, and everyone appointed for eternal life believed. 📖

## Acts 13:49

The Lord's word spread throughout the region. 📖

## Acts 13:50

The Jews stirred up prominent religious women and leading men, provoking persecution and driving Paul and Barnabas from their district. 📖

## Acts 13:51

They shook the dust from their feet in protest and went to Iconium. 📖

## Acts 13:52

The disciples were filled with joy and the Holy Spirit. 📖
