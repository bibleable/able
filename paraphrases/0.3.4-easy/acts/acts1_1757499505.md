# Acts 1 — Jesus sends his witnesses

**About this chapter**

The writer continues his account for Theophilus.

Jesus returns to heaven, and his followers choose Matthias.

## Acts 1:1

Theophilus, my first book told what Jesus began to do and teach. 📖

## Acts 1:2

It continued until he was taken up to heaven. 📖

Before that, he gave instructions through the Holy Spirit to his chosen apostles.

**Help with a word:** Apostles are messengers Jesus sent.

## Acts 1:3

After suffering, Jesus gave many clear proofs that he was alive. 📖

He appeared to them over 40 days.

He spoke about God's kingdom.

## Acts 1:4

Jesus told them, “Do not leave Jerusalem. 📖

Wait for the gift my Father promised.

You heard me speak about it.”

## Acts 1:5

“John baptized with water. 📖

In a few days you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.”

**Help with this promise:** The Spirit would come upon them with power.

## Acts 1:6

They asked, “Lord, will you now give the kingdom back to Israel?” 📖

## Acts 1:7

Jesus said, “The Father has set the times by his own authority. 📖

You are not to know those times.”

## Acts 1:8

“You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you. 📖

You will tell people about me in Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, and everywhere on earth.”

## Acts 1:9

As they watched, Jesus was taken up. 📖

A cloud hid him from sight.

## Acts 1:10

They kept looking into the sky. 📖

Suddenly, 2 men in white clothes stood beside them.

## Acts 1:11

The men said, “Men of Galilee, why keep looking up? 📖

Jesus has been taken into heaven.

He will return in the same way you saw him go.”

## Acts 1:12

They returned to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives. 📖

It was nearby, a distance allowed for travel on a Sabbath.

**Help with a word:** The Sabbath is the weekly day of rest.

## Acts 1:13

They went to the upstairs room where they stayed. 📖

They were Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, and Matthew.

They also included James son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James.

## Acts 1:14

They all kept praying together. 📖

The women, Jesus' mother Mary, and his brothers joined them.

## Acts 1:15

About 120 believers were gathered. 📖

Peter stood and spoke to them.

## Acts 1:16

“Brothers, the Scripture had to come true. 📖

The Holy Spirit spoke through David about Judas.

Judas guided those who arrested Jesus.”

## Acts 1:17

“Judas belonged to our group and shared our work.” 📖

## Acts 1:18

Judas' reward for evil was used to buy a field. 📖

There he fell headfirst.

His body burst open, and his intestines spilled out.

## Acts 1:19

Everyone in Jerusalem heard about it. 📖

In their language, they called the field Akeldama.

This means Field of Blood.

## Acts 1:20

Peter said, “Psalms says his home should be empty, with no one living there. 📖

It also says someone else should take his position.”

## Acts 1:21

“We must choose someone who was with us throughout the Lord Jesus' time among us.” 📖

## Acts 1:22

“He must have been with us from John's baptism until Jesus was taken up. 📖

He must join us in telling that Jesus rose from death.”

## Acts 1:23

They put forward 2 men. 📖

One was Joseph, called Barsabbas and also Justus.

The other was Matthias.

## Acts 1:24

They prayed, “Lord, you know everyone's heart. 📖

Show which of these 2 you have chosen.”

## Acts 1:25

“Choose someone to take this work as an apostle. 📖

Judas left it to go to his own place.”

## Acts 1:26

They cast lots, and Matthias was chosen. 📖

He joined the 11 apostles.

**Help with words:** Casting lots used marked objects to make a choice.
