# Acts 3 — Healing at the temple

**About this chapter**

Peter and John meet a man unable to walk, who is healed in Jesus' name.

Peter explains what happened and calls the crowd to turn back to God.

## Acts 3:1

One afternoon, Peter and John went up to the temple for prayer at the ninth hour. 📖

**Help:** The ninth hour was about three in the afternoon.

## Acts 3:2

A man unable to walk from birth was being carried to the temple's Beautiful Gate. 📖

Every day, he was placed there to beg from people entering the temple courts.

## Acts 3:3

Seeing Peter and John about to enter, he asked for money. 📖

## Acts 3:4

Peter and John looked straight at him, and Peter said, “Look at us!” 📖

## Acts 3:5

He paid attention, expecting something from them. 📖

## Acts 3:6

Peter said, “I have no silver or gold, but I give you what I have. 📖

In Jesus Christ of Nazareth's name, get up and walk!”

## Acts 3:7

Peter took his right hand and helped him up; immediately his feet and ankles became strong. 📖

## Acts 3:8

He sprang up and walked, entering the temple courts with them, walking, leaping, and praising God. 📖

## Acts 3:9

Everyone saw him walking and praising God. 📖

## Acts 3:10

They recognised the man who used to beg at the temple's Beautiful Gate. 📖

What had happened filled them with wonder and amazement.

## Acts 3:11

He held onto Peter and John while the amazed crowd ran to them at Solomon's Colonnade. 📖

**Help:** A colonnade is a covered walkway supported by columns.

## Acts 3:12

Peter said, “People of Israel, why are you surprised? 📖

Why stare as though our own power or godliness made him walk?

## Acts 3:13

Our ancestors' God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, glorified his servant Jesus. 📖

You handed him over and rejected him before Pilate, although Pilate had decided to release him.

## Acts 3:14

You rejected the Holy and Righteous One and asked for a murderer to be released. 📖

## Acts 3:15

You killed life's Author, but God raised him from death; we are witnesses. 📖

## Acts 3:16

Faith in Jesus' name made this man you see and know strong. 📖

Jesus' name and faith coming through him gave this man complete healing before you all.

## Acts 3:17

Brothers, I know you and your leaders acted without understanding. 📖

## Acts 3:18

God fulfilled what he foretold through all the prophets: his Christ would suffer. 📖

## Acts 3:19

So repent and turn back, so your sins may be wiped away. 📖

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

## Acts 3:20

Then refreshing times may come from the Lord's presence, and he may send Jesus, the Christ appointed for you. 📖

## Acts 3:21

Heaven must receive him until the restoration of everything, announced long ago by God through his holy prophets. 📖

## Acts 3:22

Moses said, ‘Your Lord God will raise up a prophet like me from among your brothers. 📖

Listen to everything he tells you.

## Acts 3:23

Anyone who refuses to listen will be completely cut off from their people.’ 📖

## Acts 3:24

Every prophet who spoke from Samuel onwards announced these days. 📖

## Acts 3:25

You are sons of the prophets and of God's covenant with your ancestors. 📖

He told Abraham, ‘Through your offspring, all earth's families will be blessed.’

**Help:** A covenant is a binding agreement or relationship established by God.

Offspring means descendants.

## Acts 3:26

God raised up his Servant and sent him first to you, blessing you by turning each from your wicked ways.” 📖
