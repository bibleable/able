# Romans 1 — The good news and human wrongdoing

**About this chapter**

Paul introduces his letter to the believers in Rome.

He describes the good news and begins his argument about human wrongdoing and God's judgment.

## Romans 1:1

This letter is from Paul, Christ Jesus's servant, called to be an apostle and set apart for God's good news. 📖

**Help with a word:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

## Romans 1:2

God promised this good news beforehand through his prophets in the Holy Scriptures. 📖

**Help with words:** Prophets speak God's message.

The Holy Scriptures are the holy writings.

## Romans 1:3

The good news concerns God's Son, born in David's family line according to the flesh. 📖

## Romans 1:4

Through the Spirit of holiness, his rising from the dead powerfully declared him God's Son. 📖

He is Jesus Christ our Lord.

## Romans 1:5

Through him, we received kindness and apostleship for his name's sake. 📖

We call all Gentiles to the obedience that comes from faith.

**Help with words:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

Faith means trust in Christ here.

## Romans 1:6

You too are among those called to belong to Jesus Christ. 📖

## Romans 1:7

I write to everyone in Rome loved by God and called to be his holy people. 📖

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace.

## Romans 1:8

First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you. 📖

Your faith is being made known throughout the world.

## Romans 1:9

God is my witness how constantly I remember you. 📖

I serve him with my spirit by telling the good news of his Son.

## Romans 1:10

I remember you in my prayers at all times. 📖

I ask that, at last, God's will may allow me to reach you.

## Romans 1:11

I long to see you and share a spiritual gift to strengthen you. 📖

## Romans 1:12

I mean that you and I can encourage each other through each other's faith. 📖

## Romans 1:13

Brothers and sisters, I often planned to come, but have been prevented until now. 📖

I wanted a harvest among you, as among the other Gentiles.

**Help with this picture:** A harvest pictures the results of sharing the good news.

## Romans 1:14

I have an obligation to Greeks and non-Greeks, to wise and foolish people. 📖

## Romans 1:15

So I am eager to tell the good news to you in Rome too. 📖

## Romans 1:16

I am not ashamed of the good news. 📖

It is God's power to save everyone who believes, first the Jew, then the Greek.

## Romans 1:17

It reveals God's righteousness through faith from beginning to end. 📖

The Scriptures say, “The righteous will live by faith.”

**Help with a word:** Righteousness means being right before God and acting rightly.

## Romans 1:18

God's wrath is revealed from heaven against all the godlessness and wrongdoing of people who suppress truth by wrongdoing. 📖

**Help with words:** Wrath means God's anger against wrongdoing.

Suppress means hold down or keep hidden.

## Romans 1:19

What can be known about God is plain to them because God made it plain. 📖

## Romans 1:20

Since creation, God's invisible qualities, his eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly seen through what he made. 📖

So people have no excuse.

**Help with words:** Eternal means lasting for ever.

Divine means belonging to God.

## Romans 1:21

Although they knew God, they did not honour him as God or thank him. 📖

Their thinking became useless, and their foolish hearts grew dark.

## Romans 1:22

They claimed to be wise, but became fools. 📖

## Romans 1:23

They exchanged the glory of God, who cannot die, for images of humans, birds, animals, and reptiles that die. 📖

## Romans 1:24

So God gave them over to their hearts' desires and impurity, dishonouring their bodies with each other. 📖

## Romans 1:25

They exchanged God's truth for a lie and worshipped and served created things instead of the Creator. 📖

He deserves praise for ever!

Amen.

## Romans 1:26

For this reason, God gave them over to dishonourable passions. 📖

Their women exchanged natural sexual relations for unnatural ones.

## Romans 1:27

Likewise, men left natural relations with women and burned with lust for each other. 📖

Men did indecent things with men and received in themselves the penalty due for their error.

**Help with a word:** Lust means strong wrong sexual desire.

## Romans 1:28

They did not consider it fitting to acknowledge God. 📖

So God gave them over to corrupt thinking, to do what should not be done.

## Romans 1:29

They became filled with every kind of wrongdoing, evil, greed, and corruption. 📖

They are full of envy, murder, fighting, deceit, and malice.

They gossip.

**Help with words:** Deceit means tricking people with lies.

Malice means wanting to harm others.

## Romans 1:30

They tell harmful lies, hate God, act rudely and arrogantly, and boast. 📖

They invent new evil and disobey their parents.

## Romans 1:31

They lack sense, faithfulness, love, and mercy. 📖

## Romans 1:32

They know God's righteous decree says those practising these things deserve death. 📖

Yet they keep doing them and approve of others doing them.

**Help with the setting:** The next chapter challenges people who judge others while doing the same things.
