# Romans 10 — Everyone who calls on the Lord

**About this chapter**

Paul prays for Israel's salvation and explains believing, confessing, hearing, and sharing the good news.

## Romans 10:1

Brothers and sisters, my heart's desire and prayer to God is for Israel's salvation. 📖

## Romans 10:2

I testify that they are eager for God, but without knowledge as their basis. 📖

## Romans 10:3

Not knowing God's righteousness, they tried to establish their own. 📖

They did not submit to God's righteousness.

## Romans 10:4

Christ is the end of the law, bringing righteousness to everyone believing. 📖

## Romans 10:5

About righteousness through law, Moses writes, “The person doing these things will live by them.” 📖

## Romans 10:6

Righteousness through faith says, “Do not ask in your heart, ‘Who will go up to heaven?’” 📖

That means bringing Christ down.

## Romans 10:7

“Do not ask, ‘Who will go down into the Abyss?’” 📖

That means bringing Christ up from the dead.

**Help with a word:** Abyss means a very deep place.

## Romans 10:8

What does it say? 📖

“The word is near you, in your mouth and heart.”

This is the word of faith we announce.

## Romans 10:9

Confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart God raised him from death. 📖

If you do this, you will be saved.

## Romans 10:10

With the heart, you believe and are made right. 📖

With the mouth, you confess and are saved.

## Romans 10:11

Scripture says, “Anyone believing in him will never be put to shame.” 📖

## Romans 10:12

There is no difference between Jew and Greek. 📖

The same Lord rules all and gives richly to everyone calling on him.

## Romans 10:13

“Everyone calling on the Lord's name will be saved.” 📖

## Romans 10:14

How can they call on someone they have not believed in? 📖

How can they believe without hearing of him?

How can they hear without someone announcing the message?

## Romans 10:15

How can people announce it unless sent? 📖

The Scriptures say, “How beautiful are the feet of those bringing good news!”

## Romans 10:16

But not everyone welcomed the good news. 📖

Isaiah says, “Lord, who believed our message?”

## Romans 10:17

Faith comes through hearing, and hearing through Christ's word. 📖

## Romans 10:18

Did they not hear? 📖

Indeed they did.

“Their voice went throughout the earth, their words to the world's ends.”

## Romans 10:19

Did Israel not understand? 📖

Moses first says, “I will make you jealous through those not a nation, and angry through a nation without understanding.”

## Romans 10:20

Isaiah boldly says, “Those not seeking me found me; I revealed myself to those not asking for me.” 📖

## Romans 10:21

About Israel, he says, “All day I held out my hands to disobedient, stubborn people.” 📖
