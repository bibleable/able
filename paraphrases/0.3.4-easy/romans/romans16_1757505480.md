# Romans 16 — Greetings and praise

**About this chapter**

Paul commends Phoebe and greets many fellow believers.

He warns against divisive teachers and ends with praise to God.

## Romans 16:1

I commend our sister Phoebe to you. 📖

She serves the church in Cenchrea.

## Romans 16:2

Welcome her in the Lord in a way worthy of God's people. 📖

Help her with anything she needs from you.

She has been a great help to many people, including me.

## Romans 16:3

Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus. 📖

## Romans 16:4

They risked their lives for me. 📖

I am grateful to them, and so are all the Gentile churches.

## Romans 16:5

Also greet the church that meets in their house. 📖

Greet my dear Epenetus, the first person to turn to Christ in the province of Asia.

**Help:** Asia here was a Roman province, rather than the whole continent called Asia today.

## Romans 16:6

Greet Mary, who has worked very hard for you. 📖

## Romans 16:7

Greet Andronicus and Junia, my fellow countrymen and fellow prisoners. 📖

They are outstanding among the apostles.

They belonged to Christ before I did.

**Help:** Fellow countrymen means people from Paul's own nation.

Apostles are people sent out to carry Christ's message.

## Romans 16:8

Greet Ampliatus, my dear friend in the Lord. 📖

## Romans 16:9

Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my dear Stachys. 📖

## Romans 16:10

Greet Apelles, who is approved in Christ. 📖

Greet those in the household of Aristobulus.

## Romans 16:11

Greet Herodion, my fellow countryman. 📖

Greet those in the household of Narcissus who belong to the Lord.

## Romans 16:12

Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, women who have worked hard in the Lord. 📖

Greet my dear Persis, who has worked very hard in the Lord.

## Romans 16:13

Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother. 📖

She has been a mother to me too.

## Romans 16:14

Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers and sisters with them. 📖

## Romans 16:15

Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, Olympas, and all God's people with them. 📖

## Romans 16:16

Greet one another with a holy kiss. 📖

All Christ's churches send you greetings.

**Help:** A holy kiss was a greeting expressing the believers' shared love and belonging to God.

## Romans 16:17

Brothers and sisters, watch out for people who cause divisions and obstacles against the teaching you learned. 📖

Turn away from them.

## Romans 16:18

Such people serve their own appetites instead of our Lord Christ. 📖

Their smooth words and flattery deceive people who easily trust others.

## Romans 16:19

Everyone has heard of your obedience, so I rejoice over you. 📖

I want you to be wise about good and innocent about evil.

## Romans 16:20

The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. 📖

May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

## Romans 16:21

Timothy, my fellow worker, sends greetings. 📖

So do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my fellow countrymen.

## Romans 16:22

I, Tertius, who wrote down this letter, greet you in the Lord. 📖

**Help:** Tertius identifies himself here as the person who wrote down Paul's letter.

## Romans 16:23

Gaius, who has welcomed me and the whole church as guests, sends greetings. 📖

Erastus, the city treasurer, and our brother Quartus also send greetings.

## Romans 16:24

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Romans 16:25

Now praise the one who can strengthen you through my good news and the message about Jesus Christ. 📖

This agrees with the revealing of the mystery kept hidden for ages,

## Romans 16:26

but now revealed and made known through the prophets' writings. 📖

The eternal God commanded this, to lead all nations to the obedience that comes from faith.

## Romans 16:27

To the only wise God be glory for ever through Jesus Christ! 📖

Amen.
