# Romans 8 — Nothing can separate us from God’s love

**About this chapter**

Paul describes life led by God’s Spirit and the hope God gives.

## Romans 8:1

People who belong to Christ Jesus are no longer condemned. 📖

**Help with a word:** Condemned means declared guilty and facing punishment.

## Romans 8:2

In Christ Jesus, the Spirit's law of life set you free from the law of sin and death. 📖

## Romans 8:3

Human weakness kept the law from rescuing us. 📖

God sent his own Son in a human body like ours, as an offering for sin.

In that body, God condemned sin.

## Romans 8:4

He did this so the law's right requirements could be fulfilled in us. 📖

We follow the Spirit rather than sinful human desires.

## Romans 8:5

People who follow sinful desires fill their minds with those things. 📖

People who follow the Spirit think about what the Spirit wants.

## Romans 8:6

A mind ruled by sinful desires leads to death. 📖

A mind ruled by the Spirit brings life and peace.

## Romans 8:7

A mind ruled by sinful desires is God's enemy. 📖

It does not and cannot obey God's law.

## Romans 8:8

People controlled by sinful desires cannot please God. 📖

## Romans 8:9

If God's Spirit lives in you, the Spirit controls you rather than sinful desires. 📖

Anyone without Christ's Spirit does not belong to Christ.

## Romans 8:10

If Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin. 📖

Yet your spirit is alive because you are right with God.

## Romans 8:11

God raised Jesus from death. 📖

If God's Spirit lives in you, he will give life to your bodies that can die.

He will do this through his Spirit living in you.

## Romans 8:12

Brothers and sisters, we have a duty. 📖

But it is not a duty to follow sinful desires.

## Romans 8:13

If you follow sinful desires, you will die. 📖

If the Spirit helps you put your wrong actions to death, you will live.

**Help with this picture:** Putting actions to death means stopping them, not harming your body.

## Romans 8:14

Everyone led by God's Spirit is God's child. 📖

## Romans 8:15

You did not receive a spirit that makes you a slave to fear again. 📖

You received the Spirit who makes you God's children.

Through him we cry, “Abba, Father!”

**Help with a word:** Abba is a word for father.

## Romans 8:16

The Spirit himself joins with our spirit to confirm that we are God's children. 📖

## Romans 8:17

As God's children, we share what he gives his family. 📖

We share with Christ, if we also suffer with him.

Then we will share his glory.

## Romans 8:18

Our present suffering is nothing compared with the glory to be shown in us. 📖

## Romans 8:19

Creation eagerly waits for God's children to be revealed. 📖

## Romans 8:20

Creation was made subject to frustration, not by its own choice. 📖

The one who subjected it also gave hope.

## Romans 8:21

Creation will be freed from its slavery to decay. 📖

It will share the glorious freedom of God's children.

## Romans 8:22

All creation has been groaning together like a woman in childbirth. 📖

It is still doing so now.

## Romans 8:23

We also groan within ourselves. 📖

We have the Spirit as the first part of God's gift.

We eagerly wait for our full adoption and the rescue of our bodies.

## Romans 8:24

We were saved in this hope. 📖

Hope is for something not yet seen.

No one hopes for what they already see.

## Romans 8:25

If we hope for what we do not yet see, we wait patiently. 📖

## Romans 8:26

The Spirit helps us in our weakness. 📖

We do not know how to pray as we should.

The Spirit prays for us with groans beyond words.

## Romans 8:27

God searches our hearts and knows the Spirit's mind. 📖

The Spirit prays for God's people according to God's will.

## Romans 8:28

We know God works everything together for the good of those who love him. 📖

They are called according to his purpose.

## Romans 8:29

God knew these people beforehand. 📖

He planned for them to become like his Son.

His Son would be the firstborn among many brothers and sisters.

## Romans 8:30

Those God planned for, he also called. 📖

Those he called, he also made right with himself.

Those he made right, he also glorified.

**Help with a word:** Glorified means given a share in God's honour and greatness.

## Romans 8:31

What shall we say about this? 📖

If God is for us, who can stand against us?

## Romans 8:32

God did not spare his own Son, but gave him up for us all. 📖

Will he not also freely give us everything with him?

## Romans 8:33

Who can accuse the people God has chosen? 📖

God is the one who makes them right with himself.

## Romans 8:34

Who can condemn them? 📖

Christ Jesus died and was raised to life.

He is at God's right hand, praying for us.

## Romans 8:35

Can anything separate us from Christ's love? 📖

Can trouble, distress, attacks, hunger, lack of clothing, danger, or war?

## Romans 8:36

Scripture says, “For your sake we face death all day. 📖

We are treated like sheep being taken to be killed.”

## Romans 8:37

In all these things, we win more than victory through the one who loved us. 📖

## Romans 8:38

I am certain that death, life, angels, rulers, the present, the future, and spiritual powers cannot separate us. 📖

## Romans 8:39

Neither can height, depth, or anything else in all creation. 📖

Nothing can separate us from God's love in Christ Jesus our Lord.
