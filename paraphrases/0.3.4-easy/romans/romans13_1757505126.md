# Romans 13 — Authority, love, and the coming day

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches about governing authorities, love for neighbours, and living ready for salvation.

## Romans 13:1

Everyone must submit to the governing authorities. 📖

No authority exists except from God.

God has appointed the authorities that exist.

## Romans 13:2

So anyone who resists authority opposes what God has put in place. 📖

Those who do this bring judgment on themselves.

## Romans 13:3

Rulers cause fear for bad conduct, rather than good conduct. 📖

Do you want to be free from fear of the ruler?

Do what is right, and you will receive his approval.

## Romans 13:4

He is God's servant for your good. 📖

But if you do wrong, be afraid.

He does not carry the sword without a purpose.

He is God's servant to bring punishment on the person doing wrong.

## Romans 13:5

So you must submit to authority, both to avoid punishment and because of your conscience. 📖

**Help:** Your conscience is your sense of right and wrong.

## Romans 13:6

This is also why you pay taxes. 📖

The authorities are God's servants, giving their time to this work.

## Romans 13:7

Give everyone what you owe them. 📖

Pay taxes and other payments to those you owe.

Give respect to those you owe respect, and honour to those you owe honour.

## Romans 13:8

Owe no one anything, except the duty to love one another. 📖

Anyone who loves their neighbour has fulfilled the law.

## Romans 13:9

The commands say, “Do not commit adultery,” “Do not murder,” “Do not steal,” and “Do not covet.” 📖

These and every other command are summed up in this: “Love your neighbour as yourself.”

**Help:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

To covet means to desire what belongs to someone else.

## Romans 13:10

Love does no wrong to a neighbour. 📖

So love fulfils the law.

## Romans 13:11

Do this while understanding the time. 📖

The hour has come to wake from sleep.

Our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed.

## Romans 13:12

The night is nearly over, and the day is near. 📖

Let us put aside the deeds of darkness and put on the armour of light.

**Help:** Darkness pictures evil deeds here.

Armour of light pictures being equipped to live rightly.

## Romans 13:13

Let us behave properly, as in daylight. 📖

Avoid wild parties and drunkenness, sexual wrongdoing and shameless excess, quarrels and jealousy.

## Romans 13:14

Instead, clothe yourselves with the Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

Do not make plans to satisfy the desires of the flesh.

**Help:** Clothing yourself with Christ pictures taking on his way of life.

The flesh here refers to the sinful desires Paul warns against.
