# Romans 14 — Welcome one another

**About this chapter**

Paul addresses disagreements about food and special days.

He teaches believers to avoid judging each other and causing each other to stumble.

## Romans 14:1

Accept the person whose faith is weak, without judging their opinions. 📖

## Romans 14:2

One person has faith to eat anything. 📖

Another, whose faith is weak, eats only vegetables.

## Romans 14:3

Those who eat everything must not look down on those who do not. 📖

Those who do not eat everything must not judge those who do.

God has accepted them.

## Romans 14:4

Who are you to judge someone else's servant? 📖

Their own master decides whether they stand or fall.

They will stand, because the Lord can make them stand.

## Romans 14:5

One person considers one day more important than another. 📖

Someone else considers every day alike.

Each person should be fully convinced in their own mind.

## Romans 14:6

Anyone who observes a special day does so for the Lord. 📖

Anyone who eats does so for the Lord, giving thanks to God.

Anyone who does not eat does so for the Lord and gives thanks to God.

## Romans 14:7

None of us lives only for ourselves, and none of us dies only for ourselves. 📖

## Romans 14:8

If we live, we live for the Lord. 📖

If we die, we die for the Lord.

Whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord.

## Romans 14:9

Christ died and returned to life to be Lord of both the dead and the living. 📖

## Romans 14:10

Why do you judge your brother or sister? 📖

Why do you look down on them?

We will all stand before God's judgment seat.

## Romans 14:11

The Scriptures say, “As surely as I live, says the Lord, every knee will bow before me. 📖

Every tongue will confess to God.”

## Romans 14:12

So each of us will answer to God for ourselves. 📖

## Romans 14:13

Let us stop judging one another. 📖

Decide instead to put no stumbling block or obstacle in your brother or sister's way.

**Help:** A stumbling block pictures something that leads another person into sin.

## Romans 14:14

In the Lord Jesus, I am fully convinced that nothing is unclean in itself. 📖

But if someone considers something unclean, it is unclean for that person.

**Help:** Unclean here means considered unacceptable to God, rather than physically dirty.

## Romans 14:15

If your food distresses your brother or sister, you are no longer acting in love. 📖

Do not destroy someone Christ died for through what you eat.

## Romans 14:16

Do not let what you consider good be spoken of as evil. 📖

## Romans 14:17

God's kingdom concerns righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit, rather than eating and drinking. 📖

**Help:** Righteousness means being right with God and living rightly.

## Romans 14:18

Anyone who serves Christ in this way pleases God and receives people's approval. 📖

## Romans 14:19

Let us pursue what brings peace and helps us build each other up. 📖

## Romans 14:20

Do not destroy God's work for the sake of food. 📖

All food is clean.

But it is wrong to eat in a way that causes someone to stumble.

## Romans 14:21

It is better to avoid meat, wine, or anything else that causes your brother or sister to stumble. 📖

## Romans 14:22

Keep your belief about these matters between yourself and God. 📖

Blessed is the person who does not condemn themselves by what they approve.

## Romans 14:23

But anyone who doubts is condemned if they eat, because their eating does not come from faith. 📖

Everything that does not come from faith is sin.
