# Romans 6 — Alive to God

**About this chapter**

Paul explains sharing in Christ's death and life.

He uses slavery as a picture of serving sin or serving God.

## Romans 6:1

What shall we say? 📖

Should we keep sinning so kindness increases?

## Romans 6:2

Certainly not! 📖

We died to sin, so how can we keep living in it?

## Romans 6:3

Do you not know that everyone baptized into Christ Jesus was baptized into his death? 📖

## Romans 6:4

Through baptism, we were buried with him into death. 📖

Christ was raised from the dead through the Father's glory so we too could live a new life.

## Romans 6:5

If we have been united with him in a death like his, we will also share his resurrection. 📖

## Romans 6:6

Our old self was crucified with him so the body of sin could be made powerless. 📖

Then we would no longer be sin's slaves.

## Romans 6:7

Anyone who has died has been freed from sin. 📖

## Romans 6:8

If we died with Christ, we believe we will also live with him. 📖

## Romans 6:9

We know Christ cannot die again now that he has been raised. 📖

Death no longer rules over him.

## Romans 6:10

He died to sin once for all. 📖

The life he lives, he lives to God.

## Romans 6:11

Count yourselves dead to sin, but alive to God in Christ Jesus. 📖

## Romans 6:12

Do not let sin rule your body that can die, making you obey its desires. 📖

## Romans 6:13

Do not offer your body parts to sin as tools for wrongdoing. 📖

Offer yourselves to God as people brought from death to life.

Offer him your body parts as tools for righteousness.

## Romans 6:14

Sin will not be your master, because you are under kindness rather than law. 📖

## Romans 6:15

Should we sin because we are under kindness rather than law? 📖

Certainly not!

## Romans 6:16

When you offer yourselves as obedient slaves, you serve the one you obey. 📖

You are either slaves to sin leading to death, or to obedience leading to righteousness.

## Romans 6:17

Thank God! 📖

Although you were once sin's slaves, you wholeheartedly obeyed the pattern of teaching to which you were committed.

## Romans 6:18

You were freed from sin and became slaves to righteousness. 📖

## Romans 6:19

I use human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. 📖

You once offered your body parts as slaves to impurity and increasing wrongdoing.

Now offer them as slaves to righteousness leading to holiness.

## Romans 6:20

When you were sin's slaves, you were free from obligation to righteousness. 📖

## Romans 6:21

What fruit came from the things you now feel ashamed of? 📖

Their outcome is death.

## Romans 6:22

Now you are freed from sin and serve God as slaves. 📖

The fruit leads to holiness, and the outcome is life lasting for ever.

## Romans 6:23

Sin's wages are death. 📖

God's gift is life lasting for ever in Christ Jesus our Lord.

**Help with these pictures:** Wages are earned pay, while a gift is freely given.

Fruit pictures the results of a way of life.
