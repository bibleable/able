# Romans 12 — Offer your life to God

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches believers how to live because of God’s mercy.

## Romans 12:1

Brothers and sisters, remember God's mercy. 📖

Offer your bodies as living sacrifices, set apart and pleasing to God.

This is your spiritual worship.

**Help with this picture:** A living sacrifice means giving your whole life to serve God.

## Romans 12:2

Do not copy this world's ways. 📖

Let God change you by making your mind new.

Then you can recognise God's good, pleasing, and perfect will.

## Romans 12:3

By God's kindness to me, I tell everyone not to think too highly of themselves. 📖

Judge yourself honestly, according to the faith God has given you.

## Romans 12:4

One body has many parts. 📖

The parts do different jobs.

## Romans 12:5

In Christ, we are many people but one body. 📖

We belong to each other.

## Romans 12:6

God's kindness gives us different gifts. 📖

If your gift is speaking God's message, use it according to your faith.

## Romans 12:7

If your gift is serving, serve. 📖

If it is teaching, teach.

## Romans 12:8

If your gift is encouraging, encourage. 📖

If it is giving, give generously.

If it is leading, work hard.

If it is showing mercy, do it gladly.

## Romans 12:9

Love sincerely. 📖

Hate evil and hold firmly to what is good.

## Romans 12:10

Love each other as family. 📖

Be eager to honour each other.

## Romans 12:11

Do not become lazy in your eagerness. 📖

Keep your spirit full of energy as you serve the Lord.

## Romans 12:12

Be joyful in hope. 📖

Be patient in trouble.

Keep praying.

## Romans 12:13

Share with God's people who need help. 📖

Welcome guests into your home.

## Romans 12:14

Ask God to bless those who attack you. 📖

Bless them rather than curse them.

## Romans 12:15

Be glad with those who are glad. 📖

Cry with those who cry.

## Romans 12:16

Live peacefully together. 📖

Do not be proud.

Spend time with people of low position.

Do not think you know everything.

## Romans 12:17

Do not repay evil with evil. 📖

Take care to do what everyone can recognise as right.

## Romans 12:18

As far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone if possible. 📖

## Romans 12:19

Dear friends, do not take revenge. 📖

Leave punishment to God.

Scripture says, “The Lord says punishment belongs to me.

I will repay.”

## Romans 12:20

“Feed your enemy if hungry. 📖

Give your enemy a drink if thirsty.

By doing so, you will heap burning coals on your enemy's head.”

**Help with this picture:** The coals describe the powerful effect of kindness.

The instruction is to help your enemy, not burn them.

## Romans 12:21

Do not let evil defeat you. 📖

Defeat evil with good.
