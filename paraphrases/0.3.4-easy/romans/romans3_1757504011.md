# Romans 3 — Everyone needs God’s rescue

**About this chapter**

Paul explains that both Jews and Gentiles need rescue through Jesus.

Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Romans 3:1

What advantage is there in being Jewish? 📖

What value is there in circumcision?

**Help with a word:** Circumcision is a physical sign of Jewish identity.

## Romans 3:2

There are many advantages. 📖

First, God trusted the Jewish people with his own words.

## Romans 3:3

Some were unfaithful. 📖

Does their unfaithfulness cancel God's faithfulness?

## Romans 3:4

Certainly not! 📖

God tells the truth, even if everyone else lies.

Scripture says God is proved right when he speaks and wins when he judges.

## Romans 3:5

Our wrong actions may show God's goodness more clearly. 📖

Does that make God unfair to punish us?

I am speaking as people might argue.

## Romans 3:6

Certainly not! 📖

If God were unfair, how could he judge the world?

## Romans 3:7

Someone might ask, “If my lie makes God's truth clearer and brings him honour, why am I judged a sinner?” 📖

## Romans 3:8

Why not say, “Let us do evil so that good will come”? 📖

Some falsely accuse us of teaching this.

Those who say this deserve judgment.

## Romans 3:9

Are we Jews better off in this matter? 📖

Not at all.

We have already shown that Jews and Greeks are all under sin's power.

## Romans 3:10

Scripture says, “No one is righteous, not even one.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Righteous means right before God.

## Romans 3:11

“No one understands. 📖

No one seeks God.”

## Romans 3:12

“Everyone has turned away. 📖

Together they have become useless.

No one does good, not even one.”

## Romans 3:13

“Their throats are like open graves. 📖

Their tongues tell lies.

Their lips carry snake poison.”

**Help with these pictures:** Their words spread lies and deadly harm.

## Romans 3:14

“Their mouths are full of curses and bitter words.” 📖

## Romans 3:15

“They hurry to kill people.” 📖

## Romans 3:16

“They leave destruction and misery behind them.” 📖

## Romans 3:17

“They do not know the way of peace.” 📖

## Romans 3:18

“They have no fear of God.” 📖

## Romans 3:19

The law speaks to those under it. 📖

Then no one can make excuses.

The whole world must answer to God.

## Romans 3:20

No one becomes right before God by doing the law's works. 📖

The law shows us our sin.

## Romans 3:21

Now God has shown a way to be right with him apart from the law. 📖

The Law and the Prophets speak about it.

## Romans 3:22

This righteousness comes from God through faith in Jesus Christ to everyone who believes. 📖

There is no difference between groups.

## Romans 3:23

Everyone has sinned. 📖

Everyone falls short of God's glory.

**Help with words:** We fail to share fully in the goodness and honour God intends.

## Romans 3:24

God freely makes people right with him through his kindness. 📖

Christ Jesus paid the price to set them free.

## Romans 3:25

God gave Jesus as the sacrifice dealing with sin through his blood, received by faith. 📖

This showed God's justice.

God had patiently held back punishment for earlier sins.

## Romans 3:26

God did this to show his justice now. 📖

He is just, and makes those who trust Jesus right with him.

## Romans 3:27

Then no one can boast. 📖

This is because of faith, rather than works.

## Romans 3:28

We hold that a person is made right with God by faith, apart from doing the law's works. 📖

## Romans 3:29

Is God only the God of Jews? 📖

No, he is the God of Gentiles too.

## Romans 3:30

There is only one God. 📖

He makes circumcised and uncircumcised people right with him through the same faith.

## Romans 3:31

Does faith cancel the law? 📖

Certainly not!

Instead, we uphold the law.
