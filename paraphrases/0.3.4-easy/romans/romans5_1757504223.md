# Romans 5 — Peace with God and life through Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul describes peace and hope through Jesus.

He compares Adam's disobedience with Christ's obedience.

## Romans 5:1

Made right through faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## Romans 5:2

Through him, faith gives us access to this kindness in which we stand. 📖

We rejoice in the hope of God's glory.

## Romans 5:3

We also rejoice in suffering, knowing that suffering produces endurance. 📖

## Romans 5:4

Endurance produces tested character, and tested character produces hope. 📖

## Romans 5:5

Hope does not disappoint us. 📖

God poured his love into our hearts through the Holy Spirit he gave us.

## Romans 5:6

At just the right time, while we were still powerless, Christ died for the ungodly. 📖

## Romans 5:7

Rarely will anyone die for a righteous person. 📖

Yet someone might dare to die for a good person.

## Romans 5:8

God proves his love this way: Christ died for us while we were still sinners. 📖

## Romans 5:9

Now made right by his blood, how much more will we be saved from wrath through him! 📖

## Romans 5:10

If his Son's death brought us back while we were God's enemies, how much more will his life save us! 📖

We have now been brought back to God.

## Romans 5:11

We also rejoice in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we received reconciliation. 📖

**Help with a word:** Reconciliation means enemies being brought back into a right relationship.

## Romans 5:12

Sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin. 📖

Death spread to everyone because everyone sinned.

## Romans 5:13

Sin existed before the law was given. 📖

But sin is not counted when there is no law.

## Romans 5:14

Death ruled from Adam to Moses, even over people who did not sin as Adam did. 📖

Adam is a pattern of the One to come.

## Romans 5:15

The gift is different from the wrongdoing. 📖

If many died through one man's wrongdoing, how much more did God's kindness and gift overflow to many!

The gift came through the kindness of one man, Jesus Christ.

## Romans 5:16

The gift differs from the result of one man's sin. 📖

Judgment after one sin brought condemnation.

But the gift after many wrongdoings brought justification.

**Help with words:** Condemnation means being judged guilty.

Justification means being declared right before God.

## Romans 5:17

If death ruled through one man's wrongdoing, how much more will those receiving God's gifts rule in life! 📖

They receive his abundant kindness and the gift of righteousness.

This comes through the one man, Jesus Christ.

## Romans 5:18

One wrongdoing brought condemnation to all people. 📖

In the same way, one righteous act brought justification and life for all people.

## Romans 5:19

Through one man's disobedience, the many became sinners. 📖

Through one man's obedience, the many will be made righteous.

## Romans 5:20

The law came so wrongdoing would increase. 📖

But where sin increased, kindness increased even more.

## Romans 5:21

Sin ruled in death. 📖

So kindness might rule through righteousness, bringing life lasting for ever through Jesus Christ our Lord.
