# Romans 2 — God judges fairly

**About this chapter**

Paul warns people who judge others while doing the same wrongs.

He discusses Jews and Gentiles.

Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Romans 2:1

You have no excuse when you judge another person. 📖

You do the same things.

By judging them, you condemn yourself.

## Romans 2:2

We know God judges those actions according to truth. 📖

## Romans 2:3

You judge others but do the same things. 📖

Do you think you will escape God's judgment?

## Romans 2:4

Do you ignore God's great kindness, patience, and willingness to wait? 📖

His kindness is meant to lead you to turn from wrong.

## Romans 2:5

Your heart is stubborn, and you refuse to change. 📖

You are storing up punishment for the day God reveals his fair judgment.

## Romans 2:6

God will repay each person according to their actions. 📖

## Romans 2:7

Some keep doing good and seek glory, honour, and life that cannot die. 📖

God will give them life that lasts forever.

## Romans 2:8

Others seek only their own interests. 📖

They reject truth and follow evil.

They will face God's anger.

## Romans 2:9

Everyone who does evil will suffer trouble and distress. 📖

This applies first to Jews, then to Greeks.

## Romans 2:10

Everyone who does good will receive glory, honour, and peace. 📖

This applies first to Jews, then to Greeks.

## Romans 2:11

God does not favour one person over another. 📖

## Romans 2:12

People who sin without knowing the law will perish without it. 📖

People who sin under the law will be judged by it.

**Help with a word:** Sin means doing wrong against God.

## Romans 2:13

Hearing the law does not make people right before God. 📖

Those who obey it will be declared right.

## Romans 2:14

Gentiles do not have the law. 📖

But when they naturally do what it requires, they show a law working within them.

## Romans 2:15

They show that the law's requirements are written in their hearts. 📖

Their conscience and thoughts accuse them or defend them.

**Help with a word:** Conscience is the inner sense of right and wrong.

## Romans 2:16

My good news says that God will judge people's secrets through Christ Jesus on that day. 📖

## Romans 2:17

Suppose you call yourself a Jew. 📖

You rely on the law and boast about belonging to God.

## Romans 2:18

You know what God wants. 📖

The law teaches you to recognise what is best.

## Romans 2:19

You are sure you can guide blind people and give light to those in darkness. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Blindness and darkness picture a lack of understanding.

## Romans 2:20

You think you can instruct foolish people and teach beginners. 📖

You have knowledge and truth in the law.

## Romans 2:21

You teach others. 📖

Do you teach yourself?

You tell others not to steal.

Do you steal?

## Romans 2:22

You forbid adultery. 📖

Do you commit it?

You hate idols.

Do you rob temples?

**Help with words:** Adultery means having sex with someone married to another person.

Idols are objects worshipped as gods.

## Romans 2:23

You boast about the law. 📖

Do you dishonour God by breaking it?

## Romans 2:24

Scripture says, “Because of you, Gentiles speak against God's name.” 📖

## Romans 2:25

Circumcision has value if you obey the law. 📖

If you break the law, it is as though you were not circumcised.

**Help with a word:** Circumcision removes skin from the end of a male's penis.

It was a physical sign of belonging to God's people.

## Romans 2:26

If an uncircumcised man obeys the law, will God not count him as circumcised? 📖

## Romans 2:27

An uncircumcised person who obeys will condemn you. 📖

You have the written law and circumcision, but break the law.

## Romans 2:28

Being Jewish is not only something outward. 📖

True circumcision is not only a physical mark.

## Romans 2:29

Being Jewish is something inward. 📖

True circumcision is a change of heart by the Spirit, beyond the written law.

Such a person receives praise from God, rather than people.
