# Romans 15 — Hope and Paul's plans

**About this chapter**

Paul urges believers to support one another.

He explains his work among the Gentiles and his plans to travel.

## Romans 15:1

We who are strong should bear with the weaknesses of those who are weak, rather than please ourselves. 📖

## Romans 15:2

Each of us should please our neighbour for their good, to build them up. 📖

## Romans 15:3

Even Christ did not please himself. 📖

The Scriptures say, “The insults of those who insult you have fallen on me.”

## Romans 15:4

Everything written in the past was written to teach us. 📖

Through endurance and the encouragement of the Scriptures, we can have hope.

## Romans 15:5

May God, who gives endurance and encouragement, help you live in harmony with one another in Christ Jesus. 📖

## Romans 15:6

Then, with one mind and one voice, you can glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

**Help:** To glorify God means to give him praise and honour.

## Romans 15:7

Accept one another, just as Christ accepted you, to bring glory to God. 📖

## Romans 15:8

I tell you that Christ became a servant of the circumcised to uphold God's truth. 📖

He did this to confirm the promises made to their ancestors,

**Help:** The circumcised here means the Jewish people.

The ancestors are the founding fathers of Israel, such as Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

## Romans 15:9

and so Gentiles could glorify God for his mercy. 📖

The Scriptures say, “Therefore I will praise you among the Gentiles.

I will sing songs of praise to your name.”

**Help:** Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## Romans 15:10

Again, the Scriptures say, “Gentiles, rejoice with his people.” 📖

## Romans 15:11

Again, “Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles. 📖

Praise him highly, all you peoples.”

## Romans 15:12

Isaiah also says, “The Root of Jesse will appear. 📖

He will rise to rule over the Gentiles.

The Gentiles will put their hope in him.”

**Help:** Root of Jesse is a picture pointing to Jesus, who comes from Jesse's family.

## Romans 15:13

May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe in him. 📖

Then you can overflow with hope through the Holy Spirit's power.

## Romans 15:14

My brothers and sisters, I am convinced you are full of goodness and knowledge, able to teach one another. 📖

## Romans 15:15

But I have written boldly to remind you of some things, because of the kindness God gave me. 📖

## Romans 15:16

He gave me this kindness to serve Christ Jesus among the Gentiles. 📖

I serve God's good news like a priest, so the Gentiles can become an offering God accepts.

The Holy Spirit makes them holy.

## Romans 15:17

So I rejoice in Christ Jesus in my service to God. 📖

## Romans 15:18

I will speak only of what Christ has done through me. 📖

He has led Gentiles to obey through words and deeds,

## Romans 15:19

through powerful signs and wonders, and through the power of God's Spirit. 📖

From Jerusalem all the way round to Illyricum, I have fully preached Christ's good news.

**Help:** Signs and wonders are powerful acts that show God's power.

Illyricum was a region beside the Adriatic Sea.

## Romans 15:20

My aim has been to preach the good news where Christ was unknown. 📖

Then I would not be building on someone else's foundation.

**Help:** Building on another person's foundation pictures continuing work another person began.

## Romans 15:21

Instead, the Scriptures say, “Those who were not told about him will see. 📖

Those who have not heard will understand.”

## Romans 15:22

This is why I have often been prevented from coming to you. 📖

## Romans 15:23

But now there are no further opportunities for me in these regions. 📖

For many years, I have wanted to visit you.

## Romans 15:24

I hope to see you on my way to Spain. 📖

After enjoying your company for a while, I hope you will equip me for my journey.

## Romans 15:25

Now, however, I am going to Jerusalem to serve the believers there. 📖

## Romans 15:26

Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to give money for the poor among the believers in Jerusalem. 📖

**Help:** Macedonia and Achaia were regions with churches.

## Romans 15:27

They were pleased to give, and they owe this help to those believers. 📖

If Gentiles have shared their spiritual blessings, they have a duty to share material blessings with them.

## Romans 15:28

After finishing this service and safely delivering this gift, I will leave for Spain and visit you on the way. 📖

## Romans 15:29

I know that when I come, I will come with the full blessing of Christ. 📖

## Romans 15:30

Brothers and sisters, I appeal to you through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the Spirit's love. 📖

Join my struggle by praying to God for me.

## Romans 15:31

Pray that I will be rescued from the unbelievers in Judea. 📖

Pray also that my service in Jerusalem will be accepted by the believers there,

## Romans 15:32

so that, by God's will, I can come to you with joy. 📖

Then we can be refreshed together.

## Romans 15:33

May the God of peace be with you all. 📖

Amen.
