# Romans 11 — God's gifts and call remain

**About this chapter**

Paul discusses Israel and Gentile believers.

He uses an olive tree as a picture and warns Gentiles against pride.

## Romans 11:1

Did God reject his people? 📖

Certainly not!

I am an Israelite, Abraham's descendant from Benjamin's tribe.

## Romans 11:2

God did not reject his people, whom he knew beforehand. 📖

Do you not know how Scripture describes Elijah appealing to God against Israel?

## Romans 11:3

“Lord, they killed your prophets and destroyed your altars. 📖

Only I remain, and they seek my life too.”

## Romans 11:4

What did God answer? 📖

“I kept seven thousand men for myself who have not bowed to Baal.”

## Romans 11:5

So now too, a remnant has been chosen by kindness. 📖

**Help with a word:** A remnant is a group remaining from a larger people.

## Romans 11:6

If it is by kindness, it is no longer by works. 📖

Otherwise, kindness would no longer be kindness.

## Romans 11:7

Israel did not obtain what it sought, but the chosen did. 📖

The others were hardened.

## Romans 11:8

The Scriptures say, “God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes not seeing and ears not hearing, to this day.” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Stupor pictures being unresponsive to God's truth.

## Romans 11:9

David says, “Let their table become a snare, trap, stumbling block, and punishment for them.” 📖

## Romans 11:10

“Let their eyes be darkened so they cannot see, and their backs bent for ever.” 📖

## Romans 11:11

Did they stumble beyond recovery? 📖

Certainly not!

Through their wrongdoing, salvation reached Gentiles to make Israel jealous.

## Romans 11:12

If their wrongdoing brings riches to the world and failure brings riches to Gentiles, how much more their fullness brings! 📖

## Romans 11:13

I speak to you Gentiles. 📖

As apostle to the Gentiles, I honour my ministry.

## Romans 11:14

I hope to make my own people jealous and save some of them. 📖

## Romans 11:15

If their rejection reconciles the world, what will their acceptance be but life from death? 📖

## Romans 11:16

If the first dough is holy, the whole batch is holy. 📖

If the root is holy, the branches are holy too.

## Romans 11:17

Some branches were broken off, and you, a wild olive shoot, were grafted among the others. 📖

You share the olive root's nourishment.

**Help with these words:** Grafting joins a branch to another tree so it can grow there.

## Romans 11:18

Do not boast over the branches. 📖

Remember, you do not support the root; the root supports you.

## Romans 11:19

You will say, “Branches were broken off so I could be grafted in.” 📖

## Romans 11:20

True, they were broken off through unbelief, but you stand through faith. 📖

Be afraid, rather than arrogant.

## Romans 11:21

If God did not spare the natural branches, he will not spare you either. 📖

## Romans 11:22

Notice God's kindness and severity. 📖

Severity is for those who fell, but kindness for you if you continue in his kindness.

Otherwise, you too will be cut off.

## Romans 11:23

If they do not persist in unbelief, they will be grafted in again, because God can do this. 📖

## Romans 11:24

You were cut from a wild olive and, against nature, grafted into a cultivated tree. 📖

How much more readily will natural branches return to their own olive tree!

## Romans 11:25

Brothers and sisters, know this mystery so you will not be proud. 📖

Part of Israel has been hardened until the full number of Gentiles comes in.

## Romans 11:26

And so all Israel will be saved. 📖

The Scriptures say, “The Deliverer will come from Zion and remove godlessness from Jacob.”

## Romans 11:27

“This is my covenant with them when I take away their sins.” 📖

## Romans 11:28

Regarding the good news, they are enemies for your sake. 📖

Regarding election, they are loved for the ancestors' sake.

## Romans 11:29

God's gifts and call cannot be taken back. 📖

## Romans 11:30

You once disobeyed God but now received mercy through their disobedience. 📖

## Romans 11:31

They too now disobeyed so they too may receive mercy through the mercy shown to you. 📖

## Romans 11:32

God handed everyone over to disobedience so he could show mercy to everyone. 📖

## Romans 11:33

How deep are God's riches of wisdom and knowledge! 📖

His judgments cannot be searched out, nor his ways traced.

## Romans 11:34

“Who has known the Lord's mind or been his adviser?” 📖

## Romans 11:35

“Who gave to God first, so God should repay them?” 📖

## Romans 11:36

All things are from him, through him, and to him. 📖

May he have glory for ever!

Amen.
