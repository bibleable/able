# Romans 7 — The law and the struggle with sin

**About this chapter**

Paul uses marriage as an example of release from the law.

He uses “I” to describe the struggle with sin.

## Romans 7:1

Brothers and sisters, I speak to people who know the law. 📖

Do you not know the law rules a person only while that person lives?

## Romans 7:2

A married woman is bound by law to her husband while he lives. 📖

If he dies, she is released from the marriage law.

## Romans 7:3

If she joins another man while her husband lives, she is called an adulteress. 📖

If her husband dies, she is free from that law and is not an adulteress when marrying another man.

## Romans 7:4

Brothers and sisters, you too died to the law through Christ's body. 📖

You can belong to another, the One raised from the dead, and bear fruit for God.

## Romans 7:5

When we lived according to the flesh, sinful passions stirred by the law worked in our bodies, bearing death's fruit. 📖

**Help with these pictures:** Flesh here describes human life ruled by sinful desires.

Fruit pictures the results of that life.

## Romans 7:6

Now we have died to what held us and have been released from the law. 📖

We serve in the Spirit's new way, rather than the written code's old way.

## Romans 7:7

Is the law sin? 📖

Certainly not!

I would not have known sin without the law.

I would not have known coveting unless the law said, “Do not covet.”

**Help with a word:** Coveting means wrongly wanting what belongs to others.

## Romans 7:8

Sin took its chance through the command and produced every kind of covetous desire in me. 📖

Apart from law, sin is dead.

## Romans 7:9

Once I lived apart from law. 📖

When the command came, sin sprang to life, and I died.

## Romans 7:10

I found that the command meant for life actually brought death. 📖

## Romans 7:11

Sin took its chance through the command, deceived me, and through it put me to death. 📖

## Romans 7:12

So the law is holy, and the command is holy, righteous, and good. 📖

## Romans 7:13

Did something good become death to me? 📖

Certainly not!

Sin produced death in me through what was good, exposing sin as sin.

Through the command, sin became utterly sinful.

## Romans 7:14

The law is spiritual, but I am unspiritual, sold as sin's slave. 📖

## Romans 7:15

I do not understand what I do. 📖

I do not do what I want, but do what I hate.

## Romans 7:16

If I do what I do not want, I admit the law is good. 📖

## Romans 7:17

Then it is no longer I doing it, but sin living in me. 📖

## Romans 7:18

Nothing good lives in me, that is, in my flesh. 📖

I want to do good but cannot carry it out.

## Romans 7:19

I do not do the good I want. 📖

Instead, I keep doing the evil I do not want.

## Romans 7:20

If I do what I do not want, it is no longer I doing it, but sin living in me. 📖

## Romans 7:21

I find this principle: when I want to do good, evil is right there with me. 📖

## Romans 7:22

In my inner being, I delight in God's law. 📖

## Romans 7:23

But another law works in my body, fighting my mind's law and making me captive to sin's law within me. 📖

## Romans 7:24

What a wretched man I am! 📖

Who will rescue me from this body of death?

## Romans 7:25

Thank God, through Jesus Christ our Lord! 📖

My mind serves God's law, but my flesh serves sin's law.
