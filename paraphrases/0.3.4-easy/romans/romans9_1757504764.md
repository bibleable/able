# Romans 9 — God's promise and mercy

**About this chapter**

Paul grieves for his own people, Israel.

He discusses God's promise, choice, mercy, and judgment.

## Romans 9:1

I speak truth in Christ, not lies. 📖

My conscience confirms this in the Holy Spirit.

## Romans 9:2

I have deep sorrow and pain that never stops in my heart. 📖

## Romans 9:3

I could wish myself cursed and cut off from Christ for my brothers, my own flesh and blood. 📖

## Romans 9:4

They are Israel's people. 📖

Theirs are adoption, divine glory, covenants, the giving of the law, temple worship, and promises.

## Romans 9:5

Theirs are the ancestors, and Christ's human family line comes from them. 📖

He is God over all, worthy of praise for ever!

Amen.

## Romans 9:6

God's word has not failed. 📖

Not everyone descended from Israel is Israel.

## Romans 9:7

Not all Abraham's descendants are his children. 📖

Instead, “Your offspring will be counted through Isaac.”

## Romans 9:8

God's children are those of the promise, rather than children of the flesh. 📖

They are counted as offspring.

## Romans 9:9

The promise said, “I will return at the appointed time, and Sarah will have a son.” 📖

## Romans 9:10

Rebecca's children also had one father, our ancestor Isaac. 📖

## Romans 9:11

This was before the twins were born or had done anything good or bad. 📖

God's plan of election would stand.

**Help with a word:** Election here means God's choosing.

## Romans 9:12

It depends on the One who calls, rather than works. 📖

Rebecca was told, “The older will serve the younger.”

## Romans 9:13

The Scriptures say, “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.” 📖

## Romans 9:14

What shall we say? 📖

Is God unjust?

Certainly not!

## Romans 9:15

God tells Moses, “I will show mercy to whom I show mercy, and compassion to whom I show compassion.” 📖

## Romans 9:16

So this depends on God's mercy, rather than human desire or effort. 📖

## Romans 9:17

Scripture tells Pharaoh, “I raised you up to display my power in you and proclaim my name throughout the earth.” 📖

## Romans 9:18

God shows mercy to whom he wants and hardens whom he wants. 📖

## Romans 9:19

Someone will ask me, “Then why does God still find fault? 📖

Who can resist his will?”

## Romans 9:20

But who are you, a human, to answer back to God? 📖

Can something formed ask its maker, “Why did you make me like this?”

## Romans 9:21

Does a potter not have the right to shape one lump of clay into vessels for different uses? 📖

One can be for special use and another for common use.

**Help with these words:** A potter makes things from clay.

A vessel is a container.

## Romans 9:22

What if God bore with vessels of wrath prepared for destruction with great patience? 📖

What if he intended to show his wrath and make his power known?

## Romans 9:23

What if he did this to show the riches of his glory to vessels of mercy? 📖

He prepared them beforehand for glory.

## Romans 9:24

This includes us, called from both Jews and Gentiles. 📖

## Romans 9:25

God says in Hosea, “I will call those not my people ‘My People,’ and her not beloved ‘My Beloved.’” 📖

## Romans 9:26

“Where they were told, ‘You are not my people,’ they will be called ‘children of the living God.’” 📖

## Romans 9:27

Isaiah cries about Israel, “Though Israelites number like the sea's sand, only the remnant will be saved.” 📖

**Help with a word:** A remnant is a group remaining from a larger people.

## Romans 9:28

“The Lord will carry out his sentence on earth fully and decisively.” 📖

## Romans 9:29

Isaiah foretold, “Unless the Lord of Hosts had left descendants, we would have become like Sodom and Gomorrah.” 📖

**Help with a title:** Lord of Hosts means Lord of the heavenly armies.

## Romans 9:30

What shall we say? 📖

Gentiles who did not pursue righteousness obtained it, righteousness by faith.

## Romans 9:31

But Israel pursued a law of righteousness without attaining it. 📖

## Romans 9:32

Why? 📖

They pursued it as if by works, rather than faith.

They stumbled over the stumbling stone.

## Romans 9:33

The Scriptures say, “I lay in Zion a stumbling stone and a rock of offence. 📖

Anyone believing in him will never be put to shame.”
