# Romans 4 — Abraham believed God's promise

**About this chapter**

Paul uses Abraham and David to explain righteousness through faith.

He discusses God's promise before the law and before circumcision.

## Romans 4:1

What did Abraham, our ancestor according to the flesh, discover? 📖

## Romans 4:2

If Abraham was made right by works, he had something to boast about, but not before God. 📖

## Romans 4:3

What do the Scriptures say? 📖

“Abraham believed God, and God counted it as righteousness.”

**Help with a word:** Righteousness means being right before God.

## Romans 4:4

A worker's wages are counted as something owed, rather than a gift. 📖

## Romans 4:5

Someone may not work, but believe in God who makes the ungodly right. 📖

That person's faith is counted as righteousness.

## Romans 4:6

David also describes the blessing of someone whom God counts righteous apart from works. 📖

## Romans 4:7

“Blessed are those whose lawless actions are forgiven and whose sins are covered.” 📖

## Romans 4:8

“Blessed is the person whose sin the Lord will never count against them.” 📖

## Romans 4:9

Is this blessing only for the circumcised, or also for the uncircumcised? 📖

We have said Abraham's faith was counted as righteousness.

## Romans 4:10

Was it counted after he was circumcised, or before? 📖

It was before, rather than after.

## Romans 4:11

Circumcision was a sign and seal of the righteousness he had by faith while uncircumcised. 📖

So he is father of all uncircumcised believers, so righteousness may be counted to them.

**Help with words:** Circumcision removes the foreskin, the skin covering the end of the penis.

A seal confirms something as true.

## Romans 4:12

He is also father of circumcised people who follow the faith he had before circumcision. 📖

## Romans 4:13

God promised Abraham and his offspring he would inherit the world. 📖

This came through righteousness by faith, rather than through the law.

## Romans 4:14

If those relying on the law are heirs, faith is useless and the promise worthless. 📖

## Romans 4:15

The law brings wrath. 📖

Where there is no law, there is no lawbreaking.

## Romans 4:16

So the promise comes by faith and rests on kindness, guaranteed to all Abraham's offspring. 📖

This includes those of the law and those sharing Abraham's faith.

He is father of us all.

## Romans 4:17

The Scriptures say, “I made you father of many nations.” 📖

He is our father before the God he believed.

God gives life to the dead and calls into existence what does not exist.

## Romans 4:18

Against all hope, Abraham believed in hope and became father of many nations. 📖

He was told, “So shall your offspring be.”

## Romans 4:19

Without weakening in faith, he recognised how worn out his body was, being about 100 years old. 📖

He recognised the lifelessness of Sarah's womb.

## Romans 4:20

He did not waver in unbelief about God's promise. 📖

Instead, his faith grew strong, and he gave glory to God.

## Romans 4:21

He was fully sure God could do what he promised. 📖

## Romans 4:22

That is why “it was counted as righteousness.” 📖

## Romans 4:23

“It was counted to him” was written for more than Abraham alone. 📖

## Romans 4:24

It was written for us too, who believe in the One who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead. 📖

Righteousness will be counted to us.

## Romans 4:25

Jesus was handed over to death for our wrongdoing and raised to life for our justification. 📖

**Help with a word:** Justification means being declared right before God.
