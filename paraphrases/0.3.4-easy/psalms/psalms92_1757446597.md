# Psalms 92 — A song for the Sabbath

**About this chapter**

The song gives thanks for God’s works and contrasts wicked people with righteous people.

## Psalms 92:1

A psalm. 📖

A song for the Sabbath day.

It is good to praise the LORD and sing praise to Your name, Most High,

**Help with Sabbath:** The weekly day set apart for rest and worship.

## Psalms 92:2

announcing Your faithful love in the morning and Your faithfulness at night, 📖

## Psalms 92:3

with the ten-stringed harp and the lyre’s melody. 📖

**Help with harp and lyre:** Stringed musical instruments.

## Psalms 92:4

LORD, Your deeds have made me glad. 📖

I sing for joy at the works of Your hands.

## Psalms 92:5

LORD, Your works are so great! 📖

Your thoughts are so deep!

## Psalms 92:6

A senseless person does not know. 📖

A fool does not understand,

## Psalms 92:7

even though wicked people grow like grass and evildoers flourish, they will be destroyed forever. 📖

## Psalms 92:8

But LORD, You are high above all forever! 📖

## Psalms 92:9

LORD, Your enemies will surely perish. 📖

Yes, Your enemies will perish.

Everyone doing evil will be scattered.

## Psalms 92:10

You have lifted my horn high like a wild ox’s horn. 📖

I have been anointed with fine oil.

**Help with horn and anointed:** The horn pictures strength.

Anointed means having oil poured on someone.

## Psalms 92:11

My eyes see my enemies’ downfall. 📖

My ears hear my wicked enemies’ cries.

## Psalms 92:12

Righteous people will flourish like a palm tree. 📖

They will grow like a cedar in Lebanon.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 92:13

Planted in the LORD’s house, they will flourish in our God’s courts. 📖

**Help with courts:** Open areas belonging to the place of worship.

## Psalms 92:14

Even in old age, they will bear fruit. 📖

They will remain healthy and green,

## Psalms 92:15

announcing, “The LORD is upright. 📖

He is my Rock.

There is no unrighteousness in Him.”

**Help with upright and unrighteousness:** Completely right, and wrongdoing.

Rock pictures strength and protection.
