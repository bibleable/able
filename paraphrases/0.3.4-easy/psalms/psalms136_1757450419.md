# Psalms 136 — His faithful love lasts forever

**About this chapter**

The song repeats the same response after every line.

It praises God’s creation, rescue, judgments, and care.

## Psalms 136:1

Thank the LORD because He is good. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:2

Thank the God of gods. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:3

Thank the Lord of lords. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:4

He alone does great wonders. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:5

He made the heavens with understanding. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:6

He spread the earth over the waters. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:7

He made the great lights, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:8

the sun to rule the day, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:9

and the moon and stars to govern the night. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:10

He struck down Egypt’s firstborn, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:11

and brought Israel out from among them, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:12

with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:13

He divided the Red Sea in two, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:14

and led Israel through its middle, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:15

but swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:16

He led His people through the wilderness. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:17

He struck down great kings, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:18

and killed mighty kings, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:19

Sihon king of the Amorites, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:20

and Og king of Bashan. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:21

He gave their land as an inheritance, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:22

a share for His servant Israel. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:23

He remembered us when we were low, 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:24

and freed us from our enemies. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:25

He gives food to every creature. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 136:26

Thank the God of heaven! 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.
