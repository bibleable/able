# Psalms 35 — David asks the LORD to fight for him

**About this chapter**

David asks for protection and severe judgment against enemies.

He contrasts his concern for them with their attacks on him.

## Psalms 35:1

Of David. 📖

LORD, oppose my opponents.

Fight those who fight me.

## Psalms 35:2

Take up Your shield and buckler. 📖

Rise and help me.

**Help with buckler:** A kind of shield.

David pictures God as a warrior defending him.

## Psalms 35:3

Draw Your spear and javelin against those chasing me. 📖

Say to my soul, “I am your rescue.”

**Help with javelin:** A spear thrown as a weapon.

## Psalms 35:4

May people seeking my life be disgraced and shamed. 📖

May people planning to harm me be driven back and confused.

## Psalms 35:5

May they be like chaff in the wind as the LORD’s angel drives them away. 📖

**Help with chaff:** Light plant parts separated from grain.

## Psalms 35:6

May their path be dark and slippery as the LORD’s angel pursues them. 📖

## Psalms 35:7

Without cause, they laid a net for me. 📖

Without reason, they dug a pit for my soul.

## Psalms 35:8

May ruin fall on them without warning. 📖

May their hidden net trap them.

May they fall into the danger they created.

## Psalms 35:9

Then my soul will rejoice in the LORD. 📖

I will greatly rejoice in His rescue.

## Psalms 35:10

All my bones will cry, “LORD, who is like You? 📖

You rescue suffering people from attackers.

You rescue poor and needy people from robbers.”

## Psalms 35:11

Hostile witnesses come forward. 📖

They accuse me of things I know nothing about.

## Psalms 35:12

They repay me evil for good. 📖

They leave my soul grieving as if bereaved.

**Help with bereaved:** Grieving because someone has died.

## Psalms 35:13

But when they were ill, I wore sackcloth. 📖

I humbled myself by fasting.

Yet my prayers came back unanswered.

**Help with sackcloth and fasting:** Rough mourning clothes, and going without food.

## Psalms 35:14

I walked about as if grieving for my friend or brother. 📖

I bent down with grief like someone mourning his mother.

## Psalms 35:15

But when I stumbled, they gathered in delight. 📖

They gathered against me.

Attackers I did not know kept speaking lies against me.

## Psalms 35:16

Like godless people mocking at a feast, they ground their teeth at me. 📖

## Psalms 35:17

Lord, how long will You watch? 📖

Rescue my soul from their destruction.

Rescue my precious life from these lions.

## Psalms 35:18

Then I will thank You in the great gathering. 📖

I will praise You among many people.

## Psalms 35:19

Do not let enemies celebrate over me without cause. 📖

Do not let people who hate me without reason wink with harmful intent.

## Psalms 35:20

They do not speak peace. 📖

They plan lies against people living quietly in the land.

## Psalms 35:21

They open their mouths wide at me. 📖

They say, “Aha, aha!

Our eyes have seen!”

## Psalms 35:22

LORD, You have seen it. 📖

Do not be silent.

Lord, do not be far from me.

## Psalms 35:23

Awake and rise to my defense! 📖

My God and my Lord, stand up for my case!

## Psalms 35:24

LORD my God, prove me right by Your righteousness. 📖

Do not let them celebrate over me.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 35:25

Do not let them think, “Aha, just what we wanted!” 📖

Do not let them say, “We have swallowed him up!”

## Psalms 35:26

May everyone delighting in my distress be ashamed and confused. 📖

May everyone raising themselves above me be clothed with shame and disgrace.

## Psalms 35:27

May people who want me proved right shout with joy and gladness. 📖

May they always say, “Exalted be the LORD!

He delights in His servant’s well-being.”

**Help with exalted:** Honored as great.

## Psalms 35:28

Then my tongue will announce Your righteousness. 📖

I will praise You all day long.
