# Psalms 75 — God judges at His chosen time

**About this chapter**

Asaph’s song gives thanks and includes words about God’s judgment.

It warns against pride.

## Psalms 75:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” 📖

A psalm of Asaph.

A song.

God, we give You thanks.

We give thanks because Your Name is near.

People tell of Your wonderful works.

## Psalms 75:2

God says, “When I choose a time, I will judge fairly. 📖

## Psalms 75:3

When the earth and everyone living on it shake, I hold up its pillars. 📖

Selah.

**Help with pillars:** The song pictures the earth resting on supports held by God.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 75:4

I tell proud people, ‘Do not boast.’ 📖

I tell wicked people, ‘Do not lift your horn.

## Psalms 75:5

Do not lift your horn against heaven or speak with your neck stretched out.’” 📖

**Help with horn and stretched neck:** Pictures of proud strength and defiance.

## Psalms 75:6

Being raised high does not come from east or west or from the desert. 📖

## Psalms 75:7

God is the Judge. 📖

He brings one person down and raises another.

## Psalms 75:8

The LORD holds a cup full of foaming wine mixed with spices. 📖

He pours from it.

All the earth’s wicked people drink it down to the last drops.

**Help with the cup:** A picture of God’s judgment.

## Psalms 75:9

But I will announce Him forever. 📖

I will sing praise to Jacob’s God.

## Psalms 75:10

“I will cut off all wicked people’s horns. 📖

But righteous people’s horns will be lifted high.”

**Help with horns:** Pictures of power and honor.

Righteous means right before God.

The final words announce judgment; their speaking voice remains for editorial review.
