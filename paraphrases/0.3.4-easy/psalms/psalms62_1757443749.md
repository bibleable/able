# Psalms 62 — Rest in God alone

**About this chapter**

David speaks about trust in God and warns against trusting power or wealth.

## Psalms 62:1

For the choir leader, according to Jeduthun. 📖

A psalm of David.

My soul rests in God alone.

My rescue comes from Him.

## Psalms 62:2

He alone is my rock and rescue. 📖

He is my fortress.

I will never be shaken.

**Help with rock and fortress:** Pictures of strong protection.

## Psalms 62:3

How long will you threaten a man? 📖

Will all of you throw him down like a leaning wall or an unsteady fence?

## Psalms 62:4

They fully intend to throw him down from his high place. 📖

They delight in lies.

Their mouths bless, but inside they curse.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 62:5

My soul, rest in God alone. 📖

My hope comes from Him.

## Psalms 62:6

He alone is my rock and rescue. 📖

He is my fortress.

I will not be shaken.

## Psalms 62:7

My rescue and honor depend on God, my strong rock. 📖

My refuge is in God.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 62:8

People, trust Him at all times. 📖

Pour out your hearts before Him.

God is our refuge.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 62:9

People of low rank are only vapor. 📖

People of high rank are only a lie.

On the scales they rise up.

Together they weigh no more than vapor.

**Help with vapor and scales:** Pictures showing that human rank and power give no lasting security.

## Psalms 62:10

Do not trust in extortion or put false hope in stolen goods. 📖

If your riches grow, do not set your heart on them.

**Help with extortion:** Forcing people to give money or property through threats.

## Psalms 62:11

God has spoken once. 📖

I have heard this twice: power belongs to God,

## Psalms 62:12

and faithful love belongs to You, Lord. 📖

You repay each person according to what he does.
