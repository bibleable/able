# Psalms 16 — The LORD is my chosen portion

**About this chapter**

David declares his trust in the LORD.

He speaks of the LORD as his treasure and source of life.

## Psalms 16:1

A Miktam of David. 📖

Keep me safe, God.

I take refuge in You.

**Help with Miktam:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

Refuge means safety.

## Psalms 16:2

I said to the LORD, “You are my Lord. 📖

Apart from You, I have no good thing.”

## Psalms 16:3

The saints in the land are excellent. 📖

All my delight is in them.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 16:4

Sorrows will increase for people who chase other gods. 📖

I will not pour out their drink offerings of blood.

I will not speak their names.

## Psalms 16:5

The LORD is my chosen portion and my cup. 📖

You have made my lot secure.

**Help with portion, cup, and lot:** Pictures of what David receives and depends on for his life.

## Psalms 16:6

My boundary lines have fallen in pleasant places. 📖

My inheritance is truly delightful.

**Help with inheritance:** Property or a share someone receives.

The song pictures a good gift of land.

## Psalms 16:7

I will bless the LORD, who advises me. 📖

Even at night, my conscience teaches me.

**Help with conscience:** The sense of whether your actions are right or wrong.

## Psalms 16:8

I always keep the LORD before me. 📖

He is at my right hand, so I will not be shaken.

## Psalms 16:9

So my heart is glad and my tongue rejoices. 📖

My body will also rest safely.

## Psalms 16:10

You will not abandon my soul to Sheol. 📖

You will not let Your Holy One see decay.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

Decay means rotting away.

## Psalms 16:11

You have shown me the path of life. 📖

You will fill me with joy in Your presence.

At Your right hand are pleasures that last forever.
