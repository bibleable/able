# Psalms 103 — Bless the LORD and remember His kindness

**About this chapter**

David calls on his soul and all creation to bless the LORD.

He describes forgiveness, human frailty, and faithful love.

## Psalms 103:1

Of David. 📖

My soul, bless the LORD.

Everything within me, bless His holy name.

## Psalms 103:2

My soul, bless the LORD. 📖

Do not forget all His kind deeds.

## Psalms 103:3

He forgives all your sins. 📖

He heals all your diseases.

## Psalms 103:4

He rescues your life from the Pit. 📖

He crowns you with faithful love and compassion.

**Help with the Pit and compassion:** The grave or place of the dead, and deep care for someone who suffers.

## Psalms 103:5

He satisfies you with good things. 📖

Your youth is renewed like an eagle’s.

## Psalms 103:6

The LORD brings righteousness and justice for everyone treated cruelly. 📖

**Help with righteousness and justice:** Doing what is right and judging fairly.

## Psalms 103:7

He made His ways known to Moses. 📖

He made His deeds known to Israel’s people.

## Psalms 103:8

The LORD is compassionate and gracious. 📖

He is slow to anger and full of faithful love.

**Help with gracious:** Freely kind.

## Psalms 103:9

He will not accuse us forever. 📖

He will not hold on to His anger forever.

## Psalms 103:10

He has not treated us as our sins deserve. 📖

He has not repaid us according to our wrongdoing.

## Psalms 103:11

His faithful love for people who fear Him is as great as heaven’s height above earth. 📖

**Help with fear Him:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 103:12

He has removed our offenses as far from us as east is from west. 📖

## Psalms 103:13

As a father has compassion on his children, the LORD has compassion on people who fear Him. 📖

## Psalms 103:14

He knows how we are made. 📖

He remembers that we are dust.

## Psalms 103:15

A person’s days are like grass. 📖

He blooms like a flower in a field.

## Psalms 103:16

The wind passes over it, and it is gone. 📖

Its place remembers it no more.

## Psalms 103:17

But the LORD’s faithful love is from everlasting to everlasting for people who fear Him. 📖

His righteousness reaches their children’s children,

**Help with everlasting:** Without beginning or end.

## Psalms 103:18

for those who keep His covenant and remember to obey His instructions. 📖

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Psalms 103:19

The LORD established His throne in heaven. 📖

His kingdom rules over everything.

## Psalms 103:20

Bless the LORD, all His powerful angels who carry out His word. 📖

You listen to His commands.

## Psalms 103:21

Bless the LORD, all His hosts. 📖

You are His servants who do His will.

**Help with hosts:** His heavenly armies.

## Psalms 103:22

Bless the LORD, all His works everywhere He rules. 📖

My soul, bless the LORD!
