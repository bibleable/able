# Psalms 32 — Confession and forgiveness

**About this chapter**

David tells of confessing his sin and receiving forgiveness.

The song also includes instruction about the way to live.

## Psalms 32:1

Of David. 📖

A Maskil.

Blessed is the person whose wrongs are forgiven and whose sins are covered.

**Help with Maskil:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 32:2

Blessed is the person whose sin the LORD does not count against him. 📖

There is no deceit in his spirit.

## Psalms 32:3

When I kept silent, my bones became brittle. 📖

I groaned all day long.

**Help with brittle:** Easily broken.

David describes the effect of his distress.

## Psalms 32:4

Your hand was heavy on me day and night. 📖

My strength drained away as in summer heat.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 32:5

Then I admitted my sin to You. 📖

I did not hide my wrongdoing.

I said, “I will confess my offenses to the LORD.”

You forgave the guilt of my sin.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 32:6

So let all godly people pray to You while You may be found. 📖

When great waters rise, they will surely not come near.

**Help with godly:** Devoted to God.

Great waters picture overwhelming danger.

## Psalms 32:7

You are my hiding place. 📖

You protect me from trouble.

You surround me with songs of rescue.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 32:8

I will instruct you and teach you the way to go. 📖

I will advise you and watch over you.

## Psalms 32:9

Do not be like a horse or mule that has no understanding. 📖

A bit and bridle must control them to make them come to you.

**Help with bit and bridle:** Equipment fitted to an animal’s mouth and head to control it.

## Psalms 32:10

Wicked people have many sorrows. 📖

But faithful love surrounds the person who trusts the LORD.

## Psalms 32:11

Righteous people, be glad in the LORD and rejoice! 📖

Everyone whose heart is upright, shout for joy!

**Help with righteous and upright:** Right before God and honest in heart.
