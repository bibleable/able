# Psalms 96 — Sing a new song to the LORD

**About this chapter**

The song calls all nations and creation to praise the LORD, who comes to judge justly.

## Psalms 96:1

Sing a new song to the LORD. 📖

All the earth, sing to the LORD.

## Psalms 96:2

Sing to the LORD and bless His name. 📖

Announce His rescue day after day.

## Psalms 96:3

Tell the nations His glory. 📖

Tell all peoples His wonders.

## Psalms 96:4

The LORD is great and greatly deserves praise. 📖

He must be feared above all gods.

## Psalms 96:5

All the nations’ gods are idols. 📖

But the LORD made the heavens.

**Help with idols:** Images worshiped as gods.

## Psalms 96:6

Splendor and majesty stand before Him. 📖

Strength and beauty fill His sanctuary.

**Help with splendor and majesty:** Great royal glory.

A sanctuary is a holy place of worship.

## Psalms 96:7

Families of the nations, give the LORD honor. 📖

Acknowledge the LORD’s glory and strength.

## Psalms 96:8

Give the LORD the glory His name deserves. 📖

Bring an offering and enter His courts.

**Help with offering and courts:** A gift in worship, and open areas belonging to the place of worship.

## Psalms 96:9

Worship the LORD in His holiness’s splendor. 📖

All the earth, tremble before Him.

**Help with holiness:** God’s complete purity and greatness.

## Psalms 96:10

Tell the nations, “The LORD reigns!” 📖

The world is firmly established and cannot be moved.

He will judge the peoples fairly.

## Psalms 96:11

Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice. 📖

Let the sea and everything filling it sound out.

## Psalms 96:12

Let the fields and everything in them rejoice. 📖

Then all the forest’s trees will sing for joy,

## Psalms 96:13

before the LORD, because He is coming. 📖

He is coming to judge the earth.

He will judge the world rightly and the peoples in His faithfulness.
