# Psalms 95 — Worship and listen today

**About this chapter**

The song calls people to worship their Maker.

It then gives God’s warning about hardened hearts.

## Psalms 95:1

Come, let us sing for joy to the LORD. 📖

Let us shout to the Rock of our rescue!

**Help with Rock:** A picture of strength and protection.

## Psalms 95:2

Let us come into His presence with thanks. 📖

Let us make a joyful sound to Him in song.

## Psalms 95:3

The LORD is a great God. 📖

He is a great King above all gods.

## Psalms 95:4

The earth’s depths are in His hand. 📖

The mountain peaks belong to Him.

## Psalms 95:5

The sea is His because He made it. 📖

His hands formed dry land.

## Psalms 95:6

Come, let us worship and bow down. 📖

Let us kneel before the LORD our Maker.

## Psalms 95:7

He is our God. 📖

We are His pasture’s people, sheep under His care.

Today, if you hear His voice,

## Psalms 95:8

do not harden your hearts as you did at Meribah, on the day at Massah in the wilderness, 📖

**Help with harden your hearts:** Refuse to listen and obey.

## Psalms 95:9

where your fathers tested and tried Me, although they had seen My work. 📖

**Help with the speaker:** God is speaking about His people’s past disobedience.

## Psalms 95:10

For forty years I was angry with that generation. 📖

I said, “Their hearts go wrong.

They have not known My ways.”

## Psalms 95:11

So I swore in My anger, “They will never enter My rest.” 📖
