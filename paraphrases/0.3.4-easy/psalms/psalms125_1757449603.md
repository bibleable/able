# Psalms 125 — The LORD surrounds His people

**About this chapter**

The song compares trust in the LORD with a mountain that cannot be moved.

## Psalms 125:1

A song of ascents. 📖

People who trust the LORD are like Mount Zion.

It cannot be moved and lasts forever.

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 125:2

Mountains surround Jerusalem. 📖

In the same way, the LORD surrounds His people now and forever.

## Psalms 125:3

The wicked scepter will not remain over the land given to righteous people. 📖

Then righteous people will not put their hands to injustice.

**Help with scepter:** A ruler’s staff, picturing royal power.

Righteous means right before God.

## Psalms 125:4

LORD, do good to good people and those whose hearts are upright. 📖

**Help with upright:** Honest and right.

## Psalms 125:5

But the LORD will drive away people who turn to twisted ways, together with evildoers. 📖

May peace be on Israel.
