# Psalms 69 — A prayer from deep distress

**About this chapter**

David describes suffering and asks God for rescue.

He also asks for severe judgment against his enemies.

## Psalms 69:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “Lilies.” 📖

Of David.

God, save me.

The waters have reached my neck.

## Psalms 69:2

I have sunk into deep mud with nowhere to stand. 📖

I have drifted into deep water.

The flood overwhelms me.

## Psalms 69:3

I am tired from crying. 📖

My throat is dry.

My eyes fail as I look for my God.

## Psalms 69:4

People who hate me without cause outnumber the hairs on my head. 📖

Many enemies want to destroy me for no reason.

I did not steal, but I must repay.

## Psalms 69:5

God, You know my foolishness. 📖

My guilt is not hidden from You.

## Psalms 69:6

Lord GOD of Hosts, do not let people who hope in You be ashamed because of me. 📖

God of Israel, do not let those seeking You be dishonored because of me.

**Help with GOD of Hosts:** God who commands heavenly armies.

## Psalms 69:7

I have endured scorn because of You. 📖

Shame has covered my face.

## Psalms 69:8

I have become a stranger to my brothers. 📖

I am a foreigner to my mother’s sons,

## Psalms 69:9

because zeal for Your house has consumed me. 📖

The insults of people insulting You have fallen on me.

**Help with zeal:** Strong devotion and eager care.

## Psalms 69:10

I wept and fasted, but people scorned me for it. 📖

**Help with fasted:** Went without food.

## Psalms 69:11

I wore sackcloth. 📖

I became a joke to them.

**Help with sackcloth:** Rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Psalms 69:12

People sitting at the gate mock me. 📖

Drunk people sing about me.

## Psalms 69:13

But LORD, my prayer is for a time of favor. 📖

God, in Your great faithful love, answer me with Your sure rescue.

## Psalms 69:14

Rescue me from the mud. 📖

Do not let me sink.

Rescue me from my enemies and the deep waters.

## Psalms 69:15

Do not let floods overwhelm me or the depths swallow me. 📖

Do not let the Pit shut its mouth over me.

**Help with the Pit:** The grave or place of the dead.

The waters picture deadly distress.

## Psalms 69:16

LORD, answer me because Your faithful love is good. 📖

Turn to me according to Your great compassion.

**Help with compassion:** Deep care for someone who suffers.

## Psalms 69:17

Do not hide Your face from Your servant. 📖

I am in distress.

Answer me quickly!

## Psalms 69:18

Come near my soul and rescue me. 📖

Ransom me because of my enemies.

**Help with ransom:** Buy someone’s freedom.

## Psalms 69:19

You know my scorn, shame, and disgrace. 📖

All my enemies are before You.

## Psalms 69:20

Insults have broken my heart, and I have lost hope. 📖

I looked for sympathy, but there was none.

I looked for comforters, but found no one.

## Psalms 69:21

They poisoned my food with gall. 📖

They gave me vinegar for my thirst.

**Help with gall and vinegar:** A bitter poisonous substance, and a sour drink.

## Psalms 69:22

May their table become a snare. 📖

May it become a punishment and a trap.

**Help with snare:** A trap.

## Psalms 69:23

May their eyes become dark so they cannot see. 📖

May their backs be bent forever.

## Psalms 69:24

Pour Your wrath on them. 📖

Let Your burning anger overtake them.

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Psalms 69:25

May their home be deserted. 📖

Let no one live in their tents.

## Psalms 69:26

They attack the one You struck. 📖

They tell of the pain of people You wounded.

## Psalms 69:27

Add sin to their sin. 📖

Do not let them share in Your righteousness.

**Help with righteousness:** Being right before God.

## Psalms 69:28

May they be erased from the Book of Life. 📖

Do not list them with righteous people.

**Help with Book of Life:** A picture of God’s record of those who have life.

## Psalms 69:29

But I am in pain and distress. 📖

God, let Your rescue protect me.

## Psalms 69:30

I will praise God’s name in song. 📖

I will honor Him as great with thanks.

## Psalms 69:31

This will please the LORD more than an ox. 📖

It will please Him more than a bull with horns and hooves.

## Psalms 69:32

Humble people will see and rejoice. 📖

People who seek God, let your hearts come to life again!

## Psalms 69:33

The LORD listens to needy people. 📖

He does not despise His captive people.

## Psalms 69:34

Let heaven and earth praise Him. 📖

Let the seas and everything moving in them praise Him.

## Psalms 69:35

God will save Zion and rebuild Judah’s cities. 📖

Then His people may live there and possess the land.

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 69:36

His servants’ descendants will inherit it. 📖

People who love His name will settle there.

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.
