# Psalms 126 — From tears to joyful harvest

**About this chapter**

The people remember return from captivity and pray for restoration again.

## Psalms 126:1

A song of ascents. 📖

When the LORD restored Zion’s captives, we were like dreamers.

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 126:2

Our mouths filled with laughter. 📖

Our tongues shouted for joy.

Nations said, “The LORD has done great things for them.”

## Psalms 126:3

The LORD has done great things for us. 📖

We are full of joy.

## Psalms 126:4

LORD, restore our captives like streams in the Negev. 📖

**Help with Negev:** A dry southern region.

Streams there picture sudden renewal.

## Psalms 126:5

People who plant in tears will harvest with joyful shouts. 📖

## Psalms 126:6

The person going out weeping and carrying a trail of seed will surely return with joyful shouts. 📖

He will carry bundles of grain.
