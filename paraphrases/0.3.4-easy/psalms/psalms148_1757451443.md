# Psalms 148 — Let all creation praise the LORD

**About this chapter**

The song calls heaven, earth, creatures, and people of every age to praise God.

## Psalms 148:1

Hallelujah! 📖

Praise the LORD from heaven.

Praise Him in the highest places.

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.

## Psalms 148:2

All His angels, praise Him. 📖

All His heavenly hosts, praise Him.

**Help with hosts:** Armies.

## Psalms 148:3

Sun and moon, praise Him. 📖

All shining stars, praise Him.

## Psalms 148:4

Highest heavens and waters above the skies, praise Him. 📖

## Psalms 148:5

Let them praise the LORD’s name. 📖

He commanded, and they were created.

## Psalms 148:6

He established them forever and ever. 📖

He issued a decree that will never pass away.

**Help with decree:** An authoritative command.

## Psalms 148:7

Praise the LORD from the earth, great sea creatures and ocean depths, 📖

## Psalms 148:8

lightning, hail, snow, clouds, and powerful wind carrying out His word, 📖

## Psalms 148:9

mountains, all hills, fruit trees, and all cedars, 📖

## Psalms 148:10

wild animals, all cattle, crawling creatures, and flying birds, 📖

## Psalms 148:11

earth’s kings, all peoples, princes, and all the earth’s rulers, 📖

## Psalms 148:12

young men, young women, old and young together. 📖

## Psalms 148:13

Let them praise the LORD’s name. 📖

His name alone is raised high.

His splendor is above earth and heaven.

**Help with splendor:** Great glory.

## Psalms 148:14

He raised a horn for His people, the praise of all His saints. 📖

They are Israel, a people near Him.

Hallelujah!

**Help with horn and saints:** A picture of strength and honor, and people set apart for God.
