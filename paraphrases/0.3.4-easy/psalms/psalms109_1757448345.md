# Psalms 109 — A prayer against cruel accusers

**About this chapter**

David describes false accusations and asks for judgment.

The prayer includes severe requests about an enemy and his family.

## Psalms 109:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

God whom I praise, do not be silent.

## Psalms 109:2

Wicked, deceitful mouths open against me. 📖

Their tongues speak lies against me.

## Psalms 109:3

They surround me with hateful words. 📖

They attack me without cause.

## Psalms 109:4

They accuse me in return for my love. 📖

But I am a man of prayer.

## Psalms 109:5

They repay my good with evil. 📖

They repay my love with hatred.

## Psalms 109:6

Put a wicked man over him. 📖

Let an accuser stand at his right hand.

## Psalms 109:7

When he is tried, let him be found guilty. 📖

May his prayer be counted as sin.

## Psalms 109:8

May his days be few. 📖

May someone else take his position.

## Psalms 109:9

May his children have no father. 📖

May his wife become a widow.

**Help with widow:** A woman whose husband has died.

## Psalms 109:10

May his children wander and beg. 📖

May they seek food far from their ruined homes.

## Psalms 109:11

May the creditor take everything he owns. 📖

May strangers steal the fruit of his work.

**Help with creditor:** Someone to whom a debt is owed.

## Psalms 109:12

May no one show him kindness. 📖

May no one show favor to his fatherless children.

## Psalms 109:13

May his descendants be cut off. 📖

May their name be erased from the next generation.

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

## Psalms 109:14

May his fathers’ sins be remembered before the LORD. 📖

May his mother’s sin never be erased.

## Psalms 109:15

May their sins always remain before the LORD. 📖

Then He may cut their memory off from the earth.

## Psalms 109:16

He never thought to show kindness. 📖

He hunted poor, needy, and brokenhearted people, even to their death.

## Psalms 109:17

May the cursing he loved fall on him. 📖

May the blessing he refused to enjoy stay far from him.

## Psalms 109:18

He wore cursing like a coat. 📖

May it soak into his body like water and his bones like oil.

## Psalms 109:19

May it be like a robe wrapped around him. 📖

May it be like a belt tied around him forever.

## Psalms 109:20

May this be the LORD’s reward for my accusers and those speaking evil against me. 📖

## Psalms 109:21

But GOD, the Lord, be kind to me for Your name’s sake. 📖

Rescue me through the goodness of Your faithful love.

## Psalms 109:22

I am poor and needy. 📖

My heart is wounded within me.

## Psalms 109:23

I fade like a shadow growing long. 📖

I am shaken off like a locust.

## Psalms 109:24

My knees are weak from fasting. 📖

My body grows thin and wasted.

**Help with fasting:** Going without food.

## Psalms 109:25

My accusers scorn me. 📖

When they see me, they shake their heads.

## Psalms 109:26

LORD my God, help me. 📖

Save me according to Your faithful love.

## Psalms 109:27

Let them know this is Your hand. 📖

LORD, You have done it.

## Psalms 109:28

They may curse, but You will bless. 📖

When they rise, they will be ashamed.

But Your servant will rejoice.

## Psalms 109:29

May my accusers be clothed with disgrace. 📖

May they wear their shame like a robe.

## Psalms 109:30

My mouth will thank the LORD greatly. 📖

I will praise Him before many people.

## Psalms 109:31

He stands at the needy person’s right hand. 📖

He saves him from those condemning his soul.
