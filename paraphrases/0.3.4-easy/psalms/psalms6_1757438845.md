# Psalms 6 — A prayer in great distress

**About this chapter**

David asks for mercy and healing.

He describes his grief and declares that the LORD has heard him.

## Psalms 6:1

For the choir leader, with stringed instruments, according to Sheminith. 📖

A psalm of David.

LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger.

Do not discipline me in Your fury.

**Help with Sheminith:** A musical term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

To rebuke is to speak strongly against wrongdoing.

Discipline is correction.

## Psalms 6:2

Have mercy on me, LORD, because I am weak. 📖

Heal me, LORD, because my bones are in terrible pain.

## Psalms 6:3

My soul is deeply troubled. 📖

How long, LORD?

How long?

## Psalms 6:4

Turn, LORD, and rescue my soul. 📖

Save me because of Your faithful love.

## Psalms 6:5

No one mentions You in death. 📖

Who can praise You from Sheol?

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

These are David’s words in his distress.

## Psalms 6:6

I am tired from groaning. 📖

All night I flood my bed with weeping.

I soak my couch with tears.

## Psalms 6:7

My eyes fail because of grief. 📖

They grow dim because of all my enemies.

## Psalms 6:8

Go away from me, all you who do evil. 📖

The LORD has heard my weeping.

## Psalms 6:9

The LORD has heard my cry for mercy. 📖

The LORD accepts my prayer.

## Psalms 6:10

All my enemies will be ashamed and troubled. 📖

They will turn back in sudden disgrace.
