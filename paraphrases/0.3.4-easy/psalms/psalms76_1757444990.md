# Psalms 76 — God’s judgment brings stillness

**About this chapter**

Asaph praises God’s power and calls people to honor Him.

## Psalms 76:1

For the choir leader, with stringed instruments. 📖

A psalm of Asaph.

A song.

God is known in Judah.

His name is great in Israel.

## Psalms 76:2

His tent is in Salem. 📖

His home is in Zion.

**Help with Salem and Zion:** Names associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 76:3

There He shattered flaming arrows, shields, swords, and weapons of war. 📖

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 76:4

You shine with light. 📖

You are more majestic than mountains full of wild game.

**Help with majestic and game:** Showing great glory, and animals hunted for food.

## Psalms 76:5

Brave warriors lie robbed. 📖

They sleep their final sleep.

No mighty man could lift a hand.

## Psalms 76:6

God of Jacob, at Your rebuke both horse and rider lie stunned. 📖

**Help with rebuke:** A strong expression of anger and correction.

## Psalms 76:7

You alone must be feared. 📖

When You are angry, who can stand before You?

## Psalms 76:8

You announced judgment from heaven. 📖

The earth feared and became still,

## Psalms 76:9

when God rose to judge and save all the earth’s lowly people. 📖

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 76:10

Even human anger will praise You. 📖

You will clothe Yourself with the survivors of wrath.

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

This clothing image is retained; its precise meaning needs further editorial review.

## Psalms 76:11

Make vows to the LORD your God and keep them. 📖

Let all neighboring lands bring tribute to the One who must be feared.

**Help with vows and tribute:** Serious promises, and gifts or payments showing submission to a ruler.

## Psalms 76:12

He breaks princes’ spirits. 📖

The earth’s kings fear Him.
