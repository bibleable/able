# Psalms 143 — Teach me to do Your will

**About this chapter**

David asks for mercy, rescue, and the guidance of God’s Spirit.

## Psalms 143:1

A psalm of David. 📖

LORD, hear my prayer.

Listen to my appeal in Your faithfulness.

Answer me in Your righteousness.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 143:2

Do not bring Your servant to judgment. 📖

No living person is righteous before You.

## Psalms 143:3

The enemy chased my soul and crushed my life to the ground. 📖

He made me live in darkness like people long dead.

## Psalms 143:4

My spirit grows weak within me. 📖

My heart is troubled inside me.

## Psalms 143:5

I remember old days. 📖

I think deeply about all Your works.

I consider what Your hands have done.

## Psalms 143:6

I stretch my hands toward You. 📖

My soul thirsts for You like dry land.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 143:7

LORD, answer quickly, because my spirit fails. 📖

Do not hide Your face, or I will be like people going down to the Pit.

**Help with the Pit:** The grave or place of the dead.

## Psalms 143:8

Let me hear Your faithful love in the morning because I trust You. 📖

Teach me the way to walk because I lift my soul to You.

## Psalms 143:9

LORD, rescue me from my enemies. 📖

I flee to You for refuge.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 143:10

Teach me to do Your will, because You are my God. 📖

May Your good Spirit lead me on level ground.

## Psalms 143:11

LORD, give me life again for Your name’s sake. 📖

In Your righteousness, bring my soul out of trouble.

## Psalms 143:12

In Your faithful love, cut off my enemies. 📖

Destroy everyone who makes me suffer, because I am Your servant.
