# Psalms 57 — Shelter under God’s wings

**About this chapter**

David asks for mercy while fleeing Saul.

He speaks of danger, trust, and praise.

## Psalms 57:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” 📖

A Miktam of David, when he fled from Saul into the cave.

God, have mercy on me, have mercy.

My soul takes refuge in You.

I will shelter under Your wings until the danger passes.

**Help with Miktam:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

Refuge under wings pictures protection like a bird gives its young.

## Psalms 57:2

I cry to God Most High. 📖

God fulfills His purpose for me.

## Psalms 57:3

He reaches down from heaven and saves me. 📖

He rebukes those who trample me.

Selah.

God sends His faithful love and truth.

**Help with rebukes:** Speaks strongly against their wrongdoing.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 57:4

My soul is among lions. 📖

I lie among hungry beasts.

These are men whose teeth are spears and arrows, whose tongues are sharp swords.

**Help with lions and weapons:** Pictures of dangerous people and harmful speech.

## Psalms 57:5

God, be honored high above the heavens. 📖

May Your glory cover all the earth.

## Psalms 57:6

They spread a net for my feet. 📖

My soul was deeply discouraged.

They dug a pit before me, but they fell into it themselves!

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 57:7

My heart is steady, God. 📖

My heart is steady.

I will sing and make music.

## Psalms 57:8

Awake, my glory! 📖

Awake, harp and lyre!

I will wake the dawn.

**Help with harp and lyre:** Stringed musical instruments.

David calls on himself and his instruments to praise God.

## Psalms 57:9

Lord, I will praise You among the nations. 📖

I will sing Your praises among the peoples.

## Psalms 57:10

Your faithful love reaches the heavens. 📖

Your faithfulness reaches the clouds.

## Psalms 57:11

God, be honored high above the heavens. 📖

May Your glory cover all the earth.
