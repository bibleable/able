# Psalms 14 — The LORD sees human evil

**About this chapter**

David describes people turning from God.

He asks for Israel’s rescue.

## Psalms 14:1

For the choir leader. 📖

Of David.

The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.”

They are corrupt, and their actions are vile.

No one does good.

**Help with corrupt and vile:** Twisted by evil and deeply wrong.

## Psalms 14:2

The LORD looks down from heaven at human beings. 📖

He looks for anyone who understands and seeks God.

## Psalms 14:3

All have turned away. 📖

Together they have become corrupt.

No one does good, not even one.

## Psalms 14:4

Will people who do evil never learn? 📖

They eat my people as if eating bread.

They refuse to call on the LORD.

**Help with eating people:** A picture of cruelly using and destroying them.

## Psalms 14:5

There they are, filled with fear. 📖

God is among righteous people.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 14:6

You sinners spoil the plans of people treated cruelly. 📖

Yet the LORD is those people’s shelter.

## Psalms 14:7

If only Israel’s rescue would come from Zion! 📖

When the LORD restores His captive people, let Jacob rejoice!

Let Israel be glad!

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem.

Jacob is also used as a name for Israel’s people.
