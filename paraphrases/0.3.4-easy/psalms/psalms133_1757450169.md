# Psalm 133 — Living together in peace

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

**About this song**

David wrote this song.

The Bible calls it a song of ascents.

**Help with a word:** Ascents means going up.

This is a song for people going up to Jerusalem.

## Psalms 133:1

Look how good it is when brothers live together in peace! 🤝

Living together in peace brings joy.

## Psalms 133:2

This peace is like fine oil poured on Aaron's head. 🫙

The oil runs down his beard.

The oil runs down to the collar of his robe.

**Help with this picture**

Aaron was a priest.

A priest led people in worship of God.

Oil was poured on Aaron to set him apart to serve God.

A robe is a long piece of clothing.

## Psalms 133:3

This peace is like dew from Mount Hermon. 💧

The dew falls on the mountains of Zion.

There the LORD gives the gift of life that lasts forever.

**Help with this picture**

Dew is made of small drops of water.

These drops form on cool surfaces, often at night.

Hermon is a mountain.

Zion is a name used for Jerusalem and the place where God is worshipped.
