# Psalms 68 — God rises and leads His people

**About this chapter**

David sings of God’s power, care, and victory.

The song includes difficult battle images and God’s own words.

## Psalms 68:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

A song.

God rises.

His enemies scatter.

People who hate Him flee His presence.

## Psalms 68:2

You drive them away like smoke blown away. 📖

As wax melts before fire, wicked people perish before God.

## Psalms 68:3

But righteous people will be glad and rejoice before God. 📖

They will celebrate with joy.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 68:4

Sing to God! 📖

Sing praises to His name.

Lift high the One who rides on clouds.

His name is the LORD.

Rejoice before Him.

## Psalms 68:5

God in His holy home is a father to children without fathers. 📖

He defends widows.

**Help with widows:** Women whose husbands have died.

## Psalms 68:6

God places lonely people in families. 📖

He leads prisoners out to a life of plenty.

But rebellious people live in a land scorched by sun.

## Psalms 68:7

God, You went out before Your people. 📖

You marched through the wasteland.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 68:8

Then the earth shook, and the heavens poured down rain before God. 📖

He is the One on Sinai, the God of Israel.

## Psalms 68:9

God, You sent plentiful rain. 📖

You refreshed Your weary inheritance.

**Help with inheritance:** The land and people belonging to God.

## Psalms 68:10

Your flock settled there. 📖

God, You provided for poor people from Your plenty.

**Help with flock:** A picture of God’s people as sheep under His care.

## Psalms 68:11

The Lord gives the command. 📖

A great crowd of women announces it:

## Psalms 68:12

“Kings and their armies flee quickly. 📖

The woman who waits at home divides the plunder.

**Help with plunder:** Goods taken after a battle.

## Psalms 68:13

Even though you lie among the sheepfolds, the dove’s wings are covered with silver. 📖

Her feathers shine with gold.”

**Help with sheepfolds:** Enclosed places for sheep.

The exact connection between these poetic pictures needs further editorial review.

## Psalms 68:14

When the Almighty scattered the kings in the land, it was like snow falling on Zalmon. 📖

## Psalms 68:15

Mount Bashan is a mountain of God. 📖

Mount Bashan has many peaks.

## Psalms 68:16

Mountains with many peaks, why do you gaze with envy? 📖

This is the mountain God chose for His home.

The LORD will surely live there forever.

## Psalms 68:17

God’s chariots number tens of thousands, thousands of thousands. 📖

The Lord is in His sanctuary as He was at Sinai.

**Help with chariots and sanctuary:** Wheeled vehicles used in battle, and a holy place of worship.

## Psalms 68:18

You have gone up on high. 📖

You have led captives away.

You received gifts from people, even rebellious people, so the LORD God may live there.

## Psalms 68:19

Blessed be the Lord! 📖

He carries our burden every day.

He is the God who saves us.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 68:20

Our God is a God of rescue. 📖

The Lord GOD rescues us from death.

## Psalms 68:21

God will surely crush His enemies’ heads. 📖

He will crush the hairy crowns of people who keep following guilty ways.

**Help with crowns:** Here, the tops of their heads.

## Psalms 68:22

The Lord said, “I will bring them back from Bashan. 📖

I will bring them up from the sea’s depths,

## Psalms 68:23

so your foot may be dipped in your enemies’ blood. 📖

Your dogs’ tongues may be dipped in it too.”

## Psalms 68:24

God, people have seen Your procession. 📖

My God and King marches into the sanctuary.

**Help with procession:** A group moving together for a special event.

## Psalms 68:25

Singers go first, and musicians follow. 📖

Young women playing tambourines are among them.

**Help with tambourines:** Musical instruments shaken or struck.

## Psalms 68:26

Bless God in the great gathering. 📖

Bless the LORD from Israel’s fountain.

**Help with fountain:** A poetic picture of Israel’s source or people.

Its precise sense needs further editorial review.

## Psalms 68:27

There is Benjamin, the youngest, ruling them. 📖

Judah’s princes are in their company.

Zebulun’s and Naphtali’s princes are there too.

## Psalms 68:28

God, summon Your power. 📖

God, show the strength You have used for us.

## Psalms 68:29

Kings will bring You gifts because of Your temple at Jerusalem. 📖

## Psalms 68:30

Rebuke the beast in the reeds and the herd of bulls among the nations’ calves. 📖

Do this until it submits and brings silver bars.

Scatter the nations that delight in war.

**Help with rebuke:** Speak strongly against wrongdoing.

The animals picture hostile powers; their precise identities remain for editorial review.

## Psalms 68:31

Messengers will arrive from Egypt. 📖

Cush will stretch her hands toward God.

## Psalms 68:32

Kingdoms of the earth, sing to God. 📖

Sing praises to the Lord.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 68:33

He rides on the highest heavens of old. 📖

Listen, His mighty voice sounds!

## Psalms 68:34

Acknowledge God’s power. 📖

His majesty is over Israel.

His strength is in the skies.

**Help with majesty:** Great royal power and glory.

## Psalms 68:35

God, You are awesome in Your sanctuary. 📖

Israel’s God Himself gives His people strength and power.

Blessed be God!

**Help with awesome:** Inspiring deep wonder and fear.
