# Psalm 139 — LORD, you know me

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

**About this song**

David wrote this song for the leader of the singers.

David speaks to God through most of this song.

## Psalms 139:1

LORD, you have looked deeply into my life. 🔍

You know me.

## Psalms 139:2

You know when I sit down. 💭

You know when I get up.

Even from far away, you understand my thoughts.

## Psalms 139:3

You know where I go. 👣

You know when I lie down.

You know everything I do.

## Psalms 139:4

LORD, before I say a word, you already know it. 🗣️

## Psalms 139:5

You are all around me, behind me and in front of me. 🤝

You have placed your hand on me.

## Psalms 139:6

The way you know me is too wonderful for me to understand. 💭

Your knowledge is far beyond mine.

## Psalms 139:7

Where could I go to get away from your Spirit? 🌍

Where could I run to get away from you?

**Help with words:** God's Spirit is God's living presence.

God is present wherever David goes.

## Psalms 139:8

If I go up to the heavens, you are there. 🌌

If I lie down in Sheol, you are there.

**Help with words**

The heavens are the skies and what is above them.

Sheol is the Bible's name for the place of the dead.

## Psalms 139:9

If I could fly away with the first light of morning, you would still be there. 🌅

If I lived beyond the farthest sea, you would still be there.

**Help with this picture**

The BSB says “the wings of the dawn.”

This pictures travelling far away with the morning light.

## Psalms 139:10

Even there, your hand would guide me. 🤝

Your right hand would hold me firmly.

## Psalms 139:11

I might say, “Surely the darkness will hide me. 🌙

“The light around me will turn to night.”

## Psalms 139:12

But even darkness is not dark to you. 🌙

For you, the night shines like the day.

Darkness and light are the same to you.

## Psalms 139:13

You made the parts deep inside my body. 👶

You put me together inside my mother before I was born.

**Help with the Bible's picture**

The BSB says that God “knit me together.”

This pictures God carefully making a person.

## Psalms 139:14

I praise you because you made me in a wonderful way. 🙌

The way you made me fills me with wonder and respect.

Everything you make is wonderful.

I know this very well.

**Help with a word:** To praise God is to say how good and great God is.

## Psalms 139:15

You could see my body when I was being made in secret. 👶

You saw me being put together in the deep places of the earth.

**Help with this picture**

The song pictures David's body being woven together like cloth.

The deep places of the earth are a picture of a hidden place.

The song has just spoken about David growing inside his mother.

## Psalms 139:16

You saw my body before it had fully formed. 📖

You wrote all my days in your book.

You planned those days before any of them began.

## Psalms 139:17

God, your thoughts are precious to me. 💭

There are so many of them!

**Help with a word:** Precious means very valuable or important.

## Psalms 139:18

If I counted your thoughts, there would be more than all the grains of sand. 🏖️

When I wake up, I am still with you.

## Psalms 139:19

God, I wish you would kill the people who do evil. ⚖️

You people who want to kill others, get away from me!

**Help with this verse:** David is speaking to God first.

Then David speaks to the violent people.

## Psalms 139:20

These people tell lies about you, God. 🗣️

Your enemies use your name without respect.

## Psalms 139:21

LORD, I hate the people who hate you. 💔

I am disgusted by the people who turn against you.

**Help with a word:** Disgusted means feeling very strongly that something is wrong and hateful.

## Psalms 139:22

I hate them completely. 💔

I count them as my enemies.

**Help with this verse:** David is telling God how he feels about God's enemies.

## Psalms 139:23

God, look deeply into my life and know my heart. 🔍

Test me and know my worries.

**Help with this picture:** My heart means my inner thoughts, feelings, and wishes.

## Psalms 139:24

See if there is anything in my life that offends you. 👣

Lead me in the way that lasts forever.

**Help with words:** To offend God is to do something that is wrong in God's sight.

**Help with this picture:** The way is a picture of how a person lives.
