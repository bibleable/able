# Psalms 88 — A prayer from deep darkness

**About this chapter**

Heman’s prayer describes suffering without a joyful ending.

The questions and accusations remain his words of distress.

## Psalms 88:1

A song. 📖

A psalm of the sons of Korah.

For the choir leader, according to Mahalath Leannoth.

A Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite.

LORD, God who saves me, I cry before You day and night.

**Help with Mahalath Leannoth and Maskil:** Song terms whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 88:2

May my prayer come before You. 📖

Listen closely to my cry.

## Psalms 88:3

My soul is full of trouble. 📖

My life comes near Sheol.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Psalms 88:4

I am counted among people going down to the Pit. 📖

I am like a man without strength.

**Help with the Pit:** The grave or place of the dead.

## Psalms 88:5

I am abandoned among the dead. 📖

I am like the killed people lying in graves.

You remember them no more.

They are cut off from Your care.

## Psalms 88:6

You have laid me in the lowest Pit, in the darkest depths. 📖

## Psalms 88:7

Your wrath weighs heavily on me. 📖

All Your waves have covered me.

Selah.

**Help with wrath and waves:** Fierce anger, pictured as overwhelming waters.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 88:8

You have taken my friends away. 📖

You made me disgusting to them.

I am shut in and cannot escape.

## Psalms 88:9

My eyes grow dim with grief. 📖

LORD, I call You every day.

I spread my hands toward You.

## Psalms 88:10

Do You do wonders for the dead? 📖

Do departed spirits rise to praise You?

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 88:11

Can Your faithful love be announced in the grave? 📖

Can Your faithfulness be announced in Abaddon?

**Help with Abaddon:** A name connected with destruction and the place of the dead.

## Psalms 88:12

Will Your wonders be known in darkness? 📖

Will Your righteousness be known in the land where everything is forgotten?

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 88:13

But LORD, I cry to You for help. 📖

My prayer comes before You in the morning.

## Psalms 88:14

LORD, why do You reject me? 📖

Why do You hide Your face from me?

## Psalms 88:15

I have suffered and been near death since youth. 📖

I have carried Your terrors.

I have lost hope.

## Psalms 88:16

Your wrath has swept over me. 📖

Your terrors have destroyed me.

## Psalms 88:17

All day they surround me like water. 📖

They close in on every side.

## Psalms 88:18

You have taken away those I love and my friend. 📖

Darkness is my closest companion.
