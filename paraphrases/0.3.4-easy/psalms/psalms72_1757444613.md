# Psalms 72 — A prayer for the king’s just rule

**About this chapter**

This song prays for the king and for people under his rule.

It ends with praise to God and a closing statement about David’s prayers.

## Psalms 72:1

Of Solomon. 📖

God, give the king Your justice.

Give the king’s son Your righteousness.

**Help with justice and righteousness:** Fair judgment and doing what is right.

## Psalms 72:2

May he judge Your people rightly. 📖

May he give justice to people who suffer.

## Psalms 72:3

May the mountains bring peace to the people. 📖

May the hills bring righteousness.

## Psalms 72:4

May he defend suffering people’s rights. 📖

May he save needy people’s children.

May he crush the cruel oppressor.

## Psalms 72:5

May people fear him as long as the sun shines and the moon remains. 📖

May this last through all generations.

## Psalms 72:6

May he be like rain falling on newly cut grass. 📖

May he be like spring showers watering the earth.

## Psalms 72:7

May righteous people flourish in his days. 📖

May plenty abound until the moon is no more.

## Psalms 72:8

May he rule from sea to sea. 📖

May he rule from the Euphrates to the earth’s ends.

## Psalms 72:9

May wandering peoples bow before him. 📖

May his enemies lick the dust.

**Help with lick the dust:** A picture of complete defeat and submission.

## Psalms 72:10

May Tarshish’s kings and distant shores bring tribute. 📖

May Sheba’s and Seba’s kings offer gifts.

**Help with tribute:** Gifts or payments showing submission to a ruler.

## Psalms 72:11

May all kings bow before him. 📖

May all nations serve him.

## Psalms 72:12

He will rescue needy people who cry out. 📖

He will rescue suffering people who have no helper.

## Psalms 72:13

He will pity poor and needy people. 📖

He will save the lives of people treated cruelly.

## Psalms 72:14

He will rescue them from cruelty and violence. 📖

Their blood is precious in his sight.

**Help with their blood:** Their lives.

## Psalms 72:15

May he live long! 📖

May he receive gold from Sheba.

May people always pray for him and bless him all day.

## Psalms 72:16

May grain be plentiful in the land and wave on the hilltops. 📖

May its fruit trees flourish like Lebanon’s forests.

May its city people flourish like grass in a field.

## Psalms 72:17

May his name last forever, as long as the sun shines. 📖

May all nations be blessed in him.

May they call him blessed.

## Psalms 72:18

Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel! 📖

He alone does wonderful deeds.

## Psalms 72:19

Blessed be His glorious name forever! 📖

May all the earth be filled with His glory.

Amen and amen.

**Help with Amen:** An expression of agreement: let it be so.

## Psalms 72:20

This ends the prayers of David son of Jesse. 📖
