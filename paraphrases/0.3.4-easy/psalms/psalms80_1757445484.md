# Psalms 80 — Restore us and let Your face shine

**About this chapter**

Asaph asks God to restore His people.

The song pictures them as a flock and as a vine.

## Psalms 80:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “The Lilies of the Covenant.” 📖

A psalm of Asaph.

Shepherd of Israel, hear us.

You lead Joseph like a flock.

You sit enthroned between the cherubim.

Shine out,

**Help with cherubim:** Heavenly beings.

Joseph is used here for God’s people.

## Psalms 80:2

before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh. 📖

Stir up Your mighty power and come to save us.

## Psalms 80:3

God, restore us. 📖

Make Your face shine on us so we may be saved.

**Help with shining face:** A picture of showing favor and care.

## Psalms 80:4

LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your anger burn against Your people’s prayers? 📖

**Help with God of Hosts:** God who commands heavenly armies.

## Psalms 80:5

You fed them bread made of tears. 📖

You made them drink a full measure of tears.

**Help with tears as food and drink:** A picture of deep, continuing sorrow.

## Psalms 80:6

You make us fight with our neighbors. 📖

Our enemies mock us.

## Psalms 80:7

God of Hosts, restore us. 📖

Make Your face shine on us so we may be saved.

## Psalms 80:8

You pulled a vine out of Egypt. 📖

You drove out nations and planted it.

**Help with vine:** A picture of Israel.

## Psalms 80:9

You cleared the ground for it. 📖

It took root and filled the land.

## Psalms 80:10

Its shade covered the mountains. 📖

Its branches covered the mighty cedars.

## Psalms 80:11

Its branches reached the Sea. 📖

Its shoots reached toward the River.

**Help with Sea and River:** The Mediterranean Sea and the Euphrates River are commonly understood here.

This is an editorial identification, not additional source wording.

## Psalms 80:12

Why have You broken its walls? 📖

Everyone passing by picks its fruit.

## Psalms 80:13

A forest boar tears it apart. 📖

Creatures of the field feed on it.

**Help with boar:** A wild pig.

## Psalms 80:14

God of Hosts, please return! 📖

Look down from heaven and see.

Care for this vine,

## Psalms 80:15

for the root Your right hand planted. 📖

Care for the son You raised for Yourself.

## Psalms 80:16

Your vine has been cut down and burned. 📖

They perish when Your face rebukes them.

**Help with rebukes:** Expresses strong anger and correction.

## Psalms 80:17

Let Your hand rest on the man at Your right hand. 📖

Let it rest on the son of man You raised for Yourself.

**Help with son of man:** A human being.

The precise identity here remains for further editorial review.

## Psalms 80:18

Then we will not turn away from You. 📖

Give us life again, and we will call on Your name.

## Psalms 80:19

LORD God of Hosts, restore us. 📖

Make Your face shine on us so we may be saved.
