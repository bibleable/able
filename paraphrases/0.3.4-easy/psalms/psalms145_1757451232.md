# Psalms 145 — Praise God’s everlasting kingdom

**About this chapter**

David praises God’s greatness, kindness, and rule.

He calls every creature to bless God’s name.

## Psalms 145:1

A psalm of praise. 📖

Of David.

My God and King, I will lift You high.

I will bless Your name forever and ever.

## Psalms 145:2

Every day I will bless You. 📖

I will praise Your name forever and ever.

## Psalms 145:3

The LORD is great and greatly deserves praise. 📖

No one can fully discover His greatness.

## Psalms 145:4

One generation will praise Your works to the next. 📖

They will announce Your mighty acts,

## Psalms 145:5

and the glorious splendor of Your majesty. 📖

I will think deeply about Your wonderful works.

**Help with splendor and majesty:** Great royal glory.

## Psalms 145:6

They will announce the power of Your awesome deeds. 📖

I will tell Your greatness.

**Help with awesome:** Inspiring deep wonder and fear.

## Psalms 145:7

They will praise Your abundant goodness and sing joyfully about Your righteousness. 📖

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 145:8

The LORD is gracious and compassionate. 📖

He is slow to anger and full of faithful love.

**Help with gracious and compassionate:** Freely kind, and deeply caring toward people who suffer.

## Psalms 145:9

The LORD is good to everyone. 📖

His compassion rests on everything He made.

## Psalms 145:10

LORD, everything You made will thank You. 📖

Your saints will bless You.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 145:11

They will tell Your kingdom’s glory and speak of Your might, 📖

## Psalms 145:12

making Your mighty acts known to people. 📖

They will tell Your kingdom’s glorious splendor.

## Psalms 145:13

Your kingdom lasts forever. 📖

Your rule lasts through all generations.

The LORD is faithful in all His words and kind in all His actions.

## Psalms 145:14

The LORD supports everyone who falls. 📖

He lifts everyone bowed down.

## Psalms 145:15

Everyone’s eyes look to You. 📖

You give them food at the right time.

## Psalms 145:16

You open Your hand. 📖

You satisfy every living thing’s desire.

## Psalms 145:17

The LORD is righteous in all His ways. 📖

He is kind in all His deeds.

## Psalms 145:18

The LORD is near everyone who calls Him, everyone who calls Him in truth. 📖

## Psalms 145:19

He fulfills the desires of people who fear Him. 📖

He hears their cry and saves them.

**Help with fear Him:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 145:20

The LORD guards everyone who loves Him. 📖

But He will destroy all wicked people.

## Psalms 145:21

My mouth will announce the LORD’s praise. 📖

Let every creature bless His holy name forever and ever.
