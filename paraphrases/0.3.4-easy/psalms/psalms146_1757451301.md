# Psalms 146 — Trust the Maker of heaven and earth

**About this chapter**

The singer contrasts mortal rulers with the faithful LORD, who helps people in need.

## Psalms 146:1

Hallelujah! 📖

My soul, praise the LORD.

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.

## Psalms 146:2

I will praise the LORD all my life. 📖

I will sing praises to my God as long as I exist.

## Psalms 146:3

Do not trust princes or mortal people who cannot save. 📖

**Help with mortal:** Human and subject to death.

## Psalms 146:4

When his spirit leaves, he returns to the ground. 📖

That very day, his plans perish.

## Psalms 146:5

Blessed is the person whose help is Jacob’s God. 📖

His hope is in the LORD his God,

## Psalms 146:6

who made heaven, earth, the sea, and everything in them. 📖

He stays faithful forever.

## Psalms 146:7

He gives justice to people treated cruelly and food to hungry people. 📖

The LORD frees prisoners.

## Psalms 146:8

The LORD opens blind people’s eyes. 📖

The LORD lifts people weighed down.

The LORD loves righteous people.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 146:9

The LORD protects foreigners. 📖

He supports children without fathers and widows.

But He defeats wicked people’s ways.

**Help with widows:** Women whose husbands have died.

## Psalms 146:10

The LORD reigns forever. 📖

Zion, your God reigns through all generations.

Hallelujah!
