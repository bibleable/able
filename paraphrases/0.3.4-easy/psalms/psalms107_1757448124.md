# Psalms 107 — Give thanks for the LORD’s rescues

**About this chapter**

The song describes people in different kinds of trouble.

It repeats their cries, the LORD’s rescue, and the call to thank Him.

## Psalms 107:1

Thank the LORD, because He is good. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 107:2

Let the people the LORD rescued say this. 📖

He rescued them from the enemy’s hand,

## Psalms 107:3

and gathered them from lands in east, west, north, and south. 📖

## Psalms 107:4

Some wandered in desert wastelands. 📖

They found no path to a city where they could live.

## Psalms 107:5

They were hungry and thirsty. 📖

Their souls grew weak within them.

## Psalms 107:6

Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. 📖

He rescued them from their distress.

## Psalms 107:7

He led them on a straight path to a city where they could live. 📖

## Psalms 107:8

Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love and His wonders for human beings. 📖

## Psalms 107:9

He satisfies thirsty people. 📖

He fills hungry people with good things.

## Psalms 107:10

Some sat in darkness and death’s shadow. 📖

They were prisoners suffering in chains,

## Psalms 107:11

because they rebelled against God’s words. 📖

They despised the Most High’s advice.

## Psalms 107:12

He humbled their hearts with hard work. 📖

They stumbled, and no one helped.

## Psalms 107:13

Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. 📖

He saved them from their distress.

## Psalms 107:14

He brought them out of darkness and death’s shadow. 📖

He broke their chains.

## Psalms 107:15

Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love and His wonders for human beings. 📖

## Psalms 107:16

He broke bronze gates and cut through iron bars. 📖

## Psalms 107:17

Foolish people suffered because of their rebellious ways and sins. 📖

## Psalms 107:18

They hated all food. 📖

They came near death’s gates.

## Psalms 107:19

Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. 📖

He saved them from their distress.

## Psalms 107:20

He sent His word and healed them. 📖

He rescued them from the Pit.

**Help with the Pit:** The grave or place of the dead.

## Psalms 107:21

Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love and His wonders for human beings. 📖

## Psalms 107:22

Let them give sacrifices of thanks. 📖

Let them tell His works with rejoicing.

**Help with sacrifices:** Offerings given to God.

## Psalms 107:23

Others sailed in ships and traded on mighty waters. 📖

## Psalms 107:24

They saw the LORD’s works and His wonders in the deep. 📖

## Psalms 107:25

He spoke and raised a violent storm. 📖

It lifted the sea’s waves.

## Psalms 107:26

They rose to the heavens and sank to the depths. 📖

Their courage melted in their distress.

## Psalms 107:27

They swayed and staggered like drunk people. 📖

All their skill was useless.

## Psalms 107:28

Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. 📖

He brought them out of their distress.

## Psalms 107:29

He quieted the storm to a whisper. 📖

The sea’s waves became still.

## Psalms 107:30

They rejoiced at the quiet. 📖

He guided them to the harbor they wanted.

**Help with harbor:** A sheltered place for ships.

## Psalms 107:31

Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love and His wonders for human beings. 📖

## Psalms 107:32

Let them lift Him high in the people’s gathering. 📖

Let them praise Him in the elders’ council.

## Psalms 107:33

He turns rivers into deserts. 📖

He turns springs into thirsty ground,

## Psalms 107:34

and fruitful land into salt fields because its people are wicked. 📖

## Psalms 107:35

He turns desert into pools of water. 📖

He turns dry land into flowing springs.

## Psalms 107:36

He settles hungry people there. 📖

They establish a city to live in.

## Psalms 107:37

They plant fields and vineyards that give a fruitful harvest. 📖

## Psalms 107:38

He blesses them, and they increase greatly. 📖

He does not let their herds shrink.

## Psalms 107:39

When cruelty, evil, and sorrow make them fewer and bring them low, 📖

## Psalms 107:40

He pours contempt on nobles. 📖

He makes them wander in a wasteland without paths.

**Help with contempt and nobles:** Scorn, and leaders with high rank.

## Psalms 107:41

But He lifts needy people from suffering. 📖

He increases their families like flocks.

## Psalms 107:42

Upright people see and rejoice. 📖

All evil shuts its mouth.

**Help with upright:** Honest and right.

## Psalms 107:43

Let wise people pay attention to these things. 📖

Let them think about the LORD’s faithful love.
