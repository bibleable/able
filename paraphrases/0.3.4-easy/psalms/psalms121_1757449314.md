# Psalms 121 — The LORD watches over you

**About this chapter**

The singer speaks of help from the Maker of heaven and earth.

## Psalms 121:1

A song of ascents. 📖

I lift my eyes to the hills.

Where does my help come from?

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 121:2

My help comes from the LORD, who made heaven and earth. 📖

## Psalms 121:3

He will not let your foot slip. 📖

Your Protector will not doze.

## Psalms 121:4

Look, Israel’s Protector does not doze or sleep. 📖

## Psalms 121:5

The LORD keeps you safe. 📖

The LORD is the shade at your right hand.

## Psalms 121:6

The sun will not strike you by day. 📖

The moon will not strike you by night.

## Psalms 121:7

The LORD will guard you from all evil. 📖

He will preserve your soul.

## Psalms 121:8

The LORD will watch your coming and going now and forever. 📖
