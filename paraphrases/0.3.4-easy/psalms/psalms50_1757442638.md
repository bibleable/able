# Psalms 50 — God calls His people to account

**About this chapter**

Asaph’s song describes God coming to judge.

God speaks to His people and then to wicked people.

## Psalms 50:1

A psalm of Asaph. 📖

The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks.

He calls the earth from sunrise to sunset.

## Psalms 50:2

God shines from Zion, perfect in beauty. 📖

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem and the LORD’s holy mountain.

## Psalms 50:3

Our God comes and will not be silent! 📖

Fire that destroys goes before Him.

A violent storm rages around Him.

## Psalms 50:4

He calls the heavens above and the earth. 📖

He comes to judge His people.

## Psalms 50:5

He says, “Gather My saints to Me. 📖

They made a covenant with Me through sacrifice.”

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

A covenant is a serious agreement.

A sacrifice is an offering to God.

## Psalms 50:6

The heavens announce His righteousness. 📖

God Himself is Judge.

Selah.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 50:7

He says, “My people, hear Me, and I will speak. 📖

Israel, I will testify against you.

I am God, your God.

## Psalms 50:8

I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices. 📖

Your burnt offerings are always before Me.

**Help with rebuke:** Strongly correct.

Burnt offerings are sacrifices burned in worship.

## Psalms 50:9

I do not need a bull from your stall. 📖

I do not need goats from your pens.

## Psalms 50:10

Every forest animal is Mine. 📖

The cattle on a thousand hills are Mine.

## Psalms 50:11

I know every bird in the mountains. 📖

The field’s creatures are Mine.

## Psalms 50:12

If I were hungry, I would not tell you. 📖

The world and everything in it are Mine.

## Psalms 50:13

Do I eat bulls’ flesh? 📖

Do I drink goats’ blood?

## Psalms 50:14

Give God a thank offering. 📖

Keep your vows to the Most High.

**Help with thank offering and vows:** An offering expressing thanks, and serious promises.

## Psalms 50:15

Call on Me on the day of trouble. 📖

I will rescue you, and you will honor Me.”

## Psalms 50:16

But God says to wicked people, “What right have you to recite My laws? 📖

What right have you to speak My covenant?

## Psalms 50:17

You hate My instruction. 📖

You throw My words behind you.

## Psalms 50:18

When you see a thief, you befriend him. 📖

You join with adulterers.

**Help with adulterers:** People who have sex with another person’s husband or wife.

## Psalms 50:19

You let your mouth speak evil. 📖

You put your tongue to work telling lies.

## Psalms 50:20

You sit and speak against your brother. 📖

You spread lies about your own mother’s son.

## Psalms 50:21

You did these things, and I kept silent. 📖

You thought I was just like you.

But now I rebuke you and accuse you to your face.

## Psalms 50:22

People who forget God, consider this now. 📖

Otherwise I may tear you to pieces, with no one to rescue you.

## Psalms 50:23

The person who sacrifices a thank offering honors Me. 📖

I will show God’s rescue to the person who rightly orders his way.”
