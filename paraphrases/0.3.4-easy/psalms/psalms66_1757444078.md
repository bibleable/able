# Psalms 66 — Praise and thanks for God’s rescue

**About this chapter**

The song calls everyone to praise God.

It moves from the people’s experience to one person’s thanks.

## Psalms 66:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A song.

A psalm.

All the earth, make a joyful sound to God!

## Psalms 66:2

Sing about His name’s glory. 📖

Make His praise glorious.

## Psalms 66:3

Say to God, “How awesome are Your deeds! 📖

Your power is so great that Your enemies crouch in fear before You.

**Help with awesome:** Inspiring deep wonder and fear.

## Psalms 66:4

All the earth bows before You. 📖

They sing praise to You.

They sing praise to Your name.”

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 66:5

Come and see God’s works. 📖

His deeds toward human beings are awesome.

## Psalms 66:6

He turned the sea into dry land. 📖

They walked through the waters.

There we rejoiced in Him.

## Psalms 66:7

He rules forever by His power. 📖

His eyes watch the nations.

Do not let rebellious people raise themselves up.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 66:8

Peoples, bless our God. 📖

Let the sound of His praise be heard.

## Psalms 66:9

He keeps us alive. 📖

He keeps our feet from slipping.

## Psalms 66:10

God, You have tested us. 📖

You have purified us like silver.

## Psalms 66:11

You led us into the net. 📖

You put heavy loads on our backs.

## Psalms 66:12

You let men ride over our heads. 📖

We went through fire and water.

But You brought us to a place of plenty.

## Psalms 66:13

I will enter Your house with burnt offerings. 📖

I will keep my vows to You,

**Help with burnt offerings and vows:** Sacrifices burned as offerings to God, and serious promises.

## Psalms 66:14

## Psalms 66:15

I will offer well-fed animals to You as burnt offerings. 📖

I will offer rams with fragrant smoke.

I will offer bulls and goats.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 66:16

Come and listen, everyone who fears God. 📖

I will tell what He has done for me.

**Help with fears God:** Respects Him and takes His authority seriously.

## Psalms 66:17

I cried to Him with my mouth. 📖

I praised Him with my tongue.

## Psalms 66:18

If I had treasured evil in my heart, the Lord would not have listened. 📖

## Psalms 66:19

But God has surely heard. 📖

He has listened to the sound of my prayer.

## Psalms 66:20

Blessed be God! 📖

He has not rejected my prayer or kept His faithful love from me.
