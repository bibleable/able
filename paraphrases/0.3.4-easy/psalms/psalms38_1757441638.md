# Psalms 38 — A prayer amid illness, guilt, and rejection

**About this chapter**

David describes his own suffering and sin.

He asks the LORD for help.

His words do not say that every person’s illness is caused by sin.

## Psalms 38:1

A psalm of David, for remembrance. 📖

LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger.

Do not discipline me in Your fury.

**Help with rebuke and discipline:** Strong correction for wrongdoing.

## Psalms 38:2

Your arrows have pierced me deeply. 📖

Your hand has pressed down on me.

**Help with arrows and hand:** David pictures his suffering as blows from God.

## Psalms 38:3

No part of my body is healthy because of Your anger. 📖

My bones have no rest because of my sin.

## Psalms 38:4

My sins have overwhelmed me. 📖

They are a load too heavy to carry.

## Psalms 38:5

My wounds stink and are infected because of my sinful foolishness. 📖

## Psalms 38:6

I am bent over and brought low. 📖

I go about mourning all day.

## Psalms 38:7

My lower body is full of burning pain. 📖

No part of my body is healthy.

## Psalms 38:8

I am numb and badly crushed. 📖

My heart is in anguish, and I groan.

## Psalms 38:9

Lord, all my desires lie before You. 📖

My groaning is not hidden from You.

## Psalms 38:10

My heart pounds. 📖

My strength fails.

Even the light of my eyes has faded.

## Psalms 38:11

Those I love and my friends keep away from my disease. 📖

My relatives stand far away.

## Psalms 38:12

People who seek my life set traps. 📖

People who wish me harm speak of destruction.

They plan lies all day.

## Psalms 38:13

But like a man who cannot hear, I do not hear. 📖

Like a man who cannot speak, I do not open my mouth.

## Psalms 38:14

I am like someone who cannot hear. 📖

My mouth gives no reply.

## Psalms 38:15

LORD, I wait for You. 📖

Lord my God, You will answer.

## Psalms 38:16

I said, “Do not let them celebrate over me. 📖

They mock me when my foot slips.”

## Psalms 38:17

I am ready to fall. 📖

My pain is always with me.

## Psalms 38:18

Yes, I confess my wrongdoing. 📖

I am troubled by my sin.

## Psalms 38:19

Many are my enemies without cause. 📖

Many hate me without reason.

## Psalms 38:20

People who repay my good with evil attack me because I pursue good. 📖

## Psalms 38:21

LORD, do not abandon me. 📖

My God, do not be far from me.

## Psalms 38:22

Come quickly to help me, Lord my Savior. 📖
