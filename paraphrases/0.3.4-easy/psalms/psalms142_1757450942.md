# Psalms 142 — A prayer from the cave

**About this chapter**

David describes being alone and asks the LORD for rescue.

## Psalms 142:1

A Maskil of David, when he was in the cave. 📖

A prayer.

I cry aloud to the LORD.

I raise my voice to the LORD for mercy.

**Help with Maskil:** A song term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 142:2

I pour my complaint before Him. 📖

I tell Him my trouble.

## Psalms 142:3

My spirit grows weak within me, but You know my way. 📖

They have hidden a trap along my path.

## Psalms 142:4

Look to my right and see. 📖

No one pays attention to me.

There is no refuge for me.

No one cares for my soul.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 142:5

I cry to You, LORD: “You are my refuge and my portion in the land of the living.” 📖

**Help with portion:** What David receives and depends on for his life.

## Psalms 142:6

Listen to my cry because I have been brought very low. 📖

Rescue me from those chasing me.

They are too strong for me.

## Psalms 142:7

Free my soul from prison so I may praise Your name. 📖

Righteous people will gather around me because of Your goodness to me.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.
