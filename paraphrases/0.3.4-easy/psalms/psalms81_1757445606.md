# Psalms 81 — Listen to the LORD who rescued you

**About this chapter**

Asaph’s song calls for celebration.

It includes the LORD’s words about rescue, disobedience, and the blessing His people refused.

## Psalms 81:1

For the choir leader, according to Gittith. 📖

Of Asaph.

Sing for joy to God our strength.

Make a joyful sound to Jacob’s God.

**Help with Gittith:** A musical term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 81:2

Raise a song and strike the tambourine. 📖

Play the sweet-sounding harp and lyre.

**Help with instruments:** A tambourine is shaken or struck.

Harps and lyres have strings.

## Psalms 81:3

Sound the ram’s horn at the New Moon. 📖

Sound it at the full moon on our Feast day.

**Help with ram’s horn:** An animal horn used as a trumpet.

## Psalms 81:4

This is a law for Israel. 📖

It is a command from Jacob’s God.

## Psalms 81:5

He appointed it as a testimony for Joseph when he went over Egypt’s land. 📖

There I heard an unfamiliar language:

**Help with testimony:** A declaration to remember.

The pronoun and speaker transitions here need further editorial review.

## Psalms 81:6

“I lifted the load from his shoulder. 📖

His hands were freed from the basket.

## Psalms 81:7

You called in distress, and I rescued you. 📖

I answered from the thundercloud.

I tested you at Meribah’s waters.

Selah.

**Help with the speaker:** These are the LORD’s words.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 81:8

My people, hear Me, and I will warn you. 📖

Israel, if only you would listen to Me!

## Psalms 81:9

There must be no strange god among you. 📖

You must not bow to a foreign god.

## Psalms 81:10

I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt. 📖

Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.

## Psalms 81:11

But My people would not listen to Me. 📖

Israel would not obey Me.

## Psalms 81:12

So I handed them over to their stubborn hearts. 📖

I let them follow their own plans.

## Psalms 81:13

If only My people would listen to Me! 📖

If only Israel would follow My ways,

## Psalms 81:14

I would soon bring their enemies down. 📖

I would turn My hand against their foes!

## Psalms 81:15

People who hate the LORD would pretend to obey. 📖

Their doom would last forever.

## Psalms 81:16

But I would feed you the finest wheat. 📖

I would satisfy you with honey from the rock.”
