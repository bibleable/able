# Psalms 71 — A prayer for lifelong help

**About this chapter**

The singer remembers God’s care since birth.

He asks for rescue and help to tell the next generation about God.

## Psalms 71:1

LORD, I have taken refuge in You. 📖

Never let me be put to shame.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 71:2

Rescue and save me in Your justice. 📖

Listen closely and save me.

## Psalms 71:3

Be my rock of refuge where I can always go. 📖

Give the command to save me.

You are my rock and fortress.

**Help with rock and fortress:** Pictures of strong protection.

## Psalms 71:4

My God, rescue me from wicked people’s hands. 📖

Rescue me from the grip of unjust and cruel people.

## Psalms 71:5

Lord GOD, You are my hope. 📖

I have trusted You since my youth.

## Psalms 71:6

I have depended on You since birth. 📖

You brought me from my mother’s womb.

My praise is always for You.

## Psalms 71:7

I have become a portent to many people. 📖

But You are my strong refuge.

**Help with portent:** A sign that makes people wonder or fear.

## Psalms 71:8

My mouth is full of Your praise and splendor all day. 📖

**Help with splendor:** Great glory.

## Psalms 71:9

Do not throw me away when I am old. 📖

Do not abandon me when my strength fails.

## Psalms 71:10

My enemies speak against me. 📖

People waiting to take my life plan together.

## Psalms 71:11

They say, “God has abandoned him. 📖

Chase him and seize him.

No one will rescue him.”

## Psalms 71:12

God, do not be far from me. 📖

My God, hurry to help me.

## Psalms 71:13

May those accusing my soul be ashamed and destroyed. 📖

May people seeking my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace.

## Psalms 71:14

But I will always hope. 📖

I will praise You more and more.

## Psalms 71:15

My mouth will tell of Your righteousness and rescue all day. 📖

I cannot know their full measure.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 71:16

I will come in the Lord GOD’s strength. 📖

I will announce Your righteousness, Yours alone.

## Psalms 71:17

God, You have taught me since my youth. 📖

To this day I tell of Your wonderful deeds.

## Psalms 71:18

God, do not abandon me even when I am old and gray. 📖

Let me tell the next generation about Your power.

Let me tell everyone yet to come about Your might.

## Psalms 71:19

God, Your righteousness reaches the heavens. 📖

You have done great things.

God, who is like You?

## Psalms 71:20

You have made me experience many troubles and disasters. 📖

But You will give me life again.

You will bring me back up, even from the earth’s depths.

## Psalms 71:21

You will increase my honor. 📖

You will comfort me again.

## Psalms 71:22

My God, I will praise Your faithfulness with the harp. 📖

Holy One of Israel, I will sing praise to You with the lyre.

**Help with harp and lyre:** Stringed musical instruments.

## Psalms 71:23

My lips will shout for joy when I praise You. 📖

My soul, which You rescued, will rejoice too.

## Psalms 71:24

My tongue will tell of Your righteousness all day. 📖

People seeking my harm are disgraced and confused.
