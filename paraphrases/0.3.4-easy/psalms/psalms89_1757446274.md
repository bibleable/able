# Psalms 89 — God’s promise to David and a cry after rejection

**About this chapter**

Ethan praises God and recalls His promises to David.

He then asks how those promises fit the king’s present humiliation.

## Psalms 89:1

A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite. 📖

I will sing of the LORD’s faithful love forever.

I will announce Your faithfulness to all generations.

**Help with Maskil:** A song term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 89:2

I said, “Faithful love is built up forever. 📖

You establish Your faithfulness in the heavens.”

## Psalms 89:3

You said, “I made a covenant with My chosen one. 📖

I swore to David My servant:

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Psalms 89:4

‘I will establish your descendants forever. 📖

I will build up your throne for all generations.’”

Selah.

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 89:5

LORD, the heavens praise Your wonders. 📖

The gathering of holy ones praises Your faithfulness too.

**Help with holy ones:** Heavenly beings.

## Psalms 89:6

Who in the skies compares with the LORD? 📖

Which heavenly being is like the LORD?

## Psalms 89:7

God is greatly feared in the holy ones’ council. 📖

He is more awesome than everyone around Him.

**Help with council and awesome:** A gathering for discussion, and inspiring deep wonder and fear.

## Psalms 89:8

LORD God of Hosts, who is like You? 📖

Mighty LORD, Your faithfulness surrounds You.

**Help with God of Hosts:** God who commands heavenly armies.

## Psalms 89:9

You rule the raging sea. 📖

When its waves rise, You quiet them.

## Psalms 89:10

You crushed Rahab like a dead body. 📖

You scattered Your enemies with Your mighty arm.

**Help with Rahab:** A name used in poetry for a force opposed to God.

## Psalms 89:11

The heavens and earth belong to You. 📖

You founded the earth and everything in it.

## Psalms 89:12

You created north and south. 📖

Tabor and Hermon shout for joy at Your name.

**Help with Tabor and Hermon:** Mountains pictured as rejoicing.

## Psalms 89:13

Your arm is mighty. 📖

Your hand is strong.

Your right hand is lifted high.

## Psalms 89:14

Righteousness and justice support Your throne. 📖

Faithful love and faithfulness go before You.

**Help with righteousness and justice:** Doing what is right and judging fairly.

## Psalms 89:15

Blessed are people who know the joyful sound. 📖

LORD, they walk in the light of Your presence.

## Psalms 89:16

They rejoice in Your name all day. 📖

They rejoice greatly in Your righteousness.

## Psalms 89:17

You are the glory of their strength. 📖

Through Your favor, our horn is lifted high.

**Help with horn:** A picture of strength and honor.

## Psalms 89:18

Our shield surely belongs to the LORD. 📖

Our king belongs to the Holy One of Israel.

## Psalms 89:19

You once spoke in a vision to Your godly ones. 📖

You said, “I gave help to a warrior.

I raised up one chosen from the people.

**Help with godly:** Devoted to God.

## Psalms 89:20

I found My servant David. 📖

I anointed him with My sacred oil.

**Help with anointed:** Appointed through a ceremony using oil.

## Psalms 89:21

My hand will support him. 📖

My arm will surely strengthen him.

## Psalms 89:22

No enemy will demand tribute from him. 📖

No wicked man will treat him cruelly.

**Help with tribute:** Payments showing submission to a ruler.

## Psalms 89:23

I will crush his enemies before him. 📖

I will strike down those who hate him.

## Psalms 89:24

My faithfulness and faithful love will be with him. 📖

Through My name, his horn will be lifted high.

## Psalms 89:25

I will set his hand over the sea. 📖

I will set his right hand on the rivers.

**Help with hand over waters:** A picture of authority extending over these places.

## Psalms 89:26

He will call to Me, ‘You are my Father, my God, the Rock of my rescue.’ 📖

## Psalms 89:27

I will appoint him as My firstborn. 📖

He will be the highest of the earth’s kings.

**Help with firstborn:** Here, a position of highest honor.

## Psalms 89:28

I will keep My faithful love for him forever. 📖

My covenant with him will stand firm.

## Psalms 89:29

I will establish his line forever. 📖

His throne will last as long as the heavens.

**Help with his line:** His descendants.

## Psalms 89:30

If his sons abandon My law and do not follow My judgments, 📖

## Psalms 89:31

if they break My laws and fail to keep My commands, 📖

## Psalms 89:32

I will punish their offenses with a rod. 📖

I will punish their sins with stripes.

**Help with rod and stripes:** A stick used for punishment, and blows leaving marks on the body.

## Psalms 89:33

But I will not take My faithful love from him. 📖

I will never betray My faithfulness.

## Psalms 89:34

I will not break My covenant. 📖

I will not change what My lips have spoken.

## Psalms 89:35

Once and for all, I swore by My holiness. 📖

I will not lie to David.

## Psalms 89:36

His descendants will last forever. 📖

His throne before Me will be like the sun,

## Psalms 89:37

like the moon established forever, a faithful witness in the sky.” 📖

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 89:38

But now You have rejected him and turned him away. 📖

You are furious with Your anointed one.

## Psalms 89:39

You have rejected Your covenant with Your servant. 📖

You have dirtied his crown in the dust.

## Psalms 89:40

You have broken all his walls. 📖

You have turned his strongholds into broken stones.

## Psalms 89:41

Everyone passing by robs him. 📖

His neighbors scorn him.

## Psalms 89:42

You have lifted his enemies’ right hand high. 📖

You have made all his enemies rejoice.

## Psalms 89:43

You have bent his sword’s edge. 📖

You have not supported him in battle.

## Psalms 89:44

You have ended his splendor. 📖

You have thrown his throne to the ground.

## Psalms 89:45

You have shortened his youth’s days. 📖

You have covered him with shame.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 89:46

How long, LORD? 📖

Will You hide forever?

Will Your wrath keep burning like fire?

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Psalms 89:47

Remember how short my life is! 📖

For what futility You have created all people!

**Help with futility:** Life or effort coming to nothing.

## Psalms 89:48

Who can live and never see death? 📖

Can he rescue his soul from Sheol’s power?

Selah.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 89:49

Lord, where is Your faithful love of old? 📖

You faithfully swore it to David.

## Psalms 89:50

Lord, remember Your servants’ disgrace. 📖

I carry it in my heart from so many people,

## Psalms 89:51

because Your enemies have mocked, LORD. 📖

They have mocked every step of Your anointed one!

## Psalms 89:52

Blessed be the LORD forever! 📖

Amen and amen.

**Help with Amen:** An expression of agreement: let it be so.
