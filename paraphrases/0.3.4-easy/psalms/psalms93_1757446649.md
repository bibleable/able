# Psalms 93 — The LORD reigns

**About this chapter**

This song praises the LORD’s lasting rule and power over the waters.

## Psalms 93:1

The LORD reigns! 📖

He wears majesty like a robe.

He has clothed and armed Himself with strength.

The world is firmly established and cannot be moved.

**Help with majesty:** Great royal power and glory.

## Psalms 93:2

Your throne was established long ago. 📖

You are from all eternity.

**Help with eternity:** Time without beginning or end.

## Psalms 93:3

LORD, the floodwaters have risen. 📖

The rivers have raised their voice.

The seas lift their pounding waves.

## Psalms 93:4

Above roaring waters and the sea’s mighty breaking waves, the LORD on high is majestic. 📖

## Psalms 93:5

Your testimonies are fully confirmed. 📖

Holiness decorates Your house, LORD, for all days to come.

**Help with testimonies and holiness:** What God declares, and His complete purity and greatness.
