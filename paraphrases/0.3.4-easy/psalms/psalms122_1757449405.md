# Psalms 122 — Pray for Jerusalem’s peace

**About this chapter**

David speaks of coming to Jerusalem and calls for prayer for its peace.

## Psalms 122:1

A song of ascents. 📖

Of David.

I was glad when they said, “Let us go to the LORD’s house.”

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 122:2

Jerusalem, our feet are standing inside your gates. 📖

## Psalms 122:3

Jerusalem is built as a city joined together, 📖

## Psalms 122:4

where the tribes go up, the LORD’s tribes. 📖

This is a testimony for Israel, to thank the LORD’s name.

**Help with testimony:** A declaration or instruction for the people to remember.

## Psalms 122:5

Thrones of judgment stand there. 📖

They are the thrones of David’s house.

**Help with David’s house:** David’s royal family and descendants.

## Psalms 122:6

Pray for Jerusalem’s peace: “May people who love you prosper. 📖

## Psalms 122:7

May peace be within your walls. 📖

May there be plenty within your fortresses.”

**Help with fortresses:** Strong places built for protection.

## Psalms 122:8

For my brothers’ and friends’ sake, I will say, “May peace be within you.” 📖

## Psalms 122:9

For the sake of the LORD our God’s house, I will seek your prosperity. 📖
