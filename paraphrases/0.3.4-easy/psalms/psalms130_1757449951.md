# Psalms 130 — Waiting for the LORD’s forgiveness

**About this chapter**

The singer cries for mercy and calls Israel to hope in the LORD.

## Psalms 130:1

A song of ascents. 📖

LORD, I cry to You from the depths!

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 130:2

Lord, hear my voice. 📖

Let Your ears attend to my appeal for mercy.

## Psalms 130:3

LORD, if You kept track of sins, who could stand, Lord? 📖

## Psalms 130:4

But forgiveness is with You so that You may be feared. 📖

**Help with feared:** Respected, with serious attention to God’s authority.

## Psalms 130:5

I wait for the LORD. 📖

My soul waits.

I put my hope in His word.

## Psalms 130:6

My soul waits for the Lord more than watchmen wait for morning. 📖

More than watchmen wait for morning!

## Psalms 130:7

Israel, put your hope in the LORD. 📖

Faithful love is with Him.

He has abundant redemption.

**Help with redemption:** Rescue and freedom.

## Psalms 130:8

He will rescue Israel from all its sins. 📖
