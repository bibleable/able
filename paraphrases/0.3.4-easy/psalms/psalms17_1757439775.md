# Psalms 17 — A prayer for protection and a just decision

**About this chapter**

David asks the LORD to examine him and protect him from his enemies.

## Psalms 17:1

A prayer of David. 📖

LORD, hear my righteous appeal.

Listen to my cry.

Hear my prayer, because my lips do not lie.

**Help with righteous:** Right and just.

## Psalms 17:2

Let the decision proving me right come from You. 📖

Let Your eyes see what is right.

## Psalms 17:3

You have examined my heart and visited me at night. 📖

You have tested me and found no evil.

I have decided not to sin with my mouth.

## Psalms 17:4

As for what people do, Your words have kept me from violent people’s ways. 📖

## Psalms 17:5

My steps have kept to Your paths. 📖

My feet have not slipped.

## Psalms 17:6

I call on You, God, because You will answer me. 📖

Listen closely to me.

Hear my words.

## Psalms 17:7

Show the wonders of Your faithful love. 📖

Your right hand saves people seeking refuge from their enemies.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 17:8

Keep me as the apple of Your eye. 📖

Hide me in the shadow of Your wings,

**Help with apple of Your eye:** The eye’s delicate center, carefully protected.

Wings picture shelter like a bird gives its young.

## Psalms 17:9

away from wicked people who attack me. 📖

Hide me from deadly enemies who surround me.

## Psalms 17:10

They have shut their hard hearts. 📖

Their mouths speak proudly.

## Psalms 17:11

They have tracked us down and now surround us. 📖

They watch for a chance to throw us to the ground,

## Psalms 17:12

like a lion hungry for prey. 📖

They are like a young lion hiding to attack.

**Help with prey:** An animal hunted for food.

## Psalms 17:13

Rise up, LORD, and confront them! 📖

Bring them to their knees.

Rescue me from wicked people by Your sword,

## Psalms 17:14

and by Your hand, LORD, from worldly men whose share is in this life. 📖

Fill Your treasured people’s bellies.

Satisfy their sons so they leave plenty for their children.

## Psalms 17:15

As for me, I will see Your face in righteousness. 📖

When I awake, I will be satisfied in Your presence.
