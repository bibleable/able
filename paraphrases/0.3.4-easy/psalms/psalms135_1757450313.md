# Psalms 135 — Praise the LORD who chose Israel

**About this chapter**

The song praises the LORD’s power, recalls His deeds, and contrasts Him with idols.

## Psalms 135:1

Hallelujah! 📖

Praise the LORD’s name.

Give praise, servants of the LORD,

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.

## Psalms 135:2

who stand in the LORD’s house, in our God’s house’s courts. 📖

**Help with courts:** Open areas belonging to the place of worship.

## Psalms 135:3

Hallelujah, because the LORD is good. 📖

Sing praises to His name because it is lovely.

## Psalms 135:4

The LORD chose Jacob as His own. 📖

He chose Israel as His treasured possession.

## Psalms 135:5

I know the LORD is great. 📖

Our Lord is above all gods.

## Psalms 135:6

The LORD does everything that pleases Him in heaven and on earth. 📖

He does it in the seas and all their depths.

## Psalms 135:7

He makes clouds rise from the earth’s ends. 📖

He makes lightning with rain and brings wind from His storehouses.

## Psalms 135:8

He struck down Egypt’s firstborn, both people and animals. 📖

## Psalms 135:9

Egypt, He sent signs and wonders among you. 📖

They were against Pharaoh and all his servants.

## Psalms 135:10

He struck down many nations and killed mighty kings: 📖

## Psalms 135:11

Sihon king of the Amorites, Og king of Bashan, and all Canaan’s kings. 📖

## Psalms 135:12

He gave their land as an inheritance to His people Israel. 📖

## Psalms 135:13

LORD, Your name lasts forever. 📖

LORD, Your fame lasts through all generations.

## Psalms 135:14

The LORD will prove His people right. 📖

He will have compassion on His servants.

**Help with compassion:** Deep care for people who suffer.

## Psalms 135:15

The nations’ idols are silver and gold, made by human hands. 📖

**Help with idols:** Images worshiped as gods.

## Psalms 135:16

They have mouths but cannot speak. 📖

They have eyes but cannot see.

## Psalms 135:17

They have ears but cannot hear. 📖

There is no breath in their mouths.

## Psalms 135:18

The people making them become like them. 📖

So does everyone trusting them.

## Psalms 135:19

House of Israel, bless the LORD! 📖

House of Aaron, bless the LORD!

## Psalms 135:20

House of Levi, bless the LORD! 📖

People who fear the LORD, bless Him!

**Help with house:** A family or people group.

Fearing the LORD means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Psalms 135:21

Blessed be the LORD from Zion! 📖

He lives in Jerusalem.

Hallelujah!
