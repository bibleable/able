# Psalms 124 — If the LORD had not been with us

**About this chapter**

David calls Israel to remember the LORD’s rescue.

## Psalms 124:1

A song of ascents. 📖

Of David.

Let Israel now say, “If the LORD had not been on our side,

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 124:2

if the LORD had not been on our side when people attacked us, 📖

## Psalms 124:3

when their anger burned against us, they would have swallowed us alive. 📖

## Psalms 124:4

The floods would have covered us. 📖

The rushing water would have overwhelmed us.

## Psalms 124:5

Raging waters would have swept us away.” 📖

## Psalms 124:6

Blessed be the LORD! 📖

He did not hand us over as prey for their teeth.

**Help with prey:** An animal hunted for food.

The song pictures enemies as animals ready to eat their victims.

## Psalms 124:7

We escaped like a bird from a bird-catcher’s trap. 📖

The net broke, and we escaped.

## Psalms 124:8

Our help is in the LORD’s name. 📖

He made heaven and earth.
