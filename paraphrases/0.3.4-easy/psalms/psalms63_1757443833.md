# Psalms 63 — Longing for God in a dry land

**About this chapter**

David longs for God and remembers His help.

He also describes the fate of people seeking to kill him.

## Psalms 63:1

A psalm of David, when he was in the Wilderness of Judah. 📖

God, You are my God.

I seek You earnestly.

My soul thirsts for You.

My body longs for You in a dry, weary land without water.

## Psalms 63:2

I have seen You in the sanctuary. 📖

I have seen Your power and glory.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 63:3

Your faithful love is better than life. 📖

So my lips will glorify You.

## Psalms 63:4

I will bless You as long as I live. 📖

I will lift my hands in Your name.

## Psalms 63:5

My soul is satisfied as with the richest food. 📖

My mouth will praise You with joyful lips.

## Psalms 63:6

When I remember You on my bed, I think of You through the night’s watches. 📖

**Help with watches:** Periods into which the night was divided.

## Psalms 63:7

You are my help. 📖

I will sing for joy in the shadow of Your wings.

**Help with wings:** A picture of protection like a bird gives its young.

## Psalms 63:8

My soul holds tightly to You. 📖

Your right hand supports me.

## Psalms 63:9

But people seeking to destroy my life will go into the depths of the earth. 📖

## Psalms 63:10

They will fall by the sword. 📖

They will become food for foxes.

## Psalms 63:11

But the king will rejoice in God. 📖

Everyone who swears by Him will rejoice.

Liars’ mouths will be shut.

**Help with swears by Him:** Makes a serious promise calling on God as witness.
