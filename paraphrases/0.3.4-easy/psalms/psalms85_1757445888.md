# Psalms 85 — A prayer for restoration and peace

**About this chapter**

The people remember God’s forgiveness and ask for renewed life.

The song pictures righteousness and peace meeting together.

## Psalms 85:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of the sons of Korah.

LORD, You showed favor to Your land.

You brought Jacob back from captivity.

**Help with Jacob:** A name also used for Israel’s people.

## Psalms 85:2

You forgave Your people’s wrongdoing. 📖

You covered all their sin.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 85:3

You held back all Your fury. 📖

You turned away from Your burning anger.

## Psalms 85:4

God who saves us, restore us. 📖

Put away Your displeasure with us.

## Psalms 85:5

Will You be angry with us forever? 📖

Will You stretch Your anger through all generations?

## Psalms 85:6

Will You not give us life again so Your people may rejoice in You? 📖

## Psalms 85:7

LORD, show us Your faithful love. 📖

Give us Your rescue.

## Psalms 85:8

I will listen to what God the LORD says. 📖

He will surely speak peace to His people and saints.

He will not let them return to foolishness.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 85:9

His rescue is surely near people who fear Him. 📖

Then His glory may live in our land.

**Help with fear Him:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 85:10

Faithful love and faithfulness have joined together. 📖

Righteousness and peace have kissed.

## Psalms 85:11

Faithfulness grows from the earth. 📖

Righteousness looks down from heaven.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

These qualities are pictured as people meeting and acting.

## Psalms 85:12

The LORD will indeed provide good things. 📖

Our land will produce its harvest.

## Psalms 85:13

Righteousness will go before Him. 📖

It will prepare the way for His steps.
