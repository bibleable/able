# Psalms 60 — An appeal after defeat

**About this chapter**

David’s song asks God to restore His people.

It includes God’s words about lands and nations.

## Psalms 60:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “The Lily of the Covenant.” 📖

A Miktam of David, for instruction.

He fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah.

Joab returned and struck down 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

God, You have rejected and broken us.

You have been angry.

Restore us!

**Help with Miktam:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 60:2

You have shaken the land and torn it open. 📖

Heal its cracks, because it is shaking.

## Psalms 60:3

You have made Your people suffer hardship. 📖

We stagger from the wine You made us drink.

**Help with wine:** A picture of suffering that leaves people unsteady.

## Psalms 60:4

You have raised a banner for people who fear You. 📖

They can flee the bow.

Selah.

**Help with banner:** A flag or sign raised for people to see.

Fearing God means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 60:5

Answer and save us with Your right hand. 📖

Let those You love be rescued.

## Psalms 60:6

God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! 📖

I will divide Shechem and share out the Valley of Succoth.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 60:7

Gilead is Mine. 📖

Manasseh is Mine.

Ephraim is My helmet.

Judah is My scepter.

**Help with helmet and scepter:** Pictures of protection and royal authority.

The names refer to lands and tribes.

## Psalms 60:8

Moab is My washbasin. 📖

I throw My sandal on Edom.

I shout in victory over Philistia.”

**Help with basin and sandal:** These images express God’s power over these nations.

## Psalms 60:9

Who will bring me to the fortified city? 📖

Who will lead me to Edom?

**Help with fortified:** Protected by strong walls or defenses.

## Psalms 60:10

God, have You not rejected us? 📖

God, will You no longer march with our armies?

## Psalms 60:11

Help us against the enemy. 📖

Human help is worthless.

## Psalms 60:12

With God we will act bravely. 📖

He will trample our enemies.
