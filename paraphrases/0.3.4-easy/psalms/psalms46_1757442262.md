# Psalms 46 — God is our refuge and strength

**About this chapter**

The song declares confidence in God amid upheaval and war.

God speaks in verse 10.

## Psalms 46:1

For the choir leader. 📖

Of the sons of Korah, according to Alamoth.

A song.

God is our refuge and strength.

He is always present to help in trouble.

**Help with Alamoth:** A musical term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

Refuge means safety and protection.

## Psalms 46:2

So we will not fear even if the earth changes. 📖

We will not fear if mountains fall into the depths of the seas,

## Psalms 46:3

even if the waters roar and foam. 📖

Even if the mountains shake as the waters surge, we will not fear.

Selah.

## Psalms 46:4

A river’s streams bring joy to God’s city. 📖

It is the holy place where the Most High lives.

## Psalms 46:5

God is within the city. 📖

It will not be moved.

God will help it when morning dawns.

## Psalms 46:6

Nations rage, and kingdoms crumble. 📖

The earth melts when He raises His voice.

## Psalms 46:7

Jacob’s God is our fortress.

Selah.
