# Psalms 42 — Longing for the living God

**About this chapter**

The singer longs to be in God’s presence.

He speaks to God and to his own soul.

## Psalms 42:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A Maskil of the sons of Korah.

As a deer pants for streams of water, my soul longs for You, God.

**Help with Maskil:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

The sons of Korah were a group associated with worship songs.

## Psalms 42:2

My soul thirsts for God, the living God. 📖

When will I come and appear in God’s presence?

## Psalms 42:3

My tears have been my food day and night. 📖

People ask me all day, “Where is your God?”

## Psalms 42:4

I remember these things as I pour out my soul. 📖

I walked with the crowd and led the joyful procession to God’s house.

We shouted with joy and praise.

**Help with procession:** A group moving together for a special event.

## Psalms 42:5

My soul, why are you so sad? 📖

Why are you troubled within me?

Hope in God.

I will yet praise Him for the rescue His presence brings.

## Psalms 42:6

My God, my soul loses hope within me. 📖

So I remember You from the land of Jordan and the peaks of Hermon, even Mount Mizar.

## Psalms 42:7

Deep calls to deep in Your waterfalls’ roar. 📖

All Your breaking waves have rolled over me.

**Help with the waters:** The singer pictures overwhelming distress.

## Psalms 42:8

The LORD commands His faithful love by day. 📖

At night His song is with me, a prayer to the God of my life.

## Psalms 42:9

I say to God my Rock, “Why have You forgotten me? 📖

Why must I walk in sorrow because of my enemy’s cruelty?”

**Help with Rock:** A picture of strength and protection.

## Psalms 42:10

My enemies’ mocking is like crushing my bones. 📖

All day they ask me, “Where is your God?”

## Psalms 42:11

My soul, why are you so sad? 📖

Why are you troubled within me?

Hope in God.

I will yet praise Him, my Savior and my God.
