# Psalms 59 — Rescue from those waiting to kill

**About this chapter**

David prays while Saul’s men watch his house to kill him.

He asks for judgment and declares trust in God.

## Psalms 59:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” 📖

A Miktam of David.

Saul sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him.

My God, rescue me from my enemies.

Protect me from those rising against me.

**Help with Miktam:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 59:2

Rescue me from people who do evil. 📖

Save me from men who shed blood.

## Psalms 59:3

See how they wait secretly for me. 📖

LORD, fierce men plan together against me without any offense or sin of mine.

## Psalms 59:4

Through no fault of mine, they hurry to attack me. 📖

Rise to help me and take notice.

## Psalms 59:5

LORD God of Hosts, God of Israel, rouse Yourself to punish all nations. 📖

Show wicked traitors no mercy.

Selah.

**Help with LORD God of Hosts:** God who commands heavenly armies.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 59:6

They return in the evening, snarling like dogs. 📖

They prowl around the city.

## Psalms 59:7

Look at what pours from their mouths. 📖

Their lips speak sharp words: “Who can hear us?”

## Psalms 59:8

But LORD, You laugh at them. 📖

You mock all the nations.

## Psalms 59:9

My Strength, I will watch for You. 📖

God, You are my fortress.

**Help with fortress:** A strong place of protection.

## Psalms 59:10

My God of faithful love will come to meet me. 📖

God will let me stare down my enemies.

## Psalms 59:11

Do not kill them, or my people will forget. 📖

Scatter them by Your power and bring them down.

Lord, You are our shield.

## Psalms 59:12

Let their sinful mouths and lips trap them in their pride. 📖

Let their curses and lies trap them.

## Psalms 59:13

Destroy them in anger! 📖

Destroy them until they are gone.

Let the earth’s ends know that God rules over Jacob.

Selah.

**Help with Jacob:** A name also used for Israel’s people.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 59:14

They return in the evening, snarling like dogs. 📖

They prowl around the city.

## Psalms 59:15

They search for food. 📖

They growl if they are not satisfied.

## Psalms 59:16

But I will sing of Your strength. 📖

In the morning I will announce Your faithful love.

You are my fortress and refuge in trouble.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 59:17

My Strength, I sing praises to You. 📖

God, You are my fortress, my God of faithful love.
