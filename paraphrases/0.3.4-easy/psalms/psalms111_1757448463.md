# Psalms 111 — Remember the LORD’s wonderful works

**About this chapter**

The singer gives thanks and praises the LORD’s works, covenant, and wisdom.

## Psalms 111:1

Hallelujah! 📖

I will thank the LORD with all my heart in the upright people’s council and the gathering.

**Help with Hallelujah and upright:** Praise the LORD, and honest and right.

## Psalms 111:2

The LORD’s works are great. 📖

Everyone who delights in them thinks deeply about them.

## Psalms 111:3

His work is splendid and majestic. 📖

His righteousness lasts forever.

**Help with splendid and majestic:** Showing wonderful royal glory.

Righteousness means what is right and just.

## Psalms 111:4

He makes His wonders remembered. 📖

The LORD is gracious and compassionate.

**Help with gracious and compassionate:** Freely kind, and deeply caring toward people who suffer.

## Psalms 111:5

He gives food to people who fear Him. 📖

He remembers His covenant forever.

**Help with fear Him and covenant:** Respect God and take His authority seriously, and a serious agreement.

## Psalms 111:6

He showed His people His works’ power. 📖

He gave them the nations’ inheritance.

## Psalms 111:7

His hands’ works are truth and justice. 📖

All His instructions are trustworthy.

## Psalms 111:8

They are upheld forever and ever. 📖

They are carried out in truth and uprightness.

## Psalms 111:9

He sent rescue to His people. 📖

He established His covenant forever.

His name is holy and awesome.

**Help with holy and awesome:** Completely pure and set apart, and inspiring deep wonder and fear.

## Psalms 111:10

Fear of the LORD is wisdom’s beginning. 📖

Everyone following His instructions gains rich understanding.

His praise lasts forever!
