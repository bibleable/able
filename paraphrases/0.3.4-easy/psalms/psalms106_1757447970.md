# Psalms 106 — Confession of repeated rebellion

**About this chapter**

The song recounts Israel’s sins, God’s judgments, and His mercy.

It includes child sacrifice and other difficult events without leaving them out.

## Psalms 106:1

Hallelujah! 📖

Thank the LORD, because He is good.

His faithful love lasts forever.

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.

## Psalms 106:2

Who can describe the LORD’s mighty acts? 📖

Who can fully tell His praise?

## Psalms 106:3

Blessed are people who uphold justice. 📖

They do what is right at all times.

## Psalms 106:4

LORD, remember me when You favor Your people. 📖

Come to me with Your rescue,

## Psalms 106:5

so I may see Your chosen people’s success. 📖

Let me rejoice in Your nation’s joy and give glory with Your inheritance.

**Help with inheritance:** The people belonging to God.

## Psalms 106:6

We have sinned like our fathers. 📖

We have done wrong and acted wickedly.

## Psalms 106:7

Our fathers in Egypt did not understand Your wonders or remember Your great kindness. 📖

They rebelled beside the sea, at the Red Sea.

## Psalms 106:8

Yet He saved them for His name’s sake. 📖

He made His power known.

## Psalms 106:9

He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up. 📖

He led them through the depths as through a desert.

**Help with rebuked:** Gave a strong command of correction.

## Psalms 106:10

He saved them from those who hated them. 📖

He rescued them from the enemy’s hand.

## Psalms 106:11

Waters covered their enemies. 📖

Not one remained.

## Psalms 106:12

Then they believed His promises and sang His praise. 📖

## Psalms 106:13

But they quickly forgot His works. 📖

They did not wait for His advice.

## Psalms 106:14

They craved food intensely in the wilderness. 📖

They tested God in the desert.

## Psalms 106:15

He granted their request. 📖

But He sent a disease that wasted their bodies away.

## Psalms 106:16

In the camp they envied Moses. 📖

They also envied Aaron, the LORD’s holy one.

## Psalms 106:17

The earth opened and swallowed Dathan. 📖

It covered Abiram’s gathering.

## Psalms 106:18

Fire blazed through their group. 📖

Flames burned up wicked people.

## Psalms 106:19

At Horeb they made a calf. 📖

They worshiped an image cast from metal.

## Psalms 106:20

They exchanged their Glory for an image of an ox that eats grass. 📖

**Help with their Glory:** A reference to God, whom they exchanged for an idol.

## Psalms 106:21

They forgot God their Savior, who did great things in Egypt, 📖

## Psalms 106:22

wonders in Ham’s land and awesome deeds at the Red Sea. 📖

**Help with Ham’s land:** A poetic name for Egypt here.

Awesome means inspiring deep wonder and fear.

## Psalms 106:23

He said He would destroy them. 📖

But Moses, His chosen one, stood before Him in the breach.

He turned aside God’s wrath so He did not destroy them.

**Help with breach and wrath:** A gap in a defensive wall, and fierce anger.

Moses is pictured standing in the gap to protect the people.

## Psalms 106:24

They despised the pleasant land. 📖

They did not believe His promise.

## Psalms 106:25

They complained in their tents. 📖

They did not listen to the LORD’s voice.

## Psalms 106:26

So He raised His hand and swore to bring them down in the wilderness, 📖

## Psalms 106:27

and scatter their descendants among nations and throughout the lands. 📖

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

## Psalms 106:28

They joined themselves to Baal of Peor. 📖

They ate sacrifices offered to lifeless gods.

**Help with sacrifices:** Offerings given in worship.

## Psalms 106:29

Their deeds made the LORD angry. 📖

A plague broke out among them.

**Help with plague:** A deadly outbreak of disease.

## Psalms 106:30

But Phinehas stood and stepped in. 📖

The plague was stopped.

## Psalms 106:31

This was counted to him as righteousness for endless generations to come. 📖

**Help with righteousness:** Being right before God.

## Psalms 106:32

At Meribah’s waters they angered the LORD. 📖

Trouble came to Moses because of them.

## Psalms 106:33

They rebelled against His Spirit. 📖

Moses spoke without care.

## Psalms 106:34

They did not destroy the peoples as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Psalms 106:35

Instead, they mixed with the nations and took up their customs. 📖

## Psalms 106:36

They worshiped their idols. 📖

The idols became a trap for them.

**Help with idols:** Images worshiped as gods.

## Psalms 106:37

They sacrificed their sons and daughters to demons. 📖

## Psalms 106:38

They shed innocent blood, their sons’ and daughters’ blood. 📖

They sacrificed them to Canaan’s idols.

The land was polluted with blood.

## Psalms 106:39

Their actions made them unholy. 📖

Their deeds were like prostitution.

**Help with prostitution:** Sexual activity for payment.

Here the song pictures unfaithfulness to God through worship of other gods.

## Psalms 106:40

The LORD’s anger burned against His people. 📖

He hated His own inheritance.

## Psalms 106:41

He handed them over to the nations. 📖

People who hated them ruled over them.

## Psalms 106:42

Their enemies treated them cruelly. 📖

They brought them under their power.

## Psalms 106:43

He rescued them many times. 📖

But they were determined to rebel and sank into their sin.

## Psalms 106:44

Yet He heard their cry. 📖

He took notice of their distress.

## Psalms 106:45

He remembered His covenant with them. 📖

In His great faithful love, He relented.

**Help with covenant and relented:** A serious agreement, and drawing back from intended punishment.

## Psalms 106:46

He caused all their captors to show them compassion. 📖

**Help with compassion:** Deep care for people who suffer.

## Psalms 106:47

LORD our God, save us and gather us from the nations. 📖

Then we may thank Your holy name and glory in Your praise.

## Psalms 106:48

Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God, from everlasting to everlasting. 📖

Let all the people say, “Amen!”

Hallelujah!

**Help with everlasting and Amen:** Without beginning or end, and an expression of agreement: let it be so.

Hallelujah means praise the LORD.
