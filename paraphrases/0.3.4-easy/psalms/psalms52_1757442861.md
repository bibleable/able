# Psalms 52 — Trusting God’s love instead of destructive wealth

**About this chapter**

David addresses someone who boasts of evil.

He contrasts destruction with trust in God’s faithful love.

## Psalms 52:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A Maskil of David.

Doeg the Edomite went to Saul and said, “David has gone to Ahimelech’s house.”

This song follows that report.

Mighty man, why do you boast about evil?

God’s faithful love lasts all day long.

**Help with Maskil:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 52:2

You who deceive, your tongue plans destruction like a sharp razor. 📖

## Psalms 52:3

You love evil more than good. 📖

You love lies more than speaking truth.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 52:4

Deceitful tongue, you love every word that devours. 📖

**Help with devours:** A picture of words destroying people.

## Psalms 52:5

God will surely bring you down to ruin forever. 📖

He will snatch you up and tear you from your tent.

He will uproot you from the land of the living.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 52:6

Righteous people will see and fear. 📖

They will mock the evildoer, saying:

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 52:7

“Look at the man who did not make God his refuge! 📖

He trusted in his great wealth.

He made himself strong through destruction.”

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 52:8

But I am like an olive tree growing well in God’s house. 📖

I trust God’s faithful love forever and ever.

## Psalms 52:9

I will praise You forever because You have done it. 📖

I will wait on Your name, because it is good, in the presence of Your saints.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.
