# Psalms 15 — Who may stay with the LORD?

**About this chapter**

David asks who may live in the LORD’s presence.

The song describes honest and just living.

## Psalms 15:1

A psalm of David. 📖

LORD, who may stay in Your tent?

Who may live on Your holy mountain?

## Psalms 15:2

The person who lives with integrity and does what is right. 📖

He speaks truth from his heart.

**Help with integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

## Psalms 15:3

He does not spread lies about people. 📖

He does not harm his neighbor.

He does not speak scornfully about his friend.

## Psalms 15:4

He despises the vile person but honors people who fear the LORD. 📖

He keeps an oath even when it costs him.

**Help with vile:** Deeply evil and shameful.

An oath is a serious promise.

Fearing the LORD means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Psalms 15:5

He lends money without charging interest. 📖

He refuses bribes against innocent people.

Whoever does these things will never be shaken.

**Help with interest:** Extra money charged on a loan.

A bribe is a gift used to make someone act dishonestly.
