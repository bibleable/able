# Psalms 115 — Trust the living God rather than idols

**About this chapter**

The song gives glory to the LORD and contrasts Him with lifeless idols.

## Psalms 115:1

Not to us, LORD, not to us! 📖

Give glory to Your name because of Your faithful love and faithfulness.

## Psalms 115:2

Why should nations ask, “Where is their God?” 📖

## Psalms 115:3

Our God is in heaven. 📖

He does what pleases Him.

## Psalms 115:4

Their idols are silver and gold, made by human hands. 📖

**Help with idols:** Images worshiped as gods.

## Psalms 115:5

They have mouths but cannot speak. 📖

They have eyes but cannot see.

## Psalms 115:6

They have ears but cannot hear. 📖

They have noses but cannot smell.

## Psalms 115:7

They have hands but cannot feel. 📖

They have feet but cannot walk.

They cannot even clear their throats.

## Psalms 115:8

The people making them become like them. 📖

So does everyone trusting them.

## Psalms 115:9

Israel, trust the LORD! 📖

He is their help and shield.

## Psalms 115:10

House of Aaron, trust the LORD! 📖

He is their help and shield.

**Help with house of Aaron:** Aaron’s family and descendants.

## Psalms 115:11

People who fear the LORD, trust the LORD! 📖

He is their help and shield.

**Help with fear the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 115:12

The LORD remembers us and will bless us. 📖

He will bless Israel’s house.

He will bless Aaron’s house.

## Psalms 115:13

He will bless everyone who fears the LORD, both small and great. 📖

## Psalms 115:14

May the LORD increase you, both you and your children. 📖

## Psalms 115:15

May the LORD bless you. 📖

He made heaven and earth.

## Psalms 115:16

The highest heavens belong to the LORD. 📖

But He gave the earth to human beings.

## Psalms 115:17

The dead do not praise the LORD. 📖

Neither does anyone going down into silence.

## Psalms 115:18

But we will bless the LORD now and forever. 📖

Hallelujah!

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.
