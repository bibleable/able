# Psalms 56 — Trust when afraid

**About this chapter**

David prays after being seized in Gath.

He describes his enemies and repeats his trust in God.

## Psalms 56:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “A Dove on Distant Oaks.” 📖

A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath.

God, have mercy on me.

People hound me and press their attack all day.

**Help with Miktam:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 56:2

My enemies pursue me all day. 📖

Many people proudly attack me.

## Psalms 56:3

When I am afraid, I trust You. 📖

## Psalms 56:4

I praise God’s word. 📖

I trust in God and will not be afraid.

What can a human being do to me?

## Psalms 56:5

They twist my words all day. 📖

All their thoughts are about destroying me.

## Psalms 56:6

They plan together, hide, and watch my steps. 📖

They wait to take my life.

## Psalms 56:7

Will they escape despite such sin? 📖

God, bring the nations down in Your anger.

## Psalms 56:8

You have kept account of my wanderings. 📖

Put my tears in Your bottle.

Are they not in Your book?

**Help with bottle and book:** Pictures of God remembering David’s grief.

## Psalms 56:9

Then my enemies will retreat on the day I cry for help. 📖

This will show me that God is on my side.

## Psalms 56:10

I praise God’s word. 📖

I praise the LORD’s word.

## Psalms 56:11

I trust God and will not be afraid. 📖

What can a human being do to me?

## Psalms 56:12

God, I am bound by my vows to You. 📖

I will give You thank offerings.

**Help with vows and thank offerings:** Serious promises, and offerings expressing thanks.

## Psalms 56:13

You have rescued my soul from death and my feet from stumbling. 📖

So I can walk before God in the light of life.
