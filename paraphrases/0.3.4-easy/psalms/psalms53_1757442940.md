# Psalms 53 — God sees people turning away

**About this chapter**

This song describes human wrongdoing and asks for Israel’s rescue.

## Psalms 53:1

For the choir leader, according to Mahalath. 📖

A Maskil of David.

The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.”

They are corrupt, and their ways are vile.

No one does good.

**Help with Mahalath and Maskil:** Song terms whose exact meaning is uncertain.

Corrupt and vile mean twisted by evil and deeply wrong.

## Psalms 53:2

God looks down from heaven at human beings. 📖

He looks for anyone who understands and seeks God.

## Psalms 53:3

All have turned away. 📖

Together they have become corrupt.

No one does good, not even one.

## Psalms 53:4

Will people who do evil never learn? 📖

They eat my people as if eating bread.

They refuse to call on God.

**Help with eating people:** A picture of cruelly using and destroying them.

## Psalms 53:5

There they are, overwhelmed with dread where there was nothing to fear. 📖

God has scattered the bones of those who surrounded you for attack.

You put them to shame because God despised them.

## Psalms 53:6

If only Israel’s rescue would come from Zion! 📖

When God restores His captive people, let Jacob rejoice!

Let Israel be glad!

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem.

Jacob is also used as a name for Israel’s people.
