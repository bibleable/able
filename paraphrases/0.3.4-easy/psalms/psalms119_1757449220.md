# Psalm 119 — Your word is a light for my path

**About this chapter**

The writer prays to God about loving and obeying his teaching.

Law, commands, rules, instructions, and decisions describe different parts of God’s teaching.

When the writer says “you,” he is speaking to God, except when addressing wrongdoers in verse 115.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.
