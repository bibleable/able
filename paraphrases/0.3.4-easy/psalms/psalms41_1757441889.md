# Psalms 41 — A prayer during sickness and betrayal

**About this chapter**

David speaks of care for poor people and describes his own distress.

Verses 5 and 8 include enemies’ cruel words.

## Psalms 41:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

Blessed is the person who cares for poor people.

The LORD will rescue him on the day of trouble.

## Psalms 41:2

The LORD will protect him and keep him alive. 📖

He will bless him in the land.

He will not hand him over to his enemies’ wishes.

## Psalms 41:3

The LORD will support him on his sickbed. 📖

He will restore him from his bed of illness.

## Psalms 41:4

I said, “LORD, be kind to me. 📖

Heal me, because I have sinned against You.”

## Psalms 41:5

My enemies say with harmful intent, “When will he die and be forgotten?” 📖

## Psalms 41:6

My visitor tells lies. 📖

He gathers harmful stories in his heart.

He goes out and spreads them.

## Psalms 41:7

Everyone who hates me whispers against me. 📖

They imagine the worst for me, saying:

## Psalms 41:8

“A vile disease has been poured into him. 📖

He will never get up from where he lies!”

**Help with vile disease:** These are the enemies’ words about his illness.

They are not an editorial judgment about people who are ill.

## Psalms 41:9

Even my trusted close friend, who shared my bread, has lifted his heel against me. 📖

**Help with lifted his heel:** A picture of turning against someone.

## Psalms 41:10

But LORD, be kind to me and raise me up. 📖

Then I can repay them.

## Psalms 41:11

I know You delight in me because my enemy does not win over me. 📖

## Psalms 41:12

You support me in my integrity. 📖

You set me in Your presence forever.

**Help with integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

## Psalms 41:13

Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. 📖

Amen and Amen.

**Help with everlasting:** Without beginning or end.

Amen expresses agreement: let it be so.
