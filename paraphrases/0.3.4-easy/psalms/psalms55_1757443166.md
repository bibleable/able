# Psalms 55 — A prayer after a close friend’s betrayal

**About this chapter**

David describes fear and betrayal.

He asks for severe judgment and speaks of trusting the LORD.

## Psalms 55:1

For the choir leader, with stringed instruments. 📖

A Maskil of David.

God, listen to my prayer.

Do not ignore my appeal.

**Help with Maskil:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 55:2

Pay attention to me and answer me. 📖

I am restless in my complaint and deeply upset,

## Psalms 55:3

because of the enemy’s voice and wicked people’s pressure. 📖

They bring disaster on me.

In their anger, they resent me.

## Psalms 55:4

My heart pounds within me. 📖

The terrors of death attack me.

## Psalms 55:5

Fear and trembling grip me. 📖

Horror overwhelms me.

## Psalms 55:6

I said, “If only I had wings like a dove! 📖

I would fly away and rest.

## Psalms 55:7

I would flee far away! 📖

I would stay in the wilderness.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 55:8

I would hurry to my shelter, far from this raging storm.” 📖

## Psalms 55:9

Lord, confuse and mix up their speech. 📖

I see violence and conflict in the city.

## Psalms 55:10

Day and night they go around its walls. 📖

Harmful intent and trouble are inside it.

## Psalms 55:11

Destruction is inside. 📖

Cruelty and deceit never leave its streets.

## Psalms 55:12

It is not an enemy insulting me. 📖

I could bear that.

It is not a foe rising against me.

I could hide from him.

## Psalms 55:13

But it is you, a man like me. 📖

You are my companion and close friend.

## Psalms 55:14

We shared sweet friendship. 📖

We walked with the crowd into God’s house.

## Psalms 55:15

Let death take them by surprise. 📖

Let them go down to Sheol alive.

Evil is with them in their homes.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Psalms 55:16

But I call to God. 📖

The LORD saves me.

## Psalms 55:17

Morning, noon, and night, I cry in distress. 📖

He hears my voice.

## Psalms 55:18

He rescues my soul in peace from the battle against me. 📖

Many people oppose me.

## Psalms 55:19

God will hear and humble them. 📖

He is the One enthroned through the ages.

Selah.

They do not change, and they have no fear of God.

**Help with Selah:** A song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

Fearing God means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Psalms 55:20

My companion attacks his friends. 📖

He breaks his covenant.

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Psalms 55:21

His speech is smooth as butter, but war is in his heart. 📖

His words are softer than oil, yet they are drawn swords.

## Psalms 55:22

Put your burden on the LORD, and He will support you. 📖

He will never let righteous people be shaken.

**Help with burden:** A picture of the troubles someone carries.

Righteous means right before God.

## Psalms 55:23

But God, You will bring them down to the Pit of destruction. 📖

Men who shed blood and deceive will not live half their days.

But I will trust You.
