# Psalms 3 — David asks the LORD to save him

**About this chapter**

David prays while fleeing from his son Absalom.

The prayer includes a request for judgment against his enemies.

## Psalms 3:1

A psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom. 📖

LORD, my enemies have become so many!

So many people rise against me!

**Help with psalm:** A song or prayer.

## Psalms 3:2

Many say about me, “God will not rescue him.” 📖

Selah.

## Psalms 3:3

But You, LORD, are a shield around me. 📖

You are my glory.

You lift my head.

**Help with shield and lifted head:** David pictures protection and restored honor.

## Psalms 3:4

I cry aloud to the LORD. 📖

He answers me from His holy mountain.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

## Psalms 3:5

I lie down and sleep. 📖

I wake again because the LORD keeps me alive.

## Psalms 3:6

I will not fear the many thousands set against me on every side. 📖

## Psalms 3:7

Rise up, LORD! 📖

Save me, my God!

Strike all my enemies on the jaw.

Break wicked people’s teeth.

## Psalms 3:8

Rescue belongs to the LORD. 📖

May Your blessing rest on Your people.

Selah.
