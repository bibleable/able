# Psalms 36 — Human evil and God’s faithful love

**About this chapter**

David contrasts wicked behavior with the LORD’s faithful love.

He prays for continuing care and protection.

## Psalms 36:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David, the LORD’s servant.

A message is in my heart about the wicked man’s sin.

There is no fear of God before his eyes.

**Help with fear of God:** Respect for God and serious attention to His authority.

## Psalms 36:2

His eyes are too full of pride to recognize or hate his own sin. 📖

## Psalms 36:3

His words are wicked and dishonest. 📖

He has stopped being wise and doing good.

## Psalms 36:4

Even on his bed he plans evil. 📖

He sets himself on a path that is not good.

He fails to reject evil.

## Psalms 36:5

LORD, Your faithful love reaches the heavens. 📖

Your faithfulness reaches the clouds.

## Psalms 36:6

Your righteousness is like the highest mountains. 📖

Your judgments are like the deepest sea.

LORD, You keep people and animals alive.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 36:7

God, how precious is Your faithful love! 📖

Human beings take refuge in the shadow of Your wings.

**Help with refuge under wings:** A picture of shelter like a bird gives its young.

## Psalms 36:8

They feast on Your house’s plenty. 📖

You give them drink from Your river of delights.

## Psalms 36:9

The fountain of life is with You. 📖

In Your light, we see light.

**Help with fountain and light:** Pictures of God as the source of life and of seeing clearly.

## Psalms 36:10

Continue Your faithful love toward people who know You. 📖

Continue Your righteousness toward people whose hearts are upright.

**Help with upright:** Honest and right.

## Psalms 36:11

Do not let proud people’s feet come against me. 📖

Do not let wicked people’s hands drive me away.

## Psalms 36:12

There lie the people who do evil. 📖

They have fallen, thrown down and unable to rise.
