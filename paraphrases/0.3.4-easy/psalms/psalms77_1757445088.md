# Psalms 77 — Remembering God’s wonders in distress

**About this chapter**

Asaph describes a night of distress and questions.

He then remembers God’s past rescue.

## Psalms 77:1

For the choir leader, according to Jeduthun. 📖

A psalm of Asaph.

I cried out to God.

I cried aloud for God to hear me.

## Psalms 77:2

On the day of trouble, I sought the Lord. 📖

All night my outstretched hands did not grow tired.

My soul refused comfort.

## Psalms 77:3

I remembered You, God, and groaned. 📖

I thought deeply, and my spirit grew weak.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 77:4

You kept my eyes from closing. 📖

I am too troubled to speak.

## Psalms 77:5

I thought about old days and years long past. 📖

## Psalms 77:6

At night I remembered my song. 📖

I thought deeply in my heart.

My spirit asked:

## Psalms 77:7

“Will the Lord reject us forever? 📖

Will He never show favor again?

## Psalms 77:8

Has His faithful love gone forever? 📖

Has His promise failed for all time?

## Psalms 77:9

Has God forgotten to be kind? 📖

Has His anger shut off His compassion?”

Selah.

**Help with compassion:** Deep care for someone who suffers.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 77:10

So I said, “I grieve because the Most High’s right hand has changed.” 📖

## Psalms 77:11

I will remember the LORD’s works. 📖

Yes, I will remember Your ancient wonders.

## Psalms 77:12

I will think about everything You have done. 📖

I will consider Your mighty deeds.

## Psalms 77:13

God, Your way is holy. 📖

What god is as great as our God?

## Psalms 77:14

You are the God who works wonders. 📖

You show Your strength among the peoples.

## Psalms 77:15

With power You rescued Your people, Jacob’s and Joseph’s sons. 📖

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 77:16

God, the waters saw You. 📖

They saw You and swirled.

Even the depths shook.

## Psalms 77:17

The clouds poured down water. 📖

Thunder sounded in the skies.

Your arrows flashed back and forth.

## Psalms 77:18

Your thunder sounded in the whirlwind. 📖

Lightning lit the world.

The earth shook and trembled.

**Help with whirlwind:** A powerful, swirling wind.

## Psalms 77:19

Your path went through the sea. 📖

Your way went through mighty waters.

But Your footprints could not be found.

## Psalms 77:20

You led Your people like a flock through Moses and Aaron. 📖
