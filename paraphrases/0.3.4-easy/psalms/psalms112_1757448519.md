# Psalms 112 — The life of a person who fears the LORD

**About this chapter**

The song describes a generous, just person who trusts the LORD.

## Psalms 112:1

Hallelujah! 📖

Blessed is the person who fears the LORD and greatly delights in His commands.

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.

Fearing the LORD means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Psalms 112:2

His descendants will be mighty in the land. 📖

The upright generation will be blessed.

**Help with descendants and upright:** Children and later generations, and honest and right.

## Psalms 112:3

Wealth and riches are in his house. 📖

His righteousness lasts forever.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 112:4

Light dawns in darkness for upright people. 📖

They are gracious, compassionate, and righteous.

**Help with gracious and compassionate:** Freely kind, and deeply caring toward people who suffer.

## Psalms 112:5

Things go well for a generous person who lends freely. 📖

He manages his affairs with justice.

## Psalms 112:6

He will surely never be shaken. 📖

A righteous person will be remembered forever.

## Psalms 112:7

He does not fear bad news. 📖

His heart is steady, trusting the LORD.

## Psalms 112:8

His heart is confident. 📖

He does not fear until he sees victory over his enemies.

## Psalms 112:9

He gives his gifts widely to poor people. 📖

His righteousness lasts forever.

His horn will be lifted high in honor.

**Help with horn:** A picture of strength and honor.

## Psalms 112:10

A wicked man sees and is upset. 📖

He grinds his teeth and wastes away.

Wicked people’s desires will perish.
