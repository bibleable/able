# Psalms 105 — Remember God’s covenant and rescue

**About this chapter**

The song recalls God’s promise to Abraham and His care for Israel.

It recounts Joseph’s suffering, Egypt’s judgments, and the journey to the promised land.

## Psalms 105:1

Thank the LORD and call His name. 📖

Tell the nations what He has done.

## Psalms 105:2

Sing to Him and sing His praises. 📖

Tell all His wonders.

## Psalms 105:3

Glory in His holy name. 📖

Let the hearts of people seeking the LORD rejoice.

## Psalms 105:4

Seek the LORD and His strength. 📖

Always seek His face.

**Help with seek His face:** Turn to God and seek His presence.

## Psalms 105:5

Remember His wonders, marvelous deeds, and spoken judgments, 📖

## Psalms 105:6

you descendants of His servant Abraham. 📖

You are Jacob’s sons, His chosen ones.

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

## Psalms 105:7

He is the LORD our God. 📖

His judgments reach throughout the earth.

## Psalms 105:8

He remembers His covenant forever. 📖

He commanded His word for a thousand generations.

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Psalms 105:9

He made this covenant with Abraham. 📖

He swore this oath to Isaac.

## Psalms 105:10

He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree. 📖

He confirmed it to Israel as an everlasting covenant.

**Help with decree and everlasting:** An authoritative decision, and lasting forever.

## Psalms 105:11

He said, “I will give you Canaan’s land as your share of the inheritance.” 📖

## Psalms 105:12

They were very few in number. 📖

They were strangers in the land.

## Psalms 105:13

They wandered from nation to nation. 📖

They went from one kingdom to another.

## Psalms 105:14

He let no one treat them cruelly. 📖

He rebuked kings for their sake, saying:

**Help with rebuked:** Spoke strongly against wrongdoing.

## Psalms 105:15

“Do not touch My anointed ones! 📖

Do not harm My prophets!”

**Help with anointed and prophets:** People appointed by God, and people who speak His message.

## Psalms 105:16

He called famine onto the land. 📖

He cut off all their food supplies.

**Help with famine:** A time when there is very little food.

## Psalms 105:17

He sent a man ahead of them. 📖

Joseph was sold as a slave.

## Psalms 105:18

Chains bruised Joseph’s feet. 📖

Iron bands were put around his neck,

## Psalms 105:19

until his prediction came true. 📖

The LORD’s word proved him right.

## Psalms 105:20

The king sent for him and released him. 📖

The ruler of peoples set him free.

## Psalms 105:21

He made Joseph master of his household. 📖

He put him in charge of all his possessions,

## Psalms 105:22

so Joseph could instruct his princes as he pleased. 📖

He could teach the king’s elders wisdom.

## Psalms 105:23

Then Israel entered Egypt. 📖

Jacob lived in Ham’s land.

**Help with Ham’s land:** A poetic name for Egypt here.

## Psalms 105:24

The LORD made His people very fruitful. 📖

They became more numerous than their enemies.

## Psalms 105:25

He turned their enemies’ hearts to hate His people. 📖

They plotted against His servants.

## Psalms 105:26

He sent His servant Moses and Aaron, whom He had chosen. 📖

## Psalms 105:27

They performed His miraculous signs among them. 📖

They did wonders in Ham’s land.

## Psalms 105:28

He sent darkness, and it became dark. 📖

Yet they defied His words.

## Psalms 105:29

He turned their waters into blood. 📖

He made their fish die.

## Psalms 105:30

Their land was full of frogs, even in royal rooms. 📖

## Psalms 105:31

He spoke, and insects swarmed. 📖

Gnats filled their country.

## Psalms 105:32

He gave them hail instead of rain. 📖

Lightning flashed throughout their land.

## Psalms 105:33

He struck their vines and fig trees. 📖

He shattered their country’s trees.

## Psalms 105:34

He spoke, and locusts came. 📖

Young locusts came in numbers beyond counting.

## Psalms 105:35

They ate every plant in the land. 📖

They ate everything its soil produced.

## Psalms 105:36

Then He struck all their firstborn, the firstfruits of all their strength. 📖

**Help with firstborn and firstfruits:** The oldest children, pictured as the first yield of their parents’ strength.

## Psalms 105:37

He brought Israel out with silver and gold. 📖

No one among His tribes stumbled.

## Psalms 105:38

Egypt was glad when they left. 📖

Fear of Israel had fallen on the Egyptians.

## Psalms 105:39

He spread a cloud to cover them. 📖

He spread fire to light the night.

## Psalms 105:40

They asked, and He brought quail. 📖

He satisfied them with heaven’s bread.

**Help with quail:** Birds provided as food.

## Psalms 105:41

He opened a rock, and water rushed out. 📖

It flowed like a river in the desert.

## Psalms 105:42

He remembered His holy promise to His servant Abraham. 📖

## Psalms 105:43

He brought His people out with rejoicing. 📖

His chosen people shouted for joy.

## Psalms 105:44

He gave them nations’ lands. 📖

They inherited the fruit of other people’s work,

## Psalms 105:45

so they might keep His commands and obey His laws. 📖

Hallelujah!

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.
