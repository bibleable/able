# Psalms 58 — A prayer for judgment against unjust rulers

**About this chapter**

David speaks against injustice and asks for judgment.

The prayer includes disturbing images of death and blood.

## Psalms 58:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” 📖

A Miktam of David.

Rulers, do you really speak justly?

Human beings, do you judge rightly?

**Help with Miktam:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 58:2

No, you plan injustice in your hearts. 📖

Your hands hand out violence on the earth.

## Psalms 58:3

Wicked people are estranged from the womb. 📖

Liars go wrong from birth.

**Help with estranged:** Separated or turned away.

## Psalms 58:4

Their poison is like a snake’s poison. 📖

They are like a cobra that shuts its ears,

## Psalms 58:5

refusing to hear the charmer’s tune, though he skillfully works his spell. 📖

**Help with cobra and charmer:** A poisonous snake and someone trying to control it.

The image describes refusal to listen.

## Psalms 58:6

God, smash the teeth in their mouths. 📖

LORD, tear out the lions’ sharp teeth.

## Psalms 58:7

May they disappear like water flowing away. 📖

When they draw their bows, may their arrows have no sharp points.

## Psalms 58:8

May they be like a slug dissolving in its slime. 📖

Like a woman’s stillborn child, may they never see the sun.

**Help with stillborn:** Born dead.

This is a severe prayer for judgment, not a statement of a child’s guilt.

## Psalms 58:9

Before your pots feel burning thorns, green or dry, He will sweep them away. 📖

**Help with burning thorns:** Twigs burned to heat cooking pots.

The image describes sudden judgment.

## Psalms 58:10

Righteous people will rejoice when they see revenge taken for them. 📖

They will wash their feet in wicked people’s blood.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 58:11

Then people will say, “Righteous people surely have a reward! 📖

There is surely a God who judges the earth!”
