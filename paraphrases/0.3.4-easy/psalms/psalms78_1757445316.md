# Psalms 78 — Remember God’s works and teach the next generation

**About this chapter**

Asaph recounts Israel’s history of rescue, rebellion, judgment, and mercy.

The song includes severe judgments and ends with David’s leadership.

## Psalms 78:1

A Maskil of Asaph. 📖

My people, listen to my instruction.

Hear the words of my mouth.

**Help with Maskil:** A song term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 78:2

I will speak in parables. 📖

I will tell things hidden from the beginning,

**Help with parables:** Teachings using stories or comparisons.

## Psalms 78:3

which we have heard and known. 📖

Our fathers passed them on to us.

## Psalms 78:4

We will not hide them from their children. 📖

We will tell the next generation the LORD’s praises, might, and wonderful works.

## Psalms 78:5

He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed a law in Israel. 📖

He commanded our fathers to teach these to their children,

**Help with testimony and law:** What God declares and His teaching about how to live.

## Psalms 78:6

so the next generation would know them, even children not yet born. 📖

They would grow up and tell their own children,

## Psalms 78:7

so they would trust God. 📖

They would not forget His works but would keep His commands.

## Psalms 78:8

Then they would not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation. 📖

Their hearts were not loyal.

Their spirits were not faithful to God.

## Psalms 78:9

Ephraim’s archers turned back on the day of battle. 📖

**Help with archers:** People who shoot arrows.

## Psalms 78:10

They did not keep God’s covenant. 📖

They refused to live by His law.

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Psalms 78:11

They forgot His deeds and the wonders He showed them. 📖

## Psalms 78:12

He worked wonders before their fathers in Egypt, in the region of Zoan. 📖

## Psalms 78:13

He split the sea and brought them through. 📖

He made the waters stand like a wall.

## Psalms 78:14

He led them with a cloud by day. 📖

He led them with fire’s light all night.

## Psalms 78:15

He split rocks in the wilderness. 📖

He gave them as much water to drink as the seas.

## Psalms 78:16

He brought streams from stone. 📖

He made water flow down like rivers.

## Psalms 78:17

But they kept sinning against Him. 📖

They rebelled against the Most High in the desert.

## Psalms 78:18

They deliberately tested God by demanding the food they craved. 📖

## Psalms 78:19

They spoke against God, saying, “Can God really prepare a table in the wilderness? 📖

## Psalms 78:20

He struck the rock, and water rushed out in floods. 📖

But can He also give bread or provide meat for His people?”

## Psalms 78:21

The LORD heard and became furious. 📖

Fire was lit against Jacob.

His anger burned against Israel,

## Psalms 78:22

because they did not believe God or depend on His rescue. 📖

## Psalms 78:23

Yet He commanded the clouds above. 📖

He opened heaven’s doors.

## Psalms 78:24

He rained manna for them to eat. 📖

He gave them grain from heaven.

**Help with manna:** The food God provided during the wilderness journey.

## Psalms 78:25

People ate angels’ bread. 📖

He sent them plentiful food.

## Psalms 78:26

He stirred the east wind from heaven. 📖

By His power He drove the south wind.

## Psalms 78:27

He rained meat on them like dust. 📖

Winged birds were as many as grains of sand beside the sea.

## Psalms 78:28

He made the birds fall inside their camp, all around their homes. 📖

## Psalms 78:29

They ate and were well filled. 📖

He gave them what they craved.

## Psalms 78:30

But before their desire was filled, while food was still in their mouths, 📖

## Psalms 78:31

God’s anger burned against them. 📖

He killed their strongest and brought down Israel’s young men.

## Psalms 78:32

Despite all this, they kept sinning. 📖

Despite His wonderful works, they did not believe.

## Psalms 78:33

So He ended their days in futility. 📖

He ended their years in sudden terror.

**Help with futility:** Effort or life that comes to nothing.

## Psalms 78:34

When He killed them, they would seek Him. 📖

They repented and searched for God.

**Help with repented:** Turned away from wrongdoing.

## Psalms 78:35

They remembered that God was their Rock. 📖

God Most High was their Redeemer.

**Help with Rock and Redeemer:** Pictures of strength, protection, and rescue.

## Psalms 78:36

But their mouths deceived Him. 📖

Their tongues lied to Him.

## Psalms 78:37

Their hearts were disloyal. 📖

They were unfaithful to His covenant.

## Psalms 78:38

Yet He had compassion. 📖

He forgave their sin and did not destroy them.

He often held back His anger.

He did not release all His fury.

**Help with compassion:** Deep care for someone who suffers.

## Psalms 78:39

He remembered they were only flesh. 📖

They were a passing breeze that does not return.

## Psalms 78:40

How often they disobeyed Him in the wilderness! 📖

How often they grieved Him in the desert!

## Psalms 78:41

Again and again they tested God. 📖

They provoked the Holy One of Israel.

## Psalms 78:42

They did not remember His power or the day He rescued them from the enemy, 📖

## Psalms 78:43

when He performed signs in Egypt and wonders in Zoan’s fields. 📖

## Psalms 78:44

He turned their rivers into blood. 📖

They could not drink from their streams.

## Psalms 78:45

He sent swarms of flies that devoured them. 📖

He sent frogs that devastated them.

## Psalms 78:46

He gave their crops to grasshoppers. 📖

He gave the fruit of their work to locusts.

## Psalms 78:47

He killed their vines with hailstones. 📖

He killed their sycamore-fig trees with sleet.

**Help with hail and sleet:** Ice falling from clouds, and icy rain.

## Psalms 78:48

He handed their cattle over to hail. 📖

He handed their livestock over to lightning bolts.

## Psalms 78:49

He let loose His fury, anger, outrage, and disaster against them. 📖

A band of destroying angels came.

## Psalms 78:50

He cleared a path for His anger. 📖

He did not spare them from death.

He handed their lives over to plague.

**Help with plague:** A deadly outbreak of disease.

## Psalms 78:51

He struck all Egypt’s firstborn, the strength of manhood in Ham’s tents. 📖

**Help with firstborn and Ham’s tents:** The oldest children.

Ham’s tents is a poetic way of referring here to Egypt.

The strength of manhood refers to the firstborn.

## Psalms 78:52

He led His people out like sheep. 📖

He guided them like a flock in the wilderness.

## Psalms 78:53

He led them safely so they did not fear. 📖

But the sea swallowed their enemies.

## Psalms 78:54

He brought them to His holy land. 📖

He brought them to the mountain His right hand had acquired.

## Psalms 78:55

He drove nations out before them and divided their inheritance. 📖

He settled Israel’s tribes in their tents.

## Psalms 78:56

But they tested and disobeyed God Most High. 📖

They did not keep His decrees.

**Help with decrees:** Authoritative commands.

## Psalms 78:57

They turned back and were faithless like their fathers. 📖

They were twisted like a faulty bow.

## Psalms 78:58

Their high places made Him angry. 📖

Their idols provoked His jealousy.

**Help with high places and idols:** Worship sites on raised ground, and images worshiped as gods.

## Psalms 78:59

God heard and became furious. 📖

He rejected Israel completely.

## Psalms 78:60

He abandoned Shiloh’s tabernacle, the tent He had set up among people. 📖

**Help with tabernacle:** A tent used for worship.

## Psalms 78:61

He handed His strength over to captivity. 📖

He handed His splendor over to the enemy.

**Help with strength and splendor:** Poetic descriptions associated with God’s presence among His people.

Their precise reference needs further editorial review.

## Psalms 78:62

He handed His people over to the sword. 📖

He was furious with His inheritance.

## Psalms 78:63

Fire burned up His young men. 📖

His young women had no wedding songs.

## Psalms 78:64

His priests died by the sword. 📖

Their widows could not mourn.

**Help with widows:** Women whose husbands have died.

## Psalms 78:65

Then the Lord awoke as if from sleep. 📖

He was like a mighty warrior overcome by wine.

**Help with this comparison:** The song uses a vivid warrior image; it does not state that God literally drank wine.

## Psalms 78:66

He drove His enemies back. 📖

He put them to shame forever.

## Psalms 78:67

He rejected Joseph’s tent. 📖

He refused the tribe of Ephraim.

## Psalms 78:68

But He chose Judah’s tribe and Mount Zion, which He loved. 📖

## Psalms 78:69

He built His sanctuary like the heights. 📖

He built it like the earth He established forever.

## Psalms 78:70

He chose David His servant. 📖

He took him from the sheepfolds,

**Help with sheepfolds:** Enclosed places for sheep.

## Psalms 78:71

from caring for female sheep. 📖

He brought David to shepherd His people Jacob, Israel His inheritance.

## Psalms 78:72

So David shepherded them with an honest heart. 📖

He guided them with skillful hands.
