# Psalms 19 — God’s glory and His teaching

**About this chapter**

David praises what creation shows about God and what the LORD’s teaching does.

He ends with a prayer about his own faults and words.

## Psalms 19:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

The heavens declare God’s glory.

The skies announce the work of His hands.

## Psalms 19:2

Day after day they pour out speech. 📖

Night after night they reveal knowledge.

## Psalms 19:3

There is no speech or language. 📖

There is no sound to hear,

## Psalms 19:4

yet their voice has gone throughout the earth. 📖

Their words reach the world’s ends.

In the heavens God has put up a tent for the sun.

## Psalms 19:5

The sun is like a bridegroom coming out of his room. 📖

It is like a champion rejoicing to run his race.

**Help with bridegroom:** A man at his wedding.

## Psalms 19:6

It rises at one end of the heavens and travels to the other. 📖

Nothing is kept from its warmth.

## Psalms 19:7

The LORD’s Law is perfect and restores the soul. 📖

The LORD’s testimony is trustworthy and makes people with little understanding wise.

**Help with Law and testimony:** God’s teaching and what He declares to be true.

## Psalms 19:8

The LORD’s instructions are right and bring joy to the heart. 📖

The LORD’s commands shine brightly and give light to the eyes.

**Help with light to the eyes:** A picture of giving understanding.

## Psalms 19:9

Fear of the LORD is pure and lasts forever. 📖

The LORD’s judgments are true and completely righteous.

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect for God and serious attention to His authority.

Righteous means right and just.

## Psalms 19:10

They are more precious than gold, even much pure gold. 📖

They are sweeter than honey from the honeycomb.

## Psalms 19:11

They warn Your servant. 📖

There is great reward in keeping them.

## Psalms 19:12

Who can recognize his own errors? 📖

Cleanse me from my hidden faults.

## Psalms 19:13

Also keep Your servant from deliberate sins. 📖

Do not let them rule me.

Then I will be blameless and cleansed of great wrongdoing.

## Psalms 19:14

LORD, my Rock and Redeemer, may my words please You. 📖

May the thoughts deep in my heart please You too.

**Help with Rock and Redeemer:** Pictures of strength, protection, and rescue.
