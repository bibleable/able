# Psalms 140 — A prayer against violent people

**About this chapter**

David asks for protection, judgment on violent enemies, and justice for needy people.

## Psalms 140:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

LORD, rescue me from evil men.

Protect me from violent men,

## Psalms 140:2

who plan evil in their hearts and stir up war all day. 📖

## Psalms 140:3

They sharpen their tongues like snakes. 📖

Vipers’ poison is on their lips.

Selah.

**Help with vipers:** Poisonous snakes.

The picture describes harmful speech.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 140:4

LORD, guard me from wicked people’s hands. 📖

Keep me safe from violent men who plan to make me stumble.

## Psalms 140:5

Proud people hide a trap for me. 📖

Their net’s ropes spread along my path.

They set out lures for me.

Selah.

**Help with lures:** Things used to draw someone into a trap.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 140:6

I say to the LORD, “You are my God.” 📖

LORD, hear my cry for help.

## Psalms 140:7

GOD the Lord, You are the strength of my rescue. 📖

You shield my head on the day of battle.

## Psalms 140:8

LORD, do not grant wicked people’s desires. 📖

Do not help their evil plans succeed, or they may be raised high.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 140:9

May the trouble their lips caused cover the heads of those surrounding me. 📖

## Psalms 140:10

May burning coals fall on them. 📖

May they be thrown into fire and muddy pits, never to rise again.

## Psalms 140:11

May no one spreading lies be established in the land. 📖

May disaster hunt down violent men.

## Psalms 140:12

I know the LORD upholds justice for poor people. 📖

He defends needy people’s cause.

## Psalms 140:13

Righteous people will surely praise Your name. 📖

Upright people will live in Your presence.

**Help with righteous and upright:** Right before God and living honestly.
