# Psalms 9 — Thanks to the LORD who judges fairly

**About this chapter**

David gives thanks and asks the LORD to help people who suffer.

## Psalms 9:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “The Death of the Son.” 📖

A psalm of David.

I will thank the LORD with all my heart.

I will tell about all Your wonders.

## Psalms 9:2

I will be glad and rejoice in You. 📖

Most High, I will sing praise to Your name.

## Psalms 9:3

When my enemies retreat, they stumble and perish before You. 📖

## Psalms 9:4

You have upheld my just cause. 📖

You sit on Your throne and judge rightly.

## Psalms 9:5

You have rebuked the nations. 📖

You have destroyed wicked people.

You have erased their name forever and ever.

**Help with rebuked:** Spoken strongly against their wrongdoing.

## Psalms 9:6

The enemy has come to ruin forever. 📖

You have uprooted their cities.

Even their memory has disappeared.

## Psalms 9:7

But the LORD remains forever. 📖

He has established His throne for judgment.

## Psalms 9:8

He judges the world with justice. 📖

He governs the peoples fairly.

## Psalms 9:9

The LORD is a refuge for people treated cruelly. 📖

He is a strong place of safety in trouble.

**Help with refuge:** A place to go for protection.

## Psalms 9:10

People who know Your name trust You. 📖

LORD, You have not abandoned those who seek You.

## Psalms 9:11

Sing praises to the LORD, who lives in Zion. 📖

Tell the nations about His deeds.

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem and the LORD’s holy mountain.

## Psalms 9:12

He remembers bloodshed and acts to punish it. 📖

He does not ignore suffering people’s cries.

## Psalms 9:13

LORD, have mercy on me. 📖

See how my enemies make me suffer!

Lift me from death’s gates,

## Psalms 9:14

so I can declare all Your praises. 📖

Within Daughter Zion’s gates, let me rejoice in Your rescue.

**Help with Daughter Zion:** A poetic name for Jerusalem and its people.

## Psalms 9:15

The nations have fallen into a pit they made. 📖

Their feet are caught in a net they hid.

## Psalms 9:16

The LORD is known by the justice He brings. 📖

Wicked people are trapped by their own work.

Higgaion.

Selah.

**Help with Higgaion and Selah:** Terms used in this song.

Their exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 9:17

Wicked people will return to Sheol. 📖

So will all nations that forget God.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Psalms 9:18

Needy people will not always be forgotten. 📖

The hope of people treated cruelly will not be destroyed forever.

## Psalms 9:19

Rise up, LORD! 📖

Do not let human beings win.

Let the nations be judged in Your presence.

## Psalms 9:20

LORD, fill them with terror. 📖

Let the nations know they are only human.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.
