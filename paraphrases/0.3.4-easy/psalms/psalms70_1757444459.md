# Psalms 70 — An urgent call for help

**About this chapter**

David asks God to hurry to help him and to bring shame on his enemies.

## Psalms 70:1

For the choir leader. 📖

Of David, to bring remembrance.

God, hurry to rescue me!

LORD, hurry to help me!

## Psalms 70:2

May those seeking my life be ashamed and confused. 📖

May those wishing me harm be driven back and humiliated.

## Psalms 70:3

May people saying, “Aha, aha!” retreat because of their shame. 📖

## Psalms 70:4

May everyone seeking You rejoice and be glad in You. 📖

May those who love Your rescue always say, “Let God be praised as great!”

## Psalms 70:5

But I am poor and needy. 📖

God, hurry to me.

You are my help and rescuer.

LORD, do not delay.
