# Psalms 48 — God’s city and His lasting care

**About this chapter**

The song praises God in His city and remembers His protection.

## Psalms 48:1

A song. 📖

A psalm of the sons of Korah.

Great is the LORD!

He greatly deserves praise in our God’s city, His holy mountain.

## Psalms 48:2

Mount Zion is beautiful in its height. 📖

Like Zaphon’s peaks, it is the joy of all the earth.

It is the great King’s city.

**Help with Zion and Zaphon:** Mountain names used in the song.

Zion is associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 48:3

God is in the city’s citadels. 📖

He has shown Himself to be a fortress.

**Help with citadels and fortress:** Strong places built for protection.

## Psalms 48:4

Look, kings gathered together. 📖

They all advanced together.

## Psalms 48:5

They saw and were amazed. 📖

They fled in terror.

## Psalms 48:6

Trembling seized them there. 📖

They suffered anguish like a woman giving birth.

## Psalms 48:7

With an east wind, You wrecked the ships of Tarshish. 📖

## Psalms 48:8

We have seen what we heard about in the LORD of Hosts’ city. 📖

It is our God’s city.

God will establish it forever.

Selah.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** The LORD who commands heavenly armies.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 48:9

God, in Your temple we think deeply about Your faithful love. 📖

## Psalms 48:10

God, Your name reaches the earth’s ends, as Your praise does. 📖

Your right hand is full of righteousness.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 48:11

Mount Zion is glad. 📖

Judah’s daughters rejoice because of Your judgments.

**Help with Judah’s daughters:** A poetic name for Judah’s towns and their people.

## Psalms 48:12

Walk around Zion. 📖

Go all around it and count its towers.

## Psalms 48:13

Consider its walls and tour its citadels. 📖

Then you can tell the next generation.

## Psalms 48:14

This God is our God forever and ever. 📖

He will guide us even until death.
