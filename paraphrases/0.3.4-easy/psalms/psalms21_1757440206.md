# Psalms 21 — Thanks for the king’s blessings

**About this chapter**

The song thanks the LORD for helping the king.

It also describes severe judgment against enemies.

## Psalms 21:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

LORD, the king rejoices in Your strength.

He greatly rejoices in Your rescue!

## Psalms 21:2

You granted his heart’s desire. 📖

You did not refuse the request from his lips.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 21:3

You welcomed him with rich blessings. 📖

You put a crown of pure gold on his head.

## Psalms 21:4

He asked You for life. 📖

You gave it to him: long days, forever and ever.

## Psalms 21:5

His glory is great through Your rescue. 📖

You give him splendor and majesty.

**Help with splendor and majesty:** Great glory and royal honor.

## Psalms 21:6

You give him blessings forever. 📖

You cheer him with joy in Your presence.

## Psalms 21:7

The king trusts in the LORD. 📖

Through the Most High’s faithful love, he will not be shaken.

## Psalms 21:8

Your hand will catch all Your enemies. 📖

Your right hand will seize everyone who hates You.

## Psalms 21:9

You will put them in a burning furnace when You appear. 📖

In His anger the LORD will swallow them up.

Fire will burn them up.

## Psalms 21:10

You will wipe their descendants from the earth. 📖

Their children will disappear from among human beings.

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

## Psalms 21:11

They may intend to harm You. 📖

But the plans they make will not succeed.

## Psalms 21:12

You will make them flee when You aim Your bow at them. 📖

## Psalms 21:13

LORD, be raised high in Your strength. 📖

We will sing and praise Your power.
