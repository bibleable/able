# Psalms 12 — A prayer when lies are everywhere

**About this chapter**

David asks for help against dishonest speech.

The LORD speaks in verse 5.

## Psalms 12:1

For the choir leader, according to Sheminith. 📖

A psalm of David.

Help, LORD!

Godly people are gone.

Faithful people have disappeared from among human beings.

**Help with Sheminith:** A musical term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

Godly people are devoted to God.

## Psalms 12:2

People lie to each other. 📖

Their lips flatter while their hearts are divided.

**Help with flatter:** Give insincere praise to gain favor.

## Psalms 12:3

May the LORD cut off every flattering lip and every boastful tongue. 📖

## Psalms 12:4

They say, “Our tongues will win. 📖

Our lips belong to us.

Who can be our master?”

## Psalms 12:5

The LORD says, “Because people are treated cruelly and needy people groan, I will now rise up. 📖

I will bring safety to the person who longs for it.”

## Psalms 12:6

The LORD’s words have no flaw. 📖

They are like silver purified in a furnace.

They are like gold purified seven times.

## Psalms 12:7

LORD, You will keep us safe. 📖

You will guard us from this generation forever.

## Psalms 12:8

Wicked people walk about freely. 📖

People honor things that are vile.

**Help with vile:** Deeply evil and shameful.
