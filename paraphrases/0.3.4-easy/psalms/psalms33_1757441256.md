# Psalms 33 — Praise the LORD who made and watches the world

**About this chapter**

This song calls people to praise the LORD.

It speaks of His creation, plans, and care for people who trust Him.

## Psalms 33:1

Righteous people, rejoice in the LORD. 📖

It is fitting for upright people to praise Him.

**Help with righteous and upright:** Right before God and living honestly.

## Psalms 33:2

Praise the LORD with the harp. 📖

Make music to Him with ten strings.

## Psalms 33:3

Sing Him a new song. 📖

Play skillfully and shout for joy.

## Psalms 33:4

The LORD’s word is upright. 📖

All His work is trustworthy.

## Psalms 33:5

The LORD loves righteousness and justice. 📖

The earth is full of His faithful love.

**Help with righteousness and justice:** Doing what is right and treating people fairly.

## Psalms 33:6

The heavens were made by the LORD’s word. 📖

All the stars were made by the breath of His mouth.

## Psalms 33:7

He piles up the sea’s waters. 📖

He puts the depths in storehouses.

## Psalms 33:8

Let all the earth fear the LORD. 📖

Let all the world’s people deeply respect Him.

**Help with fear the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 33:9

He spoke, and it came to be. 📖

He commanded, and it stood firm.

## Psalms 33:10

The LORD defeats the nations’ plans. 📖

He stops the peoples’ schemes.

## Psalms 33:11

The LORD’s plans stand forever. 📖

His heart’s purposes last through all generations.

## Psalms 33:12

Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD! 📖

Blessed are the people He chose as His inheritance!

**Help with inheritance:** The song describes the people as His own possession.

## Psalms 33:13

The LORD looks down from heaven. 📖

He sees all human beings.

## Psalms 33:14

From His home He watches everyone who lives on earth. 📖

## Psalms 33:15

He shapes each person’s heart. 📖

He considers everything they do.

## Psalms 33:16

No king is saved by his huge army. 📖

No warrior is rescued by his great strength.

## Psalms 33:17

A horse is an empty hope for rescue. 📖

Even its great strength cannot save.

## Psalms 33:18

The LORD’s eyes are surely on people who fear Him. 📖

Their hope is in His faithful love.

## Psalms 33:19

He watches to rescue them from death. 📖

He keeps them alive in famine.

**Help with famine:** A time when there is very little food.

## Psalms 33:20

Our soul waits for the LORD. 📖

He is our help and shield.

## Psalms 33:21

Our hearts rejoice in Him because we trust in His holy name. 📖

## Psalms 33:22

LORD, may Your faithful love rest on us as we put our hope in You. 📖
