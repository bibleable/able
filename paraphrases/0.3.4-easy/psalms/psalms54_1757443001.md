# Psalms 54 — A prayer when David is betrayed

**About this chapter**

David asks God to rescue him after people report his hiding place to Saul.

## Psalms 54:1

For the choir leader, with stringed instruments. 📖

A Maskil of David.

The Ziphites went to Saul and asked, “Is David not hiding among us?”

God, save me by Your name.

Prove me right by Your might!

**Help with Maskil:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 54:2

God, hear my prayer. 📖

Listen to the words of my mouth.

## Psalms 54:3

Strangers rise against me. 📖

Cruel men seek my life.

They have no regard for God.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 54:4

God is surely my helper. 📖

The Lord keeps my soul alive.

## Psalms 54:5

He will repay my enemies with evil. 📖

Destroy them in Your faithfulness.

## Psalms 54:6

I will freely offer sacrifices to You. 📖

LORD, I will praise Your name because it is good.

**Help with sacrifices:** Offerings given to God.

## Psalms 54:7

He has rescued me from every trouble. 📖

My eyes have looked in victory at my enemies.
