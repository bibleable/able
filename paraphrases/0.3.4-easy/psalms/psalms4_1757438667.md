# Psalms 4 — A prayer for help and peaceful rest

**About this chapter**

David calls on God and addresses other people.

The song includes instructions to trust the LORD.

## Psalms 4:1

For the choir leader, with stringed instruments. 📖

A psalm of David.

Answer when I call, God of my righteousness!

You have relieved my distress.

Show me grace and hear my prayer.

**Help with righteousness:** Being right before God.

Grace is kindness freely given.

## Psalms 4:2

Men, how long will you speak against my honor? 📖

How long will you love worthless things and seek lies?

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 4:3

Know that the LORD has set godly people apart for Himself. 📖

The LORD hears when I call Him.

**Help with godly:** Devoted to God.

## Psalms 4:4

Be angry, but do not sin. 📖

Search your heart on your bed.

Be still.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 4:5

Offer the sacrifices of righteous people. 📖

Trust in the LORD.

**Help with sacrifices:** Offerings given to God.

## Psalms 4:6

Many ask, “Who can show us good?” 📖

LORD, shine the light of Your face on us.

**Help with light of Your face:** A picture of God showing favor and care.

## Psalms 4:7

You have filled my heart with greater joy than people have when grain and new wine are plentiful. 📖

## Psalms 4:8

I will lie down and sleep in peace. 📖

You alone, LORD, make me live in safety.
