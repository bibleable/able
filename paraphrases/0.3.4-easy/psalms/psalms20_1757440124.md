# Psalms 20 — A prayer for the king’s rescue

**About this chapter**

This song asks God to help the king.

Its requests are prayers, rather than unconditional promises to every reader.

## Psalms 20:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

May the LORD answer you on the day of trouble.

May the name of Jacob’s God protect you.

## Psalms 20:2

May He send help from the sanctuary. 📖

May He support you from Zion.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

Zion is a name associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 20:3

May He remember all your gifts. 📖

May He look kindly on your burnt offerings.

Selah.

**Help with burnt offerings:** Sacrifices burned as offerings to God.

Selah is a word in the song whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 20:4

May He give you your heart’s desires. 📖

May He make all your plans succeed.

## Psalms 20:5

May we shout for joy at your victory. 📖

May we raise a banner in our God’s name.

May the LORD grant all your requests.

**Help with banner:** A flag or sign lifted up for people to see.

## Psalms 20:6

Now I know that the LORD saves His anointed. 📖

He answers him from His holy heaven with His right hand’s saving power.

**Help with anointed:** The king appointed through a ceremony using oil.

## Psalms 20:7

Some trust in chariots, and others trust in horses. 📖

But we trust in the name of the LORD our God.

**Help with chariots:** Wheeled vehicles used in battle.

## Psalms 20:8

They collapse and fall. 📖

But we rise and stand firm.

## Psalms 20:9

LORD, save the king. 📖

Answer us on the day we call.
