# Psalms 67 — May all peoples praise God

**About this chapter**

This song asks for God’s blessing so that all nations may know Him.

## Psalms 67:1

For the choir leader, with stringed instruments. 📖

A psalm.

A song.

May God be kind to us and bless us.

May His face shine on us.

Selah.

**Help with shining face:** A picture of God showing favor and care.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 67:2

Then Your ways may be known on earth. 📖

Your rescue may be known among all nations.

## Psalms 67:3

God, let the peoples praise You. 📖

Let all the peoples praise You.

## Psalms 67:4

Let the nations be glad and sing for joy. 📖

You judge the peoples fairly and lead the earth’s nations.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 67:5

God, let the peoples praise You. 📖

Let all the peoples praise You.

## Psalms 67:6

The earth has given its harvest. 📖

God, our God, blesses us.

## Psalms 67:7

God blesses us so that all the earth’s ends will fear Him. 📖

**Help with fear Him:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.
