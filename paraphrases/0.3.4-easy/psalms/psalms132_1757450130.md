# Psalms 132 — Remember David and the LORD’s promise

**About this chapter**

The song recalls David’s vow and the LORD’s promises about his family and Zion.

## Psalms 132:1

A song of ascents. 📖

LORD, remember all David’s hardships for his sake,

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 132:2

how he swore an oath to the LORD. 📖

He vowed to Jacob’s Mighty One:

## Psalms 132:3

“I will not enter my house or get into my bed. 📖

## Psalms 132:4

I will not let my eyes sleep or my eyelids doze, 📖

## Psalms 132:5

until I find a place for the LORD. 📖

I will find a home for Jacob’s Mighty One.”

## Psalms 132:6

We heard the ark was in Ephrathah. 📖

We found it in Jaar’s fields.

**Help with ark:** The sacred chest associated with God’s covenant and presence.

## Psalms 132:7

Let us go to His home. 📖

Let us worship at His footstool.

**Help with footstool:** A low support for the feet, picturing worship before God as King.

## Psalms 132:8

LORD, rise to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength. 📖

## Psalms 132:9

May Your priests wear righteousness like clothing. 📖

May Your saints shout for joy.

**Help with righteousness and saints:** What is right before God, and people set apart for Him.

## Psalms 132:10

For Your servant David’s sake, do not reject Your anointed one. 📖

**Help with anointed:** The king appointed through a ceremony using oil.

## Psalms 132:11

The LORD swore David an oath He will not take back. 📖

He said, “I will put one of your descendants on your throne.

**Help with descendants:** Children and later generations.

## Psalms 132:12

If your sons keep My covenant and the testimony I teach them, their sons will sit on your throne forever.” 📖

**Help with covenant and testimony:** A serious agreement, and what God declares for His people to keep.

## Psalms 132:13

The LORD chose Zion. 📖

He wanted it for His home.

## Psalms 132:14

He said, “This is My resting place forever and ever. 📖

I will live here because I desired this home.

## Psalms 132:15

I will bless it with plentiful supplies. 📖

I will satisfy its poor people with bread.

## Psalms 132:16

I will clothe its priests with rescue. 📖

Its saints will sing for joy.

## Psalms 132:17

There I will make a horn grow for David. 📖

I have prepared a lamp for My anointed one.

**Help with horn and lamp:** Pictures of royal strength and continuing life or rule.

## Psalms 132:18

I will clothe his enemies with shame. 📖

But his crown will shine.”
