# Psalm 51 — God, give me a clean heart

**About this chapter**

David wrote this song for the leader of the singers.

Nathan came to David after David committed adultery with Bathsheba.

Adultery means having sex with someone married to another person.

David is praying to God.

## Psalms 51:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

Nathan the prophet came to David after David’s adultery with Bathsheba.

God, have mercy on me because of your faithful love.

Because of your great compassion, erase my wrongs.

**Help with words:** A prophet speaks God’s message.

Adultery means having sex with another person’s husband or wife.

Compassion means deep care for someone who needs help.

## Psalms 51:2

Wash away my wrongdoing. 📖

Make me clean from my sin.

**Help with this picture:** Washing pictures God's forgiveness and removal of wrongdoing.

## Psalms 51:3

I know the wrongs I have done. 📖

My sin is always before me.

## Psalms 51:4

I have sinned against you, you alone. 📖

I did what you see as evil.

So you may be proved right when you speak and blameless when you judge.

## Psalms 51:5

I was sinful from my birth. 📖

I was sinful from the time my mother conceived me.

## Psalms 51:6

You want truth deep inside me. 📖

You teach me wisdom in my inner life.

## Psalms 51:7

Cleanse me with hyssop, and I will be clean. 📖

Wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.

**Help with a word:** Hyssop is a plant used in cleansing ceremonies.

## Psalms 51:8

Let me hear joy and gladness. 📖

Let the bones you crushed rejoice.

**Help with this picture:** Crushed bones picture David's deep pain under God's correction.

## Psalms 51:9

Turn your face away from my sins. 📖

Erase all my wrongdoing.

## Psalms 51:10

God, create a clean heart in me. 📖

Renew a right spirit within me.

**Help with clean heart and right spirit:** Inner thoughts, desires, and attitudes that are right before God.

## Psalms 51:11

Do not send me away from you. 📖

Do not take your Holy Spirit from me.

## Psalms 51:12

Give me back the joy of your rescue. 📖

Help me with a spirit willing to obey.

## Psalms 51:13

Then I will teach wrongdoers your ways. 📖

People who sin will return to you.

## Psalms 51:14

God, my rescuer, free me from the guilt of shedding blood. 📖

Then I will sing about your righteousness.

**Help with words:** Shedding blood means causing death.

Righteousness means God's goodness and justice.

## Psalms 51:15

Lord, open my lips. 📖

My mouth will speak your praise.

## Psalms 51:16

You do not delight in sacrifice, or I would bring it. 📖

Burnt offerings do not give you pleasure.

## Psalms 51:17

The sacrifice God wants is a broken spirit. 📖

God, you will not reject a broken heart that is truly sorry.

## Psalms 51:18

As you are pleased to do, make Zion prosper. 📖

Build up Jerusalem’s walls.

**Help with a name:** Zion is a name associated with Jerusalem.

Prosper means grow strong and do well.

## Psalms 51:19

Then you will delight in right sacrifices and whole burnt offerings. 📖

Bulls will be offered on your altar.

**Help with a word:** An altar is a place where offerings are given to God.
