# Psalms 31 — Trusting God in distress

**About this chapter**

David prays for rescue from enemies and speaks of the LORD’s care.

He also asks for judgment against wicked people.

## Psalms 31:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

LORD, I have taken refuge in You.

Never let me be put to shame.

Save me by Your righteousness.

**Help with refuge and righteousness:** Safety and protection, and doing what is right.

## Psalms 31:2

Listen closely to me. 📖

Come quickly to rescue me.

Be my rock of refuge and my strong place of rescue.

## Psalms 31:3

You are my rock and fortress. 📖

Lead and guide me for the sake of Your name.

## Psalms 31:4

You free me from the net set for me. 📖

You are my refuge.

## Psalms 31:5

I place my spirit in Your hands. 📖

LORD, God of truth, You have rescued me.

## Psalms 31:6

I hate people who cling to worthless idols. 📖

But I trust in the LORD.

**Help with idols:** Images worshiped as gods.

## Psalms 31:7

I will be glad and rejoice in Your faithful love. 📖

You have seen my suffering.

You have known my soul’s deep distress.

## Psalms 31:8

You have not handed me over to the enemy. 📖

You have set my feet in an open place.

## Psalms 31:9

Have mercy on me, LORD, because I am in distress. 📖

My eyes fail from sorrow.

My soul and body fail too.

## Psalms 31:10

My life is used up with grief. 📖

My years are filled with groaning.

My sin has drained my strength.

My bones are wasting away.

## Psalms 31:11

I am a disgrace among all my enemies, and even more among my neighbors. 📖

My friends dread me.

They flee when they see me in the street.

## Psalms 31:12

I am forgotten like a dead man, out of mind. 📖

I am like a broken pot.

## Psalms 31:13

I hear many people speaking lies against me. 📖

Terror is on every side.

They plan together against me.

They plan to take my life.

## Psalms 31:14

But I trust in You, LORD. 📖

I say, “You are my God.”

## Psalms 31:15

My times are in Your hands. 📖

Rescue me from my enemies and those who chase me.

## Psalms 31:16

Make Your face shine on Your servant. 📖

Save me by Your faithful love.

**Help with shining face:** A picture of showing favor and care.

## Psalms 31:17

LORD, do not let me be ashamed, because I called to You. 📖

Let wicked people be put to shame.

Let them lie silent in Sheol.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Psalms 31:18

Let lying lips be silenced. 📖

They speak proudly against righteous people.

They are full of pride and contempt.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 31:19

How great is the goodness You have stored for people who fear You! 📖

Before human beings, You have given it to people who take refuge in You.

**Help with fear You:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 31:20

You hide them in the secret place of Your presence from people’s plots. 📖

You shelter them from accusing tongues.

## Psalms 31:21

Blessed be the LORD! 📖

In a city surrounded for attack, He showed me His faithful love.

## Psalms 31:22

In my alarm I said, “I am cut off from Your sight!” 📖

But You heard my appeal for mercy when I called for help.

## Psalms 31:23

All His saints, love the LORD. 📖

The LORD keeps faithful people safe.

But He fully repays proud people.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 31:24

All who hope in the LORD, be strong and courageous. 📖
