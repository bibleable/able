# Psalms 22 — A cry of abandonment and a song of praise

**About this chapter**

David’s song begins with great distress and ends with praise.

It includes enemies’ mocking words and vivid pictures of suffering.

## Psalms 22:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “The Doe of the Dawn.” 📖

A psalm of David.

My God, my God, why have You abandoned me?

Why are You so far from saving me and from my groaning words?

**Help with doe:** A female deer.

## Psalms 22:2

My God, I cry out by day, but You do not answer. 📖

I cry at night, but I have no rest.

## Psalms 22:3

Yet You are holy. 📖

You sit enthroned on Israel’s praises.

## Psalms 22:4

Our fathers trusted in You. 📖

They trusted, and You rescued them.

## Psalms 22:5

They cried to You and were set free. 📖

They trusted You and were not disappointed.

## Psalms 22:6

But I am a worm, not a man. 📖

People scorn me and despise me.

**Help with worm:** The singer pictures how low and worthless others make him feel.

## Psalms 22:7

Everyone who sees me mocks me. 📖

They sneer and shake their heads, saying:

## Psalms 22:8

“He trusts in the LORD. 📖

Let the LORD rescue him!

Let the LORD save him, since He delights in him!”

## Psalms 22:9

Yet You brought me from the womb. 📖

You kept me secure at my mother’s breast.

## Psalms 22:10

From birth I was placed in Your care. 📖

You have been my God since my mother’s womb.

## Psalms 22:11

Do not be far from me. 📖

Trouble is near, and no one helps.

## Psalms 22:12

Many bulls surround me. 📖

Strong bulls from Bashan circle me.

**Help with Bashan:** A region known for its cattle.

The song pictures dangerous enemies as strong animals.

## Psalms 22:13

They open their jaws against me like lions that roar and tear their prey. 📖

## Psalms 22:14

I am poured out like water. 📖

All my bones are out of joint.

My heart is like wax, melting inside me.

## Psalms 22:15

My strength has dried up like a piece of broken pottery. 📖

My tongue sticks to the roof of my mouth.

You lay me in death’s dust.

## Psalms 22:16

Dogs surround me. 📖

A group of evil men circles me.

They have pierced my hands and feet.

## Psalms 22:17

I can count all my bones. 📖

They stare and look at me with cruel pleasure.

## Psalms 22:18

They divide my clothes among themselves. 📖

They cast lots for my clothing.

**Help with cast lots:** Use objects drawn or thrown to decide who gets something.

## Psalms 22:19

But LORD, do not be far away. 📖

My Strength, come quickly to help me!

## Psalms 22:20

Rescue my soul from the sword. 📖

Rescue my precious life from wild dogs’ power.

## Psalms 22:21

Save me from the lion’s mouth. 📖

At the wild oxen’s horns, You have answered me!

## Psalms 22:22

I will announce Your name to my brothers. 📖

I will praise You in the gathering.

## Psalms 22:23

People who fear the LORD, praise Him! 📖

All Jacob’s descendants, honor Him!

All Israel’s children, revere Him!

**Help with fear and revere:** Respect God deeply and take His authority seriously.

Descendants are children and later generations.

## Psalms 22:24

He has not despised or hated the suffering person’s torment. 📖

He has not hidden His face from him.

He has heard his cry for help.

## Psalms 22:25

My praise for You sounds out in the great gathering. 📖

I will keep my vows before people who fear You.

**Help with vows:** Serious promises.

## Psalms 22:26

Poor people will eat and be satisfied. 📖

People who seek the LORD will praise Him.

May your hearts live forever!

## Psalms 22:27

All the earth’s ends will remember and turn to the LORD. 📖

All the nations’ families will bow before Him.

## Psalms 22:28

Royal authority belongs to the LORD. 📖

He rules over the nations.

## Psalms 22:29

All the earth’s rich people will feast and worship. 📖

Everyone going down to the dust will kneel before Him.

Even people who cannot keep themselves alive will kneel.

## Psalms 22:30

Future generations will serve Him. 📖

They will tell a new generation about the Lord.

## Psalms 22:31

They will come and tell people not yet born about His righteousness. 📖

They will tell everything He has done.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.
