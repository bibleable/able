# Psalms 27 — The LORD is my light and rescue

**About this chapter**

David expresses confidence and asks for help.

He ends by urging patient hope in the LORD.

## Psalms 27:1

Of David. 📖

The LORD is my light and my rescue.

Whom should I fear?

The LORD is my life’s stronghold.

Whom should I dread?

**Help with stronghold:** A strong place of protection.

## Psalms 27:2

Wicked people came to eat my flesh. 📖

But my enemies stumbled and fell.

## Psalms 27:3

Even if an army camps around me, my heart will not fear. 📖

Even if war breaks out against me, I will keep trusting.

## Psalms 27:4

I have asked the LORD for one thing. 📖

I desire to live in His house all my life.

I want to gaze on the LORD’s beauty and seek Him in His temple.

## Psalms 27:5

On the day of trouble He will hide me in His shelter. 📖

He will hide me under His tent’s covering.

He will set me high on a rock.

## Psalms 27:6

Then my head will be held high above the enemies around me. 📖

At His tabernacle I will offer sacrifices with joyful shouts.

I will sing and make music to the LORD.

**Help with tabernacle:** A tent used for worship.

Sacrifices are offerings given to God.

## Psalms 27:7

LORD, hear my voice when I call. 📖

Have mercy and answer me.

## Psalms 27:8

My heart said, “Seek His face.” 📖

LORD, I will seek Your face.

**Help with seek His face:** Turn to God and seek His presence.

## Psalms 27:9

Do not hide Your face from me. 📖

Do not turn Your servant away in anger.

You have been my helper.

God who saves me, do not leave or abandon me.

## Psalms 27:10

Even if my father and mother abandon me, the LORD will receive me. 📖

## Psalms 27:11

LORD, teach me Your way. 📖

Lead me on a level path because of those who treat me cruelly.

## Psalms 27:12

Do not hand me over to my enemies’ wishes. 📖

False witnesses rise against me, breathing out violence.

## Psalms 27:13

Yet I am certain I will see the LORD’s goodness in the land of the living. 📖

## Psalms 27:14

Wait patiently for the LORD. 📖

Be strong and courageous.

Wait patiently for the LORD!
