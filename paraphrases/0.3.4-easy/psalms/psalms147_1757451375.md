# Psalms 147 — Praise God’s care, power, and word

**About this chapter**

The song praises the LORD for restoring His people and caring for creation.

## Psalms 147:1

Hallelujah! 📖

It is good to sing praises to our God.

Praising Him is pleasant and lovely.

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.

## Psalms 147:2

The LORD builds Jerusalem. 📖

He gathers Israel’s exiles.

**Help with exiles:** People forced away from their homeland.

## Psalms 147:3

He heals brokenhearted people and bandages their wounds. 📖

## Psalms 147:4

He decides the number of stars. 📖

He calls each by name.

## Psalms 147:5

Our Lord is great and mighty in power. 📖

His understanding has no limit.

## Psalms 147:6

The LORD supports humble people. 📖

But He throws wicked people to the ground.

## Psalms 147:7

Sing to the LORD with thanks. 📖

Make music to our God on the harp,

## Psalms 147:8

who covers the sky with clouds and prepares rain for the earth. 📖

He makes grass grow on hills.

## Psalms 147:9

He feeds animals and young ravens when they call. 📖

**Help with ravens:** Large black birds.

## Psalms 147:10

He does not delight in a horse’s strength. 📖

He takes no pleasure in a man’s legs.

## Psalms 147:11

The LORD is pleased with people who fear Him and hope in His faithful love. 📖

**Help with fear Him:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 147:12

Jerusalem, lift the LORD high in praise. 📖

Zion, praise your God!

## Psalms 147:13

He makes your gates’ bars strong and blesses the children inside you. 📖

## Psalms 147:14

He gives peace at your borders. 📖

He fills you with the finest wheat.

## Psalms 147:15

He sends His command to earth. 📖

His word runs swiftly.

## Psalms 147:16

He spreads snow like wool. 📖

He scatters frost like ashes.

## Psalms 147:17

He throws hail like pebbles. 📖

Who can stand against His icy blast?

**Help with hail:** Balls of ice falling from clouds.

## Psalms 147:18

He sends His word and melts them. 📖

He releases His winds, and waters flow.

## Psalms 147:19

He tells His word to Jacob. 📖

He tells His laws and judgments to Israel.

## Psalms 147:20

He has done this for no other nation. 📖

They do not know His judgments.

Hallelujah!
