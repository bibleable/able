# Psalms 127 — Unless the LORD builds the house

**About this chapter**

Solomon’s song speaks of depending on God and the gift of children.

## Psalms 127:1

A song of ascents. 📖

Of Solomon.

Unless the LORD builds the house, the builders work for nothing.

Unless the LORD protects the city, its guards watch for nothing.

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 127:2

You rise early and stay up late, working hard for bread, but for nothing. 📖

He gives sleep to those He loves.

## Psalms 127:3

Children are an inheritance from the LORD. 📖

The womb’s fruit is His reward.

**Help with inheritance and womb’s fruit:** A gift someone receives, and children.

## Psalms 127:4

Children born in a person’s youth are like arrows in a warrior’s hand. 📖

## Psalms 127:5

Blessed is the man whose quiver is full of them. 📖

He will not be ashamed when confronting enemies at the gate.

**Help with quiver:** A container for arrows.

The city gate was also a place for public and legal business.
