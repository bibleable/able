# Psalm 113 — Praise the LORD

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Psalms 113:1

Praise the LORD! 🙌

All of you who serve the LORD, praise him.

Praise the LORD's name.

**Help with a word:** To praise God is to say how good and great God is.

## Psalms 113:2

Praise the LORD's name now and forever. 🙌

**Help with a word:** To praise God is to say how good and great God is.

## Psalms 113:3

The sun rises in the east. 🌅

The sun sets in the west.

From east to west, people praise the LORD's name.

**Help with a word:** To praise God is to say how good and great God is.

## Psalms 113:4

The LORD rules over all nations. 🌍

His greatness is above the heavens.

**Help with words:** Nations are countries and their people.

The heavens are the skies and what is above them.

## Psalms 113:5

Who is like the LORD our God? 👑

He sits as King high above us.

## Psalms 113:6

The LORD bends down to look at the heavens and the earth. 🌍

**Help with words:** The heavens are the skies and what is above them.

## Psalms 113:7

The LORD lifts poor people from the dust. 🤝

He lifts people who need help from the rubbish heap.

**Help with this picture**

A rubbish heap is a pile of things people throw away.

This verse pictures God lifting people out of great need.

## Psalms 113:8

The LORD gives these people seats with leaders. 🪑

They sit with the leaders of his people.

**Help with this picture:** Sitting with leaders shows that these people are treated as important.

## Psalms 113:9

The LORD gives a woman who could not have children a home with children. 🏠

He makes her a happy mother.

Praise the LORD!

**Help with a word:** To praise God is to say how good and great God is.
