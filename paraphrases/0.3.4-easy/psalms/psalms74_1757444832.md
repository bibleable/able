# Psalms 74 — A prayer after the sanctuary is destroyed

**About this chapter**

Asaph describes the destruction of God’s meeting place.

He remembers God’s power and asks Him to act.

## Psalms 74:1

A Maskil of Asaph. 📖

God, why have You rejected us forever?

Why does Your anger burn against the sheep of Your pasture?

**Help with Maskil:** A song term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

The sheep picture God’s people.

## Psalms 74:2

Remember Your gathering of people, whom You bought long ago. 📖

You rescued them as the tribe of Your inheritance.

Remember Mount Zion, where You live.

**Help with inheritance:** The people belonging to God.

## Psalms 74:3

Walk toward the lasting ruins. 📖

Look at everything the enemy destroyed in the sanctuary.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 74:4

Your enemies roared inside Your meeting place. 📖

They spread out their banners as signs,

**Help with banners:** Flags or signs showing their power.

## Psalms 74:5

like men swinging axes among thick trees, 📖

## Psalms 74:6

smashing all the carvings with hatchets and picks. 📖

**Help with hatchets and picks:** Tools for cutting and breaking things.

## Psalms 74:7

They burned Your sanctuary to the ground. 📖

They made the home of Your Name unholy.

## Psalms 74:8

They thought, “We will crush them completely.” 📖

They burned every place in the land where God met us.

## Psalms 74:9

There are no signs for us to see. 📖

There is no prophet anymore.

None of us knows how long this will last.

**Help with prophet:** Someone who speaks God’s message.

## Psalms 74:10

God, how long will the enemy mock You? 📖

Will Your enemy insult Your name forever?

## Psalms 74:11

Why do You hold back Your strong right hand? 📖

Stretch it out to destroy them!

## Psalms 74:12

Yet God has been my King since ancient times. 📖

He brings rescue on the earth.

## Psalms 74:13

You divided the sea by Your strength. 📖

You smashed the heads of the sea’s dragons.

## Psalms 74:14

You crushed Leviathan’s heads. 📖

You gave him as food to desert creatures.

**Help with Leviathan and dragons:** Powerful creatures named in the song’s images of God’s victory.

No modern animal identification is supplied here.

## Psalms 74:15

You broke open the fountain and flood. 📖

You dried up rivers that always flow.

## Psalms 74:16

The day belongs to You, and so does the night. 📖

You established the moon and sun.

## Psalms 74:17

You set all the earth’s boundaries. 📖

You made summer and winter.

## Psalms 74:18

LORD, remember how the enemy mocked You. 📖

A foolish people rejected Your name with contempt.

## Psalms 74:19

Do not hand Your dove’s soul over to wild animals. 📖

Do not forget Your suffering people’s lives forever.

**Help with dove:** A picture of God’s vulnerable people.

## Psalms 74:20

Remember Your covenant. 📖

Violent places fill the land’s dark corners.

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Psalms 74:21

Do not let people treated cruelly retreat in shame. 📖

May poor and needy people praise Your name.

## Psalms 74:22

Rise, God, and defend Your cause! 📖

Remember how the fool mocks You all day.

## Psalms 74:23

Do not ignore Your enemies’ noise. 📖

Their uproar rises continually.
