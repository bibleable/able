# Psalms 141 — Guard my mouth and keep me from evil

**About this chapter**

David asks for help with his own words and conduct, and for rescue from wicked people.

## Psalms 141:1

A psalm of David. 📖

LORD, I call You.

Come quickly to me.

Hear my voice when I call.

## Psalms 141:2

May my prayer be set before You like incense. 📖

May my lifted hands be like the evening offering.

**Help with incense:** A substance burned to give a sweet smell in worship.

## Psalms 141:3

LORD, set a guard over my mouth. 📖

Watch the door of my lips.

**Help with this picture:** David asks for help to control his speech.

## Psalms 141:4

Do not let my heart be drawn toward any evil. 📖

Do not let me join wicked men in evil works.

Do not let me feast on their rich foods.

## Psalms 141:5

Let a righteous man strike me. 📖

Let his rebuke be faithful love.

It is oil for my head.

Do not let me refuse it.

My prayer is always against wicked people’s deeds.

**Help with righteous and rebuke:** Right before God, and strong correction for wrongdoing.

Oil pictures welcome and beneficial correction.

## Psalms 141:6

When their rulers are thrown from cliffs, people will listen to my words because they are pleasant. 📖

## Psalms 141:7

As when someone plows and breaks soil, our bones are scattered at Sheol’s mouth. 📖

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

The verse uses a severe picture of scattered bones.

## Psalms 141:8

But GOD the Lord, my eyes are fixed on You. 📖

I seek refuge in You.

Do not leave my soul without defense.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 141:9

Keep me from the traps they laid and from evildoers’ lures. 📖

## Psalms 141:10

Let wicked people fall into their own nets while I pass safely. 📖
