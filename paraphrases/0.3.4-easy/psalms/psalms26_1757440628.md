# Psalms 26 — David asks God to examine him

**About this chapter**

David appeals to the LORD and speaks of his trust, conduct, and love for God’s house.

## Psalms 26:1

Of David. 📖

LORD, prove me right!

I have lived with integrity.

I have trusted the LORD without wavering.

**Help with integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

Without wavering means without turning away.

## Psalms 26:2

LORD, test me and try me. 📖

Examine my heart and mind.

## Psalms 26:3

Your faithful love is before my eyes. 📖

I have walked in Your truth.

## Psalms 26:4

I do not sit with men who lie. 📖

I do not keep company with hypocrites.

**Help with hypocrites:** People who pretend to be better than they are.

## Psalms 26:5

I hate the gathering of people who do evil. 📖

I refuse to sit with wicked people.

## Psalms 26:6

I wash my hands in innocence. 📖

Then I may go around Your altar, LORD,

**Help with altar:** A place for offerings to God.

Washing hands pictures innocence.

## Psalms 26:7

raising my voice in thanks. 📖

I will tell about all Your wonderful works.

## Psalms 26:8

LORD, I love the house where You live. 📖

I love the place where Your glory rests.

## Psalms 26:9

Do not take my soul away with sinners. 📖

Do not take my life with men who shed blood.

## Psalms 26:10

Their hands hold wicked plans. 📖

Their right hands are full of bribes.

**Help with bribes:** Gifts used to make someone act dishonestly.

## Psalms 26:11

But I will live with integrity. 📖

Rescue me and have mercy on me.

## Psalms 26:12

My feet stand on level ground. 📖

In the gatherings I will bless the LORD.
