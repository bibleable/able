# Psalms 117 — All nations, praise the LORD

**About this chapter**

This short song calls every nation and people to praise the LORD.

## Psalms 117:1

All nations, praise the LORD! 📖

All peoples, honor Him as great!

## Psalms 117:2

His faithful love toward us is great. 📖

The LORD’s faithfulness lasts forever.

Hallelujah!

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.
