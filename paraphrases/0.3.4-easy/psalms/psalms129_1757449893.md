# Psalms 129 — Israel remembers attacks from its youth

**About this chapter**

Israel speaks of repeated suffering and asks for its enemies to be put to shame.

## Psalms 129:1

A song of ascents. 📖

Let Israel now say, “They have attacked me many times since my youth,

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 129:2

many times since my youth. 📖

But they have not defeated me.

## Psalms 129:3

Plowmen plowed over my back. 📖

They made their furrows long.”

**Help with furrows:** Lines cut in soil by a plow.

The song uses this painful picture for suffering.

## Psalms 129:4

The LORD is righteous. 📖

He cut me free from wicked people’s ropes.

**Help with righteous:** Right and just.

## Psalms 129:5

May everyone who hates Zion be turned back in shame. 📖

## Psalms 129:6

May they be like grass on roofs that dries before it grows, 📖

## Psalms 129:7

giving too little to fill a harvester’s hand or the arms of someone tying grain bundles. 📖

## Psalms 129:8

May no passerby say to them, “The LORD’s blessing be on you. 📖

We bless you in the LORD’s name.”
