# Psalm 94 — God helps people who are hurt

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

**About this song**

The writer asks God to punish people who do evil.

The writer also thanks God for his help.

## Psalms 94:1

LORD, you are the God who punishes evil. ⚖️

God who punishes evil, show yourself!

## Psalms 94:2

God, you judge everyone on earth. ⚖️

Rise up!

Give proud people the punishment they deserve.

**Help with a word:** Here, proud people think they are better than other people.

## Psalms 94:3

LORD, how much longer will people who do evil celebrate? ⏳

How much longer will they enjoy their success?

## Psalms 94:4

They keep saying how great they are. 🗣️

All these people boast about the evil they do.

**Help with a word:** To boast is to talk as if you are very great or important.

## Psalms 94:5

LORD, they hurt your people badly. 💔

They treat the people who belong to you cruelly.

## Psalms 94:6

They kill women whose husbands have died. 💔

They kill people who have come from other countries.

They murder children whose fathers have died.

## Psalms 94:7

These people say, “The LORD does not see us. 🗣️

“The God of Jacob pays no attention.”

**Help with a name:** Jacob was an ancestor of the people of Israel.

An ancestor is a family member who lived long ago.

## Psalms 94:8

You people who act without understanding, pay attention! 👂

When will you learn to be wise?

**Help with a word:** To be wise is to understand and choose what is right.

## Psalms 94:9

God made our ears. 👂

Surely God can hear!

God made our eyes.

Surely God can see!

## Psalms 94:10

God corrects the nations. ⚖️

Surely God can punish people!

God teaches people.

Surely God knows what they do!

**Help with words:** Nations are countries and their people.

To correct someone is to show them what they have done wrong.

## Psalms 94:11

The LORD knows what people think. 💭

He knows that their thoughts achieve nothing.

## Psalms 94:12

LORD, good comes to the person you correct. 📖

You teach that person from your law.

**Help with words:** To correct someone is to show them what they have done wrong.

God's law tells people how God wants them to live.

## Psalms 94:13

You give that person rest from times of trouble. 🕊️

This rest lasts until a pit is dug for people who do evil.

**Help with this picture**

A pit is a deep hole in the ground.

Here, the pit is a picture of the end of people who do evil.

## Psalms 94:14

The LORD will not leave his people. 🤝

He will never give up the people who belong to him.

## Psalms 94:15

Judges will make fair decisions again. ⚖️

Everyone who wants to do right will follow what is fair.

## Psalms 94:16

Who will help me stand against people who do evil? 🤝

Who will stand with me against people who do wrong?

## Psalms 94:17

The LORD helped me. 🤝

Without his help, I would soon have died.

**Help with the Bible's picture**

The BSB calls death a place of silence in this verse.

## Psalms 94:18

When I say, “My foot is slipping,” you hold me up, LORD. 🤝

Your faithful love supports me.

**Help with words:** Faithful love is love that can be trusted.

**Help with this picture:** Slipping is a picture of being in danger or losing support.

## Psalms 94:19

When I have too many worries, you comfort me. 🕊️

Your comfort brings me joy.

**Help with a word:** To comfort someone is to help them feel less sad or afraid.

## Psalms 94:20

Can rulers who do evil be on your side, God? ⚖️

These rulers make laws that hurt people.

## Psalms 94:21

These rulers join together to attack people who do right. 💔

They sentence people to death who have done nothing wrong.

## Psalms 94:22

But the LORD is my strong place of safety. 🪨

My God is like a rock where I can find shelter.

**Help with this picture:** A strong rock is a picture of God's protection.

## Psalms 94:23

God will make their own evil come back on them. ⚖️

God will destroy them because of the evil they do.

The LORD our God will destroy them.
