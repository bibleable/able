# Psalms 7 — David appeals to the righteous Judge

**About this chapter**

David asks the LORD to judge fairly.

He includes a conditional appeal about his own actions and pictures judgment against evil.

## Psalms 7:1

A Shiggaion of David, sung to the LORD concerning the words of Cush, a Benjamite. 📖

LORD my God, I take refuge in You.

Save me from all who pursue me.

Rescue me,

**Help with Shiggaion:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

A Benjamite belongs to the tribe of Benjamin.

Refuge means safety.

## Psalms 7:2

or they will tear my soul apart like a lion. 📖

They will tear me to pieces, with no one to rescue me.

## Psalms 7:3

LORD my God, if I have done this, if my hands have done injustice, 📖

## Psalms 7:4

if I have repaid an ally with evil, or robbed an enemy without cause, 📖

**Help with ally:** Someone on your side.

## Psalms 7:5

let my enemy chase me and catch me. 📖

Let him trample me to the ground.

Let him leave my honor in the dust.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 7:6

Rise up in Your anger, LORD! 📖

Rise against my enemies’ fury.

Awake, my God, and order judgment.

## Psalms 7:7

Let the gathered peoples surround You. 📖

Take Your seat high above them.

## Psalms 7:8

The LORD judges the peoples. 📖

LORD, prove me right according to my righteousness and integrity.

**Help with righteousness and integrity:** Being right and living honestly.

## Psalms 7:9

End wicked people’s evil. 📖

Make righteous people secure.

Righteous God, You examine hearts and minds.

## Psalms 7:10

My shield is with God. 📖

He saves people whose hearts are upright.

**Help with shield and upright:** God is pictured as protection.

Upright means honest and right.

## Psalms 7:11

God is a righteous judge. 📖

He feels anger at evil each day.

## Psalms 7:12

If someone does not repent, God will sharpen His sword. 📖

He has bent His bow and fitted its string.

**Help with repent:** Turn away from wrongdoing.

## Psalms 7:13

He has prepared His deadly weapons. 📖

He makes His arrows burn with fire.

## Psalms 7:14

Look, the wicked man labors to give birth to evil. 📖

He becomes pregnant with trouble and gives birth to lies.

**Help with birth:** A picture of planning and producing evil.

## Psalms 7:15

He digs a hole and makes it deep. 📖

He falls into the pit he made.

## Psalms 7:16

His trouble comes back on him. 📖

His violence falls on his own head.

## Psalms 7:17

I will thank the LORD for His righteousness. 📖

I will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.
