# Psalms 30 — From mourning to dancing

**About this chapter**

David thanks the LORD for healing and rescue.

He recalls his earlier confidence, distress, and prayer.

## Psalms 30:1

A psalm. 📖

A song for the temple’s dedication.

Of David.

LORD, I will lift You high.

You lifted me up.

You did not let my enemies celebrate over me.

**Help with dedication:** Setting the temple apart for worship.

## Psalms 30:2

LORD my God, I cried to You for help. 📖

You healed me.

## Psalms 30:3

LORD, You pulled me up from Sheol. 📖

You kept me from going down to the Pit.

**Help with Sheol and the Pit:** Names connected with the grave and the place of the dead.

## Psalms 30:4

You His saints, sing to the LORD. 📖

Praise His holy name.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 30:5

His anger lasts only a moment. 📖

His favor lasts a lifetime.

Weeping may stay for the night.

But joy comes in the morning.

## Psalms 30:6

When I was successful, I said, “I will never be shaken.” 📖

## Psalms 30:7

LORD, You showed me favor and made my mountain stand strong. 📖

When You hid Your face, I was troubled.

**Help with mountain:** A picture of David’s strong position.

## Psalms 30:8

I called to You, LORD. 📖

I begged my Lord for mercy:

## Psalms 30:9

“What is gained by my bloodshed, by my going down to the Pit? 📖

Will dust praise You?

Will it announce Your faithfulness?

## Psalms 30:10

LORD, hear me and have mercy. 📖

LORD, be my helper.”

## Psalms 30:11

You turned my mourning into dancing. 📖

You took off my sackcloth and clothed me with joy,

**Help with sackcloth:** Rough clothing worn to show grief.

## Psalms 30:12

so my heart may sing Your praises and not be silent. 📖

LORD my God, I will give thanks forever.
