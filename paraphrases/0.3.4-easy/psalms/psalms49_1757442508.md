# Psalms 49 — Wealth cannot buy lasting life

**About this chapter**

The song asks everyone to listen.

It contrasts trust in wealth with hope in God’s rescue from death.

## Psalms 49:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of the sons of Korah.

All peoples, hear this.

Everyone living in the world, listen,

## Psalms 49:2

both low and high, rich and poor alike. 📖

## Psalms 49:3

My mouth will speak wisdom. 📖

My heart’s deep thoughts will bring understanding.

## Psalms 49:4

I will listen closely to a proverb. 📖

I will express my riddle with the harp.

**Help with proverb and riddle:** A wise saying and a puzzling question.

A harp is a stringed musical instrument.

## Psalms 49:5

Why should I fear in times of trouble when wicked people who seize others’ place surround me? 📖

## Psalms 49:6

They trust in their wealth. 📖

They boast about their great riches.

## Psalms 49:7

No one can buy his brother’s freedom from death. 📖

No one can pay his ransom to God.

**Help with ransom:** A payment to set someone free.

## Psalms 49:8

The price for his soul is too high. 📖

No payment can ever be enough,

## Psalms 49:9

so that he could live forever and not see decay. 📖

**Help with decay:** Rotting away after death.

## Psalms 49:10

Clearly, wise people die. 📖

Foolish and senseless people also perish.

They leave their wealth to others.

## Psalms 49:11

Their graves are their homes forever. 📖

They stay there for endless generations, even though lands were named after them.

## Psalms 49:12

Even with wealth, a person cannot last. 📖

He is like an animal that dies.

## Psalms 49:13

This is the fate of foolish people. 📖

It is also the fate of followers who approve their sayings.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 49:14

Like sheep, they are destined for Sheol. 📖

Death will shepherd them.

Upright people will rule them in the morning.

Their bodies will decay in Sheol, far from their grand home.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

Upright means honest and right.

The song pictures death as a shepherd leading them.

## Psalms 49:15

But God will rescue my life from Sheol. 📖

He will surely take me to Himself.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 49:16

Do not be afraid when a man becomes rich. 📖

Do not fear when his house’s splendor grows.

**Help with splendor:** Great displays of wealth and glory.

## Psalms 49:17

When he dies, he will carry nothing away. 📖

His wealth will not follow him down.

## Psalms 49:18

During his life, he may bless his soul. 📖

People praise you when you become successful.

## Psalms 49:19

But he will join his fathers’ generation. 📖

They will never see daylight.

## Psalms 49:20

A person with riches but no understanding is like an animal that dies. 📖
