# Psalms 110 — A royal and priestly promise

**About this chapter**

David reports the LORD’s words to his Lord.

The song includes priesthood, rule, and severe judgment.

## Psalms 110:1

A psalm of David. 📖

The LORD said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand.

Stay until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”

**Help with footstool:** A low support for the feet, picturing complete victory over enemies.

## Psalms 110:2

The LORD extends Your mighty scepter from Zion. 📖

He says, “Rule among Your enemies.”

**Help with scepter and Zion:** A ruler’s staff showing authority, and a name associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 110:3

Your people will be willing on Your day of battle. 📖

In holy splendor, from dawn’s womb, the dew of Your youth belongs to You.

**Help with these images:** The verse uses poetic pictures of readiness, holiness, dawn, and youth.

Their exact relationship needs further editorial review.

## Psalms 110:4

The LORD swore and will not change His mind. 📖

He said, “You are a priest forever in Melchizedek’s order.”

**Help with order:** The kind or pattern of priesthood associated with Melchizedek.

## Psalms 110:5

The Lord is at Your right hand. 📖

He will crush kings on the day of His wrath.

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Psalms 110:6

He will judge the nations and pile up the dead. 📖

He will crush leaders far and wide.

## Psalms 110:7

He will drink from the stream beside the road. 📖

So He will lift His head.
