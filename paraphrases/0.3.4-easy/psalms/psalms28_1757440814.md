# Psalms 28 — A cry for mercy and thanks for help

**About this chapter**

David asks for mercy and judgment.

He then thanks the LORD and prays for His people.

## Psalms 28:1

Of David. 📖

LORD, I call to You.

My Rock, do not refuse to hear me.

If You stay silent, I will be like those going down to the Pit.

**Help with Rock:** A picture of strong protection.

The Pit means the grave or place of the dead.

## Psalms 28:2

Hear my cry for mercy when I call for help. 📖

Hear me when I lift my hands toward Your holy sanctuary.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 28:3

Do not drag me away with wicked people who do evil. 📖

They speak peace to their neighbors but hold harmful plans in their hearts.

## Psalms 28:4

Repay them according to their deeds and evil works. 📖

Repay what their hands have done.

Give them back what they deserve.

## Psalms 28:5

They have no regard for the LORD’s works or what His hands have done. 📖

So He will tear them down and never rebuild them.

## Psalms 28:6

Blessed be the LORD! 📖

He has heard my cry for mercy.

## Psalms 28:7

The LORD is my strength and shield. 📖

My heart trusts Him, and I am helped.

So my heart rejoices.

I thank Him with my song.

## Psalms 28:8

The LORD is His people’s strength. 📖

He is a stronghold of rescue for His anointed.

**Help with anointed:** The king appointed through a ceremony using oil.

A stronghold is a protected place.

## Psalms 28:9

Save Your people and bless Your inheritance. 📖

Shepherd them and carry them forever.

**Help with inheritance and shepherd:** The people belong to God.

The song asks Him to care for them as a shepherd cares for sheep.
