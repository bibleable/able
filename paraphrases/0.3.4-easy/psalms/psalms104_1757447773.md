# Psalms 104 — The LORD provides for His creation

**About this chapter**

The song praises God’s wisdom and care for the living world.

It also asks for wickedness to end.

## Psalms 104:1

My soul, bless the LORD! 📖

LORD my God, You are very great.

You wear splendor and majesty like clothing.

**Help with splendor and majesty:** Great royal glory.

## Psalms 104:2

He wraps Himself in light like clothing. 📖

He stretches the heavens like a tent.

## Psalms 104:3

He lays His upper rooms’ beams in the waters above. 📖

He makes clouds His chariot.

He walks on the wind’s wings.

**Help with chariot:** A wheeled vehicle.

The song uses pictures of God’s great power over creation.

## Psalms 104:4

He makes winds His messengers. 📖

Flames of fire are His servants.

## Psalms 104:5

He set the earth on its foundations. 📖

It will never be moved.

## Psalms 104:6

You covered it with the deep like clothing. 📖

Waters stood above the mountains.

## Psalms 104:7

At Your rebuke the waters fled. 📖

At Your thunder’s sound they hurried away.

**Help with rebuke:** A strong command expressing correction.

## Psalms 104:8

Mountains rose and valleys sank to the places You assigned them. 📖

## Psalms 104:9

You set a boundary the waters cannot cross. 📖

They may never cover the earth again.

## Psalms 104:10

He sends springs into valleys. 📖

They flow between mountains.

## Psalms 104:11

They give water to every animal in the fields. 📖

Wild donkeys satisfy their thirst.

## Psalms 104:12

Birds nest beside the springs. 📖

They sing among the branches.

## Psalms 104:13

He waters mountains from His upper rooms. 📖

The earth is satisfied with the fruit of His works.

## Psalms 104:14

He grows grass for livestock and crops for people to cultivate. 📖

He brings food from the earth:

## Psalms 104:15

wine to gladden a person’s heart, oil to make his face shine, and bread to strengthen his heart. 📖

## Psalms 104:16

The LORD’s trees have their fill. 📖

The cedars He planted in Lebanon have enough.

## Psalms 104:17

Birds build nests there. 📖

The stork makes her home in cypress trees.

## Psalms 104:18

High mountains are for wild goats. 📖

Cliffs shelter rock badgers.

**Help with rock badgers:** Small animals that live among rocks.

## Psalms 104:19

He made the moon to mark seasons. 📖

The sun knows when to set.

## Psalms 104:20

You bring darkness, and night comes. 📖

All the forest’s wild animals prowl.

## Psalms 104:21

Young lions roar for prey. 📖

They seek their food from God.

**Help with prey:** Animals hunted for food.

## Psalms 104:22

The sun rises, and they retreat. 📖

They lie in their dens.

## Psalms 104:23

People go out to work. 📖

They labor until evening.

## Psalms 104:24

LORD, Your works are so many! 📖

You made them all with wisdom.

The earth is full of Your creatures.

## Psalms 104:25

Here is the sea, vast and wide. 📖

It is full of countless creatures, both large and small.

## Psalms 104:26

Ships pass there. 📖

Leviathan is there, whom You formed to play there.

**Help with Leviathan:** A powerful creature named in the song.

No definite modern animal identification is supplied.

## Psalms 104:27

All creatures look to You for food at the right time. 📖

## Psalms 104:28

When You give food, they gather it. 📖

When You open Your hand, good things satisfy them.

## Psalms 104:29

When You hide Your face, they are terrified. 📖

When You take their breath away, they die and return to dust.

## Psalms 104:30

When You send Your Spirit, they are created. 📖

You renew the earth’s face.

## Psalms 104:31

May the LORD’s glory last forever. 📖

May the LORD rejoice in His works.

## Psalms 104:32

He looks at the earth, and it shakes. 📖

He touches mountains, and they smolder.

## Psalms 104:33

I will sing to the LORD all my life. 📖

I will praise my God as long as I exist.

## Psalms 104:34

May my thoughts please Him. 📖

I rejoice in the LORD.

## Psalms 104:35

May sinners disappear from the earth. 📖

May wicked people be gone.

My soul, bless the LORD.

Hallelujah!

**Help with Hallelujah:** Praise the LORD.
