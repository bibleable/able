# Psalms 118 — Give thanks for the LORD’s victory

**About this chapter**

The song celebrates rescue and calls the people to give thanks.

It includes repeated declarations and prayers.

## Psalms 118:1

Thank the LORD because He is good. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## Psalms 118:2

Let Israel say, “His faithful love lasts forever.” 📖

## Psalms 118:3

Let Aaron’s house say, “His faithful love lasts forever.” 📖

**Help with Aaron’s house:** Aaron’s family and descendants.

## Psalms 118:4

Let people who fear the LORD say, “His faithful love lasts forever.” 📖

**Help with fear the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 118:5

In my distress I called the LORD. 📖

He answered and set me free.

## Psalms 118:6

The LORD is on my side. 📖

I will not be afraid.

What can a human being do to me?

## Psalms 118:7

The LORD is on my side and helps me. 📖

So I will see victory over those who hate me.

## Psalms 118:8

Taking refuge in the LORD is better than trusting people. 📖

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 118:9

Taking refuge in the LORD is better than trusting princes. 📖

## Psalms 118:10

All nations surrounded me. 📖

But in the LORD’s name I cut them off.

## Psalms 118:11

They surrounded me on every side. 📖

But in the LORD’s name I cut them off.

## Psalms 118:12

They swarmed around me like bees. 📖

They went out like burning thorns.

In the LORD’s name I cut them off.

## Psalms 118:13

I was pushed so hard that I was falling. 📖

But the LORD helped me.

## Psalms 118:14

The LORD is my strength and song. 📖

He has become my rescue.

## Psalms 118:15

Joyful shouts of rescue sound in righteous people’s tents. 📖

They say, “The LORD’s right hand acts bravely!

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 118:16

The LORD’s right hand is lifted high! 📖

The LORD’s right hand acts bravely!”

## Psalms 118:17

I will not die but live. 📖

I will tell what the LORD has done.

## Psalms 118:18

The LORD corrected me severely. 📖

But He did not hand me over to death.

## Psalms 118:19

Open righteousness’s gates for me. 📖

Let me enter and thank the LORD.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right before God.

## Psalms 118:20

This is the LORD’s gate. 📖

Righteous people will enter through it.

## Psalms 118:21

I will thank You because You answered me. 📖

You have become my rescue.

## Psalms 118:22

The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. 📖

**Help with cornerstone:** A main stone in a building.

The picture shows something rejected becoming important.

## Psalms 118:23

This comes from the LORD. 📖

It is wonderful in our eyes.

## Psalms 118:24

This is the day the LORD made. 📖

We will rejoice and be glad in it.

## Psalms 118:25

LORD, please save us. 📖

LORD, we beg You to make us prosper.

## Psalms 118:26

Blessed is the one coming in the LORD’s name. 📖

From the LORD’s house we bless you.

## Psalms 118:27

The LORD is God. 📖

He made His light shine on us.

Tie the festival sacrifice with ropes to the altar’s horns.

**Help with altar’s horns:** Projections at its corners.

The altar is the place for sacrifices.

## Psalms 118:28

You are my God, and I will thank You. 📖

You are my God, and I will lift You high in praise.

## Psalms 118:29

Thank the LORD because He is good. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.
