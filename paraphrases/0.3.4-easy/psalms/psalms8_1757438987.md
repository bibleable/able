# Psalms 8 — The LORD’s glory and His care for people

**About this chapter**

David praises the LORD’s greatness.

He wonders at the place God has given human beings in creation.

## Psalms 8:1

For the choir leader, according to Gittith. 📖

A psalm of David.

LORD, our Lord, how majestic Your name is throughout the earth!

You have set Your glory above the heavens.

**Help with Gittith:** A musical term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

Majestic means showing great power and glory.

## Psalms 8:2

You have established praise from children’s and babies’ mouths because of Your opponents. 📖

You do this to silence the enemy and avenger.

**Help with avenger:** Someone seeking to repay a wrong.

## Psalms 8:3

I look at Your heavens, the work of Your fingers. 📖

I look at the moon and stars You put in place.

## Psalms 8:4

What is a human being, that You remember him? 📖

What is a son of man, that You care for him?

**Help with son of man:** A human being.

## Psalms 8:5

You made him a little lower than the angels. 📖

You crowned him with glory and honor.

## Psalms 8:6

You made him ruler over the works of Your hands. 📖

You put everything under his feet:

## Psalms 8:7

all sheep and oxen, and also the wild animals, 📖

## Psalms 8:8

birds of the sky and fish of the sea. 📖

You included everything that swims the seas’ paths.

## Psalms 8:9

LORD, our Lord, how majestic Your name is throughout the earth! 📖
