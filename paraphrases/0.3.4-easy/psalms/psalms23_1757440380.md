# Psalm 23 — The LORD cares for me

**About this chapter**

David wrote this song.

David compares the LORD to a shepherd who cares for sheep.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Psalms 23:1

A psalm of David. 📖

The LORD is my shepherd.

I have everything I need.

**Help with words:** A psalm is a song or prayer.

A shepherd looks after sheep.

## Psalms 23:2

He lets me rest in fields of green grass. 📖

He leads me beside quiet water.

## Psalms 23:3

He restores my soul. 📖

He leads me to live in the right way.

He does this for the honour of his name.

**Help with restores my soul:** Gives back life and strength to the whole person.

## Psalms 23:4

Even when I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil. 📖

You are with me.

Your shepherd’s rod and staff comfort me.

**Help with this picture:** A shepherd uses these tools to guide and protect sheep.

The valley pictures deep danger and darkness.

## Psalms 23:5

You prepare a meal for me in the presence of my enemies. 📖

You pour oil on my head.

My cup is so full that it runs over.

**Help with this picture:** The meal, oil, and full cup show generous care and welcome.

## Psalms 23:6

Surely your goodness and mercy will follow me all my life. 📖

I will live in the LORD’s house forever.

**Help with mercy:** Kindness and care shown to someone in need.
