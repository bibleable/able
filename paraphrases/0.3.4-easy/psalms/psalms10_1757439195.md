# Psalms 10 — A prayer against cruel people

**About this chapter**

The singer describes wicked people’s behavior and words.

The prayer asks the LORD to act for their victims.

## Psalms 10:1

LORD, why do You stand far away? 📖

Why do You hide in times of trouble?

## Psalms 10:2

Proud, wicked people hunt needy people. 📖

Let them be caught in their own plans.

## Psalms 10:3

The wicked man boasts about his heart’s desires. 📖

He blesses greedy people and speaks against the LORD with contempt.

## Psalms 10:4

In his pride, the wicked man does not seek God. 📖

There is no God in any of his plans.

## Psalms 10:5

He is always secure in his ways. 📖

Your high judgments are far from him.

He sneers at all his enemies.

## Psalms 10:6

He says to himself, “Nothing will move me. 📖

From age to age I am free from trouble.”

## Psalms 10:7

His mouth is full of curses, lies, and violence. 📖

Trouble and harmful intent are under his tongue.

## Psalms 10:8

He waits near villages. 📖

He hides and kills innocent people.

His eyes secretly watch for helpless people.

## Psalms 10:9

He waits like a lion in thick bushes. 📖

He hides to seize people treated cruelly.

He catches the lowly in his net.

## Psalms 10:10

They are crushed and beaten down. 📖

Helpless people fall victim to his strength.

## Psalms 10:11

He says to himself, “God has forgotten. 📖

He hides His face and never sees.”

## Psalms 10:12

Rise up, LORD! 📖

Lift Your hand, God!

Do not forget helpless people.

## Psalms 10:13

Why has the wicked man rejected God? 📖

He says to himself, “You will never make me answer for what I do.”

## Psalms 10:14

But You have seen trouble and grief. 📖

You watch in order to repay it with Your hand.

The victim trusts himself to You.

You help children without fathers.

## Psalms 10:15

Break the arm of wicked people who do evil. 📖

Make the wicked man answer for his evil until none remains to be found.

## Psalms 10:16

The LORD is King forever and ever. 📖

The nations perish from His land.

## Psalms 10:17

LORD, You have heard humble people’s desire. 📖

You will strengthen their hearts.

You will listen closely,

## Psalms 10:18

to defend children without fathers and people treated cruelly. 📖

Then people of the earth will bring terror no more.
