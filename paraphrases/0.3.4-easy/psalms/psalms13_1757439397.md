# Psalms 13 — How long, LORD?

**About this chapter**

David asks how long his suffering will last.

He ends by expressing trust and thanks.

## Psalms 13:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

How long, LORD?

Will You forget me forever?

How long will You hide Your face from me?

## Psalms 13:2

How long must I struggle in my soul and have sorrow in my heart every day? 📖

How long will my enemy rule over me?

## Psalms 13:3

Look at me and answer, LORD my God. 📖

Give light to my eyes, so I do not sleep in death,

**Help with light to my eyes:** A picture of renewed life.

Sleep in death means dying.

## Psalms 13:4

so my enemy will not say, “I have defeated him.” 📖

Do not let my enemies rejoice when I fall.

## Psalms 13:5

But I have trusted in Your faithful love. 📖

My heart will rejoice in Your rescue.

## Psalms 13:6

I will sing to the LORD. 📖

He has been good to me.
