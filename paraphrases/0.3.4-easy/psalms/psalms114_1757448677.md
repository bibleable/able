# Psalms 114 — Creation trembles before Jacob’s God

**About this chapter**

The song recalls Israel leaving Egypt and pictures creation responding to God.

## Psalms 114:1

Israel left Egypt. 📖

Jacob’s house left people speaking a foreign language.

## Psalms 114:2

Judah became God’s sanctuary. 📖

Israel became the land He ruled.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place where God dwells.

## Psalms 114:3

The sea saw and fled. 📖

The Jordan turned back.

## Psalms 114:4

Mountains skipped like rams. 📖

Hills skipped like lambs.

## Psalms 114:5

Sea, why did you flee? 📖

Jordan, why did you turn back?

## Psalms 114:6

Mountains, why did you skip like rams? 📖

Hills, why did you skip like lambs?

## Psalms 114:7

Earth, tremble before the Lord, before Jacob’s God, 📖

## Psalms 114:8

who turned rock into a pool and flint into a fountain of water! 📖

**Help with flint:** A hard kind of stone.
