# Psalms 128 — Blessing for the person who fears the LORD

**About this chapter**

This song speaks of home, work, and blessing, and prays for Jerusalem and Israel.

## Psalms 128:1

A song of ascents. 📖

Blessed is everyone who fears the LORD and follows His ways!

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

Fearing the LORD means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Psalms 128:2

When you eat the fruit of your work, blessing and plenty will be yours. 📖

## Psalms 128:3

Your wife will be like a fruitful vine growing within your house. 📖

Your sons around your table will be like olive shoots.

**Help with vine and olive shoots:** Pictures of family fruitfulness and growth.

## Psalms 128:4

This is how the man who fears the LORD will be blessed. 📖

## Psalms 128:5

May the LORD bless you from Zion. 📖

May you see Jerusalem’s prosperity all your life,

## Psalms 128:6

and see your children’s children. 📖

May peace be on Israel!
