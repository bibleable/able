# Psalms 37 — Wait for the LORD and do good

**About this chapter**

David contrasts wicked people’s temporary success with the future of people who trust the LORD.

## Psalms 37:1

Of David. 📖

Do not become upset over people who do evil.

Do not envy people who do wrong.

## Psalms 37:2

They quickly dry up like grass. 📖

They wilt like young plants.

## Psalms 37:3

Trust in the LORD and do good. 📖

Live in the land and grow in faithfulness.

## Psalms 37:4

Delight in the LORD. 📖

He will give you your heart’s desires.

## Psalms 37:5

Put your way in the LORD’s hands. 📖

Trust Him, and He will act.

## Psalms 37:6

He will bring your righteousness out like dawn. 📖

He will bring your justice out like the midday sun.

**Help with righteousness and justice:** Being right and being treated fairly.

Light pictures these things becoming visible.

## Psalms 37:7

Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for Him. 📖

Do not become upset when people succeed in their ways and carry out wicked plans.

## Psalms 37:8

Hold back from anger. 📖

Give up fury.

Do not become upset, because it only brings harm.

## Psalms 37:9

People who do evil will be cut off. 📖

But people who hope in the LORD will inherit the land.

## Psalms 37:10

In a little while, wicked people will be gone. 📖

Even if you look for them, you will not find them.

## Psalms 37:11

But meek people will inherit the land. 📖

They will enjoy great plenty.

**Help with meek:** Humble and gentle.

## Psalms 37:12

Wicked people plan against righteous people. 📖

They grind their teeth at them.

## Psalms 37:13

But the Lord laughs at them. 📖

He sees that their day is coming.

**Help with their day:** Their time for judgment.

## Psalms 37:14

Wicked people draw swords and bend bows to bring down poor and needy people. 📖

They want to kill people whose ways are upright.

**Help with upright:** Honest and right.

## Psalms 37:15

But their swords will pierce their own hearts. 📖

Their bows will be broken.

## Psalms 37:16

The little that a righteous person has is better than many wicked people’s great wealth. 📖

## Psalms 37:17

Wicked people’s arms will be broken. 📖

But the LORD supports righteous people.

## Psalms 37:18

The LORD knows the days of blameless people. 📖

Their inheritance will last forever.

## Psalms 37:19

In times of evil they will not be ashamed. 📖

In days of famine they will have enough.

**Help with famine:** A time when there is very little food.

## Psalms 37:20

But wicked people and the LORD’s enemies will perish like the fields’ beauty. 📖

They will disappear and fade like smoke.

## Psalms 37:21

Wicked people borrow and do not repay. 📖

But righteous people are kind and give freely.

## Psalms 37:22

People He blesses will surely inherit the land. 📖

But cursed people will be destroyed.

## Psalms 37:23

The LORD directs a person’s steps. 📖

He takes delight in that person’s journey.

## Psalms 37:24

Even if he falls, he will not be overwhelmed. 📖

The LORD holds his hand.

## Psalms 37:25

I was young, and now I am old. 📖

Yet I have never seen righteous people abandoned or their children begging for bread.

## Psalms 37:26

They are always generous and ready to lend. 📖

Their children are a blessing.

## Psalms 37:27

Turn from evil and do good. 📖

Then you will live forever.

## Psalms 37:28

The LORD loves justice. 📖

He will not abandon His saints.

They are protected forever.

But wicked people’s children will be cut off.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 37:29

Righteous people will inherit the land. 📖

They will live in it forever.

## Psalms 37:30

A righteous person’s mouth speaks wisdom. 📖

His tongue speaks justice.

## Psalms 37:31

His God’s law is in his heart. 📖

His steps do not slip.

## Psalms 37:32

Wicked people may wait secretly for righteous people and try to kill them. 📖

## Psalms 37:33

But the LORD will not leave righteous people in their power. 📖

He will not let them be condemned at judgment.

## Psalms 37:34

Wait for the LORD and keep His way. 📖

He will raise you up to inherit the land.

When wicked people are cut off, you will see it.

## Psalms 37:35

I have seen a wicked, cruel man growing strong like a native tree with deep roots. 📖

## Psalms 37:36

But he passed away and was gone. 📖

I searched for him, but he could not be found.

## Psalms 37:37

Consider blameless people and watch upright people. 📖

A man of peace has generations to come after him.

## Psalms 37:38

But all who do wrong will be destroyed. 📖

Wicked people’s future will be cut off.

## Psalms 37:39

Righteous people’s rescue comes from the LORD. 📖

He is their strong place of safety in trouble.

## Psalms 37:40

The LORD helps and rescues them. 📖

He saves them from wicked people because they take refuge in Him.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.
