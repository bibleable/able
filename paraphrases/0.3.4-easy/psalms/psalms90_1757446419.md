# Psalms 90 — Teach us to number our days

**About this chapter**

Moses prays about God’s eternity and human life’s shortness.

He asks for compassion, joy, and lasting work.

## Psalms 90:1

A prayer of Moses, the man of God. 📖

Lord, You have been our home through all generations.

## Psalms 90:2

Before mountains were born, before You brought forth the earth and world, You were God. 📖

From everlasting to everlasting, You are God.

**Help with everlasting:** Without beginning or end.

## Psalms 90:3

You return human beings to dust. 📖

You say, “Return, children of mortals.”

**Help with mortals:** Human beings who die.

## Psalms 90:4

In Your sight, a thousand years are like a day that passes. 📖

They are like one watch of the night.

**Help with watch:** A period into which the night was divided.

## Psalms 90:5

You sweep them away in their sleep. 📖

They are like new morning grass,

## Psalms 90:6

which grows fresh in the morning but fades and dries by evening. 📖

## Psalms 90:7

Your anger consumes us. 📖

Your wrath terrifies us.

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Psalms 90:8

You have put our sins before You. 📖

Our secret sins are in the light of Your presence.

## Psalms 90:9

All our days fade under Your fury. 📖

We finish our years with a sigh.

## Psalms 90:10

Our days last seventy years, or eighty if we are strong. 📖

Yet their pride is only hard work and sorrow.

They quickly pass, and we fly away.

## Psalms 90:11

Who knows Your anger’s power? 📖

Your wrath matches the fear You deserve.

## Psalms 90:12

Teach us to count our days so we may bring a wise heart. 📖

## Psalms 90:13

Return, LORD! 📖

How long will it be?

Have compassion on Your servants.

**Help with compassion:** Deep care for people who suffer.

## Psalms 90:14

Satisfy us in the morning with Your faithful love. 📖

Then we may sing for joy and be glad all our days.

## Psalms 90:15

Make us glad for as many days as You made us suffer. 📖

Give us joy for as many years as we saw evil.

## Psalms 90:16

May Your servants see Your work. 📖

May their children see Your splendor.

**Help with splendor:** Great glory.

## Psalms 90:17

May the favor of the Lord our God rest on us. 📖

Make the work of our hands last.

Yes, make the work of our hands last!
