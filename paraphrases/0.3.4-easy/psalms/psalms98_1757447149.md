# Psalms 98 — All the earth, sing for joy

**About this chapter**

The song calls people and creation to celebrate God’s rescue and just judgment.

## Psalms 98:1

A psalm. 📖

Sing a new song to the LORD because He has done wonders.

His right hand and holy arm have won victory for Him.

## Psalms 98:2

The LORD has announced His rescue. 📖

He has shown His righteousness to the nations.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Psalms 98:3

He remembered His love and faithfulness to Israel’s house. 📖

All the earth’s ends have seen our God’s rescue.

**Help with Israel’s house:** Israel’s people.

## Psalms 98:4

All the earth, make a joyful sound to the LORD. 📖

Break out in ringing cries and songs of praise!

## Psalms 98:5

Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre. 📖

Sing tunefully with the harp.

**Help with lyre and harp:** Stringed musical instruments.

## Psalms 98:6

With trumpets and the ram’s horn sounding, shout for joy before the LORD, the King. 📖

**Help with ram’s horn:** An animal horn used as a trumpet.

## Psalms 98:7

Let the sea and everything filling it sound out. 📖

Let the world and everyone living in it sound out.

## Psalms 98:8

Let the rivers clap their hands. 📖

Let the mountains sing together for joy,

## Psalms 98:9

before the LORD, because He comes to judge the earth. 📖

He will judge the world rightly and the peoples fairly.
