# Psalms 64 — A prayer against secret attacks

**About this chapter**

David describes destructive speech and secret plans.

He speaks of God bringing judgment.

## Psalms 64:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

God, hear my complaint.

Protect my life from fear of the enemy.

## Psalms 64:2

Hide me from wicked people’s plans. 📖

Hide me from the crowd of people who do evil,

## Psalms 64:3

who sharpen their tongues like swords. 📖

They aim their bitter words like arrows,

## Psalms 64:4

hiding to attack innocent people in secret. 📖

They shoot suddenly, without fear.

## Psalms 64:5

They hold firmly to their evil purpose. 📖

They discuss hiding their traps.

They say, “Who will see them?”

## Psalms 64:6

They plan injustice and say, “We have perfected a secret plan.” 📖

A person’s inner thoughts and heart are mysterious.

## Psalms 64:7

But God will shoot them with arrows. 📖

Suddenly they will be wounded.

## Psalms 64:8

They will stumble as their own tongues turn against them. 📖

Everyone who sees will shake their heads.

## Psalms 64:9

Then all people will fear and announce God’s work. 📖

They will think deeply about what He has done.

## Psalms 64:10

Let righteous people rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in Him. 📖

Let everyone with an upright heart rejoice.

**Help with righteous and upright:** Right before God and honest in heart.

Refuge means safety and protection.
