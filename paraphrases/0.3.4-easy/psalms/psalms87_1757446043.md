# Psalms 87 — God’s city and the peoples

**About this chapter**

The song speaks of Zion and the peoples God recognizes as belonging there.

## Psalms 87:1

A psalm of the sons of Korah. 📖

A song.

He founded His city on the holy mountains.

## Psalms 87:2

The LORD loves Zion’s gates more than all Jacob’s homes. 📖

**Help with Zion and Jacob:** Zion is associated with Jerusalem.

Jacob is also used as a name for Israel’s people.

## Psalms 87:3

City of God, people say glorious things about you. 📖

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 87:4

“I will name Rahab and Babylon among those who know Me. 📖

I will include Philistia, Tyre, and Cush.

I will say, ‘This one was born in Zion.’”

**Help with Rahab:** A poetic name for Egypt here.

The quoted words describe God recognizing peoples.

## Psalms 87:5

People will say about Zion, “This one and that one were born there. 📖

The Most High Himself will establish it.”

## Psalms 87:6

The LORD will write in the record of the peoples, “This one was born in Zion.” 📖

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 87:7

Singers and pipe players will announce, “All my springs of joy are in You.” 📖

**Help with pipes and springs:** Pipes are instruments played by blowing.

Springs picture the sources of joy.
