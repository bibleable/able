# Psalms 101 — David promises to rule with integrity

**About this chapter**

David sings to the LORD and declares how he will live and govern.

## Psalms 101:1

A psalm of David. 📖

I will sing of Your faithful love and justice.

LORD, I will sing praises to You.

## Psalms 101:2

I will think carefully about the blameless way. 📖

When will You come to me?

I will live in my house with an honest heart.

## Psalms 101:3

I will set nothing worthless before my eyes. 📖

I hate the work of people who turn away.

It will not cling to me.

## Psalms 101:4

A twisted heart will be far from me. 📖

I will have nothing to do with evil.

## Psalms 101:5

I will silence anyone who secretly spreads lies about his neighbor. 📖

I will not tolerate proud eyes and a proud heart.

## Psalms 101:6

I look with favor on faithful people in the land so they may live with me. 📖

Anyone who lives with integrity will serve me.

**Help with integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

## Psalms 101:7

No one who practices deceit will live in my house. 📖

No one who tells lies will stand in my presence.

## Psalms 101:8

Every morning I will remove all wicked people from the land. 📖

I will cut off everyone doing evil from the LORD’s city.
