# Psalms 2 — The LORD and His Anointed One

**About this chapter**

This song describes rulers resisting the LORD and His Anointed One.

It includes their words, the LORD’s words, and the appointed King’s words.

## Psalms 2:1

Why do nations rage? 📖

Why do peoples make plans that achieve nothing?

## Psalms 2:2

The earth’s kings take their stand. 📖

Rulers gather together against the LORD and His Anointed One.

**Help with Anointed One:** The ruler chosen and appointed by God.

Anointing involved putting oil on someone.

## Psalms 2:3

They say, “Let us break Their chains. 📖

Let us throw off Their ropes.”

**Help with chains and ropes:** The rulers picture the LORD’s authority as something holding them captive.

## Psalms 2:4

The One sitting on heaven’s throne laughs. 📖

The Lord mocks them.

## Psalms 2:5

Then He speaks against them in anger. 📖

His fierce anger terrifies them.

## Psalms 2:6

He says, “I have appointed My King on Zion, My holy mountain.” 📖

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem and the LORD’s holy mountain.

## Psalms 2:7

I will announce the LORD’s decree to Me. 📖

He said, “You are My Son.

Today I have become Your Father.

**Help with the speaker:** The King tells what the LORD said to Him.

A decree is an authoritative decision.

## Psalms 2:8

Ask Me, and I will give You the nations as Your inheritance. 📖

The ends of the earth will be Your possession.

## Psalms 2:9

You will break them with an iron scepter. 📖

You will smash them like pottery.”

**Help with scepter:** A ruler’s staff that shows royal authority.

## Psalms 2:10

So be wise, kings. 📖

Accept this warning, judges of the earth.

## Psalms 2:11

Serve the LORD with fear. 📖

Rejoice with trembling.

## Psalms 2:12

Kiss the Son so that He does not become angry. 📖

Otherwise you may perish in your rebellion when His anger suddenly burns.

Blessed are all who take refuge in Him.

**Help with kiss the Son:** Show loyalty and submission to Him.

Taking refuge means going to someone for safety.
