# Psalms 120 — A cry among people who hate peace

**About this chapter**

The singer asks for rescue from lies and speaks of living among people who want war.

## Psalms 120:1

A song of ascents. 📖

In my distress I cried to the LORD.

He answered me.

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 120:2

LORD, rescue my soul from lying lips and a deceitful tongue. 📖

## Psalms 120:3

Deceitful tongue, what will He do to you? 📖

What more will He add to you?

## Psalms 120:4

A warrior’s sharp arrows will come with burning coals from the broom tree! 📖

**Help with broom tree:** A desert plant whose wood could be burned.

## Psalms 120:5

How terrible for me to live in Meshech! 📖

I live among Kedar’s tents!

## Psalms 120:6

I have lived too long among people who hate peace. 📖

## Psalms 120:7

I am for peace. 📖

But when I speak, they want war.
