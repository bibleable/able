# Psalms 65 — Praise for forgiveness and a fruitful earth

**About this chapter**

David praises God for answering prayer, forgiving sin, and providing water and food.

## Psalms 65:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

A song.

God, praise waits for You in Zion.

We will keep our vows to You.

**Help with Zion and vows:** A name associated with Jerusalem, and serious promises.

## Psalms 65:2

You listen to prayer. 📖

All people will come to You.

## Psalms 65:3

When sins overpower me, You atone for our offenses. 📖

**Help with atone:** Deal with guilt so that sin is forgiven.

## Psalms 65:4

Blessed is the person You choose and bring near to live in Your courts! 📖

Your house’s goodness and Your temple’s holiness fill us.

**Help with courts:** Open areas belonging to the place of worship.

## Psalms 65:5

God who saves us, You answer us with awesome deeds of righteousness. 📖

You are the hope of the earth’s farthest ends and most distant seas.

**Help with awesome and righteousness:** Inspiring deep wonder and fear, and doing what is right.

## Psalms 65:6

You formed the mountains by Your power. 📖

You clothed Yourself with might.

## Psalms 65:7

You quieted the roaring seas, pounding waves, and noisy nations. 📖

## Psalms 65:8

People far away fear Your wonders. 📖

You make dawn and sunset shout for joy.

## Psalms 65:9

You care for the earth and water it. 📖

You make it very rich.

God’s streams are full of water.

You prepare our grain by providing for the earth.

## Psalms 65:10

You soak its furrows and flatten its ridges. 📖

You soften it with showers and bless its growth.

**Help with furrows and ridges:** Lines cut in soil for planting, and the raised soil between them.

## Psalms 65:11

You crown the year with Your generous gifts. 📖

Your paths overflow with plenty.

## Psalms 65:12

The wilderness pastures overflow. 📖

The hills wear robes of joy.

## Psalms 65:13

The pastures wear flocks like clothing. 📖

The valleys are covered with grain.

They shout in victory and sing.

**Help with clothing and singing:** The song pictures the land as richly clothed and joyful.
