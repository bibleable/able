# Psalms 97 — Rejoice in the LORD’s reign

**About this chapter**

This song describes the LORD’s power, judgment, and care for His people.

## Psalms 97:1

The LORD reigns! 📖

Let the earth rejoice.

Let distant shores be glad.

## Psalms 97:2

Clouds and darkness surround Him. 📖

Righteousness and justice support His throne.

**Help with righteousness and justice:** Doing what is right and judging fairly.

## Psalms 97:3

Fire goes before Him. 📖

It burns up enemies on every side.

## Psalms 97:4

His lightning lights the world. 📖

The earth sees and trembles.

## Psalms 97:5

Mountains melt like wax before the LORD. 📖

They melt before the Lord of all the earth.

## Psalms 97:6

The heavens announce His righteousness. 📖

All peoples see His glory.

## Psalms 97:7

Everyone worshiping images and boasting in idols is put to shame. 📖

All you gods, worship Him!

**Help with idols:** Images worshiped as gods.

## Psalms 97:8

Zion hears and rejoices. 📖

Judah’s towns rejoice because of Your judgments, LORD.

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 97:9

LORD, You are Most High over all the earth. 📖

You are raised far above all gods.

## Psalms 97:10

People who love the LORD, hate evil! 📖

He guards His saints’ souls.

He rescues them from wicked people’s hands.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 97:11

Light shines on righteous people. 📖

Gladness shines on people whose hearts are upright.

**Help with upright:** Honest and right.

## Psalms 97:12

Righteous people, rejoice in the LORD. 📖

Praise His holy name.
