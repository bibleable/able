# Psalms 47 — Praise God, King of all the earth

**About this chapter**

The song calls all peoples to praise God as King.

## Psalms 47:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of the sons of Korah.

All you peoples, clap your hands.

Shout to God with a voice of victory.

## Psalms 47:2

The LORD Most High is awesome! 📖

He is the great King over all the earth.

**Help with awesome:** Inspiring deep wonder and fear.

## Psalms 47:3

He brings nations down beneath us. 📖

He puts peoples under our feet.

## Psalms 47:4

He chooses our inheritance for us. 📖

It is the pride of Jacob, whom He loves.

Selah.

**Help with inheritance:** The share of land and blessing received.

Jacob is also used as a name for Israel’s people.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 47:5

God has gone up amid joyful shouts. 📖

The LORD has gone up with the sound of the horn.

## Psalms 47:6

Sing praises to God, sing praises! 📖

Sing praises to our King, sing praises!

## Psalms 47:7

God is King of all the earth. 📖

Sing Him a psalm of praise.

## Psalms 47:8

God rules over the nations. 📖

God sits on His holy throne.

## Psalms 47:9

The nations’ nobles have gathered as the people of Abraham’s God. 📖

The earth’s shields belong to God.

He is honored high above all.

**Help with nobles and shields:** High-ranking leaders.

Shields picture the earth’s protective powers and rulers.
