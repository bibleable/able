# Psalms 108 — Praise and a prayer for victory

**About this chapter**

David praises God and asks for help.

The song includes God’s words about lands and nations.

## Psalms 108:1

A song. 📖

A psalm of David.

God, my heart is steady.

I will sing and make music with my whole being.

## Psalms 108:2

Awake, harp and lyre! 📖

I will wake the dawn.

**Help with harp and lyre:** Stringed musical instruments.

## Psalms 108:3

LORD, I will praise You among the nations. 📖

I will sing Your praises among the peoples.

## Psalms 108:4

Your faithful love reaches beyond the heavens. 📖

Your faithfulness reaches the clouds.

## Psalms 108:5

God, be honored high above the heavens. 📖

May Your glory cover all the earth.

## Psalms 108:6

Answer and save us with Your right hand so Your loved ones may be rescued. 📖

## Psalms 108:7

God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! 📖

I will divide Shechem and share out the Valley of Succoth.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 108:8

Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine. 📖

Ephraim is My helmet.

Judah is My scepter.

**Help with helmet and scepter:** Pictures of protection and royal authority.

## Psalms 108:9

Moab is My washbasin. 📖

I throw My sandal on Edom.

I shout in victory over Philistia.”

**Help with basin and sandal:** Images expressing God’s power over these nations.

## Psalms 108:10

Who will bring me to the fortified city? 📖

Who will lead me to Edom?

**Help with fortified:** Protected by strong walls or defenses.

## Psalms 108:11

God, have You not rejected us? 📖

God, will You no longer march with our armies?

## Psalms 108:12

Help us against the enemy. 📖

Human help is worthless.

## Psalms 108:13

With God we will act bravely. 📖

He will trample our enemies.
