# Psalms 82 — God judges unjust rulers

**About this chapter**

Asaph’s song describes God judging in a divine gathering.

The identity of those called gods remains a matter for further editorial review.

## Psalms 82:1

A psalm of Asaph. 📖

God leads the divine gathering.

He judges among the gods.

## Psalms 82:2

He says, “How long will you judge unfairly and favor wicked people? 📖

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 82:3

Defend weak people and children without fathers. 📖

Uphold the rights of suffering people and those treated cruelly.

## Psalms 82:4

Rescue weak and needy people. 📖

Save them from wicked people’s hands.

## Psalms 82:5

They do not know or understand. 📖

They wander in darkness.

All the earth’s foundations shake.

## Psalms 82:6

I said, ‘You are gods. 📖

You are all sons of the Most High.’

## Psalms 82:7

But you will die like mortals. 📖

You will fall like rulers.”

**Help with mortals:** Human beings who die.

## Psalms 82:8

Rise up, God, and judge the earth. 📖

All the nations are Your inheritance.

**Help with inheritance:** What belongs to God.
