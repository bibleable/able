# Psalms 149 — Praise and the written judgment

**About this chapter**

The song calls God’s people to rejoice.

It also describes armed judgment against nations and rulers.

## Psalms 149:1

Hallelujah! 📖

Sing the LORD a new song.

Sing His praise in the gathering of godly people.

**Help with Hallelujah and godly:** Praise the LORD, and devoted to God.

## Psalms 149:2

Let Israel rejoice in their Maker. 📖

Let Zion’s children rejoice in their King.

## Psalms 149:3

Let them praise His name with dancing. 📖

Let them make music with tambourine and harp.

**Help with tambourine and harp:** An instrument shaken or struck, and a stringed instrument.

## Psalms 149:4

The LORD delights in His people. 📖

He decorates suffering people with rescue.

## Psalms 149:5

Let the saints rejoice in glory. 📖

Let them shout for joy on their beds.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 149:6

May high praises to God be in their mouths. 📖

May a sword with two sharp edges be in their hands,

## Psalms 149:7

bringing vengeance on nations and punishment on peoples, 📖

**Help with vengeance:** Punishment in return for a wrong.

## Psalms 149:8

binding kings with chains and nobles with iron shackles, 📖

**Help with nobles and shackles:** Leaders with high rank, and metal restraints.

## Psalms 149:9

carrying out the judgment written against them. 📖

This honor belongs to all His saints.

Hallelujah!
