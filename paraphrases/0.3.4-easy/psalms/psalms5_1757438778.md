# Psalms 5 — David asks for guidance and judgment

**About this chapter**

David prays to his King and God.

He asks for guidance, protection, and judgment against people who do evil.

## Psalms 5:1

For the choir leader, with flutes. 📖

A psalm of David.

LORD, listen to my words.

Consider my groaning.

**Help with flutes:** Musical instruments played by blowing.

## Psalms 5:2

Listen to my cry, my King and my God. 📖

I pray to You.

## Psalms 5:3

LORD, You hear my voice in the morning. 📖

At daybreak I put my request before You.

I wait with hope.

## Psalms 5:4

You are not a God who takes pleasure in wickedness. 📖

No evil can live with You.

## Psalms 5:5

Boastful people cannot stand in Your presence. 📖

You hate everyone who does evil.

## Psalms 5:6

You destroy people who tell lies. 📖

The LORD hates people who shed blood and deceive.

## Psalms 5:7

But I will enter Your house because Your faithful love is so great. 📖

In reverence I will bow toward Your holy temple.

**Help with reverence:** Deep respect for God.

## Psalms 5:8

LORD, lead me in Your righteousness because of my enemies. 📖

Make Your way straight before me.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right before God.

A straight way is a picture of a clear path to follow.

## Psalms 5:9

Not one word they say can be trusted. 📖

Destruction is inside them.

Their throats are open graves.

Their tongues tell lies.

**Help with open graves:** David pictures their speech as dangerous and bringing death.

## Psalms 5:10

God, declare them guilty. 📖

Let their own plans bring them down.

Drive them out for their many sins.

They have rebelled against You.

## Psalms 5:11

Let everyone who takes refuge in You rejoice. 📖

Let them always shout for joy.

Shelter them, so those who love Your name can rejoice in You.

**Help with refuge:** A place of safety.

## Psalms 5:12

LORD, You surely bless righteous people. 📖

You surround them with the shield of Your favor.

**Help with shield of favor:** A picture of protection through God’s kindness.
