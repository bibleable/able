# Psalms 39 — A prayer about the shortness of life

**About this chapter**

David speaks about silence, sorrow, and life’s shortness.

He asks the LORD for relief.

## Psalms 39:1

For the choir leader, for Jeduthun. 📖

A psalm of David.

I said, “I will watch my ways so my tongue does not sin.

I will muzzle my mouth while wicked people are present.”

**Help with muzzle:** A covering that keeps an animal’s mouth shut.

David pictures keeping himself from speaking.

## Psalms 39:2

I was silent and still. 📖

I did not even speak good words.

My sorrow grew.

## Psalms 39:3

My heart grew hot within me. 📖

As I thought deeply, the fire burned.

Then I spoke.

**Help with fire:** A picture of feelings growing strong inside David.

## Psalms 39:4

“LORD, show me my end and how many days I have. 📖

Let me know how quickly my life passes.

## Psalms 39:5

You have made my days only handbreadths. 📖

My lifetime is nothing before You.

Even at his best, each person is only a breath.

Selah.

**Help with handbreadth:** The width of a hand, a short measurement.

A breath pictures life’s shortness.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 39:6

Each person moves about like a phantom. 📖

He rushes about for nothing.

He piles up riches without knowing who will take them away.

**Help with phantom:** A picture of something that passes quickly and has little substance.

## Psalms 39:7

Now, Lord, what am I waiting for? 📖

My hope is in You.

## Psalms 39:8

Rescue me from all my offenses. 📖

Do not make me an object of fools’ scorn.

## Psalms 39:9

I have become silent. 📖

I do not open my mouth because of what You have done.

## Psalms 39:10

Take Your whip away from me. 📖

I am dying under the force of Your hand.

**Help with whip:** David pictures his suffering as painful punishment.

## Psalms 39:11

You discipline and correct a person for his sin. 📖

Like a moth, You eat away what he holds dear.

Each person is only a vapor.

Selah.

**Help with vapor:** Mist that quickly disappears.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 39:12

LORD, hear my prayer and cry for help. 📖

Do not refuse to hear my weeping.

I am a foreigner living with You, a stranger like all my fathers.

## Psalms 39:13

Look away from me so I can be cheerful again. 📖

Let this happen before I go away and am no more.”
