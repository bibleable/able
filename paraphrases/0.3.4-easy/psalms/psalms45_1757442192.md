# Psalms 45 — A royal love song

**About this chapter**

This song addresses a king and a royal bride.

It uses words of royal praise and addresses God in verse 6.

## Psalms 45:1

For the choir leader, to the tune of “The Lilies.” 📖

A Maskil of the sons of Korah.

A love song.

A noble subject stirs my heart as I recite my verses to the king.

My tongue is a skilled writer’s pen.

**Help with Maskil:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

The pen pictures skillful speech.

## Psalms 45:2

You are the most handsome of men. 📖

Grace has anointed your lips.

So God has blessed you forever.

**Help with grace on lips:** A picture of gracious and pleasing speech.

## Psalms 45:3

Mighty warrior, strap your sword at your side. 📖

Appear in your majesty and splendor.

**Help with majesty and splendor:** Great royal power and glory.

## Psalms 45:4

In your splendor, ride out in victory for truth, humility, and justice. 📖

May your right hand show your awesome deeds.

## Psalms 45:5

Your arrows pierce the hearts of the king’s enemies. 📖

Nations fall beneath your feet.

## Psalms 45:6

God, Your throne lasts forever and ever. 📖

Justice is the scepter of Your kingdom.

**Help with scepter:** A ruler’s staff showing royal authority.

The wording addresses God, while the surrounding song addresses the king.

The relationship between these addresses needs further editorial consideration.

## Psalms 45:7

You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness. 📖

So God, your God, has anointed you above your companions with the oil of joy.

**Help with righteousness and anointed:** Doing what is right, and being appointed through a ceremony using oil.

## Psalms 45:8

All your clothes smell of myrrh, aloes, and cassia. 📖

Harps from ivory palaces make you glad.

**Help with myrrh, aloes, and cassia:** Materials used for perfume.

Ivory comes from animal tusks.

## Psalms 45:9

Kings’ daughters are among your honored women. 📖

The queen stands at your right hand, wearing gold from Ophir.

**Help with Ophir:** A place known for gold.

## Psalms 45:10

Listen, daughter. 📖

Think carefully and pay attention.

Forget your people and your father’s house,

## Psalms 45:11

and the king will desire your beauty. 📖

Bow to him, because he is your lord.

## Psalms 45:12

The Daughter of Tyre will come with a gift. 📖

Wealthy men will seek your favor.

**Help with Daughter of Tyre:** A poetic way of referring to Tyre and its people.

## Psalms 45:13

The princess is glorious in her room. 📖

Her gown is embroidered with gold.

**Help with embroidered:** Decorated with stitched designs.

## Psalms 45:14

Wearing colorful clothes, she is led to the king. 📖

Her virgin companions are brought before you.

**Help with virgin:** Someone who has not had sex.

## Psalms 45:15

They are led in with joy and gladness. 📖

They enter the king’s palace.

## Psalms 45:16

Your sons will take your fathers’ place. 📖

You will make them princes throughout the land.

## Psalms 45:17

I will keep your name remembered through all generations. 📖

So the nations will praise you forever and ever.
