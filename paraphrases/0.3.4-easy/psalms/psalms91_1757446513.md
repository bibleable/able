# Psalms 91 — Shelter with the Most High

**About this chapter**

This song speaks of protection for someone who takes refuge in God.

God speaks in verses 14–16.

The protective promises retain their source setting and conditions.

## Psalms 91:1

The person living in the Most High’s shelter will stay in the Almighty’s shadow. 📖

## Psalms 91:2

I will say to the LORD, “You are my refuge and fortress. 📖

You are my God, whom I trust.”

**Help with refuge and fortress:** Safety and a strong place of protection.

## Psalms 91:3

He will surely rescue you from the bird-catcher’s trap and the deadly plague. 📖

**Help with plague:** A deadly outbreak of disease.

## Psalms 91:4

He will cover you with His feathers. 📖

You will find safety under His wings.

His faithfulness is a shield and protective wall.

**Help with wings and shield:** Pictures of God’s care and protection.

## Psalms 91:5

You will not fear the night’s terror or an arrow flying by day. 📖

## Psalms 91:6

You will not fear deadly disease moving in darkness or disaster destroying at noon. 📖

## Psalms 91:7

A thousand may fall at your side and ten thousand at your right hand. 📖

But no harm will come near you.

## Psalms 91:8

You will only see it with your eyes. 📖

You will witness wicked people’s punishment.

## Psalms 91:9

You have made the LORD your home, the Most High who is my refuge. 📖

## Psalms 91:10

Because of this, no evil will fall on you. 📖

No plague will come near your tent.

## Psalms 91:11

He will command His angels to guard you in all your ways. 📖

## Psalms 91:12

They will lift you in their hands so your foot does not strike a stone. 📖

## Psalms 91:13

You will step on the lion and cobra. 📖

You will trample the young lion and serpent.

**Help with cobra and serpent:** A poisonous snake, and a snake.

## Psalms 91:14

God says, “Because he loves Me, I will rescue him. 📖

Because he knows My name, I will protect him.

## Psalms 91:15

When he calls Me, I will answer. 📖

I will be with him in trouble.

I will rescue and honor him.

## Psalms 91:16

I will satisfy him with long life. 📖

I will show him My rescue.”
