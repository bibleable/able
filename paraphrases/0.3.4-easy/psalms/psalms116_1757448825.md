# Psalms 116 — Thanks for rescue from death

**About this chapter**

The singer thanks the LORD for hearing his prayer.

He promises public thanks and offerings.

## Psalms 116:1

I love the LORD because He heard my voice, my appeal for mercy. 📖

## Psalms 116:2

He listened closely to me. 📖

So I will call Him as long as I live.

## Psalms 116:3

Death’s ropes tangled around me. 📖

Sheol’s anguish overwhelmed me.

Trouble and sorrow faced me.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Psalms 116:4

Then I called the LORD’s name: “LORD, rescue my soul!” 📖

## Psalms 116:5

The LORD is gracious and righteous. 📖

Our God is full of compassion.

**Help with these words:** Gracious means freely kind.

Righteous means right and just.

Compassion is deep care for someone who suffers.

## Psalms 116:6

The LORD protects simplehearted people. 📖

I was helpless, and He saved me.

**Help with simplehearted:** People who trust without having great knowledge or power.

## Psalms 116:7

My soul, return to rest. 📖

The LORD has been good to you.

## Psalms 116:8

You rescued my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from stumbling. 📖

## Psalms 116:9

I will walk before the LORD in the land of the living. 📖

## Psalms 116:10

I believed, so I said, “I suffer greatly.” 📖

## Psalms 116:11

In my alarm I said, “Everyone is a liar!” 📖

## Psalms 116:12

How can I repay the LORD for all His goodness to me? 📖

## Psalms 116:13

I will lift the cup of rescue. 📖

I will call the LORD’s name.

**Help with cup of rescue:** A cup raised in thanks for God’s saving work.

## Psalms 116:14

I will keep my vows to the LORD before all His people. 📖

**Help with vows:** Serious promises.

## Psalms 116:15

His saints’ death is precious in the LORD’s sight. 📖

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

The verse’s sense of precious or costly remains for further editorial review.

## Psalms 116:16

LORD, I truly am Your servant. 📖

I am Your servant, Your female servant’s son.

You broke my bonds.

## Psalms 116:17

I will give You a sacrifice of thanks. 📖

I will call the LORD’s name.

**Help with sacrifice:** An offering to God.

## Psalms 116:18

I will keep my vows to the LORD before all His people, 📖

## Psalms 116:19

in the courts of the LORD’s house, in your midst, Jerusalem. 📖

Hallelujah!

**Help with courts:** Open areas belonging to the place of worship.

Hallelujah means praise the LORD.
