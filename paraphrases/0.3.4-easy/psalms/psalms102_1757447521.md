# Psalms 102 — A suffering person prays to the eternal LORD

**About this chapter**

The singer describes deep suffering and asks for help.

He contrasts his short life with the LORD’s lasting reign.

## Psalms 102:1

A suffering person’s prayer, when he grows weak and pours out his lament before the LORD. 📖

LORD, hear my prayer.

Let my cry for help come before You.

**Help with lament:** A cry of grief.

## Psalms 102:2

Do not hide Your face on my day of distress. 📖

Listen closely.

Answer quickly when I call.

## Psalms 102:3

My days disappear like smoke. 📖

My bones burn like glowing coals.

## Psalms 102:4

My heart suffers and dries up like grass. 📖

I even forget to eat my bread.

## Psalms 102:5

My loud groaning leaves my skin hanging on my bones. 📖

## Psalms 102:6

I am like a desert owl. 📖

I am like an owl among ruins.

## Psalms 102:7

I lie awake. 📖

I am like a lone bird on a roof.

## Psalms 102:8

My enemies mock me all day. 📖

They ridicule and curse me.

## Psalms 102:9

I have eaten ashes like bread. 📖

I have mixed my drink with tears,

## Psalms 102:10

because of Your outrage and wrath. 📖

You picked me up and threw me aside.

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Psalms 102:11

My days are like shadows growing long. 📖

I dry up like grass.

## Psalms 102:12

But LORD, You sit enthroned forever. 📖

Your fame lasts through all generations.

## Psalms 102:13

You will rise and have compassion on Zion. 📖

It is time to favor it.

The appointed time has come.

**Help with compassion and Zion:** Deep care for suffering people, and a name associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 102:14

Your servants delight in its stones. 📖

They pity its dust.

## Psalms 102:15

Then nations will fear the LORD’s name. 📖

All the earth’s kings will fear Your glory.

## Psalms 102:16

The LORD will rebuild Zion. 📖

He has appeared in His glory.

## Psalms 102:17

He will turn toward the prayer of people with nothing. 📖

He will not despise their prayer.

## Psalms 102:18

Let this be written for a future generation. 📖

Then a people not yet created may praise the LORD.

## Psalms 102:19

He looked down from His sanctuary’s heights. 📖

The LORD looked from heaven to earth,

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 102:20

hearing a prisoner’s groans and freeing people sentenced to death, 📖

## Psalms 102:21

so they may announce the LORD’s name in Zion and praise Him in Jerusalem, 📖

## Psalms 102:22

when peoples and kingdoms gather to serve the LORD. 📖

## Psalms 102:23

He broke my strength on the way. 📖

He cut my days short.

## Psalms 102:24

I say, “My God, do not take me in the middle of my days! 📖

Your years continue through all generations.

## Psalms 102:25

In the beginning You laid the earth’s foundations. 📖

The heavens are Your hands’ work.

## Psalms 102:26

They will perish, but You remain. 📖

They will all wear out like clothes.

You will change them like clothing, and they will pass away.

## Psalms 102:27

But You stay the same. 📖

Your years will never end.

## Psalms 102:28

Your servants’ children will live securely. 📖

Their descendants will be established before You.”

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.
