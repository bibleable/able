# Psalms 84 — Longing for the LORD’s house

**About this chapter**

The song describes love for God’s dwelling and the journey to worship Him.

## Psalms 84:1

For the choir leader, according to Gittith. 📖

A psalm of the sons of Korah.

LORD of Hosts, how lovely is Your home!

**Help with Gittith:** A musical term whose exact meaning is uncertain.

LORD of Hosts means the LORD who commands heavenly armies.

## Psalms 84:2

My soul longs for the LORD’s courts, even growing faint. 📖

My heart and flesh cry to the living God.

**Help with courts:** Open areas belonging to the place of worship.

## Psalms 84:3

Even the sparrow found a home. 📖

The swallow found a nest for her young near Your altars.

LORD of Hosts, You are my King and my God.

**Help with sparrow and swallow:** Small birds.

Altars are places for offerings.

## Psalms 84:4

Blessed are people who live in Your house! 📖

They always praise You.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 84:5

Blessed are people whose strength is in You. 📖

Their hearts are set on pilgrimage.

**Help with pilgrimage:** A journey to a holy place.

## Psalms 84:6

As they pass through the Valley of Baca, they make it a place of springs. 📖

Autumn rain covers it with pools too.

## Psalms 84:7

They grow stronger and stronger. 📖

Each one appears before God in Zion.

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 84:8

LORD God of Hosts, hear my prayer. 📖

God of Jacob, listen.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 84:9

God, take notice of our shield. 📖

Look with favor on Your anointed’s face.

**Help with shield and anointed:** Pictures associated with the appointed king.

Anointing involved a ceremony using oil.

## Psalms 84:10

One day in Your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere. 📖

I would rather guard my God’s doorway than live in wicked people’s tents.

## Psalms 84:11

The LORD God is a sun and shield. 📖

The LORD gives grace and glory.

He withholds no good thing from people who live with integrity.

**Help with sun and shield:** Pictures of light, life, and protection.

Grace is freely given kindness.

Integrity means being honest and holding to what is right.

## Psalms 84:12

LORD of Hosts, blessed is the person who trusts You! 📖
