# Psalms 18 — David praises the LORD for rescue

**About this chapter**

David sings about rescue from Saul and all his enemies.

The song uses storm, battle, and protection pictures.

## Psalms 18:1

For the choir leader. 📖

Of David, the LORD’s servant.

David sang this on the day the LORD rescued him from all his enemies and from Saul.

He said, “I love You, LORD, my strength.”

## Psalms 18:2

The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my rescuer. 📖

My God is my rock, where I take refuge.

He is my shield, my stronghold, and the horn of my salvation.

**Help with these pictures:** Rock, fortress, shield, and stronghold describe protection.

A horn pictures strength.

Salvation means rescue.

## Psalms 18:3

I will call on the LORD, who deserves praise. 📖

So I will be saved from my enemies.

## Psalms 18:4

Death’s ropes surrounded me. 📖

Floods of chaos overwhelmed me.

## Psalms 18:5

Sheol’s ropes tangled around me. 📖

Death’s traps faced me.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

The ropes and traps picture deadly danger.

## Psalms 18:6

In my distress I called to the LORD. 📖

I cried to my God for help.

From His temple He heard my voice.

My cry for help reached His ears.

## Psalms 18:7

Then the earth shook and trembled. 📖

The mountains’ foundations shook because His anger burned.

## Psalms 18:8

Smoke rose from His nostrils. 📖

Fire that destroys came from His mouth.

Glowing coals blazed out.

## Psalms 18:9

He opened the heavens and came down. 📖

Dark clouds were beneath His feet.

## Psalms 18:10

He rode a cherub and flew. 📖

He soared on the wind’s wings.

**Help with cherub:** A heavenly being.

These verses picture the LORD coming in great power.

## Psalms 18:11

He made darkness His hiding place. 📖

Storm clouds formed a covering around Him.

## Psalms 18:12

His clouds advanced from the brightness of His presence. 📖

Hailstones and burning coals came.

## Psalms 18:13

The LORD thundered from heaven. 📖

The Most High’s voice sounded.

Hailstones and burning coals came.

## Psalms 18:14

He shot His arrows and scattered His enemies. 📖

He threw lightning and made them flee.

## Psalms 18:15

The sea’s channels appeared. 📖

The world’s foundations were exposed at Your rebuke, LORD.

This happened at the blast of breath from Your nostrils.

**Help with rebuke:** A strong expression of anger and correction.

## Psalms 18:16

He reached down from above and took hold of me. 📖

He pulled me out of deep waters.

## Psalms 18:17

He rescued me from my powerful enemy. 📖

He rescued me from enemies too strong for me.

## Psalms 18:18

They confronted me on my day of disaster. 📖

But the LORD supported me.

## Psalms 18:19

He brought me into an open place. 📖

He rescued me because He delighted in me.

## Psalms 18:20

The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness. 📖

He repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands.

**Help with righteousness and clean hands:** Living rightly and not doing evil.

## Psalms 18:21

I have kept the LORD’s ways. 📖

I have not wickedly turned away from my God.

## Psalms 18:22

All His rules are before me. 📖

I have not ignored His laws.

## Psalms 18:23

I have been blameless before Him. 📖

I have kept myself from evil.

## Psalms 18:24

So the LORD repaid me according to my righteousness. 📖

He repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands in His sight.

## Psalms 18:25

You show Yourself faithful to faithful people. 📖

You show Yourself blameless to blameless people.

## Psalms 18:26

You show Yourself pure to pure people. 📖

But You show Yourself shrewd to crooked people.

**Help with shrewd and crooked:** God deals wisely with people who twist what is right.

## Psalms 18:27

You save people who suffer. 📖

But You humble people with proud eyes.

## Psalms 18:28

LORD, You light my lamp. 📖

My God lights my darkness.

## Psalms 18:29

With You, I can charge an army. 📖

With my God, I can climb over a wall.

## Psalms 18:30

God’s way is perfect. 📖

The LORD’s word has no flaw.

He is a shield for everyone who takes refuge in Him.

## Psalms 18:31

Who is God except the LORD? 📖

Who is the Rock except our God?

## Psalms 18:32

God equips me with strength. 📖

He makes my way clear.

## Psalms 18:33

He makes my feet like a deer’s feet. 📖

He places me on the heights.

## Psalms 18:34

He trains my hands for battle. 📖

My arms can bend a bronze bow.

## Psalms 18:35

You have given me Your shield of rescue. 📖

Your right hand supports me.

Your gentleness raises me up.

## Psalms 18:36

You make the path beneath me wide. 📖

Then my ankles do not give way.

## Psalms 18:37

I chased my enemies and caught them. 📖

I did not turn back until they were destroyed.

## Psalms 18:38

I crushed them so they could not rise. 📖

They fell under my feet.

## Psalms 18:39

You equipped me with strength for battle. 📖

You brought my enemies down beneath me.

## Psalms 18:40

You made my enemies retreat before me. 📖

I destroyed those who hated me.

## Psalms 18:41

They cried for help, but no one saved them. 📖

They cried to the LORD, but He did not answer.

## Psalms 18:42

I ground them like dust before the wind. 📖

I trampled them like mud in the streets.

## Psalms 18:43

You rescued me from the people’s conflicts. 📖

You made me head over nations.

People I had not known will serve me.

## Psalms 18:44

When they hear me, they obey me. 📖

Foreigners crouch in fear before me.

## Psalms 18:45

Foreigners lose courage. 📖

They come trembling out of their strongholds.

## Psalms 18:46

The LORD lives! 📖

Blessed be my Rock!

Let the God of my rescue be raised high!

## Psalms 18:47

He is the God who takes revenge for me. 📖

He brings nations down beneath me.

## Psalms 18:48

He rescues me from my enemies. 📖

You raise me above my enemies.

You rescue me from violent men.

## Psalms 18:49

So I will praise You among the nations, LORD. 📖

I will sing praise to Your name.

## Psalms 18:50

He brings great rescue to His king. 📖

He shows faithful love to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever.

**Help with anointed:** A person appointed through a ceremony using oil.

Descendants are children and the generations born after them.
