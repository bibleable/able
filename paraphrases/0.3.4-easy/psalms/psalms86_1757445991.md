# Psalms 86 — Teach me Your way, LORD

**About this chapter**

David asks for mercy, teaching, and rescue.

He praises God’s greatness and faithful love.

## Psalms 86:1

A prayer of David. 📖

LORD, listen closely and answer me.

I am poor and needy.

## Psalms 86:2

Keep my soul alive, because I am godly. 📖

You are my God.

Save Your servant who trusts You.

**Help with godly:** Devoted to God.

## Psalms 86:3

Lord, have mercy on me. 📖

I call to You all day.

## Psalms 86:4

Bring joy to Your servant. 📖

Lord, I lift my soul to You.

## Psalms 86:5

Lord, You are kind and forgiving. 📖

You have rich faithful love for everyone who calls You.

## Psalms 86:6

LORD, hear my prayer. 📖

Listen to my appeal for mercy.

## Psalms 86:7

I call to You on my day of distress because You answer me. 📖

## Psalms 86:8

Lord, no one among the gods is like You. 📖

No works are like Yours.

## Psalms 86:9

Lord, all nations You made will come and bow before You. 📖

They will glorify Your name.

## Psalms 86:10

You are great and do wonders. 📖

You alone are God.

## Psalms 86:11

LORD, teach me Your way so I may walk in Your truth. 📖

Give me an undivided heart so I may fear Your name.

**Help with undivided heart:** A heart wholly devoted to God.

Fearing His name means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Psalms 86:12

Lord my God, I will praise You with all my heart. 📖

I will glorify Your name forever.

## Psalms 86:13

Your faithful love for me is great. 📖

You rescued me from Sheol’s depths.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Psalms 86:14

God, proud people rise against me. 📖

A group of cruel men seeks my life.

They have no regard for You.

## Psalms 86:15

But Lord, You are compassionate and gracious. 📖

You are slow to anger and rich in faithful love and faithfulness.

**Help with compassionate and gracious:** Deeply caring, and freely kind.

## Psalms 86:16

Turn to me and have mercy. 📖

Give Your strength to Your servant.

Save Your female servant’s son.

## Psalms 86:17

Show me a sign of Your goodness so my enemies see and feel ashamed. 📖

LORD, You have helped and comforted me.
