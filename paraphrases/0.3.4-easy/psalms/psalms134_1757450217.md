# Psalms 134 — Bless the LORD through the night

**About this chapter**

The song calls night servants to bless the LORD and speaks a blessing from Zion.

## Psalms 134:1

A song of ascents. 📖

Come, bless the LORD, all His servants serving at night in His house!

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 134:2

Lift your hands toward the sanctuary and bless the LORD! 📖

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 134:3

May the LORD, who made heaven and earth, bless you from Zion. 📖
