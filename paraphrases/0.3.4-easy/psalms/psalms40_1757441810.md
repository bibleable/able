# Psalms 40 — Thanks for rescue and a renewed appeal

**About this chapter**

David tells of the LORD’s help and his response.

He then asks for rescue again.

## Psalms 40:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A psalm of David.

I waited patiently for the LORD.

He turned toward me and heard my cry.

## Psalms 40:2

He lifted me from the pit of despair and the sticky mud. 📖

He set my feet on a rock.

He made my steps firm.

## Psalms 40:3

He put a new song in my mouth, a song of praise to our God. 📖

Many will see, fear, and trust in the LORD.

## Psalms 40:4

Blessed is the person who trusts the LORD. 📖

He has not turned to proud people or those who turn aside into lies.

## Psalms 40:5

LORD my God, You have done many wonders and made many plans for us. 📖

No one compares with You.

If I tell about them, they are more than I can count.

## Psalms 40:6

You did not desire sacrifice and offering. 📖

But You opened my ears.

You did not require burnt offerings or sin offerings.

**Help with offerings:** Gifts or sacrifices in worship.

Burnt offerings were burned.

Sin offerings dealt with wrongdoing.

Opened ears pictures being made ready to listen.

## Psalms 40:7

Then I said, “Here I am. 📖

I have come.

It is written about me in the scroll.

**Help with scroll:** A long sheet rolled up to hold writing.

## Psalms 40:8

My God, I delight to do Your will. 📖

Your law is in my heart.”

## Psalms 40:9

I announce righteousness in the great gathering. 📖

I do not seal my lips, as You know, LORD.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right before God.

## Psalms 40:10

I have not hidden Your righteousness in my heart. 📖

I have announced Your faithfulness and rescue.

I have not hidden Your faithful love and faithfulness from the great gathering.

## Psalms 40:11

LORD, do not keep Your mercy from me. 📖

Your faithful love and faithfulness will always guard me.

## Psalms 40:12

Countless evils surround me. 📖

My sins have overtaken me so I cannot see.

They are more than the hairs on my head.

My heart has failed within me.

## Psalms 40:13

LORD, be pleased to rescue me. 📖

LORD, hurry to help me.

## Psalms 40:14

May people seeking my life be ashamed and confused. 📖

May people wishing me harm be driven back and humiliated.

## Psalms 40:15

May people saying, “Aha, aha!” be horrified at their own shame. 📖

## Psalms 40:16

May everyone seeking You rejoice and be glad in You. 📖

May those who love Your rescue always say, “The LORD be praised as great!”

## Psalms 40:17

But I am poor and needy. 📖

May the Lord think of me.

You are my helper and rescuer.

My God, do not delay.
