# Psalms 150 — Let everything with breath praise the LORD

**About this chapter**

The final psalm calls for praise with many instruments and every living breath.

## Psalms 150:1

Hallelujah! 📖

Praise God in His sanctuary.

Praise Him in His mighty heavens.

**Help with Hallelujah and sanctuary:** Praise the LORD, and a holy place of worship.

## Psalms 150:2

Praise Him for His mighty acts. 📖

Praise Him for His excellent greatness.

## Psalms 150:3

Praise Him with the horn’s sound. 📖

Praise Him with harp and lyre.

**Help with harp and lyre:** Stringed musical instruments.

## Psalms 150:4

Praise Him with tambourine and dancing. 📖

Praise Him with strings and flute.

## Psalms 150:5

Praise Him with clashing cymbals. 📖

Praise Him with ringing cymbals.

**Help with cymbals:** Metal plates struck together to make music.

## Psalms 150:6

Let everything with breath praise the LORD! 📖

Hallelujah!
