# Psalms 131 — A quiet soul and lasting hope

**About this chapter**

David describes humility and quiet trust, then calls Israel to hope in the LORD.

## Psalms 131:1

A song of ascents. 📖

Of David.

LORD, my heart is not proud, and my eyes are not haughty.

I do not seek great things or matters too high for me.

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

Haughty means proud and looking down on others.

## Psalms 131:2

I have stilled and quieted my soul. 📖

My soul is like a weaned child with his mother.

Like a weaned child, it rests within me.

**Help with weaned:** No longer feeding at the mother’s breast.

## Psalms 131:3

Israel, hope in the LORD now and forever. 📖
