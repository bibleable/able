# Psalms 43 — A prayer to return to God’s house

**About this chapter**

The singer asks for a just decision and for guidance into God’s presence.

## Psalms 43:1

God, prove me right. 📖

Defend my case against a nation that rejects You.

Rescue me from dishonest and unjust men.

## Psalms 43:2

You are the God of my refuge. 📖

Why have You rejected me?

Why must I walk in sorrow because of my enemy’s cruelty?

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 43:3

Send Your light and truth to lead me. 📖

Let them bring me to Your holy mountain and the place where You live.

## Psalms 43:4

Then I will go to God’s altar, to God, my greatest joy. 📖

God, my God, I will praise You with the harp.

**Help with altar and harp:** A place for offerings and a stringed musical instrument.

## Psalms 43:5

My soul, why are you so sad? 📖

Why are you troubled within me?

Hope in God.

I will yet praise Him, my Savior and my God.
