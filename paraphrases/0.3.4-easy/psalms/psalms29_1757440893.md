# Psalms 29 — The powerful voice of the LORD

**About this chapter**

David describes the LORD’s power with pictures of a mighty storm.

## Psalms 29:1

A psalm of David. 📖

Heavenly beings, give the LORD glory and strength.

Acknowledge His glory and strength.

## Psalms 29:2

Give the LORD the glory His name deserves. 📖

Worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness.

**Help with holiness:** God’s complete purity and greatness, setting Him apart.

Splendor means wonderful glory.

## Psalms 29:3

The LORD’s voice is over the waters. 📖

The God of glory thunders.

The LORD is heard over many waters.

## Psalms 29:4

The LORD’s voice is powerful. 📖

The LORD’s voice is majestic.

**Help with majestic:** Showing great power and glory.

## Psalms 29:5

The LORD’s voice breaks cedar trees. 📖

The LORD shatters Lebanon’s cedars.

## Psalms 29:6

He makes Lebanon skip like a calf. 📖

He makes Sirion skip like a young wild ox.

**Help with Lebanon and Sirion:** A region and a mountain.

The song pictures them shaking under the LORD’s power.

## Psalms 29:7

The LORD’s voice strikes with flames of fire. 📖

## Psalms 29:8

The LORD’s voice shakes the wilderness. 📖

The LORD shakes the Wilderness of Kadesh.

## Psalms 29:9

The LORD’s voice twists oak trees and strips forests bare. 📖

Everyone in His temple cries, “Glory!”

## Psalms 29:10

The LORD sits enthroned over the flood. 📖

The LORD sits as King forever.

## Psalms 29:11

The LORD gives His people strength. 📖

The LORD blesses His people with peace.
