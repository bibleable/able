# Psalms 100 — A song of thanks

**About this chapter**

This song calls everyone to worship the LORD with joy and thanks.

## Psalms 100:1

A psalm of thanksgiving. 📖

All the earth, make a joyful sound to the LORD.

## Psalms 100:2

Serve the LORD gladly. 📖

Come into His presence with joyful songs.

## Psalms 100:3

Know that the LORD is God. 📖

He made us, and we belong to Him.

We are His people, the sheep of His pasture.

## Psalms 100:4

Enter His gates with thanks and His courts with praise. 📖

Thank Him and bless His name.

**Help with courts:** Open areas belonging to the place of worship.

## Psalms 100:5

The LORD is good. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

His faithfulness continues through all generations.
