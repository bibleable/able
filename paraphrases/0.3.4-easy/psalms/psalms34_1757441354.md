# Psalms 34 — Taste and see that the LORD is good

**About this chapter**

David praises the LORD for rescue.

He calls others to trust God and turn away from evil.

## Psalms 34:1

Of David, when he pretended to be insane before Abimelech, so the king drove him away. 📖

I will bless the LORD at all times.

His praise will always be on my lips.

**Help with the heading:** It describes David pretending, rather than describing people with mental illness as evil.

## Psalms 34:2

My soul boasts in the LORD. 📖

Let people treated cruelly hear and rejoice.

## Psalms 34:3

Praise the LORD’s greatness with me. 📖

Let us lift His name high together.

## Psalms 34:4

I sought the LORD, and He answered me. 📖

He rescued me from all my fears.

## Psalms 34:5

People who look to Him shine with joy. 📖

Their faces will never be ashamed.

## Psalms 34:6

This poor man called, and the LORD heard him. 📖

He saved him from all his troubles.

## Psalms 34:7

The LORD’s angel camps around people who fear Him. 📖

He rescues them.

**Help with fear Him:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Psalms 34:8

Taste and see that the LORD is good. 📖

Blessed is the person who takes refuge in Him!

**Help with taste and refuge:** Experience His goodness.

Take refuge means seek safety and protection.

## Psalms 34:9

His saints, fear the LORD. 📖

People who fear Him lack nothing.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 34:10

Young lions lack food and go hungry. 📖

But people who seek the LORD lack no good thing.

## Psalms 34:11

Come, children, and listen to me. 📖

I will teach you the fear of the LORD.

## Psalms 34:12

Who delights in life? 📖

Who wants to see good days?

## Psalms 34:13

Keep your tongue from evil. 📖

Keep your lips from lying.

## Psalms 34:14

Turn from evil and do good. 📖

Seek peace and keep pursuing it.

## Psalms 34:15

The LORD’s eyes watch righteous people. 📖

His ears listen closely to their cry.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 34:16

But the LORD’s face is against people who do evil. 📖

He will wipe all memory of them from the earth.

## Psalms 34:17

Righteous people cry out, and the LORD hears. 📖

He rescues them from all their troubles.

## Psalms 34:18

The LORD is near people whose hearts are broken. 📖

He saves people whose spirits are contrite.

**Help with contrite:** Deeply sorry and humbled.

## Psalms 34:19

A righteous person has many troubles. 📖

But the LORD rescues him from them all.

## Psalms 34:20

He protects all his bones. 📖

Not one will be broken.

## Psalms 34:21

Evil will kill wicked people. 📖

People who hate righteous people will be condemned.

## Psalms 34:22

The LORD rescues His servants. 📖

No one who takes refuge in Him will be condemned.
