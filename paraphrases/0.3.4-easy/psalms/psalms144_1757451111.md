# Psalms 144 — A prayer for rescue and the people’s well-being

**About this chapter**

David praises God’s help in battle and asks for rescue.

He describes the well-being of a blessed people.

## Psalms 144:1

Of David. 📖

Blessed be the LORD, my Rock!

He trains my hands for war and my fingers for battle.

**Help with Rock:** A picture of strength and protection.

## Psalms 144:2

He is my steady love and fortress, my stronghold and rescuer. 📖

He is my shield, where I take refuge.

He brings peoples under me.

**Help with fortress, stronghold, and refuge:** Strong places of safety and protection.

## Psalms 144:3

LORD, what is a human being, that You notice him? 📖

What is a son of man, that You think of him?

## Psalms 144:4

A person is like a breath. 📖

His days are like a passing shadow.

## Psalms 144:5

LORD, open Your heavens and come down. 📖

Touch the mountains so they smoke.

## Psalms 144:6

Flash Your lightning and scatter them. 📖

Shoot Your arrows and make them flee.

## Psalms 144:7

Reach down from above. 📖

Free me and rescue me from deep waters and foreigners’ grip,

## Psalms 144:8

whose mouths speak lies and whose right hands deceive. 📖

## Psalms 144:9

God, I will sing You a new song. 📖

I will make music to You on a ten-stringed harp,

## Psalms 144:10

for You give kings victory. 📖

You free Your servant David from the deadly sword.

## Psalms 144:11

Free me and rescue me from foreigners’ grip. 📖

Their mouths speak lies, and their right hands deceive.

## Psalms 144:12

Then our sons will be like plants cared for in their youth. 📖

Our daughters will be like corner pillars carved to decorate a palace.

## Psalms 144:13

Our storehouses will be full of every kind of produce. 📖

Our flocks will produce thousands and tens of thousands in our fields.

## Psalms 144:14

Our oxen will carry great loads. 📖

No wall will be broken, no one taken captive, and no mourning cry heard in our streets.

## Psalms 144:15

Blessed are the people who have this! 📖

Blessed are the people whose God is the LORD!
