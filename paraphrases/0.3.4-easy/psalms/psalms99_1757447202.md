# Psalms 99 — The LORD our God is holy

**About this chapter**

The song praises the LORD’s rule, justice, forgiveness, and holiness.

## Psalms 99:1

The LORD reigns. 📖

Let the nations tremble!

He sits enthroned above the cherubim.

Let the earth shake!

**Help with cherubim:** Heavenly beings.

## Psalms 99:2

The LORD is great in Zion. 📖

He is high above all peoples.

**Help with Zion:** A name associated with Jerusalem.

## Psalms 99:3

Let them praise Your great and awesome name. 📖

He is holy!

**Help with awesome and holy:** Inspiring deep wonder and fear, and completely pure and set apart.

## Psalms 99:4

The mighty King loves justice. 📖

You established fairness.

You have carried out justice and righteousness in Jacob.

**Help with Jacob:** A name also used for Israel’s people.

Righteousness means what is right and just.

## Psalms 99:5

Lift the LORD our God high in praise. 📖

Worship at His footstool.

He is holy!

**Help with footstool:** A low support for a seated person’s feet.

It pictures worship before God as King.

## Psalms 99:6

Moses and Aaron were among His priests. 📖

Samuel was among those who called His name.

They called the LORD, and He answered.

## Psalms 99:7

He spoke to them from the cloud pillar. 📖

They kept His decrees and the laws He gave them.

**Help with decrees:** Authoritative commands.

## Psalms 99:8

LORD our God, You answered them. 📖

You forgave them, yet You punished their wrong deeds.

## Psalms 99:9

Lift the LORD our God high in praise. 📖

Worship at His holy mountain.

The LORD our God is holy.
