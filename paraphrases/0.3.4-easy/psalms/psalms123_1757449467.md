# Psalms 123 — Looking to God for mercy

**About this chapter**

The people look to the LORD while enduring contempt.

## Psalms 123:1

A song of ascents. 📖

I lift my eyes to You, the One enthroned in heaven.

**Help with ascents:** Going up.

These songs are associated with going up to Jerusalem for worship.

## Psalms 123:2

Servants’ eyes look to their master’s hand. 📖

A female servant’s eyes look to her mistress’s hand.

In the same way, our eyes look to the LORD our God until He shows mercy.

## Psalms 123:3

LORD, have mercy on us, have mercy. 📖

We have endured much contempt.

**Help with contempt:** Being treated as worthless.

## Psalms 123:4

We have endured much scorn from arrogant people. 📖

We have endured much contempt from proud people.
