# Psalms 44 — The people appeal to God after defeat

**About this chapter**

The people remember God’s past help and describe their present defeat.

They say they have remained faithful and ask Him to rescue them.

## Psalms 44:1

For the choir leader. 📖

A Maskil of the sons of Korah.

God, we have heard with our ears.

Our fathers told us the work You did long ago, in their days.

**Help with Maskil:** A term for this song whose exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 44:2

With Your hand You drove out nations and planted our fathers there. 📖

You crushed the peoples and threw them out.

## Psalms 44:3

They did not take the land by their own sword. 📖

Their arm did not win victory.

Your right hand, arm, and shining face did this because You favored them.

## Psalms 44:4

God, You are my King. 📖

You order victories for Jacob.

**Help with Jacob:** A name also used for Israel’s people.

## Psalms 44:5

Through You we drive our enemies back. 📖

Through Your name we trample our enemies.

## Psalms 44:6

I do not trust my bow. 📖

My sword does not save me.

## Psalms 44:7

You save us from our enemies. 📖

You shame those who hate us.

## Psalms 44:8

We have boasted in God all day. 📖

We will praise Your name forever.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 44:9

But You have rejected and humbled us. 📖

You no longer go out with our armies.

## Psalms 44:10

You have made us retreat from the enemy. 📖

People who hate us have robbed us.

## Psalms 44:11

You have given us up like sheep to be eaten. 📖

You have scattered us among the nations.

## Psalms 44:12

You sell Your people for nothing. 📖

You gain no profit from selling them.

## Psalms 44:13

You have made us an object of our neighbors’ scorn. 📖

People around us mock and insult us.

## Psalms 44:14

You have made us a saying of scorn among the nations. 📖

The peoples laugh at us.

## Psalms 44:15

My disgrace is before me all day. 📖

Shame covers my face,

## Psalms 44:16

because of the voices that mock and insult. 📖

My enemy is determined to take revenge.

## Psalms 44:17

All this has happened, although we have not forgotten You. 📖

We have not betrayed Your covenant.

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Psalms 44:18

Our hearts have not turned back. 📖

Our steps have not left Your path.

## Psalms 44:19

But You have crushed us where jackals live. 📖

You have covered us with deepest darkness.

**Help with jackals:** Wild animals living in deserted places.

## Psalms 44:20

If we had forgotten our God’s name or lifted our hands to a foreign god, 📖

## Psalms 44:21

would God not have discovered it? 📖

He knows the heart’s secrets.

## Psalms 44:22

Yet because of You we face death all day. 📖

We are treated like sheep ready to be killed.

## Psalms 44:23

Wake up, Lord! 📖

Why are You sleeping?

Rise up!

Do not reject us forever.

## Psalms 44:24

Why do You hide Your face? 📖

Why do You forget our suffering and cruel treatment?

## Psalms 44:25

Our soul has sunk into the dust. 📖

Our bodies cling to the ground.

## Psalms 44:26

Rise up and help us! 📖

Rescue us because of Your faithful love.
