# Psalms 83 — A prayer against a hostile alliance

**About this chapter**

Asaph describes nations plotting to destroy Israel.

He asks God to act against them and make His name known.

## Psalms 83:1

A song. 📖

A psalm of Asaph.

God, do not be silent.

Do not remain speechless.

God, do not stay still.

## Psalms 83:2

See how Your enemies rage. 📖

Your foes raise their heads.

## Psalms 83:3

They cleverly plan against Your people. 📖

They plot together against those You cherish,

## Psalms 83:4

saying, “Come, let us wipe them out as a nation. 📖

Let Israel’s name be remembered no more.”

## Psalms 83:5

They plot together with one mind. 📖

They form an alliance against You:

**Help with alliance:** An agreement to act together.

## Psalms 83:6

Edom’s tents and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites, 📖

## Psalms 83:7

Gebal, Ammon, Amalek, and Philistia with Tyre’s people. 📖

## Psalms 83:8

Even Assyria joined them. 📖

They lend strength to Lot’s sons.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 83:9

Do to them what You did to Midian. 📖

Do what You did to Sisera and Jabin at the River Kishon.

## Psalms 83:10

They died at Endor. 📖

They became like dung on the ground.

**Help with dung:** Solid body waste.

## Psalms 83:11

Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb. 📖

Make all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna,

**Help with nobles and princes:** Leaders with high rank.

## Psalms 83:12

who said, “Let us take God’s pastures for ourselves.” 📖

## Psalms 83:13

My God, make them like tumbleweed. 📖

Make them like chaff before the wind.

**Help with tumbleweed and chaff:** A dry plant blown along the ground, and light plant parts separated from grain.

## Psalms 83:14

Fire burns up a forest. 📖

Flames set mountains on fire.

## Psalms 83:15

In the same way, chase them with Your violent storm. 📖

Terrify them with Your storm.

## Psalms 83:16

LORD, cover their faces with shame so they may seek Your name. 📖

## Psalms 83:17

May they always be ashamed and terrified. 📖

May they perish in disgrace.

## Psalms 83:18

May they know that You alone, whose name is the LORD, are Most High over all the earth. 📖
