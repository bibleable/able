# Psalms 79 — A prayer after Jerusalem’s destruction

**About this chapter**

Asaph describes the destruction of Jerusalem and its people.

The prayer asks for mercy, rescue, and judgment against the attackers.

## Psalms 79:1

A psalm of Asaph. 📖

God, nations have invaded Your inheritance.

They have made Your holy temple unholy and turned Jerusalem into broken stones.

**Help with inheritance:** The land and people belonging to God.

## Psalms 79:2

They gave Your servants’ dead bodies to birds for food. 📖

They gave Your saints’ flesh to wild animals.

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Psalms 79:3

They poured out blood like water all around Jerusalem. 📖

No one remains to bury the dead.

## Psalms 79:4

Our neighbors scorn us. 📖

People around us mock and insult us.

## Psalms 79:5

How long, LORD? 📖

Will You be angry forever?

Will Your jealousy burn like fire?

## Psalms 79:6

Pour Your wrath on nations that do not acknowledge You. 📖

Pour it on kingdoms refusing to call on Your name,

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Psalms 79:7

because they have devoured Jacob and ruined his homeland. 📖

**Help with Jacob:** A name also used for Israel’s people.

## Psalms 79:8

Do not hold past sins against us. 📖

Let Your compassion come quickly.

We have been brought very low.

**Help with compassion:** Deep care for someone who suffers.

## Psalms 79:9

God who saves us, help us for Your name’s glory. 📖

Rescue us and atone for our sins for Your name’s sake.

**Help with atone:** Deal with guilt so that sin is forgiven.

## Psalms 79:10

Why should nations ask, “Where is their God?” 📖

Before our eyes, show the nations Your vengeance for Your servants’ bloodshed.

**Help with vengeance:** Punishment in return for a wrong.

## Psalms 79:11

May captives’ groans reach You. 📖

By Your strong arm, keep people sentenced to death alive.

## Psalms 79:12

Lord, pay our neighbors back seven times over for the insults they threw at You. 📖

## Psalms 79:13

Then we, Your people and pasture’s sheep, will thank You forever. 📖

We will tell Your praise from generation to generation.
