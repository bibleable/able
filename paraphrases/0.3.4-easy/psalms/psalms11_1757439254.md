# Psalms 11 — Trusting the LORD when danger comes

**About this chapter**

David trusts the LORD.

Verses 1–3 include advice other people give him.

## Psalms 11:1

For the choir leader. 📖

Of David.

I take refuge in the LORD.

How can you tell me, “Flee like a bird to your mountain!

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 11:2

Look, wicked people bend their bows. 📖

They fit arrows to the strings.

They shoot from the shadows at people whose hearts are upright.

**Help with upright:** Honest and right.

## Psalms 11:3

If the foundations are destroyed, what can righteous people do?” 📖

**Help with foundations:** A picture of what holds society together.

Righteous means right before God.

## Psalms 11:4

The LORD is in His holy temple. 📖

The LORD is on His throne in heaven.

His eyes watch closely and examine human beings.

## Psalms 11:5

The LORD tests righteous people and wicked people. 📖

His soul hates anyone who loves violence.

## Psalms 11:6

He will rain burning coals and sulfur on wicked people. 📖

A burning hot wind will be their share.

**Help with sulfur:** A substance that burns.

## Psalms 11:7

The LORD is righteous. 📖

He loves justice.

Upright people will see His face.
