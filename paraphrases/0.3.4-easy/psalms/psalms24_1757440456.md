# Psalms 24 — The King of Glory

**About this chapter**

David sings about the LORD’s ownership of the earth.

The song asks who may come before Him and welcomes the King of Glory.

## Psalms 24:1

A psalm of David. 📖

The earth belongs to the LORD, with everything in it.

The world and everyone living in it belong to Him.

## Psalms 24:2

He founded it on the seas. 📖

He established it on the waters.

## Psalms 24:3

Who may climb the LORD’s hill? 📖

Who may stand in His holy place?

## Psalms 24:4

The person with clean hands and a pure heart. 📖

He does not give his soul to an idol or swear falsely.

**Help with clean hands and pure heart:** Actions and thoughts free from evil.

An idol is an image worshiped as a god.

## Psalms 24:5

He will receive blessing from the LORD. 📖

The God who saves him will declare him right.

## Psalms 24:6

This is the generation of people who seek Him. 📖

They seek Your face, God of Jacob.

Selah.

**Help with Selah:** This word appears in the song.

Its exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 24:7

Lift your heads, gates! 📖

Rise up, ancient doors, so the King of Glory may enter!

**Help with gates and doors:** The song pictures them rising to welcome the King.

## Psalms 24:8

Who is this King of Glory? 📖

The LORD, strong and mighty.

The LORD, mighty in battle.

## Psalms 24:9

Lift your heads, gates! 📖

Rise up, ancient doors, so the King of Glory may enter!

## Psalms 24:10

Who is He, this King of Glory? 📖

The LORD of Hosts is the King of Glory.

Selah.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** A title showing the LORD’s command over heavenly armies.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.
