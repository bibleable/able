# Psalms 25 — A prayer for teaching, forgiveness, and rescue

**About this chapter**

David asks the LORD to guide him and forgive him.

He also asks for Israel’s rescue.

## Psalms 25:1

Of David. 📖

LORD, I lift my soul to You.

## Psalms 25:2

My God, I trust in You. 📖

Do not let me be put to shame.

Do not let my enemies celebrate over me.

## Psalms 25:3

No one who waits for You will be put to shame. 📖

But people who betray others without cause will be disgraced.

## Psalms 25:4

LORD, show me Your ways. 📖

Teach me Your paths.

## Psalms 25:5

Guide me in Your truth and teach me. 📖

You are the God who saves me.

I wait for You all day long.

## Psalms 25:6

LORD, remember Your compassion and faithful love. 📖

They have lasted from age to age.

## Psalms 25:7

Do not remember the sins of my youth or my rebellious acts. 📖

LORD, remember me with Your faithful love because of Your goodness.

## Psalms 25:8

The LORD is good and upright. 📖

So He shows sinners the way.

**Help with upright:** Completely right and honest.

## Psalms 25:9

He guides humble people in what is right. 📖

He teaches them His way.

## Psalms 25:10

All the LORD’s ways are loving and faithful toward people who keep His covenant and decrees. 📖

**Help with covenant and decrees:** His serious agreement and His commands.

## Psalms 25:11

LORD, forgive my great sin for the sake of Your name. 📖

## Psalms 25:12

Who is the person who fears the LORD? 📖

The LORD will teach him the path chosen for him.

**Help with fears the LORD:** Respects God and takes His authority seriously.

## Psalms 25:13

He will live with plenty. 📖

His descendants will inherit the land.

**Help with descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

## Psalms 25:14

The LORD shares His confidence with people who fear Him. 📖

He makes His covenant known to them.

## Psalms 25:15

My eyes are always on the LORD. 📖

He will free my feet from the net.

## Psalms 25:16

Turn toward me and be kind. 📖

I am lonely and suffering.

## Psalms 25:17

My heart’s troubles grow. 📖

Free me from my distress.

## Psalms 25:18

Look at my suffering and trouble. 📖

Take away all my sins.

## Psalms 25:19

Look at my many enemies. 📖

They hate me with fierce hatred.

## Psalms 25:20

Guard my soul and rescue me. 📖

Do not let me be put to shame.

I take refuge in You.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 25:21

May integrity and uprightness protect me, because I wait for You. 📖

**Help with integrity and uprightness:** Being honest and doing what is right.

## Psalms 25:22

God, rescue Israel from all its distress. 📖
