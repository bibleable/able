# Psalms 73 — From envy to trust in God

**About this chapter**

Asaph describes his struggle when wicked people seem successful.

His understanding changes when he enters God’s sanctuary.

## Psalms 73:1

A psalm of Asaph. 📖

God is surely good to Israel, to people whose hearts are pure.

## Psalms 73:2

But my feet had almost stumbled. 📖

My steps had nearly slipped.

## Psalms 73:3

I envied proud people when I saw wicked people’s success. 📖

## Psalms 73:4

They have no struggle when they die. 📖

Their bodies are well fed.

## Psalms 73:5

They do not carry other people’s burdens. 📖

They do not suffer as others do.

## Psalms 73:6

So pride is their necklace. 📖

Violence covers them like clothing.

## Psalms 73:7

Evil comes from their wealth and success. 📖

Their hearts’ imaginations run wild.

## Psalms 73:8

They mock and speak with harmful intent. 📖

They proudly threaten to treat people cruelly.

## Psalms 73:9

They set their mouths against heaven. 📖

Their tongues march proudly across the earth.

**Help with mouths and tongues:** Pictures of proud speech against God and people.

## Psalms 73:10

So their people return here and drink plenty of water. 📖

**About this picture:** The exact connection of this water image with the surrounding words needs further editorial review.

## Psalms 73:11

Wicked people say, “How can God know? 📖

Does the Most High have knowledge?”

## Psalms 73:12

Look at these wicked people! 📖

They are always carefree and keep gaining wealth.

## Psalms 73:13

Surely I kept my heart pure for nothing. 📖

I washed my hands in innocence for nothing.

## Psalms 73:14

I suffer all day. 📖

I am punished every morning.

## Psalms 73:15

If I had said, “I will speak this way,” I would have betrayed Your children. 📖

## Psalms 73:16

I tried to understand all this. 📖

It troubled me,

## Psalms 73:17

until I entered God’s sanctuary. 📖

Then I understood their end.

**Help with sanctuary:** A holy place of worship.

## Psalms 73:18

You surely put them on slippery ground. 📖

You throw them down into ruin.

## Psalms 73:19

They are destroyed so suddenly! 📖

Terrors sweep them away completely.

## Psalms 73:20

Lord, when You awake, You despise their form. 📖

It is like waking from a dream.

## Psalms 73:21

When my heart was bitter with grief and I was pierced inside, 📖

## Psalms 73:22

I was senseless and ignorant. 📖

I was like a brute beast before You.

**Help with brute beast:** Asaph compares his lack of understanding to an animal’s.

## Psalms 73:23

Yet I am always with You. 📖

You hold my right hand.

## Psalms 73:24

You guide me with Your advice. 📖

Afterward You receive me in glory.

## Psalms 73:25

Who do I have in heaven except You? 📖

On earth, I desire no one besides You.

## Psalms 73:26

My flesh and heart may fail. 📖

But God is my heart’s strength and my portion forever.

**Help with portion:** What Asaph receives and depends on for his life.

## Psalms 73:27

People far from You will surely perish. 📖

You destroy everyone unfaithful to You.

## Psalms 73:28

But it is good for me to draw near to God. 📖

I have made the Lord GOD my refuge so I can announce all Your works.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.
