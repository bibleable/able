# Psalms 1 — Two ways to live

**About this chapter**

This song contrasts people who follow the LORD’s teaching with wicked people.

## Psalms 1:1

Blessed is the person who does not follow wicked people’s advice. 📖

He does not step onto sinners’ path.

He does not sit with people who mock.

## Psalms 1:2

Instead, he delights in the LORD’s Law. 📖

He thinks deeply about His law day and night.

**Help with Law:** The LORD’s teaching about how to live.

## Psalms 1:3

He is like a tree planted beside streams. 📖

It gives fruit at the right time.

Its leaves do not dry up.

He succeeds in everything he does.

## Psalms 1:4

Wicked people are not like that! 📖

They are like chaff blown away by the wind.

**Help with chaff:** Light plant parts left after grain is separated.

The song pictures wicked people as having no lasting security.

## Psalms 1:5

So wicked people will not stand in the judgment. 📖

Sinners will not stand in the gathering of righteous people.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Psalms 1:6

The LORD guards the path of righteous people. 📖

But wicked people’s way will perish.
