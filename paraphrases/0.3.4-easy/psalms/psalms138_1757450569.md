# Psalms 138 — Thanks for God’s faithful help

**About this chapter**

David gives thanks and speaks of the LORD’s care and purpose.

## Psalms 138:1

Of David. 📖

I thank You with all my heart.

I sing Your praises before the gods.

## Psalms 138:2

I bow toward Your holy temple. 📖

I thank Your name for Your faithful love and faithfulness.

You have raised Your name and word above everything else.

## Psalms 138:3

On the day I called, You answered. 📖

You gave me courage and strengthened my soul.

## Psalms 138:4

LORD, all the earth’s kings will thank You when they hear Your words. 📖

## Psalms 138:5

They will sing of the LORD’s ways. 📖

The LORD’s glory is great.

## Psalms 138:6

Though the LORD is high above all, He cares for lowly people. 📖

But He knows proud people from far away.

## Psalms 138:7

If I walk among troubles, You keep me from my enemies’ anger. 📖

You stretch out Your hand.

Your right hand saves me.

## Psalms 138:8

The LORD will fulfill His purpose for me. 📖

LORD, Your faithful love lasts forever.

Do not abandon the works of Your hands.
