# Psalms 61 — A prayer for shelter and the king

**About this chapter**

David asks God for protection and prays for the king’s life.

## Psalms 61:1

For the choir leader, with stringed instruments. 📖

Of David.

God, hear my cry.

Pay attention to my prayer.

## Psalms 61:2

From the earth’s ends I call You when my heart grows weak. 📖

Lead me to the rock higher than I am.

**Help with the rock:** A picture of strong protection beyond David’s own strength.

## Psalms 61:3

You have been my refuge. 📖

You have been a strong tower against the enemy.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Psalms 61:4

Let me live in Your tent forever. 📖

Let me find safety under Your sheltering wings.

Selah.

**Help with wings:** A picture of care like a bird gives its young.

Selah is a song term whose exact musical meaning is uncertain.

## Psalms 61:5

God, You have heard my vows. 📖

You gave me the inheritance kept for people who fear Your name.

**Help with vows and inheritance:** Serious promises, and a share someone receives.

Fearing God means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Psalms 61:6

Add days to the king’s life. 📖

May his years stretch through many generations.

## Psalms 61:7

May he sit on his throne in God’s presence forever. 📖

Appoint Your faithful love and faithfulness to guard him.

## Psalms 61:8

Then I will always sing praise to Your name. 📖

I will keep my vows day after day.
