# Ezekiel 29 — Egypt and the great river monster

**About this chapter**

God pictures Pharaoh as a great river monster and Egypt as an unreliable walking stick.

He announces forty years of ruin, followed by a return to a weaker kingdom.

A later message names Egypt as payment for Babylon’s army.

## Ezekiel 29:1

The LORD spoke to me in the tenth year, on the twelfth day of the tenth month. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:2

God said, “Son of man, turn toward Pharaoh, king of Egypt. 📖

Speak My message against him and all Egypt.

## Ezekiel 29:3

Tell him what the Lord GOD says. 📖

Look, Pharaoh, king of Egypt!

I am against you, great monster lying among your rivers.

You say, ‘The Nile is mine.

I made it myself.’

**About this picture:** God describes Pharaoh as a river monster.

The Nile is Egypt’s great river.

## Ezekiel 29:4

But I will put hooks in your jaws. 📖

I will make the fish of your streams cling to your scales.

I will drag you out of your rivers.

All the fish of your streams will cling to your scales.

**Help with “scales”:** These are hard plates covering some animals’ bodies.

## Ezekiel 29:5

I will leave you and all the fish of your streams in the desert. 📖

You will fall in the open field.

No one will take you away or gather you for burial.

I have given you as food for the animals of the earth and the birds of the air.

## Ezekiel 29:6

Then all the people of Egypt will know that I am the LORD. 📖

You were only a walking stick made of reeds for Israel’s people.

**Help with this picture:** Reeds are plants with weak stems.

A reed walking stick cannot give strong support.

Egypt could not give Israel the support it relied on.

## Ezekiel 29:7

When Israel took hold of you, you split and tore all their shoulders. 📖

When they leaned on you, you broke and twisted their backs painfully.

## Ezekiel 29:8

So the Lord GOD says, “I will bring a sword against you. 📖

I will cut off people and animals from you.

## Ezekiel 29:9

The land of Egypt will become empty and ruined. 📖

Then they will know that I am the LORD.

You said, ‘The Nile is mine.

I made it.’

## Ezekiel 29:10

So I am against you and your rivers. 📖

I will turn Egypt into an empty, ruined land.

This will stretch from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border of Cush.

## Ezekiel 29:11

No person or animal will walk through it. 📖

No one will live there for forty years.

## Ezekiel 29:12

I will make Egypt empty among empty lands. 📖

Her cities will lie empty for forty years among ruined cities.

I will spread the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the countries.

## Ezekiel 29:13

The Lord GOD says, “At the end of forty years, I will gather the Egyptians from the nations. 📖

They were scattered to those nations.

## Ezekiel 29:14

I will bring Egypt’s people back from captivity. 📖

I will return them to Pathros, the land where they began.

There they will be a lowly kingdom.

**Help with “captivity”:** This means being held as prisoners.

## Ezekiel 29:15

Egypt will be the lowest of kingdoms. 📖

It will never again lift itself above the nations.

I will make Egypt weak, so it will never again rule over the nations.

## Ezekiel 29:16

Israel’s people will never again put their trust in Egypt. 📖

Egypt will remind them of their sin in turning to the Egyptians.

Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.”

## Ezekiel 29:17

The LORD spoke to me in the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:18

God said, “Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, made his army work very hard against Tyre. 📖

Every head became bald, and every shoulder became raw.

But he and his army received no payment from Tyre for all their work.

## Ezekiel 29:19

So the Lord GOD says, “I will give Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. 📖

He will carry off its wealth.

He will seize its goods and carry away its possessions.

This will be payment for his army.

## Ezekiel 29:20

I have given him Egypt as a reward for his work. 📖

He did that work for Me, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 29:21

On that day, I will make a horn grow for Israel’s people. 📖

I will open your mouth to speak among them.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

**Help with “a horn”:** An animal’s horn is a picture of strength.

Here the growing horn pictures renewed strength for Israel.
