# Ezekiel 43 — God’s glory returns to the temple

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel sees God’s glory enter the temple.

God gives instructions about the temple and the altar.

The passage includes measurements and the offerings for setting the altar apart.

## Ezekiel 43:1

The man brought me back to the gate facing east. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:2

I saw the glory of Israel’s God coming from the east. 📖

His voice sounded like the roar of many waters.

The earth shone with His glory.

## Ezekiel 43:3

The vision was like the one I saw when He came to destroy the city. 📖

It was like the visions I saw beside the River Kebar.

I fell with my face to the ground,

## Ezekiel 43:4

and the LORD’s glory entered the temple through the east gate. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:5

Then the Spirit lifted me and brought me into the inner courtyard. 📖

The LORD’s glory filled the temple.

## Ezekiel 43:6

The man stood beside me. 📖

I heard someone speaking to me from inside the temple.

## Ezekiel 43:7

He said, “Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the soles of My feet. 📖

I will live among the Israelites here forever.

Neither Israel’s people nor their kings will ever again make My holy name unholy.

They will stop their prostitution and the funeral offerings for their kings when they die.

**About these words:** Prostitution is used as a picture of unfaithfulness to God.

The source also condemns funeral offerings for kings.

## Ezekiel 43:8

They put their entrance floor beside Mine and their doorposts beside Mine, with only a wall between us. 📖

Their horrible sins made My holy name unholy.

So I destroyed them in My anger.

## Ezekiel 43:9

Now let them take their prostitution and funeral offerings for their kings far away from Me. 📖

Then I will live among them forever.

## Ezekiel 43:10

Son of man, describe the temple to Israel’s people, so they may be ashamed of their sins. 📖

Let them measure its plan.

## Ezekiel 43:11

If they are ashamed of everything they have done, show them the temple’s design. 📖

Show its arrangement, exits, entrances, and whole design, with all its rules, forms, and laws.

Write it down where they can see.

Then they may keep its complete design and all its rules and carry them out.

## Ezekiel 43:12

This is the temple’s law. 📖

All the territory around it on the mountaintop will be most holy.

Yes, this is the temple’s law.

## Ezekiel 43:13

These are the altar’s measurements in long cubits. 📖

Each is one cubit and one handbreadth.

Its channel must be one cubit deep and one cubit wide.

A rim one span wide must go around its edge.

This is the altar’s height.

**Help with the measurements:** A cubit measures from elbow to fingertips.

A handbreadth is the width of a hand.

A span is the distance across a spread hand, from thumb to little finger.

**Help with “channel”:** BSB calls this a “gutter.”

## Ezekiel 43:14

From the channel on the ground to the lower ledge must be two cubits. 📖

The ledge must be one cubit wide.

From the smaller ledge to the larger ledge must be four cubits.

The ledge must be one cubit wide.

## Ezekiel 43:15

The altar’s burning area must be four cubits high. 📖

Four horns must stick up from it.

**Help with these words:** BSB calls the burning area the “hearth.”

The horns are raised points on the altar.

## Ezekiel 43:16

The burning area must be square at its four corners, twelve cubits long and twelve cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:17

The ledge must also be square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide. 📖

Its rim must be half a cubit, with a channel one cubit wide all around.

The altar’s steps must face east.”

## Ezekiel 43:18

Then He told me, “Son of man, the Lord GOD says this. 📖

These are the altar’s rules on the day it is built.

Burnt offerings may be sacrificed on it, and blood may be splashed on it.

**Help with “burnt offerings”:** These are sacrifices burned on an altar for God.

## Ezekiel 43:19

Give a young bull from the herd as a sin offering to the Levite priests from Zadok’s family. 📖

They approach Me to serve before Me, declares the Lord GOD.

**Help with “sin offering”:** This is a sacrifice connected with dealing with sin.

## Ezekiel 43:20

Take some of its blood and put it on the altar’s four horns. 📖

Put it on the ledge’s four corners and all around the rim.

In this way, cleanse the altar and make atonement for it.

**Help with “atonement”:** Here this is the required action to remove uncleanness and set the altar right before God.

## Ezekiel 43:21

Then take away the bull for the sin offering. 📖

Burn it in the appointed part of the temple area, outside the holy place.

## Ezekiel 43:22

On the second day, present a male goat without any defect as a sin offering. 📖

The altar must be cleansed as it was with the bull.

## Ezekiel 43:23

When you finish the cleansing, present a young bull and a male sheep from the flock. 📖

Both must be without any defect.

## Ezekiel 43:24

Present them before the LORD. 📖

The priests must sprinkle salt on them.

They must sacrifice them as a burnt offering to the LORD.

## Ezekiel 43:25

For seven days, provide a male goat each day for a sin offering. 📖

Also provide a young bull and a male sheep from the flock, both without any defect.

## Ezekiel 43:26

For seven days, the priests must make atonement for the altar and cleanse it. 📖

In this way, they will set it apart as holy.

## Ezekiel 43:27

When those days end, from the eighth day onward, the priests must present your offerings on the altar. 📖

These are your burnt offerings and peace offerings.

Then I will accept you, declares the Lord GOD.”

**Help with “peace offerings”:** These are sacrifices connected with fellowship and peace with God.
