# Ezekiel 38 — Gog comes against Israel

**About this chapter**

God speaks against Gog of Magog and the many peoples with him.

The passage describes an attack against Israel and God’s judgment.

The draft keeps the names and future descriptions without assigning them to modern nations or events.

## Ezekiel 38:1

The LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:2

God said, “Son of man, turn against Gog from the land of Magog. 📖

He is the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

Speak My message against him.

## Ezekiel 38:3

Announce what the Lord GOD says. 📖

Look, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal!

I am against you.

## Ezekiel 38:4

I will turn you around and put hooks in your jaws. 📖

I will bring you out with all your army.

You will have horses and horsemen in full armor.

Your great company will carry large shields and small shields.

All will wave their swords.

**Help with the hooks:** This picture shows God controlling Gog’s movement.

## Ezekiel 38:5

Persia, Cush, and Put will come with them, all with shields and helmets. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:6

Gomer will come with all its troops. 📖

Beth-togarmah from the far north will come with all its troops.

Many nations will be with you.

## Ezekiel 38:7

Get ready! 📖

Prepare yourself and all your company gathered around you.

You will be their guard.

## Ezekiel 38:8

After a long time, you will be called. 📖

In the later years, you will enter a land that has recovered from war.

Its people were gathered from many nations to Israel’s mountains, which had long been empty.

They were brought out from the nations.

Now they all live safely.

## Ezekiel 38:9

You will advance like a thunderstorm with all your troops and many peoples. 📖

You will be like a cloud covering the land.

## Ezekiel 38:10

The Lord GOD says, “On that day, thoughts will rise in your mind. 📖

You will make an evil plan.

## Ezekiel 38:11

You will say, ‘I will attack a land of villages without walls. 📖

I will come against peaceful people who live safely.

None of them have walls, bars, or gates.

## Ezekiel 38:12

I will seize goods and carry them off. 📖

I will attack places that were empty but now have people living in them.

I will attack people gathered from the nations who have gained livestock and possessions.

They live at the center of the land.’

**Help with “livestock”:** These are farm animals.

## Ezekiel 38:13

Sheba, Dedan, and the traders of Tarshish with all its villages will ask you a question. 📖

They will ask, ‘Have you come to seize goods?

Have you gathered your great crowds to carry off stolen wealth?

Have you come to take silver, gold, cattle, and goods?

Have you come to seize a great amount?’

## Ezekiel 38:14

So, son of man, speak My message. 📖

Tell Gog what the Lord GOD says.

On that day, My people Israel will be living safely.

Will you not notice this?

## Ezekiel 38:15

You will come from your place in the far north, with many peoples. 📖

All will ride horses.

You will be a mighty crowd, a huge army.

## Ezekiel 38:16

You will advance against My people Israel like a cloud covering the land. 📖

Gog, this will happen in the later days.

I will bring you against My land.

I will show My holiness through you before the nations’ eyes, so they may know Me.

## Ezekiel 38:17

The Lord GOD says, “Are you the one I spoke about long ago through My servants, Israel’s prophets? 📖

In those times, they spoke for years about My bringing you against them.

## Ezekiel 38:18

On that day, when Gog comes against Israel’s land, My fierce anger will rise, declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:19

In My jealous, burning anger, I announce a great earthquake in Israel’s land on that day. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:20

The fish of the sea, birds of the air, and animals of the field will tremble before Me. 📖

Every creature crawling on the ground will tremble.

All people on earth will tremble before Me.

The mountains will be thrown down.

The cliffs will collapse, and every wall will fall to the ground.

## Ezekiel 38:21

I will call for a sword against Gog on all My mountains, declares the Lord GOD. 📖

Every man’s sword will be against his brother.

## Ezekiel 38:22

I will carry out judgment on him with deadly disease and bloodshed. 📖

I will pour heavy rain, hailstones, fire, and sulfur on him.

They will fall on his troops and the many nations with him.

**Help with “sulfur”:** This is a substance that burns.

## Ezekiel 38:23

I will show My greatness and holiness. 📖

I will reveal Myself before many nations.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
