# Ezekiel 22 — The sins of Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

God names the violence, sexual wrongdoing, dishonest gain, and failed leadership in Jerusalem.

He describes His judgment using the picture of metal melted in a furnace.

## Ezekiel 22:1

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:2

God said, “Son of man, will you judge her? 📖

Will you pass judgment on this city of bloodshed?

Then confront her with all her horrible sins.

## Ezekiel 22:3

Tell her what the Lord GOD says. 📖

City, you bring your own destruction by spilling blood inside your walls.

You make idols and make yourself unclean.

## Ezekiel 22:4

You are guilty because of the blood you have spilled. 📖

You are unclean because of the idols you have made.

You have brought your days to a close.

Your years have reached their end.

So I have made you a disgrace among the nations.

All the lands make fun of you.

## Ezekiel 22:5

People near and far will make fun of you. 📖

You are a city known for evil and full of disorder.

## Ezekiel 22:6

Look! 📖

Every prince of Israel among you has used his power to spill blood.

## Ezekiel 22:7

People treat fathers and mothers with scorn. 📖

Within your walls, people take advantage of foreigners who live there.

They oppress children without fathers and women whose husbands have died.

## Ezekiel 22:8

You have despised My holy things. 📖

You have treated My Sabbaths as unholy.

**Help with “Sabbaths”:** These are the special days of rest that God commanded His people to keep.

## Ezekiel 22:9

Men among you tell harmful lies to cause bloodshed. 📖

People eat at the places of worship on the mountains.

They do shameful sexual things.

## Ezekiel 22:10

Among you, men uncover their fathers’ nakedness. 📖

They violate women during the uncleanness of their monthly bleeding.

**Help with “fathers’ nakedness”:** This is an expression for forbidden sexual relations involving a father’s wife.

**About “uncleanness”:** This refers to the worship laws about monthly bleeding.

Monthly bleeding does not make a woman morally bad.

## Ezekiel 22:11

One man commits a horrible sexual sin with his neighbor’s wife. 📖

Another wickedly defiles his son’s wife.

Another violates his sister, who is his own father’s daughter.

**Help with “defiles” and “violates”:** These words describe the sexual wrongdoing condemned here.

## Ezekiel 22:12

People among you accept bribes to kill. 📖

You lend money with interest and take too much interest.

You force your neighbors to give you money.

But you have forgotten Me, declares the Lord GOD.

**Help with “bribes”:** These are payments given to make someone do something wrong.

**About lending:** BSB uses “usury” and “excess interest.”

The verse condemns these ways of gaining money.

## Ezekiel 22:13

Look! 📖

I clap My hands against your dishonest gain.

I clap My hands against the blood spilled among you.

## Ezekiel 22:14

Will you still be brave when I deal with you? 📖

Will your hands still be strong?

I, the LORD, have spoken.

I will act.

## Ezekiel 22:15

I will spread you among the nations. 📖

I will scatter you throughout the lands.

I will remove your uncleanness.

**Help with “uncleanness”:** Here this describes the people’s evil, which God is judging.

## Ezekiel 22:16

You will make yourself unclean in the eyes of the nations. 📖

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 22:17

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:18

God said, “Son of man, the people of Israel have become waste metal to Me. 📖

All of them are copper, tin, iron, and lead inside a furnace.

They are only the waste left from silver.

**Help with this picture:** A furnace is a very hot oven used to melt metal.

Waste left when silver is purified is called “dross.”

God compares Israel to this waste.

## Ezekiel 22:19

The Lord GOD says, “All of you have become waste metal. 📖

So look!

I will gather you into Jerusalem.

## Ezekiel 22:20

People gather silver, copper, iron, lead, and tin into a furnace. 📖

They melt these metals with a blast of fire.

In the same way, I will gather you in My fierce anger.

I will leave you there and melt you.

## Ezekiel 22:21

Yes, I will gather you together. 📖

I will blow the fire of My fierce anger on you.

You will be melted inside the city.

## Ezekiel 22:22

Silver melts in a furnace. 📖

In the same way, you will be melted inside the city.

Then you will know that I, the LORD, have poured My fierce anger over you.”

## Ezekiel 22:23

The LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:24

God said, “Son of man, speak to the land. 📖

You are a land that has not been cleansed.

No rain has fallen on you in the day of anger.”

## Ezekiel 22:25

The princes in Jerusalem plot together. 📖

They are like a roaring lion tearing the animal it has caught.

They devour people and seize treasures and valuable things.

They make more women in the city lose their husbands.

**Help with the lion picture:** This describes leaders violently harming people and taking what they own.

## Ezekiel 22:26

Her priests attack My law and treat My holy things as unholy. 📖

They make no difference between holy things and ordinary things.

They fail to distinguish between what is clean and what is unclean.

They ignore My Sabbaths.

So I am treated as unholy among them.

**Help with “clean” and “unclean”:** These words refer to God’s worship laws.

## Ezekiel 22:27

Her officials are like wolves tearing the animals they catch. 📖

They spill blood and destroy lives to gain money dishonestly.

## Ezekiel 22:28

Her prophets cover up these deeds with false visions and lying messages about the future. 📖

They claim, “This is what the Lord GOD says.”

But the LORD has not spoken.

**Help with “cover up”:** BSB uses the picture of whitewashing.

A coat of whitewash can hide a wall’s faults.

## Ezekiel 22:29

The people of the land force others to give them money and commit robbery. 📖

They oppress poor and needy people.

They take advantage of foreigners who live there and deny them justice.

## Ezekiel 22:30

I looked for a man among them to repair the wall. 📖

I wanted him to stand before Me in the gap on behalf of the land.

Then I would not destroy the land.

But I found no one.

**Help with this picture:** The broken wall pictures the land’s need for protection.

God looked for someone to stand before Him for the land.

## Ezekiel 22:31

So I poured My anger over them. 📖

I destroyed them with the fire of My fierce anger.

I brought the result of their own ways down on their heads, declares the Lord GOD.
