# Ezekiel 44 — The gate and the priests

**About this chapter**

God gives rules for the closed east gate and for service in the temple.

He distinguishes the duties of Levites who went astray from those of Zadok’s priestly family.

The worship rules and their exceptions are kept.

## Ezekiel 44:1

The man brought me back to the holy place’s outer gate facing east. 📖

It was shut.

## Ezekiel 44:2

The LORD told me, “This gate must stay shut. 📖

It must not be opened, and no man may enter through it.

The LORD, Israel’s God, has entered through it.

So it must stay shut.

## Ezekiel 44:3

Only the prince himself may sit inside the gateway to eat before the LORD. 📖

He must enter through the gateway’s porch and leave the same way.”

## Ezekiel 44:4

The man brought me to the temple’s front through the north gate. 📖

I saw the LORD’s glory filling His temple.

I fell with my face to the ground.

## Ezekiel 44:5

The LORD told me, “Son of man, pay attention. 📖

Look carefully and listen closely to everything I tell you about all the LORD’s temple rules and laws.

Carefully note the temple’s entrance and all the holy place’s exits.

## Ezekiel 44:6

Tell Israel’s rebellious people what the Lord GOD says. 📖

People of Israel, I have had enough of all your horrible sins.

## Ezekiel 44:7

Besides all your other horrible sins, you brought foreigners uncircumcised in heart and body to occupy My holy place. 📖

You made My temple unclean when you offered My food, the fat and blood.

You broke My covenant.

**Help with these words:** Circumcision removes the foreskin and was a sign of God’s covenant with Israel.

Being uncircumcised in heart pictures a heart that refuses to obey God.

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Ezekiel 44:8

You have not looked after My holy things. 📖

You appointed others to look after My holy place for you.

## Ezekiel 44:9

The Lord GOD says, “No foreigner uncircumcised in heart and body may enter My holy place. 📖

This includes a foreigner living among the Israelites.

## Ezekiel 44:10

The Levites wandered away from Me when Israel went astray. 📖

They left Me to follow their idols.

They will certainly bear the results of their sin.

**Help with “Levites”:** These are members of the tribe of Levi.

## Ezekiel 44:11

Yet they will serve in My holy place, looking after the temple gates and serving there. 📖

They will kill the burnt offerings and other sacrifices for the people.

They will stand before the people to serve them.

## Ezekiel 44:12

They served before their idols and became a stumbling block of sin to Israel’s people. 📖

So I raised My hand and swore an oath about them.

They will bear the results of their sin, declares the Lord GOD.

**Help with “stumbling block”:** Here it pictures something that leads people into wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 44:13

They must not approach Me to serve as priests. 📖

They must not come near any of My holy things or most holy things.

They will bear the shame of the horrible sins they committed.

## Ezekiel 44:14

Yet I will put them in charge of all the temple’s work and everything to be done there. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:15

But the Levite priests descended from Zadok looked after My holy place when the Israelites went astray from Me. 📖

They must approach Me to serve before Me.

They will stand before Me to offer fat and blood, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 44:16

They alone may enter My holy place and approach My table to serve before Me. 📖

They will carry out My instructions.

## Ezekiel 44:17

When they enter the inner courtyard’s gates, they must wear linen clothes. 📖

They must wear no wool while serving at the inner courtyard’s gates or inside the temple.

**Help with “linen”:** This is cloth made from the flax plant.

## Ezekiel 44:18

They must wear linen turbans on their heads and linen underwear around their waists. 📖

They must wear nothing that makes them sweat.

**Help with “turbans”:** These are cloth head coverings.

## Ezekiel 44:19

Before going to the people in the outer courtyard, they must take off their service clothes. 📖

They must leave them in the holy rooms and put on other clothes.

Then their clothes will not pass holiness to the people.

**About passing holiness:** The passage treats the service clothes as holy.

This is part of its worship rules.

## Ezekiel 44:20

They must not shave their heads or let their hair grow long. 📖

They must carefully trim their hair.

## Ezekiel 44:21

No priest may drink wine before entering the inner courtyard. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:22

They must not marry a widow or a divorced woman. 📖

They must marry a virgin descended from Israel’s people, or a priest’s widow.

**Help with these words:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

The second sentence keeps the stated exception for a priest’s widow.

## Ezekiel 44:23

They must teach My people the difference between the holy and the ordinary. 📖

They must show them how to tell what is clean and what is unclean.

**About “clean” and “unclean”:** These words refer to the worship laws.

## Ezekiel 44:24

In any disagreement, they must serve as judges and judge by My rules. 📖

They must keep My laws and rules about all My appointed feasts.

They must keep My Sabbaths holy.

## Ezekiel 44:25

A priest must not make himself unclean by going near a dead person. 📖

But he may do so for his father, mother, son, daughter, brother, or unmarried sister.

## Ezekiel 44:26

After he is cleansed, he must count seven days for himself. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:27

On the day he enters the holy place’s inner courtyard to serve, he must present his sin offering. 📖

This is what the Lord GOD declares.

## Ezekiel 44:28

As for their inheritance, I am their inheritance. 📖

Give them no property in Israel, because I am their possession.

**Help with this statement:** God Himself takes the place of a land inheritance for these priests.

## Ezekiel 44:29

They must eat the grain offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings. 📖

Everything in Israel devoted to the LORD will belong to them.

## Ezekiel 44:30

The best of all the firstfruits and every contribution from all your offerings will belong to the priests. 📖

Give your first batch of dough to the priest, so a blessing may rest on your homes.

**Help with “firstfruits”:** These are the first produce gathered from a harvest.

## Ezekiel 44:31

The priests may not eat any bird or animal found dead or torn by wild animals.” 📖
