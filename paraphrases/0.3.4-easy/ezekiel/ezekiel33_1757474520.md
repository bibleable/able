# Ezekiel 33 — The watchman and the fall of Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

God tells Ezekiel about his responsibility to warn Israel.

He explains the need to turn from evil.

Someone who escapes from Jerusalem brings news that the city has been captured.

## Ezekiel 33:1

The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:2

God said, “Son of man, speak to your people. 📖

Suppose I bring a sword against a land.

The people choose one of their men and appoint him as their watchman.

**Help with “watchman”:** This is someone who watches for danger and warns people.

## Ezekiel 33:3

He sees the sword coming against the land. 📖

He blows a ram’s horn to warn the people.

**Help with “ram’s horn”:** This is a male sheep’s horn used as a trumpet.

## Ezekiel 33:4

Suppose someone hears the horn but ignores the warning. 📖

The sword comes and takes his life.

He is responsible for his own blood.

**Help with “his own blood”:** This means responsibility for his own death.

## Ezekiel 33:5

He heard the horn but ignored the warning. 📖

So he is responsible for his own blood.

If he had listened to the warning, he would have saved his life.

## Ezekiel 33:6

But suppose the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the horn to warn the people. 📖

The sword comes and takes someone’s life.

That person dies in his sin.

But I will hold the watchman responsible for his blood.

## Ezekiel 33:7

Son of man, I have made you a watchman for Israel’s people. 📖

So listen to the word from My mouth.

Give them My warning.

## Ezekiel 33:8

Suppose I tell a wicked man, ‘Wicked man, you will certainly die.’ 📖

You do not speak to warn him to stop his evil ways.

That wicked man will die in his sin.

But I will hold you responsible for his blood.

## Ezekiel 33:9

But suppose you warn the wicked man to turn from his ways, and he does not turn. 📖

He will die in his sin.

But you will have saved your own life.

## Ezekiel 33:10

Now, son of man, tell Israel’s people what they have said. 📖

They said, ‘Our wrong actions and sins weigh heavily on us.

We are wasting away because of them!

How can we live?’

## Ezekiel 33:11

Tell them, ‘As surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I find no pleasure in wicked people’s death. 📖

I want them to turn from their ways and live.

Turn!

Turn from your evil ways!

People of Israel, why should you die?’

## Ezekiel 33:12

Son of man, tell your people this. 📖

A good man’s right actions will not save him on the day he does wrong.

A wicked man’s wickedness will not bring him down on the day he turns from it.

A good man cannot survive because of his past goodness on the day he sins.

## Ezekiel 33:13

Suppose I tell a good man that he will certainly live. 📖

But he trusts in his goodness and does wrong.

Then none of his right actions will be remembered.

He will die because of the wrong he has done.

## Ezekiel 33:14

But suppose I tell a wicked man, ‘You will certainly die.’ 📖

He turns from his sin and does what is fair and right.

## Ezekiel 33:15

He returns what a borrower gave him as a promise of payment. 📖

He pays back what he stole.

He follows the laws of life without doing wrong.

Then he will certainly live.

He will not die.

## Ezekiel 33:16

None of the sins he committed will be held against him. 📖

He has done what is fair and right.

He will certainly live.

## Ezekiel 33:17

Yet your people say, ‘The Lord’s way is not fair.’ 📖

But their own way is not fair.

## Ezekiel 33:18

If a good man turns from doing right and does wrong, he will die for it. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:19

But if a wicked man turns from his wickedness and does what is fair and right, he will live. 📖

He will live because of this.

## Ezekiel 33:20

Yet you say, ‘The Lord’s way is not fair.’ 📖

People of Israel, I will judge each of you by your ways.”

## Ezekiel 33:21

In the twelfth year of our exile, someone who escaped from Jerusalem came to me. 📖

It was the fifth day of the tenth month.

He reported, “The city has been captured!”

**Help with “exile”:** This was the time when we were forced to live away from our land.

## Ezekiel 33:22

The evening before the man arrived, the LORD’s hand was on me. 📖

He enabled me to speak before the man came in the morning.

My mouth was opened, and I could speak again.

**Help with “the LORD’s hand”:** This describes God’s power acting on Ezekiel.

## Ezekiel 33:23

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:24

God said, “Son of man, people living in Israel’s ruins are saying something. 📖

They say, ‘Abraham was only one man, but he owned the land.

We are many.

Surely the land has been given to us to own.’

## Ezekiel 33:25

So tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

You eat meat with blood in it.

You look up to your idols and spill blood.

Should you own the land?

## Ezekiel 33:26

You have relied on your swords. 📖

You have done horrible things.

Each of you has defiled his neighbor’s wife.

Should you own the land?

**Help with “defiled”:** Here this refers to sexual wrongdoing with a neighbor’s wife.

## Ezekiel 33:27

Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

As surely as I live, those in the ruins will be killed with swords.

I will give those in the open field to wild animals to eat.

Those in defended places and caves will die from deadly disease.

## Ezekiel 33:28

I will make the land empty and ruined. 📖

Its proud strength will end.

The mountains of Israel will be empty, so no one will pass through.

## Ezekiel 33:29

I will make the land empty and ruined because of all the horrible sins they have committed. 📖

Then they will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 33:30

Son of man, your people talk about you near the city walls and in their doorways. 📖

Each says to his brother, ‘Come and hear the message that has come from the LORD!’

## Ezekiel 33:31

So My people come to you as usual and sit before you. 📖

They hear your words, but do not do what you say.

Their mouths express love, but their hearts seek dishonest gain.

## Ezekiel 33:32

To them, you are like a singer of love songs with a beautiful voice. 📖

You are like someone who plays an instrument skillfully.

They hear your words, but do not do what you say.

## Ezekiel 33:33

When it happens, they will know that a prophet has been among them. 📖

It will certainly happen!”
