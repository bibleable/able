# Ezekiel 5 — Hair divided as a sign

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Ezekiel to divide his shaved hair.

The three portions picture different judgments against Jerusalem, with a few strands kept and some of those burned.

## Ezekiel 5:1

“Son of man, take a sharp sword and use it as a barber’s razor. 📖

Shave your head and beard, then use scales to divide the hair.

## Ezekiel 5:2

“After the siege days, burn a third of the hair inside the city. 📖

Slash another third with the sword around the city and scatter a third to the wind.

I will send a sword after them.

## Ezekiel 5:3

“But take a few hairs and secure them in your clothing’s folds. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:4

“Take a few of these again and throw them into the fire to burn. 📖

From there, fire will spread to Israel’s whole house.

## Ezekiel 5:5

“The Lord GOD says, ‘This is Jerusalem, which I placed among the nations with countries surrounding her. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:6

“‘She rebelled against My laws more wickedly than the nations and surrounding countries. 📖

Her people rejected My rules and did not follow My commands.’

## Ezekiel 5:7

“The Lord GOD says, ‘You were more disobedient than the surrounding nations. 📖

You did not follow My commands or keep My rules.

You did not even follow the surrounding nations’ rules.’

## Ezekiel 5:8

“The Lord GOD says, ‘Jerusalem, I Myself oppose you and will carry out judgments before the nations. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:9

“‘Because of all your disgusting practices, I will do what I never did before and will never do again. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:10

“‘Fathers among you will eat sons, and sons will eat fathers. 📖

I will judge you and scatter all your survivors to every wind.’

**About this difficult warning**

It describes cannibalism, eating human flesh.

The severe detail is retained.

## Ezekiel 5:11

The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, you polluted My holy place with detestable idols and disgusting acts. 📖

So I Myself will withdraw My favor, show no pity, and not spare you.

## Ezekiel 5:12

“A third will die from plague or famine within you, and a third by swords outside your walls. 📖

I will scatter a third to every wind and send a sword after them.

**Help with plague and famine**

A plague is a widespread, deadly disease.

Famine is a severe shortage of food.

## Ezekiel 5:13

“When I pour out all My anger and fury against them, I will be satisfied. 📖

Then they will know that I, the LORD, spoke in My fierce determination.

## Ezekiel 5:14

“I will make you ruined and disgraced among surrounding nations, before everyone passing. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:15

“When I judge in anger, wrath, and raging fury, you will be insulted and mocked. 📖

You will be a warning and horror to surrounding nations.

I, the LORD, have spoken.

## Ezekiel 5:16

“I will shower you with deadly arrows of famine and destruction to destroy you. 📖

I will make famine worse and cut off your food supply.

## Ezekiel 5:17

“I will send famine and wild animals to leave you childless. 📖

Plague and bloodshed will sweep through you, and I will bring a sword.

I, the LORD, have spoken.”
