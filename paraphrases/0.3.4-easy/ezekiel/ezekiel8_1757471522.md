# Ezekiel 8 — A vision of false worship in Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel is taken to Jerusalem in visions of God.

He sees different acts of worship that the Lord condemns, each followed by a warning of worse acts.

## Ezekiel 8:1

It was the sixth year, the fifth day of the sixth month. 📖

I sat at home with Judah’s elders before me, and the Lord GOD’s hand fell on me there.

## Ezekiel 8:2

I saw a figure like a man. 📖

From His waist down, He looked like fire.

From His waist up, He shone like amber.

## Ezekiel 8:3

He stretched something like a hand and took me by my hair. 📖

The Spirit lifted me between earth and heaven and carried me in visions of God to Jerusalem.

I reached the inner court’s north-gate entrance, where the idol provoking jealousy sat.

## Ezekiel 8:4

There I saw Israel’s God’s glory, like the vision I saw on the plain. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:5

He said, “Son of man, look north.” 📖

I looked north and saw the jealousy-provoking idol in the entrance north of the Altar Gate.

## Ezekiel 8:6

He said, “Son of man, do you see Israel’s house’s great disgusting acts? 📖

They drive Me far from My holy place.

Yet you will see still greater disgusting acts.”

## Ezekiel 8:7

He brought me to the court entrance, where I saw a hole in the wall. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:8

He said, “Son of man, dig through the wall.” 📖

I dug and found a doorway.

## Ezekiel 8:9

He said, “Enter and see their wicked, disgusting acts here.” 📖

## Ezekiel 8:10

I entered and looked. 📖

Every kind of crawling creature and detestable animal was engraved around the wall, with all Israel’s house’s idols.

## Ezekiel 8:11

Seventy elders of Israel’s house stood before them, including Jaazaniah son of Shaphan. 📖

Each held a censer, and a sweet-smelling cloud of incense rose.

**Help with censer and incense**

A censer holds incense, a substance burned to give a sweet smell.

## Ezekiel 8:12

He said, “Son of man, do you see Israel’s elders acting in darkness, each at his own idol’s shrine? 📖

They say, ‘The LORD does not see us.

The LORD abandoned the land.’”

**Help with shrine**

A shrine is a place of worship.

## Ezekiel 8:13

He told me again, “You will see them doing still greater disgusting acts.” 📖

## Ezekiel 8:14

He brought me to the LORD’s house’s north-gate entrance. 📖

Women sat there weeping for Tammuz.

**Help with Tammuz**

Tammuz is the name of a god being mourned in this scene.

## Ezekiel 8:15

He said, “Son of man, do you see this? 📖

You will see even greater disgusting acts.”

## Ezekiel 8:16

He brought me to the LORD’s house’s inner court. 📖

At the temple entrance between the porch and altar stood about twenty-five men.

Their backs faced the LORD’s temple and their faces east.

They bowed east to worship the sun.

## Ezekiel 8:17

He said, “Son of man, do you see this? 📖

Is Judah’s house not content with these disgusting acts?

Must they also fill the land with violence and keep angering Me?

Look, they even put the branch to their nose!

**About the branch**

The gesture is retained without assigning it a single certain meaning.

## Ezekiel 8:18

“So I will answer in fury, show no pity, and not spare them. 📖

Though they shout loudly in My ears, I will not listen.”
