# Ezekiel 4 — A sign of Jerusalem’s siege

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel is commanded to act out a siege and eat measured food and water.

The chapter keeps the exact days, foods, and measures given in BSB.

## Ezekiel 4:1

“Son of man, take a brick, set it before you, and draw Jerusalem on it. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:2

“Surround it as in a siege. 📖

Build a siege wall and ramp, set up camps, and place battering rams around every side.

**Help with siege and battering rams**

A siege is an army surrounding a place to capture it.

Battering rams are heavy objects used to break walls or gates.

## Ezekiel 4:3

“Place an iron plate as a wall between you and the city. 📖

Face it and surround it as in a siege.

This will be a sign to Israel’s house.

## Ezekiel 4:4

“Lie on your left side and put Israel’s house’s wrongdoing on yourself. 📖

Bear their wrongdoing for the number of days you lie on your side.

## Ezekiel 4:5

“I assign you 390 days for the years of their wrongdoing. 📖

You will bear Israel’s house’s wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 4:6

“After these days, lie on your right side and bear Judah’s house’s wrongdoing. 📖

I assign forty days, one day for each year.

## Ezekiel 4:7

“Face Jerusalem’s siege with your arm uncovered and prophesy against it. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:8

“I will tie you with ropes so you cannot turn sides until your siege days are finished. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:9

“Take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt in one container and make bread. 📖

Eat this during the 390 days lying on your side.

**Help with these foods**

They are grains and other edible seeds.

## Ezekiel 4:10

“Weigh twenty shekels of food each day and eat at set times. 📖

**Help with shekel**

A shekel is a unit of weight.

## Ezekiel 4:11

“Measure a sixth of a hin of drinking water and drink at set times. 📖

**Help with hin**

A hin is a unit for measuring liquids.

The source amount is retained without conversion.

## Ezekiel 4:12

“Eat it like a barley cake, baked over dried human excrement where people can see.” 📖

**Help with excrement**

Excrement means body waste.

Here it is named as fuel for baking, not an ingredient in the bread.

## Ezekiel 4:13

The LORD said, “This is how Israelites will eat polluted bread among the nations where I drive them.” 📖

## Ezekiel 4:14

I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! 📖

I never polluted myself.

Since youth, I have not eaten anything found dead or torn by wild animals.

No unclean meat entered my mouth.”

**Help with polluted and unclean**

These terms concern food and worship requirements.

They do not mean ordinary illness or disability is moral wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 4:15

He replied, “I will let you bake bread over cow dung instead of human excrement.” 📖

## Ezekiel 4:16

He told me, “Son of man, I will cut off Jerusalem’s food supply. 📖

They will anxiously eat bread weighed in portions and despairingly drink measured water.

## Ezekiel 4:17

“They will lack food and water and be horrified to see each other wasting away in wrongdoing.” 📖
