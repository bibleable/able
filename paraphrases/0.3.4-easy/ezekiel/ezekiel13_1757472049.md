# Ezekiel 13 — False prophets and false promises of peace

**About this chapter**

The LORD condemns prophets speaking from their own imaginations.

The chapter uses a collapsing wall and trapped birds to describe deception and rescue.

## Ezekiel 13:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:2

“Son of man, prophesy against Israel’s prophets now speaking from their own imaginations. 📖

Tell them to hear the LORD’s word!

## Ezekiel 13:3

“The Lord GOD says, ‘How terrible for foolish prophets following their own spirit when they saw nothing! 📖

## Ezekiel 13:4

“‘Israel, your prophets are like foxes among ruins. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:5

“‘You did not go to the gaps or repair Israel’s house’s wall. 📖

You did not prepare it to stand in battle on the Day of the LORD.

**Help with the wall**

The image describes the prophets’ failure to prepare and protect the people.

The Day of the LORD names a time when God acts in judgment.

## Ezekiel 13:6

“‘They see false visions and tell lying fortunes. 📖

They say, “The LORD declares,” though He did not send them.

Yet they expect their message to come true.

## Ezekiel 13:7

“‘Did you not see a false vision and tell a lying fortune? 📖

You say, “The LORD declares,” though I did not speak.

## Ezekiel 13:8

The Lord GOD declares, “You spoke empty words and saw false visions, so I oppose you. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:9

“My hand will oppose prophets seeing false visions and telling lying fortunes. 📖

They will not belong to My people’s council or be recorded in Israel’s house’s register.

They will not enter Israel’s land.

Then you will know I am the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 13:10

“They misled My people, saying ‘Peace’ when there is no peace. 📖

They covered any weak wall that was built with whitewash,

**Help with whitewash**

Whitewash is a coating that makes a surface look clean or sound.

Here it pictures false reassurance that hides weakness.

## Ezekiel 13:11

“so tell those coating it that it will fall. 📖

Heavy rain will come, I will send falling hailstones, and a windstorm will burst out.

## Ezekiel 13:12

“When the wall falls, you surely will not be asked, ‘Where is the whitewash you covered it with?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 13:13

“The Lord GOD says, ‘In My wrath, I will release a windstorm. 📖

In My anger, rain and hail will fall with destructive fury.

## Ezekiel 13:14

“‘I will tear down your whitewashed wall, level it, and expose its foundation. 📖

The city will fall, and you will be destroyed within it.

Then you will know I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 13:15

“‘After I pour My fury on the wall and those coating it, I will say this. 📖

‘The wall and its whitewashers are gone,

## Ezekiel 13:16

The Lord GOD declares, “‘“those Israelite prophets who promised Jerusalem peace in a vision when there was no peace.”’ 📖

## Ezekiel 13:17

“Son of man, face your people’s daughters who prophesy from their own imaginations. 📖

Prophesy against them,

## Ezekiel 13:18

“and tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘How terrible for women sewing magic charms on wrists and making head veils for people of every height!

They do this to trap souls.

Will you trap My people’s souls but keep yourselves alive?

**Help with the charms and veils**

These are objects used in the practices being condemned.

The passage addresses these deceptive practices, not all women who speak as prophets.

## Ezekiel 13:19

“‘You dishonored Me among My people for handfuls of barley and scraps of bread. 📖

You lied to people willing to listen, killing those who should not die and sparing those who should not live.

## Ezekiel 13:20

“‘The Lord GOD says, “I oppose the magic charms with which you trap souls like birds. 📖

I will tear them from your arms and free the souls you trapped like birds.

## Ezekiel 13:21

“‘“I will tear off your veils and rescue My people from your hands. 📖

They will no longer be prey in your hands.

Then you will know I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 13:22

“‘“Your lies discouraged the righteous, though I caused them no grief. 📖

You encouraged wicked people not to turn from evil and save their lives,

## Ezekiel 13:23

“‘“so you will no longer see false visions or tell fortunes. 📖

I will rescue My people from your hands.

Then you will know I am the LORD.”’”
