# Ezekiel 32 — Songs of grief for Pharaoh and Egypt

**About this chapter**

God gives Ezekiel songs of grief for Pharaoh and Egypt.

The pictures include a captured monster, darkness, and nations in the place of the dead.

The repeated descriptions of death and disgrace are kept.

## Ezekiel 32:1

The LORD spoke to me in the twelfth year, on the first day of the twelfth month. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:2

God said, “Son of man, sing a song of grief for Pharaoh, king of Egypt. 📖

Tell him, ‘You are like a lion among the nations and like a monster in the seas.

You thrash around in your rivers.

Your feet stir up the water and make the streams muddy.’

## Ezekiel 32:3

The Lord GOD says, “I will spread My net over you with a crowd from many peoples. 📖

They will pull you up in My net.

## Ezekiel 32:4

I will leave you on the land and throw you into the open field. 📖

I will make all the birds of the air settle on you.

All the animals of the earth will eat their fill of you.

## Ezekiel 32:5

I will put your flesh on the mountains and fill the valleys with your remains. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:6

I will soak the land with your flowing blood, all the way to the mountains. 📖

The deep valleys will be filled.

## Ezekiel 32:7

When I put out your light, I will cover the sky and darken its stars. 📖

I will cover the sun with a cloud.

The moon will not give its light.

## Ezekiel 32:8

I will darken all the shining lights in the sky above you. 📖

I will bring darkness over your land, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 32:9

I will trouble the hearts of many peoples when I bring your destruction among the nations. 📖

This will happen in countries you do not know.

## Ezekiel 32:10

I will make many peoples horrified at what happens to you. 📖

Their kings will shake with horror because of you when I wave My sword before them.

On the day you fall, each will tremble every moment for his life.

## Ezekiel 32:11

The Lord GOD says, “The sword of Babylon’s king will come against you! 📖

## Ezekiel 32:12

I will make your great crowds fall by the swords of mighty men from the cruelest of all nations. 📖

They will destroy Egypt’s proud strength.

All her great crowds will be destroyed.

## Ezekiel 32:13

I will kill all her cattle beside the plentiful waters. 📖

No human foot will make the waters muddy again.

No cattle hoof will disturb them.

## Ezekiel 32:14

Then I will let her waters settle. 📖

I will make her rivers flow like oil, declares the Lord GOD.

**Help with “like oil”:** This pictures the water flowing smoothly.

## Ezekiel 32:15

I will make Egypt an empty, ruined land. 📖

I will empty it of everything that filled it and strike down all who live there.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 32:16

This is the song of grief they will sing for her. 📖

The daughters of the nations will sing it.

They will sing it over Egypt and all her great crowds, declares the Lord GOD.”

**About “daughters of the nations”:** This describes women connected with the nations who mourn for Egypt.

## Ezekiel 32:17

The LORD spoke to me in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month. 📖

**About the date:** This verse does not name the month.

## Ezekiel 32:18

God said, “Son of man, cry out in grief for Egypt’s great crowds. 📖

Send her and the daughters of the mighty nations down to the depths of the earth.

They will be with those who go down to the Pit.

**Help with “the Pit”:** This pictures the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 32:19

Whose beauty do you surpass? 📖

Go down and lie with the uncircumcised!

**Help with “uncircumcised”:** This means males whose foreskin has not been removed.

Circumcision was a sign of God’s covenant with Israel.

Here the term is part of the picture of shame in death.

## Ezekiel 32:20

They will fall among those killed with swords. 📖

The sword has been appointed!

Let them drag her away, together with all her great crowds.

## Ezekiel 32:21

Mighty leaders will speak from within Sheol about Egypt and her supporters. 📖

They will say, ‘They have come down and lie with the uncircumcised, with those killed with swords.’

**Help with “Sheol”:** This is a name for the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 32:22

Assyria is there with her whole company. 📖

Her graves are all around her.

All of them were killed, struck down with swords.

## Ezekiel 32:23

Her graves are in the depths of the Pit. 📖

Her company is all around her grave.

All were killed, struck down with swords.

They once spread terror in the land of the living.

## Ezekiel 32:24

Elam is there with all her great crowds around her grave. 📖

All were killed, struck down with swords.

They went down uncircumcised to the earth below.

They once spread terror in the land of the living.

They bear their disgrace with those who go down to the Pit.

## Ezekiel 32:25

A resting place is prepared among the dead for Elam and all her great crowds. 📖

Her graves are all around her.

All are uncircumcised, killed with swords, although they once spread terror in the land of the living.

They bear their disgrace with those who go down to the Pit.

They are placed among the dead.

## Ezekiel 32:26

Meshech and Tubal are there with all their great crowds. 📖

Their graves are all around them.

All are uncircumcised, killed with swords, because they spread terror in the land of the living.

## Ezekiel 32:27

They do not lie with the fallen soldiers of long ago who went down to Sheol with their weapons. 📖

Those soldiers had swords placed under their heads and shields resting on their bones.

They had once brought the terror of the mighty into the land of the living.

## Ezekiel 32:28

But you too will be shattered. 📖

You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed with swords.

## Ezekiel 32:29

Edom is there, with all her kings and princes. 📖

Despite their might, they are laid among those killed with swords.

They lie with the uncircumcised, with those who go down to the Pit.

## Ezekiel 32:30

All the leaders of the north and all the Sidonians are there. 📖

They went down in disgrace with the dead, despite their terrifying might.

They lie uncircumcised with those killed with swords.

They bear their shame with those who go down to the Pit.

## Ezekiel 32:31

Pharaoh will see them and be comforted about all his great crowds. 📖

Pharaoh and all his army will be killed with swords, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 32:32

I will spread My terror in the land of the living. 📖

Then Pharaoh and all his great crowds will be laid to rest among the uncircumcised.

They will be with those killed with swords, declares the Lord GOD.”
