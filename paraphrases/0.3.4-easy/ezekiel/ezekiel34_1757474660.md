# Ezekiel 34 — God cares for His flock

**About this chapter**

God compares Israel’s leaders to shepherds who fail to care for their sheep.

He promises to gather and care for His people Himself.

He also announces judgment between the sheep and promises one shepherd, His servant David.

## Ezekiel 34:1

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:2

God said, “Son of man, speak My message against Israel’s shepherds. 📖

Tell them what the Lord GOD says.

How terrible for Israel’s shepherds who feed only themselves!

Should shepherds not feed their flock?

**Help with this picture:** The shepherds picture Israel’s leaders.

The flock pictures the people in their care.

## Ezekiel 34:3

You eat the fat, wear the wool, and kill the well-fed sheep. 📖

But you do not feed the flock.

## Ezekiel 34:4

You have not strengthened the weak or healed the sick. 📖

You have not bandaged the injured or brought back those who wandered away.

You have not searched for the lost.

Instead, you have ruled them with violence and cruelty.

## Ezekiel 34:5

They were scattered because they had no shepherd. 📖

When they were scattered, they became food for all the wild animals.

## Ezekiel 34:6

My flock wandered on all the mountains and every high hill. 📖

They were scattered over the whole earth.

No one searched or looked for them.

## Ezekiel 34:7

So, shepherds, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:8

As surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, My flock has no shepherd. 📖

They have become prey and food for every wild animal.

My shepherds did not search for My flock.

Instead, they fed themselves.

## Ezekiel 34:9

So, shepherds, hear the LORD’s word! 📖

## Ezekiel 34:10

The Lord GOD says, “Look! 📖

I am against the shepherds.

I will demand My flock back from them.

I will stop them caring for the flock, so they can no longer feed themselves.

I will rescue My flock from their mouths.

My flock will no longer be food for them.

## Ezekiel 34:11

The Lord GOD says, “Look! 📖

I Myself will search and look for My flock.

## Ezekiel 34:12

A shepherd among his flock looks for his scattered sheep. 📖

In the same way, I will look for My flock.

I will rescue them from every place where they were scattered on a cloudy, dark day.

## Ezekiel 34:13

I will bring them out from the peoples and gather them from the countries. 📖

I will bring them into their own land.

I will feed them on Israel’s mountains, in its deep valleys, and in all the land’s settlements.

## Ezekiel 34:14

I will feed them on good grassland. 📖

The high mountains of Israel will be their feeding ground.

There they will lie down on good grassland.

They will feed on rich grassland on Israel’s mountains.

## Ezekiel 34:15

I will care for My flock and make them lie down, declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:16

I will seek the lost and bring back those who wandered away. 📖

I will bandage the injured and strengthen the weak.

But I will destroy the well-fed and strong.

I will shepherd them with justice.

**About this judgment:** The next verses describe sheep that harm the rest of the flock.

## Ezekiel 34:17

The Lord GOD says to you, My flock, “I will judge between one sheep and another. 📖

I will judge between the male sheep and the goats.

## Ezekiel 34:18

Is feeding on good grassland not enough for you? 📖

Must you also crush the rest of the grass under your feet?

Is drinking clear water not enough for you?

Must you also make the rest muddy with your feet?

## Ezekiel 34:19

Why must My flock eat grass that your feet have crushed? 📖

Why must they drink water that your feet have made muddy?”

## Ezekiel 34:20

So the Lord GOD says to them, “Look! 📖

I Myself will judge between the fat sheep and the thin sheep.

## Ezekiel 34:21

You push with your sides and shoulders. 📖

You strike all the weak ones with your horns until you have scattered them far away.

## Ezekiel 34:22

So I will save My flock. 📖

They will no longer be prey.

I will judge between one sheep and another.

## Ezekiel 34:23

I will appoint one shepherd over them, My servant David. 📖

He will feed them.

He will feed them and be their shepherd.

## Ezekiel 34:24

I, the LORD, will be their God. 📖

My servant David will be a prince among them.

I, the LORD, have spoken.

## Ezekiel 34:25

I will make a covenant of peace with them. 📖

I will remove wild animals from the land.

Then they can live safely in the wilderness and sleep in the forest.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Ezekiel 34:26

I will make them and the places around My hill a blessing. 📖

I will send rain at the right times.

These will be showers of blessing.

## Ezekiel 34:27

The trees of the field will give fruit. 📖

The land will produce crops.

My flock will be safe in their land.

I will break the bars of their yoke and rescue them from those who enslaved them.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.

**Help with “yoke”:** This is a wooden frame used to control working animals.

Here it pictures slavery.

## Ezekiel 34:28

They will no longer be prey for the nations. 📖

The animals of the earth will not eat them.

They will live safely, and no one will frighten them.

## Ezekiel 34:29

I will grow a garden for them that becomes famous. 📖

They will no longer suffer famine in the land.

They will no longer bear the scorn of the nations.

**Help with “famine”:** This is a time when there is not enough food.

## Ezekiel 34:30

Then they will know that I, the LORD their God, am with them. 📖

They, the people of Israel, are My people, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 34:31

You are My flock, the sheep of My grassland, My people. 📖

I am your God, declares the Lord GOD.”
