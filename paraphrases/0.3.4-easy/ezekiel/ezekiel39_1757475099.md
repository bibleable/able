# Ezekiel 39 — Gog’s defeat and Israel’s restoration

**About this chapter**

God announces Gog’s defeat and gives details about burning weapons and burying the dead.

Birds and animals are called to a terrible feast.

God also promises to gather His people and pour out His Spirit.

## Ezekiel 39:1

God said, “Son of man, speak My message against Gog. 📖

Announce what the Lord GOD says.

Look, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal!

I am against you.

## Ezekiel 39:2

I will turn you around and drive you along. 📖

I will bring you from the far north and send you against Israel’s mountains.

## Ezekiel 39:3

Then I will knock the bow from your left hand. 📖

I will knock the arrows from your right hand.

## Ezekiel 39:4

You will fall on Israel’s mountains, with all your troops and the nations with you. 📖

I will give you as food to every kind of hungry bird and wild animal.

## Ezekiel 39:5

You will fall in the open field. 📖

I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 39:6

I will send fire on Magog and those who live safely in the coastlands. 📖

They will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 39:7

I will make My holy name known among My people Israel. 📖

I will no longer allow it to be treated as unholy.

Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel.

## Ezekiel 39:8

Yes, it is coming. 📖

It will certainly happen, declares the Lord GOD.

This is the day I spoke about.

## Ezekiel 39:9

Then the people in Israel’s cities will go out and light fires to burn the weapons. 📖

They will burn small shields, large shields, bows, arrows, clubs, and spears.

They will use them as fuel for seven years.

## Ezekiel 39:10

They will not gather wood from the countryside or cut it from the forests. 📖

They will use the weapons as fuel.

They will rob those who robbed them and seize goods from those who seized theirs, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 39:11

On that day, I will give Gog a burial place in Israel. 📖

It will be in the Valley of the Travelers, east of the Sea.

Gog and all his great crowds will be buried there, blocking travelers’ way.

So it will be called the Valley of Hamon-gog.

## Ezekiel 39:12

Israel’s people will spend seven months burying them to cleanse the land. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:13

All the people of the land will bury them. 📖

This will bring them honor on the day I show My glory, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 39:14

Men will be employed to pass through the land continually. 📖

They will cleanse it by burying the attackers left on the ground.

At the end of the seven months, they will begin their search.

## Ezekiel 39:15

As they pass through the land, anyone seeing a human bone will put a marker beside it. 📖

It will stay until the gravediggers bury the bone in the Valley of Hamon-gog.

## Ezekiel 39:16

Even the city will be named Hamonah. 📖

In this way, they will cleanse the land.

## Ezekiel 39:17

Son of man, the Lord GOD says this. 📖

Call to every kind of bird and every animal of the field.

Say, ‘Gather together and come from all around to the sacrificial feast I am preparing for you.

It is a great feast on Israel’s mountains.

There you will eat flesh and drink blood.’

**About this feast:** The passage pictures the defeated army as a sacrifice eaten by birds and animals.

## Ezekiel 39:18

You will eat the flesh of mighty men and drink the blood of the earth’s princes. 📖

They will be like male sheep, lambs, goats, and bulls, all the well-fed animals of Bashan.

## Ezekiel 39:19

At the sacrifice I am preparing, you will eat fat until you are completely full. 📖

You will drink blood until you are drunk.

## Ezekiel 39:20

At My table, you will eat your fill of horses and riders. 📖

You will eat mighty men and soldiers of every kind, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 39:21

I will show My glory among the nations. 📖

All the nations will see the judgment I carry out and the hand I lay on them.

**Help with “the hand”:** This describes God’s power acting against them.

## Ezekiel 39:22

From that day onward, Israel’s people will know that I am the LORD their God. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:23

The nations will know that Israel’s people went into exile because of their sin. 📖

They were unfaithful to Me.

So I hid My face from them and handed them over to their enemies.

They all fell by the sword.

**Help with these words:** Exile means being forced to live away from their land.

Hiding His face pictures God withdrawing His favor and protection.

## Ezekiel 39:24

I dealt with them according to their uncleanness and wrong actions. 📖

I hid My face from them.

## Ezekiel 39:25

So the Lord GOD says, “Now I will bring Jacob back from captivity. 📖

I will have compassion on the whole people of Israel.

I will be jealous for My holy name.

## Ezekiel 39:26

They will live safely in their land, with no one to frighten them. 📖

Then they will forget their disgrace and all their acts of betrayal against Me.

## Ezekiel 39:27

I will bring them back from the peoples and gather them from their enemies’ lands. 📖

Then I will show My holiness through them before many nations.

## Ezekiel 39:28

I sent them into exile among the nations. 📖

When I gather them back to their own land, I will leave none of them behind.

Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.

## Ezekiel 39:29

I will no longer hide My face from them. 📖

I will pour out My Spirit on Israel’s people, declares the Lord GOD.”
