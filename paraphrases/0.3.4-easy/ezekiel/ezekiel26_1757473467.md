# Ezekiel 26 — Judgment against Tyre

**About this chapter**

Tyre celebrates Jerusalem’s fall.

God announces that Tyre will also be destroyed.

The rulers of the sea will mourn for the city.

## Ezekiel 26:1

The LORD spoke to me in the twelfth year, on the first day of the eleventh month. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:2

God said, “Son of man, Tyre has spoken about Jerusalem. 📖

She said, ‘Aha!

The gate to the nations is broken.

It has swung open to me.

Now that Jerusalem lies in ruins, I will be filled.’

**Help with this picture:** Tyre expects to benefit from Jerusalem’s destruction.

## Ezekiel 26:3

So the Lord GOD says, “Look, Tyre! 📖

I am against you.

I will bring many nations against you, like waves rising from the sea.

## Ezekiel 26:4

They will destroy Tyre’s walls and tear down her towers. 📖

I will scrape away her soil and make her a bare rock.

## Ezekiel 26:5

She will become a place in the sea where nets are spread. 📖

I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

The nations will rob her.

## Ezekiel 26:6

The people in her villages on the mainland will be killed with swords. 📖

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 26:7

The Lord GOD says, “Look! 📖

From the north I will bring Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, against Tyre.

He is king of kings.

He will come with horses, chariots, soldiers on horses, and a great army.

**Help with “chariots”:** These were vehicles pulled by horses and used in war.

## Ezekiel 26:8

He will kill the people of your mainland villages with swords. 📖

He will build structures to attack you and a ramp to reach your walls.

He will raise his shields against you.

## Ezekiel 26:9

He will strike your walls with heavy beams used to break defenses. 📖

He will tear down your towers with his axes.

**Help with the beams:** BSB calls these “battering rams.”

## Ezekiel 26:10

His many horses will cover you with dust. 📖

He will enter your gates like an army entering a city whose walls have been broken through.

Your walls will shake at the noise of horsemen, wagons, and chariots.

## Ezekiel 26:11

His horses’ hooves will trample all your streets. 📖

He will kill your people with swords.

Your mighty pillars will fall to the ground.

## Ezekiel 26:12

They will steal your wealth and take the goods you sell. 📖

They will tear down your walls and your beautiful homes.

They will throw your stones, wood, and soil into the water.

## Ezekiel 26:13

I will silence the sound of your songs. 📖

The music of your lyres will no longer be heard.

**Help with “lyres”:** These are musical instruments with strings.

## Ezekiel 26:14

I will make you a bare rock. 📖

You will become a place for spreading fishing nets.

You will never be rebuilt.

I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.”

## Ezekiel 26:15

The Lord GOD says to Tyre, “Will the coastlands not shake at the sound of your fall? 📖

Wounded people will groan while killing takes place inside you.

## Ezekiel 26:16

All the princes of the sea will come down from their thrones. 📖

They will remove their robes and their clothes decorated with stitching.

Terror will cover them like clothes.

They will sit on the ground, trembling every moment.

They will be horrified at what has happened to you.

## Ezekiel 26:17

Then they will sing a song of grief for you. 📖

They will say, ‘You famous city, how you have been destroyed!

You were full of people who sailed the seas.

You and your people were powerful on the sea.

You terrified all peoples.

## Ezekiel 26:18

Now the coastlands tremble on the day you fall. 📖

The islands in the sea are filled with fear at your end.’

## Ezekiel 26:19

The Lord GOD says, “I will make you an empty city, like other deserted cities. 📖

I will bring the deep sea against you, so its mighty waters cover you.

## Ezekiel 26:20

Then I will bring you down with those who go down to the Pit, to people of long ago. 📖

I will make you live in the earth below, like ancient ruins.

You will be with those who go down to the Pit.

No one will live in you again.

You will have no place of beauty in the land of the living.

**Help with “the Pit”:** This pictures the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 26:21

I will make you something that horrifies people. 📖

You will be no more.

People will look for you, but you will never be found, declares the Lord GOD.”
