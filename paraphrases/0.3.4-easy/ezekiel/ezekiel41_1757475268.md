# Ezekiel 41 — Inside the temple

**About this chapter**

The man measures the temple’s holy rooms, side rooms, walls, and nearby building.

Ezekiel describes the carved figures, windows, doors, and wooden altar.

The measurements keep the source’s cubits.

## Ezekiel 41:1

The man brought me into the outer holy room. 📖

He measured the side pillars as six cubits wide on each side.

## Ezekiel 41:2

The entrance was ten cubits wide. 📖

Its sides were five cubits on each side.

He measured the outer holy room as forty cubits long and twenty cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 41:3

He went into the inner holy room. 📖

He measured the side pillars at its entrance as two cubits wide.

The entrance was six cubits wide.

The walls on each side were seven cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 41:4

He measured the room next to the inner holy room as twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. 📖

He told me, “This is the Most Holy Place.”

**Help with “Most Holy Place”:** This is the temple’s most sacred area.

## Ezekiel 41:5

Next, he measured the temple wall as six cubits thick. 📖

Each side room around the temple was four cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 41:6

The side rooms were arranged in three levels, one above another, with thirty rooms on each level. 📖

Ledges around the temple wall supported the side rooms.

So their supports were not fastened into the temple wall itself.

## Ezekiel 41:7

The side rooms around the temple became wider at each higher level. 📖

The structure rose in stages as the temple wall became narrower higher up.

A stairway went from the lowest level through the middle level to the highest.

## Ezekiel 41:8

I saw a raised base all around the temple, forming the foundation of the side rooms. 📖

It measured a full rod, six long cubits.

**Help with the measurements:** A long cubit is one cubit plus a handbreadth.

A cubit measures from elbow to fingertips.

A handbreadth is the width of a hand.

## Ezekiel 41:9

The outer wall of the side rooms was five cubits thick. 📖

The open area between the temple’s side rooms

## Ezekiel 41:10

and the outer rooms was twenty cubits wide all around the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:11

The side rooms opened into this area. 📖

One entrance was on the north and another on the south.

The open area was five cubits wide all around.

## Ezekiel 41:12

The building facing the temple courtyard on the west was seventy cubits wide. 📖

Its wall was five cubits thick all around.

Its length was ninety cubits.

## Ezekiel 41:13

He measured the temple as a hundred cubits long. 📖

The temple courtyard and the building with its walls were also a hundred cubits long.

## Ezekiel 41:14

The temple courtyard’s width on the east, including the temple’s front, was a hundred cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:15

He measured the building facing the courtyard behind the temple, including its covered walkways on each side. 📖

It was a hundred cubits long.

The outer holy room, inner holy room, and porches facing the courtyard,

## Ezekiel 41:16

with the entrance floors, windows with sloping edges, and surrounding covered walkways, were covered with wood on all sides. 📖

The walkways had three levels opposite the entrance floor.

Wood panels covered the walls from the ground to the windows.

The windows were covered.

**Help with these words:** BSB calls the entrance floors “thresholds” and the windows “beveled.”

It calls the covered walkways “galleries” and the porches “porticoes.”

## Ezekiel 41:17

Above the outside of the inner holy room’s entrance, on all the walls, there were evenly spaced carvings. 📖

They went around the inner and outer holy rooms,

## Ezekiel 41:18

with cherubim and palm trees taking turns. 📖

Each cherub had two faces.

**Help with “cherubim”:** These are heavenly beings.

“Cherub” means one of them.

## Ezekiel 41:19

The human face looked toward the palm tree on one side. 📖

The young lion’s face looked toward the palm tree on the other side.

They were carved all around the temple.

## Ezekiel 41:20

Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the outer holy room’s wall. 📖

They stretched from the floor to the space above the entrance.

## Ezekiel 41:21

The outer holy room had a rectangular doorframe. 📖

The holy place’s doorframe was similar.

## Ezekiel 41:22

There was a wooden altar three cubits high and two cubits square. 📖

Its corners, base, and sides were wood.

The man told me, “This is the table before the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 41:23

Both the outer and inner holy rooms had double doors, 📖

## Ezekiel 41:24

and each door had two swinging panels. 📖

One door had two panels, and the other had two.

## Ezekiel 41:25

Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the outer holy room’s doors, like those on the walls. 📖

Outside, on the porch’s front, was a wooden covering overhead.

## Ezekiel 41:26

The porch’s sidewalls had windows with sloping edges and palm trees. 📖

The temple’s side rooms also had coverings overhead.
