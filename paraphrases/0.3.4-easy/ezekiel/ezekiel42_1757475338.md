# Ezekiel 42 — The holy rooms and outer boundary

**About this chapter**

The man shows Ezekiel rooms used by priests.

He explains where the priests eat holy offerings and change their clothes.

He measures the temple area’s outer boundary.

## Ezekiel 42:1

The man led me north into the outer courtyard. 📖

He brought me to the group of rooms on the north side.

They were opposite the temple courtyard and outer wall.

## Ezekiel 42:2

The building with the door facing north was a hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:3

Covered walkways faced each other on three levels. 📖

They faced the twenty-cubit area belonging to the inner courtyard.

They also faced the outer courtyard’s paved area.

**Help with “covered walkways”:** BSB calls these “galleries.”

## Ezekiel 42:4

In front of the rooms was an inner walkway ten cubits wide and a hundred cubits long. 📖

Their doors were on the north.

## Ezekiel 42:5

The upper rooms were smaller. 📖

The covered walkways took away space compared with the rooms on the lower and middle floors.

## Ezekiel 42:6

The rooms were arranged on three floors. 📖

Unlike the courtyards, they had no pillars.

So the upper rooms were set farther back than the lower and middle floors.

## Ezekiel 42:7

An outer wall in front of the rooms was fifty cubits long. 📖

It ran alongside the rooms and the outer courtyard.

## Ezekiel 42:8

The rooms on the outer courtyard were fifty cubits long. 📖

Those facing the temple were a hundred cubits long.

## Ezekiel 42:9

Below these rooms was an entrance on the east side. 📖

This was the entrance from the outer courtyard.

## Ezekiel 42:10

On the south side, rooms ran along the outer courtyard wall. 📖

They were beside the courtyard and opposite the building,

## Ezekiel 42:11

with a passage in front, just like the north rooms. 📖

They had the same length and width, with similar exits and measurements.

## Ezekiel 42:12

A door matched the doors of the rooms facing south. 📖

It was in front of the walkway running alongside the wall that extended east.

## Ezekiel 42:13

The man told me, “The north and south rooms facing the temple courtyard are holy rooms. 📖

Priests who approach the LORD will eat the most holy offerings there.

They will put the most holy grain offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings there.

The place is holy.

**About the offerings:** These are gifts and sacrifices presented to God.

Sin and guilt offerings are connected with dealing with wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 42:14

Once priests enter the holy area, they must leave their service clothes there before entering the outer courtyard. 📖

Those clothes are holy.

They must put on other clothes before going to the people’s areas.”

## Ezekiel 42:15

When the man finished measuring inside the temple area, he led me out through the east gate. 📖

He measured the area all around.

## Ezekiel 42:16

He used a measuring rod to measure the east side as five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:17

He measured the north side as five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:18

He measured the south side as five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:19

He went around and measured the west side as five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:20

He measured all four sides. 📖

The area had a wall all around, five hundred cubits long and five hundred cubits wide.

It separated the holy from the ordinary.
