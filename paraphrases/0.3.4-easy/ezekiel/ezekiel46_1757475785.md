# Ezekiel 46 — Worship, the prince, and the kitchens

**About this chapter**

God gives instructions for worship, offerings, and the prince’s property.

The man also shows Ezekiel places where offerings are cooked.

The different rules for gates, occasions, and gifts are kept.

## Ezekiel 46:1

The Lord GOD says, “Keep the inner courtyard’s east gate shut during the six working days. 📖

Open it on the Sabbath and New Moon.

**Help with these days:** The Sabbath is the special weekly rest day.

The New Moon marks the beginning of a month.

## Ezekiel 46:2

The prince must enter from outside through the gateway’s porch and stand beside the gatepost. 📖

The priests will sacrifice his burnt offerings and peace offerings.

He must bow in worship at the gate’s entrance floor and leave.

But the gate must stay open until evening.

## Ezekiel 46:3

On Sabbaths and New Moons, the people of the land must also bow before the LORD at that gateway’s entrance. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:4

The prince’s burnt offering to the LORD on the Sabbath must be six male lambs and one male sheep. 📖

All must be without any defect.

## Ezekiel 46:5

The grain offering with the male sheep must be one ephah. 📖

With the lambs, give as much grain as he is able.

Give one hin of oil for each ephah.

**Help with the measures:** An ephah measures dry goods.

A hin measures liquids.

## Ezekiel 46:6

On the New Moon, he must offer a young bull without any defect. 📖

He must also offer six lambs and a male sheep without any defect.

## Ezekiel 46:7

He must give an ephah of grain with the bull and an ephah with the male sheep. 📖

With the lambs, give as much as he is able.

Give one hin of oil for each ephah.

## Ezekiel 46:8

The prince must enter through the gateway’s porch and leave the same way. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:9

When the people come before the LORD at the appointed feasts, they must follow this rule. 📖

Anyone entering the north gate to worship must leave through the south gate.

Anyone entering the south gate must leave through the north gate.

No one may leave through the gate they entered.

Each must leave through the opposite gate.

## Ezekiel 46:10

The prince must enter with the people when they enter. 📖

He must leave when they leave.

## Ezekiel 46:11

At festivals and appointed feasts, give an ephah of grain with a bull and an ephah with a male sheep. 📖

With the lambs, give as much as one is able.

Give one hin of oil for each ephah.

## Ezekiel 46:12

The prince may freely choose to give the LORD a burnt offering or peace offering. 📖

When he does, open the east gate for him.

He must offer it just as he does on the Sabbath.

Then he must leave, and the gate must be shut after him.

## Ezekiel 46:13

Provide a one-year-old lamb without any defect as a daily burnt offering to the LORD. 📖

Offer it every morning.

## Ezekiel 46:14

Every morning, also provide one sixth of an ephah of grain with it. 📖

Give one third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour.

This is a grain offering to the LORD.

This rule is permanent.

## Ezekiel 46:15

They must provide the lamb, grain offering, and oil every morning as a regular burnt offering. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:16

The Lord GOD says this. 📖

If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons as an inheritance, it belongs to his descendants.

It will become their property by inheritance.

**Help with “inheritance”:** This is property given to someone to keep.

## Ezekiel 46:17

But if he gives one of his servants a gift from his inheritance, the rule is different. 📖

It belongs to that servant until the year of freedom.

Then it returns to the prince.

His inheritance belongs only to his sons.

It must be theirs.

**Help with “the year of freedom”:** This is the year when the gift must be returned under this rule.

## Ezekiel 46:18

The prince must not take the people’s inheritance by forcing them off their property. 📖

He must provide his sons’ inheritance from his own property.

Then none of My people will be driven from their property.”

## Ezekiel 46:19

The man brought me through the entrance beside the gate into the priests’ holy rooms facing north. 📖

He showed me a place at the far western end,

## Ezekiel 46:20

and said, “Here the priests must boil the guilt offering and sin offering and bake the grain offering. 📖

Then they will not bring these into the outer courtyard and pass holiness to the people.”

**About passing holiness:** This is part of the passage’s rules for keeping holy things separate.

## Ezekiel 46:21

He brought me into the outer courtyard and led me around its four corners. 📖

I saw a separate courtyard in each corner.

## Ezekiel 46:22

The outer courtyard’s four corners had enclosed courtyards, each forty cubits long and thirty cubits wide. 📖

All four corner areas had the same measurements.

## Ezekiel 46:23

Inside each of the four courtyards was a row of stonework all around. 📖

Ovens were built at the base of the walls on every side.

## Ezekiel 46:24

He told me, “These are the kitchens. 📖

Those serving at the temple will cook the people’s sacrifices here.”
