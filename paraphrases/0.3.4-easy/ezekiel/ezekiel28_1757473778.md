# Ezekiel 28 — Tyre’s pride and Sidon’s judgment

**About this chapter**

God speaks against the ruler of Tyre and gives a song of grief for Tyre’s king.

The song uses pictures of Eden, precious stones, and a guardian cherub.

God also announces judgment against Sidon and a future gathering of Israel.

## Ezekiel 28:1

The LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:2

God said, “Son of man, tell the ruler of Tyre what the Lord GOD says. 📖

Your heart is proud.

You have said, ‘I am a god.

I sit in the seat of gods in the heart of the sea.’

Yet you are a man, not a god.

You have thought of your heart as the heart of a god.

## Ezekiel 28:3

Look! 📖

You are wiser than Daniel.

No secret is hidden from you!

**About these words:** God is addressing the ruler’s pride.

The draft keeps the source’s comparison with Daniel.

## Ezekiel 28:4

Your wisdom and understanding have gained you wealth. 📖

You have gathered gold and silver in your treasure stores.

## Ezekiel 28:5

Your great skill in trading has increased your wealth. 📖

But your heart has become proud because of your wealth.

## Ezekiel 28:6

So the Lord GOD says this. 📖

You think of your heart as the heart of a god.

## Ezekiel 28:7

So look! 📖

I will bring foreigners against you, the cruelest of nations.

They will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom.

They will ruin your splendor.

## Ezekiel 28:8

They will bring you down to the Pit. 📖

You will die a violent death in the heart of the seas.

**Help with “the Pit”:** This pictures the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 28:9

Will you still say, ‘I am a god,’ before those who kill you? 📖

In the hands of those who wound you, you will be only a man, not a god.

## Ezekiel 28:10

Foreigners will kill you with the death of the uncircumcised. 📖

I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.”

**Help with “uncircumcised”:** This means males whose foreskin has not been removed.

Circumcision was a sign of God’s covenant with Israel.

Here the term is part of the announced shameful death.

## Ezekiel 28:11

The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:12

God said, “Son of man, sing a song of grief for the king of Tyre. 📖

Tell him what the Lord GOD says.

You were the seal of perfection, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.

**Help with “seal of perfection”:** This pictures someone who shows complete perfection.

**About the song:** God addresses Tyre’s king using the pictures that follow.

The draft does not add another identity to the person addressed.

## Ezekiel 28:13

You were in Eden, the garden of God. 📖

Every kind of precious stone decorated you.

There were ruby, topaz, diamond, beryl, onyx, jasper, sapphire, turquoise, and emerald.

The holders and settings for your stones were made of gold.

They were prepared on the day you were created.

**Help with “settings”:** These hold precious stones in place.

## Ezekiel 28:14

You were anointed as a guardian cherub, because I had appointed you. 📖

You were on God’s holy mountain.

You walked among the fiery stones.

**Help with these words:** “Anointed” means set apart for a special role.

A cherub is a heavenly being.

This cherub’s role was to guard.

## Ezekiel 28:15

From the day you were created, your ways were without blame. 📖

Then wickedness was found in you.

## Ezekiel 28:16

Your great amount of trade filled you with violence, and you sinned. 📖

So I drove you in disgrace from God’s mountain.

Guardian cherub, I forced you away from among the fiery stones.

## Ezekiel 28:17

Your heart became proud because of your beauty. 📖

You spoiled your wisdom because of your splendor.

So I threw you down to the earth.

I made you a sight for kings to stare at.

## Ezekiel 28:18

Your many sins and dishonest trading made your holy places unholy. 📖

So I made fire come from inside you.

It burned you up.

I turned you into ashes on the ground as everyone watched.

## Ezekiel 28:19

All the nations that know you are horrified at what happened to you. 📖

You have come to a horrible end.

You will be no more.”

## Ezekiel 28:20

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:21

God said, “Son of man, turn toward Sidon. 📖

Speak My message against her.

## Ezekiel 28:22

Announce what the Lord GOD says. 📖

Look, Sidon!

I am against you.

I will receive glory within you.

They will know that I am the LORD when I carry out judgments against her.

I will show My holiness through her.

## Ezekiel 28:23

I will send a deadly disease against her and spill blood in her streets. 📖

People will fall dead inside her while swords attack her from every side.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 28:24

The people of Israel will no longer face a sharp bush or painful thorn from neighbors who scorn them. 📖

Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.”

**Help with this picture:** The sharp bush and thorn picture harm caused by Israel’s hostile neighbors.

## Ezekiel 28:25

The Lord GOD says, “I will gather Israel’s people from the peoples among whom they have been scattered. 📖

When I do, I will show My holiness among them before the nations.

Then they will live in their own land, which I gave to My servant Jacob.

## Ezekiel 28:26

They will live there safely, build houses, and plant vineyards. 📖

They will live safely when I carry out judgments against all their neighbors who scorn them.

Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.”

**Help with “vineyards”:** These are places where grapevines grow.
