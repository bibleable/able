# Ezekiel 31 — The great cedar falls

**About this chapter**

God compares Assyria to a great cedar tree that became proud and was cut down.

He uses this picture to warn Pharaoh and all his people.

The picture includes their descent to the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 31:1

The LORD spoke to me in the eleventh year, on the first day of the third month. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:2

God said, “Son of man, speak to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and all his people. 📖

Ask, ‘Who can compare with your greatness?’

## Ezekiel 31:3

Look at Assyria, a cedar tree in Lebanon. 📖

Its beautiful branches shaded the forest.

It rose high, with its top among the clouds.

**About this picture:** The tree pictures Assyria’s greatness.

## Ezekiel 31:4

Water made it grow. 📖

Deep springs made it tall.

Their streams flowed all around its base.

Their channels carried water to all the trees of the field.

## Ezekiel 31:5

So it rose higher than all the trees of the field. 📖

Its branches increased.

Its large branches grew long and spread out because it had so much water.

## Ezekiel 31:6

All the birds of the air nested in its branches. 📖

All the animals of the field gave birth beneath its large branches.

All the great nations lived in its shade.

## Ezekiel 31:7

It was beautiful because of its greatness and the length of its branches. 📖

Its roots reached plenty of water.

## Ezekiel 31:8

The cedars in God’s garden could not equal it. 📖

The cypress trees could not compare with its branches.

The plane trees could not match its large branches.

No tree in God’s garden could compare with its beauty.

**Help with the names:** Cedar, cypress, and plane are kinds of trees.

## Ezekiel 31:9

I made it beautiful with its many branches. 📖

All the trees of Eden in God’s garden envied it.

## Ezekiel 31:10

So the Lord GOD says, “It grew tall and put its top among the clouds. 📖

Its height made it proud.

## Ezekiel 31:11

So I handed it over to the ruler of the nations. 📖

He was to deal with it according to its wickedness.

I have driven it away.

## Ezekiel 31:12

Foreigners, the cruelest of the nations, cut it down and left it. 📖

Its branches fell on the mountains and in every valley.

Its large branches lay broken in all the earth’s deep valleys.

All the peoples of the earth left its shade and abandoned it.

## Ezekiel 31:13

All the birds of the air nested on its fallen trunk. 📖

All the animals of the field lived among its large branches.

## Ezekiel 31:14

This happened so no other trees beside water would grow tall with their tops among the clouds. 📖

No other well-watered trees would reach that height.

They have all been handed over to death, to the depths of the earth.

They are with the people who go down to the Pit.

**Help with “the Pit”:** This pictures the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 31:15

The Lord GOD says, “On the day it was brought down to Sheol, I caused mourning. 📖

I covered the deep waters because of it.

I held back its rivers and stopped its great waters.

I made Lebanon mourn for it.

All the trees of the field lost their strength because of it.

**Help with “Sheol”:** This is a name for the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 31:16

I made the nations shake at the sound of its fall. 📖

I threw it down to Sheol with those who go down to the Pit.

Then all the trees of Eden were comforted in the earth below.

These were the finest and best trees of Lebanon, all the well-watered trees.

## Ezekiel 31:17

They too went down with it to Sheol, to those killed with swords. 📖

They had been its supporters, living in its shade among the nations.

## Ezekiel 31:18

Who among the trees of Eden is like you in glory and greatness? 📖

You too will be brought down to the depths of the earth with the trees of Eden.

You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed with swords.

This is Pharaoh and all his people, declares the Lord GOD.”

**Help with “uncircumcised”:** These are males whose foreskin has not been removed.

Circumcision was a sign of God’s covenant with Israel.

Here the word forms part of the picture of shame in death.
