# Ezekiel 11 — Judgment and a new heart

**About this chapter**

The vision shows leaders in Jerusalem and brings a warning against them.

The LORD promises a new heart to returning exiles, then His glory moves east of the city.

## Ezekiel 11:1

The Spirit lifted me to the LORD’s house’s east-facing gate. 📖

Twenty-five men stood at the entrance.

I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, leaders of the people.

## Ezekiel 11:2

The LORD told me, “Son of man, these men plan evil and give wicked advice in this city. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:3

“They say, ‘Is the time not near to build houses? 📖

The city is the cooking pot, and we are the meat.’

## Ezekiel 11:4

“So prophesy against them, son of man! 📖

Prophesy!”

## Ezekiel 11:5

The LORD’s Spirit fell on me and told me to announce the LORD’s words. 📖

“Israel’s house, that is what you think, and I know the thoughts arising in your minds.

## Ezekiel 11:6

“You killed many in this city and filled its streets with the dead. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:7

“The Lord GOD says, ‘The dead you put in this city are the meat, and the city is the pot. 📖

But I will remove you.

## Ezekiel 11:8

The Lord GOD declares, “You fear the sword, so I will bring the sword against you. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:9

“I will bring you from the city, hand you to foreigners, and judge you. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:10

“You will fall by swords, and I will judge you to Israel’s borders. 📖

Then you will know I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 11:11

“The city will not be your pot, and you will not be its meat. 📖

I will judge you to Israel’s borders.

## Ezekiel 11:12

“Then you will know I am the LORD. 📖

You did not follow My commands or keep My rules, but followed surrounding nations’ rules.”

## Ezekiel 11:13

As I prophesied, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. 📖

I fell facedown and cried loudly, “Oh, Lord GOD!

Will You completely destroy Israel’s survivors?”

## Ezekiel 11:14

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:15

“Son of man, Jerusalem’s people speak this way about your brothers, relatives, fellow exiles, and Israel’s whole house. 📖

‘They are far from the LORD.

This land was given to us.’

## Ezekiel 11:16

“Announce what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘I sent them far among nations and scattered them in countries.

Yet for a little while, I have been their holy shelter in those countries.’

**Help with holy shelter**

The source calls God a sanctuary for the scattered people.

Sanctuary means a holy place of refuge or God’s presence.

## Ezekiel 11:17

“Announce what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘I will gather you from the peoples and countries where you were scattered and return Israel’s land to you.’

## Ezekiel 11:18

“When they return, they will remove all its detestable things and disgusting practices. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:19

“I will give them one heart and a new spirit within. 📖

I will remove their stone heart and give them a heart of flesh,

**Help with the hearts**

The stone and flesh picture a hard, unresponsive heart changed into a living, responsive one.

## Ezekiel 11:20

“so that they follow My commands, keep My rules, and practice them. 📖

They will be My people, and I will be their God.

## Ezekiel 11:21

The Lord GOD declares, “But I will bring the conduct of those pursuing disgusting things down on their own heads.” 📖

## Ezekiel 11:22

The cherubim spread their wings with the wheels beside them, and Israel’s God’s glory was above them. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:23

The LORD’s glory rose from within the city and stood over the mountain east of it. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:24

The Spirit lifted me and took me back to Chaldea’s exiles in the vision given by God’s Spirit. 📖

The vision then left me,

## Ezekiel 11:25

and I told the exiles everything the LORD showed me. 📖
