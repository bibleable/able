# Ezekiel 1 — A vision of the LORD’s glory

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel sees a vision beside the River Kebar.

The descriptions repeatedly say things looked like something familiar.

Those comparisons and the vision’s unusual details are retained.

## Ezekiel 1:1

In the thirtieth year, on the fifth day of the fourth month, I was among exiles beside the River Kebar. 📖

The heavens opened, and I saw visions of God.

**Help with exiles**

Exiles are people forced away from their own land.

## Ezekiel 1:2

It was the fifth day of the month, in the fifth year of King Jehoiachin’s exile. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:3

The LORD’s word came directly to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi, in the Chaldeans’ land beside the River Kebar. 📖

The LORD’s hand was on him there.

**Help with the LORD’s hand**

The image describes God’s power acting on Ezekiel.

## Ezekiel 1:4

I looked and saw a whirlwind from the north, a great cloud with flashing fire and bright light around it. 📖

In the fire’s center was a gleam like amber,

**Help with amber**

Amber is a shining yellow or orange material.

Here its appearance is a comparison for the glow.

## Ezekiel 1:5

and within it were shapes of four living creatures. 📖

They had a human form,

## Ezekiel 1:6

but each had four faces and four wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:7

Their legs were straight. 📖

The bottoms of their feet were like calf hooves, shining like polished bronze.

## Ezekiel 1:8

They had human hands under their wings on their four sides. 📖

All four living creatures had faces and wings,

## Ezekiel 1:9

and their wings touched each other. 📖

They did not turn as they moved, but each went straight ahead.

## Ezekiel 1:10

Their faces had the form of a human face. 📖

Each of the four also had a lion’s face on the right and an ox’s face on the left.

Each also had an eagle’s face.

## Ezekiel 1:11

These were their faces. 📖

Their wings spread upward, two touching the creatures on either side and two covering each creature’s body.

## Ezekiel 1:12

Each creature went straight ahead wherever the spirit wanted to go. 📖

They did not turn as they moved.

## Ezekiel 1:13

Among the creatures was something like glowing coals or torches. 📖

Fire moved between them, bright and flashing lightning.

## Ezekiel 1:14

The creatures darted back and forth as quickly as lightning flashes. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:15

I looked at the creatures and saw a wheel on the ground beside each four-faced creature. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:16

The wheels shone like beryl, and all four looked alike. 📖

They looked like a wheel within a wheel.

**Help with beryl**

Beryl is a kind of precious stone.

The word describes how the wheels shone.

## Ezekiel 1:17

They moved in any of four directions without turning as they went. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:18

Their rims were high and awe-inspiring. 📖

All four rims were full of eyes all around.

## Ezekiel 1:19

When the living creatures moved, the wheels moved beside them. 📖

When the creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose too.

## Ezekiel 1:20

They went wherever the spirit wanted to go. 📖

The wheels rose beside them because the creatures’ spirit was in the wheels.

## Ezekiel 1:21

When the creatures moved, the wheels moved. 📖

When the creatures stood still, the wheels stood still.

When the creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose beside them.

The creatures’ spirit was in the wheels.

## Ezekiel 1:22

Above their heads spread something like an awe-inspiring expanse shining like crystal. 📖

**Help with expanse**

An expanse is a broad space or surface stretching out.

## Ezekiel 1:23

Under it, their wings stretched toward one another. 📖

Each also had two wings covering its body.

## Ezekiel 1:24

When they moved, I heard wings like roaring waters, like the Almighty’s voice, like the noise of an army. 📖

When they stood still, they lowered their wings.

## Ezekiel 1:25

A voice came from above the expanse over their heads as they stood still with lowered wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:26

Above the expanse over their heads was something like a throne with the appearance of sapphire. 📖

On the throne high above was a figure like a man.

**Help with sapphire**

Sapphire is a precious stone.

## Ezekiel 1:27

From what seemed His waist upward, I saw a gleam like amber, with fire-like brightness within it all around. 📖

From what seemed His waist downward, I saw something like fire.

Bright light surrounded Him.

## Ezekiel 1:28

The light around Him looked like a rainbow in a cloud on a rainy day. 📖

This was the appearance of the likeness of the LORD’s glory.

When I saw it, I fell facedown and heard a voice speak.

**Help with glory**

Glory describes God’s greatness and splendor made known.

The vision uses comparisons rather than claiming the figure is an ordinary human being.
