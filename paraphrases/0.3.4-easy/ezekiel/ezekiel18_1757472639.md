# Ezekiel 18 — Turn and live

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges a saying about fathers and children.

He gives examples of three generations and explains how turning toward or away from righteousness changes the outcome.

## Ezekiel 18:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:2

“What do you mean by this saying about Israel’s land? 📖

‘Fathers ate sour grapes, and their children’s teeth feel the sourness.’

**Help with the saying**

It pictures children suffering for what their fathers did.

## Ezekiel 18:3

The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, you will no longer use this saying in Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:4

“Every soul belongs to Me, father and son alike. 📖

The soul who sins is the one who dies.

## Ezekiel 18:5

“Suppose a man is righteous and acts justly and rightly. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:6

“He does not eat on the mountain or look to Israel’s house’s idols. 📖

He does not sexually defile his neighbor’s wife or approach a woman during her period.

**Help with these actions**

Mountain meals here are connected with idol worship.

Defiling a neighbor’s wife means sexual unfaithfulness.

Approaching a woman during her period refers to sexual relations during menstruation.

The source prohibition is retained without describing menstruating people as morally bad.

## Ezekiel 18:7

“He does not oppress others and returns a debtor’s pledge. 📖

He does not rob, but shares bread with hungry people and clothes naked people.

**Help with pledge and debtor**

A debtor owes a payment.

A pledge is property held as security for that payment.

## Ezekiel 18:8

“He does not lend at excessive interest or take excess interest. 📖

He refuses wrongdoing and gives true justice between people.

## Ezekiel 18:9

The Lord GOD declares, “He follows My commands and faithfully keeps My rules. 📖

That man is righteous and will surely live.

## Ezekiel 18:10

“But suppose he has a violent son who sheds blood or does these things, 📖

## Ezekiel 18:11

“though his father did none of them. 📖

The son eats on the mountain and sexually defiles his neighbor’s wife.

## Ezekiel 18:12

“He oppresses poor and needy people, robs, and fails to return a pledge. 📖

He looks to idols and does disgusting things.

## Ezekiel 18:13

“He lends at excessive interest and takes excess interest. 📖

Will he live?

No!

He will surely die for all these disgusting acts, with his blood on his own head.

## Ezekiel 18:14

“Suppose that son has a son who sees all his father’s sins, considers them, and refuses to do the same. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:15

“He does not eat on the mountain, look to Israel’s idols, or sexually defile his neighbor’s wife. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:16

“He does not oppress, keep a pledge, or rob. 📖

He gives bread to hungry people and clothes naked people.

## Ezekiel 18:17

“He does not harm poor people or take interest or usury. 📖

He keeps My rules and follows My commands.

He will not die for his father’s wrongdoing, but will surely live.

**Help with interest and usury**

Interest is an extra payment charged on a loan.

Usury means lending at excessive interest.

This verse prohibits both, while earlier verses name excessive interest.

## Ezekiel 18:18

“His father will die for his own wrongdoing. 📖

He used threats for gain, robbed his brother, and did wrong among his people.

## Ezekiel 18:19

“You ask, ‘Why should the son not bear his father’s wrongdoing?’ 📖

The son acted justly and rightly and carefully kept all My commands.

He will surely live.

## Ezekiel 18:20

“The soul who sins will die. 📖

A son will not bear his father’s wrongdoing, nor a father his son’s wrongdoing.

A righteous man’s righteousness will be his own, and a wicked man’s wickedness his own.

## Ezekiel 18:21

“A wicked man may leave all his sins, keep all My commands, and act justly and rightly. 📖

If he does this, he will surely live, not die.

## Ezekiel 18:22

“None of his offenses will be held against him. 📖

He will live because of the righteousness he practiced.

## Ezekiel 18:23

The Lord GOD declares, “Do I enjoy wicked people’s death? 📖

Would I not prefer them to turn from their ways and live?

## Ezekiel 18:24

“But if a righteous man turns to wrongdoing and does wicked people’s disgusting acts, will he live? 📖

None of his righteous deeds will be remembered.

He will die for his unfaithfulness and sin.

## Ezekiel 18:25

“You say, ‘The Lord’s way is unjust.’ 📖

Hear, Israel’s house!

Is My way unjust, or are your ways unjust?

## Ezekiel 18:26

“If a righteous man turns from righteousness and does wrong, he will die for his wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:27

“If a wicked man turns from wickedness and does what is just and right, he saves his life. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:28

“Because he considered and turned from all his offenses, he will surely live and not die. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:29

“Israel’s house says, ‘The Lord’s way is unjust.’ 📖

Are My ways unjust, Israel’s house, or are yours?

## Ezekiel 18:30

The Lord GOD declares, “Israel’s house, I will judge each of you by his ways. 📖

Turn back from all offenses so wrongdoing does not bring your downfall.

## Ezekiel 18:31

“Throw away all your offenses and make yourselves a new heart and spirit. 📖

Why should you die, Israel’s house?

## Ezekiel 18:32

The Lord GOD declares, “I take no pleasure in anyone’s death. 📖

So turn back and live!”
