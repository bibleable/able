# Ezekiel 2 — Sent to a rebellious people

**About this chapter**

God tells Ezekiel to speak His words, whether people listen or refuse.

Ezekiel then sees a scroll written on both sides.

## Ezekiel 2:1

He told me, “Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak to you.” 📖

**Help with son of man**

This form of address calls Ezekiel a human being.

## Ezekiel 2:2

As He spoke, the Spirit entered me and set me on my feet. 📖

I heard Him speaking.

## Ezekiel 2:3

He said, “Son of man, I send you to the Israelites, a rebellious nation that rebelled against Me. 📖

They and their fathers have rebelled to this very day.

## Ezekiel 2:4

“They are stubborn children who refuse to change. 📖

I send you to them to say, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’

## Ezekiel 2:5

“Whether they listen or refuse, they will know a prophet has been among them. 📖

They are a rebellious house.

**Help with prophet and house**

A prophet speaks God’s message.

House here means the people as a family or community.

## Ezekiel 2:6

“Son of man, do not fear them or their words. 📖

Do not fear, though briers and thorns surround you and you live among scorpions.

Do not fear their words or be distressed by their presence, though they are rebellious.

**Help with thorns and scorpions**

Briers are thorny bushes.

Scorpions are small creatures with painful stings.

These images describe the danger and hostility Ezekiel faces.

## Ezekiel 2:7

“Speak My words whether they listen or refuse, because they are rebellious. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:8

“Son of man, listen to what I tell you. 📖

Do not rebel like that rebellious house.

Open your mouth and eat what I give you.”

## Ezekiel 2:9

I looked and saw a hand reaching toward me with a scroll. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:10

He unrolled it before me. 📖

Words of lament, mourning, and woe were written on its front and back.

**Help with lament and woe**

These are words of grief, sorrow, and coming trouble.
