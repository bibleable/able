# Ezekiel 27 — A song of grief for Tyre

**About this chapter**

God describes Tyre as a beautiful trading ship.

The song names its materials, workers, soldiers, trading partners, and goods.

The ship’s destruction pictures Tyre’s fall.

## Ezekiel 27:1

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:2

God said, “Now, son of man, sing a song of grief for Tyre. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:3

Tyre lives at the entrance to the sea. 📖

She trades with peoples on many coasts.

Tell her what the Lord GOD says.

Tyre, you said, ‘My beauty is perfect.’

## Ezekiel 27:4

Your borders are in the heart of the seas. 📖

Your builders made your beauty perfect.

**Help with “the heart of the seas”:** This means far out in the sea.

## Ezekiel 27:5

They made all your wooden boards from cypress trees from Senir. 📖

They took a cedar tree from Lebanon to make your mast.

**Help with “mast”:** This is the tall pole that holds a ship’s sails.

## Ezekiel 27:6

They made your oars from oak trees from Bashan. 📖

They made your deck from wood from the coasts of Cyprus and set ivory into it.

**Help with these words:** Oars are long poles with wide ends, used to move a boat through water.

A deck is the floor of a ship.

Ivory is a hard material from animal tusks.

## Ezekiel 27:7

They made your sail from fine linen cloth from Egypt, decorated with stitching. 📖

It served as your flag.

They made your shade covering from blue and purple cloth from the coasts of Elishah.

## Ezekiel 27:8

Men from Sidon and Arvad rowed your ship. 📖

Tyre, your skilled men were there as captains.

## Ezekiel 27:9

The elders of Gebal were on board as shipbuilders, repairing your leaks. 📖

All the ships of the sea and their sailors came alongside you.

They exchanged goods for the things you sold.

## Ezekiel 27:10

Men from Persia, Lydia, and Put served as soldiers in your army. 📖

They hung their shields and helmets on your walls.

They made you look splendid.

## Ezekiel 27:11

Men from Arvad and Helech served on your walls all around. 📖

Men from Gammad were in your towers.

They hung their shields around your walls.

They made your beauty perfect.

## Ezekiel 27:12

Tarshish traded with you because you had so many goods. 📖

They exchanged silver, iron, tin, and lead for your goods.

## Ezekiel 27:13

Javan, Tubal, and Meshech traded with you. 📖

They exchanged slaves and bronze tools and containers for your goods.

**Help with “slaves”:** These were people treated as property and forced to serve others.

The passage lists this as part of Tyre’s trade.

## Ezekiel 27:14

Men from Beth-togarmah exchanged horses, war horses, and mules for your goods. 📖

**Help with “mules”:** These animals have a horse parent and a donkey parent.

## Ezekiel 27:15

Men from Dedan were your customers. 📖

Many coastlands were your markets.

They paid you with ivory tusks and ebony wood.

**Help with “ebony”:** This is a hard, dark wood.

## Ezekiel 27:16

Aram bought from you because you had many products. 📖

They exchanged turquoise, purple cloth, decorated stitching, fine linen cloth, coral, and rubies for your goods.

**Help with these goods:** Turquoise and rubies are valuable stones.

Coral is a hard material formed in the sea.

## Ezekiel 27:17

Judah and the land of Israel traded with you. 📖

They exchanged wheat from Minnith, cakes and honey, oil and balm for your goods.

**Help with “balm”:** This is a sweet-smelling substance from plants, used for healing.

## Ezekiel 27:18

Damascus traded with you because of your many products and your great wealth of goods. 📖

They traded wine from Helbon and wool from Zahar,

## Ezekiel 27:19

and barrels of wine from Izal for your goods. 📖

Worked iron, cassia, and sweet cane were exchanged for the things you sold.

**Help with these goods:** Cassia is a sweet-smelling plant product.

Sweet cane is a sweet-smelling reed.

## Ezekiel 27:20

Dedan traded with you in cloths used under saddles for riding. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:21

Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were your customers. 📖

They traded lambs, male sheep, and goats.

## Ezekiel 27:22

The traders of Sheba and Raamah traded with you. 📖

They exchanged gold, the finest of all spices, and valuable stones for your goods.

## Ezekiel 27:23

Haran, Canneh, and Eden traded with you. 📖

So did the traders of Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad.

## Ezekiel 27:24

In your marketplace they traded fine blue clothes, decorated stitching, and rugs of many colors. 📖

The rugs had cords twisted and knotted tightly.

## Ezekiel 27:25

The ships of Tarshish carried your goods. 📖

You were filled with heavy cargo in the heart of the sea.

**Help with “cargo”:** This is the load a ship carries.

## Ezekiel 27:26

Your rowers brought you far out to sea. 📖

But the east wind will break you apart in the heart of the sea.

## Ezekiel 27:27

Your wealth and all your goods will sink into the heart of the sea on the day you fall. 📖

Your sailors, captains, shipbuilders, traders, and all your soldiers will sink too.

All the other people on board will sink with them.

## Ezekiel 27:28

The countryside will shake when your sailors cry out. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:29

All who work the oars will leave their ships. 📖

The sailors and all the captains of the sea will stand on the shore.

## Ezekiel 27:30

They will raise their voices for you and cry bitterly. 📖

They will throw dust on their heads and roll in ashes.

**About these actions:** These are signs of deep grief.

## Ezekiel 27:31

They will shave their heads for you and wrap themselves in rough sackcloth. 📖

They will weep over you with deep pain and bitter grief.

**Help with “sackcloth”:** This is rough cloth worn as a sign of grief.

## Ezekiel 27:32

As they cry and mourn over you, they will sing a song of grief. 📖

They will say, ‘Who was ever like Tyre, now silent in the middle of the sea?

## Ezekiel 27:33

When your goods went out to sea, you supplied many nations. 📖

Your great wealth and goods made the kings of the earth rich.

## Ezekiel 27:34

Now the seas have broken you apart in the deep waters. 📖

Your goods and the people with you have sunk with you.

## Ezekiel 27:35

All the people of the coastlands are horrified at what happened to you. 📖

Their kings shake with fear.

Their faces twist with distress.

## Ezekiel 27:36

The traders among the nations hiss at you. 📖

You have come to a horrible end.

You will be no more.’”

**Help with “hiss”:** This sound shows scorn or horror.
