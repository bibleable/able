# Ezekiel 16 — Jerusalem pictured as an unfaithful wife

**About this chapter**

The LORD addresses Jerusalem through an extended story.

The city is pictured first as an abandoned baby, then as an adult bride who becomes unfaithful.

The chapter includes sexual imagery, child sacrifice, exposure, and violent judgment.

The images describe the city and its relationships; they are not instructions for treating another person.

## Ezekiel 16:1

Again the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:2

“Son of man, confront Jerusalem with her disgusting acts, 📖

## Ezekiel 16:3

“and tell her what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘Jerusalem, your origin and birth were in the Canaanites’ land.

Your father was an Amorite and your mother a Hittite.

## Ezekiel 16:4

“On your birth day, your cord was not cut, and you were not washed clean with water. 📖

No one rubbed you with salt or wrapped you in cloths.

**Help with cord**

The cord connects a baby to the mother before birth.

The verse lists care that the abandoned baby did not receive.

## Ezekiel 16:5

“No one had enough compassion to do even one of these things. 📖

You were thrown into an open field because you were despised on your birth day.

## Ezekiel 16:6

“I passed and saw you lying in your blood. 📖

I told you as you lay in blood, “Live!”

I said to you there, “Live!”

## Ezekiel 16:7

“I made you thrive like a field plant. 📖

You grew, matured, and became very beautiful.

Your breasts formed and your hair grew, but you were naked and bare.

## Ezekiel 16:8

The Lord GOD declares, “I passed and saw you old enough for love. 📖

I spread My cloak over you and covered your nakedness.

I pledged Myself, made a covenant with you, and you became Mine.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

The cloak and pledge picture a marriage commitment.

## Ezekiel 16:9

“I bathed you, washed away your blood, and anointed you with oil. 📖

**Help with anointed**

Here it means applying oil to the person.

## Ezekiel 16:10

“I dressed you in embroidered cloth and gave fine leather sandals. 📖

I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you with silk.

**Help with embroidered and linen**

Embroidered cloth has patterns sewn into it.

Linen is cloth made from flax plant fibers.

## Ezekiel 16:11

“I decorated you with jewelry, bracelets on your wrists and a chain around your neck. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:12

“I put a ring in your nose, earrings in your ears, and a beautiful crown on your head. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:13

“You wore gold and silver, fine linen, silk, and embroidered cloth. 📖

You ate fine flour, honey, and oil.

You became very beautiful and rose to be queen.

## Ezekiel 16:14

The Lord GOD declares, “Your beauty made you famous among nations. 📖

It was perfect because of the splendor I gave you.

## Ezekiel 16:15

“But you trusted your beauty and acted as a prostitute because of your fame. 📖

You gave favors to every passerby, and your beauty was theirs for the asking.

**Help with prostitute**

A prostitute sells sexual services.

The story uses sexual unfaithfulness to describe Jerusalem’s unfaithfulness to God.

## Ezekiel 16:16

“You used some clothes to make colorful high places and acted as a prostitute on them. 📖

Such things should never have happened!

**Help with high places**

These were places used for worship.

## Ezekiel 16:17

“You took My gifts of fine gold and silver jewelry and made male idols to act as a prostitute with. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:18

“You covered them with your embroidered clothes and put My oil and incense before them. 📖

**Help with incense**

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Ezekiel 16:19

The Lord GOD declares, “You offered My food before them as a pleasing smell. 📖

It was the fine flour, oil, and honey I provided.

That is what happened.

## Ezekiel 16:20

“You even took sons and daughters born to Me and sacrificed them as food to idols. 📖

Was your prostitution not enough?

## Ezekiel 16:21

“You killed My children and gave them through fire to idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:22

“Throughout your disgusting acts and prostitution, you forgot your youth, naked and bare, lying in your own blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:23

The Lord GOD declares, “How terrible! 📖

How terrible for you!

Besides all your other wickedness,

## Ezekiel 16:24

“you built yourself a mound and a high shrine in every public square. 📖

**Help with shrine**

A shrine is a place of worship.

## Ezekiel 16:25

“You built high shrines at every street’s head and degraded your beauty. 📖

You spread your legs to every passerby, increasing your sexual unfaithfulness.

## Ezekiel 16:26

“You acted as a prostitute with your lustful Egyptian neighbors and increased your sexual unfaithfulness to anger Me. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:27

“I stretched My hand against you and reduced your portion. 📖

I handed you to those hating you, the Philistines’ daughters, who were ashamed of your shameful sexual conduct.

## Ezekiel 16:28

“You then acted as a prostitute with the Assyrians because you were not satisfied. 📖

Even afterward, you remained unsatisfied.

## Ezekiel 16:29

“You extended your sexual unfaithfulness to Chaldea, the merchants’ land. 📖

Even this did not satisfy you!

## Ezekiel 16:30

The Lord GOD declares, “How weak-willed your heart is, doing all these acts of a shameless prostitute! 📖

## Ezekiel 16:31

“You built mounds at every street’s head and high shrines in every public square. 📖

Yet you were unlike a prostitute, because you despised payment.

## Ezekiel 16:32

“Adulterous wife, you receive strangers instead of your husband! 📖

**Help with adulterous**

Adultery is sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

## Ezekiel 16:33

“Men give gifts to prostitutes, but you gave gifts to all your lovers. 📖

You bribed them to come from everywhere for unlawful sexual favors.

## Ezekiel 16:34

“Your prostitution is the opposite of other women’s. 📖

No one asked for your favors, and you paid instead of receiving payment.

You are the very opposite!

## Ezekiel 16:35

“So, prostitute, hear the LORD’s word! 📖

## Ezekiel 16:36

The Lord GOD says, “You poured out wealth and exposed your nakedness with lovers and detestable idols. 📖

You also gave them your children’s blood.

## Ezekiel 16:37

“So I will gather all lovers you enjoyed, everyone you loved and hated. 📖

I will gather them from all around against you and expose you completely naked before them.

## Ezekiel 16:38

“I will sentence you to the punishment of adulterous women and people shedding blood. 📖

I will bring on you the wrath of your bloodshed and jealousy.

**Help with wrath**

Wrath means fierce anger.

The difficult phrase combining bloodshed and jealousy is retained for further editorial review.

## Ezekiel 16:39

“I will hand you to your lovers. 📖

They will level mounds and tear down high shrines.

They will strip your clothes, take fine jewelry, and leave you naked and bare.

## Ezekiel 16:40

“They will bring a mob to stone you and cut you apart with swords. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:41

“They will burn your houses and judge you before many women. 📖

I will end your prostitution, and you will never pay lovers again.

**About this violent judgment**

The allegory includes public exposure, stoning, and cutting apart.

An allegory is a story used to describe something else.

These details describe judgment on Jerusalem; they do not authorize abuse of a spouse or another person.

## Ezekiel 16:42

“My anger will rest, and My jealousy will turn away. 📖

I will be calm and no longer angry.

## Ezekiel 16:43

The Lord GOD declares, “You forgot your youth and angered Me with all these things. 📖

I will bring your deeds on your own head.

Did you not add this shameful sexual conduct to all your other disgusting acts?

## Ezekiel 16:44

“Everyone using sayings will say this about you, ‘Like mother, like daughter.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 16:45

“You are your mother’s daughter. 📖

Your mother despised her husband and children.

You are your sisters’ sister, and they despised their husbands and children.

Your mother was a Hittite and your father an Amorite.

## Ezekiel 16:46

“Your older sister was Samaria, with her daughters north of you. 📖

Your younger sister was Sodom, with her daughters south of you.

## Ezekiel 16:47

“You followed their ways and disgusting acts, then soon became worse than they were. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:48

The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, your sister Sodom and her daughters never did what you did. 📖

Neither did they do what your daughters did.

## Ezekiel 16:49

“Your sister Sodom and her daughters were proud, overfed, and unconcerned. 📖

They did not help poor and needy people.

## Ezekiel 16:50

“They were arrogant and did disgusting things before Me. 📖

So I removed them, as you saw.

## Ezekiel 16:51

“Samaria did not commit half your sins. 📖

Your disgusting acts surpassed theirs and made your sisters appear righteous.

## Ezekiel 16:52

“Bear your disgrace, because you gave your sisters reason to appear justified. 📖

Your sins were worse, so they appear more righteous than you.

Bear shame and disgrace for making them appear righteous.

**Help with righteous and justified**

These words concern being right or shown to be right.

Here the comparison is relative to Jerusalem’s worse behavior.

## Ezekiel 16:53

“But I will restore Sodom and her daughters from captivity, and Samaria and her daughters too. 📖

I will restore you with them.

## Ezekiel 16:54

“You will bear disgrace and shame for everything you did that comforted them. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:55

“Your sisters Sodom and Samaria, with their daughters, will return to their former state. 📖

You and your daughters will return to your former state too.

## Ezekiel 16:56

“Did you not scorn your sister Sodom in your proud days, 📖

## Ezekiel 16:57

“before your wickedness was uncovered? 📖

Now Edom’s daughters and everyone around her scorn you.

So do the Philistines’ daughters and all around you who despise you.

## Ezekiel 16:58

The LORD declares, “You will bear the consequences of your shameful sexual conduct and disgusting acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:59

The Lord GOD says, “I will treat you according to your deeds. 📖

You despised the oath by breaking the covenant.

## Ezekiel 16:60

“But I will remember the covenant from your youth and establish an everlasting covenant with you. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:61

“You will remember your ways and be ashamed when you receive your older and younger sisters. 📖

I will give them to you as daughters, but not because of My covenant with you.

## Ezekiel 16:62

“I will establish My covenant with you, and you will know I am the LORD, 📖

## Ezekiel 16:63

The Lord GOD declares, “so that when I make atonement for all you did, you remember and feel shame. 📖

Because of your disgrace, you will never open your mouth again.”

**Help with atonement**

Atonement means dealing with sin so that guilt is removed.

The promise of atonement remains alongside the chapter’s judgment and shame.
