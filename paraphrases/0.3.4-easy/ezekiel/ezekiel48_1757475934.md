# Ezekiel 48 — The tribes’ portions and the city’s gates

**About this chapter**

God names the land portions for the tribes, priests, Levites, city, and prince.

He gives the city’s measurements and names its gates.

The city receives the name “THE LORD IS THERE.”

## Ezekiel 48:1

God said, “These are the tribes’ names. 📖

At the northern boundary, Dan will have one portion.

It will border the road from Hethlon to Lebo-hamath and run to Hazar-enan on Damascus’s border.

Hamath will be to the north.

The portion will stretch from east to west.

## Ezekiel 48:2

Asher will have one portion beside Dan’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:3

Naphtali will have one portion beside Asher’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:4

Manasseh will have one portion beside Naphtali’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:5

Ephraim will have one portion beside Manasseh’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:6

Reuben will have one portion beside Ephraim’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:7

Judah will have one portion beside Reuben’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:8

The portion you must set apart will border Judah’s territory from east to west. 📖

It will be 25,000 cubits wide and as long as a tribal portion from east to west.

The holy place will be in its center.

**Help with “cubit”:** This measures from elbow to fingertips.

## Ezekiel 48:9

The special portion you set apart for the LORD must be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:10

This will be the holy portion for the priests. 📖

Its north side will be 25,000 cubits long.

Its west side will be 10,000 cubits wide.

Its east side will be 10,000 cubits wide.

Its south side will be 25,000 cubits long.

The LORD’s holy place will be in its center.

## Ezekiel 48:11

It will be for the priests set apart as holy, the descendants of Zadok. 📖

They kept My instructions and did not go astray like the Levites when the Israelites went astray.

## Ezekiel 48:12

It will be a special portion set apart for them from the land. 📖

It will be a most holy portion beside the Levites’ territory.

## Ezekiel 48:13

The Levites will have land beside the priests’ territory, 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. 📖

The whole length will be 25,000 cubits, and the width 10,000 cubits.

## Ezekiel 48:14

They must not sell or exchange any of it. 📖

They must not transfer this best part of the land, because it is holy to the LORD.

## Ezekiel 48:15

The remaining land, 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long, will be for the city’s ordinary use. 📖

It will be for houses and grassland.

The city will be in its center,

## Ezekiel 48:16

with these measurements. 📖

The north side will be 4,500 cubits.

The south side will be 4,500 cubits.

The east side will be 4,500 cubits.

The west side will be 4,500 cubits.

## Ezekiel 48:17

The city’s grassland will reach 250 cubits north, 250 cubits south, 250 cubits east, and 250 cubits west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:18

The remaining length running beside the holy portion will be 10,000 cubits east and 10,000 cubits west. 📖

Its crops will feed the city’s workers.

## Ezekiel 48:19

The city’s workers who farm it will come from all Israel’s tribes. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:20

The whole portion will be a square, 25,000 cubits by 25,000 cubits. 📖

Set apart the holy portion along with the city’s property.

## Ezekiel 48:21

The land left on both sides of the holy portion and city property will belong to the prince. 📖

His land will be beside the tribal portions.

It will stretch east from the holy district’s 25,000 cubits to the eastern boundary.

It will stretch west from the 25,000 cubits to the western boundary.

The holy portion and temple’s holy place will be in the center.

## Ezekiel 48:22

The Levites’ property and the city’s property will be in the center of the prince’s land. 📖

This area is between Judah’s and Benjamin’s boundaries.

## Ezekiel 48:23

For the remaining tribes, Benjamin will have one portion from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:24

Simeon will have one portion beside Benjamin’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:25

Issachar will have one portion beside Simeon’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:26

Zebulun will have one portion beside Issachar’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:27

Gad will have one portion beside Zebulun’s territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:28

Gad’s southern border will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh. 📖

Then it will follow the Brook of Egypt to the Great Sea.

## Ezekiel 48:29

This is the land you must share as an inheritance among Israel’s tribes. 📖

These will be their portions, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 48:30

These will be the city’s exits. 📖

Begin with the north side, which will be 4,500 cubits long.

## Ezekiel 48:31

The city’s gates will be named after Israel’s tribes. 📖

The north side will have three gates: Reuben’s gate, Judah’s gate, and Levi’s gate.

## Ezekiel 48:32

The east side will be 4,500 cubits long. 📖

It will have three gates: Joseph’s gate, Benjamin’s gate, and Dan’s gate.

## Ezekiel 48:33

The south side will be 4,500 cubits long. 📖

It will have three gates: Simeon’s gate, Issachar’s gate, and Zebulun’s gate.

## Ezekiel 48:34

The west side will be 4,500 cubits long. 📖

It will have three gates: Gad’s gate, Asher’s gate, and Naphtali’s gate.

## Ezekiel 48:35

The distance around the city will be 18,000 cubits. 📖

From that day onward, the city’s name will be THE LORD IS THERE.”
