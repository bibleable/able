# Ezekiel 47 — The life-giving river and the land’s boundaries

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel sees water flowing from the temple and becoming a deep river.

The man describes its effects on water, fish, and trees.

God also gives boundaries and rules for sharing land, including shares for foreigners.

## Ezekiel 47:1

The man brought me back to the temple entrance. 📖

I saw water flowing east from under its entrance floor, because the temple faced east.

It flowed from under the temple’s south side, south of the altar.

## Ezekiel 47:2

He brought me out through the north gate. 📖

He led me around outside to the outer east gate.

There I saw water trickling from the south side.

## Ezekiel 47:3

The man went east with a measuring line in his hand. 📖

He measured a thousand cubits and led me through water reaching my ankles.

**Help with “cubit”:** This measures from elbow to fingertips.

## Ezekiel 47:4

He measured another thousand cubits and led me through water reaching my knees. 📖

Again he measured a thousand cubits and led me through water reaching my waist.

## Ezekiel 47:5

He measured another thousand cubits. 📖

Now it was a river I could not cross.

The water had risen and was deep enough to swim in.

It could not be crossed on foot.

## Ezekiel 47:6

He asked, “Son of man, do you see this?” 📖

Then he led me back to the riverbank.

## Ezekiel 47:7

When I arrived, I saw a great many trees along both riverbanks. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:8

He told me, “This water flows to the eastern region and goes down into the Arabah. 📖

When it enters the Dead Sea, the water there becomes fresh.

## Ezekiel 47:9

Wherever the river flows, there will be swarms of living creatures and many fish. 📖

The river flows there and makes the waters fresh.

So everything will flourish wherever the river flows.

## Ezekiel 47:10

Fishermen will stand along the shore from En-gedi to En-eglaim, spreading their nets. 📖

They will catch many kinds of fish, like the fish of the Great Sea.

## Ezekiel 47:11

But the swamps and marshes will not become fresh. 📖

They will be left for salt.

## Ezekiel 47:12

All kinds of fruit trees will grow along both riverbanks. 📖

Their leaves will not dry up, and their fruit will not fail.

They will bear fruit every month because water from the holy place flows to them.

Their fruit will be food, and their leaves will be for healing.”

## Ezekiel 47:13

The Lord GOD says, “These are the boundaries for dividing the land as an inheritance among Israel’s twelve tribes. 📖

Joseph must receive two portions.

**Help with “inheritance”:** This is land given to them to keep as their own.

## Ezekiel 47:14

Divide it equally among them. 📖

I raised My hand and swore to give it to your ancestors.

So this land will be your inheritance.

## Ezekiel 47:15

This will be the land’s boundary. 📖

On the north, it will run from the Great Sea by Hethlon, through Lebo-hamath to Zedad,

## Ezekiel 47:16

Berothah, and Sibraim, which is on the border between Damascus and Hamath. 📖

It will reach Hazer-hatticon, on the border of Hauran.

## Ezekiel 47:17

The border will run from the Sea to Hazar-enan, along Damascus’s northern border. 📖

Hamath’s territory is to the north.

This will be the northern boundary.

## Ezekiel 47:18

On the east, the border will run between Hauran and Damascus. 📖

It will follow the Jordan between Gilead and Israel’s land.

It will reach the Eastern Sea and go as far as Tamar.

This will be the eastern boundary.

## Ezekiel 47:19

On the south, it will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh. 📖

It will follow the Brook of Egypt to the Great Sea.

This will be the southern boundary.

## Ezekiel 47:20

On the west, the Great Sea will be the boundary up to a point opposite Lebo-hamath. 📖

This will be the western boundary.

## Ezekiel 47:21

Divide this land among yourselves according to Israel’s tribes. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:22

Give shares as an inheritance for yourselves and the foreigners living among you who have children. 📖

Treat them as Israelites born in the land.

Along with you, they must receive an inheritance among Israel’s tribes.

## Ezekiel 47:23

Give a foreigner his inheritance in the tribe where he lives, declares the Lord GOD.” 📖
