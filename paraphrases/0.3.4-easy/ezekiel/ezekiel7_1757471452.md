# Ezekiel 7 — The end has come

**About this chapter**

The LORD repeatedly announces the coming end and judgment.

Wealth, military preparation, and appeals to leaders will not prevent it.

## Ezekiel 7:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:2

“Son of man, the Lord GOD says to Israel’s land, ‘The end! 📖

The end has come on the land’s four corners!

## Ezekiel 7:3

“‘The end is on you now, and I will unleash My anger. 📖

I will judge your ways and repay all your disgusting practices.

## Ezekiel 7:4

“‘I will show no pity or spare you. 📖

I will punish your ways and disgusting practices among you.

Then you will know I am the LORD.’

## Ezekiel 7:5

“The Lord GOD says, ‘Disaster! 📖

A disaster unlike any before is coming!

## Ezekiel 7:6

“‘The end has come! 📖

The end has come!

It rose against you.

Look, it has come!

## Ezekiel 7:7

“‘People of the land, doom has come. 📖

The time has come and the day is near.

There is panic on the mountains instead of joyful shouts.

## Ezekiel 7:8

“‘Very soon, I will pour My anger and fury over you. 📖

I will judge your ways and repay all your disgusting practices.

## Ezekiel 7:9

“‘I will show no pity or spare you. 📖

I will punish your ways and disgusting practices among you.

Then you will know I, the LORD, struck the blow.

## Ezekiel 7:10

“‘Look, the day is here! 📖

It has come!

Doom went out, the rod grew buds, and arrogance blossomed.

## Ezekiel 7:11

“‘Their violence grew into a rod punishing wickedness. 📖

None will remain, not their crowds, wealth, or anything valuable.

**Help with rod and growth**

A rod is a stick used for striking.

The images describe wrongdoing growing into punishment.

## Ezekiel 7:12

“‘The time has come and the day arrived. 📖

Buyers must not rejoice, and sellers must not mourn, because anger is over the whole crowd.

## Ezekiel 7:13

“‘The seller will not recover what he sold while both remain alive. 📖

The vision about the whole crowd will not be canceled.

Because of their wrongdoing, not one will save his life.

## Ezekiel 7:14

“‘They blew the trumpet and prepared everything, but no one goes to war. 📖

My anger is over the whole crowd.

## Ezekiel 7:15

“‘The sword is outside, and plague and famine inside. 📖

Those in the country will die by swords, and famine and plague will consume those in the city.

## Ezekiel 7:16

“‘Survivors will escape to the mountains, moaning like valley doves, each for his own wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:17

“‘Every hand will hang weakly, and every knee turn to water. 📖

**Help with knees turning to water**

The image describes losing strength through fear.

## Ezekiel 7:18

“‘They will wear sackcloth, and terror will overwhelm them. 📖

Shame will cover every face, and all heads will be shaved.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Ezekiel 7:19

“‘They will throw silver in streets, and gold will seem unclean. 📖

Silver and gold cannot save them on the LORD’s day of anger or fill hungry stomachs.

Their wealth became the stumbling block leading to their wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 7:20

“‘They turned His beautiful ornaments into pride and used them to make vile images and detestable idols. 📖

I will make these unclean to them.

**Help with unclean**

Here the word describes something treated as polluted or unacceptable.

## Ezekiel 7:21

“‘I will hand these things to foreigners and wicked people as stolen goods, and they will pollute them. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:22

“‘I will turn My face away, and they will pollute My treasured place. 📖

Violent men will enter and pollute it.

## Ezekiel 7:23

“‘Make the chain, because the land is full of bloodshed crimes and the city full of violence. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:24

“‘I will bring the most wicked nations to take their houses. 📖

I will end the mighty people’s pride, and their holy places will be made unholy.

## Ezekiel 7:25

“‘Anguish is coming! 📖

They will seek peace but find none.

## Ezekiel 7:26

“‘Disaster will follow disaster, and rumor follow rumor. 📖

They will seek a prophet’s vision, but priests’ instruction and elders’ advice will disappear.

## Ezekiel 7:27

“‘The king will mourn, the prince wear despair, and the land’s people’s hands tremble. 📖

I will treat them according to their conduct and judge by their own standards.

Then they will know I am the LORD.’”
