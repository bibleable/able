# Ezekiel 17 — Two eagles and a vine

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives a riddle and explains its political meaning.

He then promises to plant a cedar shoot on Israel’s mountain heights.

## Ezekiel 17:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:2

“Son of man, give Israel’s house a riddle and a parable, 📖

**Help with riddle and parable**

These tell a message through a puzzling comparison or story.

## Ezekiel 17:3

“and tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘A great eagle with great wings, long wing feathers, and many-colored plumage came to Lebanon.

He took the cedar’s top,

## Ezekiel 17:4

“‘plucked its highest shoot, carried it to the merchants’ land, and planted it in a traders’ city. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:5

“‘He took some seed from the land and planted it in fertile soil beside much water, like a willow. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:6

“‘It grew into a low, spreading vine with branches turned toward him and roots remaining where it stood. 📖

It produced branches and shoots.

## Ezekiel 17:7

“‘Another great eagle had great wings and many feathers. 📖

The vine turned its roots and stretched its branches from its planting bed toward him to be watered.

## Ezekiel 17:8

“‘It had been planted in good soil beside much water to grow branches, bear fruit, and become a splendid vine.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 17:9

“Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘Will it flourish?

Will it not be uprooted, stripped of fruit, and shrivel?

All its leaves will wither.

Pulling its roots will not need a strong arm or many people.

## Ezekiel 17:10

“‘Even if moved and planted again, will it flourish? 📖

Will the east wind not make it wither completely?

It will wither in the bed where it grew.’”

## Ezekiel 17:11

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:12

“Ask this rebellious house, ‘Do you not understand?’ 📖

Tell them, ‘Babylon’s king came to Jerusalem, took its king and officials, and brought them to Babylon.

## Ezekiel 17:13

“‘He took a royal family member, made a covenant with him, and put him under oath. 📖

He took away the land’s leading men,

**Help with covenant and oath**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

An oath is a solemn promise.

## Ezekiel 17:14

“‘to keep the kingdom low and unable to rise, surviving only by keeping his covenant. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:15

“‘But this king rebelled against Babylon and sent messengers to Egypt for horses and a large army. 📖

Will he flourish or escape after this?

Can he break a covenant and escape?’

## Ezekiel 17:16

The Lord GOD declares, “‘As surely as I live, he will die in Babylon. 📖

He will die in the land of the king who enthroned him, whose oath and covenant he broke.

## Ezekiel 17:17

“‘Pharaoh’s mighty army and vast crowd will not help in battle. 📖

Attack ramps and siege walls will be built to destroy many lives.

## Ezekiel 17:18

“‘He despised the oath and broke the covenant after giving his hand in pledge. 📖

He did all this and will not escape!

## Ezekiel 17:19

The Lord GOD says, “‘As surely as I live, I will bring My oath and covenant on his head. 📖

He despised My oath and broke My covenant.

## Ezekiel 17:20

“‘“I will spread My net and catch him in My trap. 📖

I will bring him to Babylon and judge his treason against Me.

## Ezekiel 17:21

“‘“All his best troops will fall by swords, and survivors will scatter to every wind. 📖

Then you will know I, the LORD, have spoken.”

## Ezekiel 17:22

“The Lord GOD says, ‘I will take a tender shoot from the cedar’s highest top. 📖

I will take it from the topmost shoots and plant it on a high mountain.

## Ezekiel 17:23

“‘I will plant it on Israel’s mountain heights to grow branches, bear fruit, and become a majestic cedar. 📖

Birds of every kind will nest under it and shelter in its branches’ shade.

## Ezekiel 17:24

“‘All field trees will know I am the LORD. 📖

I bring tall trees low and make low trees tall.

I dry green trees and make withered trees flourish.

I, the LORD, spoke and did it.’”
