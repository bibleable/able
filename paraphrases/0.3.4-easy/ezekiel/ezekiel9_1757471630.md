# Ezekiel 9 — A mark and judgment in the city

**About this chapter**

In the vision, a man marks those grieving over Jerusalem’s wrongdoing.

Others are commanded to kill, with a clear exception for those marked.

Ezekiel cries out about Israel’s remaining people.

## Ezekiel 9:1

I heard Him call loudly, “City executioners, come near, each holding a destructive weapon!” 📖

## Ezekiel 9:2

I saw six men coming from the Upper Gate facing north, each carrying a weapon for killing. 📖

Another man with them wore linen and carried a writing kit at his side.

They entered and stood beside the bronze altar.

## Ezekiel 9:3

Israel’s God’s glory rose from above the cherubim to the temple’s threshold. 📖

He called the linen-clothed man with the writing kit at his side.

**Help with cherubim and threshold**

Cherubim are heavenly beings.

A threshold is the entrance’s sill or boundary.

## Ezekiel 9:4

The LORD said, “Go through Jerusalem. 📖

Mark the foreheads of men sighing and groaning over all the disgusting acts committed there.”

## Ezekiel 9:5

As I listened, He told the others, “Follow him through the city and start killing. 📖

Show no pity and spare no one!

## Ezekiel 9:6

“Kill old men, young men and women, women and children. 📖

But do not go near anyone with the mark.

Begin at My holy place.”

They began with elders before the temple.

**About this severe command**

The vision includes a command to kill children and adults.

The exception for marked people is retained.

This is not an instruction for readers to carry out violence.

## Ezekiel 9:7

He said, “Pollute the temple and fill the courts with the dead. 📖

Go!”

They went out and began killing through the city.

## Ezekiel 9:8

As they killed, I was left alone. 📖

I fell facedown and cried, “Oh, Lord GOD!

Will You destroy all Israel’s survivors when You pour Your anger on Jerusalem?”

## Ezekiel 9:9

He answered, “Israel’s house’s and Judah’s wrongdoing is extremely great. 📖

The land is full of bloodshed and the city of corruption.

They say, ‘The LORD abandoned the land and does not see.’

## Ezekiel 9:10

“But I will show no pity or spare them. 📖

I will bring their deeds down on their own heads.”

## Ezekiel 9:11

The linen-clothed man with the writing kit at his side returned and reported, “I did as You commanded.” 📖
