# Ezekiel 14 — Idols in the heart and individual responsibility

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges elders whose hearts are set on idols.

He describes four judgments and says even Noah, Daniel, and Job could save only themselves.

## Ezekiel 14:1

Some elders of Israel came and sat before me. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:2

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:3

“Son of man, these men set idols in their hearts and wicked stumbling blocks before their faces. 📖

Should I consult with them at all?

**Help with idols and stumbling blocks**

Idols are objects worshiped as gods.

The heart and stumbling images describe loyalties that lead people into wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 14:4

“Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘An Israelite may set idols in his heart and a wicked stumbling block before his face.

When he comes to a prophet, I, the LORD, will answer according to his great idol worship,

## Ezekiel 14:5

“‘so that I may take hold of Israel’s people’s hearts. 📖

Their idols have separated them all from Me.’

## Ezekiel 14:6

“Tell Israel’s house what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘Turn back from idols and turn your faces from all your disgusting practices.

## Ezekiel 14:7

“‘An Israelite or foreigner living in Israel may separate himself from Me and set idols in his heart. 📖

He may put a wicked stumbling block before his face and ask a prophet to inquire of Me.

I, the LORD, will answer him Myself.

## Ezekiel 14:8

“‘I will turn against that man and make him a sign and a saying. 📖

I will cut him off from My people.

Then you will know I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 14:9

“‘If a prophet is enticed to speak, I, the LORD, enticed him. 📖

I will stretch My hand against him and destroy him from My people Israel.

**About this difficult statement**

Entice means draw or persuade someone into an action.

The verse attributes the enticement to the LORD and also announces punishment.

Both claims are retained without changing the first into permission alone.

## Ezekiel 14:10

“‘The person asking and the prophet will bear the same punishment, 📖

## Ezekiel 14:11

The Lord GOD declares, “‘so Israel’s house no longer strays from Me or pollutes itself with offenses. 📖

They will be My people, and I will be their God.’”

## Ezekiel 14:12

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:13

“Son of man, suppose a land sins by being unfaithful to Me. 📖

I may stretch My hand against it, stop its food, send famine, and remove people and animals.

## Ezekiel 14:14

The Lord GOD declares, “Even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were there, their righteousness could rescue only themselves. 📖

**Help with righteousness**

Righteousness means what is right before God.

## Ezekiel 14:15

“Or I may send wild animals to leave the land childless and deserted. 📖

No one will pass through for fear of the animals.

## Ezekiel 14:16

The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, even those three could not save sons or daughters. 📖

Only they would be rescued, and the land would be deserted.

## Ezekiel 14:17

“Or I may bring a sword against the land, saying, ‘Let a sword pass through it.’ 📖

I would remove both people and animals.

## Ezekiel 14:18

The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, even those three could not save sons or daughters. 📖

Only they would be rescued.

## Ezekiel 14:19

“Or I may send plague into the land and pour My anger through bloodshed. 📖

I would remove both people and animals.

## Ezekiel 14:20

The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, even Noah, Daniel, and Job could not save sons or daughters. 📖

Their righteousness could rescue only themselves.

## Ezekiel 14:21

“The Lord GOD says, ‘How much worse when I send My four terrible judgments against Jerusalem! 📖

I will send sword, famine, wild animals, and plague to remove people and animals.

## Ezekiel 14:22

“‘Yet some survivors will remain, sons and daughters brought out to you. 📖

When you see their conduct and actions, you will be comforted about all My disaster against Jerusalem.

## Ezekiel 14:23

The Lord GOD declares, “‘Their conduct and actions will comfort you. 📖

You will know I did not do all these things there without cause.’”
