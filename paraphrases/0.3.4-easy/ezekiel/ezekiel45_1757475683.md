# Ezekiel 45 — Land, honest measures, and offerings

**About this chapter**

God gives instructions for land set apart for Him, the priests, the Levites, the city, and the prince.

He requires honest measures and tells the people and prince what offerings to provide.

The source’s units and amounts are kept.

## Ezekiel 45:1

God said, “When you divide the land by lot as an inheritance, set apart a holy portion for the LORD. 📖

It must be 25,000 cubits long and 20,000 cubits wide.

All this land will be holy.

**Help with these words:** Lots are marked objects used to decide shares.

An inheritance is property given to someone to keep.

A cubit measures from elbow to fingertips.

## Ezekiel 45:2

Within this area, set apart a square of 500 cubits for the holy place. 📖

Leave open land 50 cubits wide around it.

## Ezekiel 45:3

From this holy portion, measure 25,000 cubits in length and 10,000 cubits in width. 📖

The holy place, the Most Holy Place, will be in it.

## Ezekiel 45:4

This holy land will be for the priests who serve in the holy place. 📖

They draw near to serve before the LORD.

It will have space for their houses and a holy area for the holy place.

## Ezekiel 45:5

The Levites serving in the temple will have a neighboring area 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. 📖

It will be their property for towns to live in.

**Help with “Levites”:** These are members of the tribe of Levi.

## Ezekiel 45:6

Set apart land for the city, 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long, beside the holy district. 📖

It will belong to the whole people of Israel.

## Ezekiel 45:7

The prince will have land on each side of the holy district and the city’s property. 📖

It will stretch west from their western side and east from their eastern side.

It will run lengthwise from the western boundary to the eastern boundary, alongside one tribal portion.

## Ezekiel 45:8

This land will be his property in Israel. 📖

My princes will no longer oppress My people.

They will give the rest of the land to Israel’s people according to their tribes.

## Ezekiel 45:9

The Lord GOD says, “Enough, princes of Israel! 📖

Stop your violence and oppression.

Do what is fair and right.

Stop driving My people from their property, declares the Lord GOD.”

## Ezekiel 45:10

You must use honest weighing scales and honest ephah and bath measures. 📖

**Help with the measures:** An ephah measures dry goods.

A bath measures liquids.

## Ezekiel 45:11

The ephah and bath must hold the same amount. 📖

A bath must be one tenth of a homer.

An ephah must also be one tenth of a homer.

The homer must be the standard for both.

## Ezekiel 45:12

A shekel must weigh twenty gerahs. 📖

Twenty shekels plus twenty-five shekels plus fifteen shekels must equal one mina.

**Help with these words:** Shekel, gerah, and mina are units of weight.

## Ezekiel 45:13

This is the contribution you must offer. 📖

Give one sixth of an ephah from each homer of wheat.

Give one sixth of an ephah from each homer of barley.

## Ezekiel 45:14

Measure the required portion of oil by the bath. 📖

Give one tenth of a bath from each cor.

A cor is ten baths, or one homer.

Ten baths equal one homer.

## Ezekiel 45:15

Give one sheep from each flock of two hundred from Israel’s well-watered grasslands. 📖

These are for grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings, to make atonement for the people.

This is what the Lord GOD declares.

**Help with the offerings:** These are gifts and sacrifices to God.

Burnt offerings are burned on the altar.

Peace offerings are connected with fellowship and peace with God.

Atonement deals with wrongdoing and makes things right before God.

## Ezekiel 45:16

All the people of the land must share in this contribution for Israel’s prince. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:17

The prince must provide burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings for the feasts, New Moons, and Sabbaths. 📖

This covers all Israel’s appointed feasts.

He must provide sin offerings, grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings to make atonement for Israel’s people.

**Help with these words:** New Moons mark the beginning of months.

Sabbaths are the special days of rest God commanded.

Sin offerings are sacrifices connected with dealing with sin.

## Ezekiel 45:18

The Lord GOD says, “On the first day of the first month, take a young bull without any defect. 📖

Cleanse the holy place.

## Ezekiel 45:19

The priest must take some blood from the sin offering. 📖

He must put it on the temple’s doorposts, the altar ledge’s four corners, and the inner courtyard’s gateposts.

## Ezekiel 45:20

Do the same on the seventh day of the month for anyone who does wrong unintentionally or without knowing. 📖

In this way, make atonement for the temple.

## Ezekiel 45:21

On the fourteenth day of the first month, keep the Passover. 📖

This is a seven-day feast when bread without yeast must be eaten.

**Help with “Passover”:** This feast remembers God bringing Israel out of Egypt.

## Ezekiel 45:22

On that day, the prince must provide a bull as a sin offering. 📖

It is for himself and all the people of the land.

## Ezekiel 45:23

Each day of the seven-day feast, he must provide seven bulls and seven male sheep without any defect. 📖

These are a burnt offering to the LORD.

He must also provide a male goat for a sin offering.

## Ezekiel 45:24

He must provide an ephah of grain for each bull and an ephah for each male sheep. 📖

He must provide a hin of olive oil for each ephah of grain.

**Help with “hin”:** This is a measure for liquids.

## Ezekiel 45:25

A seven-day feast begins on the fifteenth day of the seventh month. 📖

During it, he must make the same provision for sin offerings, burnt offerings, grain offerings, and oil.”
