# Ezekiel 36 — A new heart and a restored land

**About this chapter**

God promises to restore Israel’s mountains and gather His people.

He says He will act for His holy name.

He promises to cleanse His people, give them a new heart, and put His Spirit within them.

## Ezekiel 36:1

God said, “Son of man, speak My message to Israel’s mountains. 📖

Say, ‘Mountains of Israel, hear the LORD’s word.’

## Ezekiel 36:2

The Lord GOD says, “The enemy said about you, ‘Aha! 📖

The ancient high places have become ours.’

## Ezekiel 36:3

So speak My message and announce what the Lord GOD says. 📖

They have made you empty and crushed you on every side.

The rest of the nations have taken you as their own.

People have spread harmful lies about you.

## Ezekiel 36:4

So, mountains of Israel, hear the Lord GOD’s word. 📖

The Lord GOD speaks to the mountains, hills, deep valleys, and valleys.

He speaks to the empty ruins and abandoned cities.

The rest of the nations around you have robbed you and mocked you.

## Ezekiel 36:5

So the Lord GOD says, “In My burning, jealous anger, I have spoken against the rest of the nations. 📖

I have also spoken against all Edom.

They took My land as their own with wholehearted joy and complete scorn.

They seized its grasslands.

## Ezekiel 36:6

So speak My message about Israel’s land. 📖

Tell the mountains, hills, deep valleys, and valleys what the Lord GOD says.

Look!

I have spoken in My burning, jealous anger because you have suffered the nations’ scorn.

## Ezekiel 36:7

So the Lord GOD says, “I have raised My hand and sworn an oath. 📖

The nations around you will certainly suffer scorn themselves.

## Ezekiel 36:8

But you, mountains of Israel, will grow branches and bear fruit for My people Israel. 📖

They will soon come home.

## Ezekiel 36:9

Look! 📖

I am on your side.

I will turn toward you.

Your soil will be worked and planted with seed.

## Ezekiel 36:10

I will increase the people on you, the whole people of Israel. 📖

People will live in the cities, and the ruins will be rebuilt.

## Ezekiel 36:11

I will fill you with people and animals. 📖

They will increase and have many young.

I will make you as full of people as you once were.

I will make you prosper more than before.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 36:12

Yes, I will make My people Israel walk on you. 📖

They will own you, and you will be their inheritance.

You will no longer take their children away from them.

**Help with “inheritance”:** This means land given to them to keep as their own.

## Ezekiel 36:13

The Lord GOD says, “People say to you, ‘You eat people and take your nation’s children away.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 36:14

So you will no longer eat people or take your nation’s children away, declares the Lord GOD. 📖

**About this picture:** The land is pictured as eating people and taking away children.

The promise reverses this accusation.

## Ezekiel 36:15

I will no longer let the nations’ insults be heard against you. 📖

You will no longer suffer the peoples’ scorn or cause your nation to stumble, declares the Lord GOD.”

## Ezekiel 36:16

The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:17

God said, “Son of man, Israel’s people once lived in their land. 📖

They made it unclean by their ways and deeds.

Before Me, their behavior was like the uncleanness of a woman’s monthly bleeding.

**About “uncleanness”:** This comparison uses the worship laws about monthly bleeding.

Monthly bleeding itself does not make a woman morally bad.

The passage uses the comparison to speak about Israel’s wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 36:18

So I poured My fierce anger over them because of the blood they spilled on the land. 📖

They had also made it unclean with their idols.

## Ezekiel 36:19

I spread them among the nations. 📖

They were scattered throughout the lands.

I judged them according to their ways and deeds.

## Ezekiel 36:20

Wherever they went among the nations, they treated My holy name as unholy. 📖

People said about them, ‘These are the LORD’s people, yet they had to leave His land.’

## Ezekiel 36:21

But I cared about My holy name. 📖

Israel’s people had treated it as unholy among the nations where they went.

## Ezekiel 36:22

So tell Israel’s people what the Lord GOD says. 📖

People of Israel, I will act for My holy name, rather than for your sake.

You treated My name as unholy among the nations where you went.

## Ezekiel 36:23

I will show the holiness of My great name. 📖

It has been treated as unholy among the nations.

You treated it as unholy among them.

I will show My holiness through you before their eyes.

Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 36:24

I will take you from among the nations and gather you from all the countries. 📖

I will bring you back into your own land.

## Ezekiel 36:25

I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you will be clean. 📖

I will cleanse you from all your impurities and all your idols.

**Help with “cleanse”:** Here God promises to remove what makes His people unclean before Him.

## Ezekiel 36:26

I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you. 📖

I will remove your heart of stone and give you a heart of flesh.

**Help with this picture:** A stone heart pictures a hard, unresponsive heart.

A heart of flesh pictures a living heart able to respond to God.

## Ezekiel 36:27

I will put My Spirit within you. 📖

I will cause you to follow My laws and carefully obey My rules.

## Ezekiel 36:28

Then you will live in the land I gave your ancestors. 📖

You will be My people, and I will be your God.

## Ezekiel 36:29

I will save you from all your uncleanness. 📖

I will call for grain and make it plentiful.

I will not bring famine on you.

**Help with “famine”:** This is a time when there is not enough food.

## Ezekiel 36:30

I will also make the trees’ fruit and the fields’ crops plentiful. 📖

Then you will no longer suffer the nations’ scorn because of famine.

## Ezekiel 36:31

Then you will remember your evil ways and wicked deeds. 📖

You will hate yourselves because of your sins and horrible acts.

## Ezekiel 36:32

Know this, declares the Lord GOD. 📖

I will not act for your sake.

People of Israel, be ashamed and disgraced because of your ways!

## Ezekiel 36:33

The Lord GOD says, “On the day I cleanse you from all your sins, I will have the cities resettled. 📖

I will have the ruins rebuilt.

## Ezekiel 36:34

The empty land will be farmed. 📖

It will no longer lie empty for everyone passing by to see.

## Ezekiel 36:35

Then people will say, ‘This empty land has become like the garden of Eden. 📖

The cities were once ruined, empty, and destroyed.

Now they have strong defenses, and people live in them.’

## Ezekiel 36:36

Then the nations left around you will know that I, the LORD, have rebuilt what was destroyed. 📖

I have replanted what was empty.

I, the LORD, have spoken.

I will do it.

## Ezekiel 36:37

The Lord GOD says, “Once again, I will hear Israel’s plea and do this for them. 📖

I will increase their people like a flock.

## Ezekiel 36:38

There are great flocks for sacrifices at Jerusalem during its appointed feasts. 📖

In the same way, the ruined cities will be filled with flocks of people.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
