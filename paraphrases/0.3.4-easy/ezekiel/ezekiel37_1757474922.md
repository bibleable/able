# Ezekiel 37 — Dry bones and two joined sticks

**About this chapter**

God shows Ezekiel a valley of dry bones and explains the vision as a promise for Israel.

Two sticks joined together picture a united people.

God promises His Spirit, one ruler, peace, and His holy place among them.

## Ezekiel 37:1

The LORD’s hand was on me. 📖

He brought me out by His Spirit.

He set me in the middle of a valley full of bones.

**Help with “the LORD’s hand”:** This describes God’s power acting on Ezekiel.

## Ezekiel 37:2

He led me all around among the bones. 📖

I saw very many bones on the valley floor.

They were very dry.

## Ezekiel 37:3

He asked me, “Son of man, can these bones come to life?” 📖

I replied, “Lord GOD, only You know.”

## Ezekiel 37:4

He said to me, “Speak My message about these bones. 📖

Tell them, ‘Dry bones, hear the LORD’s word!

## Ezekiel 37:5

The Lord GOD says to these bones, “I will make breath enter you. 📖

You will come to life.

## Ezekiel 37:6

I will attach tendons to you and make flesh grow on you. 📖

I will cover you with skin.

I will put breath in you, so you come to life.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

**Help with “tendons”:** These connect muscles to bones.

## Ezekiel 37:7

So I spoke God’s message as I had been commanded. 📖

As I spoke, there was suddenly a rattling sound.

The bones came together, bone to bone.

## Ezekiel 37:8

As I watched, tendons appeared on them. 📖

Flesh grew, and skin covered them.

But there was no breath in them.

## Ezekiel 37:9

Then God said to me, “Son of man, speak My message to the breath. 📖

Tell the breath what the Lord GOD says.

Breath, come from the four winds!

Breathe into these people who were killed, so they may live!”

## Ezekiel 37:10

So I spoke God’s message as He had commanded me. 📖

The breath entered them.

They came to life and stood on their feet, a very large army.

## Ezekiel 37:11

Then God said to me, “Son of man, these bones are the whole people of Israel. 📖

Look!

They say, ‘Our bones are dried up, and our hope has died.

We are cut off.’

## Ezekiel 37:12

So speak My message and tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

My people, I will open your graves and bring you up from them.

I will bring you back to the land of Israel.

## Ezekiel 37:13

My people, I will open your graves and bring you up from them. 📖

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 37:14

I will put My Spirit in you, and you will live. 📖

I will settle you in your own land.

Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken.

I will do it, declares the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 37:15

The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:16

God said, “Son of man, take one stick and write on it. 📖

Write, ‘Belonging to Judah and the Israelites joined with him.’

Take another stick and write on it.

Write, ‘Belonging to Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and all Israel’s people joined with him.’

## Ezekiel 37:17

Join the sticks together into one stick. 📖

They will become one in your hand.

## Ezekiel 37:18

Your people will ask you, ‘Will you explain what you mean by these?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 37:19

Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

I will take Joseph’s stick, which is in Ephraim’s hand.

I will take the tribes of Israel joined with him.

I will put them together with Judah’s stick.

I will make them one stick.

They will become one in My hand.

## Ezekiel 37:20

Hold the sticks you have written on in your hand, where the people can clearly see them. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:21

Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

I will take the Israelites out of the nations where they have gone.

I will gather them from all around and bring them into their own land.

## Ezekiel 37:22

I will make them one nation in the land, on Israel’s mountains. 📖

One king will rule them all.

They will no longer be two nations.

They will never again be divided into two kingdoms.

## Ezekiel 37:23

They will no longer make themselves unclean with their idols, horrible images, or any of their wrong actions. 📖

I will save them from all their unfaithfulness through which they sinned.

I will cleanse them.

Then they will be My people, and I will be their God.

## Ezekiel 37:24

My servant David will be king over them. 📖

There will be one shepherd for them all.

They will follow My rules and carefully obey My laws.

## Ezekiel 37:25

They will live in the land I gave My servant Jacob, where your ancestors lived. 📖

They will live there forever with their children and grandchildren.

My servant David will be their prince forever.

## Ezekiel 37:26

I will make a covenant of peace with them. 📖

It will be a covenant that lasts forever.

I will establish them and increase them.

I will put My holy place among them forever.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Ezekiel 37:27

My home will be with them. 📖

I will be their God, and they will be My people.

## Ezekiel 37:28

My holy place will be among them forever. 📖

Then the nations will know that I, the LORD, make Israel holy.”
