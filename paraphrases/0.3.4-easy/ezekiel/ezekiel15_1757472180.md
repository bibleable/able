# Ezekiel 15 — The vine’s wood in the fire

**About this chapter**

The LORD compares Jerusalem’s people with vine wood used as fuel.

The comparison leads to a warning of further destruction.

## Ezekiel 15:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 15:2

“Son of man, how is vine wood better than any other branch among forest trees? 📖

## Ezekiel 15:3

“Can its wood make anything useful? 📖

Can it make a peg for hanging utensils?

**Help with utensils**

Utensils are tools or objects used for everyday work.

## Ezekiel 15:4

“No, it is thrown into the fire as fuel. 📖

Fire consumes both ends and burns the middle black.

Can it be useful for anything?

## Ezekiel 15:5

“Even whole, it could not be made useful. 📖

How much less useful after fire consumed and burned it!

## Ezekiel 15:6

“The Lord GOD says, ‘I gave forest vine wood to the fire as fuel. 📖

I will give Jerusalem’s people up in the same way.

## Ezekiel 15:7

“‘I will turn against them. 📖

Though they escaped fire, another fire will consume them.

When I turn against them, you will know I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 15:8

The Lord GOD declares, “‘I will make the land deserted because they acted unfaithfully.’” 📖
