# Ezekiel 3 — The scroll and the watchman’s task

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel eats the scroll and receives his task among the exiles.

God explains his responsibility to warn both wicked and righteous people.

## Ezekiel 3:1

He said, “Son of man, eat what is here. 📖

Eat this scroll, then speak to Israel’s house.”

## Ezekiel 3:2

I opened my mouth, and He fed me the scroll. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:3

He said, “Son of man, fill your stomach with this scroll I give you.” 📖

I ate it, and it tasted sweet as honey.

## Ezekiel 3:4

He told me, “Son of man, go to Israel’s house and speak My words. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:5

“You are not sent to people with unfamiliar speech or difficult language, but to Israel’s house, 📖

## Ezekiel 3:6

“not to many peoples whose unfamiliar, difficult words you cannot understand. 📖

If I sent you to them, they would surely listen.

## Ezekiel 3:7

“But Israel’s house will not listen to you because they will not listen to Me. 📖

The whole house is hard-headed and hard-hearted.

## Ezekiel 3:8

“I will make your face hard as theirs and your forehead hard as their foreheads. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:9

“I will make your forehead like diamond, harder than flint. 📖

Do not fear or be distressed before them, though they are rebellious.”

**Help with diamond and flint**

These hard materials picture firmness to face opposition.

## Ezekiel 3:10

He added, “Son of man, hear all My words carefully and take them to heart. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:11

“Go to your people, the exiles, and tell them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’ 📖

Speak whether they listen or refuse.”

## Ezekiel 3:12

The Spirit lifted me, and I heard loud rumbling behind me. 📖

“Blessed be the LORD’s glory in His dwelling place!”

## Ezekiel 3:13

It was the loud rumble of creatures’ wings brushing each other and wheels beside them. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:14

The Spirit lifted and took me away. 📖

I went bitter and angry in my spirit, with the LORD’s strong hand on me.

## Ezekiel 3:15

I reached the exiles at Tel-abib beside the River Kebar. 📖

I sat where they sat for seven days, overwhelmed among them.

## Ezekiel 3:16

After seven days, the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:17

“Son of man, I made you a watchman for Israel’s house. 📖

When you hear a word from My mouth, warn them from Me.

**Help with watchman**

A watchman looks out for danger and warns people.

## Ezekiel 3:18

“I may tell a wicked man, ‘You will surely die.’ 📖

If you do not warn him to turn from wicked ways and save his life, he will die in wrongdoing.

I will hold you responsible for his blood.

## Ezekiel 3:19

“But if you warn him and he refuses to leave his wickedness and evil way, he will die in wrongdoing. 📖

You will have saved yourself.

## Ezekiel 3:20

“If a righteous man turns from righteousness to wrongdoing and I put a stumbling block before him, he will die. 📖

If you did not warn him, he will die in sin, and his righteous deeds will not be remembered.

I will hold you responsible for his blood.

## Ezekiel 3:21

“But if you warn a righteous man not to sin and he does not sin, he will live. 📖

He listened to your warning, and you will have saved yourself.”

**Help with righteous**

Righteous means right before God.

The warnings distinguish the person’s response from Ezekiel’s responsibility to speak.

## Ezekiel 3:22

The LORD’s hand was on me there. 📖

He said, “Get up and go to the plain, where I will speak with you.”

## Ezekiel 3:23

I rose and went to the plain. 📖

The LORD’s glory was there, like the glory I saw beside the River Kebar.

I fell facedown.

## Ezekiel 3:24

The Spirit entered me and set me on my feet. 📖

He told me, “Go and shut yourself inside your house.

## Ezekiel 3:25

“Son of man, they will tie you with ropes so that you cannot go out among the people. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:26

“I will make your tongue stick to your mouth’s roof. 📖

You will be silent and unable to rebuke them, though they are rebellious.

## Ezekiel 3:27

“But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth. 📖

Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’

Let those who listen hear, and those who refuse refuse, because they are rebellious.”
