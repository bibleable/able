# Ezekiel 12 — Baggage for exile

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel acts out leaving home as an exile.

The LORD explains the sign, commands another sign with food and water, and challenges claims that fulfillment will be delayed.

## Ezekiel 12:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:2

“Son of man, you live among a rebellious house. 📖

They have eyes but do not see, and ears but do not hear, because they are rebellious.

**Help with sight and hearing**

The image describes refusal to understand and obey.

It does not make a physical disability a moral fault.

## Ezekiel 12:3

“Son of man, pack for exile. 📖

In full daylight, leave your place for another where they can watch.

Perhaps they will understand, though rebellious.

## Ezekiel 12:4

“Bring out your exile baggage by day as they watch. 📖

In the evening, leave as exiles do, still where they can watch.

## Ezekiel 12:5

“As they watch, dig through the wall and carry your things through it. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:6

“As they watch, lift your bags to your shoulder and carry them out at dusk. 📖

Cover your face so you cannot see the land.

I made you a sign to Israel’s house.”

## Ezekiel 12:7

I did as commanded and brought out my exile bags by day. 📖

In the evening, I dug through the wall by hand.

I carried my things on my shoulder at dusk as they watched.

## Ezekiel 12:8

In the morning, the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:9

“Son of man, did rebellious Israel’s house not ask, ‘What are you doing?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 12:10

“Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘This burden concerns Jerusalem’s prince and all Israel’s house there.’

**Help with burden**

Here burden means a weighty message of warning.

## Ezekiel 12:11

“Say, ‘I am a sign to you.’ 📖

What happened here will happen to them, and they will go into exile as captives.

## Ezekiel 12:12

“At dusk, their prince will shoulder his bags and leave. 📖

They will dig through the wall to bring him out.

He will cover his face so he cannot see the land.

## Ezekiel 12:13

“I will spread My net over him and catch him in My trap. 📖

I will take him to Babylon in the Chaldeans’ land.

He will not see it, yet he will die there.

## Ezekiel 12:14

“I will scatter all his attendants and troops to every wind and draw a sword to pursue them. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:15

“They will know I am the LORD when I scatter them among nations and countries. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:16

“But I will spare a few from sword, famine, and plague. 📖

In the nations they reach, they can tell all their disgusting acts.

Then they will know I am the LORD.”

**Help with famine and plague**

Famine is a severe shortage of food.

A plague is a widespread, deadly disease.

## Ezekiel 12:17

The LORD’s word also came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:18

“Son of man, tremble as you eat bread and shake with anxiety as you drink water. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:19

“Tell the land’s people what the Lord GOD says about Jerusalem’s and Israel’s residents. 📖

‘They will eat anxiously and drink in dread.

Their land will be stripped of everything because of all its residents’ violence.

## Ezekiel 12:20

“‘Inhabited cities will be ruined, and the land deserted. 📖

Then you will know I am the LORD.’”

## Ezekiel 12:21

Again the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:22

“Son of man, what is this saying in Israel’s land? 📖

‘Days pass, and every vision fails.’

## Ezekiel 12:23

“Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘I will end this saying, and Israel will stop repeating it.’

Tell them, ‘The days when every vision will be fulfilled are near.

## Ezekiel 12:24

“‘There will be no false visions or flattering fortune-telling in Israel’s house, 📖

## Ezekiel 12:25

The Lord GOD declares, “‘because I, the LORD, will speak, and My word will happen without delay. 📖

In your own days, rebellious house, I will speak and carry it out.’”

## Ezekiel 12:26

The LORD’s word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:27

“Son of man, Israel’s house says, ‘His vision is for many years ahead. 📖

His prophecy concerns the distant future.’

## Ezekiel 12:28

“Tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘None of My words will be delayed anymore.

The message I speak will happen,’ declares the Lord GOD.”
