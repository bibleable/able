# Ezekiel 20 — Repeated rebellion and the LORD’s name

**About this chapter**

The LORD answers elders by recalling repeated rebellion in Egypt, the wilderness, and the land.

He speaks of judgment and gathering, then gives a warning about fire in the Negev.

## Ezekiel 20:1

It was the seventh year, the tenth day of the fifth month. 📖

Some elders of Israel came to inquire of the LORD and sat before me.

## Ezekiel 20:2

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:3

“Son of man, tell Israel’s elders what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘Did you come to inquire of Me?

As surely as I live, I will not let you consult Me,’ declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 20:4

“Will you judge them, son of man? 📖

Will you judge them?

Confront them with their fathers’ disgusting acts,

## Ezekiel 20:5

“and tell them what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘On the day I chose Israel, I swore to Jacob’s house’s descendants and revealed Myself in Egypt.

With raised hand, I said, “I am the LORD your God.”

## Ezekiel 20:6

“That day, I swore to bring them from Egypt to a land I searched out. 📖

It flowed with milk and honey, the glory of all lands.

**Help with milk and honey**

The image describes a rich, productive land.

## Ezekiel 20:7

“I said, “Each must throw away the disgusting things before his eyes. 📖

Do not pollute yourselves with Egypt’s idols.

I am the LORD your God.”

## Ezekiel 20:8

“But they rebelled and refused to listen. 📖

None threw away those disgusting things or abandoned Egypt’s idols.

I decided to pour My anger and fury on them in Egypt.

## Ezekiel 20:9

“But I acted for My name’s sake, so nations among whom they lived would not dishonor it. 📖

I revealed Myself before those nations by bringing Israel from Egypt.

## Ezekiel 20:10

“I brought them from Egypt into the wilderness. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:11

“I gave My commands and made My rules known. 📖

A person who does these things will live by them.

## Ezekiel 20:12

“I gave My Sabbaths as a sign between us, so they knew I am the LORD who makes them holy. 📖

**Help with Sabbath and holy**

The Sabbath is the weekly day set apart for rest and worship.

Holy means set apart for God.

## Ezekiel 20:13

“Yet Israel’s house rebelled in the wilderness and rejected My commands and rules. 📖

Whoever does these things will live by them, but they completely dishonored My Sabbaths.

I decided to pour My anger on them and end them in the wilderness.

## Ezekiel 20:14

“But I acted for My name’s sake, so nations seeing their departure would not dishonor it. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:15

“With raised hand in the wilderness, I swore not to bring them to the land I gave them. 📖

It flowed with milk and honey, the glory of all lands.

## Ezekiel 20:16

“They kept rejecting My rules, refusing My commands, and dishonoring My Sabbaths. 📖

Their hearts kept pursuing idols.

## Ezekiel 20:17

“Yet I pitied them and did not destroy or end them in the wilderness. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:18

“I told their children in the wilderness, “Do not follow your fathers’ commands or keep their rules. 📖

Do not pollute yourselves with their idols.

## Ezekiel 20:19

“I am the LORD your God. 📖

Follow My commands, keep My rules, and do them.

## Ezekiel 20:20

“Keep My Sabbaths holy as a sign between us so you know I am the LORD your God.” 📖

## Ezekiel 20:21

“But the children rebelled, ignored My commands and rules, and dishonored My Sabbaths. 📖

Whoever does these things will live by them.

I decided to pour My anger and fury on them in the wilderness.

## Ezekiel 20:22

“But I held My hand back for My name’s sake, so nations seeing their departure would not dishonor it. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:23

“Yet I raised My hand and swore in the wilderness to scatter them among nations and lands. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:24

“They did not practice My rules, rejected My commands, dishonored My Sabbaths, and fixed their eyes on their fathers’ idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:25

“I also gave them over to commands that were not good and rules by which they could not live. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:26

“I declared them unclean through their gifts, sacrificing every firstborn in fire. 📖

I did this to devastate them so they would know I am the LORD.

**About this difficult statement**

Verses 25–26 attribute these actions to the LORD, including judgment through destructive practices.

The wording does not become a command for readers to sacrifice children.

The difficult divine agency is retained for further editorial review.

## Ezekiel 20:27

“Son of man, tell Israel’s house what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘Your fathers also insulted Me by this unfaithfulness.

## Ezekiel 20:28

“I brought them into the promised land. 📖

Wherever they saw high hills or leafy trees, they offered sacrifices and gifts that angered Me.

They sent up fragrant incense and poured drink offerings there.

## Ezekiel 20:29

“I asked, “What is this high place you go to?” 📖

It is called Bamah to this day.

**Help with Bamah**

Bamah means high place, a place used for worship.

## Ezekiel 20:30

“Tell Israel’s house what the Lord GOD says. 📖

‘Will you pollute yourselves as your fathers did, prostituting yourselves with their disgusting practices?

## Ezekiel 20:31

“By gifts and sons sacrificed in fire, you keep polluting yourselves with idols to this day. 📖

Should Israel’s house consult Me?

As surely as I live, I will not let you consult Me,’ declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 20:32

“You say, “Let us be like other nations and peoples, serving wood and stone.” 📖

What you plan will never happen.

## Ezekiel 20:33

The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, I will rule you. 📖

I will use a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and outpoured wrath.

## Ezekiel 20:34

“With a strong hand, outstretched arm, and outpoured wrath, I will bring you from the peoples. 📖

I will gather you from the lands where you were scattered.

## Ezekiel 20:35

“I will bring you into the nations’ wilderness and judge you face to face. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:36

The Lord GOD declares, “I judged your fathers in Egypt’s wilderness, and I will judge you in the same way. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:37

“I will make you pass under the rod and bring you into the covenant’s bond. 📖

**Help with rod and covenant**

The rod pictures being counted or examined under God’s authority.

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Ezekiel 20:38

“I will remove rebels and offenders from among you. 📖

I will bring them from the land where they live, but they will not enter Israel’s land.

Then you will know I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 20:39

The Lord GOD tells Israel’s house, “Go, each of you, and serve your idols! 📖

But afterward, you will listen to Me and stop polluting My holy name with gifts and idols.

## Ezekiel 20:40

The Lord GOD declares, “On My holy mountain, Israel’s high mountain, Israel’s whole house will serve Me in the land. 📖

There I will accept them and require offerings, choice gifts, and all holy sacrifices.

## Ezekiel 20:41

“When I gather you from peoples and lands where you were scattered, I will accept you like a pleasing smell. 📖

I will show My holiness through you before the nations.

## Ezekiel 20:42

“You will know I am the LORD when I bring you to Israel’s land, which I swore to your fathers. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:43

“There you will remember the ways and deeds that polluted you. 📖

You will hate yourselves for all your evil.

## Ezekiel 20:44

The Lord GOD declares, “Israel’s house, you will know I am the LORD. 📖

I will deal with you for My name’s sake, not according to your wicked ways and corrupt acts.”

## Ezekiel 20:45

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:46

“Son of man, face south, preach against it, and prophesy against the Negev’s forest. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:47

“Tell the Negev’s forest to hear the LORD’s word. 📖

The Lord GOD says, ‘I will light a fire consuming every tree, green and dry.

It will not be put out, and every face from south to north will be scorched.

## Ezekiel 20:48

“Everyone will see that I, the LORD, lit it. 📖

It will not be put out.”

## Ezekiel 20:49

I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! 📖

They say about me, ‘Is he only telling parables?”
