# Ezekiel 6 — Judgment on Israel’s places of false worship

**About this chapter**

The LORD addresses Israel’s mountains, hills, ravines, and valleys.

He announces destruction and also speaks about a group surviving among the nations.

## Ezekiel 6:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:2

“Son of man, face Israel’s mountains and prophesy against them. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:3

“Say, ‘Mountains of Israel, hear the Lord GOD’s word! 📖

The Lord GOD speaks to mountains, hills, ravines, and valleys.

I will bring a sword against you and destroy your high places.

**Help with high places**

These were places used for worship.

## Ezekiel 6:4

“‘Your altars will be demolished and incense altars smashed. 📖

I will throw your dead before your idols.

## Ezekiel 6:5

“‘I will lay Israelites’ bodies before their idols and scatter your bones around your altars. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:6

“‘Wherever you live, cities and high places will be ruined. 📖

Your altars will be ruined and made unholy, your idols smashed and destroyed.

Your incense altars will be cut down and your works wiped out.

## Ezekiel 6:7

“‘The dead will fall among you, and you will know I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:8

“‘Yet I will leave survivors. 📖

Some will escape the sword when you are scattered through nations and lands.

## Ezekiel 6:9

“‘Your captive survivors will remember Me in those nations. 📖

Their adulterous hearts turned away, and their eyes desired idols, grieving Me.

They will hate themselves for their evil and all their disgusting acts.

**Help with adulterous hearts**

Adultery is unfaithfulness in marriage.

Here the heart image describes unfaithfulness to God.

The self-loathing is part of this judgment description, not a general instruction to hate oneself.

## Ezekiel 6:10

“‘They will know I am the LORD and did not speak empty words about bringing this disaster. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:11

“‘The Lord GOD says, “Clap your hands, stamp your feet, and cry ‘Alas!’ 📖

Israel’s house has done wicked, disgusting things and will fall by sword, famine, and plague.

**Help with Alas and these judgments**

Alas is a cry of grief.

Famine is a severe shortage of food, and plague is a widespread deadly disease.

## Ezekiel 6:12

“‘“Those far away will die by plague, those near by sword, and those remaining by famine. 📖

I will pour My fury on them.

## Ezekiel 6:13

“‘“You will know I am the LORD when their dead lie among idols around altars. 📖

They will lie on every high hill and mountaintop, under every green tree and leafy oak.

These were places where they offered fragrant incense to all their idols.

**Help with incense**

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Ezekiel 6:14

“‘“I will stretch My hand against them. 📖

Wherever they live, I will make the land deserted, from the wilderness to Diblah.

Then they will know I am the LORD.”’”
