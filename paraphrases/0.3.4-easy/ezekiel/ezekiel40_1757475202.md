# Ezekiel 40 — The vision of a measured temple

**About this chapter**

God takes Ezekiel to Israel in a vision.

A man shows him a temple area and measures its walls, gates, courtyards, and rooms.

The measurements use cubits and a measuring rod, as in BSB.

## Ezekiel 40:1

It was the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year. 📖

It was the tenth day of the month, fourteen years after Jerusalem had been struck down.

On that very day, the LORD’s hand was on me.

He took me there.

**Help with these words:** Exile means being forced to live away from our land.

The LORD’s hand describes His power acting on Ezekiel.

## Ezekiel 40:2

In visions from God, He took me to the land of Israel. 📖

He set me on a very high mountain.

On its southern slope was a structure that looked like a city.

## Ezekiel 40:3

He took me there, and I saw a man who looked like bronze. 📖

He stood in the gateway with a linen cord and a measuring rod in his hand.

## Ezekiel 40:4

The man said to me, “Son of man, look with your eyes and listen with your ears. 📖

Pay attention to everything I will show you.

That is why you have been brought here.

Tell Israel’s people everything you see.”

## Ezekiel 40:5

I saw a wall around the temple area. 📖

The man’s measuring rod was six long cubits long.

Each long cubit was one cubit and one handbreadth.

He measured the wall as one rod thick and one rod high.

**Help with the measurements:** A cubit measures from the elbow to the fingertips.

A handbreadth is the width of a hand.

The measuring rod here is six long cubits long.

The draft keeps the source’s units.

## Ezekiel 40:6

He came to the gate facing east and climbed its steps. 📖

He measured the gate’s entrance floor as one rod deep.

**Help with “entrance floor”:** BSB calls this the “threshold.”

## Ezekiel 40:7

Each gate room was one rod long and one rod wide. 📖

There were five cubits between the gate rooms.

The inner entrance floor beside the porch facing inward was one rod deep.

**Help with “porch”:** BSB calls this a “portico,” a covered entrance area.

## Ezekiel 40:8

Then he measured the porch inside the gateway. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:9

It was eight cubits deep. 📖

Its doorway sides were two cubits thick.

The gateway’s porch faced the temple.

**Help with “doorway sides”:** BSB calls these “jambs.”

## Ezekiel 40:10

There were three gate rooms on each side of the east gate. 📖

All had the same measurements.

The gateposts on either side also had the same measurements.

## Ezekiel 40:11

He measured the gateway entrance as ten cubits wide and thirteen cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:12

In front of each gate room was a wall one cubit high. 📖

The gate rooms were six cubits square.

**Help with “square”:** Each side had the same length.

## Ezekiel 40:13

He measured from the roof of one gate room to the roof of the room opposite. 📖

It was twenty-five cubits from doorway to doorway.

## Ezekiel 40:14

Next, he measured the gateposts as sixty cubits high. 📖

The gateway extended around to the courtyard’s gatepost.

## Ezekiel 40:15

The distance from the gateway entrance to the far end of its inner porch was fifty cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:16

The gate rooms and their side pillars had windows with sloping edges all around inside the gateway. 📖

The porches also had windows all around inside.

Each side pillar was decorated with palm trees.

**Help with “sloping edges”:** BSB describes the windows as “beveled.”

## Ezekiel 40:17

He brought me into the outer courtyard. 📖

Rooms and a paved area were arranged all around the courtyard.

Thirty rooms faced the paved area,

## Ezekiel 40:18

which ran beside the gateways and matched the gates’ length. 📖

This was the lower paved area.

## Ezekiel 40:19

He measured from the front of the lower gateway to the outside of the inner courtyard. 📖

It was a hundred cubits on both the east side and the north side.

## Ezekiel 40:20

He also measured the length and width of the outer courtyard’s gateway facing north. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:21

It had three gate rooms on each side, side pillars, and a porch. 📖

Their measurements matched the first gate’s measurements.

It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:22

Its windows, porch, and palm trees had the same measurements as the east gate’s. 📖

Seven steps led up to it, with its porch opposite them.

## Ezekiel 40:23

The inner courtyard had a gate facing the north gate, just as on the east side. 📖

He measured a hundred cubits from gateway to gateway.

## Ezekiel 40:24

Then he led me to the south side. 📖

I saw a gateway facing south.

He measured its side pillars and porch.

Their measurements matched the others.

## Ezekiel 40:25

The gateway and its porch had windows all around, like the other windows. 📖

It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:26

Seven steps led up to it. 📖

Its porch was opposite them.

Its side pillars had palm trees, one on each side.

## Ezekiel 40:27

The inner courtyard also had a gate facing south. 📖

He measured a hundred cubits from gateway to gateway toward the south.

## Ezekiel 40:28

Next, he brought me into the inner courtyard through the south gate. 📖

He measured the south gate.

Its measurements matched the others.

## Ezekiel 40:29

Its gate rooms, side pillars, and porch had the same measurements as the others. 📖

The gateway and its porch had windows all around.

It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:30

The porches around the inner courtyard were twenty-five cubits long and five cubits deep. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:31

Its porch faced the outer courtyard. 📖

Its side pillars were decorated with palm trees.

Eight steps led up to it.

## Ezekiel 40:32

He brought me to the inner courtyard on the east side. 📖

He measured the gateway.

Its measurements matched the others.

## Ezekiel 40:33

Its gate rooms, side pillars, and porch had the same measurements as the others. 📖

The gateway and its porch had windows all around.

It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:34

Its porch faced the outer courtyard. 📖

Its side pillars were decorated with palm trees on each side.

Eight steps led up to it.

## Ezekiel 40:35

Then he brought me to the north gate and measured it. 📖

Its measurements matched the others,

## Ezekiel 40:36

as did those of its gate rooms, side pillars, and porch. 📖

It also had windows all around.

It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:37

Its porch faced the outer courtyard. 📖

Its side pillars were decorated with palm trees on each side.

Eight steps led up to it.

## Ezekiel 40:38

By the porch in each inner gateway was a room with a doorway. 📖

The burnt offering was to be washed there.

**Help with “burnt offering”:** This is a sacrifice burned on an altar for God.

## Ezekiel 40:39

Inside the gateway’s porch were two tables on each side. 📖

The burnt offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings were to be killed on them.

**About the offerings:** Sin offerings and guilt offerings are sacrifices connected with dealing with wrongdoing.

## Ezekiel 40:40

Outside, going up to the north gateway’s entrance, there were two tables on one side. 📖

Two more tables were on the other side of the gate’s porch.

## Ezekiel 40:41

There were four tables inside the gateway and four outside, eight tables in all. 📖

The sacrifices were to be killed on them.

## Ezekiel 40:42

There were also four tables of shaped stone for the burnt offering. 📖

Each was one and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one cubit high.

The tools for killing the burnt offerings and other sacrifices were placed on them.

## Ezekiel 40:43

Hooks with two points were fixed all around the inside of the room. 📖

Each hook was one handbreadth long.

The meat of the offering was to be placed on the tables.

## Ezekiel 40:44

Outside the inner gate, within the inner courtyard, were two rooms. 📖

One was beside the north gate and faced south.

The other was beside the south gate and faced north.

## Ezekiel 40:45

The man told me, “The room facing south is for the priests responsible for the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:46

The room facing north is for the priests responsible for the altar. 📖

These are Zadok’s sons, the only Levites who may approach the LORD to serve before Him.”

**Help with “Levites”:** These are members of the tribe of Levi.

## Ezekiel 40:47

Next, he measured the courtyard. 📖

It was square, a hundred cubits long and a hundred cubits wide.

The altar was in front of the temple.

## Ezekiel 40:48

He brought me to the temple’s porch. 📖

He measured the porch’s side pillars as five cubits on each side.

The gateway was fourteen cubits wide.

Its sidewalls were three cubits on either side.

## Ezekiel 40:49

The porch was twenty cubits wide and twelve cubits deep. 📖

Ten steps led up to it.

There were columns beside the side pillars, one on each side.
