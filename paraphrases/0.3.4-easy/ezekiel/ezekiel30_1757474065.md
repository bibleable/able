# Ezekiel 30 — The coming fall of Egypt

**About this chapter**

God announces a day of judgment for Egypt and those who support it.

He names the affected peoples and cities.

He also pictures Pharaoh’s defeat as broken arms.

## Ezekiel 30:1

The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:2

God said, “Son of man, speak My message. 📖

Announce what the Lord GOD says.

Cry out in grief, ‘How terrible that day will be!’

## Ezekiel 30:3

The day is near. 📖

The Day of the LORD is near.

It will be a day of clouds, a time of destruction for the nations.

**Help with “the Day of the LORD”:** Here this means the time when God brings judgment.

## Ezekiel 30:4

A sword will come against Egypt. 📖

People will be killed there.

Egypt’s wealth will be taken away, and its foundations will be torn down.

Then Cush will be in deep distress.

## Ezekiel 30:5

Cush, Put, Lud, and all the various peoples will fall with Egypt by the sword. 📖

So will Libya and the men of the covenant land.

**About “the covenant land”:** The draft keeps this description without adding a name not given in this verse.

## Ezekiel 30:6

The LORD says, “Those who support Egypt will fall. 📖

Her proud strength will collapse.

From Migdol to Syene, they will fall by the sword inside her, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 30:7

They will be empty among empty lands. 📖

Their cities will lie among ruined cities.

## Ezekiel 30:8

I will set fire to Egypt, and all her helpers will be shattered. 📖

Then they will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 30:9

On that day, messengers will go out from Me in ships. 📖

They will frighten Cush out of its careless sense of safety.

Distress will come on Cush on the day of Egypt’s destruction.

That day is certainly coming.

## Ezekiel 30:10

The Lord GOD says, “I will use Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, to end Egypt’s great crowds. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:11

He and his people are the cruelest of the nations. 📖

They will be brought in to destroy the land.

They will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with dead bodies.

## Ezekiel 30:12

I will dry up the streams and sell the land to wicked people. 📖

Through foreigners, I will ruin the land and everything in it.

I, the LORD, have spoken.

## Ezekiel 30:13

The Lord GOD says, “I will destroy the idols and end the images in Memphis. 📖

There will no longer be a prince in Egypt.

I will fill that land with fear.

## Ezekiel 30:14

I will ruin Pathros, set fire to Zoan, and carry out judgment on Thebes. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:15

I will pour My fierce anger over Pelusium, Egypt’s place of strong defense. 📖

I will cut off the crowds of Thebes.

## Ezekiel 30:16

I will set fire to Egypt. 📖

Pelusium will twist in deep pain.

Thebes will be split open.

Memphis will face distress every day.

## Ezekiel 30:17

The young men of On and Pi-beseth will be killed with swords. 📖

Those cities will be taken into captivity.

## Ezekiel 30:18

Daylight will grow dark in Tahpanhes when I break Egypt’s yoke and end her proud strength. 📖

A cloud will cover her.

Her daughters will be taken into captivity.

**Help with “yoke”:** A yoke is a wooden frame used to control working animals.

Here it pictures Egypt’s power over others.

**About “her daughters”:** Cities and their dependent communities are pictured as a mother and daughters.

## Ezekiel 30:19

So I will carry out judgment on Egypt. 📖

They will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 30:20

The LORD spoke to me in the eleventh year, on the seventh day of the first month. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:21

God said, “Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh, king of Egypt. 📖

Look!

It has not been bandaged for healing.

It has not been supported to make it strong enough to hold a sword.

**Help with this picture:** The broken arm pictures Pharaoh’s weakened power.

## Ezekiel 30:22

So the Lord GOD says, “Look! 📖

I am against Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

I will break his arms, both the strong one and the one already broken.

I will make the sword fall from his hand.

## Ezekiel 30:23

I will spread the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:24

I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king and put My sword in his hand. 📖

But I will break Pharaoh’s arms.

Pharaoh will groan before Babylon’s king like a man with a wound that will kill him.

## Ezekiel 30:25

I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king. 📖

But Pharaoh’s arms will hang weakly.

I will put My sword in the hand of Babylon’s king.

He will use it against Egypt.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 30:26

I will spread the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands. 📖

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
