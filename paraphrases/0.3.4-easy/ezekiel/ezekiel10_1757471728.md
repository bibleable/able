# Ezekiel 10 — The glory moves from the temple

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel sees the cherubim and wheels again.

Fire is taken from among them, and the LORD’s glory moves from the temple’s threshold.

## Ezekiel 10:1

I saw something like a sapphire throne above the expanse over the cherubim’s heads. 📖

**Help with these words**

Sapphire is a precious stone.

An expanse is a broad space or surface.

Cherubim are heavenly beings.

## Ezekiel 10:2

The LORD told the linen-clothed man, “Go among the wheels under the cherubim. 📖

Fill your hands with burning coals from among them and scatter them over the city.”

I watched him enter.

## Ezekiel 10:3

The cherubim stood south of the temple when the man entered. 📖

A cloud filled the inner court.

## Ezekiel 10:4

The LORD’s glory rose above the cherubim and stood over the temple’s threshold. 📖

The cloud filled the temple, and the brightness of His glory filled the court.

## Ezekiel 10:5

The cherubim’s wings sounded as far as the outer court, like God Almighty’s voice when He speaks. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:6

The LORD commanded the linen-clothed man, “Take fire from among the wheels and cherubim.” 📖

He went in and stood beside a wheel.

## Ezekiel 10:7

One cherub reached with his hand and took fire from among them. 📖

He put it in the linen-clothed man’s hands, and the man received it and left.

## Ezekiel 10:8

The cherubim seemed to have human hands under their wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:9

I saw four wheels beside the cherubim, one beside each cherub. 📖

They shone like beryl stone.

**Help with beryl**

Beryl is a kind of precious stone.

## Ezekiel 10:10

All four looked alike, like a wheel within a wheel. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:11

They moved in any of four directions without turning. 📖

The cherubim went wherever the head faced, without turning as they moved.

## Ezekiel 10:12

Their whole bodies, backs, hands, and wings were full of eyes all around. 📖

Their four wheels were full of eyes too.

## Ezekiel 10:13

I heard the wheels called “the whirling wheels.” 📖

## Ezekiel 10:14

Each cherub had four faces. 📖

The first was a cherub’s, the second a man’s, the third a lion’s, and the fourth an eagle’s.

## Ezekiel 10:15

The cherubim rose upward. 📖

They were the living creatures I saw beside the River Kebar.

## Ezekiel 10:16

When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved beside them. 📖

Even when they spread wings to rise, the wheels never moved away from their side.

## Ezekiel 10:17

When the cherubim stood still, the wheels stood still. 📖

When they rose, the wheels rose with them, because the creatures’ spirit was in the wheels.

## Ezekiel 10:18

The LORD’s glory left the temple’s threshold and stood above the cherubim. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:19

I watched the cherubim lift wings and rise from the ground with the wheels beside them. 📖

They stopped at the LORD’s house’s east-gate entrance, with Israel’s God’s glory above them.

## Ezekiel 10:20

These were the creatures I saw beneath Israel’s God beside the River Kebar. 📖

I knew they were cherubim.

## Ezekiel 10:21

Each had four faces, four wings, and something like human hands under the wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:22

Their faces were like those I saw beside the River Kebar. 📖

Each creature went straight ahead.
