# Ezekiel 35 — Judgment against Mount Seir

**About this chapter**

God speaks against Mount Seir and Edom.

Their long-standing hatred and their joy at Israel’s ruin bring judgment on them.

## Ezekiel 35:1

The LORD also spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:2

God said, “Son of man, turn toward Mount Seir. 📖

Speak My message against it.

## Ezekiel 35:3

Announce what the Lord GOD says. 📖

Look, Mount Seir!

I am against you.

I will stretch out My hand against you and make you empty and ruined.

## Ezekiel 35:4

I will turn your cities into ruins. 📖

You will become an empty land.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 35:5

You held on to an old hatred. 📖

You handed the Israelites over to the sword during their disaster.

It was the final stage of their punishment.

## Ezekiel 35:6

So, as surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will hand you over to bloodshed. 📖

It will chase you.

You did not hate bloodshed, so it will chase you.

## Ezekiel 35:7

I will make Mount Seir empty and ruined. 📖

I will cut off those who come and go from it.

## Ezekiel 35:8

I will fill its mountains with dead bodies. 📖

Those killed with swords will fall on your hills, in your valleys, and in all your deep valleys.

## Ezekiel 35:9

I will make you empty forever. 📖

No one will live in your cities.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 35:10

You said, ‘These two nations and countries will be ours. 📖

We will own them.’

You said this even though the LORD was there.

## Ezekiel 35:11

So, as surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will deal with you according to your behavior. 📖

You showed anger and jealousy in your hatred against them.

When I judge you, I will make Myself known among them.

## Ezekiel 35:12

Then you will know that I, the LORD, heard every word of scorn you spoke against Israel’s mountains. 📖

You said, ‘They are empty.

They have been given to us to devour!’

**Help with “devour”:** Here this pictures seizing and using the land for themselves.

## Ezekiel 35:13

You boasted against Me with your mouth. 📖

You spoke more and more words against Me.

I heard it Myself!

## Ezekiel 35:14

The Lord GOD says, “While the whole earth celebrates, I will make you empty. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:15

You celebrated when the land belonging to Israel’s people became empty. 📖

So I will do the same to you.

Mount Seir, you will become empty, and so will all Edom.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
