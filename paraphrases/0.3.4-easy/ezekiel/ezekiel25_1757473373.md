# Ezekiel 25 — Judgment against neighboring nations

**About this chapter**

God announces judgment against Ammon, Moab, Edom, and the Philistines.

The reasons include celebrating Judah’s suffering and seeking revenge.

## Ezekiel 25:1

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:2

God said, “Son of man, turn toward the Ammonites. 📖

Speak My message against them.

## Ezekiel 25:3

Tell the Ammonites to listen to the Lord GOD’s word. 📖

The Lord GOD says, “You shouted with delight when My holy place was made unholy.

You celebrated when the land of Israel was ruined and Judah’s people were taken away as prisoners.

## Ezekiel 25:4

So I will certainly hand you over to the people of the East as their property. 📖

They will set up their camps and tents among you.

They will eat your fruit and drink your milk.

## Ezekiel 25:5

I will make Rabbah a place where camels feed. 📖

I will make Ammon a resting place for sheep.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 25:6

The Lord GOD says, “You clapped your hands and stamped your feet. 📖

You celebrated over the land of Israel with hearts full of scorn.

## Ezekiel 25:7

So I will certainly stretch out My hand against you. 📖

I will hand you over to the nations to be robbed.

I will cut you off from the peoples and wipe you out from the countries.

I will destroy you.

You will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 25:8

The Lord GOD says, “Moab and Seir said, ‘Look! 📖

Judah’s people are like all the other nations.’

## Ezekiel 25:9

So I will certainly lay open the side of Moab to attack. 📖

I will begin with its border cities, Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim.

These cities are the glory of the land.

## Ezekiel 25:10

I will give Moab, along with the Ammonites, to the people of the East as their property. 📖

Then the Ammonites will no longer be remembered among the nations.

## Ezekiel 25:11

So I will carry out judgments on Moab. 📖

They will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 25:12

The Lord GOD says, “Edom took revenge against Judah’s people. 📖

By doing this, Edom became terribly guilty.

## Ezekiel 25:13

So the Lord GOD says this. 📖

I will stretch out My hand against Edom.

I will cut off both people and animals from it.

I will make it an empty, ruined land.

From Teman to Dedan, they will be killed with swords.

## Ezekiel 25:14

I will use My people Israel to carry out My revenge against Edom. 📖

They will deal with Edom according to My fierce anger.

Then they will know My revenge, declares the Lord GOD.”

## Ezekiel 25:15

The Lord GOD says, “The Philistines acted in revenge. 📖

They took revenge with evil hatred in their hearts.

Their long-standing hatred led them to try to destroy Judah.

## Ezekiel 25:16

So the Lord GOD says this. 📖

Look!

I will stretch out My hand against the Philistines.

I will cut off the Cherethites.

I will destroy those left along the coast.

## Ezekiel 25:17

I will take great revenge against them with angry punishment. 📖

When I bring My revenge on them, they will know that I am the LORD.”
