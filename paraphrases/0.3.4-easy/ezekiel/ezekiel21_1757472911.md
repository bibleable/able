# Ezekiel 21 — The sword of judgment

**About this chapter**

God tells Ezekiel to announce judgment against Jerusalem and the Ammonites.

The sword is a picture of the coming killing and destruction.

## Ezekiel 21:1

The LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:2

God said, “Son of man, turn toward Jerusalem. 📖

Speak against its holy places.

Speak My message against the land of Israel.

**Help with “son of man”:** God uses this name when speaking to Ezekiel.

It reminds Ezekiel that he is human.

## Ezekiel 21:3

Tell Israel what the LORD says. 📖

I am against you.

I will pull My sword from its sheath.

I will cut off both good people and wicked people from you.

**Help with “sheath”:** A sheath is a cover that holds a sword.

**About this judgment:** The warning includes both righteous and wicked people.

The draft keeps this difficult part of the source.

## Ezekiel 21:4

I will cut off both good people and wicked people. 📖

So My sword will come out against everyone from south to north.

## Ezekiel 21:5

Then everyone will know that I, the LORD, have pulled My sword from its sheath. 📖

I will not put it back again.”

## Ezekiel 21:6

God said, “But you, son of man, groan! 📖

Groan where they can see you.

Let them see your broken heart and bitter grief.

**Help with “groan”:** A groan is a deep sound of pain or sadness.

## Ezekiel 21:7

They will ask you why you are groaning. 📖

Tell them about the news that is coming.

Every heart will melt.

Every hand will hang weakly.

Every spirit will lose strength.

Every knee will turn to water.

Yes, it is coming.

It will certainly happen, declares the Lord GOD.”

**Help with these pictures:** Melting hearts and watery knees describe people becoming terrified and weak.

## Ezekiel 21:8

The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:9

God said, “Son of man, speak My message. 📖

Tell them what the Lord says.

A sword!

A sword has been sharpened and polished!

## Ezekiel 21:10

It is sharpened for killing. 📖

It is polished to flash like lightning.

Should we celebrate the royal stick of My son?

The sword despises every such stick.

**Help with “royal stick”:** BSB calls this a “scepter.”

A ruler holds a scepter as a sign of authority.

**About this difficult picture:** The sword treats the royal stick as something worthless.

The question about “My son” is kept for further editorial review.

## Ezekiel 21:11

The sword is to be polished so someone can hold it. 📖

It is sharpened and polished to be put into the killer’s hand.

## Ezekiel 21:12

Cry out and weep loudly, son of man! 📖

The sword is against My people.

It is against all the princes of Israel.

They are thrown to the sword along with My people.

So strike your thigh!

**About striking a thigh:** This action shows deep grief.

## Ezekiel 21:13

Testing will certainly come! 📖

What if even the royal stick does not last?

The sword despises that stick, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 21:14

Son of man, speak My message. 📖

Clap your hands together.

Let the sword strike two times, even three.

It is a sword that kills.

It brings great killing and closes in on every side!

## Ezekiel 21:15

I have placed a killing sword at all their gates. 📖

Their hearts will melt, and many will stumble.

Yes, the sword is ready to flash like lightning.

It is drawn for killing.

## Ezekiel 21:16

Sword, slash to the right! 📖

Turn your blade to the left!

Strike wherever your blade is directed!

## Ezekiel 21:17

I too will clap My hands together. 📖

I will fully carry out My fierce anger.

I, the LORD, have spoken.”

## Ezekiel 21:18

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:19

God said, “Now, son of man, mark out two roads for the sword of Babylon’s king. 📖

Both roads must begin in the same land.

Put a signpost where the road divides toward each city.

## Ezekiel 21:20

Mark one road toward Rabbah of the Ammonites. 📖

Mark the other toward Judah, into Jerusalem with its strong defenses.

## Ezekiel 21:21

The king of Babylon stands where the two roads divide. 📖

He looks for a sign about what to do.

He shakes arrows.

He asks idols for answers.

He examines a liver.

**About these actions:** The king uses these practices to seek a message about the future.

The passage describes his actions.

## Ezekiel 21:22

The sign in his right hand points to Jerusalem. 📖

There he must set up heavy beams to break through defenses.

He must call for killing and raise a battle shout.

He must aim those beams against the gates.

He must build a ramp and a wall to surround the city.

**Help with the beams:** BSB calls them “battering rams.”

Soldiers use these tools to break gates and walls.

## Ezekiel 21:23

Those who swore loyalty to him will think the sign is false. 📖

But it will bring their guilt to attention.

They will be taken prisoner.

## Ezekiel 21:24

The Lord GOD says, “You have brought your guilt to attention. 📖

You have exposed your wrong actions.

All your deeds show your sins.

Because you have been brought to mind, you will be seized.

## Ezekiel 21:25

You are an unholy and wicked prince of Israel. 📖

The day for your final punishment has come.”

## Ezekiel 21:26

The Lord GOD says, “Remove the turban. 📖

Take off the crown.

Things will change.

Raise up those who are low.

Bring down those who are high.

**Help with “turban”:** A turban is a cloth wrapped around the head.

## Ezekiel 21:27

A ruin! 📖

A ruin!

I will make it a ruin!

It will not be restored until the One it belongs to arrives.

I have given Him the right to judge.”

**About the One who arrives:** This verse does not give His name.

## Ezekiel 21:28

God said, “Now, son of man, speak My message. 📖

Announce what the Lord GOD says about the Ammonites and their scorn.

A sword!

A sword is drawn for killing.

It is polished to destroy and flash like lightning.

## Ezekiel 21:29

People offer false visions for you and lying messages about your future. 📖

Meanwhile, the sword is to be placed on the necks of wicked people who are killed.

Their day has come.

It is the time of their final punishment.

## Ezekiel 21:30

Put the sword back into its sheath! 📖

I will judge you in the place where you were created.

I will judge you in the land where you began.

## Ezekiel 21:31

I will pour My anger over you. 📖

I will breathe the fire of My fierce anger against you.

I will hand you over to cruel men who are skilled at destroying.

## Ezekiel 21:32

You will be fuel for the fire. 📖

Your blood will stain your own land.

You will not be remembered.

I, the LORD, have spoken.”
