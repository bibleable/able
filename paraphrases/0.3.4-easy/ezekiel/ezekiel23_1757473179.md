# Ezekiel 23 — Two sisters picture two cities

**About this chapter**

God uses a disturbing story about two sisters to describe Samaria and Jerusalem.

The story includes sexual acts, child sacrifice, and violent punishment.

The explanations identify the picture without removing these parts of the source.

## Ezekiel 23:1

The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:2

God said, “Son of man, there were two women. 📖

They were daughters of the same mother.

## Ezekiel 23:3

They acted as prostitutes in Egypt, beginning when they were young. 📖

There, people touched their breasts sexually while they were still virgins.

**Help with “prostitutes”:** Prostitutes provide sexual acts in exchange for payment or other benefits.

**About this story:** The women picture two cities.

Sexual wrongdoing pictures the cities’ unfaithfulness to God.

The wording reports the passage’s accusations.

## Ezekiel 23:4

The older sister was named Oholah. 📖

The other sister was named Oholibah.

They became Mine and had sons and daughters.

Oholah is Samaria.

Oholibah is Jerusalem.

## Ezekiel 23:5

Oholah acted as a prostitute while she was still Mine. 📖

She strongly desired her lovers, the Assyrian soldiers.

## Ezekiel 23:6

They wore blue clothes. 📖

They were governors and commanders.

All were desirable young men, riding horses.

## Ezekiel 23:7

She offered sexual acts to all the leading men of Assyria. 📖

She made herself unclean with all the idols of the men she desired.

## Ezekiel 23:8

She did not stop the prostitution she began in Egypt. 📖

There, men had sex with her when she was young.

They touched her virgin breasts sexually and poured out their sexual desire on her.

## Ezekiel 23:9

So I handed her over to her lovers, the Assyrians she desired. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:10

They exposed her naked body. 📖

They seized her sons and daughters and killed her with a sword.

Women used her name as a warning.

Her attackers carried out judgment against her.

## Ezekiel 23:11

Her sister Oholibah saw this. 📖

Yet her sexual desire and prostitution became even more evil than her sister’s.

## Ezekiel 23:12

She too strongly desired the Assyrians. 📖

These were governors and commanders, soldiers in splendid clothes, and horsemen riding horses.

All were desirable young men.

## Ezekiel 23:13

I saw that she too had made herself unclean. 📖

Both sisters had followed the same path.

## Ezekiel 23:14

But Oholibah went even further in her prostitution. 📖

She saw pictures of men on a wall.

These were images of Chaldeans, carved and colored bright red.

**Help with the color:** BSB calls this color “vermilion.”

## Ezekiel 23:15

They wore belts around their waists and flowing cloth turbans on their heads. 📖

They all looked like Babylonian officers from Chaldea, the land where they were born.

## Ezekiel 23:16

When she saw them, she strongly desired them. 📖

She sent messengers to them in Chaldea.

## Ezekiel 23:17

Then the Babylonians came to her bed to have sex. 📖

Their sexual desire made her unclean.

But after this, she turned away from them in disgust.

## Ezekiel 23:18

Oholibah openly acted as a prostitute and exposed her naked body. 📖

Then I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had turned away from her sister.

## Ezekiel 23:19

Yet she increased her sexual wrongdoing. 📖

She remembered her younger days, when she acted as a prostitute in Egypt.

## Ezekiel 23:20

She strongly desired their lovers. 📖

Their sexual organs were like those of donkeys.

Their release of semen was like that of male horses.

**About this comparison:** The source uses this explicit picture in its accusation against Jerusalem.

Semen is the fluid released from male sexual organs.

## Ezekiel 23:21

You returned to the shameful sexual acts of your youth. 📖

Then the Egyptians had touched and pressed your young breasts sexually.

## Ezekiel 23:22

So, Oholibah, the Lord GOD says this. 📖

I will stir up your lovers against you.

You turned away from them in disgust.

I will bring them against you from every side.

## Ezekiel 23:23

They include the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans. 📖

They include the men of Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, with all the Assyrians.

All are desirable young men, governors and commanders, officers and famous men, riding horses.

## Ezekiel 23:24

They will come against you with many peoples, weapons, chariots, and wagons. 📖

They will take positions against you on every side with small shields, large shields, and helmets.

I will give them the authority to judge you.

They will punish you by their own rules.

**Help with “chariots”:** These were vehicles pulled by horses and used in war.

## Ezekiel 23:25

I will turn My jealous, fierce anger against you. 📖

They will deal with you in fury.

They will cut off your noses and ears.

Those who survive will be killed with swords.

They will seize your sons and daughters.

Those left will be destroyed by fire.

## Ezekiel 23:26

They will strip off your clothes and take your fine jewelry. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:27

So I will end your shameful sexual acts and prostitution, which began in Egypt. 📖

You will not look toward them or remember Egypt anymore.

## Ezekiel 23:28

The Lord GOD says, “I will certainly hand you over to those you hate. 📖

You turned away from them in disgust.

## Ezekiel 23:29

They will treat you with hatred. 📖

They will take everything you have worked for.

They will leave you completely naked.

The shame of your prostitution will be exposed.

Your shameful sexual acts and repeated sexual wrongdoing

## Ezekiel 23:30

have brought these things on you. 📖

You acted as a prostitute with the nations.

You made yourself unclean with their idols.

## Ezekiel 23:31

You have followed your sister’s path. 📖

So I will put her cup in your hand.”

**Help with “cup”:** Here the cup pictures the punishment that the city must experience.

## Ezekiel 23:32

The Lord GOD says, “You will drink your sister’s cup. 📖

It is deep and wide.

It will bring scorn and mockery, because it holds so much.

## Ezekiel 23:33

You will be filled with drunkenness and grief. 📖

This cup holds ruin and complete destruction.

It is the cup of your sister Samaria.

## Ezekiel 23:34

You will drink it and empty it. 📖

You will smash it to pieces and tear your breasts.

I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 23:35

The Lord GOD says, “You have forgotten Me and thrown Me behind your back. 📖

So you must bear the results of your shameful sexual acts and prostitution.”

**Help with “behind your back”:** This pictures deliberately ignoring God.

## Ezekiel 23:36

Then the LORD said to me, “Son of man, will you pass judgment against Oholah and Oholibah? 📖

Tell them about their horrible sins.

## Ezekiel 23:37

They have been unfaithful in marriage, and blood is on their hands. 📖

They have been unfaithful with their idols.

They even sacrificed the children they bore to Me.

They burned those children as food for their idols.

**Help with these pictures:** Being unfaithful in marriage pictures their unfaithfulness to God.

Blood on their hands means they are responsible for killing people.

## Ezekiel 23:38

They also did this to Me. 📖

On that very same day, they made My holy place unclean and treated My Sabbaths as unholy.

## Ezekiel 23:39

On the very day they killed their children for their idols, they entered My holy place and made it unholy. 📖

Yes, they did this inside My house.

## Ezekiel 23:40

Also, you sisters sent messengers for men from far away. 📖

When the men arrived, you bathed for them.

You painted your eyes and put on jewelry.

## Ezekiel 23:41

You sat on a luxurious couch with a table set before it. 📖

You had put My incense and My oil on the table.

**Help with “incense”:** This is something burned to give a sweet smell.

## Ezekiel 23:42

The sound of a carefree crowd was there. 📖

Drunk men were brought from the desert, along with men from a disorderly crowd.

They put bracelets on your wrists and beautiful crowns on your head.

## Ezekiel 23:43

Then I spoke about the woman who had grown old in being unfaithful. 📖

I said, “Now let them use her as a prostitute.

That is all she is!”

**About these words:** This is the harsh accusation in God’s story about the cities.

## Ezekiel 23:44

They had sex with her as they would with a prostitute. 📖

They had sex with Oholah and Oholibah, those women who did shameful sexual acts.

## Ezekiel 23:45

But men who do right will sentence them to the punishment for unfaithfulness in marriage and bloodshed. 📖

These women are unfaithful in marriage, with blood on their hands.

## Ezekiel 23:46

The Lord GOD says, “Bring a violent crowd against them. 📖

Hand them over to terror and robbery.

## Ezekiel 23:47

The crowd will stone them and cut them down with swords. 📖

They will kill their sons and daughters.

They will burn down their houses.

**Help with “stone”:** Here it means killing someone by throwing stones at them.

## Ezekiel 23:48

So I will end shameful sexual acts in the land. 📖

All the women will be warned against copying your behavior.

## Ezekiel 23:49

They will repay you for your shameful sexual acts. 📖

You will bear the results of your sins of worshiping idols.

Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD.”
