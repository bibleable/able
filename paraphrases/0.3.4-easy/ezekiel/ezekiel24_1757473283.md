# Ezekiel 24 — The cooking pot and Ezekiel’s loss

**About this chapter**

God gives Ezekiel a story about a cooking pot to describe judgment against Jerusalem.

Ezekiel’s wife dies, and God gives him unusual commands about mourning.

These events become a sign for the people.

## Ezekiel 24:1

The LORD spoke to me in the ninth year, on the tenth day of the tenth month. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:2

God said, “Son of man, write down today’s date. 📖

On this very day, the king of Babylon has surrounded Jerusalem to attack it.

## Ezekiel 24:3

Tell this rebellious people a story with a meaning. 📖

Tell them what the Lord GOD says.

Put the pot on the fire.

Put it on and pour in water.

**Help with “rebellious”:** This means refusing to obey.

## Ezekiel 24:4

Put in the pieces of meat. 📖

Put in every good piece, thigh and shoulder.

Fill the pot with the best bones.

## Ezekiel 24:5

Take the best animal from the flock. 📖

Pile the fuel underneath the pot.

Bring it to a boil.

Cook the bones in it.

## Ezekiel 24:6

The Lord GOD says, “How terrible for the city of bloodshed! 📖

It is like a pot covered with rust that will not come off.

Empty it piece by piece.

Do not cast lots to choose its contents.

**Help with “cast lots”:** People used marked objects to make a choice.

**Help with the pot picture:** Jerusalem is pictured as a cooking pot.

Its lasting rust pictures the evil that has not been removed.

## Ezekiel 24:7

The blood she spilled is still inside her. 📖

She poured it on bare rock.

She did not pour it on the ground and cover it with dust.

## Ezekiel 24:8

I have put her blood on bare rock so it cannot be covered. 📖

I did this to stir up anger and bring punishment in return.

## Ezekiel 24:9

Yes, the Lord GOD says, “How terrible for the city of bloodshed! 📖

I too will pile the firewood high.

## Ezekiel 24:10

Pile on the logs and light the fire. 📖

Cook the meat well and mix in the spices.

Let the bones burn.

## Ezekiel 24:11

Put the empty pot on its coals until it gets hot and its copper glows. 📖

Then the uncleanness inside will melt.

Its rust will be burned away.

## Ezekiel 24:12

The pot has defeated every effort to clean it. 📖

Even the fire has not removed its thick rust.

## Ezekiel 24:13

Your uncleanness includes shameful sexual acts. 📖

I tried to cleanse you, but you would not be cleansed from your filth.

You will not be pure again until My fierce anger against you has settled.

**Help with “uncleanness”:** Here the picture describes the city’s evil.

## Ezekiel 24:14

I, the LORD, have spoken. 📖

The time is coming, and I will act.

I will not hold back or show pity.

I will not change My decision.

I will judge you by your ways and deeds, declares the Lord GOD.”

## Ezekiel 24:15

Then the LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:16

God said, “Son of man, look! 📖

I am about to take away the delight of your eyes with a blow that kills.

But you must not mourn, weep, or let tears flow.

**Help with “the delight of your eyes”:** This means someone Ezekiel deeply loves.

Verse 18 tells us that his wife dies.

## Ezekiel 24:17

Groan quietly. 📖

Do not mourn for the dead.

Put on your turban and fasten your sandals on your feet.

Do not cover your lips or eat the bread of mourners.”

**About mourning:** Covering the lips and eating this bread were customs connected with mourning.

A turban is a cloth wrapped around the head.

## Ezekiel 24:18

So I spoke to the people in the morning. 📖

In the evening, my wife died.

The next morning, I did as I had been commanded.

## Ezekiel 24:19

The people asked me, “Will you tell us what your actions mean for us?” 📖

## Ezekiel 24:20

I answered them, “The LORD spoke to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:21

He told me to tell Israel what the Lord GOD says. 📖

I am about to make My holy place unholy.

It is the pride of your power, the delight of your eyes, and the joy of your soul.

The sons and daughters you left behind will be killed with swords.

## Ezekiel 24:22

Then you will do what I have done. 📖

You will not cover your lips or eat the bread of mourners.

## Ezekiel 24:23

Your turbans will stay on your heads and your sandals on your feet. 📖

You will not mourn or weep.

But you will waste away because of your sins.

You will groan among yourselves.

## Ezekiel 24:24

God says, “Ezekiel will be a sign for you. 📖

You will do everything he has done.

When this happens, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.”

## Ezekiel 24:25

God said, “Son of man, I will take away their place of safety and their sons and daughters. 📖

That place is their pride and joy.

It delights their eyes and lifts their spirits.

## Ezekiel 24:26

On that day, someone who has escaped will come to tell you the news. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:27

On that day, you will be able to speak to the person who has escaped. 📖

You will speak and no longer be silent.

You will be a sign to them.

They will know that I am the LORD.”
