# 3 John 1 — Welcome those serving the truth

**About this chapter**

The elder writes to Gaius about welcoming fellow believers and supporting their work.

He also writes about Diotrephes and Demetrius.

## 3 John 1:1

This letter is from the elder to dear Gaius, whom I love in the truth. 📖

## 3 John 1:2

Dear friend, I pray you do well in every way and enjoy good health, as your soul does well. 📖

## 3 John 1:3

I was overjoyed when fellow believers came and told me about your faithfulness to the truth. 📖

You keep living in it.

## 3 John 1:4

Nothing brings me greater joy than hearing that my children live in the truth. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The elder calls believers his children to express their close bond in faith.

## 3 John 1:5

Dear friend, you act faithfully in helping fellow believers, especially when they are strangers to you. 📖

## 3 John 1:6

They told the church about your love. 📖

You will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.

## 3 John 1:7

They went out for the sake of the Name, accepting nothing from the Gentiles. 📖

**Help with words:** The Name refers to Jesus.

Gentiles here refers to people outside the faith.

## 3 John 1:8

So we ought to support people like them and become fellow workers for the truth. 📖

## 3 John 1:9

I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes loves to be first and will not accept our instruction. 📖

## 3 John 1:10

If I come, I will point out the harmful lies he tells against us. 📖

He also refuses to welcome fellow believers.

He stops others who want to welcome them and even puts those people out of the church.

## 3 John 1:11

Dear friend, copy what is good, rather than evil. 📖

Anyone doing good belongs to God.

Anyone doing evil has not seen God.

## 3 John 1:12

Everyone speaks well of Demetrius, and the truth itself testifies for him. 📖

We testify for him too, and you know our testimony is true.

## 3 John 1:13

I have much to write, but prefer not to do it with pen and ink. 📖

## 3 John 1:14

I hope to see you soon and speak face to face. 📖

Peace to you.

Our friends here send greetings.

Greet each of our friends there by name.
