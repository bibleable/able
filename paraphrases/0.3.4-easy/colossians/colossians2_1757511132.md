# Colossians 2 — Keep living in Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul wants the believers to understand the riches they have in Christ.

He warns against false teaching and rules that replace trust in Christ.

## Colossians 2:1

I want you to know how hard I am struggling for you. 📖

I also struggle for the people in Laodicea and everyone who has not met me in person.

## Colossians 2:2

I want their hearts to be encouraged and joined together in love. 📖

May they have the full riches of complete understanding.

Then they can know God's mystery, which is Christ.

**Help with a word:** Mystery here means something once hidden that God now reveals.

## Colossians 2:3

All the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden in Christ. 📖

## Colossians 2:4

I say this so no one tricks you with words that sound convincing. 📖

## Colossians 2:5

I am away from you in body, but with you in spirit. 📖

I am glad to see your good order and firm trust in Christ.

## Colossians 2:6

You received Christ Jesus as Lord. 📖

So keep living in him.

## Colossians 2:7

Be rooted and built up in him. 📖

Be firmly established in the faith you were taught.

Overflow with thanks.

**Help with these pictures:** Roots and strong buildings picture a life held firmly in Christ.

Overflowing pictures being full of thanks.

## Colossians 2:8

Do not let anyone take you captive with philosophy and empty lies. 📖

These depend on human traditions and the world's spiritual forces, rather than Christ.

**Help with words:** Philosophy means ideas about life and the world.

Paul warns about teaching based on something other than Christ.

Being taken captive pictures being trapped by that teaching.

## Colossians 2:9

All the fullness of God lives in Christ in bodily form. 📖

**Help with a word:** Fullness means all that God is.

## Colossians 2:10

You have been made complete in Christ. 📖

He is the head over every ruler and authority.

## Colossians 2:11

In Christ, you were also circumcised. 📖

Your sinful nature was taken off.

Christ did this circumcision, not human hands.

**Help with this picture:** Physical circumcision removes the foreskin, the skin covering the end of the penis.

Here Paul uses circumcision as a picture of Christ removing the sinful nature.

## Colossians 2:12

You were buried with Christ in baptism. 📖

You were also raised with him through faith in God's power.

God raised Christ from the dead.

**Help with words:** Baptism uses water as a sign of belonging to Christ.

Paul connects baptism with sharing in Christ's death and rising to life.

## Colossians 2:13

You were dead in your wrongdoing and in the uncircumcision of your sinful nature. 📖

God made you alive with Christ.

He forgave all our wrongdoing.

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes separation from God's life as being dead.

His picture of circumcision concerns the sinful nature, as explained in verse 11.

## Colossians 2:14

God cancelled the written debt and its rules that stood against us. 📖

He took it away by nailing it to the cross.

**Help with this picture:** Paul pictures the charge against us as a written debt removed through Christ's death.

## Colossians 2:15

God took away the weapons of the powers and authorities. 📖

He showed their defeat openly.

He won the victory over them through the cross.

## Colossians 2:16

So do not let anyone judge you about what you eat or drink. 📖

Do not let them judge you about a festival, a New Moon, or a Sabbath.

**Help with names:** A New Moon was a religious occasion marking the start of a month.

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## Colossians 2:17

These things are a shadow of what was to come. 📖

The body that casts the shadow belongs to Christ.

**Help with this picture:** A shadow points to something real.

Paul says that the reality belongs to Christ.

## Colossians 2:18

Do not let anyone disqualify you with speculation about what they have seen. 📖

Such a person delights in false humility and worshipping angels.

Their unspiritual mind makes them proud without any good reason.

**Help with words:** Disqualify means to rule someone out of receiving a prize.

False humility means pretending to be humble.

Speculation means guesses or ideas that are not certain.

## Colossians 2:19

Such a person has lost connection with the head. 📖

From the head, the whole body is supported and joined by its joints and ligaments.

It grows as God causes it to grow.

**Help with this picture:** The head is Christ, and the body pictures the church.

Joints and ligaments connect parts of a body.

## Colossians 2:20

If you died with Christ to the world's spiritual forces, why follow its rules? 📖

Why act as if you still belong to the world?

## Colossians 2:21

These rules say, “Do not handle! 📖

Do not taste!

Do not touch!”

## Colossians 2:22

These things are used up and disappear. 📖

Such rules are based on human commands and teachings.

## Colossians 2:23

These rules may look wise. 📖

They involve worship people invent, false humility, and harsh treatment of the body.

But they do not stop people from giving in to sinful desires.
