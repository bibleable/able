# Colossians 1 — Christ is above all things

**About this chapter**

Paul and Timothy greet the believers in Colossae.

Paul thanks God and prays for them.

He explains who Christ is and why he serves Christ's church.

## Colossians 1:1

This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus because God wanted this. 📖

It is also from Timothy, our brother.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

Paul calls Timothy a brother because they share faith in Christ.

## Colossians 1:2

We write to God's people in Colossae, our faithful brothers and sisters who belong to Christ. 📖

May God our Father give you kindness and peace.

## Colossians 1:3

Whenever we pray for you, we thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## Colossians 1:4

We have heard about your faith in Christ Jesus and your love for all God's people. 📖

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in Christ here.

## Colossians 1:5

Your faith and love come from the hope kept for you in heaven. 📖

You already heard about this hope in the true message, the good news.

## Colossians 1:6

This good news has reached you. 📖

It is growing and bearing fruit all over the world.

It has done the same among you since you heard and truly understood God's kindness.

**Help with this picture:** Bearing fruit means producing good results.

## Colossians 1:7

You learned this from Epaphras, our dear fellow servant. 📖

He faithfully serves Christ on our behalf.

## Colossians 1:8

Epaphras also told us about your love in the Spirit. 📖

## Colossians 1:9

Since we heard about you, we have not stopped praying for you. 📖

We ask God to fill you with knowledge of what he wants.

May you have all the wisdom and understanding that come from the Spirit.

## Colossians 1:10

Then you can live in a way worthy of the Lord and please him in every way. 📖

May every good work bear fruit.

May you grow in knowing God.

## Colossians 1:11

May God's glorious power give you all the strength you need. 📖

Then you can keep going with full patience and endurance, and have joy.

**Help with words:** Glorious describes God's wonderful greatness.

Endurance means continuing through difficulty without giving up.

## Colossians 1:12

Joyfully thank the Father. 📖

He has made you able to share the inheritance of his people in the light.

**Help with these pictures:** An inheritance is something received as a family member.

Here it pictures what God gives his people.

Light pictures the life of belonging to God.

## Colossians 1:13

God rescued us from the rule of darkness. 📖

He brought us into the kingdom of his Son, whom he loves.

**Help with words:** Darkness pictures the evil rule from which God rescues his people.

A kingdom is where a king rules.

## Colossians 1:14

In his Son, we are set free. 📖

Our sins are forgiven.

**Help with a word:** Sins are wrong things done against God.

## Colossians 1:15

The Son is the image of God, whom no one can see. 📖

He is the firstborn over all creation.

**Help with a title:** Firstborn here is a title of highest rank and inheritance.

The next verses describe all things being made through the Son.

## Colossians 1:16

All things were created in him, in heaven and on earth. 📖

This includes things we can see and things we cannot see.

It includes thrones, powers, rulers, and authorities.

Everything was created through him and for him.

**Help with words:** These names describe different kinds of rule and power.

## Colossians 1:17

He exists before all things. 📖

All things hold together in him.

## Colossians 1:18

He is the head of the body, the church. 📖

He is the beginning and the firstborn from among the dead.

So he has first place in everything.

**Help with these pictures:** The church is pictured as a body, with Christ as its head.

Firstborn from the dead describes Christ's leading place in rising from death.

## Colossians 1:19

God was pleased to have all his fullness live in the Son. 📖

**Help with a word:** Fullness here means all that God is.

## Colossians 1:20

God was pleased to bring all things back to himself through the Son. 📖

This includes things on earth and in heaven.

He made peace through the blood of the Son's cross.

**Help with this picture:** The blood of the cross refers to Christ's death on the cross.

## Colossians 1:21

Once you were separated from God. 📖

You were enemies in your thoughts and did evil things.

## Colossians 1:22

Now God has brought you back to himself through the death of Christ's physical body. 📖

He did this to present you holy, without fault or blame, before him.

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Colossians 1:23

This is true if you continue in your faith, firmly rooted and steady. 📖

Do not move away from the hope of the good news you heard.

This message has been told to every creature under heaven.

I, Paul, have become its servant.

**Help with this picture:** Being rooted pictures holding firmly to your faith, like a tree with strong roots.

## Colossians 1:24

Now I rejoice in my sufferings for you. 📖

For Christ's body, the church, I fill up in my own body what is lacking in Christ's sufferings.

**Help with this passage:** Paul is describing his suffering while serving Christ's church.

The body is a picture for the church.

## Colossians 1:25

God gave me the task of serving the church. 📖

My task is to tell you God's whole message.

## Colossians 1:26

This message was a mystery hidden for ages and generations. 📖

Now God has made it known to his people.

**Help with a word:** Mystery here means something once hidden that God now reveals.

## Colossians 1:27

God chose to show his people the glorious riches of this mystery among people who are not Jews. 📖

The mystery is Christ in you, the hope of glory.

**Help with a word:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## Colossians 1:28

We tell people about Christ. 📖

We warn and teach everyone with all wisdom.

We want to present everyone perfect in Christ.

## Colossians 1:29

That is why I work hard and struggle. 📖

I use all Christ's energy, which works powerfully in me.
