# Colossians 3 — Live a new life in Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches believers how to live together.

Christ is a title for Jesus, the King God promised to send.

## Colossians 3:1

You have been raised with Christ. 📖

Seek the things above, where Christ sits at God's right hand.

**Help with this picture:** The right-hand seat is a place of honour and power.

## Colossians 3:2

Set your thoughts on things above, rather than earthly things. 📖

## Colossians 3:3

You died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God. 📖

**Help with this picture:** This describes leaving the old life and belonging to Christ.

## Colossians 3:4

Christ is your life. 📖

When he appears, you will appear with him in glory.

**Help with a word:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## Colossians 3:5

Put to death your old earthly ways. 📖

Reject wrong sexual behaviour, impurity, lust, evil desires, and greed.

Greed is worshipping an idol.

**Help with this picture:** Put to death means stop following these ways.

Lust is a strong wrong sexual desire.

## Colossians 3:6

Because of these things, God's anger is coming on people who disobey. 📖

## Colossians 3:7

You used to live that way too. 📖

## Colossians 3:8

Now remove anger, rage, the wish to harm, harmful lies, and filthy speech from your life. 📖

## Colossians 3:9

Do not lie to each other. 📖

You have taken off your old self and its actions.

## Colossians 3:10

You have put on the new self. 📖

It is being made new in knowledge, to be like its Creator.

**Help with this picture:** Changing clothes pictures changing your way of life.

## Colossians 3:11

Here, being Greek or Jewish does not separate people. 📖

Being circumcised or uncircumcised does not separate them.

Neither does being a barbarian, a Scythian, a slave, or free.

Christ is everything and is in everyone.

**Help with words:** Circumcision is a physical sign of Jewish identity.

Barbarian was a label for people whose language and culture seemed foreign.

Scythians were a people others often looked down on.

Slaves were people treated as property.

## Colossians 3:12

God has chosen you, loves you, and sets you apart for himself. 📖

Put on deep care, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience, like clothes.

**Help with a word:** Humility means not thinking you are above others.

## Colossians 3:13

Be patient with each other. 📖

Forgive any wrong someone has done to you.

Forgive as the Lord forgave you.

## Colossians 3:14

Above all, put on love. 📖

Love joins everything together in complete unity.

## Colossians 3:15

Let Christ's peace guide your hearts. 📖

God called you to peace as members of one body.

Be thankful.

**Help with this picture:** One body pictures believers belonging together.

## Colossians 3:16

Let Christ's message fill your lives. 📖

Teach and correct each other wisely.

Sing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, thanking God in your hearts.

**Help with words:** These are different kinds of songs of worship.

## Colossians 3:17

Whatever you say or do, do everything in the Lord Jesus' name. 📖

Thank God the Father through him.

## Colossians 3:18

Wives, submit to your husbands, as is fitting for people who belong to the Lord. 📖

**Help with a word:** Submitting means accepting someone's authority.

## Colossians 3:19

Husbands, love your wives. 📖

Do not treat them harshly.

## Colossians 3:20

Children, obey your parents in everything. 📖

This pleases the Lord.

## Colossians 3:21

Fathers, do not keep upsetting your children. 📖

They may lose heart.

## Colossians 3:22

Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything. 📖

Do not work only when watched to gain approval.

Work sincerely, with respect for the Lord.

**Help with a word:** Slaves were people owned by others.

Paul is addressing a household system of his time.

## Colossians 3:23

Whatever you do, work with your whole heart. 📖

Work as for the Lord, rather than only for people.

## Colossians 3:24

You know the Lord will give you an inheritance as your reward. 📖

You are serving Christ the Lord.

**Help with a word:** An inheritance is something given to a member of a family.

## Colossians 3:25

Anyone who does wrong will be repaid for that wrong. 📖

God does not favour one person over another.
