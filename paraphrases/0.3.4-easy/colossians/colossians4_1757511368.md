# Colossians 4 — Pray and speak wisely

**About this chapter**

Paul finishes his letter with instructions and greetings.

## Colossians 4:1

Masters, give your slaves what is right and fair. 📖

Remember that you also have a Master in heaven.

**Help with a word:** Slaves were people treated as property and forced to serve others.

## Colossians 4:2

Keep praying. 📖

Stay alert and thankful.

## Colossians 4:3

Pray for us too. 📖

Ask God to open a door for us to share his message.

We want to explain the truth about Christ that God has now made known.

I am in chains because of this message.

**Help with this picture:** An open door pictures a chance to share God's message.

## Colossians 4:4

Pray that I will explain the message clearly, as I should. 📖

## Colossians 4:5

Act wisely towards people outside the faith. 📖

Make the best use of every opportunity.

## Colossians 4:6

Always speak kindly. 📖

Let your words be like food seasoned with salt.

Then you will know how to answer each person.

**Help with this picture:** Salt pictures words that are thoughtful and helpful.

## Colossians 4:7

Tychicus will tell you all my news. 📖

He is a dear fellow believer, a faithful worker, and a fellow servant in the Lord.

## Colossians 4:8

I am sending him to tell you how we are. 📖

He will encourage you.

## Colossians 4:9

Onesimus is coming with him. 📖

He is a faithful, dear fellow believer from your own community.

They will tell you everything happening here.

## Colossians 4:10

Aristarchus, in prison with me, sends greetings. 📖

So does Mark, Barnabas' cousin.

You have instructions to welcome Mark if he visits.

## Colossians 4:11

Jesus, also called Justus, sends greetings. 📖

These are the only Jewish workers with me for God's kingdom.

They have comforted me.

## Colossians 4:12

Epaphras sends greetings. 📖

He comes from your community and serves Christ Jesus.

He prays hard for you to be mature and fully sure of all God wants.

## Colossians 4:13

I can tell you he works very hard for you. 📖

He also works for the people in Laodicea and Hierapolis.

## Colossians 4:14

Our dear doctor Luke and Demas send greetings. 📖

## Colossians 4:15

Greet the believers in Laodicea. 📖

Greet Nympha and the church meeting in her house.

## Colossians 4:16

After reading this letter, have it read to the church in Laodicea. 📖

Read the letter from Laodicea yourselves too.

## Colossians 4:17

Tell Archippus to complete the work he received in the Lord. 📖

## Colossians 4:18

I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. 📖

Remember that I am in chains.

May God's freely given kindness be with you.
