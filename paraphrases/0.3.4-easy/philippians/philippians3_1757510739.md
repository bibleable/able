# Philippians 3 — Knowing Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul warns about teachers who put their trust in human credentials and religious practices.

He describes his own background and the greater value of knowing Christ.

He urges the believers to keep moving towards God's call.

## Philippians 3:1

Finally, my brothers and sisters, rejoice in the Lord. 📖

Writing the same things again is no trouble for me.

It helps keep you safe.

## Philippians 3:2

Watch out for those dogs, those people doing evil, those who cut the flesh! 📖

**Help with these words:** Paul uses harsh words against particular teachers whom he opposes.

He is using dogs as an insult.

Cutting the flesh refers here to circumcision.

He is not saying that every Jewish person or circumcised person does evil.

## Philippians 3:3

We are the true circumcision. 📖

We worship by God's Spirit and take pride in Christ Jesus.

We do not put our trust in the flesh.

**Help with words:** Circumcision is cutting off the foreskin, the skin covering the end of the penis.

It was a sign of belonging to God's covenant people.

Paul contrasts relying on human background and outward practices with relying on Christ.

## Philippians 3:4

I myself could have reasons to trust in the flesh. 📖

If someone else thinks they have reasons for that trust, I have more.

## Philippians 3:5

I was circumcised on the eighth day. 📖

I belong to the people of Israel and the tribe of Benjamin.

I am a Hebrew born to Hebrew parents.

As for God's law, I was a Pharisee.

**Help with words:** Benjamin was one of Israel's tribes, or large family groups.

Hebrew describes Paul's Jewish family background.

Pharisees were a Jewish religious group that carefully followed the law.

## Philippians 3:6

I was so eager for my religion that I treated the church cruelly. 📖

As for doing right by the law, no fault could be found in me.

## Philippians 3:7

But the things I once counted as gains, I now count as losses because of Christ. 📖

## Philippians 3:8

More than that, knowing Christ Jesus my Lord is worth far more than everything else. 📖

Compared with that, I count all things as losses.

For him, I have lost everything.

I count it as rubbish so that I may gain Christ.

## Philippians 3:9

I want to be found in Christ. 📖

I do not have a right standing of my own gained by keeping the law.

I have the right standing that comes through faith in Christ.

It comes from God and is based on faith.

**Help with words:** Right standing means being accepted as right before God.

Faith means trust in Christ here.

## Philippians 3:10

I want to know Christ and the power of his rising from the dead. 📖

I want to share his sufferings and become like him in his death.

## Philippians 3:11

Then, somehow, I hope to reach the rising from the dead. 📖

## Philippians 3:12

I have not already gained all this or already become perfect. 📖

Christ Jesus took hold of me for a purpose.

I keep pressing on to take hold of that.

## Philippians 3:13

Brothers and sisters, I do not think I have taken hold of it yet. 📖

But I do one thing.

I forget what is behind and reach forward towards what lies ahead.

## Philippians 3:14

I keep moving towards the goal and the prize. 📖

The prize is God's call to heaven in Christ Jesus.

**Help with this picture:** Paul compares his life of faith to running towards a prize in a race.

## Philippians 3:15

All of us who are mature should think this way. 📖

If you think differently about something, God will make that clear to you too.

**Help with a word:** Mature here means grown strong in faith.

## Philippians 3:16

Still, we must live up to what we have already reached. 📖

## Philippians 3:17

Brothers and sisters, join together in following my example. 📖

Watch carefully those who live by the pattern we gave you.

## Philippians 3:18

I have often told you this before, and I tell you again with tears. 📖

Many people live as enemies of Christ's cross.

## Philippians 3:19

Their end is destruction. 📖

Their stomach is their god.

They are proud of things they should be ashamed of.

Their minds are set on earthly things.

**Help with this picture:** Making their stomach their god pictures letting their desires rule them.

## Philippians 3:20

But we are citizens of heaven. 📖

We eagerly wait for a Saviour from there, the Lord Jesus Christ.

**Help with a word:** Citizens belong to a country or community.

Paul says that the believers belong to heaven.

A Saviour rescues people.

## Philippians 3:21

Christ has power to bring everything under his rule. 📖

With that power, he will change our weak bodies to be like his glorious body.

**Help with a word:** Glorious describes greatness and honour made known.
