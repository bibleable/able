# Philippians 2 — Have the same attitude as Jesus

**About this chapter**

Paul asks the believers to love each other and serve with humility.

He points to Jesus as their example.

He also writes about Timothy and Epaphroditus.

## Philippians 2:1

If Christ encourages you, his love comforts you, you share life with the Spirit, and you feel loving care... 📖

**Help with the sentence:** Paul's request continues in the next verse.

## Philippians 2:2

Then make my joy complete. 📖

Think together in the same way.

Have the same love.

Be united in spirit and purpose.

## Philippians 2:3

Do nothing just to make yourself important or to show off. 📖

Be humble and consider others more important than yourselves.

**Help with a word:** Being humble means not putting yourself above others.

## Philippians 2:4

Each of you should care about other people's needs as well as your own. 📖

## Philippians 2:5

Have the same attitude that Christ Jesus had. 📖

## Philippians 2:6

He existed in the form of God. 📖

He did not see equality with God as something to grasp.

**Help with words:** Equality means being equal.

Grasp means to seize or hold tightly.

## Philippians 2:7

Instead, he emptied himself. 📖

He took the form of a servant.

He became like human beings.

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes Christ choosing a humble place of service.

## Philippians 2:8

He appeared as a man and humbled himself. 📖

He obeyed even to the point of death.

He even died on a cross.

## Philippians 2:9

So God lifted him to the highest place. 📖

God gave him the name above every other name.

## Philippians 2:10

At the name of Jesus, every knee should bend. 📖

This includes those in heaven, on earth, and under the earth.

**Help with this action:** Bending the knee pictures bowing in honour before Jesus.

## Philippians 2:11

Every tongue should declare that Jesus Christ is Lord. 📖

This brings glory to God the Father.

**Help with words:** Every tongue means everyone speaking.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## Philippians 2:12

My dear friends, you have always obeyed. 📖

You obeyed when I was with you.

Now that I am away, obey even more.

Keep working out your salvation with fear and trembling.

**Help with words:** Salvation means being saved by God.

Paul calls them to live out their salvation with deep seriousness before God.

## Philippians 2:13

God is working in you. 📖

He helps you both to want and to do what pleases him.

## Philippians 2:14

Do everything without complaining or arguing. 📖

## Philippians 2:15

Then you can be pure and without blame. 📖

You can be God's children without fault among people who are dishonest and do wrong.

You shine among them like lights in the world.

## Philippians 2:16

Hold out the message of life. 📖

Then, on the day of Christ, I can be proud that my running and hard work were not wasted.

**Help with this picture:** Paul pictures his work as running a race.

## Philippians 2:17

Even if I am poured out like a drink offering with your faithful sacrifice and service, I am glad. 📖

I rejoice with all of you.

**Help with this picture:** A drink offering was liquid poured out as a gift in worship.

Paul pictures giving his life alongside their service of faith.

## Philippians 2:18

You should also be glad and rejoice with me. 📖

## Philippians 2:19

I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon. 📖

Hearing how you are doing will cheer me too.

## Philippians 2:20

I have no one else like Timothy who will truly care about your needs. 📖

## Philippians 2:21

All the others look after their own interests instead of those of Jesus Christ. 📖

## Philippians 2:22

You know how Timothy has proved himself. 📖

He has served with me to spread the good news, like a child working with his father.

## Philippians 2:23

I hope to send Timothy as soon as I see what will happen to me. 📖

## Philippians 2:24

I trust in the Lord that I will come soon myself. 📖

## Philippians 2:25

I thought I needed to send Epaphroditus back to you. 📖

He is my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier.

He is also your messenger, sent to care for my needs.

**Help with these pictures:** Paul calls Epaphroditus a brother because they share faith in Christ.

Fellow soldier pictures them serving and struggling together.

## Philippians 2:26

Epaphroditus has been longing for all of you. 📖

He is upset because you heard that he was ill.

## Philippians 2:27

He really was ill and nearly died. 📖

But God had mercy on him.

God also had mercy on me, sparing me sorrow upon sorrow.

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## Philippians 2:28

So I am even more eager to send him. 📖

You can rejoice when you see him again.

I can worry less.

## Philippians 2:29

Welcome him in the Lord with great joy. 📖

Honour people like him.

## Philippians 2:30

He nearly died for Christ's work. 📖

He risked his life to give me the help you could not give yourselves.
