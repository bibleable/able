# Philippians 4 — Trust God and care for each other

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to the believers in Philippi.

He thanks them for helping him.

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Philippians 4:1

My dear brothers and sisters, I love you and want to see you. 📖

You bring me joy and honour.

Keep trusting the Lord in this way.

## Philippians 4:2

Euodia and Syntyche, I ask you to agree with each other as people who belong to the Lord. 📖

## Philippians 4:3

My true partner, please help these women. 📖

They worked hard with me to spread the good news.

Clement and my other workers helped too.

Their names are written in the Book of Life.

**Help with a name:** The Book of Life records those who belong to God.

## Philippians 4:4

Always be glad in the Lord. 📖

I say it again: be glad!

## Philippians 4:5

Let everyone see that you are gentle. 📖

The Lord is near.

## Philippians 4:6

Do not worry about anything. 📖

Instead, pray to God about everything.

Ask God for what you need, and thank him.

## Philippians 4:7

God will give you peace beyond anything we can understand. 📖

His peace will guard your thoughts and feelings in Christ Jesus.

## Philippians 4:8

Brothers and sisters, think about what is true and worthy of respect. 📖

Think about what is right, pure, lovely, and good.

Think about things that are excellent and deserve praise.

## Philippians 4:9

Do what you learned and received from me. 📖

Follow what you heard me say and saw me do.

Then the God who gives peace will be with you.

## Philippians 4:10

I am very glad in the Lord that you have shown care for me again. 📖

You cared before, but had no chance to show it.

## Philippians 4:11

I am not saying this because I need something. 📖

I have learned to be satisfied whatever happens to me.

## Philippians 4:12

I know how to live with little or with plenty. 📖

I have learned to be satisfied in every situation.

I can face being full or hungry, having enough or being in need.

## Philippians 4:13

I can face all these things through Christ, who gives me strength. 📖

**Help with this verse:** Paul has just spoken about facing both plenty and need.

## Philippians 4:14

Still, it was good of you to share in my troubles. 📖

## Philippians 4:15

You Philippians remember when you first heard the good news. 📖

When I left Macedonia, only your church shared with me in giving and receiving.

## Philippians 4:16

Even in Thessalonica, you sent help for my needs more than once. 📖

## Philippians 4:17

I am not trying to get a gift. 📖

I want your giving to bring good results that God credits to you.

## Philippians 4:18

I have enough, and more than enough. 📖

Epaphroditus brought me your gifts.

They are like a sweet-smelling gift offered to God.

God accepts them and is pleased.

## Philippians 4:19

My God will meet all your needs through his great riches in Christ Jesus. 📖

## Philippians 4:20

May our God and Father receive honour forever. 📖

Amen.

**Help with a word:** Amen expresses agreement with a prayer.

## Philippians 4:21

Give my greetings to everyone who belongs to Christ Jesus. 📖

The believers with me send you greetings.

## Philippians 4:22

All God's people here greet you. 📖

Those who serve in Caesar's household especially send greetings.

**Help with a name:** Caesar was the Roman emperor.

## Philippians 4:23

May the kindness of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. 📖
