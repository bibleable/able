# Philippians 1 — Joy and courage in Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to believers in Philippi while he is a prisoner.

He thanks God for them and speaks about his hopes, struggles, and trust in Christ.

Paul speaks as “I” in this letter.

## Philippians 1:1

This letter is from Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus. 📖

It is for all God's people who belong to Christ Jesus in Philippi.

It is also for their overseers and deacons.

**Help with words:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

Overseers care for and lead a church.

Deacons serve the church and help with its needs.

## Philippians 1:2

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace. 📖

## Philippians 1:3

I thank my God every time I remember you. 📖

## Philippians 1:4

Whenever I pray for all of you, I always pray with joy. 📖

## Philippians 1:5

You have shared in the work of the good news from the first day until now. 📖

That is why I pray with joy.

## Philippians 1:6

I am sure of this. 📖

God began a good work in you.

He will keep working until it is complete on the day of Christ Jesus.

## Philippians 1:7

It is right for me to feel this way about all of you. 📖

You are dear to my heart.

You all share God's kindness with me, both in my imprisonment and in defending and confirming the good news.

## Philippians 1:8

God knows how deeply I long for all of you. 📖

I love you with the love of Christ Jesus.

## Philippians 1:9

I pray that your love will grow more and more. 📖

May it grow with knowledge and deep understanding.

## Philippians 1:10

Then you will be able to judge what is best. 📖

You can be pure and without blame for the day of Christ.

## Philippians 1:11

May you be full of the fruit of right living that comes through Jesus Christ. 📖

May this bring honour and praise to God.

**Help with this picture:** Fruit means the good results of a life made right through Jesus.

## Philippians 1:12

Brothers and sisters, I want you to know something. 📖

What has happened to me has actually helped spread the good news.

## Philippians 1:13

The whole palace guard and everyone else now know why I am a prisoner. 📖

I am in chains because of Christ.

## Philippians 1:14

Because of my imprisonment, most of the believers trust the Lord more boldly. 📖

They are much braver about speaking God's message without fear.

## Philippians 1:15

Some people speak about Christ because they are jealous and want to compete. 📖

Others do it because they want good for others.

## Philippians 1:16

Those with good intentions speak out of love. 📖

They know I have been appointed to defend the good news.

## Philippians 1:17

The others speak about Christ to make themselves important. 📖

They are not sincere.

They think they can make my suffering in prison worse.

## Philippians 1:18

What matters, then? 📖

Whether their reasons are false or true, Christ is being made known.

That makes me glad.

Yes, I will keep being glad.

## Philippians 1:19

I know that my troubles will lead to my rescue. 📖

Your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ will bring this about.

## Philippians 1:20

I eagerly hope that I will not be ashamed in any way. 📖

I hope to have complete courage.

Then, as always, Christ will be honoured through my body, whether I live or die.

## Philippians 1:21

For me, living means Christ. 📖

Dying means gain.

**Help with these words:** Paul explains below that death would mean being with Christ.

## Philippians 1:22

If I keep living in this body, I can do work that brings good results. 📖

Which would I choose?

I do not know.

## Philippians 1:23

I feel pulled in both directions. 📖

I want to leave this life and be with Christ.

That is far better.

## Philippians 1:24

But you need me to stay alive in this body more. 📖

## Philippians 1:25

I am sure of this. 📖

I know I will stay and continue with all of you.

I will help you grow and have joy in your faith.

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in Christ here.

## Philippians 1:26

Then, when I come to you again, you will have even more reason to rejoice in Christ Jesus. 📖

My coming will bring this joy.

## Philippians 1:27

Whatever happens, live in a way worthy of Christ's good news. 📖

I may come and see you, or hear about you while I am away.

Either way, I want to know that you stand firm together in one spirit.

Work together as one for the faith of the good news.

## Philippians 1:28

Do not let your opponents frighten you at all. 📖

This clearly shows that they face destruction, but you will be saved.

This comes from God.

## Philippians 1:29

For Christ's sake, God has given you more than believing in him. 📖

He has also given you suffering for him.

## Philippians 1:30

You face the same struggle you saw me face. 📖

Now you hear that I am still facing it.
