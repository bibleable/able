# Lamentations 5 — Remember and restore us

**About this chapter**

The community asks the LORD to see its suffering and restore it.

The prayer ends with an unresolved condition about rejection and anger.

## Lamentations 5:1

LORD, remember what happened to us. 📖

Look and see our disgrace!

## Lamentations 5:2

Our inheritance was given to strangers and our houses to foreigners. 📖

## Lamentations 5:3

We became children without fathers, and our mothers are widows. 📖

## Lamentations 5:4

We must pay for drinking water and buy our wood. 📖

## Lamentations 5:5

We are closely pursued, tired, and without rest. 📖

## Lamentations 5:6

We submitted to Egypt and Assyria to get enough bread. 📖

## Lamentations 5:7

Our fathers sinned and are gone, but we bear their punishment. 📖

## Lamentations 5:8

Slaves rule us, and no one rescues us from their hands. 📖

## Lamentations 5:9

We risk our lives to get bread because of the sword in the wilderness. 📖

## Lamentations 5:10

Our skin is hot as an oven with fever from hunger. 📖

## Lamentations 5:11

Women were raped in Zion, and virgins in Judah’s cities. 📖

**About the violence**

Rape means forcing someone into sexual activity.

The verse’s report of sexual violence remains explicit.

## Lamentations 5:12

Princes were hung by their hands, and elders receive no respect. 📖

## Lamentations 5:13

Young men work hard at millstones, and boys stumble under loads of wood. 📖

**Help with millstones**

These heavy stones grind grain into flour.

## Lamentations 5:14

Elders left the city gate, and young men stopped their music. 📖

## Lamentations 5:15

Joy left our hearts, and our dancing turned to mourning. 📖

## Lamentations 5:16

The crown fell from our head. 📖

How terrible for us, because we sinned!

## Lamentations 5:17

Because of this, our hearts are weak and our eyes grow dim, 📖

## Lamentations 5:18

because Mount Zion lies deserted, with foxes moving through it. 📖

## Lamentations 5:19

LORD, You rule forever. 📖

Your throne lasts from generation to generation.

## Lamentations 5:20

Why have You forgotten us forever? 📖

Why have You abandoned us for so long?

## Lamentations 5:21

LORD, restore us to Yourself so that we may return. 📖

Renew our days as of old,

## Lamentations 5:22

unless You have completely rejected us and remain angry with us beyond measure. 📖

**About the ending**

The prayer ends with this painful condition.

The draft does not add an answer or remove the uncertainty.
