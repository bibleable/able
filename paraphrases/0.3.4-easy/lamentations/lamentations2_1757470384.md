# Lamentations 2 — The Lord’s anger and the people’s tears

**About this chapter**

The poem describes the Lord acting against Jerusalem.

It includes the poet’s grief, the enemies’ mockery, a call to cry out, and Jerusalem’s prayer.

## Lamentations 2:1

How the Lord covered Daughter Zion with His anger’s cloud! 📖

He threw Israel’s glory from heaven to earth and abandoned His footstool on His day of anger.

**Help with footstool**

A footstool is a low support for someone’s feet.

Here it pictures the sacred earthly place associated with God’s presence.

## Lamentations 2:2

Without pity, the Lord swallowed all Jacob’s homes. 📖

In fury, He demolished Daughter Judah’s fortified cities.

He threw down and polluted her kingdom and princes.

## Lamentations 2:3

In fierce anger, He cut off every horn of Israel and withdrew His right hand when the enemy approached. 📖

He burned in Jacob like a flaming fire consuming everything around.

**Help with horn and hand**

The horn pictures strength.

Withdrawing His right hand pictures removing protection.

## Lamentations 2:4

He bent His bow like an enemy, His right hand ready. 📖

Like a foe, He killed everyone pleasing to the eye.

He poured anger like fire on Daughter Zion’s tent.

## Lamentations 2:5

The Lord is like an enemy who swallowed Israel and all her palaces. 📖

He destroyed her fortresses and greatly increased Daughter Judah’s mourning and grief.

## Lamentations 2:6

He destroyed His tabernacle like a shelter in a garden and ruined His meeting place. 📖

The LORD made Zion forget appointed feasts and Sabbaths.

In fierce anger, He despised king and priest alike.

**Help with tabernacle and Sabbath**

The tabernacle is a sacred dwelling or meeting place.

The Sabbath is the weekly day set apart for rest and worship.

## Lamentations 2:7

The Lord rejected His altar and abandoned His holy place. 📖

He handed her palace walls to the enemy.

They shouted in the LORD’s house as on an appointed feast day.

## Lamentations 2:8

The LORD decided to destroy Daughter Zion’s wall. 📖

He stretched a measuring line and did not withdraw His destroying hand.

He made the defenses and walls mourn and waste away together.

**Help with measuring line**

A measuring line is used in building.

Here it pictures destruction carried out deliberately.

## Lamentations 2:9

Her gates sank into the ground, and He broke their bars. 📖

Her king and princes are exiled among nations.

The law is no more, and her prophets find no vision from the LORD.

## Lamentations 2:10

Daughter Zion’s elders sit silently on the ground. 📖

They threw dust on their heads and put on sackcloth.

Jerusalem’s young women bowed their heads to the ground.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Lamentations 2:11

My eyes fail from weeping, and I churn inside. 📖

My heart pours out in grief for the destruction of the daughter of my people.

Children and babies faint in the city’s streets.

**About the voice**

The poet grieves for the community, pictured as a daughter.

## Lamentations 2:12

They cry to their mothers, “Where are grain and wine?” 📖

They faint like wounded people in the streets, their lives fading in their mothers’ arms.

## Lamentations 2:13

What can I say for you, Daughter Jerusalem? 📖

What can compare with you, Virgin Daughter Zion, so that I can comfort you?

Your wound is as deep as the sea.

Who can heal you?

## Lamentations 2:14

Your prophets’ visions were empty and deceptive. 📖

They did not expose your guilt to prevent your captivity.

Their messages for you were empty and misleading.

## Lamentations 2:15

Everyone passing claps in scorn, hisses, and shakes his head at Daughter Jerusalem. 📖

They ask, “Is this the city called perfect in beauty, the joy of all the earth?”

## Lamentations 2:16

All your enemies open their mouths, hiss, and grind their teeth against you. 📖

They say, “We swallowed her!

We waited for this day and lived to see it!”

## Lamentations 2:17

The LORD did what He planned and fulfilled the command He gave long ago. 📖

He overthrew you without pity, let the enemy gloat, and raised your foes’ horn.

**Help with the horn**

The image describes the enemies gaining power.

## Lamentations 2:18

The people’s hearts cry to the Lord. 📖

Wall of Daughter Zion, let tears run like a river, day and night.

Give yourself no relief and your eyes no rest.

## Lamentations 2:19

Get up and cry in the night, from its first watch. 📖

Pour your heart out like water before the Lord.

Lift your hands for your children’s lives, as they faint from hunger at every street corner.

**Help with first watch**

This is the first period of the night’s guarding hours.

## Lamentations 2:20

“LORD, look and consider! 📖

Whom have You treated like this?

Should women eat their children, the babies they cared for?

Should priests and prophets be killed in the Lord’s holy place?

**About this difficult prayer**

The prayer asks about cannibalism and killing in the sanctuary.

Cannibalism means eating human flesh.

The disturbing questions are retained.

## Lamentations 2:21

“Young and old lie together in the streets’ dust. 📖

My young men and women fell by the sword.

You killed them on Your day of anger, slaughtering without compassion.

## Lamentations 2:22

“You called terrors from every side as though to an appointed feast. 📖

On the LORD’s day of anger, no one escaped or survived.

My enemy destroyed those I cared for and raised.”
