# Lamentations 3 — Suffering, hope, and a plea for justice

**About this chapter**

An unnamed man describes suffering and remembers reasons for hope.

The poem moves between personal words and the community’s prayer, ending with a request for judgment.

## Lamentations 3:1

I am the man who has seen suffering under the rod of God’s anger. 📖

## Lamentations 3:2

He drove me away and made me walk in darkness instead of light. 📖

## Lamentations 3:3

He keeps turning His hand against me all day. 📖

## Lamentations 3:4

He wore away my flesh and skin and broke my bones. 📖

## Lamentations 3:5

He surrounded me like an army, with bitterness and hardship. 📖

## Lamentations 3:6

He made me live in darkness like those dead long ago. 📖

## Lamentations 3:7

He walled me in so I cannot escape and weighed me down with chains. 📖

## Lamentations 3:8

Even when I cry and plead for help, He shuts out my prayer. 📖

## Lamentations 3:9

He blocked my ways with cut stones and made my paths crooked. 📖

## Lamentations 3:10

He is like a bear waiting or a lion hiding to attack. 📖

## Lamentations 3:11

He forced me off my path, tore me apart, and left me without help. 📖

## Lamentations 3:12

He bent His bow and made me His arrow’s target. 📖

## Lamentations 3:13

He pierced my kidneys with His arrows. 📖

**Help with these images**

The bodily wounds, animal attacks, and blocked paths describe the speaker’s overwhelming suffering.

## Lamentations 3:14

All my people laugh at me and mock me in songs all day. 📖

## Lamentations 3:15

He filled me with bitterness and made me drunk with wormwood. 📖

**Help with wormwood**

Wormwood is a bitter plant.

Here it pictures painful suffering.

## Lamentations 3:16

He ground my teeth with gravel and trampled me in dust. 📖

## Lamentations 3:17

My soul has no peace, and I forgot what prosperity is. 📖

**Help with prosperity**

Prosperity means doing well and having what is needed.

## Lamentations 3:18

I say, “My strength is gone, along with my hope from the LORD.” 📖

## Lamentations 3:19

Remember my suffering and wandering, the wormwood and gall. 📖

**Help with gall**

Gall is a bitter substance used here as an image of suffering.

## Lamentations 3:20

My soul surely remembers and is humbled within me. 📖

## Lamentations 3:21

Yet I remember this, and so I have hope. 📖

## Lamentations 3:22

The LORD’s devoted love keeps us from being consumed, because His mercies never fail. 📖

## Lamentations 3:23

They are new every morning. 📖

Great is Your faithfulness!

## Lamentations 3:24

My soul says, “The LORD is my portion, so I will hope in Him.” 📖

**Help with portion**

The image describes the LORD as the One the speaker has to depend on.

## Lamentations 3:25

The LORD is good to those waiting for Him, to the soul seeking Him. 📖

## Lamentations 3:26

It is good to wait quietly for the LORD’s salvation. 📖

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Lamentations 3:27

It is good for a man to bear the yoke while young. 📖

**Help with yoke**

A yoke is a bar laid on working animals.

Here it pictures a burden or discipline to endure.

## Lamentations 3:28

Let him sit alone silently, because the LORD laid it on him. 📖

## Lamentations 3:29

Let him bury his face in dust. 📖

Perhaps hope remains.

## Lamentations 3:30

Let him offer his cheek to the person striking him and be filled with insults. 📖

**About this difficult saying**

The saying describes enduring humiliation.

Verses 34–36 make clear that the Lord does not approve injustice.

## Lamentations 3:31

The Lord will not reject us forever. 📖

## Lamentations 3:32

Even if He causes grief, He will show compassion according to His abundant devoted love. 📖

## Lamentations 3:33

He does not willingly make human beings suffer or grieve. 📖

## Lamentations 3:34

Crushing all the land’s prisoners underfoot, 📖

## Lamentations 3:35

denying a man justice before the Most High, 📖

## Lamentations 3:36

and cheating a man in his lawsuit are things the Lord does not approve. 📖

**Help with Most High**

This title refers to God.

The sentence lists injustices across verses 34–36.

## Lamentations 3:37

Who can speak and make something happen unless the Lord ordered it? 📖

## Lamentations 3:38

Do not hardship and good both come from the Most High’s mouth? 📖

## Lamentations 3:39

Why should a living man complain in view of his sins? 📖

## Lamentations 3:40

Let us examine and test our ways and return to the LORD. 📖

## Lamentations 3:41

Let us lift our hearts and hands to God in heaven. 📖

## Lamentations 3:42

“We sinned and rebelled. 📖

You have not forgiven.”

## Lamentations 3:43

You covered Yourself in anger and pursued us, killing without pity. 📖

## Lamentations 3:44

You covered Yourself with a cloud that no prayer can pass through. 📖

## Lamentations 3:45

You made us scum and rubbish among the nations. 📖

## Lamentations 3:46

All our enemies open their mouths against us. 📖

## Lamentations 3:47

Panic and a pitfall came upon us, devastation and destruction. 📖

## Lamentations 3:48

Tears stream from my eyes over the destruction of the daughter of my people. 📖

## Lamentations 3:49

My eyes overflow without stopping or relief, 📖

## Lamentations 3:50

until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees. 📖

## Lamentations 3:51

My eyes grieve my soul because of all my city’s daughters. 📖

## Lamentations 3:52

Without cause, my enemies hunted me like a bird. 📖

## Lamentations 3:53

They dropped me alive into a pit and threw stones on me. 📖

## Lamentations 3:54

Water flowed over my head, and I thought I would die. 📖

## Lamentations 3:55

LORD, I called Your name from the depths of the Pit. 📖

## Lamentations 3:56

You heard my plea, “Do not ignore my cry for relief.” 📖

## Lamentations 3:57

You came near when I called and said, “Do not fear.” 📖

## Lamentations 3:58

Lord, You defend my case and redeem my life. 📖

**Help with redeem**

Redeem means rescue or set free.

## Lamentations 3:59

LORD, You saw the wrong done to me. 📖

Show that my case is right!

## Lamentations 3:60

You saw all their hatred and plans against me. 📖

## Lamentations 3:61

LORD, You heard their insults and all their plans against me, 📖

## Lamentations 3:62

the harmful accusations and muttering of those attacking me all day. 📖

## Lamentations 3:63

Watch when they sit and rise, how they mock me in song. 📖

## Lamentations 3:64

LORD, You will repay them what they deserve for their hands’ work. 📖

## Lamentations 3:65

Put a veil of suffering over their hearts. 📖

May Your curse fall on them!

## Lamentations 3:66

LORD, You will pursue them angrily and destroy them from under Your heavens. 📖

**About the ending**

The speaker asks for a curse and anticipates the enemies’ destruction.

The severe ending is retained rather than replaced with a statement of comfort.
