# Lamentations 1 — The city has no comforter

**About this chapter**

Jerusalem is pictured as a woman who has lost her people and freedom.

The voice moves between describing her suffering and her own words of grief and prayer.

## Lamentations 1:1

How lonely the city lies, once so full of people! 📖

She was great among nations but has become a widow.

The princess of the provinces has become a slave.

## Lamentations 1:2

She weeps aloud at night, with tears on her cheeks. 📖

None of her lovers comforts her.

All her friends betrayed her and became her enemies.

**Help with lovers and friends**

The city is pictured as a woman abandoned by those she depended on.

## Lamentations 1:3

Judah went into exile under suffering and harsh slavery. 📖

She lives among nations but finds no resting place.

All her pursuers caught her in her distress.

**Help with exile**

Exile means being forced away from one’s own land.

## Lamentations 1:4

The roads to Zion mourn because no one comes to her appointed feasts. 📖

All her gates are empty.

Her priests groan, her young women grieve, and she suffers bitterly.

## Lamentations 1:5

Her foes became her masters, and her enemies live at ease. 📖

The LORD brought her grief because of her many offenses.

Her children went away captive before the enemy.

## Lamentations 1:6

All beauty and glory left Daughter Zion. 📖

Her princes are like deer without pasture, too weak to flee from a hunter.

**Help with Daughter Zion**

Jerusalem and its people are pictured as a daughter.

## Lamentations 1:7

In suffering and wandering, Jerusalem remembers all her old treasures. 📖

When her people fell into enemy hands, no one helped her.

Her enemies watched and laughed at her fall.

## Lamentations 1:8

Jerusalem sinned greatly and became an object of scorn. 📖

Those who honored her despise her now because they saw her nakedness.

She groans and turns away.

## Lamentations 1:9

Her uncleanness stains her skirts, and she did not consider her end. 📖

Her fall was astonishing, and no one comforted her.

“LORD, look at my suffering, because the enemy won!”

**Help with the stained clothing**

The image pictures Jerusalem’s shame and wrongdoing.

The closing words are Jerusalem’s cry to the LORD.

## Lamentations 1:10

The enemy seized all her treasures. 📖

She saw nations enter her holy place, those You forbade to enter Your gathering.

**About You**

The words address God, who had set the boundary for His assembly.

## Lamentations 1:11

All her people groan as they search for bread. 📖

They traded treasures for food to stay alive.

“LORD, look and consider, because I have become despised.”

## Lamentations 1:12

“All who pass, is this nothing to you? 📖

Look and see!

Is any sorrow like mine, inflicted on me by the LORD on the day of His fierce anger?

## Lamentations 1:13

“He sent fire from above that overpowered my bones. 📖

He spread a net for my feet and turned me back.

He left me deserted and weak all day.

## Lamentations 1:14

“His hand bound my offenses into a yoke and hung them around my neck. 📖

The Lord broke my strength and handed me to those I cannot withstand.

**Help with yoke**

A yoke is a bar laid on working animals.

Here it pictures the crushing burden of Jerusalem’s wrongdoing and defeat.

## Lamentations 1:15

“The Lord rejected all my mighty men and called an army to crush my young warriors. 📖

He trampled the Virgin Daughter of Judah like grapes in a winepress.

**Help with winepress**

A winepress is a place where grapes are crushed.

The image describes the people being crushed in judgment.

## Lamentations 1:16

“I weep over these things, and tears flow from my eyes. 📖

No one is near to comfort me or revive my soul.

My children have nothing because the enemy won.”

## Lamentations 1:17

Zion stretches out her hands, but no one comforts her. 📖

The LORD decreed that Jacob’s neighbors become his enemies.

Jerusalem became an unclean thing among them.

**Help with unclean**

Here the word describes being treated as polluted and rejected.

It does not make ordinary illness or disability a moral fault.

## Lamentations 1:18

“The LORD is righteous, but I rebelled against His command. 📖

All you people, listen and look at my suffering.

My young men and women went into captivity.

**Help with righteous**

Righteous means right and just.

## Lamentations 1:19

“I called my lovers, but they betrayed me. 📖

My priests and elders died in the city while seeking food to stay alive.

## Lamentations 1:20

“LORD, see my distress! 📖

I churn inside, and my heart pounds because I have greatly rebelled.

Outside, the sword takes loved ones; inside, there is death.

## Lamentations 1:21

“People heard my groans, but no one comforts me. 📖

My enemies heard my trouble and are glad You caused it.

Bring the day You announced so that they become like me.

## Lamentations 1:22

“Let all their wickedness come before You. 📖

Deal with them as You dealt with me because of all my offenses.

My groans are many, and my heart is weak.”

**About this prayer**

Jerusalem asks God to bring similar judgment on her enemies.

The severe request is retained.
