# James 4 — Come near to God

**About this chapter**

James warns about quarrels, divided loyalty, judging others, and proud plans.

He calls people to humble themselves before God.

## James 4:1

What causes fights and quarrels among you? 📖

Do they not come from the desires fighting inside you?

## James 4:2

You want what you do not have. 📖

You kill and desire what others have, but you cannot get it.

You quarrel and fight.

You do not have because you do not ask.

## James 4:3

When you ask, you do not receive because you ask for the wrong reasons. 📖

You want to waste it on your pleasures.

## James 4:4

You adulteresses! 📖

Do you not know that friendship with the world means being God's enemy?

Anyone choosing to be the world's friend makes themselves God's enemy.

**Help with this picture:** James uses broken marriage faithfulness as a picture of turning from God.

## James 4:5

Do you think the Scriptures say this without reason? 📖

The Spirit God caused to live in us longs with jealousy.

**Help with the setting:** James is talking about loyalty to God in this passage.

## James 4:6

But God gives us more kindness. 📖

That is why the Scriptures say, “God opposes the proud but gives kindness to the humble.”

## James 4:7

So submit to God. 📖

Stand against the devil, and he will run from you.

## James 4:8

Come near to God, and he will come near to you. 📖

Clean your hands, you sinners.

Make your hearts pure, you people with divided loyalties.

**Help with these pictures:** Clean hands and pure hearts picture turning from wrongdoing to faithfulness to God.

## James 4:9

Grieve, mourn, and cry. 📖

Let your laughter turn to mourning and your joy to sadness.

## James 4:10

Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you up. 📖

## James 4:11

Brothers and sisters, do not speak falsely against each other. 📖

Anyone speaking against a fellow believer or judging them speaks against the law and judges it.

If you judge the law, you are its judge rather than someone who obeys it.

## James 4:12

There is only one Lawgiver and Judge, who can save and destroy. 📖

But who are you to judge your neighbour?

## James 4:13

Now listen, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go to this city or that one. 📖

We will stay a year, do business, and make money.”

## James 4:14

You do not even know what will happen tomorrow! 📖

What is your life?

You are a mist that appears briefly and then disappears.

## James 4:15

Instead, say, “If the Lord wants, we will live and do this or that.” 📖

## James 4:16

But now you boast about your proud plans. 📖

All such boasting is evil.

## James 4:17

Anyone who knows the right thing to do but does not do it is guilty of sin. 📖
