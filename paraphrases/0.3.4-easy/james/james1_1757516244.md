# James 1 — Trust God and put his word into practice

**About this chapter**

James writes to the 12 tribes scattered outside their homeland.

He teaches about hardship, temptation, and obeying God.

## James 1:1

James, a servant of God and the Lord Jesus Christ, sends greetings. 📖

This is for the 12 tribes scattered far away.

## James 1:2

Brothers and sisters, count it as great joy when you face many kinds of trouble. 📖

## James 1:3

You know that testing your faith teaches you to keep going. 📖

## James 1:4

Let this endurance finish its work. 📖

Then you will be mature and complete, lacking nothing.

**Help with a word:** Endurance means continuing through difficulty.

## James 1:5

If you need wisdom, ask God. 📖

God gives generously to everyone without blaming them.

He will give it to you.

## James 1:6

Ask with trust, without doubting. 📖

Someone who doubts is like a sea wave blown about by wind.

## James 1:7

That person should not expect to receive anything from the Lord. 📖

## James 1:8

Their mind is divided. 📖

They cannot stay steady in anything they do.

## James 1:9

A believer who is poor should be glad about the high place God gives them. 📖

## James 1:10

A rich believer should be glad about being brought low. 📖

Rich people will pass away like wild flowers.

## James 1:11

The hot sun dries the plant. 📖

Its flower falls, and its beauty is gone.

Rich people will also fade while busy with their plans.

## James 1:12

God blesses the person who keeps going through testing. 📖

After passing the test, that person receives the crown of life.

God promised it to those who love him.

**Help with this picture:** The crown pictures the reward of life with God.

## James 1:13

When tempted, do not say God is tempting you. 📖

Evil cannot tempt God.

God does not tempt anyone to do evil.

## James 1:14

People are tempted when their own evil desires lure them away. 📖

## James 1:15

Desire gives birth to sin. 📖

Sin, when fully grown, gives birth to death.

**Help with this picture:** Wrong desires grow into wrong actions and deadly results.

## James 1:16

My dear brothers and sisters, do not be fooled. 📖

## James 1:17

Every good and perfect gift comes from above. 📖

It comes from the Father who made the heavenly lights.

He does not change like shifting shadows.

## James 1:18

God chose to give us new birth through the message of truth. 📖

We would be like the first part of his harvest of creation.

**Help with this picture:** The first harvest is a special beginning belonging to God.

## James 1:19

My dear brothers and sisters, remember this. 📖

Be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to become angry.

## James 1:20

Human anger does not produce the right living God wants. 📖

## James 1:21

Remove all evil and moral filth. 📖

Humbly accept God's message planted in you.

It can save your lives.

**Help with this picture:** A planted message is one received deeply within you.

## James 1:22

Do what God's message says. 📖

Do not only listen.

Otherwise, you fool yourselves.

## James 1:23

Someone who listens without obeying is like a person looking in a mirror. 📖

## James 1:24

The person looks, walks away, and immediately forgets their own face. 📖

## James 1:25

Look carefully into God's perfect law that brings freedom. 📖

Keep following it.

Do not forget what you hear, but do it.

God will bless you in what you do.

## James 1:26

Anyone who thinks they serve God but does not control their speech fools themselves. 📖

Their religion is worthless.

## James 1:27

God our Father accepts pure worship. 📖

Care for children without parents and women whose husbands have died when they are in trouble.

Keep the world's evil from staining your life.
