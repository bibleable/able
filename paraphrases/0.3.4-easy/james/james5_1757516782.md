# James 5 — Be patient and pray

**About this chapter**

James warns rich people about injustice.

He urges believers to wait patiently for the Lord, pray, and help people return to the truth.

## James 5:1

Now listen, you rich people. 📖

Cry and wail because of the misery coming on you.

## James 5:2

Your riches have rotted, and moths have eaten your clothes. 📖

## James 5:3

Your gold and silver have corroded. 📖

Their corrosion will speak against you and eat your flesh like fire.

You have stored up treasure in the last days.

**Help with a word:** Corrosion is damage like rust on metal.

## James 5:4

Look, you kept back the wages of the workers who cut your fields. 📖

Their unpaid wages cry out against you.

The harvest workers' cries have reached the ears of the Lord of Hosts.

**Help with words:** Wages are money earned by working.

Lord of Hosts means Lord of the heavenly armies.

## James 5:5

You lived on earth in luxury, giving yourselves every pleasure. 📖

You fattened your hearts in the day of slaughter.

**Help with this picture:** James pictures enjoying luxury as being fattened before being killed.

## James 5:6

You condemned and murdered the righteous, who did not resist you. 📖

## James 5:7

Brothers and sisters, be patient until the Lord comes. 📖

A farmer waits patiently for the precious crop from the ground.

The farmer waits for the autumn and spring rains.

## James 5:8

You too must be patient and strengthen your hearts. 📖

The Lord's coming is near.

## James 5:9

Brothers and sisters, do not complain about each other, so that you will not be judged. 📖

Look, the Judge is standing at the door!

**Help with this picture:** The door pictures how near the Judge's coming is.

## James 5:10

Brothers and sisters, remember the prophets who spoke in the Lord's name. 📖

Take their patience in suffering as your example.

## James 5:11

We call those who kept going blessed. 📖

You have heard how Job kept going and seen what the Lord brought about.

The Lord is full of deep care and mercy.

## James 5:12

Above all, my brothers and sisters, do not make oaths. 📖

Do not swear by heaven, earth, or anything else.

Let your “Yes” be yes, and your “No” be no.

Then you will not fall under judgment.

**Help with a word:** An oath is a serious promise used to show that words are true.

## James 5:13

Is anyone suffering? 📖

They should pray.

Is anyone cheerful?

They should sing praises.

## James 5:14

Is anyone sick? 📖

They should call the church elders to pray over them.

The elders should put oil on them in the Lord's name.

**Help with a word:** Elders are leaders who care for a church.

## James 5:15

The prayer offered in faith will restore the sick person. 📖

The Lord will raise them up.

If they have sinned, they will be forgiven.

**Help with the condition:** The verse says “if” the sick person has sinned.

It does not say that every illness is caused by a person's sin.

## James 5:16

So confess your sins to each other and pray for each other, so that you may be healed. 📖

A righteous person's prayer has great power to succeed.

**Help with words:** Confess means admit what you have done wrong.

Righteous means right before God.

## James 5:17

Elijah was a human being like us. 📖

He prayed earnestly for no rain.

It did not rain on the land for three and a half years.

## James 5:18

He prayed again, and the sky gave rain. 📖

The earth produced its crops.

## James 5:19

My brothers and sisters, one of you may wander from the truth, and someone may bring that person back. 📖

## James 5:20

Remember this. 📖

Anyone who turns a sinner from the wrong way will save that person's soul from death.

They will cover many sins.
