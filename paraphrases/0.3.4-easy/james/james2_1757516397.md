# James 2 — Faith must lead to action

**About this chapter**

James warns against favouring rich people over poor people.

He explains that faith without action is dead.

## James 2:1

My brothers and sisters, you hold faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

Do not favour some people over others.

## James 2:2

Suppose a man enters your meeting wearing a gold ring and fine clothes. 📖

A poor man in worn-out clothes also enters.

## James 2:3

You give special attention to the man in fine clothes. 📖

You say, “Here is a place of honour.”

But you tell the poor man, “Stand there,” or “Sit at my feet.”

## James 2:4

Have you not made unfair distinctions among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts? 📖

## James 2:5

Listen, my dear brothers and sisters. 📖

Has God not chosen the world's poor to be rich in faith?

They will inherit the kingdom he promised to those who love him.

**Help with words:** Faith means trust in God here.

God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## James 2:6

But you have dishonoured poor people. 📖

Are rich people not the ones who oppress you and drag you into court?

## James 2:7

Are they not the ones insulting the noble name by which you have been called? 📖

## James 2:8

If you really keep the royal law in the Scriptures, you do well. 📖

It says, “Love your neighbour as yourself.”

**Help with words:** Royal means belonging to a king.

The Scriptures are the holy writings.

## James 2:9

But if you favour some people over others, you sin. 📖

The law finds you guilty of breaking it.

## James 2:10

Anyone who keeps the whole law but fails at one point is guilty of breaking all of it. 📖

## James 2:11

God said, “Do not commit adultery,” and also, “Do not murder.” 📖

Even if you do not commit adultery, murdering someone makes you a lawbreaker.

**Help with a word:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

## James 2:12

Speak and act as people who will be judged by the law that gives freedom. 📖

## James 2:13

Anyone who has shown no mercy will face judgment without mercy. 📖

Mercy wins over judgment.

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## James 2:14

Brothers and sisters, what good is claiming to have faith without actions? 📖

Can that kind of faith save a person?

## James 2:15

Suppose a brother or sister has no clothes or daily food. 📖

## James 2:16

One of you says, “Go in peace. 📖

Keep warm and eat well.”

But you give them nothing their body needs.

What good is that?

## James 2:17

In the same way, faith on its own is dead if it does not lead to action. 📖

## James 2:18

Someone may say, “You have faith, and I have actions.” 📖

Show me your faith without actions.

I will show you my faith by my actions.

## James 2:19

You believe that God is one. 📖

Good!

Even evil spirits believe that, and they shake with fear.

## James 2:20

You foolish person, do you want proof that faith without actions is worthless? 📖

## James 2:21

Was our father Abraham not made right by what he did when he offered his son Isaac on the altar? 📖

**Help with a word:** An altar is a place where gifts are offered in worship.

## James 2:22

You see that his faith worked together with his actions. 📖

His actions made his faith complete.

## James 2:23

This fulfilled the Scripture saying, “Abraham believed God, and God counted this as righteousness.” 📖

He was called God's friend.

**Help with a word:** Righteousness means being right before God.

## James 2:24

You see that a person is made right by actions, rather than by faith alone. 📖

## James 2:25

In the same way, was Rahab the prostitute not made right by her actions? 📖

She welcomed the spies and sent them away by a different route.

**Help with a word:** A prostitute is someone who has sex in exchange for money.

## James 2:26

A body without the spirit is dead. 📖

In the same way, faith without actions is dead.
