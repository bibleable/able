# James 3 — Words and wisdom

**About this chapter**

James warns about the power of speech.

He compares true wisdom with jealousy and selfish ambition.

## James 3:1

My brothers and sisters, not many of you should become teachers. 📖

You know that we who teach will be judged more strictly.

## James 3:2

We all stumble in many ways. 📖

Anyone never at fault in speech is a perfect person, able to control their whole body.

## James 3:3

We put bits in horses' mouths to make them obey us. 📖

With these, we can guide the whole animal.

**Help with a word:** A bit is part of the equipment used to guide a horse.

## James 3:4

Think about ships too. 📖

They are large and driven by strong winds.

Yet a very small rudder steers them wherever the pilot wants.

**Help with words:** A rudder is a movable part at the back of a boat that guides its direction.

The pilot is the person steering it.

## James 3:5

The tongue is also a small part of the body, but it boasts of great things. 📖

Think how a small spark can set a great forest on fire.

## James 3:6

The tongue is a fire, a whole world of evil among our body parts. 📖

It spoils the whole person and sets the course of life on fire.

Hell itself sets it on fire.

**Help with this picture:** James pictures the destructive power of evil speech as a spreading fire.

## James 3:7

People tame, and have tamed, all kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and sea creatures. 📖

## James 3:8

But no human person can tame the tongue. 📖

It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.

**Help with these pictures:** Taming pictures controlling speech.

Poison pictures the harm evil words can cause.

## James 3:9

With the tongue, we bless our Lord and Father. 📖

With it, we also curse people made in God's likeness.

## James 3:10

Blessing and cursing come from the same mouth. 📖

My brothers and sisters, this should not happen!

## James 3:11

Can fresh water and salt water flow from the same spring? 📖

## James 3:12

Brothers and sisters, can a fig tree grow olives, or a grapevine grow figs? 📖

A salt spring cannot produce fresh water either.

## James 3:13

Who among you is wise and understanding? 📖

Show it through good conduct and actions done with the humility wisdom brings.

**Help with a word:** Humility means not putting yourself above others.

## James 3:14

But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast or deny the truth. 📖

## James 3:15

That wisdom does not come from above. 📖

It is earthly, unspiritual, and from evil spirits.

## James 3:16

Where jealousy and selfish ambition are found, there will be disorder and every evil practice. 📖

## James 3:17

Wisdom from above is first pure, then peace-loving, gentle, and willing to listen. 📖

It is full of mercy and good fruit, fair to everyone, and sincere.

**Help with these words:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

Good fruit pictures good results in life.

## James 3:18

People who make peace plant in peace and gather the fruit of righteousness. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Planting and gathering fruit picture good actions and the right living they produce.
