# 1 Timothy 3 — Those who serve the church

**About this chapter**

Paul describes the conduct required of overseers and deacons.

He explains why he is writing and gives a statement of faith about Christ.

## 1 Timothy 3:1

This saying is trustworthy. 📖

Anyone wanting to become an overseer desires a good task.

**Help with a word:** An overseer cares for and leads a church.

## 1 Timothy 3:2

An overseer must be above blame and the husband of one wife. 📖

He must be steady, self-controlled, respectable, welcoming to guests, and able to teach.

## 1 Timothy 3:3

He must not depend on wine or be violent. 📖

He must be gentle, peaceable, and free from the love of money.

## 1 Timothy 3:4

He must manage his own household well and keep his children under control with complete dignity. 📖

## 1 Timothy 3:5

If someone cannot manage his own household, how can he care for God's church? 📖

## 1 Timothy 3:6

He must not be someone who only recently became a believer. 📖

Otherwise, he may become proud and fall under the same condemnation as the devil.

## 1 Timothy 3:7

He must also have a good name among outsiders. 📖

Then he will not fall into disgrace and the devil's trap.

## 1 Timothy 3:8

Deacons too must be worthy of respect. 📖

They must not speak in two different ways, drink too much wine, or be greedy for money.

**Help with words:** Deacons serve the church and help with its needs.

Speaking in two ways means saying different things to different people dishonestly.

## 1 Timothy 3:9

They must hold to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience. 📖

**Help with a word:** Mystery here means truth that God has made known.

## 1 Timothy 3:10

They must first be tested. 📖

Then, if they are above blame, let them serve as deacons.

## 1 Timothy 3:11

Likewise, the women must be worthy of respect, not slanderers, but steady and faithful in everything. 📖

**Help with a word:** Slanderers tell harmful false accusations about others.

## 1 Timothy 3:12

A deacon must be the husband of one wife. 📖

He must manage his children and his household well.

## 1 Timothy 3:13

Those serving well as deacons gain a good standing and great confidence in their faith in Christ Jesus. 📖

## 1 Timothy 3:14

I hope to come to you soon, but I am writing these things now. 📖

## 1 Timothy 3:15

If I am delayed, you will know how people must behave in God's household. 📖

This is the living God's church, the pillar and foundation of the truth.

**Help with this picture:** Pillars and foundations hold up buildings.

Paul pictures the church supporting the truth.

## 1 Timothy 3:16

We all confess that the mystery of godliness is great. 📖

He appeared in the flesh.

The Spirit showed him to be right.

Angels saw him.

He was announced among the nations.

People throughout the world believed in him.

He was taken up in glory.

**Help with these words:** These statements describe Christ.

In the flesh means as a real human being.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.
