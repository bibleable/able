# 1 Timothy 2 — Prayer and conduct in worship

**About this chapter**

Paul urges prayer for everyone.

He gives instructions about men and women in worship.

## 1 Timothy 2:1

First, I urge you to offer requests, prayers, prayers for others, and thanksgiving for everyone. 📖

## 1 Timothy 2:2

Pray for kings and everyone in authority. 📖

Then we may live peaceful and quiet lives, devoted to God and worthy of respect.

## 1 Timothy 2:3

This is good and pleases God our Saviour. 📖

## 1 Timothy 2:4

He wants everyone to be saved and come to know the truth. 📖

## 1 Timothy 2:5

There is one God and one mediator between God and people, the man Christ Jesus. 📖

**Help with a word:** A mediator stands between people to bring them together.

## 1 Timothy 2:6

He gave himself as a ransom for everyone. 📖

This testimony was given at just the right time.

**Help with words:** A ransom is a price paid to set someone free.

Testimony is a witness's account of what is true.

## 1 Timothy 2:7

I was appointed to announce this as a preacher, an apostle, and a faithful, true teacher of Gentiles. 📖

I tell the truth.

I am not lying about anything.

**Help with a word:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## 1 Timothy 2:8

I want men everywhere to pray, lifting up holy hands without anger or quarrelling. 📖

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## 1 Timothy 2:9

Likewise, women should dress respectably, with modesty and self-control. 📖

They should not decorate themselves with braided hair, gold, pearls, or expensive clothes.

**Help with a word:** Modesty means behaving and dressing without trying to draw improper attention.

## 1 Timothy 2:10

Instead, they should dress themselves with good actions, as is fitting for women claiming to worship God. 📖

## 1 Timothy 2:11

A woman must learn quietly and with full submission. 📖

**Help with a word:** Submission means accepting another person's authority.

## 1 Timothy 2:12

I do not permit a woman to teach or exercise authority over a man. 📖

She is to remain quiet.

## 1 Timothy 2:13

Adam was formed first, then Eve. 📖

## 1 Timothy 2:14

Adam was not deceived. 📖

The woman was deceived and fell into wrongdoing.

## 1 Timothy 2:15

Women will be saved through childbearing if they continue in faith, love, and holiness, with self-control. 📖

**Help with this difficult passage:** Childbearing means giving birth to children.

Paul includes the condition about continuing in faith, love, holiness, and self-control.
