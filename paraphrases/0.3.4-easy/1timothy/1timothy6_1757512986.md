# 1 Timothy 6 — Be content and rich in good works

**About this chapter**

Paul writes about slaves and masters, false teaching, money, and faithful service.

He urges Timothy to guard what God has entrusted to him.

## 1 Timothy 6:1

Everyone under the yoke of slavery should consider their masters worthy of full honour. 📖

Then God's name and our teaching will not be spoken against.

**Help with these words:** Slaves were people owned by others.

A yoke pictures their state of being controlled.

## 1 Timothy 6:2

Slaves with believing masters must not disrespect them because they are fellow believers. 📖

Instead, serve them even more, because those benefiting from the service are dear believers.

Teach and encourage these things.

## 1 Timothy 6:3

Someone may teach different beliefs and reject the reliable words of our Lord Jesus Christ and teaching about godliness. 📖

## 1 Timothy 6:4

Such a person is proud and understands nothing. 📖

They have an unhealthy interest in disputes and arguments about words.

These cause envy, fighting, abusive talk, and evil suspicions.

## 1 Timothy 6:5

They also cause constant friction among people with corrupt minds who lack the truth. 📖

These people think serving God is a way to gain wealth.

## 1 Timothy 6:6

But living for God with contentment is great gain. 📖

**Help with a word:** Contentment means being satisfied with what you have.

## 1 Timothy 6:7

We brought nothing into the world and cannot take anything out. 📖

## 1 Timothy 6:8

If we have food and clothing, we will be content with these. 📖

## 1 Timothy 6:9

People wanting to be rich fall into temptation and a trap. 📖

Many foolish, harmful desires drag them into ruin and destruction.

## 1 Timothy 6:10

The love of money is the root of all kinds of evil. 📖

Some craving money have wandered from the faith and pierced themselves with many sorrows.

**Help with these pictures:** A root pictures what produces something.

Piercing with sorrows pictures the pain they have brought on themselves.

## 1 Timothy 6:11

But you, man of God, flee from these things. 📖

Seek righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, and gentleness.

**Help with words:** Righteousness means being right before God and living rightly.

Godliness means living in devotion to God.

Endurance means continuing through difficulty without giving up.

## 1 Timothy 6:12

Fight the good fight of faith. 📖

Take hold of life that lasts for ever.

God called you to it when you made your good confession before many witnesses.

## 1 Timothy 6:13

I give you this charge before God, who gives life to all things. 📖

I also charge you before Christ Jesus, who made the good confession in his testimony before Pontius Pilate.

## 1 Timothy 6:14

Keep this command without stain or blame until our Lord Jesus Christ appears. 📖

## 1 Timothy 6:15

God will bring this about at his own time. 📖

He is the blessed and only Ruler, the King of kings and Lord of lords.

## 1 Timothy 6:16

He alone cannot die and lives in light no one can come near. 📖

No one has seen him or can see him.

May honour and lasting rule belong to him!

Amen.

## 1 Timothy 6:17

Tell rich people in this present age not to be proud or place their hope in uncertain wealth. 📖

They must hope in God, who richly gives us everything to enjoy.

## 1 Timothy 6:18

Tell them to do good, be rich in good actions, be generous, and be ready to share. 📖

## 1 Timothy 6:19

Then they will store up a firm foundation for the future and take hold of what is truly life. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul pictures generosity as storing treasure and building for the future.

## 1 Timothy 6:20

Timothy, guard what has been entrusted to you. 📖

Avoid disrespectful empty talk and the opposing arguments of so-called “knowledge.”

## 1 Timothy 6:21

Some have claimed this knowledge and wandered from the faith. 📖

May God's kindness be with you all.
