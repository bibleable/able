# 1 Timothy 4 — Train yourself to live for God

**About this chapter**

Paul warns about false teaching and urges Timothy to be a faithful example.

## 1 Timothy 4:1

The Spirit clearly says that in later times some people will leave the faith. 📖

They will follow deceitful spirits and the teachings of evil spirits.

## 1 Timothy 4:2

They will be influenced by lying hypocrites whose consciences are burned with a hot iron. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul pictures a conscience no longer sensitive to wrongdoing.

Hypocrites pretend to be faithful while their actions show otherwise.

## 1 Timothy 4:3

These teachers will forbid marriage and order people to avoid certain foods. 📖

God made these foods for believers who know the truth to receive with thanks.

## 1 Timothy 4:4

Everything God created is good. 📖

Nothing received with thanks should be rejected.

## 1 Timothy 4:5

God's word and prayer make it holy. 📖

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## 1 Timothy 4:6

By teaching these things to fellow believers, you will be Christ Jesus's good servant. 📖

The words of faith and reliable teaching you have followed nourish you.

**Help with this picture:** Nourishing pictures strengthening someone, as food strengthens the body.

## 1 Timothy 4:7

Reject silly myths that disrespect God. 📖

Instead, train yourself to live for God.

## 1 Timothy 4:8

Physical exercise has some value. 📖

But living for God has value in every way.

It holds promise for this life and the life to come.

## 1 Timothy 4:9

This saying is trustworthy and should be fully accepted. 📖

## 1 Timothy 4:10

This is why we work hard and struggle. 📖

We have set our hope on the living God, the Saviour of everyone, especially believers.

## 1 Timothy 4:11

Command and teach these things. 📖

## 1 Timothy 4:12

Do not let anyone look down on you because you are young. 📖

Set an example for believers in speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity.

## 1 Timothy 4:13

Until I come, devote yourself to reading Scripture publicly, encouraging people, and teaching. 📖

**Help with a word:** Scripture means the holy writings.

## 1 Timothy 4:14

Do not neglect the gift in you. 📖

It was given through the prophecy spoken over you when the elders placed their hands on you.

**Help with a word:** Elders are leaders who care for a church.

## 1 Timothy 4:15

Work hard at these things and give yourself fully to them. 📖

Then everyone will see your progress.

## 1 Timothy 4:16

Watch your life and your teaching closely. 📖

Keep going in these things.

By doing this, you will save both yourself and those who hear you.
