# 1 Timothy 1 — Teach the truth with love

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to Timothy about false teaching, the law, and God's mercy.

Paul speaks as “I” in this letter.

## 1 Timothy 1:1

This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus. 📖

God our Saviour and Christ Jesus our hope commanded me to serve as an apostle.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

A Saviour rescues people.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## 1 Timothy 1:2

I write to Timothy, my true child in the faith. 📖

May God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord give you kindness, mercy, and peace.

**Help with words:** Paul calls Timothy his child because of their close bond in faith.

Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## 1 Timothy 1:3

When I left for Macedonia, I urged you to stay in Ephesus. 📖

Tell certain men to stop teaching false beliefs.

## 1 Timothy 1:4

Tell them to stop giving their attention to myths and endless family histories. 📖

These lead to guesses, rather than carrying out God's work through faith.

**Help with words:** Myths here means stories used in the false teaching.

Family histories are lists of ancestors and their relationships.

Faith means trust in God here.

## 1 Timothy 1:5

Our teaching aims at love from a pure heart, a clear conscience, and sincere faith. 📖

## 1 Timothy 1:6

Some people have left these things and turned to empty talk. 📖

## 1 Timothy 1:7

They want to teach the law. 📖

But they do not understand what they say or what they claim so confidently.

## 1 Timothy 1:8

We know the law is good if someone uses it properly. 📖

## 1 Timothy 1:9

The law is not made for the righteous, but for people who reject it and rebel. 📖

It is for the ungodly, sinful, unholy, and disrespectful of sacred things.

It is for those who kill their fathers or mothers, and for murderers.

**Help with a word:** Righteous means right before God.

## 1 Timothy 1:10

The law is for wrong sexual behaviour and for people who have sex with people of the same sex. 📖

It is for slave traders, liars, people who lie under oath, and anyone else opposing reliable teaching.

**Help with words:** Slave traders buy and sell people as property.

An oath is a serious promise used to show that words are true.

## 1 Timothy 1:11

This teaching agrees with the glorious good news of the blessed God. 📖

He entrusted this good news to me.

## 1 Timothy 1:12

I thank Christ Jesus our Lord for giving me strength. 📖

He considered me faithful and appointed me to serve.

## 1 Timothy 1:13

Before, I insulted God, persecuted his people, and acted violently. 📖

Yet I received mercy because I acted without understanding and without belief.

## 1 Timothy 1:14

Our Lord's kindness overflowed to me, along with the faith and love found in Christ Jesus. 📖

## 1 Timothy 1:15

This saying is trustworthy and should be fully accepted. 📖

Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners.

I am the worst of them.

## 1 Timothy 1:16

That is why I received mercy. 📖

In me, the worst sinner, Christ Jesus could show his complete patience.

I would be an example to those who would believe in him for life that lasts for ever.

## 1 Timothy 1:17

May the only God, the eternal King whom no one can see and who cannot die, receive honour and glory. 📖

May this be for ever and ever.

Amen.

**Help with words:** Eternal means lasting for ever.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## 1 Timothy 1:18

Timothy, my child, I entrust you with this command in keeping with the earlier prophecies about you. 📖

Through them, fight the good fight.

**Help with these words:** Prophecies are messages spoken as coming from God.

Fighting the good fight pictures serving faithfully through opposition.

## 1 Timothy 1:19

Hold on to faith and a good conscience. 📖

Some rejected a good conscience and shipwrecked their faith.

**Help with this picture:** A shipwreck pictures the ruin of their faith.

## 1 Timothy 1:20

Hymenaeus and Alexander are among them. 📖

I handed them over to Satan so they would learn not to insult God.
