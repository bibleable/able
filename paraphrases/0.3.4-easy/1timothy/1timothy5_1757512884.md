# 1 Timothy 5 — Care for families, widows, and elders

**About this chapter**

Paul gives instructions about care, responsibility, and conduct within the church.

He also gives Timothy personal advice.

## 1 Timothy 5:1

Do not speak harshly to an older man. 📖

Appeal to him as to a father.

Treat younger men as brothers.

## 1 Timothy 5:2

Treat older women as mothers and younger women as sisters, with complete purity. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:3

Honour widows who are truly widows. 📖

**Help with these words:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

Paul explains below the needs of widows left without family support.

## 1 Timothy 5:4

If a widow has children or grandchildren, they must first show devotion to God by caring for their family. 📖

They must repay their parents.

This pleases God.

## 1 Timothy 5:5

A widow truly in need and all alone puts her hope in God. 📖

She keeps asking and praying night and day.

## 1 Timothy 5:6

But the widow living for pleasure is dead even while alive. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Dead describes her spiritual condition, rather than the death of her body.

## 1 Timothy 5:7

Give the believers these instructions so they will be above blame. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:8

Anyone not providing for their own people, especially their household, has denied the faith. 📖

That person is worse than an unbeliever.

## 1 Timothy 5:9

Put a widow on the list if she is at least 60 years old and was faithful to her husband. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:10

She must be known for good actions. 📖

These include raising children, welcoming strangers, washing believers' feet, helping suffering people, and devoting herself to every good work.

## 1 Timothy 5:11

Do not put younger widows on the list. 📖

When their desires draw them away from Christ, they will want to marry.

## 1 Timothy 5:12

Then they will face judgment because they set aside their first faith. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:13

They also learn to be idle, going from house to house. 📖

They become gossips and busybodies too, talking about things they should not mention.

**Help with words:** Idle means not working.

Gossips spread talk about other people.

Busybodies interfere in other people's business.

## 1 Timothy 5:14

So I advise younger widows to marry, have children, and manage their households. 📖

Then the enemy will have no opportunity for slander.

## 1 Timothy 5:15

Some have already turned aside to follow Satan. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:16

If a believing woman has widows depending on her, she must help them. 📖

The church must not be burdened, so it can help widows truly in need.

## 1 Timothy 5:17

Elders who lead well deserve double honour, especially those working hard at preaching and teaching. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:18

Scripture says, “Do not muzzle an ox while it walks over the grain to separate the seeds.” 📖

It also says, “The worker deserves his wages.”

**Help with words:** A muzzle stops an animal eating.

An ox is a large farm animal.

Wages are money earned by working.

## 1 Timothy 5:19

Do not accept an accusation against an elder without the testimony of two or three witnesses. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:20

Correct those who keep sinning in front of everyone, so the others will fear sin. 📖

## 1 Timothy 5:21

Before God, Christ Jesus, and the chosen angels, I seriously charge you to keep these instructions without bias. 📖

Do nothing out of favouritism.

## 1 Timothy 5:22

Do not be too quick to place your hands on people. 📖

Otherwise, you share in others' sins.

Keep yourself pure.

## 1 Timothy 5:23

Stop drinking only water. 📖

Use a little wine because of your stomach and your frequent illnesses.

## 1 Timothy 5:24

Some people's sins are clear and go ahead of them to judgment. 📖

Others' sins appear only later.

## 1 Timothy 5:25

In the same way, good actions are clear. 📖

Even those not easily seen cannot stay hidden.
