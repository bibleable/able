# Titus 1 — Leaders and reliable teaching

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to Titus about appointing leaders in Crete and correcting false teachers.

Paul speaks as “I” in this letter.

## Titus 1:1

This letter is from Paul, God's servant and an apostle of Jesus Christ. 📖

I serve the faith of God's chosen people and their knowledge of truth that leads to godliness.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

Godliness means living in devotion to God.

## Titus 1:2

This rests on hope of life that lasts for ever. 📖

God, who cannot lie, promised this before time began.

## Titus 1:3

At his own time, God made his word known through the message entrusted to me. 📖

God our Saviour commanded me to announce it.

## Titus 1:4

I write to Titus, my true child in the faith we share. 📖

May God the Father and Christ Jesus our Saviour give you kindness and peace.

**Help with a word:** A Saviour rescues people.

## Titus 1:5

I left you in Crete to put unfinished matters in order and appoint elders in every town. 📖

This is what I directed you to do.

**Help with a word:** Elders are leaders who care for a church.

## Titus 1:6

An elder must be without blame, the husband of one wife, with believing children. 📖

His children must not be open to accusations of reckless behaviour or rebellion.

## Titus 1:7

An overseer manages God's household and must be above blame. 📖

He must not be self-willed, quick-tempered, drunken, violent, or greedy for money.

**Help with a word:** An overseer cares for and leads a church.

## Titus 1:8

Instead, he must welcome guests, love what is good, and be self-controlled, upright, holy, and disciplined. 📖

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Titus 1:9

He must hold firmly to the reliable message as he was taught. 📖

Then he can encourage others with reliable teaching and show why those opposing it are wrong.

## Titus 1:10

Many people are rebellious, speaking empty words and deceiving others, especially those of the circumcision. 📖

**Help with a name:** The circumcision refers to the Jewish group discussed here.

Circumcision removes the foreskin, the skin covering the end of the penis.

## Titus 1:11

They must be silenced. 📖

For dishonest gain, they ruin whole households and teach things they should not.

## Titus 1:12

One of their own prophets said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, and lazy gluttons.” 📖

**Help with these words:** Cretans are people from Crete.

Gluttons are people who eat greedily.

Paul quotes these harsh words in his warning about the teachers.

## Titus 1:13

This testimony is true. 📖

So correct them firmly, so they will be sound in the faith.

## Titus 1:14

They must stop listening to Jewish myths and commands from people who have rejected the truth. 📖

## Titus 1:15

To the pure, everything is pure. 📖

But to the defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure.

Their minds and consciences are both defiled.

**Help with a word:** Defiled here describes being spoiled by wrongdoing.

## Titus 1:16

They claim to know God, but deny him by their actions. 📖

They are detestable, disobedient, and unfit for any good work.
