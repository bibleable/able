# Titus 2 — A life that fits the teaching

**About this chapter**

Paul gives instructions to older and younger people and to slaves.

He describes the life God's kindness teaches believers to live.

## Titus 2:1

But you must speak what agrees with reliable teaching. 📖

## Titus 2:2

Older men must be steady, worthy of respect, self-controlled, and sound in faith, love, and endurance. 📖

**Help with a word:** Endurance means continuing through difficulty without giving up.

## Titus 2:3

Likewise, older women must behave reverently, not tell harmful lies or be addicted to much wine. 📖

They must teach what is good.

## Titus 2:4

Then they can train younger women to love their husbands and children. 📖

## Titus 2:5

They should be self-controlled, pure, good household managers, kind, and submissive to their own husbands. 📖

Then God's word will not be spoken against.

**Help with a word:** Submissive means accepting another person's authority.

## Titus 2:6

In the same way, urge younger men to be self-controlled. 📖

## Titus 2:7

In everything, be an example by doing good works. 📖

Teach with honesty and dignity.

## Titus 2:8

Use helpful speech that is above blame. 📖

Then opponents will be ashamed, with nothing bad to say about us.

## Titus 2:9

Slaves should submit to their own masters in everything. 📖

They should try to please them and not argue.

## Titus 2:10

They must not steal, but show complete trustworthiness. 📖

In every way, they should make the teaching about God our Saviour attractive.

## Titus 2:11

God's kindness has appeared, bringing salvation to everyone. 📖

**Help with a word:** Salvation means being saved by God.

## Titus 2:12

It teaches us to reject ungodliness and worldly desires. 📖

It teaches us to live sensibly, rightly, and with devotion to God in this present age.

## Titus 2:13

We wait for the blessed hope and glorious appearing of our great God and Saviour, Jesus Christ. 📖

## Titus 2:14

He gave himself to free us from all lawlessness. 📖

He did this to purify a people for himself, his own people eager to do good works.

## Titus 2:15

Speak these things, encouraging and correcting with full authority. 📖

Let no one look down on you.
