# Titus 3 — Saved by mercy and ready to do good

**About this chapter**

Paul reminds believers how God saved them.

He urges good conduct and gives final instructions and greetings.

## Titus 3:1

Remind believers to submit to rulers and authorities, obey, and be ready for every good work. 📖

## Titus 3:2

They must speak maliciously about no one. 📖

They must be peaceable and gentle, showing full consideration to everyone.

## Titus 3:3

We too were once foolish, disobedient, misled, and slaves to many desires and pleasures. 📖

We lived in malice and envy, hated by others and hating each other.

**Help with a word:** Malice means wanting to harm someone.

## Titus 3:4

But the kindness of God our Saviour and his love for humanity appeared. 📖

## Titus 3:5

He saved us because of his mercy, rather than righteous actions we had done. 📖

He saved us through the washing of new birth and renewal by the Holy Spirit.

**Help with these words:** New birth and renewal describe receiving new life from God.

## Titus 3:6

God poured this Spirit richly on us through Jesus Christ our Saviour. 📖

## Titus 3:7

Made right by his kindness, we become heirs with the hope of life that lasts for ever. 📖

**Help with a word:** Heirs receive an inheritance as family members.

## Titus 3:8

This saying is trustworthy. 📖

I want you to stress these things so those believing God will devote themselves carefully to good works.

These things are excellent and helpful to people.

## Titus 3:9

Avoid foolish disputes, family histories, arguments, and quarrels about the law. 📖

They are pointless and worthless.

## Titus 3:10

Reject a person who causes divisions after warning them once and then a second time. 📖

## Titus 3:11

You know such a person is corrupt and sinful, condemned by their own conduct. 📖

## Titus 3:12

When I send Artemas or Tychicus, do your best to come to me at Nicopolis. 📖

I have decided to spend the winter there.

## Titus 3:13

Do your best to equip Zenas the lawyer and Apollos with everything they need. 📖

## Titus 3:14

Our people must learn to devote themselves to good works that meet others' urgent needs. 📖

Then they will not be unfruitful.

**Help with this picture:** Fruit pictures useful results from their lives.

## Titus 3:15

Everyone with me sends greetings. 📖

Greet those who love us in the faith.

May God's kindness be with you all.
