# Amos 4 — Israel refuses to return to God

**About this chapter**

Amos warns Israel about cruelty, false worship, and coming judgment.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Amos 4:1

Listen, you women on Mount Samaria, whom Amos calls cows of Bashan. 📖

You treat poor people cruelly and crush those in need.

You tell your husbands to bring more drink.

**Help with this picture:** Amos compares these wealthy women to well-fed cattle.

## Amos 4:2

The Lord GOD swears by his holiness. 📖

“The time is coming when people will drag you away with hooks.

Your descendants will be taken with fishhooks.”

**Help with this picture:** Hooks picture being helplessly dragged into captivity.

## Amos 4:3

The LORD says, “You will each go straight out through broken walls. 📖

You will be thrown out towards Harmon.”

**Help with a name:** Harmon is a place name whose exact location is uncertain.

## Amos 4:4

“Go to Bethel and sin! 📖

Go to Gilgal and rebel even more!

Bring sacrifices every morning and tithes every 3 days.”

**Help with these words:** God is mocking their false worship, not telling them to sin.

Tithes are one-tenth portions given as offerings.

## Amos 4:5

The Lord GOD says, “Offer bread made with yeast as a thank offering. 📖

Announce your freely chosen gifts loudly.

That is what you Israelites love doing.”

## Amos 4:6

The LORD says, “I gave all your cities clean teeth and all your towns a lack of bread. 📖

Still you did not return to me.”

**Help with “clean teeth”:** This pictures people having no food to eat.

## Amos 4:7

“I held back rain 3 months before harvest. 📖

I sent rain to one city but not another.

One field received rain while another dried up.”

## Amos 4:8

The LORD says, “People staggered between cities searching for drinking water. 📖

They could not find enough.

Still you did not return to me.”

## Amos 4:9

The LORD says, “I struck your growing gardens and vineyards with blight and mildew. 📖

Locusts ate your fig and olive trees.

Still you did not return to me.”

**Help with these words:** Blight and mildew are diseases that damage plants.

Locusts are insects that can destroy crops in large groups.

## Amos 4:10

The LORD says, “I sent plagues among you like those in Egypt. 📖

I killed your young men with the sword, along with your captured horses.

I filled your nostrils with your camp’s terrible smell.

Still you did not return to me.”

## Amos 4:11

The LORD says, “I destroyed some of you as I destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. 📖

You were like a burning stick pulled from a fire.

Still you did not return to me.”

**Help with this picture:** Some survived, barely escaping destruction.

## Amos 4:12

“Israel, I will do this to you. 📖

Because I will act against you, prepare to meet your God!”

## Amos 4:13

He forms mountains and creates wind. 📖

He makes his thoughts known to people.

He turns dawn into darkness and walks on earth's high places.

His name is the LORD, God of armies.
