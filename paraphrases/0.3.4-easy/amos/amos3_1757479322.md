# Amos 3 — The LORD speaks against Israel

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks against the people He brought from Egypt.

Questions show the connection between events and their causes.

The chapter announces judgment against Samaria and Israel’s false security.

## Amos 3:1

People of Israel, hear the word the LORD has spoken against you. 📖

It is against the whole family I brought out of Egypt.

## Amos 3:2

God says, “Of all the earth’s families, I have known only you. 📖

So I will punish you for all your sins.”

## Amos 3:3

Can two people walk together without agreeing where to go? 📖

## Amos 3:4

Does a lion roar in the forest when he has no prey? 📖

Does a young lion growl in his den if he has caught nothing?

## Amos 3:5

Does a bird land in a trap where no bait has been set? 📖

Does a trap spring from the ground when it has nothing to catch?

## Amos 3:6

If a ram’s horn sounds in a city, do the people not tremble? 📖

If disaster comes to a city, has the LORD not caused it?

**Help with “ram’s horn”:** This is used as a trumpet to sound an alarm.

## Amos 3:7

The Lord GOD certainly does nothing without revealing His plan to His servants the prophets. 📖

## Amos 3:8

The lion has roared. 📖

Who will not fear?

The Lord GOD has spoken.

Who will not speak His message?

## Amos 3:9

Announce this to the fortresses of Ashdod and Egypt. 📖

Say, “Gather on Samaria’s mountains.

See the city’s great disorder and the oppression within it.”

**Help with “oppression”:** This means treating people cruelly and unfairly.

## Amos 3:10

The LORD declares, “They do not know how to do right. 📖

They store up violence and destruction in their fortresses.”

## Amos 3:11

So the Lord GOD says, “An enemy will surround the land. 📖

He will pull down your defended places and rob your fortresses.”

## Amos 3:12

The LORD says, “A shepherd snatches two legs or a piece of an ear from a lion’s mouth. 📖

In the same way, Israelites living in Samaria will be rescued with only a bed’s corner or a couch’s cushion.

**About this comparison:** The image describes very little left after destruction.

## Amos 3:13

Hear and speak as witnesses against Jacob’s people, declares the Lord GOD, the God of Hosts. 📖

**Help with “God of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Amos 3:14

On the day I punish Israel’s sins, I will destroy Bethel’s altars. 📖

The altar’s horns will be cut off and fall to the ground.

**Help with “horns”:** These are raised points on the altar.

## Amos 3:15

I will tear down the winter house and the summer house. 📖

The ivory houses will also perish, and the great houses will end, declares the LORD.”

**Help with “ivory”:** This is a hard material from animal tusks.
