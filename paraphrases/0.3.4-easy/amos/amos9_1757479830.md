# Amos 9 — Judgment and restoration

**About this chapter**

Amos sees the Lord beside the altar.

The LORD announces judgment that no hiding place can prevent.

He also promises to preserve Jacob’s people and restore them to their land.

## Amos 9:1

I saw the Lord standing beside the altar. 📖

He said, “Strike the pillars’ tops so the entrance floors shake.

Knock them onto all the people’s heads.

I will kill the rest with the sword.

None who flee will get away.

No fugitive will escape.

## Amos 9:2

Even if they dig down to Sheol, My hand will take them from there. 📖

Even if they climb to heaven, I will pull them down.

**Help with “Sheol”:** This is the place of the dead.

## Amos 9:3

Even if they hide on Carmel’s summit, I will find and seize them there. 📖

Even if they hide from Me at the sea’s bottom, I will order the serpent to bite them there.

## Amos 9:4

Even if their enemies take them into captivity, I will order the sword to kill them there. 📖

I will fix My eyes on them for harm rather than good.”

## Amos 9:5

The Lord GOD of Hosts touches the earth, and it melts. 📖

All its people mourn.

All the land rises like the Nile and then sinks like Egypt’s river.

**Help with “God of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Amos 9:6

He builds His upper rooms in heaven and sets His vault on the earth. 📖

He calls for the sea’s waters and pours them over the earth.

His name is the LORD.

**Help with “vault”:** This is an arched covering.

The picture describes God’s dwelling and power over the world.

## Amos 9:7

The LORD asks, “People of Israel, are you not like the Cushites to Me? 📖

Did I not bring Israel out of Egypt, the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Arameans from Kir?

## Amos 9:8

The Lord GOD’s eyes are on the sinful kingdom. 📖

I will destroy it from the earth.

Yet I will not completely destroy Jacob’s people, declares the LORD.

## Amos 9:9

I will certainly give the command. 📖

I will shake Israel’s people among all the nations like grain shaken in a sieve.

But not a pebble will reach the ground.

**Help with “sieve”:** This tool separates materials by shaking them through small holes.

The draft keeps BSB’s word “pebble.”

## Amos 9:10

All sinners among My people will die by the sword. 📖

These are those who say, ‘Disaster will never come near us or confront us.’

## Amos 9:11

On that day, I will restore David’s fallen tent. 📖

I will repair its gaps, restore its ruins, and rebuild it as in earlier days.

## Amos 9:12

Then they may possess Edom’s survivors and all the nations bearing My name. 📖

This is what the LORD declares.

He will do this.

## Amos 9:13

The days are coming, declares the LORD. 📖

The plowman will overtake the harvester, and the grape-treader will overtake the seed-sower.

The mountains will drip with sweet wine, and all the hills will flow with it.

**Help with the picture:** The harvests are pictured as so plentiful that the work overlaps.

## Amos 9:14

I will bring My people Israel back from captivity. 📖

They will rebuild ruined cities and live in them.

They will plant vineyards and drink their wine.

They will make gardens and eat their fruit.

## Amos 9:15

I will plant them firmly in their own land. 📖

They will never again be uprooted from the land I have given them, says the LORD your God.”
