# Amos 5 — Seek the LORD and live

**About this chapter**

Amos sings a song of grief for Israel.

God calls the people to seek Him, love good, and establish justice.

He rejects their worship and warns that His day will bring darkness.

## Amos 5:1

People of Israel, hear this word, this song of grief I sing against you. 📖

## Amos 5:2

Virgin Israel has fallen, never to rise again. 📖

She lies abandoned on her land, with no one to lift her.

**About “Virgin Israel”:** The nation is pictured as a young woman.

## Amos 5:3

The Lord GOD says, “An Israelite city marching out with a thousand will have only a hundred left. 📖

One marching out with a hundred will have only ten left.”

## Amos 5:4

The LORD tells Israel’s people, “Seek Me and live! 📖

## Amos 5:5

Do not seek Bethel, go to Gilgal, or travel to Beersheba. 📖

Gilgal will certainly be forced into exile, and Bethel will come to nothing.

## Amos 5:6

Seek the LORD and live. 📖

Otherwise, He will sweep like fire through Joseph’s people and burn everything.

No one at Bethel will put it out.

## Amos 5:7

Some turn justice into bitter wormwood and throw righteousness to the ground. 📖

**Help with these words:** Wormwood is a bitter plant.

Righteousness means what is right and just.

The pictures describe corrupting justice.

## Amos 5:8

He made the Pleiades and Orion. 📖

He turns darkness into dawn and darkens day into night.

He calls for the sea’s waters and pours them over the earth.

His name is the LORD.

**Help with “Pleiades” and “Orion”:** These are names for groups of stars.

## Amos 5:9

He flashes destruction on the strong, bringing fury against their defended place. 📖

## Amos 5:10

Some hate the person who corrects wrongdoing at the gate. 📖

They despise anyone who speaks honestly.

**About “the gate”:** Public judgments were made at the city gate.

## Amos 5:11

You trample poor people and demand a grain tax from them. 📖

So you will never live in the stone houses you built.

You will never drink wine from the rich vineyards you planted.

## Amos 5:12

I know your many wrong actions and numerous sins. 📖

You oppress righteous people by taking bribes and deny poor people justice at the gate.

**Help with “bribes”:** These are payments given to make someone do something wrong.

## Amos 5:13

So the careful person keeps silent in such times, because the days are evil. 📖

## Amos 5:14

Seek good rather than evil, so you may live. 📖

Then the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be with you, as you have claimed.

**Help with “God of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Amos 5:15

Hate evil and love good. 📖

Establish justice at the gate.

Perhaps the LORD, the God of Hosts, will show kindness to Joseph’s survivors.”

## Amos 5:16

So the LORD, the God of Hosts, the Lord, says this. 📖

There will be wailing in all public squares and cries of ‘Alas!

Alas!’ in all the streets.

Farmers will be called to mourn, and mourners to wail.

## Amos 5:17

The LORD says, “There will be wailing in all the vineyards, because I will pass among you.” 📖

## Amos 5:18

How terrible for you who long for the Day of the LORD! 📖

What will that day mean for you?

It will be darkness rather than light.

**Help with “Day of the LORD”:** Here this is the time of God’s judgment.

## Amos 5:19

It will be like someone escaping a lion, only to meet a bear. 📖

Or someone enters his house and rests his hand on the wall, only to be bitten by a snake.

## Amos 5:20

Will the Day of the LORD not be darkness rather than light, gloom without any brightness? 📖

## Amos 5:21

God says, “I hate and despise your feasts! 📖

I cannot stand the smell of your solemn gatherings.

## Amos 5:22

Though you offer Me burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. 📖

I will give no regard to your peace offerings of well-fed cattle.

**Help with the offerings:** These are sacrifices given to God in worship.

## Amos 5:23

Take away the noise of your songs! 📖

I will not listen to your harps’ music.

## Amos 5:24

But let justice flow like a river and righteousness like a stream that never stops. 📖

## Amos 5:25

People of Israel, did you bring Me sacrifices and offerings for forty years in the wilderness? 📖

## Amos 5:26

You carried Sakkuth your king and Kaiwan your star god, the idols you made for yourselves. 📖

## Amos 5:27

So I will send you into exile beyond Damascus, says the LORD, whose name is the God of Hosts.” 📖
