# Amos 8 — The basket of fruit and the coming end

**About this chapter**

Amos sees a basket of summer fruit.

God announces the end for Israel and condemns dishonest trade and exploitation.

A coming famine will be a lack of hearing the LORD’s words.

## Amos 8:1

The Lord GOD showed me a basket of summer fruit. 📖

## Amos 8:2

He asked, “Amos, what do you see?” 📖

I answered, “A basket of summer fruit.”

The LORD told me, “The end has come for My people Israel.

I will no longer spare them.”

## Amos 8:3

The Lord GOD declares, “On that day, the temple’s songs will turn to wailing. 📖

Many dead bodies will lie everywhere in silence!”

## Amos 8:4

Hear this, you who trample needy people and destroy the land’s poor people. 📖

## Amos 8:5

You ask, “When will the New Moon end, so we can sell grain? 📖

When will the Sabbath end, so we can sell wheat?

Let us make the ephah smaller and the shekel larger.

Let us cheat with dishonest scales.

**Help with these words:** The New Moon marks the beginning of a month.

The Sabbath is the special rest day.

An ephah measures grain.

A shekel is a weight.

They want to give less grain and demand more payment.

## Amos 8:6

Let us buy poor people for silver and needy people for a pair of sandals. 📖

Let us even sell the chaff with the wheat!”

**Help with “chaff”:** This is the waste separated from grain.

## Amos 8:7

The LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob, “I will never forget any of their deeds. 📖

## Amos 8:8

Will the land not shake because of this, and all its people mourn? 📖

All of it will swell like the Nile.

It will rise and then sink like the Nile in Egypt.

## Amos 8:9

On that day, declares the Lord GOD, I will make the sun set at noon. 📖

I will darken the earth during daylight.

## Amos 8:10

I will turn your feasts into mourning and all your songs into songs of grief. 📖

I will make everyone wear sackcloth and every head be shaved.

I will make it like mourning for an only son, ending like a bitter day.

**Help with “sackcloth”:** This is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Amos 8:11

Look! 📖

The days are coming, declares the Lord GOD, when I will send a famine on the land.

It will not be a lack of bread or thirst for water.

It will be a lack of hearing the LORD’s words.

## Amos 8:12

People will stagger from sea to sea and wander from north to east. 📖

They will seek the LORD’s word but will not find it.

## Amos 8:13

On that day, the beautiful young women and the young men will faint from thirst. 📖

## Amos 8:14

Some swear by Samaria’s guilt and say, ‘As surely as your god lives, Dan.’ 📖

Others say, ‘As surely as the way of Beersheba lives.’

They will fall, never to rise again.”
