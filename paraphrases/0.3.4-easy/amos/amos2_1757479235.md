# Amos 2 — Judgment against Moab, Judah, and Israel

**About this chapter**

The LORD announces judgment against Moab, Judah, and Israel.

He condemns violence, rejected law, exploitation, and false worship.

He recalls His help and describes the failure of every means of escape.

## Amos 2:1

The LORD says, “For three sins of Moab, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

He burned Edom’s king’s bones into lime.

**Help with “lime”:** This is the powdery material left by this burning.

## Amos 2:2

So I will send fire against Moab to burn up Kerioth’s fortresses. 📖

Moab will die in disorder, with battle cries and the ram’s horn sounding.

**Help with “ram’s horn”:** This is a male sheep’s horn used as a trumpet.

## Amos 2:3

I will cut off Moab’s ruler and kill all the officials with him, says the LORD. 📖

## Amos 2:4

The LORD says, “For three sins of Judah, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

They reject the LORD’s Law and do not keep His rules.

The lies their ancestors followed lead them astray.

## Amos 2:5

So I will send fire on Judah to burn up Jerusalem’s fortresses.” 📖

## Amos 2:6

The LORD says, “For three sins of Israel, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

They sell righteous people for silver and needy people for a pair of sandals.

**Help with “righteous”:** This means those who do what is right.

## Amos 2:7

They trample poor people’s heads like dust on the earth and push needy people out of their way. 📖

A man and his father have sex with the same girl.

In this way, they make My holy name unholy.

## Amos 2:8

Beside every altar, they lie on clothes taken as a pledge for payment. 📖

In their God’s house, they drink wine gained through fines.

**Help with “pledge”:** This is something held until a debt is paid.

## Amos 2:9

Yet I destroyed the Amorite before them. 📖

He was tall as cedars and strong as oaks.

But I destroyed his fruit above and roots below.

**Help with the tree picture:** It describes complete destruction.

## Amos 2:10

I brought you out of Egypt and led you forty years in the wilderness. 📖

Then you could take the Amorite’s land.

## Amos 2:11

I raised prophets from your sons and Nazirites from your young men. 📖

Is this not true, people of Israel?

This is what the LORD declares.

**Help with these words:** Prophets speak God’s messages.

Nazirites are people set apart for God by special promises.

## Amos 2:12

God says, “But you made the Nazirites drink wine and ordered the prophets not to speak God’s messages. 📖

## Amos 2:13

Look! 📖

I am about to crush you where you are, like a cart full of grain.

## Amos 2:14

The swift will not escape. 📖

The strong will not succeed by their strength, and the mighty will not save their lives.

## Amos 2:15

The archer will not stand his ground. 📖

The fast runner will not escape, and the horseman will not save his life.

## Amos 2:16

Even the bravest mighty men will flee naked on that day, declares the LORD.” 📖
