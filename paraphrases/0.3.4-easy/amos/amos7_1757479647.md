# Amos 7 — Three visions and a priest’s opposition

**About this chapter**

Amos sees locusts, fire, and a plumb line.

He pleads for Jacob, and God changes two announced plans.

Amaziah opposes Amos, who answers with the LORD’s message.

## Amos 7:1

The Lord GOD showed me this. 📖

He was preparing swarms of locusts just after the king’s harvest, as the late spring crop grew.

**Help with “locusts”:** These insects can gather in large numbers and eat crops.

## Amos 7:2

When the locusts had eaten every green plant in the land, I spoke. 📖

I said, “Lord GOD, please forgive!

How can Jacob survive?

He is so small!”

## Amos 7:3

The LORD changed this plan. 📖

He said, “It will not happen.”

## Amos 7:4

The Lord GOD showed me this. 📖

He called for judgment by fire.

It burned up the great deep and ate up the land.

## Amos 7:5

I said, “Lord GOD, please stop! 📖

How can Jacob survive?

He is so small!”

## Amos 7:6

The LORD changed this plan too. 📖

The Lord GOD said, “This will not happen either.”

## Amos 7:7

He showed me the Lord standing beside a wall that was straight and upright. 📖

A plumb line was in His hand.

**Help with “plumb line”:** This is a cord with a weight, used to check whether a wall is upright.

## Amos 7:8

The LORD asked, “Amos, what do you see?” 📖

I answered, “A plumb line.”

The Lord said, “Look!

I am setting a plumb line among My people Israel.

I will no longer spare them.

## Amos 7:9

Isaac’s high places of worship will be deserted, and Israel’s holy places will be ruined. 📖

I will rise against Jeroboam’s family with My sword.”

## Amos 7:10

Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent a message to Jeroboam, king of Israel. 📖

He said, “Amos has plotted against you among Israel’s people.

The land cannot bear all his words.

## Amos 7:11

Amos has said, ‘Jeroboam will die by the sword. 📖

Israel will certainly be forced into exile, away from their homeland.’”

## Amos 7:12

Amaziah told Amos, “Go away, you seer! 📖

Flee to Judah.

Earn your bread and speak your prophecies there.

**Help with “seer”:** This is someone who receives visions or messages.

## Amos 7:13

Never speak prophecies at Bethel again. 📖

It is the king’s holy place and the kingdom’s temple.”

## Amos 7:14

Amos replied, “I was not a prophet or the son of a prophet. 📖

I was a herdsman who also cared for sycamore-fig trees.

## Amos 7:15

But the LORD took me from following the flock. 📖

He told me, ‘Go and speak My message to My people Israel.’

## Amos 7:16

Now hear the LORD’s word. 📖

You say, ‘Do not speak prophecies against Israel or preach against Isaac’s people.’

## Amos 7:17

So the LORD says this. 📖

Your wife will become a prostitute in the city, and your sons and daughters will fall by the sword.

Your land will be divided with a measuring line.

You yourself will die on pagan soil.

Israel will certainly be forced into exile, away from their homeland.”

**Help with these words:** A prostitute provides sexual acts in exchange for payment or other benefits.

Pagan soil means land where other gods are worshiped.
