# Amos 1 — Judgment against the neighboring nations

**About this chapter**

Amos records what he saw concerning Israel.

The LORD announces judgment against neighboring nations for violence, exile, and betrayal.

The repeated words about three sins, even four, are kept.

## Amos 1:1

These are the words of Amos, one of the shepherds of Tekoa. 📖

He saw these things concerning Israel two years before the earthquake.

Uzziah was king of Judah, and Jeroboam, son of Jehoash, was king of Israel.

## Amos 1:2

Amos said, “The LORD roars from Zion and raises His voice from Jerusalem. 📖

The shepherds’ pastures mourn, and Carmel’s summit dries up.”

## Amos 1:3

The LORD says, “For three sins of Damascus, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

They threshed Gilead with iron sledges.

**Help with the picture:** Threshing sledges were heavy tools used to separate grain.

The image describes crushing violence against Gilead.

## Amos 1:4

So I will send fire on Hazael’s house to burn up Ben-hadad’s fortresses. 📖

## Amos 1:5

I will break Damascus’s gates and cut off the ruler from the Valley of Aven. 📖

I will cut off the one holding the royal staff in Beth-eden.

Aram’s people will be forced away to Kir, says the LORD.

**Help with “royal staff”:** BSB calls this a “scepter.”

It is a sign of a ruler’s authority.

## Amos 1:6

The LORD says, “For three sins of Gaza, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

They forced a whole population away and handed them over to Edom.

## Amos 1:7

So I will send fire on Gaza’s walls to burn up its fortresses. 📖

## Amos 1:8

I will cut off Ashdod’s ruler and the one holding the royal staff in Ashkelon. 📖

I will turn My hand against Ekron.

The remaining Philistines will die, says the Lord GOD.

## Amos 1:9

The LORD says, “For three sins of Tyre, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

They handed a whole group of exiles over to Edom and broke a covenant of brotherhood.

**Help with these words:** Exiles are people forced away from their land.

A covenant of brotherhood is a binding agreement of close loyalty.

## Amos 1:10

So I will send fire on Tyre’s walls to burn up its fortresses.” 📖

## Amos 1:11

The LORD says, “For three sins of Edom, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

He chased his brother with a sword and suppressed all compassion.

His anger raged continually, and his fury burned without stopping.

## Amos 1:12

So I will send fire on Teman to burn up Bozrah’s fortresses.” 📖

## Amos 1:13

The LORD says, “For three sins of the Ammonites, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. 📖

They ripped open Gilead’s pregnant women to enlarge their territory.

## Amos 1:14

So I will light a fire in Rabbah’s walls to burn up its fortresses. 📖

There will be battle cries on the day of war and violent wind on the day of storm.

## Amos 1:15

Their king and princes will be forced away together, says the LORD. 📖
