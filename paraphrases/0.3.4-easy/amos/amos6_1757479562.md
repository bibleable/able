# Amos 6 — Luxury and false security

**About this chapter**

Amos warns people who feel safe while others suffer.

God announces exile, death, and the destruction of houses.

The chapter keeps its disturbing scene of removing bodies and its warning against pride.

## Amos 6:1

How terrible for those at ease in Zion and feeling safe on Mount Samaria! 📖

They are the leading people of the foremost nation, to whom Israel’s people come.

## Amos 6:2

Cross to Calneh and look. 📖

Go from there to great Hamath, then down to Gath of the Philistines.

Are you better than those kingdoms?

Is their territory larger than yours?

## Amos 6:3

You push the day of disaster out of mind and bring a rule of violence near. 📖

## Amos 6:4

You lie on beds decorated with ivory and stretch out on couches. 📖

You eat lambs from the flock and calves from the stall.

**Help with “ivory”:** This is a hard material from animal tusks.

## Amos 6:5

You make up songs on the harp like David and invent your own musical instruments. 📖

## Amos 6:6

You drink wine by the bowlful and put the finest oils on yourselves. 📖

But you do not grieve over Joseph’s ruin.

## Amos 6:7

So you will now be the first captives taken into exile. 📖

Your feasting and lounging will end.

## Amos 6:8

The Lord GOD has sworn by Himself. 📖

The LORD, the God of Hosts, declares, “I hate Jacob’s pride and despise his fortresses.

I will hand over the city and everything in it.”

**Help with “God of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Amos 6:9

If ten men remain in one house, they too will die. 📖

## Amos 6:10

A relative responsible for burning the bodies will pick them up to carry them from the house. 📖

He will call to someone inside, “Is anyone else with you?”

That person will answer, “No one.”

The relative will say, “Quiet!

The LORD’s name must not be called on.”

## Amos 6:11

The LORD commands, “The great house will be smashed to pieces and the small house to rubble. 📖

## Amos 6:12

Do horses gallop on cliffs? 📖

Does anyone plow the sea with oxen?

But you have turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness into bitter wormwood.

**Help with “wormwood”:** This is a bitter plant.

The picture describes corrupting what should be right and just.

## Amos 6:13

You celebrate Lo-debar and say, ‘Did we not capture Karnaim by our own strength?’ 📖

## Amos 6:14

Look, people of Israel! 📖

I will raise a nation against you, declares the LORD, the God of Hosts.

They will oppress you from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of the Arabah.”
