# Habakkuk 3 — Habakkuk’s prayer

**About this chapter**

Habakkuk prays about the LORD’s power, mercy, and rescue.

He speaks of rejoicing in God even when food and animals are gone.

## Habakkuk 3:1

This is a prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to Shigionoth. 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet receives messages from God.

**About Shigionoth:** This word gives a direction connected with the prayer’s musical form.

Its precise meaning is uncertain.

## Habakkuk 3:2

Habakkuk prays, “LORD, I have heard the report about you. 📖

LORD, your deeds fill me with awe.

Do these deeds again in these years.

Make them known in these years.

In your anger, remember mercy!

**Help with awe:** Awe means wonder and deep respect.

**Help with mercy:** Mercy means compassion and a willingness to spare people.

## Habakkuk 3:3

God came from Teman, and the Holy One came from Mount Paran. 📖

Selah.

His glory covered the heavens, and his praise filled the earth.

**Help with Holy One:** This is a title for God.

**Help with glory:** Glory means greatness and honour.

**About Selah:** Selah is a word used in songs.

Its precise meaning is uncertain.

## Habakkuk 3:4

God’s brightness was like sunlight. 📖

Rays flashed from his hand, where his power is hidden.

## Habakkuk 3:5

Plague went before God, and fever followed his steps. 📖

**Help with plague:** A plague is a deadly disease that spreads among people.

## Habakkuk 3:6

God stood and measured the earth. 📖

God looked, and the nations were startled.

The ancient mountains crumbled, and the lasting hills fell down.

His ways last forever.

## Habakkuk 3:7

I saw the tents of Cushan in distress. 📖

The tent curtains of Midian trembled.

## Habakkuk 3:8

LORD, were you angry with the rivers? 📖

Was your anger against the streams?

Were you raging at the sea when you rode your horses and your chariots of salvation?

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

**Help with salvation:** Salvation means being saved.

The chariots here picture God coming to save.

## Habakkuk 3:9

You held your bow ready and called for many arrows. 📖

Selah.

You split the earth with rivers.

**Help with bow:** A bow is a weapon for shooting arrows.

**About Selah:** Selah is a word used in songs.

Its precise meaning is uncertain.

## Habakkuk 3:10

The mountains saw you and shook. 📖

Rushing floods swept past.

The deep water roared with its voice and lifted its hands high.

**Help with this picture:** The deep water is described like a person reacting to God.

## Habakkuk 3:11

The sun and moon stood still in their places. 📖

Your flying arrows flashed, and your shining spear blazed bright.

## Habakkuk 3:12

You marched across the earth in fury. 📖

In anger, you threshed the nations.

**Help with threshed:** Threshing separates grain from its covering by beating or crushing it.

Here, it pictures judgment against nations.

## Habakkuk 3:13

You went out to save your people, to save your anointed one. 📖

You crushed the head of the wicked man’s house and stripped him from head to toe.

Selah.

**Help with anointed one:** This is someone chosen and set apart for a role.

**About the head and house:** This difficult image is retained for further interpretation.

**About Selah:** Selah is a word used in songs.

Its precise meaning is uncertain.

## Habakkuk 3:14

You pierced his head with his own spear. 📖

His warriors rushed out to scatter us.

They gloated as though ready to eat up weak people in secret.

**Help with eat up:** This pictures destroying people.

**Help with gloated:** Gloated means enjoyed another person’s suffering.

## Habakkuk 3:15

You trampled the sea with your horses. 📖

You churned up the great waters.

## Habakkuk 3:16

I heard, and I trembled inside. 📖

My lips shook at the sound.

Decay entered my bones, and I trembled where I stood.

Yet I must wait patiently for the day of trouble to come on those who invade us.

**Help with decay in my bones:** This is a strong picture of the speaker’s weakness and fear.

## Habakkuk 3:17

The fig tree may have no buds, and the vines may have no fruit. 📖

The olive crop may fail, and the fields may produce no food.

The sheep may be gone from the pen, and the stalls may have no cattle.

## Habakkuk 3:18

Even then, I will rejoice greatly in the LORD. 📖

I will be glad in the God of my salvation!

**Help with salvation:** Salvation means being saved.

## Habakkuk 3:19

GOD the Lord is my strength. 📖

He makes my feet like a deer’s feet.

He makes me walk on the heights!”

For the music leader.

With stringed instruments.

**Help with deer’s feet:** This pictures steady steps in high places.

**About the final directions:** The words about the music leader and instruments give instructions for performing the prayer.
