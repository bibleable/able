# Habakkuk 2 — The LORD answers Habakkuk

**About this chapter**

Habakkuk waits for the LORD’s answer.

The LORD’s message speaks of faith, pride, violence, exploitation, and idols.

## Habakkuk 2:1

Habakkuk says, “I will stand at my guard post on the walls. 📖

I will watch to see what God says to me.

I will see how I should answer when corrected.”

## Habakkuk 2:2

The LORD answered me, “Write down this vision clearly on tablets. 📖

Then a messenger may run with it.

**Help with vision:** A vision is something God shows a person.

**Help with tablets:** Here, tablets are flat surfaces used for writing.

## Habakkuk 2:3

The vision waits for its set time. 📖

It speaks of the end and does not lie.

Though it seems slow, wait for it.

It will certainly come and will not delay.

**Help with vision:** This means the message God has shown Habakkuk.

## Habakkuk 2:4

Look at the proud person. 📖

His soul is not right.

But the righteous will live by faith.

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means right before God.

**Help with faith:** Faith means trust.

The relationship between faith and faithfulness here needs further editorial discussion.

## Habakkuk 2:5

Wealth betrays the proud man. 📖

He is never at rest.

He makes his appetite as wide as Sheol.

Like Death, he is never satisfied.

He gathers all nations and collects all peoples for himself.

**Help with Sheol:** Sheol refers to the place of the dead.

The picture compares the man’s endless desire to the way death keeps taking lives.

## Habakkuk 2:6

Will all these peoples not mock him with scorn? 📖

They will say, ‘Woe to the man who piles up what is not his!

He makes himself rich with many loans.

How long will this go on?’

**Help with woe:** Woe announces disaster or judgment.

## Habakkuk 2:7

Will your creditors not suddenly rise? 📖

Will those who trouble you not wake up?

Then you will become their prey.

**Help with creditors:** Creditors are people to whom money is owed.

**Help with prey:** This pictures a person being caught and taken from.

## Habakkuk 2:8

You robbed many nations, so the remaining peoples will rob you. 📖

This is because you shed human blood.

You used violence against the land, the city, and everyone living in them.

## Habakkuk 2:9

Woe to the man who builds his family’s house through dishonest gain! 📖

He puts his nest high up to escape disaster’s hand.

**Help with woe:** Woe announces disaster or judgment.

**Help with nest and hand:** The high nest pictures security.

Disaster’s hand pictures disaster’s power.

## Habakkuk 2:10

You planned shame for your family’s house by cutting off many peoples. 📖

You gave up your own life by doing this.

## Habakkuk 2:11

The stones in the wall will cry out. 📖

The wooden beams will answer from the woodwork.

**Help with this picture:** Even the building materials are pictured as speaking against the wrongdoer.

## Habakkuk 2:12

Woe to the man who builds a city through bloodshed! 📖

He builds a town through wrongdoing!

**Help with woe:** Woe announces disaster or judgment.

## Habakkuk 2:13

Does this not come from the LORD of Hosts? 📖

The peoples’ work only feeds the fire.

The nations wear themselves out for nothing.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with feeds the fire:** This pictures work being destroyed.

## Habakkuk 2:14

The earth will be filled with knowledge of the LORD’s glory, as water covers the sea. 📖

**Help with glory:** Glory means greatness and honour.

## Habakkuk 2:15

Woe to the man who gives drink to his neighbours! 📖

He pours from his wineskin until they are drunk, so he can look at their nakedness.

**Help with woe:** Woe announces disaster or judgment.

**Help with wineskin:** A wineskin is a container made from animal skin.

## Habakkuk 2:16

You will be filled with shame instead of glory. 📖

You too must drink and expose your uncircumcision!

The cup in the LORD’s right hand will come around to you.

Complete disgrace will cover your glory.

**Help with uncircumcision:** Circumcision removes the foreskin, the skin covering the tip of the penis.

Here, exposure is part of the passage’s shaming imagery.

**Help with the cup:** Drinking from this cup pictures experiencing God’s judgment.

**Help with glory:** Glory means greatness and honour.

## Habakkuk 2:17

Your violence against Lebanon will overwhelm you. 📖

Your destruction of animals will terrify you.

This is because you shed human blood.

You used violence against the land, the city, and everyone living in them.

## Habakkuk 2:18

What use is an idol carved by a craftsman? 📖

What use is an image that teaches lies?

Its maker trusts in what he made.

He makes idols that cannot speak.

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## Habakkuk 2:19

Woe to the man who tells wood, ‘Wake up!’ 📖

Woe to the man who tells silent stone, ‘Get up!’

Can it give guidance?

Gold and silver cover it, but it has no breath at all.”

**Help with woe:** Woe announces disaster or judgment.

## Habakkuk 2:20

But the LORD is in his holy temple. 📖

Let all the earth be silent before him.

**Help with holy temple:** A temple is a place of worship set apart for God.
