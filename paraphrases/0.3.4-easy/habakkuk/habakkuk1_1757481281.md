# Habakkuk 1 — Habakkuk asks the LORD about violence

**About this chapter**

Habakkuk asks why the LORD allows wrongdoing.

The LORD answers about the Chaldeans, and Habakkuk asks more questions.

## Habakkuk 1:1

This is the message Habakkuk the prophet received in a vision. 📖

**Help with prophet and vision:** A prophet receives messages from God.

A vision is something God shows a person.

## Habakkuk 1:2

Habakkuk asks, “How long, LORD, must I call for help while you do not hear? 📖

How long must I cry, ‘Violence!’ while you do not save?

## Habakkuk 1:3

Why do you make me see wrongdoing? 📖

Why do you allow wrong actions?

Destruction and violence are before me.

Fighting goes on, and conflict is everywhere.

## Habakkuk 1:4

The law cannot act, and justice never goes out. 📖

Wicked people surround those who do right.

So justice is twisted.”

**Help with justice:** Justice means treating people fairly and putting wrongs right.

## Habakkuk 1:5

The LORD says, “Look at the nations and watch! 📖

Be completely amazed!

I am doing something in your days that you would not believe, even if someone told you.

## Habakkuk 1:6

I am raising up the Chaldeans, a cruel nation that acts quickly without restraint. 📖

They march across the earth and seize homes that do not belong to them.

**Help with Chaldeans:** Here, this name refers to the Babylonian people.

## Habakkuk 1:7

People dread and fear them. 📖

They decide for themselves what is just and what gives them the right to rule.

## Habakkuk 1:8

Their horses are faster than leopards and fiercer than wolves of the night. 📖

Their riders charge ahead, and their horsemen come from far away.

They fly like a vulture swooping down to eat.

**Help with vulture:** A vulture is a large bird that feeds on dead animals.

The bird pictures the attackers’ speed and hunger to destroy.

## Habakkuk 1:9

They all come wanting violence. 📖

Their great crowds advance like the east wind.

They gather prisoners like sand.

**Help with sand:** This picture describes a great number of prisoners.

## Habakkuk 1:10

They mock kings and scorn rulers. 📖

They laugh at every fortress.

They build ramps against it and capture it.

**Help with fortress and ramps:** A fortress is a strong place protected from attack.

Attackers piled up earth to make ramps to reach its walls.

## Habakkuk 1:11

They sweep past like the wind and move on. 📖

They are guilty.

Their own strength is their god.”

## Habakkuk 1:12

Habakkuk says, “LORD, my God, my Holy One, have you not existed forever? 📖

We will not die.

LORD, you have appointed them to carry out judgment.

Rock, you have put them in place to bring correction.

**Help with Holy One:** This is a title for God, who is pure and set apart.

**Help with Rock:** This pictures God’s strength and firmness.

## Habakkuk 1:13

Your eyes are too pure to look at evil. 📖

You cannot bear wrongdoing.

So why do you allow people who break faith?

Why are you silent while the wicked swallow up people more righteous than themselves?

**Help with swallow up:** This pictures destroying people.

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means doing what is right before God.

## Habakkuk 1:14

You have made people like fish in the sea. 📖

They are like crawling creatures with no ruler.

## Habakkuk 1:15

The enemy pulls them all up with a hook. 📖

He catches them in his large net and gathers them in his fishing net.

So he rejoices gladly.

**Help with this picture:** The enemy’s capture of people is compared to catching fish.

## Habakkuk 1:16

He offers sacrifices to his large net and burns incense to his fishing net. 📖

These nets give him rich portions and fine food.

**Help with sacrifices and incense:** Sacrifices are offerings to a god.

Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

Here, the enemy honours the means that make him rich.

## Habakkuk 1:17

Will he keep emptying his net and killing nations without mercy?” 📖

**Help with net:** The net continues the picture of the enemy capturing peoples.

**Help with mercy:** Mercy means compassion and a willingness to spare people.
