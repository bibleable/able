# 1 Thessalonians 3 — Timothy brings good news

**About this chapter**

Paul recalls sending Timothy to encourage the believers.

Timothy's report brings comfort, and Paul prays for them.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:1

When we could bear it no longer, we were willing to be left alone in Athens. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 3:2

We sent Timothy, our brother. 📖

He works with us for God in telling Christ's good news.

He would strengthen and encourage you in your faith.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:3

We did not want these trials to shake any of you. 📖

You know we are destined for this.

**Help with a word:** Destined means appointed for something that will happen.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:4

When we were with you, we kept warning that we would suffer persecution. 📖

As you know, it happened.

**Help with a word:** Persecution means being treated cruelly for your faith.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:5

When I could bear it no longer, I sent to find out about your faith. 📖

I feared the tempter had tempted you and our work had been wasted.

**Help with a word:** The tempter is Satan, who tries to lead people into wrongdoing.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:6

Now Timothy has returned from you with good news about your faith and love. 📖

You always remember us fondly and long to see us, just as we long to see you.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:7

Brothers and sisters, your faith has reassured us through all our distress and persecution. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 3:8

Now we can go on living, as long as you stand firm in the Lord. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 3:9

How can we thank God enough for you and the great joy you bring us before him? 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 3:10

Night and day, we pray very earnestly to see you face to face. 📖

We want to supply what is lacking in your faith.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:11

May our God and Father himself, and our Lord Jesus, guide our way to you. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 3:12

May the Lord make your love grow and overflow for each other and everyone else. 📖

This is how our love overflows for you.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:13

May he establish your hearts in holiness and without blame before our God and Father. 📖

May this be so when our Lord Jesus comes with all his holy ones.

Amen.

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.
