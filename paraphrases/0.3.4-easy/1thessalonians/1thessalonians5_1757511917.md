# 1 Thessalonians 5 — Stay ready and care for each other

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches about the day of the Lord and how believers should live together.

He ends with prayer, greetings, and a blessing.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:1

Brothers and sisters, we do not need to write about the times and seasons. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:2

You know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night. 📖

**Help with this picture:** A thief comes unexpectedly.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:3

While people say, “Peace and safety,” destruction will come suddenly, like birth pains on a pregnant woman. 📖

They will not escape.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:4

But, brothers and sisters, you are not in darkness for this day to catch you like a thief. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:5

You are all children of the light and of the day. 📖

We do not belong to night or darkness.

**Help with these pictures:** Light and day picture belonging to God and being ready.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:6

So let us stay awake and sober, rather than sleep as others do. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:7

People who sleep do so at night. 📖

People who get drunk get drunk at night.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:8

We belong to the day, so let us be sober. 📖

Put on faith and love as a breastplate, and the hope of salvation as a helmet.

**Help with these pictures:** A breastplate protects a soldier's chest, and a helmet protects the head.

Paul pictures faith, love, and hope as protection.

Salvation means being saved by God.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:9

God appointed us to receive salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ, rather than suffer wrath. 📖

**Help with a word:** Wrath means God's anger against wrongdoing.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:10

Jesus died for us so that, awake or asleep, we may live together with him. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:11

So encourage and build each other up, just as you already do. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:12

Brothers and sisters, recognise those who work hard among you, care for you in the Lord, and correct you. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:13

Value them very highly in love because of their work. 📖

Live at peace with each other.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:14

Brothers and sisters, we urge you to warn the unruly, encourage the discouraged, and help the weak. 📖

Be patient with everyone.

**Help with a word:** Unruly means refusing to live in an orderly way.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:15

Make sure no one pays back evil with evil. 📖

Always seek what is good for each other and everyone else.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:16

Rejoice at all times. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Keep praying without stopping. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Give thanks in every situation. 📖

This is what God wants for you in Christ Jesus.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:19

Do not put out the Spirit's fire. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Putting out a fire pictures stopping the Spirit's work.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:20

Do not look down on prophecies. 📖

**Help with a word:** Prophecies are messages spoken as coming from God.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:21

Test everything. 📖

Hold on to what is good.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:22

Stay away from every kind of evil. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:23

May the God of peace himself make you completely holy. 📖

May your whole spirit, soul, and body be kept without blame when our Lord Jesus Christ comes.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:24

The One calling you is faithful, and he will do it. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:25

Brothers and sisters, pray for us too. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:26

Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:27

Before the Lord, I charge you to have this letter read to all the brothers and sisters. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 5:28

May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. 📖
