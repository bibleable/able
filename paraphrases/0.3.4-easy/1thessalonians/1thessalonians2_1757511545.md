# 1 Thessalonians 2 — Care and longing for the believers

**About this chapter**

Paul recalls how he and his fellow workers lived among the believers.

He writes about suffering, opposition, and his longing to see them again.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:1

Brothers and sisters, you know our visit to you was not wasted. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 2:2

You know we had suffered and been treated shamefully in Philippi. 📖

Yet God gave us courage to tell you his good news despite strong opposition.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:3

Our appeal does not come from lies, hidden wrong motives, or trickery. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 2:4

God approved us and entrusted us with the good news. 📖

We speak to please God, who examines our hearts, rather than people.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:5

You know we never used flattery or hid greed behind an excuse. 📖

God is our witness!

**Help with words:** Flattery means praise used to get something from someone.

Greed means always wanting more for yourself.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:6

We did not seek praise from you or anyone else. 📖

As Christ's apostles, we had authority to demand it.

**Help with a word:** Apostles are people sent out for a special task.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:7

Instead, we were gentle among you, like a mother feeding and caring for her children. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 2:8

We loved you so deeply that we gladly shared both God's good news and our own lives with you. 📖

You had become that dear to us.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:9

Brothers and sisters, you remember our hard work. 📖

We worked night and day so we would not burden anyone while telling you God's good news.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:10

You and God are witnesses of our holy, righteous, and blameless conduct among you who believed. 📖

**Help with words:** Holy means set apart for God.

Righteous means right before God.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:11

You know we treated each of you as a father treats his own children. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 2:12

We encouraged, comforted, and urged you to live in a way worthy of God. 📖

He calls you into his own kingdom and glory.

**Help with words:** God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:13

We continually thank God because you received the message you heard from us as God's word, rather than human words. 📖

It truly is God's word, and it is at work in you who believe.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:14

Brothers and sisters, you followed the example of God's churches in Judea that belong to Christ Jesus. 📖

You suffered from your own people just as they suffered from the Jews.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:15

They killed the Lord Jesus and their own prophets and drove us out too. 📖

They displease God and are hostile to everyone.

**Help with the setting:** Paul is speaking about the opponents described in this passage.

Paul himself was Jewish, as he explains in Galatians 2:15.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:16

They stop us telling Gentiles how to be saved. 📖

They keep piling their sins up to the full limit.

The utmost wrath has come on them.

**Help with words:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

Wrath means God's anger against wrongdoing.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:17

Brothers and sisters, we were torn away from you briefly in person, but not in heart. 📖

We longed even more deeply to see you face to face.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:18

We wanted to come to you. 📖

I, Paul, tried again and again, but Satan blocked us.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:19

Who is our hope, joy, and crown of pride before our Lord Jesus when he comes? 📖

Is it not you?

**Help with this picture:** The believers are pictured as a crown bringing joy and honour to the writers.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:20

You truly are our glory and our joy. 📖
