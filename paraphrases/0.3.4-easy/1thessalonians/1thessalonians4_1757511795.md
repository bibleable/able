# 1 Thessalonians 4 — A holy life and a lasting hope

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches about sexual conduct, love, and work.

He also explains the hope believers have when fellow believers die.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:1

Finally, brothers and sisters, we ask and encourage you in the Lord Jesus to live to please God. 📖

This is what we taught you, and how you already live.

Keep doing so more and more.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:2

You know the instructions we gave you by the Lord Jesus's authority. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 4:3

God wants you to be holy. 📖

Stay away from wrong sexual behaviour.

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:4

Each of you must know how to control your own body in holiness and honour. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 4:5

Do not live in lustful passion like the Gentiles who do not know God. 📖

**Help with words:** Lustful passion means strong wrong sexual desire.

Gentiles means people who are not Jews.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:6

No one should wrong or exploit a fellow believer in this matter. 📖

The Lord will punish all such actions, as we already told you and seriously warned you.

**Help with a word:** Exploit means use someone unfairly for your own benefit.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:7

God called us to holiness, rather than impurity. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 4:8

Anyone rejecting this command rejects God, rather than a human person. 📖

God is the One who gives you his Holy Spirit.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:9

You do not need anyone to write about loving fellow believers. 📖

God himself has taught you to love each other.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:10

You show this love to all believers throughout Macedonia. 📖

But we urge you, brothers and sisters, to keep growing in it.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:11

Aim to live quietly, mind your own business, and work with your own hands, as we instructed you. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 4:12

Then you will behave properly towards outsiders and will not depend on anyone. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 4:13

Brothers and sisters, we want you to know about those who sleep in death. 📖

Then you will not grieve like those with no hope.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:14

We believe Jesus died and rose again. 📖

So we also believe God will bring with Jesus those who have fallen asleep in him.

**Help with this picture:** Falling asleep pictures dying here.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:15

We tell you this by the Lord's word. 📖

Those of us still alive when the Lord comes will not go ahead of those who have fallen asleep.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:16

The Lord himself will come down from heaven with a loud command. 📖

There will be an archangel's voice and God's trumpet.

The dead in Christ will rise first.

**Help with a word:** An archangel is a chief angel.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:17

Then those of us still alive will be caught up with them in the clouds. 📖

We will meet the Lord in the air.

So we will always be with the Lord.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:18

Encourage each other with these words. 📖
