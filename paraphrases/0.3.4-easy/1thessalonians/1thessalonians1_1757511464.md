# 1 Thessalonians 1 — Faith that becomes known

**About this chapter**

Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy write to the church in Thessalonica.

They thank God for the believers' faith, love, and hope.

## 1 Thessalonians 1:1

This letter is from Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy. 📖

It is for the Thessalonian church in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

May you have kindness and peace.

## 1 Thessalonians 1:2

We always thank God for all of you and remember you in our prayers. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 1:3

Before our God and Father, we continually remember the work your faith produces. 📖

We remember your hard work of love and your lasting hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.

## 1 Thessalonians 1:4

Brothers and sisters loved by God, we know he has chosen you. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 1:5

Our good news came with words, power, the Holy Spirit, and deep conviction. 📖

You know how we lived among you for your sake.

**Help with a word:** Conviction means being deeply sure that something is true.

## 1 Thessalonians 1:6

You followed our example and the Lord's example. 📖

Despite great suffering, you welcomed the message with joy from the Holy Spirit.

## 1 Thessalonians 1:7

So you became an example to all believers in Macedonia and Achaia. 📖

## 1 Thessalonians 1:8

The Lord's message rang out from you to Macedonia and Achaia, and your faith in God became known everywhere. 📖

We do not need to say anything more.

## 1 Thessalonians 1:9

People themselves tell how you welcomed us. 📖

They tell how you turned from idols to God to serve the living and true God.

**Help with a word:** An idol is something worshipped in God's place.

## 1 Thessalonians 1:10

You wait for God's Son from heaven, whom God raised from the dead. 📖

He is Jesus, who rescues us from the coming wrath.

**Help with a word:** Wrath means God's anger against wrongdoing.
