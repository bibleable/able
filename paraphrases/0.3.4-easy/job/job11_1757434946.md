# Job 11 — Zophar accuses Job

**About this chapter**

Zophar now answers Job.

His accusations and promises are his own claims.

## Job 11:1

Then Zophar the Naamathite answered: 📖

## Job 11:2

Should this stream of words go unanswered? 📖

Should someone who speaks like this be declared right?

## Job 11:3

Should your empty talk silence others? 📖

Will no one correct your mocking words?

## Job 11:4

You have said, “What I teach is right. 📖

I am pure in Your sight.”

## Job 11:5

If only God would speak! 📖

If only He would open His mouth against you,

## Job 11:6

and show you wisdom’s secrets! 📖

True wisdom has two sides.

Know this: God demands less from you than your sin deserves.

## Job 11:7

Can you understand God’s deep things? 📖

Can you discover the Almighty’s limits?

## Job 11:8

They are higher than the heavens. 📖

What can you do?

They are deeper than Sheol.

What can you know?

**Help — Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Job 11:9

Their measure is longer than the earth and wider than the sea. 📖

## Job 11:10

If He comes to put you in prison, who can stop Him? 📖

If He calls a court together, who can stop Him?

## Job 11:11

He certainly knows when people deceive others. 📖

If He sees evil, does He not notice?

## Job 11:12

A senseless man can no more become wise than a wild donkey’s young can be born human! 📖

## Job 11:13

But suppose you set your heart right. 📖

Suppose you lift your hands to God in prayer,

## Job 11:14

put away the evil in your hand, and allow no injustice in your tents. 📖

## Job 11:15

Then you will lift your face without shame. 📖

You will stand firm and unafraid.

## Job 11:16

You will forget your misery. 📖

You will remember it only like water that has flowed past.

## Job 11:17

Your life will be brighter than noon. 📖

Its darkness will be like morning.

## Job 11:18

You will be safe because there is hope. 📖

You will look around and lie down in safety.

## Job 11:19

You will lie down without fear. 📖

Many people will try to gain your favor.

## Job 11:20

But wicked people’s eyes will fail. 📖

They will find no escape.

Their hope will be to breathe their last breath.
