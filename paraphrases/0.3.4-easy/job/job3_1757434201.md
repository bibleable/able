# Job 3 — Job curses his birth day

**About this chapter**

Job speaks out of deep suffering.

He curses the day of his birth and asks why he was given life.

These are Job’s words of lament.

## Job 3:1

Job began speaking and cursed his birth day. 📖

## Job 3:2

He said, 📖

## Job 3:3

“Let my birth day perish, and the night saying, ‘A boy is conceived.’ 📖

## Job 3:4

Let that day become darkness. 📖

Let God above disregard it and no light shine.

## Job 3:5

Let darkness, gloom, and a cloud claim it. 📖

Let blackness overwhelm the day.

## Job 3:6

Let darkness take that night from the year’s days and every month. 📖

## Job 3:7

Let it be barren, with no joyful voice. 📖

## Job 3:8

Let day-cursers curse it, those ready to rouse Leviathan. 📖

**Help with Leviathan:** A powerful creature named in this poetry.

## Job 3:9

Let its morning stars darken. 📖

Let it wait vainly for daylight and never see dawn.

## Job 3:10

It failed to shut the womb’s doors and hide sorrow from me. 📖

**Help with womb’s doors:** Job pictures birth as a doorway that could have stayed closed.

## Job 3:11

Why did I not die at birth, coming from the womb? 📖

## Job 3:12

Why did knees receive me and breasts nurse me? 📖

## Job 3:13

Then I would lie peacefully, asleep and resting, 📖

## Job 3:14

with earthly kings and advisers who built cities now ruined, 📖

## Job 3:15

or princes with gold and houses full of silver. 📖

## Job 3:16

Why was I not hidden like a stillborn child, an infant never seeing daylight? 📖

**Help with stillborn:** Born dead.

## Job 3:17

There the wicked stop raging and the weary rest. 📖

## Job 3:18

Captives rest without hearing an oppressor. 📖

## Job 3:19

Small and great are there, and slaves are free of masters. 📖

## Job 3:20

Why give light to the miserable and life to those bitter in soul, 📖

## Job 3:21

longing for death that never comes and seeking it like hidden treasure, 📖

## Job 3:22

rejoicing greatly when they find the grave? 📖

## Job 3:23

Why give life to someone whose way is hidden, whom God has hedged in? 📖

**Help with hedged in:** Job pictures being trapped with no clear way forward.

## Job 3:24

I sigh at food, and my groans flow like water. 📖

## Job 3:25

My fears have overtaken me; what I dreaded happened. 📖

## Job 3:26

I have no ease, quiet, or rest. 📖

Trouble has come.”
