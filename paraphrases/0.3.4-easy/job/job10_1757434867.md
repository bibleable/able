# Job 10 — Job asks why God is against him

**About this chapter**

Job continues speaking.

He says what he wants to say to God.

He describes his pain and questions God’s treatment of him.

## Job 10:1

I hate my own life. 📖

I will express my complaint.

I will speak from my bitter heart.

## Job 10:2

I will say to God, “Do not condemn me! 📖

Tell me why You bring charges against me.”

## Job 10:3

Does it please You to treat me cruelly? 📖

Do You enjoy rejecting the work of Your hands?

Do You favor wicked people’s plans?

## Job 10:4

Do You have human eyes? 📖

Do You see as people see?

## Job 10:5

Are Your days like a mortal person’s days? 📖

Are Your years like a man’s years,

## Job 10:6

so that You must look for my wrongdoing and search for my sin? 📖

## Job 10:7

You know that I am not guilty. 📖

And no one can rescue me from Your hand.

## Job 10:8

Your hands shaped every part of me. 📖

Will You now turn and destroy me?

## Job 10:9

Please remember that You shaped me like clay. 📖

Will You now turn me back into dust?

## Job 10:10

Did You not pour me out like milk? 📖

Did You not make me solid like cheese?

**Help — milk and cheese:** Job uses these pictures to describe God forming his body.

## Job 10:11

You clothed me with skin and flesh. 📖

You joined me together with bones and sinews.

**Help — sinews:** Strong bands of tissue that connect parts of the body.

## Job 10:12

You gave me life and faithful love. 📖

Your care kept my spirit safe.

## Job 10:13

Yet You hid these things in Your heart. 📖

I know this was in Your mind:

## Job 10:14

If I sinned, You would notice. 📖

You would not clear me of my wrongdoing.

## Job 10:15

If I am guilty, how terrible for me! 📖

Even if I am innocent, I cannot lift my head.

I am full of shame.

I know my suffering.

## Job 10:16

If I held my head high, You would hunt me like a lion. 📖

Again You would show Your power against me.

## Job 10:17

You bring new witnesses against me. 📖

Your anger toward me keeps growing.

Troubles attack me in wave after wave.

## Job 10:18

Why did You bring me out of the womb? 📖

If only I had died before anyone saw me!

## Job 10:19

If only I had never existed! 📖

If only I had been carried straight from the womb to the grave!

## Job 10:20

Are my days not few? 📖

Draw back from me so I can have a little comfort,

## Job 10:21

before I go to a land of darkness and gloom. 📖

I will never return from there.

## Job 10:22

It is a land of complete darkness, deep shadows, and disorder. 📖

Even its light is like darkness.
