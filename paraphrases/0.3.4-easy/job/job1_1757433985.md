# Job 1 — Job’s life and terrible losses

**About this chapter**

Job is introduced as upright and God-fearing.

Satan challenges Job’s motives, and the LORD sets a limit.

Job suffers terrible losses and mourns.

## Job 1:1

Job lived in Uz. 📖

He was blameless and upright, feared God, and turned from evil.

## Job 1:2

He had 7 sons and 3 daughters. 📖

## Job 1:3

He owned 7,000 sheep, 3,000 camels, 500 pairs of oxen, and 500 female donkeys. 📖

He had a very large number of servants.

He was the greatest man among all the people of the East.

## Job 1:4

His sons took turns holding feasts in their homes. 📖

They invited their 3 sisters to eat and drink with them.

## Job 1:5

After each feast period, Job summoned his children to purify them. 📖

He rose early and offered burnt offerings for each.

He thought they might have sinned and cursed God inwardly.

He did this regularly.

**Help with purify:** Prepare for sacred worship.

## Job 1:6

One day the sons of God presented themselves before the LORD, with Satan among them. 📖

**Help with sons of God:** This phrase refers here to heavenly beings.

## Job 1:7

The LORD asked Satan where he came from. 📖

He answered, “Roaming the earth and walking back and forth.”

## Job 1:8

The LORD asked Satan, “Have you considered My servant Job? 📖

Nobody on earth is like him.

He is blameless and upright, fears God, and turns from evil.”

## Job 1:9

Satan answered, “Does Job fear God for nothing? 📖

## Job 1:10

Have You not placed a hedge around him, his household, and everything he owns? 📖

You have blessed his work.

His possessions have increased in the land.

**Help with hedge:** Satan uses this picture to describe God’s protection.

## Job 1:11

Stretch your hand against everything he owns, and he will curse you to your face.” 📖

## Job 1:12

The LORD said, “Everything he owns is in your hands. 📖

Do not touch Job himself.”

Satan left the LORD’s presence.

## Job 1:13

One day Job’s children ate and drank wine at their oldest brother’s house. 📖

## Job 1:14

A messenger told Job, “The oxen ploughed, and donkeys grazed nearby. 📖

## Job 1:15

Sabeans raided and took them, killing servants with swords. 📖

Only I escaped to tell you!”

## Job 1:16

Before he finished, another messenger came. 📖

He said, “God’s fire fell from heaven.

It burned up the sheep and servants.

Only I escaped to tell you!”

**About this report:** The messenger calls it “God’s fire”.

## Job 1:17

Before he finished, another messenger came. 📖

He said, “Chaldeans formed 3 bands, raided the camels, and took them away.

They killed the servants with swords.

Only I escaped to tell you!”

## Job 1:18

Before he finished, another messenger came. 📖

He said, “Your sons and daughters ate and drank wine at their oldest brother’s house.

## Job 1:19

A great desert wind struck its 4 corners. 📖

It collapsed on them, and they died.

Only I escaped to tell you!”

## Job 1:20

Job rose, tore his robe, shaved his head, fell to the ground, and worshipped. 📖

**Help with these actions:** Tearing clothes and shaving expressed mourning.

## Job 1:21

“Naked I came from my mother’s womb, and naked I will return. 📖

The LORD gave and took away.

Blessed be his name.”

## Job 1:22

In all this Job neither sinned nor accused God of wrongdoing. 📖
