# Job 32 — Elihu begins to speak

**About this chapter**

The narrator introduces Elihu.

From verse 6, Elihu explains why he wants to speak.

## Job 32:1

These three men stopped answering Job. 📖

He was righteous in his own eyes.

**Help — righteous in his own eyes:** Job considered himself right.

## Job 32:2

Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, from the family of Ram, became angry. 📖

He was angry because Job justified himself rather than God.

**Help — justified himself:** Argued that he was right.

## Job 32:3

Elihu was also angry with Job’s three friends. 📖

They had failed to prove Job wrong, yet they had condemned him.

## Job 32:4

Elihu had waited to speak to Job because the others were older than he was. 📖

## Job 32:5

But when he saw that the three men had no more answer, he became angry. 📖

## Job 32:6

Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite said, “I am young, and you are old. 📖

So I was timid and afraid to tell you what I know.

## Job 32:7

I thought older people should speak. 📖

I thought many years should teach wisdom.

## Job 32:8

But there is a spirit in a person. 📖

The Almighty’s breath gives him understanding.

## Job 32:9

Old people are not the only wise people. 📖

Elderly people are not the only ones who understand justice.

## Job 32:10

So I say, ‘Listen to me. 📖

I too will tell you what I know.’

## Job 32:11

I waited while you spoke. 📖

I listened to your reasoning as you searched for words.

## Job 32:12

I gave you my full attention. 📖

But no one proved Job wrong.

Not one of you answered his arguments.

## Job 32:13

So do not claim, ‘We have found wisdom. 📖

Let God prove him wrong, rather than a human being.’

## Job 32:14

But Job has not aimed his words at me. 📖

I will not answer him with your arguments.

## Job 32:15

Job’s friends are dismayed and have nothing more to say. 📖

Words have left them.

## Job 32:16

Must I wait now that they are silent? 📖

They stand there and no longer answer.

## Job 32:17

I too will answer. 📖

Yes, I will tell what I know.

## Job 32:18

I am full of words. 📖

My spirit within me drives me to speak.

## Job 32:19

My belly is like wine with no opening for its gas. 📖

It is about to burst like a new wineskin.

**Help — wineskin:** An animal-skin container for wine.

Elihu pictures the pressure he feels to speak.

## Job 32:20

I must speak and find relief. 📖

I must open my lips and answer.

## Job 32:21

I will not unfairly take anyone’s side. 📖

I will not flatter anyone.

**Help — flatter:** Praise someone insincerely to gain favor.

## Job 32:22

I do not know how to flatter. 📖

If I did, my Maker would remove me in an instant.”
