# Job 25 — Bildad speaks about God and human beings

**About this chapter**

Bildad speaks briefly.

His comparison of people to worms is part of his argument.

## Job 25:1

Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: 📖

## Job 25:2

God has authority and inspires awe. 📖

He establishes peace in the heights of heaven.

**Help — awe:** Deep wonder and fear before greatness.

## Job 25:3

Can anyone count His troops? 📖

Is there anyone His light does not shine on?

## Job 25:4

How then can a man be right before God? 📖

How can someone born of a woman be pure?

## Job 25:5

Even the moon does not shine before Him. 📖

Even the stars are not pure in His sight.

## Job 25:6

How much less pure is a human being, who is only a maggot! 📖

A son of man is only a worm!

**Help — maggot:** A small creature that feeds on rotting material.

Bildad uses these comparisons to describe human beings as low and unclean.
