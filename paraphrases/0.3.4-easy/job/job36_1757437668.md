# Job 36 — Elihu speaks about correction and God’s greatness

**About this chapter**

Elihu continues.

His claims about suffering, correction, and judgment are part of his speech.

## Job 36:1

Elihu continued: 📖

## Job 36:2

Be patient with me a little longer. 📖

I will show you there is more to say on God’s behalf.

## Job 36:3

I get my knowledge from far away. 📖

I will declare that my Maker is just.

## Job 36:4

My words are truly free from lies. 📖

Someone with perfect knowledge is with you.

**Help — perfect knowledge:** This is Elihu’s claim about his own speech.

## Job 36:5

God is mighty, but He despises no one. 📖

His strength of understanding is mighty.

## Job 36:6

He does not keep wicked people alive. 📖

He gives justice to people who suffer.

## Job 36:7

He does not take His eyes off righteous people. 📖

He seats them on thrones with kings.

He raises them high forever.

**Help — righteous:** Right before God.

## Job 36:8

People may be tied with chains. 📖

They may be caught in suffering’s ropes.

## Job 36:9

Then He tells them what they have done. 📖

He shows them how proudly they have sinned.

## Job 36:10

He opens their ears to correction. 📖

He commands them to turn away from evil.

## Job 36:11

If they obey and serve Him, their remaining days will be successful. 📖

They will spend their years in happiness.

## Job 36:12

But if they do not obey, they will die by the sword. 📖

They will die without knowledge.

## Job 36:13

People who reject God in their hearts hold on to anger. 📖

Even when He binds them, they do not cry for help.

## Job 36:14

They die while young, among male shrine prostitutes. 📖

**Help — male shrine prostitutes:** Men involved in sexual activity connected with worship at a shrine.

A shrine is a place of worship.

## Job 36:15

God rescues suffering people through their suffering. 📖

He opens their ears through cruel treatment.

## Job 36:16

He drew you from distress’s jaws to a broad, open place. 📖

He brought you to a table full of rich food.

**Help — distress’s jaws:** Elihu pictures trouble as an animal that traps someone in its mouth.

## Job 36:17

But now you are loaded with the judgment due to wicked people. 📖

Judgment and justice have seized you.

## Job 36:18

Be careful that no one draws you in with riches. 📖

Do not let a large bribe lead you astray.

**Help — bribe:** A gift used to make someone act dishonestly.

## Job 36:19

Can your wealth keep you from distress? 📖

Can all your strong efforts do that?

## Job 36:20

Do not long for the night when people vanish from their homes. 📖

## Job 36:21

Be careful not to turn to evil. 📖

You have preferred it to suffering.

## Job 36:22

Look, God is high in His power. 📖

Who teaches like Him?

## Job 36:23

Who has set His way for Him? 📖

Who has told Him, “You have done wrong”?

## Job 36:24

Remember to praise His work as great. 📖

People have praised it in song.

## Job 36:25

All people have seen it. 📖

They look at it from far away.

## Job 36:26

God is certainly great beyond what we know. 📖

No one can discover the number of His years.

## Job 36:27

He draws up drops of water. 📖

They form rain from the mist,

## Job 36:28

which the clouds pour out. 📖

They shower it generously on people.

## Job 36:29

Who can understand how the clouds spread out? 📖

Who can understand thunder roaring from His pavilion?

**Help — pavilion:** A large tent.

Elihu pictures the sky as God’s dwelling.

## Job 36:30

See how He spreads His lightning around Him. 📖

He covers the depths of the sea.

## Job 36:31

With these He judges the nations. 📖

He provides plenty of food.

## Job 36:32

He fills His hands with lightning. 📖

He commands it to hit its target.

## Job 36:33

Thunder announces His presence. 📖

Even cattle pay attention to the rising storm.
