# Job 5 — Eliphaz continues

**About this chapter**

Eliphaz is still speaking to Job.

These are Eliphaz’s claims and advice.

## Job 5:1

Call out if you want to. 📖

But who will answer?

Which of the holy ones will you turn to?

**Help — holy ones:** Heavenly beings.

## Job 5:2

Anger that you hold on to kills a foolish person. 📖

Envy kills a person who lacks understanding.

## Job 5:3

I have seen a foolish person taking root like a plant. 📖

But suddenly his house was cursed.

**Help — taking root:** Becoming settled and successful.

## Job 5:4

His sons are far from safety. 📖

They are crushed in court.

No one defends them.

## Job 5:5

Hungry people eat his harvest. 📖

They even take it from among the thorns.

Thirsty people eagerly seek his wealth.

## Job 5:6

Suffering does not rise from the dust. 📖

Trouble does not grow from the ground.

## Job 5:7

Yet people are born to trouble, just as surely as sparks fly upward. 📖

## Job 5:8

But if I were you, I would appeal to God. 📖

I would put my case before Him.

## Job 5:9

He does great things that no one can fully understand. 📖

His wonders cannot be counted.

## Job 5:10

He gives rain to the earth. 📖

He sends water on the fields.

## Job 5:11

He raises up people who are low. 📖

He lifts people who mourn to safety.

## Job 5:12

He defeats the plans of people who use tricks. 📖

Their work does not succeed.

## Job 5:13

He catches wise people in their own tricks. 📖

He sweeps away the plans of clever deceivers.

## Job 5:14

They meet darkness during the day. 📖

At noon they feel their way as if it were night.

## Job 5:15

He saves needy people from the sword in their enemies’ mouths. 📖

He saves them from powerful people’s grip.

**Help — sword in their mouths:** Words pictured as weapons that hurt people.

## Job 5:16

So poor people have hope. 📖

Injustice shuts its mouth.

**Help — injustice:** Treating people unfairly.

Eliphaz pictures injustice as someone who can no longer speak.

## Job 5:17

The person God corrects is blessed. 📖

So do not reject the Almighty’s discipline.

**Help — discipline:** Correction that teaches someone how to live.

## Job 5:18

He wounds, but He also puts bandages on wounds. 📖

He strikes, but His hands also heal.

## Job 5:19

He will rescue you from six disasters. 📖

In seven, no harm will touch you.

## Job 5:20

When there is famine, He will save you from death. 📖

In battle, He will save you from the sword.

**Help — famine:** A time when there is very little food.

## Job 5:21

You will be protected from cruel words that strike like a whip. 📖

You will not fear destruction when it comes.

## Job 5:22

You will laugh at destruction and famine. 📖

You will not need to fear wild animals.

## Job 5:23

You will have a covenant with the stones in the field. 📖

Wild animals will be at peace with you.

**Help — covenant:** A serious agreement.

Eliphaz pictures even stones and animals as agreeing not to harm Job.

## Job 5:24

You will know that your tent is safe. 📖

When you check your home, you will find nothing wrong.

## Job 5:25

You will know that you have many children. 📖

Your descendants will be as many as blades of grass.

**Help — descendants:** Your children and the generations born after them.

## Job 5:26

You will go to the grave still full of strength. 📖

You will be like a bundle of grain gathered at the right time.

## Job 5:27

We have looked into this, and it is true! 📖

Listen to it.

Learn it for yourself.
