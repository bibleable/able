# Job 41 — The LORD describes Leviathan

**About this chapter**

The LORD continues speaking.

He describes Leviathan’s strength with vivid poetic pictures.

## Job 41:1

Can you pull Leviathan in with a hook? 📖

Can you tie his tongue down with a rope?

**Help — Leviathan:** The name of the powerful creature described in this chapter.

## Job 41:2

Can you put a cord through his nose? 📖

Can you pierce his jaw with a hook?

## Job 41:3

Will he beg you for mercy? 📖

Will he speak softly to you?

## Job 41:4

Will he make a covenant with you? 📖

Will he agree to become your slave for life?

**Help — covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Job 41:5

Can you play with him like a pet bird? 📖

Can you put him on a leash for your young women?

## Job 41:6

Will traders bargain for him? 📖

Will they divide him among merchants?

## Job 41:7

Can you fill his skin with harpoons? 📖

Can you fill his head with fishing spears?

**Help — harpoons:** Spears used to catch large water animals.

## Job 41:8

If you lay a hand on him, you will remember the battle. 📖

You will never do it again!

## Job 41:9

The hope of defeating him is surely false. 📖

Does the very sight of him not overwhelm you?

## Job 41:10

No one is fierce enough to stir up Leviathan. 📖

Then who can stand against Me?

## Job 41:11

Who has given Me something that I must repay? 📖

Everything under heaven belongs to Me.

## Job 41:12

I cannot keep silent about his limbs, his power, and his graceful form. 📖

## Job 41:13

Who can strip off his outer coat? 📖

Who can approach him with a bridle?

**Help — bridle:** Straps fitted to an animal’s head to control it.

## Job 41:14

Who can open his jaws? 📖

His terrifying teeth surround them.

## Job 41:15

His rows of scales are his pride. 📖

They are sealed tightly together.

## Job 41:16

Each scale is so close to the next that no air can pass between them. 📖

## Job 41:17

They are joined together. 📖

They hold fast and cannot be separated.

## Job 41:18

His snorting flashes with light. 📖

His eyes are like rays of dawn.

## Job 41:19

Burning pieces stream from his mouth. 📖

Fiery sparks shoot out!

## Job 41:20

Smoke pours from his nostrils. 📖

It is like smoke from a boiling pot over burning reeds.

## Job 41:21

His breath sets coals on fire. 📖

Flames pour from his mouth.

## Job 41:22

Strength lives in his neck. 📖

Dismay leaps ahead of him.

**Help — dismay:** Fear and distress.

## Job 41:23

The folds of his flesh are joined tightly. 📖

They are firm and cannot be moved.

## Job 41:24

His chest is as hard as rock. 📖

It is as hard as a lower millstone!

**Help — lower millstone:** A heavy stone beneath another stone used for grinding grain.

## Job 41:25

When Leviathan rises, mighty people are terrified. 📖

They pull back before his violent movements.

## Job 41:26

A sword that reaches him has no effect. 📖

Neither does a spear, dart, or arrow.

## Job 41:27

He treats iron like straw. 📖

He treats bronze like rotten wood.

## Job 41:28

No arrow can make him run away. 📖

Stones from a sling are like chaff to him.

**Help — sling:** A tool for throwing stones.

Chaff is light plant material separated from grain.

## Job 41:29

He treats a club like straw. 📖

He laughs at the sound of a lance.

**Help — club and lance:** A heavy stick and a long spear used as weapons.

## Job 41:30

His underside is like sharp pieces of broken pottery. 📖

It spreads across the mud like a threshing sledge.

**Help — threshing sledge:** A board with sharp pieces underneath, dragged over crops to separate grain.

## Job 41:31

He makes the depths boil like a cooking pot. 📖

He makes the sea like a jar of ointment.

**Help — ointment:** A soft, oily mixture.

These pictures describe the sea stirred up by Leviathan.

## Job 41:32

He leaves a shining trail in the water. 📖

You would think the deep had white hair!

## Job 41:33

Nothing on earth is his equal. 📖

He is a creature without fear!

## Job 41:34

He looks down on all who are haughty. 📖

He is king over all the proud.

**Help — haughty:** Proud and looking down on others.
