# Job 37 — Elihu speaks about the storm

**About this chapter**

Elihu finishes his speech.

He describes weather and urges Job to consider God’s wonders.

## Job 37:1

At this my heart pounds too. 📖

It leaps from its place.

## Job 37:2

Listen closely to the thunder of His voice. 📖

Listen to the rumbling from His mouth.

## Job 37:3

He lets His lightning loose under the whole sky. 📖

He sends it to the ends of the earth.

## Job 37:4

Then a roaring sound comes. 📖

He thunders with His majestic voice.

He does not hold back the lightning when His voice sounds.

**Help — majestic:** Showing great power and glory.

## Job 37:5

God’s voice thunders in wonderful ways. 📖

He does great things we cannot understand.

## Job 37:6

He says to snow, “Fall on the earth.” 📖

He says to gentle rain, “Pour out a mighty downpour.”

## Job 37:7

He seals up every person’s hand. 📖

Then everyone can know His work.

**Help — seals up the hand:** Stops people from doing their usual work.

## Job 37:8

Wild animals go into their shelters. 📖

They settle in their dens.

## Job 37:9

The violent storm comes from its chamber. 📖

Cold comes from the driving north winds.

**Help — chamber:** A room.

Elihu pictures the storm coming from a place where it is stored.

## Job 37:10

God’s breath forms the ice. 📖

Wide areas of water freeze.

## Job 37:11

He loads the clouds with water. 📖

He scatters His lightning through them.

## Job 37:12

They turn and swirl as He directs. 📖

They do everything He commands across the whole earth.

## Job 37:13

He does this for punishment or for His land. 📖

He does it in His faithful love.

## Job 37:14

Listen to this, Job. 📖

Stand still and think about God’s wonders.

## Job 37:15

Do you know how God sends the clouds? 📖

Do you know how He makes lightning flash?

## Job 37:16

Do you understand how clouds float? 📖

These are wonders of the One whose knowledge is perfect.

## Job 37:17

Your clothes grow hot when the land lies quiet under the south wind. 📖

## Job 37:18

Can you spread out the skies as He does? 📖

Can you make them as strong as a bronze mirror?

**Help — bronze mirror:** A polished metal surface used as a mirror.

## Job 37:19

Teach us what to say to Him. 📖

We cannot prepare our case because we are in darkness.

**Help — darkness:** A picture of lacking understanding.

## Job 37:20

Should He be told that I want to speak? 📖

Would a man ask to be swallowed up?

## Job 37:21

No one can stare at the sun shining brightly in the sky after the wind has swept it clear. 📖

## Job 37:22

He comes from the north in golden splendor. 📖

Awesome majesty surrounds Him.

**Help — splendor and majesty:** Great glory that fills people with wonder and fear.

## Job 37:23

The Almighty is beyond our reach. 📖

His power is great!

In His justice and great righteousness, He does not treat people cruelly.

## Job 37:24

So people fear Him. 📖

He does not unfairly favor those who are wise in heart.
