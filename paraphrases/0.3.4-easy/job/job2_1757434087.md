# Job 2 — Job’s illness and his friends’ arrival

**About this chapter**

Satan challenges Job again, and the LORD sets another limit.

Job suffers painful sores.

His friends come and sit with him in silence.

## Job 2:1

Another day, the sons of God presented themselves before the LORD. 📖

Satan also came to present himself.

## Job 2:2

The LORD asked where he came from. 📖

Satan answered, “Roaming the earth and walking back and forth.”

## Job 2:3

The LORD said, “Have you considered My servant Job? 📖

Nobody on earth is like him.

He is blameless and upright, fears God, and turns from evil.

He still keeps his integrity.

You urged Me to ruin him without cause.”

**Help with integrity:** Faithfulness to what is right.

## Job 2:4

Satan replied, “Skin for skin! 📖

A man gives all possessions to save his life.

**Help with skin for skin:** Satan claims Job will give anything to protect himself.

## Job 2:5

But stretch out Your hand and strike his flesh and bones. 📖

Then he will surely curse You to Your face.”

## Job 2:6

The LORD said to Satan, “Very well. 📖

He is in your hands, but you must spare his life.”

## Job 2:7

Satan left the LORD’s presence. 📖

He struck Job with terrible boils from the soles of his feet to the top of his head.

**Help with boils:** Painful swollen sores.

## Job 2:8

Job scraped himself with broken pottery while sitting in ashes. 📖

## Job 2:9

His wife asked, “Do you still keep your integrity? 📖

Curse God and die!”

## Job 2:10

He answered, “You speak like a foolish woman. 📖

Should we accept only good from God, not trouble?”

Job did not sin in what he said.

## Job 2:11

Friends Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite heard his troubles. 📖

They left their homes and agreed to sympathise and comfort him.

## Job 2:12

From far away they barely recognised him. 📖

They wept loudly, tore robes, and threw dust over their heads.

## Job 2:13

They sat on the ground with him 7 days and nights. 📖

Nobody spoke, seeing his terrible suffering.
