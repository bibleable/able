# Job 29 — Job remembers his former life

**About this chapter**

Job continues speaking.

He remembers the care, respect, and plenty he once enjoyed.

## Job 29:1

Job continued his speech: 📖

## Job 29:2

How I long for the months gone by! 📖

I long for the days when God watched over me,

## Job 29:3

when His lamp shone above my head. 📖

By His light I walked through darkness.

## Job 29:4

I long for the time when I was in my prime. 📖

God’s friendship rested on my tent.

**Help — in my prime:** In the strong and successful period of life.

## Job 29:5

The Almighty was still with me. 📖

My children were around me.

## Job 29:6

My steps were bathed in cream. 📖

The rock poured out streams of oil for me!

**Help — cream and oil:** Job pictures the plenty he enjoyed.

## Job 29:7

I went out to the city gate. 📖

I took my seat in the public square.

## Job 29:8

Young men saw me and stepped back. 📖

Old men stood up.

## Job 29:9

Princes stopped speaking. 📖

They covered their mouths with their hands.

## Job 29:10

The voices of nobles became quiet. 📖

Their tongues stuck to the roofs of their mouths.

**Help — nobles:** People with high rank.

## Job 29:11

People who heard me called me blessed. 📖

People who saw me praised me.

## Job 29:12

I rescued poor people who cried out. 📖

I rescued children without fathers who had no helper.

## Job 29:13

A dying man blessed me. 📖

I made a widow’s heart sing for joy.

**Help — widow:** A woman whose husband has died.

## Job 29:14

I put on righteousness, and it clothed me. 📖

Justice was my robe and turban.

**Help — righteousness and justice:** Doing what is right and treating people fairly.

Job pictures these as his clothing.

A turban is a cloth head covering.

## Job 29:15

I was eyes for blind people. 📖

I was feet for people who could not walk well.

**Help — eyes and feet:** Job helped people do things they could not do alone.

## Job 29:16

I was a father to needy people. 📖

I took up a stranger’s case.

## Job 29:17

I broke the sharp teeth of unjust people. 📖

I snatched their prey from their teeth.

**Help — teeth and prey:** Job pictures cruel people as hunting animals.

He rescued their victims.

## Job 29:18

So I thought, “I will die in my nest. 📖

My days will be as many as grains of sand.

## Job 29:19

My roots will reach the waters. 📖

Dew will rest on my branches every night.

## Job 29:20

My honor will always be new. 📖

My bow will be made strong again in my hand.”

**Help — nest, roots, and bow:** Job pictures a safe home, lasting life, honor, and strength.

## Job 29:21

People listened to me eagerly. 📖

They waited quietly for my advice.

## Job 29:22

After I spoke, they said no more. 📖

My speech settled on them like dew.

## Job 29:23

They waited for me as they waited for rain. 📖

They drank in my words like spring showers.

## Job 29:24

When I smiled at them, they could hardly believe it. 📖

The light of my face was precious to them.

## Job 29:25

I chose their course and led as their chief. 📖

I lived like a king among his troops.

I was like someone comforting people who mourn.
