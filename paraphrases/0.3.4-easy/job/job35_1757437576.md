# Job 35 — Elihu questions Job’s words

**About this chapter**

Elihu continues speaking.

He challenges Job’s words and gives his own account of unanswered cries.

## Job 35:1

Elihu went on to say: 📖

## Job 35:2

Do you think this is just? 📖

You say, “I am more righteous than God.”

**Help — righteous:** Right and just.

## Job 35:3

You ask, “What do I gain? 📖

What benefit do I get by not sinning?”

## Job 35:4

I will answer you and your friends too. 📖

## Job 35:5

Look at the heavens and see. 📖

Gaze at the clouds high above you.

## Job 35:6

If you sin, what do you achieve against Him? 📖

If you commit many offenses, what do you do to Him?

## Job 35:7

If you do what is right, what do you give Him? 📖

What does He receive from your hand?

## Job 35:8

Your wickedness affects only a person like yourself. 📖

Your righteousness affects only another human being.

## Job 35:9

People cry out under great cruelty. 📖

They plead for relief from powerful people’s arm.

## Job 35:10

But no one asks, “Where is God my Maker? 📖

He gives us songs in the night.

## Job 35:11

He teaches us more than the earth’s animals. 📖

He makes us wiser than the birds of the sky.”

## Job 35:12

They cry out there, but He does not answer. 📖

This is because of evil men’s pride.

## Job 35:13

God certainly does not listen to empty appeals. 📖

The Almighty pays no attention to them.

## Job 35:14

How much less when you say you do not see Him! 📖

You say your case is before Him and you must wait for Him.

## Job 35:15

You also say that He has not punished in His anger. 📖

You say He has not paid much attention to foolishness.

## Job 35:16

So Job opens his mouth for nothing. 📖

He keeps speaking more words without knowledge.
