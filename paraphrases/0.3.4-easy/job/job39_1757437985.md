# Job 39 — The LORD asks about wild animals

**About this chapter**

The LORD continues speaking to Job.

He asks about the lives and strength of animals.

## Job 39:1

Do you know when mountain goats give birth? 📖

Have you watched a female deer give birth to her fawn?

**Help — fawn:** A young deer.

## Job 39:2

Can you count the months they are pregnant? 📖

Do you know when they give birth?

## Job 39:3

They crouch down and give birth to their young. 📖

They deliver their newborn.

## Job 39:4

Their young grow strong in the open field. 📖

They leave and do not return.

## Job 39:5

Who set the wild donkey free? 📖

Who released the swift donkey from its harness?

**Help — harness:** Straps used to control a working animal.

## Job 39:6

I made the wilderness his home. 📖

I gave him the salt flats to live in.

**Help — salt flats:** Flat areas of ground covered with salt.

## Job 39:7

He looks down on the city’s noise. 📖

He never hears a driver shouting.

## Job 39:8

He roams the mountains for pasture. 📖

He searches for anything green to eat.

## Job 39:9

Will the wild ox agree to serve you? 📖

Will he stay by your manger at night?

**Help — manger:** A container that holds animal food.

## Job 39:10

Can you use a harness to keep him in a furrow? 📖

Will he plow the valleys behind you?

**Help — furrow:** A line cut into soil for planting.

## Job 39:11

Can you rely on his great strength? 📖

Will you leave your hard work to him?

## Job 39:12

Can you trust him to bring in your grain? 📖

Will he gather it to your threshing floor?

**Help — threshing floor:** A place where grain is separated from the rest of the plant.

## Job 39:13

The ostrich flaps her wings joyfully. 📖

But her wings and feathers cannot match the stork’s.

**Help — ostrich and stork:** Large birds.

## Job 39:14

She leaves her eggs on the ground. 📖

She lets them warm in the sand.

## Job 39:15

She forgets that a foot may crush them. 📖

A wild animal may step on them.

## Job 39:16

She treats her young harshly, as if they were not hers. 📖

She does not care that her work may have been wasted.

## Job 39:17

God has kept wisdom from her. 📖

He has not given her understanding.

## Job 39:18

Yet when she proudly spreads her wings, she laughs at the horse and its rider. 📖

## Job 39:19

Do you give the horse his strength? 📖

Do you decorate his neck with a mane?

**Help — mane:** Long hair on a horse’s neck.

## Job 39:20

Do you make him leap like a locust? 📖

His proud snorting brings terror.

**Help — locust:** An insect that can jump.

Snorting is blowing loudly through the nose.

## Job 39:21

He paws the ground in the valley. 📖

He delights in his strength.

He charges into battle.

## Job 39:22

He laughs at fear and is frightened of nothing. 📖

He does not turn back from the sword.

## Job 39:23

A quiver rattles at his side. 📖

A spear and lance flash there too.

**Help — quiver:** A container for arrows.

A lance is a long weapon like a spear.

## Job 39:24

He trembles with excitement and races across the distance. 📖

He cannot stand still when the ram’s horn sounds.

**Help — ram’s horn:** An animal horn used as a trumpet.

## Job 39:25

At the horn’s blast, he snorts eagerly. 📖

He smells battle from far away.

He hears captains shouting and the cry of war.

## Job 39:26

Does your understanding make the hawk fly? 📖

Does it make him spread his wings toward the south?

## Job 39:27

Does the eagle soar at your command? 📖

Does he build his nest high up because you tell him to?

## Job 39:28

He lives on a cliff and stays there. 📖

His safe place is a high, rocky peak.

## Job 39:29

From there he looks for food. 📖

His eyes see it far away.

## Job 39:30

His young feed on blood. 📖

Where the dead bodies lie, there he is.
