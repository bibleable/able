# Job 22 — Eliphaz makes further accusations

**About this chapter**

Eliphaz speaks.

His charges against Job are accusations in the debate.

They are not the narrator’s description of Job.

## Job 22:1

Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: 📖

## Job 22:2

Can a man be useful to God? 📖

Can even a wise man benefit Him?

## Job 22:3

Does the Almighty take pleasure in your being righteous? 📖

Does He gain anything if your ways are blameless?

**Help — righteous:** Right before God.

## Job 22:4

Does He correct you because you respect Him? 📖

Is that why He brings you to judgment?

## Job 22:5

Is your wickedness not great? 📖

Are your sins not endless?

## Job 22:6

You demanded guarantees from your brothers when there was no need. 📖

You took clothing from people who had nothing else to wear.

**Help — guarantees:** Property kept to make sure a debt is paid.

## Job 22:7

You gave tired people no water. 📖

You kept food from starving people,

## Job 22:8

while a powerful man owned the land. 📖

A man of honor lived on it.

## Job 22:9

You sent widows away with nothing. 📖

The strength of children without fathers was crushed.

**Help — widows:** Women whose husbands have died.

## Job 22:10

That is why traps surround you. 📖

Sudden danger terrifies you.

## Job 22:11

It is so dark that you cannot see. 📖

A flood covers you.

## Job 22:12

Is God not as high as the heavens? 📖

Look how high the highest stars are!

## Job 22:13

Yet you say, “What does God know? 📖

Can He judge through thick darkness?

## Job 22:14

Thick clouds hide Him, so He does not see us. 📖

He walks across the dome of heaven.”

**Help — dome:** Eliphaz’s quoted picture of the sky as a curved roof.

## Job 22:15

Will you stay on the old path that wicked men walked? 📖

## Job 22:16

They were taken away before their time. 📖

A flood swept away their foundations.

## Job 22:17

They said to God, “Leave us! 📖

What can the Almighty do to us?”

## Job 22:18

But He was the one who filled their houses with good things. 📖

So I stay far from wicked people’s advice.

## Job 22:19

Righteous people see this and are glad. 📖

Innocent people mock them, saying:

## Job 22:20

“Our enemies have surely been destroyed. 📖

Fire has burned up what they had left.”

## Job 22:21

Now make peace with God. 📖

Be at peace with Him, and good will come to you.

## Job 22:22

Accept teaching from His mouth. 📖

Store His words in your heart.

## Job 22:23

If you return to the Almighty, you will be restored. 📖

If you remove injustice from your tents,

## Job 22:24

put your gold in the dust. 📖

Put the gold of Ophir among the stones in the ravines.

**Help — Ophir:** A place known for gold.

Ravines are deep, narrow valleys.

## Job 22:25

Then the Almighty will be your gold. 📖

He will be your finest silver.

**Help — gold and silver:** Eliphaz pictures God as Job’s greatest treasure.

## Job 22:26

Then you will certainly delight in the Almighty. 📖

You will lift your face to God.

## Job 22:27

You will pray to Him, and He will hear you. 📖

You will keep your vows.

**Help — vows:** Serious promises.

## Job 22:28

What you decide will be done. 📖

Light will shine on your paths.

## Job 22:29

When people are brought low, you will say, “Lift them up!” 📖

Then God will save the lowly.

## Job 22:30

He will rescue even someone who is not innocent. 📖

He will rescue that person because your hands are clean.

**Help — clean hands:** A picture of doing what is right.
