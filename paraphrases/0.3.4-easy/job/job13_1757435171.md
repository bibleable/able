# Job 13 — Job wants to present his case to God

**About this chapter**

Job continues answering his friends.

From verse 20, he speaks directly to God.

## Job 13:1

My eyes have seen all this. 📖

My ears have heard and understood.

## Job 13:2

I know what you know. 📖

I am no less than you.

## Job 13:3

But I want to speak to the Almighty. 📖

I want to argue my case before God.

## Job 13:4

You cover things with lies. 📖

You are all useless doctors.

**Help — doctors:** Job means his friends have failed to help him.

## Job 13:5

If only you would stay silent! 📖

That would be wisdom for you!

## Job 13:6

Now hear my argument. 📖

Listen to my appeal.

## Job 13:7

Will you speak evil on God’s behalf? 📖

Will you tell lies for Him?

## Job 13:8

Will you unfairly take His side? 📖

Will you argue in His defense?

## Job 13:9

Would things go well if He examined you? 📖

Could you deceive Him as you could deceive a person?

## Job 13:10

He would surely correct you if you secretly took sides unfairly. 📖

## Job 13:11

Would His greatness not terrify you? 📖

Would fear of Him not fall on you?

## Job 13:12

Your wise sayings are sayings made of ashes. 📖

Your defenses are made of clay.

**Help — ashes and clay:** Job means that their sayings and arguments are weak and easily destroyed.

## Job 13:13

Be silent, and I will speak. 📖

Then let whatever happens happen to me.

## Job 13:14

Why do I put myself at risk? 📖

Why do I take my life in my own hands?

## Job 13:15

Even if He kills me, I will hope in Him. 📖

I will still defend my ways to His face.

## Job 13:16

This will also save me. 📖

No person who rejects God can come before Him.

## Job 13:17

Listen carefully to my words. 📖

Let what I say ring in your ears.

## Job 13:18

Look, I have prepared my case. 📖

I know that I will be proved right.

## Job 13:19

Can anyone bring a charge against me? 📖

If so, I will be silent and die.

## Job 13:20

God, give me just these two things. 📖

Then I will not need to hide from You:

## Job 13:21

Take Your hand away from me. 📖

Do not let Your terror frighten me.

## Job 13:22

Then call me, and I will answer. 📖

Or let me speak, and You can reply.

## Job 13:23

How many wrongs and sins have I done? 📖

Show me my offenses and sin.

## Job 13:24

Why do You hide Your face? 📖

Why do You treat me as Your enemy?

## Job 13:25

Would You frighten a leaf blown by the wind? 📖

Would You chase dry chaff?

**Help — chaff:** Light, useless plant parts left after grain is separated.

Job compares himself to weak things.

## Job 13:26

You write bitter charges against me. 📖

You make me carry the wrongs I did when I was young.

## Job 13:27

You lock my feet in wooden blocks. 📖

You watch all my paths.

You set a limit for the soles of my feet.

## Job 13:28

So a person wastes away like something rotten. 📖

He is like clothing eaten by moths.
