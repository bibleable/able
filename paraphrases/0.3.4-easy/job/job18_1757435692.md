# Job 18 — Bildad describes the fate of wicked people

**About this chapter**

Bildad speaks again.

His harsh description of wicked people’s fate is part of his argument with Job.

## Job 18:1

Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: 📖

## Job 18:2

How long until you end these speeches? 📖

Show some sense.

Then we can talk.

## Job 18:3

Why do you treat us like cattle? 📖

Why do you think we are stupid?

## Job 18:4

You tear yourself in anger. 📖

Should the earth be abandoned because of you?

Should rocks be moved from their place?

## Job 18:5

The lamp of a wicked man is put out. 📖

His fire’s flame does not shine.

## Job 18:6

The light in his tent grows dark. 📖

The lamp beside him goes out.

**Help — lamp and light:** Bildad pictures the man’s life and security ending.

## Job 18:7

His strong steps become short. 📖

His own plans trip him up.

## Job 18:8

His own feet lead him into a net. 📖

He walks into its mesh.

## Job 18:9

A trap catches his heel. 📖

A snare grips him.

**Help — snare:** A trap used to catch an animal.

## Job 18:10

A loop of rope is hidden on the ground to catch him. 📖

A trap lies in his path.

## Job 18:11

Terrors frighten him from every side. 📖

They pursue him at every step.

## Job 18:12

His strength is used up. 📖

Disaster stands ready beside him.

## Job 18:13

It eats patches of his skin. 📖

Death’s firstborn eats his arms and legs.

**Help — death’s firstborn:** A poetic picture of a deadly power.

## Job 18:14

He is torn from the safety of his tent. 📖

He is marched away to the king of terrors.

**Help — king of terrors:** A picture of death as a terrifying ruler.

## Job 18:15

Fire lives in his tent. 📖

Burning sulfur rains down on his home.

**Help — sulfur:** A substance that burns.

## Job 18:16

The roots below him dry up. 📖

The branches above him wither.

**Help — roots and branches:** Bildad pictures the man as a dying plant.

## Job 18:17

His memory disappears from the earth. 📖

His name is no longer known in the land.

## Job 18:18

He is driven from light into darkness. 📖

He is chased from the world where people live.

## Job 18:19

He has no children or descendants among his people. 📖

No one survives where he once lived.

**Help — descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

## Job 18:20

People in the west are horrified at his fate. 📖

People in the east shake with terror.

## Job 18:21

Surely this is the home of wicked people. 📖

This is the place of someone who does not know God.
