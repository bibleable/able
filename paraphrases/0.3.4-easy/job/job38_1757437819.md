# Job 38 — The LORD answers Job from the storm

**About this chapter**

The LORD now answers Job.

He asks Job about creation and its care.

These questions use many poetic pictures.

## Job 38:1

Then the LORD answered Job from the whirlwind. 📖

He said:

**Help — whirlwind:** A powerful, swirling wind.

## Job 38:2

Who is this who makes My counsel unclear with words that lack knowledge? 📖

**Help — counsel:** God’s wise plans and purpose.

## Job 38:3

Now prepare yourself like a man. 📖

I will ask you questions.

You must tell Me the answers.

## Job 38:4

Where were you when I laid the earth’s foundations? 📖

Tell Me, if you understand.

## Job 38:5

Who decided its measurements? 📖

Surely you know!

Who stretched a measuring line across it?

## Job 38:6

What were its foundations set on? 📖

Who laid its cornerstone,

**Help — cornerstone:** A main stone in the foundation of a building.

God pictures the earth as a building.

## Job 38:7

while the morning stars sang together? 📖

All the sons of God shouted for joy.

**Help — sons of God:** Heavenly beings.

## Job 38:8

Who shut the sea behind doors when it burst out of the womb, 📖

## Job 38:9

when I clothed it with clouds and wrapped it in thick darkness? 📖

## Job 38:10

I fixed its boundaries. 📖

I put its bars and doors in place.

## Job 38:11

I said, “You may come this far, but no farther. 📖

Here your proud waves must stop.”

**Help — birth and doors:** God pictures the sea as a newborn and as something He keeps within limits.

## Job 38:12

Have you ever commanded the morning? 📖

Have you given the dawn its place,

## Job 38:13

so that it spreads to the ends of the earth and shakes wicked people out of it? 📖

## Job 38:14

The earth takes shape like clay under a seal. 📖

Its hills stand out like folds in clothing.

**Help — seal:** An object pressed into clay to leave a pattern.

Dawn reveals the earth’s shape.

## Job 38:15

Light is kept from wicked people. 📖

Their raised arm is broken.

## Job 38:16

Have you traveled to the sea’s openings? 📖

Have you walked in the deep sea’s trenches?

**Help — trenches:** Deep, narrow hollows.

## Job 38:17

Have death’s gates been shown to you? 📖

Have you seen the gates of death’s shadow?

## Job 38:18

Have you looked over the whole width of the earth? 📖

Tell Me, if you know all this.

## Job 38:19

Where is the way to light’s home? 📖

Do you know where darkness lives,

## Job 38:20

so you can take it back to its border? 📖

Do you know the paths to its home?

## Job 38:21

Surely you know, because you were already born! 📖

You have lived so many days!

**Help — these words:** The LORD challenges Job’s limited age and knowledge.

## Job 38:22

Have you entered the snow’s storehouses? 📖

Have you seen the hail’s storehouses,

**Help — hail:** Balls of ice that fall from clouds.

## Job 38:23

which I keep ready for times of trouble? 📖

I keep them for the day of war and battle.

## Job 38:24

Which way is lightning spread? 📖

Which way is the east wind scattered across the earth?

## Job 38:25

Who cuts a channel for a flood? 📖

Who clears a path for a thunderbolt,

## Job 38:26

to bring rain on empty land, on a desert where no one lives, 📖

## Job 38:27

to satisfy the dry wasteland and make young grass grow? 📖

## Job 38:28

Does rain have a father? 📖

Who has given birth to the drops of dew?

## Job 38:29

Whose womb does ice come from? 📖

Who gives birth to frost from heaven,

## Job 38:30

when water becomes hard as stone? 📖

The surface of the deep freezes.

## Job 38:31

Can you tie the chains of the Pleiades? 📖

Can you loosen Orion’s belt?

**Help — Pleiades and Orion:** Groups of stars.

## Job 38:32

Can you bring out the groups of stars in their seasons? 📖

Can you lead out the Bear and her cubs?

**Help — Bear and cubs:** Names used for stars.

## Job 38:33

Do you know the laws of the heavens? 📖

Can you establish their rule over the earth?

## Job 38:34

Can you command the clouds so a flood of water covers you? 📖

## Job 38:35

Can you send lightning bolts on their way? 📖

Do they report to you, “Here we are”?

## Job 38:36

Who put wisdom in the heart? 📖

Who gave understanding to the mind?

## Job 38:37

Who has the wisdom to count the clouds? 📖

Who can tip heaven’s water jars,

## Job 38:38

when dust hardens into a mass and lumps of earth stick together? 📖

**Help — water jars:** A picture of clouds pouring rain.

## Job 38:39

Can you hunt food for a lioness? 📖

Can you satisfy young lions’ hunger,

## Job 38:40

when they crouch in their dens and wait in thick bushes? 📖

## Job 38:41

Who feeds the raven when its young cry out to God? 📖

They wander about because they have no food.

**Help — raven:** A large black bird.
