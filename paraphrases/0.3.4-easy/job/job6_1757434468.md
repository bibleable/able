# Job 6 — Job asks his friends for kindness

**About this chapter**

Job answers Eliphaz.

He describes his suffering and challenges his friends.

## Job 6:1

Then Job answered: 📖

## Job 6:2

If only my grief could be weighed! 📖

If only it could be put on scales together with my disaster!

## Job 6:3

It would weigh more than the sand of the seas. 📖

No wonder I have spoken without care.

## Job 6:4

The Almighty’s arrows have pierced me. 📖

My spirit drinks their poison.

God’s terrors are lined up against me.

**Help — arrows and poison:** Job pictures his suffering as an attack.

## Job 6:5

Does a wild donkey cry out when it has fresh grass? 📖

Does an ox make hungry sounds over its food?

## Job 6:6

Do people eat tasteless food without salt? 📖

Is there flavor in the white of an egg?

## Job 6:7

I refuse to touch such food. 📖

It disgusts me.

## Job 6:8

If only God would grant my request! 📖

If only He would give me what I hope for:

## Job 6:9

If only God would agree to crush me! 📖

If only He would let His hand loose and end my life!

## Job 6:10

This still comforts me and brings me joy through pain that never stops. 📖

I have not denied the Holy One’s words.

**Help — the Holy One:** God.

## Job 6:11

What strength do I have left to keep hoping? 📖

What future do I have to wait patiently for?

## Job 6:12

Am I as strong as stone? 📖

Is my flesh made of bronze?

## Job 6:13

Can I help myself now that success has been driven away from me? 📖

## Job 6:14

A friend should show kindness to a person who has lost hope. 📖

This is true even if that person stops fearing the Almighty.

**Help — fearing the Almighty:** Respecting God and taking His authority seriously.

## Job 6:15

But my brothers are as unreliable as seasonal streams. 📖

These streams overflow at certain times,

## Job 6:16

dark with ice and melting snow. 📖

## Job 6:17

But in the dry season they stop flowing. 📖

In the heat, they disappear from their channels.

## Job 6:18

Groups of travelers turn off their routes to find them. 📖

The travelers go into the wasteland and die.

## Job 6:19

The groups of travelers from Tema look for water. 📖

The travelers from Sheba hope to find it.

## Job 6:20

They are confused because they had hoped for water. 📖

When they arrive, they are disappointed.

## Job 6:21

Now you are no help to me. 📖

You see something terrible, and you are afraid.

**Help — the streams:** Job compares his friends to streams that fail people when they need water.

## Job 6:22

Have I ever asked you to give me something? 📖

Have I asked you to use your wealth to offer a bribe for me?

## Job 6:23

Have I asked you to save me from an enemy’s hand? 📖

Have I asked you to buy my freedom from cruel people?

## Job 6:24

Teach me, and I will be quiet. 📖

Show me where I have gone wrong.

## Job 6:25

Honest words can be painful! 📖

But what does your argument prove?

## Job 6:26

Do you mean to correct my words? 📖

Will you treat my hopeless cries as nothing but wind?

## Job 6:27

You would even cast lots for an orphan. 📖

You would trade away your friend.

**Help — cast lots:** Use an object drawn or thrown to make a choice.

An orphan is a child whose parents have died.

## Job 6:28

Please look at me now. 📖

Would I lie to your face?

## Job 6:29

Think again. 📖

Do not treat me unfairly.

Think again, because whether I am innocent is at stake.

## Job 6:30

Do I speak evil? 📖

Can my mouth not recognize harmful words?
