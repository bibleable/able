# Job 33 — Elihu speaks to Job about God’s correction

**About this chapter**

Elihu continues speaking.

He quotes what he says he heard Job say.

Then he describes his understanding of how God corrects and rescues people.

## Job 33:1

Now, Job, hear my speech. 📖

Listen to all my words.

## Job 33:2

Look, I will open my mouth. 📖

My speech is on the tip of my tongue.

## Job 33:3

My words come from an upright heart. 📖

My lips sincerely speak what I know.

**Help — upright:** Honest and right.

## Job 33:4

God’s Spirit has made me. 📖

The Almighty’s breath gives me life.

## Job 33:5

Prove me wrong if you can. 📖

Prepare your case and face me.

## Job 33:6

I am just like you before God. 📖

I too was formed from clay.

## Job 33:7

You should not be terrified of me. 📖

My hand will not be heavy on you.

## Job 33:8

You have spoken where I could hear you. 📖

I have heard these very words:

## Job 33:9

“I am pure and have done no wrong. 📖

I am clean, with no sin in me.

## Job 33:10

Yet God finds reasons to act against me. 📖

He counts me as His enemy.

## Job 33:11

He locks my feet in wooden blocks. 📖

He watches all my paths.”

## Job 33:12

Look, you are not right about this. 📖

I will answer you, because God is greater than a human being.

## Job 33:13

Why do you complain to Him that He answers none of a person’s questions? 📖

## Job 33:14

God speaks in one way and in another. 📖

Yet no one notices.

## Job 33:15

He speaks in dreams and visions at night. 📖

People are in deep sleep on their beds.

## Job 33:16

Then He opens their ears. 📖

He terrifies them with warnings,

## Job 33:17

to turn a person away from wrongdoing and keep him from pride. 📖

## Job 33:18

He keeps that person’s soul from the Pit. 📖

He keeps his life from ending by the sword.

**Help — the Pit:** A name for the grave or place of the dead.

## Job 33:19

A man is also corrected through pain on his bed. 📖

His bones are in constant distress.

## Job 33:20

So he hates his bread. 📖

He cannot bear even his favorite food.

## Job 33:21

His flesh wastes away until it cannot be seen. 📖

Bones that were hidden stick out.

## Job 33:22

He comes near the Pit. 📖

His life comes near death’s messengers.

## Job 33:23

But suppose there is a messenger on his side. 📖

Suppose there is one mediator in a thousand to tell him what is right for him,

**Help — mediator:** Someone who stands between two sides to help settle a matter.

## Job 33:24

and to show him kindness. 📖

The messenger says, “Spare him from going down to the Pit.

I have found his ransom.”

**Help — ransom:** A payment that sets someone free.

## Job 33:25

Then his flesh becomes fresh like a child’s. 📖

He returns to the days of his youth.

## Job 33:26

He prays to God and finds favor. 📖

He sees God’s face and shouts for joy.

God restores His righteousness to that man.

**Help — righteousness:** Being right before God.

## Job 33:27

Then he sings before people, “I sinned and twisted what was right. 📖

Yet I did not receive what I deserved.

## Job 33:28

God rescued my soul from going down to the Pit. 📖

I will live to see the light.”

## Job 33:29

God does all these things for a person two or even three times, 📖

## Job 33:30

to bring his soul back from the Pit. 📖

Then the light of life can shine on him.

## Job 33:31

Pay attention, Job, and listen to me. 📖

Be silent, and I will speak.

## Job 33:32

But if you have something to say, answer me. 📖

Speak, because I want to prove you right.

## Job 33:33

Otherwise, listen to me. 📖

Be quiet, and I will teach you wisdom.
