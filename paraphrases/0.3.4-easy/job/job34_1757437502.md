# Job 34 — Elihu argues that God is just

**About this chapter**

Elihu continues.

He quotes and challenges Job, then states his own argument about God’s justice.

## Job 34:1

Elihu continued: 📖

## Job 34:2

Wise men, hear my words. 📖

Learned men, listen to me.

## Job 34:3

The ear tests words as the mouth tastes food. 📖

## Job 34:4

Let us choose for ourselves what is right. 📖

Let us learn together what is good.

## Job 34:5

Job has said, “I am righteous, yet God has denied me justice. 📖

**Help — righteous:** Right before God.

## Job 34:6

Would I lie about my case? 📖

My wound cannot be healed, although I have done no wrong.”

## Job 34:7

Who is like Job? 📖

He drinks mockery like water.

**Help — drinks mockery:** Elihu pictures Job taking in scorn as readily as water.

## Job 34:8

He keeps company with people who do evil. 📖

He walks with wicked men.

## Job 34:9

He has said, “A man gains nothing by taking delight in God.” 📖

## Job 34:10

So listen to me, men of understanding. 📖

God would never do wrong!

The Almighty would never act unjustly!

## Job 34:11

He repays a person for his actions. 📖

He brings consequences that match a person’s ways.

## Job 34:12

It is certainly true that God does not act wickedly. 📖

The Almighty does not twist justice.

## Job 34:13

Who put Him in charge of the earth? 📖

Who appointed Him over the whole world?

## Job 34:14

If He decided to take back His Spirit and breath, 📖

## Job 34:15

all living flesh would die together. 📖

Human beings would return to dust.

## Job 34:16

If you have understanding, hear this. 📖

Listen to my words.

## Job 34:17

Could someone who hates justice govern? 📖

Will you condemn the One who is just and mighty?

## Job 34:18

He says to kings, “You are worthless!” 📖

He says to nobles, “You are wicked!”

**Help — nobles:** People with high rank.

## Job 34:19

He does not unfairly favor princes. 📖

He does not favor rich people over poor people.

They are all the work of His hands.

## Job 34:20

They die in an instant, in the middle of the night. 📖

People shake violently and pass away.

Powerful people are removed without a human hand.

## Job 34:21

His eyes watch a person’s ways. 📖

He sees every step.

## Job 34:22

There is no darkness or deep shadow where people who do evil can hide. 📖

## Job 34:23

God does not need to examine a man further. 📖

He does not need to bring him forward for judgment.

## Job 34:24

He breaks powerful people without an inquiry. 📖

He puts others in their place.

## Job 34:25

He knows what they do. 📖

He overthrows them at night, and they are crushed.

## Job 34:26

He strikes them for their wickedness where everyone can see, 📖

## Job 34:27

because they turned away from Him. 📖

They paid no attention to any of His ways.

## Job 34:28

They made poor people’s cries rise before Him. 📖

He heard the cries of those who suffered.

## Job 34:29

But when He stays silent, who can condemn Him? 📖

When He hides His face, who can see Him?

Yet He watches both people and nations,

## Job 34:30

so that men who reject God will not rule or set traps for the people. 📖

## Job 34:31

Suppose someone says to God, “I have borne my punishment. 📖

I will do wrong no more.

## Job 34:32

Teach me what I cannot see. 📖

If I have done wrong, I will not do it again.”

## Job 34:33

Should God repay you on your own terms when you have rejected Him? 📖

You must choose.

I cannot choose for you.

So tell me what you know.

## Job 34:34

Men of understanding will tell me this. 📖

Wise men who hear me will say:

## Job 34:35

“Job speaks without knowledge. 📖

His words lack understanding.”

## Job 34:36

If only Job were tested to the fullest for answering like a wicked man! 📖

## Job 34:37

He adds rebellion to his sin. 📖

He claps his hands among us.

He speaks more and more words against God.
