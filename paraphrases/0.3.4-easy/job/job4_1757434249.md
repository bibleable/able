# Job 4 — Eliphaz begins his reply

**About this chapter**

Eliphaz offers his understanding of suffering and recalls a frightening experience.

His speech includes questions, images, and words he says he heard.

## Job 4:1

Eliphaz the Temanite replied, 📖

## Job 4:2

“Would a word weary you? 📖

Yet who can remain silent?

## Job 4:3

You taught many and strengthened weak hands. 📖

## Job 4:4

Your words steadied stumbling people and braced buckling knees. 📖

## Job 4:5

Now trouble strikes you, and you are weary and dismayed. 📖

## Job 4:6

Is your respect for God not your confidence? 📖

Is your upright life not your hope?

**Help with upright:** Living in an honest and right way.

## Job 4:7

Consider: what innocent person ever perished? 📖

Where were upright people destroyed?

## Job 4:8

I see those ploughing evil and sowing trouble harvest the same. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Eliphaz compares actions and consequences to sowing and harvesting.

## Job 4:9

God’s breath destroys them; his anger’s blast consumes them. 📖

## Job 4:10

Lions roar and growl, but young lions’ teeth break. 📖

## Job 4:11

Old lions die without prey; lionesses’ cubs scatter. 📖

**Help with these lions:** Eliphaz uses animal images in his account of powerful people brought down.

## Job 4:12

A secret word reached me, a whisper in my ears. 📖

## Job 4:13

Amid disturbing night visions when people sleep deeply, 📖

## Job 4:14

fear and trembling shook all my bones. 📖

## Job 4:15

A spirit passed my face, making my body hair stand. 📖

## Job 4:16

It stood, but I could not recognise its appearance. 📖

A form was before me, and a voice whispered:

## Job 4:17

‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God, or purer than their Maker? 📖

## Job 4:18

If God distrusts servants and charges angels with error, 📖

## Job 4:19

how much more those who live in clay houses! 📖

Their foundations are in the dust.

They can be crushed like a moth!

**Help with clay houses:** A picture of fragile human life.

## Job 4:20

From dawn to dusk they are crushed, perishing forever unnoticed. 📖

## Job 4:21

Are their tent cords not pulled up, so they die without wisdom?’ 📖

**Help with tent cords:** Pulling these up pictures life ending.
