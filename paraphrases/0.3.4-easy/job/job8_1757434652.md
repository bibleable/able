# Job 8 — Bildad answers Job

**About this chapter**

Bildad now speaks.

His accusations and promises are his own claims.

The chapter records what he says to Job.

## Job 8:1

Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: 📖

## Job 8:2

How long will you keep saying these things? 📖

Your words are like a strong, noisy wind.

## Job 8:3

Does God twist justice? 📖

Does the Almighty twist what is right?

## Job 8:4

When your children sinned against Him, He handed them over to the results of their rebellion. 📖

## Job 8:5

But suppose you sincerely seek God. 📖

Suppose you ask the Almighty for mercy,

## Job 8:6

and you are pure and upright. 📖

Then even now He will act for you.

He will restore your rightful home and possessions.

**Help — upright:** Living in an honest and right way.

## Job 8:7

Your beginning was small. 📖

But your later days will be very successful.

## Job 8:8

Please ask the generations before us. 📖

Think about what their fathers discovered.

## Job 8:9

We were born only yesterday and know nothing. 📖

Our days on earth are only a shadow.

**Help — yesterday and shadow:** Bildad means that human lives and experience are very short.

## Job 8:10

Will those people not teach you and tell you? 📖

Will they not speak from what they understand?

## Job 8:11

Can papyrus grow where there is no marsh? 📖

Can reeds grow well without water?

**Help — papyrus and reeds:** Plants that grow in wet places.

A marsh is wet ground.

## Job 8:12

Before these young plants are cut, they dry up faster than grass. 📖

## Job 8:13

This is what happens to everyone who forgets God. 📖

The hope of people who reject God will die.

## Job 8:14

Such a person’s confidence is easily broken. 📖

His safety is a spider’s web.

## Job 8:15

He leans on his web, but it breaks. 📖

He holds on tightly, but it does not last.

**Help — spider’s web:** Bildad pictures this person’s trust as something too weak to support him.

## Job 8:16

He is like a plant with plenty of water in the sunshine. 📖

His shoots spread across the garden.

## Job 8:17

His roots wrap around a pile of rocks. 📖

He looks for a home among the stones.

## Job 8:18

If he is pulled out of his place, that place rejects him. 📖

It says, “I never saw you.”

## Job 8:19

Surely this is the joy his way brings! 📖

Yet other plants will grow from the dust.

**Help — the plant:** Bildad uses this picture to describe a person who forgets God.

## Job 8:20

Look, God does not reject a blameless person. 📖

He will not strengthen people who do evil.

## Job 8:21

He will yet fill your mouth with laughter. 📖

He will make your lips shout with joy.

## Job 8:22

Your enemies will be covered in shame. 📖

The tent of wicked people will be gone.
