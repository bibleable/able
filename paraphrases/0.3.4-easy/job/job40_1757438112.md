# Job 40 — Job replies, and the LORD speaks again

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks in verses 1–2.

Job answers in verses 3–5.

The LORD then continues and describes Behemoth.

## Job 40:1

The LORD said to Job: 📖

## Job 40:2

Will someone who finds fault argue with the Almighty? 📖

Let the person who argues with God answer.

## Job 40:3

Then Job answered the LORD: 📖

## Job 40:4

Look, I am insignificant. 📖

How can I answer You?

I put my hand over my mouth.

**Help — insignificant:** Very small and unimportant.

## Job 40:5

I have spoken once, but I have no answer. 📖

I have spoken twice, but I have nothing to add.

## Job 40:6

Then the LORD answered Job from the whirlwind. 📖

He said:

**Help — whirlwind:** A powerful, swirling wind.

## Job 40:7

Now prepare yourself like a man. 📖

I will ask you questions.

You must tell Me the answers.

## Job 40:8

Would you really cancel My justice? 📖

Would you condemn Me to prove yourself right?

## Job 40:9

Do you have an arm like God’s? 📖

Can your voice thunder like His?

## Job 40:10

Then decorate yourself with majesty and splendor. 📖

Clothe yourself with honor and glory.

**Help — majesty and splendor:** Great royal power and glory.

## Job 40:11

Let loose your fierce anger. 📖

Look at every proud man and bring him low.

## Job 40:12

Look at every proud man and humble him. 📖

Trample wicked people where they stand.

## Job 40:13

Bury them together in the dust. 📖

Shut them in the grave.

## Job 40:14

Then I will admit to you that your own right hand can save you. 📖

## Job 40:15

Look at Behemoth, whom I made along with you. 📖

He eats grass like an ox.

**Help — Behemoth:** The name of the powerful creature described here.

## Job 40:16

See the strength in his hips. 📖

See the power in his belly muscles.

## Job 40:17

His tail swings like a cedar tree. 📖

The sinews in his thighs are tightly joined.

**Help — sinews:** Strong bands of tissue that connect parts of the body.

## Job 40:18

His bones are tubes of bronze. 📖

His legs are rods of iron.

**Help — bronze and iron:** Pictures of the creature’s great strength.

## Job 40:19

He stands foremost among God’s works. 📖

Only his Maker can draw a sword against him.

## Job 40:20

The hills give him their crops. 📖

All the wild animals play nearby.

## Job 40:21

He lies under lotus plants. 📖

He hides among reeds in the marsh.

**Help — lotus and reeds:** Plants that grow in wet places.

A marsh is wet ground.

## Job 40:22

Lotus plants hide him in their shade. 📖

Willow trees by the stream surround him.

## Job 40:23

Even when the river rages, Behemoth is not afraid. 📖

He stays secure even when the Jordan rushes up to his mouth.

## Job 40:24

Can anyone capture him while he is watching? 📖

Can anyone pierce his nose with a snare?

**Help — snare:** A trap.
