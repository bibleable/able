# Job 16 — Job says his friends give no comfort

**About this chapter**

Job answers his friends.

He describes his suffering as an attack and asks for someone to speak for him.

## Job 16:1

Then Job answered: 📖

## Job 16:2

I have heard many things like these. 📖

You are all terrible comforters.

## Job 16:3

Will your long speeches never end? 📖

What drives you to keep arguing?

## Job 16:4

If you were in my place, I could speak like you. 📖

I could pile up words against you.

I could shake my head at you.

## Job 16:5

But I would encourage you with my words. 📖

The comfort from my lips would bring relief.

## Job 16:6

If I speak, my pain does not ease. 📖

If I keep quiet, how will it go away?

## Job 16:7

God has worn me out. 📖

You have ruined my whole family.

## Job 16:8

You have bound me, and this has become evidence against me. 📖

My weak body rises up as a witness against me.

## Job 16:9

His anger has torn me and fought against me. 📖

He grinds His teeth at me.

My enemy’s eyes pierce me.

## Job 16:10

People open their mouths against me. 📖

They strike my cheeks to shame me.

They gather together against me.

## Job 16:11

God has handed me over to unjust men. 📖

He has thrown me into wicked people’s grip.

## Job 16:12

I was at ease, but He broke me. 📖

He grabbed my neck and crushed me.

He has made me His target.

## Job 16:13

His archers surround me. 📖

He pierces my kidneys without mercy.

He spills my gall on the ground.

**Help — archers:** People who shoot arrows.

Kidneys are organs inside the body.

Gall is a bitter fluid inside the body.

Job pictures his suffering as a deadly attack.

## Job 16:14

He breaks me with wound after wound. 📖

He rushes at me like a mighty warrior.

## Job 16:15

I have sewn sackcloth over my skin. 📖

I have buried my horn in the dust.

**Help — sackcloth:** Rough cloth worn to show grief.

The horn is a picture of strength or honor brought low.

## Job 16:16

My face is red from crying. 📖

Deep shadows circle my eyes.

## Job 16:17

Yet my hands have done no violence. 📖

My prayer is pure.

## Job 16:18

Earth, do not cover my blood! 📖

May my cry for help never be silenced!

## Job 16:19

Even now, my witness is in heaven. 📖

The one who speaks for me is on high.

## Job 16:20

My friends mock me. 📖

My eyes pour out tears to God.

## Job 16:21

If only a man could plead with God as he pleads with his neighbor! 📖

## Job 16:22

Only a few years will pass. 📖

Then I will go on the journey from which no one returns.
