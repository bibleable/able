# Job 28 — Where can wisdom be found?

**About this chapter**

The speech turns to a poem about wisdom.

It compares searching for treasure with searching for understanding.

## Job 28:1

There is a mine for silver. 📖

There is a place where gold is purified.

## Job 28:2

Iron is taken from the earth. 📖

Copper is melted out of ore.

**Help — ore:** Rock containing metal.

## Job 28:3

People put an end to darkness. 📖

They search the deepest, darkest places for ore.

## Job 28:4

Far from where people live, a miner cuts a deep shaft. 📖

Human feet have forgotten that place.

Far from people, he hangs and swings.

**Help — shaft:** A deep opening made to reach a mine.

## Job 28:5

The earth produces food above. 📖

But below, it is changed as if by fire.

## Job 28:6

Its rocks contain sapphires and small pieces of gold. 📖

**Help — sapphires:** Valuable stones.

## Job 28:7

No hunting bird knows that path. 📖

No falcon’s eye has seen it.

**Help — falcon:** A bird that hunts other animals.

## Job 28:8

Proud wild animals have never walked there. 📖

No lion has ever roamed there.

## Job 28:9

The miner strikes the flint. 📖

He overturns mountains at their base.

**Help — flint:** A hard kind of stone.

## Job 28:10

He cuts channels through rock. 📖

His eyes spot every treasure.

## Job 28:11

He blocks the sources of streams. 📖

He brings hidden things into the light.

## Job 28:12

But where can wisdom be found? 📖

Where does understanding live?

## Job 28:13

No one can know its value. 📖

It is not found in the land of the living.

## Job 28:14

The ocean depths say, “It is not in me.” 📖

The sea says, “It is not with me.”

## Job 28:15

It cannot be bought with gold. 📖

Its price cannot be weighed out in silver.

## Job 28:16

It cannot be valued in gold from Ophir. 📖

It cannot be valued in precious onyx or sapphire.

**Help — Ophir:** A place known for gold.

Onyx and sapphire are valuable stones.

## Job 28:17

Neither gold nor crystal can compare with it. 📖

Jewels of fine gold cannot be traded for it.

**Help — crystal:** A clear, valuable stone.

## Job 28:18

Coral and quartz are not worth mentioning. 📖

Wisdom’s price is greater than rubies.

**Help — coral, quartz, and rubies:** Materials used for valuable objects and jewelry.

## Job 28:19

Topaz from Cush cannot compare with it. 📖

It cannot be valued in pure gold.

**Help — topaz:** A valuable stone.

Cush is a place name.

## Job 28:20

Where then does wisdom come from? 📖

Where does understanding live?

## Job 28:21

It is hidden from every living thing’s eyes. 📖

It is hidden from the birds of the sky.

## Job 28:22

Abaddon and Death say, “We have heard a rumor about it.” 📖

**Help — Abaddon:** A name connected with destruction and the place of the dead.

The poem pictures Abaddon and Death speaking.

## Job 28:23

But God understands the way to wisdom. 📖

He knows its place.

## Job 28:24

He looks to the ends of the earth. 📖

He sees everything under the heavens.

## Job 28:25

God fixed the weight of the wind. 📖

He measured out the waters.

## Job 28:26

He set a limit for the rain. 📖

He set a path for the thunderbolt.

## Job 28:27

Then He looked at wisdom and assessed it. 📖

He established it and searched it out.

## Job 28:28

He said to people, “Look, fearing the Lord is wisdom. 📖

Turning away from evil is understanding.”

**Help — fearing the Lord:** Respecting God and taking His authority seriously.
