# Job 27 — Job holds to his innocence

**About this chapter**

Job continues speaking.

He insists on his integrity and describes judgment on wicked people.

## Job 27:1

Job continued his speech: 📖

## Job 27:2

As surely as God lives, I make this promise. 📖

He has denied me justice.

The Almighty has made my heart bitter.

## Job 27:3

As long as I still have breath, as long as God’s breath remains in my nostrils, 📖

## Job 27:4

my lips will not speak evil. 📖

My tongue will not tell lies.

## Job 27:5

I will never say you are right. 📖

I will hold to my integrity until I die.

**Help — integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

## Job 27:6

I will hold tightly to my righteousness and never let go. 📖

As long as I live, my conscience will not accuse me.

**Help — righteousness:** Being right before God.

Conscience is the sense of whether your own actions are right or wrong.

## Job 27:7

May my enemy be like a wicked person. 📖

May my opponent be like someone who is unjust.

## Job 27:8

What hope does someone who rejects God have when he is cut off? 📖

What hope is left when God takes his life?

## Job 27:9

Will God hear his cry when trouble comes? 📖

## Job 27:10

Will he take delight in the Almighty? 📖

Will he call on God at all times?

## Job 27:11

I will teach you about God’s power. 📖

I will not hide the Almighty’s ways.

## Job 27:12

All of you have surely seen this yourselves. 📖

Why then do you keep talking so emptily?

## Job 27:13

This is what God assigns to a wicked man. 📖

This is the inheritance cruel people receive from the Almighty.

## Job 27:14

He may have many sons, but they are destined for the sword. 📖

His children will never have enough food.

## Job 27:15

Those who survive him will die and be buried because of plague. 📖

Their widows will not weep for them.

**Help — plague:** A deadly outbreak of disease.

Widows are women whose husbands have died.

## Job 27:16

He may pile up silver like dust. 📖

He may pile up clothing like clay.

## Job 27:17

But righteous people will wear the clothing he stores. 📖

Innocent people will divide his silver.

## Job 27:18

The house he builds is like a moth’s cocoon. 📖

It is like a hut put up by a watchman.

**Help — cocoon:** A covering around an insect as it changes.

These pictures describe a weak or temporary home.

## Job 27:19

He lies down rich, but he will not do so again. 📖

When he opens his eyes, everything is gone.

## Job 27:20

Terrors catch him like a flood. 📖

A violent storm sweeps him away in the night.

## Job 27:21

The east wind carries him away, and he is gone. 📖

It sweeps him out of his place.

## Job 27:22

It throws itself against him without mercy. 📖

He runs desperately from its power.

## Job 27:23

It claps its hands at him. 📖

It hisses him out of his place.

**Help — the wind:** Job pictures it acting like someone who drives a person away with contempt.
