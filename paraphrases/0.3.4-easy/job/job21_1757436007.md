# Job 21 — Job challenges his friends’ account of evil

**About this chapter**

Job answers Zophar.

He points to wicked people who seem to live safely and successfully.

## Job 21:1

Then Job answered: 📖

## Job 21:2

Listen carefully to my words. 📖

Let that be the comfort you give me.

## Job 21:3

Be patient with me while I speak. 📖

After I have spoken, you can keep mocking.

## Job 21:4

Is my complaint against a human being? 📖

Then why should I not be impatient?

## Job 21:5

Look at me and be horrified. 📖

Put your hand over your mouth.

## Job 21:6

When I remember, terror takes hold of me. 📖

My body shakes with horror.

## Job 21:7

Why do wicked people keep living? 📖

Why do they grow old and become more powerful?

## Job 21:8

Their descendants are settled around them. 📖

Their children are before their eyes.

**Help — descendants:** Children and the generations born after them.

## Job 21:9

Their homes are safe from fear. 📖

God’s rod of punishment does not strike them.

## Job 21:10

Their bulls mate successfully. 📖

Their cows give birth to calves and do not lose their young before birth.

## Job 21:11

They send their little ones out like a flock. 📖

Their children skip about,

## Job 21:12

singing with the tambourine and lyre. 📖

They enjoy the sound of the flute.

**Help — instruments:** A tambourine is shaken or struck.

A lyre has strings.

A flute is played by blowing.

## Job 21:13

They spend their days with wealth and success. 📖

They go down to Sheol in peace.

**Help — Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Job 21:14

Yet they say to God, “Leave us alone! 📖

We do not want to know Your ways.

## Job 21:15

Who is the Almighty, that we should serve Him? 📖

What would we gain by praying to Him?”

## Job 21:16

Still, their success is not in their own hands. 📖

So I stay far from wicked people’s advice.

## Job 21:17

How often is a wicked person’s lamp put out? 📖

Does disaster fall on them?

Does God assign destruction in His anger?

## Job 21:18

Are they like straw before the wind? 📖

Are they like chaff swept away by a storm?

**Help — chaff:** Light plant parts separated from grain.

These questions challenge the friends’ claims that wicked people are quickly swept away.

## Job 21:19

People say God saves a man’s punishment for his children. 📖

Let God repay the man himself so he knows it!

## Job 21:20

Let his own eyes see his destruction. 📖

Let him drink the Almighty’s anger for himself!

**Help — drink anger:** Experience punishment.

## Job 21:21

What does he care about his household after he is gone? 📖

The number of his months has run out.

## Job 21:22

Can anyone teach God knowledge? 📖

He judges those on high.

## Job 21:23

One man dies full of strength. 📖

He is completely safe and at ease.

## Job 21:24

His body is well fed. 📖

His bones are full of rich marrow.

**Help — marrow:** Soft tissue inside bones.

## Job 21:25

Another man dies with a bitter heart. 📖

He has never enjoyed wealth or success.

## Job 21:26

But both lie down together in the dust. 📖

Worms cover them both.

## Job 21:27

Look, I know your thoughts very well. 📖

I know the plans you use to wrong me.

## Job 21:28

You say, “Where is the nobleman’s house now? 📖

Where are the tents where wicked people live?”

**Help — nobleman:** A man with high rank.

## Job 21:29

Have you never asked people who travel the roads? 📖

Do you not accept what they report?

## Job 21:30

An evil man is spared on the day of disaster. 📖

He is rescued on the day of anger.

## Job 21:31

Who tells him to his face that his behavior is wrong? 📖

Who repays him for what he has done?

## Job 21:32

He is carried to the grave. 📖

People keep watch over his tomb.

## Job 21:33

The valley’s lumps of earth are sweet to him. 📖

Everyone follows him.

Countless people have gone before him.

## Job 21:34

So how can you comfort me with empty words? 📖

Your answers are still full of lies.
