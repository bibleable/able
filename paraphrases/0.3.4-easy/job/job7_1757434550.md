# Job 7 — Job speaks to God about his pain

**About this chapter**

Job continues speaking.

He turns to God with questions and a wish to die.

These words express Job’s distress.

## Job 7:1

Are people not given hard work on earth? 📖

Are their days not like a hired worker’s days?

## Job 7:2

A slave longs for shade. 📖

A hired worker waits for pay.

## Job 7:3

In the same way, I have been given months that seem useless. 📖

Nights of misery have been assigned to me.

## Job 7:4

When I lie down, I think, “When will I get up?” 📖

But the night drags on.

I turn from side to side until dawn.

## Job 7:5

Worms and hard dirt cover my flesh. 📖

My skin is cracked and full of infected sores.

## Job 7:6

My days move faster than a weaver’s shuttle. 📖

They end without hope.

**Help — weaver’s shuttle:** A tool moved quickly back and forth to make cloth.

## Job 7:7

Remember that my life is only a breath. 📖

My eyes will never see happiness again.

**Help — a breath:** Job means that his life is very short.

## Job 7:8

The eyes that see me now will no longer see me. 📖

You will look for me, but I will be gone.

## Job 7:9

A cloud fades away and disappears. 📖

In the same way, someone who goes down to Sheol does not come back up.

**Help — Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Job 7:10

That person never returns home. 📖

His place remembers him no more.

## Job 7:11

So I will not hold back my words. 📖

I will speak from my deep distress.

I will complain from my bitter heart.

## Job 7:12

Am I the sea or a monster of the deep? 📖

Is that why You must guard me?

## Job 7:13

I think my bed will comfort me. 📖

I think my couch will ease my complaint.

## Job 7:14

But then You frighten me with dreams. 📖

You terrify me with visions.

## Job 7:15

So I would choose strangling and death over life in this body. 📖

## Job 7:16

I hate my life! 📖

I would not live forever.

Leave me alone.

My days are only a breath.

## Job 7:17

What are human beings, that You make so much of them? 📖

Why do You give them Your attention,

## Job 7:18

visit them every morning, and test them every moment? 📖

## Job 7:19

Will You never look away from me? 📖

Will You not leave me alone long enough to swallow my saliva?

## Job 7:20

If I have sinned, what have I done to You, watcher of mankind? 📖

Why have You made me Your target?

Why am I a burden to You?

## Job 7:21

Why do You not forgive my wrongdoing and take away my sin? 📖

Soon I will lie down in the dust.

You will look for me, but I will be gone.
