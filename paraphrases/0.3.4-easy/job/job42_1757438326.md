# Job 42 — Job answers, and the LORD restores him

**About this chapter**

Job answers the LORD in verses 1–6.

The LORD then addresses Eliphaz and his friends.

The narrator tells what happens afterward.

## Job 42:1

Then Job answered the LORD: 📖

## Job 42:2

I know You can do all things. 📖

No plan of Yours can be stopped.

## Job 42:3

You asked, “Who hides My counsel without knowledge?” 📖

I spoke about things I did not understand.

They were too wonderful for me to know.

**Help — counsel:** God’s wise plans and purpose.

## Job 42:4

You said, “Listen now, and I will speak. 📖

I will ask you questions.

You must tell Me the answers.”

## Job 42:5

My ears had heard of You. 📖

But now my eyes have seen You.

## Job 42:6

So I despise myself. 📖

I repent in dust and ashes.

**Help — repent:** Turn away from wrongdoing.

Dust and ashes show grief and humility.

## Job 42:7

After speaking to Job, the LORD spoke to Eliphaz the Temanite. 📖

He said, “My anger burns against you and your two friends.

You have not spoken accurately about Me, as My servant Job has.

## Job 42:8

Now take seven bulls and seven rams. 📖

Go to My servant Job.

Offer a burnt offering for yourselves.

My servant Job will pray for you.

I will accept his prayer.

I will not treat you as your foolishness deserves.

You have not spoken accurately about Me, as My servant Job has.”

**Help — burnt offering:** An animal sacrifice burned as an offering to God.

## Job 42:9

Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite went and obeyed the LORD. 📖

The LORD accepted Job’s prayer.

## Job 42:10

After Job prayed for his friends, the LORD restored his wealth and success. 📖

He gave Job twice as many possessions as before.

## Job 42:11

All Job’s brothers, sisters, and former acquaintances came to eat with him in his house. 📖

They comforted him about all the trouble the LORD had brought on him.

Each gave him a piece of silver and a gold ring.

## Job 42:12

The LORD blessed Job’s later days more than his first days. 📖

Job owned 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 pairs of oxen, and 1,000 female donkeys.

## Job 42:13

He also had seven sons and three daughters. 📖

## Job 42:14

He named his first daughter Jemimah. 📖

He named his second Keziah.

He named his third Keren-happuch.

## Job 42:15

No women in the land were as beautiful as Job’s daughters. 📖

Their father gave them an inheritance along with their brothers.

## Job 42:16

After this, Job lived 140 years. 📖

He saw his children and their children down to the fourth generation.

## Job 42:17

So Job died, old and full of years. 📖
