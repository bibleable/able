# Job 15 — Eliphaz accuses Job again

**About this chapter**

Eliphaz speaks again.

The accusations and account of wicked people’s fate are his claims.

## Job 15:1

Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: 📖

## Job 15:2

Does a wise man answer with empty advice? 📖

Does he fill his belly with the hot east wind?

**Help — hot east wind:** Eliphaz compares Job’s words to hot air with no value.

## Job 15:3

Should he argue with useless words? 📖

Should he make speeches that do no good?

## Job 15:4

You even weaken respect for God. 📖

You hinder quiet thought before Him.

## Job 15:5

Your sin teaches your mouth what to say. 📖

You choose the language of people who use tricks.

## Job 15:6

Your own mouth condemns you. 📖

Mine does not.

Your own lips speak against you.

## Job 15:7

Were you the first person ever born? 📖

Were you born before the hills?

## Job 15:8

Do you listen in on God’s council? 📖

Does wisdom belong only to you?

**Help — council:** A gathering for discussion or advice.

## Job 15:9

What do you know that we do not know? 📖

What do you understand that is not clear to us?

## Job 15:10

People with gray hair and people of great age are on our side. 📖

They are much older than your father.

## Job 15:11

Is God’s comfort not enough for you? 📖

Are gently spoken words not enough?

## Job 15:12

Why has your heart carried you away? 📖

Why do your eyes flash,

## Job 15:13

so that you turn your spirit against God? 📖

Why do you pour such words from your mouth?

## Job 15:14

What is a human being, that he could be pure? 📖

How could someone born of a woman be righteous?

**Help — righteous:** Right before God.

## Job 15:15

God puts no trust in His holy ones. 📖

Even the heavens are not pure in His eyes.

**Help — holy ones:** Heavenly beings.

Eliphaz is stating his argument.

## Job 15:16

How much less pure is a human being! 📖

He is disgusting and corrupt.

He drinks injustice like water.

**Help — drinks injustice:** Eliphaz pictures people doing wrong as readily as drinking water.

## Job 15:17

Listen to me, and I will tell you. 📖

I will describe what I have seen,

## Job 15:18

what wise men declared and did not hide from their fathers. 📖

## Job 15:19

The land was given to them alone. 📖

No foreigner passed among them.

## Job 15:20

A wicked man twists in pain all his days. 📖

Only a few years are left for a cruel man.

## Job 15:21

Terrifying sounds fill his ears. 📖

While he is successful, the destroyer attacks him.

## Job 15:22

He has no hope of returning from darkness. 📖

He is marked out for the sword.

## Job 15:23

He wanders about as food for vultures. 📖

He knows the day of darkness is near.

**Help — vultures:** Birds that eat dead animals.

## Job 15:24

Trouble and distress terrify him. 📖

They overpower him like a king ready to attack.

## Job 15:25

He has stretched out his hand against God. 📖

He has proudly challenged the Almighty,

## Job 15:26

rushing straight at Him with a thick shield covered in studs. 📖

**Help — studs:** Hard pieces fixed to the shield.

## Job 15:27

His face may be covered with fat. 📖

His waist may bulge with flesh.

## Job 15:28

But he will live in ruined cities. 📖

He will live in empty houses that are going to become piles of broken stone.

## Job 15:29

He will no longer be rich. 📖

His wealth will not last.

His possessions will not spread across the land.

## Job 15:30

He will not escape the darkness. 📖

Flames will dry up his shoots.

The breath from God’s mouth will carry him away.

**Help — shoots:** Eliphaz pictures the wicked person as a plant being destroyed.

## Job 15:31

He must not deceive himself by trusting in emptiness. 📖

Emptiness will be his reward.

## Job 15:32

He will receive that reward in full before his time. 📖

His branch will not grow well.

## Job 15:33

He will be like a vine stripped of grapes before they are ripe. 📖

He will be like an olive tree losing its flowers.

## Job 15:34

The group that rejects God will produce nothing. 📖

Fire will burn up the tents of people who take bribes.

**Help — bribes:** Gifts given to make someone act dishonestly.

## Job 15:35

They become pregnant with trouble and give birth to evil. 📖

Their womb carries lies.

**Help — pregnancy and birth:** Eliphaz pictures these people planning and producing evil.
