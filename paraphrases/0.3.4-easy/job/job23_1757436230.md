# Job 23 — Job longs to find God

**About this chapter**

Job answers Eliphaz.

He wants to put his case before God, but cannot find Him.

## Job 23:1

Then Job answered: 📖

## Job 23:2

Even today my complaint is bitter. 📖

God’s hand is heavy on me despite my groaning.

## Job 23:3

If only I knew where to find Him! 📖

Then I could go to His seat.

## Job 23:4

I would present my case before Him. 📖

I would fill my mouth with arguments.

## Job 23:5

I would learn how He would answer. 📖

I would think about what He said.

## Job 23:6

Would He fight against me with His great power? 📖

No, He would certainly pay attention to me.

## Job 23:7

Then an upright man could reason with Him. 📖

I would be freed forever from my Judge.

**Help — upright:** Living in an honest and right way.

## Job 23:8

If I go east, He is not there. 📖

If I go west, I cannot find Him.

## Job 23:9

When He works in the north, I cannot see Him. 📖

When He turns south, I cannot see Him.

## Job 23:10

Yet He knows the way I have taken. 📖

When He has tested me, I will come out like gold.

**Help — like gold:** Job pictures himself being proved true, as gold is tested for purity.

## Job 23:11

My feet have followed His tracks. 📖

I have kept His way without turning aside.

## Job 23:12

I have not turned away from His commands. 📖

I have treasured His words more than my daily bread.

## Job 23:13

But He does not change. 📖

Who can oppose Him?

He does what He wants.

## Job 23:14

He carries out what He has decided against me. 📖

He has many plans like this.

## Job 23:15

So I am terrified in His presence. 📖

When I think about this, I fear Him.

## Job 23:16

God has made my heart weak with fear. 📖

The Almighty has terrified me.

## Job 23:17

Yet the darkness has not silenced me. 📖

The thick darkness covering my face has not silenced me.
