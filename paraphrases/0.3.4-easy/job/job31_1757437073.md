# Job 31 — Job gives his final defense

**About this chapter**

Job lists wrongs he denies committing.

He calls down consequences on himself if he has done them.

These conditional statements include difficult punishments.

## Job 31:1

I made a covenant with my eyes. 📖

How then could I look with desire at a virgin?

**Help — covenant:** A serious agreement.

A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

## Job 31:2

What portion comes from God above? 📖

What inheritance comes from the Almighty on high?

## Job 31:3

Does disaster not come to unjust people? 📖

Does trouble not come to people who do evil?

## Job 31:4

Does He not see my ways? 📖

Does He not count every step?

## Job 31:5

If I have walked in falsehood, or hurried to deceive, 📖

## Job 31:6

let God weigh me on honest scales. 📖

Then He will know my integrity.

**Help — scales:** Job pictures God judging him fairly.

Integrity means being honest and holding to what is right.

## Job 31:7

If I left the path, if my heart followed my eyes, or if evil stuck to my hands, 📖

## Job 31:8

then let someone else eat what I planted. 📖

Let my crops be pulled up.

## Job 31:9

If my heart has been drawn to my neighbor’s wife, or if I have waited secretly at his door, 📖

## Job 31:10

let my own wife grind grain for another man. 📖

Let other men sleep with her.

## Job 31:11

That would be a terrible crime. 📖

It would be a sin deserving judgment.

## Job 31:12

It is a fire that burns down to Abaddon. 📖

It would destroy my whole harvest.

**Help — Abaddon:** A name connected with destruction and the place of the dead.

Job pictures this sin as a destructive fire.

## Job 31:13

If I rejected my male or female servant’s case when they complained against me, 📖

## Job 31:14

what would I do when God rose to judge? 📖

How would I answer when He called me to account?

## Job 31:15

Did the One who made me in the womb not also make them? 📖

Did the same One not form us?

## Job 31:16

If I refused what poor people needed, or let a widow’s eyes fail, 📖

**Help — widow’s eyes fail:** Let her wait for help until her hope was exhausted.

## Job 31:17

if I ate my food alone without sharing with a child who had no father, 📖

## Job 31:18

although from my youth I raised him like a father. 📖

From my mother’s womb I guided the widow.

**Help — from my mother’s womb:** Job’s way of saying he had cared for widows from his earliest days.

## Job 31:19

If I saw someone dying for lack of clothing, or a needy man without a cloak, 📖

## Job 31:20

if his heart did not bless me for warming him with wool from my sheep, 📖

## Job 31:21

if I raised my hand against a child without a father because I had support at the city gate, 📖

**Help — city gate:** A place where legal cases were heard.

## Job 31:22

let my arm fall from my shoulder! 📖

Let it be torn from its socket!

## Job 31:23

Disaster from God terrifies me. 📖

I cannot overpower His splendor.

**Help — splendor:** God’s great glory.

## Job 31:24

If I trusted in gold, or called pure gold my security, 📖

## Job 31:25

if I rejoiced in my great wealth because my own hand had gained so much, 📖

## Job 31:26

if I looked at the sun shining brightly or the moon moving in glory, 📖

## Job 31:27

and my heart was secretly drawn to them, and my hand blew them a kiss, 📖

## Job 31:28

that too would be a sin deserving judgment. 📖

I would have denied God on high.

## Job 31:29

If I rejoiced when my enemy was ruined, or celebrated when evil came to him, 📖

## Job 31:30

I have not let my mouth sin by cursing him and asking for his life. 📖

## Job 31:31

If the men of my house have not said, “Who has not eaten his fill?” 📖

## Job 31:32

No stranger had to sleep in the street. 📖

My door has been open to travelers.

## Job 31:33

If I covered my offenses like Adam, hiding my guilt in my heart, 📖

## Job 31:34

because I greatly feared crowds and the clans’ scorn terrified me, 📖

if that kept me silent and indoors,

**Help — clans:** Groups of related families.

## Job 31:35

If only someone would hear me! 📖

Here is my signature.

Let the Almighty answer me!

Let my accuser write out his charges.

## Job 31:36

I would carry those charges on my shoulder. 📖

I would wear them like a crown.

## Job 31:37

I would account for all my steps. 📖

I would approach Him like a prince.

## Job 31:38

If my land cries out against me, and its furrows weep together, 📖

**Help — furrows:** Lines cut in soil for planting.

Job pictures the land itself complaining.

## Job 31:39

if I ate its crops without paying, or broke the spirit of its tenants, 📖

**Help — tenants:** People who use land belonging to someone else.

## Job 31:40

let thorn bushes grow instead of wheat! 📖

Let stinkweed grow instead of barley!

This is the end of Job’s words.

**Help — stinkweed:** A bad-smelling plant.
