# Job 19 — Job asks for pity and speaks of his Redeemer

**About this chapter**

Job answers Bildad.

He describes rejection, asks his friends for pity, and expresses hope in his Redeemer.

## Job 19:1

Then Job answered: 📖

## Job 19:2

How long will you torment me? 📖

How long will you crush me with your words?

## Job 19:3

You have blamed me ten times now. 📖

You mistreat me without shame.

## Job 19:4

Even if I really have gone wrong, my error concerns me alone. 📖

## Job 19:5

Suppose you really want to raise yourselves above me. 📖

Suppose you use my shame against me.

## Job 19:6

Then understand that God has wronged me. 📖

He has drawn His net around me.

## Job 19:7

I cry out, “Violence!” 📖

But no one answers.

I call for help, but there is no justice.

## Job 19:8

He has blocked my way so I cannot pass. 📖

He has covered my paths with darkness.

## Job 19:9

He has stripped away my honor. 📖

He has taken the crown from my head.

## Job 19:10

He tears me down on every side until I am gone. 📖

He pulls up my hope like a tree.

## Job 19:11

His anger burns against me. 📖

He counts me among His enemies.

## Job 19:12

His troops advance together. 📖

They build an attack ramp against me.

They camp around my tent.

**Help — ramp:** A slope built to help soldiers attack.

Job pictures God attacking him with an army.

## Job 19:13

He has taken my brothers far from me. 📖

People I know have abandoned me.

## Job 19:14

My relatives have failed me. 📖

My friends have forgotten me.

## Job 19:15

My guests and female servants treat me as a stranger. 📖

They see me as a foreigner.

## Job 19:16

I call my servant, but he does not answer. 📖

I beg him with my own mouth.

## Job 19:17

My wife finds my breath disgusting. 📖

My own family cannot bear me.

## Job 19:18

Even little boys look down on me. 📖

When I appear, they mock me.

## Job 19:19

All my closest friends hate me. 📖

The people I love have turned against me.

## Job 19:20

My skin and flesh cling to my bones. 📖

I have escaped by the skin of my teeth.

**Help — skin of my teeth:** Job means that he has only just survived.

## Job 19:21

Have pity on me, my friends! 📖

Have pity, because God’s hand has struck me!

## Job 19:22

Why do you attack me as God does? 📖

Will you never have enough of my flesh?

**Help — my flesh:** Job pictures their attacks as eating him.

## Job 19:23

If only my words were recorded! 📖

If only they were written in a book,

## Job 19:24

written on lead with an iron tool, or carved in stone forever! 📖

**Help — lead:** A soft metal that can be written on.

## Job 19:25

But I know that my Redeemer lives. 📖

In the end, He will stand on the earth.

**Help — Redeemer:** Someone who rescues another person and defends that person’s rights.

## Job 19:26

Even after my skin has been destroyed, I will see God in my flesh. 📖

## Job 19:27

I will see Him for myself. 📖

My own eyes will see Him, and not as a stranger.

My heart longs so much for this!

## Job 19:28

Suppose you say, “Let us attack him. 📖

The root of the problem lies with him.”

## Job 19:29

Then you should fear the sword yourselves. 📖

Anger brings punishment by the sword.

Then you will know that there is a judgment.
