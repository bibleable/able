# Job 20 — Zophar describes punishment for evil

**About this chapter**

Zophar answers Job.

The description of punishment is Zophar’s argument.

## Job 20:1

Then Zophar the Naamathite answered: 📖

## Job 20:2

My worried thoughts force me to answer. 📖

I am deeply troubled inside.

## Job 20:3

I have heard a correction that insults me. 📖

My understanding moves me to reply.

## Job 20:4

Do you not know what has been true since ancient times? 📖

It has been true since people were placed on earth:

## Job 20:5

Wicked people’s victory has been brief. 📖

The joy of people who reject God lasts only a moment.

## Job 20:6

A wicked man’s pride may reach the heavens. 📖

His head may touch the clouds.

## Job 20:7

But he will disappear forever like his own dung. 📖

People who saw him will ask, “Where is he?”

**Help — dung:** Solid body waste.

## Job 20:8

He will fly away like a dream and never be found. 📖

He will be chased away like a vision in the night.

## Job 20:9

The eyes that saw him will see him no more. 📖

His place will no longer see him.

## Job 20:10

His sons will seek poor people’s favor. 📖

His own hands must give back his wealth.

## Job 20:11

The strength of youth that fills his bones will lie down with him in the dust. 📖

## Job 20:12

Evil may taste sweet in his mouth. 📖

He may hide it under his tongue,

## Job 20:13

unwilling to let it go, keeping it in his mouth. 📖

## Job 20:14

But his food turns sour in his stomach. 📖

Inside him it becomes cobra poison.

**Help — cobra:** A poisonous snake.

Zophar pictures evil as pleasant food that becomes poison.

## Job 20:15

He swallows wealth but vomits it out. 📖

God will force it out of his stomach.

## Job 20:16

He will suck cobra poison. 📖

A viper’s fangs will kill him.

**Help — viper and fangs:** A poisonous snake and its sharp teeth.

## Job 20:17

He will not enjoy the streams or the rivers flowing with honey and cream. 📖

**Help — honey and cream:** A picture of rich, plentiful good things.

## Job 20:18

He must give back what he worked for without eating it. 📖

He cannot enjoy the profit from his trading.

## Job 20:19

He has treated poor people cruelly and abandoned them. 📖

He has taken houses he did not build.

## Job 20:20

His appetite is never satisfied. 📖

So he cannot escape with his treasure.

## Job 20:21

Nothing is left for him to eat. 📖

So his wealth and success will not last.

## Job 20:22

Even while he has plenty, he will be in distress. 📖

Misery will fall on him with full force.

## Job 20:23

When he has filled his stomach, God will pour His fierce anger on him. 📖

God will rain it on him as he eats.

## Job 20:24

He may flee from an iron weapon. 📖

But an arrow with a bronze point will pierce him.

## Job 20:25

The arrow is pulled out of his back. 📖

Its shining point comes out of his liver.

Terrors fall on him.

**Help — liver:** An organ inside the body.

## Job 20:26

Complete darkness is kept for his treasures. 📖

A fire that no one fans will burn him up.

It will burn whatever remains in his tent.

## Job 20:27

The heavens will reveal his evil. 📖

The earth will rise against him.

## Job 20:28

His household’s possessions will be taken away. 📖

They will flow away on the day of God’s anger.

## Job 20:29

This is what God assigns to a wicked man. 📖

This is the inheritance God has chosen for him.
