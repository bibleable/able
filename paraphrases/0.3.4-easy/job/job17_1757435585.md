# Job 17 — Job sees little hope

**About this chapter**

Job continues speaking in distress.

He asks God to stand behind his case and challenges his friends.

## Job 17:1

My spirit is broken. 📖

My days are going out like a light.

The grave waits for me.

## Job 17:2

People who mock me surround me. 📖

My eyes must look at their rebellion.

## Job 17:3

Please give me the guarantee You demand, God. 📖

Who else will guarantee my case?

**Help — guarantee:** A promise or pledge that gives security.

## Job 17:4

You have closed their minds to understanding. 📖

So You will not raise them up.

## Job 17:5

If a man speaks against his friends for money, his children’s eyes will fail. 📖

## Job 17:6

God has made me a name that people use with scorn. 📖

They spit in my face.

## Job 17:7

My eyes have grown dim with grief. 📖

My whole body is only a shadow.

**Help — a shadow:** Job means his body has become very thin and weak.

## Job 17:8

Upright people are horrified by this. 📖

Innocent people are stirred up against those who reject God.

**Help — upright:** Living in an honest and right way.

## Job 17:9

Yet a righteous person keeps to his way. 📖

A person with clean hands grows stronger.

**Help — righteous and clean hands:** Doing what is right and not doing evil.

## Job 17:10

But come back and try again, all of you! 📖

I will not find a wise man among you.

## Job 17:11

My days have passed. 📖

My plans are broken off.

Even my heart’s desires are broken off.

## Job 17:12

They turn night into day. 📖

In the face of darkness, they say light is near.

## Job 17:13

Suppose I look to Sheol as my home. 📖

Suppose I spread my bed in darkness,

**Help — Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Job 17:14

and say to decay, “You are my father.” 📖

Suppose I call the worm “My mother” or “My sister.”

## Job 17:15

Where then is my hope? 📖

Who can see any hope for me?

## Job 17:16

Will my hope go down to Sheol’s gates? 📖

Will we go down into the dust together?
