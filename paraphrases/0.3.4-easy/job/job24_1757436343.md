# Job 24 — Job describes injustice and questions delayed judgment

**About this chapter**

Job continues speaking.

He describes people suffering under injustice and asks about God’s judgment.

## Job 24:1

Why does the Almighty not set times for judgment? 📖

Why do people who know Him never see His days of judgment?

## Job 24:2

Men move boundary stones. 📖

They feed stolen flocks in pastures.

**Help — boundary stones:** Stones marking where someone’s land ends.

Moving them steals land.

## Job 24:3

They drive away the donkey of a child without a father. 📖

They take a widow’s ox as a guarantee for debt.

**Help — widow:** A woman whose husband has died.

## Job 24:4

They push needy people off the road. 📖

They force all the poor people in the land to hide.

## Job 24:5

Like wild donkeys in the desert, poor people search for food as their work. 📖

The wasteland provides food for their children.

## Job 24:6

They gather animal food in the fields. 📖

They gather leftover grapes in wicked people’s vineyards.

## Job 24:7

They spend the night naked, without clothing. 📖

They have no covering against the cold.

## Job 24:8

Mountain rain soaks them. 📖

They crowd against rocks because they have no shelter.

## Job 24:9

A baby without a father is snatched from its mother’s breast. 📖

A poor person’s nursing child is taken to pay a debt.

## Job 24:10

They wander naked, without clothing. 📖

They carry bundles of grain but still go hungry.

## Job 24:11

They crush olives inside their walls. 📖

They tread grapes in winepresses but go thirsty.

**Help — winepresses:** Places where grapes are crushed to make wine.

## Job 24:12

Men groan from the city. 📖

Wounded people cry out.

Yet God charges no one with doing wrong.

## Job 24:13

Others rebel against the light. 📖

They do not know its ways or stay on its paths.

## Job 24:14

When daylight is gone, the murderer gets up to kill poor and needy people. 📖

At night he is like a thief.

## Job 24:15

The adulterer watches for twilight. 📖

He thinks, “No eye will see me.”

He covers his face.

**Help — adulterer:** Someone who has sex with another person’s husband or wife.

## Job 24:16

In the dark, they break through houses. 📖

During the day, they shut themselves in.

They never experience the light.

## Job 24:17

Deep darkness is their morning. 📖

They are surely friends with the terrors of darkness!

## Job 24:18

They are only foam on the water’s surface. 📖

Their share of the land is cursed.

No one turns toward their vineyards.

## Job 24:19

Dry weather and heat consume melting snow. 📖

In the same way, Sheol takes people who have sinned.

**Help — Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Job 24:20

The womb forgets them. 📖

Worms feed on them.

No one remembers them anymore.

So injustice is broken like a tree.

**Help — the womb:** The mother who gave birth to them.

## Job 24:21

They take advantage of women who cannot have children and have no children. 📖

They show widows no kindness.

## Job 24:22

Yet God drags powerful people away by His power. 📖

Even when they rise up, they cannot be sure of life.

## Job 24:23

He lets them feel safe. 📖

But His eyes watch their ways.

## Job 24:24

They are raised high for a moment, then they are gone. 📖

They are brought low and gathered up like everyone else.

They are cut off like heads of grain.

## Job 24:25

If this is not so, who can prove me a liar? 📖

Who can make my words worth nothing?
