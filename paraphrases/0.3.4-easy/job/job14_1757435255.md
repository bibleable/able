# Job 14 — Job speaks about short lives and death

**About this chapter**

Job continues speaking to God.

He describes death and asks whether a person can live again.

## Job 14:1

A human being born of a woman has few days. 📖

Those days are full of trouble.

## Job 14:2

He opens like a flower, then dries up. 📖

He passes like a shadow and does not last.

## Job 14:3

Do You watch someone like this? 📖

Will You bring him before You for judgment?

## Job 14:4

Who can bring something clean out of something unclean? 📖

No one!

## Job 14:5

You have decided how many days he has. 📖

You know the number of his months.

You have set limits he cannot go beyond.

## Job 14:6

So look away from him and let him rest. 📖

Let him enjoy his day like a hired worker.

## Job 14:7

There is hope for a tree. 📖

If it is cut down, it will grow again.

Its new shoots will not fail.

## Job 14:8

Its roots may grow old in the ground. 📖

Its stump may die in the soil.

## Job 14:9

But at the scent of water it will grow buds. 📖

It will grow twigs like a young tree.

## Job 14:10

But a man dies and falls down. 📖

He breathes his last breath.

Where is he then?

## Job 14:11

Water disappears from the sea. 📖

A river dries up and becomes empty.

## Job 14:12

In the same way, a man lies down and does not rise. 📖

Until the heavens are gone, he will not wake from sleep.

## Job 14:13

If only You would hide me in Sheol! 📖

Keep me hidden until Your anger has passed.

If only You would set a time for me and then remember me!

**Help — Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Job 14:14

If a man dies, will he live again? 📖

Through all the days of my hard service, I will wait until my renewal comes.

**Help — renewal:** Being restored or made new.

## Job 14:15

You will call, and I will answer. 📖

You will long for the work of Your hands.

## Job 14:16

Then You would count my steps. 📖

But You would not keep track of my sin.

## Job 14:17

My wrongdoing would be sealed in a bag. 📖

You would cover over my sin.

**Help — sealed and covered:** Job pictures his sin being put away.

## Job 14:18

But a mountain wears away and crumbles. 📖

A rock is moved from its place.

## Job 14:19

Water wears away stones. 📖

Floods wash away soil.

In the same way, You destroy a person’s hope.

## Job 14:20

You overpower him forever, and he passes away. 📖

You change his face and send him away.

## Job 14:21

If his sons receive honor, he does not know. 📖

If they are brought low, he is unaware.

## Job 14:22

He feels only his own body’s pain. 📖

He mourns only for himself.
