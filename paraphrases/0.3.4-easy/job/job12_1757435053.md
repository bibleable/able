# Job 12 — Job answers his friends about God’s power

**About this chapter**

Job answers Zophar and the other friends.

He challenges their wisdom and describes God’s power.

## Job 12:1

Then Job answered: 📖

## Job 12:2

You really are the people who have all wisdom! 📖

Wisdom itself will die with you!

**Help — Job’s words:** Job is speaking sharply to challenge his friends’ claim to wisdom.

## Job 12:3

But I have a mind too. 📖

I am no less than you.

Who does not know these things?

## Job 12:4

My friends laugh at me, although I called on God and He answered. 📖

They laugh at an innocent, upright man.

**Help — upright:** Living in an honest and right way.

## Job 12:5

A person who is comfortable looks down on people in trouble. 📖

He thinks trouble belongs to those whose feet are slipping.

## Job 12:6

Robbers’ tents are safe. 📖

People who make God angry are secure.

They carry their god in their hands.

## Job 12:7

But ask the animals, and they will teach you. 📖

Ask the birds of the sky, and they will tell you.

## Job 12:8

Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you. 📖

Let the fish of the sea tell you.

## Job 12:9

Which of all these does not know that the LORD’s hand has done this? 📖

## Job 12:10

Every living thing’s life is in His hand. 📖

So is the breath of all people.

## Job 12:11

Does the ear not test words as the tongue tastes food? 📖

## Job 12:12

Wisdom is found among old people. 📖

Understanding comes with a long life.

## Job 12:13

Wisdom and strength belong to God. 📖

Advice and understanding are His.

## Job 12:14

What He tears down cannot be rebuilt. 📖

A man He puts in prison cannot be released.

## Job 12:15

If He holds back the waters, they dry up. 📖

If He lets them go, they flood the land.

## Job 12:16

True wisdom and power belong to Him. 📖

Both the deceived person and the deceiver belong to Him.

## Job 12:17

He leads advisers away barefoot. 📖

He makes fools of judges.

## Job 12:18

He loosens the bonds that kings have put on people. 📖

He fastens a belt around the kings’ waists.

**Help — bonds and belt:** A picture of powerful rulers losing their freedom and power.

## Job 12:19

He leads priests away barefoot. 📖

He overthrows people who are firmly established.

## Job 12:20

He takes speech away from trusted people. 📖

He takes understanding away from elders.

## Job 12:21

He pours shame on nobles. 📖

He takes weapons away from the mighty.

**Help — nobles:** People with high rank.

## Job 12:22

He reveals things hidden deep in darkness. 📖

He brings deep shadows into the light.

## Job 12:23

He makes nations great, and He destroys them. 📖

He makes nations larger, then scatters them.

## Job 12:24

He takes reason away from the earth’s leaders. 📖

He makes them wander in a wasteland with no paths.

## Job 12:25

They feel their way in darkness without light. 📖

He makes them stagger like drunk people.
