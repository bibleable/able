# Job 26 — Job describes the edges of God’s power

**About this chapter**

Job answers Bildad sharply.

He then describes God’s power in poetic pictures.

## Job 26:1

Then Job answered: 📖

## Job 26:2

How you have helped those without power! 📖

How you have saved the weak arm!

## Job 26:3

What advice you have given to someone without wisdom! 📖

What complete and sound understanding you have supplied!

**Help — Job’s reply:** Job uses sharp words to challenge whether Bildad has helped at all.

## Job 26:4

Who have you spoken these words to? 📖

Whose spirit spoke through you?

## Job 26:5

The dead tremble. 📖

Those beneath the waters and those who dwell in them tremble.

## Job 26:6

Sheol is naked before God. 📖

Abaddon has no covering.

**Help — Sheol and Abaddon:** Names connected with death and destruction.

Nothing there is hidden from God.

## Job 26:7

He stretches out the north over empty space. 📖

He hangs the earth on nothing.

## Job 26:8

He wraps the waters in His clouds. 📖

Yet the clouds do not burst under their weight.

## Job 26:9

He covers the face of the full moon. 📖

He spreads His cloud over it.

## Job 26:10

He marks a horizon on the surface of the waters. 📖

It is the boundary between light and darkness.

**Help — horizon:** The line where the sky seems to meet the earth or sea.

## Job 26:11

Heaven’s foundations shake. 📖

They are astonished when He rebukes them.

**Help — foundations:** Supports pictured as holding heaven up.

To rebuke is to speak strongly against something.

## Job 26:12

By His power He stirred the sea. 📖

By His understanding He broke Rahab.

**Help — Rahab:** A name in poetry for a force opposed to God.

## Job 26:13

By His breath the skies were cleared. 📖

His hand pierced the fleeing serpent.

**Help — serpent:** A snake.

## Job 26:14

These are only the edges of His ways. 📖

We hear only a faint whisper of Him!

Who can understand the thunder of His power?

**Help — whisper and thunder:** Job pictures how little people understand of God’s great power.
