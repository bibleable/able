# Job 30 — Job describes his present misery

**About this chapter**

Job contrasts his present suffering with his former life.

His harsh descriptions of others are part of his speech.

From verse 20, he speaks directly to God.

## Job 30:1

But now younger men mock me. 📖

I would have refused to put their fathers with my sheep dogs.

## Job 30:2

What use was their strength to me? 📖

Their energy had left them.

## Job 30:3

Thin from poverty and hunger, they gnawed at the dry land. 📖

At night they gnawed at the empty wasteland.

## Job 30:4

They picked mallow among the bushes. 📖

Broom tree roots were their food.

**Help — mallow and broom:** Plants named as their food.

## Job 30:5

They were driven away from other people. 📖

People shouted at them as if they were thieves.

## Job 30:6

So they lived on the slopes of wadis. 📖

They lived among rocks and in holes in the ground.

**Help — wadis:** Valleys or stream channels that may be dry.

## Job 30:7

They cried out among bushes. 📖

They crowded together under nettles.

**Help — nettles:** Plants with stinging leaves.

## Job 30:8

They were a senseless group with no name. 📖

They were driven out of the land.

## Job 30:9

Now they mock me in songs. 📖

My name has become a saying of scorn among them.

## Job 30:10

They hate me and stay far away. 📖

They do not hesitate to spit in my face.

## Job 30:11

God has loosened my bowstring and made me suffer. 📖

So they no longer hold themselves back in my presence.

**Help — bowstring:** A bow cannot shoot properly with a loose string.

Job pictures losing his strength.

## Job 30:12

The mob rises at my right side. 📖

They set traps for my feet.

They build attack ramps against me.

## Job 30:13

They tear up my path. 📖

They gain from my destruction.

No one holds them back.

## Job 30:14

They advance as if through a wide break in a wall. 📖

Through the ruins they keep pouring in.

**Help — attack:** Job compares these people to an army attacking a city.

## Job 30:15

Terrors are let loose against me. 📖

They drive my dignity away like wind.

My success has passed like a cloud.

## Job 30:16

Now my life is poured out within me. 📖

Days of suffering grip me.

## Job 30:17

Night pierces my bones. 📖

The pains that gnaw at me never rest.

## Job 30:18

With great force God grabs my clothing. 📖

He seizes the collar of my tunic.

**Help — tunic:** A piece of clothing worn over the body.

## Job 30:19

He throws me into the mud. 📖

I have become like dust and ashes.

## Job 30:20

I cry to You for help, but You do not answer. 📖

When I stand up, You only look at me.

## Job 30:21

You have turned on me without mercy. 📖

You oppose me with Your strong hand.

## Job 30:22

You snatch me up into the wind and drive me before it. 📖

You toss me about in the storm.

## Job 30:23

Yes, I know You will bring me down to death. 📖

It is the place appointed for everyone living.

## Job 30:24

Yet no one raises a hand against a ruined man when he cries for help in distress. 📖

## Job 30:25

Have I not cried for people in trouble? 📖

Has my heart not grieved for needy people?

## Job 30:26

I hoped for good, but evil came. 📖

I looked for light, but darkness fell.

## Job 30:27

I churn inside and cannot rest. 📖

Days of suffering face me.

## Job 30:28

I go about blackened, but not by the sun. 📖

I stand in the gathering and cry for help.

## Job 30:29

I have become a brother of jackals. 📖

I am a companion of ostriches.

**Help — jackals and ostriches:** Wild animals and large birds.

Job compares their cries with his own mourning.

## Job 30:30

My skin grows black and peels. 📖

My bones burn with fever.

## Job 30:31

My harp is tuned to mourning. 📖

My flute sounds like weeping.

**Help — harp and flute:** Musical instruments.

Job’s music now expresses sorrow.
