# Leviticus 2 — Grain offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives instructions for grain offerings.

The rules distinguish what is burned from what belongs to the priests.

They also explain oil, incense, leaven, honey, and salt.

## Leviticus 2:1

“When bringing a grain offering to the LORD, use fine flour. 📖

Pour olive oil on it and add frankincense,

**Help with “frankincense”:** This is a fragrant material used as incense.

## Leviticus 2:2

and bring it to Aaron’s sons, the priests. 📖

The priest must take a handful of flour and oil with all the frankincense.

Burn it as the memorial portion on the altar, a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.

**Help with “memorial portion”:** This is the part burned as a reminder before God.

## Leviticus 2:3

The rest belongs to Aaron and his sons. 📖

It is a most holy part of the LORD’s food offerings.

## Leviticus 2:4

For an oven-baked offering, use fine flour. 📖

Make unleavened cakes mixed with oil or unleavened wafers coated with oil.

**Help with these foods:** Unleavened means made without leaven, such as yeast, that makes dough rise.

Wafers are thin baked cakes.

## Leviticus 2:5

For a grain offering cooked on a griddle, use unleavened bread of fine flour mixed with oil. 📖

**Help with “griddle”:** This is a flat cooking surface.

## Leviticus 2:6

Crumble it and pour oil over it. 📖

It is a grain offering.

## Leviticus 2:7

For a grain offering cooked in a pan, use fine flour with oil. 📖

## Leviticus 2:8

Present a grain offering made any of these ways to the LORD through the priest. 📖

The priest must take it to the altar.

## Leviticus 2:9

He must remove the memorial portion and burn it as a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 2:10

The rest belongs to Aaron and his sons. 📖

It is a most holy part of the LORD’s food offerings.

## Leviticus 2:11

Use no leaven in any grain offering to the LORD. 📖

Burn no leaven or honey as a food offering to Him.

## Leviticus 2:12

Leaven and honey may be brought to the LORD as firstfruits. 📖

They must not go on the altar as a pleasing smell.

**Help with “firstfruits”:** This means the first part of the produce offered to God.

## Leviticus 2:13

Season every grain offering with salt. 📖

Do not leave out the salt of your God’s covenant.

Add salt to every offering.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement or promise.

## Leviticus 2:14

For a firstfruits grain offering to the LORD, bring crushed heads of new grain roasted over fire. 📖

## Leviticus 2:15

Put oil and frankincense on it. 📖

It is a grain offering.

## Leviticus 2:16

The priest must burn a memorial portion of the crushed grain and oil with all its frankincense. 📖

It is a food offering to the LORD.”
