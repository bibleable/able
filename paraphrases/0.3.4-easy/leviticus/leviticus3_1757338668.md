# Leviticus 3 — Peace offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives instructions for peace offerings.

The chapter repeats the rules for cattle, lambs, and goats.

It names the parts belonging to the LORD.

## Leviticus 3:1

“For a peace offering from the herd, bring a male or female without blemish before the LORD. 📖

**Help with these words:** A peace offering is connected with fellowship and peace with God.

Without blemish means without a defect.

## Leviticus 3:2

Put your hand on its head and kill it at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

Aaron’s sons, the priests, must splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 3:3

Bring the fat covering the inner organs and all fat on them as a food offering to the LORD, 📖

## Leviticus 3:4

with both kidneys and their fat near the loins. 📖

Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys too.

**Help with these words:** The kidneys and liver are organs inside the body.

Loins are the lower back area.

A lobe is a part of an organ.

## Leviticus 3:5

Aaron’s sons must burn these on the altar, on the burnt offering atop the burning wood. 📖

It is a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.

## Leviticus 3:6

For a peace offering to the LORD from the flock, bring a male or female without blemish. 📖

## Leviticus 3:7

If it is a lamb, present it before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 3:8

Put your hand on its head and kill it before the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Aaron’s sons must splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 3:9

From it, bring a food offering to the LORD. 📖

Bring the whole fat tail, cut close to the backbone.

Bring the fat covering the inner organs and all fat on them,

## Leviticus 3:10

with both kidneys and their fat near the loins. 📖

Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys too.

## Leviticus 3:11

The priest must burn these on the altar as food, a food offering to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 3:12

If offering a goat, present it before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 3:13

Put your hand on its head and kill it before the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Aaron’s sons must splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 3:14

Bring the fat covering the inner organs and all fat on them as a food offering to the LORD, 📖

## Leviticus 3:15

with both kidneys and their fat near the loins. 📖

Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys too.

## Leviticus 3:16

The priest must burn this food on the altar as a food offering with a pleasing smell. 📖

All the fat belongs to the LORD.

## Leviticus 3:17

This is a permanent law for coming generations, wherever you live. 📖

Eat no fat or blood.”
