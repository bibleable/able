# Leviticus 16 — The Day of Atonement

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks after two of Aaron’s sons die.

Aaron receives instructions for entering the Most Holy Place and making atonement.

The live goat carries the people’s sins away.

The annual date and all the separate offerings are included.

## Leviticus 16:1

The LORD spoke to Moses after two of Aaron’s sons died when approaching His presence. 📖

## Leviticus 16:2

He said, “Tell Aaron, your brother, not to enter the Most Holy Place whenever he wishes. 📖

This is behind the veil, before the mercy seat on the ark.

Otherwise he will die, because I appear in the cloud above the mercy seat.

**Help with these words:** The veil is the separating curtain.

The mercy seat is the ark’s special cover.

## Leviticus 16:3

Aaron must enter the Holy Place with a young bull as a sin offering. 📖

He must bring a ram as a burnt offering too.

## Leviticus 16:4

He must wear the holy linen tunic, linen undergarments, linen sash, and linen turban. 📖

Bathe with water before wearing these holy garments.

## Leviticus 16:5

Take two male goats as a sin offering and a ram as a burnt offering from Israel’s congregation. 📖

## Leviticus 16:6

Aaron must present his sin-offering bull for atonement for himself and his household. 📖

**Help with “atonement”:** This is the ritual action for dealing with sin or uncleanness before God.

## Leviticus 16:7

Present both goats before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Leviticus 16:8

Cast lots for them, one for the LORD and one for the scapegoat. 📖

**Help with these words:** Casting lots means making a selection with marked objects.

Here the scapegoat is the goat sent away alive.

## Leviticus 16:9

Present the goat chosen for the LORD and kill it as a sin offering. 📖

## Leviticus 16:10

Present the scapegoat alive before the LORD for atonement by sending it into the wilderness. 📖

## Leviticus 16:11

Aaron must offer and kill his sin-offering bull for atonement for himself and his household. 📖

## Leviticus 16:12

Take a censer full of burning coals from the altar before the LORD. 📖

Take two handfuls of finely ground fragrant incense.

Bring them inside the veil.

**Help with “censer”:** This is a container for burning incense.

## Leviticus 16:13

Put the incense on the fire before the LORD. 📖

Its cloud must cover the mercy seat above the Testimony so he will not die.

## Leviticus 16:14

Use his finger to sprinkle some bull’s blood on the mercy seat’s east side. 📖

Use his finger to sprinkle blood seven times before the mercy seat.

## Leviticus 16:15

Kill the people’s sin-offering goat and bring its blood behind the veil. 📖

As with the bull’s blood, sprinkle it against the mercy seat and before it.

## Leviticus 16:16

Make atonement for the Most Holy Place because of Israel’s impurities, rebellions, and all their sins. 📖

Do the same for the Tent of Meeting among their impurities.

## Leviticus 16:17

No one else may be in the Tent of Meeting while Aaron makes atonement in the Most Holy Place. 📖

This applies from his entry until he leaves.

Before leaving, he must make atonement for himself, his household, and all Israel’s assembly.

## Leviticus 16:18

Go out to the altar before the LORD and make atonement for it. 📖

Put both bull’s and goat’s blood on all its horns.

## Leviticus 16:19

Sprinkle blood with his finger seven times to cleanse and consecrate it from Israel’s uncleanness. 📖

**Help with “consecrate”:** This means set apart for God.

## Leviticus 16:20

After purifying the Most Holy Place, Tent of Meeting, and altar, bring the live goat. 📖

## Leviticus 16:21

Put both hands on its head and confess all Israel’s wrongdoing, rebellion, and sins over it. 📖

Put them on its head.

An appointed man must take it into the wilderness.

## Leviticus 16:22

The goat carries all their wrongdoing to a solitary place. 📖

The man must release it into the wilderness.

## Leviticus 16:23

Aaron must enter the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Remove the linen garments he put on before entering the Most Holy Place and leave them there.

## Leviticus 16:24

Bathe with water in a holy place and put on his own clothes. 📖

Offer his and the people’s burnt offerings for atonement for himself and them.

## Leviticus 16:25

Burn the sin offering’s fat on the altar. 📖

## Leviticus 16:26

The man releasing the scapegoat must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

Then he may reenter camp.

## Leviticus 16:27

Take the sin-offering bull and goat outside camp. 📖

Their blood was brought into the Most Holy Place for atonement.

Burn their hides, meat, and dung.

## Leviticus 16:28

The person burning them must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

Then he may reenter camp.

## Leviticus 16:29

This is a permanent law. 📖

On day ten of month seven, humble yourselves and do no work.

This applies to natives and foreigners living among you,

**Help with “humble yourselves”:** This means lower yourselves before God.

## Leviticus 16:30

because atonement will cleanse you that day. 📖

You will be clean from all your sins before the LORD.

## Leviticus 16:31

It is a Sabbath of complete rest to humble yourselves. 📖

This is a permanent law.

## Leviticus 16:32

The priest anointed and ordained to succeed his father as high priest must make atonement. 📖

He must wear the holy linen garments

## Leviticus 16:33

and make atonement for the Most Holy Place, Tent of Meeting, altar, priests, and all the assembly’s people. 📖

## Leviticus 16:34

This is a permanent law. 📖

Once a year, make atonement for all Israel’s sins.”

Everything was done as the LORD commanded Moses.
