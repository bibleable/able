# Leviticus 26 — Covenant promises and judgments

**About this chapter**

The LORD describes blessings for obedience and escalating judgments for disobedience.

The chapter includes war, famine, disease, cannibalism, and exile.

It also speaks of confession and God remembering His covenant.

## Leviticus 26:1

“Make no idols, carved images, or sacred pillars. 📖

Set up no sculpted stone to bow before.

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 26:2

Keep My Sabbaths and revere My sanctuary. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 26:3

If you follow My laws and carefully keep My commands, 📖

## Leviticus 26:4

I will give rain in season. 📖

The land will produce crops and trees will bear fruit.

## Leviticus 26:5

Threshing will last until grape harvest, and grape harvest until sowing. 📖

You will eat your fill and live securely.

**Help with “threshing”:** This means separating grain from the cut stalks.

## Leviticus 26:6

I will give peace so you can lie down without fear. 📖

I will remove dangerous animals, and no sword will pass through your land.

**Help with “sword”:** Here this pictures war.

## Leviticus 26:7

You will chase your enemies, and they will fall by the sword before you. 📖

## Leviticus 26:8

Five will chase a hundred, and a hundred will chase ten thousand. 📖

Your enemies will fall by the sword before you.

## Leviticus 26:9

I will turn toward you, make you fruitful, multiply you, and establish My covenant. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement or promise.

## Leviticus 26:10

You will still be eating old grain when you clear it for the new. 📖

## Leviticus 26:11

I will live among you. 📖

My soul will not despise you.

## Leviticus 26:12

I will walk among you and be your God. 📖

You will be My people.

## Leviticus 26:13

I am the LORD your God who brought you from Egypt, freeing you from Egyptian slavery. 📖

I broke your yoke’s bars so you could walk upright.

**Help with this image:** A yoke is a frame used to control working animals.

The broken yoke pictures release from slavery.

## Leviticus 26:14

But if you disobey and do not carry out these commands, 📖

## Leviticus 26:15

if you reject My laws, despise My rulings, and fail My commands, breaking My covenant, 📖

## Leviticus 26:16

I will bring sudden terror, wasting disease, and fever destroying sight and draining life. 📖

You will sow for nothing, because enemies will eat it.

## Leviticus 26:17

I will turn against you, and enemies will defeat you. 📖

Those hating you will rule you.

You will flee when no one pursues.

## Leviticus 26:18

If you still refuse to obey, I will punish your sins sevenfold. 📖

**Help with “sevenfold”:** This means seven times as much.

## Leviticus 26:19

I will break your stubborn pride. 📖

Your sky will be like iron and your land like bronze,

**Help with this picture:** The metal image describes an unyielding sky and land.

## Leviticus 26:20

and your effort will be wasted. 📖

Neither land nor trees will produce crops.

## Leviticus 26:21

If you remain hostile and refuse to obey, I will multiply your plagues seven times for your sins. 📖

## Leviticus 26:22

I will send wild animals to rob you of children, destroy livestock, and reduce your numbers. 📖

Your roads will be deserted.

## Leviticus 26:23

If you still reject discipline and remain hostile, 📖

## Leviticus 26:24

I will act with hostility and strike you sevenfold for your sins. 📖

## Leviticus 26:25

I will bring a sword to avenge the covenant. 📖

If you retreat into cities, I will send plague and deliver you into enemy power.

## Leviticus 26:26

I will cut off your bread supply. 📖

Ten women will bake in one oven and give bread by weight.

You will eat without being satisfied.

## Leviticus 26:27

If you still disobey and remain hostile, 📖

## Leviticus 26:28

I Myself will act in fury and punish your sins sevenfold. 📖

## Leviticus 26:29

You will eat the flesh of your own sons and daughters. 📖

## Leviticus 26:30

I will destroy your high places and incense altars. 📖

I will heap your dead bodies on your lifeless idols.

My soul will despise you.

**Help with “high places”:** These are worship sites.

## Leviticus 26:31

I will ruin your cities and sanctuaries. 📖

I will refuse to smell your sacrifices’ pleasing aroma.

## Leviticus 26:32

I will ruin the land so enemies living there are appalled. 📖

## Leviticus 26:33

I will scatter you among nations and draw a sword after you. 📖

Your land will be desolate and cities ruined.

## Leviticus 26:34

The land will enjoy its Sabbaths while desolate and while you are in enemy lands. 📖

Then it will rest and enjoy its Sabbaths.

## Leviticus 26:35

While desolate, it will receive the Sabbath rest it lacked when you lived there. 📖

## Leviticus 26:36

I will put faintness in survivors’ hearts in enemy lands. 📖

A windblown leaf’s sound will make them flee as from a sword.

They will fall with no pursuer.

## Leviticus 26:37

They will stumble over each other as if fleeing a sword, though no one follows. 📖

You cannot stand against enemies.

## Leviticus 26:38

You will perish among nations. 📖

Your enemies’ land will consume you.

**Help with this picture:** The land consuming people pictures their dying there.

## Leviticus 26:39

Survivors in enemy lands will waste away in their wrongdoing and decay in their fathers’ sins. 📖

## Leviticus 26:40

But if they confess their wrongdoing and their fathers’ wrongdoing, their unfaithfulness and hostility toward Me, 📖

## Leviticus 26:41

(I opposed them, bringing them to enemy lands.) 📖

If their uncircumcised hearts become humble and they make amends for wrongdoing,

**Help with “uncircumcised hearts”:** This pictures resistance to God.

Making amends means answering for the wrong done.

## Leviticus 26:42

I will remember My covenant with Jacob, My covenant with Isaac, and My covenant with Abraham. 📖

I will remember the land.

## Leviticus 26:43

The abandoned land will enjoy its Sabbaths, lying desolate without them. 📖

They will pay for wrongdoing because they rejected My rulings and abhorred My laws.

## Leviticus 26:44

Yet in enemy lands, I will not reject or despise them so as to destroy them and break My covenant. 📖

I am the LORD their God.

## Leviticus 26:45

For their sake, I will remember the covenant with their fathers. 📖

I brought them from Egypt in the nations’ sight to be their God.

I am the LORD.”

## Leviticus 26:46

These laws, rulings, and commands were established between the LORD and Israel through Moses on Mount Sinai. 📖
