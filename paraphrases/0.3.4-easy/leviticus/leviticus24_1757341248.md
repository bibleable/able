# Leviticus 24 — Lamps, bread, and judgments

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives instructions for the lamps and bread.

A man is brought to Moses after cursing the Name.

The chapter records the judgment and laws about death, injury, and equal treatment.

## Leviticus 24:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 24:2

“Command Israel to bring pure oil from pressed olives to keep the lamps burning continually. 📖

## Leviticus 24:3

Aaron must tend them continually before the LORD evening to morning in the Tent of Meeting, outside the Testimony’s veil. 📖

This is a permanent law through coming generations.

## Leviticus 24:4

He must keep tending the pure gold lampstand’s lamps before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 24:5

Bake twelve loaves from fine flour, using two-tenths of an ephah each. 📖

## Leviticus 24:6

Place them in two rows of six on the pure gold table before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 24:7

Put pure frankincense near each row as the bread’s memorial portion, a food offering to the LORD. 📖

**Help with “frankincense”:** This is a fragrant material burned as incense.

## Leviticus 24:8

Set the bread before the LORD every Sabbath on Israel’s behalf as a permanent covenant. 📖

## Leviticus 24:9

It belongs to Aaron and his sons to eat in a holy place. 📖

It is a most holy part of the LORD’s food offerings, his portion forever.”

## Leviticus 24:10

The son of an Israelite mother and Egyptian father went among Israel. 📖

He fought an Israelite in camp.

## Leviticus 24:11

He blasphemed the Name with a curse, and they brought him to Moses. 📖

His mother was Shelomith, Dibri’s daughter from Dan’s tribe.

**Help with these words:** The Name refers to the LORD’s name.

Blaspheme means speak abusively or disrespectfully about God.

## Leviticus 24:12

They held him in custody until the LORD’s will became clear. 📖

## Leviticus 24:13

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 24:14

“Take him outside camp. 📖

Everyone who heard him must put their hands on his head.

The whole assembly must stone him.

## Leviticus 24:15

Tell Israel: Anyone cursing his God must bear his sin’s consequences. 📖

## Leviticus 24:16

Anyone blaspheming the LORD’s name must be put to death. 📖

The whole assembly must stone him, whether native or resident foreigner.

Blaspheming the Name brings death.

## Leviticus 24:17

Anyone taking another person’s life must be put to death. 📖

## Leviticus 24:18

Anyone killing an animal must repay, life for life. 📖

## Leviticus 24:19

Whatever injury someone gives his neighbor must be done to him: 📖

## Leviticus 24:20

fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. 📖

Inflict the same injury on him.

## Leviticus 24:21

Anyone killing an animal must repay. 📖

Anyone killing a man must be put to death.

## Leviticus 24:22

Use the same law for natives and resident foreigners. 📖

I am the LORD your God.”

## Leviticus 24:23

Moses told Israel. 📖

They took the blasphemer outside camp and stoned him, as the LORD commanded Moses.
