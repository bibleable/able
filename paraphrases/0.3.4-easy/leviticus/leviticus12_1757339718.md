# Leviticus 12 — Purification after childbirth

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules for a woman after childbirth.

The periods differ for a son and a daughter.

The chapter includes a less costly offering for a woman who cannot afford a lamb.

Clean and unclean describe ritual status here, not a mother’s or child’s human worth.

## Leviticus 12:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 12:2

“Tell Israel: A woman who becomes pregnant and gives birth to a son is unclean seven days. 📖

This is like the days of her menstruation.

**Help with “menstruation”:** This is the regular bleeding from the womb during a menstrual cycle.

## Leviticus 12:3

On day eight, circumcise the boy’s foreskin. 📖

**Help with these words:** The foreskin is skin covering the end of the penis.

Circumcision means removal of that skin.

## Leviticus 12:4

She must continue purification from bleeding for thirty-three days. 📖

Until this ends, she must not touch sacred things or enter the sanctuary.

## Leviticus 12:5

After a daughter’s birth, she is unclean two weeks, as during menstruation. 📖

She must then continue purification from bleeding for sixty-six days.

**About the different times:** The chapter gives these periods without explaining why they differ.

## Leviticus 12:6

After her purification ends, whether for a son or daughter, she must bring offerings to the priest. 📖

Bring them to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance.

Bring a year-old lamb as a burnt offering and a young pigeon or turtledove as a sin offering.

## Leviticus 12:7

The priest must present them before the LORD and make atonement for her. 📖

She will be ceremonially clean from her bleeding.

This is the law for a woman bearing a boy or girl.

**Help with these words:** Atonement is the ritual action for dealing with sin or uncleanness before God.

Here the offering is part of the cleansing rules after childbirth.

Ceremonially clean means meeting the requirements of these worship rules.

## Leviticus 12:8

If she cannot afford a lamb, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons. 📖

One is a burnt offering, the other a sin offering.

The priest will make atonement, and she will be clean.”
