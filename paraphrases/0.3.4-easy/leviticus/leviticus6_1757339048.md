# Leviticus 6 — Repayment and the priests’ duties

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules for returning property and making a guilt offering.

He then speaks about the priests’ work with burnt, grain, and sin offerings.

Some offerings are eaten, while others must be burned completely.

## Leviticus 6:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 6:2

“Suppose someone sins and acts unfaithfully against the LORD by deceiving his neighbor. 📖

It may involve a deposit, property entrusted as security, theft, or extortion,

**Help with these words:** A deposit is something left with another person to keep safe.

Security is property held as a guarantee.

Extortion means taking something by force or threats.

## Leviticus 6:3

or finding lost property and lying and swearing falsely about it, or any similar sin. 📖

## Leviticus 6:4

When guilty, he must return stolen goods, anything taken by extortion, any entrusted deposit, or the lost property, 📖

## Leviticus 6:5

or anything else he swore falsely about. 📖

Repay its full value plus a fifth to its owner on the day he admits guilt.

**Help with “a fifth”:** This means one of five equal parts, added to the full repayment.

## Leviticus 6:6

Bring the priest a ram without blemish and of proper value as a guilt offering to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 6:7

The priest will make atonement before the LORD. 📖

The person will be forgiven for whatever he did to incur guilt.”

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Leviticus 6:8

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 6:9

“Give Aaron and his sons the burnt-offering law. 📖

It must stay on the altar’s hearth all night until morning.

Keep the altar’s fire burning.

## Leviticus 6:10

The priest must wear his linen robe and linen undergarments. 📖

Remove the burnt offering’s ashes and place them beside the altar.

## Leviticus 6:11

Then change into other clothes and carry the ashes to a ceremonially clean place outside camp. 📖

**Help with “ceremonially clean”:** This means meeting the requirements of these worship rules.

## Leviticus 6:12

Keep the fire burning. 📖

Never let it go out.

Every morning, the priest must add wood, arrange the burnt offering, and burn the peace offerings’ fat portions.

## Leviticus 6:13

Keep the altar fire burning continually. 📖

Never let it go out.

## Leviticus 6:14

This is the grain-offering law. 📖

Aaron’s sons must present it before the LORD in front of the altar.

## Leviticus 6:15

The priest must take a handful of fine flour and olive oil with all the frankincense. 📖

Burn this memorial portion on the altar as a pleasing smell to the LORD.

## Leviticus 6:16

Aaron and his sons must eat the rest without leaven. 📖

Eat it in a holy place, the Tent of Meeting’s courtyard.

## Leviticus 6:17

Do not bake it with leaven. 📖

I assigned it as their portion of My food offerings.

It is most holy, like sin and guilt offerings.

**Help with “leaven”:** This makes dough rise, as yeast does.

## Leviticus 6:18

Any male among Aaron’s sons may eat it. 📖

It is their permanent portion of the LORD’s food offerings through coming generations.

Anything touching them becomes holy.”

## Leviticus 6:19

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 6:20

“On Aaron’s anointing day, he and his sons must bring this regular grain offering to the LORD. 📖

Bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour, half in the morning and half in the evening.

**Help with these words:** Anointing means applying oil to set someone apart.

An ephah measures dry goods.

## Leviticus 6:21

Prepare it with oil on a griddle. 📖

Bring it well-kneaded and broken into pieces as a grain offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.

**Help with these words:** A griddle is a flat cooking surface.

Kneading means pressing and working dough.

## Leviticus 6:22

The anointed priest succeeding Aaron must prepare it. 📖

It is a permanent portion for the LORD and must be burned completely.

## Leviticus 6:23

Every grain offering for a priest must be completely burned. 📖

It must not be eaten.”

## Leviticus 6:24

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 6:25

“Give Aaron and his sons the sin-offering law. 📖

Kill the sin offering before the LORD where burnt offerings are killed.

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 6:26

The priest offering it must eat it in a holy place, the Tent of Meeting’s courtyard. 📖

## Leviticus 6:27

Anything touching its meat becomes holy. 📖

If blood splashes on clothing, wash it in a holy place.

## Leviticus 6:28

Break the clay pot used to boil it. 📖

If a bronze pot was used, scrub and rinse it with water.

## Leviticus 6:29

Any male priest may eat it. 📖

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 6:30

If a sin offering’s blood entered the Tent of Meeting for atonement in the Holy Place, do not eat it. 📖

Burn it.”
