# Leviticus 22 — Sacred offerings and acceptable animals

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules about who may eat sacred food.

He also gives conditions for accepting animal offerings.

The chapter includes specific exceptions, waiting periods, and penalties.

## Leviticus 22:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 22:2

“Tell Aaron and his sons to respect the sacred offerings Israel sets apart for Me. 📖

They must not profane My holy name.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:3

In coming generations, any unclean descendant approaching Israel’s sacred offerings must be cut off from My presence. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:4

An Aaron descendant with skin disease or a discharge must not eat sacred offerings until clean. 📖

Anyone touching something defiled by a corpse or a man’s semen emission,

## Leviticus 22:5

or a crawling creature or person making him unclean, whatever the uncleanness, 📖

## Leviticus 22:6

remains unclean until evening. 📖

He must bathe with water before eating sacred offerings.

## Leviticus 22:7

After sunset, he is clean and may eat the sacred offerings because they are his food. 📖

## Leviticus 22:8

He must not eat anything found dead or torn by wild animals, making him unclean. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:9

Priests must keep My command so they do not bear guilt and die for profaning it. 📖

I am the LORD who sanctifies them.

## Leviticus 22:10

No outsider to a priest’s family, priest’s guest, or hired worker may eat sacred offerings. 📖

## Leviticus 22:11

A slave bought with a priest’s own money or born in his household may eat his food. 📖

## Leviticus 22:12

A priest’s daughter married to a non-priest must not eat sacred contributions. 📖

## Leviticus 22:13

If a priest’s childless daughter is widowed or divorced and returns to her father’s house, she may share his food. 📖

This is as in her youth.

No outsider may eat it.

## Leviticus 22:14

If anyone eats a sacred offering by mistake, he must add a fifth of its value. 📖

Give the sacred offering to the priest.

## Leviticus 22:15

Priests must not profane Israel’s sacred offerings to the LORD 📖

## Leviticus 22:16

by letting people eat them and bear punishment for guilt. 📖

I am the LORD who sanctifies them.”

## Leviticus 22:17

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 22:18

Tell Aaron, his sons, and Israel: An Israelite or resident foreigner may bring a burnt offering to the LORD. 📖

This may be for a vow or given freely,

## Leviticus 22:19

For acceptance, bring a male without blemish from cattle, sheep, or goats. 📖

## Leviticus 22:20

Offer nothing with a defect. 📖

It will not be accepted for you.

## Leviticus 22:21

A peace offering from herd or flock may fulfill a vow or be given freely. 📖

It must have no blemish or defect to be accepted.

## Leviticus 22:22

Do not present blind, injured, or maimed animals, or animals with running sores, oozing rashes, or scabs. 📖

Put none on the altar as food offerings to the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:23

An ox or sheep with a deformed or stunted limb may be a freewill offering, but not a vow offering. 📖

## Leviticus 22:24

Do not present an animal with bruised, crushed, torn, or cut testicles to the LORD. 📖

Do not sacrifice them in your land.

## Leviticus 22:25

Neither you nor a foreigner may offer God food from such animals. 📖

They are deformed and flawed and will not be accepted for you.”

## Leviticus 22:26

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 22:27

“A newborn ox, sheep, or goat must stay with its mother seven days. 📖

From day eight, it may be accepted as a food offering to the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:28

Do not kill an ox or sheep and its young on the same day. 📖

## Leviticus 22:29

Present a thanksgiving sacrifice to the LORD so it may be accepted for you. 📖

## Leviticus 22:30

Eat it that same day. 📖

Leave none until morning.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:31

Keep and practice My commands. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:32

Do not profane My holy name. 📖

Israel must acknowledge Me as holy.

I am the LORD who sanctifies you,

## Leviticus 22:33

who brought you from Egypt to be your God. 📖

I am the LORD.”
