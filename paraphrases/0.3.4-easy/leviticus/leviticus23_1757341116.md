# Leviticus 23 — The LORD’s appointed feasts

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Israel the dates and offerings for sacred gatherings.

The chapter includes the Sabbath, Passover, harvest feasts, Day of Atonement, and Feast of Tabernacles.

The repeated dates and different work restrictions are included.

## Leviticus 23:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 23:2

“Tell Israel: These are My appointed feasts, the LORD’s feasts to announce as sacred gatherings. 📖

## Leviticus 23:3

Work may be done six days. 📖

Day seven is a holy gathering and Sabbath of complete rest.

Do no work.

Wherever you live, it is the LORD’s Sabbath.

## Leviticus 23:4

Announce the LORD’s appointed feasts as sacred gatherings at their appointed times. 📖

## Leviticus 23:5

The LORD’s Passover begins at twilight on day fourteen of month one. 📖

## Leviticus 23:6

On day fifteen of that month, the LORD’s Feast of Unleavened Bread begins. 📖

Eat unleavened bread seven days.

**Help with “unleavened”:** This means made without leaven, such as yeast, which makes dough rise.

## Leviticus 23:7

Hold a sacred gathering on day one. 📖

Do no regular work.

## Leviticus 23:8

Present food offerings to the LORD seven days. 📖

Hold a sacred gathering on day seven and do no regular work.”

## Leviticus 23:9

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 23:10

“Tell Israel: When you enter My gift of land and harvest it, bring the priest a sheaf of firstfruits. 📖

**Help with these words:** A sheaf is a bundle of cut grain stalks.

Firstfruits means the first part of the harvest.

## Leviticus 23:11

The priest must wave it before the LORD for your acceptance on the day after the Sabbath. 📖

## Leviticus 23:12

That day, offer a year-old lamb without blemish as a burnt offering to the LORD, 📖

## Leviticus 23:13

with two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering. 📖

It is a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.

Offer a quarter hin of wine as a drink offering.

**Help with these units:** An ephah measures dry goods.

A hin measures liquids.

## Leviticus 23:14

Eat no bread, roasted grain, or new grain until the day you bring this offering to God. 📖

This is a permanent law through coming generations wherever you live.

## Leviticus 23:15

Count seven full weeks from the day after the Sabbath when you brought the waved sheaf. 📖

## Leviticus 23:16

Count fifty days to the day after the seventh Sabbath. 📖

Present new grain to the LORD.

## Leviticus 23:17

Bring two loaves from your homes as a wave offering of firstfruits to the LORD. 📖

Each must use two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour, baked with leaven.

## Leviticus 23:18

With the bread, bring seven year-old male lambs without blemish, one young bull, and two rams. 📖

They are burnt offerings with grain and drink offerings, a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.

## Leviticus 23:19

Also offer one male goat for sin and two year-old male lambs as a peace offering. 📖

## Leviticus 23:20

The priest must wave the lambs and firstfruits bread before the LORD. 📖

The bread and two lambs are holy to the LORD for the priest.

## Leviticus 23:21

Announce a sacred gathering that day. 📖

Do no regular work.

This is a permanent law through coming generations wherever you live.

## Leviticus 23:22

Do not harvest to the field’s edges or gather what remains. 📖

Leave it for poor people and resident foreigners.

I am the LORD your God.”

## Leviticus 23:23

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 23:24

“Tell Israel: Day one of month seven is a rest day and sacred gathering announced with trumpet blasts. 📖

## Leviticus 23:25

Do no regular work. 📖

Present a food offering to the LORD.”

## Leviticus 23:26

The LORD told Moses again, 📖

## Leviticus 23:27

“Day ten of month seven is the Day of Atonement. 📖

Hold a sacred gathering, humble yourselves, and present a food offering to the LORD.

**Help with these words:** Atonement deals with sin and uncleanness before God.

Humbling yourselves means lowering yourselves before God.

## Leviticus 23:28

Do no work that day. 📖

It is the Day of Atonement, when atonement is made for you before the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 23:29

Anyone not humbling himself that day must be cut off from his people. 📖

## Leviticus 23:30

I will destroy from among his people anyone working that day. 📖

## Leviticus 23:31

Do no work at all. 📖

This is a permanent law through coming generations wherever you live.

## Leviticus 23:32

Keep a Sabbath of complete rest and humble yourselves. 📖

Observe it from the ninth day’s evening to the following evening.”

## Leviticus 23:33

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 23:34

“Tell Israel: The LORD’s Feast of Tabernacles begins on day fifteen of month seven and lasts seven days. 📖

**Help with “Tabernacles”:** This means temporary shelters or booths.

## Leviticus 23:35

Hold a sacred gathering on day one. 📖

Do no regular work.

## Leviticus 23:36

Present food offerings to the LORD seven days. 📖

On day eight, hold a solemn sacred gathering and present a food offering.

Do no regular work.

## Leviticus 23:37

These are the LORD’s appointed feasts, announced as sacred gatherings. 📖

Present food offerings, burnt offerings, grain offerings, sacrifices, and drink offerings, each on its assigned day.

## Leviticus 23:38

These are additional to the LORD’s Sabbath offerings, your gifts, all vow offerings, and all freewill offerings. 📖

## Leviticus 23:39

After gathering the land’s produce, celebrate the LORD’s feast seven days from day fifteen of month seven. 📖

Rest completely on days one and eight.

## Leviticus 23:40

On day one, gather majestic trees’ fruit, palm branches, and branches of leafy trees and brook willows. 📖

Rejoice before the LORD your God seven days.

**Help with “majestic”:** This means great and splendid.

## Leviticus 23:41

Celebrate this feast seven days each year in month seven. 📖

This is a permanent law through coming generations.

## Leviticus 23:42

Live in booths seven days. 📖

All native-born Israelites must live in booths,

## Leviticus 23:43

so descendants know I made Israel live in booths when I brought them from Egypt. 📖

I am the LORD your God.”

## Leviticus 23:44

Moses announced the LORD’s appointed feasts to Israel. 📖
