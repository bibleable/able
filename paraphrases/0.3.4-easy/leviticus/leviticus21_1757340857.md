# Leviticus 21 — Restrictions for priests

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives different rules for priests and the high priest.

These concern death, mourning, marriage, and priestly service.

Some physical conditions restrict duties, while the men concerned may still eat sacred food.

## Leviticus 21:1

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron’s priestly sons not to defile themselves for a dead person among their people, 📖

## Leviticus 21:2

except immediate family: mother, father, son, daughter, or brother, 📖

## Leviticus 21:3

or an unmarried sister near him, since she has no husband. 📖

## Leviticus 21:4

He must not defile himself for relatives by marriage and so profane himself. 📖

**Help with these words:** Defile means make unclean or dishonored.

Profane means treat what is holy with disrespect.

## Leviticus 21:5

Priests must not make bald head spots, shave their beard edges, or cut their bodies. 📖

## Leviticus 21:6

They must be holy to God and not profane His name. 📖

They present the LORD’s food offerings, God’s food, so they must be holy.

## Leviticus 21:7

A priest must not marry a woman defiled by prostitution or divorced by her husband. 📖

He is holy to his God.

## Leviticus 21:8

Regard him as holy because he presents your God’s food. 📖

He must be holy to you because I, the LORD who sets you apart, am holy.

## Leviticus 21:9

If a priest’s daughter defiles herself by prostitution, she profanes her father. 📖

She must be burned in fire.

## Leviticus 21:10

The priest highest among his brothers had anointing oil poured on his head. 📖

He was ordained to wear the priestly garments.

He must not leave his hair loose or tear his clothes.

## Leviticus 21:11

He must not approach any dead body or defile himself, even for father or mother. 📖

## Leviticus 21:12

He must not leave or desecrate God’s sanctuary. 📖

His God’s anointing oil sets him apart.

I am the LORD.

**Help with “desecrate”:** This means treat a holy place or thing as unholy.

## Leviticus 21:13

He must marry a virgin. 📖

**Help with “virgin”:** This means someone who has not had sex.

## Leviticus 21:14

He must not marry a widow, divorced woman, or woman defiled by prostitution. 📖

Marry a virgin from his people,

## Leviticus 21:15

so his offspring among them are not defiled. 📖

I am the LORD who sanctifies him.”

**Help with “sanctifies”:** This means sets apart as holy.

## Leviticus 21:16

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 21:17

“Tell Aaron: In coming generations, no descendant with a physical defect may approach to offer God’s food. 📖

**About “physical defect”:** This is the source’s term for the conditions listed next.

The passage restricts duties while permitting sacred food.

## Leviticus 21:18

No man with a defect may approach: blind, lame, disfigured, or deformed; 📖

**Help with these words:** Lame describes difficulty walking.

Disfigured or deformed describe differences in the body’s shape.

## Leviticus 21:19

One with a broken foot or hand; 📖

## Leviticus 21:20

One with a hunched back, dwarfism, an eye defect, an oozing rash, scabs, or a crushed testicle. 📖

**Help with these words:** A hunched back curves forward.

Dwarfism refers to very short growth.

Testicles are organs in the skin pouch below the penis.

## Leviticus 21:21

No descendant of Aaron the priest with a defect may approach to present the LORD’s food offerings. 📖

Because he has a defect, he must not offer God’s food.

## Leviticus 21:22

He may eat God’s most holy food and holy food, 📖

## Leviticus 21:23

but must not approach the veil or altar because of his defect. 📖

He must not desecrate My sanctuaries.

I am the LORD who sanctifies them.”

## Leviticus 21:24

Moses told Aaron, his sons, and all Israel. 📖
