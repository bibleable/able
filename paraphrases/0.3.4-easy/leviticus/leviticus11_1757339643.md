# Leviticus 11 — Animals and ritual cleanness

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to Moses and Aaron about animals and food.

The chapter also gives rules for contact with dead animals.

The animal names and descriptions follow BSB.

Clean and unclean describe status under these rules, not a person’s human worth.

## Leviticus 11:1

The LORD spoke again to Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Leviticus 11:2

He said, “Tell Israel which land animals they may eat. 📖

## Leviticus 11:3

You may eat animals with completely divided split hooves that also chew the cud. 📖

**Help with these words:** Hooves are the hard coverings of some animals’ feet.

Chewing cud means chewing food brought back up from the stomach.

## Leviticus 11:4

Do not eat animals with only one of these features. 📖

The camel chews cud but lacks a divided hoof.

It is unclean for you.

## Leviticus 11:5

The rock badger chews cud but lacks a divided hoof. 📖

It is unclean for you.

## Leviticus 11:6

The rabbit chews cud but lacks a divided hoof. 📖

It is unclean for you.

## Leviticus 11:7

The pig has completely divided split hooves but does not chew cud. 📖

It is unclean for you.

## Leviticus 11:8

Do not eat their meat or touch their dead bodies. 📖

They are unclean for you.

## Leviticus 11:9

You may eat water creatures with fins and scales, whether from seas or streams. 📖

## Leviticus 11:10

All creatures teeming in seas or streams without fins and scales are detestable to you. 📖

**Help with “detestable”:** This means to be strongly rejected under these rules.

## Leviticus 11:11

Treat them as an abomination. 📖

Do not eat their meat, and detest their dead bodies.

**Help with “abomination”:** This means something deeply detestable.

## Leviticus 11:12

All water creatures without fins and scales are detestable to you. 📖

## Leviticus 11:13

Detest these birds and do not eat them: the eagle, bearded vulture, and black vulture, 📖

## Leviticus 11:14

The kite and every kind of falcon, 📖

## Leviticus 11:15

Every kind of raven, 📖

## Leviticus 11:16

The ostrich, screech owl, gull, and every kind of hawk, 📖

## Leviticus 11:17

The little owl, cormorant, and great owl, 📖

## Leviticus 11:18

The white owl, desert owl, and osprey, 📖

## Leviticus 11:19

The stork, every kind of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat. 📖

## Leviticus 11:20

All flying insects walking on all fours are detestable to you. 📖

## Leviticus 11:21

But some flying insects walking on all fours may be eaten. 📖

They must have jointed legs above their feet for hopping on the ground.

## Leviticus 11:22

These include every kind of locust, katydid, cricket, and grasshopper. 📖

## Leviticus 11:23

All other four-legged flying insects are detestable to you. 📖

## Leviticus 11:24

These creatures make you unclean. 📖

Anyone touching their dead bodies is unclean until evening,

## Leviticus 11:25

and anyone picking up a dead body must wash his clothes and remain unclean until evening. 📖

## Leviticus 11:26

Animals without completely divided hooves or without cud-chewing are unclean for you. 📖

Anyone touching them becomes unclean.

## Leviticus 11:27

All four-footed animals walking on paws are unclean for you. 📖

Anyone touching their dead bodies is unclean until evening,

## Leviticus 11:28

and anyone picking one up must wash his clothes and remain unclean until evening. 📖

They are unclean for you.

## Leviticus 11:29

These ground-moving creatures are unclean for you: the mole, mouse, and every kind of great lizard, 📖

## Leviticus 11:30

The gecko, monitor lizard, common lizard, skink, and chameleon. 📖

## Leviticus 11:31

These are unclean among crawling creatures. 📖

Anyone touching them dead is unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 11:32

Anything one falls on when dead becomes unclean. 📖

This includes wood, clothing, leather, sackcloth, or any work tool.

Rinse it with water.

It remains unclean until evening, then becomes clean.

**Help with “sackcloth”:** This is rough cloth.

## Leviticus 11:33

If one falls in a clay pot, everything inside becomes unclean. 📖

Break the pot.

## Leviticus 11:34

Food touching water from that pot becomes unclean. 📖

Any drink in such a container becomes unclean.

## Leviticus 11:35

Anything their dead bodies fall on becomes unclean. 📖

Smash an oven or cooking pot affected this way.

It is unclean and remains unclean for you.

## Leviticus 11:36

A spring or water-filled cistern remains clean. 📖

But anyone touching the dead body in it becomes unclean.

**Help with “cistern”:** This is a container or pit for storing water.

## Leviticus 11:37

Seed for sowing remains clean if a dead body falls on it. 📖

## Leviticus 11:38

If water was put on the seed first, and a dead body falls on it, it is unclean for you. 📖

## Leviticus 11:39

If an animal you may eat dies, anyone touching its dead body is unclean until evening. 📖

## Leviticus 11:40

Anyone eating from its dead body must wash his clothes and remain unclean until evening. 📖

Anyone picking it up must also wash his clothes and remain unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 11:41

Every ground-moving creature is detestable. 📖

Do not eat it.

## Leviticus 11:42

Do not eat ground-moving creatures crawling on their bellies or walking on four or more feet. 📖

They are detestable.

## Leviticus 11:43

Do not defile yourselves with crawling creatures. 📖

Do not become unclean or defiled by them.

**Help with “defile”:** Here this means make unclean under these rules.

## Leviticus 11:44

I am the LORD your God. 📖

Set yourselves apart and be holy, because I am holy.

Do not defile yourselves with ground-crawling creatures.

## Leviticus 11:45

I am the LORD who brought you from Egypt to be your God. 📖

Be holy, because I am holy.

## Leviticus 11:46

This is the law about animals, birds, all water creatures, and all ground-crawling creatures. 📖

## Leviticus 11:47

Distinguish unclean from clean and animals you may eat from those you may not.” 📖
