# Leviticus 25 — Sabbath years, Jubilee, and redemption

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules for the land’s rest and the Jubilee.

He describes buying back property and securing release from service.

The chapter gives different rules for Israelites and foreign slaves, including lifelong slavery.

## Leviticus 25:1

The LORD told Moses on Mount Sinai, 📖

## Leviticus 25:2

“Tell Israel: When you enter My gift of land, the land must keep a Sabbath to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 25:3

You may sow fields, prune vineyards, and gather crops six years. 📖

## Leviticus 25:4

In year seven, the land must have complete Sabbath rest to the LORD. 📖

Do not sow fields or prune vineyards.

## Leviticus 25:5

Do not reap the harvest’s aftergrowth or gather grapes from untended vines. 📖

The land must rest a whole year.

## Leviticus 25:6

The Sabbath year’s growth is food for you, male and female servants, hired workers, and foreigners staying with you, 📖

## Leviticus 25:7

and for your livestock and wild animals. 📖

All its growth may be food.

## Leviticus 25:8

Count seven Sabbaths of years: seven times seven years, totaling forty-nine years. 📖

## Leviticus 25:9

Sound the horn throughout the land on day ten of month seven, the Day of Atonement. 📖

## Leviticus 25:10

Set year fifty apart and announce freedom for all the land’s inhabitants. 📖

It is your Jubilee.

Each must return to his property and clan.

**Help with “Jubilee”:** This is the special year of return and release described here.

## Leviticus 25:11

Year fifty is Jubilee. 📖

Do not sow, reap aftergrowth, or harvest untended vines.

## Leviticus 25:12

It is holy Jubilee. 📖

Eat only crops taken directly from the field.

## Leviticus 25:13

Each must return to his property in Jubilee. 📖

## Leviticus 25:14

Do not take advantage of each other when buying or selling. 📖

## Leviticus 25:15

Base purchases on the years since the last Jubilee. 📖

The seller must count the remaining harvest years.

## Leviticus 25:16

More years mean a higher price; fewer mean a lower price. 📖

He sells a number of harvests.

## Leviticus 25:17

Do not take advantage of each other. 📖

Fear your God.

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 25:18

Keep My laws and carefully follow My judgments to live securely in the land. 📖

## Leviticus 25:19

The land will produce fruit. 📖

You will eat your fill and live safely.

## Leviticus 25:20

You may ask, ‘What will we eat in year seven without sowing or gathering produce?’ 📖

## Leviticus 25:21

I will bless year six with enough crops for three years. 📖

## Leviticus 25:22

While sowing in year eight, you will eat the previous harvest until year nine’s harvest arrives. 📖

## Leviticus 25:23

Do not sell land permanently. 📖

It is Mine, and you are foreigners and residents with Me.

## Leviticus 25:24

Provide for redemption of every piece of land you possess. 📖

**Help with “redemption”:** This means buying back or securing release by payment.

## Leviticus 25:25

If your brother becomes poor and sells property, his nearest relative may redeem what he sold. 📖

## Leviticus 25:26

If he has no redeemer but prospers enough to redeem the land, 📖

## Leviticus 25:27

calculate the years since sale, pay the balance to the buyer, and return to the property. 📖

## Leviticus 25:28

If unable to repay, the buyer keeps it until Jubilee. 📖

Then release it so he can return.

## Leviticus 25:29

A house sold in a walled city may be redeemed during the full year after sale. 📖

## Leviticus 25:30

After a full year without redemption, it belongs permanently to the buyer and his descendants. 📖

It is not released in Jubilee.

## Leviticus 25:31

Houses in unwalled villages count as open fields. 📖

They may be redeemed and must be released in Jubilee.

## Leviticus 25:32

Levites always have the right to redeem houses in the cities they possess. 📖

## Leviticus 25:33

Their city houses may be redeemed and must be released in Jubilee. 📖

These houses are the Levites’ possession among Israel.

## Leviticus 25:34

Do not sell their cities’ surrounding pastureland. 📖

It is their permanent possession.

## Leviticus 25:35

If your countryman is too poor to support himself, help him as a foreigner or stranger to live among you. 📖

## Leviticus 25:36

Take no interest or profit from him. 📖

Fear your God so your countryman may live among you.

## Leviticus 25:37

Do not lend silver at interest or sell him food for profit. 📖

## Leviticus 25:38

I am the LORD your God who brought you from Egypt to give you Canaan and be your God. 📖

## Leviticus 25:39

If your countryman becomes poor and sells himself to you, do not force him into slave labor. 📖

## Leviticus 25:40

Treat him as a hired worker or temporary resident working until Jubilee. 📖

## Leviticus 25:41

Then release him and his children to return to his clan and fathers’ property. 📖

## Leviticus 25:42

Israelites are My servants, brought from Egypt. 📖

They must not be sold as slaves.

## Leviticus 25:43

Do not rule them harshly. 📖

Fear your God.

## Leviticus 25:44

You may buy male and female slaves from surrounding nations. 📖

## Leviticus 25:45

You may also buy resident foreigners or people from their clans living here who were born in your land. 📖

They may become your property.

## Leviticus 25:46

You may leave them to your sons as inherited property. 📖

You may enslave them for life.

But no Israelite may rule his brother harshly.

## Leviticus 25:47

If a resident foreigner prospers, but your countryman becomes poor and sells himself to him or his clan, 📖

## Leviticus 25:48

he keeps the right to redemption. 📖

One of his brothers may redeem him:

## Leviticus 25:49

His uncle, cousin, or any close clan relative. 📖

If he prospers, he may redeem himself.

## Leviticus 25:50

He and his buyer must count the years from sale until Jubilee. 📖

Base the price on those years and a hired worker’s daily wages.

## Leviticus 25:51

If many years remain, repay proportionally to the purchase price for redemption. 📖

## Leviticus 25:52

If few years remain until Jubilee, calculate redemption by the remaining years. 📖

## Leviticus 25:53

Treat him as a worker hired year by year. 📖

A foreign owner must not rule him harshly in your sight.

## Leviticus 25:54

Even if not redeemed in these ways, release him and his children in Jubilee. 📖

## Leviticus 25:55

Israelites are My servants. 📖

They are My servants whom I brought from Egypt.

I am the LORD your God.”
