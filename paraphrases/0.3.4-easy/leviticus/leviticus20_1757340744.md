# Leviticus 20 — Penalties and separation for Israel

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Moses laws for Israel with severe penalties.

The rules distinguish death, being cut off, bearing guilt, and remaining childless.

They include judgments about worship and sexual conduct.

## Leviticus 20:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 20:2

“Tell Israel: Any Israelite or resident foreigner giving children to Molech must be put to death. 📖

The people of the land must stone him.

## Leviticus 20:3

I will set My face against him and cut him off from his people. 📖

Giving children to Molech defiles My sanctuary and profanes My holy name.

**Help with these words:** God setting His face against someone pictures judgment.

Profane means treat what is holy with disrespect.

## Leviticus 20:4

If the people overlook someone giving a child to Molech and do not execute him, 📖

## Leviticus 20:5

I will turn against that man and his family. 📖

I will cut him and all who follow him in prostitution with Molech off from their people.

**Help with this image:** The prostitution language pictures unfaithful worship.

## Leviticus 20:6

I will turn against anyone seeking mediums or spiritists and prostituting himself with them. 📖

I will cut him off from his people.

**Help with these words:** Mediums and spiritists claim to communicate with spirits.

## Leviticus 20:7

Set yourselves apart and be holy. 📖

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 20:8

Keep and practice My laws. 📖

I am the LORD who sanctifies you.

**Help with “sanctifies”:** This means sets apart as holy.

## Leviticus 20:9

Anyone cursing father or mother must be put to death. 📖

He cursed his parent, and his blood is upon him.

**Help with “his blood is upon him”:** This assigns responsibility for the death judgment in this law.

## Leviticus 20:10

If a man commits adultery with his neighbor’s wife, both the man and woman must be put to death. 📖

## Leviticus 20:11

If a man has sex with his father’s wife, he uncovered his father’s nakedness. 📖

Both must be put to death.

Their blood is upon them.

**Help with this image:** Uncovering a father’s nakedness describes dishonoring him through this relationship.

## Leviticus 20:12

If a man has sex with his daughter-in-law, both must be put to death. 📖

They acted perversely.

Their blood is upon them.

## Leviticus 20:13

If a man has sex with a man as with a woman, both committed an abomination. 📖

They must be put to death.

Their blood is upon them.

**Help with “abomination”:** This is the strong word of condemnation used here.

## Leviticus 20:14

If a man marries a woman and her mother, it is depraved. 📖

The man and both women must be burned in fire so no depravity remains among you.

## Leviticus 20:15

If a man has sex with an animal, put him to death and kill the animal. 📖

## Leviticus 20:16

If a woman approaches an animal to mate with it, kill both. 📖

They must be put to death.

Their blood is upon them.

## Leviticus 20:17

If a man marries his sister, his father’s or mother’s daughter, and they have sex, it is disgraceful. 📖

They must be cut off before their people.

He uncovered his sister’s nakedness and bears guilt.

## Leviticus 20:18

If a man has sex with a menstruating woman, he exposed her flow’s source and she uncovered her blood’s source. 📖

Both must be cut off from their people.

## Leviticus 20:19

Do not have sex with your mother’s or father’s sister. 📖

That exposes your own kin.

Both bear guilt.

## Leviticus 20:20

If a man has sex with his uncle’s wife, he uncovered his uncle’s nakedness. 📖

They bear their sin and will die childless.

## Leviticus 20:21

If a man marries his brother’s wife, it is impurity. 📖

He uncovered his brother’s nakedness.

They will be childless.

## Leviticus 20:22

Keep all My laws and rulings so the land where I bring you will not vomit you out. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Vomiting people out pictures their being expelled.

## Leviticus 20:23

Do not follow the laws of the nations I drive out. 📖

I abhorred them because they did all these things.

**Help with “abhorred”:** This means strongly rejected with disgust.

## Leviticus 20:24

I promised you their land as your inheritance, a land flowing with milk and honey. 📖

I am the LORD your God who set you apart from the peoples.

**Help with “flowing with milk and honey”:** This pictures plentiful good food.

## Leviticus 20:25

Distinguish clean from unclean animals and birds. 📖

Do not become contaminated by animals, birds, or ground-crawling things I set apart as unclean.

## Leviticus 20:26

Be holy to Me because I, the LORD, am holy. 📖

I set you apart from the nations as My own.

## Leviticus 20:27

A man or woman who is a medium or spiritist must be put to death. 📖

Stone them.

Their blood is upon them.”
