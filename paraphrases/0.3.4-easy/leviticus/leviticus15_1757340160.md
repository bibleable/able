# Leviticus 15 — Bodily discharges and ritual status

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules about bodily discharges, semen, menstruation, and other bleeding.

The washing, waiting periods, and offerings differ between cases.

Clean and unclean describe ritual status, not a person’s human worth.

## Leviticus 15:1

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Leviticus 15:2

“Tell Israel: When a man has a bodily discharge, the discharge is unclean. 📖

**Help with “discharge”:** This means fluid coming from the body.

## Leviticus 15:3

This applies whether his body lets it flow or blocks it. 📖

His discharge brings uncleanness.

## Leviticus 15:4

Any bed he lies on or furniture he sits on becomes unclean. 📖

## Leviticus 15:5

Anyone touching his bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

He remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:6

Anyone sitting where that man sat must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

He remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:7

Anyone touching the man’s body must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

He remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:8

If he spits on a clean person, that person must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

He remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:9

Any saddle he rides becomes unclean. 📖

## Leviticus 15:10

Anyone touching something beneath him is unclean until evening. 📖

Anyone carrying it must wash his clothes and bathe with water, remaining unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:11

If he touches someone without first rinsing his hands with water, the person touched becomes unclean. 📖

That person must wash his clothes and bathe with water.

He remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:12

Break any clay pot he touches. 📖

Rinse any wooden tool with water.

## Leviticus 15:13

When cleansed of the discharge, he must count seven days for cleansing. 📖

Wash his clothes and bathe in fresh water.

He will be clean.

## Leviticus 15:14

On day eight, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

Give them to the priest.

## Leviticus 15:15

The priest must offer one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering. 📖

He will make atonement before the LORD for the man’s discharge.

**Help with “atonement”:** This is the ritual action for dealing with sin or uncleanness before God.

## Leviticus 15:16

After an emission of semen, a man must wash his whole body with water. 📖

He remains unclean until evening.

**Help with “semen”:** This is fluid released from the penis.

## Leviticus 15:17

Wash clothing or leather with semen on it with water. 📖

It remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:18

If a man has sex with a woman and releases semen, both must bathe with water. 📖

They remain unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:19

A woman with menstrual bleeding is unclean seven days. 📖

Anyone touching her is unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:20

Anything she lies or sits on during menstruation becomes unclean, 📖

## Leviticus 15:21

and anyone touching her bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

He remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:22

Anyone touching furniture she sat on must wash his clothes and bathe with water. 📖

He remains unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:23

Whether bed or furniture she sat on, anyone touching it is unclean until evening. 📖

## Leviticus 15:24

If a man has sex with her and her menstrual blood touches him, he is unclean seven days. 📖

Any bed he lies on becomes unclean.

## Leviticus 15:25

If she bleeds many days outside her menstrual period or beyond its end, she remains unclean throughout that discharge. 📖

This is like her menstrual uncleanness.

## Leviticus 15:26

Beds she lies on and furniture she sits on during this discharge are unclean, as during her period. 📖

## Leviticus 15:27

Anyone touching these becomes unclean. 📖

He must wash his clothes and bathe with water, remaining unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:28

After her discharge is cleansed, count seven days. 📖

Then she will be ceremonially clean.

## Leviticus 15:29

On day eight, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Leviticus 15:30

The priest must offer one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering. 📖

He will make atonement before the LORD for her unclean discharge.

## Leviticus 15:31

Keep Israel separate from their uncleanness. 📖

Otherwise they will die by defiling My tabernacle among them.

## Leviticus 15:32

This is the law for a man with a discharge or an emission of semen making him unclean, 📖

## Leviticus 15:33

a menstruating woman, any male or female with a discharge, and a man having sex with an unclean woman.” 📖
