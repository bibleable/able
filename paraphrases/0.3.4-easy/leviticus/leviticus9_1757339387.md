# Leviticus 9 — Aaron begins his priestly service

**About this chapter**

On the eighth day, Aaron offers sacrifices for himself and the people.

Moses and Aaron bless the people.

The LORD’s glory appears, and fire consumes the offerings.

## Leviticus 9:1

On day eight, Moses called Aaron, his sons, and Israel’s elders. 📖

## Leviticus 9:2

He told Aaron, “Bring a young bull as a sin offering and a ram as a burnt offering. 📖

Both must be without blemish.

Present them before the LORD.

## Leviticus 9:3

Tell Israel to bring a male goat for a sin offering. 📖

For a burnt offering, bring a calf and lamb, each a year old and without blemish,

## Leviticus 9:4

an ox and ram as a peace offering before the LORD, and a grain offering mixed with oil. 📖

Today the LORD will appear to you.”

## Leviticus 9:5

They brought what Moses commanded before the Tent of Meeting. 📖

All the people approached and stood before the LORD.

## Leviticus 9:6

Moses said, “Do what the LORD commands so His glory may appear to you.” 📖

## Leviticus 9:7

He told Aaron, “Offer your sin and burnt offerings to make atonement for yourself and the people. 📖

Offer the people’s sacrifice to make atonement for them, as the LORD commanded.”

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Leviticus 9:8

Aaron approached the altar and killed the calf as his sin offering. 📖

## Leviticus 9:9

His sons brought the blood. 📖

He dipped his finger in it and put blood on the altar’s horns.

He poured the rest at its base.

## Leviticus 9:10

He burned the sin offering’s fat, kidneys, and liver’s lobe on the altar, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Leviticus 9:11

He burned the meat and hide outside camp. 📖

## Leviticus 9:12

Aaron killed the burnt offering. 📖

His sons brought the blood, and he splattered it on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 9:13

They brought him each piece, including the head. 📖

He burned them on the altar.

## Leviticus 9:14

He washed the inner organs and legs and burned them atop the burnt offering. 📖

## Leviticus 9:15

Aaron presented the people’s offering. 📖

He killed their male sin-offering goat and offered it like the first sin offering.

## Leviticus 9:16

He presented the burnt offering according to the rule. 📖

## Leviticus 9:17

He brought the grain offering and burned a handful on the altar, besides the morning burnt offering. 📖

## Leviticus 9:18

He killed the people’s peace-offering ox and ram. 📖

His sons brought the blood, and he splattered it on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 9:19

They brought their fat portions: the fat tail, fat covering the inner organs, kidneys, and liver’s lobe, 📖

## Leviticus 9:20

and placed them on the breasts. 📖

Aaron burned the fat on the altar,

## Leviticus 9:21

but waved the breasts and right thigh before the LORD as a wave offering, as Moses commanded. 📖

## Leviticus 9:22

Aaron raised his hands toward the people and blessed them. 📖

After the sin, burnt, and peace offerings, he stepped down.

## Leviticus 9:23

Moses and Aaron entered the Tent of Meeting. 📖

They came out and blessed the people.

The LORD’s glory appeared to everyone.

## Leviticus 9:24

Fire from the LORD’s presence consumed the burnt offering and fat on the altar. 📖

Everyone saw, shouted for joy, and fell facedown.
