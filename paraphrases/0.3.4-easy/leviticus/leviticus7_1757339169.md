# Leviticus 7 — The portions and eating rules for offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives further rules for guilt, grain, and peace offerings.

The rules say who receives each portion and when meat may be eaten.

They also include restrictions about uncleanness, fat, and blood.

## Leviticus 7:1

“This is the guilt-offering law. 📖

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 7:2

Kill it where burnt offerings are killed. 📖

The priest must splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 7:3

Offer all its fat: the fat tail, the fat covering the inner organs, 📖

## Leviticus 7:4

both kidneys with their fat near the loins, and the liver’s lobe removed with the kidneys. 📖

**Help with these words:** The kidneys and liver are organs inside the body.

Loins are the lower back area.

A lobe is a part of an organ.

## Leviticus 7:5

The priest must burn these on the altar as a food offering to the LORD. 📖

It is a guilt offering.

## Leviticus 7:6

Any male priest may eat it, but only in a holy place. 📖

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 7:7

The guilt and sin offerings have the same law. 📖

They belong to the priest who makes atonement with them.

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Leviticus 7:8

The priest presenting anyone’s burnt offering receives its hide. 📖

## Leviticus 7:9

A grain offering may be baked in an oven, cooked in a pan, or prepared on a griddle. 📖

It belongs to the priest presenting it,

## Leviticus 7:10

and every grain offering, dry or mixed with oil, belongs equally to all Aaron’s sons. 📖

## Leviticus 7:11

This is the law of peace offerings presented to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 7:12

For thanksgiving, bring unleavened cakes mixed with olive oil and unleavened wafers coated with oil. 📖

Also bring well-kneaded cakes of fine flour mixed with oil.

Bring these with the thanksgiving sacrifice.

**Help with these foods:** Unleavened means made without leaven, such as yeast, that makes dough rise.

Wafers are thin baked cakes.

Kneading means pressing and working dough.

## Leviticus 7:13

Bring leavened bread cakes too with the thanksgiving peace offering. 📖

## Leviticus 7:14

Give one portion of each kind as a contribution to the LORD. 📖

It belongs to the priest sprinkling the peace offering’s blood.

## Leviticus 7:15

Eat the thanksgiving peace offering’s meat the day it is offered. 📖

Leave none until morning.

## Leviticus 7:16

For a vow or freewill sacrifice, eat it on the offering day. 📖

The rest may be eaten the next day.

**Help with these words:** A vow is a solemn promise.

A freewill offering is given willingly.

## Leviticus 7:17

Burn any meat remaining on day three. 📖

## Leviticus 7:18

If any peace-offering meat is eaten on day three, the offering is not accepted or credited to its giver. 📖

It is an abomination, and whoever eats it bears guilt.

**Help with “abomination”:** This means something deeply detestable.

## Leviticus 7:19

Do not eat meat touching anything unclean. 📖

Burn it.

Anyone ceremonially clean may eat the other meat.

**Help with these words:** Clean and unclean describe status under these worship rules.

They do not describe a person’s human worth.

## Leviticus 7:20

Anyone unclean who eats the LORD’s peace-offering meat must be cut off from his people. 📖

**Help with “cut off”:** This is a serious judgment removing a person from the people.

The verse does not say exactly how it is carried out.

## Leviticus 7:21

Anyone touching human uncleanness, an unclean animal, or any unclean detestable thing becomes unclean. 📖

If he then eats the LORD’s peace-offering meat, he must be cut off from his people.”

## Leviticus 7:22

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 7:23

“Tell Israel, ‘Eat no ox, sheep, or goat fat. 📖

## Leviticus 7:24

Fat from an animal found dead or torn by wild beasts may be used for other purposes. 📖

Do not eat it.

## Leviticus 7:25

If an animal may be presented as a food offering to the LORD, do not eat its fat. 📖

Anyone eating it must be cut off from his people.

## Leviticus 7:26

Eat no bird’s or animal’s blood in any of your homes. 📖

## Leviticus 7:27

Anyone eating blood must be cut off from his people.’” 📖

## Leviticus 7:28

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 7:29

“Tell Israel, ‘Anyone presenting a peace offering to the LORD must bring it as his sacrifice to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 7:30

Bring the food offerings with your own hands. 📖

Bring the fat and breast, and wave the breast before the LORD as a wave offering.

## Leviticus 7:31

The priest must burn the fat on the altar. 📖

The breast belongs to Aaron and his sons.

## Leviticus 7:32

Give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your peace offering. 📖

## Leviticus 7:33

The son of Aaron presenting the peace offering’s blood and fat receives the right thigh. 📖

## Leviticus 7:34

I took Israel’s waved breast and contributed thigh from their peace offerings. 📖

I gave them to Aaron the priest and his sons as their permanent portion from Israel.’”

## Leviticus 7:35

This is Aaron’s and his sons’ portion of the LORD’s food offerings from their appointment to priestly service. 📖

## Leviticus 7:36

The LORD commanded Israel to give it on the day they were anointed. 📖

It is a permanent portion through coming generations.

## Leviticus 7:37

These are the laws of burnt, grain, sin, guilt, ordination, and peace offerings, 📖

## Leviticus 7:38

which the LORD gave Moses on Mount Sinai. 📖

That day, He commanded Israel to present offerings to Him in Sinai’s wilderness.
