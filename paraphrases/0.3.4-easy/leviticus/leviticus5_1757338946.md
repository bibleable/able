# Leviticus 5 — Confession and offerings for guilt

**About this chapter**

The LORD names further cases of guilt.

The required offering varies with what a person can afford.

The chapter also gives rules for harm involving holy things and for unknowing wrongdoing.

## Leviticus 5:1

“If someone hears a public charge about something he witnessed, he must testify. 📖

If he fails, he bears guilt, whether he saw it or learned of it.

## Leviticus 5:2

Anyone touching anything unclean becomes unclean and guilty, even if unaware. 📖

This includes dead unclean wild animals, livestock, and crawling creatures.

**Help with “unclean”:** This describes a status under these worship rules.

It does not mean someone has less human worth.

## Leviticus 5:3

If someone touches any human uncleanness, he may not know at first. 📖

When he realizes it, he is guilty.

## Leviticus 5:4

If someone thoughtlessly swears to do good or evil, he may not know what he has done. 📖

Whatever the rash oath, he is guilty when he realizes it.

**Help with “oath”:** This is a solemn promise.

## Leviticus 5:5

Anyone guilty in these ways must confess the sin, 📖

## Leviticus 5:6

and bring the LORD a guilt offering for it. 📖

Bring a female lamb or goat as a sin offering.

The priest will make atonement for his sin.

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Leviticus 5:7

If he cannot afford a lamb, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the LORD for his sin. 📖

One is a sin offering, the other a burnt offering.

## Leviticus 5:8

Bring them to the priest, who must offer the sin-offering bird first. 📖

He must twist its head at the front of its neck without separating it from the body.

## Leviticus 5:9

Sprinkle some blood on the altar’s side and drain the rest at its base. 📖

It is a sin offering.

## Leviticus 5:10

The priest must prepare the second bird as a burnt offering according to the rule. 📖

He will make atonement for the person’s sin, and the person will be forgiven.

## Leviticus 5:11

If he cannot afford the two birds, bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a sin offering. 📖

Put no olive oil or frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering.

**Help with these words:** An ephah measures dry goods.

Frankincense is a fragrant material used as incense.

## Leviticus 5:12

Bring it to the priest. 📖

He must take a handful as the memorial portion and burn it atop the LORD’s food offerings.

It is a sin offering.

## Leviticus 5:13

The priest will make atonement for any of these sins, and the person will be forgiven. 📖

The rest belongs to the priest, as with the grain offering.”

## Leviticus 5:15

“If someone acts unfaithfully and unintentionally sins against the LORD’s holy things, he must bring a guilt offering. 📖

Bring a ram without blemish from the flock, of proper value in silver sanctuary shekels.

It is a guilt offering.

**Help with these words:** A ram is a male sheep.

Shekels are units of weight.

## Leviticus 5:16

Repay for the holy thing harmed and add a fifth of its value. 📖

Give it to the priest.

He will make atonement with the guilt-offering ram, and the person will be forgiven.

**Help with “a fifth”:** This means one of five equal parts, added to the full repayment.

## Leviticus 5:17

Anyone breaking the LORD’s commands, even unknowingly, is guilty and must bear punishment. 📖

## Leviticus 5:18

Bring the priest a ram without blemish and of proper value as a guilt offering. 📖

He will make atonement for the unknowing wrong, and the person will be forgiven.

## Leviticus 5:19

It is a guilt offering. 📖

He was certainly guilty before the LORD.”
