# Leviticus 1 — Burnt offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to Moses from the Tent of Meeting.

He gives instructions for burnt offerings from cattle, sheep, goats, or birds.

The person bringing the offering and the priests have different tasks.

## Leviticus 1:1

The LORD called Moses and spoke from the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Leviticus 1:2

He said, “Tell Israel: When you bring the LORD an animal offering, bring it from your herd or flock. 📖

## Leviticus 1:3

For a burnt offering from the herd, bring a male without blemish. 📖

Bring it to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance for acceptance before the LORD.

**Help with “without blemish”:** Here this means an animal without a defect.

## Leviticus 1:4

Put your hand on its head. 📖

It will be accepted for you to make atonement for you.

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Leviticus 1:5

Kill the young bull before the LORD. 📖

Aaron’s sons, the priests, must present its blood and splatter it on all sides of the altar at the entrance.

## Leviticus 1:6

Skin the animal and cut it into pieces. 📖

## Leviticus 1:7

Aaron the priest’s sons must put fire on the altar and arrange wood on it. 📖

## Leviticus 1:8

They must arrange the pieces, including head and fat, on the burning wood. 📖

## Leviticus 1:9

Wash the inner organs and legs with water. 📖

The priest must burn everything on the altar.

It is a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.

## Leviticus 1:10

For a burnt offering from sheep or goats, bring a male without blemish. 📖

## Leviticus 1:11

Kill it before the LORD on the altar’s north side. 📖

Aaron’s sons, the priests, must splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 1:12

Cut it into pieces. 📖

The priest must arrange them, including head and fat, on the altar’s burning wood.

## Leviticus 1:13

Wash the inner organs and legs with water. 📖

The priest must present everything and burn it on the altar.

It is a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.

## Leviticus 1:14

For a burnt offering of birds to the LORD, bring a turtledove or young pigeon. 📖

## Leviticus 1:15

The priest must bring it to the altar, twist off its head, and burn it there. 📖

Drain its blood onto the altar’s side.

## Leviticus 1:16

The priest must remove the crop and its contents. 📖

He must throw them on the altar’s east side, where the ashes go.

**Help with “crop”:** This is a pouch where a bird stores food.

## Leviticus 1:17

Tear it open by its wings without dividing it completely. 📖

The priest must burn it on the altar’s burning wood.

It is a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD.”
