# Leviticus 19 — Holiness and treatment of others

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Israel laws for worship and everyday conduct.

These include harvest provision, fair judgments, honest measures, and care for neighbors and foreigners.

The chapter also includes difficult laws about slavery and punishment.

## Leviticus 19:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 19:2

“Tell all Israel: Be holy because I, the LORD your God, am holy. 📖

## Leviticus 19:3

Respect your mother and father and keep My Sabbaths. 📖

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 19:4

Do not turn to idols or make cast-metal gods. 📖

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 19:5

Offer a peace sacrifice to the LORD in a way that brings acceptance for you. 📖

## Leviticus 19:6

Eat it on the offering day or the next day. 📖

Burn what remains on day three.

## Leviticus 19:7

If eaten on day three, it is tainted and will not be accepted. 📖

## Leviticus 19:8

Whoever eats it bears guilt for profaning what is holy to the LORD. 📖

That person must be cut off from his people.

## Leviticus 19:9

Do not harvest to your field’s very edges or gather what is left from harvesting. 📖

## Leviticus 19:10

Do not strip your vineyard bare or collect fallen grapes. 📖

Leave them for poor people and foreigners.

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 19:11

Do not steal, lie, or deceive one another. 📖

## Leviticus 19:12

Do not swear falsely by My name and profane your God’s name. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:13

Do not cheat or rob your neighbor. 📖

Do not keep a hired worker’s wages until morning.

## Leviticus 19:14

Do not curse deaf people or put obstacles before blind people. 📖

Fear your God.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:15

Do not twist justice, favor poor people, or favor rich people. 📖

Judge your neighbor fairly.

## Leviticus 19:16

Do not spread slander among your people or endanger your neighbor’s life. 📖

I am the LORD.

**Help with “slander”:** This means false statements that harm someone’s reputation.

## Leviticus 19:17

Do not keep hatred for your brother in your heart. 📖

Correct your neighbor directly so you do not incur guilt because of him.

## Leviticus 19:18

Do not take revenge or hold grudges against your people. 📖

Love your neighbor as yourself.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:19

Keep My laws. 📖

Do not breed two livestock kinds together.

Do not sow a field with two kinds of seed or wear clothing made from two materials.

## Leviticus 19:20

If a man has sex with a female slave promised to another man, punishment is required. 📖

This applies when she has not been redeemed or freed.

They must not be put to death because she was not free.

**About this rule:** The source gives a different penalty because she is enslaved.

## Leviticus 19:21

The man must bring a ram to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance as his guilt offering to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 19:22

The priest will make atonement before the LORD with the guilt-offering ram. 📖

The man will be forgiven his sin.

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and uncleanness before God.

## Leviticus 19:23

When you enter the land and plant food trees, regard their fruit as forbidden three years. 📖

Do not eat it.

## Leviticus 19:24

In year four, all its fruit must be set apart as a praise offering to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 19:25

In year five, you may eat it, and your harvest will increase. 📖

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 19:26

Do not eat anything with blood in it. 📖

Do not practice divination or sorcery.

**Help with these words:** Divination means seeking hidden knowledge through special signs or practices.

Sorcery means practicing magic.

## Leviticus 19:27

Do not cut off your head’s side hair or clip your beard’s edges. 📖

## Leviticus 19:28

Do not cut your bodies for the dead or put tattoo marks on yourselves. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:29

Do not defile your daughter by making her a prostitute. 📖

Otherwise the land becomes prostituted and filled with depravity.

**Help with these words:** A prostitute provides sexual acts for payment or benefits.

Depravity means serious wrongdoing.

## Leviticus 19:30

Keep My Sabbaths and revere My sanctuary. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:31

Do not turn to mediums or spiritists or seek them out. 📖

They will defile you.

I am the LORD your God.

**Help with these words:** These are people who claim to communicate with spirits.

## Leviticus 19:32

Stand in the presence of elderly people, honor them, and fear your God. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:33

Do not oppress foreigners living in your land. 📖

## Leviticus 19:34

Treat a resident foreigner as native-born and love him as yourself. 📖

You were foreigners in Egypt.

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 19:35

Do not use dishonest measures of length, weight, or volume. 📖

## Leviticus 19:36

Keep honest scales, weights, ephah, and hin. 📖

I am the LORD your God who brought you from Egypt.

**Help with these units:** An ephah measures dry goods, and a hin measures liquids.

## Leviticus 19:37

Keep and follow all My laws and rulings. 📖

I am the LORD.”
