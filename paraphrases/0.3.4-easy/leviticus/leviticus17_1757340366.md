# Leviticus 17 — Sacrifices, blood, and life

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Moses commands for Aaron, his sons, and Israel.

Sacrifices must be brought to the Tent of Meeting.

The chapter also gives rules about blood, hunting, and eating animals found dead.

## Leviticus 17:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 17:2

“Tell Aaron, his sons, and all Israel what the LORD commands. 📖

## Leviticus 17:3

Anyone of Israel who kills an ox, lamb, or goat inside or outside camp, 📖

## Leviticus 17:4

He must bring it to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance, before the LORD’s tabernacle, as an offering to Him. 📖

Otherwise he incurs bloodguilt.

He shed blood and must be cut off from his people.

**Help with these words:** Bloodguilt means guilt for shedding blood.

“Cut off” is a serious judgment removing a person from the people.

This verse does not explain exactly how it is carried out.

## Leviticus 17:5

Israel must bring the LORD the sacrifices formerly offered in open fields. 📖

Bring them to the priest at the tent’s entrance as peace offerings to the LORD.

## Leviticus 17:6

The priest must splatter blood on the LORD’s altar at the tent’s entrance. 📖

Burn the fat as a pleasing smell to the LORD.

## Leviticus 17:7

Stop sacrificing to the goat demons to whom they prostituted themselves. 📖

This is a permanent law through coming generations.

**Help with these words:** “Goat demons” is the name used in BSB.

Prostituting themselves pictures unfaithful worship.

## Leviticus 17:8

Tell them: If any Israelite or resident foreigner offers a burnt offering or sacrifice, 📖

## Leviticus 17:9

he must bring it to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance to sacrifice it to the LORD. 📖

Otherwise he must be cut off from his people.

## Leviticus 17:10

If any Israelite or resident foreigner eats blood, I will set My face against him. 📖

I will cut him off from his people.

**Help with “set My face against”:** This pictures God turning against someone in judgment.

## Leviticus 17:11

The life of the flesh is in the blood. 📖

I gave it on the altar to make atonement for your souls.

Blood makes atonement for the soul.

**Help with these words:** Atonement deals with sin and uncleanness before God.

Here the text connects soul, life, and blood.

## Leviticus 17:12

I tell Israel: None of you or your resident foreigners may eat blood. 📖

## Leviticus 17:13

If an Israelite or resident foreigner hunts an edible wild animal or bird, he must drain its blood. 📖

Cover it with dirt.

## Leviticus 17:14

Every living thing’s life is its blood. 📖

I told Israel not to eat any living thing’s blood, because life is in it.

Anyone eating it must be cut off.

## Leviticus 17:15

If a native or foreigner eats an animal found dead or torn by beasts, he must wash his clothes. 📖

He must also bathe with water.

He remains unclean until evening, then becomes clean.

## Leviticus 17:16

If he does not wash his clothes and bathe, he bears his guilt.” 📖
