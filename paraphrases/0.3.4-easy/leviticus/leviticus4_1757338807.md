# Leviticus 4 — Sin offerings for unintentional wrongdoing

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives different procedures for an anointed priest, the whole congregation, a leader, and an ordinary person.

Each procedure includes the offering, blood, fat, and atonement.

The repeated details belong to each case.

## Leviticus 4:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 4:2

“Tell Israel what to do when someone sins unintentionally, breaking any of the LORD’s commands by doing something forbidden. 📖

**Help with “unintentionally”:** This means without meaning to do it.

## Leviticus 4:3

If the anointed priest sins and brings guilt on the people, he must bring the LORD a sin offering. 📖

It must be a young bull without blemish for his sin.

**Help with these words:** Anointed means set apart with oil.

Without blemish means without a defect.

## Leviticus 4:4

Bring it before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

Put a hand on its head and kill it before the LORD.

## Leviticus 4:5

The anointed priest must take some blood inside the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Leviticus 4:6

He must dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD. 📖

Do this in front of the sanctuary’s veil.

## Leviticus 4:7

Put some blood on the incense altar’s horns before the LORD inside the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Pour the rest at the burnt-offering altar’s base, at the tent’s entrance.

**Help with “horns”:** These are projections at the altar’s corners.

## Leviticus 4:8

Remove all the sin-offering bull’s fat covering the inner organs and all fat on them, 📖

## Leviticus 4:9

both kidneys with their fat near the loins, and the liver’s lobe with the kidneys, 📖

**Help with these words:** The kidneys and liver are organs inside the body.

Loins are the lower back area.

A lobe is a part of an organ.

## Leviticus 4:10

as with the peace-offering ox’s fat. 📖

The priest must burn these on the burnt-offering altar.

## Leviticus 4:11

But take the hide, all the meat, head, legs, inner organs, and dung, 📖

## Leviticus 4:12

all the rest of the bull, outside camp to the ceremonially clean place where ashes are poured. 📖

Burn it there on a wood fire on the ash heap.

**Help with these words:** Ceremonially clean means meeting the requirements of these worship rules.

Dung is animal waste.

## Leviticus 4:13

If all Israel’s congregation sins unintentionally without noticing, they incur guilt by breaking any of the LORD’s commands. 📖

## Leviticus 4:14

When they discover their sin, the assembly must bring a young bull as a sin offering. 📖

Present it before the Tent of Meeting.

## Leviticus 4:15

The elders must put their hands on its head before the LORD. 📖

Kill it before the LORD.

## Leviticus 4:16

The anointed priest must bring some blood inside the Tent of Meeting, 📖

## Leviticus 4:17

dip his finger in it, and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD in front of the veil. 📖

## Leviticus 4:18

Put some on the altar’s horns before the LORD inside the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Pour the rest at the burnt-offering altar’s base, at the tent’s entrance.

## Leviticus 4:19

Remove all the fat and burn it on the altar. 📖

## Leviticus 4:20

Offer this bull just like the first sin-offering bull. 📖

The priest will make atonement for them, and they will be forgiven.

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Leviticus 4:21

Take the bull outside camp and burn it like the first. 📖

It is the assembly’s sin offering.

## Leviticus 4:22

If a leader unintentionally does anything the LORD his God forbids, he incurs guilt. 📖

## Leviticus 4:23

When he discovers his sin, he must bring a male goat without blemish. 📖

## Leviticus 4:24

He must put his hand on its head and kill it before the LORD where burnt offerings are killed. 📖

It is a sin offering.

## Leviticus 4:25

The priest must take some blood with his finger and put it on the burnt-offering altar’s horns. 📖

Pour the rest at the altar’s base.

## Leviticus 4:26

Burn all its fat on the altar, like the peace offerings’ fat. 📖

The priest will make atonement for the leader’s sin, and he will be forgiven.

## Leviticus 4:27

If an ordinary person unintentionally does anything the LORD forbids, he incurs guilt. 📖

## Leviticus 4:28

When he discovers his sin, he must bring a female goat without blemish for that sin. 📖

## Leviticus 4:29

He must put his hand on its head and kill it at the burnt-offering place. 📖

## Leviticus 4:30

The priest must take some blood with his finger and put it on the burnt-offering altar’s horns. 📖

Pour the rest at the altar’s base.

## Leviticus 4:31

Remove all the fat as with the peace offering. 📖

The priest must burn it as a pleasing smell to the LORD.

He will make atonement for the person, who will be forgiven.

## Leviticus 4:32

If bringing a lamb as a sin offering, bring a female without blemish. 📖

## Leviticus 4:33

Put your hand on its head. 📖

Kill it as a sin offering where burnt offerings are killed.

## Leviticus 4:34

The priest must take some blood with his finger and put it on the burnt-offering altar’s horns. 📖

Pour the rest at the altar’s base.

## Leviticus 4:35

Remove all the fat as with a peace-offering lamb. 📖

Burn it on the altar with the LORD’s food offerings.

The priest will make atonement for the person’s sin, and he will be forgiven.”
