# Leviticus 27 — Values for vows and dedicated property

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives values and rules for vows involving people, animals, houses, and land.

The values differ by age and sex.

The chapter distinguishes things that may be bought back from things that may not.

## Leviticus 27:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 27:2

“Tell Israel: When someone makes a special vow to the LORD involving people’s value, 📖

## Leviticus 27:3

value a male aged twenty to sixty at fifty silver sanctuary shekels. 📖

**Help with these values:** These are payments for the vows described here.

Shekels are units of weight.

## Leviticus 27:4

For a female, the value is thirty shekels. 📖

## Leviticus 27:5

From ages five to twenty, value a male at twenty shekels and a female at ten. 📖

## Leviticus 27:6

From one month to five years, value a male at five silver shekels and a female at three. 📖

## Leviticus 27:7

At sixty or older, value a male at fifteen shekels and a female at ten. 📖

## Leviticus 27:8

If the vow-maker cannot afford the value, present the person before the priest. 📖

He must set the value according to what the vow-maker can afford.

## Leviticus 27:9

An animal vowed as an offering to the LORD becomes holy. 📖

## Leviticus 27:10

Do not replace it, good for bad or bad for good. 📖

If substituted, both animals become holy.

## Leviticus 27:11

Present an unclean vowed animal that cannot be offered to the LORD before the priest. 📖

## Leviticus 27:12

The priest sets its value, high or low. 📖

That price stands.

## Leviticus 27:13

If the owner redeems it, add a fifth of its value. 📖

**Help with “redeems”:** This means buys back.

A fifth is one of five equal parts, added to the full value.

## Leviticus 27:14

If a man sets his house apart as holy to the LORD, the priest must value it, good or bad. 📖

That value stands.

## Leviticus 27:15

If he redeems his dedicated house, add a fifth to the value. 📖

It will be his.

## Leviticus 27:16

If he dedicates part of his own land to the LORD, set its value by the seed needed. 📖

Charge fifty silver shekels per homer of barley seed.

**Help with “homer”:** This is a measure of dry goods.

## Leviticus 27:17

If dedicated in Jubilee, the full value stands. 📖

## Leviticus 27:18

If dedicated after Jubilee, the priest must calculate years left until the next Jubilee and reduce the value. 📖

## Leviticus 27:19

If its dedicator redeems the field, add a fifth to the value. 📖

The field will be his.

## Leviticus 27:20

If he does not redeem it, or sold it to someone else, it cannot be redeemed again. 📖

## Leviticus 27:21

When released in Jubilee, it becomes holy as land devoted to the LORD. 📖

It belongs to the priests.

## Leviticus 27:22

If a man dedicates a purchased field not part of his own property, 📖

## Leviticus 27:23

The priest must calculate its value until Jubilee. 📖

The man must pay it that day as a sacred offering to the LORD.

## Leviticus 27:24

In Jubilee, return it to the seller, the land’s original owner. 📖

## Leviticus 27:25

Use the sanctuary shekel for every valuation, twenty gerahs per shekel. 📖

## Leviticus 27:26

Do not consecrate a firstborn of the livestock, because it already belongs to the LORD. 📖

Ox or sheep, it is the LORD’s.

## Leviticus 27:27

An unclean animal may be redeemed at its value plus a fifth. 📖

If not redeemed, sell it at the assessed value.

## Leviticus 27:28

Nothing devoted to the LORD from a man’s possessions may be sold or redeemed. 📖

This includes a person, animal, or inherited land.

Everything devoted is most holy to the LORD.

## Leviticus 27:29

No person set apart for destruction may be ransomed. 📖

He must be put to death.

**Help with “ransomed”:** This means released by payment.

## Leviticus 27:30

Every land tithe, from seed or tree fruit, belongs to the LORD and is holy. 📖

**Help with “tithe”:** This means one-tenth.

## Leviticus 27:31

To redeem part of a tithe, add a fifth of its value. 📖

## Leviticus 27:32

Every tenth herd or flock animal passing under the shepherd’s rod is holy to the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 27:33

Do not inspect good or bad or substitute animals. 📖

If substituted, both animals become holy and cannot be redeemed.”

## Leviticus 27:34

These are the commands the LORD gave Moses for Israel on Mount Sinai. 📖
