# Leviticus 18 — Forbidden sexual relations and worship

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Israel laws about sexual relations and worship.

The prohibited relationships are named separately.

The chapter includes strong judgments and an image of the land expelling its inhabitants.

## Leviticus 18:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 18:2

“Tell Israel: I am the LORD your God. 📖

## Leviticus 18:3

Do not follow Egypt’s practices, where you lived, or Canaan’s practices, where I am bringing you. 📖

Do not follow their customs.

## Leviticus 18:4

Follow My judgments and laws. 📖

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 18:5

Keep My laws and judgments. 📖

Whoever does them will live by them.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 18:6

Do not approach a close relative for sexual relations. 📖

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 18:7

Do not expose your father’s nakedness by having sex with your mother. 📖

She is your mother.

Do not have sex with her.

**Help with this image:** The language about a father’s nakedness describes dishonoring him through this relationship.

## Leviticus 18:8

Do not have sex with your father’s wife. 📖

That dishonors your father.

## Leviticus 18:9

Do not have sex with your sister, your father’s or mother’s daughter, born in your home or elsewhere. 📖

## Leviticus 18:10

Do not have sex with your son’s daughter or daughter’s daughter. 📖

That shames your family.

## Leviticus 18:11

Do not have sex with your father’s wife’s daughter born to your father. 📖

She is your sister.

## Leviticus 18:12

Do not have sex with your father’s sister. 📖

She is his close relative.

## Leviticus 18:13

Do not have sex with your mother’s sister. 📖

She is her close relative.

## Leviticus 18:14

Do not dishonor your father’s brother by having sex with his wife. 📖

She is your aunt.

## Leviticus 18:15

Do not have sex with your daughter-in-law. 📖

She is your son’s wife.

Do not have sex with her.

## Leviticus 18:16

Do not have sex with your brother’s wife. 📖

That shames your brother.

## Leviticus 18:17

Do not have sex with both a woman and her daughter. 📖

Do not marry her son’s daughter or daughter’s daughter and have sex with her.

They are close relatives.

It is depraved.

**Help with “depraved”:** This is a strong judgment of serious wrongdoing.

## Leviticus 18:18

While your wife lives, do not marry her sister as a rival wife and have sex with her. 📖

## Leviticus 18:19

Do not approach a woman for sex during her menstrual period. 📖

## Leviticus 18:20

Do not have sex with your neighbor’s wife and defile yourself with her. 📖

**Help with “defile”:** This means make unclean or dishonored.

## Leviticus 18:21

Do not give any children to be sacrificed to Molech. 📖

Do not profane your God’s name.

I am the LORD.

**Help with “profane”:** This means treat what is holy with disrespect.

## Leviticus 18:22

Do not have sex with a man as with a woman. 📖

It is an abomination.

**Help with “abomination”:** This is the strong word of condemnation used here.

## Leviticus 18:23

Do not have sex with any animal and defile yourself. 📖

A woman must not present herself to an animal to mate with it.

It is a perversion.

## Leviticus 18:24

Do not defile yourselves with these practices. 📖

The nations I drive out defiled themselves by all these things.

## Leviticus 18:25

The land became defiled. 📖

I am punishing it for its sin, and it will vomit out its inhabitants.

**Help with this picture:** The land vomiting people out pictures their being expelled from it.

## Leviticus 18:26

Keep My laws and rulings. 📖

No native-born person or resident foreigner may commit these abominations.

## Leviticus 18:27

The people before you did all these abominations and defiled the land. 📖

## Leviticus 18:28

If you defile it, it will vomit you out as it expelled those nations. 📖

## Leviticus 18:29

Anyone doing these abominations must be cut off from his people. 📖

**Help with “cut off”:** This is a serious judgment removing a person from the people.

## Leviticus 18:30

Keep My command against those earlier abominable customs, so you do not defile yourselves. 📖

I am the LORD your God.”
