# Leviticus 14 — Cleansing after disease and mildew in houses

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules for cleansing a person whose skin disease has healed.

A less costly offering is included for someone who is poor.

The chapter then gives rules for examining and cleansing houses in Canaan.

## Leviticus 14:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 14:2

“This is the law for a person with skin disease on the day of cleansing. 📖

Bring him to the priest.

## Leviticus 14:3

The priest must go outside camp to examine him. 📖

If the disease healed,

## Leviticus 14:4

bring two live clean birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop for his cleansing, as the priest orders. 📖

**Help with these words:** Cedar is a kind of tree.

Scarlet means bright red.

Hyssop is a plant used in these rites.

## Leviticus 14:5

The priest must order one bird killed over fresh water in a clay pot. 📖

## Leviticus 14:6

Take the live bird, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop. 📖

Dip them in the blood of the bird killed over the fresh water.

## Leviticus 14:7

He must sprinkle the person seven times, declare him clean, and release the live bird into the open field. 📖

## Leviticus 14:8

The person must wash his clothes, shave all his hair, and bathe with water. 📖

He will be ceremonially clean and may enter camp.

He must stay outside his tent seven days.

**Help with “ceremonially clean”:** This means meeting the requirements of these worship rules.

## Leviticus 14:9

On day seven, shave all hair: head, beard, eyebrows, and the rest. 📖

Wash clothes and bathe with water to be clean.

## Leviticus 14:10

On day eight, bring two male lambs without blemish and a year-old ewe lamb without blemish. 📖

For a grain offering, bring three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with olive oil.

Also bring one log of olive oil.

**Help with these words:** A ewe is a female sheep.

An ephah measures dry goods.

A log is a liquid measure here, not a piece of wood.

## Leviticus 14:11

The cleansing priest must present him and these offerings before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Leviticus 14:12

Take one male lamb as a guilt offering with the log of olive oil. 📖

Wave them before the LORD as a wave offering.

## Leviticus 14:13

Kill the lamb in the sanctuary area where sin and burnt offerings are killed. 📖

Like the sin offering, the guilt offering belongs to the priest.

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 14:14

The priest must put guilt-offering blood on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe. 📖

## Leviticus 14:15

The priest must take some of the log of olive oil and pour it into his left palm, 📖

## Leviticus 14:16

dip his right forefinger in it, and sprinkle oil seven times before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 14:17

Put some remaining oil on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe. 📖

Put it on top of the guilt-offering blood.

## Leviticus 14:18

Put the rest of the palm’s oil on the person’s head to make atonement before the LORD. 📖

**Help with “atonement”:** This is the ritual action for dealing with sin or uncleanness before God.

## Leviticus 14:19

The priest must offer the sin offering for atonement and cleansing from uncleanness. 📖

Then kill the burnt offering

## Leviticus 14:20

and offer it on the altar with the grain offering. 📖

Make atonement for him, and he will be clean.

## Leviticus 14:21

If he is poor and cannot afford these, bring one male lamb for a waved guilt offering and atonement. 📖

Bring a grain offering of a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with olive oil.

Bring a log of olive oil,

## Leviticus 14:22

and two turtledoves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford. 📖

One is a sin offering, the other a burnt offering.

## Leviticus 14:23

On day eight, bring these for cleansing before the LORD to the priest at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Leviticus 14:24

The priest must wave the guilt-offering lamb and log of olive oil before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 14:25

Kill the guilt-offering lamb. 📖

The priest must put its blood on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe.

## Leviticus 14:26

He must pour oil into his left palm 📖

## Leviticus 14:27

and use his right forefinger to sprinkle oil from that palm seven times before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 14:28

Put oil from his palm on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe. 📖

Use the same places as the guilt-offering blood.

## Leviticus 14:29

Put the rest of the palm’s oil on the person’s head to make atonement before the LORD. 📖

## Leviticus 14:30

Then sacrifice the turtledoves or young pigeons, whichever he can afford, 📖

## Leviticus 14:31

one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering, with the grain offering. 📖

The priest will make atonement for him before the LORD.

## Leviticus 14:32

This is the law for someone with skin disease who cannot afford his cleansing.” 📖

## Leviticus 14:33

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Leviticus 14:34

“When you enter Canaan, the land I give you to possess, and I put mildew in a house there, 📖

## Leviticus 14:35

its owner must tell the priest, ‘Something like mildew appeared in my house.’ 📖

## Leviticus 14:36

The priest must order it emptied before examining it, so nothing inside becomes unclean. 📖

Then he must inspect the house.

## Leviticus 14:37

If the walls have green or red hollows looking deeper than the wall’s surface, 📖

## Leviticus 14:38

he must go outside the doorway and close the house seven days. 📖

## Leviticus 14:39

On day seven, return and inspect. 📖

If the mildew spread on the walls,

## Leviticus 14:40

order the affected stones removed and thrown in an unclean place outside the city. 📖

## Leviticus 14:41

Have all the inside scraped. 📖

Dump the scraped plaster in an unclean place outside the city.

## Leviticus 14:42

Replace the stones with different ones and use more mortar to replaster it. 📖

**Help with these words:** Mortar joins building stones.

Plaster covers walls.

## Leviticus 14:43

If mildew returns after the stones were removed and the house scraped and replastered, 📖

## Leviticus 14:44

the priest must inspect it. 📖

If it spread, it is destructive mildew.

The house is unclean.

## Leviticus 14:45

Tear down the house, including stones, timbers, and all plaster. 📖

Take them to an unclean place outside the city.

## Leviticus 14:46

Anyone entering while it is closed is unclean until evening. 📖

## Leviticus 14:47

Anyone sleeping or eating there must wash his clothes. 📖

## Leviticus 14:48

If the mildew did not spread after replastering, the inspecting priest must declare it clean. 📖

The mildew is gone.

## Leviticus 14:49

Take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop to purify it. 📖

## Leviticus 14:50

Kill one bird over fresh water in a clay pot. 📖

## Leviticus 14:51

Dip the cedar, hyssop, scarlet yarn, and live bird in the killed bird’s blood and fresh water. 📖

Sprinkle the house seven times.

## Leviticus 14:52

Cleanse the house with the bird’s blood, fresh water, live bird, cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet yarn. 📖

## Leviticus 14:53

Release the live bird into open fields outside the city. 📖

Make atonement for the house, and it will be clean.

## Leviticus 14:54

This is the law for infectious skin disease and scaly outbreaks, 📖

## Leviticus 14:55

for mildew in clothes or houses, 📖

## Leviticus 14:56

and for swellings, rashes, or spots, 📖

## Leviticus 14:57

to determine what is clean or unclean. 📖

This is the law for skin diseases and mildew.”
