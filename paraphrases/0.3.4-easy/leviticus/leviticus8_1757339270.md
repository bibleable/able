# Leviticus 8 — Aaron and his sons are ordained

**About this chapter**

Moses follows the LORD’s commands to set Aaron and his sons apart.

He washes and clothes them and presents the sacrifices.

They must remain at the Tent of Meeting for seven days.

## Leviticus 8:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Leviticus 8:2

“Take Aaron and his sons, their garments, anointing oil, the sin-offering bull, two rams, and the unleavened bread basket. 📖

## Leviticus 8:3

Gather all the people at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance.” 📖

## Leviticus 8:4

Moses obeyed the LORD, and the assembly gathered at the entrance. 📖

## Leviticus 8:5

He said, “This is what the LORD commands to be done.” 📖

## Leviticus 8:6

Moses brought Aaron and his sons and washed them with water. 📖

## Leviticus 8:7

He dressed Aaron in the tunic, tied the sash, and put on the robe and ephod. 📖

He tied the ephod’s woven band around him and fastened it.

**Help with “ephod”:** This is a priest’s special garment.

## Leviticus 8:8

He put the breastpiece on Aaron and placed the Urim and Thummim inside. 📖

**About these names:** BSB names the Urim and Thummim without describing their appearance here.

## Leviticus 8:9

He put the turban on Aaron’s head and the gold plate, the holy diadem, at its front. 📖

This was as the LORD commanded.

**Help with these words:** A turban wraps around the head.

The diadem is the special head ornament.

## Leviticus 8:10

Moses applied anointing oil to the tabernacle and everything inside, setting them apart for God. 📖

## Leviticus 8:11

He sprinkled oil on the altar seven times. 📖

He anointed it, all its tools, and the basin and stand to set them apart.

## Leviticus 8:12

He poured anointing oil on Aaron’s head to set him apart for God. 📖

## Leviticus 8:13

Moses dressed Aaron’s sons in tunics, tied their sashes and headbands, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Leviticus 8:14

He brought the sin-offering bull. 📖

Aaron and his sons put their hands on its head.

## Leviticus 8:15

Moses killed it and used his finger to put blood on all four altar horns, purifying the altar. 📖

He poured the rest at its base and set it apart for atonement.

**Help with these words:** The horns are projections at the corners.

Atonement deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Leviticus 8:16

He burned all the fat on the inner organs, the liver’s lobe, both kidneys, and their fat on the altar. 📖

## Leviticus 8:17

He burned the bull’s hide, meat, and dung outside camp, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Leviticus 8:18

Moses presented the burnt-offering ram. 📖

Aaron and his sons put their hands on its head.

## Leviticus 8:19

Moses killed it and splattered its blood on all sides of the altar. 📖

## Leviticus 8:20

He cut it into pieces and burned the head, pieces, and fat. 📖

## Leviticus 8:21

He washed the inner organs and legs with water and burned the whole ram on the altar. 📖

It was a burnt offering, a pleasing smell and food offering to the LORD, as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Leviticus 8:22

He presented the other ram, for ordination. 📖

Aaron and his sons put their hands on its head.

**Help with “ordination”:** This means appointment to priestly service.

## Leviticus 8:23

Moses killed it and put blood on Aaron’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe. 📖

## Leviticus 8:24

He brought Aaron’s sons and put blood on their right earlobes, right thumbs, and right big toes. 📖

He splattered blood on all sides of the altar.

## Leviticus 8:25

He took the fat tail, all fat on the inner organs, liver’s lobe, both kidneys with fat, and right thigh. 📖

## Leviticus 8:26

He took bread from the unleavened bread basket before the LORD. 📖

He took one unleavened cake, one cake made with oil, and one wafer.

He placed them on the fat portions and right thigh.

## Leviticus 8:27

He put these in Aaron’s and his sons’ hands and waved them before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖

## Leviticus 8:28

Moses took them from their hands and burned them with the burnt offering on the altar. 📖

It was an ordination offering, a pleasing smell and food offering to the LORD.

## Leviticus 8:29

He waved the breast before the LORD as a wave offering, as the LORD commanded. 📖

It was Moses’ portion of the ordination ram.

## Leviticus 8:30

Moses sprinkled anointing oil and altar blood on Aaron, his sons, and their garments. 📖

He set Aaron, his sons, and all their garments apart for God.

## Leviticus 8:31

Moses told them, “Boil the meat at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

Eat it there with the ordination basket’s bread.

As I commanded, ‘Aaron and his sons are to eat it.’

## Leviticus 8:32

Burn the leftover meat and bread. 📖

## Leviticus 8:33

Do not leave the Tent of Meeting’s entrance for seven days, until your ordination is complete. 📖

It takes seven days to ordain you.

## Leviticus 8:34

The LORD commanded today’s actions to make atonement for you. 📖

## Leviticus 8:35

Stay at the entrance day and night for seven days. 📖

Keep the LORD’s command so you will not die.

This is what I was commanded.”

## Leviticus 8:36

Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖
