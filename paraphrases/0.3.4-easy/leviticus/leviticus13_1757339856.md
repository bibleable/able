# Leviticus 13 — Examinations of skin and fabric

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives priests rules for examining skin, hair, and fabric.

Each finding leads to a specified ritual judgment.

The descriptions follow BSB without assigning a modern disease name.

Clean and unclean describe status under these rules, not a person’s human worth.

## Leviticus 13:1

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Leviticus 13:2

“A swelling, rash, or bright skin spot may be an infectious skin disease. 📖

Bring the person to Aaron the priest or one of his priestly sons.

## Leviticus 13:3

The priest must examine it. 📖

If the hair turned white and the sore looks deeper than the skin, it is a skin disease.

After examining, he must declare the person unclean.

## Leviticus 13:4

If the spot is white but not deeper than the skin, and the hair has not turned white, isolate him. 📖

The priest must keep him isolated seven days.

## Leviticus 13:5

On day seven, the priest must examine him again. 📖

If unchanged and not spreading, isolate him another seven days.

## Leviticus 13:6

On day seven, examine him again. 📖

If faded and not spreading, declare him clean.

It is a rash.

He must wash his clothes and be clean.

## Leviticus 13:7

If the rash spreads after he was shown to the priest for cleansing, he must return to the priest. 📖

## Leviticus 13:8

The priest must examine him again. 📖

If the rash spread, declare him unclean.

It is a skin disease.

## Leviticus 13:9

Bring anyone developing a skin disease to the priest. 📖

## Leviticus 13:10

The priest must examine him. 📖

If a white swelling has turned the hair white and contains raw flesh,

**Help with “raw flesh”:** This means exposed flesh.

## Leviticus 13:11

it is a chronic skin disease. 📖

Declare him unclean.

There is no need to isolate him, because he is unclean.

**Help with “chronic”:** This means lasting a long time.

## Leviticus 13:12

If the disease covers all his skin from head to foot, as far as the priest can see, 📖

## Leviticus 13:13

examine him. 📖

If it covers the whole body, declare the person clean.

It has all turned white, so he is clean.

## Leviticus 13:14

Whenever raw flesh appears, he is unclean. 📖

## Leviticus 13:15

When the priest sees raw flesh, he must declare the person unclean. 📖

The raw flesh is unclean.

It is a skin disease.

## Leviticus 13:16

If the raw flesh changes and turns white, he must go to the priest. 📖

## Leviticus 13:17

The priest must examine him again. 📖

If the infection turned white, declare him clean.

He is clean.

## Leviticus 13:18

When a boil on someone’s skin heals, 📖

## Leviticus 13:19

but a white swelling or reddish-white spot appears there, he must show it to the priest. 📖

## Leviticus 13:20

If it looks beneath the skin and the hair turned white, the priest must declare him unclean. 📖

A diseased infection has broken out in the boil.

## Leviticus 13:21

The priest must examine it. 📖

If there is no white hair, the spot is not beneath the skin, and it faded, isolate him seven days.

## Leviticus 13:22

If it spreads farther on the skin, declare him unclean. 📖

It is an infection.

## Leviticus 13:23

If the spot is unchanged and does not spread, it is a boil’s scar. 📖

Declare him clean.

## Leviticus 13:24

If a skin burn’s raw area becomes reddish-white or white, 📖

## Leviticus 13:25

examine it. 📖

If its hair turned white and the spot looks deeper than the skin, disease has broken out in the burn.

Declare him unclean.

It is a diseased infection.

## Leviticus 13:26

The priest must examine it. 📖

If there is no white hair, the spot is not beneath the skin, and it faded, isolate him seven days.

## Leviticus 13:27

On day seven, examine him again. 📖

If it spread farther on the skin, declare him unclean.

It is a diseased infection.

## Leviticus 13:28

If unchanged, not spreading, and faded, it is swelling from the burn. 📖

The priest must declare him clean.

It is only a burn’s scar.

## Leviticus 13:29

If a man or woman has an infection on the head or chin, 📖

## Leviticus 13:30

examine it. 📖

If it looks deeper than the skin with thin yellow hair, declare the person unclean.

It is a scaly outbreak, an infectious head or chin disease.

## Leviticus 13:31

The priest must examine the scaly infection. 📖

If it is not deeper than the skin and has no black hair, isolate the person seven days.

## Leviticus 13:32

On day seven, examine the outbreak again. 📖

If it has not spread, has no yellow hair, and is not deeper than the skin,

## Leviticus 13:33

he must shave except for the scaly area. 📖

The priest must isolate him another seven days.

## Leviticus 13:34

On day seven, examine it. 📖

If it has not spread and is not deeper than the skin, declare him clean.

He must wash his clothes and be clean.

## Leviticus 13:35

If the outbreak spreads after his cleansing, 📖

## Leviticus 13:36

examine him. 📖

If it spread, the priest need not look for yellow hair.

The person is unclean.

## Leviticus 13:37

If the outbreak is unchanged and black hair grew in it, it has healed. 📖

He is clean, and the priest must declare him clean.

## Leviticus 13:38

If a man or woman has white skin spots, 📖

## Leviticus 13:39

the priest must examine them. 📖

If dull white, it is a harmless rash.

The person is clean.

## Leviticus 13:40

A man losing his hair and becoming bald is clean. 📖

## Leviticus 13:41

A man whose hairline recedes, leaving his forehead bald, is clean too. 📖

## Leviticus 13:42

If a reddish-white sore appears on a bald head or forehead, an infectious disease is breaking out. 📖

## Leviticus 13:43

The priest must examine him. 📖

If the swelling on the bald head or forehead is reddish-white like a skin disease,

## Leviticus 13:44

he is diseased and unclean. 📖

The priest must declare him unclean because of the head infection.

## Leviticus 13:45

The diseased person must wear torn clothes, leave his hair loose, cover his mouth, and cry, ‘Unclean, unclean!’ 📖

## Leviticus 13:46

He remains unclean while the infection lasts. 📖

He must live alone outside camp.

## Leviticus 13:47

If mildew contaminates wool or linen fabric, 📖

## Leviticus 13:48

a linen or wool weave or knit, or any leather article, 📖

## Leviticus 13:49

and its mark is green or red, it is contaminated with mildew. 📖

Show the fabric, leather, weave, knit, or leather article to the priest.

## Leviticus 13:50

The priest must examine the mildew and isolate the fabric seven days. 📖

## Leviticus 13:51

On day seven, examine it again. 📖

If mildew spread in the fabric, weave, knit, or leather, it is harmful mildew.

This applies whatever the item’s use.

It is unclean.

## Leviticus 13:52

Burn the contaminated fabric, weave, knit, or leather article, whether wool, linen, or leather. 📖

The mildew is harmful, so burn it up.

## Leviticus 13:53

If the priest sees the mildew has not spread in the fabric, weave, knit, or leather, 📖

## Leviticus 13:54

he must order it washed and isolated another seven days. 📖

## Leviticus 13:55

Examine it after washing. 📖

If its appearance did not change, it is unclean, even without spreading.

Burn it, whether the rot is on the front or back.

## Leviticus 13:56

If the mildew faded after washing, cut the affected part out of the fabric, leather, weave, or knit. 📖

## Leviticus 13:57

If it returns in the fabric, weave, knit, or leather article, it is spreading. 📖

Burn the contaminated item.

## Leviticus 13:58

If washing removed it from fabric, weave, knit, or leather, wash the item again. 📖

It will be clean.

## Leviticus 13:59

This is the law for judging mildew in wool or linen fabric, weave, knit, or leather articles clean or unclean.” 📖
