# Leviticus 10 — Nadab and Abihu die

**About this chapter**

Aaron’s sons Nadab and Abihu offer fire the LORD did not authorize.

They die before Him.

The chapter gives further instructions and records a difficult exchange about eating a sin offering.

## Leviticus 10:1

Aaron’s sons Nadab and Abihu put fire and incense in their censers. 📖

They offered unauthorized fire before the LORD, against His command.

**Help with these words:** Censers are containers for burning incense.

Unauthorized means not permitted.

## Leviticus 10:2

Fire came from the LORD’s presence and consumed them. 📖

They died before the LORD.

## Leviticus 10:3

Moses told Aaron, “The LORD meant this: ‘I will show My holiness to those approaching Me. 📖

I will reveal My glory before all the people.’”

Aaron stayed silent.

## Leviticus 10:4

Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, sons of Aaron’s uncle Uzziel. 📖

He said, “Carry your cousins’ bodies away from the sanctuary’s front to outside camp.”

## Leviticus 10:5

They carried them outside, still in their tunics, as Moses directed. 📖

## Leviticus 10:6

Moses told Aaron, Eleazar, and Ithamar, “Do not leave your hair disordered or tear your clothes. 📖

Otherwise you will die, and the LORD will be angry with all the congregation.

Your brothers, all Israel, may mourn the fire the LORD ignited.

**About these actions:** Disordered hair and torn clothes are signs of mourning in this account.

## Leviticus 10:7

Do not leave the Tent of Meeting’s entrance, or you will die. 📖

The LORD’s anointing oil is on you.”

They obeyed Moses.

## Leviticus 10:8

The LORD told Aaron, 📖

## Leviticus 10:9

“You and your sons must drink no wine or strong drink when entering the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Otherwise you will die.

This is a permanent law through coming generations.

## Leviticus 10:10

Distinguish holy from common, and clean from unclean, 📖

**Help with these words:** Holy means set apart for God.

Common means ordinary.

Clean and unclean describe status under these worship rules.

so you can teach Israel all the laws the LORD gave through Moses.” 📖

## Leviticus 10:12

Moses told Aaron and his remaining sons Eleazar and Ithamar, “Eat the remaining grain offering beside the altar without leaven. 📖

It is from the LORD’s food offerings and is most holy.

## Leviticus 10:13

Eat it in a holy place. 📖

It is your share and your sons’ share of the LORD’s food offerings.

This is what I was commanded.

## Leviticus 10:14

You, your sons, and your daughters may eat the waved breast and contributed thigh in a ceremonially clean place. 📖

They are your children’s and your portions from Israel’s peace offerings.

## Leviticus 10:15

Bring the contributed thigh, waved breast, and food-offering fat to wave before the LORD. 📖

The breast and thigh permanently belong to you and your children, as the LORD commanded.”

## Leviticus 10:16

Moses carefully searched for the sin-offering goat and found it burned. 📖

Angry with Aaron’s remaining sons Eleazar and Ithamar, he asked,

## Leviticus 10:17

“Why did you not eat it in the holy place? 📖

It is most holy.

It was given to remove the congregation’s guilt by making atonement before the LORD.

## Leviticus 10:18

Its blood was not brought inside the holy place. 📖

You should have eaten it in the sanctuary area, as I commanded.”

## Leviticus 10:19

Aaron answered Moses, “Today they presented their sin offering and burnt offering before the LORD. 📖

These things happened to me.

If I ate the sin offering today, would the LORD accept it?”

## Leviticus 10:20

Moses heard this explanation and was satisfied. 📖
