# Luke 22 — Supper, prayer, and arrest

**About this chapter**

Jesus shares Passover, teaches his apostles, prays, and faces betrayal, denial, and a council hearing.

## Luke 22:1

The Unleavened Bread feast, called Passover, was approaching. 📖

**Help:** Unleavened bread is made without yeast; Passover remembers God's rescue of Israel from Egypt.

## Luke 22:2

Chief priests and law teachers sought a way to kill Jesus, fearing the people. 📖

## Luke 22:3

Satan entered Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve. 📖

## Luke 22:4

Judas went to discuss with chief priests and temple officers how to betray Jesus. 📖

## Luke 22:5

They were delighted and agreed to give him money. 📖

## Luke 22:6

He agreed and looked for a chance to betray Jesus without a crowd present. 📖

## Luke 22:7

The day came for sacrificing the Passover lamb during Unleavened Bread. 📖

## Luke 22:8

Jesus sent Peter and John to prepare their Passover meal. 📖

## Luke 22:9

They asked where he wanted it prepared. 📖

## Luke 22:10

He said, “Entering the city, you will meet a man carrying a water jug. 📖

Follow him into the house he enters.

## Luke 22:11

Tell the owner, ‘The Teacher asks where the guest room is for eating Passover with his disciples.’ 📖

## Luke 22:12

He will show a large furnished room upstairs; prepare there.” 📖

## Luke 22:13

They found everything as Jesus said and prepared the meal. 📖

## Luke 22:14

When the hour came, Jesus reclined at table with the apostles. 📖

**Help:** People sometimes ate leaning on couches.

## Luke 22:15

He said, “I eagerly wanted to eat this Passover with you before suffering. 📖

## Luke 22:16

I tell you, I will not eat it again until it is fulfilled in God's kingdom.” 📖

## Luke 22:17

He took a cup, gave thanks, and told them to take it and share it. 📖

## Luke 22:18

“I tell you, from now I will not drink the vine's fruit until God's kingdom comes.” 📖

**Help:** The vine's fruit means a drink made from grapes.

## Luke 22:19

He took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them. 📖

He said, “This is my body, given for you; do this to remember me.”

## Luke 22:20

Likewise, after supper he took the cup. 📖

He said, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood, poured out for you.

**Help:** A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Luke 22:21

Look, my betrayer's hand is with mine on the table. 📖

## Luke 22:22

The Son of Man will go as determined, but woe to his betrayer!” 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself; woe warns of grief and judgment.

## Luke 22:23

They wondered among themselves which of them would do this. 📖

## Luke 22:24

They also argued about which disciple should be considered greatest. 📖

## Luke 22:25

Jesus said, “Gentile kings act as masters over people, and their rulers call themselves benefactors. 📖

**Help:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews; benefactors are people who do good for others.

## Luke 22:26

You must be different: your greatest should be like the youngest, and your leader like a servant. 📖

## Luke 22:27

Who is greater, the person reclining at table or the servant? 📖

Is it not the person at table?

Yet I am among you as one serving.

## Luke 22:28

You stood by me through my trials. 📖

## Luke 22:29

I give you a kingdom, just as my Father gave one to me. 📖

## Luke 22:30

You may eat and drink at my kingdom's table and sit on thrones judging Israel's twelve tribes. 📖

## Luke 22:31

Simon, Simon, Satan asked to sift all of you like wheat. 📖

**Help:** Sifting separates grain; this pictures a severe testing of all the disciples.

## Luke 22:32

But I prayed for you, Simon, that your faith would not fail. 📖

When you turn back, strengthen your brothers.”

## Luke 22:33

Peter said, “Lord, I am ready for prison and death with you.” 📖

## Luke 22:34

Jesus said, “I tell you, Peter: the rooster will not crow today until you deny knowing me three times.” 📖

## Luke 22:35

He asked whether they lacked anything when sent without purse, bag, or sandals. 📖

They answered, “Nothing.”

## Luke 22:36

He said, “Now take a purse and bag if you have them. 📖

Anyone without a sword should sell his cloak and buy one.

## Luke 22:37

This Scripture must be fulfilled in me: ‘He was counted with lawbreakers.’ 📖

What was written about me is being fulfilled.”

## Luke 22:38

They said, “Lord, here are two swords.” 📖

He answered, “That is enough.”

## Luke 22:39

Jesus went as usual to the Mount of Olives, and his disciples followed. 📖

## Luke 22:40

There he told them to pray so they would not enter temptation. 📖

**Help:** Temptation is pressure to do wrong.

## Luke 22:41

He moved about a stone's throw away, knelt, and prayed. 📖

**Help:** A stone's throw describes a short distance.

## Luke 22:42

“Father, if you are willing, take this cup from me. 📖

Yet let your will be done, rather than mine.”

**Help:** The cup pictures the suffering ahead of Jesus.

## Luke 22:43

An angel from heaven appeared and strengthened him. 📖

## Luke 22:44

In anguish, he prayed more earnestly. 📖

His sweat became like drops of blood falling to the ground.

**Help:** Anguish means very deep distress; earnestly means with serious attention and feeling.

## Luke 22:45

He rose from prayer and returned to the disciples. 📖

He found them asleep, exhausted by sorrow.

## Luke 22:46

He asked why they slept and told them to rise and pray to avoid entering temptation. 📖

## Luke 22:47

While he spoke, a crowd arrived, led by Judas, one of the Twelve. 📖

Judas approached to kiss him.

## Luke 22:48

Jesus asked, “Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?” 📖

## Luke 22:49

Those around Jesus saw what was about to happen. 📖

They asked, “Lord, should we strike with our swords?”

## Luke 22:50

One struck the high priest's servant and cut off his right ear. 📖

## Luke 22:51

Jesus said, “No more of this!” 📖

He touched the man's ear and healed him.

## Luke 22:52

Jesus addressed the chief priests, temple officers, and elders who came for him. 📖

“Have you brought swords and clubs as against an outlaw?

## Luke 22:53

I was with you daily in the temple courts, and you did not seize me. 📖

But this hour belongs to you and to the power of darkness.”

## Luke 22:54

They seized Jesus, led him away, and took him into the high priest's house. 📖

Peter followed at a distance.

## Luke 22:55

People lit a fire in the courtyard's middle and sat together, and Peter sat among them. 📖

## Luke 22:56

A servant girl examined Peter in the firelight and said he had been with Jesus too. 📖

## Luke 22:57

He denied it, saying, “Woman, I do not know him.” 📖

## Luke 22:58

Soon another person said he too was one of them, but Peter answered, “Man, I am not.” 📖

## Luke 22:59

About an hour later, another man insisted he had been with Jesus because he too was Galilean. 📖

## Luke 22:60

Peter said, “Man, I do not know what you mean.” 📖

Immediately, while he still spoke, a rooster crowed.

## Luke 22:61

The Lord turned and looked at Peter. 📖

Peter remembered the Lord predicting three denials before the rooster crowed that day.

## Luke 22:62

He went outside and cried bitterly. 📖

## Luke 22:63

The men holding Jesus mocked and beat him. 📖

## Luke 22:64

They blindfolded him and kept demanding, “Prophesy: who hit you?” 📖

## Luke 22:65

They said many other blasphemous things against him. 📖

**Help:** Blasphemous means speaking against God.

## Luke 22:66

At dawn, the people's elders, chief priests, and law teachers met and brought Jesus into their Sanhedrin. 📖

**Help:** The Sanhedrin was a religious council.

## Luke 22:67

They asked him to say whether he was the Christ. 📖

He answered, “If I tell you, you will not believe.

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Luke 22:68

If I ask you, you will not answer. 📖

## Luke 22:69

From now, the Son of Man will sit at the right hand of God's power.” 📖

## Luke 22:70

Everyone asked whether he was God's Son, and he answered, “You say that I am.” 📖

## Luke 22:71

They said no more evidence was needed, because they heard it themselves from his lips. 📖
