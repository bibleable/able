# Luke 4 — Temptation and the start of teaching

**About this chapter**

Jesus faces the devil's temptations, teaches in synagogues, and heals people.

## Luke 4:1

Jesus returned from the Jordan full of the Holy Spirit, who led him into the wilderness. 📖

## Luke 4:2

The devil tempted him for forty days, and Jesus ate nothing during that time. 📖

When the days ended, he was hungry.

## Luke 4:3

The devil said, “If you are God's Son, tell this stone to become bread.” 📖

## Luke 4:4

Jesus answered, “Scripture says people do not live on bread alone.” 📖

## Luke 4:5

The devil led him to a high place and instantly showed him all the world's kingdoms. 📖

## Luke 4:6

The devil said, “I will give you authority over these kingdoms and all their glory. 📖

They have been handed to me, and I can give them to anyone I choose.

**Help:** These are the devil's claims, not the narrator's statements about what he owns.

## Luke 4:7

If you worship me, it will all be yours.” 📖

## Luke 4:8

Jesus answered, “Scripture says to worship the Lord your God and serve only him.” 📖

## Luke 4:9

The devil took him to Jerusalem and put him on the temple's highest point. 📖

He said, “If you are God's Son, throw yourself down.

## Luke 4:10

Scripture says God will command his angels to guard you carefully. 📖

## Luke 4:11

They will lift you in their hands so you do not strike your foot against a stone.” 📖

## Luke 4:12

Jesus answered, “Scripture also says not to test the Lord your God.” 📖

## Luke 4:13

After finishing every temptation, the devil left Jesus until a suitable time. 📖

## Luke 4:14

Jesus returned to Galilee in the Spirit's power, and news spread throughout the surrounding area. 📖

## Luke 4:15

He taught in their synagogues, and everyone praised him. 📖

**Help:** Synagogues are Jewish places of worship and teaching.

## Luke 4:16

Jesus came to Nazareth, where he grew up, and entered the synagogue on the Sabbath as usual. 📖

He stood to read.

**Help:** The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish day of rest.

## Luke 4:17

They handed him Isaiah the prophet's scroll, and he unrolled it to this passage: 📖

**Help:** A scroll is a long sheet rolled up for storage.

## Luke 4:18

“The Lord's Spirit is on me because he anointed me to preach good news to poor people. 📖

He sent me to announce freedom for prisoners and sight restored to blind people, and to release those oppressed.

**Help:** Anointed means chosen and set apart for a task; oppressed people are treated cruelly by others with power.

## Luke 4:19

He sent me to announce the year of the Lord's favour.” 📖

## Luke 4:20

Jesus rolled the scroll up, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. 📖

Everyone in the synagogue watched him closely.

## Luke 4:21

He began, “Today, this Scripture has been fulfilled as you heard it.” 📖

## Luke 4:22

Everyone spoke well of him, amazed by his gracious words, and asked whether he was Joseph's son. 📖

## Luke 4:23

Jesus said, “Surely you will tell me the proverb, ‘Doctor, heal yourself!’ 📖

You will tell me to do here in my hometown what you heard I did in Capernaum.”

## Luke 4:24

He added, “I tell you truly, no prophet is accepted in his hometown. 📖

## Luke 4:25

I tell you truly, Israel had many widows in Elijah's time. 📖

The sky gave no rain for three and a half years, and terrible hunger covered the whole land.

## Luke 4:26

Yet Elijah was sent to none of those widows, only to a widow in Zarephath in Sidon. 📖

## Luke 4:27

Israel had many people with leprosy when Elisha was a prophet. 📖

Yet none were cleansed except Naaman the Syrian.”

**Help:** Leprosy names a serious skin disease; cleansing here includes healing and religious acceptance, not moral worth.

## Luke 4:28

Hearing this, everyone in the synagogue became furious. 📖

## Luke 4:29

They drove Jesus outside town to the edge of the hill where it was built. 📖

They wanted to throw him over the cliff.

## Luke 4:30

But he passed through the crowd and went away. 📖

## Luke 4:31

Jesus went down to Capernaum in Galilee and taught the people on the Sabbath. 📖

## Luke 4:32

His teaching amazed them because his message had authority. 📖

## Luke 4:33

In the synagogue, a man with an unclean demon spirit cried out loudly. 📖

**Help:** An unclean spirit is an evil spirit; unclean does not mean physical dirt or disability here.

## Luke 4:34

“Ha, what do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? 📖

Have you come to destroy us?

I know you are God's Holy One!”

## Luke 4:35

Jesus rebuked the demon, telling it to be silent and come out. 📖

The demon threw the man down before everyone and left without harming him.

## Luke 4:36

Everyone was amazed, asking, “What is this message?” 📖

“He commands unclean spirits with authority and power, and they come out!”

## Luke 4:37

News about Jesus spread throughout the surrounding area. 📖

## Luke 4:38

Leaving the synagogue, Jesus went to Simon's home, where Simon's mother-in-law had a high fever. 📖

They asked him to help her.

## Luke 4:39

He stood over her and rebuked the fever, and it left. 📖

She immediately got up and served them.

## Luke 4:40

At sunset, people brought everyone with various illnesses to Jesus. 📖

He laid his hands on each person and healed them.

## Luke 4:41

Demons came out of many, shouting that he was God's Son. 📖

He rebuked them and stopped them speaking because they knew he was the Christ.

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Luke 4:42

At dawn, Jesus went to a place alone, and the crowds searched for him. 📖

They reached him and tried to stop him leaving.

## Luke 4:43

He said, “I must preach the good news of God's kingdom to other towns too. 📖

That is why I was sent.”

## Luke 4:44

He continued preaching in Judea's synagogues. 📖
