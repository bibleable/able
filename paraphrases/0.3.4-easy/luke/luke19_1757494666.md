# Luke 19 — Zacchaeus and Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jesus meets Zacchaeus, tells a story about a king, and enters Jerusalem.

## Luke 19:1

Jesus entered Jericho and passed through. 📖

## Luke 19:2

Zacchaeus was a chief tax collector and very rich. 📖

## Luke 19:3

He wanted to see who Jesus was, but was too short to see over the crowd. 📖

## Luke 19:4

He ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree, because Jesus would pass that way. 📖

## Luke 19:5

Jesus reached that spot, looked up, and said, “Zacchaeus, hurry down; I must stay at your house today.” 📖

## Luke 19:6

He hurried down and welcomed Jesus joyfully. 📖

## Luke 19:7

Everyone seeing this grumbled, “He has gone to be a sinful man's guest!” 📖

## Luke 19:8

Zacchaeus stood and told the Lord, “Lord, I give half my possessions to poor people. 📖

If I cheated anyone, I will repay four times as much.”

## Luke 19:9

Jesus said, “Today salvation came to this house, because he too is Abraham's son. 📖

**Help:** Salvation means rescue.

## Luke 19:10

The Son of Man came to seek and save the lost.” 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 19:11

Jesus told the listeners a story because he was near Jerusalem and they expected God's kingdom to appear immediately. 📖

## Luke 19:12

“A nobleman travelled to a distant country to claim his kingship and return. 📖

**Help:** A nobleman belongs to a family with high social rank.

## Luke 19:13

Before leaving, he called ten servants, gave them ten minas, and told them to do business until he returned. 📖

**Help:** A mina was an amount of money.

## Luke 19:14

His subjects hated him and sent representatives after him, saying they did not want him as king. 📖

## Luke 19:15

Returning as king, he called the servants to find out what each earned with his money. 📖

## Luke 19:16

The first said, ‘Master, your mina earned ten more minas.’ 📖

## Luke 19:17

He answered, ‘Well done, good servant; your faithfulness in a very small matter earns authority over ten cities.’ 📖

## Luke 19:18

The second said, ‘Master, your mina made five minas.’ 📖

## Luke 19:19

He gave him authority over five cities. 📖

## Luke 19:20

Another said, ‘Master, here is your mina, which I kept in a cloth. 📖

## Luke 19:21

I feared you because you are harsh, withdrawing what you never deposited and harvesting what you never planted.’ 📖

## Luke 19:22

He answered, ‘Wicked servant, I will judge you by your own words. 📖

You knew I am harsh, withdrawing what I never deposited and harvesting what I never planted?

## Luke 19:23

Why not put my money in the bank, so I could collect it with interest on returning?’ 📖

**Help:** Interest is extra money paid for keeping or lending money.

## Luke 19:24

He told people nearby to take the mina and give it to the servant with ten. 📖

## Luke 19:25

They said, ‘Master, he already has ten!’ 📖

## Luke 19:26

He replied, ‘I tell you, everyone who has will receive more. 📖

Whoever does not have will lose even what he has.

## Luke 19:27

Bring my enemies who did not want me as king, and kill them before me.’” 📖

## Luke 19:28

After saying this, Jesus went ahead, going up to Jerusalem. 📖

## Luke 19:29

Near Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives, he sent two disciples. 📖

## Luke 19:30

“Go into the village ahead. 📖

As you enter, you will find a tied colt nobody has ever sat on.

Untie it and bring it here.

**Help:** A colt is a young animal, here used for riding.

## Luke 19:31

If asked why you are untying it, say the Lord needs it.” 📖

## Luke 19:32

They went and found it just as he said. 📖

## Luke 19:33

As they untied it, its owners asked why. 📖

## Luke 19:34

They answered that the Lord needed it. 📖

## Luke 19:35

They brought it to Jesus, threw cloaks on it, and put him on it. 📖

## Luke 19:36

People spread cloaks on the road as he rode. 📖

## Luke 19:37

As he neared the way down the Mount of Olives, the whole crowd of disciples began praising God. 📖

They praised loudly and joyfully for all the miracles they had seen.

## Luke 19:38

“Blessed is the King coming in the Lord's name! 📖

Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!”

## Luke 19:39

Some Pharisees in the crowd told him, “Teacher, rebuke your disciples!” 📖

## Luke 19:40

He answered, “I tell you, if they stay silent, the stones will cry out.” 📖

## Luke 19:41

Approaching Jerusalem, he saw the city and cried over it. 📖

## Luke 19:42

“If only you knew today what would bring peace! 📖

But now it is hidden from your eyes.

## Luke 19:43

Days are coming when enemies will build barriers, surround you, and trap you on every side. 📖

## Luke 19:44

They will flatten you and your children within your walls, leaving no stone on another. 📖

This is because you did not recognize the time God visited you.”

## Luke 19:45

Jesus entered the temple courts and began driving out the sellers. 📖

## Luke 19:46

He said, “Scripture calls my house a house of prayer, but you made it a robbers' den.” 📖

## Luke 19:47

He taught daily at the temple, while chief priests, law teachers, and people's leaders sought to kill him. 📖

## Luke 19:48

They could not find a way, because everyone listened closely to his words. 📖
