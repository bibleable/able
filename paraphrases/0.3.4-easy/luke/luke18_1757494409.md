# Luke 18 — Prayer, humility, and following Jesus

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches prayer, welcomes children, speaks to a rich ruler, predicts his death, and heals a blind man.

## Luke 18:1

Jesus told them a story about always praying and not losing heart. 📖

## Luke 18:2

“A town had a judge who neither feared God nor respected people. 📖

## Luke 18:3

A widow there kept asking him for justice against her opponent. 📖

## Luke 18:4

For a time he refused, but later thought, ‘I neither fear God nor respect people. 📖

## Luke 18:5

But this widow keeps bothering me, so I will give her justice. 📖

Otherwise, her constant requests will wear me out.’”

## Luke 18:6

The Lord said, “Listen to that unjust judge's words. 📖

## Luke 18:7

Will God not give justice to his chosen people crying to him day and night? 📖

Will he delay helping them?

## Luke 18:8

I tell you, he will bring justice promptly. 📖

Yet when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on earth?”

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 18:9

He told another story to people trusting their own righteousness and looking down on others. 📖

**Help:** Righteousness means being right before God.

## Luke 18:10

“Two men went to the temple to pray, one a Pharisee and one a tax collector. 📖

## Luke 18:11

The Pharisee stood alone and prayed. 📖

‘God, thank you that I am not like other people: cheats, evildoers, adulterers, or even this tax collector.

**Help:** Adulterers break faithfulness in marriage through a sexual relationship with someone else; these are the Pharisee's words.

## Luke 18:12

I fast twice weekly and give a tenth of everything I acquire.’ 📖

**Help:** Fast means choose not to eat for a time; giving a tenth is called tithing.

## Luke 18:13

The tax collector stood far away, unwilling even to look up to heaven. 📖

He beat his chest and said, ‘God, have mercy on me, a sinner!’

## Luke 18:14

I tell you, this man went home justified, rather than the Pharisee. 📖

Anyone raising himself high will be brought low; anyone making himself low will be raised high.”

**Help:** Justified means accepted as right before God.

## Luke 18:15

People brought even babies for Jesus to touch, but the disciples rebuked those bringing them. 📖

## Luke 18:16

Jesus called the children and said, “Let them come to me, and do not hinder them. 📖

God's kingdom belongs to people like these.

## Luke 18:17

I tell you truly, anyone not receiving God's kingdom like a little child will never enter it.” 📖

## Luke 18:18

A ruler asked, “Good Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?” 📖

**Help:** Eternal life is life without end; inherit means receive something passed on to you.

## Luke 18:19

Jesus asked, “Why call me good? 📖

Only God is good.

## Luke 18:20

You know the commands: do not commit adultery, murder, steal, or give false witness. 📖

Honour your father and mother.”

## Luke 18:21

The man said he had kept all these since his youth. 📖

## Luke 18:22

Hearing this, Jesus said he lacked one thing. 📖

“Sell everything and give to poor people, and you will have treasure in heaven.

Then come and follow me.”

## Luke 18:23

The ruler heard and became very sad, because he was extremely wealthy. 📖

## Luke 18:24

Seeing his sadness, Jesus said, “How hard it is for rich people to enter God's kingdom! 📖

## Luke 18:25

A camel passes through a needle's eye more easily than a rich man enters God's kingdom.” 📖

**Help:** A needle's eye is the small hole for thread; Jesus uses a strong image about difficulty.

## Luke 18:26

Those hearing asked who could be saved. 📖

## Luke 18:27

Jesus said what was impossible for people was possible with God. 📖

## Luke 18:28

Peter said they had left everything they had to follow him. 📖

## Luke 18:29

Jesus answered, “Truly, anyone leaving home, wife, brothers, parents, or children for God's kingdom will receive this. 📖

## Luke 18:30

They will receive many times more now, and eternal life in the coming age.” 📖

## Luke 18:31

Jesus took the Twelve aside, saying they were going up to Jerusalem. 📖

Everything the prophets wrote about the Son of Man would be fulfilled.

## Luke 18:32

“He will be handed to the Gentiles, mocked, insulted, and spat on. 📖

**Help:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Luke 18:33

They will whip him and kill him, and on the third day he will rise again.” 📖

## Luke 18:34

The disciples understood none of this, because the meaning was hidden from them. 📖

They did not grasp what he meant.

## Luke 18:35

As Jesus approached Jericho, a blind man sat begging beside the road. 📖

## Luke 18:36

Hearing a crowd pass, he asked what was happening. 📖

## Luke 18:37

They told him Jesus of Nazareth was passing. 📖

## Luke 18:38

He cried, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” 📖

## Luke 18:39

People leading the way told him to be quiet, but he shouted louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!” 📖

## Luke 18:40

Jesus stopped, ordered the man brought to him, and asked him a question when he came near. 📖

## Luke 18:41

“What do you want me to do for you?” 📖

He replied, “Lord, let me see again.”

## Luke 18:42

Jesus said, “Receive your sight; your faith has healed you.” 📖

## Luke 18:43

He immediately saw and followed Jesus, praising God. 📖

Everyone saw this and praised God too.
