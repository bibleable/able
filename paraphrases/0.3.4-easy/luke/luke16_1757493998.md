# Luke 16 — Money, faithfulness, and listening

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells his disciples about a manager, then speaks about money, God's law, and a rich man and Lazarus.

## Luke 16:1

Jesus told his disciples about a rich man whose manager was accused of wasting his possessions. 📖

## Luke 16:2

“The master called him in and asked what these reports meant. 📖

He demanded an account of his management, because he could no longer be manager.

## Luke 16:3

The manager wondered what to do, now his master was removing him. 📖

He said he was too weak to dig and too ashamed to beg.

## Luke 16:4

He decided on a way for people to welcome him into their homes after his removal. 📖

## Luke 16:5

He called each person owing his master money and asked the first how much he owed. 📖

## Luke 16:6

The man owed a hundred measures of olive oil. 📖

The manager told him to take his bill, sit down quickly, and write fifty.

## Luke 16:7

He asked another man how much he owed. 📖

The man owed a hundred measures of wheat.

The manager told him to take his bill and write eighty.

## Luke 16:8

The master praised the dishonest manager for acting shrewdly. 📖

This age's sons deal more shrewdly with their own kind than the sons of light do.

**Help:** Shrewd means clever in judging how to gain an advantage; this does not make the manager's dishonesty good.

Sons of light pictures people belonging to God's light.

## Luke 16:9

I tell you this. 📖

Use worldly wealth to make friends, so when it is gone, they welcome you into eternal homes.

## Luke 16:10

Anyone faithful with very little will be faithful with much. 📖

Anyone dishonest with very little will be dishonest with much.

## Luke 16:11

If you were unfaithful with worldly wealth, who will trust you with true riches? 📖

## Luke 16:12

If unfaithful with someone else's belongings, who will give you your own? 📖

## Luke 16:13

No servant can serve two masters. 📖

He will hate one and love the other, or be devoted to one and despise the other.

You cannot serve God and money.”

## Luke 16:14

The Pharisees loved money, heard all this, and mocked Jesus. 📖

## Luke 16:15

He said, “You justify yourselves before people, but God knows your hearts. 📖

What people prize is hateful before God.

**Help:** Justify yourselves means show yourselves to be right.

## Luke 16:16

The Law and the Prophets were proclaimed until John. 📖

Since then, God's kingdom's good news is preached, and everyone forces his way into it.

**Help:** The Law and the Prophets are Jewish Scripture; the saying about force needs further interpretation.

## Luke 16:17

It is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for one pen stroke to leave the Law. 📖

## Luke 16:18

A man divorcing his wife and marrying another woman commits adultery. 📖

A man marrying a divorced woman commits adultery too.

**Help:** Adultery means breaking faithfulness in marriage through a sexual relationship with someone else.

## Luke 16:19

A rich man wore purple and fine linen and lived in joyful splendour every day. 📖

## Luke 16:20

A beggar named Lazarus lay at his gate, covered with sores. 📖

## Luke 16:21

He longed to eat crumbs that fell from the rich man's table. 📖

Even dogs came and licked his sores.

## Luke 16:22

One day, the beggar died, and angels carried him to Abraham's side. 📖

The rich man also died and was buried.

## Luke 16:23

In Hades, suffering torment, he looked up and saw Abraham far away with Lazarus beside him. 📖

**Help:** Hades names the place of the dead; torment means severe suffering.

## Luke 16:24

He cried, ‘Father Abraham, have mercy and send Lazarus to dip his fingertip in water and cool my tongue. 📖

I am in agony in this fire.’

## Luke 16:25

Abraham answered, ‘Child, remember your good things in life and Lazarus's bad things. 📖

Now he is comforted here, while you are in agony.

## Luke 16:26

A great gap is fixed between us and you, so nobody can cross either way, even if they wish.’ 📖

## Luke 16:27

He begged, ‘Father, then send Lazarus to my father's house. 📖

## Luke 16:28

I have five brothers. 📖

Let him warn them so they do not come to this place of torment.’

## Luke 16:29

Abraham replied, ‘They have Moses and the Prophets; let your brothers listen to them.’ 📖

## Luke 16:30

He said, ‘No, Father Abraham; if someone is sent to them from the dead, they will repent.’ 📖

**Help:** Repent means turn from wrongdoing back to God.

## Luke 16:31

Abraham answered, ‘If they do not listen to Moses and the Prophets, even someone's resurrection will not persuade them.’” 📖

**Help:** Resurrection means rising from death.
