# Luke 8 — Hearing, believing, and healing

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches about hearing God's word, calms a storm, and helps people in great distress.

## Luke 8:1

Soon afterwards, Jesus travelled through towns and villages, preaching the good news of God's kingdom. 📖

The Twelve were with him.

## Luke 8:2

Some women healed of evil spirits and illnesses were also with him. 📖

They included Mary called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out.

## Luke 8:3

Others included Joanna, wife of Chuza, Herod's household manager, and Susanna. 📖

These women and many others supported them from their own resources.

## Luke 8:4

A large crowd gathered from town after town, and Jesus told this story: 📖

## Luke 8:5

“A farmer went to sow seed. 📖

Some fell along the path, where feet trampled it and birds ate it.

## Luke 8:6

Some fell on rocky ground, sprouted, and dried up because it lacked moisture. 📖

## Luke 8:7

Some fell among thorns, which grew with the plants and choked them. 📖

## Luke 8:8

Some fell on good soil, grew, and produced a hundred times what was sown.” 📖

Jesus called out, “Anyone with ears to hear should listen!”

## Luke 8:9

His disciples asked what the story meant. 📖

## Luke 8:10

He answered, “You have been given knowledge of the mysteries of God's kingdom, but I speak to others in stories. 📖

This is so they may see without seeing and hear without understanding.

**Help:** Mysteries are things hidden until made known; Jesus is speaking about understanding his teaching.

## Luke 8:11

The seed means God's word. 📖

## Luke 8:12

The path seeds are people who hear, but the devil takes the word from their hearts. 📖

This is so they will not believe and be saved.

## Luke 8:13

The rocky-ground seeds are people receiving the word joyfully, but having no root. 📖

They believe for a while, but turn away when tested.

## Luke 8:14

The thorn seeds are people hearing the word, then being choked by life's worries, riches, and pleasures. 📖

Their fruit does not ripen.

## Luke 8:15

The good-soil seeds are people with noble, good hearts, hearing the word and holding it firmly. 📖

They keep going and produce a crop.

## Luke 8:16

Nobody lights a lamp and covers it with a jar or puts it under a bed. 📖

They put it on a stand so people entering can see the light.

## Luke 8:17

Every hidden thing will be revealed, and everything concealed will be made known and brought into light. 📖

## Luke 8:18

So pay attention to how you listen. 📖

Whoever has will receive more; whoever does not have will lose even what he thinks he has.”

## Luke 8:19

Jesus' mother and brothers came but could not reach him through the crowd. 📖

## Luke 8:20

Someone told him his mother and brothers were outside wanting to see him. 📖

## Luke 8:21

He answered, “My mother and brothers are those hearing God's word and doing it.” 📖

## Luke 8:22

One day, Jesus told his disciples to cross the lake. 📖

He got into a boat with them, and they set off.

## Luke 8:23

He fell asleep as they sailed, and a windstorm swept down onto the lake. 📖

Water flooded the boat, putting them in great danger.

## Luke 8:24

They woke him, crying, “Master, Master, we are dying!” 📖

Jesus got up and rebuked the wind and wild water.

They subsided and everything became calm.

## Luke 8:25

He asked, “Where is your faith?” 📖

Afraid and amazed, they asked each other who he was.

They said, “He commands even wind and water, and they obey him!”

## Luke 8:26

They sailed to the Gerasenes' region, across the lake from Galilee. 📖

## Luke 8:27

A man from town with demons met Jesus as he stepped ashore. 📖

For a long time, he had worn no clothes and lived in tombs instead of a house.

## Luke 8:28

Seeing Jesus, he cried out, fell before him, and shouted, “What do you want with me, Jesus? 📖

You are the Most High God's Son; I beg you not to torture me!”

## Luke 8:29

Jesus had commanded the unclean spirit to leave him. 📖

It often seized him.

Though bound with chains and shackles, he broke the chains.

The demon drove him into lonely places.

**Help:** Unclean spirits are evil spirits; this does not mean physical dirt, illness, or disability.

Shackles are metal restraints.

## Luke 8:30

Jesus asked his name, and he answered, “Legion,” because many demons had entered him. 📖

**Help:** A legion was a large army group; this name points to the many demons.

## Luke 8:31

The demons kept begging Jesus not to send them into the Abyss. 📖

**Help:** Abyss means a deep pit; here it refers to a place feared by the demons.

## Luke 8:32

A large herd of pigs fed on the hillside. 📖

The demons begged to enter the pigs, and Jesus allowed them.

## Luke 8:33

The demons left the man and entered the pigs.

The herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned. 📖

## Luke 8:34

The people tending the pigs saw this, ran away, and reported it in town and countryside. 📖

## Luke 8:35

People came to see and found the man sitting at Jesus' feet, clothed and in his right mind. 📖

The demons had left him, and the people were afraid.

## Luke 8:36

Witnesses told them how the man with demons had been healed. 📖

## Luke 8:37

All the Gerasenes' people asked Jesus to leave because they were greatly afraid. 📖

He got into the boat and started back.

## Luke 8:38

The freed man begged to go with Jesus, but Jesus sent him away. 📖

## Luke 8:39

He said, “Go home and tell how much God has done for you.” 📖

The man went throughout town telling how much Jesus had done for him.

## Luke 8:40

When Jesus returned, the waiting crowd welcomed him. 📖

## Luke 8:41

Jairus, a synagogue leader, fell at Jesus' feet and begged him to come home with him. 📖

## Luke 8:42

His only daughter was about twelve and was dying. 📖

As Jesus went with him, crowds pressed around.

## Luke 8:43

A woman there had bled for twelve years and spent all her money on doctors, but nobody could heal her. 📖

## Luke 8:44

She approached behind Jesus and touched his cloak's fringe, and immediately the bleeding stopped. 📖

## Luke 8:45

Jesus asked who touched him, but everyone denied it. 📖

Peter said, “Master, people are crowding and pressing against you.”

## Luke 8:46

Jesus said someone had touched him, because he knew power had gone out from him. 📖

## Luke 8:47

The woman saw she could not hide, and came trembling and fell before him. 📖

Before everyone, she explained why she touched him and how she was immediately healed.

## Luke 8:48

He said, “Daughter, your faith has healed you; go in peace.” 📖

## Luke 8:49

While Jesus spoke, someone came from the synagogue leader's home. 📖

He told Jairus his daughter was dead and not to trouble the Teacher further.

## Luke 8:50

Hearing this, Jesus said to Jairus, “Do not fear; just believe, and she will be healed.” 📖

## Luke 8:51

He entered the house, allowing only Peter, John, James, and the child's parents to go in with him. 📖

## Luke 8:52

Everyone cried and mourned, but Jesus told them to stop crying because she was asleep rather than dead. 📖

## Luke 8:53

They laughed at him, knowing she was dead. 📖

## Luke 8:54

Jesus took her hand and called, “Child, get up!” 📖

## Luke 8:55

Her spirit returned, and she immediately stood up. 📖

He told them to give her food.

## Luke 8:56

Her parents were amazed, but he ordered them to tell nobody what happened. 📖
