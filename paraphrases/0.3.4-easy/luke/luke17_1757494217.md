# Luke 17 — Forgiveness, faith, and the coming day

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches his disciples, heals ten people, and speaks about God's kingdom and the Son of Man.

## Luke 17:1

Jesus told his disciples, “Stumbling blocks will certainly come, but woe to whoever brings them! 📖

**Help:** Stumbling blocks picture things leading people into sin; woe warns of grief and judgment.

## Luke 17:2

It would be better for him to have a millstone round his neck and be thrown into the sea. 📖

That would be better than causing one of these little ones to stumble.

**Help:** A millstone is a heavy stone for grinding grain; Jesus uses a severe warning about causing sin.

## Luke 17:3

Watch yourselves; if your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him. 📖

**Help:** Rebuke means tell someone firmly that their action is wrong; repent means turn away from wrongdoing.

## Luke 17:4

Suppose he sins against you seven times in a day and returns seven times saying, ‘I repent.’ 📖

You must forgive him.”

## Luke 17:5

The apostles asked the Lord to increase their faith. 📖

## Luke 17:6

He answered, “If your faith is a mustard seed's size, you can command this mulberry tree to move. 📖

Tell it to be uprooted and planted in the sea, and it will obey.

**Help:** Mustard seeds are small; Jesus uses the tree as a picture of faith's power.

## Luke 17:7

Would you tell a servant returning from fieldwork, ploughing or tending sheep, to sit and eat at once? 📖

## Luke 17:8

Would you not tell him to prepare your meal and dress to serve while you eat and drink? 📖

He can eat and drink afterwards.

## Luke 17:9

Do you thank the servant for doing what he was told? 📖

## Luke 17:10

Likewise, after doing everything commanded, say, ‘We are unworthy servants; we only did our duty.’” 📖

## Luke 17:11

Going to Jerusalem, Jesus passed between Samaria and Galilee. 📖

## Luke 17:12

Entering a village, he met ten people with leprosy, standing at a distance. 📖

**Help:** Leprosy names a serious skin disease; it does not mean a person is morally bad.

## Luke 17:13

They shouted, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!” 📖

## Luke 17:14

Jesus saw them and told them to show themselves to the priests. 📖

As they went, they were cleansed.

**Help:** Cleansed here concerns healing and religious acceptance, not a person's worth.

## Luke 17:15

One saw he was healed and returned, praising God loudly. 📖

## Luke 17:16

He fell facedown at Jesus' feet and thanked him; he was a Samaritan. 📖

## Luke 17:17

Jesus asked, “Were all ten not cleansed? 📖

Where are the other nine?

## Luke 17:18

Did nobody except this foreigner return to give God glory?” 📖

## Luke 17:19

He told him to rise and go, saying his faith had made him well. 📖

## Luke 17:20

The Pharisees asked when God's kingdom would come. 📖

Jesus answered, “It will not come with signs people can observe.

## Luke 17:21

Nor will people say, ‘Here it is!’ or ‘There it is!’ 📖

God's kingdom is in your midst.”

**Help:** In your midst means among you; the interpretation of this statement needs further assessment.

## Luke 17:22

He told his disciples, “A time will come when you long for one of the Son of Man's days. 📖

But you will not see it.

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 17:23

People will point here or there and say he is there, but do not go or chase after them. 📖

## Luke 17:24

As lightning flashes across the sky from end to end, so will the Son of Man be in his day. 📖

## Luke 17:25

But first he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation. 📖

## Luke 17:26

The Son of Man's days will be like Noah's days. 📖

## Luke 17:27

People ate, drank, married, and were given in marriage until Noah entered the ark. 📖

Then the flood came and destroyed them all.

**Help:** The ark was the large boat in Noah's flood account.

## Luke 17:28

Likewise, in Lot's days people ate, drank, bought, sold, planted, and built. 📖

## Luke 17:29

The day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained from heaven and destroyed them all. 📖

**Help:** Sulfur is a substance that burns; this passage describes judgment.

## Luke 17:30

It will be like that when the Son of Man is revealed. 📖

## Luke 17:31

That day, anyone on a roof must not come down for possessions. 📖

Anyone in a field must not return for anything left behind.

## Luke 17:32

Remember Lot's wife! 📖

## Luke 17:33

Whoever tries to save his life will lose it; whoever loses his life will preserve it. 📖

## Luke 17:34

I tell you, that night two people will be in one bed; one will be taken and one left. 📖

## Luke 17:35

Two women will grind grain together; one will be taken and one left.” 📖

## Luke 17:36

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Luke 17:37

They asked, “Where, Lord?” 📖

Jesus answered, “Wherever a dead body lies, vultures gather.”

**Help:** Vultures are birds that eat dead animals.
