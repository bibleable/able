# Luke 24 — Jesus is risen

**About this chapter**

The women find an empty tomb; Jesus appears to followers, teaches them, and is carried into heaven.

## Luke 24:1

Very early on the week's first day, the women came to the tomb with their prepared spices. 📖

## Luke 24:2

They found the stone rolled away from the tomb. 📖

## Luke 24:3

They entered but did not find the Lord Jesus' body. 📖

## Luke 24:4

While they wondered, two men in shining clothes suddenly stood beside them. 📖

## Luke 24:5

Terrified, they bowed facedown, and the men asked why they sought the living among the dead. 📖

## Luke 24:6

“He is not here; he has risen! 📖

Remember what he told you in Galilee.

## Luke 24:7

The Son of Man must be handed to sinful men, crucified, and rise on the third day.” 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself; crucified means killed on a cross.

## Luke 24:8

They remembered his words. 📖

## Luke 24:9

Returning from the tomb, they told everything to the Eleven and all the others. 📖

## Luke 24:10

Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women told the apostles. 📖

## Luke 24:11

The apostles thought their words were nonsense and did not believe them. 📖

## Luke 24:12

Peter rose and ran to the tomb, bent down, and saw only linen cloths. 📖

He left wondering to himself what happened.

## Luke 24:13

That day, two followers travelled to Emmaus, about seven miles from Jerusalem. 📖

## Luke 24:14

They discussed everything that happened. 📖

## Luke 24:15

As they talked and considered it, Jesus approached and walked with them. 📖

## Luke 24:16

Their eyes were kept from recognizing him. 📖

## Luke 24:17

He asked what they discussed so intently as they walked. 📖

They stopped, looking sad.

## Luke 24:18

Cleopas, one of them, asked, “Are you the only visitor to Jerusalem who does not know what happened there recently?” 📖

## Luke 24:19

He asked what happened, and they told him about Jesus of Nazareth. 📖

“He was a prophet, powerful in words and actions before God and everyone.

## Luke 24:20

Our chief priests and rulers handed him over for the death sentence and crucified him. 📖

## Luke 24:21

We hoped he would redeem Israel, and this is the third day since it happened. 📖

**Help:** Redeem means rescue or set free.

## Luke 24:22

Some of our women amazed us; they went to the tomb early this morning. 📖

## Luke 24:23

They found no body, but reported a vision of angels saying Jesus was alive. 📖

## Luke 24:24

Some companions found the tomb as the women said, but did not see him.” 📖

## Luke 24:25

Jesus said, “You foolish people, slow in heart to believe everything the prophets said! 📖

## Luke 24:26

Was it not necessary for the Christ to suffer and then enter his glory?” 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Luke 24:27

Starting with Moses and all the Prophets, he explained what all Scripture said about himself. 📖

## Luke 24:28

Near their destination village, he seemed to be going farther. 📖

## Luke 24:29

They pleaded with him to stay because evening approached and the day was nearly over. 📖

He went inside to stay.

## Luke 24:30

Reclining at table, he took bread, blessed it, broke it, and gave it to them. 📖

## Luke 24:31

Their eyes opened and they recognized him, then he disappeared from sight. 📖

## Luke 24:32

They asked whether their hearts burned while he spoke on the road and opened Scripture to them. 📖

**Help:** Burning hearts pictures their powerful response to Jesus' words.

## Luke 24:33

That hour, they rose and returned to Jerusalem, finding the Eleven gathered with others. 📖

## Luke 24:34

Those gathered said the Lord had truly risen and appeared to Simon. 📖

## Luke 24:35

The two described the road events and recognizing him when he broke bread. 📖

## Luke 24:36

While they described this, Jesus stood among them and said, “Peace be with you.” 📖

## Luke 24:37

They were startled and afraid, thinking they saw a spirit. 📖

## Luke 24:38

Jesus asked why they were troubled and why doubts arose in their hearts. 📖

## Luke 24:39

“Look at my hands and feet; it is me. 📖

Touch and see, because a spirit has no flesh and bones, as you see I have.”

## Luke 24:40

He showed his hands and feet. 📖

## Luke 24:41

They still could not believe because of their joy and amazement. 📖

He asked whether they had anything to eat.

## Luke 24:42

They gave him a piece of broiled fish. 📖

**Help:** Broiled fish is cooked with direct heat.

## Luke 24:43

He took it and ate before them. 📖

## Luke 24:44

He said, “I told you while I was still with you that everything written about me must be fulfilled. 📖

This includes Moses' Law, the Prophets, and the Psalms.”

## Luke 24:45

He opened their minds to understand Scripture. 📖

## Luke 24:46

He said, “Scripture says the Christ will suffer and rise from death on the third day. 📖

## Luke 24:47

Repentance and forgiveness of sins will be preached in his name to all nations, starting in Jerusalem. 📖

**Help:** Repentance means turning from wrongdoing back to God; sins are wrongs against God.

## Luke 24:48

You are witnesses of these things. 📖

## Luke 24:49

I am sending my Father's promise upon you; remain in the city until clothed with power from above.” 📖

**Help:** Clothed with power pictures receiving power from God.

## Luke 24:50

He led them as far as Bethany, raised his hands, and blessed them. 📖

## Luke 24:51

While blessing them, he left and was carried into heaven. 📖

## Luke 24:52

They worshipped him and returned to Jerusalem with great joy. 📖

## Luke 24:53

They continually praised God in the temple. 📖
