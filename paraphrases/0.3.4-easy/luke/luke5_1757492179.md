# Luke 5 — Following Jesus

**About this chapter**

Jesus calls followers, heals people, forgives sins, and answers questions about fasting.

## Luke 5:1

Jesus stood beside Lake Gennesaret, with crowds pressing around to hear God's word. 📖

## Luke 5:2

He saw two boats by the lake; the fishermen had left them to wash their nets. 📖

## Luke 5:3

Jesus entered Simon's boat and asked him to move a little from shore. 📖

He sat and taught the people from the boat.

## Luke 5:4

After speaking, he told Simon to go into deep water and lower the nets for a catch. 📖

## Luke 5:5

Simon replied, “Master, we worked hard all night and caught nothing. 📖

But because you say so, I will lower the nets.”

## Luke 5:6

They did this and caught so many fish that the nets started tearing. 📖

## Luke 5:7

They signalled their partners in the other boat for help. 📖

The partners came and filled both boats so full that they started sinking.

## Luke 5:8

Seeing this, Simon Peter fell at Jesus' knees.

He said, “Go away from me, Lord, because I am a sinful man.” 📖

**Help:** Sin is wrongdoing against God.

## Luke 5:9

He and his companions were amazed at their catch. 📖

## Luke 5:10

His partners James and John, Zebedee's sons, were amazed too. 📖

Jesus told Simon, “Do not fear; from now on you will catch people.”

**Help:** Catching people pictures bringing people to follow Jesus, not trapping them.

## Luke 5:11

They brought the boats ashore, left everything, and followed him. 📖

## Luke 5:12

In one town, a man covered with leprosy saw Jesus and fell facedown. 📖

He begged, “Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean.”

**Help:** Leprosy names a serious skin disease; being clean here concerns healing and religious acceptance, not a person's worth.

## Luke 5:13

Jesus reached out and touched him, saying, “I am willing; be clean!” 📖

The leprosy immediately left him.

## Luke 5:14

Jesus told him not to tell anyone, but to show himself to the priest. 📖

He must give Moses' required offering for cleansing as evidence to them.

## Luke 5:15

Yet news about Jesus spread even more, and great crowds came to hear him and be healed. 📖

## Luke 5:16

He often withdrew into the wilderness to pray. 📖

## Luke 5:17

One day, Pharisees and law teachers sat nearby as Jesus taught. 📖

People had come from Jerusalem and every village of Galilee and Judea.

The Lord's power was present for him to heal sick people.

## Luke 5:18

Some men carried a paralyzed man on a mat and tried to bring him inside before Jesus. 📖

**Help:** Paralyzed means unable to move parts of the body.

## Luke 5:19

They could not get through the crowd, so they went onto the roof. 📖

They lowered the man on his mat through the roof tiles, right before Jesus in the crowd's middle.

## Luke 5:20

Seeing their faith, Jesus said, “Friend, your sins are forgiven.” 📖

## Luke 5:21

The law teachers and Pharisees thought, “Who is this man speaking blasphemy? 📖

Only God can forgive sins!”

**Help:** Blasphemy means speaking against God; this is the leaders' accusation.

## Luke 5:22

Jesus knew their thoughts and asked why they thought these things in their hearts. 📖

## Luke 5:23

He asked whether saying “Your sins are forgiven” was easier than saying “Get up and walk.” 📖

## Luke 5:24

He said this would show that the Son of Man had authority on earth to forgive sins. 📖

He told the paralyzed man to get up, take his mat, and go home.

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself; authority means the right and power to act.

## Luke 5:25

Immediately, the man stood before everyone, picked up his mat, and went home praising God. 📖

## Luke 5:26

Everyone was amazed and praised God. 📖

Filled with awe, they said they had seen remarkable things that day.

## Luke 5:27

Afterwards, Jesus went out, saw Levi the tax collector at his booth, and told him to follow. 📖

## Luke 5:28

Levi got up, left everything, and followed him. 📖

## Luke 5:29

Levi gave Jesus a great feast at his home. 📖

Many tax collectors and others ate with them.

## Luke 5:30

The Pharisees and their law teachers asked Jesus' disciples why they ate and drank with tax collectors and sinners. 📖

## Luke 5:31

Jesus answered, “Sick people need a doctor, rather than healthy people. 📖

## Luke 5:32

I came to call sinners to repentance, rather than the righteous.” 📖

**Help:** Repentance means turning from wrongdoing back to God; righteous means right before God.

## Luke 5:33

They said John's disciples and the Pharisees' disciples often fasted and prayed, but Jesus' disciples kept eating and drinking. 📖

**Help:** Fasting means choosing not to eat for a time.

## Luke 5:34

Jesus asked, “Can you make the bridegroom's guests fast while he is with them? 📖

**Help:** A bridegroom is a man getting married; Jesus uses a wedding picture.

## Luke 5:35

The time will come when the bridegroom is taken away, and then they will fast.” 📖

## Luke 5:36

He told another story: “Nobody tears cloth from new clothing to patch old clothing. 📖

That would tear the new clothing, and the new patch would not match the old.

## Luke 5:37

Nobody puts new wine in old wineskins. 📖

The new wine would burst them, spilling the wine and ruining the skins.

**Help:** Wineskins are containers made from animal skin.

## Luke 5:38

New wine goes into new wineskins. 📖

## Luke 5:39

Nobody wants new wine after drinking old wine, because they say the old is better.” 📖
