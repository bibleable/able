# Luke 20 — Questions about authority

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches at the temple and answers challenges from religious leaders.

## Luke 20:1

One day, Jesus taught the people and preached good news in the temple courts. 📖

Chief priests, law teachers, and elders approached.

## Luke 20:2

They asked what authority he had for his actions and who gave it. 📖

## Luke 20:3

Jesus answered that he too would ask a question. 📖

## Luke 20:4

“Did John's baptism come from heaven or people?” 📖

## Luke 20:5

They discussed it, saying, “If we say heaven, he will ask why we did not believe John. 📖

## Luke 20:6

If we say people, everyone will stone us, believing John was a prophet.” 📖

**Help:** Stone means attack by throwing stones.

## Luke 20:7

They answered that they did not know its source. 📖

## Luke 20:8

Jesus said he would not tell them his authority for these actions either. 📖

## Luke 20:9

He told the people a story: “A man planted a vineyard and rented it to tenants. 📖

He went away for a long time.

**Help:** A vineyard grows grapes; tenants rent property.

## Luke 20:10

At harvest, he sent a servant to collect his share of fruit. 📖

The tenants beat him and sent him away with nothing.

## Luke 20:11

He sent another, whom they beat and shamed, sending him away with nothing. 📖

## Luke 20:12

He sent a third, whom they wounded and threw out. 📖

## Luke 20:13

The owner wondered what to do and decided to send his beloved son, hoping they would respect him. 📖

## Luke 20:14

Seeing the son, they discussed killing him because he was the heir, so the inheritance would be theirs. 📖

**Help:** An heir is someone due to receive another person's property.

## Luke 20:15

They threw him outside the vineyard and killed him. 📖

What will the owner do to them?

## Luke 20:16

He will come, kill those tenants, and give the vineyard to others.” 📖

The listeners said, “May that never happen!”

## Luke 20:17

Jesus looked straight at them and asked, “What does this Scripture mean? 📖

The stone rejected by builders became the cornerstone.

**Help:** A cornerstone is a main stone in a building.

## Luke 20:18

Anyone falling on this stone will break into pieces; anyone it falls on will be crushed.” 📖

## Luke 20:19

The law teachers and chief priests knew he meant them and wanted to arrest him that hour. 📖

But they feared the people.

## Luke 20:20

They watched him and sent spies pretending to be sincere, hoping to trap him with his words. 📖

They wanted to hand him to the governor's rule and authority.

## Luke 20:21

They said, “Teacher, you speak and teach correctly, without favouring people, and teach God's way truthfully. 📖

## Luke 20:22

Is paying taxes to Caesar lawful for us or not?” 📖

**Help:** Caesar was the Roman emperor's title.

## Luke 20:23

Jesus saw their deceit and spoke. 📖

## Luke 20:24

He asked for a denarius and asked whose picture and writing it carried. 📖

They answered, “Caesar's.”

**Help:** A denarius was a coin.

## Luke 20:25

He said, “Give Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and God what belongs to God.” 📖

## Luke 20:26

They could not trap his words before the people, and amazed by his answer, fell silent. 📖

## Luke 20:27

Some Sadducees, who deny resurrection, came with a question. 📖

**Help:** Resurrection means rising from death; Sadducees were a Jewish religious group.

## Luke 20:28

They said Moses wrote about a man whose brother dies, leaving a wife but no children. 📖

That man must marry his brother's widow and raise children for his brother.

## Luke 20:29

Seven brothers lived; the first married but died childless. 📖

## Luke 20:30

Then the second married the widow. 📖

## Luke 20:31

The third did too; likewise, all seven died without children. 📖

## Luke 20:32

Finally, the woman died. 📖

## Luke 20:33

They asked whose wife she would be in the resurrection, since all seven married her. 📖

## Luke 20:34

Jesus answered, “People of this age marry and are given in marriage. 📖

## Luke 20:35

Those considered worthy to share in the coming age and resurrection will not marry. 📖

They will not be given in marriage.

## Luke 20:36

They cannot die anymore, being like angels. 📖

As sons of the resurrection, they are sons of God.

## Luke 20:37

Moses showed the dead are raised in the burning-bush passage. 📖

He calls the Lord the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

## Luke 20:38

He is God of the living, not the dead, because all are alive to him.” 📖

## Luke 20:39

Some law teachers said, “Teacher, you spoke well!” 📖

## Luke 20:40

They dared ask no more questions. 📖

## Luke 20:41

Jesus asked, “How can people say the Christ is David's Son? 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Luke 20:42

David himself says in Psalms, ‘The Lord told my Lord to sit at his right hand. 📖

## Luke 20:43

He must sit there until the Lord makes his enemies a footstool for his feet.’ 📖

**Help:** Enemies as a footstool pictures their defeat; sitting at God's right hand pictures honour and authority.

## Luke 20:44

David calls him Lord, so how is he David's son?” 📖

## Luke 20:45

With everyone listening, Jesus told his disciples: 📖

## Luke 20:46

“Beware of law teachers. 📖

They like walking round in long robes.

They love marketplace greetings, main synagogue seats, and places of honour at feasts.

## Luke 20:47

They cheat widows out of their houses and pray long prayers for show. 📖

They will receive greater condemnation.”

**Help:** Condemnation means judgment declaring guilt and punishment.
