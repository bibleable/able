# Luke 14 — Guests and the cost of following

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals a man, teaches about feasts, and explains the cost of being his disciple.

## Luke 14:1

One Sabbath, Jesus went to eat at a leading Pharisee's home, where people watched him closely. 📖

**Help:** The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish day of rest.

## Luke 14:2

A man with dropsy was there before him. 📖

**Help:** Dropsy is an old name for swelling in the body.

## Luke 14:3

Jesus asked the law experts and Pharisees whether healing on the Sabbath was lawful or not. 📖

## Luke 14:4

They stayed silent, and he took hold of the man, healed him, and sent him away. 📖

## Luke 14:5

He asked whether they would immediately rescue a son or ox falling into a pit on the Sabbath. 📖

## Luke 14:6

They could not answer these questions. 📖

## Luke 14:7

Noticing guests choosing places of honour, Jesus told them this story: 📖

## Luke 14:8

“When invited to a wedding feast, do not take the place of honour. 📖

Someone more important may have been invited.

## Luke 14:9

The host who invited you both will tell you to give that person your seat. 📖

Then, ashamed, you will have to take the last place.

## Luke 14:10

Instead, take the last place, so your host can say, ‘Friend, move to a better place.’ 📖

Then everyone at the table will see you honoured.

## Luke 14:11

Everyone raising himself high will be brought low; whoever makes himself low will be raised high.” 📖

## Luke 14:12

Jesus told his host, “When giving dinner or a feast, do not invite friends, brothers, relatives, or rich neighbours. 📖

They may invite you back, and you will be repaid.

## Luke 14:13

Instead, invite poor people, people with physical disabilities, people unable to walk properly, and blind people. 📖

## Luke 14:14

You will be blessed because they cannot repay you. 📖

You will be repaid when the righteous are raised from death.”

**Help:** Righteous means right before God.

## Luke 14:15

A guest heard this and said, “Blessed is everyone eating at the feast in God's kingdom.” 📖

## Luke 14:16

Jesus answered, “A man prepared a great feast and invited many. 📖

## Luke 14:17

At feast time, he sent his servant to tell the invited guests, ‘Come, because everything is ready.’ 📖

## Luke 14:18

They all began making excuses. 📖

The first asked to be excused because he had bought a field and needed to see it.

## Luke 14:19

Another asked to be excused because he had bought five pairs of oxen and was going to try them. 📖

## Luke 14:20

Another said he had married a wife and could not come. 📖

## Luke 14:21

The servant reported this to his master, who became angry. 📖

He ordered him quickly into the city's streets and alleys.

He must bring poor people, people with physical disabilities, blind people, and people unable to walk properly.

## Luke 14:22

The servant said his orders were completed, but there was still room. 📖

## Luke 14:23

The master told him to go to the highways and hedges and compel people to come, filling his house. 📖

**Help:** Compel means press people strongly to do something.

## Luke 14:24

He said none of the originally invited men would taste his feast.” 📖

## Luke 14:25

Large crowds travelled with Jesus, and he turned to speak to them. 📖

## Luke 14:26

“Anyone coming to me must hate his father, mother, wife, children, brothers, sisters, and even his own life. 📖

Otherwise, he cannot be my disciple.

**Help:** Disciple means someone learning from and following Jesus.

This is a severe saying about loyalty; its interpretation needs further assessment.

## Luke 14:27

Anyone not carrying his cross and following me cannot be my disciple. 📖

**Help:** The cross pictures the cost and danger of following Jesus.

## Luke 14:28

Would anyone wanting to build a tower not first sit down and calculate whether he could afford to finish? 📖

## Luke 14:29

Otherwise, after laying foundations without being able to finish, everyone seeing it will mock him. 📖

## Luke 14:30

They will say he started building but could not finish. 📖

## Luke 14:31

Would a king going to war not first sit down to consider his strength? 📖

Can his ten thousand men fight the king approaching with twenty thousand?

## Luke 14:32

If not, he sends representatives while the other king remains distant, asking for peace terms. 📖

## Luke 14:33

Likewise, anyone not giving up everything he has cannot be my disciple. 📖

## Luke 14:34

Salt is good, but if it loses its flavour, how can it be seasoned? 📖

## Luke 14:35

It is useful neither for soil nor the manure pile, and people throw it away. 📖

Anyone with ears to hear should listen.”

**Help:** Manure is animal waste used to help soil grow crops.
