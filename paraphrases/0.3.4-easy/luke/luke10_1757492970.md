# Luke 10 — Sent out to serve

**About this chapter**

Jesus sends seventy-two people, teaches through a Samaritan's story, and visits Martha and Mary.

## Luke 10:1

The Lord appointed seventy-two others, sending them in pairs to every town and place he would visit. 📖

## Luke 10:2

He said, “The harvest is large but workers are few, so ask its Lord to send workers into his harvest. 📖

**Help:** Harvest pictures people ready to hear God's message and the work of reaching them.

## Luke 10:3

Go, I send you like lambs among wolves. 📖

**Help:** This pictures vulnerable messengers facing danger.

## Luke 10:4

Carry no purse, bag, or sandals, and greet nobody along the road. 📖

## Luke 10:5

When entering a house, first say, ‘Peace to this house.’ 📖

## Luke 10:6

If a man of peace is there, your peace will rest on him; otherwise, it will return to you. 📖

**Help:** A man of peace describes someone ready to receive the greeting of peace.

## Luke 10:7

Stay in that house, eating and drinking what they offer, because a worker deserves wages. 📖

Do not move from house to house.

## Luke 10:8

If a town welcomes you, eat whatever they serve. 📖

## Luke 10:9

Heal sick people there and tell them God's kingdom is near them. 📖

## Luke 10:10

If a town refuses to welcome you, go into its streets and announce this: 📖

## Luke 10:11

‘We wipe away even your town's dust from our feet as evidence against you. 📖

But know for certain that God's kingdom is near.’

## Luke 10:12

I tell you, on that day, Sodom will find it more bearable than that town. 📖

## Luke 10:13

Woe to you, Chorazin and Bethsaida! 📖

If your miracles had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago.

They would have sat in sackcloth and ashes.

**Help:** Woe warns of grief and judgment; repentance means turning from wrongdoing back to God.

Sackcloth is rough clothing; sackcloth and ashes showed sorrow.

## Luke 10:14

Tyre and Sidon will find judgment more bearable than you. 📖

## Luke 10:15

Capernaum, will you rise to heaven? 📖

No, you will be brought down to Hades!

**Help:** Hades names the place of the dead.

## Luke 10:16

Whoever listens to you listens to me.

Whoever rejects you rejects me.

Whoever rejects me rejects the one who sent me.” 📖

## Luke 10:17

The seventy-two returned joyfully, saying, “Lord, even demons obey us in your name.” 📖

## Luke 10:18

He answered, “I saw Satan fall from heaven like lightning. 📖

## Luke 10:19

I have given you authority to step on snakes and scorpions and over all the enemy's power. 📖

Nothing will harm you.

## Luke 10:20

But rejoice that your names are written in heaven, rather than that spirits obey you.” 📖

## Luke 10:21

Jesus rejoiced in the Holy Spirit. 📖

He said, “I praise you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth.

You hid these things from wise, learned people and revealed them to little children.

Yes, Father, this pleased you.

## Luke 10:22

My Father has trusted me with everything. 📖

Only the Father knows who the Son is.

Only the Son knows who the Father is, along with those to whom the Son chooses to reveal him.”

## Luke 10:23

Turning privately to the disciples, he said, “Blessed are the eyes seeing what you see. 📖

## Luke 10:24

Many prophets and kings longed to see and hear what you see and hear, but did not.” 📖

## Luke 10:25

A law expert stood to test him, asking, “Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?” 📖

**Help:** Eternal life is life without end; inherit means receive something passed on to you.

## Luke 10:26

Jesus asked what the Law said and how he read it. 📖

## Luke 10:27

He answered, “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, strength, and mind. 📖

Love your neighbour as yourself.”

## Luke 10:28

Jesus said he answered correctly and told him, “Do this and you will live.” 📖

## Luke 10:29

Wanting to justify himself, the man asked who his neighbour was. 📖

**Help:** Justify himself means show himself to be right.

## Luke 10:30

Jesus answered, “A man travelled down from Jerusalem to Jericho and was attacked by robbers. 📖

They stripped him, beat him, and left him half dead.

## Luke 10:31

By chance, a priest travelled that road, saw him, and passed on the other side. 📖

## Luke 10:32

A Levite likewise reached the spot, saw him, and passed on the other side. 📖

**Help:** Levites helped with worship at the temple.

## Luke 10:33

A Samaritan on a journey reached him and felt compassion when he saw him. 📖

## Luke 10:34

He went to him, poured oil and wine on his wounds, and bandaged them. 📖

He put him on his own animal, took him to an inn, and cared for him.

## Luke 10:35

Next day, he gave two denarii to the innkeeper, asking him to care for the man. 📖

He promised to repay any extra expense when he returned.

**Help:** Denarii were coins; an inn provided food and lodging for travellers.

## Luke 10:36

Which of the three was a neighbour to the man attacked by robbers?” 📖

## Luke 10:37

The law expert answered, “The one showing mercy.” 📖

Jesus told him to go and do the same.

## Luke 10:38

As they travelled, Jesus entered a village where Martha welcomed him into her home. 📖

## Luke 10:39

Her sister Mary sat at the Lord's feet, listening to his message. 📖

## Luke 10:40

Martha was distracted by all the preparations. 📖

She said, “Lord, do you not care that my sister leaves me to serve alone?

Tell her to help!”

## Luke 10:41

The Lord answered, “Martha, Martha, you worry and are upset about many things. 📖

## Luke 10:42

Only one thing is needed, and Mary has chosen the good portion. 📖

It will not be taken from her.”

**Help:** Good portion pictures Mary's choice to listen to Jesus.
