# Luke 6 — Mercy and putting teaching into action

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals on the Sabbath, chooses twelve apostles, and teaches his followers.

## Luke 6:1

One Sabbath, Jesus walked through grainfields. 📖

His disciples picked grain heads, rubbed them in their hands, and ate them.

**Help:** The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish day of rest.

## Luke 6:2

Some Pharisees asked why they were doing something unlawful on the Sabbath. 📖

## Luke 6:3

Jesus asked, “Have you not read what David did when he and his companions were hungry? 📖

## Luke 6:4

He entered God's house, took the holy bread, ate it, and gave some to his companions. 📖

Only priests were allowed to eat that bread.”

## Luke 6:5

Jesus said the Son of Man was Lord of the Sabbath. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 6:6

On another Sabbath, Jesus taught in a synagogue, where a man's right hand was withered. 📖

**Help:** Withered means the hand had become weak and shrunken; a synagogue is a Jewish place of worship and teaching.

## Luke 6:7

The law teachers and Pharisees watched closely for Sabbath healing, looking for a reason to accuse Jesus. 📖

## Luke 6:8

Knowing their thoughts, Jesus told the man with the withered hand to stand among them. 📖

The man got up and stood there.

## Luke 6:9

Jesus asked, “Is doing good or evil lawful on the Sabbath? 📖

Is saving life or destroying it lawful?”

## Luke 6:10

He looked around at everyone, then told the man to stretch out his hand. 📖

The man did, and his hand was restored.

## Luke 6:11

The law teachers and Pharisees became furious and discussed what they might do to Jesus. 📖

## Luke 6:12

Jesus went to a mountain and spent the whole night praying to God. 📖

## Luke 6:13

At daylight, he called his disciples and chose twelve, whom he named apostles. 📖

**Help:** Disciples learn from and follow someone; apostles are people sent with a task and message.

## Luke 6:14

They included Simon, whom he named Peter, and Simon's brother Andrew. 📖

There were James, John, Philip, and Bartholomew.

## Luke 6:15

There were Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, and Simon called the Zealot. 📖

## Luke 6:16

There were Judas son of James and Judas Iscariot, who became a traitor. 📖

## Luke 6:17

Jesus came down with them and stood on level ground. 📖

A large crowd of disciples and many other people were there.

They came from all over Judea, Jerusalem, and the coasts of Tyre and Sidon.

## Luke 6:18

They came to hear him and be healed of illnesses; those troubled by unclean spirits were healed too. 📖

**Help:** Unclean spirits are evil spirits; this does not describe physical dirt or disability.

## Luke 6:19

Everyone tried to touch him, because power came from him and healed them all. 📖

## Luke 6:20

Looking at his disciples, Jesus said, “Blessed are you poor people, because God's kingdom is yours. 📖

## Luke 6:21

Blessed are you hungry now, because you will be filled. 📖

Blessed are you crying now, because you will laugh.

## Luke 6:22

You are blessed when people hate, exclude, insult, and reject your name as evil because of the Son of Man. 📖

## Luke 6:23

Rejoice that day and leap for joy, because your reward in heaven is great. 📖

Their ancestors treated the prophets the same way.

## Luke 6:24

But woe to you rich people, because you have already received your comfort. 📖

**Help:** Woe warns of grief and judgment.

## Luke 6:25

Woe to you well fed now, because you will be hungry. 📖

Woe to you laughing now, because you will grieve and cry.

## Luke 6:26

Woe to you when everyone speaks well of you, because their ancestors treated false prophets the same way. 📖

## Luke 6:27

I tell those willing to listen: love your enemies and do good to those hating you. 📖

## Luke 6:28

Bless those cursing you and pray for those mistreating you. 📖

## Luke 6:29

If someone hits one cheek, offer the other too. 📖

If someone takes your cloak, do not withhold your tunic.

**Help:** A cloak is an outer coat; a tunic is clothing worn underneath.

## Luke 6:30

Give to everyone asking, and do not demand your possessions back from someone taking them. 📖

## Luke 6:31

Treat others as you want them to treat you. 📖

## Luke 6:32

If you love people who love you, what credit is that? 📖

Even sinners love those loving them.

**Help:** Credit here means recognition for doing something good.

## Luke 6:33

If you do good to those doing good to you, what credit is that? 📖

Even sinners do the same.

## Luke 6:34

If you lend only to those you expect to repay you, what credit is that? 📖

Even sinners lend to sinners, expecting full repayment.

## Luke 6:35

Love your enemies, do good, and lend without expecting anything back. 📖

Your reward will be great, and you will be sons of the Most High.

He is kind to ungrateful and wicked people.

**Help:** Most High is a name for God.

## Luke 6:36

Be merciful, just as your Father is merciful. 📖

## Luke 6:37

Do not judge, and you will not be judged. 📖

Do not condemn, and you will not be condemned.

Forgive, and you will be forgiven.

**Help:** Condemn means pronounce someone guilty and deserving punishment.

## Luke 6:38

Give, and you will receive. 📖

A good measure will be poured into your lap, pressed down, shaken together, and overflowing.

The measure you use will be used for you.”

**Help:** The image describes measuring a generous amount; a lap could hold goods in folded clothing.

## Luke 6:39

Jesus told a story, asking, “Can one blind man lead another? 📖

Will they not both fall into a pit?

## Luke 6:40

A disciple is not above his teacher, but every fully trained disciple will be like his teacher. 📖

## Luke 6:41

Why notice a speck in your brother's eye but miss the beam in your own? 📖

**Help:** A beam is a large piece of wood; these pictures contrast another person's fault with one's own larger fault.

## Luke 6:42

How can you offer to remove your brother's speck when you cannot see your own beam? 📖

You hypocrite, first remove your own beam.

Then you will see clearly to remove your brother's speck.

**Help:** A hypocrite pretends to be different from what they really are; this continues the picture about faults.

## Luke 6:43

No good tree grows bad fruit, and no bad tree grows good fruit. 📖

## Luke 6:44

Each tree is known by its fruit. 📖

People do not gather figs from thornbushes or grapes from brambles.

**Help:** Brambles are bushes with thorns.

## Luke 6:45

A good person brings good from the good treasure in his heart. 📖

An evil person brings evil from the evil treasure in his heart.

The mouth speaks from what overflows in the heart.

## Luke 6:46

Why call me ‘Lord, Lord’ but not do what I say? 📖

## Luke 6:47

I will show what someone is like who comes to me, hears my words, and acts on them. 📖

## Luke 6:48

He resembles a house builder digging deep and laying foundations on rock. 📖

Floodwater crashed against the house but could not shake it, because it was well built.

## Luke 6:49

Someone hearing without acting resembles a builder putting a house on the ground without foundations. 📖

Floodwater crashed against that house, which immediately fell and was utterly destroyed.”
