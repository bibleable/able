# Luke 1 — Promises about John and Jesus

**About this chapter**

This account begins with messages about the births of John and Jesus.

## Luke 1:1

Many people have tried to write accounts of what has been fulfilled among us. 📖

## Luke 1:2

These things were passed to us by people who saw them from the beginning and served the message. 📖

## Luke 1:3

Most excellent Theophilus, I too carefully investigated everything from the beginning. 📖

I decided to write an account for you in an orderly way.

## Luke 1:4

This will help you be sure of what you have been taught. 📖

## Luke 1:5

When Herod ruled Judea, a priest named Zechariah belonged to Abijah's group of priests. 📖

His wife Elizabeth came from Aaron's family.

## Luke 1:6

Both were righteous before God and followed all the Lord's commands and rules without blame. 📖

**Help:** Righteous means right before God.

## Luke 1:7

They had no children because Elizabeth could not have a baby, and both were old. 📖

## Luke 1:8

Zechariah's group was on duty, and he served as a priest before God. 📖

## Luke 1:9

Following the priests' custom, lots were used to choose him to enter the Lord's temple and burn incense. 📖

**Help:** Lots were a way to choose by chance; incense gives a sweet smell when burned.

## Luke 1:10

At the time for the incense offering, all the gathered people prayed outside. 📖

## Luke 1:11

An angel of the Lord appeared, standing to the right of the incense altar. 📖

**Help:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

## Luke 1:12

Zechariah saw him and was startled and very afraid. 📖

## Luke 1:13

The angel said, “Do not fear, Zechariah; your prayer has been heard. 📖

Your wife Elizabeth will have a son, and you must name him John.

## Luke 1:14

He will bring you joy and delight, and many will celebrate his birth. 📖

## Luke 1:15

He will be great before the Lord and must never drink wine or strong drink. 📖

He will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before birth.

## Luke 1:16

He will turn many of Israel's people back to the Lord their God. 📖

## Luke 1:17

He will go ahead of the Lord in Elijah's spirit and power. 📖

He will turn fathers' hearts towards their children, and disobedient people towards the wisdom of the righteous.

He will make people ready for the Lord.”

**Help:** Elijah was a prophet; the angel describes John's work using Elijah's spirit and power.

## Luke 1:18

Zechariah asked the angel, “How can I be sure? 📖

My wife and I are old.”

## Luke 1:19

The angel replied, “I am Gabriel, and I stand before God. 📖

I was sent to speak to you and bring this good news.

## Luke 1:20

You did not believe my words, which will happen at the right time. 📖

So you will be silent, unable to speak until the day these things happen.”

## Luke 1:21

Outside, the people waited and wondered why Zechariah stayed in the temple so long. 📖

## Luke 1:22

When he emerged unable to speak, they realized he had seen a vision inside. 📖

He kept making signs, but could not speak.

**Help:** A vision is something a person is shown beyond ordinary sight.

## Luke 1:23

After finishing his days of service, Zechariah went home. 📖

## Luke 1:24

After this, Elizabeth became pregnant and stayed apart from others for five months. 📖

## Luke 1:25

She said, “The Lord has done this for me. 📖

He has shown me favour and taken away my disgrace among people.”

**Help:** Elizabeth describes how people treated her; not having children does not make someone less valuable.

## Luke 1:26

In Elizabeth's sixth month of pregnancy, God sent Gabriel to Nazareth, a town in Galilee. 📖

## Luke 1:27

He went to Mary, a virgin promised in marriage to Joseph, who belonged to David's family. 📖

**Help:** A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

## Luke 1:28

The angel greeted Mary, saying she was highly favoured and the Lord was with her. 📖

## Luke 1:29

His words deeply troubled Mary, who wondered what this greeting meant. 📖

## Luke 1:30

The angel said, “Do not fear, Mary; you have found favour with God. 📖

## Luke 1:31

You will become pregnant and have a son, and must name him Jesus. 📖

## Luke 1:32

He will be great and be called the Most High's Son. 📖

The Lord God will give him the throne of his father David.

**Help:** Most High is a name for God; David is described as Jesus' ancestor.

## Luke 1:33

He will rule Jacob's family forever, and his kingdom will never end.” 📖

## Luke 1:34

Mary asked how this could happen, since she was a virgin. 📖

## Luke 1:35

The angel answered, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the Most High's power will overshadow you. 📖

So the holy one born will be called God's Son.

**Help:** Overshadow pictures God's power coming over Mary; it does not describe a sexual act.

## Luke 1:36

Your relative Elizabeth has also become pregnant with a son in her old age. 📖

People called her unable to have children, but she is in her sixth month.

## Luke 1:37

No word from God will ever fail.” 📖

## Luke 1:38

Mary answered, “I am the Lord's servant; let it happen as you have said.” 📖

The angel left her.

## Luke 1:39

Mary got ready and hurried to a town in Judah's hill country. 📖

## Luke 1:40

She entered Zechariah's home and greeted Elizabeth. 📖

## Luke 1:41

When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby leaped inside her.

Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit. 📖

## Luke 1:42

Elizabeth cried loudly, “You are blessed among women, and your baby is blessed! 📖

## Luke 1:43

Why am I so honoured that my Lord's mother comes to me? 📖

## Luke 1:44

As soon as I heard your greeting, the baby inside me leaped for joy. 📖

## Luke 1:45

Blessed is the woman who believed that the Lord would fulfil his words to her.” 📖

## Luke 1:46

Mary said, “My soul praises the Lord's greatness. 📖

## Luke 1:47

My spirit rejoices in God my Saviour. 📖

## Luke 1:48

He has looked kindly on his servant's low position. 📖

From now on, all generations will call me blessed.

## Luke 1:49

The Mighty One has done great things for me; his name is holy. 📖

## Luke 1:50

His mercy reaches those who fear him, from generation to generation. 📖

**Help:** Fearing God includes taking him seriously and treating him with deep respect.

## Luke 1:51

He has done mighty things with his arm.

He has scattered people who think proud thoughts in their hearts. 📖

**Help:** God's arm pictures his strength.

## Luke 1:52

He has brought rulers down from their thrones and raised the humble. 📖

## Luke 1:53

He has filled hungry people with good things, but sent rich people away empty. 📖

## Luke 1:54

He has helped his servant Israel, remembering to be merciful. 📖

## Luke 1:55

This is what he promised our ancestors, Abraham and his descendants forever.” 📖

## Luke 1:56

Mary stayed with Elizabeth for about three months, then returned home. 📖

## Luke 1:57

When Elizabeth's time came, she gave birth to a son. 📖

## Luke 1:58

Her neighbours and relatives heard of the Lord's great mercy towards her and celebrated with her. 📖

## Luke 1:59

On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child and wanted to name him Zechariah after his father. 📖

**Help:** Circumcision removes skin from the end of a male's penis.

## Luke 1:60

His mother refused, saying his name must be John. 📖

## Luke 1:61

They said none of her relatives had that name. 📖

## Luke 1:62

They made signs to his father, asking what name he wanted for the child. 📖

## Luke 1:63

Zechariah asked for a writing tablet and wrote, “His name is John,” amazing everyone. 📖

## Luke 1:64

Immediately his mouth and tongue were freed, and he spoke, praising God. 📖

## Luke 1:65

All their neighbours were filled with awe. 📖

People throughout Judea's hill country discussed these events.

## Luke 1:66

Everyone hearing this wondered in their hearts what the child would become.

The Lord's hand was with him. 📖

**Help:** The Lord's hand pictures God's help and power.

## Luke 1:67

His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke this prophecy: 📖

## Luke 1:68

“Blessed is the Lord, Israel's God, because he has visited and rescued his people. 📖

## Luke 1:69

He has raised a horn of salvation for us in his servant David's family. 📖

**Help:** A horn pictures strength; salvation means rescue.

## Luke 1:70

He spoke about this through his holy prophets long ago. 📖

## Luke 1:71

This is salvation from our enemies and from the hands of everyone hating us. 📖

## Luke 1:72

He does this to show mercy to our ancestors and remember his holy covenant. 📖

**Help:** A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Luke 1:73

This is the oath he swore to our ancestor Abraham. 📖

## Luke 1:74

He promised rescue from our enemies' hands, so we could serve him without fear. 📖

## Luke 1:75

We could serve before him in holiness and righteousness all our lives. 📖

**Help:** Holiness means being set apart for God; righteousness means being right before him.

## Luke 1:76

You, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High. 📖

You will go ahead of the Lord to prepare his way.

## Luke 1:77

You will teach his people about salvation through their sins being forgiven. 📖

**Help:** Sin is wrongdoing against God.

## Luke 1:78

Because of our God's tender mercy, the Dawn will visit us from above. 📖

**Help:** Dawn pictures the light of God's rescue coming to people.

## Luke 1:79

It will shine on those living in darkness and death's shadow, and guide our feet into the path of peace.” 📖

**Help:** These pictures describe danger, death, and the hope of peace.

## Luke 1:80

The child grew and became strong in spirit. 📖

He lived in the wilderness until he appeared publicly to Israel.
