# Luke 2 — Jesus is born

**About this chapter**

This chapter tells about Jesus as a baby and as a boy.

## Luke 2:1

Caesar Augustus ordered everyone in the Roman Empire to register for a census. 📖

**Help with a word:** A census counts and records the people.

## Luke 2:2

This was the first census while Quirinius governed Syria. 📖

## Luke 2:3

Everyone went to their own town to register. 📖

## Luke 2:4

Joseph went from Nazareth in Galilee to Bethlehem in Judea. 📖

Bethlehem was David's town.

Joseph came from King David's family.

## Luke 2:5

Joseph went to register with Mary. 📖

They were promised in marriage.

Mary was expecting a baby.

## Luke 2:6

While they were there, the time came for her baby to be born. 📖

## Luke 2:7

Mary gave birth to her first child, a son. 📖

She wrapped him in cloths and laid him in an animal feeding box.

There was no room for them at the inn.

## Luke 2:8

Nearby, shepherds stayed in the fields at night, watching their sheep. 📖

## Luke 2:9

An angel of the Lord stood before them. 📖

The Lord's bright glory shone around them.

They were terrified.

## Luke 2:10

The angel said, “Do not be afraid. 📖

I bring good news that will give all the people great joy.”

## Luke 2:11

“Today a Saviour has been born for you in David's town. 📖

He is Christ the Lord!”

**Help with titles:** A Saviour rescues people.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Luke 2:12

“This sign will help you find him. 📖

The baby will be wrapped in cloths and lying in an animal feeding box.”

## Luke 2:13

Suddenly, a great crowd of heaven's army appeared with the angel. 📖

They praised God.

## Luke 2:14

“Give glory to God in the highest heaven! 📖

Peace on earth to people who receive his favour!”

## Luke 2:15

The angels went back to heaven. 📖

The shepherds said, “Let us go to Bethlehem.

Let us see what the Lord has told us about.”

## Luke 2:16

They hurried there. 📖

They found Mary and Joseph.

The baby lay in the animal feeding box.

## Luke 2:17

After seeing the baby, they told people what the angel had said about him. 📖

## Luke 2:18

Everyone who heard the shepherds was amazed. 📖

## Luke 2:19

Mary kept all these things in her mind. 📖

She thought deeply about them.

## Luke 2:20

The shepherds returned, praising and honouring God. 📖

Everything they heard and saw was just as the angel had said.

## Luke 2:21

When the baby was 8 days old, he was circumcised and named Jesus. 📖

The angel had given this name before Mary became pregnant.

**Help with a word:** Circumcision removes skin from the end of a male's penis.

## Luke 2:22

The time for religious cleansing under Moses' law ended. 📖

Jesus' parents brought him to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord.

**Help with words:** Religious cleansing was a ceremony required after childbirth.

## Luke 2:23

The Lord's law says every firstborn male must be set apart for the Lord. 📖

## Luke 2:24

His parents also brought the offering required by the Lord's law. 📖

It was a pair of turtledoves or 2 young pigeons.

## Luke 2:25

Simeon lived in Jerusalem. 📖

He did right and served God faithfully.

He waited for God to comfort Israel.

The Holy Spirit was upon him.

## Luke 2:26

The Holy Spirit had told Simeon he would see the Lord's Christ before he died. 📖

## Luke 2:27

The Spirit led Simeon into the temple courts. 📖

Jesus' parents brought the child there to do what the law required.

## Luke 2:28

Simeon held Jesus in his arms and praised God. 📖

## Luke 2:29

“Lord and Master, you have kept your promise. 📖

Now you release your servant in peace.”

## Luke 2:30

“My eyes have seen the rescue you bring.” 📖

## Luke 2:31

“You have prepared this rescue for all people to see.” 📖

## Luke 2:32

“He is a light to make you known to the Gentiles. 📖

He brings glory to your people Israel.”

**Help with a word:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Luke 2:33

Jesus' father and mother were amazed by what Simeon said. 📖

## Luke 2:34

Simeon blessed them and spoke to Mary. 📖

“This child is appointed to cause many in Israel to fall and rise.

He will be a sign that people speak against.”

## Luke 2:35

“The thoughts of many hearts will be shown. 📖

A sword will pierce your own soul too.”

**Help with this picture:** The sword pictures the deep pain Mary will suffer.

## Luke 2:36

Anna was a prophet who spoke God's messages. 📖

She was Phanuel's daughter, from Asher's tribe.

She was old.

Her husband had died after 7 years of marriage.

## Luke 2:37

Anna was now a widow aged 84. 📖

She never left the temple, but worshipped night and day.

She prayed and fasted.

**Help with a word:** Fasting means choosing not to eat for a time to focus on God.

## Luke 2:38

Anna came up then and thanked God. 📖

She spoke about the child to everyone waiting for God to rescue Jerusalem.

## Luke 2:39

Jesus' parents finished everything the Lord's law required. 📖

They returned to Nazareth, their own town in Galilee.

## Luke 2:40

The child grew strong and full of wisdom. 📖

God's kindness was with him.

## Luke 2:41

Every year, his parents went to Jerusalem for Passover. 📖

**Help with a name:** Passover remembers God freeing Israel from Egypt.

## Luke 2:42

When Jesus was 12, they went to the festival as usual. 📖

## Luke 2:43

After the festival, his parents started home. 📖

Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem.

His parents did not know.

## Luke 2:44

They thought he was with the travelling group. 📖

After a day's journey, they looked for him among relatives and friends.

## Luke 2:45

They could not find him. 📖

They returned to Jerusalem to search.

## Luke 2:46

After 3 days, they found him in the temple courts. 📖

He sat among the teachers, listening and asking questions.

## Luke 2:47

Everyone who heard him was amazed at his understanding and answers. 📖

## Luke 2:48

His parents were astonished. 📖

Mary said, “Child, why have you done this to us?

Your father and I have been very worried, looking for you.”

## Luke 2:49

Jesus said, “Why were you looking for me? 📖

Did you not know I had to be in my Father's house?”

## Luke 2:50

They did not understand what Jesus meant. 📖

## Luke 2:51

Jesus returned to Nazareth with them and obeyed them. 📖

His mother kept all these things in her heart.

## Luke 2:52

Jesus grew taller and wiser. 📖

He grew in favour with God and people.
