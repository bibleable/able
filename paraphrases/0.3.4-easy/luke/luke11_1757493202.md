# Luke 11 — Prayer and the light within

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches prayer, answers accusations, and warns religious leaders.

## Luke 11:1

After Jesus finished praying one day, a disciple asked, “Lord, teach us to pray as John taught his disciples.” 📖

## Luke 11:2

He said, “When praying, say: ‘Father, may your name be treated as holy. 📖

May your kingdom come.

## Luke 11:3

Give us our daily bread each day. 📖

## Luke 11:4

Forgive our sins, because we also forgive everyone sinning against us. 📖

Do not lead us into temptation.’”

**Help:** Sin is wrongdoing against God; temptation is pressure to do wrong.

## Luke 11:5

Jesus said, “Suppose you visit a friend at midnight and ask to borrow three loaves. 📖

## Luke 11:6

You say a travelling friend arrived and you have nothing to serve him. 📖

## Luke 11:7

Suppose your friend inside tells you not to bother him. 📖

His door is shut, and he and his children are in bed.

He says he cannot get up to give anything.

## Luke 11:8

I tell you, even though friendship does not make him rise, your persistence will. 📖

He will give as much as you need.

**Help:** Persistence means continuing to ask without giving up.

## Luke 11:9

I tell you: ask, and it will be given to you. 📖

Seek, and you will find.

Knock, and the door will be opened to you.

## Luke 11:10

Everyone asking receives, everyone seeking finds, and the door opens to everyone knocking. 📖

## Luke 11:11

Which father among you would give his son a snake when asked for a fish? 📖

## Luke 11:12

Would he give a scorpion when asked for an egg? 📖

## Luke 11:13

You who are evil know how to give good gifts to your children. 📖

How much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those asking him!”

## Luke 11:14

Jesus drove out a mute demon, and the man who could not speak then spoke, amazing the crowds. 📖

## Luke 11:15

Some said he drove out demons by Beelzebul, their prince. 📖

**Help:** This is an accusation about the source of Jesus' power; Beelzebul names the demons' ruler.

## Luke 11:16

Others tested him by demanding a sign from heaven. 📖

## Luke 11:17

Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said, “A kingdom fighting itself will be ruined.

A house fighting itself will fall. 📖

## Luke 11:18

If Satan fights himself, how can his kingdom stand? 📖

You say I drive out demons by Beelzebul.

## Luke 11:19

If that is how I drive them out, by whom do your sons drive them out? 📖

So they will be your judges.

## Luke 11:20

But if I drive them out by God's finger, God's kingdom has come upon you. 📖

**Help:** God's finger pictures God's power at work.

## Luke 11:21

When a fully armed strong man guards his house, his belongings are safe. 📖

## Luke 11:22

When someone stronger attacks and defeats him, he takes the armour the man trusted and shares the goods he takes. 📖

## Luke 11:23

Whoever is not with me is against me; whoever does not gather with me scatters. 📖

## Luke 11:24

An unclean spirit leaves a man and travels dry places seeking rest without finding it. 📖

It decides to return to its former house.

**Help:** Unclean spirit means evil spirit, not physical dirt or disability; the house pictures the person it left.

## Luke 11:25

Returning, it finds the house swept clean and orderly. 📖

## Luke 11:26

It goes and brings seven spirits more wicked than itself, and they enter and live there. 📖

The man's final state is worse than his first.”

## Luke 11:27

A woman in the crowd shouted, “Blessed is the womb you were born from and the breasts that fed you!” 📖

## Luke 11:28

He answered, “Rather, blessed are people hearing God's word and obeying it.” 📖

## Luke 11:29

As crowds grew, Jesus said, “This wicked generation demands a sign, but will receive only Jonah's sign. 📖

## Luke 11:30

As Jonah was a sign to Nineveh's people, the Son of Man will be a sign to this generation. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 11:31

The Queen of the South will rise at judgment with this generation's men and condemn them. 📖

She came from earth's ends to hear Solomon's wisdom, but someone greater than Solomon is here.

## Luke 11:32

Nineveh's men will rise at judgment with this generation and condemn it. 📖

They repented at Jonah's preaching, but someone greater than Jonah is here.

**Help:** Repent means turn from wrongdoing back to God.

## Luke 11:33

Nobody lights a lamp and puts it in a cellar or under a basket. 📖

They put it on a stand so people entering can see.

## Luke 11:34

Your eye is your body's lamp. 📖

When your eyes are good, your whole body has light; when bad, your body has darkness.

**Help:** Jesus uses sight and light as a picture; this does not blame anyone for sight loss.

## Luke 11:35

Be careful that the light within you is not darkness. 📖

## Luke 11:36

If your whole body has light and no dark part, you will shine brightly. 📖

It will be like a lamp shining on you.”

## Luke 11:37

While Jesus spoke, a Pharisee invited him to eat, and he entered and reclined at the table. 📖

**Help:** People sometimes ate leaning on couches.

## Luke 11:38

The Pharisee was surprised Jesus did not wash before eating. 📖

**Help:** This concerns a religious washing custom.

## Luke 11:39

The Lord said, “You Pharisees clean cups and dishes outside, but inside you are full of greed and wickedness. 📖

## Luke 11:40

You fools, did the one making the outside not also make the inside? 📖

## Luke 11:41

Give as gifts to the poor the things within you, and everything will be clean for you. 📖

**Help:** This difficult statement connects giving and inner cleansing; its interpretation needs further assessment.

## Luke 11:42

Woe to you Pharisees, giving a tenth of mint, rue, and every herb but neglecting justice and love for God! 📖

You should practise these without neglecting the others.

**Help:** Woe warns of grief and judgment; mint and rue are herbs; giving a tenth is called tithing.

## Luke 11:43

Woe to you Pharisees, loving the main synagogue seats and marketplace greetings! 📖

## Luke 11:44

Woe to you, like unmarked graves people walk over without knowing!” 📖

## Luke 11:45

A law expert said, “Teacher, saying this insults us too.” 📖

## Luke 11:46

He answered, “Woe to you law experts too! 📖

You put heavy burdens on people but will not lift one finger to help.

## Luke 11:47

Woe to you, building prophets' tombs although your ancestors killed them! 📖

## Luke 11:48

You witness and agree to your ancestors' deeds: they killed the prophets and you build their tombs. 📖

## Luke 11:49

So God's wisdom said, ‘I will send them prophets and apostles. 📖

They will kill some and persecute others.’

**Help:** Apostles are sent with a task and message; persecuted means mistreated because of beliefs.

## Luke 11:50

This generation will therefore answer for all the prophets' blood shed since the world's foundation. 📖

## Luke 11:51

This includes Abel's blood through Zechariah's, who was killed between the altar and sanctuary. 📖

Yes, I tell you, this generation will answer for all of it.

**Help:** The altar and sanctuary were parts of the temple used for worship.

## Luke 11:52

Woe to you law experts, taking away knowledge's key! 📖

You did not enter, and you hindered those entering.”

**Help:** The key pictures access to knowledge; Jesus describes teachers stopping others from understanding.

## Luke 11:53

Leaving there, Jesus faced fierce opposition from law teachers and Pharisees, who questioned him about many things. 📖

## Luke 11:54

They waited to trap him in something he might say. 📖
