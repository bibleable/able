# Luke 15 — Lost and found

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells stories about a lost sheep, a lost coin, and a father with two sons.

## Luke 15:1

All the tax collectors and sinners gathered to listen to Jesus. 📖

**Help:** Sin means wrongdoing against God.

## Luke 15:2

The Pharisees and law teachers grumbled because he welcomed sinners and ate with them. 📖

## Luke 15:3

Jesus told them this story. 📖

## Luke 15:4

“If you had a hundred sheep and lost one, would you not leave the ninety-nine in the pasture? 📖

Would you not search for the lost one until you found it?

## Luke 15:5

When you find it, you joyfully put it on your shoulders. 📖

## Luke 15:6

At home, you call your friends and neighbours to celebrate because you found your lost sheep. 📖

## Luke 15:7

Likewise, heaven will have more joy over one sinner repenting than over ninety-nine righteous people needing no repentance. 📖

**Help:** Repentance means turning from wrongdoing back to God; righteous means right before God.

## Luke 15:8

If a woman with ten silver coins loses one, does she not light a lamp and sweep her house? 📖

Does she not search carefully until she finds it?

## Luke 15:9

Finding it, she calls friends and neighbours to rejoice because she found her lost coin. 📖

## Luke 15:10

Likewise, I tell you, there is joy before God's angels over one sinner repenting.” 📖

## Luke 15:11

Jesus said, “A man had two sons. 📖

## Luke 15:12

The younger asked his father for his share of the estate, and the father divided his property between them. 📖

**Help:** Estate means the property a person owns.

## Luke 15:13

A few days later, the younger son gathered everything and left for a distant country. 📖

There he wasted his wealth in wild living.

## Luke 15:14

After he spent everything, a severe famine struck that country, and he became needy. 📖

**Help:** Famine means a time of very little food.

## Luke 15:15

He hired himself to a citizen there, who sent him into his fields to feed pigs. 📖

## Luke 15:16

He longed to fill his stomach with the pigs' food pods, but nobody gave him anything. 📖

**Help:** Pods are seed cases from plants.

## Luke 15:17

Coming to his senses, he thought, ‘My father's hired servants have plenty of food, but I am starving to death! 📖

## Luke 15:18

I will go back and say, “Father, I sinned against heaven and you. 📖

## Luke 15:19

I am no longer worthy to be called your son; make me like a hired servant.”’ 📖

## Luke 15:20

He got up and went back. 📖

While he was still far away, his father saw him and felt compassion.

He ran to his son, embraced him, and kissed him.

## Luke 15:21

The son said, ‘Father, I sinned against heaven and you. 📖

I am no longer worthy to be called your son.’

## Luke 15:22

His father told the servants to hurry, bring the best robe, and dress him. 📖

They must put a ring on his finger and sandals on his feet.

## Luke 15:23

He told them to bring and kill the fattened calf so they could feast and celebrate. 📖

## Luke 15:24

‘My son was dead and is alive again; he was lost and is found!’ 📖

They began celebrating.

## Luke 15:25

The older son was in the field, and nearing home, heard music and dancing. 📖

## Luke 15:26

He called a servant and asked what was happening. 📖

## Luke 15:27

The servant said his brother had returned, and his father killed the fattened calf because he was safe and well. 📖

## Luke 15:28

The older son became angry and refused to enter, so his father came out and pleaded. 📖

## Luke 15:29

He said, ‘I served you all these years and never disobeyed your commands. 📖

Yet you never gave me even a young goat to celebrate with my friends.

## Luke 15:30

But when this son of yours returns after wasting your wealth with prostitutes, you kill the fattened calf for him!’ 📖

**Help:** Prostitutes receive money for sex; this is the older son's accusation.

## Luke 15:31

His father said, ‘Son, you are always with me, and everything I have is yours. 📖

## Luke 15:32

But it was right to celebrate and be glad, because your brother was dead and is alive again. 📖

He was lost and is found.’”
