# Luke 3 — John prepares the way

**About this chapter**

John calls people to turn back to God; Jesus is baptized, and his family line is listed.

## Luke 3:1

It was the fifteenth year of Tiberius Caesar's rule, and Pontius Pilate governed Judea. 📖

Herod was tetrarch of Galilee, and his brother Philip was tetrarch of Ituraea and Trachonitis.

Lysanias was tetrarch of Abilene.

**Help:** Tetrarch was a title for a regional ruler.

## Luke 3:2

Annas and Caiaphas were high priests when God's word came to John, Zechariah's son, in the wilderness. 📖

## Luke 3:3

John travelled throughout the Jordan region, preaching baptism of repentance for sins to be forgiven. 📖

**Help:** Baptism uses water; repentance means turning away from wrongdoing and back to God.

## Luke 3:4

Isaiah the prophet's book says a voice calls in the wilderness, “Prepare the Lord's way and make his paths straight. 📖

## Luke 3:5

Every valley will be filled, and every mountain and hill lowered. 📖

Crooked ways will become straight, and rough ways smooth.

## Luke 3:6

All humanity will see God's salvation.” 📖

**Help:** Salvation means rescue; the road pictures preparing for the Lord.

## Luke 3:7

John told the crowds coming for baptism, “You family of vipers, who warned you to flee the coming wrath? 📖

**Help:** Vipers are poisonous snakes; wrath means fierce anger in judgment.

John uses a severe picture in his warning.

## Luke 3:8

Produce fruit that matches repentance, and do not tell yourselves you have Abraham as your father. 📖

I tell you, God can raise children for Abraham from these stones.

**Help:** Fruit pictures actions showing that a person has turned back to God.

## Luke 3:9

The axe is ready at the trees' roots. 📖

Every tree without good fruit will be cut down and thrown into fire.”

## Luke 3:10

The crowds asked what they should do. 📖

## Luke 3:11

John said anyone with two tunics should share with someone who has none. 📖

Anyone with food should do the same.

**Help:** A tunic is a piece of clothing.

## Luke 3:12

Even tax collectors came for baptism and asked, “Teacher, what should we do?” 📖

## Luke 3:13

He told them to collect no more than they were allowed. 📖

## Luke 3:14

Soldiers also asked what to do. 📖

He told them not to take money through force or false accusations, and to be content with their wages.

## Luke 3:15

The people waited eagerly, all wondering in their hearts whether John might be the Christ. 📖

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Luke 3:16

John answered everyone, “I baptize with water, but someone more powerful is coming. 📖

I am unworthy even to untie his sandal straps.

He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.

## Luke 3:17

His winnowing fork is in his hand to clear his threshing floor and gather wheat into his barn. 📖

He will burn the chaff with fire that cannot be put out.”

**Help:** These farming tools separate useful grain from chaff, the unwanted husks; John uses them as a judgment picture.

## Luke 3:18

With these and many other urgent appeals, John told the people the good news. 📖

## Luke 3:19

John rebuked Herod the tetrarch about his brother's wife Herodias and all his evil deeds. 📖

## Luke 3:20

Herod added another evil: he locked John in prison. 📖

## Luke 3:21

When all the people were baptized, Jesus was baptized too. 📖

While he prayed, heaven opened.

## Luke 3:22

The Holy Spirit came down on him in bodily form like a dove. 📖

A voice from heaven said, “You are my beloved Son; you please me greatly.”

## Luke 3:23

Jesus was about thirty when he began his public work. 📖

People regarded him as Joseph's son.

Joseph was Heli's son.

**Help:** The following verses list the family line backwards, from child to parent.
