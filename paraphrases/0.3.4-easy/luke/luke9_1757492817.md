# Luke 9 — Mission and the cost of following

**About this chapter**

Jesus sends the Twelve, feeds a crowd, reveals his glory, and teaches about following him.

## Luke 9:1

Jesus gathered the Twelve and gave them power and authority over all demons and to cure illnesses. 📖

## Luke 9:2

He sent them to announce God's kingdom and heal sick people. 📖

## Luke 9:3

He said, “Take nothing for the journey: no staff, bag, bread, money, or second tunic. 📖

**Help:** A staff is a walking stick; a tunic is a piece of clothing.

## Luke 9:4

Stay in the house you enter until you leave that area. 📖

## Luke 9:5

If people do not welcome you, shake dust from your feet when leaving town as evidence against them.” 📖

## Luke 9:6

They travelled from village to village, preaching good news and healing people everywhere. 📖

## Luke 9:7

Herod the tetrarch heard about everything happening and was puzzled, because some said John had risen from death. 📖

**Help:** Tetrarch was a title for a regional ruler.

## Luke 9:8

Others said Elijah appeared, or an ancient prophet had risen. 📖

## Luke 9:9

Herod said he had beheaded John and wondered who this man was he kept hearing about. 📖

He kept trying to see Jesus.

## Luke 9:10

The apostles returned and told Jesus everything they had done. 📖

He took them away privately to Bethsaida.

## Luke 9:11

Crowds found out and followed, and he welcomed them, spoke about God's kingdom, and healed those needing healing. 📖

## Luke 9:12

Near day's end, the Twelve asked him to dismiss the crowd to nearby villages and countryside for food and lodging. 📖

They said this place was deserted.

## Luke 9:13

Jesus told them to feed the people themselves. 📖

They answered, “We have only five loaves and two fish, unless we buy food for everyone.”

## Luke 9:14

There were about five thousand men. 📖

He told the disciples to seat them in groups of about fifty.

## Luke 9:15

They did so, and everyone sat down. 📖

## Luke 9:16

Jesus took the five loaves and two fish, looked to heaven, blessed them, and broke them. 📖

He gave them to the disciples to serve the people.

## Luke 9:17

Everyone ate and was satisfied, and the disciples collected twelve baskets of leftover pieces. 📖

## Luke 9:18

One day, Jesus prayed in private with the disciples there and asked who the crowds said he was. 📖

## Luke 9:19

They answered, “John the Baptist, Elijah, or an ancient prophet risen from death.” 📖

## Luke 9:20

Jesus asked who they themselves said he was. 📖

Peter answered, “God's Christ.”

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one.

## Luke 9:21

Jesus strictly warned them to tell nobody. 📖

## Luke 9:22

He said the Son of Man must suffer much and be rejected by elders, chief priests, and law teachers. 📖

He must be killed and raised to life on the third day.

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 9:23

He told everyone, “Anyone wanting to follow me must deny himself, take up his cross daily, and follow me. 📖

**Help:** Denying oneself means putting Jesus before one's own wishes; the cross pictures the cost and danger of following him.

## Luke 9:24

Whoever wants to save his life will lose it; whoever loses his life for my sake will save it. 📖

## Luke 9:25

What benefit is gaining the whole world if a person loses or forfeits his very self? 📖

**Help:** Forfeit means lose or be forced to give up.

## Luke 9:26

If someone is ashamed of me and my words, the Son of Man will be ashamed of him. 📖

This will happen when he comes in his own glory, his Father's glory, and the holy angels' glory.

## Luke 9:27

I tell you truly, some standing here will not die before seeing God's kingdom.” 📖

## Luke 9:28

About eight days later, Jesus took Peter, John, and James up a mountain to pray. 📖

## Luke 9:29

While praying, his face changed appearance and his clothes shone white. 📖

## Luke 9:30

Suddenly, two men, Moses and Elijah, talked with him. 📖

## Luke 9:31

They appeared in glory, discussing his departure that he would soon accomplish in Jerusalem. 📖

**Help:** Departure points to Jesus' coming death and leaving; this remains an image in the source.

## Luke 9:32

Peter and his companions were overcome by sleep. 📖

When awake, they saw Jesus' glory and both men standing with him.

## Luke 9:33

As Moses and Elijah were leaving, Peter said, “Master, it is good to be here. 📖

Let us build three shelters, one each for you, Moses, and Elijah.”

He did not know what he was saying.

## Luke 9:34

While Peter spoke, a cloud surrounded them, and they were afraid as they entered it. 📖

## Luke 9:35

A voice from the cloud said, “This is my Son, whom I have chosen; listen to him!” 📖

## Luke 9:36

After the voice, only Jesus remained with them. 📖

The disciples kept silent, telling nobody in those days what they saw.

## Luke 9:37

The next day, they came down the mountain and a large crowd met Jesus. 📖

## Luke 9:38

A man suddenly cried from the crowd, “Teacher, I beg you to look at my son, my only child. 📖

## Luke 9:39

A spirit seizes him, making him suddenly scream and shake violently, foaming at the mouth. 📖

It keeps hurting him and rarely leaves him.

## Luke 9:40

I begged your disciples to drive it out, but they could not.” 📖

## Luke 9:41

Jesus answered, “You unbelieving and corrupt generation, how long must I stay and put up with you? 📖

Bring your son here.”

## Luke 9:42

As the boy approached, the demon threw him down shaking violently. 📖

Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, healed the boy, and returned him to his father.

## Luke 9:43

Everyone was amazed at God's greatness. 📖

While they marvelled at all Jesus did, he spoke to his disciples.

## Luke 9:44

“Listen carefully: the Son of Man is about to be handed over to men.” 📖

## Luke 9:45

They did not understand, because the meaning was hidden from them so they could not grasp it. 📖

They feared asking him about it.

## Luke 9:46

The disciples argued over which of them would be greatest. 📖

## Luke 9:47

Jesus knew their hearts' thoughts and placed a little child beside him. 📖

## Luke 9:48

He said, “Whoever welcomes this little child in my name welcomes me.

Whoever welcomes me welcomes the one who sent me. 📖

The least among you all is the greatest.”

## Luke 9:49

John said, “Master, we saw someone driving out demons in your name and tried to stop him. 📖

He does not travel with us.”

## Luke 9:50

Jesus told him not to stop the man, because whoever was not against them was for them. 📖

## Luke 9:51

As the time approached for Jesus to be taken up, he firmly set out for Jerusalem. 📖

## Luke 9:52

He sent messengers ahead into a Samaritan village to prepare for him. 📖

## Luke 9:53

The people refused to welcome him because he was going to Jerusalem. 📖

## Luke 9:54

Seeing this, James and John asked, “Lord, should we call fire from heaven to destroy them?” 📖

## Luke 9:55

Jesus turned and rebuked them. 📖

## Luke 9:56

He and his disciples went to another village. 📖

## Luke 9:57

On the road, someone promised to follow Jesus wherever he went. 📖

## Luke 9:58

Jesus answered, “Foxes have dens and birds have nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head.” 📖

## Luke 9:59

He told another man to follow him. 📖

The man said, “Lord, let me first bury my father.”

## Luke 9:60

Jesus answered, “Let the dead bury their own dead; you go and announce God's kingdom.” 📖

## Luke 9:61

Another said, “I will follow you, Lord, but first let me say goodbye to my family.” 📖

## Luke 9:62

Jesus answered, “Nobody putting a hand to the plough and looking back is fit for God's kingdom.” 📖

**Help:** A plough prepares soil for planting; Jesus uses an image of moving forward without turning back.
