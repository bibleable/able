# Luke 13 — Turn back to God

**About this chapter**

Jesus warns people, heals a woman, teaches about God's kingdom, and grieves over Jerusalem.

## Luke 13:1

Some people told Jesus about Galileans whose blood Pilate had mixed with their sacrifices. 📖

## Luke 13:2

He asked, “Do you think their suffering means they were worse sinners than all other Galileans? 📖

## Luke 13:3

No, I tell you; unless you repent, you too will all perish. 📖

**Help:** Repent means turn from wrongdoing back to God; perish means die or be destroyed.

## Luke 13:4

What about the eighteen killed when Siloam's tower fell on them? 📖

Were they more sinful than everyone else in Jerusalem?

## Luke 13:5

No, I tell you; unless you repent, you too will all perish.” 📖

## Luke 13:6

Jesus told a story about a man with a fig tree planted in his vineyard. 📖

He looked for fruit but found none.

**Help:** A vineyard is land where grapes grow.

## Luke 13:7

“He told the keeper, ‘For three years I have looked for fruit on this tree and found none. 📖

Cut it down; why should it use up the soil?’

## Luke 13:8

The keeper answered, ‘Sir, leave it this year while I dig around it and add fertilizer. 📖

**Help:** Fertilizer is material added to soil to help plants grow.

## Luke 13:9

If it bears fruit next year, good; otherwise, you can cut it down.’” 📖

## Luke 13:10

One Sabbath, Jesus taught in a synagogue. 📖

**Help:** The Sabbath is the weekly Jewish rest day; a synagogue is a place of Jewish worship and teaching.

## Luke 13:11

A woman there had been disabled by a spirit for eighteen years. 📖

She was bent over and could not stand straight.

**Help:** This describes this woman's condition; it does not say every disability is caused by a spirit.

## Luke 13:12

Jesus saw her, called her over, and said, “Woman, you are freed from your disability.” 📖

## Luke 13:13

He placed his hands on her, and she immediately straightened and praised God. 📖

## Luke 13:14

The synagogue leader was angry because Jesus healed on the Sabbath. 📖

He told the crowd, “There are six workdays; come for healing then, not on the Sabbath.”

## Luke 13:15

The Lord answered, “You hypocrites! 📖

Do you not each untie an ox or donkey from its stall on the Sabbath?

Do you not lead it to water?

**Help:** Hypocrites pretend to be different from what they really are.

## Luke 13:16

Should this daughter of Abraham, bound by Satan for eighteen long years, not be freed on the Sabbath?” 📖

## Luke 13:17

His words shamed all his opponents, while the whole crowd rejoiced at his glorious deeds. 📖

## Luke 13:18

Jesus asked, “What is God's kingdom like, and what can I compare it to? 📖

## Luke 13:19

It is like a mustard seed a man put in his garden. 📖

It grew into a tree, and birds nested in its branches.”

## Luke 13:20

He asked again what he could compare God's kingdom to. 📖

## Luke 13:21

“It is like yeast a woman mixed into three measures of flour until it spread through all of it.” 📖

**Help:** Yeast makes dough rise; the source gives three measures without a modern weight.

## Luke 13:22

Jesus taught through towns and villages as he travelled towards Jerusalem. 📖

## Luke 13:23

Someone asked, “Lord, will only a few people be saved?” 📖

He answered them.

## Luke 13:24

“Try hard to enter through the narrow door. 📖

I tell you, many will try but be unable.

## Luke 13:25

After the master rises and shuts the door, you will stand outside knocking. 📖

You will say, ‘Lord, open the door for us.’

He will answer, ‘I do not know where you are from.’

## Luke 13:26

You will say, ‘We ate and drank with you, and you taught in our streets.’ 📖

## Luke 13:27

He will answer, ‘I tell you, I do not know where you are from. 📖

Leave me, all you evildoers.’

## Luke 13:28

You will see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all the prophets in God's kingdom, but you yourselves will be thrown out. 📖

There will be crying and grinding of teeth.

## Luke 13:29

People will come from east, west, north, and south to recline at the table in God's kingdom. 📖

**Help:** People sometimes ate leaning on couches.

## Luke 13:30

Some who are last will be first, and some who are first will be last.” 📖

## Luke 13:31

At that hour, some Pharisees told Jesus to leave because Herod wanted to kill him. 📖

## Luke 13:32

He said, “Go tell that fox I will keep driving out demons and healing today and tomorrow. 📖

On the third day I will reach my goal.

**Help:** Fox is a sharp image Jesus uses for Herod.

## Luke 13:33

Yet I must keep going today, tomorrow, and the next day. 📖

It is not acceptable for a prophet to perish outside Jerusalem.

## Luke 13:34

Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you kill prophets and stone those sent to you! 📖

How often I wanted to gather your children like a hen gathering chicks under her wings!

But you were unwilling.

## Luke 13:35

Look, your house is left empty and ruined. 📖

I tell you, you will not see me again until you say these words.

‘Blessed is the one coming in the Lord's name.’”
