# Luke 7 — Faith, mercy, and forgiveness

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals a servant, raises a widow's son, speaks about John, and forgives a woman.

## Luke 7:1

After the people heard all his teaching, Jesus went to Capernaum. 📖

## Luke 7:2

A centurion's servant, whom he valued highly, was sick and near death. 📖

**Help:** A centurion was a Roman army officer.

## Luke 7:3

Hearing about Jesus, the centurion sent Jewish elders to ask him to come and heal his servant. 📖

## Luke 7:4

They strongly urged Jesus to help, saying the man deserved this. 📖

## Luke 7:5

They said he loved their nation and had built their synagogue. 📖

**Help:** A synagogue is a Jewish place of worship and teaching.

## Luke 7:6

Jesus went with them, but near the house the centurion sent friends with a message. 📖

“Lord, do not trouble yourself; I am unworthy to have you under my roof.

## Luke 7:7

That is why I did not think myself worthy to come to you. 📖

Just say the word, and my servant will be healed.

## Luke 7:8

I too am under authority, with soldiers under me. 📖

I tell one to go and he goes, another to come and he comes.

I tell my servant to do something, and he does it.”

## Luke 7:9

Jesus heard this and marvelled at the centurion. 📖

Turning to the following crowd, he said, “Not even in Israel have I found such great faith.”

## Luke 7:10

The messengers returned home and found the servant well. 📖

## Luke 7:11

Soon afterwards, Jesus went to Nain with his disciples and a large crowd. 📖

## Luke 7:12

Near the town gate, a dead man was being carried out, his mother's only son. 📖

She was a widow, and a large crowd from town was with her.

## Luke 7:13

The Lord saw her, felt compassion, and told her not to cry. 📖

## Luke 7:14

He approached and touched the coffin, and those carrying it stopped. 📖

He said, “Young man, I tell you, get up!”

## Luke 7:15

The dead man sat up and spoke, and Jesus gave him back to his mother. 📖

## Luke 7:16

Everyone was filled with awe and praised God. 📖

They said a great prophet had appeared among them and God had visited his people.

## Luke 7:17

News about Jesus spread throughout Judea and the surrounding area. 📖

## Luke 7:18

John's disciples told him about all these things. 📖

## Luke 7:19

John called two disciples and sent them to the Lord with a question. 📖

“Are you the one who was to come, or should we expect someone else?”

## Luke 7:20

The men came to Jesus and gave John the Baptist's question. 📖

“Are you the one who was to come, or should we expect someone else?”

## Luke 7:21

That very hour, Jesus healed many of illnesses, suffering, and evil spirits, and gave many blind people sight. 📖

## Luke 7:22

He answered, “Tell John what you saw and heard. 📖

Blind people see, people unable to walk now walk, and people with leprosy are cleansed.

Deaf people hear, dead people are raised, and poor people hear good news.

**Help:** Leprosy names a serious skin disease; cleansing here concerns healing and religious acceptance, not a person's worth.

## Luke 7:23

Blessed is anyone who does not turn away because of me.” 📖

## Luke 7:24

After John's messengers left, Jesus spoke to the crowds about John. 📖

“What did you go into the wilderness to see?

A reed swaying in the wind?

**Help:** A reed is a tall, thin plant.

## Luke 7:25

What else did you go to see, a man wearing fine clothes? 📖

People wearing elegant clothes and living in luxury are in palaces.

## Luke 7:26

What did you go to see, a prophet? 📖

Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.

## Luke 7:27

Scripture speaks about him: ‘I will send my messenger ahead of you to prepare your way.’ 📖

## Luke 7:28

I tell you, nobody born of women is greater than John. 📖

Yet the least person in God's kingdom is greater than he is.”

## Luke 7:29

Everyone hearing this, even tax collectors, acknowledged God's justice, having received John's baptism. 📖

## Luke 7:30

But the Pharisees and law experts rejected God's purpose for themselves, not having been baptized by John. 📖

## Luke 7:31

Jesus asked, “What can I compare this generation's people to? 📖

What are they like?

## Luke 7:32

They resemble children sitting in the marketplace, calling to each other. 📖

‘We played the flute and you did not dance; we sang a funeral song and you did not cry.’

## Luke 7:33

John the Baptist came without eating bread or drinking wine, and you say he has a demon. 📖

## Luke 7:34

The Son of Man came eating and drinking. 📖

You call him a greedy eater and drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners.

**Help:** Son of Man is Jesus' title for himself; these insults are other people's accusations.

## Luke 7:35

But wisdom is proved right by all her children.” 📖

**Help:** Wisdom's children pictures those whose lives show wisdom's results.

## Luke 7:36

A Pharisee invited Jesus to eat, and Jesus entered his house and reclined at the table. 📖

**Help:** People sometimes ate leaning on couches.

## Luke 7:37

A sinful woman from that town learned he was eating there and brought an alabaster jar of perfume. 📖

**Help:** Sin means wrongdoing against God; alabaster is a stone used to make jars.

The passage does not name her or say what her sins were.

## Luke 7:38

She stood behind Jesus at his feet, crying. 📖

She wet his feet with tears, wiped them with her hair, kissed them, and poured perfume on them.

## Luke 7:39

The Pharisee thought to himself. 📖

“If he were a prophet, he would know who is touching him and what kind of woman she is.

She is a sinner!”

## Luke 7:40

Jesus said, “Simon, I have something to tell you.” 📖

Simon answered, “Tell me, Teacher.”

## Luke 7:41

“Two men owed money to a lender, one five hundred denarii and the other fifty. 📖

**Help:** Denarii were coins; denarius is the name for one coin.

## Luke 7:42

Neither could repay, so he forgave both debts. 📖

Which will love him more?”

## Luke 7:43

Simon supposed the one forgiven more, and Jesus said he had judged correctly. 📖

## Luke 7:44

Jesus turned towards the woman and asked Simon, “Do you see her? 📖

You gave no water for my feet when I entered.

She wet my feet with tears and wiped them with her hair.

## Luke 7:45

You gave me no greeting kiss, but she has not stopped kissing my feet since I arrived. 📖

## Luke 7:46

You put no oil on my head, but she put perfume on my feet. 📖

## Luke 7:47

So I tell you, her many sins have been forgiven, for she has loved much. 📖

Whoever has been forgiven little loves little.”

## Luke 7:48

He told her, “Your sins are forgiven.” 📖

## Luke 7:49

Those at the table wondered to themselves who this was who even forgave sins. 📖

## Luke 7:50

Jesus told the woman, “Your faith has saved you; go in peace.” 📖
