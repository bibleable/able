# Luke 21 — Warnings and watchfulness

**About this chapter**

Jesus sees a widow's gift and teaches about coming trouble, Jerusalem, and his coming.

## Luke 21:1

Jesus looked up and saw rich people putting gifts in the treasury. 📖

**Help:** The treasury held temple offerings.

## Luke 21:2

A poor widow put in two small copper coins. 📖

## Luke 21:3

He said, “Truly, this poor widow gave more than all the others. 📖

## Luke 21:4

They gave from their surplus, but she gave everything she had to live on in her poverty.” 📖

**Help:** Surplus means what is left beyond what someone needs.

## Luke 21:5

Some disciples admired the temple's beautiful stones and gifts set apart for God. 📖

Jesus spoke.

## Luke 21:6

“A time will come when none of these stones remains on another; all will be thrown down.” 📖

## Luke 21:7

They asked, “Teacher, when will it happen? 📖

What sign will show it is about to happen?”

## Luke 21:8

Jesus said, “Do not be deceived. 📖

Many will come in my name claiming, ‘I am he’ and ‘The time is near.’

Do not follow them.

## Luke 21:9

Do not fear wars and rebellions; these must happen first, but the end will not come immediately.” 📖

## Luke 21:10

He continued, “Nations will rise against nations, and kingdoms against kingdoms. 📖

## Luke 21:11

There will be great earthquakes, famines, and widespread diseases in different places, with terrifying sights and great heavenly signs. 📖

## Luke 21:12

Before these things, they will seize and persecute you, delivering you to synagogues and prisons because of my name. 📖

They will bring you before kings and governors.

**Help:** Persecute means mistreat people because of their beliefs.

## Luke 21:13

This will be your chance to be witnesses. 📖

## Luke 21:14

Decide not to worry beforehand about defending yourselves. 📖

## Luke 21:15

I will give you speech and wisdom no opponent can resist or contradict. 📖

## Luke 21:16

Even parents, brothers, relatives, and friends will betray you, and some of you will be killed. 📖

## Luke 21:17

Everyone will hate you because of my name. 📖

## Luke 21:18

Yet not one hair of your head will perish. 📖

**Help:** This promise stands alongside the warning of death in verse 16; their relationship needs further assessment.

## Luke 21:19

By patient endurance, you will gain your souls. 📖

## Luke 21:20

When armies surround Jerusalem, know its ruin is near. 📖

## Luke 21:21

Those in Judea must flee to the mountains. 📖

Those inside the city must leave.

Those in the countryside must stay out of the city.

## Luke 21:22

These are days of vengeance, fulfilling everything written. 📖

**Help:** Vengeance means punishment for wrongs.

## Luke 21:23

How terrible for pregnant mothers and mothers feeding babies! 📖

The land will suffer great distress, with wrath against this people.

**Help:** Wrath means fierce anger in judgment.

## Luke 21:24

They will die by the sword and be taken captive to all nations. 📖

Gentiles will trample Jerusalem until the Gentiles' times are fulfilled.

**Help:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews; the time statement needs further interpretation.

## Luke 21:25

Signs will appear in sun, moon, and stars, and nations will be distressed. 📖

They will be confused by the sea's roar and surging waves.

## Luke 21:26

People will faint with fear and worry over what comes upon earth, because heaven's powers will shake. 📖

## Luke 21:27

They will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 21:28

When these things begin, stand and raise your heads, because your redemption is near.” 📖

**Help:** Redemption means rescue or being set free.

## Luke 21:29

Jesus told a story: “Look at the fig tree and all trees. 📖

## Luke 21:30

When leaves sprout, you see for yourselves that summer is near. 📖

## Luke 21:31

Likewise, seeing these things happen tells you God's kingdom is near. 📖

## Luke 21:32

Truly, this generation will not pass away until all these things happen. 📖

## Luke 21:33

Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words never will. 📖

## Luke 21:34

Watch yourselves, or wild living, drunkenness, and life's worries will weigh down your hearts. 📖

That day will catch you suddenly like a trap.

## Luke 21:35

It will come upon everyone on all the earth. 📖

## Luke 21:36

Always keep watch, praying for strength to escape everything about to happen and stand before the Son of Man.” 📖

## Luke 21:37

Jesus taught at the temple every day. 📖

Each evening he went out to spend the night on the Mount of Olives.

## Luke 21:38

Early each morning, all the people came to hear him at the temple. 📖
