# Luke 23 — Trial, death, and burial

**About this chapter**

Jesus is taken before Pilate and Herod, killed on a cross, and buried.

## Luke 23:1

The whole council rose and took Jesus to Pilate. 📖

## Luke 23:2

They accused him of undermining their nation, forbidding taxes to Caesar, and claiming to be Christ, a King. 📖

**Help:** These are the council's accusations, not the narrator's confirmation that they are true.

## Luke 23:3

Pilate asked whether he was King of the Jews, and Jesus answered, “You have said so.” 📖

## Luke 23:4

Pilate told the chief priests and crowds he found no basis for a charge. 📖

## Luke 23:5

They insisted his teaching stirred people throughout Judea, beginning in Galilee and reaching here. 📖

## Luke 23:6

Hearing this, Pilate asked whether Jesus was Galilean. 📖

## Luke 23:7

Learning Jesus was under Herod's rule, he sent him to Herod, who was then in Jerusalem. 📖

## Luke 23:8

Herod was delighted, having long wanted to see Jesus after hearing of him and hoping for a miracle. 📖

## Luke 23:9

He questioned Jesus at length but received no answer. 📖

## Luke 23:10

Chief priests and law teachers stood fiercely accusing him. 📖

## Luke 23:11

Herod and his soldiers ridiculed and mocked him, dressed him in a fine robe, and sent him to Pilate. 📖

## Luke 23:12

That day Herod and Pilate became friends, although they had been enemies. 📖

## Luke 23:13

Pilate gathered the chief priests, rulers, and people. 📖

## Luke 23:14

He said, “You brought him as someone stirring the people to rebellion. 📖

I examined him here before you and found him not guilty of your charges.

## Luke 23:15

Herod found the same and sent him back; he has done nothing deserving death. 📖

## Luke 23:16

I will therefore punish and release him.” 📖

## Luke 23:17

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Luke 23:18

They all shouted together for Jesus to be taken away and Barabbas released. 📖

## Luke 23:19

Barabbas was imprisoned for rebellion in the city and murder. 📖

## Luke 23:20

Pilate wanted Jesus released and addressed them again. 📖

## Luke 23:21

But they kept shouting, “Crucify him, crucify him!” 📖

**Help:** Crucify means kill on a cross.

## Luke 23:22

For the third time, Pilate asked what evil he had done, finding no offense deserving death. 📖

He said he would punish and release him.

## Luke 23:23

They loudly insisted he be crucified, and their shouting won. 📖

## Luke 23:24

Pilate ruled that their demand be carried out. 📖

## Luke 23:25

He released the man jailed for rebellion and murder, as they asked, and handed Jesus over to their will. 📖

## Luke 23:26

As soldiers led Jesus away, they seized Simon of Cyrene, who was coming in from the countryside. 📖

They placed the cross on him to carry behind Jesus.

## Luke 23:27

Many followed, including women mourning and crying loudly for him. 📖

## Luke 23:28

Jesus turned and said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, weep for yourselves and your children, rather than me. 📖

## Luke 23:29

Days are coming when people will call women unable to have children blessed. 📖

They will bless wombs that never gave birth and breasts that never fed babies.

## Luke 23:30

Then they will ask mountains to fall on them and hills to cover them. 📖

## Luke 23:31

If people do this while the tree is green, what will happen when it is dry?” 📖

**Help:** The green and dry tree is a warning image whose interpretation needs further assessment.

## Luke 23:32

Two criminals were also led out to be executed with Jesus. 📖

## Luke 23:33

At the place called The Skull, they crucified Jesus and the criminals, one on each side. 📖

## Luke 23:34

Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, because they do not know what they do.” 📖

They divided his clothes by casting lots.

**Help:** Casting lots means choosing by chance.

## Luke 23:35

The people stood watching, and rulers sneered at him. 📖

“He saved others; let him save himself if he is God's Christ, the Chosen One.”

**Help:** Christ means God's anointed one; this is the rulers' mocking speech.

## Luke 23:36

Soldiers also mocked him and came to offer sour wine. 📖

## Luke 23:37

They said, “If you are King of the Jews, save yourself!” 📖

## Luke 23:38

Above him was written: THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS. 📖

## Luke 23:39

One hanging criminal insulted him, saying, “Are you not the Christ? 📖

Save yourself and us!”

## Luke 23:40

The other rebuked him. 📖

“Do you not fear God, since you are under the same judgment?

## Luke 23:41

We deserve our punishment for our actions, but this man did nothing wrong.” 📖

## Luke 23:42

He said, “Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.” 📖

## Luke 23:43

Jesus answered, “Truly I tell you, today you will be with me in Paradise.” 📖

**Help:** Paradise names the blessed place Jesus promises here.

## Luke 23:44

About the sixth hour, darkness covered all the land until the ninth hour. 📖

**Help:** This is about midday until three in the afternoon.

## Luke 23:45

The sun darkened, and the temple curtain tore down the middle. 📖

## Luke 23:46

Jesus cried loudly, “Father, I put my spirit in your hands,” then breathed his last. 📖

**Help:** Jesus died.

## Luke 23:47

Seeing this, the centurion praised God and said Jesus was surely righteous. 📖

**Help:** A centurion was a Roman army officer; righteous means right before God.

## Luke 23:48

Everyone gathered to watch saw what happened and returned home beating their chests. 📖

## Luke 23:49

Everyone knowing him, including the women who followed from Galilee, watched from a distance. 📖

## Luke 23:50

Joseph, a council member, was good and righteous. 📖

## Luke 23:51

He had not agreed to their decision or actions. 📖

He came from Arimathea in Judea and waited for God's kingdom.

## Luke 23:52

He asked Pilate for Jesus' body. 📖

## Luke 23:53

He took it down, wrapped it in linen, and laid it in a rock-cut tomb never used before. 📖

## Luke 23:54

It was Preparation Day, and the Sabbath was beginning. 📖

**Help:** Preparation Day came before the Sabbath, the weekly Jewish day of rest.

## Luke 23:55

The women who came with Jesus from Galilee followed. 📖

They saw the tomb and how his body was laid.

## Luke 23:56

They returned to prepare spices and perfumes, then rested on the Sabbath as commanded. 📖
