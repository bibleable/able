# Luke 12 — Trust, readiness, and responsibility

**About this chapter**

Jesus warns against hypocrisy and greed, teaches trust, and describes readiness and coming division.

## Luke 12:1

Many thousands gathered, trampling each other.

Jesus spoke first to his disciples. 📖

“Beware the Pharisees' yeast, which is hypocrisy.

**Help:** Yeast spreads through dough; hypocrisy means pretending to be different from what you really are.

## Luke 12:2

Everything concealed will be revealed, and everything hidden made known. 📖

## Luke 12:3

Words spoken in darkness will be heard in daylight. 📖

Things whispered in inner rooms will be announced from rooftops.

## Luke 12:4

My friends, do not fear those killing the body who can do nothing more afterwards. 📖

## Luke 12:5

I will show you whom to fear. 📖

Fear the One who has authority to throw you into hell after you are killed.

Yes, I tell you, fear him!

## Luke 12:6

Are five sparrows not sold for two pennies? 📖

Yet God forgets none of them.

## Luke 12:7

Even every hair on your head is counted. 📖

Do not fear; you are worth more than many sparrows.

## Luke 12:8

I tell you, whoever acknowledges me before people will be acknowledged by the Son of Man before God's angels. 📖

**Help:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 12:9

Whoever denies me before people will be denied before God's angels. 📖

## Luke 12:10

Anyone speaking against the Son of Man will be forgiven. 📖

Anyone blaspheming against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven.

**Help:** Blasphemy means speaking against God; this is a severe warning whose interpretation needs further assessment.

## Luke 12:11

When brought before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, do not worry about defending yourselves or what to say. 📖

## Luke 12:12

The Holy Spirit will teach you what to say at that time.” 📖

## Luke 12:13

Someone in the crowd asked, “Teacher, tell my brother to share the inheritance with me.” 📖

**Help:** Inheritance means property received when someone dies.

## Luke 12:14

Jesus asked, “Man, who made me your judge or the person to divide your property?” 📖

## Luke 12:15

He told them, “Be alert and guard against every kind of greed. 📖

Life does not consist of having many possessions.”

## Luke 12:16

He told a story: “A rich man's land produced plentiful crops. 📖

## Luke 12:17

He wondered what to do, having nowhere to store them. 📖

## Luke 12:18

He decided to pull down his barns and build bigger ones for all his grain and goods. 📖

## Luke 12:19

He would tell himself, ‘You have good things stored for many years. 📖

Relax, eat, drink, and enjoy yourself!’

## Luke 12:20

But God said, ‘You fool, your life will be required of you this very night! 📖

Who will own everything you stored?’

**Help:** For his life to be required means he will die.

## Luke 12:21

This is how it will be for anyone storing treasure for himself without being rich towards God.” 📖

## Luke 12:22

Jesus told his disciples, “So do not worry about your life and food, or your body and clothes. 📖

## Luke 12:23

Life is more than food, and the body more than clothes. 📖

## Luke 12:24

Consider ravens: they neither plant nor harvest and have no storehouse or barn, yet God feeds them. 📖

You are worth much more than birds!

**Help:** Ravens are large black birds.

## Luke 12:25

Can any of you add one hour to your life by worrying? 📖

## Luke 12:26

If you cannot do even this small thing, why worry about the rest? 📖

## Luke 12:27

Consider lilies: they grow without working or spinning thread. 📖

Not even Solomon in all his glory was dressed like one of them.

## Luke 12:28

If God clothes grass, here today and thrown into the furnace tomorrow, how much more will he clothe you? 📖

You have so little faith!

**Help:** A furnace is a very hot oven.

## Luke 12:29

Do not be concerned or worry about what to eat or drink. 📖

## Luke 12:30

The world's Gentiles chase these things, but your Father knows you need them. 📖

**Help:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Luke 12:31

Seek his kingdom, and these things will be given to you too. 📖

## Luke 12:32

Do not fear, little flock; your Father is pleased to give you the kingdom. 📖

**Help:** Flock pictures Jesus' followers as sheep in a shepherd's care.

## Luke 12:33

Sell your possessions and give to poor people. 📖

Provide purses that never wear out, treasure in heaven that never runs out.

There, no thief comes near and no moth destroys.

## Luke 12:34

Your heart will be where your treasure is. 📖

## Luke 12:35

Be dressed for service and keep your lamps burning. 📖

## Luke 12:36

Be like servants waiting for their master to return from a wedding feast. 📖

When he knocks, they can open immediately.

## Luke 12:37

Those servants are blessed if their master finds them watching on his return. 📖

Truly, he will dress to serve, seat them reclining at the table, and serve them himself.

## Luke 12:38

Even if he comes in the second or third night watch, servants he finds alert will be blessed. 📖

**Help:** A watch is part of the night; the master may come late.

## Luke 12:39

Understand this: if a householder knew the hour of a thief's coming, he would prevent his house being broken into. 📖

## Luke 12:40

Be ready too, because the Son of Man will come at an hour you do not expect.” 📖

## Luke 12:41

Peter asked, “Lord, is this story for us or everyone else too?” 📖

## Luke 12:42

The Lord answered, “Who is the faithful, wise manager put over the master's servants to give their food on time? 📖

## Luke 12:43

That servant is blessed if his master returns and finds him doing so. 📖

## Luke 12:44

I tell you truly, he will put him over everything he owns. 📖

## Luke 12:45

But suppose the servant thinks his master will take a long time to return. 📖

He starts beating male and female servants, eating, drinking, and getting drunk.

## Luke 12:46

His master will come on a day and hour he does not expect. 📖

He will cut him into pieces and give him a place with unbelievers.

## Luke 12:47

The servant knowing his master's wishes but failing to prepare or follow them will receive many blows. 📖

## Luke 12:48

The one unknowingly doing things deserving punishment will receive few blows. 📖

Much will be required from anyone given much.

Even more will be demanded from anyone trusted with much.

## Luke 12:49

I came to light a fire on earth, and how I wish it were already burning! 📖

## Luke 12:50

I have a baptism to undergo, and am deeply distressed until it is completed. 📖

**Help:** Baptism here pictures suffering Jesus must go through.

## Luke 12:51

Do you think I came to bring peace on earth? 📖

No, I tell you, but division.

## Luke 12:52

From now, five in one household will divide, three against two and two against three. 📖

## Luke 12:53

Father will oppose son and son oppose father.

Mother will oppose daughter and daughter oppose mother. 📖

Mother-in-law will oppose daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law oppose mother-in-law.”

## Luke 12:54

He told the crowds, “When you see a cloud rising in the west, you predict a shower, and it happens. 📖

## Luke 12:55

When the south wind blows, you predict heat, and it comes. 📖

## Luke 12:56

You hypocrites, you understand the earth and sky's appearance, so why not understand the present time? 📖

## Luke 12:57

Why do you not decide for yourselves what is right? 📖

## Luke 12:58

Try hard to settle with your opponent while going to the magistrate. 📖

Otherwise, he may drag you to the judge, who may hand you to the officer.

The officer may throw you into prison.

**Help:** A magistrate is an official who hears legal disputes.

## Luke 12:59

I tell you, you will not leave until you pay the very last penny.” 📖
