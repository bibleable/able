# Proverbs 25 — Further sayings of Solomon

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and daily conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 25:1

These are more proverbs of Solomon. 📖

The men of Hezekiah king of Judah copied them.

## Proverbs 25:2

God’s glory is to hide a matter. 📖

Kings’ glory is to search it out.

## Proverbs 25:3

Heaven is high, and earth is deep. 📖

In the same way, kings’ hearts cannot be searched.

## Proverbs 25:4

Remove impurities from silver. 📖

Then a vessel can come forth for the silversmith.

## Proverbs 25:5

Remove wicked people from the king’s presence. 📖

Then righteousness will establish his throne.

## Proverbs 25:6

Do not raise yourself high before the king. 📖

Do not stand in great men’s place.

## Proverbs 25:7

Better to hear, “Come up here!” than be lowered before the prince. 📖

Even what you saw yourself,

## Proverbs 25:8

do not rush to bring to court. 📖

Otherwise, what will you do when your neighbor shames you in the end?

## Proverbs 25:9

Argue your case with your neighbor without revealing another person’s secret, 📖

## Proverbs 25:10

or someone hearing will shame you. 📖

Your bad reputation may never go away.

## Proverbs 25:11

A fitting word is like golden apples in silver settings. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The precious setting pictures the beauty and value of a well-chosen word.

## Proverbs 25:12

A wise man’s rebuke to a listening ear is like a gold earring or fine gold ornament. 📖

**Help with rebuke:** Strong correction.

**Help with this picture:** Gold jewelry pictures the value of wise correction that is heard.

## Proverbs 25:13

A trustworthy messenger refreshes his masters’ souls like snow’s cold at harvest. 📖

## Proverbs 25:14

A man boasting about gifts never given is like clouds and wind without rain. 📖

## Proverbs 25:15

Patience can persuade a ruler. 📖

A gentle tongue can break a bone.

**Help with this picture:** Breaking a bone pictures gentle words overcoming hard resistance.

## Proverbs 25:16

If you find honey, eat only what you need. 📖

Too much will make you vomit.

## Proverbs 25:17

Visit your neighbor’s house seldom, or he may tire of you and hate you. 📖

## Proverbs 25:18

A false witness against his neighbor is like a club, sword, or sharp arrow. 📖

## Proverbs 25:19

Trusting an unfaithful man in trouble is like a broken tooth or a foot out of joint. 📖

## Proverbs 25:20

Singing to a heavy heart is like removing clothing on a cold day or pouring vinegar on a wound. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The comparisons picture an inappropriate response that increases distress rather than helping.

## Proverbs 25:21

If your enemy is hungry, give him food. 📖

If thirsty, give him water.

## Proverbs 25:22

This heaps burning coals on his head. 📖

The LORD will reward you.

**About the coals:** The strong image is retained; its precise meaning needs further editorial review.

## Proverbs 25:23

A tongue speaking behind someone’s back brings angry looks, as north wind brings rain. 📖

## Proverbs 25:24

Better to live on a roof’s corner than share a house with an argumentative wife. 📖

## Proverbs 25:25

Good news from far away is like cold water to a weary soul. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Cold water pictures the refreshment of good news.

## Proverbs 25:26

A righteous man yielding to wicked people is like a muddy spring or polluted well. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Polluted water pictures the harm when a righteous person gives way to evil.

## Proverbs 25:27

Eating too much honey is not good. 📖

Neither is seeking your own glory.

## Proverbs 25:28

A man without control of his temper is like a city with broken walls. 📖
