# Proverbs 23 — Self-control, wise listening, and warning about drink

**About this chapter**

A teacher addresses his son and warns about wealth, greed, sexual unfaithfulness, and drunkenness.

The strong disciplinary sayings and images are retained.

## Proverbs 23:1

When dining with a ruler, carefully consider what is before you, 📖

## Proverbs 23:2

and put a knife to your throat if you have a great appetite. 📖

**Help with this image:** A strong picture of restraining your appetite.

It is not an editorial instruction to injure yourself.

## Proverbs 23:3

Do not crave his rich foods. 📖

That food is deceptive.

## Proverbs 23:4

Do not wear yourself out becoming rich. 📖

Be wise enough to hold yourself back.

## Proverbs 23:5

You glance at wealth, and it disappears. 📖

It makes wings and flies like an eagle into the sky.

## Proverbs 23:6

Do not eat a stingy man’s bread. 📖

Do not crave his rich foods.

## Proverbs 23:7

He keeps count inside himself, calculating the cost. 📖

He tells you, “Eat and drink,” but his heart is not with you.

## Proverbs 23:8

You will vomit the little you ate and waste your pleasant words. 📖

## Proverbs 23:9

Do not speak to a fool. 📖

He will despise your words’ wisdom.

## Proverbs 23:10

Do not move an ancient boundary stone or invade fatherless children’s fields, 📖

## Proverbs 23:11

because their Redeemer is strong. 📖

He will defend their case against you.

**Help with Redeemer:** Someone who rescues people and defends their rights.

## Proverbs 23:12

Apply your heart to instruction and your ears to knowledge’s words. 📖

## Proverbs 23:13

Do not withhold discipline from a child. 📖

Though you strike him with a rod, he will not die.

**Help with rod:** A stick used for punishment.

This difficult disciplinary saying is retained for editorial review, not presented as a medical safety guarantee.

## Proverbs 23:14

Strike him with a rod, and you will rescue his soul from Sheol. 📖

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Proverbs 23:15

My son, if your heart is wise, my own heart will rejoice. 📖

## Proverbs 23:16

My innermost being will rejoice when your lips speak rightly. 📖

## Proverbs 23:17

Do not envy sinners in your heart. 📖

Always continue in fear of the LORD.

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 23:18

There is surely a future. 📖

Your hope will not be cut off.

## Proverbs 23:19

My son, listen and be wise. 📖

Guide your heart on the right course.

## Proverbs 23:20

Do not join people drinking too much wine or stuffing themselves with meat. 📖

## Proverbs 23:21

A drunkard and glutton will become poor. 📖

Drowsiness will clothe them in rags.

## Proverbs 23:22

Listen to your father who gave you life. 📖

Do not despise your mother when she is old.

## Proverbs 23:23

Buy truth and never sell it. 📖

Buy wisdom, instruction, and understanding.

## Proverbs 23:24

A righteous man’s father will greatly rejoice. 📖

The father of a wise son delights in him.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Proverbs 23:25

May your father and mother be glad! 📖

May the woman who bore you rejoice!

## Proverbs 23:26

My son, give me your heart. 📖

Let your eyes delight in my ways.

## Proverbs 23:27

A prostitute is a deep pit. 📖

An adulteress is a narrow well.

**Help with prostitute and adulteress:** Someone who has sex for payment, and a woman sexually unfaithful to her husband.

**Help with this picture:** The pit and narrow well picture deadly danger from sexual unfaithfulness.

## Proverbs 23:28

She waits like a robber. 📖

She increases unfaithful men’s numbers.

## Proverbs 23:29

Who has misery and sorrow? 📖

Who has arguments and complaints?

Who has needless wounds and bloodshot eyes?

## Proverbs 23:30

People lingering over wine and going to taste mixed drinks. 📖

## Proverbs 23:31

Do not gaze at red wine sparkling in the cup and going down smoothly. 📖

## Proverbs 23:32

In the end it bites like a snake and stings like a viper. 📖

**Help with viper:** A poisonous snake.

## Proverbs 23:33

Your eyes will see strange things. 📖

Your mind will speak twisted things.

## Proverbs 23:34

You will be like someone sleeping on the high seas or lying atop a mast. 📖

**Help with mast:** A tall pole on a ship.

## Proverbs 23:35

“They struck me, but I feel no pain! 📖

They beat me, but I did not know!

When can I wake to find another drink?”
