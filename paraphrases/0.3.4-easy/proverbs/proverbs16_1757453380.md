# Proverbs 16 — Plans, motives, and the LORD’s purpose

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and everyday conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 16:1

People make plans in their hearts. 📖

But the tongue’s answer comes from the LORD.

## Proverbs 16:2

All a man’s ways look pure to him. 📖

But the LORD weighs his motives.

## Proverbs 16:3

Put your work in the LORD’s hands. 📖

Your plans will be achieved.

## Proverbs 16:4

The LORD made everything for His purpose, even wicked people for the day of disaster. 📖

## Proverbs 16:5

The LORD detests everyone proud in heart. 📖

Be certain: they will not escape punishment.

## Proverbs 16:6

Faithful love and faithfulness atone for sin. 📖

Fear of the LORD turns people from evil.

**Help with atone:** Deal with guilt so sin is forgiven.

Fearing the LORD means respecting Him and taking His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 16:7

When a man’s ways please the LORD, He makes even his enemies live peacefully with him. 📖

## Proverbs 16:8

Better a little with righteousness than great gain with injustice. 📖

## Proverbs 16:9

A man’s heart plans his course. 📖

But the LORD decides his steps.

## Proverbs 16:10

A divine verdict is on a king’s lips. 📖

His mouth must not betray justice.

**Help with divine verdict:** A judgment associated with God’s authority.

## Proverbs 16:11

Honest scales and balances come from the LORD. 📖

Every weight in the bag concerns Him.

## Proverbs 16:12

Wicked behavior is detestable for kings. 📖

Righteousness establishes a throne.

## Proverbs 16:13

Righteous lips delight a king. 📖

Someone speaking honestly is loved.

## Proverbs 16:14

A king’s anger is a messenger of death. 📖

But a wise man calms it.

## Proverbs 16:15

When a king’s face brightens, there is life. 📖

His favor is like a spring raincloud.

## Proverbs 16:16

Gaining wisdom is much better than gold! 📖

Understanding is more desirable than silver.

## Proverbs 16:17

Upright people’s highway leads away from evil. 📖

A person guarding his way protects his life.

## Proverbs 16:18

Pride goes before destruction. 📖

A haughty spirit goes before a fall.

**Help with haughty:** Proud and looking down on others.

## Proverbs 16:19

Better a lowly spirit among humble people than sharing plunder with proud people. 📖

## Proverbs 16:20

Listening to instruction brings success. 📖

Blessed is the person trusting the LORD.

## Proverbs 16:21

Wise-hearted people are called discerning. 📖

Pleasant speech helps instruction.

## Proverbs 16:22

Understanding is a fountain of life for its owner. 📖

But fools’ discipline is foolishness.

## Proverbs 16:23

A wise man’s heart teaches his mouth. 📖

It makes his words more persuasive.

## Proverbs 16:24

Pleasant words are a honeycomb. 📖

They are sweet to the soul and healing to bones.

**Help with this picture:** Honey pictures the sweetness and benefit of pleasant speech.

## Proverbs 16:25

A way may seem right to a man. 📖

But its end is death’s way.

## Proverbs 16:26

A worker’s appetite works for him. 📖

His hunger drives him onward.

## Proverbs 16:27

A worthless man digs up evil. 📖

His speech is like burning fire.

## Proverbs 16:28

A twisted man spreads conflict. 📖

A gossip separates close friends.

## Proverbs 16:29

A violent man entices his neighbor. 📖

He leads him onto a bad path.

## Proverbs 16:30

A person winking his eye plans twisted things. 📖

A person pressing his lips together is intent on evil.

## Proverbs 16:31

Gray hair is a glorious crown. 📖

It is gained along righteousness’s path.

**Help with this picture:** The crown pictures honor associated with a life of righteousness.

## Proverbs 16:32

Someone slow to anger is better than a warrior. 📖

Controlling your temper is greater than capturing a city.

## Proverbs 16:33

The lot is cast into the lap. 📖

But every decision comes from the LORD.

**Help with lot:** An object thrown or drawn to make a choice.
