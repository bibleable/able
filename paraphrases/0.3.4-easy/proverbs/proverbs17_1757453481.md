# Proverbs 17 — Peace, friendship, and careful words

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and everyday conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 17:1

Better a dry mouthful with quiet than a house full of feasting with conflict. 📖

## Proverbs 17:2

A wise servant will rule a disgraceful son. 📖

He will share the inheritance as one of the brothers.

## Proverbs 17:3

A crucible tests silver, and a furnace tests gold. 📖

But the LORD tests hearts.

**Help with crucible:** A container for heating metal.

## Proverbs 17:4

A wicked man listens to evil lips. 📖

A liar listens to destructive speech.

## Proverbs 17:5

Mocking poor people insults their Maker. 📖

Delighting in disaster will not go unpunished.

## Proverbs 17:6

Grandchildren are old people’s crown. 📖

A son’s glory is his father.

## Proverbs 17:7

Eloquent words do not fit a fool. 📖

Lying lips fit a ruler even less!

**Help with eloquent:** Skillful and impressive in speech.

## Proverbs 17:8

A bribe seems like a charm to its giver. 📖

Wherever he turns, he succeeds.

**Help with charm and bribe:** An object believed to bring success, and a gift used to make someone act dishonestly.

The saying describes the giver’s perspective.

## Proverbs 17:9

Covering an offense promotes love. 📖

But bringing it up separates friends.

## Proverbs 17:10

A rebuke reaches an understanding man deeper than a hundred lashes reach a fool. 📖

**Help with rebuke and lashes:** Strong correction, and blows from a whip.

## Proverbs 17:11

An evil man seeks only rebellion. 📖

A cruel messenger will be sent against him.

## Proverbs 17:12

Better to meet a bear robbed of her cubs than a fool in his foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 17:13

If someone repays good with evil, evil will never leave his house. 📖

## Proverbs 17:14

Starting an argument releases a flood. 📖

So leave a dispute before it breaks out.

## Proverbs 17:15

The LORD detests both clearing guilty people and condemning righteous people. 📖

## Proverbs 17:16

Why does a fool have money in his hand when he has no intention of buying wisdom? 📖

## Proverbs 17:17

A friend loves at all times. 📖

A brother is born for trouble.

## Proverbs 17:18

A man lacking judgment shakes hands in pledge and guarantees his neighbor’s debt. 📖

## Proverbs 17:19

A person loving wrongdoing loves conflict. 📖

A person building his gate high invites destruction.

**About the gate:** The image is retained; its precise sense remains for editorial review.

## Proverbs 17:20

A twisted heart finds no good. 📖

A deceitful tongue falls into trouble.

## Proverbs 17:21

A man fathering a fool brings himself grief. 📖

A fool’s father has no joy.

## Proverbs 17:22

A joyful heart is good medicine. 📖

But a broken spirit dries the bones.

**Help with this picture:** The medicine and dry-bone images contrast joy’s benefit with deep discouragement.

## Proverbs 17:23

A wicked man takes a secret bribe to twist justice. 📖

## Proverbs 17:24

Wisdom is an understanding person’s focus. 📖

But a fool’s eyes wander to the earth’s ends.

## Proverbs 17:25

A foolish son grieves his father. 📖

He brings bitterness to the mother who bore him.

## Proverbs 17:26

It is not good to punish innocent people or whip a noble for honesty. 📖

## Proverbs 17:27

A knowledgeable man holds his words back. 📖

A man with understanding keeps a calm spirit.

## Proverbs 17:28

Even a fool is thought wise when silent. 📖

Holding his tongue makes him seem discerning.
