# Proverbs 4 — Guard your heart and follow wisdom

**About this chapter**

A father teaches his sons and recalls his own father’s instruction.

Wisdom is pictured as a woman whose guidance protects.

## Proverbs 4:1

My sons, listen to a father’s instruction. 📖

Pay attention and gain understanding.

## Proverbs 4:2

I give you sound teaching. 📖

Do not abandon my instruction.

## Proverbs 4:3

When I was my father’s son, tender and my mother’s only child, 📖

## Proverbs 4:4

he taught me, saying, “Let your heart hold my words. 📖

Keep my commands, and you will live.

## Proverbs 4:5

Get wisdom and understanding. 📖

Do not forget my words or turn away.

## Proverbs 4:6

Do not abandon wisdom, and she will protect you. 📖

Love her, and she will guard you.

## Proverbs 4:7

Wisdom is supreme, so get wisdom. 📖

Whatever else you gain, gain understanding.

**Help with supreme:** Highest in importance.

## Proverbs 4:8

Value her highly, and she will lift you up. 📖

Embrace her, and she will honor you.

## Proverbs 4:9

She will put a graceful garland on your head. 📖

She will give you a beautiful crown.”

**Help with garland and crown:** Head decorations picturing honor.

## Proverbs 4:10

My son, listen and accept my words. 📖

Then your life’s years will be many.

## Proverbs 4:11

I will guide you in wisdom’s way. 📖

I will lead you on straight paths.

## Proverbs 4:12

When you walk, nothing will block your steps. 📖

When you run, you will not stumble.

## Proverbs 4:13

Hold instruction tightly. 📖

Do not let it go.

Guard it, because it is your life.

## Proverbs 4:14

Do not step onto wicked people’s path. 📖

Do not walk in evildoers’ way.

## Proverbs 4:15

Avoid it. 📖

Do not travel on it.

Turn away and pass it by.

## Proverbs 4:16

They cannot sleep unless they do evil. 📖

They lose sleep until they make someone fall.

## Proverbs 4:17

They eat wickedness’s bread and drink violence’s wine. 📖

**Help with bread and wine:** A picture of evil filling their lives like daily food and drink.

## Proverbs 4:18

Righteous people’s path is like dawn’s first light. 📖

It shines brighter and brighter until noon.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Proverbs 4:19

But wicked people’s way is like deepest darkness. 📖

They do not know what makes them stumble.

## Proverbs 4:20

My son, pay attention to my words. 📖

Listen closely to my sayings.

## Proverbs 4:21

Do not lose sight of them. 📖

Keep them inside your heart.

## Proverbs 4:22

They are life for people who find them. 📖

They are health for the whole body.

## Proverbs 4:23

Guard your heart with great care. 📖

Life’s springs flow from it.

**Help with heart and springs:** Your inner thoughts and desires shape the course of life.

## Proverbs 4:24

Put deceit away from your mouth. 📖

Keep twisted speech from your lips.

## Proverbs 4:25

Let your eyes look forward. 📖

Fix your gaze straight ahead.

## Proverbs 4:26

Make a level path for your feet. 📖

Then all your ways will be secure.

## Proverbs 4:27

Do not turn right or left. 📖

Turn your feet away from evil.
