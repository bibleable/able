# Proverbs 3 — Trust the LORD with your whole heart

**About this chapter**

A teacher speaks to a son about wisdom, trust, and treating neighbours well.

Wisdom means understanding and choosing what is right.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Proverbs 3:1

My son, do not forget my teaching. 📖

Keep my commands in your heart.

## Proverbs 3:2

They will add days and years to your life. 📖

They will bring you peace.

## Proverbs 3:3

Never let faithful love or faithfulness leave you. 📖

Tie them around your neck.

Write them on your heart’s tablet.

**Help with this picture:** Remember them and make them part of your inner life.

## Proverbs 3:4

Then God and people will favour and respect you. 📖

## Proverbs 3:5

Trust the LORD with your whole heart. 📖

Do not lean on your own understanding.

**Help with lean on:** Depend on something for support.

## Proverbs 3:6

Acknowledge him in all your ways. 📖

He will make your paths straight.

**Help with acknowledge:** Recognize God and take account of Him in everything you do.

Straight paths picture a life guided rightly.

## Proverbs 3:7

Do not think you are wise in your own eyes. 📖

Fear the LORD and turn from evil.

## Proverbs 3:8

This will bring healing to your body and new strength to your bones. 📖

## Proverbs 3:9

Honour the LORD with your wealth. 📖

Give him the first part of every harvest.

## Proverbs 3:10

Then your barns will be full. 📖

Your wine containers will overflow with new wine.

## Proverbs 3:11

My son, do not reject the LORD's correction. 📖

Do not hate his rebuke.

**Help with a word:** Rebuke means words that show someone has done wrong.

## Proverbs 3:12

The LORD corrects those he loves. 📖

He is like a father correcting a son he delights in.

## Proverbs 3:13

God blesses the person who finds wisdom and gains understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 3:14

Wisdom brings more profit than silver. 📖

It is worth more than fine gold.

## Proverbs 3:15

Wisdom is worth more than rubies. 📖

Nothing you want can compare with it.

**Help with a word:** Rubies are valuable red stones.

## Proverbs 3:16

Long life is in wisdom’s right hand. 📖

Riches and honour are in her left hand.

**Help with this picture:** Wisdom is pictured as a woman bringing gifts.

## Proverbs 3:17

Wisdom's ways are pleasant. 📖

All its paths are peaceful.

## Proverbs 3:18

Wisdom is a tree of life to those who hold it. 📖

Those who keep it are blessed.

**Help with this picture:** A tree of life pictures wisdom giving life and well-being.

## Proverbs 3:19

The LORD founded the earth by wisdom. 📖

He established the heavens by understanding.

## Proverbs 3:20

By his knowledge, the deep waters broke open. 📖

The clouds dripped with dew.

**Help with a word:** Dew is small drops of water that form on cool surfaces.

## Proverbs 3:21

My son, keep good judgment and understanding. 📖

Do not lose sight of them.

## Proverbs 3:22

They will be life to your soul and an ornament around your neck. 📖

**Help with ornament:** Something beautiful worn for decoration, here picturing honor.

## Proverbs 3:23

Then you will travel safely. 📖

Your foot will not stumble.

## Proverbs 3:24

When you lie down, you will not fear. 📖

You will sleep peacefully.

## Proverbs 3:25

Do not fear sudden danger or the destruction coming on evil people. 📖

## Proverbs 3:26

The LORD will give you confidence. 📖

He will keep your foot out of the trap.

## Proverbs 3:27

Do not hold back good from someone who deserves it when you have power to help. 📖

## Proverbs 3:28

Do not tell your neighbour, “Come back tomorrow, and I will provide,” when you already have the means. 📖

## Proverbs 3:29

Do not plan harm against a neighbour who trusts you and lives beside you. 📖

## Proverbs 3:30

Do not accuse someone without reason when they have done you no harm. 📖

## Proverbs 3:31

Do not envy violent people or choose their ways. 📖

## Proverbs 3:32

The LORD detests twisted people. 📖

But he is a friend to upright people.

**Help with upright:** Honest and right.

## Proverbs 3:33

The LORD's curse rests on evil people's homes. 📖

He blesses the homes of people who do right.

## Proverbs 3:34

He mocks those who mock. 📖

He gives kindness to humble people.

## Proverbs 3:35

Wise people receive honour. 📖

Foolish people are shown up in shame.
