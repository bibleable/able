# Proverbs 28 — Justice, honest living, and mercy

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and daily conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 28:1

Wicked people flee though no one chases. 📖

But righteous people are bold as lions.

## Proverbs 28:2

A rebellious land has many rulers. 📖

But a man with knowledge and understanding keeps order.

## Proverbs 28:3

A destitute leader oppressing poor people is like driving rain leaving no food. 📖

**Help with destitute:** Having nothing.

## Proverbs 28:4

People abandoning the law praise wicked people. 📖

People keeping the law resist them.

## Proverbs 28:5

Evil men do not understand justice. 📖

But people seeking the LORD understand fully.

## Proverbs 28:6

Better a poor man living honestly than a rich man with twisted ways. 📖

## Proverbs 28:7

A discerning son keeps the law. 📖

But a gluttons’ companion disgraces his father.

**Help with gluttons:** People who eat far too much.

## Proverbs 28:8

Anyone growing wealth through interest and usury stores it for someone kind to poor people. 📖

**Help with interest and usury:** Charges added to loans, including excessive charges.

## Proverbs 28:9

Anyone refusing to hear the law has a detestable prayer too. 📖

## Proverbs 28:10

Leading upright people to evil’s path brings a person into his own pit. 📖

But blameless people inherit good.

## Proverbs 28:11

A rich man thinks himself wise. 📖

But a poor man with understanding sees through him.

## Proverbs 28:12

Righteous people’s victory brings great glory. 📖

But people hide when wicked people rise.

## Proverbs 28:13

Hiding sins prevents prosperity. 📖

But confessing and abandoning them brings mercy.

## Proverbs 28:14

Blessed is the man always reverent. 📖

But hardening your heart brings trouble.

**Help with reverent:** Showing deep respect.

**Help with this picture:** Hardening the heart pictures stubborn refusal to listen.

## Proverbs 28:15

A wicked ruler over helpless people is like a roaring lion or charging bear. 📖

## Proverbs 28:16

A leader lacking judgment is a great oppressor. 📖

But hating dishonest profit lengthens a man’s life.

## Proverbs 28:17

A man burdened with bloodguilt flees to the Pit. 📖

Let no one support him.

**Help with bloodguilt and Pit:** Guilt for causing death, and the grave or place of the dead.

## Proverbs 28:18

Living with integrity keeps a person safe. 📖

But twisted ways bring a sudden fall.

## Proverbs 28:19

Working your land brings plenty of food. 📖

But chasing fantasies brings plenty of poverty.

## Proverbs 28:20

A faithful man has many blessings. 📖

But eagerness for riches will not escape punishment.

## Proverbs 28:21

Favoring one side unfairly is not good. 📖

Yet a man will do wrong for a piece of bread.

## Proverbs 28:22

A stingy man hurries after wealth. 📖

He does not know poverty waits for him.

## Proverbs 28:23

Correcting someone later brings more favor than flattering with your tongue. 📖

## Proverbs 28:24

Anyone robbing his parents and saying, “It is not wrong,” is a destroyer’s companion. 📖

## Proverbs 28:25

A greedy man stirs conflict. 📖

But anyone trusting the LORD will prosper.

## Proverbs 28:26

Someone trusting himself is a fool. 📖

But walking wisely keeps a person safe.

## Proverbs 28:27

Giving to poor people prevents need. 📖

But hiding your eyes brings many curses.

## Proverbs 28:28

People hide when wicked people gain power. 📖

But righteous people flourish when the wicked perish.
