# Proverbs 30 — Agur’s words and numbered sayings

**About this chapter**

Agur speaks about his limits and God’s word.

The chapter includes a prayer and numbered groups of observations.

## Proverbs 30:1

These are Agur son of Jakeh’s words, the burden he declared to Ithiel. 📖

He said, “God, I am weary and worn out.

## Proverbs 30:2

I am surely the most ignorant of men. 📖

I lack a man’s understanding.

## Proverbs 30:3

I have not learned wisdom. 📖

I have no knowledge of the Holy One.

**Help with Holy One:** God.

## Proverbs 30:4

Who went up to heaven and came down? 📖

Who gathered wind in His hands?

Who wrapped waters in His cloak?

Who established all earth’s ends?

What is His name and His Son’s name?

Surely you know!

## Proverbs 30:5

Every word of God is flawless. 📖

He shields people taking refuge in Him.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Proverbs 30:6

Do not add to His words. 📖

Otherwise He will rebuke you and prove you a liar.

**Help with rebuke:** Strongly correct wrongdoing.

## Proverbs 30:7

I ask You for two things. 📖

Do not refuse me before I die:

## Proverbs 30:8

Keep lies and deceitful words far from me. 📖

Give me neither poverty nor riches.

Feed me my share of bread.

## Proverbs 30:9

Otherwise I may have too much, deny You, and ask, ‘Who is the LORD?’ 📖

Or I may become poor and steal, dishonoring my God’s name.

## Proverbs 30:10

Do not slander a servant to his master. 📖

He may curse you, and you will bear guilt.

## Proverbs 30:11

There is a generation cursing fathers and not blessing mothers. 📖

## Proverbs 30:12

There is a generation pure in its own eyes but unwashed from its filth. 📖

## Proverbs 30:13

There is a generation with such proud eyes and arrogant glances! 📖

## Proverbs 30:14

There is a generation with sword teeth and knife jaws. 📖

It devours oppressed people from earth and needy people from humanity.

## Proverbs 30:15

The leech has two daughters named Give and Give. 📖

Three things are never satisfied.

Four never say, ‘Enough!’

**Help with this picture:** A leech is a creature that sucks blood.

The named daughters picture its constant demand for more.

## Proverbs 30:16

Sheol, the barren womb, land never satisfied with water, and fire never saying, ‘Enough!’ 📖

**Help with Sheol and barren womb:** The place of the dead, and a womb unable to bear children.

## Proverbs 30:17

May valley ravens pluck out the eye mocking a father and scorning obedience to a mother. 📖

May young vultures devour it.

**Help with this picture:** This is a severe judgment wish against contempt for parents.

The violent image is retained.

## Proverbs 30:18

Three things are too wonderful for me. 📖

Four I cannot understand:

## Proverbs 30:19

An eagle’s way in the sky and a snake’s way on rock. 📖

A ship’s way at sea and a man’s way with a maiden.

## Proverbs 30:20

An adulteress acts like this: she eats, wipes her mouth, and says, ‘I did nothing wrong.’ 📖

**Help with adulteress:** A woman sexually unfaithful to her husband.

## Proverbs 30:21

Three things make the earth tremble. 📖

Under four it cannot hold up:

## Proverbs 30:22

a servant becoming king, a fool filled with food, 📖

## Proverbs 30:23

an unloved woman marrying, and a female servant replacing her mistress. 📖

## Proverbs 30:24

Four earthly things are small but exceedingly wise: 📖

## Proverbs 30:25

ants have little strength but store food in summer; 📖

## Proverbs 30:26

rock badgers have little power but make homes in rocks; 📖

## Proverbs 30:27

locusts have no king but all advance in formation; 📖

## Proverbs 30:28

and a lizard can be caught by hand but is found in kings’ palaces. 📖

## Proverbs 30:29

Three things have a stately stride. 📖

Four have an impressive walk:

**Help with this picture:** Stately means impressive and dignified in movement.

## Proverbs 30:30

a lion, mighty among beasts, retreating before nothing; 📖

## Proverbs 30:31

a strutting rooster, a male goat, and a king with his army around him. 📖

## Proverbs 30:32

If you foolishly raised yourself high or planned evil, put your hand over your mouth. 📖

## Proverbs 30:33

Churning milk makes butter. 📖

Twisting a nose draws blood.

In the same way, stirring anger produces conflict.”
