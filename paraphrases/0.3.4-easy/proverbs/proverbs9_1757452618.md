# Proverbs 9 — Wisdom and Folly invite people

**About this chapter**

Wisdom and Folly are pictured as women offering rival invitations.

The chapter contrasts their outcomes.

## Proverbs 9:1

Wisdom built her house. 📖

She carved its seven pillars.

## Proverbs 9:2

She prepared her meat, mixed her wine, and set her table. 📖

## Proverbs 9:3

She sent her female servants. 📖

She calls from the city’s heights.

## Proverbs 9:4

She tells people without good judgment, “Everyone inexperienced, turn in here! 📖

## Proverbs 9:5

Come, eat my bread and drink the wine I mixed. 📖

## Proverbs 9:6

Leave foolishness behind, and you will live. 📖

Walk in understanding’s way.”

## Proverbs 9:7

Correcting a mocker brings shame on yourself. 📖

Rebuking a wicked man stains you.

## Proverbs 9:8

Do not rebuke a mocker, or he will hate you. 📖

Rebuke a wise man, and he will love you.

**Help with rebuke:** Strongly correct wrongdoing.

## Proverbs 9:9

Instruct a wise man, and he becomes wiser. 📖

Teach a righteous man, and he learns more.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Proverbs 9:10

Fear of the LORD is wisdom’s beginning. 📖

Knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

The Holy One is God.

## Proverbs 9:11

Wisdom will multiply your days and add years to your life. 📖

## Proverbs 9:12

If you are wise, you benefit from your wisdom. 📖

But if you mock, you alone bear the results.

## Proverbs 9:13

The woman named Folly is loud. 📖

She is naive and knows nothing.

**Help with Folly and naive:** Foolishness, pictured as a woman, and easily misled.

## Proverbs 9:14

She sits at her house’s door on a seat high in the city, 📖

## Proverbs 9:15

calling to passersby who make their paths straight. 📖

## Proverbs 9:16

She tells people without good judgment, “Everyone inexperienced, turn in here! 📖

## Proverbs 9:17

Stolen water is sweet. 📖

Bread eaten secretly tastes good!”

## Proverbs 9:18

But they do not know the dead are there. 📖

Her guests are in Sheol’s depths.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.
