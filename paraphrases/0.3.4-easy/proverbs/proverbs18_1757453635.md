# Proverbs 18 — Listening, speaking, and friendship

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and everyday conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 18:1

A person separating himself pursues selfish desires. 📖

He rebels against all sound judgment.

## Proverbs 18:2

A fool does not delight in understanding. 📖

He only wants to voice his opinions.

## Proverbs 18:3

Contempt comes with a wicked man. 📖

Disgrace accompanies shame.

## Proverbs 18:4

A man’s words are deep waters. 📖

Wisdom’s fountain is a bubbling stream.

**Help with this picture:** Words are compared with deep water, and wisdom with a refreshing flowing stream.

## Proverbs 18:5

Favoring wicked people is not good. 📖

Neither is denying innocent people justice.

## Proverbs 18:6

A fool’s lips bring conflict. 📖

His mouth invites a beating.

## Proverbs 18:7

A fool’s mouth ruins him. 📖

His lips trap his soul.

## Proverbs 18:8

Gossip’s words are like delicious mouthfuls. 📖

They go deep inside a person.

**Help with this picture:** Delicious mouthfuls picture how eagerly gossip is taken in and kept inside.

## Proverbs 18:9

A person lazy in his work is brother to a destroyer. 📖

## Proverbs 18:10

The LORD’s name is a strong tower. 📖

Righteous people run there and are safe.

**Help with this picture:** The tower pictures strong protection.

## Proverbs 18:11

A rich man’s wealth is his fortified city. 📖

In his imagination, it is a high wall.

**Help with this picture:** The rich man imagines his wealth gives him protection like high walls.

## Proverbs 18:12

A man’s heart is proud before his downfall. 📖

But humility comes before honor.

## Proverbs 18:13

Answering before hearing is foolish and disgraceful. 📖

## Proverbs 18:14

A man’s spirit can endure sickness. 📖

But who can survive a broken spirit?

## Proverbs 18:15

An understanding heart gains knowledge. 📖

A wise ear seeks it.

## Proverbs 18:16

A man’s gift opens doors and brings him before great men. 📖

## Proverbs 18:17

The first person presenting a case seems right, until another comes and questions him. 📖

## Proverbs 18:18

Casting lots ends arguments and separates strong opponents. 📖

**Help with lots:** Objects drawn or thrown to make a choice.

## Proverbs 18:19

An offended brother is harder to win than a fortified city. 📖

Disputes are like a castle’s bars.

**Help with this picture:** Walls and bars picture the difficulty of overcoming offense and conflict.

## Proverbs 18:20

A man’s belly fills with his mouth’s fruit. 📖

His lips’ harvest satisfies him.

**Help with fruit and harvest:** The results of speech.

## Proverbs 18:21

Life and death are in the tongue’s power. 📖

People who love it will eat its fruit.

**Help with this picture:** Fruit pictures the results of the speech a person loves.

## Proverbs 18:22

A man finding a wife finds a good thing. 📖

He gains the LORD’s favor.

## Proverbs 18:23

A poor man pleads for mercy. 📖

But a rich man answers harshly.

## Proverbs 18:24

A man with many companions may be ruined. 📖

But a friend can stay closer than a brother.
