# Proverbs 27 — Friendship, self-knowledge, and care for flocks

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and daily conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 27:1

Do not boast about tomorrow. 📖

You do not know what a day brings.

## Proverbs 27:2

Let someone else praise you, not your own mouth. 📖

Let a stranger praise you, not your own lips.

## Proverbs 27:3

A stone is heavy, and sand is a burden. 📖

But annoyance from a fool weighs more than both.

## Proverbs 27:4

Wrath is cruel, and anger is a flood. 📖

But who can stand against jealousy?

## Proverbs 27:5

Better open correction than hidden love. 📖

## Proverbs 27:6

A friend’s wounds are faithful. 📖

But an enemy’s kisses deceive.

**Help with this picture:** The contrast pictures a friend’s faithful correction and an enemy’s false affection.

## Proverbs 27:7

A full soul hates honey. 📖

But to a hungry soul, anything bitter is sweet.

## Proverbs 27:8

A man wandering from home is like a bird straying from its nest. 📖

## Proverbs 27:9

Oil and incense gladden the heart. 📖

A friend’s advice is sweetness to the soul.

## Proverbs 27:10

Do not abandon your friend or your father’s friend. 📖

Do not go to your brother’s house in disaster.

Better a nearby neighbor than a distant brother.

## Proverbs 27:11

My son, be wise and gladden my heart. 📖

Then I can answer someone mocking me.

## Proverbs 27:12

Careful people see danger and hide. 📖

Inexperienced people keep going and pay the penalty.

## Proverbs 27:13

Take the clothing of someone guaranteeing a stranger’s debt. 📖

Take security if it is for a foreigner.

## Proverbs 27:14

A loud blessing to your neighbor early in the morning will be counted as a curse. 📖

## Proverbs 27:15

A constantly dripping rainy day and an argumentative woman are alike. 📖

## Proverbs 27:16

Restraining her is like holding back wind or grasping oil with your right hand. 📖

## Proverbs 27:17

As iron sharpens iron, one man sharpens another. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Sharpening iron pictures people helping improve one another.

## Proverbs 27:18

Caring for a fig tree brings its fruit to eat. 📖

Looking after your master brings honor.

## Proverbs 27:19

Water reflects a face. 📖

In the same way, the heart reflects the true person.

**Help with this picture:** The heart shows a person’s character as water shows a face.

## Proverbs 27:20

Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied. 📖

Human eyes are never satisfied either.

**Help with Sheol and Abaddon:** Names connected with death and destruction.

## Proverbs 27:21

A crucible tests silver, and a furnace tests gold. 📖

But praise tests a man.

**Help with crucible:** A container for heating metal.

## Proverbs 27:22

Even grinding a fool like grain with mortar and pestle will not remove his foolishness. 📖

**Help with mortar and pestle:** A bowl and tool used to crush things.

## Proverbs 27:23

Know your flocks’ condition well. 📖

Pay close attention to your herds.

## Proverbs 27:24

Riches do not last forever. 📖

A crown does not last through every generation.

## Proverbs 27:25

Hay is removed, new growth appears, and grass is gathered from the hills. 📖

## Proverbs 27:26

Then lambs provide clothing and goats provide a field’s price. 📖

## Proverbs 27:27

You will have plenty of goats’ milk to feed you and your household. 📖

It will nourish your female servants.
