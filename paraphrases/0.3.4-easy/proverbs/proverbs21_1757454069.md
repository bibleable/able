# Proverbs 21 — Justice and the LORD’s victory

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wise conduct, speech, work, and justice.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 21:1

A king’s heart is a waterway in the LORD’s hand. 📖

He directs it wherever He pleases.

**Help with this picture:** The waterway pictures God directing the king’s heart.

## Proverbs 21:2

Every man’s ways seem right to him. 📖

But the LORD weighs the heart.

## Proverbs 21:3

The LORD desires righteousness and justice more than sacrifice. 📖

## Proverbs 21:4

Proud eyes and a proud heart guide wicked people. 📖

They are sin.

## Proverbs 21:5

Hardworking people’s plans bring plenty. 📖

Haste leads just as surely to poverty.

## Proverbs 21:6

Wealth made through lies is a vanishing mist and a deadly pursuit. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Mist pictures dishonest riches disappearing.

## Proverbs 21:7

Wicked people’s violence sweeps them away because they refuse justice. 📖

## Proverbs 21:8

A guilty man’s way is crooked. 📖

But innocent people’s conduct is upright.

## Proverbs 21:9

Better to live on a roof’s corner than share a house with an argumentative wife. 📖

## Proverbs 21:10

A wicked man’s soul craves evil. 📖

His neighbor finds no favor in his eyes.

## Proverbs 21:11

When a mocker is punished, inexperienced people gain wisdom. 📖

When a wise man is taught, he gains knowledge.

## Proverbs 21:12

The Righteous One considers wicked people’s house. 📖

He brings them to ruin.

## Proverbs 21:13

Anyone shutting his ears to poor people’s cries will also cry and receive no answer. 📖

## Proverbs 21:14

A secret gift soothes anger. 📖

A hidden bribe calms fierce wrath.

## Proverbs 21:15

Justice brings righteous people joy. 📖

But it terrifies people doing evil.

## Proverbs 21:16

A man leaving understanding’s path will rest in the gathering of the dead. 📖

## Proverbs 21:17

Loving pleasure makes a person poor. 📖

Loving wine and oil will never make him rich.

## Proverbs 21:18

Wicked people become a ransom for righteous people. 📖

Unfaithful people become a ransom for upright people.

**Help with ransom:** A payment to set someone free.

The exact exchange pictured here remains for editorial review.

## Proverbs 21:19

Better to live in the desert than with an argumentative, bad-tempered wife. 📖

## Proverbs 21:20

Wise people’s homes contain precious treasures and oil. 📖

But a foolish man consumes them.

## Proverbs 21:21

Anyone pursuing righteousness and faithful love finds life, righteousness, and honor. 📖

## Proverbs 21:22

A wise man climbs the mighty people’s city. 📖

He pulls down the stronghold they trust.

## Proverbs 21:23

Guarding your mouth and tongue keeps your soul from distress. 📖

## Proverbs 21:24

The proud, arrogant man is named Mocker. 📖

He acts with excessive pride.

## Proverbs 21:25

A lazy person’s craving kills him because his hands refuse to work. 📖

## Proverbs 21:26

All day he longs for more. 📖

But righteous people give without holding back.

## Proverbs 21:27

Wicked people’s sacrifice is detestable. 📖

How much more when offered with evil intent!

## Proverbs 21:28

A lying witness will perish. 📖

But a man listening to truth will speak forever.

## Proverbs 21:29

A wicked man hardens his face. 📖

But an upright man makes his way secure.

## Proverbs 21:30

No wisdom, understanding, or advice can defeat the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 21:31

A horse is prepared for battle day. 📖

But victory belongs to the LORD.
