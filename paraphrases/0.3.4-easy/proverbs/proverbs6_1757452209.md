# Proverbs 6 — Debt, work, deceit, and adultery

**About this chapter**

A parent warns about debt guarantees, idleness, destructive conduct, and adultery.

The chapter includes strong descriptions of punishment.

## Proverbs 6:1

My son, suppose you guaranteed your neighbor’s debt or shook hands to pledge yourself for a stranger. 📖

## Proverbs 6:2

Suppose your own words have trapped you. 📖

Your mouth’s words have caught you.

## Proverbs 6:3

My son, do this to free yourself, because you are in your neighbor’s hands. 📖

Go, humble yourself, and press your appeal to your neighbor.

## Proverbs 6:4

Do not let your eyes sleep or your eyelids doze. 📖

## Proverbs 6:5

Free yourself like a gazelle escaping a hunter’s hand, like a bird escaping a bird-catcher’s trap. 📖

**Help with gazelle:** A swift animal like a small deer.

## Proverbs 6:6

You who avoid work, go to the ant. 📖

Watch its ways and become wise.

## Proverbs 6:7

It has no commander, supervisor, or ruler, 📖

## Proverbs 6:8

yet it prepares supplies in summer and gathers food at harvest. 📖

## Proverbs 6:9

How long will you lie there, you who avoid work? 📖

When will you get up?

## Proverbs 6:10

A little sleep, a little dozing, a little folding your hands to rest, 📖

## Proverbs 6:11

and poverty will come like a robber. 📖

Need will come like a bandit.

## Proverbs 6:12

A worthless, wicked man goes about speaking twisted words, 📖

## Proverbs 6:13

winking his eyes, signaling with his feet, and pointing his fingers. 📖

## Proverbs 6:14

His heart holds deceit, and he plans evil. 📖

He constantly spreads conflict.

## Proverbs 6:15

So disaster will suddenly come on him. 📖

In an instant he will be broken beyond recovery.

## Proverbs 6:16

The LORD hates six things. 📖

Seven things are detestable to Him:

## Proverbs 6:17

proud eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood, 📖

## Proverbs 6:18

a heart planning wicked schemes, feet hurrying to evil, 📖

## Proverbs 6:19

a false witness telling lies, and someone stirring conflict among brothers. 📖

## Proverbs 6:20

My son, keep your father’s command. 📖

Do not abandon your mother’s teaching.

## Proverbs 6:21

Always bind them on your heart. 📖

Tie them around your neck.

## Proverbs 6:22

When you walk, they will guide you. 📖

When you lie down, they will watch over you.

When you wake, they will speak to you.

## Proverbs 6:23

This command is a lamp, and this teaching is light. 📖

Discipline’s corrections are the way to life,

## Proverbs 6:24

keeping you from the evil woman and the adulteress’s smooth tongue. 📖

**Help with adulteress:** A woman having sex with someone other than her husband.

## Proverbs 6:25

Do not desire her beauty in your heart. 📖

Do not let her capture you with her eyes.

## Proverbs 6:26

A prostitute’s cost is poverty. 📖

An adulteress hunts your very life.

**Help with prostitute:** Someone who has sex for payment.

## Proverbs 6:27

Can a man hold fire close without burning his clothes? 📖

## Proverbs 6:28

Can a man walk on hot coals without burning his feet? 📖

## Proverbs 6:29

The same is true of a man sleeping with another man’s wife. 📖

No one touching her will escape punishment.

## Proverbs 6:30

People do not despise a thief who steals to satisfy his hunger. 📖

## Proverbs 6:31

But if caught, he must repay seven times as much. 📖

He must give up all his house’s wealth.

## Proverbs 6:32

A man committing adultery lacks judgment. 📖

Whoever does it destroys himself.

## Proverbs 6:33

He will suffer wounds and dishonor. 📖

His disgrace will never be erased.

## Proverbs 6:34

Jealousy makes a husband furious. 📖

He will show no mercy on the day of revenge.

## Proverbs 6:35

No ransom will satisfy him. 📖

Even lavish gifts will not persuade him.

**Help with ransom:** A payment to set someone free.
