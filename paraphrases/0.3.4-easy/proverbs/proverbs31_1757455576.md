# Proverbs 31 — Wise leadership and a capable wife

**About this chapter**

The chapter begins with advice from King Lemuel’s mother.

It then praises a wife of noble character.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Proverbs 31:1

These are King Lemuel's words. 📖

His mother taught him this important message.

## Proverbs 31:2

What shall I say, my son? 📖

You are the son I bore, the son connected with my vows.

**Help with a word:** Vows are serious promises.

## Proverbs 31:3

Do not waste your strength on women. 📖

Do not spend your energy on those who ruin kings.

## Proverbs 31:4

Lemuel, kings should not drink wine. 📖

Rulers should not crave strong drink.

## Proverbs 31:5

They might drink, forget the law, and deny justice to people being oppressed. 📖

## Proverbs 31:6

Give strong drink to someone who is dying. 📖

Give wine to someone in bitter distress.

## Proverbs 31:7

Let them drink and forget their poverty. 📖

Let them stop remembering their misery.

## Proverbs 31:8

Speak for people who cannot speak for themselves. 📖

Defend everyone whose rights have been taken away.

## Proverbs 31:9

Speak out and judge fairly. 📖

Defend poor people and those in need.

## Proverbs 31:10

Who can find a wife of noble character? 📖

She is worth much more than rubies.

**Help with words:** Noble character means strong, good character.

Rubies are valuable red stones.

## Proverbs 31:11

Her husband trusts her completely. 📖

He lacks nothing valuable.

## Proverbs 31:12

She does him good rather than harm throughout her life. 📖

## Proverbs 31:13

She chooses wool and flax. 📖

She works eagerly with her hands.

**Help with a word:** Flax is a plant used to make linen cloth.

## Proverbs 31:14

She is like trading ships. 📖

She brings her food from far away.

## Proverbs 31:15

She gets up before daylight. 📖

She provides food for her household and portions for her female servants.

## Proverbs 31:16

She studies a field and buys it. 📖

With her earnings, she plants a vineyard.

**Help with a word:** A vineyard is a place where grapes grow.

## Proverbs 31:17

She prepares herself to work strongly. 📖

Her arms are strong.

## Proverbs 31:18

She sees that her business earns a good return. 📖

Her lamp does not go out at night.

## Proverbs 31:19

She holds the distaff and spindle in her hands. 📖

**Help with these words:** These are tools for turning fibres into thread.

## Proverbs 31:20

She opens her arms to poor people. 📖

She reaches out to those who need help.

## Proverbs 31:21

She does not fear for her household when it snows. 📖

They all have scarlet clothing.

**Help with a word:** Scarlet is a rich red colour.

## Proverbs 31:22

She makes bed coverings. 📖

She wears fine linen and purple clothes.

## Proverbs 31:23

Her husband is respected at the city gates. 📖

He sits with the land's elders.

**Help with this setting:** Leaders met at city gates to discuss and judge public matters.

## Proverbs 31:24

She makes linen clothes and sells them. 📖

She supplies belts and sashes to traders.

## Proverbs 31:25

Strength and honour are like her clothing. 📖

She can laugh at the days ahead without fear.

## Proverbs 31:26

She speaks wisely. 📖

Her teaching is faithful and kind.

## Proverbs 31:27

She watches over her household's work. 📖

She does not live lazily.

## Proverbs 31:28

Her children stand and call her blessed. 📖

Her husband praises her too.

## Proverbs 31:29

He says, “Many women have done well. 📖

You are better than them all!”

## Proverbs 31:30

Charm can deceive. 📖

Beauty fades.

A woman who fears the LORD deserves praise.

## Proverbs 31:31

Give her the reward of her work. 📖

Let her deeds bring her praise at the city gates.
