# Proverbs 7 — A warning about seductive words

**About this chapter**

A parent tells his sons about a young man drawn into adultery.

The woman’s invitation is quoted as part of the warning.

## Proverbs 7:1

My son, keep my words. 📖

Treasure my commands within you.

## Proverbs 7:2

Keep my commands and live. 📖

Guard my teaching like the apple of your eye.

**Help with apple of your eye:** The eye’s delicate center, protected carefully.

## Proverbs 7:3

Tie them to your fingers. 📖

Write them on your heart’s tablet.

**Help with tablet:** A surface used for writing.

These pictures describe keeping the teaching close and remembering it.

## Proverbs 7:4

Tell wisdom, “You are my sister.” 📖

Call understanding your relative,

## Proverbs 7:5

so they keep you from the adulteress and the stranger’s seductive words. 📖

**Help with adulteress:** A woman having sex with someone other than her husband.

## Proverbs 7:6

I looked through the lattice at my house’s window. 📖

**Help with lattice:** A frame with crossing strips and openings.

## Proverbs 7:7

Among inexperienced people and youths, I noticed a young man without good judgment. 📖

## Proverbs 7:8

He crossed the street near her corner. 📖

He strolled toward her house,

## Proverbs 7:9

at twilight, as daylight faded into night’s darkness. 📖

## Proverbs 7:10

A woman came to meet him. 📖

She wore a prostitute’s clothing and had a cunning heart.

**Help with prostitute and cunning:** Someone who has sex for payment, and skilled at using tricks.

## Proverbs 7:11

She is loud and defiant. 📖

Her feet do not stay at home.

## Proverbs 7:12

She waits in streets and squares, hiding at every corner. 📖

## Proverbs 7:13

She grabs and kisses him. 📖

Without shame, she says:

## Proverbs 7:14

“I have made my peace offerings. 📖

Today I kept my vows.

**Help with peace offerings and vows:** Sacrifices connected with fellowship and thanks, and serious promises.

## Proverbs 7:15

So I came to meet you. 📖

I looked for you and found you.

## Proverbs 7:16

I covered my bed with coverings and colored linen from Egypt. 📖

## Proverbs 7:17

I perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. 📖

**Help with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon:** Materials used for pleasant smells.

## Proverbs 7:18

Come, let us fill ourselves with love until morning. 📖

Let us enjoy loving caresses!

## Proverbs 7:19

My husband is not home. 📖

He has gone on a long journey.

## Proverbs 7:20

He took a bag of money. 📖

He will not return until the full moon.”

## Proverbs 7:21

Her persuasive words draw him in. 📖

Her flattering lips lure him.

## Proverbs 7:22

He follows her without thinking, like an ox going to be killed. 📖

He is like a deer jumping into a trap,

## Proverbs 7:23

until an arrow pierces his liver. 📖

He is like a bird flying into a snare, not knowing it will cost his life.

**Help with liver and snare:** An organ inside the body, and a trap.

## Proverbs 7:24

Now, my sons, listen to me. 📖

Pay attention to my words.

## Proverbs 7:25

Do not let your heart turn to her ways. 📖

Do not wander onto her paths.

## Proverbs 7:26

She has brought many down to death. 📖

Many are the people she killed.

## Proverbs 7:27

Her house is the road to Sheol. 📖

It goes down to death’s rooms.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.
