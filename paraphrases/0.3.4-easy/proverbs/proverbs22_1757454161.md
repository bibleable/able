# Proverbs 22 — A good name and the sayings of the wise

**About this chapter**

The chapter includes sayings about wisdom and injustice.

From verse 17, a teacher introduces instruction addressed to the learner.

## Proverbs 22:1

A good name is more desirable than great riches. 📖

Favor is better than silver and gold.

## Proverbs 22:2

Rich and poor people share this: the LORD made them all. 📖

## Proverbs 22:3

Careful people see danger and hide. 📖

Inexperienced people keep going and suffer the results.

## Proverbs 22:4

Humility and fear of the LORD bring wealth, honor, and life. 📖

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 22:5

Thorns and traps lie on twisted people’s path. 📖

Anyone guarding his soul stays far from them.

## Proverbs 22:6

Train a child in the way he should go. 📖

When old, he will not leave it.

## Proverbs 22:7

Rich people rule poor people. 📖

A borrower is a lender’s slave.

## Proverbs 22:8

Anyone planting injustice harvests disaster. 📖

The rod of his fury will be destroyed.

**Help with this picture:** Sowing and harvesting picture conduct and consequences.

The rod pictures the angry person’s power to harm.

## Proverbs 22:9

A generous man will be blessed because he shares bread with poor people. 📖

## Proverbs 22:10

Drive out the mocker, and conflict leaves. 📖

Quarrels and insults will stop.

## Proverbs 22:11

Anyone loving a pure heart and gracious lips will have the king as a friend. 📖

## Proverbs 22:12

The LORD’s eyes guard knowledge. 📖

But He defeats unfaithful people’s words.

## Proverbs 22:13

A person avoiding work says, “A lion is outside! 📖

I will be killed in the streets!”

## Proverbs 22:14

An adulteress’s mouth is a deep pit. 📖

Anyone under the LORD’s wrath falls into it.

**Help with adulteress and wrath:** A woman sexually unfaithful to her husband, and fierce anger.

## Proverbs 22:15

Foolishness is bound to a child’s heart. 📖

But discipline’s rod drives it far away.

**Help with rod:** A stick used for punishment.

## Proverbs 22:16

Oppressing poor people to enrich yourself or giving rich people gifts will surely lead to poverty. 📖

## Proverbs 22:17

Listen closely to wise people’s words. 📖

Apply your mind to my knowledge.

## Proverbs 22:18

Keeping them within you is pleasing. 📖

Let them always be on your lips.

## Proverbs 22:19

I teach you today so your trust may be in the LORD. 📖

Yes, I teach you!

## Proverbs 22:20

Have I not written thirty sayings about advice and knowledge for you, 📖

## Proverbs 22:21

showing you true, reliable words so you can answer those who sent you well? 📖

## Proverbs 22:22

Do not rob a poor man because he is poor. 📖

Do not crush suffering people at the gate,

## Proverbs 22:23

because the LORD will defend their case and rob those who rob them. 📖

## Proverbs 22:24

Do not befriend an angry man. 📖

Do not keep company with someone hot-tempered,

## Proverbs 22:25

or you may learn his ways and trap yourself. 📖

## Proverbs 22:26

Do not pledge yourself as a guarantee for debts. 📖

## Proverbs 22:27

If you have nothing to pay, why should your bed be taken from under you? 📖

## Proverbs 22:28

Do not move an ancient boundary stone your fathers placed. 📖

**Help with boundary stone:** A marker showing where someone’s land ends.

## Proverbs 22:29

Do you see a skilled worker? 📖

He will stand before kings, not obscure men.

**Help with obscure:** Little known and without high rank.
