# Proverbs 29 — Wise rule and faithful correction

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and daily conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 29:1

A man remaining stubborn after much correction will suddenly be broken beyond recovery. 📖

## Proverbs 29:2

When righteous people flourish, people rejoice. 📖

But people groan when wicked people rule.

## Proverbs 29:3

Loving wisdom gives a man’s father joy. 📖

But a prostitutes’ companion wastes his wealth.

## Proverbs 29:4

Justice makes a king’s land stable. 📖

But demanding tribute demolishes it.

## Proverbs 29:5

A man flattering his neighbor spreads a net for his feet. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The net pictures a trap made through insincere praise.

## Proverbs 29:6

An evil man’s own sin traps him. 📖

But a righteous man sings and rejoices.

## Proverbs 29:7

Righteous people consider poor people’s cause. 📖

But wicked people have no regard for it.

## Proverbs 29:8

Mockers inflame a city. 📖

But wise people turn anger away.

## Proverbs 29:9

A wise man taking a fool to court meets raging and laughter without resolution. 📖

## Proverbs 29:10

Bloodthirsty men hate a blameless man. 📖

But upright people care for his life.

## Proverbs 29:11

A fool releases all his anger. 📖

But a wise man holds it back.

## Proverbs 29:12

If a ruler listens to lies, all his officials will be wicked. 📖

## Proverbs 29:13

A poor man and an oppressor share this: the LORD gives light to both their eyes. 📖

## Proverbs 29:14

A king judging poor people fairly will have his throne established forever. 📖

## Proverbs 29:15

A rod of correction gives wisdom. 📖

But a child left to himself disgraces his mother.

**Help with rod:** A stick used for punishment.

## Proverbs 29:16

When wicked people thrive, rebellion grows. 📖

But righteous people will see their downfall.

## Proverbs 29:17

Discipline your son, and he will give you rest. 📖

He will delight your soul.

## Proverbs 29:18

Without vision, people throw off restraint. 📖

But anyone keeping the Law is blessed.

**Help with vision:** Revelation or guidance from God in this setting.

## Proverbs 29:19

Words alone cannot correct a servant. 📖

He understands, but will not respond.

## Proverbs 29:20

Do you see someone speaking hastily? 📖

A fool has more hope than he does.

## Proverbs 29:21

A servant pampered from youth will bring grief in the end. 📖

## Proverbs 29:22

An angry man stirs conflict. 📖

A hot-tempered man commits many offenses.

## Proverbs 29:23

Pride brings a man low. 📖

But a humble spirit gains honor.

## Proverbs 29:24

A thief’s partner hates his own soul. 📖

He hears the oath but does not testify.

**Help with oath:** A solemn call connected with giving evidence; its precise setting needs editorial review.

## Proverbs 29:25

Fear of people is a trap. 📖

But trusting the LORD sets a person safely on high.

## Proverbs 29:26

Many people seek a ruler’s favor. 📖

But a man receives justice from the LORD.

## Proverbs 29:27

Righteous people detest an unjust man. 📖

Wicked people detest someone whose way is upright.
