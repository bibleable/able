# Proverbs 1 — Listen to wisdom and refuse evil

**About this chapter**

The book begins with its purpose and a parent’s warning.

From verse 20, wisdom is pictured as a woman speaking in public.

## Proverbs 1:1

These are the proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel. 📖

**Help with proverbs:** Short sayings that teach wisdom.

## Proverbs 1:2

They help people gain wisdom and discipline, and understand words of insight. 📖

**Help with discipline and insight:** Correction that teaches, and understanding what things mean.

## Proverbs 1:3

They give instruction in wise living, righteousness, justice, and fairness. 📖

**Help with righteousness:** Doing what is right before God.

## Proverbs 1:4

They teach careful judgment to people with little experience. 📖

They give young people knowledge and the ability to choose wisely.

## Proverbs 1:5

Let wise people listen and learn more. 📖

Let people with understanding gain wise advice,

## Proverbs 1:6

by understanding proverbs, parables, wise sayings, and riddles. 📖

**Help with parables and riddles:** Teaching through stories or comparisons, and puzzling questions.

## Proverbs 1:7

Fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge. 📖

But fools despise wisdom and discipline.

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 1:8

My son, listen to your father’s instruction. 📖

Do not abandon your mother’s teaching.

## Proverbs 1:9

They are a graceful garland on your head and a pendant around your neck. 📖

**Help with garland and pendant:** A decorative head wreath and an ornament worn around the neck.

They picture the honor that instruction brings.

## Proverbs 1:10

My son, if sinners try to draw you in, do not give in. 📖

## Proverbs 1:11

They may say, “Come with us. 📖

Let us hide and wait to shed blood.

Let us attack innocent people without cause.

## Proverbs 1:12

Let us swallow them alive like Sheol. 📖

Let us swallow them whole, like those going down to the Pit.

**Help with Sheol and the Pit:** Names connected with the place of the dead.

## Proverbs 1:13

We will find all kinds of valuable goods. 📖

We will fill our houses with stolen things.

## Proverbs 1:14

Join your lot to ours. 📖

Let us all share one purse.”

**Help with lot and purse:** Join their group and share their money.

## Proverbs 1:15

My son, do not walk their road. 📖

Do not set foot on their path.

## Proverbs 1:16

Their feet run toward evil. 📖

They hurry to shed blood.

## Proverbs 1:17

Spreading a net where any bird can see it is useless! 📖

## Proverbs 1:18

But these people wait to shed their own blood. 📖

They secretly attack their own lives.

## Proverbs 1:19

This is the end of everyone greedy for unjust gain. 📖

That gain takes the life of the person who has it.

## Proverbs 1:20

Wisdom calls in the street. 📖

She raises her voice in the square.

## Proverbs 1:21

She cries at the busy crossroads. 📖

She speaks at the city gates:

## Proverbs 1:22

“How long will you inexperienced people love your simple ways? 📖

How long will mockers enjoy scorn and fools hate knowledge?

## Proverbs 1:23

If you had repented at my rebuke, I would have poured my spirit on you. 📖

I would have made my words known to you.

**Help with repented and rebuke:** Turned from wrongdoing when strongly corrected.

## Proverbs 1:24

But you refused my call. 📖

No one took my outstretched hand.

## Proverbs 1:25

You ignored all my advice. 📖

You wanted none of my correction.

## Proverbs 1:26

So I will mock your disaster. 📖

I will sneer when terror strikes you,

## Proverbs 1:27

when your fear comes like a storm and your destruction like a whirlwind. 📖

Distress and anguish will overwhelm you.

**Help with whirlwind:** A powerful, swirling wind.

## Proverbs 1:28

Then they will call me, but I will not answer. 📖

They will search eagerly for me, but not find me.

## Proverbs 1:29

They hated knowledge. 📖

They chose not to fear the LORD.

## Proverbs 1:30

They accepted none of my advice. 📖

They despised all my correction.

## Proverbs 1:31

So they will eat the fruit of their own way. 📖

They will be filled with their own plans.

**Help with fruit:** The results of what they have done.

## Proverbs 1:32

The wandering ways of inexperienced people will kill them. 📖

Fools’ careless confidence will destroy them.

## Proverbs 1:33

But anyone who listens to me will live safely. 📖

They will be secure from fear of evil.”
