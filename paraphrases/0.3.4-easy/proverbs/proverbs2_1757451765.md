# Proverbs 2 — The protection wisdom gives

**About this chapter**

A parent urges his son to seek wisdom.

The results follow the conditions stated in verses 1–4.

## Proverbs 2:1

My son, if you accept my words and store my commands within you, 📖

## Proverbs 2:2

if you turn your ear to wisdom and your heart to understanding, 📖

## Proverbs 2:3

if you truly call for insight and raise your voice for understanding, 📖

## Proverbs 2:4

if you seek it like silver and search for it like hidden treasure, 📖

## Proverbs 2:5

you will understand fear of the LORD. 📖

You will discover knowledge of God.

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 2:6

The LORD gives wisdom. 📖

Knowledge and understanding come from His mouth.

## Proverbs 2:7

He stores sound wisdom for upright people. 📖

He is a shield for people living with integrity,

**Help with upright and integrity:** Honest and right, and holding to what is right.

## Proverbs 2:8

guarding the paths of justice and protecting His saints’ way. 📖

**Help with saints:** People set apart for God.

## Proverbs 2:9

Then you will understand righteousness, justice, and fairness. 📖

You will understand every good path.

**Help with righteousness:** Doing what is right before God.

## Proverbs 2:10

Wisdom will enter your heart. 📖

Knowledge will delight your soul.

## Proverbs 2:11

Careful judgment will watch over you. 📖

Understanding will guard you,

## Proverbs 2:12

rescuing you from evil’s way and the man who speaks twisted things, 📖

## Proverbs 2:13

from people who leave straight paths to walk in darkness, 📖

## Proverbs 2:14

from people who enjoy evil and rejoice in its twisted ways. 📖

## Proverbs 2:15

Their paths are crooked. 📖

Their ways are dishonest.

## Proverbs 2:16

Wisdom will rescue you from the forbidden woman, the stranger who speaks to seduce you, 📖

**Help with forbidden woman:** A woman outside the marriage relationship who draws someone into sexual unfaithfulness.

## Proverbs 2:17

who abandons her youthful partner and forgets her God’s covenant. 📖

**Help with covenant:** A serious agreement.

## Proverbs 2:18

Her house sinks toward death. 📖

Her tracks lead to departed spirits.

## Proverbs 2:19

No one going to her returns or finds the paths of life. 📖

## Proverbs 2:20

So you will follow good people’s ways. 📖

You will keep righteous people’s paths.

## Proverbs 2:21

Upright people will live in the land. 📖

Blameless people will remain there.

## Proverbs 2:22

But wicked people will be cut off from the land. 📖

Unfaithful people will be uprooted.
