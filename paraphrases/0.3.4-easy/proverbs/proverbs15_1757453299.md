# Proverbs 15 — Gentle words and a teachable heart

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and everyday conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 15:1

A gentle answer turns anger away. 📖

But a harsh word stirs anger.

## Proverbs 15:2

A wise tongue presents knowledge well. 📖

But a fool’s mouth pours out foolishness.

## Proverbs 15:3

The LORD’s eyes are everywhere. 📖

He watches evil and good.

## Proverbs 15:4

A soothing tongue is a tree of life. 📖

But a twisted tongue crushes the spirit.

**Help with this picture:** A tree of life pictures the help that soothing speech gives.

## Proverbs 15:5

A fool rejects his father’s discipline. 📖

But someone listening to correction has good judgment.

## Proverbs 15:6

Righteous people’s house has great treasure. 📖

But wicked people’s income is trouble.

## Proverbs 15:7

Wise lips spread knowledge. 📖

But fools’ hearts do not.

## Proverbs 15:8

The LORD detests wicked people’s sacrifice. 📖

But upright people’s prayer delights Him.

## Proverbs 15:9

The LORD detests wicked people’s way. 📖

But He loves people pursuing righteousness.

## Proverbs 15:10

Harsh discipline waits for someone leaving the path. 📖

Anyone hating correction will die.

## Proverbs 15:11

Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD. 📖

How much more human hearts!

**Help with Sheol and Abaddon:** Names connected with death and destruction.

## Proverbs 15:12

A mocker does not love correction. 📖

He will not consult wise people.

## Proverbs 15:13

A joyful heart makes a cheerful face. 📖

But heart sorrow crushes the spirit.

## Proverbs 15:14

A discerning heart seeks knowledge. 📖

But a fool’s mouth feeds on foolishness.

## Proverbs 15:15

All oppressed people’s days are bad. 📖

But a cheerful heart has a continual feast.

**Help with this picture:** The continuing feast pictures a cheerful heart’s enjoyment.

## Proverbs 15:16

Better a little with fear of the LORD than great treasure with turmoil. 📖

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 15:17

Better vegetables with love than a fattened ox with hatred. 📖

## Proverbs 15:18

A hot-tempered man stirs conflict. 📖

But someone slow to anger calms disputes.

## Proverbs 15:19

The way of a person avoiding work is like a thorn hedge. 📖

But upright people’s path is a highway.

**Help with this picture:** Thorns picture obstruction.

The highway pictures a clear way forward.

## Proverbs 15:20

A wise son gives his father joy. 📖

But a foolish man despises his mother.

## Proverbs 15:21

Foolishness delights someone lacking judgment. 📖

But a man with understanding walks straight.

## Proverbs 15:22

Plans fail without advice. 📖

But many advisers make them succeed.

## Proverbs 15:23

A fitting answer brings joy. 📖

A word at the right time is so good!

## Proverbs 15:24

A wise person’s life path leads upward, away from Sheol below. 📖

## Proverbs 15:25

The LORD tears down proud people’s house. 📖

But He protects a widow’s boundaries.

**Help with widow:** A woman whose husband has died.

## Proverbs 15:26

The LORD detests wicked thoughts. 📖

But pure people’s words please Him.

## Proverbs 15:27

Greed for unjust gain troubles a household. 📖

But someone hating bribes will live.

**Help with bribes:** Gifts used to make someone act dishonestly.

## Proverbs 15:28

A righteous heart considers how to answer. 📖

But a wicked mouth blurts out evil.

## Proverbs 15:29

The LORD is far from wicked people. 📖

But He hears righteous people’s prayer.

## Proverbs 15:30

Bright eyes cheer the heart. 📖

Good news nourishes the bones.

**Help with this picture:** The verse uses eyes and bones in pictures of encouragement reaching the whole person.

## Proverbs 15:31

Anyone listening to life-giving correction will live among wise people. 📖

## Proverbs 15:32

Anyone ignoring discipline despises himself. 📖

But someone listening to correction gains understanding.

## Proverbs 15:33

Fear of the LORD teaches wisdom. 📖

Humility comes before honor.
