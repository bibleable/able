# Proverbs 14 — Wise judgment and care for others

**About this chapter**

These sayings contrast wise and foolish choices, including speech, work, justice, and leadership.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 14:1

A wise woman builds her house. 📖

But a foolish woman tears it down with her own hands.

**Help with this picture:** Building or destroying a house can also picture strengthening or ruining a household.

## Proverbs 14:2

A person living uprightly fears the LORD. 📖

But someone using dishonest ways despises Him.

**Help with fears the LORD:** Respects God and takes His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 14:3

A fool’s proud speech brings a rod to his back. 📖

But wise people’s lips protect them.

## Proverbs 14:4

Without oxen, the manger is empty. 📖

But an ox’s strength brings a plentiful harvest.

**Help with manger:** A container for animal food.

## Proverbs 14:5

An honest witness does not deceive. 📖

But a dishonest witness pours out lies.

## Proverbs 14:6

A mocker seeks wisdom and finds none. 📖

But knowledge comes easily to someone with understanding.

## Proverbs 14:7

Stay away from a foolish man. 📖

You will learn nothing from his speech.

## Proverbs 14:8

A careful person’s wisdom helps him understand his way. 📖

But fools’ foolishness deceives them.

## Proverbs 14:9

Fools mock making amends. 📖

But goodwill is among upright people.

**Help with making amends:** Putting a wrong right.

## Proverbs 14:10

The heart knows its own bitterness. 📖

No stranger shares its joy.

## Proverbs 14:11

Wicked people’s house will be destroyed. 📖

But upright people’s tent will flourish.

## Proverbs 14:12

A way may seem right to a man. 📖

But it ends on death’s way.

## Proverbs 14:13

Even in laughter, a heart may ache. 📖

Joy may end in sorrow.

## Proverbs 14:14

A heart turned back receives its own ways in full. 📖

But a good man is rewarded for his ways.

## Proverbs 14:15

An inexperienced man believes every word. 📖

But a careful man watches his steps.

## Proverbs 14:16

A wise man fears and turns from evil. 📖

But a fool is careless and reckless.

## Proverbs 14:17

A quick-tempered man acts foolishly. 📖

A dishonest man is hated.

## Proverbs 14:18

Inexperienced people inherit foolishness. 📖

But careful people are crowned with knowledge.

## Proverbs 14:19

Evil people bow before good people. 📖

Wicked people bow at righteous people’s gates.

## Proverbs 14:20

A poor man is hated even by his neighbor. 📖

But many people love a rich man.

## Proverbs 14:21

Anyone despising his neighbor sins. 📖

But anyone kind to poor people is blessed.

## Proverbs 14:22

Do people planning evil not go astray? 📖

But people planning good find faithful love and faithfulness.

## Proverbs 14:23

All work brings profit. 📖

But mere talk leads only to poverty.

## Proverbs 14:24

Wise people’s wealth is their crown. 📖

But fools’ effort is foolishness.

**Help with this picture:** The crown pictures honor.

## Proverbs 14:25

A truthful witness saves lives. 📖

But someone telling lies is deceitful.

## Proverbs 14:26

A person fearing the LORD has secure confidence. 📖

His children have a place of refuge.

**Help with refuge:** Safety and protection.

## Proverbs 14:27

Fear of the LORD is a fountain of life. 📖

It turns a man from death’s traps.

## Proverbs 14:28

A large population is a king’s splendor. 📖

But few subjects bring a prince to ruin.

## Proverbs 14:29

A patient man has great understanding. 📖

But a quick-tempered man promotes foolishness.

## Proverbs 14:30

A calm heart gives life to the body. 📖

But envy rots the bones.

**Help with this picture:** The verse contrasts calm that supports life with envy that harms a person deeply.

## Proverbs 14:31

Anyone treating poor people cruelly insults their Maker. 📖

But kindness to needy people honors Him.

## Proverbs 14:32

A wicked man’s own sin throws him down. 📖

But a righteous man has refuge even in death.

## Proverbs 14:33

Wisdom rests in a discerning heart. 📖

Even among fools she is known.

## Proverbs 14:34

Righteousness lifts a nation high. 📖

But sin disgraces any people.

## Proverbs 14:35

A king delights in a wise servant. 📖

But his anger falls on someone bringing shame.
