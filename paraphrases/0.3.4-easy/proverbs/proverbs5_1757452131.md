# Proverbs 5 — Faithfulness in marriage

**About this chapter**

A parent warns his sons against sexual unfaithfulness.

He uses water, sweetness, and animal pictures while speaking directly about married love.

## Proverbs 5:1

My son, pay attention to my wisdom. 📖

Listen closely to my understanding,

## Proverbs 5:2

so you can keep good judgment and your lips can preserve knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 5:3

The forbidden woman’s lips drip honey. 📖

Her speech is smoother than oil.

**Help with forbidden woman:** A woman outside the marriage relationship who draws someone into sexual unfaithfulness.

## Proverbs 5:4

But her end is bitter as wormwood and sharp as a sword with two edges. 📖

**Help with wormwood:** A very bitter plant.

## Proverbs 5:5

Her feet go down to death. 📖

Her steps lead straight to Sheol.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Proverbs 5:6

She does not consider life’s path. 📖

She does not know her ways are unsteady.

## Proverbs 5:7

Now, my sons, listen to me. 📖

Do not turn away from my words.

## Proverbs 5:8

Keep your path far from her. 📖

Do not approach her house’s door,

## Proverbs 5:9

or you may give your strength to others and your years to someone cruel. 📖

## Proverbs 5:10

Strangers may feast on your wealth. 📖

Your hard work may make a foreigner’s house rich.

## Proverbs 5:11

At life’s end you will groan when your flesh and body are used up. 📖

## Proverbs 5:12

You will say, “How I hated discipline! 📖

My heart despised correction!

## Proverbs 5:13

I did not listen to my teachers or pay attention to my guides. 📖

## Proverbs 5:14

I am close to complete ruin in the middle of the whole gathering.” 📖

## Proverbs 5:15

Drink water from your own cistern and running water from your own well. 📖

**Help with cistern:** A container for storing water.

The water images in these verses picture faithfulness in marriage.

## Proverbs 5:16

Why should your springs flow in the streets and your streams in public squares? 📖

## Proverbs 5:17

Let them belong to you alone. 📖

Never share them with strangers.

## Proverbs 5:18

May your fountain be blessed. 📖

May you rejoice in the wife of your youth.

## Proverbs 5:19

She is a loving doe and graceful fawn. 📖

May her breasts always satisfy you.

May her love captivate you forever.

**Help with doe and fawn:** A female deer and a young deer.

Captivate means hold someone’s love and attention.

## Proverbs 5:20

My son, why be captivated by an adulteress? 📖

Why embrace a stranger’s breasts?

**Help with adulteress:** A woman having sex with someone other than her husband.

## Proverbs 5:21

A man’s ways are before the LORD’s eyes. 📖

The LORD examines all his paths.

## Proverbs 5:22

A wicked man’s sins trap him. 📖

His sin’s ropes tangle around him.

## Proverbs 5:23

He dies from lack of discipline. 📖

His own great foolishness leads him astray.
