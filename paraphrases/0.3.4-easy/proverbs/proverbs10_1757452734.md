# Proverbs 10 — Sayings about wisdom, work, and speech

**About this chapter**

These sayings contrast wise and foolish conduct, and righteous and wicked lives.

Righteous means right before God.

## Proverbs 10:1

Solomon’s proverbs. 📖

A wise son gives his father joy.

A foolish son gives his mother grief.

## Proverbs 10:2

Treasure gained wrongly is no help. 📖

But righteousness rescues from death.

## Proverbs 10:3

The LORD does not let righteous people go hungry. 📖

But He denies wicked people’s cravings.

## Proverbs 10:4

Idle hands make a person poor. 📖

Hardworking hands bring wealth.

## Proverbs 10:5

A son gathering in summer is wise. 📖

A son sleeping at harvest brings disgrace.

## Proverbs 10:6

Blessings rest on righteous people’s heads. 📖

But wicked people’s mouths hide violence.

## Proverbs 10:7

Remembering righteous people is a blessing. 📖

But wicked people’s names will rot.

**Help with this picture:** A rotting name pictures a reputation ending in disgrace.

## Proverbs 10:8

A wise heart accepts commands. 📖

Foolish lips come to ruin.

## Proverbs 10:9

A person living honestly walks securely. 📖

But someone twisting his ways will be found out.

## Proverbs 10:10

A person winking his eye causes grief. 📖

Foolish lips come to ruin.

**Help with winking:** Here, a signal connected with deceit.

## Proverbs 10:11

A righteous person’s mouth is a fountain of life. 📖

But wicked people’s mouths hide violence.

**Help with this picture:** A fountain of life pictures speech that gives help and life.

## Proverbs 10:12

Hatred stirs up conflict. 📖

But love covers all offenses.

## Proverbs 10:13

People with understanding speak wisdom. 📖

But a rod is for the back of someone without judgment.

**Help with rod:** A stick used for punishment.

## Proverbs 10:14

Wise people store knowledge. 📖

A fool’s mouth invites destruction.

## Proverbs 10:15

A rich man’s wealth is his fortified city. 📖

But poverty ruins poor people.

**Help with fortified city:** A city with defenses, picturing protection.

## Proverbs 10:16

Righteous people’s work leads to life. 📖

But wicked people’s gain brings punishment.

## Proverbs 10:17

Anyone listening to instruction follows life’s path. 📖

But anyone ignoring correction goes astray.

## Proverbs 10:18

A person hiding hatred has lying lips. 📖

Anyone spreading harmful lies is a fool.

## Proverbs 10:19

When words are many, sin cannot be avoided. 📖

But someone holding back his lips is wise.

## Proverbs 10:20

A righteous person’s tongue is choice silver. 📖

But a wicked heart has little worth.

**Help with this picture:** The silver picture describes the value of good speech.

## Proverbs 10:21

Righteous lips feed many people. 📖

But fools die from lack of judgment.

**Help with this picture:** Feeding many people pictures helping them through good speech.

## Proverbs 10:22

The LORD’s blessing brings wealth. 📖

He adds no sorrow to it.

## Proverbs 10:23

A fool enjoys shameful conduct. 📖

But a man with understanding has wisdom.

## Proverbs 10:24

What a wicked man fears will overtake him. 📖

But righteous people’s desire will be granted.

## Proverbs 10:25

After a whirlwind passes, wicked people are gone. 📖

But righteous people are secure forever.

**Help with whirlwind:** A powerful, swirling wind.

## Proverbs 10:26

A person avoiding work is like vinegar on teeth or smoke in eyes to those sending him. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The comparison pictures the irritation and disappointment caused by an unreliable worker.

## Proverbs 10:27

Fear of the LORD lengthens life. 📖

But wicked people’s years are cut short.

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 10:28

Righteous people’s hope is joy. 📖

But wicked people’s expectations will perish.

## Proverbs 10:29

The LORD’s way is a refuge for upright people. 📖

But destruction waits for evildoers.

**Help with refuge and upright:** Safety, and honest and right.

## Proverbs 10:30

Righteous people will never be shaken. 📖

But wicked people will not live in the land.

## Proverbs 10:31

Righteous people’s mouths bring wisdom. 📖

But a twisted tongue will be cut out.

## Proverbs 10:32

Righteous lips know what is fitting. 📖

But wicked mouths speak twisted things.
