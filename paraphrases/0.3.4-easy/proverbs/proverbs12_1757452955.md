# Proverbs 12 — Words, work, and the way of life

**About this chapter**

These sayings contrast wisdom and foolishness in everyday conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 12:1

Anyone loving discipline loves knowledge. 📖

But someone hating correction is stupid.

**About this word:** The saying rebukes rejecting correction; it is not a diagnosis of disability.

## Proverbs 12:2

A good man gains the LORD’s favor. 📖

But the LORD condemns someone planning evil.

## Proverbs 12:3

Wickedness cannot make a person secure. 📖

But righteous people cannot be uprooted.

**Help with this picture:** Being rooted pictures lasting stability.

## Proverbs 12:4

A wife of noble character is her husband’s crown. 📖

But a wife bringing shame is like decay in his bones.

**Help with this picture:** The crown pictures honor.

Decay in bones pictures deep harm.

## Proverbs 12:5

Righteous people’s plans are just. 📖

But wicked people’s advice leads to deceit.

## Proverbs 12:6

Wicked people’s words wait to shed blood. 📖

But upright people’s speech rescues them.

**Help with this picture:** Words are pictured as dangerous attackers or rescuers.

## Proverbs 12:7

Wicked people are overthrown and perish. 📖

But righteous people’s house stands.

## Proverbs 12:8

A man is praised for his wisdom. 📖

But a twisted mind is despised.

## Proverbs 12:9

Better to have little respect but a servant than to think yourself important but have no food. 📖

## Proverbs 12:10

A righteous man cares about his animal’s life. 📖

But even wicked people’s tender mercies are cruel.

## Proverbs 12:11

A person working his land has plenty of food. 📖

But someone chasing fantasies lacks judgment.

## Proverbs 12:12

Wicked people desire evil men’s stolen goods. 📖

But righteous people’s root grows well.

**Help with this picture:** The root pictures the source and stability of a righteous life.

## Proverbs 12:13

A wicked man’s rebellious speech traps him. 📖

But a righteous man escapes trouble.

## Proverbs 12:14

The fruit of a man’s speech fills him with good things. 📖

His hands’ work returns to him.

**Help with this picture:** Fruit pictures the results of speech and work.

## Proverbs 12:15

A fool thinks his own way is right. 📖

But a wise man listens to advice.

## Proverbs 12:16

A fool’s anger shows at once. 📖

But a person with good judgment overlooks an insult.

## Proverbs 12:17

A truthful speaker declares what is right. 📖

But a false witness speaks deceit.

## Proverbs 12:18

Careless speech is like a piercing sword. 📖

But wise people’s tongues bring healing.

**Help with this picture:** The sword and healing contrast harmful speech with words that help.

## Proverbs 12:19

Truthful lips last forever. 📖

But a lying tongue lasts only a moment.

## Proverbs 12:20

People planning evil have deceit in their hearts. 📖

But advisers of peace have joy.

## Proverbs 12:21

No harm falls on righteous people. 📖

But wicked people are filled with trouble.

## Proverbs 12:22

The LORD detests lying lips. 📖

But people acting faithfully delight Him.

## Proverbs 12:23

A person with good judgment keeps knowledge to himself. 📖

But a foolish heart announces its foolishness.

## Proverbs 12:24

Hardworking hands will rule. 📖

But laziness ends in forced labor.

## Proverbs 12:25

Anxiety weighs down a man’s heart. 📖

But a good word cheers it.

## Proverbs 12:26

A righteous man is careful in friendship. 📖

But wicked people’s ways lead them astray.

## Proverbs 12:27

A lazy man does not roast the animal he hunted. 📖

But a hardworking man values his possessions.

## Proverbs 12:28

Life is on righteousness’s path. 📖

But another path leads to death.
