# Proverbs 8 — Wisdom speaks about her worth and beginning

**About this chapter**

Wisdom is pictured as a woman speaking.

She describes her worth, her presence before creation, and the results of listening to her.

## Proverbs 8:1

Does wisdom not call? 📖

Does understanding not raise her voice?

## Proverbs 8:2

She stands on heights above the road, at the crossroads. 📖

## Proverbs 8:3

She cries beside the city gates and entrances: 📖

## Proverbs 8:4

“I call to you, men. 📖

My cry is to human beings.

## Proverbs 8:5

Inexperienced people, learn good judgment. 📖

Fools, gain understanding.

## Proverbs 8:6

Listen, because I speak noble things. 📖

My lips reveal what is right.

## Proverbs 8:7

My mouth speaks truth. 📖

My lips hate wickedness.

## Proverbs 8:8

All my words are righteous. 📖

None are crooked or twisted.

**Help with righteous:** Right and just.

## Proverbs 8:9

All are plain to people with understanding. 📖

They are upright to those who find knowledge.

## Proverbs 8:10

Accept my instruction instead of silver. 📖

Choose knowledge rather than pure gold.

## Proverbs 8:11

Wisdom is more precious than rubies. 📖

Nothing you desire compares with her.

## Proverbs 8:12

I, wisdom, live with careful judgment. 📖

I find knowledge and wise decision-making.

## Proverbs 8:13

Fearing the LORD means hating evil. 📖

I hate arrogant pride, evil conduct, and twisted speech.

## Proverbs 8:14

Advice and sound judgment belong to me. 📖

I have understanding and strength.

## Proverbs 8:15

By me kings reign and rulers make just laws. 📖

## Proverbs 8:16

By me princes rule, and all nobles who govern fairly. 📖

**Help with nobles:** Leaders with high rank.

## Proverbs 8:17

I love those who love me. 📖

Those seeking me early will find me.

## Proverbs 8:18

Riches and honor are with me. 📖

Lasting wealth and righteousness are with me.

## Proverbs 8:19

My fruit is better than gold, even pure gold. 📖

My harvest is better than choice silver.

**Help with fruit and harvest:** Pictures of what wisdom produces.

## Proverbs 8:20

I walk in righteousness’s way, along justice’s paths, 📖

## Proverbs 8:21

giving wealth to people who love me and filling their treasuries. 📖

## Proverbs 8:22

The LORD created me as His first course, before His ancient works. 📖

**About this verse:** This is wisdom speaking in the poem.

The wording follows BSB without identifying wisdom with a particular later figure.

## Proverbs 8:23

I was established from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth began. 📖

## Proverbs 8:24

I was brought forth before watery depths existed, before springs overflowed. 📖

## Proverbs 8:25

I was brought forth before mountains settled and before hills, 📖

## Proverbs 8:26

before He made land, fields, or any earth dust. 📖

## Proverbs 8:27

I was there when He established heaven and marked a circle on the deep’s surface, 📖

## Proverbs 8:28

when He established clouds above and deep fountains burst out, 📖

## Proverbs 8:29

when He set the sea’s boundary so waters would not go beyond His command. 📖

I was there when He marked the earth’s foundations.

## Proverbs 8:30

Then I was a skilled craftsman beside Him. 📖

I was His delight every day, always rejoicing in His presence.

## Proverbs 8:31

I rejoiced in His whole world. 📖

I shared delight in human beings.

## Proverbs 8:32

Now, my sons, listen to me. 📖

Blessed are people who keep my ways.

## Proverbs 8:33

Listen to instruction and be wise. 📖

Do not ignore it.

## Proverbs 8:34

Blessed is the person listening to me, watching daily at my doors and waiting at my doorway posts. 📖

## Proverbs 8:35

Whoever finds me finds life. 📖

He gains the LORD’s favor.

## Proverbs 8:36

But whoever fails to find me harms himself. 📖

Everyone hating me loves death.”
