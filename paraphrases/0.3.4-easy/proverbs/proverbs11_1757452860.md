# Proverbs 11 — Honesty, justice, and generosity

**About this chapter**

These sayings describe integrity, wrongdoing, wealth, and care for others.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 11:1

The LORD detests dishonest scales. 📖

But an accurate weight delights Him.

**Help with scales:** Equipment for weighing goods in trade.

## Proverbs 11:2

Pride comes, and disgrace follows. 📖

Humility brings wisdom.

## Proverbs 11:3

Upright people’s integrity guides them. 📖

But unfaithful people’s twisted ways destroy them.

**Help with integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

## Proverbs 11:4

Riches are worthless on the day of wrath. 📖

But righteousness rescues from death.

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Proverbs 11:5

Blameless people’s righteousness directs their path. 📖

But wicked people fall through their own wickedness.

## Proverbs 11:6

Upright people’s righteousness rescues them. 📖

But unfaithful people’s desires trap them.

## Proverbs 11:7

When a wicked man dies, his hope perishes. 📖

The hope of his strength disappears.

## Proverbs 11:8

A righteous man is rescued from trouble. 📖

A wicked man goes into it instead.

## Proverbs 11:9

A man rejecting God destroys his neighbor with his mouth. 📖

But knowledge rescues righteous people.

## Proverbs 11:10

When righteous people thrive, the city rejoices. 📖

When wicked people perish, people shout for joy.

## Proverbs 11:11

Upright people’s blessing builds a city up. 📖

Wicked people’s mouths tear it down.

## Proverbs 11:12

Showing contempt for your neighbor shows lack of judgment. 📖

But someone with understanding stays silent.

## Proverbs 11:13

A gossip reveals secrets. 📖

But a trustworthy person keeps things private.

## Proverbs 11:14

Without guidance, a nation falls. 📖

But many advisers bring rescue.

## Proverbs 11:15

Guaranteeing a stranger’s debt will surely bring suffering. 📖

But someone hating indebtedness is secure.

## Proverbs 11:16

A gracious woman gains honor. 📖

But cruel men gain only wealth.

**Help with gracious:** Kind and considerate.

## Proverbs 11:17

A kind man benefits himself. 📖

But a cruel man brings trouble on himself.

## Proverbs 11:18

A wicked man earns an empty wage. 📖

But someone planting righteousness harvests a true reward.

**Help with this picture:** Planting and harvesting picture actions and their results.

## Proverbs 11:19

Genuine righteousness leads to life. 📖

But chasing evil brings death.

## Proverbs 11:20

The LORD detests people with twisted hearts. 📖

But people living blamelessly delight Him.

## Proverbs 11:21

Be certain: wicked people will not escape punishment. 📖

But righteous people’s children will escape.

## Proverbs 11:22

A beautiful woman without good judgment is like a gold ring in a pig’s snout. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The image contrasts outward beauty with lack of good judgment.

## Proverbs 11:23

Righteous people’s desire leads only to good. 📖

But wicked people’s hope brings wrath.

## Proverbs 11:24

One person gives freely and gains more. 📖

Another holds back what is right and becomes poor.

## Proverbs 11:25

A generous person will prosper. 📖

Anyone refreshing others will himself be refreshed.

## Proverbs 11:26

People curse a person hoarding grain. 📖

But blessing crowns the person selling it.

## Proverbs 11:27

Seeking good finds favor. 📖

But evil comes to anyone seeking it.

## Proverbs 11:28

A person trusting his riches will fall. 📖

But righteous people thrive like leaves.

**Help with this picture:** Thriving leaves picture life and growth.

## Proverbs 11:29

A person troubling his household inherits wind. 📖

A fool becomes servant to someone wise in heart.

**Help with inherits wind:** Gains nothing lasting.

## Proverbs 11:30

Righteous people’s fruit is a tree of life. 📖

A person winning souls is wise.

**Help with these images:** Fruit pictures results of a life.

Winning souls is retained without narrowing its sense to one particular activity.

## Proverbs 11:31

If righteous people receive their due on earth, how much more the ungodly and sinners! 📖
