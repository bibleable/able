# Proverbs 19 — Wisdom, generosity, and correction

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wisdom, speech, justice, and everyday conduct.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 19:1

Better a poor man living honestly than a fool with twisted lips. 📖

## Proverbs 19:2

Even zeal is no good without knowledge. 📖

Rushing your steps misses the mark.

**Help with zeal:** Strong eagerness.

## Proverbs 19:3

A man’s foolishness ruins his way. 📖

Yet his heart rages against the LORD.

## Proverbs 19:4

Wealth attracts many friends. 📖

But a poor man’s friend deserts him.

## Proverbs 19:5

A false witness will not escape punishment. 📖

Someone telling lies will not escape.

## Proverbs 19:6

Many people seek a prince’s favor. 📖

Everyone is a gift-giver’s friend.

## Proverbs 19:7

All a poor man’s brothers hate him. 📖

His friends avoid him even more!

He chases them with appeals, but cannot find them.

## Proverbs 19:8

Gaining wisdom shows love for yourself. 📖

Guarding understanding brings success.

## Proverbs 19:9

A false witness will not escape punishment. 📖

Someone pouring out lies will perish.

## Proverbs 19:10

Luxury does not suit a fool. 📖

A slave ruling princes is even worse.

## Proverbs 19:11

A man’s insight makes him patient. 📖

Overlooking an offense is his virtue.

## Proverbs 19:12

A king’s rage is like a lion’s roar. 📖

But his favor is like dew on grass.

**Help with this picture:** The lion pictures danger.

Dew pictures refreshing favor.

## Proverbs 19:13

A foolish son ruins his father. 📖

An argumentative wife is like constant dripping.

**Help with this picture:** Constant dripping pictures ongoing disturbance and irritation.

## Proverbs 19:14

Houses and wealth are inherited from fathers. 📖

But a wife with good judgment comes from the LORD.

## Proverbs 19:15

Laziness brings deep sleep. 📖

An idle soul will go hungry.

## Proverbs 19:16

Keeping a command protects your soul. 📖

But careless conduct brings death.

## Proverbs 19:17

Kindness to poor people is a loan to the LORD. 📖

He will repay the lender.

## Proverbs 19:18

Discipline your son, because there is hope in that. 📖

Do not take part in his death.

## Proverbs 19:19

A man with great anger must pay the penalty. 📖

If you rescue him, you will have to do it again.

## Proverbs 19:20

Listen to advice and accept discipline. 📖

Then you may be wise for the rest of your days.

## Proverbs 19:21

A man’s heart holds many plans. 📖

But the LORD’s purpose will prevail.

## Proverbs 19:22

What a man desires is faithful love. 📖

Better to be poor than a liar.

## Proverbs 19:23

Fear of the LORD leads to life. 📖

A person can rest satisfied, without harm visiting him.

**Help with fear of the LORD:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Proverbs 19:24

A person avoiding work buries his hand in the dish. 📖

He will not even bring it to his mouth.

## Proverbs 19:25

Strike a mocker, and inexperienced people will beware. 📖

Rebuke someone with understanding, and he gains knowledge.

## Proverbs 19:26

A son attacking his father or driving out his mother brings shame and disgrace. 📖

## Proverbs 19:27

My son, stop listening to instruction, and you will wander from knowledge’s words. 📖

## Proverbs 19:28

A corrupt witness mocks justice. 📖

A wicked mouth swallows sin.

## Proverbs 19:29

Judgments are prepared for mockers. 📖

Beatings are prepared for fools’ backs.
