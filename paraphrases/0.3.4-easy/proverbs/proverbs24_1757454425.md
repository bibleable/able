# Proverbs 24 — Rescue those in danger and act justly

**About this chapter**

These sayings teach wise conduct and just judgment.

The final verses describe a neglected field.

## Proverbs 24:1

Do not envy wicked men or want their company. 📖

## Proverbs 24:2

Their hearts plan violence. 📖

Their lips declare trouble.

## Proverbs 24:3

Wisdom builds a house. 📖

Understanding makes it secure.

## Proverbs 24:4

Knowledge fills its rooms with every precious and beautiful treasure. 📖

## Proverbs 24:5

A wise man is strong. 📖

A knowledgeable man increases his strength.

## Proverbs 24:6

Wage war only with sound guidance. 📖

Victory comes through many advisers.

## Proverbs 24:7

Wisdom is too high for a fool. 📖

He does not speak in the meeting place.

## Proverbs 24:8

A man plotting evil will be called a schemer. 📖

## Proverbs 24:9

A foolish scheme is sin. 📖

People detest a mocker.

## Proverbs 24:10

If you faint in distress, your strength is small! 📖

## Proverbs 24:11

Rescue people being led to death. 📖

Hold back people stumbling toward slaughter.

## Proverbs 24:12

If you say, “We did not know,” does the One weighing hearts not consider it? 📖

Does your life’s Guardian not know?

Will He not repay a person according to his deeds?

## Proverbs 24:13

My son, eat honey because it is good. 📖

Honey from the comb tastes sweet.

## Proverbs 24:14

Know that wisdom is sweet to your soul. 📖

If you find it, you have a future.

Your hope will never be cut off.

## Proverbs 24:15

Wicked man, do not hide near a righteous person’s home to attack. 📖

Do not destroy his resting place.

## Proverbs 24:16

A righteous man may fall seven times, but he gets up. 📖

Wicked people stumble in bad times.

## Proverbs 24:17

Do not delight when your enemy falls. 📖

Do not rejoice in your heart when he stumbles,

## Proverbs 24:18

or the LORD will see and disapprove. 📖

He will turn His wrath away from him.

**Help with wrath:** Fierce anger.

## Proverbs 24:19

Do not become upset over evildoers. 📖

Do not envy wicked people.

## Proverbs 24:20

An evil man has no future. 📖

Wicked people’s lamp will go out.

## Proverbs 24:21

My son, fear the LORD and the king. 📖

Do not join rebellious people.

## Proverbs 24:22

They bring sudden destruction. 📖

Who knows the ruin they can bring?

## Proverbs 24:23

These also are wise people’s sayings. 📖

Favoring one side unfairly in judgment is not good.

## Proverbs 24:24

People will curse anyone telling guilty people, “You are innocent.” 📖

Nations will denounce him.

## Proverbs 24:25

But things will go well for those convicting guilty people. 📖

Rich blessing will come on them.

## Proverbs 24:26

An honest answer is like a kiss on the lips. 📖

## Proverbs 24:27

Finish your outdoor work and prepare your field. 📖

Afterward, build your house.

## Proverbs 24:28

Do not testify against your neighbor without cause. 📖

Do not deceive with your lips.

## Proverbs 24:29

Do not say, “I will do what he did to me. 📖

I will repay him according to his work.”

## Proverbs 24:30

I passed a lazy person’s field. 📖

I passed a vineyard belonging to a man lacking judgment.

## Proverbs 24:31

Thorns grew everywhere. 📖

Thistles covered the ground.

The stone wall was broken down.

## Proverbs 24:32

I observed and took it to heart. 📖

I looked and learned:

## Proverbs 24:33

A little sleep, a little dozing, a little folding hands to rest, 📖

## Proverbs 24:34

and poverty comes like a robber. 📖

Need comes like a bandit.
