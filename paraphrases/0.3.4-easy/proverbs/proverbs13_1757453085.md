# Proverbs 13 — Listen to correction and choose wise companions

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern instruction, speech, desires, wealth, and justice.

Righteous means right before God.

## Proverbs 13:1

A wise son listens to his father’s discipline. 📖

But a mocker does not listen to correction.

## Proverbs 13:2

A man enjoys good things from his lips’ fruit. 📖

But unfaithful people desire violence.

## Proverbs 13:3

Guarding your mouth protects your life. 📖

But opening your lips widely invites your own ruin.

## Proverbs 13:4

A person avoiding work craves but has nothing. 📖

But a hardworking person’s soul is fully satisfied.

## Proverbs 13:5

Righteous people hate lies. 📖

But wicked people bring shame and disgrace.

## Proverbs 13:6

Righteousness guards a man of integrity. 📖

But wickedness undermines a sinner.

**Help with integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

## Proverbs 13:7

One person pretends to be rich but has nothing. 📖

Another pretends to be poor but has great wealth.

## Proverbs 13:8

Riches may ransom a man’s life. 📖

But a poor man hears no threat.

**Help with ransom:** Buy someone’s freedom.

## Proverbs 13:9

Righteous people’s light shines brightly. 📖

But wicked people’s lamp goes out.

**Help with this picture:** Light and a lamp picture life, well-being, and security.

## Proverbs 13:10

Pride leads only to conflict. 📖

But wisdom is with people who accept good advice.

## Proverbs 13:11

Dishonest wealth shrinks. 📖

But wealth earned through hard work grows.

## Proverbs 13:12

Delayed hope makes the heart sick. 📖

But a fulfilled desire is a tree of life.

**Help with this picture:** The tree of life pictures the life-giving joy of fulfilled hope.

## Proverbs 13:13

Anyone despising instruction pays a penalty. 📖

But someone respecting a command is rewarded.

## Proverbs 13:14

Wise teaching is a fountain of life. 📖

It turns people from death’s traps.

**Help with this picture:** The fountain and traps picture life-giving teaching and deadly danger.

## Proverbs 13:15

Good understanding wins favor. 📖

But unfaithful people’s way is difficult.

## Proverbs 13:16

A careful person acts with knowledge. 📖

But a fool displays his foolishness.

## Proverbs 13:17

A wicked messenger falls into trouble. 📖

But a faithful messenger brings healing.

## Proverbs 13:18

Ignoring discipline brings poverty and shame. 📖

But listening to correction brings honor.

## Proverbs 13:19

A fulfilled desire is sweet to the soul. 📖

But fools detest turning from evil.

## Proverbs 13:20

Walking with wise people makes you wise. 📖

But a fool’s companion will be destroyed.

## Proverbs 13:21

Disaster chases sinners. 📖

But prosperity rewards righteous people.

## Proverbs 13:22

A good man leaves an inheritance to his children’s children. 📖

But a sinner’s wealth passes to righteous people.

## Proverbs 13:23

Poor people’s unplowed land has plentiful food. 📖

But lack of justice sweeps it away.

**About this saying:** It names injustice as the cause of loss, rather than blaming poverty on laziness.

## Proverbs 13:24

A man sparing the rod hates his son. 📖

But a man loving his son disciplines him carefully.

**Help with rod:** A stick used for punishment.

The strong contrast in the source is retained.

## Proverbs 13:25

A righteous man eats until satisfied. 📖

But wicked people’s stomachs are empty.
