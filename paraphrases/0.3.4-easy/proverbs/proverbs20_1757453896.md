# Proverbs 20 — Careful judgment and honest conduct

**About this chapter**

These sayings concern wise conduct, speech, work, and justice.

Righteous means right before God; upright means honest and right.

## Proverbs 20:1

Wine mocks, and strong drink fights. 📖

Anyone led astray by them is not wise.

## Proverbs 20:2

A king’s terror is like a lion’s roar. 📖

Provoking him costs a person his life.

## Proverbs 20:3

Resolving a dispute brings honor. 📖

But any fool will quarrel.

## Proverbs 20:4

A person avoiding work does not plow at the right time. 📖

At harvest he looks, but finds nothing.

## Proverbs 20:5

A man’s heart intentions are deep waters. 📖

But someone with understanding draws them out.

**Help with this picture:** Deep water pictures intentions that are not easy to see.

Understanding helps bring them into the open.

## Proverbs 20:6

Many people announce their faithful love. 📖

But who can find a trustworthy man?

## Proverbs 20:7

A righteous man lives with integrity. 📖

His children after him are blessed.

**Help with integrity:** Being honest and holding to what is right.

## Proverbs 20:8

A king sitting to judge sifts out all evil with his eyes. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Sifting pictures identifying and separating out evil.

## Proverbs 20:9

Who can say, “I kept my heart pure. 📖

I am clean from my sin”?

## Proverbs 20:10

The LORD detests both differing weights and unequal measures. 📖

**Help with weights and measures:** Equipment used in trade.

Using unequal standards cheats people.

## Proverbs 20:11

Even a young man is known by his actions, whether his conduct is pure and upright. 📖

## Proverbs 20:12

The LORD made both hearing ears and seeing eyes. 📖

## Proverbs 20:13

Do not love sleep, or you will become poor. 📖

Open your eyes, and you will have plenty of food.

## Proverbs 20:14

“Worthless, worthless!” says the buyer. 📖

But he goes away boasting.

## Proverbs 20:15

Gold and rubies are plentiful. 📖

But knowledgeable lips are a rare treasure.

## Proverbs 20:16

Take the clothing of someone guaranteeing a stranger’s debt. 📖

Take security if it is for a foreigner.

**Help with security:** Property held to make sure a debt is paid.

## Proverbs 20:17

Food gained by fraud tastes sweet. 📖

But afterward a man’s mouth fills with gravel.

**Help with this picture:** Sweet food becoming gravel pictures dishonest gain turning painful.

## Proverbs 20:18

Establish plans by seeking advice. 📖

Wage war with sound guidance.

## Proverbs 20:19

Someone revealing secrets is a constant gossip. 📖

Avoid people whose lips babble.

## Proverbs 20:20

Anyone cursing his father or mother will have his lamp put out in deepest darkness. 📖

**Help with this picture:** An extinguished lamp pictures the loss of life or well-being.

## Proverbs 20:21

An inheritance gained quickly will not be blessed in the end. 📖

## Proverbs 20:22

Do not say, “I will take revenge for this evil!” 📖

Wait for the LORD, and He will save you.

## Proverbs 20:23

The LORD detests unequal weights. 📖

Dishonest scales are no good.

## Proverbs 20:24

A man’s steps come from the LORD. 📖

So how can anyone understand his own way?

## Proverbs 20:25

A man traps himself by dedicating something hastily and only later reconsidering his vows. 📖

**Help with dedicating and vows:** Setting something apart for God, and serious promises.

## Proverbs 20:26

A wise king separates wicked people and drives the threshing wheel over them. 📖

**Help with threshing wheel:** Equipment used to separate grain, here picturing crushing judgment.

## Proverbs 20:27

A man’s spirit is the LORD’s lamp, searching his innermost being. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The lamp pictures inner examination in God’s light.

## Proverbs 20:28

Faithful love and faithfulness preserve a king. 📖

Through them he maintains his throne.

## Proverbs 20:29

Young men’s glory is their strength. 📖

Old people’s splendor is gray hair.

## Proverbs 20:30

Whip blows and wounds scour evil away. 📖

Beatings cleanse the innermost parts.

**Help with this picture:** Scouring and cleansing picture removing evil through punishment.

The source’s severe bodily language is retained.
