# Zechariah 1 — Return to the LORD and visions in the night

**About this chapter**

The LORD calls His people to return to Him.

Zechariah sees horses, horns, and craftsmen.

He hears messages of judgment and mercy for Jerusalem.

## Zechariah 1:1

In Darius’s second year, in the eighth month, the LORD’s word came to the prophet Zechariah. 📖

He was Berechiah’s son and Iddo’s grandson.

## Zechariah 1:2

God said, “The LORD was very angry with your ancestors. 📖

## Zechariah 1:3

Tell the people what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

Return to Me, declares the LORD of Hosts, and I will return to you, says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with “LORD of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Zechariah 1:4

Do not be like your ancestors. 📖

Earlier prophets told them what the LORD of Hosts said.

Turn now from your evil ways and deeds.

But they did not listen or pay attention to Me, declares the LORD.

## Zechariah 1:5

Where are your ancestors now? 📖

Do the prophets live forever?

## Zechariah 1:6

Did My words and rules not catch up with your ancestors? 📖

I commanded My servants the prophets to speak them.

Your ancestors repented and said, ‘The LORD of Hosts planned to deal with us according to our ways and deeds.

He has done just that.’”

**Help with “repented”:** This means turning away from wrongdoing and turning back to God.

## Zechariah 1:7

The LORD’s word came to the prophet Zechariah, Berechiah’s son and Iddo’s grandson. 📖

It was Darius’s second year, the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, called Shebat.

## Zechariah 1:8

I looked in the night and saw a man riding a red horse. 📖

He stood among myrtle trees in a hollow.

Behind him were red, sorrel, and white horses.

**Help with these words:** Myrtle is a kind of tree.

Sorrel is a reddish-brown color.

## Zechariah 1:9

I asked, “What are these, my lord?” 📖

The angel speaking with me replied, “I will show you.”

## Zechariah 1:10

The man among the myrtle trees explained, “These are the ones the LORD sent to patrol the earth.” 📖

## Zechariah 1:11

The riders answered the LORD’s angel, who stood among the myrtle trees. 📖

They said, “We have patrolled the earth.

All the earth is resting and quiet.”

## Zechariah 1:12

The LORD’s angel said, “LORD of Hosts, how long will You hold back mercy from Jerusalem and Judah’s cities? 📖

You have been angry with them for seventy years.”

## Zechariah 1:13

The LORD spoke kind, comforting words to the angel speaking with me. 📖

## Zechariah 1:14

The angel told me, “Announce what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

I am very jealous for Jerusalem and Zion,

## Zechariah 1:15

but I am fiercely angry with the nations that feel at ease. 📖

I was a little angry, but they added to the disaster.

## Zechariah 1:16

So the LORD says, “I will return to Jerusalem with mercy. 📖

My house will be rebuilt there, declares the LORD of Hosts.

A measuring line will be stretched over Jerusalem.”

## Zechariah 1:17

Announce more of what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

My cities will overflow with prosperity again.

The LORD will comfort Zion again and choose Jerusalem.”

## Zechariah 1:18

I looked up and saw four horns. 📖

## Zechariah 1:19

I asked the angel speaking with me, “What are these?” 📖

He told me, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”

**Help with the horns:** Horns picture power.

## Zechariah 1:20

The LORD showed me four craftsmen. 📖

## Zechariah 1:21

I asked, “What are they coming to do?” 📖

He replied, “These horns scattered Judah, so no one could raise his head.

But the craftsmen have come to terrify the horns and throw them down.

These nations raised their horns against Judah’s land to scatter it.”
