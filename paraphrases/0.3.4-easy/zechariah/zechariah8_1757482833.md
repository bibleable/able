# Zechariah 8 — Truth, peace, and a restored city

**About this chapter**

The LORD promises to return to Jerusalem and save His people.

He calls them to speak truth and judge fairly.

Days of fasting will become joyful feasts, and nations will seek Him.

## Zechariah 8:1

The word of the LORD of Hosts came to me again. 📖

## Zechariah 8:2

The LORD of Hosts says, “I am very jealous for Zion. 📖

I am jealous for her with burning passion.”

## Zechariah 8:3

The LORD says, “I will return to Zion and live in Jerusalem. 📖

Jerusalem will be called the City of Truth.

The LORD of Hosts’ mountain will be called the Holy Mountain.”

## Zechariah 8:4

The LORD of Hosts says, “Old men and women will again sit in Jerusalem’s streets. 📖

Each will hold a staff because of great age.

## Zechariah 8:5

The city’s streets will be full of boys and girls playing.” 📖

## Zechariah 8:6

The LORD of Hosts says, “If this seems impossible to this people’s survivors now, should it seem impossible to Me? 📖

This is what the LORD of Hosts declares.

## Zechariah 8:7

The LORD of Hosts says, “I will save My people from the eastern and western lands. 📖

## Zechariah 8:8

I will bring them back to live in Jerusalem. 📖

They will be My people, and I will be their faithful and righteous God.”

## Zechariah 8:9

The LORD of Hosts says, “Let your hands be strong, you who hear these words now. 📖

The prophets who spoke them were there when the foundations were laid.

The temple, the house of the LORD of Hosts, was to be rebuilt.

## Zechariah 8:10

Before those days, neither people nor animals received wages. 📖

No one coming or going was safe from the enemy.

I had turned everyone against their neighbor.

## Zechariah 8:11

But now I will treat this people’s survivors differently from the past, declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Zechariah 8:12

The seed will prosper, the grapevine will bear fruit, and the ground will produce crops. 📖

The skies will give dew.

I will give all these things to this people’s survivors as an inheritance.

**Help with these words:** Dew is water that settles outdoors.

An inheritance is something given to people to keep.

## Zechariah 8:13

People of Judah and Israel, you have been a curse among the nations. 📖

I will save you, and you will be a blessing.

Do not fear.

Let your hands be strong.”

## Zechariah 8:14

The LORD of Hosts says, “When your ancestors made Me angry, I decided to bring disaster on you. 📖

I did not change My decision, says the LORD of Hosts.

## Zechariah 8:15

Now I have decided to do good again to Jerusalem and Judah. 📖

Do not fear.

## Zechariah 8:16

You must do these things. 📖

Speak truth to one another.

Give true and sound judgments at your gates.

**About “gates”:** Public judgments were made at city gates.

## Zechariah 8:17

Do not plan evil against your neighbor in your hearts. 📖

Do not love swearing falsely.

I hate all these things, declares the LORD.”

## Zechariah 8:18

The word of the LORD of Hosts came to me. 📖

## Zechariah 8:19

He said, “The LORD of Hosts says this. 📖

The fasts of the fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months will become joyful, cheerful feasts for Judah’s people.

So love both truth and peace.”

## Zechariah 8:20

The LORD of Hosts says, “People from many cities will yet come. 📖

## Zechariah 8:21

People of one city will go to another. 📖

They will say, ‘Let us go at once to plead before the LORD and seek the LORD of Hosts.

I myself am going.’

## Zechariah 8:22

Many peoples and strong nations will come to seek the LORD of Hosts in Jerusalem and plead before Him.” 📖

## Zechariah 8:23

The LORD of Hosts says this. 📖

In those days, ten men from nations of every language will tightly grasp a Jew’s robe.

They will say, “Let us go with you.

We have heard that God is with you.”
