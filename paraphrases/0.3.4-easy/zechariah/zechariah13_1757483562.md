# Zechariah 13 — Cleansing and the struck Shepherd

**About this chapter**

God promises cleansing and the removal of false worship and false prophecy.

The chapter also describes the Shepherd being struck and a third of the people being refined.

## Zechariah 13:1

God says, “On that day, a fountain will open for David’s family and Jerusalem’s people. 📖

It will cleanse them from sin and impurity.

## Zechariah 13:2

On that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, I will erase the idols’ names from the land. 📖

They will no longer be remembered.

I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of impurity from the land.

## Zechariah 13:3

If anyone still prophesies, his own father and mother will tell him he must not stay alive. 📖

He has spoken falsely in the LORD’s name.

When he prophesies, his own father and mother will pierce him through.

## Zechariah 13:4

On that day, every prophet who prophesies will be ashamed of his vision. 📖

He will not wear a hairy cloak to deceive people.

## Zechariah 13:5

He will say, ‘I am not a prophet. 📖

I work the land, because I was bought as a servant when I was young.’

## Zechariah 13:6

If someone asks, ‘What are these wounds on your chest?’ he will answer. 📖

He will say, ‘I received these wounds in my friends’ house.’

## Zechariah 13:7

Sword, wake against My Shepherd, the man who is My Companion, declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

Strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will scatter.

I will turn My hand against the little ones.

## Zechariah 13:8

In all the land, declares the LORD, two-thirds will be cut off and die. 📖

A third will remain.

## Zechariah 13:9

I will bring this third through fire. 📖

I will refine them like silver and test them like gold.

They will call My name, and I will answer.

I will say, ‘They are My people.’

They will say, ‘The LORD is our God.’”

**Help with the refining picture:** Fire removes waste from precious metal.

The picture describes testing and cleansing the remaining people.
