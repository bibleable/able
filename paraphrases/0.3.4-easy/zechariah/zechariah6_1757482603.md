# Zechariah 6 — Four chariots and a crown

**About this chapter**

Zechariah sees four chariots with different horses.

The LORD gives instructions for a crown and a message about the Branch.

The final promise includes a condition of careful obedience.

## Zechariah 6:1

I looked up again and saw four chariots coming from between two mountains of bronze. 📖

**Help with “chariots”:** These are vehicles pulled by horses.

## Zechariah 6:2

The first chariot had red horses, and the second had black horses. 📖

## Zechariah 6:3

The third had white horses, and the fourth had dappled horses. 📖

All were strong.

**Help with “dappled”:** This means having patches of different colors.

## Zechariah 6:4

I asked the angel speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?” 📖

## Zechariah 6:5

He told me, “These are heaven’s four spirits, going out from their place before the Lord of all the earth. 📖

## Zechariah 6:6

The black horses are going north, the white horses west, and the dappled horses south.” 📖

## Zechariah 6:7

The strong horses went out, eager to patrol the earth. 📖

The LORD said, “Go and patrol the earth.”

So they patrolled it.

## Zechariah 6:8

The LORD called me and said, “Look! 📖

Those going north have given My Spirit rest in the northern land.”

## Zechariah 6:9

The LORD’s word also came to me. 📖

## Zechariah 6:10

He said, “Take an offering from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, exiles who arrived from Babylon. 📖

On that same day, go to Josiah’s house.

He is Zephaniah’s son.

## Zechariah 6:11

Take silver and gold and make a richly decorated crown. 📖

Put it on the head of Joshua, the high priest, son of Jehozadak.

## Zechariah 6:12

Tell him what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

Here is a man named the Branch.

He will branch out from His place and build the LORD’s temple.

**Help with “the Branch”:** This is the title of the promised man in the passage.

## Zechariah 6:13

Yes, He will build the LORD’s temple. 📖

He will be dressed in splendor and sit on His throne to rule.

He will be a priest on His throne.

There will be peaceful counsel between the two.

**About “the two”:** The draft keeps this expression without supplying extra names.

## Zechariah 6:14

The crown will stay in the LORD’s temple as a memorial. 📖

It will remember Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and the gracious son of Zephaniah.

**About the names:** Verse 10 says “Heldai,” while this verse says “Helem.”

Both source forms are kept.

## Zechariah 6:15

Even people far away will come and build the LORD’s temple. 📖

You will know that the LORD of Hosts sent Me to you.

This will happen if you carefully obey the LORD your God’s voice.”
