# Zechariah 3 — Joshua’s clothes and God’s promise

**About this chapter**

Zechariah sees Joshua the high priest before the LORD’s angel.

Joshua’s filthy clothes are removed as a sign that his wrongdoing is removed.

God gives a conditional charge and promises His servant, the Branch.

## Zechariah 3:1

The angel showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the LORD’s angel. 📖

Satan stood at Joshua’s right to accuse him.

## Zechariah 3:2

The LORD told Satan, “The LORD rebukes you, Satan! 📖

The LORD, who chose Jerusalem, rebukes you!

Is this man not a burning stick snatched from the fire?”

**Help with these words:** Rebuking means speaking firmly against someone.

The burning-stick picture describes rescue from destruction.

## Zechariah 3:3

Joshua stood before the angel wearing filthy clothes. 📖

## Zechariah 3:4

The angel told those standing before him, “Take off his filthy clothes!” 📖

He told Joshua, “See, I have removed your wrongdoing.

I will dress you in splendid robes.”

## Zechariah 3:5

I said, “Let them put a clean turban on his head.” 📖

They put a clean turban on him and dressed him while the LORD’s angel stood nearby.

**Help with “turban”:** This is a cloth wrapped around the head.

## Zechariah 3:6

The LORD’s angel gave Joshua this charge. 📖

## Zechariah 3:7

He said, “The LORD of Hosts says this. 📖

If you follow My ways and keep My instructions, you will govern My house and care for My courtyards.

I will give you a place among those standing here.

**Help with “LORD of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Zechariah 3:8

High priest Joshua, listen, with your companions sitting before you. 📖

You are a sign.

Look!

I will bring My servant, the Branch.

**About “the Branch”:** This is the title given to God’s promised servant here.

## Zechariah 3:9

See the stone I placed before Joshua. 📖

That one stone has seven eyes.

I will carve a message on it, declares the LORD of Hosts.

I will remove this land’s wrongdoing in a single day.

## Zechariah 3:10

The LORD of Hosts declares this. 📖

On that day, each of you will invite your neighbor under your grapevine and fig tree.”
