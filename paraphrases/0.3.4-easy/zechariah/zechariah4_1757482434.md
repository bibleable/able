# Zechariah 4 — The lampstand, olive trees, and Zerubbabel

**About this chapter**

An angel shows Zechariah a gold lampstand and two olive trees.

The LORD’s message concerns Zerubbabel and the completion of the temple.

The vision’s numbers and its explanation are kept.

## Zechariah 4:1

The angel speaking with me returned and woke me, like a man waking from sleep. 📖

## Zechariah 4:2

He asked, “What do you see?” 📖

I replied, “A solid gold lampstand with a bowl on top.

It has seven lamps and seven spouts leading to the lamps.

## Zechariah 4:3

Two olive trees stand beside it, one to the bowl’s right and one to its left.” 📖

## Zechariah 4:4

I asked the angel speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?” 📖

## Zechariah 4:5

The angel replied, “Do you not know?” 📖

I answered, “No, my lord.”

## Zechariah 4:6

He told me, “This is the LORD’s word to Zerubbabel. 📖

Not by might or power, but by My Spirit, says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with “LORD of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Zechariah 4:7

Great mountain, what are you? 📖

Before Zerubbabel, you will become flat land.

He will bring out the capstone with shouts of ‘Grace, grace to it!’”

**Help with these pictures:** The mountain pictures an obstacle.

The capstone is a stone that completes a building.

“Grace” calls for God’s favor.

## Zechariah 4:8

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Zechariah 4:9

The message said, “Zerubbabel’s hands laid this house’s foundation, and his hands will finish it. 📖

Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts sent me to you.

## Zechariah 4:10

Who has despised the day of small things? 📖

These seven eyes of the LORD scan the whole earth.

They will rejoice to see the plumb line in Zerubbabel’s hand.”

**Help with “plumb line”:** This is a weighted cord used to check whether a structure is upright.

## Zechariah 4:11

I asked the angel, “What are the two olive trees on the lampstand’s right and left?” 📖

## Zechariah 4:12

I also asked, “What are the two olive branches beside the two gold pipes that pour golden oil?” 📖

## Zechariah 4:13

He asked, “Do you not know what they are?” 📖

I replied, “No, my lord.”

## Zechariah 4:14

He said, “These are the two anointed ones standing beside the Lord of all the earth.” 📖

**Help with “anointed”:** This means set apart for a special role.
