# Zechariah 10 — The LORD gathers and strengthens His people

**About this chapter**

The people are called to ask the LORD for rain.

God condemns false guidance and promises to gather, restore, and strengthen His people.

## Zechariah 10:1

Ask the LORD for rain in spring. 📖

He makes the storm clouds.

He will give everyone showers and crops in the field.

## Zechariah 10:2

Idols speak lies, and diviners see false visions. 📖

They tell false dreams and offer empty comfort.

So people wander like sheep, oppressed because they have no shepherd.

**Help with “diviners”:** These are people who claim to discover hidden messages.

## Zechariah 10:3

God says, “My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the leaders. 📖

The LORD of Hosts cares for His flock, Judah’s people.

He will make them like His royal horse in battle.

## Zechariah 10:4

The cornerstone, tent peg, battle bow, and every ruler together will come from Judah. 📖

**About these pictures:** The stone, peg, and bow picture strength, support, and leadership.

## Zechariah 10:5

They will be like mighty men in battle, trampling enemies in street mud. 📖

They will fight because the LORD is with them and put the horsemen to shame.

## Zechariah 10:6

I will strengthen Judah’s people and save Joseph’s people. 📖

I will restore them because I have compassion on them.

They will be as though I had not rejected them.

I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.

## Zechariah 10:7

Ephraim will be like a mighty man, with hearts glad as with wine. 📖

Their children will see and be joyful.

Their hearts will rejoice in the LORD.

## Zechariah 10:8

I will whistle to gather them, because I have redeemed them. 📖

They will be as many as they once were.

**Help with “redeemed”:** This means rescued or bought back.

## Zechariah 10:9

Though I scatter them like seed among nations, they will remember Me in distant lands. 📖

They and their children will live and return.

## Zechariah 10:10

I will bring them back from Egypt and gather them from Assyria. 📖

I will bring them to Gilead and Lebanon until there is no more room for them.

## Zechariah 10:11

They will pass through the sea of distress and strike its waves. 📖

All the Nile’s depths will dry up.

Assyria’s pride will be brought down, and Egypt’s royal staff will depart.

**Help with “royal staff”:** BSB calls this a “scepter,” a sign of authority.

## Zechariah 10:12

I will strengthen them in the LORD, and they will walk in His name, declares the LORD.” 📖
