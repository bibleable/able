# Zechariah 12 — Jerusalem defended and a day of mourning

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks about defending Jerusalem and Judah.

He promises a spirit of grace and prayer.

The people mourn for the pierced one, with each clan named separately.

## Zechariah 12:1

This is the LORD’s message about Israel. 📖

The LORD stretches out heaven, lays earth’s foundation, and forms the human spirit within each person.

He declares,

## Zechariah 12:2

“Look! 📖

I will make Jerusalem a cup of drunkenness for all surrounding peoples.

Judah and Jerusalem will both be surrounded for attack.

**Help with the cup picture:** It describes the peoples being overwhelmed as if drunk.

## Zechariah 12:3

On that day, all earth’s nations will gather against Jerusalem. 📖

I will make her a heavy stone for all peoples.

Everyone trying to lift her away will be severely injured.

## Zechariah 12:4

On that day, declares the LORD, I will make every horse panic and every rider lose his mind. 📖

I will watch over Judah’s people.

But I will strike all the nations’ horses with blindness.

## Zechariah 12:5

Judah’s leaders will say in their hearts, ‘Jerusalem’s people are my strength, because the LORD of Hosts is their God.’ 📖

## Zechariah 12:6

On that day, I will make Judah’s clans like a pot of fire in a woodpile. 📖

They will be like a flaming torch among bundles of grain.

They will burn up all surrounding peoples, right and left.

Jerusalem’s people will stay safe there.

## Zechariah 12:7

The LORD will save Judah’s tents first. 📖

Then David’s family and Jerusalem’s people will not have greater glory than Judah.

## Zechariah 12:8

On that day, the LORD will defend Jerusalem’s people. 📖

The weakest among them will be like David.

David’s family will be like God, like the LORD’s angel going ahead of them.

## Zechariah 12:9

On that day, I will set out to destroy all the nations coming against Jerusalem. 📖

## Zechariah 12:10

I will pour a spirit of grace and prayer on David’s family and Jerusalem’s people. 📖

They will look on Me, the One they pierced.

They will mourn for Him as for an only child.

They will grieve bitterly for Him as for a firstborn son.

**About the wording:** BSB says “Me” and then “Him.”

Both are kept without adding a name.

Grace means undeserved favor.

## Zechariah 12:11

On that day, Jerusalem’s wailing will be as great as the wailing of Hadad-rimmon in Megiddo’s plain. 📖

## Zechariah 12:12

The land will mourn, each clan separately. 📖

David’s family clan and their wives will mourn.

Nathan’s family clan and their wives will mourn,

## Zechariah 12:13

as will Levi’s family clan and their wives, and Shimei’s clan and their wives, 📖

## Zechariah 12:14

and all the remaining clans and their wives.” 📖
