# Zechariah 14 — The LORD’s day and reign

**About this chapter**

The chapter describes Jerusalem’s capture, the LORD’s intervention, and His rule over all the earth.

It includes sexual violence, plague, changes to the land, and conditions for the nations’ worship.

Its difficult details are retained as part of the text draft.

## Zechariah 14:1

Look! 📖

A day of the LORD is coming.

Goods taken from you will be divided in front of you.

## Zechariah 14:2

God says, “I will gather all nations to fight Jerusalem. 📖

The city will be captured, houses robbed, and women raped.

Half the city will go into exile, but the remaining people will not be removed.”

**Help with “exile”:** This means being forced to live away from their homeland.

## Zechariah 14:3

Then the LORD will go out and fight those nations, as He fights on a day of battle. 📖

## Zechariah 14:4

On that day, His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem. 📖

The mountain will split east to west, making a great valley.

Half will move north and half south.

## Zechariah 14:5

You will flee through My mountain valley, which will reach Azal. 📖

You will flee as from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah.

Then the LORD my God will come, with all the holy ones.

## Zechariah 14:6

On that day, there will be no light, no cold, and no frost. 📖

## Zechariah 14:7

It will be a unique day, known only to the LORD, without day or night. 📖

But at evening, there will be light.

## Zechariah 14:8

On that day, living water will flow from Jerusalem. 📖

Half will flow toward the Eastern Sea and half toward the Western Sea.

This will happen in summer and winter alike.

## Zechariah 14:9

The LORD will be King over all the earth that day. 📖

The LORD alone, and His name alone!

## Zechariah 14:10

All the land from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem, will become flat land. 📖

But Jerusalem will be raised and remain in its place.

It will stretch from the Benjamin Gate to the First Gate’s site and the Corner Gate.

It will stretch from the Tower of Hananel to the royal winepresses.

## Zechariah 14:11

People will live there. 📖

There will never again be complete destruction.

Jerusalem will be safe.

## Zechariah 14:12

This is the plague with which the LORD will strike all peoples who fought Jerusalem. 📖

Their flesh will rot as they stand.

Their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths.

## Zechariah 14:13

On that day, the LORD will cause great panic among them. 📖

Each will seize another’s hand and raise his hand against the other.

## Zechariah 14:14

Judah will also fight at Jerusalem. 📖

The wealth of all surrounding nations will be gathered: great amounts of gold, silver, and clothes.

## Zechariah 14:15

A similar plague will strike horses, mules, camels, donkeys, and all animals in those camps. 📖

## Zechariah 14:16

All survivors from the nations that attacked Jerusalem will go up year after year. 📖

They will worship the King, the LORD of Hosts, and celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles.

**Help with “Feast of Tabernacles”:** This is the feast when people live in temporary shelters.

## Zechariah 14:17

If any of earth’s families do not go to Jerusalem to worship the King, they will receive no rain. 📖

The King is the LORD of Hosts.

## Zechariah 14:18

If Egypt’s people do not go up and enter, rain will not fall on them. 📖

This will be the plague the LORD sends on nations not celebrating the Feast of Tabernacles.

## Zechariah 14:19

This will punish Egypt and all nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles. 📖

## Zechariah 14:20

On that day, the horses’ bells will carry the words HOLY TO THE LORD. 📖

Cooking pots in the LORD’s house will be like sprinkling bowls before the altar.

## Zechariah 14:21

Every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to the LORD of Hosts. 📖

Everyone offering sacrifices will take some pots and cook in them.

On that day, no Canaanite will remain in the LORD of Hosts’ house.

**About “Canaanite”:** The draft keeps this source word without substituting a different term.
