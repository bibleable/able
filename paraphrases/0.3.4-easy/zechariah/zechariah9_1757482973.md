# Zechariah 9 — Judgment and the coming King

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks against neighboring places and promises protection for His house.

A humble King comes on a donkey.

The chapter also promises rescue and describes victory in battle.

## Zechariah 9:1

This is the LORD’s message against Hadrach’s land, resting on Damascus. 📖

The eyes of people and all Israel’s tribes are on the LORD.

## Zechariah 9:2

The message is also against neighboring Hamath, and Tyre and Sidon, though they are very shrewd. 📖

## Zechariah 9:3

Tyre built herself a fortress. 📖

She piled silver like dust and gold like dirt in the streets.

## Zechariah 9:4

Look! 📖

The Lord will make her poor and throw her wealth into the sea.

Fire will burn her up.

## Zechariah 9:5

Ashkelon will see and fear. 📖

Gaza and Ekron will twist in agony, because Ekron’s hope will dry up.

Gaza will have no king, and no one will live in Ashkelon.

## Zechariah 9:6

God says, “People of mixed descent will occupy Ashdod. 📖

I will cut off the Philistines’ pride.

## Zechariah 9:7

I will remove the blood from their mouths and horrible things from between their teeth. 📖

They too will become a remnant for our God.

They will become like a clan in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusites.

**Help with “remnant”:** This means the people who remain.

## Zechariah 9:8

I will camp around My house because of the army and those marching back and forth. 📖

No oppressor will overrun My people again.

Now I watch with My own eyes.”

## Zechariah 9:9

Daughter of Zion, rejoice greatly! 📖

Daughter of Jerusalem, shout in victory!

Your King comes to you, righteous and victorious, humble and riding a donkey.

He rides a colt, a donkey’s young foal.

**About the daughters:** Zion and Jerusalem are pictured as women.

Righteous means right and just.

## Zechariah 9:10

God says, “I will remove the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem. 📖

The battle bow will be broken.

He will announce peace to the nations.

His rule will reach from sea to sea, from the Euphrates to the earth’s ends.

## Zechariah 9:11

Because of My covenant’s blood, I will free your prisoners from the pit without water. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Zechariah 9:12

Prisoners of hope, return to your defended place! 📖

Even today, I declare that I will restore double to you.

## Zechariah 9:13

I will bend Judah as My bow and fit it with Ephraim. 📖

Zion, I will stir your sons against Greece’s sons.

I will make you like a mighty man’s sword.”

## Zechariah 9:14

The LORD will appear above them, and His arrow will fly like lightning. 📖

The Lord GOD will sound the ram’s horn and advance in whirlwinds from the south.

**Help with “ram’s horn”:** This is a male sheep’s horn used as a trumpet.

## Zechariah 9:15

The LORD of Hosts will protect them. 📖

They will destroy and conquer with slingstones and drink and roar as with wine.

They will be filled like sprinkling bowls and drenched like the altar’s corners.

**Help with these pictures:** Slingstones are stones thrown from a sling.

Sprinkling bowls hold liquid used in worship.

The passage uses strong battle and altar images.

## Zechariah 9:16

That day, the LORD their God will save them as His people’s flock. 📖

They will shine over His land like jewels in a crown.

## Zechariah 9:17

How lovely and beautiful they will be! 📖

Grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine will make the young women flourish.
