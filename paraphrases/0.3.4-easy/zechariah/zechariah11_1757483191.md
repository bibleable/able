# Zechariah 11 — The flock and the rejected shepherd

**About this chapter**

The chapter describes ruined trees and a flock marked for slaughter.

The shepherd breaks two named staffs and receives thirty pieces of silver.

A worthless shepherd and severe judgment follow.

## Zechariah 11:1

Lebanon, open your doors so fire can burn up your cedars! 📖

## Zechariah 11:2

Cypress, wail! 📖

The cedar has fallen, and the majestic trees are ruined.

Oaks of Bashan, wail!

The thick forest has been cut down.

## Zechariah 11:3

Listen to the shepherds wailing because their glory is ruined. 📖

Listen to young lions roaring because the Jordan’s thickets are destroyed.

## Zechariah 11:4

The LORD my God says, “Feed the flock marked for slaughter. 📖

## Zechariah 11:5

The buyers kill them without regret. 📖

The sellers say, ‘Praise the LORD!

I am rich!’

Even their own shepherds have no compassion on them.

## Zechariah 11:6

I will no longer have compassion on the land’s people, declares the LORD. 📖

I will hand each person over to his neighbor and his king.

They will ruin the land, and I will not rescue it from their hands.”

## Zechariah 11:7

I fed the flock marked for slaughter, especially its suffering sheep. 📖

I took two staffs and named them Favor and Union.

I fed the flock.

## Zechariah 11:8

In one month, I dismissed three shepherds. 📖

I became impatient with the flock, and they hated me too.

## Zechariah 11:9

I said, “I will no longer shepherd you. 📖

Let the dying die and the perishing perish.

Let those remaining eat one another’s flesh.”

## Zechariah 11:10

I took my staff Favor and cut it in two. 📖

This canceled the covenant I had made with all the nations.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Zechariah 11:11

It was canceled that day. 📖

The suffering sheep watching me knew this was the LORD’s word.

## Zechariah 11:12

I told them, “If you think it right, give me my wages. 📖

If not, keep them.”

They weighed out thirty pieces of silver as my wages.

## Zechariah 11:13

The LORD told me, “Throw it to the potter.” 📖

This was the magnificent price at which they valued me.

I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw them to the potter in the LORD’s house.

**About the price:** The passage describes this price with bitter irony.

## Zechariah 11:14

I cut my second staff, Union, in two. 📖

This broke the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

## Zechariah 11:15

The LORD told me, “Take the equipment of a foolish shepherd again. 📖

## Zechariah 11:16

I will raise a shepherd in the land who will not care for the lost or seek the young. 📖

He will not heal the injured or feed the healthy.

He will eat the best sheep’s flesh and tear off their hooves.

## Zechariah 11:17

How terrible for the worthless shepherd who abandons the flock! 📖

May a sword strike his arm and right eye!

May his arm dry up completely and his right eye become completely blind!”

**About this judgment:** These words announce harm to the unfaithful shepherd.

They do not describe blindness or physical impairment as moral faults.
