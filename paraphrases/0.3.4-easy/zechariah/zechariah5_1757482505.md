# Zechariah 5 — The flying scroll and the basket

**About this chapter**

Zechariah sees a flying scroll carrying a curse.

He also sees a basket containing a woman called Wickedness.

The angel explains where the basket is taken.

## Zechariah 5:1

I looked up again and saw a flying scroll before me. 📖

**Help with “scroll”:** This is a long piece of writing material that can be rolled up.

## Zechariah 5:2

The angel asked, “What do you see?” 📖

I replied, “A flying scroll, twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide.”

**Help with “cubit”:** This measures from elbow to fingertips.

## Zechariah 5:3

He told me, “This is the curse going out over all the land. 📖

One side of the scroll says every thief will be removed.

The other side says everyone who swears falsely will be removed.

## Zechariah 5:4

I will send it out, declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

It will enter the thief’s house and the house of anyone swearing falsely by My name.

It will stay there and destroy the house, including its wood and stones.”

## Zechariah 5:5

The angel speaking with me came forward. 📖

He told me, “Look up now and see what is coming.”

## Zechariah 5:6

I asked, “What is it?” 📖

He replied, “A measuring basket is going out.”

He added, “This is their wrongdoing in all the land.”

## Zechariah 5:7

The lead cover was lifted, and a woman sat inside the basket. 📖

## Zechariah 5:8

He said, “This is Wickedness.” 📖

He pushed her into the basket and pushed the lead cover over its opening.

**About the woman:** Wickedness is pictured as a woman in this vision.

The picture does not say that women as a group are wicked.

## Zechariah 5:9

I looked up and saw two women coming, with wind in their wings. 📖

Their wings were like a stork’s wings.

They lifted the basket between heaven and earth.

## Zechariah 5:10

I asked the angel speaking with me, “Where are they taking the basket?” 📖

## Zechariah 5:11

He replied, “To build a house for it in Shinar. 📖

When it is ready, the basket will be placed there on its raised base.”
