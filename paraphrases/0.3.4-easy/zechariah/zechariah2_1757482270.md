# Zechariah 2 — Jerusalem measured and protected

**About this chapter**

Zechariah sees a man measuring Jerusalem.

The LORD promises to protect the city, dwell among His people, and gather many nations.

The draft keeps the passage’s changes between “His” and “Me.”

## Zechariah 2:1

I looked up and saw a man holding a measuring line. 📖

## Zechariah 2:2

I asked, “Where are you going?” 📖

He replied, “To measure Jerusalem’s width and length.”

## Zechariah 2:3

The angel speaking with me went out. 📖

Another angel came to meet him

## Zechariah 2:4

and said, “Run and tell that young man this. 📖

Jerusalem will be a city without walls, because it will have so many people and animals.

## Zechariah 2:5

I will be a wall of fire around it, declares the LORD. 📖

I will be the glory within it.”

## Zechariah 2:6

The LORD declares, “Get up! 📖

Get up!

Flee from the northern land.

I scattered you like heaven’s four winds, declares the LORD.

## Zechariah 2:7

Get up, Zion! 📖

Escape, you who live with the Daughter of Babylon!”

**Help with “Daughter of Babylon”:** Babylon and its people are pictured as a woman.

## Zechariah 2:8

The LORD of Hosts says, “After His Glory has sent Me against the nations that robbed you, I will act. 📖

Whoever touches you touches the apple of His eye.

**Help with “apple of His eye”:** This pictures something especially precious and carefully protected.

**About the speaker:** The source uses “His Glory” and “Me.”

The draft does not add another name for the speaker.

## Zechariah 2:9

I will certainly wave My hand over them. 📖

Their own servants will rob them.

Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts sent Me.”

## Zechariah 2:10

The LORD declares, “Daughter of Zion, shout with joy and be glad! 📖

I am coming to live among you.

## Zechariah 2:11

On that day, many nations will join the LORD and become My people. 📖

I will live among you.

You will know that the LORD of Hosts sent Me to you.

## Zechariah 2:12

The LORD will take Judah as His own portion in the Holy Land. 📖

He will choose Jerusalem again.

## Zechariah 2:13

All people, be silent before the LORD! 📖

He has risen from His holy home.”
