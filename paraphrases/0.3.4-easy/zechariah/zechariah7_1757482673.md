# Zechariah 7 — Fasting, justice, and refusal to listen

**About this chapter**

People ask whether they should continue fasting.

The LORD asks whom their fasting serves.

He recalls commands for justice and compassion, and the ancestors’ refusal to listen.

## Zechariah 7:1

The LORD’s word came to Zechariah in King Darius’s fourth year. 📖

It was the fourth day of the ninth month, Chislev.

## Zechariah 7:2

Bethel’s people sent Sharezer, Regem-melech, and their men to plead before the LORD. 📖

## Zechariah 7:3

They asked the priests of the LORD of Hosts’ house and the prophets a question. 📖

They said, “Should I weep and fast in the fifth month, as I have for many years?”

**Help with “fast”:** This means going without food for a time.

## Zechariah 7:4

The word of the LORD of Hosts came to me. 📖

## Zechariah 7:5

He said, “Ask all the land’s people and the priests this. 📖

For seventy years, you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months.

Was your fasting really for Me?

## Zechariah 7:6

When you ate and drank, were you not simply doing it for yourselves? 📖

## Zechariah 7:7

Are these not the words the LORD announced through the earlier prophets? 📖

Jerusalem and its surrounding towns were then full of people and prospering.

People also lived in the Negev and foothills.”

## Zechariah 7:8

The LORD’s word came to Zechariah. 📖

## Zechariah 7:9

The LORD of Hosts says, “Give true justice. 📖

Show faithful love and compassion to one another.

## Zechariah 7:10

Do not oppress widows, children without fathers, foreigners, or poor people. 📖

Do not plan evil against one another in your hearts.

**Help with “oppress”:** This means treating people cruelly and unfairly.

## Zechariah 7:11

But they refused to pay attention. 📖

They turned a stubborn shoulder and blocked their ears from hearing.

**Help with the shoulder picture:** It describes deliberately turning away.

## Zechariah 7:12

They made their hearts like flint and would not listen to the law. 📖

They would not hear the words the LORD of Hosts sent by His Spirit through the earlier prophets.

So great anger came from the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with “flint”:** This is a hard stone.

It pictures their stubborn refusal to listen.

## Zechariah 7:13

I called, and they would not listen. 📖

So when they called, I would not listen, says the LORD of Hosts.

## Zechariah 7:14

I scattered them like a whirlwind among all the nations they had not known. 📖

The land was left empty behind them, and no one could come or go.

They turned the pleasant land into ruins.”
