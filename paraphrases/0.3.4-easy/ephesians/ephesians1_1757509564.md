# Ephesians 1 — God's blessings in Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul greets the believers and praises God for what he has done in Christ.

Paul prays that they will know God's hope, riches, and power.

## Ephesians 1:1

This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus because God wanted this. 📖

It is for God's people in Ephesus, who are faithful in Christ Jesus.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Ephesians 1:2

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace. 📖

## Ephesians 1:3

Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! 📖

In Christ, he has given us every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places.

## Ephesians 1:4

God chose us in Christ before the world was made. 📖

He chose us to be holy and without blame before him.

He acted in love.

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Ephesians 1:5

God decided beforehand to adopt us as his children through Jesus Christ. 📖

He did this because it pleased him and was what he wanted.

**Help with a word:** Adoption means welcoming someone into a family as a son or daughter.

## Ephesians 1:6

This brings praise to God's glorious kindness. 📖

He freely gave us this kindness in the One he loves.

**Help with words:** Glorious describes God's wonderful greatness.

The One he loves is Jesus Christ.

## Ephesians 1:7

In Christ, his blood has set us free. 📖

Our wrongdoing is forgiven because God's kindness is so rich.

**Help with this picture:** His blood refers to Christ's death for us.

## Ephesians 1:8

God poured out this kindness on us with all wisdom and understanding. 📖

## Ephesians 1:9

God made known the mystery of what he wants. 📖

This was the purpose he was pleased to plan in Christ.

**Help with a word:** Mystery here means something once hidden that God now reveals.

## Ephesians 1:10

God's plan is for the time when everything is ready. 📖

He will bring all things in heaven and on earth together in Christ.

## Ephesians 1:11

In Christ, we were also chosen to belong to God. 📖

God decided this beforehand, according to his plan.

He works out everything according to what he decides and wants.

## Ephesians 1:12

We were the first to hope in Christ. 📖

God intended us to bring praise to his glory.

**Help with a word:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## Ephesians 1:13

You also heard and believed the true message, the good news of your salvation. 📖

In Christ, you were marked with a seal, the promised Holy Spirit.

**Help with words:** Salvation means being saved by God.

A seal is a mark showing ownership or confirming a promise.

## Ephesians 1:14

The Spirit guarantees our inheritance until God sets his own people free. 📖

This brings praise to his glory.

**Help with a word:** An inheritance is something received as a family member.

Here it pictures what God gives his people.

## Ephesians 1:15

I have heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all God's people. 📖

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in Jesus here.

## Ephesians 1:16

Ever since then, I have not stopped thanking God for you. 📖

I remember you in my prayers.

## Ephesians 1:17

I pray to the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father. 📖

May he give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation as you come to know him.

**Help with a word:** Revelation means God making something known.

## Ephesians 1:18

I ask God to give light to the eyes of your heart. 📖

Then you can know the hope of his call and the riches of his glorious inheritance among his people.

**Help with this picture:** Eyes of the heart pictures understanding within a person.

## Ephesians 1:19

May you also know how wonderfully great his power is for us who believe. 📖

This power comes from his mighty strength at work.

## Ephesians 1:20

God used this strength in Christ when he raised him from the dead. 📖

He seated Christ at his right hand in the heavenly places.

**Help with this picture:** The right hand is a place of great honour and authority.

## Ephesians 1:21

Christ is far above every ruler, authority, power, and kingdom. 📖

He is above every name, both now and in the time to come.

## Ephesians 1:22

God placed everything under Christ's feet. 📖

He made Christ the head over everything for the church.

**Help with these pictures:** Under his feet means under his rule.

The head pictures Christ's leading place.

## Ephesians 1:23

The church is Christ's body. 📖

It is the fullness of the One who fills everything in every way.

**Help with this picture:** A body pictures the church's close connection with Christ.
