# Ephesians 2 — God saves us and brings us together

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to believers about new life in Jesus.

**Help with words:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Ephesians 2:1

You were dead because of your sins and wrong actions. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Dead describes being cut off from life with God.

## Ephesians 2:2

You used to live in those sins. 📖

You followed this world's ways and the ruler of the power of the air.

That spirit now works in people who disobey God.

**Help with this title:** The ruler here is an evil spiritual power.

## Ephesians 2:3

We all once lived among them. 📖

We followed our sinful wants and thoughts.

Like everyone else, our nature put us under God's anger.

## Ephesians 2:4

But God's mercy is very great. 📖

God loved us deeply.

**Help with a word:** Mercy means showing kindness to people who need forgiveness and help.

## Ephesians 2:5

Even when we were dead in our sins, God made us alive with Christ. 📖

You have been saved by God's freely given kindness!

## Ephesians 2:6

God raised us with Christ. 📖

In Christ Jesus, God seated us with him in the heavenly places.

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes believers sharing in Christ's life and place before God.

## Ephesians 2:7

In the ages to come, God will show how rich his kindness is. 📖

He shows this kindness to us in Christ Jesus.

## Ephesians 2:8

You have been saved by God's freely given kindness, through faith. 📖

This does not come from you.

It is God's gift.

## Ephesians 2:9

You cannot earn this gift by your work. 📖

So no one can boast about earning it.

## Ephesians 2:10

God has made us what we are. 📖

In Christ Jesus, God created us to do good things.

God prepared these things beforehand for us to do.

## Ephesians 2:11

Remember that you were born Gentiles. 📖

People who were circumcised called you “uncircumcised.”

Their circumcision was something done to the body by human hands.

**Help with a word:** Circumcision removes skin from the end of a male's penis.

## Ephesians 2:12

At that time you were apart from Christ. 📖

You did not belong to Israel.

You had no share in God's agreements and promises.

You lived in the world without hope and without God.

## Ephesians 2:13

Once you were far away. 📖

Now, in Christ Jesus, his blood has brought you near.

**Help with this picture:** His blood points to his death for us.

## Ephesians 2:14

Christ himself is our peace. 📖

He made Jews and Gentiles one people.

He broke down the wall of hatred dividing them.

## Ephesians 2:15

Through his body, Christ ended the law with its commands and rules. 📖

He made the 2 groups into one new people in himself.

In this way he made peace.

## Ephesians 2:16

Through the cross, Christ brought both groups back to God in one body. 📖

He ended their hatred of each other.

## Ephesians 2:17

Christ came with the good news of peace. 📖

He spoke to those far away and those near.

## Ephesians 2:18

Through Christ, both groups can come to the Father by the same Spirit. 📖

## Ephesians 2:19

You are no longer strangers or foreigners. 📖

You are fellow citizens with God's people.

You are members of God's family.

**Help with a word:** Citizens belong to the same country or community.

## Ephesians 2:20

The apostles and prophets are the foundation of this family. 📖

Christ Jesus himself is the most important foundation stone.

**Help with this picture:** God's people are compared to a building.

Apostles are messengers Jesus sent, and prophets speak God's messages.

## Ephesians 2:21

In Christ, the whole building fits together. 📖

It grows into a holy temple in the Lord.

**Help with words:** A holy temple is a place set apart for God.

## Ephesians 2:22

In Christ, you too are being built together. 📖

Through his Spirit, God lives among you.
