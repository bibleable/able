# Ephesians 5 — Live in love and light

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches believers to live in love, light, and wisdom.

He gives instructions about marriage and compares Christ's care for the church with a husband's love for his wife.

## Ephesians 5:1

Follow God's example as children whom he loves. 📖

## Ephesians 5:2

Live in love, just as Christ loved us and gave himself for us. 📖

He was a sweet-smelling offering and sacrifice to God.

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes Christ giving himself as an offering that pleases God.

## Ephesians 5:3

Among you, there must not be even a hint of wrong sexual behaviour, impurity, or greed. 📖

These things do not fit God's holy people.

**Help with words:** Impurity here means moral wrongdoing, rather than having a dirty body.

Greed means always wanting more for yourself.

Holy means set apart for God.

## Ephesians 5:4

There must be no filthy talk, foolish talk, or rude jokes. 📖

These do not fit your new life.

Instead, give thanks.

## Ephesians 5:5

You can be sure of this. 📖

No sexually immoral, impure, or greedy person has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.

Greed is worshipping an idol.

**Help with words:** An inheritance is something received as a family member.

God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

An idol is something worshipped in God's place.

## Ephesians 5:6

Do not let anyone trick you with empty words. 📖

Because of these things, God's anger is coming on people who disobey.

## Ephesians 5:7

So do not join them in these things. 📖

## Ephesians 5:8

You were once darkness. 📖

Now you are light in the Lord.

Live as children of light.

**Help with these pictures:** Darkness pictures the former life of wrongdoing.

Light pictures the new life in the Lord.

## Ephesians 5:9

The fruit of the light is every kind of goodness, right living, and truth. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Fruit means the results that come from living in the light.

## Ephesians 5:10

Find out and make sure of what pleases the Lord. 📖

## Ephesians 5:11

Do not share in the useless deeds of darkness. 📖

Instead, expose them.

**Help with a word:** Expose means to show what something really is.

## Ephesians 5:12

It is shameful even to speak about what disobedient people do in secret. 📖

## Ephesians 5:13

Everything exposed by the light becomes visible. 📖

Everything lit up becomes a light itself.

## Ephesians 5:14

That is why it is said, “Wake up, sleeper! 📖

Rise from the dead.

Christ will shine on you.”

**Help with these pictures:** Sleeping and being dead describe the need to wake to the life Christ gives.

## Ephesians 5:15

Be very careful about how you live. 📖

Live wisely, rather than unwisely.

## Ephesians 5:16

Make the best use of your time, because the days are evil. 📖

## Ephesians 5:17

So do not be foolish. 📖

Understand what the Lord wants.

## Ephesians 5:18

Do not get drunk on wine, which leads to reckless behaviour. 📖

Instead, be filled with the Spirit.

## Ephesians 5:19

Speak to each other with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs. 📖

Sing and make music in your hearts to the Lord.

**Help with words:** These are different kinds of worship songs.

## Ephesians 5:20

Always thank God the Father for everything in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## Ephesians 5:21

Submit to each other out of deep respect for Christ. 📖

**Help with a word:** Submit means to accept another person's authority or place yourself under them.

## Ephesians 5:22

Wives, submit to your husbands as to the Lord. 📖

## Ephesians 5:23

The husband is the head of the wife, as Christ is the head of the church. 📖

The church is his body, and he is its Saviour.

**Help with these pictures:** Head describes a leading place.

A body pictures the church's close connection with Christ.

A Saviour rescues people.

## Ephesians 5:24

The church submits to Christ. 📖

In the same way, wives should submit to their husbands in everything.

## Ephesians 5:25

Husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the church and gave himself for her. 📖

## Ephesians 5:26

He did this to make her holy. 📖

He cleansed her by washing with water through the word.

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes Christ making the church clean and set apart for God.

## Ephesians 5:27

He did this to present the church to himself in glory. 📖

She would have no stain, wrinkle, or any other fault.

She would be holy and without blame.

**Help with this picture:** The church is pictured as a person made ready for Christ.

## Ephesians 5:28

In the same way, husbands should love their wives as their own bodies. 📖

A man who loves his wife loves himself.

## Ephesians 5:29

No one ever hated his own body. 📖

He feeds it and cares for it, just as Christ cares for the church.

## Ephesians 5:30

We are members of Christ's body. 📖

## Ephesians 5:31

“For this reason, a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife. 📖

The two will become one flesh.”

**Help with this picture:** One flesh describes the close union of husband and wife in marriage.

## Ephesians 5:32

This mystery is very deep. 📖

I am speaking about Christ and the church.

## Ephesians 5:33

Each of you must love his wife as he loves himself. 📖

The wife must respect her husband.
