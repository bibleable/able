# Ephesians 6 — Stand firm in God's strength

**About this chapter**

Paul gives instructions to children, fathers, slaves, and masters.

He uses armour as a picture of the strength and protection God gives.

He finishes with requests for prayer and greetings.

## Ephesians 6:1

Children, obey your parents in the Lord, because this is right. 📖

## Ephesians 6:2

“Honour your father and mother.” 📖

This is the first command with a promise.

## Ephesians 6:3

The promise is “that things may go well for you and you may live long on the earth.” 📖

## Ephesians 6:4

Fathers, do not make your children angry. 📖

Instead, bring them up with the Lord's training and teaching.

## Ephesians 6:5

Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect and fear, with sincere hearts, just as you would obey Christ. 📖

**Help with a word:** Slaves were people owned by others and forced to serve them.

Paul is addressing the household system of his time.

## Ephesians 6:6

Do not work only to please them while they watch you. 📖

Work as Christ's servants, doing what God wants from your heart.

## Ephesians 6:7

Serve willingly, as serving the Lord rather than people. 📖

## Ephesians 6:8

You know the Lord will reward each person for whatever good they do. 📖

This is true whether they are a slave or free.

## Ephesians 6:9

Masters, act in the same way towards your slaves. 📖

Stop threatening them.

Their Master and yours is in heaven.

He does not favour one person over another.

## Ephesians 6:10

Finally, be strong in the Lord and his mighty power. 📖

## Ephesians 6:11

Put on all of God's armour. 📖

Then you can stand against the devil's plans.

**Help with this picture:** Armour is protective clothing worn by soldiers.

Paul uses armour to picture God's protection and help in resisting evil.

## Ephesians 6:12

Our struggle is not against human beings. 📖

It is against rulers, authorities, the powers of this world's darkness, and evil spiritual forces in the heavenly places.

## Ephesians 6:13

So take up all of God's armour. 📖

Then, when the evil day comes, you can stand your ground.

After doing everything, you can still stand firm.

## Ephesians 6:14

Stand firm, with truth buckled like a belt around your waist. 📖

Wear right living like a breastplate.

**Help with a word:** A breastplate protects a soldier's chest.

## Ephesians 6:15

Fit your feet with the readiness that comes from the good news of peace. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul pictures this readiness as footwear for a soldier.

## Ephesians 6:16

Along with all this, take up the shield of faith. 📖

With it, you can put out all the evil one's burning arrows.

**Help with words:** A shield protects a soldier from attacks.

Faith means trust in God here.

## Ephesians 6:17

Take the helmet of salvation and the Spirit's sword, which is God's word. 📖

**Help with words:** A helmet protects a person's head.

Salvation means being saved by God.

God's word is pictured as a sword in this struggle against evil.

## Ephesians 6:18

Pray in the Spirit at all times, with every kind of prayer and request. 📖

Stay alert and keep praying for all God's people without giving up.

## Ephesians 6:19

Pray for me too. 📖

Ask that I may be given words whenever I speak.

Then I can boldly make known the mystery of the good news.

**Help with a word:** Mystery here means something once hidden that God now reveals.

## Ephesians 6:20

I am an ambassador in chains for this good news. 📖

Pray that I will speak about it without fear, as I should.

**Help with a word:** An ambassador represents someone and carries their message.

Paul serves Christ while he is a prisoner.

## Ephesians 6:21

Tychicus will tell you everything about me and what I am doing. 📖

He is a dear brother and a faithful servant in the Lord.

## Ephesians 6:22

I have sent him so that you can know how we are. 📖

He will encourage your hearts.

## Ephesians 6:23

May God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give the brothers and sisters peace, love, and faith. 📖

## Ephesians 6:24

May God's kindness be with everyone who loves our Lord Jesus Christ with a love that never dies. 📖
