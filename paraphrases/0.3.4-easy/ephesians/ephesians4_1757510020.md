# Ephesians 4 — One body and a new way of life

**About this chapter**

Paul urges believers to stay united and grow together in Christ.

He teaches them to leave their old life and live truthfully with each other.

## Ephesians 4:1

As a prisoner in the Lord, I urge you to live in a way worthy of your calling. 📖

## Ephesians 4:2

Be completely humble and gentle. 📖

Be patient, putting up with each other in love.

## Ephesians 4:3

Work hard to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace. 📖

**Help with these words:** Unity means being joined together.

Peace is pictured as a bond that holds people together.

## Ephesians 4:4

There is one body and one Spirit. 📖

God called you to one hope when he called you.

**Help with this picture:** One body pictures believers belonging together in Christ.

## Ephesians 4:5

There is one Lord, one faith, and one baptism. 📖

**Help with words:** Faith means trust in Christ here.

Baptism uses water as a sign of belonging to Christ.

## Ephesians 4:6

There is one God and Father of all. 📖

He is over all, through all, and in all.

## Ephesians 4:7

Christ has given each of us kindness according to the measure of his gift. 📖

## Ephesians 4:8

That is why the Scriptures say, “When he went up on high, he led captives away. 📖

He gave gifts to people.”

**Help with words:** The Scriptures are the holy writings.

Captives are people taken prisoner.

## Ephesians 4:9

What does “he went up” mean? 📖

It means that he also went down to the lower parts of the earth.

**Help with this passage:** Paul contrasts going down with going up above the heavens.

## Ephesians 4:10

The One who went down is also the One who went up above all the heavens. 📖

He did this to fill all things.

## Ephesians 4:11

Christ gave some people to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers. 📖

**Help with words:** Apostles are sent out for a special task.

Prophets speak God's message.

Evangelists tell the good news.

Pastors care for and lead believers.

Teachers help people learn.

## Ephesians 4:12

They prepare God's people for works of service and build up Christ's body. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Christ's body means the church.

## Ephesians 4:13

This continues until we all are united in faith and in knowing the Son of God. 📖

We are to become mature, reaching the full measure of Christ's stature.

**Help with this picture:** Paul pictures growing in faith as growing from a child to an adult.

Stature means full growth here.

## Ephesians 4:14

Then we will no longer be babies, tossed about by waves and blown around by every wind of teaching. 📖

We will not be carried away by clever people and their dishonest plans.

**Help with these pictures:** Paul pictures people easily led astray as babies or boats pushed around by wind and waves.

## Ephesians 4:15

Instead, speak the truth in love. 📖

In every way, we will grow into Christ himself, who is the head.

## Ephesians 4:16

From Christ, the whole body is fitted and held together by every supporting ligament. 📖

As each part does its work, the body grows and builds itself up in love.

**Help with this picture:** Believers are pictured as parts of a body joined together under Christ, the head.

Ligaments are strong bands connecting parts of a body.

## Ephesians 4:17

I insist on this in the Lord. 📖

You must no longer live as the Gentiles do, with their empty thinking.

**Help with a word:** Gentiles means people who are not Jews.

Paul contrasts the believers' new life with the way of life they left.

## Ephesians 4:18

Their understanding is darkened, and they are separated from God's life. 📖

This is because they do not know him, since their hearts are hard.

**Help with this picture:** Hard hearts describes being stubborn and unwilling to listen.

## Ephesians 4:19

They have lost their sense of shame. 📖

They give themselves to uncontrolled sexual behaviour and every kind of impurity.

They keep wanting more.

## Ephesians 4:20

But that is not how you came to know Christ. 📖

## Ephesians 4:21

Surely you heard about him and were taught in him, according to the truth in Jesus. 📖

## Ephesians 4:22

You were taught to take off your old self and your former way of life. 📖

Its deceitful desires are ruining it.

**Help with this picture:** Taking off old clothes pictures leaving the old way of life.

Deceitful desires promise good but lead people into wrong.

## Ephesians 4:23

You were taught to be made new in the spirit of your minds. 📖

**Help with these words:** Paul calls for a new inner way of thinking.

## Ephesians 4:24

You were taught to put on the new self. 📖

It is created to be like God in true right living and holiness.

**Help with these words:** Putting on new clothes pictures living the new life God gives.

Holiness means being set apart for God.

## Ephesians 4:25

So each of you must put away lies and speak truthfully to your neighbour. 📖

We are all members of one another.

## Ephesians 4:26

“Be angry, but do not sin.” 📖

Do not let the sun go down while you are still angry.

**Help with a word:** Sin is wrongdoing against God.

## Ephesians 4:27

Do not give the devil a foothold. 📖

**Help with this picture:** A foothold is a place to stand or begin climbing.

Paul means not giving the devil a chance to gain influence.

## Ephesians 4:28

Anyone who has been stealing must stop. 📖

Instead, work with your own hands, doing something good.

Then you will have something to share with people in need.

## Ephesians 4:29

Do not let harmful talk come from your mouths. 📖

Speak only what helps people grow according to their needs.

Let your words bring kindness to those who listen.

## Ephesians 4:30

Do not make God's Holy Spirit sad. 📖

You were marked with his seal for the day of being set free.

**Help with this picture:** A seal is a mark showing ownership or confirming a promise.

## Ephesians 4:31

Get rid of bitterness, rage, anger, shouting, harmful lies, and every wish to hurt others. 📖

**Help with a word:** Bitterness here means holding on to angry, resentful feelings.

## Ephesians 4:32

Be kind and tender towards each other. 📖

Forgive each other, just as God forgave you in Christ.
