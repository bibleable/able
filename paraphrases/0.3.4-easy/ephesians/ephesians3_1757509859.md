# Ephesians 3 — Christ's love is beyond measure

**About this chapter**

Paul describes his task of sharing the good news with people who are not Jews.

He prays for believers to be strong and know Christ's love.

## Ephesians 3:1

For this reason, I, Paul, am a prisoner of Christ Jesus for you Gentiles. 📖

**Help with a word:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

Paul returns to his prayer in verse 14 after explaining his work.

## Ephesians 3:2

Surely you have heard about the task God gave me for you through his kindness. 📖

## Ephesians 3:3

God revealed his mystery to me. 📖

I have already written briefly about it.

**Help with a word:** Mystery here means something once hidden that God now reveals.

## Ephesians 3:4

When you read this, you can understand what I know about the mystery of Christ. 📖

## Ephesians 3:5

People in earlier generations did not know this mystery as it is known now. 📖

The Spirit has now revealed it to God's holy apostles and prophets.

**Help with words:** Apostles are people sent out for a special task.

Prophets speak God's message.

Holy means set apart for God.

## Ephesians 3:6

Through the good news, Gentiles share the inheritance and belong to the same body. 📖

They share the promise in Christ Jesus.

**Help with these words:** Gentile believers share these gifts with Jewish believers.

The body pictures God's people belonging together.

## Ephesians 3:7

God's freely given kindness made me a servant of this good news. 📖

He gave me this gift through his power at work.

## Ephesians 3:8

I am less than the least of all God's people. 📖

Yet God gave me this kindness.

I am to tell Gentiles about Christ's riches, which are beyond measure.

## Ephesians 3:9

I am to help everyone see how God carries out this mystery. 📖

For ages, it was hidden in God, who made all things.

## Ephesians 3:10

God intended the church to make known his wisdom in all its many forms. 📖

Now his wisdom is made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places.

## Ephesians 3:11

This follows God's eternal purpose, which he carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord. 📖

**Help with a word:** Eternal means lasting for ever.

## Ephesians 3:12

In Christ and through faith in him, we can come before God boldly and confidently. 📖

## Ephesians 3:13

So please do not lose courage because I suffer for you. 📖

My sufferings are your glory.

**Help with a word:** Glory here speaks of honour, rather than disgrace.

## Ephesians 3:14

For this reason, I kneel before the Father. 📖

## Ephesians 3:15

Every family in heaven and on earth receives its name from him. 📖

## Ephesians 3:16

I ask him to draw on the riches of his glory. 📖

May he strengthen your inner being with power through his Spirit.

**Help with these words:** Inner being means who you are inside.

## Ephesians 3:17

May Christ live in your hearts through faith. 📖

May you be rooted and firmly grounded in love.

**Help with these pictures:** A heart pictures the inner life.

Roots and a firm foundation picture being held securely in love.

## Ephesians 3:18

Together with all God's people, may you have power to understand its length, width, height, and depth. 📖

**Help with the sentence:** Paul is describing Christ's love, named in the next verse.

## Ephesians 3:19

May you know Christ's love, which goes beyond knowledge. 📖

May you be filled with all God's fullness.

## Ephesians 3:20

God can do far more than anything we ask or imagine. 📖

He does this through his power at work in us.

## Ephesians 3:21

May God receive glory in the church and in Christ Jesus through every generation, for ever and ever. 📖

Amen.

**Help with a word:** Amen expresses agreement with a prayer.
