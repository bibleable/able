# Nahum 1 — The LORD’s message against Nineveh

**About this chapter**

Nahum records a vision about Nineveh.

The message describes the LORD’s anger against enemies and care for those who trust him.

## Nahum 1:1

This is the message against Nineveh. 📖

This book records the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

**Help with vision:** A vision is something God shows a person.

## Nahum 1:2

The LORD is a jealous God who takes revenge. 📖

The LORD takes revenge and is full of anger.

The LORD takes revenge on those who oppose him.

He keeps anger for his enemies.

**Help with jealous:** Here, jealous means demanding loyalty.

**Help with revenge:** Revenge means paying back wrong done.

## Nahum 1:3

The LORD is slow to anger and great in power. 📖

He will certainly not leave guilty people without punishment.

His path is in the whirlwind and storm.

Clouds are like dust beneath his feet.

**Help with this picture:** The storm and clouds describe the LORD’s great power.

## Nahum 1:4

The LORD speaks sternly to the sea and dries it up. 📖

He makes all the rivers dry.

Bashan and Carmel dry up.

The flowers of Lebanon wilt.

## Nahum 1:5

The mountains shake before the LORD, and the hills melt away. 📖

The earth trembles in his presence.

The world and everyone in it tremble.

## Nahum 1:6

Who can stand against his fierce anger? 📖

Who can bear his burning anger?

His anger pours out like fire.

Even rocks break apart before him.

**Help with fire:** Fire pictures the force of the LORD’s anger.

## Nahum 1:7

The LORD is good. 📖

He is a strong place of safety in times of trouble.

He cares for those who trust him.

## Nahum 1:8

But with an overwhelming flood, the LORD will put an end to Nineveh. 📖

He will chase his enemies into darkness.

## Nahum 1:9

Whatever you plan against the LORD, he will bring to an end. 📖

Trouble will not rise up a 2nd time.

## Nahum 1:10

They will be tangled up as with thorns. 📖

They will be used up like the drink of someone who is drunk.

They will be consumed like completely dry stalks.

**Help with these pictures:** Thorns, drink, and dry stalks picture the enemies’ destruction.

## Nahum 1:11

Nineveh, someone comes from you who plans evil against the LORD. 📖

He advises wickedness.

**Help with wickedness:** Wickedness means serious wrongdoing.

## Nahum 1:12

The LORD says, “Even though they are allies and very many, they will be cut down and pass away. 📖

Judah, although I have made you suffer, I will make you suffer no longer.

**Help with allies:** Allies are people or nations who agree to help each other.

## Nahum 1:13

I will now break their yoke from your neck. 📖

I will tear away your chains.”

**Help with yoke:** A yoke is a wooden frame placed on working animals.

Here, the yoke and chains picture Judah being under another power.

## Nahum 1:14

The LORD has given this command about you, Nineveh. 📖

“You will have no descendants to carry on your name.

I will remove carved images and cast idols from the house of your gods.

I will prepare your grave, because you are contemptible.”

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

Cast idols are made by pouring material into a mould.

**Help with contemptible:** Contemptible means deserving strong dislike and scorn.

## Nahum 1:15

Look at the mountains! 📖

Someone is coming with good news, announcing peace!

Judah, celebrate your feasts and keep your vows.

The wicked will never again march through you.

They will be completely cut off.

**Help with vows:** Vows are serious promises.
