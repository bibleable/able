# Nahum 2 — Nineveh is attacked

**About this chapter**

Nahum describes an attack on Nineveh.

The chapter compares the city’s power and violence to lions.

## Nahum 2:1

Nineveh, one who scatters is coming against you. 📖

Guard the fortress!

Watch the road!

Get ready!

Gather all your strength!

**Help with fortress:** A fortress is a strong place protected from attack.

## Nahum 2:2

The LORD will restore Jacob’s splendour, like Israel’s splendour. 📖

Destroyers have ruined them and the branches of their vine.

**Help with splendour:** Splendour means greatness and beauty.

**Help with the vine:** The people’s life and strength are pictured as a vine with branches.

## Nahum 2:3

The attacker’s mighty men have red shields. 📖

The brave warriors wear bright red clothes.

The metal parts of the chariots flash like fire on the day they are made ready.

Cypress spears are waved.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

**Help with cypress:** Cypress is a kind of tree whose wood can be used for spears.

## Nahum 2:4

The chariots race through the streets and rush around the open squares. 📖

They look like burning torches and dart like lightning.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

## Nahum 2:5

He calls his nobles. 📖

They stumble as they move forward.

They race to the wall, and the protective shield is put in place.

**Help with nobles:** Nobles are important leaders.

**About he:** The source does not name this person at this verse.

The draft does not supply a name.

## Nahum 2:6

The river gates are thrown open. 📖

The palace falls down.

## Nahum 2:7

It has been ordered that the city be sent into exile and carried away. 📖

Her servant women moan like doves and beat their chests.

**Help with exile:** Exile means being forced away from your own land.

**Help with doves and beating chests:** These describe the women’s sounds and actions of grief.

## Nahum 2:8

Nineveh has been like a pool of water throughout her days. 📖

But now the water is draining away.

People cry, “Stop!

Stop!”

But no one turns back.

**Help with this picture:** The draining pool pictures people leaving the city.

## Nahum 2:9

“Steal the silver! 📖

Steal the gold!”

There is treasure without end, and great amounts of every precious thing.

## Nahum 2:10

Nineveh is empty, deserted, and ruined! 📖

Hearts melt, knees knock, bodies shake, and every face turns pale!

**Help with hearts melt:** This pictures people losing courage.

## Nahum 2:11

Where is the lions’ den, the place where young lions fed? 📖

The lion and lioness walked there with their cubs.

Nothing frightened them away.

**Help with these lions:** The lion family pictures Nineveh’s power and former safety.

## Nahum 2:12

The lion tore apart enough prey for its cubs. 📖

It strangled prey for the lioness.

It filled its dens with dead animals and torn prey.

**Help with prey:** Prey means animals caught for food.

This picture continues the comparison of Nineveh to lions.

## Nahum 2:13

The LORD of Hosts declares, “I am against you. 📖

I will burn your chariots to ashes.

The sword will eat up your young lions.

I will remove your prey from the earth.

Your messengers’ voices will no longer be heard.”

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

**Help with young lions and prey:** These pictures continue the message about Nineveh’s power and violent gains.
