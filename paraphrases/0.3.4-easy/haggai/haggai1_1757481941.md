# Haggai 1 — The LORD tells the people to rebuild

**About this chapter**

Haggai brings the LORD’s message to Judah’s leaders and people.

The people begin work on the LORD’s house.

## Haggai 1:1

It was year 2 of Darius’s rule, day 1 of month 6. 📖

The LORD’s word came through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah.

It also came to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

**Help with high priest:** The high priest is the leading priest.

## Haggai 1:2

The LORD of Hosts says, “These people say it is not yet time to rebuild the LORD’s house.” 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with house:** Here, the LORD’s house means his temple, a place of worship.

## Haggai 1:3

The LORD’s word came through Haggai the prophet. 📖

The LORD said,

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## Haggai 1:4

“Is this a time for you to live in houses with fine wooden panels? 📖

This house still lies in ruins!”

**Help with this house:** The LORD means his temple, a place of worship.

## Haggai 1:5

The LORD of Hosts says, “Think carefully about your ways. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Haggai 1:6

You planted much but harvested little. 📖

You eat but never have enough.

You drink but never have your fill.

You put on clothes but never get warm.

You earn wages and put them into a bag full of holes.

**Help with the bag:** This picture describes earnings that do not last.

## Haggai 1:7

The LORD of Hosts says, “Think carefully about your ways. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Haggai 1:8

Go up into the hills and bring down wood. 📖

Build the house, so that I may take pleasure in it and be honoured,” says the LORD.

**Help with the house:** The house is the LORD’s temple, a place of worship.

## Haggai 1:9

“You expected much, but it became little. 📖

I blew away what you brought home.

Why?” declares the LORD of Hosts.

“Because my house still lies in ruins while each of you is busy with your own house.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with my house:** The LORD means his temple, a place of worship.

## Haggai 1:10

Because of you, the heavens have held back their dew. 📖

The earth has held back its crops.

**Help with dew:** Dew is moisture that forms on cool surfaces.

## Haggai 1:11

I called for a drought on the fields and mountains. 📖

It affects the grain, new wine, oil, and everything the ground produces.

It affects people, animals, and all the work of your hands.”

**Help with drought:** A drought is a long time with very little rain.

## Haggai 1:12

Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and all the remaining people obeyed their God. 📖

They obeyed the LORD’s voice and Haggai the prophet’s words, because the LORD their God had sent him.

The people feared the LORD.

**Help with fear:** Here, fear includes respect for God’s authority.

**Help with prophet and high priest:** A prophet brings God’s messages.

The high priest is the leading priest.

## Haggai 1:13

Haggai, the LORD’s messenger, gave the people the LORD’s message. 📖

“I am with you,” declares the LORD.

## Haggai 1:14

The LORD stirred the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah. 📖

He also stirred the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

He stirred the spirit of all the remaining people too.

They came and began work on the house of the LORD of Hosts, their God.

**Help with stirred the spirit:** This means moved people inwardly to act.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with house:** Here, house means the temple, a place of worship.

## Haggai 1:15

They began on day 24 of month 6, in year 2 of King Darius. 📖
