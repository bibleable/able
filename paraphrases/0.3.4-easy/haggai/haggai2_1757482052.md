# Haggai 2 — The LORD promises to be with his people

**About this chapter**

The LORD encourages the builders and speaks about holiness, blessing, and Zerubbabel.

Haggai gives messages on the dates recorded here.

## Haggai 2:1

On day 21 of month 7, the LORD’s word came through Haggai the prophet. 📖

The LORD said,

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## Haggai 2:2

“Speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest. 📖

Speak to the remaining people too.

Ask them,

**Help with high priest:** The high priest is the leading priest.

## Haggai 2:3

‘Who among you is left who saw this house in its earlier glory? 📖

How does it look to you now?

Does it seem like nothing compared with before?’

**Help with this house:** This means the LORD’s temple, a place of worship.

**Help with glory:** Here, glory means greatness and splendour.

## Haggai 2:4

But be strong now, Zerubbabel,” declares the LORD. 📖

“Be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

Be strong, all you people of the land,” declares the LORD.

“Work, for I am with you,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with high priest:** The high priest is the leading priest.

## Haggai 2:5

“This is the promise I made when you came out of Egypt. 📖

My Spirit stays among you.

Do not be afraid.”

## Haggai 2:6

The LORD of Hosts says, “Once more, in a little while, I will shake the heavens and the earth. 📖

I will shake the sea and the dry land.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Haggai 2:7

I will shake all nations, and they will come with all their treasures. 📖

I will fill this house with glory,” says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with this house:** This means the LORD’s temple, a place of worship.

**Help with glory:** Glory means greatness and honour.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Haggai 2:8

“The silver is mine, and the gold is mine,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Haggai 2:9

“This house’s later glory will be greater than its earlier glory,” says the LORD of Hosts. 📖

“I will provide peace in this place,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with house and glory:** The house is the temple.

Glory means greatness and honour.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Haggai 2:10

On day 24 of month 9, in year 2 of Darius, the LORD’s word came to Haggai the prophet. 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## Haggai 2:11

The LORD of Hosts said, “Ask the priests for a ruling. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with ruling:** A ruling is a decision about how a law applies.

## Haggai 2:12

Suppose a man carries holy meat in a fold of his clothing. 📖

The fold touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or other food.

Does that food become holy?”

The priests answered, “No.”

**Help with holy:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Haggai 2:13

Haggai asked, “Suppose someone becomes unclean by touching a dead body. 📖

If that person touches any of this food, does it become unclean?”

The priests answered, “Yes, it becomes unclean.”

**Help with unclean:** This means not ready for worship under the religious rules.

It does not mean that touching a dead person makes someone morally bad.

## Haggai 2:14

Haggai said, “The LORD declares this. 📖

This people and this nation are like that before me.

All the work of their hands is like that.

Whatever they offer there is unclean.

**Help with unclean:** Here, the word concerns the people’s standing before God and their offerings.

## Haggai 2:15

Now think carefully from this day forward. 📖

Think about the time before one stone was laid on another in the LORD’s temple.

**Help with temple:** A temple is a place of worship.

## Haggai 2:16

When someone expected a heap of 20 ephahs of grain, there were only 10. 📖

When someone came to draw 50 baths from the winepress, there were only 20.

**Help with ephahs and baths:** These were measures for grain and liquids.

**Help with winepress:** A winepress is a place where grapes are crushed to get their juice.

## Haggai 2:17

I struck you and all the work of your hands with blight, mildew, and hail. 📖

Yet you did not turn to me,” declares the LORD.

**Help with blight and mildew:** These damage plants and crops.

**Help with hail:** Hail is frozen rain falling as pieces of ice.

## Haggai 2:18

“Think carefully from this day forward, day 24 of month 9. 📖

This is the day the foundation of the LORD’s temple was laid.

Think carefully.

**Help with foundation:** A foundation is the base on which a building stands.

**Help with temple:** A temple is a place of worship.

## Haggai 2:19

Is there still seed in the barn? 📖

The vine, fig, pomegranate, and olive tree have not yet produced fruit.

But from this day on, I will bless you.”

**Help with bless:** Here, bless means give good things to the people.

## Haggai 2:20

The LORD’s word came to Haggai a 2nd time that day, day 24 of the month. 📖

## Haggai 2:21

The LORD said, “Tell Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, that I am about to shake the heavens and the earth. 📖

## Haggai 2:22

I will overturn royal thrones and destroy the power of the nations’ kingdoms. 📖

I will overturn chariots and their riders.

Horses and riders will fall, each by his brother’s sword.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

## Haggai 2:23

On that day, I will take you, my servant Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel,” declares the LORD. 📖

“I will make you like my signet ring, because I have chosen you,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with signet ring:** This ring made a mark showing its owner’s authority.

The LORD uses it as a picture of choosing Zerubbabel.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.
