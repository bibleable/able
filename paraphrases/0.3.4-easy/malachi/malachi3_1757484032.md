# Malachi 3 — The messenger, judgment, and returning to God

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks of a coming messenger, judgment, offerings, and faithfulness.

Questions and complaints are kept with their speakers.

## Malachi 3:1

The LORD of Hosts says, “I will send my messenger to prepare the way before me. 📖

The Lord you seek will suddenly come to his temple.

The Messenger of the covenant, whom you delight in, is coming.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with temple:** A temple is a place of worship.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## Malachi 3:2

Who can bear the day he comes? 📖

Who can stand when he appears?

He will be like a metalworker’s refining fire and a clothes washer’s soap.

**Help with these pictures:** Fire removes unwanted material from metal.

Soap cleans clothes.

Both picture making something pure.

## Malachi 3:3

He will sit as one who refines and purifies silver. 📖

He will purify Levi’s sons and refine them like gold and silver.

Then they will bring offerings to the LORD in righteousness.

**Help with refine and purify:** These mean remove unwanted material and make pure.

**Help with righteousness:** Righteousness means being right before God.

## Malachi 3:4

Then Judah’s and Jerusalem’s offerings will please the LORD, as in the days and years long ago. 📖

## Malachi 3:5

The LORD of Hosts says, “I will come near to judge you. 📖

I will quickly give evidence against those who practise sorcery, commit adultery, or lie under oath.

I will testify against those who oppress widows and children without fathers.

I will testify against those who cheat workers of wages and deny justice to foreigners.

They do not fear me.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with sorcery:** Sorcery means practices that seek magical power.

**Help with adultery:** Adultery means sex with someone else’s husband or wife.

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise to tell the truth.

**Help with widows:** Widows are women whose husbands have died.

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.

## Malachi 3:6

I, the LORD, do not change. 📖

That is why you descendants of Jacob have not been destroyed.

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

## Malachi 3:7

But since your ancestors’ days, you have turned away from my laws and not kept them. 📖

Return to me, and I will return to you,” says the LORD of Hosts.

“You ask, ‘How can we return?’

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 3:8

Will a person rob God? 📖

Yet you are robbing me!

You ask, ‘How are we robbing you?’

In tithes and offerings.

**Help with tithe:** A tithe is a tenth given to God.

**Help with offerings:** Offerings are gifts given in worship.

## Malachi 3:9

You are under a curse, but the whole nation is still robbing me. 📖

**Help with curse:** Here, curse means a declaration of judgment.

## Malachi 3:10

Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, so there is food in my house. 📖

Test me in this,” says the LORD of Hosts.

“See whether I will open heaven’s windows and pour out blessing beyond measure.

**Help with tithe:** A tithe is a tenth given to God.

**Help with house:** Here, God’s house is the temple, a place of worship.

**Help with heaven’s windows:** This pictures a generous supply from God.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 3:11

I will rebuke the devourer for you, so it does not destroy your land’s fruit. 📖

The vine in your field will not fail to produce fruit,” says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with devourer:** Here, this describes what eats or destroys crops.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 3:12

“Then all nations will call you blessed, because you will be a delightful land,” says the LORD of Hosts. 📖

**Help with blessed:** Blessed means receiving good from God.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 3:13

The LORD says, “Your words against me have been harsh. 📖

Yet you ask, ‘What have we said against you?’

## Malachi 3:14

You have said, ‘There is no point in serving God. 📖

What have we gained by keeping his requirements and walking mournfully before the LORD of Hosts?

**Help with mournfully:** Mournfully means showing sadness.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 3:15

Now we call proud people blessed. 📖

Those who do evil prosper.

They even test God and escape.’”

**About these statements:** These are the people’s complaints, not promises made by the narrator.

## Malachi 3:16

Those who feared the LORD spoke with each other. 📖

The LORD listened and heard them.

A scroll of remembrance was written before him.

It concerned those who feared the LORD and honoured his name.

**Help with scroll:** A scroll is a rolled-up writing surface.

**Help with feared:** Here, feared includes deep respect.

## Malachi 3:17

“They will be mine,” says the LORD of Hosts, “on the day I prepare my treasured possession. 📖

I will spare them as a man spares his own son who serves him.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 3:18

You will again see the difference between the righteous and the wicked. 📖

You will see the difference between those who serve God and those who do not.”

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means right before God.
