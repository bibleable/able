# Malachi 1 — Love, honour, and offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to Israel through Malachi.

The chapter includes questions and answers about love, honour, and offerings.

## Malachi 1:1

This is the message of the LORD’s word to Israel through Malachi. 📖

## Malachi 1:2

The LORD says, “I have loved you.” 📖

But you ask, “How have you loved us?”

The LORD declares, “Was Esau not Jacob’s brother?

Yet I loved Jacob,

## Malachi 1:3

but I hated Esau. 📖

I made his mountains a wasteland and left his inheritance to desert jackals.”

**Help with inheritance:** Inheritance is property passed on in a family.

**Help with jackals:** Jackals are wild animals.

**About loved and hated:** These difficult words are retained without weakening the contrast or settling its interpretation.

## Malachi 1:4

Edom may say, “We have been ruined, but we will rebuild.” 📖

But the LORD of Hosts says, “They may build, but I will tear it down.

They will be called the Land of Wickedness, a people under the LORD’s anger forever.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with wickedness:** Wickedness means serious wrongdoing.

## Malachi 1:5

You will see this with your own eyes. 📖

You will say, ‘The LORD is great, even beyond Israel’s borders.’”

## Malachi 1:6

The LORD of Hosts speaks to you priests who despise his name. 📖

“A son honours his father, and a servant honours his master.

If I am a father, where is my honour?

If I am a master, where is your fear of me?

You ask, ‘How have we despised your name?’

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with despise my name:** This means treating God himself with scorn.

## Malachi 1:7

You put defiled food on my altar. 📖

But you ask, ‘How have we defiled you?’

You do this by saying the LORD’s table deserves scorn.

**Help with defiled:** Defiled means unfit for this worship.

**Help with altar and table:** The LORD’s table here refers to the altar used for offerings.

## Malachi 1:8

Is it not wrong when you offer blind animals as sacrifices? 📖

Is it not wrong when you offer animals that cannot walk properly or are sick?

Try giving them to your governor!

Would he be pleased or show you favour?” asks the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**About these animals:** The passage concerns requirements for animal offerings.

It does not say that disability makes a person morally bad.

## Malachi 1:9

“Now ask for God’s favour. 📖

Will he be kind to you?

Since this has come from your hands, will he show you favour?” asks the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 1:10

“If only one of you would shut the temple doors! 📖

Then you would stop lighting useless fires on my altar.

I take no pleasure in you,” says the LORD of Hosts.

“I will accept no offering from your hands.

**Help with temple and altar:** The temple is a place of worship.

The altar is a place for offerings to God.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 1:11

My name will be great among the nations, from sunrise to sunset. 📖

Everywhere, incense and pure offerings will be given in my name.

My name will be great among the nations,” says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with incense:** Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

**Help with in my name:** This links the worship with God himself.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 1:12

“But you dishonour my name by saying the Lord’s table is defiled. 📖

You say its produce, its food, deserves scorn.

**Help with table and defiled:** The table is the altar.

Defiled means unfit for this worship.

## Malachi 1:13

You also say, ‘What a nuisance!’ 📖

You turn up your nose at it,” says the LORD of Hosts.

“You bring stolen animals, animals that cannot walk properly, and sick animals as offerings.

Should I accept these from your hands?” asks the LORD.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with turn up your nose:** This means show scorn.

## Malachi 1:14

“Cursed is the cheat who promises an acceptable male from his flock but sacrifices a defective animal to the Lord. 📖

I am a great King,” says the LORD of Hosts.

“My name is to be feared among the nations.

**Help with cursed:** Cursed means placed under judgment.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with feared:** Here, feared includes treated with deep respect.
