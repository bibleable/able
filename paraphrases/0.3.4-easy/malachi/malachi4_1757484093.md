# Malachi 4 — The coming day and Elijah

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks of judgment, healing, Moses’ law, and the coming of Elijah.

The final warning keeps its condition.

## Malachi 4:1

The LORD of Hosts says, “The day is coming, burning like a furnace. 📖

All the proud and everyone who does evil will be dry stalks.

On that day, I will set them on fire.

No root or branch will be left to them.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with these pictures:** Fire burning dry stalks, roots, and branches pictures complete destruction.

## Malachi 4:2

But for you who fear my name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings. 📖

You will go out and leap like calves released from the stall.

**Help with fear my name:** This means treat God with deep respect.

**Help with sun and wings:** This image joins the light of righteousness with healing.

Its interpretation needs further editorial discussion.

**Help with calves:** Calves are young cattle.

## Malachi 4:3

You will trample the wicked. 📖

They will be ashes under your feet on the day I am preparing,” says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 4:4

“Remember the law of my servant Moses. 📖

Remember the laws and rules I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel.

## Malachi 4:5

I will send you Elijah the prophet before the great and awesome Day of the LORD comes. 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

**Help with awesome:** Awesome means causing wonder and awe.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

## Malachi 4:6

Elijah will turn fathers’ hearts towards their children and children’s hearts towards their fathers. 📖

Otherwise, I will come and strike the land with a curse.”

**Help with hearts:** Here, hearts pictures the people’s care and commitment.

**Help with curse:** Here, curse means a declaration of judgment.
