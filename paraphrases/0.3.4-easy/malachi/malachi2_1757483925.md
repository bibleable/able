# Malachi 2 — Priests and people must keep faith

**About this chapter**

The LORD warns priests about their teaching.

The chapter also addresses broken trust in marriage and the people’s questions.

## Malachi 2:1

The LORD says, “Priests, this command is for you. 📖

## Malachi 2:2

If you do not listen or take to heart the need to honour my name, I will send a curse. 📖

I will curse your blessings,” says the LORD of Hosts.

“I have already begun, because you are not taking it to heart.

**Help with curse:** Here, curse means a declaration of judgment.

**Help with take to heart:** This means treat as important and respond seriously.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 2:3

I will rebuke your descendants. 📖

I will spread dung on your faces, the waste from your feasts.

You will be carried away with it.

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

**Help with dung:** Dung means animal waste.

This degrading description is retained from the source.

## Malachi 2:4

Then you will know I sent this command so my covenant with Levi may continue,” says the LORD of Hosts. 📖

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 2:5

“My covenant with Levi was one of life and peace, which I gave him. 📖

It called for deep respect, and he respected me.

He stood in awe of my name.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

**Help with awe:** Awe means wonder and deep respect.

## Malachi 2:6

He gave true teaching, and nothing false came from his lips. 📖

He walked with me in peace and right conduct.

He turned many people away from wrongdoing.

**Help with walked with me:** This pictures living in a close relationship with God.

## Malachi 2:7

A priest’s lips should preserve knowledge. 📖

People should seek teaching from his mouth.

He is the messenger of the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 2:8

But you have turned from the way, and your teaching has made many stumble. 📖

You have broken Levi’s covenant,” says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with stumble:** This pictures being led into wrong actions.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 2:9

“So I have made all the people despise and humiliate you. 📖

You have not kept my ways.

You have favoured some people over others in matters of the law.”

## Malachi 2:10

Do we not all have one Father? 📖

Did one God not create us?

Why do we break trust with each other and dishonour our ancestors’ covenant?

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## Malachi 2:11

Judah has broken trust. 📖

A hateful act has been done in Israel and Jerusalem.

Judah has dishonoured the LORD’s beloved holy place by marrying the daughter of a foreign god.

**Help with daughter of a foreign god:** This describes a religious allegiance in marriage.

The image and its application need further editorial discussion.

## Malachi 2:12

As for the man who does this, may the LORD cut off everyone awake and aware from Jacob’s tents. 📖

May this happen even if he brings an offering to the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with Jacob’s tents:** This refers to the community descended from Jacob.

**About awake and aware:** This difficult source wording is kept for further interpretation.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 2:13

You do something else too. 📖

You cover the LORD’s altar with tears, weeping, and groaning.

He no longer pays attention to your offerings or receives them gladly from your hands.

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

## Malachi 2:14

Yet you ask, “Why?” 📖

The LORD has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth.

You broke faith with her, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## Malachi 2:15

Has the LORD not made them one, with a portion of the Spirit? 📖

Why one?

He seeks godly children.

Guard yourselves in your spirit.

Do not break faith with the wife of your youth.

**About this verse:** Its difficult wording about one and the Spirit is retained for further editorial discussion.

**Help with godly:** Godly means living in a way that honours God.

## Malachi 2:16

“I hate divorce,” says the LORD, the God of Israel. 📖

“The man who divorces his wife covers his clothing with violence,” says the LORD of Hosts.

Guard yourselves in your spirit and do not break faith.

**Help with divorce:** Divorce legally ends a marriage.

**Help with clothing and violence:** This is the source’s image of violence linked with divorce.

Its application needs further editorial discussion.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Malachi 2:17

You have tired the LORD with your words. 📖

Yet you ask, “How have we tired him?”

You say, “Everyone who does evil is good in the LORD’s sight, and he delights in them.”

Or you ask, “Where is the God of justice?”

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.
