# Galatians 1 — The good news Paul received

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to the churches in Galatia.

He warns them against a different message and explains how Jesus Christ called him.

Paul speaks as “I” in this letter.

## Galatians 1:1

This letter is from Paul, an apostle. 📖

No human person or group sent me.

Jesus Christ and God the Father sent me.

God raised Jesus from the dead.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Galatians 1:2

All the brothers and sisters with me join in this letter. 📖

We write to the churches in Galatia.

## Galatians 1:3

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace. 📖

## Galatians 1:4

Jesus gave himself for our sins to rescue us from this present evil age. 📖

He did this according to what our God and Father wanted.

**Help with words:** Sins are wrong things done against God.

This present age means the time and world in which people now live.

## Galatians 1:5

May God receive glory for ever and ever. 📖

Amen.

**Help with words:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.

Amen expresses agreement with a prayer.

## Galatians 1:6

I am amazed that you are leaving God so quickly. 📖

He called you through Christ's kindness.

Yet you are turning to a different good news.

## Galatians 1:7

It is not really good news at all. 📖

Some people are troubling you and trying to twist Christ's good news.

## Galatians 1:8

Even we or an angel from heaven might tell you a message against the good news we taught you. 📖

If so, let that messenger be under a curse!

**Help with a word:** A curse calls for judgment, rather than blessing.

## Galatians 1:9

We said this before, and I say it again. 📖

If anyone teaches you a message against the good news you received, let that person be under a curse!

## Galatians 1:10

Am I now trying to win people's approval, or God's? 📖

Am I trying to please people?

If I were still trying to please people, I would not be Christ's servant.

## Galatians 1:11

Brothers and sisters, I want you to know this. 📖

The good news I taught did not come from human ideas.

## Galatians 1:12

I did not receive it from a human person. 📖

No human teacher taught it to me.

Jesus Christ revealed it to me.

**Help with a word:** Revealed means made something known.

## Galatians 1:13

You have heard about my former life in Judaism. 📖

I treated God's church very cruelly and tried to destroy it.

**Help with a word:** Judaism is the Jewish faith and way of life.

## Galatians 1:14

I was advancing in Judaism beyond many people my own age. 📖

I was extremely eager to follow the traditions of my ancestors.

**Help with words:** Traditions are teachings and practices passed down from earlier people.

Ancestors are family members who lived before us.

## Galatians 1:15

But God set me apart before I was born. 📖

He called me through his kindness.

## Galatians 1:16

God was pleased to reveal his Son in me so I could tell Gentiles about him. 📖

I did not immediately ask other people for advice.

**Help with a word:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Galatians 1:17

I did not go to Jerusalem to see those who were apostles before me. 📖

Instead, I went to Arabia and later returned to Damascus.

## Galatians 1:18

After three years, I went to Jerusalem to meet Cephas. 📖

I stayed with him for 15 days.

**Help with a name:** Cephas is another name for Peter.

## Galatians 1:19

I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother. 📖

## Galatians 1:20

Before God, I assure you that what I am writing is not a lie. 📖

## Galatians 1:21

Later, I went to the regions of Syria and Cilicia. 📖

## Galatians 1:22

The churches in Judea that belonged to Christ did not know me personally. 📖

## Galatians 1:23

They only heard this report. 📖

“The man who once treated us cruelly now teaches the faith he once tried to destroy.”

## Galatians 1:24

They praised God because of me. 📖
