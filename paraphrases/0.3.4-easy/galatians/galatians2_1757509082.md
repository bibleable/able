# Galatians 2 — Made right through faith in Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul describes meeting the leaders in Jerusalem.

He also tells how he opposed Cephas in Antioch.

He explains that people are made right with God through faith in Christ.

## Galatians 2:1

Fourteen years later, I went to Jerusalem again with Barnabas. 📖

I also took Titus.

## Galatians 2:2

I went because God revealed that I should go. 📖

I explained the good news I teach among the Gentiles.

I spoke privately with the recognised leaders.

I feared that my work, past or present, might be wasted.

**Help with a word:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Galatians 2:3

Even Titus, who was with me, was not forced to be circumcised, although he was Greek. 📖

**Help with a word:** Circumcision removes the foreskin, the skin covering the end of the penis.

It was a physical sign of belonging to the Jewish people.

## Galatians 2:4

This issue arose because false brothers secretly came in, pretending to belong with us. 📖

They spied on our freedom in Christ Jesus because they wanted to make us slaves.

## Galatians 2:5

We did not give in to them for a moment. 📖

We wanted the truth of the good news to remain with you.

## Galatians 2:6

The leaders added nothing to my message. 📖

Their high standing makes no difference to me.

God does not favour one person over another.

## Galatians 2:7

Instead, they saw that I was trusted to tell the good news to the uncircumcised. 📖

Peter was trusted to tell it to the circumcised.

**Help with these names:** The uncircumcised means Gentiles here.

The circumcised means Jewish people.

## Galatians 2:8

God worked through Peter as an apostle to the circumcised. 📖

He also worked through me as an apostle to the Gentiles.

## Galatians 2:9

James, Cephas, and John recognised the kindness God had given me. 📖

People regarded them as pillars.

They offered Barnabas and me their right hands as a sign of fellowship.

We would go to the Gentiles, and they would go to the circumcised.

**Help with these pictures:** Pillars hold up a building and picture important leaders here.

The right hand of fellowship showed their agreement to work together.

Cephas is another name for Peter.

## Galatians 2:10

They only asked us to remember poor people. 📖

I was already eager to do that.

## Galatians 2:11

But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him face to face. 📖

He was clearly in the wrong.

## Galatians 2:12

Before some men came from James, Cephas ate with Gentiles. 📖

When those men arrived, he drew back and separated himself.

He was afraid of the circumcision group.

## Galatians 2:13

The other Jews joined him in this pretence. 📖

Even Barnabas was led astray by their pretence.

## Galatians 2:14

I saw that they were not living according to the truth of the good news. 📖

In front of everyone, I told Cephas, “You are Jewish, but you live like a Gentile.

How can you force Gentiles to live like Jews?”

## Galatians 2:15

We are Jews by birth, rather than Gentile “sinners.” 📖

**Help with the quotation marks:** Paul uses a label applied to Gentiles while explaining that everyone needs faith in Christ.

## Galatians 2:16

We know that keeping the law does not make a person right with God. 📖

Faith in Jesus Christ does.

So we too have believed in Christ Jesus to be made right through faith in him.

Keeping the law will not make anyone right with God.

## Galatians 2:17

We seek to be made right with God in Christ. 📖

But if we ourselves are found to be sinners, does that make Christ a servant of sin?

Certainly not!

## Galatians 2:18

If I rebuild what I already tore down, I show that I break the law. 📖

## Galatians 2:19

Through the law, I died to the law so that I could live for God. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes a change in his relationship to the law and his new life for God.

## Galatians 2:20

I have been crucified with Christ. 📖

I no longer live, but Christ lives in me.

The life I live in this body, I live by faith in God's Son.

He loved me and gave himself for me.

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes sharing in Christ's death and living a new life through him.

Crucifixion was death by being fastened to a cross.

## Galatians 2:21

I do not set aside God's freely given kindness. 📖

If keeping the law could make people right with God, Christ died for nothing.
