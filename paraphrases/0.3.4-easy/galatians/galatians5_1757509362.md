# Galatians 5 — Live by the Spirit

**About this chapter**

Paul writes about freedom in Christ and life led by the Holy Spirit.

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Galatians 5:1

Christ set us free so we could live in freedom. 📖

Stand firm.

Do not put on the yoke of slavery again.

**Help with this picture:** A yoke is a wooden bar used to control working animals.

Paul compares being forced under the law for acceptance by God with slavery.

## Galatians 5:2

Listen to me, Paul. 📖

If you accept circumcision in this way, Christ will be of no help to you.

**Help with a word:** Circumcision removes skin from the end of a male's penis.

Paul opposes making this a requirement for being accepted by God.

## Galatians 5:3

I say again that every man who accepts circumcision this way must obey the whole law. 📖

## Galatians 5:4

If you try to be made right with God by the law, you are cut off from Christ. 📖

You have turned away from God's freely given kindness.

## Galatians 5:5

Through the Spirit, we eagerly wait by faith for the righteousness we hope for. 📖

**Help with a word:** Righteousness means being right with God.

## Galatians 5:6

For people in Christ Jesus, being circumcised or not being circumcised does not matter. 📖

What matters is faith shown through love.

## Galatians 5:7

You were running well. 📖

Who stopped you from obeying the truth?

**Help with this picture:** Running well pictures living faithfully.

## Galatians 5:8

This teaching does not come from God, who calls you. 📖

## Galatians 5:9

A little yeast spreads through a whole batch of dough. 📖

**Help with this picture:** A little false teaching can affect a whole group.

## Galatians 5:10

I trust in the Lord that you will agree with me. 📖

God will judge whoever is troubling you.

## Galatians 5:11

Brothers and sisters, if I still taught circumcision, why would people attack me? 📖

Then the message of the cross would no longer offend them.

## Galatians 5:12

I wish those upsetting you would go further and cut off their own male parts! 📖

**Help with this verse:** Paul uses harsh words against teachers demanding circumcision.

## Galatians 5:13

Brothers and sisters, God called you to freedom. 📖

Do not use freedom to follow sinful desires.

Instead, serve each other in love.

**Help with words:** Sinful desires lead people to do wrong against God.

## Galatians 5:14

The whole law is fulfilled in this command. 📖

“Love your neighbour as yourself.”

## Galatians 5:15

If you keep biting and eating each other, be careful! 📖

You may destroy each other.

**Help with this picture:** Biting and eating picture people attacking each other.

## Galatians 5:16

Let God's Spirit lead your life. 📖

Then you will not carry out sinful desires.

## Galatians 5:17

Sinful desires fight against the Spirit. 📖

The Spirit fights against sinful desires.

They oppose each other, so you do not do what you want.

## Galatians 5:18

If the Spirit leads you, you are not under the law. 📖

## Galatians 5:19

The results of sinful desires are clear. 📖

They include wrong sexual behaviour, impure actions, and uncontrolled shameful living.

## Galatians 5:20

They include worshipping idols and using magic. 📖

They include hatred, fighting, jealousy, angry outbursts, selfish rivalry, divisions, and rival groups.

**Help with a word:** An idol is something worshipped as a god.

## Galatians 5:21

They include envy, drunkenness, wild drinking and sex parties, and similar things. 📖

I warn you again.

People who live this way will not receive a place in God's kingdom.

## Galatians 5:22

But the Spirit produces love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, and faithfulness in people's lives. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The Bible calls these qualities the Spirit's fruit.

## Galatians 5:23

The Spirit also produces gentleness and self-control. 📖

No law is against these things.

**Help with a word:** Self-control means managing your actions and desires.

## Galatians 5:24

People who belong to Christ Jesus have nailed their sinful nature and its desires to the cross. 📖

**Help with this picture:** They have put their old sinful way of life to death.

## Galatians 5:25

The Spirit gives us life. 📖

So let us follow the Spirit's lead.

## Galatians 5:26

Let us not think we are better than others. 📖

Let us not upset each other or envy each other.
