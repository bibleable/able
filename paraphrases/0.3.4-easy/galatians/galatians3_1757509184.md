# Galatians 3 — God's promise and faith

**About this chapter**

Paul compares trusting God's promise with relying on the law.

He writes about Abraham, Christ, and the purpose of the law.

## Galatians 3:1

Foolish Galatians, who has put you under a spell? 📖

Jesus Christ was clearly shown to you as crucified, as if before your eyes.

**Help with these words:** Paul uses strong language about being misled.

Crucified means killed by being fastened to a cross.

## Galatians 3:2

I want to learn just one thing from you. 📖

Did you receive the Spirit by keeping the law, or by hearing and believing?

## Galatians 3:3

Are you so foolish? 📖

You began with the Spirit.

Are you now finishing by the flesh?

**Help with a word:** Flesh here speaks of relying on human effort rather than the Spirit.

## Galatians 3:4

Have you suffered so much for nothing, if it really was for nothing? 📖

## Galatians 3:5

God gives you his Spirit generously and does miracles among you. 📖

Is this because you keep the law, or because you hear and believe?

## Galatians 3:6

In the same way, “Abraham believed God, and God counted this as righteousness.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Righteousness means being right before God.

## Galatians 3:7

Understand this, then. 📖

People who have faith are Abraham's children.

## Galatians 3:8

The Scriptures foresaw that God would make Gentiles right with him through faith. 📖

They announced this good news to Abraham beforehand.

“All nations will be blessed through you.”

**Help with words:** The Scriptures are the holy writings.

Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

## Galatians 3:9

So people who have faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith. 📖

## Galatians 3:10

Everyone who relies on keeping the law is under a curse. 📖

The Scriptures say, “Everyone who does not keep doing everything in the Book of the Law is cursed.”

**Help with a word:** A curse calls for judgment, rather than blessing.

## Galatians 3:11

Clearly, the law makes no one right before God. 📖

The Scriptures say, “The righteous will live by faith.”

## Galatians 3:12

The law is not based on faith. 📖

Instead, it says, “The person who does these things will live by them.”

## Galatians 3:13

Christ set us free from the law's curse by becoming a curse for us. 📖

The Scriptures say, “Everyone who is hung on a tree is cursed.”

**Help with this quotation:** Paul connects the words about hanging on a tree with Christ's death on the cross.

## Galatians 3:14

He did this so Abraham's promised blessing would reach Gentiles in Christ Jesus. 📖

Through faith, we could receive the promised Spirit.

## Galatians 3:15

Brothers and sisters, here is an example from human life. 📖

Once a human agreement is formally confirmed, no one cancels it or adds to it.

## Galatians 3:16

God spoke the promises to Abraham and to his seed. 📖

The Scriptures do not say “seeds,” meaning many.

They say “your seed,” meaning one person, who is Christ.

**Help with a word:** Seed here means someone born in a person's family line.

## Galatians 3:17

This is what I mean. 📖

The law came 430 years later.

It cannot cancel the agreement God confirmed earlier and make his promise count for nothing.

## Galatians 3:18

If the inheritance depends on the law, it no longer depends on a promise. 📖

But God freely gave it to Abraham through a promise.

**Help with a word:** An inheritance is something received as a family member.

## Galatians 3:19

Why was the law given, then? 📖

It was added because of wrongdoing until the seed came.

The promise referred to that seed.

It was given through angels by a mediator.

**Help with a word:** A mediator stands between people to pass on or arrange an agreement.

## Galatians 3:20

A mediator is not needed when there is only one side. 📖

But God is one.

## Galatians 3:21

Does the law oppose God's promises? 📖

Certainly not!

If a law could have given life, then keeping the law would have made people right with God.

## Galatians 3:22

But the Scriptures say that everything is held prisoner by sin. 📖

So the promise is given through faith in Jesus Christ to those who believe.

**Help with this picture:** Being held prisoner pictures being under sin's power.

## Galatians 3:23

Before this faith came, the law held us in custody. 📖

We were locked up until faith was revealed.

## Galatians 3:24

So the law became our guardian to lead us to Christ. 📖

Then we could be made right with God through faith.

**Help with a word:** A guardian cares for someone who is not yet grown up.

## Galatians 3:25

Now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian. 📖

## Galatians 3:26

You are all God's children through faith in Christ Jesus. 📖

## Galatians 3:27

All of you who were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Clothing pictures the new life and belonging that believers have in Christ.

Baptism uses water as a sign of belonging to Christ.

## Galatians 3:28

There is neither Jew nor Greek, slave nor free, male nor female. 📖

You are all one in Christ Jesus.

## Galatians 3:29

If you belong to Christ, you are Abraham's seed and heirs according to the promise. 📖

**Help with words:** Paul describes belonging to Abraham's family and receiving what God promised.
