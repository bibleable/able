# Galatians 4 — Children of the promise

**About this chapter**

Paul compares life under the law with a child's life under guardians.

He appeals to the believers personally.

Then he uses Hagar and the free woman as pictures of two covenants.

## Galatians 4:1

Here is what I mean. 📖

While an heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, although everything belongs to him.

**Help with words:** An heir is someone who will receive family property.

A slave is someone owned and controlled by another person.

## Galatians 4:2

The child is under guardians and people who manage his property until the time his father has set. 📖

## Galatians 4:3

In the same way, when we were children, the world's basic principles held us as slaves. 📖

**Help with these words:** Paul uses childhood and slavery as pictures for their former way of life.

Basic principles means the elementary rules that controlled that life.

## Galatians 4:4

But when the right time fully came, God sent his Son. 📖

He was born from a woman and born under the law.

## Galatians 4:5

God sent him to set free those under the law. 📖

Then we could be adopted as his children.

**Help with a word:** Adoption means welcoming someone into a family as a son or daughter.

## Galatians 4:6

Because you are his children, God sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts. 📖

The Spirit cries out, “Abba, Father!”

**Help with a word:** Abba means Father.

## Galatians 4:7

So you are no longer a slave, but a child. 📖

Because you are God's child, you are also an heir through God.

## Galatians 4:8

Before you knew God, you were slaves to those who are not really gods. 📖

## Galatians 4:9

But now you know God, or rather God knows you. 📖

Why are you turning back to those weak and worthless principles?

Do you want to be their slaves again?

## Galatians 4:10

You are keeping special days, months, seasons, and years! 📖

## Galatians 4:11

I fear for you. 📖

Perhaps all my work for you has been wasted.

## Galatians 4:12

Brothers and sisters, I beg you to become like me, because I became like you. 📖

You have done me no wrong.

## Galatians 4:13

You know that an illness led me to tell you the good news the first time. 📖

## Galatians 4:14

My illness was a trial for you. 📖

Yet you did not despise or reject me.

You welcomed me as if I were God's angel, or Christ Jesus himself.

## Galatians 4:15

What has happened to your blessing? 📖

I can say that, if possible, you would have torn out your eyes and given them to me.

**Help with this picture:** Paul uses strong words to recall how much they wanted to help him.

## Galatians 4:16

Have I become your enemy because I tell you the truth? 📖

## Galatians 4:17

Those people are eager to win you over, but their purpose is not good. 📖

They want to cut you off from us so you will be eager for them.

## Galatians 4:18

It is good to be eager for a good purpose at any time. 📖

This is true even when I am not with you.

## Galatians 4:19

My children, I am again in the pains of childbirth for you until Christ is formed in you. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul compares his struggle for their growth in Christ with the pain of giving birth.

## Galatians 4:20

I wish I could be with you now and change my tone. 📖

I am so puzzled about you.

## Galatians 4:21

Tell me, you who want to be under the law, do you not understand what the law says? 📖

## Galatians 4:22

The Scriptures say that Abraham had two sons. 📖

One was born to the slave woman and the other to the free woman.

## Galatians 4:23

The slave woman's son was born according to the flesh. 📖

The free woman's son was born through God's promise.

**Help with these words:** According to the flesh describes ordinary human birth in this comparison.

## Galatians 4:24

These things are pictures. 📖

The women stand for two covenants.

One covenant comes from Mount Sinai and bears children into slavery.

This is Hagar.

**Help with a word:** A covenant is an agreement with binding promises.

## Galatians 4:25

Hagar stands for Mount Sinai in Arabia. 📖

She corresponds to the present Jerusalem, because she and her children are in slavery.

**Help with the time:** The present Jerusalem means Jerusalem in Paul's time.

## Galatians 4:26

But the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother. 📖

## Galatians 4:27

The Scriptures say, “Rejoice, woman who cannot have children, who has never given birth. 📖

Shout aloud, you who have never felt birth pains.

The woman left alone has more children than the woman with a husband.”

## Galatians 4:28

Now, brothers and sisters, you are children of promise, like Isaac. 📖

## Galatians 4:29

At that time, the son born according to the flesh treated the son born by the Spirit cruelly. 📖

It is the same now.

## Galatians 4:30

But what do the Scriptures say? 📖

“Send away the slave woman and her son.

Her son will never share the inheritance with the free woman's son.”

## Galatians 4:31

So, brothers and sisters, we are children of the free woman, rather than the slave woman. 📖
