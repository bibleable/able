# Galatians 6 — Help each other and keep doing good

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches believers to help each other and take responsibility for their own actions.

He ends by pointing to Christ's cross and the new creation.

## Galatians 6:1

Brothers and sisters, someone may be caught doing wrong. 📖

You who live by the Spirit should gently help that person return to the right way.

Watch yourself too, or you may be tempted.

## Galatians 6:2

Carry each other's burdens. 📖

In this way, you will fulfil Christ's law.

**Help with this picture:** Carrying burdens pictures helping people with the difficulties they face.

## Galatians 6:3

Someone may think they are important when they are nothing. 📖

That person is fooling themselves.

## Galatians 6:4

Each person should test their own work. 📖

Then their reason for pride will be their own work, rather than someone else's.

## Galatians 6:5

Each person should carry their own load. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul calls people to take responsibility for their own work, while also helping others.

## Galatians 6:6

Anyone taught God's message must share all good things with their teacher. 📖

## Galatians 6:7

Do not be fooled. 📖

God cannot be mocked.

A person will gather the crop from whatever they plant.

## Galatians 6:8

Anyone who plants to please their sinful nature will gather destruction from it. 📖

Anyone who plants to please the Spirit will gather life that lasts for ever from the Spirit.

**Help with this picture:** Planting and gathering a crop picture actions and the results they bring.

## Galatians 6:9

Let us not grow tired of doing good. 📖

At the right time, we will gather a crop if we do not give up.

## Galatians 6:10

So, whenever we have the chance, let us do good to everyone. 📖

Especially help the family of faith.

**Help with these words:** The family of faith means fellow believers.

## Galatians 6:11

Look at the large letters I use as I write to you with my own hand! 📖

## Galatians 6:12

Some people want to make a good outward impression. 📖

They try to force you to be circumcised.

They do this only to avoid suffering attacks because of Christ's cross.

**Help with a word:** Circumcision removes the foreskin, the skin covering the end of the penis.

## Galatians 6:13

Those circumcised people do not even keep the law themselves. 📖

Yet they want you circumcised so they can boast about your flesh.

**Help with these words:** Paul refers to those pressuring the believers to accept circumcision.

## Galatians 6:14

May I never boast about anything except the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

Through the cross, the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.

**Help with this picture:** Paul describes a decisive break between his life in Christ and the world's control.

## Galatians 6:15

Circumcision or lack of circumcision means nothing. 📖

What counts is a new creation.

## Galatians 6:16

May peace and mercy come to all who live by this rule, even to the Israel of God. 📖

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## Galatians 6:17

From now on, let no one cause me trouble. 📖

My body carries the marks of Jesus.

**Help with these words:** Paul refers to marks connected with suffering as Jesus's servant.

## Galatians 6:18

Brothers and sisters, may the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. 📖

Amen.
