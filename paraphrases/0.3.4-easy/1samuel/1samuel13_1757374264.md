# 1 Samuel 13 — Saul offers a sacrifice before Samuel comes

**About this chapter**

The Philistines gather against Israel.

Saul offers a sacrifice, and Samuel tells him his kingdom will not last.

## 1 Samuel 13:1

Saul was 30 when he became king and ruled Israel for 42 years. 📖

**About these numbers:** These are the numbers in the supplied BSB source.

## 1 Samuel 13:2

Saul chose 3,000 Israelites. 📖

2,000 stayed with Saul at Michmash and Bethel’s hill country.

1,000 stayed with Jonathan at Gibeah of Benjamin.

Saul sent the other troops home.

## 1 Samuel 13:3

Jonathan attacked the Philistine outpost at Geba, and the Philistines heard. 📖

Saul blew the ram’s horn throughout the land, saying, “Let the Hebrews hear!”

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

**Help with Hebrews:** Here, Hebrews means Israelites.

## 1 Samuel 13:4

All Israel heard, “Saul attacked a Philistine outpost. 📖

Israel has become a stench to the Philistines!”

The people were called to Saul at Gilgal.

**Help with stench:** This pictures becoming deeply offensive to the Philistines.

## 1 Samuel 13:5

The Philistines gathered 3,000 chariots, 6,000 horsemen, and troops as numerous as the sand beside the sea. 📖

They camped at Michmash, east of Beth-aven.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

## 1 Samuel 13:6

Israel’s men saw the danger and the pressure on their troops. 📖

They hid in caves, thickets, rocks, cellars, and cisterns.

**Help with thickets:** Thickets are dense bushes.

**Help with cisterns:** Cisterns are pits used to store water.

## 1 Samuel 13:7

Some Hebrews crossed the Jordan to Gad and Gilead. 📖

Saul stayed at Gilgal, with all his troops shaking in fear.

**Help with Hebrews:** Here, Hebrews means Israelites.

## 1 Samuel 13:8

Saul waited 7 days for Samuel’s appointed time. 📖

Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the troops began leaving Saul.

## 1 Samuel 13:9

Saul said, “Bring me the burnt offering and peace offerings.” 📖

He gave the burnt offering.

**Help with offerings:** A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

A peace offering expresses peace and fellowship with God.

## 1 Samuel 13:10

Saul finished the burnt offering just as Samuel arrived. 📖

Saul went to greet him.

## 1 Samuel 13:11

Samuel asked, “What have you done?” 📖

Saul answered, “I saw troops leaving, you had not come on time, and Philistines were gathering at Michmash.

## 1 Samuel 13:12

I thought, ‘The Philistines will attack me at Gilgal, and I have not asked for the LORD’s favour.’ 📖

I felt forced to give the burnt offering.”

**Help with burnt offering:** This is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

## 1 Samuel 13:13

Samuel said, “You acted foolishly and did not keep the LORD your God’s command. 📖

If you had, the LORD would have established your kingdom over Israel for all time.

## 1 Samuel 13:14

Now your kingdom will not last. 📖

The LORD sought a man after his own heart and appointed him ruler of his people.

You did not keep the LORD’s command.”

**Help with after his own heart:** This pictures someone who fits God’s purposes.

## 1 Samuel 13:15

Samuel left Gilgal for Gibeah in Benjamin. 📖

Saul counted about 600 men with him.

## 1 Samuel 13:16

Saul, his son Jonathan, and their troops stayed at Geba of Benjamin. 📖

The Philistines camped at Michmash.

## 1 Samuel 13:17

Philistine raiders left their camp in 3 groups. 📖

One went towards Ophrah in Shual.

## 1 Samuel 13:18

Another went towards Beth-horon. 📖

The 3rd took the border road overlooking Zeboim’s Valley towards the wilderness.

## 1 Samuel 13:19

No blacksmith was found anywhere in Israel. 📖

The Philistines had said, “The Hebrews must not make swords or spears.”

**Help with blacksmith:** A blacksmith makes and sharpens metal tools.

**Help with Hebrews:** Here, Hebrews means Israelites.

## 1 Samuel 13:20

Israelites went to the Philistines to sharpen plough blades, mattocks, axes, and sickles. 📖

**Help with tools:** Plough blades and mattocks work soil.

Sickles are curved blades for cutting crops.

## 1 Samuel 13:21

Sharpening a plough blade or mattock cost 1 pim. 📖

Sharpening a pitchfork or axe cost a third of a shekel.

Repointing an oxgoad cost a third of a shekel.

**Help with pim and shekel:** These were measures of weight used in payment.

**Help with oxgoad:** An oxgoad is a pointed stick for guiding cattle.

## 1 Samuel 13:22

On battle day, Saul’s and Jonathan’s troops had no swords or spears. 📖

Only Saul and his son Jonathan had weapons.

## 1 Samuel 13:23

A Philistine garrison had gone to the pass at Michmash. 📖

**Help with garrison:** A garrison is a group of soldiers stationed in a place.
