# 1 Samuel 30 — David recovers the captives

**About this chapter**

David finds Ziklag burned and the people taken captive.

He pursues the raiders and establishes equal shares for fighters and those guarding supplies.

## 1 Samuel 30:1

On the 3rd day, David and his men reached Ziklag. 📖

Amalekites had raided the Negev, attacked Ziklag, and burned it.

## 1 Samuel 30:2

They captured the women and everyone there, young and old. 📖

They killed no one but took them away.

## 1 Samuel 30:3

David and his men found the city burned and their wives, sons, and daughters captured. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:4

They cried aloud until they had no strength left. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:5

David’s wives Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, Nabal of Carmel’s widow, were captured too. 📖

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Samuel 30:6

David was deeply distressed because the men spoke of stoning him. 📖

Each grieved for his sons and daughters.

David found strength in the LORD his God.

**Help with stoning:** Stoning means killing someone by throwing stones.

## 1 Samuel 30:7

David told Abiathar the priest, son of Ahimelech, “Bring the ephod.” 📖

Abiathar brought it.

**Help with ephod:** An ephod is a special garment used in worship.

## 1 Samuel 30:8

David asked the LORD, “Should I chase these raiders? 📖

Will I catch them?”

The LORD answered, “Chase them.

You will certainly catch them and rescue the captives.”

## 1 Samuel 30:9

David and his 600 men reached Besor’s stream, where some stayed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:10

200 were too exhausted to cross, but David and 400 continued chasing. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:11

They found an Egyptian in the field and brought him to David. 📖

They gave him water and food.

## 1 Samuel 30:12

He ate a piece of fig cake and 2 raisin clusters and revived. 📖

He had eaten and drunk nothing for 3 days and 3 nights.

## 1 Samuel 30:13

David asked, “Whose servant are you? 📖

Where are you from?”

He answered, “I am an Egyptian, an Amalekite’s slave.

My master abandoned me 3 days ago when I became ill.

## 1 Samuel 30:14

We raided the Cherethites’ Negev, Judah’s territory, and Caleb’s Negev. 📖

We burned Ziklag.”

## 1 Samuel 30:15

David asked, “Will you lead me to the raiders?” 📖

The man answered, “Swear by God not to kill me or return me to my master.

Then I will lead you.”

## 1 Samuel 30:16

He led David to the Amalekites, spread across the land. 📖

They ate, drank, and celebrated the many goods taken from Philistine and Judah territory.

## 1 Samuel 30:17

David struck them from twilight until the next evening. 📖

Only 400 young men escaped, riding camels.

## 1 Samuel 30:18

David recovered everything Amalek took, including his 2 wives. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:19

Nothing was missing: young or old, sons or daughters, or goods. 📖

David brought everything back.

## 1 Samuel 30:20

David took all the flocks and herds. 📖

His men drove them ahead of the other livestock, shouting, “These are David’s captured goods!”

## 1 Samuel 30:21

David returned to the 200 exhausted men left at Besor’s stream. 📖

They came to meet him and his troops, and he greeted them.

## 1 Samuel 30:22

Wicked and worthless men among David’s fighters said, “They did not come, so give them no recovered goods. 📖

Give them only their wives and children and send them away.”

## 1 Samuel 30:23

David answered, “My brothers, do not treat the LORD’s gifts this way. 📖

He protected us and put the attacking raiders in our power.

## 1 Samuel 30:24

Who will accept your proposal? 📖

Those fighting and those guarding supplies will receive equal shares.”

## 1 Samuel 30:25

David made this a law for Israel, remaining from that day until today. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:26

At Ziklag, David sent some captured goods to his friends, Judah’s elders. 📖

He said, “Here are gifts from the LORD’s enemies’ goods.”

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 1 Samuel 30:27

He sent gifts to Bethel, Ramoth Negev, and Jattir. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:28

He also sent gifts to Aroer, Siphmoth, and Eshtemoa. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:29

Gifts went to Racal and the Jerahmeelites’ and Kenites’ cities. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:30

They also went to Hormah, Bor-ashan, and Athach. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:31

Gifts went to Hebron and every place David and his men had travelled. 📖
