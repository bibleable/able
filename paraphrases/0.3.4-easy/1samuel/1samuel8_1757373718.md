# 1 Samuel 8 — Israel asks for a king

**About this chapter**

Israel’s elders ask Samuel to appoint a king.

Samuel gives the LORD’s warning about what a king will take.

## 1 Samuel 8:1

When Samuel grew old, he made his sons judges over Israel. 📖

**Help with judges:** Judges were leaders who settled disputes.

## 1 Samuel 8:2

His first son was Joel, and his 2nd was Abijah. 📖

They were judges at Beersheba.

**Help with judges:** Judges were leaders who settled disputes.

## 1 Samuel 8:3

His sons did not follow his ways. 📖

They sought dishonest gain, accepted bribes, and twisted justice.

**Help with bribes:** Bribes are gifts given to influence decisions unfairly.

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.

## 1 Samuel 8:4

All Israel’s elders gathered and came to Samuel at Ramah. 📖

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 1 Samuel 8:5

They said, “You are old, and your sons do not follow your ways. 📖

Appoint a king to judge us, like all the other nations.”

## 1 Samuel 8:6

Samuel was displeased when they asked for a king to judge them. 📖

He prayed to the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 8:7

The LORD told Samuel, “Listen to everything the people tell you. 📖

They have rejected me as king, not you.

## 1 Samuel 8:8

Since I brought them from Egypt, they have left me and served other gods. 📖

They are treating you the same way.

## 1 Samuel 8:9

Listen to them, but warn them seriously. 📖

Show them how the king ruling over them will behave.”

## 1 Samuel 8:10

Samuel told the people asking for a king all the LORD’s words. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:11

Samuel said, “Your king will behave this way. 📖

He will take your sons to serve his chariots and horses and run before his chariots.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

## 1 Samuel 8:12

He will make some commanders of 1,000 and 50. 📖

Others will plough his ground, harvest his crops, and make his weapons and chariot equipment.

## 1 Samuel 8:13

He will take your daughters to make perfumes, cook, and bake. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:14

He will take your best fields, vineyards, and olive groves and give them to his servants. 📖

**Help with vineyards and groves:** Vineyards grow grapes.

Olive groves are groups of olive trees.

## 1 Samuel 8:15

He will take a tenth of your grain and grape harvest for his officials and servants. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:16

He will take your male and female servants, best cattle, and donkeys for his own use. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:17

He will take a tenth of your flocks. 📖

You yourselves will become his slaves.

## 1 Samuel 8:18

You will beg for relief from the king you chose. 📖

But the LORD will not answer you that day.”

## 1 Samuel 8:19

The people refused to listen to Samuel. 📖

They said, “No!

We must have a king.

## 1 Samuel 8:20

Then we will be like other nations. 📖

Our king will judge us, lead us, and fight our battles.”

## 1 Samuel 8:21

Samuel listened to everything the people said and repeated it before the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:22

The LORD told Samuel, “Listen to them and appoint a king.” 📖

Samuel told Israel’s men, “Go back to your own cities.”
