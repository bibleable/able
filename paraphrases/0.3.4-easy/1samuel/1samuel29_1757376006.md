# 1 Samuel 29 — The Philistines send David back

**About this chapter**

Philistine commanders refuse to take David into battle.

Achish sends David and his men away.

## 1 Samuel 29:1

All Philistine forces gathered at Aphek, while Israel camped beside Jezreel’s spring. 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:2

Philistine leaders marched with units of 100 and 1,000. 📖

David and his men followed with Achish.

## 1 Samuel 29:3

The commanders asked, “What about these Hebrews?” 📖

Achish answered, “This is David, King Saul of Israel’s servant.

He has stayed with me these days, even years.

Since he defected, I found no fault.”

**Help with Hebrews:** Here, Hebrews means Israelites.

**Help with defected:** Defected means left one side to join another.

## 1 Samuel 29:4

The commanders became angry. 📖

They said, “Send him back to his assigned place.

He must not turn against us in battle.

What better way to regain his master’s favour than our men’s heads?

## 1 Samuel 29:5

They dance and sing of David, ‘Saul killed his thousands, and David his tens of thousands.’” 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:6

Achish called David. 📖

“As surely as the LORD lives, you have acted rightly.

I approve of your fighting beside me.

I found no fault since you came.

But the leaders disapprove.

## 1 Samuel 29:7

Go back in peace and do not displease the Philistine leaders.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:8

David asked, “What have I done? 📖

What fault have you found since I came?

Why stop me fighting my lord the king’s enemies?”

## 1 Samuel 29:9

Achish answered, “You please me like God’s angel. 📖

But the commanders insist you must not fight with us.

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

## 1 Samuel 29:10

Rise early with your master’s servants who came with you. 📖

Leave at first light.”

## 1 Samuel 29:11

David and his men rose early to return to Philistine land. 📖

The Philistines marched to Jezreel.
