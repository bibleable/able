# 1 Samuel 16 — David is anointed and serves Saul

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends Samuel to Jesse’s family.

David is anointed, then comes to play the harp for Saul.

## 1 Samuel 16:1

The LORD asked Samuel, “How long will you grieve for Saul, whom I rejected as Israel’s king? 📖

Fill your horn with oil and go to Jesse of Bethlehem.

I chose a king from his sons.”

**Help with horn:** Here, a hollow animal horn holds oil.

## 1 Samuel 16:2

Samuel asked, “How can I go? 📖

Saul will hear and kill me!”

The LORD answered, “Take a young cow and say you came to sacrifice to the LORD.

**Help with sacrifice:** Sacrifice means give an offering to God.

## 1 Samuel 16:3

Invite Jesse to the sacrifice. 📖

I will show you what to do.

Anoint the one I show you.”

**Help with anoint:** Anoint means mark someone for a role by putting oil on them.

## 1 Samuel 16:4

Samuel obeyed and went to Bethlehem. 📖

The town’s elders trembled when they met him and asked, “Do you come in peace?”

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 1 Samuel 16:5

Samuel answered, “In peace, to sacrifice to the LORD. 📖

Set yourselves apart and come.”

He set Jesse and his sons apart and invited them to the sacrifice.

**Help with set apart:** Here, this means prepare for worship.

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

## 1 Samuel 16:6

They arrived, and Samuel saw Eliab. 📖

He said, “Surely the LORD’s anointed one stands before him.”

**Help with anointed one:** This means someone chosen and set apart for a role.

## 1 Samuel 16:7

The LORD told Samuel, “Do not judge his appearance or height. 📖

I rejected him.

People see outward appearance, but the LORD sees the heart.”

**Help with heart:** Here, heart describes a person’s inner life.

## 1 Samuel 16:8

Jesse brought Abinadab before Samuel. 📖

Samuel said, “The LORD has not chosen him either.”

## 1 Samuel 16:9

Jesse brought Shammah. 📖

Samuel said, “The LORD has not chosen him either.”

## 1 Samuel 16:10

Jesse presented 7 sons. 📖

Samuel said, “The LORD has chosen none of these.”

## 1 Samuel 16:11

Samuel asked, “Are these all your sons?” 📖

Jesse answered, “The youngest is tending sheep.”

Samuel said, “Send for him.

We will not sit to eat until he arrives.”

## 1 Samuel 16:12

Jesse brought his youngest son, who had a reddish complexion, beautiful eyes, and a handsome appearance. 📖

The LORD said, “Rise and anoint him.

He is the one.”

**Help with anoint:** Anoint means mark someone for a role by putting oil on them.

## 1 Samuel 16:13

Samuel anointed David with oil from the horn before his brothers. 📖

The LORD’s Spirit rushed upon David from that day onward.

Samuel left for Ramah.

**Help with anointed:** Anointed means marked for a role by the use of oil.

## 1 Samuel 16:14

The LORD’s Spirit left Saul, and a spirit of distress from the LORD tormented him. 📖

**About this spirit:** The source attributes the spirit to the LORD.

The draft does not replace this with a medical diagnosis or a general explanation of mental distress.

## 1 Samuel 16:15

Saul’s servants said, “A spirit of distress from God torments you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:16

Command us to find a skilled harp player. 📖

When the spirit of distress from God comes, he will play and you will be well.”

**Help with harp:** A harp is a musical instrument with strings.

## 1 Samuel 16:17

Saul ordered, “Find someone who plays well and bring him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:18

A servant answered, “I saw a son of Jesse of Bethlehem who plays the harp. 📖

He is brave, a mighty warrior, a skilled speaker, and handsome.

The LORD is with him.”

## 1 Samuel 16:19

Saul sent Jesse messengers, saying, “Send your son David, who tends the sheep.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:20

Jesse loaded a donkey with bread, a skin of wine, and 1 young goat. 📖

He sent these with David to Saul.

**Help with skin:** Here, skin means a container made from animal skin.

## 1 Samuel 16:21

David entered Saul’s service. 📖

Saul loved him greatly, and David became his armour-bearer.

**Help with armour-bearer:** This is someone who carries a soldier’s protective equipment and weapons.

## 1 Samuel 16:22

Saul told Jesse, “Let David stay in my service. 📖

He pleases me.”

## 1 Samuel 16:23

Whenever the spirit from God came on Saul, David played his harp. 📖

Saul found relief and felt better, and the spirit of distress left him.
