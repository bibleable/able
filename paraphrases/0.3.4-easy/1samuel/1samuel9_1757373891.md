# 1 Samuel 9 — Saul meets Samuel

**About this chapter**

Saul searches for his father’s lost donkeys.

His search brings him to Samuel, whom the LORD has told about Saul.

## 1 Samuel 9:1

Kish was a powerful Benjamite. 📖

He was the son of Abiel, son of Zeror, son of Becorath, son of Aphiah of Benjamin.

## 1 Samuel 9:2

Kish had a son named Saul, an outstanding and handsome man. 📖

No Israelite equalled him.

He stood a head taller than anyone else.

## 1 Samuel 9:3

Kish’s donkeys wandered away. 📖

He told Saul, “Take a servant and look for the donkeys.”

## 1 Samuel 9:4

Saul and his servant searched Ephraim’s hill country and Shalishah without finding the donkeys. 📖

They searched Shaalim and Benjamin’s land, but the donkeys were not there.

## 1 Samuel 9:5

At Zuph, Saul told his servant, “Let us go back. 📖

Otherwise, my father will stop worrying about the donkeys and worry about us.”

## 1 Samuel 9:6

The servant said, “A highly respected man of God lives in this city. 📖

Everything he says happens.

Let us go there.

Perhaps he will tell us which way to go.”

## 1 Samuel 9:7

Saul answered, “If we go, what can we give him? 📖

Our bread is gone, and we have no gift for the man of God.

What do we have?”

## 1 Samuel 9:8

The servant answered, “I have a quarter shekel of silver here. 📖

I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way.”

**Help with shekel:** A shekel was a measure of weight used for silver payment.

## 1 Samuel 9:9

In earlier times, Israelites going to ask God said, “Let us go to the seer.” 📖

Today’s prophet was formerly called a seer.

**Help with seer and prophet:** These words describe someone who receives messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 9:10

Saul said, “Good, let us go.” 📖

They went towards the man of God’s city.

## 1 Samuel 9:11

While climbing the hill to the city, they met young women going to draw water. 📖

They asked, “Is the seer here?”

**Help with seer:** A seer is someone who receives messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 9:12

The women answered, “Yes, he is ahead of you. 📖

Hurry!

He came today because the people have a sacrifice at the high place.

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

**Help with high place:** This was a raised place used for worship.

## 1 Samuel 9:13

Enter the city, and you will find him before he goes to eat at the high place. 📖

The people wait for him to bless the sacrifice before the guests eat.

Go now, and you will find him.”

**Help with high place:** This was a raised place used for worship.

## 1 Samuel 9:14

Saul and his servant approached the city. 📖

As they entered, Samuel came towards them on his way to the high place.

**Help with high place:** This was a raised place used for worship.

## 1 Samuel 9:15

The day before Saul arrived, the LORD revealed this to Samuel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:16

“Tomorrow at this time, I will send you a man from Benjamin. 📖

Anoint him ruler over my people Israel.

He will save them from Philistine power.

I have seen my people, because their cry reached me.”

**Help with anoint:** Anoint means mark someone for a role by putting oil on them.

## 1 Samuel 9:17

Samuel saw Saul, and the LORD said, “This is the man I mentioned. 📖

He will rule my people.”

## 1 Samuel 9:18

Saul approached Samuel at the gateway. 📖

He asked, “Please tell me where the seer’s house is.”

**Help with seer:** A seer is someone who receives messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 9:19

Samuel answered, “I am the seer. 📖

Go ahead to the high place and eat with me today.

Tomorrow morning, when I send you away, I will tell you everything in your heart.

**Help with high place:** This was a raised place used for worship.

## 1 Samuel 9:20

Do not worry about the donkeys lost 3 days ago. 📖

They have been found.

Whom does all Israel desire, if not you and your father’s whole family?”

## 1 Samuel 9:21

Saul answered, “I am a Benjamite from Israel’s smallest tribe. 📖

My family group is the least in Benjamin.

Why say this to me?”

**Help with tribe:** A tribe is a large family group.

## 1 Samuel 9:22

Samuel brought Saul and his servant into the hall. 📖

He seated them in the place of honour among about 30 guests.

## 1 Samuel 9:23

Samuel told the cook, “Bring the portion I gave you and told you to set aside.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:24

The cook brought the leg and what was attached to it and set it before Saul. 📖

Samuel said, “This was kept for you.

Eat it.

It was saved for this occasion from when I said, ‘I have invited the people.’”

Saul ate with Samuel that day.

## 1 Samuel 9:25

They came from the high place into the city. 📖

Samuel spoke with Saul on his house’s roof.

**Help with high place:** This was a raised place used for worship.

## 1 Samuel 9:26

They got up early. 📖

Just before dawn, Samuel called to Saul on the roof, “Get ready, and I will send you away!”

Saul got ready, and he and Samuel went out together.

## 1 Samuel 9:27

Near the city’s edge, Samuel told Saul, “Send the servant ahead. 📖

Stay here a while, and I will tell you God’s word.”

The servant went ahead.
