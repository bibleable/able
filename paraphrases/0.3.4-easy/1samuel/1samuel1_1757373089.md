# 1 Samuel 1 — Hannah prays and Samuel is born

**About this chapter**

Hannah asks the LORD for a son.

When Samuel is young, she brings him to serve the LORD at Shiloh.

## 1 Samuel 1:1

Elkanah lived in Ramathaim-zophim, in Ephraim’s hill country. 📖

He was the son of Jeroham, son of Elihu, son of Tohu, son of Zuph, an Ephraimite.

## 1 Samuel 1:2

Elkanah had 2 wives, Hannah and Peninnah. 📖

Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none.

## 1 Samuel 1:3

Every year, Elkanah went from his city to Shiloh to worship and sacrifice to the LORD of Hosts. 📖

Eli’s 2 sons, Hophni and Phinehas, served as the LORD’s priests there.

**Help with sacrifice:** Sacrifice means give an offering to God.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 1 Samuel 1:4

On the day of his sacrifice, Elkanah gave portions to Peninnah and all her sons and daughters. 📖

**Help with sacrifice:** Here, this offering included a meal.

## 1 Samuel 1:5

Elkanah gave Hannah a double portion because he loved her, although the LORD had closed her womb. 📖

**Help with closed her womb:** This means Hannah could not become pregnant.

The source attributes this to the LORD without saying Hannah had done wrong.

## 1 Samuel 1:6

Because the LORD had closed Hannah’s womb, her rival cruelly provoked and mocked her. 📖

**Help with rival:** This refers to Peninnah, Elkanah’s other wife.

**Help with closed her womb:** This means Hannah could not become pregnant.

## 1 Samuel 1:7

This happened year after year. 📖

Whenever Hannah went to the LORD’s house, her rival mocked her until she cried and would not eat.

**Help with the LORD’s house:** This means the place of worship.

## 1 Samuel 1:8

Elkanah asked, “Hannah, why are you crying? 📖

Why will you not eat?

Why is your heart so sad?

Am I not better to you than 10 sons?”

## 1 Samuel 1:9

After they finished eating and drinking at Shiloh, Hannah stood up. 📖

Eli the priest sat on a chair beside the doorpost of the LORD’s temple.

**Help with temple:** A temple is a place of worship.

## 1 Samuel 1:10

Hannah prayed to the LORD in bitter distress and cried many tears. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:11

Hannah made a vow. 📖

“LORD of Hosts, please look at your servant’s suffering and remember me.

Do not forget your servant.

If you give me a son, I will dedicate him to the LORD for his whole life.

No razor will ever touch his head.”

**Help with vow:** A vow is a serious promise.

**Help with dedicate:** Dedicate means set apart for God.

**Help with razor:** Here, Hannah promises that his hair will not be cut.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 1 Samuel 1:12

As Hannah kept praying before the LORD, Eli watched her mouth. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:13

Hannah prayed in her heart. 📖

Her lips moved, but no sound could be heard.

Eli thought she was drunk.

## 1 Samuel 1:14

Eli said, “How long will you be drunk? 📖

Put away your wine!”

## 1 Samuel 1:15

Hannah answered, “No, my lord. 📖

I am deeply troubled in spirit.

I have drunk neither wine nor strong drink.

I have poured out my soul before the LORD.

**Help with poured out my soul:** Hannah means she has told God her deep distress.

## 1 Samuel 1:16

Do not think your servant is a wicked woman. 📖

All this time, I have prayed from my deep pain and grief.”

## 1 Samuel 1:17

Eli replied, “Go in peace. 📖

May Israel’s God grant the request you made to him.”

## 1 Samuel 1:18

Hannah said, “May you show your servant favour.” 📖

She left, began eating, and no longer looked sad.

## 1 Samuel 1:19

Early the next morning, they worshipped before the LORD and returned home to Ramah. 📖

Elkanah had sex with his wife Hannah, and the LORD remembered her.

## 1 Samuel 1:20

In time, Hannah became pregnant and gave birth to a son. 📖

She named him Samuel, saying, “I asked the LORD for him.”

## 1 Samuel 1:21

Elkanah and his whole household went to give the annual sacrifice to the LORD and fulfil his vow. 📖

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

**Help with vow:** A vow is a serious promise.

## 1 Samuel 1:22

Hannah did not go. 📖

She told her husband, “When the boy is weaned, I will bring him before the LORD to stay permanently.”

**Help with weaned:** Weaned means no longer feeding on his mother’s milk.

## 1 Samuel 1:23

Elkanah answered, “Do what you think best. 📖

Stay until you have weaned him.

May the LORD confirm his word.”

Hannah stayed and fed her son her milk until he was weaned.

**Help with weaned:** Weaned means no longer feeding on his mother’s milk.

## 1 Samuel 1:24

After weaning Samuel, Hannah took him to the LORD’s house at Shiloh, although he was still young. 📖

She brought a 3-year-old bull, 1 ephah of flour, and a skin of wine.

**Help with ephah:** An ephah was a measure for dry goods.

**Help with skin:** Here, skin means a container made from animal skin.

## 1 Samuel 1:25

They slaughtered the bull and brought the boy to Eli. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:26

Hannah said, “Please, my lord, as surely as you live, I am the woman who stood beside you here. 📖

I was praying to the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 1:27

I prayed for this boy, and the LORD gave me what I asked. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:28

Now I dedicate him to the LORD. 📖

He is dedicated to the LORD for as long as he lives.”

They worshipped the LORD there.

**Help with dedicate:** Dedicate means set apart for God.
