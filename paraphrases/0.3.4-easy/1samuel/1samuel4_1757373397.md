# 1 Samuel 4 — Israel loses the ark

**About this chapter**

Israel fights the Philistines and loses the ark of God.

Eli and his sons die, and Phinehas’s wife dies after giving birth.

## 1 Samuel 4:1

Samuel’s word came to all Israel. 📖

Israel went to fight the Philistines and camped at Ebenezer.

The Philistines camped at Aphek.

## 1 Samuel 4:2

The Philistines took positions against Israel. 📖

As the battle spread, they defeated Israel and killed about 4,000 men on the battlefield.

## 1 Samuel 4:3

The troops returned, and Israel’s elders asked, “Why has the LORD brought defeat on us before the Philistines today? 📖

Bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant from Shiloh.

Let it go with us and save us from our enemies.”

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

**Help with ark of the covenant:** This sacred chest was associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Samuel 4:4

Men went to Shiloh and brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD of Hosts. 📖

The LORD sits as king between the cherubim.

Eli’s 2 sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were with God’s ark.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with cherubim:** Cherubim are heavenly beings represented on the ark.

## 1 Samuel 4:5

The ark of the LORD’s covenant entered the camp. 📖

All Israel shouted so loudly that the ground shook.

**Help with ark of the covenant:** This sacred chest was associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 4:6

The Philistines heard and asked, “Why is there such loud shouting in the Hebrews’ camp?” 📖

They realised the LORD’s ark had entered the camp.

**Help with Hebrews:** Here, Hebrews means the Israelites.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 4:7

The Philistines became afraid. 📖

They said, “Gods have entered their camp!

Woe to us!

Nothing like this happened before.

**Help with woe:** Woe expresses fear of disaster.

**About gods:** This is how the Philistines describe what they think has happened.

## 1 Samuel 4:8

Woe to us! 📖

Who will save us from these mighty gods?

These gods struck Egypt with all kinds of plagues in the wilderness.

**About these claims:** The Philistines are speaking.

Their words are retained as their account.

**Help with plagues:** Plagues are severe disasters or spreading diseases.

## 1 Samuel 4:9

Be brave and be men, Philistines! 📖

Otherwise, you will serve the Hebrews as they served you.

Be men and fight!”

**About be men:** The speakers link fighting courage with being men.

The source’s wording is retained as their speech.

## 1 Samuel 4:10

The Philistines fought and defeated Israel. 📖

Each Israelite fled to his tent.

The killing was very great: 30,000 Israelite foot soldiers died.

## 1 Samuel 4:11

God’s ark was captured, and Eli’s 2 sons, Hophni and Phinehas, died. 📖

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 4:12

That day, a Benjamite ran from the battle to Shiloh with torn clothes and dirt on his head. 📖

**Help with torn clothes and dirt:** These were signs of grief.

## 1 Samuel 4:13

Eli sat on his chair beside the road, watching anxiously for God’s ark. 📖

His heart trembled for it.

The man entered the city and reported, and the whole city cried out.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 4:14

Eli heard the cries and asked, “Why this uproar?” 📖

The man hurried to report to Eli.

## 1 Samuel 4:15

Eli was 98 years old. 📖

His gaze was fixed because he could not see.

## 1 Samuel 4:16

The man said, “I have just come from the battle. 📖

I fled today.”

Eli asked, “What happened, my son?”

## 1 Samuel 4:17

The messenger answered, “Israel fled before the Philistines, and many people were killed. 📖

Your sons Hophni and Phinehas are both dead.

God’s ark was captured.”

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 4:18

When God’s ark was mentioned, Eli fell backwards from his chair beside the city gate. 📖

He was old and heavy, and he broke his neck and died.

Eli had judged Israel for 40 years.

**Help with judged:** Here, judged means served as a leader.

## 1 Samuel 4:19

Eli’s daughter-in-law, Phinehas’s wife, was pregnant and near birth. 📖

She heard that God’s ark was captured and her father-in-law and husband were dead.

She collapsed and gave birth as labour pains seized her.

**Help with father-in-law:** A father-in-law is the father of a person’s husband or wife.

## 1 Samuel 4:20

As she was dying, the women helping her said, “Do not fear! 📖

You have given birth to a son!”

She did not answer or pay attention.

## 1 Samuel 4:21

She named the boy Ichabod. 📖

She said, “The glory has left Israel.”

God’s ark had been captured, and her father-in-law and husband had been killed.

**Help with glory:** Glory means greatness and honour.

## 1 Samuel 4:22

She said, “The glory has left Israel, because God’s ark was captured.” 📖

**Help with glory:** Glory means greatness and honour.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.
