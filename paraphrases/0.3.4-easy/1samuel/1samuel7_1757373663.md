# 1 Samuel 7 — Israel turns to the LORD

**About this chapter**

Samuel calls Israel to leave other gods.

He prays during a Philistine attack, and the LORD answers.

## 1 Samuel 7:1

Kiriath-jearim’s men took the LORD’s ark to Abinadab’s house on the hill. 📖

They set apart his son Eleazar to guard it.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 7:2

The ark stayed in Kiriath-jearim a long time: 20 years. 📖

All Israel’s people sought the LORD with grief.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 7:3

Samuel told all Israel, “If you return to the LORD with all your hearts, remove the foreign gods and Ashtoreths. 📖

Prepare your hearts for the LORD and serve only him.

He will rescue you from the Philistines’ power.”

**Help with Ashtoreths:** These were goddesses worshipped by peoples around Israel.

## 1 Samuel 7:4

Israel removed the Baals and Ashtoreths and served only the LORD. 📖

**Help with Baals and Ashtoreths:** These were gods and goddesses worshipped by peoples around Israel.

## 1 Samuel 7:5

Samuel said, “Gather all Israel at Mizpah. 📖

I will pray to the LORD for you.”

## 1 Samuel 7:6

They gathered at Mizpah, drew water, and poured it out before the LORD. 📖

They fasted and confessed, “We have sinned against the LORD.”

Samuel judged Israel at Mizpah.

**Help with fasted:** Fasted means did not eat for a time.

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong against God.

**Help with judged:** Here, judged means led and settled disputes.

## 1 Samuel 7:7

The Philistine rulers heard Israel had gathered at Mizpah and marched towards Israel. 📖

Israel heard and feared the Philistines.

## 1 Samuel 7:8

Israel told Samuel, “Keep crying to the LORD our God for us, so he saves us from the Philistines.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:9

Samuel offered a lamb still feeding on its mother’s milk as a whole burnt offering to the LORD. 📖

He cried to the LORD for Israel, and the LORD answered.

**Help with burnt offering:** This is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

## 1 Samuel 7:10

Samuel offered the sacrifice as the Philistines approached to fight Israel. 📖

That day, the LORD thundered loudly against the Philistines.

He threw them into confusion, and they fled before Israel.

## 1 Samuel 7:11

Israel’s men charged from Mizpah and chased the Philistines. 📖

They struck them down as far as the area below Beth-car.

## 1 Samuel 7:12

Samuel set up a stone between Mizpah and Shen. 📖

He named it Ebenezer, saying, “The LORD has helped us this far.”

## 1 Samuel 7:13

The Philistines were subdued and stopped invading Israel’s territory. 📖

The LORD’s hand opposed them throughout Samuel’s life.

**Help with subdued:** Subdued means brought under control.

**Help with hand:** This pictures the LORD’s power.

## 1 Samuel 7:14

The cities the Philistines took, from Ekron to Gath, were restored to Israel. 📖

Israel also rescued the surrounding land from Philistine power.

Israel and the Amorites were at peace.

## 1 Samuel 7:15

Samuel judged Israel for his whole life. 📖

**Help with judged:** Here, judged means led and settled disputes.

## 1 Samuel 7:16

Every year, Samuel travelled from Bethel to Gilgal to Mizpah, judging Israel in all these places. 📖

**Help with judging:** Here, judging means leading and settling disputes.

## 1 Samuel 7:17

Samuel returned to Ramah, where he lived and judged Israel. 📖

He built an altar to the LORD there.

**Help with judged:** Here, judged means led and settled disputes.

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.
