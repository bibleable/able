# 1 Samuel 24 — David spares Saul in a cave

**About this chapter**

David cuts Saul’s robe but refuses to kill him.

He speaks to Saul, who acknowledges David’s good treatment.

## 1 Samuel 24:1

Saul returned from chasing the Philistines and heard David was in En-gedi’s wilderness. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:2

Saul took 3,000 chosen Israelites to seek David near the Rocks of the Wild Goats. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:3

By roadside sheep pens, Saul entered a cave to relieve himself. 📖

David and his men hid deep inside.

**Help with relieve himself:** This means use a place as a toilet.

## 1 Samuel 24:4

David’s men said, “The LORD promised you this day, handing over your enemy to do what you wish.” 📖

David crept up and secretly cut a corner from Saul’s robe.

**About the promise:** The men make this claim to David.

## 1 Samuel 24:5

David’s conscience troubled him for cutting Saul’s robe. 📖

**Help with conscience:** Conscience is a person’s sense of right and wrong.

## 1 Samuel 24:6

David said, “May the LORD forbid me from acting against my master, his anointed one. 📖

I must not raise my hand against the LORD’s anointed.”

**Help with anointed one:** This means someone chosen and set apart for a role, here as king.

## 1 Samuel 24:7

David stopped his men from attacking Saul. 📖

Saul left the cave and continued.

## 1 Samuel 24:8

David came from the cave and called, “My lord the king!” 📖

Saul looked back, and David bowed face down in respect.

## 1 Samuel 24:9

David said, “Why listen to people claiming I plan to harm you? 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:10

Today you saw the LORD put you in my power in the cave. 📖

People told me to kill you, but I spared you.

I said I would not strike my lord, the LORD’s anointed one.

**Help with anointed one:** Here, this means the king, chosen through anointing with oil.

## 1 Samuel 24:11

My father, see your robe’s corner in my hand. 📖

I cut it but did not kill you.

See that I have no evil or rebellion in my hands.

I did not sin against you, although you hunt me to kill me.

**Help with sin:** Here, sin means do wrong.

## 1 Samuel 24:12

May the LORD judge between us and take revenge on you. 📖

My hand will never act against you.

## 1 Samuel 24:13

The old proverb says, ‘Wickedness comes from wicked people.’ 📖

My hand will never act against you.

**Help with proverb:** A proverb is a saying people repeat.

## 1 Samuel 24:14

Whom has Israel’s king come against? 📖

Whom are you chasing?

A dead dog?

A flea?

**Help with these pictures:** David compares himself to something insignificant.

## 1 Samuel 24:15

May the LORD judge us, notice my case, speak for me, and rescue me from your power.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:16

David finished speaking. 📖

Saul called, “Is that your voice, David my son?”

Saul cried aloud.

## 1 Samuel 24:17

Saul told David, “You are more righteous than I. 📖

You repaid me with good, but I repaid you with evil.

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means right before God.

## 1 Samuel 24:18

Today you showed your kindness. 📖

The LORD put me in your power, but you did not kill me.

## 1 Samuel 24:19

Does someone find an enemy and send him away unharmed? 📖

May the LORD reward you with good for today’s action.

## 1 Samuel 24:20

I know for certain you will be king and Israel’s kingdom will be established in your hands. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:21

Swear by the LORD not to cut off my descendants or erase my name from my father’s family.” 📖

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

## 1 Samuel 24:22

David swore to Saul. 📖

Saul went home, and David and his men went to the stronghold.

**Help with stronghold:** A stronghold is a place protected from attack.
