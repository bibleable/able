# 1 Samuel 19 — David escapes Saul

**About this chapter**

Jonathan speaks for David, and Michal helps David escape.

David goes to Samuel, and Saul’s pursuit leads to Naioth.

## 1 Samuel 19:1

Saul ordered Jonathan and all his servants to kill David. 📖

Jonathan greatly delighted in David.

## 1 Samuel 19:2

Jonathan warned David, “My father intends to kill you. 📖

Be careful tomorrow morning.

Find a secret place and hide.

## 1 Samuel 19:3

I will stand with my father in the field where you are and ask about you. 📖

If I learn anything, I will tell you.”

## 1 Samuel 19:4

Jonathan praised David to Saul. 📖

He said, “The king should not sin against David.

David did not sin against you.

His actions helped you greatly.

**Help with sin:** Here, sin means do wrong.

## 1 Samuel 19:5

He risked his life killing the Philistine, and the LORD brought Israel great rescue. 📖

You saw and rejoiced.

Why sin against innocent blood by killing David without reason?”

**Help with innocent blood:** This means the death of someone who should not be killed.

## 1 Samuel 19:6

Saul listened to Jonathan and swore, “As surely as the LORD lives, David will not die.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:7

Jonathan called David and told him everything. 📖

He brought David back to Saul as before.

## 1 Samuel 19:8

War began again, and David struck the Philistines so powerfully that they fled. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:9

Saul sat at home with his spear, and a spirit of distress from the LORD came on him. 📖

David played the harp.

## 1 Samuel 19:10

Saul tried pinning David to the wall with his spear. 📖

David escaped, and the spear struck the wall.

David fled that night.

## 1 Samuel 19:11

Saul sent messengers to watch David’s house and kill him in the morning. 📖

Michal warned David, “Escape tonight, or you will be dead tomorrow!”

## 1 Samuel 19:12

Michal lowered David through the window, and he escaped. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:13

Michal put a household idol in the bed with goat hair at its head and a garment over it. 📖

**Help with idol:** An idol is an object treated as a god.

## 1 Samuel 19:14

Saul sent men to seize David. 📖

Michal said, “He is ill.”

## 1 Samuel 19:15

Saul sent the men back to see David. 📖

He ordered, “Bring him in his bed so I can kill him.”

## 1 Samuel 19:16

They entered and found the idol with goat hair at its head. 📖

**Help with idol:** An idol is an object treated as a god.

## 1 Samuel 19:17

Saul asked Michal, “Why trick me and let my enemy escape?” 📖

Michal answered, “He threatened, ‘Help me escape, or I will kill you!’”

**About the threat:** Michal gives this account to Saul.

The narrator does not say that David made this threat.

## 1 Samuel 19:18

David escaped to Samuel at Ramah and told him everything Saul did. 📖

David and Samuel went to Naioth and stayed.

## 1 Samuel 19:19

Saul heard David was at Naioth in Ramah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:20

Saul sent men to seize David. 📖

They saw prophets prophesying under Samuel’s leadership.

God’s Spirit came on the messengers, and they prophesied too.

**Help with prophets and prophesied:** These words concern speaking messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 19:21

Saul heard and sent more men, who prophesied too. 📖

He sent men a 3rd time, and they also prophesied.

**Help with prophesied:** Prophesied means spoke a message from God.

## 1 Samuel 19:22

Saul went himself to Ramah. 📖

At Secu’s large cistern, he asked where Samuel and David were.

People answered, “At Naioth in Ramah.”

**Help with cistern:** A cistern is a pit used to store water.

## 1 Samuel 19:23

Saul went to Naioth in Ramah. 📖

God’s Spirit came on him too, and he prophesied all the way there.

**Help with prophesied:** Prophesied means spoke a message from God.

## 1 Samuel 19:24

Saul took off his robes and prophesied before Samuel. 📖

He collapsed and lay naked all day and night.

This is why people say, “Is Saul among the prophets too?”

**Help with prophesied and prophets:** These words concern speaking messages from God.
