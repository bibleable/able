# 1 Samuel 2 — Hannah’s prayer and Eli’s sons

**About this chapter**

Hannah praises the LORD.

Samuel serves at Shiloh, while Eli’s sons do wrong and a man of God announces judgment.

## 1 Samuel 2:1

Hannah prayed, “My heart rejoices in the LORD. 📖

My horn is lifted high in the LORD.

I speak boldly against my enemies because I rejoice in your salvation.

**Help with horn:** An animal’s horn pictures strength and honour.

**Help with salvation:** Salvation means being saved.

## 1 Samuel 2:2

No one is holy like the LORD. 📖

There is no one besides you!

There is no Rock like our God.

**Help with holy:** Holy means pure and set apart.

**Help with Rock:** This pictures God’s strength and reliability.

## 1 Samuel 2:3

Do not boast proudly or speak arrogantly. 📖

The LORD is a God who knows, and he weighs actions.

**Help with weighs actions:** This means judges what people do.

## 1 Samuel 2:4

The mighty warriors’ bows are broken, but weak people are given strength. 📖

**Help with bows:** Bows are weapons for shooting arrows.

## 1 Samuel 2:5

Those who had plenty hire themselves out for food, but hungry people hunger no more. 📖

A woman without children gives birth to 7, but a woman with many sons wastes away.

## 1 Samuel 2:6

The LORD brings death and gives life. 📖

He brings down to Sheol and raises up.

**Help with Sheol:** Sheol refers to the place of the dead.

## 1 Samuel 2:7

The LORD sends poverty and wealth. 📖

He brings people low and lifts them high.

## 1 Samuel 2:8

He raises poor people from the dust and needy people from the ash heap. 📖

He seats them among princes and gives them a throne of honour.

The earth’s foundations belong to the LORD, and he set the world on them.

**Help with these pictures:** Dust and ashes picture low status.

A throne pictures honour and authority.

## 1 Samuel 2:9

He guards his faithful people’s steps, but wicked people perish in darkness. 📖

No one wins by their own strength.

## 1 Samuel 2:10

Those who oppose the LORD will be shattered. 📖

He will thunder against them from heaven.

The LORD will judge the earth’s farthest parts and give power to his king.

He will lift high the horn of his anointed one.”

**Help with horn:** This pictures strength and honour.

**Help with anointed one:** This means someone chosen and set apart for a role.

## 1 Samuel 2:11

Elkanah went home to Ramah. 📖

The boy served the LORD under Eli the priest.

## 1 Samuel 2:12

Eli’s sons were wicked men who had no regard for the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:13

They had no regard for the priests’ custom with the people. 📖

While a sacrifice’s meat boiled, the priest’s servant came with a 3-pronged meat fork.

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

**Help with 3-pronged:** The fork had 3 pointed parts.

## 1 Samuel 2:14

The servant plunged the fork into the pan, kettle, large pot, or cooking pot. 📖

The priest took whatever the fork brought up.

They treated all Israelites coming to Shiloh this way.

## 1 Samuel 2:15

Even before the fat burned, the priest’s servant came to the person sacrificing. 📖

The servant said, “Give the priest meat to roast.

He will accept only raw meat, not boiled.”

## 1 Samuel 2:16

Someone might say, “Burn the fat first, then take what you want.” 📖

The servant would answer, “No, give it now!

If you refuse, I will take it by force!”

## 1 Samuel 2:17

These young men’s sin was very serious before the LORD. 📖

They treated the LORD’s offering with contempt.

**Help with sin:** Sin means wrongdoing against God.

**Help with contempt:** Contempt means scorn.

## 1 Samuel 2:18

The boy Samuel served before the LORD, wearing a linen ephod. 📖

**Help with linen ephod:** This was a special garment made from linen cloth.

## 1 Samuel 2:19

Every year, Samuel’s mother made him a little robe. 📖

She brought it when she and her husband came to give the annual sacrifice.

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

## 1 Samuel 2:20

Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife. 📖

He said, “May the LORD give you children by this woman instead of the one she dedicated to him.”

Then they went home.

**Help with dedicated:** Dedicated means set apart for God.

## 1 Samuel 2:21

The LORD cared for Hannah. 📖

She became pregnant and gave birth to 3 sons and 2 daughters.

Samuel grew up in the LORD’s presence.

## 1 Samuel 2:22

Eli was very old. 📖

He heard everything his sons did to all Israel.

They also had sex with women serving at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance.

**Help with Tent of Meeting:** This was a place associated with meeting God in worship.

## 1 Samuel 2:23

Eli asked his sons, “Why do you do these things? 📖

All these people tell me about your wicked deeds.

## 1 Samuel 2:24

No, my sons. 📖

The report spreading among the LORD’s people is not good.

## 1 Samuel 2:25

If someone sins against another person, God can intercede. 📖

If someone sins against the LORD, who can intercede?”

The sons would not listen, because the LORD intended to put them to death.

**Help with sins:** Sins means does wrong against God.

**Help with intercede:** Intercede means speak or act on someone’s behalf.

## 1 Samuel 2:26

Samuel kept growing in height and in favour with the LORD and people. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:27

A man of God came to Eli. 📖

He said, “The LORD asks, ‘Did I not clearly reveal myself to your father’s family in Egypt under Pharaoh’s household?

**Help with Pharaoh:** Pharaoh was the title of Egypt’s king.

## 1 Samuel 2:28

From all Israel’s tribes, I chose your father as my priest. 📖

He would offer sacrifices at my altar, burn incense, and wear an ephod before me.

I gave your father’s family all Israel’s food offerings.

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

**Help with incense:** Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

**Help with ephod:** An ephod is a special garment.

## 1 Samuel 2:29

Why do you kick at the sacrifices and offerings I commanded for my dwelling place? 📖

You honour your sons more than me.

You fatten yourselves on the best of all my people Israel’s offerings.’

**Help with kick at:** This pictures treating God’s offerings with contempt.

## 1 Samuel 2:30

The LORD, Israel’s God, declares, ‘I said your family and your father’s family would walk before me forever. 📖

But now the LORD declares: never!

I will honour those who honour me.

Those who despise me will be scorned.

**Help with walk before me:** This means live in service to God.

**Help with despise:** Despise means treat with scorn.

## 1 Samuel 2:31

Days are coming when I will cut off your strength and your father’s family’s strength. 📖

No one in it will reach old age.

## 1 Samuel 2:32

You will see distress in my dwelling place. 📖

Despite all the good in Israel, no one in your family will ever again reach old age.

## 1 Samuel 2:33

For any of you I do not cut off from my altar, I will make your eyes fail.

I will make your heart grieve. 📖

All your descendants will die by human swords.

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

## 1 Samuel 2:34

This will be your sign concerning your 2 sons, Hophni and Phinehas. 📖

Both will die on the same day.

## 1 Samuel 2:35

I will raise up a faithful priest for myself. 📖

He will do everything in my heart and mind.

I will establish a lasting family for him.

He will walk before my anointed one for all time.

**Help with walk before:** This means live in service before someone.

**Help with anointed one:** This means someone chosen and set apart for a role.

## 1 Samuel 2:36

Everyone left in your family will bow before him for a piece of silver or a little bread. 📖

They will beg, “Please give me a priestly position so I can eat a piece of bread.”’”
