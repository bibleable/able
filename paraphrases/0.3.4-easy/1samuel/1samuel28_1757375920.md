# 1 Samuel 28 — Saul visits the medium at Endor

**About this chapter**

The LORD does not answer Saul’s inquiry.

Saul visits a medium, and the account describes Samuel speaking to him.

## 1 Samuel 28:1

The Philistines gathered to fight Israel. 📖

Achish told David, “You and your men must fight with me.”

## 1 Samuel 28:2

David answered, “You will see what your servant can do.” 📖

Achish said, “Then you will be my bodyguard for life.”

## 1 Samuel 28:3

Samuel had died, and all Israel had grieved and buried him in his city Ramah. 📖

Saul had removed mediums and spiritists from the land.

**Help with mediums and spiritists:** These are people who claim to consult the dead or spirits.

## 1 Samuel 28:4

The Philistines camped at Shunem. 📖

Saul gathered Israel at Gilboa.

## 1 Samuel 28:5

Saul saw the Philistine army and trembled violently in fear. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:6

Saul asked the LORD, but received no answer through dreams, Urim, or prophets. 📖

**Help with Urim:** Urim was used in seeking a decision from God.

**Help with prophets:** Prophets bring messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 28:7

Saul ordered his servants to find a woman who was a medium to consult. 📖

They answered, “There is one at Endor.”

**Help with medium:** A medium claims to consult the dead or spirits.

## 1 Samuel 28:8

Saul disguised himself in different clothes and went at night with 2 men. 📖

He asked the woman, “Consult a spirit and bring up the one I name.”

## 1 Samuel 28:9

She answered, “You know Saul killed the mediums and spiritists. 📖

Why trap me and get me killed?”

**Help with mediums and spiritists:** These are people who claim to consult the dead or spirits.

**About killed:** This is the woman’s account of Saul’s actions.

## 1 Samuel 28:10

Saul swore by the LORD, “As surely as the LORD lives, you will not be punished for this.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:11

She asked whom to bring up. 📖

Saul answered, “Samuel.”

## 1 Samuel 28:12

The woman saw Samuel and cried loudly. 📖

She told Saul, “Why deceive me?

You are Saul!”

**About Samuel:** The narrator names Samuel here.

The draft retains that identification without replacing it with a different explanation.

## 1 Samuel 28:13

The king said, “Do not fear. 📖

What do you see?”

She answered, “A god coming from the earth.”

**About a god:** This is the woman’s description.

## 1 Samuel 28:14

Saul asked what he looked like. 📖

She described an old man wearing a robe.

Saul knew it was Samuel and bowed face down in respect.

## 1 Samuel 28:15

Samuel asked, “Why disturb me by bringing me up?” 📖

Saul answered, “I am deeply troubled.

Philistines attack, and God has left me.

He no longer answers by prophets or dreams.

I called you to tell me what to do.”

## 1 Samuel 28:16

Samuel asked, “Why consult me when the LORD left you and became your enemy? 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:17

The LORD did what he said through me. 📖

He tore the kingdom from you and gave it to your neighbour David.

## 1 Samuel 28:18

You did not obey or carry out his burning anger against Amalek. 📖

That is why the LORD did this today.

## 1 Samuel 28:19

The LORD will hand you and Israel over to the Philistines. 📖

Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me.

The LORD will hand Israel’s army over too.”

**Help with with me:** Samuel speaks as one who has died.

The draft does not add a detailed account of life after death.

## 1 Samuel 28:20

Saul fell flat, terrified by Samuel’s words. 📖

He had no strength because he had eaten nothing all day and night.

## 1 Samuel 28:21

The woman saw Saul’s distress and said, “Your servant obeyed and risked her life for you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:22

Please listen now and let me give you bread. 📖

Eat and gain strength to leave.”

## 1 Samuel 28:23

Saul refused, “I will not eat.” 📖

His servants and the woman urged him, and he listened.

He rose and sat on the bed.

## 1 Samuel 28:24

The woman quickly slaughtered her fattened calf. 📖

She kneaded flour and baked unleavened bread.

**Help with unleavened:** Unleavened bread is made without yeast or another raising agent.

## 1 Samuel 28:25

She served Saul and his servants. 📖

They ate, rose, and left that night.
