# 1 Samuel 11 — Saul rescues Jabesh-gilead

**About this chapter**

Nahash threatens the people of Jabesh-gilead.

Saul gathers Israel and defeats the Ammonites.

## 1 Samuel 11:1

Nahash the Ammonite surrounded Jabesh-gilead. 📖

Jabesh’s men said, “Make a treaty with us, and we will serve you.”

**Help with treaty:** A treaty is a formal agreement.

## 1 Samuel 11:2

Nahash answered, “I will agree only if I can put out everyone’s right eye and shame all Israel.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:3

Jabesh’s elders answered, “Give us 7 days to send messengers throughout Israel. 📖

If no one saves us, we will surrender.”

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 1 Samuel 11:4

The messengers reached Gibeah of Saul and told the people. 📖

Everyone cried aloud.

## 1 Samuel 11:5

Saul returned from the field behind his oxen. 📖

He asked, “What is wrong?

Why are the people crying?”

They told him Jabesh’s message.

**Help with oxen:** Oxen are large cattle used for work.

## 1 Samuel 11:6

Saul heard, and God’s Spirit rushed upon him. 📖

He burned with great anger.

## 1 Samuel 11:7

Saul cut 2 oxen into pieces and sent the pieces throughout Israel by messengers. 📖

He proclaimed, “This will happen to anyone’s oxen who does not follow Saul and Samuel.”

Terror from the LORD fell on the people, and they came out as one.

**Help with oxen:** Oxen are large cattle used for work.

## 1 Samuel 11:8

Saul counted them at Bezek: 300,000 Israelites and 30,000 men of Judah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:9

They told the messengers, “Tell Jabesh-gilead’s men they will be rescued tomorrow when the sun is hot.” 📖

The messengers told Jabesh’s men, and they rejoiced.

## 1 Samuel 11:10

Jabesh’s men told Nahash, “Tomorrow we will come out. 📖

Do whatever seems good to you.”

## 1 Samuel 11:11

The next day, Saul divided the troops into 3 groups. 📖

During the morning watch, they entered the Ammonites’ camp and killed them until the day’s hottest time.

The survivors scattered so widely that no 2 remained together.

## 1 Samuel 11:12

The people asked Samuel, “Who said Saul should not rule us? 📖

Bring them here to be killed!”

## 1 Samuel 11:13

Saul ordered, “No one will die today. 📖

Today the LORD has saved Israel.”

## 1 Samuel 11:14

Samuel told the people, “Come to Gilgal and renew the kingship.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:15

Everyone went to Gilgal and confirmed Saul as king before the LORD. 📖

They sacrificed peace offerings, and Saul and all Israel rejoiced greatly.

**Help with peace offerings:** These sacrifices express peace and fellowship with God.
