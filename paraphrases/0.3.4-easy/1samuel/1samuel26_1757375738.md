# 1 Samuel 26 — David spares Saul again

**About this chapter**

David and Abishai enter Saul’s sleeping camp.

David takes Saul’s spear and water jug but refuses to kill him.

## 1 Samuel 26:1

The Ziphites told Saul at Gibeah, “Is David not hiding on Hachilah’s hill, opposite Jeshimon?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:2

Saul took 3,000 chosen Israelites to search Ziph’s wilderness for David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:3

Saul camped by the road on Hachilah’s hill, opposite Jeshimon. 📖

David lived in the wilderness and learned Saul had followed.

## 1 Samuel 26:4

David sent spies to confirm Saul had arrived. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:5

David went to Saul’s camp and saw where Saul and army commander Abner son of Ner lay. 📖

Saul lay inside the camp’s inner circle, surrounded by troops.

## 1 Samuel 26:6

David asked Ahimelech the Hittite and Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab’s brother, “Who will enter Saul’s camp with me?” 📖

Abishai answered, “I will.”

## 1 Samuel 26:7

That night, David and Abishai reached Saul, asleep inside the camp. 📖

His spear stood in the ground beside his head.

Abner and the troops lay around him.

## 1 Samuel 26:8

Abishai said, “God put your enemy in your power today. 📖

Let me spear him into the ground with one blow.

I will not need a second!”

## 1 Samuel 26:9

David answered, “Do not kill him. 📖

Who can strike the LORD’s anointed and remain innocent?”

**Help with anointed:** This means chosen and set apart, here as king.

## 1 Samuel 26:10

David added, “As surely as the LORD lives, he himself will strike Saul. 📖

Saul’s day will come to die, or he will die in battle.

## 1 Samuel 26:11

May the LORD forbid me from striking his anointed one. 📖

Take the spear and water jug by his head, and let us go.”

**Help with anointed one:** Here, this means the king, chosen through anointing with oil.

## 1 Samuel 26:12

David took Saul’s spear and water jug, and they left. 📖

No one saw, knew, or woke.

Everyone slept because the LORD had sent a deep sleep.

## 1 Samuel 26:13

David crossed over and stood on a distant mountaintop, with a wide gap between them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:14

David called to the troops and Abner son of Ner, “Will you not answer, Abner?” 📖

Abner asked, “Who calls to the king?”

## 1 Samuel 26:15

David answered, “Are you not a man without equal in Israel? 📖

Why did you not protect your king when someone came to kill him?

## 1 Samuel 26:16

You did wrong. 📖

As surely as the LORD lives, you all deserve death for not guarding your lord, the LORD’s anointed.

Where are the king’s spear and water jug from beside his head?”

**Help with anointed:** Here, this means the king, chosen through anointing with oil.

## 1 Samuel 26:17

Saul recognised David’s voice and asked, “Is that your voice, David my son?” 📖

David answered, “It is, my lord and king.”

## 1 Samuel 26:18

David continued, “Why chase your servant? 📖

What did I do?

What evil is in my hand?

## 1 Samuel 26:19

Please hear me. 📖

If the LORD stirred you against me, may he accept an offering.

If people did, may they be cursed before the LORD!

They drove me from sharing the LORD’s inheritance, saying, ‘Serve other gods.’

**Help with cursed:** Cursed means placed under judgment.

**Help with inheritance:** Here, this refers to a share among God’s people.

## 1 Samuel 26:20

Do not let my blood fall far from the LORD’s presence. 📖

Israel’s king hunts a flea, like someone hunting a partridge in the mountains.”

**Help with partridge:** A partridge is a bird.

David pictures himself as an insignificant target.

## 1 Samuel 26:21

Saul answered, “I sinned. 📖

Return, David my son.

I will never harm you again, because you valued my life today.

I acted foolishly and made a terrible mistake!”

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong.

## 1 Samuel 26:22

David answered, “Here is the king’s spear. 📖

Send a young man to get it.

## 1 Samuel 26:23

May the LORD repay each person’s righteousness and faithfulness. 📖

He put you in my power today, but I would not strike his anointed.

**Help with righteousness:** Righteousness means right conduct before God.

**Help with anointed:** Here, this means the king, chosen through anointing with oil.

## 1 Samuel 26:24

As I valued your life, may the LORD value mine and rescue me from every trouble.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:25

Saul said, “May you be blessed, David my son. 📖

You will do great things and certainly succeed.”

David continued on his way, and Saul went home.
