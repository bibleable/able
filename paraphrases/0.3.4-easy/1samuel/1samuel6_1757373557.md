# 1 Samuel 6 — The Philistines return the ark

**About this chapter**

The Philistines send the ark back with gold objects.

People at Beth-shemesh receive it, but some die after looking inside.

## 1 Samuel 6:1

The LORD’s ark had been in Philistine land for 7 months. 📖

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 6:2

Tell us how to send it back.”

**Help with diviners:** Diviners claim to discover hidden knowledge or what a god wants.

**Help with hand:** Here, hand pictures God’s power acting against them.
