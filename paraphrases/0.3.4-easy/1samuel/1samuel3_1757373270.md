# 1 Samuel 3 — The LORD calls Samuel

**About this chapter**

Samuel hears the LORD calling.

The LORD gives him a message about Eli’s family.

## 1 Samuel 3:1

The boy Samuel served the LORD under Eli. 📖

The LORD’s word was rare in those days, and visions were few.

**Help with visions:** Visions are things God shows a person.

## 1 Samuel 3:2

Eli lay in his room. 📖

His sight had grown so dim that he could not see.

## 1 Samuel 3:3

God’s lamp had not yet gone out. 📖

Samuel lay in the LORD’s temple, where God’s ark was.

**Help with temple:** A temple is a place of worship.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 3:4

The LORD called Samuel. 📖

Samuel answered, “Here I am.”

## 1 Samuel 3:5

Samuel ran to Eli and said, “Here I am. 📖

You called me.”

Eli answered, “I did not call.

Go and lie down.”

Samuel went and lay down.

## 1 Samuel 3:6

The LORD called again, “Samuel!” 📖

Samuel got up, went to Eli, and said, “Here I am.

You called me.”

Eli answered, “My son, I did not call.

Go and lie down.”

## 1 Samuel 3:7

Samuel did not yet know the LORD. 📖

The LORD’s word had not yet been revealed to him.

## 1 Samuel 3:8

The LORD called Samuel a 3rd time. 📖

Samuel got up, went to Eli, and said, “Here I am.

You called me.”

Eli realised the LORD was calling the boy.

## 1 Samuel 3:9

Eli told Samuel, “Go and lie down. 📖

If he calls, say, ‘Speak, LORD, your servant is listening.’”

Samuel went and lay in his place.

## 1 Samuel 3:10

The LORD came and stood there, calling as before, “Samuel! 📖

Samuel!”

Samuel answered, “Speak, your servant is listening.”

## 1 Samuel 3:11

The LORD told Samuel, “I am about to do something in Israel that will make every hearer’s ears tingle. 📖

**Help with ears tingle:** This pictures the shock caused by hearing the news.

## 1 Samuel 3:12

On that day, I will carry out everything I said against Eli’s family, from beginning to end. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:13

I told Eli I would judge his family forever for the wrongdoing he knows about. 📖

His sons blasphemed God, and he did not stop them.

**Help with blasphemed:** Blasphemed means spoke or acted with serious disrespect towards God.

## 1 Samuel 3:14

I swore that no sacrifice or offering would ever atone for Eli’s family’s wrongdoing.” 📖

**Help with atone:** Atone means deal with wrongdoing and make things right before God.

Sacrifices and offerings are gifts given in worship.

## 1 Samuel 3:15

Samuel lay until morning, then opened the LORD’s house’s doors. 📖

He feared telling Eli the vision.

**Help with the LORD’s house:** This means the place of worship.

**Help with vision:** A vision is something God shows a person.

## 1 Samuel 3:16

Eli called, “Samuel, my son.” 📖

Samuel answered, “Here I am.”

## 1 Samuel 3:17

Eli asked, “What did he tell you? 📖

Do not hide it.

May God punish you very severely if you hide anything he said!”

## 1 Samuel 3:18

Samuel told Eli everything and hid nothing. 📖

Eli answered, “He is the LORD.

Let him do what seems good to him.”

## 1 Samuel 3:19

Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him. 📖

The LORD let none of Samuel’s words fall to the ground.

**Help with fall to the ground:** This means his words did not fail.

## 1 Samuel 3:20

All Israel from Dan to Beersheba knew Samuel was confirmed as the LORD’s prophet. 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 3:21

The LORD kept appearing at Shiloh. 📖

There, the LORD revealed himself to Samuel through his word.
