# 1 Samuel 25 — Abigail prevents bloodshed

**About this chapter**

Nabal refuses David’s request.

Abigail brings gifts and persuades David not to take revenge.

## 1 Samuel 25:1

Samuel died, and all Israel gathered to grieve. 📖

They buried him at his Ramah home.

David went to Paran’s wilderness.

## 1 Samuel 25:2

A man lived in Maon and had his business at Carmel. 📖

He was very wealthy, with 1,000 goats and 3,000 sheep being shorn at Carmel.

**Help with shorn:** Shorn means having wool cut off.

## 1 Samuel 25:3

The man was Nabal, a Calebite, with a wife named Abigail. 📖

Abigail was intelligent and beautiful, but Nabal was harsh and evil in his dealings.

## 1 Samuel 25:4

David heard in the wilderness that Nabal was shearing sheep. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:5

David sent 10 young men to Nabal at Carmel. 📖

He said, “Greet him in my name.

## 1 Samuel 25:6

Say, ‘Long life and peace to you, your household, and everything you own. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:7

I hear it is shearing time. 📖

We did not harass your shepherds, and nothing went missing while they were with us at Carmel.

## 1 Samuel 25:8

Ask your young men. 📖

Show mine favour, because we came on a feast day.

Give whatever you can spare to your servants and your son David.’”

## 1 Samuel 25:9

The men told Nabal all David’s words and waited. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:10

Nabal asked, “Who is David, Jesse’s son? 📖

Many servants break from their masters these days.

## 1 Samuel 25:11

Why give my bread, water, and meat for my shearers to men whose origins I do not know?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:12

David’s men returned and reported everything. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:13

David ordered, “Fasten your swords!” 📖

They all did.

About 400 followed David, while 200 guarded the supplies.

## 1 Samuel 25:14

A young man told Abigail, “David sent greetings from the wilderness, but our master screamed at his messengers. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:15

Those men treated us very well. 📖

They never harassed us, and nothing went missing while we lived among them.

## 1 Samuel 25:16

They were a wall around us day and night while we kept sheep near them. 📖

**Help with wall:** This pictures protection.

## 1 Samuel 25:17

Think what to do. 📖

Disaster threatens our master and household.

He is such a scoundrel no one can speak to him!”

**Help with scoundrel:** A scoundrel is someone who behaves very badly.

## 1 Samuel 25:18

Abigail hurried to load donkeys with 200 loaves, 2 skins of wine, and 5 butchered sheep. 📖

She added 5 seahs of roasted grain, 100 raisin clusters, and 200 fig cakes.

**Help with skins:** These were containers made from animal skin.

**Help with seahs:** A seah was a measure for dry goods.

## 1 Samuel 25:19

She told her young men, “Go ahead. 📖

I will follow.”

She did not tell Nabal.

## 1 Samuel 25:20

Abigail rode her donkey into a mountain ravine and met David and his men coming towards her. 📖

**Help with ravine:** A ravine is a narrow, deep valley.

## 1 Samuel 25:21

David had said, “I protected everything this man had in the wilderness for nothing. 📖

Nothing went missing, yet he repaid good with evil.

## 1 Samuel 25:22

May God punish David very severely if any male belonging to Nabal survives until morning.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:23

Abigail saw David, quickly dismounted, and bowed face down. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:24

She fell at his feet. 📖

She said, “My lord, let all blame be mine.

Please hear your servant.

## 1 Samuel 25:25

Ignore this scoundrel Nabal. 📖

He lives up to his name, Fool, with his foolishness.

I did not see the young men you sent.

**Help with scoundrel:** A scoundrel is someone who behaves very badly.

## 1 Samuel 25:26

As surely as the LORD and you live, the LORD has stopped your bloodshed and personal revenge. 📖

May your enemies and those seeking your harm be like Nabal.

## 1 Samuel 25:27

Give this gift I brought to your young men. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:28

Forgive your servant’s offence. 📖

The LORD will give you a lasting dynasty because you fight his battles.

May no evil be found in you throughout your life.

**Help with dynasty:** A dynasty is a continuing line of rulers from one family.

## 1 Samuel 25:29

If someone hunts you, the LORD your God will keep your life secure in the bundle of the living. 📖

He will fling your enemies’ lives away like stones from a sling.

**Help with bundle:** This pictures the LORD keeping life safe.

**Help with sling:** A sling throws stones from a pouch swung on cords.

## 1 Samuel 25:30

The LORD will do all the good he promised and appoint you ruler of Israel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:31

Then you will have no guilt or remorse over needless bloodshed and revenge. 📖

When the LORD treats you well, remember your servant.”

## 1 Samuel 25:32

David told Abigail, “Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God, who sent you today! 📖

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed expresses praise.

## 1 Samuel 25:33

Blessed are your discernment and you, because you stopped my bloodshed and personal revenge. 📖

**Help with discernment:** Discernment means understanding and judging wisely.

## 1 Samuel 25:34

The LORD, Israel’s God, restrained me from harming you. 📖

As surely as he lives, without your quick arrival, no male belonging to Nabal would survive until morning.”

## 1 Samuel 25:35

David accepted the gifts. 📖

He said, “Go home in peace.

I listened and granted your request.”

## 1 Samuel 25:36

Abigail returned to Nabal, who held a feast fit for a king. 📖

He was cheerful and very drunk, so she said nothing until morning.

## 1 Samuel 25:37

In the morning, when Nabal was sober, she told him. 📖

His heart failed, and he became like stone.

**About this description:** The source’s image is retained without assigning a medical diagnosis.

## 1 Samuel 25:38

About 10 days later, the LORD struck Nabal dead. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:39

David heard and said, “Blessed be the LORD, who defended my case against Nabal’s insult and restrained me from evil. 📖

The LORD brought Nabal’s wickedness on his own head.”

David asked Abigail to become his wife.

**Help with on his own head:** This pictures consequences falling on the wrongdoer.

## 1 Samuel 25:40

David’s servants came to Abigail at Carmel. 📖

They said, “David sent us to take you as his wife.”

## 1 Samuel 25:41

She rose and bowed face down. 📖

She said, “Your servant is ready to serve and wash my lord’s servants’ feet.”

## 1 Samuel 25:42

Abigail hurried onto a donkey and followed David’s messengers with 5 servant women. 📖

She became David’s wife.

## 1 Samuel 25:43

David had also married Ahinoam of Jezreel. 📖

Both women were his wives.

## 1 Samuel 25:44

Saul had given Michal, his daughter and David’s wife, to Palti son of Laish from Gallim. 📖
