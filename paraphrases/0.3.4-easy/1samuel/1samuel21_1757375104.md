# 1 Samuel 21 — David at Nob and Gath

**About this chapter**

David receives bread and a sword at Nob.

He flees to Gath and pretends to be mentally unwell.

## 1 Samuel 21:1

David came to Ahimelech the priest at Nob. 📖

Ahimelech trembled and asked, “Why are you alone?

Why is no one with you?”

## 1 Samuel 21:2

David answered, “The king sent me on a secret mission. 📖

No one must know about it.

I arranged to meet my young men at a certain place.”

**About the mission:** This is David’s account to Ahimelech.

## 1 Samuel 21:3

David asked, “What food do you have? 📖

Give me 5 loaves or whatever you can find.”

## 1 Samuel 21:4

The priest answered, “There is no ordinary bread, only holy bread, provided your young men have kept from women.” 📖

**Help with holy:** Holy means set apart for God.

**Help with kept from women:** Here, this means not had sex.

## 1 Samuel 21:5

David said, “We have kept from women as usual when I set out. 📖

The young men’s bodies are holy even on ordinary missions, much more today!”

**About holy bodies:** These words concern religious preparation, not a judgment of women as morally unclean.

## 1 Samuel 21:6

The priest gave David holy bread, the only bread there. 📖

This was the Bread of the Presence, removed from before the LORD and replaced that day with hot bread.

**Help with Bread of the Presence:** This bread was placed before the LORD in worship.

## 1 Samuel 21:7

Doeg the Edomite, Saul’s chief shepherd, was there that day, detained before the LORD. 📖

**About detained:** The source does not explain why he was required to stay.

## 1 Samuel 21:8

David asked Ahimelech, “Do you have a sword or spear? 📖

The king’s mission was urgent, so I brought no weapons.”

## 1 Samuel 21:9

The priest answered, “Goliath the Philistine’s sword is wrapped behind the ephod. 📖

You killed him in Elah’s Valley.

Take it if you want.

It is the only sword here.”

David said, “None is like it.

Give it to me.”

**Help with ephod:** An ephod is a special garment.

## 1 Samuel 21:10

David fled Saul that day and went to Achish king of Gath. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:11

Achish’s servants asked, “Is this not David, the land’s king? 📖

They danced and sang, ‘Saul killed his thousands, and David his tens of thousands.’”

## 1 Samuel 21:12

David took this seriously and greatly feared Achish king of Gath. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:13

David changed his behaviour and pretended madness while in their hands. 📖

He scratched the gate’s doors and let saliva run down his beard.

**About madness:** The source describes behaviour David deliberately pretends.

It does not provide a medical diagnosis.

## 1 Samuel 21:14

Achish told his servants, “The man is insane! 📖

Why bring him to me?

**About insane:** This is Achish’s description of what he sees.

## 1 Samuel 21:15

Do I lack madmen, that you bring him to rave before me? 📖

Must he enter my house?”

**About madmen:** Achish uses this dismissive word.

His speech is retained as his attitude, not as guidance about mental illness.
