# 1 Samuel 27 — David lives in Philistine land

**About this chapter**

David moves to Achish’s territory and receives Ziklag.

He raids other peoples but gives Achish a different account.

## 1 Samuel 27:1

David thought, “One day Saul will sweep me away. 📖

My best choice is escape to Philistine land.

Saul will stop searching Israel, and I will escape his power.”

## 1 Samuel 27:2

David and his 600 men went to Achish son of Maoch, king of Gath. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:3

David and his men settled with Achish in Gath, each with his family. 📖

David had 2 wives: Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail of Carmel, Nabal’s widow.

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Samuel 27:4

Saul heard David had fled to Gath and stopped searching. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:5

David told Achish, “If you favour me, give me a place in an outlying town. 📖

Why should your servant live with you in the royal city?”

## 1 Samuel 27:6

Achish gave David Ziklag that day. 📖

It still belongs to Judah’s kings.

## 1 Samuel 27:7

David lived in Philistine territory for 1 year and 4 months. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:8

David and his men raided the Geshurites, Girzites, and Amalekites. 📖

These peoples had long lived in the land extending to Shur and Egypt.

## 1 Samuel 27:9

When David attacked, he left no man or woman alive. 📖

He took flocks, herds, donkeys, camels, and clothing, then returned to Achish.

## 1 Samuel 27:10

Achish asked, “Where did you raid today?” 📖

David answered, “Judah’s Negev,” “Jerahmeel’s Negev,” or “the Kenites’ Negev.”

**About the answers:** David’s reports differ from the narrator’s account of his targets.

## 1 Samuel 27:11

David left no man or woman alive to be brought to Gath. 📖

He said, “Otherwise, they will report what David did.”

This was his practice throughout his time in Philistine territory.

## 1 Samuel 27:12

Achish trusted David. 📖

He thought, “David has become a stench to his people Israel.

He will serve me forever.”

**Help with stench:** This pictures becoming deeply offensive to a people.
