# 1 Samuel 5 — The ark among the Philistines

**About this chapter**

The Philistines place God’s ark beside Dagon’s statue.

The cities receiving the ark suffer, and their rulers discuss sending it away.

## 1 Samuel 5:1

The Philistines captured God’s ark and took it from Ebenezer to Ashdod. 📖

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 5:2

They carried it into Dagon’s temple and placed it beside his statue. 📖

**Help with Dagon:** Dagon was a god worshipped by the Philistines.

**Help with temple:** A temple is a place of worship.

## 1 Samuel 5:3

Early the next morning, Ashdod’s people found Dagon fallen face down before the LORD’s ark. 📖

They put Dagon back in his place.

**Help with Dagon:** Here, Dagon refers to his statue.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 5:4

Early the next morning, Dagon lay face down before the LORD’s ark again. 📖

His head and hands lay broken off on the threshold.

Only his body remained.

**Help with threshold:** A threshold is the base of a doorway.

## 1 Samuel 5:5

That is why Dagon’s priests and everyone entering his temple in Ashdod still avoid stepping on the threshold. 📖

**Help with threshold:** A threshold is the base of a doorway.

## 1 Samuel 5:6

The LORD’s hand was heavy on Ashdod’s people and those nearby. 📖

He devastated them and gave them tumours.

**Help with hand:** This pictures the LORD’s power acting against them.

**Help with tumours:** Tumours are swellings or lumps in the body.

## 1 Samuel 5:7

Ashdod’s men saw what happened. 📖

They said, “The ark of Israel’s God must not stay with us.

His hand is heavy on us and our god Dagon.”

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

**Help with hand:** This pictures God’s power acting against them.

## 1 Samuel 5:8

They gathered all the Philistine rulers and asked, “What should we do with the ark of Israel’s God?” 📖

The rulers answered, “Move it to Gath.”

They carried it away.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 5:9

After the ark reached Gath, the LORD’s hand was against that city too. 📖

Great confusion spread, and young and old men suffered an outbreak of tumours.

**Help with hand:** This pictures God’s power acting against them.

**Help with tumours:** Tumours are swellings or lumps in the body.

## 1 Samuel 5:10

They sent God’s ark to Ekron. 📖

As it arrived, Ekron’s people cried, “They brought Israel’s God’s ark to kill us and our people!”

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 5:11

Ekron’s people gathered all the Philistine rulers. 📖

They said, “Send Israel’s God’s ark back to its place, so it does not kill us and our people!”

Deadly confusion filled the city, and God’s hand was very heavy on it.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

**Help with hand:** This pictures God’s power acting against the city.

## 1 Samuel 5:12

Those who survived suffered from tumours. 📖
