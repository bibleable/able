# 1 Samuel 15 — The LORD rejects Saul as king

**About this chapter**

Saul attacks Amalek but does not carry out all the command.

Samuel confronts Saul and kills Agag.

## 1 Samuel 15:1

Samuel told Saul, “The LORD sent me to anoint you king over his people Israel. 📖

Listen to the LORD’s words.

**Help with anoint:** Anoint means mark someone for a role by putting oil on them.

## 1 Samuel 15:2

The LORD of Hosts says, ‘I saw what Amalek did against Israel on the journey from Egypt. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 1 Samuel 15:3

Attack Amalek and devote everything they own to destruction. 📖

Spare no one.

Kill men, women, children, babies, oxen, sheep, camels, and donkeys.’”

**Help with devote to destruction:** This means set apart for complete destruction under the command.

**About this command:** The source attributes this command, including the deaths of children, to the LORD.

It is retained without turning it into an instruction for readers today.

## 1 Samuel 15:4

Saul gathered and counted 200,000 foot soldiers and 10,000 men of Judah at Telaim. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:5

Saul reached Amalek’s city and waited secretly in the valley. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:6

Saul warned the Kenites, “You showed kindness to all Israel when they left Egypt. 📖

Leave the Amalekites, or I will sweep you away with them.”

The Kenites moved away.

## 1 Samuel 15:7

Saul struck Amalek from Havilah to Shur, east of Egypt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:8

Saul captured Amalek’s king Agag alive, but destroyed all the others with the sword. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:9

Saul and the troops spared Agag, the best sheep and cattle, fat calves, lambs, and everything valuable. 📖

They refused to destroy these, but destroyed everything despised and worthless.

## 1 Samuel 15:10

The LORD’s word came to Samuel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:11

The LORD said, “I regret making Saul king. 📖

He turned from following me and did not carry out my commands.”

Samuel was distressed and cried to the LORD all night.

## 1 Samuel 15:12

Samuel got up early to confront Saul. 📖

He heard, “Saul went to Carmel, set up a monument for himself, and went down to Gilgal.”

## 1 Samuel 15:13

Samuel arrived, and Saul said, “May the LORD bless you. 📖

I carried out the LORD’s instructions.”

## 1 Samuel 15:14

Samuel answered, “Then why do I hear sheep bleating and cattle lowing?” 📖

**Help with bleating and lowing:** These are the sounds sheep and cattle make.

## 1 Samuel 15:15

Saul answered, “The troops brought them from Amalek. 📖

They spared the best sheep and cattle to sacrifice to the LORD your God.

We destroyed the rest.”

**Help with sacrifice:** Sacrifice means give an offering to God.

## 1 Samuel 15:16

Samuel exclaimed, “Stop! 📖

Let me tell you what the LORD said last night.”

Saul answered, “Tell me.”

## 1 Samuel 15:17

Samuel said, “You once seemed small to yourself, but have you not become head of Israel’s tribes? 📖

The LORD anointed you king.

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

**Help with anointed:** Anointed means marked for a role by the use of oil.

## 1 Samuel 15:18

He sent you to destroy the sinful Amalekites and fight until they were wiped out. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:19

Why did you disobey the LORD? 📖

Why rush to the captured goods and do evil in the LORD’s sight?”

## 1 Samuel 15:20

Saul answered, “I obeyed the LORD and went on his mission. 📖

I brought Agag king of Amalek and destroyed the Amalekites.

## 1 Samuel 15:21

The troops took the best sheep and cattle marked for destruction to sacrifice to the LORD your God at Gilgal.” 📖

**Help with sacrifice:** Sacrifice means give an offering to God.

## 1 Samuel 15:22

Samuel declared, “Does the LORD delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices as much as obedience? 📖

Obedience is better than sacrifice.

Listening is better than rams’ fat.

**Help with sacrifices:** These are offerings to God.

**Help with rams:** Rams are male sheep.

## 1 Samuel 15:23

Rebellion is like divination’s sin, and arrogance is like idolatry’s wickedness. 📖

You rejected the LORD’s word, so he rejected you as king.”

**Help with divination:** Divination seeks hidden knowledge through religious practices.

**Help with idolatry:** Idolatry means worshipping objects or gods instead of the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 15:24

Saul told Samuel, “I sinned and broke the LORD’s command and your instructions. 📖

I feared the people and obeyed them.

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong against God.

## 1 Samuel 15:25

Please forgive my sin and return with me to worship the LORD.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:26

Samuel answered, “I will not return. 📖

You rejected the LORD’s word, and he rejected you as Israel’s king.”

## 1 Samuel 15:27

Samuel turned away, and Saul grabbed his robe’s hem. 📖

It tore.

## 1 Samuel 15:28

Samuel said, “Today the LORD has torn Israel’s kingdom from you and given it to your neighbour, better than you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:29

Israel’s Glory does not lie or change his mind. 📖

He is not a human who changes his mind.”

**Help with Israel’s Glory:** This is a title for God.

**About change and regret:** The chapter retains both this statement and the LORD’s regret in other verses.

Their relationship needs further editorial interpretation.

## 1 Samuel 15:30

Saul answered, “I sinned. 📖

Please honour me before my people’s elders and Israel.

Return with me so I can worship the LORD your God.”

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 1 Samuel 15:31

Samuel went back with Saul, and Saul worshipped the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:32

Samuel ordered, “Bring Agag king of the Amalekites.” 📖

Agag came cheerfully, thinking, “Surely death’s bitterness is past.”

## 1 Samuel 15:33

Samuel said, “Your sword made women childless, so your mother will be childless among women.” 📖

Samuel hacked Agag into pieces before the LORD at Gilgal.

## 1 Samuel 15:34

Samuel went to Ramah, and Saul went home to Gibeah of Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:35

Samuel never visited Saul again before Samuel died. 📖

Samuel grieved for Saul, and the LORD regretted making Saul king over Israel.
