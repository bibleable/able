# 1 Samuel 22 — Saul orders the priests killed

**About this chapter**

David gathers followers and protects his parents.

Saul accuses Ahimelech and orders the priests at Nob killed.

## 1 Samuel 22:1

David left Gath and found shelter in Adullam’s cave. 📖

His brothers and father’s whole household heard and came.

## 1 Samuel 22:2

People in distress, debt, or discontent gathered around David. 📖

He became their leader, with about 400 men.

## 1 Samuel 22:3

David went to Mizpeh of Moab. 📖

He asked Moab’s king, “Let my parents stay until I learn what God will do for me.”

## 1 Samuel 22:4

His parents stayed with Moab’s king throughout David’s time in the stronghold. 📖

**Help with stronghold:** A stronghold is a place protected from attack.

## 1 Samuel 22:5

Gad the prophet told David, “Leave the stronghold and go to Judah.” 📖

David went to Hereth’s forest.

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 22:6

Saul heard David and his men had been found. 📖

Saul sat under Gibeah’s hilltop tamarisk tree, spear in hand, surrounded by servants.

**Help with tamarisk:** Tamarisk is a kind of tree.

## 1 Samuel 22:7

Saul said, “Listen, Benjamites! 📖

Will Jesse’s son give you all fields and vineyards and make you commanders of 1,000 or 100?

**Help with vineyards:** Vineyards are places where grapes grow.

## 1 Samuel 22:8

Is that why you all plot against me? 📖

No one told me my son made a covenant with Jesse’s son.

No one cares or tells me my son stirred my servant to wait in ambush against me!”

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

**About the plot:** These are Saul’s accusations.

## 1 Samuel 22:9

Doeg the Edomite, standing with Saul’s servants, answered, “I saw Jesse’s son at Nob with Ahimelech son of Ahitub. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:10

Ahimelech asked the LORD for him, gave him food, and gave him Goliath the Philistine’s sword.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:11

The king summoned Ahimelech son of Ahitub and his father’s whole family, the priests at Nob. 📖

All came.

## 1 Samuel 22:12

Saul said, “Listen, son of Ahitub!” 📖

Ahimelech answered, “Here I am, my lord.”

## 1 Samuel 22:13

Saul asked, “Why did you and Jesse’s son plot against me? 📖

You gave him bread and a sword and asked God for him.

You helped him rise and wait in ambush against me!”

## 1 Samuel 22:14

Ahimelech answered, “Who is as faithful as David, your son-in-law, bodyguard commander, and honoured member of your household? 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:15

Was that my first time asking God for him? 📖

Never!

Do not accuse me or my father’s family.

I knew nothing of this affair, in whole or part.”

## 1 Samuel 22:16

The king said, “You and your father’s whole family will certainly die, Ahimelech!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:17

The king ordered his guards, “Kill the LORD’s priests! 📖

They sided with David, knowing he fled and not telling me.”

The guards refused to strike the LORD’s priests.

## 1 Samuel 22:18

The king told Doeg, “You kill the priests!” 📖

Doeg the Edomite killed 85 men wearing linen ephods that day.

**Help with linen ephods:** These were special garments made from linen cloth.

## 1 Samuel 22:19

He also killed Nob’s people and animals with the sword. 📖

He killed men, women, children, babies, oxen, donkeys, and sheep in the priests’ city.

## 1 Samuel 22:20

Abiathar, a son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub, escaped to David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:21

Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the LORD’s priests. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:22

David told Abiathar, “I knew Doeg the Edomite was there that day and would tell Saul. 📖

I am responsible for everyone in your father’s family’s lives.

## 1 Samuel 22:23

Stay with me without fear. 📖

The one seeking your life seeks mine too.

You will be safe with me.”
