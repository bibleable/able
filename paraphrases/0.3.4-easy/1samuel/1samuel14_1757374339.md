# 1 Samuel 14 — Jonathan attacks and Saul makes an oath

**About this chapter**

Jonathan and his armour-bearer attack the Philistines.

Saul’s food oath brings trouble, and the people protect Jonathan.

## 1 Samuel 14:1

One day, Jonathan son of Saul told his young armour-bearer, “Come across to the Philistine outpost.” 📖

Jonathan did not tell his father.

**Help with armour-bearer:** This is someone who carries a soldier’s protective equipment and weapons.

## 1 Samuel 14:2

Saul stayed under Migron’s pomegranate tree, near Gibeah’s edge, with about 600 men. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:3

Ahijah was there, wearing an ephod. 📖

Ahijah’s father was Ahitub, Ichabod’s brother.

Ahitub was Phinehas’s son, and Phinehas was Eli’s son.

Eli was the LORD’s priest at Shiloh.

The troops did not know Jonathan had left.

**Help with ephod:** An ephod is a special garment.

## 1 Samuel 14:4

Cliffs stood on both sides of Jonathan’s intended crossing to the Philistine outpost. 📖

One was Bozez, the other Seneh.

## 1 Samuel 14:5

One cliff faced Michmash to the north, the other Geba to the south. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:6

Jonathan told his armour-bearer, “Let us cross to these uncircumcised men’s outpost. 📖

Perhaps the LORD will act for us.

Nothing stops the LORD saving by many or few.”

**Help with uncircumcised:** Circumcision removes the skin covering the tip of the penis.

Here, Jonathan uses this word for the Philistines.

## 1 Samuel 14:7

The armour-bearer answered, “Do everything in your heart. 📖

Go ahead.

I am with you heart and soul.”

**Help with heart and soul:** This means wholehearted support.

## 1 Samuel 14:8

Jonathan said, “We will cross and show ourselves. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:9

If they say, ‘Wait until we come,’ we will stay and not go up. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:10

If they say, ‘Come up,’ we will go. 📖

This will be our sign that the LORD has put them in our power.”

## 1 Samuel 14:11

They showed themselves. 📖

The Philistines exclaimed, “Look!

The Hebrews are coming out of their hiding holes!”

**Help with Hebrews:** Here, Hebrews means Israelites.

## 1 Samuel 14:12

The outpost’s men called, “Come up, and we will teach you a lesson!” 📖

Jonathan told his armour-bearer, “Follow me.

The LORD has put them in Israel’s power.”

## 1 Samuel 14:13

Jonathan climbed on hands and feet, with his armour-bearer behind. 📖

The Philistines fell before Jonathan, and his armour-bearer followed and finished them off.

## 1 Samuel 14:14

In the first attack, Jonathan and his armour-bearer killed about 20 men in about half an acre. 📖

**Help with acre:** An acre measures an area of land.

## 1 Samuel 14:15

Panic struck the Philistine camp, fields, and everyone. 📖

Outpost troops and raiders trembled too.

The earth shook, and panic spread from God.

## 1 Samuel 14:16

Saul’s watchmen at Gibeah in Benjamin saw the troops breaking up and scattering everywhere. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:17

Saul ordered a roll call to see who had left. 📖

They found Jonathan and his armour-bearer missing.

## 1 Samuel 14:18

Saul told Ahijah, “Bring God’s ark.” 📖

It was with Israel then.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Samuel 14:19

While Saul spoke to the priest, the uproar in the Philistine camp grew. 📖

Saul told the priest, “Withdraw your hand.”

**About the instruction:** The source gives this short instruction without explaining its precise action.

## 1 Samuel 14:20

Saul and all his troops gathered and marched into battle. 📖

They found the Philistines in complete confusion, each fighting his neighbour with a sword.

## 1 Samuel 14:21

Hebrews who had joined the Philistines in nearby camps changed sides. 📖

They joined Israel with Saul and Jonathan.

**Help with Hebrews:** Here, Hebrews means Israelites.

## 1 Samuel 14:22

All the Israelites hiding in Ephraim’s hill country heard the Philistines were fleeing. 📖

They joined the battle and chased closely.

## 1 Samuel 14:23

The LORD saved Israel that day. 📖

The battle moved beyond Beth-aven.

## 1 Samuel 14:24

Israel’s men suffered because Saul had placed them under an oath. 📖

Saul said, “Cursed is anyone eating before evening, before I take revenge on my enemies.”

No troops tasted food.

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise or binding declaration.

**Help with cursed:** Cursed means placed under judgment.

## 1 Samuel 14:25

All the troops entered the forest, where honey lay on the ground. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:26

They saw flowing honey, but no one ate it because they feared the oath. 📖

**Help with oath:** This refers to Saul’s binding declaration.

## 1 Samuel 14:27

Jonathan had not heard his father’s oath. 📖

He dipped his staff’s end in the honeycomb and brought his hand to his mouth.

His eyes brightened.

**Help with eyes brightened:** This pictures renewed energy.

## 1 Samuel 14:28

A soldier told him, “Your father declared under oath, ‘Cursed is anyone eating today.’ 📖

That is why the people are faint.”

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise or binding declaration.

## 1 Samuel 14:29

Jonathan answered, “My father has troubled the land. 📖

See how my eyes brightened after a little honey.

## 1 Samuel 14:30

How much better if the troops ate freely from today’s captured goods! 📖

Would they not have killed far more Philistines?”

## 1 Samuel 14:31

Israel struck the Philistines from Michmash to Aijalon. 📖

That day, the people became very faint.

## 1 Samuel 14:32

They rushed greedily to the captured sheep, cattle, and calves. 📖

They slaughtered them on the ground and ate meat with blood still in it.

## 1 Samuel 14:33

Saul heard, “The troops sin against the LORD by eating meat with blood in it.” 📖

Saul said, “You have broken faith.

Roll a large stone here now.”

**Help with sin:** Sin means do wrong against God.

## 1 Samuel 14:34

Saul ordered, “Tell everyone to bring an ox or sheep here, slaughter it, and eat. 📖

Do not sin against the LORD by eating meat with blood in it.”

Everyone brought his ox and slaughtered it there that night.

## 1 Samuel 14:35

Saul built an altar to the LORD for the first time. 📖

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

## 1 Samuel 14:36

Saul said, “Chase the Philistines tonight, take their goods until dawn, and leave no man alive!” 📖

The troops answered, “Do what seems good.”

The priest said, “We must ask God here.”

## 1 Samuel 14:37

Saul asked God, “Should I chase the Philistines? 📖

Will you put them in Israel’s power?”

God did not answer that day.

## 1 Samuel 14:38

Saul told the troop leaders, “Come, let us find how this sin happened today. 📖

**Help with sin:** Sin means wrongdoing against God.

## 1 Samuel 14:39

As surely as the LORD who saves Israel lives, the guilty person must die, even if it is Jonathan!” 📖

No soldier spoke.

## 1 Samuel 14:40

Saul told Israel, “Stand on one side. 📖

Jonathan and I will stand on the other.”

The troops answered, “Do what seems good.”

## 1 Samuel 14:41

Saul asked the LORD, Israel’s God, “Why have you not answered today? 📖

If Jonathan or I am at fault, answer with Urim.

If Israel’s men are at fault, answer with Thummim.”

Saul and Jonathan were selected, and the people were cleared.

**Help with Urim and Thummim:** These were used in seeking a decision from God.

## 1 Samuel 14:42

Saul said, “Cast the lot between Jonathan and me.” 📖

Jonathan was selected.

**Help with cast the lot:** This means use marked objects to seek a decision.

## 1 Samuel 14:43

Saul demanded, “What did you do?” 📖

Jonathan answered, “I tasted a little honey from my staff’s end.

Must I die now?”

## 1 Samuel 14:44

Saul declared, “May God punish me very severely unless you certainly die, Jonathan!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:45

The people said, “Must Jonathan die after bringing Israel this great rescue? 📖

Never!

As surely as the LORD lives, not one hair of his head will fall.

He did this today with God’s help.”

They rescued Jonathan, and he did not die.

## 1 Samuel 14:46

Saul stopped chasing, and the Philistines returned to their land. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:47

After becoming Israel’s king, Saul fought all his surrounding enemies. 📖

These were Moab, Ammon, Edom, Zobah’s kings, and the Philistines.

Wherever he turned, he drove them away.

## 1 Samuel 14:48

He fought bravely, defeated the Amalekites, and rescued Israel from those taking its goods. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:49

Saul’s sons were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchishua. 📖

His 2 daughters were Merab, the older, and Michal, the younger.

## 1 Samuel 14:50

Saul’s wife was Ahinoam daughter of Ahimaaz. 📖

His army commander was Abner, son of Saul’s uncle Ner.

## 1 Samuel 14:51

Saul’s father Kish and Abner’s father Ner were Abiel’s sons. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:52

Saul fought fierce wars with the Philistines throughout his life. 📖

Whenever he saw a strong or brave man, he enlisted him.
