# 1 Samuel 31 — Saul and his sons die

**About this chapter**

The Philistines defeat Israel at Gilboa.

Saul and his sons die, and Jabesh-gilead’s men recover their bodies.

## 1 Samuel 31:1

The Philistines fought Israel, and Israel’s men fled. 📖

Many were killed on Mount Gilboa.

## 1 Samuel 31:2

The Philistines chased Saul and his sons closely. 📖

They killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, Saul’s sons.

## 1 Samuel 31:3

The battle pressed hard against Saul. 📖

Archers caught up and wounded him critically.

**Help with archers:** Archers shoot arrows.

## 1 Samuel 31:4

Saul told his armour-bearer, “Draw your sword and run me through. 📖

Otherwise, these uncircumcised men will run me through and torture me!”

The armour-bearer was terrified and refused.

Saul took his own sword and fell on it.

**Help with armour-bearer:** This is someone who carries a soldier’s protective equipment and weapons.

**Help with uncircumcised:** Circumcision removes the skin covering the tip of the penis.

Here, Saul uses this word for the Philistines.

**Help with run me through:** This means kill with a sword.

## 1 Samuel 31:5

The armour-bearer saw Saul dead, fell on his own sword, and died with him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:6

Saul, his 3 sons, his armour-bearer, and all his men died together that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:7

Israelites along the valley and beyond the Jordan saw the army flee and Saul and his sons dead. 📖

They abandoned their cities, which the Philistines occupied.

## 1 Samuel 31:8

The next day, Philistines stripping the dead found Saul and his 3 sons on Mount Gilboa. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:9

They cut off Saul’s head and removed his armour. 📖

Messengers announced the news throughout Philistine land, among the people and in their idols’ temples.

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## 1 Samuel 31:10

They put his armour in the Ashtoreths’ temple and hung his body on Beth-shan’s wall. 📖

**Help with Ashtoreths:** These were goddesses worshipped by peoples around Israel.

## 1 Samuel 31:11

Jabesh-gilead’s people heard what the Philistines did to Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:12

All their brave men travelled all night to recover Saul’s and his sons’ bodies from Beth-shan’s wall. 📖

They brought the bodies to Jabesh and burned them.

## 1 Samuel 31:13

They buried the bones under Jabesh’s tamarisk tree and fasted 7 days. 📖

**Help with tamarisk:** Tamarisk is a kind of tree.

**Help with fasted:** Fasted means did not eat for a time.
