# 1 Samuel 23 — David rescues Keilah and escapes Saul

**About this chapter**

David saves Keilah, then leaves after asking the LORD about Saul.

Jonathan visits, and Saul continues pursuing David.

## 1 Samuel 23:1

David heard, “The Philistines attack Keilah and steal from the threshing floors.” 📖

**Help with threshing floors:** These are places where grain is separated from its dry covering.

## 1 Samuel 23:2

David asked the LORD, “Should I attack them?” 📖

The LORD answered, “Attack and save Keilah.”

## 1 Samuel 23:3

David’s men said, “We fear here in Judah. 📖

How much more if we face Philistine armies at Keilah?”

## 1 Samuel 23:4

David asked again. 📖

The LORD answered, “Go now to Keilah.

I will put the Philistines in your power.”

## 1 Samuel 23:5

David and his men fought at Keilah and took the Philistines’ livestock. 📖

They struck them powerfully and saved Keilah’s people.

## 1 Samuel 23:6

Abiathar son of Ahimelech had brought the ephod when he fled to David at Keilah. 📖

**Help with ephod:** An ephod is a special garment used in worship.

## 1 Samuel 23:7

Saul heard David was at Keilah and said, “God put him in my power. 📖

He trapped himself in a town with gates and bars.”

**About this claim:** Saul interprets the news this way.

## 1 Samuel 23:8

Saul called all his troops to surround David and his men at Keilah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:9

David learned Saul plotted harm and told Abiathar the priest, “Bring the ephod.” 📖

**Help with ephod:** An ephod is a special garment used in worship.

## 1 Samuel 23:10

David prayed, “LORD, Israel’s God, I heard Saul plans to destroy Keilah because of me. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:11

Will Keilah’s people hand me over? 📖

Will Saul come as I heard?

Please tell your servant, LORD, Israel’s God.”

The LORD said, “He will.”

## 1 Samuel 23:12

David asked, “Will Keilah’s people hand me and my men to Saul?” 📖

The LORD answered, “They will.”

## 1 Samuel 23:13

David and about 600 men left Keilah, moving from place to place. 📖

Saul heard they escaped and did not march there.

## 1 Samuel 23:14

David stayed in wilderness strongholds and Ziph’s wilderness hills. 📖

Saul searched daily, but God did not hand David over.

**Help with strongholds:** Strongholds are places protected from attack.

## 1 Samuel 23:15

At Horesh in Ziph’s wilderness, David saw Saul had come to kill him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:16

Saul’s son Jonathan came to Horesh and strengthened David’s trust in God. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:17

Jonathan said, “Do not fear. 📖

Saul my father will not seize you.

You will be Israel’s king, and I will be second to you.

My father knows it.”

## 1 Samuel 23:18

They made a covenant before the LORD. 📖

David stayed at Horesh, and Jonathan went home.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Samuel 23:19

The Ziphites told Saul at Gibeah, “David hides among us at Horesh’s strongholds on Hachilah’s hill, south of Jeshimon. 📖

**Help with strongholds:** Strongholds are places protected from attack.

## 1 Samuel 23:20

Come whenever you wish, king. 📖

We will hand him over.”

## 1 Samuel 23:21

Saul answered, “May the LORD bless you for caring about me. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:22

Prepare further. 📖

Watch where he goes and find who has seen him.

I hear he is very cunning.

## 1 Samuel 23:23

Find every hiding place and return with certain information. 📖

I will go with you.

If he is in the land, I will search all Judah’s family groups.”

## 1 Samuel 23:24

They went ahead to Ziph. 📖

David and his men were in Maon’s wilderness in the Arabah, south of Jeshimon.

## 1 Samuel 23:25

Saul and his men searched. 📖

David heard and went down to the rock in Maon’s wilderness.

Saul heard and followed.

## 1 Samuel 23:26

Saul went along one side of the mountain, David and his men the other. 📖

David hurried away, but Saul’s men closed in to capture them.

## 1 Samuel 23:27

A messenger told Saul, “Hurry! 📖

The Philistines raided the land!”

## 1 Samuel 23:28

Saul stopped pursuing David and went against the Philistines. 📖

That is why the place is called Sela-hammahlekoth.

## 1 Samuel 23:29

David left and lived in En-gedi’s strongholds. 📖

**Help with strongholds:** Strongholds are places protected from attack.
