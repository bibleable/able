# 1 Samuel 18 — Saul becomes jealous of David

**About this chapter**

Jonathan makes a covenant with David.

Saul grows jealous and tries to use the Philistines against David.

## 1 Samuel 18:1

After David spoke to Saul, Jonathan’s and David’s souls were joined closely together. 📖

Jonathan loved David as himself.

**Help with joined souls:** This pictures their close bond.

## 1 Samuel 18:2

From that day, Saul kept David and did not let him return to his father’s house. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:3

Jonathan made a covenant with David because he loved him as himself. 📖

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Samuel 18:4

Jonathan gave David his robe, tunic, sword, bow, and belt. 📖

**Help with tunic:** A tunic is a garment worn on the body.

## 1 Samuel 18:5

David went out and succeeded in everything Saul sent him to do. 📖

Saul put him over the soldiers.

Everyone, including Saul’s officers, was pleased.

## 1 Samuel 18:6

After David killed the Philistine, troops returned home. 📖

Women came from all Israel’s cities to meet King Saul.

They sang and danced joyfully with tambourines and other instruments.

**Help with tambourines:** Tambourines are small hand-held drums.

## 1 Samuel 18:7

As they danced, they sang, “Saul killed his thousands, and David his tens of thousands.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:8

Saul was furious and resented the song. 📖

He said, “They give David tens of thousands but me only thousands.

What remains for him except the kingdom?”

## 1 Samuel 18:9

From then on, Saul watched David jealously. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:10

The next day, a spirit of distress from God came on Saul. 📖

Saul prophesied inside while David played his harp as usual.

Saul held a spear.

**Help with prophesied:** The source uses the word for prophetic speech here.

Its form in this scene needs further editorial discussion.

## 1 Samuel 18:11

Saul threw the spear, thinking, “I will pin David to the wall.” 📖

David escaped him twice.

## 1 Samuel 18:12

Saul feared David because the LORD was with David but had left Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:13

Saul sent David away to command 1,000 men. 📖

David led them to battle and back.

## 1 Samuel 18:14

David succeeded in all his ways because the LORD was with him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:15

Saul saw David’s great success and feared him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:16

All Israel and Judah loved David because he led them to battle and back. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:17

Saul told David, “Marry my older daughter Merab. 📖

Be brave for me and fight the LORD’s battles.”

Saul thought, “Let the Philistines act against him instead of me.”

## 1 Samuel 18:18

David answered, “Who am I, and what is my family or father’s clan in Israel, to marry the king’s daughter?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:19

When Merab was due to marry David, she was given to Adriel of Meholah instead. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:20

Saul’s daughter Michal loved David. 📖

Saul heard and was pleased.

## 1 Samuel 18:21

Saul thought, “I will give her to David as a trap, so the Philistines attack him.” 📖

Saul told David, “You can become my son-in-law a 2nd time.”

**Help with son-in-law:** A son-in-law is a man married to a person’s daughter.

## 1 Samuel 18:22

Saul ordered his servants to speak privately to David. 📖

“Tell him the king is pleased and all his servants love him.

Tell him to become the king’s son-in-law.”

## 1 Samuel 18:23

The servants told David. 📖

He answered, “Is marrying the king’s daughter a small matter?

I am poor and not highly regarded.”

## 1 Samuel 18:24

The servants reported David’s words to Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:25

Saul said, “Tell David the king wants only 100 Philistine foreskins as marriage payment and revenge on enemies.” 📖

Saul intended the Philistines to kill David.

**Help with foreskins:** A foreskin is the skin covering the tip of the penis.

The servants told David these terms. 📖

He was pleased to marry the king’s daughter.

## 1 Samuel 18:27

David and his men killed 200 Philistines. 📖

David brought their foreskins as full payment to become the king’s son-in-law.

Saul gave Michal to David as his wife.

**Help with foreskins:** A foreskin is the skin covering the tip of the penis.

## 1 Samuel 18:28

Saul realised the LORD was with David and Michal loved him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:29

Saul feared David even more and remained his enemy from then on. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:30

Whenever Philistine commanders came to battle, David outperformed all Saul’s officers. 📖

His name became highly honoured.
