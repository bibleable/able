# 1 Samuel 20 — Jonathan and David make a plan

**About this chapter**

Jonathan promises loyalty to David and tests Saul’s intentions.

He uses arrows as a signal, then meets David to say goodbye.

## 1 Samuel 20:1

David fled Naioth in Ramah and asked Jonathan, “What have I done wrong? 📖

How have I sinned against your father, that he wants to kill me?”

**Help with sinned:** Here, sinned means done wrong.

## 1 Samuel 20:2

Jonathan answered, “Never! 📖

You will not die.

My father tells me everything, great or small.

Why hide this?

It cannot be true!”

## 1 Samuel 20:3

David swore again, “Your father knows you favour me. 📖

He says, ‘Jonathan must not know, or he will grieve.’

As surely as the LORD and you live, only a step separates me from death.”

**Help with a step:** This pictures being very close to death.

## 1 Samuel 20:4

Jonathan told David, “I will do whatever you ask.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:5

David said, “Tomorrow is the New Moon, and I should eat with the king. 📖

Let me hide in the field until the 3rd evening.

**Help with New Moon:** This marked the beginning of a month.

## 1 Samuel 20:6

If your father misses me, say I urgently asked to visit Bethlehem for my family’s annual sacrifice. 📖

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

## 1 Samuel 20:7

If he says, ‘Good,’ I am safe. 📖

If he is furious, you will know he intends evil.

## 1 Samuel 20:8

Show your servant kindness. 📖

You brought me into a covenant with you before the LORD.

If I am guilty, kill me yourself.

Why bring me to your father?”

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Samuel 20:9

Jonathan answered, “Never! 📖

If I knew my father intended evil, would I not tell you?”

## 1 Samuel 20:10

David asked, “Who will tell me if your father answers harshly?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:11

Jonathan said, “Come to the field.” 📖

They went together.

## 1 Samuel 20:12

Jonathan said, “By the LORD, Israel’s God, I will test my father by tomorrow or the next day. 📖

If he favours you, will I not send and tell you?

## 1 Samuel 20:13

If he intends harm, may the LORD punish me very severely unless I tell you and send you safely away. 📖

May the LORD be with you as with my father.

## 1 Samuel 20:14

While I live, show me the LORD’s faithful love, so I do not die. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:15

Never cut off your faithful love from my household, even when the LORD removes all David’s enemies from the earth.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:16

Jonathan made a covenant with David’s family. 📖

He said, “May the LORD hold David’s enemies accountable.”

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Samuel 20:17

Jonathan had David renew his vow out of love. 📖

Jonathan loved David as himself.

**Help with vow:** A vow is a serious promise.

## 1 Samuel 20:18

Jonathan said, “Tomorrow is the New Moon. 📖

Your empty seat will make people miss you.

**Help with New Moon:** This marked the beginning of a month.

## 1 Samuel 20:19

After 3 days, hurry to your first hiding place when this trouble began. 📖

Stay beside the stone Ezel.

## 1 Samuel 20:20

I will shoot 3 arrows beside it as though at a target. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:21

I will send a boy to find the arrows. 📖

If I say, ‘The arrows are on this side; bring them,’ come.

As surely as the LORD lives, you are safe, without danger.

## 1 Samuel 20:22

If I say, ‘The arrows are beyond you,’ leave, because the LORD has sent you away. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:23

The LORD witnesses what we discussed between us forever.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:24

David hid in the field. 📖

At the New Moon, the king sat to eat.

## 1 Samuel 20:25

Saul sat as usual by the wall, opposite Jonathan and beside Abner. 📖

David’s seat was empty.

## 1 Samuel 20:26

Saul said nothing that day. 📖

He thought, “Something made David ceremonially unclean.

Surely he is unclean.”

**Help with ceremonially unclean:** This means not ready for worship under religious rules.

This is Saul’s explanation, not a confirmed account of David’s absence.

## 1 Samuel 20:27

On the 2nd day, after the New Moon, David’s seat remained empty. 📖

Saul asked Jonathan, “Why has Jesse’s son missed the meal yesterday and today?”

## 1 Samuel 20:28

Jonathan answered, “David urgently asked permission to visit Bethlehem. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:29

He said, ‘Our family sacrifices in the city, and my brother ordered me there. 📖

If you favour me, let me see my brothers.’

That is why he is absent.”

## 1 Samuel 20:30

Saul became furious. 📖

He told Jonathan, “You son of a twisted, rebellious woman!

You chose Jesse’s son, shaming yourself and the mother who bore you!

**About this insult:** Saul’s attack on Jonathan and his mother is retained as Saul’s angry speech.

## 1 Samuel 20:31

While Jesse’s son lives, neither you nor your kingdom will be established. 📖

Send for him.

He must die!”

## 1 Samuel 20:32

Jonathan asked, “Why must he die? 📖

What did he do?”

## 1 Samuel 20:33

Saul hurled his spear to kill Jonathan. 📖

Jonathan knew his father intended to kill David.

## 1 Samuel 20:34

Jonathan left the table fiercely angry and ate nothing that 2nd day of the month. 📖

He grieved over his father’s shameful treatment of David.

## 1 Samuel 20:35

In the morning, Jonathan went to his appointment with David in the field, taking a small boy. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:36

He told the boy, “Run and find my arrows.” 📖

As the boy ran, Jonathan shot beyond him.

## 1 Samuel 20:37

The boy reached the arrow’s place, and Jonathan called, “Is the arrow not beyond you?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:38

Jonathan shouted, “Hurry! 📖

Be quick!

Do not delay!”

The boy picked up the arrow and returned.

## 1 Samuel 20:39

The boy knew nothing about the plan. 📖

Only Jonathan and David knew.

## 1 Samuel 20:40

Jonathan gave the boy his equipment. 📖

He said, “Take it to the city.”

## 1 Samuel 20:41

The boy left, and David rose from south of the stone. 📖

He fell face down and bowed 3 times.

David and Jonathan kissed and cried, but David cried more.

## 1 Samuel 20:42

Jonathan said, “Go in peace. 📖

We swore in the LORD’s name that he would witness between us and our descendants forever.”

David left, and Jonathan returned to the city.

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.
