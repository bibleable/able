# 1 Samuel 17 — David and Goliath

**About this chapter**

Goliath challenges Israel.

David fights him, trusting the LORD for rescue.

## 1 Samuel 17:1

The Philistines gathered for war at Socoh in Judah. 📖

They camped between Socoh and Azekah in Ephes-dammim.

## 1 Samuel 17:2

Saul and Israel’s men camped in Elah’s Valley and formed battle lines against the Philistines. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:3

The armies stood on opposite hills with the valley between. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:4

Goliath, a champion from Gath, came from the Philistine camp. 📖

He was 6 cubits and a span tall.

**Help with cubit and span:** These were measures of length.

## 1 Samuel 17:5

Goliath wore a bronze helmet and a bronze coat of mail weighing 5,000 shekels. 📖

**Help with shekels:** A shekel was a measure of weight.

**Help with coat of mail:** This is protective body armour.

## 1 Samuel 17:6

He wore bronze leg armour and carried a bronze javelin between his shoulders. 📖

**Help with javelin:** A javelin is a spear designed for throwing.

## 1 Samuel 17:7

His spear’s shaft was like a weaver’s beam, and its iron point weighed 600 shekels. 📖

His shield-bearer walked ahead.

**Help with weaver’s beam:** This is a large bar in equipment used to make cloth.

**Help with shekels:** A shekel was a measure of weight.

## 1 Samuel 17:8

Goliath shouted to Israel’s ranks, “Why come out for battle? 📖

Am I not a Philistine, and are you not Saul’s servants?

Choose a man to fight me.

## 1 Samuel 17:9

If he kills me, we will serve you. 📖

If I kill him, you will serve and work for us.”

## 1 Samuel 17:10

Goliath said, “Today I defy Israel’s ranks! 📖

Give me a man to fight!”

**Help with defy:** Defy means openly challenge or oppose.

## 1 Samuel 17:11

Saul and all Israel heard and became distressed and very afraid. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:12

David was Jesse’s son. 📖

Jesse was an Ephrathite from Bethlehem in Judah, with 8 sons.

In Saul’s days, Jesse was very old.

## 1 Samuel 17:13

Jesse’s 3 oldest sons followed Saul to battle. 📖

They were Eliab the oldest, Abinadab the 2nd, and Shammah the 3rd.

## 1 Samuel 17:14

David was youngest. 📖

The 3 oldest followed Saul.

## 1 Samuel 17:15

David went back and forth from Saul to tend his father’s sheep at Bethlehem. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:16

For 40 days, the Philistine came forward morning and evening. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:17

Jesse told David, “Hurry to your brothers’ camp with this ephah of roasted grain and 10 loaves. 📖

**Help with ephah:** An ephah was a measure for dry goods.

## 1 Samuel 17:18

Take 10 portions of cheese to their commander. 📖

Check your brothers’ welfare and bring assurance from them.

## 1 Samuel 17:19

They are with Saul and Israel’s men in Elah’s Valley, fighting the Philistines.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:20

David rose early, left the sheep with a keeper, loaded up, and obeyed Jesse. 📖

He reached camp as the army marched out shouting its battle cry.

## 1 Samuel 17:21

Israel and the Philistines formed lines against each other. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:22

David left his supplies with the official responsible for them and ran to the battle line. 📖

He asked his brothers how they were.

## 1 Samuel 17:23

While they talked, Goliath, the Philistine champion from Gath, came from the ranks and shouted his usual challenge. 📖

David heard.

## 1 Samuel 17:24

All Israel’s men saw Goliath and fled in great fear. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:25

The men had said, “See how he challenges Israel! 📖

The king will greatly enrich whoever kills him.

He will give his daughter in marriage and free his father’s household from taxes in Israel.”

## 1 Samuel 17:26

David asked nearby men, “What will be done for the man who kills this Philistine and removes Israel’s disgrace? 📖

Who is this uncircumcised Philistine to defy the living God’s armies?”

**Help with uncircumcised:** Circumcision removes the skin covering the tip of the penis.

Here, David uses this word for the Philistine.

## 1 Samuel 17:27

They explained what would be done for whoever killed him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:28

Eliab, David’s oldest brother, heard and grew angry. 📖

He asked, “Why come here?

Who has those few sheep in the wilderness?

I know your pride and wicked heart.

You came to watch the battle!”

**About Eliab’s accusation:** These are Eliab’s claims about David’s motives.

## 1 Samuel 17:29

David asked, “What have I done now? 📖

Was it not just a question?”

## 1 Samuel 17:30

David turned to someone else and asked about the offer. 📖

They answered as before.

## 1 Samuel 17:31

David’s words reached Saul, who sent for him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:32

David told Saul, “Let no one lose heart because of this Philistine. 📖

Your servant will fight him!”

## 1 Samuel 17:33

Saul answered, “You cannot fight him. 📖

You are a boy, but he has fought since his youth.”

## 1 Samuel 17:34

David answered, “I tended my father’s sheep. 📖

When a lion or bear took a lamb,

## 1 Samuel 17:35

I chased and struck it, rescuing the lamb from its mouth. 📖

If it rose against me, I grabbed its fur, struck it, and killed it.

## 1 Samuel 17:36

I killed lions and bears. 📖

This uncircumcised Philistine will be like them because he defied the living God’s armies.”

**Help with uncircumcised:** Circumcision removes the skin covering the tip of the penis.

Here, David uses this word for the Philistine.

## 1 Samuel 17:37

David added, “The LORD rescued me from the lion’s and bear’s claws. 📖

He will rescue me from this Philistine’s power.”

Saul said, “Go, and may the LORD be with you.”

## 1 Samuel 17:38

Saul dressed David in his own tunic, a bronze helmet, and armour. 📖

**Help with tunic:** A tunic is a garment worn on the body.

**Help with armour:** Armour is protective equipment worn in battle.

## 1 Samuel 17:39

David fastened the sword over the tunic and tried walking, but was not used to the equipment. 📖

He told Saul, “I cannot walk in these.

I am not used to them.”

David took them off.

## 1 Samuel 17:40

David took his staff and chose 5 smooth stones from the stream. 📖

He put them in his shepherd’s bag’s pouch and approached the Philistine, carrying his sling.

**Help with sling:** A sling throws stones from a pouch swung on cords.

## 1 Samuel 17:41

The Philistine came closer, with his shield-bearer ahead. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:42

The Philistine saw David and despised him as a handsome boy with a reddish complexion. 📖

**Help with despised:** Despised means looked down on with scorn.

## 1 Samuel 17:43

He asked David, “Am I a dog that you attack with sticks?” 📖

He cursed David by his gods.

## 1 Samuel 17:44

He called, “Come here! 📖

I will give your flesh to the birds and wild animals!”

## 1 Samuel 17:45

David answered, “You come with sword, spear, and javelin. 📖

I come in the name of the LORD of Hosts, God of Israel’s armies, whom you defied.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 1 Samuel 17:46

Today the LORD will put you in my power. 📖

I will strike you down and cut off your head.

I will give the Philistine army’s bodies to the birds and earth’s creatures.

The whole world will know there is a God in Israel.

## 1 Samuel 17:47

Everyone here will know the LORD saves without sword or spear. 📖

The battle belongs to the LORD.

He will put all of you in our power.”

## 1 Samuel 17:48

The Philistine moved forward to attack. 📖

David ran quickly towards the battle line to meet him.

## 1 Samuel 17:49

David took a stone from his bag and slung it into the Philistine’s forehead. 📖

It sank into his forehead, and he fell face down.

## 1 Samuel 17:50

David defeated and killed the Philistine with a sling and stone, without holding a sword. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:51

David ran and stood over him.

He drew the Philistine’s sword from its cover and killed him. 📖

He cut off his head.

The Philistines saw their hero dead and fled.

**About the killing:** Both this verse’s sword action and the preceding verse’s sling account are retained.

## 1 Samuel 17:52

Israel’s and Judah’s men shouted and chased the Philistines to Gath’s entrance and Ekron’s gates. 📖

Philistine bodies lay along the Shaaraim road to Gath and Ekron.

## 1 Samuel 17:53

Israel returned from chasing and took goods from the Philistine camps. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:54

David brought the Philistine’s head to Jerusalem and put Goliath’s weapons in his own tent. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:55

As Saul watched David approach the Philistine, he asked army commander Abner, “Whose son is this young man?” 📖

Abner answered, “As surely as you live, king, I do not know.”

## 1 Samuel 17:56

The king ordered, “Find out whose son he is!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:57

David returned holding the Philistine’s head. 📖

Abner brought him before Saul.

## 1 Samuel 17:58

Saul asked, “Whose son are you?” 📖

David answered, “Your servant Jesse of Bethlehem’s son.”
