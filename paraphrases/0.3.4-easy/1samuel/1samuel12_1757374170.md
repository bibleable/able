# 1 Samuel 12 — Samuel speaks to Israel

**About this chapter**

Samuel reviews his leadership and the LORD’s acts.

He warns Israel and its king to follow the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 12:1

Samuel told all Israel, “I listened to everything you said and set a king over you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:2

Your king now walks before you. 📖

I am old and grey, and my sons are here.

I have walked before you from my youth until today.

**Help with walks before you:** Here, this describes leadership among the people.

## 1 Samuel 12:3

Here I am. 📖

Give evidence against me before the LORD and his anointed one.

Whose ox or donkey have I taken?

Whom have I cheated or oppressed?

Whose bribe have I accepted to close my eyes?

Tell me, and I will repay it.”

**Help with anointed one:** Here, this means the king, chosen through anointing with oil.

**Help with bribe:** A bribe is a gift influencing a decision unfairly.

**Help with close my eyes:** This means deliberately ignore wrongdoing.

## 1 Samuel 12:4

They answered, “You have not cheated or oppressed us or taken anything from anyone.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:5

Samuel said, “The LORD and his anointed one witness today that you found nothing in my hands.” 📖

They answered, “He is a witness.”

**Help with anointed one:** Here, this means the king, chosen through anointing with oil.

## 1 Samuel 12:6

Samuel said, “The LORD appointed Moses and Aaron and brought your ancestors from Egypt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:7

Come forward, and I will confront you before the LORD with his righteous acts for you and your ancestors. 📖

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means right before God.

## 1 Samuel 12:8

Jacob went to Egypt, and your ancestors cried to the LORD. 📖

The LORD sent Moses and Aaron, who brought them from Egypt and settled them here.

## 1 Samuel 12:9

They forgot the LORD their God. 📖

He sold them into the power of Sisera, Hazor’s army commander, the Philistines, and Moab’s king.

These enemies fought them.

**Help with sold them:** This pictures handing them over to enemy power.

## 1 Samuel 12:10

They cried, ‘We sinned by leaving the LORD and serving the Baals and Ashtoreths. 📖

Rescue us from our enemies, so we can serve you.’

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong against God.

**Help with Baals and Ashtoreths:** These were gods and goddesses worshipped by peoples around Israel.

## 1 Samuel 12:11

The LORD sent Jerubbaal, Barak, Jephthah, and Samuel. 📖

He rescued you from enemies on every side, and you lived safely.

## 1 Samuel 12:12

You saw Nahash, the Ammonites’ king, coming against you. 📖

You told me, ‘No, we must have a king,’ although the LORD your God was your king.

## 1 Samuel 12:13

Here is the king you chose and requested. 📖

The LORD has set a king over you.

## 1 Samuel 12:14

All will be well if you fear, serve, and obey the LORD without rebelling against his command. 📖

This also requires both you and your king to follow the LORD your God.

**Help with fear:** Here, fear includes deep respect for God.

**About this promise:** Its conditions apply to both the people and their king.

## 1 Samuel 12:15

If you disobey the LORD and rebel, his hand will oppose you as it opposed your ancestors. 📖

**Help with hand:** This pictures God’s power.

## 1 Samuel 12:16

Come forward and see the great thing the LORD will do before your eyes. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:17

Is today not the wheat harvest? 📖

I will ask the LORD for thunder and rain.

Then you will see and know the great evil of asking for a king before the LORD.”

## 1 Samuel 12:18

Samuel called, and the LORD sent thunder and rain that day. 📖

Everyone greatly feared the LORD and Samuel.

## 1 Samuel 12:19

They begged Samuel, “Pray to the LORD your God for us, so we do not die! 📖

We added asking for a king to all our other sins.”

**Help with sins:** Sins are wrong things done against God.

## 1 Samuel 12:20

Samuel answered, “Do not fear. 📖

You did all this evil, but do not turn from following the LORD.

Serve him with all your heart.

## 1 Samuel 12:21

Do not follow worthless things that cannot help or rescue you. 📖

They are empty.

## 1 Samuel 12:22

For his great name’s sake, the LORD will not abandon his people. 📖

He was pleased to make you his own.

## 1 Samuel 12:23

I would never sin against the LORD by stopping my prayers for you. 📖

I will keep teaching you the good and right way.

**Help with sin:** Sin means do wrong against God.

## 1 Samuel 12:24

Above all, fear the LORD and serve him faithfully with all your heart. 📖

Think about the great things he has done for you.

**Help with fear:** Here, fear includes deep respect for God.

## 1 Samuel 12:25

If you keep doing evil, both you and your king will be swept away.” 📖

**Help with swept away:** This pictures destruction.
