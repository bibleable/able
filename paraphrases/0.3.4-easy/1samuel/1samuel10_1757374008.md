# 1 Samuel 10 — Saul is chosen as king

**About this chapter**

Samuel anoints Saul and gives him signs.

Later, Samuel gathers the people, and Saul is chosen publicly.

## 1 Samuel 10:1

Samuel poured a flask of oil on Saul’s head, kissed him, and spoke. 📖

“Has the LORD not anointed you ruler over his inheritance?

**Help with anointed:** Anointed means marked for a role by the use of oil.

**Help with inheritance:** Here, inheritance means Israel as the LORD’s people.

## 1 Samuel 10:2

Today, after leaving me, you will meet 2 men at Rachel’s tomb at Zelzah on Benjamin’s border. 📖

They will say, ‘The donkeys are found.

Your father no longer worries about them.

He worries about you, asking, “What should I do about my son?”’

## 1 Samuel 10:3

Go on to the Oak of Tabor. 📖

You will meet 3 men going to God at Bethel.

One carries 3 young goats, one carries 3 loaves, and one carries a skin of wine.

**Help with skin:** Here, skin means a container made from animal skin.

## 1 Samuel 10:4

They will greet you and give you 2 loaves. 📖

Accept them.

## 1 Samuel 10:5

Then you will reach Gibeah of God, where the Philistines have an outpost. 📖

Near the city, you will meet prophets coming down from the high place, prophesying.

Harps, tambourines, flutes, and lyres will go before them.

**Help with prophets and prophesying:** Prophets bring messages from God.

Prophesying means speaking such messages.

**Help with high place:** This was a raised place used for worship.

**Help with lyres:** Lyres are musical instruments with strings.

## 1 Samuel 10:6

The LORD’s Spirit will rush upon you. 📖

You will prophesy with them and become a different person.

**Help with prophesy:** Prophesy means speak a message from God.

## 1 Samuel 10:7

When these signs happen, do what the occasion calls for. 📖

God is with you.

## 1 Samuel 10:8

Go before me to Gilgal. 📖

I will come to give burnt offerings and peace offerings.

Wait 7 days until I come and show you what to do.”

**Help with offerings:** A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

A peace offering expresses peace and fellowship with God.

## 1 Samuel 10:9

As Saul turned to leave Samuel, God changed Saul’s heart. 📖

All the signs happened that day.

## 1 Samuel 10:10

Saul and his servant reached Gibeah, and prophets met him. 📖

God’s Spirit rushed upon Saul, and he prophesied with them.

**Help with prophets and prophesied:** Prophets bring messages from God.

Prophesied means spoke such messages.

## 1 Samuel 10:11

Everyone who knew Saul before saw him prophesying and asked each other, “What happened to Kish’s son? 📖

Is Saul among the prophets too?”

**Help with prophesying and prophets:** These words concern speaking messages from God.

## 1 Samuel 10:12

A local man answered, “Who is their father?” 📖

This became a proverb: “Is Saul among the prophets too?”

**Help with proverb:** A proverb is a saying people repeat.

**About their father:** The source’s question is retained without supplying an answer.

## 1 Samuel 10:13

After prophesying, Saul went to the high place. 📖

**Help with prophesying:** Prophesying means speaking a message from God.

**Help with high place:** This was a raised place used for worship.

## 1 Samuel 10:14

Saul’s uncle asked him and his servant, “Where did you go?” 📖

Saul answered, “We looked for the donkeys.

When we could not find them, we went to Samuel.”

## 1 Samuel 10:15

Saul’s uncle asked, “What did Samuel tell you?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:16

Saul answered, “He assured us the donkeys were found.” 📖

Saul did not tell his uncle what Samuel said about kingship.

## 1 Samuel 10:17

Samuel gathered the people before the LORD at Mizpah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:18

Samuel told Israel, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I brought Israel out of Egypt. 📖

I rescued you from the Egyptians’ power and all the kingdoms oppressing you.’

**Help with oppressing:** Oppressing means treating harshly and holding under power.

## 1 Samuel 10:19

Today, you rejected your God, who saves you from all troubles and suffering. 📖

You told him, ‘No, give us a king.’

Present yourselves before the LORD by tribes and family groups.”

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

## 1 Samuel 10:20

Samuel brought all Israel’s tribes forward, and Benjamin’s tribe was selected. 📖

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

## 1 Samuel 10:21

Samuel brought Benjamin forward by family groups, and Matri’s group was selected. 📖

Saul son of Kish was selected, but they could not find him.

## 1 Samuel 10:22

They asked the LORD again, “Has the man arrived?” 📖

The LORD answered, “He is hiding among the baggage.”

## 1 Samuel 10:23

They ran and brought Saul. 📖

Standing among the people, he was a head taller than anyone else.

## 1 Samuel 10:24

Samuel told everyone, “See the LORD’s chosen one! 📖

No one among the people is like him.”

Everyone shouted, “Long live the king!”

## 1 Samuel 10:25

Samuel explained the rights of kingship, wrote them on a scroll, and placed it before the LORD. 📖

He sent everyone home.

**Help with scroll:** A scroll is a rolled-up writing surface.

## 1 Samuel 10:26

Saul went home to Gibeah. 📖

Brave men whose hearts God had touched went with him.

## 1 Samuel 10:27

Some worthless men said, “How can this man save us?” 📖

They despised Saul and brought no gifts.

Saul stayed silent.

**Help with despised:** Despised means treated with scorn.
