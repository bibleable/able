# Joshua 20 — Cities of refuge

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Joshua to designate refuge cities.

The rules protect someone who kills unintentionally while requiring a trial and a stay in the city.

The cities are for Israelites and foreigners among them.

## Joshua 20:1

The LORD told Joshua, 📖

## Joshua 20:2

“Tell Israel to choose cities of refuge, as I instructed through Moses, 📖

**Help with “refuge”:** This means a place of shelter or safety.

## Joshua 20:3

where someone killing another unintentionally or accidentally may flee from the avenger of blood. 📖

**Help with “avenger of blood”:** This is the person seeking to avenge the death.

## Joshua 20:4

The fleeing person must stand at the city gate’s entrance and present his case to the elders. 📖

They must bring him into the city and give him a place to live among them.

## Joshua 20:5

If the avenger pursues, they must not surrender him, because he killed his neighbor accidentally, without earlier malice. 📖

## Joshua 20:6

He must stay until trial before the assembly and until the high priest serving then dies. 📖

Then he may return to his own home in the city he fled.”

## Joshua 20:7

They chose Kedesh in Galilee, in Naphtali’s hills, Shechem in Ephraim’s hills, and Kiriath-arba, Hebron, in Judah’s hills. 📖

## Joshua 20:8

East beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho, they chose Bezer on Reuben’s wilderness plateau. 📖

They chose Ramoth in Gad’s Gilead and Golan in Manasseh’s Bashan.

## Joshua 20:9

These cities were for all Israelites and foreigners among them, so an unintentional killer could flee there. 📖

He must not die by the avenger’s hand before trial before the assembly.
