# Joshua 16 — Joseph’s allotment and Ephraim’s inheritance

**About this chapter**

The chapter describes Joseph’s allotment and Ephraim’s boundaries.

It includes Ephraim’s towns within Manasseh’s inheritance.

The Canaanites in Gezer remain as forced laborers.

## Joshua 16:1

Joseph’s descendants’ allotment ran from the Jordan at Jericho to Jericho’s waters eastward. 📖

It crossed the wilderness rising from Jericho to Bethel’s hills.

## Joshua 16:2

From Bethel, Luz, it reached the Archite border at Ataroth. 📖

## Joshua 16:3

It descended west to the Japhletite border, to Lower Beth-horon’s border, Gezer, and the Sea. 📖

## Joshua 16:4

Joseph’s sons Ephraim and Manasseh received their inheritance. 📖

## Joshua 16:5

Ephraim’s clans’ inherited border began in the east at Ataroth-addar and went to Upper Beth-horon 📖

## Joshua 16:6

and out toward the Sea. 📖

From Michmethath on the north, it turned east toward Taanath-shiloh, passing by it to Janoah on the east.

## Joshua 16:7

From Janoah it descended to Ataroth and Naarah, reached Jericho, and ended at the Jordan. 📖

## Joshua 16:8

From Tappuah it went west to the Brook of Kanah and ended at the Sea. 📖

This was Ephraim’s tribal clans’ inheritance,

## Joshua 16:9

with all cities and villages assigned Ephraim’s descendants inside Manasseh’s inheritance. 📖

## Joshua 16:10

They did not drive out Gezer’s Canaanites. 📖

These still live among Ephraim’s people as forced laborers.
