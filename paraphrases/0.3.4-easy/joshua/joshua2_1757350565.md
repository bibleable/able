# Joshua 2 — Rahab hides the spies

**About this chapter**

Joshua sends two spies to Jericho.

Rahab hides them and speaks about the LORD.

Her request and the spies’ oath retain all the conditions for protecting her family.

## Joshua 2:1

Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two spies from Shittim. 📖

“Inspect the land, especially Jericho.”

They stayed at the house of Rahab, a prostitute.

**Help with “prostitute”:** This means someone paid for sex.

## Joshua 2:2

Jericho’s king heard, “Israelite men came tonight to spy out the land.” 📖

## Joshua 2:3

He sent to Rahab, “Bring out the men who entered your house. 📖

They came to spy on the whole land.”

## Joshua 2:4

She had hidden both men. 📖

She said, “Yes, they came, but I did not know where from.

## Joshua 2:5

At dusk, before the gate closed, they left. 📖

I do not know which way.

Chase quickly, and you may catch them!”

## Joshua 2:6

But Rahab had taken them to the roof, hidden among flax stalks she laid there. 📖

**Help with “flax”:** This is a plant whose fibers can be used to make cloth.

## Joshua 2:7

The king’s men chased along the road to the Jordan’s fords. 📖

After they left, the gate shut.

**Help with “fords”:** These are shallow places for crossing a river.

## Joshua 2:8

Before the spies lay down that night, Rahab went to the roof 📖

## Joshua 2:9

and said, “I know the LORD gave you this land. 📖

Fear of you fell on us, and all its inhabitants melt with fear.

## Joshua 2:10

We heard how He dried the Red Sea before you when you left Egypt. 📖

We heard of Sihon and Og, the two Amorite kings across the Jordan, whom you devoted to destruction.

**Help with “devoted to destruction”:** Here this means set apart for complete destruction.

## Joshua 2:11

Our hearts melted, and everyone lost courage when we heard. 📖

The LORD your God is God in heaven above and earth below.

**Help with “hearts melted”:** This pictures loss of courage.

## Joshua 2:12

Please swear by the LORD to show my family kindness, as I showed you. 📖

Give a sure sign

## Joshua 2:13

you will spare my father, mother, brothers, sisters, and all belonging to them, saving us from death.” 📖

## Joshua 2:14

The men said, “Our lives for yours! 📖

If you do not report our mission, we will show kindness and faithfulness when the LORD gives the land.”

## Joshua 2:15

Rahab lowered them by rope through the window. 📖

Her house was built into the city wall.

## Joshua 2:16

She said, “Go to the hills so pursuers cannot find you. 📖

Hide three days until they return, then go on your way.”

## Joshua 2:17

The men said, “We are not bound by this oath you made us swear 📖

## Joshua 2:18

unless, when we enter the land, you tie this scarlet cord in our escape window and bring your family inside. 📖

Bring your father, mother, brothers, and all your family into your house.

**Help with “scarlet”:** This means bright red.

## Joshua 2:19

Anyone leaving your door for the street has his blood on his own head, and we are innocent. 📖

Anyone harmed inside with you has his blood on our heads.

**Help with this picture:** Blood on someone’s head describes responsibility for death.

## Joshua 2:20

If you report our mission, we are released from our oath.” 📖

## Joshua 2:21

She replied, “Let it be as you say.” 📖

She sent them away and tied the scarlet cord in the window after they left.

## Joshua 2:22

They stayed in the hills three days. 📖

The pursuers searched all along the road, found no one, and returned.

## Joshua 2:23

The two came down from the hills, crossed the river, and returned to Joshua son of Nun. 📖

They told everything that happened.

## Joshua 2:24

They told Joshua, “The LORD surely handed the whole land over to us. 📖

All its inhabitants melt with fear of us.”
