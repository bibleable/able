# Joshua 23 — Joshua’s warning to Israel’s leaders

**About this chapter**

The elderly Joshua addresses Israel’s leaders after a long rest from war.

He recalls the LORD’s help and commands loyalty.

The warning about intermarriage, other gods, and loss of the land remains conditional.

## Joshua 23:1

Long after the LORD gave Israel rest from all surrounding enemies, Joshua was old and advanced in years, 📖

## Joshua 23:2

and called all Israel, elders, leaders, judges, and officers. 📖

He said, “I am old and advanced in years,

## Joshua 23:3

and you saw everything the LORD your God did to these nations for you. 📖

He fought for you.

## Joshua 23:4

I allotted these remaining nations and all I destroyed as tribal inheritance, from the Jordan west to the Great Sea. 📖

## Joshua 23:5

The LORD your God will push them from your way and drive them out before you. 📖

This lets you possess their land, as He promised.

## Joshua 23:6

Be very strong to keep all written in the Book of the Law of Moses, turning neither right nor left. 📖

## Joshua 23:7

Do not associate with these remaining nations. 📖

Do not call on their gods’ names or swear by them.

Do not serve them or bow to them.

## Joshua 23:8

Hold fast to the LORD your God, as until today. 📖

## Joshua 23:9

He drove out great, powerful nations, and none can stand against you today. 📖

## Joshua 23:10

One of you can rout a thousand because the LORD your God fights for you, as He promised. 📖

## Joshua 23:11

Carefully watch yourselves, loving the LORD your God. 📖

## Joshua 23:12

If you turn and cling to these remaining nations, intermarrying and associating, 📖

## Joshua 23:13

Know He will no longer drive these nations out. 📖

They will be snares and traps, scourges in your sides and thorns in your eyes.

This lasts until you perish from the good land the LORD your God gave you.

**Help with these pictures:** Snares and traps catch their victims.

Scourges are whips.

These images describe harm and suffering.

## Joshua 23:14

Today I go the way of all the earth. 📖

You know with all your heart and soul that none of His good promises failed.

Everything was fulfilled; not one promise failed.

**Help with “the way of all the earth”:** Joshua is speaking of his approaching death.

## Joshua 23:15

As every promised good thing came, He will bring threatened disaster until you are destroyed from His good land. 📖

## Joshua 23:16

If you break His commanded covenant, serve other gods, and bow to them, His anger will burn. 📖

You will quickly perish from His good land.”

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.
