# Joshua 22 — The eastern tribes’ altar

**About this chapter**

Joshua sends the eastern tribes home.

Their new altar raises fear of rebellion and brings a delegation.

The chapter distinguishes the accusation from the tribes’ explanation and the final peaceful response.

## Joshua 22:1

Joshua called Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe 📖

## Joshua 22:2

and said, “You obeyed all Moses, the LORD’s servant, commanded, and all I commanded. 📖

## Joshua 22:3

You never abandoned your brothers throughout this time, up to today. 📖

You kept the LORD your God’s charge.

## Joshua 22:4

He gave your brothers rest as promised. 📖

Return home to the land Moses, His servant, gave you across the Jordan.

## Joshua 22:5

Carefully keep the command and law Moses, the LORD’s servant, gave you. 📖

Love the LORD your God, follow all His ways, keep His commands, and hold fast to Him.

Serve Him with all your heart and soul.”

## Joshua 22:6

Joshua blessed and sent them, and they went home. 📖

## Joshua 22:7

Moses gave Manasseh’s half-tribe Bashan, and Joshua gave the other half land west of the Jordan among their brothers. 📖

Joshua blessed them as they went home,

## Joshua 22:8

saying, “Return with great wealth, immense herds, silver, gold, bronze, iron, and very many clothes. 📖

Share the enemy’s spoil with your brothers.”

## Joshua 22:9

Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe left Israel at Shiloh in Canaan for their own Gilead land. 📖

They acquired it by the LORD’s command through Moses.

## Joshua 22:10

Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe reached Geliloth near the Jordan in Canaan. 📖

They built a great, imposing altar beside the Jordan.

## Joshua 22:11

Israel heard, “Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe built an altar on Canaan’s border. 📖

It is at Geliloth near the Jordan on Israel’s side.”

## Joshua 22:12

All Israel assembled at Shiloh to wage war against them. 📖

## Joshua 22:13

Israel sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe in Gilead. 📖

## Joshua 22:14

Ten chiefs went with him, one family leader per tribe, each a head of an Israelite clan’s family. 📖

## Joshua 22:15

They addressed Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe in Gilead: 📖

## Joshua 22:16

“The LORD’s whole assembly asks, ‘Why break faith with Israel’s God today by turning from the LORD? 📖

Why build yourselves an altar to rebel against Him today?

## Joshua 22:17

Was Peor’s sin not enough? 📖

We remain uncleansed of it today, and it brought plague on the LORD’s assembly.

## Joshua 22:18

Would you now turn from Him? 📖

Rebel today, and tomorrow He will be angry with all Israel.

## Joshua 22:19

If your inherited land is unclean, come share the LORD’s land among us, where His tabernacle stands. 📖

Do not rebel against Him or us with an altar besides the LORD our God’s altar.

## Joshua 22:20

Did Achan son of Zerah not sin with devoted things, bringing wrath on all Israel? 📖

He was not the only one who died for his sin!’”

## Joshua 22:21

Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe answered Israel’s clan leaders: 📖

## Joshua 22:22

“The LORD, the Mighty One, is God! 📖

The LORD, the Mighty One, is God!

He knows; may Israel know too.

If we rebelled or broke faith with Him, do not spare us today.

## Joshua 22:23

Suppose we built this altar to turn from Him for burnt offerings and grain offerings or peace sacrifices. 📖

May the LORD Himself hold us accountable.

## Joshua 22:24

We feared your future descendants might tell ours, ‘What have you to do with the LORD, Israel’s God? 📖

## Joshua 22:25

The LORD set the Jordan between us and you Reubenites and Gadites. 📖

You have no share in Him!’

Your descendants might stop ours fearing Him.

## Joshua 22:26

So we said, ‘Let us build an altar, but not for burnt offerings or sacrifices. 📖

## Joshua 22:27

It will witness between us, you, and future generations. 📖

It shows we will worship the LORD in His presence with burnt offerings, sacrifices, and peace offerings.’

In the future, your descendants cannot say to ours, ‘You have no share in the LORD!’

## Joshua 22:28

We said, ‘If they say this to us or our descendants, we will show our fathers’ replica of His altar. 📖

It is not for burnt offerings or sacrifices but a witness between us and you.’

**Help with “replica”:** This means a copy.

## Joshua 22:29

Far be it from us to rebel or turn from the LORD today. 📖

We would not build an altar for burnt, grain, or other sacrifices besides the LORD our God’s altar.

His altar stands before His tabernacle.”

## Joshua 22:30

Phinehas the priest and the chiefs, Israel’s clan heads with him, were satisfied by Reuben’s, Gad’s, and Manasseh’s answer. 📖

## Joshua 22:31

Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest told Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh, “Today we know the LORD is among us. 📖

You did not break faith.

You delivered Israel from His hand.”

**Help with “His hand”:** Here this refers to the LORD’s threatened judgment.

## Joshua 22:32

Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the other leaders returned to Israel in Canaan. 📖

They reported about Reuben and Gad in Gilead.

## Joshua 22:33

Israel welcomed the report and blessed God. 📖

They stopped speaking of war to destroy Reuben’s and Gad’s land.

## Joshua 22:34

Reuben and Gad named the altar Witness, saying, “It witnesses between us that the LORD is God.” 📖
