# Joshua 11 — The northern kings and the rest from war

**About this chapter**

Northern kings gather against Israel.

The LORD promises victory, and Joshua attacks.

The account retains hamstringing horses, the destruction of people, the cities not burned, and the long campaign.

## Joshua 11:1

Jabin king of Hazor heard and sent to Jobab king of Madon and the kings of Shimron and Achshaph, 📖

## Joshua 11:2

northern mountain kings, those in Arabah south of Chinnereth, the foothills, and Naphoth-dor westward, 📖

## Joshua 11:3

Canaanites east and west, and Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites in the hills. 📖

He sent to Hivites below Hermon in Mizpah.

## Joshua 11:4

The kings came with all their armies, numerous as seashore sand, with many horses and chariots. 📖

## Joshua 11:5

They united and camped at Merom’s waters to fight Israel. 📖

## Joshua 11:6

The LORD told Joshua, “Do not fear. 📖

By this time tomorrow I will hand them all over slain before Israel.

Hamstring their horses and burn their chariots.”

**Help with “hamstring”:** This means cutting tendons in the backs of the horses’ legs.

## Joshua 11:7

Joshua and his whole army suddenly attacked them at Merom’s waters, 📖

## Joshua 11:8

and the LORD handed them over. 📖

Israel pursued to Greater Sidon and Misrephoth-maim, east to the Valley of Mizpeh.

They struck them down, leaving no survivors.

## Joshua 11:9

Joshua obeyed the LORD, hamstringing horses and burning chariots. 📖

## Joshua 11:10

He turned back, captured Hazor, and killed its king by sword. 📖

Hazor was formerly head of these kingdoms.

## Joshua 11:11

Israel killed everyone in Hazor by sword, devoted to destruction. 📖

Nothing breathing remained, and Joshua burned Hazor.

## Joshua 11:12

Joshua captured all these kings and cities and killed them by sword. 📖

He devoted them to destruction as Moses, the LORD’s servant, commanded.

## Joshua 11:13

Israel did not burn the cities built on mounds, except Hazor, which Joshua burned. 📖

## Joshua 11:14

Israel took all city plunder and livestock for themselves. 📖

They killed all the people by sword, completely destroying them, sparing nothing breathing.

## Joshua 11:15

The LORD commanded Moses, His servant; Moses commanded Joshua. 📖

Joshua did everything the LORD commanded Moses, leaving nothing undone.

## Joshua 11:16

Joshua took all this region: hills, all Negev, all Goshen, western foothills, Arabah, Israel’s mountains and foothills, 📖

## Joshua 11:17

from Mount Halak toward Seir to Baal-gad in Lebanon’s valley below Mount Hermon. 📖

He captured and killed all their kings.

## Joshua 11:18

Joshua fought these kings a long time. 📖

## Joshua 11:19

Only Gibeon’s Hivites made peace with Israel. 📖

Every other city was taken in battle.

## Joshua 11:20

The LORD hardened their hearts to fight Israel, to be devoted to destruction without mercy. 📖

They were annihilated as He commanded Moses.

**Help with “hardened their hearts”:** This describes making them stubborn in their decision to fight.

## Joshua 11:21

Joshua eliminated the Anakim from Hebron, Debir, and Anab’s hills, and all Judah’s and Israel’s hill country. 📖

He devoted them and their cities to destruction.

## Joshua 11:22

No Anakim remained in Israel’s land. 📖

Some survived only in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod.

## Joshua 11:23

Joshua took the whole land as the LORD told Moses. 📖

He gave Israel the land as an inheritance by tribal allotments.

The land rested from war.
