# Joshua 4 — Stones to remember the crossing

**About this chapter**

The LORD commands twelve stones to be taken from the Jordan.

Joshua also places twelve stones in the river.

The crossing ends, and the stones at Gilgal become a memorial for future children.

## Joshua 4:1

After the whole nation crossed the Jordan, the LORD told Joshua, 📖

## Joshua 4:2

“Choose twelve men, one from each tribe, 📖

## Joshua 4:3

and command, ‘Take twelve stones from the Jordan’s middle, where the priests stood. 📖

Carry them and put them where you spend tonight.’”

## Joshua 4:4

Joshua called the twelve Israelites he appointed, one per tribe, 📖

## Joshua 4:5

and said, “Go before the LORD your God’s ark into the Jordan’s middle. 📖

Each must carry a stone on his shoulder, matching Israel’s number of tribes.

## Joshua 4:6

This is a sign among you. 📖

When your children ask in the future, ‘What do these stones mean to you?’

## Joshua 4:7

say, ‘The Jordan’s waters were cut off before the LORD’s covenant ark. 📖

When it crossed, the waters were cut off.’

These stones are Israel’s memorial forever.”

## Joshua 4:8

Israel obeyed Joshua and took twelve stones from the Jordan’s middle, one per tribe, as the LORD told him. 📖

They carried them to camp and set them down.

## Joshua 4:9

Joshua also placed twelve stones in the Jordan’s middle, where the ark-carrying priests stood. 📖

They remain there today.

**About the stones:** The chapter describes stones at Gilgal and stones in the river separately.

## Joshua 4:10

The priests carrying the ark stood in the Jordan’s middle until everything was done. 📖

The people completed the LORD’s commands through Joshua, as Moses directed him.

The people hurried across,

## Joshua 4:11

and after all crossed, the priests and LORD’s ark crossed before their eyes. 📖

## Joshua 4:12

Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe crossed armed ahead of Israel, as Moses commanded. 📖

## Joshua 4:13

About 40,000 troops armed for battle crossed before the LORD into Jericho’s plains. 📖

## Joshua 4:14

The LORD exalted Joshua before all Israel that day. 📖

They revered him throughout his life as they revered Moses.

**Help with “revered”:** This means regarded with deep respect.

## Joshua 4:15

The LORD told Joshua, 📖

## Joshua 4:16

“Command the priests carrying the ark of the Testimony to come from the Jordan.” 📖

**Help with “Testimony”:** This refers to God’s covenant witness given to Israel.

## Joshua 4:17

Joshua told the priests, “Come from the Jordan.” 📖

## Joshua 4:18

When the priests with the LORD’s covenant ark left the Jordan and stepped on dry land, the waters returned. 📖

They overflowed all the banks as before.

## Joshua 4:19

On day ten of month one, the people left the Jordan and camped at Gilgal on Jericho’s eastern border. 📖

## Joshua 4:20

Joshua set the twelve stones taken from the Jordan at Gilgal. 📖

## Joshua 4:21

He told Israel, “When future children ask their fathers, ‘What do these stones mean?’ 📖

## Joshua 4:22

say, ‘Israel crossed the Jordan on dry ground.’ 📖

## Joshua 4:23

The LORD your God dried the Jordan before you until you crossed, as He dried the Red Sea before us. 📖

He kept it dry until we crossed.

## Joshua 4:24

He did this so all earth’s peoples may know His hand is mighty. 📖

It is so you may always fear the LORD your God.”

**Help with “hand”:** This pictures God’s power.
