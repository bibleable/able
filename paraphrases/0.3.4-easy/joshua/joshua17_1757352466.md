# Joshua 17 — Manasseh’s inheritance and Joseph’s request

**About this chapter**

Manasseh’s clans receive their allotment, including Zelophehad’s daughters.

The chapter describes boundaries and the Canaanites who remain.

Joseph’s descendants ask for more land, and Joshua tells them to clear the forest.

## Joshua 17:1

Manasseh was Joseph’s firstborn. 📖

His firstborn Machir, father of the Gileadites, received Gilead and Bashan because he was a warrior.

## Joshua 17:2

This allotment was for Manasseh’s other descendants: Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida’s clans. 📖

These were the other male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph’s clans.

## Joshua 17:3

Zelophehad, Hepher’s son, had no sons, only daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 📖

Hepher was Gilead’s son, Gilead Machir’s son, and Machir Manasseh’s son.

## Joshua 17:4

They approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders. 📖

“The LORD commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our brothers,” they said.

Joshua gave it among their father’s brothers, as the LORD commanded.

## Joshua 17:5

Manasseh received ten shares, besides Gilead and Bashan beyond the Jordan, 📖

## Joshua 17:6

because his daughters inherited among his sons. 📖

The rest of Manasseh’s sons owned Gilead.

## Joshua 17:7

Manasseh’s border ran from Asher to Michmethath near Shechem, then south to include En-tappuah’s inhabitants. 📖

## Joshua 17:8

Tappuah’s region belonged to Manasseh, but Tappuah itself on Manasseh’s border belonged to Ephraim. 📖

## Joshua 17:9

The border continued south to the Brook of Kanah. 📖

Ephraim had cities among Manasseh’s cities.

Manasseh’s border lay north of the brook and ended at the Sea.

## Joshua 17:10

Ephraim was south, Manasseh north, with the Sea as border. 📖

Manasseh adjoined Asher northward and Issachar eastward.

## Joshua 17:11

Within Issachar and Asher, Manasseh received Beth-shean, Ibleam, Dor, also called Naphath, Endor, Taanach, and Megiddo. 📖

Each had surrounding settlements.

## Joshua 17:12

Manasseh’s descendants could not occupy these cities because the Canaanites insisted on staying. 📖

## Joshua 17:13

When Israel grew stronger, they made the Canaanites forced laborers but did not drive them out completely. 📖

## Joshua 17:14

Joseph’s sons asked Joshua, “Why only one inherited portion? 📖

We are many because the LORD blessed us greatly.”

## Joshua 17:15

Joshua replied, “If you are many and Ephraim’s hills too small, clear forest land among the Perizzites and Rephaim.” 📖

## Joshua 17:16

They said, “The hill country is not enough for us. 📖

All the Canaanites living in the valley have iron chariots.

This includes Beth-shean with its towns and the Valley of Jezreel.”

## Joshua 17:17

Joshua told Joseph’s house, Ephraim and Manasseh, “You are many and strong. 📖

You will have more than one allotment,

## Joshua 17:18

because the hills are yours too. 📖

Clear the forest, and its farthest limits will be yours.

You can drive the Canaanites out, though strong with iron chariots.”
