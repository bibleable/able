# Joshua 14 — The inheritance and Caleb’s request

**About this chapter**

Eleazar, Joshua, and the family heads distribute Canaan by lot.

Caleb recalls the promise made forty-five years earlier and asks for the hill country.

Joshua gives him Hebron.

## Joshua 14:1

Israel’s Canaan inheritances were distributed by Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and Israel’s tribal family heads. 📖

## Joshua 14:2

The nine and a half tribes received by lot, as the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖

**Help with “lot”:** This means using lots to make an allocation rather than a person choosing.

## Joshua 14:3

Moses gave the other two and a half tribes inheritance east of the Jordan, but none among them to Levites. 📖

## Joshua 14:4

Joseph’s descendants became two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. 📖

Levites received only cities to live in and pastureland for flocks and herds, no land portion.

## Joshua 14:5

Israel divided the land as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Joshua 14:6

Judah’s sons approached Joshua at Gilgal. 📖

Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite spoke.

“You know what the LORD said to Moses, the man of God, at Kadesh-barnea about you and me.

## Joshua 14:7

I was forty when Moses, the LORD’s servant, sent me from Kadesh-barnea to scout. 📖

I brought an honest report.

## Joshua 14:8

My fellow scouts melted the people’s hearts with fear, but I stayed loyal to the LORD my God. 📖

**Help with “melted hearts”:** This pictures loss of courage.

## Joshua 14:9

Moses swore that day, ‘The land your feet walked will be your inheritance and your children’s forever. 📖

This is because you wholly followed the LORD my God.’

## Joshua 14:10

He kept me alive these forty-five years, as He promised. 📖

He spoke this word to Moses while Israel wandered in the wilderness.

I am now eighty-five years old,

## Joshua 14:11

as strong as when Moses sent me, for war and coming and going. 📖

## Joshua 14:12

Give me these hills promised that day. 📖

You heard of the Anakim there and their great, fortified cities.

Perhaps, with the LORD’s help, I will drive them out, as He said.”

**Help with “fortified”:** This means protected by strong defenses.

## Joshua 14:13

Joshua blessed Caleb son of Jephunneh and gave him Hebron as inheritance. 📖

## Joshua 14:14

Hebron remains Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite’s inheritance because he wholly followed the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## Joshua 14:15

Hebron was called Kiriath-arba, after Arba, greatest among the Anakim. 📖

The land rested from war.
