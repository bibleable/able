# Joshua 5 — Gilgal and the Commander of the LORD’s army

**About this chapter**

Israel is circumcised at Gilgal and keeps Passover.

The manna stops after the people eat the land’s produce.

Joshua meets the Commander of the LORD’s army near Jericho.

## Joshua 5:1

All Amorite kings west of the Jordan and Canaanite coastal kings heard the LORD dried the Jordan until Israel crossed. 📖

Their hearts melted and spirits failed with fear of Israel.

**Help with “hearts melted”:** This pictures loss of courage.

## Joshua 5:2

The LORD told Joshua, “Make flint knives and circumcise Israel’s sons again.” 📖

**Help with these words:** Flint is hard stone.

Circumcision removes the foreskin.

The following verses explain which generation is being circumcised.

## Joshua 5:3

Joshua made flint knives and circumcised Israel’s sons at Gibeath-haaraloth. 📖

## Joshua 5:4

Joshua did this because all the men of war leaving Egypt died on the wilderness journey after leaving. 📖

## Joshua 5:5

All who left Egypt were circumcised, but none born during the wilderness journey from Egypt were circumcised. 📖

## Joshua 5:6

Israel wandered forty years until all its fighting men who left Egypt died, because they disobeyed the LORD. 📖

The LORD vowed never to let them see the land He swore to their fathers to give us.

It flows with milk and honey.

**Help with “milk and honey”:** These picture plentiful good food.

## Joshua 5:7

He raised their sons in their place. 📖

Joshua circumcised these sons, who were uncircumcised because this had not been done on the journey.

## Joshua 5:8

After all the nation was circumcised, they stayed in camp until healed. 📖

## Joshua 5:9

The LORD told Joshua, “Today I rolled Egypt’s reproach away from you.” 📖

That place is called Gilgal to this day.

**Help with “reproach”:** This means disgrace.

Rolling it away pictures removing it.

## Joshua 5:10

At Gilgal on Jericho’s plains, Israel kept Passover on the month’s fourteenth evening. 📖

## Joshua 5:11

The very day after Passover, they ate the land’s produce: unleavened bread and roasted grain. 📖

**Help with “unleavened”:** This means made without yeast.

## Joshua 5:12

The day after they ate land produce, manna stopped. 📖

Israel had no more manna and ate Canaan’s crops that year.

**Help with “manna”:** This was the food given during the wilderness journey.

## Joshua 5:13

Near Jericho, Joshua saw a man before him holding a drawn sword. 📖

He approached and asked, “Are You for us or our enemies?”

## Joshua 5:14

He replied, “Neither. 📖

I come as Commander of the LORD’s army.”

Joshua fell facedown in reverence and asked, “What does my Lord say to His servant?”

## Joshua 5:15

The Commander replied, “Remove your sandals. 📖

Where you stand is holy.”

Joshua obeyed.
