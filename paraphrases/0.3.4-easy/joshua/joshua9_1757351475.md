# Joshua 9 — Gibeon’s deception and the sworn treaty

**About this chapter**

Gibeon’s people pretend to be distant travelers.

Israel makes a treaty without asking the LORD.

The leaders preserve their oath, and Joshua assigns the Gibeonites lasting service.

## Joshua 9:1

All kings west of the Jordan heard: those in hills, foothills, and the Great Sea’s coast toward Lebanon. 📖

They were Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

## Joshua 9:2

They united to fight Joshua and Israel. 📖

## Joshua 9:3

Gibeon’s people heard what Joshua did to Jericho and Ai 📖

## Joshua 9:4

and deceived Israel, setting out as envoys. 📖

Their donkeys carried worn sacks and old, cracked, repaired wineskins.

**Help with these words:** Envoys are messengers sent to represent others.

Wineskins are skin containers for wine.

## Joshua 9:5

They wore worn, patched sandals and threadbare clothes. 📖

All their bread was dry and moldy.

## Joshua 9:6

At Gilgal’s camp they told Joshua and Israel’s men, “We come from far away. 📖

Make a treaty with us.”

## Joshua 9:7

Israel’s men told these Hivites, “Maybe you live nearby. 📖

How can we make a treaty?”

## Joshua 9:8

They told Joshua, “We are your servants.” 📖

He asked, “Who are you, and where from?”

## Joshua 9:9

They replied, “We come from very far because of the LORD your God’s fame. 📖

We heard everything He did in Egypt

## Joshua 9:10

and to two Amorite kings beyond the Jordan: Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan, ruling Ashtaroth. 📖

## Joshua 9:11

Our elders and people told us, ‘Take supplies, meet them, and say: We are your servants. 📖

Please make a treaty.’

## Joshua 9:12

This bread was warm when we packed it at home on departure day. 📖

Now look: dry and moldy.

## Joshua 9:13

Our wineskins were new when filled; now they are cracked. 📖

Clothes and sandals wore out on our very long journey.”

## Joshua 9:14

Israel’s men sampled their supplies but did not seek the LORD’s counsel. 📖

## Joshua 9:15

Joshua made a peace treaty to let them live. 📖

The assembly’s leaders swore them an oath.

## Joshua 9:16

Three days after the Gibeonite treaty, Israel learned these were neighbors living among them. 📖

## Joshua 9:17

Israel set out and reached their cities on day three: Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath-jearim. 📖

## Joshua 9:18

Israel did not attack because the leaders swore by the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

The whole assembly complained against the leaders.

## Joshua 9:19

All leaders replied, “We swore by the LORD, Israel’s God, and cannot touch them. 📖

## Joshua 9:20

Let them live, so wrath does not fall on us because of our oath.” 📖

## Joshua 9:21

They said, “Let them live as woodcutters and water carriers for the whole assembly.” 📖

They kept their promise.

## Joshua 9:22

Joshua summoned Gibeon’s people: “Why lie that you live far away, when you live among us? 📖

## Joshua 9:23

You are cursed to serve forever as woodcutters and water carriers for my God’s house.” 📖

## Joshua 9:24

They answered, “We were clearly told the LORD your God commanded His servant Moses to give you all the land. 📖

He was to destroy every inhabitant before you.

We greatly feared for our lives because of you, so did this.

## Joshua 9:25

We are in your hands. 📖

Do whatever seems good and right.”

## Joshua 9:26

Joshua protected them from Israel, and Israel did not kill them. 📖

## Joshua 9:27

That day he made them woodcutters and water carriers for the LORD’s assembly and altar at His chosen place. 📖

They remain so today.
