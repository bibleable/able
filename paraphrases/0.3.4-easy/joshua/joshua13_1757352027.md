# Joshua 13 — Land remaining and inheritances east of the Jordan

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells the elderly Joshua that much land remains to be possessed.

The chapter lists that land and recalls the inheritances Moses gave east of the Jordan.

The repeated statement about Levi’s inheritance is retained.

## Joshua 13:1

Joshua was old, advanced in years. 📖

The LORD said, “You are old, but much land remains to possess.

## Joshua 13:2

The remaining land includes all Philistine and Geshurite territory, 📖

## Joshua 13:3

from Shihor east of Egypt to Ekron northward, considered Canaanite territory. 📖

It includes the five Philistine rulers’ territories: Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, Ekron, and the Avvites’ territory,

## Joshua 13:4

all Canaanite land southward, from Mearah of the Sidonians to Aphek, to the Amorite border, 📖

## Joshua 13:5

Gebalite land and all Lebanon eastward, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath. 📖

## Joshua 13:6

I Myself will drive out all hill-country inhabitants from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, all Sidonians. 📖

Divide it by lot for Israel’s inheritance, as I commanded.

**Help with “lot”:** Lots were used to assign portions of land.

## Joshua 13:7

Divide this land among nine tribes and Manasseh’s half-tribe.” 📖

## Joshua 13:8

Manasseh’s other half, Reuben, and Gad received Moses’ inheritance east beyond the Jordan, as Moses, the LORD’s servant, assigned: 📖

## Joshua 13:9

Aroer at Arnon Valley’s rim, the city in the valley’s middle, and all Medeba’s plateau to Dibon, 📖

## Joshua 13:10

all cities of Sihon, Amorite king reigning in Heshbon, to the Ammonite border, 📖

## Joshua 13:11

Gilead, Geshurite and Maacathite territory, all Mount Hermon, all Bashan to Salecah, 📖

## Joshua 13:12

This was Og’s whole kingdom in Bashan. 📖

He reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei and remained among the Rephaim.

Moses defeated them and drove them out,

## Joshua 13:13

but Israel did not drive out Geshurites and Maacathites. 📖

Geshur and Maacath still live among Israel.

## Joshua 13:14

Moses gave Levi no inheritance. 📖

Food offerings to the LORD, Israel’s God, are their inheritance, as He promised.

## Joshua 13:15

Moses gave Reuben’s tribal clans this: 📖

## Joshua 13:16

land from Aroer at Arnon Valley’s rim, with its middle-valley city, to all the plateau beyond Medeba, 📖

## Joshua 13:17

Heshbon and all its plateau cities: Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon, 📖

## Joshua 13:18

Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath, 📖

## Joshua 13:19

Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley, 📖

## Joshua 13:20

Beth-peor, Pisgah’s slopes, Beth-jeshimoth, 📖

## Joshua 13:21

all plateau cities and Sihon’s whole Amorite kingdom at Heshbon. 📖

Moses killed Sihon and Midian’s chiefs, Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, Sihon’s princes living there.

## Joshua 13:22

Israel also killed the diviner Balaam son of Beor by sword with the others. 📖

**Help with “diviner”:** This means someone seeking hidden knowledge or predictions through spiritual practices.

## Joshua 13:23

Reuben’s border was the Jordan’s bank. 📖

This was Reuben’s clans’ inheritance, cities and villages.

## Joshua 13:24

Moses gave Gad’s tribal clans this: 📖

## Joshua 13:25

Jazer’s territory, all Gilead’s cities, and half Ammonite land to Aroer near Rabbah, 📖

## Joshua 13:26

from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and Mahanaim to Debir’s border, 📖

## Joshua 13:27

valley cities Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, with the rest of Sihon king of Heshbon’s kingdom. 📖

It lay east of the Jordan to the Sea of Chinnereth’s edge.

## Joshua 13:28

This was Gad’s clans’ inheritance, cities and villages. 📖

## Joshua 13:29

Moses gave Manasseh’s half-tribe’s clans, half Manasseh’s descendants, this: 📖

## Joshua 13:30

from Mahanaim through all Bashan, Og king of Bashan’s whole kingdom, including Jair’s sixty towns in Bashan, 📖

## Joshua 13:31

half Gilead, Ashtaroth, and Edrei, Og’s royal Bashan cities. 📖

These went to Machir son of Manasseh’s descendant clans, half Machir’s descendants.

## Joshua 13:32

Moses gave these portions on Moab’s plains beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho. 📖

## Joshua 13:33

Moses gave Levi no inheritance. 📖

The LORD, Israel’s God, is their inheritance, as He promised.
