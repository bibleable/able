# Joshua 1 — Be strong and brave

**About this chapter**

After Moses dies, God gives Joshua the work of leading Israel into the promised land.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Joshua 1:1

Moses, the LORD's servant, had died. 📖

The LORD spoke to Joshua, son of Nun.

Joshua had been Moses' helper.

## Joshua 1:2

“My servant Moses is dead. 📖

Get ready to cross the Jordan River with all these people.

Enter the land I am giving the Israelites.”

## Joshua 1:3

“I give you every place where you walk. 📖

This is what I promised Moses.”

## Joshua 1:4

“Your land will reach from the desert and Lebanon to the great Euphrates River. 📖

It will include all the Hittites' land.

It will reach west to the Great Sea.”

**Help with a name:** The Great Sea is the Mediterranean Sea.

## Joshua 1:5

“No one will defeat you during your life. 📖

I will be with you as I was with Moses.

I will never leave you or give up on you.”

## Joshua 1:6

“Be strong and brave. 📖

You will lead these people to receive their land.

I promised their ancestors that I would give them this land.”

**Help with a word:** Ancestors are family members who lived long ago.

## Joshua 1:7

“Be strong and very brave. 📖

Carefully obey all the law my servant Moses gave you.

Do not turn away from it.

Then you will succeed wherever you go.”

**Help with words:** God's law tells his people how to live.

## Joshua 1:8

“Keep speaking about this Book of the Law. 📖

Think about it day and night.

Carefully do everything it says.

Then your work will go well and succeed.”

## Joshua 1:9

“I have commanded you to be strong and brave. 📖

Do not be afraid or lose courage.

The LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”

## Joshua 1:10

Joshua gave orders to the people's officers. 📖

## Joshua 1:11

“Go through the camp and tell everyone to prepare food. 📖

In 3 days you will cross the Jordan River.

You will take the land the LORD your God is giving you.”

## Joshua 1:12

Joshua spoke to the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh. 📖

**Help with a word:** A tribe is a large family group.

## Joshua 1:13

“Remember what Moses, the LORD's servant, told you. 📖

The LORD your God is giving you rest and this land.”

## Joshua 1:14

“Your wives, young children, and animals can stay in the land Moses gave you east of the Jordan. 📖

But all your strong fighting men must cross ahead of the others.

Take your weapons and help your brothers.”

## Joshua 1:15

“Help until the LORD gives them rest too. 📖

Help them receive the land the LORD your God gives them.

Then return to the land Moses gave you east of the Jordan.”

## Joshua 1:16

They answered Joshua, “We will do everything you command. 📖

We will go wherever you send us.”

## Joshua 1:17

“We will obey you as we obeyed Moses. 📖

May the LORD your God be with you as he was with Moses.”

## Joshua 1:18

“Anyone who rebels and refuses to obey your commands must die. 📖

Only be strong and brave!”
