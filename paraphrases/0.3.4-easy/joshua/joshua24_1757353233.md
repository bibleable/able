# Joshua 24 — The covenant at Shechem and three burials

**About this chapter**

Joshua gathers Israel at Shechem and repeats the LORD’s account of their history.

The people choose to serve the LORD, and Joshua warns them and makes a covenant.

The chapter records Joshua’s death, Joseph’s burial, and Eleazar’s death and burial.

## Joshua 24:1

Joshua assembled all Israel’s tribes at Shechem, summoning elders, leaders, judges, and officers. 📖

They presented themselves before God.

## Joshua 24:2

Joshua told everyone, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says: ‘Your fathers lived beyond the Euphrates long ago and worshiped other gods. 📖

These included Terah, father of Abraham and Nahor.

## Joshua 24:3

I took your father Abraham from beyond the Euphrates and led him through all Canaan. 📖

I multiplied his descendants and gave him Isaac,

## Joshua 24:4

To Isaac I gave Jacob and Esau. 📖

I gave Esau Mount Seir to possess, but Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt.

## Joshua 24:5

I sent Moses and Aaron and afflicted the Egyptians by what I did there. 📖

Afterward I brought you out.

## Joshua 24:6

When I brought your fathers from Egypt, you reached the Red Sea. 📖

Egyptians pursued them with chariots and horsemen as far as the Red Sea.

## Joshua 24:7

Your fathers cried to the LORD. 📖

He set darkness between you and the Egyptians and brought the sea over them, engulfing them.

Your own eyes saw what I did to the Egyptians.

You lived long in the wilderness.

## Joshua 24:8

Later I brought you to the Amorites’ land beyond the Jordan. 📖

They fought you, but I handed them over and destroyed them before you so you possessed their land.

## Joshua 24:9

Balak son of Zippor, Moab’s king, set out to fight Israel. 📖

He summoned Balaam son of Beor to curse you,

## Joshua 24:10

but I refused to hear Balaam. 📖

He blessed you again and again, and I delivered you from his hand.

## Joshua 24:11

You crossed the Jordan to Jericho, whose people fought you. 📖

So did Amorites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hittites, Girgashites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

I handed them over.

## Joshua 24:12

I sent the hornet ahead, driving out the two Amorite kings, not by your sword or bow. 📖

**Help with “hornet”:** This is a stinging insect.

## Joshua 24:13

I gave land you did not toil on and cities you did not build. 📖

You live there and eat from vineyards and olive groves you never planted.’

## Joshua 24:14

Fear the LORD and serve Him sincerely and truly. 📖

Reject the gods your fathers served beyond the Euphrates and in Egypt, and serve Him.

## Joshua 24:15

If you dislike serving Him, choose today: your fathers’ gods beyond the Euphrates, or the Amorites’ gods where you live. 📖

My household and I will serve the LORD!”

## Joshua 24:16

The people replied, “We would never abandon the LORD for other gods! 📖

## Joshua 24:17

The LORD our God brought us and our fathers from Egypt’s slavery and did great signs before our eyes. 📖

He protected our whole journey and among every nation we passed.

## Joshua 24:18

He drove out all nations, including the land’s Amorites. 📖

We too will serve Him because He is our God!”

## Joshua 24:19

Joshua said, “You cannot serve the LORD. 📖

For He is a holy God, a jealous God, and will not forgive your rebellion or sins.

**Help with “jealous”:** Here this concerns God’s claim on their undivided worship.

## Joshua 24:20

If you abandon Him for foreign gods, He will turn, bring disaster, and consume you, even after doing you good.” 📖

## Joshua 24:21

They replied, “No! 📖

We will serve the LORD!”

## Joshua 24:22

Joshua said, “You witness against yourselves that you chose to serve the LORD.” 📖

They said, “We are witnesses!”

## Joshua 24:23

He said, “Remove foreign gods among you and incline your hearts to the LORD, Israel’s God.” 📖

**Help with “incline your hearts”:** This means turn your inward desires toward Him.

## Joshua 24:24

They told Joshua, “We will serve the LORD our God and obey His voice.” 📖

## Joshua 24:25

That day Joshua made a covenant for the people and established a law and ruling at Shechem. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Joshua 24:26

He recorded this in the Book of the Law of God. 📖

He set a large stone under the oak near the LORD’s sanctuary.

## Joshua 24:27

Joshua told everyone, “This stone will witness against us: it heard all the LORD’s words to us. 📖

It witnesses against you if you deny your God.”

**Help with this picture:** The stone is pictured as hearing the words and standing as a lasting witness.

## Joshua 24:28

He sent everyone to their own inheritance. 📖

## Joshua 24:29

Later Joshua son of Nun, the LORD’s servant, died aged 110. 📖

## Joshua 24:30

They buried him in his inherited land at Timnath-serah, in Ephraim’s hills north of Mount Gaash. 📖

## Joshua 24:31

Israel served the LORD throughout Joshua’s life and the lives of elders who outlived him. 📖

Those elders experienced all the LORD’s works for Israel.

## Joshua 24:32

Joseph’s bones, brought from Egypt, were buried at Shechem. 📖

Jacob bought the plot from the sons of Hamor, Shechem’s father, for a hundred silver pieces.

It became Joseph’s descendants’ inheritance.

## Joshua 24:33

Eleazar son of Aaron died too. 📖

They buried him at Gibeah, given to his son Phinehas in Ephraim’s hills.
