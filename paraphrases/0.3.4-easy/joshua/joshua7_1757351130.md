# Joshua 7 — Defeat at Ai and Achan’s confession

**About this chapter**

Israel is defeated at Ai, and Joshua prays.

The LORD explains the offense involving things set apart for destruction.

Achan confesses, and the chapter records his death and the deaths of the others taken with him.

## Joshua 7:1

Israel acted unfaithfully with things devoted to destruction. 📖

Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from Judah, took some.

The LORD’s anger burned against Israel.

## Joshua 7:2

Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, near Beth-aven east of Bethel. 📖

“Go spy the land.”

They scouted Ai.

## Joshua 7:3

They returned: “Send only two or three thousand to attack Ai. 📖

Its people are few, so do not exhaust everyone.”

## Joshua 7:4

About three thousand went, but fled Ai’s men. 📖

## Joshua 7:5

Ai’s men killed about thirty-six, chasing from the gate to the quarries, striking on the slopes. 📖

The people’s hearts melted like water.

**Help with this picture:** Melting hearts picture loss of courage.

## Joshua 7:6

Joshua tore his clothes and fell facedown before the LORD’s ark until evening, with Israel’s elders. 📖

All sprinkled dust on their heads.

## Joshua 7:7

Joshua prayed, “O, Lord GOD, why bring us across the Jordan to the Amorites for destruction? 📖

If only we stayed content on the other side!

## Joshua 7:8

O Lord, what can I say after Israel fled its enemies? 📖

## Joshua 7:9

Canaanites and everyone in the land will hear, surround us, and erase our name from earth. 📖

What will You do for Your great Name?”

## Joshua 7:10

The LORD said, “Stand up! 📖

Why lie facedown?

## Joshua 7:11

Israel sinned and broke My commanded covenant. 📖

They took devoted things, stole, lied, and put them with their possessions.

**Help with these words:** A covenant is a solemn agreement.

The devoted things were set apart for destruction.

## Joshua 7:12

They cannot stand against enemies, but flee because they are set apart for destruction. 📖

I will no longer be with you unless you remove the devoted things.

## Joshua 7:13

Rise and consecrate the people. 📖

Say, ‘Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow.

The LORD, Israel’s God, says devoted things are among you.

You cannot stand against enemies until you remove them.

**Help with “consecrate”:** This means set yourselves apart for God.

## Joshua 7:14

In the morning, present yourselves tribe by tribe. 📖

The LORD’s selected tribe comes clan by clan, the selected clan family by family, the selected family man by man.

## Joshua 7:15

The one caught with devoted things must be burned with all belonging to him. 📖

He broke the LORD’s covenant and committed an outrage in Israel.’”

## Joshua 7:16

Early next morning, Joshua brought Israel tribe by tribe, and Judah was selected. 📖

## Joshua 7:17

Judah’s clans came, and the Zerahites were selected. 📖

They came, and Zabdi’s family was selected.

## Joshua 7:18

Zabdi’s family came man by man. 📖

Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from Judah, was selected.

## Joshua 7:19

Joshua told Achan, “My son, glorify the LORD, Israel’s God, and confess to Him. 📖

Tell me what you did; hide nothing.”

## Joshua 7:20

Achan replied, “Truly, I sinned against the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

Here is what I did:

## Joshua 7:21

I saw a beautiful Shinar cloak, two hundred silver shekels, and a gold bar weighing fifty shekels among the plunder. 📖

I coveted and took them.

They lie buried in my tent, silver underneath.”

**Help with these words:** Shekels measure weight.

Coveted means wrongly wanted to have.

## Joshua 7:22

Joshua’s messengers ran to the tent and found everything hidden, silver underneath. 📖

## Joshua 7:23

They brought it to Joshua and all Israel and spread it before the LORD. 📖

## Joshua 7:24

Joshua and all Israel took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, cloak, and gold bar to the Valley of Achor. 📖

They took his sons, daughters, oxen, donkeys, sheep, tent, and everything he owned.

## Joshua 7:25

Joshua said, “Why bring trouble on us? 📖

Today the LORD brings trouble on you!”

All Israel stoned him to death, then stoned the others and burned their bodies.

## Joshua 7:26

They piled many rocks over Achan, still there today. 📖

The LORD turned from burning anger.

The place is called the Valley of Achor to this day.
