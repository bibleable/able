# Joshua 19 — Six tribal inheritances and Joshua’s city

**About this chapter**

The chapter lists the remaining tribal allotments.

Every town, boundary, and stated total is retained.

Israel also gives Joshua the city he requested, and the land division is completed.

## Joshua 19:1

The second lot fell to Simeon’s clans, an inheritance within Judah, 📖

## Joshua 19:2

including Beersheba, or Sheba, Moladah, 📖

## Joshua 19:3

Hazar-shual, Balah, Ezem, 📖

## Joshua 19:4

Eltolad, Bethul, Hormah, 📖

## Joshua 19:5

Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susah, 📖

## Joshua 19:6

Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen: thirteen cities with their villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:7

Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan: four cities with their villages, 📖

## Joshua 19:8

and surrounding villages as far as Baalath-beer, Ramah of the Negev. 📖

This was Simeon’s tribal clans’ inheritance.

## Joshua 19:9

Simeon received within Judah because Judah’s portion was too large for its descendants. 📖

## Joshua 19:10

The third lot fell to Zebulun’s clans, whose border stretched to Sarid. 📖

## Joshua 19:11

It rose west to Maralah, reached Dabbesheth, and met the brook east of Jokneam. 📖

## Joshua 19:12

From Sarid it turned east along Chisloth-tabor’s border to Daberath and up to Japhia. 📖

## Joshua 19:13

It crossed east to Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin, reached Rimmon, and curved toward Neah. 📖

## Joshua 19:14

It curved around Neah’s north side to Hannathon, ending at the Valley of Iphtah-el. 📖

## Joshua 19:15

It included Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem: twelve cities with their villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:16

This was Zebulun’s tribal clans’ inheritance, these cities and villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:17

The fourth lot fell to Issachar’s clans, 📖

## Joshua 19:18

including Jezreel, Chesulloth, Shunem, 📖

## Joshua 19:19

Hapharaim, Shion, Anaharath, 📖

## Joshua 19:20

Rabbith, Kishion, Ebez, 📖

## Joshua 19:21

Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez. 📖

## Joshua 19:22

The border reached Tabor, Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh, ending at the Jordan. 📖

Sixteen cities with their villages.

## Joshua 19:23

This was Issachar’s tribal clans’ inheritance, these cities and villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:24

The fifth lot fell to Asher’s clans, 📖

## Joshua 19:25

including Helkath, Hali, Beten, Achshaph, 📖

## Joshua 19:26

Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal. 📖

Westward, the border touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath,

## Joshua 19:27

It turned east toward Beth-dagon, touching Zebulun and the Valley of Iphtah-el. 📖

It went north to Beth-emek and Neiel, passing Cabul on the left.

## Joshua 19:28

It reached Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, Kanah, and Greater Sidon. 📖

## Joshua 19:29

It turned back toward Ramah as far as fortified Tyre, then toward Hosah. 📖

It came out at the Sea in the Achzib region,

**Help with “fortified”:** This means protected by strong defenses.

## Joshua 19:30

Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob: twenty-two cities with their villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:31

This was Asher’s tribal clans’ inheritance, these cities and villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:32

The sixth lot fell to Naphtali’s clans, 📖

## Joshua 19:33

from Heleph and Zaanannim’s great tree, passing Adami-nekeb and Jabneel to Lakkum and the Jordan. 📖

## Joshua 19:34

It turned west to Aznoth-tabor and Hukkok, touching Zebulun southward, Asher westward, and Judah at the Jordan eastward. 📖

**About “Judah”:** This is the name BSB uses here.

The boundary description is retained without substituting another tribe.

## Joshua 19:35

The fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, Chinnereth, 📖

## Joshua 19:36

Adamah, Ramah, Hazor, 📖

## Joshua 19:37

Kedesh, Edrei, En-hazor, 📖

## Joshua 19:38

Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh: nineteen cities with their villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:39

This was Naphtali’s tribal clans’ inheritance, these cities and villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:40

The seventh lot fell to Dan’s clans, 📖

## Joshua 19:41

including Zorah, Eshtaol, Ir-shemesh, 📖

## Joshua 19:42

Shaalabbin, Aijalon, Ithlah, 📖

## Joshua 19:43

Elon, Timnah, Ekron, 📖

## Joshua 19:44

Eltekeh, Gibbethon, Baalath, 📖

## Joshua 19:45

Jehud, Bene-berak, Gath-rimmon, 📖

## Joshua 19:46

Me-jarkon, Rakkon, and territory opposite Joppa. 📖

## Joshua 19:47

Later, Dan lost its territory. 📖

They attacked Leshem, captured it, and put it to the sword.

They took possession of Leshem, settled there, and renamed it after their father Dan.

**Help with “put it to the sword”:** This describes killing with swords.

## Joshua 19:48

This was Dan’s tribal clans’ inheritance, these cities and villages. 📖

## Joshua 19:49

After dividing the land into territories, Israel gave Joshua son of Nun an inheritance among them, 📖

## Joshua 19:50

as the LORD commanded. 📖

They gave him his requested city, Timnath-serah in Ephraim’s hills.

He rebuilt and settled there.

## Joshua 19:51

Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and family heads distributed these inheritances by lot to Israel’s tribes. 📖

They did so at Shiloh before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance, finishing the division.
