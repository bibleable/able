# Joshua 18 — Seven portions and Benjamin’s inheritance

**About this chapter**

Israel gathers at Shiloh and sets up the Tent of Meeting.

Joshua sends men to survey the land and casts lots.

Benjamin’s boundaries and cities are listed.

## Joshua 18:1

All Israel assembled at Shiloh and set up the Tent of Meeting. 📖

The land was subdued before them,

**Help with “subdued”:** This means brought under control by force.

## Joshua 18:2

but seven tribes had not received their inheritance. 📖

## Joshua 18:3

Joshua asked Israel, “How long will you delay possessing the land the LORD, your fathers’ God, gave you? 📖

## Joshua 18:4

Choose three men per tribe. 📖

I will send them to survey and map the land according to each inheritance, then return to me

## Joshua 18:5

and divide it into seven portions. 📖

Judah stays in its southern territory, and Joseph’s house in its northern territory.

## Joshua 18:6

Bring me the map of seven portions, and I will cast lots here before the LORD our God. 📖

**Help with “lots”:** These were used to assign portions of land.

## Joshua 18:7

Levites have no portion: their inheritance is the LORD’s priesthood. 📖

Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh’s half-tribe already received their inheritance east beyond the Jordan.

Moses, the LORD’s servant, gave it to them.”

## Joshua 18:8

As the men left, Joshua commanded, “Survey and map the land, then return. 📖

Here at Shiloh I will cast lots before the LORD.”

## Joshua 18:9

They went throughout the land, mapping it city by city into seven portions. 📖

Then they returned with the document to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh.

## Joshua 18:10

Joshua cast lots before the LORD at Shiloh and divided the land according to Israel’s divisions. 📖

## Joshua 18:11

The first lot fell to Benjamin’s clans, territory between Judah and Joseph. 📖

## Joshua 18:12

The north border began at the Jordan, past Jericho’s northern slope, west through hills to Beth-aven’s wilderness. 📖

## Joshua 18:13

It crossed the southern slope of Luz, also called Bethel. 📖

It descended to Ataroth-addar on the hill south of Lower Beth-horon.

## Joshua 18:14

On the west, it curved south from the hill facing Beth-horon on the south to Kiriath-baal, Kiriath-jearim. 📖

This city of Judah marked the western side.

## Joshua 18:15

The south border began outside Kiriath-jearim, extending west to the spring at the Waters of Nephtoah. 📖

## Joshua 18:16

It descended to the hill’s foot facing the Valley of Ben-hinnom at the Valley of Rephaim’s northern end. 📖

It ran down Hinnom’s valley toward the Jebusites’ southern slope, then down to En-rogel.

## Joshua 18:17

It curved north to En-shemesh and Geliloth, opposite the Ascent of Adummim. 📖

It continued down to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben.

## Joshua 18:18

It reached Beth-arabah’s northern slope and descended into the valley. 📖

## Joshua 18:19

It reached Beth-hoglah’s northern slope and the Salt Sea’s northern bay at the Jordan’s mouth. 📖

This was the southern border.

## Joshua 18:20

The Jordan was the east border. 📖

These boundaries enclosed Benjamin’s clans’ inheritance.

## Joshua 18:21

Benjamin’s clans’ cities were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz, 📖

## Joshua 18:22

Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel, 📖

## Joshua 18:23

Avvim, Parah, Ophrah, 📖

## Joshua 18:24

Chephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba: twelve cities with their villages. 📖

## Joshua 18:25

Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth, 📖

## Joshua 18:26

Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah, 📖

## Joshua 18:27

Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah, 📖

## Joshua 18:28

Zelah, Haeleph, Jebus, also called Jerusalem, Gibeah, and Kiriath-jearim: fourteen cities with their villages. 📖

This was Benjamin’s tribal clans’ inheritance.
