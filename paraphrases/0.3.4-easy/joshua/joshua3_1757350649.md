# Joshua 3 — Crossing the Jordan

**About this chapter**

Joshua leads Israel from Shittim to the Jordan.

The ark goes ahead, and the priests stand in the river.

The account retains the distance, river conditions, place names, and dry crossing.

## Joshua 3:1

Early next morning Joshua left Shittim with all Israel. 📖

They camped at the Jordan before crossing.

## Joshua 3:2

After three days, officers went through camp 📖

## Joshua 3:3

They commanded, “When you see Levitical priests carrying the LORD your God’s covenant ark, leave your positions and follow it. 📖

**Help with these words:** Levitical priests belong to Levi’s tribe.

The ark is the sacred chest associated with God’s covenant, or solemn agreement.

## Joshua 3:4

Keep about two thousand cubits from the ark. 📖

Do not approach, so you can see the way, because you never traveled here before.”

**Help with “cubit”:** This is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Joshua 3:5

Joshua said, “Consecrate yourselves. 📖

Tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you.”

**Help with “consecrate”:** This means set yourselves apart for God.

## Joshua 3:6

He told the priests, “Carry the covenant ark ahead of the people.” 📖

They carried it ahead.

## Joshua 3:7

The LORD told Joshua, “Today I begin exalting you before all Israel. 📖

They will know I am with you as with Moses.

## Joshua 3:8

Tell the ark-carrying priests, ‘At the water’s edge, stand in the Jordan.’” 📖

## Joshua 3:9

Joshua told Israel, “Come hear the LORD your God’s words.” 📖

## Joshua 3:10

He said, “This shows the living God is among you and will surely drive these peoples out before you. 📖

They are Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites.

## Joshua 3:11

The covenant ark of the Lord of all the earth goes ahead into the Jordan. 📖

## Joshua 3:12

Choose twelve men from Israel’s tribes, one per tribe. 📖

## Joshua 3:13

The priests carry the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth. 📖

When their feet touch Jordan’s water, its flowing waters will stop and stand in a heap.”

## Joshua 3:14

The people left camp to cross the Jordan, with priests carrying the covenant ark ahead. 📖

## Joshua 3:15

The Jordan overflows its banks all harvest season. 📖

The priests reached it, their feet touching the water’s edge,

## Joshua 3:16

and the flowing water stood still. 📖

It backed up as far upstream as Adam, a city in Zarethan’s area.

Water toward the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, was completely cut off.

The people crossed opposite Jericho.

## Joshua 3:17

The priests carrying the LORD’s covenant ark stood firmly on dry ground in the Jordan’s middle. 📖

All Israel crossed on dry ground until the whole nation had crossed.
