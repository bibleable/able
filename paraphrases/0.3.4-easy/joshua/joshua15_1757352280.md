# Joshua 15 — Judah’s boundaries, Caleb’s family, and towns

**About this chapter**

Judah receives its tribal allotment.

The chapter describes boundaries, Caleb’s family, and every town list.

The stated totals follow BSB, and the Jebusites’ continued presence is retained.

## Joshua 15:1

Judah’s clans received land to Edom’s border, to Zin’s wilderness at the far south. 📖

## Joshua 15:2

The southern border began at the bay on the Salt Sea’s southern tip, 📖

## Joshua 15:3

It passed south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, continued to Zin, and went south of Kadesh-barnea. 📖

It ran past Hezron up to Addar and curved toward Karka.

## Joshua 15:4

It reached Azmon, joined the Brook of Egypt, and ended at the Sea. 📖

That was the southern border.

## Joshua 15:5

The east border was the Salt Sea to the Jordan’s mouth. 📖

The north border began at the sea bay at the Jordan’s mouth,

## Joshua 15:6

went up to Beth-hoglah, north of Beth-arabah, then up to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben. 📖

## Joshua 15:7

It rose from Achor’s valley to Debir, turning north to Gilgal opposite Adummim’s Ascent south of the ravine. 📖

It followed En-shemesh’s waters to En-rogel.

## Joshua 15:8

It climbed the Valley of Ben-hinnom along Jerusalem’s southern Jebusite slope. 📖

It reached the hilltop facing Hinnom’s valley on the west, at the Valley of Rephaim’s northern end.

## Joshua 15:9

It curved from the hilltop to the spring of the Waters of Nephtoah, then Mount Ephron’s cities. 📖

It bent toward Baalah, Kiriath-jearim.

## Joshua 15:10

It curved west from Baalah to Mount Seir, along Mount Jearim’s northern slope, Chesalon. 📖

It descended to Beth-shemesh and crossed to Timnah.

## Joshua 15:11

It reached Ekron’s north slope, curved to Shikkeron, continued to Mount Baalah and Jabneel, and ended at the Sea. 📖

## Joshua 15:12

The western border was the Great Sea’s coast. 📖

These boundaries enclosed Judah’s descendant clans.

## Joshua 15:13

As the LORD commanded Joshua, he gave Caleb son of Jephunneh Kiriath-arba, Hebron, among Judah. 📖

Arba was Anak’s forefather.

## Joshua 15:14

Caleb drove out Anak’s three sons, descendants of Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, Anak’s children. 📖

## Joshua 15:15

He marched against Debir’s people. 📖

Debir was formerly Kiriath-sepher.

## Joshua 15:16

Caleb said, “I will give my daughter Acsah as wife to whoever defeats and captures Kiriath-sepher.” 📖

## Joshua 15:17

Othniel, son of Caleb’s brother Kenaz, captured it. 📖

Caleb gave him Acsah as wife.

## Joshua 15:18

One day Acsah came to Othniel and urged him to ask her father for a field. 📖

She dismounted her donkey, and Caleb asked, “What do you want?”

## Joshua 15:19

She replied, “Give me a blessing. 📖

You gave me Negev land; give water springs too.”

Caleb gave upper and lower springs.

## Joshua 15:20

This is Judah’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖

## Joshua 15:21

Judah’s southernmost Negev cities toward Edom’s border were Kabzeel, Eder, Jagur, 📖

## Joshua 15:22

Kinah, Dimonah, Adadah, 📖

## Joshua 15:23

Kedesh, Hazor, Ithnan, 📖

## Joshua 15:24

Ziph, Telem, Bealoth, 📖

## Joshua 15:25

Hazor-hadattah, Kerioth-hezron, also called Hazor, 📖

## Joshua 15:26

Amam, Shema, Moladah, 📖

## Joshua 15:27

Hazar-gaddah, Heshmon, Beth-pelet, 📖

## Joshua 15:28

Hazar-shual, Beersheba, Biziothiah, 📖

## Joshua 15:29

Baalah, Iim, Ezem, 📖

## Joshua 15:30

Eltolad, Chesil, Hormah, 📖

## Joshua 15:31

Ziklag, Madmannah, Sansannah, 📖

## Joshua 15:32

Lebaoth, Shilhim, Ain, and Rimmon: twenty-nine cities with their villages. 📖

**About the stated total:** The list has thirty-six name entries when the explicit alternate name Hazor is not counted again.

BSB states twenty-nine cities.

Both the names and stated total remain for further editorial review.
