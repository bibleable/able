# Joshua 10 — Gibeon rescued and southern cities conquered

**About this chapter**

Five kings attack Gibeon, and Joshua comes to help.

The chapter includes hailstones, the sun and moon standing still, and the kings’ executions.

Each conquest and its reported destruction is retained.

## Joshua 10:1

Jerusalem’s king Adoni-zedek heard Joshua captured and destroyed Ai and its king, as Jericho and its king. 📖

He heard Gibeon made peace with Israel and lived nearby.

## Joshua 10:2

He and his people greatly feared: Gibeon was a great city like a royal city, larger than Ai. 📖

All its men were mighty.

## Joshua 10:3

Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem sent word to Hoham king of Hebron and Piram king of Jarmuth. 📖

He also sent to Japhia king of Lachish and Debir king of Eglon, saying,

## Joshua 10:4

“Come up and help me. 📖

We will attack Gibeon because they made peace with Joshua and Israel.”

## Joshua 10:5

The five Amorite kings united all armies: Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon. 📖

They camped before Gibeon and fought.

## Joshua 10:6

Gibeon sent Joshua at Gilgal, “Do not abandon your servants! 📖

Come quickly, save and help us!

All the Amorite hill-country kings united against us.”

## Joshua 10:7

Joshua came from Gilgal with the whole army, all his mighty fighting men. 📖

## Joshua 10:8

The LORD said, “Do not fear. 📖

I handed them over; none will stand against you.”

## Joshua 10:9

Joshua marched all night from Gilgal and surprised them. 📖

## Joshua 10:10

The LORD confused them before Israel, who slaughtered them at Gibeon. 📖

Israel pursued up Beth-horon’s ascent and struck them to Azekah and Makkedah.

## Joshua 10:11

While they fled down from Beth-horon to Azekah, the LORD sent large hailstones from the sky. 📖

More died from hailstones than Israel’s swords.

## Joshua 10:12

That day, when the LORD handed the Amorites to Israel, Joshua spoke to Him before Israel. 📖

“Sun, stand still over Gibeon!

Moon, over the Valley of Aijalon!”

## Joshua 10:13

The sun stood and moon stopped until the nation avenged itself. 📖

Is this not written in the Book of Jashar?

“The sun stopped in mid-sky, delaying sunset about a full day.”

## Joshua 10:14

No day before or since was like it, when the LORD heard a man’s voice. 📖

He did this because He fought for Israel.

## Joshua 10:15

Joshua returned with all Israel to Gilgal’s camp. 📖

## Joshua 10:16

The five kings fled into Makkedah’s cave. 📖

## Joshua 10:17

Joshua heard, “The five kings are hiding in Makkedah’s cave.” 📖

## Joshua 10:18

He said, “Roll large stones over its mouth and post guards. 📖

## Joshua 10:19

But keep chasing the enemy and attack from behind. 📖

Stop them reaching their cities, because the LORD your God handed them over.”

## Joshua 10:20

Joshua and Israel slaughtered them until finished. 📖

The remaining survivors escaped to fortified cities.

## Joshua 10:21

The whole army returned safely to Joshua at Makkedah’s camp. 📖

No one dared speak against Israel.

## Joshua 10:22

Joshua commanded, “Open the cave and bring the five kings.” 📖

## Joshua 10:23

They brought the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon from the cave. 📖

## Joshua 10:24

Joshua called all Israel’s men and told the commanders who came with him, “Put your feet on these kings’ necks.” 📖

They did so.

## Joshua 10:25

Joshua said, “Do not fear or lose courage. 📖

Be strong and courageous.

The LORD will do this to every enemy you fight.”

## Joshua 10:26

Joshua struck and killed the kings, hanging their bodies on five trees until evening. 📖

## Joshua 10:27

At sunset he ordered them taken down and thrown in their hiding cave. 📖

Large stones sealed its mouth and remain today.

## Joshua 10:28

That day he captured Makkedah, killing its king and all its people by sword, devoted to destruction. 📖

None survived.

He treated its king like Jericho’s.

## Joshua 10:29

Joshua and all Israel went from Makkedah to Libnah and fought it. 📖

## Joshua 10:30

The LORD handed over Libnah and its king. 📖

Joshua killed all its people by sword, none surviving, and treated its king like Jericho’s.

## Joshua 10:31

Joshua and all Israel went from Libnah to Lachish, besieging and fighting it. 📖

## Joshua 10:32

The LORD handed over Lachish. 📖

Joshua captured it on day two and killed all its people by sword, as Libnah.

## Joshua 10:33

Horam king of Gezer came to help Lachish. 📖

Joshua killed him and his people, leaving no survivor.

## Joshua 10:34

Joshua and all Israel went from Lachish to Eglon, besieging and fighting it. 📖

## Joshua 10:35

They captured Eglon that day, killing by sword and devoting everyone to destruction, as Lachish. 📖

## Joshua 10:36

Joshua and all Israel went up from Eglon to Hebron and fought. 📖

## Joshua 10:37

They captured Hebron, killing its king, all villages, and everyone by sword. 📖

As at Eglon, none survived: Joshua devoted Hebron and everyone to destruction.

## Joshua 10:38

Joshua and all Israel turned toward Debir and fought. 📖

## Joshua 10:39

They captured Debir, its king, and all villages, killing by sword and devoting all inhabitants to destruction. 📖

None survived.

He treated Debir and its king like Hebron and like Libnah and its king.

## Joshua 10:40

Joshua conquered the hills, Negev, foothills, slopes, and all their kings, leaving no survivors. 📖

He devoted everything breathing to destruction as the LORD, Israel’s God, commanded.

**Help with “devoted to destruction”:** This means set apart for complete destruction.

## Joshua 10:41

He conquered from Kadesh-barnea to Gaza, and all Goshen’s region to Gibeon. 📖

## Joshua 10:42

He captured all these kings and their land in one campaign because the LORD, Israel’s God, fought for Israel. 📖

## Joshua 10:43

Joshua returned with all Israel to Gilgal’s camp. 📖
