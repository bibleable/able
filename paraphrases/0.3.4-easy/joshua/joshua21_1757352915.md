# Joshua 21 — The Levites’ cities

**About this chapter**

The Levites ask for the cities and pastureland commanded through Moses.

Every city, tribe, repeated pastureland provision, and stated total is retained.

The chapter closes with the fulfillment of the LORD’s promises.

## Joshua 21:1

Levi’s family heads approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and Israel’s other tribal heads 📖

## Joshua 21:2

at Shiloh in Canaan: “The LORD commanded through Moses that we receive cities to live in and pastureland for livestock.” 📖

## Joshua 21:3

At His command, Israel gave Levites these cities and pasturelands from their own inheritance: 📖

## Joshua 21:4

The first lot fell to Kohath’s clans. 📖

Aaron the priest’s Levite descendants received thirteen cities by lot from Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin.

## Joshua 21:5

Kohath’s remaining descendants received ten cities by lot from Ephraim, Dan, and Manasseh’s half-tribe. 📖

## Joshua 21:6

Gershon’s descendants received thirteen cities by lot from Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Manasseh’s half-tribe in Bashan. 📖

## Joshua 21:7

Merari’s descendants received twelve cities from Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun. 📖

## Joshua 21:8

Israel assigned these cities and pastureland to Levites as the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖

## Joshua 21:9

Judah and Simeon gave these named cities 📖

## Joshua 21:10

to Aaron’s descendants from Levi’s Kohathite clans, because theirs was the first lot: 📖

## Joshua 21:11

Kiriath-arba, Hebron, in Judah’s hills, with surrounding pastureland. 📖

Arba was Anak’s father.

## Joshua 21:12

But the surrounding fields and villages belonged to Caleb son of Jephunneh. 📖

## Joshua 21:13

Aaron the priest’s descendants received Hebron, refuge for someone killing another, and Libnah, with pasturelands, 📖

**About refuge:** Chapter 20 explains who qualifies for this protection.

## Joshua 21:14

Jattir, Eshtemoa, 📖

## Joshua 21:15

Holon, Debir, 📖

## Joshua 21:16

Ain, Juttah, and Beth-shemesh: nine cities from these two tribes, with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:17

Benjamin gave Gibeon, Geba, 📖

## Joshua 21:18

Anathoth, and Almon: four cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:19

Aaron’s priestly descendants received thirteen cities altogether, with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:20

Levi’s other Kohathite clans received these cities from Ephraim: 📖

## Joshua 21:21

Shechem in Ephraim’s hills, refuge for someone killing another, Gezer, 📖

## Joshua 21:22

Kibzaim, and Beth-horon: four cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:23

Dan gave Elteke, Gibbethon, 📖

## Joshua 21:24

Aijalon, and Gath-rimmon: four cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:25

Manasseh’s half-tribe gave Taanach and Gath-rimmon: two cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:26

Kohath’s other clans received ten cities altogether, with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:27

Levi’s Gershonite clans received Golan in Bashan, refuge for someone killing another, and Beeshterah from Manasseh’s half-tribe. 📖

Two cities with pasturelands.

## Joshua 21:28

Issachar gave Kishion, Daberath, 📖

## Joshua 21:29

Jarmuth, and En-gannim: four cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:30

Asher gave Mishal, Abdon, 📖

## Joshua 21:31

Helkath, and Rehob: four cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:32

Naphtali gave Kedesh in Galilee, refuge for someone killing another, Hammoth-dor, and Kartan: three cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:33

Gershon’s clans received thirteen cities altogether, with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:34

Merari’s clan, the remaining Levites, received Jokneam and Kartah from Zebulun, 📖

## Joshua 21:35

Dimnah, and Nahalal: four cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:36

Reuben gave Bezer, Jahaz, 📖

## Joshua 21:37

Kedemoth, and Mephaath: four cities with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:38

Gad gave Ramoth in Gilead, refuge for someone killing another, Mahanaim, 📖

## Joshua 21:39

Heshbon, and Jazer: four cities altogether, with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:40

Merari’s remaining Levite clans received twelve cities altogether. 📖

## Joshua 21:41

Levites received forty-eight cities altogether within Israel’s territory, with pasturelands. 📖

## Joshua 21:42

Every city had its own surrounding pasturelands. 📖

This was true of all cities.

## Joshua 21:43

The LORD gave Israel all land sworn to their fathers. 📖

They possessed and settled it.

## Joshua 21:44

He gave rest on every side as sworn to their fathers. 📖

No enemy could stand against them because He handed all enemies over.

## Joshua 21:45

Not one good promise He made to Israel’s house failed. 📖

Everything was fulfilled.
