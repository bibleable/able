# Joshua 8 — Ai’s destruction and the reading of the law

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Joshua a plan for Ai.

The account retains the troop numbers, ambush, destruction, and execution of the king.

Joshua then builds an altar and reads the whole law to the assembly.

## Joshua 8:1

The LORD told Joshua, “Do not fear or lose courage. 📖

Take the whole army and attack Ai.

I handed over its king, people, city, and land.

## Joshua 8:2

Do to Ai and its king as to Jericho and its king, but take plunder and livestock for yourselves. 📖

Set an ambush behind the city.”

**Help with “ambush”:** This is a hidden force waiting to attack.

## Joshua 8:3

Joshua and the whole army set out against Ai. 📖

He chose 30,000 mighty fighting men and sent them at night

## Joshua 8:4

with orders: “Listen. 📖

Hide behind the city, not too far away, all ready.

## Joshua 8:5

I and my troops will advance. 📖

When they attack as before, we will flee.

## Joshua 8:6

They will follow until drawn away, thinking, ‘Israel flees as before.’ 📖

While we flee,

## Joshua 8:7

rise from hiding and seize the city, because the LORD your God will hand it over. 📖

## Joshua 8:8

After capture, burn it. 📖

Do as the LORD commands!

These are my orders.”

## Joshua 8:9

They waited in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai. 📖

Joshua stayed among the people that night.

## Joshua 8:10

Early next morning, he gathered his men. 📖

He and Israel’s elders marched ahead up to Ai.

## Joshua 8:11

All his troops approached and camped north of Ai, with the valley between them and the city. 📖

## Joshua 8:12

Joshua had set about five thousand men in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the city. 📖

**About the numbers:** Verse 3 gives 30,000 chosen men.

This verse gives about five thousand in ambush.

Both figures remain without supplying an explanation of how the groups relate.

## Joshua 8:13

The main camp was north and rear guard west of the city. 📖

That night Joshua entered the valley.

## Joshua 8:14

Ai’s king saw Israel and hurried out early with the city’s men to fight at an appointed place. 📖

The place overlooked the Arabah.

He knew nothing of the ambush behind the city.

## Joshua 8:15

Joshua and all Israel let themselves be driven back and fled toward the wilderness. 📖

## Joshua 8:16

All Ai’s men were called to pursue. 📖

They followed Joshua and were drawn away.

## Joshua 8:17

Every man in Ai and Bethel pursued Israel, leaving the city wide open. 📖

## Joshua 8:18

The LORD told Joshua, “Hold your battle lance toward Ai. 📖

I will hand you the city.”

He held it toward Ai,

**Help with “lance”:** This is a long weapon like a spear.

## Joshua 8:19

and immediately the ambushers rose, rushed forward, entered, and captured the city, setting fire to it at once. 📖

## Joshua 8:20

Ai’s men looked back and saw smoke rising to the sky. 📖

They could escape nowhere, and the fleeing troops turned against their pursuers.

## Joshua 8:21

Joshua and all Israel saw the city captured and smoke rising. 📖

They turned and struck down Ai’s men.

## Joshua 8:22

The ambushers came from the city, trapping Ai’s men between Israel’s forces. 📖

Israel struck them until no survivor or fugitive remained.

## Joshua 8:23

They captured Ai’s king alive and brought him to Joshua. 📖

## Joshua 8:24

Israel killed every man of Ai who pursued them into field and wilderness. 📖

When every last one fell by sword, all Israel returned to Ai and put it to the sword.

## Joshua 8:25

Twelve thousand men and women died that day, all Ai’s people. 📖

## Joshua 8:26

Joshua kept his lance-holding hand stretched out until he devoted all Ai’s inhabitants to destruction. 📖

**Help with “devoted to destruction”:** This means set apart for complete destruction.

## Joshua 8:27

Israel took only the city’s cattle and plunder for themselves, as the LORD commanded Joshua. 📖

## Joshua 8:28

Joshua burned Ai, making it a permanent ruin, desolate to this day. 📖

## Joshua 8:29

He hung Ai’s king on a tree until evening. 📖

At sunset he ordered the body taken down and thrown at the city gate’s entrance.

They piled rocks over it, still there today.

## Joshua 8:30

Then Joshua built the LORD, Israel’s God, an altar on Mount Ebal, 📖

## Joshua 8:31

as Moses, His servant, commanded Israel. 📖

It matched the Book of the Law of Moses: uncut stones untouched by iron tools.

They offered burnt and peace offerings to the LORD.

## Joshua 8:32

Before Israel, Joshua inscribed on the stones a copy of Moses’ written law. 📖

## Joshua 8:33

All Israel, foreigners and citizens, elders, officers, and judges, stood on both sides of the LORD’s covenant ark. 📖

They faced the Levitical priests carrying it.

Half stood before Mount Gerizim, half before Mount Ebal, as Moses, the LORD’s servant, commanded earlier to bless Israel.

## Joshua 8:34

Joshua then read all the law’s words, blessings and curses, according to everything written in the Book of the Law. 📖

## Joshua 8:35

Joshua omitted no word of all Moses commanded before Israel’s whole assembly. 📖

This included women, little ones, and the foreigners living among them.
