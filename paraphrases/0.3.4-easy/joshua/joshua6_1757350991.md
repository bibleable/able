# Joshua 6 — Jericho falls and Rahab is spared

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Joshua instructions for marching around Jericho.

The chapter describes the city’s destruction and the rescue of Rahab’s household.

The killing, destruction rules, and rebuilding curse remain explicit.

## Joshua 6:1

Jericho was tightly shut because of Israel. 📖

No one entered or left.

## Joshua 6:2

The LORD told Joshua, “I handed over Jericho, its king, and its mighty fighting men. 📖

## Joshua 6:3

March all the men of war around the city once each day for six days. 📖

## Joshua 6:4

Seven priests must carry seven rams’ horns before the ark. 📖

On day seven, circle the city seven times while they blow horns.

## Joshua 6:5

When you hear a long ram’s-horn blast, everyone must shout loudly. 📖

The wall will collapse, and all your people will charge straight in.”

## Joshua 6:6

Joshua son of Nun called the priests. 📖

“Carry the covenant ark, with seven priests carrying seven rams’ horns before the LORD’s ark.”

## Joshua 6:7

He told the people, “Advance around the city, armed troops ahead of the LORD’s ark.” 📖

## Joshua 6:8

After Joshua spoke, seven priests advanced before the LORD, carrying and blowing seven rams’ horns. 📖

The LORD’s covenant ark followed.

## Joshua 6:9

Armed troops went ahead of the horn-blowing priests, and the rear guard followed the ark. 📖

The horns kept sounding.

## Joshua 6:10

Joshua commanded, “No battle cry, voice, or single word until the day I command a shout. 📖

Then shout!”

## Joshua 6:11

He had the LORD’s ark carried around once, then the people returned and spent the night in camp. 📖

## Joshua 6:12

Early next morning, Joshua rose, and the priests took the LORD’s ark. 📖

## Joshua 6:13

Seven priests with seven rams’ horns kept marching and blowing before the LORD’s ark. 📖

Armed troops went in front, rear guard behind the ark, and horns kept sounding.

## Joshua 6:14

They circled once on day two and returned to camp. 📖

They did this six days.

## Joshua 6:15

On day seven, they rose at dawn and circled seven times the same way. 📖

Only that day did they circle seven times.

## Joshua 6:16

After the seventh circuit, the priests blew horns. 📖

Joshua commanded, “Shout!

The LORD gave you the city!

## Joshua 6:17

The city and everything in it must be devoted to the LORD for destruction. 📖

Only Rahab the prostitute and those in her house will live, because she hid our spies.

**Help with “devoted for destruction”:** This means set apart to be completely destroyed.

## Joshua 6:18

Avoid devoted things, lest you too be set apart for destruction. 📖

Taking them makes Israel’s camp devoted to destruction and brings disaster on it.

## Joshua 6:19

All silver, gold, bronze, and iron articles are holy to the LORD and must go to His treasury.” 📖

## Joshua 6:20

The people shouted when the rams’ horns sounded. 📖

They heard the blast, shouted mightily, and the wall collapsed.

Everyone charged straight in and captured the city.

## Joshua 6:21

With swords they devoted everything to destruction: men, women, young, old, oxen, sheep, and donkeys. 📖

## Joshua 6:22

Joshua told the two spies, “Enter the prostitute’s house and bring her and everyone with her out, as you promised.” 📖

## Joshua 6:23

The young spies brought out Rahab, her father, mother, brothers, and everyone belonging to her. 📖

They brought her whole family to settle outside Israel’s camp.

## Joshua 6:24

Israel burned the city and everything in it. 📖

Silver, gold, bronze, and iron articles went into the LORD’s house treasury.

## Joshua 6:25

Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute, her father’s household, and all belonging to her because she hid his Jericho spies. 📖

She lives among Israel to this day.

## Joshua 6:26

Joshua swore, “Cursed before the LORD is anyone rebuilding Jericho. 📖

At his firstborn’s cost he lays foundations; at his youngest’s cost he sets gates.”

**About this curse:** The wording links rebuilding with the loss of his firstborn and youngest.

## Joshua 6:27

The LORD was with Joshua, and his fame spread through all the land. 📖
