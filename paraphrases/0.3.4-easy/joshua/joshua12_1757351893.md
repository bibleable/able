# Joshua 12 — The conquered kings

**About this chapter**

The account lists kings defeated east and west of the Jordan.

It describes their territories and the land given to Israel’s tribes.

Each king and each count is retained.

## Joshua 12:1

Israel defeated these kings east beyond the Jordan and took their lands. 📖

The land stretched from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon, including all Arabah eastward:

## Joshua 12:2

Sihon, Amorite king at Heshbon, ruled from Aroer on Arnon Valley’s rim through the valley’s middle to the Jabbok River. 📖

This was the Ammonite border, half of Gilead,

## Joshua 12:3

with Arabah east of the Sea of Chinnereth to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea. 📖

It extended east through Beth-jeshimoth and south below Pisgah’s slopes.

## Joshua 12:4

Og king of Bashan was one of the remaining Rephaim. 📖

He lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei.

## Joshua 12:5

He ruled Mount Hermon, Salecah, and all Bashan to the Geshurite and Maacathite border. 📖

He ruled half Gilead to Sihon king of Heshbon’s border.

## Joshua 12:6

Moses, the LORD’s servant, and Israel defeated them. 📖

Their land became Reuben’s, Gad’s, and Manasseh’s half-tribe’s inheritance.

## Joshua 12:7

Joshua and Israel defeated these kings west beyond the Jordan. 📖

The land ran from Baal-gad in Lebanon’s valley to Mount Halak toward Seir.

Joshua gave their lands as Israel’s tribal inheritances,

## Joshua 12:8

in the hills, foothills, Arabah, slopes, wilderness, and Negev. 📖

These were Hittite, Amorite, Canaanite, Perizzite, Hivite, and Jebusite lands:

## Joshua 12:9

Jericho’s king, one. 📖

Ai’s king near Bethel, one.

## Joshua 12:10

Jerusalem’s king, one. 📖

Hebron’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:11

Jarmuth’s king, one. 📖

Lachish’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:12

Eglon’s king, one. 📖

Gezer’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:13

Debir’s king, one. 📖

Geder’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:14

Hormah’s king, one. 📖

Arad’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:15

Libnah’s king, one. 📖

Adullam’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:16

Makkedah’s king, one. 📖

Bethel’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:17

Tappuah’s king, one. 📖

Hepher’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:18

Aphek’s king, one. 📖

Lasharon’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:19

Madon’s king, one. 📖

Hazor’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:20

Shimron-meron’s king, one. 📖

Achshaph’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:21

Taanach’s king, one. 📖

Megiddo’s king, one.

## Joshua 12:22

Kedesh’s king, one. 📖

Jokneam’s king in Carmel, one.

## Joshua 12:23

Dor’s king in Naphath-dor, one. 📖

Goiim’s king in Gilgal, one.

## Joshua 12:24

Tirzah’s king, one. 📖

Thirty-one kings altogether.
