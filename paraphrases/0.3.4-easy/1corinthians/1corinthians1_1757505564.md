# 1 Corinthians 1 — Christ and the divided church

**About this chapter**

Paul and Sosthenes greet the church in Corinth.

Paul addresses divisions and explains God's wisdom in Christ crucified.

## 1 Corinthians 1:1

This letter is from Paul, called by God's will to be an apostle of Christ Jesus, and our brother Sosthenes. 📖

**Help:** An apostle is someone sent to carry Christ's message.

## 1 Corinthians 1:2

To God's church in Corinth, made holy in Christ Jesus and called to be holy. 📖

This also includes everyone everywhere who calls on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, their Lord and ours.

**Help:** Holy means set apart for God.

## 1 Corinthians 1:3

May kindness and peace come to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:4

I always thank my God for you because of the kindness he gave you in Christ Jesus. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:5

In him, you have been made rich in every way, in all speech and all knowledge, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:6

because our witness about Christ was confirmed in you. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:7

So you lack no spiritual gift as you eagerly wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to be revealed. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:8

He will keep you strong to the end. 📖

Then you will be without blame on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 1:9

God is faithful. 📖

He called you into shared life with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 1:10

Brothers and sisters, I appeal to you in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ: agree with one another. 📖

Let there be no divisions among you.

Be united in your thinking and beliefs.

## 1 Corinthians 1:11

My brothers and sisters, some people from Chloe's household have told me about quarrels among you. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:12

I mean that people among you say, “I follow Paul,” “I follow Apollos,” “I follow Cephas,” or “I follow Christ.” 📖

**Help:** Cephas is another name for Peter.

## 1 Corinthians 1:13

Is Christ divided? 📖

Was Paul crucified for you?

Were you baptized into Paul's name?

**Help:** Crucified means killed on a cross.

Baptism uses water as a sign of belonging to Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 1:14

I thank God I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:15

so no one can say you were baptized into my name. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:16

Yes, I also baptized Stephanas's household. 📖

Beyond that, I do not remember whether I baptized anyone else.

## 1 Corinthians 1:17

Christ sent me to preach the good news, rather than to baptize. 📖

I must not use wise-sounding words that would empty Christ's cross of its power.

## 1 Corinthians 1:18

The message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing. 📖

But to us who are being saved, it is God's power.

## 1 Corinthians 1:19

The Scriptures say, “I will destroy the wisdom of the wise. 📖

I will defeat the understanding of the intelligent.”

## 1 Corinthians 1:20

Where is the wise person? 📖

Where is the expert in the written law?

Where is this age's thinker?

Has God not made the world's wisdom foolish?

## 1 Corinthians 1:21

In God's wisdom, the world did not know him through its wisdom. 📖

God was pleased to save believers through the foolishness of what was preached.

## 1 Corinthians 1:22

Jews demand signs, and Greeks look for wisdom. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:23

But we preach Christ crucified. 📖

This is a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to Gentiles.

**Help:** A stumbling block pictures an obstacle to accepting the message.

Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## 1 Corinthians 1:24

But for those God calls, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is God's power and God's wisdom. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:25

God's foolishness is wiser than human wisdom. 📖

God's weakness is stronger than human strength.

**Help:** Paul uses these comparisons to challenge human judgments about God's work through the cross.

## 1 Corinthians 1:26

Brothers and sisters, think about when God called you. 📖

Not many of you were wise by human standards.

Not many were powerful or born into important families.

## 1 Corinthians 1:27

But God chose the world's foolish things to shame the wise. 📖

God chose the world's weak things to shame the strong.

## 1 Corinthians 1:28

He chose the world's lowly and despised things, and things that are not, to bring existing things to nothing. 📖

**Help:** Paul contrasts what the world values with what it treats as worthless.

## 1 Corinthians 1:29

He did this so no one could boast in his presence. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 1:30

Because of God, you are in Christ Jesus. 📖

Christ has become wisdom from God for us: our righteousness, holiness, and redemption.

**Help:** Righteousness means being right before God.

Holiness means being set apart for God.

Redemption means being set free at a cost.

## 1 Corinthians 1:31

So, as the Scriptures say, “Anyone who boasts should boast in the Lord.” 📖
