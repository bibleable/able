# 1 Corinthians 15 — Christ raised and the resurrection

**About this chapter**

Paul reminds the church of the good news and Christ's appearances after rising from death.

He explains why resurrection matters and describes the change from dying bodies to bodies that cannot die.

## 1 Corinthians 15:1

Brothers and sisters, I remind you of the good news I preached to you. 📖

You received it, and you stand firmly in it.

## 1 Corinthians 15:2

This good news saves you if you hold firmly to the message I preached. 📖

Otherwise, you believed for nothing.

## 1 Corinthians 15:3

I passed on what I received as most important: Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:4

he was buried, and he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:5

He appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve. 📖

**Help:** Cephas is another name for Peter.

The Twelve is the name used for Jesus' group of apostles.

## 1 Corinthians 15:6

After that, he appeared to more than five hundred believers at once. 📖

Most are still living, though some have fallen asleep.

**Help:** Fallen asleep here means died.

## 1 Corinthians 15:7

Then he appeared to James, and then to all the apostles. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:8

Last of all, he also appeared to me, as to someone born at the wrong time. 📖

**About this picture:** Paul compares his own unusual calling to an untimely birth.

He explains his unworthiness and God's kindness in the next verses.

## 1 Corinthians 15:9

I am the least of the apostles, unworthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted God's church. 📖

**Help:** Persecuted means treated people cruelly because of their faith.

## 1 Corinthians 15:10

But by God's kindness, I am what I am, and his kindness to me was not wasted. 📖

I worked harder than all of them.

Yet it was God's kindness with me, rather than I myself.

## 1 Corinthians 15:11

Whether it was I or they, this is what we preach and what you believed. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:12

If we preach that Christ was raised from death, how can some of you say the dead are not raised? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:13

If there is no resurrection of the dead, then even Christ has not been raised. 📖

**Help:** Resurrection means being raised from death to life.

## 1 Corinthians 15:14

If Christ has not been raised, our preaching is worthless, and so is your faith. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:15

Then we are also shown to be false witnesses about God. 📖

We testified that God raised Christ.

But he did not raise Christ if the dead really are not raised.

## 1 Corinthians 15:16

If the dead are not raised, then even Christ has not been raised. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:17

If Christ has not been raised, your faith achieves nothing, and you are still in your sins. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:18

Then those who have fallen asleep in Christ have also perished. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:19

If we hope in Christ only for this life, we deserve pity more than anyone. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:20

But Christ has truly been raised from death, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep. 📖

**Help:** Firstfruits means the first part of a harvest.

Here Christ's resurrection is the first part, pointing to the resurrection of those who belong to him.

## 1 Corinthians 15:21

Death came through a man, and the resurrection of the dead also comes through a man. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:22

Just as all die in Adam, all will be made alive in Christ. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:23

But each comes in their own turn: Christ the firstfruits, then those who belong to him when he comes. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:24

Then the end comes. 📖

Christ hands the kingdom to God the Father after destroying every rule, authority, and power.

## 1 Corinthians 15:25

Christ must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet. 📖

**Help:** Under his feet pictures being placed under his rule.

## 1 Corinthians 15:26

The last enemy to be destroyed is death. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:27

“God has put everything under his feet.” 📖

When it says everything is under Christ, this clearly excludes God, who put everything under him.

## 1 Corinthians 15:28

When everything is under the Son, the Son himself will be under God, who put everything under him. 📖

Then God may be all in all.

## 1 Corinthians 15:29

If these things are not so, what will those baptized for the dead do? 📖

If the dead are not raised at all, why are people baptized for them?

**About this difficult verse:** Paul refers to people being baptized for the dead.

The practice and his use of it need further editorial review.

This draft does not replace the question with a new instruction.

## 1 Corinthians 15:30

And why do we face danger every hour? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:31

Brothers and sisters, I face death daily, as surely as I boast about you in Christ Jesus our Lord. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:32

If I fought wild beasts in Ephesus for human reasons, what did I gain? 📖

If the dead are not raised, “Let us eat and drink, because tomorrow we die.”

## 1 Corinthians 15:33

Do not be deceived: “Bad company ruins good character.” 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:34

Come to your senses as you should, and stop sinning. 📖

Some of you do not know God.

I say this to shame you.

## 1 Corinthians 15:35

Someone will ask, “How are the dead raised? 📖

What kind of body will they have?”

## 1 Corinthians 15:36

You fool! 📖

What you plant does not come to life unless it dies.

## 1 Corinthians 15:37

You do not plant the body that will grow, but only a seed, perhaps wheat or something else. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:38

God gives it the body he has planned. 📖

He gives each kind of seed its own body.

## 1 Corinthians 15:39

Not all flesh is the same. 📖

Humans, animals, birds, and fish each have their own kind of flesh.

## 1 Corinthians 15:40

There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies. 📖

The splendour of heavenly bodies is of one kind, and the splendour of earthly bodies is of another.

**Help:** Splendour means beauty or glory.

## 1 Corinthians 15:41

The sun, moon, and stars each have different splendour. 📖

One star differs from another in splendour.

## 1 Corinthians 15:42

It is the same with the resurrection of the dead. 📖

The body planted can perish; the body raised cannot perish.

## 1 Corinthians 15:43

It is planted in dishonour and raised in glory. 📖

It is planted in weakness and raised in power.

## 1 Corinthians 15:44

It is planted a natural body and raised a spiritual body. 📖

If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body.

**About these words:** Paul describes a changed body rather than saying there will be no body.

Further interpretation of natural and spiritual needs editorial review.

## 1 Corinthians 15:45

The Scriptures say, “The first man, Adam, became a living being.” 📖

The last Adam became a life-giving spirit.

**Help:** The last Adam refers to Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 15:46

The spiritual did not come first. 📖

The natural came first, and then the spiritual.

## 1 Corinthians 15:47

The first man came from the earth's dust; the second man came from heaven. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:48

Those of the earth are like the earthly man. 📖

Those of heaven are like the heavenly man.

## 1 Corinthians 15:49

Just as we have carried the earthly man's likeness, we will also carry the heavenly man's likeness. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:50

Brothers and sisters, I tell you: flesh and blood cannot inherit God's kingdom. 📖

What can perish cannot inherit what cannot perish.

## 1 Corinthians 15:51

Listen, I tell you a mystery: we will not all sleep, but we will all be changed, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:52

in an instant, in the blink of an eye, at the last trumpet. 📖

The trumpet will sound.

The dead will be raised unable to perish, and we will be changed.

## 1 Corinthians 15:53

What can perish must put on what cannot perish. 📖

What can die must put on life that cannot die.

## 1 Corinthians 15:54

Suppose what can perish has put on what cannot perish. 📖

What can die has also put on life that cannot die.

Then the written saying will come true: “Death has been swallowed up in victory.”

## 1 Corinthians 15:55

“Death, where is your victory? 📖

Death, where is your sting?”

## 1 Corinthians 15:56

Death's sting is sin, and sin's power is the law. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 15:57

But thanks be to God! 📖

He gives us victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 15:58

So, my dearly loved brothers and sisters, stand firmly and do not be moved. 📖

Always excel in the Lord's work, because you know your work in the Lord is not wasted.
