# 1 Corinthians 3 — God's field and building

**About this chapter**

Paul challenges jealousy and divisions.

He uses pictures of planting, building, and God's temple to explain the church and its workers.

## 1 Corinthians 3:1

Brothers and sisters, I could not speak to you as spiritual people. 📖

I had to speak to you as worldly people, like babies in Christ.

**Help:** Babies here pictures believers who are not yet mature in their life with Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 3:2

I gave you milk instead of solid food, because you were not ready for solid food. 📖

In fact, you are still not ready.

**Help:** Milk pictures basic teaching.

Solid food pictures teaching for mature believers.

## 1 Corinthians 3:3

You are still worldly. 📖

Since you have jealousy and quarrels among you, are you not worldly?

Are you not living in a merely human way?

## 1 Corinthians 3:4

When one says, “I follow Paul,” and another says, “I follow Apollos,” are you not acting as mere humans? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 3:5

What is Apollos? 📖

What is Paul?

They are servants through whom you believed, each with a task the Lord assigned.

## 1 Corinthians 3:6

I planted the seed, and Apollos watered it, but God made it grow. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 3:7

Neither the planter nor the one who waters is anything. 📖

Only God, who makes things grow, matters.

## 1 Corinthians 3:8

The planter and the one who waters share one purpose. 📖

Each will receive a reward according to their own work.

## 1 Corinthians 3:9

We are God's fellow workers. 📖

You are God's field and God's building.

## 1 Corinthians 3:10

By God's kindness to me, I laid a foundation like an expert builder. 📖

Someone else is building on it.

Each person must be careful how they build.

## 1 Corinthians 3:11

No one can lay any foundation other than the one already laid: Jesus Christ. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 3:12

Suppose someone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 3:13

Their work will become clear, because the Day will bring it to light. 📖

It will be revealed with fire.

The fire will test the quality of each person's work.

**Help:** The Day points to God's judgment.

Paul uses building materials and fire to describe the testing of people's work.

## 1 Corinthians 3:14

If someone's building survives, that person will receive a reward. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 3:15

If it burns up, that person will suffer loss. 📖

They themselves will be saved, but only as if through flames.

## 1 Corinthians 3:16

Do you not know that you together are God's temple, and God's Spirit lives in you? 📖

**Help:** Paul speaks to the believers together as God's temple, the place where God lives.

## 1 Corinthians 3:17

If anyone destroys God's temple, God will destroy that person. 📖

God's temple is holy, and you are that temple.

## 1 Corinthians 3:18

Let no one deceive themselves. 📖

If you think you are wise in this age, become a fool so you can become wise.

## 1 Corinthians 3:19

This world's wisdom is foolishness in God's sight. 📖

The Scriptures say, “He catches the wise in their clever tricks.”

## 1 Corinthians 3:20

Again, “The Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise achieve nothing.” 📖

## 1 Corinthians 3:21

So stop boasting about people. 📖

All things are yours,

## 1 Corinthians 3:22

whether Paul, Apollos, Cephas, the world, life, death, the present, or the future. 📖

All these belong to you,

## 1 Corinthians 3:23

and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God. 📖
