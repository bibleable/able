# 1 Corinthians 13 — Love is greatest

**About this chapter**

Paul explains why love matters more than impressive gifts.

## 1 Corinthians 13:1

I might speak human languages and even the languages of angels. ❤️

Without love, I am only a noisy gong or clashing cymbal.

**Help with words:** Gongs and cymbals are metal instruments that make loud sounds.

## 1 Corinthians 13:2

I might speak God's messages and understand every mystery and all knowledge. ❤️

My faith might be strong enough to move mountains.

Without love, I am nothing.

## 1 Corinthians 13:3

I might give everything I own to poor people. ❤️

I might even boast about giving up my body.

Without love, I gain nothing.

## 1 Corinthians 13:4

Love is patient and kind. ❤️

Love does not envy others.

Love does not boast or think it is better than others.

**Help with words:** Envy means wanting what someone else has.

Boasting means talking as if you are very great.

## 1 Corinthians 13:5

Love is not rude. ❤️

Love does not always seek its own way.

Love does not get angry easily.

Love does not keep a list of wrongs.

## 1 Corinthians 13:6

Love is not glad about evil. ❤️

Love is glad about the truth.

## 1 Corinthians 13:7

Love bears every trouble. ❤️

Love keeps trusting and hoping.

Love keeps going through everything.

## 1 Corinthians 13:8

Love never ends. ❤️

The gift of speaking God's messages will end.

The gift of speaking other languages will stop.

The gift of knowledge will pass away.

## 1 Corinthians 13:9

Now we know only part of the truth. ❤️

We speak only part of God's message.

## 1 Corinthians 13:10

When what is complete comes, what is only partly known will pass away. ❤️

## 1 Corinthians 13:11

As a child, I spoke, thought, and understood like a child. ❤️

When I became an adult, I left childish ways behind.

## 1 Corinthians 13:12

Now we see like someone looking at a dim reflection in a mirror. ❤️

Then we will see face to face.

Now I know only partly.

Then I will know fully, just as I am fully known.

## 1 Corinthians 13:13

These 3 remain: faith, hope, and love. ❤️

The greatest is love.

**Help with words:** Faith is trusting God.

Hope is looking forward to what God promises.
