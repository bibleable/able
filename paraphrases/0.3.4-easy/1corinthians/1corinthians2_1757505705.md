# 1 Corinthians 2 — Wisdom revealed by the Spirit

**About this chapter**

Paul explains his preaching and the wisdom God reveals through the Holy Spirit.

## 1 Corinthians 2:1

Brothers and sisters, I came to tell you the witness about God. 📖

I did not use impressive speech or wisdom.

## 1 Corinthians 2:2

While with you, I decided to know nothing except Jesus Christ and him crucified. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 2:3

I came to you weak, afraid, and trembling greatly. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 2:4

My message and preaching showed the Spirit's power, rather than using persuasive words of wisdom. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 2:5

Then your faith would rest on God's power, rather than human wisdom. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 2:6

Yet we speak wisdom among those who are mature. 📖

It is not this age's wisdom, or the wisdom of this age's rulers, who are coming to nothing.

## 1 Corinthians 2:7

We speak God's mysterious, hidden wisdom. 📖

Before time began, God planned this wisdom for our glory.

## 1 Corinthians 2:8

None of this age's rulers understood it. 📖

If they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

## 1 Corinthians 2:9

Instead, the Scriptures say, “No eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no heart has imagined these things. 📖

God has prepared them for those who love him.”

## 1 Corinthians 2:10

But God has revealed them to us through the Spirit. 📖

The Spirit searches everything, even God's deep things.

## 1 Corinthians 2:11

Who knows a person's thoughts except that person's own spirit within them? 📖

In the same way, no one knows God's thoughts except God's Spirit.

## 1 Corinthians 2:12

We received the Spirit from God, rather than the world's spirit. 📖

Then we can understand what God has freely given us.

## 1 Corinthians 2:13

We speak about these things in words taught by the Spirit, rather than human wisdom. 📖

We express spiritual truths in spiritual words.

## 1 Corinthians 2:14

The natural person does not accept what comes from God's Spirit. 📖

These things seem foolish to that person.

They cannot understand them, because they are understood spiritually.

**Help:** Natural person here describes someone without the understanding given by God's Spirit.

Paul is not describing a learning disability.

## 1 Corinthians 2:15

The spiritual person judges all things, but is not subject to anyone's judgment. 📖

**Help:** Spiritual person means someone guided by God's Spirit.

Paul continues addressing wrong conduct within the church in the next chapter.

## 1 Corinthians 2:16

“Who has known the Lord's mind well enough to teach him?” 📖

But we have Christ's mind.
