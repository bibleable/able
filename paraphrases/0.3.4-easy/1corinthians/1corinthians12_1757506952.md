# 1 Corinthians 12 — Different gifts, one body

**About this chapter**

Paul explains that the one Spirit gives different gifts for everyone's good.

He compares the church to a body whose parts need one another.

## 1 Corinthians 12:1

Brothers and sisters, I do not want you to be uninformed about spiritual gifts. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:2

You know that when you were pagans, you were influenced and led astray to idols that cannot speak. 📖

**Help:** Pagans here describes their former life of worshipping other gods.

Idols are objects worshipped as gods.

## 1 Corinthians 12:3

So I tell you: no one speaking by God's Spirit says, “Jesus be cursed.” 📖

No one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” except by the Holy Spirit.

## 1 Corinthians 12:4

There are different gifts, but the same Spirit. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:5

There are different kinds of service, but the same Lord. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:6

There are different ways of working, but the same God works everything in everyone. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:7

Each person is given a way for the Spirit to be made known, for everyone's good. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:8

One receives a message of wisdom through the Spirit. 📖

Another receives a message of knowledge through the same Spirit.

## 1 Corinthians 12:9

Another receives faith through the same Spirit. 📖

Another receives gifts of healing through that one Spirit.

## 1 Corinthians 12:10

Another receives the working of miracles; another, prophecy; another, the ability to distinguish between spirits. 📖

Another speaks in various tongues; another interprets tongues.

**Help:** Miracles are powerful acts beyond ordinary human ability.

Prophecy is a message from God.

Distinguishing between spirits means recognising which spirit is at work.

Tongues refers to Spirit-given speech or languages; interpretation makes the meaning understood.

## 1 Corinthians 12:11

All these are the work of the same Spirit. 📖

He gives them to each person as he decides.

## 1 Corinthians 12:12

A body is one unit with many parts. 📖

Although there are many parts, they form one body.

It is the same with Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 12:13

In one Spirit, we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, slaves or free. 📖

We were all given one Spirit to drink.

**Help:** The body here pictures the united community belonging to Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 12:14

The body has many parts, rather than just one. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:15

Suppose the foot says, “Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body.” 📖

That would not make it any less part of the body.

## 1 Corinthians 12:16

Suppose the ear says, “Because I am not an eye, I do not belong to the body.” 📖

That would not make it any less part of the body.

## 1 Corinthians 12:17

If the whole body were an eye, where would hearing be? 📖

If the whole body were an ear, where would smell be?

## 1 Corinthians 12:18

God has arranged every part of the body just as he planned. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:19

If all were one part, where would the body be? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:20

As it is, there are many parts but one body. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:21

The eye cannot tell the hand, “I do not need you.” 📖

The head cannot tell the feet, “I do not need you.”

## 1 Corinthians 12:22

Instead, the parts of the body that seem weaker are essential, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:23

and we give greater honour to parts we consider less honourable. 📖

We treat our unpresentable parts with special modesty,

**Help:** Unpresentable parts are parts people normally keep covered.

## 1 Corinthians 12:24

while our presentable parts need no such treatment. 📖

But God has put the body together, giving greater honour to the parts that lacked it,

## 1 Corinthians 12:25

so there will be no division in the body. 📖

Its parts should care for one another.

## 1 Corinthians 12:26

If one part suffers, every part suffers with it. 📖

If one part receives honour, every part rejoices with it.

## 1 Corinthians 12:27

You are Christ's body, and each of you is part of it. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 12:28

In the church, God appointed first apostles, second prophets, and third teachers. 📖

Then come miracle workers and those with gifts of healing, helping, organising, and various tongues.

**Help:** Apostles are people sent to carry Christ's message.

Prophets speak messages from God.

## 1 Corinthians 12:29

Are all apostles? 📖

Are all prophets?

Are all teachers?

Do all work miracles?

## 1 Corinthians 12:30

Do all have gifts of healing? 📖

Do all speak in tongues?

Do all interpret?

## 1 Corinthians 12:31

Eagerly desire the greater gifts. 📖

Now I will show you the most excellent way.
