# 1 Corinthians 4 — Faithful servants

**About this chapter**

Paul explains the apostles' service and suffering.

He warns the church against pride and urges them to follow his example.

## 1 Corinthians 4:1

People should see us as Christ's servants, trusted to care for God's mysteries. 📖

**Help:** God's mysteries are truths God makes known.

## 1 Corinthians 4:2

Those trusted with this care must be faithful. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 4:3

It matters very little to me whether you or any human court judges me. 📖

I do not even judge myself.

## 1 Corinthians 4:4

My conscience is clear, but that does not prove I am innocent. 📖

The Lord is the one who judges me.

**Help:** Conscience means a person's sense of right and wrong.

## 1 Corinthians 4:5

So judge nothing before the appointed time. 📖

Wait until the Lord comes.

He will bring hidden things out of darkness and reveal the reasons in people's hearts.

Then each person will receive praise from God.

## 1 Corinthians 4:6

Brothers and sisters, I have applied these things to myself and Apollos for your benefit. 📖

Learn from us not to go beyond what is written.

Then you will not proudly favour one person over another.

## 1 Corinthians 4:7

Who makes you so much better? 📖

What do you have that you did not receive?

If you received it, why boast as though you had not?

## 1 Corinthians 4:8

Already you have everything you want! 📖

Already you have become rich!

You have become kings without us!

I wish you really were kings, so we could be kings with you.

**Help:** Paul uses sharp irony to challenge their pride.

## 1 Corinthians 4:9

It seems to me God has displayed us apostles last in the procession, like prisoners sentenced to death. 📖

The whole world, both angels and humans, watches us as a spectacle.

**Help:** A procession is a line of people moving along.

A spectacle is something displayed for people to watch.

## 1 Corinthians 4:10

We are fools for Christ, but you are wise in Christ! 📖

We are weak, but you are strong!

You receive honour, but we receive dishonour.

## 1 Corinthians 4:11

Even now, we are hungry, thirsty, and poorly clothed. 📖

We are treated brutally and have no homes.

## 1 Corinthians 4:12

We work hard with our own hands. 📖

When people insult us, we bless them.

When they persecute us, we endure it.

**Help:** Persecute means treat people cruelly because of their faith.

## 1 Corinthians 4:13

When people tell lies against us, we answer gently. 📖

Even now, we have become like the earth's scum and the world's rubbish.

## 1 Corinthians 4:14

I write this to warn you as my dearly loved children, rather than to shame you. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 4:15

Even if you have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers. 📖

Through the good news, I became your father in Christ Jesus.

**Help:** A guardian looks after someone.

Paul uses father as a picture of his relationship with those who believed through his preaching.

## 1 Corinthians 4:16

So I urge you to follow my example. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 4:17

That is why I sent Timothy, my dearly loved and faithful child in the Lord. 📖

He will remind you how I live in Christ Jesus.

That way of life agrees exactly with what I teach everywhere in every church.

## 1 Corinthians 4:18

Some of you have become proud, as though I were not coming to you. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 4:19

But I will come soon, if the Lord is willing. 📖

Then I will learn both what these proud people say and what power they have.

## 1 Corinthians 4:20

God's kingdom is a matter of power, rather than talk. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 4:21

Which do you prefer? 📖

Should I come with a rod, or with love and a gentle spirit?

**Help:** The rod pictures discipline.
