# 1 Corinthians 8 — Knowledge, love, and food

**About this chapter**

Paul answers a question about food offered to idols.

He warns believers against using their freedom in ways that harm others.

## 1 Corinthians 8:1

Now about food sacrificed to idols: we know we all have knowledge. 📖

Knowledge makes people proud, but love builds people up.

**Help:** An idol is something worshipped as a god.

## 1 Corinthians 8:2

Someone who thinks they know something does not yet know as they should. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 8:3

But someone who loves God is known by God. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 8:4

About eating food sacrificed to idols: we know an idol is nothing at all in the world. 📖

There is only one God.

## 1 Corinthians 8:5

Even if there are so-called gods in heaven or on earth, 📖

there are many so-called gods and lords.

## 1 Corinthians 8:6

Yet for us there is one God, the Father. 📖

Everything came from him, and we exist for him.

There is one Lord, Jesus Christ.

Everything came through him, and we exist through him.

## 1 Corinthians 8:7

But not everyone knows this. 📖

Some people are still so used to idols that they eat this food as something offered to an idol.

Their weak conscience is then defiled.

**Help:** Conscience means a person's sense of right and wrong.

Defiled here means made impure in a religious or moral sense.

Paul describes the harm of acting against conscience, rather than a lack of physical cleanliness.

## 1 Corinthians 8:8

Food does not bring us closer to God. 📖

We are no worse if we do not eat, and no better if we do.

## 1 Corinthians 8:9

Be careful that your freedom does not become a stumbling block to those who are weak. 📖

**Help:** A stumbling block pictures something that leads another person into sin.

## 1 Corinthians 8:10

Suppose someone with a weak conscience sees you, who have knowledge, eating in an idol's temple. 📖

Will that not encourage them to eat food sacrificed to idols?

## 1 Corinthians 8:11

So your knowledge destroys this weak brother or sister, for whom Christ died. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 8:12

When you sin against your brothers and sisters this way and wound their weak conscience, you sin against Christ. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 8:13

If what I eat makes my brother or sister stumble, I will never eat meat again. 📖

Then I will not cause them to stumble.
