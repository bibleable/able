# 1 Corinthians 7 — Marriage, singleness, and God's call

**About this chapter**

Paul answers questions about marriage and singleness.

He distinguishes the Lord's commands from his own judgment and keeps the conditions attached to his advice.

## 1 Corinthians 7:1

Now about the matters you wrote about: it is good to avoid sexual relations. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:2

But because there is so much sexual wrongdoing, each man should have his own wife. 📖

Each woman should have her own husband.

## 1 Corinthians 7:3

A husband should fulfil his marriage duty to his wife, and a wife to her husband. 📖

**Help:** Marriage duty here refers to their sexual relationship.

## 1 Corinthians 7:4

A wife does not have authority over her own body; her husband does. 📖

Likewise, a husband does not have authority over his own body; his wife does.

## 1 Corinthians 7:5

Do not deny each other, except by mutual agreement for a time, so you can give yourselves to prayer. 📖

Then come together again, so Satan will not tempt you through your lack of self-control.

**Help:** Mutual agreement means both husband and wife agree.

## 1 Corinthians 7:6

I say this as something permitted, rather than as a command. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:7

I wish everyone were as I am. 📖

But each person has their own gift from God, one with this gift and another with that.

## 1 Corinthians 7:8

To unmarried people and widows, I say: it is good to remain unmarried, as I am. 📖

**Help:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Corinthians 7:9

But if they cannot control themselves, they should marry. 📖

It is better to marry than to burn with strong sexual desire.

## 1 Corinthians 7:10

To married people I give this command, from the Lord rather than myself: a wife must not leave her husband. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:11

But if she does leave, she must remain unmarried or be brought back together with her husband. 📖

A husband must not divorce his wife.

## 1 Corinthians 7:12

To the rest, I say this myself, rather than the Lord. 📖

If a believer's unbelieving wife is willing to live with him, he must not divorce her.

## 1 Corinthians 7:13

If a woman's unbelieving husband is willing to live with her, she must not divorce him. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:14

The unbelieving husband is made holy through his believing wife. 📖

The unbelieving wife is made holy through her believing husband.

Otherwise your children would be unclean, but now they are holy.

**About these words:** Holy means set apart for God.

Unclean here concerns religious standing, rather than physical dirt or disability.

Paul asks about a spouse's salvation in verse 16; this verse should not become a promise of automatic salvation.

## 1 Corinthians 7:15

But if the unbelieving partner leaves, let them leave. 📖

The believing brother or sister is not bound in such cases.

God has called you to live in peace.

## 1 Corinthians 7:16

Wife, how do you know whether you will save your husband? 📖

Husband, how do you know whether you will save your wife?

## 1 Corinthians 7:17

In any case, each person should live the life the Lord assigned them, to which God called them. 📖

This is what I direct in all the churches.

## 1 Corinthians 7:18

Was a man already circumcised when called? 📖

He should not become uncircumcised.

Was a man uncircumcised when called?

He should not be circumcised.

**Help:** Circumcision removes the foreskin, a piece of skin on the penis.

It was a sign of God's covenant with Abraham's descendants.

## 1 Corinthians 7:19

Circumcision and uncircumcision are nothing. 📖

Keeping God's commands is what matters.

## 1 Corinthians 7:20

Each person should remain in the situation they were in when called. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:21

Were you a slave when called? 📖

Do not let that concern you.

But if you can gain your freedom, take the opportunity.

## 1 Corinthians 7:22

A person who was a slave when the Lord called them is the Lord's freed person. 📖

A person who was free when called is Christ's slave.

**Help:** A freed person was someone released from slavery.

Here, slave of Christ describes belonging to Christ and serving him.

## 1 Corinthians 7:23

You were bought at a price. 📖

Do not become slaves of humans.

## 1 Corinthians 7:24

Brothers and sisters, each person should remain in the situation they were in when God called them. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:25

Now about virgins: I have no command from the Lord. 📖

But I give my judgment as someone the Lord's mercy has made trustworthy.

**Help:** A virgin is someone who has not had sexual relations.

## 1 Corinthians 7:26

Because of the present crisis, I think it is good for a man to remain as he is. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:27

Are you committed to a wife? 📖

Do not seek release.

Are you free from such a commitment?

Do not look for a wife.

## 1 Corinthians 7:28

But if you marry, you have not sinned. 📖

If a virgin marries, she has not sinned.

Those who marry will face troubles in this life, and I want to spare you these.

## 1 Corinthians 7:29

Brothers and sisters, I mean that time is short. 📖

From now on, those with wives should live as though they had none.

## 1 Corinthians 7:30

Those who weep should live as though they did not. 📖

Those who rejoice should live as though they did not.

Those who buy should live as though they owned nothing.

## 1 Corinthians 7:31

Those who use this world's things should live as though they did not depend on them. 📖

This world in its present form is passing away.

## 1 Corinthians 7:32

I want you to be free from concern. 📖

An unmarried man is concerned with the Lord's work and how to please the Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 7:33

But a married man is concerned with this world's affairs and how to please his wife, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 7:34

and his interests are divided. 📖

An unmarried woman or virgin is concerned with the Lord's work, to be holy in body and spirit.

But a married woman is concerned with this world's affairs and how to please her husband.

## 1 Corinthians 7:35

I say this for your good, rather than to restrict you. 📖

I want to encourage proper behaviour and devotion to the Lord without divided interests.

## 1 Corinthians 7:36

Suppose a man thinks he is acting improperly towards the virgin he is engaged to. 📖

If she is beyond her youth and they ought to marry, let him do as he wishes.

He is not sinning.

They should marry.

## 1 Corinthians 7:37

But suppose a man is firmly decided in his heart, without being forced, and has control over his own will. 📖

If he has decided in his heart not to marry the virgin, he will do well.

## 1 Corinthians 7:38

So the man who marries the virgin does well. 📖

But the man who does not marry her does even better.

## 1 Corinthians 7:39

A wife is bound to her husband as long as he lives. 📖

If he dies, she is free to marry anyone she wishes, provided he belongs to the Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 7:40

But in my judgment, she is happier if she remains as she is. 📖

I think that I too have God's Spirit.
