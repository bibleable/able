# 1 Corinthians 10 — Warnings and care for others

**About this chapter**

Paul uses Israel's history to warn the church against sin.

He teaches about the Lord's table, idol worship, and care for another person's conscience.

## 1 Corinthians 10:1

Brothers and sisters, I want you to know that our ancestors were all under the cloud. 📖

They all passed through the sea.

**Help:** This recalls Israel leaving Egypt, guided by God's cloud and passing through the sea.

## 1 Corinthians 10:2

They were all baptized into Moses in the cloud and the sea. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:3

They all ate the same spiritual food 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:4

and drank the same spiritual drink. 📖

They drank from the spiritual rock that went with them, and that rock was Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 10:5

Yet God was not pleased with most of them. 📖

They were struck down in the wilderness.

## 1 Corinthians 10:6

These events became examples to keep us from craving evil things as they did. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:7

Do not worship idols as some of them did. 📖

The Scriptures say, “The people sat down to eat and drink, then got up for wild celebrations.”

## 1 Corinthians 10:8

We must not commit sexual wrongdoing as some did. 📖

In one day, twenty-three thousand of them died.

## 1 Corinthians 10:9

We must not test Christ as some did. 📖

They were killed by snakes.

## 1 Corinthians 10:10

Do not complain as some did. 📖

They were killed by the destroying angel.

## 1 Corinthians 10:11

These events happened to them as examples. 📖

They were written as warnings for us, on whom the fulfilment of the ages has come.

**Help:** Fulfilment of the ages points to God's purposes reaching their intended time.

## 1 Corinthians 10:12

So anyone who thinks they stand firmly should be careful not to fall. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:13

Every temptation you face is common to humans. 📖

God is faithful.

He will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear.

When tempted, he will also provide a way out, so you can endure it.

## 1 Corinthians 10:14

So, my dearly loved friends, run away from idol worship. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:15

I speak to reasonable people. 📖

Judge for yourselves what I say.

## 1 Corinthians 10:16

Is the cup of blessing we bless not a sharing in Christ's blood? 📖

Is the bread we break not a sharing in Christ's body?

## 1 Corinthians 10:17

Because there is one loaf, we who are many are one body. 📖

We all share in that one loaf.

## 1 Corinthians 10:18

Think about Israel's people. 📖

Do those who eat the sacrifices not share in the altar?

## 1 Corinthians 10:19

Am I saying that food offered to an idol is anything, or that an idol is anything? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:20

No, but pagans offer their sacrifices to demons rather than God. 📖

I do not want you to share with demons.

**Help:** Pagans here means people worshipping gods other than the God Paul proclaims.

Demons are evil spirits.

## 1 Corinthians 10:21

You cannot drink both the Lord's cup and demons' cup. 📖

You cannot share at both the Lord's table and demons' table.

## 1 Corinthians 10:22

Are we trying to make the Lord jealous? 📖

Are we stronger than he is?

## 1 Corinthians 10:23

“Everything is allowed,” but not everything is helpful. 📖

“Everything is allowed,” but not everything builds people up.

## 1 Corinthians 10:24

Seek the good of others rather than your own good. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:25

Eat anything sold in the meat market without asking questions of conscience, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:26

because “The earth and everything in it belong to the Lord.” 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:27

If an unbeliever invites you to eat and you want to go, eat whatever they serve. 📖

Do not raise questions of conscience.

## 1 Corinthians 10:28

But if someone says, “This food was offered to idols,” do not eat it. 📖

Do this for the sake of the person who told you, and for conscience,

## 1 Corinthians 10:29

meaning the other person's conscience, rather than your own. 📖

Why should someone else's conscience decide my freedom?

**Help:** Conscience means a person's sense of right and wrong.

## 1 Corinthians 10:30

If I share a meal thankfully, why am I condemned for something I thank God for? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:31

Whether you eat, drink, or do anything else, do it all for God's glory. 📖

**Help:** God's glory means his greatness and honour.

## 1 Corinthians 10:32

Do not cause Jews, Greeks, or God's church to stumble, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 10:33

just as I try to please everyone in all I do. 📖

I seek the good of many rather than my own good, so they may be saved.
