# 1 Corinthians 5 — Wrongdoing within the church

**About this chapter**

Paul addresses a reported sexual relationship and the church's proud response.

He distinguishes judging people within the church from judging those outside it.

## 1 Corinthians 5:1

Sexual wrongdoing is actually reported among you, of a kind even pagans cannot tolerate. 📖

A man has his father's wife.

**Help:** This describes a sexual relationship with his father's wife.

Pagans here means people outside the Christian faith.

## 1 Corinthians 5:2

Yet you are proud! 📖

Should you not have been deeply grieved instead?

Should you not have removed the man who did this from your shared fellowship?

## 1 Corinthians 5:3

Although physically absent, I am present with you in spirit. 📖

I have already judged the man who did this, just as if I were there.

## 1 Corinthians 5:4

When you gather in our Lord Jesus' name, I am with you in spirit, along with the Lord Jesus' power. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 5:5

Hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh. 📖

Then his spirit may be saved on the Day of the Lord.

**About this difficult passage:** Paul gives this instruction to the gathered church.

The meaning of destruction of the flesh needs careful editorial review.

The draft retains the wording rather than replacing it with a claim about exactly what will happen.

## 1 Corinthians 5:6

Your boasting is not good. 📖

Do you not know that a little yeast works through the whole batch of dough?

**Help:** Yeast makes dough rise.

Here it pictures wrongdoing spreading through the church.

## 1 Corinthians 5:7

Remove the old yeast so you can be a new batch without yeast, as you really are. 📖

Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed.

**Help:** Passover remembers God rescuing Israel from slavery in Egypt.

Paul connects the removal of yeast at Passover with removing wrongdoing.

## 1 Corinthians 5:8

So let us keep the feast with bread without yeast, the bread of sincerity and truth. 📖

Let us avoid the old bread with the yeast of malice and wickedness.

**Help:** Malice means wanting to harm someone.

Sincerity means being honest and genuine.

Paul uses the bread as a picture of the believers' way of life.

## 1 Corinthians 5:9

In my letter, I wrote that you should not associate with people who act sexually wrongly. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 5:10

I did not mean this world's sexually immoral people, greedy people, cheats, or idol worshippers. 📖

To avoid all of them, you would have to leave the world.

## 1 Corinthians 5:11

Now I say: do not associate with someone claiming to be a believer while living in these ways. 📖

They may be sexually immoral, greedy, an idol worshipper, a verbal abuser, a drunkard, or a cheat.

Do not even eat with such a person.

**Help:** An idol is something worshipped as a god.

A verbal abuser uses words to attack and harm people.

## 1 Corinthians 5:12

What business is it of mine to judge those outside the church? 📖

Are you not to judge those inside?

## 1 Corinthians 5:13

God will judge those outside. 📖

“Remove the wicked man from among you.”
