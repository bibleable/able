# 1 Corinthians 9 — Rights and serving others

**About this chapter**

Paul explains his right to support as an apostle and why he gives up that right.

He describes serving others and keeping disciplined for the good news.

## 1 Corinthians 9:1

Am I not free? 📖

Am I not an apostle?

Have I not seen Jesus our Lord?

Are you not the result of my work in the Lord?

## 1 Corinthians 9:2

Even if I am not an apostle to others, surely I am to you. 📖

You are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.

**Help:** A seal confirms something as genuine.

Apostleship means the work and calling of an apostle, someone sent to carry Christ's message.

## 1 Corinthians 9:3

This is my defence to those who examine me. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 9:4

Do we not have a right to food and drink? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 9:5

Do we not have a right to take a believing wife with us? 📖

The other apostles, the Lord's brothers, and Cephas do this.

**Help:** Cephas is another name for Peter.

## 1 Corinthians 9:6

Or are Barnabas and I the only apostles who must work for a living? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 9:7

Who serves as a soldier at their own expense? 📖

Who plants a vineyard without eating its fruit?

Who looks after a flock without drinking its milk?

## 1 Corinthians 9:8

Am I speaking only from a human point of view? 📖

Does the Law not say the same?

## 1 Corinthians 9:9

The Law of Moses says, “Do not muzzle an ox while it treads out grain.” 📖

Is God concerned about oxen here?

**Help:** A muzzle covers an animal's mouth to stop it eating.

An ox could walk over harvested grain to separate the grain from its husks.

## 1 Corinthians 9:10

Is he not actually speaking for our sake? 📖

Yes, this was written for us.

The person ploughing and the person separating grain should expect to share in the harvest.

## 1 Corinthians 9:11

If we planted spiritual seed among you, is it too much to receive a material harvest from you? 📖

**Help:** Paul pictures sharing spiritual teaching as planting seed.

The material harvest pictures support for their daily needs.

## 1 Corinthians 9:12

If others have a right to your support, do we not have even more right? 📖

But we did not use this right.

We put up with anything rather than get in the way of Christ's good news.

## 1 Corinthians 9:13

Do you not know that temple workers eat its food? 📖

Those serving at the altar share in its offerings.

**Help:** An altar is a place for offering sacrifices.

## 1 Corinthians 9:14

Likewise, the Lord directed that those preaching the good news should make their living from the good news. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 9:15

But I have used none of these rights. 📖

I am not writing to ask you to do something for me.

I would rather die than let anyone take away my reason for boasting.

## 1 Corinthians 9:16

Preaching the good news gives me no reason to boast, because I am required to preach. 📖

How terrible for me if I do not preach the good news!

## 1 Corinthians 9:17

If I preach willingly, I have a reward. 📖

But if unwillingly, I am still trusted with a responsibility.

## 1 Corinthians 9:18

What is my reward, then? 📖

It is to preach the good news free of charge, without fully using my rights in preaching it.

## 1 Corinthians 9:19

Although free from obligation to anyone, I make myself everyone's slave to win as many people as possible. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 9:20

To Jews, I became like a Jew to win Jews. 📖

To those under the law, I became like someone under the law to win them.

Yet I myself am not under the law.

## 1 Corinthians 9:21

To those without the law, I became like someone without the law to win them. 📖

Yet I am not outside God's law; I am under Christ's law.

## 1 Corinthians 9:22

To the weak, I became weak to win the weak. 📖

I have become all things to all people, so by every possible means I might save some.

## 1 Corinthians 9:23

I do all this for the good news, so I can share in its blessings. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 9:24

Do you not know that all runners run in a race, but only one wins the prize? 📖

Run in a way that wins the prize.

## 1 Corinthians 9:25

Everyone competing in the games trains with strict discipline. 📖

They do this for a crown that will not last, but we do it for a crown that lasts.

## 1 Corinthians 9:26

So I do not run without a goal. 📖

I do not fight like someone punching the air.

## 1 Corinthians 9:27

I discipline my body and make it my slave. 📖

Then, after preaching to others, I myself will not be disqualified.

**Help:** Paul uses running and fighting as pictures of his disciplined service.

Disqualified means ruled out of receiving the prize.
