# 1 Corinthians 11 — Worship and the Lord's Supper

**About this chapter**

Paul gives instructions about head coverings in worship.

He also corrects the church's behaviour when sharing the Lord's Supper.

## 1 Corinthians 11:1

Follow my example, just as I follow Christ's example. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:2

I praise you for remembering me in everything. 📖

You keep the traditions just as I passed them on to you.

## 1 Corinthians 11:3

But I want you to understand: Christ is the head of every man. 📖

Man is the head of woman, and God is the head of Christ.

**About this image:** Paul uses head to describe these relationships.

Its meaning and application need careful editorial review alongside the whole passage, including verses 11–12.

## 1 Corinthians 11:4

Every man who prays or prophesies with his head covered dishonours his head. 📖

**Help:** Prophesying means speaking a message from God.

## 1 Corinthians 11:5

Every woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered dishonours her head. 📖

It is just as if her head were shaved.

## 1 Corinthians 11:6

If a woman does not cover her head, she should have her hair cut off. 📖

If cutting or shaving her hair is shameful for her, she should cover her head.

## 1 Corinthians 11:7

A man should not cover his head, since he is God's image and glory. 📖

But woman is man's glory.

**Help:** Glory means honour and greatness made known.

## 1 Corinthians 11:8

Man did not come from woman, but woman from man. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:9

Man was not created for woman, but woman for man. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:10

For this reason, a woman should have a sign of authority on her head, because of the angels. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:11

Yet in the Lord, woman is not independent of man, and man is not independent of woman. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:12

Just as woman came from man, man is born of woman. 📖

But everything comes from God.

## 1 Corinthians 11:13

Judge for yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:14

Does nature itself not teach you that long hair on a man is a disgrace to him, 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:15

but long hair on a woman is her glory? 📖

Her long hair is given to her as a covering.

## 1 Corinthians 11:16

If anyone wants to argue about this, we have no other practice, and neither do God's churches. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:17

In the instructions that follow, I offer no praise. 📖

Your gatherings do more harm than good.

## 1 Corinthians 11:18

First, I hear there are divisions when you gather as a church, and I partly believe it. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:19

Indeed, there must be differences among you to show which of you are approved. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:20

When you gather, it is not the Lord's Supper you eat. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:21

Each goes ahead with their own meal without sharing. 📖

One remains hungry while another gets drunk.

## 1 Corinthians 11:22

Do you not have homes for eating and drinking? 📖

Or do you despise God's church and shame those who have nothing?

What can I say?

Should I praise you for this?

No, I will not!

## 1 Corinthians 11:23

I received from the Lord what I also passed on to you. 📖

On the night he was betrayed, the Lord Jesus took bread,

## 1 Corinthians 11:24

gave thanks, broke it, and said, “This is my body, which is for you. 📖

Do this to remember me.”

## 1 Corinthians 11:25

Likewise, after supper he took the cup and said, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood. 📖

Whenever you drink it, do this to remember me.”

**Help:** A covenant is a binding agreement or relationship established by God.

## 1 Corinthians 11:26

Whenever you eat this bread and drink this cup, you announce the Lord's death until he comes. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:27

Anyone eating the bread or drinking the Lord's cup in an unworthy way sins against the Lord's body and blood. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:28

Each person must examine themselves before eating the bread and drinking the cup. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:29

Anyone eating and drinking without recognising the body eats and drinks judgment on themselves. 📖

**About these words:** The meaning of recognising the body needs review with the whole passage about Christ and the church.

## 1 Corinthians 11:30

That is why many among you are weak and sick, and a number have fallen asleep. 📖

**Help:** Fallen asleep here means died.

Paul addresses this church's situation; he does not say that every illness results from this wrongdoing.

## 1 Corinthians 11:31

If we judged ourselves properly, we would not come under judgment. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:32

When the Lord judges us, he disciplines us so we will not be condemned with the world. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:33

So, my brothers and sisters, wait for one another when you gather to eat. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 11:34

If anyone is hungry, they should eat at home so your gatherings will not bring judgment. 📖

I will give instructions about the remaining matters when I come.
