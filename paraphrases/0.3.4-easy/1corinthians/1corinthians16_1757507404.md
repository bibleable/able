# 1 Corinthians 16 — Gifts, plans, and greetings

**About this chapter**

Paul gives instructions for a collection and explains his travel plans.

He closes with encouragement, a warning, and greetings.

## 1 Corinthians 16:1

About the collection for God's people: do as I directed the churches in Galatia. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:2

On each week's first day, each of you should save part of your income. 📖

Then no collections will be needed when I come.

## 1 Corinthians 16:3

When I arrive, I will send letters with the people you recommend to carry your gift to Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:4

If it is wise for me to go too, they can travel with me. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:5

I will come to you after passing through Macedonia, because I will be travelling through Macedonia. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:6

Perhaps I will stay with you for a while, or even spend the winter. 📖

Then you can help me on my journey, wherever I go.

## 1 Corinthians 16:7

I do not want to see you now only in passing. 📖

I hope to spend time with you, if the Lord permits.

## 1 Corinthians 16:8

But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost, 📖

**Help:** Pentecost is a feast held fifty days after Passover.

## 1 Corinthians 16:9

because a great door for effective work has opened to me, although many oppose me. 📖

**Help:** An open door pictures an opportunity.

## 1 Corinthians 16:10

If Timothy comes, make sure he has nothing to fear while with you. 📖

He is doing the Lord's work, just as I am.

## 1 Corinthians 16:11

No one should treat him with contempt. 📖

Send him on his way in peace so he can return to me.

I am expecting him with the brothers.

**Help:** Contempt means treating someone as worthless or beneath you.

## 1 Corinthians 16:12

About our brother Apollos: I strongly urged him to visit you with the brothers. 📖

He did not want to go now, but he will go when he has the opportunity.

## 1 Corinthians 16:13

Be alert. 📖

Stand firmly in the faith.

Be courageous.

Be strong.

## 1 Corinthians 16:14

Do everything in love. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:15

You know Stephanas and his household were the first converts in Achaia. 📖

They have given themselves to serving God's people.

Now I urge you, brothers and sisters,

## 1 Corinthians 16:16

to submit to people like these, and to everyone who works and serves alongside them. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:17

I am glad Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus have arrived. 📖

They have supplied what was lacking from you.

## 1 Corinthians 16:18

They refreshed my spirit and yours too. 📖

So show appreciation for people like them.

## 1 Corinthians 16:19

The churches in the province of Asia send greetings. 📖

Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, and so does the church meeting in their house.

**Help:** Asia here was a Roman province, rather than the whole continent now called Asia.

## 1 Corinthians 16:20

All the brothers here send greetings. 📖

Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**Help:** A holy kiss was a greeting expressing believers' shared love and belonging to God.

## 1 Corinthians 16:21

I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:22

If anyone does not love the Lord, let that person be under a curse. 📖

Come, Lord!

## 1 Corinthians 16:23

May the Lord Jesus' kindness be with you. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 16:24

May my love be with you all in Christ Jesus. 📖

Amen.
