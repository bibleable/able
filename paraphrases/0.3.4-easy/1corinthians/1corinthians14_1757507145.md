# 1 Corinthians 14 — Gifts that build up the church

**About this chapter**

Paul explains why understandable speech helps the gathered church.

He gives instructions for orderly worship, including difficult statements that need careful interpretation alongside chapter 11.

## 1 Corinthians 14:1

Pursue love earnestly and eagerly desire spiritual gifts, especially prophecy. 📖

**Help:** Prophecy means speaking a message from God.

## 1 Corinthians 14:2

Someone speaking in a tongue speaks to God rather than people. 📖

No one understands them.

They speak mysteries in the Spirit.

**Help:** Tongues refers to Spirit-given speech or languages.

Interpretation makes the meaning understood.

## 1 Corinthians 14:3

But someone prophesying speaks to people to build them up, encourage them, and comfort them. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:4

Someone speaking in a tongue builds themselves up. 📖

Someone prophesying builds up the church.

## 1 Corinthians 14:5

I wish you all could speak in tongues, but I would rather you prophesied. 📖

Someone prophesying is greater than someone speaking in tongues, unless they interpret so the church can be built up.

## 1 Corinthians 14:6

Brothers and sisters, how will I help you if I come speaking in tongues? 📖

I must bring you some revelation, knowledge, prophecy, or teaching.

**Help:** A revelation is something God makes known.

## 1 Corinthians 14:7

Think of lifeless instruments like a flute or harp. 📖

How can anyone recognise their tune unless the notes are clear and different?

## 1 Corinthians 14:8

If a trumpet gives an unclear call, who will prepare for battle? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:9

It is the same with you. 📖

Unless your tongue speaks words people can understand, how will anyone know what you say?

You will just speak into the air.

## 1 Corinthians 14:10

There are certainly many different languages in the world, and none lacks meaning. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:11

If I do not understand someone's language, I am a foreigner to them, and they are a foreigner to me. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:12

It is the same with you. 📖

Since you eagerly desire spiritual gifts, try to excel in gifts that build up the church.

## 1 Corinthians 14:13

So anyone speaking in a tongue should pray to be able to interpret. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:14

If I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind bears no fruit. 📖

**Help:** Bears no fruit pictures producing no useful result.

## 1 Corinthians 14:15

What should I do, then? 📖

I will pray with my spirit and also with my mind.

I will sing with my spirit and also with my mind.

## 1 Corinthians 14:16

If you give a blessing in spirit, how can someone without understanding say “Amen” to your thanks? 📖

They do not know what you are saying.

**Help:** Amen expresses agreement with a prayer or thanksgiving.

## 1 Corinthians 14:17

You may give thanks well, but the other person is not built up. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:18

I thank God that I speak in tongues more than you all. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:19

But in church, I prefer five understandable words that teach others to ten thousand words in a tongue. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:20

Brothers and sisters, stop thinking like children. 📖

Be like babies regarding evil, but be mature in your thinking.

## 1 Corinthians 14:21

The Law says, “Through strange tongues and foreign lips, I will speak to this people. 📖

Even then they will not listen to me, says the Lord.”

## 1 Corinthians 14:22

So tongues are a sign for unbelievers rather than believers. 📖

Prophecy, however, is for believers rather than unbelievers.

## 1 Corinthians 14:23

Suppose the whole church gathers and everyone speaks in tongues. 📖

If uninstructed people or unbelievers enter, will they not say you are out of your minds?

## 1 Corinthians 14:24

But suppose an unbeliever or uninstructed person enters while everyone prophesies. 📖

Everyone's words will convict that person and call them to account,

## 1 Corinthians 14:25

and the secrets of their heart will become known. 📖

They will fall facedown, worship God, and declare, “God is truly among you!”

## 1 Corinthians 14:26

What should we say, then, brothers and sisters? 📖

When you gather, each has a psalm, teaching, revelation, tongue, or interpretation.

Everything must build up the church.

**Help:** A psalm is a song of worship.

## 1 Corinthians 14:27

If anyone speaks in a tongue, two or at most three should speak, one at a time. 📖

Someone must interpret.

## 1 Corinthians 14:28

If no interpreter is present, the speaker should be silent in church and speak only to themselves and God. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:29

Two or three prophets should speak, and the others should carefully weigh what they say. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:30

If someone seated receives a revelation, the first speaker should stop. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:31

You can all prophesy one at a time, so everyone can be taught and encouraged. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:32

The spirits of prophets are subject to prophets. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:33

God is a God of peace rather than disorder, as in all the churches of God's people. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:34

Women are to be silent in the churches. 📖

They are not permitted to speak, but must submit, as the law says.

**About this difficult instruction:** This draft retains the BSB statement rather than narrowing or expanding it.

Chapter 11:5 also discusses women praying and prophesying.

How these passages fit together and apply needs independent editorial review.

## 1 Corinthians 14:35

If they want to ask about something, they should ask their own husbands at home. 📖

It is dishonourable for a woman to speak in church.

## 1 Corinthians 14:36

Did God's word begin with you? 📖

Or are you the only people it has reached?

## 1 Corinthians 14:37

Anyone who considers themselves a prophet or spiritual person must recognise that what I write is the Lord's command. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:38

But anyone who ignores this will themselves be ignored. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:39

So, my brothers and sisters, eagerly desire to prophesy, and do not forbid speaking in tongues. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 14:40

Everything must be done properly and in good order. 📖
