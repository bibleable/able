# 1 Corinthians 6 — Disputes and bodies belonging to God

**About this chapter**

Paul challenges lawsuits between believers and warns against wrongdoing.

He teaches that believers' bodies belong to the Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 6:1

If you have a complaint against another believer, how dare you take it before unrighteous judges instead of God's people! 📖

## 1 Corinthians 6:2

Do you not know that God's people will judge the world? 📖

If you will judge the world, are you not able to judge small cases?

## 1 Corinthians 6:3

Do you not know that we will judge angels? 📖

How much more should we judge matters of this life!

## 1 Corinthians 6:4

If you need to settle everyday matters, do you choose judges with no standing in the church? 📖

## 1 Corinthians 6:5

I say this to shame you. 📖

Is no one among you wise enough to settle a dispute between believers?

## 1 Corinthians 6:6

Instead, one believer takes another to court, in front of unbelievers! 📖

## 1 Corinthians 6:7

Having lawsuits among you already means you are thoroughly defeated. 📖

Why not accept being wronged instead?

Why not accept being cheated?

## 1 Corinthians 6:8

Instead, you yourselves cheat and do wrong, even to your own brothers and sisters! 📖

## 1 Corinthians 6:9

Do you not know that wicked people will not inherit God's kingdom? 📖

Do not be deceived.

This includes sexually immoral people, idol worshippers, adulterers, and men who receive or perform sexual acts with other men,

**Help:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

Paul's list continues in the next verse.

## 1 Corinthians 6:10

as well as thieves, greedy people, drunkards, verbal abusers, and cheats. 📖

These will not inherit God's kingdom.

## 1 Corinthians 6:11

Some of you were like this. 📖

But you were washed, made holy, and declared right with God.

This happened in the Lord Jesus Christ's name and by the Spirit of our God.

## 1 Corinthians 6:12

“Everything is allowed for me,” but not everything is helpful. 📖

“Everything is allowed for me,” but I will not let anything master me.

**About these words:** The quoted claims and Paul's replies should be read together.

## 1 Corinthians 6:13

“Food is for the stomach, and the stomach for food,” but God will destroy both. 📖

The body is for the Lord, rather than sexual wrongdoing, and the Lord is for the body.

## 1 Corinthians 6:14

By his power, God raised the Lord from death, and he will raise us too. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 6:15

Do you not know that your bodies are parts of Christ? 📖

Should I take parts of Christ and join them to a prostitute?

Never!

**Help:** A prostitute is a person who has sex for payment.

## 1 Corinthians 6:16

Do you not know that someone joined to a prostitute becomes one body with her? 📖

The Scriptures say, “The two will become one flesh.”

## 1 Corinthians 6:17

But someone joined to the Lord is one with him in spirit. 📖

## 1 Corinthians 6:18

Run away from sexual wrongdoing. 📖

Every other sin a person commits is outside their body.

But someone who sins sexually sins against their own body.

## 1 Corinthians 6:19

Do you not know your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you received from God? 📖

You do not belong to yourselves,

## 1 Corinthians 6:20

because you were bought at a price. 📖

So give God glory with your body.

**Help:** A temple is a place where God lives.

Being bought pictures the cost of setting believers free to belong to God.

Giving God glory means honouring him.
