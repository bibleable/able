# Ruth 1 — Naomi and Ruth go to Bethlehem

**About this chapter**

Naomi’s husband and sons die in Moab.

Ruth chooses to stay with Naomi and go with her to Judah.

## Ruth 1:1

When the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. 📖

A man from Bethlehem in Judah went to live in Moab with his wife and 2 sons.

**Help with judges:** These were leaders who governed Israel before its kings.

**Help with famine:** A famine is a time when there is very little food.

## Ruth 1:2

The man was named Elimelech, and his wife was Naomi. 📖

Their sons were Mahlon and Chilion.

They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah.

They went into Moab and settled there.

**Help with Ephrathites:** This name identifies the family’s people and home area.

## Ruth 1:3

Naomi’s husband Elimelech died. 📖

Naomi was left with her 2 sons.

## Ruth 1:4

The sons married women from Moab. 📖

One woman was named Orpah, and the other was Ruth.

They lived in Moab for about 10 years.

## Ruth 1:5

Mahlon and Chilion both died too. 📖

Naomi was left without her 2 sons and without her husband.

## Ruth 1:6

In Moab, Naomi heard that the LORD had cared for his people by giving them food. 📖

Naomi and her daughters-in-law prepared to leave Moab.

**Help with daughters-in-law:** These are women married to a person’s sons.

## Ruth 1:7

Naomi left the place where she had lived. 📖

Her 2 daughters-in-law went with her on the road back to Judah.

**Help with daughters-in-law:** These are women married to a person’s sons.

## Ruth 1:8

Naomi told her daughters-in-law, “Each of you should go back to your mother’s home. 📖

May the LORD show you faithful love.

You have shown that love to your dead and to me.

**Help with daughters-in-law:** These are women married to a person’s sons.

## Ruth 1:9

May the LORD give each of you rest in a new husband’s home.” 📖

Naomi kissed them, and they cried aloud.

## Ruth 1:10

The women said, “We will certainly go back with you to your people.” 📖

## Ruth 1:11

Naomi answered, “Go home, my daughters. 📖

Why would you go with me?

Do I still have sons in my womb to become your husbands?

**Help with womb:** A womb is the part inside a woman’s body where a baby grows.

## Ruth 1:12

Go home, my daughters. 📖

Go on, because I am too old to have another husband.

Even suppose I hoped to have a husband tonight and give birth to sons.

## Ruth 1:13

Would you wait for them to grow up? 📖

Would you hold back from having husbands?

No, my daughters.

This is much more bitter for me than for you.

The LORD’s hand has gone out against me.”

**Help with the LORD’s hand:** Naomi describes her suffering as the LORD acting against her.

## Ruth 1:14

They cried aloud again. 📖

Orpah kissed her mother-in-law goodbye, but Ruth held tightly to Naomi.

**Help with mother-in-law:** A mother-in-law is the mother of a person’s husband or wife.

## Ruth 1:15

Naomi said, “Look, your sister-in-law has gone back to her people and her gods. 📖

Go back home with her.”

**Help with sister-in-law:** Here, Orpah had been married to the brother of Ruth’s husband.

## Ruth 1:16

Ruth answered, “Do not urge me to leave you or stop following you. 📖

Where you go, I will go.

Where you live, I will live.

Your people will be my people.

Your God will be my God.

## Ruth 1:17

Where you die, I will die, and I will be buried there. 📖

May the LORD punish me very severely if anything but death separates you and me.”

## Ruth 1:18

Naomi saw that Ruth was determined to go with her. 📖

So Naomi stopped trying to persuade her.

## Ruth 1:19

Naomi and Ruth travelled until they reached Bethlehem. 📖

Their arrival stirred up the whole town.

The women exclaimed, “Can this be Naomi?”

## Ruth 1:20

Naomi answered, “Do not call me Naomi. 📖

Call me Mara, because the Almighty has made my life very bitter.

**Help with names:** Naomi is linked with pleasantness.

Mara is linked with bitterness.

**Help with Almighty:** Almighty is a title for God.

## Ruth 1:21

I went away full, but the LORD brought me back empty. 📖

Why call me Naomi?

The LORD has spoken against me, and the Almighty has made me suffer.”

**Help with full and empty:** Naomi uses these words to describe her losses.

**Help with Almighty:** Almighty is a title for God.

## Ruth 1:22

Naomi returned from Moab with her daughter-in-law Ruth, the Moabite woman. 📖

They reached Bethlehem as the barley harvest began.

**Help with daughter-in-law:** A daughter-in-law is a woman married to a person’s son.

**Help with barley:** Barley is a grain grown for food.
