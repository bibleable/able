# Ruth 4 — Boaz marries Ruth

**About this chapter**

Boaz meets the nearer relative and witnesses at the town gate.

The chapter tells of Ruth’s marriage, her son, and the family line leading to David.

## Ruth 4:1

Boaz went to the gate and sat down. 📖

Soon the kinsman-redeemer he had spoken about came past.

Boaz said, “Come here, my friend, and sit down.”

The man came over and sat down.

**Help with gate:** The town gate was also a place for public business.

**Help with kinsman-redeemer:** This is a close relative with responsibilities to protect a family and its property.

## Ruth 4:2

Boaz asked 10 of the town’s elders to sit there. 📖

They did so.

**Help with elders:** Elders were leaders in the community.

## Ruth 4:3

Boaz told the kinsman-redeemer, “Naomi has returned from Moab. 📖

She is selling the land that belonged to our brother Elimelech.

**Help with brother:** Here, brother describes a family relationship.

**Help with kinsman-redeemer:** This is a close relative with responsibilities to protect the family and its property.

## Ruth 4:4

I thought I should tell you, so you may buy it back before these people and my people’s elders. 📖

If you want to redeem it, do so.

If you will not, tell me so I know.

You have the first right to redeem it, and I am next after you.”

The man answered, “I will redeem it.”

**Help with redeem:** Here, redeem means buy back the family’s land.

**Help with elders:** Elders were leaders in the community.

## Ruth 4:5

Boaz said, “When you buy the land from Naomi and from Ruth the Moabite woman, there is another duty.

On that day, you must also acquire the dead man’s widow. 📖

This is to keep the dead man’s name alive on his inheritance.”

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

**Help with acquire the widow:** This refers to marriage in this family-property arrangement.

**Help with inheritance:** Inheritance is property passed on in a family.

## Ruth 4:6

The kinsman-redeemer answered, “I cannot redeem it myself, because I would put my own inheritance at risk. 📖

Take my right to redeem it.

I cannot do it.”

**Help with redeem:** Here, redeem means carry out this family-property responsibility.

**Help with inheritance:** Inheritance is property passed on in a family.

## Ruth 4:7

Long ago in Israel, a man would take off his sandal and give it to the other person. 📖

This made a property redemption or exchange legally binding.

That was how Israel confirmed such matters.

**Help with redemption:** Here, redemption means buying back family property.

## Ruth 4:8

The kinsman-redeemer took off his sandal. 📖

He told Boaz, “Buy it for yourself.”

**Help with kinsman-redeemer:** This is a close relative with responsibilities to protect the family and its property.

## Ruth 4:9

Boaz told the elders and all the people, “You are witnesses today. 📖

I am buying from Naomi everything that belonged to Elimelech, Chilion, and Mahlon.

**Help with elders:** Elders were community leaders.

**Help with witnesses:** Witnesses are people who can confirm what happened.

## Ruth 4:10

I have also acquired Ruth the Moabite woman, Mahlon’s widow, as my wife. 📖

This will keep the dead man’s name alive through his inheritance.

His name will not disappear from his brothers or from the gate of his home.

You are witnesses today.”

**Help with inheritance:** Inheritance is property passed on in a family.

**Help with witnesses:** Witnesses are people who can confirm what happened.

**Help with gate:** Here, the gate is linked to the man’s home community.

## Ruth 4:11

The elders and all the people at the gate said, “We are witnesses. 📖

May the LORD make the woman coming into your home like Rachel and Leah.

Together they built up Israel’s family.

May you prosper in Ephrathah and become famous in Bethlehem.

**Help with elders and witnesses:** Elders were community leaders.

Witnesses are people who can confirm what happened.

## Ruth 4:12

May your family become like the family of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah. 📖

May this happen through the children the LORD gives you by this young woman.”

## Ruth 4:13

Boaz took Ruth as his wife. 📖

Boaz had sex with Ruth.

The LORD enabled Ruth to become pregnant, and she gave birth to a son.

## Ruth 4:14

The women told Naomi, “Blessed be the LORD! 📖

He has not left you today without a kinsman-redeemer.

May his name become famous in Israel.

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed expresses praise to God.

**Help with kinsman-redeemer:** This is a family member who provides help and protection.

## Ruth 4:15

He will renew your life and support you in your old age. 📖

Your daughter-in-law loves you and is better to you than 7 sons.

She has given birth to him.”

**Help with daughter-in-law:** A daughter-in-law is a woman married to a person’s son.

## Ruth 4:16

Naomi took the child and placed him on her lap.

She became his carer. 📖

## Ruth 4:17

The neighbour women said, “A son has been born to Naomi.” 📖

They named him Obed.

Obed became Jesse’s father, and Jesse became David’s father.

## Ruth 4:18

This is the family line of Perez. 📖

Perez was Hezron’s father.

## Ruth 4:19

Hezron was Ram’s father. 📖

Ram was Amminadab’s father.

## Ruth 4:20

Amminadab was Nahshon’s father. 📖

Nahshon was Salmon’s father.

## Ruth 4:21

Salmon was Boaz’s father. 📖

Boaz was Obed’s father.

## Ruth 4:22

Obed was Jesse’s father. 📖

Jesse was David’s father.
