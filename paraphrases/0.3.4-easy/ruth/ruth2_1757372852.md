# Ruth 2 — Ruth gathers grain in Boaz’s field

**About this chapter**

Ruth gathers grain left by the harvesters.

Boaz offers protection and help.

## Ruth 2:1

Naomi had a relative on her husband’s side named Boaz. 📖

Boaz was an important man of good character from Elimelech’s family group.

## Ruth 2:2

Ruth the Moabite woman asked Naomi, “Please let me go to the fields and gather leftover heads of grain. 📖

Someone may look kindly on me.”

Naomi said, “Go ahead, my daughter.”

## Ruth 2:3

Ruth went out and gathered grain behind the harvesters. 📖

She happened to come to the part of the field belonging to Boaz.

Boaz was from Elimelech’s family group.

## Ruth 2:4

Just then, Boaz arrived from Bethlehem. 📖

Boaz told the harvesters, “The LORD be with you.”

They answered, “The LORD bless you.”

## Ruth 2:5

Boaz asked the man in charge of his harvesters, “Whose young woman is this?” 📖

## Ruth 2:6

The man answered, “She is the Moabite woman who came back with Naomi from Moab. 📖

## Ruth 2:7

She asked to gather leftover grain among the bundles after the harvesters. 📖

She came and has worked from morning until now.

She rested only a short time in the shelter.”

## Ruth 2:8

Boaz told Ruth, “Listen, my daughter. 📖

Do not go to gather grain in another field.

Do not leave this place.

Stay here close to my servant girls.

## Ruth 2:9

Watch the field they are harvesting and follow these girls. 📖

I have ordered the young men not to touch you.

When you are thirsty, drink from the jars the young men have filled.”

## Ruth 2:10

Ruth fell with her face towards the ground, bowing low. 📖

She asked Boaz, “Why have you shown me such kindness and taken notice of me?

I am a foreigner.”

## Ruth 2:11

Boaz answered, “I have heard fully about everything you have done for your mother-in-law since your husband died. 📖

You left your father, mother, and the land where you were born.

You came to people you did not know before.

**Help with mother-in-law:** A mother-in-law is the mother of a person’s husband or wife.

## Ruth 2:12

May the LORD repay your work. 📖

May the LORD, the God of Israel, give you a rich reward.

You have come for safety under his wings.”

**Help with wings:** Boaz compares God’s protection to a bird sheltering its young.

## Ruth 2:13

Ruth said, “My lord, may you continue to look kindly on me. 📖

You have comforted me and spoken kindly to your servant.

Yet I am not like one of your servant girls.”

## Ruth 2:14

At mealtime, Boaz told Ruth, “Come here. 📖

Have some bread and dip it in the vinegar sauce.”

Ruth sat beside the harvesters, and Boaz offered her roasted grain.

She ate until she was full and had some left over.

## Ruth 2:15

When Ruth got up to gather grain, Boaz ordered his young men, “Do not insult her. 📖

Even if she gathers among the bundles, do not insult her.

## Ruth 2:16

Instead, pull some stalks out of the bundles for her. 📖

Leave them for her to gather.

Do not scold her.”

## Ruth 2:17

Ruth gathered grain in the field until evening. 📖

She beat out the grain she had gathered.

There was about 1 ephah of barley.

**Help with ephah:** An ephah was a measure for grain.

**Help with barley:** Barley is a grain grown for food.

## Ruth 2:18

Ruth carried the grain into town, and Naomi saw what Ruth had gathered. 📖

Ruth also brought out the food she had saved from her meal and gave it to Naomi.

## Ruth 2:19

Naomi asked, “Where did you gather grain and work today? 📖

May the man who noticed you be blessed.”

Ruth told Naomi where she had worked.

Ruth said, “The man I worked with today is named Boaz.”

**Help with blessed:** Here, Naomi wishes good from God for the man.

## Ruth 2:20

Naomi told Ruth, “May the LORD bless him. 📖

The LORD has not taken away his kindness from the living or the dead.”

Naomi added, “The man is a close relative.

He is one of our kinsman-redeemers.”

**Help with kinsman-redeemer:** This is a close relative with responsibilities to help protect a family and its property.

## Ruth 2:21

Ruth the Moabite woman said, “He also told me to stay with his young men. 📖

He said to stay until they finish gathering all his harvest.”

## Ruth 2:22

Naomi told her daughter-in-law Ruth, “My daughter, it is good for you to work with his young women. 📖

Then nothing will happen to you in another field.”

**Help with daughter-in-law:** A daughter-in-law is a woman married to a person’s son.

## Ruth 2:23

Ruth stayed close to Boaz’s servant girls to gather grain until the barley and wheat harvests ended. 📖

Ruth lived with her mother-in-law.

**Help with barley and wheat:** These are grains grown for food.

**Help with mother-in-law:** A mother-in-law is the mother of a person’s husband or wife.
