# Ruth 3 — Ruth asks Boaz for help

**About this chapter**

Naomi tells Ruth to visit Boaz at the threshing floor.

Boaz speaks about his responsibility and a nearer relative.

## Ruth 3:1

One day, Ruth’s mother-in-law Naomi said, “My daughter, should I not seek a resting place for you? 📖

I want things to go well for you.

**Help with mother-in-law:** A mother-in-law is the mother of a person’s husband or wife.

**Help with resting place:** Here, Naomi speaks of finding a secure home for Ruth.

## Ruth 3:2

Boaz is our relative, and you have worked with his servant girls. 📖

Tonight he is winnowing barley on the threshing floor.

**Help with winnowing:** Winnowing uses moving air to separate grain from its dry covering.

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where people separate grain from its covering.

**Help with barley:** Barley is a grain grown for food.

## Ruth 3:3

Wash, put on perfume, and dress in your best clothes. 📖

Go down to the threshing floor.

Do not let Boaz know you are there until he finishes eating and drinking.

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where people separate grain from its covering.

## Ruth 3:4

Notice where he lies down. 📖

Then go in, uncover his feet, and lie down.

He will tell you what to do.”

## Ruth 3:5

Ruth answered, “I will do everything you say.” 📖

## Ruth 3:6

Ruth went down to the threshing floor and did everything Naomi had told her. 📖

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where people separate grain from its covering.

## Ruth 3:7

Boaz finished eating and drinking and felt cheerful. 📖

He went to lie down at the end of the heap of grain.

Ruth came in secretly, uncovered his feet, and lay down.

## Ruth 3:8

At midnight, Boaz was startled and turned over. 📖

A woman was lying at his feet!

## Ruth 3:9

Boaz asked, “Who are you?” 📖

Ruth answered, “I am your servant Ruth.

Spread the corner of your clothing over me, because you are a kinsman-redeemer.”

**Help with kinsman-redeemer:** This is a close relative with responsibilities to help protect a family and its property.

**Help with clothing:** Ruth’s request uses covering with clothing as a picture of protection and family commitment.

## Ruth 3:10

Boaz said, “May the LORD bless you, my daughter. 📖

You have shown more kindness now than before.

You did not run after younger men, whether rich or poor.

## Ruth 3:11

Do not be afraid, my daughter. 📖

I will do whatever you ask.

Everyone in my town knows you are a woman of good character.

## Ruth 3:12

I am indeed a kinsman-redeemer, but another redeemer is a nearer relative than I am. 📖

**Help with redeemer:** Here, this means a relative with responsibilities to protect the family and its property.

## Ruth 3:13

Stay here tonight. 📖

If he wants to redeem you in the morning, good.

Let him do it.

If he does not want to, as surely as the LORD lives, I will redeem you.

Lie here until morning.”

**Help with redeem:** Here, redeem means carry out the close relative’s responsibility to protect Ruth’s family and inheritance.

## Ruth 3:14

Ruth lay at Boaz’s feet until morning. 📖

She got up before anyone else could recognise her.

Boaz said, “Do not let people know that a woman came to the threshing floor.”

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where people separate grain from its covering.

## Ruth 3:15

Boaz told Ruth, “Bring your shawl and hold it out.” 📖

Ruth did so, and Boaz poured 6 measures of barley into it and placed it on her.

Then Boaz went into the city.

**Help with shawl:** A shawl is a large cloth worn around the shoulders.

**Help with barley:** Barley is a grain grown for food.

**About measures:** The source gives no name for the measure used here.

## Ruth 3:16

When Ruth returned to Naomi, Naomi asked, “How did it go, my daughter?” 📖

Ruth told Naomi everything Boaz had done for her.

## Ruth 3:17

Ruth said, “He gave me these 6 measures of barley. 📖

He told me not to return to my mother-in-law empty-handed.”

**Help with barley:** Barley is a grain grown for food.

**Help with mother-in-law:** A mother-in-law is the mother of a person’s husband or wife.

## Ruth 3:18

Naomi said, “Wait, my daughter, until you learn how things turn out. 📖

Boaz will not rest until he settles the matter today.”
