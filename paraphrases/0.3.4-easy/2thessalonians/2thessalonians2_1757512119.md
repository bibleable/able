# 2 Thessalonians 2 — Stand firm in the truth

**About this chapter**

Paul corrects a claim that the day of the Lord has already come.

He describes the man of lawlessness and urges the believers to stand firm.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:1

Brothers and sisters, here is our request about our Lord Jesus Christ's coming and our gathering to him. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 2:2

Do not be easily shaken or alarmed by a spirit, message, or letter that seems to come from us. 📖

It may claim that the day of the Lord has already come.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:3

Do not let anyone trick you in any way. 📖

That day will not come until the rebellion happens and the man of lawlessness is revealed.

He is the son of destruction.

**Help with words:** Lawlessness means rejecting God's law.

Son of destruction describes someone destined for destruction.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:4

He will oppose and lift himself above every so-called god or object of worship. 📖

He will sit in God's temple and claim to be God.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:5

Do you not remember that I told you this when I was still with you? 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 2:6

You know what is holding him back now, so he may be revealed at the proper time. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 2:7

The mystery of lawlessness is already at work. 📖

But the one holding it back will continue until he is taken out of the way.

**Help with a word:** Mystery means something hidden.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:8

Then the lawless one will be revealed. 📖

The Lord Jesus will kill him with the breath of his mouth and destroy him by his glorious coming.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:9

The lawless one's coming will be through Satan's work, with every kind of power, sign, and false wonder. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 2:10

He will use every wicked deception against those who are perishing. 📖

They refused to love the truth that would have saved them.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:11

For this reason, God will send them a powerful delusion so they believe the lie. 📖

**Help with a word:** Delusion means believing something false as if it were true.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:12

Then judgment will come on everyone who disbelieved the truth and delighted in evil. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 2:13

But we should always thank God for you, brothers and sisters loved by the Lord. 📖

God chose you from the beginning to be saved through the Spirit making you holy and through faith in truth.

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:14

God called you to this through our good news so you could share our Lord Jesus Christ's glory. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 2:15

So, brothers and sisters, stand firm. 📖

Hold on to the traditions we taught you, whether by speaking or by letter.

**Help with a word:** Traditions here means teachings passed on to them.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:16

We pray to our Lord Jesus Christ himself and God our Father. 📖

Our Father loved us and gave us lasting comfort and good hope through his kindness.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:17

May they encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good word and action. 📖
