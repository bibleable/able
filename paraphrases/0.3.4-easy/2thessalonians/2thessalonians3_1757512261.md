# 2 Thessalonians 3 — Pray, work, and keep doing good

**About this chapter**

Paul asks for prayer and gives instructions about work and disorderly conduct.

He ends with a prayer for peace and his own written greeting.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:1

Finally, brothers and sisters, pray for us. 📖

Ask that the Lord's word will spread quickly and be honoured, just as it was among you.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:2

Pray that we may be rescued from wicked and evil people. 📖

Not everyone has faith.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:3

But the Lord is faithful. 📖

He will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:4

We are confident in the Lord that you do what we command and will keep doing it. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 3:5

May the Lord guide your hearts into God's love and Christ's endurance. 📖

**Help with a word:** Endurance means continuing through difficulty without giving up.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:6

Brothers and sisters, we command you in our Lord Jesus Christ's name. 📖

Keep away from any fellow believer living in a disorderly way against the teaching you received from us.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:7

You know how you should follow our example. 📖

We did not live in a disorderly way among you.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:8

We did not eat anyone's food without paying. 📖

We worked hard night and day so we would not burden any of you.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:9

We had the right to receive support. 📖

But we wanted to give you an example to follow.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:10

Even when we were with you, we gave this command. 📖

“Anyone unwilling to work must not eat.”

**Help with the wording:** Paul says unwilling to work, rather than unable to work.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:11

We hear that some of you live in a disorderly way. 📖

They do no work, but interfere in other people's business.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:12

By our Lord Jesus Christ, we command and urge such people to work quietly and earn their own living. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 3:13

But, brothers and sisters, do not grow tired of doing good. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 3:14

Take note of anyone not obeying the instructions in this letter. 📖

Do not associate with that person, so they may be ashamed.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:15

But treat them as a fellow believer to warn, rather than an enemy. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 3:16

May the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times and in every way. 📖

May the Lord be with you all.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:17

I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. 📖

This is my mark in every letter.

This is how I write.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:18

May our Lord Jesus Christ's kindness be with you all. 📖
