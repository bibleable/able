# 2 Thessalonians 1 — God judges rightly

**About this chapter**

Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy encourage believers who are suffering.

They speak about God's judgment and pray for the believers.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:1

This letter is from Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy. 📖

It is for the Thessalonian church in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:2

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 1:3

Brothers and sisters, we ought to thank God for you always. 📖

This is right, because your faith keeps growing and your love for each other keeps increasing.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:4

We proudly tell God's churches about your endurance and faith through all the persecution and suffering you bear. 📖

**Help with words:** Endurance means continuing through difficulty without giving up.

Persecution means being treated cruelly for your faith.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:5

All this clearly shows that God judges rightly. 📖

You will be counted worthy of God's kingdom, for which you suffer.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:6

It is right for God to repay those who trouble you with trouble. 📖

## 2 Thessalonians 1:7

He will give relief to you who suffer and to us too. 📖

This will happen when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his mighty angels.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:8

He will come in blazing fire. 📖

He will punish those who do not know God and do not obey our Lord Jesus's good news.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:9

Their punishment will be eternal destruction. 📖

They will be separated from the Lord's presence and the glory of his power.

**Help with words:** Eternal means lasting for ever.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:10

On that day, he will come to be honoured among his holy people and admired by all who believe. 📖

This includes you, because you believed our testimony.

**Help with a word:** Testimony is a witness's account of what is true.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:11

That is why we always pray for you. 📖

May our God count you worthy of his call.

May his power fulfil every good desire and every work of faith in you.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:12

Then our Lord Jesus's name will be honoured in you, and you in him. 📖

This is according to the kindness of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
