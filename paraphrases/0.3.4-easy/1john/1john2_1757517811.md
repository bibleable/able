# 1 John 2 — Remain in Christ

**About this chapter**

John teaches about obedience, love, and remaining in Christ.

He warns about people who deny the Father and the Son.

## 1 John 2:1

My little children, I write so you will not sin. 📖

But if anyone sins, we have someone who speaks for us before the Father.

He is Jesus Christ, the Righteous One.

**Help with words:** Righteous means right before God.

John calls the believers his children to express his care for them.

## 1 John 2:2

Jesus himself is the sacrifice that deals with our sins. 📖

He deals with the sins of the whole world, as well as ours.

## 1 John 2:3

We can be sure we know him if we keep his commands. 📖

## 1 John 2:4

Anyone saying, “I know him,” while not keeping his commands is a liar. 📖

The truth is not in that person.

## 1 John 2:5

But if anyone keeps his word, God's love has truly been made complete in that person. 📖

This is how we know we are in him.

## 1 John 2:6

Anyone claiming to remain in him must live as Jesus lived. 📖

## 1 John 2:7

Dear friends, I am writing an old command you have had from the beginning, rather than a new one. 📖

This command is the message you heard.

## 1 John 2:8

Yet I am also writing a new command, which is true in Christ and in you. 📖

The darkness is passing away, and the true light is already shining.

## 1 John 2:9

Anyone claiming to be in the light while hating a fellow believer is still in darkness. 📖

## 1 John 2:10

Anyone loving a fellow believer remains in the light. 📖

There is no cause of stumbling in that person.

**Help with this picture:** Stumbling pictures falling into wrongdoing.

## 1 John 2:11

Anyone hating a fellow believer is in darkness and walks in darkness. 📖

They do not know where they are going, because darkness has blinded their eyes.

**Help with this picture:** Darkness pictures losing the way through hatred.

## 1 John 2:12

Little children, I write to you because your sins have been forgiven through his name. 📖

## 1 John 2:13

Fathers, I write because you know the One who is from the beginning. 📖

Young men, I write because you have overcome the evil one.

Children, I have written because you know the Father.

## 1 John 2:14

Fathers, I have written because you know the One who is from the beginning. 📖

Young men, I have written because you are strong, God's word remains in you, and you overcame the evil one.

## 1 John 2:15

Do not love the world or anything in it. 📖

If anyone loves the world, the Father's love is not in that person.

## 1 John 2:16

The world's desires of the flesh, desires of the eyes, and pride of life do not come from the Father. 📖

They come from the world.

**Help with these words:** John describes desires and pride opposed to God.

## 1 John 2:17

The world and its desires are passing away. 📖

But anyone doing what God wants remains for ever.

## 1 John 2:18

Children, it is the last hour. 📖

You heard that the antichrist is coming.

Now many antichrists have appeared.

That is how we know it is the last hour.

**Help with a word:** Antichrist means someone opposed to Christ.

## 1 John 2:19

They left us, but they did not belong to us. 📖

If they had belonged, they would have stayed with us.

Their leaving showed that none of them belonged to us.

## 1 John 2:20

But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and all of you know the truth. 📖

**Help with a word:** Anointing pictures a gift from the Holy One that teaches and remains with believers.

Verse 27 continues this picture.

## 1 John 2:21

I write because you know the truth, rather than because you do not know it. 📖

No lie comes from the truth.

## 1 John 2:22

Who is the liar, if not the person denying that Jesus is the Christ? 📖

This is the antichrist, denying the Father and the Son.

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## 1 John 2:23

Anyone denying the Son does not have the Father. 📖

Anyone acknowledging the Son has the Father too.

## 1 John 2:24

Let the message you heard from the beginning remain in you. 📖

If it does, you will remain in the Son and in the Father.

## 1 John 2:25

He himself promised us life that lasts for ever. 📖

## 1 John 2:26

I have written about those who are trying to trick you. 📖

## 1 John 2:27

The anointing you received from him remains in you. 📖

You do not need anyone to teach you.

His anointing teaches you about everything and is true, not false.

Remain in him, as you have been taught.

## 1 John 2:28

Now, little children, remain in Christ. 📖

Then, when he appears, we may be confident and unashamed before him at his coming.

## 1 John 2:29

If you know he is righteous, you know that everyone practising righteousness has been born of him. 📖
