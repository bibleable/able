# 1 John 3 — Love through actions

**About this chapter**

John describes God's children and the difference between right living and sin.

He urges believers to love through actions, following Jesus's example.

## 1 John 3:1

Look at the love the Father has given us! 📖

We are called God's children, and that is what we are.

The world does not know us because it did not know him.

## 1 John 3:2

Dear friends, we are God's children now. 📖

What we will be has not yet been shown.

We know that when Christ appears, we will be like him, because we will see him as he is.

## 1 John 3:3

Everyone with this hope in Christ makes themselves pure, just as Christ is pure. 📖

## 1 John 3:4

Anyone practising sin also breaks God's law. 📖

Sin is breaking God's law.

## 1 John 3:5

You know that Christ appeared to take away sins. 📖

There is no sin in him.

## 1 John 3:6

No one remaining in Christ keeps on sinning. 📖

No one continuing to sin has seen him or known him.

## 1 John 3:7

Little children, do not let anyone trick you. 📖

Anyone practising righteousness is righteous, just as Christ is righteous.

**Help with a word:** Righteousness means being right before God and living rightly.

## 1 John 3:8

Anyone practising sin belongs to the devil, because the devil has sinned from the beginning. 📖

God's Son appeared to destroy the devil's works.

## 1 John 3:9

Anyone born of God refuses to keep practising sin, because God's seed remains in that person. 📖

They cannot go on sinning, because they have been born of God.

**Help with this picture:** Seed pictures the source of the new life God gives.

## 1 John 3:10

This shows the difference between God's children and the devil's children. 📖

Anyone not practising righteousness is not of God.

Neither is anyone who does not love a fellow believer.

## 1 John 3:11

This is the message you heard from the beginning. 📖

We should love each other.

## 1 John 3:12

Do not be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one and murdered his brother. 📖

Why did he murder him?

Cain's actions were evil, but his brother's were righteous.

## 1 John 3:13

Brothers and sisters, do not be surprised if the world hates you. 📖

## 1 John 3:14

We know we have passed from death to life because we love our fellow believers. 📖

Anyone who does not love remains in death.

## 1 John 3:15

Anyone who hates a fellow believer is a murderer. 📖

You know that life lasting for ever does not remain in a murderer.

## 1 John 3:16

We know love by this: Jesus gave his life for us. 📖

We should also give our lives for our fellow believers.

## 1 John 3:17

Someone with possessions may see a fellow believer in need but refuse to show care. 📖

How can God's love remain in that person?

## 1 John 3:18

Little children, let us love through actions and truth, rather than only through words and speech. 📖

## 1 John 3:19

By this, we will know we belong to the truth and reassure our hearts before God. 📖

## 1 John 3:20

Even if our hearts condemn us, God is greater than our hearts and knows everything. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Our hearts condemning us means feeling accused within ourselves.

## 1 John 3:21

Dear friends, if our hearts do not condemn us, we have confidence before God. 📖

## 1 John 3:22

Then we receive whatever we ask from him because we keep his commands and do what pleases him. 📖

## 1 John 3:23

His command is to believe in the name of his Son, Jesus Christ. 📖

We must also love each other, just as he commanded.

## 1 John 3:24

Anyone keeping his commands remains in God, and God remains in that person. 📖

We know he remains in us by the Spirit he gave us.
