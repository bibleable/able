# 1 John 4 — God is love

**About this chapter**

John teaches believers to test what they hear and to love each other.

## 1 John 4:1

Dear friends, do not believe every spirit. 📖

Test whether the spirits come from God.

Many false prophets are in the world.

**Help with words:** False prophets claim to speak for God but teach lies.

## 1 John 4:2

This is how to recognise God's Spirit. 📖

A spirit from God acknowledges that Jesus Christ came as a real human being.

## 1 John 4:3

A spirit that refuses to acknowledge Jesus is not from God. 📖

It is the spirit of the antichrist.

You heard it was coming.

It is already in the world.

**Help with a word:** Antichrist means someone opposed to Christ.

## 1 John 4:4

Dear children, you belong to God and have defeated these false teachers. 📖

The one in you is greater than the one in the world.

## 1 John 4:5

They belong to the world. 📖

They speak from the world's view, and the world listens.

## 1 John 4:6

We belong to God. 📖

Anyone who knows God listens to us.

Anyone who does not belong to God refuses to listen.

This shows the difference between the Spirit of truth and a spirit of lies.

## 1 John 4:7

Dear friends, let us love each other. 📖

Love comes from God.

Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.

## 1 John 4:8

Anyone who does not love does not know God. 📖

God is love.

## 1 John 4:9

God showed his love by sending his one and only Son into the world. 📖

Through him we can live.

## 1 John 4:10

Love begins with God loving us, not with us loving God. 📖

He sent his Son as the sacrifice that deals with our sins.

**Help with a word:** Sins are wrongs against God.

## 1 John 4:11

Dear friends, God loved us so much. 📖

We should love each other too.

## 1 John 4:12

No one has ever seen God. 📖

If we love each other, God lives in us.

His love reaches its full purpose in us.

## 1 John 4:13

God has given us his Spirit. 📖

This shows that we live in him and he lives in us.

## 1 John 4:14

We have seen and tell others this truth. 📖

The Father sent his Son to be the Saviour of the world.

**Help with a word:** A Saviour rescues people.

## 1 John 4:15

Anyone who acknowledges Jesus as God's Son lives in God. 📖

God lives in that person too.

## 1 John 4:16

We know and trust God's love for us. 📖

God is love.

Anyone who lives in love lives in God, and God in them.

## 1 John 4:17

Love reaches its full purpose among us in this way. 📖

We can face the day of judgment with confidence.

In this world, we are like Jesus.

## 1 John 4:18

Love has no room for fear. 📖

Complete love drives fear away, because fear expects punishment.

Anyone still afraid of punishment has not yet been made complete in love.

## 1 John 4:19

We love because God loved us first. 📖

## 1 John 4:20

Someone who claims to love God but hates a fellow believer is lying. 📖

They have seen that person.

If they cannot love that person, they cannot love the God they have not seen.

## 1 John 4:21

God gave us this command. 📖

Anyone who loves God must also love fellow believers.
