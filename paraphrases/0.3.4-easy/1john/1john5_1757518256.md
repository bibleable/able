# 1 John 5 — Life in God's Son

**About this chapter**

John writes about faith in Jesus, God's testimony, prayer, and life that lasts for ever.

## 1 John 5:1

Everyone believing Jesus is the Christ has been born of God. 📖

Everyone loving the Father also loves those born of him.

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## 1 John 5:2

We know we love God's children when we love God and keep his commands. 📖

## 1 John 5:3

Loving God means keeping his commands. 📖

His commands are not a heavy burden.

## 1 John 5:4

Everyone born of God overcomes the world. 📖

Our faith is the victory that has overcome the world.

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in Christ here.

## 1 John 5:5

Who overcomes the world? 📖

Only the person believing Jesus is God's Son.

## 1 John 5:6

Jesus Christ is the One who came by water and blood. 📖

He came by water and blood, rather than water alone.

The Spirit testifies to this, because the Spirit is the truth.

**Help with a word:** Testify means to speak as a witness to what is true.

## 1 John 5:7

There are three witnesses. 📖

## 1 John 5:8

They are the Spirit, the water, and the blood. 📖

These three agree.

## 1 John 5:9

Even if we accept human testimony, God's testimony is greater. 📖

This is the testimony God has given about his Son.

## 1 John 5:10

Anyone believing in God's Son has this testimony within them. 📖

Anyone not believing God makes him out to be a liar.

They have not believed God's testimony about his Son.

## 1 John 5:11

This is the testimony. 📖

God has given us life that lasts for ever, and this life is in his Son.

## 1 John 5:12

Anyone who has the Son has life. 📖

Anyone who does not have God's Son does not have life.

## 1 John 5:13

I write to you who believe in the name of God's Son. 📖

I want you to know you have life that lasts for ever.

## 1 John 5:14

This is our confidence before God. 📖

If we ask anything according to what he wants, he hears us.

## 1 John 5:15

If we know he hears whatever we ask, we know we already have what we asked him for. 📖

## 1 John 5:16

If you see a fellow believer committing a sin not leading to death, ask God. 📖

God will give life to those committing this kind of sin.

There is a sin leading to death.

I am not saying you should ask about that sin.

## 1 John 5:17

All wrongdoing is sin, yet there is sin that does not lead to death. 📖

## 1 John 5:18

We know that anyone born of God does not keep on sinning. 📖

The One born of God protects that person, and the evil one cannot touch them.

## 1 John 5:19

We know that we belong to God. 📖

The whole world is under the evil one's power.

## 1 John 5:20

We know God's Son has come and given us understanding to know the One who is true. 📖

We are in the One who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ.

He is the true God and life that lasts for ever.

## 1 John 5:21

Little children, keep away from idols. 📖

**Help with a word:** An idol is something worshipped in God's place.
