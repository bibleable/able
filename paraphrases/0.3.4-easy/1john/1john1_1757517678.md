# 1 John 1 — Walk in the light

**About this chapter**

John writes about the Word of life, Jesus Christ.

He describes sharing life with God and being honest about sin.

## 1 John 1:1

We write about the One who was from the beginning, the Word of life. 📖

We heard him, saw him with our own eyes, looked closely at him, and touched him with our hands.

## 1 John 1:2

This life was revealed, and we have seen it. 📖

We testify to it and tell you about the life that lasts for ever.

This life was with the Father and was made known to us.

## 1 John 1:3

We tell you what we have seen and heard so you can share life with us. 📖

We share this life with the Father and his Son, Jesus Christ.

## 1 John 1:4

We write these things so our joy may be complete. 📖

## 1 John 1:5

This is the message we heard from him and tell you. 📖

God is light.

There is no darkness in him at all.

**Help with this picture:** Light pictures God's truth and goodness.

## 1 John 1:6

If we say we share life with God but walk in darkness, we lie and do not practise the truth. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Walking in darkness pictures living in wrongdoing.

## 1 John 1:7

If we walk in the light, as God is in the light, we share life with each other. 📖

Then the blood of Jesus, his Son, cleanses us from all sin.

**Help with words:** Jesus's blood refers to his death for us.

Sin means wrongdoing against God.

## 1 John 1:8

If we say we have no sin, we fool ourselves. 📖

The truth is not in us.

## 1 John 1:9

If we confess our sins, God will forgive them and cleanse us from all wrongdoing. 📖

He is faithful and just.

**Help with words:** Confess means admit what we have done wrong.

Just means fair and right.

## 1 John 1:10

If we say we have not sinned, we make God out to be a liar. 📖

His word is not in us.
