# 2 Samuel 23 — David’s last words and mighty men

**About this chapter**

David gives his last words.

This chapter also names his mighty fighting men and tells what they did.

## 2 Samuel 23:1

These are David’s last words. 📖

“This is the message of David son of Jesse, the man raised on high.

The God of Jacob anointed him.

He is Israel’s sweet writer of songs of praise.

**Help with anointed:** Anointing means setting someone apart for a role, often by putting oil on them.

## 2 Samuel 23:2

The Spirit of the LORD spoke through me. 📖

His word was on my tongue.

**Help with this picture:** David says God gave him words to speak.

## 2 Samuel 23:3

The God of Israel spoke. 📖

The Rock of Israel said to me, ‘A ruler who judges the people fairly rules with fear of God.

**Help with Rock:** This pictures God’s strength and protection.

**Help with fear of God:** This means deep respect for God and his authority.

## 2 Samuel 23:4

Such a ruler is like morning light when the sun rises in a cloudless sky. 📖

He is like the brightness after rain on new grass growing from the earth.’

**Help with these pictures:** David compares good rule to light and fresh growth.

## 2 Samuel 23:5

Is my family not right with God? 📖

God has made a covenant with me that lasts forever.

Every part is in order and secure.

Will God not bring about my full salvation and everything I desire?

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

**Help with salvation:** Salvation means being saved.

## 2 Samuel 23:6

But worthless people are all like thorns pushed aside. 📖

Thorns cannot be gathered by hand.

**About this picture:** David uses thorns to picture people he condemns.

This is part of David’s message.

## 2 Samuel 23:7

Anyone touching them needs iron or the shaft of a spear. 📖

Fire burns them to ashes where they lie.”

**Help with shaft:** A shaft is a spear’s long handle.

## 2 Samuel 23:8

These are the names of David’s mighty men. 📖

Josheb-basshebeth the Tahchemonite was chief of the Three.

He used his spear against 800 men and killed them at one time.

**Help with the Three:** This is a title for a group of mighty fighting men.

## 2 Samuel 23:9

Next in command was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, one of the 3 mighty men. 📖

He went with David to challenge the Philistines gathered for battle at Pas-dammim.

The men of Israel pulled back.

## 2 Samuel 23:10

Eleazar stood his ground and struck the Philistines. 📖

He fought until his hand grew tired and stuck to his sword.

The LORD brought about a great victory that day.

The troops returned, but only to take goods from the dead.

## 2 Samuel 23:11

After Eleazar came Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. 📖

The Philistines gathered near a field full of lentils.

Israel’s troops ran away from them.

**Help with lentils:** Lentils are small seeds grown for food.

## 2 Samuel 23:12

Shammah stood in the middle of the field and defended it. 📖

He struck down the Philistines.

The LORD brought about a great victory.

## 2 Samuel 23:13

At harvest time, 3 of the 30 chief men went down to David at the cave of Adullam. 📖

A group of Philistines was camping in the Valley of Rephaim.

## 2 Samuel 23:14

David was then in the stronghold. 📖

Philistine soldiers were stationed at Bethlehem.

**Help with stronghold:** A stronghold is a place protected from attack.

## 2 Samuel 23:15

David longed for water. 📖

He said, “If only someone would bring me water from the well near Bethlehem’s gate!”

## 2 Samuel 23:16

The 3 mighty men broke through the Philistine camp. 📖

They drew water from the well near Bethlehem’s gate and brought it to David.

But David would not drink it.

Instead, he poured it out to the LORD.

## 2 Samuel 23:17

David said, “LORD, I could never do this! 📖

Is this not the blood of the men who risked their lives?”

David refused to drink it.

These were the great deeds of the 3 mighty men.

**Help with blood:** David compares this water to blood because getting it put the men’s lives in danger.

## 2 Samuel 23:18

Abishai, Joab’s brother and Zeruiah’s son, was chief of the Three. 📖

He used his spear against 300 men and killed them.

He became famous along with the Three.

**Help with the Three:** This is the title used for mighty fighting men in this account.

## 2 Samuel 23:19

Was Abishai not more honoured than the Three? 📖

He became their commander, although he was not included among the Three.

**About the Three:** The group title and the distinction are kept as the source gives them.

## 2 Samuel 23:20

Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a brave man from Kabzeel who did many great deeds. 📖

He killed 2 champions of Moab.

On a snowy day, he went down into a pit and killed a lion.

**Help with champions:** Here, champions are strong fighters.

## 2 Samuel 23:21

Benaiah also killed a huge Egyptian man. 📖

The Egyptian held a spear, but Benaiah came against him with a club.

Benaiah grabbed the spear from the Egyptian’s hand.

Benaiah killed him with his own spear.

## 2 Samuel 23:22

These were the great deeds of Benaiah son of Jehoiada. 📖

He became famous along with the 3 mighty men.

## 2 Samuel 23:23

Benaiah was the most honoured among the Thirty, but he did not become one of the Three. 📖

David put him in charge of his guard.

**Help with these titles:** The Thirty and the Three name groups of fighting men.

## 2 Samuel 23:24

These men belonged to the Thirty: 📖

Asahel, Joab’s brother.

Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem.

**Help with the Thirty:** This is a title for a group of fighting men.

## 2 Samuel 23:25

The list continues with Shammah the Harodite. 📖

Elika the Harodite.

## 2 Samuel 23:26

Helez the Paltite. 📖

Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite.

## 2 Samuel 23:27

Abiezer the Anathothite. 📖

Mebunnai the Hushathite.

## 2 Samuel 23:28

Zalmon the Ahohite. 📖

Maharai the Netophathite.

## 2 Samuel 23:29

Heled son of Baanah the Netophathite. 📖

Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah of the Benjamites.

## 2 Samuel 23:30

Benaiah the Pirathonite. 📖

Hiddai from the brooks of Gaash.

**Help with brooks:** Brooks are small streams.

## 2 Samuel 23:31

Abi-albon the Arbathite. 📖

Azmaveth the Barhumite.

## 2 Samuel 23:32

The sons of Jashen.

Jonathan,

## 2 Samuel 23:33

son of Shammah the Hararite. 📖

Ahiam son of Sharar the Hararite.

Eliphelet son of Ahasbai the Maacathite. 📖

## 2 Samuel 23:35

Hezro the Carmelite. 📖

Igal son of Nathan of Zobah. 📖

Bani the Gadite.

## 2 Samuel 23:37

Naharai the Beerothite, who carried the weapons of Joab son of Zeruiah.

## 2 Samuel 23:38

Ira the Ithrite. 📖

Gareb the Ithrite.

## 2 Samuel 23:39

And Uriah the Hittite. 📖

There were 37 in all.
