# 2 Samuel 17 — Hushai’s advice and David’s escape

**About this chapter**

Absalom chooses Hushai’s advice over Ahithophel’s.

Messengers warn David, and supporters bring supplies.

## 2 Samuel 17:1

Ahithophel told Absalom, “Let me choose 12,000 men and chase David tonight. 📖

## 2 Samuel 17:2

I will attack while he is weak and tired and throw him into panic. 📖

Everyone with him will flee.

I will kill only the king.

## 2 Samuel 17:3

I will bring everyone back to you like a bride returning to her husband. 📖

You seek only one man’s life.

Then all the people will be at peace.”

**Help with bride:** A bride is a woman at the time of her marriage.

Ahithophel compares the people’s return to a marriage reunion.

## 2 Samuel 17:4

This plan seemed good to Absalom and all Israel’s elders. 📖

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 2 Samuel 17:5

Absalom said, “Call Hushai the Archite too. 📖

Let us hear his advice.”

## 2 Samuel 17:6

Hushai came, and Absalom told him Ahithophel’s plan. 📖

Absalom asked, “Should we do it?

If not, what do you advise?”

## 2 Samuel 17:7

Hushai answered, “Ahithophel’s advice is not good this time.” 📖

## 2 Samuel 17:8

Hushai continued, “You know your father and his men. 📖

They are mighty and fierce, like a wild bear robbed of her cubs.

Your father is a soldier who will not spend the night with the troops.

## 2 Samuel 17:9

He must already be hiding in a cave or elsewhere. 📖

If some of your men die first, people will hear and say this.

‘Absalom’s followers have been slaughtered!’

## 2 Samuel 17:10

Even the bravest soldier with a lion’s heart will melt with fear. 📖

All Israel knows your father is mighty and has brave men with him.

**Help with lion’s heart:** This pictures great courage.

**Help with melt:** Here, melt pictures losing courage.

## 2 Samuel 17:11

I advise gathering all Israel to you, from Dan to Beersheba, as many as the sand beside the sea. 📖

You yourself should lead them into battle.

## 2 Samuel 17:12

We will attack David wherever we find him. 📖

We will fall on him like dew on the ground.

Not one of the men with him will survive.

**Help with dew:** Dew is moisture that covers cool surfaces.

It pictures the attack spreading over David and his men.

## 2 Samuel 17:13

If he retreats into a city, all Israel will bring ropes. 📖

We will drag the city down into the valley until not even a pebble is left.”

## 2 Samuel 17:14

Absalom and all Israel’s men said, “Hushai the Archite’s advice is better than Ahithophel’s.” 📖

The LORD had planned to defeat Ahithophel’s good advice to bring disaster on Absalom.

## 2 Samuel 17:15

Hushai told Zadok and Abiathar the priests both plans. 📖

He told them what Ahithophel had advised Absalom and Israel’s elders, and what he himself had advised.

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 2 Samuel 17:16

Hushai said, “Send David an urgent warning. 📖

Do not spend the night at the wilderness crossings.

Be sure to cross over, or the king and everyone with him will be swallowed up.”

**Help with crossings:** These were shallow places where people could cross the water.

**Help with swallowed up:** This pictures being destroyed.

## 2 Samuel 17:17

Jonathan and Ahimaaz stayed at En-rogel. 📖

A servant girl brought them information, which they took to King David.

They did not dare be seen entering the city.

## 2 Samuel 17:18

A young man saw them and told Absalom. 📖

They hurried to a man’s house in Bahurim.

He had a well in his courtyard, and they climbed down inside it.

## 2 Samuel 17:19

The man’s wife covered the well’s opening and scattered grain over the cover. 📖

No one knew anything about it.

## 2 Samuel 17:20

Absalom’s servants came to the woman and asked, “Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?” 📖

She answered, “They crossed the stream.”

The men searched but did not find them and returned to Jerusalem.

## 2 Samuel 17:21

After the men left, Ahimaaz and Jonathan climbed out of the well and went to tell David. 📖

They said, “Get up and cross the river now!

Ahithophel has advised this against you.”

## 2 Samuel 17:22

David and everyone with him got up and crossed the Jordan. 📖

By dawn, everyone had crossed.

## 2 Samuel 17:23

Ahithophel saw that his advice had not been followed. 📖

He saddled his donkey and went home to his hometown.

He put his affairs in order and hanged himself.

He died and was buried in his father’s tomb.

## 2 Samuel 17:24

David went to Mahanaim. 📖

Absalom crossed the Jordan with all Israel’s men.

## 2 Samuel 17:25

Absalom appointed Amasa to command the army instead of Joab. 📖

Amasa’s father was Ithra the Ishmaelite, who had married Abigail.

Abigail was Nahash’s daughter and the sister of Zeruiah, Joab’s mother.

## 2 Samuel 17:26

Absalom and the Israelites camped in Gilead. 📖

## 2 Samuel 17:27

When David reached Mahanaim, 3 men met him. 📖

They were Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the Ammonites, and Machir son of Ammiel from Lo-debar.

Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim also met him.

## 2 Samuel 17:28

They brought beds, basins, clay containers, wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, and lentils. 📖

**Help with barley and lentils:** Barley is a grain.

Lentils are small seeds grown for food.

## 2 Samuel 17:29

They also brought honey, curds, sheep, and cheese from the herd for David and his people to eat. 📖

They said, “The people have become hungry, tired, and thirsty in the wilderness.”

**Help with curds:** Curds are thickened parts of milk.
