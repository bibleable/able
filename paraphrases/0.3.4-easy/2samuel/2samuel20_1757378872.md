# 2 Samuel 20 — Sheba rebels and Joab kills Amasa

**About this chapter**

Sheba draws Israel’s men away from David.

Joab kills Amasa, pursues Sheba, and speaks with a wise woman at Abel-beth-maacah.

## 2 Samuel 20:1

A worthless Benjamite named Sheba son of Bichri happened to be there. 📖

He blew the ram’s horn and shouted, “We have no share in David, no inheritance in Jesse’s son!

Israel, every man to his tent!”

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

**Help with inheritance:** Here, this means a share in the king’s rule.

## 2 Samuel 20:2

All Israel’s men deserted David to follow Sheba son of Bichri. 📖

Judah’s men stayed with their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem.

## 2 Samuel 20:3

David returned to his Jerusalem palace and took the 10 concubines left to care for it. 📖

He put them in a guarded house and provided for them, but no longer had sex with them.

They remained confined until death, living as widows.

**Help with concubines:** Concubines were women in marriage-like relationships with fewer rights than wives.

**Help with widows:** Widows are women whose husbands have died.

Here, the women’s confined lives are compared to widowhood.

## 2 Samuel 20:4

The king told Amasa, “Gather Judah’s men to me within 3 days. 📖

Be here yourself too.”

## 2 Samuel 20:5

Amasa went to gather Judah, but took longer than the time he had been given. 📖

## 2 Samuel 20:6

David told Abishai, “Sheba son of Bichri will harm us more than Absalom. 📖

Take your lord’s servants and chase him.

Otherwise, he will find protected cities and escape us.”

## 2 Samuel 20:7

Joab’s men, the Cherethites, Pelethites, and all the mighty men marched from Jerusalem after Sheba son of Bichri. 📖

## 2 Samuel 20:8

At Gibeon’s great stone, Amasa joined them. 📖

Joab wore military clothing with a dagger fastened to his belt.

As he stepped forward, he slipped the dagger from its cover.

**Help with dagger:** A dagger is a short weapon with a blade.

## 2 Samuel 20:9

Joab asked Amasa, “Are you well, my brother?” 📖

With his right hand, Joab grabbed Amasa’s beard to kiss him.

## 2 Samuel 20:10

Amasa was not guarding against the dagger in Joab’s hand. 📖

Joab stabbed Amasa’s stomach, spilling his intestines onto the ground.

Amasa died, so Joab did not need to strike again.

Joab and his brother Abishai chased Sheba son of Bichri.

**Help with intestines:** Intestines are the long tubes inside the body that process food.

## 2 Samuel 20:11

One of Joab’s young men stood beside Amasa. 📖

He said, “Anyone who favours Joab and supports David should follow Joab!”

## 2 Samuel 20:12

Amasa lay in his blood in the road, and the troops kept stopping there. 📖

The young man dragged the body into a field and covered it with a garment.

## 2 Samuel 20:13

Once Amasa’s body was off the road, all the men followed Joab after Sheba son of Bichri. 📖

## 2 Samuel 20:14

Sheba travelled through all Israel’s tribes to Abel-beth-maacah and throughout the Berites’ region. 📖

The Berites gathered and followed him.

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

## 2 Samuel 20:15

Joab’s troops surrounded Sheba in Abel-beth-maacah. 📖

They built an attack ramp against the city’s outer defences.

All Joab’s troops battered the wall to knock it down.

**Help with attack ramp:** Soldiers piled up earth to reach a city’s defences.

## 2 Samuel 20:16

A wise woman called from the city, “Listen! 📖

Listen!

Please tell Joab to come here so I can speak to him.”

## 2 Samuel 20:17

Joab came near, and the woman asked, “Are you Joab?” 📖

He answered, “I am.”

She said, “Listen to your servant.”

He answered, “I am listening.”

## 2 Samuel 20:18

The woman said, “Long ago, people said, ‘Seek advice at Abel.’ 📖

That was how disputes were settled.

## 2 Samuel 20:19

I am among Israel’s peaceful and faithful people. 📖

But you are trying to destroy a city that is a mother in Israel.

Why would you swallow up the LORD’s inheritance?”

**Help with mother:** The woman pictures the city as an important source of care and life.

**Help with swallow up:** This means destroy.

**Help with inheritance:** Here, inheritance refers to what belongs to the LORD.

## 2 Samuel 20:20

Joab declared, “Never! 📖

I would never swallow up or destroy!

## 2 Samuel 20:21

That is not the case. 📖

Sheba son of Bichri, from Ephraim’s hill country, has raised his hand against King David.

Hand over this one man, and I will leave the city.”

The woman answered, “His head will be thrown over the wall to you.”

**Help with raised his hand:** This means rebelled against the king.

## 2 Samuel 20:22

The woman gave all the people wise advice. 📖

They cut off Sheba son of Bichri’s head and threw it to Joab.

Joab blew the ram’s horn, and his men left the city and went home.

Joab returned to the king in Jerusalem.

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

## 2 Samuel 20:23

Joab commanded Israel’s whole army. 📖

Benaiah son of Jehoiada commanded the Cherethites and Pelethites.

## 2 Samuel 20:24

Adoram managed the forced labour. 📖

Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder.

**Help with recorder:** This was an official responsible for records.

## 2 Samuel 20:25

Sheva was the scribe. 📖

Zadok and Abiathar were priests.

**Help with scribe:** A scribe is an official who writes and keeps records.

## 2 Samuel 20:26

Ira the Jairite was David’s priest. 📖
