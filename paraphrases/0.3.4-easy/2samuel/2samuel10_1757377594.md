# 2 Samuel 10 — War with the Ammonites and Arameans

**About this chapter**

Hanun humiliates David’s messengers.

David’s men fight the Ammonites and their hired allies.

## 2 Samuel 10:1

Later, the Ammonites’ king died, and his son Hanun became king. 📖

## 2 Samuel 10:2

David said, “I will show kindness to Hanun son of Nahash, as his father showed kindness to me.” 📖

David sent servants to comfort Hanun about his father’s death.

They arrived in the Ammonites’ land.

## 2 Samuel 10:3

The Ammonite princes told Hanun their lord, “David sent you comforters. 📖

Do you really think he respects your father?

Has he not sent his servants to explore the city, spy on it, and overthrow it?”

**Help with princes:** Princes are royal leaders.

**About the accusation:** These are the princes’ suspicions, not a confirmed purpose of David’s visit.

## 2 Samuel 10:4

Hanun took David’s servants and shaved off half of each man’s beard. 📖

He cut their clothes off at the hips and sent them away.

## 2 Samuel 10:5

David heard and sent messengers to meet the men, because they had been deeply humiliated. 📖

The king said, “Stay in Jericho until your beards grow back.

Then return.”

## 2 Samuel 10:6

The Ammonites realised they had become a stench to David. 📖

They hired 20,000 Aramean foot soldiers from Beth-rehob and Zoba.

They also hired 1,000 men from Maacah’s king and 12,000 from Tob.

**Help with stench:** This pictures becoming deeply offensive to David.

## 2 Samuel 10:7

David heard and sent Joab with the whole army of mighty men. 📖

## 2 Samuel 10:8

The Ammonites took battle positions at the city gate’s entrance. 📖

The Arameans of Zobah and Rehob and the men of Tob and Maacah stood separately in the open country.

## 2 Samuel 10:9

Joab saw enemy battle lines in front of him and behind him. 📖

He chose some of Israel’s best men and placed them against the Arameans.

## 2 Samuel 10:10

Joab put the remaining troops under his brother Abishai. 📖

Abishai placed them against the Ammonites.

## 2 Samuel 10:11

Joab said, “If the Arameans are too strong for me, come and help me. 📖

If the Ammonites are too strong for you, I will help you.

## 2 Samuel 10:12

Be strong. 📖

Let us fight bravely for our people and our God’s cities.

May the LORD do what is good in his sight.”

## 2 Samuel 10:13

Joab and his troops moved forward to fight the Arameans. 📖

The Arameans fled before him.

## 2 Samuel 10:14

The Ammonites saw the Arameans flee. 📖

They too fled before Abishai and went into the city.

Joab returned from fighting the Ammonites and came to Jerusalem.

## 2 Samuel 10:15

The Arameans saw that Israel had defeated them and gathered again. 📖

## 2 Samuel 10:16

Hadadezer sent messengers for more Arameans from beyond the Euphrates. 📖

They came to Helam, led by Shobach, the commander of Hadadezer’s army.

## 2 Samuel 10:17

David heard and gathered all Israel. 📖

He crossed the Jordan and went to Helam.

The Arameans took positions against David and fought him.

## 2 Samuel 10:18

The Arameans fled before Israel. 📖

David killed 700 chariot drivers and 40,000 foot soldiers.

He also struck Shobach, their army commander, who died there.

## 2 Samuel 10:19

The kings under Hadadezer saw that Israel had defeated them. 📖

They made peace with Israel and came under Israel’s rule.

The Arameans were afraid to help the Ammonites anymore.
