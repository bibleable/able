# 2 Samuel 19 — David returns as king

**About this chapter**

Joab confronts David about his grief.

David returns, meets people who come to him, and faces an argument between Israel and Judah.

## 2 Samuel 19:1

Joab heard, “The king is crying and grieving over Absalom.” 📖

## 2 Samuel 19:2

That day’s victory became grief for everyone. 📖

They had heard that the king was grieving for his son.

## 2 Samuel 19:3

They returned quietly to the city, like people slipping away in shame after fleeing a battle. 📖

## 2 Samuel 19:4

The king covered his face and cried at the top of his voice. 📖

“My son Absalom!

Absalom, my son, my son!”

## 2 Samuel 19:5

Joab entered the house and told the king, “Today you have disgraced all your servants who saved your life. 📖

They saved your sons, daughters, wives, and concubines.

**Help with concubines:** Concubines were women in marriage-like relationships with fewer rights than wives.

## 2 Samuel 19:6

You love those who hate you and hate those who love you! 📖

Today you have shown that your commanders and soldiers mean nothing to you.

I know you would be pleased if Absalom lived and all of us died!

## 2 Samuel 19:7

Get up now! 📖

Go out and comfort your servants.

I swear by the LORD: if you do not go, no man will stay with you tonight.

This will be worse than all your troubles from your youth until now!”

## 2 Samuel 19:8

The king got up and sat in the gate. 📖

The people heard the king was sitting there, and all came before him.

Meanwhile, each Israelite had fled home.

## 2 Samuel 19:9

Throughout Israel’s tribes, people argued about the king. 📖

“He rescued us from our enemies and the Philistines’ power.

But he has fled the land because of Absalom.

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

## 2 Samuel 19:10

Absalom, whom we anointed over us, died in battle. 📖

Why are you silent about bringing the king back?”

**Help with anointed:** This means set apart as king, marked by the use of oil.

## 2 Samuel 19:11

David sent a message to Zadok and Abiathar the priests. 📖

“Tell Judah’s elders that all Israel’s talk has reached the king where he is staying.

Ask, ‘Why should you be last to bring the king back to his palace?

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 2 Samuel 19:12

You are my brothers, my own flesh and blood. 📖

Why should you be last to bring back the king?’

**Help with flesh and blood:** This means they are related.

## 2 Samuel 19:13

Tell Amasa, ‘Are you not my flesh and blood? 📖

May God punish me very severely unless you command my army instead of Joab from now on!’”

**Help with flesh and blood:** This means they are related.

## 2 Samuel 19:14

David won all Judah’s men over as though they were one man. 📖

They sent the king word, “Return with all your servants.”

## 2 Samuel 19:15

The king returned and reached the Jordan. 📖

Judah’s men came to Gilgal to meet him and escort him across the Jordan.

## 2 Samuel 19:16

Shimei son of Gera, a Benjamite from Bahurim, hurried down with Judah’s men to meet King David. 📖

## 2 Samuel 19:17

1,000 Benjamites came with him. 📖

Ziba, the manager of Saul’s household, came with his 15 sons and 20 servants.

They hurried to the Jordan before the king.

## 2 Samuel 19:18

They crossed at the shallow crossing to bring the king’s household over and do what pleased him. 📖

Shimei son of Gera crossed the Jordan and fell before the king.

## 2 Samuel 19:19

Shimei said, “My lord, do not hold me guilty. 📖

Do not remember my wrongdoing when my lord the king left Jerusalem.

May the king not take it to heart.

## 2 Samuel 19:20

Your servant knows he has sinned. 📖

I am the first from all Joseph’s family to come down and meet my lord the king today.”

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong against God.

## 2 Samuel 19:21

Abishai son of Zeruiah said, “Should Shimei not die for cursing the LORD’s anointed one?” 📖

**Help with anointed one:** Here, this means David, chosen as king.

## 2 Samuel 19:22

David answered, “Sons of Zeruiah, what have I to do with you? 📖

Why oppose me today?

Should anyone in Israel be put to death today?

Do I not know that today I am king over Israel?”

## 2 Samuel 19:23

The king told Shimei, “You will not die.” 📖

The king swore an oath to him.

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise.

## 2 Samuel 19:24

Saul’s grandson Mephibosheth came down to meet the king. 📖

He had not cared for his feet, trimmed his moustache, or washed his clothes since the king left.

He had waited until the king returned safely.

## 2 Samuel 19:25

Mephibosheth came from Jerusalem to meet the king. 📖

The king asked, “Mephibosheth, why did you not go with me?”

## 2 Samuel 19:26

Mephibosheth answered, “My lord the king, I have difficulty walking. 📖

I said, ‘I will have my donkey saddled to ride with the king.’

But my servant Ziba tricked me.

## 2 Samuel 19:27

Ziba has told my lord the king lies about your servant. 📖

Yet my lord the king is like God’s angel.

Do what seems good to you.

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

**About the accounts:** This is Mephibosheth’s answer to the earlier report by Ziba.

The draft does not silently choose one account over the other.

## 2 Samuel 19:28

All my grandfather’s family deserves death from my lord the king. 📖

Yet you gave your servant a place among those eating at your table.

What right have I to keep appealing to the king?”

## 2 Samuel 19:29

The king answered, “Why say more? 📖

You and Ziba must divide the land.”

## 2 Samuel 19:30

Mephibosheth told the king, “Let Ziba take it all instead! 📖

My lord the king has returned safely to his own home.”

## 2 Samuel 19:31

Barzillai the Gileadite came from Rogelim to cross the Jordan with the king and send him on his way. 📖

## 2 Samuel 19:32

Barzillai was very old, 80 years of age. 📖

He was very wealthy and had provided for the king at Mahanaim.

## 2 Samuel 19:33

The king told Barzillai, “Cross over with me. 📖

I will provide for you beside me in Jerusalem.”

## 2 Samuel 19:34

Barzillai answered, “How many years have I left, that I should go to Jerusalem with the king? 📖

## 2 Samuel 19:35

I am 80 now. 📖

Can I tell what is good or bad?

Can your servant taste his food and drink?

Can I still hear men and women singing?

Why should I be another burden to my lord the king?

**About these questions:** Barzillai describes his own age and experience.

The draft does not make these statements about all older people.

## 2 Samuel 19:36

I will go only a short distance beyond the Jordan with the king. 📖

Why should the king give me such a reward?

## 2 Samuel 19:37

Please let your servant return to die in my own city, near my father’s and mother’s tomb. 📖

Here is your servant Chimham.

Let him cross with my lord the king.

Do for him whatever seems good to you.”

## 2 Samuel 19:38

The king answered, “Chimham will cross with me. 📖

I will do for him what seems good to you.

I will do whatever you desire of me.”

## 2 Samuel 19:39

All the people crossed the Jordan, and then the king crossed. 📖

The king kissed Barzillai and blessed him.

Barzillai went home.

## 2 Samuel 19:40

The king crossed to Gilgal with Chimham. 📖

All Judah’s troops and half Israel’s troops escorted the king.

## 2 Samuel 19:41

All Israel’s men came to the king. 📖

They asked, “Why did our brothers from Judah take you away secretly?

They brought the king, his household, and all David’s men across the Jordan.”

## 2 Samuel 19:42

All Judah’s men answered Israel’s men, “The king is our relative. 📖

Why does this anger you?

Have we eaten at his expense or taken anything for ourselves?”

## 2 Samuel 19:43

Israel’s men answered, “We have 10 shares in the king, so we have more claim to David than you. 📖

Why do you despise us?

Were we not first to speak of bringing our king back?”

But Judah’s men spoke more fiercely than Israel’s men.

**Help with despise:** Despise means treat with scorn.
