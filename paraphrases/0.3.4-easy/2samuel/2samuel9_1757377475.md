# 2 Samuel 9 — David shows kindness to Mephibosheth

**About this chapter**

David remembers Jonathan and asks about Saul’s surviving family.

Mephibosheth comes to David and receives land and a place at the king’s table.

## 2 Samuel 9:1

David asked, “Is anyone left from Saul’s family? 📖

I want to show kindness for Jonathan’s sake.”

## 2 Samuel 9:2

Saul’s family had a servant named Ziba. 📖

They called him to David.

The king asked, “Are you Ziba?”

Ziba answered, “I am your servant.”

## 2 Samuel 9:3

The king asked, “Is anyone left from Saul’s family to whom I can show God’s kindness?” 📖

Ziba answered, “Jonathan’s son is still alive.

Both his feet are disabled.”

## 2 Samuel 9:4

The king asked, “Where is he?” 📖

Ziba answered, “In Lo-debar, at the house of Machir son of Ammiel.”

## 2 Samuel 9:5

King David had him brought from Machir son of Ammiel’s house in Lo-debar. 📖

## 2 Samuel 9:6

Mephibosheth came to David and bowed with his face to the ground in respect.

Mephibosheth was Jonathan’s son.

Jonathan was Saul’s son. 📖

David said, “Mephibosheth!”

He answered, “I am your servant.”

## 2 Samuel 9:7

David said, “Do not fear. 📖

I will certainly show you kindness for the sake of your father Jonathan.

I will restore all your grandfather Saul’s land to you.

You will always eat at my table.”

## 2 Samuel 9:8

Mephibosheth bowed and said, “What is your servant? 📖

Why should you notice a dead dog like me?”

**Help with dead dog:** Mephibosheth uses a picture of low status to describe himself.

This is his speech, not a judgment of his worth.

## 2 Samuel 9:9

The king called Saul’s servant Ziba. 📖

David said, “I have given your master’s grandson everything that belonged to Saul and his whole family.

## 2 Samuel 9:10

You, your sons, and your servants must farm his land and bring in the harvest. 📖

Then your master’s grandson will have food.

But Mephibosheth, your master’s grandson, will always eat at my table.”

Ziba had 15 sons and 20 servants.

## 2 Samuel 9:11

Ziba told the king, “Your servant will do everything my lord the king has commanded.” 📖

Mephibosheth ate at David’s table like one of the king’s own sons.

## 2 Samuel 9:12

Mephibosheth had a young son named Mica. 📖

Everyone living in Ziba’s household served Mephibosheth.

## 2 Samuel 9:13

Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem because he always ate at the king’s table. 📖

Both his feet were disabled.
