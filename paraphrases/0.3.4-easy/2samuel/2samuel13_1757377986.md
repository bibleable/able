# 2 Samuel 13 — Amnon attacks Tamar and is killed

**About this chapter**

Amnon rapes his sister Tamar.

Absalom later arranges Amnon’s murder and flees.

## 2 Samuel 13:1

Later, David’s son Amnon fell in love with Tamar. 📖

Tamar was the beautiful sister of David’s son Absalom.

## 2 Samuel 13:2

Amnon felt so frustrated about his sister Tamar that he became ill. 📖

She was a virgin, and doing anything to her seemed impossible to him.

**Help with virgin:** A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

## 2 Samuel 13:3

Amnon had a friend named Jonadab, son of David’s brother Shimeah. 📖

Jonadab was very shrewd.

**Help with shrewd:** Here, shrewd means clever at making plans.

## 2 Samuel 13:4

Jonadab asked, “Why are you so sad every morning, although you are the king’s son? 📖

Will you not tell me?”

Amnon answered, “I love Tamar, my brother Absalom’s sister.”

## 2 Samuel 13:5

Jonadab said, “Lie on your bed and pretend to be ill. 📖

When your father visits, ask for your sister Tamar to give you food.

Ask her to prepare it where you can watch and eat from her hand.”

## 2 Samuel 13:6

Amnon lay down and pretended to be ill. 📖

The king visited him.

Amnon said, “Please let my sister Tamar make 2 cakes where I can watch.

Then I can eat from her hand.”

## 2 Samuel 13:7

David sent word to Tamar at the palace. 📖

David said, “Please go to your brother Amnon’s house and prepare a meal for him.”

## 2 Samuel 13:8

Tamar went to Amnon’s house, where he was lying down. 📖

She took dough, kneaded it, made cakes where he could see, and baked them.

**Help with kneaded:** Kneaded means worked dough by pressing and folding it.

## 2 Samuel 13:9

Tamar brought the pan and set it before Amnon, but he refused to eat. 📖

Amnon said, “Send everyone away!”

Everyone left.

## 2 Samuel 13:10

Amnon told Tamar, “Bring the food to the bedroom, so I can eat from your hand.” 📖

Tamar took the cakes she had made into her brother Amnon’s bedroom.

## 2 Samuel 13:11

When Tamar brought the food for him to eat, Amnon grabbed her. 📖

He said, “Come and have sex with me, my sister!”

## 2 Samuel 13:12

Tamar cried, “No, my brother! 📖

Do not violate me!

This must never be done in Israel.

Do not do this disgraceful thing!

**Help with violate me:** Tamar refuses the sexual attack.

## 2 Samuel 13:13

Where could I take my shame? 📖

You would be like one of Israel’s fools!

Please speak to the king.

He will not keep me from you.”

**About Tamar’s appeal:** These are Tamar’s words while resisting the attack.

The draft does not turn her statement into a ruling about marriage.

## 2 Samuel 13:14

Amnon would not listen. 📖

He was stronger than Tamar and raped her.

**Help with raped:** Raped means forced someone to have sex without consent.

## 2 Samuel 13:15

Then Amnon hated Tamar intensely. 📖

His hatred was greater than his earlier love.

He told her, “Get up!

Go away!”

## 2 Samuel 13:16

Tamar answered, “No! 📖

Sending me away is worse than the great wrong you have already done!”

Amnon would not listen.

## 2 Samuel 13:17

Amnon called his attendant. 📖

He said, “Throw this woman out and lock the door behind her!”

## 2 Samuel 13:18

The attendant threw Tamar out and locked the door. 📖

Tamar wore a robe of many colours.

The king’s virgin daughters wore such robes.

**Help with virgin:** A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

## 2 Samuel 13:19

Tamar put ashes on her head and tore her robe. 📖

She put her hand on her head and went away crying aloud.

**Help with ashes and torn clothes:** These were signs of grief.

## 2 Samuel 13:20

Her brother Absalom asked, “Has your brother Amnon been with you? 📖

Be quiet for now, my sister.

He is your brother.

Do not take this to heart.”

Tamar lived as a desolate woman in Absalom’s house.

**Help with desolate:** Here, desolate means devastated and left in sorrow.

**About Absalom’s words:** His response is retained as his speech.

It is not guidance for responding to sexual violence.

## 2 Samuel 13:21

King David heard everything and became furious. 📖

## 2 Samuel 13:22

Absalom said nothing to Amnon, good or bad. 📖

He hated Amnon because Amnon had violated his sister Tamar.

**Help with violated:** This refers to Amnon’s rape of Tamar.

## 2 Samuel 13:23

2 years later, Absalom’s sheepshearers were at Baal-hazor near Ephraim. 📖

Absalom invited all the king’s sons.

**Help with sheepshearers:** Sheepshearers cut wool from sheep.

## 2 Samuel 13:24

Absalom went to the king. 📖

He said, “Your servant has hired shearers.

Please will the king and his servants come with me?”

**Help with shearers:** Here, shearers are workers who cut wool from sheep.

## 2 Samuel 13:25

The king answered, “No, my son. 📖

We should not all go and become a burden to you.”

Absalom urged him, but the king would not go.

The king gave Absalom his blessing.

## 2 Samuel 13:26

Absalom said, “Then please let my brother Amnon come with us.” 📖

The king asked, “Why should he go with you?”

## 2 Samuel 13:27

Absalom urged the king, so the king sent Amnon and his other sons. 📖

## 2 Samuel 13:28

Absalom had ordered his young men, “Watch until wine makes Amnon cheerful. 📖

When I order you to strike him, kill him.

Do not fear.

Have I not commanded you?

Be brave and strong!”

## 2 Samuel 13:29

Absalom’s young men did to Amnon what Absalom ordered. 📖

The king’s other sons got up, each fleeing on his mule.

**Help with mule:** A mule is an animal with a horse and a donkey as parents.

## 2 Samuel 13:30

While they travelled, David heard, “Absalom has killed all the king’s sons! 📖

None is left!”

**About the report:** This report is incorrect, as the following verses show.

## 2 Samuel 13:31

The king stood up, tore his clothes, and lay on the ground. 📖

His servants stood beside him with their clothes torn.

**Help with torn clothes:** Tearing clothes was a sign of grief.

## 2 Samuel 13:32

Jonadab, son of David’s brother Shimeah, said, “My lord must not think all the king’s sons were killed. 📖

Only Amnon died.

Absalom planned this from the day Amnon violated Tamar.

**Help with violated:** This refers to Amnon’s rape of Tamar.

## 2 Samuel 13:33

My lord the king, do not take to heart the report that all your sons are dead. 📖

Only Amnon is dead.”

## 2 Samuel 13:34

Absalom had fled. 📖

The young watchman saw many people coming down the road west of him, along the hillside.

He told the king, “I see men coming from Horonaim’s direction, along the hillside.”

## 2 Samuel 13:35

Jonadab told the king, “Look, the king’s sons have arrived! 📖

It is just as your servant said.”

## 2 Samuel 13:36

As Jonadab finished speaking, the king’s sons came in, crying loudly. 📖

The king and all his servants also cried very bitterly.

## 2 Samuel 13:37

Absalom fled to Talmai son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. 📖

David grieved for his son every day.

## 2 Samuel 13:38

After fleeing to Geshur, Absalom stayed there for 3 years. 📖

## 2 Samuel 13:39

King David longed to go to Absalom. 📖

David had been comforted over Amnon’s death.
