# 2 Samuel 7 — The LORD’s promise and David’s prayer

**About this chapter**

David considers building a house for God.

The LORD speaks about David’s family and kingdom, and David responds in prayer.

## 2 Samuel 7:1

The king settled in his palace. 📖

The LORD had given him rest from all his surrounding enemies.

## 2 Samuel 7:2

David told Nathan the prophet, “I live in a cedar house, but God’s ark stays in a tent.” 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

**Help with cedar:** Cedar is a tree whose wood is used for building.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 7:3

Nathan told the king, “Do everything that is in your heart. 📖

The LORD is with you.”

## 2 Samuel 7:4

But that night, the LORD’s word came to Nathan. 📖

## 2 Samuel 7:5

The LORD said, “Go and tell my servant David this. 📖

The LORD asks, ‘Are you the one to build a house for me to live in?

## 2 Samuel 7:6

Since I brought Israel out of Egypt, I have not lived in a house. 📖

Until today, I have moved about with a tent as my home.

## 2 Samuel 7:7

I travelled with all Israel. 📖

Did I ever ask any leader appointed to shepherd my people Israel this?

Why have you not built me a cedar house?

**Help with shepherd:** Here, shepherd means lead and care for people.

**Help with cedar:** Cedar is a tree whose wood is used for building.

## 2 Samuel 7:8

Tell my servant David what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

I took you from the grassland, from following sheep, to rule my people Israel.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 2 Samuel 7:9

I have been with you wherever you went and removed all your enemies before you. 📖

I will make your name like the names of the greatest people in the land.

## 2 Samuel 7:10

I will provide a place for my people Israel and plant them there. 📖

They will live in their own place and no longer be disturbed.

Wicked people will no longer oppress them as they did at the beginning.

**Help with plant them:** This pictures giving people a settled home.

**Help with oppress:** Oppress means treat harshly and hold under power.

## 2 Samuel 7:11

They have oppressed them since I appointed judges over my people Israel. 📖

I will give you rest from all your enemies.

The LORD declares that he himself will establish a house for you.

**Help with house:** Here, house means David’s family line, not a building.

**Help with judges:** Judges were leaders of Israel before its kings.

## 2 Samuel 7:12

When your days are complete and you rest with your ancestors, I will raise up your descendant after you. 📖

He will come from your own body, and I will establish his kingdom.

**Help with rest with your ancestors:** This means die.

**Help with descendant:** A descendant is someone from a person’s later family.

## 2 Samuel 7:13

He will build a house for my Name. 📖

I will establish his kingdom’s throne forever.

**Help with house for my Name:** This means a temple for worship of the LORD.

**Help with throne:** The throne represents royal rule.

## 2 Samuel 7:14

I will be his Father, and he will be my son. 📖

When he does wrong, I will discipline him with human rods and human blows.

**Help with rods and blows:** These words describe punishment carried out through people.

## 2 Samuel 7:15

But I will never take my faithful love from him as I took it from Saul. 📖

I removed Saul from before you.

## 2 Samuel 7:16

Your family and kingdom will last forever before me. 📖

Your throne will be established forever.’”

**Help with throne:** Here, throne represents royal rule.

## 2 Samuel 7:17

Nathan told David all the words of this entire revelation. 📖

**Help with revelation:** Revelation means something God makes known.

## 2 Samuel 7:18

King David went in, sat before the LORD, and prayed. 📖

“Lord GOD, who am I, and what is my family, that you have brought me this far?

## 2 Samuel 7:19

Lord GOD, as though this were small to you, you also spoke about your servant’s family in the future. 📖

Lord GOD, is this your usual way with people?

## 2 Samuel 7:20

What more can David say to you? 📖

Lord GOD, you know your servant.

## 2 Samuel 7:21

Because of your word and according to your own heart, you have done this great thing. 📖

You have made it known to your servant.

## 2 Samuel 7:22

How great you are, Lord GOD! 📖

No one is like you.

There is no God except you, according to everything we have heard with our own ears.

## 2 Samuel 7:23

Who is like your people Israel? 📖

God went to redeem this one nation on earth as his own people and make his name great.

You did great and awesome wonders, driving out nations and their gods before your people.

You redeemed your people for yourself from Egypt.

**Help with redeem:** Here, redeem means rescue and claim as one’s own.

**Help with awesome:** Awesome means causing wonder and awe.

## 2 Samuel 7:24

You established Israel as your very own people forever. 📖

You, LORD, became their God.

## 2 Samuel 7:25

LORD God, confirm forever what you said about your servant and his family. 📖

Do as you promised,

## 2 Samuel 7:26

so your name will be praised as great forever. 📖

People will say, ‘The LORD of Hosts is God over Israel.’

Your servant David’s family will be established before you.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 2 Samuel 7:27

LORD of Hosts, God of Israel, you revealed this to your servant. 📖

You said, ‘I will build a house for you.’

So your servant found courage to offer this prayer to you.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with house:** Here, house means David’s family line.

## 2 Samuel 7:28

Lord GOD, you are God! 📖

Your words are true.

You promised this goodness to your servant.

## 2 Samuel 7:29

May it please you to bless your servant’s family, so it continues forever before you. 📖

Lord GOD, you have spoken.

With your blessing, your servant’s family will be blessed forever.”

**Help with blessing:** Here, blessing means good that comes from God.
