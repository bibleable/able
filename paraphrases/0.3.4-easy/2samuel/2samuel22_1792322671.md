# 2 Samuel 22 — David’s song to the LORD

**About this chapter**

David sings to the LORD about rescue and victory.

The song uses strong pictures of storms, danger, safety, and violent battle.

## 2 Samuel 22:1

David sang this song to the LORD. 📖

It was the day the LORD rescued him from all his enemies and from Saul.

## 2 Samuel 22:2

David said, “The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my rescuer. 📖

**Help with rock and fortress:** David compares God’s protection to a strong rock and a safe building.

## 2 Samuel 22:3

My God is my rock, where I find safety. 📖

God is my shield and the horn of my salvation.

God is my strong place, my shelter, and my Saviour.

You save me from violence.

**Help with these pictures:** Rock, shield, strong place, and shelter describe God’s protection.

A horn is a picture of strength.

Salvation means being saved.

## 2 Samuel 22:4

I will call to the LORD, who deserves praise. 📖

So I will be saved from my enemies.

## 2 Samuel 22:5

Waves of death closed over me. 📖

Rushing floods of chaos overwhelmed me.

**Help with these pictures:** David describes deadly danger as water covering him.

Chaos means great disorder.

## 2 Samuel 22:6

The ropes of Sheol tangled around me. 📖

Death’s traps faced me.

**Help with Sheol:** Sheol refers to the place of the dead.

Ropes and traps picture danger that holds a person.

## 2 Samuel 22:7

In my trouble, I called to the LORD. 📖

I cried out to my God.

From his temple, God heard my voice.

My cry for help reached his ears.

**Help with temple:** A temple is a holy place of worship.

## 2 Samuel 22:8

Then the earth shook and trembled. 📖

The foundations of the heavens shook because God burned with anger.

**Help with foundations:** Foundations are what something rests on.

Here, the song pictures even the heavens shaking.

## 2 Samuel 22:9

Smoke rose from God’s nostrils. 📖

Fire that burns things up came from his mouth.

Glowing coals blazed out.

**Help with this picture:** Smoke and fire show the force of God’s anger in the song.

## 2 Samuel 22:10

God opened the heavens and came down. 📖

Dark clouds were beneath his feet.

## 2 Samuel 22:11

God rode on a cherub and flew. 📖

He flew high on the wings of the wind.

**Help with cherub:** A cherub is a heavenly being.

**Help with wings of the wind:** The song pictures the wind carrying God.

## 2 Samuel 22:12

God made darkness like a covering around him. 📖

Water gathered, and thick clouds surrounded him.

## 2 Samuel 22:13

Burning coals blazed out from the brightness of God’s presence. 📖

## 2 Samuel 22:14

The LORD thundered from heaven. 📖

The voice of the Most High rang out.

**Help with Most High:** This is a title for God.

## 2 Samuel 22:15

God shot his arrows and scattered the enemies. 📖

He threw lightning and drove them away.

## 2 Samuel 22:16

The LORD spoke a stern rebuke, and the breath from his nostrils blasted out. 📖

The sea’s channels appeared.

The foundations of the world were uncovered.

**Help with this picture:** The song describes God’s power reaching to the world’s deepest parts.

## 2 Samuel 22:17

God reached down from above and took hold of me. 📖

He pulled me out of deep waters.

**Help with deep waters:** David pictures rescue from danger as being pulled from water.

## 2 Samuel 22:18

God rescued me from my powerful enemy. 📖

He rescued me from enemies too strong for me.

## 2 Samuel 22:19

My enemies faced me on the day of my disaster. 📖

But the LORD supported me.

## 2 Samuel 22:20

God brought me out into an open place. 📖

He rescued me because he delighted in me.

**Help with delighted:** God delighted in David means God took pleasure in him.

## 2 Samuel 22:21

The LORD rewarded me for my righteousness. 📖

He repaid me for the cleanness of my hands.

**Help with righteousness:** Righteousness means living in a way that is right before God.

**Help with clean hands:** David uses clean hands as a picture of being free from wrongdoing.

## 2 Samuel 22:22

I have followed the LORD’s ways. 📖

I have not wickedly turned away from my God.

## 2 Samuel 22:23

All God’s laws are before me. 📖

I have not ignored his commands.

## 2 Samuel 22:24

I have been without blame before God. 📖

I have kept myself from wrongdoing.

## 2 Samuel 22:25

The LORD repaid me for my righteousness. 📖

He repaid me for being clean in his sight.

**Help with righteousness and clean:** David speaks of right conduct before God, using cleanness as a picture.

## 2 Samuel 22:26

You show yourself faithful to those who are faithful. 📖

You show yourself without blame to those who are without blame.

**Help with faithful:** Faithful means keeping trust and commitments.

## 2 Samuel 22:27

You show yourself pure to those who are pure. 📖

But you show yourself shrewd to those who are crooked.

**Help with these words:** Pure means free from wrong.

Crooked describes people who act dishonestly.

Shrewd means wise and not easily tricked.

## 2 Samuel 22:28

You save people who suffer. 📖

But you watch proud people to bring them down.

## 2 Samuel 22:29

LORD, you are my lamp. 📖

The LORD lights up my darkness.

**Help with this picture:** David compares God’s help to light in a dark place.

## 2 Samuel 22:30

With you I can charge against an army. 📖

With my God I can climb over a wall.

## 2 Samuel 22:31

God’s way is perfect. 📖

The LORD’s word has no fault.

God is a shield for everyone who finds safety in him.

**Help with shield:** A shield protects a person.

Here, it pictures God’s protection.

## 2 Samuel 22:32

Who is God besides the LORD? 📖

Who is the Rock except our God?

**Help with Rock:** David compares God to a firm, safe rock.

## 2 Samuel 22:33

God is my strong fortress. 📖

God makes my way clear.

**Help with fortress:** A fortress is a strong place that protects people.

David uses this as a picture of God’s protection.

## 2 Samuel 22:34

God makes my feet like a deer’s feet. 📖

God places me safely on the high places.

**Help with deer’s feet:** This pictures steady steps in high places.

## 2 Samuel 22:35

God trains my hands for battle. 📖

My arms can bend a bronze bow.

**Help with bow:** A bow is a weapon for shooting arrows.

## 2 Samuel 22:36

You have given me your shield of salvation. 📖

Your gentleness lifts me up.

**Help with shield of salvation:** David pictures God’s saving help as a shield that protects him.

## 2 Samuel 22:37

You make the path wide beneath me. 📖

So my ankles do not give way.

## 2 Samuel 22:38

I chased my enemies and destroyed them. 📖

I did not turn back until they were wiped out.

## 2 Samuel 22:39

I destroyed and crushed my enemies so they could not get up. 📖

They fell beneath my feet.

## 2 Samuel 22:40

You gave me strength for battle. 📖

You brought my enemies down beneath me.

## 2 Samuel 22:41

You made my enemies turn and run from me. 📖

I destroyed those who hated me.

## 2 Samuel 22:42

My enemies looked for help, but no one saved them. 📖

They looked to the LORD, but he did not answer.

## 2 Samuel 22:43

I ground them down like the dust of the earth. 📖

I crushed them and stamped on them like mud in the streets.

**About these pictures:** David sings of destroying his enemies.

These violent words are retained from the song.

## 2 Samuel 22:44

You rescued me from conflict among my people. 📖

You kept me as head of nations.

A people I did not know will serve me.

## 2 Samuel 22:45

Foreigners shrink back in fear before me. 📖

When they hear me, they obey me.

## 2 Samuel 22:46

Foreigners lose courage. 📖

They come shaking from their strong places.

## 2 Samuel 22:47

The LORD lives! 📖

May my Rock be blessed!

May God, the Rock of my salvation, be lifted high!

**Help with these words:** Rock pictures safety and strength.

Salvation means being saved.

Blessed and lifted high express praise to God.

## 2 Samuel 22:48

God takes revenge for me. 📖

God brings nations down beneath me.

## 2 Samuel 22:49

God frees me from my enemies. 📖

You lift me above those who oppose me.

You rescue me from violent men.

## 2 Samuel 22:50

So I will praise you among the nations, LORD. 📖

I will sing praise to your name.

**Help with your name:** Here, praising God’s name means praising God himself.

## 2 Samuel 22:51

God brings great salvation to his king. 📖

He shows faithful love to his anointed one, to David and his descendants forever.”

**Help with salvation:** Salvation means being saved.

**Help with anointed one:** This is a person chosen and set apart for a role.

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.
