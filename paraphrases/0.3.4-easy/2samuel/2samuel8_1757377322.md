# 2 Samuel 8 — David’s victories and officials

**About this chapter**

David defeats neighbouring peoples and takes goods from them.

The chapter also names officials in his kingdom.

## 2 Samuel 8:1

Later, David defeated the Philistines and brought them under his power. 📖

He took Metheg-ammah from them.

## 2 Samuel 8:2

David defeated the Moabites and made them lie on the ground. 📖

He measured them with a cord.

Those measured by 2 lengths were killed, and those measured by 1 length were spared.

The Moabites came under David’s rule and brought him tribute.

**Help with tribute:** Tribute is payment made to a ruler.

**About this killing:** The source describes this treatment of captives.

The account is retained without turning it into an instruction for readers.

## 2 Samuel 8:3

David defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah. 📖

Hadadezer had marched out to restore his rule along the Euphrates River.

## 2 Samuel 8:4

David captured 1,000 chariots, 7,000 chariot drivers, and 20,000 foot soldiers from Hadadezer. 📖

David hamstrung all the horses except 100 kept for the chariots.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

**Help with hamstrung:** This means cut the tendons in the horses’ legs, disabling them.

## 2 Samuel 8:5

Arameans from Damascus came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah. 📖

David killed 22,000 of their men.

## 2 Samuel 8:6

David stationed soldiers in Aram of Damascus. 📖

The Arameans came under David’s rule and brought him tribute.

The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

**Help with tribute:** Tribute is payment made to a ruler.

## 2 Samuel 8:7

David took the gold shields belonging to Hadadezer’s officers and brought them to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Samuel 8:8

King David took a large amount of bronze from Betah and Berothai, cities of Hadadezer. 📖

## 2 Samuel 8:9

King Toi of Hamath heard that David had defeated Hadadezer’s whole army. 📖

## 2 Samuel 8:10

Toi sent his son Joram to greet King David and bless him for fighting and defeating Hadadezer. 📖

Hadadezer had been at war with Toi.

Joram brought objects of silver, gold, and bronze.

## 2 Samuel 8:11

King David set these things apart for the LORD. 📖

He also dedicated silver and gold from all the nations he had brought under his power.

**Help with dedicated:** Dedicated means set apart for God.

## 2 Samuel 8:12

These came from Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, Philistines, and Amalekites. 📖

They also came from goods taken from Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

## 2 Samuel 8:13

David became famous when he returned from killing 18,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. 📖

## 2 Samuel 8:14

David stationed soldiers throughout Edom, and all the Edomites came under his rule. 📖

The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

## 2 Samuel 8:15

David ruled all Israel. 📖

He gave justice and right judgment to all his people.

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.

## 2 Samuel 8:16

Joab son of Zeruiah commanded the army. 📖

Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder.

**Help with recorder:** This was an official responsible for records.

## 2 Samuel 8:17

Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests. 📖

Seraiah was the scribe.

**Help with scribe:** A scribe is an official who writes and keeps records.

## 2 Samuel 8:18

Benaiah son of Jehoiada commanded the Cherethites and Pelethites. 📖

David’s sons were priestly leaders.

**About priestly leaders:** The source’s description is retained for further editorial discussion.
