# 2 Samuel 2 — David becomes king of Judah

**About this chapter**

David rules Judah from Hebron.

Abner makes Ish-bosheth king over Israel, and the two sides fight.

## 2 Samuel 2:1

Later, David asked the LORD, “Should I go up to one of Judah’s towns?” 📖

The LORD answered, “Go up.”

David asked, “Where should I go?”

The LORD said, “To Hebron.”

## 2 Samuel 2:2

David went with his 2 wives. 📖

They were Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal of Carmel.

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 2 Samuel 2:3

David took the men with him, each with his household. 📖

They settled in the towns near Hebron.

## 2 Samuel 2:4

Judah’s men came to Hebron and anointed David king over Judah’s family. 📖

They told David, “The men of Jabesh-gilead buried Saul.”

**Help with anointed:** They used oil to mark David as king.

**Help with Judah’s family:** Here, this means the people of Judah.

## 2 Samuel 2:5

David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh-gilead. 📖

He said, “May the LORD bless you.

You showed kindness to Saul your lord by burying him.

## 2 Samuel 2:6

May the LORD now show you faithful love and faithfulness. 📖

I too will show you this favour because of what you did.

## 2 Samuel 2:7

Be strong and brave now. 📖

Saul your lord is dead, but Judah’s family has anointed me as their king.”

**Help with anointed:** This means marked out as king by the use of oil.

## 2 Samuel 2:8

Meanwhile, Abner son of Ner, Saul’s army commander, took Saul’s son Ish-bosheth to Mahanaim. 📖

## 2 Samuel 2:9

Abner made Ish-bosheth king over Gilead, Asher, Jezreel, Ephraim, and Benjamin: over all Israel. 📖

## 2 Samuel 2:10

Ish-bosheth son of Saul was 40 years old when he began ruling Israel. 📖

He ruled for 2 years.

But Judah’s people followed David.

## 2 Samuel 2:11

David was king over Judah’s people in Hebron for 7 years and 6 months. 📖

## 2 Samuel 2:12

One day, Abner son of Ner marched from Mahanaim to Gibeon. 📖

The servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul went with him.

## 2 Samuel 2:13

Joab son of Zeruiah and David’s servants went out and met them by Gibeon’s pool. 📖

The two groups took positions on opposite sides of the pool.

## 2 Samuel 2:14

Abner told Joab, “Let the young men get up and compete before us.” 📖

Joab answered, “Let them get up.”

## 2 Samuel 2:15

They got up and were counted off. 📖

There were 12 for Benjamin and Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and 12 for David.

## 2 Samuel 2:16

Each man grabbed his opponent’s head and drove his sword into his opponent’s side. 📖

They all fell together.

So this place in Gibeon is called Helkath-hazzurim.

## 2 Samuel 2:17

The battle that day was fierce. 📖

David’s servants defeated Abner and Israel’s men.

## 2 Samuel 2:18

Zeruiah’s 3 sons were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. 📖

Asahel ran as fast as a wild gazelle.

**Help with gazelle:** A gazelle is a swift animal similar to a deer.

## 2 Samuel 2:19

Asahel chased Abner without turning right or left. 📖

## 2 Samuel 2:20

Abner looked back and asked, “Is that you, Asahel?” 📖

Asahel answered, “It is.”

## 2 Samuel 2:21

Abner said, “Turn right or left. 📖

Seize one of the young men and take his equipment for yourself.”

But Asahel would not stop chasing Abner.

## 2 Samuel 2:22

Abner warned Asahel again, “Stop chasing me. 📖

Why should I strike you to the ground?

How could I face your brother Joab?”

## 2 Samuel 2:23

Asahel refused to turn away. 📖

Abner drove the back end of his spear into Asahel’s stomach, and it came out through his back.

Asahel fell dead there.

Everyone stopped when they came to the place where Asahel had fallen and died.

## 2 Samuel 2:24

Joab and Abishai kept chasing Abner. 📖

At sunset, they reached Ammah’s hill, opposite Giah, on the road to Gibeon’s wilderness.

## 2 Samuel 2:25

The Benjamites gathered around Abner as one group and stood on top of a hill. 📖

## 2 Samuel 2:26

Abner called to Joab, “Must the sword keep eating forever? 📖

Do you not know this will end only in bitterness?

How long before you tell the troops to stop chasing their brothers?”

**Help with sword eating:** This pictures continuing killing.

**Help with brothers:** The fighting groups belong to the same people, Israel.

## 2 Samuel 2:27

Joab answered, “As surely as God lives, without your words the troops would have chased their brothers until morning.” 📖

**Help with brothers:** Here, brothers means their fellow Israelites.

## 2 Samuel 2:28

Joab blew the ram’s horn, and all the troops stopped. 📖

They stopped chasing Israel and stopped fighting.

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

## 2 Samuel 2:29

Abner and his men marched through the Arabah all that night. 📖

They crossed the Jordan, marched all morning, and reached Mahanaim.

## 2 Samuel 2:30

Joab came back from chasing Abner and gathered all the troops. 📖

Besides Asahel, 19 of David’s servants were missing.

## 2 Samuel 2:31

But David’s servants had killed 360 Benjamites who were with Abner. 📖

## 2 Samuel 2:32

They buried Asahel in his father’s tomb in Bethlehem. 📖

Joab and his men marched all night and reached Hebron at dawn.
