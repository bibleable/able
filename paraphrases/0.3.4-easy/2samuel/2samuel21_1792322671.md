# 2 Samuel 21 — Famine, deaths, and battles

**About this chapter**

David asks about a famine.

This chapter describes the execution of 7 people, a mother guarding bodies, and battles.

## 2 Samuel 21:1

During David’s rule, there was a famine for 3 years in a row. 📖

David sought the LORD.

The LORD said, “Saul and his family shed blood by killing the Gibeonites.

That is the reason.”

**Help with famine:** A famine is a time when there is very little food.

**Help with sought the LORD:** David turned to God for an answer.

## 2 Samuel 21:2

David called the Gibeonites and spoke to them. 📖

The Gibeonites were survivors from the Amorites, not Israelites.

Israel had made a promise about them under oath.

But Saul had tried to kill them in his zeal for Israel and Judah.

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise.

**Help with zeal:** Here, zeal means strong commitment to a cause.

## 2 Samuel 21:3

David asked the Gibeonites, “What shall I do for you? 📖

How can I make up for the wrong, so you may bless the LORD’s inheritance?”

**Help with inheritance:** Here, the LORD’s inheritance refers to Israel, his people.

## 2 Samuel 21:4

The Gibeonites said, “We want no silver or gold from Saul or his family. 📖

You should not put anyone in Israel to death for us.”

David answered, “I will do whatever you ask.”

## 2 Samuel 21:5

They answered the king, “The man who destroyed us also made plans against us. 📖

He wanted to wipe us out from every part of Israel.

## 2 Samuel 21:6

Give us 7 of his male descendants. 📖

We will hang them before the LORD at Gibeah of Saul, the LORD’s chosen one.”

The king said, “I will give them to you.”

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are a person’s children and later family.

**About this request:** The Gibeonites request these deaths, and David agrees.

This describes what they did.

It is not an instruction for readers to do the same.

## 2 Samuel 21:7

The king spared Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan, Saul’s son. 📖

David and Jonathan son of Saul had made an oath before the LORD.

That oath was why David spared him.

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise.

## 2 Samuel 21:8

The king took Armoni and Mephibosheth, Saul’s 2 sons by Rizpah daughter of Aiah. 📖

He also took the 5 sons of Merab daughter of Saul.

Their father was Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite.

**About Mephibosheth:** The son of Saul here is different from Jonathan’s son in verse 7.

## 2 Samuel 21:9

David handed these men over to the Gibeonites. 📖

The Gibeonites hanged them on the hill before the LORD.

All 7 died together.

They were put to death during the first days of harvest, as the barley harvest began.

**Help with barley:** Barley is a grain grown for food.

## 2 Samuel 21:10

Rizpah daughter of Aiah spread rough sackcloth on a rock for herself. 📖

She stayed from the start of harvest until rain from heaven poured down on the bodies.

By day, she kept birds from landing on the bodies.

By night, she kept wild animals away.

**Help with sackcloth:** This rough cloth was used to show grief.

## 2 Samuel 21:11

David heard what Rizpah daughter of Aiah, Saul’s concubine, had done. 📖

**Help with concubine:** A concubine was a woman in a marriage-like relationship with fewer rights than a wife.

## 2 Samuel 21:12

David went to get the bones of Saul and Jonathan his son from the men of Jabesh-gilead. 📖

Those men had stolen the bones from the public square of Beth-shan.

The Philistines had hung the bodies there after killing Saul at Gilboa.

## 2 Samuel 21:13

David had the bones of Saul and Jonathan his son brought from there. 📖

They also gathered the bones of those who had been hanged.

## 2 Samuel 21:14

They buried Saul’s and Jonathan’s bones in Zela, in Benjamin, in the tomb of Saul’s father Kish. 📖

They did everything the king commanded.

Then God answered their prayers for the land.

## 2 Samuel 21:15

The Philistines went to war against Israel again. 📖

David and his servants went down and fought the Philistines.

David became very tired.

## 2 Samuel 21:16

Ishbi-benob, a descendant of Rapha, planned to kill David. 📖

His bronze spear weighed 300 shekels, and he carried a new sword.

**Help with descendant:** A descendant is someone from a person’s later family.

**Help with shekels:** A shekel was a measure of weight.

## 2 Samuel 21:17

Abishai son of Zeruiah came to help David. 📖

Abishai struck the Philistine and killed him.

David’s men swore, “You must never go into battle with us again.

The lamp of Israel must not go out.”

**Help with the lamp:** The men compare David’s life to a lamp that gives Israel light.

## 2 Samuel 21:18

Later, another battle with the Philistines took place at Gob. 📖

Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Saph, a descendant of Rapha.

**Help with descendant:** A descendant is someone from a person’s later family.

## 2 Samuel 21:19

Another battle with the Philistines took place at Gob. 📖

Elhanan son of Jair the Bethlehemite killed the brother of Goliath the Gittite.

The shaft of this man’s spear was like a weaver’s beam.

**Help with shaft and beam:** A shaft is a spear’s long handle.

A weaver’s beam is a large bar used in equipment for making cloth.

## 2 Samuel 21:20

There was another battle at Gath. 📖

A very large man there had 6 fingers on each hand and 6 toes on each foot.

He had 24 in all.

He too was a descendant of Rapha.

**Help with descendant:** A descendant is someone from a person’s later family.

## 2 Samuel 21:21

This man mocked Israel. 📖

Jonathan, son of David’s brother Shimei, killed him.

## 2 Samuel 21:22

These 4 descendants of Rapha in Gath were killed by David and his servants. 📖

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are people from a person’s later family.
