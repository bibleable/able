# 2 Samuel 16 — Ziba, Shimei, and Absalom

**About this chapter**

Ziba meets David with supplies, and Shimei curses David.

In Jerusalem, Absalom follows Ahithophel’s advice about David’s concubines.

## 2 Samuel 16:1

A little beyond the summit, David met Mephibosheth’s servant Ziba. 📖

Ziba had 2 saddled donkeys loaded with supplies.

There were 200 loaves, 100 clusters of raisins, 100 summer fruits, and a skin of wine.

**Help with skin:** Here, skin means a container made from animal skin.

## 2 Samuel 16:2

The king asked, “Why have you brought these?” 📖

Ziba answered, “The donkeys are for the king’s household to ride.

The bread and summer fruit are for the young men.

The wine will refresh those exhausted in the wilderness.”

## 2 Samuel 16:3

The king asked, “Where is your master’s grandson?” 📖

Ziba answered, “He is staying in Jerusalem.

He said, ‘Today, Israel’s family will restore my grandfather’s kingdom to me.’”

**About this report:** Ziba is reporting what he says Mephibosheth said.

Mephibosheth later gives a different account.

## 2 Samuel 16:4

The king told Ziba, “Everything belonging to Mephibosheth is now yours!” 📖

Ziba said, “I bow humbly before you.

May you show me favour, my lord the king!”

## 2 Samuel 16:5

King David came near Bahurim. 📖

Shimei son of Gera came out from Saul’s family.

As he approached, he kept shouting curses.

## 2 Samuel 16:6

Shimei threw stones at David and all the king’s servants. 📖

The troops and mighty men were on David’s right and left.

## 2 Samuel 16:7

Shimei cursed and shouted, “Get out! 📖

Get out, you worthless man of bloodshed!

## 2 Samuel 16:8

You ruled in Saul’s place. 📖

The LORD has paid you back for all the blood of Saul’s family.

The LORD has put the kingdom in your son Absalom’s hands.

You have come to ruin because you are a man of bloodshed!”

**About this accusation:** Shimei makes these claims.

They are not presented as the narrator’s judgment of why David is suffering.

## 2 Samuel 16:9

Abishai son of Zeruiah asked the king, “Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? 📖

Let me go and cut off his head!”

**Help with dead dog:** Abishai uses this as an insult against Shimei.

## 2 Samuel 16:10

The king answered, “What have I to do with you, sons of Zeruiah? 📖

Suppose he curses because the LORD told him, ‘Curse David.’

Who can then ask, ‘Why did you do this?’”

## 2 Samuel 16:11

David told Abishai and his servants, “My own son, my flesh and blood, wants to kill me. 📖

How much more might this Benjamite!

Leave him alone and let him curse.

The LORD has told him to do so.

## 2 Samuel 16:12

Perhaps the LORD will see my suffering. 📖

Perhaps he will repay me with good for today’s cursing.”

## 2 Samuel 16:13

David and his men kept going along the road. 📖

Shimei walked on the opposite hillside, shouting curses, throwing stones, and flinging dust at David.

## 2 Samuel 16:14

The king and all his people finally arrived, exhausted. 📖

David refreshed himself there.

## 2 Samuel 16:15

Absalom and all Israel’s men came to Jerusalem, with Ahithophel. 📖

## 2 Samuel 16:16

David’s friend Hushai the Archite came to Absalom. 📖

Hushai said, “Long live the king!

Long live the king!”

## 2 Samuel 16:17

Absalom asked, “Is this your loyalty to your friend? 📖

Why did you not go with him?”

## 2 Samuel 16:18

Hushai answered, “No! 📖

I will belong to the one chosen by the LORD, this people, and all Israel’s men.

I will stay with him.

## 2 Samuel 16:19

Who should I serve, if not his son? 📖

As I served before your father, I will serve before you.”

## 2 Samuel 16:20

Absalom told Ahithophel, “Give me advice. 📖

What should we do?”

## 2 Samuel 16:21

Ahithophel answered, “Have sex with your father’s concubines, whom he left to care for the palace. 📖

All Israel will hear you have become a stench to your father.

Then everyone with you will be strengthened.”

**Help with concubines:** Concubines were women in marriage-like relationships with fewer rights than wives.

**Help with stench:** This pictures becoming deeply offensive to David.

**About this advice:** Ahithophel recommends this act to strengthen Absalom’s rebellion.

## 2 Samuel 16:22

They set up a tent for Absalom on the roof. 📖

He had sex with his father’s concubines in the sight of all Israel.

**Help with concubines:** Concubines were women in marriage-like relationships with fewer rights than wives.

The source’s public sexual humiliation is retained for further editorial assessment.

## 2 Samuel 16:23

In those days, Ahithophel’s advice was valued like asking for God’s word. 📖

Both David and Absalom regarded his advice this way.
