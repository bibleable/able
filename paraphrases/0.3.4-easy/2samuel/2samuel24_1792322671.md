# 2 Samuel 24 — David’s count and the plague

**About this chapter**

David orders a count of the troops.

The chapter describes David’s guilt, a deadly plague, prayer, and offerings.

## 2 Samuel 24:1

The LORD’s anger burned against Israel again. 📖

The LORD stirred David up against them.

He said, “Go and count the people of Israel and Judah.”

**About this difficult passage:** This chapter attributes the stirring to the LORD.

That statement is kept without replacing it with an explanation from another account.

## 2 Samuel 24:2

The king spoke to Joab, the army commander, who was with him. 📖

David said, “Go through all Israel’s tribes, from Dan to Beersheba, and count the troops.

I want to know their number.”

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups within Israel.

## 2 Samuel 24:3

Joab answered, “May the LORD your God make the troops 100 times as many. 📖

May my lord the king see it happen.

But why does my lord the king want to do this?”

## 2 Samuel 24:4

The king’s order overruled Joab and the army commanders. 📖

They left the king to count Israel’s troops.

## 2 Samuel 24:5

They crossed the Jordan and camped near Aroer. 📖

They were south of the town in the middle of the valley.

Then they went towards Gad and Jazer.

## 2 Samuel 24:6

They went to Gilead and the land of Tahtim-hodshi. 📖

Then they went to Dan-jaan and around to Sidon.

## 2 Samuel 24:7

They went towards the fortress of Tyre and all the cities of the Hivites and Canaanites. 📖

Finally, they went to Beersheba in the Negev of Judah.

**Help with fortress:** A fortress is a strong place protected from attack.

## 2 Samuel 24:8

They went through the whole land. 📖

After 9 months and 20 days, they returned to Jerusalem.

## 2 Samuel 24:9

Joab told the king the total number of troops. 📖

Israel had 800,000 brave men who could use a sword.

Judah had 500,000.

## 2 Samuel 24:10

After counting the troops, David felt guilty in his conscience. 📖

He told the LORD, “I have sinned greatly by doing this.

LORD, please take away your servant’s wrongdoing.

I have acted very foolishly.”

**Help with conscience:** Conscience is a person’s sense of right and wrong.

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong against God.

## 2 Samuel 24:11

When David got up in the morning, the LORD’s word had come to Gad. 📖

Gad was a prophet and David’s seer.

**Help with prophet and seer:** These words describe someone who receives messages from God.

## 2 Samuel 24:12

The LORD said, “Go and tell David this. 📖

The LORD offers you 3 choices.

Choose one, and I will carry it out against you.”

## 2 Samuel 24:13

Gad went to David and asked, “Do you choose 3 years of famine in your land? 📖

Or 3 months of running away while your enemies chase you?

Or 3 days of plague in your land?

Think it over.

Decide what answer I should give the one who sent me.”

**Help with famine:** Famine means a severe shortage of food.

**Help with plague:** Here, plague means a deadly disease spreading among people.

## 2 Samuel 24:14

David answered Gad, “I am in great distress. 📖

Please let us fall into the LORD’s hands, because his mercy is great.

Do not let me fall into human hands.”

**Help with hands:** Falling into someone’s hands means being under that person’s power.

**Help with mercy:** Mercy means compassion and a willingness to spare people.

## 2 Samuel 24:15

The LORD sent a plague on Israel from that morning until the set time. 📖

70,000 people died, from Dan to Beersheba.

**Help with plague:** Here, plague means a deadly disease spreading among people.

## 2 Samuel 24:16

The angel stretched out his hand to destroy Jerusalem. 📖

But the LORD stopped the disaster.

He told the angel destroying the people, “Enough!

Pull back your hand now!”

The LORD’s angel was beside the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where people separate grain from its dry covering.

## 2 Samuel 24:17

David saw the angel striking the people. 📖

David told the LORD, “I am the shepherd who sinned and did wrong.

But these sheep, what have they done?

Please let your hand fall on me and my father’s family.”

**Help with shepherd and sheep:** David compares himself to a shepherd and the people to sheep in his care.

**Help with your hand:** David asks for the punishment to fall on his own family.

## 2 Samuel 24:18

That day, Gad told David, “Go up and build an altar to the LORD. 📖

Build it on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.”

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offering sacrifices to God.

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where people separate grain from its dry covering.

## 2 Samuel 24:19

David went up as Gad had told him, just as the LORD commanded. 📖

## 2 Samuel 24:20

Araunah looked out and saw the king and his servants coming. 📖

Araunah went out and bowed with his face to the ground before the king.

## 2 Samuel 24:21

Araunah asked, “Why has my lord the king come to his servant?” 📖

David said, “To buy your threshing floor and build an altar to the LORD.

Then the plague on the people may stop.”

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where people separate grain from its dry covering.

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for sacrifices to God.

**Help with plague:** Here, plague means a deadly disease.

## 2 Samuel 24:22

Araunah told David, “May my lord the king take and offer whatever he thinks is good. 📖

Here are the oxen for a burnt offering.

Here are the threshing sledges and ox yokes for wood.

**Help with these words:** Oxen are large cattle used for work.

A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

Threshing sledges are tools dragged over grain to loosen its covering.

Yokes are wooden frames for joining working animals.

## 2 Samuel 24:23

O king, Araunah gives all these things to the king.” 📖

Araunah also said, “May the LORD your God accept you.”

## 2 Samuel 24:24

The king answered, “No, I insist on paying. 📖

I will not give the LORD my God burnt offerings that cost me nothing.”

David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for 50 shekels of silver.

**Help with burnt offerings:** These are sacrifices burned on an altar.

**Help with shekels:** Shekels measure the weight of silver used for payment.

## 2 Samuel 24:25

David built an altar to the LORD there. 📖

He gave burnt offerings and peace offerings.

The LORD answered the prayers for the land.

The plague on Israel stopped.

**Help with offerings:** A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

A peace offering is a sacrifice expressing peace and fellowship with God.

**Help with plague:** Here, plague means a deadly disease.
