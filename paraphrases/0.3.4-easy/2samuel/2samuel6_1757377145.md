# 2 Samuel 6 — The ark comes to David’s city

**About this chapter**

David brings the ark of God towards his city.

Uzzah dies beside the ark, and David later brings it with celebration.

## 2 Samuel 6:1

David gathered Israel’s chosen men again, 30,000 in all. 📖

## 2 Samuel 6:2

David and all his troops set out for Baale of Judah to bring God’s ark from there. 📖

The ark is called by the Name, the name of the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD sits as king between the cherubim on the ark.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with cherubim:** Cherubim are heavenly beings represented on the ark.

## 2 Samuel 6:3

They put God’s ark on a new cart and brought it from Abinadab’s house on the hill. 📖

Abinadab’s sons Uzzah and Ahio guided the new cart.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 6:4

They brought God’s ark with the cart, and Ahio walked in front of the ark. 📖

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 6:5

David and all Israel’s people celebrated before the LORD with all kinds of wooden instruments. 📖

They played harps, stringed instruments, tambourines, sistrums, and cymbals.

**Help with instruments:** Harps have strings, and tambourines are small hand-held drums.

Sistrums are shaken to make a rattling sound.

Cymbals are metal plates struck together.

## 2 Samuel 6:6

At Nacon’s threshing floor, the oxen stumbled. 📖

Uzzah reached out and held God’s ark.

**Help with threshing floor:** This is a place where grain is separated from its dry covering.

**Help with oxen:** Oxen are large cattle used for work.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 6:7

The LORD’s anger burned against Uzzah. 📖

God struck Uzzah down there for his lack of reverence.

Uzzah died beside God’s ark.

**Help with reverence:** Reverence means deep respect.

**About this death:** The source attributes Uzzah’s death to God’s judgment.

This difficult statement is retained rather than replaced with a natural explanation.

## 2 Samuel 6:8

David became angry because the LORD had burst out against Uzzah. 📖

David named the place Perez-uzzah, as it is still called today.

## 2 Samuel 6:9

That day, David feared the LORD. 📖

David asked, “How can the LORD’s ark ever come to me?”

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 6:10

David would not move the LORD’s ark to the City of David. 📖

He took it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite instead.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 6:11

The LORD’s ark stayed in Obed-edom the Gittite’s house for 3 months. 📖

The LORD blessed Obed-edom and his whole household.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed means gave good things to.

## 2 Samuel 6:12

King David heard, “The LORD has blessed Obed-edom’s household and everything he has because of God’s ark.” 📖

David went and brought God’s ark from Obed-edom’s house to the City of David with joy.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 6:13

When those carrying the LORD’s ark had walked 6 steps, David sacrificed an ox and a fattened calf. 📖

**Help with sacrificed:** This means killed the animals as offerings to God.

## 2 Samuel 6:14

David wore a linen ephod and danced with all his strength before the LORD. 📖

**Help with linen ephod:** This was a special garment made from linen cloth.

## 2 Samuel 6:15

David and all Israel’s people brought up the LORD’s ark with shouts and the sound of the ram’s horn. 📖

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

## 2 Samuel 6:16

As the LORD’s ark entered the City of David, Saul’s daughter Michal looked from a window. 📖

She saw King David leaping and dancing before the LORD.

She despised David in her heart.

**Help with despised:** Despised means looked down on with strong dislike.

## 2 Samuel 6:17

They brought the LORD’s ark into the tent David had set up for it and put it in place. 📖

David gave burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.

**Help with offerings:** A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

A peace offering expresses peace and fellowship with God.

## 2 Samuel 6:18

When David finished the burnt offerings and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the LORD of Hosts’ name. 📖

**Help with blessed:** Here, David calls for good from God for the people.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 2 Samuel 6:19

David gave every man and woman in Israel’s crowd a loaf of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake. 📖

Everyone went home.

## 2 Samuel 6:20

David returned to bless his household, and Saul’s daughter Michal came out to meet him. 📖

She said, “How Israel’s king has distinguished himself today!

He uncovered himself before his subjects’ servant women, like a vulgar person!”

**Help with distinguished:** Michal speaks mockingly about how David has made himself noticed.

**Help with vulgar:** Here, vulgar means behaving without decency.

## 2 Samuel 6:21

David answered Michal, “I danced before the LORD. 📖

He chose me rather than your father and his whole family.

He appointed me ruler of the LORD’s people Israel.

I will celebrate before the LORD.

## 2 Samuel 6:22

I will make myself even more humble and humiliated than this. 📖

Yet the servant women you mentioned will honour me.”

## 2 Samuel 6:23

Saul’s daughter Michal had no children for the rest of her life. 📖
