# 2 Samuel 3 — Abner is murdered

**About this chapter**

Abner offers to bring Israel under David’s rule.

Joab kills Abner, and David grieves and condemns the killing.

## 2 Samuel 3:1

The war between Saul’s family and David’s family lasted a long time. 📖

David grew stronger and stronger, while Saul’s family grew weaker and weaker.

## 2 Samuel 3:2

Sons were born to David in Hebron. 📖

His first son was Amnon, by Ahinoam of Jezreel.

## 2 Samuel 3:3

His 2nd son was Chileab, by Abigail, the widow of Nabal of Carmel. 📖

His 3rd was Absalom, by Maacah, daughter of King Talmai of Geshur.

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 2 Samuel 3:4

His 4th son was Adonijah, by Haggith. 📖

His 5th was Shephatiah, by Abital.

## 2 Samuel 3:5

His 6th son was Ithream, by David’s wife Eglah. 📖

These sons were born to David in Hebron.

## 2 Samuel 3:6

During the war between Saul’s family and David’s family, Abner kept strengthening his position in Saul’s family. 📖

## 2 Samuel 3:7

Saul had a concubine named Rizpah daughter of Aiah. 📖

Ish-bosheth asked Abner, “Why did you sleep with my father’s concubine?”

**Help with concubine:** A concubine was a woman in a marriage-like relationship with fewer rights than a wife.

**Help with sleep with:** Here, this means have sex with.

## 2 Samuel 3:8

Abner became furious at Ish-bosheth’s accusation. 📖

Abner asked, “Am I the head of a dog belonging to Judah?

All this time, I have been loyal to your father Saul’s family, his brothers, and his friends.

I have not handed you over to David.

But now you accuse me of wrongdoing with this woman!

**Help with head of a dog:** Abner repeats an insulting picture to protest how he is being treated.

## 2 Samuel 3:9

May God punish Abner very severely if I do not do for David what the LORD swore to him. 📖

## 2 Samuel 3:10

I will transfer the kingdom from Saul’s family. 📖

I will establish David’s throne over Israel and Judah, from Dan to Beersheba.”

## 2 Samuel 3:11

Ish-bosheth feared Abner and did not dare say another word to him. 📖

## 2 Samuel 3:12

Abner sent messengers to David on his behalf. 📖

They said, “Whose land is it?

Make a covenant with me.

I will help you bring all Israel over to you.”

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 2 Samuel 3:13

David answered, “Good, I will make a covenant with you. 📖

But I require one thing.

Do not come to see me unless you bring Saul’s daughter Michal.”

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 2 Samuel 3:14

David sent messengers to Ish-bosheth son of Saul. 📖

David said, “Give back my wife Michal.

I became engaged to her for 100 Philistine foreskins.”

**Help with foreskins:** A foreskin is the skin covering the tip of the penis.

**About this payment:** The disturbing detail is retained from the source.

## 2 Samuel 3:15

Ish-bosheth sent for Michal and took her from her husband Paltiel son of Laish. 📖

## 2 Samuel 3:16

Paltiel followed her, crying all the way to Bahurim. 📖

Abner told him, “Go back.”

Paltiel went home.

## 2 Samuel 3:17

Abner spoke with Israel’s elders. 📖

Abner said, “In the past, you wanted David as your king.

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## 2 Samuel 3:18

Act now. 📖

The LORD told David, ‘Through my servant David, I will save my people Israel.

I will save them from the Philistines’ power and from all their enemies.’”

## 2 Samuel 3:19

Abner also spoke to the Benjamites. 📖

He went to Hebron to tell David everything that seemed good to Israel and all Benjamin’s family.

## 2 Samuel 3:20

Abner came to David in Hebron with 20 men. 📖

David held a feast for them.

## 2 Samuel 3:21

Abner told David, “Let me go now and gather all Israel to my lord the king. 📖

They can make a covenant with you, and you can rule over everything your heart desires.”

David sent Abner away, and he left in peace.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 2 Samuel 3:22

Just then, David’s soldiers and Joab returned from a raid with many goods taken in the raid. 📖

Abner was not with David in Hebron, because David had sent him away in peace.

## 2 Samuel 3:23

Joab and his troops arrived and heard this report. 📖

“Abner son of Ner came to see the king, who sent him away in peace.”

## 2 Samuel 3:24

Joab went to the king and said, “What have you done? 📖

Abner came to you!

Why did you send him away?

Now he is escaping!

## 2 Samuel 3:25

Surely you know Abner son of Ner came to trick you. 📖

He came to track your movements and everything you do.”

## 2 Samuel 3:26

After leaving David, Joab sent messengers after Abner. 📖

They brought Abner back from Sirah’s well.

David did not know about this.

## 2 Samuel 3:27

When Abner returned to Hebron, Joab drew him aside into the gateway, as though to speak privately. 📖

Joab stabbed Abner in the stomach there.

Abner died because of the blood of Joab’s brother Asahel.

**Help with blood:** This refers to Asahel’s death, for which Joab took revenge.

## 2 Samuel 3:28

David later heard about it and said, “My kingdom and I are forever innocent before the LORD of Abner’s death. 📖

Abner was the son of Ner.

## 2 Samuel 3:29

May the blame turn on Joab’s head and on all his father’s family. 📖

May Joab’s family never lack someone with a discharge or skin disease.

May it never lack someone leaning on a staff, dying by the sword, or lacking food.”

**Help with discharge:** A discharge is fluid leaking from the body.

**About this curse:** David calls for illness, disability, death, and hunger in Joab’s family.

These are David’s words in the source.

They are not a claim that people who have these conditions are morally bad.

## 2 Samuel 3:30

Joab and his brother Abishai murdered Abner. 📖

Abner had killed their brother Asahel in the battle at Gibeon.

## 2 Samuel 3:31

David ordered Joab and everyone with him, “Tear your clothes, put on sackcloth, and grieve before Abner.” 📖

King David walked behind the frame carrying Abner’s body.

**Help with sackcloth and torn clothes:** These were signs of grief.

## 2 Samuel 3:32

They buried Abner in Hebron. 📖

The king cried aloud at Abner’s tomb, and all the people cried.

## 2 Samuel 3:33

The king sang this song of grief for Abner. 📖

“Should Abner die the death of a fool?

## 2 Samuel 3:34

Your hands were not tied, and your feet were not chained. 📖

You fell as someone falls before wicked people.”

All the people cried over Abner even more.

## 2 Samuel 3:35

The people urged David to eat while it was still day. 📖

David swore, “May God punish me very severely if I taste bread or anything else before sunset!”

## 2 Samuel 3:36

All the people noticed and were pleased. 📖

Everything the king did pleased them.

## 2 Samuel 3:37

That day, all the troops and all Israel were convinced the king had no part in the murder.

The murdered man was Abner son of Ner. 📖

## 2 Samuel 3:38

The king told his servants, “Do you not know a great prince has fallen in Israel today? 📖

## 2 Samuel 3:39

I am weak today, although anointed king. 📖

These men, Zeruiah’s sons, are too fierce for me.

May the LORD repay the wrongdoer according to his evil!”

**Help with anointed:** This means set apart as king, marked by the use of oil.
