# 2 Samuel 12 — Nathan confronts David

**About this chapter**

Nathan tells David a story and brings the LORD’s judgment.

The chapter includes a child’s death, David’s prayer, and war against Rabbah.

## 2 Samuel 12:1

The LORD sent Nathan to David. 📖

Nathan arrived and said, “Two men lived in a city.

One was rich, and the other was poor.

## 2 Samuel 12:2

The rich man had many sheep and cattle. 📖

## 2 Samuel 12:3

The poor man had only 1 little female lamb, which he had bought and raised. 📖

It grew up with him and his children.

It shared his food, drank from his cup, and slept in his arms.

It was like a daughter to him.

## 2 Samuel 12:4

A traveller came to the rich man. 📖

The rich man would not take his own sheep or cattle to prepare food for the traveller.

Instead, he took the poor man’s lamb and prepared it for his guest.”

## 2 Samuel 12:5

David became fiercely angry with the man. 📖

David told Nathan, “As surely as the LORD lives, the man who did this deserves to die!

## 2 Samuel 12:6

He must pay for the lamb 4 times over. 📖

He did this and showed no pity.”

## 2 Samuel 12:7

Nathan told David, “You are that man! 📖

The LORD, the God of Israel, says this.

‘I anointed you king over Israel and rescued you from Saul’s power.

**Help with anointed:** This means set apart as king, marked by the use of oil.

## 2 Samuel 12:8

I gave you your master’s household and his wives into your arms. 📖

I gave you Israel’s and Judah’s people.

If this was not enough, I would have given you more.

## 2 Samuel 12:9

Why did you despise the LORD’s command by doing evil in his sight? 📖

You killed Uriah the Hittite with the sword and took his wife for yourself.

You killed him by the Ammonites’ sword.

**Help with despise:** Despise means treat with scorn.

## 2 Samuel 12:10

The sword will never leave your family. 📖

You despised me and took Uriah the Hittite’s wife as your own.’

**Help with sword:** Here, the sword pictures continued violent deaths.

## 2 Samuel 12:11

The LORD says, ‘I will raise up trouble against you from your own family. 📖

Before your eyes, I will take your wives and give them to another man.

He will have sex with them in broad daylight.

## 2 Samuel 12:12

You acted secretly, but I will do this in broad daylight before all Israel.’” 📖

**About this judgment:** The source describes sexual humiliation as part of judgment against David.

This difficult passage is retained for further editorial assessment.

## 2 Samuel 12:13

David told Nathan, “I have sinned against the LORD.” 📖

Nathan answered, “The LORD has taken away your sin.

You will not die.

**Help with sinned and sin:** These words mean doing wrong against God.

## 2 Samuel 12:14

But this deed showed complete contempt for the LORD’s word. 📖

The son born to you will certainly die.”

**Help with contempt:** Contempt means treating someone or something with scorn.

## 2 Samuel 12:15

Nathan went home. 📖

The LORD struck the child whom Uriah’s wife had borne to David, and the child became ill.

**About the illness:** The source attributes this illness to the LORD.

The draft retains that statement without making it a general explanation of children’s illnesses.

## 2 Samuel 12:16

David begged God for the boy. 📖

David fasted, went into his house, and lay all night in sackcloth on the ground.

**Help with fasted:** Fasted means did not eat for a time.

**Help with sackcloth:** Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## 2 Samuel 12:17

The elders of David’s household stood beside him and tried to help him up. 📖

David would not get up or eat with them.

**Help with elders:** Elders are senior members or leaders.

## 2 Samuel 12:18

On the 7th day, the child died. 📖

David’s servants feared telling him.

They said, “He would not listen while the child lived.

How can we tell him the child is dead?

He might harm himself.”

## 2 Samuel 12:19

David saw his servants whispering and realised the child had died. 📖

David asked, “Is the child dead?”

They answered, “He is dead.”

## 2 Samuel 12:20

David got up, washed, put oil on himself, and changed his clothes. 📖

He went to the LORD’s house and worshipped.

Then he went home and asked for food.

They served him, and he ate.

**Help with the LORD’s house:** This means the place of worship.

## 2 Samuel 12:21

His servants asked, “Why have you done this? 📖

While the child lived, you fasted and cried.

When he died, you got up and ate.”

**Help with fasted:** Fasted means did not eat for a time.

## 2 Samuel 12:22

David answered, “While the child lived, I fasted and cried. 📖

I thought, ‘Who knows?

The LORD may be kind to me and let him live.’

**Help with fasted:** Fasted means did not eat for a time.

## 2 Samuel 12:23

Now he is dead, why should I fast? 📖

Can I bring him back?

I will go to him, but he will not return to me.”

**Help with fast:** Fast means choose not to eat for a time.

**About go to him:** David speaks about joining the dead.

The draft does not add a detailed account of life after death.

## 2 Samuel 12:24

David comforted his wife Bathsheba and had sex with her. 📖

She gave birth to a son, and they named him Solomon.

The LORD loved the child.

## 2 Samuel 12:25

The LORD sent word through Nathan the prophet to name the child Jedidiah, because the LORD loved him. 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## 2 Samuel 12:26

Meanwhile, Joab fought Rabbah of the Ammonites and captured the royal fortress. 📖

**Help with fortress:** A fortress is a strong place protected from attack.

## 2 Samuel 12:27

Joab sent messengers to David. 📖

Joab said, “I have fought Rabbah and captured the city’s water supply.

## 2 Samuel 12:28

Gather the remaining troops, surround the city, and capture it. 📖

Otherwise, I will capture it, and it will be named after me.”

## 2 Samuel 12:29

David gathered all the troops and went to Rabbah. 📖

He fought the city and captured it.

## 2 Samuel 12:30

David took the crown from their king’s head. 📖

It weighed 1 talent of gold and had precious stones.

It was placed on David’s head.

David took many goods from the city.

**Help with talent:** A talent was a measure of weight.

## 2 Samuel 12:31

David brought the people out and put them to work with saws, iron picks, and axes. 📖

He made them work at brick kilns.

He did this in all the Ammonite cities.

David and all his troops returned to Jerusalem.

**Help with kilns:** Kilns are furnaces used to make bricks hard.
