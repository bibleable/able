# 2 Samuel 4 — Ish-bosheth is murdered

**About this chapter**

Two brothers kill Ish-bosheth and bring his head to David.

David condemns their act and orders their deaths.

## 2 Samuel 4:1

Ish-bosheth son of Saul heard that Abner had died in Hebron. 📖

Ish-bosheth lost courage, and all Israel was distressed.

## 2 Samuel 4:2

Saul’s son had 2 leaders of raiding groups, Baanah and Rechab. 📖

They were sons of Rimmon the Beerothite from Benjamin’s tribe.

Beeroth is considered part of Benjamin.

**Help with tribe:** A tribe is a large family group.

## 2 Samuel 4:3

The Beerothites had fled to Gittaim. 📖

They have lived there as foreigners to this day.

## 2 Samuel 4:4

Jonathan son of Saul had a son whose feet were disabled. 📖

He was 5 when news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel.

His nurse picked him up and fled.

As she hurried away, he fell and became unable to walk properly.

His name was Mephibosheth.

## 2 Samuel 4:5

Rechab and Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, travelled to Ish-bosheth’s house in the heat of the day. 📖

The king was taking his midday sleep.

## 2 Samuel 4:6

They went inside as if to get wheat and stabbed Ish-bosheth in the stomach. 📖

Rechab and his brother Baanah slipped away.

## 2 Samuel 4:7

They had entered while Ish-bosheth lay on his bed. 📖

They stabbed him, killed him, and cut off his head.

They took his head and travelled through the Arabah all night.

## 2 Samuel 4:8

They brought Ish-bosheth’s head to David at Hebron. 📖

They told the king, “Here is Ish-bosheth son of Saul’s head.

Saul was your enemy who tried to kill you.

Today the LORD has given my lord the king revenge against Saul and his offspring.”

**Help with offspring:** Offspring means children and later family.

**About the LORD:** These men claim the LORD has given revenge.

This claim belongs to their speech.

## 2 Samuel 4:9

David answered Rechab and Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite. 📖

“As surely as the LORD lives, he has rescued my life from every trouble.

## 2 Samuel 4:10

Someone told me, ‘Saul is dead,’ thinking he brought good news. 📖

I seized him and put him to death at Ziklag.

That was his reward for the news!

## 2 Samuel 4:11

How much more should I act when wicked men kill a righteous man in his own house, on his bed? 📖

Should I not hold you responsible for his blood and remove you from the earth?”

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means right before God.

**Help with his blood:** Here, blood refers to Ish-bosheth’s death.

## 2 Samuel 4:12

David ordered his young men to kill Rechab and Baanah, and they did. 📖

They cut off the brothers’ hands and feet and hung their bodies beside Hebron’s pool.

But they buried Ish-bosheth’s head in Abner’s tomb in Hebron.
