# 2 Samuel 5 — David rules Israel and takes Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Israel’s tribes accept David as king.

David captures Jerusalem and fights the Philistines.

## 2 Samuel 5:1

All Israel’s tribes came to David at Hebron. 📖

They said, “We are your own flesh and blood.

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

**Help with flesh and blood:** This means they belong to the same people.

## 2 Samuel 5:2

Even when Saul was king, you led Israel out and brought them back. 📖

The LORD told you, ‘You will shepherd my people Israel and rule over them.’”

**Help with shepherd:** This compares caring for people to a shepherd caring for sheep.

## 2 Samuel 5:3

All Israel’s elders came to King David at Hebron. 📖

David made a covenant with them before the LORD.

They anointed David king over Israel.

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

**Help with anointed:** They used oil to mark David as king.

## 2 Samuel 5:4

David was 30 years old when he became king. 📖

He ruled for 40 years.

## 2 Samuel 5:5

David ruled Judah from Hebron for 7 years and 6 months. 📖

He ruled all Israel and Judah from Jerusalem for 33 years.

## 2 Samuel 5:6

The king and his men marched to Jerusalem against the Jebusites living there. 📖

The Jebusites told David, “You will never get in.

Even blind and disabled people can drive you away.”

They thought David could not enter.

**About this taunt:** The Jebusites use disability in a mocking claim about the city’s defences.

Their words are retained as their speech, not as a judgment of disabled people’s worth.

## 2 Samuel 5:7

But David captured Zion’s fortress, which is the City of David. 📖

**Help with fortress:** A fortress is a strong place protected from attack.

## 2 Samuel 5:8

David said that day, “Anyone attacking the Jebusites must use the water shaft. 📖

They must reach the lame and blind, whom David despises.”

That is why people say, “The blind and lame will never enter the palace.”

**Help with water shaft:** A shaft is a narrow passage connected with the water supply.

**About lame and blind:** Lame means having difficulty walking.

The source attributes this contempt to David and records the saying.

This difficult passage is retained for further interpretation without endorsing contempt for disabled people.

## 2 Samuel 5:9

David lived in the fortress and called it the City of David. 📖

He built up the city all around, from the supporting terraces inward.

**Help with fortress:** A fortress is a strong place protected from attack.

**Help with terraces:** These are built-up levels that support the city.

## 2 Samuel 5:10

David became greater and greater, because the LORD God of Hosts was with him. 📖

**Help with LORD God of Hosts:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 2 Samuel 5:11

Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David. 📖

He also sent cedar logs, carpenters, and workers who built with stone.

They built a palace for David.

**Help with cedar:** Cedar is a tree whose wood is used for building.

## 2 Samuel 5:12

David knew the LORD had established him as Israel’s king. 📖

The LORD had raised up his kingdom for the sake of his people Israel.

## 2 Samuel 5:13

After coming from Hebron, David took more concubines and wives from Jerusalem. 📖

More sons and daughters were born to him.

**Help with concubines:** Concubines were women in marriage-like relationships with fewer rights than wives.

## 2 Samuel 5:14

These children were born to David in Jerusalem: 📖

Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.

## 2 Samuel 5:15

The list continues with Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, and Japhia. 📖

## 2 Samuel 5:16

It also includes Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet. 📖

## 2 Samuel 5:17

The Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over Israel. 📖

They all went to look for him.

David heard about this and went down to the stronghold.

**Help with anointed:** This means marked out as king by the use of oil.

**Help with stronghold:** A stronghold is a place protected from attack.

## 2 Samuel 5:18

The Philistines came and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim. 📖

## 2 Samuel 5:19

David asked the LORD, “Should I attack the Philistines? 📖

Will you put them in my power?”

The LORD answered, “Go up.

I will certainly put the Philistines in your power.”

## 2 Samuel 5:20

David went to Baal-perazim and defeated the Philistines. 📖

David said, “The LORD has burst against my enemies before me like a bursting flood.”

So David named the place Baal-perazim.

**Help with the flood:** David pictures the LORD’s victory as water breaking through.

## 2 Samuel 5:21

The Philistines left their idols there. 📖

David and his men carried the idols away.

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## 2 Samuel 5:22

The Philistines came again and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim. 📖

## 2 Samuel 5:23

David asked the LORD. 📖

The LORD said, “Do not march straight up.

Go around behind them and attack in front of the balsam trees.

**Help with balsam:** Balsam is a kind of tree.

## 2 Samuel 5:24

When you hear marching in the tops of the balsam trees, move quickly. 📖

This means the LORD has gone ahead of you to strike the Philistines’ camp.”

**Help with balsam:** Balsam is a kind of tree.

## 2 Samuel 5:25

David did as the LORD commanded. 📖

He struck down the Philistines from Gibeon all the way to Gezer.
