# 2 Samuel 15 — Absalom rebels and David leaves

**About this chapter**

Absalom gathers support for himself as king.

David leaves Jerusalem and arranges for news from the city.

## 2 Samuel 15:1

Later, Absalom got a chariot, horses, and 50 men to run ahead of him. 📖

**Help with chariot:** A chariot is a vehicle pulled by horses.

## 2 Samuel 15:2

Absalom got up early and stood beside the road to the city gate. 📖

People came to the king for decisions about complaints.

Absalom called and asked, “What city are you from?”

A person might answer, “Your servant is from one of Israel’s tribes.”

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

## 2 Samuel 15:3

Absalom would say, “Your claims are good and right. 📖

But the king has no official to hear you.”

## 2 Samuel 15:4

Absalom added, “If only I were appointed judge in the land! 📖

Everyone with a complaint or dispute could come to me.

I would give them justice.”

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.

## 2 Samuel 15:5

When someone came to bow before Absalom, he reached out, took hold of him, and kissed him. 📖

## 2 Samuel 15:6

Absalom did this to all the Israelites who came to the king for justice. 📖

This is how he stole the hearts of Israel’s men.

**Help with stole the hearts:** This means won their loyalty.

## 2 Samuel 15:7

After 4 years, Absalom told the king, “Please let me go to Hebron to fulfil my vow to the LORD. 📖

**Help with vow:** A vow is a serious promise.

## 2 Samuel 15:8

While living in Geshur of Aram, your servant made this vow. 📖

If the LORD brought me back to Jerusalem, I would worship the LORD in Hebron.”

**Help with vow:** A vow is a serious promise.

## 2 Samuel 15:9

The king said, “Go in peace.” 📖

Absalom got up and went to Hebron.

## 2 Samuel 15:10

Absalom sent spies throughout Israel’s tribes with a message. 📖

“When you hear the horn, say, ‘Absalom rules in Hebron!’”

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

## 2 Samuel 15:11

200 men from Jerusalem went with Absalom. 📖

They were invited guests and went innocently, knowing nothing about the plan.

## 2 Samuel 15:12

While offering sacrifices, Absalom sent for Ahithophel the Gilonite, David’s adviser, from his hometown Giloh. 📖

The plot grew stronger, and more people followed Absalom.

**Help with sacrifices:** Sacrifices are offerings to God.

## 2 Samuel 15:13

A messenger told David, “Israel’s men have given their hearts to Absalom.” 📖

**Help with hearts:** Here, hearts means loyalty.

## 2 Samuel 15:14

David told his servants in Jerusalem, “Get up, and let us flee! 📖

Otherwise, we will not escape Absalom.

Leave quickly, or he will catch us, bring disaster on us, and put the city to the sword.”

**Help with put to the sword:** This means kill the people.

## 2 Samuel 15:15

The king’s servants answered, “We are your servants. 📖

We will do whatever our lord the king decides.”

## 2 Samuel 15:16

The king left, and his whole household followed. 📖

He left 10 concubines to care for the palace.

**Help with concubines:** Concubines were women in marriage-like relationships with fewer rights than wives.

## 2 Samuel 15:17

The king left with all the people following and stopped at the last house. 📖

## 2 Samuel 15:18

All his servants marched past him. 📖

These included all the Cherethites and Pelethites and 600 Gittites who had followed him from Gath.

## 2 Samuel 15:19

The king asked Ittai the Gittite, “Why should you come with us? 📖

Go back and stay with the new king.

You are a foreigner and an exile from your homeland.

**Help with exile:** An exile is someone living away from their own land, often forced out.

## 2 Samuel 15:20

You arrived only yesterday. 📖

Should I make you wander with us today, when I do not know where I am going?

Go back and take your brothers.

May the LORD show you faithful love and faithfulness.”

## 2 Samuel 15:21

Ittai answered, “As surely as the LORD lives and my lord the king lives, I will be wherever you are. 📖

Whether it means life or death, your servant will be there!”

## 2 Samuel 15:22

David told Ittai, “Then march on.” 📖

Ittai the Gittite marched past with all his men and the little children with him.

## 2 Samuel 15:23

Everyone in the countryside cried aloud as the people passed. 📖

The king crossed the Kidron Valley, and all the people went towards the wilderness road.

## 2 Samuel 15:24

Zadok was there, with all the Levites carrying the ark of God’s covenant. 📖

They set God’s ark down.

Abiathar offered sacrifices until all the people had left the city.

**Help with Levites:** Levites were members of Levi’s tribe who served in worship.

**Help with ark of the covenant:** This sacred chest was associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

**Help with sacrifices:** Sacrifices are offerings to God.

## 2 Samuel 15:25

The king told Zadok, “Take God’s ark back to the city. 📖

If the LORD shows me favour, he will bring me back.

He will let me see the ark and his dwelling place again.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 15:26

But if he says, ‘I take no pleasure in you,’ here I am. 📖

Let him do whatever seems good to him.”

## 2 Samuel 15:27

The king told Zadok the priest, “Are you not a seer? 📖

Return to the city in peace with your son Ahimaaz, and Abiathar with his son Jonathan.

**Help with seer:** A seer is someone who receives messages from God.

## 2 Samuel 15:28

I will wait at the wilderness crossings until you send word to inform me.” 📖

**Help with crossings:** These were shallow places where people could cross the water.

## 2 Samuel 15:29

Zadok and Abiathar took God’s ark back to Jerusalem and stayed there. 📖

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 2 Samuel 15:30

David went up the Mount of Olives, crying. 📖

His head was covered, and his feet were bare.

Everyone with David covered their heads and went up crying.

## 2 Samuel 15:31

David heard, “Ahithophel is among those plotting with Absalom.” 📖

David prayed, “LORD, please turn Ahithophel’s advice into foolishness!”

## 2 Samuel 15:32

David reached the summit where he used to worship God. 📖

Hushai the Archite met him there with his robe torn and dust on his head.

**Help with torn robe and dust:** These were signs of grief.

## 2 Samuel 15:33

David told Hushai, “If you come with me, you will be a burden. 📖

## 2 Samuel 15:34

But return to the city and tell Absalom, ‘I will be your servant, my king. 📖

I served your father before, but now I will serve you.’

Then you can defeat Ahithophel’s advice for me.

## 2 Samuel 15:35

Will Zadok and Abiathar the priests not be there with you? 📖

Tell them everything you hear from the king’s palace.

## 2 Samuel 15:36

Their 2 sons are there: Ahimaaz son of Zadok and Jonathan son of Abiathar. 📖

Send them to me with everything you hear.”

## 2 Samuel 15:37

David’s friend Hushai reached Jerusalem just as Absalom was entering the city. 📖
