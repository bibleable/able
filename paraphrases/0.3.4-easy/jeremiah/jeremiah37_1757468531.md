# Jeremiah 37 — Jeremiah is accused and imprisoned

**About this chapter**

Zedekiah asks for prayer but refuses the LORD’s words.

Jeremiah is falsely accused of deserting and later asks not to be returned to a deadly prison.

## Jeremiah 37:1

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made Zedekiah son of Josiah king of Judah. 📖

He ruled instead of Coniah son of Jehoiakim.

## Jeremiah 37:2

Zedekiah, his officials, and the land’s people refused the LORD’s words spoken through Jeremiah the prophet. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:3

King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah to Jeremiah the prophet. 📖

Their message was, “Please pray to the LORD our God for us!”

## Jeremiah 37:4

Jeremiah could still come and go among the people. 📖

He had not yet been imprisoned.

## Jeremiah 37:5

Pharaoh’s army left Egypt. 📖

When the Chaldeans surrounding Jerusalem heard, they withdrew from Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 37:6

The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah the prophet. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:7

“The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘Tell Judah’s king who sent to Me this. 📖

Pharaoh’s army has marched to help you, but it will return to its own land, Egypt.

## Jeremiah 37:8

“‘The Chaldeans will return, fight this city, capture it, and burn it. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:9

“‘The LORD says, “Do not deceive yourselves by saying, ‘The Chaldeans will leave for good.’ 📖

They will not!

## Jeremiah 37:10

“‘“Even if you struck down all the Chaldeans fighting you, leaving only wounded men in tents, this would happen. 📖

They would still rise and burn this city.”’”

## Jeremiah 37:11

The Chaldean army withdrew from Jerusalem because it feared Pharaoh’s army. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:12

Then Jeremiah started to leave Jerusalem for Benjamin’s land to claim his share there among the people. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:13

At Benjamin’s Gate, the guard captain Irijah son of Shelemiah, son of Hananiah, seized him. 📖

He said, “You are deserting to the Chaldeans!”

## Jeremiah 37:14

Jeremiah replied, “That is a lie! 📖

I am not deserting to the Chaldeans!”

Irijah would not listen.

He arrested Jeremiah and took him to the officials.

## Jeremiah 37:15

The officials grew angry, beat Jeremiah, and imprisoned him in Jonathan the scribe’s house. 📖

They had made it a prison.

## Jeremiah 37:16

Jeremiah entered a dungeon cell and stayed a long time. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:17

Later, King Zedekiah brought Jeremiah to his palace and asked privately, “Is there a word from the LORD?” 📖

Jeremiah replied, “Yes.

You will be handed to Babylon’s king.”

## Jeremiah 37:18

Jeremiah asked King Zedekiah, “How did I sin against you, your servants, or these people, that you imprisoned me? 📖

## Jeremiah 37:19

“Where are your prophets who said, ‘Babylon’s king will not come against you or this land’? 📖

## Jeremiah 37:20

“Please listen, my lord the king, and hear my plea. 📖

Do not send me back to Jonathan the scribe’s house, or I will die there.”

## Jeremiah 37:21

King Zedekiah ordered Jeremiah to stay in the guard’s courtyard and receive a loaf daily from the bakers’ street. 📖

This continued until the city’s bread ran out.

Jeremiah remained in the guard’s courtyard.
