# Jeremiah 33 — Restoration and lasting promises

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks again while Jeremiah is confined.

He promises healing and restoration, then speaks about His commitments to David and the Levites.

## Jeremiah 33:1

While Jeremiah remained confined in the guard’s courtyard, the LORD’s word came to him a second time. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:2

“The LORD made the earth, formed it, and established it. 📖

His name is the LORD.

This is what He says.

## Jeremiah 33:3

“Call to Me, and I will answer. 📖

I will show you great things beyond your discovery, things you do not know.

## Jeremiah 33:4

“The LORD, Israel’s God, speaks about this city’s houses and Judah’s kings’ palaces. 📖

They were torn down for defense against attack ramps and swords.

## Jeremiah 33:5

“The Chaldeans come to fight and fill those places with dead bodies. 📖

I will strike those men in My anger and fury.

I hid My face from this city because of all its wickedness.

## Jeremiah 33:6

“Yet I will bring health and healing to it and heal its people. 📖

I will reveal abundant peace and truth to them.

## Jeremiah 33:7

“I will restore Judah and Israel from captivity and rebuild them as before. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:8

“I will cleanse all the wrongdoing they committed against Me and forgive all their rebellious sins. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:9

“This city will bring Me fame, joy, praise, and glory before all earth’s nations. 📖

They will hear all the good I do for it.

They will tremble in awe at all the goodness and prosperity I provide.

## Jeremiah 33:10

“The LORD says, ‘You call this place a wasteland without people or animals. 📖

Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets are deserted, without people or animals.

But sounds will be heard again,

## Jeremiah 33:11

“‘sounds of joy and gladness, voices of brides and bridegrooms, and people bringing thank offerings to the LORD’s house. 📖

They will say, “Thank the LORD of Hosts, because the LORD is good.

His devoted love lasts forever.”

I will restore the land from captivity as before,’ says the LORD.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 33:12

“The LORD of Hosts says, ‘This deserted place and all its cities have no people or animals. 📖

But there will again be pastures where shepherds rest their flocks.

## Jeremiah 33:13

“‘Flocks will again pass under the hands of the one counting them,’ says the LORD. 📖

‘This will happen in hill-country cities, the foothills, the Negev, Benjamin’s land, Jerusalem’s surrounding cities, and Judah’s cities.’

## Jeremiah 33:14

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when I will fulfill My gracious promise to Israel’s house and Judah’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:15

“In those days and at that time, I will make a righteous Branch grow for David. 📖

He will give justice and righteousness in the land.

**Help with Branch**

The plant image describes a ruler connected with David’s royal line.

## Jeremiah 33:16

“In those days, Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will live safely. 📖

It will be called The LORD Our Righteousness.

## Jeremiah 33:17

“The LORD says, ‘David will never lack a man to sit on Israel’s house’s throne, 📖

## Jeremiah 33:18

“‘and the Levite priests will never lack a man before Me offering burnt offerings, burning grain offerings, and presenting sacrifices.’” 📖

**Help with Levites and offerings**

Levites are members of Levi’s tribe.

These offerings are gifts presented in worship.

## Jeremiah 33:19

The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:20

The LORD says, “If you could break My covenant with day and night so that their appointed times stopped, 📖

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

The next verse continues this condition.

## Jeremiah 33:21

“then My covenant with David My servant and My servants the Levite priests could also be broken. 📖

David would no longer have a son ruling on his throne.

## Jeremiah 33:22

“Heaven’s army cannot be counted, and seashore sand cannot be measured. 📖

In the same way, I will multiply My servant David’s descendants and the Levites serving before Me.”

## Jeremiah 33:23

The LORD’s word also came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:24

“Have you noticed what these people say? 📖

‘The LORD rejected the two families He chose.’

They despise My people and no longer consider them a nation.

## Jeremiah 33:25

“The LORD says, ‘If I had not established My covenant with day and night and heaven’s and earth’s fixed order, 📖

## Jeremiah 33:26

“‘then I would also reject Jacob’s descendants and My servant David’s descendants. 📖

I would not choose his descendants to rule Abraham’s, Isaac’s, and Jacob’s descendants.

But I will restore them from captivity and have compassion on them.’”
