# Jeremiah 24 — Two baskets of figs

**About this chapter**

The LORD shows Jeremiah good figs and bad figs.

He explains how they picture different futures for Judah’s exiles and those left behind.

## Jeremiah 24:1

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, away from Jerusalem to Babylon. 📖

He also took Judah’s officials, craftsmen, and metalworkers.

Afterward, the LORD showed me two baskets of figs in front of His temple.

## Jeremiah 24:2

One basket held very good figs, like early-ripening figs. 📖

The other held figs so bad they could not be eaten.

## Jeremiah 24:3

The LORD asked, “Jeremiah, what do you see?” 📖

I replied, “Figs!

The good ones are very good, and the bad ones are too bad to eat.”

## Jeremiah 24:4

Then the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:5

The LORD, Israel’s God, says, “Like these good figs, I regard Judah’s exiles as good. 📖

I sent them from this place to the Chaldeans’ land.

## Jeremiah 24:6

“I will watch over them for good and return them to this land. 📖

I will build them up, not tear them down, and plant them, not uproot them.

## Jeremiah 24:7

“I will give them a heart to know Me, that I am the LORD. 📖

They will be My people, and I will be their God.

They will return to Me with all their heart.

## Jeremiah 24:8

The LORD says, “The bad figs are too bad to eat. 📖

I will treat Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, and Jerusalem’s remaining people like them.

This includes those still in this land and those living in Egypt.

## Jeremiah 24:9

“I will make them a horror and an offense to all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

They will be disgraced, scorned, mocked, and cursed wherever I drive them.

## Jeremiah 24:10

“I will send sword, famine, and plague against them. 📖

They will perish from the land I gave them and their fathers.”
