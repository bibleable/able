# Jeremiah 5 — The search for truth in Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges Jerusalem’s injustice and unfaithfulness.

Jeremiah finds failure among both poor people and powerful leaders.

## Jeremiah 5:1

“Go through Jerusalem’s streets and search its public squares. 📖

Look and pay attention.

If you find even one person acting justly and seeking truth, I will forgive the city.”

## Jeremiah 5:2

“They say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives,’ but they swear falsely.” 📖

## Jeremiah 5:3

LORD, do Your eyes not look for truth? 📖

You struck them, but they felt no pain.

You finished them off, but they refused discipline.

They made their faces harder than stone and refused to turn back.

**Help with hard faces**

The image describes stubborn refusal to change.

## Jeremiah 5:4

I said, “These are only poor people. 📖

They have acted foolishly because they do not know the LORD’s way or their God’s justice.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah reports his own first explanation.

The next verse describes what he finds among powerful people too.

## Jeremiah 5:5

“I will go and speak to the powerful. 📖

They surely know the LORD’s way and their God’s justice.”

But they too had all broken the yoke and torn off the chains.

**Help with the yoke**

The broken yoke and chains picture rejecting God’s authority.

## Jeremiah 5:6

So a forest lion will strike them down, and a desert wolf will tear them apart. 📖

A leopard will wait near their cities and tear apart everyone who goes out.

Their rebellions and unfaithful deeds are many.

## Jeremiah 5:7

“Why should I forgive you? 📖

Your children abandoned Me and swore by gods that are not gods.

I met their needs, but they committed adultery and gathered at prostitutes’ houses.

**Help with adultery and prostitutes**

Adultery is sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

Prostitutes sell sexual services.

## Jeremiah 5:8

“They are well-fed male horses full of desire, each neighing for his neighbor’s wife. 📖

**Help with the horses**

The image describes sexual desire directed toward other people’s wives.

## Jeremiah 5:9

The LORD declares, “Should I not punish them for these things? 📖

Should I not take vengeance for Myself on a nation like this?

## Jeremiah 5:10

“Go through her vineyards and ruin them, but do not destroy them completely. 📖

Strip off her branches, because they do not belong to the LORD.

## Jeremiah 5:11

“Israel’s house and Judah’s house have been completely unfaithful to Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:12

They have lied about the LORD and said, “He will do nothing. 📖

No harm will come to us.

We will not see sword or famine.

## Jeremiah 5:13

“The prophets are only wind, because the word is not in them. 📖

Let their own predictions happen to them!”

**About these claims**

These are the people’s words, not the narrator’s assurance of safety.

## Jeremiah 5:14

The LORD God of Hosts says, “Because you said this, I will make My words fire in your mouth. 📖

These people will be the wood it burns.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 5:15

The LORD declares, “Israel’s house, I am bringing a distant nation against you. 📖

It is an established, ancient nation.

You do not know its language or understand its speech.

## Jeremiah 5:16

“Their arrow cases are like open graves. 📖

They are all mighty men.

## Jeremiah 5:17

“They will consume your harvest and food, your sons and daughters. 📖

They will eat your flocks and herds, your vines and fig trees.

With swords, they will destroy the fortified cities you trust.”

## Jeremiah 5:18

The LORD declares, “Yet even in those days, I will not completely destroy you. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:19

“The people will ask, ‘Why has the LORD our God done all this to us?’ 📖

Tell them, ‘You abandoned Me and served foreign gods in your land.

So you will serve foreigners in a land that is not your own.’”

## Jeremiah 5:20

Announce this in Jacob’s house and proclaim it in Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:21

“Hear this, foolish people without understanding, who have eyes but do not see and ears but do not hear. 📖

**Help with the image**

The passage challenges refusal to listen.

It does not make a physical disability a moral fault.

## Jeremiah 5:22

The LORD declares, “Do you not fear Me? 📖

Do you not tremble before Me?

I set sand as the sea’s boundary, a lasting barrier it cannot cross.

The waves rise but cannot overcome it.

They roar but cannot cross it.

## Jeremiah 5:23

“But these people have stubborn, rebellious hearts. 📖

They have turned aside and gone away.

## Jeremiah 5:24

“They have not said in their hearts, ‘Let us fear the LORD our God. 📖

He gives autumn and spring rain in season.

He keeps the appointed harvest weeks for us.’

## Jeremiah 5:25

“Your wrongdoing has turned these things away. 📖

Your sins have kept My good gifts from you.

## Jeremiah 5:26

“Wicked men are among My people. 📖

They watch like bird catchers waiting in hiding.

They set traps to capture people.

**Help with the trap image**

The bird-catching comparison describes people using deception to gain power over others.

The passage condemns these actions.

## Jeremiah 5:27

“Their houses are full of deceit, like cages full of birds. 📖

So they have become powerful and rich.

## Jeremiah 5:28

“They have grown fat and sleek and surpassed others in wicked deeds. 📖

They do not defend children without fathers so that those children may prosper.

They do not defend needy people’s rights.

## Jeremiah 5:29

The LORD declares, “Should I not punish them for these things? 📖

Should I not take vengeance for Myself on a nation like this?

## Jeremiah 5:30

“Something horrible and shocking has happened in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:31

“The prophets speak false messages, and the priests rule by their own authority. 📖

My people love it this way.

But what will you do at the end?”
