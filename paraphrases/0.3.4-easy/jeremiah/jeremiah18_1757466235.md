# Jeremiah 18 — The potter and the clay

**About this chapter**

The LORD teaches Jeremiah through a potter’s work.

The people reject the warning, some plot against Jeremiah, and he prays for severe judgment.

## Jeremiah 18:1

This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:2

“Go at once to the potter’s house. 📖

There I will give you My message.”

**Help with potter**

A potter shapes clay into useful objects.

## Jeremiah 18:3

I went to the potter’s house and saw him working at the wheel. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:4

The clay vessel he shaped became flawed in his hand. 📖

So he shaped it into another vessel, as he thought best.

**Help with vessel and wheel**

A vessel is a container.

A potter uses a turning wheel to shape clay.

## Jeremiah 18:5

Then the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:6

The LORD declares, “House of Israel, can I not treat you as this potter treats his clay? 📖

You are in My hand like clay in the potter’s hand, house of Israel.

## Jeremiah 18:7

“At any time, I may announce that a nation or kingdom will be uprooted, torn down, and destroyed. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:8

“But if that warned nation turns from its evil, I will change My mind about the disaster I planned. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:9

“At another time, I may announce that I will build and establish a nation or kingdom. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:10

“But if it does evil in My sight and ignores My voice, I will withdraw the good I intended. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:11

“Tell Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s residents what the LORD says. 📖

‘Look!

I am planning disaster for you and making a plan against you.

Each must turn from evil ways and correct your ways and actions.’

## Jeremiah 18:12

“But they will reply, ‘It is hopeless. 📖

We will follow our own plans, and each will follow his stubborn, evil heart.’”

## Jeremiah 18:13

The LORD says, “Ask among the nations, ‘Who has heard such things?’ 📖

Virgin Israel has done something very terrible.

## Jeremiah 18:14

“Does Lebanon’s snow leave its rocky slopes? 📖

Do its cool waters flowing from far away run dry?

## Jeremiah 18:15

“Yet My people forgot Me. 📖

They burn incense to worthless idols that make them stumble.

They leave the ancient roads for rough side paths instead of the highway.

**Help with incense and paths**

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

Leaving the proper road pictures leaving God’s ways.

## Jeremiah 18:16

“They made their land deserted, a lasting object of scorn. 📖

Everyone passing will be horrified and shake his head.

## Jeremiah 18:17

“I will scatter them before the enemy like an east wind. 📖

On their day of disaster, I will show them My back instead of My face.”

## Jeremiah 18:18

Some said, “Come, let us plan against Jeremiah. 📖

The priest will never lose the law, the wise will never lose advice, or the prophet a divine message.

Let us accuse him and ignore all his words.”

## Jeremiah 18:19

LORD, pay attention to me. 📖

Hear what my accusers say!

## Jeremiah 18:20

Should good be repaid with evil? 📖

Yet they dug a pit for me.

Remember how I stood before You to speak good for them and turn Your anger away.

## Jeremiah 18:21

So give their children to famine and pour the sword’s power over them. 📖

Let their wives become childless widows and their husbands die by disease.

Let their young men be killed by swords in battle.

**About this severe prayer**

Jeremiah asks for deaths of children, husbands, and young men.

These requests remain his prayer; they are not instructions to readers.

## Jeremiah 18:22

Let cries come from their houses when You suddenly bring raiders against them. 📖

They dug a pit to capture me and hid traps for my feet.

## Jeremiah 18:23

LORD, You know all their deadly plans against me. 📖

Do not wipe out their guilt or remove their sin from Your sight.

Let them be overthrown before You.

Deal with them in Your anger’s time.
