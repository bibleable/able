# Jeremiah 11 — The broken covenant and a plot against Jeremiah

**About this chapter**

The LORD condemns the breaking of His covenant.

Jeremiah then learns about a plot to kill him and asks God for judgment.

## Jeremiah 11:1

This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:2

“Listen to this covenant’s words and tell them to Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s residents. 📖

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Jeremiah 11:3

“Tell them what the LORD, Israel’s God, says. 📖

‘Cursed is the person who does not obey this covenant’s words,

## Jeremiah 11:4

“‘which I commanded your ancestors when I brought them from Egypt, from the iron furnace. 📖

I said, “Obey Me and do everything I command.

You will be My people, and I will be your God.”

**Help with iron furnace**

A furnace is a very hot place for working metal.

The image describes Egypt as a place of harsh suffering.

## Jeremiah 11:5

“‘This was to establish My oath to your ancestors, giving them land flowing with milk and honey, as today.’” 📖

I answered, “Amen, LORD.”

**Help with these words**

Milk and honey picture a rich, productive land.

Amen expresses agreement.

## Jeremiah 11:6

The LORD told me, “Announce all these words in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. 📖

Say, ‘Hear this covenant’s words and do them.

## Jeremiah 11:7

“‘From the day I brought your fathers from Egypt until today, I strongly warned them again and again. 📖

I said, “Obey My voice.”

## Jeremiah 11:8

“‘But they would not obey or turn their ears to Me. 📖

Each followed his stubborn, evil heart.

So I brought all this covenant’s curses on them.

I had commanded them to follow it, but they did not.’”

## Jeremiah 11:9

The LORD told me, “There is a secret plot among Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s residents. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:10

“They returned to the sins of ancestors who refused to obey My words. 📖

They followed and served other gods.

Israel’s house and Judah’s house broke the covenant I made with their fathers.

## Jeremiah 11:11

“So the LORD says, ‘I will bring disaster they cannot escape. 📖

They will cry to Me, but I will not listen.

## Jeremiah 11:12

“‘Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s residents will cry to the gods they burned incense to. 📖

But those gods will certainly not save them in disaster.

## Jeremiah 11:13

“‘Judah, your gods are as many as your cities. 📖

Your shameful altars for burning incense to Baal are as many as Jerusalem’s streets.’

**Help with Baal and incense**

Baal is the name of another god.

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Jeremiah 11:14

“As for you, do not pray for these people or cry out for them. 📖

I will not listen when they call to Me in their disaster.

## Jeremiah 11:15

“What right does My beloved have in My house after so many evil plans? 📖

Can meat set apart as holy prevent your doom?

When you act wickedly, you rejoice.

## Jeremiah 11:16

“The LORD once called you a flourishing olive tree, beautiful with well-shaped fruit. 📖

But with a mighty roar, He will set it on fire and burn its branches.

## Jeremiah 11:17

“The LORD of Hosts planted you and has ordered disaster against you. 📖

Israel’s house and Judah’s house brought this on themselves by evil.

They angered Me by burning incense to Baal.”

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 11:18

The LORD told me, so I knew. 📖

Then You showed me their deeds.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah speaks of what the LORD showed him.

## Jeremiah 11:19

I was like a gentle lamb led to be killed. 📖

I did not know they plotted against me.

They said, “Let us destroy the tree with its fruit.

Let us cut him off from the living so that his name is remembered no more.”

**Help with the images**

The plotters want Jeremiah dead and forgotten.

## Jeremiah 11:20

LORD of Hosts, You judge rightly and examine hearts and minds. 📖

Let me see Your vengeance on them, because I have entrusted my case to You.

## Jeremiah 11:21

The LORD speaks about Anathoth’s people, who want to kill you. 📖

They say, “Do not prophesy in the LORD’s name, or we will kill you.”

## Jeremiah 11:22

The LORD of Hosts says, “I will punish them. 📖

Their young men will die by swords, and their sons and daughters by famine.

## Jeremiah 11:23

“No survivors will remain. 📖

I will bring disaster on Anathoth’s people in the year of their punishment.”
