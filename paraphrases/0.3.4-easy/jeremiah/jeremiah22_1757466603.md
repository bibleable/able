# Jeremiah 22 — A warning to Judah’s royal house

**About this chapter**

The LORD commands kings to act justly and warns particular rulers about judgment and exile.

## Jeremiah 22:1

The LORD says, “Go down to Judah’s king’s palace and announce this message. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:2

“Say, ‘Hear the LORD’s word, king of Judah sitting on David’s throne. 📖

Your officials and your people entering these gates must hear too.

## Jeremiah 22:3

“‘The LORD says, “Give justice and do what is right. 📖

Rescue robbery victims from those who oppress them.

Do no wrong or violence to foreigners, children without fathers, or widows.

Do not shed innocent blood here.

## Jeremiah 22:4

“‘“If you truly obey these commands, kings sitting on David’s throne will enter this palace’s gates. 📖

They, their officials, and their people will ride chariots and horses.

## Jeremiah 22:5

“‘“But if you disobey these words, I swear by Myself,” declares the LORD, “this house will become rubble.”’” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:6

The LORD says about Judah’s king’s house, “You are like Gilead to Me, like Lebanon’s summit. 📖

But I will surely turn you into a desert, like empty cities.

## Jeremiah 22:7

“I will appoint destroyers against you, each carrying his weapons. 📖

They will cut down your finest cedars and throw them into fire.

## Jeremiah 22:8

“Many nations will pass this city and ask each other, ‘Why did the LORD do this to this great city?’ 📖

## Jeremiah 22:9

“People will answer, ‘They abandoned the LORD their God’s covenant and worshiped and served other gods.’” 📖

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Jeremiah 22:10

Do not weep or mourn for the dead man. 📖

Weep bitterly for the exiled man, because he will never return to see his native land.

## Jeremiah 22:11

The LORD speaks about Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who succeeded his father Josiah but left this place. 📖

“He will never return,

## Jeremiah 22:12

“but will die in the place where he was exiled. 📖

He will never see this land again.”

**Help with exile**

Exile means being forced away from one’s own land.

## Jeremiah 22:13

“How terrible for the man building his palace and upper rooms through wrongdoing and injustice! 📖

He makes his countrymen work without pay and does not give them their wages.

## Jeremiah 22:14

“He says, ‘I will build a great palace with spacious upper rooms.’ 📖

He cuts windows, covers it with cedar panels, and paints it bright red.

**Help with bright red**

The source names the color vermilion.

## Jeremiah 22:15

“Does having the finest cedar make you a king? 📖

Did your father not eat and drink?

He gave justice and did right, so things went well for him.

## Jeremiah 22:16

“He defended poor and needy people’s cause, so things went well for him. 📖

Is this not what it means to know Me?” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 22:17

“But your eyes and heart seek only dishonest gain, innocent bloodshed, and the practice of threats and oppression.” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:18

The LORD says about Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, “They will not mourn for him. 📖

They will not say, ‘Alas, my brother!

Alas, my sister!’

They will not say, ‘Alas, my master!

Alas, his splendor!’

**Help with Alas**

Alas is a cry of grief.

## Jeremiah 22:19

“He will be buried like a donkey, dragged away and thrown outside Jerusalem’s gates. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:20

“Go up to Lebanon and cry out. 📖

Raise your voice in Bashan and cry from Abarim.

All your lovers have been crushed.

## Jeremiah 22:21

“I warned you when you felt secure, but you said, ‘I will not listen.’ 📖

Since youth, this has been your way, refusing My voice.

## Jeremiah 22:22

“The wind will drive all your shepherds away, and your lovers will go into captivity. 📖

Then all your wickedness will leave you ashamed and humiliated.

## Jeremiah 22:23

“You who live in Lebanon, nesting in cedars, how you will groan when pain grips you like childbirth!” 📖

**Help with nesting in cedars**

The image addresses those living securely in costly surroundings.

## Jeremiah 22:24

The LORD declares, “As surely as I live, Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, I would pull you off. 📖

Even if you were a signet ring on My right hand, I would remove you.

**Help with signet ring**

A signet ring bears a personal mark used as a seal.

The image describes a valued position being taken away.

## Jeremiah 22:25

“I will hand you to those you dread, who want to kill you. 📖

They are Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and the Chaldeans.

## Jeremiah 22:26

“I will throw you and the mother who bore you into another land where neither of you was born. 📖

Both of you will die there.

## Jeremiah 22:27

“You will never return to the land you long for.” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:28

Is this man Coniah a despised, broken pot, a jar no one wants? 📖

Why are he and his descendants thrown into an unfamiliar land?

## Jeremiah 22:29

Land, land, land, hear the LORD’s word! 📖

## Jeremiah 22:30

The LORD says, “Record this man as childless, a man who will not prosper during his lifetime. 📖

None of his descendants will prosper to sit on David’s throne or rule again in Judah.”

**About childless**

The preceding verse mentions his descendants.

The judgment here specifically denies success in royal succession.

Both the word “childless” and the reference to descendants are retained.
