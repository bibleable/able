# Jeremiah 1 — The LORD calls Jeremiah

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah describes when the LORD spoke to him and called him as a prophet.

The LORD promises help but also warns Jeremiah to obey.

## Jeremiah 1:1

These are the words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah. 📖

He was one of the priests in Anathoth, in Benjamin’s territory.

## Jeremiah 1:2

The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah in the thirteenth year of Josiah’s rule. 📖

Josiah son of Amon was king of Judah.

## Jeremiah 1:3

The LORD continued speaking through the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. 📖

This continued until the fifth month of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah.

Then Jerusalem’s people went into exile.

**Help with exile**

Exile means being forced away from one’s own land.

## Jeremiah 1:4

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

**About the voice**

Jeremiah now tells what happened to him.

## Jeremiah 1:5

“Before I formed you in the womb, I knew you. 📖

Before your birth, I set you apart and appointed you as a prophet to the nations.”

**Help with a prophet**

A prophet speaks God’s message.

## Jeremiah 1:6

I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! 📖

I do not know how to speak, because I am only a child!”

## Jeremiah 1:7

But the LORD told me, “Do not say, ‘I am only a child.’ 📖

You must go to everyone I send you to.

You must say everything I command.

## Jeremiah 1:8

“Do not fear them, because I am with you to rescue you,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:9

Then the LORD reached out His hand and touched my mouth. 📖

He told me, “Look!

I have put My words in your mouth.

## Jeremiah 1:10

“Today I appoint you over nations and kingdoms. 📖

You are to uproot and tear down, destroy and overthrow, build and plant.”

**Help with these images**

Pulling out plants, destroying buildings, and rebuilding picture judgment and restoration.

## Jeremiah 1:11

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

He asked, “Jeremiah, what do you see?”

I replied, “I see a branch from an almond tree.”

## Jeremiah 1:12

The LORD said, “You have seen correctly. 📖

I am watching over My word to carry it out.”

## Jeremiah 1:13

Again the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

He asked, “What do you see?”

I replied, “I see a boiling pot tilting toward us from the north.”

## Jeremiah 1:14

The LORD told me, “Disaster from the north will be poured over everyone living in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:15

“I am about to call all the clans and kingdoms of the north,” declares the LORD. 📖

“Their kings will set up thrones at the entrance to Jerusalem’s gates.

They will attack all its surrounding walls and all Judah’s other cities.

## Jeremiah 1:16

“I will announce My judgments against them for all their wickedness. 📖

They have abandoned Me, burned incense to other gods, and worshiped things made by their own hands.

**Help with incense**

Incense is a substance burned to give a sweet smell.

## Jeremiah 1:17

“Get ready. 📖

Stand up and tell them everything I command you.

Do not let them frighten you, or I will terrify you before them.

## Jeremiah 1:18

“Look! 📖

Today I have made you like a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the whole land.

You will stand against Judah’s kings, officials, priests, and the people of the land.

**Help with the images**

The strong city, pillar, and walls picture strength God gives Jeremiah to face opposition.

## Jeremiah 1:19

“They will fight you but never overcome you. 📖

I am with you to rescue you,” declares the LORD.
