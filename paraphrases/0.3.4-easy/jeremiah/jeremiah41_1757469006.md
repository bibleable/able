# Jeremiah 41 — Murder at Mizpah

**About this chapter**

Ishmael murders Gedaliah and others and takes captives.

Johanan recovers the captives, but fear of Babylon leads the group toward Egypt.

## Jeremiah 41:1

In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, came to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah. 📖

Ishmael belonged to the royal family and was a chief royal officer.

Ten men came with him, and they ate together.

## Jeremiah 41:2

Ishmael son of Nethaniah and his ten men rose and killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, with swords. 📖

They killed Babylon’s king’s appointed governor of the land.

## Jeremiah 41:3

Ishmael also killed all the Jews with Gedaliah at Mizpah and the Chaldean soldiers there. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:4

On the second day after Gedaliah’s murder, no one yet knew about it. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:5

Eighty men came from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria with grain offerings and frankincense for the LORD’s house. 📖

They had shaved their beards, torn their clothes, and cut themselves.

**About these actions**

They show mourning in this account.

They are not instructions for readers to injure themselves.

Frankincense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Jeremiah 41:6

Ishmael son of Nethaniah left Mizpah to meet them, weeping as he went. 📖

He told them, “Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam.”

## Jeremiah 41:7

When they entered the city, Ishmael son of Nethaniah and his men killed them. 📖

They threw them into a water pit.

## Jeremiah 41:8

But ten men told Ishmael, “Do not kill us! 📖

We have hidden wheat, barley, oil, and honey in the field!”

So he spared them instead of killing them with the others.

## Jeremiah 41:9

The pit held the bodies of the men Ishmael killed along with Gedaliah. 📖

It was a large pit King Asa made because he feared Baasha king of Israel.

Ishmael son of Nethaniah filled it with the dead.

## Jeremiah 41:10

Ishmael captured all Mizpah’s remaining people, the king’s daughters and everyone else there. 📖

Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, had placed them under Gedaliah son of Ahikam.

Ishmael son of Nethaniah took them captive and started toward the Ammonites.

## Jeremiah 41:11

Johanan son of Kareah and all his army commanders heard all Ishmael son of Nethaniah’s crimes. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:12

They took all their men to fight Ishmael son of Nethaniah. 📖

They found him near Gibeon’s great pool.

## Jeremiah 41:13

Everyone with Ishmael rejoiced when they saw Johanan son of Kareah and all his army commanders. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:14

All the people Ishmael captured at Mizpah turned and went to Johanan son of Kareah. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:15

But Ishmael son of Nethaniah and eight men escaped Johanan and went to the Ammonites. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:16

Johanan son of Kareah and all his commanders took the people they recovered from Ishmael son of Nethaniah. 📖

Ishmael had killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam.

The recovered people from Mizpah included soldiers, women, children, and court officials brought back from Gibeon.

## Jeremiah 41:17

They went to Geruth Chimham near Bethlehem and stayed there, planning to continue into Egypt, 📖

## Jeremiah 41:18

to escape the Chaldeans. 📖

They feared them because Ishmael son of Nethaniah killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, Babylon’s king’s appointed governor.
