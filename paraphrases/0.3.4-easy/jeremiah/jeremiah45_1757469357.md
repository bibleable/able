# Jeremiah 45 — A word to Baruch

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah gives Baruch a personal message from the LORD while Baruch writes the scroll.

## Jeremiah 45:1

Baruch son of Neriah wrote these words on a scroll as the prophet Jeremiah dictated. 📖

It was Jehoiakim son of Josiah’s fourth year as king of Judah.

Jeremiah spoke this message to Baruch.

## Jeremiah 45:2

“The LORD, Israel’s God, says to you, Baruch, 📖

## Jeremiah 45:3

“‘You said, “How terrible for me! 📖

The LORD added sorrow to my pain.

I am tired from groaning and have found no rest.”’”

## Jeremiah 45:4

Jeremiah was to tell Baruch this. 📖

“The LORD says, ‘Throughout the land, I will destroy what I built and uproot what I planted.

## Jeremiah 45:5

“‘Do you seek great things for yourself? 📖

Stop seeking them!’ declares the LORD.

‘I will bring disaster on every living creature.

But wherever you go, I will give you your life like goods gained in war.’”

**Help with life as goods gained in war**

The image means Baruch will escape with his life.
