# Jeremiah 51 — Babylon sinks and will not rise

**About this chapter**

The LORD announces Babylon’s destruction in repeated images and commands.

Jeremiah sends a scroll to Babylon and instructs Seraiah to sink it in the Euphrates.

## Jeremiah 51:1

The LORD says, “I will stir a destroyer’s spirit against Babylon and Leb-kamai’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:2

“I will send strangers to winnow Babylon and empty its land. 📖

They will come from every side on its day of disaster.

**Help with winnow**

Winnowing separates grain from light unwanted material.

Here the image pictures people swept away.

## Jeremiah 51:3

“Do not let the archer bend his bow or put on armor. 📖

Do not spare her young men.

Give her whole army over to destruction!

## Jeremiah 51:4

“They will fall killed in the Chaldeans’ land, pierced in her streets. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:5

“Israel and Judah have not been abandoned by their God, the LORD of Hosts. 📖

Their land is full of guilt before the Holy One of Israel, but He has not abandoned them.”

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 51:6

Flee Babylon and escape with your lives! 📖

Do not be destroyed in her punishment.

This is the LORD’s time of vengeance, and He will repay what she deserves.

## Jeremiah 51:7

Babylon was a gold cup in the LORD’s hand that made the whole earth drunk. 📖

The nations drank her wine, so they lost their senses.

## Jeremiah 51:8

Babylon has suddenly fallen and shattered. 📖

Wail for her and get balm for her pain.

Perhaps she can be healed.

**Help with balm**

Balm is a substance used to soothe or treat wounds.

## Jeremiah 51:9

“We tried to heal Babylon, but she could not be healed. 📖

Leave her!

Let each return to his own land, because her judgment reaches the sky and clouds.”

## Jeremiah 51:10

“The LORD has shown that we are right. 📖

Come, let us tell in Zion what the LORD our God has done.”

## Jeremiah 51:11

Sharpen arrows and fill the arrow cases! 📖

The LORD stirred the Medes’ kings because His plan is to destroy Babylon.

It is the LORD’s vengeance, vengeance for His temple.

## Jeremiah 51:12

Raise a banner against Babylon’s walls, post guards and watchmen, and prepare a surprise attack. 📖

The LORD planned and carried out what He spoke against Babylon’s people.

## Jeremiah 51:13

You who live by many waters and have rich treasures, your end has come. 📖

Your life’s thread is cut.

## Jeremiah 51:14

The LORD of Hosts swore by Himself, “I will fill you with men like locusts. 📖

They will shout in victory over you.”

## Jeremiah 51:15

The LORD made the earth by His power and established the world by His wisdom. 📖

He stretched out the heavens by His understanding.

## Jeremiah 51:16

When He thunders, waters in the heavens roar. 📖

He makes clouds rise from earth’s ends.

He makes lightning with rain and brings wind from His storehouses.

## Jeremiah 51:17

Every man lacks understanding and knowledge. 📖

Every goldsmith is ashamed of his idols, because his cast images are false and have no breath.

**Help with cast images**

These are objects made by pouring melted metal into a mold.

## Jeremiah 51:18

They are worthless, something to mock. 📖

They will perish when their punishment comes.

## Jeremiah 51:19

The Portion of Jacob is different, because He is the Maker of everything and of His own tribe. 📖

His name is the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with Portion and tribe**

The title describes the LORD as the One belonging to His people.

The tribe of His inheritance describes the people as His own.

## Jeremiah 51:20

“You are My war club, My weapon for battle. 📖

With you, I shatter nations and ruin kingdoms.

## Jeremiah 51:21

“With you, I shatter horse and rider, chariot and driver. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:22

“With you, I shatter man and woman, old man and youth, young man and young woman. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:23

“With you, I shatter shepherd and flock, farmer and oxen, governors and officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:24

The LORD declares, “Before your eyes, I will repay Babylon and all Chaldea’s people for all their evil in Zion. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:25

The LORD declares, “Destroying mountain, you devastate the whole earth, and I oppose you. 📖

I will stretch My hand against you, roll you over cliffs, and make you a burned mountain.

**Help with the mountain**

The mountain pictures Babylon’s great destructive power being brought down.

## Jeremiah 51:26

The LORD declares, “No one will take a cornerstone or foundation stone from you, because you will be deserted forever. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:27

“Raise a banner in the land and sound the ram’s horn among nations! 📖

Prepare the nations and call Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz against her.

Appoint a captain and bring horses like swarming locusts.

## Jeremiah 51:28

“Prepare nations for battle against her, the Medes’ kings, governors, all their officials, and all lands they rule. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:29

“The earth shakes and twists because the LORD’s plan stands. 📖

He will make Babylon’s land deserted, without people.

## Jeremiah 51:30

“Babylon’s warriors stopped fighting and sit in their fortresses. 📖

Their strength is gone, and they have become like women.

Babylon’s homes burn, and its gate bars break.

**About the comparison**

The source compares defeated warriors with women in this scene.

It does not establish that all women lack strength.

## Jeremiah 51:31

“Messenger races to meet messenger, one following another, to tell Babylon’s king his city is captured from end to end. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:32

“The shallow river crossings are seized, the marshes burned, and the soldiers terrified.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:33

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Daughter Babylon is like a threshing floor when it is trampled. 📖

Her harvest time will come very soon.

**Help with threshing floor**

This is a place where grain is separated from its stalks.

Here it pictures Babylon being struck in judgment.

## Jeremiah 51:34

“Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon consumed and crushed me. 📖

He set me aside like an empty container and swallowed me like a monster.

He filled his stomach with my fine foods and vomited me out.

## Jeremiah 51:35

Zion’s resident says, “May the violence against me and my body fall on Babylon.” 📖

Jerusalem says, “May my blood fall on Chaldea’s people.”

**About the voices**

Zion or Jerusalem speaks as a person harmed by Babylon.

The preceding verse gives the reason for this request.

## Jeremiah 51:36

The LORD says, “I will argue your case and take vengeance for you. 📖

I will dry her sea and make her springs dry.

## Jeremiah 51:37

“Babylon will become rubble, a home for jackals, a horror and object of scorn, without people. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:38

“They will roar together like young lions and growl like lion cubs. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:39

The LORD declares, “While they are hot, I will give them a feast and make them drunk so they celebrate. 📖

Then they will sleep forever and never wake.

## Jeremiah 51:40

“I will bring them to slaughter like lambs, like rams with male goats. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:41

“How Sheshach is captured! 📖

The praise of all the earth is seized!

What a horror Babylon became among the nations!

## Jeremiah 51:42

“The sea rose over Babylon and covered her with violent waves. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:43

“Her cities became deserted, dry, and parched, where no one lives or passes. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:44

“I will punish Bel in Babylon and make him vomit what he swallowed. 📖

Nations will no longer flow to him, and even Babylon’s wall will fall.

**Help with Bel**

Bel is a name associated with Babylon’s god.

## Jeremiah 51:45

“My people, come out! 📖

Each must save his life from the LORD’s fierce anger.

## Jeremiah 51:46

“Do not lose heart or fear when rumors reach the land. 📖

One year comes a rumor, and the next year another, of violence and ruler against ruler.

## Jeremiah 51:47

“The days are coming when I will punish Babylon’s idols. 📖

Her whole land will be ashamed, with all her dead lying within her.

## Jeremiah 51:48

The LORD declares, “Heaven, earth, and everything in them will shout for joy over Babylon. 📖

Destroyers from the north will attack her.

## Jeremiah 51:49

“Babylon must fall because of Israel’s slain, just as people across the earth fell because of Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:50

“You who escaped the sword, leave without delay! 📖

Remember the LORD from far away, and bring Jerusalem to mind.”

## Jeremiah 51:51

“We are ashamed because we heard insults. 📖

Disgrace covers our faces because foreigners entered the LORD’s house’s holy places.”

## Jeremiah 51:52

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when I will punish her idols. 📖

The wounded will groan throughout her land.

## Jeremiah 51:53

The LORD declares, “Even if Babylon rises to heaven and strengthens her high fortress, My destroyers will attack her. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:54

“Cries sound from Babylon, great destruction from the Chaldeans’ land! 📖

## Jeremiah 51:55

“The LORD will destroy Babylon and silence her mighty voice. 📖

Waves will roar like great waters, and their voices will sound loudly.

## Jeremiah 51:56

“A destroyer attacks Babylon. 📖

Her warriors will be captured, and their bows broken.

The LORD is a God of repayment and will repay fully.

## Jeremiah 51:57

The King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, declares, “I will make her princes and wise men drunk. 📖

Her governors, officials, and warriors will become drunk too.

They will sleep forever and never wake.”

## Jeremiah 51:58

The LORD of Hosts says, “Babylon’s thick walls will be leveled, and its high gates burned. 📖

The peoples’ labor will be for nothing.

The nations will exhaust themselves to feed the flames.”

## Jeremiah 51:59

The prophet Jeremiah gave this message to the quartermaster Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah. 📖

He went to Babylon with Zedekiah king of Judah in Zedekiah’s fourth year.

**Help with quartermaster**

A quartermaster is an official responsible for arrangements and supplies.

## Jeremiah 51:60

Jeremiah wrote on one scroll all the disaster coming on Babylon, all these words about Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:61

Jeremiah told Seraiah, “When you reach Babylon, read all these words aloud, 📖

## Jeremiah 51:62

“and say, ‘LORD, You promised to remove everyone from this place, people and animals alike. 📖

It will be deserted forever.’

## Jeremiah 51:63

“When you finish reading, tie a stone to the scroll and throw it into the Euphrates. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:64

“Say, ‘Babylon will sink in the same way and never rise because of the disaster I bring on her. 📖

Her people will grow weary.’”

Here Jeremiah’s words end.
