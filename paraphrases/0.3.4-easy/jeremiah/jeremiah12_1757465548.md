# Jeremiah 12 — Jeremiah asks why wicked people prosper

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah questions the LORD and asks for judgment.

The LORD answers, describes His people’s unfaithfulness, and speaks about the nations around them.

## Jeremiah 12:1

LORD, You are righteous when I bring my case before You. 📖

Yet I want to question Your judgments.

Why do wicked people prosper?

Why do all the unfaithful live at ease?

## Jeremiah 12:2

You planted them, and they took root, grew, and produced fruit. 📖

You are always on their lips but far from their hearts.

## Jeremiah 12:3

But LORD, You know and see me and test my heart toward You. 📖

Drag the wicked away like sheep to be killed.

Set them apart for the day of slaughter.

**About this prayer**

Jeremiah asks for severe judgment on wicked people.

His request is kept as his prayer.

## Jeremiah 12:4

How long will the land mourn and every field’s grass wither? 📖

Animals and birds have been swept away because of the residents’ evil.

The people said, “He cannot see what our end will be.”

## Jeremiah 12:5

“If racing people on foot wears you out, how can you compete with horses? 📖

If you stumble in peaceful land, how will you manage in the Jordan’s thickets?

**About the answer**

The LORD answers Jeremiah.

The comparisons challenge him to face greater difficulty.

## Jeremiah 12:6

“Even your brothers, your own father’s household, have betrayed you and cried out against you. 📖

Do not trust them, even when they speak well of you.

## Jeremiah 12:7

“I abandoned My house and My inheritance. 📖

I handed the beloved of My soul over to her enemies.

## Jeremiah 12:8

“My inheritance became like a forest lion to Me. 📖

She roared against Me, so I hate her.

**Help with inheritance**

Here the LORD’s inheritance means His people.

The lion image describes their hostility toward Him.

## Jeremiah 12:9

“Is My inheritance not like a spotted bird that hunts, with other hunting birds circling against her? 📖

Go and gather all the field’s animals.

Bring them to eat her.

## Jeremiah 12:10

“Many shepherds destroyed My vineyard and trampled My plot of ground. 📖

They turned My pleasant field into a deserted wasteland.

**Help with shepherds and vineyard**

These images describe rulers and the people or land they ruin.

## Jeremiah 12:11

“They made it deserted, and it mourns before Me. 📖

All the land is ruined, but no one takes it to heart.

## Jeremiah 12:12

“Destroyers have come over all the wilderness’s bare heights. 📖

The LORD’s sword consumes from one end of the earth to the other.

No human being has peace.

## Jeremiah 12:13

“They sowed wheat but harvested thorns. 📖

They exhausted themselves for nothing.

Bear the shame of your harvest because of the LORD’s fierce anger.”

## Jeremiah 12:14

The LORD says, “My evil neighbors attack the inheritance I gave My people Israel. 📖

I will uproot them from their land and uproot Judah’s house from among them.

## Jeremiah 12:15

“But after uprooting them, I will have compassion again. 📖

I will return each to his inheritance and land.

## Jeremiah 12:16

“If they carefully learn My people’s ways and swear by My name, they will be established among My people. 📖

They must say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives,’ just as they taught My people to swear by Baal.

## Jeremiah 12:17

“But if they will not obey, I will uproot that nation. 📖

I will uproot and destroy it,” declares the LORD.
