# Jeremiah 31 — A new covenant and a returning people

**About this chapter**

The LORD promises to gather Israel, comfort mourners, and make a new covenant.

The chapter includes prayers, future promises, and exact boundaries for the rebuilt city.

## Jeremiah 31:1

The LORD declares, “At that time, I will be God of all Israel’s families, and they will be My people.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:2

The LORD says, “The people who survived the sword found favor in the wilderness when Israel went to find rest.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:3

The LORD appeared to us in the past and said, “I loved you with everlasting love. 📖

So I drew you with devoted love.

## Jeremiah 31:4

“I will build you again, Virgin Israel, and you will be rebuilt. 📖

You will again take tambourines and go out dancing joyfully.

## Jeremiah 31:5

“You will plant vineyards again on Samaria’s hills. 📖

The farmers will plant and enjoy the fruit.

## Jeremiah 31:6

“A day will come when watchmen on Ephraim’s hills call, ‘Get up! 📖

Let us go to Zion, to the LORD our God!’”

## Jeremiah 31:7

The LORD says, “Sing joyfully for Jacob and shout for the foremost of nations! 📖

Let your praise be heard.

Say, ‘LORD, save Your people, Israel’s remaining people!’

## Jeremiah 31:8

“I will bring them from the north and gather them from earth’s farthest parts. 📖

They include blind people, people with difficulty walking, pregnant women, and women in labor.

They will return as a great gathering!

## Jeremiah 31:9

“They will come weeping, and I will lead them as they plead. 📖

I will make them walk beside streams on a level path where they will not stumble.

I am Israel’s Father, and Ephraim is My firstborn.”

## Jeremiah 31:10

Nations, hear the LORD’s word and announce it in distant coastlands. 📖

“The One who scattered Israel will gather them and guard them like a shepherd guarding his flock.

## Jeremiah 31:11

“The LORD has ransomed Jacob and redeemed him from the hand that overpowered him. 📖

**Help with ransomed and redeemed**

These words describe rescue or release from another’s power.

## Jeremiah 31:12

“They will come and shout for joy on Zion’s heights, shining with joy over the LORD’s good gifts. 📖

These are grain, new wine, oil, and the young of flocks and herds.

Their life will be like a well-watered garden, and they will never weaken again.

## Jeremiah 31:13

“Young women will rejoice in dancing, along with young and old men. 📖

I will turn their mourning to joy and replace sorrow with comfort and gladness.

## Jeremiah 31:14

The LORD declares, “I will fill the priests’ souls abundantly and satisfy My people with My goodness.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:15

The LORD says, “A voice is heard in Ramah, mourning and weeping greatly. 📖

Rachel weeps for her children and refuses comfort, because they are no more.”

## Jeremiah 31:16

The LORD says, “Stop your voice from weeping and your eyes from tears. 📖

Your work will be rewarded,” declares the LORD.

“Your children will return from enemy land.

## Jeremiah 31:17

The LORD declares, “There is hope for your future, and your children will return to their own land. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:18

“I have surely heard Ephraim moaning, ‘You disciplined me severely, like an untrained calf. 📖

Restore me so that I may return, because You are the LORD my God.

## Jeremiah 31:19

“‘After returning, I repented. 📖

After being taught, I struck my thigh in grief.

I was ashamed and humiliated because I bore my youth’s disgrace.’

## Jeremiah 31:20

“Is Ephraim not a precious son to Me, a delightful child? 📖

Though I often speak against him, I still remember him.

My heart longs for him, and I have great compassion,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 31:21

“Set up road markers and signposts. 📖

Remember the highway, the road you traveled.

Return, Virgin Israel, return to your cities.

## Jeremiah 31:22

“Unfaithful daughter, how long will you wander? 📖

The LORD has created something new in the land, a woman will shelter a man.”

**About the image**

The unusual woman-and-man image is retained without replacing it with a single interpretation.

## Jeremiah 31:23

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “When I restore them from captivity, these words will return to Judah. 📖

They will be spoken in its land and cities.

‘May the LORD bless you, righteous dwelling place, holy mountain.’

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 31:24

“Judah and all its cities will live together in the land, farmers and those moving with the flocks, 📖

## Jeremiah 31:25

“because I will refresh weary souls and fill all who are weak.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:26

Then I woke and looked around. 📖

My sleep had been very pleasant.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah reports waking from sleep.

## Jeremiah 31:27

The LORD declares, “The days are coming. 📖

I will sow Israel’s house and Judah’s house with the seed of people and animals.

## Jeremiah 31:28

The LORD declares, “I watched over them to uproot, tear down, demolish, destroy, and bring disaster. 📖

In the same way, I will watch over them to build and plant.

## Jeremiah 31:29

“In those days, people will no longer say, ‘The fathers ate sour grapes, and their children’s teeth feel the sourness.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 31:30

“Instead, each will die for his own wrongdoing. 📖

Whoever eats sour grapes will feel the sourness in his own teeth.

**Help with sour grapes**

The saying pictures one generation suffering for another’s actions.

The answer stresses each person’s responsibility.

## Jeremiah 31:31

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when I will make a new covenant with Israel’s house and Judah’s house. 📖

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Jeremiah 31:32

“It will differ from the covenant I made with their fathers when I led them by hand from Egypt. 📖

They broke that covenant, though I was their husband,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 31:33

The LORD declares, “After those days, this is the covenant I will make with Israel’s house. 📖

I will put My law in their minds and write it on their hearts.

I will be their God, and they will be My people.

## Jeremiah 31:34

The LORD declares, “No one will teach his neighbor or brother, ‘Know the LORD.’ 📖

They will all know Me, from least to greatest.

I will forgive their wrongdoing and remember their sins no more.”

## Jeremiah 31:35

The LORD gives the sun for daylight and orders the moon and stars for light at night. 📖

He stirs the sea so that its waves roar.

His name is the LORD of Hosts.

This is what He says.

## Jeremiah 31:36

The LORD declares, “Only if this fixed order left My presence would Israel’s descendants stop being a nation before Me.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:37

The LORD says, “Only if heaven could be measured and earth’s foundations fully searched would I reject all Israel’s descendants. 📖

This would be for all they have done,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 31:38

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when this city will be rebuilt for Me. 📖

It will stretch from Hananel’s tower to the Corner Gate.

## Jeremiah 31:39

“The measuring line will stretch straight again to Gareb’s hill, then turn toward Goah. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:40

“The whole valley of dead bodies and ashes will be holy to the LORD. 📖

So will all the fields to Kidron Valley and the corner of the Horse Gate to the east.

It will never be uprooted or demolished again.”
