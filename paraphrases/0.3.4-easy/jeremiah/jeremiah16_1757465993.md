# Jeremiah 16 — Commands that become signs of loss

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Jeremiah not to marry, mourn at funeral meals, or join feasts in this place.

He announces judgment but also promises a future return.

## Jeremiah 16:1

Then the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:2

“You must not marry or have sons or daughters in this place.” 📖

**About this command**

The LORD gives this instruction to Jeremiah in the setting described here.

## Jeremiah 16:3

The LORD speaks about the children born here and the mothers and fathers who brought them into this land. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:4

“They will die from deadly diseases. 📖

No one will mourn or bury them.

They will lie like dung on the ground.

Sword and famine will finish them, and their bodies will feed the birds and animals of the earth.

## Jeremiah 16:5

The LORD says, “Do not enter a house where people share a funeral meal. 📖

Do not go to mourn or show sympathy.

I have removed My peace, devoted love, and compassion from these people,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 16:6

“Great and small alike will die here. 📖

No one will bury or mourn them, cut himself, or shave his head for them.

**About the mourning actions**

The verse names customs associated with grief.

It reports their absence and does not instruct readers to injure themselves.

## Jeremiah 16:7

“No food will comfort those mourning the dead. 📖

No cup of comfort will be given, even for the loss of a father or mother.

## Jeremiah 16:8

“Do not enter a house of feasting and sit with them to eat and drink. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:9

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘Before your own eyes and during your lifetime, I will remove joyful sounds. 📖

I will remove gladness and the voices of brides and bridegrooms from this place.’

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 16:10

“When you tell the people all this, they will ask, ‘Why has the LORD announced this great disaster against us? 📖

What is our wrongdoing?

What sin have we committed against the LORD our God?’

## Jeremiah 16:11

“Answer them, ‘Your fathers abandoned Me,’ declares the LORD. 📖

‘They followed, served, and worshiped other gods.

They abandoned Me and did not keep My teaching.

## Jeremiah 16:12

“‘You have done more evil than your fathers. 📖

Each follows his own stubborn, evil heart instead of obeying Me.

## Jeremiah 16:13

“‘So I will throw you from this land into a land neither you nor your fathers knew. 📖

There you will serve other gods day and night, because I will show you no favor.’

## Jeremiah 16:14

The LORD declares, “Yet the days are coming when people will no longer say this. 📖

‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites from Egypt.’

## Jeremiah 16:15

“Instead, they will say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites from the northern land. 📖

He brought them from all the other lands where He had driven them.’

I will return them to their land, which I gave their ancestors.

## Jeremiah 16:16

The LORD declares, “For now, I will send many fishermen, and they will catch them. 📖

Then I will send many hunters.

They will hunt them on every mountain and hill, even from cracks in the rocks.

**Help with fishermen and hunters**

These images describe people being pursued and captured in judgment.

## Jeremiah 16:17

“My eyes see all their ways. 📖

They are not hidden from Me, and their guilt is not hidden from My eyes.

## Jeremiah 16:18

“First, I will repay them double for their wrongdoing and sin. 📖

They polluted My land with the dead bodies of their detestable idols.

They filled My inheritance with their disgusting objects.”

## Jeremiah 16:19

LORD, my strength, fortress, and shelter in distress, nations will come to You from earth’s ends. 📖

They will say, “Our fathers inherited only lies, worthless idols that could not help.

## Jeremiah 16:20

“Can a person make gods? 📖

Such things are not gods!”

## Jeremiah 16:21

“So look! 📖

I will teach them, and this time I will make them know My power and might.

Then they will know My name is the LORD.”

**About the speaker**

The LORD answers with a declaration of His power.
