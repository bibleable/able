# Jeremiah 39 — Jerusalem falls

**About this chapter**

Jerusalem is captured, and Zedekiah suffers severe punishment.

Jeremiah is protected, and an earlier promise to Ebed-melech is recorded.

## Jeremiah 39:1

In Zedekiah king of Judah’s ninth year, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon marched against Jerusalem. 📖

His whole army came and surrounded it.

## Jeremiah 39:2

The city was broken into on the ninth day of the fourth month of Zedekiah’s eleventh year. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:3

All Babylon’s king’s officials entered and sat at the Middle Gate. 📖

They included Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all the other officials.

**Help with the titles**

Rabsaris and Rabmag are titles of Babylonian officials.

## Jeremiah 39:4

Zedekiah king of Judah and all the soldiers saw them and fled. 📖

They left at night through the king’s garden and the gate between the two walls.

They took the route toward the Arabah.

## Jeremiah 39:5

The Chaldean army chased them and caught Zedekiah on Jericho’s plains. 📖

They seized him and brought him to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon at Riblah in Hamath’s land.

There the king pronounced judgment.

## Jeremiah 39:6

At Riblah, Babylon’s king killed Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes. 📖

He also killed all Judah’s nobles.

## Jeremiah 39:7

Then he put out Zedekiah’s eyes and bound him with bronze chains to take him to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:8

The Chaldeans burned the king’s palace and the people’s houses. 📖

They broke down Jerusalem’s walls.

## Jeremiah 39:9

Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, took the remaining city people to Babylon. 📖

He also took those who deserted to him.

## Jeremiah 39:10

But Nebuzaradan left some poor people with no property in Judah’s land. 📖

At that time, he gave them vineyards and fields.

## Jeremiah 39:11

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had given Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, these orders about Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:12

“Take him and care for him. 📖

Let no harm come to him, and do whatever he says.”

## Jeremiah 39:13

Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, acted with Nebushazban the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all Babylon’s king’s captains. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:14

They brought Jeremiah from the guard’s courtyard and handed him to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan. 📖

Gedaliah was to take him home.

Jeremiah stayed among his own people.

## Jeremiah 39:15

While Jeremiah was confined in the guard’s courtyard, the LORD’s word had come to him. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:16

“Tell Ebed-melech the Cushite what the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says. 📖

‘I will fulfill My words against this city for harm instead of good.

On that day, it will happen before your eyes.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 39:17

The LORD declares, “‘But I will rescue you that day. 📖

You will not be handed to the men you fear.

## Jeremiah 39:18

The LORD declares, “‘I will surely rescue you from death by the sword. 📖

Because you trusted Me, you will escape with your life like goods gained in war.’”
