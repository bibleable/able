# Jeremiah 32 — Jeremiah buys a field during the siege

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah buys land while Jerusalem is under attack.

He asks the LORD about the purchase, and the LORD answers with judgment and a promise of restoration.

## Jeremiah 32:1

This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD in Zedekiah king of Judah’s tenth year. 📖

It was Nebuchadnezzar’s eighteenth year.

## Jeremiah 32:2

Babylon’s king’s army was surrounding Jerusalem. 📖

The prophet Jeremiah was imprisoned in the guard’s courtyard in Judah’s king’s palace.

## Jeremiah 32:3

Zedekiah king of Judah had imprisoned him and asked, “Why prophesy this? 📖

You claim the LORD says, ‘I will hand this city to Babylon’s king, who will capture it.

## Jeremiah 32:4

“‘Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape the Chaldeans. 📖

He will be handed to Babylon’s king and speak to him face to face, seeing him eye to eye.

## Jeremiah 32:5

The LORD declares, “‘He will take Zedekiah to Babylon, where he will stay until I attend to him. 📖

If you fight the Chaldeans, you will not succeed.’”

## Jeremiah 32:6

Jeremiah replied, “The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:7

“‘Look! 📖

Your uncle Shallum’s son Hanamel will come and say, “Buy my field in Anathoth.

You have the right of redemption to buy it.”’

**Help with right of redemption**

This is a family member’s right to buy property back or keep it within the family.

## Jeremiah 32:8

“My cousin Hanamel came to the guard’s courtyard, as the LORD said. 📖

He urged me, ‘Buy my field in Anathoth, in Benjamin’s land.

You have the rights of inheritance and redemption.

Buy it for yourself.’”

Then I knew this was the LORD’s word.

## Jeremiah 32:9

I bought the Anathoth field from my cousin Hanamel and weighed seventeen shekels of silver. 📖

**Help with shekels**

A shekel was a unit of weight used for silver payments.

## Jeremiah 32:10

I signed and sealed the deed, called witnesses, and weighed the silver on scales. 📖

**Help with deed**

A deed is a written record of a property purchase.

## Jeremiah 32:11

I took the purchase deed, both the sealed copy with terms and conditions and the open copy, 📖

## Jeremiah 32:12

and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah. 📖

My cousin Hanamel, the witnesses signing the agreement, and all Jews sitting in the guard’s courtyard saw it.

## Jeremiah 32:13

In front of them, I instructed Baruch. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:14

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘Take both purchase deeds, the sealed and open copies. 📖

Put them in a clay jar to preserve them for a long time.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 32:15

“‘The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Houses, fields, and vineyards will again be bought in this land.”’” 📖

## Jeremiah 32:16

After giving the purchase deed to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:17

“Oh, Lord GOD! 📖

You made the heavens and earth by Your great power and outstretched arm.

Nothing is too difficult for You!

## Jeremiah 32:18

“You show devoted love to thousands but lay fathers’ wrongdoing in their children’s laps after them. 📖

Great and mighty God, Your name is the LORD of Hosts,

## Jeremiah 32:19

“great in advice and mighty in action, watching all human ways. 📖

You reward each person according to his ways and the fruit of his deeds.

## Jeremiah 32:20

“You did signs and wonders in Egypt and continue today in Israel and among all humanity. 📖

You have made a name for Yourself that remains today.

## Jeremiah 32:21

“You brought Your people Israel from Egypt with signs and wonders. 📖

You used a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and great terror.

## Jeremiah 32:22

“You gave them this land, flowing with milk and honey, as You swore to their fathers. 📖

**Help with milk and honey**

The image describes a rich, productive land.

## Jeremiah 32:23

“They entered and possessed it but did not obey Your voice or follow Your law. 📖

They failed to do everything You commanded, so You brought all this disaster.

## Jeremiah 32:24

“See the attack ramps set up to capture the city. 📖

Sword, famine, and plague have handed the city to the Chaldeans fighting it.

What You said has happened, as You now see!

## Jeremiah 32:25

“Yet, Lord GOD, You told me, ‘Buy the field with silver and call witnesses. 📖

Do this even though the city has been handed to the Chaldeans!’”

## Jeremiah 32:26

Then the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:27

“Look! 📖

I am the LORD, God of all flesh.

Is anything too difficult for Me?

## Jeremiah 32:28

“The LORD says, ‘I will hand this city to the Chaldeans and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. 📖

He will capture it.

## Jeremiah 32:29

“‘The Chaldeans fighting this city will enter and burn it, including the houses where people angered Me. 📖

On those rooftops, they burned incense to Baal and poured drink offerings to other gods.

**Help with Baal and incense**

Baal is the name of another god.

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Jeremiah 32:30

The LORD declares, “‘Israel’s and Judah’s children have done only evil in My sight since youth. 📖

They have only angered Me with their hands’ work.

## Jeremiah 32:31

“‘This city has stirred My anger and fury from the day it was built until now. 📖

So I will remove it from My presence,

## Jeremiah 32:32

“‘because of all the evil Israel’s and Judah’s children did to anger Me. 📖

This includes their kings, officials, priests, prophets, Judah’s men, and Jerusalem’s residents.

## Jeremiah 32:33

“‘They turned their backs to Me instead of their faces. 📖

I taught them again and again, but they would not listen or respond to discipline.

## Jeremiah 32:34

“‘They put their disgusting objects in the house bearing My Name and polluted it. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:35

“‘They built Baal’s high places in Ben-hinnom Valley to make their sons and daughters pass through fire to Molech. 📖

I never commanded or even thought of such a disgusting act, causing Judah to sin.

**About this act**

The warning condemns the sacrifice of children by fire.

The LORD denies commanding it.

## Jeremiah 32:36

“‘You say this city will be handed to Babylon’s king through sword, famine, and plague. 📖

Now the LORD, Israel’s God, says this.

## Jeremiah 32:37

“‘I will gather My people from all the lands where I drove them in furious anger and great wrath. 📖

I will bring them here and let them live safely.

## Jeremiah 32:38

“‘They will be My people, and I will be their God. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:39

“‘I will give them one heart and one way, so that they always fear Me. 📖

This is for their good and their children’s good after them.

## Jeremiah 32:40

“‘I will make an everlasting covenant with them and never stop doing them good. 📖

I will put fear of Me in their hearts so that they never turn from Me.

## Jeremiah 32:41

“‘I will rejoice in doing them good. 📖

With all My heart and soul, I will faithfully plant them in this land.

## Jeremiah 32:42

“‘The LORD says, “I brought all this great disaster on these people. 📖

In the same way, I will bring all the good I promised.”

## Jeremiah 32:43

“‘Fields will be bought in this land that you call deserted, without people or animals, handed to the Chaldeans. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:44

“‘Fields will be bought with silver, with deeds signed, sealed, and witnessed. 📖

This will happen in Benjamin’s land, Jerusalem’s surroundings, and Judah’s cities, in the hill country, foothills, and Negev.

I will restore them from captivity,’ declares the LORD.”
