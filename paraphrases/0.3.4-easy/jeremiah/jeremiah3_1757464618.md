# Jeremiah 3 — Return and acknowledge your guilt

**About this chapter**

Israel and Judah are pictured as unfaithful sisters.

The LORD calls for a true return, and the chapter ends with the people’s confession.

## Jeremiah 3:1

The LORD declares, “If a man divorces his wife and she marries another, can he return to her? 📖

Would the land not be completely polluted?

Yet you have acted as a prostitute with many lovers, and would you return to Me?

**About the image**

The LORD compares His people’s worship of other gods to marriage unfaithfulness.

## Jeremiah 3:2

“Look up at the bare heights. 📖

Where have you not been violated?

You sat by the roads waiting for lovers, like a wandering desert dweller.

Your prostitution and wickedness polluted the land.

## Jeremiah 3:3

“So showers were held back, and no spring rain fell. 📖

Yet you have a prostitute’s bold look and refuse to be ashamed.

## Jeremiah 3:4

“Have you not just called to Me with these words? 📖

‘My Father, You have been my friend since youth.

## Jeremiah 3:5

“‘Will He stay angry forever? 📖

Will His fury last to the end?’

You have said this but keep doing all the evil you can.”

## Jeremiah 3:6

In King Josiah’s days, the LORD told me, “Have you seen what faithless Israel has done? 📖

She went to every high hill and under every green tree to act as a prostitute.

## Jeremiah 3:7

“I thought she would return to Me after doing all this. 📖

But she did not, and her unfaithful sister Judah saw it.

## Jeremiah 3:8

“She saw that I gave faithless Israel a divorce document and sent her away because she committed adultery. 📖

Yet unfaithful Judah, her sister, had no fear and acted as a prostitute too.

## Jeremiah 3:9

“Israel treated her unfaithfulness lightly, polluted the land, and committed adultery with stones and trees. 📖

**Help with the sisters and adultery**

Israel and Judah are pictured as sisters.

Adultery means sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

Here the stones and trees are connected with the people’s worship of other gods.

## Jeremiah 3:10

“Despite all this, unfaithful Judah did not return to Me with all her heart. 📖

She only pretended,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 3:11

The LORD told me, “Faithless Israel has shown herself more righteous than unfaithful Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:12

“Go and announce this message toward the north. 📖

‘Return, faithless Israel,’ declares the LORD.

‘I will no longer look at you angrily, because I am merciful,’ declares the LORD.

‘I will not stay angry forever.

## Jeremiah 3:13

“‘Only admit your guilt, that you rebelled against the LORD your God. 📖

You gave your favors to foreign gods under every green tree.

You have not obeyed My voice,’” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 3:14

The LORD declares, “Return, faithless children, because I am your master. 📖

I will take you, one from a city and two from a family, and bring you to Zion.

## Jeremiah 3:15

“I will give you shepherds after My own heart. 📖

They will feed you with knowledge and understanding.”

**Help with shepherds**

These shepherds picture leaders who care for the people as God desires.

## Jeremiah 3:16

The LORD declares, “In those days, you will multiply and increase in the land. 📖

People will no longer discuss the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

It will not come to mind, be remembered, or be missed.

No other ark will be made.

**Help with the ark**

The ark was a sacred chest connected with the LORD’s covenant, His binding agreement with Israel.

## Jeremiah 3:17

“At that time, Jerusalem will be called The Throne of the LORD. 📖

All nations will gather there to honor the LORD’s name.

They will stop following their stubborn, evil hearts.

## Jeremiah 3:18

“In those days, Judah’s house will walk with Israel’s house. 📖

Together they will come from the northern land to the land I gave your fathers as an inheritance.

## Jeremiah 3:19

“I said, ‘How I long to make you My sons and give you a desirable land! 📖

It is the most beautiful inheritance among all nations.’

I thought you would call Me ‘Father’ and never stop following Me.

## Jeremiah 3:20

“But like a woman betraying her husband, you have betrayed Me, house of Israel,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:21

A voice is heard on the bare heights. 📖

Israel’s children weep and beg for mercy.

They have twisted their ways and forgotten the LORD their God.

## Jeremiah 3:22

“Return, faithless children, and I will heal your faithlessness.” 📖

The people answer, “Here we are.

We come to You, because You are the LORD our God.

## Jeremiah 3:23

“The hills surely bring deception, and the mountains bring uproar. 📖

Israel’s salvation is surely in the LORD our God.

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Jeremiah 3:24

“Since our youth, that shameful god has consumed the results of our fathers’ work. 📖

It has consumed their flocks and herds, their sons and daughters.

## Jeremiah 3:25

“Let us lie down in shame and be covered by disgrace. 📖

We and our fathers have sinned against the LORD our God.

From our youth to this day, we have not obeyed the LORD our God’s voice.”
