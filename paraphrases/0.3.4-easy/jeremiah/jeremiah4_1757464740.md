# Jeremiah 4 — A warning from the north

**About this chapter**

The LORD calls Israel to return and warns Judah about disaster.

Jeremiah speaks with grief as he describes invasion and devastation.

## Jeremiah 4:1

The LORD declares, “Israel, if you return, return to Me. 📖

If you remove your detestable idols from My sight and stop wavering,

## Jeremiah 4:2

“and swear ‘As surely as the LORD lives’ truthfully, justly, and rightly, the nations will be blessed by Him. 📖

They will glory in Him.”

**About the condition**

This continues verse 1’s call to return, remove idols, and stop wavering.

## Jeremiah 4:3

The LORD tells the men of Judah and Jerusalem, “Break up your unplowed ground. 📖

Do not sow among thorns.

**Help with the ground**

The farming image calls the people to prepare for a true change.

## Jeremiah 4:4

“Circumcise yourselves to the LORD and remove the foreskins of your hearts, men of Judah and people of Jerusalem. 📖

Otherwise, My anger will burst out like fire because of your evil deeds.

It will burn with no one to put it out.”

**Help with circumcision of the heart**

Circumcision removes the foreskin of the penis and was a sign of Israel’s covenant.

Here the heart image calls for inward faithfulness.

It is not an instruction to injure the body.

## Jeremiah 4:5

Announce in Judah and Jerusalem, “Blow the ram’s horn throughout the land! 📖

Cry aloud, ‘Gather together, and let us flee to the fortified cities!’

## Jeremiah 4:6

“Raise a signal flag toward Zion. 📖

Seek shelter without delay!

I am bringing disaster and terrible destruction from the north.

## Jeremiah 4:7

“A lion has left his thicket. 📖

A destroyer of nations has set out from his den to ruin your land.

Your cities will become ruins, with no one living there.

## Jeremiah 4:8

“So put on sackcloth, mourn, and cry out. 📖

The LORD’s fierce anger has not turned away from us.”

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Jeremiah 4:9

The LORD declares, “On that day, the king and officials will lose courage. 📖

Priests will tremble with fear, and prophets will be amazed.”

## Jeremiah 4:10

Then I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! 📖

You have completely deceived these people and Jerusalem by saying, ‘You will have peace.’

Yet a sword is at our throats!”

**About this difficult statement**

Jeremiah speaks directly to God in distress.

His accusation of deception is retained rather than softened.

## Jeremiah 4:11

At that time, this will be said to the people and Jerusalem. 📖

“A scorching wind from the desert’s bare heights blows toward the daughter of My people.

It does not come to winnow or sift,

**Help with winnow and sift**

These actions separate useful grain from unwanted material.

The wind described here is too strong for that careful work.

## Jeremiah 4:12

“but is too strong for that and comes from Me. 📖

Now I also announce judgments against them.”

## Jeremiah 4:13

Look! 📖

He advances like clouds, his chariots like a whirlwind.

His horses are faster than eagles.

How terrible for us!

We are ruined!

## Jeremiah 4:14

Jerusalem, wash evil from your heart so that you may be saved. 📖

How long will you keep wicked thoughts within you?

## Jeremiah 4:15

A voice sounds from Dan, announcing disaster from Ephraim’s hills. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:16

Warn the nations now! 📖

Announce to Jerusalem, “An army comes from a distant land to surround the city.

They raise their voices against Judah’s cities.

## Jeremiah 4:17

“They surround her like men guarding a field, because she rebelled against Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:18

“Your ways and actions brought this on you. 📖

This is your punishment.

How bitter it is, piercing to the heart!”

## Jeremiah 4:19

My pain, my pain! 📖

I twist in suffering!

My chest hurts, and my heart pounds within me.

I cannot be silent.

I have heard the horn’s sound, the alarm of battle.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah expresses his anguish.

## Jeremiah 4:20

Disaster after disaster is announced, because the whole land is ruined. 📖

My tents are destroyed in an instant, my curtains in a moment.

## Jeremiah 4:21

How long must I see the signal flag and hear the horn? 📖

## Jeremiah 4:22

“My people are fools and do not know Me. 📖

They are foolish children without understanding.

They are skilled at evil but do not know how to do good.”

**About the speaker**

“My people” identifies the LORD as speaking.

## Jeremiah 4:23

I looked at the earth, and it had no shape and was empty. 📖

I looked at the heavens, and they had no light.

## Jeremiah 4:24

I looked at the mountains, and they were shaking. 📖

All the hills were swaying.

## Jeremiah 4:25

I looked, and no human being remained. 📖

All the birds of the air had fled.

## Jeremiah 4:26

I looked, and the fruitful land was a desert. 📖

All its cities were torn down before the LORD, before His fierce anger.

## Jeremiah 4:27

The LORD says, “The whole land will be deserted, but I will not complete its destruction. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:28

“So the earth will mourn, and the heavens above will become dark. 📖

I have spoken and planned it.

I will not change My mind or turn back.”

## Jeremiah 4:29

Every city flees at the sound of horsemen and archers. 📖

People enter thickets and climb among rocks.

Every city is abandoned, with no one left living there.

## Jeremiah 4:30

You devastated one, what will you do? 📖

You dress in scarlet, put on gold jewelry, and enlarge your eyes with paint.

But you beautify yourself for nothing.

Your lovers despise you and want to kill you.

**Help with the woman**

The devastated city and its people are pictured as a woman.

## Jeremiah 4:31

I hear a cry like a woman giving birth, suffering as she bears her first child. 📖

It is Daughter Zion’s cry, gasping and stretching out her hands.

She says, “How terrible for me!

My soul grows faint before the murderers!”
