# Jeremiah 36 — The king burns the scroll

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah dictates the LORD’s words to Baruch.

The scroll is read, burned by the king, and written again with further words.

## Jeremiah 36:1

This word came from the LORD to Jeremiah in Jehoiakim son of Josiah’s fourth year as king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:2

“Take a scroll and write all My words about Israel, Judah, and all nations. 📖

Include everything from when I first spoke to you during Josiah’s rule until today.

## Jeremiah 36:3

“Perhaps Judah’s people will hear all My planned disaster and each turn from wicked ways. 📖

Then I will forgive their wrongdoing and sin.”

## Jeremiah 36:4

Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah. 📖

Jeremiah dictated, and Baruch wrote all the LORD’s words to Jeremiah on a scroll.

**Help with dictated**

Jeremiah spoke the words, and Baruch wrote them down.

## Jeremiah 36:5

Jeremiah commanded Baruch, “I am restricted and cannot enter the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:6

“Go there on a fasting day and read the LORD’s words from the scroll you wrote as I dictated. 📖

Let all Judah’s people coming from their cities hear.

## Jeremiah 36:7

“Perhaps they will bring a plea to the LORD and each turn from wicked ways. 📖

The LORD’s announced anger and fury against these people are great.”

## Jeremiah 36:8

Baruch son of Neriah did everything the prophet Jeremiah commanded. 📖

He read the LORD’s words from the scroll in the LORD’s house.

## Jeremiah 36:9

In Jehoiakim son of Josiah’s fifth year as Judah’s king, it was the ninth month. 📖

A fast before the LORD was announced for everyone in Jerusalem and all coming from Judah’s cities.

**Help with fast**

A fast is a time of going without food, often for worship or prayer.

## Jeremiah 36:10

Baruch read Jeremiah’s words from the scroll where everyone could hear, from Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe’s room. 📖

It was in the upper courtyard at the LORD’s house’s New Gate entrance.

## Jeremiah 36:11

Micaiah son of Gemariah, son of Shaphan, heard all the LORD’s words from the scroll. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:12

He went to the scribe’s room in the king’s palace, where all the officials sat. 📖

They included Elishama the scribe, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Achbor, and Gemariah son of Shaphan.

Zedekiah son of Hananiah and all the other officials were there too.

## Jeremiah 36:13

Micaiah reported to the officials all the words he heard Baruch read from the scroll to the people. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:14

All the officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah, son of Shelemiah, son of Cushi, to Baruch. 📖

They said, “Bring the scroll you read to the people and come here.”

Baruch son of Neriah took it and went.

## Jeremiah 36:15

They said, “Please sit and read it where we can hear.” 📖

Baruch read it to them.

## Jeremiah 36:16

When they heard all the words, they turned to each other in fear. 📖

They told Baruch, “We must report all these words to the king.”

## Jeremiah 36:17

They asked Baruch, “How did you write all these words? 📖

Did Jeremiah dictate them?”

## Jeremiah 36:18

Baruch replied, “He dictated all these words to me. 📖

I wrote them in ink on the scroll.”

## Jeremiah 36:19

The officials told Baruch, “You and Jeremiah must hide. 📖

Tell no one where you are.”

## Jeremiah 36:20

They stored the scroll in Elishama the scribe’s room and went to the king in the courtyard. 📖

They reported everything.

## Jeremiah 36:21

The king sent Jehudi for the scroll. 📖

He took it from Elishama the scribe’s room and read it to the king and all officials standing beside him.

## Jeremiah 36:22

It was the ninth month. 📖

The king sat in his winter rooms with a fire burning before him.

## Jeremiah 36:23

Whenever Jehudi read three or four columns, Jehoiakim cut them off with a scribe’s knife. 📖

He threw them into the firepot until the whole scroll had burned.

## Jeremiah 36:24

The king and his servants heard all these words but did not become afraid or tear their clothes. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:25

Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah urged the king not to burn the scroll. 📖

But he would not listen.

## Jeremiah 36:26

The king ordered Jerahmeel, the king’s son, Seraiah son of Azriel, and Shelemiah son of Abdeel to seize them. 📖

They were to seize Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet.

But the LORD hid them.

## Jeremiah 36:27

The king burned the scroll of words Baruch had written as Jeremiah dictated. 📖

Afterward, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 36:28

“Take another scroll and write the same words again. 📖

They were on the first scroll Jehoiakim king of Judah burned.

## Jeremiah 36:29

“Announce what the LORD says about Jehoiakim king of Judah. 📖

‘You burned the scroll and asked, “Why write that Babylon’s king will come and destroy this land?

Why say he will remove people and animals from it?”

## Jeremiah 36:30

“‘The LORD says about Jehoiakim king of Judah, “No one of his will sit on David’s throne. 📖

His body will be thrown out, exposed to daytime heat and nighttime frost.

## Jeremiah 36:31

“‘“I will punish him, his descendants, and his servants for their wrongdoing. 📖

I will bring every disaster I warned of on them, Jerusalem’s residents, and Judah’s men.

They did not listen.”’”

## Jeremiah 36:32

Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah, the scribe. 📖

As Jeremiah dictated, Baruch wrote all the words of the scroll Jehoiakim king of Judah burned.

Many similar words were added.
