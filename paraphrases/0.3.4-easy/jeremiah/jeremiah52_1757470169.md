# Jeremiah 52 — Jerusalem’s destruction and the exiles

**About this chapter**

The chapter records Jerusalem’s siege, punishment, and destruction.

It lists temple objects and exile counts, then records Jehoiachin’s release from prison.

## Jeremiah 52:1

Zedekiah was twenty-one when he became king and ruled Jerusalem for eleven years. 📖

His mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah, from Libnah.

## Jeremiah 52:2

Zedekiah did evil in the LORD’s sight, as Jehoiakim did. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:3

All this happened in Jerusalem and Judah because of the LORD’s anger, until He drove them from His presence. 📖

Zedekiah also rebelled against Babylon’s king.

## Jeremiah 52:4

In Zedekiah’s ninth year, on the tenth day of the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon marched against Jerusalem. 📖

His whole army camped outside and built a siege wall around it.

## Jeremiah 52:5

The city remained surrounded until King Zedekiah’s eleventh year. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:6

By the ninth day of the fourth month, famine was so severe that the land’s people had no food. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:7

The city was broken into. 📖

The Chaldeans surrounded it, but all the warriors fled at night.

They went through the gate between the two walls near the king’s garden.

They headed toward the Arabah,

## Jeremiah 52:8

but the Chaldean army chased the king and caught Zedekiah on Jericho’s plains. 📖

His whole army deserted him.

## Jeremiah 52:9

The Chaldeans seized Zedekiah and brought him to Babylon’s king at Riblah in Hamath’s land. 📖

There the king pronounced judgment.

## Jeremiah 52:10

At Riblah, Babylon’s king killed Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes and killed all Judah’s officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:11

He put out Zedekiah’s eyes, bound him in bronze chains, and took him to Babylon. 📖

He kept Zedekiah imprisoned until his death.

## Jeremiah 52:12

In Nebuchadnezzar’s nineteenth year ruling Babylon, on the tenth day of the fifth month, Nebuzaradan entered Jerusalem. 📖

He was captain of the guard and Babylon’s king’s servant.

## Jeremiah 52:13

He burned the LORD’s house, the royal palace, and all Jerusalem’s houses, every important building. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:14

The whole Chaldean army under the guard captain broke down all Jerusalem’s surrounding walls. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:15

Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, exiled some poorest people, the remaining city people, and the rest of the craftsmen. 📖

He also exiled those who deserted to Babylon’s king.

## Jeremiah 52:16

But Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, left some poorest people to care for vineyards and fields. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:17

The Chaldeans broke the bronze pillars, stands, and bronze Sea in the LORD’s house. 📖

They carried all the bronze to Babylon.

**Help with Sea**

The Sea was a large water basin at the temple.

## Jeremiah 52:18

They also took pots, shovels, wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, dishes, and all bronze objects used in temple service. 📖

**Help with wick trimmers**

These tools trimmed the burning part of lamps.

## Jeremiah 52:19

The guard captain also took basins, censers, sprinkling bowls, pots, lampstands, pans, and drink-offering bowls. 📖

He took everything made from pure gold or fine silver.

**Help with censers**

Censers are containers for burning incense.

## Jeremiah 52:20

The bronze’s weight could not be measured. 📖

These objects were the two pillars, the Sea, twelve bronze bulls beneath it, and movable stands.

King Solomon made them for the LORD’s house.

## Jeremiah 52:21

Each pillar was eighteen cubits high and twelve cubits around. 📖

Each was hollow, four fingers thick.

**Help with cubits and fingers**

These are units of length or thickness.

The source measurements are retained without conversion.

## Jeremiah 52:22

One pillar’s bronze capital was five cubits high, with a network of bronze pomegranates around it. 📖

The second pillar and its pomegranates were similar.

**Help with capital and pomegranates**

A capital is the top of a pillar.

The pomegranates were decorations shaped like the fruit.

## Jeremiah 52:23

Each capital had ninety-six pomegranates on its sides. 📖

A hundred pomegranates in total were above the surrounding network.

## Jeremiah 52:24

The guard captain also took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second-ranking priest, and three doorkeepers. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:25

From those still in the city, he took a court official responsible for warriors and seven trusted royal advisers. 📖

He took the army captain’s scribe, who enlisted the land’s people, and sixty men found in the city.

## Jeremiah 52:26

Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, took them to Babylon’s king at Riblah. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:27

There at Riblah in Hamath’s land, Babylon’s king killed them. 📖

Judah was taken into exile, away from its own land.

## Jeremiah 52:28

These are the people Nebuchadnezzar took away. 📖

In the seventh year, he took 3,023 Jews,

## Jeremiah 52:29

in Nebuchadnezzar’s eighteenth year, 832 people from Jerusalem, 📖

## Jeremiah 52:30

In Nebuchadnezzar’s twenty-third year, Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, took 745 Jews. 📖

Altogether, 4,600 people were taken away.

## Jeremiah 52:31

It was the thirty-seventh year of Jehoiachin king of Judah’s exile. 📖

Evil-merodach king of Babylon pardoned him and released him from prison.

This happened on the twenty-fifth day of the twelfth month, in Evil-merodach’s first year ruling Babylon.

## Jeremiah 52:32

He spoke kindly to Jehoiachin and placed his throne above the other kings’ thrones with him in Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:33

Jehoiachin changed from prison clothes and regularly ate at the king’s table for the rest of his life. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:34

Babylon’s king provided Jehoiachin a daily portion for the rest of his life, until the day he died. 📖
