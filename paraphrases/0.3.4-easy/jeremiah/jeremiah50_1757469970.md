# Jeremiah 50 — Babylon falls and Israel returns

**About this chapter**

The LORD announces Babylon’s capture, the defeat of its gods, and the return of Israel and Judah.

The chapter retains repeated commands of judgment and images of lost sheep and hostile lions.

## Jeremiah 50:1

The LORD spoke this word through Jeremiah the prophet about Babylon and the Chaldeans’ land. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:2

“Announce it to the nations, raise a banner, and hide nothing. 📖

Say, ‘Babylon is captured, Bel ashamed, and Marduk shattered.

Its images are disgraced, and its idols broken.’

**Help with Bel and Marduk**

These are names associated with Babylon’s gods.

A banner is a raised sign people can gather around.

## Jeremiah 50:3

“A northern nation will attack and leave its land deserted. 📖

No one will live there, and people and animals will flee.”

## Jeremiah 50:4

The LORD declares, “In those days and at that time, Israel’s and Judah’s children will come together. 📖

They will come weeping and seek the LORD their God.

## Jeremiah 50:5

“They will ask the way to Zion and turn toward it. 📖

They will join the LORD in an everlasting covenant that will never be forgotten.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Jeremiah 50:6

“My people are lost sheep. 📖

Their shepherds led them astray, making them wander mountains and hills until they forgot their resting place.

## Jeremiah 50:7

“Everyone finding them consumed them. 📖

Their enemies said, ‘We are not guilty.

They sinned against the LORD, their true pasture, the LORD, their fathers’ hope.’

**Help with the images**

The sheep picture the people, and shepherds picture their leaders.

The enemies use the people’s sin as an excuse for attacking them.

## Jeremiah 50:8

“Flee Babylon and leave the Chaldeans’ land. 📖

Be like male goats leading the flock.

## Jeremiah 50:9

“I am stirring and bringing great northern nations together against Babylon. 📖

They will line up and capture it from the north.

Their arrows will be like skilled warriors who never return empty-handed.

## Jeremiah 50:10

The LORD declares, “Chaldea will be robbed, and everyone robbing it will have their fill. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:11

“You who rob My inheritance rejoice and sing in victory. 📖

You play like a young cow treading grain and neigh like male horses.

## Jeremiah 50:12

“So your mother who bore you will be deeply ashamed and disgraced. 📖

She will become the least of nations, a wilderness, dry land, and desert.

**Help with mother**

The city or nation is pictured as the people’s mother.

## Jeremiah 50:13

“The LORD’s anger will leave her completely deserted, without people. 📖

Everyone passing Babylon will be horrified and hiss at all its wounds.

## Jeremiah 50:14

“All archers, line up around Babylon and shoot! 📖

Spare no arrows, because she sinned against the LORD.

## Jeremiah 50:15

“Raise battle cries against her from every side! 📖

She raised her hands in surrender, her towers fell, and her walls broke.

This is the LORD’s vengeance.

Take vengeance on her and do to her what she did.

## Jeremiah 50:16

“Remove Babylon’s sower and the one using a sickle at harvest. 📖

Before the oppressor’s sword, each will return to his own people and flee to his own land.

**Help with sickle**

A sickle is a curved tool used to cut crops.

## Jeremiah 50:17

“Israel is a scattered flock chased by lions. 📖

Assyria’s king consumed him first, and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon crushed his bones last.”

## Jeremiah 50:18

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “I will punish Babylon’s king and land as I punished Assyria’s king. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 50:19

“I will return Israel to his pasture to graze on Carmel and Bashan. 📖

His soul will be satisfied on Ephraim’s and Gilead’s hills.

## Jeremiah 50:20

The LORD declares, “In those days, people will search for Israel’s guilt and find none. 📖

They will seek Judah’s sins, but none will be found.

I will forgive the survivors I preserve.

## Jeremiah 50:21

The LORD declares, “Attack Merathaim’s land and Pekod’s people. 📖

Kill them, give them over to destruction, and do everything I commanded.

## Jeremiah 50:22

“Battle and great destruction sound in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:23

“How broken and shattered is the hammer of the whole earth! 📖

What a horror Babylon became among the nations!

## Jeremiah 50:24

“Babylon, I set a trap, and you were caught before you knew it. 📖

You were found and captured because you challenged the LORD.

## Jeremiah 50:25

“The LORD opened His weapons store and brought out His weapons of anger. 📖

This is the Lord GOD of Hosts’ work in the Chaldeans’ land.

## Jeremiah 50:26

“Come from the farthest border against her. 📖

Break open her grain stores and pile her up like heaps of grain.

Give her over to destruction and leave no survivors.

## Jeremiah 50:27

“Kill all her young bulls and let them go to slaughter. 📖

How terrible for them, because their day has come, the time of punishment!

## Jeremiah 50:28

“Listen to people fleeing Babylon. 📖

In Zion, they announce the LORD our God’s vengeance, vengeance for His temple.

## Jeremiah 50:29

“Call every archer with a strung bow against Babylon and camp all around. 📖

Let no one escape.

Repay her deeds and do to her as she did.

She defied the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.

## Jeremiah 50:30

The LORD declares, “Her young men will fall in the streets, and all her warriors will be silenced that day. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:31

The Lord GOD of Hosts declares, “Proud one, I am against you. 📖

Your day has come, the time I punish you.

## Jeremiah 50:32

“The proud one will stumble and fall, with no one to raise him. 📖

I will light fire in his cities to consume everyone around him.”

## Jeremiah 50:33

The LORD of Hosts says, “Israel’s sons and Judah’s sons are oppressed. 📖

All their captors hold them tightly and refuse to release them.

## Jeremiah 50:34

“Their Redeemer is strong, and His name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

He will earnestly argue their case, bringing rest to the earth but trouble to Babylon’s people.

**Help with Redeemer**

A Redeemer rescues or sets people free.

## Jeremiah 50:35

The LORD declares, “A sword is against the Chaldeans, Babylon’s residents, its officials, and its wise men. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:36

“A sword is against its false prophets, and they will become fools. 📖

A sword is against its warriors, and terror will fill them.

## Jeremiah 50:37

“A sword is against its horses, chariots, and all foreigners within it, who will become like women. 📖

A sword is against its treasures, which will be taken.

**About the comparison**

The source compares the threatened fighters with women in this scene of fear.

It does not establish that all women are weak or fearful.

## Jeremiah 50:38

“Drought will dry its waters. 📖

It is a land of carved images, and the people go mad over idols.

## Jeremiah 50:39

“Desert creatures, hyenas, and ostriches will live there. 📖

People will never live there again, from generation to generation.

## Jeremiah 50:40

The LORD declares, “As God overthrew Sodom, Gomorrah, and their neighbors, no one will live or stay there. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:41

“Look! 📖

An army comes from the north.

A great nation and many kings rise from earth’s ends.

## Jeremiah 50:42

“They hold bows and spears and are cruel and without mercy. 📖

Their voices roar like the sea as they ride horses.

They line up for battle against you, Daughter of Babylon.

## Jeremiah 50:43

“Babylon’s king heard the report, and his hands hang weakly. 📖

Pain grips him like a woman in labor.

## Jeremiah 50:44

“Look! 📖

Someone comes like a lion from Jordan’s thickets to watered pasture.

I will chase Babylon from its land in an instant.

Whom will I choose and appoint?

Who is like Me or can challenge Me?

What shepherd can stand against Me?”

## Jeremiah 50:45

Hear the LORD’s plans against Babylon and His strategy against the Chaldeans’ land. 📖

The flock’s little ones will surely be dragged away, and their pasture ruined because of them.

## Jeremiah 50:46

The earth will shake at the sound of Babylon’s capture, and a cry will be heard among the nations. 📖
