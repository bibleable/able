# Jeremiah 2 — The people abandon the source of living water

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges Israel and Judah for abandoning Him.

The chapter uses water, plants, animals, marriage, and a court case to describe their unfaithfulness.

## Jeremiah 2:1

The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:2

“Go and announce this where Jerusalem can hear. 📖

The LORD says, ‘I remember your youthful devotion, your love as a bride.

You followed Me in the wilderness, in land that had not been planted.

## Jeremiah 2:3

“‘Israel was holy to the LORD, the first part of His harvest. 📖

Everyone who swallowed her up was found guilty, and disaster came on them,’” declares the LORD.

**Help with the images**

The first harvest was set apart for God.

Israel is pictured as belonging to Him.

Swallowing pictures enemies attacking His people.

## Jeremiah 2:4

House of Jacob and all families of Israel’s house, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:5

The LORD says, “What fault did your fathers find in Me, to go so far away? 📖

They followed worthless idols and became worthless themselves.

## Jeremiah 2:6

“They did not ask, ‘Where is the LORD who brought us from Egypt and led us through the wilderness? 📖

He led us through deserts and pits, through drought and darkness.

He led us through land where no one travels or lives.’

## Jeremiah 2:7

“I brought you into fertile land to eat its fruit and good produce. 📖

But you polluted My land and made My inheritance detestable.

**Help with inheritance**

Here the land is described as belonging to God.

## Jeremiah 2:8

“The priests did not ask, ‘Where is the LORD?’ 📖

The law experts no longer knew Me, and the leaders rebelled against Me.

The prophets spoke messages by Baal and followed useless idols.

**Help with Baal**

Baal is the name of a god worshiped by people in the land.

## Jeremiah 2:9

“So I will bring a case against you again,” declares the LORD. 📖

“I will also bring a case against your grandchildren.

## Jeremiah 2:10

“Cross to Cyprus’s coasts and look. 📖

Send to Kedar and consider carefully.

See whether anything like this has ever happened.

## Jeremiah 2:11

“Has a nation ever changed its gods, even though they are not really gods? 📖

Yet My people exchanged their Glory for useless idols.

**Help with Glory**

Here Glory refers to the LORD, whom the people have exchanged for idols.

## Jeremiah 2:12

“Heavens, be stunned, shocked, and completely horrified at this!” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:13

“My people have done two evil things. 📖

They abandoned Me, the spring of living water.

They dug their own water storage pits, broken pits that cannot hold water.

**Help with the water**

A spring supplies fresh, flowing water.

The contrast pictures leaving God’s life-giving care for something that cannot sustain life.

## Jeremiah 2:14

“Is Israel a slave? 📖

Was he born into slavery?

Why has he become prey?

## Jeremiah 2:15

“Young lions roar at him and raise their voices. 📖

They have ruined his land.

His cities are devastated, with no one living there.

## Jeremiah 2:16

“The men of Memphis and Tahpanhes have shaved the top of your head. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:17

“Did you not bring this on yourself by abandoning the LORD your God when He led you along the way? 📖

## Jeremiah 2:18

“What will you gain by going to Egypt to drink the Nile’s waters? 📖

What will you gain by going to Assyria to drink the Euphrates’ waters?

## Jeremiah 2:19

“Your own evil will discipline you, and your turning away will rebuke you. 📖

Think and understand how evil and bitter it is to abandon the LORD your God.

You have no fear of Me,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 2:20

“Long ago, you broke your yoke and tore off your chains. 📖

You said, ‘I will not serve!’

On every high hill and under every green tree, you lay down as a prostitute.

**Help with the images**

A yoke controls working animals.

The broken yoke pictures rejecting service to God.

The prostitute image describes unfaithfulness to Him through other worship.

## Jeremiah 2:21

“I planted you like a choice vine from the very best seed. 📖

How could you turn into a rotten, wild vine before Me?

## Jeremiah 2:22

“Even if you wash with lye and plenty of soap, your guilt’s stain remains before Me,” declares the Lord GOD. 📖

**Help with lye**

Lye is a strong substance used for cleaning.

The stain pictures guilt that ordinary washing cannot remove.

## Jeremiah 2:23

“How can you say, ‘I am not polluted; I have not chased the Baals’? 📖

Look at your behavior in the valley and admit what you did.

You are a swift young female camel, running here and there,

## Jeremiah 2:24

“a wild donkey at home in the wilderness, sniffing the wind in the heat of desire. 📖

Who can restrain her passion?

Those seeking her need not tire themselves.

They will find her in mating season.

**Help with the animals**

The images compare the people’s pursuit of other gods to animals driven by sexual desire.

## Jeremiah 2:25

“You should have kept your feet from becoming bare and your throat from becoming thirsty. 📖

But you said, ‘It is hopeless!

I love foreign gods and must follow them.’

## Jeremiah 2:26

“A thief is ashamed when caught. 📖

Israel’s house is disgraced in the same way, with its kings, officials, priests, and prophets.

## Jeremiah 2:27

“They tell a tree, ‘You are my father,’ and a stone, ‘You gave me birth.’ 📖

They turn their backs to Me instead of their faces.

Yet in trouble, they say, ‘Rise and save us!’

## Jeremiah 2:28

“Where are the gods you made for yourselves? 📖

Let them rise and save you in trouble, if they can.

Judah, your gods are as many as your cities.

## Jeremiah 2:29

“Why do you bring a case against Me? 📖

You have all rebelled against Me,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 2:30

“I struck your sons for nothing. 📖

They accepted no discipline.

Your own sword swallowed your prophets like a hungry lion.”

## Jeremiah 2:31

People of this generation, consider the LORD’s word. 📖

“Have I been a wilderness or a land of thick darkness to Israel?

Why do My people say these words?

‘We are free to wander.

We will not come to You anymore.’

## Jeremiah 2:32

“Does a young woman forget her jewelry or a bride her wedding sash? 📖

Yet My people have forgotten Me for more days than can be counted.

## Jeremiah 2:33

“How skillfully you seek love! 📖

Even the most immoral women could learn from your ways.

## Jeremiah 2:34

“Your skirts are stained with the blood of innocent poor people, though you did not catch them breaking in. 📖

Yet despite all this,

## Jeremiah 2:35

“you say, ‘I am innocent. 📖

Surely His anger will turn away from me.’

Look!

I will judge you because you say, ‘I have not sinned.’

## Jeremiah 2:36

“How quickly you change your ways! 📖

Egypt will disappoint you just as Assyria did.

## Jeremiah 2:37

“You will leave that place with your hands on your head. 📖

The LORD has rejected those you trust.

Their help will not bring you success.”
