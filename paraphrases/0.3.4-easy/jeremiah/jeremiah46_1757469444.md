# Jeremiah 46 — Messages about Egypt

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks about Egypt’s defeat and a later attack.

The chapter also promises Egypt future habitation and Jacob rescue with just discipline.

## Jeremiah 46:1

The LORD’s word about the nations came to Jeremiah the prophet. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:2

It concerned Egypt and Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt’s army. 📖

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon defeated that army at Carchemish on the Euphrates River.

This happened in Jehoiakim son of Josiah’s fourth year as king of Judah.

## Jeremiah 46:3

“Prepare your small and large shields. 📖

Advance for battle!

## Jeremiah 46:4

“Harness the horses and mount them. 📖

Take positions with helmets on.

Polish spears and put on armor!

## Jeremiah 46:5

The LORD declares, “Why do I see them terrified and retreating? 📖

Their warriors are defeated.

They flee quickly without looking back, with terror on every side!

## Jeremiah 46:6

“Swift runners cannot flee, and warriors cannot escape. 📖

In the north beside the Euphrates River, they stumble and fall.

## Jeremiah 46:7

“Who rises like the Nile, like rivers with churning waters? 📖

## Jeremiah 46:8

“Egypt rises like the Nile, its waters churning like rivers. 📖

It boasts, ‘I will rise and cover the earth.

I will destroy cities and their people.’

## Jeremiah 46:9

“Horses, advance! 📖

Chariots, race fiercely!

Let warriors come, Cush and Put with shields, and men of Lydia drawing bows.

## Jeremiah 46:10

“That day belongs to the Lord GOD of Hosts, a day of vengeance against His enemies. 📖

The sword will consume until satisfied, until its thirst is filled with their blood.

The Lord GOD of Hosts will have a sacrifice in the northern land beside the Euphrates River.

**Help with the images**

The sword and sacrifice describe deadly judgment.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 46:11

“Virgin Daughter of Egypt, go to Gilead for balm. 📖

You try many remedies for nothing, because there is no healing for you.

**Help with balm**

Balm is a substance used to soothe or treat wounds.

Egypt and its people are pictured as a wounded woman.

## Jeremiah 46:12

“The nations heard your shame, and your cries fill the earth. 📖

Warrior stumbles over warrior, and both fall together.”

## Jeremiah 46:13

The LORD spoke this word to Jeremiah the prophet about Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon coming to strike Egypt. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:14

“Announce it in Egypt, Migdol, Memphis, and Tahpanhes. 📖

‘Take positions and prepare, because the sword consumes those around you.’

## Jeremiah 46:15

“Why are your warriors brought down? 📖

They cannot stand, because the LORD pushed them down.

## Jeremiah 46:16

“They keep stumbling and falling over each other. 📖

They say, ‘Get up!

Let us return to our people and native land, away from the oppressor’s sword.’

## Jeremiah 46:17

“There they will cry, ‘Pharaoh king of Egypt was only noise! 📖

He let the appointed time pass.’

## Jeremiah 46:18

The King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, declares, “As surely as I live, someone will come. 📖

He will be like Tabor among mountains and Carmel beside the sea.

## Jeremiah 46:19

“Daughter living in Egypt, pack for exile. 📖

Memphis will be ruined, destroyed, and empty.

**Help with exile**

Exile means being forced away from one’s own land.

## Jeremiah 46:20

“Egypt is a beautiful young cow, but a biting fly comes against her from the north. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:21

“Even her hired soldiers are like fattened calves. 📖

They will all turn and flee instead of standing firm.

Their day of disaster is coming, the time of their punishment.

## Jeremiah 46:22

“Egypt will hiss like a fleeing snake, because the enemy advances in force. 📖

They come with axes like woodcutters cutting trees.

## Jeremiah 46:23

The LORD declares, “They will cut down her forest, however thick. 📖

They are more than locusts and cannot be counted.

## Jeremiah 46:24

“Daughter Egypt will be ashamed and handed to the northern people.” 📖

## Jeremiah 46:25

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “I will punish Amon god of Thebes, Pharaoh, Egypt’s gods and kings. 📖

I will also punish those trusting Pharaoh.

## Jeremiah 46:26

“I will hand them to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his officials, who seek their lives. 📖

But afterward, Egypt will be inhabited as in old days,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 46:27

“Jacob My servant, do not fear. 📖

Israel, do not be distressed.

I will save you from far away and your descendants from the land of captivity.

Jacob will return to quiet and ease, with no one to frighten him.

## Jeremiah 46:28

The LORD declares, “My servant Jacob, do not fear, because I am with you. 📖

I will completely destroy the nations where I banished you, but I will not completely destroy you.

Yet I will discipline you justly and certainly not leave you unpunished.”
