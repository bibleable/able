# Jeremiah 15 — Judgment and Jeremiah’s distress

**About this chapter**

The LORD announces severe judgment.

Jeremiah speaks about his own pain, and the LORD gives him a conditional promise of restoration.

## Jeremiah 15:1

The LORD told me, “Even if Moses and Samuel stood before Me, I would not pity these people. 📖

Send them from My presence and let them go!

## Jeremiah 15:2

“If they ask, ‘Where should we go?’ tell them what the LORD says. 📖

‘Those destined for death go to death, and those for the sword to the sword.

Those destined for famine go to famine, and those for captivity to captivity.’

## Jeremiah 15:3

The LORD declares, “I will appoint four kinds of destroyers over them. 📖

Swords will kill, dogs will drag away, and birds and earth’s animals will consume and destroy.

## Jeremiah 15:4

“I will make them a horror to all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

This is because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, did in Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 15:5

“Jerusalem, who will pity you? 📖

Who will mourn for you?

Who will turn aside to ask how you are?

## Jeremiah 15:6

The LORD declares, “You abandoned Me and turned your back. 📖

I will stretch My hand against you and destroy you.

I am tired of showing compassion.

## Jeremiah 15:7

“I will scatter them with a winnowing fork at the land’s gates. 📖

I will take away their children and destroy My people, who have not changed their ways.

**Help with winnowing fork**

This tool tosses harvested grain so wind can separate light unwanted material.

Here it pictures people scattered by judgment.

## Jeremiah 15:8

“Their widows will be more than the sea’s sand. 📖

At noon, I will bring a destroyer against the mothers of young men.

I will suddenly bring them anguish and distress.

## Jeremiah 15:9

“The mother of seven will grow faint and breathe her last. 📖

Her sun will set while it is still day.

She will be disgraced and humiliated.

I will put the rest to the sword before their enemies,” declares the LORD.

**Help with her sun**

The setting sun pictures life ending.

## Jeremiah 15:10

How terrible for me, my mother, that you gave birth to me! 📖

I am a man of conflict and struggle throughout the land.

I neither lent nor borrowed, yet everyone curses me.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah speaks about his distress.

## Jeremiah 15:11

The LORD said, “I will surely rescue you for a good purpose. 📖

In trouble and distress, I will surely plead with your enemy for you.

## Jeremiah 15:12

“Can anyone break iron, iron from the north, or bronze? 📖

## Jeremiah 15:13

“I will give your wealth and treasures away as stolen goods, without charge. 📖

This is for all your sins throughout all your borders.

## Jeremiah 15:14

“I will enslave you to your enemies in a land you do not know. 📖

My anger will light a fire that burns against you.”

## Jeremiah 15:15

LORD, You understand. 📖

Remember and care for me.

Take vengeance for me against those who pursue me.

In Your patience, do not take me away.

Know that I bear insults for Your honor.

## Jeremiah 15:16

I found Your words and ate them. 📖

They became my joy and my heart’s delight.

I bear Your name, LORD God of Hosts.

**Help with eating words**

The image describes receiving God’s words deeply.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 15:17

I never sat or celebrated with the crowd of partygoers. 📖

I sat alone because Your hand was on me, and You filled me with anger.

## Jeremiah 15:18

Why does my pain never end? 📖

Why does my wound refuse to heal?

You have become like a mirage to me, water that is not there.

**Help with mirage**

A mirage looks like something present, such as water, when it is not there.

Jeremiah’s painful complaint to God is retained.

## Jeremiah 15:19

The LORD says, “If you return, I will restore you, and you will stand in My presence. 📖

If you speak worthy words instead of worthless ones, you will be My spokesman.

They must turn to you, but you must not turn to them.

## Jeremiah 15:20

“Then I will make you a fortified bronze wall to these people. 📖

They will fight you but not overcome you.

I am with you to save and rescue you,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 15:21

“I will rescue you from wicked people’s hands and free you from cruel people’s grasp.” 📖
