# Jeremiah 49 — Messages about neighboring nations

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks about Ammon, Edom, Damascus, Kedar, Hazor, and Elam.

Judgments and specific promises of restoration are retained for the groups named.

## Jeremiah 49:1

The LORD says about the Ammonites, “Does Israel have no sons or heir? 📖

Why has Milcom taken Gad, and why have his people settled in Israel’s cities?

**Help with Milcom**

Milcom is the god of the Ammonites.

## Jeremiah 49:2

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when I will sound battle cries against Rabbah of the Ammonites. 📖

It will become rubble, and its villages will burn.

Israel will drive out those who drove them out,” says the LORD.

## Jeremiah 49:3

“Heshbon, wail, because Ai is destroyed! 📖

Daughters of Rabbah, cry, wear sackcloth, and mourn!

Run back and forth within your walls.

Milcom will be exiled with his priests and officials.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn in mourning.

## Jeremiah 49:4

“Unfaithful daughter, why boast about your fruitful valleys? 📖

You trust riches and say, ‘Who can attack me?’

## Jeremiah 49:5

The Lord GOD of Hosts declares, “I will bring terror from all around you. 📖

Each will be driven headlong, with no one to gather the fleeing people.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 49:6

The LORD declares, “Yet afterward, I will restore the Ammonites from captivity.” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:7

The LORD of Hosts says about Edom, “Is there no wisdom left in Teman? 📖

Have wise people lost their advice?

Has their wisdom decayed?

## Jeremiah 49:8

“Dedan’s people, turn and run! 📖

Lie low, because I will bring disaster on Esau when I punish him.

## Jeremiah 49:9

“If grape gatherers came, would they not leave some grapes behind? 📖

If thieves came at night, would they not take only what they wanted?

## Jeremiah 49:10

“But I will strip Esau bare and uncover his hiding places. 📖

He will not be able to hide.

His descendants, relatives, and neighbors will be destroyed, and he will be no more.

## Jeremiah 49:11

“Leave your orphans; I will keep them alive. 📖

Let your widows trust Me.”

**About this promise**

The LORD speaks within His judgment on Edom.

He gives these orphaned children and widows a particular word of care.

## Jeremiah 49:12

The LORD says, “If those who do not deserve to drink the cup must drink, can you escape punishment? 📖

You will not escape, because you must drink too.

**Help with cup**

Drinking the cup pictures receiving judgment.

## Jeremiah 49:13

The LORD declares, “I swore by Myself that Bozrah will become deserted, disgraced, ruined, and cursed. 📖

All its cities will be ruined forever.”

## Jeremiah 49:14

I heard a message from the LORD. 📖

A messenger was sent to the nations, saying, “Gather to march against her!

Rise for battle!”

## Jeremiah 49:15

“Look! 📖

I will make you small among nations and despised among people.

## Jeremiah 49:16

“You live in rock cracks and occupy mountain heights. 📖

The terror you cause and your heart’s pride have deceived you.

Even if you raise your nest like an eagle, I will bring you down,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 49:17

“Edom will become a horror. 📖

Everyone passing will be horrified and mock all its wounds.

## Jeremiah 49:18

The LORD says, “As Sodom, Gomorrah, and their neighbors were overthrown, no one will live or stay there. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:19

“Look! 📖

Someone comes like a lion from Jordan’s thickets to watered pasture.

In an instant, I will chase Edom from its land.

Whom will I choose and appoint for this?

Who is like Me or can challenge Me?

What shepherd can stand against Me?”

## Jeremiah 49:20

Hear the LORD’s plans against Edom and His strategy against Teman’s people. 📖

The flock’s little ones will surely be dragged away, and their pasture ruined because of them.

## Jeremiah 49:21

The sound of their fall will shake the earth. 📖

Their cry will reach the Red Sea.

## Jeremiah 49:22

Look! 📖

An eagle will soar, swoop, and spread its wings over Bozrah.

That day, Edom’s mighty men’s hearts will be like a woman’s heart in labor.

## Jeremiah 49:23

About Damascus, “Hamath and Arpad are ashamed because they heard bad news. 📖

They are stirred like the sea, and their anxiety cannot be calmed.

## Jeremiah 49:24

“Damascus is weak and turns to flee. 📖

Panic grips her, with pain and distress like a woman giving birth.

## Jeremiah 49:25

“How is the city of praise not abandoned, the town bringing Me joy? 📖

## Jeremiah 49:26

The LORD of Hosts declares, “Her young men will fall in the streets, and all her warriors will be silenced. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:27

“I will light a fire in Damascus’s walls, and it will consume Ben-hadad’s fortresses.” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:28

The LORD speaks about Kedar and Hazor’s kingdoms, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon defeated. 📖

“Rise, advance against Kedar, and destroy the eastern people!

## Jeremiah 49:29

“They will take tents, flocks, tent curtains, and all their goods, and take their camels. 📖

They will shout, ‘Terror is on every side!’

## Jeremiah 49:30

The LORD declares, “Hazor’s residents, run, escape quickly, and lie low! 📖

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has planned against you and made his strategy.

## Jeremiah 49:31

The LORD declares, “Rise and advance against a nation at ease, living securely. 📖

They have no gates or bars and live alone.

## Jeremiah 49:32

“Their camels and great herds will be taken as goods. 📖

I will scatter those who shave their temples in every direction.

I will bring disaster from all sides,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 49:33

“Hazor will become a home for jackals, deserted forever. 📖

No one will live or stay there.”

## Jeremiah 49:34

The LORD’s word about Elam came to Jeremiah the prophet early in Zedekiah king of Judah’s rule. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:35

The LORD of Hosts says, “I will break Elam’s bow, the main support of their strength. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:36

“I will bring four winds against Elam from heaven’s four corners and scatter them to all these winds. 📖

Elam’s exiles will go to every nation.

## Jeremiah 49:37

The LORD declares, “I will shatter Elam before enemies seeking their lives. 📖

I will bring disaster, My fierce anger.

I will send the sword after them until I finish them.

## Jeremiah 49:38

The LORD declares, “I will set My throne in Elam and destroy its king and officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:39

The LORD declares, “Yet in the last days, I will restore Elam from captivity.” 📖
