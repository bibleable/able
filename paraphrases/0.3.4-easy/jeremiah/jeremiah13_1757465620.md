# Jeremiah 13 — The ruined cloth and Judah’s pride

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah carries out an action with a linen cloth.

The LORD uses it to describe Judah’s ruined pride and warns about captivity and public shame.

## Jeremiah 13:1

The LORD told me, “Buy a linen loincloth and put it around your waist. 📖

Do not let it touch water.”

**Help with loincloth**

A loincloth is cloth worn around the waist and lower body.

Linen is cloth made from flax plant fibers.

## Jeremiah 13:2

I bought a loincloth as the LORD said and put it around my waist. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:3

The LORD’s word came to me a second time. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:4

“Take the loincloth you bought and are wearing. 📖

Go at once to Perath and hide it in a crack in the rocks.”

## Jeremiah 13:5

I went and hid it at Perath, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:6

Many days later, the LORD told me, “Get up and go to Perath. 📖

Get the loincloth I told you to hide there.”

## Jeremiah 13:7

I went to Perath, dug up the loincloth, and took it from its hiding place. 📖

It was ruined and completely useless.

## Jeremiah 13:8

Then the LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:9

“The LORD says, ‘In the same way, I will ruin Judah’s pride and Jerusalem’s great pride. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:10

“‘These evil people refuse My words, follow their stubborn hearts, and pursue other gods to serve and worship them. 📖

They will be like this loincloth, completely useless.

## Jeremiah 13:11

“‘A loincloth clings to a man’s waist. 📖

I made all Israel’s house and all Judah’s house cling to Me,’ declares the LORD.

‘They were to be My people for My fame, praise, and glory.

But they did not listen.

## Jeremiah 13:12

“Tell them what the LORD, Israel’s God, says. 📖

‘Every wineskin will be filled with wine.’

They will answer, ‘Do we not know that every wineskin should be filled with wine?’

**Help with wineskin**

A wineskin is a container made from animal skin for holding wine.

## Jeremiah 13:13

“Then tell them what the LORD says. 📖

‘I will fill everyone in this land with drunkenness.

This includes the kings on David’s throne, the priests, the prophets, and all Jerusalem’s people.

## Jeremiah 13:14

“‘I will smash them against each other, fathers and sons alike,’ declares the LORD. 📖

‘No mercy, pity, or compassion will stop Me destroying them.’”

## Jeremiah 13:15

Listen and pay attention. 📖

Do not be proud, because the LORD has spoken.

## Jeremiah 13:16

Give glory to the LORD your God before He brings darkness and your feet stumble on darkening mountains. 📖

You wait for light, but He turns it into deep gloom and thick darkness.

## Jeremiah 13:17

If you do not listen, I will weep secretly because of your pride. 📖

My eyes will overflow with tears because the LORD’s flock has been taken captive.

**Help with the flock**

The sheep picture the LORD’s people.

## Jeremiah 13:18

Tell the king and queen mother, “Sit in a low place. 📖

Your glorious crowns have fallen from your heads.”

## Jeremiah 13:19

The Negev’s cities are shut tight, and no one can open them. 📖

All Judah has been taken into exile, completely captured.

**Help with exile**

Exile means being forced away from one’s own land.

## Jeremiah 13:20

Lift your eyes and see those coming from the north. 📖

Where is the flock entrusted to you, the sheep that were your pride?

## Jeremiah 13:21

What will you say when He puts your close allies over you, the ones you trained? 📖

Will pain not grip you like a woman giving birth?

## Jeremiah 13:22

You may ask yourself, “Why did this happen to me?” 📖

Your great wrongdoing is why your skirts were stripped off and your body exposed.

## Jeremiah 13:23

Can an Ethiopian change his skin, or a leopard its spots? 📖

Neither can you do good, you who are used to doing evil.

**About this comparison**

The comparison describes how deeply the people’s harmful habits are established.

It does not say Ethiopian identity or skin color is evil.

## Jeremiah 13:24

“I will scatter you like chaff driven by desert wind. 📖

**Help with chaff**

Chaff is the light covering removed from grain.

## Jeremiah 13:25

The LORD declares, “This is your lot, the portion I measured for you. 📖

You forgot Me and trusted lies.

## Jeremiah 13:26

“So I will pull your skirts up over your face to expose your shame. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:27

“I have seen your adulteries, lustful neighing, and shameless prostitution on hills and in fields, your detestable acts. 📖

How terrible for you, Jerusalem!

How long will you remain unclean?

**Help with these images**

Jerusalem is pictured as a sexually unfaithful woman.

Adultery means sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

Neighing compares desire to the cries of a horse.

The exposure describes humiliation; it is not an instruction for readers to humiliate someone.
