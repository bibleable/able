# Jeremiah 29 — God’s message to people taken to Babylon

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah sends God’s message to people forced from Jerusalem to Babylon.

These promises address that community in exile.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Jeremiah 29:1

Jeremiah sent a letter from Jerusalem to the surviving elders, priests, prophets, and other exiles in Babylon. 📖

King Nebuchadnezzar had taken them there.

**Help with a word:** Exiles are people forced to live away from their homeland.

## Jeremiah 29:2

King Jeconiah, the queen mother, officials, craftsmen, and metalworkers had already been taken from Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:3

Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah carried the letter. 📖

Judah's King Zedekiah sent them to Babylon's King Nebuchadnezzar.

## Jeremiah 29:4

The LORD of armies, Israel's God, speaks to everyone he sent from Jerusalem to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:5

“Build houses and settle down. 📖

Plant gardens and eat what grows.”

## Jeremiah 29:6

“Marry and have children. 📖

Arrange marriages for your sons and daughters so they have children too.

Grow in number there rather than decrease.”

## Jeremiah 29:7

“Work for the good of the city where I sent you. 📖

Pray to the LORD for it.

When it does well, you will do well too.”

## Jeremiah 29:8

The LORD of armies says, “Do not let your prophets and fortune-tellers fool you. 📖

Do not listen to dreams you encourage them to tell.”

## Jeremiah 29:9

The LORD says, “They speak lies in my name. 📖

I did not send them.”

## Jeremiah 29:10

The LORD says, “When Babylon's 70 years are finished, I will help you. 📖

I will keep my promise and bring you back here.”

## Jeremiah 29:11

The LORD says, “I know my plans for you. 📖

They are for your good, not your harm.

They will give you a future and hope.”

## Jeremiah 29:12

“You will call to me and come to pray. 📖

I will listen.”

## Jeremiah 29:13

“You will seek me and find me when you search with your whole heart.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:14

The LORD says, “You will find me. 📖

I will free you from captivity.

I will gather you from every nation and place where I sent you.

I will bring you back to the place you left.”

## Jeremiah 29:15

You may say the LORD has given you prophets in Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:16

But hear what the LORD says about the king on David's throne. 📖

Hear about your fellow people who stayed in Jerusalem instead of going into exile.

## Jeremiah 29:17

The LORD of armies says, “I will send war, hunger, and disease against them. 📖

They will be like figs too rotten to eat.”

## Jeremiah 29:18

“I will pursue them with war, hunger, and disease. 📖

Every kingdom will be horrified by them.

Wherever I scatter them, people will curse, mock, and insult them.”

## Jeremiah 29:19

The LORD says, “They refused my words. 📖

I sent my servants the prophets again and again.

You exiles also refused to listen.”

## Jeremiah 29:20

All you exiles sent from Jerusalem to Babylon, hear the LORD's word. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:21

The LORD of armies speaks about Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah. 📖

They tell lies in his name.

“I will give them to Nebuchadnezzar.

He will kill them before your eyes.”

## Jeremiah 29:22

“Judah's exiles in Babylon will use their fate as a curse. 📖

They will say, ‘May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and Ahab.

Babylon's king roasted them in the fire.’”

## Jeremiah 29:23

The LORD says, “They did shameful things in Israel. 📖

They slept with their neighbours' wives and told lies in my name.

I did not command this.

I know and have witnessed it.”

## Jeremiah 29:24

Tell Shemaiah the Nehelamite this message. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:25

The LORD of armies says, “You sent letters in your own name to Jerusalem's people and priests. 📖

You wrote to the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah.”

## Jeremiah 29:26

“You told him the LORD had made him priest instead of Jehoiada and chief officer in the temple. 📖

You said he must restrain any ‘madman’ claiming to be a prophet with stocks and neck irons.”

**Help with words:** These were devices used to hold prisoners.

The insulting label is Shemaiah's accusation.

## Jeremiah 29:27

“You asked why he had not stopped Jeremiah of Anathoth from acting as a prophet.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:28

“You said Jeremiah had written to Babylon that the exile would be long. 📖

He told people to build homes, settle down, and plant gardens for food.”

## Jeremiah 29:29

Zephaniah the priest had read Shemaiah's letter to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:30

Then the LORD gave Jeremiah a message. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:31

“Tell all the exiles what the LORD says about Shemaiah the Nehelamite. 📖

I did not send him.

He spoke as a prophet and made you trust a lie.”

## Jeremiah 29:32

The LORD says, “I will punish Shemaiah and his descendants. 📖

None will remain among this people.

He will not see the good I bring my people.

He taught rebellion against the LORD.”
