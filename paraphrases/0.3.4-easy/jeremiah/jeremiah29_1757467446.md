# Jeremiah 29 — God’s message to people taken to Babylon

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah sends God’s message to people forced from Jerusalem to Babylon.

These promises address that community in exile.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Jeremiah 29:1

Jeremiah sent a letter from Jerusalem to the surviving elders, priests, prophets, and other exiles in Babylon. 📖

King Nebuchadnezzar had taken them there.

**Help with a word:** Exiles are people forced to live away from their homeland.

## Jeremiah 29:2

This was after King Jeconiah, the queen mother, and the court officials were exiled from Jerusalem. 📖

Judah’s and Jerusalem’s officials, craftsmen, and metalworkers were exiled too.

## Jeremiah 29:3

Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah carried the letter. 📖

Judah's King Zedekiah sent them to Babylon's King Nebuchadnezzar.

## Jeremiah 29:4

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, speaks to all the exiles carried from Jerusalem to Babylon. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 29:5

“Build houses and settle down. 📖

Plant gardens and eat what grows.”

## Jeremiah 29:6

“Take wives and have sons and daughters. 📖

Find wives for your sons and give your daughters in marriage so that they also have sons and daughters.

Multiply there; do not decrease.”

## Jeremiah 29:7

“Seek the prosperity of the city where I sent you as exiles. 📖

Pray to the LORD for it, because if it prospers, you will prosper too.”

**Help with prosperity**

Prosperity means doing well and having what is needed.

## Jeremiah 29:8

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Do not let your prophets and fortune-tellers deceive you. 📖

Do not listen to dreams you encourage them to tell.”

## Jeremiah 29:9

The LORD says, “They speak lies in my name. 📖

I did not send them.”

## Jeremiah 29:10

The LORD says, “When Babylon's 70 years are finished, I will help you. 📖

I will keep my promise and bring you back here.”

## Jeremiah 29:11

The LORD declares, “I know My plans for you. 📖

They are plans for your prosperity, not your harm, to give you a future and hope.”

## Jeremiah 29:12

“You will call to me and come to pray. 📖

I will listen.”

## Jeremiah 29:13

“You will seek me and find me when you search with your whole heart.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:14

The LORD declares, “You will find Me. 📖

I will restore you from captivity and gather you from all nations and places where I drove you.

I will bring you back to the place from which I sent you into exile,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 29:15

You may say the LORD has given you prophets in Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:16

But hear what the LORD says about the king on David's throne. 📖

Hear about your fellow people who stayed in Jerusalem instead of going into exile.

## Jeremiah 29:17

The LORD of Hosts says, “I will send sword, famine, and plague against them. 📖

I will make them like rotten figs, too bad to eat.

**Help with famine and plague**

Famine is a severe shortage of food.

A plague is a widespread, deadly disease.

## Jeremiah 29:18

“I will pursue them with sword, famine, and plague. 📖

I will make them a horror to all earth’s kingdoms.

They will be a curse, a ruin, and an object of scorn and insults wherever I drive them.”

## Jeremiah 29:19

The LORD declares, “I will do this because they did not listen to My words. 📖

I sent those words repeatedly through My servants the prophets.

You exiles did not listen either,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 29:20

The LORD says, “Hear My word, all you exiles I sent from Jerusalem to Babylon.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:21

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, speaks about Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah. 📖

They prophesy lies to you in His name.

“I will hand them to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.

He will kill them before your own eyes.”

## Jeremiah 29:22

“Judah's exiles in Babylon will use their fate as a curse. 📖

They will say, ‘May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and Ahab.

Babylon's king roasted them in the fire.’”

## Jeremiah 29:23

The LORD says, “They did shameful things in Israel. 📖

They slept with their neighbours' wives and told lies in my name.

I did not command this.

I know and have witnessed it.”

## Jeremiah 29:24

Tell Shemaiah the Nehelamite this message. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:25

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “You sent letters in your own name to all Jerusalem’s people and priests. 📖

You wrote to the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah and said these things.

## Jeremiah 29:26

“You told him the LORD had made him priest instead of Jehoiada and chief officer in the temple. 📖

You said he must restrain any ‘madman’ claiming to be a prophet with stocks and neck irons.”

**Help with words:** These were devices used to hold prisoners.

The insulting label is Shemaiah's accusation.

## Jeremiah 29:27

“You asked why he had not stopped Jeremiah of Anathoth from acting as a prophet.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:28

“You said Jeremiah had written to Babylon that the exile would be long. 📖

He told people to build homes, settle down, and plant gardens for food.”

## Jeremiah 29:29

Zephaniah the priest had read Shemaiah's letter to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:30

Then the LORD gave Jeremiah a message. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:31

“Tell all the exiles what the LORD says about Shemaiah the Nehelamite. 📖

I did not send him.

He spoke as a prophet and made you trust a lie.”

## Jeremiah 29:32

The LORD says, “I will surely punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his descendants. 📖

He will have no one left among these people or see the good I bring My people.

He preached rebellion against the LORD,” declares the LORD.
