# Jeremiah 14 — Drought, false promises, and prayer

**About this chapter**

A drought leaves people and animals without water or food.

Jeremiah prays, while the LORD rejects false prophecies of peace.

## Jeremiah 14:1

This word about the drought came from the LORD to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:2

“Judah mourns, and its gates weaken. 📖

Its people cry over the land, and a cry rises from Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 14:3

“The nobles send servants for water. 📖

They go to the water storage pits but find no water.

They return with empty jars, ashamed and humiliated, covering their heads.

## Jeremiah 14:4

“The ground is cracked because no rain has fallen. 📖

The farmers are ashamed and cover their heads.

## Jeremiah 14:5

“Even the female deer in the field abandons her newborn fawn because there is no grass. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:6

“Wild donkeys stand on bare heights, panting for air like jackals. 📖

Their eyes fail because there is no pasture.”

## Jeremiah 14:7

LORD, our wrongdoing testifies against us, but act for Your name’s sake. 📖

Our rebellions are many, and we have sinned against You.

## Jeremiah 14:8

Hope of Israel, its Savior in distress, why are You like a stranger in the land? 📖

Why are You like a traveler staying only one night?

## Jeremiah 14:9

Why are You like a man taken by surprise, like a warrior unable to save? 📖

Yet LORD, You are among us, and we bear Your name.

Do not abandon us!

**About the voice**

These questions and requests are part of a prayer to the LORD.

## Jeremiah 14:10

The LORD says about these people, “They love to wander and have not restrained their feet. 📖

So the LORD does not accept them.

He will now remember their wrongdoing and punish their sins.”

## Jeremiah 14:11

The LORD told me, “Do not pray for these people’s well-being. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:12

“Even if they fast, I will not hear their cry. 📖

Even if they bring burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them.

Instead, I will finish them with sword, famine, and plague.”

**Help with these words**

Fasting means going without food for a time.

Offerings are gifts given in worship.

A plague is a widespread, deadly disease.

## Jeremiah 14:13

I replied, “Ah, Lord GOD! 📖

The prophets tell them, ‘You will not see swords or suffer famine.

I will give you lasting peace here.’”

## Jeremiah 14:14

The LORD replied, “The prophets tell lies in My name. 📖

I did not send them, appoint them, or speak to them.

Their messages are false visions, worthless fortune-telling, and useless deceptions from their own minds.

## Jeremiah 14:15

“The LORD says this about prophets speaking in My name whom I did not send. 📖

They say, ‘No sword or famine will touch this land.’

Those very prophets will meet their end by sword and famine!

## Jeremiah 14:16

“The people they speak to will be thrown into Jerusalem’s streets because of famine and sword. 📖

No one will bury them, their wives, sons, or daughters.

I will pour their own evil over them.

## Jeremiah 14:17

“Tell them these words. 📖

‘My eyes overflow with tears and never stop, day or night.

The virgin daughter of my people has been shattered by a crushing blow, a terribly painful wound.

**Help with the daughter**

The community is pictured as a young woman who has been severely hurt.

## Jeremiah 14:18

“‘In the country, I see people killed by swords. 📖

In the city, I see people ravaged by famine!

Both prophet and priest travel to a land they do not know.’”

## Jeremiah 14:19

Have You completely rejected Judah? 📖

Do You despise Zion?

Why have You struck us beyond healing?

We hoped for peace, but no good came.

We hoped for healing, but there was only terror.

## Jeremiah 14:20

LORD, we admit our wickedness and our fathers’ guilt. 📖

We have indeed sinned against You.

## Jeremiah 14:21

For Your name’s sake, do not despise us or disgrace Your glorious throne. 📖

Remember Your covenant with us.

Do not break it.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Jeremiah 14:22

Can the nations’ worthless idols bring rain? 📖

Can the skies send showers by themselves?

Does this not come from You, LORD our God?

We put our hope in You because You have done all these things.
