# Jeremiah 34 — A broken promise to free slaves

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to Zedekiah during the war.

The people free enslaved Hebrews, then force them back into slavery and face judgment for breaking their promise.

## Jeremiah 34:1

This word came from the LORD to Jeremiah while Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon fought Jerusalem and all its surrounding cities. 📖

His whole army, all earth’s kingdoms under his control, and all other nations fought with him.

## Jeremiah 34:2

The LORD, Israel’s God, told Jeremiah to speak to Zedekiah king of Judah. 📖

“The LORD says, ‘I will hand this city to Babylon’s king, and he will burn it.

## Jeremiah 34:3

“‘You will not escape him but will be captured and handed over. 📖

You will see Babylon’s king eye to eye and speak face to face.

You will go to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 34:4

“‘Yet hear the LORD’s word, Zedekiah king of Judah. 📖

The LORD says, “You will not die by the sword,

## Jeremiah 34:5

“‘“but in peace. 📖

People will burn spices for you as they did for your fathers, the former kings.

They will mourn, ‘Alas, master!’

I Myself have spoken this word,” declares the LORD.’”

## Jeremiah 34:6

The prophet Jeremiah told Zedekiah king of Judah all these words in Jerusalem, 📖

## Jeremiah 34:7

while Babylon’s king’s army fought Jerusalem and Judah’s remaining cities, Lachish and Azekah. 📖

These were Judah’s only fortified cities still remaining.

## Jeremiah 34:8

King Zedekiah made a covenant with everyone in Jerusalem to announce freedom. 📖

Afterward, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Jeremiah 34:9

Each man was to free his Hebrew slaves, male and female. 📖

No one was to keep a fellow Jew enslaved.

## Jeremiah 34:10

All officials and people entering the covenant agreed to free their male and female slaves and stop holding them. 📖

They obeyed and released them,

## Jeremiah 34:11

but later changed their minds. 📖

They took back the men and women they had freed and forced them into slavery again.

## Jeremiah 34:12

Then the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:13

“The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I made a covenant with your ancestors when I brought them from Egypt. 📖

I brought them out of the house of slavery.

## Jeremiah 34:14

“‘I said, “Every seventh year, free your Hebrew brother who sold himself to you. 📖

He may serve six years, but then you must release him.”

But your fathers did not listen or turn their ears to hear.

## Jeremiah 34:15

“‘Recently, you turned back and did what pleased Me, each announcing freedom for his neighbor. 📖

You made a covenant before Me in the house bearing My Name.

## Jeremiah 34:16

“‘But now you changed your minds and dishonored My name. 📖

You took back the male and female slaves you freed to go wherever they wished.

You forced them to be your slaves again.

## Jeremiah 34:17

“‘The LORD says, “You disobeyed Me and did not announce freedom for your brother and neighbor. 📖

So I announce freedom for you,” declares the LORD, “freedom to fall by sword, plague, and famine!

I will make you a horror to all earth’s kingdoms.

**Help with freedom here**

The word is used bitterly in the judgment.

It describes being handed over to death instead of protected.

## Jeremiah 34:18

“‘“Those who broke My covenant failed to keep its terms before Me. 📖

I will treat them like the calf they cut in two to walk between its pieces.

**Help with the calf**

The people had passed between pieces of a cut animal as part of their agreement.

The judgment threatens them with a similar fate.

## Jeremiah 34:19

“‘“These are Judah’s and Jerusalem’s officials, court officials, priests, and all the land’s people who passed between the calf’s pieces. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:20

“‘“I will hand them to enemies seeking to kill them. 📖

Their bodies will feed the birds of the air and the animals of the earth.

## Jeremiah 34:21

“‘“I will hand Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials to enemies seeking their lives. 📖

I will give them to Babylon’s king’s army, which withdrew from you.

## Jeremiah 34:22

The LORD declares, “‘“I will command and bring them back to this city. 📖

They will fight, capture, and burn it.

I will make Judah’s cities deserted, with no one living there.”’”
