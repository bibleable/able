# Jeremiah 26 — Jeremiah faces a death sentence

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah brings a warning at the temple and is threatened with death.

The chapter compares what happened to Micah and Uriah and records who protected Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 26:1

This word came from the LORD at the beginning of Jehoiakim’s rule. 📖

Jehoiakim son of Josiah was king of Judah.

## Jeremiah 26:2

The LORD says, “Stand in My house’s courtyard. 📖

Speak all the words I command to the people of Judah’s cities coming to worship there.

Do not leave out a word.

## Jeremiah 26:3

“Perhaps each will listen and turn from his evil way of life. 📖

Then I may change My mind about the disaster I plan because of their evil deeds.

## Jeremiah 26:4

“Tell them what the LORD says. 📖

‘If you do not listen to Me or follow the law I set before you,

## Jeremiah 26:5

“‘and if you do not hear My servants the prophets, whom I repeatedly sent though you did not listen, 📖

## Jeremiah 26:6

“‘then I will make this house like Shiloh. 📖

I will make this city an object of cursing among all earth’s nations.’”

## Jeremiah 26:7

The priests, prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:8

When he finished everything the LORD commanded, the priests, prophets, and all the people seized him. 📖

They shouted, “You must die!

## Jeremiah 26:9

“How dare you prophesy in the LORD’s name that this house will be like Shiloh and this city deserted!” 📖

Everyone gathered against Jeremiah in the LORD’s house.

## Jeremiah 26:10

Judah’s officials heard and went from the king’s palace to the LORD’s house. 📖

They sat at the New Gate’s entrance.

## Jeremiah 26:11

The priests and prophets told the officials and everyone, “This man deserves death! 📖

You heard him prophesy against this city with your own ears!”

## Jeremiah 26:12

Jeremiah told all the officials and people, “The LORD sent me to prophesy these words against this house and city. 📖

You have heard them all.

## Jeremiah 26:13

“Correct your ways and actions and obey the LORD your God. 📖

Then He may change His mind about the disaster He announced.

## Jeremiah 26:14

“I am in your hands. 📖

Do to me what you think is good and right.

## Jeremiah 26:15

“But know this for certain. 📖

If you kill me, you bring innocent blood on yourselves, this city, and its residents.

The LORD truly sent me to speak all these words to you.”

## Jeremiah 26:16

The officials and all the people told the priests and prophets, “This man does not deserve death. 📖

He spoke to us in the LORD our God’s name!”

## Jeremiah 26:17

Some elders of the land stood and spoke to the whole gathering. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:18

“Micah the Moreshite prophesied in Hezekiah king of Judah’s days. 📖

He told all Judah’s people what the LORD of Hosts says.

‘Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become rubble, and the temple mountain a wooded ridge.’

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 26:19

“Did Hezekiah king of Judah or anyone in Judah kill him? 📖

Did Hezekiah not fear the LORD and seek His favor?

Did the LORD not change His mind about the announced disaster?

But we are about to bring great harm on ourselves!”

## Jeremiah 26:20

Another man, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim, also prophesied in the LORD’s name. 📖

He spoke against this city and land as Jeremiah did.

## Jeremiah 26:21

King Jehoiakim, all his mighty men, and officials heard. 📖

The king wanted to kill Uriah.

When Uriah learned this, he fled in fear to Egypt.

## Jeremiah 26:22

King Jehoiakim sent Elnathan son of Achbor and other men to Egypt. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:23

They brought Uriah from Egypt to King Jehoiakim. 📖

The king had him killed by the sword and his body thrown in the common people’s burial place.

## Jeremiah 26:24

But Ahikam son of Shaphan supported Jeremiah. 📖

So Jeremiah was not handed to the people to be killed.
