# Jeremiah 25 — Seventy years and the cup of judgment

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah recalls years of ignored warnings.

The LORD announces Babylon’s rule and later punishment, then describes judgment on the nations through a cup of wine.

## Jeremiah 25:1

This word about all Judah’s people came to Jeremiah in Jehoiakim’s fourth year. 📖

Jehoiakim son of Josiah was king of Judah.

It was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.

## Jeremiah 25:2

The prophet Jeremiah spoke these words to all Judah’s people and everyone living in Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:3

“The LORD’s word has come to me since Josiah’s thirteenth year until today, twenty-three years. 📖

Josiah son of Amon was king of Judah.

I spoke to you again and again, but you did not listen.

## Jeremiah 25:4

“The LORD repeatedly sent all His servants the prophets. 📖

But you did not listen or turn your ears to hear.

## Jeremiah 25:5

“They said, ‘Each of you must turn from evil ways and actions. 📖

Then you can live in the land the LORD gave you and your fathers forever and ever.

## Jeremiah 25:6

“‘Do not follow, serve, or worship other gods. 📖

Do not anger Me with things your hands make.

Then I will do you no harm.’

## Jeremiah 25:7

The LORD declares, “‘But you did not listen to Me, to your own harm. 📖

You angered Me with things your hands made.’

## Jeremiah 25:8

“The LORD of Hosts says, ‘Because you have not obeyed My words, 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 25:9

The LORD declares, “‘I will call all the northern families and send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. 📖

I will bring them against this land, its people, and all surrounding nations.

I will give them over to destruction, horror, contempt, and everlasting ruin.

## Jeremiah 25:10

“‘I will remove sounds of joy and gladness, voices of brides and bridegrooms, grinding millstones, and lamplight. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:11

“‘This whole land will become a deserted wasteland. 📖

These nations will serve Babylon’s king for seventy years.

## Jeremiah 25:12

The LORD declares, “‘When seventy years end, I will punish Babylon’s king and nation, the Chaldeans’ land, for their guilt. 📖

I will make it an everlasting ruin.

## Jeremiah 25:13

“‘I will bring on that land everything I announced against it. 📖

This includes everything in this book that Jeremiah prophesied against all nations.

## Jeremiah 25:14

“‘Many nations and great kings will enslave them. 📖

I will repay them according to their deeds and the work of their hands.’”

## Jeremiah 25:15

The LORD, Israel’s God, told me, “Take this cup of the wine of anger from My hand. 📖

Make all the nations I send you to drink it.

**Help with the cup**

Drinking the cup pictures receiving God’s judgment.

## Jeremiah 25:16

“They will drink, stagger, and lose their senses because of the sword I send among them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 25:17

I took the cup from the LORD’s hand and made every nation He sent me to drink it. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:18

These included Jerusalem, Judah’s cities, and its kings and officials. 📖

They became ruins, objects of horror, contempt, and cursing, as they are today.

## Jeremiah 25:19

They included Pharaoh king of Egypt, his officials, leaders, and all his people, 📖

## Jeremiah 25:20

all the mixed tribes, all Uz’s kings, and all the Philistines’ kings. 📖

These were Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and Ashdod’s survivors,

## Jeremiah 25:21

Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites, 📖

## Jeremiah 25:22

all Tyre’s and Sidon’s kings, and the kings of coastlands across the sea, 📖

## Jeremiah 25:23

Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who cut the corners of their hair, 📖

## Jeremiah 25:24

all Arabia’s kings and all kings of the mixed tribes living in the desert, 📖

## Jeremiah 25:25

all the kings of Zimri, Elam, and Media, 📖

## Jeremiah 25:26

and all the northern kings, near and far, one after another, all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

After all of them, Sheshach’s king will drink too.

## Jeremiah 25:27

“Tell them what the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says. 📖

‘Drink, become drunk, and vomit.

Fall and never rise again because of the sword I send among you.’

## Jeremiah 25:28

“If they refuse the cup from your hand, tell them what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

‘You certainly must drink it!

## Jeremiah 25:29

The LORD of Hosts declares, “‘I am beginning disaster in the city bearing My Name. 📖

How could you escape punishment?

You will not escape.

I am calling a sword against everyone living on earth.’

## Jeremiah 25:30

“Prophesy all these words against them. 📖

Say, ‘The LORD will roar from above and raise His voice from His holy home.

He will roar loudly over His pasture.

Like those treading grapes, He will shout against everyone living on earth.

## Jeremiah 25:31

The LORD declares, “‘The uproar will reach earth’s ends, because the LORD brings a charge against the nations. 📖

He judges all humanity and puts wicked people to the sword.’”

## Jeremiah 25:32

The LORD of Hosts says, “Look! 📖

Disaster spreads from nation to nation, and a mighty storm rises from earth’s ends.”

## Jeremiah 25:33

Those the LORD kills that day will lie from one end of the earth to the other. 📖

They will not be mourned, gathered, or buried.

They will be like dung on the ground.

## Jeremiah 25:34

Shepherds, wail and cry out! 📖

Leaders of the flock, roll in dust!

Your days of slaughter have come.

You will fall and shatter like fine pottery.

**Help with shepherds**

The flock pictures the people, and the shepherds picture their leaders.

## Jeremiah 25:35

The shepherds will not be able to flee. 📖

The flock’s leaders will not escape.

## Jeremiah 25:36

Hear the shepherds’ cries and the flock’s leaders mourning! 📖

The LORD is destroying their pasture.

## Jeremiah 25:37

The peaceful meadows are silent because of the LORD’s burning anger. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:38

He has left His den like a lion. 📖

Their land is ruined by the oppressor’s sword and the LORD’s fierce anger.
