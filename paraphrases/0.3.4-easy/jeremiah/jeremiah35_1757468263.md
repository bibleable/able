# Jeremiah 35 — The Rechabites keep their ancestor’s command

**About this chapter**

The Rechabites refuse wine because of their ancestor’s command.

The LORD contrasts their obedience with Judah’s refusal to listen.

## Jeremiah 35:1

This word came from the LORD to Jeremiah in Jehoiakim son of Josiah’s days as king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:2

“Go to the Rechabites’ house and speak to them. 📖

Bring them into a room of the LORD’s house and offer them wine.”

## Jeremiah 35:3

I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, son of Habazziniah, his brothers, and all his sons, the whole Rechabite house. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:4

I brought them to the LORD’s house, to the room of Hanan son of Igdaliah’s sons. 📖

Hanan was a man of God.

The room was beside the officials’ room and above the room of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the doorkeeper.

## Jeremiah 35:5

I put pitchers full of wine and cups before the Rechabite men and said, “Drink some wine.” 📖

## Jeremiah 35:6

They replied, “We do not drink wine. 📖

Our ancestor Jonadab son of Rechab commanded, ‘You and your descendants must never drink wine.

## Jeremiah 35:7

“‘You must never build houses, sow seed, or plant vineyards. 📖

Those things are not for you.

Live in tents all your lives, so that you live long in the land where you wander.’

## Jeremiah 35:8

“We obeyed everything our ancestor Jonadab son of Rechab commanded. 📖

None of us has drunk wine all our lives, neither we, our wives, sons, nor daughters.

## Jeremiah 35:9

“We have not built houses to live in or owned vineyards, fields, or crops. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:10

“We lived in tents and did exactly what our ancestor Jonadab commanded. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:11

“When Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon entered the land, we said, ‘Let us go into Jerusalem. 📖

We must escape the Chaldean and Aramean armies.’

So we stayed in Jerusalem.”

## Jeremiah 35:12

The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:13

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘Tell Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s residents this. 📖

Will you not accept discipline and obey My words?’ declares the LORD.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 35:14

“Jonadab son of Rechab’s words were obeyed. 📖

He forbade his sons to drink wine, and they have obeyed their ancestor to this day.

But I spoke to you again and again, and you did not obey Me!

## Jeremiah 35:15

“I repeatedly sent all My servants the prophets with this message. 📖

‘Each must turn from wicked ways, correct your actions, and stop following other gods to serve them.

Live in the land I gave you and your fathers.’

But you did not turn your ears or listen to Me.

## Jeremiah 35:16

“Jonadab son of Rechab’s sons obeyed their ancestor’s command. 📖

But these people did not listen to Me.

## Jeremiah 35:17

“The LORD God of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘Look! 📖

I will bring on Judah and all Jerusalem’s residents every disaster I announced.

I spoke, but they did not obey.

I called, but they did not answer.’”

## Jeremiah 35:18

Jeremiah told the Rechabites, “The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘You obeyed your ancestor Jonadab’s command. 📖

You kept all his commands and did everything he charged you to do.

## Jeremiah 35:19

“‘So the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Jonadab son of Rechab will never lack a man standing before Me.”’” 📖
