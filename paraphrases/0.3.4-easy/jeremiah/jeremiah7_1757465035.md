# Jeremiah 7 — The temple does not excuse wrongdoing

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends Jeremiah to speak at the temple gate.

He challenges false security, calls for justice, and condemns burning children as sacrifices.

## Jeremiah 7:1

This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:2

“Stand at the gate of the LORD’s house and announce this message. 📖

All you people of Judah entering these gates to worship the LORD, hear His word.

## Jeremiah 7:3

“The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, ‘Correct your ways and actions. 📖

Then I will let you live in this place.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 7:4

“‘Do not trust deceptive words, saying, “This is the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD. 📖

It is the temple of the LORD.”

## Jeremiah 7:5

“‘If you truly correct your ways and actions, if you treat each other justly, 📖

## Jeremiah 7:6

“‘You must also stop oppressing foreigners, children without fathers, and widows. 📖

You must stop shedding innocent blood here and following other gods to your own harm,

## Jeremiah 7:7

“‘then I will let you live here, in the land I gave your fathers forever and ever. 📖

**About the condition**

This promise continues the conditions in verses 5 and 6.

## Jeremiah 7:8

“‘But look! 📖

You keep trusting deceptive words that do you no good.

## Jeremiah 7:9

“‘Will you steal, murder, commit adultery, and swear falsely? 📖

Will you burn incense to Baal and follow other gods you have not known,

**Help with these words**

Adultery is sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

Baal is the name of another god.

## Jeremiah 7:10

“‘and then come and stand before Me in this house bearing My Name? 📖

Will you say, “We are rescued, so we can continue all these disgusting practices”?

## Jeremiah 7:11

“‘Has this house bearing My Name become a robbers’ den in your eyes? 📖

Yes, I have seen it too,’ declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 7:12

“‘Go to Shiloh, where I first made a dwelling for My Name. 📖

See what I did there because of My people Israel’s wickedness.

## Jeremiah 7:13

“‘Now you have done all these things,’ declares the LORD. 📖

‘I spoke to you again and again, but you would not listen.

I called, but you would not answer.

## Jeremiah 7:14

“‘So I will do to this house bearing My Name what I did to Shiloh. 📖

This is the house you trust, the place I gave you and your fathers.

## Jeremiah 7:15

“‘I will throw you out of My presence. 📖

I threw out all your brothers, all Ephraim’s descendants, in the same way.’

## Jeremiah 7:16

“As for you, do not pray for these people or plead for them. 📖

Do not beg Me, because I will not listen to you.

**About the listener**

The LORD speaks to Jeremiah.

The prohibition on praying for these people remains explicit.

## Jeremiah 7:17

“Do you not see what they do in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets? 📖

## Jeremiah 7:18

“The sons gather wood, the fathers light fires, and the women knead dough for cakes for the Queen of Heaven. 📖

They pour drink offerings to other gods to make Me angry.

**Help with Queen of Heaven**

This is a title for a goddess worshiped by the people.

## Jeremiah 7:19

“But am I the One they provoke?” declares the LORD. 📖

“Are they not harming themselves, to their own shame?

**Help with provoke**

To provoke someone is to stir up anger.

## Jeremiah 7:20

“The Lord GOD says, ‘Look! 📖

My anger and fury will pour over this place, over people and animals, field trees and the land’s produce.

It will burn and not be put out.’

## Jeremiah 7:21

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘Add your burnt offerings to your other sacrifices and eat the meat yourselves! 📖

## Jeremiah 7:22

“‘When I brought your fathers from Egypt, I did not merely command them about burnt offerings and sacrifices. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:23

“‘I gave this command, “Obey Me, and I will be your God, and you will be My people. 📖

Walk in all the ways I commanded, so that it may go well for you.”

## Jeremiah 7:24

“‘But they did not listen or turn their ears toward Me. 📖

They followed their own stubborn, evil hearts.

They went backward instead of forward.

## Jeremiah 7:25

“‘From the day your fathers left Egypt until today, I repeatedly sent all My servants the prophets. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:26

“‘But they did not listen to Me or turn their ears toward Me. 📖

They stiffened their necks and did more evil than their fathers.

**Help with stiff necks**

The image describes stubborn refusal to listen or change.

## Jeremiah 7:27

“When you tell them all this, they will not listen. 📖

When you call, they will not answer.

## Jeremiah 7:28

“So tell them, ‘This nation would not listen to the LORD its God or accept correction. 📖

Truth has died and disappeared from their lips.

## Jeremiah 7:29

“‘Cut off your hair and throw it away. 📖

Raise a song of grief on the bare heights.

The LORD has rejected and abandoned the generation under His anger.’

## Jeremiah 7:30

“The LORD declares, ‘Judah’s people have done evil in My sight. 📖

They put their disgusting objects in the house bearing My Name and polluted it.

## Jeremiah 7:31

“‘They built Topheth’s high places in the Valley of Ben-hinnom to burn their sons and daughters in fire. 📖

I never commanded this.

It did not even enter My mind.

## Jeremiah 7:32

“‘The days are coming,’ declares the LORD, ‘when this place will no longer be called Topheth or Ben-hinnom Valley. 📖

It will be called the Valley of Slaughter.

They will bury the dead in Topheth until no room remains.

## Jeremiah 7:33

“‘These people’s dead bodies will feed the birds of the air and the animals of the earth. 📖

No one will frighten them away.

## Jeremiah 7:34

“‘I will remove joy, gladness, and the voices of brides and bridegrooms from Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. 📖

The land will become a wasteland.’”
