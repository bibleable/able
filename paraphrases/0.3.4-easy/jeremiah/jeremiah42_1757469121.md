# Jeremiah 42 — The people ask what to do

**About this chapter**

The people promise to obey whatever answer the LORD gives.

After ten days, Jeremiah brings an answer with clear conditions about staying in Judah or going to Egypt.

## Jeremiah 42:1

All the commanders approached with Johanan son of Kareah, Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah, and everyone from least to greatest. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:2

They told Jeremiah the prophet, “Please hear our request and pray to the LORD your God for all these survivors. 📖

You can see how few remain of the many.

## Jeremiah 42:3

“Pray that the LORD your God tells us which way to go and what to do.” 📖

## Jeremiah 42:4

Jeremiah the prophet replied, “I heard you and will surely pray to the LORD your God as requested. 📖

I will tell you everything He answers and hide no word.”

## Jeremiah 42:5

They told Jeremiah, “May the LORD be a true, faithful witness against us if we disobey any word He sends. 📖

We will act on every word the LORD your God sends you to tell us.

## Jeremiah 42:6

“Pleasant or unpleasant, we will obey the LORD our God, to whom we send you. 📖

Then things will go well because we obey the LORD our God’s voice.”

## Jeremiah 42:7

After ten days, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:8

He called Johanan son of Kareah, all his commanders, and everyone from least to greatest. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:9

Jeremiah told them, “The LORD, Israel’s God, to whom you sent me with your request, says this. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:10

“‘If you truly stay in this land, I will build you up, not tear you down. 📖

I will plant you, not uproot you.

I will change My mind about the disaster I brought on you.

## Jeremiah 42:11

The LORD declares, “‘Do not fear Babylon’s king, whom you now fear. 📖

Do not fear him, because I am with you to save and rescue you from him.

## Jeremiah 42:12

“‘I will show you compassion, and he will show you compassion and restore you to your land.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:13

“But you may disobey the LORD your God and say, ‘We will not stay in this land.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:14

“You may say, ‘We will live in Egypt, where we will not see war, hear the horn, or lack bread.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:15

“Then hear the LORD’s word, Judah’s remaining people. 📖

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘If you are determined to go and live in Egypt,

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 42:16

“‘the sword you fear will catch you there, and famine will follow you into Egypt. 📖

You will die there.

## Jeremiah 42:17

“‘All who decide to live in Egypt will die by sword, famine, and plague. 📖

None will survive or escape My disaster.’

**Help with famine and plague**

Famine is a severe shortage of food.

A plague is a widespread, deadly disease.

## Jeremiah 42:18

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘My anger and fury poured over Jerusalem’s residents. 📖

In the same way, My fury will pour over you if you go to Egypt.

You will be cursed, feared as a horror, insulted, and disgraced.

You will never see this place again.’

## Jeremiah 42:19

“Judah’s remaining people, the LORD told you, ‘Do not go to Egypt.’ 📖

Know for certain that I warned you today!

## Jeremiah 42:20

“You deceived yourselves when you sent me to the LORD your God. 📖

You said, ‘Pray to the LORD our God for us.

Tell us everything He says, and we will do it.’

## Jeremiah 42:21

“I told you today, but you did not obey everything the LORD your God sent me to say. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:22

“Know for certain that you will die by sword, famine, and plague where you want to go and live.” 📖
