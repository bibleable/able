# Jeremiah 23 — The righteous Branch and false prophets

**About this chapter**

The LORD condemns destructive shepherds and promises a righteous King.

He challenges prophets who claim His authority for messages He did not give.

## Jeremiah 23:1

The LORD declares, “How terrible for shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of My pasture! 📖

**Help with shepherds**

The sheep picture the people, and the shepherds picture their leaders.

## Jeremiah 23:2

The LORD, Israel’s God, speaks about the shepherds tending His people. 📖

“You scattered My flock, drove them away, and did not care for them.

Look!

I will deal with you for your evil deeds,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 23:3

“I Myself will gather My flock’s survivors from all the lands where I drove them. 📖

I will return them to their pasture, where they will be fruitful and multiply.

## Jeremiah 23:4

The LORD declares, “I will appoint shepherds to care for them. 📖

They will no longer fear or be distressed, and none will go missing.

## Jeremiah 23:5

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when I will raise a righteous Branch for David. 📖

He will rule wisely as King and give justice and righteousness in the land.

**Help with Branch**

The plant image describes a ruler connected with David’s royal line.

## Jeremiah 23:6

“In His days, Judah will be saved, and Israel will live safely. 📖

His name will be The LORD Our Righteousness.

**Help with righteousness**

Righteousness means what is right before God.

## Jeremiah 23:7

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when people will no longer say this. 📖

‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites from Egypt.’

## Jeremiah 23:8

“Instead, they will say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought and led Israel’s descendants from the northern land. 📖

He brought them from all other lands where He drove them.’

They will live in their own land again.”

## Jeremiah 23:9

About the prophets, my heart is broken, and all my bones tremble. 📖

I am like a drunk man overcome by wine because of the LORD and His holy words.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah describes his distress.

## Jeremiah 23:10

The land is full of adulterers. 📖

Because of the curse, the land mourns, and the wilderness pastures dry up.

Their course is evil, and their power is misused.

**Help with adulterers**

Adulterers are people who are sexually unfaithful in marriage.

## Jeremiah 23:11

The LORD declares, “Prophet and priest alike are ungodly. 📖

I have found wickedness even in My house.

## Jeremiah 23:12

The LORD declares, “Their path will become slippery. 📖

They will be driven into darkness and fall there.

I will bring disaster in the year of their punishment.

## Jeremiah 23:13

“Among Samaria’s prophets, I saw something offensive. 📖

They spoke messages by Baal and led My people Israel the wrong way.

## Jeremiah 23:14

“Among Jerusalem’s prophets, I saw something horrible. 📖

They commit adultery, live by lies, and strengthen evildoers so that none turns from wickedness.

They are all like Sodom to Me, and Jerusalem’s people are like Gomorrah.”

## Jeremiah 23:15

The LORD of Hosts says about the prophets, “I will feed them wormwood and give them poisoned water. 📖

Ungodliness spread throughout the land from Jerusalem’s prophets.

**Help with these words**

Wormwood is a bitter plant used here in a picture of suffering.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 23:16

The LORD of Hosts says, “Do not listen to these prophets’ words. 📖

They fill you with false hopes.

They speak visions from their own minds, not from the LORD’s mouth.

## Jeremiah 23:17

“They keep telling those who despise Me, ‘The LORD says you will have peace.’ 📖

They tell everyone following a stubborn heart, ‘No harm will come to you.’

## Jeremiah 23:18

“Which of them stood in the LORD’s council to see and hear His word? 📖

Who paid attention and obeyed it?

## Jeremiah 23:19

“Look! 📖

The LORD’s storm has gone out in fury, a whirlwind swirling down on wicked people’s heads.

## Jeremiah 23:20

“The LORD’s anger will not turn back until He has fully carried out His heart’s purposes. 📖

In coming days, you will understand clearly.

## Jeremiah 23:21

“I did not send these prophets, yet they ran with their message. 📖

I did not speak to them, yet they prophesied.

## Jeremiah 23:22

“If they had stood in My council, they would have announced My words to My people. 📖

They would have turned them from evil ways and deeds.”

## Jeremiah 23:23

The LORD declares, “Am I only a God nearby, and not a God far away?” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:24

The LORD declares, “Can someone hide in secret places where I cannot see?” 📖

The LORD declares, “Do I not fill the heavens and earth?”

## Jeremiah 23:25

“I heard the prophets speaking lies in My name. 📖

They say, ‘I had a dream!

I had a dream!’

## Jeremiah 23:26

“How long will this go on in the hearts of prophets speaking lies and their own minds’ deceptions? 📖

## Jeremiah 23:27

“They think the dreams they tell each other will make My people forget My name. 📖

Their fathers forgot My name by worshiping Baal.

## Jeremiah 23:28

“Let a prophet with a dream tell it, but let someone with My word speak it truthfully. 📖

What is straw compared with grain?” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 23:29

The LORD declares, “Is My word not like fire and like a hammer smashing rock?” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:30

The LORD declares, “Look! 📖

I oppose prophets who steal words from each other and claim they come from Me.”

## Jeremiah 23:31

The LORD declares, “Yes! 📖

I oppose prophets using their own tongues and saying, ‘The LORD declares it.’”

## Jeremiah 23:32

The LORD declares, “I oppose those who tell false dreams and lead My people astray with reckless lies. 📖

I did not send or command them.

They give these people no benefit at all,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 23:33

“When these people, a prophet, or a priest asks you, ‘What is the LORD’s burden?’ give this answer. 📖

‘What burden?

I will abandon you,’ declares the LORD.

**Help with burden**

“Burden” can name a weighty message from God.

The words that follow challenge the misuse of that claim.

## Jeremiah 23:34

“I will punish the prophet, priest, or anyone claiming, ‘This is the LORD’s burden,’ along with his household. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:35

“Each must ask his friend and brother, ‘What has the LORD answered?’ 📖

Or ask, ‘What has the LORD spoken?’

## Jeremiah 23:36

“Do not speak of the LORD’s burden anymore. 📖

Each person’s own word becomes the burden, twisting the words of the living God, the LORD of Hosts, our God.

## Jeremiah 23:37

“Ask the prophet, ‘What has the LORD answered you?’ 📖

Ask, ‘What has the LORD spoken?’

## Jeremiah 23:38

“But if you claim, ‘This is the LORD’s burden,’ the LORD says this. 📖

You said, ‘This is the LORD’s burden,’ even though I specifically forbade that claim.

## Jeremiah 23:39

“So I will surely forget you and throw you from My presence. 📖

I will throw out both you and the city I gave you and your fathers.

## Jeremiah 23:40

“I will bring everlasting shame and lasting humiliation on you that will never be forgotten.” 📖
