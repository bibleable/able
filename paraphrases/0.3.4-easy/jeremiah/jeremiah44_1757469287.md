# Jeremiah 44 — The people refuse the LORD’s warning in Egypt

**About this chapter**

The Jews in Egypt defend their worship of the Queen of Heaven.

Jeremiah brings the LORD’s answer, including judgment and a sign involving Egypt’s king.

## Jeremiah 44:1

This word came to Jeremiah about all Jews living in Egypt, in Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis, and Pathros. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:2

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “You saw all My disaster against Jerusalem and Judah’s cities. 📖

Today they lie empty and ruined,

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 44:3

“because of their evil. 📖

They angered Me by burning incense and serving other gods unknown to them, you, or your fathers.

**Help with incense**

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Jeremiah 44:4

“I repeatedly sent all My servants the prophets, saying, ‘Do not do this detestable thing I hate.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 44:5

“But they did not listen, turn their ears, leave wickedness, or stop burning incense to other gods. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:6

“So My fury and anger poured out and burned in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. 📖

They became the deserted ruins they are today.

## Jeremiah 44:7

The LORD God of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Why harm yourselves so greatly? 📖

Why remove men, women, children, and babies from Judah, leaving no survivors?

## Jeremiah 44:8

“Why anger Me with your hands’ work, burning incense to other gods in Egypt where you settled? 📖

You will be cut off and become cursed and insulted among all earth’s nations.

## Jeremiah 44:9

“Did you forget your fathers’ wickedness, Judah’s kings’ and wives’ wickedness, and your and your wives’ wickedness? 📖

You committed it in Judah’s land and Jerusalem’s streets.

## Jeremiah 44:10

“To this day, they have not humbled themselves, shown reverence, or followed My teaching and commands. 📖

I placed these before you and your fathers.

## Jeremiah 44:11

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “I will turn against all Judah to bring disaster and wipe them out. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:12

“I will take away Judah’s remaining people who decided to live in Egypt. 📖

They will meet their end.

From least to greatest, all will die by sword or famine.

They will become cursed, feared as a horror, insulted, and disgraced.

## Jeremiah 44:13

“I will punish those living in Egypt as I punished Jerusalem, by sword, famine, and plague. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:14

“None of Judah’s remaining people living in Egypt will survive to return to Judah where they long to live. 📖

No one will return except a few who escape.”

**About the exception**

The text states a severe general judgment and then allows a few fugitives.

The exception is retained.

## Jeremiah 44:15

The men knew their wives burned incense to other gods. 📖

They and the women standing there, a great gathering, answered Jeremiah.

All the people living in Egypt and Pathros joined them.

## Jeremiah 44:16

“We will not listen to the word you spoke in the LORD’s name! 📖

## Jeremiah 44:17

“We will do everything we vowed, burning incense and pouring drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven. 📖

We, our fathers, kings, and officials did this in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets.

Then we had plenty of food and good things and saw no disaster.

**Help with Queen of Heaven**

This is a title for a goddess.

The people claim this worship brought them prosperity.

## Jeremiah 44:18

“Since we stopped incense and drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven, we lacked everything. 📖

We have been dying by sword and famine.”

## Jeremiah 44:19

The women added, “Our husbands knew when we burned incense and poured drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven. 📖

Was it without their knowledge that we made sacrificial cakes in her image and poured drink offerings?”

## Jeremiah 44:20

Jeremiah answered all the men and women who were speaking to him. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:21

“Did the LORD not remember the incense burned in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets? 📖

You, your fathers, kings, officials, and the land’s people burned it.

## Jeremiah 44:22

“The LORD could no longer bear your evil deeds and disgusting acts. 📖

So your land became deserted, a horror and a curse, without people, as today.

## Jeremiah 44:23

“You burned incense, sinned against the LORD, and did not obey His voice, teaching, commands, or testimonies. 📖

So this disaster happened, as you see today.”

## Jeremiah 44:24

Jeremiah told everyone, including all the women, “All Judah’s people in Egypt, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:25

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘You and your wives spoke and carried out your words. 📖

You said, “We will fulfill our vows, burning incense and pouring drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven.”

Go ahead, then!

Do what you promised and keep your vows!

## Jeremiah 44:26

The LORD says, “‘But hear My word, all Judah’s people living in Egypt. 📖

I have sworn by My great name.

No man of Judah living in Egypt will ever invoke My name again.

None will say, “As surely as the Lord GOD lives.”

## Jeremiah 44:27

“‘I watch over them for harm instead of good. 📖

Every man of Judah in Egypt will die by sword or famine until they are finished.

## Jeremiah 44:28

“‘Few who escape the sword will return from Egypt to Judah. 📖

All Judah’s remaining people who went to live in Egypt will know whose word stands, Mine or theirs!

## Jeremiah 44:29

The LORD declares, “‘This will be a sign that I will punish you here. 📖

Then you will know My threats of harm will surely stand.

## Jeremiah 44:30

“‘The LORD says, “I will hand Pharaoh Hophra king of Egypt to enemies seeking his life. 📖

I handed Zedekiah king of Judah to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the enemy seeking his life, in the same way.”’”
