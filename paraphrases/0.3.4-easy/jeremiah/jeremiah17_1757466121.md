# Jeremiah 17 — Trust, the heart, and the Sabbath

**About this chapter**

The LORD contrasts trust in human strength with trust in Him.

Jeremiah prays, and the LORD sends him to speak about keeping the Sabbath.

## Jeremiah 17:1

“Judah’s sin is written with an iron stylus, engraved with a diamond point. 📖

It is on the tablets of their hearts and the horns of their altars.

**Help with the engraving**

A stylus is a pointed writing tool.

The deep marks picture firmly established sin.

Altar horns were raised corners on altars.

## Jeremiah 17:2

“Even their children remember the altars and Asherah poles beside green trees and on high hills. 📖

**Help with Asherah poles**

These were objects used in worship of the goddess Asherah.

## Jeremiah 17:3

“My mountain in the countryside, I will give your wealth and all your treasures to be taken. 📖

This is because of the sin of your high places throughout all your borders.

## Jeremiah 17:4

“You will give up the inheritance I gave you. 📖

I will enslave you to your enemies in an unfamiliar land.

You have lit My anger, and it will burn forever.”

## Jeremiah 17:5

The LORD says, “Cursed is the man who trusts human beings and makes human flesh his strength. 📖

His heart turns from the LORD.

## Jeremiah 17:6

“He is like a desert shrub that does not see good times when they come. 📖

He lives in dry desert places, in a salty land where no one lives.

## Jeremiah 17:7

“But blessed is the man who trusts the LORD and has confidence in Him. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:8

“He is like a tree planted beside water, sending roots toward the stream. 📖

It does not fear heat, and its leaves stay green.

It does not worry in a drought year or stop producing fruit.

## Jeremiah 17:9

“The heart is more deceitful than anything else and beyond cure. 📖

Who can understand it?

## Jeremiah 17:10

“I, the LORD, search the heart and examine the mind. 📖

I reward each person according to his ways and what his deeds deserve.

## Jeremiah 17:11

“A person gaining wealth unjustly is like a partridge hatching eggs it did not lay. 📖

In the middle of his life, his riches will leave him.

In the end, he will be the fool.”

**Help with the partridge**

A partridge is a bird.

The comparison describes someone trying to keep wealth that is not rightfully his.

## Jeremiah 17:12

A glorious throne, high from the beginning, is the place of our holy sanctuary. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:13

LORD, Israel’s hope, all who abandon You will be ashamed. 📖

Those who turn away will be written in dust.

They abandoned the LORD, the spring of living water.

**Help with dust and water**

The images contrast insecure lives with God as the source of life.

## Jeremiah 17:14

LORD, heal me, and I will be healed. 📖

Save me, and I will be saved, because You are my praise.

## Jeremiah 17:15

Look! 📖

They keep telling me, “Where is the LORD’s word?

Let it come now!”

## Jeremiah 17:16

I have not run from being Your shepherd or desired the day of despair. 📖

You know that my lips spoke in Your presence.

## Jeremiah 17:17

Do not become a terror to me. 📖

You are my shelter on the day of disaster.

## Jeremiah 17:18

Let those pursuing me be ashamed, but do not let me be ashamed. 📖

Let them be terrified, but do not let me be terrified.

Bring them disaster and shatter them with double destruction.

**About the prayer**

Jeremiah asks for judgment on his persecutors.

The request for double destruction is retained.

## Jeremiah 17:19

The LORD told me, “Stand at the people’s gate, where Judah’s kings go in and out. 📖

Stand at all Jerusalem’s other gates too.

## Jeremiah 17:20

“Say, ‘Hear the LORD’s word, kings of Judah and all Judah’s and Jerusalem’s people entering these gates. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:21

“‘The LORD says, “Pay careful attention. 📖

Do not carry a load or bring it through Jerusalem’s gates on the Sabbath.

## Jeremiah 17:22

“‘“Do not carry loads from your houses or do any work on the Sabbath. 📖

Keep the Sabbath holy, as I commanded your ancestors.

**Help with Sabbath**

The Sabbath is the weekly day set apart for rest and worship.

## Jeremiah 17:23

“‘“But they did not listen or turn their ears toward Me. 📖

They stiffened their necks, refusing to listen or accept My discipline.

## Jeremiah 17:24

“‘“But if you listen carefully to Me,” says the LORD, “bring no loads through these gates on the Sabbath. 📖

Keep the Sabbath holy and do no work on it.

## Jeremiah 17:25

“‘“Then kings and princes will enter this city’s gates and sit on David’s throne. 📖

They will ride chariots and horses with their officials, Judah’s men, and Jerusalem’s residents.

People will live in this city forever.

## Jeremiah 17:26

“‘“People will come from Judah’s cities, Jerusalem’s surroundings, Benjamin’s land, the foothills, the hill country, and the Negev. 📖

They will bring burnt offerings, sacrifices, grain offerings, frankincense, and thank offerings to the LORD’s house.

**Help with the offerings**

These are gifts brought in worship.

Frankincense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Jeremiah 17:27

“‘“Keep the Sabbath holy by not carrying loads through Jerusalem’s gates. 📖

If you refuse to listen to Me, I will light a fire in its gates that cannot be put out.

It will consume Jerusalem’s fortresses.”’”
