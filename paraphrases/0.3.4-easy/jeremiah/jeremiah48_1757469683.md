# Jeremiah 48 — A message about Moab

**About this chapter**

Moab’s pride, destruction, and grief are described at length.

The LORD also promises a later restoration from captivity.

## Jeremiah 48:1

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says about Moab, “How terrible for Nebo, which will be ruined! 📖

Kiriathaim will be captured and disgraced, and the fortress shattered and dismantled.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 48:2

“Moab receives praise no longer. 📖

In Heshbon, they plan harm against her, saying, ‘Come, let us end her nationhood.’

Madmen’s people, you too will be silenced, and the sword will pursue you.

## Jeremiah 48:3

“A voice cries from Horonaim, ‘Devastation and great destruction!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 48:4

“Moab will be shattered, and her little ones will cry. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:5

“They weep bitterly as they climb to Luhith. 📖

On the descent to Horonaim, cries of distress sound over destruction.

## Jeremiah 48:6

“‘Flee! 📖

Run for your lives!

Become like a juniper in the desert.’

**Help with juniper**

A juniper is a kind of shrub or tree.

## Jeremiah 48:7

“Because you trust your works and treasures, you too will be captured. 📖

Chemosh will go into exile with his priests and officials.

**Help with Chemosh**

Chemosh is Moab’s god.

Exile means being forced away from one’s own land.

## Jeremiah 48:8

“The destroyer will attack every city, and no town will escape. 📖

The valley will be ruined and the high plain destroyed, as the LORD said.

## Jeremiah 48:9

“Put salt on Moab, because she will be ruined. 📖

Her cities will be deserted, with no one living there.

## Jeremiah 48:10

“Cursed is the one who carelessly does the LORD’s work. 📖

Cursed is the one who holds his sword back from bloodshed.

**About this severe statement**

This belongs to the announced judgment on Moab.

It is retained without turning it into an instruction for readers to shed blood.

## Jeremiah 48:11

“Moab has lived at ease since youth, like wine settled on its dregs. 📖

He was not poured between containers or sent into exile.

His flavor and smell remained unchanged.

**Help with dregs**

Dregs are material that settles at the bottom of wine.

The wine image pictures Moab’s undisturbed life.

## Jeremiah 48:12

The LORD declares, “Days are coming when I will send wanderers to pour him out. 📖

They will empty his containers and break his jars.

## Jeremiah 48:13

“Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, as Israel’s house was ashamed of trusting Bethel. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:14

“How can you say, ‘We are warriors, mighty men ready for battle’? 📖

## Jeremiah 48:15

The King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, declares, “Moab is destroyed, its towns invaded. 📖

Its best young men have gone down to slaughter.

## Jeremiah 48:16

“Moab’s disaster is near, and his suffering rushes quickly. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:17

“All surrounding him and knowing his name, mourn for him! 📖

Tell how the mighty royal rod, the glorious staff, is broken!

## Jeremiah 48:18

“Daughter living in Dibon, come down from glory and sit on dry ground. 📖

Moab’s destroyer came against you and ruined your fortresses.

## Jeremiah 48:19

“Aroer’s resident, stand beside the road and watch! 📖

Ask the fleeing man or escaping woman, ‘What happened?’

## Jeremiah 48:20

“Moab is ashamed because it is shattered. 📖

Wail and cry out!

Announce beside the Arnon that Moab is destroyed.

## Jeremiah 48:21

“Judgment came on the high plain, on Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath, 📖

## Jeremiah 48:22

“on Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim, 📖

## Jeremiah 48:23

“on Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon, 📖

## Jeremiah 48:24

“on Kerioth, Bozrah, and all Moab’s towns, far and near. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:25

The LORD declares, “Moab’s horn is cut off, and his arm is broken. 📖

**Help with horn and arm**

These images describe lost strength and power.

## Jeremiah 48:26

“Make him drunk because he made himself great against the LORD. 📖

Moab will roll in his own vomit and become a laughingstock too.

## Jeremiah 48:27

“Did you not ridicule Israel? 📖

Was he found among thieves?

Whenever you speak of him, you shake your head.

## Jeremiah 48:28

“Moab’s people, leave the towns and live among rocks! 📖

Be like a dove nesting at a cave’s mouth.

## Jeremiah 48:29

“We heard of Moab’s excessive pride and conceit, his proud arrogance and haughty heart. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:30

The LORD declares, “I know his arrogance, but it is useless. 📖

His boasts are as empty as his deeds.

## Jeremiah 48:31

“So I will wail for Moab, cry for all Moab, and moan for Kir-heres’s men. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:32

“Vine of Sibmah, I will weep for you more than for Jazer. 📖

Your shoots reached the sea, even reaching Jazer.

The destroyer came on your summer fruit and grape harvest.

## Jeremiah 48:33

“Joy and gladness are removed from Moab’s orchard and fields. 📖

I stopped wine flowing from the presses.

No one treads grapes shouting for joy.

Their shouts are not joyful.

## Jeremiah 48:34

“Cries rise from Heshbon to Elealeh and Jahaz, from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah. 📖

Even Nimrim’s waters have dried up.

## Jeremiah 48:35

The LORD declares, “I will end those offering at Moab’s high places and burning incense to their gods. 📖

**Help with incense**

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Jeremiah 48:36

“My heart mourns like a flute for Moab and Kir-heres’s men, because their acquired wealth perished. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:37

“Every head is shaved and every beard cut. 📖

Every hand has a cut, and every waist wears sackcloth.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn in mourning.

The cuts are described grief customs, not instructions to harm oneself.

## Jeremiah 48:38

The LORD declares, “Everyone mourns on all Moab’s rooftops and public squares. 📖

I shattered Moab like an unwanted jar.”

## Jeremiah 48:39

“How shattered it is! 📖

How they wail!

Moab turned his back in shame!

He became an object of ridicule and horror to everyone around.”

## Jeremiah 48:40

The LORD says, “Look! 📖

An eagle swoops and spreads its wings against Moab.

## Jeremiah 48:41

“Kirioth is taken, and the strongholds seized. 📖

That day, Moab’s warriors’ hearts will be like a woman’s heart in labor.

## Jeremiah 48:42

“Moab will be destroyed as a nation because he made himself great against the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:43

The LORD declares, “Moab’s resident, terror, a pit, and a trap await you. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:44

“Whoever flees panic will fall into the pit. 📖

Whoever climbs out will be trapped.

I will bring Moab the year of punishment,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 48:45

“Those fleeing will stand helpless in Heshbon’s shadow. 📖

Fire went out from Heshbon, a flame from within Sihon.

It consumes Moab’s foreheads and the skulls of the sons of uproar.

## Jeremiah 48:46

“How terrible for you, Moab! 📖

Chemosh’s people perished, because your sons were exiled and your daughters taken captive.

## Jeremiah 48:47

The LORD declares, “Yet in later days, I will restore Moab from captivity.” 📖

Here the judgment on Moab ends.
