# Jeremiah 47 — A message about the Philistines

**About this chapter**

The LORD announces destruction coming against the Philistines.

The chapter pictures the attack as rising water and a sword that cannot rest.

## Jeremiah 47:1

This word came from the LORD to Jeremiah the prophet about the Philistines before Pharaoh struck Gaza. 📖

## Jeremiah 47:2

The LORD says, “Look at the waters rising from the north into an overflowing flood. 📖

They will cover the land, everything in it, the cities, and their people.

Everyone in the land will cry and wail,

## Jeremiah 47:3

“at galloping horses’ hooves, rumbling chariots, and clattering wheels. 📖

Fathers will not turn back for their sons.

Their hands will hang weakly.

## Jeremiah 47:4

“The day has come to destroy all the Philistines and remove every remaining ally of Tyre and Sidon. 📖

The LORD will destroy the Philistines, the survivors from Caphtor’s coasts.

## Jeremiah 47:5

“Gaza’s people will shave their heads in mourning, and Ashkelon will be silent. 📖

Survivors in their valley, how long will you cut yourselves?

**About the mourning actions**

The verse describes grief customs.

It is not an instruction for readers to injure themselves.

## Jeremiah 47:6

“‘Alas, sword of the LORD, how long before you rest? 📖

Return to your sheath!

Stop and be still!’

**Help with sheath**

A sheath is a cover that holds a sword.

## Jeremiah 47:7

“How can it rest when the LORD commanded it? 📖

He appointed it against Ashkelon and the shore of its coastland.”
