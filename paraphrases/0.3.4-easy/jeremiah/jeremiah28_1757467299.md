# Jeremiah 28 — Hananiah breaks the yoke

**About this chapter**

Hananiah claims that Babylon’s power will end within two years.

Jeremiah challenges that message, and the LORD gives a different answer.

## Jeremiah 28:1

In that same year, the fourth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, it was the fifth month. 📖

This was near the beginning of his rule.

The prophet Hananiah son of Azzur, from Gibeon, spoke to me in the LORD’s house.

The priests and all the people were present.

## Jeremiah 28:2

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘I have broken Babylon’s king’s yoke. 📖

**About the claim**

Hananiah claims to speak for the LORD.

His message is later challenged.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 28:3

“‘Within two years, I will bring all the LORD’s house’s objects back here. 📖

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took them from here to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 28:4

Hananiah continued, “The LORD declares, ‘I will bring back Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah. 📖

I will bring back all Judah’s exiles in Babylon.

I will break Babylon’s king’s yoke.’”

## Jeremiah 28:5

The prophet Jeremiah answered the prophet Hananiah before the priests and all the people standing in the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:6

Jeremiah said, “Amen! 📖

May the LORD do this and fulfill your prophecy!

May He bring His house’s objects and all the exiles back here from Babylon.

**Help with Amen**

Amen expresses agreement or a wish that something be so.

## Jeremiah 28:7

“But listen to this message I speak where you and all the people can hear. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:8

“Prophets before you and me spoke of war, disaster, and plague against many lands and great kingdoms. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:9

“A prophet may prophesy peace. 📖

Only if his word comes true will he be recognized as truly sent by the LORD.”

## Jeremiah 28:10

The prophet Hananiah took the yoke from the prophet Jeremiah’s neck and broke it. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:11

Hananiah announced before everyone, “The LORD says, ‘This is how I will break Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon’s yoke. 📖

Within two years, I will remove it from all nations’ necks.’”

Then the prophet Jeremiah went away.

## Jeremiah 28:12

Shortly after the prophet Hananiah broke the yoke from his neck, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:13

“Tell Hananiah what the LORD says. 📖

‘You broke a wooden yoke, but in its place you made an iron yoke.’

## Jeremiah 28:14

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘I put an iron yoke on all these nations’ necks. 📖

They must serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and they will serve him.

I even gave him control over the field’s animals.’”

## Jeremiah 28:15

The prophet Jeremiah told the prophet Hananiah, “Listen, Hananiah! 📖

The LORD did not send you.

You persuaded these people to trust a lie.

## Jeremiah 28:16

“So the LORD says, ‘I will remove you from the earth’s face. 📖

You will die this year because you preached rebellion against the LORD.’”

## Jeremiah 28:17

In the seventh month of that same year, the prophet Hananiah died. 📖
