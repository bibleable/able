# Jeremiah 19 — The broken clay jar

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Jeremiah to break a clay jar before elders and priestly leaders.

The warning includes child sacrifice, siege, and people eating human flesh.

## Jeremiah 19:1

The LORD says, “Buy a clay jar from a potter. 📖

Take some elders of the people and leaders of the priests.

## Jeremiah 19:2

“Go to the Valley of Ben-hinnom near the Potsherd Gate’s entrance. 📖

Announce there the words I give you.

**Help with Potsherd**

A potsherd is a piece of broken pottery.

## Jeremiah 19:3

“Say, ‘Kings of Judah and Jerusalem’s residents, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “I will bring disaster here that makes every hearer’s ears ring.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 19:4

“‘“They abandoned Me and made this a foreign place. 📖

They burned incense to other gods unknown to them, their fathers, or Judah’s kings.

They filled this place with innocent blood.

## Jeremiah 19:5

“‘“They built high places for Baal to burn their children as offerings to Baal. 📖

I never commanded or mentioned this, and it did not enter My mind.

**Help with Baal and incense**

Baal is the name of another god.

Incense is a substance burned to give a sweet smell.

The LORD explicitly rejects burning children as offerings.

## Jeremiah 19:6

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when this place will not be called Topheth or Ben-hinnom Valley. 📖

It will be called the Valley of Slaughter.

## Jeremiah 19:7

“Here I will ruin Judah’s and Jerusalem’s plans. 📖

I will make them fall by swords before their enemies, those seeking to kill them.

I will give their dead bodies to feed birds and earth’s animals.

## Jeremiah 19:8

“I will make this city deserted and an object of scorn. 📖

Everyone passing will be horrified and mock all its wounds.

## Jeremiah 19:9

“I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and daughters and one another. 📖

This will happen during the siege and distress brought by enemies seeking to kill them.’

**About this difficult warning**

The text describes cannibalism during a siege.

Cannibalism means eating human flesh.

The severe details and the LORD’s stated action are retained.

## Jeremiah 19:10

“Then break the jar in front of the men with you. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:11

“Announce what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

‘I will shatter this nation and city like a potter’s jar that can never be repaired.

They will bury the dead in Topheth until there is no more room.

## Jeremiah 19:12

“‘This is what I will do to this place and its residents,’ declares the LORD. 📖

‘I will make this city like Topheth.

## Jeremiah 19:13

“‘Jerusalem’s houses and the houses of Judah’s kings will be polluted like Topheth. 📖

On all those rooftops, they burned incense to all heaven’s army and poured drink offerings to other gods.’”

**Help with heaven’s army**

Here it refers to heavenly bodies worshiped by the people.

## Jeremiah 19:14

Jeremiah returned from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy. 📖

He stood in the LORD’s house courtyard and announced to everyone,

## Jeremiah 19:15

“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘Look! 📖

I will bring every disaster I announced on this city and all its surrounding villages.

They have stiffened their necks and refused My words.’”

**Help with stiff necks**

The image describes stubborn refusal to listen.
