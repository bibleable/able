# Jeremiah 6 — Jerusalem refuses to listen

**About this chapter**

The LORD warns of an attack from the north.

He challenges false promises of peace and leaders who refuse His instruction.

## Jeremiah 6:1

“Sons of Benjamin, run for safety! 📖

Flee Jerusalem!

Sound the ram’s horn in Tekoa and raise a signal over Beth-haccherem.

Disaster and great destruction approach from the north.

## Jeremiah 6:2

“Daughter Zion is beautiful and delicate, but I will destroy her. 📖

**Help with Daughter Zion**

Jerusalem and its people are pictured as a daughter.

## Jeremiah 6:3

“Shepherds and their flocks will come against her. 📖

They will pitch tents all around her, each tending his own part.

## Jeremiah 6:4

“They will say, ‘Prepare for battle against her! 📖

Rise, and let us attack at noon.

How terrible for us!

Daylight is fading, and evening shadows grow long.

## Jeremiah 6:5

“‘Rise, and let us attack at night and destroy her fortresses!’” 📖

## Jeremiah 6:6

The LORD of Hosts says, “Cut down trees and build a ramp to attack Jerusalem. 📖

This city must be punished.

It has nothing but oppression within it.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 6:7

“As a well pours out water, she pours out evil. 📖

Violence and destruction sound within her.

Sickness and wounds are always before Me.

## Jeremiah 6:8

“Accept this warning, Jerusalem, or I will turn away from you. 📖

I will make you a deserted land with no one living there.”

## Jeremiah 6:9

The LORD of Hosts says, “Gather Israel’s survivors as thoroughly as someone gathering leftover grapes from a vine. 📖

Pass your hand over the branches once more like a grape gatherer.”

## Jeremiah 6:10

To whom can I give this warning? 📖

Who will listen to me?

Their ears are closed, so they cannot hear.

The LORD’s word offends them, and they find no pleasure in it.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah speaks about people refusing to listen.

## Jeremiah 6:11

I am full of the LORD’s anger and tired of holding it back. 📖

“Pour it over children in the street and young men gathered together.

Husband and wife will be captured, the old and very old alike.

**About the speaker change**

Jeremiah first describes his anger.

The quoted command that follows announces the LORD’s judgment.

## Jeremiah 6:12

“Their houses, fields, and wives will be handed to others. 📖

I will stretch My hand against those living in the land,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 6:13

“From the least to the greatest, all are greedy for gain. 📖

From prophet to priest, all practice deceit.

## Jeremiah 6:14

“They treat My people’s wound with very little care. 📖

They say, ‘Peace, peace,’ when there is no peace at all.

## Jeremiah 6:15

“Are they ashamed of their disgusting deeds? 📖

No, they have no shame and do not even know how to blush.

So they will fall among the fallen.

When I punish them, they will collapse,” says the LORD.

## Jeremiah 6:16

The LORD says, “Stand at the crossroads and look. 📖

Ask for the ancient paths, ‘Where is the good way?’

Walk in it, and you will find rest for your souls.

But they said, ‘We will not walk in it!’

## Jeremiah 6:17

“I appointed watchmen over you and said, ‘Listen for the ram’s horn.’ 📖

But they answered, ‘We will not listen!’

## Jeremiah 6:18

“So hear, nations! 📖

Gathered peoples, learn what will happen to them.

## Jeremiah 6:19

“Earth, hear! 📖

I am bringing disaster on these people, the fruit of their own plans.

They ignored My word and rejected My teaching.

## Jeremiah 6:20

“What use to Me is frankincense from Sheba or sweet cane from a distant land? 📖

Your burnt offerings are unacceptable, and your sacrifices do not please Me.”

**Help with frankincense and sweet cane**

These were fragrant substances used in worship.

Burnt offerings and sacrifices were gifts offered to God.

## Jeremiah 6:21

The LORD says, “I will put stumbling blocks before these people. 📖

Fathers and sons will stumble together.

Friends and neighbors will die.”

## Jeremiah 6:22

The LORD says, “Look! 📖

An army comes from the north.

A great nation is stirred up from the ends of the earth.

## Jeremiah 6:23

“They hold bows and spears and are cruel and without mercy. 📖

Their voice roars like the sea.

They ride horses and line up for battle against you, Daughter Zion.”

## Jeremiah 6:24

We heard the report, and our hands hang weakly. 📖

Suffering grips us, with pain like a woman giving birth.

## Jeremiah 6:25

Do not go into the fields or walk on the road. 📖

The enemy has a sword, and terror is on every side.

## Jeremiah 6:26

Daughter of my people, put on sackcloth and roll in ashes. 📖

Mourn with bitter cries, as for an only son.

The destroyer will suddenly come upon us.

**Help with sackcloth and ashes**

Rough sackcloth and ashes were used to show deep grief.

## Jeremiah 6:27

“I appointed you to examine My people like ore, so that you may know and test their ways. 📖

**Help with ore**

Ore is rock containing metal.

Testing metal pictures Jeremiah’s work of examining the people’s conduct.

## Jeremiah 6:28

“They are all stubborn rebels who go around spreading harmful accusations. 📖

They are bronze and iron, all corrupt.

## Jeremiah 6:29

“The bellows blow fiercely, burning away lead with fire. 📖

The refining goes on without success, because the wicked are not removed.

**Help with bellows and refining**

Bellows blow air to make a fire hotter.

Refining uses heat to remove unwanted material from metal.

Here the failed process pictures people who refuse correction.

## Jeremiah 6:30

“They are called rejected silver, because the LORD has rejected them.” 📖
