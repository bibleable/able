# Jeremiah 40 — Jeremiah stays with the people

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah is freed and stays with Gedaliah at Mizpah.

People gather and harvest food, but a warning about a murder plot is rejected.

## Jeremiah 40:1

The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah after Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, released him at Ramah. 📖

Nebuzaradan found him chained among Jerusalem’s and Judah’s captives being exiled to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 40:2

The guard captain found Jeremiah and said, “The LORD your God decreed this disaster on this place. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:3

“Now the LORD fulfilled it and did as He said. 📖

This happened because you people sinned against the LORD and did not obey His voice.

## Jeremiah 40:4

“Today I free you from the chains on your wrists. 📖

If you wish to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will care for you.

If you think that is wrong, go no farther.

The whole land is before you.

Go wherever seems good and right.”

## Jeremiah 40:5

Before Jeremiah left, Nebuzaradan added, “Return to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan. 📖

Babylon’s king appointed him over Judah’s cities.

Stay with him among the people, or go anywhere you think right.”

The guard captain gave Jeremiah food and a gift and released him.

## Jeremiah 40:6

Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah. 📖

He stayed with him among the people left in the land.

## Jeremiah 40:7

All army commanders and men in the field heard that Babylon’s king appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam over the land. 📖

He was responsible for the poorest men, women, and children who had not been exiled to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 40:8

They and their men came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. 📖

They included Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan and Jonathan sons of Kareah, and Seraiah son of Tanhumeth.

There were also Ephai the Netophathite’s sons and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite.

## Jeremiah 40:9

Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, swore to them and their men. 📖

“Do not fear serving the Chaldeans.

Live in the land and serve Babylon’s king, and things will go well.

## Jeremiah 40:10

“I will stay in Mizpah to represent you before the Chaldeans coming to us. 📖

Gather wine grapes, summer fruit, and oil, put them in storage jars, and live in the cities you took.”

## Jeremiah 40:11

All Jews in Moab, Ammon, Edom, and all other lands heard that Babylon’s king left survivors in Judah. 📖

He appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, over them.

## Jeremiah 40:12

All returned from the places where they were driven away and came to Gedaliah at Mizpah in Judah. 📖

They gathered plentiful wine grapes and summer fruit.

## Jeremiah 40:13

Johanan son of Kareah and all army commanders in the field came to Gedaliah at Mizpah, 📖

## Jeremiah 40:14

and asked, “Do you know that Baalis king of the Ammonites sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to kill you?” 📖

But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them.

## Jeremiah 40:15

Johanan son of Kareah spoke privately to Gedaliah at Mizpah. 📖

“Let me kill Ishmael son of Nethaniah.

No one will know.

Why let him kill you, scatter Judah’s people gathered to you, and destroy Judah’s remaining people?”

## Jeremiah 40:16

Gedaliah son of Ahikam told Johanan son of Kareah, “Do not do that! 📖

What you say about Ishmael is a lie.”
