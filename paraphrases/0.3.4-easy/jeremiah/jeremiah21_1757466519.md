# Jeremiah 21 — Life and death before Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

King Zedekiah asks Jeremiah to seek the LORD’s help.

The LORD announces that surrender is the way to survive and commands the royal house to act justly.

## Jeremiah 21:1

The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah when King Zedekiah sent messengers to him. 📖

They were Pashhur son of Malchijah and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah.

They spoke to him.

**About Pashhur**

This Pashhur is named as Malchijah’s son.

The Pashhur in chapter 20 is named as Immer’s son.

## Jeremiah 21:2

“Please ask the LORD for us, because Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon is fighting us. 📖

Perhaps the LORD will do something like His past wonders so that Nebuchadnezzar withdraws.”

## Jeremiah 21:3

Jeremiah answered, “Tell Zedekiah this. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:4

“The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I will turn your weapons against you. 📖

You are using them against Babylon’s king and the Chaldeans surrounding you outside the wall.

I will gather their forces in this city’s center.

## Jeremiah 21:5

“‘I Myself will fight you with an outstretched hand and powerful arm, in anger, fury, and great wrath. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:6

“‘I will strike the people and animals living in this city. 📖

They will die in a terrible plague.’

**Help with plague**

A plague is a widespread, deadly disease.

## Jeremiah 21:7

The LORD declares, “‘Afterward, I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, and this city’s remaining people. 📖

These are the survivors of plague, sword, and famine.

They will fall into Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon’s hands, into the hands of enemies seeking their lives.

He will put them to the sword without sparing them or showing pity or compassion.’

## Jeremiah 21:8

“Also tell the people what the LORD says. 📖

‘Look!

I set the way of life and the way of death before you.

## Jeremiah 21:9

“‘Anyone staying in this city will die by sword, famine, and plague. 📖

Anyone leaving and surrendering to the Chaldeans surrounding you will live.

He will keep his life like goods gained in war.

**About this promise**

The promise of survival is tied to leaving and surrendering.

## Jeremiah 21:10

The LORD declares, “‘I have turned against this city to bring disaster instead of good. 📖

It will be handed to Babylon’s king, who will burn it.’

## Jeremiah 21:11

“Tell Judah’s royal house to hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:12

“House of David, the LORD says, ‘Give justice every morning. 📖

Rescue robbery victims from those who oppress them.

Otherwise, My anger will burst out like fire because of their evil deeds.

It will burn with no one to put it out.

## Jeremiah 21:13

The LORD declares, “‘Look! 📖

I oppose you who live above the valley, on the rocky plateau.

You say, “Who can come against us or enter our homes?”

## Jeremiah 21:14

The LORD declares, “‘I will punish you as your deeds deserve. 📖

I will light a fire in your forest that consumes everything around you.’”
