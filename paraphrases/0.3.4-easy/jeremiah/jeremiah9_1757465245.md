# Jeremiah 9 — Tears, lies, and knowing the LORD

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah mourns the deaths of his people.

The LORD challenges deceit and says what truly gives reason to boast.

## Jeremiah 9:1

If only my head were a spring of water and my eyes a fountain of tears! 📖

I would weep day and night for the slain daughter of my people.

**Help with the image**

The daughter pictures the community whose people have been killed.

## Jeremiah 9:2

If only I had a travelers’ lodge in the wilderness! 📖

I would leave my people and go away.

They are all adulterers, a crowd of unfaithful people.

**Help with adulterers**

Adulterers are people who are sexually unfaithful in marriage.

Unfaithfulness also describes the people turning from God.

## Jeremiah 9:3

The LORD declares, “They bend their tongues like bows. 📖

Lies win over truth in the land.

They go from evil to evil and do not take Me into account.

## Jeremiah 9:4

“Everyone must beware of his neighbor and not trust any brother. 📖

Every brother acts deceitfully, and every friend spreads harmful accusations.

## Jeremiah 9:5

“Each betrays his friend, and no one tells the truth. 📖

They have taught their tongues to lie and tire themselves doing wrong.

## Jeremiah 9:6

“You live among deceitful people. 📖

In their deceit, they refuse to know Me,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 9:7

The LORD of Hosts says, “Look! 📖

I will refine and test them.

What else can I do because of the daughter of My people?

**Help with refining**

Refining removes unwanted material from metal.

Here it pictures testing the people.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 9:8

“Their tongues are deadly arrows, speaking deceit. 📖

A man speaks peace to his neighbor with his mouth but lays a trap in his heart.

## Jeremiah 9:9

“Should I not punish them for these things?” declares the LORD. 📖

“Should I not take vengeance for Myself on a nation like this?”

## Jeremiah 9:10

I will weep and cry out for the mountains and sing grief over the wilderness pasture. 📖

They are burned, so no one passes through and no cattle are heard.

Birds and animals have fled and gone away.

## Jeremiah 9:11

“I will make Jerusalem a pile of rubble, a home for jackals. 📖

I will make Judah’s cities deserted, with no one living there.”

**About the speaker**

The LORD announces what He will do to the cities.

## Jeremiah 9:12

Who is wise enough to understand this? 📖

To whom has the LORD’s mouth spoken, so that he can explain it?

Why is the land destroyed and scorched like a desert, with no one able to pass through?

## Jeremiah 9:13

The LORD answered, “They abandoned My law that I placed before them. 📖

They did not follow it or obey My voice.

## Jeremiah 9:14

“Instead, they followed their stubborn hearts and pursued the Baals, as their fathers taught them.” 📖

**Help with Baals**

These are gods worshiped under the name Baal.

## Jeremiah 9:15

The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, “Look! 📖

I will feed these people wormwood and give them poisoned water.

**Help with wormwood**

Wormwood is a bitter plant.

Here it appears in a judgment of suffering.

## Jeremiah 9:16

“I will scatter them among nations neither they nor their fathers knew. 📖

I will send a sword after them until I finish them off.”

## Jeremiah 9:17

The LORD of Hosts says, “Pay attention and call the women who lead mourning. 📖

Send for the most skilled among them.

## Jeremiah 9:18

“Let them come quickly and lead mourning over us. 📖

Let our eyes overflow with tears and our eyelids pour out water.

## Jeremiah 9:19

“A sound of wailing comes from Zion. 📖

‘How ruined we are!

How great our shame is!

We left the land because our homes were torn down.’”

## Jeremiah 9:20

Women, hear the LORD’s word and open your ears to what His mouth says. 📖

Teach your daughters to wail and teach one another to mourn.

## Jeremiah 9:21

Death has climbed through our windows and entered our fortresses. 📖

It removes children from the streets and young men from the public squares.

## Jeremiah 9:22

Announce what the LORD says. 📖

“Men’s dead bodies will fall like dung on an open field.

They will be like newly cut grain behind a harvester, with no one to gather it.”

## Jeremiah 9:23

The LORD says, “The wise man must not boast in his wisdom. 📖

The strong man must not boast in his strength, or the rich man in his wealth.

## Jeremiah 9:24

“Whoever boasts should boast that he understands and knows Me. 📖

I am the LORD, acting with devoted love, justice, and righteousness on earth.

I delight in these things,” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 9:25

The LORD declares, “Look! 📖

The days are coming when I will punish all who are circumcised only in their bodies,

## Jeremiah 9:26

“Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon, Moab, and all desert dwellers who cut the hair at their temples. 📖

All these nations are uncircumcised, and Israel’s whole house is uncircumcised in heart.”

**Help with circumcision**

Circumcision removes the foreskin of the penis and was a sign of Israel’s covenant.

The heart image challenges inward unfaithfulness despite an outward sign.
