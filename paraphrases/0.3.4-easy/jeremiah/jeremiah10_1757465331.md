# Jeremiah 10 — The living God and handmade idols

**About this chapter**

The chapter contrasts the living Creator with idols that people must carry.

It also describes coming exile, grief, and a prayer for correction.

## Jeremiah 10:1

House of Israel, hear the word the LORD speaks to you. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:2

The LORD says, “Do not learn the nations’ ways or fear signs in the heavens. 📖

The nations themselves are terrified by them.

## Jeremiah 10:3

“The peoples’ customs are worthless. 📖

They cut a tree from the forest, and a craftsman shapes it with a chisel.

## Jeremiah 10:4

“They decorate it with silver and gold. 📖

They use hammer and nails to fasten it so that it will not wobble.

## Jeremiah 10:5

“Their idols cannot speak, like scarecrows in a cucumber field. 📖

They must be carried because they cannot walk.

Do not fear them.

They cannot do harm or good.”

**Help with idols**

Idols are objects worshiped as gods.

The passage contrasts their powerlessness with God’s power.

## Jeremiah 10:6

LORD, no one is like You. 📖

You are great, and Your name is mighty in power.

## Jeremiah 10:7

King of nations, who would not fear You? 📖

This is what You deserve.

No one among all the nations’ wise men or in all their kingdoms is like You.

## Jeremiah 10:8

But they are all foolish and without understanding, taught by worthless wooden idols! 📖

## Jeremiah 10:9

Hammered silver comes from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz. 📖

It is a craftsman’s work from a goldsmith’s hands.

The idols’ clothes are blue and purple, all made by skilled workers.

## Jeremiah 10:10

But the LORD is the true God, the living God and eternal King. 📖

The earth shakes at His anger.

The nations cannot bear His fury.

## Jeremiah 10:11

Tell them, “These gods made neither the heavens nor the earth. 📖

They will disappear from the earth and from under these heavens.”

## Jeremiah 10:12

The LORD made the earth by His power and established the world by His wisdom. 📖

He stretched out the heavens by His understanding.

## Jeremiah 10:13

When He thunders, the waters in heaven roar. 📖

He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth.

He makes lightning with the rain and brings wind from His storehouses.

## Jeremiah 10:14

Every man lacks understanding and knowledge. 📖

Every goldsmith is ashamed of his idols.

His cast images are false and have no breath in them.

**Help with cast images**

These are objects made by pouring melted metal into a mold.

## Jeremiah 10:15

They are worthless and deserve mockery. 📖

They will perish when their punishment comes.

## Jeremiah 10:16

The Portion of Jacob is different, because He is the Maker of all things. 📖

Israel is the tribe of His inheritance.

His name is the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with these titles**

The Portion of Jacob describes the LORD as the One belonging to His people.

His inheritance describes Israel as His own people.

LORD of Hosts describes Him as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 10:17

You who live under siege, gather your belongings from this land. 📖

**Help with siege**

A siege is an army surrounding a place to capture it.

## Jeremiah 10:18

The LORD says, “Look! 📖

At this time, I will throw the land’s people out and bring distress so that they may be captured.”

## Jeremiah 10:19

How terrible for me because I am broken! 📖

My wound is severe!

But I said, “This truly is my sickness, and I must bear it.”

## Jeremiah 10:20

My tent is destroyed, and all its ropes are broken. 📖

My sons have left me and are no more.

No one remains to pitch my tent or set up my curtains.

**Help with the tent**

The speaker pictures the ruined community as a destroyed home.

## Jeremiah 10:21

The shepherds have lost understanding and do not seek the LORD. 📖

So they have not prospered, and all their flock is scattered.

**Help with shepherds**

These shepherds picture the people’s leaders.

## Jeremiah 10:22

Listen! 📖

News is coming, a great uproar from the north.

It will make Judah’s cities deserted, a home for jackals.

## Jeremiah 10:23

LORD, I know that a person’s way is not his own. 📖

No one walking directs his own steps.

## Jeremiah 10:24

LORD, correct me, but only with justice. 📖

Do not correct me in Your anger, or You will reduce me to nothing.

## Jeremiah 10:25

Pour Your anger on the nations that do not acknowledge You, on families that do not call Your name. 📖

They have swallowed Jacob up, consumed him, and finished him off.

They have ruined his homeland.

**About the prayer**

The speaker asks for judgment on the nations that destroyed Jacob.

The severe request is retained.
