# Jeremiah 8 — Refusal to return and grief for the people

**About this chapter**

The LORD describes people who refuse to listen and return.

The chapter includes the people’s despair and Jeremiah’s grief.

## Jeremiah 8:1

The LORD declares, “At that time, bones will be taken from their graves. 📖

These are the bones of Judah’s kings, officials, priests, prophets, and Jerusalem’s people.

## Jeremiah 8:2

“They will be exposed to the sun, moon, and all heaven’s army. 📖

These are what the people loved, served, followed, consulted, and worshiped.

Their bones will not be gathered or buried, but will lie like dung on the ground.

**Help with heaven’s army**

Here the phrase refers to heavenly bodies worshiped by the people.

## Jeremiah 8:3

“Wherever I have driven them away, the survivors of this evil family will choose death over life.” 📖

The LORD of Hosts declares this.

**About this despair**

The passage reports the survivors’ terrible distress.

It does not instruct readers to choose death.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 8:4

Tell them what the LORD says. 📖

“Do people fall and not get up?

Does someone turn away and not return?

## Jeremiah 8:5

“Why have these people turned away? 📖

Why does Jerusalem always turn away?

They hold tightly to deceit and refuse to return.

## Jeremiah 8:6

“I have listened and heard, but they do not speak rightly. 📖

No one turns from wickedness and asks, ‘What have I done?’

Everyone follows his own course like a horse charging into battle.

## Jeremiah 8:7

“Even the stork in the sky knows its appointed seasons. 📖

The turtledove, swift, and thrush keep their times of migration.

But My people do not know the LORD’s requirements.

**Help with migration**

These birds travel at particular seasons.

## Jeremiah 8:8

“How can you say, ‘We are wise and have the LORD’s Law’? 📖

The scribes’ lying pen has actually produced deceit.

**Help with scribes**

Scribes wrote and copied documents.

## Jeremiah 8:9

“The wise will be ashamed, distressed, and trapped. 📖

They rejected the LORD’s word, so what wisdom do they really have?

## Jeremiah 8:10

“I will give their wives to other men and their fields to new owners. 📖

From least to greatest, all are greedy for gain.

From prophet to priest, all practice deceit.

## Jeremiah 8:11

“They treat the wound of the daughter of My people with very little care. 📖

They say, ‘Peace, peace,’ when there is no peace at all.

## Jeremiah 8:12

“Are they ashamed of their disgusting acts? 📖

No, they have no shame and do not even know how to blush.

They will fall among the fallen.

When I punish them, they will collapse,” says the LORD.

## Jeremiah 8:13

The LORD declares, “I will take away their harvest. 📖

There will be no grapes on the vine or figs on the tree.

Even the leaves will wither.

Everything I gave them will be lost to them.”

## Jeremiah 8:14

“Why are we sitting here? 📖

Gather together, and let us flee to fortified cities and die there.

The LORD our God has doomed us.

He gave us poisoned water because we sinned against Him.

**About the voice**

The people speak in despair about what has happened to them.

## Jeremiah 8:15

“We hoped for peace, but no good came. 📖

We hoped for healing, but there was only terror.

## Jeremiah 8:16

“The snorting of enemy horses is heard from Dan. 📖

The whole land shakes at the neighing of powerful horses.

They come to consume the land and everything in it, the city and everyone living there.”

## Jeremiah 8:17

The LORD declares, “Look! 📖

I will send snakes among you, vipers that cannot be charmed.

They will bite you.”

**Help with vipers**

Vipers are dangerous snakes.

Charming means trying to control a snake by special actions or sounds.

## Jeremiah 8:18

My sorrow cannot be healed. 📖

My heart grows weak within me.

## Jeremiah 8:19

Listen to the cry of the daughter of my people from a distant land. 📖

“Is the LORD no longer in Zion?

Is its King no longer there?”

God answers, “Why have they angered Me with carved images and worthless foreign idols?”

**About the voices**

The people’s questions are followed by God’s challenge.

The daughter image pictures the community.

## Jeremiah 8:20

“The harvest has passed, and summer has ended, but we have not been saved.” 📖

## Jeremiah 8:21

I am crushed because the daughter of my people is broken. 📖

I mourn, and horror grips me.

## Jeremiah 8:22

Is there no balm in Gilead? 📖

Is there no doctor there?

Why has the daughter of my people not regained health?

**Help with balm**

Balm is a substance used to soothe or treat wounds.

The healing questions express grief over the people’s suffering.
