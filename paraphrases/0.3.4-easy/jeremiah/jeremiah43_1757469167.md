# Jeremiah 43 — The people go to Egypt

**About this chapter**

The leaders reject Jeremiah’s answer and take the remaining people into Egypt.

At Tahpanhes, the LORD gives Jeremiah an action involving stones and announces Babylon’s coming.

## Jeremiah 43:1

Jeremiah finished telling everyone all the LORD their God’s words, everything He sent Jeremiah to say. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:2

Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah, and all the arrogant men told Jeremiah, “You lie! 📖

The LORD our God did not send you to say, ‘You must not live in Egypt.’

## Jeremiah 43:3

“Baruch son of Neriah is stirring you against us to hand us to the Chaldeans. 📖

They will kill us or exile us to Babylon!”

**About this claim**

These are the leaders’ accusations, not the narrator’s explanation of Jeremiah’s message.

## Jeremiah 43:4

Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders disobeyed the LORD’s command to stay in Judah’s land. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:5

Instead, Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders took all Judah’s remaining people. 📖

They included those who returned to Judah from every nation where they were scattered,

## Jeremiah 43:6

the men, women, children, the king’s daughters, and everyone Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, left with Gedaliah. 📖

Gedaliah was the son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan.

They also took Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah.

## Jeremiah 43:7

They entered Egypt because they disobeyed the LORD’s voice. 📖

They went as far as Tahpanhes.

## Jeremiah 43:8

The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah at Tahpanhes. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:9

“Before the Jews’ eyes, take large stones and bury them in the clay of the brick pavement. 📖

Do this at the entrance to Pharaoh’s palace in Tahpanhes.

**Help with Pharaoh**

Pharaoh is a title for Egypt’s king.

## Jeremiah 43:10

“Tell them what the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says. 📖

‘I will send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.

I will set his throne over these stones I buried, and he will spread his royal tent over them.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 43:11

“‘He will come and strike Egypt’s land. 📖

He will bring death to those destined for death, captivity to those for captivity, and swords to those for swords.

## Jeremiah 43:12

“‘I will light a fire in Egypt’s gods’ temples. 📖

Nebuchadnezzar will burn them and take their gods captive.

He will wrap himself with Egypt’s land like a shepherd wrapping himself in clothing.

He will leave unharmed.

## Jeremiah 43:13

“‘He will destroy the sacred pillars of Egypt’s sun temple and burn Egypt’s gods’ temples.’” 📖
