# Jeremiah 38 — Jeremiah is rescued from the mud

**About this chapter**

Officials put Jeremiah in a water storage pit.

Ebed-melech rescues him, and Jeremiah gives Zedekiah another warning about surrender.

## Jeremiah 38:1

Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Jucal son of Shelemiah, and Pashhur son of Malchijah heard Jeremiah’s message. 📖

He was telling all the people,

## Jeremiah 38:2

“The LORD says, ‘Anyone staying in this city will die by sword, famine, and plague. 📖

Anyone surrendering to the Chaldeans will live.

He will keep his life like goods gained in war and survive.

## Jeremiah 38:3

“‘The LORD says, “This city will surely be handed to Babylon’s king’s army, and he will capture it.”’” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:4

The officials told the king, “This man should die. 📖

His words discourage the warriors still in the city and all the people.

He seeks their ruin, not their well-being.”

## Jeremiah 38:5

King Zedekiah replied, “He is in your hands. 📖

The king can do nothing to stop you.”

## Jeremiah 38:6

They dropped Jeremiah into the water storage pit of Malchiah, the king’s son, in the guard’s courtyard. 📖

They lowered him with ropes.

It had no water, only mud, and Jeremiah sank into it.

## Jeremiah 38:7

Ebed-melech the Cushite, a palace court official, heard that Jeremiah was in the pit. 📖

The king was sitting at Benjamin’s Gate.

## Jeremiah 38:8

Ebed-melech left the king’s palace and spoke to the king. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:9

“My lord the king, these men acted wickedly in everything they did to Jeremiah the prophet. 📖

They dropped him into the pit, where he will starve because the city has no more bread.”

## Jeremiah 38:10

The king commanded Ebed-melech the Cushite, “Take thirty men from here. 📖

Pull Jeremiah the prophet from the pit before he dies.”

## Jeremiah 38:11

Ebed-melech took the men to a place below the king’s palace storehouse. 📖

He took old rags and worn clothes and lowered them by ropes to Jeremiah in the pit.

## Jeremiah 38:12

Ebed-melech the Cushite called to Jeremiah, “Put the old rags and clothes under your arms to pad the ropes.” 📖

Jeremiah did so,

## Jeremiah 38:13

and they pulled him up with ropes and lifted him from the pit. 📖

Jeremiah stayed in the guard’s courtyard.

## Jeremiah 38:14

King Zedekiah brought Jeremiah the prophet to the LORD’s house’s third entrance. 📖

He said, “I will ask you something.

Hide nothing from me.”

## Jeremiah 38:15

Jeremiah replied, “If I tell you, you will surely kill me. 📖

Even if I advise you, you will not listen.”

## Jeremiah 38:16

King Zedekiah secretly swore to Jeremiah, “As surely as the LORD lives, who gave us life, I promise this. 📖

I will not kill you or hand you to these men seeking your life.”

## Jeremiah 38:17

Jeremiah told Zedekiah, “The LORD God of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. 📖

‘If you truly surrender to Babylon’s king’s officers, you will live.

The city will not be burned, and you and your household will survive.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 38:18

“‘But if you do not surrender to Babylon’s king’s officers, this city will be handed to the Chaldeans. 📖

They will burn it, and you will not escape them.’”

## Jeremiah 38:19

King Zedekiah told Jeremiah, “I fear the Jews who deserted to the Chaldeans. 📖

The Chaldeans may hand me to them to be abused.”

## Jeremiah 38:20

Jeremiah replied, “They will not hand you over. 📖

Obey the LORD’s voice in what I tell you, so that things go well and you live.

## Jeremiah 38:21

“But if you refuse to surrender, the LORD has shown me this. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:22

“All the women left in Judah’s king’s palace will be brought to Babylon’s king’s officials. 📖

They will say, ‘Your trusted friends misled you and overcame you.

Your feet sank into mud, and they abandoned you.’

## Jeremiah 38:23

“All your wives and children will be brought to the Chaldeans. 📖

You will not escape, because Babylon’s king will seize you and this city will burn.”

## Jeremiah 38:24

Zedekiah warned Jeremiah, “Let no one know this conversation, or you will die. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:25

“Officials may hear that I spoke with you and come demanding, ‘Tell us what you and the king said. 📖

Hide nothing, or we will kill you.’

## Jeremiah 38:26

“Then tell them, ‘I asked the king not to return me to Jonathan’s house to die there.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:27

All the officials questioned Jeremiah. 📖

He told them exactly what the king commanded.

They said no more, because no one overheard the conversation.

## Jeremiah 38:28

Jeremiah stayed in the guard’s courtyard until Jerusalem was captured. 📖
