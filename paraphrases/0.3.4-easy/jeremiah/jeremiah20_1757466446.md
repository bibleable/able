# Jeremiah 20 — Jeremiah is punished and cries out

**About this chapter**

Pashhur punishes Jeremiah, who then announces judgment against him.

Jeremiah’s words move between accusation, trust, praise, and deep despair.

## Jeremiah 20:1

Pashhur son of Immer was a priest and chief official in the LORD’s house. 📖

He heard Jeremiah prophesying these things.

## Jeremiah 20:2

He had Jeremiah the prophet beaten and put in stocks at Benjamin’s Upper Gate beside the LORD’s house. 📖

**Help with stocks**

Stocks were a device used to hold a prisoner in place.

## Jeremiah 20:3

The next day, Pashhur released Jeremiah from the stocks. 📖

Jeremiah told him, “The LORD calls you Magor-missabib, not Pashhur.

**Help with Magor-missabib**

This name means “Terror on every side.”

## Jeremiah 20:4

“The LORD says, ‘I will make you a terror to yourself and all your friends. 📖

They will fall by enemies’ swords before your own eyes.

I will hand Judah to Babylon’s king.

He will take them to Babylon and put them to the sword.

## Jeremiah 20:5

“‘I will give all this city’s wealth to their enemies, all its products, valuables, and Judah’s kings’ treasures. 📖

They will rob, seize, and carry them to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 20:6

“‘Pashhur, you and everyone in your house will go into captivity. 📖

You will go to Babylon, die there, and be buried there.

So will all your friends to whom you spoke these lying prophecies.’”

## Jeremiah 20:7

LORD, You deceived me, and I was deceived. 📖

You overpowered me and won.

I am laughed at all day, and everyone mocks me.

**About the voice**

Jeremiah speaks to God.

His accusation is retained as part of his lament.

## Jeremiah 20:8

Whenever I speak, I cry out and announce violence and destruction. 📖

The LORD’s word brings me insults and mockery all day.

## Jeremiah 20:9

If I say, “I will not mention Him or speak in His name anymore,” His message becomes fire. 📖

It burns in my heart, shut inside my bones.

I grow tired of holding it in and cannot succeed.

## Jeremiah 20:10

I hear many whispering, “Terror on every side! 📖

Report him!

Let us report him!”

All my trusted friends watch for my fall.

They say, “Perhaps he will be deceived so that we can overcome him and take vengeance.”

## Jeremiah 20:11

But the LORD is with me like a terrifying warrior. 📖

So those pursuing me will stumble and fail.

Their failure will leave them completely ashamed, with everlasting disgrace that will never be forgotten.

## Jeremiah 20:12

LORD of Hosts, You examine the righteous and see hearts and minds. 📖

Let me see Your vengeance on them, because I have entrusted my case to You.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 20:13

Sing to the LORD! 📖

Praise the LORD!

He rescues needy people’s lives from evildoers’ hands.

## Jeremiah 20:14

May the day I was born be cursed! 📖

May the day my mother bore me never be blessed.

## Jeremiah 20:15

May the man who brought my father this news be cursed! 📖

He said, “A son has been born to you,” bringing my father great joy.

## Jeremiah 20:16

May that man become like the cities the LORD overthrew without compassion. 📖

May he hear an outcry in the morning and a battle cry at noon,

## Jeremiah 20:17

because he did not kill me in the womb. 📖

My mother could have been my grave, and her womb enlarged forever.

**About this severe lament**

Jeremiah wishes he had died before birth and curses the person who announced it.

These words express his despair; they do not instruct readers to harm anyone.

## Jeremiah 20:18

Why did I come from the womb to see only trouble and sorrow and end my days in shame? 📖
