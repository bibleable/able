# Jeremiah 30 — Jacob’s distress and restoration

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Jeremiah to record His words about Israel and Judah.

He promises rescue and restoration without removing the warning of just discipline.

## Jeremiah 30:1

This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:2

The LORD, Israel’s God, says, “Write all the words I have spoken to you in a book. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:3

The LORD declares, “The days are coming when I will restore My people Israel and Judah from captivity. 📖

I will bring them to the land I gave their fathers, and they will possess it.”

## Jeremiah 30:4

These are the LORD’s words about Israel and Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:5

The LORD says, “A cry of panic is heard, a cry of terror instead of peace. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:6

“Ask and see, can a male give birth? 📖

Why do I see every man holding his stomach like a woman in labor, every face pale?

## Jeremiah 30:7

“How terrible that day will be! 📖

None will be like it!

It is Jacob’s time of distress, but he will be saved from it.

## Jeremiah 30:8

The LORD of Hosts declares, “On that day, I will break their necks’ yoke and tear off their bonds. 📖

Strangers will no longer enslave them.

**Help with yoke and LORD of Hosts**

A yoke is a bar used on working animals.

Here it pictures a ruler’s control.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 30:9

“They will serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:10

The LORD declares, “Jacob My servant, do not fear. 📖

Israel, do not be distressed.

I will save you from far away and your descendants from the land of captivity.

Jacob will return to quiet and ease, with no one to frighten him.

## Jeremiah 30:11

The LORD declares, “I am with you to save you. 📖

I will completely destroy the nations where I scattered you, but I will not completely destroy you.

Yet I will discipline you justly and will certainly not leave you unpunished.”

## Jeremiah 30:12

The LORD says, “Your injury cannot be cured, and your wound is severe. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:13

“No one pleads your case. 📖

There is no remedy for your sores and no recovery for you.

## Jeremiah 30:14

“All your lovers forgot you and no longer seek you. 📖

I struck you like an enemy, with a cruel person’s discipline.

Your wrongdoing is great, and your sins are many.

## Jeremiah 30:15

“Why cry over your wound? 📖

Your pain has no cure!

I did these things because of your great wrongdoing and many sins.

## Jeremiah 30:16

“Yet all who consume you will be consumed, and every enemy will go into exile. 📖

Those who rob you will be robbed, and those who raid you will be raided.

## Jeremiah 30:17

The LORD declares, “But I will restore your health and heal your wounds. 📖

They call you an outcast, Zion, whom no one cares for.”

## Jeremiah 30:18

The LORD says, “I will restore Jacob’s tents and show compassion to his homes. 📖

The city will be rebuilt on its own ruins, and the palace will stand in its rightful place.

## Jeremiah 30:19

“Thanksgiving and celebration will come from them. 📖

I will multiply them, not decrease them, and honor them so that they will not be belittled.

## Jeremiah 30:20

“Their children will be as in old days, and their gathering will be established before Me. 📖

I will punish all who oppress them.

## Jeremiah 30:21

“Their leader will be one of their own, their ruler from among them. 📖

I will bring him near, and he will approach Me.

Who would dare approach Me on his own?” declares the LORD.

## Jeremiah 30:22

“You will be My people, and I will be your God.” 📖

## Jeremiah 30:23

Look! 📖

The LORD’s storm has gone out in fury, a whirlwind swirling down on wicked people’s heads.

## Jeremiah 30:24

The LORD’s fierce anger will not turn back until He fully carries out His heart’s purposes. 📖

In coming days, you will understand this.
