# Jeremiah 27 — The yoke of Babylon

**About this chapter**

The LORD uses a yoke to show the nations’ submission to Babylon.

Jeremiah challenges false promises about freedom and the temple’s objects.

## Jeremiah 27:1

This word came from the LORD to Jeremiah at the beginning of Zedekiah’s rule. 📖

Zedekiah son of Josiah was king of Judah.

## Jeremiah 27:2

The LORD told me, “Make a yoke with leather straps and put it on your neck. 📖

**Help with yoke**

A yoke is a bar placed on working animals.

Here it pictures submission to a ruler.

## Jeremiah 27:3

“Send word to Edom’s, Moab’s, Ammon’s, Tyre’s, and Sidon’s kings. 📖

Send it through the messengers who came to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah.

## Jeremiah 27:4

“Give them this message from the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, to take to their masters. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Jeremiah 27:5

“‘By My great power and outstretched arm, I made earth and its people and animals. 📖

I give it to whoever pleases Me.

## Jeremiah 27:6

“‘I have now put all these lands under My servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. 📖

I have even put the field’s animals under his control.

## Jeremiah 27:7

“‘All nations will serve him, his son, and his grandson until his own land’s time comes. 📖

Then many nations and great kings will enslave him.

## Jeremiah 27:8

The LORD declares, “‘A nation may refuse to serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon or put its neck under his yoke. 📖

I will punish that nation with sword, famine, and plague until I destroy it by his hand.

**Help with plague**

A plague is a widespread, deadly disease.

## Jeremiah 27:9

“‘Do not listen to your prophets, fortune-tellers, dream interpreters, mediums, or sorcerers. 📖

They say, “You will not serve Babylon’s king.”

**Help with these roles**

Mediums claim to contact spirits or the dead.

Sorcerers practice magic.

## Jeremiah 27:10

“‘Their prophecy is a lie that will remove you from your land. 📖

I will drive you away, and you will die.

## Jeremiah 27:11

The LORD declares, “‘But I will leave the nation that submits to Babylon’s king and serves him in its land. 📖

It will farm and live there.’”

## Jeremiah 27:12

I gave Zedekiah king of Judah the same message. 📖

“Put your necks under Babylon’s king’s yoke.

Serve him and his people, and live!

## Jeremiah 27:13

“Why should you and your people die by sword, famine, and plague? 📖

The LORD decreed this against any nation refusing to serve Babylon’s king.

## Jeremiah 27:14

“Do not listen to prophets saying, ‘You must not serve Babylon’s king.’ 📖

Their prophecy is a lie.

## Jeremiah 27:15

The LORD declares, “‘I did not send them, yet they prophesy falsely in My name. 📖

So I will drive you away.

You and the prophets speaking to you will die.’”

## Jeremiah 27:16

I told the priests and all the people, “The LORD says, ‘Do not listen to your prophets. 📖

They say, “The LORD’s house’s objects will soon come back from Babylon.”

Their prophecy is a lie.

## Jeremiah 27:17

“‘Do not listen. 📖

Serve Babylon’s king and live!

Why should this city become ruins?

## Jeremiah 27:18

“‘If they are prophets and have the LORD’s word, let them plead with the LORD of Hosts. 📖

Let them ask that the remaining objects not be taken to Babylon.

These objects are in the LORD’s house, Judah’s king’s palace, and Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 27:19

“‘The LORD of Hosts speaks about the pillars, the sea, the bases, and the city’s other remaining objects. 📖

**Help with sea**

Here the sea names the large water basin used at the temple.

## Jeremiah 27:20

“‘Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take them when he exiled Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah. 📖

He took Jeconiah and all Judah’s and Jerusalem’s nobles from Jerusalem to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 27:21

“‘The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, speaks about objects remaining in His house, Judah’s king’s palace, and Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:22

The LORD declares, “‘They will be taken to Babylon and stay until I attend to them again. 📖

Then I will bring them back and restore them here.’”
