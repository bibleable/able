# Jonah 4 — Jonah’s anger and God’s care

**About this chapter**

Jonah is angry that Nineveh was spared.

The LORD questions Jonah about a plant and the city’s people.

## Jonah 4:1

Jonah was very displeased and became angry. 📖

## Jonah 4:2

Jonah prayed, “LORD, is this not what I said while I was still in my own country? 📖

This is why I hurried to run towards Tarshish.

I knew you are a kind and compassionate God.

You are slow to anger and full of faithful love.

You turn from sending disaster.

**Help with compassionate:** Compassionate means caring about suffering and wanting to help.

## Jonah 4:3

LORD, please take my life now. 📖

It is better for me to die than to live.”

## Jonah 4:4

The LORD answered, “Do you have any right to be angry?” 📖

## Jonah 4:5

Jonah left the city and sat east of it. 📖

He made a shelter and sat in its shade.

He wanted to see what would happen to the city.

## Jonah 4:6

The LORD God appointed a vine to grow over Jonah’s head and give him shade. 📖

This eased Jonah’s discomfort.

Jonah was very pleased with the plant.

**Help with vine:** A vine is a climbing or spreading plant.

## Jonah 4:7

At dawn the next day, God appointed a worm. 📖

The worm attacked the plant, and it dried up.

## Jonah 4:8

As the sun rose, God appointed a burning hot east wind. 📖

The sun beat down on Jonah’s head, and he felt faint.

Jonah wished to die.

He said, “It is better for me to die than to live.”

## Jonah 4:9

God asked Jonah, “Do you have any right to be angry about the plant?” 📖

Jonah answered, “I do!

I am angry enough to die!”

## Jonah 4:10

The LORD said, “You cared about the plant, although you did not tend it or make it grow. 📖

It grew up in a night and died in a night.

## Jonah 4:11

Should I not care about the great city of Nineveh? 📖

It has more than 120,000 people who cannot tell their right hand from their left.

It has many cattle too.”

**About right and left:** This difficult description is kept from the source.

Its meaning needs further editorial discussion.

The draft does not identify these people as a particular age or disability group.
