# Jonah 3 — Nineveh turns from wrongdoing

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends Jonah to Nineveh again.

The people hear the warning and change their actions.

## Jonah 3:1

The LORD’s word came to Jonah a 2nd time. 📖

## Jonah 3:2

The LORD said, “Get up! 📖

Go to the great city of Nineveh.

Tell it the message I give you.”

## Jonah 3:3

This time, Jonah got up and went to Nineveh, as the LORD had said. 📖

Nineveh was a very great city, requiring a journey of 3 days.

## Jonah 3:4

On the first day of his journey into the city, Jonah announced his message. 📖

He said, “40 more days, and Nineveh will be overthrown!”

## Jonah 3:5

The people of Nineveh believed God. 📖

They announced a fast.

Everyone, from the greatest to the least, put on sackcloth.

**Help with fast:** A fast is a time of not eating.

**Help with sackcloth:** Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief or sorrow for wrongdoing.

## Jonah 3:6

The king of Nineveh heard the message. 📖

He got up from his throne and took off his royal robe.

He put on sackcloth and sat in ashes.

**Help with sackcloth and ashes:** These were signs of grief or sorrow for wrongdoing.

## Jonah 3:7

The king sent out this announcement in Nineveh. 📖

“The king and his nobles order this.

No person or animal, herd or flock, may taste anything.

They must not eat or drink.

**Help with nobles:** Nobles are important leaders.

## Jonah 3:8

Let people and animals be covered with sackcloth. 📖

Let everyone call to God with all their heart.

Let everyone turn from their evil ways and the violence they do.

**Help with sackcloth:** This rough cloth was a sign of grief or sorrow for wrongdoing.

## Jonah 3:9

Who knows? 📖

God may turn and decide not to send the disaster.

God may turn from his fierce anger, so that we will not die.”

## Jonah 3:10

God saw what they did. 📖

They had turned from their evil ways.

God decided not to bring the disaster he had threatened.
