# Jonah 2 — Jonah prays inside the fish

**About this chapter**

Jonah prays to the LORD from inside the fish.

His prayer uses pictures of drowning and rescue.

## Jonah 2:1

From inside the fish, Jonah prayed to the LORD his God. 📖

## Jonah 2:2

Jonah said, “In my distress, I called to the LORD, and he answered me. 📖

From the belly of Sheol, I called for help.

You heard my voice.

**Help with Sheol:** Sheol refers to the place of the dead.

Jonah uses this picture to describe being close to death.

## Jonah 2:3

You threw me into the deep water, into the heart of the seas. 📖

The current swirled around me.

All your breaking waves swept over me.

**Help with heart of the seas:** This means deep within the sea.

## Jonah 2:4

I said, ‘I have been driven from your sight. 📖

Yet I will look again towards your holy temple.’

**Help with holy temple:** A temple is a place of worship set apart for God.

## Jonah 2:5

The waters covered me and threatened my life. 📖

The deep water closed around me.

Seaweed wrapped around my head.

## Jonah 2:6

I went down to the roots of the mountains. 📖

The earth below shut me in forever!

But you brought my life up from the pit, LORD my God!

**Help with these pictures:** The roots of the mountains means their deepest parts.

The pit pictures death, from which Jonah says God rescued him.

## Jonah 2:7

As my life faded away, I remembered the LORD. 📖

My prayer went up to you, to your holy temple.

**Help with holy temple:** A temple is a place of worship set apart for God.

## Jonah 2:8

Those who hold on to worthless idols turn away from his faithful love. 📖

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## Jonah 2:9

But I will offer a sacrifice to you with a voice of thanks. 📖

I will do what I have promised.

Salvation comes from the LORD!”

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

**Help with salvation:** Salvation means being saved.

## Jonah 2:10

The LORD commanded the fish. 📖

The fish vomited Jonah onto dry land.
