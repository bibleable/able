# Jonah 1 — Jonah runs away

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends Jonah to Nineveh.

Jonah boards a ship going to Tarshish instead.

## Jonah 1:1

The LORD’s word came to Jonah son of Amittai. 📖

The LORD said,

## Jonah 1:2

“Get up! 📖

Go to the great city of Nineveh and speak against it.

Its wickedness has come before me.”

**Help with wickedness:** Wickedness means serious wrongdoing.

## Jonah 1:3

But Jonah got up to run to Tarshish, away from the LORD’s presence. 📖

Jonah went down to Joppa and found a ship sailing to Tarshish.

Jonah paid the fare and got aboard.

He sailed towards Tarshish, away from the LORD’s presence.

**Help with fare:** A fare is the money paid for a journey.

## Jonah 1:4

The LORD sent a great wind over the sea. 📖

A violent storm rose, and the ship was in danger of breaking apart.

## Jonah 1:5

The sailors were afraid, and each cried out to his own god. 📖

They threw the ship’s goods into the sea to make the load lighter.

But Jonah had gone to the lowest part of the ship.

He lay there, deeply asleep.

## Jonah 1:6

The captain came to Jonah and said, “How can you sleep? 📖

Get up and call to your God.

Perhaps this God will care about us, so that we will not die.”

## Jonah 1:7

The sailors said to each other, “Come, let us cast lots. 📖

We will find out who is responsible for this disaster.”

They cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.

**Help with cast lots:** People used marked objects to make a choice or seek an answer.

## Jonah 1:8

The sailors demanded, “Tell us who is to blame for this disaster! 📖

What work do you do?

Where have you come from?

What is your country?

Who are your people?”

## Jonah 1:9

Jonah answered, “I am a Hebrew. 📖

I worship the LORD, the God of the heavens.

He made the sea and the dry land.”

**Help with Hebrew:** Here, Hebrew identifies Jonah as one of Israel’s people.

## Jonah 1:10

The men became even more afraid. 📖

They asked Jonah, “What have you done?”

Jonah had told them he was running away from the LORD’s presence.

So they knew about this.

## Jonah 1:11

The sea grew worse and worse. 📖

The sailors asked Jonah, “What must we do to you to make the sea calm for us?”

## Jonah 1:12

Jonah answered, “Pick me up and throw me into the sea. 📖

Then it will become calm for you.

I know I am to blame for this violent storm that has come upon you.”

## Jonah 1:13

Instead, the men rowed hard to reach dry land. 📖

But they could not do it.

The sea raged against them more and more.

## Jonah 1:14

They cried to the LORD, “Please, LORD, do not let us die because of this man’s life! 📖

Do not hold us guilty of shedding innocent blood!

You, LORD, have done what pleased you.”

**Help with innocent blood:** This means the death of a person who should not be killed.

## Jonah 1:15

They picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea. 📖

The raging sea became calm.

## Jonah 1:16

The men feared the LORD greatly. 📖

They offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made vows to him.

**Help with sacrifice:** A sacrifice is an offering to God.

**Help with vows:** Vows are serious promises.

## Jonah 1:17

The LORD had appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah. 📖

Jonah spent 3 days and 3 nights inside the fish’s belly.
