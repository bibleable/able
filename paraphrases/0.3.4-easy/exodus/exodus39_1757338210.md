# Exodus 39 — The garments and the finished work

**About this chapter**

The workers make the priests’ garments.

The gemstone order and all the attachments are described.

They bring the completed work to Moses, who inspects it and blesses them.

## Exodus 39:1

They made woven service garments for the sanctuary from blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 📖

They made Aaron’s holy garments, as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Exodus 39:2

Bezalel made the ephod of finely spun linen, embroidered with gold and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 📖

**Help with “ephod”:** This is a priest’s special garment.

## Exodus 39:3

They hammered gold into thin sheets and cut threads from them. 📖

A skilled worker wove them with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen.

## Exodus 39:4

They attached shoulder pieces at two ephod corners so it could be fastened. 📖

## Exodus 39:5

Its skillfully woven waistband was one piece with it, of the same workmanship. 📖

It used gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen, as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Exodus 39:6

They mounted onyx stones in gold filigree settings, engraved like seals with Israel’s sons’ names. 📖

**Help with these words:** Onyx is a valuable stone.

Filigree is delicate decorative metalwork.

Engraving cuts a design or writing into a surface.

## Exodus 39:7

They fastened them on the ephod’s shoulders as memorial stones for Israel’s sons, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:8

He made the breastpiece with the ephod’s workmanship. 📖

It used gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen.

## Exodus 39:9

Folded double, it was square, one span long and one span wide. 📖

**Help with “span”:** This is a length based on a spread hand, from thumb to little finger.

## Exodus 39:10

They mounted four gemstone rows. 📖

The first had ruby, topaz, and emerald;

## Exodus 39:11

The second turquoise, sapphire, and diamond; 📖

## Exodus 39:12

The third jacinth, agate, and amethyst; 📖

## Exodus 39:13

The fourth beryl, onyx, and jasper. 📖

All were mounted in gold filigree settings.

## Exodus 39:14

The twelve stones matched Israel’s sons’ names. 📖

Each was engraved like a seal with a tribe’s name, for all twelve tribes.

## Exodus 39:15

They made pure gold chains for the breastpiece, braided like cords. 📖

## Exodus 39:16

They made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings. 📖

They fixed the rings at the breastpiece’s two corners,

## Exodus 39:17

and attached the two gold chains to those rings. 📖

## Exodus 39:18

They attached the chains’ other ends to the two filigree settings on the ephod’s front shoulder pieces. 📖

## Exodus 39:19

They made two more gold rings for the breastpiece’s other two corners. 📖

They were on its inner edge beside the ephod.

## Exodus 39:20

They made two additional gold rings for the bottom of the ephod’s front shoulder pieces. 📖

They placed them near the seam, just above the woven waistband.

## Exodus 39:21

They tied the breastpiece’s rings to the ephod’s rings with blue yarn cord. 📖

It sat above the waistband without swinging away from the ephod, as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Exodus 39:22

They wove the ephod’s robe entirely of blue cloth, 📖

## Exodus 39:23

with an opening in the center like a garment’s opening. 📖

A collar around it prevented tearing.

## Exodus 39:24

They made pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen on the robe’s lower hem. 📖

**Help with “pomegranates”:** These are round fruits represented here in the design.

## Exodus 39:25

They made pure gold bells around the hem between the pomegranates, 📖

## Exodus 39:26

alternating bells and pomegranates on the robe for service, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:27

They wove fine linen tunics for Aaron and his sons, 📖

## Exodus 39:28

They made a fine linen turban. 📖

They made ornate headbands and undergarments of finely spun linen,

**Help with these garments:** A turban wraps around the head.

Ornate means richly decorated.

## Exodus 39:29

and a fine linen sash embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:30

They made the holy crown’s plate of pure gold. 📖

They engraved it like a seal with HOLY TO THE LORD.

## Exodus 39:31

They attached a blue cord to fasten it on the turban, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:32

All the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting, was finished. 📖

Israel did everything as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Exodus 39:33

They brought Moses the tabernacle: the tent, all its furnishings, clasps, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases; 📖

## Exodus 39:34

The red-dyed ram-skin covering, fine leather covering, and shielding veil; 📖

## Exodus 39:35

The ark of the Testimony, its poles, and mercy seat; 📖

## Exodus 39:36

The table, all its tools, and Bread of the Presence; 📖

## Exodus 39:37

The pure gold lampstand, its row of lamps and all its tools, and the lighting oil; 📖

## Exodus 39:38

The gold altar, anointing oil, fragrant incense, and tent entrance curtain; 📖

## Exodus 39:39

The bronze altar, bronze grate, poles, and all its tools, and the washing basin and stand; 📖

## Exodus 39:40

The courtyard curtains, posts, bases, and gate curtain, with ropes and tent pegs. 📖

They brought all equipment for serving in the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting;

## Exodus 39:41

The woven sanctuary service garments, Aaron’s holy priestly garments, and his sons’ garments for serving as priests. 📖

## Exodus 39:42

Israel did all the work as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:43

Moses inspected everything and saw they did it as the LORD commanded. 📖

He blessed them.
