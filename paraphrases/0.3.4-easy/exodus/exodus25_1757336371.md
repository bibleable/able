# Exodus 25 — Offerings and furnishings for God’s sanctuary

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses what offerings to receive.

He gives the pattern for the ark, table, and lampstand.

Measurements use the units found in BSB, with labeled explanations.

## Exodus 25:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 25:2

“Tell Israel to bring Me an offering. 📖

Receive it from everyone whose heart moves him to give.

## Exodus 25:3

Accept gold, silver, and bronze; 📖

## Exodus 25:4

blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; fine linen and goat hair; 📖

**Help with these words:** Scarlet means bright red.

Linen is cloth made from flax.

## Exodus 25:5

red-dyed ram skins, fine leather, and acacia wood; 📖

**Help with these words:** A ram is a male sheep.

Acacia is a kind of tree.

## Exodus 25:6

olive oil for lighting, and spices for anointing oil and fragrant incense; 📖

**Help with these words:** Anointing means applying oil to set someone or something apart.

Incense gives a pleasant smell when burned.

## Exodus 25:7

onyx stones and gemstones for the ephod and breastpiece. 📖

**Help with these words:** Onyx is a valuable stone.

An ephod is a priest’s special garment.

A breastpiece is worn over the chest.

## Exodus 25:8

They must make Me a sanctuary, so I may live among them. 📖

**Help with “sanctuary”:** This is a holy place for God’s presence and worship.

## Exodus 25:9

Make the tabernacle and all its furnishings according to the pattern I show you. 📖

**Help with “tabernacle”:** This is the tent used as God’s sanctuary.

## Exodus 25:10

Make an ark of acacia wood. 📖

It must be two and a half cubits long, one and a half wide, and one and a half high.

**Help with these words:** The ark is a special chest.

A cubit is a length based on the distance from elbow to fingertip.

## Exodus 25:11

Cover its inside and outside with pure gold. 📖

Make a gold molding around it.

**Help with “molding”:** This is a shaped border.

## Exodus 25:12

Cast four gold rings and attach them to its four feet. 📖

Put two rings on each side.

**Help with “cast”:** This means shape metal by pouring it into a mold.

## Exodus 25:13

Make acacia poles and cover them with gold. 📖

## Exodus 25:14

Put them through the ark’s side rings to carry it. 📖

## Exodus 25:15

The poles must stay in the rings. 📖

Do not remove them.

## Exodus 25:16

Put the Testimony I give you inside the ark. 📖

**Help with “Testimony”:** This refers to God’s covenant witness given to Israel.

## Exodus 25:17

Make a pure gold mercy seat. 📖

It must be two and a half cubits long and one and a half wide.

**Help with “mercy seat”:** This is the special cover over the ark.

## Exodus 25:18

Make two cherubim of hammered gold at the mercy seat’s ends, 📖

**Help with “cherubim”:** These are winged beings represented here by gold figures.

## Exodus 25:19

one at each end. 📖

Make them all from one piece of gold.

## Exodus 25:20

Their wings must spread upward over the mercy seat. 📖

They must face each other, looking toward the mercy seat.

## Exodus 25:21

Place the mercy seat on the ark. 📖

Put the Testimony I give you inside.

## Exodus 25:22

I will meet you above the mercy seat, between the two cherubim over the ark of the Testimony. 📖

I will tell you all My commands for Israel.

## Exodus 25:23

Make an acacia table two cubits long, one wide, and one and a half high. 📖

## Exodus 25:24

Cover it with pure gold and make a gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 25:25

Make a rim a handbreadth wide around it, with a gold molding on the rim. 📖

**Help with “handbreadth”:** This is a length based on a hand’s width.

## Exodus 25:26

Make four gold rings and attach them at the four corners beside the four legs. 📖

## Exodus 25:27

Put the rings close to the rim to hold the table’s carrying poles. 📖

## Exodus 25:28

Make acacia poles covered with gold to carry the table. 📖

## Exodus 25:29

Make its plates, dishes, pitchers, and bowls of pure gold. 📖

The pitchers and bowls are for pouring drink offerings.

**Help with “drink offerings”:** These are liquids poured out in worship.

## Exodus 25:30

Keep the Bread of the Presence on the table before Me always. 📖

**Help with this name:** This is bread placed before God in the sanctuary.

## Exodus 25:31

Make a lampstand from pure hammered gold, all one piece. 📖

This includes its base, shaft, cups, buds, and petals.

**Help with these words:** The shaft is the upright part.

The cups, buds, and petals are shaped like parts of flowers.

## Exodus 25:32

Six branches must extend from its sides, three on each side. 📖

## Exodus 25:33

Put three almond-blossom cups with buds and petals on the first branch. 📖

Put three on the next, and do the same on all six branches.

## Exodus 25:34

The lampstand itself must have four almond-blossom cups with buds and petals. 📖

## Exodus 25:35

Put one bud under each pair of branches: the first, second, and third pairs. 📖

## Exodus 25:36

The buds, branches, and lampstand must be one piece, hammered from pure gold. 📖

## Exodus 25:37

Make seven lamps and place them on the stand to light the area in front. 📖

## Exodus 25:38

Make the wick trimmers and trays of pure gold. 📖

**Help with “wick trimmers”:** These are tools for cutting the wicks that burn in the lamps.

## Exodus 25:39

Use one talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all these utensils. 📖

**Help with “talent”:** This is a unit of weight.

## Exodus 25:40

Make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.” 📖
