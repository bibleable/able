# Exodus 4 — Signs and the return to Egypt

**About this chapter**

God gives Moses signs and Aaron as a spokesman.

Moses returns toward Egypt.

The hardening of Pharaoh’s heart, threatened deaths, and circumcision incident are retained.

Uncertain details are not filled in.

## Exodus 4:1

Moses answered, “What if they do not believe or listen to me? 📖

They may say, ‘The LORD did not appear to you.’”

## Exodus 4:2

The LORD asked, “What is in your hand?” 📖

Moses replied, “A staff.”

**Help with “staff”:** This is a long stick.

## Exodus 4:3

The LORD said, “Throw it down.” 📖

Moses threw it on the ground, and it became a snake.

He ran from it.

## Exodus 4:4

The LORD said, “Reach out and take its tail.” 📖

Moses caught it, and it became a staff in his hand.

## Exodus 4:5

The LORD said, “This may make them believe I appeared to you. 📖

I am the LORD, their fathers’ God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”

## Exodus 4:6

The LORD told Moses, “Put your hand inside your cloak.” 📖

He did, and when he took it out, it was leprous, white as snow.

**Help with “leprous”:** This is BSB’s word for the skin condition described here.

It is not identified here with a modern medical diagnosis.

## Exodus 4:7

The LORD said, “Put it inside your cloak again.” 📖

Moses did, and when he took it out, it was restored like his other skin.

## Exodus 4:8

The LORD said, “If they do not believe you or the first sign’s witness, they may believe the second. 📖

## Exodus 4:9

If they do not believe either sign or listen to you, take Nile water and pour it on dry ground. 📖

That water will become blood on the ground.”

## Exodus 4:10

Moses said, “Please, Lord, I have never spoken well, before or since You spoke to Your servant. 📖

I am slow of speech and tongue.”

**About Moses’ words:** He describes difficulty speaking.

The source gives no diagnosis.

## Exodus 4:11

The LORD asked, “Who gave people their mouths? 📖

Who makes people unable to speak, or deaf, or able to see, or blind?

Is it not I, the LORD?

**About this statement:** The LORD speaks about His power as Creator.

The verse does not say disability is a person’s moral failure.

## Exodus 4:12

Go now! 📖

I will help you speak and teach you what to say.”

## Exodus 4:13

Moses replied, “Please, Lord, send someone else.” 📖

## Exodus 4:14

The LORD became very angry with Moses. 📖

He said, “Is Aaron the Levite not your brother?

I know he speaks well.

He is coming to meet you and will be glad to see you.

## Exodus 4:15

Tell him what to say. 📖

I will help you both speak and teach you what to do.

## Exodus 4:16

He will speak to the people for you as your spokesman. 📖

It will be as if you were God to him.

**Help with “spokesman”:** This is someone who speaks for another person.

The comparison describes Moses giving Aaron the words to say.

## Exodus 4:17

Take this staff to perform the signs.” 📖

## Exodus 4:18

Moses returned to his father-in-law Jethro. 📖

He said, “Please let me return to my brothers in Egypt to see whether they live.”

Jethro replied, “Go in peace.”

## Exodus 4:19

The LORD had told Moses in Midian, “Return to Egypt. 📖

All the men who wanted to kill you are dead.”

## Exodus 4:20

Moses put his wife and sons on a donkey and headed back to Egypt. 📖

He carried God’s staff.

## Exodus 4:21

The LORD told him, “In Egypt, perform before Pharaoh all the wonders I gave you power to do. 📖

But I will harden his heart, so he will not release the people.

**Help with “harden his heart”:** This pictures making him stubborn and unwilling to listen.

The source attributes this action to the LORD.

## Exodus 4:22

Tell Pharaoh, ‘The LORD says: Israel is My firstborn son, 📖

**About this picture:** The LORD calls Israel His son to describe His relationship with the people.

## Exodus 4:23

and I told you to release My son to worship Me. 📖

You refused, so I will kill your firstborn son!’”

## Exodus 4:24

At a lodging place on the way, the LORD met Moses and was about to kill him. 📖

## Exodus 4:25

Zipporah took a flint knife and cut off her son’s foreskin. 📖

She touched it to Moses’ feet, saying, “You are a bridegroom of blood to me.”

**Help with these words:** Flint is a stone used to make a sharp blade.

The foreskin is skin covering the end of the penis.

The source does not name this son.

## Exodus 4:26

The LORD left him alone. 📖

Zipporah’s words “bridegroom of blood” referred to the circumcision.

**Help with “circumcision”:** This means removal of the foreskin.

The exact meaning of her expression remains difficult and needs further editorial review.

## Exodus 4:27

The LORD told Aaron, “Meet Moses in the wilderness.” 📖

Aaron met him at God’s mountain and kissed him.

## Exodus 4:28

Moses told Aaron everything the LORD sent him to say and all the signs He commanded. 📖

## Exodus 4:29

Moses and Aaron gathered all Israel’s elders, 📖

## Exodus 4:30

and Aaron told them everything the LORD told Moses. 📖

Moses performed the signs before the people,

## Exodus 4:31

and they believed. 📖

They heard the LORD had attended to Israel and seen their suffering.

They bowed down and worshiped.
