# Exodus 29 — Setting apart the priests

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives instructions for appointing Aaron and his sons.

The washing, clothing, oil, blood, sacrifices, and shared food each have specified places.

The chapter also gives rules for daily offerings.

## Exodus 29:1

“To set Aaron and his sons apart as My priests, take a young bull and two rams without blemish. 📖

**Help with these words:** A ram is a male sheep.

Without blemish means without a defect.

## Exodus 29:2

Bring unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers coated with oil. 📖

Use fine wheat flour.

**Help with these foods:** Unleavened means made without leaven, such as yeast, that makes dough rise.

Wafers are thin baked cakes.

## Exodus 29:3

Put them in a basket and present it with the bull and two rams. 📖

## Exodus 29:4

Bring Aaron and his sons to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance and wash them with water. 📖

## Exodus 29:5

Dress Aaron in the tunic, ephod’s robe, ephod, and breastplate. 📖

Fasten the ephod with its woven waistband.

## Exodus 29:6

Put the turban on his head and attach the holy diadem to it. 📖

**Help with “diadem”:** This is the special head ornament.

## Exodus 29:7

Pour the anointing oil on his head to anoint him. 📖

## Exodus 29:8

Bring his sons and dress them in tunics. 📖

## Exodus 29:9

Tie sashes around Aaron and his sons and headbands on them. 📖

Their priesthood is established by a permanent law.

Ordain them this way.

**Help with “ordain”:** This means appoint to priestly service.

## Exodus 29:10

Bring the bull before the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head.

## Exodus 29:11

Kill the bull before the LORD at the tent’s entrance. 📖

## Exodus 29:12

Use your finger to put some blood on the altar’s horns. 📖

Pour the rest at its base.

**Help with “horns”:** These are projections at the altar’s corners.

## Exodus 29:13

Burn all the fat covering its inner organs on the altar. 📖

Burn the liver’s lobe and both kidneys with their fat too.

**Help with these words:** The liver and kidneys are organs inside an animal’s body.

A lobe is a part of an organ.

## Exodus 29:14

Burn its meat, hide, and dung outside camp. 📖

It is a sin offering.

**Help with these words:** Dung is animal waste.

A sin offering deals with sin and purification before God.

## Exodus 29:15

Take the first ram. 📖

Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head.

## Exodus 29:16

Kill it and splatter its blood on all sides of the altar. 📖

## Exodus 29:17

Cut it into pieces. 📖

Wash its inner organs and legs, then place them with its head and other pieces.

## Exodus 29:18

Burn the whole ram on the altar. 📖

It is a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing smell and a food offering to the LORD.

## Exodus 29:19

Take the second ram. 📖

Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head.

## Exodus 29:20

Kill it and put blood on their right earlobes, right thumbs, and right big toes. 📖

Splatter the rest on all sides of the altar.

## Exodus 29:21

Sprinkle altar blood and anointing oil on Aaron, his sons, and all their garments. 📖

Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments will be set apart for God.

## Exodus 29:22

Take the fat, fat tail, fat over the inner organs, liver’s lobe, both kidneys with fat, and right thigh. 📖

This ram is for ordination.

## Exodus 29:23

Also take one loaf, one oil-made bread cake, and one wafer from the unleavened bread basket before the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 29:24

Put these in Aaron’s and his sons’ hands and wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖

**Help with “wave offering”:** This is an offering presented by moving it before the LORD.

## Exodus 29:25

Take them from their hands and burn them on top of the burnt offering. 📖

It is a pleasing smell before the LORD, a food offering to Him.

## Exodus 29:26

Wave the breast of Aaron’s ordination ram before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖

It will be your portion.

## Exodus 29:27

Set apart for Aaron and his sons the waved breast and the lifted thigh from the ordination ram. 📖

These are the wave offering and heave offering.

**Help with “heave offering”:** This is a portion lifted up as an offering.

## Exodus 29:28

This is Aaron’s and his sons’ regular portion from Israel. 📖

It is the heave offering Israel gives the LORD from peace offerings.

## Exodus 29:29

Aaron’s holy garments will pass to his sons after him, for anointing and ordination. 📖

## Exodus 29:30

The son succeeding him as priest must wear these garments seven days. 📖

This is the son who enters the Tent of Meeting to serve in the Holy Place.

## Exodus 29:31

Boil the ordination ram’s meat in a holy place. 📖

## Exodus 29:32

Aaron and his sons must eat the meat and basket’s bread at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Exodus 29:33

They must eat what made atonement for their ordination and consecration. 📖

No outsider may eat it, because it is sacred.

**Help with these words:** Atonement deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

Consecration sets them apart for God.

Sacred means set apart as holy.

## Exodus 29:34

If any ordination meat or bread remains until morning, burn it. 📖

Do not eat it, because it is sacred.

## Exodus 29:35

Follow all My commands for Aaron and his sons. 📖

Take seven days to ordain them.

## Exodus 29:36

Offer a bull for sin and atonement each day. 📖

Purify the altar by making atonement for it.

Anoint it to set it apart.

## Exodus 29:37

Make atonement for the altar and set it apart for seven days. 📖

It will become most holy, and anything touching it will be holy.

**About “holy” here:** This describes sacred status in the offering rules.

## Exodus 29:38

Offer two year-old lambs regularly on the altar each day. 📖

## Exodus 29:39

Offer one in the morning and one at twilight. 📖

## Exodus 29:40

With the first lamb, offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour. 📖

Mix it with a quarter hin of oil from pressed olives.

Also offer a quarter hin of wine as a drink offering.

**Help with these units:** An ephah measures dry goods.

A hin measures liquids.

## Exodus 29:41

Offer the second at twilight with the same grain and drink offerings as the morning. 📖

It is a pleasing smell, a food offering to the LORD.

## Exodus 29:42

Keep making this regular burnt offering at the tent’s entrance before the LORD for generations to come. 📖

I will meet and speak with you there.

## Exodus 29:43

I will meet Israel there too. 📖

My glory will make that place holy.

## Exodus 29:44

I will set apart the Tent of Meeting and the altar. 📖

I will set apart Aaron and his sons to serve Me as priests.

## Exodus 29:45

I will live among Israel and be their God. 📖

## Exodus 29:46

They will know I am the LORD their God. 📖

I brought them out of Egypt to live among them.

I am the LORD their God.”
