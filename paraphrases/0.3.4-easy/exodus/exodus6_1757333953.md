# Exodus 6 — The LORD’s promise and family records

**About this chapter**

The LORD renews His promise to bring Israel out of slavery.

The people’s suffering keeps them from listening.

Family records identify Moses and Aaron.

The names, ages, and relationships are retained.

## Exodus 6:1

The LORD told Moses, “Now you will see what I do to Pharaoh. 📖

My mighty hand will make him release them.

My strong hand will make him drive them from his land.”

**Help with this picture:** God’s hand pictures His power.

## Exodus 6:2

God also said, “I am the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 6:3

I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty. 📖

But I did not make Myself known to them by My name the LORD.

## Exodus 6:4

I made My covenant with them to give them Canaan, where they lived as foreigners. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement or promise.

## Exodus 6:5

I heard the groans of the Israelites whom the Egyptians enslave. 📖

I remembered My covenant.

## Exodus 6:6

Tell them, ‘I am the LORD. 📖

I will remove Egypt’s yoke and rescue you from slavery.

I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and mighty judgments.

**Help with these words:** A yoke is a wooden frame used to control working animals.

It pictures slavery’s burden here.

Redeem means set free or recover.

The outstretched arm pictures God’s power acting.

## Exodus 6:7

I will take you as My people and be your God. 📖

You will know I am the LORD your God, who removed Egypt’s yoke.

**Help with “Egypt’s yoke”:** This pictures their forced service to Egypt.

## Exodus 6:8

I will bring you to the land I swore to give Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

I will give it to you as your possession.

I am the LORD!’”

## Exodus 6:9

Moses told the Israelites, but they did not listen because their spirits were broken by cruel slavery. 📖

## Exodus 6:10

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 6:11

“Tell Pharaoh, Egypt’s king, to let the Israelites leave his land.” 📖

## Exodus 6:12

Moses replied before the LORD, “If Israel will not listen, why would Pharaoh? 📖

I am unskilled in speech.”

## Exodus 6:13

The LORD instructed Moses and Aaron about both Israel and Pharaoh, Egypt’s king. 📖

They were to bring the Israelites out of Egypt.

## Exodus 6:14

These were the heads of their fathers’ families. 📖

Israel’s firstborn Reuben had Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

These were Reuben’s clans.

**Help with “clans”:** These are family groups.

## Exodus 6:15

Simeon’s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mother was a Canaanite. 📖

These were Simeon’s clans.

## Exodus 6:16

Levi’s sons, by their records, were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

Levi lived 137 years.

## Exodus 6:17

Gershon’s sons were Libni and Shimei, by their clans. 📖

## Exodus 6:18

Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 📖

Kohath lived 133 years.

## Exodus 6:19

Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. 📖

These were the Levite clans by their records.

## Exodus 6:20

Amram married his father’s sister Jochebed. 📖

She bore him Aaron and Moses.

Amram lived 137 years.

## Exodus 6:21

Izhar’s sons were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri. 📖

## Exodus 6:22

Uzziel’s sons were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri. 📖

## Exodus 6:23

Aaron married Elisheba, Amminadab’s daughter and Nahshon’s sister. 📖

She bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

## Exodus 6:24

Korah’s sons were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. 📖

These were the Korahite clans.

## Exodus 6:25

Aaron’s son Eleazar married one of Putiel’s daughters, who bore him Phinehas. 📖

These were the Levite family heads by their clans.

## Exodus 6:26

The LORD told this Aaron and Moses, “Bring Israel’s people out of Egypt by their divisions.” 📖

**Help with “divisions”:** Here these are organized groups of the people.

## Exodus 6:27

This Moses and Aaron spoke to Pharaoh, Egypt’s king, to bring the Israelites out of Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 6:28

On the day the LORD spoke to Moses in Egypt, 📖

## Exodus 6:29

He said, “I am the LORD. 📖

Tell Pharaoh, Egypt’s king, everything I say to you.”

## Exodus 6:30

Moses replied before the LORD, “I am unskilled in speech. 📖

Why would Pharaoh listen?”
