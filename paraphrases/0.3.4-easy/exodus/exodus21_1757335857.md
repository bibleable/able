# Exodus 21 — Laws about service, injury, and responsibility

**About this chapter**

The LORD continues giving Moses laws for Israel.

These laws include slavery, marriage, violence, injury, and dangerous animals.

They make distinctions between free people, servants, men, and women.

## Exodus 21:1

“Set these laws before the people. 📖

## Exodus 21:2

If you buy a Hebrew servant, he must serve six years. 📖

In year seven, he goes free without paying.

## Exodus 21:3

If he came alone, he leaves alone. 📖

If he came with a wife, she leaves with him.

## Exodus 21:4

His master may give him a wife who bears sons or daughters. 📖

If so, she and her children belong to her master.

Only the man goes free.

## Exodus 21:5

But the servant may declare, ‘I love my master, wife, and children. 📖

I do not want freedom.’

## Exodus 21:6

Then his master must bring him before the judges. 📖

At the door or doorpost, the master must pierce his ear with an awl.

He will serve his master for life.

**Help with “awl”:** This is a pointed tool for making a hole.

## Exodus 21:7

If a man sells his daughter as a servant, she does not go free as male servants do. 📖

## Exodus 21:8

If her master chose her for himself but is displeased with her, he must allow her redemption. 📖

He broke faith with her, so he cannot sell her to foreigners.

**Help with “redemption”:** This means securing her release by payment.

## Exodus 21:9

If he chooses her for his son, he must treat her as a daughter. 📖

## Exodus 21:10

If he takes another wife, he must not reduce his first wife’s food, clothing, or marital rights. 📖

**Help with “marital rights”:** These are the rights of a wife within marriage, including sexual relations.

## Exodus 21:11

If he does not provide these three things, she may leave without paying. 📖

## Exodus 21:12

Anyone who strikes and kills a man must be put to death. 📖

## Exodus 21:13

If he did not plan an ambush, but God allowed it, I will appoint a place where he can flee. 📖

## Exodus 21:14

If someone deliberately plans to kill his neighbor, take him even from My altar to be put to death. 📖

## Exodus 21:15

Anyone who strikes his father or mother must be put to death. 📖

## Exodus 21:16

Anyone who kidnaps another man must be put to death. 📖

This applies whether he sells him or still holds him.

## Exodus 21:17

Anyone who curses his father or mother must be put to death. 📖

**Help with “curses”:** This means calls down harm on them.

## Exodus 21:18

If men quarrel and one hits another with a stone or fist, he may survive but stay in bed. 📖

## Exodus 21:19

If he gets up and walks outside with his staff, the attacker goes unpunished. 📖

But the attacker must pay for lost work and see that the injured man fully recovers.

## Exodus 21:20

If a man beats his male or female servant with a rod and the servant dies, he must be punished. 📖

## Exodus 21:21

If the servant gets up after a day or two, the owner is not punished. 📖

The servant is his property.

**About this law:** This states the unequal treatment of an enslaved person as property in the source.

## Exodus 21:22

If fighting men hit a pregnant woman and her child is born early without further injury, a fine is required. 📖

It must be what her husband demands and the court allows.

## Exodus 21:23

If serious injury follows, require life for life, 📖

## Exodus 21:24

eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot, 📖

## Exodus 21:25

burn for burn, wound for wound, and stripe for stripe. 📖

**Help with “stripe”:** Here it means the injury left by a blow.

## Exodus 21:26

If a man hits his male or female servant’s eye and blinds it, he must free the servant as compensation. 📖

**Help with “compensation”:** This means something given because of injury or loss.

## Exodus 21:27

If he knocks out a male or female servant’s tooth, he must free the servant as compensation. 📖

## Exodus 21:28

If an ox kills a man or woman with its horns, stone it to death. 📖

Do not eat its meat.

The owner is not held responsible.

## Exodus 21:29

If an ox habitually attacks, the owner has been warned, and he fails to restrain it, this rule applies. 📖

If it kills a man or woman, stone the ox and execute its owner.

## Exodus 21:30

If payment is demanded instead, he may save his life by paying the full amount. 📖

## Exodus 21:31

If the ox attacks a son or daughter, apply the same rule. 📖

## Exodus 21:32

If it attacks a male or female servant, pay thirty silver shekels to the servant’s master. 📖

Stone the ox to death.

**Help with “shekels”:** These are units of weight.

## Exodus 21:33

If a man opens or digs a pit and leaves it uncovered, an ox or donkey may fall in. 📖

## Exodus 21:34

The pit’s owner must pay the animal’s owner. 📖

The dead animal becomes his.

## Exodus 21:35

If one man’s ox kills his neighbor’s ox, sell the live animal and divide the money. 📖

Divide the dead animal too.

## Exodus 21:36

If the ox was known to attack, but its owner failed to restrain it, he must give full compensation. 📖

Give ox for ox, and the dead animal becomes his.”
