# Exodus 26 — Curtains and frames for the tabernacle

**About this chapter**

The LORD describes the tabernacle’s curtains, coverings, frames, and entrances.

The instructions distinguish the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place.

Measurements remain in cubits.

## Exodus 26:1

“Make the tabernacle from ten finely spun linen curtains. 📖

Work blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and skillfully made cherubim into each.

**Help with these words:** Linen is cloth made from flax.

Scarlet means bright red.

Cherubim are winged beings represented in the design.

## Exodus 26:2

Every curtain must be twenty-eight cubits long and four wide, all the same size. 📖

**Help with “cubit”:** This is a length based on the distance from elbow to fingertip.

## Exodus 26:3

Join five curtains as one set and five as another. 📖

## Exodus 26:4

Make blue loops along the first set’s end curtain edge. 📖

Do the same on the second set’s end curtain.

## Exodus 26:5

Make fifty loops on each set’s end curtain, positioned opposite one another. 📖

## Exodus 26:6

Make fifty gold clasps to join the curtains into one tabernacle. 📖

**Help with “clasps”:** These are fasteners that join things together.

## Exodus 26:7

Make eleven goat-hair curtains for the tent over the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 26:8

All eleven must be thirty cubits long and four wide. 📖

## Exodus 26:9

Join five into one set and six into another. 📖

Fold the sixth curtain double at the tent’s front.

## Exodus 26:10

Make fifty loops on the first set’s end edge and fifty on the matching edge of the second. 📖

## Exodus 26:11

Make fifty bronze clasps and put them through the loops, joining the tent into one unit. 📖

## Exodus 26:12

The remaining half curtain must hang over the tabernacle’s back. 📖

## Exodus 26:13

The tent curtains extend one cubit farther on each side. 📖

This extra covers the tabernacle’s sides.

## Exodus 26:14

Make a tent covering of ram skins dyed red. 📖

Above it, put a fine leather covering.

## Exodus 26:15

Make upright acacia frames for the tabernacle. 📖

**Help with “acacia”:** This is a kind of tree.

## Exodus 26:16

Each frame must be ten cubits long and one and a half wide. 📖

## Exodus 26:17

Each frame must have two tenons connected to each other. 📖

Make every frame this way.

**Help with “tenons”:** These are projecting pieces used to fit wooden parts together.

## Exodus 26:18

Make twenty frames for the south side, 📖

## Exodus 26:19

with forty silver bases underneath. 📖

Give each frame two bases, one for each tenon.

## Exodus 26:20

Make twenty frames for the second side, the north, 📖

## Exodus 26:21

and forty silver bases, two beneath each frame. 📖

## Exodus 26:22

Make six frames for the back, the west side, 📖

## Exodus 26:23

and two frames for the two back corners. 📖

## Exodus 26:24

Join them from bottom to top and fit them into one ring. 📖

They serve as the two corners.

## Exodus 26:25

There must be eight frames and sixteen silver bases, two per frame. 📖

## Exodus 26:26

Make five acacia crossbars for the frames on one side, 📖

## Exodus 26:27

five for the other side, and five for the back, to the west. 📖

## Exodus 26:28

The middle crossbar must run through the frames from end to end. 📖

## Exodus 26:29

Cover the frames with gold. 📖

Make gold rings to hold the crossbars, and cover the bars with gold too.

## Exodus 26:30

Set up the tabernacle according to the mountain pattern shown you. 📖

## Exodus 26:31

Make a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. 📖

Skillfully work cherubim into it.

**Help with “veil”:** This is a hanging curtain that separates spaces.

## Exodus 26:32

Hang it with gold hooks on four acacia posts covered with gold. 📖

They must stand on four silver bases.

## Exodus 26:33

Hang the veil from the clasps and place the ark of the Testimony behind it. 📖

The veil separates the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place.

**Help with these names:** These are the sanctuary’s two inner spaces.

The ark is the special chest holding God’s Testimony.

## Exodus 26:34

Put the mercy seat on the ark of the Testimony in the Most Holy Place. 📖

**Help with “mercy seat”:** This is the ark’s special cover.

## Exodus 26:35

Put the table outside the veil, on the north side. 📖

Put the lampstand opposite it, on the south.

## Exodus 26:36

Make the entrance curtain embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. 📖

**Help with “embroidered”:** This means decorated with stitched designs.

## Exodus 26:37

Make five acacia posts for the curtain. 📖

Overlay them with gold hooks and cast five bronze bases.

**About this wording:** BSB describes the posts with gold hooks here.

The construction wording needs further editorial review.
