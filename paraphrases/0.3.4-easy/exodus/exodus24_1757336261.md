# Exodus 24 — The covenant and the mountain

**About this chapter**

Moses reads the covenant words, and the people agree to obey.

Blood marks the covenant.

Moses and the leaders see God.

Moses then enters the cloud on the mountain.

## Exodus 24:1

The LORD told Moses, “Come up to the LORD with Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders. 📖

Worship at a distance.

## Exodus 24:2

Only Moses may approach the LORD. 📖

The others must stay back, and the people must not climb with him.”

## Exodus 24:3

Moses told the people all the LORD’s words and laws. 📖

Together they answered, “We will do everything the LORD said.”

## Exodus 24:4

Moses wrote all the LORD’s words. 📖

Early next morning, he built an altar at the mountain’s base.

He also built twelve pillars for Israel’s twelve tribes.

## Exodus 24:5

He sent some young Israelite men. 📖

They offered burnt offerings and sacrificed young bulls as peace offerings to the LORD.

**Help with these offerings:** Burnt offerings were burned on an altar.

Peace offerings were sacrifices connected with fellowship and peace with God.

## Exodus 24:6

Moses put half the blood in bowls and splattered half on the altar. 📖

## Exodus 24:7

He read the Book of the Covenant to the people. 📖

They replied, “We will do everything the LORD said.

We will obey.”

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement or promise.

## Exodus 24:8

Moses splattered blood on the people. 📖

He said, “This is the blood of the covenant the LORD made with you according to all these words.”

## Exodus 24:9

Moses went up with Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of Israel’s elders, 📖

## Exodus 24:10

and they saw Israel’s God. 📖

Beneath His feet was something like sapphire paving, clear as the sky itself.

**Help with “sapphire”:** This is a valuable stone.

## Exodus 24:11

God did not lay His hand on Israel’s nobles. 📖

They saw Him and ate and drank.

**Help with this picture:** Not laying His hand on them means He did not strike them.

## Exodus 24:12

The LORD told Moses, “Come up and stay with Me on the mountain. 📖

I will give you stone tablets with the law and commands I wrote to instruct them.”

**Help with “tablets”:** These are flat pieces of stone used for writing.

## Exodus 24:13

Moses set out with Joshua, his attendant. 📖

Moses went up God’s mountain.

## Exodus 24:14

He told the elders, “Wait here until we return. 📖

Aaron and Hur are here.

Anyone with a dispute can go to them.”

## Exodus 24:15

Moses climbed the mountain, and the cloud covered it. 📖

## Exodus 24:16

The LORD’s glory settled on Mount Sinai. 📖

The cloud covered it six days.

On day seven, the LORD called Moses from inside the cloud.

## Exodus 24:17

Israel saw the LORD’s glory like a consuming fire on the mountaintop. 📖

**Help with “consuming fire”:** This pictures fire burning up what it reaches.

## Exodus 24:18

Moses entered the cloud as he climbed. 📖

He stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights.
