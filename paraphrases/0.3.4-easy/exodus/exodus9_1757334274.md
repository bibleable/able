# Exodus 9 — Livestock disease, boils, and hail

**About this chapter**

Disease, painful boils, and hail strike Egypt.

The hail warning includes a way to shelter people and animals.

Some officials heed it, and others do not.

## Exodus 9:1

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Pharaoh, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, says: Release My people to worship Me. 📖

## Exodus 9:2

If you keep holding them and refuse to release them, 📖

## Exodus 9:3

My hand will bring severe disease on your livestock in the fields: horses, donkeys, camels, herds, and flocks. 📖

**Help with “My hand”:** This pictures the LORD’s power acting.

## Exodus 9:4

I will distinguish Israel’s livestock from Egypt’s. 📖

No animal belonging to the Israelites will die.’”

## Exodus 9:5

The LORD set the time, saying, “Tomorrow the LORD will do this in the land.” 📖

## Exodus 9:6

The next day, the LORD did so. 📖

All Egyptian livestock died.

Not one Israelite animal died.

## Exodus 9:7

Pharaoh sent officials and found no Israelite livestock had died. 📖

His heart was hardened, and he refused to release the people.

**Help with “heart was hardened”:** This pictures his stubborn refusal.

## Exodus 9:8

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, “Take handfuls of furnace soot. 📖

Moses must throw it into the air before Pharaoh.

**Help with “soot”:** This is black powder left by burning.

## Exodus 9:9

It will become fine dust over all Egypt. 📖

Festering boils will break out on people and animals throughout the land.”

**Help with “festering boils”:** These are painful skin swellings containing pus.

## Exodus 9:10

They took furnace soot and stood before Pharaoh. 📖

Moses threw it into the air, and festering boils appeared on people and animals.

## Exodus 9:11

The magicians could not stand before Moses because they and all the Egyptians had boils. 📖

## Exodus 9:12

The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart. 📖

He did not listen, as the LORD told Moses.

## Exodus 9:13

The LORD told Moses, “Rise early and stand before Pharaoh. 📖

Tell him, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, says: Release My people to worship Me.

## Exodus 9:14

Otherwise I will send all My plagues against you, your officials, and your people. 📖

You will know there is no one like Me in all the earth.

## Exodus 9:15

By now I could have stretched out My hand and struck you and your people with a plague. 📖

It could have wiped you from the earth.

**Help with “My hand”:** This pictures the LORD’s power acting.

A plague is a severe disaster or outbreak of disease.

## Exodus 9:16

But I raised you up to show you My power and have My name proclaimed throughout the earth. 📖

## Exodus 9:17

You still rule over My people with pride and will not release them. 📖

## Exodus 9:18

At this time tomorrow, I will send the worst hail Egypt has known since it was founded. 📖

**Help with “hail”:** These are balls or pieces of ice falling from the sky.

## Exodus 9:19

Order your livestock and everything in the fields brought under shelter. 📖

Every person or animal left outside will die when the hail strikes.’”

## Exodus 9:20

Pharaoh’s officials who feared the LORD’s word hurried their servants and livestock into shelter. 📖

## Exodus 9:21

Those who ignored His word left their servants and livestock in the fields. 📖

## Exodus 9:22

The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand toward heaven. 📖

Hail will fall throughout Egypt on people, animals, and every field plant.”

## Exodus 9:23

Moses stretched his staff toward heaven. 📖

The LORD sent thunder and hail, and lightning struck the ground.

The LORD rained hail on Egypt.

## Exodus 9:24

Lightning kept flashing through the hail. 📖

Nothing so severe had been seen in all Egypt since it became a nation.

## Exodus 9:25

Throughout Egypt, hail struck everything in the fields, both people and animals. 📖

It beat down every field plant and stripped every tree.

## Exodus 9:26

Only Goshen, where the Israelites lived, had no hail. 📖

## Exodus 9:27

Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron, saying, “This time I sinned. 📖

The LORD is righteous.

My people and I are wicked.

**Help with “righteous”:** This means right and just.

## Exodus 9:28

Pray to the LORD. 📖

God’s thunder and hail have been enough!

I will release you.

You need not stay longer.”

## Exodus 9:29

Moses said, “After leaving the city, I will spread my hands to the LORD. 📖

The thunder and hail will stop, so you know the earth belongs to the LORD.

**About spreading hands:** This is an action of prayer.

## Exodus 9:30

But I know you and your officials still do not fear the LORD our God.” 📖

## Exodus 9:31

The flax and barley were destroyed, because the barley was ripe and the flax flowering. 📖

**Help with these crops:** Flax is a plant used to make cloth.

Barley is a grain crop.

## Exodus 9:32

The wheat and spelt were not destroyed, because they ripen later. 📖

**Help with “spelt”:** This is a kind of grain.

## Exodus 9:33

Moses left Pharaoh and the city and spread his hands to the LORD. 📖

The thunder, hail, and pouring rain stopped.

## Exodus 9:34

Pharaoh saw them stop and sinned again. 📖

He and his officials hardened their hearts.

## Exodus 9:35

Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he refused to release Israel, as the LORD said through Moses. 📖
