# Exodus 8 — Frogs, gnats, and flies

**About this chapter**

Three further plagues strike Egypt.

Pharaoh makes promises and then refuses release.

The timing, prayers, conditions, and distinction given to Goshen are retained.

## Exodus 8:1

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Pharaoh, ‘The LORD says: Release My people to worship Me. 📖

## Exodus 8:2

If you refuse, I will plague your whole country with frogs. 📖

## Exodus 8:3

The Nile will swarm with them. 📖

They will enter your palace, bedroom, and bed.

They will enter your officials’ and people’s homes, ovens, and kneading bowls.

**Help with “kneading bowls”:** These are containers used to work dough before baking.

## Exodus 8:4

The frogs will come onto you, your people, and all your officials.’” 📖

## Exodus 8:5

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to stretch his hand and staff over the rivers, canals, and ponds. 📖

Make frogs come onto Egypt’s land.”

## Exodus 8:6

Aaron stretched his hand over Egypt’s waters. 📖

Frogs came up and covered the land.

## Exodus 8:7

The magicians used their magic arts to do the same, bringing frogs onto Egypt’s land. 📖

## Exodus 8:8

Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron. 📖

He said, “Pray to the LORD to remove the frogs from me and my people.

Then I will let your people go to sacrifice to the LORD.”

## Exodus 8:9

Moses said, “You may have the honor of choosing. 📖

When should I pray for you, your officials, and your people?

The frogs will leave you and your homes, except those in the Nile.”

## Exodus 8:10

Pharaoh answered, “Tomorrow.” 📖

Moses replied, “As you say.

You will know there is no one like the LORD our God.

## Exodus 8:11

The frogs will leave you, your homes, your officials, and your people. 📖

They will remain only in the Nile.”

## Exodus 8:12

Moses and Aaron left Pharaoh. 📖

Moses cried to the LORD for help with the frogs He brought against Pharaoh.

## Exodus 8:13

The LORD did as Moses asked. 📖

The frogs in houses, courtyards, and fields died.

## Exodus 8:14

They were piled into countless heaps. 📖

The land stank terribly.

## Exodus 8:15

Pharaoh saw relief and hardened his heart. 📖

He would not listen to Moses and Aaron, just as the LORD said.

**Help with “hardened his heart”:** This pictures Pharaoh making himself stubborn and refusing to listen.

## Exodus 8:16

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to stretch out his staff and strike the ground’s dust. 📖

It will become swarms of gnats throughout Egypt.”

**Help with “gnats”:** These are tiny flying insects.

## Exodus 8:17

They obeyed. 📖

Aaron struck the dust with his staff.

Gnats came upon people and animals.

All the ground’s dust became gnats throughout Egypt.

## Exodus 8:18

The magicians tried to make gnats by their magic arts but could not. 📖

Gnats remained on people and animals.

## Exodus 8:19

They told Pharaoh, “This is God’s finger.” 📖

His heart was hardened, and he would not listen, as the LORD said.

**Help with these pictures:** God’s finger pictures His power acting.

Pharaoh’s hardened heart pictures his stubborn refusal.

## Exodus 8:20

The LORD told Moses, “Rise early and meet Pharaoh when he goes to the water. 📖

Tell him, ‘The LORD says: Release My people to worship Me.

## Exodus 8:21

If you refuse, I will send swarms of flies on you, your officials, your people, and your houses. 📖

Egyptian houses and even the ground beneath them will fill with flies.

## Exodus 8:22

That day I will treat Goshen, where My people live, differently. 📖

No swarms will be there.

You will know I, the LORD, am in the land.

## Exodus 8:23

I will distinguish My people from yours. 📖

This sign will happen tomorrow.’”

## Exodus 8:24

The LORD did so. 📖

Thick swarms filled Pharaoh’s palace and his officials’ houses.

Flies ruined the land throughout Egypt.

## Exodus 8:25

Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron, saying, “Go and sacrifice to your God here in this land.” 📖

## Exodus 8:26

Moses replied, “That would not be right. 📖

Our sacrifices to the LORD our God would disgust the Egyptians.

If we offer such sacrifices before them, will they not stone us?

**Help with “stone us”:** This means attack them by throwing stones, potentially killing them.

## Exodus 8:27

We must travel three days into the wilderness and sacrifice to the LORD our God as He commands.” 📖

## Exodus 8:28

Pharaoh answered, “I will release you to sacrifice to the LORD your God in the wilderness. 📖

But do not go far.

Pray for me.”

## Exodus 8:29

Moses said, “When I leave, I will pray to the LORD. 📖

Tomorrow the flies will leave Pharaoh, his officials, and his people.

Do not deceive us again by refusing the people permission to sacrifice to the LORD.”

## Exodus 8:30

Moses left Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD, 📖

## Exodus 8:31

and the LORD did as he asked. 📖

He removed the flies from Pharaoh, his officials, and his people.

Not one remained.

## Exodus 8:32

Pharaoh hardened his heart again and would not release the people. 📖
