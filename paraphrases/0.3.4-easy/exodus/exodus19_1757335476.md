# Exodus 19 — Israel prepares at Sinai

**About this chapter**

The people arrive at Sinai.

The LORD gives a conditional covenant promise and commands preparation.

The mountain boundaries and severe warnings are part of the account.

## Exodus 19:1

Israel reached Sinai’s wilderness in the third month, on the same day of the month they had left Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 19:2

They traveled from Rephidim to Sinai’s wilderness and camped before the mountain. 📖

## Exodus 19:3

Moses went up to God. 📖

The LORD called from the mountain, “Tell Jacob’s household, the sons of Israel,

## Exodus 19:4

‘You saw what I did to Egypt. 📖

I carried you on eagles’ wings and brought you to Myself.

**Help with this picture:** Eagles are large birds.

This picture describes the LORD’s powerful care and rescue.

## Exodus 19:5

If you obey My voice and keep My covenant, you will be My treasured possession among all nations. 📖

The whole earth is Mine.

**Help with these words:** A covenant is a binding agreement or promise.

Treasured means greatly valued.

## Exodus 19:6

You will be My kingdom of priests and a holy nation.’ 📖

Speak these words to Israel.”

**Help with these words:** Priests serve in worship.

Holy means set apart for God.

## Exodus 19:7

Moses returned and called the elders. 📖

He gave them all the words the LORD commanded.

## Exodus 19:8

All the people answered together, “We will do everything the LORD says.” 📖

Moses reported their words to the LORD.

## Exodus 19:9

The LORD said, “I will come in a thick cloud. 📖

The people will hear Me speaking to you and trust you always.”

Moses told the LORD what they said.

## Exodus 19:10

The LORD said, “Set the people apart today and tomorrow. 📖

They must wash their clothes

## Exodus 19:11

and prepare for day three. 📖

That day, the LORD will descend on Mount Sinai before all the people.

## Exodus 19:12

Set a boundary around the mountain and warn them. 📖

They must not climb the mountain or touch its base.

Anyone touching it must be put to death.

## Exodus 19:13

No hand may touch that person. 📖

They must be stoned or shot with arrows.

Human or animal, they must not live.

Only when a ram’s horn sounds a long blast may they approach the mountain.”

**Help with “stoned”:** This means killed by stones being thrown.

The boundary violation carries a death judgment in this account.

## Exodus 19:14

Moses came down and set the people apart. 📖

They washed their clothes.

## Exodus 19:15

He said, “Prepare for day three. 📖

Do not have sex with a woman.”

**About this wording:** BSB says, “Do not draw near to a woman,” an expression for sexual relations.

## Exodus 19:16

On the third morning, thunder and lightning came. 📖

A thick cloud covered the mountain.

A ram’s horn sounded very loudly.

Everyone in camp trembled.

## Exodus 19:17

Moses led them out to meet God. 📖

They stood at the mountain’s foot.

## Exodus 19:18

Smoke covered all Mount Sinai because the LORD descended in fire. 📖

Smoke rose like a furnace’s smoke.

The whole mountain shook violently.

## Exodus 19:19

The ram’s horn grew louder and louder. 📖

Moses spoke, and God answered in thunder.

## Exodus 19:20

The LORD descended to Sinai’s summit and called Moses up. 📖

Moses climbed up,

## Exodus 19:21

and the LORD said, “Warn the people not to break through to see Me, or many will die. 📖

## Exodus 19:22

Even priests approaching the LORD must set themselves apart, or He will break out against them.” 📖

**Help with “break out against”:** This pictures the LORD striking them in judgment.

## Exodus 19:23

Moses replied, “They cannot climb Sinai. 📖

You firmly warned us to put a boundary around it and set it apart as holy.”

## Exodus 19:24

The LORD said, “Go down and bring Aaron. 📖

Priests and people must not break through to climb up to the LORD.

Otherwise He will break out against them.”

## Exodus 19:25

Moses went down and spoke to the people. 📖
