# Exodus 5 — Pharaoh makes the work harder

**About this chapter**

Moses and Aaron ask Pharaoh to release the people for worship.

Pharaoh refuses and makes the forced labor harsher.

The Israelite supervisors protest, and Moses brings his questions to the LORD.

## Exodus 5:1

Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh. 📖

They said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘Release My people to hold a feast for Me in the wilderness.’”

## Exodus 5:2

Pharaoh replied, “Who is the LORD that I should obey Him and release Israel? 📖

I do not know the LORD and will not release Israel.”

## Exodus 5:3

They answered, “The Hebrews’ God met us. 📖

Please let us travel three days into the wilderness to sacrifice to the LORD our God.

Otherwise He may strike us with plagues or the sword.”

**Help with these words:** Plagues are severe disasters or outbreaks of disease.

The sword pictures violent death.

## Exodus 5:4

Egypt’s king said, “Moses and Aaron, why draw the people away from work? 📖

Return to your labor!”

## Exodus 5:5

Pharaoh added, “The people are now numerous, and you would stop their work.” 📖

## Exodus 5:6

That day, Pharaoh commanded the people’s Egyptian taskmasters and their foremen, 📖

**Help with these roles:** Taskmasters forced people to work.

The foremen were supervisors working under them.

## Exodus 5:7

“Stop supplying straw for their bricks. 📖

Make them gather their own.

## Exodus 5:8

Demand as many bricks as before. 📖

Do not reduce the number.

They are lazy.

That is why they cry, ‘Let us sacrifice to our God.’

## Exodus 5:9

Make their work harder to keep them busy, so they ignore these lies.” 📖

## Exodus 5:10

The taskmasters and foremen told the people, “Pharaoh says, ‘I will give you no more straw. 📖

## Exodus 5:11

Find your own wherever you can. 📖

Your workload will not be reduced.’”

## Exodus 5:12

The people scattered throughout Egypt to gather stubble for straw. 📖

**Help with “stubble”:** These are short stalks left in fields after harvest.

## Exodus 5:13

The taskmasters kept pressing them, saying, “Make the full number each day, as when straw was supplied.” 📖

## Exodus 5:14

Pharaoh’s taskmasters had put Israelite foremen over the people. 📖

These foremen were beaten and asked, “Why did you not make enough bricks yesterday or today, as before?”

## Exodus 5:15

The Israelite foremen appealed to Pharaoh, “Why treat your servants this way? 📖

## Exodus 5:16

We get no straw, yet are told to make bricks. 📖

Your servants are beaten, but your own people are at fault.”

## Exodus 5:17

Pharaoh replied, “Lazy people! 📖

Lazy people!

That is why you keep saying, ‘Let us sacrifice to the LORD.’

## Exodus 5:18

Get to work. 📖

You will receive no straw but must deliver all the required bricks.”

## Exodus 5:19

The Israelite foremen knew they were in trouble when told not to reduce the daily brick total. 📖

## Exodus 5:20

Leaving Pharaoh, they confronted Moses and Aaron, who waited to meet them. 📖

## Exodus 5:21

They said, “May the LORD see and judge you! 📖

You made us stink before Pharaoh and his officials.

You put a sword in their hands to kill us!”

**Help with these pictures:** The foremen mean Pharaoh and his officials now hate them.

The sword pictures power to kill them.

## Exodus 5:22

Moses returned to the LORD and asked, “Lord, why did You bring trouble on these people? 📖

Is this why You sent me?

## Exodus 5:23

Since I spoke to Pharaoh in Your name, he has brought trouble on them. 📖

You have not rescued Your people at all.”
