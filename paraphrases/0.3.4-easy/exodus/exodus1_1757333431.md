# Exodus 1 — Israel’s people are oppressed

**About this chapter**

Israel’s family grows in Egypt.

A new king forces them into hard labor and orders Hebrew boys killed.

The midwives refuse his order because they fear God.

## Exodus 1:1

These are Israel’s sons who went to Egypt with Jacob. 📖

Each brought his family.

## Exodus 1:2

They were Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah; 📖

## Exodus 1:3

Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin; 📖

## Exodus 1:4

Dan and Naphtali; Gad and Asher. 📖

## Exodus 1:5

Jacob’s descendants numbered seventy altogether, including Joseph, who was already in Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 1:6

Joseph, all his brothers, and everyone in that generation died, 📖

## Exodus 1:7

but the Israelites had many children and grew rapidly. 📖

They became so numerous that they filled the land.

## Exodus 1:8

A new king came to power in Egypt. 📖

He did not know Joseph.

## Exodus 1:9

He told his people, “The Israelites have become too many and too powerful for us. 📖

## Exodus 1:10

We must deal cleverly with them, or they will increase more. 📖

If war comes, they may join our enemies, fight us, and leave the country.”

## Exodus 1:11

The Egyptians put supervisors over the Israelites to oppress them through forced labor. 📖

The Israelites built Pithom and Rameses as storage cities for Pharaoh.

**Help with “oppress”:** This means use power to treat people cruelly and unfairly.

## Exodus 1:12

The more they were oppressed, the more they grew and flourished. 📖

The Egyptians began to dread them.

## Exodus 1:13

They made the Israelites work without mercy 📖

## Exodus 1:14

and made their lives bitter with hard work making bricks and mortar and working in fields. 📖

All the work they forced on them was harsh.

**Help with “mortar”:** This is material used to join bricks.

“Bitter” pictures their painful lives.

## Exodus 1:15

Egypt’s king spoke to the Hebrew midwives Shiphrah and Puah. 📖

**Help with “midwives”:** These are women who help others give birth.

## Exodus 1:16

He said, “When helping Hebrew women give birth, watch them on the birthstools. 📖

If the baby is a boy, kill him.

If the baby is a girl, let her live.”

**Help with “birthstools”:** These are seats used during childbirth.

## Exodus 1:17

The midwives feared God and disobeyed Egypt’s king. 📖

They let the boys live.

## Exodus 1:18

The king called them and asked, “Why have you done this? 📖

Why have you let the boys live?”

## Exodus 1:19

They answered Pharaoh, “Hebrew women are strong, unlike Egyptian women. 📖

They give birth before the midwife arrives.”

**About their answer:** These are the midwives’ words to Pharaoh.

## Exodus 1:20

God treated the midwives well. 📖

The people increased and became even more numerous.

## Exodus 1:21

Because the midwives feared God, He gave them families of their own. 📖

## Exodus 1:22

Pharaoh ordered all his people, “Throw every newborn Hebrew boy into the Nile. 📖

You may let every girl live.”
