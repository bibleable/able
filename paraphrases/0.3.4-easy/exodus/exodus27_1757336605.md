# Exodus 27 — The altar, courtyard, and lamps

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives instructions for the bronze-covered altar and the courtyard.

He also commands pure olive oil for the lamps.

Aaron and his sons must tend the light.

## Exodus 27:1

“Build a square acacia altar, five cubits long, five wide, and three high. 📖

**Help with these words:** An altar is a place for offerings.

Acacia is a kind of tree.

A cubit is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Exodus 27:2

Put a horn on each of its four corners, all one piece with it. 📖

Cover it with bronze.

**Help with “horn”:** This is a projection shaped like an animal’s horn.

## Exodus 27:3

Make all its tools of bronze: ash pots, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans. 📖

## Exodus 27:4

Make a bronze mesh grate with a bronze ring at each of its four corners. 📖

**Help with these words:** Mesh is a network with spaces between its strands.

A grate is an open frame supporting what is burned.

## Exodus 27:5

Put the grate below the altar’s ledge, with the mesh halfway up the altar. 📖

## Exodus 27:6

Make acacia poles covered with bronze. 📖

## Exodus 27:7

Put them through the rings so they lie on two sides when the altar is carried. 📖

## Exodus 27:8

Make the altar hollow from boards, according to what you saw on the mountain. 📖

## Exodus 27:9

Make a courtyard for the tabernacle. 📖

On its south side, put finely spun linen curtains a hundred cubits long.

## Exodus 27:10

Use twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, with silver hooks and bands on the posts. 📖

## Exodus 27:11

On the north, use curtains a hundred cubits long, twenty posts, and twenty bronze bases. 📖

Give the posts silver hooks and bands.

## Exodus 27:12

On the west, make curtains fifty cubits wide, with ten posts and ten bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:13

The east side, toward sunrise, must be fifty cubits wide. 📖

## Exodus 27:14

Make curtains fifteen cubits long on one side, with three posts and three bases, 📖

## Exodus 27:15

and fifteen cubits on the other side, with three posts and three bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:16

The courtyard gate must be twenty cubits long. 📖

Make its curtain from finely spun linen, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn.

Give it four posts and four bases.

**Help with these words:** Scarlet means bright red.

Embroidered means decorated with stitched designs.

## Exodus 27:17

All courtyard posts must have silver bands, silver hooks, and bronze bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:18

The courtyard must be a hundred cubits long and fifty wide. 📖

Its fine linen curtains must be five cubits high, with bronze bases.

## Exodus 27:19

All tabernacle tools for every use must be bronze. 📖

This includes all its tent pegs and the courtyard’s tent pegs.

## Exodus 27:20

Command Israel to bring pure oil from pressed olives for lighting. 📖

Keep the lamps burning continually.

## Exodus 27:21

Aaron and his sons must tend them before the LORD from evening to morning in the Tent of Meeting. 📖

This is outside the veil in front of the Testimony.

This is a permanent law for Israel through coming generations.”
