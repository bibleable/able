# Exodus 34 — New tablets and the renewed covenant

**About this chapter**

Moses returns to Sinai with new stone tablets.

The LORD proclaims His name and renews covenant instructions.

Moses comes down with a shining face.

## Exodus 34:1

The LORD told Moses, “Chisel two stone tablets like the first ones. 📖

I will write the same words on them.

You broke the first tablets.

## Exodus 34:2

Be ready tomorrow morning. 📖

Come to Mount Sinai’s summit and present yourself before Me.

## Exodus 34:3

No one may climb with you or be seen anywhere on the mountain. 📖

Not even flocks or herds may graze in front of it.”

## Exodus 34:4

Moses chiseled two tablets like the first. 📖

Early next morning, he carried them up Sinai as the LORD commanded.

## Exodus 34:5

The LORD descended in a cloud, stood with him, and proclaimed His name, the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 34:6

He passed before Moses and called, “The LORD, the LORD God, compassionate and gracious. 📖

He is slow to anger, overflowing with faithful love and faithfulness,

**Help with these words:** Compassionate means caring about suffering.

Gracious means generous in showing kindness.

## Exodus 34:7

keeping faithful love for a thousand generations and forgiving wrongdoing, rebellion, and sin. 📖

Yet He never leaves the guilty unpunished.

He punishes fathers’ sins in their children and grandchildren, to the third and fourth generations.”

## Exodus 34:8

Moses immediately bowed to the ground and worshiped. 📖

## Exodus 34:9

He said, “O Lord, if I found favor, my Lord, please go with us. 📖

They are stiff-necked, but forgive our wrongdoing and sin.

Take us as Your inheritance.”

**Help with these pictures:** Stiff-necked means stubborn.

Moses asks God to receive the people as His own possession.

## Exodus 34:10

The LORD said, “I am making a covenant. 📖

Before all your people, I will do wonders never done in any nation anywhere.

Those among whom you live will see the LORD’s work.

What I do with you is awesome.

**Help with these words:** A covenant is a binding agreement or promise.

Awesome means inspiring wonder and fear.

## Exodus 34:11

Obey what I command today. 📖

I will drive out the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

## Exodus 34:12

Do not make a treaty with the land’s inhabitants, or they may become a trap among you. 📖

## Exodus 34:13

Tear down their altars, smash their sacred stones, and cut down their Asherah poles. 📖

**Help with “Asherah poles”:** These are objects used in worship connected with Asherah.

## Exodus 34:14

Worship no other god. 📖

The LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God.

**Help with “Jealous”:** The LORD requires His people’s faithful worship and does not accept their worship of other gods.

## Exodus 34:15

Do not make a covenant with the inhabitants. 📖

They prostitute themselves to their gods and sacrifice to them.

They will invite you, and you will eat their sacrifices.

**Help with this picture:** “Prostitute themselves” pictures unfaithful worship of other gods.

## Exodus 34:16

If you take their daughters as brides for your sons, those daughters will prostitute themselves to their gods. 📖

They will lead your sons to do the same.

**About the wording:** The image again describes unfaithful worship.

## Exodus 34:17

Make no cast-metal gods for yourselves. 📖

## Exodus 34:18

Keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. 📖

Eat unleavened bread seven days at the appointed time in Abib, as I commanded.

You left Egypt in Abib.

**Help with “unleavened”:** This means made without leaven, such as yeast, that makes dough rise.

## Exodus 34:19

The first offspring from every womb belongs to Me. 📖

This includes every firstborn male among cattle and sheep.

## Exodus 34:20

Redeem a firstborn donkey with a lamb. 📖

If you do not, break its neck.

Redeem every firstborn son.

No one may come before Me with empty hands.

**Help with “redeem”:** This means secure release by giving something in exchange.

## Exodus 34:21

Work six days and rest on day seven, even during plowing and harvest. 📖

## Exodus 34:22

Celebrate the Feast of Weeks with the wheat harvest’s first produce. 📖

Celebrate the Feast of Ingathering at the year’s turn.

## Exodus 34:23

Three times yearly, all your males must appear before the Lord GOD, Israel’s God. 📖

## Exodus 34:24

I will drive out nations and enlarge your borders. 📖

No one will covet your land when you appear before the LORD your God three times yearly.

**Help with “covet”:** This means desire to have what belongs to another.

## Exodus 34:25

Do not offer sacrificial blood to Me with anything leavened. 📖

Leave none of the Passover Feast’s sacrifice until morning.

## Exodus 34:26

Bring your soil’s best first produce to the LORD your God’s house. 📖

Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.”

## Exodus 34:27

The LORD told Moses, “Write these words. 📖

According to them, I made a covenant with you and Israel.”

## Exodus 34:28

Moses stayed with the LORD forty days and forty nights, eating no bread and drinking no water. 📖

He wrote the covenant words, the Ten Commandments, on the tablets.

**About the writer:** BSB says “He wrote” here without naming the writer again.

In verse 1, the LORD says He will write on the tablets.

The pronoun’s reference needs further editorial review.

## Exodus 34:29

Moses came down Sinai holding the two Testimony tablets. 📖

He did not know his face shone from speaking with the LORD.

## Exodus 34:30

Aaron and all Israel saw his shining face and feared to approach. 📖

## Exodus 34:31

Moses called them. 📖

Aaron and all the congregation’s leaders returned, and Moses spoke.

## Exodus 34:32

All the Israelites then came near. 📖

Moses commanded everything the LORD told him on Sinai.

## Exodus 34:33

After speaking, Moses put a veil over his face. 📖

## Exodus 34:34

When speaking before the LORD, Moses removed it until he came out. 📖

Outside, he told Israel what he was commanded,

## Exodus 34:35

and they saw his shining face. 📖

He covered it again until he went to speak with the LORD.
