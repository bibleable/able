# Exodus 3 — God calls Moses from the bush

**About this chapter**

God speaks to Moses and sends him to bring Israel out of Egypt.

God’s names, promises, instructions, and warning about Pharaoh are retained.

The land’s peoples are listed in full.

## Exodus 3:1

Moses tended the flock of his father-in-law Jethro, Midian’s priest. 📖

He led it beyond the wilderness to Horeb, God’s mountain.

## Exodus 3:2

The angel of the LORD appeared in a blazing fire inside a bush. 📖

Moses saw the burning bush, but it was not burned up.

## Exodus 3:3

He thought, “I must see this wonderful sight. 📖

Why is the bush not burning up?”

## Exodus 3:4

The LORD saw him go to look. 📖

God called from the bush, “Moses, Moses!”

Moses answered, “Here I am.”

## Exodus 3:5

God said, “Come no closer. 📖

Remove your sandals, because you stand on holy ground.”

**Help with “holy”:** Here it means set apart for God.

## Exodus 3:6

He said, “I am your father’s God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.” 📖

Moses hid his face, afraid to look at God.

## Exodus 3:7

The LORD said, “I have seen My people’s suffering in Egypt. 📖

I heard their cries because of their oppressors and know their pain.

**Help with “oppressors”:** These are people who use power to treat others cruelly.

## Exodus 3:8

I came down to rescue them from the Egyptians’ power and bring them out to a good, spacious land. 📖

It flows with milk and honey.

Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites live there.

**Help with “flows with milk and honey”:** This pictures land with plentiful good food.

## Exodus 3:9

The Israelites’ cry reached Me. 📖

I saw how severely the Egyptians oppress them.

## Exodus 3:10

Go! 📖

I am sending you to Pharaoh to bring My people, the Israelites, out of Egypt.”

## Exodus 3:11

Moses asked God, “Who am I to go to Pharaoh and bring the Israelites out of Egypt?” 📖

## Exodus 3:12

God said, “I will be with you. 📖

Here is the sign that I sent you.

After bringing them out of Egypt, you will all worship God on this mountain.”

## Exodus 3:13

Moses asked, “Suppose I tell the Israelites, ‘Your fathers’ God sent me.’ 📖

If they ask His name, what should I say?”

## Exodus 3:14

God said, “I AM WHO I AM. 📖

Tell the Israelites, ‘I AM sent me to you.’”

**About these words:** These are the words God gives Moses to identify Him.

## Exodus 3:15

God added, “Tell them, ‘The LORD, your fathers’ God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, sent me.’ 📖

This is My name forever.

Every generation must remember Me this way.

## Exodus 3:16

Gather Israel’s elders and say, ‘The LORD, your fathers’ God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, appeared to me. 📖

He said: I have attended to you and seen what was done to you in Egypt.

**Help with “elders”:** These are the people’s leaders.

## Exodus 3:17

I promised to bring you out of Egypt’s suffering to the Canaanites’, Hittites’, Amorites’, Perizzites’, Hivites’, and Jebusites’ land. 📖

It flows with milk and honey.’

**Help with this picture:** The milk and honey picture plentiful good food.

## Exodus 3:18

The elders will listen. 📖

Go with them to Egypt’s king and say, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, met us.

Please let us travel three days into the wilderness to sacrifice to the LORD our God.’

**Help with “sacrifice”:** This means give an offering to God.

## Exodus 3:19

I know Egypt’s king will not let you go unless a mighty hand forces him. 📖

**Help with “a mighty hand”:** This pictures great power.

## Exodus 3:20

I will stretch out My hand and strike Egypt with all the wonders I will perform among them. 📖

Afterward, he will release you.

**Help with this picture:** God’s outstretched hand pictures His power acting.

Wonders are extraordinary acts.

## Exodus 3:21

I will give you favor with the Egyptians, so you will not leave with empty hands. 📖

## Exodus 3:22

Every woman must ask her neighbor and any woman staying in her house for silver and gold jewelry and clothes. 📖

Put them on your sons and daughters.

In this way you will plunder the Egyptians.”

**Help with “plunder”:** This means take goods away from others.
