# Exodus 17 — Water and the battle with Amalek

**About this chapter**

Israel needs water at Rephidim.

Later, the Amalekites attack.

The account includes Moses’ raised hands, Joshua’s victory, and the LORD’s judgment against Amalek.

## Exodus 17:1

All Israel left the Desert of Sin, moving from place to place as the LORD commanded. 📖

They camped at Rephidim, where there was no drinking water.

## Exodus 17:2

They argued with Moses, demanding water. 📖

He asked, “Why argue with me?

Why test the LORD?”

## Exodus 17:3

Thirsty, they complained, “Why bring us from Egypt to make us, our children, and livestock die of thirst?” 📖

## Exodus 17:4

Moses cried to the LORD, “What should I do with them? 📖

Soon they will stone me!”

**Help with “stone me”:** This means kill him by throwing stones.

## Exodus 17:5

The LORD said, “Go ahead of the people with some of Israel’s elders. 📖

Carry the staff you used to strike the Nile.

## Exodus 17:6

I will stand before you by the rock at Horeb. 📖

Strike it, and water will come out for the people.”

Moses obeyed before Israel’s elders.

## Exodus 17:7

He named it Massah and Meribah because Israel quarreled and tested the LORD. 📖

They asked, “Is the LORD among us or not?”

## Exodus 17:8

The Amalekites came and attacked Israel at Rephidim. 📖

## Exodus 17:9

Moses told Joshua, “Choose some men and fight the Amalekites. 📖

Tomorrow I will stand on the hilltop with God’s staff.”

## Exodus 17:10

Joshua obeyed and fought the Amalekites. 📖

Moses, Aaron, and Hur climbed the hill.

## Exodus 17:11

While Moses held his hands up, Israel was winning. 📖

When he lowered them, Amalek was winning.

## Exodus 17:12

His hands grew heavy. 📖

They put a stone beneath him, and he sat on it.

Aaron and Hur supported his hands, one on each side.

His hands stayed steady until sunset.

## Exodus 17:13

Joshua overwhelmed Amalek and his army with the sword. 📖

## Exodus 17:14

The LORD told Moses, “Write this on a scroll as a reminder and recite it to Joshua. 📖

I will completely erase Amalek’s memory from under heaven.”

**Help with these words:** A scroll is a rolled piece of writing material.

The LORD’s words announce complete destruction, not only defeat in this battle.

## Exodus 17:15

Moses built an altar and named it The LORD Is My Banner. 📖

**Help with “banner”:** This is a flag or sign people gather under.

The name pictures the LORD leading His people.

## Exodus 17:16

He said, “A hand was lifted toward the LORD’s throne. 📖

The LORD will wage war against Amalek from generation to generation.”

**About the raised hand:** This is the picture given in BSB.

Its exact interpretation needs further editorial review.
