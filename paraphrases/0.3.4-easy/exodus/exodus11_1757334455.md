# Exodus 11 — The final plague is announced

**About this chapter**

The LORD announces the death of Egypt’s firstborn.

Moses warns Pharaoh and leaves in anger.

The chapter distinguishes the Egyptians’ losses from Israel’s protection.

## Exodus 11:1

The LORD told Moses, “I will bring one more plague on Pharaoh and Egypt. 📖

Then he will let you leave.

He will drive you out completely.

## Exodus 11:2

Tell both men and women to ask their neighbors for silver and gold articles.” 📖

## Exodus 11:3

The LORD gave the people favor with the Egyptians. 📖

Pharaoh’s officials and Egypt’s people also respected Moses highly.

## Exodus 11:4

Moses declared, “The LORD says, ‘About midnight I will go throughout Egypt, 📖

## Exodus 11:5

and every firstborn son in Egypt will die. 📖

That includes the firstborn of Pharaoh, who sits on the throne.

Even the servant girl behind the hand mill will lose her firstborn son.

All the cattle’s firstborn will die too.

**Help with “hand mill”:** This is a tool used by hand to grind grain.

## Exodus 11:6

A great cry will rise throughout Egypt. 📖

Nothing like it has been heard before or will be heard again.

## Exodus 11:7

Among all Israelites, not even a dog will snarl at a person or animal.’ 📖

You will know the LORD distinguishes Egypt from Israel.

## Exodus 11:8

All your officials will bow before me and say, ‘Leave with all the people following you!’ 📖

Then I will leave.”

Moses left Pharaoh, burning with anger.

## Exodus 11:9

The LORD told Moses, “Pharaoh will not listen, so My wonders can increase in Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 11:10

Moses and Aaron performed all these wonders before Pharaoh. 📖

But the LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, so he would not let the Israelites leave his land.

**Help with this picture:** Hardening his heart pictures making him stubborn and unwilling to listen.
