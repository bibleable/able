# Exodus 2 — Moses is rescued and leaves Egypt

**About this chapter**

A baby is rescued from the Nile and becomes the son of Pharaoh’s daughter.

He is named Moses.

As an adult, he kills an Egyptian and flees to Midian.

God hears Israel’s suffering.

## Exodus 2:1

A man from Levi’s family married a Levite woman, 📖

## Exodus 2:2

and she became pregnant and bore a son. 📖

She saw he was beautiful and hid him for three months.

## Exodus 2:3

When she could hide him no longer, she coated a papyrus basket with tar and pitch. 📖

She put him inside and placed it among reeds on the Nile’s bank.

**Help with these materials:** Papyrus is a plant used to make things such as baskets.

Tar and pitch help keep water out.

## Exodus 2:4

His sister stood some distance away to see what happened to him. 📖

## Exodus 2:5

Pharaoh’s daughter went to bathe in the Nile while her attendants walked along the bank. 📖

She saw the basket among reeds and sent her female servant to get it.

## Exodus 2:6

She opened it and saw the crying baby boy. 📖

She felt compassion and said, “This is a Hebrew child.”

## Exodus 2:7

His sister asked Pharaoh’s daughter, “Shall I call a Hebrew woman to nurse him for you?” 📖

**Help with “nurse”:** Here this means feed a baby with breast milk.

## Exodus 2:8

Pharaoh’s daughter said, “Go ahead.” 📖

The girl called the boy’s mother.

## Exodus 2:9

Pharaoh’s daughter told her, “Take him and nurse him for me. 📖

I will pay you.”

The woman took him and nursed him.

## Exodus 2:10

When he was older, she brought him to Pharaoh’s daughter, who made him her son. 📖

She named him Moses, saying, “I drew him from the water.”

## Exodus 2:11

One day, grown-up Moses visited his own people and saw their hard labor. 📖

He saw an Egyptian beating a Hebrew, one of his people.

## Exodus 2:12

Moses looked around and saw no one. 📖

He killed the Egyptian and hid his body in the sand.

## Exodus 2:13

The next day, he saw two Hebrews fighting. 📖

He asked the one doing wrong, “Why are you attacking your companion?”

## Exodus 2:14

The man replied, “Who made you ruler and judge over us? 📖

Will you kill me as you killed the Egyptian?”

Moses was afraid, thinking, “People must know what I did.”

## Exodus 2:15

Pharaoh heard and tried to kill Moses. 📖

Moses fled and settled in Midian.

There he sat beside a well.

## Exodus 2:16

Midian’s priest had seven daughters. 📖

They came to draw water and fill troughs for their father’s flock.

**Help with “troughs”:** These are long containers for animals’ food or water.

## Exodus 2:17

Shepherds came and drove them away. 📖

Moses stood up to help and watered their flock.

## Exodus 2:18

They returned to their father Reuel. 📖

He asked, “Why are you back so early?”

## Exodus 2:19

They replied, “An Egyptian saved us from the shepherds. 📖

He even drew water and watered the flock.”

## Exodus 2:20

He asked, “Where is he? 📖

Why did you leave him behind?

Invite him to eat.”

## Exodus 2:21

Moses agreed to stay with him. 📖

He gave Moses his daughter Zipporah as a wife.

## Exodus 2:22

She bore a son. 📖

Moses named him Gershom, saying, “I have become a foreigner in a foreign land.”

## Exodus 2:23

Much later, Egypt’s king died. 📖

The Israelites groaned and cried under slavery’s burden.

Their cry for rescue from slavery rose to God.

## Exodus 2:24

God heard their groaning and remembered His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement or promise.

## Exodus 2:25

God saw the Israelites and took notice. 📖
