# Exodus 10 — Locusts and darkness

**About this chapter**

Locusts consume what the hail left.

Darkness follows.

The negotiations retain exactly who and what Pharaoh offers to release and what Moses refuses to leave behind.

## Exodus 10:1

The LORD told Moses, “Go to Pharaoh. 📖

I hardened his heart and his officials’ hearts to perform My miraculous signs among them,

**Help with this picture:** Hardening hearts pictures making people stubborn.

The LORD attributes this action to Himself.

## Exodus 10:2

and so you can tell your children and grandchildren how severely I dealt with the Egyptians. 📖

Tell them about My miraculous signs, so you all know I am the LORD.”

## Exodus 10:3

Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh. 📖

They said, “The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, says, ‘How long will you refuse to humble yourself before Me?

Release My people to worship Me.

## Exodus 10:4

If you refuse, I will bring locusts into your land tomorrow. 📖

**Help with “locusts”:** These are insects that can gather in huge groups and eat crops.

## Exodus 10:5

They will cover the ground so no one can see it. 📖

They will devour what the hail left and eat every tree in your fields.

## Exodus 10:6

They will fill your houses, all your officials’ houses, and every Egyptian’s house. 📖

Your fathers and grandfathers never saw anything like it since coming here.’”

Moses turned and left Pharaoh.

## Exodus 10:7

Pharaoh’s officials asked, “How long will this man be a trap for us? 📖

Let the people worship the LORD their God.

Do you not see Egypt is ruined?”

**Help with “trap”:** They picture Moses as bringing them into danger.

## Exodus 10:8

Moses and Aaron were brought back. 📖

Pharaoh said, “Worship the LORD your God.

But exactly who will go?”

## Exodus 10:9

Moses replied, “Our young and old, sons and daughters, flocks and herds. 📖

We must hold a feast to the LORD.”

## Exodus 10:10

Pharaoh said, “May the LORD be with you if I ever release you and your little ones! 📖

You clearly intend evil.

## Exodus 10:11

No! 📖

Only the men may worship the LORD, since that is what you requested.”

Moses and Aaron were driven out.

## Exodus 10:12

The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand over Egypt. 📖

Locusts will swarm over it and devour every plant the hail left.”

## Exodus 10:13

Moses stretched his staff over Egypt. 📖

The LORD sent an east wind all that day and night.

By morning, it brought locusts.

## Exodus 10:14

They swarmed and settled over all Egypt. 📖

Never before had there been so many, and never again will there be.

## Exodus 10:15

They covered all the ground until it was black. 📖

They ate all plants and tree fruit left after the hail.

Nothing green remained on any tree or plant in Egypt.

## Exodus 10:16

Pharaoh quickly called Moses and Aaron. 📖

He said, “I sinned against the LORD your God and against you.

## Exodus 10:17

Please forgive my sin once more. 📖

Ask the LORD your God to remove this death from me.”

## Exodus 10:18

Moses left Pharaoh and appealed to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 10:19

The LORD changed the wind to a very strong west wind. 📖

It carried the locusts into the Red Sea.

Not one remained anywhere in Egypt.

## Exodus 10:20

The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he would not release Israel. 📖

## Exodus 10:21

The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand toward heaven. 📖

Darkness that can be felt will cover Egypt.”

## Exodus 10:22

Moses stretched his hand toward heaven. 📖

Total darkness covered all Egypt for three days.

## Exodus 10:23

No one could see anyone else or leave their place for three days. 📖

All Israelites had light in their homes.

## Exodus 10:24

Pharaoh called Moses, saying, “Go and worship the LORD. 📖

Even your little ones may go.

Only your flocks and herds must stay.”

## Exodus 10:25

Moses replied, “You must also provide sacrifices and burnt offerings for the LORD our God. 📖

**Help with “burnt offerings”:** These are offerings burned on an altar in worship.

## Exodus 10:26

Our livestock must go. 📖

Not a hoof will remain.

We need some animals to worship the LORD our God.

We will not know how to worship Him until we arrive.”

**Help with “not a hoof”:** This pictures not leaving even one animal behind.

## Exodus 10:27

The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he would not release them. 📖

## Exodus 10:28

Pharaoh told Moses, “Leave me! 📖

Never see my face again.

The day you see me, you will die.”

## Exodus 10:29

Moses replied, “As you say. 📖

I will never see your face again.”
