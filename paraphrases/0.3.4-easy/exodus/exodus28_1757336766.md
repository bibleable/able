# Exodus 28 — The priests’ clothing

**About this chapter**

The LORD appoints Aaron and his sons as priests.

He describes their special clothing and what it carries.

The names of the stones, the placement of the pieces, and the warnings about death are included.

## Exodus 28:1

“Bring your brother Aaron and his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar from Israel to serve Me as priests. 📖

## Exodus 28:2

Make holy garments for Aaron to give him glory and splendor. 📖

**Help with these words:** These are clothes set apart for God’s service.

Glory and splendor describe honor and impressive beauty.

## Exodus 28:3

Tell the skilled workers I filled with a spirit of wisdom to make Aaron’s garments. 📖

They are for setting him apart to serve Me as priest.

## Exodus 28:4

Make a breastpiece, ephod, robe, woven tunic, turban, and sash. 📖

Make these holy garments for Aaron and his sons to serve Me as priests.

**Help with these garments:** A breastpiece is worn over the chest.

An ephod is a priest’s special garment.

A tunic covers the body.

A turban wraps around the head, and a sash ties around the waist.

## Exodus 28:5

Use gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine linen. 📖

**Help with these words:** Scarlet means bright red.

Linen is cloth made from flax.

## Exodus 28:6

Make the ephod of finely spun linen, embroidered with gold and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 📖

**Help with “embroidered”:** This means decorated with stitched designs.

## Exodus 28:7

Attach two shoulder pieces at two corners so it can be fastened. 📖

## Exodus 28:8

Weave its waistband skillfully as one piece with it, using the same workmanship. 📖

Use gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen.

## Exodus 28:9

Take two onyx stones and engrave Israel’s sons’ names on them: 📖

**Help with these words:** Onyx is a valuable stone.

Engrave means cut writing or a design into a surface.

## Exodus 28:10

Put six names on one stone and the remaining six on the other. 📖

Follow their birth order.

## Exodus 28:11

Engrave the names as a gem cutter engraves a seal. 📖

Mount both stones in gold filigree settings.

**Help with these words:** A seal makes an identifying mark.

Filigree is delicate decorative metalwork.

Settings hold the stones.

## Exodus 28:12

Fasten them to the ephod’s shoulder pieces as memorial stones for Israel’s sons. 📖

Aaron will carry their names on both shoulders as a reminder before the LORD.

## Exodus 28:13

Make gold filigree settings 📖

## Exodus 28:14

and two pure gold chains like braided cords. 📖

Attach the chains to the settings.

## Exodus 28:15

Make a breastpiece of judgment with the ephod’s workmanship. 📖

Use gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen.

## Exodus 28:16

Fold it double into a square, one span long and one span wide. 📖

**Help with “span”:** This is a length based on a spread hand, from thumb to little finger.

## Exodus 28:17

Mount four rows of gemstones on it. 📖

The first row must have a ruby, a topaz, and an emerald.

## Exodus 28:18

The second must have a turquoise, a sapphire, and a diamond. 📖

## Exodus 28:19

The third must have a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst. 📖

## Exodus 28:20

The fourth must have a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper. 📖

Mount them in gold filigree settings.

**About these stones:** The four rows use the gemstone names given in BSB.

## Exodus 28:21

The twelve stones must match Israel’s sons’ names. 📖

Engrave each like a seal with one of the twelve tribes’ names.

## Exodus 28:22

Make braided chains of pure gold, like cords, for the breastpiece. 📖

## Exodus 28:23

Make two gold rings and fasten them at its two corners. 📖

## Exodus 28:24

Attach the two gold chains to those two rings, 📖

## Exodus 28:25

and their other ends to the two filigree settings on the ephod’s front shoulder pieces. 📖

## Exodus 28:26

Make two more gold rings. 📖

Attach them at the breastpiece’s other two corners, on its inside edge next to the ephod.

## Exodus 28:27

Make two additional gold rings for the bottoms of the ephod’s front shoulder pieces. 📖

Put them near the seam, just above the woven waistband.

## Exodus 28:28

Tie the breastpiece rings to the ephod rings with blue yarn cord. 📖

Keep the breastpiece above the waistband so it does not swing away from the ephod.

## Exodus 28:29

When Aaron enters the Holy Place, he must carry Israel’s sons’ names over his heart on the breastpiece of judgment. 📖

They are a constant reminder before the LORD.

## Exodus 28:30

Put the Urim and Thummim in it, over Aaron’s heart whenever he comes before the LORD. 📖

He must continually carry Israel’s sons’ judgment over his heart before the LORD.

**About these names:** BSB names the Urim and Thummim here without describing their appearance.

They are connected with judgment before God.

## Exodus 28:31

Make the ephod’s robe entirely of blue cloth. 📖

## Exodus 28:32

Make an opening in the middle of its top. 📖

Weave a collar around it, with an opening like a garment’s, so it will not tear.

## Exodus 28:33

Make blue, purple, and scarlet yarn pomegranates around the lower hem. 📖

Put gold bells between them.

**Help with “pomegranates”:** These are round fruits represented here in yarn.

## Exodus 28:34

Alternate a gold bell and a pomegranate all around the robe’s hem. 📖

## Exodus 28:35

Aaron must wear it when he serves. 📖

Its sound must be heard when he enters or leaves the sanctuary before the LORD, so he will not die.

## Exodus 28:36

Make a pure gold plate. 📖

Engrave it like a seal with HOLY TO THE LORD.

## Exodus 28:37

Use a blue cord to fasten it to the turban’s front. 📖

## Exodus 28:38

Aaron must wear it on his forehead to bear guilt connected with all Israel’s holy things and consecrated gifts. 📖

It must always be there so the gifts may be accepted before the LORD.

**Help with this instruction:** The plate is connected with guilt involving the people’s holy offerings.

It is part of Aaron’s priestly responsibility.

## Exodus 28:39

Weave a fine linen tunic and make a fine linen turban. 📖

Make an embroidered sash.

## Exodus 28:40

Make tunics, sashes, and headbands for Aaron’s sons, giving them glory and splendor. 📖

## Exodus 28:41

Dress your brother Aaron and his sons. 📖

Anoint, ordain, and consecrate them to serve Me as priests.

**Help with these words:** Anoint means apply special oil.

Ordain means appoint to this service.

Consecrate means set apart for God.

## Exodus 28:42

Make linen undergarments covering their bare flesh from waist to thigh. 📖

## Exodus 28:43

Aaron and his sons must wear them when entering the Tent of Meeting. 📖

They must also wear them when approaching the altar to serve in the Holy Place.

This prevents their incurring guilt and dying.

This is a permanent law for Aaron and his descendants.”
