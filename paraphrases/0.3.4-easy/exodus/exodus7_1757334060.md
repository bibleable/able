# Exodus 7 — The staff and the Nile

**About this chapter**

God sends Moses and Aaron to Pharaoh.

Aaron’s staff becomes a snake.

The Nile’s water turns to blood, but Pharaoh does not listen.

The actions of the magicians and the hardening of Pharaoh’s heart are retained.

## Exodus 7:1

The LORD told Moses, “I made you like God to Pharaoh. 📖

Your brother Aaron will be your prophet.

**Help with this comparison:** Moses receives God’s words and gives them to Aaron.

A prophet speaks a message given by God.

## Exodus 7:2

Say everything I command. 📖

Aaron must tell Pharaoh to let the Israelites leave his land.

## Exodus 7:3

I will harden Pharaoh’s heart. 📖

Although I increase My signs and wonders in Egypt,

**Help with “harden Pharaoh’s heart”:** This pictures making him stubborn and unwilling to listen.

Signs and wonders are extraordinary acts.

## Exodus 7:4

he will not listen. 📖

I will lay My hand on Egypt.

By mighty judgments, I will bring My people Israel’s divisions out of Egypt.

**Help with these pictures:** God’s hand pictures His power acting.

Divisions are organized groups of the people.

## Exodus 7:5

The Egyptians will know I am the LORD when I stretch My hand against Egypt and bring Israel out.” 📖

## Exodus 7:6

Moses and Aaron did exactly as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 7:7

Moses was eighty, and Aaron eighty-three, when they spoke to Pharaoh. 📖

## Exodus 7:8

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Exodus 7:9

“When Pharaoh asks for a miracle, tell Aaron to throw his staff down before Pharaoh. 📖

It will become a snake.”

## Exodus 7:10

They went to Pharaoh and obeyed the LORD. 📖

Aaron threw his staff down before Pharaoh and his officials.

It became a snake.

## Exodus 7:11

Pharaoh called Egypt’s wise men, sorcerers, and magicians. 📖

They did the same by their magic arts.

**Help with these words:** Sorcerers and magicians practice magic.

The account describes their acts without explaining how they did them.

## Exodus 7:12

Each threw down his staff, and it became a snake. 📖

Aaron’s staff swallowed the others’ staffs.

## Exodus 7:13

Pharaoh’s heart was hardened. 📖

He would not listen, just as the LORD said.

**Help with “heart was hardened”:** This pictures his stubborn refusal to listen.

## Exodus 7:14

The LORD told Moses, “Pharaoh’s heart will not yield. 📖

He refuses to release the people.

## Exodus 7:15

Meet him in the morning when he goes to the water. 📖

Wait on the Nile’s bank and carry the staff that became a snake.

## Exodus 7:16

Say, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, sent me to you. 📖

He says: Release My people to worship Me in the wilderness.

Until now, you have not listened.

## Exodus 7:17

The LORD says: This will show you I am the LORD. 📖

With the staff in my hand, I will strike the Nile’s water.

It will become blood.

## Exodus 7:18

The fish will die, the river will stink, and the Egyptians cannot drink its water.’” 📖

## Exodus 7:19

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to take his staff and stretch his hand over Egypt’s waters. 📖

Its rivers, canals, ponds, and all reservoirs will become blood.

Blood will be throughout Egypt, even in wooden and stone containers.”

**Help with these words:** Canals are channels made to carry water.

Reservoirs are places where water is stored.

## Exodus 7:20

Moses and Aaron obeyed the LORD. 📖

Before Pharaoh and his officials, Aaron raised his staff and struck the Nile.

All its water became blood.

## Exodus 7:21

The fish died, and the river stank so badly the Egyptians could not drink its water. 📖

Blood was throughout Egypt.

## Exodus 7:22

Egypt’s magicians did the same by their magic arts. 📖

Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he ignored Moses and Aaron, as the LORD said.

## Exodus 7:23

Pharaoh turned away, entered his palace, and paid no attention to this. 📖

## Exodus 7:24

All the Egyptians dug around the Nile for drinking water, because they could not drink the river water. 📖

## Exodus 7:25

Seven full days passed after the LORD struck the Nile. 📖
