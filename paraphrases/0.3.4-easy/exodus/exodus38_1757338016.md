# Exodus 38 — The altar, courtyard, and material totals

**About this chapter**

The work continues with the altar, washing basin, and courtyard.

The chapter then records the gold, silver, and bronze used.

The amounts remain in the source’s units.

## Exodus 38:1

Bezalel made the burnt-offering altar from acacia wood. 📖

It was square, five cubits long, five wide, and three high.

**Help with these words:** Acacia is a kind of tree.

A cubit is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Exodus 38:2

He made a horn at each of its four corners, one piece with the altar. 📖

He covered it with bronze.

## Exodus 38:3

All its tools were bronze: pots, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans. 📖

## Exodus 38:4

He made a bronze mesh grate beneath the ledge, halfway up from the bottom. 📖

**Help with these words:** Mesh is a network with spaces between its strands.

A grate is an open frame supporting what is burned.

## Exodus 38:5

He cast four rings at the grate’s four corners to hold the poles. 📖

## Exodus 38:6

He made acacia poles covered with bronze. 📖

## Exodus 38:7

He put them through the side rings to carry the altar. 📖

He made it hollow with boards.

## Exodus 38:8

He made the bronze basin and stand from mirrors belonging to women serving at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Exodus 38:9

He made the courtyard’s south side a hundred cubits long, with finely spun linen curtains, 📖

## Exodus 38:10

with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. 📖

The posts’ hooks and bands were silver.

## Exodus 38:11

The north side was a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. 📖

Its hooks and bands were silver.

## Exodus 38:12

The west side was fifty cubits long, with curtains, ten posts, and ten bases. 📖

Its hooks and bands were silver.

## Exodus 38:13

The east side, toward sunrise, was fifty cubits long. 📖

## Exodus 38:14

Curtains on one side of the entrance were fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 📖

## Exodus 38:15

Curtains on the other side were fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 📖

## Exodus 38:16

All the courtyard curtains were finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 38:17

The posts had bronze bases, silver hooks and bands, and silver plating on top. 📖

All courtyard posts were banded with silver.

## Exodus 38:18

The entrance curtain was finely spun linen, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 📖

It was twenty cubits long and five high, like the courtyard curtains.

**Help with these words:** Scarlet means bright red.

Embroidered means decorated with stitched designs.

## Exodus 38:19

It had four posts and four bronze bases. 📖

Their hooks, bands, and top plating were silver.

## Exodus 38:20

All the tabernacle’s and surrounding courtyard’s tent pegs were bronze. 📖

## Exodus 38:21

This is the inventory of the tabernacle of the Testimony. 📖

Moses ordered it recorded by the Levites under Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.

**Help with “inventory”:** This is a record of materials and items.

## Exodus 38:22

Bezalel, Uri’s son and Hur’s grandson from Judah’s tribe, made everything the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 38:23

His coworker was Oholiab, Ahisamach’s son from Dan’s tribe. 📖

He engraved, designed, and embroidered blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen.

## Exodus 38:24

The sanctuary work used 29 talents and 730 shekels of gold from the wave offering. 📖

These used the sanctuary shekel.

**Help with these units:** Talents and shekels are units of weight.

## Exodus 38:25

The silver from the people counted totaled 100 talents and 1,775 sanctuary shekels, 📖

## Exodus 38:26

one beka each, meaning half a sanctuary shekel. 📖

This came from 603,550 men counted, each twenty or older.

## Exodus 38:27

The hundred talents made the sanctuary and veil bases. 📖

There were 100 bases from 100 talents, one talent each.

## Exodus 38:28

The 1,775 silver shekels made the posts’ hooks, top coverings, and bands. 📖

## Exodus 38:29

The wave offering’s bronze totaled 70 talents and 2,400 shekels. 📖

## Exodus 38:30

It made the Tent of Meeting’s entrance bases, bronze altar, bronze grate, and all altar tools, 📖

## Exodus 38:31

and the bases of the courtyard and its gate. 📖

It also made all the tabernacle’s and surrounding courtyard’s tent pegs.
