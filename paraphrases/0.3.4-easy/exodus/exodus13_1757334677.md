# Exodus 13 — Remembering the rescue and following God

**About this chapter**

The people must remember their rescue and set apart the firstborn.

Moses explains the practices to be taught to their children.

God leads the people by cloud and fire.

## Exodus 13:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 13:2

“Set apart for Me every firstborn male. 📖

Among Israel, the firstborn from every womb belongs to Me, human or animal.”

**Help with “womb”:** This is the part of a female body where a baby develops before birth.

## Exodus 13:3

Moses told the people, “Remember this day when you left Egypt’s house of slavery. 📖

The LORD brought you out by His strong hand.

Eat nothing leavened.

**Help with these words:** God’s hand pictures His power.

Leaven, such as yeast, makes dough rise.

## Exodus 13:4

Today, in the month of Abib, you are leaving. 📖

## Exodus 13:5

The LORD will bring you to the Canaanites’, Hittites’, Amorites’, Hivites’, and Jebusites’ land. 📖

He swore to your fathers to give you this land flowing with milk and honey.

When He brings you there, keep this service in this month.

**Help with “flowing with milk and honey”:** This pictures plentiful good food.

## Exodus 13:6

Eat unleavened bread for seven days. 📖

On the seventh day, hold a feast to the LORD.

## Exodus 13:7

Eat unleavened bread those seven days. 📖

No leavened food or leaven may be found anywhere within your borders.

## Exodus 13:8

That day, tell your son, ‘This is because of what the LORD did for me when I left Egypt.’ 📖

## Exodus 13:9

It will be a sign on your hand and a reminder on your forehead. 📖

The LORD’s Law must be on your lips.

He brought you out of Egypt with a mighty hand.

**Help with these words:** The sign and reminder keep the LORD’s rescue in view.

Law on the lips describes speaking His teaching.

The mighty hand pictures His power.

## Exodus 13:10

Keep this law at the appointed time every year. 📖

## Exodus 13:11

The LORD will bring you into the Canaanites’ land and give it to you, as He swore to your fathers. 📖

He swore this to you too.

## Exodus 13:12

Then present to the LORD the firstborn male from every womb. 📖

Every firstborn male among your livestock belongs to Him.

## Exodus 13:13

Redeem every firstborn donkey with a lamb. 📖

If you do not, break its neck.

Redeem every firstborn son.

**Help with “redeem”:** This means secure release by giving something in exchange.

A lamb is given in place of the donkey.

The sons are redeemed, not sacrificed.

## Exodus 13:14

When your son asks in the future, ‘What does this mean?’ give this answer. 📖

‘The LORD’s mighty hand brought us out of Egypt’s house of slavery.

## Exodus 13:15

Pharaoh stubbornly refused to release us. 📖

The LORD killed every firstborn in Egypt, human and animal.

That is why I sacrifice the firstborn male of every womb to the LORD.

But I redeem all my firstborn sons.’

## Exodus 13:16

It will be a sign on your hand and a symbol on your forehead. 📖

The LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.”

**Help with “mighty hand”:** This pictures God’s power.

## Exodus 13:17

When Pharaoh released them, God avoided the shorter road through Philistine land. 📖

God said, “If they face war, they might change their minds and return to Egypt.”

## Exodus 13:18

He led them around through the wilderness toward the Red Sea. 📖

The Israelites left Egypt organized for battle.

## Exodus 13:19

Moses took Joseph’s bones, because Joseph made Israel’s sons swear a solemn oath. 📖

Joseph said, “God will surely attend to you.

Then carry my bones with you from here.”

## Exodus 13:20

They left Succoth and camped at Etham, at the wilderness’s edge. 📖

## Exodus 13:21

The LORD went ahead in a pillar of cloud by day to guide them. 📖

By night, He gave light in a pillar of fire.

They could travel by day or night.

**Help with “pillar”:** This means an upright column.

## Exodus 13:22

The cloud pillar by day and the fire pillar by night stayed before the people. 📖
