# Exodus 36 — The tabernacle is made

**About this chapter**

The workers receive more than enough materials.

Moses tells the people to stop bringing offerings.

The account then describes the curtains, coverings, and frames.

## Exodus 36:1

“Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person must do everything the LORD commanded. 📖

He gave them skill and understanding to build the sanctuary.”

## Exodus 36:2

Moses called Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person the LORD gifted. 📖

He called everyone willing to do the work.

## Exodus 36:3

They received all Israel’s offerings from Moses for building the sanctuary. 📖

The people kept bringing freewill offerings every morning,

## Exodus 36:4

so all the skilled workers left their sanctuary work 📖

## Exodus 36:5

and told Moses, “They bring more than enough for everything the LORD commanded.” 📖

## Exodus 36:6

Moses ordered a message throughout camp. 📖

“No man or woman should make anything more for a sanctuary offering.”

The people stopped bringing more,

## Exodus 36:7

because they had more than enough for all the work. 📖

## Exodus 36:8

The skilled workers made ten tabernacle curtains of finely spun linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 📖

Cherubim were skillfully worked into them.

**Help with these words:** Scarlet means bright red.

Linen is cloth made from flax.

Cherubim are winged beings represented in the design.

## Exodus 36:9

All curtains were twenty-eight cubits long and four wide, the same size. 📖

**Help with “cubit”:** This is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Exodus 36:10

The worker joined five curtains into one set and five into another. 📖

## Exodus 36:11

He made blue loops along the end curtain’s edge in each set. 📖

## Exodus 36:12

He made fifty loops on one curtain and fifty on the second set’s end curtain. 📖

They lined up opposite one another.

## Exodus 36:13

He made fifty gold clasps to join the curtains as one tabernacle. 📖

**Help with “clasps”:** These are fasteners that join things together.

## Exodus 36:14

He made eleven goat-hair curtains for the tent over it. 📖

## Exodus 36:15

All eleven were thirty cubits long and four wide. 📖

## Exodus 36:16

He joined five into one set and six into another. 📖

## Exodus 36:17

He made fifty loops on the first set’s end edge and fifty on the second set’s matching edge. 📖

## Exodus 36:18

He made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent as one unit. 📖

## Exodus 36:19

He made a red-dyed ram-skin covering, with a fine leather covering above it. 📖

## Exodus 36:20

He made upright acacia frames for the tabernacle. 📖

**Help with “acacia”:** This is a kind of tree.

## Exodus 36:21

Each was ten cubits long and one and a half wide. 📖

## Exodus 36:22

Each had two tenons connected to each other. 📖

All the frames were made this way.

**Help with “tenons”:** These are projecting pieces that fit wooden parts together.

## Exodus 36:23

He made twenty frames for the south side, 📖

## Exodus 36:24

with forty silver bases beneath them. 📖

Each frame had two bases, one for each tenon.

## Exodus 36:25

He made twenty frames for the second side, the north, 📖

## Exodus 36:26

and forty silver bases, two per frame. 📖

## Exodus 36:27

He made six frames for the back, the west side, 📖

## Exodus 36:28

and two frames for the two back corners, 📖

## Exodus 36:29

joined from bottom to top and fitted into one ring. 📖

Both corners were made this way.

## Exodus 36:30

There were eight frames and sixteen silver bases, two beneath each. 📖

## Exodus 36:31

He made five acacia crossbars for one side’s frames, 📖

## Exodus 36:32

five for the other side, and five for the west side at the back. 📖

## Exodus 36:33

The middle crossbar ran through the frames’ center from end to end. 📖

## Exodus 36:34

He covered the frames with gold and made gold rings to hold the crossbars. 📖

He covered the bars with gold too.

## Exodus 36:35

He made the veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. 📖

Cherubim were skillfully worked into it.

## Exodus 36:36

He made four acacia posts covered with gold, with gold hooks. 📖

He cast four silver bases for them.

**Help with “cast”:** This means shape metal by pouring it into a mold.

## Exodus 36:37

He made the tent’s entrance curtain of finely spun linen, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, 📖

## Exodus 36:38

with five posts and their hooks. 📖

He covered their tops and bands with gold.

Their five bases were bronze.
