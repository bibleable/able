# Exodus 31 — Skilled workers and the Sabbath

**About this chapter**

The LORD appoints workers for the sanctuary and its furnishings.

He renews the Sabbath command, including its severe penalties.

Moses receives the stone tablets.

## Exodus 31:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 31:2

“I called Bezalel by name. 📖

He is Uri’s son, Hur’s grandson, from Judah’s tribe.

## Exodus 31:3

I filled him with God’s Spirit, skill, ability, and knowledge in every kind of craft, 📖

## Exodus 31:4

for artistic designs in gold, silver, and bronze, 📖

## Exodus 31:5

for cutting gemstones for settings, and for carving wood. 📖

He can master every craft.

## Exodus 31:6

I chose Oholiab, Ahisamach’s son from Dan’s tribe, as his assistant. 📖

I gave skill to all the workers to make everything I commanded:

## Exodus 31:7

The Tent of Meeting, the ark of the Testimony and its mercy seat, and all the tent’s furnishings; 📖

## Exodus 31:8

The table and its tools, the pure gold lampstand and its tools, and the incense altar; 📖

## Exodus 31:9

The burnt-offering altar and its tools, and the washing basin and stand; 📖

## Exodus 31:10

The woven garments, both Aaron’s holy priestly garments and his sons’ garments for serving as priests; 📖

## Exodus 31:11

The anointing oil and fragrant incense for the Holy Place. 📖

They must make everything as I commanded.”

## Exodus 31:12

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 31:13

“Tell Israel, ‘Keep My Sabbaths. 📖

They are a sign between Me and you through coming generations.

You will know I am the LORD who sanctifies you.

**Help with “sanctifies”:** This means sets apart as holy.

## Exodus 31:14

Keep the Sabbath as holy. 📖

Anyone treating it as unholy must be put to death.

Anyone working that day must be cut off from his people.

## Exodus 31:15

Work may be done six days. 📖

The seventh is complete Sabbath rest, holy to the LORD.

Anyone working that day must be put to death.

## Exodus 31:16

Israel must keep and celebrate the Sabbath as a permanent covenant through coming generations. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement or promise.

## Exodus 31:17

It is a sign between Me and Israel forever. 📖

The LORD made heaven and earth in six days.

On the seventh, He rested and was refreshed.’”

## Exodus 31:18

When the LORD finished speaking to Moses on Mount Sinai, He gave him the two Testimony tablets. 📖

They were stone, written by God’s finger.

**Help with “tablets”:** These are flat pieces of stone used for writing.
