# Exodus 18 — Jethro visits and advises Moses

**About this chapter**

Jethro brings Moses’ wife and sons to him.

He hears about the rescue from Egypt and praises the LORD.

He advises Moses to share the work of judging disputes.

## Exodus 18:1

Jethro, Moses’ father-in-law and Midian’s priest, heard everything God did for Moses and Israel. 📖

He heard how the LORD brought Israel from Egypt.

## Exodus 18:2

After Moses sent his wife Zipporah back, Jethro received her 📖

## Exodus 18:3

and her two sons. 📖

Gershom was named because Moses said, “I have been a foreigner in a foreign land.”

## Exodus 18:4

Eliezer was named because Moses said, “My father’s God helped me and rescued me from Pharaoh’s sword.” 📖

## Exodus 18:5

Jethro came with Moses’ wife and sons to his camp in the wilderness at God’s mountain. 📖

## Exodus 18:6

He sent word, “Your father-in-law Jethro is coming with your wife and her two sons.” 📖

## Exodus 18:7

Moses went out, bowed, and kissed his father-in-law. 📖

They greeted each other and entered the tent.

## Exodus 18:8

Moses told him everything the LORD did to Pharaoh and Egypt for Israel. 📖

He told of every hardship along the way and the LORD’s rescues.

## Exodus 18:9

Jethro rejoiced at all the LORD’s goodness in rescuing Israel from Egyptian power. 📖

## Exodus 18:10

He said, “Blessed be the LORD! 📖

He rescued you from the power of Egypt and Pharaoh.

He rescued the people from Egyptian power.

## Exodus 18:11

Now I know the LORD is greater than all other gods. 📖

He did this when they treated Israel with arrogance.”

**Help with “arrogance”:** This means acting with pride and looking down on others.

## Exodus 18:12

Jethro brought a burnt offering and sacrifices to God. 📖

Aaron and all Israel’s elders ate bread with him before God.

**Help with “burnt offering”:** This is an offering burned on an altar in worship.

## Exodus 18:13

The next day, Moses sat as judge. 📖

The people stood around him from morning to evening.

## Exodus 18:14

Jethro saw all his work and asked, “What are you doing for the people? 📖

Why judge alone while everyone stands around all day?”

## Exodus 18:15

Moses replied, “The people come to ask God’s will. 📖

## Exodus 18:16

When they have disputes, they bring them to me. 📖

I judge between people and teach God’s laws and rulings.”

## Exodus 18:17

Jethro said, “This is not good. 📖

## Exodus 18:18

You and these people will wear yourselves out. 📖

The task is too heavy for you alone.

## Exodus 18:19

Listen to my advice, and may God be with you. 📖

Represent the people before God and bring their cases to Him.

## Exodus 18:20

Teach His laws and rulings. 📖

Show them how to live and what work to do.

## Exodus 18:21

Choose capable men who fear God, are trustworthy, and hate dishonest gain. 📖

Appoint them over thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.

**Help with “dishonest gain”:** This means getting money or goods by wrongdoing.

## Exodus 18:22

Let them judge at all times. 📖

They can bring major issues to you and decide minor ones themselves.

Sharing the work will lighten your load.

## Exodus 18:23

If you do this and God directs you, you can endure. 📖

All these people can go home in peace.”

## Exodus 18:24

Moses listened and did everything Jethro said. 📖

## Exodus 18:25

He chose capable men from all Israel. 📖

He appointed them as leaders over thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.

## Exodus 18:26

They judged at all times, bringing difficult cases to Moses and deciding minor cases themselves. 📖

## Exodus 18:27

Moses sent his father-in-law home. 📖

Jethro returned to his own land.
