# Exodus 32 — The golden calf

**About this chapter**

The people ask Aaron to make gods while Moses is on the mountain.

The LORD speaks of judgment, and Moses pleads for the people.

The chapter includes broken tablets, deaths, and a plague.

## Exodus 32:1

Moses was slow to come down, so the people gathered around Aaron. 📖

They said, “Make gods to go before us.

We do not know what happened to Moses, who brought us from Egypt.”

## Exodus 32:2

Aaron said, “Bring me the gold earrings your wives, sons, and daughters wear.” 📖

## Exodus 32:3

Everyone took off their gold earrings and brought them to Aaron. 📖

## Exodus 32:4

He took the gold and shaped a cast calf with an engraving tool. 📖

They said, “Israel, these are your gods who brought you from Egypt!”

**Help with “cast calf”:** This is a figure of a young cow made from melted metal.

## Exodus 32:5

Aaron saw this and built an altar before it. 📖

He announced, “Tomorrow is a feast to the LORD.”

## Exodus 32:6

The next day, they rose, offered burnt offerings, and presented peace offerings. 📖

They sat to eat and drink, then rose for wild celebration.

## Exodus 32:7

The LORD told Moses, “Go down now. 📖

Your people, whom you brought from Egypt, have corrupted themselves.

## Exodus 32:8

They quickly left My commanded way. 📖

They made a cast calf, bowed to it, and sacrificed to it.

They said, ‘Israel, these are your gods who brought you from Egypt!’”

## Exodus 32:9

The LORD added, “I have seen them. 📖

They are stiff-necked.

**Help with “stiff-necked”:** This pictures stubborn refusal to obey.

## Exodus 32:10

Leave Me alone so My anger burns against them and consumes them. 📖

I will make you a great nation.”

## Exodus 32:11

Moses pleaded for the LORD his God’s favor. 📖

He said, “O LORD, why burn with anger against Your people?

You brought them from Egypt with great power and a mighty hand.

## Exodus 32:12

Why should Egyptians say, ‘He brought them out with evil intent. 📖

He meant to kill them in the mountains and wipe them from earth’?

Turn from Your fierce anger and relent from harming Your people.

## Exodus 32:13

Remember Your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Israel and Your oath by Your very self. 📖

You promised descendants as many as sky stars.

You promised them all this land as an inheritance forever.”

## Exodus 32:14

The LORD relented from the disaster He threatened against His people. 📖

## Exodus 32:15

Moses went down with the two Testimony tablets in his hands. 📖

Both were written on front and back.

## Exodus 32:16

God made the tablets, and God’s own writing was engraved on them. 📖

## Exodus 32:17

Joshua heard the shouting and told Moses, “It sounds like war in camp.” 📖

## Exodus 32:18

Moses replied, “It is neither a victory cry nor a defeat cry. 📖

I hear singing!”

## Exodus 32:19

Near camp, Moses saw the calf and dancing. 📖

His anger burned, and he threw down the tablets, breaking them at the mountain’s base.

## Exodus 32:20

He burned the calf, ground it into powder, and scattered it over the water. 📖

He forced Israel to drink it.

## Exodus 32:21

Moses asked Aaron, “What did they do to you that you led them into such great sin?” 📖

## Exodus 32:22

Aaron replied, “Do not be angry, my lord. 📖

You know they intend evil.

## Exodus 32:23

They said, ‘Make gods to go before us. 📖

We do not know what happened to Moses, who brought us from Egypt.’

## Exodus 32:24

I told anyone with gold to take it off. 📖

They gave it to me.

I threw it in the fire, and out came this calf!”

**About Aaron’s answer:** This is his explanation.

Verse 4 describes him shaping the calf with a tool.

## Exodus 32:25

Moses saw they were out of control. 📖

Aaron had let them run wild, making them a laughingstock to their enemies.

## Exodus 32:26

Moses stood at the camp entrance. 📖

He said, “Whoever is for the LORD, come to me.”

All the Levites gathered.

## Exodus 32:27

He said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘Fasten your swords at your sides. 📖

Go back and forth from gate to gate through camp.

Kill your brother, friend, and neighbor.’”

## Exodus 32:28

The Levites obeyed Moses. 📖

About three thousand people died that day.

## Exodus 32:29

Moses said, “Today you were ordained for the LORD’s service, each going against his son and brother. 📖

The LORD blessed you today.”

**Help with “ordained”:** This means appointed to this service.

## Exodus 32:30

The next day, Moses said, “You committed a great sin. 📖

I will go to the LORD.

Perhaps I can make atonement for you.”

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Exodus 32:31

He returned to the LORD, saying, “These people committed a great sin. 📖

They made gold gods.

## Exodus 32:32

If only You would forgive their sin... 📖

If not, please erase me from the book You wrote.”

**About the book:** The source does not identify this book more fully here.

## Exodus 32:33

The LORD replied, “I will erase from My book whoever sinned against Me. 📖

## Exodus 32:34

Lead them to the place I described. 📖

My angel will go ahead.

When I settle accounts, I will punish their sin.”

**Help with “settle accounts”:** This pictures a time of judgment.

## Exodus 32:35

The LORD sent a plague because of what they did with the calf Aaron made. 📖
