# Exodus 15 — The song and the bitter water

**About this chapter**

Moses and Israel sing to the LORD after the sea crossing.

Miriam leads the women in song and dancing.

Later, the people face bitter water and hear a conditional promise.

## Exodus 15:1

Moses and Israel sang to the LORD, “I will sing to the LORD, for He is highly exalted. 📖

He threw horse and rider into the sea.

**Help with “exalted”:** This means raised high in honor.

## Exodus 15:2

The LORD is my strength and song. 📖

He became my salvation.

He is my God, and I will praise Him.

He is my father’s God, and I will exalt Him.

**Help with “salvation”:** Here this means rescue.

## Exodus 15:3

The LORD is a warrior. 📖

His name is the LORD.

## Exodus 15:4

He threw Pharaoh’s chariots and army into the sea. 📖

Pharaoh’s finest officers drowned in the Red Sea.

## Exodus 15:5

Deep waters covered them. 📖

They sank like a stone.

## Exodus 15:6

Your right hand, O LORD, is majestic in power. 📖

Your right hand shattered the enemy.

**Help with these words:** Majestic means great and splendid.

The right hand pictures God’s power acting.

## Exodus 15:7

Your great majesty overthrew Your enemies. 📖

You unleashed burning anger that consumed them like stubble.

**Help with this picture:** Stubble is dry stalks left after harvest.

The song pictures judgment like a fire consuming them.

## Exodus 15:8

The blast from Your nostrils piled up the waters. 📖

Currents stood like a wall.

The depths became firm in the sea’s heart.

**Help with these pictures:** Nostrils are the openings of the nose.

The song pictures God’s breath driving the water.

The sea’s heart means its middle.

## Exodus 15:9

The enemy said, ‘I will chase and catch them. 📖

I will divide the captured goods and fill myself with them.

I will draw my sword and destroy them with my hand.’

## Exodus 15:10

You blew with Your breath, and the sea covered them. 📖

They sank like lead in mighty waters.

**Help with “lead”:** This is a heavy metal.

## Exodus 15:11

Who among the gods is like You, O LORD? 📖

Who is majestic in holiness like You, feared and praised, doing wonders?

**Help with “holiness”:** This describes God as pure and set apart from all others.

## Exodus 15:12

You stretched Your right hand, and the earth swallowed them. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The song describes their complete destruction.

## Exodus 15:13

Your faithful love will lead the people You redeemed. 📖

Your strength will guide them to Your holy home.

**Help with “redeemed”:** This means set free or recovered.

## Exodus 15:14

Nations will hear and tremble. 📖

Great distress will grip Philistia’s people.

## Exodus 15:15

Edom’s chiefs will be dismayed. 📖

Moab’s leaders will shake with fear.

Canaan’s people will melt away,

**Help with “melt away”:** This pictures losing their strength through fear.

## Exodus 15:16

overcome by terror and dread. 📖

Your arm’s power will make them still as stone until Your people pass, O LORD.

The people You bought will pass.

**Help with these pictures:** God’s arm pictures His power.

The song pictures enemies unable to move and the people as bought free.

## Exodus 15:17

You will bring them and plant them on the mountain of Your inheritance. 📖

You prepared that place as Your home, O LORD.

Your hands established the sanctuary, O Lord.

**Help with these words:** Planting pictures settling the people securely.

A sanctuary is a holy place for God’s presence and worship.

## Exodus 15:18

The LORD will reign forever and ever!” 📖

## Exodus 15:19

Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, and horsemen entered the sea. 📖

The LORD brought the water over them.

Israel walked through on dry ground.

## Exodus 15:20

Miriam the prophetess, Aaron’s sister, took a tambourine. 📖

All the women followed, playing tambourines and dancing.

**Help with these words:** A prophetess is a woman who speaks a message from God.

A tambourine is a small hand-held musical instrument.

## Exodus 15:21

Miriam sang back, “Sing to the LORD, for He is highly exalted. 📖

He threw horse and rider into the sea.”

## Exodus 15:22

Moses led Israel from the Red Sea into the Desert of Shur. 📖

They walked three days without finding water.

## Exodus 15:23

At Marah, the water was too bitter to drink. 📖

That is why the place was named Marah.

## Exodus 15:24

The people complained against Moses, asking, “What can we drink?” 📖

## Exodus 15:25

Moses cried to the LORD, who showed him a log. 📖

Moses threw it into the water, and the water became sweet.

There the LORD gave them a law and ruling and tested them.

## Exodus 15:26

He said, “Listen carefully to the LORD your God. 📖

Do what He sees as right.

Pay attention to His commands and keep all His laws.

If you do, I will not bring on you the diseases I brought on Egypt.

I am the LORD who heals you.”

## Exodus 15:27

They came to Elim, with twelve water springs and seventy palm trees. 📖

They camped beside the water.
