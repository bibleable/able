# Exodus 22 — Laws about property and treatment of others

**About this chapter**

The laws continue with theft, losses, borrowing, and marriage.

They include death penalties and protections for foreigners, widows, orphans, and poor people.

The LORD also gives commands about offerings.

## Exodus 22:1

“If a man steals an ox or sheep and kills or sells it, he must repay more animals. 📖

Repay five oxen for one ox, or four sheep for one sheep.

## Exodus 22:2

If a thief is caught breaking in and beaten to death, no one is guilty of bloodshed. 📖

## Exodus 22:3

If this happens after sunrise, there is guilt for his bloodshed. 📖

A thief must repay in full.

If he has nothing, sell him for his theft.

## Exodus 22:4

If a stolen ox, donkey, or sheep is found alive with him, he must repay double. 📖

## Exodus 22:5

If a man lets livestock stray while grazing a field or vineyard, they may eat another person’s field. 📖

If so, he must repay from the best of his own field or vineyard.

## Exodus 22:6

If someone’s fire spreads through thornbushes and burns stacked grain, standing grain, or a field, he must repay in full. 📖

## Exodus 22:7

If money or goods left with a neighbor are stolen from his house, the thief must repay double if caught. 📖

## Exodus 22:8

If no thief is found, the householder must come before the judges. 📖

They must decide whether he took his neighbor’s property.

## Exodus 22:9

Both sides must bring disputed possession cases before judges. 📖

This includes ox, donkey, sheep, clothing, or any lost item someone claims as theirs.

The person found guilty must pay the neighbor double.

## Exodus 22:10

An animal left in a neighbor’s care may die, be injured, or be stolen while no one watches. 📖

This applies to donkeys, oxen, sheep, or any other animal.

## Exodus 22:11

They must swear before the LORD about whether the keeper took his neighbor’s property. 📖

The owner must accept the oath and require no repayment.

## Exodus 22:12

But if the animal was actually stolen from the neighbor, he must repay its owner. 📖

## Exodus 22:13

If it was torn apart, bring it as evidence. 📖

No repayment is required for the torn body.

## Exodus 22:14

If a borrowed animal is injured or dies while its owner is absent, the borrower must repay in full. 📖

## Exodus 22:15

If the owner was present, no repayment is required. 📖

If it was rented, the fee covers the loss.

## Exodus 22:16

If a man seduces a virgin not promised in marriage and has sex with her, he must pay. 📖

He must pay her full dowry for her to become his wife.

**Help with these words:** Seduces means persuades or entices someone into sexual activity.

A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

The dowry here is a marriage payment.

## Exodus 22:17

If her father firmly refuses to give her, the man must still pay the usual marriage price for a virgin. 📖

## Exodus 22:18

You must not allow a sorceress to live. 📖

**Help with “sorceress”:** This means a woman who practices magic.

## Exodus 22:19

Anyone who has sex with an animal must be put to death. 📖

## Exodus 22:20

Anyone sacrificing to any god except the LORD alone must be set apart for destruction. 📖

## Exodus 22:21

Do not exploit or oppress a foreign resident. 📖

You were foreigners in Egypt.

**Help with these words:** Exploit means take unfair advantage of someone.

Oppress means use power to treat someone cruelly.

## Exodus 22:22

Do not mistreat any widow or orphan. 📖

## Exodus 22:23

If you mistreat them and they cry to Me in distress, I will hear. 📖

## Exodus 22:24

My anger will burn, and I will kill you with the sword. 📖

Your wives will be widows and your children fatherless.

## Exodus 22:25

If you lend money to a poor person among My people, do not act like a moneylender. 📖

Charge him no interest.

**Help with “interest”:** This is an extra amount charged on a loan.

## Exodus 22:26

If you take your neighbor’s cloak as security for a debt, return it by sunset, 📖

## Exodus 22:27

because it is his only body covering. 📖

What else will he sleep in?

If he cries to Me, I will hear, because I am compassionate.

**Help with “compassionate”:** This means caring about suffering and showing mercy.

## Exodus 22:28

Do not blaspheme God or curse your people’s ruler. 📖

**Help with “blaspheme”:** This means speak abusively or disrespectfully about God.

## Exodus 22:29

Do not withhold offerings from your grain stores or vats. 📖

Give Me your firstborn sons.

**Help with these words:** Vats are large containers, such as for wine or oil.

Earlier instructions require firstborn sons to be redeemed, not sacrificed (Exodus 13:13).

## Exodus 22:30

Do the same with cattle and sheep. 📖

Leave them with their mothers seven days.

On day eight, give them to Me.

## Exodus 22:31

Be My holy people. 📖

Do not eat meat from an animal torn by beasts in the field.

Throw it to the dogs.”

**Help with “holy”:** This means set apart for God.
