# Exodus 23 — Justice, worship, and the journey ahead

**About this chapter**

The LORD commands justice and care, including care for enemies’ animals.

He gives rules for rest and feasts.

He promises to send an angel ahead and warns Israel about other gods.

These instructions and promises are addressed to Israel on its journey from Egypt.

## Exodus 23:1

“Do not spread false reports. 📖

Do not help the wicked by giving harmful false testimony.

## Exodus 23:2

Do not follow a crowd into wrongdoing. 📖

In a lawsuit, do not twist justice by siding with the crowd.

## Exodus 23:3

Do not favor a poor man in his lawsuit. 📖

## Exodus 23:4

If you find your enemy’s ox or donkey wandering, return it. 📖

## Exodus 23:5

If someone who hates you has a donkey fallen under its load, do not abandon it. 📖

Help him with it.

## Exodus 23:6

Do not deny poor people justice in their lawsuits. 📖

## Exodus 23:7

Keep far from false accusations. 📖

Do not kill innocent or just people.

I will not declare the guilty innocent.

## Exodus 23:8

Do not accept bribes. 📖

They blind those who see and twist righteous people’s words.

**Help with these words:** A bribe is a gift offered to influence a decision unfairly.

“Blind” here pictures judgment being corrupted.

## Exodus 23:9

Do not oppress a foreign resident. 📖

You know how foreigners feel, because you were foreigners in Egypt.

## Exodus 23:10

Sow your land and gather its produce for six years, 📖

## Exodus 23:11

but leave it resting and unplanted in year seven. 📖

Poor people may eat from it, and wild animals may eat what they leave.

Do the same with vineyards and olive groves.

## Exodus 23:12

Work six days, but stop on day seven. 📖

Your ox and donkey can rest.

Your female servant’s son and the foreign resident can be refreshed.

## Exodus 23:13

Pay attention to everything I told you. 📖

Do not call on other gods’ names.

They must not be heard on your lips.

## Exodus 23:14

Celebrate a feast to Me three times each year. 📖

## Exodus 23:15

Keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread as I commanded. 📖

Eat unleavened bread seven days at the appointed time in Abib, the month you left Egypt.

No one may appear before Me with empty hands.

## Exodus 23:16

Keep the Feast of Harvest with the first produce of what you sow. 📖

Keep the Feast of Ingathering at year’s end, when you gather field produce.

**Help with these words:** These feasts mark gathering the crops.

The first produce is the first part of the harvest.

## Exodus 23:17

All your males must appear before the Lord GOD three times each year. 📖

## Exodus 23:18

Do not offer My sacrifices’ blood with anything leavened. 📖

Do not leave My feast’s fat until morning.

**Help with “leavened”:** This means containing something, such as yeast, that makes dough rise.

## Exodus 23:19

Bring your soil’s best first produce to the LORD your God’s house. 📖

Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.

## Exodus 23:20

I send an angel ahead to protect your journey and bring you to the place I prepared. 📖

## Exodus 23:21

Pay attention and obey him. 📖

Do not defy him.

He will not forgive rebellion, because My Name is in him.

## Exodus 23:22

If you carefully obey his voice and everything I say, I will oppose your enemies and foes. 📖

## Exodus 23:23

My angel will lead you into the Amorites’, Hittites’, Perizzites’, Canaanites’, Hivites’, and Jebusites’ land. 📖

I will annihilate them.

**Help with “annihilate”:** This means destroy completely.

## Exodus 23:24

Do not bow to their gods, serve them, or follow their practices. 📖

Demolish them and smash their sacred stones.

**Help with “sacred stones”:** These are stones set up for worship.

## Exodus 23:25

Serve the LORD your God. 📖

He will bless your bread and water.

I will remove sickness from among you.

## Exodus 23:26

No woman in your land will miscarry or be unable to bear children. 📖

I will fulfill the number of your days.

**Help with these words:** Miscarry means lose a pregnancy before the baby can survive.

The last sentence promises a full length of life to the people addressed.

## Exodus 23:27

I will send My terror ahead and confuse every nation you meet. 📖

I will make all your enemies turn and flee.

## Exodus 23:28

I will send the hornet ahead to drive out the Hivites, Canaanites, and Hittites. 📖

**Help with “hornet”:** This is a large stinging insect.

The exact application of this expression needs further editorial review.

## Exodus 23:29

I will not drive them out in one year. 📖

Otherwise the land would become empty and ruined, and wild animals would multiply against you.

## Exodus 23:30

I will drive them out gradually until you increase and possess the land. 📖

## Exodus 23:31

I will set your borders from the Red Sea to the Sea of the Philistines. 📖

They will extend from the desert to the Euphrates.

I will put the inhabitants in your power, and you will drive them out.

## Exodus 23:32

Make no covenant with them or their gods. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Exodus 23:33

They must not stay in your land, or they may lead you to sin against Me. 📖

Serving their gods will be a trap for you.”

**Help with “trap”:** This pictures being caught in wrongdoing.
