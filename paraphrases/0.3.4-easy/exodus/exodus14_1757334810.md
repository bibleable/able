# Exodus 14 — Israel crosses the sea

**About this chapter**

Pharaoh pursues Israel.

The LORD opens a way through the sea and brings the water back over the Egyptian army.

The people see the rescue and believe.

## Exodus 14:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 14:2

“Tell the Israelites to turn back and camp before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea. 📖

Camp beside the sea, directly opposite Baal-zephon.

## Exodus 14:3

Pharaoh will say, ‘They wander in confusion. 📖

The wilderness has trapped them.’

## Exodus 14:4

I will harden Pharaoh’s heart, so he pursues them. 📖

Through Pharaoh and his whole army, I will gain honor.

The Egyptians will know I am the LORD.”

The Israelites obeyed.

**Help with “harden Pharaoh’s heart”:** This pictures making him stubborn and unwilling to listen.

## Exodus 14:5

Egypt’s king heard the people had fled. 📖

Pharaoh and his officials changed their minds.

They said, “What have we done?

We released Israel from serving us!”

## Exodus 14:6

Pharaoh prepared his chariot and took his army. 📖

## Exodus 14:7

He took 600 of the best chariots and all Egypt’s other chariots. 📖

Officers were over all of them.

## Exodus 14:8

The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and Egypt’s king pursued Israel. 📖

The Israelites were marching out defiantly.

**Help with “defiantly”:** This means boldly, showing they were no longer submitting.

## Exodus 14:9

All Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, horsemen, and troops chased Israel. 📖

They caught up at the sea camp near Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon.

## Exodus 14:10

As Pharaoh approached, Israel saw the Egyptians marching after them. 📖

They were terrified and cried to the LORD.

## Exodus 14:11

They asked Moses, “Were there no graves in Egypt, so you brought us here to die? 📖

What have you done by bringing us out of Egypt?

## Exodus 14:12

In Egypt, did we not tell you to leave us alone to serve the Egyptians? 📖

Serving them would be better than dying in the wilderness!”

## Exodus 14:13

Moses said, “Do not fear. 📖

Stand firm and see the LORD’s rescue today.

You will never again see the Egyptians you see today.

## Exodus 14:14

The LORD will fight for you. 📖

You only need to be still.”

## Exodus 14:15

The LORD told Moses, “Why cry to Me? 📖

Tell Israel to move forward.

## Exodus 14:16

Lift your staff and stretch your hand over the sea. 📖

Divide it, so Israel can cross on dry ground.

## Exodus 14:17

I will harden the Egyptians’ hearts, so they follow them in. 📖

Through Pharaoh and all his army, chariots, and horsemen, I will gain honor.

## Exodus 14:18

They will know I am the LORD when I gain honor through Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen.” 📖

## Exodus 14:19

God’s angel moved from ahead of Israel’s camp to behind it. 📖

The cloud pillar also moved from ahead to behind them,

## Exodus 14:20

between Egypt’s and Israel’s camps. 📖

The cloud was there in the darkness, but lit up the night.

Neither camp approached the other all night.

## Exodus 14:21

Moses stretched his hand over the sea. 📖

All night, the LORD drove it back with a strong east wind, making dry land.

The waters divided,

## Exodus 14:22

and Israel crossed on dry ground. 📖

Walls of water stood on their right and left.

## Exodus 14:23

All Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, and horsemen chased them into the sea. 📖

## Exodus 14:24

At the morning watch, the LORD looked from the fire and cloud pillar at Egypt’s army. 📖

He threw their camp into confusion.

**Help with “morning watch”:** This is the last part of the night before dawn.

## Exodus 14:25

He made their chariot wheels wobble, making driving difficult. 📖

They said, “Flee from Israel!

The LORD fights for them against Egypt!”

## Exodus 14:26

The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand over the sea. 📖

The water will return over the Egyptians, their chariots, and horsemen.”

## Exodus 14:27

Moses stretched his hand over the sea. 📖

At dawn, it returned to normal.

As the Egyptians retreated, the LORD swept them into the sea.

## Exodus 14:28

The water covered the chariots, horsemen, and Pharaoh’s whole army that followed Israel into the sea. 📖

Not one survived.

## Exodus 14:29

But Israel walked through on dry ground, with water walls on the right and left. 📖

## Exodus 14:30

That day, the LORD rescued Israel from Egyptian power. 📖

Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the shore.

## Exodus 14:31

Israel saw the LORD’s great power against Egypt. 📖

The people feared the LORD and believed in Him and His servant Moses.
