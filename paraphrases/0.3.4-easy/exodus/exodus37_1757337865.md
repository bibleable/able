# Exodus 37 — The ark, table, lampstand, and incense altar

**About this chapter**

Bezalel makes the sanctuary furnishings.

The account describes their materials, measurements, and parts.

It also records the making of the sacred oil and incense.

## Exodus 37:1

Bezalel made an acacia ark. 📖

It was two and a half cubits long, one and a half wide, and one and a half high.

**Help with these words:** The ark is a special chest.

Acacia is a kind of tree.

A cubit is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Exodus 37:2

He covered it inside and outside with pure gold and made a gold molding around it. 📖

**Help with “molding”:** This is a shaped border.

## Exodus 37:3

He cast four gold rings for its four feet, two on each side. 📖

## Exodus 37:4

He made acacia poles covered with gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:5

He put them through the side rings to carry the ark. 📖

## Exodus 37:6

He made a pure gold mercy seat two and a half cubits long and one and a half wide. 📖

**Help with “mercy seat”:** This is the ark’s special cover.

## Exodus 37:7

He made two cherubim of hammered gold at its ends, 📖

**Help with “cherubim”:** These are winged beings represented here by gold figures.

## Exodus 37:8

one at each end, all made from one piece of gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:9

Their wings spread upward over the mercy seat. 📖

They faced each other, looking toward it.

## Exodus 37:10

He made an acacia table two cubits long, one wide, and one and a half high. 📖

## Exodus 37:11

He covered it with pure gold and made a gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 37:12

He made a rim one handbreadth wide with a gold molding on it. 📖

**Help with “handbreadth”:** This is a length based on a hand’s width.

## Exodus 37:13

He cast four gold rings for the table and fixed them at the four corners beside its four legs. 📖

## Exodus 37:14

The rings were close to the rim to hold its carrying poles. 📖

## Exodus 37:15

He made acacia carrying poles and covered them with gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:16

He made pure gold plates, dishes, bowls, and pitchers. 📖

The bowls and pitchers were for pouring drink offerings.

## Exodus 37:17

He made the lampstand of pure hammered gold, all one piece. 📖

This included its base, shaft, cups, buds, and petals.

**Help with these words:** The shaft is the upright part.

The cups, buds, and petals are shaped like parts of flowers.

## Exodus 37:18

Six branches extended from its sides, three on each side. 📖

## Exodus 37:19

The first branch had three almond-blossom cups with buds and petals. 📖

The next had three, and so did all six branches.

## Exodus 37:20

The lampstand itself had four almond-blossom cups with buds and petals. 📖

## Exodus 37:21

One bud was under each pair of branches: the first, second, and third pairs. 📖

## Exodus 37:22

The buds and branches were one piece with the lampstand, hammered from pure gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:23

He made its seven lamps, wick trimmers, and trays of pure gold. 📖

**Help with “wick trimmers”:** These are tools for cutting the wicks that burn in the lamps.

## Exodus 37:24

He used one talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all its tools. 📖

**Help with “talent”:** This is a unit of weight.

## Exodus 37:25

He made a square acacia incense altar, one cubit long, one wide, and two high. 📖

Its horns were one piece with it.

## Exodus 37:26

He covered its top, sides, and horns with pure gold and made a gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 37:27

He made two gold rings below the molding, on opposite sides, for the carrying poles. 📖

## Exodus 37:28

He made acacia poles covered with gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:29

He made sacred anointing oil and pure fragrant incense, the work of a perfumer. 📖

**Help with these words:** Anointing oil is applied to set someone or something apart for God.

Incense gives a pleasant smell when burned.

A perfumer makes mixtures with pleasant smells.
