# Exodus 16 — Bread in the wilderness

**About this chapter**

The people complain about food.

The LORD provides meat and manna and tests their obedience.

The daily gathering rules and the different Sabbath rule are explained.

## Exodus 16:1

On the fifteenth day of the second month after leaving Egypt, all Israel left Elim. 📖

They reached the Desert of Sin, between Elim and Sinai.

## Exodus 16:2

The whole congregation complained against Moses and Aaron there. 📖

## Exodus 16:3

They said, “If only the LORD’s hand had killed us in Egypt! 📖

We sat by meat pots and ate all the bread we wanted.

You brought us here to starve this whole gathering to death!”

## Exodus 16:4

The LORD told Moses, “I will rain bread from heaven for you. 📖

Each day, gather enough for that day.

I will test whether they follow My instructions.

## Exodus 16:5

On day six, they must prepare twice as much as they gather on other days.” 📖

## Exodus 16:6

Moses and Aaron told all Israel, “Tonight you will know the LORD brought you out of Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 16:7

Tomorrow morning you will see His glory, because He heard your complaints against Him. 📖

Who are we, that you complain against us?”

## Exodus 16:8

Moses added, “The LORD will give you meat tonight and enough bread tomorrow morning. 📖

He heard your complaints against Him.

Who are we?

Your complaints are against the LORD, rather than us.”

## Exodus 16:9

Moses told Aaron, “Tell all Israel’s congregation to come before the LORD, because He heard their complaints.” 📖

## Exodus 16:10

While Aaron spoke to all the people, they looked toward the wilderness. 📖

The LORD’s glory appeared in a cloud.

## Exodus 16:11

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 16:12

“I heard Israel’s complaints. 📖

Tell them, ‘You will eat meat at twilight and be filled with bread in the morning.

You will know I am the LORD your God.’”

## Exodus 16:13

Quail covered the camp that evening. 📖

In the morning, dew lay around it.

**Help with these words:** Quail are small birds.

Dew is water that forms on cool surfaces.

## Exodus 16:14

When the dew dried, thin flakes remained on the wilderness floor, fine like frost. 📖

## Exodus 16:15

Israel saw them and asked, “What is it?” 📖

They did not know.

Moses said, “It is bread the LORD gave you to eat.

## Exodus 16:16

He commands each to gather what they need, one omer for each person in your tent.” 📖

**Help with “omer”:** This is a measure of dry goods.

## Exodus 16:17

The Israelites obeyed. 📖

Some gathered more, others less.

## Exodus 16:18

They measured by the omer. 📖

Those gathering much had no extra, and those gathering little had enough.

Each gathered what they needed to eat.

## Exodus 16:19

Moses said, “No one may keep any until morning.” 📖

## Exodus 16:20

Some ignored him and kept it until morning. 📖

It filled with maggots and stank.

Moses was angry.

**Help with “maggots”:** These are young flies that look like small worms.

## Exodus 16:21

Each morning, everyone gathered what they needed. 📖

When the sun grew hot, it melted.

## Exodus 16:22

On day six, they gathered double: two omers each. 📖

All the congregation’s leaders reported this to Moses.

## Exodus 16:23

He said, “The LORD said tomorrow is complete rest, a holy Sabbath for Him. 📖

Bake or boil what you want today.

Keep the rest until morning.”

**Help with “Sabbath”:** This is the day set apart for rest and the LORD.

## Exodus 16:24

They kept it as Moses commanded. 📖

It did not smell or contain maggots.

## Exodus 16:25

Moses said, “Eat it today, the LORD’s Sabbath. 📖

You will find none in the fields today.

## Exodus 16:26

Gather for six days. 📖

It will not be there on day seven, the Sabbath.”

## Exodus 16:27

Some went to gather on day seven but found nothing. 📖

## Exodus 16:28

The LORD asked Moses, “How long will you refuse My commands and instructions? 📖

## Exodus 16:29

The LORD gave you the Sabbath. 📖

That is why He gives two days’ bread on day six.

On day seven, everyone must stay where they are.

No one may leave their place.”

## Exodus 16:30

The people rested on day seven. 📖

## Exodus 16:31

Israel called it manna. 📖

It was white like coriander seed and tasted like honey wafers.

**Help with these words:** Coriander is a plant with seeds used for flavoring food.

Wafers are thin baked cakes.

## Exodus 16:32

Moses said, “The LORD commands you to keep an omer for future generations. 📖

They will see the bread He fed you in the wilderness when He brought you from Egypt.”

## Exodus 16:33

He told Aaron, “Fill a jar with an omer of manna. 📖

Place it before the LORD to keep for future generations.”

## Exodus 16:34

Aaron placed it before the Testimony to preserve it, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

**Help with “Testimony”:** This is a name for God’s covenant witness to Israel.

The term is kept from BSB.

## Exodus 16:35

Israel ate manna forty years until reaching a land where they could settle. 📖

They ate it until reaching Canaan’s border.

## Exodus 16:36

An omer is one-tenth of an ephah. 📖

**Help with these measures:** An ephah is a larger measure of dry goods.

Ten omers make one ephah.
