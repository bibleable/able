# Exodus 35 — Offerings and skilled workers

**About this chapter**

Moses repeats the Sabbath command.

The people willingly bring materials for the sanctuary.

The LORD gives skill to the workers, including the ability to teach others.

## Exodus 35:1

Moses gathered all Israel and said, “These are the things the LORD commands you to do. 📖

## Exodus 35:2

Work may be done six days. 📖

Day seven is holy, a Sabbath of complete rest to the LORD.

Anyone working that day must be put to death.

## Exodus 35:3

Do not light a fire in any home on the Sabbath.” 📖

## Exodus 35:4

Moses told all Israel, “The LORD commands this: 📖

## Exodus 35:5

Bring Him an offering from among you. 📖

Let everyone willing bring gold, silver, and bronze;

## Exodus 35:6

blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; fine linen and goat hair; 📖

**Help with these words:** Scarlet means bright red.

Linen is cloth made from flax.

## Exodus 35:7

red-dyed ram skins, fine leather, and acacia wood; 📖

**Help with these words:** A ram is a male sheep.

Acacia is a kind of tree.

## Exodus 35:8

olive oil for lighting, and spices for the anointing oil and fragrant incense; 📖

**Help with these words:** Anointing means applying oil to set someone or something apart.

Incense gives a pleasant smell when burned.

## Exodus 35:9

onyx stones and gemstones for the ephod and breastpiece. 📖

**Help with these words:** Onyx is a valuable stone.

An ephod is a priest’s special garment.

A breastpiece is worn over the chest.

## Exodus 35:10

Let all skilled workers come and make everything the LORD commanded: 📖

## Exodus 35:11

The tabernacle, tent, covering, clasps, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases; 📖

**Help with “tabernacle”:** This is the tent used as God’s sanctuary.

## Exodus 35:12

The ark, poles, mercy seat, and shielding veil; 📖

**Help with these words:** The ark is a special chest.

The mercy seat is its special cover.

The veil is a hanging curtain.

## Exodus 35:13

The table, its poles and tools, and the Bread of the Presence; 📖

## Exodus 35:14

The lampstand, its accessories, lamps, and lighting oil; 📖

## Exodus 35:15

The incense altar and its poles, anointing oil, fragrant incense, and entrance curtain; 📖

## Exodus 35:16

The burnt-offering altar, bronze grate, poles, and all its tools, and the washing basin and stand; 📖

## Exodus 35:17

The courtyard curtains, posts, bases, and gate curtain; 📖

## Exodus 35:18

The tabernacle’s and courtyard’s tent pegs and ropes; 📖

## Exodus 35:19

The woven garments for service in the holy place. 📖

These include Aaron’s holy priestly garments and his sons’ garments for serving as priests.”

## Exodus 35:20

All Israel’s congregation left Moses. 📖

## Exodus 35:21

Everyone whose heart and spirit moved them brought an offering to the LORD. 📖

They brought it for the Tent of Meeting’s work, all its service, and the holy garments.

## Exodus 35:22

Willing men and women brought brooches, earrings, rings, necklaces, and every kind of gold jewelry. 📖

They presented their gold as a wave offering to the LORD.

**Help with these words:** A brooch is jewelry fastened to clothing.

A wave offering is presented by moving it before the LORD.

## Exodus 35:23

Everyone with blue, purple, or scarlet yarn brought it. 📖

Those with fine linen, goat hair, red-dyed ram skins, or fine leather also brought them.

## Exodus 35:24

All who could give silver or bronze brought it to the LORD. 📖

Everyone with acacia wood for any part of the work brought it.

## Exodus 35:25

Every skilled woman spun thread by hand and brought blue, purple, or scarlet yarn, or fine linen. 📖

## Exodus 35:26

All skilled women whose hearts moved them spun the goat hair. 📖

## Exodus 35:27

The leaders brought onyx stones and gemstones for the ephod and breastpiece, 📖

## Exodus 35:28

and spices and olive oil for lighting, anointing oil, and fragrant incense. 📖

## Exodus 35:29

Willing Israelite men and women brought freewill offerings to the LORD. 📖

These were for all the work He commanded through Moses.

**Help with “freewill”:** This means given willingly.

## Exodus 35:30

Moses told Israel, “The LORD called Bezalel by name. 📖

He is Uri’s son, Hur’s grandson, from Judah’s tribe.

## Exodus 35:31

The LORD filled Bezalel with God’s Spirit, skill, ability, and knowledge in every craft, 📖

## Exodus 35:32

for artistic designs in gold, silver, and bronze, 📖

## Exodus 35:33

for cutting gemstones for settings, and carving wood. 📖

He can master every artistic craft.

## Exodus 35:34

The LORD gave him and Oholiab, Ahisamach’s son from Dan’s tribe, the ability to teach others. 📖

## Exodus 35:35

He gave them skill as engravers, designers, embroiderers, and weavers. 📖

They can work with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen, designing every kind of craft.”
