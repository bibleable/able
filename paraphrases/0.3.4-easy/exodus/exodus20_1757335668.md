# Exodus 20 — God gives his commands

**About this chapter**

God speaks to the people of Israel.

Moses is their leader.

These commands include the Ten Commandments.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Exodus 20:1

God spoke all these words. 📖

## Exodus 20:2

“I am the LORD your God. 📖

I brought you out of Egypt, where you were slaves.”

**Help with a word:** Slaves are people treated as property and forced to serve others.

## Exodus 20:3

“You must have no other gods besides me.” 📖

## Exodus 20:4

“Do not make an idol shaped like anything in the sky, on earth, or in the water.” 📖

**Help with a word:** An idol is an object people worship as a god.

## Exodus 20:5

“Do not bow down to idols or worship them. 📖

I am the LORD your God, a jealous God.

I punish fathers’ sins in their children, to the third and fourth generations of those who hate Me,

**Help with “jealous God”:** God requires His people’s faithful worship.

He does not accept their worship of other gods.

Generations are parents, children, and their descendants through time.

## Exodus 20:6

“But I show faithful love to 1,000 generations of those who love me and obey my commands.” 📖

**Help with words:** Generations are parents, children, and their descendants through time.

## Exodus 20:7

“Do not use the LORD your God's name wrongly. 📖

The LORD will punish anyone who uses his name wrongly.”

## Exodus 20:8

“Remember the Sabbath day. 📖

Keep it set apart for God.”

**Help with a word:** The Sabbath is the weekly day of rest.

## Exodus 20:9

“Do your work during 6 days.” 📖

## Exodus 20:10

“Day 7 is a Sabbath for the LORD your God. 📖

You must not work.

Your children, male and female servants, and animals must not work.

Foreigners living in your towns must not work either.”

## Exodus 20:11

“The LORD made the heavens, earth, sea, and everything in them in 6 days. 📖

He rested on day 7.

So the LORD blessed the Sabbath and set it apart for himself.”

## Exodus 20:12

“Respect your father and mother. 📖

Then you can live long in the land the LORD your God is giving you.”

## Exodus 20:13

“Do not murder.” 📖

## Exodus 20:14

“Do not commit adultery.” 📖

**Help with “adultery”:** This is sex involving a married person and someone who is not their spouse.

A spouse is a husband or wife.

## Exodus 20:15

“Do not steal.” 📖

## Exodus 20:16

“Do not tell lies as a witness against your neighbour.” 📖

**Help with words:** A witness tells what happened, including when a court decides a case.

## Exodus 20:17

“Do not desire your neighbour’s house as your own. 📖

Do not desire your neighbour’s wife, male or female servant, ox, donkey, or anything your neighbour owns.”

**Help with this verse:** This warns against wanting to take what belongs to someone else.

## Exodus 20:18

The people saw lightning and heard thunder and a ram's horn. 📖

Smoke covered the mountain.

They shook with fear and stayed far away.

## Exodus 20:19

They told Moses, “You speak to us, and we will listen. 📖

Do not let God speak to us, or we will die.”

## Exodus 20:20

Moses said, “Do not be afraid. 📖

God has come to test you.

He wants you to respect and fear him, so you will not sin.”

**Help with a word:** To sin is to do wrong against God.

## Exodus 20:21

The people stayed far away. 📖

Moses went towards the thick darkness where God was.

## Exodus 20:22

The LORD told Moses, “Tell the Israelites this. 📖

You have seen that I spoke to you from heaven.”

## Exodus 20:23

“Do not make silver or gold gods to worship beside me.” 📖

## Exodus 20:24

“Make an altar of earth for me. 📖

Offer your sheep, goats, and cattle on it as burnt offerings and peace offerings.

Wherever I cause my name to be remembered, I will come and bless you.”

**Help with words:** An altar is a place for giving offerings to God.

These offerings were animals given to God in worship.

## Exodus 20:25

“If you build me a stone altar, use stones that tools have not shaped. 📖

Using a chisel on the stones makes the altar unfit for worship.”

**Help with a word:** A chisel is a tool used to cut stone.

## Exodus 20:26

“Do not climb steps to My altar, so your nakedness is not exposed there.” 📖

**Help with these words:** Nakedness here refers to uncovered private parts.
