# Exodus 40 — The tabernacle is set up

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses to arrange and consecrate the tabernacle.

Moses follows the instructions.

The LORD’s glory fills it, and the cloud guides Israel’s journeys.

## Exodus 40:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 40:2

“Set up the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting, on the first day of the first month. 📖

## Exodus 40:3

Put the ark of the Testimony inside and screen it with the veil. 📖

## Exodus 40:4

Bring the table and arrange its contents. 📖

Bring the lampstand and set up its lamps.

## Exodus 40:5

Place the gold incense altar before the ark of the Testimony. 📖

Hang the tabernacle’s entrance curtain.

## Exodus 40:6

Put the burnt-offering altar before the entrance to the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Exodus 40:7

Put the basin between the tent and altar, and fill it with water. 📖

## Exodus 40:8

Set up the surrounding courtyard and hang its entrance curtain. 📖

## Exodus 40:9

Anoint the tabernacle and everything inside with anointing oil. 📖

Consecrate it and all its furnishings, making it holy.

**Help with these words:** Anoint means apply special oil.

Consecrate means set apart for God.

## Exodus 40:10

Anoint the burnt-offering altar and all its tools. 📖

Consecrate the altar as most holy.

## Exodus 40:11

Anoint and consecrate the basin and its stand. 📖

## Exodus 40:12

Bring Aaron and his sons to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

Wash them with water.

## Exodus 40:13

Dress Aaron in holy garments, anoint him, and set him apart to serve Me as priest. 📖

## Exodus 40:14

Bring his sons and dress them in tunics. 📖

## Exodus 40:15

Anoint them as you anointed their father to serve Me as priests. 📖

Their anointing qualifies them for permanent priesthood through their generations.”

## Exodus 40:16

Moses did everything as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 40:17

The tabernacle was set up on the first day of the first month in the second year. 📖

## Exodus 40:18

Moses laid its bases, positioned its frames, inserted the crossbars, and set up the posts. 📖

## Exodus 40:19

He spread the tent over the tabernacle and put the covering above it, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 40:20

He put the Testimony inside the ark and attached the poles. 📖

He placed the mercy seat on top.

## Exodus 40:21

He brought the ark inside, hung the veil, and shielded the ark of the Testimony, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 40:22

Moses put the table on the north side inside the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil. 📖

## Exodus 40:23

He arranged bread before the LORD, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 40:24

He put the lampstand inside, opposite the table on the south side, 📖

## Exodus 40:25

and set up its lamps before the LORD, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 40:26

Moses put the gold altar in the Tent of Meeting before the veil, 📖

## Exodus 40:27

and burned fragrant incense on it, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 40:28

He hung the tabernacle’s entrance curtain. 📖

## Exodus 40:29

He placed the burnt-offering altar near the entrance to the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. 📖

He offered burnt and grain offerings there, as the LORD commanded.

## Exodus 40:30

He placed the basin between the tent and altar, with water for washing. 📖

## Exodus 40:31

Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and feet from it. 📖

## Exodus 40:32

They washed when entering the Tent of Meeting or approaching the altar, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 40:33

Moses set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and altar and hung its entrance curtain. 📖

He finished the work.

## Exodus 40:34

The cloud covered the Tent of Meeting. 📖

The LORD’s glory filled the tabernacle.

## Exodus 40:35

Moses could not enter because the cloud settled on it and the LORD’s glory filled it. 📖

## Exodus 40:36

Whenever the cloud was lifted from above the tabernacle, Israel set out for each stage of the journey. 📖

## Exodus 40:37

If it was not lifted, they stayed until the day it was taken up. 📖

## Exodus 40:38

The LORD’s cloud stood above the tabernacle by day, with fire in it at night. 📖

All Israel saw it through all their journeys.
