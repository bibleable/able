# Exodus 12 — Passover and departure from Egypt

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Passover instructions.

Egypt’s firstborn die, and Israel leaves.

The chapter also gives continuing rules for remembering this night.

The household requirements, dates, and judgments are included.

## Exodus 12:1

The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron in Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 12:2

He said, “This month will begin your months. 📖

It will be the first month of your year.

## Exodus 12:3

Tell all Israel’s congregation that each man must choose a lamb for his family on this month’s tenth day. 📖

There must be one per household.

**Help with “congregation”:** This means the gathered people.

## Exodus 12:4

If a household is too small for a lamb, share with the nearest neighbor. 📖

Divide the lamb according to the number of people.

## Exodus 12:5

The animal must be a year-old male without blemish. 📖

You may choose from sheep or goats.

**Help with “without blemish”:** Here this means an animal without a defect.

## Exodus 12:6

Keep it until the fourteenth day. 📖

Then all Israel’s congregation must slaughter the animals at twilight.

**Help with “slaughter”:** This means kill the animals.

Twilight is the fading light in the evening.

## Exodus 12:7

Put some blood on the doorframes’ sides and tops at the houses where you eat the lambs. 📖

## Exodus 12:8

Eat the meat that night, roasted over fire, with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. 📖

**Help with these foods:** Unleavened bread is made without leaven, such as yeast, which makes dough rise.

Herbs are plants used to flavor food.

## Exodus 12:9

Do not eat it raw or boiled in water. 📖

Roast it over fire, including head, legs, and inner parts.

## Exodus 12:10

Leave none until morning. 📖

Burn any leftovers before morning.

## Exodus 12:11

Eat fully dressed for travel, sandals on your feet and staff in hand. 📖

Eat quickly.

It is the LORD’s Passover.

**Help with “Passover”:** The following verses describe the LORD passing over the blood-marked houses.

## Exodus 12:12

That night, I will pass through Egypt and kill every firstborn male, human and animal. 📖

I will judge all Egypt’s gods.

I am the LORD.

## Exodus 12:13

The blood on your houses will be a sign. 📖

When I see it, I will pass over you.

No plague will destroy you when I strike Egypt.

## Exodus 12:14

Remember this day with a feast to the LORD. 📖

Celebrate it as a lasting law for generations to come.

## Exodus 12:15

Eat unleavened bread for seven days. 📖

On the first day, remove leaven from your houses.

Anyone eating anything leavened from day one through day seven must be cut off from Israel.

**Help with these words:** Leaven makes dough rise.

“Cut off” is a serious judgment removing a person from the people.

This verse does not describe how the judgment is carried out.

Its exact application needs further editorial review.

## Exodus 12:16

Hold sacred gatherings on the first and seventh days. 📖

Do no work then except preparing meals.

That is all you may do.

**Help with “sacred”:** This means set apart for God.

## Exodus 12:17

Keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. 📖

On this very day I brought your divisions out of Egypt.

Keep this day as a lasting law for generations to come.

**Help with “divisions”:** These are organized groups of the people.

## Exodus 12:18

In the first month, eat unleavened bread from the fourteenth day’s evening until the twenty-first day’s evening. 📖

## Exodus 12:19

For seven days, no leaven may be found in your houses. 📖

Anyone eating leavened food must be cut off from Israel’s congregation, whether foreigner or native.

**Help with these words:** A native is someone born among the local people.

“Cut off” is a serious judgment removing a person from the people.

The verse does not explain exactly how this happens.

## Exodus 12:20

Eat nothing leavened. 📖

Eat unleavened bread in every home.”

## Exodus 12:21

Moses called all Israel’s elders and said, “Go now and choose a lamb for each family. 📖

Slaughter the Passover lamb.

## Exodus 12:22

Dip a bunch of hyssop in the basin’s blood and brush it onto the doorframe’s top and sides. 📖

No one may leave the house until morning.

**Help with “hyssop”:** This is a plant used here to apply the blood.

## Exodus 12:23

The LORD will pass through to strike the Egyptians. 📖

He will see the blood on the doorframe’s top and sides and pass over that doorway.

He will not let the destroyer enter your houses and strike you down.

## Exodus 12:24

Keep this command as a lasting law for you and your descendants. 📖

## Exodus 12:25

When you enter the land the LORD promised to give you, keep this service. 📖

## Exodus 12:26

When your children ask, ‘What does this service mean to you?’ 📖

## Exodus 12:27

Answer, ‘It is the Passover sacrifice to the LORD. 📖

He passed over Israel’s houses in Egypt, striking the Egyptians but sparing our homes.’”

The people bowed and worshiped.

## Exodus 12:28

The Israelites did exactly what the LORD commanded Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Exodus 12:29

At midnight, the LORD killed every firstborn male in Egypt. 📖

This included the firstborn of Pharaoh, who sat on the throne, and the firstborn of the prisoner in the dungeon.

He also killed all the livestock’s firstborn.

## Exodus 12:30

Pharaoh, all his officials, and all the Egyptians rose that night. 📖

Loud crying filled Egypt, because every house had someone dead.

## Exodus 12:31

Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron that night. 📖

He said, “Leave my people, you and the Israelites!

Worship the LORD as you requested.

## Exodus 12:32

Take your flocks and herds as you said, and leave. 📖

Bless me too.”

## Exodus 12:33

The Egyptians urged them to leave quickly, saying, “Otherwise we will all die!” 📖

## Exodus 12:34

The people carried dough before it was leavened. 📖

They wrapped their kneading bowls in clothes and carried them on their shoulders.

## Exodus 12:35

Following Moses’ words, the Israelites asked the Egyptians for silver and gold articles and clothing. 📖

## Exodus 12:36

The LORD gave them favor with the Egyptians, who granted their request. 📖

In this way they plundered the Egyptians.

**Help with “plundered”:** This means took goods away from others.

## Exodus 12:37

They traveled from Rameses to Succoth. 📖

About 600,000 men went on foot, besides women and children.

## Exodus 12:38

A mixed crowd went too, along with very large numbers of livestock, both flocks and herds. 📖

## Exodus 12:39

They baked their Egyptian dough into unleavened loaves, because it had no leaven. 📖

They had been driven out without time to wait or prepare supplies.

## Exodus 12:40

The Israelites stayed in Egypt 430 years. 📖

## Exodus 12:41

At the end of 430 years, on that very day, all the LORD’s divisions left Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 12:42

The LORD kept watch that night to bring them out of Egypt. 📖

All Israelites must keep watch that same night in honor of the LORD for generations to come.

## Exodus 12:43

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, “This is the Passover law. 📖

No foreigner may eat it.

## Exodus 12:44

A purchased slave may eat it after you circumcise him. 📖

**Help with “circumcise”:** This means remove the foreskin, skin covering the end of the penis.

## Exodus 12:45

No temporary resident or hired worker may eat the Passover. 📖

## Exodus 12:46

Eat it inside one house. 📖

Take no meat outside, and break none of its bones.

## Exodus 12:47

All Israel’s congregation must celebrate it. 📖

## Exodus 12:48

If a resident foreigner wants to celebrate the LORD’s Passover, every male in his household must be circumcised. 📖

Then he may celebrate it and be treated like a native.

No uncircumcised man may eat it.

## Exodus 12:49

The same law applies to natives and foreigners living among you.” 📖

## Exodus 12:50

All the Israelites did exactly as the LORD commanded Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Exodus 12:51

On that very day, the LORD brought the Israelites out of Egypt by their divisions. 📖
