# Exodus 33 — Moses asks for the LORD’s presence

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Israel to leave.

The people mourn, and Moses pleads for the LORD to go with them.

Moses also asks to see God’s glory.

The promises and limits of that request remain part of the account.

## Exodus 33:1

The LORD told Moses, “Leave with the people you brought from Egypt. 📖

Go to the land I promised Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob for their descendants.

## Exodus 33:2

I will send an angel ahead and drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 📖

## Exodus 33:3

Go to the land flowing with milk and honey. 📖

I will not go with you, because you are stiff-necked.

Otherwise I might destroy you on the way.”

**Help with these pictures:** Milk and honey picture plentiful good food.

Stiff-necked pictures stubborn refusal to obey.

## Exodus 33:4

They mourned at this bad news. 📖

No one wore jewelry.

## Exodus 33:5

The LORD had told Moses, “Tell Israel, ‘You are stiff-necked. 📖

If I went with you even a moment, I would destroy you.

Remove your jewelry while I decide what to do with you.’”

## Exodus 33:6

From Mount Horeb onward, Israel took off their jewelry. 📖

## Exodus 33:7

Moses used to pitch the tent far outside camp and call it the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Anyone seeking the LORD went there outside camp.

## Exodus 33:8

When Moses went there, everyone stood at their own tent entrances. 📖

They watched until he entered.

## Exodus 33:9

As Moses entered, the cloud pillar came down and stayed at the entrance. 📖

The LORD spoke with Moses.

## Exodus 33:10

Everyone saw the cloud pillar at the entrance. 📖

They stood and worshiped at their own tent entrances.

## Exodus 33:11

The LORD spoke face to face with Moses, as one speaks to a friend. 📖

Moses returned to camp, but his young assistant Joshua, Nun’s son, stayed in the tent.

## Exodus 33:12

Moses told the LORD, “You tell me to lead these people but have not told me whom You will send. 📖

You said, ‘I know you by name, and you found favor with Me.’

## Exodus 33:13

If I found favor, show me Your ways so I may know You and find favor. 📖

Remember this nation is Your people.”

## Exodus 33:14

The LORD answered, “My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.” 📖

## Exodus 33:15

Moses replied, “If Your Presence does not go with us, do not lead us from here. 📖

## Exodus 33:16

Without Your going with us, how will anyone know Your people and I found Your favor? 📖

How else will we differ from every other people on earth?”

## Exodus 33:17

The LORD said, “I will do what you asked. 📖

You found favor with Me, and I know you by name.”

## Exodus 33:18

Moses said, “Please show me Your glory.” 📖

## Exodus 33:19

The LORD replied, “All My goodness will pass before you. 📖

I will proclaim My name, the LORD, before you.

I will show mercy to whom I show mercy, and compassion to whom I show compassion.”

## Exodus 33:20

He added, “You cannot see My face. 📖

No one can see Me and live.”

## Exodus 33:21

The LORD said, “Stand on the rock in this place near Me. 📖

## Exodus 33:22

When My glory passes, I will put you in a crack in the rock. 📖

My hand will cover you until I pass.

## Exodus 33:23

Then I will remove My hand, and you will see My back. 📖

My face must not be seen.”
