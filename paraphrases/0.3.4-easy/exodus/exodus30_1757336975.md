# Exodus 30 — Incense, washing, and sacred oil

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives further sanctuary instructions.

These include the census payment, washing, anointing oil, and incense.

The formulas have exact quantities and restrictions.

## Exodus 30:1

“Make an acacia altar for burning incense. 📖

## Exodus 30:2

Make it square, one cubit long and one wide, and two cubits high. 📖

Its horns must be one piece with it.

**Help with these words:** A cubit is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

The horns are projections at the corners.

## Exodus 30:3

Cover its top, sides, and horns with pure gold. 📖

Make a gold molding around it.

## Exodus 30:4

Put two gold rings below the molding on opposite sides to hold its carrying poles. 📖

## Exodus 30:5

Make acacia poles and cover them with gold. 📖

## Exodus 30:6

Place it before the veil in front of the ark of the Testimony. 📖

This is before the mercy seat over the Testimony, where I will meet you.

**Help with these words:** The ark is the special chest.

The Testimony is God’s covenant witness, and the mercy seat is the ark’s special cover.

## Exodus 30:7

Aaron must burn fragrant incense every morning when tending the lamps. 📖

## Exodus 30:8

He must burn it when setting up the lamps at twilight too. 📖

Keep burning incense before the LORD through coming generations.

## Exodus 30:9

Offer no unauthorized incense, burnt offering, or grain offering on it. 📖

Pour no drink offering on it.

**Help with “unauthorized”:** This means not permitted.

## Exodus 30:10

Once each year, Aaron must make atonement on its horns with blood from the sin offering of atonement. 📖

Keep doing this annually through your generations.

It is most holy to the LORD.”

**Help with “atonement”:** This deals with sin and restores the relationship with God.

## Exodus 30:11

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 30:12

“When you count Israel in a census, each man counted must pay the LORD a ransom for his life. 📖

Then no plague will strike when they are counted.

**Help with these words:** A census is an official count of people.

A ransom is a payment for release or protection.

## Exodus 30:13

Everyone crossing over to be counted must give half a sanctuary shekel as an offering to the LORD. 📖

A sanctuary shekel weighs twenty gerahs.

**Help with these words:** Shekels and gerahs are units of weight.

## Exodus 30:14

Everyone counted who is twenty or older must give this offering. 📖

## Exodus 30:15

This offering to the LORD makes atonement for your lives. 📖

The rich must not give more than half a shekel.

The poor must not give less.

## Exodus 30:16

Use Israel’s atonement money for the Tent of Meeting’s service. 📖

It will be a memorial for Israel before the LORD, making atonement for your lives.”

**Help with “memorial”:** This means a lasting reminder.

## Exodus 30:17

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 30:18

“Make a bronze washing basin and bronze stand. 📖

Put it between the Tent of Meeting and altar, and fill it with water,

## Exodus 30:19

for Aaron and his sons to wash their hands and feet. 📖

## Exodus 30:20

They must wash when entering the tent or approaching the altar to serve with a food offering to the LORD. 📖

They must wash so they will not die.

## Exodus 30:21

Wash hands and feet to avoid death. 📖

This is a permanent law for Aaron and his descendants through coming generations.”

## Exodus 30:22

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 30:23

“Take the finest spices: 500 shekels of liquid myrrh and half as much fragrant cinnamon, 250 shekels. 📖

Take 250 shekels of fragrant cane,

## Exodus 30:24

500 shekels of cassia, all measured by the sanctuary shekel, and one hin of olive oil. 📖

**Help with these words:** These are the names of plant materials used for fragrance.

A hin is a unit for measuring liquid.

## Exodus 30:25

Have a perfumer blend them into fragrant sacred anointing oil. 📖

It will be sacred anointing oil.

**Help with these words:** A perfumer makes mixtures with pleasant smells.

Anointing oil is applied to set someone or something apart for God.

## Exodus 30:26

Anoint the Tent of Meeting, the ark of the Testimony, 📖

## Exodus 30:27

the table and all its tools, the lampstand and its tools, the incense altar, 📖

## Exodus 30:28

the burnt-offering altar and all its tools, and the basin and stand. 📖

## Exodus 30:29

Set them apart as most holy. 📖

Anything touching them will be holy.

## Exodus 30:30

Anoint Aaron and his sons and set them apart to serve Me as priests. 📖

## Exodus 30:31

Tell Israel, ‘This is My sacred anointing oil through coming generations. 📖

## Exodus 30:32

Do not anoint an ordinary man with it or make anything using the same formula. 📖

It is holy, and you must treat it as holy.

## Exodus 30:33

Anyone mixing perfume like it or putting it on an outsider must be cut off from his people.’” 📖

**Help with “cut off”:** This is a serious judgment removing a person from the people.

The verse does not describe exactly how it is carried out.

## Exodus 30:34

The LORD told Moses, “Take equal measures of gum resin, onycha, galbanum, and pure frankincense. 📖

These are fragrant spices.

## Exodus 30:35

Have a perfumer blend them into incense, seasoned with salt, pure and holy. 📖

## Exodus 30:36

Grind some to fine powder. 📖

Put it before the Testimony in the Tent of Meeting, where I will meet you.

It must be most holy to you.

## Exodus 30:37

Do not use this formula to make incense for yourselves. 📖

Treat it as holy to the LORD.

## Exodus 30:38

Anyone making something like it to enjoy its smell must be cut off from his people.” 📖

**About this judgment:** The source does not describe exactly how being “cut off” is carried out.
