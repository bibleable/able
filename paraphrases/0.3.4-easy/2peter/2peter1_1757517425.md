# 2 Peter 1 — Grow in faith and remember the truth

**About this chapter**

Peter urges believers to grow in their faith.

He recalls seeing Christ's glory and speaks about the prophetic message.

## 2 Peter 1:1

This letter is from Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ. 📖

It is for those receiving faith as precious as ours through the righteousness of our God and Saviour Jesus Christ.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

Righteousness means being right before God and doing right.

A Saviour rescues people.

## 2 Peter 1:2

May kindness and peace grow richly among you through knowing God and Jesus our Lord. 📖

## 2 Peter 1:3

His divine power has given us everything needed for life and godliness through knowing the One who called us. 📖

He called us by his own glory and excellence.

**Help with words:** Divine means belonging to God.

Godliness means living in devotion to God.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## 2 Peter 1:4

Through these, he gave us his precious and magnificent promises. 📖

Through them, you can share in the divine nature.

You have escaped the world's corruption caused by evil desires.

## 2 Peter 1:5

For this reason, make every effort to add goodness to your faith, and knowledge to goodness. 📖

## 2 Peter 1:6

Add self-control to knowledge, endurance to self-control, and godliness to endurance. 📖

**Help with a word:** Endurance means continuing through difficulty without giving up.

## 2 Peter 1:7

Add brotherly kindness to godliness, and love to brotherly kindness. 📖

## 2 Peter 1:8

If you have these qualities and keep growing in them, you will not be ineffective or unfruitful in knowing Christ. 📖

He is our Lord Jesus Christ.

**Help with a word:** Unfruitful pictures failing to produce good results.

## 2 Peter 1:9

Anyone lacking these things is short-sighted to the point of blindness. 📖

They have forgotten being cleansed from their past sins.

**Help with this picture:** Peter uses limited sight as a picture of forgetting this cleansing and its meaning for life.

## 2 Peter 1:10

So, brothers and sisters, work hard to make your calling and being chosen sure. 📖

If you practise these things, you will never stumble.

## 2 Peter 1:11

Then you will receive a rich welcome into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. 📖

**Help with words:** Eternal means lasting for ever.

A kingdom is where a king rules.

## 2 Peter 1:12

So I will always remind you of these things, although you know them and stand firmly in the truth. 📖

## 2 Peter 1:13

I think it right to refresh your memory as long as I live in the tent of my body. 📖

**Help with this picture:** A tent is a temporary home.

Peter pictures his body as the home he lives in during this life.

## 2 Peter 1:14

I know this tent will soon be put aside, as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Peter is speaking about his approaching death.

## 2 Peter 1:15

I will do my best to make sure you can always remember these things after my departure. 📖

## 2 Peter 1:16

We told you about our Lord Jesus Christ's power and coming, without following cleverly invented stories. 📖

We saw his majesty with our own eyes.

## 2 Peter 1:17

He received honour and glory from God the Father. 📖

The voice from the Majestic Glory said, “This is my Son, whom I love.

I am very pleased with him.”

**Help with a title:** Majestic Glory describes God's glorious presence.

## 2 Peter 1:18

We heard this voice from heaven ourselves when we were with him on the holy mountain. 📖

## 2 Peter 1:19

We also have the prophets' message, confirmed beyond doubt. 📖

Pay attention to it like a lamp shining in darkness.

Do this until day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts.

**Help with these pictures:** A lamp, dawn, and morning star picture light and understanding.

## 2 Peter 1:20

Above all, understand that no prophecy in Scripture comes from someone's own interpretation. 📖

## 2 Peter 1:21

Prophecy never came from human will. 📖

People spoke from God as the Holy Spirit carried them along.
