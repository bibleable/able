# 2 Peter 3 — The Lord keeps his promise

**About this chapter**

Peter addresses mockery about the Lord's coming.

He speaks about God's patience, judgment, and promise of a new heaven and earth.

## 2 Peter 3:1

Dear friends, this is my second letter to you. 📖

Both letters remind you of the truth and stir you to wholesome thinking.

## 2 Peter 3:2

Remember what the holy prophets foretold and what our Lord and Saviour commanded through your apostles. 📖

## 2 Peter 3:3

Above all, know that scoffers will come in the last days, mocking and following their own evil desires. 📖

**Help with a word:** Scoffers are people who mock what they reject.

## 2 Peter 3:4

They will ask, “Where is his promised coming? 📖

Since our fathers fell asleep, everything continues as it has since creation began.”

**Help with this picture:** Falling asleep means dying here.

## 2 Peter 3:5

They deliberately overlook how, long ago, the heavens existed by God's word. 📖

The earth was formed out of water and by water.

## 2 Peter 3:6

Through these waters, the world of that time perished in the flood. 📖

## 2 Peter 3:7

By the same word, the present heavens and earth are reserved for fire. 📖

They are kept for the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly people.

## 2 Peter 3:8

Dear friends, do not overlook this one thing. 📖

With the Lord, a day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like a day.

## 2 Peter 3:9

The Lord is not slow about his promise as some understand slowness. 📖

He is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish but everyone to turn from sin.

## 2 Peter 3:10

The day of the Lord will come like a thief. 📖

The heavens will disappear with a roar, and the elements will be destroyed by fire.

The earth and its works will be laid bare.

**Help with words:** Elements means the parts that make up the world.

Laid bare means uncovered for all to see.

## 2 Peter 3:11

Since everything will be destroyed this way, what kind of people should you be? 📖

Live in holiness and godliness.

## 2 Peter 3:12

Look forward to and hasten the coming of God's day. 📖

Then the heavens will be destroyed by fire, and the elements will melt in the heat.

**Help with a word:** Hasten means speed up.

## 2 Peter 3:13

According to God's promise, we look forward to a new heaven and a new earth, where righteousness lives. 📖

## 2 Peter 3:14

So, dear friends, as you wait, do your best to be found at peace, spotless and blameless before him. 📖

## 2 Peter 3:15

Remember that our Lord's patience brings salvation. 📖

Our dear brother Paul also wrote this to you with the wisdom God gave him.

## 2 Peter 3:16

He writes about these matters in all his letters. 📖

Some parts are hard to understand.

Ignorant and unstable people twist them, as they twist the other Scriptures, to their own destruction.

**Help with these words:** Peter describes people distorting the teaching, rather than condemning the difficulty of understanding itself.

## 2 Peter 3:17

Dear friends, you already know these things, so be on your guard. 📖

Do not be carried away by lawless people's error and fall from your secure standing.

## 2 Peter 3:18

Grow in the kindness and knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. 📖

May he have glory now and to the day of eternity.

Amen.
