# 2 Peter 2 — A warning about false teachers

**About this chapter**

Peter warns about false teachers and gives examples of God's judgment and rescue.

He uses strong pictures to describe corrupt conduct and its results.

## 2 Peter 2:1

There were false prophets among the people, and there will be false teachers among you. 📖

They will secretly introduce destructive false beliefs and even deny the Master who bought them.

They bring swift destruction on themselves.

## 2 Peter 2:2

Many will follow their corrupt way of life. 📖

Because of them, people will speak against the way of truth.

## 2 Peter 2:3

In their greed, these false teachers will exploit you with deceitful words. 📖

Their judgment was declared long ago and remains in force.

Their destruction does not sleep.

**Help with words:** Exploit means use someone unfairly for your own benefit.

Destruction not sleeping pictures punishment that is still coming.

## 2 Peter 2:4

God did not spare the angels when they sinned. 📖

He cast them deep into hell, in chains of darkness, to be kept for judgment.

## 2 Peter 2:5

God did not spare the ancient world when he flooded its ungodly people. 📖

But he protected Noah, who preached righteousness, among the eight.

## 2 Peter 2:6

He condemned Sodom and Gomorrah to destruction, reducing the cities to ashes. 📖

They are an example of what is coming on the ungodly.

## 2 Peter 2:7

He rescued Lot, a righteous man distressed by the lawless people's corrupt conduct. 📖

## 2 Peter 2:8

That righteous man lived among them day after day. 📖

Their lawless actions that he saw and heard tormented his righteous soul.

## 2 Peter 2:9

If all this is so, the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trials. 📖

He knows how to hold the unrighteous for punishment on judgment day.

## 2 Peter 2:10

This punishment is especially for those following corrupt fleshly desires and despising authority. 📖

Bold and self-willed, they do not fear slandering glorious beings.

## 2 Peter 2:11

Even angels, greater in strength and power, do not bring such slanderous charges against them before the Lord. 📖

## 2 Peter 2:12

These people are like animals without reason, acting by instinct, born to be caught and destroyed. 📖

They speak abusively about things they do not understand.

Like those animals, they too will be destroyed.

**Help with a word:** Instinct means acting naturally without thinking things through.

## 2 Peter 2:13

The harm they suffer will be the pay for their wrongdoing. 📖

They enjoy wild pleasure in broad daylight.

They are stains and faults, enjoying their deception while feasting with you.

## 2 Peter 2:14

Their eyes are full of adultery, and their desire for sin is never satisfied. 📖

They lure people who are not standing firm.

They are cursed children, with hearts trained in greed.

**Help with a word:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

## 2 Peter 2:15

They left the straight way and wandered onto Balaam's path. 📖

Balaam, son of Beor, loved the pay for wrongdoing.

## 2 Peter 2:16

A donkey corrected him for his wrongdoing. 📖

Though normally unable to speak, it spoke with a human voice and held back the prophet's madness.

**Help with a word:** Madness here describes Balaam's foolish wrongdoing.

## 2 Peter 2:17

These people are springs without water and mists driven by a storm. 📖

The blackest darkness is reserved for them.

## 2 Peter 2:18

With grand but empty words, they appeal to sinful bodily passions. 📖

They lure those just escaping from others living in error.

## 2 Peter 2:19

They promise freedom while they themselves are slaves to corruption. 📖

A person is a slave to whatever has mastered them.

## 2 Peter 2:20

Suppose they escaped the world's corruption through knowing our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. 📖

If they are trapped and overcome again, their final condition is worse than their first.

## 2 Peter 2:21

Knowing the way of righteousness and then rejecting the holy command passed to them is worse than never knowing it. 📖

## 2 Peter 2:22

These proverbs are true of them. 📖

“A dog returns to its vomit.”

“A washed female pig returns to rolling in the mud.”
