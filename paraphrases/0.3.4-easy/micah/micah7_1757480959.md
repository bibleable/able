# Micah 7 — Grief, hope, and God’s compassion

**About this chapter**

The chapter describes a society filled with wrongdoing and broken trust.

It turns to hope, prayer, and promises of God’s compassion.

The different voices and strong images are kept.

## Micah 7:1

How terrible for me! 📖

I am like someone gathering leftover summer fruit in a vineyard.

There is no grape cluster to eat and no early fig that I long for.

## Micah 7:2

Godly people have disappeared from the earth. 📖

No one among the people is upright.

All wait to spill blood and hunt one another with a net.

## Micah 7:3

Both hands are skilled at evil. 📖

The prince and judge demand a bribe.

When a powerful person expresses an evil desire, they all plot together.

**Help with “bribe”:** This is a payment given to make someone do something wrong.

## Micah 7:4

The best of them is like a thorn bush. 📖

The most upright is sharper than a hedge of thorns.

The day for your watchmen has come, the day of your punishment.

Now is the time of their confusion.

## Micah 7:5

Do not rely on a friend or trust a companion. 📖

Keep your mouth’s doors closed before the woman lying in your arms.

**Help with the doors picture:** This means guarding what you say.

## Micah 7:6

A son dishonors his father, and a daughter rises against her mother. 📖

A son’s wife rises against his mother.

A man’s enemies are the members of his own household.

## Micah 7:7

But I will look to the LORD. 📖

I will wait for God, who saves me.

My God will hear me.

## Micah 7:8

My enemy, do not celebrate over me! 📖

Though I have fallen, I will rise.

Though I sit in darkness, the LORD will be my light.

## Micah 7:9

I have sinned against Him, so I must bear the LORD’s fierce anger until He argues my case. 📖

He will carry out justice for me.

He will bring me into the light, and I will see His righteousness.

**Help with “righteousness”:** This means what is right and just.

## Micah 7:10

My enemy will see and be covered with shame. 📖

She asked me, “Where is the LORD your God?”

My eyes will see her.

Then she will be trampled like mud in the streets.

## Micah 7:11

The day will come to rebuild your walls and extend your boundary. 📖

## Micah 7:12

On that day, people will come to you from Assyria and Egypt’s cities. 📖

They will come from Egypt to the Euphrates, from sea to sea and mountain to mountain.

## Micah 7:13

The earth will become empty and ruined because of its people, as the result of their deeds. 📖

## Micah 7:14

Shepherd Your people with Your staff, the flock that belongs to You as Your inheritance. 📖

They live alone in woodland surrounded by pastures.

Let them feed in Bashan and Gilead as in earlier days.

**About this prayer:** The people ask God to care for them as a shepherd cares for sheep.

## Micah 7:15

God says, “I will show My wonders as in the days when you came out of Egypt.” 📖

## Micah 7:16

Nations will see and be ashamed, deprived of all their might. 📖

They will put hands over their mouths, and their ears will become deaf.

**About this image:** The passage pictures the nations’ response to God’s power.

It does not say that deaf people are morally bad.

## Micah 7:17

They will lick dust like snakes, like reptiles sliding along the ground. 📖

They will come trembling from their defended places before the LORD our God.

They will tremble in fear of You.

## Micah 7:18

Who is a God like You? 📖

You forgive wrongdoing and pass over the sins of Your inheritance’s remnant.

You do not stay angry forever, because You delight in faithful love.

**Help with “remnant”:** This means the people who remain.

## Micah 7:19

He will have compassion on us again and overcome our sins. 📖

You will throw all our sins into the depths of the sea.

**Help with the sea picture:** It pictures sins being removed far away.

## Micah 7:20

You will show faithfulness to Jacob and faithful love to Abraham. 📖

You swore this to our ancestors long ago.
