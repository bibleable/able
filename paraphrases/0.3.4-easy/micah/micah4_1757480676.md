# Micah 4 — Peace, gathering, and rescue

**About this chapter**

The chapter promises teaching, peace, and the LORD’s rule.

It also describes pain, exile to Babylon, rescue, and judgment against gathered nations.

## Micah 4:1

In the last days, the LORD’s temple mountain will be established as the chief mountain, raised above the hills. 📖

Peoples will flow to it.

## Micah 4:2

Many nations will say, “Come, let us go to the LORD’s mountain, to the house of Jacob’s God. 📖

He will teach us His ways, so we may walk in His paths.”

The law will go out from Zion and the LORD’s word from Jerusalem.

## Micah 4:3

He will judge between many peoples and settle disputes for strong nations far and wide. 📖

They will beat swords into plow blades and spears into pruning hooks.

Nation will no longer raise a sword against nation.

They will no longer train for war.

**Help with the tools:** Plow blades cut soil for planting.

Pruning hooks cut branches.

Weapons become farming tools.

## Micah 4:4

Each man will sit under his own grapevine and fig tree, with no one to frighten him. 📖

The LORD of Hosts has spoken.

**Help with “LORD of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Micah 4:5

All nations may walk in their gods’ names. 📖

Yet we will walk in the LORD our God’s name forever and ever.

## Micah 4:6

The LORD declares, “On that day, I will gather those who have difficulty walking. 📖

I will gather the outcasts, even those I have afflicted.

**About this promise:** God includes people with disabilities and people driven away.

The passage does not present disability as a moral fault.

## Micah 4:7

I will make those with difficulty walking into a remnant and the outcasts into a strong nation. 📖

The LORD will rule them on Mount Zion from that day forever.

**Help with “remnant”:** This means the people who remain.

## Micah 4:8

You, the flock’s watchtower and the Daughter of Zion’s defended place, will have your former rule restored. 📖

Royal power will come to the Daughter of Jerusalem.”

**About the city titles:** Zion and Jerusalem are pictured as a daughter.

## Micah 4:9

Why do you cry aloud now? 📖

Is there no king among you?

Has your adviser died, so pain grips you like a woman giving birth?

## Micah 4:10

Daughter of Zion, twist in pain like a woman giving birth. 📖

You will leave the city and camp in the open fields.

You will go to Babylon.

There you will be rescued.

There the LORD will redeem you from your enemies’ hands.

**Help with “redeem”:** This means rescuing or buying back.

## Micah 4:11

Now many nations have gathered against you. 📖

They say, “Let her be made unclean.

Let us feast our eyes on Zion.”

## Micah 4:12

But they do not know the LORD’s thoughts or understand His plan. 📖

He has gathered them like bundles of grain to the threshing floor.

**Help with “threshing floor”:** This is where grain is separated from its stalks.

## Micah 4:13

Rise and thresh, Daughter of Zion! 📖

I will give you iron horns and bronze hooves to break many peoples into pieces.

You will devote their gains to the LORD and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.

**About this picture:** Zion is pictured as a strong threshing animal.

Its action pictures the defeat of the gathered peoples.
