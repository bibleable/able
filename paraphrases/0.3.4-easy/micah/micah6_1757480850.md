# Micah 6 — Do right, love mercy, and walk humbly

**About this chapter**

The LORD brings a case against Israel.

The chapter uses the picture of a court hearing.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Micah 6:1

Listen to the LORD. 📖

“Stand up and present your case before the mountains.

Let the hills hear you.”

## Micah 6:2

Mountains and lasting foundations of the earth, hear the LORD's charge. 📖

He has a case against his people.

He will argue it against Israel.

## Micah 6:3

“My people, what have I done to you? 📖

Speak as witnesses against me.

Tell how I have tired you out.”

## Micah 6:4

“I brought you out of Egypt and freed you from slavery. 📖

I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam to lead you.”

## Micah 6:5

“Remember King Balak of Moab and his plan. 📖

Remember Balaam son of Beor and his answer.

Remember your journey from Shittim to Gilgal.

Then recognise the LORD's right and saving acts.”

## Micah 6:6

What should I bring when I bow before the LORD, God on high? 📖

Should I bring burnt offerings or calves aged 1 year?

## Micah 6:7

Would thousands of rams please the LORD? 📖

What about 10,000 rivers of oil?

Should I give my firstborn for my wrongdoing?

Should I give the child of my body for the sin of my soul?

**Help with this question:** The speaker asks whether even the most extreme gift could deal with wrongdoing.

## Micah 6:8

God has shown you what is good. 📖

The LORD asks you to act fairly, love mercy, and walk humbly with your God.

**Help with words:** Mercy is kindness towards people in need.

Walking humbly means living without pride before God.

## Micah 6:9

The LORD calls to the city. 📖

It is wise to fear your name, LORD.

“Pay attention to the rod and the One who ordered it.”

**Help with “rod”:** Here it pictures punishment.

## Micah 6:10

“Wicked household, can I keep overlooking your treasures gained by evil? 📖

Can I overlook the short ephah, which is cursed?”

**Help with “short ephah”:** An ephah is a measure for goods.

A short measure cheats people by giving them less than they should receive.

## Micah 6:11

“Can I excuse dishonest scales or bags of false weights?” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Sellers used scales and weights to measure goods.

False weights cheated buyers.

## Micah 6:12

“Rich people in the city are violent. 📖

Its people tell lies.

Their words cannot be trusted.”

## Micah 6:13

“I am striking you severely. 📖

I will ruin you because of your sins.”

## Micah 6:14

“You will eat but remain hungry. 📖

You will gather things but cannot keep them safe.

What you save, I will give over to the sword.”

## Micah 6:15

“You will plant but not harvest. 📖

You will press olives but not put their oil on yourselves.

You will tread grapes but not drink the wine.”

## Micah 6:16

“You followed Omri’s rules and all the practices of Ahab’s family. 📖

You listened to their advice.

So I will make your land empty and your people an object of scorn.

You will bear the nations’ insults.”

**Help with these names:** Omri and Ahab were kings who led Israel into evil.
