# Micah 2 — Stolen land and a gathered flock

**About this chapter**

The chapter condemns people who use power to take homes and fields.

It records objections to the message and a promise to gather Israel’s survivors.

## Micah 2:1

How terrible for those who plan wrongdoing and evil on their beds! 📖

At dawn, they do it because they have the power.

## Micah 2:2

They want fields and seize them. 📖

They take houses, robbing a man of his home and another of his inheritance.

**Help with “inheritance”:** This is property given to someone to keep.

## Micah 2:3

The LORD says, “I am planning a disaster against this nation from which you cannot free your necks. 📖

You will no longer walk proudly.

It will be a time of disaster.

## Micah 2:4

On that day, people will mock you with a bitter song of grief. 📖

They will say, ‘We are completely ruined!

He has changed my people’s share.

How He has taken it from me!

He has given our fields to traitors.’”

## Micah 2:5

So you will have no one in the LORD’s gathering to divide the land by lot. 📖

**Help with “by lot”:** This means using marked objects to decide shares.

## Micah 2:6

They preach, “Do not preach! 📖

Do not preach these things.

Disgrace will not overtake us.”

## Micah 2:7

Should Jacob’s people say, “Is the LORD’s Spirit impatient? 📖

Are these the things He does?”

Do My words not bring good to the person who lives rightly?

## Micah 2:8

God says, “Recently, My people have risen like an enemy. 📖

You strip fine robes from unsuspecting people passing by, like men returning from battle.

## Micah 2:9

You drive My people’s women from their pleasant homes. 📖

You take My blessing from their children forever.

## Micah 2:10

Get up and leave! 📖

This is not your resting place.

Its uncleanness brings destruction, terrible destruction!

**About “uncleanness”:** Here this describes the wrongdoing that ruins the land.

## Micah 2:11

If a man full of wind lied, ‘I will preach about wine and strong drink,’ you would welcome that preacher! 📖

**Help with “full of wind”:** This pictures empty, unreliable speech.

## Micah 2:12

I will certainly gather all of you, Jacob. 📖

I will gather Israel’s survivors.

I will bring them together like sheep in a pen, like a flock in its pasture.

They will be a noisy crowd.

## Micah 2:13

One who breaks open the way will go ahead of them. 📖

They will break through the gate and go out.

Their King will go before them, the LORD as their leader.”
