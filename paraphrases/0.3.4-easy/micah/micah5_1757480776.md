# Micah 5 — A ruler from Bethlehem

**About this chapter**

The chapter promises a ruler from Bethlehem Ephrathah.

It describes protection, the remnant among nations, and the LORD’s removal of weapons and false worship.

## Micah 5:1

Daughter of troops, gather your troops now! 📖

We are surrounded for attack.

They will strike Israel’s judge on the cheek with a rod.

**About “Daughter of troops”:** This title pictures the people under siege.

## Micah 5:2

But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, are small among Judah’s clans. 📖

From you will come One for Me to rule Israel.

His origins are from long ago, from the days of eternity.

## Micah 5:3

So Israel will be abandoned until the woman in labor has given birth. 📖

Then the rest of His brothers will return to Israel’s people.

## Micah 5:4

He will stand and shepherd His flock in the LORD’s strength and the majestic name of the LORD His God. 📖

They will live safely, because His greatness will then reach the ends of the earth.

## Micah 5:5

He will be our peace when Assyria invades our land and tramples our fortresses. 📖

We will raise seven shepherds against it, even eight leaders of men.

## Micah 5:6

They will rule Assyria with the sword and Nimrod’s land with drawn blades. 📖

He will rescue us when Assyria invades our land and marches inside our borders.

## Micah 5:7

Jacob’s remnant will be among many peoples like dew from the LORD, like showers on grass. 📖

These do not wait for people or delay for humankind.

**Help with these words:** The remnant means the people who remain.

Dew is water that settles outdoors.

## Micah 5:8

Jacob’s remnant will be among nations and many peoples like a lion among forest animals. 📖

They will be like a young lion among flocks of sheep.

It tramples and tears as it passes, and no one can rescue them.

## Micah 5:9

Your hand will be raised over your foes, and all your enemies will be cut off. 📖

## Micah 5:10

The LORD declares, “On that day, I will remove your horses and wreck your chariots. 📖

**Help with “chariots”:** These are vehicles pulled by horses and used in war.

## Micah 5:11

I will remove your land’s cities and tear down all your defended places. 📖

## Micah 5:12

I will remove sorcery from your hands, and you will have no fortune-tellers. 📖

**Help with these words:** Sorcery means using claimed magical powers.

Fortune-tellers claim to tell what will happen in the future.

## Micah 5:13

I will remove your carved images and sacred pillars. 📖

You will no longer bow to things your own hands made.

**Help with “sacred pillars”:** These are standing stones used in worship.

## Micah 5:14

I will uproot the Asherah poles among you and destroy your cities. 📖

**Help with “Asherah poles”:** These are objects connected with worship of the goddess Asherah.

## Micah 5:15

I will take revenge in fierce anger on the nations that have not obeyed Me.” 📖
