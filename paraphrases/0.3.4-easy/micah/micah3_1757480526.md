# Micah 3 — Corrupt leaders and prophets

**About this chapter**

Micah confronts rulers who harm the people and prophets who speak for payment.

God announces that their false confidence will end in destruction.

## Micah 3:1

I said, “Leaders of Jacob and rulers of Israel’s people, listen! 📖

Should you not know justice?

## Micah 3:2

You hate good and love evil. 📖

You tear skin from my people and flesh from their bones.

## Micah 3:3

You eat my people’s flesh after stripping their skin and breaking their bones. 📖

You chop them like meat for a cooking pot or a large boiling pot.”

**About these images:** Micah uses this terrible picture to describe the leaders’ cruel treatment of people.

## Micah 3:4

Then they will cry to the LORD, but He will not answer. 📖

He will hide His face from them then because of their evil deeds.

**Help with “hide His face”:** This pictures God withdrawing His favor.

## Micah 3:5

The LORD says, “The prophets lead My people astray. 📖

They announce peace while they chew food, but declare war against anyone putting nothing in their mouths.

## Micah 3:6

So night will come over you without visions, and darkness without messages about hidden things. 📖

The sun will set on these prophets, and daylight will turn black over them.

## Micah 3:7

The seers will be ashamed, and the diviners will be disgraced. 📖

All will cover their mouths, because there is no answer from God.”

**Help with these titles:** Seers receive visions.

Diviners claim to discover hidden messages.

## Micah 3:8

But I am filled with power by the LORD’s Spirit, with justice and courage. 📖

I can tell Jacob his wrongdoing and Israel his sin.

## Micah 3:9

Leaders of Jacob’s people and rulers of Israel’s people, hear this! 📖

You despise justice and twist everything that is right,

## Micah 3:10

building Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with wrongdoing. 📖

## Micah 3:11

Her leaders judge for bribes, her priests teach for payment, and her prophets seek hidden messages for money. 📖

Yet they rely on the LORD.

They say, “Is the LORD not among us?

No disaster can come on us.”

**Help with “bribes”:** These are payments given to make someone do something wrong.

## Micah 3:12

So because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field. 📖

Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins, and the temple mountain a ridge covered with trees.
