# Micah 1 — Judgment and mourning for the towns

**About this chapter**

Micah receives the LORD’s word about Samaria and Jerusalem.

He describes God’s coming judgment and mourns for the towns.

The place names and strong pictures of destruction are kept.

## Micah 1:1

The LORD’s word came to Micah the Moreshite during the rule of Judah’s kings Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah. 📖

These are the things he saw about Samaria and Jerusalem.

## Micah 1:2

All peoples, hear! 📖

Earth and everyone in it, listen!

May the Lord GOD speak as a witness against you from His holy temple.

## Micah 1:3

Look! 📖

The LORD comes from His home.

He will come down and walk on the earth’s high places.

## Micah 1:4

Mountains will melt under Him, and valleys will split apart. 📖

They will be like wax before fire, like water rushing down a slope.

## Micah 1:5

This is because of Jacob’s wrongdoing and the sins of Israel’s people. 📖

What is Jacob’s wrongdoing?

Is it not Samaria?

What is Judah’s high place of worship?

Is it not Jerusalem?

## Micah 1:6

God says, “I will make Samaria a heap of ruins in the open field, a place to plant grapevines. 📖

I will pour her stones into the valley and uncover her foundations.

## Micah 1:7

All her carved images will be smashed. 📖

All her wages will be burned, and I will destroy all her idols.

She gathered a prostitute’s wages, and they will be used on a prostitute again.”

**Help with “prostitute”:** This is someone who provides sexual acts for payment or other benefits.

**About this picture:** The passage connects Samaria’s false worship with prostitution and its payments.

## Micah 1:8

Because of this, I will grieve and wail. 📖

I will walk barefoot and naked.

I will howl like a jackal and mourn like an ostrich.

**About the speaker:** Micah describes his grief.

## Micah 1:9

Her wound cannot be healed. 📖

It has reached Judah and my people’s gate, as far as Jerusalem itself.

## Micah 1:10

Do not tell it in Gath. 📖

Do not weep at all.

Roll in the dust in Beth-leaphrah.

## Micah 1:11

People of Shaphir, leave in shameful nakedness. 📖

Zaanan’s people will not come out.

Beth-ezel mourns, and its support is taken from you.

## Micah 1:12

Maroth’s people longed for good. 📖

But disaster came from the LORD, even to Jerusalem’s gate.

## Micah 1:13

People of Lachish, harness your chariot horses. 📖

You were the beginning of sin for the Daughter of Zion.

Israel’s wrong actions were found in you.

**Help with these words:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses.

“Daughter of Zion” pictures the city and its people as a woman.

## Micah 1:14

So send farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath. 📖

Achzib’s houses will prove deceptive to Israel’s kings.

## Micah 1:15

God says, “People of Mareshah, I will again bring a conqueror against you. 📖

Israel’s glory will come to Adullam.”

## Micah 1:16

Shave yourselves bald and cut your hair in grief for your precious children. 📖

Make yourselves as bald as an eagle, because they will be taken from you into exile.

**Help with “exile”:** This means being forced to live away from their homeland.
