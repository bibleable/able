# 2 John 1 — Walk in truth and love

**About this chapter**

The elder writes to the chosen lady and her children.

He urges them to love each other and remain in Christ's teaching.

## 2 John 1:1

This letter is from the elder to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in the truth. 📖

Everyone who knows the truth loves you too.

## 2 John 1:2

We love you because of the truth that remains in us and will be with us for ever. 📖

## 2 John 1:3

Kindness, mercy, and peace will be with us in truth and love. 📖

They come from God the Father and Jesus Christ, the Father's Son.

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## 2 John 1:4

I was overjoyed to find some of your children living in the truth, as the Father commanded us. 📖

## 2 John 1:5

Now, dear lady, I urge us to love each other. 📖

This is the command we have had from the beginning, rather than a new command.

## 2 John 1:6

Love means living according to his commands. 📖

The command you heard from the beginning is to live in love.

## 2 John 1:7

Many deceivers have gone into the world. 📖

They refuse to acknowledge Jesus Christ coming as a real human being.

Such a person is the deceiver and the antichrist.

**Help with words:** A deceiver tricks people with lies.

Antichrist means someone opposed to Christ.

## 2 John 1:8

Watch yourselves, so you do not lose what we worked for, but receive your full reward. 📖

## 2 John 1:9

Anyone running ahead without remaining in Christ's teaching does not have God. 📖

Anyone remaining in his teaching has both the Father and the Son.

## 2 John 1:10

If someone comes without this teaching, do not welcome them into your home or even greet them. 📖

## 2 John 1:11

Anyone greeting such a person shares in their evil actions. 📖

## 2 John 1:12

I have much to write, but prefer not to do it with paper and ink. 📖

Instead, I hope to visit and speak face to face, so our joy may be complete.

## 2 John 1:13

The children of your chosen sister send you greetings. 📖
