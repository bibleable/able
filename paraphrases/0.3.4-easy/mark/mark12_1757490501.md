# Mark 12 — Loving God and loving your neighbour

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells a story about a vineyard.

He answers questions about taxes, rising from the dead, and God's commands.

He warns about dishonest teachers and notices a poor widow's gift.

## Mark 12:1

Jesus began speaking to them in stories with a meaning. 📖

He said, “A man planted a vineyard and built a wall around it.

He dug a place to collect grape juice and built a watchtower.

He rented the vineyard to some farmers and went away on a journey.”

**Help with words:** A vineyard is a field where grapes grow.

A watchtower is a high place where someone can watch over the land.

## Mark 12:2

Jesus said, “When the grapes were ready, the owner sent a servant to the farmers. 📖

The servant was to collect the owner's share of the fruit.”

## Mark 12:3

Jesus said, “But the farmers grabbed the servant and beat him. 📖

They sent him away with nothing.”

## Mark 12:4

Jesus said, “The owner sent another servant. 📖

They hit him on the head and treated him shamefully.”

## Mark 12:5

Jesus said, “He sent another servant, and they killed him. 📖

He sent many others.

They beat some and killed others.”

## Mark 12:6

Jesus said, “The owner had one son whom he loved. 📖

Last of all, he sent his son.

He said, ‘They will respect my son.’”

## Mark 12:7

Jesus said, “But the farmers said to each other, ‘This son will inherit the vineyard. 📖

Come, let us kill him.

Then it will belong to us.’”

**Help with a word:** Inherit means to receive someone's property after they die.

## Mark 12:8

Jesus said, “They grabbed the son, killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.” 📖

## Mark 12:9

Jesus asked, “What will the vineyard's owner do? 📖

He will come and kill those farmers.

He will give the vineyard to others.”

## Mark 12:10

Jesus asked, “Have you never read these words in the Scriptures? 📖

‘The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.’”

**Help with words:** The Scriptures are the holy writings.

A cornerstone is an important stone in a building.

The picture contrasts being rejected with being given an important place.

## Mark 12:11

Jesus continued the quotation, “‘The Lord has done this. 📖

It is wonderful to us.’”

## Mark 12:12

The leaders knew that Jesus had told the vineyard story against them. 📖

They wanted to arrest him.

But they feared the crowd, so they left him and went away.

## Mark 12:13

Later, the leaders sent some Pharisees and Herodians to Jesus. 📖

They wanted to trap him through something he said.

**Help with names:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

Herodians supported Herod's rule.

## Mark 12:14

They said, “Teacher, we know you are honest. 📖

You do not try to win people's favour or treat some as more important than others.

You teach God's way truthfully.

Does God's law allow us to pay taxes to Caesar?

Should we pay or not?”

**Help with names:** Caesar was the title of the Roman ruler.

Taxes are money people must pay to the government.

## Mark 12:15

Jesus knew they were pretending. 📖

He asked, “Why are you testing me?

Bring me a denarius to look at.”

**Help with a word:** A denarius was a Roman coin.

## Mark 12:16

They brought the coin. 📖

Jesus asked, “Whose picture and writing are on it?”

They answered, “Caesar's.”

## Mark 12:17

Jesus told them, “Give Caesar what belongs to Caesar. 📖

Give God what belongs to God.”

They were amazed at him.

## Mark 12:18

Some Sadducees came to Jesus with a question. 📖

They said that the dead do not rise again.

**Help with a name:** Sadducees were a Jewish religious group.

## Mark 12:19

They said, “Teacher, Moses wrote a rule for us. 📖

A man's brother might die, leaving a wife but no children.

The man must marry his brother's widow.

He must have children to continue his brother's family.”

**Help with a word:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## Mark 12:20

They said, “There were seven brothers. 📖

The first married and died without children.”

## Mark 12:21

They said, “The second brother married the widow. 📖

He also died without children.

The same happened with the third brother.”

## Mark 12:22

They said, “None of the seven brothers left any children. 📖

Last of all, the woman died.”

## Mark 12:23

They asked, “When the dead rise, whose wife will she be? 📖

All seven brothers married her.”

## Mark 12:24

Jesus answered, “Are you not wrong because you do not know the Scriptures or God's power?” 📖

## Mark 12:25

Jesus said, “When the dead rise, they will not marry or be given in marriage. 📖

Instead, they will be like the angels in heaven.”

**Help with these words:** Jesus compares them with angels when explaining that they will not marry.

## Mark 12:26

Jesus said, “Think about the dead rising. 📖

Have you not read about the burning bush in the Book of Moses?

God told Moses, ‘I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’”

## Mark 12:27

Jesus said, “He is the God of the living, not the dead. 📖

You are very wrong!”

## Mark 12:28

A teacher of God's law heard them debating. 📖

He noticed that Jesus had answered well.

He asked, “Which command is the most important of all?”

## Mark 12:29

Jesus answered, “This is the most important. 📖

‘Listen, Israel!

The Lord is our God.

The Lord is one.’”

## Mark 12:30

Jesus continued, “‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength.’” 📖

**Help with these words:** This means loving God with your whole self.

## Mark 12:31

Jesus said, “The second command is this. 📖

‘Love your neighbour as yourself.’

No other command is greater than these.”

## Mark 12:32

The teacher replied, “You are right, Teacher. 📖

You have spoken the truth.

God is one, and there is no other God.”

## Mark 12:33

The teacher said, “We must love God with all our heart, understanding, and strength. 📖

We must love our neighbour as ourselves.

This matters more than all burnt offerings and sacrifices.”

**Help with words:** These offerings were gifts presented to God in worship.

Burnt offerings were burned on an altar.

## Mark 12:34

Jesus saw that the man had answered wisely. 📖

He said, “You are not far from God's kingdom.”

After that, no one dared to ask Jesus any more questions.

**Help with a word:** God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## Mark 12:35

Jesus was teaching in the temple courtyards. 📖

He asked, “How can the teachers of God's law say that the Christ is David's son?”

**Help with words:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

David's son here means someone born later in King David's family line.

## Mark 12:36

Jesus said, “David himself spoke by the Holy Spirit. 📖

He said, ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand.

Stay there until I put your enemies under your feet.”’”

**Help with these pictures:** Sitting at the right hand is a place of honour.

Enemies under someone's feet pictures victory over them.

## Mark 12:37

Jesus asked, “David himself calls him ‘Lord.’ 📖

So how can he be David's son?”

The large crowd enjoyed listening to Jesus.

## Mark 12:38

As Jesus taught, he said, “Watch out for the teachers of God's law. 📖

They like walking in long robes and being greeted in the marketplaces.”

## Mark 12:39

Jesus said, “They like the most important seats in synagogues and the places of honour at special meals.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Synagogues are Jewish places for worship and teaching.

## Mark 12:40

Jesus said, “They cheat widows out of their homes. 📖

They make long prayers to impress people.

These men will receive a more severe judgment.”

## Mark 12:41

Jesus sat opposite the place where people put gifts of money for the temple. 📖

He watched the crowd putting money in.

Many rich people gave large amounts.

## Mark 12:42

A poor widow came and put in two small copper coins. 📖

Together, they were worth only a small part of a denarius.

## Mark 12:43

Jesus called his followers to him. 📖

He said, “I tell you the truth.

This poor widow has given more than everyone else.”

## Mark 12:44

Jesus said, “The others gave from their extra money. 📖

But she is poor and gave everything she had to live on.”
