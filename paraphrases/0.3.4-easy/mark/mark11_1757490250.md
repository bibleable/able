# Mark 11 — Jesus enters Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jesus enters Jerusalem while a crowd praises him.

He acts against trading in the temple and teaches about prayer.

Religious leaders question his authority.

## Mark 11:1

Jesus and his followers came near Jerusalem. 📖

They reached Bethphage and Bethany, at the Mount of Olives.

Jesus sent two of his followers ahead.

## Mark 11:2

Jesus said, “Go into the village ahead. 📖

As soon as you enter, you will find a colt tied there.

No one has ever ridden it.

Untie it and bring it here.”

**Help with a word:** A colt is a young horse or donkey.

## Mark 11:3

Jesus said, “Someone may ask why you are doing this. 📖

Tell them, ‘The Lord needs it.

He will send it back soon.’”

## Mark 11:4

They went and found the colt outside in the street. 📖

It was tied at a doorway.

They untied it.

## Mark 11:5

Some people standing there asked, “Why are you untying the colt?” 📖

## Mark 11:6

The followers answered as Jesus had told them. 📖

The people let them take it.

## Mark 11:7

They brought the colt to Jesus and laid their outer coats over it. 📖

Jesus sat on it.

## Mark 11:8

Many people spread their outer coats on the road. 📖

Others spread branches they had cut from the fields.

## Mark 11:9

People ahead of Jesus and behind him shouted, “Hosanna! 📖

Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord's name!”

**Help with a word:** Hosanna is a call meaning “Save us,” also used in praise.

## Mark 11:10

They shouted, “Blessed is the coming kingdom of our father David! 📖

Hosanna in the highest!”

**Help with these words:** David was Israel's king long before this time.

The people call him their father as an honoured ancestor.

## Mark 11:11

Jesus entered Jerusalem and went into the temple courtyards. 📖

He looked around at everything.

It was already late, so he went to Bethany with the 12 apostles.

**Help with a word:** The temple was the special place of worship in Jerusalem.

Its courtyards were open spaces around the main building.

## Mark 11:12

The next day, they left Bethany. 📖

Jesus was hungry.

## Mark 11:13

Jesus saw a fig tree with leaves in the distance. 📖

He went to see whether it had any fruit.

But he found only leaves.

It was not the season for figs.

## Mark 11:14

Jesus said to the tree, “May no one ever eat your fruit again.” 📖

His followers heard him say this.

## Mark 11:15

They reached Jerusalem, and Jesus entered the temple courtyards. 📖

He began driving out the people buying and selling there.

He turned over the money changers' tables and the seats of the people selling doves.

**Help with words:** Money changers exchanged one kind of money for another.

## Mark 11:16

Jesus would not let anyone carry goods for sale through the temple courtyards. 📖

## Mark 11:17

Jesus began teaching them. 📖

He said, “Do the Scriptures not say, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations’?

But you have made it ‘a hiding place for robbers.’”

**Help with words:** Jesus quotes the holy writings.

My house refers to God's temple.

## Mark 11:18

The chief priests and teachers of God's law heard this. 📖

They looked for a way to kill Jesus.

They feared him because the whole crowd was amazed at his teaching.

**Help with words:** Chief priests were leading priests.

## Mark 11:19

When evening came, Jesus and his followers left the city. 📖

## Mark 11:20

As they walked back in the morning, they saw the fig tree. 📖

It had dried up from the roots.

## Mark 11:21

Peter remembered what had happened. 📖

He said, “Look, Rabbi!

The fig tree you cursed has dried up.”

**Help with words:** Rabbi means teacher.

Peter is remembering Jesus's words that no one would eat the tree's fruit again.

## Mark 11:22

Jesus told them, “Have faith in God.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in God here.

## Mark 11:23

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 📖

Someone may tell this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and thrown into the sea.’

If they believe it will happen, without doubting in their heart, it will be done for them.”

## Mark 11:24

Jesus said, “So I tell you this. 📖

Whatever you ask for in prayer, believe that you have received it.

It will be yours.”

## Mark 11:25

Jesus said, “When you stand to pray, forgive anyone you hold something against. 📖

Then your Father in heaven will also forgive your wrongdoing.”

## Mark 11:26

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Mark 11:27

They returned to Jerusalem. 📖

Jesus walked in the temple courtyards.

The chief priests, teachers of God's law, and elders came to him.

**Help with a word:** Elders were leaders of the people.

## Mark 11:28

They asked, “What authority do you have to do these things? 📖

Who gave you that authority?”

**Help with a word:** Authority means the right to speak or act with power.

## Mark 11:29

Jesus answered, “I will ask you one question. 📖

If you answer me, I will tell you what authority I have to do these things.”

## Mark 11:30

Jesus asked, “Did John's baptism come from heaven or from people? 📖

Answer me!”

**Help with these words:** Jesus asks whether God sent John to baptize people.

## Mark 11:31

They discussed what to answer. 📖

They said, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ he will ask, ‘Then why did you not believe John?’”

## Mark 11:32

They said, “But if we say, ‘From people’...” 📖

They feared the crowd.

Everyone believed that John truly was a prophet.

**Help with a word:** A prophet speaks God's message.

## Mark 11:33

They answered Jesus, “We do not know.” 📖

Jesus replied, “Then I will not tell you what authority I have to do these things.”
