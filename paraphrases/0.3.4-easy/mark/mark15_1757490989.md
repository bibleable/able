# Mark 15 — Jesus dies and is buried

**About this chapter**

Jesus is brought before Pilate.

Soldiers mock him and nail him to a cross.

Jesus dies, and Joseph of Arimathea buries him.

## Mark 15:1

Early in the morning, the chief priests, elders, teachers of God's law, and the whole Sanhedrin made a plan. 📖

They tied Jesus up and led him away.

They handed him over to Pilate.

**Help with words:** The Sanhedrin was the Jewish ruling council.

Chief priests and elders were leaders.

Pilate was the Roman governor.

## Mark 15:2

Pilate asked Jesus, “Are you the King of the Jews?” 📖

Jesus answered, “You have said so.”

## Mark 15:3

The chief priests began accusing Jesus of many things. 📖

## Mark 15:4

Pilate asked again, “Have you no answer? 📖

Look at all the things they accuse you of doing!”

## Mark 15:5

Jesus gave no further answer. 📖

This amazed Pilate.

## Mark 15:6

At the festival, Pilate usually freed one prisoner chosen by the people. 📖

## Mark 15:7

A man called Barabbas was in prison with some rebels. 📖

The rebels had committed murder during a violent uprising.

**Help with words:** Rebels are people who fight against those ruling them.

An uprising is a fight against the rulers.

## Mark 15:8

The crowd came and asked Pilate to do what he usually did. 📖

## Mark 15:9

Pilate asked, “Do you want me to free the King of the Jews?” 📖

## Mark 15:10

Pilate knew that the chief priests had handed Jesus over because they were jealous of him. 📖

## Mark 15:11

But the chief priests stirred up the crowd. 📖

They wanted Pilate to free Barabbas instead.

## Mark 15:12

Pilate asked again, “Then what do you want me to do with the man you call King of the Jews?” 📖

## Mark 15:13

The crowd shouted back, “Crucify him!” 📖

**Help with a word:** Crucify means to kill someone by fastening them to a cross.

## Mark 15:14

Pilate asked, “Why? 📖

What evil has he done?”

But they shouted even louder, “Crucify him!”

## Mark 15:15

Pilate wanted to please the crowd, so he freed Barabbas. 📖

He had Jesus whipped.

Then he handed Jesus over to be crucified.

## Mark 15:16

The soldiers led Jesus into the palace, called the Praetorium. 📖

They called the whole group of soldiers together.

## Mark 15:17

They dressed Jesus in a purple robe. 📖

They twisted thorn branches into a crown and put it on his head.

## Mark 15:18

They began greeting him, “Hail, King of the Jews!” 📖

**Help with the greeting:** The soldiers were pretending to honour Jesus as a king while mocking him.

## Mark 15:19

They kept hitting Jesus's head with a stick and spitting on him. 📖

They knelt and bowed before him.

## Mark 15:20

After mocking Jesus, they took off the purple robe. 📖

They put his own clothes back on him.

They led him out to crucify him.

## Mark 15:21

Simon from Cyrene was passing by, coming in from the countryside. 📖

He was the father of Alexander and Rufus.

The soldiers forced him to carry Jesus's cross.

## Mark 15:22

They brought Jesus to Golgotha. 📖

That name means The Place of the Skull.

## Mark 15:23

They offered Jesus wine mixed with myrrh. 📖

He did not take it.

**Help with a word:** Myrrh is a substance from a tree, used in perfumes and medicines.

## Mark 15:24

They crucified Jesus. 📖

They divided his clothes by casting lots to decide what each soldier would take.

**Help with these words:** Casting lots means making a choice by chance, like drawing marked pieces.

## Mark 15:25

It was the third hour when they crucified Jesus. 📖

**Help with time:** Counting hours from sunrise, this was about 9 in the morning.

## Mark 15:26

The written charge against Jesus said, “THE KING OF THE JEWS.” 📖

## Mark 15:27

They crucified two robbers with Jesus. 📖

One was on his right and one on his left.

## Mark 15:28

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Mark 15:29

People passing by insulted Jesus and shook their heads. 📖

They said, “Aha!

You were going to destroy the temple and build it again in three days!”

## Mark 15:30

They said, “Come down from the cross and save yourself!” 📖

## Mark 15:31

The chief priests and teachers of God's law also mocked Jesus among themselves. 📖

They said, “He saved others, but he cannot save himself!”

## Mark 15:32

They said, “Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down from the cross now. 📖

Then we will see and believe!”

Even the men crucified with him insulted him.

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Mark 15:33

Darkness covered all the land from the sixth hour until the ninth hour. 📖

**Help with time:** This was about noon until 3 in the afternoon, counting hours from sunrise.

## Mark 15:34

At the ninth hour, Jesus cried out loudly, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” 📖

This means, “My God, my God, why have you left me?”

## Mark 15:35

Some people nearby heard him. 📖

They said, “Look, he is calling Elijah.”

## Mark 15:36

Someone ran and filled a sponge with sour wine. 📖

He put it on a reed and held it up for Jesus to drink.

He said, “Leave him alone.

Let us see whether Elijah comes to take him down.”

**Help with a word:** A reed is a long plant stem.

## Mark 15:37

Jesus gave a loud cry and took his last breath. 📖

## Mark 15:38

The temple curtain tore in two from top to bottom. 📖

## Mark 15:39

A Roman officer stood facing Jesus. 📖

He saw how Jesus took his last breath.

He said, “This man truly was the Son of God!”

**Help with a word:** The source calls this officer a centurion, a leader of soldiers.

## Mark 15:40

Some women watched from a distance. 📖

They included Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and Joses, and Salome.

## Mark 15:41

These women had followed Jesus and cared for his needs in Galilee. 📖

Many other women had come with him to Jerusalem.

## Mark 15:42

It was already evening on Preparation Day. 📖

This was the day before the Sabbath.

**Help with a word:** The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## Mark 15:43

Joseph from Arimathea was an important member of the Council. 📖

He was waiting for God's kingdom.

He bravely went to Pilate and asked for Jesus's body.

**Help with words:** The Council means the Sanhedrin.

God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## Mark 15:44

Pilate was surprised to hear that Jesus was already dead. 📖

He called the Roman officer and asked whether this was true.

## Mark 15:45

The officer confirmed Jesus's death. 📖

Then Pilate gave Joseph permission to take the body.

## Mark 15:46

Joseph bought a linen cloth and took Jesus's body down. 📖

He wrapped it in the cloth and placed it in a tomb cut out of rock.

He rolled a stone against the entrance.

**Help with words:** Linen is a kind of cloth made from plant fibres.

A tomb is a place where a dead person is buried.

## Mark 15:47

Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joseph saw where Jesus's body was placed. 📖
