# Mark 1 — Jesus begins his work

**About this chapter**

John prepares people for Jesus.

Jesus begins to teach, heal people, and call followers.

## Mark 1:1

This is the beginning of the good news about Jesus Christ, the Son of God. 📖

**Help with “Christ”:** This title means the Anointed One, the Messiah God promised.

## Mark 1:2

This is written in Isaiah the prophet: “I will send My messenger ahead of You. 📖

He will prepare Your way.”

**About these words:** This is a quotation speaking of God sending His messenger.

## Mark 1:3

“Someone calls out in the desert. 📖

Get the way ready for the Lord.

Make straight paths for him.”

**Help with this picture:** Preparing a path pictures getting ready to welcome the Lord.

## Mark 1:4

John the Baptist appeared in the desert. 📖

He told people to turn away from sin and be baptized, so their sins could be forgiven.

**Help with words:** Sin is wrongdoing against God.

Baptism uses water as a sign of turning to God.

## Mark 1:5

People came to John from all over Jerusalem and the countryside of Judea. 📖

They admitted their sins.

John baptized them in the Jordan River.

## Mark 1:6

John wore clothes made from camel hair, with a leather belt around his waist. 📖

He ate locusts and wild honey.

**Help with a word:** Locusts are insects like large grasshoppers.

## Mark 1:7

John said, “Someone more powerful than me will come after me. 📖

I am not worthy even to bend down and untie His sandal straps.

**Help with these words:** John speaks of being unworthy to perform even this small act of service.

## Mark 1:8

I baptize you with water. 📖

But He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.”

**Help with these words:** John describes what the coming One will do through God’s Spirit, beyond baptism with water.

## Mark 1:9

At that time, Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee. 📖

John baptized him in the Jordan River.

## Mark 1:10

As soon as Jesus came out of the water, He saw heaven being torn open. 📖

The Spirit came down on Him like a dove.

## Mark 1:11

A voice from heaven said, “You are My Son, whom I love. 📖

I am very pleased with You.”

**About the voice:** The voice addresses Jesus as “My Son.”

## Mark 1:12

At once, the Spirit drove Jesus into the desert, 📖

**Help with “drove”:** This describes a forceful movement, stronger than merely suggesting that Jesus go.

## Mark 1:13

Jesus stayed in the desert for 40 days. 📖

Satan tried to make him do wrong.

Jesus was with wild animals.

Angels cared for him.

## Mark 1:14

After John was arrested, Jesus went to Galilee. 📖

Jesus told people God's good news.

## Mark 1:15

Jesus said, “The time has come. 📖

God's kingdom is near.

Turn away from sin and believe the good news!”

**Help with a word:** God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## Mark 1:16

Jesus walked beside the Sea of Galilee. 📖

He saw Simon and Simon's brother Andrew throwing a net into the water.

Their work was catching fish.

## Mark 1:17

Jesus said, “Come and follow Me. 📖

I will make you fishers of people.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus compares calling people to follow Him with the work of fishing.

This is not a command to trap people.

## Mark 1:18

At once, Simon and Andrew left their nets and followed Jesus. 📖

## Mark 1:19

Jesus walked a little further. 📖

He saw James, the son of Zebedee, and James's brother John.

They were in a boat, fixing their nets.

## Mark 1:20

Jesus called them at once. 📖

They left their father Zebedee in the boat with the paid workers.

They followed Jesus.

## Mark 1:21

Jesus and his companions went to Capernaum. 📖

On the Sabbath, Jesus went straight into the synagogue and began to teach.

**Help with words:** The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

A synagogue is a Jewish place for worship and teaching.

## Mark 1:22

The people were amazed at Jesus's teaching. 📖

He taught with authority, unlike the teachers of God's law.

**Help with a word:** Authority means the right to speak or act with power.

## Mark 1:23

Suddenly, a man with an unclean spirit shouted in the synagogue. 📖

**Help with a word:** An unclean spirit means an evil spirit here.

It does not mean that the man was dirty.

## Mark 1:24

The spirit said, “Jesus of Nazareth, what do You want with us? 📖

Have You come to destroy us?

I know who You are.

You are God’s Holy One!”

**Help with “Holy One”:** Holy describes being pure and set apart for God.

## Mark 1:25

Jesus gave the spirit a firm command. 📖

“Be quiet!

Come out of him!”

## Mark 1:26

The unclean spirit made the man's body shake hard. 📖

Then it came out with a loud scream.

## Mark 1:27

Everyone was amazed. 📖

They asked each other, “What is this?

This is a new teaching with authority!

He even gives orders to unclean spirits.

They obey him!”

## Mark 1:28

News about Jesus spread quickly through the whole area of Galilee. 📖

## Mark 1:29

Jesus and His companions left the synagogue. 📖

Immediately they went with James and John to Simon and Andrew’s home.

## Mark 1:30

The mother of Simon's wife was sick in bed with a fever. 📖

They told Jesus about her at once.

**Help with a word:** A fever makes a person's body hotter than usual because they are ill.

## Mark 1:31

Jesus went to her and held her hand. 📖

He helped her get up.

Her fever went away.

She began to serve them.

**Help with “serve”:** Here this means attending to their needs.

## Mark 1:32

That evening, after the sun went down, people brought all the sick people to Jesus. 📖

They also brought people who had evil spirits.

## Mark 1:33

The whole town gathered at the door. 📖

## Mark 1:34

Jesus healed many people who had different diseases. 📖

He drove out many evil spirits.

He would not let the spirits speak, because they knew who he was.

## Mark 1:35

Jesus got up very early, while it was still dark. 📖

He went out to a place where he could be alone and pray.

## Mark 1:36

Simon and his companions went to look for Jesus. 📖

## Mark 1:37

They found Jesus and said, “Everyone is looking for you!” 📖

## Mark 1:38

Jesus answered, “Let us go to the towns nearby. 📖

I can tell the good news there too.

That is why I have come.”

## Mark 1:39

Jesus travelled through all of Galilee. 📖

He told the good news in their synagogues and drove out evil spirits.

## Mark 1:40

A man with leprosy came to Jesus. 📖

He knelt and begged, “If you are willing, you can make me clean.”

**Help with words:** Leprosy names a serious skin disease in this passage.

Being made clean includes healing and being accepted as clean under the religious law.

The man's illness does not mean he is a bad person.

## Mark 1:41

Jesus felt deep care for the man. 📖

Jesus reached out his hand and touched him.

He said, “I am willing.

Be clean!”

## Mark 1:42

At once, the man's leprosy went away. 📖

He was made clean.

## Mark 1:43

Jesus sent the man away at once, with a firm warning. 📖

## Mark 1:44

Jesus said, “Make sure you do not tell anyone. 📖

Go and show yourself to the priest.

Give the offering Moses commanded for being made clean.

This will be evidence for them.”

**Help with this instruction:** The priest checked whether a person could be accepted as clean under the religious law.

## Mark 1:45

But the man went out and told the news openly. 📖

He spread it widely.

Because of this, Jesus could no longer enter a town openly.

He stayed in lonely places.

Still, people came to Him from every direction.
