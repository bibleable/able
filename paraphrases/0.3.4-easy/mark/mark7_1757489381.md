# Mark 7 — What makes a person unclean?

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers questions about religious washing and human traditions.

He helps a woman's daughter and a man who cannot hear well or speak clearly.

## Mark 7:1

Pharisees and some teachers of God's law gathered around Jesus. 📖

They had come from Jerusalem.

**Help with a name:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## Mark 7:2

They saw some of Jesus's followers eating with hands they called unclean. 📖

Their hands had not been washed in the required way.

**Help with a word:** Unclean here means not meeting religious rules about washing.

## Mark 7:3

The Pharisees and all the Jews held to the elders’ tradition. 📖

They did not eat without first washing their hands in a special religious way.

**Help with these words:** Elders were leaders of the people.

Traditions are teachings and ways of doing things passed down from earlier people.

Mark describes the customs in this account.

## Mark 7:4

When they returned from the market, they did not eat without washing. 📖

They followed many other traditions too.

They washed cups, jugs, cooking pots, and the couches used for meals.

## Mark 7:5

The Pharisees and teachers of God's law questioned Jesus. 📖

They asked, “Why do your followers not follow the elders' traditions?

They eat with unclean hands.”

## Mark 7:6

Jesus answered, “Isaiah spoke God's message correctly about you hypocrites. 📖

He wrote, ‘These people honour me with their words.

But their hearts are far from me.’”

**Help with a word:** Hypocrites pretend to be faithful while their actions show otherwise.

## Mark 7:7

Jesus continued the words from Isaiah. 📖

“‘Their worship of me is empty.

They teach human rules as God's teaching.’”

## Mark 7:8

Jesus said, “You leave God's command aside. 📖

You hold on to human traditions.”

## Mark 7:9

Jesus continued, “You are clever at setting aside God's command so you can keep your own tradition.” 📖

## Mark 7:10

Jesus said, “Moses said, ‘Honour your father and mother.’ 📖

He also said, ‘Anyone who curses their father or mother must be put to death.’”

**Help with the speaker:** Jesus is quoting the law given through Moses.

## Mark 7:11

Jesus said, “But you say this. 📖

If someone tells their father or mother, ‘What you would receive from me is Corban,’

**Help with “Corban”:** This means a gift devoted to God.

The person says that what could help their parents has been set apart for God.

## Mark 7:12

you no longer let that person do anything for their father or mother. 📖

## Mark 7:13

Jesus said, “Your tradition makes God's word count for nothing. 📖

You pass this tradition on to others.

You do many other things like this.”

## Mark 7:14

Jesus called the crowd to him again. 📖

He said, “All of you, listen to me and understand.”

## Mark 7:15

Jesus said, “Nothing that enters a person from outside can make that person unclean. 📖

What comes out of a person makes them unclean.”

**Help with this teaching:** Jesus explains in the following verses that he is talking about food and wrongdoing.

## Mark 7:16

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Mark 7:17

Jesus left the crowd and entered the house. 📖

His followers asked him about the picture he had used.

## Mark 7:18

Jesus asked, “Are you still so slow to understand? 📖

Do you not understand this?

Nothing that enters a person from outside can make that person unclean.”

## Mark 7:19

Jesus said, “It does not go into their heart. 📖

It goes into their stomach, and then passes out of their body.”

In saying this, Jesus showed that all foods are clean.

**Help with a word:** Heart here means a person's inner thoughts and desires.

## Mark 7:20

Jesus continued, “What comes out of a person makes them unclean.” 📖

## Mark 7:21

Jesus said, “Wrong thoughts come from inside a person's heart. 📖

So do wrong sexual acts, stealing, murder, and adultery.”

**Help with a word:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

## Mark 7:22

Jesus continued, “So do greed, wickedness, deceit, uncontrolled shameful behavior, envy, slander, arrogance, and foolishness. 📖

**Help with these words:** Greed means always wanting more for yourself.

Deceit means misleading or tricking people.

Envy means resenting someone because they have something you want.

Slander means making false statements that harm someone’s reputation.

Arrogance means thinking and acting as though you are better than others.

## Mark 7:23

Jesus said, “All these evil things come from inside. 📖

They make a person unclean.”

## Mark 7:24

Jesus left that place and went to the area around Tyre. 📖

He entered a house and did not want anyone to know he was there.

But people found out.

## Mark 7:25

A woman soon heard about Jesus. 📖

Her little daughter had an unclean spirit.

The woman came and fell at Jesus's feet.

**Help with words:** Unclean spirit means an evil spirit here.

## Mark 7:26

The woman was Greek and came from Syrian Phoenicia. 📖

She kept asking Jesus to drive the evil spirit out of her daughter.

## Mark 7:27

Jesus told her, “Let the children eat all they need first. 📖

It is not right to take the children's bread and throw it to the dogs.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus compares help to bread.

In this picture, the children stand for Israel's people.

The woman is outside that group, and Jesus speaks of helping the children first.

## Mark 7:28

The woman replied, “Yes, Lord. 📖

Even the dogs under the table eat the children's crumbs.”

## Mark 7:29

Jesus told her, “Because of this answer, you may go. 📖

The evil spirit has left your daughter.”

## Mark 7:30

The woman went home and found her child lying on the bed. 📖

The evil spirit was gone.

## Mark 7:31

Jesus left the area around Tyre. 📖

He went through Sidon to the Sea of Galilee, into the Decapolis area.

**Help with a name:** Decapolis means Ten Towns.

It was an area with a group of towns.

## Mark 7:32

People brought Jesus a man who was deaf and could hardly speak. 📖

They begged Jesus to put his hand on the man.

## Mark 7:33

Jesus took the man aside, away from the crowd. 📖

He put his fingers into the man's ears.

Then Jesus spat and touched the man's tongue.

## Mark 7:34

Jesus looked up to heaven and sighed deeply. 📖

He said to the man, “Ephphatha!”

This means, “Be opened!”

## Mark 7:35

At once, the man could hear. 📖

His tongue was freed, and he began speaking clearly.

## Mark 7:36

Jesus ordered the people not to tell anyone. 📖

But the more he told them this, the more widely they spread the news.

## Mark 7:37

The people were completely amazed. 📖

They said, “He has done everything well!

He even makes deaf people hear and people who cannot speak talk!”
