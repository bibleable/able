# Mark 4 — Seeds, a lamp, and a storm

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells stories called parables.

These stories use familiar things to teach about God's message and kingdom.

Later, Jesus and his followers cross the sea.

## Mark 4:1

Jesus began teaching beside the sea again. ⛵

Such a large crowd gathered that he got into a boat and sat down.

The people stayed on the shore.

## Mark 4:2

Jesus taught them many things through stories with a meaning. 💬

As he taught, he said these words.

## Mark 4:3

Jesus said, “Listen! 🌱

A farmer went out to scatter seed.”

## Mark 4:4

Jesus said, “As the farmer scattered seed, some fell along the path. 🐦

Birds came and ate it.”

## Mark 4:5

Jesus said, “Some seed fell on rocky ground with little soil. 🌱

It grew quickly because the soil was shallow.”

## Mark 4:6

Jesus said, “When the sun came up, the young plants were burned. ☀️

They dried up because they had no roots.”

## Mark 4:7

Jesus said, “Other seed fell among thorn plants. 🌿

The thorns grew and crowded out the young plants.

They produced no crop.”

## Mark 4:8

Jesus said, “Other seed fell on good soil. 🌾

It sprouted, grew, and produced a crop.

Some produced 30 times what was planted.

Some produced 60 times as much.

Some produced 100 times as much.”

## Mark 4:9

Jesus said, “Anyone with ears to hear should listen!” 👂

**Help with these words:** Jesus calls people to pay attention to his message.

## Mark 4:10

Later, Jesus was away from the crowd with the 12 apostles and the others around him. 💬

They asked about the story.

## Mark 4:11

Jesus answered, “You have been given the secret of God's kingdom. 👑

But people outside hear everything through stories.”

**Help with a word:** God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## Mark 4:12

Jesus said, “This is so that they may keep looking, but never really see. 👂

They may keep hearing, but never understand.

Otherwise, they might turn back and be forgiven.”

**Help with this passage:** Jesus is quoting words from Isaiah.

The words describe seeing and hearing without understanding God's message.

## Mark 4:13

Jesus asked, “Do you not understand this story? 💬

Then how will you understand any of the stories?”

## Mark 4:14

Jesus said, “The farmer scatters God's message like seed.” 🌱

## Mark 4:15

Jesus said, “Some people are like seed along the path where the message is scattered. 🌱

As soon as they hear it, Satan comes.

He takes away the message planted in them.”

## Mark 4:16

Jesus said, “Some people are like seed on rocky ground. 🌱

They hear the message and gladly accept it at once.”

## Mark 4:17

Jesus said, “But they have no roots and last only a short time. 🌱

Trouble comes, or people treat them badly because of the message.

Then they quickly give up.”

**Help with this picture:** Having roots pictures holding firmly to the message.

## Mark 4:18

Jesus said, “Other people are like seed among thorns. 🌿

They hear the message.”

## Mark 4:19

Jesus said, “But life's worries, the false promises of wealth, and wanting other things crowd out the message. 🌿

It produces no crop.”

## Mark 4:20

Jesus said, “Other people are like seed in good soil. 🌾

They hear the message, accept it, and produce a crop.

Some produce 30 times what was planted.

Some produce 60 times as much.

Some produce 100 times as much.”

**Help with this picture:** Producing a crop pictures the results of receiving God's message.

## Mark 4:21

Jesus asked, “Do you bring in a lamp to put it under a basket or a bed? 🪔

Do you not put it on a stand?”

## Mark 4:22

Jesus said, “Everything hidden will be shown. 🪔

Every secret will be brought into the light.”

## Mark 4:23

Jesus said, “Anyone with ears to hear should listen!” 👂

## Mark 4:24

Jesus said, “Pay attention to what you hear. 👂

The measure you use will be used for you.

You will be given even more.”

**Help with this picture:** A measure is an amount used when giving or receiving something.

Jesus uses this picture while telling people to listen carefully.

## Mark 4:25

Jesus said, “Anyone who has will receive more. 💬

Anyone who does not have will lose even what they have.”

## Mark 4:26

Jesus said, “God's kingdom is like a man scattering seed on the ground.” 🌱

## Mark 4:27

Jesus said, “Night and day, the man sleeps and gets up. 🌱

The seed sprouts and grows.

He does not know how it happens.”

## Mark 4:28

Jesus said, “The ground produces a crop by itself. 🌾

First the stalk grows.

Then the head forms.

Then the grain inside becomes ripe.”

## Mark 4:29

Jesus said, “As soon as the grain is ripe, the man cuts it with his sickle. 🌾

The time to gather the crop has come.”

**Help with a word:** A sickle is a tool with a curved blade for cutting crops.

## Mark 4:30

Jesus asked, “What can we compare God's kingdom to? 👑

What story can we use to explain it?”

## Mark 4:31

Jesus said, “It is like a mustard seed. 🌱

This is the smallest of all seeds planted in the ground.”

## Mark 4:32

Jesus said, “But after planting, it grows bigger than all the other garden plants. 🌳

It grows large branches.

Birds can nest in its shade.”

## Mark 4:33

Jesus used many stories like these to teach people God's message. 💬

He taught as much as they could understand.

## Mark 4:34

Jesus always used stories when speaking to the crowd. 💬

But when he was alone with his followers, he explained everything.

## Mark 4:35

That evening, Jesus said to his followers, “Let us cross to the other side.” ⛵

## Mark 4:36

They left the crowd behind. ⛵

Jesus was already in the boat, so they took him with them.

Other boats went with him too.

## Mark 4:37

A fierce windstorm began. 🌊

Waves broke over the boat.

The boat was filling with water.

## Mark 4:38

Jesus slept on a cushion at the back of the boat. ⛵

His followers woke him.

They said, “Teacher, do you not care that we are about to die?”

## Mark 4:39

Jesus got up and gave the wind and sea a firm command. ✋

He said, “Be quiet!

Be still!”

The wind stopped.

The sea became completely calm.

## Mark 4:40

Jesus asked his followers, “Why are you so afraid? 💬

Do you still have no faith?”

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in God here.

## Mark 4:41

They were filled with fear. 😮

They asked each other, “Who is this?

Even the wind and sea obey him!”
