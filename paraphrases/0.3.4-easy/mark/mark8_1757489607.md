# Mark 8 — Who is Jesus?

**About this chapter**

Jesus feeds another large crowd and heals a man who is blind.

Peter says that Jesus is the Christ.

Jesus teaches about his coming death and what following him means.

## Mark 8:1

At that time, another very large crowd gathered. 👥

They had nothing to eat.

Jesus called his followers to him and spoke.

## Mark 8:2

Jesus said, “I feel deep care for this crowd. ❤️

They have been with me for three days and have nothing to eat.”

## Mark 8:3

Jesus said, “If I send them home hungry, they will faint on the way. 👣

Some have come from far away.”

## Mark 8:4

His followers answered, “Where could anyone find enough bread for all these people in this empty place?” 🍞

## Mark 8:5

Jesus asked, “How many loaves of bread do you have?” 🍞

They answered, “Seven.”

## Mark 8:6

Jesus told the crowd to sit on the ground. 🍞

He took the seven loaves and thanked God.

He broke the bread and gave it to his followers to hand out.

They gave it to the crowd.

## Mark 8:7

They also had a few small fish. 🐟

Jesus spoke a blessing over them.

He told his followers to hand them out too.

## Mark 8:8

The people ate and had enough. 🧺

Jesus's followers gathered seven baskets full of leftover pieces.

## Mark 8:9

About 4,000 men were there. 👥

Jesus sent the crowd away.

## Mark 8:10

At once, Jesus got into the boat with his followers. ⛵

They went to the area of Dalmanutha.

## Mark 8:11

The Pharisees came and began arguing with Jesus. 💬

To test him, they demanded a sign from heaven.

**Help with words:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

They were asking for a sign to prove that Jesus came from God.

## Mark 8:12

Jesus sighed deeply within himself. 💬

He said, “Why do the people of this time demand a sign?

I tell you the truth.

No sign will be given to them.”

## Mark 8:13

Jesus left them and got into the boat again. ⛵

He crossed to the other side.

## Mark 8:14

His followers had forgotten to bring bread. 🍞

They had only one loaf with them in the boat.

## Mark 8:15

Jesus warned them, “Watch out! 🍞

Be careful of the yeast of the Pharisees and of Herod.”

**Help with this picture:** Yeast is added to dough to make bread rise.

A little yeast spreads through the dough.

Jesus uses this picture as a warning about influence that spreads.

## Mark 8:16

His followers began talking with each other about having no bread. 💬

## Mark 8:17

Jesus knew what they were discussing. 💬

He asked, “Why are you talking about having no bread?

Do you still not see or understand?

Are your hearts so closed?”

**Help with this picture:** Closed hearts pictures being unwilling to understand.

## Mark 8:18

Jesus said, “You have eyes, but do you not see? 👀

You have ears, but do you not hear?

Do you not remember?”

## Mark 8:19

Jesus asked, “When I broke five loaves for 5,000 people, how many baskets of leftovers did you collect?” 🧺

They answered, “Twelve.”

## Mark 8:20

Jesus asked, “When I broke seven loaves for 4,000 people, how many baskets of leftovers did you collect?” 🧺

They answered, “Seven.”

## Mark 8:21

Jesus asked, “Do you still not understand?” 💬

## Mark 8:22

They arrived at Bethsaida. 🤲

Some people brought a blind man to Jesus and begged Jesus to touch him.

## Mark 8:23

Jesus took the man by the hand and led him outside the village. 🤝

Jesus spat on the man's eyes and placed his hands on him.

He asked, “Can you see anything?”

## Mark 8:24

The man looked up. 👀

He said, “I can see people.

But they look like trees walking around.”

## Mark 8:25

Jesus put his hands on the man's eyes again. 👀

The man opened his eyes, and his sight returned.

He could see everything clearly.

## Mark 8:26

Jesus sent him home. 🏠

He said, “Do not go back into the village.”

## Mark 8:27

Jesus and his followers went to the villages around Caesarea Philippi. 👣

On the way, Jesus asked, “Who do people say I am?”

## Mark 8:28

They answered, “Some say John the Baptist. 💬

Others say Elijah.

Still others say you are one of the prophets.”

**Help with a word:** A prophet speaks God's message.

## Mark 8:29

Jesus asked, “But who do you say I am?” 💬

Peter answered, “You are the Christ.”

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Mark 8:30

Jesus warned them not to tell anyone about him. 🤫

## Mark 8:31

Jesus began teaching them that the Son of Man must suffer many things. 💬

The elders, chief priests, and teachers of God's law would reject him.

He must be killed.

After three days, he would rise again.

**Help with words:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

Elders were leaders of the people.

Chief priests were leading priests.

## Mark 8:32

Jesus spoke about this openly and clearly. 💬

Peter took him aside and began telling him he was wrong to say it.

## Mark 8:33

Jesus turned and looked at his followers. ✋

He firmly corrected Peter.

He said, “Get behind me, Satan!

You are thinking about human concerns, rather than God's concerns.”

**Help with these words:** Jesus addresses Peter sharply because Peter is opposing what Jesus has said must happen.

## Mark 8:34

Jesus called the crowd and his followers to him. 👣

He said, “Anyone who wants to follow me must put aside their own wishes.

They must take up their cross and follow me.”

**Help with this picture:** A cross was used to put people to death.

Taking up a cross pictures accepting the cost of following Jesus, even suffering and death.

## Mark 8:35

Jesus said, “Anyone who wants to save their life will lose it. 💬

But anyone who loses their life for me and the good news will save it.”

## Mark 8:36

Jesus asked, “What good is gaining the whole world if you lose your soul?” 💬

**Help with a word:** Soul here refers to a person's life and deepest self.

## Mark 8:37

Jesus asked, “What could a person give in exchange for their soul?” 💬

## Mark 8:38

Jesus said, “The people of this time are unfaithful to God and sinful. 💬

If anyone is ashamed of me and my words, the Son of Man will be ashamed of them.

This will happen when he comes in his Father's glory with the holy angels.”

**Help with a word:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.
