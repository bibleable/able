# Mark 13 — Keep watch

**About this chapter**

Jesus speaks about the temple's destruction, suffering, and the coming of the Son of Man.

He warns his followers not to be fooled.

He tells them to stay ready.

## Mark 13:1

Jesus was leaving the temple. 📖

One follower said, “Teacher, look at those wonderful stones and buildings!”

## Mark 13:2

Jesus answered, “Do you see these great buildings? 📖

Not one stone here will stay on top of another.

Every stone will be thrown down.”

## Mark 13:3

Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives, across from the temple. 📖

Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him privately.

## Mark 13:4

They asked, “Tell us when these things will happen. 📖

What sign will show that they are about to take place?”

## Mark 13:5

Jesus began, “Make sure no one tricks you.” 📖

## Mark 13:6

Jesus said, “Many will come using my name. 📖

They will say, ‘I am he.’

They will lead many people astray.”

## Mark 13:7

Jesus said, “You will hear about wars and reports of wars. 📖

Do not be alarmed.

These things must happen, but the end is still to come.”

## Mark 13:8

Jesus said, “Nations and kingdoms will fight each other. 📖

There will be earthquakes in different places and times when people have no food.

These are the beginning of birth pains.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus compares these troubles with the pains that begin when a baby is being born.

## Mark 13:9

Jesus said, “Be on your guard. 📖

People will hand you over to councils and beat you in synagogues.

Because of me, you will stand before governors and kings.

You will tell them about me.”

**Help with words:** Councils were groups of leaders who judged cases.

Synagogues are Jewish places for worship and teaching.

Governors rule areas of a country or empire.

## Mark 13:10

Jesus said, “The good news must first be told to all nations.” 📖

## Mark 13:11

Jesus said, “When people arrest you and hand you over, do not worry beforehand about what to say. 📖

Say what you are given at that time.

The Holy Spirit will speak through you.”

## Mark 13:12

Jesus said, “A brother will hand over his brother to be killed. 📖

A father will do the same to his child.

Children will turn against their parents and have them killed.”

## Mark 13:13

Jesus said, “Everyone will hate you because of my name. 📖

But the person who keeps going to the end will be saved.”

## Mark 13:14

Jesus said, “You will see the hateful thing that makes a place desolate standing where it should not be. 📖

Then people in Judea must run to the mountains.”

The reader should understand this.

**Help with words:** Desolate means ruined or left empty.

The source calls this hateful thing the abomination of desolation.

It describes something deeply offensive to God.

## Mark 13:15

Jesus said, “Anyone on the roof must not go back inside to collect anything from the house.” 📖

## Mark 13:16

Jesus said, “Anyone in the field must not go back for their outer coat.” 📖

## Mark 13:17

Jesus said, “Those days will be very hard for pregnant women and mothers feeding babies with their milk!” 📖

## Mark 13:18

Jesus said, “Pray that this will not happen in winter.” 📖

## Mark 13:19

Jesus said, “Those days will bring suffering greater than any since God first made the world. 📖

Nothing like it will ever happen again.”

## Mark 13:20

Jesus said, “If the Lord had not shortened those days, no one would survive. 📖

But he has shortened them for the sake of the people he has chosen.”

## Mark 13:21

Jesus said, “Then someone may tell you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ 📖

Or they may say, ‘There he is!’

Do not believe it.”

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Mark 13:22

Jesus said, “People pretending to be the Christ and false prophets will appear. 📖

They will do signs and wonders to mislead even God's chosen people, if that were possible.”

**Help with words:** False prophets claim to speak for God when they do not.

## Mark 13:23

Jesus said, “So be on your guard. 📖

I have told you everything before it happens.”

## Mark 13:24

Jesus said, “In those days, after that suffering, ‘The sun will become dark. 📖

The moon will not give its light.’”

## Mark 13:25

Jesus continued, “‘Stars will fall from the sky. 📖

The powers in the heavens will be shaken.’”

## Mark 13:26

Jesus said, “Then people will see the Son of Man coming in the clouds with great power and glory.” 📖

**Help with words:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## Mark 13:27

Jesus said, “He will send the angels to gather his chosen people from every direction. 📖

They will gather them from the ends of the earth to the ends of heaven.”

## Mark 13:28

Jesus said, “Learn this lesson from the fig tree. 📖

When its branches become soft and leaves appear, you know summer is near.”

## Mark 13:29

Jesus said, “In the same way, when you see these things happening, know that he is near. 📖

He is right at the door.”

## Mark 13:30

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 📖

This generation will not pass away before all these things happen.”

**Help with a word:** A generation is a group of people living at the same time.

## Mark 13:31

Jesus said, “Heaven and earth will pass away. 📖

But my words will never pass away.”

## Mark 13:32

Jesus said, “No one knows that day or hour. 📖

The angels in heaven do not know, and neither does the Son.

Only the Father knows.”

## Mark 13:33

Jesus said, “Be on your guard and stay alert! 📖

You do not know when the time will come.”

## Mark 13:34

Jesus said, “It is like a man going on a journey. 📖

He leaves his house and gives each servant a task.

He tells the person at the door to keep watch.”

## Mark 13:35

Jesus said, “So keep watch. 📖

You do not know when the house's owner will return.

He may come in the evening, at midnight, when the rooster crows, or in the morning.”

## Mark 13:36

Jesus said, “Otherwise, he might arrive suddenly and find you sleeping.” 📖

## Mark 13:37

Jesus said, “What I say to you, I say to everyone. 📖

Keep watch!”
