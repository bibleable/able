# Mark 9 — Listen to Jesus

**About this chapter**

Three followers see Jesus changed in appearance on a mountain.

Jesus helps a boy, teaches about serving others, and gives serious warnings about sin.

## Mark 9:1

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 👑

Some people standing here will see God's kingdom come with power before they die.”

**Help with a word:** God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## Mark 9:2

Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John up a high mountain by themselves. ⛰️

His appearance changed in front of them.

## Mark 9:3

Jesus's clothes shone bright white. ✨

No one on earth could wash clothes that white.

## Mark 9:4

Elijah and Moses appeared before them. 💬

Elijah and Moses were talking with Jesus.

## Mark 9:5

Peter said to Jesus, “Rabbi, it is good for us to be here. ⛺

Let us make three shelters.

One will be for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”

**Help with a word:** Rabbi means teacher.

## Mark 9:6

They were all so frightened that Peter did not know what else to say. 😨

## Mark 9:7

A cloud appeared and covered them. ☁️

A voice from the cloud said, “This is my Son, whom I love.

Listen to him!”

## Mark 9:8

Suddenly, they looked around. 👀

They saw no one with them except Jesus.

## Mark 9:9

As they came down the mountain, Jesus warned them not to tell anyone what they had seen. 🤫

They must wait until the Son of Man had risen from the dead.

**Help with a title:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

## Mark 9:10

They kept this to themselves. 💬

They discussed what rising from the dead meant.

## Mark 9:11

They asked Jesus, “Why do the teachers of God's law say Elijah must come first?” 💬

## Mark 9:12

Jesus answered, “Elijah does come first and put everything right. 📜

Then why do the Scriptures say that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected?”

**Help with a word:** The Scriptures are the holy writings.

## Mark 9:13

Jesus said, “But I tell you that Elijah has already come. 📜

They did whatever they wanted to him, just as the Scriptures say about him.”

## Mark 9:14

They returned to the other followers. 👥

A large crowd surrounded them.

Teachers of God's law were arguing with them.

## Mark 9:15

As soon as the people saw Jesus, they were filled with wonder. 👣

They ran to greet him.

## Mark 9:16

Jesus asked, “What are you arguing with them about?” 💬

## Mark 9:17

A man in the crowd answered, “Teacher, I brought you my son. 💬

He has a spirit that stops him from speaking.”

## Mark 9:18

The father said, “Whenever the spirit takes hold of him, it throws him down. 💬

Foam comes from his mouth.

He grinds his teeth, and his body becomes stiff.

I asked your followers to drive the spirit out.

But they could not.”

## Mark 9:19

Jesus answered, “You people do not believe! 💬

How long must I stay with you?

How long must I put up with you?

Bring the boy to me.”

## Mark 9:20

They brought the boy to Jesus. 🤲

When the spirit saw Jesus, it made the boy's body shake at once.

The boy fell and rolled on the ground.

Foam came from his mouth.

## Mark 9:21

Jesus asked the father, “How long has this been happening to him?” 💬

The father answered, “Since he was a child.”

## Mark 9:22

The father said, “The spirit often throws him into fire or water to kill him. 🙏

If you can do anything, please have mercy on us and help us.”

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## Mark 9:23

Jesus replied, “‘If you can’? 💬

Everything is possible for someone who believes!”

## Mark 9:24

At once, the boy's father cried out, “I do believe! 🙏

Help me where I do not believe!”

## Mark 9:25

Jesus saw a crowd running towards them. ✋

He gave the unclean spirit a firm command.

He said, “You spirit that stops him from hearing and speaking, I command you to come out!

Never enter him again.”

**Help with words:** An unclean spirit means an evil spirit here.

This account does not say that everyone who cannot hear or speak has an evil spirit.

## Mark 9:26

The spirit screamed and made the boy shake violently. 🤲

Then it came out.

The boy looked like a dead body.

Many people said, “He is dead.”

## Mark 9:27

But Jesus held the boy's hand and helped him get up. 🤝

The boy stood up.

## Mark 9:28

Jesus went into the house. 🏠

His followers asked him privately, “Why could we not drive the spirit out?”

## Mark 9:29

Jesus answered, “This kind can come out only through prayer.” 🙏

## Mark 9:30

They left that place and travelled through Galilee. 👣

Jesus did not want anyone to know they were there.

## Mark 9:31

Jesus was teaching his followers. 💬

He told them, “The Son of Man will be handed over to men.

They will kill him.

After three days, he will rise again.”

## Mark 9:32

His followers did not understand what he meant. 💬

They were afraid to ask him about it.

## Mark 9:33

They came to Capernaum. 🏠

In the house, Jesus asked, “What were you discussing on the way?”

## Mark 9:34

They said nothing. 💬

On the way, they had argued about which of them was the greatest.

## Mark 9:35

Jesus sat down and called the 12 apostles. 💬

He said, “Anyone who wants to be first must be last of all and serve everyone.”

## Mark 9:36

Jesus had a little child stand among them. ❤️

He took the child in his arms and spoke to them.

## Mark 9:37

Jesus said, “Anyone who welcomes a child like this in my name welcomes me. ❤️

Anyone who welcomes me also welcomes the One who sent me.”

## Mark 9:38

John said, “Teacher, we saw someone driving out evil spirits in your name. 💬

We tried to stop him because he does not follow along with us.”

## Mark 9:39

Jesus replied, “Do not stop him. ✋

No one who does a miracle in my name can quickly turn around and speak evil about me.”

## Mark 9:40

Jesus said, “Anyone who is not against us is for us.” 💬

## Mark 9:41

Jesus said, “Someone may give you even a cup of water because you belong to Christ. 🥤

I tell you the truth.

That person will certainly receive their reward.”

**Help with a title:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Mark 9:42

Jesus said, “Someone might cause one of these little ones who believe in me to fall into sin. ✋

It would be better for that person to have a huge millstone tied around their neck.

It would be better for them to be thrown into the sea.”

**Help with this warning:** A millstone is a very heavy stone used for grinding grain.

Jesus uses a shocking picture to warn against leading believers into sin.

## Mark 9:43

Jesus said, “If your hand causes you to sin, cut it off. ✋

It is better to enter life with a missing hand.

It is worse to have two hands and go into hell, where the fire never goes out.”

**Help with this warning:** Jesus uses strong, shocking pictures to show how serious sin is.

The point is to turn away from sin, not to injure your body.

## Mark 9:44

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Mark 9:45

Jesus said, “If your foot causes you to sin, cut it off. 👣

It is better to enter life unable to walk properly.

It is worse to have two feet and be thrown into hell.”

**Help with this picture:** This continues Jesus's strong warning about turning away from sin.

## Mark 9:46

**About this verse:** The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

No Bible paraphrase has been added at this address.

## Mark 9:47

Jesus said, “If your eye causes you to sin, pull it out. 👁️

It is better to enter God's kingdom with one eye.

It is worse to have two eyes and be thrown into hell.”

**Help with this picture:** This continues Jesus's strong warning about turning away from sin.

## Mark 9:48

Jesus said, “There, ‘their worm never dies, and the fire never goes out.’” 🔥

**Help with this quotation:** Jesus quotes Isaiah's picture of judgment.

## Mark 9:49

Jesus said, “Everyone will be salted with fire.” 🔥

**Help with this picture:** Jesus uses salt and fire as pictures in this teaching.

Salt flavours food and helps keep it from spoiling.

## Mark 9:50

Jesus said, “Salt is good. 🧂

But if salt loses its saltiness, how can you make it salty again?

Have salt among yourselves.

Live at peace with each other.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus connects his picture of salt with how his followers live together.
