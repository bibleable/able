# Mark 16 — Jesus has risen

**About this chapter**

Women visit Jesus's tomb and hear that he has risen.

Jesus appears to his followers and sends them to tell the good news.

## Mark 16:1

After the Sabbath, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices. 📖

They wanted to put them on Jesus's body.

**Help with words:** The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

Spices were used to care for a body after death.

## Mark 16:2

Very early on the first day of the week, they went to the tomb. 📖

The sun had just risen.

**Help with a word:** A tomb is a place where a dead person is buried.

## Mark 16:3

They asked each other, “Who will roll the stone away from the tomb's entrance for us?” 📖

## Mark 16:4

They looked up. 📖

They saw that the stone had been rolled away, although it was very large.

## Mark 16:5

They entered the tomb. 📖

A young man in a white robe sat on the right side.

They were frightened.

## Mark 16:6

The young man said, “Do not be frightened. 📖

You are looking for Jesus from Nazareth, who was crucified.

He has risen!

He is not here!

Look at the place where they laid him.”

**Help with a word:** Crucified means killed by being fastened to a cross.

## Mark 16:7

The young man said, “Go and tell his followers and Peter. 📖

‘Jesus is going ahead of you to Galilee.

You will see him there, just as he told you.’”

## Mark 16:8

The women left the tomb and ran away. 📖

They were shaking and confused.

They said nothing to anyone because they were afraid.

## Mark 16:9

Early on the first day of the week, after Jesus had risen, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene. 📖

He had driven seven evil spirits out of her.

## Mark 16:10

Mary went and told the people who had been with Jesus. 📖

They were grieving and crying.

## Mark 16:11

They heard that Jesus was alive and that Mary had seen him. 📖

But they did not believe it.

## Mark 16:12

After this, Jesus appeared in a different form to two of them. 📖

They were walking in the countryside.

## Mark 16:13

The two went back and told the others. 📖

But the others did not believe them either.

## Mark 16:14

Later, Jesus appeared to the 11 apostles while they ate. 📖

He firmly corrected their lack of belief and their stubborn hearts.

They had refused to believe the people who saw him after he rose.

**Help with these words:** Stubborn hearts describes being unwilling to believe.

## Mark 16:15

Jesus told them, “Go into all the world. 📖

Tell the good news to all creation.”

## Mark 16:16

Jesus said, “Anyone who believes and is baptized will be saved. 📖

Anyone who does not believe will be condemned.”

**Help with words:** Baptism uses water as a sign of belonging to Jesus.

Condemned means judged guilty.

## Mark 16:17

Jesus said, “These signs will go with those who believe. 📖

They will drive out evil spirits in my name.

They will speak in new languages.”

## Mark 16:18

Jesus said, “They will pick up snakes with their hands. 📖

If they drink deadly poison, it will not harm them.

They will put their hands on sick people, and those people will become well.”

**Help with this passage:** Jesus describes signs that will accompany his followers.

These words do not tell readers to test God by handling snakes or drinking poison.

## Mark 16:19

After the Lord Jesus spoke to them, he was taken up into heaven. 📖

He sat at God's right hand.

**Help with this picture:** The right hand is a place of great honour and authority.

## Mark 16:20

His followers went out and told the good news everywhere. 📖

The Lord worked through them.

The signs that went with their message confirmed that his word was true.
