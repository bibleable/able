# Mark 2 — Jesus forgives and calls people

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals a man who cannot walk and calls Levi to follow him.

People question Jesus about meals, fasting, and the Sabbath.

## Mark 2:1

A few days later, Jesus returned to Capernaum. 🏠

People heard that he was at home.

## Mark 2:2

So many people gathered that there was no room left, even outside the door. 🚪

Jesus spoke God's message to them.

## Mark 2:3

Four men brought a man who was paralysed. 🤝

They carried him to Jesus.

**Help with a word:** Being paralysed means being unable to move part or all of the body.

## Mark 2:4

The crowd kept them from reaching Jesus. 🏠

So they opened the roof above him.

They lowered the man on his mat through the opening.

## Mark 2:5

Jesus saw their trust in him. ❤️

He said to the man, “Son, your sins are forgiven.”

**Help with a word:** Sins are wrong things people do against God.

## Mark 2:6

Some teachers of God's law were sitting there. 💭

They were thinking to themselves.

## Mark 2:7

They thought, “Why does this man speak like that? 💭

He is insulting God!

Only God can forgive sins.”

## Mark 2:8

Jesus knew at once in his spirit what they were thinking. 💬

He asked, “Why are you thinking these things?”

## Mark 2:9

Jesus asked, “Which is easier to say to this man? 💬

‘Your sins are forgiven’?

Or ‘Get up, take your mat, and walk’?”

## Mark 2:10

Jesus said, “I want you to know this. 💬

The Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins.”

Then Jesus spoke to the man who was paralysed.

**Help with words:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

Authority means the right to speak or act with power.

## Mark 2:11

Jesus said, “I tell you, get up. 👣

Take your mat and go home.”

## Mark 2:12

At once, the man got up and took his mat. 😮

He walked out in front of everyone.

They were all amazed and praised God.

They said, “We have never seen anything like this!”

## Mark 2:13

Jesus went out beside the sea again. 🌊

All the people came to him.

He taught them there.

## Mark 2:14

As Jesus walked along, he saw Levi, the son of Alphaeus. 👣

Levi sat at the place where he collected taxes.

Jesus said, “Follow me.”

Levi got up and followed him.

## Mark 2:15

Jesus was eating at Levi's house. 🍞

Many tax collectors and sinners ate with Jesus and his followers.

Many of these people followed Jesus.

**Help with words:** Tax collectors collected money that people had to pay to the government.

Sinners means people known for breaking God's law here.

## Mark 2:16

Some teachers of God's law who were Pharisees saw Jesus eating with these people. 💬

They asked his followers, “Why does he eat with tax collectors and sinners?”

**Help with a word:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## Mark 2:17

Jesus heard them. 💬

He said, “Healthy people do not need a doctor.

Sick people do.

I came to call sinners, rather than people who are right with God.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus uses the need for a doctor to explain his work among sinners.

## Mark 2:18

John's followers and the Pharisees often fasted. 💬

People asked Jesus, “Why do John's followers and the Pharisees' followers fast, but your followers do not?”

**Help with a word:** Fasting means choosing to go without food for a time, often for prayer.

## Mark 2:19

Jesus answered, “Can wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them? 💒

They cannot fast while he is with them.”

**Help with a word:** The bridegroom is the man getting married.

## Mark 2:20

Jesus said, “But a time will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them. 💒

Then they will fast.”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus compares his presence with his followers to a bridegroom being with wedding guests.

## Mark 2:21

Jesus said, “No one sews new cloth that can still shrink onto old clothes. 🧵

If they do, the new patch pulls away from the old cloth.

The tear becomes worse.”

## Mark 2:22

Jesus said, “No one pours new wine into old leather wine bags. 🍇

If they do, the wine bursts the bags.

Both the wine and the bags are ruined.

New wine goes into new bags.”

**Help with this picture:** New wine gives off gas as it develops.

New leather bags can stretch, but old ones may split.

## Mark 2:23

One Sabbath, Jesus walked through fields of grain. 🌾

His followers began picking heads of grain as they walked.

**Help with words:** The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

Heads of grain are the parts of a plant that hold its seeds.

## Mark 2:24

The Pharisees said to Jesus, “Look! 💬

Why are your followers doing something the law does not allow on the Sabbath?”

## Mark 2:25

Jesus answered, “Have you never read what David did? 📖

He and his companions were hungry and needed food.”

## Mark 2:26

Jesus said, “When Abiathar was high priest, David entered God's house. 🍞

David ate the bread set apart for God.

Only priests were allowed to eat it.

David also gave some to his companions.”

## Mark 2:27

Jesus said, “The Sabbath was made for people. 📅

People were not made for the Sabbath.”

## Mark 2:28

Jesus said, “So the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath.” 📅
