# Mark 10 — Following Jesus and serving others

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches about marriage, children, money, and serving others.

He speaks again about his coming death.

He also gives Bartimaeus his sight.

## Mark 10:1

Jesus left that place and went to the area of Judea, beyond the Jordan River. 📖

Crowds came to him again.

He taught them, as he usually did.

## Mark 10:2

Some Pharisees came to test Jesus. 📖

They asked, “Does the law allow a man to divorce his wife?”

**Help with words:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

Divorce means legally ending a marriage.

## Mark 10:3

Jesus answered, “What did Moses command you?” 📖

## Mark 10:4

They answered, “Moses allowed a man to write his wife a divorce paper and send her away.” 📖

## Mark 10:5

Jesus told them, “Moses wrote this command for you because your hearts were so hard.” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Hard hearts pictures being stubborn towards God.

## Mark 10:6

Jesus said, “But from the beginning of creation, ‘God made them male and female.’” 📖

## Mark 10:7

Jesus continued, “‘For this reason, a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife.’” 📖

## Mark 10:8

Jesus continued, “‘The two will become one flesh.’ 📖

So they are no longer two, but one flesh.”

**Help with this picture:** One flesh describes the close union of husband and wife in marriage.

## Mark 10:9

Jesus said, “So people must not separate what God has joined together.” 📖

## Mark 10:10

Back in the house, Jesus's followers asked him about this. 📖

## Mark 10:11

Jesus said, “A man who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery against his wife.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

## Mark 10:12

Jesus said, “If a woman divorces her husband and marries another man, she commits adultery.” 📖

## Mark 10:13

People brought little children to Jesus so he could put his hands on them. 📖

His followers spoke sharply to the people bringing the children.

## Mark 10:14

Jesus saw this and was angry. 📖

He said, “Let the little children come to me.

Do not stop them!

God's kingdom belongs to people like these.”

**Help with a word:** God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## Mark 10:15

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 📖

Anyone who does not receive God's kingdom like a little child will never enter it.”

## Mark 10:16

Jesus took the children in his arms. 📖

He put his hands on them and blessed them.

## Mark 10:17

Jesus started on his way. 📖

A man ran to him and knelt before him.

He asked, “Good Teacher, what must I do to receive life that lasts for ever?”

## Mark 10:18

Jesus answered, “Why do you call me good? 📖

Only God is good.”

## Mark 10:19

Jesus said, “You know the commands. 📖

Do not murder.

Do not commit adultery.

Do not steal.

Do not give false evidence against someone.

Do not cheat others.

Honour your father and mother.”

## Mark 10:20

The man replied, “Teacher, I have kept all these commands since I was young.” 📖

## Mark 10:21

Jesus looked at the man and loved him. 📖

He said, “There is one thing you lack.

Go and sell everything you own.

Give the money to poor people.

You will have treasure in heaven.

Then come and follow me.”

## Mark 10:22

The man was sad when he heard this. 📖

He went away full of sorrow, because he owned great wealth.

## Mark 10:23

Jesus looked around and spoke to his followers. 📖

He said, “It is very hard for rich people to enter God's kingdom!”

## Mark 10:24

His followers were amazed at his words. 📖

Jesus said again, “Children, it is very hard to enter God's kingdom!”

## Mark 10:25

Jesus said, “It is easier for a camel to go through a needle's eye. 📖

It is harder for a rich person to enter God's kingdom.”

**Help with this picture:** A needle's eye is the tiny hole where thread goes through.

Jesus uses an impossible picture, as his next words explain.

## Mark 10:26

His followers were even more amazed. 📖

They asked each other, “Then who can be saved?”

## Mark 10:27

Jesus looked at them. 📖

He said, “This is impossible for people, but not for God.

Everything is possible for God.”

## Mark 10:28

Peter began to say, “Look, we have left everything and followed you.” 📖

## Mark 10:29

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. 📖

Anyone who has left home, brothers, sisters, mother, father, children, or fields for Me and the good news

## Mark 10:30

will receive a hundred times as much in this present time. 📖

They will receive homes, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and fields, along with persecution.

In the age to come, they will receive eternal life.

**Help with these words:** Persecution means being treated cruelly because of belief.

Eternal life means life that lasts forever.

The promise is tied to leaving these things for Jesus and the good news.

It also includes persecution, rather than a promise of a life without trouble.

## Mark 10:31

Jesus said, “But many who are first will be last. 📖

Those who are last will be first.”

## Mark 10:32

They walked up the road to Jerusalem. 📖

Jesus walked ahead of them.

His followers were amazed, and the people following were afraid.

Jesus took the 12 aside again.

He began telling them what would happen to him.

## Mark 10:33

Jesus said, “We are going up to Jerusalem. 📖

The Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and teachers of God's law.

They will sentence him to death.

They will hand him over to people who are not Jews.”

**Help with words:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

Chief priests were leading priests.

## Mark 10:34

Jesus said, “They will make fun of him, spit on him, whip him, and kill him. 📖

After three days, he will rise again.”

## Mark 10:35

James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came to Jesus. 📖

They said, “Teacher, we want you to do whatever we ask.”

## Mark 10:36

Jesus asked, “What do you want me to do for you?” 📖

## Mark 10:37

They answered, “When you are in your glory, let one of us sit at your right. 📖

Let the other sit at your left.”

**Help with this request:** They are asking for places of great honour beside Jesus.

Glory means greatness and honour made known.

## Mark 10:38

Jesus answered, “You do not know what you are asking. 📖

Can you drink the cup I will drink?

Can you go through the baptism I will go through?”

**Help with these pictures:** Jesus uses a cup and baptism as pictures of the suffering he will face.

## Mark 10:39

The brothers answered, “We can.” 📖

Jesus said, “You will drink the cup I drink.

You will go through the baptism I go through.”

## Mark 10:40

Jesus said, “But it is not mine to give the places at my right or left. 📖

Those places belong to the people they have been prepared for.”

## Mark 10:41

The other ten apostles heard about this. 📖

They became angry with James and John.

## Mark 10:42

Jesus called them together. 📖

He said, “You know that those regarded as rulers of the Gentiles order their people around.

Their important officials use power over them.

**Help with “Gentiles”:** This means people who are not Jews.

## Mark 10:43

Jesus said, “You must not act like that. 📖

Anyone who wants to become great among you must serve you.”

## Mark 10:44

Jesus said, “Anyone who wants to be first must be everyone's slave.” 📖

**Help with a word:** A slave is a person controlled and owned by someone else.

Jesus uses this strong picture to teach his followers to serve everyone.

## Mark 10:45

Jesus said, “Even the Son of Man came to serve, rather than to be served. 📖

He came to give his life as a ransom for many.”

**Help with a word:** A ransom is a price paid to set someone free.

## Mark 10:46

They came to Jericho. 📖

As Jesus left with his followers and a large crowd, Bartimaeus sat beside the road.

He was the son of Timaeus.

He was blind and begged for money.

## Mark 10:47

Bartimaeus heard that Jesus from Nazareth was there. 📖

He began shouting, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”

**Help with words:** Son of David is a royal title linked to the King God promised to send.

Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## Mark 10:48

Many people told Bartimaeus firmly to be quiet. 📖

But he shouted even louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!”

## Mark 10:49

Jesus stopped and said, “Call him.” 📖

They called the blind man.

They said, “Be brave!

Get up!

He is calling you.”

## Mark 10:50

Bartimaeus threw off his outer coat. 📖

He jumped up and came to Jesus.

## Mark 10:51

Jesus asked, “What do you want me to do for you?” 📖

The blind man answered, “Rabboni, let me see again.”

**Help with a word:** Rabboni means my teacher.

## Mark 10:52

Jesus said, “Go.

Your faith has healed you.” 📖

At once, Bartimaeus could see.

He followed Jesus along the road.

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in Jesus here.
