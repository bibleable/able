# Mark 5 — Jesus frees and heals people

**About this chapter**

Jesus frees a man from evil spirits.

He heals a woman and brings Jairus's daughter back to life.

The chapter includes self-harm, the death of animals, and a child's death.

## Mark 5:1

Jesus and his followers crossed the sea. ⛵

They arrived in the area where the Gerasenes lived.

## Mark 5:2

As soon as Jesus left the boat, a man with an unclean spirit met him. 👣

The man came from the tombs.

**Help with words:** An unclean spirit means an evil spirit here.

Tombs are places where dead people are buried.

## Mark 5:3

The man had been living among the tombs. ⛓️

No one could hold him back any longer, even with chains.

## Mark 5:4

People had often tied him with chains and metal bands. ⛓️

But he broke the chains and smashed the bands.

No one was strong enough to hold him back.

## Mark 5:5

Night and day, the man cried out among the tombs and in the mountains. ⛰️

He cut himself with stones.

## Mark 5:6

The man saw Jesus from far away. 👣

He ran to Jesus and fell on his knees before him.

## Mark 5:7

The man shouted, “Jesus, Son of the Most High God, what do you want with me? 📣

I beg you before God, do not cause me terrible pain!”

**Help with a title:** Most High describes God as greater than all others.

## Mark 5:8

The man said this because Jesus had already given a command. ✋

Jesus had said, “Come out of this man, you unclean spirit!”

## Mark 5:9

Jesus asked, “What is your name?” 💬

The man answered, “My name is Legion, because there are many of us.”

**Help with a name:** Legion was a name for a very large group of Roman soldiers.

Here the name points to many evil spirits.

## Mark 5:10

He begged Jesus again and again not to send the spirits out of that area. 🙏

## Mark 5:11

A large group of pigs was eating on the hillside nearby. 🐖

## Mark 5:12

The evil spirits begged Jesus, “Send us into the pigs. 🐖

Let us enter them.”

## Mark 5:13

Jesus allowed them to go. 🐖

The unclean spirits came out of the man and entered the pigs.

About 2,000 pigs rushed down the steep bank into the sea.

They drowned in the water.

## Mark 5:14

The people caring for the pigs ran away. 📣

They told what had happened in the town and countryside.

People came out to see what had happened.

## Mark 5:15

They came to Jesus and saw the man who had held the many evil spirits. 👀

He sat there, wearing clothes and thinking clearly.

The people were afraid.

## Mark 5:16

The people who had seen it explained what had happened to the man. 💬

They also told what had happened to the pigs.

## Mark 5:17

The people began begging Jesus to leave their area. 👣

## Mark 5:18

Jesus was getting into the boat. ⛵

The man who had held the evil spirits begged to go with him.

## Mark 5:19

Jesus did not let him go. 🏠

He said, “Go home to your own people.

Tell them how much the Lord has done for you.

Tell them about the mercy he has shown you.”

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness shown to someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## Mark 5:20

The man went away. 📣

He told people throughout the Decapolis how much Jesus had done for him.

Everyone was amazed.

**Help with a name:** Decapolis means Ten Towns.

It was the name of an area with a group of towns.

## Mark 5:21

Jesus crossed the sea again by boat. ⛵

A large crowd gathered around him beside the sea.

## Mark 5:22

A synagogue leader named Jairus arrived. 🙏

When Jairus saw Jesus, he fell at his feet.

**Help with a word:** A synagogue is a Jewish place for worship and teaching.

## Mark 5:23

Jairus begged Jesus, “My little daughter is nearly dead. 🙏

Please come and put your hands on her.

Then she will be healed and live.”

## Mark 5:24

Jesus went with Jairus. 👣

A large crowd followed Jesus and pressed around him.

## Mark 5:25

A woman was there who had suffered from bleeding for 12 years. 🤲

## Mark 5:26

She had suffered greatly while many doctors treated her. 🤲

She had spent all her money.

But she had not become better.

Instead, she had become worse.

## Mark 5:27

The woman heard about Jesus. 🤲

She came through the crowd behind him and touched his outer coat.

## Mark 5:28

She kept saying, “If I can just touch his clothes, I will be healed.” 💬

## Mark 5:29

At once, her bleeding stopped. ✨

She felt in her body that she was healed of her illness.

## Mark 5:30

Jesus knew at once that power had gone out from him. 💬

He turned towards the crowd.

He asked, “Who touched my clothes?”

## Mark 5:31

His followers answered, “You can see the crowd pressing around you. 💬

Yet you ask, ‘Who touched me?’”

## Mark 5:32

But Jesus kept looking around to see who had done it. 👀

## Mark 5:33

The woman knew what had happened to her. 🙏

She came and fell down before Jesus, shaking with fear.

She told him the whole truth.

## Mark 5:34

Jesus said, “Daughter, your faith has healed you. ❤️

Go in peace.

Be free from your illness.”

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in Jesus here.

## Mark 5:35

While Jesus was still speaking, messengers came from Jairus's house. 💬

They said, “Your daughter is dead.

Why trouble the Teacher any longer?”

## Mark 5:36

Jesus heard what they said. 💬

He told Jairus, “Do not be afraid.

Just believe.”

## Mark 5:37

Jesus allowed only Peter, James, and James's brother John to go with him. 👣

## Mark 5:38

They reached the synagogue leader's house. 🏠

Jesus saw people making a great noise.

They were crying and weeping loudly.

## Mark 5:39

Jesus went inside. 💬

He asked, “Why are you making this noise and crying?

The child is not dead, but asleep.”

**Help with these words:** Jesus speaks of her death as sleep before bringing her back to life.

## Mark 5:40

The people laughed at Jesus. 🏠

He made them all go outside.

He took the child's father and mother, and his three companions, into the room where the child was.

## Mark 5:41

Jesus took her hand. 🤝

He said, “Talitha koum!”

This means, “Little girl, I tell you, get up!”

## Mark 5:42

At once, the girl got up and walked around. ✨

She was 12 years old.

They were completely amazed.

## Mark 5:43

Jesus gave firm orders that no one should know about this. 🤫

He told them to give the girl something to eat.
