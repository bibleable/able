# Mark 6 — Jesus sends his followers and feeds a crowd

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches and sends out his twelve apostles.

Mark also tells how John the Baptist was killed.

Later, Jesus feeds a large crowd and walks on the sea.

## Mark 6:1

Jesus left that place and went to his home town. 👣

His followers went with him.

## Mark 6:2

On the Sabbath, Jesus began teaching in the synagogue. 📖

Many people who heard him were amazed.

They asked, “Where did this man get these ideas?

What wisdom has he been given?

How can he do these great miracles?”

**Help with words:** The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

A synagogue is a Jewish place for worship and teaching.

Miracles are wonderful acts showing God's power.

## Mark 6:3

They said, “Is this not the carpenter? 🔨

He is Mary's son.

His brothers are James, Joses, Judas, and Simon.

His sisters are here with us too.”

They were offended by Jesus and would not accept him.

**Help with a word:** A carpenter makes things from wood.

## Mark 6:4

Jesus told them, “A prophet receives honour, except in his home town, among his relatives, and in his home.” 💬

**Help with a word:** A prophet speaks God's message.

## Mark 6:5

Jesus could not do miracles there, except for healing a few sick people by putting his hands on them. 🤲

## Mark 6:6

Jesus was amazed that they did not believe. 👣

He went around the villages, teaching people.

## Mark 6:7

Jesus called the 12 apostles to him. 👣

He began sending them out in pairs.

He gave them authority over unclean spirits.

**Help with words:** Apostles are people sent out for a special task.

Authority means the right to act with power.

Unclean spirits means evil spirits here.

## Mark 6:8

Jesus told them to take only a walking stick for the journey. 🦯

They must take no bread, no bag, and no money in their belts.

## Mark 6:9

They could wear sandals. 👡

But they must not take an extra tunic.

**Help with a word:** A tunic was a long piece of clothing worn next to the body.

## Mark 6:10

Jesus said, “When you enter a house, stay there until you leave that area.” 🏠

## Mark 6:11

Jesus said, “If people will not welcome you or listen, shake the dust from your feet when you leave. 👣

This will be evidence against them.”

**Help with this action:** Shaking off the dust is a sign against people who reject the message.

## Mark 6:12

The apostles went out and told people to turn away from sin. 📣

**Help with a word:** Sin is wrongdoing against God.

## Mark 6:13

They drove out many evil spirits. 🤲

They put oil on many sick people and healed them.

## Mark 6:14

King Herod heard about this, because Jesus's name had become well known. 👑

People said, “John the Baptist has risen from the dead!

That is why he has power to do miracles.”

**Help with the speaker:** This is what people were saying about Jesus.

## Mark 6:15

Other people said, “He is Elijah.” 💬

Still others said, “He is a prophet, like one of the prophets long ago.”

## Mark 6:16

When Herod heard this, he said, “John has risen from the dead! 👑

I had his head cut off!”

## Mark 6:17

Herod had ordered men to arrest John, tie him up, and put him in prison. ⛓️

He did this because of Herodias.

She had been the wife of his brother Philip.

Herod had married her.

## Mark 6:18

John had told Herod, “God's law does not allow you to have your brother's wife.” 💬

## Mark 6:19

Herodias stayed angry with John and wanted to kill him. 💬

But she could not do it.

## Mark 6:20

Herod feared John and protected him. 👑

He knew John was a man who did right and was set apart for God.

John's words puzzled Herod greatly.

Yet Herod gladly listened to him.

## Mark 6:21

Herodias got her chance on Herod's birthday. 🍽️

Herod gave a special meal for his important officials, army leaders, and the leading men of Galilee.

## Mark 6:22

Herodias's daughter came in and danced. 👑

Herod and his guests were pleased.

The king told the girl, “Ask me for anything you want.

I will give it to you.”

## Mark 6:23

Herod made a serious promise. 👑

He said, “I will give you whatever you ask, even up to half my kingdom!”

## Mark 6:24

The girl went out and asked her mother, “What should I ask for?” 💬

Her mother answered, “The head of John the Baptist.”

## Mark 6:25

The girl hurried back to the king at once. 💬

She said, “I want you to give me John the Baptist's head on a large plate, right now.”

## Mark 6:26

The king was deeply sad. 👑

But he did not want to refuse her, because of his promises and his guests.

## Mark 6:27

At once, the king ordered that John's head be brought in. ⛓️

He sent a man whose job was to carry out death sentences.

The man cut off John's head in the prison.

## Mark 6:28

The man brought John's head on a large plate and gave it to the girl. 💬

She gave it to her mother.

## Mark 6:29

John's followers heard what had happened. 🪦

They came and took his body.

They put it in a tomb.

**Help with a word:** A tomb is a place where a dead person is buried.

## Mark 6:30

The apostles gathered around Jesus. 💬

They told him everything they had done and taught.

## Mark 6:31

Jesus said, “Come away with me to a place where we can be alone. ⛵

Let us rest for a while.”

Many people were coming and going.

Jesus and his followers did not even have time to eat.

## Mark 6:32

They went away by themselves in a boat to a place away from people. ⛵

## Mark 6:33

Many people saw them leave and recognised them. 👣

People ran together from all the towns.

They reached the place before Jesus and his followers.

## Mark 6:34

Jesus stepped out of the boat and saw a large crowd. ❤️

He felt deep care for them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd.

He began teaching them many things.

**Help with this picture:** A shepherd leads and cares for sheep.

Jesus saw that these people needed care and guidance.

## Mark 6:35

It was getting late. 🌇

Jesus's followers came to him.

They said, “This place is far from people, and it is already late.”

## Mark 6:36

His followers said, “Send the crowd away. 🍞

Then they can go to the farms and villages nearby to buy food.”

## Mark 6:37

Jesus said, “You give them something to eat.” 🍞

They asked, “Should we spend 200 denarii to buy bread for them all?”

**Help with money:** Denarii were coins.

One denarius was about a day's pay for a worker.

## Mark 6:38

Jesus said, “Go and see how many loaves of bread you have.” 🍞

They checked and said, “Five loaves and two fish.”

## Mark 6:39

Jesus told his followers to have everyone sit in groups on the green grass. 🌿

## Mark 6:40

The people sat in groups of 100 and 50. 👥

## Mark 6:41

Jesus took the five loaves and two fish. 🍞

He looked up to heaven and spoke a blessing.

He broke the bread and gave it to his followers to hand to the people.

He also shared the two fish among them all.

## Mark 6:42

Everyone ate and had enough. 🍞

## Mark 6:43

The followers gathered 12 baskets full of leftover pieces of bread and fish. 🧺

## Mark 6:44

There were 5,000 men who had eaten the bread. 👥

## Mark 6:45

At once, Jesus made his followers get into the boat. ⛵

They were to go ahead of him to Bethsaida.

He sent the crowd away.

## Mark 6:46

After saying goodbye to them, Jesus went up the mountain to pray. 🙏

## Mark 6:47

When evening came, the boat was in the middle of the sea. 🌌

Jesus was alone on the land.

## Mark 6:48

Jesus saw his followers struggling to row because the wind blew against them. 🌊

About the fourth watch of the night, he came towards them, walking on the sea.

He intended to pass by them.

**Help with time:** The fourth watch was about 3 to 6 in the morning.

## Mark 6:49

His followers saw Jesus walking on the sea. 😨

They thought he was a ghost and cried out.

## Mark 6:50

They all saw him and were terrified. 💬

Jesus spoke at once.

He said, “Be brave!

It is I.

Do not be afraid.”

## Mark 6:51

Jesus climbed into the boat with them. ⛵

The wind stopped.

His followers were completely amazed.

## Mark 6:52

They had not understood what the loaves showed. 🍞

Their hearts were still closed to understanding.

## Mark 6:53

They crossed over and landed at Gennesaret. ⛵

They tied the boat there.

## Mark 6:54

As soon as they left the boat, people recognised Jesus. 👀

## Mark 6:55

The people ran through the whole area. 👣

They carried sick people on mats to wherever they heard Jesus was.

## Mark 6:56

Wherever Jesus went, people laid sick people in the marketplaces. 🤲

This happened in villages, towns, and the countryside.

They begged Jesus to let the sick people touch just the edge of his outer coat.

Everyone who touched him was healed.
