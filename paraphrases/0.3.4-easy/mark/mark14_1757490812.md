# Mark 14 — Jesus is betrayed and arrested

**About this chapter**

A woman pours perfume on Jesus, and Judas plans to betray him.

Jesus shares a meal with his followers and prays in Gethsemane.

He is arrested, and Peter denies knowing him.

## Mark 14:1

The Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread were two days away. 📖

The chief priests and teachers of God's law wanted a secret way to arrest Jesus and kill him.

**Help with names:** These festivals remembered how God freed Israel's people from Egypt.

Unleavened bread is bread made without yeast to make it rise.

Chief priests were leading priests.

## Mark 14:2

The leaders said, “We must not do it during the festival. 📖

The people might start a riot.”

**Help with a word:** A riot is a violent disturbance by a crowd.

## Mark 14:3

Jesus was in Bethany, lying beside the table for a meal at Simon the Leper's home. 📖

A woman brought an alabaster jar of expensive perfume made from pure nard.

She broke the jar open and poured the perfume on Jesus's head.

**Help with words:** Alabaster is a stone used to make jars.

Nard is a plant used to make perfume.

Simon the Leper is the name used for the host here.

Leprosy names a serious skin disease in Bible passages.

## Mark 14:4

Some people there became angry. 📖

They said to each other, “Why was this perfume wasted?”

## Mark 14:5

They said, “It could have been sold for more than 300 denarii. 📖

The money could have been given to poor people.”

They spoke harshly to the woman.

**Help with money:** A denarius was a coin worth about a day's pay for a worker.

## Mark 14:6

Jesus said, “Leave her alone. 📖

Why are you troubling her?

She has done something beautiful for me.”

## Mark 14:7

Jesus said, “You will always have poor people with you. 📖

You can help them whenever you want.

But you will not always have me.”

## Mark 14:8

Jesus said, “She has done what she could. 📖

She has poured perfume on my body to prepare for my burial.”

## Mark 14:9

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 📖

Wherever the good news is told throughout the world, people will also tell what she has done.

They will remember her.”

## Mark 14:10

Judas Iscariot was one of the 12 apostles. 📖

He went to the chief priests to hand Jesus over to them.

## Mark 14:11

The chief priests were delighted to hear this. 📖

They promised to give Judas money.

Judas began looking for a chance to hand Jesus over.

## Mark 14:12

The first day of the Feast of Unleavened Bread came. 📖

This was when the Passover lamb was to be killed as an offering.

Jesus's followers asked, “Where do you want us to prepare your Passover meal?”

## Mark 14:13

Jesus sent two followers ahead. 📖

He told them, “Go into the city.

A man carrying a jug of water will meet you.

Follow him.”

## Mark 14:14

Jesus said, “When he enters a house, speak to its owner. 📖

Say, ‘The Teacher asks where his guest room is.

He wants to eat the Passover there with his followers.’”

## Mark 14:15

Jesus said, “The owner will show you a large upstairs room. 📖

It will have furniture and be ready.

Prepare our meal there.”

## Mark 14:16

The followers left and went into the city. 📖

They found everything just as Jesus had told them.

They prepared the Passover meal.

## Mark 14:17

When evening came, Jesus arrived with the 12 apostles. 📖

## Mark 14:18

As they lay beside the table and ate, Jesus spoke. 📖

He said, “I tell you the truth.

One of you eating with me will betray me.”

**Help with a word:** Betray here means handing Jesus over to his enemies.

## Mark 14:19

They became sad. 📖

One after another, they asked, “Surely you do not mean me?”

## Mark 14:20

Jesus answered, “It is one of the 12. 📖

It is the one dipping his hand into the bowl with me.”

## Mark 14:21

Jesus said, “The Son of Man will die as the Scriptures say about him. 📖

But terrible sorrow awaits the man who betrays him!

It would be better for that man if he had never been born.”

**Help with words:** Son of Man is a title Jesus uses for himself.

The Scriptures are the holy writings.

## Mark 14:22

While they ate, Jesus took bread and spoke a blessing. 📖

He broke it and gave it to his followers.

He said, “Take it.

This is my body.”

## Mark 14:23

Jesus took the cup and gave thanks. 📖

He gave it to them.

They all drank from it.

## Mark 14:24

Jesus said, “This is my blood of the covenant, poured out for many.” 📖

**Help with a word:** A covenant is an agreement that the people involved must keep.

Jesus connects the cup with his blood and the covenant.

## Mark 14:25

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 📖

I will not drink from the fruit of the vine again until I drink it anew in God's kingdom.”

**Help with words:** Fruit of the vine refers to the wine made from grapes.

Anew means again, as something new.

God's kingdom means God's rule as King.

## Mark 14:26

They sang a song of praise. 📖

Then they went to the Mount of Olives.

## Mark 14:27

Jesus told them, “You will all turn away. 📖

The Scriptures say, ‘I will strike the shepherd.

The sheep will scatter.’”

**Help with this picture:** Jesus uses these words about a shepherd and sheep for himself and his followers.

## Mark 14:28

Jesus said, “But after I rise from the dead, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.” 📖

## Mark 14:29

Peter said, “Even if everyone else turns away, I never will.” 📖

## Mark 14:30

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. 📖

Tonight, before the rooster crows twice, you will deny knowing me three times.”

## Mark 14:31

Peter kept insisting, “Even if I must die with you, I will never deny knowing you.” 📖

All the others said the same.

## Mark 14:32

They came to a place called Gethsemane. 📖

Jesus told his followers, “Sit here while I pray.”

## Mark 14:33

Jesus took Peter, James, and John with him. 📖

He began to be deeply troubled and upset.

## Mark 14:34

Jesus told them, “My soul is so full of sorrow that I feel close to death. 📖

Stay here and keep watch.”

**Help with a word:** Soul here refers to Jesus's inner life and deepest feelings.

## Mark 14:35

Jesus went a little further and fell to the ground. 📖

He prayed that, if possible, this hour of suffering would pass without him facing it.

## Mark 14:36

Jesus said, “Abba, Father, everything is possible for you. 📖

Take this cup away from me.

Yet do what you want, rather than what I want.”

**Help with words:** Abba means Father.

Jesus uses the cup as a picture of the suffering ahead.

## Mark 14:37

Jesus returned and found the three followers sleeping. 📖

He asked, “Simon, are you asleep?

Could you not keep watch for one hour?”

## Mark 14:38

Jesus said, “Watch and pray so that you do not give in to temptation. 📖

The spirit is willing, but the body is weak.”

**Help with words:** Temptation means being drawn towards doing wrong.

Jesus describes wanting to do something, while the body struggles to do it.

## Mark 14:39

Jesus went away again and prayed the same words. 📖

## Mark 14:40

Jesus returned and found them sleeping again. 📖

Their eyes were so heavy with sleep.

They did not know what to say to him.

## Mark 14:41

Jesus returned a third time. 📖

He said, “Are you still sleeping and resting?

That is enough!

The hour has come.

Look, the Son of Man is being handed over to sinners.”

**Help with a word:** Sinners means people doing wrong against God.

## Mark 14:42

Jesus said, “Get up. 📖

Let us go.

Look, the man betraying me is coming!”

## Mark 14:43

While Jesus was still speaking, Judas arrived. 📖

He was one of the 12 apostles.

A crowd with swords and clubs came with him.

The chief priests, teachers of God's law, and elders had sent them.

**Help with a word:** Elders were leaders of the people.

## Mark 14:44

Judas had arranged a sign with the crowd. 📖

He had said, “The man I kiss is the one.

Arrest him and lead him away under guard.”

## Mark 14:45

Judas went straight to Jesus. 📖

He said, “Rabbi!” and kissed him.

**Help with a word:** Rabbi means teacher.

## Mark 14:46

The men grabbed Jesus and arrested him. 📖

## Mark 14:47

Someone standing nearby drew his sword. 📖

He struck the high priest's servant and cut off his ear.

## Mark 14:48

Jesus asked the crowd, “Have you come with swords and clubs to arrest me as if I were a criminal?” 📖

## Mark 14:49

Jesus said, “Every day I was teaching among you in the temple courtyards. 📖

You did not arrest me.

But this has happened so the Scriptures would come true.”

## Mark 14:50

Everyone left Jesus and ran away. 📖

## Mark 14:51

A young man had been following Jesus. 📖

He wore only a linen cloth around his body.

The men grabbed him.

**Help with a word:** Linen is a kind of cloth made from plant fibres.

## Mark 14:52

But he slipped out of the linen cloth and ran away naked. 📖

## Mark 14:53

They led Jesus to the high priest. 📖

All the chief priests, elders, and teachers of God's law gathered there.

## Mark 14:54

Peter followed Jesus from a distance, into the high priest's courtyard. 📖

He sat with the officers and warmed himself by the fire.

## Mark 14:55

The chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin looked for evidence against Jesus to have him put to death. 📖

But they found none.

**Help with a name:** The Sanhedrin was the Jewish ruling council.

## Mark 14:56

Many people gave false evidence against Jesus. 📖

But their accounts did not agree.

## Mark 14:57

Some men stood up and gave this false evidence against Jesus. 📖

## Mark 14:58

They said, “We heard him say, ‘I will destroy this temple made by human hands. 📖

In three days, I will build another without human hands.’”

**Help with the speaker:** These are the witnesses' claims.

Mark calls their evidence false.

## Mark 14:59

But even these accounts did not agree. 📖

## Mark 14:60

The high priest stood up before them. 📖

He asked Jesus, “Have you no answer?

What about the evidence these men are giving against you?”

## Mark 14:61

Jesus stayed silent and did not answer. 📖

The high priest asked again, “Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One?”

**Help with titles:** Christ means the King God promised to send.

The Blessed One is a name for God.

## Mark 14:62

Jesus answered, “I am. 📖

You will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power.

You will see him coming with the clouds of heaven.”

**Help with words:** Power here is a way of referring to God.

The right hand is a place of great honour.

## Mark 14:63

The high priest tore his clothes. 📖

He said, “Why do we need any more witnesses?”

## Mark 14:64

The high priest said, “You have heard him insult God. 📖

What is your decision?”

They all decided that Jesus deserved to die.

**Help with the speaker:** The high priest is accusing Jesus of insulting God.

## Mark 14:65

Some began spitting on Jesus. 📖

They covered his eyes and hit him with their fists.

They told him, “Prophesy!”

The officers took him and slapped his face.

**Help with a word:** Prophesy means to speak a message from God.

They said this to mock Jesus.

## Mark 14:66

Peter was in the courtyard below. 📖

One of the high priest's servant girls came down.

## Mark 14:67

The girl saw Peter warming himself. 📖

She looked at him and said, “You were with Jesus from Nazareth too.”

## Mark 14:68

Peter denied it. 📖

He said, “I do not know or even understand what you mean.”

He went out to the gateway.

A rooster crowed.

## Mark 14:69

The servant girl saw Peter there. 📖

She said again to the people nearby, “This man is one of them.”

## Mark 14:70

Peter denied it again. 📖

A little later, people nearby said, “You certainly are one of them.

You are from Galilee too.”

## Mark 14:71

Peter began to curse and swear. 📖

He said, “I do not know this man you are talking about!”

**Help with these words:** Peter used curses and an oath to insist that he was telling the truth.

## Mark 14:72

At once, the rooster crowed a second time. 📖

Peter remembered Jesus's words.

“Before the rooster crows twice, you will deny knowing me three times.”

Peter broke down and cried.
