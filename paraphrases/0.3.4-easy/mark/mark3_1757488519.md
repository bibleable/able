# Mark 3 — Jesus chooses twelve apostles

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals a man's hand and chooses twelve apostles.

Some people oppose Jesus.

Jesus explains who belongs to his family.

## Mark 3:1

Jesus went into the synagogue again. 📖

A man with a hand that had shrunk and could not work properly was there.

**Help with a word:** A synagogue is a Jewish place for worship and teaching.

## Mark 3:2

Some people watched Jesus closely. 📖

They wanted to accuse him if he healed the man on the Sabbath.

**Help with a word:** The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## Mark 3:3

Jesus said to the man with the damaged hand, “Stand up here among us.” 📖

## Mark 3:4

Jesus asked the people, “What does the law allow on the Sabbath? 📖

Doing good or doing evil?

Saving a life or destroying it?”

But they said nothing.

## Mark 3:5

Jesus looked around at them with anger and sorrow at their hardness of heart. 📖

He told the man, “Stretch out your hand.”

The man stretched it out, and his hand became well.

**Help with “hardness of heart”:** This pictures being unwilling to understand or respond.

## Mark 3:6

The Pharisees went out. 📖

They began planning with the Herodians how to kill Jesus.

**Help with names:** Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

Herodians supported Herod's rule.

## Mark 3:7

Jesus went away to the sea with his followers. 📖

A large crowd followed from Galilee and Judea.

## Mark 3:8

People also came from Jerusalem, Idumea, the area across the Jordan, and places near Tyre and Sidon. 📖

This large crowd came because they heard about the great things Jesus was doing.

## Mark 3:9

Jesus asked his followers to keep a boat ready for him. 📖

He did not want the crowd to crush him.

## Mark 3:10

Jesus had healed many people. 📖

So everyone with a disease pushed forward to touch him.

## Mark 3:11

When unclean spirits saw Jesus, they fell down before him. 📖

They shouted, “You are the Son of God!”

**Help with words:** Unclean spirits means evil spirits here.

## Mark 3:12

Jesus firmly warned the spirits not to tell people who he was. 📖

## Mark 3:13

Jesus went up a mountain. 📖

He called the people he wanted.

They came to him.

## Mark 3:14

Jesus chose 12 of them and called them apostles. 📖

They would stay with him.

He would send them out to tell God's message.

**Help with a word:** Apostles means people sent out for a special task.

## Mark 3:15

Jesus also gave them authority to drive out evil spirits. 📖

**Help with a word:** Authority means the right to act with power.

## Mark 3:16

These were the 12 Jesus chose. 📖

He chose Simon, whom he named Peter.

## Mark 3:17

He chose James, the son of Zebedee, and James's brother John. 📖

He named them Boanerges.

That means “Sons of Thunder.”

## Mark 3:18

He chose Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, and Thomas. 📖

He also chose James, the son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, and Simon the Zealot.

## Mark 3:19

He also chose Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus. 📖

**Help with “betrayed”:** Here this means handing Jesus over to those acting against Him.

## Mark 3:20

Jesus went home. 📖

A crowd gathered again.

Jesus and his followers could not even eat.

## Mark 3:21

Jesus's family heard about this and went to take charge of him. 📖

They said, “He is out of his mind.”

**Help with the speaker:** These are the family's words about Jesus.

## Mark 3:22

Teachers of God's law came down from Jerusalem. 📖

They said, “Beelzebul has taken control of him.

He drives out evil spirits by the power of their ruler.”

**Help with a name:** Beelzebul is a name used for the ruler of evil spirits.

## Mark 3:23

Jesus called the teachers together and spoke using stories with a meaning. 📖

He asked, “How can Satan drive out Satan?”

## Mark 3:24

Jesus said, “If a kingdom fights against itself, it cannot last.” 📖

## Mark 3:25

Jesus said, “If a family fights against itself, it cannot last.” 📖

## Mark 3:26

Jesus said, “If Satan fights against himself, he cannot last. 📖

His end has come.”

## Mark 3:27

Jesus said, “No one can enter a strong man's house and steal his things without first tying him up. 📖

Then the thief can take things from his house.”

## Mark 3:28

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. 📖

People will be forgiven all their sins and blasphemies, however many they speak.

**Help with “blasphemies”:** These are words that dishonor God.

Jesus continues with a warning in the next verse.

## Mark 3:29

But whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven. 📖

That person is guilty of eternal sin.”

**Help with these words:** Blaspheming means speaking in a way that dishonors God.

Eternal means lasting forever.

Verse 30 gives the reason for this warning: they were saying Jesus had an unclean spirit.

## Mark 3:30

Jesus said this because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.” 📖

## Mark 3:31

Jesus's mother and brothers arrived and stood outside. 📖

They sent someone to call him.

## Mark 3:32

A crowd sat around Jesus. 📖

Someone told him, “Your mother and brothers are outside.

They are asking for you.”

## Mark 3:33

Jesus answered, “Who are my mother and my brothers?” 📖

## Mark 3:34

Jesus looked at the people sitting in a circle around him. 📖

He said, “Here are my mother and my brothers!”

## Mark 3:35

Jesus said, “Anyone who does what God wants is my brother and sister and mother.” 📖
