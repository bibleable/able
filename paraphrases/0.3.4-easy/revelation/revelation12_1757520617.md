# Revelation 12 — The woman and the dragon

**About this chapter**

John sees a woman, her child, and a dragon identified as Satan.

The draft preserves the vision's symbols without assigning them to modern people or events.

## Revelation 12:1

A great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun. 📖

The moon was under her feet, and twelve stars formed a crown on her head.

## Revelation 12:2

She was pregnant, crying out in the pain and agony of childbirth. 📖

## Revelation 12:3

Another heavenly sign appeared: a huge red dragon with seven heads and ten horns. 📖

His heads wore seven royal crowns.

## Revelation 12:4

His tail swept a third of heaven's stars down to earth. 📖

He stood before the woman about to give birth, ready to devour her child immediately.

## Revelation 12:5

She gave birth to a son, a male child who will rule all nations with an iron sceptre. 📖

Her child was caught up to God and his throne.

**Help:** A sceptre is a ruler's staff, a sign of authority.

## Revelation 12:6

The woman fled to the wilderness, where God prepared a place to feed her for 1,260 days. 📖

## Revelation 12:7

War broke out in heaven. 📖

Michael and his angels fought the dragon, and the dragon and his angels fought back.

## Revelation 12:8

The dragon was not strong enough. 📖

There was no longer a place in heaven for him and his angels.

## Revelation 12:9

The great dragon was thrown down: the ancient serpent called the devil and Satan, who deceives the whole world. 📖

He and his angels were thrown to earth.

## Revelation 12:10

I heard a loud heavenly voice: “Now have come our God's salvation, power, and kingdom, and his Christ's authority. 📖

Our brothers' accuser has been thrown down, who accused them day and night before our God.

## Revelation 12:11

They conquered him through the Lamb's blood and their testimony's word. 📖

They did not love their lives so much that they avoided death.

## Revelation 12:12

So rejoice, heavens and everyone living there! 📖

But woe to earth and sea!

The devil has come down furiously, knowing he has little time.”

## Revelation 12:13

Seeing he had been thrown to earth, the dragon pursued the woman who had borne the male child. 📖

## Revelation 12:14

She received two wings of a great eagle to fly away from the serpent to her wilderness place. 📖

There she was fed for a time, times, and half a time.

**About these words:** The draft retains the vision's expression for this period rather than replacing it with a timetable.

## Revelation 12:15

The serpent poured water like a river from his mouth to overtake the woman and sweep her away. 📖

## Revelation 12:16

The earth helped her, opening its mouth to swallow the river poured from the dragon's mouth. 📖

## Revelation 12:17

Angry with the woman, the dragon went to war against her remaining children. 📖

They keep God's commands and hold to Jesus' testimony.

The dragon stood on the seashore.
