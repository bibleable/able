# Revelation 1 — John's vision of Jesus

**About this chapter**

John introduces the revelation of Jesus Christ and describes what he saw on Patmos.

The book uses visions and symbols; explanations of their meaning remain separate from the vision itself.

## Revelation 1:1

This is the revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show his servants what must happen soon. 📖

He made it known by sending his angel to his servant John.

**Help:** Revelation means something made known.

## Revelation 1:2

John testifies to everything he saw: God's word and Jesus Christ's testimony. 📖

## Revelation 1:3

Blessed is the person who reads this prophecy aloud. 📖

Blessed are those who hear and obey what is written, because the time is near.

**Help:** Prophecy is a message from God.

## Revelation 1:4

From John to the seven churches in the province of Asia. 📖

May kindness and peace come from the one who is, was, and is coming.

May they also come from the seven spirits before his throne.

**Help:** Asia here was a Roman province, rather than the whole continent now called Asia.

The meaning of the seven spirits needs further editorial review.

## Revelation 1:5

May they also come from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness and firstborn from the dead. 📖

He is the ruler of earth's kings.

He loves us and freed us from our sins by his blood.

**Help:** Firstborn from the dead describes Christ's place in resurrection and his authority.

## Revelation 1:6

He made us a kingdom, priests serving his God and Father. 📖

To him be glory and power for ever and ever!

Amen.

## Revelation 1:7

Look, he is coming with the clouds! 📖

Every eye will see him, even those who pierced him.

All earth's tribes will mourn because of him.

It will be so!

Amen.

## Revelation 1:8

“I am the Alpha and the Omega,” says the Lord God, who is, was, and is coming, the Almighty. 📖

**Help:** Alpha and Omega are the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet.

## Revelation 1:9

I, John, am your brother and partner in the suffering, kingdom, and endurance that are in Jesus. 📖

I was on Patmos island because of God's word and my testimony about Jesus.

## Revelation 1:10

On the Lord's day, I was in the Spirit and heard a loud voice behind me, like a trumpet. 📖

## Revelation 1:11

It said, “Write what you see on a scroll and send it to the seven churches. 📖

These are Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.”

**Help:** A scroll is a long sheet rolled up for storing written words.

## Revelation 1:12

I turned to see the voice speaking with me. 📖

When I turned, I saw seven golden lampstands,

## Revelation 1:13

and among them someone like the Son of Man. 📖

He wore a long robe with a golden sash round his chest.

**Help:** A sash is a long strip of cloth worn round the body.

Son of Man is a title Jesus uses.

## Revelation 1:14

His hair was white like wool, as white as snow, and his eyes were like blazing fire. 📖

## Revelation 1:15

His feet were like polished bronze refined in a furnace. 📖

His voice was like the roar of many waters.

**Help:** Bronze is a metal.

A furnace is a very hot oven used to heat metal.

## Revelation 1:16

In his right hand, he held seven stars. 📖

A sharp sword with two cutting edges came from his mouth.

His face was like the sun shining at its brightest.

## Revelation 1:17

When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead man. 📖

He put his right hand on me and said, “Do not fear.

I am the First and the Last,

## Revelation 1:18

the Living One. 📖

I was dead, but now I live for ever and ever!

I hold the keys of Death and Hades.

**Help:** Hades refers to the realm of the dead.

Keys picture authority over Death and Hades.

## Revelation 1:19

Write what you have seen, what is happening now, and what will happen after this. 📖

## Revelation 1:20

Here is the mystery of the seven stars in my right hand and the seven golden lampstands. 📖

The stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the lampstands are the seven churches.”
