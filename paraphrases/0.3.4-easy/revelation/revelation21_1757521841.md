# Revelation 21 — God makes everything new

**About this chapter**

John describes a vision of a new heaven, a new earth, and the holy city.

The Lamb is a title for Jesus.

## Revelation 21:1

I saw a new heaven and a new earth. 📖

The first heaven and earth had passed away.

The sea was gone.

## Revelation 21:2

I saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down from God in heaven. 📖

It was ready like a bride beautifully dressed for her husband.

## Revelation 21:3

A loud voice from the throne said, “God's home is now with people. 📖

He will live with them.

They will be his people.

God himself will be with them as their God.”

## Revelation 21:4

“He will wipe every tear from their eyes. 📖

There will be no more death, grief, crying, or pain.

The old things have passed away.”

## Revelation 21:5

The one on the throne said, “I am making everything new.” 📖

He told me to write this because these words are true and trustworthy.

## Revelation 21:6

He said, “It is done! 📖

I am the Alpha and Omega, the Beginning and End.

I freely give thirsty people water from the spring of life.”

**Help with words:** Alpha and Omega are the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet.

## Revelation 21:7

“Those who overcome will inherit all things. 📖

I will be their God, and they will be my children.”

## Revelation 21:8

“The cowardly, unbelieving, those doing detestable evil, and murderers will be in the lake burning with fire and sulphur. 📖

So will the sexually immoral, those using magic, idol worshippers, and all liars.

This is the second death.”

## Revelation 21:9

One of the 7 angels with 7 bowls full of the 7 final plagues came. 📖

He said, “Come.

I will show you the bride, the Lamb's wife.”

**Help with a word:** Plagues are terrible disasters.

## Revelation 21:10

In the Spirit, he carried me to a very high mountain. 📖

He showed me holy Jerusalem coming down from God in heaven.

## Revelation 21:11

The city shone with God's glory. 📖

It sparkled like a precious jasper stone, clear as crystal.

**Help with words:** Jasper is a kind of valuable stone.

## Revelation 21:12

The city had a great high wall and 12 gates. 📖

There were 12 angels at the gates.

The names of Israel's 12 tribes were written on them.

## Revelation 21:13

There were 3 gates on each side: east, north, south, and west. 📖

## Revelation 21:14

The city wall had 12 foundations. 📖

They carried the names of the Lamb's 12 apostles.

**Help with a word:** Apostles were messengers Jesus sent.

## Revelation 21:15

The angel had a gold measuring stick. 📖

He used it to measure the city, gates, and walls.

## Revelation 21:16

The city was square. 📖

He measured it with his rod.

Its length, width, and height were equal: 12,000 stadia each.

**Help with a word:** A stadion was an ancient distance measure.

12,000 stadia is about 2,200 kilometres.

## Revelation 21:17

The angel measured the wall as 144 cubits, using human measurements. 📖

**Help with a word:** A cubit was about the length from elbow to fingertip.

144 cubits is about 65 metres.

## Revelation 21:18

The wall was jasper. 📖

The city was pure gold, clear like glass.

## Revelation 21:19

The wall's foundations were decorated with every kind of precious stone. 📖

Foundation 1 was jasper, 2 sapphire, 3 chalcedony, and 4 emerald.

## Revelation 21:20

Foundation 5 was sardonyx, 6 carnelian, 7 chrysolite, and 8 beryl. 📖

Foundation 9 was topaz, 10 chrysoprase, 11 jacinth, and 12 amethyst.

**Help with these names:** These are kinds of precious stones.

## Revelation 21:21

The 12 gates were 12 pearls, each made from one pearl. 📖

The city's main street was pure gold, clear like glass.

## Revelation 21:22

I saw no temple there. 📖

The Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple.

## Revelation 21:23

The city does not need the sun or moon. 📖

God's glory lights it.

The Lamb is its lamp.

## Revelation 21:24

The nations will walk by its light. 📖

Earth's kings will bring their glory into it.

## Revelation 21:25

Its gates will never close at day's end. 📖

There will be no night there.

## Revelation 21:26

The nations' glory and honour will be brought into the city. 📖

## Revelation 21:27

Nothing impure will enter. 📖

No one who does detestable evil or tells lies will enter.

Only people named in the Lamb's Book of Life will enter.

**Help with words:** Detestable evil means deeply offensive wrongdoing.

Impure here concerns what is unacceptable to God, rather than physical dirt, illness, or disability.
