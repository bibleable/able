# Revelation 13 — Two beasts

**About this chapter**

John sees beasts exercising power, deceiving people, and demanding worship.

## Revelation 13:1

I saw a beast rise from the sea with ten horns and seven heads. 📖

Its horns wore ten royal crowns, and its heads had blasphemous names.

**Help:** Blasphemous means insulting God or falsely claiming what belongs to God.

## Revelation 13:2

It looked like a leopard, with a bear's feet and a lion's mouth. 📖

The dragon gave it his power, throne, and great authority.

## Revelation 13:3

One head seemed fatally wounded, but the deadly wound healed. 📖

The whole world marvelled and followed the beast.

## Revelation 13:4

They worshipped the dragon for giving authority to the beast. 📖

They also worshipped the beast, saying, “Who is like the beast?

Who can fight it?”

## Revelation 13:5

The beast received a mouth speaking proud, blasphemous words and authority to act for 42 months. 📖

## Revelation 13:6

It spoke blasphemies against God, slandering his name and his tabernacle, those living in heaven. 📖

**Help:** A tabernacle is a tent or dwelling place.

Slandering means speaking harmful lies against someone.

## Revelation 13:7

It was allowed to fight God's holy people and conquer them. 📖

It received authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation.

## Revelation 13:8

All earth's inhabitants whose names were not written from the world's foundation in the Book of Life will worship it. 📖

That book belongs to the Lamb who was killed.

## Revelation 13:9

Anyone with an ear should hear. 📖

## Revelation 13:10

“Anyone destined for captivity will go into captivity. 📖

Anyone who must die by the sword must be killed by the sword.”

This calls for God's people's endurance and faith.

## Revelation 13:11

Another beast rose from the earth with two horns like a lamb, but it spoke like a dragon. 📖

## Revelation 13:12

It exercised all the first beast's authority and made the earth and its inhabitants worship the first beast. 📖

That beast's deadly wound had healed.

## Revelation 13:13

The second beast performed great signs, even bringing fire from heaven to earth before the people. 📖

## Revelation 13:14

The signs it was allowed to perform for the first beast deceived earth's inhabitants. 📖

It told them to make an image of the beast wounded by the sword yet still living.

## Revelation 13:15

The second beast was allowed to give breath to the first beast's image. 📖

The image could speak and cause everyone refusing to worship it to be killed.

## Revelation 13:16

It required everyone, small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, to receive a mark. 📖

The mark was on their right hand or forehead.

## Revelation 13:17

No one could buy or sell without the mark, the beast's name or the number of its name. 📖

## Revelation 13:18

This calls for wisdom. 📖

Anyone with insight should calculate the beast's number, because it is a man's number: 666.

**About this number:** The draft retains 666 without assigning it to a modern person, product, or technology.
