# Revelation 19 — The Lamb's wedding and the rider

**About this chapter**

John hears heaven's praise, then sees a rider defeat the beast and its allies.

## Revelation 19:1

I heard what sounded like a great heavenly crowd roaring, “Hallelujah! 📖

Salvation, glory, and power belong to our God!

**Help:** Hallelujah means praise the Lord.

## Revelation 19:2

His judgments are true and just. 📖

He judged the great prostitute who corrupted earth with her immorality.

He avenged his servants' blood shed by her hand.”

## Revelation 19:3

They called a second time, “Hallelujah! 📖

Her smoke rises for ever and ever.”

## Revelation 19:4

The twenty-four elders and four living creatures fell down, worshipping God on the throne, saying, “Amen, Hallelujah!” 📖

## Revelation 19:5

A voice from the throne said, “Praise our God, all his servants and those fearing him, small and great!” 📖

## Revelation 19:6

I heard something like a great crowd's roar, rushing waters, and mighty thunder. 📖

They cried, “Hallelujah, because our Lord God Almighty reigns!

## Revelation 19:7

Let us rejoice, be glad, and give him glory! 📖

The Lamb's marriage has come, and his bride has made herself ready.

## Revelation 19:8

She received fine linen clothing, bright and pure.” 📖

Her fine linen is the righteous deeds of God's holy people.

## Revelation 19:9

The angel told me to write, “Blessed are those invited to the Lamb's wedding supper.” 📖

He added, “These are God's true words.”

## Revelation 19:10

I fell at his feet to worship him, but he said, “Do not do that! 📖

I serve alongside you and your brothers and sisters who rely on Jesus' testimony.

Worship God!

Jesus' testimony is the spirit of prophecy.”

**About these words:** Prophecy means a message from God.

The relationship between Jesus' testimony and the spirit of prophecy needs further editorial review.

## Revelation 19:11

I saw heaven opened and a white horse, whose rider is called Faithful and True. 📖

He judges and fights with righteousness.

## Revelation 19:12

His eyes are like blazing fire, and he wears many royal crowns. 📖

He has a written name known only to himself.

## Revelation 19:13

He wears a robe dipped in blood, and his name is The Word of God. 📖

## Revelation 19:14

Heaven's armies follow him on white horses, wearing fine linen, white and pure. 📖

## Revelation 19:15

A sharp sword comes from his mouth to strike the nations, and he will rule them with an iron sceptre. 📖

He treads the winepress of God Almighty's furious wrath.

**Help:** A sceptre is a ruler's staff; a winepress crushes grapes.

These images describe authority and judgment in the vision.

## Revelation 19:16

On his robe and thigh is written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS. 📖

## Revelation 19:17

An angel stood in the sun and called loudly to every bird flying overhead. 📖

“Come, gather for God's great supper.

## Revelation 19:18

Eat the flesh of kings, commanders, mighty people, horses, riders, and everyone, slave and free, small and great.” 📖

## Revelation 19:19

I saw the beast, earth's kings, and their armies gathered to fight the horse's rider and his army. 📖

## Revelation 19:20

The beast was captured with the false prophet. 📖

The prophet performed signs for the beast, deceiving those bearing its mark and worshipping its image.

Both were thrown alive into the fiery lake of burning sulphur.

## Revelation 19:21

The rest were killed by the sword coming from the rider's mouth, and all birds filled themselves with their flesh. 📖
