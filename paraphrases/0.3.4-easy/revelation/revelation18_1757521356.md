# Revelation 18 — Babylon falls

**About this chapter**

John hears Babylon's fall announced and the grief of those enriched by her trade.

## Revelation 18:1

Another angel came down from heaven with great authority, lighting earth with his glory. 📖

## Revelation 18:2

He cried mightily, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! 📖

She has become a home for demons, every unclean spirit and bird, and every detestable beast.

**Help:** Demons are evil spirits.

Unclean here concerns religious impurity rather than physical dirt, illness, or disability.

## Revelation 18:3

All nations drank the wine of the passion of her immorality. 📖

Earth's kings acted immorally with her, and earth's merchants grew rich through her extravagant luxury.”

## Revelation 18:4

Another heavenly voice said, “Come out of her, my people, to avoid sharing her sins or suffering her plagues. 📖

## Revelation 18:5

Her sins pile up to heaven, and God remembers her wrongdoing. 📖

## Revelation 18:6

Pay her back as she paid others, giving her double for what she did. 📖

Mix a double portion in her own cup.

## Revelation 18:7

Give her torment and grief equal to her self-glory and luxury. 📖

In her heart she says, ‘I sit as queen, not a widow, and will never see grief.’

## Revelation 18:8

Her plagues will come in one day: death, grief, and famine. 📖

Fire will consume her, because the Lord God judging her is mighty.”

## Revelation 18:9

Earth's kings who shared her sexual immorality and luxury will weep and wail when they see her burning smoke. 📖

## Revelation 18:10

Afraid of her torment, they will stand far away, crying, “Woe, woe to mighty Babylon, the great city! 📖

Your judgment came in a single hour.”

## Revelation 18:11

Earth's merchants will weep and mourn because no one buys their cargo any more. 📖

## Revelation 18:12

Their cargo includes gold, silver, precious stones, pearls, fine linen, purple cloth, silk, and scarlet cloth. 📖

It includes every kind of citron wood and every article of ivory, precious wood, bronze, iron, and marble.

**Help:** Citron wood is valuable fragrant wood; ivory comes from animal tusks.

## Revelation 18:13

It includes cinnamon, spice, incense, myrrh, frankincense, wine, olive oil, fine flour, and wheat. 📖

It includes cattle, sheep, horses, carriages, and the bodies and souls of slaves.

**Help:** Incense, myrrh, and frankincense are fragrant substances.

The final cargo names human beings traded as slaves.

## Revelation 18:14

They will say, “The fruit your soul desired is gone. 📖

All your luxury and splendour have vanished, never to be seen again.”

## Revelation 18:15

The merchants enriched by selling these things will stand far away, afraid of her torment, weeping and mourning. 📖

## Revelation 18:16

They will say, “Woe, woe to the great city clothed in fine linen, purple, and scarlet! 📖

She was decorated with gold, precious stones, and pearls.

## Revelation 18:17

Such enormous wealth was destroyed in one hour!” 📖

Every shipmaster, passenger, sailor, and everyone making a living from the sea will stand far away.

## Revelation 18:18

Seeing her burning smoke, they will cry, “What city was ever like this great city?” 📖

## Revelation 18:19

They will throw dust on their heads, weeping, mourning, and crying, “Woe, woe to the great city! 📖

Her wealth made everyone with ships at sea rich, but she was destroyed in one hour.”

## Revelation 18:20

Rejoice over her, heaven, God's holy people, apostles, and prophets! 📖

God has given judgment against her for you.

## Revelation 18:21

A mighty angel threw a stone like a great millstone into the sea. 📖

He said, “With such violence Babylon the great city will be thrown down, never seen again.

**Help:** A millstone is a large stone used to grind grain.

## Revelation 18:22

Harps, musicians, flutes, and trumpets will never sound in you again. 📖

No craftsman of any trade will be found in you again, nor will a millstone be heard.

## Revelation 18:23

No lamp will shine in you again, and no bride or bridegroom will be heard again. 📖

Your merchants were earth's great ones, and your sorcery deceived all nations.”

**Help:** Sorcery means using magic or spiritual powers in ways opposed to God.

## Revelation 18:24

In her was found the blood of prophets, God's holy people, and everyone killed on earth. 📖
