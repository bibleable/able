# Revelation 20 — The thousand years and final judgment

**About this chapter**

John describes Satan's binding, release, and defeat, then judgment before a great white throne.

The draft follows the vision's sequence without adding a timetable for modern events.

## Revelation 20:1

An angel came down from heaven with the Abyss's key and a great chain in his hand. 📖

## Revelation 20:2

He seized the dragon, the ancient serpent who is the devil and Satan, and bound him for a thousand years. 📖

## Revelation 20:3

He threw him into the Abyss, shut it, and sealed it over him. 📖

This stopped him deceiving the nations until the thousand years ended.

After that, he must be released briefly.

**Help:** The Abyss is a deep pit associated with destructive spiritual powers.

## Revelation 20:4

I saw thrones with people on them who had authority to judge. 📖

I saw souls beheaded for Jesus' testimony and God's word.

I also saw those who had not worshipped the beast or its image or received its forehead or hand mark.

They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.

**Help:** Beheaded means killed by having the head cut off.

The precise relationship between these descriptions of people needs further editorial review.

## Revelation 20:5

The other dead did not come to life until the thousand years ended. 📖

This is the first resurrection.

## Revelation 20:6

Blessed and holy are those sharing the first resurrection! 📖

The second death has no power over them.

They will be God's and Christ's priests, reigning with him for a thousand years.

## Revelation 20:7

When the thousand years end, Satan will be released from prison. 📖

## Revelation 20:8

He will deceive the nations at earth's four corners, Gog and Magog, gathering them for battle. 📖

They number like sand on the seashore.

## Revelation 20:9

They marched across earth's wide expanse and surrounded God's people's camp and the beloved city. 📖

But fire came down from heaven and consumed them.

## Revelation 20:10

The devil who deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulphur. 📖

The beast and false prophet had already been thrown there.

They will be tormented day and night for ever and ever.

## Revelation 20:11

I saw a great white throne and someone sitting on it. 📖

Earth and heaven fled from his presence, and no place was found for them.

## Revelation 20:12

The dead, great and small, stood before the throne. 📖

Books were opened, including the Book of Life.

The dead were judged by their deeds recorded in the books.

## Revelation 20:13

The sea gave up its dead, and Death and Hades gave up theirs. 📖

Each person was judged by their deeds.

**Help:** Hades refers to the realm of the dead.

## Revelation 20:14

Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire, which is the second death. 📖

## Revelation 20:15

Anyone whose name was not found written in the Book of Life was thrown into the lake of fire. 📖
