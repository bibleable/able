# Revelation 22 — Life, warning, and Jesus' coming

**About this chapter**

John sees the river and tree of life and records the book's closing messages.

Speakers include the angel, Jesus, John, the Spirit, and the bride.

## Revelation 22:1

The angel showed me a river of life's water, clear as crystal, flowing from God's and the Lamb's throne. 📖

## Revelation 22:2

It flowed down the middle of the city's main street. 📖

On either side stood the tree of life, bearing twelve kinds of fruit and a fresh crop each month.

Its leaves heal the nations.

## Revelation 22:3

There will be no curse any more. 📖

God's and the Lamb's throne will be in the city, and his servants will worship him.

## Revelation 22:4

They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads. 📖

## Revelation 22:5

There will be no more night, and they will need neither lamplight nor sunlight. 📖

The Lord God will shine on them, and they will reign for ever and ever.

## Revelation 22:6

The angel said, “These words are trustworthy and true. 📖

The Lord, God of the prophets' spirits, sent his angel to show his servants what must happen soon.”

## Revelation 22:7

“Look, I am coming soon. 📖

Blessed is the person keeping this book's words of prophecy.”

**About the speaker:** The closing messages identify Jesus as the one coming soon; see verses 16 and 20.

## Revelation 22:8

I, John, heard and saw these things. 📖

After hearing and seeing them, I fell at the feet of the angel showing them, to worship him.

## Revelation 22:9

He said, “Do not do that! 📖

I serve alongside you, your brothers the prophets, and those keeping this book's words.

Worship God!”

## Revelation 22:10

He told me, “Do not seal this book's words of prophecy, because the time is near. 📖

## Revelation 22:11

Let the unrighteous remain unrighteous, and the vile remain vile. 📖

Let the righteous keep doing right, and the holy remain holy.”

**Help:** Unrighteous and vile describe wrongdoing; righteous describes being right before God and doing right.

Holy means set apart for God.

## Revelation 22:12

“Look, I am coming soon with my reward, to repay each person according to their deeds. 📖

## Revelation 22:13

I am Alpha and Omega, First and Last, Beginning and End.” 📖

**Help:** Alpha and Omega are the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet.

## Revelation 22:14

Blessed are those washing their robes, to have the right to life's tree and enter the city through its gates. 📖

## Revelation 22:15

Outside are dogs, sorcerers, sexually immoral people, murderers, idol worshippers, and everyone loving and practising lies. 📖

**About these words:** Dogs is a severe image in this list; its precise referent needs independent editorial review.

Sorcerers use magic or spiritual powers in ways opposed to God.

The draft does not turn the image into a new label for a modern group.

## Revelation 22:16

“I, Jesus, sent my angel to give you this testimony for the churches. 📖

I am David's Root and Offspring, the bright Morning Star.”

**Help:** Offspring means descendant; Root and Morning Star are titles expressing Jesus' identity and promise.

## Revelation 22:17

The Spirit and bride say, “Come!” 📖

Let whoever hears say, “Come!”

Let anyone thirsty come, and anyone wanting life's water drink freely.

## Revelation 22:18

I testify to everyone hearing this book's prophecy. 📖

If anyone adds to its words, God will add the plagues described in this book to them.

## Revelation 22:19

If anyone removes words from this prophecy book, God will remove their share in life's tree and the holy city. 📖

These are described in this book.

## Revelation 22:20

The one testifying says, “Yes, I am coming soon.” 📖

Amen.

Come, Lord Jesus!

## Revelation 22:21

May the Lord Jesus' kindness be with all God's holy people. 📖

Amen.
