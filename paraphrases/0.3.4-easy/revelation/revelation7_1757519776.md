# Revelation 7 — Sealed servants and a great crowd

**About this chapter**

John hears the number sealed from Israel's tribes and sees a crowd no one can count.

## Revelation 7:1

I saw four angels at earth's four corners, holding back its four winds. 📖

No wind could blow on land, sea, or any tree.

## Revelation 7:2

Another angel rose from the east with the living God's seal. 📖

He called loudly to the four angels who had power to harm land and sea.

## Revelation 7:3

He said, “Do not harm land, sea, or trees until we seal our God's servants on their foreheads.” 📖

**Help:** A seal marks ownership or confirms something as genuine.

## Revelation 7:4

I heard the number sealed: 144,000 from all Israel's tribes. 📖

## Revelation 7:5

From Judah's tribe, 12,000 were sealed; from Reuben's, 12,000; from Gad's, 12,000. 📖

## Revelation 7:6

From Asher's tribe, 12,000; from Naphtali's, 12,000; from Manasseh's, 12,000. 📖

## Revelation 7:7

From Simeon's tribe, 12,000; from Levi's, 12,000; from Issachar's, 12,000. 📖

## Revelation 7:8

From Zebulun's tribe, 12,000; from Joseph's, 12,000; from Benjamin's, 12,000. 📖

## Revelation 7:9

Then I saw a crowd too large to count, from every nation, tribe, people, and language. 📖

They stood before the throne and the Lamb, wearing white robes and holding palm branches.

## Revelation 7:10

They cried loudly, “Salvation belongs to our God on the throne, and to the Lamb!” 📖

## Revelation 7:11

All the angels stood round the throne, the elders, and the four living creatures. 📖

They fell facedown before the throne and worshipped God,

## Revelation 7:12

saying, “Amen! 📖

Blessing, glory, wisdom, thanks, honour, power, and strength belong to our God for ever and ever!

Amen.”

## Revelation 7:13

An elder asked me, “Who are these people in white robes, and where did they come from?” 📖

## Revelation 7:14

I answered, “Sir, you know.” 📖

He replied, “They have come out of the great tribulation.

They washed their robes and made them white in the Lamb's blood.

**Help:** Tribulation means a time of severe trouble and suffering.

The washing is a vision image of cleansing through the Lamb, rather than ordinary clothes washing.

## Revelation 7:15

So they stand before God's throne and serve him day and night in his temple. 📖

The one on the throne will spread his tabernacle over them.

**Help:** A tabernacle is a tent or dwelling place; spreading it pictures God's sheltering presence.

## Revelation 7:16

They will never hunger or thirst again. 📖

The sun will not beat down on them, nor any scorching heat.

## Revelation 7:17

The Lamb in the throne's centre will be their shepherd. 📖

He will lead them to springs of living water, and God will wipe every tear from their eyes.”
