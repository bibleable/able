# Revelation 2 — Messages to four churches

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives John messages for Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, and Thyatira.

The messages include praise, commands, warnings, judgments, and promises with conditions.

## Revelation 2:1

“Write to the angel of the church in Ephesus. 📖

These words come from the one holding seven stars in his right hand and walking among seven golden lampstands.

## Revelation 2:2

I know your deeds, hard work, and endurance. 📖

You cannot bear evil people.

You tested those falsely claiming to be apostles and found them liars.

**Help:** Apostles are people sent to carry Christ's message.

## Revelation 2:3

You have patiently endured many things for my name's sake without growing tired. 📖

## Revelation 2:4

But I hold this against you: you have left your first love. 📖

## Revelation 2:5

Remember how far you have fallen. 📖

Repent and do what you did at first.

If you do not repent, I will come and remove your lampstand from its place.

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

## Revelation 2:6

But this counts in your favour: you hate the Nicolaitans' deeds, which I also hate. 📖

## Revelation 2:7

Anyone with an ear should hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖

I will let the person who overcomes eat from the tree of life in God's Paradise.

**Help:** Overcomes means remains faithful through opposition and temptation.

The call to hear asks people to pay attention to the message.

## Revelation 2:8

Write to the angel of the church in Smyrna. 📖

These words come from the First and the Last, who died and returned to life.

## Revelation 2:9

I know your suffering and poverty, though you are rich! 📖

I know the slander from those falsely claiming to be Jews, who are actually a synagogue of Satan.

**About these words:** Slander means harmful lies.

A synagogue is a Jewish place of gathering and worship.

This severe description addresses the opponents in this message; it does not describe every Jewish person or synagogue.

## Revelation 2:10

Do not fear what you are about to suffer. 📖

The devil will throw some of you into prison to test you.

You will suffer trouble for ten days.

Be faithful even to death, and I will give you the crown of life.

## Revelation 2:11

Anyone with an ear should hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖

The person who overcomes will not be harmed by the second death.

**Help:** Later visions describe the second death as the lake of fire.

## Revelation 2:12

Write to the angel of the church in Pergamum. 📖

These words come from the one holding the sharp sword with two cutting edges.

## Revelation 2:13

I know you live where Satan's throne is, yet you hold firmly to my name. 📖

You did not deny faith in me, even when Antipas, my faithful witness, was killed among you where Satan lives.

## Revelation 2:14

But I have a few things against you: some follow Balaam's teaching. 📖

He taught Balak to put a stumbling block before Israel, leading them to eat idol sacrifices and commit sexual wrongdoing.

**Help:** A stumbling block pictures something leading people into sin.

An idol is something worshipped as a god.

## Revelation 2:15

Likewise, some of you follow the Nicolaitans' teaching. 📖

## Revelation 2:16

So repent! 📖

Otherwise I will come soon and fight them with the sword of my mouth.

## Revelation 2:17

Anyone with an ear should hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖

I will give hidden manna to the person who overcomes.

I will also give them a white stone with a new name written on it, known only to its receiver.

**Help:** Manna was food God gave Israel in the wilderness.

The hidden manna and white stone are images whose full interpretation needs further review.

## Revelation 2:18

Write to the angel of the church in Thyatira. 📖

These words come from God's Son, whose eyes are like blazing fire and feet like polished bronze.

## Revelation 2:19

I know your deeds: love, faith, service, and endurance. 📖

Your recent deeds are greater than your first.

## Revelation 2:20

But I hold this against you: you tolerate that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess. 📖

Her teaching misleads my servants into sexual wrongdoing and eating idol sacrifices.

**Help:** A prophetess is a woman claiming to speak a message from God.

## Revelation 2:21

I gave her time to repent of her sexual wrongdoing, but she is unwilling. 📖

## Revelation 2:22

I will throw her onto a bed of sickness. 📖

Those committing adultery with her will suffer great trouble unless they repent of her deeds.

**Help:** Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

This judgment concerns Jezebel and those sharing her deeds; it does not say that every illness results from sin.

## Revelation 2:23

I will strike her children dead. 📖

All the churches will know that I search minds and hearts.

I will repay each of you according to your deeds.

**About this difficult verse:** This draft retains the stated judgment and the word children.

Whether children here identifies physical offspring or followers needs careful independent editorial review.

## Revelation 2:24

I speak to the rest of you in Thyatira. 📖

You reject her teaching and have not learned Satan's so-called deep things.

I place no further burden on you,

## Revelation 2:25

except to hold firmly to what you have until I come. 📖

## Revelation 2:26

I will give authority over the nations to the person who overcomes and keeps doing my work until the end. 📖

## Revelation 2:27

They will rule with an iron sceptre and shatter the nations like pottery, as I received authority from my Father. 📖

**Help:** A sceptre is a ruler's staff, a sign of authority.

## Revelation 2:28

I will also give that person the morning star. 📖

## Revelation 2:29

Anyone with an ear should hear what the Spirit says to the churches.” 📖
