# Revelation 9 — The fifth and sixth trumpets

**About this chapter**

John describes locusts, mounted troops, and the suffering that follows two trumpet blasts.

The draft keeps these as vision descriptions without identifying them with modern people or machines.

## Revelation 9:1

The fifth angel sounded his trumpet. 📖

I saw a star fallen from heaven to earth, and it received the key to the Abyss's pit.

**Help:** The Abyss is a deep pit associated here with destructive spiritual powers.

## Revelation 9:2

The star opened the pit, and smoke rose like smoke from a great furnace. 📖

The pit's smoke darkened the sun and air.

## Revelation 9:3

Locusts came down to earth from the smoke, receiving power like earth's scorpions. 📖

**Help:** Locusts are insects; scorpions have tails that sting.

The following verses describe unusual creatures in John's vision.

## Revelation 9:4

They were told not to harm grass, plants, or trees. 📖

They could harm only people without God's seal on their foreheads.

## Revelation 9:5

They received no power to kill, only to torment people for five months. 📖

The torment was like a scorpion's sting.

## Revelation 9:6

In those days, people will seek death without finding it. 📖

They will long to die, but death will escape them.

## Revelation 9:7

The locusts looked like horses ready for battle. 📖

They had something like golden crowns on their heads and faces like human faces.

## Revelation 9:8

Their hair was like women's hair, and their teeth were like lions' teeth. 📖

## Revelation 9:9

They had breastplates like iron armour. 📖

Their wings sounded like many horses and chariots roaring into battle.

**Help:** Breastplates protect the chest; chariots are wheeled vehicles pulled by horses.

## Revelation 9:10

Their tails had stingers like scorpions, with power to injure people for five months. 📖

## Revelation 9:11

Their king was the Abyss's angel, named Abaddon in Hebrew and Apollyon in Greek. 📖

**Help:** These names mean destruction and destroyer.

## Revelation 9:12

The first woe has passed; two more will follow. 📖

## Revelation 9:13

The sixth angel sounded his trumpet. 📖

I heard a voice from the golden altar's four horns before God.

## Revelation 9:14

The voice told the sixth angel with the trumpet, “Release the four angels bound at the great river Euphrates.” 📖

## Revelation 9:15

The four angels, prepared for this hour, day, month, and year, were released to kill a third of humanity. 📖

## Revelation 9:16

The mounted troops numbered two hundred million; I heard their number. 📖

## Revelation 9:17

In my vision, the riders had breastplates coloured like fire, sapphire, and sulphur. 📖

The horses had heads like lions, and fire, smoke, and sulphur came from their mouths.

**Help:** Sapphire is a precious stone; sulphur is a substance that burns.

## Revelation 9:18

These three plagues, fire, smoke, and sulphur from their mouths, killed a third of humanity. 📖

## Revelation 9:19

The horses' power was in their mouths and tails. 📖

Their tails were like snakes with heads that caused harm.

## Revelation 9:20

The people who survived these plagues still did not repent of their hands' work. 📖

They kept worshipping demons and idols made of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood.

These idols cannot see, hear, or walk.

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

Demons are evil spirits; idols are things worshipped as gods.

## Revelation 9:21

They also did not repent of murder, sorcery, sexual wrongdoing, or theft. 📖

**Help:** Sorcery means using magic or spiritual powers in ways opposed to God.
