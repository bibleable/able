# Revelation 11 — Two witnesses and the seventh trumpet

**About this chapter**

John sees two witnesses killed and raised, then hears the seventh trumpet.

## Revelation 11:1

I received a measuring rod like a staff. 📖

I was told, “Measure God's temple and altar, and count the worshippers there.

## Revelation 11:2

Leave out the temple's outer courtyard; do not measure it. 📖

It has been given to the nations, who will trample the holy city for 42 months.

## Revelation 11:3

I will give power to my two witnesses, who will prophesy for 1,260 days, wearing sackcloth.” 📖

**Help:** Prophesy means speak a message from God.

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn in mourning.

## Revelation 11:4

These witnesses are the two olive trees and two lampstands standing before earth's Lord. 📖

## Revelation 11:5

If anyone wants to harm them, fire comes from their mouths and consumes their enemies. 📖

Anyone wanting to harm them must be killed in this way.

## Revelation 11:6

They can shut the sky so no rain falls while they prophesy. 📖

They can turn water into blood and strike earth with every kind of plague as often as they wish.

## Revelation 11:7

When they finish their testimony, the beast rising from the Abyss will fight, overcome, and kill them. 📖

**Help:** The Abyss is a deep pit associated with destructive spiritual powers.

## Revelation 11:8

Their bodies will lie in the great city's street, where their Lord was crucified. 📖

The city is figuratively called Sodom and Egypt.

**Help:** Figuratively means using names as pictures rather than literal place names.

Crucified means killed on a cross.

## Revelation 11:9

For three and a half days, people from all peoples, tribes, languages, and nations will view their bodies. 📖

They will not allow their bodies to be buried in a tomb.

## Revelation 11:10

Earth's inhabitants will gloat over them, celebrate, and send gifts to each other. 📖

These two prophets had tormented them.

## Revelation 11:11

After three and a half days, God's breath of life entered the witnesses, and they stood up. 📖

Those watching were filled with great fear.

## Revelation 11:12

The witnesses heard a loud heavenly voice saying, “Come up here.” 📖

They rose to heaven in a cloud while their enemies watched.

## Revelation 11:13

That hour, a great earthquake destroyed a tenth of the city and killed seven thousand people. 📖

The rest were terrified and gave glory to heaven's God.

## Revelation 11:14

The second woe has passed; the third is coming soon. 📖

**Help:** Woe announces terrible trouble and suffering.

## Revelation 11:15

The seventh angel sounded his trumpet. 📖

Loud heavenly voices said, “The world's kingdom has become the kingdom of our Lord and his Christ.

He will reign for ever and ever.”

## Revelation 11:16

The twenty-four elders on thrones before God fell facedown and worshipped him. 📖

## Revelation 11:17

They said, “We thank you, Lord God Almighty, who is and who was. 📖

You have taken your great power and begun to reign.

## Revelation 11:18

The nations were angry, and your wrath has come. 📖

The time has come to judge the dead and reward your servants the prophets.

It is time to reward your holy people and those fearing your name, both small and great.

It is time to destroy those destroying the earth.”

**Help:** Wrath means strong anger against evil, expressed in judgment.

## Revelation 11:19

God's heavenly temple opened, and his covenant ark appeared inside. 📖

Lightning, rumblings, thunder, an earthquake, and a great hailstorm followed.

**Help:** The covenant ark was the sacred chest connected with God's covenant with Israel.

A covenant is a binding agreement or relationship established by God.
