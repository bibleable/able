# Revelation 4 — The throne in heaven

**About this chapter**

John sees God's throne, living creatures, and elders worshipping God.

## Revelation 4:1

After this, I looked and saw a door standing open in heaven. 📖

The voice I first heard, sounding like a trumpet, said, “Come up here.

I will show you what must happen after these things.”

## Revelation 4:2

At once I was in the Spirit. 📖

I saw a throne in heaven with someone sitting on it.

## Revelation 4:3

The one seated there looked like jasper and carnelian. 📖

A rainbow shining like an emerald surrounded the throne.

**Help:** Jasper, carnelian, and emerald are precious stones.

## Revelation 4:4

Twenty-four other thrones surrounded it. 📖

Twenty-four elders sat on them, wearing white clothes and golden crowns.

## Revelation 4:5

Lightning flashes, rumblings, and thunder came from the throne. 📖

Seven fiery torches burned before it; these are God's seven spirits.

## Revelation 4:6

Before the throne was something like a glass sea, clear as crystal. 📖

In the centre, round the throne, were four living creatures covered with eyes in front and behind.

## Revelation 4:7

The first creature was like a lion, the second like a calf, and the third had a human face. 📖

The fourth was like an eagle flying.

## Revelation 4:8

Each of the four creatures had six wings and was covered with eyes all round and within. 📖

They never stop saying this, day or night.

“Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty, who was, is, and is coming!”

## Revelation 4:9

The creatures give glory, honour, and thanks to the one seated on the throne, who lives for ever. 📖

## Revelation 4:10

Whenever they do, the twenty-four elders fall before the one seated on the throne. 📖

They worship him who lives for ever and ever.

They throw their crowns before the throne and say,

## Revelation 4:11

“Our Lord and God, you are worthy to receive glory, honour, and power. 📖

You created everything.

By your will, everything exists and was created.”
