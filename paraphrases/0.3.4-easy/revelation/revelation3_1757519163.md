# Revelation 3 — Messages to three churches

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives messages for Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.

## Revelation 3:1

“Write to the angel of the church in Sardis. 📖

These words come from the one holding God's seven spirits and the seven stars.

I know your deeds.

You have a name for being alive, yet you are dead.

## Revelation 3:2

Wake up and strengthen what remains, which is about to die. 📖

I have found your deeds incomplete before my God.

## Revelation 3:3

Remember what you received and heard. 📖

Keep it and repent.

If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief.

You will not know the hour when I come upon you.

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

## Revelation 3:4

A few people in Sardis have not dirtied their clothes. 📖

They will walk with me in white because they are worthy.

**Help:** The clothing pictures their spiritual condition and faithfulness.

## Revelation 3:5

Like them, the person who overcomes will wear white. 📖

I will never erase their name from the Book of Life.

I will acknowledge their name before my Father and his angels.

**Help:** Overcomes means remains faithful through opposition and temptation.

## Revelation 3:6

Anyone with an ear should hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖

## Revelation 3:7

Write to the angel of the church in Philadelphia. 📖

These words come from the holy and true one, who holds David's key.

No one can shut what he opens, or open what he shuts.

## Revelation 3:8

I know your deeds. 📖

I placed an open door before you that no one can shut.

You have little strength, yet you kept my word and did not deny my name.

## Revelation 3:9

Some belong to Satan's synagogue and claim to be Jews, but lie. 📖

I will make them come and bow at your feet.

They will know I love you.

**About these words:** A synagogue is a Jewish gathering and place of worship.

The judgment concerns the opponents described here, rather than every Jewish person or synagogue.

## Revelation 3:10

Because you kept my command to endure, I will keep you from the hour of testing. 📖

It is coming upon the whole world to test earth's inhabitants.

## Revelation 3:11

I am coming soon. 📖

Hold firmly to what you have, so no one takes your crown.

## Revelation 3:12

I will make the person who overcomes a pillar in my God's temple, never to leave it again. 📖

I will write my God's name on them, and the name of my God's city.

That city is the new Jerusalem, coming down from my God out of heaven.

I will also write my new name on them.

**Help:** A pillar supports a building.

The temple is a place where God lives.

These images express lasting belonging; their full interpretation needs further review.

## Revelation 3:13

Anyone with an ear should hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖

## Revelation 3:14

Write to the angel of the church in Laodicea. 📖

These words come from the Amen, the faithful and true Witness, the Originator of God's creation.

**Help:** Amen expresses truth and agreement; here it is a title for Christ.

Originator describes Christ's relationship to the beginning of God's creation.

## Revelation 3:15

I know your deeds: you are neither cold nor hot. 📖

I wish you were one or the other!

## Revelation 3:16

Because you are lukewarm, neither hot nor cold, I am about to vomit you out of my mouth! 📖

**Help:** Lukewarm means only slightly warm.

Jesus uses temperature and vomiting as forceful pictures of his judgment on this church.

## Revelation 3:17

You say, ‘I am rich, I have grown wealthy, and I need nothing.’ 📖

You do not realise you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind, and naked.

**Help:** Here these images expose the church's spiritual need, rather than judging people for poverty or disability.

## Revelation 3:18

I advise you to buy gold from me, refined by fire, so you can become rich. 📖

Buy white clothes so you are covered and your shameful nakedness is not exposed.

Buy ointment to put on your eyes so you can see.

## Revelation 3:19

I correct and discipline those I love. 📖

So be earnest and repent.

## Revelation 3:20

Look, I stand at the door and knock. 📖

If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will enter and eat with them, and they with me.

## Revelation 3:21

I will let the person who overcomes sit with me on my throne. 📖

I also overcame and sat with my Father on his throne.

## Revelation 3:22

Anyone with an ear should hear what the Spirit says to the churches.” 📖
