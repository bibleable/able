# Revelation 17 — Babylon and the beast

**About this chapter**

An angel explains the woman, beast, heads, horns, and waters in John's vision.

## Revelation 17:1

One of the seven angels with seven bowls said, “Come, I will show you the great prostitute's punishment. 📖

She sits on many waters.

**Help:** A prostitute is a person who has sex for payment.

Here the woman is an image of the city named later in this vision.

## Revelation 17:2

Earth's kings acted immorally with her, and earth's inhabitants became drunk on the wine of her immorality.” 📖

## Revelation 17:3

The angel carried me in the Spirit to a wilderness, where a woman sat on a scarlet beast. 📖

It was covered with blasphemous names and had seven heads and ten horns.

**Help:** Scarlet is bright red; blasphemous means insulting God or claiming what belongs to God.

## Revelation 17:4

She wore purple and scarlet, decorated with gold, precious stones, and pearls. 📖

She held a golden cup full of detestable things and the impurities of her sexual immorality.

## Revelation 17:5

A mysterious name was written on her forehead: BABYLON THE GREAT, MOTHER OF PROSTITUTES AND EARTH'S DETESTABLE THINGS. 📖

## Revelation 17:6

She was drunk with the blood of God's holy people and Jesus' witnesses. 📖

Seeing her utterly amazed me.

## Revelation 17:7

The angel asked, “Why are you so amazed? 📖

I will explain the mystery of the woman and the beast carrying her.

The beast has seven heads and ten horns.

## Revelation 17:8

The beast you saw once was, now is not, and will soon rise from the Abyss and go to destruction. 📖

Earth's inhabitants whose names were not written in the Book of Life from the world's foundation will marvel.

They will see the beast that was, is not, and yet will be.

**Help:** The Abyss is a deep pit associated with destructive spiritual powers.

## Revelation 17:9

This needs a wise mind. 📖

The seven heads are seven mountains where the woman sits.

## Revelation 17:10

There are also seven kings: five have fallen, one exists, and another has not yet come. 📖

When he comes, he must stay only briefly.

## Revelation 17:11

The beast that was and now is not is an eighth king, belonging to the seven and going to destruction. 📖

## Revelation 17:12

The ten horns are ten kings who have no kingdom yet. 📖

They will receive one hour's authority as kings with the beast.

## Revelation 17:13

They share one purpose: giving their power and authority to the beast. 📖

## Revelation 17:14

They will fight the Lamb, but he will defeat them because he is Lord of lords and King of kings. 📖

His called, chosen, faithful ones will be with him.”

## Revelation 17:15

The angel said, “The waters where the prostitute sat are peoples, crowds, nations, and languages. 📖

## Revelation 17:16

The ten horns and beast will hate the prostitute and leave her ruined and naked. 📖

They will eat her flesh and burn her with fire.

## Revelation 17:17

God put his purpose into their hearts: to unite and give their kingdom to the beast. 📖

They will do this until God's words are fulfilled.

## Revelation 17:18

The woman is the great city ruling over earth's kings.” 📖
