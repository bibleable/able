# Revelation 5 — The Lamb and the sealed scroll

**About this chapter**

John sees a sealed scroll and the Lamb who is worthy to open it.

He hears worship from heaven and every creature.

## Revelation 5:1

I saw a scroll in the right hand of the one seated on the throne. 📖

It had writing on both sides and seven seals keeping it closed.

**Help:** A scroll is a rolled sheet of writing.

Seals keep a document closed until someone authorised opens it.

## Revelation 5:2

A mighty angel called loudly, “Who is worthy to break the seals and open the scroll?” 📖

## Revelation 5:3

No one in heaven, on earth, or under the earth could open the scroll or look inside. 📖

## Revelation 5:4

I wept bitterly because no one was found worthy to open it or look inside. 📖

## Revelation 5:5

An elder told me, “Do not weep! 📖

The Lion of Judah's tribe, the Root of David, has won victory to open the scroll and its seven seals.”

**Help:** Lion of Judah and Root of David are titles pointing to Christ.

## Revelation 5:6

I saw a Lamb standing in the throne's centre, surrounded by the four living creatures and elders. 📖

He looked as though he had been killed.

He had seven horns and seven eyes, representing God's seven spirits sent throughout the earth.

**Help:** The Lamb represents Christ, killed and now living.

The full meaning of the horns and eyes needs further editorial review.

## Revelation 5:7

He came and took the scroll from the right hand of the one seated on the throne. 📖

## Revelation 5:8

When he took it, the four living creatures and twenty-four elders fell before the Lamb. 📖

Each had a harp, and they held golden bowls full of incense, which are God's people's prayers.

**Help:** A harp is a stringed musical instrument.

Incense gives a sweet smell when burned.

## Revelation 5:9

They sang a new song: “You are worthy to take the scroll and open its seals because you were killed. 📖

By your blood, you bought people for God from every tribe, language, people, and nation.

## Revelation 5:10

You made them a kingdom and priests serving our God, and they will reign on earth.” 📖

## Revelation 5:11

I looked and heard many angels surrounding the throne, the living creatures, and the elders. 📖

They numbered tens of thousands upon tens of thousands, and thousands upon thousands.

## Revelation 5:12

They called loudly, “The Lamb who was killed is worthy to receive power, riches, wisdom, strength, honour, glory, and blessing!” 📖

## Revelation 5:13

I heard every creature in heaven, on earth, under the earth, and in the sea, and everything in them. 📖

They said, “Praise, honour, glory, and power belong to the one on the throne and to the Lamb.

They belong to them for ever and ever!”

## Revelation 5:14

The four living creatures said, “Amen,” and the elders fell down and worshipped. 📖
