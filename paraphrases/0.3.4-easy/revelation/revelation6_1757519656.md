# Revelation 6 — Six seals opened

**About this chapter**

John watches the Lamb open six seals.

The visions include conquest, violence, hunger, death, killed witnesses, and the day of wrath.

## Revelation 6:1

I watched the Lamb open one of the seven seals. 📖

One of the four living creatures called like thunder, “Come!”

## Revelation 6:2

I saw a white horse whose rider held a bow. 📖

He received a crown and rode out to overcome and conquer.

## Revelation 6:3

When the Lamb opened the second seal, I heard the second creature say, “Come!” 📖

## Revelation 6:4

Another horse came out, bright red. 📖

Its rider was allowed to remove peace from the earth and make people kill one another.

He received a great sword.

## Revelation 6:5

When the Lamb opened the third seal, I heard the third creature say, “Come!” 📖

I saw a black horse whose rider held scales in his hand.

## Revelation 6:6

I heard what sounded like a voice among the four creatures. 📖

It said, “A quart of wheat for a denarius, and three quarts of barley for a denarius.

Do not harm the oil and wine.”

**Help:** A quart measures grain, roughly a litre here.

A denarius was a coin commonly worth a day's wages.

## Revelation 6:7

When the Lamb opened the fourth seal, I heard the fourth creature say, “Come!” 📖

## Revelation 6:8

I saw a pale green horse, with a rider named Death and Hades following close behind. 📖

They received authority over a fourth of the earth.

They could kill by sword, famine, plague, and earth's wild animals.

**Help:** Famine is severe lack of food; plague is deadly disease.

Hades refers to the realm of the dead.

## Revelation 6:9

When the Lamb opened the fifth seal, I saw souls beneath the altar. 📖

They had been killed for God's word and the testimony they upheld.

**Help:** An altar is a place for offering sacrifices.

## Revelation 6:10

They cried loudly, “Lord, holy and true, how long until you judge earth's inhabitants and avenge our blood?” 📖

**Help:** They ask God to bring justice for their deaths.

## Revelation 6:11

Each received a white robe and was told to rest a little longer. 📖

They must wait until the full number of their fellow servants, their brothers and sisters, were killed like them.

## Revelation 6:12

I watched the Lamb open the sixth seal, and a great earthquake came. 📖

The sun became black like sackcloth made from goat hair, and the whole moon turned blood red.

**Help:** Sackcloth is rough cloth often worn during mourning.

## Revelation 6:13

The sky's stars fell to earth like unripe figs falling from a tree shaken by a strong wind. 📖

## Revelation 6:14

The sky drew back like a scroll rolling up, and every mountain and island moved from its place. 📖

## Revelation 6:15

Earth's kings, nobles, commanders, rich people, powerful people, and every slave and free person hid. 📖

They hid in caves and among mountain rocks.

## Revelation 6:16

They told the mountains and rocks, “Fall on us! 📖

Hide us from the face of the one on the throne and from the Lamb's wrath.

## Revelation 6:17

The great day of their wrath has come, and who can stand against it?” 📖

**Help:** Wrath means strong anger against evil, expressed in judgment.
