# Revelation 10 — The small scroll

**About this chapter**

John sees a mighty angel, hears seven thunders, and is told to eat a small scroll.

## Revelation 10:1

Another mighty angel came down from heaven, wrapped in a cloud, with a rainbow above his head. 📖

His face was like the sun, and his legs like pillars of fire.

## Revelation 10:2

He held a small, open scroll in his hand. 📖

He put his right foot on the sea and his left on land.

## Revelation 10:3

He cried loudly like a roaring lion, and the seven thunders answered with their voices. 📖

## Revelation 10:4

I was about to write what the seven thunders said. 📖

But a heavenly voice said, “Seal up what the seven thunders said; do not write it down.”

## Revelation 10:5

The angel standing on sea and land raised his right hand to heaven. 📖

## Revelation 10:6

He swore by the one who lives for ever and ever. 📖

This one created heaven, earth, and sea, and everything in each of them.

The angel said, “There will be no more delay!

## Revelation 10:7

When the seventh angel begins sounding his trumpet, in those days God's mystery will be fulfilled. 📖

This is just as he announced to his servants the prophets.”

## Revelation 10:8

The heavenly voice spoke again: “Take the small scroll lying open in the angel's hand. 📖

He stands on sea and land.”

## Revelation 10:9

I went to the angel and said, “Give me the small scroll.” 📖

He said, “Take and eat it.

It will make your stomach bitter, but taste sweet as honey in your mouth.”

## Revelation 10:10

I took the scroll from his hand and ate it. 📖

It tasted sweet as honey, but after eating it, my stomach turned bitter.

## Revelation 10:11

They told me, “You must prophesy again about many peoples, nations, languages, and kings.” 📖

**Help:** Prophesy means speak a message from God.
