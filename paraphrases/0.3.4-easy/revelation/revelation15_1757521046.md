# Revelation 15 — The seven final plagues

**About this chapter**

John sees those victorious over the beast worshipping God, then angels receiving bowls of wrath.

## Revelation 15:1

Another great, wonderful sign appeared in heaven: seven angels with seven final plagues, completing God's wrath. 📖

## Revelation 15:2

I saw something like a glass sea mixed with fire. 📖

Beside it stood those who conquered the beast, its image, and its name's number.

They held harps from God.

## Revelation 15:3

They sang the song of God's servant Moses and the Lamb. 📖

“Lord God Almighty, your works are great and wonderful!

King of the nations, your ways are just and true!

## Revelation 15:4

Who will not fear you, Lord, and glorify your name? 📖

You alone are holy.

All nations will come and worship before you because your righteous acts have been revealed.”

## Revelation 15:5

After this, I saw the temple, the tabernacle of the Testimony, opened in heaven. 📖

**Help:** Tabernacle means a tent or dwelling place.

The title recalls God's presence and covenant testimony among Israel.

## Revelation 15:6

Seven angels with seven plagues came from the temple, wearing clean, bright linen and golden sashes round their chests. 📖

**Help:** Linen is a cloth; a sash is a long strip worn round the body.

## Revelation 15:7

One of the four living creatures gave them seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives forever. 📖

## Revelation 15:8

Smoke from God's glory and power filled the temple. 📖

No one could enter until the seven angels' seven plagues were finished.
