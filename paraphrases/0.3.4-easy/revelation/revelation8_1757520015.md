# Revelation 8 — The seventh seal and four trumpets

**About this chapter**

John sees incense offered with prayers, then judgments as four angels sound trumpets.

## Revelation 8:1

When the Lamb opened the seventh seal, heaven was silent for about half an hour. 📖

## Revelation 8:2

I saw the seven angels standing before God, and they received seven trumpets. 📖

## Revelation 8:3

Another angel came and stood at the altar with a golden censer. 📖

He received much incense to offer with all God's people's prayers on the golden altar before the throne.

**Help:** A censer is a container for burning incense, which gives a sweet smell.

An altar is a place for offerings.

## Revelation 8:4

Incense smoke and God's people's prayers rose before God from the angel's hand. 📖

## Revelation 8:5

The angel filled the censer with altar fire and threw it to earth. 📖

Thunder, rumblings, lightning flashes, and an earthquake followed.

## Revelation 8:6

The seven angels with the seven trumpets prepared to sound them. 📖

## Revelation 8:7

The first angel sounded his trumpet, and hail and fire mixed with blood were thrown onto earth. 📖

A third of the earth, a third of the trees, and all green grass burned up.

## Revelation 8:8

The second angel sounded his trumpet. 📖

Something like a great burning mountain was thrown into the sea, and a third of the sea became blood.

## Revelation 8:9

A third of the sea's living creatures died, and a third of the ships were destroyed. 📖

## Revelation 8:10

The third angel sounded his trumpet. 📖

A great star, burning like a torch, fell from heaven onto a third of the rivers and the water springs.

## Revelation 8:11

The star's name is Wormwood. 📖

A third of the water became bitter like wormwood oil, and many people died from it.

**Help:** Wormwood is a bitter plant.

## Revelation 8:12

The fourth angel sounded his trumpet. 📖

A third of the sun, moon, and stars were struck.

A third of the stars became dark.

A third of the day had no light, and a third of the night too.

## Revelation 8:13

As I watched, an eagle flying overhead called loudly, “Woe! 📖

Woe!

Woe to earth's inhabitants because of the trumpet blasts the remaining three angels will sound!”

**Help:** Woe announces terrible trouble and suffering.
