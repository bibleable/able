# Revelation 14 — The Lamb, warnings, and harvest

**About this chapter**

John sees the Lamb's followers, hears three angels, and watches two harvest visions.

## Revelation 14:1

I saw the Lamb standing on Mount Zion with 144,000 people. 📖

His name and his Father's name were written on their foreheads.

## Revelation 14:2

I heard a heavenly sound like roaring waters and loud thunder. 📖

It also sounded like harp players playing their harps.

## Revelation 14:3

They sang a new song before the throne, the four living creatures, and the elders. 📖

Only the 144,000 redeemed from earth could learn it.

**Help:** Redeemed means set free at a cost.

## Revelation 14:4

These are virgins who have not been defiled with women. 📖

They follow the Lamb wherever he goes.

They were redeemed from humanity as firstfruits for God and the Lamb.

**About these words:** Virgin means someone who has not had sexual relations.

Firstfruits means the first part of a harvest, here describing people belonging to God and the Lamb.

Whether the sexual description functions literally or symbolically needs independent review.

It should not be expanded into a claim that women themselves are morally impure.

## Revelation 14:5

No lie was found in their mouths; they are blameless. 📖

## Revelation 14:6

Another angel flew overhead with eternal good news for earth's inhabitants, every nation, tribe, language, and people. 📖

## Revelation 14:7

He called loudly, “Fear God and give him glory, because his judgment's hour has come. 📖

Worship the maker of heaven, earth, sea, and water springs.”

## Revelation 14:8

A second angel followed, saying, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! 📖

She made all nations drink the wine of the passion of her sexual immorality.”

**Help:** The vision pictures Babylon's corrupting influence as wine people drink.

## Revelation 14:9

A third angel followed, calling loudly, “Suppose someone worships the beast and its image and receives its mark. 📖

The mark is on their forehead or hand.

## Revelation 14:10

They too will drink God's anger like wine poured at full strength into his wrath's cup. 📖

They will be tormented with fire and sulphur before the holy angels and the Lamb.

**Help:** Wrath means strong anger against evil, expressed in judgment.

The undiluted wine pictures judgment at full strength.

## Revelation 14:11

The smoke of their torment rises for ever and ever. 📖

Those worshipping the beast and its image, or receiving its name's mark, have no rest day or night.”

## Revelation 14:12

This calls for the endurance of God's people, who keep his commands and the faith of Jesus. 📖

**About these words:** Faith of Jesus is retained here; its full meaning needs further editorial review.

## Revelation 14:13

A heavenly voice told me to write, “Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on.” 📖

“Yes,” says the Spirit, “they will rest from their work, because their deeds follow them.”

## Revelation 14:14

I saw a white cloud with someone like the Son of Man sitting on it. 📖

He wore a golden crown and held a sharp sickle.

**Help:** A sickle is a curved blade for cutting a harvest.

## Revelation 14:15

Another angel left the temple and called loudly to the one on the cloud. 📖

“Swing your sickle and harvest, because the time has come and earth's crop is ripe.”

## Revelation 14:16

The one on the cloud swung his sickle over earth, and earth was harvested. 📖

## Revelation 14:17

Another angel came from heaven's temple, also carrying a sharp sickle. 📖

## Revelation 14:18

An angel with authority over fire came from the altar and called to the angel holding the sharp sickle. 📖

He called loudly, “Swing your sharp sickle and gather earth's grape clusters, because its grapes are ripe.”

## Revelation 14:19

The angel swung his sickle over earth, gathered its grapes, and threw them into God's great winepress of wrath. 📖

**Help:** A winepress crushes grapes to make juice.

Here it is a severe image of judgment.

## Revelation 14:20

The winepress was trodden outside the city. 📖

Blood flowed from it as high as horses' bridles for 1,600 stadia.

**Help:** Bridles are straps fitted to horses' heads.

Stadia are ancient units of distance.
