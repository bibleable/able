# Revelation 16 — Seven bowls of wrath

**About this chapter**

John describes each bowl poured out, the judgments, and people's responses.

## Revelation 16:1

A loud temple voice told the seven angels, “Go and pour the seven bowls of God's wrath onto earth.” 📖

## Revelation 16:2

The first angel poured his bowl onto earth. 📖

Disgusting, harmful sores broke out on people who bore the beast's mark and worshipped its image.

## Revelation 16:3

The second poured his bowl into the sea, which became blood like a dead person's blood. 📖

Every living thing in the sea died.

## Revelation 16:4

The third poured his bowl into rivers and water springs, and they became blood. 📖

## Revelation 16:5

The waters' angel said, “Holy One, who is and was, you are righteous because you brought these judgments. 📖

## Revelation 16:6

They shed the blood of your holy people and prophets, so you gave them blood to drink. 📖

They deserve this.”

## Revelation 16:7

The altar replied, “Yes, Lord God Almighty, your judgments are true and just.” 📖

## Revelation 16:8

The fourth poured his bowl onto the sun, which received power to scorch people with fire. 📖

## Revelation 16:9

The intense heat scorched people, and they cursed God's name, though he controlled these plagues. 📖

They did not repent and give him glory.

**Help:** Repent means turn from sin towards God.

## Revelation 16:10

The fifth poured his bowl onto the beast's throne, and its kingdom became dark. 📖

People gnawed their tongues in agony.

## Revelation 16:11

They cursed heaven's God because of their pains and sores, but did not repent of their deeds. 📖

## Revelation 16:12

The sixth poured his bowl into the great Euphrates river. 📖

Its water dried up to prepare a way for the kings from the east.

## Revelation 16:13

Three unclean spirits like frogs came from the mouths of the dragon, beast, and false prophet. 📖

**Help:** Unclean here identifies evil spirits, rather than physical dirt or disability.

## Revelation 16:14

They are demonic spirits performing signs and gathering all earth's kings for battle on God Almighty's great day. 📖

## Revelation 16:15

“Look, I am coming like a thief. 📖

Blessed is the one who stays awake and clothed, so they do not go naked and expose their shame.”

**About the speaker:** This warning interrupts the vision's account of the gathered kings.

The draft keeps the warning separate rather than attributing it to the evil spirits.

## Revelation 16:16

They gathered the kings at the place called Armageddon in Hebrew. 📖

## Revelation 16:17

The seventh poured his bowl into the air. 📖

A loud voice from the temple's throne said, “It is done!”

## Revelation 16:18

Lightning, rumblings, thunder, and a great earthquake followed. 📖

No earthquake so mighty had happened since humans lived on earth.

## Revelation 16:19

The great city split into three parts, and the nations' cities collapsed. 📖

God remembered Babylon the great and gave her the cup of wine of his furious wrath.

## Revelation 16:20

Every island fled, and no mountain could be found. 📖

## Revelation 16:21

Great hailstones, each weighing almost a hundred pounds, fell on people from above. 📖

They cursed God because this hail plague was so terrible.
