# Numbers 20 — Water from the rock and Aaron’s death

**About this chapter**

Miriam dies at Kadesh.

The LORD provides water, then judges Moses and Aaron for their lack of trust.

Edom refuses passage, and Aaron dies on Mount Hor.

## Numbers 20:1

In month one, all Israel’s assembly entered Zin’s wilderness and stayed at Kadesh. 📖

Miriam died and was buried there.

## Numbers 20:2

There was no water for the assembly, so they gathered against Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Numbers 20:3

They quarreled with Moses, saying, “If only we had died with our brothers before the LORD! 📖

## Numbers 20:4

Why bring the LORD’s assembly into this wilderness, where we and our livestock will die? 📖

## Numbers 20:5

Why bring us from Egypt to this miserable place? 📖

There is no grain, figs, vines, or pomegranates, and no drinking water!”

## Numbers 20:6

Moses and Aaron left the assembly for the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

They fell facedown, and the LORD’s glory appeared to them.

## Numbers 20:7

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 20:8

“Take the staff and gather the assembly. 📖

You and your brother Aaron must speak to the rock while they watch.

It will pour out water.

You will bring out water for the assembly and their livestock to drink.”

## Numbers 20:9

Moses took the staff from the LORD’s presence as commanded. 📖

## Numbers 20:10

Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly before the rock. 📖

Moses said, “Listen, rebels!

Must we bring you water from this rock?”

## Numbers 20:11

Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff. 📖

Much water gushed out, and the assembly and their livestock drank.

## Numbers 20:12

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, “You did not trust Me to show My holiness before Israel. 📖

Therefore you will not bring this assembly into the land I gave them.”

## Numbers 20:13

These were Meribah’s waters, where Israel quarreled with the LORD and He showed His holiness among them. 📖

## Numbers 20:14

From Kadesh, Moses sent messengers to Edom’s king. 📖

They said, “Your brother Israel says: You know all our hardship.

## Numbers 20:15

Our fathers went to Egypt, where we lived many years. 📖

The Egyptians mistreated us and our fathers.

## Numbers 20:16

We cried to the LORD, and He heard, sent an angel, and brought us from Egypt. 📖

Now we are at Kadesh, a city at your border.

## Numbers 20:17

Please let us cross your land. 📖

We will enter no field or vineyard and drink from no well.

We will stay on the King’s Highway, turning neither right nor left until we pass through.”

## Numbers 20:18

Edom answered, “You may not cross our land, or we will confront you with swords.” 📖

## Numbers 20:19

Israel replied, “We will stay on the main road and pay if we or our herds drink your water. 📖

There will be no problem.

Just let us cross on foot.”

## Numbers 20:20

Edom insisted, “You may not pass.” 📖

They came against Israel with a large army and strong hand.

**Help with “strong hand”:** This pictures their force and power.

## Numbers 20:21

Edom refused Israel passage through their territory, so Israel turned away. 📖

## Numbers 20:22

All Israel’s assembly left Kadesh and came to Mount Hor. 📖

## Numbers 20:23

At Mount Hor near Edom’s border, the LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 20:24

“Aaron will be gathered to his people and not enter the land I gave Israel. 📖

You both rebelled against My command at Meribah’s waters.

**Help with “gathered to his people”:** This is an expression used here for death.

## Numbers 20:25

Take Aaron and his son Eleazar up Mount Hor. 📖

## Numbers 20:26

Remove Aaron’s priestly clothes and put them on his son Eleazar. 📖

Aaron will be gathered to his people and die there.”

## Numbers 20:27

Moses obeyed the LORD, and they climbed Mount Hor before the whole assembly. 📖

## Numbers 20:28

Moses removed Aaron’s clothes and put them on his son Eleazar. 📖

Aaron died there on the mountaintop.

Moses and Eleazar came down.

## Numbers 20:29

All the assembly saw Aaron had died, and all Israel mourned him thirty days. 📖
