# Numbers 11 — Complaints, the Spirit, and quail

**About this chapter**

The people complain, and Moses pleads about his burden.

The LORD shares the Spirit with elders and sends quail.

The chapter includes Moses’ death request and a severe plague.

## Numbers 11:1

The people complained of hardship where the LORD heard. 📖

His anger burned, and His fire consumed the camp’s outskirts.

## Numbers 11:2

They cried to Moses. 📖

He prayed to the LORD, and the fire died down.

## Numbers 11:3

The place was called Taberah because the LORD’s fire burned among them. 📖

## Numbers 11:4

The rabble craved other food, and Israel wept again. 📖

They said, “Who will feed us meat?

**Help with “rabble”:** This describes a disorderly crowd among the people.

## Numbers 11:5

We remember fish eaten freely in Egypt, cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic. 📖

## Numbers 11:6

Now our appetite is gone. 📖

We see nothing but manna!”

## Numbers 11:7

Manna resembled coriander seed and looked like gum resin. 📖

**Help with these words:** Coriander is a plant with seeds used to flavor food.

Gum resin is a substance from plants.

## Numbers 11:8

They walked about gathering manna. 📖

They ground it on a handmill or crushed it in a mortar.

They boiled it in a cooking pot or shaped it into cakes.

It tasted like pastry baked with fine oil.

**Help with these tools:** A handmill grinds by hand.

A mortar is a bowl used for crushing.

## Numbers 11:9

Manna fell with the dew at night on camp. 📖

## Numbers 11:10

Moses heard families weeping at their tent entrances. 📖

The LORD was very angry, and Moses was displeased.

## Numbers 11:11

Moses asked the LORD, “Why bring this trouble on Your servant? 📖

Why have I not found favor, so You put all these people’s burden on me?

## Numbers 11:12

Did I conceive or give birth to them? 📖

Why tell me, ‘Carry them against your chest like a nurse carries an infant’?

Must I bring them to the land You swore to their fathers?

**Help with this picture:** Moses compares caring for the people to carrying a baby.

## Numbers 11:13

Where can I get meat for all of them? 📖

They keep crying, ‘Give us meat to eat!’

## Numbers 11:14

I cannot carry them alone. 📖

The burden is too heavy.

## Numbers 11:15

If You will treat me this way, kill me now if I found Your favor. 📖

Do not let me see my own misery.”

## Numbers 11:16

The LORD said, “Bring seventy elders you know as Israel’s leaders and officers to the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Have them stand with you.

## Numbers 11:17

I will come down and speak there. 📖

I will take some of the Spirit on you and put it on them.

They will share the burden so you do not carry it alone.

## Numbers 11:18

Tell the people to set themselves apart for tomorrow’s meat. 📖

They cried before the LORD, ‘Who will feed us meat?

Egypt was better!’

The LORD will give you meat to eat.

## Numbers 11:19

You will eat it not one or two days, nor five, ten, or twenty days, 📖

## Numbers 11:20

but a whole month, until it comes from your nostrils and makes you sick. 📖

You rejected the LORD among you, crying, ‘Why did we leave Egypt?’”

**Help with this picture:** Nostrils are the nose’s openings.

The image describes being overwhelmed and sickened by the meat.

## Numbers 11:21

Moses replied, “I stand among 600,000 men on foot. 📖

Yet You promise them meat for a month.

## Numbers 11:22

Would all our slaughtered flocks and herds be enough? 📖

Would all the sea’s fish be enough?”

## Numbers 11:23

The LORD answered, “Is the LORD’s arm too short? 📖

You will see whether My word comes true.”

**Help with this image:** God’s arm pictures His power to act.

## Numbers 11:24

Moses told the people the LORD’s words and gathered seventy elders around the tent. 📖

## Numbers 11:25

The LORD descended in the cloud and spoke to Moses. 📖

He took some of the Spirit on Moses and put it on the seventy elders.

They prophesied when the Spirit rested on them, but never did so again.

## Numbers 11:26

Eldad and Medad stayed in camp. 📖

They were listed but had not gone to the tent.

The Spirit rested on them, and they prophesied in camp.

## Numbers 11:27

A young man ran to tell Moses, “Eldad and Medad are prophesying in camp.” 📖

## Numbers 11:28

Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ attendant since youth, said, “Moses, my lord, stop them!” 📖

## Numbers 11:29

Moses replied, “Are you jealous for me? 📖

I wish all the LORD’s people were prophets and He put His Spirit on them!”

## Numbers 11:30

Moses and Israel’s elders returned to camp. 📖

## Numbers 11:31

A wind from the LORD brought quail from the sea near camp. 📖

They were about two cubits above ground.

They extended a day’s journey in every direction around camp.

**Help with “cubit”:** This is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Numbers 11:32

They gathered quail all that day, night, and next day. 📖

None gathered less than ten homers, and they spread them around camp.

**Help with “homer”:** This is a measure of dry goods.

## Numbers 11:33

While meat was between their teeth, before chewing, the LORD’s anger burned. 📖

He struck them with a severe plague.

## Numbers 11:34

They named it Kibroth-hattaavah because they buried those craving other food there. 📖

## Numbers 11:35

They left Kibroth-hattaavah for Hazeroth and stayed some time. 📖
