# Numbers 12 — Miriam and Aaron speak against Moses

**About this chapter**

Miriam and Aaron criticize Moses.

The LORD speaks about His relationship with Moses.

Miriam develops the skin condition described in BSB, and Moses pleads for her healing.

## Numbers 12:1

Miriam and Aaron criticized Moses because he married a Cushite woman. 📖

He had taken a Cushite wife.

**About the wife:** Her name is not given here.

## Numbers 12:2

They asked, “Does the LORD speak only through Moses? 📖

Does He not speak through us?”

The LORD heard.

## Numbers 12:3

Moses was very humble, more than anyone on earth. 📖

## Numbers 12:4

The LORD suddenly told Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, “You three, come out to the Tent of Meeting.” 📖

All three went out,

## Numbers 12:5

and the LORD descended in a cloud pillar and stood at the Tent’s entrance. 📖

He called Aaron and Miriam, and both stepped forward.

## Numbers 12:6

He said, “Hear My words. 📖

If there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, reveal Myself in a vision and speak in a dream.

## Numbers 12:7

Not so with My servant Moses. 📖

He is faithful in all My house.

## Numbers 12:8

I speak to him face to face, clearly, without riddles. 📖

He sees the LORD’s form.

Why were you unafraid to speak against My servant Moses?”

## Numbers 12:9

The LORD’s anger burned against them, and He left. 📖

## Numbers 12:10

The cloud lifted from above the Tent. 📖

Suddenly Miriam became leprous, white as snow.

Aaron turned and saw her condition,

**Help with “leprous”:** This is BSB’s term for the skin condition in this account.

No modern medical diagnosis is given.

## Numbers 12:11

and told Moses, “My lord, do not hold our foolish sin against us. 📖

## Numbers 12:12

Please do not let her be like a stillborn infant coming from its mother’s womb. 📖

Half its flesh is consumed.”

## Numbers 12:13

Moses cried to the LORD, “O God, please heal her!” 📖

## Numbers 12:14

The LORD replied, “If her father spit in her face, would she not be disgraced seven days? 📖

Confine her outside camp seven days.

Then she may return.”

## Numbers 12:15

Miriam stayed outside camp seven days. 📖

The people did not leave until she returned.

## Numbers 12:16

Then they left Hazeroth and camped in Paran’s wilderness. 📖
