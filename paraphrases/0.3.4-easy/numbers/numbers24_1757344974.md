# Numbers 24 — Balaam’s further prophecies

**About this chapter**

Balaam blesses Israel again, and Balak becomes angry.

Balaam speaks further prophetic messages about Israel and other peoples.

The images and violent judgments are retained without identifying unnamed future figures.

## Numbers 24:1

Balaam saw that blessing Israel pleased the LORD. 📖

Unlike before, he did not seek omens but faced the wilderness.

**Help with “omens”:** These are signs someone uses to predict events.

## Numbers 24:2

He looked up and saw Israel camped tribe by tribe. 📖

God’s Spirit came upon him,

## Numbers 24:3

and he spoke an oracle: “This is Balaam son of Beor’s prophecy, from a man with open eyes, 📖

**Help with these words:** An oracle is a spoken prophetic message.

A prophecy is a message about what God reveals.

## Numbers 24:4

one hearing God’s words, seeing a vision from the Almighty, bowing down with eyes wide open: 📖

## Numbers 24:5

How lovely your tents are, Jacob, and your homes, Israel! 📖

## Numbers 24:6

They spread like palm groves, gardens beside a stream, aloes the LORD planted, and cedars beside water. 📖

**Help with these plants:** Aloes and cedars are plants named in this picture.

## Numbers 24:7

Water will flow from his buckets, and his seed will have plentiful water. 📖

His king will be greater than Agag, and his kingdom will rise to greatness.

**About “seed”:** BSB uses this word here.

The water and seed picture needs further editorial review.

## Numbers 24:8

God brought him from Egypt with strength like a wild ox. 📖

He will devour enemy nations, crush their bones, and pierce them with arrows.

## Numbers 24:9

He crouches and lies like a lion or lioness. 📖

Who dares wake him?

Blessed are those blessing you, and cursed are those cursing you.”

**About this picture:** Israel is compared to a resting lion.

## Numbers 24:10

Balak became angry, struck his hands together, and told Balaam, “I summoned you to curse my enemies. 📖

You kept blessing them these three times!

## Numbers 24:11

Flee home now! 📖

I promised rich rewards, but the LORD kept them from you.”

## Numbers 24:12

Balaam answered Balak, “Did I not already tell your messengers this? 📖

## Numbers 24:13

Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the LORD’s command. 📖

I cannot act on my own, good or bad.

I must speak whatever the LORD says.

## Numbers 24:14

I return to my people now. 📖

But come, let me warn you what these people will do to yours in days to come.”

## Numbers 24:15

Balaam spoke an oracle: “This is Balaam son of Beor’s prophecy, from a man with open eyes, 📖

## Numbers 24:16

one hearing God’s words, knowing what the Most High reveals, seeing the Almighty’s vision, bowing down with eyes wide open: 📖

## Numbers 24:17

I see him, but not now or near. 📖

A star will come from Jacob, and a scepter will rise from Israel.

He will crush Moab’s skulls and strike down all Sheth’s sons.

**Help with “scepter”:** This is a ruler’s rod of authority.

The message uses star and scepter imagery without naming the figure here.

## Numbers 24:18

Edom will become a possession, and so will Seir, his enemy. 📖

Israel will act with courage.

## Numbers 24:19

A ruler will come from Jacob and destroy the city’s survivors.” 📖

## Numbers 24:20

Balaam saw Amalek and spoke an oracle: “Amalek was first among the nations, but his end is destruction.” 📖

## Numbers 24:21

He saw the Kenites and spoke an oracle: “Your home is secure, your nest set in a cliff. 📖

## Numbers 24:22

Yet Kain will be destroyed when Asshur takes you captive.” 📖

## Numbers 24:23

He spoke another oracle: “Ah, who can live unless God has ordered it? 📖

## Numbers 24:24

Ships will come from Cyprus’s coasts and subdue Asshur and Eber. 📖

But they too will perish forever.”

**Help with “subdue”:** This means bring under control by force.

## Numbers 24:25

Balaam rose and returned to his homeland. 📖

Balak also went on his way.
