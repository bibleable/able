# Numbers 18 — Priests, Levites, and their portions

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks about the duties and responsibility of priests and Levites.

He gives rules about their share of offerings and tithes.

The warnings about guilt and death remain in this chapter.

## Numbers 18:1

The LORD told Aaron, “You, your sons, and your father’s house bear guilt involving the sanctuary. 📖

You and your sons alone bear guilt involving your priesthood.

## Numbers 18:2

Bring your fellow Levites, your father’s tribe. 📖

They must join and help you and your sons before the Tent of the Testimony.

## Numbers 18:3

They must attend to your duties and all the tent’s duties. 📖

But they must not approach the sanctuary’s furnishings or altar, or both they and you will die.

## Numbers 18:4

They must join you in all the Tent of Meeting’s duties and work. 📖

No outsider may approach you.

## Numbers 18:5

You must attend to sanctuary and altar duties so wrath does not fall on Israel again. 📖

## Numbers 18:6

I Myself chose your fellow Levites from Israel as a gift to you. 📖

They are dedicated to the LORD for the Tent of Meeting’s service.

## Numbers 18:7

Only you and your sons must perform priestly service concerning the altar and everything inside the veil. 📖

I give you this work as a gift.

Any outsider approaching the sanctuary must be put to death.”

**Help with “veil”:** This is the curtain inside the sanctuary.

## Numbers 18:8

The LORD told Aaron, “I put you in charge of My offerings. 📖

I give Israel’s sacred offerings to you and your sons as your portion under a permanent rule.

## Numbers 18:9

The portion of most holy offerings kept from the fire belongs to you and your sons. 📖

This includes the most holy portions of grain, sin, and guilt offerings they bring Me.

## Numbers 18:10

Eat it as a most holy offering. 📖

Every male may eat it.

Treat it as holy.

## Numbers 18:11

Their gifts and all Israel’s wave offerings also belong to you. 📖

I give them to you, your sons, and your daughters under a permanent rule.

Every ceremonially clean person in your household may eat them.

**Help with “ceremonially clean”:** This means meeting the requirements of worship rules.

A wave offering is presented before the LORD.

## Numbers 18:12

I give you all the freshest olive oil and finest new wine and grain Israel gives the LORD as firstfruits. 📖

**Help with “firstfruits”:** These are the first produce given from a harvest.

## Numbers 18:13

The firstfruits of everything in their land brought to the LORD belong to you. 📖

Every ceremonially clean person in your household may eat them.

## Numbers 18:14

Every devoted thing in Israel belongs to you. 📖

**Help with “devoted”:** This means set apart for the LORD.

## Numbers 18:15

Every firstborn from the womb, human or animal, offered to the LORD belongs to you. 📖

But you must redeem every firstborn son and firstborn male of unclean animals.

**Help with “redeem”:** This means paying the required price to release them from the offering obligation.

## Numbers 18:16

For a month-old male, pay your valued redemption price: five silver shekels by the sanctuary standard. 📖

Each shekel is twenty gerahs.

**Help with these measures:** Shekels and gerahs are measures of weight.

## Numbers 18:17

Do not redeem firstborn oxen, sheep, or goats. 📖

They are holy.

Splatter their blood on the altar and burn their fat as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

## Numbers 18:18

Their meat belongs to you, like the wave offering’s breast and right thigh. 📖

## Numbers 18:19

I give you, your sons, and your daughters all the holy offerings Israel presents to the LORD. 📖

This is a permanent rule.

It is a permanent covenant of salt before the LORD for you and your descendants.”

**Help with “covenant of salt”:** This expression describes a lasting covenant, or solemn agreement.

## Numbers 18:20

The LORD told Aaron, “You will have no inheritance in their land or portion among them. 📖

I am your portion and inheritance among Israel.

## Numbers 18:21

I give the Levites all Israel’s tithes as their inheritance in return for their Tent of Meeting service. 📖

**Help with “tithe”:** This means one-tenth.

## Numbers 18:22

Israel must no longer approach the Tent of Meeting, or they will become guilty and die. 📖

## Numbers 18:23

The Levites must do the Tent of Meeting’s work and bear their guilt. 📖

This rule is permanent through coming generations.

They will receive no inheritance among Israel.

## Numbers 18:24

I give them as their inheritance the tithe Israel presents to the LORD as a contribution. 📖

That is why I said they would receive no inheritance among Israel.”

## Numbers 18:25

The LORD instructed Moses, 📖

## Numbers 18:26

“Tell the Levites: When you receive Israel’s tithe as the inheritance I gave you, present an offering to the LORD. 📖

Give a tithe of that tithe.

## Numbers 18:27

Your offering will count as grain from the threshing floor or juice from the winepress. 📖

**Help with these places:** A threshing floor is where grain is separated from stalks.

A winepress is where grapes are squeezed for juice.

## Numbers 18:28

Present the LORD’s offering from all the tithes you receive from Israel. 📖

Give this offering to Aaron the priest.

## Numbers 18:29

From every gift’s best part, its holiest part, present the offering due the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 18:30

Tell the Levites: After presenting the best part, it will count as produce from the threshing floor or winepress. 📖

## Numbers 18:31

You and your households may eat the rest anywhere. 📖

It is payment for your work at the Tent of Meeting.

## Numbers 18:32

After presenting its best part, you will not become guilty because of it. 📖

But do not defile Israel’s sacred offerings, or you will die.”
