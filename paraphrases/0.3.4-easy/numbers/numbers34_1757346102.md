# Numbers 34 — Canaan’s boundaries and appointed leaders

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives the land’s boundaries and names the leaders who will distribute it.

Place names and directions are retained.

These descriptions remain text, without claiming a verified modern map.

## Numbers 34:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 34:2

“Tell Israel: When you enter Canaan, it will be your inheritance within these boundaries: 📖

## Numbers 34:3

The southern border extends from Zin’s wilderness along Edom. 📖

At the east, it begins at the Salt Sea’s end,

## Numbers 34:4

crosses south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, continues to Zin, and goes south of Kadesh-barnea. 📖

It goes to Hazar-addar and Azmon,

## Numbers 34:5

where it turns from Azmon to the Brook of Egypt and ends at the Sea. 📖

## Numbers 34:6

The Great Sea’s coastline is your western border. 📖

## Numbers 34:7

The northern border runs directly from the Great Sea to Mount Hor, 📖

## Numbers 34:8

from Mount Hor to Lebo-hamath, then Zedad, 📖

## Numbers 34:9

continues to Ziphron, and ends at Hazar-enan. 📖

This is your northern boundary.

## Numbers 34:10

The eastern border runs straight from Hazar-enan to Shepham, 📖

## Numbers 34:11

descends from Shepham to Riblah east of Ain, and follows the slopes east of the Sea of Chinnereth. 📖

## Numbers 34:12

Then it descends along the Jordan and ends at the Salt Sea. 📖

This is your land within its borders on all sides.”

## Numbers 34:13

Moses commanded Israel, “Divide this land by lot as an inheritance. 📖

The LORD commanded it be given to nine and a half tribes.

**Help with “lot”:** This means using lots to make an allocation, rather than a person choosing.

## Numbers 34:14

Reuben’s and Gad’s tribes and Manasseh’s half-tribe already received their inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 34:15

These two and a half tribes inherited across the Jordan from Jericho, toward sunrise.” 📖

## Numbers 34:16

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 34:17

“These men will assign your inherited land: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun. 📖

## Numbers 34:18

Appoint one leader per tribe to distribute it. 📖

## Numbers 34:19

Their names are Caleb son of Jephunneh from Judah; 📖

## Numbers 34:20

Shemuel son of Ammihud from Simeon; 📖

## Numbers 34:21

Elidad son of Chislon from Benjamin; 📖

## Numbers 34:22

Bukki son of Jogli, a leader from Dan; 📖

## Numbers 34:23

Hanniel son of Ephod, a leader from Manasseh son of Joseph; 📖

## Numbers 34:24

Kemuel son of Shiphtan, a leader from Ephraim; 📖

## Numbers 34:25

Eli-zaphan son of Parnach, a leader from Zebulun; 📖

## Numbers 34:26

Paltiel son of Azzan, a leader from Issachar; 📖

## Numbers 34:27

Ahihud son of Shelomi, a leader from Asher; 📖

## Numbers 34:28

and Pedahel son of Ammihud, a leader from Naphtali.” 📖

## Numbers 34:29

These are the men the LORD commanded to distribute Israel’s inheritance in Canaan. 📖
