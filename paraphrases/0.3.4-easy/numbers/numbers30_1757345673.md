# Numbers 30 — Vows and pledges

**About this chapter**

Moses gives the tribal heads the LORD’s instructions about vows.

The rules distinguish men, young women in their fathers’ homes, wives, widows, and divorced women.

The timing of a father’s or husband’s response matters.

## Numbers 30:1

Moses told Israel’s tribal heads, “This is what the LORD commanded: 📖

## Numbers 30:2

If a man vows to the LORD or makes a binding sworn pledge, he must keep his word. 📖

He must do everything he promised.

**Help with these words:** A vow is a solemn promise.

A pledge binds someone to do something.

## Numbers 30:3

If a young woman living in her father’s house vows to the LORD or makes a binding pledge, 📖

## Numbers 30:4

and her father hears but says nothing, all her vows and pledges remain binding. 📖

## Numbers 30:5

But if he forbids her on the day he hears, none of her vows or pledges stand. 📖

The LORD will release her because her father forbade her.

## Numbers 30:6

If a woman marries while bound by a vow or rash promise, 📖

**Help with “rash”:** This means made too quickly, without careful thought.

## Numbers 30:7

and her husband hears but says nothing that day, her vows and pledges stand. 📖

## Numbers 30:8

But if he forbids her when he hears, he cancels her binding vow or rash promise. 📖

The LORD will release her.

## Numbers 30:9

Every vow a widow or divorced woman pledges to fulfill binds her. 📖

## Numbers 30:10

If a woman in her husband’s house vows or makes a binding sworn pledge, 📖

## Numbers 30:11

and he hears but says nothing and does not forbid her, all her vows and pledges stand. 📖

## Numbers 30:12

But if he cancels them on the day he hears, none of her spoken vows or pledges stand. 📖

Her husband canceled them, and the LORD will release her.

## Numbers 30:13

Her husband may confirm or cancel any vow or sworn pledge to deny herself. 📖

## Numbers 30:14

If he says nothing day after day, he confirms all her binding vows and pledges. 📖

He confirmed them by saying nothing on the day he heard.

## Numbers 30:15

But if he cancels them after hearing them, he will bear her guilt.” 📖

## Numbers 30:16

These are the LORD’s rules given to Moses about a man and his wife. 📖

They also concern a father and a young daughter still in his home.
