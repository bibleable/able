# Numbers 17 — Aaron’s staff sprouts

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives a sign through the tribal leaders’ staffs.

Aaron’s staff produces buds, blossoms, and almonds.

The people express fear about approaching the tabernacle.

## Numbers 17:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 17:2

“Tell Israel to give you twelve staffs, one from each tribal leader. 📖

Write each man’s name on his staff.

**Help with “staff”:** This is a long wooden stick.

## Numbers 17:3

Write Aaron’s name on Levi’s staff. 📖

There must be one staff for each tribal head.

## Numbers 17:4

Put them in the Tent of Meeting before the Testimony, where I meet you. 📖

**Help with “Testimony”:** This is God’s covenant witness given to Israel.

## Numbers 17:5

My chosen man’s staff will sprout. 📖

I will rid Myself of Israel’s constant complaints against you.”

## Numbers 17:6

Moses told Israel, and each tribal leader gave a staff, twelve altogether. 📖

Aaron’s was among them.

## Numbers 17:7

Moses put them before the LORD in the Tent of the Testimony. 📖

## Numbers 17:8

Next day, Moses entered the Tent of the Testimony. 📖

Aaron’s staff, representing Levi’s house, had sprouted, budded, blossomed, and produced almonds.

## Numbers 17:9

Moses brought all the staffs from the LORD’s presence to all Israel. 📖

They saw them, and each man took his own.

## Numbers 17:10

The LORD told Moses, “Put Aaron’s staff back before the Testimony as a sign for the rebellious. 📖

End their complaints against Me so they do not die.”

## Numbers 17:11

Moses did as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Numbers 17:12

Israel told Moses, “Look, we are perishing! 📖

We are lost, all lost!

## Numbers 17:13

Anyone approaching the LORD’s tabernacle will die. 📖

Are we all going to perish?”

**About these words:** These are the people’s fearful words to Moses.
