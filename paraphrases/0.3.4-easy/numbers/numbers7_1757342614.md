# Numbers 7 — The leaders’ dedication offerings

**About this chapter**

Israel’s leaders bring carts, oxen, and offerings for the altar.

Each leader’s offering is recorded separately.

The chapter ends with Moses hearing the LORD speak.

## Numbers 7:1

On the day Moses finished setting up the tabernacle, he anointed and consecrated it and all its furnishings. 📖

He also anointed and consecrated the altar and all its tools.

## Numbers 7:2

Israel’s tribal leaders and family heads brought an offering. 📖

They were the men who supervised the registration.

## Numbers 7:3

They brought six covered carts and twelve oxen before the LORD and the tabernacle. 📖

Each leader gave an ox, and every two leaders gave a cart.

## Numbers 7:4

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 7:5

“Accept these for the Tent of Meeting’s work. 📖

Give them to the Levites according to each man’s service.”

## Numbers 7:6

Moses gave the carts and oxen to the Levites. 📖

## Numbers 7:7

The Gershonites received two carts and four oxen for their duties, 📖

## Numbers 7:8

and the Merarites received four carts and eight oxen for theirs, under Ithamar, Aaron the priest’s son. 📖

## Numbers 7:9

The Kohathites received none, because they must carry their holy objects on their shoulders. 📖

## Numbers 7:10

When the altar was anointed, the leaders brought its dedication offerings before it. 📖

## Numbers 7:11

The LORD told Moses, “Each day, one leader must present his altar-dedication offering.” 📖

## Numbers 7:12

On day one, Nahshon son of Amminadab from Judah brought his offering. 📖

## Numbers 7:13

One silver platter weighed 130 sanctuary shekels. 📖

One silver bowl weighed 70 sanctuary shekels.

Both held fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering.

**Help with “shekels”:** These are units of weight.

## Numbers 7:14

One gold dish weighing ten shekels held incense; 📖

## Numbers 7:15

For a burnt offering, one young bull, one ram, and one year-old male lamb; 📖

## Numbers 7:16

One male goat for a sin offering; 📖

## Numbers 7:17

For a peace offering, two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five year-old male lambs. 📖

This was Nahshon son of Amminadab’s offering.

## Numbers 7:84

The leaders’ altar-dedication offerings when it was anointed were twelve silver platters, twelve silver bowls, and twelve gold dishes. 📖

## Numbers 7:85

Each silver platter weighed 130 shekels, each bowl 70. 📖

The total silver weighed 2,400 sanctuary shekels.

## Numbers 7:86

The twelve incense-filled gold dishes weighed ten sanctuary shekels each. 📖

Their total gold weight was 120 shekels.

## Numbers 7:87

The burnt offerings totaled twelve bulls, twelve rams, and twelve year-old male lambs, with grain offerings. 📖

There were twelve male goats for sin.

## Numbers 7:88

The peace offerings totaled twenty-four bulls, sixty rams, sixty male goats, and sixty year-old male lambs. 📖

These dedicated the altar after anointing.

## Numbers 7:89

Moses entered the Tent of Meeting to speak with the LORD. 📖

He heard the voice between the two cherubim above the mercy seat on the ark of the Testimony.

The LORD spoke to him.
