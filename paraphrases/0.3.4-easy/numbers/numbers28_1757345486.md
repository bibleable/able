# Numbers 28 — Daily, Sabbath, monthly, and feast offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD sets out offerings for daily worship, Sabbaths, new months, Passover, and the Feast of Weeks.

Each amount and each additional offering is included.

An ephah measures dry goods, and a hin measures liquids.

## Numbers 28:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 28:2

“Command Israel to bring Me the food for My food offerings at its appointed time, as a pleasing aroma. 📖

## Numbers 28:3

Tell them: The regular daily burnt offering to the LORD is two year-old male lambs without blemish. 📖

**Help with “burnt offering”:** This is an offering burned on the altar.

An animal without blemish has no defect.

## Numbers 28:4

Offer one in the morning and the other at twilight, 📖

## Numbers 28:5

with a grain offering of one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour. 📖

Mix it with a quarter hin of pressed olive oil.

## Numbers 28:6

This regular burnt offering was established at Mount Sinai as a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 28:7

Give a quarter hin as a drink offering for each lamb. 📖

Pour the fermented drink offering to the LORD in the sanctuary area.

**Help with “fermented”:** This means changed by a process that produces alcohol.

## Numbers 28:8

Offer the second lamb at twilight with the same grain and drink offerings as in the morning. 📖

It is a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

## Numbers 28:9

On the Sabbath, offer two year-old male lambs without blemish. 📖

Add two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as grain offering, and a drink offering.

**Help with “Sabbath”:** This is the weekly day set apart for rest.

## Numbers 28:10

This is each Sabbath’s burnt offering, besides the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:11

At each month’s beginning, present the LORD a burnt offering. 📖

Offer two young bulls, one ram, and seven year-old male lambs, all without blemish.

## Numbers 28:12

For each bull, add three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as grain offering. 📖

For the ram, add two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil.

## Numbers 28:13

For each lamb, add one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as grain offering. 📖

This burnt offering is a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.

## Numbers 28:14

Add wine as drink offerings: half a hin per bull, a third per ram, a quarter per lamb. 📖

This is the monthly burnt offering at each new moon throughout the year.

## Numbers 28:15

Offer the LORD one male goat for sin, besides the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:16

Day fourteen of month one is the LORD’s Passover. 📖

## Numbers 28:17

Hold a feast on day fifteen of this month. 📖

Eat unleavened bread seven days.

**Help with “unleavened”:** This means made without yeast.

## Numbers 28:18

On the first day, hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work. 📖

## Numbers 28:19

Present the LORD a food offering: two young bulls, one ram, and seven year-old male lambs as burnt offerings. 📖

All must be without blemish.

## Numbers 28:20

For grain offerings, give fine flour mixed with oil. 📖

Give three-tenths of an ephah per bull and two-tenths with the ram,

## Numbers 28:21

and one-tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs. 📖

## Numbers 28:22

Include one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you. 📖

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God through the appointed offering.

## Numbers 28:23

Offer these besides the regular morning burnt offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:24

Offer the same food each day for seven days, a food offering and pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

Add its drink offering and the regular burnt offering.

## Numbers 28:25

On day seven, hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work. 📖

## Numbers 28:26

On firstfruits day during the Feast of Weeks, you present new grain to the LORD. 📖

Hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work.

**Help with “firstfruits”:** These are the first produce given from a harvest.

## Numbers 28:27

Present a burnt offering as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

Offer two young bulls, one ram, and seven year-old male lambs,

## Numbers 28:28

with grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil. 📖

Give three-tenths of an ephah per bull and two-tenths with the ram,

## Numbers 28:29

and one-tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs. 📖

## Numbers 28:30

Include one male goat to make atonement for you. 📖

## Numbers 28:31

The animals must be without blemish. 📖

Offer them with their drink offerings, besides the regular burnt offering and its grain offering.”
