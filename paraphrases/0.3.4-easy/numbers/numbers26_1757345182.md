# Numbers 26 — The census after the plague

**About this chapter**

Moses and Eleazar count Israel after the plague.

The clans, names, counts, and inheritance rules are included.

The Levites have a separate count.

## Numbers 26:1

After the plague ended, the LORD told Moses and Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, 📖

## Numbers 26:2

“Count all Israel’s assembly by their fathers’ houses. 📖

Count everyone twenty or older able to serve in Israel’s army.”

## Numbers 26:3

On Moab’s plains by the Jordan, across from Jericho, Moses and Eleazar the priest gave this instruction: 📖

## Numbers 26:4

“Count the men twenty or older, as the LORD commanded Moses.” 📖

These were the Israelites who came from Egypt:

## Numbers 26:5

Reuben was Israel’s firstborn. 📖

His descendants included the Hanochite clan from Hanoch and Palluite clan from Pallu,

**Help with “clan”:** This means a large family group.

## Numbers 26:6

the Hezronite clan from Hezron and Carmite clan from Carmi. 📖

## Numbers 26:7

Reuben’s clans numbered 43,730. 📖

## Numbers 26:8

Pallu’s son was Eliab, 📖

## Numbers 26:9

Eliab’s sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. 📖

The assembly chose Dathan and Abiram, who rebelled against Moses and Aaron with Korah’s followers.

These followers rebelled against the LORD.

## Numbers 26:10

The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them with Korah. 📖

His followers died when fire consumed 250 men.

They serve as a warning sign.

## Numbers 26:11

But Korah’s line did not die out. 📖

## Numbers 26:12

Simeon’s descendants included the Nemuelite clan from Nemuel, Jaminite clan from Jamin, and Jachinite clan from Jachin, 📖

## Numbers 26:13

the Zerahite clan from Zerah and Shaulite clan from Shaul. 📖

## Numbers 26:14

Simeon’s clans numbered 22,200 men. 📖

## Numbers 26:15

Gad’s descendants included the Zephonite clan from Zephon, Haggite clan from Haggi, and Shunite clan from Shuni, 📖

## Numbers 26:16

the Oznite clan from Ozni and Erite clan from Eri, 📖

## Numbers 26:17

the Arodite clan from Arod and Arelite clan from Areli. 📖

## Numbers 26:18

Gad’s clans numbered 40,500. 📖

## Numbers 26:19

Judah’s sons Er and Onan died in Canaan. 📖

## Numbers 26:20

Judah’s descendants included the Shelanite clan from Shelah, Perezite clan from Perez, and Zerahite clan from Zerah. 📖

## Numbers 26:21

Perez’s descendants included the Hezronite clan from Hezron and Hamulite clan from Hamul. 📖

## Numbers 26:22

Judah’s clans numbered 76,500. 📖

## Numbers 26:23

Issachar’s descendants included the Tolaite clan from Tola and Punite clan from Puvah, 📖

## Numbers 26:24

the Jashubite clan from Jashub and Shimronite clan from Shimron. 📖

## Numbers 26:25

Issachar’s clans numbered 64,300. 📖

## Numbers 26:26

Zebulun’s descendants included the Seredite clan from Sered, Elonite clan from Elon, and Jahleelite clan from Jahleel. 📖

## Numbers 26:27

Zebulun’s clans numbered 60,500. 📖

## Numbers 26:28

Joseph’s descendants included Manasseh’s and Ephraim’s clans. 📖

## Numbers 26:29

Manasseh’s descendants included the Machirite clan from Machir, Gilead’s father, and the Gileadite clan from Gilead. 📖

## Numbers 26:30

Gilead’s descendants included the Iezerite clan from Iezer and Helekite clan from Helek, 📖

## Numbers 26:31

the Asrielite clan from Asriel and Shechemite clan from Shechem, 📖

## Numbers 26:32

the Shemidaite clan from Shemida and Hepherite clan from Hepher. 📖

## Numbers 26:33

Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 📖

## Numbers 26:34

Manasseh’s clans numbered 52,700. 📖

## Numbers 26:35

Ephraim’s descendants included the Shuthelahite clan from Shuthelah, Becherite clan from Becher, and Tahanite clan from Tahan. 📖

## Numbers 26:36

Shuthelah’s descendants included the Eranite clan from Eran. 📖

## Numbers 26:37

Ephraim’s clans numbered 32,500. 📖

These clans descended from Joseph.

## Numbers 26:38

Benjamin’s descendants included the Belaite clan from Bela, Ashbelite clan from Ashbel, and Ahiramite clan from Ahiram, 📖

## Numbers 26:39

the Shuphamite clan from Shupham and Huphamite clan from Hupham. 📖

## Numbers 26:40

Bela’s descendants through Ard and Naaman included the Ardite clan from Ard and Naamite clan from Naaman. 📖

## Numbers 26:41

Benjamin’s clans numbered 45,600. 📖

## Numbers 26:42

Dan’s descendants included the Shuhamite clan from Shuham. 📖

These were Dan’s clans.

## Numbers 26:43

They were all Shuhamite clans, numbering 64,400. 📖

## Numbers 26:44

Asher’s descendants included the Imnite clan from Imnah, Ishvite clan from Ishvi, and Beriite clan from Beriah, 📖

## Numbers 26:45

Beriah’s descendants included the Heberite clan from Heber and Malchielite clan from Malchiel. 📖

## Numbers 26:46

Asher’s daughter was named Serah. 📖

## Numbers 26:47

Asher’s clans numbered 53,400. 📖

## Numbers 26:48

Naphtali’s descendants included the Jahzeelite clan from Jahzeel and Gunite clan from Guni, 📖

## Numbers 26:49

the Jezerite clan from Jezer and Shillemite clan from Shillem. 📖

## Numbers 26:50

Naphtali’s clans numbered 45,400. 📖

## Numbers 26:51

Israel’s men numbered 601,730 altogether. 📖

## Numbers 26:52

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 26:53

“Divide the land among the tribes as an inheritance, according to the number of names. 📖

## Numbers 26:54

Give a larger tribe more and a smaller tribe less. 📖

Each tribe’s inheritance must match its registered number.

## Numbers 26:55

The land must be divided by lot, according to the names of their fathers’ tribes. 📖

**Help with “lot”:** This means using lots to make a choice or allocation, rather than a person choosing.

## Numbers 26:56

Divide each inheritance by lot among the larger and smaller tribes.” 📖

## Numbers 26:57

These Levites were counted by clans: the Gershonite clan from Gershon, Kohathite clan from Kohath, and Merarite clan from Merari. 📖

## Numbers 26:58

Levi’s families were the Libnite, Hebronite, Mahlite, Mushite, and Korahite clans. 📖

Kohath was Amram’s father,

## Numbers 26:59

and Amram’s wife was Jochebed, Levi’s daughter, born to him in Egypt. 📖

She bore Amram Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam.

## Numbers 26:60

Aaron’s children were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar, 📖

## Numbers 26:61

but Nadab and Abihu died offering unauthorized fire before the LORD. 📖

**Help with “unauthorized”:** This means not permitted.

## Numbers 26:62

Levites numbered 23,000, every male one month or older. 📖

They were counted separately because they received no inheritance among Israel.

## Numbers 26:63

Moses and Eleazar the priest counted these Israelites on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, across from Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 26:64

None had been counted by Moses and Aaron the priest in Sinai’s wilderness. 📖

## Numbers 26:65

The LORD had said they would die in the wilderness. 📖

None remained except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
