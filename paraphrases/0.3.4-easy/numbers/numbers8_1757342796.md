# Numbers 8 — The lamps and the Levites

**About this chapter**

Aaron arranges the lamps.

The Levites are cleansed and presented for service.

The LORD gives the ages for beginning work and retiring.

## Numbers 8:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 8:2

“Tell Aaron to arrange the seven lamps to light the area before the lampstand.” 📖

## Numbers 8:3

Aaron faced the lamps forward as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 8:4

The lampstand was hammered gold from base to blossoms, made by the pattern the LORD showed Moses. 📖

## Numbers 8:5

The LORD spoke to Moses again, 📖

## Numbers 8:6

“Take the Levites from Israel and make them ceremonially clean. 📖

**Help with “ceremonially clean”:** This means meeting the requirements of these worship rules.

## Numbers 8:7

Sprinkle them with purification water. 📖

Have them shave their whole bodies and wash their clothes to purify themselves.

## Numbers 8:8

They must bring a young bull with fine flour mixed with oil as its grain offering. 📖

Take a second young bull for sin.

## Numbers 8:9

Bring the Levites before the Tent of Meeting and gather all Israel. 📖

## Numbers 8:10

Present them before the LORD and have Israel put their hands on them. 📖

## Numbers 8:11

Aaron must present them before the LORD as a wave offering from Israel to serve the LORD. 📖

**Help with “wave offering”:** This means an offering presented before the LORD.

The Levites themselves are being presented for service.

## Numbers 8:12

They must put their hands on the bulls’ heads. 📖

Offer one for sin and one as a burnt offering to the LORD for the Levites’ atonement.

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God through the appointed offering.

A burnt offering is burned on the altar.

## Numbers 8:13

Have them stand before Aaron and his sons and present them before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖

## Numbers 8:14

Separate them from Israel this way. 📖

They will belong to Me.

## Numbers 8:15

After cleansing and presentation as a wave offering, they may serve at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 8:16

The Levites are wholly given to Me from Israel. 📖

I took them instead of all Israel’s firstborn from the womb.

## Numbers 8:17

Every firstborn male in Israel is Mine, human and animal. 📖

I set them apart when I struck down all Egypt’s firstborn.

## Numbers 8:18

I took the Levites instead of all Israel’s firstborn. 📖

## Numbers 8:19

I gave the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from Israel. 📖

They serve Israel at the Tent of Meeting and make atonement for Israel.

This is so no plague strikes when Israel approaches the sanctuary.”

## Numbers 8:20

Moses, Aaron, and all Israel did everything the LORD commanded Moses concerning the Levites. 📖

## Numbers 8:21

The Levites purified themselves and washed their clothes. 📖

Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD and made atonement to cleanse them.

## Numbers 8:22

Then they served at the Tent of Meeting before Aaron and his sons. 📖

Everything was done as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Numbers 8:23

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 8:24

“Levite men twenty-five or older must enter service at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

**About this age:** Chapter 4 counts workers from thirty to fifty.

The starting age here is twenty-five.

The text does not explain the difference here.

## Numbers 8:25

At fifty, they must retire and no longer perform the work. 📖

## Numbers 8:26

They may help their brothers with duties at the tent, but must not do the work themselves. 📖

Assign Levite responsibilities this way.”
