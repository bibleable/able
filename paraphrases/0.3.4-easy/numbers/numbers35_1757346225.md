# Numbers 35 — Levite cities and cities of refuge

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules about Levite cities and pastureland.

He distinguishes murder from unintended killing and gives trial and refuge rules.

The chapter retains warnings about death, witnesses, payment, and bloodshed.

## Numbers 35:1

The LORD again told Moses on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, across from Jericho, 📖

## Numbers 35:2

“Tell Israel to give the Levites cities to live in and surrounding pastureland from their inherited possessions. 📖

## Numbers 35:3

The cities are their homes. 📖

The pastureland is for their herds, flocks, and all other livestock.

## Numbers 35:4

The pastureland you give the Levites extends a thousand cubits from the city wall on every side. 📖

**Help with “cubit”:** This is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Numbers 35:5

Outside the city, measure two thousand cubits east, two thousand south, two thousand west, and two thousand north. 📖

Keep the city in the center.

These areas are the cities’ larger pasturelands.

## Numbers 35:6

Appoint six of their cities as cities of refuge, where someone who kills another may flee. 📖

Give the Levites forty-two other cities as well.

**Help with “refuge”:** This means a place of shelter or safety.

## Numbers 35:7

Give the Levites forty-eight cities altogether, with their pastureland. 📖

## Numbers 35:8

Give cities from Israel’s territory in proportion to each tribe’s inheritance: more from larger tribes and fewer from smaller ones.” 📖

## Numbers 35:9

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 35:10

“Tell Israel: When you cross the Jordan into Canaan, 📖

## Numbers 35:11

select cities of refuge where anyone killing someone without intending to may flee. 📖

## Numbers 35:12

These cities must protect from the avenger, so the killer does not die before standing trial before the assembly. 📖

**Help with “avenger”:** This is the person seeking to avenge the death.

## Numbers 35:13

Select six cities of refuge. 📖

## Numbers 35:14

Choose three across the Jordan and three in Canaan. 📖

## Numbers 35:15

The six protect Israel, foreigners, and strangers among them, so anyone killing someone without intending to may flee there. 📖

## Numbers 35:16

If someone strikes another with iron and kills him, he is a murderer and must be put to death. 📖

## Numbers 35:17

If someone holds a stone large enough to kill, strikes another, and kills him, he is a murderer. 📖

The murderer must be put to death.

## Numbers 35:18

If someone holds a deadly wooden object, strikes another, and kills him, he is a murderer. 📖

The murderer must be put to death.

## Numbers 35:19

The avenger of blood must put the murderer to death when he finds him. 📖

## Numbers 35:20

Likewise, if someone pushes another with a wish to harm him, or deliberately throws something and kills him, 📖

## Numbers 35:21

or strikes him by hand in hostility and kills him, he is a murderer. 📖

He must be put to death.

The avenger of blood must kill the murderer when he finds him.

## Numbers 35:22

But if someone pushes another suddenly without hostility or throws something without intending to, 📖

## Numbers 35:23

or drops a heavy stone without looking and kills him, not being an enemy or intending harm, 📖

## Numbers 35:24

the assembly must judge between the killer and avenger of blood under these rules. 📖

## Numbers 35:25

The assembly must protect the killer from the avenger of blood and return him to his city of refuge. 📖

He must stay until the high priest dies.

That priest was anointed with holy oil.

**Help with “anointed”:** This means having oil put on someone to set them apart for a role.

## Numbers 35:26

If the killer goes outside his city of refuge’s boundaries 📖

## Numbers 35:27

and the avenger finds and kills him there, the avenger is not guilty of bloodshed, 📖

## Numbers 35:28

because the killer must remain in his refuge city until the high priest dies. 📖

Only afterward may he return to his own land.

## Numbers 35:29

This is your legal rule through coming generations, wherever you live. 📖

## Numbers 35:30

A murderer must be put to death on witnesses’ testimony. 📖

But no one may be put to death on one witness’s testimony alone.

## Numbers 35:31

Accept no ransom for a murderer deserving death. 📖

He must be put to death.

**Help with “ransom”:** This means a payment to gain release.

## Numbers 35:32

Accept no ransom allowing someone who fled to a refuge city to return and live on his own land. 📖

He must wait until the high priest dies.

## Numbers 35:33

Do not pollute your land, because bloodshed pollutes it. 📖

Nothing makes atonement for land where blood is shed except the blood of the one who shed it.

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God.

The passage connects this with the killer’s death.

## Numbers 35:34

Do not defile the land where you live and I dwell. 📖

I, the LORD, dwell among Israel.”
