# Numbers 25 — Peor, the plague, and Phinehas

**About this chapter**

Israel turns to Moab’s gods.

The chapter records commands to kill, Phinehas’ spear attack, and 24,000 plague deaths.

The LORD gives Phinehas a covenant and commands an attack on Midian.

## Numbers 25:1

While Israel stayed in Shittim, the men began having wrongful sexual relations with Moab’s daughters, 📖

## Numbers 25:2

who invited them to sacrifices for their gods. 📖

The people ate and bowed to these gods.

## Numbers 25:3

Israel joined in worshiping Baal of Peor, and the LORD’s anger burned against them. 📖

## Numbers 25:4

The LORD told Moses, “Take all the people’s leaders and execute them before the LORD in broad daylight. 📖

Then His fierce anger may turn from Israel.”

## Numbers 25:5

Moses told Israel’s judges, “Each of you must kill all his men who joined in worshiping Baal of Peor.” 📖

## Numbers 25:6

An Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his family before Moses and all Israel’s assembly. 📖

The assembly was weeping at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance.

## Numbers 25:7

Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw this. 📖

He rose from the assembly, took a spear,

## Numbers 25:8

followed the Israelite into his tent, and drove it through both of them. 📖

It passed through the man and the woman’s belly.

The plague against Israel stopped,

## Numbers 25:9

but 24,000 people died in it. 📖

## Numbers 25:10

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 25:11

“Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, turned My wrath from Israel. 📖

He was zealous for Me among them, so I did not consume Israel in My zeal.

**Help with “zealous”:** This means intensely devoted.

Zeal is intense devotion.

## Numbers 25:12

Tell him I grant him My covenant of peace. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Numbers 25:13

It is a permanent priesthood covenant for him and his descendants. 📖

He was zealous for his God and made atonement for Israel.”

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God.

## Numbers 25:14

The Israelite killed with the Midianite woman was Zimri son of Salu, leader of a Simeonite family. 📖

## Numbers 25:15

The Midianite woman killed was Cozbi daughter of Zur, a tribal chief of a Midianite family. 📖

## Numbers 25:16

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 25:17

“Attack the Midianites and strike them dead. 📖

## Numbers 25:18

They attacked you through deception, seducing you in the matter of Peor and their sister Cozbi. 📖

She was the Midianite leader’s daughter, killed on the day the plague came because of Peor.”
