# Numbers 1 — Counting those able to serve in the army

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses and Aaron to count Israel’s men able to serve in the army.

The chapter lists the leaders and each tribe’s number.

The Levites receive separate duties.

## Numbers 1:1

On day one of month two of year two after Israel left Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

He spoke from the Tent of Meeting in Sinai’s wilderness.

## Numbers 1:2

He said, “Count all Israel by clans and families. 📖

List every man by name, one by one.

**Help with “clans”:** These are family groups.

## Numbers 1:3

You and Aaron must count everyone twenty or older able to serve in Israel’s army, by their divisions. 📖

**Help with “divisions”:** These are organized groups.

## Numbers 1:4

One man from each tribe, a family head, must help you. 📖

## Numbers 1:5

These are their names: From Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur; 📖

## Numbers 1:6

From Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai; 📖

## Numbers 1:7

From Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab; 📖

## Numbers 1:8

From Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar; 📖

## Numbers 1:9

From Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon; 📖

## Numbers 1:10

From Joseph’s sons: Ephraim’s Elishama son of Ammihud and Manasseh’s Gamaliel son of Pedahzur; 📖

## Numbers 1:11

From Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni; 📖

## Numbers 1:12

From Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai; 📖

## Numbers 1:13

From Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran; 📖

## Numbers 1:14

From Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel; 📖

## Numbers 1:15

From Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.” 📖

## Numbers 1:16

These appointed men led their fathers’ tribes and headed Israel’s clans. 📖

## Numbers 1:17

Moses and Aaron took the men named to help them, 📖

## Numbers 1:18

and gathered all the people on day one of month two. 📖

They recorded ancestry by clans and families, listing names one by one from age twenty upward,

## Numbers 1:19

as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

Moses counted them in Sinai’s wilderness.

## Numbers 1:20

Reuben was Israel’s firstborn. 📖

His sons were recorded by clans and families.

They listed every male twenty or older able to serve in the army, one by one.

## Numbers 1:21

Reuben’s registered men numbered 46,500. 📖

## Numbers 1:22

Simeon’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed every male twenty or older able to serve in the army, one by one.

## Numbers 1:23

Simeon’s registered men numbered 59,300. 📖

## Numbers 1:24

Gad’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:25

Gad’s registered men numbered 45,650. 📖

## Numbers 1:26

Judah’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:27

Judah’s registered men numbered 74,600. 📖

## Numbers 1:28

Issachar’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:29

Issachar’s registered men numbered 54,400. 📖

## Numbers 1:30

Zebulun’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:31

Zebulun’s registered men numbered 57,400. 📖

## Numbers 1:32

From Joseph’s sons, Ephraim’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:33

Ephraim’s registered men numbered 40,500. 📖

## Numbers 1:34

Manasseh’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:35

Manasseh’s registered men numbered 32,200. 📖

## Numbers 1:36

Benjamin’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:37

Benjamin’s registered men numbered 35,400. 📖

## Numbers 1:38

Dan’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:39

Dan’s registered men numbered 62,700. 📖

## Numbers 1:40

Asher’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:41

Asher’s registered men numbered 41,500. 📖

## Numbers 1:42

Naphtali’s sons were recorded by clans and families. 📖

They listed everyone twenty or older able to serve in the army.

## Numbers 1:43

Naphtali’s registered men numbered 53,400. 📖

## Numbers 1:44

Moses and Aaron counted them with twelve Israelite leaders, each representing his family. 📖

## Numbers 1:45

All Israelites twenty or older able to serve in Israel’s army were counted by their families. 📖

## Numbers 1:46

The total was 603,550. 📖

## Numbers 1:47

The Levites were not counted with them by their fathers’ tribe. 📖

## Numbers 1:48

The LORD had told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 1:49

“Do not count Levi’s tribe with the other Israelites. 📖

## Numbers 1:50

Appoint them over the tabernacle of the Testimony, all its furnishings, and everything inside. 📖

They must carry it and its articles, care for it, and camp around it.

**Help with these words:** The tabernacle is the tent used as God’s sanctuary.

The Testimony is God’s covenant witness given to Israel.

## Numbers 1:51

They must take it down for moving and set it up for camping. 📖

Any outsider approaching it must be put to death.

## Numbers 1:52

Israel must camp by divisions, each man in his camp under his standard. 📖

**Help with “standard”:** This is a flag or sign marking the group.

## Numbers 1:53

The Levites must camp around and guard the tabernacle of the Testimony so no wrath falls on Israel. 📖

They are responsible for it.”

## Numbers 1:54

Israel did everything as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖
