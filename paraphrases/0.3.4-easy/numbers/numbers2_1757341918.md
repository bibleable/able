# Numbers 2 — The tribes’ camps and order of travel

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses and Aaron where the tribes must camp.

Each group has its own leader and number.

The chapter also gives the order for setting out.

## Numbers 2:1

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 2:2

“Israel must camp around the Tent of Meeting at a distance, each under his standard and family banners. 📖

**Help with these words:** Standards and banners are flags or signs marking groups.

## Numbers 2:3

Judah’s divisions must camp east, toward sunrise, under their standard. 📖

Their leader is Nahshon son of Amminadab,

## Numbers 2:4

with 74,600 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:5

Issachar must camp beside them. 📖

Its leader is Nethanel son of Zuar,

## Numbers 2:6

with 54,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:7

Next is Zebulun, led by Eliab son of Helon, 📖

## Numbers 2:8

with 57,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:9

Judah’s camp divisions total 186,400. 📖

They must set out first.

## Numbers 2:10

Reuben’s divisions must camp south under their standard. 📖

Their leader is Elizur son of Shedeur,

## Numbers 2:11

with 46,500 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:12

Simeon must camp beside them. 📖

Its leader is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,

## Numbers 2:13

with 59,300 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:14

Next is Gad, led by Eliasaph son of Deuel, 📖

## Numbers 2:15

with 45,650 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:16

Reuben’s camp divisions total 151,450. 📖

They must set out second.

## Numbers 2:17

The Tent of Meeting must travel with the Levites’ camp in the middle. 📖

Travel as you camped, each in his place under his standard.

## Numbers 2:18

Ephraim’s divisions must camp west under their standard. 📖

Their leader is Elishama son of Ammihud,

## Numbers 2:19

with 40,500 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:20

Manasseh must camp next. 📖

Its leader is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,

## Numbers 2:21

with 32,200 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:22

Next is Benjamin, led by Abidan son of Gideoni, 📖

## Numbers 2:23

with 35,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:24

Ephraim’s camp divisions total 108,100. 📖

They must set out third.

## Numbers 2:25

Dan’s divisions must camp north under their standard. 📖

Their leader is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai,

## Numbers 2:26

with 62,700 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:27

Asher must camp beside them. 📖

Its leader is Pagiel son of Ocran,

## Numbers 2:28

with 41,500 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:29

Next is Naphtali, led by Ahira son of Enan, 📖

## Numbers 2:30

with 53,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:31

Dan’s camp totals 157,600. 📖

They must set out last under their standards.”

## Numbers 2:32

These Israelites were counted by families. 📖

The camp divisions totaled 603,550.

## Numbers 2:33

The Levites were not counted with the others, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 2:34

Israel obeyed everything the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

They camped and traveled under their standards, each with his clan and family.
