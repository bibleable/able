# Numbers 27 — Daughters’ inheritance and Joshua’s appointment

**About this chapter**

Zelophehad’s daughters ask for their father’s inheritance.

The LORD gives an inheritance rule and tells Moses about his coming death.

Moses asks for a leader, and Joshua is appointed.

## Numbers 27:1

Zelophehad’s daughters belonged to the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph. 📖

Zelophehad was Hepher’s son, Hepher Gilead’s son, Gilead Machir’s son, and Machir Manasseh’s son.

The daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

They approached

## Numbers 27:2

and stood at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the leaders, and the whole assembly. 📖

They said,

## Numbers 27:3

“Our father died in the wilderness because of his own sin and had no sons. 📖

He was not among Korah’s followers who gathered against the LORD.

## Numbers 27:4

Why should his name vanish from his clan because he had no sons? 📖

Give us property among our father’s brothers.”

## Numbers 27:5

Moses brought their case before the LORD, 📖

## Numbers 27:6

and the LORD answered, 📖

## Numbers 27:7

“Zelophehad’s daughters are right. 📖

You must give them property among their father’s brothers and transfer his inheritance to them.

## Numbers 27:8

Tell Israel: If a man dies without a son, transfer his inheritance to his daughter. 📖

## Numbers 27:9

If he has no daughter, give it to his brothers. 📖

## Numbers 27:10

If he has no brothers, give it to his father’s brothers. 📖

## Numbers 27:11

If his father has no brothers, give it to the nearest relative in his clan to possess. 📖

This is Israel’s legal rule, as the LORD commanded Moses.”

## Numbers 27:12

The LORD told Moses, “Climb this mountain in the Abarim range. 📖

See the land I gave Israel.

## Numbers 27:13

After seeing it, you too will be gathered to your people, like your brother Aaron. 📖

**Help with “gathered to your people”:** This is an expression used here for death.

## Numbers 27:14

When the assembly quarreled in Zin’s wilderness, you both rebelled against My command. 📖

You were to show My holiness before them concerning the waters.”

These were Meribah’s waters at Kadesh in Zin’s wilderness.

## Numbers 27:15

Moses appealed to the LORD, 📖

## Numbers 27:16

“May the LORD, God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the assembly 📖

**Help with “all flesh”:** This expression refers to living beings.

## Numbers 27:17

to go out and come in before them, leading them out and bringing them in. 📖

Then the LORD’s assembly will not be like sheep without a shepherd.”

**Help with this picture:** Sheep without a shepherd picture people without a leader to guide them.

## Numbers 27:18

The LORD told Moses, “Take Joshua son of Nun, a man with the Spirit in him. 📖

Lay your hands on him.

## Numbers 27:19

Have him stand before Eleazar the priest and the whole assembly. 📖

Appoint him before their eyes.

## Numbers 27:20

Give him some of your authority so all Israel’s assembly will obey him. 📖

## Numbers 27:21

He must stand before Eleazar the priest, who will seek the LORD’s guidance for him by the Urim’s judgment. 📖

At his command, Joshua and all Israel with him, the whole assembly, will go out and come in.”

**Help with “Urim”:** This names a priestly means of seeking God’s guidance.

The passage does not explain its operation here.

## Numbers 27:22

Moses obeyed the LORD and had Joshua stand before Eleazar the priest and the whole assembly. 📖

## Numbers 27:23

He laid his hands on Joshua and appointed him, as the LORD instructed through Moses. 📖
