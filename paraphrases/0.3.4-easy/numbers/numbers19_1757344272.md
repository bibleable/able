# Numbers 19 — The red heifer and purification water

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules for preparing purification water.

The chapter explains the effects of contact with the dead and the steps for becoming clean.

These are the passage’s worship rules, without a modern medical explanation.

## Numbers 19:1

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 19:2

“This is the law the LORD commanded. 📖

Tell Israel to bring a red heifer without blemish or defect, never put under a yoke.

**Help with these words:** A heifer is a young female cow.

A yoke is equipment used to make an animal pull a load.

## Numbers 19:3

Give it to Eleazar the priest. 📖

He must have it taken outside camp and slaughtered before him.

## Numbers 19:4

Eleazar must take some blood on his finger and sprinkle it seven times toward the Tent of Meeting’s front. 📖

## Numbers 19:5

Burn the heifer before him: hide, flesh, blood, and dung. 📖

## Numbers 19:6

The priest must throw cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet wool onto the burning heifer. 📖

**Help with “hyssop”:** This is a plant named in these purification instructions.

## Numbers 19:7

Then he must wash his clothes and bathe in water. 📖

Afterward he may enter camp, but remains ceremonially unclean until evening.

**Help with “unclean”:** This describes a condition under worship rules, not a judgment about someone’s worth.

## Numbers 19:8

The person burning the heifer must also wash his clothes and bathe in water. 📖

He too remains ceremonially unclean until evening.

## Numbers 19:9

A ceremonially clean man must gather the ashes and store them in a ceremonially clean place outside camp. 📖

Israel’s assembly must keep them for preparing purification water.

This is for purification from sin.

## Numbers 19:10

The man gathering the ashes must wash his clothes and remains ceremonially unclean until evening. 📖

This rule is permanent for Israel and resident foreigners.

## Numbers 19:11

Anyone touching any dead body will be unclean seven days. 📖

## Numbers 19:12

He must purify himself with the water on days three and seven, and then he will be clean. 📖

Without purification on days three and seven, he will not be clean.

## Numbers 19:13

Anyone touching a human corpse without purifying himself defiles the LORD’s tabernacle and must be cut off from Israel. 📖

He remains unclean because purification water was not sprinkled on him.

His uncleanness is still on him.

## Numbers 19:14

If someone dies in a tent, everyone entering or already inside will be unclean seven days. 📖

## Numbers 19:15

Any open container without a fastened lid is unclean. 📖

## Numbers 19:16

In an open field, touching someone killed by sword or dead from natural causes makes a person unclean seven days. 📖

Touching a human bone or grave does too.

## Numbers 19:17

To purify an unclean person, put some ashes from the burnt sin offering in a jar. 📖

Pour fresh water over them.

## Numbers 19:18

A ceremonially clean man must dip hyssop in the water. 📖

He must sprinkle the tent, all its furnishings, and everyone who was there.

Also sprinkle whoever touched a bone, grave, or person dead naturally or by killing.

## Numbers 19:19

The clean man must sprinkle the unclean person on days three and seven. 📖

After purification on day seven, the person being cleansed must wash his clothes and bathe in water.

That evening he will be clean.

## Numbers 19:20

An unclean person who does not purify himself must be cut off from the assembly for defiling the LORD’s sanctuary. 📖

Purification water was not sprinkled on him.

He is unclean.

## Numbers 19:21

This rule is permanent: whoever sprinkles purification water must wash his clothes. 📖

Anyone touching the water remains unclean until evening.

## Numbers 19:22

Anything the unclean person touches becomes unclean. 📖

Anyone touching it remains unclean until evening.”
