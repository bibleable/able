# Numbers 3 — The Levites and the firstborn

**About this chapter**

The LORD assigns the Levites to help Aaron and his sons.

The chapter lists their families, numbers, locations, and duties.

The Levites take the place of Israel’s firstborn, with payment for the excess.

## Numbers 3:1

This is Aaron and Moses’ account when the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. 📖

## Numbers 3:2

Aaron’s sons were Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 📖

## Numbers 3:3

They were anointed and ordained to serve as priests. 📖

**Help with these words:** Anointed means set apart with oil.

Ordained means appointed to this service.

## Numbers 3:4

Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD after offering unauthorized fire in Sinai’s wilderness. 📖

They had no sons.

Only Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests during Aaron’s lifetime.

## Numbers 3:5

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 3:6

“Bring Levi’s tribe before Aaron the priest to help him. 📖

## Numbers 3:7

They must serve him and all the people before the Tent of Meeting, doing the tabernacle’s work. 📖

## Numbers 3:8

They must care for all the tent’s furnishings and serve Israel through the tabernacle’s duties. 📖

## Numbers 3:9

Assign them to Aaron and his sons. 📖

They are given exclusively to him from Israel.

## Numbers 3:10

Appoint Aaron and his sons to priestly duties. 📖

Any outsider approaching the tabernacle must be put to death.”

## Numbers 3:11

The LORD spoke to Moses again, 📖

## Numbers 3:12

“I took the Levites from Israel in place of every firstborn from the womb. 📖

They belong to Me,

## Numbers 3:13

because all the firstborn are Mine. 📖

On the day I killed every firstborn in Egypt, I set apart all Israel’s firstborn, human and animal.

They are Mine.

I am the LORD.”

## Numbers 3:14

The LORD told Moses in Sinai’s wilderness, 📖

## Numbers 3:15

“Count the Levites by families and clans. 📖

Count every male one month or older.”

## Numbers 3:16

Moses counted them as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Numbers 3:17

Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## Numbers 3:18

Gershon’s sons by clans were Libni and Shimei. 📖

## Numbers 3:19

Kohath’s sons by clans were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 📖

## Numbers 3:20

Merari’s sons by clans were Mahli and Mushi. 📖

These were Levi’s clans by their families.

## Numbers 3:21

The Libnite and Shimeite clans came from Gershon. 📖

They were the Gershonite clans.

## Numbers 3:22

Their males one month or older numbered 7,500. 📖

## Numbers 3:23

The Gershonites must camp west, behind the tabernacle, 📖

## Numbers 3:24

led by Eliasaph son of Lael. 📖

## Numbers 3:25

Their duties at the Tent of Meeting were the tabernacle, tent, covering, entrance curtain, 📖

## Numbers 3:26

courtyard curtains, courtyard entrance curtain around the tabernacle and altar, and cords. 📖

They must do all work for these.

## Numbers 3:27

Kohath’s clans were the Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites. 📖

They were the Kohathite clans.

## Numbers 3:28

Their males one month or older numbered 8,600. 📖

They were responsible for sanctuary duties.

## Numbers 3:29

The Kohathites must camp south of the tabernacle, 📖

## Numbers 3:30

led by Elizaphan son of Uzziel. 📖

## Numbers 3:31

Their duties were the ark, table, lampstand, altars, sanctuary articles used with them, and curtain. 📖

They must do all work for these.

## Numbers 3:32

Eleazar, Aaron the priest’s son, was chief over the Levite leaders. 📖

He oversaw those responsible for sanctuary duties.

## Numbers 3:33

From Merari came the clans of the Mahlites and Mushites. 📖

They were the Merarite clans.

## Numbers 3:34

Their males one month or older numbered 6,200. 📖

## Numbers 3:35

Zuriel son of Abihail led them. 📖

They must camp north of the tabernacle.

## Numbers 3:36

Merari’s sons must care for the frames, crossbars, posts, bases, and all tabernacle equipment. 📖

They must do all work for these,

## Numbers 3:37

and the courtyard’s posts, bases, tent pegs, and ropes. 📖

## Numbers 3:38

Moses, Aaron, and Aaron’s sons must camp east toward sunrise, before the Tent of Meeting. 📖

They must perform sanctuary duties for Israel.

Any outsider approaching must be put to death.

## Numbers 3:39

Moses and Aaron counted the Levites by clans as the LORD commanded. 📖

All males one month or older totaled 22,000.

**About these counts:** The clan figures in verses 22, 28, and 34 total 22,300.

BSB gives 22,000 here.

The difference needs further editorial review.

## Numbers 3:40

The LORD told Moses, “Count and list the names of every firstborn Israelite male one month or older. 📖

## Numbers 3:41

Take the Levites for Me instead of all Israel’s firstborn. 📖

Take their livestock instead of Israel’s firstborn livestock.

I am the LORD.”

## Numbers 3:42

Moses counted Israel’s firstborn as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Numbers 3:43

Firstborn males one month or older, listed by name, totaled 22,273. 📖

## Numbers 3:44

The LORD told Moses again, 📖

## Numbers 3:45

“Take the Levites instead of Israel’s firstborn, and their livestock instead of Israel’s livestock. 📖

The Levites are Mine.

I am the LORD.

## Numbers 3:46

To redeem the 273 firstborn Israelites exceeding the Levites, 📖

## Numbers 3:47

collect five sanctuary shekels each. 📖

Each shekel is twenty gerahs.

**Help with these words:** Redeem means secure release by payment.

Shekels and gerahs are weight units.

## Numbers 3:48

Give Aaron and his sons the money as redemption for the excess Israelites.” 📖

## Numbers 3:49

Moses collected redemption money for those exceeding the number replaced by Levites. 📖

## Numbers 3:50

He collected 1,365 sanctuary shekels from Israel’s firstborn. 📖

## Numbers 3:51

He gave the money to Aaron and his sons, exactly as the LORD commanded. 📖
