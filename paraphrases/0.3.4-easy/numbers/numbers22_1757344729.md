# Numbers 22 — Balaam’s journey and the donkey

**About this chapter**

Balak asks Balaam to curse Israel.

God forbids a curse, then permits Balaam’s journey with a restriction.

The account also describes God’s anger, the angel’s opposition, and the donkey speaking.

## Numbers 22:1

Israel traveled on and camped on Moab’s plains near the Jordan, across from Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 22:2

Balak son of Zippor saw everything Israel did to the Amorites. 📖

## Numbers 22:3

Moab was terrified because the people were so many. 📖

Moab dreaded Israel.

## Numbers 22:4

Moab told Midian’s elders, “This horde will devour everything around us like an ox licking up field grass.” 📖

Balak son of Zippor was Moab’s king then,

**About this picture:** These are Moab’s fearful words about Israel.

## Numbers 22:5

and sent messengers for Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, by the Euphrates in his people’s land. 📖

Balak’s message said, “People came from Egypt, covering the land and settling beside me.

## Numbers 22:6

Please come and curse them. 📖

They are too strong for me.

Perhaps I can defeat them and drive them from the land.

I know those you bless are blessed and those you curse are cursed.”

## Numbers 22:7

Moab’s and Midian’s elders left with divination fees and told Balaam Balak’s words. 📖

**Help with “divination”:** This means seeking hidden knowledge or predictions through spiritual practices.

## Numbers 22:8

Balaam replied, “Stay overnight, and I will give you the LORD’s answer.” 📖

Moab’s princes stayed with him.

## Numbers 22:9

God came to Balaam and asked, “Who are these men with you?” 📖

## Numbers 22:10

Balaam told God, “Balak son of Zippor, Moab’s king, sent this message: 📖

## Numbers 22:11

‘People came from Egypt, covering the land. 📖

Come and curse them for me.

Perhaps I can fight them and drive them away.’”

## Numbers 22:12

God told Balaam, “Do not go with them or curse these people. 📖

They are blessed.”

## Numbers 22:13

Next morning, Balaam told Balak’s princes, “Return to your homeland. 📖

The LORD refused to let me go with you.”

## Numbers 22:14

Moab’s princes returned and told Balak, “Balaam refused to come.” 📖

## Numbers 22:15

Balak sent more princes, more important than the first messengers. 📖

## Numbers 22:16

They told Balaam, “Balak son of Zippor says: ‘Please let nothing stop you coming to me. 📖

## Numbers 22:17

I will honor you richly and do whatever you say. 📖

Please come and curse these people for me!’”

## Numbers 22:18

Balaam told Balak’s servants, “Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not disobey. 📖

I cannot go beyond the LORD my God’s command in anything small or great.

## Numbers 22:19

Please stay overnight like the others, so I can hear what else the LORD tells me.” 📖

## Numbers 22:20

That night God told Balaam, “Since these men came to summon you, go with them. 📖

But do only what I tell you.”

## Numbers 22:21

In the morning, Balaam rose, saddled his donkey, and went with Moab’s princes. 📖

## Numbers 22:22

God became angry because Balaam was going. 📖

The angel of the LORD stood in the road against him.

Balaam rode his donkey with his two servants.

**About the journey:** The account gives both God’s permission and His anger.

It does not explain that tension here.

## Numbers 22:23

The donkey saw the LORD’s angel in the road holding a drawn sword. 📖

She turned into a field.

Balaam beat her to return her to the path.

## Numbers 22:24

The LORD’s angel stood in a narrow passage between two vineyards, with walls on both sides. 📖

## Numbers 22:25

The donkey saw the angel and pressed against the wall, crushing Balaam’s foot against it. 📖

He beat her again.

## Numbers 22:26

The LORD’s angel moved ahead to a narrow place with no room to turn right or left. 📖

## Numbers 22:27

Seeing the angel, the donkey lay down beneath Balaam. 📖

Furious, he beat her with his staff.

## Numbers 22:28

The LORD opened her mouth. 📖

She asked Balaam, “What have I done, that you beat me these three times?”

## Numbers 22:29

He answered, “You made a fool of me! 📖

If I had a sword, I would kill you now!”

## Numbers 22:30

She asked, “Am I not the donkey you have ridden all your life until today? 📖

Have I ever treated you like this?”

“No,” he replied.

## Numbers 22:31

The LORD opened Balaam’s eyes. 📖

He saw the LORD’s angel in the road holding a drawn sword.

Balaam bowed low and fell facedown.

## Numbers 22:32

The LORD’s angel asked, “Why beat your donkey these three times? 📖

I came against you because your way is perverse before me.

**Help with “perverse”:** This means wrong or twisted.

## Numbers 22:33

She saw me and turned away three times. 📖

Otherwise I would have killed you and let her live.”

## Numbers 22:34

Balaam told the LORD’s angel, “I sinned. 📖

I did not know you stood in the road against me.

If this displeases you, I will return home.”

## Numbers 22:35

The LORD’s angel said, “Go with the men, but speak only what I tell you.” 📖

Balaam went with Balak’s princes.

## Numbers 22:36

Balak heard he was coming and met him at the Moabite city on the Arnon border, at his territory’s edge. 📖

## Numbers 22:37

Balak asked Balaam, “Did I not summon you urgently? 📖

Why did you not come?

Can I not reward you richly?”

## Numbers 22:38

Balaam replied, “I have come, but can I say anything I wish? 📖

I must speak only the word God puts in my mouth.”

## Numbers 22:39

Balaam went with Balak to Kiriath-huzoth. 📖

## Numbers 22:40

Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep and gave portions to Balaam and the princes with him. 📖

## Numbers 22:41

Next morning, Balak took Balaam up to Bamoth-baal. 📖

From there he could see the people’s camp outskirts.
