# Numbers 21 — Snakes, journeys, and battles

**About this chapter**

Israel makes a vow, complains, and suffers deaths from snakes.

The LORD gives a way for bitten people to live.

The chapter includes place names, songs, and accounts of conquest.

## Numbers 21:1

Arad’s Canaanite king lived in the Negev. 📖

Hearing Israel was coming along Atharim’s road, he attacked and captured prisoners.

## Numbers 21:2

Israel vowed to the LORD, “If You hand these people over to us, we will devote their cities to destruction.” 📖

**Help with “devote to destruction”:** Here this means setting apart for complete destruction.

## Numbers 21:3

The LORD heard Israel’s plea and handed over the Canaanites. 📖

Israel devoted them and their cities to destruction and named the place Hormah.

## Numbers 21:4

They left Mount Hor by the Red Sea route to go around Edom. 📖

The people grew impatient on the journey

## Numbers 21:5

and spoke against God and Moses. 📖

“Why bring us from Egypt to die in the wilderness?

There is no bread or water.

We hate this miserable food!”

## Numbers 21:6

The LORD sent venomous snakes among them. 📖

Many in Israel were bitten and died.

**Help with “venomous”:** These snakes have poison in their bites.

## Numbers 21:7

They told Moses, “We sinned by speaking against the LORD and you. 📖

Plead with the LORD to take away the snakes.”

Moses pleaded for them.

## Numbers 21:8

The LORD told Moses, “Make a fiery serpent and put it on a pole. 📖

Anyone bitten who looks at it will live.”

**Help with “fiery serpent”:** This is the expression used in BSB for the snake to be made.

## Numbers 21:9

Moses made a bronze snake and put it on a pole. 📖

A bitten person who looked at it would live.

## Numbers 21:10

Israel moved on and camped at Oboth. 📖

## Numbers 21:11

They left Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim in the wilderness opposite Moab, on its east. 📖

## Numbers 21:12

They left there and camped in the Valley of Zered. 📖

## Numbers 21:13

They moved on and camped beyond the Arnon, in the wilderness extending into Amorite territory. 📖

The Arnon is the border between Moabites and Amorites.

## Numbers 21:14

The Book of the Wars of the LORD therefore says: “Waheb in Suphah and the wadis of the Arnon, 📖

**Help with “wadis”:** These are stream valleys that may be dry.

## Numbers 21:15

even the slopes of the wadis reaching Ar and lying along Moab’s border.” 📖

## Numbers 21:16

They went on to Beer, the well where the LORD told Moses, “Gather the people so I may give water.” 📖

## Numbers 21:17

Israel sang, “Spring up, O well! 📖

Everyone, sing to it!

## Numbers 21:18

Princes dug the well. 📖

The people’s nobles hollowed it out with their scepters and staffs.”

From the wilderness, Israel went to Mattanah,

**Help with “scepters”:** These are rods carried as signs of authority.

## Numbers 21:19

from Mattanah to Nahaliel, and from Nahaliel to Bamoth, 📖

## Numbers 21:20

and from Bamoth to Moab’s valley, where Pisgah’s top overlooks the wasteland. 📖

## Numbers 21:21

Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, saying, 📖

## Numbers 21:22

“Let us pass through your land. 📖

We will enter no field or vineyard and drink from no well.

We will stay on the King’s Highway until we pass through your territory.”

## Numbers 21:23

Sihon refused passage, gathered his whole army, and confronted Israel in the wilderness. 📖

He reached Jahaz and fought Israel.

## Numbers 21:24

Israel struck him with swords and took his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok. 📖

They stopped at the Ammonite border because it was fortified.

**Help with “fortified”:** This means protected by strong defenses.

## Numbers 21:25

Israel captured and occupied all the Amorite cities, including Heshbon and all its villages. 📖

## Numbers 21:26

Heshbon was Sihon king of the Amorites’ city. 📖

He fought Moab’s former king and took all his land as far as the Arnon.

## Numbers 21:27

The poets therefore say, “Come to Heshbon! 📖

Let it be rebuilt.

Let Sihon’s city be restored.

## Numbers 21:28

Fire went out from Heshbon, a blaze from Sihon’s city. 📖

It consumed Ar of Moab, the rulers of Arnon’s heights.

## Numbers 21:29

Woe to you, Moab! 📖

You are destroyed, people of Chemosh!

He gave up his sons as refugees and his daughters as captives to Sihon king of the Amorites.

**Help with “woe”:** This is a cry of grief or judgment.

These lines are part of the poets’ words.

## Numbers 21:30

But we overthrew them. 📖

Heshbon is destroyed as far as Dibon.

We demolished them as far as Nophah, which reaches Medeba.”

## Numbers 21:31

Israel lived in Amorite land. 📖

## Numbers 21:32

Moses sent spies to Jazer. 📖

Israel captured its villages and drove out the Amorites there.

## Numbers 21:33

They turned up the road to Bashan. 📖

Og king of Bashan and his whole army met them in battle at Edrei.

## Numbers 21:34

The LORD told Moses, “Do not fear him. 📖

I handed him, all his people, and his land over to you.

Do as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.”

## Numbers 21:35

Israel struck down Og, his sons, and his whole army until no remnant remained. 📖

They took his land.

**Help with “remnant”:** This means any part left over.
