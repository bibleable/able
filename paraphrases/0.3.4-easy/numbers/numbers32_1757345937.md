# Numbers 32 — Land east of the Jordan

**About this chapter**

Gad and Reuben ask for land suitable for their livestock.

Moses warns them, and they promise to join the fighting across the Jordan.

The agreement’s conditions and the towns given or rebuilt are included.

## Numbers 32:1

Reuben’s and Gad’s people had very large herds and flocks. 📖

They examined Jazer and Gilead and saw the region suited livestock.

## Numbers 32:2

Gad’s and Reuben’s people told Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the assembly’s leaders, 📖

## Numbers 32:3

“Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon, 📖

## Numbers 32:4

which the LORD conquered before Israel’s assembly, suit livestock. 📖

Your servants have livestock.”

## Numbers 32:5

They said, “If we found your favor, give your servants this land as a possession. 📖

Do not make us cross the Jordan.”

## Numbers 32:6

Moses asked Gad’s and Reuben’s people, “Should your brothers go to war while you sit here? 📖

## Numbers 32:7

Why discourage Israel from crossing into the land the LORD gave them? 📖

## Numbers 32:8

Your fathers did this when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to inspect the land. 📖

## Numbers 32:9

They went to Eshcol’s valley and saw the land. 📖

They discouraged Israel from entering the land the LORD gave them.

## Numbers 32:10

The LORD’s anger burned that day, and He swore: 📖

## Numbers 32:11

‘None of the men twenty or older who left Egypt will see the land. 📖

I swore to give it to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

They did not follow Me wholeheartedly—

## Numbers 32:12

except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun. 📖

They followed the LORD wholeheartedly.’

## Numbers 32:13

The LORD’s anger burned against Israel. 📖

He made them wander forty years until the whole generation who did evil before Him was gone.

## Numbers 32:14

Now you, a brood of sinners, have replaced your fathers and added to the LORD’s burning anger against Israel. 📖

**Help with “brood”:** This means a group of offspring.

## Numbers 32:15

If you stop following Him, He will leave these people in the wilderness again. 📖

You will cause their destruction.”

## Numbers 32:16

Gad’s and Reuben’s people approached Moses. 📖

“We want sheepfolds here for livestock and cities for our little ones.

**Help with “sheepfolds”:** These are enclosures for keeping sheep.

## Numbers 32:17

We will arm ourselves and be ready to go ahead of Israel until we bring them into their place. 📖

Our little ones will stay in fortified cities, protected from the land’s inhabitants.

**Help with “fortified”:** This means protected by strong defenses.

## Numbers 32:18

We will not return home until every Israelite possesses his inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 32:19

We will not inherit with them across the Jordan and beyond. 📖

Our inheritance is on the Jordan’s east side.”

## Numbers 32:20

Moses replied, “If you do this and arm yourselves for battle before the LORD, 📖

## Numbers 32:21

and every armed man crosses the Jordan before the LORD until He drives His enemies out, 📖

## Numbers 32:22

you may return when the land is subdued before the LORD. 📖

You will be free from obligation to the LORD and Israel.

This land will be your possession before the LORD.

**Help with “subdued”:** This means brought under control by force.

## Numbers 32:23

But if you do not do this, you will sin against the LORD. 📖

Be sure your sin will find you out.

## Numbers 32:24

Build cities for your little ones and folds for your flocks. 📖

Do what you promised.”

## Numbers 32:25

Gad’s and Reuben’s people told Moses, “Your servants will do as our lord commands. 📖

## Numbers 32:26

Our children, wives, livestock, and all our animals will stay here in Gilead’s cities. 📖

## Numbers 32:27

Your servants are armed for war. 📖

Every man will cross to battle before the LORD, as our lord says.”

## Numbers 32:28

Moses gave orders about them to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and Israel’s tribal family leaders. 📖

## Numbers 32:29

Moses said, “Suppose every man of Gad and Reuben crosses the Jordan with you armed for battle before the LORD. 📖

If the land is subdued before you, give them Gilead as a possession.

## Numbers 32:30

But if they do not arm themselves and cross with you, they must accept a possession among you in Canaan.” 📖

## Numbers 32:31

Gad’s and Reuben’s people replied, “We will do as the LORD told your servants. 📖

## Numbers 32:32

We will cross into Canaan armed before the LORD to have our inheritance on this side of the Jordan.” 📖

## Numbers 32:33

Moses gave land to Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph. 📖

It included Sihon king of the Amorites’ kingdom and Og king of Bashan’s kingdom, with cities and surrounding territory.

## Numbers 32:34

Gad’s people built up Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer, 📖

## Numbers 32:35

Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah, 📖

## Numbers 32:36

Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran as fortified cities, and built folds for their flocks. 📖

## Numbers 32:37

Reuben’s people built up Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim, 📖

## Numbers 32:38

Nebo and Baal-meon, whose names changed, and Sibmah. 📖

They renamed the cities they rebuilt.

## Numbers 32:39

Machir son of Manasseh’s descendants went to Gilead, captured it, and drove out the Amorites there. 📖

## Numbers 32:40

Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh’s clan, and they settled there. 📖

## Numbers 32:41

Jair, Manasseh’s descendant, captured their villages and named them Havvoth-jair. 📖

## Numbers 32:42

Nobah captured Kenath and its villages and called it Nobah after himself. 📖
