# Numbers 31 — War against Midian and division of plunder

**About this chapter**

The LORD commands vengeance against Midian.

The chapter records killing, Moses’ command about captive boys and women, purification rules, and division of captured people and animals.

All counts and the soldiers’ gold offering are included.

## Numbers 31:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 31:2

“Take vengeance on Midian for Israel. 📖

Afterward you will be gathered to your people.”

**Help with these words:** Vengeance means punishment in return for a wrong.

“Gathered to your people” is an expression used here for death.

## Numbers 31:3

Moses told the people, “Arm some men for war against Midian to carry out the LORD’s vengeance. 📖

## Numbers 31:4

Send a thousand men from each tribe of Israel into battle.” 📖

## Numbers 31:5

A thousand men were recruited from each tribe of Israel: twelve thousand armed for war. 📖

## Numbers 31:6

Moses sent the thousand from each tribe with Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest. 📖

Phinehas took the sanctuary’s vessels and signaling trumpets.

## Numbers 31:7

They fought Midian as the LORD commanded Moses and killed every male. 📖

## Numbers 31:8

The dead included Midian’s five kings: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. 📖

They also killed Balaam son of Beor with swords.

## Numbers 31:9

Israel captured Midian’s women and children and took all their herds, flocks, and goods as plunder. 📖

**Help with “plunder”:** This means things taken in war.

## Numbers 31:10

They burned all Midian’s cities and camps, 📖

## Numbers 31:11

and took away all the plunder and spoils, both people and animals. 📖

## Numbers 31:12

They brought captives and plunder to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and Israel’s assembly. 📖

The camp was on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, across from Jericho.

## Numbers 31:13

Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the assembly’s leaders met them outside camp. 📖

## Numbers 31:14

Moses was angry with the returning army officers, the commanders of thousands and hundreds. 📖

## Numbers 31:15

He asked, “Did you spare all the women? 📖

## Numbers 31:16

Through Balaam’s advice, these women led Israel to be unfaithful to the LORD at Peor. 📖

The plague therefore struck the LORD’s assembly.

## Numbers 31:17

Now kill all the boys and every woman who has had sex with a man, 📖

## Numbers 31:18

but keep alive for yourselves every girl who has never had sex with a man. 📖

## Numbers 31:19

Everyone who killed a person or touched the dead must stay outside camp seven days. 📖

Purify yourselves and your captives on days three and seven.

## Numbers 31:20

Purify all clothing, leather goods, things made of goats’ hair, and wooden articles.” 📖

## Numbers 31:21

Eleazar the priest told the soldiers who fought, “This is the law the LORD commanded Moses: 📖

## Numbers 31:22

Gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead— 📖

## Numbers 31:23

everything able to withstand fire—must pass through fire to become clean. 📖

It still needs purification water.

Anything unable to withstand fire must pass through water.

## Numbers 31:24

Wash your clothes on day seven, and you will be clean. 📖

Afterward you may enter camp.”

**Help with “clean”:** This describes meeting the worship rules given here.

## Numbers 31:25

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 31:26

“You, Eleazar the priest, and the assembly’s family heads must count the captured people and animals. 📖

## Numbers 31:27

Divide the captives between the soldiers who fought and the rest of the assembly. 📖

## Numbers 31:28

Set aside a tribute for the LORD from the soldiers’ share. 📖

Take one out of every five hundred, whether people, cattle, donkeys, or sheep.

**Help with “tribute”:** This is the required portion given as an offering.

## Numbers 31:29

Take it from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as the LORD’s offering. 📖

## Numbers 31:30

From Israel’s half, take one of every fifty people, cattle, donkeys, sheep, and other animals. 📖

Give them to the Levites responsible for the LORD’s tabernacle.”

## Numbers 31:31

Moses and Eleazar the priest obeyed the LORD’s command to Moses. 📖

## Numbers 31:32

The plunder remaining from what the soldiers took was 675,000 sheep, 📖

## Numbers 31:33

72,000 cattle, 📖

## Numbers 31:34

61,000 donkeys, 📖

## Numbers 31:35

and 32,000 women who had not slept with a man. 📖

## Numbers 31:36

The soldiers’ half was 337,500 sheep, 📖

## Numbers 31:37

including 675 as the LORD’s tribute; 📖

## Numbers 31:38

36,000 cattle, including 72 as the LORD’s tribute; 📖

## Numbers 31:39

30,500 donkeys, including 61 as the LORD’s tribute; 📖

## Numbers 31:40

and 16,000 people, including 32 as the LORD’s tribute. 📖

## Numbers 31:41

Moses gave the tribute to Eleazar the priest as the LORD’s offering, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 31:42

Moses separated Israel’s half from the soldiers’ share. 📖

## Numbers 31:43

The assembly’s half was 337,500 sheep, 📖

## Numbers 31:44

36,000 cattle, 📖

## Numbers 31:45

30,500 donkeys, 📖

## Numbers 31:46

and 16,000 people. 📖

## Numbers 31:47

From Israel’s half, Moses took one of every fifty people and animals. 📖

He gave them to the Levites responsible for the LORD’s tabernacle, as the LORD commanded.

## Numbers 31:48

The army officers, commanders of thousands and hundreds, approached Moses 📖

## Numbers 31:49

and said, “Your servants counted the soldiers under our command. 📖

Not one of us is missing.

## Numbers 31:50

We brought the LORD the gold articles each man took: armlets, bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces. 📖

This is to make atonement for ourselves before the LORD.”

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God through the appointed offering.

## Numbers 31:51

Moses and Eleazar the priest received all their gold articles. 📖

## Numbers 31:52

The gold offered to the LORD by the commanders of thousands and hundreds weighed 16,750 shekels. 📖

**Help with “shekel”:** This is a measure of weight.

## Numbers 31:53

Each soldier had taken plunder for himself. 📖

## Numbers 31:54

Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from the commanders of thousands and hundreds. 📖

They brought it into the Tent of Meeting as a memorial for Israel before the LORD.
