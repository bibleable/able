# Numbers 23 — Blessings instead of curses

**About this chapter**

Balak takes Balaam to different places and offers sacrifices.

Balaam speaks the messages the LORD gives him.

The blessings include images of dust, a wild ox, and lions.

## Numbers 23:1

Balaam told Balak, “Build me seven altars here. 📖

Prepare seven bulls and seven rams.”

## Numbers 23:2

Balak obeyed, and they offered one bull and one ram on each altar. 📖

## Numbers 23:3

Balaam said, “Stay by your burnt offering while I go. 📖

Perhaps the LORD will meet me.

I will tell you whatever He reveals.”

He went to a barren height.

## Numbers 23:4

God met him. 📖

Balaam said, “I built seven altars and offered a bull and ram on each.”

## Numbers 23:5

The LORD put a message in Balaam’s mouth, saying, “Return to Balak and give him this message.” 📖

## Numbers 23:6

Balaam returned to Balak, standing by his burnt offering with all Moab’s princes. 📖

## Numbers 23:7

Balaam spoke an oracle: “Balak, Moab’s king, brought me from Aram, from the eastern mountains. 📖

He said, ‘Come, curse Jacob for me.

Come, denounce Israel!’

**Help with “oracle”:** This is a spoken prophetic message.

To denounce someone is to speak judgment against them.

## Numbers 23:8

How can I curse those God has not cursed? 📖

How can I denounce those the LORD has not denounced?

## Numbers 23:9

I see them from rocky cliffs and watch them from hills. 📖

They live apart and do not count themselves among the nations.

## Numbers 23:10

Who can count Jacob’s dust or even a fourth of Israel? 📖

Let me die like the righteous and have an end like theirs!”

**Help with “Jacob’s dust”:** This pictures the great number of Jacob’s people.

Righteous people live rightly before God.

## Numbers 23:11

Balak told Balaam, “What have you done to me? 📖

I brought you to curse my enemies, but you only blessed them!”

## Numbers 23:12

Balaam replied, “Must I not speak exactly what the LORD puts in my mouth?” 📖

## Numbers 23:13

Balak said, “Please come to another place. 📖

You will see only their camp’s outskirts, not everyone.

Curse them for me there.”

## Numbers 23:14

Balak took him to Zophim’s field on Pisgah’s top. 📖

He built seven altars and offered a bull and ram on each.

## Numbers 23:15

Balaam told Balak, “Stay beside your burnt offering while I meet the LORD over there.” 📖

## Numbers 23:16

The LORD met Balaam and put a message in his mouth. 📖

“Return to Balak and speak what I tell you.”

## Numbers 23:17

Balaam returned to Balak, standing by his burnt offering with Moab’s princes. 📖

Balak asked, “What did the LORD say?”

## Numbers 23:18

Balaam spoke an oracle: “Rise, Balak, and listen! 📖

Hear me, son of Zippor!

## Numbers 23:19

God is not a man who lies or a son of man who changes His mind. 📖

Does He speak without acting or promise without fulfilling?

## Numbers 23:20

I received a command to bless. 📖

He blessed them, and I cannot change it.

## Numbers 23:21

He considers no disaster for Jacob and sees no trouble for Israel. 📖

The LORD their God is with them, and the King’s shout is among them.

## Numbers 23:22

God brought them from Egypt with strength like a wild ox. 📖

## Numbers 23:23

There is no spell against Jacob or divination against Israel. 📖

Now people will say of Jacob and Israel, ‘What great things God has done!’

**Help with these words:** A spell is a spoken charm.

Divination means seeking hidden knowledge or predictions through spiritual practices.

## Numbers 23:24

The people rise like a lioness and rouse themselves like a lion. 📖

They will not rest until they devour prey and drink the blood of the slain.”

**About this picture:** The message compares the people to hunting lions.

The slain are those killed.

## Numbers 23:25

Balak told Balaam, “Then do not curse them or bless them at all!” 📖

## Numbers 23:26

Balaam replied, “Did I not tell you I must do whatever the LORD says?” 📖

## Numbers 23:27

Balak said, “Please come to another place. 📖

Perhaps God will be pleased to let you curse them for me there.”

## Numbers 23:28

He took Balaam to Peor’s top, overlooking the wasteland. 📖

## Numbers 23:29

Balaam said, “Build me seven altars here. 📖

Prepare seven bulls and seven rams.”

## Numbers 23:30

Balak obeyed and offered a bull and ram on each altar. 📖
