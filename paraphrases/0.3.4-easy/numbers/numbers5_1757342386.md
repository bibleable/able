# Numbers 5 — Cleansing, repayment, and a husband’s suspicion

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives rules about the camp and repaying wrongs.

The second part describes a husband’s suspicion and the priest’s ritual.

Suspicion does not establish what the woman did.

The text names both a guilty and an innocent case.

## Numbers 5:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 5:2

“Command Israel to send outside camp anyone with skin disease, a bodily discharge, or defilement from a dead body. 📖

## Numbers 5:3

Send males and females alike outside, so they do not defile the camp where I live among them.” 📖

## Numbers 5:4

Israel sent them outside, doing exactly as the LORD told Moses. 📖

## Numbers 5:5

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 5:6

“Tell Israel: A man or woman committing a sin against another acts unfaithfully against the LORD and is guilty, 📖

## Numbers 5:7

The person must confess the sin and repay the wronged person fully, adding a fifth of the value. 📖

**Help with “a fifth”:** This means one of five equal parts added to the full repayment.

## Numbers 5:8

If the wronged man has no relative to receive payment, it belongs to the LORD. 📖

Give it to the priest with the ram for atonement.

## Numbers 5:9

Every sacred contribution Israel brings the priest belongs to him. 📖

## Numbers 5:10

Each man’s sacred gifts are his own. 📖

Whatever he gives the priest belongs to the priest.”

## Numbers 5:11

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 5:12

“Tell Israel: If a wife goes astray and is unfaithful to her husband 📖

## Numbers 5:13

by having sex with another man, hidden from her husband. 📖

Her impurity is undetected.

There is no witness, and she was not caught in the act,

## Numbers 5:14

Her husband may become jealous and suspect her whether she has defiled herself or has not. 📖

## Numbers 5:15

He must bring her to the priest with a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. 📖

Add no oil or frankincense.

It is a grain offering for jealousy, a memorial reminder of wrongdoing.

## Numbers 5:16

The priest must bring her before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 5:17

He must put holy water in a clay jar and add dust from the tabernacle floor. 📖

## Numbers 5:18

Before the LORD, he must let down her hair and place the memorial jealousy offering in her hands. 📖

He must hold the bitter water bringing a curse.

## Numbers 5:19

He must put her under oath. 📖

Say, ‘Suppose no other man slept with you and you did not stray and become defiled under your husband’s authority.

Then may you be immune to this bitter water bringing a curse.

## Numbers 5:20

But if you strayed under your husband’s authority, defiled yourself, and had sex with another man’— 📖

## Numbers 5:21

The priest must put her under the curse’s oath. 📖

He must say, ‘Then may the LORD make you a curse known among your people.

May He shrivel your thigh and swell your belly.

**Help with this wording:** Shrivel means shrink and wither.

BSB’s precise bodily expression needs further editorial review.

No modern diagnosis is supplied here.

## Numbers 5:22

May the cursing water enter your stomach, swelling your belly and shriveling your thigh.’ 📖

She must answer, ‘Amen, Amen.’

**Help with “Amen”:** This expresses agreement with the words spoken.

## Numbers 5:23

The priest must write the curses on a scroll and wash them into the bitter water. 📖

## Numbers 5:24

He must make her drink the bitter, cursing water. 📖

It will enter her and may cause bitter suffering.

## Numbers 5:25

Take the jealousy offering from her hand, wave it before the LORD, and bring it to the altar. 📖

## Numbers 5:26

Burn a handful as its memorial portion. 📖

After that, make her drink the water.

## Numbers 5:27

If she defiled herself and was unfaithful, the water will enter and cause bitter suffering. 📖

Her belly will swell and thigh shrivel.

She will be accursed among her people.

## Numbers 5:28

If she is clean and did not defile herself, she will be unharmed and able to conceive children. 📖

## Numbers 5:29

This is the jealousy law for a wife straying and defiling herself under her husband’s authority, 📖

## Numbers 5:30

or for a husband becoming jealous and suspecting her. 📖

Have her stand before the LORD, and the priest must apply this whole law.

## Numbers 5:31

The husband is free from guilt, but the woman bears her wrongdoing.” 📖
