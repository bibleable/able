# Numbers 29 — Seventh-month offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD continues the offering instructions for the seventh month.

The daily animal counts and repeated additional offerings are kept separately.

An ephah measures dry goods.

## Numbers 29:1

“On day one of month seven, hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work. 📖

This is a day to sound the trumpets.

## Numbers 29:2

Present a burnt offering as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

Offer one young bull, one ram, and seven year-old male lambs, all without blemish,

## Numbers 29:3

with grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil. 📖

Give three-tenths of an ephah with the bull and two-tenths with the ram,

## Numbers 29:4

and one-tenth of an ephah with each of the seven male lambs. 📖

## Numbers 29:5

Include one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you. 📖

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God through the appointed offering.

## Numbers 29:6

These are besides the monthly and daily burnt offerings with their required grain and drink offerings. 📖

They are a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.

## Numbers 29:7

On day ten of this seventh month, hold a sacred assembly, humble yourselves, and do no work. 📖

## Numbers 29:8

Present a burnt offering as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

Offer one young bull, one ram, and seven year-old male lambs, all without blemish,

## Numbers 29:9

with grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil. 📖

Give three-tenths of an ephah with the bull and two-tenths with the ram,

## Numbers 29:10

and one-tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs. 📖

## Numbers 29:11

Include one male goat as a sin offering. 📖

Add these to the atonement sin offering and regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings.

## Numbers 29:12

On day fifteen of month seven, hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work. 📖

Keep a feast to the LORD seven days.

## Numbers 29:13

As a pleasing aroma to the LORD, present a food offering, a burnt offering. 📖

Offer thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen year-old male lambs, all without blemish.

## Numbers 29:14

Add grain offerings: three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for each of the thirteen bulls. 📖

Give two-tenths of an ephah with each of the two rams,

## Numbers 29:15

and one-tenth of an ephah with each of the fourteen lambs. 📖

## Numbers 29:16

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:17

On day two, offer twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen year-old male lambs, all without blemish, 📖

## Numbers 29:18

with the required grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number. 📖

## Numbers 29:19

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:20

On day three, offer eleven bulls, two rams, and fourteen year-old male lambs, all without blemish, 📖

## Numbers 29:21

with the required grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number. 📖

## Numbers 29:22

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:23

On day four, offer ten bulls, two rams, and fourteen year-old male lambs, all without blemish, 📖

## Numbers 29:24

with the required grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number. 📖

## Numbers 29:25

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:26

On day five, offer nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen year-old male lambs, all without blemish, 📖

## Numbers 29:27

with the required grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number. 📖

## Numbers 29:28

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:29

On day six, offer eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen year-old male lambs, all without blemish, 📖

## Numbers 29:30

with the required grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number. 📖

## Numbers 29:31

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:32

On day seven, offer seven bulls, two rams, and fourteen year-old male lambs, all without blemish, 📖

## Numbers 29:33

with the required grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number. 📖

## Numbers 29:34

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:35

On day eight, hold a solemn assembly and do no regular work. 📖

## Numbers 29:36

As a pleasing aroma to the LORD, present a food offering, a burnt offering. 📖

Offer one bull, one ram, and seven year-old male lambs, all without blemish,

## Numbers 29:37

with the required grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number. 📖

## Numbers 29:38

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:39

Present these to the LORD at your appointed times, besides your vow and voluntary offerings. 📖

These may be burnt, grain, drink, or peace offerings.”

## Numbers 29:40

Moses told Israel all this exactly as the LORD commanded him. 📖
