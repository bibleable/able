# Numbers 10 — Trumpets and the departure from Sinai

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives trumpet signals for gathering, travel, battle, and worship.

Israel leaves Sinai in the stated order.

Moses speaks to Hobab and prays when the ark moves or rests.

## Numbers 10:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 10:2

“Make two hammered silver trumpets to call the people together and signal the camps to leave. 📖

## Numbers 10:3

When both sound, everyone must gather before you at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 10:4

When only one sounds, Israel’s leaders and clan heads must gather before you. 📖

## Numbers 10:5

Short blasts signal the east camps to leave. 📖

## Numbers 10:6

A second set of short blasts signals the south camps to leave. 📖

These blasts signal departure.

## Numbers 10:7

Call the assembly with long blasts, not short ones. 📖

## Numbers 10:8

Aaron’s priestly sons must blow them. 📖

This is a permanent law through coming generations.

## Numbers 10:9

When an enemy attacks you in your land, sound short trumpet blasts in battle. 📖

The LORD your God will remember and save you from your enemies.

## Numbers 10:10

On joyous occasions, appointed feasts, and each month’s beginning, sound trumpets over burnt and peace offerings. 📖

They serve as a reminder for you before your God.

I am the LORD your God.”

## Numbers 10:11

On day twenty of month two of year two, the cloud lifted from above the tabernacle of the Testimony, 📖

## Numbers 10:12

and Israel left Sinai’s wilderness. 📖

They traveled from place to place until it settled in Paran’s wilderness.

## Numbers 10:13

They set out this first time at the LORD’s command through Moses. 📖

## Numbers 10:14

Judah’s camp divisions left first under their standard, led by Nahshon son of Amminadab. 📖

**Help with “standard”:** This is a banner identifying the camp.

## Numbers 10:15

Nethanel son of Zuar led Issachar’s division, 📖

## Numbers 10:16

and Eliab son of Helon led Zebulun’s. 📖

## Numbers 10:17

The tabernacle was taken down. 📖

Gershonites and Merarites set out carrying it.

## Numbers 10:18

Reuben’s camp divisions followed under their standard, led by Elizur son of Shedeur. 📖

## Numbers 10:19

Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai led Simeon’s division, 📖

## Numbers 10:20

and Eliasaph son of Deuel led Gad’s. 📖

## Numbers 10:21

Kohathites followed with the holy objects. 📖

The tabernacle was to be set up before they arrived.

## Numbers 10:22

Ephraim’s camp divisions followed under their standard, led by Elishama son of Ammihud. 📖

## Numbers 10:23

Gamaliel son of Pedahzur led Manasseh’s division, 📖

## Numbers 10:24

and Abidan son of Gideoni led Benjamin’s. 📖

## Numbers 10:25

Dan’s camp divisions left last as rear guard for all units, under their standard. 📖

Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai led them.

## Numbers 10:26

Pagiel son of Ocran led Asher’s division, 📖

## Numbers 10:27

and Ahira son of Enan led Naphtali’s. 📖

## Numbers 10:28

This was the marching order of Israel’s divisions. 📖

## Numbers 10:29

Moses spoke to Hobab, son of Moses’ father-in-law Reuel the Midianite. 📖

“We go where the LORD promised to give us land.

Come, and we will treat you well.

The LORD promised Israel good.”

## Numbers 10:30

Hobab replied, “I will not go. 📖

I will return to my land and people.”

## Numbers 10:31

Moses said, “Please stay. 📖

You know wilderness campsites and can be our eyes.

**Help with this picture:** Moses asks Hobab to guide them with his knowledge.

## Numbers 10:32

If you come, we will share whatever good the LORD gives us.” 📖

## Numbers 10:33

Israel traveled three days from the LORD’s mountain. 📖

The LORD’s covenant ark went ahead those three days to seek a resting place for them.

## Numbers 10:34

The LORD’s cloud was over them by day when they left camp. 📖

## Numbers 10:35

Whenever the ark left, Moses said, “Rise up, O LORD! 📖

May Your enemies be scattered.

May those hating You flee before You.”

## Numbers 10:36

When it rested, he said, “Return, O LORD, to Israel’s countless thousands.” 📖
