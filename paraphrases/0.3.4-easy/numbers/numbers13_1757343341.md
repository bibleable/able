# Numbers 13 — The scouts’ report about Canaan

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends leaders from Israel’s tribes to examine Canaan.

They return with fruit and report what they saw.

Caleb urges the people to enter, but the other men give a frightening report.

## Numbers 13:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 13:2

“Send leaders to scout Canaan, the land I give Israel. 📖

Send one man from each fathers’ tribe.”

## Numbers 13:3

Moses sent them from Paran’s wilderness at the LORD’s command. 📖

All were Israel’s leaders.

## Numbers 13:4

Their names were: Reuben’s Shammua son of Zaccur; 📖

## Numbers 13:5

Simeon’s Shaphat son of Hori; 📖

## Numbers 13:6

Judah’s Caleb son of Jephunneh; 📖

## Numbers 13:7

Issachar’s Igal son of Joseph; 📖

## Numbers 13:8

Ephraim’s Hoshea son of Nun; 📖

## Numbers 13:9

Benjamin’s Palti son of Raphu; 📖

## Numbers 13:10

Zebulun’s Gaddiel son of Sodi; 📖

## Numbers 13:11

Manasseh’s Gaddi son of Susi, from Joseph’s tribe; 📖

## Numbers 13:12

Dan’s Ammiel son of Gemalli; 📖

## Numbers 13:13

Asher’s Sethur son of Michael; 📖

## Numbers 13:14

Naphtali’s Nahbi son of Vophsi; 📖

## Numbers 13:15

Gad’s Geuel son of Machi. 📖

## Numbers 13:16

These were the scouts Moses sent. 📖

He renamed Hoshea son of Nun Joshua.

## Numbers 13:17

He sent them to Canaan, saying, “Go through the Negev into the hill country. 📖

## Numbers 13:18

See what the land is like. 📖

Are the people strong or weak, few or many?

## Numbers 13:19

Is their land good or bad? 📖

Are their cities open camps or fortified places?

**Help with “fortified”:** This means protected by strong defenses.

## Numbers 13:20

Is the soil fruitful or unproductive? 📖

Are there trees?

Be brave and bring fruit back.”

It was the first ripe grapes’ season.

## Numbers 13:21

They scouted from Zin’s wilderness to Rehob, toward Lebo-hamath. 📖

## Numbers 13:22

They crossed the Negev to Hebron, where Anak’s descendants Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai lived. 📖

Hebron was built seven years before Egypt’s Zoan.

## Numbers 13:23

At the Valley of Eshcol, they cut a branch with one grape cluster. 📖

Two men carried it on a pole.

They also took pomegranates and figs.

## Numbers 13:24

The valley was named Eshcol because of the grape cluster Israel cut there. 📖

## Numbers 13:25

They returned after forty days of scouting, 📖

## Numbers 13:26

coming to Moses, Aaron, and all Israel at Kadesh in Paran’s wilderness. 📖

They reported to everyone and showed the fruit.

## Numbers 13:27

They told Moses, “The land you sent us to flows with milk and honey. 📖

Here is its fruit!

**Help with this picture:** Milk and honey picture plentiful good food.

## Numbers 13:28

But the people are strong and their cities large and fortified. 📖

We saw Anak’s descendants.

## Numbers 13:29

Amalekites live in the Negev. 📖

Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites live in the hills.

Canaanites live by the sea and along the Jordan.”

## Numbers 13:30

Caleb quieted them before Moses. 📖

He said, “We must enter and take the land.

We can certainly conquer it!”

## Numbers 13:31

The others replied, “We cannot fight them. 📖

They are stronger than us!”

## Numbers 13:32

They gave Israel a bad report. 📖

“The land devours its inhabitants.

Everyone we saw was very tall.

**About the report:** The men describe the land with an image of devouring people.

## Numbers 13:33

We saw the Nephilim, Anak’s descendants coming from the Nephilim! 📖

We looked like grasshoppers to ourselves and must have looked that way to them!”

**About these words:** Nephilim is the name used in BSB.

The men’s comparison and their guess about others’ view are part of their report.
