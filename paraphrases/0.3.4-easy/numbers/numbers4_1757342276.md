# Numbers 4 — The Levites’ work and transport duties

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives duties to the Kohathite, Gershonite, and Merarite clans.

Aaron and his sons prepare the holy objects before the Kohathites carry them.

The chapter counts workers aged thirty to fifty.

## Numbers 4:1

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 4:2

“Count the Kohathite Levites by clans and families, 📖

## Numbers 4:3

men thirty to fifty qualified to work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:4

Their work concerns the most holy things. 📖

## Numbers 4:5

When camp moves, Aaron and his sons must take down the veil and cover the ark of the Testimony. 📖

## Numbers 4:6

Put fine leather over it, then solid blue cloth, and insert its poles. 📖

## Numbers 4:7

Cover the table of the Presence with blue cloth. 📖

Put its plates, cups, drink-offering bowls, and pitchers on it.

Keep the regular bread offering there.

## Numbers 4:8

Spread scarlet cloth over these, cover them with fine leather, and insert the poles. 📖

**Help with “scarlet”:** This means bright red.

## Numbers 4:9

Cover the lampstand, lamps, wick trimmers, trays, and supply-oil jars with blue cloth. 📖

## Numbers 4:10

Wrap it and all its tools in fine leather and put it on the carrying frame. 📖

## Numbers 4:11

Cover the gold altar with blue cloth, then fine leather, and insert its poles. 📖

## Numbers 4:12

Put all sanctuary-service tools in blue cloth, cover them with fine leather, and place them on the carrying frame. 📖

## Numbers 4:13

Remove the bronze altar’s ashes and cover it with purple cloth, 📖

## Numbers 4:14

placing all its firepans, meat forks, shovels, sprinkling bowls, and other equipment on it. 📖

Cover it with fine leather and insert the poles.

## Numbers 4:15

After Aaron and his sons cover all holy objects and equipment, the Kohathites must carry them when camp moves. 📖

They must not touch the holy objects, or they will die.

These are their Tent of Meeting transport duties.

## Numbers 4:16

Eleazar, Aaron the priest’s son, must oversee lighting oil, fragrant incense, daily grain offerings, and anointing oil. 📖

He oversees the whole tabernacle, including all holy objects and tools.”

## Numbers 4:17

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 4:18

“Do not let the Kohathite clans be cut off from the Levites. 📖

## Numbers 4:19

Aaron and his sons must go in and assign each man his task and load. 📖

Do this so the Kohathites live and do not die near the most holy things.

## Numbers 4:20

The Kohathites must not enter to look at the holy objects, even a moment, or they will die.” 📖

## Numbers 4:21

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 4:22

“Count the Gershonites by families and clans, 📖

## Numbers 4:23

everyone thirty to fifty serving at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:24

The Gershonite clans’ work and carrying duties are these: 📖

## Numbers 4:25

The tabernacle curtains, Tent of Meeting, fine leather covering above it, and entrance curtains, 📖

## Numbers 4:26

and the surrounding courtyard curtains and gate curtains, around the tabernacle and altar. 📖

Carry their ropes and all service equipment.

Do all work needed for these.

## Numbers 4:27

Aaron and his sons direct all Gershonite carrying and other work. 📖

Assign everything they must carry.

## Numbers 4:28

Their Tent of Meeting work is under Ithamar, Aaron the priest’s son. 📖

## Numbers 4:29

Count Merari’s sons by clans and families, 📖

## Numbers 4:30

everyone thirty to fifty serving at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:31

Their service is to carry the tabernacle’s frames, crossbars, posts, and bases, 📖

## Numbers 4:32

and courtyard posts, bases, tent pegs, ropes, and all related equipment. 📖

Assign by name everything they must carry.

## Numbers 4:33

The Merarites’ work at the Tent of Meeting is under Ithamar, Aaron the priest’s son.” 📖

## Numbers 4:34

Moses, Aaron, and the people’s leaders counted the Kohathites by clans and families, 📖

## Numbers 4:35

everyone thirty to fifty serving at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:36

They totaled 2,750 by clans. 📖

## Numbers 4:37

These Kohathites could serve at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

Moses and Aaron counted them by the LORD’s command through Moses.

## Numbers 4:38

They counted the Gershonites by clans and families, 📖

## Numbers 4:39

everyone thirty to fifty serving at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:40

They totaled 2,630 by clans and families. 📖

## Numbers 4:41

Moses and Aaron counted these Gershonite Tent of Meeting workers as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Numbers 4:42

They counted the Merarites by clans and families, 📖

## Numbers 4:43

everyone thirty to fifty serving at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:44

Their men totaled 3,200 by clans. 📖

## Numbers 4:45

Moses and Aaron counted these Merarites by the LORD’s command through Moses. 📖

## Numbers 4:46

Moses, Aaron, and Israel’s leaders counted all the Levites by clans and families, 📖

## Numbers 4:47

from thirty to fifty, serving and carrying for the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:48

They totaled 8,580 men. 📖

## Numbers 4:49

At the LORD’s command through Moses, they were counted and each assigned his work and load. 📖

This was as the LORD commanded Moses.
