# Numbers 6 — The Nazirite vow and the priestly blessing

**About this chapter**

A man or woman may take a Nazirite vow to be set apart for the LORD.

The chapter gives the restrictions, what happens after accidental defilement, and the offerings at completion.

It ends with the blessing Aaron and his sons must speak.

## Numbers 6:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 6:2

“Tell Israel: A man or woman may take the special Nazirite vow to be set apart for the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 6:3

Abstain from wine and strong drink, including vinegar made from either. 📖

Drink no grape juice and eat no fresh grapes or raisins.

## Numbers 6:4

Throughout this time, eat nothing from the grapevine, not even seeds or skins. 📖

## Numbers 6:5

No razor may touch the head throughout the vow. 📖

Remain holy to the LORD until the period ends, letting the hair grow long.

## Numbers 6:6

Do not approach a dead body throughout the separation to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 6:7

Even if father, mother, brother, or sister dies, do not become defiled. 📖

God’s consecration sign is on the head.

## Numbers 6:8

Throughout the separation, the Nazirite is holy to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 6:9

If someone suddenly dies nearby, defiling the consecrated hair, shave the head on the cleansing day, day seven. 📖

## Numbers 6:10

On day eight, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 6:11

The priest must offer one for sin and one as a burnt offering for atonement. 📖

The Nazirite sinned by being near the dead body.

Set the head apart again that day.

## Numbers 6:12

Rededicate the separation time to the LORD and bring a year-old male lamb as a guilt offering. 📖

Earlier days do not count because the separation was defiled.

## Numbers 6:13

When the Nazirite’s separation ends, bring the person to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 6:14

Offer the LORD a year-old male lamb without blemish as a burnt offering. 📖

Offer a year-old female lamb without blemish for sin and an unblemished ram as a peace offering,

## Numbers 6:15

with grain and drink offerings. 📖

Bring a basket of unleavened fine-flour cakes mixed with oil and unleavened wafers coated with oil.

## Numbers 6:16

The priest must present these before the LORD and make the sin and burnt offerings. 📖

## Numbers 6:17

Offer the peace-offering ram to the LORD with the unleavened bread basket. 📖

Present its grain and drink offerings too.

## Numbers 6:18

At the Tent of Meeting’s entrance, the Nazirite must shave the consecrated head. 📖

Put the hair in the fire under the peace offering.

## Numbers 6:19

The priest must take the ram’s boiled shoulder, one unleavened cake from the basket, and one unleavened wafer. 📖

Put them in the freshly shaved Nazirite’s hands.

## Numbers 6:20

Wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖

They are a holy priestly portion, additional to the waved breast and contributed thigh.

Afterward, the Nazirite may drink wine.

## Numbers 6:21

This is the law for the Nazirite’s vowed offering for separation, besides anything else affordable. 📖

Fulfill every vow according to this law.”

## Numbers 6:22

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 6:23

“Tell Aaron and his sons to bless Israel with these words: 📖

## Numbers 6:24

‘May the LORD bless and keep you; 📖

## Numbers 6:25

May the LORD make His face shine on you and be gracious to you; 📖

**Help with this picture:** A shining face pictures favor and kindness.

## Numbers 6:26

May the LORD lift His face toward you and give you peace.’ 📖

## Numbers 6:27

They will put My name on Israel, and I will bless them.” 📖

**About this blessing:** The priests declare the LORD’s blessing over Israel.
