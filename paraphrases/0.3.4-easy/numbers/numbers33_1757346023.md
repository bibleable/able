# Numbers 33 — The stages of Israel’s journey

**About this chapter**

Moses records Israel’s journey stages at the LORD’s command.

Every departure and camp is included in order.

The chapter ends with commands about entering the land and a conditional warning.

## Numbers 33:1

These are Israel’s journeys after leaving Egypt in divisions under Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Numbers 33:2

At the LORD’s command, Moses recorded their journey’s stages by starting points: 📖

## Numbers 33:3

On day fifteen of month one, the day after Passover, Israel left Rameses. 📖

They marched out defiantly before all Egypt,

## Numbers 33:4

while Egyptians buried all their firstborn, whom the LORD struck down. 📖

The LORD had judged their gods.

## Numbers 33:5

Israel left Rameses and camped at Succoth. 📖

## Numbers 33:6

They left Succoth and camped at Etham on the wilderness edge. 📖

## Numbers 33:7

They left Etham, turned back to Pi-hahiroth opposite Baal-zephon, and camped near Migdol. 📖

## Numbers 33:8

They left Pi-hahiroth and crossed through the sea into the wilderness. 📖

They traveled three days into Etham’s wilderness and camped at Marah.

## Numbers 33:9

They left Marah for Elim, where there were twelve springs and seventy palm trees. 📖

They camped there.

## Numbers 33:10

They left Elim and camped by the Red Sea. 📖

## Numbers 33:11

They left the Red Sea and camped in the Desert of Sin. 📖

## Numbers 33:12

They left the Desert of Sin and camped at Dophkah. 📖

## Numbers 33:13

They left Dophkah and camped at Alush. 📖

## Numbers 33:14

They left Alush and camped at Rephidim, where the people had no drinking water. 📖

## Numbers 33:15

They left Rephidim and camped in the Wilderness of Sinai. 📖

## Numbers 33:16

They left the Wilderness of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-hattaavah. 📖

## Numbers 33:17

They left Kibroth-hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth. 📖

## Numbers 33:18

They left Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah. 📖

## Numbers 33:19

They left Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez. 📖

## Numbers 33:20

They left Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah. 📖

## Numbers 33:21

They left Libnah and camped at Rissah. 📖

## Numbers 33:22

They left Rissah and camped at Kehelathah. 📖

## Numbers 33:23

They left Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shepher. 📖

## Numbers 33:24

They left Mount Shepher and camped at Haradah. 📖

## Numbers 33:25

They left Haradah and camped at Makheloth. 📖

## Numbers 33:26

They left Makheloth and camped at Tahath. 📖

## Numbers 33:27

They left Tahath and camped at Terah. 📖

## Numbers 33:28

They left Terah and camped at Mithkah. 📖

## Numbers 33:29

They left Mithkah and camped at Hashmonah. 📖

## Numbers 33:30

They left Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth. 📖

## Numbers 33:31

They left Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan. 📖

## Numbers 33:32

They left Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-haggidgad. 📖

## Numbers 33:33

They left Hor-haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah. 📖

## Numbers 33:34

They left Jotbathah and camped at Abronah. 📖

## Numbers 33:35

They left Abronah and camped at Ezion-geber. 📖

## Numbers 33:36

They left Ezion-geber and camped at Kadesh in Zin’s wilderness. 📖

## Numbers 33:37

They left Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor at Edom’s edge. 📖

## Numbers 33:38

At the LORD’s command, Aaron the priest climbed Mount Hor and died there. 📖

It was day one of month five, in year forty after Israel left Egypt.

## Numbers 33:39

Aaron was 123 when he died on Mount Hor. 📖

## Numbers 33:40

Arad’s Canaanite king, living in the Negev in Canaan, heard Israel was coming. 📖

## Numbers 33:41

Israel left Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah. 📖

## Numbers 33:42

They left Zalmonah and camped at Punon. 📖

## Numbers 33:43

They left Punon and camped at Oboth. 📖

## Numbers 33:44

They left Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim on Moab’s border. 📖

## Numbers 33:45

They left Iyim and camped at Dibon-gad. 📖

## Numbers 33:46

They left Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim. 📖

## Numbers 33:47

They left Almon-diblathaim and camped in Abarim’s mountains facing Nebo. 📖

## Numbers 33:48

They left Abarim’s mountains and camped on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, across from Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 33:49

There on Moab’s plains, they camped by the Jordan from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim. 📖

## Numbers 33:50

On Moab’s plains by the Jordan, across from Jericho, the LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 33:51

“Tell Israel: When you cross the Jordan into Canaan, 📖

## Numbers 33:52

drive out all the land’s inhabitants. 📖

Destroy all their carved images and cast idols and demolish all their high places.

**Help with these words:** Cast idols are worship images made by pouring material into a mold.

High places are worship sites.

## Numbers 33:53

Take the land and settle there, because I gave it to you to possess. 📖

## Numbers 33:54

Divide the land by lot according to clans, giving larger clans larger inheritances and smaller clans smaller ones. 📖

Each will own what falls to him by lot.

You will inherit according to your fathers’ tribes.

**Help with “lot”:** This means using lots to make an allocation, rather than a person choosing.

## Numbers 33:55

If you do not drive out the inhabitants, those remaining will be barbs in your eyes. 📖

They will be thorns in your sides and trouble you in the land where you settle.

**Help with this picture:** Barbs and thorns picture the harm and trouble those remaining will cause.

## Numbers 33:56

Then I will do to you what I planned for them.” 📖
