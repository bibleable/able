# Numbers 16 — Korah’s rebellion and the plague

**About this chapter**

Korah and others challenge Moses and Aaron.

The chapter records death by the opening earth, fire, and plague.

Moses and Aaron plead for the people, and Aaron stands between the living and the dead.

## Numbers 16:1

Korah son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, joined some Reubenites. 📖

These were Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth.

They began

## Numbers 16:2

a rebellion against Moses with 250 men of Israel. 📖

These men were well-known assembly leaders and representatives.

## Numbers 16:3

They gathered against Moses and Aaron, saying, “You have taken too much on yourselves! 📖

Everyone in the assembly is holy, and the LORD is among them.

Why raise yourselves above the LORD’s assembly?”

## Numbers 16:4

Moses heard and fell facedown. 📖

## Numbers 16:5

He told Korah and his followers, “Tomorrow morning the LORD will show who belongs to Him and who is holy. 📖

He will bring that person near Himself.

He will bring His chosen person near Himself.

## Numbers 16:6

Korah, you and all your followers must take censers, 📖

**Help with “censers”:** These are containers for burning incense.

## Numbers 16:7

and tomorrow put fire and incense in them before the LORD. 📖

The man He chooses will be holy.

You sons of Levi have taken too much on yourselves!”

## Numbers 16:8

Moses also told Korah, “Listen, sons of Levi! 📖

## Numbers 16:9

Is it not enough that Israel’s God separated you from Israel’s assembly and brought you near Himself? 📖

You work at the LORD’s tabernacle and stand before the assembly to serve them.

## Numbers 16:10

He brought you and your fellow Levites near, but you seek the priesthood too. 📖

## Numbers 16:11

You and your followers have joined against the LORD! 📖

Who is Aaron, that you should complain against him?”

## Numbers 16:12

Moses summoned Dathan and Abiram, Eliab’s sons. 📖

They replied, “We will not come!

## Numbers 16:13

Was bringing us from a land flowing with milk and honey to die in the wilderness not enough? 📖

Must you make yourself our ruler too?

**About their accusation:** They describe Egypt as a land flowing with milk and honey.

## Numbers 16:14

You have not brought us to a land flowing with milk and honey. 📖

You have not given us fields and vineyards to inherit.

Will you gouge out these men’s eyes?

We will not come!”

## Numbers 16:15

Moses became very angry and told the LORD, “Do not regard their offering. 📖

I took no donkey from them and mistreated none of them.”

## Numbers 16:16

Moses told Korah, “Tomorrow you, all your followers, and Aaron must appear before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 16:17

Each man must bring his censer with incense before the LORD: 250 censers. 📖

You and Aaron must bring yours too.”

## Numbers 16:18

Each took his censer with fire and incense. 📖

They stood with Moses and Aaron at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance.

## Numbers 16:19

Korah gathered his whole assembly against them at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

The LORD’s glory appeared to the whole assembly.

## Numbers 16:20

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 16:21

“Separate yourselves from this assembly so I may consume them in an instant.” 📖

## Numbers 16:22

Moses and Aaron fell facedown, saying, “O God, God of the spirits of all flesh! 📖

When one man sins, will You be angry with the whole assembly?”

**Help with “all flesh”:** This expression refers to living beings.

## Numbers 16:23

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 16:24

“Tell the assembly to move away from Korah’s, Dathan’s, and Abiram’s homes.” 📖

## Numbers 16:25

Moses rose and went to Dathan and Abiram, followed by Israel’s elders. 📖

## Numbers 16:26

He warned, “Move away from these wicked men’s tents. 📖

Touch nothing belonging to them, or you will be swept away because of all their sins.”

## Numbers 16:27

They moved away from Korah’s, Dathan’s, and Abiram’s homes. 📖

Dathan and Abiram stood at their tent entrances with their wives, children, and infants.

## Numbers 16:28

Moses said, “This will show that the LORD sent me to do all this. 📖

It was not my own doing.

## Numbers 16:29

If these men die naturally or suffer everyone’s usual fate, the LORD has not sent me. 📖

## Numbers 16:30

But suppose the LORD does something never done before. 📖

The earth opens its mouth and swallows them and all they own, taking them alive into Sheol.

Then you will know they treated the LORD with contempt.”

**Help with “Sheol”:** This is the name used here for the place of the dead.

## Numbers 16:31

As soon as Moses finished speaking, the ground beneath them split, 📖

## Numbers 16:32

and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them, their households, all Korah’s men, and all their possessions. 📖

## Numbers 16:33

They went alive into Sheol with all they owned. 📖

The earth closed over them, and they vanished from the assembly.

## Numbers 16:34

Everyone nearby in Israel fled at their cries. 📖

They said, “The earth may swallow us too!”

## Numbers 16:35

Fire from the LORD consumed the 250 men offering incense. 📖

## Numbers 16:36

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 16:37

“Tell Eleazar son of Aaron the priest to remove the censers from the flames. 📖

Scatter the coals far away, because the censers are holy.

## Numbers 16:38

These men sinned at the cost of their lives. 📖

Hammer their censers into sheets to cover the altar.

They became holy by being presented before the LORD and will be a sign to Israel.”

## Numbers 16:39

Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers of the men burned up. 📖

He had them hammered into sheets to cover the altar,

## Numbers 16:40

as the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖

This reminded Israel that no outsider, not descended from Aaron, should approach to offer incense before the LORD.

Otherwise he would become like Korah and his followers.

## Numbers 16:41

Next day, all Israel’s assembly complained to Moses and Aaron, “You killed the LORD’s people!” 📖

## Numbers 16:42

The assembly gathered against them. 📖

Moses and Aaron turned toward the Tent of Meeting.

Suddenly the cloud covered it, and the LORD’s glory appeared.

## Numbers 16:43

Moses and Aaron went before the Tent of Meeting, 📖

## Numbers 16:44

and the LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 16:45

“Get away from this assembly so I may consume them in an instant.” 📖

Moses and Aaron fell facedown.

## Numbers 16:46

Moses told Aaron, “Put altar fire and incense in your censer. 📖

Hurry to the assembly and make atonement for them.

Wrath has gone out from the LORD.

The plague has begun.”

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God through the appointed offering.

## Numbers 16:47

Aaron took the censer as Moses ordered and ran among the assembly. 📖

He saw the plague had begun, offered incense, and made atonement for the people.

## Numbers 16:48

He stood between the living and the dead, and the plague stopped. 📖

## Numbers 16:49

The plague killed 14,700 people, besides those who died because of Korah. 📖

## Numbers 16:50

With the plague stopped, Aaron returned to Moses at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
