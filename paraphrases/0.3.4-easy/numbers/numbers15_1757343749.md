# Numbers 15 — Offerings, deliberate sin, and reminders

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives offering rules for life in the land.

The chapter distinguishes unintended sin from defiant sin.

It also records a man’s execution and instructions about clothing tassels.

## Numbers 15:1

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 15:2

“Tell Israel: After you enter the land I give you as a home 📖

## Numbers 15:3

and bring a food offering from herd or flock, it is to give the LORD a pleasing aroma. 📖

This may be a burnt offering or sacrifice for a special vow, voluntary offering, or appointed feast.

## Numbers 15:4

Also bring a grain offering of one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour. 📖

Mix it with a quarter hin of olive oil.

**Help with these measures:** An ephah measures dry goods.

A hin measures liquids.

## Numbers 15:5

For each lamb’s burnt offering or sacrifice, prepare a quarter hin of wine as a drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 15:6

For a ram, prepare two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour with a third hin of olive oil, 📖

## Numbers 15:7

and a third hin of wine as a drink offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:8

When preparing a young bull as a burnt offering or sacrifice for a vow or peace offering to the LORD, 📖

## Numbers 15:9

bring three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with half a hin of olive oil. 📖

## Numbers 15:10

Also bring half a hin of wine as a drink offering. 📖

This is a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

## Numbers 15:11

Do this for each bull, ram, lamb, or goat. 📖

## Numbers 15:12

Prepare each one this way, however many there are. 📖

## Numbers 15:13

Every native-born person must prepare food offerings this way as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:14

In coming generations, a resident foreigner or anyone else among you may want to bring a food offering. 📖

For this pleasing aroma to the LORD, they must do exactly as you do.

## Numbers 15:15

The assembly must have the same rule for you and resident foreigners, permanently through coming generations. 📖

You and the foreigner are the same before the LORD.

## Numbers 15:16

The same law and ruling apply to you and the foreigner living among you.” 📖

## Numbers 15:17

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 15:18

“Tell Israel: When you enter the land I am bringing you to 📖

## Numbers 15:19

and eat its food, lift up an offering to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:20

From your first dough, lift up a cake as a contribution. 📖

Offer it like an offering from the threshing floor.

**Help with “threshing floor”:** This is where grain is separated from its stalks.

## Numbers 15:21

Throughout your generations, give the LORD an offering from your first dough. 📖

## Numbers 15:22

If you stray without intending to and fail to obey all these commands the LORD spoke to Moses— 📖

## Numbers 15:23

all He commanded through Moses, from the day He gave them and through coming generations— 📖

## Numbers 15:24

and the assembly did this unknowingly, the whole assembly must bring one young bull as a burnt offering. 📖

It is a pleasing aroma to the LORD, with its required grain and drink offerings.

Also bring one male goat as a sin offering.

## Numbers 15:25

The priest must make atonement for all Israel’s assembly so they may be forgiven. 📖

The sin was unintended.

They brought food and sin offerings before the LORD for it.

**Help with “atonement”:** This concerns putting things right with God through the appointed offering.

## Numbers 15:26

All Israel’s assembly and the foreigners living among them will be forgiven. 📖

It happened to everyone without their intending it.

## Numbers 15:27

If one person sins without intending to, bring a year-old female goat as a sin offering. 📖

## Numbers 15:28

The priest must make atonement before the LORD for that person’s unintended sin. 📖

After atonement, the person will be forgiven.

## Numbers 15:29

The same law applies to anyone acting in error, whether native-born Israelite or resident foreigner. 📖

## Numbers 15:30

But anyone sinning defiantly, native or foreigner, insults the LORD. 📖

That person must be cut off from the people.

**Help with “defiantly”:** This means deliberately refusing to obey.

## Numbers 15:31

He must certainly be cut off because he despised the LORD’s word and broke His command. 📖

His guilt remains on him.”

## Numbers 15:32

While Israel was in the wilderness, a man was found gathering wood on the Sabbath day. 📖

**Help with “Sabbath”:** This is the weekly day set apart for rest.

## Numbers 15:33

Those finding him brought him to Moses, Aaron, and the whole assembly. 📖

## Numbers 15:34

They held him in custody because what should be done to him had not been declared. 📖

## Numbers 15:35

The LORD told Moses, “The man must be put to death. 📖

The whole assembly must stone him outside camp.”

## Numbers 15:36

The whole assembly took him outside camp and stoned him to death, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 15:37

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 15:38

“Tell Israel to make tassels on their clothing’s corners throughout coming generations. 📖

Put a blue cord on each tassel.

**Help with “tassels”:** These are hanging bundles of threads.

## Numbers 15:39

Look at these tassels to remember and obey all the LORD’s commands. 📖

Do not prostitute yourselves by following your own heart and eyes.

**Help with this picture:** Prostitution is used here as an image of unfaithfulness to God.

## Numbers 15:40

Then you will remember and obey all My commands and be holy to your God. 📖

## Numbers 15:41

I am the LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt to be your God. 📖

I am the LORD your God.”
