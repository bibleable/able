# Numbers 9 — Passover and the guiding cloud

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Passover instructions, including a later date for people unable to observe the first date.

The cloud guides Israel’s camping and travel.

The repeated waiting and departure conditions are included.

## Numbers 9:1

In month one of year two after Israel left Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses in Sinai’s wilderness. 📖

## Numbers 9:2

He said, “Israel must keep Passover at its appointed time. 📖

## Numbers 9:3

Keep it at its appointed time: twilight on day fourteen of this month. 📖

Follow all its laws and rulings.”

## Numbers 9:4

Moses told Israel to keep Passover, 📖

## Numbers 9:5

and they did, at twilight on day fourteen of month one in Sinai’s wilderness. 📖

They did everything the LORD commanded Moses.

## Numbers 9:6

Some men were unclean because of a dead body and could not keep Passover that day. 📖

They came to Moses and Aaron that same day

**Help with “unclean”:** This describes a condition under worship rules, not a judgment about someone’s worth.

## Numbers 9:7

and asked Moses, “We are unclean from a dead body. 📖

Why exclude us from the LORD’s offering with Israel at the appointed time?”

## Numbers 9:8

Moses replied, “Wait here until I learn the LORD’s command about you.” 📖

## Numbers 9:9

The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 9:10

“Tell Israel: Anyone among you or your descendants unclean from a dead body, or away traveling, may still keep Passover. 📖

## Numbers 9:11

Observe it at twilight on day fourteen of month two. 📖

Eat the lamb with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.

**Help with “unleavened”:** This means bread made without yeast.

## Numbers 9:12

Leave none until morning and break no bones. 📖

Follow all Passover laws.

## Numbers 9:13

But anyone clean and not traveling who still neglects Passover must be cut off from his people. 📖

He failed to present the LORD’s offering at the appointed time and bears his sin’s consequences.

## Numbers 9:14

A resident foreigner wanting the LORD’s Passover must follow its laws and rulings. 📖

Apply the same law to foreigners and natives.”

## Numbers 9:15

When the tabernacle, the Tent of the Testimony, was set up, the cloud covered it. 📖

From evening to morning, it appeared like fire above it.

## Numbers 9:16

This continued: cloud covered it by day and appeared like fire at night. 📖

## Numbers 9:17

When the cloud lifted from above the tent, Israel set out. 📖

Where it settled, they camped.

## Numbers 9:18

They traveled and camped at the LORD’s command. 📖

They stayed as long as the cloud remained over the tabernacle.

## Numbers 9:19

Even if it stayed many days, they obeyed the LORD and did not leave. 📖

## Numbers 9:20

If it stayed a few days, they camped and set out at the LORD’s command. 📖

## Numbers 9:21

If it stayed from evening to morning, they left when it lifted in the morning. 📖

Whenever it rose, day or night, they left.

## Numbers 9:22

If it stayed two days, a month, or longer, they stayed camped while it remained. 📖

When it lifted, they left.

## Numbers 9:23

They camped and traveled at the LORD’s command, keeping His charge as He commanded through Moses. 📖
