# Numbers 14 — Refusal, judgment, and defeat

**About this chapter**

The people refuse to enter the land.

Moses pleads, and the LORD pardons them while announcing consequences.

The people then try to go up despite the warning not to.

## Numbers 14:1

Everyone cried out and wept that night. 📖

## Numbers 14:2

All Israel complained to Moses and Aaron. 📖

“If only we had died in Egypt or this wilderness!

## Numbers 14:3

Why does the LORD bring us here to die by swords? 📖

Our wives and children will be plunder.

Would returning to Egypt not be better?”

## Numbers 14:4

They said, “Appoint a leader and return to Egypt.” 📖

## Numbers 14:5

Moses and Aaron fell facedown before all Israel’s assembly. 📖

## Numbers 14:6

Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, both scouts, tore their clothes 📖

## Numbers 14:7

and told everyone, “We explored an exceedingly good land. 📖

## Numbers 14:8

If the LORD delights in us, He will bring us there and give us the land. 📖

It flows with milk and honey.

**Help with this picture:** Milk and honey picture plentiful good food.

## Numbers 14:9

Do not rebel against the LORD or fear the land’s people. 📖

They will be like bread for us.

Their protection is gone, and the LORD is with us.

Do not fear them!”

**Help with “bread for us”:** This pictures the people as easily overcome.

## Numbers 14:10

Everyone threatened to stone Joshua and Caleb. 📖

The LORD’s glory appeared to all Israel at the Tent of Meeting.

## Numbers 14:11

The LORD asked Moses, “How long will they treat Me with contempt? 📖

How long will they refuse to believe in Me despite all My signs?

**Help with “contempt”:** This means treating someone as worthless.

## Numbers 14:12

I will strike them with a plague and destroy them. 📖

I will make you a greater, stronger nation than they are.”

## Numbers 14:13

Moses said, “Egypt will hear. 📖

Your strength brought these people out from among them.

## Numbers 14:14

They will tell this land’s inhabitants. 📖

They already heard You, O LORD, are among Your people and have been seen face to face.

Your cloud stands over them.

You lead in a cloud pillar by day and fire pillar at night.

## Numbers 14:15

If You kill these people as one man, nations hearing Your fame will say, 📖

## Numbers 14:16

‘The LORD could not bring them to the land He swore to give. 📖

He slaughtered them in the wilderness.’

## Numbers 14:17

May my Lord’s power be magnified, as You declared: 📖

## Numbers 14:18

‘The LORD is slow to anger and full of faithful love, forgiving wrongdoing and rebellion. 📖

Yet He never leaves the guilty unpunished.

He punishes fathers’ wrongdoing in their children to the third and fourth generation.’

## Numbers 14:19

Please pardon their wrongdoing by Your great faithful love, as You forgave them ever since Egypt.” 📖

## Numbers 14:20

The LORD replied, “I pardoned them as you asked. 📖

## Numbers 14:21

Yet as surely as I live and the whole earth is full of the LORD’s glory, 📖

## Numbers 14:22

Those men saw My glory and signs in Egypt and the wilderness. 📖

Yet they tested Me and disobeyed Me these ten times.

## Numbers 14:23

Not one will see the land I swore to their fathers. 📖

None treating Me with contempt will see it.

## Numbers 14:24

My servant Caleb has a different spirit and follows Me wholeheartedly. 📖

I will bring him into the land he entered, and his descendants will inherit it.

## Numbers 14:25

Amalekites and Canaanites live in the valleys. 📖

Tomorrow turn back toward the wilderness by the Red Sea route.”

## Numbers 14:26

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 14:27

“How long will this wicked assembly complain against Me? 📖

I heard Israel’s complaints.

## Numbers 14:28

Tell them: As surely as I live, says the LORD, I will do exactly what I heard you say. 📖

## Numbers 14:29

All counted in the census, twenty or older, will die in this wilderness because you complained against Me. 📖

## Numbers 14:30

None will enter the land I swore to settle you in. 📖

The exceptions are Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

## Numbers 14:31

I will bring your children, whom you called plunder, into the land you rejected. 📖

They will enjoy it.

## Numbers 14:32

Your bodies will fall in this wilderness. 📖

## Numbers 14:33

Your children will be wilderness shepherds forty years, suffering for your unfaithfulness until your last body lies there. 📖

## Numbers 14:34

For forty scouting days, bear guilt forty years, one year per day. 📖

You will experience My alienation.

**Help with “alienation”:** This means separation in the relationship.

## Numbers 14:35

I, the LORD, spoke and will do this to the whole wicked assembly conspiring against Me. 📖

They will end and die in the wilderness.”

## Numbers 14:36

The scouts Moses sent returned a bad report and made everyone complain against him, 📖

## Numbers 14:37

and those men died by plague before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 14:38

Only Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh survived among the scouts. 📖

## Numbers 14:39

Moses told all Israel these words, and they mourned bitterly. 📖

## Numbers 14:40

Early next morning, they went toward the hill country’s ridge. 📖

“We sinned,” they said, “but will go where the LORD promised.”

## Numbers 14:41

Moses said, “Why break the LORD’s command? 📖

This will not succeed!

## Numbers 14:42

Do not go up. 📖

The LORD is not among you, and enemies will strike you.

## Numbers 14:43

Amalekites and Canaanites will face you, and you will fall by swords. 📖

You turned from the LORD, and He will not be with you.”

## Numbers 14:44

They dared to climb the ridge. 📖

Neither Moses nor the LORD’s covenant ark left camp.

## Numbers 14:45

The Amalekites and Canaanites living in those hills came down and attacked. 📖

They routed Israel all the way to Hormah.
