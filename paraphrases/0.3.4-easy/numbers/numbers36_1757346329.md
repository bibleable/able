# Numbers 36 — Inherited land and marriage within the tribe

**About this chapter**

Gilead’s family heads raise a question about Zelophehad’s daughters’ inheritance.

The LORD’s instruction allows their choice of husband within their father’s tribe.

The daughters obey, and their inherited land stays within that tribe.

## Numbers 36:1

The family heads of Gilead’s clan approached Moses and Israel’s family leaders. 📖

Gilead was Machir’s son, and Machir was Manasseh’s son.

This was one of Joseph’s clans.

They said,

## Numbers 36:2

“The LORD commanded my lord to give Israel inherited land by lot. 📖

He also commanded you to give our brother Zelophehad’s inheritance to his daughters.

## Numbers 36:3

If they marry men of other Israelite tribes, their inheritance will leave our fathers’ portion and join their husbands’ tribe. 📖

Our allotted inheritance will be taken away.

## Numbers 36:4

When Israel’s Jubilee comes, their inheritance will join the tribe they married into and leave our fathers’ tribe.” 📖

**Help with “Jubilee”:** This is the special year for release and return of family land.

## Numbers 36:5

At the LORD’s word, Moses commanded Israel, “Joseph’s tribe is right. 📖

## Numbers 36:6

The LORD says Zelophehad’s daughters may marry anyone they please, provided they marry within a clan of their father’s tribe. 📖

## Numbers 36:7

No inheritance may move between Israel’s tribes. 📖

Each Israelite must keep the inheritance of his fathers’ tribe.

## Numbers 36:8

Every daughter inheriting from an Israelite tribe must marry within a clan of her father’s tribe. 📖

Then every Israelite will possess his fathers’ inheritance.

## Numbers 36:9

No inheritance may move from one tribe to another. 📖

Each Israelite tribe must keep its inheritance.”

## Numbers 36:10

Zelophehad’s daughters obeyed the LORD’s command to Moses. 📖

## Numbers 36:11

Zelophehad’s daughters Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah married cousins on their father’s side. 📖

## Numbers 36:12

They married within Manasseh son of Joseph’s descendant clans. 📖

Their inheritance stayed within their father’s clan’s tribe.

## Numbers 36:13

These are the commands and rulings the LORD gave Israel through Moses. 📖

He gave them on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, across from Jericho.
