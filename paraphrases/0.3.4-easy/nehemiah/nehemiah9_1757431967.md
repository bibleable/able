# Nehemiah 9 — Confession and the prayer of remembrance

**About this chapter**

The people confess and worship. | Levites recall God’s creation, promises, rescue, patience, and mercy alongside Israel’s repeated rebellion. | The prayer ends with present distress and a written agreement.

## Nehemiah 9:1

On the same month’s day 24, Israel gathered fasting, wearing sackcloth, with dust on their heads. 📖

**Help with sackcloth and dust:** Signs of grief and humility.

## Nehemiah 9:2

Israel’s descendants separated from all foreigners and confessed their own and ancestors’ sins. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:3

They stood and read the LORD their God’s Law for one-quarter of the day. 📖

They spent another quarter confessing and worshipping.

## Nehemiah 9:4

Levites Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani loudly cried to the LORD from the platform. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:5

Levites Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah said, “Stand and bless the LORD forever. 📖

Blessed be your glorious name, exalted above all blessing and praise.

## Nehemiah 9:6

You alone are the LORD. 📖

You made heaven, highest heavens and their host, earth and everything on it, and seas and everything in them.

You give all things life, and heaven’s host worships you.

**Help with host:** A great multitude.

## Nehemiah 9:7

You chose Abram, brought him from Ur of the Chaldeans, and named him Abraham. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:8

You found his heart faithful and covenanted to give his descendants the land. 📖

It belonged to Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Jebusites, and Girgashites.

You kept your promise because you are righteous.

**Help with covenanted:** Made a binding agreement.

## Nehemiah 9:9

You saw our ancestors’ suffering in Egypt and heard their Red Sea cry. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:10

You worked signs and wonders against Pharaoh, officials, and his people, knowing their pride against our ancestors. 📖

Your great name remains to this day.

## Nehemiah 9:11

You divided the sea for a dry-ground crossing. 📖

You threw pursuers into depths like a stone in raging waters.

## Nehemiah 9:12

Cloud pillar by day and fire pillar by night led and lit their way. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:13

You descended on Sinai, spoke from heaven, and gave just rules, true laws, and good commands. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:14

You revealed holy Sabbath and gave commands and laws through Moses your servant. 📖

**Help with Sabbath:** The weekly day of rest.

## Nehemiah 9:15

You fed their hunger with heaven’s bread and their thirst with rock water. 📖

You told them to take the land you swore to give.

## Nehemiah 9:16

Our ancestors grew proud and stiff-necked and disobeyed. 📖

**Help with stiff-necked:** Stubbornly refusing to obey.

## Nehemiah 9:17

They refused to listen and failed to remember your wonders. 📖

They stiffened their necks and appointed a leader to return them to Egypt’s slavery.

But you are a forgiving God, gracious, compassionate, slow to anger, and rich in faithful love.

You did not abandon them.

## Nehemiah 9:18

They even cast a calf image and said, ‘This is your God who brought you from Egypt.’ 📖

They committed terrible insults against you.

## Nehemiah 9:19

Yet in your great compassion you did not abandon them in the wilderness. 📖

Cloud and fire pillars kept guiding their way day and night.

## Nehemiah 9:20

Your good Spirit taught them. 📖

You gave manna for food and water for thirst.

**Help with manna:** The food God supplied in the wilderness.

## Nehemiah 9:21

For 40 wilderness years they lacked nothing. 📖

Clothes never wore out, and feet never swelled.

## Nehemiah 9:22

You gave kingdoms and peoples, assigning every corner of the land. 📖

They took Sihon king of Heshbon’s and Og king of Bashan’s lands.

## Nehemiah 9:23

You multiplied descendants like heaven’s stars and brought them to their ancestors’ promised land. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:24

Their descendants possessed it. 📖

You subdued Canaanites and handed kings and peoples to them to do as they wished.

## Nehemiah 9:25

They took defended cities, fertile land, stocked houses, dug wells, vineyards, olive groves, and plentiful fruit trees. 📖

They ate fully, grew fat, and delighted in your goodness.

## Nehemiah 9:26

They rebelled, threw your Law behind their backs, and killed prophets urging return. 📖

They terribly insulted you.

**Help with Law behind their backs:** This pictures deliberate rejection.

## Nehemiah 9:27

You handed them to oppressors. 📖

In distress they cried, and you heard from heaven and compassionately sent rescuers.

## Nehemiah 9:28

With rest, they did evil again. 📖

You abandoned them to enemies’ rule.

Repeated cries met your heavenly hearing and compassionate rescue many times.

## Nehemiah 9:29

You urged return to your Law, but they proudly disobeyed. 📖

They sinned against rules giving life to whoever practises them.

They turned a stubborn shoulder, stiffened their necks, and refused obedience.

**Help with shoulder and neck:** Images of stubborn refusal.

## Nehemiah 9:30

For many years your Spirit patiently warned through prophets. 📖

They refused to listen, so you handed them to neighbouring peoples.

## Nehemiah 9:31

Your great compassion did not destroy or abandon them. 📖

You are gracious and compassionate.

## Nehemiah 9:32

Our great, mighty, awesome God, you keep your gracious covenant. 📖

Do not regard lightly our hardship from Assyria’s kings’ days until now.

Kings, leaders, priests, prophets, ancestors, and all your people have suffered.

## Nehemiah 9:33

You acted justly and faithfully in everything that happened. 📖

We acted wickedly.

## Nehemiah 9:34

Our kings, leaders, priests, and ancestors disobeyed your Law, commands, and warnings. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:35

Even with their kingdom, your abundant goodness, and spacious fertile land, they refused service or turning from evil. 📖

## Nehemiah 9:36

Now we are slaves in the land you gave our ancestors for its good produce. 📖

We are slaves!

## Nehemiah 9:37

Its abundant harvest goes to kings you placed over us because of our sins. 📖

They control our bodies and livestock as they please.

We suffer greatly.

## Nehemiah 9:38

Because of all this, we write a binding agreement, sealed with our leaders’, Levites’, and priests’ names.” 📖
