## Nehemiah 10:28

“The remaining people include priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and those separating from surrounding peoples to obey God’s Law. 📖

Their wives, sons, and daughters able to understand join them,

## Nehemiah 10:29

joining noble brothers in a sworn oath to follow God’s Law through Moses. 📖

We will carefully keep every command, rule, and law of the LORD our Lord.

## Nehemiah 10:30

We will not marry our daughters to the land’s peoples or take their daughters for our sons. 📖

## Nehemiah 10:31

We will not buy goods or grain they bring on Sabbath or holy days. 📖

Every 7th year, fields will rest and every debt be cancelled.

## Nehemiah 10:32

We will give one-third of a shekel yearly for God’s house’s service: 📖

**Help with shekel:** A unit of weight used for silver.

## Nehemiah 10:33

It pays for showbread, regular grain and burnt offerings, Sabbath, New Moon, and appointed-feast offerings. 📖

It pays for holy offerings, sin offerings for Israel’s atonement, and all duties of our God’s house.

**Help with these words:** Showbread is bread set before the LORD.

Atonement concerns dealing with sin and restoring relationship with God.

## Nehemiah 10:34

Priests, Levites, and people cast lots for families’ yearly wood donations at appointed times. 📖

We bring wood to burn on the LORD our God’s altar as the Law requires.

**Help with lots:** A way of assigning duties by drawing marked objects.

## Nehemiah 10:35

We will bring firstfruits of our land and every fruit tree to the LORD’s house annually. 📖

**Help with firstfruits:** The first produce.

## Nehemiah 10:36

We bring firstborn sons and livestock as the Law requires, and firstborn herds and flocks to God’s house’s priests. 📖

**About the firstborn:** This verse says they are brought according to the Law.

It does not say sons are killed.

## Nehemiah 10:37

We bring priests firstfruits of dough, grain offerings, all tree fruit, new wine, and oil in temple storerooms. 📖

Levites receive one-tenth of produce in every town where we work.

## Nehemiah 10:38

An Aaron-line priest accompanies Levites collecting tithes. 📖

Levites bring one-tenth of those tithes to God’s house’s treasury storerooms.

**Help with tithe:** One-tenth.

## Nehemiah 10:39

Israelites and Levites bring grain, new wine, and oil to storerooms holding sanctuary objects. 📖

Serving priests, gatekeepers, and singers stay there.

We will not neglect our God’s house.”
