# Nehemiah 8 — The people hear and understand the Law

**About this chapter**

Ezra reads Moses’s Law before the people. | Levites explain it, and the people are urged to rejoice and share food. | They keep the feast in shelters.

## Nehemiah 8:1

Everyone gathered in the square before Water Gate and asked scribe Ezra to bring Moses’s Book of the Law. 📖

The LORD commanded it for Israel.

## Nehemiah 8:2

On month 7, day 1, Ezra the priest brought it before men, women, and everyone able to listen and understand. 📖

## Nehemiah 8:3

He read from dawn until noon before Water Gate’s square, facing men, women, and everyone understanding. 📖

Everyone listened attentively.

## Nehemiah 8:4

He stood on a wooden platform built for this. 📖

On his right stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah.

On his left stood Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hash-baddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.

## Nehemiah 8:5

He opened the book where all could see, standing above them. 📖

Everyone stood when he opened it.

## Nehemiah 8:6

Ezra blessed the great LORD God. 📖

Everyone raised hands and said, “Amen, Amen!”

They bowed facedown and worshipped.

**Help with Amen:** Agreement, meaning “let it be so”.

## Nehemiah 8:7

The Levites were Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, and Pelaiah. 📖

They taught the Law while people stood in place.

## Nehemiah 8:8

They read God’s Law, explained it, and gave understanding of what was read. 📖

## Nehemiah 8:9

Governor Nehemiah, priest-scribe Ezra, and teaching Levites said, “Today is holy to the LORD your God. 📖

Do not mourn or weep.”

Everyone had wept at the Law.

## Nehemiah 8:10

Nehemiah said, “Eat rich food, drink sweet drinks, and send portions to those with nothing prepared. 📖

Today is holy to our Lord.

Do not grieve, because the LORD’s joy is your strength.”

## Nehemiah 8:11

Levites calmed everyone, saying, “Be still; today is holy. 📖

Do not grieve.”

## Nehemiah 8:12

People ate, drank, shared portions, and greatly rejoiced because they understood the words explained to them. 📖

## Nehemiah 8:13

On day 2, family heads, priests, and Levites gathered to Ezra to study the Law. 📖

## Nehemiah 8:14

They found the LORD’s command through Moses to live in shelters during month 7’s feast, 📖

## Nehemiah 8:15

They were to announce this in their towns and Jerusalem. 📖

“Bring olive, wild olive, myrtle, palm, and other leafy branches from the hills.

Make shelters as written.”

## Nehemiah 8:16

They made shelters on rooftops, in courtyards, in God’s house’s courts, and in Water Gate’s and Ephraim Gate’s squares. 📖

## Nehemiah 8:17

All returned exiles made and lived in shelters with great joy. 📖

Israel had not celebrated like this since Joshua son of Nun.

## Nehemiah 8:18

Ezra read God’s Law daily from first to last. 📖

They kept the feast 7 days and held the required assembly on day 8.
