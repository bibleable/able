# Nehemiah 11 — Residents of Jerusalem and other towns

**About this chapter**

Leaders and selected people live in Jerusalem. | The record names families, officers, service groups, and other settlements. | Family lines and totals follow the source.

## Nehemiah 11:1

Leaders lived in Jerusalem. 📖

Others cast lots for 1 in 10 to live in the holy city, with 9 remaining in their towns.

## Nehemiah 11:2

People blessed those volunteering to live in Jerusalem. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:3

These provincial heads settled in Jerusalem. 📖

In Judah’s villages, Israelites, priests, Levites, temple servants, and Solomon’s servants’ descendants lived on their own property.

## Nehemiah 11:4

Some Judah and Benjamin descendants lived in Jerusalem. 📖

Judah included Athaiah son of Uzziah, grandson of Zechariah.

Zechariah was Amariah’s son, Amariah was Shephatiah’s son, and Shephatiah was Mahalalel’s son, descended from Perez.

## Nehemiah 11:5

Maaseiah was son of Baruch, grandson of Col-hozeh. 📖

Col-hozeh was Hazaiah’s son, Hazaiah was Adaiah’s son, Adaiah was Joiarib’s son, and Joiarib was Zechariah’s son.

Maaseiah descended from Shelah.

## Nehemiah 11:6

Perez’s Jerusalem descendants numbered 468 brave men. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:7

Benjamin included Sallu son of Meshullam, grandson of Joed. 📖

Joed was Pedaiah’s son, Pedaiah was Kolaiah’s son, and Kolaiah was Maaseiah’s son.

Maaseiah was Ithiel’s son, and Ithiel was Jeshaiah’s son.

## Nehemiah 11:8

His followers Gabbai and Sallai numbered 928 men. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:9

Joel son of Zichri led them. 📖

Judah son of Hassenuah managed the city’s Second District.

## Nehemiah 11:10

Priests included Jedaiah son of Joiarib and Jachin, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:11

and Seraiah son of Hilkiah, grandson of Meshullam. 📖

Meshullam was Zadok’s son, Zadok was Meraioth’s son, and Meraioth was Ahitub’s son.

Seraiah was God’s house’s chief official.

## Nehemiah 11:12

Their temple-working associates numbered 822. 📖

Adaiah was son of Jeroham, grandson of Pelaliah.

Pelaliah was Amzi’s son, Amzi was Zechariah’s son, Zechariah was Pashhur’s son, and Pashhur was Malchijah’s son.

## Nehemiah 11:13

His associated family heads numbered 242. 📖

Amashai was son of Azarel, grandson of Ahzai, great-grandson of Meshillemoth.

Meshillemoth was Immer’s son.

## Nehemiah 11:14

His associates numbered 128 brave warriors under overseer Zabdiel son of Haggedolim. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:15

Levites included Shemaiah son of Hasshub, grandson of Azrikam, great-grandson of Hashabiah. 📖

Hashabiah was Bunni’s son.

## Nehemiah 11:16

and Shabbethai and Jozabad, Levite leaders supervising work outside God’s house, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:17

Mattaniah son of Mica, grandson of Zabdi, great-grandson of Asaph, who led thanks and prayer. 📖

Bakbukiah was second among associates.

Abda was son of Shammua, grandson of Galal, great-grandson of Jeduthun.

## Nehemiah 11:18

The holy city had 284 Levites. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:19

Gatekeepers Akkub, Talmon, and associates numbered 172. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:20

Other Israelites, priests, and Levites lived throughout Judah’s villages on their own inherited property. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:21

Ophel’s temple servants were led by Ziha and Gishpa. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:22

Jerusalem’s Levite overseer Uzzi was son of Bani, grandson of Hashabiah. 📖

Hashabiah was Mattaniah’s son, and Mattaniah was Mica’s son.

Uzzi descended from Asaph, whose singers managed God’s house’s service.

## Nehemiah 11:23

The king ordered and regulated the singers’ daily duties. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:24

Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, descended from Zerah son of Judah, represented the king in all the people’s affairs. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:25

Judah’s villagers lived in Kiriath-arba, Dibon, Jekabzeel, and their villages, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:26

Jeshua, Moladah, Beth-pelet, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:27

Hazar-shual, Beersheba and its villages, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:28

Ziklag, Meconah and its villages, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:29

En-rimmon, Zorah, Jarmuth, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:30

Zanoah, Adullam and their villages, Lachish and its fields, and Azekah and its villages. 📖

They settled from Beersheba to Hinnom Valley.

## Nehemiah 11:31

Benjamin’s descendants from Geba lived in Michmash, Aija, Bethel and its villages, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:32

Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:33

Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:34

Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat, 📖

## Nehemiah 11:35

Lod, Ono, and the Valley of the Craftsmen. 📖

## Nehemiah 11:36

Some Levite divisions from Judah settled in Benjamin. 📖
