# Nehemiah 2 — Permission to rebuild and the night inspection

**About this chapter**

The king grants Nehemiah’s request to go to Jerusalem. | Nehemiah inspects its defences at night. | He calls the people to rebuild and answers opponents.

## Nehemiah 2:1

In month Nisan, Artaxerxes’s year 20, I served wine to the king. 📖

I had never appeared sad before him.

## Nehemiah 2:2

He asked, “Why look sad when you are not ill? 📖

This must be sadness of heart.”

I was terribly afraid.

## Nehemiah 2:3

I answered, “May the king live forever! 📖

Why not be sad when my ancestors’ burial city is ruined and its gates burned?”

## Nehemiah 2:4

He asked my request. 📖

I prayed to heaven’s God

## Nehemiah 2:5

and said, “If you please and favour your servant, send me to Judah to rebuild my ancestors’ burial city.” 📖

## Nehemiah 2:6

The queen sat beside him as he asked my journey’s length and return date. 📖

He agreed to send me, and I set a time.

## Nehemiah 2:7

I asked, “If it pleases the king, give me letters to governors west of the Euphrates. 📖

They can grant safe passage to Judah.”

## Nehemiah 2:8

I asked for a letter to Asaph, royal forest keeper, for timber beams. 📖

These were for the temple citadel’s gates, city wall, and my house.

God’s gracious hand was on me, and the king granted this.

**Help with God’s hand:** This pictures God’s help.

## Nehemiah 2:9

I gave the royal letters to governors west of the Euphrates. 📖

The king sent army officers and cavalry with me.

## Nehemiah 2:10

Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite official were deeply troubled that someone sought Israel’s wellbeing. 📖

## Nehemiah 2:11

I arrived in Jerusalem and stayed 3 days. 📖

## Nehemiah 2:12

I went out at night with a few men, telling nobody what God put in my heart for Jerusalem. 📖

My mount was the only animal.

## Nehemiah 2:13

I left through Valley Gate towards the Well of the Serpent and Dung Gate. 📖

I inspected broken walls and burned gates by night.

## Nehemiah 2:14

At Fountain Gate and the King’s Pool, my animal could not pass. 📖

## Nehemiah 2:15

I went up the valley at night, inspected the wall, and returned through Valley Gate. 📖

## Nehemiah 2:16

Officials did not know my movements or purpose. 📖

I had told no Jews, priests, nobles, officials, or workers.

## Nehemiah 2:17

I said, “See our trouble, Jerusalem’s ruins and burned gates. 📖

Let us rebuild its wall and end our disgrace.”

## Nehemiah 2:18

I told them God’s gracious hand was on me and what the king said. 📖

They agreed to rebuild and began the good work.

## Nehemiah 2:19

Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite official, and Geshem the Arab mocked us. 📖

“What are you doing?

Rebelling against the king?”

## Nehemiah 2:20

I answered, “Heaven’s God will give success. 📖

We his servants will rebuild.

You have no share, right, or claim in Jerusalem.”
