# Nehemiah 5 — Debt, freedom, and Nehemiah’s example

**About this chapter**

People complain about hunger, debt, and enslaved children. | Nehemiah confronts nobles and officials. | He describes his own refusal to burden the people.

## Nehemiah 5:1

People and their wives cried out against fellow Jews. 📖

## Nehemiah 5:2

Some said, “There are many of us, including our sons and daughters. 📖

We need grain to live.”

## Nehemiah 5:3

Others mortgaged fields, vineyards, and homes for grain in famine. 📖

**Help with mortgaged:** Pledged property to secure a loan.

## Nehemiah 5:4

Others borrowed to pay the king’s tax on fields and vineyards. 📖

## Nehemiah 5:5

“Our families are like theirs, yet we enslave our sons and daughters. 📖

Some daughters are already enslaved.

We cannot buy them back because others own our fields and vineyards.”

## Nehemiah 5:6

I heard and became extremely angry. 📖

## Nehemiah 5:7

After serious thought, I rebuked nobles and officials for charging their own brothers usury. 📖

I called a great assembly against them.

**Help with usury:** Interest charged on loans, adding to what borrowers owe.

## Nehemiah 5:8

“We bought back Jews sold to foreigners as far as we could. 📖

Now you sell your own brothers to be sold back to us!”

They had no answer.

## Nehemiah 5:9

I said, “This is wrong. 📖

Fear our God and avoid our foreign enemies’ reproach.

## Nehemiah 5:10

My brothers, servants, and I lend money and grain too. 📖

Let us stop this usury.

## Nehemiah 5:11

Return their fields, vineyards, olive groves, and houses now. 📖

Return the percentage charged on money, grain, new wine, and oil.”

## Nehemiah 5:12

They promised to return it and demand nothing more. 📖

Before priests, I made nobles and officials swear to fulfil this.

## Nehemiah 5:13

I shook my robe’s folds, asking God to shake every promise-breaker from home and property, leaving nothing. 📖

Everyone said “Amen” and praised the LORD.

They kept their promise.

**Help with Amen:** Agreement, meaning “let it be so”.

## Nehemiah 5:14

Artaxerxes appointed me Judah’s governor from year 20 to year 32, 12 years. 📖

My brothers and I did not take the governor’s food allowance.

## Nehemiah 5:15

Earlier governors burdened people with bread, wine, and 40 silver shekels, and their servants oppressed them. 📖

I refused because I feared God.

**Help with shekel:** A unit of weight used for silver.

## Nehemiah 5:16

I devoted myself to building the wall with all my servants. 📖

We bought no land.

## Nehemiah 5:17

150 Jews and officials ate at my table, besides foreign neighbours visiting. 📖

## Nehemiah 5:18

One ox, 6 choice sheep, and birds were prepared daily, with plentiful varieties of wine every 10 days. 📖

I demanded no governor’s allowance because the people’s burden was heavy.

## Nehemiah 5:19

My God, remember favourably everything I did for this people. 📖
