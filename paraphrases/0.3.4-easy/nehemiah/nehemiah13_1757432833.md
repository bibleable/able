# Nehemiah 13 — Nehemiah’s later reforms

**About this chapter**

Nehemiah reports action over temple rooms, neglected support, Sabbath trade, and marriages. | His account includes exclusion, threats, curses, and physical violence. | It closes with a prayer to be remembered favourably.

## Nehemiah 13:1

Moses’s Book was read publicly. 📖

They found no Ammonite or Moabite could ever enter God’s assembly,

## Nehemiah 13:2

because they withheld food and water from Israel and hired Balaam to curse them. 📖

Our God turned the curse to blessing.

## Nehemiah 13:3

On hearing the Law, they excluded everyone of foreign descent from Israel. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:4

Earlier, priest Eliashib, Tobiah’s relative, managed God’s house’s storerooms 📖

## Nehemiah 13:5

and prepared Tobiah a large room formerly storing grain offerings, frankincense, sacred objects, and priestly contributions. 📖

It held prescribed grain, new-wine, and oil tithes for Levites, singers, and gatekeepers.

**Help with frankincense:** A fragrant substance burned in worship.

## Nehemiah 13:6

During this, I was not in Jerusalem, having returned to Artaxerxes king of Babylon in his year 32. 📖

Later I obtained leave from the king

## Nehemiah 13:7

and returned to Jerusalem, discovering Eliashib’s evil in giving Tobiah a temple-courtyard room. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:8

Angrily, I threw Tobiah’s household goods out. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:9

I ordered rooms purified and returned God’s house’s objects, grain offerings, and frankincense. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:10

Levites and singers returned to their fields because their portions were not supplied. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:11

I rebuked officials for neglecting God’s house and returned Levites and singers to their posts. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:12

All Judah brought one-tenth of grain, new wine, and oil into storerooms. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:13

I appointed priest Shelemiah, scribe Zadok, and Levite Pedaiah as treasurers, assisted by Hanan son of Zaccur, grandson of Mattaniah. 📖

They were trustworthy and distributed supplies to fellow Levites.

## Nehemiah 13:14

My God, remember me and do not erase my faithful deeds for your house and service. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:15

I saw Judah working winepresses, bringing grain on donkeys, wine, grapes, figs, and all goods into Jerusalem on Sabbath. 📖

I warned against selling food then.

## Nehemiah 13:16

Tyre’s resident men sold imported fish and goods to Judah in Jerusalem on Sabbath. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:17

I rebuked Judah’s nobles for this evil of profaning Sabbath. 📖

**Help with profaning:** Treating something holy as ordinary.

## Nehemiah 13:18

“Our ancestors did the same and brought God’s disaster on us and this city. 📖

You rekindle his wrath against Israel by profaning Sabbath!”

## Nehemiah 13:19

Before Sabbath, as evening shadows fell, I shut Jerusalem’s gates until Sabbath ended. 📖

My servants guarded against any loads entering.

## Nehemiah 13:20

Merchants camped outside Jerusalem once or twice. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:21

I warned them, “Why camp before the wall? 📖

If you do it again, I will lay hands on you.”

They stopped coming on Sabbath.

**Help with lay hands on you:** Here this is a threat of force.

## Nehemiah 13:22

I ordered Levites to purify themselves and guard gates, keeping Sabbath holy. 📖

My God, remember this too and show mercy according to your abundant faithful love.

## Nehemiah 13:23

I saw Jews marrying women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:24

Half their children spoke Ashdod’s or other peoples’ languages, unable to speak Judah’s language. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:25

I rebuked them and called curses on them, beat some men, and pulled out their hair. 📖

I made them swear before God.

“Do not give your daughters to their sons.

Do not take their daughters as wives for your sons or yourselves!”

## Nehemiah 13:26

“Israel’s King Solomon sinned like this. 📖

No king among many nations was like him, loved by God and made king over all Israel.

Yet foreign women led him into sin.

## Nehemiah 13:27

Must we hear you also commit this terrible evil, betraying our God by marrying foreign women?” 📖

## Nehemiah 13:28

One of Jehoiada son of high priest Eliashib’s sons married Sanballat the Horonite’s daughter. 📖

I drove him away.

## Nehemiah 13:29

My God, remember their defiling the priesthood and priestly and Levite covenant. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:30

I purified priests and Levites from everything foreign and assigned each their duties. 📖

## Nehemiah 13:31

I arranged wood at appointed times and firstfruits. 📖

My God, remember me favourably.
