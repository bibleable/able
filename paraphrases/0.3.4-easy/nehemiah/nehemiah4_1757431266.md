# Nehemiah 4 — Building while under threat

**About this chapter**

Opponents mock the builders and plan an attack. | Nehemiah prays, guards the wall, and organises work. | His prayer includes a request for judgment against the enemies.

## Nehemiah 4:1

Sanballat heard we rebuilt the wall and angrily mocked the Jews. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:2

Before his associates and Samaria’s army he asked, “What are these feeble Jews doing? 📖

Can they rebuild alone?

Will they sacrifice or finish in one day?

Can burned stones live again from rubble?”

## Nehemiah 4:3

Tobiah the Ammonite beside him said, “Even a fox climbing their stone wall would break it!” 📖

## Nehemiah 4:4

Hear us, God, because we are despised. 📖

Turn their scorn on their own heads.

Let them be taken as plunder into captivity.

## Nehemiah 4:5

Do not cover their guilt or erase their sin from your sight, because they provoked the builders. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:6

We joined the whole wall to half its height because the people were eager to work. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:7

Sanballat, Tobiah, Arabs, Ammonites, and Ashdodites grew furious as Jerusalem’s wall repairs advanced and gaps closed. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:8

They plotted together to attack Jerusalem and hinder us. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:9

We prayed and guarded against them day and night. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:10

Judah’s people said, “Workers’ strength is failing. 📖

There is too much rubble to rebuild the wall.”

## Nehemiah 4:11

Enemies said, “We will enter unseen, kill them, and stop the work.” 📖

## Nehemiah 4:12

Nearby Jews warned us 10 times that attacks could come wherever we turned. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:13

I placed families behind low, exposed wall sections with swords, spears, and bows. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:14

After inspecting, I told nobles, officials, and everyone, “Do not fear. 📖

Remember the great, awesome Lord.

Fight for brothers, sons, daughters, wives, and homes.”

## Nehemiah 4:15

Enemies heard we knew their plan and God frustrated it. 📖

We each returned to our wall work.

## Nehemiah 4:16

Half my servants worked while half held spears, shields, bows, and armour. 📖

Officers stood behind Judah’s people

## Nehemiah 4:17

rebuilding the wall. 📖

Those carrying materials worked with one hand and held a weapon with the other.

## Nehemiah 4:18

Each builder wore a sword. 📖

The trumpeter stayed beside me.

## Nehemiah 4:19

I told nobles, officials, and everyone, “The work is extensive, and we are far apart on the wall. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:20

Gather where you hear the horn. 📖

Our God will fight for us!”

## Nehemiah 4:21

We worked while half the men held spears from dawn until stars appeared. 📖

## Nehemiah 4:22

I told everyone, “Each man and servant must stay inside Jerusalem overnight, guarding at night and working by day.” 📖

## Nehemiah 4:23

I, my brothers, servants, and guards stayed in our clothes. 📖

Each carried a weapon, even for water.
