# Nehemiah 7 — Guarding Jerusalem and the returnee register

**About this chapter**

Nehemiah appoints guards and finds the register of the first returnees. | Names, counts, gifts, and totals follow this chapter’s source. | They are not changed to match Ezra’s parallel list.

## Nehemiah 7:1

After rebuilding the wall and installing doors, I appointed gatekeepers, singers, and Levites. 📖

## Nehemiah 7:2

I placed my brother Hanani and fortress commander Hananiah over Jerusalem. 📖

Hananiah was faithful and feared God more than most.

## Nehemiah 7:3

I said, “Keep Jerusalem’s gates closed until the sun is hot. 📖

Keep them shut and barred while guards are on duty.

Appoint residents to guard posts and their homes.”

## Nehemiah 7:4

The city was large and spacious but had few people, and houses were not rebuilt. 📖

## Nehemiah 7:64

They could not find family records and were excluded from priesthood as unclean. 📖

**Help with unclean here:** They lacked required evidence for priestly status.

This does not mean they had less worth as people.

## Nehemiah 7:65

**Help with Urim and Thummim:** Terms for a priestly way of seeking God’s decision.
