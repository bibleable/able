# Nehemiah 1 — News from Jerusalem and Nehemiah’s prayer

**About this chapter**

Nehemiah hears Jerusalem’s survivors are in trouble. | He mourns and prays, confessing Israel’s sins and recalling God’s conditional promise. | Nehemiah speaks in his own voice.

## Nehemiah 1:1

These are Nehemiah son of Hacaliah’s words. 📖

In month Chislev, year 20, I was in Susa’s citadel.

**Help with citadel:** A strongly defended part of a city.

## Nehemiah 1:2

My brother Hanani arrived with men from Judah. 📖

I asked about Jews surviving exile and about Jerusalem.

**Help with exile:** Being forced to leave one’s homeland.

## Nehemiah 1:3

They said, “The survivors in the province suffer great trouble and disgrace. 📖

Jerusalem’s wall is broken, and its gates are burned.”

## Nehemiah 1:4

I sat and wept, mourning for days, fasting and praying to heaven’s God. 📖

**Help with fasting:** Going without food as part of seeking God.

## Nehemiah 1:5

“LORD, heaven’s God, great and awesome, you keep your covenant of faithful love with those loving and obeying you. 📖

**Help with covenant:** A binding agreement or promise.

## Nehemiah 1:6

Open your eyes and hear your servant’s day-and-night prayer for Israel. 📖

I confess our sins against you, including mine and my father’s family’s.

## Nehemiah 1:7

We acted corruptly and failed to keep the commands, laws, and rules you gave Moses your servant. 📖

## Nehemiah 1:8

Remember your word to Moses. 📖

‘If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among nations.

## Nehemiah 1:9

If you return and obey my commands, I will gather even exiles from the farthest horizon. 📖

I will bring them to my chosen dwelling for my Name.’

## Nehemiah 1:10

They are your servants and people, redeemed by your great power and mighty hand. 📖

**Help with redeemed:** Rescued and claimed as one’s own.

## Nehemiah 1:11

Lord, hear my prayer and those of your servants delighting to honour your name. 📖

Give me success today and mercy before this man.”

I was the king’s cupbearer.

**Help with cupbearer:** An official who served the king’s drink.
