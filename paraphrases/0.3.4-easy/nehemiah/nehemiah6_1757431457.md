# Nehemiah 6 — Plots fail and the wall is finished

**About this chapter**

Opponents try to lure Nehemiah away and accuse him of rebellion. | A hired prophecy seeks to frighten him into sin. | The wall is completed, but hostile letters continue.

## Nehemiah 6:1

Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and other enemies heard the wall had no gaps. 📖

Gate doors were not yet installed.

## Nehemiah 6:2

Sanballat and Geshem invited me to a village in Ono’s plain. 📖

They planned to harm me.

## Nehemiah 6:3

I replied, “I am doing great work and cannot come down. 📖

Why stop it while I leave to meet you?”

## Nehemiah 6:4

They sent the same request 4 times, and I gave the same answer. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:5

The 5th time, Sanballat sent a young servant with an unsealed letter. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:6

It claimed nations reported, and Geshem agreed, that we Jews plotted rebellion and built the wall to make me king. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:7

It claimed I appointed prophets in Jerusalem to announce a king in Judah. 📖

“The king will hear these reports, so meet us.”

## Nehemiah 6:8

I replied that these rumours were invented in his mind, with nothing to them. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:9

They wanted to frighten and weaken us so work would never finish. 📖

My God, strengthen my hands.

**Help with hands here:** Nehemiah asks for strength to continue.

## Nehemiah 6:10

I visited Shemaiah son of Delaiah, grandson of Mehetabel, confined at home. 📖

He said, “Meet in God’s temple and shut the doors.

They come to kill you at night!”

## Nehemiah 6:11

I said, “Should someone like me flee or enter the temple to save his life? 📖

I will not go!”

## Nehemiah 6:12

I realised God did not send him. 📖

Tobiah and Sanballat hired him to give this prophecy against me.

## Nehemiah 6:13

He was hired to frighten me into sin by following his suggestion. 📖

They wanted a bad name for me, to discredit me.

## Nehemiah 6:14

My God, remember Tobiah’s and Sanballat’s deeds, and prophetess Noadiah and other prophets who tried to frighten me. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:15

The wall was completed in 52 days, on Elul 25. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:16

Enemies and surrounding nations feared and lost heart, realising God accomplished this work. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:17

Judah’s nobles and Tobiah exchanged many letters then. 📖

## Nehemiah 6:18

Many swore loyalty because Tobiah married a daughter of Shecaniah son of Arah. 📖

Tobiah’s son Jehohanan married Meshullam son of Berechiah’s daughter.

## Nehemiah 6:19

They told me Tobiah’s good deeds and reported my words to him. 📖

His letters tried to frighten me.
