# Nehemiah 3 — The wall’s builders

**About this chapter**

The record follows people repairing Jerusalem’s wall and gates. | It names workers, family relationships, duties, and locations. | Repeated names and each section’s boundaries are kept.

## Nehemiah 3:1

High priest Eliashib and fellow priests rebuilt Sheep Gate, dedicated it, and installed doors. 📖

They dedicated the wall as far as the Tower of the Hundred and Tower of Hananel.

## Nehemiah 3:2

Jericho’s men built next to Eliashib. 📖

Zaccur son of Imri built next.

## Nehemiah 3:3

Hassenaah’s sons rebuilt Fish Gate, laying beams and installing doors, bolts, and bars. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:4

Meremoth son of Uriah, grandson of Hakkoz, repaired next. 📖

Next were Meshullam son of Berechiah, grandson of Meshezabel, and Zadok son of Baana.

## Nehemiah 3:5

The Tekoites repaired next, but their nobles would not work under their supervisors. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:6

Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah repaired Jeshanah Gate’s beams, doors, bolts, and bars. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:7

Melatiah the Gibeonite, Jadon the Meronothite, and Gibeon’s and Mizpah’s men repaired next. 📖

They were under the governor west of the Euphrates.

## Nehemiah 3:8

Goldsmith Uzziel son of Harhaiah repaired next, then perfumer Hananiah. 📖

They strengthened Jerusalem to the Broad Wall.

**Help with these jobs:** Goldsmiths make gold objects; perfumers make scented products.

## Nehemiah 3:9

Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of half Jerusalem’s district, repaired next, 📖

## Nehemiah 3:10

followed by Jedaiah son of Harumaph opposite his house, then Hattush son of Hashabneiah. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:11

Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired another section and the Tower of the Ovens. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:12

Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of Jerusalem’s other half-district, repaired next with his daughters. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:13

Hanun and Zanoah’s residents rebuilt Valley Gate with doors, bolts, and bars. 📖

They repaired 1,000 cubits of wall to Dung Gate.

**Help with cubit:** An ancient unit roughly a forearm’s length.

## Nehemiah 3:14

Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of Beth-haccherem’s district, rebuilt Dung Gate with doors, bolts, and bars. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:15

Shallun son of Col-hozeh, ruler of Mizpah’s district, rebuilt Fountain Gate, its roof, doors, bolts, and bars. 📖

He repaired Shelah Pool’s wall by the king’s garden to the stairs descending from the City of David.

## Nehemiah 3:16

Nehemiah son of Azbuk ruled half Beth-zur’s district. 📖

He repaired opposite David’s tombs to the artificial pool and House of the Mighty.

## Nehemiah 3:17

Levites repaired next under Rehum son of Bani. 📖

Hashabiah, ruler of half Keilah’s district, repaired for his district.

## Nehemiah 3:18

Their countrymen repaired next under Binnui son of Henadad, ruler of Keilah’s other half-district. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:19

Ezer son of Jeshua, Mizpah’s ruler, repaired another section opposite the armoury ascent by the wall’s angle. 📖

**Help with armoury:** A store for weapons.

## Nehemiah 3:20

Baruch son of Zabbai diligently repaired from the angle to high priest Eliashib’s house’s doorway. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:21

Meremoth son of Uriah, grandson of Hakkoz, repaired from Eliashib’s doorway to his house’s end. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:22

Priests from the surrounding area repaired next. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:23

Benjamin and Hasshub repaired before their house. 📖

Azariah son of Maaseiah, grandson of Ananiah, repaired beside his house.

## Nehemiah 3:24

Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section from Azariah’s house to the angle and corner. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:25

Palal son of Uzai repaired opposite the angle and tower projecting from the upper palace by the guard courtyard. 📖

Next was Pedaiah son of Parosh,

## Nehemiah 3:26

with Ophel’s temple servants, repairing opposite the eastern Water Gate and projecting tower. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:27

Tekoites repaired another section opposite the projecting great tower to Ophel’s wall. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:28

Above Horse Gate, priests repaired before their own houses. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:29

Zadok son of Immer repaired opposite his house. 📖

Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, East Gate’s guard, repaired next.

## Nehemiah 3:30

Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, Zalaph’s 6th son, repaired another section. 📖

Meshullam son of Berechiah repaired opposite his quarters.

## Nehemiah 3:31

Goldsmith Malchijah repaired to the temple servants’ and merchants’ house opposite Inspection Gate, and the upper room above the corner. 📖

## Nehemiah 3:32

Goldsmiths and merchants repaired between that upper room and Sheep Gate. 📖
