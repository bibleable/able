# Nehemiah 12 — Priests, Levites, and the wall dedication

**About this chapter**

The record lists priests and Levites across several generations. | Two thanksgiving choirs walk along the wall. | The people rejoice and provide for worship service.

## Nehemiah 12:1

Priests and Levites returning with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua included Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:2

Amariah, Malluch, Hattush, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:3

Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:4

Iddo, Ginnethon, Abijah, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:5

Mijamin, Maadiah, Bilgah, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:6

Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:7

Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, and Jedaiah. 📖

These led priests and associates during Jeshua’s time.

## Nehemiah 12:8

Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah, who led thanksgiving songs with associates. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:9

Associates Bakbukiah and Unni stood opposite in service. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:10

Jeshua fathered Joiakim, Joiakim fathered Eliashib, and Eliashib fathered Joiada. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:11

Joiada fathered Jonathan, and Jonathan fathered Jaddua. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:12

In Joiakim’s time, priestly family heads included Meraiah of Seraiah’s family and Hananiah of Jeremiah’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:13

Meshullam of Ezra’s and Jehohanan of Amariah’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:14

Jonathan of Malluchi’s and Joseph of Shebaniah’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:15

Adna of Harim’s and Helkai of Meraioth’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:16

Zechariah of Iddo’s and Meshullam of Ginnethon’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:17

Zichri of Abijah’s, and of Miniamin and Moadiah, Piltai, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:18

Shammua of Bilgah’s and Jonathan of Shemaiah’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:19

Mattenai of Joiarib’s and Uzzi of Jedaiah’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:20

Kallai of Sallai’s and Eber of Amok’s, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:21

Hashabiah of Hilkiah’s and Nethanel of Jedaiah’s. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:22

Levite and priestly family heads were recorded during Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua’s days, under Darius the Persian. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:23

Levi’s family heads were recorded in the Book of the Chronicles until Johanan son of Eliashib’s time. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:24

Levite leaders Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Jeshua son of Kadmiel alternated praise and thanks with associates opposite them. 📖

David the man of God prescribed this.

## Nehemiah 12:25

Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub guarded gate storerooms. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:26

They served during Joiakim son of Jeshua, grandson of Jozadak, and during governor Nehemiah and priest-scribe Ezra. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:27

Levites came from all their homes for Jerusalem’s wall dedication, joyfully thanking and singing with cymbals, harps, and lyres. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:28

Singers gathered from around Jerusalem and Netophathite villages, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:29

Beth-gilgal, and Geba’s and Azmaveth’s fields. 📖

They had built villages around Jerusalem.

## Nehemiah 12:30

Priests and Levites purified themselves, the people, gates, and wall. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:31

I brought Judah’s leaders onto the wall and appointed 2 great thanksgiving choirs. 📖

One went right towards Dung Gate.

## Nehemiah 12:32

Hoshaiah and half Judah’s leaders followed, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:33

with Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:34

Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, Jeremiah, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:35

priests with trumpets, and Zechariah son of Jonathan, grandson of Shemaiah. 📖

Shemaiah was Mattaniah’s son, Mattaniah was Micaiah’s son, Micaiah was Zaccur’s son, and Zaccur was Asaph’s son.

## Nehemiah 12:36

His associates were Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani, playing David-prescribed instruments. 📖

Scribe Ezra led.

## Nehemiah 12:37

At Fountain Gate they climbed the City of David’s stairs to the wall. 📖

They passed above David’s house to eastern Water Gate.

## Nehemiah 12:38

The second choir went left. 📖

I and half the people followed along the wall past the Tower of the Ovens to Broad Wall,

## Nehemiah 12:39

over Ephraim Gate, Jeshanah Gate, Fish Gate, Tower of Hananel, Tower of the Hundred, and Sheep Gate. 📖

They stopped at Gate of the Guard.

## Nehemiah 12:40

The choirs, I, and half the accompanying officials stood in God’s house, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:41

with trumpeting priests Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah, 📖

## Nehemiah 12:42

and Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer. 📖

Choirs sang under Jezrahiah’s direction.

## Nehemiah 12:43

They offered great sacrifices and rejoiced because God gave great joy. 📖

Women and children rejoiced too, and Jerusalem’s joy was heard far away.

## Nehemiah 12:44

That same day, men managed storerooms for supplies, contributions, firstfruits, and tithes. 📖

Village fields supplied priests’ and Levites’ lawful portions because Judah rejoiced in their service.

**Help with these words:** Firstfruits are the first produce; tithes are one-tenth.

## Nehemiah 12:45

They served God and performed purification with singers and gatekeepers as David and Solomon prescribed. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:46

Since David’s and Asaph’s days, directors led singers and songs of praise and thanks to God. 📖

## Nehemiah 12:47

In Zerubbabel’s and Nehemiah’s time, all Israel supplied singers’ and gatekeepers’ daily portions. 📖

They set aside daily portions for Levites, who set aside daily portions for Aaron’s descendants.
