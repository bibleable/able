# Ecclesiastes 3 — A time for everything

**About this chapter**

The teacher reflects on time, work, justice, and death.

## Ecclesiastes 3:1

There is a season for everything. 📖

There is a time for every purpose under heaven.

## Ecclesiastes 3:2

There is a time to be born and a time to die. 📖

There is a time to plant and a time to pull up plants.

## Ecclesiastes 3:3

There is a time to kill and a time to heal. 📖

There is a time to tear down and a time to build.

## Ecclesiastes 3:4

There is a time to cry and a time to laugh. 📖

There is a time to grieve and a time to dance.

## Ecclesiastes 3:5

There is a time to throw stones away and a time to gather them. 📖

There is a time to embrace and a time not to embrace.

## Ecclesiastes 3:6

There is a time to search and a time to accept a loss. 📖

There is a time to keep and a time to throw away.

## Ecclesiastes 3:7

There is a time to tear and a time to mend. 📖

There is a time to be silent and a time to speak.

## Ecclesiastes 3:8

There is a time to love and a time to hate. 📖

There is a time for war and a time for peace.

## Ecclesiastes 3:9

What does a worker gain from all the hard work? 📖

## Ecclesiastes 3:10

I have seen the burden God gives people to keep them occupied. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 3:11

God has made everything beautiful in its time. 📖

He has placed eternity in people's hearts.

Yet they cannot understand all his work from beginning to end.

**Help with a word:** Eternity means time beyond our limited lives, without an end.

## Ecclesiastes 3:12

I know people can do nothing better than be glad and do good while they live. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 3:13

They should eat, drink, and find satisfaction in their work. 📖

This is God's gift.

## Ecclesiastes 3:14

Everything God does lasts forever. 📖

Nothing can be added or taken away.

God acts so people will fear him.

**Help with fear him:** Respect God and take His authority seriously.

## Ecclesiastes 3:15

What exists now has existed before. 📖

What will come has also been before.

God calls the past to account.

## Ecclesiastes 3:16

I saw wickedness under the sun in the place of judgment. 📖

I saw wickedness in the place of righteousness.

**Help with righteousness:** What is right and just.

## Ecclesiastes 3:17

I told myself, “God will judge righteous and wicked people. 📖

There is a time for every activity and deed.”

## Ecclesiastes 3:18

I told myself, “God tests human beings so they may see for themselves that they are only beasts.” 📖

**About this statement:** This is the Teacher’s comparison.

The next verses speak about people and animals dying and returning to dust.

## Ecclesiastes 3:19

People and animals meet the same end. 📖

Both die and share the same breath.

People have no advantage over animals.

Everything is futile.

**Help with futile:** Unable to achieve a lasting result.

## Ecclesiastes 3:20

All go to the same place. 📖

All come from dust and return to dust.

## Ecclesiastes 3:21

Who knows whether a human spirit rises up and an animal's spirit goes down into the earth? 📖

## Ecclesiastes 3:22

I saw that a person can do nothing better than enjoy their work. 📖

That is their share.

Who can show them what will happen after they are gone?
