# Ecclesiastes 6 — Wealth without enjoyment

**About this chapter**

The Teacher considers prosperity that brings no satisfaction.

He includes difficult comparisons about life, death, and stillbirth.

## Ecclesiastes 6:1

I saw another evil under the sun. 📖

It weighs heavily on humanity.

## Ecclesiastes 6:2

God gives a man riches, wealth, and honor, leaving no heart’s desire unmet. 📖

But God does not let him enjoy them.

A stranger enjoys them instead.

This is futile and terrible suffering.

## Ecclesiastes 6:3

A man may father a hundred children and live many years. 📖

Yet suppose he is unsatisfied with his prosperity and receives no proper burial.

However long he lives, I say a stillborn child is better off.

**Help with stillborn:** Born dead.

This comparison is the Teacher’s difficult observation.

## Ecclesiastes 6:4

The stillborn child comes in futility and leaves in darkness. 📖

His name is hidden and unknown.

## Ecclesiastes 6:5

The child neither saw the sun nor knew anything. 📖

Yet he has more rest than that man,

## Ecclesiastes 6:6

even if the man lives a thousand years twice without enjoying his prosperity. 📖

Do they not all go to the same place?

## Ecclesiastes 6:7

All a man’s work is for his mouth. 📖

Yet his appetite is never satisfied.

## Ecclesiastes 6:8

What advantage does a wise man have over a fool? 📖

What does a poor man gain by knowing how to behave before others?

## Ecclesiastes 6:9

Better what the eye sees than desire wandering. 📖

This too is futile and chasing wind.

## Ecclesiastes 6:10

Everything existing was named long ago. 📖

What a human being is has been known.

But he cannot argue against someone stronger than himself.

## Ecclesiastes 6:11

More words bring more futility. 📖

How does that help anyone?

## Ecclesiastes 6:12

Who knows what is good during a man’s few, fleeting days passing like a shadow? 📖

Who can tell him what comes after him under the sun?
