# Ecclesiastes 4 — Oppression, loneliness, and companionship

**About this chapter**

The Teacher considers oppression and the limits of success.

He contrasts lonely labor with the help companions give.

## Ecclesiastes 4:1

I looked again at all the oppression under the sun. 📖

I saw oppressed people’s tears, and no one comforted them.

Their oppressors held power.

There was no comforter.

**Help with oppression:** Cruel and unjust treatment.

## Ecclesiastes 4:2

So I considered people already dead better off than those still living. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 4:3

Better than both is someone not yet existing, who has not seen evil under the sun. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 4:4

I saw all labor and success springing from envy of a neighbor. 📖

This too is futile and chasing wind.

## Ecclesiastes 4:5

A fool folds his hands and eats his own flesh. 📖

**Help with this image:** The fool’s inactivity is pictured as consuming his own life.

## Ecclesiastes 4:6

Better one handful with calm than two handfuls with hard work and chasing wind. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 4:7

Again, I saw futility under the sun. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 4:8

A man is alone, without son or brother. 📖

His work never ends, yet his eyes are not satisfied with wealth.

He asks, “Who am I working for and denying myself enjoyment?”

This too is futile, a miserable task.

## Ecclesiastes 4:9

Two are better than one. 📖

They have a good return for their work.

## Ecclesiastes 4:10

If one falls, his companion can lift him. 📖

But pity someone falling without anyone to help!

## Ecclesiastes 4:11

If two lie together, they keep warm. 📖

But how can one keep warm alone?

## Ecclesiastes 4:12

One may be overpowered, but two can resist. 📖

A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.

## Ecclesiastes 4:13

Better a poor, wise youth than an old, foolish king no longer able to accept a warning. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 4:14

The youth came from prison to kingship, although born poor in his own kingdom. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 4:15

I saw everyone living and walking under the sun follow the second one, the youth succeeding the king. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 4:16

There is no limit to all the people before them. 📖

Yet later people will not celebrate the successor.

This too is futile and chasing wind.
