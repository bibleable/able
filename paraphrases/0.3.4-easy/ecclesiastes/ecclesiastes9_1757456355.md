# Ecclesiastes 9 — Death, enjoyment, and unexpected outcomes

**About this chapter**

The Teacher considers the common fate of people and the uncertain outcomes of work.

He also tells of an overlooked wise man.

## Ecclesiastes 9:1

I took all this to heart. 📖

I concluded that righteous and wise people, and their deeds, are in God’s hands.

A man does not know what lies ahead, love or hate.

## Ecclesiastes 9:2

The same fate comes to everyone: righteous and wicked, good and bad, clean and unclean. 📖

It comes to people offering sacrifices and those not offering them.

It comes to good people and sinners, people making vows and those refusing vows.

**Help with clean and unclean:** Religious categories connected with worship.

They do not mean that illness or disability makes someone morally bad.

## Ecclesiastes 9:3

This is an evil in everything under the sun: everyone has one fate. 📖

People’s hearts are full of evil and madness while they live.

Afterward they join the dead.

## Ecclesiastes 9:4

Yet anyone among the living has hope. 📖

Even a living dog is better than a dead lion.

## Ecclesiastes 9:5

Living people know they will die. 📖

But dead people know nothing.

They have no further reward because their memory is forgotten.

## Ecclesiastes 9:6

Their love, hatred, and envy are gone. 📖

They will never again share in anything done under the sun.

## Ecclesiastes 9:7

Go, eat bread with joy and drink wine with a cheerful heart. 📖

God has already approved your works.

## Ecclesiastes 9:8

Let your clothes always be white. 📖

Never spare oil for your head.

**Help with clothing and oil:** Pictures associated with celebration and enjoyment.

## Ecclesiastes 9:9

Enjoy life with your beloved wife throughout the fleeting life God gives you under the sun. 📖

Enjoy all your fleeting days.

This is your share in life and work under the sun.

## Ecclesiastes 9:10

Whatever your hands find to do, do it with all your strength. 📖

In Sheol, where you are going, there is no work, planning, knowledge, or wisdom.

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Ecclesiastes 9:11

I saw something else under the sun. 📖

The fastest do not always win races, nor the strongest battles.

Bread does not always go to the wise, wealth to the intelligent, or favor to the skillful.

Time and chance happen to everyone.

## Ecclesiastes 9:12

No one knows his time. 📖

Fish are caught in cruel nets, and birds in traps.

In the same way, people are caught when an evil time suddenly falls.

## Ecclesiastes 9:13

I also saw this wisdom under the sun. 📖

It seemed great to me:

## Ecclesiastes 9:14

A small city had few men. 📖

A mighty king surrounded it and built great attack ramps against it.

## Ecclesiastes 9:15

A poor, wise man was found there. 📖

His wisdom saved the city.

Yet no one remembered him.

## Ecclesiastes 9:16

I said, “Wisdom is better than strength. 📖

But the poor man’s wisdom is despised.

His words are not heard.”

## Ecclesiastes 9:17

Wise people’s calm words are heard above a ruler’s shouts among fools. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 9:18

Wisdom is better than war weapons. 📖

But one sinner destroys much good.
