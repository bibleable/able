# Ecclesiastes 10 — Wisdom and foolishness in daily life

**About this chapter**

The Teacher gives sayings about rulers, work, speech, and judgment.

## Ecclesiastes 10:1

Dead flies make a perfumer’s oil stink. 📖

In the same way, a little foolishness outweighs wisdom and honor.

## Ecclesiastes 10:2

A wise man’s heart turns right. 📖

A fool’s heart turns left.

**Help with right and left:** The directions picture contrasting choices.

## Ecclesiastes 10:3

Even walking along the road, a fool lacks sense. 📖

He shows everyone he is a fool.

## Ecclesiastes 10:4

If a ruler’s anger rises against you, do not leave your post. 📖

Calmness lays great offenses to rest.

## Ecclesiastes 10:5

I saw an evil under the sun, a mistake coming from the ruler: 📖

## Ecclesiastes 10:6

foolishness is given high office, while rich people sit in low positions. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 10:7

I saw slaves riding horses while princes walked like slaves. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 10:8

Anyone digging a pit may fall into it. 📖

Anyone breaking a wall may be bitten by a snake.

## Ecclesiastes 10:9

Someone cutting stones may be hurt by them. 📖

Someone splitting logs puts himself in danger.

## Ecclesiastes 10:10

A dull axe with an unsharpened blade needs more strength. 📖

But skill brings success.

## Ecclesiastes 10:11

If a snake bites before being charmed, the charmer gains nothing. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 10:12

A wise man’s words are gracious. 📖

But a fool’s lips consume him.

**Help with gracious:** Kind and considerate.

## Ecclesiastes 10:13

His talk begins in foolishness. 📖

It ends in evil madness.

## Ecclesiastes 10:14

Yet a fool keeps adding words. 📖

No one knows what is coming.

Who can tell him what comes after him?

## Ecclesiastes 10:15

A fool’s work tires him out. 📖

He does not know the way to the city.

## Ecclesiastes 10:16

How terrible for a land with a young king and princes feasting in the morning! 📖

## Ecclesiastes 10:17

Blessed is a land whose king is a son of nobles. 📖

Its princes feast at the right time for strength, not drunkenness.

## Ecclesiastes 10:18

Laziness makes a roof collapse. 📖

Idle hands leave a house leaking.

## Ecclesiastes 10:19

A feast is made for laughter. 📖

Wine makes life merry.

But money answers everything.

## Ecclesiastes 10:20

Do not curse the king even in thought, or rich people even in your bedroom. 📖

A bird may carry your words.

A winged creature may report your speech.

**Help with the bird:** A picture of secret words being carried to others.
