# Ecclesiastes 11 — Act despite uncertainty and remember judgment

**About this chapter**

The Teacher urges action, enjoyment, and awareness of God’s judgment.

## Ecclesiastes 11:1

Throw your bread onto the waters. 📖

After many days you will find it again.

**Help with this image:** It pictures action whose benefit comes later.

Its precise application remains for editorial review.

## Ecclesiastes 11:2

Divide your share among seven, or even eight. 📖

You do not know what disaster may strike the land.

## Ecclesiastes 11:3

Full clouds pour rain on the earth. 📖

A tree may fall south or north.

Wherever it falls, it lies.

## Ecclesiastes 11:4

Anyone watching the wind fails to plant. 📖

Anyone watching clouds fails to harvest.

## Ecclesiastes 11:5

You do not know the wind’s path or how bones form in a mother’s womb. 📖

In the same way, you cannot understand God’s work.

He makes everything.

## Ecclesiastes 11:6

Plant seed in the morning. 📖

Do not rest your hands in the evening.

You do not know which will succeed, this or that, or whether both will thrive equally.

## Ecclesiastes 11:7

Light is sweet. 📖

Seeing the sun pleases the eyes.

## Ecclesiastes 11:8

If a man lives many years, let him rejoice in all of them. 📖

But remember the many days of darkness.

Everything coming is futile.

## Ecclesiastes 11:9

Young man, rejoice while young. 📖

Let your heart be glad in youth.

Follow your heart’s ways and what your eyes see.

But know that God will judge you for all these things.

## Ecclesiastes 11:10

Banish sorrow from your heart and pain from your body. 📖

Youth and vigor quickly pass.
