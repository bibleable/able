# Ecclesiastes 1 — The Teacher asks what people gain

**About this chapter**

The Teacher speaks about repeated patterns, human work, and the limits of wisdom.

His difficult observations are retained as his words.

## Ecclesiastes 1:1

These are the Teacher’s words, the son of David, king in Jerusalem: 📖

## Ecclesiastes 1:2

The Teacher says, “Futility of futilities! 📖

Futility of futilities!

Everything is futile!”

**Help with futility:** Effort that does not produce lasting gain.

The repeated phrase stresses the Teacher’s strong conclusion.

## Ecclesiastes 1:3

What does a man gain from all his hard work under the sun? 📖

**Help with under the sun:** Life here on earth.

## Ecclesiastes 1:4

Generations come and go. 📖

But the earth remains forever.

## Ecclesiastes 1:5

The sun rises and sets. 📖

It hurries back to where it rises.

## Ecclesiastes 1:6

The wind blows south and turns north. 📖

It swirls around and around, always returning on its course.

## Ecclesiastes 1:7

All rivers flow into the sea, but the sea never fills. 📖

Streams flow again to the place they came from.

## Ecclesiastes 1:8

Everything is tiring, more than anyone can describe. 📖

Seeing does not satisfy the eye.

Hearing does not satisfy the ear.

## Ecclesiastes 1:9

What has been will happen again. 📖

What has been done will be done again.

Nothing is new under the sun.

## Ecclesiastes 1:10

Can anyone say, “Look, this is new”? 📖

It already existed in ages before us.

## Ecclesiastes 1:11

People who came before are not remembered. 📖

Future people will not be remembered by those following them either.

## Ecclesiastes 1:12

I, the Teacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 1:13

I set my mind to use wisdom to explore everything done under heaven. 📖

God gave people this miserable task to keep them occupied!

## Ecclesiastes 1:14

I saw everything done under the sun. 📖

I found it all futile, a pursuit of the wind.

**Help with pursuit of the wind:** A picture of chasing something that cannot be caught or held.

## Ecclesiastes 1:15

What is crooked cannot be straightened. 📖

What is missing cannot be counted.

## Ecclesiastes 1:16

I told myself, “My wisdom has grown beyond everyone ruling Jerusalem before me. 📖

My mind has seen much wisdom and knowledge.”

## Ecclesiastes 1:17

I set my mind to know wisdom, madness, and foolishness. 📖

I learned that this too is chasing the wind.

## Ecclesiastes 1:18

Much wisdom brings much sorrow. 📖

As knowledge grows, grief increases.
