# Ecclesiastes 8 — Kingship, injustice, and what people cannot know

**About this chapter**

The Teacher considers royal authority, delayed justice, and the limits of understanding God’s work.

## Ecclesiastes 8:1

Who is like a wise man? 📖

Who knows a matter’s meaning?

Wisdom brightens a man’s face and changes its sternness.

## Ecclesiastes 8:2

I say, keep the king’s command because of your oath before God. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 8:3

Do not hurry to leave his presence. 📖

Do not persist in a bad cause.

He does whatever he pleases.

## Ecclesiastes 8:4

A king’s word is supreme. 📖

Who can ask him, “What are you doing?”

## Ecclesiastes 8:5

Anyone keeping his command comes to no harm. 📖

A wise heart knows the right time and procedure.

## Ecclesiastes 8:6

Every purpose has a right time and procedure, though a man’s misery weighs heavily on him. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 8:7

No one knows what will happen. 📖

Who can tell him what is coming?

## Ecclesiastes 8:8

No man can hold back the wind or control his death day. 📖

No one is discharged in wartime.

Wickedness will not release people practicing it.

## Ecclesiastes 8:9

I saw all this while considering every deed under the sun. 📖

There is a time when one man rules another to his own harm.

## Ecclesiastes 8:10

I also saw wicked people buried who once entered and left the holy place. 📖

They were praised in the city where they did this.

This too is futile.

## Ecclesiastes 8:11

When punishment for crime is delayed, people’s hearts become fully set on evil. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 8:12

A sinner may do evil a hundred times and still live long. 📖

Yet I know things will go well for people fearing God and respecting His presence.

## Ecclesiastes 8:13

Wicked people do not fear God. 📖

Things will not go well for them.

Their days will not lengthen like a shadow.

## Ecclesiastes 8:14

Another futility happens on earth. 📖

Righteous people receive what wicked actions deserve.

Wicked people receive what righteous actions deserve.

I say this too is futile.

## Ecclesiastes 8:15

So I praised enjoying life. 📖

Nothing is better under the sun than eating, drinking, and being glad.

This joy accompanies a man’s work during the life God gives him under the sun.

## Ecclesiastes 8:16

I set my mind to know wisdom and observe people’s earthly task. 📖

Their eyes find no sleep by day or night.

## Ecclesiastes 8:17

I saw all God’s work. 📖

People cannot understand the work done under the sun.

However hard they search, they cannot find its meaning.

Even a wise man claiming knowledge cannot understand it.
