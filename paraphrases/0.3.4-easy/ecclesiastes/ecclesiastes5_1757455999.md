# Ecclesiastes 5 — Careful worship and the limits of wealth

**About this chapter**

The Teacher speaks about listening before God, keeping vows, and the troubles wealth can bring.

## Ecclesiastes 5:1

Guard your steps when you enter God’s house. 📖

Go near to listen rather than offer fools’ sacrifices.

They do not know they are doing wrong.

## Ecclesiastes 5:2

Do not speak quickly or hastily bring words before God in your heart. 📖

God is in heaven, and you are on earth.

So speak few words.

## Ecclesiastes 5:3

Many worries bring dreams. 📖

Many words bring a fool’s speech.

## Ecclesiastes 5:4

When you vow to God, do not delay keeping it. 📖

He takes no pleasure in fools.

Keep your vow.

**Help with vow:** A serious promise.

## Ecclesiastes 5:5

Better not to vow than to vow and not keep it. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 5:6

Do not let your mouth cause your flesh to sin. 📖

Do not tell the messenger your vow was a mistake.

Why should God grow angry at your words and destroy your hands’ work?

## Ecclesiastes 5:7

Many dreams and many words bring futility. 📖

So fear God.

**Help with futility and fear God:** Lack of lasting gain, and respecting God and taking His authority seriously.

## Ecclesiastes 5:8

If you see poor people oppressed and justice and right judgment denied in a province, do not be surprised. 📖

A superior watches each official, and still higher officials are over them.

## Ecclesiastes 5:9

Everyone takes the earth’s produce. 📖

Even the king profits from fields.

## Ecclesiastes 5:10

Anyone loving money is never satisfied with money. 📖

Anyone loving wealth is never satisfied with income.

This too is futile.

## Ecclesiastes 5:11

As goods increase, so do the people consuming them. 📖

What benefit does the owner have except looking at them?

## Ecclesiastes 5:12

A worker sleeps sweetly, whether he eats little or much. 📖

But a rich man’s plenty prevents his sleep.

## Ecclesiastes 5:13

I saw a terrible evil under the sun: wealth stored to its owner’s harm, 📖

## Ecclesiastes 5:14

or wealth lost through a project that failed. 📖

When the man has a son, he has nothing to pass on.

## Ecclesiastes 5:15

A man leaves naked, just as he came from his mother’s womb. 📖

He carries nothing in his hands from his work.

## Ecclesiastes 5:16

This too is terrible suffering. 📖

A man leaves exactly as he was born.

What does he gain by working for the wind?

## Ecclesiastes 5:17

He eats in darkness all his days, with much sorrow, sickness, and anger. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 5:18

I saw what is good and fitting: eat, drink, and enjoy all your work under the sun. 📖

God has given only a few days of life.

This is a person’s share.

## Ecclesiastes 5:19

God has also given every man riches and wealth. 📖

He enables him to enjoy them, accept his share, and rejoice in his work.

This is God’s gift.

## Ecclesiastes 5:20

A man seldom considers his life’s days because God keeps his heart occupied with joy. 📖
