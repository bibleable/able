# Ecclesiastes 12 — Remember your Creator

**About this chapter**

The closing poem uses vivid images of aging and death.

The book ends with words about the Teacher and a call to fear God and keep His commands.

## Ecclesiastes 12:1

Remember your Creator while young, before days of trouble come. 📖

Years will come when you say, “I find no pleasure in them.”

## Ecclesiastes 12:2

Remember before sunlight, moonlight, and starlight grow dark, and clouds return after rain, 📖

## Ecclesiastes 12:3

when the house’s keepers tremble and strong men bend. 📖

People grinding stop because they are few.

Those looking through windows see dimly,

## Ecclesiastes 12:4

when street doors close and the mill’s sound fades. 📖

A bird’s sound wakes a person.

All the daughters of song grow faint,

**Help with these images:** The poem pictures weakening life through a household losing its strength and activity.

The exact details of each comparison remain for editorial review.

## Ecclesiastes 12:5

when people fear heights and road dangers. 📖

The almond tree blooms, the grasshopper loses its spring, and the caper berry shrivels.

Then a man goes to his eternal home.

Mourners walk the streets.

**Help with almond tree, grasshopper, and caper berry:** A tree, an insect, and a plant’s fruit in the aging poem.

## Ecclesiastes 12:6

Remember Him before the silver cord snaps and the golden bowl is crushed. 📖

Remember before the pitcher shatters at the spring and the wheel breaks at the well,

**Help with the broken objects:** Images of life ending.

## Ecclesiastes 12:7

before dust returns to the ground it came from and the spirit returns to God who gave it. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 12:8

The Teacher says, “Futility of futilities! 📖

Everything is futile!”

**Help with futility:** Lack of lasting gain, expressed with strong repetition.

## Ecclesiastes 12:9

The Teacher was wise and also taught people knowledge. 📖

He considered, searched out, and arranged many proverbs.

**Help with proverbs:** Short sayings teaching wisdom.

## Ecclesiastes 12:10

The Teacher sought delightful sayings and accurate words of truth to write down. 📖

## Ecclesiastes 12:11

Wise people’s words are like goads. 📖

The masters’ collected sayings are like firmly fixed nails, driven by one Shepherd.

**Help with goads:** Pointed sticks used to guide animals.

The pictures show words that prompt action and hold teaching firmly.

## Ecclesiastes 12:12

My son, be warned further by these. 📖

Making many books never ends.

Much study tires the body.

## Ecclesiastes 12:13

After everything is heard, this is the conclusion: fear God and keep His commands. 📖

This is humanity’s whole duty.

**Help with fear God:** Respect Him and take His authority seriously.

## Ecclesiastes 12:14

God will judge every deed and every hidden thing, whether good or evil. 📖
