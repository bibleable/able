# 2 Timothy 2 — Serve faithfully

**About this chapter**

Paul encourages Timothy to keep teaching the truth about Jesus.

## 2 Timothy 2:1

My dear child, be strong through the kindness found in Christ Jesus. 📖

**Help with this phrase:** Paul calls Timothy his child because he cares for him as a teacher.

## 2 Timothy 2:2

You heard my teaching in front of many witnesses. 📖

Give this teaching to trustworthy people who can teach others too.

## 2 Timothy 2:3

Join me in suffering like a good soldier of Christ Jesus. 📖

## 2 Timothy 2:4

A soldier does not get caught up in everyday civilian business. 📖

The soldier wants to please the person who enlisted him.

**Help with a word:** Civilians are people who are not soldiers.

## 2 Timothy 2:5

An athlete receives the winner's crown only by competing according to the rules. 📖

## 2 Timothy 2:6

The farmer who works hard should be the first to share in the crops. 📖

## 2 Timothy 2:7

Think about what I am saying. 📖

The Lord will give you understanding in all things.

## 2 Timothy 2:8

Remember Jesus Christ. 📖

He was raised from death and came from David's family.

This is the good news I teach.

## 2 Timothy 2:9

Because of this good news, I suffer in chains like a criminal. 📖

But God's message cannot be chained!

## 2 Timothy 2:10

I endure everything for the people God has chosen. 📖

I want them to receive the rescue found in Christ Jesus, with glory that lasts forever.

## 2 Timothy 2:11

This saying can be trusted. 📖

If we died with Christ, we will also live with him.

**Help with this picture:** Sharing in Christ's death and life pictures belonging to him.

## 2 Timothy 2:12

If we keep going, we will also rule with him. 📖

If we deny him, he will also deny us.

**Help with a word:** To deny Christ is to refuse to acknowledge him.

## 2 Timothy 2:13

If we are unfaithful, he remains faithful. 📖

He cannot act against who he is.

## 2 Timothy 2:14

Remind the believers of these things. 📖

Warn them before God not to fight about words.

Such fighting helps no one and ruins those who listen.

## 2 Timothy 2:15

Work hard to be a worker God approves. 📖

Be someone who has no reason for shame.

Teach the message of truth correctly.

## 2 Timothy 2:16

Avoid empty talk that disrespects God. 📖

It leads people further from God.

## 2 Timothy 2:17

Their teaching spreads like an infection that destroys the body. 📖

Hymenaeus and Philetus are among these teachers.

## 2 Timothy 2:18

They have left the truth. 📖

They say the raising of the dead has already happened.

They are damaging some people's faith.

## 2 Timothy 2:19

But God's strong foundation still stands. 📖

It bears this seal: “The Lord knows those who belong to him.”

It also says, “Everyone who calls on the Lord must turn away from evil.”

**Help with a word:** A seal is a mark showing ownership or confirming something as true.

## 2 Timothy 2:20

A large house has gold and silver containers, and wooden and clay containers. 📖

Some are used for special purposes.

Others are for ordinary use.

## 2 Timothy 2:21

Anyone who removes what is wrong becomes like a container for special use. 📖

That person is set apart for God and useful to the Master.

That person is ready for every good work.

## 2 Timothy 2:22

Run from the wrong desires of youth. 📖

Seek right living, faith, love, and peace.

Do this with people who call on the Lord with pure hearts.

## 2 Timothy 2:23

Refuse foolish arguments based on ignorance. 📖

You know they lead to quarrels.

## 2 Timothy 2:24

The Lord's servant must not be someone who quarrels. 📖

Be kind to everyone.

Be able to teach and be patient.

## 2 Timothy 2:25

Gently correct those who oppose you. 📖

God may bring them to turn from wrong and understand the truth.

## 2 Timothy 2:26

Then they will come to their senses and escape the devil's trap. 📖

The devil has captured them to do what he wants.
