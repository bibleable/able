# 2 Timothy 3 — Keep learning from Scripture

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to Timothy about difficult times and the value of Scripture.

**Help with a word:** Scripture means the sacred writings of the Bible.

## 2 Timothy 3:1

Understand this: terrible times will come in the last days. 📖

## 2 Timothy 3:2

People will love themselves and money. 📖

They will boast, act proud, and insult others.

They will disobey their parents.

They will be ungrateful and have no respect for holy things.

## 2 Timothy 3:3

They will lack love and refuse to forgive. 📖

They will tell harmful lies and lack self-control.

They will be cruel and hate what is good.

## 2 Timothy 3:4

They will betray others and act carelessly. 📖

They will think too highly of themselves.

They will love pleasure more than God.

## 2 Timothy 3:5

They will seem to serve God, but refuse the power of a godly life. 📖

Stay away from such people.

## 2 Timothy 3:6

They enter homes by trickery. 📖

They gain control over vulnerable women.

These women are burdened by sins and led by many desires.

**Help with a word:** Vulnerable means at risk of being hurt or used unfairly by others.

Paul describes these particular women, not all women.

## 2 Timothy 3:7

These women are always learning. 📖

But they are never able to come to know the truth.

## 2 Timothy 3:8

Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses. 📖

In the same way, these men oppose the truth.

Their minds are corrupt.

Their faith fails the test.

## 2 Timothy 3:9

They will not get much further. 📖

Everyone will see their foolishness, as happened with Jannes and Jambres.

## 2 Timothy 3:10

But you have followed my teaching and way of life. 📖

You know my purpose, faith, patience, love, and willingness to keep going.

## 2 Timothy 3:11

You know how people attacked me. 📖

You know what I suffered in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra.

I endured terrible attacks.

But the Lord rescued me from them all.

## 2 Timothy 3:12

Everyone who wants to live for God in Christ Jesus will face attacks for their faith. 📖

## 2 Timothy 3:13

Evil people and those who pretend will grow worse. 📖

They will trick others and be tricked themselves.

## 2 Timothy 3:14

Keep following what you learned and firmly believe. 📖

You know the people who taught you.

## 2 Timothy 3:15

You have known the Holy Scriptures since you were a baby. 📖

They can teach you how to be saved through faith in Christ Jesus.

## 2 Timothy 3:16

All Scripture comes from God, like breath from his mouth. 📖

It is useful for teaching and showing what is wrong.

It corrects people and trains them to do right.

## 2 Timothy 3:17

In this way, a person who serves God can be fully prepared for every good work. 📖
