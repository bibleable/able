# 2 Timothy 4 — Keep telling the message

**About this chapter**

Paul urges Timothy to keep teaching faithfully.

Paul expects his own death and looks forward to the Lord's reward.

He asks Timothy to visit and gives news and greetings.

## 2 Timothy 4:1

I give you this serious charge before God and Christ Jesus. 📖

Christ will judge the living and the dead.

I give this charge in view of his appearing and his kingdom.

**Help with words:** A charge is a serious instruction.

A kingdom is where a king rules.

## 2 Timothy 4:2

Tell God's message. 📖

Be ready when the time seems suitable and when it does not.

Show people what is wrong, correct them firmly, and encourage them.

Teach them with every kind of patience.

## 2 Timothy 4:3

A time will come when people will not put up with reliable teaching. 📖

Their ears will itch for something else.

They will gather teachers who tell them what they want to hear.

**Help with this picture:** Itching ears pictures wanting teaching that pleases their own desires.

## 2 Timothy 4:4

They will turn their ears away from the truth and turn to made-up stories. 📖

## 2 Timothy 4:5

But you must keep a clear mind in every situation. 📖

Endure hardship.

Do the work of telling the good news.

Carry out your service fully.

## 2 Timothy 4:6

I am already being poured out like a drink offering. 📖

The time for me to leave has come.

**Help with these pictures:** A drink offering was liquid poured out as a gift in worship.

Paul describes giving his life and says that his death is near.

## 2 Timothy 4:7

I have fought the good fight. 📖

I have finished the race.

I have kept the faith.

**Help with these pictures:** Paul compares serving Christ faithfully with fighting a battle and finishing a race.

## 2 Timothy 4:8

The crown of righteousness is now kept for me. 📖

The Lord, the Judge who does right, will give it to me on that day.

He will give it to everyone who longs for his appearing too.

**Help with words:** A crown pictures a winner's reward.

Righteousness means being right before God.

## 2 Timothy 4:9

Do your best to come to me quickly. 📖

## 2 Timothy 4:10

Demas loved this world and left me. 📖

He went to Thessalonica.

Crescens went to Galatia, and Titus went to Dalmatia.

## 2 Timothy 4:11

Only Luke is with me. 📖

Get Mark and bring him with you.

He is useful to me in the work of serving.

## 2 Timothy 4:12

I sent Tychicus to Ephesus. 📖

## 2 Timothy 4:13

When you come, bring the outer coat I left with Carpus at Troas. 📖

Bring my scrolls, especially the parchments.

**Help with words:** Scrolls are rolled sheets used for writing.

Parchments are animal skins prepared for writing.

## 2 Timothy 4:14

Alexander, who works with copper, did me great harm. 📖

The Lord will repay him for what he did.

## 2 Timothy 4:15

You must watch out for him too. 📖

He strongly opposed our message.

## 2 Timothy 4:16

At my first defence, no one stood with me. 📖

Everyone left me.

May this not be counted against them.

## 2 Timothy 4:17

But the Lord stood by me and gave me strength. 📖

Through me, the message was fully told so all the Gentiles could hear it.

I was rescued from the lion's mouth.

**Help with words:** Gentiles are people who are not Jews.

A lion's mouth pictures deadly danger.

## 2 Timothy 4:18

The Lord will rescue me from every evil action and bring me safely into his heavenly kingdom. 📖

May he receive glory for ever and ever.

Amen.

**Help with words:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.

Amen expresses agreement with a prayer.

## 2 Timothy 4:19

Greet Prisca, Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus. 📖

## 2 Timothy 4:20

Erastus stayed at Corinth. 📖

I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.

## 2 Timothy 4:21

Do your best to come before winter. 📖

Eubulus sends greetings, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers and sisters.

## 2 Timothy 4:22

May the Lord be with your spirit. 📖

May God's kindness be with you all.
