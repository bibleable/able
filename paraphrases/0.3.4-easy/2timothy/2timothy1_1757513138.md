# 2 Timothy 1 — Guard the good gift

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to Timothy, whom he loves as his child in the faith.

He urges Timothy to use God's gift and be brave about the good news.

Paul speaks as “I” in this letter.

## 2 Timothy 1:1

This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus because God wanted this. 📖

My task follows the promise of life in Christ Jesus.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## 2 Timothy 1:2

I write to Timothy, my dear child. 📖

May God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord give you kindness, mercy, and peace.

**Help with words:** Paul calls Timothy his child because of their close bond in the faith.

Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## 2 Timothy 1:3

I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience, as my ancestors did. 📖

Night and day, I constantly remember you in my prayers.

**Help with words:** A clear conscience means knowing you are not deliberately doing wrong.

Ancestors are family members who lived before us.

## 2 Timothy 1:4

I remember your tears and long to see you. 📖

Seeing you would fill me with joy.

## 2 Timothy 1:5

I remember your sincere faith. 📖

It first lived in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice.

I am sure it lives in you too.

**Help with a word:** Faith means trust in Christ here.

## 2 Timothy 1:6

That is why I remind you to fan God's gift into flame. 📖

This gift is in you through my placing my hands on you.

**Help with this picture:** Paul compares using God's gift with making a fire burn strongly.

## 2 Timothy 1:7

God has not given us a spirit of fear. 📖

He has given us a spirit of power, love, and self-control.

## 2 Timothy 1:8

Do not be ashamed to tell others about our Lord. 📖

Do not be ashamed of me, his prisoner.

Instead, join me in suffering for the good news, with God's power.

## 2 Timothy 1:9

God saved us and called us to a holy calling. 📖

He did this because of his own purpose and kindness, rather than our works.

He gave us this kindness in Christ Jesus before time began.

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## 2 Timothy 1:10

Now God has shown this kindness through the appearing of our Saviour, Christ Jesus. 📖

Christ has defeated death.

Through the good news, he has brought life and immortality into the light.

**Help with words:** A Saviour rescues people.

Immortality means life that cannot end in death.

Bringing something into the light pictures making it known.

## 2 Timothy 1:11

I was appointed to announce this good news as a preacher, apostle, and teacher. 📖

## 2 Timothy 1:12

That is why I suffer, but I am not ashamed. 📖

I know the One I have trusted.

I am sure he can guard what I have entrusted to him for that day.

**Help with a word:** Entrusted means placed in someone's care.

## 2 Timothy 1:13

Hold on to the pattern of reliable teaching you heard from me. 📖

Do this with the faith and love found in Christ Jesus.

## 2 Timothy 1:14

Guard the good treasure placed in your care. 📖

The Holy Spirit, who lives in us, will help you.

**Help with this picture:** Paul pictures the message Timothy received as a precious treasure to protect.

## 2 Timothy 1:15

You know that everyone in the Province of Asia has left me. 📖

This includes Phygelus and Hermogenes.

**Help with a place:** Asia here was a Roman province, not the whole continent now called Asia.

## 2 Timothy 1:16

May the Lord show mercy to Onesiphorus's household. 📖

He often refreshed me and was not ashamed that I was in chains.

## 2 Timothy 1:17

When Onesiphorus arrived in Rome, he searched hard until he found me. 📖

## 2 Timothy 1:18

May the Lord give Onesiphorus his mercy on that day. 📖

You know very well how much he helped me in Ephesus.
