# Song of Solomon 4 — The bride’s beauty and the garden

**About this chapter**

**Speaker guide — editorial**

The man praises his bride in verses 1–15.

The woman answers in verse 16.

The poem compares the bride and their love with animals, jewels, fragrant plants, and a garden.

## Song of Solomon 4:1

You are beautiful, my darling, so very beautiful! 📖

Your eyes behind your veil are like doves.

Your hair is like goats streaming down Mount Gilead.

## Song of Solomon 4:2

Your teeth are like newly shorn sheep coming from washing. 📖

Each has its twin.

Not one is missing.

**Help with shorn:** Having wool cut off.

The image praises the teeth’s appearance and completeness.

## Song of Solomon 4:3

Your lips are like a scarlet ribbon, and your mouth is lovely. 📖

Your brow behind your veil is like a pomegranate slice.

**Help with scarlet and pomegranate:** A rich red color, and a fruit with many seeds.

## Song of Solomon 4:4

Your neck is like David’s tower, built with rows of stones. 📖

A thousand shields hang there, all warriors’ shields.

## Song of Solomon 4:5

Your breasts are like two fawns, gazelle twins feeding among lilies. 📖

**Help with fawns and gazelle:** Young deer, and a swift deerlike animal.

## Song of Solomon 4:6

Before dawn comes and shadows flee, I will go to myrrh’s mountain and frankincense’s hill. 📖

**Help with myrrh and frankincense:** Fragrant substances used in these poetic images.

## Song of Solomon 4:7

You are completely beautiful, my darling. 📖

You have no flaw.

## Song of Solomon 4:8

Come with me from Lebanon, my bride. 📖

Come with me from Lebanon!

Come down from Amana’s peak and Senir’s and Hermon’s summits.

Come from lions’ dens and leopards’ mountains.

## Song of Solomon 4:9

You captured my heart, my sister, my bride. 📖

One glance of your eyes and one jewel on your neck stole my heart.

**About sister and bride:** The speaker uses both addresses for his beloved.

The draft retains both without inventing a biographical family relationship.

## Song of Solomon 4:10

My sister, my bride, your love is delightful! 📖

Your love is better than wine, and your perfume more fragrant than all spices.

## Song of Solomon 4:11

My bride, your lips drip sweetness like honeycomb. 📖

Honey and milk are under your tongue.

Your clothes smell like Lebanon.

## Song of Solomon 4:12

My sister, my bride, you are a locked garden, an enclosed spring, a sealed fountain. 📖

**Help with these images:** The man pictures his bride as a private, protected garden and water source.

## Song of Solomon 4:13

Your branches are a pomegranate orchard with the choicest fruit, henna, and nard, 📖

**Help with henna and nard:** Plants valued for fragrance.

## Song of Solomon 4:14

with nard, saffron, calamus, cinnamon, every frankincense tree, myrrh, aloes, and all the finest spices. 📖

**Help with these names:** Plants and substances associated with fragrance in the garden poem.

## Song of Solomon 4:15

You are a garden spring, a fresh-water well flowing down from Lebanon. 📖

## Song of Solomon 4:16

Awake, north wind, and come, south wind. 📖

Blow on my garden and spread its spices’ fragrance.

Let my beloved enter his garden and taste its finest fruits.
