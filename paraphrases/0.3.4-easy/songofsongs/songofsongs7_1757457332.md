# Song of Solomon 7 — Praise and an invitation to the countryside

**About this chapter**

**Speaker guide — editorial**

The man praises the woman’s body in verses 1–9.

A change of voice may occur within verse 9.

The woman speaks in verses 10–13.

These assignments are provisional.

## Song of Solomon 7:1

Your sandaled feet are beautiful, prince’s daughter! 📖

Your thighs’ curves are like jewels made by a master.

## Song of Solomon 7:2

Your navel is a rounded goblet never lacking mixed wine. 📖

Your waist is a mound of wheat surrounded by lilies.

**Help with navel and goblet:** The belly button, and a drinking cup.

The poem praises the body through comparisons with beautiful, plentiful things.

## Song of Solomon 7:3

Your breasts are like two fawns, gazelle twins. 📖

## Song of Solomon 7:4

Your neck is like an ivory tower. 📖

Your eyes are like Heshbon’s pools by Bath-rabbim’s gate.

Your nose is like Lebanon’s tower facing Damascus.

## Song of Solomon 7:5

Your head crowns you like Mount Carmel. 📖

Your hair is like purple threads.

The king is captured in your tresses.

**Help with tresses:** Locks of hair.

Captured pictures being held by admiration and desire.

## Song of Solomon 7:6

How beautiful and pleasant you are, love, with your delights! 📖

## Song of Solomon 7:7

Your height and form are like a palm tree. 📖

Your breasts are clusters of fruit.

## Song of Solomon 7:8

I said, “I will climb the palm tree and hold its fruit.” 📖

May your breasts be like vine clusters and your breath smell like apples,

## Song of Solomon 7:9

and your mouth be like the finest wine. 📖

May it flow smoothly to my beloved, gently over lips and teeth.

## Song of Solomon 7:10

I belong to my beloved. 📖

His desire is for me.

## Song of Solomon 7:11

Come, my beloved, let us go into the countryside. 📖

Let us spend the night among wildflowers.

## Song of Solomon 7:12

Let us go early to the vineyards. 📖

Let us see whether vines have budded, blossoms opened, and pomegranates flowered.

There I will give you my love.

## Song of Solomon 7:13

Mandrakes give their fragrance. 📖

At our door are all delicacies, new and old, that I stored for you, my beloved.

**Help with mandrakes:** Plants with fragrant fruit in this love poem.
