# Song of Solomon 8 — Love’s strength and the final invitation

**About this chapter**

**Speaker guide — editorial**

The woman speaks in verses 1–4 and in much of verses 5–7.

Verse 5 starts with an observer’s question.

A family group speaks about a little sister in verses 8–9; the woman answers in verses 10–12.

The man calls in verse 13, and she answers in verse 14.

Some voice assignments remain uncertain.

## Song of Solomon 8:1

If only you were like a brother nursed at my mother’s breasts! 📖

If I found you outside, I would kiss you, and no one would despise me.

## Song of Solomon 8:2

I would lead you to the house of my mother, who taught me. 📖

I would give you spiced wine and nectar from my pomegranates.

## Song of Solomon 8:3

His left hand is under my head. 📖

His right arm embraces me.

## Song of Solomon 8:4

Daughters of Jerusalem, I solemnly charge you: do not stir or awaken love until the right time. 📖

## Song of Solomon 8:5

Who comes from the wilderness, leaning on her beloved? 📖

I awakened you under the apple tree.

Your mother conceived you there.

There she labored and gave you birth.

## Song of Solomon 8:6

Set me like a seal over your heart and on your arm. 📖

Love is strong as death.

Its jealousy is relentless as Sheol.

Its sparks are flames, the fiercest blaze.

**Help with seal and Sheol:** A mark of belonging and commitment, and the place of the dead.

The images describe love’s fierce strength.

## Song of Solomon 8:7

Mighty waters cannot put love out. 📖

Rivers cannot sweep it away.

If a man offered all his household wealth for love, people would utterly scorn it.

## Song of Solomon 8:8

We have a little sister whose breasts are not yet grown. 📖

What shall we do for her when someone speaks for her?

## Song of Solomon 8:9

If she is a wall, we will build a silver tower on her. 📖

If she is a door, we will enclose her with cedar panels.

**Help with wall and door:** Images in the family’s discussion of the sister’s future.

Their precise interpretation remains for editorial review.

## Song of Solomon 8:10

I am a wall, and my breasts are like towers. 📖

In his eyes, I became like someone bringing peace.

## Song of Solomon 8:11

Solomon had a vineyard at Baal-hamon. 📖

He rented it to tenants.

Each was to bring a thousand silver shekels for its fruit.

**Help with tenants and shekels:** People using rented land, and a unit used to weigh silver.

## Song of Solomon 8:12

But my own vineyard is mine to give. 📖

Solomon, the thousand shekels are for you.

Two hundred are for those tending its fruit.

## Song of Solomon 8:13

You who live in the gardens, my companions listen for your voice. 📖

Let me hear it!

## Song of Solomon 8:14

Come away, my beloved. 📖

Be like a gazelle or young stag on the mountains of spices.
