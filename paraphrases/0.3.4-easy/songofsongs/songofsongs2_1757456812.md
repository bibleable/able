# Song of Solomon 2 — An invitation as winter ends

**About this chapter**

**Speaker guide — editorial**

The woman speaks in verse 1; her beloved answers in verse 2.

She speaks in verses 3–13, quoting his invitation in verses 10–13.

Verses 14–15 contain further calls whose precise speakers are not named.

She speaks again in verses 16–17.

The guide is provisional.

## Song of Solomon 2:1

I am a rose of Sharon, a lily of the valley. 📖

## Song of Solomon 2:2

My darling is like a lily among thorns compared with the young women. 📖

## Song of Solomon 2:3

My beloved among young men is like an apple tree among forest trees. 📖

I delight to sit in his shade.

His fruit tastes sweet to me.

## Song of Solomon 2:4

He brought me to the house of wine. 📖

His banner over me is love.

**Help with banner:** A flag or sign.

The poem pictures love displayed over her.

## Song of Solomon 2:5

Strengthen me with raisins. 📖

Refresh me with apples.

I am faint with love.

## Song of Solomon 2:6

His left hand is under my head. 📖

His right arm embraces me.

## Song of Solomon 2:7

Daughters of Jerusalem, I solemnly charge you by the gazelles and does of the field. 📖

Do not stir or awaken love until the right time.

**Help with gazelles and does:** Swift deerlike animals and female deer.

Solemnly charge means make a very serious request.

## Song of Solomon 2:8

Listen, my beloved approaches! 📖

Look, he comes leaping over mountains and bounding over hills.

## Song of Solomon 2:9

My beloved is like a gazelle or young stag. 📖

Look, he stands behind our wall.

He looks through windows and peers through the lattice.

**Help with stag and lattice:** A male deer, and a frame of crossing strips with openings.

## Song of Solomon 2:10

My beloved calls, “Rise, my darling. 📖

Come away with me, my beautiful one.

## Song of Solomon 2:11

Winter has passed. 📖

Rain is over and gone.

## Song of Solomon 2:12

Flowers appear in the countryside. 📖

Singing season has come.

Turtledoves coo in our land.

## Song of Solomon 2:13

The fig tree ripens figs. 📖

Flowering vines spread their fragrance.

Rise, come away, my darling.

Come away with me, my beautiful one.”

## Song of Solomon 2:14

My dove in rock openings and cliff cracks, let me see your face. 📖

Let me hear your voice.

Your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely.

## Song of Solomon 2:15

Catch the foxes for us, the little foxes ruining vineyards. 📖

Our vineyards are flowering.

**About the vineyards:** The image is retained without reducing it to one settled interpretation.

## Song of Solomon 2:16

My beloved is mine, and I am his. 📖

He feeds his flock among lilies.

## Song of Solomon 2:17

Before dawn comes and shadows flee, turn, my beloved. 📖

Be like a gazelle or young stag on Bether’s mountains.
