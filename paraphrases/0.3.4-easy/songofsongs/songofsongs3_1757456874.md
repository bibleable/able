# Song of Solomon 3 — A night search and Solomon’s procession

**About this chapter**

**Speaker guide — editorial**

The woman recounts her search in verses 1–5.

Verses 6–11 describe Solomon’s approaching carriage and wedding.

The observer’s identity in those verses is not stated.

## Song of Solomon 3:1

On my bed at night, I sought the one I love. 📖

I searched but did not find him.

## Song of Solomon 3:2

I said, “I will rise and go around the city, through streets and squares. 📖

I will seek the one I love.”

I searched but did not find him.

## Song of Solomon 3:3

I met the watchmen going around the city. 📖

I asked, “Have you seen the one I love?”

## Song of Solomon 3:4

Just after passing them, I found the one I love. 📖

I held him and would not let go.

I brought him into my mother’s house, to the room of the woman who conceived me.

## Song of Solomon 3:5

Daughters of Jerusalem, I solemnly charge you by gazelles and does of the field. 📖

Do not stir or awaken love until the right time.

**Help with gazelles and does:** Swift deerlike animals and female deer.

Solemnly charge means make a very serious request.

## Song of Solomon 3:6

Who comes from the wilderness like a column of smoke? 📖

It smells of myrrh, frankincense, and all the merchant’s spices.

**Help with myrrh and frankincense:** Fragrant substances.

## Song of Solomon 3:7

Look, it is Solomon’s carriage! 📖

Sixty of Israel’s mightiest men escort it.

## Song of Solomon 3:8

All are skilled with swords and experienced in war. 📖

Each has a sword at his side, ready for night terrors.

## Song of Solomon 3:9

King Solomon made his carriage from Lebanon’s timber. 📖

## Song of Solomon 3:10

He made silver posts, a gold base, and a purple fabric seat. 📖

The daughters of Jerusalem decorated its inside with love.

## Song of Solomon 3:11

Daughters of Zion, come and gaze at King Solomon. 📖

He wears the crown his mother gave him on his wedding day, his heart’s joyful day.
