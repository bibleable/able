# Song of Solomon 5 — A missed meeting and praise of the beloved

**About this chapter**

**Speaker guide — editorial**

The man speaks at the start of verse 1; its closing invitation is not explicitly assigned.

The woman recounts a night encounter in verses 2–8 and describes her beloved in verses 10–16.

Others ask her the question in verse 9.

The guide is provisional.

Gold, stone, trees, and perfume are poetic pictures of the beloved’s beauty.

## Song of Solomon 5:1

I entered my garden, my sister, my bride. 📖

I gathered my myrrh and spice.

I ate my honeycomb and honey.

I drank my wine and milk.

Eat, friends, and drink.

Drink freely, beloved.

## Song of Solomon 5:2

I sleep, but my heart is awake. 📖

A sound!

My beloved knocks: “Open for me, my sister, darling, dove, flawless one.

My head is soaked with dew, my hair with the night’s damp.”

## Song of Solomon 5:3

I took off my robe. 📖

Must I put it on again?

I washed my feet.

Must I dirty them again?

## Song of Solomon 5:4

My beloved put his hand to the latch. 📖

My heart pounded for him.

## Song of Solomon 5:5

I rose to open for my beloved. 📖

My hands dripped myrrh.

Flowing myrrh was on my fingers and the bolt’s handles.

**Help with myrrh and bolt:** A fragrant substance, and the bar used to lock the door.

## Song of Solomon 5:6

I opened for my beloved, but he had turned away and gone. 📖

My heart sank when he left.

I searched but did not find him.

I called, but he did not answer.

## Song of Solomon 5:7

I met watchmen going around the city. 📖

They beat and bruised me.

Those wall guardians took my cloak.

## Song of Solomon 5:8

Daughters of Jerusalem, I solemnly charge you: if you find my beloved, tell him I am sick with love. 📖

## Song of Solomon 5:9

Most beautiful woman, how is your beloved better than others? 📖

How is he better than another, that you charge us like this?

## Song of Solomon 5:10

My beloved is dazzling and ruddy, outstanding among ten thousand. 📖

**Help with ruddy:** Having a healthy red color.

## Song of Solomon 5:11

His head is purest gold. 📖

His hair is wavy and black as a raven.

## Song of Solomon 5:12

His eyes are like doves beside water streams, washed in milk and set like jewels. 📖

## Song of Solomon 5:13

His cheeks are like spice beds and perfume towers. 📖

His lips are like lilies dripping flowing myrrh.

## Song of Solomon 5:14

His arms are gold rods set with beryl. 📖

His body is polished ivory decorated with sapphires.

**Help with beryl, ivory, and sapphires:** Beryl and sapphires are precious stones.

Ivory is material from animal tusks.

These are admiring images, rather than literal body materials.

## Song of Solomon 5:15

His legs are marble pillars on pure gold bases. 📖

His appearance is like Lebanon, majestic as cedars.

## Song of Solomon 5:16

His mouth is most sweet. 📖

He is completely lovely.

Daughters of Jerusalem, this is my beloved and my friend.
