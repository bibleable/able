# Isaiah 4 — A Branch and shelter for Zion

**About this chapter**

The chapter first describes a plea after judgment.

It then speaks of the LORD’s Branch, cleansing, and protection for the survivors.

## Isaiah 4:1

That day seven women will seize one man. 📖

They will say, “We will provide our own bread and clothes.

Just let us be called by your name.

Take away our disgrace!”

## Isaiah 4:2

That day the LORD’s Branch will be beautiful and glorious. 📖

The land’s fruit will be Israel’s survivors’ pride and glory.

**Help with Branch:** A growth image used in this promise.

Its precise identity is not supplied here.

## Isaiah 4:3

Everyone remaining in Zion and Jerusalem will be called holy. 📖

These are all in Jerusalem recorded among the living,

## Isaiah 4:4

when the Lord washes away Zion’s daughters’ filth.

He will cleanse the bloodstains from Jerusalem’s heart. 📖

He will do this by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of fire.

## Isaiah 4:5

The LORD will create a smoky cloud by day over all Mount Zion and its gatherings. 📖

At night there will be a glowing flame.

A canopy will cover all the glory,

## Isaiah 4:6

a shelter giving daytime shade from heat. 📖

It will be a refuge and hiding place from storm and rain.
