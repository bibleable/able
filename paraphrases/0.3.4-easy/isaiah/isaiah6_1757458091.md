# Isaiah 6 — Isaiah’s vision and commission

**About this chapter**

Isaiah recounts a vision of the Lord, the cleansing of his lips, and a difficult message he must carry.

## Isaiah 6:1

In the year King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord sitting on a high, exalted throne. 📖

His robe’s train filled the temple.

**Help with train:** The long trailing part of a robe.

## Isaiah 6:2

Seraphim stood above Him, each with six wings. 📖

Two covered their faces, two covered their feet, and two were for flying.

**Help with seraphim:** Heavenly beings described in the vision.

## Isaiah 6:3

They called to each other, “Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Hosts! 📖

All the earth is full of His glory.”

**Help with holy and LORD of Hosts:** Completely pure and set apart, and the LORD who commands heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 6:4

Their voices shook the doorposts and thresholds. 📖

Smoke filled the temple.

## Isaiah 6:5

I said, “Disaster to me! 📖

I am ruined.

My lips are unclean, and I live among people with unclean lips.

My eyes saw the King, the LORD of Hosts.”

## Isaiah 6:6

A seraph flew to me carrying a glowing coal. 📖

He had taken it from the altar with tongs.

**Help with altar and tongs:** A place for offerings, and a tool for gripping hot things.

## Isaiah 6:7

He touched my mouth with it and said, “This touched your lips. 📖

Your wrongdoing is removed.

Your sin is atoned for.”

**Help with atoned for:** Its guilt has been dealt with so it is forgiven.

## Isaiah 6:8

I heard the Lord ask, “Whom shall I send? 📖

Who will go for Us?”

I said, “Here I am.

Send me!”

## Isaiah 6:9

He answered, “Go and tell this people, ‘Keep hearing, but never understand. 📖

Keep seeing, but never perceive.’

## Isaiah 6:10

Make this people’s hearts calloused. 📖

Deafen their ears and close their eyes.

Otherwise they might see, hear, understand in their hearts, turn, and be healed.”

**Help with these images:** Hearing, seeing, and the heart describe receiving and understanding the message.

The difficult command is retained without changing its force.

## Isaiah 6:11

I asked, “How long, Lord?” 📖

He answered, “Until cities are ruined without residents, houses are empty, and the land is devastated,

## Isaiah 6:12

until the LORD drives people far away and the land is entirely abandoned. 📖

## Isaiah 6:13

Even if a tenth remains, it will be burned again. 📖

Terebinth and oak leave stumps when cut down.

In the same way, the holy seed will be a stump in the land.”

**Help with terebinth and holy seed:** A tree, and an image of what remains after judgment.
