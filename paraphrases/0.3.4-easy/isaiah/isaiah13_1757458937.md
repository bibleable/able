# Isaiah 13 — A message against Babylon

**About this chapter**

Isaiah son of Amoz receives a message about Babylon’s destruction.

The chapter includes war, murder, and sexual violence.

## Isaiah 13:1

This is the message against Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz received. 📖

## Isaiah 13:2

Raise a banner on a bare hilltop. 📖

Call aloud to them.

Wave your hand so that they enter the gates of the nobles.

**Help with a banner**

A banner is a raised sign used to gather people.

## Isaiah 13:3

God says, “I have commanded those I set apart. 📖

I have called My warriors to carry out My anger and rejoice in My victory.”

## Isaiah 13:4

Listen to the noise on the mountains, like a great crowd! 📖

Listen to the uproar among kingdoms, like nations gathered together!

The LORD of Hosts is gathering an army for war.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 13:5

They come from faraway lands, from the ends of the heavens. 📖

The LORD and the weapons of His anger come to destroy the whole country.

## Isaiah 13:6

Cry out in grief, because the Day of the LORD is near! 📖

It will come as destruction from the Almighty.

**Help with Day of the LORD**

Here the phrase names the time when God acts in judgment.

## Isaiah 13:7

Every hand will hang weakly. 📖

Every man’s heart will melt.

**Help with a melting heart**

This image describes people losing courage.

## Isaiah 13:8

Terror, pain, and suffering will grip them. 📖

They will twist in pain like a woman giving birth.

They will look at one another, their faces hot with fear.

## Isaiah 13:9

Look! 📖

The Day of the LORD is coming, cruel and full of fury and burning anger.

It will leave the earth deserted and destroy the sinners in it.

## Isaiah 13:10

The stars in heaven and their groups will not give light. 📖

The rising sun will become dark.

The moon will not give its light.

## Isaiah 13:11

God says, “I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their wrongdoing. 📖

I will end the arrogance of proud people.

I will bring down the pride of cruel people.

## Isaiah 13:12

“I will make people harder to find than pure gold. 📖

Human beings will be rarer than the gold of Ophir.

## Isaiah 13:13

“I will make the heavens tremble. 📖

The earth will be shaken from its place.

This will happen at the anger of the LORD of Hosts, on the day of His burning anger.”

## Isaiah 13:14

Each person will return to his own people and flee to his own country. 📖

They will be like a hunted gazelle or a sheep without a shepherd.

## Isaiah 13:15

Anyone caught will be stabbed. 📖

Anyone captured will die by the sword.

## Isaiah 13:16

Their babies will be smashed to pieces before their eyes. 📖

Their houses will be robbed.

Their wives will be raped.

**About this violence**

The passage describes killing babies and forcing women into sex.

These disturbing details remain part of the warning.

## Isaiah 13:17

God says, “Look! 📖

I will stir up the Medes against them.

The Medes have no regard for silver and no desire for gold.

## Isaiah 13:18

“Their bows will smash young men to pieces. 📖

They will have no mercy on the children of the womb.

They will look on children without pity.”

## Isaiah 13:19

Babylon is the jewel among kingdoms, the glory of the Chaldeans’ pride. 📖

But God will overthrow Babylon as He overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah.

## Isaiah 13:20

No one will ever live or settle there, from one generation to the next. 📖

No wandering person will pitch a tent there.

No shepherd will rest a flock there.

## Isaiah 13:21

Desert creatures will lie down there. 📖

Howling creatures will fill Babylon’s houses.

Ostriches will live there, and wild goats will jump about.

## Isaiah 13:22

Hyenas will howl in its forts, and jackals in its grand palaces. 📖

Babylon’s time is near.

Its days will not be extended.
