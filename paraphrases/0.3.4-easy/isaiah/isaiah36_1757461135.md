# Isaiah 36 — Assyria threatens Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Assyria’s representative brings a threatening message to Jerusalem.

His words about God are his own claims in the story.

## Isaiah 36:1

In the fourteenth year of Hezekiah’s rule, Sennacherib king of Assyria attacked all Judah’s fortified cities. 📖

He captured them.

## Isaiah 36:2

Assyria’s king sent the Rabshakeh with a large army from Lachish to King Hezekiah at Jerusalem. 📖

The Rabshakeh stopped beside the water channel of the upper pool.

It was on the road to the Launderer’s Field.

**Help with Rabshakeh**

This is a title for the Assyrian official who speaks in this chapter.

## Isaiah 36:3

Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the palace administrator, went out to him. 📖

So did Shebna the scribe and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder.

**Help with these jobs**

The administrator managed the palace.

A scribe wrote documents, and a recorder kept records.

## Isaiah 36:4

The Rabshakeh told them, “Tell Hezekiah what the great king, the king of Assyria, says. 📖

What makes you so confident?

## Isaiah 36:5

“You claim to have plans and strength for war, but these are empty words. 📖

Whom do you trust, now that you have rebelled against me?

## Isaiah 36:6

“Look! 📖

You trust Egypt, a broken reed used as a staff.

It pierces the hand of anyone who leans on it.

That is what Pharaoh king of Egypt is like for everyone who trusts him.

**Help with the reed**

A reed is a plant with a long stem.

The image describes help that fails and hurts the person relying on it.

## Isaiah 36:7

“You may say, ‘We trust the LORD our God.’ 📖

But is He not the One whose high places and altars Hezekiah removed?

Hezekiah told Judah and Jerusalem, ‘You must worship before this altar.’

**About this argument**

This is the Assyrian official’s challenge.

It is not the narrator’s judgment on Hezekiah’s actions.

## Isaiah 36:8

“Make a bargain with my master, the king of Assyria. 📖

I will give you two thousand horses, if you can put riders on them!

## Isaiah 36:9

“How can you drive back even one officer among the least of my master’s servants? 📖

You depend on Egypt for chariots and horsemen.

## Isaiah 36:10

“Did I come to destroy this land without the LORD? 📖

The LORD Himself told me, ‘Go against this land and destroy it.’”

**About this claim**

The Rabshakeh claims that the LORD sent him.

The chapter reports his words.

## Isaiah 36:11

Eliakim, Shebna, and Joah told the Rabshakeh, “Please speak to your servants in Aramaic, because we understand it. 📖

Do not speak Hebrew where the people on the wall can hear.”

## Isaiah 36:12

The Rabshakeh replied, “Did my master send me to speak only to you and your master? 📖

Did he not send me to the men sitting on the wall too?

With you, they are destined to eat their own dung and drink their own urine.”

**Help with these words**

Dung and urine are body waste.

The official threatens the people with extreme hunger and thirst during a siege.

## Isaiah 36:13

The Rabshakeh stood and shouted in Hebrew. 📖

“Hear the words of the great king, the king of Assyria!

## Isaiah 36:14

“The king says, ‘Do not let Hezekiah deceive you. 📖

He cannot rescue you.

## Isaiah 36:15

“‘Do not let Hezekiah persuade you to trust the LORD. 📖

He says, “The LORD will surely rescue us.

This city will not be handed over to Assyria’s king.”

## Isaiah 36:16

“‘Do not listen to Hezekiah. 📖

The king of Assyria says, “Make peace with me and come out to me.

Then each of you will eat from your own vine and fig tree.

Each will drink water from your own water storage pit,

## Isaiah 36:17

“‘“until I come and take you to a land like your own. 📖

It is a land of grain and new wine, of bread and vineyards.”

## Isaiah 36:18

“‘Do not let Hezekiah mislead you by saying, “The LORD will rescue us.” 📖

Has any nation’s god rescued its land from Assyria’s king?

## Isaiah 36:19

“‘Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? 📖

Where are the gods of Sepharvaim?

Have they rescued Samaria from my hand?

## Isaiah 36:20

“‘Which of all these lands’ gods has rescued its land from my hand? 📖

How can the LORD rescue Jerusalem from my hand?’”

## Isaiah 36:21

But the people stayed silent and did not answer a word. 📖

Hezekiah had ordered, “Do not answer him.”

## Isaiah 36:22

Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the palace administrator, came to Hezekiah. 📖

Shebna the scribe and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, came too.

Their clothes were torn.

They told Hezekiah the Rabshakeh’s words.

**Help with torn clothes**

Tearing clothes was a way to show grief or distress.
