# Isaiah 12 — Songs of thanks

**About this chapter**

The people are given words of praise to speak to God and to one another.

## Isaiah 12:1

On that day, you will say, “LORD, I will praise You. 📖

You were angry with me, but Your anger has turned away.

You have comforted me.

## Isaiah 12:2

“God is my salvation. 📖

I will trust and not be afraid.

The LORD GOD is my strength and my song.

He has also become my salvation.”

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 12:3

With joy, you will draw water from the springs of salvation. 📖

**Help with springs**

The water image describes receiving God’s rescue as a life-giving gift.

## Isaiah 12:4

On that day, you will say, “Give praise to the LORD! 📖

Announce His name!

Make His works known among the peoples.

Declare that His name is high above all.

## Isaiah 12:5

“Sing to the LORD, because He has done glorious things. 📖

Let the whole earth know about this.

## Isaiah 12:6

“People living in Zion, cry out and sing! 📖

The Holy One of Israel is great among you.”
