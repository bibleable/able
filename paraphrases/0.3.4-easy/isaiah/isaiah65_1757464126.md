# Isaiah 65 — Judgment and a new creation

**About this chapter**

The LORD distinguishes His servants from those who reject Him.

He promises new heavens, a new earth, and joy for Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 65:1

“I showed Myself to those who did not ask for Me. 📖

Those who did not seek Me found Me.

To a nation not calling My name, I said, ‘Here I am!

Here I am!’

## Isaiah 65:2

“All day, I held My hands out to stubborn people. 📖

They walk on the wrong path and follow their own ideas,

## Isaiah 65:3

“continually provoking Me to My face. 📖

They offer sacrifices in gardens and burn incense on brick altars,

## Isaiah 65:4

“sit among graves, and spend nights in secret places. 📖

They eat pig meat and polluted broth from their bowls.

**Help with incense and polluted broth**

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

Polluted broth means liquid food treated as unclean under worship requirements.

## Isaiah 65:5

“They say, ‘Keep to yourself! 📖

Do not come near me, because I am holier than you!’

These people are smoke in My nose, a fire burning all day.

## Isaiah 65:6

“Look! 📖

It is written before Me.

I will not stay silent, but repay them.

I will pay it back into their laps,

## Isaiah 65:7

“both for your wrongdoing and your fathers’ wrongdoing,” says the LORD. 📖

“They burned incense on mountains and insulted Me on hills.

So I will measure full payment for their earlier deeds into their laps.”

## Isaiah 65:8

The LORD says, “New wine is found in a cluster of grapes. 📖

People say, ‘Do not destroy it, because it contains a blessing.’

In the same way, I will act for My servants and not destroy them all.

## Isaiah 65:9

“I will bring descendants from Jacob and heirs from Judah. 📖

My chosen people will possess My mountains, and My servants will live there.

## Isaiah 65:10

“Sharon will become pasture for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a resting place for herds. 📖

This is for My people who seek Me.

## Isaiah 65:11

“But you abandon the LORD and forget My holy mountain. 📖

You set a table for Fortune and fill bowls of mixed wine for Destiny.

**Help with Fortune and Destiny**

These names refer to gods honored by the people being rebuked.

## Isaiah 65:12

“I will assign you to the sword. 📖

You will all kneel to be killed.

I called, but you did not answer.

I spoke, but you did not listen.

You did evil in My sight and chose what did not please Me.”

## Isaiah 65:13

The Lord GOD says, “My servants will eat, but you will be hungry. 📖

My servants will drink, but you will be thirsty.

My servants will rejoice, but you will be ashamed.

## Isaiah 65:14

“My servants will shout for joy with glad hearts. 📖

But you will cry out with heavy hearts and wail with broken spirits.

## Isaiah 65:15

“You will leave your name as a curse for My chosen ones. 📖

The Lord GOD will kill you.

But He will give His servants another name.

## Isaiah 65:16

“Whoever calls for a blessing in the land will do so by the God of truth. 📖

Whoever takes an oath in the land will swear by the God of truth.

The earlier troubles will be forgotten and hidden from My sight.

## Isaiah 65:17

“Look! 📖

I will create new heavens and a new earth.

The former things will not be remembered or come to mind.

## Isaiah 65:18

“Be glad and rejoice forever in what I create. 📖

I will create Jerusalem to be a joy and its people a delight.

## Isaiah 65:19

“I will rejoice in Jerusalem and delight in My people. 📖

Weeping and crying will no longer be heard there.

## Isaiah 65:20

“A nursing baby will no longer live only a few days. 📖

An old man will not fail to live out his years.

Someone dying at a hundred will be considered young.

Anyone not reaching a hundred will be considered cursed.

**About these words**

The promise describes long life in the restored community.

The reference to being considered cursed is retained.

It is not a judgment on people who die young today.

## Isaiah 65:21

“They will build houses and live in them. 📖

They will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

## Isaiah 65:22

“They will not build houses for others to occupy or plant for others to eat. 📖

My people’s days will be like a tree’s lifetime.

My chosen ones will fully enjoy their hands’ work.

## Isaiah 65:23

“They will not work for nothing or bear children doomed to disaster. 📖

They and their descendants with them will be a people blessed by the LORD.

## Isaiah 65:24

“Even before they call, I will answer. 📖

While they are still speaking, I will hear.

## Isaiah 65:25

“The wolf and lamb will feed together. 📖

The lion will eat straw like the ox, but the snake will eat dust.

They will not harm or destroy anywhere on My holy mountain,” says the LORD.
