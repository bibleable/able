# Isaiah 21 — Watch for the news

**About this chapter**

Isaiah reports visions and messages about the Desert by the Sea, Dumah, and Arabia.

The speakers include the Lord, a lookout, and people reporting what they see.

## Isaiah 21:1

This is the message against the Desert by the Sea. 📖

Like whirlwinds sweeping through the Negev, an invader comes from the desert, from a land of terror.

## Isaiah 21:2

A terrible vision is told to me. 📖

“The betrayer still betrays, and the destroyer still destroys.

Go up, Elam!

Surround the city, Media!

I will end all her groaning.”

**About the voice**

The vision brings a command and a declaration of judgment.

## Isaiah 21:3

So my body is full of suffering. 📖

Pain grips me like the pains of a woman giving birth.

What I hear confuses me.

What I see terrifies me.

## Isaiah 21:4

My heart grows weak. 📖

Fear makes me tremble.

The twilight I wanted has become a horror.

## Isaiah 21:5

They prepare a table and spread a carpet. 📖

They eat and drink!

Get up, princes!

Put oil on the shields!

## Isaiah 21:6

The Lord says to me, “Go and post a lookout. 📖

Tell him to report what he sees.

## Isaiah 21:7

“When he sees chariots with teams of horsemen, riders on donkeys, and riders on camels, he must watch very carefully.” 📖

## Isaiah 21:8

Then the lookout shouted, “My lord, day after day I stand on the watchtower. 📖

Night after night I stay at my post.

## Isaiah 21:9

“Look! 📖

Here come riders, horsemen in pairs.”

Someone answered, “Babylon has fallen, fallen!

All the images of its gods lie smashed on the ground!”

## Isaiah 21:10

My people, crushed on the threshing floor, I tell you what I heard from the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God. 📖

**Help with a threshing floor**

This is a place where grain is beaten to separate it from its stalks.

The image describes the people’s suffering.

LORD of Hosts is a title for the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 21:11

This is the message against Dumah. 📖

Someone calls to me from Seir.

“Watchman, how much of the night is left?

Watchman, how much of the night is left?”

## Isaiah 21:12

The watchman replies, “Morning has come, but night comes too. 📖

If you want to ask, then ask.

Come back again.”

## Isaiah 21:13

This is the message against Arabia. 📖

Traveling groups of Dedanites, you must spend the night in Arabia’s thickets.

## Isaiah 21:14

People living in Tema, bring water to those who are thirsty. 📖

Meet the fleeing people with food.

## Isaiah 21:15

They flee from the sword that has been drawn and the bow that is ready to shoot. 📖

They flee from the strain of battle.

## Isaiah 21:16

The Lord says to me, “Within one year, counted as a hired worker counts it, all Kedar’s glory will disappear. 📖

## Isaiah 21:17

“Few archers will remain among Kedar’s warriors.” 📖

The LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken.
