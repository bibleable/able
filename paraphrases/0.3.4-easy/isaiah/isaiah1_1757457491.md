# Isaiah 1 — Rebellion, judgment, and a call to turn

**About this chapter**

Isaiah’s vision concerns Judah and Jerusalem.

The chapter moves between the LORD’s speech and the prophet’s descriptions and appeals.

## Isaiah 1:1

This is the vision Isaiah son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem. 📖

He saw it during the reigns of Judah’s kings Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah.

## Isaiah 1:2

Heavens, listen! 📖

Earth, hear!

The LORD has spoken: “I raised children and brought them up.

But they rebelled against Me.

## Isaiah 1:3

An ox knows its owner, and a donkey its master’s manger. 📖

But Israel does not know.

My people do not understand.”

**Help with manger:** A container for animal food.

## Isaiah 1:4

How terrible for this sinful nation, people loaded with evil, a brood of evildoers and corrupt children! 📖

They abandoned the LORD.

They despised Israel’s Holy One and turned their backs on Him.

## Isaiah 1:5

Why seek more beatings? 📖

Why keep rebelling?

Your head has a great wound.

Your whole heart suffers.

## Isaiah 1:6

Nothing is healthy from your feet’s soles to your head’s top. 📖

There are only wounds, welts, and infected sores.

They are not cleaned, bandaged, or soothed with oil.

**Help with the wounded body:** The nation’s condition is pictured as a body covered in untreated injuries.

## Isaiah 1:7

Your land is empty and ruined. 📖

Your cities are burned.

Before your eyes, foreigners consume your fields.

Strangers have demolished and devastated the land.

## Isaiah 1:8

Daughter Zion is abandoned like a vineyard shelter or a shack in a cucumber field. 📖

She is like a city surrounded for attack.

**Help with Daughter Zion:** A poetic name for Jerusalem and its people.

## Isaiah 1:9

Without the few survivors the LORD of Hosts left us, we would be like Sodom and Gomorrah. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** The LORD who commands heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 1:10

Rulers of Sodom, hear the LORD’s word! 📖

People of Gomorrah, listen to our God’s instruction!

**About these names:** Isaiah addresses Judah’s people by the names of cities associated with judgment.

## Isaiah 1:11

The LORD says, “What good are your many sacrifices to Me? 📖

I have had enough burnt rams and well-fed cattle’s fat.

Bulls’, lambs’, and goats’ blood gives Me no delight.

## Isaiah 1:12

When you come before Me, who demanded this trampling of My courts? 📖

**Help with courts:** Open areas belonging to the place of worship.

## Isaiah 1:13

Bring no more worthless offerings. 📖

I detest your incense.

New Moons, Sabbaths, and sacred gatherings: I cannot bear evil in a solemn assembly.

**Help with incense and Sabbaths:** A fragrant substance burned in worship, and weekly holy rest days.

New Moons and gatherings were appointed worship occasions.

## Isaiah 1:14

I hate your New Moons and appointed feasts. 📖

They burden Me.

I am tired of carrying them.

## Isaiah 1:15

When you spread your hands in prayer, I will hide My eyes. 📖

Even if you pray many prayers, I will not listen.

Your hands are covered in blood.

## Isaiah 1:16

Wash and cleanse yourselves. 📖

Remove your evil deeds from My sight.

Stop doing evil!

## Isaiah 1:17

Learn what is right. 📖

Seek justice and correct the oppressor.

Defend fatherless children.

Plead the widow’s case.”

**Help with oppressor and widow:** Someone treating others cruelly, and a woman whose husband has died.

## Isaiah 1:18

The LORD says, “Come now, let us reason together. 📖

Though your sins are scarlet, they will be white as snow.

Though red as crimson, they will become like wool.

**Help with scarlet and crimson:** Deep red colors.

The colors picture sin being removed.

## Isaiah 1:19

If willing and obedient, you will eat the land’s best. 📖

## Isaiah 1:20

But if you resist and rebel, the sword will devour you.” 📖

The LORD’s mouth has spoken.

## Isaiah 1:21

See how the faithful city became a prostitute! 📖

She once was full of justice and righteousness.

Now murderers live there!

**Help with prostitute:** Someone who has sex for payment.

Here the city is pictured as unfaithful.

Righteousness means what is right and just.

## Isaiah 1:22

Your silver became dross. 📖

Water weakens your fine wine.

**Help with dross:** Waste removed when metal is purified.

These are pictures of corruption.

## Isaiah 1:23

Your rulers are rebels and thieves’ friends. 📖

They all love bribes and chase rewards.

They do not defend fatherless children.

Widows’ pleas never reach them.

## Isaiah 1:24

The Lord GOD of Hosts, Israel’s Mighty One, declares: “I will find relief from My foes. 📖

I will take revenge on My enemies.

## Isaiah 1:25

I will turn My hand against you. 📖

I will thoroughly purge your dross and remove all impurities.

## Isaiah 1:26

I will restore your judges and advisers as at the beginning. 📖

Afterward you will be called the City of Righteousness, the Faithful City.”

## Isaiah 1:27

Zion will be rescued through justice. 📖

Her repentant people will be rescued through righteousness.

**Help with repentant:** Turning away from wrongdoing.

## Isaiah 1:28

Rebels and sinners will be broken together. 📖

People abandoning the LORD will perish.

## Isaiah 1:29

You will be ashamed of sacred oaks you delighted in. 📖

You will be embarrassed by the gardens you chose.

**Help with sacred oaks and gardens:** Places associated here with rejected worship practices.

## Isaiah 1:30

You will become like an oak with dried leaves or a garden without water. 📖

## Isaiah 1:31

The strong man will become tinder, and his work a spark. 📖

Both will burn together, and no one will put out the flames.

**Help with tinder:** Dry material that catches fire easily.
