# Isaiah 2 — The LORD’s mountain and the humbling of pride

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes the nations learning the LORD’s ways.

He then speaks of pride, idols, and the LORD’s coming judgment.

## Isaiah 2:1

This message concerning Judah and Jerusalem was revealed to Isaiah son of Amoz: 📖

## Isaiah 2:2

In the last days, the LORD’s house’s mountain will stand above all mountains. 📖

It will rise above hills, and all nations will stream to it.

## Isaiah 2:3

Many peoples will say, “Come to the LORD’s mountain, to Jacob’s God’s house. 📖

He will teach His ways so we may walk His paths.”

Law will come from Zion, and the LORD’s word from Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 2:4

He will judge between nations and settle disputes for many peoples. 📖

They will hammer swords into plowshares and spears into pruning hooks.

Nations will stop fighting each other with swords.

They will no longer train for war.

**Help with plowshares and pruning hooks:** Farming tools for breaking soil and cutting plants.

## Isaiah 2:5

House of Jacob, come, let us walk in the LORD’s light. 📖

## Isaiah 2:6

You abandoned Your people, Jacob’s house, because eastern influences fill them. 📖

They tell fortunes like Philistines and shake hands with foreigners’ children.

## Isaiah 2:7

Their land is full of silver and gold, with treasures beyond limit. 📖

Their land is full of horses and countless chariots.

## Isaiah 2:8

Their land is full of idols. 📖

They bow to what their hands and fingers made.

**Help with idols:** Images worshiped as gods.

## Isaiah 2:9

Humanity is brought low, and people are humbled. 📖

Do not forgive them!

## Isaiah 2:10

Enter the rocks and hide in dust from the LORD’s terror and His glorious majesty. 📖

**Help with majesty:** Great royal power and glory.

## Isaiah 2:11

Proud looks and human loftiness will be humbled. 📖

The LORD alone will be lifted high that day.

## Isaiah 2:12

The Day of the LORD of Hosts comes against everything proud, lofty, and raised high. 📖

It will be humbled,

## Isaiah 2:13

against all Lebanon’s tall cedars and all Bashan’s oaks, 📖

## Isaiah 2:14

against every tall mountain and high hill, 📖

## Isaiah 2:15

against every high tower and fortified wall, 📖

## Isaiah 2:16

against every ship of Tarshish and every stately vessel. 📖

**Help with fortified and stately vessel:** Built with defenses, and an impressive ship.

## Isaiah 2:17

Human pride and loftiness will be humbled. 📖

The LORD alone will be lifted high that day,

## Isaiah 2:18

and idols will disappear completely. 📖

## Isaiah 2:19

People will flee into rock caves and ground holes. 📖

They flee the LORD’s terror and glorious majesty when He rises to shake the earth.

## Isaiah 2:20

That day people will throw their silver and gold idols to moles and bats. 📖

They made those idols to worship.

## Isaiah 2:21

They will flee into rock caverns and cliff cracks. 📖

They flee the LORD’s terror and glorious majesty when He rises to shake the earth.

## Isaiah 2:22

Stop trusting human beings, who have only breath in their nostrils. 📖

What are they worth?
