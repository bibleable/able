# Isaiah 66 — Worship, comfort, and final judgment

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges false worship and promises comfort for Jerusalem.

The book ends with gathering from the nations, worship, and a severe picture of judgment.

## Isaiah 66:1

The LORD says, “Heaven is My throne, and earth is My footstool. 📖

What kind of house will you build for Me?

Where will My resting place be?

## Isaiah 66:2

“Did My hand not make all these things, so that they came to exist?” declares the LORD. 📖

“I will value the person who is humble, sorry for sin in spirit, and trembling at My word.

## Isaiah 66:3

“Whoever kills an ox is like someone killing a man. 📖

Whoever sacrifices a lamb is like someone breaking a dog’s neck.

Whoever brings a grain offering is like someone offering pig’s blood.

Whoever offers frankincense is like someone blessing an idol.

They have chosen their own ways and delighted in their disgusting practices.

**About these comparisons**

The passage compares the people’s offerings with acts that are violent or unacceptable in worship.

Frankincense is a substance burned for its sweet smell.

## Isaiah 66:4

“So I will choose their punishment and bring terror on them. 📖

I called, but no one answered.

I spoke, but no one listened.

They did evil in My sight and chose what did not please Me.”

## Isaiah 66:5

You who tremble at His word, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

“Your brothers hate you and exclude you because of My name.

They say, ‘Let the LORD be glorified so that we can see your joy!’

But they will be ashamed.”

## Isaiah 66:6

Hear the uproar from the city and the voice from the temple! 📖

It is the LORD’s voice, repaying His enemies what they deserve!

## Isaiah 66:7

“Before she was in labor, she gave birth. 📖

Before she had pain, she delivered a boy.

## Isaiah 66:8

“Who has heard or seen such things? 📖

Can a country be born in a day or a nation delivered in an instant?

Yet as soon as Zion was in labor, she gave birth to her children.

**Help with the birth image**

Zion’s people are pictured as children born to a mother.

## Isaiah 66:9

“Would I bring a baby to the point of birth and not deliver it?” says the LORD. 📖

“Would I who deliver close the womb?” says your God.

## Isaiah 66:10

All who love Jerusalem, be glad for it and rejoice over it. 📖

All who mourn over it, rejoice greatly with it,

## Isaiah 66:11

so that you may nurse and be satisfied at its comforting breasts. 📖

You may drink deeply and delight in its glorious plenty.

**Help with nursing**

The city is pictured as a mother feeding and comforting her children.

## Isaiah 66:12

The LORD says, “I will extend peace to it like a river, and the nations’ wealth like a flowing stream. 📖

You will nurse, be carried on its arm, and bounced on its knees.

## Isaiah 66:13

“As a mother comforts her son, I will comfort you. 📖

You will be comforted over Jerusalem.”

## Isaiah 66:14

When you see this, you will rejoice and flourish like grass. 📖

The LORD’s hand will be shown to His servants, but His anger to His enemies.

## Isaiah 66:15

Look! 📖

The LORD will come with fire, His chariots like a whirlwind.

He will carry out His anger with fury and His rebuke with flames.

## Isaiah 66:16

The LORD will carry out judgment on all humanity with fire and His sword. 📖

Many will be killed by the LORD.

## Isaiah 66:17

The LORD declares, “Some set themselves apart and purify themselves to enter the groves. 📖

They follow someone in the center of those eating pig meat, vermin, and rats.

They will die together.

**Help with groves and vermin**

Groves are groups of trees, used here for worship.

Vermin means animals considered harmful or unclean in this passage.

The preparation described is for worship the LORD condemns.

## Isaiah 66:18

“I know their deeds and thoughts. 📖

I am coming to gather all nations and languages.

They will come and see My glory.

## Isaiah 66:19

“I will set a sign among them and send survivors to the nations. 📖

They will go to Tarshish, Put, Lud’s archers, Tubal, Javan, and distant islands.

These have not heard of My fame or seen My glory.

The survivors will announce My glory among the nations.

## Isaiah 66:20

“They will bring all your brothers from all nations as a gift to the LORD. 📖

They will come on horses, chariots, wagons, mules, and camels to My holy mountain Jerusalem,” says the LORD.

“It will be like Israelites bringing an offering in a clean vessel to the LORD’s house.

**Help with clean vessel**

A vessel is a container.

Clean here means fit for use in worship.

## Isaiah 66:21

“I will choose some of them as priests and Levites,” says the LORD. 📖

**Help with Levites**

Levites are members of Levi’s tribe who served in worship.

## Isaiah 66:22

“The new heavens and new earth I make will endure before Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

“In the same way, your descendants and your name will endure.

## Isaiah 66:23

“From one New Moon to another and one Sabbath to another, all humanity will come to worship before Me.” 📖

This is what the LORD says.

**Help with New Moon and Sabbath**

The New Moon marked the beginning of a month.

The Sabbath is the weekly day set apart for rest and worship.

## Isaiah 66:24

“As they go out, they will see the dead bodies of the men who rebelled against Me. 📖

Their worm will never die, and their fire will never be put out.

They will be a horror to all humanity.”

**About this difficult ending**

The final verse describes corpses, an undying worm, and fire that is never put out.

The severe warning remains in the draft.
