# Isaiah 30 — A warning about Egypt and a promise of help

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges an alliance with Egypt.

He speaks of rejected teaching, future mercy, and judgment on Assyria.

## Isaiah 30:1

The LORD declares, “How terrible for rebellious children who carry out a plan that is not Mine! 📖

They form an alliance against My will and pile sin on sin.

**Help with alliance**

An alliance is an agreement to support one another.

## Isaiah 30:2

“They go down to Egypt without asking My advice. 📖

They seek shelter under Pharaoh’s protection and safety in Egypt’s shade.

**Help with Pharaoh**

Pharaoh is a title for Egypt’s king.

## Isaiah 30:3

“But Pharaoh’s protection will become your shame. 📖

The safety of Egypt’s shade will become your disgrace.

## Isaiah 30:4

“Their princes are at Zoan. 📖

Their messengers have arrived in Hanes.

## Isaiah 30:5

“But everyone will be ashamed because these people are useless to them. 📖

They bring no help or benefit, only shame and disgrace.”

## Isaiah 30:6

This is the message against the animals of the Negev. 📖

They travel through a land of hardship and distress, of lionesses and lions, vipers and flying snakes.

They carry wealth on donkeys’ backs and treasures on camels’ humps to a people who cannot help them.

## Isaiah 30:7

Egypt’s help is useless and empty. 📖

So I have called it Rahab Who Sits Still.

**Help with this name**

The name mocks Egypt’s claim to be powerful while it gives no useful help.

## Isaiah 30:8

Go now and write it on a tablet in front of them. 📖

Write it on a scroll.

It will be a witness for the days to come, forever and ever.

## Isaiah 30:9

These are rebellious people, children who tell lies. 📖

They are children unwilling to obey the LORD’s teaching.

## Isaiah 30:10

They tell the seers, “Stop seeing visions!” 📖

They tell the prophets, “Do not tell us true messages!

Tell us pleasant things.

Prophesy things that only seem true.

## Isaiah 30:11

“Get out of the way. 📖

Turn off the road.

Get rid of the Holy One of Israel for us!”

## Isaiah 30:12

The Holy One of Israel says, “You have rejected this message. 📖

You trust in oppression and depend on deceit.

## Isaiah 30:13

“So your wrongdoing is like a bulging gap in a high wall that is about to fail. 📖

It will collapse suddenly, in an instant!

## Isaiah 30:14

“It will break like a potter’s jar, shattered into pieces. 📖

No piece in the dust will be big enough to scoop coals from a hearth.

None will be big enough to skim water from a storage pool.”

## Isaiah 30:15

The Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel, has said, “By turning back and resting, you would be saved. 📖

Your strength would be in quiet trust.

But you were unwilling.”

## Isaiah 30:16

You say, “No! 📖

We will flee on horses.”

So you will flee!

You say, “We will ride swift horses.”

But those chasing you will be faster.

## Isaiah 30:17

A thousand will flee at the threat of one. 📖

At the threat of five, you will all flee.

You will be left alone like a pole on a mountaintop, like a banner on a hill.

## Isaiah 30:18

So the LORD longs to be kind to you. 📖

He rises to show you compassion, because the LORD is a just God.

Blessed are all who wait for Him.

## Isaiah 30:19

People in Zion who live in Jerusalem, you will weep no more. 📖

He will surely be kind when you cry for help.

When He hears, He will answer you.

## Isaiah 30:20

The Lord will give you the bread of hardship and the water of suffering. 📖

But your Teacher will no longer hide Himself.

Your own eyes will see Him.

**Help with bread and water**

The images describe hardship as part of the people’s daily experience.

## Isaiah 30:21

Whether you turn right or left, you will hear this command behind you. 📖

“This is the way.

Walk in it.”

## Isaiah 30:22

You will make your silver-covered idols and gold-covered images unfit for worship. 📖

You will throw them away like cloths used during menstruation.

You will tell them, “Go away!”

**Help with the comparison**

Menstruation is bleeding that many women experience regularly.

The image describes throwing idols away like used cloths.

It does not say menstruating people are morally bad.

## Isaiah 30:23

Then He will send rain for the seed you planted in the ground. 📖

Food from your land will be rich and plentiful.

On that day, your cattle will graze in open pastures.

## Isaiah 30:24

The oxen and donkeys working the ground will eat salted animal feed. 📖

It will be cleaned by tossing it with a shovel and pitchfork.

**Help with cleaning the feed**

Tossing it lets the wind separate light unwanted material from the grain.

## Isaiah 30:25

Streams will flow from every high mountain and every raised hill. 📖

This will happen on the day of great slaughter, when the towers fall.

## Isaiah 30:26

The moon’s light will be as bright as the sun. 📖

The sun’s light will be seven times brighter, like the light of seven days.

This will happen when the LORD binds up His people’s brokenness and heals the wounds He inflicted.

## Isaiah 30:27

Look! 📖

The Name of the LORD comes from far away with burning anger and thick smoke.

His lips are full of fury.

His tongue is like a fire that burns things up.

## Isaiah 30:28

His breath is like a rushing flood that rises to the neck. 📖

He comes to sift the nations through a sieve of destruction.

He puts a bridle in the peoples’ jaws to lead them astray.

**Help with sieve and bridle**

A sieve separates material by letting smaller pieces pass through.

A bridle controls an animal’s movements.

Here these objects picture God judging and directing nations.

## Isaiah 30:29

You will sing as on the night of a holy festival. 📖

Your heart will rejoice like someone walking to flute music.

That person goes up to the LORD’s mountain, to the Rock of Israel.

**Help with Rock of Israel**

This is an image of God’s strength and protection.

## Isaiah 30:30

The LORD will make His majestic voice heard and His powerful arm seen. 📖

He will strike in fierce anger with fire that burns things up.

He will strike with heavy rain, storm, and hailstones.

## Isaiah 30:31

Assyria will be shattered at the LORD’s voice. 📖

He will strike them with His royal rod.

## Isaiah 30:32

Every time the LORD’s rod of punishment strikes them, tambourines and lyres will sound. 📖

He will fight with weapons raised.

**Help with lyres**

Lyres are stringed musical instruments.

## Isaiah 30:33

Topheth has long been prepared. 📖

It has been made ready for the king.

Its pile for burning the dead is deep and wide, with plenty of fire and wood.

The LORD’s breath lights it like a flood of burning sulfur.

**Help with sulfur**

Sulfur is a substance that burns.

The passage uses it in a picture of terrible destruction.
