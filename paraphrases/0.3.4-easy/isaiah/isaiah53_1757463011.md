# Isaiah 53 — The servant suffers for others

**About this chapter**

This chapter describes the suffering of God’s servant and what it achieves for others.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Isaiah 53:1

Who has believed our message? 📖

Who has seen the LORD's arm revealed?

**Help with this picture:** The LORD's arm pictures his power at work.

## Isaiah 53:2

The servant grew before God like a young plant, like a root in dry ground. 📖

He had no royal appearance or beauty to attract us.

## Isaiah 53:3

People despised and rejected him. 📖

He knew sorrow and suffering.

People turned their faces away.

We did not value him.

## Isaiah 53:4

He carried our weaknesses and sorrows. 📖

But we thought God was striking and punishing him.

## Isaiah 53:5

He was pierced because of our wrongs. 📖

He was crushed because of our sins.

His punishment brought us peace.

His wounds bring us healing.

## Isaiah 53:6

We all wandered like sheep. 📖

Each followed our own way.

The LORD placed all our wrongdoing on him.

## Isaiah 53:7

People hurt and oppressed him, but he did not speak. 📖

He was like a lamb led to be killed.

He was silent like a sheep before people cutting its wool.

## Isaiah 53:8

He was taken away through cruel treatment and judgment. 📖

Who can tell of his descendants?

He was cut off from the living.

He was struck for my people's wrongs.

**Help with words:** Cut off from the living means killed.

## Isaiah 53:9

He was assigned a grave with wicked people. 📖

In death he was with a rich man.

Yet he had done no violence and told no lies.

## Isaiah 53:10

The LORD chose to crush him and make him suffer. 📖

When his life is made an offering for guilt, he will see his offspring and live long.

The LORD's purpose will succeed through him.

**Help with a word:** Offspring means children or descendants.

## Isaiah 53:11

After deep suffering, he will see the light of life and be satisfied. 📖

God says his righteous servant will make many right with him through his knowledge.

The servant will carry their wrongs.

## Isaiah 53:12

God will give him a share with the great. 📖

He will share the rewards of victory with the strong.

He gave his life to death and was counted among wrongdoers.

He carried many people's sins and prayed for wrongdoers.
