# Isaiah 53 — The servant suffers for others

**About this chapter**

This chapter describes the suffering of God’s servant and what it achieves for others.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Isaiah 53:1

Who has believed our message? 📖

Who has seen the LORD's arm revealed?

**Help with this picture:** The LORD's arm pictures his power at work.

## Isaiah 53:2

The Servant grew before God like a tender shoot, like a root from dry ground. 📖

He had no grand appearance or majesty to attract us.

He had no beauty that made us desire Him.

**Help with shoot and root**

These plant images describe the Servant’s growth in humble conditions.

## Isaiah 53:3

People despised and rejected Him. 📖

He was a man of sorrows who knew grief.

Like someone people hide their faces from, He was despised.

We did not value Him.

## Isaiah 53:4

He surely took up our weaknesses and carried our sorrows. 📖

Yet we considered Him stricken, struck down by God, and afflicted.

**Help with stricken and afflicted**

These words describe being hit by suffering or trouble.

The second sentence reports what “we” thought about Him.

## Isaiah 53:5

He was pierced for our offenses. 📖

He was crushed for our wrongdoing.

The punishment that brought us peace was on Him.

By His stripes, we are healed.

**Help with stripes**

Stripes here means wounds from being beaten.

## Isaiah 53:6

We all wandered like sheep. 📖

Each followed our own way.

The LORD placed all our wrongdoing on him.

## Isaiah 53:7

He was oppressed and made to suffer, but He did not open His mouth. 📖

He was led like a lamb to be killed.

Like a sheep silent before people cutting its wool, He did not open His mouth.

## Isaiah 53:8

He was taken away through cruel treatment and judgment. 📖

Who can tell of his descendants?

He was cut off from the living.

He was struck for my people's wrongs.

**Help with words:** Cut off from the living means killed.

## Isaiah 53:9

He was assigned a grave with wicked people. 📖

In death he was with a rich man.

Yet he had done no violence and told no lies.

## Isaiah 53:10

Yet it was the LORD’s will to crush Him and make Him suffer. 📖

When His soul is made a guilt offering, He will see His offspring and have His days prolonged.

The LORD’s good purpose will succeed in His hand.

**Help with these words**

A guilt offering was an offering made to deal with wrongdoing.

Here the Servant’s own soul or life is described as an offering.

Offspring means children or descendants.

## Isaiah 53:11

After His soul’s suffering, He will see the light of life and be satisfied. 📖

“By His knowledge, My righteous Servant will justify many.

He will carry their wrongdoing.”

**About the voice and words**

“My righteous Servant” identifies God as speaking about the Servant.

Justify means to declare or make right.

This remains a provisional explanation for further editorial review.

## Isaiah 53:12

“So I will give Him a share with the great. 📖

He will divide goods taken in victory with the strong.

This is because He poured out His life to death and was counted with wrongdoers.

Yet He carried the sin of many and prayed for wrongdoers.”

**About the speaker**

God continues speaking about the Servant.

Praying for others is also called intercession.
