# Isaiah 47 — Babylon’s pride is brought low

**About this chapter**

Babylon is pictured as a proud queen who is humiliated.

The chapter includes forced labor, exposure, bereavement, and failed magic.

## Isaiah 47:1

“Virgin Daughter of Babylon, go down and sit in the dust. 📖

Daughter of the Chaldeans, sit on the ground without a throne!

You will no longer be called tender or delicate.

**Help with these names**

Babylon and its people are pictured as a woman losing royal power.

## Isaiah 47:2

“Take millstones and grind flour. 📖

Remove your veil and strip off your skirt.

Uncover your thigh and wade through the streams.

**Help with millstones**

Millstones are heavy stones used to grind grain.

## Isaiah 47:3

“Your nakedness will be uncovered, and your shame exposed. 📖

I will take vengeance.

I will spare no one.”

## Isaiah 47:4

Our Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel. 📖

His name is the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with these titles**

A Redeemer rescues or sets people free.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 47:5

“Daughter of the Chaldeans, sit silently and go into darkness. 📖

You will no longer be called queen of kingdoms.

## Isaiah 47:6

“I was angry with My people. 📖

I treated My own inheritance as unholy and put them under your control.

You showed them no mercy.

Even on elderly people, you laid a very heavy yoke.

**Help with inheritance and yoke**

God’s inheritance refers to His people.

A yoke is a wooden bar laid on working animals.

Here it pictures harsh control.

## Isaiah 47:7

“You said, ‘I will be queen forever.’ 📖

You did not take these things to heart or think about their result.

## Isaiah 47:8

“Now hear this, you who love luxury and sit securely. 📖

You tell yourself, ‘I am, and no one is beside me.

I will never be a widow or lose children.’

## Isaiah 47:9

“Both things will overtake you in a moment, in a single day. 📖

You will lose children and become a widow.

They will come in full measure, despite your many acts of magic and powerful spells.

## Isaiah 47:10

“You felt secure in your wickedness and said, ‘No one sees me.’ 📖

Your wisdom and knowledge led you the wrong way.

You told yourself, ‘I am, and no one is beside me.’

## Isaiah 47:11

“But disaster will come, and you will not know how to charm it away. 📖

Trouble will fall on you, and you cannot keep it away.

Destruction will come suddenly and unexpectedly.

## Isaiah 47:12

“So stand firm with your spells and the many acts of magic that have tired you since youth. 📖

Perhaps you will succeed.

Perhaps you will cause terror!

## Isaiah 47:13

“Your many advisers have tired you. 📖

Let them come forward now and save you.

Let your astrologers come, who watch the stars and predict your fate each month.

**Help with astrologers**

Astrologers claim to predict events from the positions of stars.

## Isaiah 47:14

“They are like stubble, and fire will burn them up. 📖

They cannot rescue themselves from the flame’s power.

This is no bed of coals to warm them, no fire to sit beside.

**Help with stubble**

Stubble is the stalk left after grain is cut.

## Isaiah 47:15

“That is what these people are to you, those you worked and traded with since youth. 📖

Each goes his own way.

Not one can save you.”
