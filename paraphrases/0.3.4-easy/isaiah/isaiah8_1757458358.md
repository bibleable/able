# Isaiah 8 — A child’s name and the coming flood

**About this chapter**

Isaiah reports the LORD’s instructions and warnings.

The chapter uses a flood, a stumbling stone, and darkness as pictures of judgment.

## Isaiah 8:1

The LORD told me, “Take a large scroll. 📖

Write Maher-shalal-hash-baz on it with an ordinary stylus.

**Help with scroll and stylus:** A rolled writing sheet, and a writing tool.

## Isaiah 8:2

I will appoint trustworthy witnesses for Myself: Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberekiah.” 📖

## Isaiah 8:3

I had sex with the prophetess. 📖

She conceived and bore a son.

The LORD told me, “Name him Maher-shalal-hash-baz.

**Help with prophetess:** A woman who speaks God’s message.

## Isaiah 8:4

Before he can cry ‘Father’ or ‘Mother,’ Assyria’s king will carry off Damascus’s wealth and Samaria’s plunder.” 📖

## Isaiah 8:5

The LORD spoke further to me: 📖

## Isaiah 8:6

“This people rejected Shiloah’s gentle waters. 📖

They rejoiced in Rezin and Remaliah’s son.

## Isaiah 8:7

So the Lord will bring mighty Euphrates floodwaters against them: Assyria’s king in all his splendor. 📖

It will overflow its channels and banks.

## Isaiah 8:8

It will pour into Judah, swirling and sweeping up to the neck. 📖

Its spreading streams will cover your whole land, Immanuel!

**Help with the flood:** The king’s invasion is pictured as overwhelming waters.

## Isaiah 8:9

Huddle together, peoples, and be shattered! 📖

Distant lands, listen.

Prepare for battle and be shattered!

Prepare for battle and be shattered!

## Isaiah 8:10

Make a plan, but it will be defeated. 📖

State a proposal, but it will not happen.

God is with us.”

## Isaiah 8:11

The LORD spoke to me with a strong hand. 📖

He warned me not to follow this people’s way:

## Isaiah 8:12

“Do not call everything conspiracy that these people call conspiracy. 📖

Do not fear what they fear or live in dread.

## Isaiah 8:13

Regard the LORD of Hosts as holy. 📖

Fear and dread Him alone.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** The LORD who commands heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 8:14

He will be a sanctuary. 📖

But to both Israel’s houses, He will be a stumbling stone and offending rock.

He will be a trap and snare to Jerusalem’s people.

**Help with sanctuary and snare:** A holy place of safety, and a trap.

## Isaiah 8:15

Many will stumble, fall, and break over these. 📖

They will be trapped and captured.”

## Isaiah 8:16

Bind the testimony and seal the law among my disciples. 📖

**Help with testimony and disciples:** The message declared, and those learning from the speaker.

## Isaiah 8:17

I will wait for the LORD, who hides His face from Jacob’s house. 📖

I will trust Him.

## Isaiah 8:18

Here I am with the children the LORD gave me as signs and symbols in Israel. 📖

They come from the LORD of Hosts, who lives on Mount Zion.

## Isaiah 8:19

People may tell you to consult mediums and spiritists who whisper and mutter. 📖

Should people not consult their God?

Why ask the dead on behalf of the living?

**Help with mediums and spiritists:** People claiming to communicate with the dead.

## Isaiah 8:20

To the law and testimony! 📖

If they do not speak according to this word, they have no dawn’s light.

## Isaiah 8:21

They will wander through the land, miserable and hungry. 📖

When starving, they will rage.

They will look up and curse their king and God.

## Isaiah 8:22

Then they will look at earth and see only distress, darkness, and anguish’s gloom. 📖

They will be driven into complete darkness.
