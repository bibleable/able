# Isaiah 28 — Pride, false safety, and wise farming

**About this chapter**

The chapter warns Ephraim and Jerusalem’s rulers.

It includes repeated words about teaching, a foundation stone, and pictures from farming.

## Isaiah 28:1

How terrible for the grand crown of Ephraim’s drunkards! 📖

Their glorious splendor is a fading flower on the hilltop above the fertile valley.

It is the pride of those overcome by wine.

**Help with the crown**

The crown and flower picture Ephraim’s proud splendor.

## Isaiah 28:2

Look! 📖

The Lord has someone strong and mighty.

He is like a hailstorm, a destructive storm, driving rain, or a flood.

He will smash that crown to the ground.

## Isaiah 28:3

The grand crown of Ephraim’s drunkards will be trampled underfoot. 📖

## Isaiah 28:4

The beautiful flower of his splendor, above the fertile valley, is fading. 📖

It will be like a ripe fig before the summer harvest.

Whoever sees it will take it in his hand and swallow it.

## Isaiah 28:5

On that day, the LORD of Hosts will be a crown of glory for His remaining people. 📖

He will be their splendid royal headband.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 28:6

He will be a spirit of justice for the one who sits as judge. 📖

He will give strength to those who drive back the attack at the gate.

## Isaiah 28:7

These people also stagger from wine and stumble from strong drink. 📖

Priests and prophets reel from strong drink and are confused by wine.

They stumble because of strong drink.

Their visions are confused, and their judgments go wrong.

## Isaiah 28:8

All their tables are covered with vomit. 📖

There is no place without filth.

## Isaiah 28:9

Whom is He trying to teach? 📖

To whom is He explaining His message?

To babies just taken off milk?

To babies removed from the breast?

## Isaiah 28:10

They hear, “Order on order, order on order. 📖

Line on line, line on line.

A little here, a little there.”

**About the repeated words**

The repeated pattern is important to the passage.

It appears again in verse 13.

## Isaiah 28:11

Yes, He will speak to these people with mocking lips and foreign languages. 📖

## Isaiah 28:12

He had told them, “This is the resting place. 📖

Let tired people rest.

This is the place of quiet rest.”

But they would not listen.

## Isaiah 28:13

Then the LORD’s word to them will become, “Order on order, order on order. 📖

Line on line, line on line.

A little here, a little there.”

So they will stumble backward and be injured, trapped, and captured.

## Isaiah 28:14

So hear the LORD’s word, you mocking rulers of these people in Jerusalem. 📖

## Isaiah 28:15

You said, “We have made a covenant with death and an agreement with Sheol. 📖

When the overwhelming whip passes through, it will not touch us.

We have made lies our shelter and falsehood our hiding place.”

**Help with the images**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

Sheol names the place of the dead.

The rulers boast that they are safe from death and destruction.

## Isaiah 28:16

The Lord GOD says, “Look! 📖

I am laying a stone in Zion.

It is a tested stone, a precious cornerstone, a firm foundation.

The one who believes will never be shaken.

**Help with a cornerstone**

A cornerstone is an important stone in a building’s foundation.

Here the stone pictures a sure basis for trust.

## Isaiah 28:17

“I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the level. 📖

Hail will sweep away your shelter of lies.

Water will flood your hiding place.

**Help with the tools**

A measuring line and a level help builders check their work.

Here they picture God’s standard of what is right.

## Isaiah 28:18

“Your covenant with death will be canceled. 📖

Your agreement with Sheol will not stand.

When the overwhelming whip passes through, it will trample you.

## Isaiah 28:19

“Whenever it passes through, it will carry you away. 📖

It will sweep through morning after morning, by day and by night.”

Understanding this message will bring complete terror.

## Isaiah 28:20

The bed is too short to stretch out on. 📖

The blanket is too small to wrap around you.

**Help with the bed and blanket**

These images describe protection that is not enough.

## Isaiah 28:21

The LORD will rise up as at Mount Perazim. 📖

He will stir Himself as in the Valley of Gibeon.

He will do His work, His strange work.

He will carry out His task, His disturbing task.

## Isaiah 28:22

So do not mock now, or your chains will become heavier. 📖

I have heard a command of destruction against the whole land from the Lord GOD of Hosts.

## Isaiah 28:23

Listen and hear my voice. 📖

Pay attention and hear what I say.

## Isaiah 28:24

Does a farmer plow for planting every day? 📖

Does he keep loosening and breaking up the soil?

## Isaiah 28:25

After leveling the ground, does he not sow caraway and scatter cumin? 📖

He plants wheat in rows, barley in plots, and rye along its border.

**Help with caraway and cumin**

These plants produce seeds used to flavor food.

## Isaiah 28:26

His God guides him and teaches him properly. 📖

## Isaiah 28:27

Caraway is not threshed with a heavy sledge. 📖

A cartwheel is not rolled over cumin.

Caraway is beaten with a stick, and cumin with a rod.

**Help with threshing**

Threshing separates the seeds from the rest of the plant.

Different plants need different methods.

## Isaiah 28:28

Grain for bread must be ground, but it is not threshed without end. 📖

The cartwheels roll over it, but the horses do not crush it.

## Isaiah 28:29

This also comes from the LORD of Hosts. 📖

His guidance is wonderful, and His wisdom is excellent.
