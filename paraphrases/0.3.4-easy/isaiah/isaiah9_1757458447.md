# Isaiah 9 — A child and a lasting kingdom

**About this chapter**

Isaiah announces light and a ruler whose peace will last.

He also warns Israel about continued judgment.

## Isaiah 9:1

There will be no more gloom for those who are suffering. 📖

In the past, God brought the lands of Zebulun and Naphtali low.

In the future, He will honor the Way of the Sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations.

## Isaiah 9:2

The people walking in darkness have seen a great light. 📖

Light has dawned on those living in the land of the shadow of death.

**Help with darkness and light**

These images describe distress and the hope that comes to people in distress.

## Isaiah 9:3

You have made the nation larger and increased its joy. 📖

The people rejoice before You as people rejoice at harvest time.

They rejoice like men sharing goods taken in battle.

**About the speaker**

Isaiah is speaking to God.

## Isaiah 9:4

You have shattered the yoke that burdened them, as You did in the day of Midian. 📖

You have broken the bar across their shoulders and their oppressor’s rod.

**Help with a yoke**

A yoke is a wooden bar laid on animals to pull a load.

Here it pictures a heavy burden under a cruel ruler.

## Isaiah 9:5

Every boot that marches in battle will be burned as fuel for the fire. 📖

Every piece of clothing rolled in blood will also be burned.

## Isaiah 9:6

A child is born to us. 📖

A son is given to us.

The government will rest on His shoulders.

He will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.

**Help with shoulders**

This image means He will carry the responsibility of ruling.

## Isaiah 9:7

His government and peace will keep increasing without end. 📖

He will rule on David’s throne and over David’s kingdom.

He will establish it and keep it strong with justice and what is right, from that time forever.

The LORD of Hosts will do this with His great determination.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title speaks of the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 9:8

The Lord has sent a message against Jacob. 📖

It has come upon Israel.

## Isaiah 9:9

All the people will know this, including Ephraim and those living in Samaria. 📖

With proud and arrogant hearts, they will say these words.

## Isaiah 9:10

“The bricks have fallen, but we will rebuild with carefully cut stone. 📖

The sycamore trees have been cut down, but we will replace them with cedars.”

## Isaiah 9:11

The LORD has raised Rezin’s foes against him. 📖

He has brought his enemies together.

## Isaiah 9:12

Aram from the east and Philistia from the west have swallowed Israel with open mouths. 📖

Even after all this, God’s anger has not turned away.

His hand is still raised.

**Help with these images**

Swallowing pictures enemies destroying Israel.

God’s raised hand pictures judgment that continues.

## Isaiah 9:13

But the people did not return to the One who struck them. 📖

They did not seek the LORD of Hosts.

## Isaiah 9:14

So the LORD will cut off Israel’s head and tail in a single day. 📖

He will cut off both palm branch and reed.

**Help with these images**

The next verse identifies the head and tail.

Palm branches and reeds picture different parts of the people.

## Isaiah 9:15

The elder and honored man is the head. 📖

The prophet who teaches lies is the tail.

## Isaiah 9:16

Those who guide these people lead them the wrong way. 📖

Those they mislead are swallowed up.

## Isaiah 9:17

So the Lord takes no pleasure in their young men. 📖

He has no compassion on their children without fathers or their widows.

Every one of them is godless and wicked.

Every mouth speaks foolishness.

Even after all this, His anger has not turned away.

His hand is still raised.

**About this judgment**

The passage describes the wrongdoing of these people.

It does not say that losing a parent or a husband is itself wrongdoing.

## Isaiah 9:18

Wickedness burns like a fire that eats up thorns and briers. 📖

It sets thick parts of the forest on fire.

Clouds of smoke roll upward.

## Isaiah 9:19

The anger of the LORD of Hosts burns the land. 📖

The people become fuel for the fire.

No man even spares his brother.

## Isaiah 9:20

They cut off what is on the right, but they are still hungry. 📖

They eat what is on the left, but they are still not satisfied.

Each one eats the flesh of his own children.

**About this difficult passage**

The text describes extreme destruction and violence, including against people’s own children.

The disturbing image is retained.

## Isaiah 9:21

Manasseh swallows Ephraim, and Ephraim swallows Manasseh. 📖

Together they turn against Judah.

Even after all this, God’s anger has not turned away.

His hand is still raised.
