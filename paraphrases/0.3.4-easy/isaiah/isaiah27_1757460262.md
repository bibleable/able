# Isaiah 27 — God guards His vineyard

**About this chapter**

God promises to guard a vineyard and gather His people.

The chapter also speaks about punishment, removal of sin, and a deserted city.

## Isaiah 27:1

On that day, the LORD will take His sharp, great, powerful sword. 📖

He will judge Leviathan, the fleeing snake, Leviathan, the coiling snake.

He will kill the dragon of the sea.

**Help with Leviathan**

Leviathan is named here as a great serpent.

The passage describes God defeating a terrifying enemy.

## Isaiah 27:2

On that day, “Sing about a fruitful vineyard. 📖

## Isaiah 27:3

“I, the LORD, keep it. 📖

I water it continually.

I guard it night and day so that no one can disturb it.

## Isaiah 27:4

“I am not angry. 📖

If thorns and briers came against Me, I would march against them and trample them.

I would burn them to the ground.

## Isaiah 27:5

“Or let them claim My protection. 📖

Let them make peace with Me.

Yes, let them make peace with Me.”

## Isaiah 27:6

In the days to come, Jacob will take root. 📖

Israel will bud and blossom.

It will fill the whole world with fruit.

**Help with the plant images**

The vineyard, roots, flowers, and fruit picture the people growing and flourishing.

## Isaiah 27:7

Has the LORD struck Israel as He struck those who oppressed her? 📖

Was she killed like those who killed her?

## Isaiah 27:8

You struggled against her through war and exile. 📖

You removed her with a fierce wind, as on a day when the east wind blows.

**Help with exile**

Exile means being forced away from one’s own land.

## Isaiah 27:9

So Jacob’s guilt will be atoned for. 📖

This will be the full result of his sin being removed.

He will make all the altar stones like crushed pieces of chalk.

No Asherah poles or incense altars will remain standing.

**Help with these words**

Atonement means dealing with sin so that guilt is removed.

Asherah poles were objects used to worship the goddess Asherah.

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Isaiah 27:10

The strong city lies empty. 📖

Its homes are abandoned.

It is a deserted wilderness.

Calves graze and lie down there.

They strip its branches bare.

## Isaiah 27:11

When its branches dry, they are broken off. 📖

Women come and use them to start fires.

These people have no understanding.

So their Maker has no compassion on them.

Their Creator shows them no favor.

## Isaiah 27:12

On that day, the LORD will thresh from the flowing Euphrates to the Wadi of Egypt. 📖

Israelites, you will be gathered one by one.

**Help with threshing and wadi**

Threshing separates grain from its stalks.

Here it pictures God gathering His people.

A wadi is a valley or streambed that may be dry at some times.

## Isaiah 27:13

On that day, a great ram’s horn will sound. 📖

Those who were dying in Assyria and those exiled in Egypt will come.

They will worship the LORD on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.
