# Isaiah 58 — The fast God chooses

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges fasting that goes together with injustice.

He calls for freedom, care for people in need, and honor for the Sabbath.

## Isaiah 58:1

“Cry aloud without holding back! 📖

Raise your voice like a ram’s horn.

Tell My people their offenses and Jacob’s house their sins.”

**About the speaker**

The LORD commands that His people’s wrongdoing be announced.

## Isaiah 58:2

“They seek Me day after day and delight to know My ways. 📖

They act like a nation doing right that has not abandoned its God’s justice.

They ask Me for righteous decisions and delight to be near God.”

## Isaiah 58:3

The people ask, “Why have we fasted, but You have not seen? 📖

Why have we humbled ourselves, but You have not noticed?”

God answers, “Look!

On your fasting day, you do as you please and oppress all your workers.

**Help with fasting**

Fasting means going without food for a time, often as an act of worship.

## Isaiah 58:4

“You fast while quarreling and fighting, striking cruelly with your fist. 📖

You cannot fast as you do today and expect your voice to be heard in heaven.

## Isaiah 58:5

“Is this the fast I chose, a day to deny oneself? 📖

Is it bowing your head like a reed and spreading sackcloth and ashes?

Would you call this a fast and a day the LORD accepts?

**Help with these images**

A reed is a plant with a long stem that can bend.

Rough sackcloth and ashes were used to show grief or humility.

## Isaiah 58:6

“Is this not the fast I chose? 📖

Break the chains of wickedness and untie the yoke’s cords.

Free those who are oppressed and tear off every yoke.

**Help with yoke**

A yoke is a wooden bar used to control working animals.

Here it pictures oppressive control over people.

## Isaiah 58:7

“Is it not sharing your bread with hungry people and bringing poor, homeless people into your home? 📖

Is it not clothing a naked person when you see him?

Is it not refusing to turn away from your own flesh and blood?

**Help with flesh and blood**

This means your own family or kin.

## Isaiah 58:8

“Then your light will break out like dawn, and your healing will come quickly. 📖

Your righteousness will go before you.

The LORD’s glory will guard you from behind.

## Isaiah 58:9

“Then you will call, and the LORD will answer. 📖

You will cry out, and He will say, ‘Here I am.’

If you remove the yoke from among you, the accusing finger, and harmful talk,

## Isaiah 58:10

“and give yourself to hungry people and satisfy those suffering in their souls, then your light will shine in darkness. 📖

Your night will become like midday.

**About the condition**

This continues verse 9’s condition.

The promise follows removing oppression and giving oneself to people in need.

## Isaiah 58:11

“The LORD will always guide you, satisfy you in sun-scorched land, and strengthen your body. 📖

You will be like a well-watered garden, like a spring whose water never fails.

## Isaiah 58:12

“Your people will rebuild ancient ruins and restore foundations laid long ago. 📖

You will be called Repairer of the Breach, Restorer of the Streets of Dwelling.

**Help with the names**

A breach is a gap in a wall.

These names describe rebuilding places where people can live.

## Isaiah 58:13

“Stop your foot from breaking the Sabbath by doing as you please on My holy day. 📖

Call the Sabbath a delight and honor the LORD’s holy day.

Honor it without going your own way, seeking your own pleasure, or speaking idle words.

## Isaiah 58:14

“If you do this, you will delight in the LORD. 📖

I will make you ride on the land’s heights and feed you with your father Jacob’s inheritance.”

The LORD’s own mouth has spoken.
