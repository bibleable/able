# Isaiah 48 — Listen and leave Babylon

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges Jacob’s stubbornness and announces His purpose.

He calls His people out of Babylon and recalls His care in the desert.

## Isaiah 48:1

“Listen, house of Jacob, you who are called Israel and come from Judah’s line. 📖

You swear by the LORD’s name and call on Israel’s God.

But you do not do this in truth or righteousness.

## Isaiah 48:2

“You call yourselves by the holy city’s name and rely on Israel’s God. 📖

His name is the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 48:3

“Long ago, I foretold the earlier things. 📖

They came from My mouth, and I announced them.

Suddenly I acted, and they happened.

## Isaiah 48:4

“I knew you were stubborn. 📖

Your neck is iron, and your forehead is bronze.

**Help with iron and bronze**

These hard metals picture refusal to change or listen.

## Isaiah 48:5

“So I told you long ago, before it happened. 📖

Then you could not say, ‘My idol did this.

My carved image and cast god ordered it.’

## Isaiah 48:6

“You have heard these things. 📖

Look at them all.

Will you not acknowledge them?

From now on, I will tell you new things, hidden things you do not know.

## Isaiah 48:7

“They are created now, not long ago. 📖

You had not heard them before today.

So you cannot say, ‘I already knew them!’

## Isaiah 48:8

“You never heard or understood. 📖

For a long time, your ears have not been open.

I knew how deceitful you were.

You have been called a rebel from birth.

## Isaiah 48:9

“For My name’s sake, I will delay My anger. 📖

For My praise’s sake, I will hold it back so that you are not cut off.

## Isaiah 48:10

“See! 📖

I refined you, but not as silver.

I tested you in the furnace of suffering.

**Help with refining**

Refining uses heat to remove unwanted material from metal.

Here the furnace pictures testing through suffering.

## Isaiah 48:11

“For My own sake, My very own sake, I will act. 📖

How could I let My name be dishonored?

I will not give My glory to another.

## Isaiah 48:12

“Listen to Me, Jacob and Israel whom I called. 📖

I am He.

I am the first and the last.

## Isaiah 48:13

“My own hand founded the earth. 📖

My right hand spread out the heavens.

When I call them, they stand up together.

## Isaiah 48:14

“All of you, gather and listen. 📖

Which idol foretold these things?

The LORD’s chosen ally will carry out His desire against Babylon.

His arm will act against the Chaldeans.

## Isaiah 48:15

“I, yes I, have spoken. 📖

I have called him and brought him.

He will succeed in his task.

## Isaiah 48:16

“Come near Me and listen. 📖

From the beginning, I have not spoken in secret.

From the time it happened, I was there.”

Now the Lord GOD has sent me, accompanied by His Spirit.

**About the voices**

The closing sentence reports being sent by the Lord GOD with His Spirit.

It is kept distinct from the preceding direct speech.

## Isaiah 48:17

The LORD your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, says, “I am the LORD your God. 📖

I teach you for your good.

I guide you in the way you should go.

## Isaiah 48:18

“If only you had listened to My commands! 📖

Your peace would have been like a river, and your righteousness like sea waves.

## Isaiah 48:19

“Your descendants would have been countless as sand, your children as many as its grains. 📖

Their name would never have been cut off or removed from My presence.”

## Isaiah 48:20

Leave Babylon! 📖

Flee from the Chaldeans!

Announce it with a shout of joy.

Proclaim it to the ends of the earth.

Say, “The LORD has redeemed His servant Jacob!”

**Help with redeemed**

Redeemed means rescued or set free.

## Isaiah 48:21

They were not thirsty when He led them through deserts. 📖

He made water flow from the rock for them.

He split the rock, and water gushed out.

## Isaiah 48:22

The LORD says, “There is no peace for the wicked.” 📖
