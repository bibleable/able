# Isaiah 45 — The LORD calls Cyrus and the nations

**About this chapter**

The LORD calls Cyrus for the sake of His people.

He declares His authority as Creator and calls the ends of the earth to be saved.

## Isaiah 45:1

The LORD speaks to Cyrus, His anointed, whose right hand He has taken. 📖

He will bring nations under Cyrus’s power and disarm kings.

He will open doors before him so that gates will not be shut.

**Help with anointed**

Anointed means set apart for a role.

Here the title is given to Cyrus.

## Isaiah 45:2

“I will go before you and level the mountains. 📖

I will break down bronze gates and cut through iron bars.

## Isaiah 45:3

“I will give you treasures from darkness and riches hidden in secret places. 📖

Then you may know that I am the LORD, Israel’s God, who calls you by name.

## Isaiah 45:4

“For Jacob My servant and Israel My chosen one, I call you by name. 📖

I have given you a title of honor, though you have not known Me.

## Isaiah 45:5

“I am the LORD, and there is no other. 📖

There is no God except Me.

I will equip you for battle, though you have not known Me,

## Isaiah 45:6

“so that all may know, from sunrise to sunset, that there is none except Me. 📖

I am the LORD, and there is no other.

## Isaiah 45:7

“I form light and create darkness. 📖

I bring prosperity and create disaster.

I, the LORD, do all these things.

## Isaiah 45:8

“Heavens, drip down from above. 📖

Let the skies pour down righteousness.

Let the earth open so that salvation sprouts and righteousness grows with it.

I, the LORD, have created it.

**Help with righteousness and salvation**

Righteousness means what is right before God.

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

The rain and growth picture these things coming from God.

## Isaiah 45:9

“How terrible for someone who argues with his Maker, one clay pot among many! 📖

Does clay ask the potter, ‘What are you making?’

Does your work say, ‘He has no hands’?

## Isaiah 45:10

“How terrible for someone who asks his father, ‘What have you fathered?’ 📖

Or who asks his mother, ‘What have you given birth to?’”

## Isaiah 45:11

The LORD, Israel’s Holy One and Maker, says, “About things to come, do you question Me about My sons? 📖

Do you instruct Me in the work of My hands?

## Isaiah 45:12

“I made the earth and created human beings on it. 📖

My hands stretched out the heavens, and I ordered all their army.

## Isaiah 45:13

“I will raise up Cyrus in righteousness and make all his ways straight. 📖

He will rebuild My city and free My exiles, without payment or reward,” says the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 45:14

The LORD says, “Egypt’s products and Cush’s goods will come to you and be yours. 📖

So will the Sabeans, men of great height.

They will walk heavily behind you, come in chains, and bow to you.

They will confess, ‘God is truly with you, and there is no other.

There is no other God.’”

**About this difficult passage**

The promise includes other people coming in chains and submitting.

That detail has been retained.

## Isaiah 45:15

You truly are a God who hides Himself, God of Israel, the Savior. 📖

## Isaiah 45:16

They will all be ashamed and humiliated. 📖

Idol makers will leave together in disgrace.

## Isaiah 45:17

But the LORD will save Israel with everlasting salvation. 📖

You will not be ashamed or humiliated, through ages without end.

## Isaiah 45:18

The LORD created the heavens. 📖

He is God.

He formed, made, and established the earth.

He did not create it to be empty, but formed it for people to live in.

He says, “I am the LORD, and there is no other.

## Isaiah 45:19

“I have not spoken secretly from a place in a dark land. 📖

I did not tell Jacob’s descendants, ‘Seek Me in a wasteland.’

I, the LORD, speak truth and say what is right.

## Isaiah 45:20

“You who escaped from the nations, come together and draw near. 📖

Those who carry wooden idols and pray to a god that cannot save have no understanding.

## Isaiah 45:21

“Speak and present your case. 📖

Yes, let them discuss it together.

Who foretold this long ago and announced it in ancient times?

Was it not I, the LORD?

There is no God except Me, a righteous God and Savior.

There is none except Me.

## Isaiah 45:22

“All the ends of the earth, turn to Me and be saved. 📖

I am God, and there is no other.

## Isaiah 45:23

“I have sworn by Myself. 📖

Truth has gone from My mouth, a word that will not be taken back.

Every knee will bow before Me.

Every tongue will swear loyalty.

## Isaiah 45:24

“They will surely say about Me, ‘Only in the LORD are righteousness and strength.’” 📖

All who rage against Him will come to Him and be ashamed.

## Isaiah 45:25

In the LORD, all Israel’s descendants will be declared right and rejoice greatly. 📖
