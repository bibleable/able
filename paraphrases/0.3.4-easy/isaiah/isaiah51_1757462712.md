# Isaiah 51 — Listen, wake, and take courage

**About this chapter**

The LORD calls His people to remember their beginnings and trust His lasting salvation.

Jerusalem’s cup of suffering is taken away.

## Isaiah 51:1

“Listen to Me, you who pursue righteousness and seek the LORD. 📖

Look at the rock you were cut from and the quarry you were taken from.

**Help with rock and quarry**

A quarry is a place where stone is cut from the ground.

The image introduces the people’s beginnings in Abraham and Sarah.

## Isaiah 51:2

“Look to Abraham your father and Sarah who gave you birth. 📖

Abraham was only one man when I called him.

Then I blessed him and made him many.

## Isaiah 51:3

“The LORD will comfort Zion and show compassion on all its ruins. 📖

He will make its wilderness like Eden and its desert like the LORD’s garden.

Joy, gladness, thanksgiving, and beautiful song will be found there.

## Isaiah 51:4

“My people, pay attention to Me. 📖

My nation, listen to Me.

A law will go out from Me.

My justice will become a light to the nations.

I will make it happen quickly.

## Isaiah 51:5

“My righteousness is near, and My salvation is coming. 📖

My arms will bring justice to the nations.

The islands will look for Me and wait in hope for My arm.

**Help with these words**

Righteousness means what is right before God.

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

God’s arm pictures His power at work.

## Isaiah 51:6

“Look up at the heavens and down at the earth. 📖

The heavens will disappear like smoke.

The earth will wear out like clothing, and its people will die like gnats.

But My salvation will last forever, and My righteousness will never fail.

## Isaiah 51:7

“Listen to Me, you who know what is right, people with My law in your hearts. 📖

Do not fear people’s scorn or be broken by their insults.

## Isaiah 51:8

“Moths will eat them like clothing, and worms will eat them like wool. 📖

But My righteousness will last forever, and My salvation through all generations.”

## Isaiah 51:9

Wake up, wake up, arm of the LORD! 📖

Put on strength.

Wake up as in past days and ancient generations.

Was it not You who cut Rahab to pieces and pierced the dragon?

**Help with arm and Rahab**

The arm pictures the LORD’s power.

Rahab and the dragon are images of a defeated enemy here.

## Isaiah 51:10

Was it not You who dried the sea, the waters of the great deep? 📖

Did You not make a road in the sea’s depths for the redeemed to cross?

**Help with redeemed**

The redeemed are people rescued or set free.

## Isaiah 51:11

The LORD’s redeemed will return and enter Zion with singing. 📖

Everlasting joy will be their crown.

Gladness and joy will overtake them.

Sorrow and sighing will flee.

## Isaiah 51:12

“I, yes I, am the One who comforts you. 📖

Why fear a human being who must die, a person who withers like grass?

## Isaiah 51:13

“You have forgotten the LORD your Maker, who stretched out the heavens and laid earth’s foundations. 📖

You live in terror all day because of the fury of the oppressor determined to destroy.

But where is that oppressor’s fury?

## Isaiah 51:14

“The captive will soon be freed. 📖

He will not die in the dungeon or lack bread.

## Isaiah 51:15

“I am the LORD your God, who stirs the sea so that its waves roar. 📖

His name is the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 51:16

“I have put My words in your mouth and covered you with the shadow of My hand. 📖

This is to establish the heavens, found the earth, and tell Zion, ‘You are My people.’”

## Isaiah 51:17

Wake up, wake up! 📖

Rise, Jerusalem!

You have drunk the cup of the LORD’s fury from His hand.

You drained the cup to its last drops, the cup that makes people stagger.

**Help with the cup**

The cup pictures experiencing God’s judgment.

## Isaiah 51:18

Among all the sons she bore, none guides her. 📖

Among all the sons she raised, none takes her hand.

## Isaiah 51:19

These pairs have come upon you, devastation and destruction, famine and sword. 📖

Who will grieve for you?

Who can comfort you?

## Isaiah 51:20

Your sons have fainted. 📖

They lie at the head of every street like an antelope in a net.

They are full of the LORD’s anger and your God’s rebuke.

## Isaiah 51:21

So hear this now, you suffering one, drunk but not from wine. 📖

## Isaiah 51:22

Your Lord, the LORD your God, who defends His people, says, “Look! 📖

I have taken the cup of staggering from your hand.

You will never again drink from that cup of My fury.

## Isaiah 51:23

“I will put it in the hands of those who tormented you. 📖

They told you, ‘Lie down so that we can walk over you.’

You made your back like the ground, like a street to walk on.”
