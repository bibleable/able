# Isaiah 16 — Moab seeks shelter

**About this chapter**

Moab’s displaced people seek protection.

The message also describes Moab’s pride, ruined harvests, grief, and coming judgment.

## Isaiah 16:1

Send lambs as tribute to the ruler of the land. 📖

Send them from Sela in the desert to the mountain of Daughter Zion.

**Help with tribute**

Tribute is a payment to a ruler.

Daughter Zion refers to Jerusalem and its people.

## Isaiah 16:2

At the shallow crossings of the Arnon, Moab’s daughters are like fluttering birds pushed out of their nest. 📖

## Isaiah 16:3

They say, “Give us guidance. 📖

Make a decision.

At midday, shelter us with shade as dark as night.

Hide the refugees.

Do not betray the person who flees.

## Isaiah 16:4

“Let my fleeing people stay with you. 📖

Protect Moab from the destroyer.”

The oppressor will go away.

Destruction will stop.

Those who oppress will disappear from the land.

## Isaiah 16:5

Then a throne will be established in David’s tent through devoted love. 📖

A judge will sit on it faithfully.

He will seek justice and be quick to do what is right.

**Help with David’s tent**

The image refers to David’s royal house.

## Isaiah 16:6

We have heard about Moab’s great pride and conceit. 📖

His arrogance overflows.

But his boasting is empty.

## Isaiah 16:7

So let Moab cry out in grief. 📖

Let everyone cry out together for Moab.

You who are completely crushed, mourn for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth.

## Isaiah 16:8

The fields of Heshbon and the grapevines of Sibmah have withered. 📖

The nations’ rulers have trampled the finest vines.

Those vines had reached Jazer and spread toward the desert.

Their shoots had spread out and crossed the sea.

## Isaiah 16:9

So I weep with Jazer for Sibmah’s vines. 📖

I soak Heshbon and Elealeh with my tears.

The shouts of victory over your summer fruit and harvest have fallen silent.

## Isaiah 16:10

Joy and gladness are taken from the orchard. 📖

No one sings or shouts in the vineyards.

No one treads grapes in the winepresses.

God says, “I have ended the cheering.”

**Help with a winepress**

A winepress is a place where people crush grapes to make wine.

## Isaiah 16:11

So my heart mourns for Moab like a harp. 📖

Deep inside, I mourn for Kir-heres.

**Help with a harp**

The image compares inward grief to the sound of a stringed instrument.

## Isaiah 16:12

Moab will appear at the high place and wear himself out there. 📖

He will enter his holy place to pray.

But it will do him no good.

## Isaiah 16:13

This is the message the LORD spoke earlier about Moab. 📖

## Isaiah 16:14

Now the LORD says, “In three years, counted as a hired worker counts them, Moab’s splendor will be despised. 📖

This will happen despite all its many people.

Those left will be few and weak.”

**Help with counting years**

The hired worker image stresses a definite period of time.
