# Isaiah 3 — Failed leadership and the judgment of Zion

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes judgment on Jerusalem and Judah.

The LORD addresses unjust leaders and the proud daughters of Zion.

The harsh descriptions are retained in their prophetic setting.

## Isaiah 3:1

Look, the Lord GOD of Hosts will remove support and supplies from Jerusalem and Judah. 📖

He will remove all their food and water,

## Isaiah 3:2

mighty men, warriors, judges, prophets, fortune-tellers, and elders, 📖

## Isaiah 3:3

commanders of fifty, dignitaries, advisers, skilled magicians, and clever enchanters. 📖

**Help with dignitaries and enchanters:** High-ranking people, and people using spells.

## Isaiah 3:4

He says, “I will make mere boys their leaders. 📖

Children will rule them.”

## Isaiah 3:5

People will oppress each other, man against man and neighbor against neighbor. 📖

Young people will rise against old people.

Dishonorable people will rise against honorable people.

## Isaiah 3:6

A man will seize his brother in their father’s house. 📖

He will say, “You have a cloak.

Be our leader!

Take charge of these ruins.”

## Isaiah 3:7

That day the brother will cry, “I am not a healer. 📖

My house has no food or clothing.

Do not make me the people’s leader!”

## Isaiah 3:8

Jerusalem stumbled, and Judah fell. 📖

Their words and deeds opposed the LORD, defying His glorious presence.

## Isaiah 3:9

Their faces testify against them. 📖

Like Sodom, they display their sin instead of hiding it.

Disaster is theirs!

They brought it on themselves.

## Isaiah 3:10

Tell righteous people things will go well. 📖

They will enjoy their work’s fruit.

**Help with righteous:** Right before God.

## Isaiah 3:11

Disaster is on wicked people! 📖

They will be repaid for what their hands did.

## Isaiah 3:12

Youths oppress My people, and women rule them. 📖

My people, your guides mislead you and turn you from your paths.

**About the speaker:** “My people” identifies the LORD as speaking here.

The verse describes the leaders and oppression in this setting.

## Isaiah 3:13

The LORD rises to argue His case. 📖

He stands to judge the people.

## Isaiah 3:14

The LORD charges His people’s elders and leaders: “You devoured the vineyard. 📖

Your houses hold goods stolen from poor people.

## Isaiah 3:15

Why crush My people and grind poor people’s faces?” 📖

The Lord GOD of Hosts declares this.

## Isaiah 3:16

The LORD also says, “Zion’s daughters are proud. 📖

They walk with raised heads and alluring eyes, prancing and skipping, jingling ankle bracelets.

## Isaiah 3:17

So the Lord will put sores on the heads of Zion’s daughters. 📖

The LORD will make their foreheads bare.”

## Isaiah 3:18

That day the Lord will remove their finery: anklets, headbands, and crescents, 📖

**Help with finery and crescents:** Decorative clothing and jewelry, and moon-shaped ornaments.

## Isaiah 3:19

pendants, bracelets, and veils, 📖

## Isaiah 3:20

headdresses, ankle chains, sashes, perfume bottles, and charms, 📖

## Isaiah 3:21

signet rings and nose rings, 📖

**Help with signet rings:** Rings bearing a mark used as a personal seal.

## Isaiah 3:22

festival robes, capes, cloaks, and purses, 📖

## Isaiah 3:23

mirrors, linen clothes, tiaras, and shawls. 📖

**Help with tiaras:** Decorative headpieces.

## Isaiah 3:24

Stench will replace fragrance. 📖

Rope will replace a belt.

Baldness will replace styled hair.

Sackcloth will replace fine clothes.

Shame will replace beauty.

**Help with sackcloth:** Rough cloth worn for mourning.

## Isaiah 3:25

Your men will die by the sword. 📖

Your warriors will fall in battle.

## Isaiah 3:26

Zion’s gates will grieve and mourn. 📖

Without anything, she will sit on the ground.
