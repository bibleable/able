# Isaiah 52 — Good news for Zion and the LORD’s Servant

**About this chapter**

Zion is called to rise from captivity.

The chapter ends by describing the Servant’s suffering and exaltation.

## Isaiah 52:1

Wake up, wake up, Zion! 📖

Clothe yourself with strength.

Jerusalem, holy city, put on splendid clothes!

Those who are uncircumcised and unclean will no longer enter you.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision removes the foreskin of the penis and was a sign of Israel’s covenant.

“Unclean” describes being unfit for what is holy here.

The terms belong to this promise about the holy city.

## Isaiah 52:2

Shake off your dust! 📖

Rise and sit on your throne, Jerusalem.

Captive Daughter of Zion, remove the chains from your neck.

**Help with Daughter of Zion**

The city and its people are pictured as a woman freed from captivity.

## Isaiah 52:3

The LORD says, “You were sold for nothing. 📖

You will be redeemed without money.”

**Help with redeemed**

Redeemed means rescued or set free.

## Isaiah 52:4

The Lord GOD says, “My people first went down to Egypt to live. 📖

Then Assyria oppressed them without cause.

## Isaiah 52:5

“Now what do I have here?” declares the LORD. 📖

“My people have been taken without cause.

Their rulers mock,” declares the LORD, “and My name is insulted all day long.

## Isaiah 52:6

“So My people will know My name. 📖

On that day, they will know that I am the One speaking.

Here I am!”

## Isaiah 52:7

How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of those bringing good news! 📖

They announce peace, bring good tidings, and announce salvation.

They tell Zion, “Your God rules!”

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 52:8

Listen! 📖

Your watchmen raise their voices and shout together for joy.

Every eye will see when the LORD returns to Zion.

## Isaiah 52:9

Ruins of Jerusalem, burst into joy and sing together! 📖

The LORD has comforted His people and redeemed Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 52:10

The LORD has uncovered His holy arm in the sight of all nations. 📖

All the ends of the earth will see our God’s salvation.

**Help with His arm**

The image describes God showing His power.

## Isaiah 52:11

Leave, leave, go out from there! 📖

Touch nothing unclean.

Come out and purify yourselves, you who carry the LORD’s vessels.

**Help with vessels and purification**

Vessels are containers or objects used in worship.

Purification means becoming fit for holy service.

It does not mean ordinary illness or disability is a moral fault.

## Isaiah 52:12

You will not leave in a hurry or run away in haste. 📖

The LORD goes before you.

The God of Israel guards you from behind.

## Isaiah 52:13

“Look! 📖

My Servant will prosper.

He will be raised, lifted up, and placed very high.

**About the speaker**

The LORD speaks about His Servant.

## Isaiah 52:14

“Many were horrified at Him. 📖

His appearance was disfigured beyond that of any man.

His body was damaged beyond human likeness.

## Isaiah 52:15

“So He will sprinkle many nations. 📖

Kings will close their mouths because of Him.

They will see what they were not told and understand what they had not heard.”

**Help with sprinkle**

Sprinkling appears in ceremonies of cleansing or setting apart.

The image and its application to nations remain for further editorial review.
