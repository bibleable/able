# Isaiah 5 — The vineyard and the warnings of judgment

**About this chapter**

Isaiah sings about his beloved’s vineyard.

The vineyard’s owner speaks, and verse 7 identifies the people pictured.

Warnings and a description of an approaching force follow.

## Isaiah 5:1

I will sing my beloved a song about his vineyard. 📖

My beloved had a vineyard on a very fertile hill.

## Isaiah 5:2

He dug it, cleared stones, and planted the finest vines. 📖

He built a watchtower in its middle and dug a winepress.

He expected good grapes, but it produced sour fruit!

## Isaiah 5:3

“People of Jerusalem and Judah, judge between Me and My vineyard. 📖

## Isaiah 5:4

What more could I have done for it? 📖

I expected sweet grapes.

Why did it produce sour fruit?

## Isaiah 5:5

I will tell you what I will do. 📖

I will remove its hedge, and it will be consumed.

I will break its wall, and it will be trampled.

## Isaiah 5:6

I will make it a wasteland, neither pruned nor cultivated. 📖

Thorns and briers will grow.

I will command clouds not to rain on it.”

**Help with pruned and cultivated:** Trimmed for healthy growth, and worked to grow crops.

## Isaiah 5:7

The LORD of Hosts’ vineyard is Israel’s house. 📖

Judah’s people are His delightful planting.

He looked for justice but saw bloodshed.

He looked for righteousness but heard distressed cries.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** The LORD who commands heavenly armies.

Righteousness means what is right and just.

## Isaiah 5:8

Disaster to people adding house to house and field to field until no space remains! 📖

You live alone in the land.

## Isaiah 5:9

I heard the LORD of Hosts declare, “Many houses will surely become empty ruins. 📖

Great mansions will have no residents.

## Isaiah 5:10

Ten acres of vineyard will yield only a bath of wine. 📖

A homer of seed will produce only an ephah of grain.”

**Help with bath, homer, and ephah:** A measure for wine, and measures for seed or grain.

## Isaiah 5:11

Disaster to people rising early to seek strong drink! 📖

They linger into evening, burning with wine.

## Isaiah 5:12

Their feasts have lyres, harps, tambourines, flutes, and wine. 📖

But they ignore the LORD’s acts and do not see His hands’ work.

## Isaiah 5:13

So My people will go into exile for lacking understanding. 📖

Their dignitaries starve, and their crowds are parched with thirst.

**Help with exile and dignitaries:** Forced departure from their homeland, and high-ranking people.

## Isaiah 5:14

Sheol widens its throat and opens enormous jaws. 📖

Zion’s nobles, crowds, revelers, and partygoers go down!

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead, pictured here as swallowing people.

## Isaiah 5:15

Humanity will be brought low. 📖

Each person will be humbled.

Proud people will lower their eyes.

## Isaiah 5:16

But the LORD of Hosts will be lifted high through justice. 📖

The holy God will show His holiness through righteousness.

## Isaiah 5:17

Lambs will graze as in their own pastures. 📖

Strangers will feed among wealthy people’s ruins.

## Isaiah 5:18

Disaster to people dragging sin with deceitful cords and pulling wrongdoing with cart ropes! 📖

## Isaiah 5:19

They say, “Let Him hurry His work so we can see! 📖

Let Israel’s Holy One’s plan come so we can know it!”

## Isaiah 5:20

Disaster to people calling evil good and good evil! 📖

They make darkness light and light darkness, bitter sweet and sweet bitter.

## Isaiah 5:21

Disaster to people wise in their own eyes and clever in their own sight! 📖

## Isaiah 5:22

Disaster to wine-drinking heroes and champions of mixing strong drink, 📖

## Isaiah 5:23

who clear guilty people for a bribe and deny innocent people justice! 📖

## Isaiah 5:24

Fire’s tongue consumes straw, and dry grass shrivels in flame. 📖

In the same way, their roots will decay and flowers blow away like dust.

They rejected the LORD of Hosts’ instruction and despised Israel’s Holy One’s word.

## Isaiah 5:25

So the LORD’s anger burns against His people. 📖

His hand rises to strike them down.

Mountains shake, and bodies lie like rubbish in streets.

Yet His anger has not turned away.

His hand is still raised.

## Isaiah 5:26

He raises a banner for distant nations and whistles to earth’s ends. 📖

Look how swiftly they come!

**Help with banner:** A raised flag or sign calling people together.

## Isaiah 5:27

None grows tired or stumbles. 📖

None dozes or sleeps.

No belt is loose, and no sandal strap is broken.

## Isaiah 5:28

Their arrows are sharp, and their bows are strung. 📖

Their horses’ hooves are like flint.

Their chariot wheels are like a whirlwind.

**Help with flint and whirlwind:** A hard stone, and a powerful, swirling wind.

## Isaiah 5:29

They roar like lions, like young lions. 📖

They growl and seize prey.

They carry it away, and no one can rescue it.

## Isaiah 5:30

That day they will roar over the land like the sea. 📖

Look at the land: darkness and distress are there.

Even light is hidden by clouds.
