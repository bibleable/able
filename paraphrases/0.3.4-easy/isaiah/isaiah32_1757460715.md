# Isaiah 32 — Just rule and coming peace

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes rulers who act rightly.

He warns overconfident women about coming loss and speaks of the Spirit bringing peace.

## Isaiah 32:1

Look! 📖

A king will rule with righteousness.

Princes will rule with justice.

## Isaiah 32:2

Each will be like shelter from the wind and safety from the storm. 📖

Each will be like streams in a dry land, like the shade of a great rock in dry country.

## Isaiah 32:3

Then the eyes of those who see will no longer be closed. 📖

The ears of those who hear will listen.

## Isaiah 32:4

The minds of people who act too quickly will know and understand. 📖

People who stammer will speak clearly and smoothly.

**About these promises**

The passage describes coming restoration.

It does not say that a speech difficulty is itself wrongdoing.

## Isaiah 32:5

A fool will no longer be called noble. 📖

A dishonest person will no longer be respected.

## Isaiah 32:6

A fool speaks foolishness and plans wrongdoing in his mind. 📖

He acts against God and speaks falsely about the LORD.

He leaves hungry people empty and keeps thirsty people from drinking.

## Isaiah 32:7

The dishonest person’s weapons destroy. 📖

He plans to destroy poor people with lies, even when needy people’s plea is just.

## Isaiah 32:8

But a noble man makes honorable plans. 📖

He stands up for worthy causes.

## Isaiah 32:9

Women who feel secure without concern, stand up and listen to me! 📖

Daughters who are too confident, hear my words.

## Isaiah 32:10

In a little more than a year, you who feel secure will tremble. 📖

The grape harvest will fail.

The fruit harvest will not come.

## Isaiah 32:11

You women living at ease, shudder! 📖

You daughters who feel secure without concern, tremble!

Strip yourselves bare and put sackcloth around your waists.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Isaiah 32:12

Beat your breasts in grief for the pleasant fields and fruitful vines, 📖

## Isaiah 32:13

and for my people’s land, covered with thorns and briers. 📖

Grieve even for every happy house in this celebrating city.

## Isaiah 32:14

The palace will be deserted, and the busy city abandoned. 📖

The hill and watchtower will become caves forever, a delight for wild donkeys and pasture for flocks,

## Isaiah 32:15

until the Spirit is poured out on us from above. 📖

Then the desert will become an orchard, and the orchard will seem like a forest.

## Isaiah 32:16

Then justice will live in the wilderness. 📖

Righteousness will live in the fertile field.

**Help with righteousness**

Righteousness means what is right before God.

## Isaiah 32:17

The work of righteousness will bring peace. 📖

Its service will bring quiet confidence forever.

## Isaiah 32:18

Then my people will live in a peaceful place. 📖

They will have safe, secure places to rest.

## Isaiah 32:19

But hail will level the forest. 📖

The city will sink to the depths.

## Isaiah 32:20

Blessed are those who sow beside plentiful waters and let oxen and donkeys wander freely. 📖
