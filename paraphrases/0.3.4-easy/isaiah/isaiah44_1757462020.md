# Isaiah 44 — The Maker and the things people make

**About this chapter**

The LORD promises His Spirit and declares that no other God exists.

The chapter contrasts God’s work with people making idols and names Cyrus.

## Isaiah 44:1

But now listen, Jacob My servant, Israel whom I chose. 📖

## Isaiah 44:2

The LORD is your Maker, who formed you from the womb and will help you. 📖

He says, “Do not fear, Jacob My servant, Jeshurun whom I chose.

**Help with Jeshurun**

Jeshurun is another name for Israel.

## Isaiah 44:3

“I will pour water on thirsty land and streams on dry ground. 📖

I will pour My Spirit on your descendants and My blessing on your children.

## Isaiah 44:4

“They will grow among the grass like willows beside flowing streams. 📖

## Isaiah 44:5

“One will say, ‘I belong to the LORD.’ 📖

Another will call himself by Jacob’s name.

Another will write ‘The LORD’s’ on his hand and take Israel’s name.”

## Isaiah 44:6

The LORD, Israel’s King and Redeemer, the LORD of Hosts, says, “I am the first and the last. 📖

There is no God except Me.

**Help with these titles**

A Redeemer rescues or sets people free.

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 44:7

“Who is like Me? 📖

Let him say so and present his case before Me, since I established an ancient people.

Let him tell the things to come and what will happen.

## Isaiah 44:8

“Do not tremble or fear. 📖

Did I not tell you and announce this long ago?

You are My witnesses!

Is there any God except Me?

There is no other Rock.

I know not one.”

**Help with Rock**

The Rock pictures God’s strength and reliability.

## Isaiah 44:9

All who make idols are nothing, and the things they treasure are worthless. 📖

Their witnesses fail to see or understand, so they are ashamed.

## Isaiah 44:10

Who shapes a god or casts an idol that gives him no benefit? 📖

**Help with an idol**

An idol is an object worshiped as a god.

Casting means making an object by pouring melted metal into a mold.

## Isaiah 44:11

Look! 📖

All his companions will be ashamed, because the craftsmen themselves are only human.

Let them all gather and take their stand.

They will all be terrified and ashamed.

## Isaiah 44:12

The blacksmith takes a tool and works over the coals. 📖

He shapes an idol with hammers and forms it with his strong arms.

Yet he grows hungry and loses strength.

He does not drink water and becomes faint.

## Isaiah 44:13

The woodworker stretches out a measuring line and marks the wood with a stylus. 📖

He shapes it with chisels and outlines it with a compass.

He makes it look like a human being in all human glory, to live in a shrine.

**Help with these words**

A stylus marks material, chisels cut it, and a compass draws circles.

A shrine is a place of worship.

## Isaiah 44:14

He cuts down cedars or gets a cypress or oak. 📖

He lets it grow strong among the forest’s trees.

He plants a laurel, and rain makes it grow.

## Isaiah 44:15

The wood becomes fuel for a man. 📖

He takes some to warm himself and lights a fire to bake bread.

He also shapes it into a god and worships it.

He makes an idol and bows to it.

## Isaiah 44:16

He burns half in the fire and roasts meat over that half. 📖

He eats the roast and is satisfied.

He also warms himself and says, “Ah!

I am warm.

I see the fire.”

## Isaiah 44:17

He makes a god from the rest, his carved image. 📖

He bows to it and worships.

He prays to it, saying, “Save me, because you are my god.”

## Isaiah 44:18

They do not understand or recognize what is happening. 📖

He has shut their eyes so that they cannot see.

He has closed their minds so that they cannot understand.

**About the image**

The passage attributes their failure to understand to God’s action.

That difficult claim is retained.

It does not make physical blindness a moral fault.

## Isaiah 44:19

No one thinks about it in his heart. 📖

No one has the knowledge or insight to say, “I burned half in the fire.

I baked bread on its coals, roasted meat, and ate.

Should I make something detestable from the rest?

Should I bow to a block of wood?”

## Isaiah 44:20

He feeds on ashes. 📖

His deceived heart has led him the wrong way.

He cannot rescue himself or ask, “Is this thing in my right hand not a lie?”

**Help with ashes**

This image describes relying on something worthless.

## Isaiah 44:21

Remember these things, Jacob, because you are My servant, Israel. 📖

I made you, and you are My servant.

Israel, I will never forget you.

## Isaiah 44:22

I have wiped away your offenses like a cloud and your sins like mist. 📖

Return to Me, because I have redeemed you.

## Isaiah 44:23

Heavens, sing for joy, because the LORD has done this! 📖

Depths of the earth, shout aloud!

Mountains, forests, and all your trees, burst into song!

The LORD has redeemed Jacob and shown His glory in Israel.

## Isaiah 44:24

The LORD, your Redeemer who formed you from the womb, says, “I am the LORD who made everything. 📖

I alone stretched out the heavens.

I spread out the earth by Myself.

## Isaiah 44:25

“I defeat the signs of false prophets and make fools of those who tell fortunes. 📖

I confuse the wise and turn their knowledge into nonsense.

## Isaiah 44:26

“I confirm My servant’s message and carry out My messengers’ advice. 📖

I say about Jerusalem, ‘People will live there.’

I say about Judah’s cities, ‘They will be rebuilt, and I will restore their ruins.’

## Isaiah 44:27

“I tell the sea’s depths, ‘Be dry. 📖

I will dry up your currents.’

## Isaiah 44:28

“I say about Cyrus, ‘My shepherd will do everything I desire.’ 📖

I say about Jerusalem, ‘It will be rebuilt.’

I say about the temple, ‘Let its foundation be laid.’”

**Help with My shepherd**

God uses a shepherd image for Cyrus’s role in carrying out His purpose.
