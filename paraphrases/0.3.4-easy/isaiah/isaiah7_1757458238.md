# Isaiah 7 — Ahaz receives a sign and a warning

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends Isaiah to Ahaz during a threatened invasion.

The chapter preserves the sign, its timing, and the warning about Assyria.

## Isaiah 7:1

Ahaz son of Jotham, Uzziah’s son, was king of Judah. 📖

Rezin king of Aram marched against Jerusalem with Pekah son of Remaliah, Israel’s king.

But he could not overpower the city.

## Isaiah 7:2

David’s house heard that Aram had joined Ephraim. 📖

Ahaz’s and his people’s hearts shook like forest trees in wind.

## Isaiah 7:3

The LORD told Isaiah, “Take your son Shear-jashub to meet Ahaz. 📖

Meet at the upper pool’s aqueduct end, on the road to the Launderer’s Field.

**Help with aqueduct:** A channel carrying water.

## Isaiah 7:4

Tell him: Calm down and be quiet. 📖

Do not fear or lose heart over these two smoking firewood stubs.

Do not fear Rezin’s and Aram’s fierce anger, or Remaliah’s son.

## Isaiah 7:5

Aram, Ephraim, and Remaliah’s son plotted your ruin, saying: 📖

## Isaiah 7:6

‘Let us invade Judah, terrify it, and divide it among us. 📖

We will make Tabeal’s son king over it.’

## Isaiah 7:7

But the Lord GOD says, ‘It will not arise. 📖

It will not happen.

## Isaiah 7:8

Aram’s head is Damascus, and Damascus’s head is Rezin. 📖

Within sixty-five years Ephraim will be shattered as a people.

## Isaiah 7:9

Ephraim’s head is Samaria, and Samaria’s head is Remaliah’s son. 📖

If you do not stand firm in faith, you will not stand at all.’”

## Isaiah 7:10

The LORD spoke to Ahaz again: 📖

## Isaiah 7:11

“Ask the LORD your God for a sign, from Sheol’s depths or heaven’s heights.” 📖

**Help with Sheol:** The place of the dead.

## Isaiah 7:12

Ahaz said, “I will not ask. 📖

I will not test the LORD.”

## Isaiah 7:13

Isaiah said, “Listen, house of David! 📖

Is tiring people’s patience not enough?

Will you tire my God’s patience too?

## Isaiah 7:14

The Lord Himself will give you a sign. 📖

Look, the virgin will conceive and bear a son.

She will call Him Immanuel.

**Help with virgin:** Someone who has not had sex.

The name and wording are retained without adding a later identification.

## Isaiah 7:15

By the time He knows to reject evil and choose good, He will eat curds and honey. 📖

**Help with curds:** Thickened milk.

## Isaiah 7:16

Before the boy knows to reject evil and choose good, the two kings’ land you fear will become a wasteland. 📖

## Isaiah 7:17

The LORD will bring a time unlike any since Ephraim separated from Judah. 📖

He will bring it on you, your people, and your father’s house.

He will bring Assyria’s king.”

## Isaiah 7:18

That day the LORD will whistle to flies at the Nile’s farthest streams and bees in Assyria. 📖

## Isaiah 7:19

They will settle in steep ravines, rock cracks, thornbushes, and watering holes. 📖

## Isaiah 7:20

That day the Lord will use a hired razor from beyond the Euphrates, Assyria’s king. 📖

It will shave your head, legs, and beard.

**Help with the razor:** The king is pictured as an instrument of humiliating judgment.

## Isaiah 7:21

That day a man will raise one young cow and two sheep, 📖

## Isaiah 7:22

and eat curds from their plentiful milk. 📖

Everyone left in the land will eat curds and honey.

## Isaiah 7:23

That day places with a thousand vines worth a thousand silver shekels will have only briers and thorns. 📖

**Help with shekels:** Units used to weigh silver.

## Isaiah 7:24

People will go there with bows and arrows. 📖

Briers and thorns will cover the land.

## Isaiah 7:25

Fear of briers and thorns will keep you from hills once worked with a hoe. 📖

Oxen will graze there, and sheep will trample them.
