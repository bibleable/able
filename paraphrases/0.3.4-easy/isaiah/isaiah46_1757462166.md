# Isaiah 46 — God carries His people

**About this chapter**

Bel and Nebo cannot rescue those who carry their images.

The LORD promises to carry His people and carry out His purpose.

## Isaiah 46:1

Bel crouches, and Nebo cowers. 📖

Their idols weigh down animals and cattle.

The images you carry are heavy burdens for tired animals.

**Help with Bel and Nebo**

These are names of gods worshiped in Babylon.

## Isaiah 46:2

The gods cower and crouch together. 📖

They cannot relieve the burden.

They themselves go into captivity.

## Isaiah 46:3

“Listen to Me, house of Jacob, all the survivors of Israel’s house. 📖

I have supported you from the womb and carried you since birth.

## Isaiah 46:4

“I will be the same even in your old age. 📖

I will support you when your hair turns gray.

I made you, and I will carry you.

I will support and rescue you.

**About the speaker**

The LORD speaks to His people, contrasting His care with the burden of carrying idols.

## Isaiah 46:5

“Whom will you say I am like? 📖

Whom will you count equal to Me?

Whom will you compare Me with, as if we were alike?

## Isaiah 46:6

“They pour out bags of gold and weigh silver on scales. 📖

They hire a goldsmith to make it into a god.

Then they bow down and worship.

## Isaiah 46:7

“They lift it onto their shoulder and carry it. 📖

They set it in its place, and it stands there without moving.

They cry to it, but it does not answer.

It saves no one from trouble.

## Isaiah 46:8

“Remember this and be brave. 📖

You who do wrong, take it to heart!

## Isaiah 46:9

“Remember what happened long ago. 📖

I am God, and there is no other.

I am God, and no one is like Me.

## Isaiah 46:10

“I announce the end from the beginning and tell in ancient times what is still to come. 📖

I say, ‘My purpose will stand.

I will do everything that pleases Me.’

## Isaiah 46:11

“I call a bird that hunts from the east, a man for My purpose from a distant land. 📖

I have truly spoken, and I will make it happen.

I have planned it, and I will surely do it.

**Help with the bird**

The image describes a man called to carry out God’s purpose.

## Isaiah 46:12

“Listen to Me, stubborn people who are far from righteousness. 📖

## Isaiah 46:13

“I am bringing My righteousness near. 📖

It is not far away.

My salvation will not be delayed.

I will give salvation to Zion and clothe Israel with My splendor.

**Help with righteousness and salvation**

Righteousness means what is right before God.

Salvation means being saved or rescued.
