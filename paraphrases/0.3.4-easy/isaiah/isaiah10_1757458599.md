# Isaiah 10 — Assyria and God’s judgment

**About this chapter**

God condemns unjust rulers and the pride of Assyria.

Isaiah also speaks of survivors returning to God.

## Isaiah 10:1

How terrible for those who make unjust laws and issue commands that oppress people! 📖

## Isaiah 10:2

They keep poor people from being treated fairly. 📖

They deny justice to the oppressed among My people.

They make widows their prey and rob children without parents.

**About the speaker**

“My people” refers to God’s people.

Prey pictures people treated like animals hunted for another person’s gain.

## Isaiah 10:3

What will you do on the day you must answer for your actions, when destruction comes from far away? 📖

To whom will you run for help?

Where will you leave your wealth?

## Isaiah 10:4

Nothing will remain except to crouch among prisoners or fall among those killed. 📖

Even after all this, God’s anger has not turned away.

His hand is still raised.

## Isaiah 10:5

“How terrible for Assyria, the rod of My anger! 📖

The staff in their hands is My fury.”

**About the speaker and image**

God speaks here.

The rod and staff picture Assyria used to carry out His judgment.

## Isaiah 10:6

“I will send Assyria against a godless nation. 📖

I will send him against people destined for My anger.

He will take goods by force and seize their possessions.

He will trample them like clay in the streets.”

## Isaiah 10:7

But this is not what Assyria intends or plans. 📖

In his heart, he wants to destroy and wipe out many nations.

**About Assyria**

The passage speaks of Assyria and its king as one acting power.

## Isaiah 10:8

The king says, “Are not all my commanders kings? 📖

## Isaiah 10:9

“Is not Calno like Carchemish? 📖

Is not Hamath like Arpad?

Is not Samaria like Damascus?

## Isaiah 10:10

“My hand seized kingdoms that worship idols. 📖

Their images were greater than those of Jerusalem and Samaria.

## Isaiah 10:11

“I dealt with Samaria and its idols. 📖

Will I not do the same to Jerusalem and its idols?”

## Isaiah 10:12

The Lord will complete all His work against Mount Zion and Jerusalem. 📖

Then He will say, “I will punish the king of Assyria for what his arrogant heart produces.

I will punish the proud look in his eyes.

## Isaiah 10:13

“The king says, ‘I have done this by my own strength and wisdom, because I am clever. 📖

I have removed the borders of nations and stolen their treasures.

Like a mighty man, I brought their rulers under my power.

## Isaiah 10:14

“‘My hand reached for the nations’ wealth as someone reaches into a nest. 📖

I gathered the whole earth like someone gathering abandoned eggs.

No wing moved, and no beak opened or chirped.’”

**Help with the nest**

The king boasts that he took the nations’ wealth without anyone resisting.

## Isaiah 10:15

Does an axe make itself greater than the person swinging it? 📖

Does a saw boast over the person using it?

That would be like a rod waving the person who lifts it.

Or it would be like a wooden staff lifting someone who is not wood!

**Help with these tools**

The questions challenge Assyria’s boast that its power is entirely its own.

## Isaiah 10:16

So the Lord GOD of Hosts will send a disease that wastes away Assyria’s strong warriors. 📖

Beneath Assyria’s splendor, a fire will be lit like a burning flame.

## Isaiah 10:17

The Light of Israel will become a fire. 📖

Israel’s Holy One will become a flame.

In a single day, it will burn up Assyria’s thorns and thistles.

**Help with these titles**

The Light of Israel and Israel’s Holy One refer to God.

## Isaiah 10:18

It will completely destroy the glory of Assyria’s forests and orchards, both soul and body. 📖

It will be like a sickness that consumes a man.

**Help with the image**

The forests and orchards picture Assyria’s strength and splendor.

“Soul and body” stresses complete destruction.

## Isaiah 10:19

So few trees will remain in its forests that a child could count them. 📖

## Isaiah 10:20

On that day, Israel’s remaining people and the survivors of Jacob’s house will stop depending on their attacker. 📖

They will truly rely on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 10:21

A remaining group will return to the Mighty God. 📖

They will be survivors of Jacob.

## Isaiah 10:22

Israel, even if your people are as many as the sand of the sea, only a remaining group will return. 📖

Destruction has been ordered, overflowing with righteousness.

**Help with righteousness**

Here the word describes what is right and just in God’s judgment.

## Isaiah 10:23

The Lord GOD of Hosts will carry out the ordered destruction throughout the whole land. 📖

## Isaiah 10:24

The Lord GOD of Hosts says, “My people living in Zion, do not fear Assyria. 📖

Assyria strikes you with a rod and raises a staff against you, as the Egyptians did.

## Isaiah 10:25

“In just a little while, My fury against you will end. 📖

My anger will turn toward their destruction.”

## Isaiah 10:26

The LORD of Hosts will swing a whip against them, as He struck Midian at the rock of Oreb. 📖

He will raise His staff over the sea, as He did in Egypt.

## Isaiah 10:27

On that day, the burden will be lifted from your shoulders. 📖

The yoke will be removed from your neck.

The yoke will break because your neck will be too large.

**Help with the yoke**

A yoke is a wooden bar used to control working animals.

Here it pictures Assyria’s control over the people.

## Isaiah 10:28

Assyria has entered Aiath and passed through Migron. 📖

They store their supplies at Michmash.

## Isaiah 10:29

They have crossed at the place where the water is shallow. 📖

They say, “We will spend the night at Geba.”

Ramah trembles.

Gibeah of Saul flees.

## Isaiah 10:30

Cry aloud, Daughter of Gallim! 📖

Listen, Laishah!

Poor Anathoth!

**Help with Daughter of Gallim**

The name addresses the town’s people.

## Isaiah 10:31

Madmenah flees. 📖

The people of Gebim seek a safe place.

## Isaiah 10:32

Today they will stop at Nob. 📖

They will shake a fist at the mountain of Daughter Zion, the hill of Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 10:33

Look! 📖

The Lord GOD of Hosts will cut off the branches with terrifying power.

The tall trees will be cut down.

The high trees will fall.

## Isaiah 10:34

He will clear the thick forest with an axe. 📖

Lebanon will fall before the Mighty One.

**Help with the forest**

The passage pictures proud power cut down like trees.
