# Isaiah 26 — Trust, prayer, and hope

**About this chapter**

A song in Judah expresses trust, grief, and hope in God.

The chapter speaks of judgment and of the dead rising.

## Isaiah 26:1

On that day, this song will be sung in Judah. 📖

“We have a strong city.

God’s salvation is its walls and defenses.”

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 26:2

Open the gates so that a righteous nation can enter. 📖

This is a nation that remains faithful.

## Isaiah 26:3

You will keep the person whose mind is steady in perfect peace, because he trusts in You. 📖

## Isaiah 26:4

Trust in the LORD forever. 📖

GOD the LORD is the everlasting Rock.

**Help with Rock**

The image describes God’s lasting strength and safety.

## Isaiah 26:5

He has brought down those who live high above others. 📖

He brings the high city low, down to the ground and into the dust.

## Isaiah 26:6

Feet trample it down. 📖

These are the feet of oppressed people, the steps of poor people.

## Isaiah 26:7

The path of righteous people is level. 📖

You clear a straight path for those who do right.

## Isaiah 26:8

Yes, LORD, we wait for You. 📖

We walk in the path of Your judgments.

Deep inside, we desire Your name and honor.

## Isaiah 26:9

Deep inside, I long for You at night. 📖

My spirit seeks You at dawn.

When Your judgments come on earth, the world’s people learn what is right.

## Isaiah 26:10

Even when kindness is shown to a wicked man, he does not learn what is right. 📖

In a land of righteousness, he acts unjustly.

He fails to see the LORD’s greatness.

## Isaiah 26:11

LORD, Your hand is raised, but they do not see it. 📖

They will see Your deep commitment to Your people and be ashamed.

The fire prepared for Your enemies will burn them up!

## Isaiah 26:12

LORD, You will establish peace for us. 📖

Everything we have achieved, You have done for us.

## Isaiah 26:13

LORD our God, other lords have ruled us besides You. 📖

But we acknowledge Your name alone.

## Isaiah 26:14

The dead will not live. 📖

The departed spirits will not rise.

You have punished and destroyed them.

You have wiped out all memory of them.

**About these words**

This follows the mention of other lords who ruled the people.

Verse 19 also speaks of dead people living again.

Both statements are retained for further editorial review.

## Isaiah 26:15

LORD, You have made the nation larger. 📖

You have made the nation larger.

You have gained glory for Yourself.

You have extended all the land’s borders.

## Isaiah 26:16

LORD, they sought You in their distress. 📖

When You disciplined them, they poured out a quiet prayer.

## Isaiah 26:17

A pregnant woman about to give birth twists and cries out in pain. 📖

LORD, that is how we were in Your presence.

## Isaiah 26:18

We were pregnant and twisted in pain, but we gave birth to wind. 📖

We have brought no salvation to the earth.

We have brought no life into the world.

**Help with birth and wind**

The image describes painful effort that failed to bring the hoped-for result.

## Isaiah 26:19

Your dead will live. 📖

Their bodies will rise.

You who live in the dust, wake up and sing!

Your dew is like the dew of morning.

The earth will bring out its dead.

**Help with dew**

Dew is water that forms on cool surfaces.

Here it appears in a promise of renewed life.

## Isaiah 26:20

Go, my people, into your rooms. 📖

Shut your doors behind you.

Hide for a little while until the anger has passed.

## Isaiah 26:21

Look! 📖

The LORD is coming from His dwelling to punish earth’s people for their wrongdoing.

The earth will reveal its bloodshed.

It will no longer hide those who were killed.
