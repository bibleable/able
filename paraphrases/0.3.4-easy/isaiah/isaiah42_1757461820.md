# Isaiah 42 — The LORD’s Servant and justice

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks about His Servant and calls the world to praise Him.

The chapter also challenges people who fail to listen.

## Isaiah 42:1

“Here is My Servant, whom I support, My Chosen One, in whom I delight deeply. 📖

I will put My Spirit on Him.

He will bring justice to the nations.”

**About the speaker**

The LORD speaks about His Servant.

## Isaiah 42:2

“He will not cry out or raise His voice. 📖

He will not make His voice heard in the streets.

## Isaiah 42:3

“He will not break a bruised reed or put out a wick that is barely burning. 📖

He will faithfully bring justice.

**Help with reed and wick**

A reed is a plant with a long stem.

A wick is the part of a lamp that burns.

These images describe gentle care toward what is weak.

## Isaiah 42:4

“He will not grow weak or discouraged before establishing justice on earth. 📖

The islands will put their hope in His law.”

## Isaiah 42:5

God the LORD created the heavens and stretched them out. 📖

He spread out the earth and all that comes from it.

He gives breath to its people and life to those who walk on it.

This is what He says.

## Isaiah 42:6

“I, the LORD, have called you for a righteous purpose. 📖

I will take your hand and keep you.

I will appoint you as a covenant for the people and a light for the nations,

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

Here God speaks of His Servant’s role in His commitment to the people.

## Isaiah 42:7

“to open blind eyes, bring prisoners out of dungeons, and free those sitting in darkness in prison. 📖

## Isaiah 42:8

“I am the LORD. 📖

That is My name!

I will not give My glory to another or My praise to idols.

## Isaiah 42:9

“Look! 📖

The earlier things have happened.

Now I announce new things.

I tell you about them before they appear.”

## Isaiah 42:10

Sing a new song to the LORD. 📖

Praise Him from the ends of the earth.

You who go to sea, and everything in it, praise Him.

You islands and all who live there, praise Him.

## Isaiah 42:11

Let the desert and its cities raise their voices. 📖

Let Kedar’s villages cry aloud.

Let Sela’s people sing for joy and shout from the mountaintops.

## Isaiah 42:12

Let them give glory to the LORD. 📖

Let them announce His praise in the islands.

## Isaiah 42:13

The LORD goes out like a mighty man. 📖

He stirs His determination like a warrior.

He shouts and roars in victory over His enemies.

## Isaiah 42:14

“I have been silent since ages past. 📖

I have kept quiet and held Myself back.

But now I will groan like a woman giving birth.

I will gasp and pant at once.

## Isaiah 42:15

“I will ruin mountains and hills and dry up all their plants. 📖

I will turn rivers into dry land and drain the marshes.

## Isaiah 42:16

“I will lead blind people along a way they did not know. 📖

I will guide them on unfamiliar paths.

I will turn darkness into light before them and rough places into level ground.

I will do these things for them and will not abandon them.

## Isaiah 42:17

“But those who trust idols and tell cast images, ‘You are our gods!’ will turn back in complete shame. 📖

**Help with cast images**

These are objects made by pouring melted metal into a mold.

## Isaiah 42:18

“Deaf people, listen! 📖

Blind people, look so that you may see!

## Isaiah 42:19

“Who is blind but My servant? 📖

Who is deaf like the messenger I send?

Who is blind like My covenant partner, blind like the LORD’s servant?

## Isaiah 42:20

“You see many things but do not keep watch. 📖

Your ears are open, but you do not hear.”

**About sight and hearing**

The challenge uses blindness and deafness to describe failure to pay attention to God.

It does not say a physical disability is wrongdoing.

## Isaiah 42:21

For the sake of His righteousness, the LORD was pleased to make His law great and glorious. 📖

## Isaiah 42:22

But these people have been robbed and plundered. 📖

They are all trapped in caves or held in dungeons.

They have become stolen goods, with no one to rescue them.

No one says, “Send them back!”

## Isaiah 42:23

Who among you will pay attention to this? 📖

Who will listen and obey from now on?

## Isaiah 42:24

Who gave Jacob up to be robbed, and Israel to those who plunder? 📖

Was it not the LORD, against whom we sinned?

They refused to walk in His ways or obey His law.

## Isaiah 42:25

So He poured His furious anger and fierce battle on them. 📖

Flames surrounded them, but they did not understand.

The fire consumed them, but they did not take it to heart.
