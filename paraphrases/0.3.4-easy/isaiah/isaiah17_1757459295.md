# Isaiah 17 — Damascus and Jacob lose their strength

**About this chapter**

The message describes ruined cities and a small group of survivors.

People turn from the things they made to their Maker.

## Isaiah 17:1

This is the message against Damascus. 📖

“Look!

Damascus is no longer a city.

It has become a pile of ruins.

## Isaiah 17:2

“The cities of Aroer are abandoned. 📖

They will be left for flocks to lie down, with no one to frighten them.

## Isaiah 17:3

“Ephraim’s fortress will disappear. 📖

Damascus will lose its power to rule.

What remains of Aram will be like the Israelites’ splendor,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 17:4

“On that day, Jacob’s splendor will fade. 📖

The fat of his body will waste away.

## Isaiah 17:5

“It will be like a worker gathering standing grain and harvesting the heads with his arm. 📖

It will be like someone collecting leftover grain in the Valley of Rephaim.

## Isaiah 17:6

“Yet a few will remain, like olives left after a tree is beaten. 📖

Two or three olives remain at the top.

Four or five remain on its fruitful branches,” declares the LORD, the God of Israel.

**Help with the harvest images**

Most of the people are removed, as crops are gathered.

A few survivors remain, like a few olives left on a tree.

## Isaiah 17:7

On that day, people will look to their Maker. 📖

They will turn their eyes to the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 17:8

They will not look to the altars they made with their hands. 📖

They will not look to the Asherahs and incense altars they made with their fingers.

**Help with Asherahs**

These were objects used in worship of the goddess Asherah.

Incense is a substance burned to give a sweet smell.

## Isaiah 17:9

On that day, their strong cities will be like abandoned thickets and hilltops. 📖

They will be abandoned to the Israelites and completely ruined.

## Isaiah 17:10

You have forgotten the God who saves you. 📖

You have failed to remember the Rock where you find safety.

So you plant delightful gardens and cuttings from foreign vines.

**Help with the Rock**

The Rock is an image of God as a strong place of safety.

## Isaiah 17:11

On the day you plant them, you make them grow. 📖

That morning, you help your seed sprout.

Yet your harvest will disappear on the day of disease and pain that cannot be cured.

## Isaiah 17:12

How terrible is the noise of many peoples! 📖

They rage like roaring seas.

The nations roar and rumble like crashing, powerful waters.

## Isaiah 17:13

The nations rage like many rushing waters. 📖

But God rebukes them, and they flee far away.

The wind drives them like chaff on the hills, like tumbleweeds before a storm.

**Help with chaff**

Chaff is the light covering removed from grain.

The wind can easily blow it away.

## Isaiah 17:14

In the evening, sudden terror comes! 📖

Before morning, they are gone!

This is the share of those who rob us.

This is what awaits those who take our possessions.
