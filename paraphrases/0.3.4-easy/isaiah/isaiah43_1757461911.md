# Isaiah 43 — You are Mine

**About this chapter**

The LORD promises to gather His people and declares that He alone is God.

He also challenges Israel’s failures and speaks of forgiveness and judgment.

## Isaiah 43:1

The LORD created you, Jacob, and formed you, Israel. 📖

Now He says, “Do not fear, because I have redeemed you.

I have called you by name.

You are Mine!

**Help with redeemed**

Redeemed means rescued or set free.

## Isaiah 43:2

“When you pass through waters, I will be with you. 📖

When you go through rivers, they will not overwhelm you.

When you walk through fire, you will not be scorched.

The flames will not burn you.

## Isaiah 43:3

“I am the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior. 📖

I give Egypt as your ransom, Cush and Seba in your place.

**Help with ransom**

A ransom is a price given to release someone.

## Isaiah 43:4

“You are precious and honored in My sight, and I love you. 📖

So I will give people in exchange for you and nations in place of your life.

## Isaiah 43:5

“Do not fear, because I am with you. 📖

I will bring your children from the east and gather you from the west.

## Isaiah 43:6

“I will tell the north, ‘Give them up!’ 📖

I will tell the south, ‘Do not hold them back!’

Bring My sons from far away and My daughters from the ends of the earth.

## Isaiah 43:7

“Bring everyone called by My name and created for My glory. 📖

I have indeed formed and made them.”

## Isaiah 43:8

Bring out a people who have eyes but are blind, who have ears but are deaf. 📖

**Help with the image**

The passage pictures people failing to recognize what God has done.

It does not make a physical disability a moral fault.

## Isaiah 43:9

All nations gather together, and the peoples assemble. 📖

Who among them can announce this and tell us the earlier things?

Let them bring witnesses to prove their case.

Then others can hear and say, “It is true.”

## Isaiah 43:10

The LORD declares, “You are My witnesses and My servant whom I chose. 📖

You are to consider and believe Me and understand that I am He.

No god was formed before Me, and none will come after Me.

## Isaiah 43:11

“I, yes I, am the LORD. 📖

There is no Savior except Me.

## Isaiah 43:12

“I alone ordered, saved, and announced it, not some foreign god among you. 📖

So you are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “that I am God.

## Isaiah 43:13

“Even from eternity, I am He. 📖

No one can rescue anyone from My hand.

When I act, who can reverse it?”

## Isaiah 43:14

The LORD, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, says, “For your sake, I will send to Babylon. 📖

I will bring them all as fleeing people, even the Chaldeans, in the ships they rejoice in.

## Isaiah 43:15

“I am the LORD, your Holy One, the Creator of Israel, and your King.” 📖

## Isaiah 43:16

The LORD makes a way in the sea and a path through the rushing waters. 📖

## Isaiah 43:17

He brings out chariots and horses, armies and warriors together. 📖

They lie down, never to rise again.

They are put out like a lamp’s wick.

This is what He says.

## Isaiah 43:18

“Do not remember the former things or pay attention to things of long ago. 📖

## Isaiah 43:19

“Look! 📖

I am about to do something new.

It is already coming.

Do you not see it?

I will make a way in the wilderness and streams in the desert.

## Isaiah 43:20

“The animals of the field will honor Me, the jackals and ostriches. 📖

I provide water in the wilderness and rivers in the desert to give My chosen people a drink.

## Isaiah 43:21

“The people I formed for Myself will announce My praise. 📖

## Isaiah 43:22

“But you have not called on Me, Jacob. 📖

You have grown tired of Me, Israel.

## Isaiah 43:23

“You have not brought Me sheep for burnt offerings or honored Me with your sacrifices. 📖

I have not burdened you with offerings or tired you with frankincense.

**Help with offerings and frankincense**

Offerings and sacrifices are gifts given in worship.

A burnt offering was burned on an altar.

Frankincense is a substance burned to give a sweet smell.

## Isaiah 43:24

“You have not used silver to buy Me sweet cane or satisfied Me with your sacrifices’ fat. 📖

Instead, you have burdened Me with your sins and tired Me with your wrongdoing.

**Help with sweet cane**

This is an aromatic plant used in worship.

## Isaiah 43:25

“I, yes I, am the One who wipes out your offenses for My own sake. 📖

I will remember your sins no more.

## Isaiah 43:26

“Remind Me. 📖

Let us argue the matter together.

State your case so that you may be shown to be right.

## Isaiah 43:27

“Your first father sinned, and your spokesmen rebelled against Me. 📖

## Isaiah 43:28

“So I will disgrace the princes of your holy place. 📖

I will give Jacob over to destruction and Israel to insults.”
