# Isaiah 24 — The earth is shaken

**About this chapter**

The chapter describes judgment across the earth.

Songs of praise are heard, while Isaiah also expresses grief.

## Isaiah 24:1

Look! 📖

The LORD ruins the earth and leaves it devastated.

He will twist its surface and scatter its people.

## Isaiah 24:2

This affects people and priest alike, servant and master, maid and mistress. 📖

It affects buyer and seller, lender and borrower, creditor and debtor.

**Help with creditor and debtor**

A creditor is owed a payment.

A debtor owes a payment.

## Isaiah 24:3

The earth will be completely ruined and thoroughly robbed. 📖

The LORD has spoken this word.

## Isaiah 24:4

The earth mourns and withers. 📖

The world weakens and fades.

The earth’s great people waste away.

## Isaiah 24:5

The earth is polluted by its people. 📖

They have broken the laws and gone against the commands.

They have broken the everlasting covenant.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Isaiah 24:6

So a curse has consumed the earth. 📖

Its people must bear their guilt.

The earth’s people have been burned, and only a few survive.

## Isaiah 24:7

The new wine dries up. 📖

The vine withers.

All who celebrated now groan.

## Isaiah 24:8

The joyful tambourines have stopped. 📖

The noise of those celebrating has ended.

The joyful harp is silent.

## Isaiah 24:9

They no longer sing while drinking wine. 📖

Strong drink tastes bitter to those who drink it.

## Isaiah 24:10

The city of chaos is shattered. 📖

Every house is closed so that no one can enter.

## Isaiah 24:11

They cry out for wine in the streets. 📖

All joy turns to gloom.

Rejoicing is driven out of the land.

## Isaiah 24:12

The city is left in ruins. 📖

Its gate is broken into rubble.

## Isaiah 24:13

This is how it will be on earth and among the nations. 📖

It will be like an olive tree after harvest, or grapes left after gathering.

**Help with the harvest image**

The picture describes only a few remaining.

## Isaiah 24:14

They raise their voices and shout for joy. 📖

From the west, they announce the LORD’s greatness.

## Isaiah 24:15

So honor the LORD in the east. 📖

Praise the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, in the islands of the sea.

## Isaiah 24:16

From the ends of the earth, we hear singing. 📖

“Glory to the Righteous One!”

But I said, “I am wasting away!

I am wasting away!

How terrible for me!”

Betrayers betray.

Those who cannot be trusted keep betraying.

**About the voices**

The singers praise the Righteous One.

Isaiah answers with grief over treachery.

## Isaiah 24:17

People living on earth, terror, a pit, and a trap await you. 📖

## Isaiah 24:18

Anyone fleeing the sound of panic will fall into the pit. 📖

Anyone climbing out of the pit will be caught in the trap.

The windows of heaven are open.

The earth’s foundations are shaken.

## Isaiah 24:19

The earth is completely broken apart. 📖

The earth is split open.

The earth is shaken violently.

## Isaiah 24:20

The earth staggers like a drunk person and sways like a hut. 📖

Its rebellion weighs it down.

It falls and will never rise again.

## Isaiah 24:21

On that day, the LORD will punish the heavenly army above and the kings of the earth below. 📖

## Isaiah 24:22

They will be gathered like prisoners in a pit. 📖

They will be locked in a dungeon and punished after many days.

## Isaiah 24:23

The moon will be confused, and the sun will be ashamed. 📖

The LORD of Hosts will rule on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem.

He will rule with great glory before His elders.
