# Isaiah 64 — A prayer from ruined Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

The prayer asks God to come down and act.

The people confess their sins and ask their Father and Maker for mercy.

## Isaiah 64:1

If only You would tear the heavens apart and come down! 📖

Then mountains would shake at Your presence,

## Isaiah 64:2

as fire sets dry branches alight and makes water boil. 📖

Make Your name known to Your enemies so that the nations tremble at Your presence!

## Isaiah 64:3

When You did awesome things we did not expect, You came down. 📖

The mountains shook at Your presence.

## Isaiah 64:4

From ancient times, no one has heard, no ear has understood, and no eye has seen any God except You. 📖

You act for those who wait for You.

## Isaiah 64:5

You welcome people who gladly do right and remember Your ways. 📖

You were surely angry because we sinned.

How can we be saved if we remain in our sins?

## Isaiah 64:6

Each of us has become like something unclean, and all our righteous acts are like filthy rags. 📖

We all wither like leaves, and our wrongdoing carries us away like wind.

**Help with unclean and rags**

The speakers confess that even their righteous acts are stained by wrongdoing.

The comparison does not make ordinary dirt, illness, or disability a moral fault.

## Isaiah 64:7

No one calls on Your name or works to take hold of You. 📖

You have hidden Your face from us and handed us over to our wrongdoing’s power.

## Isaiah 64:8

But now, LORD, You are our Father. 📖

We are the clay, and You are the potter.

We are all the work of Your hand.

**Help with potter**

A potter shapes clay into objects.

The image recognizes God as the people’s Maker.

## Isaiah 64:9

LORD, do not be angry beyond measure or remember our wrongdoing forever. 📖

Please look at us.

We are all Your people!

## Isaiah 64:10

Your holy cities have become a wilderness. 📖

Zion has become a wasteland, and Jerusalem a ruin.

## Isaiah 64:11

Our holy, beautiful temple, where our fathers praised You, has been burned. 📖

Everything dear to us lies in ruins.

## Isaiah 64:12

LORD, after all this, will You hold Yourself back? 📖

Will You stay silent and make us suffer beyond measure?
