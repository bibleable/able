# Isaiah 23 — Tyre loses its trade

**About this chapter**

The message announces destruction for the trading city of Tyre.

It also describes what happens after seventy years.

## Isaiah 23:1

This is the message against Tyre. 📖

Wail, ships of Tarshish!

Tyre is ruined, without house or harbor.

The news has reached them from Cyprus.

## Isaiah 23:2

Be silent, people of the coastland, merchants of Sidon! 📖

Your traders have crossed the sea.

## Isaiah 23:3

Shihor’s grain came across the great waters. 📖

The Nile’s harvest brought income to Tyre.

Tyre traded with the nations.

## Isaiah 23:4

Be ashamed, Sidon, stronghold of the sea! 📖

The sea has spoken.

“I have not gone through labor or given birth.

I have not raised young men or brought up young women.”

**Help with the sea’s speech**

The message pictures the sea as a parent speaking about children.

## Isaiah 23:5

When the report reaches Egypt, people will twist in pain over the news of Tyre. 📖

## Isaiah 23:6

Cross over to Tarshish. 📖

People of the coastland, cry out in grief!

## Isaiah 23:7

Is this your joyful city, founded so long ago? 📖

Did its feet carry it to settle far away?

**Help with feet**

The city is pictured as someone traveling to establish settlements.

## Isaiah 23:8

Who planned this against Tyre, the giver of crowns? 📖

Its traders are princes.

Its merchants are famous throughout the earth.

## Isaiah 23:9

The LORD of Hosts planned it. 📖

He will spoil all its glorious beauty.

He will disgrace all the famous people of the earth.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 23:10

Daughter of Tarshish, farm your land like the Nile. 📖

There is no longer a harbor.

**Help with Daughter of Tarshish**

This name addresses the people of Tarshish.

## Isaiah 23:11

The LORD has stretched His hand over the sea. 📖

He has made kingdoms tremble.

He has commanded that Canaan’s strongholds be destroyed.

## Isaiah 23:12

He said, “Oppressed Virgin Daughter of Sidon, you will rejoice no more. 📖

Get up and cross to Cyprus.

Even there, you will find no rest.”

**Help with Virgin Daughter of Sidon**

The city and its people are pictured as a young woman.

## Isaiah 23:13

Look at the Chaldeans’ land! 📖

They are now a people of no importance.

The Assyrians assigned it to desert creatures.

They set up towers to attack it and stripped its palaces.

They left it ruined.

## Isaiah 23:14

Wail, ships of Tarshish! 📖

Your harbor has been destroyed!

## Isaiah 23:15

At that time, Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years, the length of a king’s life. 📖

At the end of seventy years, Tyre will be like the prostitute in this song.

## Isaiah 23:16

“Forgotten prostitute, take your harp and walk through the city. 📖

Play sweet music and sing many songs so that people will remember you.”

## Isaiah 23:17

At the end of seventy years, the LORD will restore Tyre. 📖

Then it will return to hiring itself out as a prostitute.

It will sell itself to all the kingdoms on earth.

**Help with the prostitute image**

The city’s renewed trade with many kingdoms is pictured as selling sexual services.

## Isaiah 23:18

Yet its profits and wages will be set apart for the LORD. 📖

They will not be stored or saved.

Its profits will go to those who live before the LORD, for plenty of food and fine clothing.
