# Isaiah 31 — Look to the LORD for protection

**About this chapter**

Isaiah warns against relying on Egypt’s army.

The LORD promises to protect Jerusalem and defeat Assyria.

## Isaiah 31:1

How terrible for those who go to Egypt for help! 📖

They rely on horses, many chariots, and great numbers of horsemen.

They do not look to the Holy One of Israel or seek the LORD.

## Isaiah 31:2

Yet He too is wise and brings disaster. 📖

He does not take back His words.

He will rise against the house of wicked people and those who help evildoers.

## Isaiah 31:3

The Egyptians are human, not God. 📖

Their horses are flesh, not spirit.

When the LORD stretches His hand out, the helper will stumble and the one helped will fall.

Both will die together.

## Isaiah 31:4

The LORD has told me, “A lion or young lion roars over its prey. 📖

A group of shepherds is called against it.

But their shouting does not frighten it, and their noise does not overcome it.

In the same way, the LORD of Hosts will come down to fight on Mount Zion and its heights.

## Isaiah 31:5

“Like birds hovering above, the LORD of Hosts will protect Jerusalem. 📖

He will shield it and rescue it.

He will pass over it and keep it safe.”

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 31:6

Children of Israel, return to the One you have openly rebelled against. 📖

## Isaiah 31:7

On that day, every one of you will reject your silver and gold idols. 📖

Your own hands sinned by making them.

## Isaiah 31:8

God says, “Assyria will fall, but not by a human sword. 📖

A sword will consume them, but it will not be one made by mortals.

They will flee from the sword.

Their young men will be forced to work.

## Isaiah 31:9

“Their rock will pass away in fear. 📖

Their princes will panic at the sight of the battle banner,” declares the LORD.

His fire is in Zion, and His furnace is in Jerusalem.

**Help with rock and banner**

The rock pictures a source of strength or safety.

A banner is a raised sign used in battle.
