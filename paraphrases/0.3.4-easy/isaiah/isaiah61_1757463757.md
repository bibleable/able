# Isaiah 61 — Good news and lasting joy

**About this chapter**

A speaker anointed by the LORD announces good news and freedom.

The chapter promises restoration and ends in joyful praise.

## Isaiah 61:1

The Spirit of the Lord GOD is on Me. 📖

The LORD has anointed Me to bring good news to poor people.

He sent Me to bind up broken hearts and announce freedom to captives and prisoners.

**Help with anointed**

Anointed means set apart for a role.

Binding broken hearts pictures care for people in deep grief.

## Isaiah 61:2

He sent Me to announce the year of the LORD’s favor and the day of our God’s vengeance. 📖

He sent Me to comfort all who mourn,

## Isaiah 61:3

and to comfort Zion’s mourners. 📖

He sent Me to give them a crown of beauty instead of ashes, joy’s oil instead of mourning.

He sent Me to give them praise’s clothing instead of a spirit of despair.

They will be called oaks of righteousness, planted by the LORD so that He may be glorified.

**Help with the images**

Ashes were a sign of grief, and oil could be used in celebration.

The changed clothing pictures a change from grief to joy.

Oaks picture people made strong in what is right.

## Isaiah 61:4

They will rebuild ancient ruins and restore places devastated long ago. 📖

They will renew ruined cities, places left empty for many generations.

## Isaiah 61:5

Strangers will stand and feed your flocks. 📖

Foreigners will plow your fields and care for your vineyards.

## Isaiah 61:6

But you will be called the LORD’s priests and servants of our God. 📖

You will feed on the nations’ wealth and boast in their riches.

## Isaiah 61:7

Instead of shame, My people will receive a double share. 📖

Instead of humiliation, they will rejoice in their portion.

They will inherit a double share in their land and have everlasting joy.

## Isaiah 61:8

I, the LORD, love justice and hate robbery and wrongdoing. 📖

In My faithfulness, I will give them their repayment and make an everlasting covenant with them.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Isaiah 61:9

Their descendants will be known among the nations and peoples. 📖

All who see them will acknowledge that they are a people the LORD has blessed.

## Isaiah 61:10

I will rejoice greatly in the LORD, and my soul will rejoice in my God. 📖

He has clothed me with garments of salvation and wrapped me in a robe of righteousness.

I am like a bridegroom wearing a priestly headdress, like a bride putting on her jewels.

**Help with the clothing**

The wedding images describe joy in God’s rescue and righteousness.

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

Righteousness means what is right before God.

## Isaiah 61:11

The earth produces growth, and a garden makes seed sprout. 📖

In the same way, the Lord GOD will make righteousness and praise grow before all nations.
