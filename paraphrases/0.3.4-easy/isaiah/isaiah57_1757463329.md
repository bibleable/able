# Isaiah 57 — False worship and healing for the humble

**About this chapter**

The chapter condemns false worship, sexual unfaithfulness, and killing children.

The LORD also promises to revive and heal humble people.

## Isaiah 57:1

Righteous people die, and no one takes it to heart. 📖

Devoted people are taken away, and no one considers why.

The righteous are taken away from the presence of evil.

## Isaiah 57:2

Those who live rightly enter peace. 📖

They lie down in death and find rest.

## Isaiah 57:3

“But come here, you sons of a woman who practices magic! 📖

You are children of adulterers and prostitutes!

**Help with these words**

Adultery is sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

A prostitute sells sexual services.

The rebuke uses family and sexual images to condemn unfaithfulness.

## Isaiah 57:4

“Whom are you mocking? 📖

At whom do you sneer and stick out your tongue?

Are you not children of wrongdoing, offspring of deceit,

## Isaiah 57:5

“burning with desire among oaks and under every leafy tree? 📖

You kill your children in the valleys, under the cracks in the rocks.

## Isaiah 57:6

“Your share is among the valley’s smooth stones. 📖

They are indeed your portion.

You even poured drink offerings to them and brought grain offerings.

Should I hold back because of these?

**About the offerings**

The stones are treated as objects of worship.

The LORD challenges these acts.

## Isaiah 57:7

“You made your bed on a high hill. 📖

You went up there to offer sacrifices.

## Isaiah 57:8

“You set up your memorial behind the door and doorpost. 📖

You abandoned Me, uncovered your bed, climbed up, and opened it wide.

You made an agreement with those whose bed you loved.

You looked at their nakedness.

## Isaiah 57:9

“You went to Molech with oil and increased your perfumes. 📖

You sent messengers far away and went down even to Sheol itself.

**Help with Molech and Sheol**

Molech is named here in the rebuke of false worship.

Sheol names the place of the dead.

## Isaiah 57:10

“Your many journeys tired you. 📖

But you did not say, ‘There is no hope!’

You found new strength, so you did not grow weak.

## Isaiah 57:11

“Whom did you dread and fear so much that you lied? 📖

You failed to remember Me or take this to heart.

Is it because I have long been silent that you do not fear Me?

## Isaiah 57:12

“I will expose your righteousness and your deeds. 📖

They will not benefit you.

## Isaiah 57:13

“When you cry out, let your groups of idols rescue you! 📖

The wind will carry them all away.

A breath will take them.

But whoever seeks shelter in Me will inherit the land and possess My holy mountain.”

## Isaiah 57:14

It will be said, “Build it up, build it up! 📖

Prepare the way.

Remove every obstacle from My people’s path.”

## Isaiah 57:15

The One who is high and lifted up, who lives in eternity and whose name is Holy, says this. 📖

“I live in a high and holy place, and with those who are oppressed and humble in spirit.

I restore the spirit of the lowly and revive the hearts of those sorry for their sin.

## Isaiah 57:16

“I will not accuse forever or always be angry. 📖

Otherwise, the human spirit would grow weak before Me, the breath of life I made.

## Isaiah 57:17

“I was furious because of his sinful greed, so I struck him. 📖

I hid My face in anger.

Yet he kept turning back to his heart’s desires.

## Isaiah 57:18

“I have seen his ways, but I will heal him. 📖

I will guide him and bring comfort back to him and those who mourn for him,

## Isaiah 57:19

“bringing praise to their lips. 📖

Peace, peace to those far away and near,” says the LORD.

“I will heal them.”

## Isaiah 57:20

But the wicked are like a sea tossed by storms. 📖

It cannot be still, and its waves stir up mud and dirt.

## Isaiah 57:21

My God says, “There is no peace for the wicked.” 📖
