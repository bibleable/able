# Isaiah 39 — Visitors from Babylon

**About this chapter**

Hezekiah shows Babylon’s visitors his treasures.

Isaiah brings a warning about what will happen to the treasures and Hezekiah’s descendants.

## Isaiah 39:1

Merodach-baladan son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah at that time. 📖

He had heard about Hezekiah’s illness and recovery.

## Isaiah 39:2

Hezekiah gladly welcomed the messengers. 📖

He showed them his treasure house, including silver, gold, spices, and precious oil.

He showed them his entire store of weapons and everything in his storehouses.

He showed them everything in his palace and throughout all the land he ruled.

## Isaiah 39:3

The prophet Isaiah asked King Hezekiah, “Where did those men come from? 📖

What did they say to you?”

Hezekiah replied, “They came from a distant land, from Babylon.”

## Isaiah 39:4

Isaiah asked, “What have they seen in your palace?” 📖

Hezekiah answered, “They have seen everything in my palace.

There is nothing among my treasures that I did not show them.”

## Isaiah 39:5

Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Hear the word of the LORD of Hosts. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 39:6

“The time will surely come when everything in your palace will be carried to Babylon. 📖

Everything your fathers stored until this day will be taken.

Nothing will remain, says the LORD.

## Isaiah 39:7

“Some of your descendants, your own flesh and blood, will be taken away. 📖

They will become eunuchs in the palace of Babylon’s king.”

**Help with eunuchs**

Eunuchs were men serving in royal courts, often after their testicles were removed.

The warning includes the descendants’ loss of freedom and this bodily harm.

## Isaiah 39:8

Hezekiah told Isaiah, “The word of the LORD you have spoken is good.” 📖

He thought, “At least there will be peace and security during my lifetime.”
