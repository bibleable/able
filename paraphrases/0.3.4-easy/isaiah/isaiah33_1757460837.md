# Isaiah 33 — The LORD is Judge and King

**About this chapter**

The chapter warns a destroyer and includes a prayer for help.

It describes judgment, right living, and safety for Zion.

## Isaiah 33:1

How terrible for you, destroyer who has never been destroyed! 📖

How terrible for you, betrayer who has never been betrayed!

When you finish destroying, you will be destroyed.

When you finish betraying, you will be betrayed.

## Isaiah 33:2

LORD, be kind to us! 📖

We wait for You.

Be our strength every morning.

Be our salvation in times of trouble.

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 33:3

The peoples flee from Your thundering voice. 📖

The nations scatter when You rise.

## Isaiah 33:4

Nations, your goods are gathered as locusts gather food. 📖

People sweep over them like a swarm of locusts.

## Isaiah 33:5

The LORD is high above all, because He lives on high. 📖

He has filled Zion with justice and righteousness.

## Isaiah 33:6

He will be the firm foundation for your times. 📖

He will be a storehouse of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge.

Fear of the LORD is Zion’s treasure.

**Help with fear of the LORD**

This means deep respect for God and His authority.

## Isaiah 33:7

Look! 📖

Their brave men cry aloud in the streets.

The messengers of peace weep bitterly.

## Isaiah 33:8

The highways are empty. 📖

Travel has stopped.

The treaty is broken, witnesses are despised, and human life is disregarded.

## Isaiah 33:9

The land mourns and wastes away. 📖

Lebanon is ashamed and decaying.

Sharon is like a desert.

Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves.

## Isaiah 33:10

The LORD says, “Now I will rise. 📖

Now I will lift Myself high.

Now I will be exalted.

## Isaiah 33:11

“You become pregnant with chaff and give birth to stubble. 📖

Your breath is a fire that will burn you up.

**Help with chaff and stubble**

Chaff is the light covering removed from grain.

Stubble is the stalk left after cutting grain.

The images describe plans producing nothing useful and bringing destruction.

## Isaiah 33:12

“The peoples will be burned to ashes. 📖

They will be like thorns cut down and set on fire.

## Isaiah 33:13

“You who are far away, hear what I have done. 📖

You who are near, acknowledge My power.”

## Isaiah 33:14

The sinners in Zion are afraid. 📖

Those who live without regard for God tremble.

They ask, “Who among us can live with a fire that burns things up?

Who among us can live with everlasting flames?”

## Isaiah 33:15

The answer is the person who acts rightly and speaks sincerely. 📖

He refuses money gained by threats and never takes a bribe.

He refuses to listen to murder plots or look at evil.

## Isaiah 33:16

That person will live on the heights. 📖

A mountain fortress will be his shelter.

His food will be provided, and his water will be sure.

## Isaiah 33:17

Your eyes will see the King in His beauty. 📖

They will see a land stretching far away.

## Isaiah 33:18

Your mind will think about the former terror. 📖

“Where is the one who counts?

Where is the one who weighs?

Where is the one who counts the towers?”

## Isaiah 33:19

You will no longer see the arrogant people. 📖

Their speech cannot be understood.

They stammer in a language you cannot understand.

**About this description**

The passage describes a foreign people whose words the hearers cannot understand.

It does not make a speech difficulty a moral fault.

## Isaiah 33:20

Look at Zion, the city of our appointed feasts. 📖

Your eyes will see Jerusalem as a peaceful pasture, a tent that does not move.

Its pegs will not be pulled up.

None of its ropes will break.

## Isaiah 33:21

There the Majestic One, our LORD, will be a place of rivers and wide canals for us. 📖

No ship with oars will row there.

No grand ship will pass through.

## Isaiah 33:22

The LORD is our Judge. 📖

The LORD gives us laws.

The LORD is our King.

He will save us.

## Isaiah 33:23

Your ropes hang loose. 📖

They cannot hold the mast firm or spread the sail.

Then plenty of goods taken in battle will be shared.

Even people who cannot walk easily will carry goods away.

**Help with the ship**

The loose ropes and failed sail picture lost strength.

## Isaiah 33:24

No one living in Zion will say, “I am sick.” 📖

The people living there will be forgiven for their wrongdoing.

**About these promises**

The passage promises healing and forgiveness to Zion.

It does not say every illness is caused by personal wrongdoing.
