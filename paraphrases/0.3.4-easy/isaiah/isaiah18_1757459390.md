# Isaiah 18 — A message along the rivers of Cush

**About this chapter**

Messengers travel from a powerful nation.

The LORD watches and acts, and gifts are brought to Him at Mount Zion.

## Isaiah 18:1

How terrible for the land of whirring wings along the rivers of Cush! 📖

## Isaiah 18:2

That land sends messengers by sea in papyrus boats on the water. 📖

Go, swift messengers, to a tall people with smooth skin.

Go to a people feared far and wide.

Go to a powerful nation with unfamiliar speech, whose land is divided by rivers.

**Help with papyrus**

Papyrus is a water plant used to make boats and other materials.

## Isaiah 18:3

All people of the world, all who live on earth, you will see a banner raised on the mountains. 📖

You will hear a ram’s horn when it sounds.

**Help with a banner**

A banner is a raised sign that people can see and gather around.

## Isaiah 18:4

The LORD has told me, “I will quietly watch from My dwelling place. 📖

I will be like shimmering heat in sunshine, like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.”

**About the voice**

Isaiah reports what the LORD told him.

## Isaiah 18:5

Before the harvest, the blossoms fade and the flowers become ripening grapes. 📖

Then He will cut off the shoots with a pruning knife.

He will remove the branches and throw them away.

## Isaiah 18:6

They will all be left to the mountain birds that hunt and to the animals of the land. 📖

The birds will feed on them in summer.

All the wild animals will feed on them in winter.

## Isaiah 18:7

At that time, gifts will be brought to the LORD of Hosts. 📖

They will come from a tall people with smooth skin, feared far and wide.

They will come from a powerful nation with unfamiliar speech, whose land is divided by rivers.

They will come to Mount Zion, the place of the Name of the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.
