# Isaiah 40 — God gives strength to the weary

**About this chapter**

God comforts his people and declares his greatness.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Isaiah 40:1

Your God says, “Comfort my people. 📖

Comfort them.”

## Isaiah 40:2

“Speak gently to Jerusalem. 📖

Tell her the time of hard service is over and her sins are forgiven.

She has received double punishment from the LORD for all her sins.”

**Help with this picture:** Jerusalem is spoken of as a woman, representing its people.

## Isaiah 40:3

A voice calls, “Prepare a way for the LORD in the desert. 📖

Make a straight highway for our God.”

## Isaiah 40:4

“Every valley will be raised. 📖

Every mountain and hill will be lowered.

Rough ground will become smooth, and uneven land will become flat.”

## Isaiah 40:5

“The LORD's glory will be shown. 📖

All people will see it together.

The LORD himself has spoken.”

**Help with a word:** Glory is God's greatness made known.

## Isaiah 40:6

A voice says, “Cry out!” 📖

I asked, “What should I cry out?”

“All people are like grass.

All their glory is like flowers in a field.

## Isaiah 40:7

“Grass dries up and flowers fall when the LORD's breath blows on them. 📖

People really are like grass.”

## Isaiah 40:8

“Grass dries up and flowers fall. 📖

But our God's word stands forever.”

## Isaiah 40:9

Zion, bringer of good news, climb a high mountain. 📖

Jerusalem, shout loudly without fear.

Tell Judah's cities, “Here is your God!”

## Isaiah 40:10

The Lord GOD comes with power. 📖

His strong arm establishes his rule.

He brings his reward and payment with him.

## Isaiah 40:11

He cares for His flock like a shepherd. 📖

He gathers lambs in His arms and carries them close to His heart.

He gently leads mother sheep feeding their lambs.

**Help with this picture:** The shepherd pictures God’s tender care for His people.

## Isaiah 40:12

Who measured the waters in the hollow of his hand? 📖

Who marked off the heavens with the width of his outstretched hand?

Who held earth’s dust in a basket?

Who weighed mountains on a scale and hills on a balance?

## Isaiah 40:13

Who has guided the LORD's Spirit or advised him? 📖

## Isaiah 40:14

Whom did He ask for advice to gain understanding? 📖

Who taught Him the paths of justice?

Who gave Him knowledge and showed Him the way of understanding?

## Isaiah 40:15

Nations are like a drop in a bucket or dust on scales before him. 📖

He lifts islands as if they were fine dust.

## Isaiah 40:16

Lebanon would not provide enough wood for fuel. 📖

Its animals would not be enough for a burnt offering.

**Help with a burnt offering**

This was an offering burned on an altar.

## Isaiah 40:17

All nations are like nothing before him. 📖

He counts them as emptiness.

## Isaiah 40:18

Who can you compare God with? 📖

What image could be like him?

## Isaiah 40:19

Could he be like an idol cast by a craftsman? 📖

A metalworker covers it with gold and adds silver chains.

**Help with a word:** An idol is an object worshipped as a god.

## Isaiah 40:20

Someone too poor for that chooses wood that will not rot. 📖

They find a skilled worker to make an idol that will not fall over.

## Isaiah 40:21

Do you not know? 📖

Have you not heard?

Has it not been told to you from the beginning?

Have you not understood since the earth’s foundation?

## Isaiah 40:22

God sits on His throne above the circle of the earth. 📖

Its people are like grasshoppers.

He stretches the heavens like a curtain.

He spreads them like a tent to live in.

**Help with the images**

They picture God’s greatness above the world and its people.

## Isaiah 40:23

He makes powerful leaders into nothing. 📖

He makes earth's rulers powerless.

## Isaiah 40:24

They have hardly been planted or sown. 📖

Their stems have hardly taken root in the ground.

Then He blows on them, and they wither.

A whirlwind sweeps them away like stubble.

**Help with stubble**

Stubble is the stalk left after grain is cut.

The image describes rulers losing power quickly.

## Isaiah 40:25

The Holy One asks, “Who is like me? 📖

Who is my equal?”

**Help with this title:** The Holy One means God, who is completely pure and set apart.

## Isaiah 40:26

Lift your eyes high. 📖

Who created all these stars?

He brings out the starry army by number and calls each one by name.

Because of His great power and mighty strength, not one is missing.

## Isaiah 40:27

Jacob, why do you say these words? 📖

Israel, why do you insist on them?

“My way is hidden from the LORD.

My God ignores my claim.”

## Isaiah 40:28

Do you not know? 📖

Have you not heard?

The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth.

He does not grow tired or weary.

No one can fully search out His understanding.

## Isaiah 40:29

He gives power to tired people. 📖

He makes weak people stronger.

## Isaiah 40:30

Even young people grow tired. 📖

Even strong young men stumble and fall.

## Isaiah 40:31

But people who wait for the LORD will receive new strength. 📖

They will rise on wings like eagles.

They will run without growing weary and walk without fainting.

**Help with this picture:** The eagle pictures strength renewed by God.
