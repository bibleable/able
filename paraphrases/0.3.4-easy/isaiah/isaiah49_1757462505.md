# Isaiah 49 — The Servant and Zion’s children

**About this chapter**

The Servant speaks about the LORD’s call.

The LORD answers Zion’s fear of being forgotten and promises to gather her children.

## Isaiah 49:1

Islands, listen to Me. 📖

Distant peoples, pay attention.

The LORD called Me from the womb.

He named Me from My mother’s body.

**About the voice**

The Servant speaks here and reports the LORD’s words.

## Isaiah 49:2

He made My mouth like a sharp sword. 📖

He hid Me in the shadow of His hand.

He made Me like a polished arrow and hid Me in His arrow case.

**Help with these images**

They picture the Servant prepared and protected for His task.

## Isaiah 49:3

He told Me, “You are My Servant, Israel. 📖

I will display My glory in You.”

## Isaiah 49:4

But I said, “I have worked for nothing. 📖

I have spent My strength without useful result.

Yet the LORD will show that I am right, and My reward is with My God.”

## Isaiah 49:5

The LORD formed Me from the womb as His Servant to bring Jacob back to Him. 📖

He did this so that Israel might be gathered to Him.

I am honored in the LORD’s sight, and My God is My strength.

Now the LORD speaks.

## Isaiah 49:6

He says, “It is not enough for You to be My Servant, raising Jacob’s tribes and restoring Israel’s protected ones. 📖

I will also make You a light for the nations.

You will bring My salvation to the ends of the earth.”

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 49:7

The LORD, Israel’s Redeemer and Holy One, speaks to the One the nation despised and hated, the Servant of rulers. 📖

“Kings will see You and rise.

Princes will bow because of the faithful LORD, the Holy One of Israel, who chose You.”

## Isaiah 49:8

The LORD says, “At the time of favor, I will answer You. 📖

On the day of salvation, I will help You.

I will keep You and appoint You as a covenant for the people.

You will restore the land and assign its deserted inheritances,

**Help with covenant and inheritances**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

Inheritances here are portions of land belonging to families.

## Isaiah 49:9

“telling prisoners, ‘Come out,’ and those in darkness, ‘Show yourselves.’ 📖

They will feed along the paths and find pasture on every bare hill.

## Isaiah 49:10

“They will not hunger or thirst. 📖

Scorching heat and sun will not beat down on them.

The One who has compassion on them will guide them beside springs of water.

## Isaiah 49:11

“I will turn all My mountains into roads. 📖

My highways will be raised up.

## Isaiah 49:12

“Look! 📖

They will come from far away, from the north, the west, and the land of Aswan.”

## Isaiah 49:13

Heavens, shout for joy! 📖

Earth, rejoice!

Mountains, burst into song!

The LORD has comforted His people.

He will have compassion on His suffering ones.

## Isaiah 49:14

But Zion said, “The LORD has abandoned me! 📖

The Lord has forgotten me!”

**Help with Zion**

The city and its people are pictured as a woman speaking.

## Isaiah 49:15

“Can a woman forget the child she nurses or lack compassion for the son of her womb? 📖

Even if she could forget, I will not forget you!

## Isaiah 49:16

“Look! 📖

I have written you on the palms of My hands.

Your walls are always before Me.

## Isaiah 49:17

“Your builders hurry back. 📖

Those who destroyed and ruined you leave you.

## Isaiah 49:18

“Lift your eyes and look around. 📖

They all gather and come to you.

As surely as I live,” declares the LORD, “you will wear them all like jewelry.

You will put them on like a bride.

## Isaiah 49:19

“Your ruined, deserted places and devastated land will now be too small for your people. 📖

Those who swallowed you up will be far away.

## Isaiah 49:20

“Yet the children of your bereavement will say where you can hear them, ‘This place is too small. 📖

Make room for us to live here.’

**Help with bereavement**

Bereavement means loss through death.

The image addresses Zion after the loss of her children.

## Isaiah 49:21

“Then you will say in your heart, ‘Who fathered these for me? 📖

I lost my children and could not bear children.

I was exiled and rejected.

Who brought these children up?

Look!

I was left alone.

Where did they come from?’”

## Isaiah 49:22

The Lord GOD says, “Look! 📖

I will lift My hand to the nations and raise My banner to the peoples.

They will bring your sons in their arms and carry your daughters on their shoulders.

## Isaiah 49:23

“Kings will be your foster fathers, and queens will be mothers who nurse you. 📖

They will bow facedown to you and lick the dust at your feet.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

Those who hope in Me will never be ashamed.”

## Isaiah 49:24

Can stolen goods be taken from a mighty man? 📖

Can a cruel ruler’s captives be rescued?

## Isaiah 49:25

The LORD says, “Even the mighty man’s captives will be taken away. 📖

The cruel ruler’s stolen goods will be recovered.

I will oppose those who oppose you.

I will save your children.

## Isaiah 49:26

“I will make those who oppress you eat their own flesh. 📖

They will become drunk on their own blood as if it were wine.

Then all humanity will know that I, the LORD, am your Savior and Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.”

**About this difficult image**

The judgment uses the disturbing images of eating flesh and drinking blood.

They are retained rather than replaced by a general statement of defeat.
