# Isaiah 41 — Do not fear, for I am with you

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to the nations and encourages Israel.

He challenges the claims of idols.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Isaiah 41:1

“Islands, be silent before me. 📖

Let the nations gather strength and come forward to speak.

Let us meet for judgment.”

## Isaiah 41:2

“Who raised up a man from the east and called him to serve a right purpose? 📖

Who gave nations and kings into his power?

His sword turns them to dust.

His bow scatters them like dry grain husks.”

## Isaiah 41:3

“He chases them safely. 📖

He moves so quickly his feet hardly touch the path.”

## Isaiah 41:4

“Who has done this and called each generation from the beginning? 📖

I, the LORD, am the first and the last.

I am he.”

## Isaiah 41:5

The islands see and fear. 📖

People at earth's farthest places tremble.

They come forward together.

## Isaiah 41:6

Each helps the other. 📖

Each tells a neighbour to be strong.

## Isaiah 41:7

Craftsmen encourage goldsmiths. 📖

Workers using hammers and anvils encourage each other.

They praise the joined metal.

They nail the idol down so it cannot fall.

## Isaiah 41:8

“But you, Israel, are my servant. 📖

Jacob, I chose you.

You are the descendants of my friend Abraham.”

## Isaiah 41:9

“I brought you from earth's farthest places. 📖

I called you my servant.

I chose you and have not rejected you.”

## Isaiah 41:10

“Do not fear. 📖

I am with you.

Do not be afraid.

I am your God.

I will strengthen and help you.

My powerful, righteous right hand will hold you up.”

**Help with this picture:** God's hand pictures his power to do right and protect.

## Isaiah 41:11

“Those angry with you will be ashamed and disgraced. 📖

Those fighting you will become nothing and perish.”

## Isaiah 41:12

“You will look for your enemies and not find them. 📖

Those making war against you will become nothing.”

## Isaiah 41:13

“I am the LORD your God. 📖

I hold your right hand.

I tell you not to fear, because I will help you.”

## Isaiah 41:14

The LORD says, “Jacob, you are like a small worm. 📖

You few people of Israel, do not fear.

I will help you.

The Holy One of Israel is your Redeemer.”

**Help with words:** The worm pictures Israel's weakness.

A Redeemer frees or rescues people.

## Isaiah 41:15

“I will make you like a new, sharp threshing tool with many teeth. 📖

You will crush mountains and turn hills into grain husks.”

**Help with this picture:** A threshing tool separates grain from stalks.

It pictures power to overcome great obstacles.

## Isaiah 41:16

“You will toss them up, and the wind will scatter them. 📖

But you will rejoice in the LORD.

You will find honour in the Holy One of Israel.”

## Isaiah 41:17

“Poor and needy people search for water and find none. 📖

Their tongues are dry with thirst.

I, the LORD, will answer.

I, Israel's God, will not leave them.”

## Isaiah 41:18

“I will open rivers on bare hills and springs in valleys. 📖

I will turn desert into pools and dry land into springs.”

## Isaiah 41:19

“I will plant cedars, acacias, myrtles, and olive trees in the wilderness. 📖

I will put cypresses, elms, and boxwood together in the desert.”

**Help with these names:** These are different kinds of trees.

## Isaiah 41:20

“Then everyone can see, know, and understand who did this. 📖

The LORD's hand did it.

The Holy One of Israel created it.”

## Isaiah 41:21

The LORD, Jacob's King, says, “Present your case. 📖

Bring your arguments.”

## Isaiah 41:22

“Let the idols tell what will happen. 📖

Let them explain past events so we can understand their outcome.

Or let them announce the future.”

## Isaiah 41:23

“Tell the future so we can know you are gods. 📖

Do something, good or evil, so we can be amazed together.”

## Isaiah 41:24

“You are nothing. 📖

Your work has no value.

Choosing you is hateful.”

## Isaiah 41:25

“I raised someone from the north, and he has come. 📖

He comes from the east and calls on my name.

He tramples rulers like a potter treading clay.”

## Isaiah 41:26

“Who announced this long ago so we could know and call him right? 📖

None of you announced it.

No one heard your words.”

## Isaiah 41:27

“I first told Zion to look at those coming. 📖

I gave Jerusalem a messenger of good news.”

## Isaiah 41:28

“I look, but no one can advise. 📖

When I ask, none can answer.”

## Isaiah 41:29

“They are all false. 📖

Their works are nothing.

Their images are empty like wind.”
