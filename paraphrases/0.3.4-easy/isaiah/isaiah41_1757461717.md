# Isaiah 41 — Do not fear, for I am with you

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to the nations and encourages Israel.

He challenges the claims of idols.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Isaiah 41:1

“Islands, be silent before me. 📖

Let the nations gather strength and come forward to speak.

Let us meet for judgment.”

## Isaiah 41:2

“Who raised up someone from the east and called him to his feet in righteousness? 📖

He hands nations over to him and brings kings under his power.

He turns them to dust with his sword and to windblown chaff with his bow.

**Help with righteousness and chaff**

Righteousness means what is right before God.

Chaff is the light covering removed from grain.

No name for the person from the east is supplied in this verse.

## Isaiah 41:3

“He chases them safely. 📖

He moves so quickly his feet hardly touch the path.”

## Isaiah 41:4

“Who has done this and called each generation from the beginning? 📖

I, the LORD, am the first and the last.

I am he.”

## Isaiah 41:5

The islands see and fear. 📖

People at earth's farthest places tremble.

They come forward together.

## Isaiah 41:6

Each one helps the other and tells his brother, “Be strong!” 📖

## Isaiah 41:7

The craftsman encourages the goldsmith. 📖

The worker swinging a hammer encourages the worker striking an anvil.

He says of the joined metal, “It is good.”

He nails it down so it will not fall over.

**Help with an anvil**

An anvil is a heavy block used when shaping metal.

## Isaiah 41:8

“But you, Israel, are my servant. 📖

Jacob, I chose you.

You are the descendants of my friend Abraham.”

## Isaiah 41:9

“I brought you from the ends of the earth and called you from its farthest corners. 📖

I said, ‘You are My servant.’

I have chosen you and have not rejected you.”

## Isaiah 41:10

“Do not fear. 📖

I am with you.

Do not be afraid.

I am your God.

I will strengthen and help you.

My powerful, righteous right hand will hold you up.”

**Help with this picture:** God's hand pictures his power to do right and protect.

## Isaiah 41:11

“Those angry with you will be ashamed and disgraced. 📖

Those fighting you will become nothing and perish.”

## Isaiah 41:12

“You will look for your enemies and not find them. 📖

Those making war against you will become nothing.”

## Isaiah 41:13

“I am the LORD your God. 📖

I hold your right hand.

I tell you not to fear, because I will help you.”

## Isaiah 41:14

The LORD says, “Jacob, you are like a small worm. 📖

You few people of Israel, do not fear.

I will help you.

The Holy One of Israel is your Redeemer.”

**Help with words:** The worm pictures Israel's weakness.

A Redeemer frees or rescues people.

## Isaiah 41:15

“Look! 📖

I will make you a threshing sledge, new and sharp, with many teeth.

You will thresh and crush the mountains.

You will turn the hills into chaff.

**Help with threshing sledge and chaff**

A threshing sledge is a heavy tool dragged over cut grain to separate grain from stalks.

Chaff is the light covering removed from grain.

Here the images picture power to overcome great obstacles.

## Isaiah 41:16

“You will toss them into the air, and the wind will carry them away. 📖

A strong wind will scatter them.

But you will rejoice in the LORD.

You will glory in the Holy One of Israel.”

## Isaiah 41:17

“Poor and needy people search for water and find none. 📖

Their tongues are dry with thirst.

I, the LORD, will answer.

I, Israel's God, will not leave them.”

## Isaiah 41:18

“I will open rivers on bare hills and springs in valleys. 📖

I will turn desert into pools and dry land into springs.”

## Isaiah 41:19

“I will plant cedars, acacias, myrtles, and olive trees in the wilderness. 📖

I will put cypresses, elms, and boxwood together in the desert.”

**Help with these names:** These are different kinds of trees.

## Isaiah 41:20

“Then all can see and know, think about and understand, that the LORD’s hand has done this. 📖

The Holy One of Israel has created it.”

## Isaiah 41:21

The LORD, Jacob's King, says, “Present your case. 📖

Bring your arguments.”

## Isaiah 41:22

“Let the idols tell what will happen. 📖

Let them explain past events so we can understand their outcome.

Or let them announce the future.”

## Isaiah 41:23

“Tell us what will come so that we may know you are gods. 📖

Yes, do something good or evil so that together we may look on in dismay.”

## Isaiah 41:24

“Look! 📖

You are nothing, and your work has no value.

Anyone who chooses you is detestable.”

## Isaiah 41:25

“I raised up someone from the north, and he has come. 📖

He comes from the east and calls on My name.

He will march over rulers as if they were mortar, like a potter treading clay.

**Help with mortar and potter**

Mortar is a soft mixture used to hold building stones together.

A potter makes things from clay.

The image pictures rulers crushed beneath the conqueror.

## Isaiah 41:26

“Who announced this from the beginning so that we could know? 📖

Who told it long ago so that we could say, ‘He was right’?

No one announced it.

No one foretold it.

No one heard your words.”

## Isaiah 41:27

“I was the first to tell Zion, ‘Look, here they are!’ 📖

I gave Jerusalem a messenger of good news.”

## Isaiah 41:28

“When I look, there is no one. 📖

There is no adviser among them.

When I ask, they have nothing to say.”

## Isaiah 41:29

“They are all false. 📖

Their works are nothing.

Their images are empty like wind.”
