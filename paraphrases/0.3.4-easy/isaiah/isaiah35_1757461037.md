# Isaiah 35 — A road home with joy

**About this chapter**

The desert blooms, people are restored, and those the LORD rescues return to Zion.

## Isaiah 35:1

The wilderness and dry land will be glad. 📖

The desert will rejoice and blossom like a rose.

## Isaiah 35:2

It will bloom richly and rejoice with joy and singing. 📖

It will receive Lebanon’s glory and the splendor of Carmel and Sharon.

They will see the LORD’s glory, the splendor of our God.

## Isaiah 35:3

Strengthen weak hands. 📖

Steady weak knees!

## Isaiah 35:4

Tell those with anxious hearts, “Be strong! 📖

Do not fear!

Look!

Your God will come with vengeance.

He will come with divine repayment to save you.”

## Isaiah 35:5

Then blind people’s eyes will be opened. 📖

Deaf people’s ears will be opened.

## Isaiah 35:6

Then people who have difficulty walking will leap like deer. 📖

Those who cannot speak will shout for joy.

Water will burst out in the wilderness.

Streams will flow in the desert.

**About these promises**

The passage celebrates restoration.

It does not say disability is a moral fault or that disabled people lack worth.

## Isaiah 35:7

The dry ground will become a pool. 📖

The thirsty land will become springs of water.

Grass, reeds, and papyrus will grow where jackals once lay.

**Help with papyrus**

Papyrus is a plant that grows near water.

## Isaiah 35:8

There will be a highway called the Way of Holiness. 📖

The unclean will not travel on it, only those who walk in the Way.

Fools will not wander onto it.

**Help with holiness and unclean**

Holiness describes being set apart for God.

“Unclean” describes being unfit for what is holy in this passage.

It does not mean that having a disability makes someone morally bad.

## Isaiah 35:9

No lion will be there. 📖

No fierce animal will go up on that road or be found there.

But the redeemed will walk on it.

**Help with redeemed**

The redeemed are people who have been rescued or set free.

## Isaiah 35:10

Those the LORD has redeemed will return and enter Zion with singing. 📖

Everlasting joy will be their crown.

Gladness and joy will overtake them.

Sorrow and sighing will flee away.
