# Isaiah 62 — Zion receives new names

**About this chapter**

The speaker will not be silent until Jerusalem is restored.

The chapter calls for prayer, promises protection for the harvest, and announces the Savior’s coming.

## Isaiah 62:1

For Zion’s sake, I will not be silent. 📖

For Jerusalem’s sake, I will not keep still.

I will continue until its righteousness shines brightly and its salvation burns like a torch.

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 62:2

Nations will see your righteousness, and all kings will see your glory. 📖

You will receive a new name from the LORD’s own mouth.

## Isaiah 62:3

You will be a crown of glory in the LORD’s hand, a royal headband in your God’s palm. 📖

## Isaiah 62:4

You will no longer be called Forsaken, and your land will no longer be named Desolate. 📖

You will be called Hephzibah, and your land Beulah.

The LORD will delight in you, and your land will be His bride.

**Help with the names**

Forsaken means abandoned, and Desolate means empty and ruined.

Hephzibah means “My delight is in her.”

Beulah means “Married.”

## Isaiah 62:5

As a young man marries a young woman, your sons will marry you. 📖

As a bridegroom rejoices over his bride, your God will rejoice over you.

**About the marriage image**

The city and land are pictured as a bride.

The unusual image of sons marrying the city is retained for further editorial review.

## Isaiah 62:6

Jerusalem, I have placed watchmen on your walls. 📖

They will never be silent by day or night.

You who call on the LORD, take no rest,

## Isaiah 62:7

and give Him no rest until He establishes Jerusalem and makes it the praise of the earth. 📖

## Isaiah 62:8

The LORD has sworn by His right hand and powerful arm. 📖

“Never again will I give your grain to your enemies for food.

Foreigners will not drink the new wine you worked to produce.

## Isaiah 62:9

“Those who harvest grain will eat it and praise the LORD. 📖

Those who gather grapes will drink the wine in My holy courts.”

## Isaiah 62:10

Go out, go out through the gates! 📖

Prepare the people’s way!

Build up, build up the highway!

Clear away stones and raise a banner for the nations!

**Help with a banner**

A banner is a raised sign people can gather around.

## Isaiah 62:11

Look! 📖

The LORD has announced to the ends of the earth, “Tell Daughter Zion, ‘Look, your Savior is coming!

His reward is with Him, and His repayment goes before Him.’”

**Help with Daughter Zion**

The city and its people are pictured as a daughter.

## Isaiah 62:12

They will be called the Holy People, the Redeemed of The LORD. 📖

You will be called Sought Out, A City Not Forsaken.

**Help with redeemed**

Redeemed means rescued or set free.
