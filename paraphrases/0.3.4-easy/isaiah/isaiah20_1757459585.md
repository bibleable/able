# Isaiah 20 — Isaiah becomes a sign

**About this chapter**

Isaiah’s nakedness becomes a sign of the coming captivity of Egypt and Cush.

The chapter describes public humiliation.

## Isaiah 20:1

Sargon king of Assyria sent his chief commander to Ashdod. 📖

The commander attacked Ashdod and captured it.

Before the year this happened, the LORD had spoken to Isaiah as follows.

## Isaiah 20:2

The LORD had already spoken through Isaiah son of Amoz. 📖

He said, “Remove the sackcloth from your waist and the sandals from your feet.”

Isaiah did this and walked around naked and barefoot.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth often worn to show grief.

## Isaiah 20:3

Then the LORD said, “My servant Isaiah has gone naked and barefoot for three years. 📖

He has been a sign and warning against Egypt and Cush.

## Isaiah 20:4

“In the same way, Assyria’s king will lead Egypt’s captives and Cush’s exiles away. 📖

Young and old alike will be naked and barefoot, with their buttocks uncovered, to Egypt’s shame.

## Isaiah 20:5

“Those who put their hope in Cush and boasted about Egypt will be distressed and ashamed. 📖

## Isaiah 20:6

“On that day, people of this coastland will speak. 📖

They will say, ‘Look what happened to those we hoped in!

We fled to them for help and rescue from Assyria’s king.

How can we escape now?’”
