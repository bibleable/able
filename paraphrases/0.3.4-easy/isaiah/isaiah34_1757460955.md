# Isaiah 34 — Judgment on Edom

**About this chapter**

The nations are called to hear about judgment.

Edom’s destruction is described with blood, fire, and wild creatures.

## Isaiah 34:1

Nations, come near and listen. 📖

Peoples, pay attention.

Let the earth and everything in it hear.

Let the world and everything that grows from it hear.

## Isaiah 34:2

The LORD is angry with all nations and furious with all their armies. 📖

He will give them over to destruction and slaughter.

## Isaiah 34:3

Their dead will be left unburied. 📖

The stink of their bodies will rise.

The mountains will flow with their blood.

## Isaiah 34:4

All the stars in heaven will dissolve. 📖

The skies will be rolled up like a scroll.

All their stars will fall like dried leaves from a vine, like leaves from a fig tree.

## Isaiah 34:5

God says, “When My sword has drunk its fill in the heavens, it will come down on Edom. 📖

It will fall on the people I have given over to destruction.”

## Isaiah 34:6

The LORD’s sword is bathed in blood and drips with fat. 📖

It has the blood of lambs and goats, and the fat of rams’ kidneys.

The LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah, a great slaughter in Edom.

**About the sacrifice image**

The passage describes judgment through the image of animals killed for sacrifice.

## Isaiah 34:7

Wild oxen will fall with them, young bulls with strong bulls. 📖

Their land will be soaked with blood.

Their soil will be soaked with fat.

## Isaiah 34:8

The LORD has a day of vengeance. 📖

He has a year of repayment for Zion’s cause.

## Isaiah 34:9

Edom’s streams will turn to tar and its soil to sulfur. 📖

Its land will become burning pitch.

**Help with tar, pitch, and sulfur**

Tar and pitch are thick substances that burn.

Sulfur is also a substance that burns.

## Isaiah 34:10

The fire will not be put out, day or night. 📖

Its smoke will rise forever.

The land will stay deserted from generation to generation.

No one will ever pass through it again.

## Isaiah 34:11

Desert owls and screech owls will possess it. 📖

Great owls and ravens will live there.

The LORD will stretch a measuring line of chaos and a plumb line of destruction over Edom.

**Help with measuring lines**

Builders use lines to measure and check their work.

Here the image describes planned devastation.

## Isaiah 34:12

No nobles will be left to announce a king. 📖

All its princes will come to nothing.

## Isaiah 34:13

Thorns will cover its towers. 📖

Thistles and briers will cover its forts.

It will become a home for jackals and ostriches.

## Isaiah 34:14

Desert creatures will meet hyenas. 📖

One wild goat will call to another.

The night creature will settle there and find a resting place.

## Isaiah 34:15

The owl will make its nest there. 📖

It will lay eggs, hatch them, and gather its young under its shade.

Birds that hunt will also gather there, each with its mate.

## Isaiah 34:16

Search and read the LORD’s scroll. 📖

Not one of these creatures will be missing.

Not one will lack its mate.

His mouth has ordered it.

His Spirit will gather them.

## Isaiah 34:17

He has assigned their share. 📖

His hand has measured it out for them.

They will possess it forever.

They will live in it from generation to generation.
