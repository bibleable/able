# Isaiah 22 — The Valley of Vision and the palace officials

**About this chapter**

The message challenges Jerusalem’s response to danger.

God then addresses Shebna and describes Eliakim’s appointment.

## Isaiah 22:1

This is the message against the Valley of Vision. 📖

What is wrong with you now?

Why have you all gone up to the rooftops?

## Isaiah 22:2

You are a noisy city, a town full of celebrations! 📖

Your dead were not killed by the sword.

They did not die in battle.

## Isaiah 22:3

All your rulers fled together and were captured without a bow. 📖

All your fleeing people were captured together, though they had run far away.

## Isaiah 22:4

So I said, “Turn away from me. 📖

Let me weep bitterly!

Do not try to comfort me over the destruction of the daughter of my people.”

**About the voice and image**

Isaiah expresses grief.

“Daughter of my people” pictures his community as a daughter.

## Isaiah 22:5

The Lord GOD of Hosts has set a day of uproar, trampling, and confusion in the Valley of Vision. 📖

Walls are broken down.

Cries go out to the mountains.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 22:6

Elam takes up a case of arrows, with chariots and horsemen. 📖

Kir uncovers the shield.

## Isaiah 22:7

Your finest valleys are full of chariots. 📖

Horsemen are stationed at the gates.

## Isaiah 22:8

He has uncovered Judah’s defenses. 📖

On that day, you looked to the weapons in the House of the Forest.

**About the House of the Forest**

This is the name of the building where the weapons were kept.

## Isaiah 22:9

You saw many gaps in the walls of the City of David. 📖

You gathered water from the lower pool.

## Isaiah 22:10

You counted Jerusalem’s houses. 📖

You tore houses down to strengthen the wall.

## Isaiah 22:11

You built a water storage pool between the walls for water from the old pool. 📖

But you did not look to the One who made it.

You did not consider Him who planned it long ago.

**About the One**

The passage points to God, whose purpose the people ignored.

## Isaiah 22:12

On that day, the Lord GOD of Hosts called for weeping and wailing. 📖

He called for shaved heads and sackcloth.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth.

Wearing it and shaving the head were ways to show grief.

## Isaiah 22:13

But look! 📖

There is joy and gladness.

People butcher cattle and kill sheep.

They eat meat and drink wine.

They say, “Let us eat and drink, because tomorrow we die!”

## Isaiah 22:14

The LORD of Hosts revealed this in my hearing. 📖

“Until your dying day, this sin of yours will never be atoned for,” says the Lord GOD of Hosts.

**Help with atoned for**

This means the sin being dealt with so that guilt is removed.

The warning says this will not happen for this sin during their lives.

## Isaiah 22:15

The Lord GOD of Hosts says, “Go and speak to Shebna, the official in charge of the palace. 📖

## Isaiah 22:16

“What are you doing here? 📖

Who gave you permission to carve yourself a tomb here?

Why chisel your tomb high up and cut your resting place in the rock?

## Isaiah 22:17

“Look, mighty man! 📖

The LORD is about to shake you violently.

He will take hold of you.

## Isaiah 22:18

“He will roll you into a ball and throw you into a wide land. 📖

You will die there, and your glorious chariots will remain there.

You are a disgrace to your master’s house.

## Isaiah 22:19

“I will remove you from your office. 📖

You will be driven from your position.

## Isaiah 22:20

“On that day, I will call My servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah. 📖

## Isaiah 22:21

“I will dress him in your robe and tie your sash around him. 📖

I will give him your authority.

He will be a father to Jerusalem’s people and Judah’s house.

**Help with a father**

This image describes his responsibility toward the people.

## Isaiah 22:22

“I will place the key to David’s house on his shoulder. 📖

No one can shut what he opens.

No one can open what he shuts.

**Help with the key**

The key pictures authority over the royal household.

## Isaiah 22:23

“I will drive him like a peg into a firm place. 📖

He will be a throne of glory for his father’s house.

## Isaiah 22:24

“They will hang all the glory of his father’s house on him. 📖

This includes the descendants and branches of the family.

They will be all the smaller containers, from bowls to every kind of jar.

**Help with the peg and containers**

The picture describes a family depending on one person.

## Isaiah 22:25

“On that day,” declares the LORD of Hosts, “the peg driven into a firm place will give way. 📖

It will be cut off and fall.

The load on it will be cut down.”

The LORD has indeed spoken.
