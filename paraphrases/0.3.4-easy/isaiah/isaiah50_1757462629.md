# Isaiah 50 — The Servant listens and trusts

**About this chapter**

The LORD challenges His people.

The Servant describes listening, suffering, and trusting the Lord GOD.

## Isaiah 50:1

The LORD says, “Where is your mother’s divorce document with which I sent her away? 📖

To which of My creditors did I sell you?

Look!

You were sold because of your wrongdoing.

Your mother was sent away because of your offenses.

**Help with these images**

The mother pictures the people’s community.

A creditor is someone owed a payment.

The questions challenge the people’s understanding of their separation from God.

## Isaiah 50:2

“Why was no one there when I arrived? 📖

Why did no one answer when I called?

Is My hand too short to redeem you?

Do I lack strength to rescue you?

Look!

My rebuke dries up the sea.

I turn rivers into desert.

Fish rot for lack of water and die of thirst.

## Isaiah 50:3

“I dress the heavens in black and cover them with sackcloth.” 📖

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Isaiah 50:4

The Lord GOD has given Me the speech of a disciple to strengthen tired people with a word. 📖

Morning by morning, He wakes Me.

He wakes My ear to listen as a disciple.

**About the voice**

The Servant speaks of being taught by the Lord GOD.

A disciple is a learner or follower.

## Isaiah 50:5

The Lord GOD has opened My ears. 📖

I have not rebelled or turned back.

## Isaiah 50:6

I offered My back to those who hit Me and My cheeks to those who pulled out My beard. 📖

I did not hide My face from insults and spit.

## Isaiah 50:7

The Lord GOD helps Me, so I have not been disgraced. 📖

I have set My face like flint.

I know I will not be ashamed.

**Help with flint**

Flint is a hard stone.

The image describes firm determination.

## Isaiah 50:8

The One who shows I am right is near. 📖

Who dares oppose Me?

Let us face each other!

Who has a case against Me?

Let him approach Me!

## Isaiah 50:9

The Lord GOD surely helps Me. 📖

Who can condemn Me?

Look!

They will all wear out like clothing.

Moths will eat them.

## Isaiah 50:10

Who among you fears the LORD and obeys His Servant’s voice? 📖

Who walks in darkness without light?

Let him trust in the LORD’s name and rely on his God.

## Isaiah 50:11

Look, all you who light a fire and surround yourselves with burning torches! 📖

Walk in your fire’s light and the torches you lit!

This is what you will receive from My hand.

You will lie down in a place of torment.

**About the warning**

The fire image contrasts with trusting God in verse 10.

The ending is a warning, not a promise of safety.
