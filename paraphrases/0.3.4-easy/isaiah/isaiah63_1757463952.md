# Isaiah 63 — Judgment, remembered mercy, and prayer

**About this chapter**

A figure in bloodstained clothes describes judgment on the nations.

The chapter then recalls the LORD’s mercy and turns to prayer.

## Isaiah 63:1

Who comes from Edom, from Bozrah, with clothes stained crimson? 📖

Who wears splendid robes and marches in great strength?

“It is I, announcing vindication, mighty to save.”

**Help with crimson and vindication**

Crimson is deep red.

Vindication means being shown to be right.

## Isaiah 63:2

Why are Your clothes red, like those of someone treading grapes in a winepress? 📖

**Help with winepress**

A winepress is a place where grapes are crushed to make wine.

## Isaiah 63:3

“I have trodden the winepress alone. 📖

No one from the nations was with Me.

I trampled them in My anger and trod them down in My fury.

Their blood splashed My garments and stained all My clothes.

## Isaiah 63:4

“The day of vengeance was in My heart, and the year of My redemption had come. 📖

**Help with redemption**

Redemption means rescue or release.

## Isaiah 63:5

“I looked, but no one helped. 📖

I was horrified that no one assisted.

So My own arm brought Me salvation, and My own anger supported Me.

## Isaiah 63:6

“I trampled the nations in My anger. 📖

In My fury, I made them drunk and poured their blood on the ground.”

**About the image**

The passage describes fierce judgment through trampling grapes and spilling blood.

Its violence has not been removed.

## Isaiah 63:7

I will tell of the LORD’s devoted love and deeds worthy of praise. 📖

He has done so much for us.

He has done many good things for Israel’s house according to His great compassion and devoted love.

## Isaiah 63:8

He said, “They are surely My people, children who will not be disloyal.” 📖

So He became their Savior.

## Isaiah 63:9

In all their distress, He also suffered, and the Angel of His Presence saved them. 📖

In love and compassion, He redeemed them.

He lifted and carried them through all the days of old.

**Help with redeemed**

Redeemed means rescued or set free.

The title Angel of His Presence is retained without adding an identity.

## Isaiah 63:10

But they rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit. 📖

So He turned and became their enemy.

He Himself fought against them.

## Isaiah 63:11

Then His people remembered the old days, the days of Moses. 📖

Where is He who brought them through the sea with the shepherds of His flock?

Where is the One who placed His Holy Spirit among them,

## Isaiah 63:12

who sent His glorious arm to lead them by Moses’ right hand? 📖

Who divided the waters before them to gain an everlasting name for Himself,

## Isaiah 63:13

and led them through the depths like a horse in the wilderness, so that they did not stumble? 📖

## Isaiah 63:14

Like cattle going down to a valley, they received rest from the LORD’s Spirit. 📖

You led Your people this way to make a glorious name for Yourself.

## Isaiah 63:15

Look down from heaven and see, from Your holy, glorious home. 📖

Where are Your determination and power?

Your deep concern and compassion for me are held back.

## Isaiah 63:16

Yet You are our Father, though Abraham does not know us and Israel does not acknowledge us. 📖

LORD, You are our Father.

Your name is our Redeemer from Everlasting.

## Isaiah 63:17

LORD, why do You make us stray from Your ways? 📖

Why do You harden our hearts against fearing You?

Return for Your servants’ sake, the tribes of Your inheritance.

**About this difficult prayer**

The speaker asks God about causing straying and hardening.

The question is retained rather than changed into a statement about human choice alone.

## Isaiah 63:18

Your people possessed Your holy place for a short time. 📖

But our enemies trampled Your holy place.

## Isaiah 63:19

We have become like people You never ruled, like people not called by Your name. 📖
