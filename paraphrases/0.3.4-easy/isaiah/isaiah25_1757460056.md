# Isaiah 25 — God removes death and disgrace

**About this chapter**

The chapter praises God for His faithful plans and protection.

It describes a feast, the end of death, and Moab’s defeat.

## Isaiah 25:1

LORD, You are my God! 📖

I will lift You high and praise Your name.

You have done wonderful things.

With perfect faithfulness, You have carried out plans made long ago.

## Isaiah 25:2

You have made the city a pile of rubble and the strong town a ruin. 📖

The foreigners’ fortress is no longer a city.

It will never be rebuilt.

## Isaiah 25:3

So a strong people will honor You. 📖

The cities of cruel nations will deeply respect You.

## Isaiah 25:4

You have been a place of safety for poor people. 📖

You have been a strong shelter for needy people in distress.

You have been shelter from the storm and shade from the heat.

The breath of cruel people is like rain beating against a wall,

## Isaiah 25:5

like heat in a dry land. 📖

You quiet the uproar of foreigners.

A cloud’s shade cools the heat.

In the same way, the song of cruel people is silenced.

## Isaiah 25:6

On this mountain, the LORD of Hosts will prepare a rich feast for all peoples. 📖

There will be choice meat and fine wine that has been aged well.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 25:7

On this mountain, He will swallow the burial covering that wraps all peoples. 📖

He will swallow the sheet that covers all nations.

## Isaiah 25:8

He will swallow death forever. 📖

The Lord GOD will wipe tears from every face.

He will remove His people’s disgrace from the whole earth.

The LORD has spoken.

**Help with swallowing death**

This image describes the complete defeat of death.

## Isaiah 25:9

On that day, people will say, “This is our God! 📖

We waited for Him, and He has saved us.

This is the LORD we waited for.

Let us rejoice and be glad because He has saved us.”

## Isaiah 25:10

The LORD’s hand will rest on this mountain. 📖

But Moab will be trampled in his place, like straw trampled into a pile of dung.

## Isaiah 25:11

Moab will spread his hands in it, like a swimmer spreading his arms to swim. 📖

His pride will be brought low, despite the skill of his hands.

## Isaiah 25:12

The fortress with high walls will be brought down. 📖

It will be thrown to the ground, into the dust.
