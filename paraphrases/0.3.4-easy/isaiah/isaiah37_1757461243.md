# Isaiah 37 — Hezekiah prays and Jerusalem is saved

**About this chapter**

Hezekiah asks for help when Assyria threatens Jerusalem.

The chapter distinguishes the king’s threats, Hezekiah’s prayer, the LORD’s answer, and the narrator’s report.

## Isaiah 37:1

When King Hezekiah heard the report, he tore his clothes and put on sackcloth. 📖

He entered the LORD’s house.

**Help with sackcloth**

Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief or distress.

## Isaiah 37:2

He sent Eliakim the palace administrator, Shebna the scribe, and the leading priests to the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz. 📖

They all wore sackcloth.

## Isaiah 37:3

They told Isaiah, “Hezekiah says, ‘Today is a day of distress, rebuke, and disgrace. 📖

Children have reached the point of birth, but there is no strength to deliver them.

**Help with the birth image**

Hezekiah pictures a crisis that his people have no strength to overcome.

## Isaiah 37:4

“‘Perhaps the LORD your God will hear the Rabshakeh’s words. 📖

His master, Assyria’s king, sent him to insult the living God.

Perhaps God will rebuke him for the words the LORD your God heard.

So pray for the remaining people who still survive.’”

## Isaiah 37:5

King Hezekiah’s servants went to Isaiah. 📖

## Isaiah 37:6

Isaiah replied, “Tell your master what the LORD says. 📖

‘Do not fear the words you heard.

The servants of Assyria’s king have spoken those words against Me.

## Isaiah 37:7

“‘Look! 📖

I will put a spirit in him so that he hears a rumor and returns to his own land.

There I will cause him to die by the sword.’”

## Isaiah 37:8

The Rabshakeh heard that Assyria’s king had left Lachish. 📖

He withdrew and found the king fighting Libnah.

## Isaiah 37:9

Sennacherib received a warning about Tirhakah king of Cush. 📖

“He has set out to fight you.”

When Sennacherib heard this, he sent messengers to Hezekiah with these words.

## Isaiah 37:10

“Give Hezekiah king of Judah this message. 📖

‘Do not let the God you trust deceive you.

He says Jerusalem will not be handed over to Assyria’s king.

## Isaiah 37:11

“‘You have surely heard what Assyria’s kings did to all the other countries. 📖

They gave them over to destruction.

Will you be spared?

## Isaiah 37:12

“‘Did the gods of the nations my fathers destroyed rescue those nations? 📖

What about Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Telassar?

## Isaiah 37:13

“‘Where are the kings of Hamath, Arpad, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’” 📖

## Isaiah 37:14

Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers and read it. 📖

He went up to the LORD’s house and spread the letter before the LORD.

## Isaiah 37:15

Hezekiah prayed to the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 37:16

“LORD of Hosts, God of Israel, You sit on Your throne above the cherubim. 📖

You alone are God over all the earth’s kingdoms.

You made the heavens and the earth.

**Help with these words**

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

Cherubim are heavenly beings.

## Isaiah 37:17

“LORD, turn Your ear and hear. 📖

LORD, open Your eyes and see.

Hear all the words Sennacherib sent to insult the living God.

## Isaiah 37:18

“LORD, Assyria’s kings have truly ruined all these countries and their lands. 📖

## Isaiah 37:19

“They threw those nations’ gods into the fire and destroyed them. 📖

Those were not gods, but only wood and stone made by human hands.

## Isaiah 37:20

“Now, LORD our God, save us from his hand. 📖

Then all the earth’s kingdoms may know that You alone, LORD, are God.”

## Isaiah 37:21

Isaiah son of Amoz sent Hezekiah a message. 📖

“The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘You have prayed to Me about Sennacherib king of Assyria.

## Isaiah 37:22

“‘So this is the LORD’s message against him. 📖

The Virgin Daughter of Zion despises and mocks you.

The Daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head behind you.

**Help with these daughters**

The city and its people are pictured as a daughter mocking the enemy king.

## Isaiah 37:23

“‘Whom have you mocked and insulted? 📖

Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes proudly?

Against the Holy One of Israel!

## Isaiah 37:24

“‘Through your servants, you have mocked the Lord. 📖

You said, “With my many chariots, I climbed the mountain heights, to Lebanon’s remote peaks.

I cut down its tallest cedars and finest cypresses.

I reached its farthest heights and thickest forests.

## Isaiah 37:25

“‘“I dug wells and drank foreign waters. 📖

With the soles of my feet, I dried up all Egypt’s streams.”

## Isaiah 37:26

“‘Have you not heard? 📖

Long ago, I ordered this.

In ancient days, I planned it.

Now I have made it happen, that you should crush fortified cities into piles of rubble.

## Isaiah 37:27

“‘So their people have no power. 📖

They are distressed and ashamed.

They are like field plants and tender green shoots.

They are like grass on rooftops, scorched before it grows.

## Isaiah 37:28

“‘But I know when you sit down, go out, and come in. 📖

I know your rage against Me.

## Isaiah 37:29

“‘Your rage and pride against Me have reached My ears. 📖

So I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your mouth.

I will send you back the way you came.’

**Help with hook and bit**

These objects picture God forcing the proud king to return.

## Isaiah 37:30

“Hezekiah, this will be a sign for you. 📖

This year, you will eat what grows by itself.

In the second year, you will eat what grows from that.

But in the third year, you will sow and harvest, plant vineyards, and eat their fruit.

## Isaiah 37:31

“The survivors of Judah’s house will take root below and bear fruit above again. 📖

**Help with roots and fruit**

The plant image describes the surviving people growing strong and fruitful again.

## Isaiah 37:32

“A remaining group will come from Jerusalem, and survivors from Mount Zion. 📖

The great determination of the LORD of Hosts will do this.

## Isaiah 37:33

“The LORD says this about Assyria’s king. 📖

‘He will not enter this city or shoot an arrow into it.

He will not approach it with a shield or build a ramp to attack it.

## Isaiah 37:34

“‘He will return the way he came. 📖

He will not enter this city,’ declares the LORD.

## Isaiah 37:35

“‘I will defend this city and save it for My own sake and for My servant David’s sake.’” 📖

## Isaiah 37:36

Then the LORD’s angel went out and killed 185,000 men in the Assyrian camp. 📖

When people got up the next morning, all the dead bodies were there!

## Isaiah 37:37

Sennacherib king of Assyria broke camp and withdrew. 📖

He returned to Nineveh and stayed there.

## Isaiah 37:38

One day, he worshiped in the temple of his god Nisroch. 📖

His sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with the sword and escaped to Ararat.

His son Esar-haddon became king in his place.
