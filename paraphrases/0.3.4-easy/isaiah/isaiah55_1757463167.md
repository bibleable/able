# Isaiah 55 — Come, listen, and seek the LORD

**About this chapter**

The LORD invites thirsty people to receive what they cannot buy.

He calls people to return to Him and promises that His word will accomplish His purpose.

## Isaiah 55:1

“Come, all who are thirsty, come to the waters! 📖

You who have no money, come, buy and eat!

Come, buy wine and milk without money and without cost!

## Isaiah 55:2

“Why spend money on what is not bread? 📖

Why spend your work on what does not satisfy?

Listen carefully to Me and eat what is good.

Your soul will delight in the richest food.

## Isaiah 55:3

“Turn your ear and come to Me. 📖

Listen so that your soul may live.

I will make an everlasting covenant with you, My devoted love promised to David.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Isaiah 55:4

“Look! 📖

I have made him a witness to the nations, a leader and commander of the peoples.

## Isaiah 55:5

“You will call a nation you do not know. 📖

Nations that do not know you will run to you.

The LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, has given you glory.”

## Isaiah 55:6

Seek the LORD while He may be found. 📖

Call on Him while He is near.

## Isaiah 55:7

Let the wicked man leave his way and the unrighteous man leave his thoughts. 📖

Let him return to the LORD, who may have compassion.

Let him return to our God, who will freely forgive.

## Isaiah 55:8

The LORD declares, “My thoughts are not your thoughts, and your ways are not My ways. 📖

## Isaiah 55:9

“The heavens are higher than the earth. 📖

In the same way, My ways are higher than your ways, and My thoughts higher than your thoughts.

## Isaiah 55:10

“Rain and snow fall from heaven. 📖

They do not return without watering the earth and making it bud and sprout.

They provide seed to sow and food to eat.

## Isaiah 55:11

“In the same way, the word from My mouth will not return to Me empty. 📖

It will do what I please.

It will succeed where I send it.

## Isaiah 55:12

“You will go out with joy and be led in peace. 📖

Mountains and hills will burst into song before you.

All the trees of the field will clap their hands.

## Isaiah 55:13

“Cypress will grow instead of thornbushes, and myrtle will grow instead of briers. 📖

This will make a name for the LORD, an everlasting sign that will never be destroyed.”

**Help with the images**

The singing hills, clapping trees, and changed plants picture joy and restoration.
