# Isaiah 54 — Lasting compassion for Zion

**About this chapter**

Zion is pictured as a childless woman, a deserted wife, and a city rebuilt in beauty.

The LORD promises lasting compassion and peace.

## Isaiah 54:1

The LORD says, “Childless woman who bears no children, shout for joy! 📖

You who have never given birth, burst into song and cry aloud!

The deserted woman has more children than the woman with a husband.

**About the woman**

The passage pictures Zion and its people as a woman.

It is not a promise that every childless reader will have children.

## Isaiah 54:2

“Make the place for your tent larger. 📖

Stretch out your home’s curtains without holding back.

Lengthen the ropes and drive the stakes deep.

## Isaiah 54:3

“You will spread to the right and left. 📖

Your descendants will drive nations from their land and live in deserted cities.

## Isaiah 54:4

“Do not fear, because you will not be ashamed. 📖

Do not be frightened, because you will not be humiliated.

You will forget your youth’s shame and no longer remember your widowhood’s disgrace.

## Isaiah 54:5

“Your Maker is your husband. 📖

His name is the LORD of Hosts.

The Holy One of Israel is your Redeemer.

He is called the God of all the earth.

**Help with these titles**

LORD of Hosts describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

A Redeemer rescues or sets people free.

## Isaiah 54:6

“The LORD has called you back like a deserted wife wounded in spirit. 📖

You are like a rejected wife married in youth,” says your God.

## Isaiah 54:7

“I abandoned you for a brief moment. 📖

But with great compassion, I will bring you back.

## Isaiah 54:8

“In a surge of anger, I hid My face from you for a moment. 📖

But with everlasting kindness, I will have compassion on you,” says the LORD your Redeemer.

## Isaiah 54:9

“To Me, this is like Noah’s days. 📖

I swore that Noah’s waters would never cover the earth again.

So I have sworn not to be angry with you or rebuke you.

## Isaiah 54:10

“Mountains may be removed, and hills may shake. 📖

But My devoted love will not leave you, and My covenant of peace will not be broken.

This is what the LORD who has compassion on you says.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Isaiah 54:11

“Suffering city, beaten by storms and without comfort, I will set your stones in antimony. 📖

I will lay your foundations with sapphires.

**Help with these materials**

Antimony is a dark mineral material.

Sapphires are precious stones.

The picture describes a city rebuilt with beauty.

## Isaiah 54:12

“I will make your high points from rubies, your gates from sparkling jewels, and all your walls from precious stones. 📖

## Isaiah 54:13

“All your sons will be taught by the LORD. 📖

Their prosperity will be great.

## Isaiah 54:14

“You will be established in righteousness, far from oppression, because you will not fear. 📖

Terror will be far away and will not come near you.

## Isaiah 54:15

“If anyone attacks you, the attack does not come from Me. 📖

Whoever attacks you will fall before you.

## Isaiah 54:16

“Look! 📖

I created the craftsman who fans coals into flame and shapes a weapon suited to its task.

I also created the destroyer to cause destruction.

## Isaiah 54:17

“No weapon made against you will succeed. 📖

You will answer and disprove every accusing tongue.

This is the inheritance of the LORD’s servants.

Their vindication comes from Me,” declares the LORD.

**Help with vindication**

Vindication means being shown to be right.
