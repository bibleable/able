# Isaiah 29 — Ariel and the sealed scroll

**About this chapter**

The LORD warns Ariel, the city where David camped.

The chapter describes siege, failure to understand, and coming restoration.

## Isaiah 29:1

How terrible for you, Ariel, city of Ariel where David camped! 📖

Let your festivals come again, year after year.

**Help with Ariel**

Ariel is a name used here for Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 29:2

God says, “I will press Ariel hard. 📖

There will be mourning and crying.

It will be like an altar’s burning hearth before Me.

## Isaiah 29:3

“I will camp in a circle around you. 📖

I will surround you with towers and build structures to attack you.

## Isaiah 29:4

“You will be brought low and speak from the ground. 📖

Your words will be hard to hear from the dust.

Your voice will be like a spirit from the ground.

Your speech will whisper from the dust.”

## Isaiah 29:5

But your many enemies will become like fine dust. 📖

The many cruel people will become like chaff blowing away.

Then, suddenly, in an instant,

**Help with chaff**

Chaff is the light covering removed from grain.

## Isaiah 29:6

the LORD of Hosts will visit you with thunder, earthquake, and loud noise. 📖

He will come with violent wind, storm, and fire that burns things up.

**Help with LORD of Hosts**

This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Isaiah 29:7

All the many nations fighting Ariel will become like a dream, like a vision at night. 📖

This includes everyone who surrounds it and attacks it.

## Isaiah 29:8

A hungry man dreams of eating but wakes up hungry. 📖

A thirsty man dreams of drinking but wakes up weak and thirsty.

That is how it will be for all the nations fighting Mount Zion.

## Isaiah 29:9

Stop and be amazed! 📖

Blind yourselves and be unable to see.

Be drunk, but not from wine.

Stagger, but not from strong drink.

## Isaiah 29:10

The LORD has poured a spirit of deep sleep over you. 📖

Prophets, He has shut your eyes.

Seers, He has covered your heads.

**Help with these images**

The passage uses sight, sleep, and drunkenness to describe people failing to understand.

It is not a command for readers to harm their eyes.

Seers are people who receive visions.

## Isaiah 29:11

The whole vision will be like words in a sealed scroll to you. 📖

If someone is asked to read it, he will say, “I cannot, because it is sealed.”

## Isaiah 29:12

If the scroll is given to someone who cannot read, he will say, “I cannot read.” 📖

## Isaiah 29:13

The Lord said, “These people come near Me with their mouths and honor Me with their lips. 📖

But their hearts are far from Me.

Their worship of Me is only rules taught by people.

## Isaiah 29:14

“So I will again amaze these people with wonder after wonder. 📖

The wisdom of the wise will disappear.

The understanding of those who understand will be hidden.”

## Isaiah 29:15

How terrible for those who dig deep to hide their plans from the LORD! 📖

They do their deeds in darkness.

They say, “Who sees us?

Who will know?”

## Isaiah 29:16

You have turned things upside down, as if a potter were clay. 📖

Can the thing made say about its maker, “He did not make me”?

Can pottery say about the potter, “He has no understanding”?

**Help with a potter**

A potter makes objects from clay.

These questions challenge people who deny their Maker’s authority or understanding.

## Isaiah 29:17

In a very short time, will Lebanon not become an orchard? 📖

Will the orchard not seem like a forest?

## Isaiah 29:18

On that day, deaf people will hear the scroll’s words. 📖

Blind people’s eyes will see out of deep darkness.

## Isaiah 29:19

Humble people will have more and more joy in the LORD. 📖

Poor people will rejoice in the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 29:20

Cruel people will disappear. 📖

Mockers will be gone.

All who look for evil will be cut down.

## Isaiah 29:21

They accuse someone with a word. 📖

They trap the person who settles disputes at the gate.

With false charges, they deny justice to innocent people.

## Isaiah 29:22

The LORD who rescued Abraham says about Jacob’s house, “Jacob will no longer be ashamed. 📖

His face will no longer grow pale.

## Isaiah 29:23

“He will see his children around him, the work of My hands. 📖

They will honor My name.

They will honor the Holy One of Jacob as holy.

They will stand in awe of the God of Israel.

## Isaiah 29:24

“Then those who have gone wrong in spirit will understand. 📖

Those who complain will accept teaching.”
