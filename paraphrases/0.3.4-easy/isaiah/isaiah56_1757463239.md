# Isaiah 56 — A house of prayer for all nations

**About this chapter**

The LORD welcomes faithful foreigners and eunuchs.

He also challenges Israel’s careless, selfish leaders.

## Isaiah 56:1

The LORD says, “Maintain justice and do what is right. 📖

My salvation is coming soon, and My righteousness will be revealed.

**Help with salvation and righteousness**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

Righteousness means what is right before God.

## Isaiah 56:2

“Blessed is the person who does this and holds firmly to it. 📖

He keeps the Sabbath without making it unholy and keeps his hand from doing any evil.”

**Help with Sabbath**

The Sabbath is the weekly day set apart for rest and worship.

## Isaiah 56:3

Let no foreigner who has joined the LORD say, “The LORD will completely exclude me from His people.” 📖

Let no eunuch say, “I am only a dry tree.”

**Help with eunuch and dry tree**

Eunuchs were men often unable to have children because their testicles had been removed.

The dry tree pictures being unable to have children.

The passage rejects the idea that this makes them without a place among God’s people.

## Isaiah 56:4

The LORD says, “Some eunuchs keep My Sabbaths, choose what pleases Me, and hold firmly to My covenant. 📖

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Isaiah 56:5

“To them, I will give a memorial and a name in My house and within My walls. 📖

This memorial and name will be better than that of sons and daughters.

I will give them an everlasting name that will not be cut off.

## Isaiah 56:6

“Foreigners join themselves to the LORD to serve Him and love His name as His servants. 📖

They keep the Sabbath without making it unholy and hold firmly to My covenant.

## Isaiah 56:7

“I will bring them to My holy mountain and give them joy in My house of prayer. 📖

Their burnt offerings and sacrifices will be accepted on My altar.

My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations.”

**Help with offerings**

Burnt offerings and sacrifices were gifts offered to God in worship.

## Isaiah 56:8

The Lord GOD gathers Israel’s scattered people. 📖

He declares, “I will gather still others to them, besides those already gathered.”

## Isaiah 56:9

Come, all animals of the field! 📖

All animals of the forest, eat greedily!

## Isaiah 56:10

Israel’s watchmen are blind and know nothing. 📖

They are all silent dogs that cannot bark.

They dream and lie around, loving sleep.

**Help with watchmen and dogs**

Watchmen should warn people about danger.

The images criticize leaders who fail to notice danger or warn their people.

Physical blindness is not itself a moral fault.

## Isaiah 56:11

They are like hungry dogs that are never satisfied. 📖

They are shepherds without understanding.

Each turns to his own way and seeks his own gain.

## Isaiah 56:12

They say, “Come, let me get wine! 📖

Let us drink strong drink.

Tomorrow will be like today, only much better!”
