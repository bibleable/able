# Isaiah 15 — Grief over Moab

**About this chapter**

The message describes destruction, mourning, and people fleeing from Moab.

## Isaiah 15:1

This is the message against Moab. 📖

Ar in Moab is ruined, destroyed in a night!

Kir in Moab is devastated, destroyed in a night!

## Isaiah 15:2

Dibon goes up to its temple to weep at its high places. 📖

Moab cries out over Nebo and Medeba.

Every head is shaved, and every beard is cut off.

**Help with mourning customs**

Cutting hair and wearing rough sackcloth were ways people showed grief.

High places were places of worship.

## Isaiah 15:3

People wear sackcloth in the streets. 📖

On rooftops and in public squares, everyone cries out.

They fall down weeping.

## Isaiah 15:4

Heshbon and Elealeh cry out. 📖

Their voices are heard as far as Jahaz.

So Moab’s soldiers cry out.

They tremble deep inside.

## Isaiah 15:5

My heart cries out over Moab. 📖

Its people flee as far as Zoar and Eglath-shelishiyah.

They go up Luhith’s slope, weeping.

On the road to Horonaim, they mourn their destruction.

**About the voice**

The message expresses personal grief over Moab’s suffering.

## Isaiah 15:6

The waters of Nimrim have dried up. 📖

The grass has withered.

The plants are gone.

Nothing green remains.

## Isaiah 15:7

So they carry their wealth and belongings across the Brook of the Willows. 📖

## Isaiah 15:8

Their cries echo as far as Moab’s border. 📖

Their wailing reaches Eglaim and is heard in Beer-elim.

## Isaiah 15:9

The waters of Dimon are full of blood. 📖

But God says, “I will bring still more against Dimon.

I will bring a lion against Moab’s fleeing people and those left in the land.”
