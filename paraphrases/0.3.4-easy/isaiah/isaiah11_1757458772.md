# Isaiah 11 — The Branch and the returning people

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes a ruler from Jesse’s line, peace, and the gathering of God’s scattered people.

## Isaiah 11:1

A shoot will grow from Jesse’s stump. 📖

A Branch from his roots will bear fruit.

**Help with the Branch**

The plant image introduces a ruler from Jesse’s family.

## Isaiah 11:2

The Spirit of the LORD will rest on Him. 📖

This is the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, of guidance and strength.

This is the Spirit of knowledge and fear of the LORD.

**Help with fear of the LORD**

This phrase includes deep respect for God and His authority.

## Isaiah 11:3

He will delight in the fear of the LORD. 📖

He will not judge by what His eyes see.

He will not decide by what His ears hear.

## Isaiah 11:4

He will judge poor people with righteousness. 📖

He will decide fairly for the lowly people of the earth.

He will strike the earth with the rod of His mouth.

He will kill the wicked with the breath of His lips.

**Help with the images**

The mouth and breath images describe the power of His words to carry out judgment.

## Isaiah 11:5

Righteousness will be the belt around His hips. 📖

Faithfulness will be the sash around His waist.

**Help with the clothing**

These images describe a ruler whose actions are right and faithful.

## Isaiah 11:6

The wolf will live with the lamb. 📖

The leopard will lie down with the goat.

The calf, young lion, and well-fed animal will be together.

A little child will lead them.

## Isaiah 11:7

The cow will graze with the bear. 📖

Their young will lie down together.

The lion will eat straw like the ox.

## Isaiah 11:8

A baby will play by the cobra’s den. 📖

A young child will reach into the viper’s nest.

**About these animals**

Cobras and vipers are dangerous snakes.

This passage describes promised peace.

It is not an instruction for children to approach dangerous animals.

## Isaiah 11:9

They will not harm or destroy anywhere on My holy mountain. 📖

The earth will be full of the knowledge of the LORD, as the sea is full of water.

**About the speaker**

“My holy mountain” identifies the mountain as God’s.

## Isaiah 11:10

On that day, the Root of Jesse will stand like a banner for the peoples. 📖

The nations will seek Him.

His resting place will be glorious.

**Help with Root and banner**

The Root continues the family image from verse 1.

A banner is a raised sign that people can gather around.

## Isaiah 11:11

On that day, the Lord will reach out His hand a second time to recover His remaining people. 📖

They will come from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the islands of the sea.

## Isaiah 11:12

He will raise a banner for the nations and gather Israel’s people who were driven away. 📖

He will gather Judah’s scattered people from the four corners of the earth.

**Help with four corners**

This image means every direction.

## Isaiah 11:13

Ephraim’s jealousy will go away. 📖

Judah’s enemies will be cut off.

Ephraim will no longer envy Judah.

Judah will no longer trouble Ephraim.

## Isaiah 11:14

They will swoop down on the Philistines’ slopes to the west. 📖

Together they will take goods from the peoples of the east.

They will lay their hands on Edom and Moab.

The Ammonites will be under their rule.

## Isaiah 11:15

The LORD will give the gulf of the Sea of Egypt over to destruction. 📖

With a scorching wind, He will sweep His hand over the Euphrates.

He will divide it into seven streams.

People will cross with dry sandals.

## Isaiah 11:16

There will be a highway for His remaining people who are left in Assyria. 📖

It will be like Israel’s road when they came up from Egypt.
