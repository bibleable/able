# Isaiah 60 — Light and restoration for Zion

**About this chapter**

Zion receives light, returning children, and the wealth of nations.

The promises include worship, safety, and the LORD as lasting light.

## Isaiah 60:1

Rise and shine, because your light has come. 📖

The LORD’s glory rises on you.

## Isaiah 60:2

Look! 📖

Darkness covers the earth, and thick darkness covers the peoples.

But the LORD will rise on you, and His glory will appear over you.

## Isaiah 60:3

Nations will come to your light, and kings to the brightness of your dawn. 📖

## Isaiah 60:4

Lift your eyes and look around. 📖

They all gather and come to you.

Your sons will come from far away, and your daughters will be carried on the arm.

## Isaiah 60:5

Then you will look and shine with joy. 📖

Your heart will tremble and grow full of joy.

The sea’s riches will be brought to you, and the nations’ wealth will come to you.

## Isaiah 60:6

Traveling groups of camels will cover your land, young camels from Midian and Ephah. 📖

All from Sheba will come with gold and frankincense, announcing the LORD’s praises.

**Help with frankincense**

Frankincense is a substance burned to give a sweet smell.

## Isaiah 60:7

All Kedar’s flocks will gather to you, and Nebaioth’s rams will serve you. 📖

They will be accepted on My altar.

I will beautify My glorious house.

**About the speaker**

“My altar” and “My house” identify the LORD as speaking.

## Isaiah 60:8

Who are these flying like clouds, like doves to their shelters? 📖

## Isaiah 60:9

The islands will surely wait for Me, with Tarshish’s ships in the lead. 📖

They will bring your children from far away, with their silver and gold.

This honors the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, because He has glorified you.

## Isaiah 60:10

Foreigners will rebuild your walls, and their kings will serve you. 📖

I struck you in anger, but in favor I will show you mercy.

## Isaiah 60:11

Your gates will always be open, never shut by day or night. 📖

The nations’ wealth will be brought in, with their kings led in a procession.

## Isaiah 60:12

The nation or kingdom refusing to serve you will die. 📖

It will be completely destroyed.

## Isaiah 60:13

Lebanon’s glory will come to you, its cypress, elm, and boxwood together. 📖

They will beautify My holy place.

I will make the place of My feet glorious.

**Help with these images**

The tree names describe valuable timber.

The place of God’s feet pictures His presence in His holy place.

## Isaiah 60:14

Your oppressors’ sons will come and bow to you. 📖

All who insulted you will fall facedown at your feet.

They will call you the City of the LORD, Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 60:15

You have been abandoned and despised, with no one passing through. 📖

But I will make you an everlasting source of pride and a joy from age to age.

## Isaiah 60:16

You will drink the nations’ milk and nurse at royalty’s breasts. 📖

You will know that I, the LORD, am your Savior and Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.

**Help with milk and nursing**

These images picture the nations providing support for Zion.

A Redeemer rescues or sets people free.

## Isaiah 60:17

I will bring gold instead of bronze, silver instead of iron, bronze instead of wood, and iron instead of stones. 📖

I will appoint peace as your governor and righteousness as your ruler.

## Isaiah 60:18

Violence will no longer be heard in your land, nor ruin or destruction within your borders. 📖

You will call your walls Salvation and your gates Praise.

**Help with Salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 60:19

The sun will no longer be your light by day. 📖

The moon’s brightness will no longer light your night.

The LORD will be your everlasting light, and your God will be your splendor.

## Isaiah 60:20

Your sun will no longer set, and your moon will not grow dim. 📖

The LORD will be your everlasting light.

Your days of sorrow will end.

## Isaiah 60:21

All your people will be righteous and possess the land forever. 📖

They are the branch I planted, the work of My hands, so that I may be glorified.

## Isaiah 60:22

The least of you will become a thousand, and the smallest a mighty nation. 📖

I am the LORD.

At its time, I will do this quickly.
