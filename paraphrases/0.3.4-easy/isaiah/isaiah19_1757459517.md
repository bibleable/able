# Isaiah 19 — Judgment and healing for Egypt

**About this chapter**

Egypt faces conflict, failed crops, and confused leaders.

The chapter also promises healing and shared worship with Assyria and Israel.

## Isaiah 19:1

This is the message against Egypt. 📖

Look!

The LORD rides on a swift cloud and comes to Egypt.

Egypt’s idols will tremble before Him.

The Egyptians’ hearts will melt within them.

**Help with melting hearts**

This image describes people losing courage.

## Isaiah 19:2

God says, “I will stir up Egyptians against Egyptians. 📖

Brothers will fight brothers, neighbors will fight neighbors, cities will fight cities, and kingdoms will fight kingdoms.

## Isaiah 19:3

“The Egyptians’ spirit will drain away. 📖

I will defeat their plans.

They will turn to idols and spirits of the dead, to mediums and spiritists.

**Help with mediums and spiritists**

These are people who claim to contact spirits or the dead.

## Isaiah 19:4

“I will hand the Egyptians over to a harsh master. 📖

A fierce king will rule them,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.

## Isaiah 19:5

The Nile’s waters will dry up. 📖

The riverbed will be dry and empty.

## Isaiah 19:6

The canals will stink. 📖

Egypt’s streams will become a trickle and dry up.

The reeds and rushes will wither.

## Isaiah 19:7

The bulrushes beside the Nile and its mouth will wither. 📖

All the fields planted along the Nile will wither, blow away, and be gone.

**Help with reeds, rushes, and bulrushes**

These are plants that grow near water.

## Isaiah 19:8

The fishermen will mourn. 📖

All who put a hook into the Nile will grieve.

Those who spread nets on the water will waste away.

## Isaiah 19:9

The workers who prepare flax will be distressed. 📖

Those who weave fine linen will turn pale.

**Help with flax and linen**

Flax is a plant whose fibers are used to make linen cloth.

## Isaiah 19:10

The cloth workers will be deeply discouraged. 📖

All the hired workers will be sick at heart.

**Help with sick at heart**

This means deeply troubled or sad.

## Isaiah 19:11

The princes of Zoan are only fools. 📖

Pharaoh’s wise advisers give advice that makes no sense.

How can you tell Pharaoh, “I am one of the wise, a son of eastern kings”?

**Help with Pharaoh**

Pharaoh is a title for Egypt’s king.

## Isaiah 19:12

Where are your wise men now? 📖

Let them tell you what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt.

Let them reveal it.

## Isaiah 19:13

The princes of Zoan have become fools. 📖

The princes of Memphis are deceived.

The leaders who support Egypt’s tribes have led Egypt the wrong way.

**About the leaders**

The source calls them “cornerstones,” an image of people who support and guide others.

## Isaiah 19:14

The LORD has poured a spirit of confusion into Egypt. 📖

Egypt is led the wrong way in everything it does.

It is like a drunk person stumbling through his own vomit.

## Isaiah 19:15

Egypt can do nothing, whether head or tail, palm branch or reed. 📖

**Help with these pairs**

These images include all parts of the people, from high to low.

## Isaiah 19:16

On that day, the Egyptians will be like women. 📖

They will tremble with fear when the LORD of Hosts raises His hand and swings it against them.

**About the comparison**

The source uses a comparison with women to describe fear in this scene.

It does not establish that every woman is fearful.

## Isaiah 19:17

The land of Judah will terrify Egypt. 📖

Whenever Judah is mentioned, Egypt will tremble at what the LORD of Hosts has planned against it.

## Isaiah 19:18

On that day, five cities in Egypt will speak Canaan’s language. 📖

They will swear loyalty to the LORD of Hosts.

One will be called the City of the Sun.

## Isaiah 19:19

On that day, an altar to the LORD will stand in the center of Egypt. 📖

A pillar to the LORD will stand near its border.

## Isaiah 19:20

It will be a sign and witness to the LORD of Hosts in Egypt. 📖

The people will cry to the LORD because of those who oppress them.

He will send them a savior and defender to rescue them.

## Isaiah 19:21

The LORD will make Himself known to Egypt. 📖

On that day, Egypt will acknowledge the LORD.

They will worship Him with sacrifices and offerings.

They will make vows to the LORD and keep them.

**Help with vows**

Vows are serious promises.

## Isaiah 19:22

The LORD will strike Egypt with a plague. 📖

He will strike them, but He will heal them.

They will turn to the LORD.

He will hear their prayers and heal them.

## Isaiah 19:23

On that day, a highway will connect Egypt and Assyria. 📖

Assyrians will go to Egypt, and Egyptians will go to Assyria.

Egyptians and Assyrians will worship together.

## Isaiah 19:24

On that day, Israel will join Egypt and Assyria in an alliance of three. 📖

This will be a blessing on the earth.

## Isaiah 19:25

The LORD of Hosts will bless them. 📖

He will say, “Blessed be Egypt My people, Assyria the work of My hands, and Israel My inheritance.”

**Help with inheritance**

Here the word describes Israel as belonging to God in a special way.
