# Isaiah 59 — Sin, confession, and the Redeemer

**About this chapter**

The people’s wrongdoing is described, followed by a confession.

The LORD acts to bring justice and salvation.

## Isaiah 59:1

The LORD’s arm is surely not too short to save. 📖

His ear is not too dull to hear.

## Isaiah 59:2

But your wrongdoing has built barriers between you and your God. 📖

Your sins have hidden His face from you, so that He does not hear.

## Isaiah 59:3

Your hands are stained with blood and your fingers with wrongdoing. 📖

Your lips speak lies, and your tongue mutters injustice.

## Isaiah 59:4

No one calls for justice or argues his case honestly. 📖

They rely on empty arguments and tell lies.

They become pregnant with trouble and give birth to wrongdoing.

**Help with the birth image**

This pictures plans that produce evil.

## Isaiah 59:5

They hatch vipers’ eggs and weave a spider’s web. 📖

Whoever eats their eggs will die.

Break one open, and a viper hatches.

**Help with vipers**

Vipers are dangerous snakes.

The eggs picture harm produced by wrongdoing.

## Isaiah 59:6

Their spiderwebs cannot become clothes. 📖

They cannot cover themselves with their works.

Their deeds are sinful, and their hands do violence.

## Isaiah 59:7

Their feet run to evil. 📖

They hurry to shed innocent blood.

Their thoughts are sinful, and they leave ruin and destruction behind.

## Isaiah 59:8

They do not know the way of peace. 📖

There is no justice in their tracks.

They have made their paths crooked.

No one walking on them will know peace.

## Isaiah 59:9

So justice is far from us, and righteousness does not reach us. 📖

We hope for light, but there is darkness.

We hope for brightness, but we walk in gloom.

**About the voice**

The words now speak as “we” and confess the people’s condition.

## Isaiah 59:10

Like blind people, we feel our way along the wall, searching like people without eyes. 📖

We stumble at midday as if it were twilight.

Among strong, lively people, we are like the dead.

**Help with the image**

The comparison describes the speakers’ loss of direction.

It does not make physical blindness a moral fault.

## Isaiah 59:11

We all growl like bears and moan like doves. 📖

We hope for justice but find none.

We hope for salvation, but it is far from us.

**Help with salvation**

Salvation means being saved or rescued.

## Isaiah 59:12

Our offenses have multiplied before You. 📖

Our sins testify against us.

Our offenses are with us, and we know our wrongdoing.

## Isaiah 59:13

We rebel and deny the LORD. 📖

We turn away from our God.

We speak oppression and rebellion.

We plan and speak lies from the heart.

## Isaiah 59:14

So justice is turned away, and righteousness stands far off. 📖

Truth has stumbled in the public square, and honesty cannot enter.

## Isaiah 59:15

Truth is missing, and anyone turning from evil becomes prey. 📖

The LORD looked and was displeased that there was no justice.

## Isaiah 59:16

He saw that there was no man. 📖

He was amazed that no one stepped in for others.

So His own arm brought salvation, and His own righteousness supported Him.

## Isaiah 59:17

He put on righteousness like armor over His chest and salvation like a helmet on His head. 📖

He put on clothes of vengeance and wrapped Himself in a cloak of burning determination.

**Help with the armor**

The image pictures God prepared to act in justice and rescue.

## Isaiah 59:18

He will repay people according to their deeds. 📖

He will give fury to His enemies, repayment to His foes, and what is due to the islands.

## Isaiah 59:19

People will fear the LORD’s name where the sun sets and His glory where it rises. 📖

He will come like a raging flood driven by the LORD’s breath.

## Isaiah 59:20

The LORD declares, “The Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from wrongdoing.” 📖

**Help with Redeemer**

A Redeemer rescues or sets people free.

The promise names those who turn from wrongdoing.

## Isaiah 59:21

The LORD says, “This is My covenant with them. 📖

My Spirit will not leave you.

My words that I put in your mouth will not leave your mouth or your children’s or grandchildren’s mouths.

This is from now and forevermore,” says the LORD.

**Help with covenant**

A covenant is a binding agreement.
