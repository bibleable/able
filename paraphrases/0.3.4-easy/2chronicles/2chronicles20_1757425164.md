# 2 Chronicles 20 — Judah seeks God against an invading army

**About this chapter**

Jehoshaphat prays when enemies threaten Judah. | Jahaziel brings the LORD’s answer, and the people praise. | The account also records Jehoshaphat’s later failed alliance.

## 2 Chronicles 20:1

Moab, Ammon, and some Meunites came to fight Jehoshaphat. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:2

He heard, “A vast army comes from Edom beyond the Sea. 📖

They are at Hazazon-tamar,” also called En-gedi.

## 2 Chronicles 20:3

Alarmed, Jehoshaphat sought the LORD and ordered a fast throughout Judah. 📖

**Help with fast:** Going without food as part of seeking God.

## 2 Chronicles 20:4

People gathered from all Judah’s cities to seek the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:5

Jehoshaphat stood before Judah and Jerusalem at the LORD’s house before the new courtyard. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:6

“LORD, our ancestors’ God, do you not rule in heaven over all nations’ kingdoms? 📖

Power and might are yours; nobody can resist you.

## 2 Chronicles 20:7

You drove out this land’s inhabitants before Israel and gave it forever to your friend Abraham’s descendants. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:8

They lived here and built a sanctuary for your Name, saying, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:9

‘If disaster comes, whether sword, judgment, plague, or famine, we will stand before you and this temple. 📖

Your Name is here.

We will cry in distress, and you will hear and save.’

## 2 Chronicles 20:10

Now Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir attack. 📖

You forbade Israel to invade them after leaving Egypt, so Israel turned away without destroying them.

## 2 Chronicles 20:11

They repay us by trying to drive us from your gift, our inheritance. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:12

Our God, will you not judge them? 📖

We cannot face this vast army.

We do not know what to do, but our eyes look to you.”

## 2 Chronicles 20:13

All Judah’s men, wives, children, and little ones stood before the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:14

The LORD’s Spirit came on Jahaziel son of Zechariah, grandson of Benaiah, in the assembly. 📖

Benaiah was Jeiel’s son, and Jeiel was Mattaniah’s son.

Jahaziel was a Levite descended from Asaph.

## 2 Chronicles 20:15

He told Judah, Jerusalem, and Jehoshaphat, “The LORD says not to fear or lose heart. 📖

This battle belongs to God, not you.

## 2 Chronicles 20:16

March against them tomorrow. 📖

They will come up Ziz’s Ascent at the valley’s end facing Jeruel’s wilderness.

## 2 Chronicles 20:17

You need not fight. 📖

Stand in position and see the LORD’s salvation for Judah and Jerusalem.

Do not fear or lose heart.

Face them tomorrow, because the LORD is with you.”

## 2 Chronicles 20:18

Jehoshaphat bowed facedown, and all Judah and Jerusalem fell to worship the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:19

Kohathite and Korahite Levites stood and loudly praised the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:20

Early they left for Tekoa’s wilderness. 📖

Jehoshaphat told Judah and Jerusalem, “Believe the LORD your God, and you will be upheld.

Believe his prophets, and you will succeed.”

## 2 Chronicles 20:21

After consulting the people, he appointed singers to praise the splendour of the LORD’s holiness before the army. 📖

They sang, “Thank the LORD; his faithful love lasts forever.”

## 2 Chronicles 20:22

As praise began, the LORD set ambushes against Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir, defeating them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:23

Ammon and Moab rose against Mount Seir’s people, devoting them to destruction. 📖

After destroying them, they destroyed one another.

**Help with devoted to destruction:** This describes complete destruction under a religious ban.

## 2 Chronicles 20:24

Judah reached the wilderness lookout and looked for the vast army. 📖

Only dead bodies lay on the ground.

Nobody had escaped.

## 2 Chronicles 20:25

Jehoshaphat and his people found more goods and valuables on the bodies than they could carry. 📖

There was so much plunder that gathering it took 3 days.

**Help with plunder:** Goods taken after battle.

## 2 Chronicles 20:26

On day 4, they blessed the LORD in Beracah Valley, its name to this day. 📖

**Help with Beracah:** The name is linked with blessing.

“To this day” refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Chronicles 20:27

With Jehoshaphat leading, Judah and Jerusalem returned joyfully. 📖

The LORD made them rejoice over their enemies.

## 2 Chronicles 20:28

They entered Jerusalem and the LORD’s house with harps, lyres, and trumpets. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:29

All kingdoms feared God when they heard he fought Israel’s enemies. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:30

Jehoshaphat’s kingdom had peace because God gave rest on every side. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:31

He ruled Judah, becoming king at 35 and reigning in Jerusalem for 25 years. 📖

His mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.

## 2 Chronicles 20:32

He followed his father Asa without turning aside, doing right before the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:33

But the high places remained. 📖

People had not set their hearts on their ancestors’ God.

## 2 Chronicles 20:34

His other acts from beginning to end are in the Chronicles of Jehu son of Hanani. 📖

These are recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 20:35

Later Jehoshaphat king of Judah allied with Israel’s wicked King Ahaziah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:36

They built ships at Ezion-geber to sail to Tarshish. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:37

Eliezer son of Dodavahu of Mareshah said, “Your alliance with Ahaziah caused the LORD to destroy your works.” 📖

The ships wrecked and could not sail to Tarshish.
