# 2 Chronicles 32 — Assyria threatens Hezekiah

**About this chapter**

Assyria invades after Hezekiah’s faithful work. | Hezekiah prepares Jerusalem and encourages trust in the LORD. | The chapter also describes deliverance, pride, humility, and testing.

## 2 Chronicles 32:1

After these faithful acts, Sennacherib king of Assyria invaded Judah and surrounded defended cities to take them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:2

Hezekiah saw Sennacherib intended war against Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:3

He consulted leaders and warriors about blocking springs outside the city, and they helped. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:4

Many blocked springs and the stream through the land. 📖

“Why let Assyria’s kings find plentiful water?”

## 2 Chronicles 32:5

Hezekiah resolutely rebuilt broken walls and raised towers on them. 📖

He built an outer wall, strengthened the City of David’s supporting terraces, and made many weapons and shields.

## 2 Chronicles 32:6

He appointed commanders and encouraged the people gathered in the gate square. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:7

“Be strong and courageous. 📖

Do not fear Assyria’s king and vast army.

A greater One is with us.

## 2 Chronicles 32:8

He has only an arm of flesh. 📖

We have the LORD our God to help and fight.”

His words strengthened the people.

**Help with arm of flesh:** Human strength.

## 2 Chronicles 32:9

While Sennacherib and his forces surrounded Lachish, he sent a message to Hezekiah and Judah’s people in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:10

“Assyria’s King Sennacherib asks why you trust enough to stay under siege. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:11

Does Hezekiah not mislead you towards famine and thirst when he promises the LORD will save you from Assyria’s king? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:12

Did he not remove the LORD’s high places and altars and command Judah and Jerusalem to sacrifice at one altar? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:13

You know what my ancestors and I did to all these peoples. 📖

Did their gods save their lands from me?

## 2 Chronicles 32:14

Which of the nations my ancestors completely destroyed had a god who saved its people from me? 📖

How will your God save you?

## 2 Chronicles 32:15

Do not believe Hezekiah’s deception. 📖

No nation’s or kingdom’s god saved its people from me or my ancestors.

How much less will yours!”

## 2 Chronicles 32:16

Sennacherib’s servants spoke further against the LORD God and Hezekiah his servant. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:17

His letters mocked the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

“As other nations’ gods failed, Hezekiah’s God will not save his people from me.”

## 2 Chronicles 32:18

Assyria shouted in Hebrew to people on Jerusalem’s wall to frighten them and capture the city. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:19

They spoke of Jerusalem’s God as though he were like other peoples’ gods made by human hands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:20

Hezekiah and Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet prayed to heaven. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:21

The LORD’s angel destroyed every mighty warrior, leader, and commander in Assyria’s camp. 📖

The king returned home ashamed.

Some sons killed him with swords in his god’s temple.

## 2 Chronicles 32:22

The LORD saved Hezekiah and Jerusalem from Sennacherib king of Assyria and all others, giving rest on every side. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:23

Many brought the LORD offerings and Hezekiah valuable gifts. 📖

All nations then honoured him.

## 2 Chronicles 32:24

Hezekiah became deathly ill, prayed, and received the LORD’s word and a sign. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:25

His pride kept him from repaying the kindness, bringing wrath on him, Judah, and Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:26

He and Jerusalem humbled their proud hearts, so wrath did not fall during his reign. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:27

He had great wealth and honour and treasuries for silver, gold, precious stones, spices, shields, and all valuables. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:28

He built grain, new-wine, and oil storehouses, livestock stalls, and flock pens. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:29

He built cities and acquired many sheep and cattle because God gave great wealth. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:30

He blocked Gihon Spring’s upper outlet and channelled water to the City of David’s west side. 📖

He prospered in everything.

## 2 Chronicles 32:31

Babylon’s rulers sent messengers about the land’s wonder. 📖

God left him alone to test him and know all his heart.

## 2 Chronicles 32:32

Hezekiah’s other acts and faithful deeds are in the vision of Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet. 📖

This is in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 32:33

He died and was buried in David’s descendants’ upper tombs. 📖

Judah and Jerusalem honoured him at death.

His son Manasseh became king.
