# 2 Chronicles 33 — Manasseh humbles himself; Amon does not

**About this chapter**

Manasseh leads Judah into evil, is taken captive, and humbles himself. | The LORD restores him, and he reforms worship. | Amon continues wrongdoing and is killed.

## 2 Chronicles 33:1

Manasseh became king at 12 and ruled in Jerusalem for 55 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:2

He did evil before the LORD, following detestable practices of nations the LORD drove out before Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:3

He rebuilt Hezekiah’s demolished high places, made Baal altars and Asherah poles, and worshipped all heaven’s host. 📖

**Help with heaven’s host:** Here this refers to heavenly bodies worshipped as gods.

## 2 Chronicles 33:4

He built altars in the LORD’s house, where the LORD said his Name would remain in Jerusalem forever. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:5

He built altars to all heaven’s host in both temple courtyards. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:6

He sacrificed sons in fire in Ben-hinnom Valley, practised sorcery, divination, and witchcraft, and consulted mediums and spiritists. 📖

His great evil angered the LORD.

**Help with these practices:** They seek supernatural power or knowledge, including claimed contact with spirits of the dead.

## 2 Chronicles 33:7

He placed his carved image in God’s house. 📖

God told David and Solomon, “I chose Jerusalem from all Israel’s tribes.

My Name will be in this temple and Jerusalem forever.

## 2 Chronicles 33:8

Israel will not leave their ancestors’ land again if they carefully obey all my commands through Moses. 📖

These include all laws, rules, and judgments.”

## 2 Chronicles 33:9

Manasseh led Judah and Jerusalem into greater evil than nations the LORD destroyed before Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:10

The LORD spoke to Manasseh and his people, but they refused to listen. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:11

He brought Assyria’s commanders, who captured Manasseh, hooked his nose, bound him with bronze chains, and took him to Babylon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:12

In distress Manasseh sought the LORD his God and deeply humbled himself before his ancestors’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:13

The LORD heard his prayer and plea and restored him to Jerusalem and his kingdom. 📖

Manasseh knew the LORD is God.

## 2 Chronicles 33:14

He rebuilt the City of David’s outer wall west of Gihon in the valley to Fish Gate’s entrance. 📖

He encircled Ophel hill and greatly raised the wall.

He stationed commanders in Judah’s defended cities.

## 2 Chronicles 33:15

He removed foreign gods, the temple idol, and his temple-mount and Jerusalem altars, dumping them outside. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:16

He restored the LORD’s altar, offered peace and thank offerings, and commanded Judah to serve the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:17

People still sacrificed at high places, but only to the LORD their God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:18

Manasseh’s other acts, prayer, and seers’ words are recorded in the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

The seers spoke in the name of the LORD, Israel’s God.

**Help with seers:** Prophets who receive God’s messages or visions.

## 2 Chronicles 33:19

The Records of the Seers describe his prayer, God’s response, sins, and unfaithfulness. 📖

They name the high places, Asherah poles, and idol sites he set up before humbling himself.

## 2 Chronicles 33:20

He died and was buried at his palace. 📖

His son Amon became king.

## 2 Chronicles 33:21

Amon became king at 22 and ruled in Jerusalem for 2 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:22

Amon did evil before the LORD like his father Manasseh. 📖

He served and sacrificed to all the idols Manasseh made.

## 2 Chronicles 33:23

He did not humble himself like Manasseh but increased his guilt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:24

His servants plotted and killed him in his palace. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:25

The people killed all conspirators and made his son Josiah king. 📖
