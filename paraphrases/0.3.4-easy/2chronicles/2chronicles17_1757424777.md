# 2 Chronicles 17 — Jehoshaphat teaches Judah

**About this chapter**

Jehoshaphat strengthens Judah and sends teachers with the LORD’s Law. | Neighbours fear the LORD and bring gifts. | The account lists his commanders and troops.

## 2 Chronicles 17:1

Asa’s son Jehoshaphat succeeded him and strengthened his position against Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:2

He stationed troops in Judah’s defended cities and in Ephraim’s cities captured by Asa. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:3

The LORD was with him because he followed his ancestor David’s earlier ways. 📖

He did not seek the Baals,

## 2 Chronicles 17:4

but sought his father’s God and followed his commands instead of Israel’s practices. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:5

The LORD secured his kingdom. 📖

All Judah brought tribute, and he had abundant wealth and honour.

**Help with tribute:** Gifts or payments acknowledging a ruler.

## 2 Chronicles 17:6

He delighted in the LORD’s ways and removed Judah’s high places and Asherah poles. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:7

In year 3, he sent officials Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah to teach in Judah’s cities. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:8

Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah accompanied them. 📖

Priests Elishama and Jehoram also went.

## 2 Chronicles 17:9

They took the Book of the Law of the LORD throughout Judah’s towns and taught the people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:10

Surrounding kingdoms feared the LORD and did not fight Jehoshaphat. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:11

Some Philistines brought gifts and silver tribute. 📖

Arabs brought 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats.

## 2 Chronicles 17:12

Jehoshaphat grew stronger and built fortresses and storage cities in Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:13

He kept vast supplies in Judah’s cities and brave warriors in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:14

These were their numbers by family. 📖

Judah’s commanders of thousands included Adnah with 300,000 brave warriors,

## 2 Chronicles 17:15

Next was commander Jehohanan with 280,000 men, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:16

and Amasiah son of Zichri, a volunteer for the LORD, with 200,000 brave warriors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:17

Benjamin’s brave Eliada had 200,000 men with bows and shields, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:18

and Jehozabad had 180,000 armed for battle. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:19

These served the king besides those stationed in Judah’s defended cities. 📖
