# 2 Chronicles 27 — Jotham’s reign

**About this chapter**

Jotham builds and fights successfully. | He follows the LORD, though the people remain corrupt.

## 2 Chronicles 27:1

Jotham became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years. 📖

His mother was Jerushah daughter of Zadok.

## 2 Chronicles 27:2

He did right before the LORD like Uzziah, but did not enter the LORD’s temple. 📖

The people still acted corruptly.

## 2 Chronicles 27:3

He rebuilt the LORD’s house’s Upper Gate and worked extensively on Ophel hill’s wall. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:4

He built cities in Judah’s hills and forest fortresses and towers. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:5

He defeated Ammon’s king. 📖

They paid 100 silver talents, 10,000 cors of wheat, and 10,000 cors of barley that year.

They paid the same in years 2 and 3.

**Help with talent and cor:** A talent measured weight; a cor measured supplies.

## 2 Chronicles 27:6

He grew powerful because he ordered his ways before the LORD his God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:7

His other acts, wars, and ways are in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:8

He became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:9

He died and was buried in the City of David. 📖

His son Ahaz became king.
