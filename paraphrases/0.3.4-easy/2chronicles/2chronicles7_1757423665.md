# 2 Chronicles 7 — The LORD’s glory and reply

**About this chapter**

Fire consumes the sacrifices, and Israel worships. | The people dedicate the temple and celebrate. | The LORD answers Solomon with promises and warnings that include conditions.

## 2 Chronicles 7:1

Solomon finished praying. 📖

Fire from heaven burned up the offerings and sacrifices, and the LORD’s glory filled the temple.

## 2 Chronicles 7:2

The priests could not enter the LORD’s house because his glory filled it. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:3

All Israel saw the fire and glory above the temple. 📖

They bowed facedown on the pavement, worshipping and thanking the LORD.

“He is good; his faithful love lasts forever.”

## 2 Chronicles 7:4

The king and all the people sacrificed before the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:5

Solomon offered 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep. 📖

The king and people dedicated God’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 7:6

Priests stood at their posts, and Levites held the LORD’s instruments David made for thankful praise. 📖

David had praised with them, “His faithful love lasts forever.”

Priests blew trumpets opposite the Levites while all Israel stood.

## 2 Chronicles 7:7

Solomon consecrated the courtyard’s middle before the LORD’s house for burnt offerings and peace-offering fat. 📖

His bronze altar could not hold them all.

**Help with consecrated:** Set apart for sacred use.

## 2 Chronicles 7:8

Solomon and a vast assembly of Israel celebrated the feast for 7 days. 📖

They came from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt.

## 2 Chronicles 7:9

On day 8 they held a solemn gathering. 📖

The altar dedication lasted 7 days, and the feast another 7.

## 2 Chronicles 7:10

On month 7, day 23, Solomon sent everyone home. 📖

They rejoiced over the LORD’s goodness to David, Solomon, and Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 7:11

Solomon completed the LORD’s house and his palace, successfully doing all he intended for both. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:12

The LORD appeared at night. 📖

“I heard your prayer and chose this place as a house of sacrifice.

## 2 Chronicles 7:13

If I shut the sky without rain, command locusts to eat the land, or send plague among my people, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:14

and my people bearing my name humble themselves, pray, seek my face, and turn from evil, I will hear. 📖

From heaven I will forgive their sin and heal their land.

**Help with seek my face:** Seek God’s presence and help.

## 2 Chronicles 7:15

My eyes will be open and my ears attentive to prayers here. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:16

I chose and consecrated this temple for my Name forever. 📖

My eyes and heart will remain here for all time.

## 2 Chronicles 7:17

If you follow me as David did, obeying all my commands, laws, and rules, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:18

I will establish your royal throne as I promised David. 📖

‘You will always have a man to rule Israel.’

## 2 Chronicles 7:19

But if you abandon my laws and commands and worship and serve other gods, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:20

I will uproot Israel from the land I gave them. 📖

I will banish this temple, sanctified for my Name, from my presence.

I will make it mocked and scorned among all peoples.

**Help with sanctified:** Set apart as holy.

## 2 Chronicles 7:21

The ruined temple will appal passers-by. 📖

They will ask why the LORD did this to the land and temple.

## 2 Chronicles 7:22

Others will answer, ‘They abandoned the LORD, their ancestors’ God, who brought them from Egypt. 📖

They embraced, worshipped, and served other gods.

That is why he brought this disaster.’”
