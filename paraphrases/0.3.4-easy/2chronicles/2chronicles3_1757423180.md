# 2 Chronicles 3 — Solomon builds the temple

**About this chapter**

The temple is built on Mount Moriah. | The account gives dimensions, materials, decorations, and the names of the pillars. | Measurements follow this BSB text.

## 2 Chronicles 3:1

Solomon began the LORD’s house on Mount Moriah in Jerusalem, where the LORD appeared to his father David. 📖

David prepared the site at Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor.

**Help with threshing floor:** A place for separating grain from stalks.

## 2 Chronicles 3:2

Building began in his reign’s 4th year, month 2, day 2. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:3

The foundation was 60 cubits long and 20 wide by the old standard. 📖

**Help with cubit:** An ancient unit roughly a forearm’s length.

## 2 Chronicles 3:4

The front porch stretched across the temple’s width, 20 cubits long and 20 high. 📖

He covered its inside with pure gold.

## 2 Chronicles 3:5

He panelled the main room with cypress, covered it with fine gold, and decorated it with palm trees and chains. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:6

He decorated the temple with beautiful precious stones. 📖

Its gold came from Parvaim.

## 2 Chronicles 3:7

He covered beams, thresholds, walls, and doors with gold and carved cherubim on the walls. 📖

**Help with cherubim:** Heavenly beings represented by winged figures.

## 2 Chronicles 3:8

The Most Holy Place was 20 cubits long and 20 wide, matching the temple’s width. 📖

He covered its inside with 600 talents of fine gold.

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.

## 2 Chronicles 3:9

The nails weighed 50 shekels of gold. 📖

He covered the upper rooms with gold too.

**Help with shekel:** A unit of weight.

## 2 Chronicles 3:10

He made 2 sculpted cherubim in the Most Holy Place and covered them with gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:11

Their total wingspan was 20 cubits. 📖

The first cherub’s 5-cubit wing touched the wall.

Its other 5-cubit wing touched the other cherub’s wing.

## 2 Chronicles 3:12

The second cherub’s 5-cubit wing touched the wall. 📖

Its other 5-cubit wing touched the first cherub’s wing.

## 2 Chronicles 3:13

Their wingspan totalled 20 cubits. 📖

They stood on their feet facing the main room.

## 2 Chronicles 3:14

He made a curtain of blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine linen, with woven cherubim. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:15

He made 2 pillars before the temple, together 35 cubits high. 📖

Each had a 5-cubit capital.

**Help with capital:** The decorated top of a pillar.

## 2 Chronicles 3:16

He placed interwoven chains on the pillars’ tops and fastened 100 pomegranates in the chainwork. 📖

**Help with pomegranates:** Here these are decorations shaped like the fruit.

## 2 Chronicles 3:17

He put one pillar south and one north before the temple. 📖

He named the southern one Jachin and the northern one Boaz.
