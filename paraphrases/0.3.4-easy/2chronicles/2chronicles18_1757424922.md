# 2 Chronicles 18 — Micaiah’s warning and Ahab’s death

**About this chapter**

Jehoshaphat joins Ahab in war. | Micaiah’s message differs from the other prophets. | Ahab goes disguised, but is wounded and dies.

## 2 Chronicles 18:1

Jehoshaphat had great wealth and honour and allied with Ahab through marriage. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:2

Years later he visited Ahab in Samaria. 📖

Ahab sacrificed many sheep and cattle for him and his companions and urged an attack on Ramoth-gilead.

## 2 Chronicles 18:3

Ahab king of Israel asked Jehoshaphat king of Judah to join him against Ramoth-gilead. 📖

Jehoshaphat answered, “I am as you are, my people are yours, and we will join your war.”

## 2 Chronicles 18:4

Jehoshaphat told Israel’s king, “First ask for the LORD’s word.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:5

Israel’s king gathered 400 prophets. 📖

“Should we attack Ramoth-gilead or hold back?”

They answered, “Attack, and God will give it to the king.”

## 2 Chronicles 18:6

Jehoshaphat asked for another prophet of the LORD to consult. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:7

Israel’s king said, “We can ask Micaiah son of Imlah, but I hate him. 📖

He always prophesies bad for me, never good.”

Jehoshaphat said, “The king should not say that!”

## 2 Chronicles 18:8

Israel’s king ordered an official to bring Micaiah son of Imlah at once. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:9

Israel’s king and Jehoshaphat king of Judah sat on thrones in royal clothes. 📖

They were at the threshing floor by Samaria’s gate, with all prophets prophesying before them.

**Help with threshing floor:** A place for separating grain from stalks.

## 2 Chronicles 18:10

Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns. 📖

“The LORD says you will gore Aram with these until they are destroyed.”

**Help with gore:** Stab with horns like an attacking animal.

## 2 Chronicles 18:11

All the prophets promised victory at Ramoth-gilead, saying the LORD would hand it to the king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:12

The messenger told Micaiah, “All prophets favour the king. 📖

Agree and speak favourably too.”

## 2 Chronicles 18:13

Micaiah answered, “As surely as the LORD lives, I will say whatever my God tells me.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:14

The king asked Micaiah whether to attack Ramoth-gilead or hold back. 📖

Micaiah answered, “Attack and triumph.

They will be handed to you.”

## 2 Chronicles 18:15

The king said, “How often must I make you swear to speak only truth in the LORD’s name?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:16

Micaiah said, “I saw all Israel scattered on hills like sheep without a shepherd. 📖

The LORD said they have no master and should each return home peacefully.”

## 2 Chronicles 18:17

Israel’s king told Jehoshaphat, “Did I not say he only prophesies bad for me?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:18

Micaiah continued, “Hear the LORD’s word. 📖

I saw him enthroned, with all heaven’s army standing right and left.

## 2 Chronicles 18:19

The LORD asked who would entice Ahab king of Israel to march up and fall at Ramoth-gilead. 📖

Different suggestions were made.

## 2 Chronicles 18:20

A spirit stood before the LORD and offered to entice him. 📖

The LORD asked how.

## 2 Chronicles 18:21

It answered, ‘I will be a lying spirit in all his prophets’ mouths.’ 📖

The LORD said it would succeed and told it to go.

## 2 Chronicles 18:22

The LORD put a lying spirit in your prophets’ mouths and declared disaster for you.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:23

Zedekiah son of Chenaanah struck Micaiah’s face. 📖

“Which way did the LORD’s Spirit leave me to speak to you?”

## 2 Chronicles 18:24

Micaiah answered, “You will see when you hide in an inner room.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:25

Israel’s king ordered, “Return Micaiah to Amon the city governor and Joash the king’s son. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:26

Tell them to imprison him with only bread and water until I return safely.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:27

Micaiah said, “If you return safely, the LORD has not spoken through me.” 📖

He added, “Listen, all you people!”

## 2 Chronicles 18:28

Israel’s king and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went to Ramoth-gilead. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:29

Israel’s king told Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself for battle. 📖

You wear your royal robes.”

He disguised himself and entered battle.

## 2 Chronicles 18:30

Aram’s king told chariot commanders to fight only Israel’s king, nobody else small or great. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:31

They mistook Jehoshaphat for Israel’s king and attacked. 📖

Jehoshaphat cried, and the LORD helped.

God drew them away.

## 2 Chronicles 18:32

They realised he was not Israel’s king and stopped pursuing him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:33

A man shot without special aim, striking Israel’s king between armour joints. 📖

He ordered his driver, “Turn around and take me away.

I am badly wounded!”

## 2 Chronicles 18:34

The battle raged all day. 📖

Israel’s king propped himself in his chariot facing Aram until evening.

At sunset he died.
