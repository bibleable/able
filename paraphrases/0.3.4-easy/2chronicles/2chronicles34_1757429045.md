# 2 Chronicles 34 — Josiah and the Book of the Law

**About this chapter**

Josiah removes idols and repairs the temple. | Hilkiah finds the Law, and Huldah brings the LORD’s answer. | Josiah and the people renew their covenant.

## 2 Chronicles 34:1

Josiah became king at 8 and ruled in Jerusalem for 31 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:2

He did right before the LORD and followed David, turning neither right nor left. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:3

In year 8, while young, he sought his ancestor David’s God. 📖

In year 12, he began removing high places, Asherah poles, carved idols, and cast images from Judah and Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 34:4

Before him, Baal altars were demolished and incense altars above them broken. 📖

He crushed poles and idols to dust and scattered it on their worshippers’ graves.

## 2 Chronicles 34:5

He burned priests’ bones on their altars, cleansing Judah and Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:6

He did likewise in Manasseh’s, Ephraim’s, and Simeon’s cities to Naphtali and their surrounding ruins. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:7

He demolished altars and Asherah poles, crushed idols, and smashed incense altars throughout Israel, then returned to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:8

In year 18, Josiah sent men to repair the LORD his God’s house, cleansing land and temple. 📖

They were Shaphan son of Azaliah, city governor Maaseiah, and recorder Joah son of Joahaz.

## 2 Chronicles 34:9

They gave high priest Hilkiah the money door-guarding Levites collected for God’s house. 📖

Manasseh, Ephraim, Israel’s remnant, Judah, Benjamin, and Jerusalem contributed.

## 2 Chronicles 34:10

Supervisors received it and paid workers restoring and repairing the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:11

Carpenters and builders bought cut stone, timber for joints, and beams for buildings Judah’s kings let decay. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:12

Workers served faithfully under Merarite Levites Jahath and Obadiah and Kohathites Zechariah and Meshullam. 📖

Other instrument-skilled Levites

## 2 Chronicles 34:13

supervised labourers task by task. 📖

Some were secretaries, officers, and gatekeepers.

## 2 Chronicles 34:14

While bringing out temple money, Hilkiah found the Book of the Law of the LORD given through Moses. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:15

He told Shaphan the scribe, “I found the Book of the Law in the LORD’s house!” 📖

He gave it to him.

## 2 Chronicles 34:16

Shaphan brought it to the king. 📖

“Your servants are doing all their assigned work.

## 2 Chronicles 34:17

They paid out the temple money to supervisors and workers.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:18

Shaphan told the king Hilkiah gave him a book and read it before him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:19

The king heard the Law and tore his clothes. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:20

He commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the scribe, and royal servant Asaiah: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:21

“Ask the LORD about this book for me and Israel’s and Judah’s survivors. 📖

His great wrath fell because our ancestors failed to do all this book commands.”

## 2 Chronicles 34:22

They consulted Huldah the prophetess in Jerusalem’s Second District. 📖

Her husband Shallum was son of Tokhath, grandson of Hasrah, the clothing keeper.

**Help with prophetess:** A woman who brings God’s message.

## 2 Chronicles 34:23

Huldah said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says to tell the man who sent you: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:24

I will bring disaster on this place and people according to every curse read before Judah’s king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:25

They abandoned me and burned incense to other gods. 📖

Their handiwork angered me, and my wrath here will not be quenched.

## 2 Chronicles 34:26

Tell Judah’s king who asked that the LORD, Israel’s God, says this about what he heard: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:27

You heard my words against this place and people with a tender heart. 📖

You humbled yourself, tore your clothes, and wept before me.

I heard you, declares the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 34:28

I will gather you to your ancestors and your grave in peace. 📖

You will not see all this place’s and people’s disaster.’”

They reported to the king.

## 2 Chronicles 34:29

He gathered all Judah’s and Jerusalem’s elders. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:30

He entered the LORD’s house with Judah, Jerusalem, priests, Levites, and all people great and small. 📖

He read every word of the Book of the Covenant found there.

## 2 Chronicles 34:31

By the pillar, he covenanted to follow the LORD wholeheartedly and keep his commands and laws. 📖

He would carry out this book’s covenant.

**Help with covenant:** A binding agreement.

## 2 Chronicles 34:32

Jerusalem and Benjamin stood in agreement. 📖

Jerusalem’s people carried out their ancestors’ God’s covenant.

## 2 Chronicles 34:33

Josiah removed all detestable things from Israel’s lands and required everyone to serve the LORD. 📖

Throughout his reign they followed their ancestors’ God without turning aside.
