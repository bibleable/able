# 2 Chronicles 14 — Asa seeks the LORD

**About this chapter**

Asa removes foreign worship and strengthens cities during peace. | A large Cushite army attacks. | Asa prays, and the LORD gives Judah victory.

## 2 Chronicles 14:1

Abijah died and was buried in the City of David. 📖

His son Asa became king.

The land had peace for 10 years.

## 2 Chronicles 14:2

Asa did good and right before the LORD his God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:3

He removed foreign altars and high places, smashed sacred pillars, and cut Asherah poles. 📖

**Help with these objects:** They were used in worship of other gods.

## 2 Chronicles 14:4

He ordered Judah to seek the LORD, their ancestors’ God, and keep his Law and commands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:5

He removed high places and incense altars from all Judah’s cities. 📖

His kingdom had peace.

## 2 Chronicles 14:6

Asa built defended cities during peace. 📖

Nobody fought him because the LORD gave rest.

## 2 Chronicles 14:7

He told Judah, “Build walls, towers, doors, and bars around these cities. 📖

We still have the land because we sought the LORD our God.

He gave rest on every side.”

They built and prospered.

## 2 Chronicles 14:8

Asa had 300,000 Judah men with large shields and spears. 📖

Benjamin supplied 280,000 with small shields and bows.

All were brave warriors.

## 2 Chronicles 14:9

Zerah the Cushite attacked with 1,000,000 men and 300 chariots, advancing to Mareshah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:10

Asa marched out and lined up for battle in Zephathah Valley near Mareshah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:11

Asa prayed, “LORD, only you help the powerless against the mighty. 📖

Help us, LORD our God.

We rely on you and face this multitude in your name.

You are our God.

Do not let a mortal prevail against you.”

## 2 Chronicles 14:12

The LORD struck the Cushites before Asa and Judah, and they fled. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:13

Asa’s army pursued to Gerar. 📖

The Cushites fell without recovering, crushed before the LORD and his army.

Judah took much plunder,

## 2 Chronicles 14:14

and attacked Gerar’s surrounding cities, which feared the LORD. 📖

They plundered all these cities, which held many goods.

**Help with plunder:** Goods taken by force.

## 2 Chronicles 14:15

They attacked herdsmen’s tents and took many sheep and camels. 📖

They returned to Jerusalem.
