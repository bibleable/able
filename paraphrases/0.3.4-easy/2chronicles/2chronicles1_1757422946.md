# 2 Chronicles 1 — Solomon asks for wisdom

**About this chapter**

Solomon worships at Gibeon and asks God for wisdom to govern. | God also grants wealth and honour. | The chapter describes Solomon’s horses and trade.

## 2 Chronicles 1:1

Solomon son of David secured his kingdom. 📖

The LORD his God was with him and greatly exalted him.

## 2 Chronicles 1:2

Solomon addressed all Israel: commanders of thousands and hundreds, judges, leaders, and family heads. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:3

He and all the assembly went to Gibeon’s high place. 📖

God’s Tent of Meeting was there, made in the wilderness by Moses, the LORD’s servant.

**Help with high place:** A worship site.

## 2 Chronicles 1:4

David had brought God’s ark from Kiriath-jearim to Jerusalem. 📖

He had prepared its place and pitched a tent for it.

**Help with ark:** The sacred chest associated with God’s covenant.

## 2 Chronicles 1:5

Bezalel son of Uri, grandson of Hur, made the bronze altar at Gibeon before the LORD’s tabernacle. 📖

Solomon and the assembly sought God there.

**Help with tabernacle:** The sacred tent used for worship.

## 2 Chronicles 1:6

Solomon offered 1,000 burnt offerings before the LORD on the bronze altar at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:7

That night God appeared to Solomon. 📖

“Ask, and I will give it to you!”

## 2 Chronicles 1:8

Solomon answered, “You showed great faithful love to my father David and made me king instead. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:9

LORD God, fulfil your promise to my father David. 📖

You made me king over people as numerous as earth’s dust.

## 2 Chronicles 1:10

Give me wisdom and knowledge to lead them. 📖

Who can govern this great people of yours?”

## 2 Chronicles 1:11

God answered, “You desired wisdom and knowledge to govern my people, whom I made you rule. 📖

You did not ask for riches, wealth, honour, enemies’ deaths, or even long life.

## 2 Chronicles 1:12

Therefore I grant you wisdom and knowledge. 📖

I will also give riches, wealth, and honour unlike those of kings before or after you.”

## 2 Chronicles 1:13

Solomon left Gibeon’s high place and Tent of Meeting for Jerusalem. 📖

He ruled Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 1:14

He gathered 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horses, stationed in chariot cities and with him in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:15

He made silver and gold as common as stones in Jerusalem, and cedar as plentiful as foothill sycamores. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:16

His horses came from Egypt and Kue. 📖

Royal merchants bought them from Kue.

## 2 Chronicles 1:17

An Egyptian chariot cost 600 silver shekels, and a horse 150. 📖

They exported them to all Hittite kings and Aram’s kings.

**Help with shekel:** A unit of weight used for silver.
