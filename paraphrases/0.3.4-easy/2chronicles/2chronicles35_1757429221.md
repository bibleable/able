# 2 Chronicles 35 — Josiah’s Passover and death

**About this chapter**

Josiah arranges a great Passover. | Priests and Levites prepare offerings and serve the people. | Later Josiah fights Neco and is killed.

## 2 Chronicles 35:1

Josiah kept the LORD’s Passover in Jerusalem, killing the lamb on month 1, day 14. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:2

He assigned and encouraged priests in the LORD’s house’s service. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:3

He told holy Levites teaching Israel, “Put the holy ark in Solomon son of David king of Israel’s temple. 📖

Stop carrying it on shoulders.

Serve the LORD your God and Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 35:4

Prepare by family and division following David’s and Solomon’s written instructions. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:5

Stand in the Holy Place by divisions of ordinary people’s families and Levite families. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:6

Kill Passover lambs, consecrate yourselves, and prepare for your countrymen according to the LORD’s word through Moses.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:7

Josiah gave 30,000 lambs and goats and 3,000 bulls from his property for everyone’s Passover offerings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:8

Officials gave willingly for people, priests, and Levites. 📖

Temple chiefs Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel gave priests 2,600 Passover offerings and 300 bulls.

## 2 Chronicles 35:9

Levite officers Conaniah, his brothers Shemaiah and Nethanel, Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad gave 5,000 Passover offerings and 500 bulls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:10

Priests and Levite divisions stood ready as the king ordered. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:11

They killed Passover lambs, priests splashed the blood handed them, and Levites skinned the animals. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:12

They assigned burnt offerings to family divisions to offer as Moses’s Book required. 📖

They did likewise with bulls.

## 2 Chronicles 35:13

They roasted Passover animals as required and boiled other sacred offerings in pots, kettles, and bowls. 📖

They quickly served everyone.

## 2 Chronicles 35:14

Levites then prepared for themselves and Aaron’s priestly descendants, who offered burnt offerings and fat until night. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:15

Asaph’s singers served as David, Asaph, Heman, and royal seer Jeduthun commanded. 📖

Gatekeepers stayed at every gate because fellow Levites prepared for them.

## 2 Chronicles 35:16

The whole LORD’s service was completed that day, Passover and burnt offerings on his altar, as Josiah ordered. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:17

Israel there kept Passover and Unleavened Bread for 7 days. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:18

No Passover like this had been kept since Samuel the prophet. 📖

No Israelite king matched Josiah’s Passover with priests, Levites, Judah, Israelites present, and Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 35:19

It was kept in Josiah’s 18th year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:20

After temple arrangements, Neco king of Egypt went to fight at Carchemish by the Euphrates. 📖

Josiah confronted him.

## 2 Chronicles 35:21

Neco sent word, “Judah’s king, what is between you and me? 📖

I am not attacking you but another dynasty.

God ordered haste.

God is with me.

Stop opposing him, or he will destroy you!”

## 2 Chronicles 35:22

Josiah refused and disguised himself to fight at Megiddo’s Plain. 📖

He ignored Neco’s words from God’s mouth.

## 2 Chronicles 35:23

Archers shot him. 📖

“Take me away; I am badly wounded!”

## 2 Chronicles 35:24

Servants moved him from his chariot to his second chariot and brought him to Jerusalem, where he died. 📖

They buried him in his ancestors’ tomb, and Judah and Jerusalem mourned.

## 2 Chronicles 35:25

Jeremiah lamented Josiah. 📖

Male and female singers still recite laments for him to this day, established as a rule for Israel.

They are in the Book of Laments.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Chronicles 35:26

His other acts and faithful deeds according to the LORD’s Law, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:27

from beginning to end, are in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. 📖
