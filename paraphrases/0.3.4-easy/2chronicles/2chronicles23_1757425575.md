# 2 Chronicles 23 — Joash is made king

**About this chapter**

Jehoiada prepares the guards and reveals the king’s son. | Athaliah is removed and killed. | The people renew their covenant and restore worship.

## 2 Chronicles 23:1

In year 7, Jehoiada strengthened himself and covenanted with commanders of hundreds. 📖

They were Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, and Azariah son of Obed.

Maaseiah son of Adaiah and Elishaphat son of Zichri also joined.

## 2 Chronicles 23:2

They gathered Levites from Judah’s cities and Israelite family heads and came to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:3

The assembly covenanted with the king in God’s house. 📖

Jehoiada said, “Here is the king’s son!

He must rule as the LORD promised David’s descendants.

**Help with covenanted:** Made a binding agreement.

## 2 Chronicles 23:4

One-third of priests and Levites starting Sabbath duty must guard the doors, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:5

one-third the palace, and one-third the Foundation Gate. 📖

Everyone else must remain in the LORD’s house’s courts.

## 2 Chronicles 23:6

Only priests and serving Levites may enter the LORD’s house because they are consecrated. 📖

Everyone must obey the LORD’s requirement.

**Help with consecrated:** Prepared and set apart for sacred service.

## 2 Chronicles 23:7

Levites must surround the king with weapons. 📖

Anyone entering the temple must be killed.

Stay close wherever the king goes.”

## 2 Chronicles 23:8

Levites and all Judah obeyed Jehoiada. 📖

They brought men starting and ending Sabbath duty, because he released no division.

## 2 Chronicles 23:9

Jehoiada gave the commanders David’s spears and large and small shields from God’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:10

He placed armed troops around the king by altar and temple, from the temple’s south to north. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:11

Jehoiada and his sons crowned the king’s son, gave him the Testimony, and proclaimed him king. 📖

They anointed him and shouted, “Long live the king!”

**Help with Testimony:** The written covenant law.

Anointing involved pouring oil to mark appointment.

## 2 Chronicles 23:12

Athaliah heard people running and cheering and went to the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:13

She saw the king at his entrance pillar with officers and trumpeters. 📖

People rejoiced and blew trumpets while singers led praise with instruments.

She tore her clothes and cried, “Treason, treason!”

**Help with treason:** Betrayal of a ruler or country.

## 2 Chronicles 23:14

Jehoiada ordered commanders, “Take her between the ranks and kill anyone following her.” 📖

He forbade killing her in the LORD’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 23:15

They seized and killed her at the Horse Gate’s entrance on palace grounds. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:16

Jehoiada covenanted with king and people that they would be the LORD’s people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:17

They demolished Baal’s temple and smashed altars and idols. 📖

They killed Mattan, Baal’s priest, before the altars.

## 2 Chronicles 23:18

Jehoiada gave temple oversight to Levitical priests appointed by David. 📖

They offered burnt offerings according to Moses’s Law with rejoicing and song as David ordered.

## 2 Chronicles 23:19

Gatekeepers kept anyone unclean from entering the LORD’s house. 📖

**Help with unclean:** Not meeting the law’s requirements for sacred access.

This does not mean a person has less worth.

## 2 Chronicles 23:20

Commanders, nobles, rulers, and all the people brought Joash from the LORD’s house through the Upper Gate into the palace. 📖

They seated him on the throne.

## 2 Chronicles 23:21

Everyone rejoiced. 📖

The city was quiet because Athaliah had been killed.
