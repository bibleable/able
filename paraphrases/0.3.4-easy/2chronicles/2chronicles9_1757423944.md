# 2 Chronicles 9 — The queen of Sheba and Solomon’s wealth

**About this chapter**

Sheba’s queen tests Solomon and praises his wisdom. | The chapter describes his wealth, trade, and rule. | It ends with his death and Rehoboam’s succession.

## 2 Chronicles 9:1

Sheba’s queen heard Solomon’s fame and came to test him with hard questions. 📖

She reached Jerusalem with a great caravan of camels carrying spices, much gold, and precious stones.

She told Solomon everything on her mind.

## 2 Chronicles 9:2

Solomon answered every question. 📖

Nothing was too hard to explain.

## 2 Chronicles 9:3

Sheba’s queen saw Solomon’s wisdom and palace, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:4

food, servants’ seating, attendants’ service and clothes, cupbearers’ clothes, and burnt offerings at the LORD’s house. 📖

It took her breath away.

**Help with took her breath away:** She was overwhelmed with amazement.

## 2 Chronicles 9:5

She said, “My country’s report about your words and wisdom was true. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:6

I did not believe until I saw for myself. 📖

Not half your great wisdom was reported!

You far surpass what I heard.

## 2 Chronicles 9:7

Your men are blessed! 📖

Your servants are blessed to stand continually before you and hear your wisdom!

## 2 Chronicles 9:8

Bless the LORD your God, who delighted to put you on his throne as king for him. 📖

He loved Israel to establish them forever and made you king to carry out justice and righteousness.”

## 2 Chronicles 9:9

She gave Solomon 120 talents of gold, much spice, and precious stones. 📖

There had never been spices like Sheba’s queen’s gifts.

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.

## 2 Chronicles 9:10

Hiram’s and Solomon’s servants brought gold from Ophir, also algum wood and precious stones. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:11

The king used algum for steps in the LORD’s house and palace and harps and lyres for singers. 📖

Judah had never seen anything like them.

## 2 Chronicles 9:12

Solomon gave Sheba’s queen everything she asked, far more than she brought. 📖

She returned to her country with her servants.

## 2 Chronicles 9:13

Solomon received 666 talents of gold each year, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:14

besides merchants’ and traders’ revenue. 📖

All Arabian kings and the land’s governors brought gold and silver.

## 2 Chronicles 9:15

He made 200 large hammered-gold shields, using 600 shekels each. 📖

**Help with shekel:** A unit of weight.

## 2 Chronicles 9:16

He made 300 small hammered-gold shields, using 300 shekels each. 📖

He put them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.

## 2 Chronicles 9:17

He made a great ivory throne covered with pure gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:18

The throne had 6 steps and an attached gold footstool. 📖

It had armrests on both sides, with a lion beside each.

## 2 Chronicles 9:19

12 lions stood on the 6 steps, one at either end of each step. 📖

No kingdom had anything like it.

## 2 Chronicles 9:20

All Solomon’s drinking cups were gold, and the House of the Forest of Lebanon’s utensils were pure gold. 📖

None was silver, which was considered worthless then.

## 2 Chronicles 9:21

His Tarshish ships sailed with Hiram’s servants. 📖

Every 3 years they brought gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks.

## 2 Chronicles 9:22

Solomon surpassed all earth’s kings in wealth and wisdom. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:23

All earth’s kings sought to hear the wisdom God put in Solomon’s heart. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:24

Each visitor brought yearly tribute: silver and gold objects, clothes, weapons, spices, horses, and mules. 📖

**Help with tribute:** A payment or gift acknowledging a ruler.

## 2 Chronicles 9:25

Solomon had 4,000 stalls for horses and chariots, and 12,000 horses. 📖

He stationed them in chariot cities and with him in Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 9:26

He ruled all kings from the Euphrates to Philistine territory and Egypt’s border. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:27

He made silver as common as stones in Jerusalem and cedar as abundant as foothill sycamores. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:28

His horses came from Egypt and all lands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:29

Solomon’s other acts from beginning to end are in the Records of Nathan the Prophet. 📖

They are also in the Prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite and the Visions of Iddo the Seer.

These visions concern Jeroboam son of Nebat.

## 2 Chronicles 9:30

Solomon ruled all Israel in Jerusalem for 40 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:31

He died and was buried in his father David’s city. 📖

His son Rehoboam became king.
