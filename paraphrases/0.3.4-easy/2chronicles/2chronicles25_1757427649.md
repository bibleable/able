# 2 Chronicles 25 — Amaziah’s victory, pride, and defeat

**About this chapter**

Amaziah obeys one warning but later worships Edom’s gods. | He challenges Israel’s king and is defeated. | His reign ends in conspiracy and death.

## 2 Chronicles 25:1

Amaziah became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 29 years. 📖

His mother was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 25:2

He did right before the LORD, but not wholeheartedly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:3

Once secure, he executed the servants who murdered his father. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:4

He spared their sons, following the LORD’s command in Moses’s Book of the Law. 📖

“Do not execute fathers for children or children for fathers.

Each dies for their own sin.”

## 2 Chronicles 25:5

He organised Judah’s and Benjamin’s families under commanders of thousands and hundreds. 📖

Counting men aged 20 and above, he found 300,000 chosen soldiers able to use spear and shield.

## 2 Chronicles 25:6

He hired 100,000 brave Israelite warriors for 100 silver talents. 📖

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.

## 2 Chronicles 25:7

A man of God warned, “Do not take Israel’s army. 📖

The LORD is not with Israel or any Ephraimites.

## 2 Chronicles 25:8

Even if you fight bravely, God will overthrow you. 📖

He can help and overthrow.”

## 2 Chronicles 25:9

Amaziah asked about the 100 talents paid. 📖

The man answered, “The LORD can give much more.”

## 2 Chronicles 25:10

He sent Ephraim’s troops home. 📖

They returned furious with Judah.

## 2 Chronicles 25:11

Amaziah strengthened himself and killed 10,000 Seir men in the Valley of Salt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:12

Judah captured another 10,000 alive and threw them from a cliff, smashing all to pieces. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:13

The dismissed troops raided Judah’s cities from Samaria to Beth-horon, killing 3,000 and taking much plunder. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:14

Returning from Edom’s slaughter, Amaziah brought Seir’s gods, made them his gods, bowed to them, and burned sacrifices. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:15

The LORD angrily sent a prophet. 📖

“Why seek gods that could not save their people from you?”

## 2 Chronicles 25:16

The king interrupted, “Are you my adviser? 📖

Stop, or be struck down!”

The prophet stopped but said God determined to destroy him for this act and refusing advice.

## 2 Chronicles 25:17

Amaziah consulted advisers and challenged Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, grandson of Jehu, Israel’s king, to meet face to face. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:18

Jehoash replied, “A Lebanon thistle asked a cedar for his daughter to marry its son. 📖

A wild animal trampled the thistle.

**Help with this story:** A small prickly plant challenges a great tree.

The king warns Amaziah against challenging a stronger opponent.

## 2 Chronicles 25:19

Your Edom victory made you proud and boastful. 📖

Stay home.

Why bring trouble and ruin on yourself and Judah?”

## 2 Chronicles 25:20

Amaziah refused to listen. 📖

God brought this about to hand them to Jehoash because they sought Edom’s gods.

## 2 Chronicles 25:21

Jehoash king of Israel faced Amaziah king of Judah at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:22

Israel defeated Judah, and every man fled home. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:23

Jehoash captured Amaziah son of Joash, grandson of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh and brought him to Jerusalem. 📖

He broke 400 cubits of wall from Ephraim Gate to Corner Gate.

**Help with cubit:** An ancient unit roughly a forearm’s length.

## 2 Chronicles 25:24

He took all silver, gold, and objects in God’s house with Obed-edom and the royal treasuries, plus hostages. 📖

He returned to Samaria.

## 2 Chronicles 25:25

Amaziah son of Joash, Judah’s king, outlived Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, Israel’s king, by 15 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:26

His other acts from beginning to end are in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:27

A plot formed in Jerusalem after he abandoned the LORD. 📖

He fled to Lachish, but pursuers killed him there.

## 2 Chronicles 25:28

They brought him on horses and buried him with his ancestors in the City of Judah. 📖
