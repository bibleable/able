# 2 Chronicles 4 — The temple’s furnishings

**About this chapter**

The account describes the altar, Sea, basins, lampstands, tables, and other objects. | It names materials, measurements, and quantities. | The Sea is a large basin.

## 2 Chronicles 4:1

He made a bronze altar 20 cubits long, 20 wide, and 10 high. 📖

**Help with cubit:** An ancient unit roughly a forearm’s length.

## 2 Chronicles 4:2

He cast the round metal Sea, 10 cubits across, 5 high, and 30 around. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:3

Below its rim, ox figures encircled it, 10 per cubit. 📖

They were cast in 2 rows as part of the Sea.

## 2 Chronicles 4:4

The Sea rested on 12 oxen, 3 facing each direction: north, west, south, and east. 📖

Their backs faced the centre.

## 2 Chronicles 4:5

It was a handbreadth thick, with a rim like a cup’s edge or lily blossom. 📖

It held 3,000 baths.

**Help with these units:** A handbreadth is a hand’s width.

A bath measured liquid.

## 2 Chronicles 4:6

He made 10 washing basins, 5 south and 5 north. 📖

Burnt-offering parts were rinsed there.

Priests washed in the Sea.

## 2 Chronicles 4:7

He made 10 gold lampstands according to their instructions, placing 5 south and 5 north in the temple. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:8

He made 10 tables, placing 5 south and 5 north in the temple, and 100 gold bowls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:9

He made the priests’ courtyard and large court with doors covered in bronze. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:10

He put the Sea at the southeast corner on the south side. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:11

Huram made pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls, completing his work for Solomon in God’s house: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:12

2 pillars, 2 bowl-shaped capitals on them, and 2 networks covering those capitals, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:13

400 pomegranates for the 2 networks, 2 rows for each network covering the bowl-shaped capitals, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:14

stands and their basins, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:15

Sea and the 12 oxen under it, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:16

pots, shovels, meat forks, and all other objects. 📖

Everything Huram-abi made for Solomon for the LORD’s house was polished bronze.

## 2 Chronicles 4:17

The king cast them in clay moulds on the Jordan plain between Succoth and Zeredah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:18

Solomon made so many objects that the bronze’s weight could not be determined. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:19

He made all God’s house’s furnishings: gold altar and tables for the Bread of the Presence, 📖

**Help with Bread of the Presence:** Bread set before the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 4:20

pure-gold lampstands and lamps to burn before the inner sanctuary as prescribed, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:21

flowers, lamps, and tongs of the purest gold, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:22

and wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, ladles, and incense burners of pure gold. 📖

The temple’s gold doors included the inner doors to the Most Holy Place and the main hall’s doors.

**Help with wick trimmers:** Tools for trimming lamps’ wicks.
