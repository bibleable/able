# 2 Chronicles 11 — Rehoboam strengthens Judah

**About this chapter**

The LORD stops Rehoboam from fighting his brothers. | Rehoboam strengthens cities and receives priests and other followers. | His family and succession plans are recorded.

## 2 Chronicles 11:1

Rehoboam gathered 180,000 chosen warriors from Judah and Benjamin in Jerusalem. 📖

They would fight Israel to restore his kingdom.

## 2 Chronicles 11:2

The LORD spoke to Shemaiah the man of God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:3

“Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon, Judah’s king, and all Israel in Judah and Benjamin: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:4

The LORD forbids you to fight your brothers. 📖

Go home, because this is my doing.”

They obeyed and turned back from attacking Jeroboam.

## 2 Chronicles 11:5

Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and strengthened Judah’s cities for defence. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:6

He strengthened Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:7

Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:8

Gath, Mareshah, Ziph, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:9

Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:10

Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, defended cities in Judah and Benjamin. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:11

He strengthened their defences and appointed officers with food, oil, and wine supplies. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:12

He put shields and spears in every city and greatly strengthened them. 📖

Judah and Benjamin belonged to him.

## 2 Chronicles 11:13

Priests and Levites from every Israelite district supported Rehoboam. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:14

Levites left pastures and property for Judah and Jerusalem. 📖

Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them as the LORD’s priests.

## 2 Chronicles 11:15

Jeroboam appointed his own priests for high places, goat demons, and the calf idols he made. 📖

**Help with high places and goat demons:** These were worship sites and goat-like beings worshipped there.

## 2 Chronicles 11:16

People from every tribe determined to seek the LORD followed the Levites to Jerusalem. 📖

They sacrificed to the LORD, their ancestors’ God.

## 2 Chronicles 11:17

They strengthened Judah and supported Rehoboam son of Solomon for 3 years. 📖

For those 3 years they followed David’s and Solomon’s way.

## 2 Chronicles 11:18

Rehoboam married Mahalath, daughter of David’s son Jerimoth and Abihail, daughter of Jesse’s son Eliab. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:19

She bore Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:20

Next he married Maacah daughter of Absalom. 📖

She bore Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.

## 2 Chronicles 11:21

He loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and concubines. 📖

He had 18 wives, 60 concubines, 28 sons, and 60 daughters.

**Help with concubines:** Women in recognised relationships with a man, with lower status than wives.

## 2 Chronicles 11:22

He made Maacah’s son Abijah chief prince among his brothers, intending him to become king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:23

He wisely spread sons throughout Judah’s and Benjamin’s districts and defended cities. 📖

He gave them plentiful supplies and sought many wives for them.
