# 2 Chronicles 22 — Ahaziah’s death and Joash’s rescue

**About this chapter**

Ahaziah follows Ahab’s family and dies during Jehu’s judgment. | Athaliah destroys royal heirs. | Joash is rescued and hidden.

## 2 Chronicles 22:1

Jerusalem made Jehoram’s youngest son Ahaziah king of Judah. 📖

Raiders with the Arabs had killed all older sons.

## 2 Chronicles 22:2

He became king at 22 and ruled in Jerusalem for 1 year. 📖

His mother was Athaliah, Omri’s granddaughter.

## 2 Chronicles 22:3

He followed Ahab’s family because his mother advised him in wickedness. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:4

He did evil before the LORD like Ahab’s family. 📖

They advised him after his father’s death, leading to his destruction.

## 2 Chronicles 22:5

Following their advice, he joined Joram son of Ahab, Israel’s king, against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead. 📖

Aram wounded Joram.

## 2 Chronicles 22:6

Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from wounds received at Ramah fighting Hazael king of Aram. 📖

Ahaziah son of Jehoram, Judah’s king, visited wounded Joram son of Ahab there.

## 2 Chronicles 22:7

God brought Ahaziah’s downfall through this visit. 📖

He joined Joram to meet Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the LORD anointed to destroy Ahab’s family.

## 2 Chronicles 22:8

While judging Ahab’s family, Jehu found and killed Judah’s rulers and Ahaziah’s brothers’ sons who served him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:9

Jehu’s soldiers found Ahaziah hiding in Samaria and brought him to Jehu to be killed. 📖

They buried him as Jehoshaphat’s grandson, because Jehoshaphat sought the LORD wholeheartedly.

Nobody in Ahaziah’s family remained strong enough to rule.

## 2 Chronicles 22:10

Ahaziah’s mother Athaliah saw her son dead and began destroying Judah’s royal heirs. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:11

Jehoshabeath, King Jehoram’s daughter, rescued Joash son of Ahaziah from the sons being murdered. 📖

She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom.

She was Ahaziah’s sister and Jehoiada the priest’s wife.

She hid Joash from Athaliah, saving him.

## 2 Chronicles 22:12

Joash hid with them in God’s house for 6 years while Athaliah ruled. 📖
