# 2 Chronicles 26 — Uzziah’s strength and pride

**About this chapter**

Uzziah becomes powerful with God’s help. | He enters the temple to burn incense despite the priests’ warning. | He develops leprosy and lives separately until death.

## 2 Chronicles 26:1

All Judah made 16-year-old Uzziah king instead of his father Amaziah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:2

He rebuilt Eloth and restored it to Judah after Amaziah died. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:3

Uzziah became king at 16 and ruled in Jerusalem for 52 years. 📖

His mother was Jecoliah from Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 26:4

He did right before the LORD like Amaziah his father. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:5

He sought God while Zechariah instructed him in fear of God. 📖

As long as he sought the LORD, God gave success.

## 2 Chronicles 26:6

He fought the Philistines and demolished Gath’s, Jabneh’s, and Ashdod’s walls. 📖

He built cities near Ashdod and among Philistines.

## 2 Chronicles 26:7

God helped against Philistines, Gur-baal’s Arabs, and Meunites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:8

Ammon paid tribute, and his fame reached Egypt’s border because he grew very powerful. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:9

He fortified towers in Jerusalem at Corner Gate, Valley Gate, and the wall’s angle. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:10

He built desert towers and many cisterns for plentiful livestock in foothills and plains. 📖

Loving the soil, he employed farmers and vine workers in hills and fertile fields.

**Help with cisterns:** Places built to store water.

## 2 Chronicles 26:11

His ready army served by divisions recorded by Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer under royal official Hananiah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:12

His brave warriors’ family heads numbered 2,600. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:13

They led 307,500 trained soldiers, a strong force supporting him against enemies. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:14

He supplied shields, spears, helmets, armour, bows, and slingstones to the whole army. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:15

He made devices in Jerusalem to shoot arrows and throw large stones from towers and corners. 📖

Great help made him powerful and famous everywhere.

## 2 Chronicles 26:16

Power made him proud, leading to destruction. 📖

He betrayed the LORD by entering his temple to burn incense on the incense altar.

## 2 Chronicles 26:17

Azariah the priest and 80 brave priests followed. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:18

They warned, “Uzziah, only Aaron’s consecrated priestly descendants may burn incense to the LORD. 📖

Leave the sanctuary.

You acted unfaithfully and will gain no honour from the LORD God.”

## 2 Chronicles 26:19

He raged at the priests, holding an incense burner. 📖

Before them in the LORD’s house by the incense altar, leprosy broke out on his forehead.

**Help with leprosy:** This Bible term covers serious skin diseases.

## 2 Chronicles 26:20

Chief priest Azariah and the priests saw it and rushed him out. 📖

He hurried too, because the LORD afflicted him.

## 2 Chronicles 26:21

He had leprosy until death, living separately and excluded from the LORD’s house. 📖

His son Jotham managed the palace and governed the people.

**About this illness:** The account describes a particular divine judgment.

It does not say all skin disease results from wrongdoing.

## 2 Chronicles 26:22

Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet recorded Uzziah’s other acts from beginning to end. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:23

He died and was buried near his ancestors in the kings’ burial field because people said he had leprosy. 📖

His son Jotham became king.
