# 2 Chronicles 28 — Ahaz’s unfaithfulness and Judah’s suffering

**About this chapter**

Ahaz follows other gods, and Judah suffers invasion. | Oded warns Israel to return their captives. | Ahaz seeks Assyria’s help and becomes more unfaithful.

## 2 Chronicles 28:1

Ahaz became king at 20 and ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years. 📖

Unlike David, he did not do right before the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 28:2

He followed Israel’s kings and cast Baal images. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:3

He burned incense in Ben-hinnom Valley and sacrificed his sons in fire. 📖

He followed detestable practices of nations the LORD drove out before Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 28:4

He sacrificed and burned incense at high places, on hills, and under every green tree. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:5

The LORD his God handed him to Aram’s king, who took many captives to Damascus. 📖

Israel’s king also struck him a great blow.

## 2 Chronicles 28:6

Pekah son of Remaliah killed 120,000 brave Judah men in one day because they abandoned their ancestors’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:7

Zichri, a mighty Ephraimite, killed the king’s son Maaseiah, palace governor Azrikam, and Elkanah, second to the king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:8

Israel took 200,000 captive relatives, women, sons, and daughters, and much plunder to Samaria. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:9

Oded, a LORD’s prophet, met the returning army at Samaria. 📖

“The LORD, your ancestors’ God, angrily handed Judah to you.

But your killing rage reaches heaven.

## 2 Chronicles 28:10

Now you would enslave Judah’s and Jerusalem’s men and women. 📖

Are you not guilty before the LORD too?

## 2 Chronicles 28:11

Return your captive relatives, because his fierce anger is on you.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:12

Ephraimite leaders opposed the returning army. 📖

They were Azariah son of Jehohanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai.

## 2 Chronicles 28:13

“Do not bring captives here and add to our guilt and sins before the LORD. 📖

Our guilt is great, and fierce anger rests on Israel.”

## 2 Chronicles 28:14

The soldiers left captives and plunder before leaders and assembly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:15

The named men clothed the naked from the plunder, giving sandals, food, and drink. 📖

They treated wounds with oil and put weak people on donkeys.

They brought them to their relatives in Jericho, the City of Palms, then returned to Samaria.

## 2 Chronicles 28:16

Ahaz asked Assyria’s king for help. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:17

Edom again attacked Judah and took captives. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:18

Philistines raided Judah’s foothills and Negev. 📖

They captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Soco, Timnah, and Gimzo with their villages and settled there.

## 2 Chronicles 28:19

The LORD humbled Judah because Ahaz king of Israel cast off restraint in Judah and greatly betrayed the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:20

Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria troubled Ahaz instead of strengthening him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:21

Ahaz gave him part of the LORD’s house’s, palace’s, and princes’ possessions, but it did not help. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:22

In distress, Ahaz became even more unfaithful. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:23

He sacrificed to Damascus’s gods, who defeated him. 📖

“Aram’s kings’ gods helped them, so I will sacrifice for their help.”

These gods ruined Ahaz and all Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 28:24

He cut God’s house’s objects to pieces, shut its doors, and built altars at every Jerusalem street corner. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:25

He built high places for incense to other gods in every Judah city, angering the LORD, his ancestors’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:26

His other acts and ways are in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:27

He died and was buried in Jerusalem, outside Israel’s kings’ tombs. 📖

His son Hezekiah became king.
