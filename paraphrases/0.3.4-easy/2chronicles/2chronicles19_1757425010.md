# 2 Chronicles 19 — Jehoshaphat appoints judges

**About this chapter**

Jehu rebukes Jehoshaphat’s alliance but recognises good in him. | Jehoshaphat turns people to the LORD and appoints judges. | He instructs them to judge faithfully and fairly.

## 2 Chronicles 19:1

Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned safely home to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:2

Jehu son of Hanani the seer confronted him. 📖

“Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD?

His wrath is on you because of this.

## 2 Chronicles 19:3

But there is good in you. 📖

You removed Asherah poles and determined to seek God.”

## 2 Chronicles 19:4

Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem. 📖

He again visited people from Beersheba to Ephraim’s hills, turning them to the LORD, their ancestors’ God.

## 2 Chronicles 19:5

He appointed judges in all Judah’s defended cities. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:6

He said, “Judge carefully. 📖

You judge for the LORD, not people, and he is with you in judgment.

## 2 Chronicles 19:7

Fear the LORD and act carefully. 📖

Our God has no injustice, favouritism, or bribery.”

## 2 Chronicles 19:8

He appointed Levites, priests, and Israelite family heads in Jerusalem to judge for the LORD and settle disputes. 📖

They lived there.

## 2 Chronicles 19:9

He commanded, “Serve faithfully and wholeheartedly in fear of the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:10

Warn your brothers in every case from their cities, whether bloodshed or violations of commands, laws, or rules. 📖

Keep them from guilt before the LORD and wrath on you and them.

Do this, and you will not incur guilt.

## 2 Chronicles 19:11

Chief priest Amariah leads you in the LORD’s matters. 📖

Zebadiah son of Ishmael, Judah’s ruler, leads in royal matters.

Levites serve as officers.

Act firmly; may the LORD be with the upright!”
