# 2 Chronicles 6 — Solomon’s dedication prayer

**About this chapter**

Solomon blesses Israel and recalls the LORD’s promise. | He prays for justice, forgiveness, and help, including for foreigners. | His requests name the circumstances and responses involved.

## 2 Chronicles 6:1

Solomon declared, “The LORD said he would dwell in the thick cloud. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:2

I built you an exalted house to dwell in forever.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:3

All Israel stood as the king turned and blessed them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:4

He said, “Bless the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

His hand fulfilled what his mouth promised my father David:

## 2 Chronicles 6:5

‘Since bringing Israel from Egypt, I chose no city from any tribe to build a house for my Name. 📖

I chose nobody to rule my people Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 6:6

Now I chose Jerusalem for my Name and David to rule Israel.’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:7

My father David wanted to build a house for the Name of the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:8

The LORD told David, ‘Your desire to build for my Name is good. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:9

You will not build it. 📖

Your own son will build the house for my Name.’

## 2 Chronicles 6:10

The LORD fulfilled his word. 📖

I succeeded my father David on Israel’s throne as promised.

I built the house for the Name of the LORD, Israel’s God.

## 2 Chronicles 6:11

I placed the ark there, containing the LORD’s covenant with Israel.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:12

Solomon stood before the LORD’s altar facing all Israel and spread his hands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:13

He made a bronze platform 5 cubits long, 5 wide, and 3 high in the courtyard’s middle. 📖

He stood on it, knelt before everyone, and spread his hands towards heaven.

**Help with cubit:** An ancient unit roughly a forearm’s length.

## 2 Chronicles 6:14

“LORD, Israel’s God, none in heaven or earth is like you. 📖

You keep your covenant of faithful love with servants who follow you wholeheartedly.

## 2 Chronicles 6:15

You kept your promise to your servant, my father David. 📖

Your hand fulfilled today what your mouth spoke.

## 2 Chronicles 6:16

LORD, Israel’s God, keep your promise to my father David. 📖

You promised an unbroken line before you on Israel’s throne if his descendants carefully followed your Law as he did.

## 2 Chronicles 6:17

LORD, Israel’s God, confirm your promise to your servant David. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:18

Will God truly dwell with people on earth? 📖

Even the highest heavens cannot contain you, much less this temple I built.

## 2 Chronicles 6:19

LORD my God, attend to your servant’s prayer and plea. 📖

Hear my cry and prayer before you.

## 2 Chronicles 6:20

Watch this temple day and night, where you promised your Name would be. 📖

Hear your servant’s prayer towards this place.

## 2 Chronicles 6:21

Hear your servant’s and Israel’s pleas when they pray towards this place. 📖

Hear from heaven, your home, and forgive.

## 2 Chronicles 6:22

When a man sins against a neighbour and must swear an oath, he may come before your altar here, 📖

**Help with oath:** A serious sworn statement.

## 2 Chronicles 6:23

Then hear from heaven and judge your servants. 📖

Condemn the wicked by bringing their actions on their own heads.

Justify the righteous by rewarding their righteousness.

**Help with justify:** Declare someone to be in the right.

## 2 Chronicles 6:24

Suppose Israel is defeated because they sinned against you. 📖

They return, confess your name, and pray and plead before you in this temple.

## 2 Chronicles 6:25

Then hear from heaven and forgive Israel’s sin. 📖

Restore them to the land you gave them and their ancestors.

## 2 Chronicles 6:26

Suppose the sky is shut without rain because they sinned against you. 📖

They pray towards this place, confess your name, and turn from sin because you afflicted them.

## 2 Chronicles 6:27

Then hear from heaven and forgive your servants Israel. 📖

Teach them the good way to live.

Send rain on the land you gave as their inheritance.

## 2 Chronicles 6:28

Suppose famine, plague, blight, mildew, locusts, grasshoppers, enemy siege, or any sickness comes on the land. 📖

**Help with these words:** Famine is severe food shortage.

Blight and mildew damage crops.

## 2 Chronicles 6:29

Each Israelite knows their own suffering and spreads hands towards this temple. 📖

Whatever prayer or plea they make,

## 2 Chronicles 6:30

Hear from heaven, your home. 📖

Forgive and repay everyone according to their ways.

You alone know human hearts.

## 2 Chronicles 6:31

Let them fear you and follow your ways throughout their lives in the land you gave our ancestors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:32

A foreigner may come from far away because of your great name, mighty hand, and outstretched arm. 📖

This person is not from Israel but comes and prays towards this temple.

**Help with hand and arm:** These images describe God’s power.

## 2 Chronicles 6:33

Hear from heaven and do all the foreigner asks. 📖

Let all earth’s peoples know and fear your name, like Israel.

Let them know this house I built is called by your Name.

## 2 Chronicles 6:34

Suppose your people fight enemies wherever you send them. 📖

They pray towards your chosen city and this house I built for your Name.

## 2 Chronicles 6:35

Then hear their prayers and pleas from heaven and uphold their cause. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:36

Suppose they sin against you, as everyone does. 📖

You become angry and hand them to enemies who take them captive far or near.

## 2 Chronicles 6:37

Suppose they come to their senses in captivity, turn from sin, and plead with you. 📖

They say, ‘We sinned and did wrong.

We acted wickedly.’

## 2 Chronicles 6:38

Suppose they return wholeheartedly to you in their captors’ land. 📖

They pray towards their ancestors’ land, your chosen city, and this house I built for your Name.

## 2 Chronicles 6:39

Then hear their prayers and pleas from heaven, your home. 📖

Uphold their cause and forgive your people’s sins against you.

## 2 Chronicles 6:40

My God, open your eyes and listen to prayer in this place. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:41

Arise, LORD God, and enter your resting place with the ark of your might. 📖

Clothe your priests with salvation.

Let your faithful people rejoice in goodness.

**Help with clothed with salvation:** This pictures being surrounded by God’s rescue.

## 2 Chronicles 6:42

LORD God, do not reject your anointed one. 📖

Remember your faithful love to David your servant.”

**Help with anointed one:** Someone appointed to serve, marked by pouring oil.
