# 2 Chronicles 10 — Israel rejects Rehoboam

**About this chapter**

The people ask Rehoboam to reduce their burden. | He rejects the elders’ advice and threatens harsher treatment. | Israel rebels against David’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 10:1

Rehoboam went to Shechem, where all Israel gathered to make him king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:2

Jeroboam son of Nebat heard and returned from Egypt, where he fled King Solomon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:3

They sent for Jeroboam. 📖

He and all Israel told Rehoboam,

## 2 Chronicles 10:4

“Your father put a heavy yoke on us. 📖

Lighten his harsh service and heavy yoke, and we will serve you.”

**Help with yoke:** A load-bearing frame used on animals.

Here it pictures burdens imposed on people.

## 2 Chronicles 10:5

Rehoboam told them to return in 3 days. 📖

They left.

## 2 Chronicles 10:6

He asked elders who served his father Solomon, “How should I answer these people?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:7

They answered, “Be kind and speak pleasing, kind words. 📖

They will serve you forever.”

## 2 Chronicles 10:8

Rehoboam rejected their advice and asked young men who grew up with him and served him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:9

He asked, “How should we answer those asking me to lighten my father’s yoke?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:10

They said, “Those people want your father’s heavy yoke lightened. 📖

Tell them, ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist!

**Help with this image:** He boasts that his rule will be harsher than his father’s.

## 2 Chronicles 10:11

My father gave you a heavy yoke; I will add more. 📖

He whipped you; I will whip you with scorpions.’”

**Help with scorpions:** This threatening image suggests even harsher punishment.

## 2 Chronicles 10:12

Jeroboam and all the people returned after 3 days, as Rehoboam ordered. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:13

Rehoboam answered harshly, rejecting the elders’ advice. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:14

He followed the young men’s advice. 📖

“I will add to my father’s heavy yoke.

He whipped you; I will whip you with scorpions.”

## 2 Chronicles 10:15

The king refused to listen. 📖

God caused this turn to fulfil the LORD’s word through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

## 2 Chronicles 10:16

Israel saw he would not listen. 📖

“What share have we in David, or inheritance in Jesse’s son?

To your tents, Israel!

Look after your own house, David!”

All Israel went home.

**Help with tents:** Here the call means return home.

## 2 Chronicles 10:17

Rehoboam ruled the Israelites living in Judah’s cities. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:18

Rehoboam sent Hadoram, head of forced labour. 📖

Israel stoned him to death.

Rehoboam hurried onto his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 10:19

Israel remains in rebellion against David’s house to this day. 📖

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.
