# 2 Chronicles 30 — Hezekiah invites Israel to Passover

**About this chapter**

Hezekiah invites Israel and Judah to keep Passover in Jerusalem. | Some mock the invitation; others come. | He prays for those not purified, and the LORD hears.

## 2 Chronicles 30:1

Hezekiah invited all Israel and Judah, writing Ephraim and Manasseh to keep the LORD, Israel’s God’s Passover in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:2

King, officials, and Jerusalem’s assembly agreed to keep Passover in month 2. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:3

Too few priests were consecrated and the people had not gathered, so they could not keep its regular time. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:4

The plan pleased king and assembly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:5

They announced throughout Israel from Beersheba to Dan that all should keep the LORD’s Passover in Jerusalem. 📖

It had not been kept by great numbers as required.

## 2 Chronicles 30:6

Royal couriers carried letters through Israel and Judah. 📖

“Return to the LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel.

May he return to you who escaped Assyria’s kings.

## 2 Chronicles 30:7

Do not imitate unfaithful ancestors and brothers whom the LORD made horrifying examples, as you see. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:8

Do not stiffen your necks like your ancestors. 📖

Submit, come to his forever-consecrated sanctuary, and serve the LORD your God to turn away his anger.

**Help with stiffen your necks:** Stubbornly refuse to obey.

## 2 Chronicles 30:9

If you return, your brothers and sons will receive captors’ mercy and return here. 📖

The LORD your God is gracious and merciful.

He will not turn his face away if you return.”

## 2 Chronicles 30:10

Couriers went through Ephraim and Manasseh to Zebulun, but people mocked them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:11

Some from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:12

God gave Judah one heart to obey the king’s and officials’ command according to the LORD’s word. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:13

A vast assembly gathered in Jerusalem in month 2 for Unleavened Bread. 📖

**Help with Unleavened Bread:** The feast with bread made without yeast.

## 2 Chronicles 30:14

They removed Jerusalem’s altars and incense altars, throwing them into Kidron Valley. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:15

In month 2, day 14, they killed the Passover lamb. 📖

Ashamed priests and Levites consecrated themselves and brought burnt offerings.

## 2 Chronicles 30:16

They served at their posts according to Moses the man of God’s Law. 📖

Priests splashed blood received from Levites.

## 2 Chronicles 30:17

Many were not consecrated, so Levites killed Passover lambs for each unclean person, consecrating the lambs to the LORD. 📖

**Help with unclean:** Not meeting ritual preparation requirements, not a statement about a person’s worth.

## 2 Chronicles 30:18

Many from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun ate Passover without purification, contrary to the written rules. 📖

Hezekiah prayed that the good LORD would provide atonement for everyone

## 2 Chronicles 30:19

who set their heart to seek their ancestors’ God, even without the sanctuary’s required purification. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:20

The LORD heard Hezekiah and healed the people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:21

Israel in Jerusalem celebrated Unleavened Bread joyfully for 7 days. 📖

Priests and Levites praised the LORD daily with loud instruments.

## 2 Chronicles 30:22

Hezekiah encouraged the skilled Levites. 📖

For 7 days they ate their portions, sacrificed peace offerings, and thanked the LORD, their ancestors’ God.

## 2 Chronicles 30:23

Everyone agreed to celebrate another 7 days, keeping them joyfully. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:24

Hezekiah gave 1,000 bulls and 7,000 sheep; officials gave 1,000 bulls and 10,000 sheep. 📖

Many priests consecrated themselves.

## 2 Chronicles 30:25

Judah, priests, Levites, the assembly from Israel, and foreigners from Israel and Judah rejoiced. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:26

Jerusalem had not seen rejoicing like this since Solomon son of David, Israel’s king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:27

Priests and Levites blessed the people. 📖

God heard their voices and prayer in his holy heavenly home.
