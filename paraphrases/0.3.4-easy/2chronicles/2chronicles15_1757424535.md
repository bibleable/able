# 2 Chronicles 15 — Asa and the covenant

**About this chapter**

Azariah urges Asa and the people to seek the LORD. | They renew their covenant and remove idols. | The account also records a death penalty for those refusing to seek God.

## 2 Chronicles 15:1

God’s Spirit came on Azariah son of Oded. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:2

He told Asa, Judah, and Benjamin, “The LORD is with you when you are with him. 📖

If you seek him, you will find him.

If you abandon him, he will abandon you.

## 2 Chronicles 15:3

For many years Israel lacked the true God, a teaching priest, and the Law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:4

In distress, they sought the LORD, Israel’s God, and found him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:5

Travellers were unsafe because the lands’ inhabitants had many conflicts. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:6

Nations and cities crushed one another because God afflicted them with every kind of trouble. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:7

Be strong and do not lose heart. 📖

Your work will be rewarded.”

## 2 Chronicles 15:8

Asa heard Azariah son of Oded’s words and prophecy and took courage. 📖

He removed detestable idols throughout Judah, Benjamin, and captured cities in Ephraim’s hills.

He restored the LORD’s altar before the temple porch.

## 2 Chronicles 15:9

He gathered Judah, Benjamin, and settlers from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon. 📖

Many from Israel had joined him because they saw the LORD his God was with him.

## 2 Chronicles 15:10

They gathered in Jerusalem in Asa’s 15th year, month 3. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:11

They sacrificed 700 oxen and 7,000 sheep to the LORD from their captured goods. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:12

They made a covenant to seek the LORD, their ancestors’ God, wholeheartedly. 📖

**Help with covenant:** A binding agreement.

## 2 Chronicles 15:13

Anyone refusing to seek the LORD, Israel’s God, would be killed, young or old, male or female. 📖

**About this penalty:** This records the covenant’s penalty in the account, not an instruction to readers.

## 2 Chronicles 15:14

They swore to the LORD loudly, with shouts, trumpets, and ram’s horns. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:15

All Judah rejoiced in their wholehearted oath. 📖

They sought him earnestly and found him.

The LORD gave rest on every side.

## 2 Chronicles 15:16

Asa removed his grandmother Maacah as queen mother because she made a detestable Asherah pole. 📖

He chopped, crushed, and burned it in Kidron Valley.

**Help with Asherah pole:** An object associated with worship of the goddess Asherah.

## 2 Chronicles 15:17

Israel’s high places remained, but Asa’s heart was fully devoted throughout his life. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:18

He brought his and his father’s dedicated silver, gold, and objects into God’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:19

There was no war until Asa’s 35th year. 📖
