# 2 Chronicles 8 — Solomon’s projects and worship arrangements

**About this chapter**

Solomon builds cities and organises labour and worship. | The account distinguishes forced labourers from Israelite officials and soldiers. | It ends with a voyage to Ophir.

## 2 Chronicles 8:1

Solomon spent 20 years building the LORD’s house and his palace. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:2

He rebuilt cities Hiram gave him and settled Israelites there. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:3

He attacked and captured Hamath-zobah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:4

He built Tadmor in the wilderness and storage cities in Hamath. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:5

He rebuilt Upper and Lower Beth-horon with walls, gates, and bars, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:6

and Baalath, all his storage cities, and cities for chariots and horses. 📖

He built whatever he desired in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his kingdom.

## 2 Chronicles 8:7

The remaining Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites were not Israelites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:8

Solomon required their descendants whom Israel had not destroyed to do forced labour. 📖

They remain forced labourers to this day.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Chronicles 8:9

He did not make Israelites slave labourers. 📖

They were soldiers, chief captains, and commanders of chariots and cavalry.

## 2 Chronicles 8:10

They included 250 chief supervisors for Solomon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:11

Solomon moved Pharaoh’s daughter from the City of David to her palace. 📖

“My wife must not live in David king of Israel’s house.

Places the LORD’s ark entered are holy.”

## 2 Chronicles 8:12

Solomon offered burnt offerings on the LORD’s altar before the porch. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:13

He kept Moses’s daily offering requirements for Sabbaths, New Moons, and 3 annual feasts. 📖

These were Unleavened Bread, Weeks, and Tabernacles.

**Help with these feasts:** Unleavened Bread uses bread without yeast.

Weeks celebrates harvest; Tabernacles recalls living in shelters.

## 2 Chronicles 8:14

Following his father David’s rules, Solomon assigned priestly divisions and Levites to their duties. 📖

They praised and served before priests as each day required.

He assigned gatekeepers by division to every gate, as David the man of God commanded.

## 2 Chronicles 8:15

They obeyed all the king’s commands about priests, Levites, and treasuries. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:16

All Solomon’s work was carried through from the LORD’s house’s foundation to its completion. 📖

The LORD’s house was finished.

## 2 Chronicles 8:17

Solomon went to Ezion-geber and Eloth on Edom’s coast. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:18

Hiram sent ships captained by his servants with experienced sailors. 📖

They went with Solomon’s servants to Ophir and brought Solomon 450 talents of gold.

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.
