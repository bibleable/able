# 2 Chronicles 2 — Materials and workers for the temple

**About this chapter**

Solomon asks Hiram of Tyre for timber and skilled help. | Their messages describe the temple’s purpose and the agreed supplies. | Foreign residents are assigned building work.

## 2 Chronicles 2:1

Solomon decided to build a house for the LORD’s Name and a royal palace for himself. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:2

He conscripted 70,000 carriers, 80,000 mountain stonecutters, and 3,600 supervisors. 📖

**Help with conscripted:** Required people to serve.

## 2 Chronicles 2:3

He told Hiram king of Tyre, “Send me cedar as you did for my father David’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:4

I will dedicate a house to the LORD my God’s Name. 📖

We will burn fragrant incense and continually display showbread there.

Burnt offerings will be given morning and evening, on Sabbaths, New Moons, and the LORD our God’s appointed feasts.

This is required for Israel forever.

**Help with these words:** Showbread is bread set before God.

Sabbath is the weekly rest day; New Moon marks a month’s beginning.

## 2 Chronicles 2:5

The house will be great because our God is greater than all gods. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:6

Who can build a house for him when even the highest heavens cannot contain him? 📖

Who am I to build for him except a place to burn sacrifices before him?

## 2 Chronicles 2:7

Send a skilled engraver working with gold, silver, bronze, iron, and purple, crimson, and blue yarn. 📖

He will join craftsmen my father David provided in Judah and Jerusalem.

**Help with engraver:** A worker who cuts designs into materials.

## 2 Chronicles 2:8

Send cedar, cypress, and algum logs from Lebanon. 📖

Your servants know how to cut its timber.

Mine will work with yours

## 2 Chronicles 2:9

preparing plentiful timber for the great, wonderful temple I am building. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:10

I will pay your woodcutters 20,000 cors of ground wheat and 20,000 cors of barley. 📖

I will also pay 20,000 baths of wine and 20,000 baths of olive oil.”

**Help with cor and bath:** Units used to measure supplies.

## 2 Chronicles 2:11

Hiram king of Tyre replied by letter, “The LORD loves his people, so he made you their king.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:12

Hiram added, “Bless the LORD, Israel’s God, who made heaven and earth! 📖

He gave David a wise son with insight and understanding to build the LORD’s temple and his own palace.

## 2 Chronicles 2:13

I am sending Huram-abi, a skilled, creative man. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:14

His mother is from Dan and his father from Tyre. 📖

He works in gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, wood, purple, blue, and crimson yarn, and fine linen.

He knows every kind of engraving and can carry out any design given him.

He will work with your craftsmen and those of my lord, your father David.

## 2 Chronicles 2:15

Send your promised wheat, barley, olive oil, and wine to your servants. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:16

We will cut all the Lebanon timber you need and float it by sea in rafts to Joppa. 📖

You can take it to Jerusalem.”

## 2 Chronicles 2:17

Solomon counted Israel’s foreign men following his father David’s census. 📖

There were 153,600.

## 2 Chronicles 2:18

He made 70,000 carriers, 80,000 mountain stonecutters, and 3,600 supervisors. 📖
