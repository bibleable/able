# 2 Chronicles 5 — The ark enters the temple

**About this chapter**

The finished temple receives the ark and sacred furnishings. | Priests and musicians praise the LORD. | A cloud fills God’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 5:1

Solomon finished all the LORD’s house’s work. 📖

He brought David’s dedicated silver, gold, and furnishings into God’s house’s treasuries.

## 2 Chronicles 5:2

He gathered Israel’s elders, tribal heads, and family leaders in Jerusalem. 📖

They would bring the LORD’s covenant ark from Zion, the City of David.

## 2 Chronicles 5:3

All Israel’s men gathered to the king at the feast in month 7. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:4

All Israel’s elders arrived, and the Levites lifted the ark. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:5

The Levitical priests carried the ark, Tent of Meeting, and all its sacred furnishings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:6

Solomon and all assembled Israel sacrificed countless sheep and oxen before the ark. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:7

The priests placed the LORD’s covenant ark in the inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place, beneath the cherubim’s wings. 📖

**Help with cherubim:** Heavenly beings represented by winged figures.

## 2 Chronicles 5:8

The cherubim’s wings spread over and sheltered the ark and poles. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:9

The poles’ ends were visible from in front of the inner sanctuary but not outside the Holy Place. 📖

They remain there to this day.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Chronicles 5:10

The ark held only the 2 tablets Moses placed there at Horeb. 📖

The LORD made his covenant with Israel there after they left Egypt.

**Help with covenant:** A binding agreement or promise.

## 2 Chronicles 5:11

All priests present consecrated themselves regardless of division. 📖

They came from the Holy Place.

**Help with consecrated:** Prepared and set apart for sacred service.

## 2 Chronicles 5:12

All Levite singers, Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, their sons, and relatives, stood east of the altar in fine linen. 📖

They played cymbals, harps, and lyres with 120 priests blowing trumpets.

## 2 Chronicles 5:13

Trumpeters and singers united to thank and praise the LORD with one voice. 📖

With trumpets, cymbals, and instruments they sang, “He is good; his faithful love lasts forever.”

A cloud filled the LORD’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 5:14

The cloud prevented the priests from standing to serve. 📖

The LORD’s glory filled God’s house.
