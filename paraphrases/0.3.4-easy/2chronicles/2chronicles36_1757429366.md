# 2 Chronicles 36 — Exile and Cyrus’s proclamation

**About this chapter**

Judah’s last kings refuse the LORD’s warnings. | Babylon destroys Jerusalem and takes survivors away. | Cyrus later calls the LORD’s people to go to Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 36:1

The people made Josiah’s son Jehoahaz king in Jerusalem instead of his father. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:2

Jehoahaz became king at 23 and ruled in Jerusalem for 3 months. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:3

Egypt’s king removed him and demanded 100 silver talents and 1 gold talent from Judah. 📖

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.

## 2 Chronicles 36:4

Neco king of Egypt made Jehoahaz’s brother Eliakim king over Judah and Jerusalem and renamed him Jehoiakim. 📖

He took Eliakim’s brother Jehoahaz to Egypt.

## 2 Chronicles 36:5

Jehoiakim became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. 📖

He did evil before the LORD his God.

## 2 Chronicles 36:6

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon attacked and bound him in bronze chains to take to Babylon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:7

He took some objects from the LORD’s house to his Babylonian temple. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:8

Jehoiakim’s other acts, detestable deeds, and charges against him are in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. 📖

His son Jehoiachin became king.

## 2 Chronicles 36:9

Jehoiachin became king at 18 and ruled in Jerusalem for 3 months and 10 days. 📖

He did evil before the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 36:10

In spring, Nebuchadnezzar summoned him to Babylon with valuable temple objects. 📖

He made Jehoiachin’s relative Zedekiah king over Judah and Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 36:11

Zedekiah became king at 21 and ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:12

He did evil before the LORD and did not humble himself before Jeremiah the prophet speaking for the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:13

He rebelled against Nebuchadnezzar despite swearing to him by God. 📖

He stiffened his neck and hardened his heart against returning to the LORD, Israel’s God.

**Help with these images:** They describe stubborn refusal to obey.

## 2 Chronicles 36:14

Priestly leaders and people multiplied unfaithfulness, following nations’ detestable practices and defiling the LORD’s consecrated house in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:15

The LORD, their ancestors’ God, repeatedly sent messengers because he pitied his people and dwelling. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:16

They mocked messengers, despised his words, and scoffed at prophets until the LORD’s wrath rose beyond remedy. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:17

He brought the Chaldeans’ king, who killed young men in the sanctuary. 📖

He spared neither young men, young women, elderly people, nor infirm people.

God handed everyone to Nebuchadnezzar,

## 2 Chronicles 36:18

who took every large and small temple object and all temple, royal, and officials’ treasures to Babylon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:19

Chaldeans burned God’s house, demolished Jerusalem’s wall, burned every palace, and destroyed all valuables. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:20

Nebuchadnezzar exiled survivors to Babylon as servants of him and his sons until Persia’s rule. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:21

The desolate land enjoyed Sabbath rest until 70 years passed, fulfilling the LORD’s word through Jeremiah. 📖

**Help with Sabbath rest:** The abandoned land rested from cultivation.

## 2 Chronicles 36:22

In Cyrus king of Persia’s 1st year, the LORD stirred him to fulfil Jeremiah’s prophecy. 📖

He announced and wrote this throughout his kingdom:

## 2 Chronicles 36:23

“Cyrus king of Persia says the LORD, heaven’s God, gave me all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

He appointed me to build him a house in Jerusalem, Judah.

Let everyone belonging to his people go up, and may the LORD their God be with them.”
