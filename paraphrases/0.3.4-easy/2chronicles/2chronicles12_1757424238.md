# 2 Chronicles 12 — Rehoboam and Shishak

**About this chapter**

Rehoboam abandons the LORD’s Law. | Egypt attacks, and Judah’s leaders humble themselves. | They are spared complete destruction but become Shishak’s servants.

## 2 Chronicles 12:1

When Rehoboam secured royal power, he and all Israel with him abandoned the LORD’s Law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:2

Because they were unfaithful, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem in Rehoboam’s 5th year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:3

He brought 1,200 chariots, 60,000 horsemen, and countless Libyan, Sukkite, and Cushite troops from Egypt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:4

He captured Judah’s defended cities and reached Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:5

Shemaiah the prophet addressed Rehoboam and Judah’s leaders gathered in Jerusalem because of Shishak. 📖

“The LORD says, ‘You abandoned me, so I abandoned you to Shishak.’”

## 2 Chronicles 12:6

The king and Israel’s leaders humbled themselves. 📖

“The LORD is righteous.”

## 2 Chronicles 12:7

The LORD saw their humility and told Shemaiah, “I will not destroy them. 📖

I will soon rescue them.

My wrath will not pour on Jerusalem through Shishak.

## 2 Chronicles 12:8

They will still serve him, learning the difference between serving me and other lands’ kings.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:9

Shishak king of Egypt took all temple and palace treasures in Jerusalem, including Solomon’s gold shields. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:10

Rehoboam replaced them with bronze shields under palace-entrance guard captains’ care. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:11

Whenever the king entered the LORD’s house, guards carried the shields with him, then returned them to the guardroom. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:12

Rehoboam humbled himself, so the LORD’s anger turned away without destroying him completely. 📖

Conditions were good in Judah.

## 2 Chronicles 12:13

Rehoboam secured his reign in Jerusalem. 📖

He became king at 41 and ruled for 17 years.

The LORD chose Jerusalem from all Israel’s tribes for his Name.

Rehoboam’s mother was Naamah the Ammonite.

## 2 Chronicles 12:14

He did evil because he did not set his heart to seek the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:15

His acts from first to last are in Shemaiah the Prophet’s and Iddo the Seer’s records concerning genealogies. 📖

Rehoboam and Jeroboam fought throughout their lives.

**Help with genealogies:** Family records.

## 2 Chronicles 12:16

Rehoboam died and was buried in the City of David. 📖

His son Abijah became king.
