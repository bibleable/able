# 2 Chronicles 31 — Worship and support for priests and Levites

**About this chapter**

The people destroy false-worship objects and return home. | Hezekiah assigns duties and contributions. | Named officials distribute supplies to priests, Levites, and their families.

## 2 Chronicles 31:1

Israel’s assembled people destroyed pillars, Asherah poles, high places, and altars throughout Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh. 📖

They returned to their cities and property.

## 2 Chronicles 31:2

Hezekiah restored priestly and Levite divisions for burnt and peace offerings, service, thanks, and praise at the LORD’s dwelling’s gates. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:3

He gave his possessions for morning, evening, Sabbath, New Moon, and feast burnt offerings according to the LORD’s Law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:4

He ordered Jerusalem to support priests and Levites so they could devote themselves to the LORD’s Law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:5

Israel generously brought firstfruits of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and all field produce. 📖

They brought abundant tithes of everything.

**Help with these words:** Firstfruits are the first produce.

A tithe is one-tenth.

## 2 Chronicles 31:6

Israelites and Judahites in Judah’s cities brought tithes of herds, flocks, and holy things consecrated to the LORD. 📖

They piled them in heaps.

## 2 Chronicles 31:7

They began heaps in month 3 and finished in month 7. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:8

Hezekiah and officials saw them and blessed the LORD and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:9

Hezekiah asked priests and Levites about the heaps. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:10

Chief priest Azariah of Zadok’s household said, “Since contributions began, we have enough food and much left. 📖

The LORD blessed his people.

This abundance is left over.”

## 2 Chronicles 31:11

Hezekiah ordered temple storerooms prepared, and they were. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:12

They faithfully brought contributions, tithes, and dedicated gifts. 📖

Levite Conaniah led, with his brother Shimei second.

## 2 Chronicles 31:13

Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah supervised under Conaniah and Shimei. 📖

Hezekiah and temple chief Azariah appointed them.

## 2 Chronicles 31:14

Levite Kore son of Imnah, eastern gatekeeper, distributed voluntary offerings, the LORD’s contributions, and consecrated gifts. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:15

Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah served under him. 📖

They faithfully distributed priestly portions by division, old and young alike.

## 2 Chronicles 31:16

They also provided for registered males aged 3 and above, all entering the LORD’s house for their divisions’ daily service, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:17

for priests registered by family, and Levites aged 20 and above by duty and division. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:18

The record included little ones, wives, sons, and daughters of the whole assembly. 📖

They faithfully consecrated themselves as holy.

## 2 Chronicles 31:19

Named men distributed portions to every male priest and registered Levite living on farmlands around priestly cities or elsewhere. 📖

These priests descended from Aaron.

## 2 Chronicles 31:20

Hezekiah did this throughout Judah, doing good, upright, true things before the LORD his God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:21

He sought God wholeheartedly in temple service, Law, and commands. 📖

He prospered.
