# 2 Chronicles 29 — Hezekiah restores temple worship

**About this chapter**

Hezekiah opens the temple and calls priests and Levites to prepare it. | They cleanse the house and restore its objects. | The people worship and bring many offerings.

## 2 Chronicles 29:1

Hezekiah became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 29 years. 📖

His mother was Abijah daughter of Zechariah.

## 2 Chronicles 29:2

He did right before the LORD like David his ancestor. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:3

In year 1, month 1, he opened and repaired the LORD’s house’s doors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:4

He gathered priests and Levites in the eastern square. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:5

“Levites, consecrate yourselves and the LORD, your ancestors’ God’s house. 📖

Remove every impurity from the Holy Place.

**Help with consecrate:** Prepare and set apart for sacred use.

## 2 Chronicles 29:6

Our ancestors betrayed and did evil before the LORD. 📖

They abandoned him and turned their faces and backs from his dwelling.

## 2 Chronicles 29:7

They shut the porch doors, put out lamps, and stopped incense and burnt offerings in Israel’s God’s Holy Place. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:8

The LORD’s wrath made Judah and Jerusalem objects of terror, horror, and scorn, as you see. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:9

That is why our fathers died by swords and our wives, sons, and daughters are captives. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:10

I want to covenant with the LORD, Israel’s God, to turn away his fierce anger. 📖

**Help with covenant:** Make a binding agreement.

## 2 Chronicles 29:11

My sons, do not neglect your duties. 📖

The LORD chose you to stand and serve before him and burn incense.”

## 2 Chronicles 29:12

Kohathite Levites Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah began work. 📖

Merarites Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel joined.

Gershonites Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah joined,

## 2 Chronicles 29:13

with Elizaphanites Shimri and Jeuel, Asaphites Zechariah and Mattaniah, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:14

Hemanites Jehiel and Shimei, and Jeduthunites Shemaiah and Uzziel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:15

They gathered relatives, consecrated themselves, and cleansed the LORD’s house as the king commanded by the LORD’s words. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:16

Priests brought all unclean things from the temple into the courtyard. 📖

Levites carried them to Kidron Valley.

**Help with unclean:** Not meeting the religious requirements for sacred use.

## 2 Chronicles 29:17

They began in month 1, day 1, reached the porch on day 8, and worked 8 more days inside. 📖

They finished on month 1, day 16.

## 2 Chronicles 29:18

They told Hezekiah they cleansed the whole LORD’s house, burnt-offering altar and utensils, and showbread table and utensils. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:19

“We prepared and consecrated everything unfaithful Ahaz discarded. 📖

They stand before the LORD’s altar.”

## 2 Chronicles 29:20

Early next morning, Hezekiah gathered city officials and entered the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:21

They brought 7 bulls, 7 rams, 7 lambs, and 7 male goats for kingdom, sanctuary, and Judah. 📖

These were a sin offering.

He ordered Aaron’s priestly descendants to offer them on the LORD’s altar.

## 2 Chronicles 29:22

They killed the bulls, and priests splashed the blood on the altar. 📖

They killed rams and splashed their blood, then lambs and splashed their blood.

## 2 Chronicles 29:23

They brought sin-offering goats before king and assembly, who laid hands on them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:24

Priests killed the goats and put blood on the altar to make atonement for all Israel. 📖

The king ordered burnt and sin offerings for all Israel.

**Help with atonement:** Dealing with sin to restore relationship with God.

## 2 Chronicles 29:25

He stationed Levites with cymbals, harps, and lyres as David, Gad the king’s seer, and Nathan the prophet commanded. 📖

The LORD gave this command through his prophets.

## 2 Chronicles 29:26

Levites held David’s instruments and priests held trumpets. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:27

Hezekiah ordered the burnt offering. 📖

As it began, the LORD’s song and trumpets began with David king of Israel’s instruments.

## 2 Chronicles 29:28

The whole assembly worshipped while singers and trumpeters continued until the burnt offering ended. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:29

The king and everyone bowed and worshipped after the offerings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:30

Hezekiah and officials ordered Levites to praise with David’s and Asaph the seer’s words. 📖

They sang gladly, bowed, and worshipped.

## 2 Chronicles 29:31

Hezekiah invited the consecrated people to bring sacrifices and thank offerings. 📖

They brought these, and all willing hearts brought burnt offerings.

## 2 Chronicles 29:32

They brought 70 bulls, 100 rams, and 200 lambs as burnt offerings to the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:33

Consecrated offerings numbered 600 bulls and 3,000 sheep. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:34

Too few priests were ready to skin the burnt offerings, so Levites helped until completion and the priests’ consecration. 📖

Levites had prepared more diligently than priests.

## 2 Chronicles 29:35

Many burnt offerings, peace-offering fat, and accompanying drink offerings were presented. 📖

The LORD’s house’s service was established.

## 2 Chronicles 29:36

Hezekiah and everyone rejoiced at God’s preparation for them, because it happened so quickly. 📖
