# 2 Chronicles 21 — Jehoram’s violence and judgment

**About this chapter**

Jehoram kills his brothers and leads Judah away from the LORD. | Elijah’s letter announces judgment. | The account describes invasion, painful illness, and Jehoram’s death.

## 2 Chronicles 21:1

Jehoshaphat died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. 📖

His son Jehoram became king.

## 2 Chronicles 21:2

Jehoram’s brothers were Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah, sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:3

Their father gave them silver, gold, precious gifts, and defended cities in Judah. 📖

He gave Jehoram the kingdom because he was firstborn.

## 2 Chronicles 21:4

Jehoram secured his rule by killing all his brothers and some princes of Israel with swords. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:5

He became king at 32 and ruled in Jerusalem for 8 years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:6

He followed Israel’s kings and Ahab’s family. 📖

He married Ahab’s daughter and did evil before the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 21:7

The LORD would not destroy David’s house because of his covenant with David. 📖

He promised David and his descendants a lamp forever.

**Help with lamp:** This pictures the continuation of David’s royal line.

## 2 Chronicles 21:8

Edom rebelled against Judah and appointed its own king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:9

Jehoram entered Edom with officers and all his chariots. 📖

Edom surrounded him and his chariot commanders, and he attacked by night.

## 2 Chronicles 21:10

Edom remains in rebellion against Judah to this day. 📖

Libnah also rebelled because Jehoram abandoned the LORD, his ancestors’ God.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Chronicles 21:11

He built high places in Judah’s hills and led Judah astray, causing Jerusalem’s people to prostitute themselves. 📖

**Help with prostitute themselves:** This image describes religious unfaithfulness.

## 2 Chronicles 21:12

Elijah the prophet sent a letter. 📖

“The LORD, your ancestor David’s God, says you did not follow Jehoshaphat or Asa king of Judah.

## 2 Chronicles 21:13

You followed Israel’s kings and caused Judah and Jerusalem to prostitute themselves like Ahab’s house. 📖

You killed your brothers, your father’s family, who were better than you.

## 2 Chronicles 21:14

The LORD will strike your people, sons, wives, and all your possessions with a serious blow. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:15

You will suffer severe bowel disease daily until your bowels come out.” 📖

**Help with bowels:** The intestines inside the body.

## 2 Chronicles 21:16

The LORD stirred Philistines and Arabs living near Cushites against Jehoram. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:17

They invaded Judah and took all palace possessions, his sons, and wives. 📖

Only his youngest son Jehoahaz remained.

## 2 Chronicles 21:18

The LORD then afflicted Jehoram with incurable bowel disease. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:19

After 2 full years, his intestines came out, and he died in severe pain. 📖

His people made no honouring fire as they did for his ancestors.

## 2 Chronicles 21:20

Jehoram became king at 32 and ruled in Jerusalem for 8 years. 📖

Nobody regretted his death.

He was buried in the City of David, outside the kings’ tombs.
