# 2 Chronicles 13 — Abijah and Jeroboam fight

**About this chapter**

Abijah addresses Jeroboam and Israel about the LORD’s covenant and worship. | Jeroboam sets an ambush. | Judah calls to the LORD and defeats Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 13:1

Abijah became Judah’s king in Jeroboam’s 18th year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:2

He ruled in Jerusalem for 3 years. 📖

His mother was Micaiah daughter of Uriel from Gibeah.

He fought Jeroboam.

## 2 Chronicles 13:3

Abijah brought 400,000 chosen soldiers. 📖

Jeroboam faced him with 800,000 chosen brave warriors.

## 2 Chronicles 13:4

Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim in Ephraim’s hills. 📖

“Listen, Jeroboam and Israel!

## 2 Chronicles 13:5

The LORD, Israel’s God, gave Israel’s kingship to David and his descendants forever by a salt covenant. 📖

Do you not know this?

**Help with salt covenant:** This expression describes a lasting, binding covenant.

## 2 Chronicles 13:6

Jeroboam son of Nebat, Solomon son of David’s servant, rebelled against his master. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:7

Worthless, wicked men joined him against Rehoboam son of Solomon, who was young, inexperienced, and unable to resist. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:8

You think you can resist the LORD’s kingdom held by David’s descendants. 📖

Your army is vast, with gold calves Jeroboam made as gods.

## 2 Chronicles 13:9

You drove out the LORD’s priests, Aaron’s sons, and the Levites. 📖

You made priests like other nations do.

Anyone bringing a young bull and 7 rams for consecration can become a priest of things that are not gods.

**Help with consecration:** Setting apart for religious service.

## 2 Chronicles 13:10

The LORD is our God, and we have not abandoned him. 📖

Aaron’s sons serve him as priests, and Levites perform their duties.

## 2 Chronicles 13:11

They offer burnt offerings and fragrant incense morning and evening. 📖

They arrange showbread on the ceremonially clean table and light the gold lampstand each evening.

We keep the LORD our God’s requirements; you abandoned him.

**Help with ceremonially clean:** Prepared according to religious rules for sacred use.

Showbread is bread set before the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 13:12

God leads us, and his priests sound battle trumpets against you. 📖

Israel, do not fight the LORD, your ancestors’ God.

You will not succeed.”

## 2 Chronicles 13:13

Jeroboam sent troops behind Judah in ambush while he remained in front. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:14

Judah saw enemies before and behind. 📖

They cried to the LORD, and priests blew trumpets.

## 2 Chronicles 13:15

Judah raised the battle cry. 📖

God routed Jeroboam and Israel before Abijah and Judah.

## 2 Chronicles 13:16

Israel fled, and God handed them over to Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:17

Abijah and his people struck a great blow, killing 500,000 chosen Israelite men. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:18

Israel was subdued. 📖

Judah won because they relied on the LORD, their ancestors’ God.

## 2 Chronicles 13:19

Abijah pursued Jeroboam and captured Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron with their villages. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:20

Jeroboam never recovered his power during Abijah’s reign. 📖

The LORD struck him, and he died.

## 2 Chronicles 13:21

Abijah grew strong, married 14 wives, and had 22 sons and 16 daughters. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:22

His other acts, ways, and words are in the Treatise of the Prophet Iddo. 📖

**Help with treatise:** A written account.
