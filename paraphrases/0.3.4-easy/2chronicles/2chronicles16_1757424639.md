# 2 Chronicles 16 — Asa relies on Aram

**About this chapter**

Asa pays Aram’s king to break his treaty with Baasha. | Hanani rebukes Asa for failing to rely on the LORD. | Asa imprisons him and later becomes seriously ill.

## 2 Chronicles 16:1

In Asa’s 36th year, Baasha king of Israel attacked Judah and strengthened Ramah. 📖

He sought to block entry to and exit from Asa king of Judah’s territory.

## 2 Chronicles 16:2

Asa sent silver and gold from the LORD’s house and palace treasuries to Ben-hadad king of Aram in Damascus. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:3

“Let us make a treaty like our fathers’. 📖

I sent silver and gold.

Break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel so he withdraws.”

## 2 Chronicles 16:4

Ben-hadad sent commanders against Israel, capturing Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all Naphtali’s storage cities. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:5

Baasha heard and abandoned Ramah’s building work. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:6

Asa gathered Judah’s men to carry away Ramah’s stones and Baasha’s timber. 📖

He used them to strengthen Geba and Mizpah.

## 2 Chronicles 16:7

Hanani the seer told Judah’s King Asa, “You relied on Aram’s king instead of the LORD your God. 📖

Aram’s army therefore escaped you.

**Help with seer:** A prophet who receives God’s messages or visions.

## 2 Chronicles 16:8

The Cushites and Libyans had a vast army, many chariots, and horsemen. 📖

You relied on the LORD, and he handed them to you.

## 2 Chronicles 16:9

The LORD’s eyes search all earth to show his strength for those wholly devoted to him. 📖

You acted foolishly, and from now on you will have war.”

## 2 Chronicles 16:10

Asa became furious and imprisoned the seer. 📖

He also oppressed some of the people.

## 2 Chronicles 16:11

Asa’s acts from beginning to end are in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:12

In his 39th year, Asa’s foot disease became very severe. 📖

He sought only physicians, not the LORD.

**About this passage:** It describes Asa’s response.

It does not tell readers to avoid medical treatment.

## 2 Chronicles 16:13

Asa died in his 41st year and joined his ancestors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:14

He was buried in his prepared tomb in the City of David. 📖

They laid him on a funeral bed full of spices and blended perfumes.

They made a great fire to honour him.
