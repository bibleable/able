# 2 Chronicles 24 — Joash repairs the temple, then turns away

**About this chapter**

Joash restores the temple during Jehoiada’s life. | Later he follows Judah’s officials and orders Zechariah killed. | Aram attacks, and Joash’s servants kill him.

## 2 Chronicles 24:1

Joash became king at 7 and ruled in Jerusalem for 40 years. 📖

His mother was Zibiah from Beersheba.

## 2 Chronicles 24:2

He did right before the LORD throughout Jehoiada the priest’s life. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:3

Jehoiada chose 2 wives for him, and he had sons and daughters. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:4

Joash decided to repair the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:5

He told priests and Levites to collect annual money from all Israel in Judah’s cities for repairs. 📖

“Do it quickly.”

They did not hurry.

## 2 Chronicles 24:6

He asked high priest Jehoiada why Levites had not collected Judah’s and Jerusalem’s tax for the Tent of the Testimony. 📖

Moses the LORD’s servant and Israel’s assembly imposed it.

## 2 Chronicles 24:7

Wicked Athaliah’s sons broke into God’s house and used its sacred objects for the Baals. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:8

The king ordered a chest made and placed outside the LORD’s house’s gate. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:9

Judah and Jerusalem were told to bring the LORD the wilderness tax Moses, God’s servant, imposed on Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:10

Officials and people gladly brought contributions until the chest was full. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:11

Levites brought it to royal overseers when it held much money. 📖

The royal scribe and high priest’s officer emptied and returned it.

They did this daily and collected much money.

## 2 Chronicles 24:12

The king and Jehoiada paid supervisors to hire stonecutters, carpenters, and iron and bronze workers for temple repairs. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:13

The work progressed. 📖

They restored God’s house to its specifications and strengthened it.

## 2 Chronicles 24:14

They returned the remaining money to king and Jehoiada. 📖

It paid for temple service and burnt-offering utensils, dishes, and gold and silver objects.

Burnt offerings continued regularly throughout Jehoiada’s life.

## 2 Chronicles 24:15

Jehoiada died very old, aged 130. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:16

He was buried with kings in the City of David for his good to Israel, God, and temple. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:17

After his death, Judah’s officials honoured Joash, and he listened to them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:18

They abandoned the LORD, their ancestors’ God’s house, and served Asherah poles and idols. 📖

Their guilt brought wrath on Judah and Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 24:19

The LORD sent prophets to bring them back and testify against them. 📖

They refused to listen.

## 2 Chronicles 24:20

God’s Spirit came on Zechariah son of Jehoiada the priest. 📖

He told everyone, “God asks why you break his commands so you cannot prosper.

You abandoned the LORD, and he abandoned you.”

## 2 Chronicles 24:21

They plotted against Zechariah and stoned him in the LORD’s house’s courtyard at the king’s order. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:22

Joash forgot Jehoiada’s kindness and killed his son. 📖

Dying, Zechariah said, “May the LORD see and call you to account.”

## 2 Chronicles 24:23

In spring Aram invaded Judah and Jerusalem, destroyed all the leaders, and sent plunder to their king in Damascus. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:24

The LORD handed a great army to Aram’s few men because Judah abandoned their ancestors’ God. 📖

Judgment fell on Joash.

## 2 Chronicles 24:25

Aram left Joash badly wounded. 📖

His servants killed him on his bed for shedding Jehoiada the priest’s son’s blood.

He was buried in the City of David, outside the kings’ tombs.

## 2 Chronicles 24:26

The plotters were Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonitess and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabitess. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:27

His sons, many pronouncements about him, and temple restoration are in the Treatise of the Book of the Kings. 📖

His son Amaziah became king.
