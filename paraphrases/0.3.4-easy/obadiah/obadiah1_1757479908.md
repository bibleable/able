# Obadiah 1 — The LORD’s judgment on Edom

**About this chapter**

Obadiah gives a message about Edom, Judah, and the LORD’s kingdom.

The message includes judgment against Edom for violence towards Jacob’s people.

## Obadiah 1:1

This is the vision of Obadiah. 📖

The Lord GOD speaks about Edom.

We have heard a message from the LORD.

A messenger has been sent among the nations.

The message says, “Get up!

Let us go to war against Edom!”

**Help with vision:** A vision is something God shows a person.

## Obadiah 1:2

The LORD says, “I will make you small among the nations. 📖

People will deeply despise you.

**Help with despise:** Despise means look down on with strong dislike.

## Obadiah 1:3

Your proud heart has tricked you. 📖

You live in cracks in the rocks, with your homes high above.

You say to yourselves, ‘Who can bring me down to the ground?’

## Obadiah 1:4

You may fly high like an eagle. 📖

You may make your nest among the stars.

Even from there, I will bring you down,” declares the LORD.

**Help with this picture:** Edom’s high homes and pride are compared to an eagle’s high nest.

## Obadiah 1:5

The LORD says, “Suppose thieves or robbers came to you at night. 📖

Would they not steal only what they wanted?

But you will be ruined!

Suppose people came to gather grapes.

Would they not leave some grapes behind?

## Obadiah 1:6

But Esau will be robbed completely! 📖

His hidden treasures will be searched out!

**Help with Esau:** Here, Esau refers to Edom, the people descended from Esau.

## Obadiah 1:7

All your allies will drive you to the border. 📖

Those at peace with you will trick you and overpower you.

Those who eat your bread will set a trap for you without you knowing.

**Help with allies:** Allies are people or nations who agree to help each other.

## Obadiah 1:8

The LORD declares, “On that day, will I not destroy Edom’s wise men? 📖

Will I not destroy the men of understanding in Esau’s mountains?

**Help with Esau:** Here, Esau refers to Edom, his descendants.

## Obadiah 1:9

Teman, your mighty men will be terrified. 📖

Everyone in Esau’s mountains will be cut down in the killing.

**Help with Esau:** Here, Esau refers to Edom, his descendants.

## Obadiah 1:10

You used violence against your brother Jacob. 📖

Because of this, shame will cover you, and you will be cut off forever.

**Help with brother Jacob:** Jacob and Esau were brothers.

Here, their names also refer to the peoples descended from them.

## Obadiah 1:11

You stood apart while strangers carried away Jacob’s wealth. 📖

Foreigners entered his gate and cast lots for Jerusalem.

You were just like one of them.

**Help with cast lots:** People used marked objects to make a choice, such as dividing property.

## Obadiah 1:12

You should not enjoy your brother’s misfortune. 📖

You should not rejoice over Judah’s people when they are destroyed.

You should not boast when they are in distress.

## Obadiah 1:13

You should not enter my people’s gate on the day of their disaster. 📖

You should not enjoy their suffering on the day of their disaster.

You should not steal their wealth on the day of their disaster.

## Obadiah 1:14

You should not stand at the crossroads to stop people running away. 📖

You should not hand over survivors on the day of their distress.

## Obadiah 1:15

The Day of the LORD is near for all nations. 📖

What you have done will be done to you.

The punishment you deserve will come back on your own head.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

**Help with on your own head:** This pictures people receiving the consequences of their actions.

## Obadiah 1:16

You drank on my holy mountain. 📖

In the same way, all the nations will drink continually.

They will drink and gulp it down.

They will be as though they had never existed.

**Help with drink:** This passage uses drinking as a picture of experiencing judgment.

## Obadiah 1:17

But there will be rescue on Mount Zion. 📖

It will be holy.

Jacob’s family will take back what belongs to them.

**Help with holy:** Holy means set apart for God.

**Help with Jacob’s family:** Here, the family means the people descended from Jacob.

## Obadiah 1:18

Jacob’s family will be a blazing fire. 📖

Joseph’s family will be a burning flame.

But Esau’s family will be dry stalks.

Jacob will set them on fire and burn them up.

No survivor will remain from Esau’s family.”

The LORD has spoken.

**Help with this picture:** Fire burning dry stalks describes the destruction of Esau’s people, Edom.

## Obadiah 1:19

People from the Negev will possess Esau’s mountains. 📖

People from the foothills will possess the Philistines’ land.

They will occupy the fields of Ephraim and Samaria.

Benjamin will possess Gilead.

## Obadiah 1:20

The exiles from this army of Israelites will possess the Canaanites’ land as far as Zarephath. 📖

Exiles from Jerusalem who are in Sepharad will possess the cities of the Negev.

**Help with exiles:** Exiles are people living away from their own land, often because they were forced out.

## Obadiah 1:21

The rescuers will go up Mount Zion to rule over Esau’s mountains. 📖

The kingdom will belong to the LORD.
