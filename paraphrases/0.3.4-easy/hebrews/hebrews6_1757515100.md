# Hebrews 6 — Hold firmly to hope

**About this chapter**

The writer warns about falling away and encourages faithful service.

God's promise and oath give a firm hope.

## Hebrews 6:1

Let us move beyond the first teachings about Christ and grow to maturity. 📖

Do not lay again the foundation of turning from dead works and trusting God.

**Help with these words:** Dead works describes works that do not bring life.

## Hebrews 6:2

The foundation also includes teaching about baptisms, placing hands on people, raising the dead, and judgment that lasts for ever. 📖

## Hebrews 6:3

We will do this if God permits. 📖

## Hebrews 6:4

Some people have once been given light, tasted the heavenly gift, and shared in the Holy Spirit. 📖

## Hebrews 6:5

They have tasted the goodness of God's word and the powers of the coming age. 📖

## Hebrews 6:6

If they then fall away, it is impossible to restore them to repentance. 📖

They themselves are crucifying God's Son again and exposing him to public shame.

**Help with a word:** Repentance means turning from wrongdoing towards God.

## Hebrews 6:7

Land drinks the rain often falling on it. 📖

If it produces crops useful to those it is farmed for, it receives God's blessing.

## Hebrews 6:8

But land producing thorns and thistles is worthless and near a curse. 📖

In the end, it will be burned.

## Hebrews 6:9

Dear friends, although we speak this way, we are sure of better things in your case, things accompanying salvation. 📖

## Hebrews 6:10

God is not unjust. 📖

He will not forget your work and love for his name as you have served, and still serve, his people.

## Hebrews 6:11

We want each of you to show the same care and effort to the end, making your hope sure. 📖

## Hebrews 6:12

Then you will not be sluggish. 📖

You will follow those who inherit the promises through faith and patience.

## Hebrews 6:13

When God promised Abraham, he swore by himself because no one greater existed to swear by. 📖

## Hebrews 6:14

He said, “I will surely bless you and multiply your descendants.” 📖

## Hebrews 6:15

After waiting patiently, Abraham received the promise. 📖

## Hebrews 6:16

People swear by someone greater than themselves. 📖

Their oath confirms what they say and ends the argument.

## Hebrews 6:17

God wanted the heirs of the promise to know clearly that his purpose would never change. 📖

He guaranteed it with an oath.

## Hebrews 6:18

These are two unchangeable things in which God cannot lie. 📖

They strongly encourage us who fled to take hold of the hope before us.

## Hebrews 6:19

We have this hope as a firm, secure anchor for the soul. 📖

It enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain.

**Help with these pictures:** An anchor holds a boat steady.

The sanctuary is a holy place of worship.

## Hebrews 6:20

Jesus went there ahead of us on our behalf. 📖

He became high priest for ever in the order of Melchizedek.
