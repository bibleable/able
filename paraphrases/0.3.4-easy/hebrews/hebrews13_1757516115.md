# Hebrews 13 — Love, faithfulness, and a final prayer

**About this chapter**

The writer gives instructions about love, conduct, worship, and leaders.

He ends with prayer, news, and greetings.

## Hebrews 13:1

Keep loving each other as brothers and sisters. 📖

## Hebrews 13:2

Do not forget to welcome strangers. 📖

Some have welcomed angels by doing this without knowing it.

## Hebrews 13:3

Remember prisoners as if you were chained with them. 📖

Remember mistreated people as if you were suffering with them.

## Hebrews 13:4

Everyone should honour marriage and keep the marriage bed undefiled. 📖

God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers.

**Help with these words:** The marriage bed pictures sexual faithfulness within marriage.

Adultery means breaking marriage faithfulness through a sexual relationship with someone else.

## Hebrews 13:5

Keep free from the love of money and be content with what you have. 📖

God said, “I will never leave you or abandon you.”

## Hebrews 13:6

So we confidently say, “The Lord is my helper; I will not fear. 📖

What can a human person do to me?”

## Hebrews 13:7

Remember the leaders who spoke God's word to you. 📖

Consider the outcome of their lives and follow their faith.

## Hebrews 13:8

Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and for ever. 📖

## Hebrews 13:9

Do not be carried away by strange teachings. 📖

God's kindness strengthens the heart, rather than foods that did not benefit those devoted to them.

## Hebrews 13:10

We have an altar from which those serving the tabernacle have no right to eat. 📖

## Hebrews 13:11

The high priest brings animal blood into the Holy Place as a sacrifice for sin. 📖

But the bodies are burned outside the camp.

## Hebrews 13:12

Jesus too suffered outside the city gate, making the people holy through his own blood. 📖

## Hebrews 13:13

So let us go to him outside the camp, bearing the disgrace he bore. 📖

## Hebrews 13:14

We have no permanent city here, but seek the city to come. 📖

## Hebrews 13:15

Through Jesus, continually offer God a sacrifice of praise, the fruit of lips acknowledging his name. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Praise is pictured as fruit and an offering to God.

## Hebrews 13:16

Do not neglect doing good and sharing, because such sacrifices please God. 📖

## Hebrews 13:17

Obey and submit to your leaders, who watch over your souls and must give an account. 📖

Let them lead with joy rather than grief, which would not benefit you.

## Hebrews 13:18

Pray for us. 📖

We are convinced we have a clear conscience and want to live honourably in every way.

## Hebrews 13:19

I especially urge you to pray that I may return to you soon. 📖

## Hebrews 13:20

The God of peace brought our Lord Jesus back from the dead through the blood of the eternal covenant. 📖

Jesus is the great Shepherd of the sheep.

## Hebrews 13:21

May God equip you with every good thing to do his will. 📖

May he work in us what pleases him through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory for ever and ever.

Amen.

## Hebrews 13:22

Brothers and sisters, bear with my words of encouragement. 📖

I have written only briefly.

## Hebrews 13:23

Our brother Timothy has been released. 📖

If he arrives soon, I will come with him to see you.

## Hebrews 13:24

Greet all your leaders and all God's people. 📖

Those from Italy send greetings.

## Hebrews 13:25

May God's kindness be with you all. 📖
