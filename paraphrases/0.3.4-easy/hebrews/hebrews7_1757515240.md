# Hebrews 7 — A priesthood that lasts for ever

**About this chapter**

The writer compares Melchizedek with the priests descended from Levi.

He explains Jesus's lasting priesthood and his offering of himself.

## Hebrews 7:1

Melchizedek was king of Salem and priest of God Most High. 📖

He met Abraham returning from killing the kings and blessed him.

## Hebrews 7:2

Abraham gave him a tenth of everything. 📖

His name first means “king of righteousness.”

Also, “king of Salem” means “king of peace.”

## Hebrews 7:3

Without father, mother, or family history, without beginning of days or end of life, he remains a priest for ever. 📖

He is like God's Son.

**Help with the passage:** The writer uses this description when comparing Melchizedek with the Son of God.

## Hebrews 7:4

Think how great Melchizedek was. 📖

Even Abraham, the great ancestor, gave him a tenth of the goods taken in battle.

## Hebrews 7:5

The law commands Levi's descendants who become priests to collect a tenth from their own people. 📖

Those people also descend from Abraham.

## Hebrews 7:6

Melchizedek did not descend from Levi, but he collected a tenth from Abraham and blessed the man holding the promises. 📖

## Hebrews 7:7

Without doubt, the lesser is blessed by the greater. 📖

## Hebrews 7:8

Among the Levites, men who die collect the tenth. 📖

But concerning Melchizedek, it is testified that he lives on.

## Hebrews 7:9

In a way, Levi, who collects the tenth, paid it through Abraham. 📖

## Hebrews 7:10

When Melchizedek met Abraham, Levi was still in his ancestor's loins. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Abraham was Levi's ancestor.

Levi had not yet been born.

## Hebrews 7:11

If the Levitical priesthood could bring perfection, why was another priest needed? 📖

The people received the law on the basis of this priesthood.

Why did another priest need to appear in Melchizedek's order, rather than Aaron's?

## Hebrews 7:12

When the priesthood changes, the law must change too. 📖

## Hebrews 7:13

The One spoken about belongs to a different tribe, from which no one ever served at the altar. 📖

## Hebrews 7:14

Our Lord clearly descended from Judah. 📖

Moses said nothing about priests from that tribe.

## Hebrews 7:15

This is even clearer if another priest like Melchizedek appears. 📖

## Hebrews 7:16

His priesthood does not depend on a rule about family descent. 📖

It depends on the power of a life that cannot be destroyed.

## Hebrews 7:17

The testimony says, “You are a priest for ever in the order of Melchizedek.” 📖

## Hebrews 7:18

The former command is set aside because it was weak and useless. 📖

## Hebrews 7:19

The law made nothing perfect. 📖

A better hope is introduced, through which we come near to God.

## Hebrews 7:20

An oath was part of this. 📖

The other priests became priests without an oath.

## Hebrews 7:21

But Jesus became a priest with an oath from the One who told him these words. 📖

“The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind: ‘You are a priest for ever.’”

## Hebrews 7:22

Through this oath, Jesus became the guarantee of a better covenant. 📖

**Help with a word:** A covenant is an agreement with binding promises.

## Hebrews 7:23

There were many other priests because death stopped them continuing in their work. 📖

## Hebrews 7:24

But Jesus lives for ever, so his priesthood is permanent. 📖

## Hebrews 7:25

He can completely save those coming to God through him, because he always lives to intercede for them. 📖

**Help with a word:** Intercede means speak or pray on someone else's behalf.

## Hebrews 7:26

We need such a high priest, holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and lifted above the heavens. 📖

## Hebrews 7:27

He does not need daily sacrifices for his own sins and then the people's, as other high priests do. 📖

He sacrificed for sin once for all when he offered himself.

## Hebrews 7:28

The law appoints weak men as high priests. 📖

But the oath after the law appointed the Son, made perfect for ever.
