# Hebrews 1 — God speaks through his Son

**About this chapter**

The writer describes God's Son and compares him with angels.

Quotations from the holy writings form much of this chapter.

## Hebrews 1:1

Long ago, God spoke to our ancestors through the prophets on many occasions and in many ways. 📖

**Help with words:** Ancestors are family members who lived before us.

Prophets speak God's message.

## Hebrews 1:2

In these last days, God has spoken to us through his Son. 📖

God appointed him heir of everything and made the universe through him.

**Help with a word:** An heir receives an inheritance.

## Hebrews 1:3

The Son shines with God's glory and shows exactly what God is like. 📖

He holds everything together by his powerful word.

After providing cleansing for sins, he sat at the right hand of the Majesty on high.

**Help with words:** Glory means greatness and honour made known.

Majesty on high refers to God.

The right hand is a place of honour and authority.

## Hebrews 1:4

He became far greater than the angels. 📖

The name he inherited is far more excellent than theirs.

## Hebrews 1:5

To which angel did God ever say, “You are my Son; today I have become your Father”? 📖

Or, “I will be his Father, and he will be my Son”?

## Hebrews 1:6

When God brings his firstborn into the world, he also says, “Let all God's angels worship him.” 📖

## Hebrews 1:7

About the angels, God says, “He makes his angels winds and his servants flames of fire.” 📖

## Hebrews 1:8

But about the Son, he says, “Your throne, O God, lasts for ever and ever. 📖

Justice is the sceptre of your kingdom.”

**Help with a word:** A sceptre is a ruler's staff, a sign of royal authority.

## Hebrews 1:9

“You loved righteousness and hated wickedness. 📖

So God, your God, anointed you above your companions with the oil of joy.”

**Help with words:** Righteousness means being right before God and doing right.

Anointing means pouring or putting oil on someone.

## Hebrews 1:10

“In the beginning, Lord, you laid the earth's foundations, and the heavens are the work of your hands.” 📖

## Hebrews 1:11

“They will perish, but you remain. 📖

They will all wear out like clothes.”

## Hebrews 1:12

“You will roll them up like a robe, and they will be changed like clothes. 📖

But you remain the same, and your years will never end.”

## Hebrews 1:13

To which angel did God ever say, “Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies your footstool”? 📖

**Help with this picture:** Enemies as a footstool pictures victory and rule over them.

## Hebrews 1:14

Are angels not serving spirits, sent to help those who will inherit salvation? 📖

**Help with a word:** Salvation means being saved by God.
