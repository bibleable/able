# Hebrews 12 — Run with endurance

**About this chapter**

The writer urges believers to keep looking to Jesus and accept God's discipline.

He contrasts the fearful mountain with the heavenly Jerusalem.

## Hebrews 12:1

Such a great cloud of witnesses surrounds us. 📖

So throw off every weight and the sin that easily entangles us.

Run with endurance the race before us.

**Help with these pictures:** Witnesses are pictured as a great crowd around runners.

Running pictures continuing faithfully, without burdens that hold us back.

## Hebrews 12:2

Fix your eyes on Jesus, the author and perfecter of our faith. 📖

For the joy before him, he endured the cross, disregarding its shame.

He sat at the right hand of God's throne.

**Help with these words:** Jesus begins and completes faith.

## Hebrews 12:3

Think about him enduring such hostility from sinners, so you do not grow tired and lose heart. 📖

## Hebrews 12:4

In your struggle against sin, you have not yet resisted to the point of shedding your blood. 📖

## Hebrews 12:5

You have forgotten the encouragement addressing you as children. 📖

“My son, do not take the Lord's discipline lightly or lose heart when he corrects you.”

## Hebrews 12:6

“The Lord disciplines those he loves and punishes every child he receives.” 📖

## Hebrews 12:7

Endure suffering as discipline. 📖

God treats you as children.

What son is not disciplined by his father?

## Hebrews 12:8

If you do not share in discipline like everyone else, you are illegitimate children, rather than true sons. 📖

**Help with this family picture:** Illegitimate here pictures not belonging as recognised children in the Father's household.

## Hebrews 12:9

We had earthly fathers who disciplined us, and we respected them. 📖

Should we not submit much more to the Father of our spirits and live?

## Hebrews 12:10

Our fathers disciplined us briefly as they thought best. 📖

God disciplines us for our good, so we can share his holiness.

## Hebrews 12:11

Discipline feels painful rather than enjoyable at the time. 📖

Later, those trained by it receive a harvest of righteousness and peace.

## Hebrews 12:12

So strengthen your limp hands and weak knees. 📖

## Hebrews 12:13

Make straight paths for your feet, so the lame may be healed rather than disabled. 📖

**Help with the picture:** The writer continues the race picture with weak hands, knees, and feet.

He urges strength and a straight way forward.

## Hebrews 12:14

Pursue peace with everyone and holiness, without which no one will see the Lord. 📖

## Hebrews 12:15

Make sure no one falls short of God's kindness. 📖

Let no root of bitterness grow up, cause trouble, and defile many.

**Help with these words:** A root growing pictures something spreading and producing harmful results.

Defile means spoil through wrongdoing.

## Hebrews 12:16

Let no one be sexually immoral or godless like Esau, who sold his birthright for one meal. 📖

**Help with a word:** A birthright is the special inheritance belonging to a firstborn child.

## Hebrews 12:17

Later, when Esau wanted to inherit the blessing, he was rejected. 📖

He found no ground for repentance, although he sought the blessing with tears.

## Hebrews 12:18

You have not come to a mountain that can be touched, burning with fire, or to darkness, gloom, and storm. 📖

## Hebrews 12:19

You have not come to a trumpet blast or a voice making hearers beg for no further words. 📖

## Hebrews 12:20

They could not bear the command, “Even an animal touching the mountain must be killed with stones.” 📖

## Hebrews 12:21

The sight was so terrifying that Moses said, “I am trembling with fear.” 📖

## Hebrews 12:22

Instead, you have come to Mount Zion, the living God's city, the heavenly Jerusalem. 📖

You have come to tens of thousands of angels.

## Hebrews 12:23

You have come to their joyful gathering and the congregation of the firstborn, whose names are written in heaven. 📖

You have come to God, everyone's Judge, and to the spirits of righteous people made perfect.

## Hebrews 12:24

You have come to Jesus, mediator of a new covenant. 📖

You have come to sprinkled blood speaking a better word than Abel's blood.

## Hebrews 12:25

Do not refuse the One who speaks. 📖

Those refusing his warning on earth did not escape.

How much less will we escape if we reject his warning from heaven?

## Hebrews 12:26

Then his voice shook the earth. 📖

Now he promises, “Once more I will shake both earth and heaven.”

## Hebrews 12:27

“Once more” means removing what can be shaken, the created things, so what cannot be shaken remains. 📖

## Hebrews 12:28

We are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken. 📖

So be thankful and worship God acceptably with reverence and awe.

**Help with these words:** Reverence and awe mean deep respect before God's greatness.

## Hebrews 12:29

“Our God is a consuming fire.” 📖
