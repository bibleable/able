# Hebrews 8 — A better covenant

**About this chapter**

The writer describes Jesus's heavenly service and quotes God's promise of a new covenant.

## Hebrews 8:1

Here is our main point. 📖

We have such a high priest, seated at the right hand of the Majesty's throne in heaven.

**Help with a title:** Majesty refers to God.

## Hebrews 8:2

He serves in the sanctuary and true tabernacle set up by the Lord, rather than human hands. 📖

**Help with words:** A sanctuary is a holy place of worship.

A tabernacle is a tent used as a place of worship.

## Hebrews 8:3

Every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices. 📖

So this high priest also needed something to offer.

## Hebrews 8:4

If he were on earth, he would not be a priest. 📖

Priests already offer gifts according to the law.

## Hebrews 8:5

Their place of service is a copy and shadow of what is in heaven. 📖

Before Moses built the tabernacle, God warned him.

“Make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.”

## Hebrews 8:6

Jesus has been given a far better ministry. 📖

The covenant he mediates is better and based on better promises.

**Help with words:** Ministry means the work of serving God and his people.

Mediates means acting between people in bringing an agreement into effect.

## Hebrews 8:7

If the first covenant had been without fault, there would have been no need for a second. 📖

## Hebrews 8:8

But God found fault with the people and said these words. 📖

“The days are coming, says the Lord, when I will make a new covenant with Israel's house and Judah's house.”

## Hebrews 8:9

“It will not be like my covenant with their ancestors when I led them by the hand from Egypt. 📖

They did not keep my covenant, and I disregarded them, says the Lord.”

## Hebrews 8:10

“After those days, I will make this covenant with Israel's house, says the Lord. 📖

I will put my laws in their minds and write them on their hearts.

I will be their God, and they will be my people.”

## Hebrews 8:11

“No one will teach a neighbour or brother, ‘Know the Lord.’ 📖

They will all know me, from the least to the greatest.”

## Hebrews 8:12

“I will forgive their wrongdoing and remember their sins no more.” 📖

## Hebrews 8:13

By speaking of a new covenant, God made the first one out of date. 📖

What is out of date and growing old will soon disappear.
