# Hebrews 2 — Jesus shares our humanity

**About this chapter**

The writer warns against neglecting salvation.

He explains how Jesus shared human life, suffering, and death to help his people.

## Hebrews 2:1

So we must pay closer attention to what we heard, so that we do not drift away. 📖

## Hebrews 2:2

The message spoken through angels was binding, and every wrongdoing and disobedience received a fair punishment. 📖

## Hebrews 2:3

Then how will we escape if we neglect such a great salvation? 📖

The Lord first announced it, and those who heard him confirmed it to us.

## Hebrews 2:4

God confirmed it through signs, wonders, different miracles, and gifts of the Holy Spirit given according to his will. 📖

## Hebrews 2:5

God did not place the coming world we speak about under angels' rule. 📖

## Hebrews 2:6

Someone has testified in these words. 📖

“What is a human being, that you remember him, or the son of man, that you care for him?”

## Hebrews 2:7

“You made him a little lower than the angels and crowned him with glory and honour.” 📖

## Hebrews 2:8

“You placed everything under his feet.” 📖

In placing everything under him, God left nothing outside his control.

Yet we do not currently see everything under his rule.

## Hebrews 2:9

But we see Jesus, made a little lower than the angels, now crowned with glory and honour because he died. 📖

By God's kindness, he would taste death for everyone.

**Help with this picture:** Tasting death means experiencing death.

## Hebrews 2:10

God is bringing many children to glory. 📖

Everything exists for him and through him.

It was fitting for him to make the author of their salvation perfect through suffering.

**Help with these words:** The author of salvation is Jesus, who leads them into salvation.

## Hebrews 2:11

The One making people holy and those made holy belong to the same family. 📖

So Jesus is not ashamed to call them brothers and sisters.

## Hebrews 2:12

He says, “I will tell my brothers and sisters your name and sing your praises in the gathering.” 📖

## Hebrews 2:13

He also says, “I will put my trust in him.” 📖

And again, “Here I am, with the children God has given me.”

## Hebrews 2:14

The children share flesh and blood, so Jesus shared their humanity too. 📖

Through his death, he could destroy the devil, who holds the power of death.

## Hebrews 2:15

He could free those who were slaves all their lives to the fear of death. 📖

## Hebrews 2:16

Surely he helps Abraham's descendants, rather than angels. 📖

## Hebrews 2:17

So he had to become like his brothers and sisters in every way. 📖

Then he could be a merciful and faithful high priest serving God, making atonement for the people's sins.

**Help with words:** A high priest is a leading priest who serves God on behalf of the people.

Atonement means dealing with sins to bring people into a right relationship with God.

## Hebrews 2:18

Because Jesus suffered when he was tempted, he can help people being tempted. 📖
