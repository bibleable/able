# Hebrews 10 — One offering and a call to endure

**About this chapter**

The writer contrasts repeated sacrifices with Christ's one offering.

He urges believers to meet, encourage each other, and remain faithful.

## Hebrews 10:1

The law is a shadow of coming good things, rather than the realities themselves. 📖

The same sacrifices offered yearly can never make those coming to worship perfect.

## Hebrews 10:2

If they could, would the offerings not have stopped? 📖

Worshippers would have been cleansed once for all and no longer felt guilty about their sins.

## Hebrews 10:3

Instead, those sacrifices remind people of sins every year. 📖

## Hebrews 10:4

Bulls' and goats' blood cannot take away sins. 📖

## Hebrews 10:5

When Christ came into the world, he said these words. 📖

“You did not desire sacrifice and offering, but you prepared a body for me.”

## Hebrews 10:6

“You took no pleasure in burnt offerings and sin offerings.” 📖

## Hebrews 10:7

“Then I said, ‘Here I am. 📖

The scroll speaks about me.

I have come to do your will, O God.’”

## Hebrews 10:8

First, he says, “You did not desire or delight in sacrifices, offerings, burnt offerings, or sin offerings.” 📖

Yet the law requires these offerings.

## Hebrews 10:9

Then he says, “Here I am; I have come to do your will.” 📖

He takes away the first to establish the second.

## Hebrews 10:10

By that will, we have been made holy through the offering of Jesus Christ's body once for all. 📖

## Hebrews 10:11

Every priest stands daily to serve and repeatedly offer the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins. 📖

## Hebrews 10:12

But this Priest offered one sacrifice for sins for all time, then sat at God's right hand. 📖

## Hebrews 10:13

Since then, he waits for his enemies to become his footstool. 📖

## Hebrews 10:14

By one offering, he has made perfect for all time those being made holy. 📖

## Hebrews 10:15

The Holy Spirit also testifies about this. 📖

First he says these words.

## Hebrews 10:16

“After those days, this is the covenant I will make with them, says the Lord. 📖

I will put my laws in their hearts and write them on their minds.”

## Hebrews 10:17

Then he adds, “I will remember their sins and lawless actions no more.” 📖

## Hebrews 10:18

Where these are forgiven, an offering for sin is no longer needed. 📖

## Hebrews 10:19

Brothers and sisters, we have confidence to enter the Most Holy Place through Jesus's blood. 📖

## Hebrews 10:20

He opened a new and living way for us through the curtain of his body. 📖

## Hebrews 10:21

We have a great priest over God's house. 📖

## Hebrews 10:22

So let us come near with sincere hearts and full assurance of faith. 📖

Our hearts have been sprinkled to cleanse a guilty conscience, and our bodies washed with pure water.

## Hebrews 10:23

Hold firmly to the hope we confess, because the One who promised is faithful. 📖

## Hebrews 10:24

Think how to encourage each other to love and good works. 📖

## Hebrews 10:25

Do not neglect meeting together, as some habitually do. 📖

Encourage each other even more as you see the Day approaching.

## Hebrews 10:26

If we deliberately keep sinning after receiving knowledge of the truth, no further sacrifice for sins remains. 📖

## Hebrews 10:27

Only a fearful expectation of judgment remains, and raging fire that will consume all enemies. 📖

## Hebrews 10:28

Anyone rejecting Moses's law died without mercy on the testimony of two or three witnesses. 📖

## Hebrews 10:29

Think how much more punishment someone deserves if they trample God's Son. 📖

Such a person profanes the covenant's blood that made them holy and insults the Spirit of kindness.

**Help with a word:** Profanes means treats something holy as common or unholy.

## Hebrews 10:30

We know the One who said, “Vengeance is mine; I will repay.” 📖

He also said, “The Lord will judge his people.”

## Hebrews 10:31

It is fearful to fall into the living God's hands. 📖

## Hebrews 10:32

Remember the early days when you received the light. 📖

You endured a great struggle through suffering.

## Hebrews 10:33

Sometimes you faced public ridicule and persecution. 📖

At other times, you shared with those treated that way.

## Hebrews 10:34

You cared about prisoners and joyfully accepted your property being taken, knowing you had a better, permanent possession. 📖

## Hebrews 10:35

So do not throw away your confidence, which brings a great reward. 📖

## Hebrews 10:36

You need endurance so that, after doing God's will, you receive his promise. 📖

## Hebrews 10:37

“In just a little while, the One coming will come and will not delay.” 📖

## Hebrews 10:38

“My righteous one will live by faith. 📖

But if he shrinks back, I will take no pleasure in him.”

## Hebrews 10:39

We belong to those having faith and preserving their souls, rather than those shrinking back to destruction. 📖
