# Hebrews 11 — People who trusted God

**About this chapter**

This chapter gives examples of faith.

Faith means trusting God and what he promises.

## Hebrews 11:1

Faith means being sure of what we hope for. 📖

It means being certain about what we cannot see.

## Hebrews 11:2

God approved the people of long ago because of their faith. 📖

## Hebrews 11:3

By faith, we understand that God made the universe by his command. 📖

What we see was not made from visible things.

## Hebrews 11:4

By faith, Abel gave God a better offering than Cain did. 📖

God approved his gifts and called him righteous.

Although Abel died, his faith still speaks.

**Help with a word:** Righteous means right with God.

## Hebrews 11:5

By faith, Enoch was taken away without dying. 📖

No one could find him because God took him.

Before this, God approved him as someone who pleased God.

## Hebrews 11:6

Without faith, no one can please God. 📖

Anyone coming to God must believe he exists.

They must believe he rewards those who truly seek him.

## Hebrews 11:7

God warned Noah about things no one had seen. 📖

By faith and respect for God, Noah built a great boat to save his family.

His faith condemned the world.

He received the righteousness that comes through faith.

## Hebrews 11:8

By faith, Abraham obeyed God's call to leave home. 📖

He went towards land he would later receive.

He did not know where he was going.

## Hebrews 11:9

By faith, Abraham lived as a foreigner in the promised land. 📖

He lived in tents, as Isaac and Jacob did.

They shared the same promise.

## Hebrews 11:10

Abraham looked forward to a city with strong foundations. 📖

God designed and built that city.

## Hebrews 11:11

By faith, Sarah was able to have a child, though she had been unable and was too old. 📖

She trusted the God who promised.

## Hebrews 11:12

From one very old man came countless descendants. 📖

They were as many as the stars or grains of sand beside the sea.

## Hebrews 11:13

All these people died still trusting God. 📖

They had not received the promised things.

But they saw and welcomed them from far away.

They admitted they were strangers living away from home on earth.

## Hebrews 11:14

People who speak this way show they are seeking their own homeland. 📖

## Hebrews 11:15

If they had meant the country they left, they could have returned. 📖

## Hebrews 11:16

They wanted a better country, a heavenly one. 📖

God is not ashamed to be called their God.

He has prepared a city for them.

## Hebrews 11:17

When tested, Abraham offered Isaac by faith. 📖

He had received God's promises.

Yet he was ready to offer his one and only son.

## Hebrews 11:18

God had told him that his promised family line would come through Isaac. 📖

## Hebrews 11:19

Abraham believed God could raise the dead. 📖

In a picture of this, he received Isaac back from death.

## Hebrews 11:20

By faith, Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau about their future. 📖

## Hebrews 11:21

By faith, dying Jacob blessed each of Joseph's sons. 📖

He worshipped while leaning on his walking stick.

## Hebrews 11:22

By faith, Joseph spoke near death about Israel leaving Egypt. 📖

He gave instructions about taking his bones with them.

## Hebrews 11:23

By faith, Moses' parents hid him for 3 months after his birth. 📖

They saw he was a beautiful child.

They were not afraid of the king's order.

## Hebrews 11:24

By faith, grown-up Moses refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter. 📖

## Hebrews 11:25

He chose to suffer with God's people. 📖

He refused the brief pleasure of sin.

## Hebrews 11:26

He valued suffering disgrace for Christ more than Egypt's treasures. 📖

He looked ahead to his reward.

## Hebrews 11:27

By faith, Moses left Egypt without fearing the king's anger. 📖

He kept going as if he could see the invisible God.

## Hebrews 11:28

By faith, he kept Passover and had blood sprinkled. 📖

Then the destroyer of firstborn children would not harm Israel's firstborn.

## Hebrews 11:29

By faith, the people crossed the Red Sea as on dry ground. 📖

When the Egyptians followed, they drowned.

## Hebrews 11:30

By faith, the walls of Jericho fell. 📖

The people had marched around them for 7 days.

## Hebrews 11:31

By faith, Rahab welcomed the spies peacefully. 📖

She did not die with those who disobeyed.

Rahab had been a prostitute.

**Help with a word:** A prostitute is someone paid for sex.

## Hebrews 11:32

There is not enough time to tell about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets. 📖

## Hebrews 11:33

Through faith, they defeated kingdoms, ruled fairly, and received promises. 📖

They shut lions' mouths.

## Hebrews 11:34

They stopped fierce fire and escaped swords. 📖

They became strong from weakness.

They fought powerfully and drove away foreign armies.

## Hebrews 11:35

Women received their dead family members back alive. 📖

Others were tortured and refused release.

They hoped to rise to a better life.

## Hebrews 11:36

Others suffered insults and whipping. 📖

They were chained and put in prison.

## Hebrews 11:37

Some were killed with stones.

Some were sawed in half.

Others were killed with swords. 📖

They wore sheep and goat skins.

They were poor, oppressed, and badly treated.

## Hebrews 11:38

The world was not worthy of them. 📖

They wandered in deserts and mountains.

They hid in caves and holes in the ground.

## Hebrews 11:39

God approved all these people for their faith. 📖

Yet they did not receive what was promised.

## Hebrews 11:40

God had planned something better for us. 📖

They would be made complete only together with us.
