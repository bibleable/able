# Hebrews 9 — Christ enters with his own blood

**About this chapter**

The writer describes the earthly worship tent and compares its sacrifices with Christ's offering.

## Hebrews 9:1

The first covenant had rules for worship and an earthly sanctuary. 📖

## Hebrews 9:2

A tabernacle was prepared. 📖

Its first room held the lampstand, table, and bread set apart for God.

This room was called the Holy Place.

## Hebrews 9:3

Behind the second curtain was the room called the Most Holy Place. 📖

## Hebrews 9:4

It held the golden incense altar and the gold-covered ark of the covenant. 📖

Inside the ark were a gold jar of manna, Aaron's staff that had sprouted, and the covenant's stone tablets.

**Help with words:** Incense makes sweet-smelling smoke when burned.

The ark was a sacred chest.

Manna was the food God provided in the desert.

## Hebrews 9:5

Above the ark were the cherubim of glory, overshadowing the mercy seat. 📖

But we cannot discuss these things in detail now.

**Help with words:** Cherubim are heavenly beings pictured above the ark.

The mercy seat was the ark's special cover.

## Hebrews 9:6

With everything prepared this way, priests regularly entered the first room to perform their sacred duties. 📖

## Hebrews 9:7

Only the high priest entered the second room, once a year. 📖

He never entered without blood, offered for himself and for the people's sins committed in ignorance.

## Hebrews 9:8

The Holy Spirit showed that the way into the Most Holy Place remained hidden while the first tabernacle stood. 📖

## Hebrews 9:9

This illustrates the present time. 📖

The gifts and sacrifices offered could not cleanse the worshipper's conscience.

## Hebrews 9:10

They concern only food, drink, and special washings, outward rules imposed until the time of reform. 📖

**Help with a word:** Reform means putting things right in a new arrangement.

## Hebrews 9:11

Christ came as high priest of the good things that have come. 📖

He passed through the greater and more perfect tabernacle.

Human hands did not make it, and it is not part of this creation.

## Hebrews 9:12

He entered the Most Holy Place once for all through his own blood, rather than goats' and calves' blood. 📖

He secured freedom that lasts for ever.

## Hebrews 9:13

If goats' and bulls' blood and a heifer's ashes make the unclean holy and physically clean... 📖

These are sprinkled on those considered unclean.

**Help with words:** A heifer is a young cow.

Unclean here means not meeting religious purity rules.

## Hebrews 9:14

How much more will Christ's blood cleanse our consciences from works of death so we may serve the living God! 📖

Through the eternal Spirit, Christ offered himself without fault to God.

## Hebrews 9:15

Christ therefore mediates a new covenant so those called can receive the promised eternal inheritance. 📖

He died to free them from the wrongdoing committed under the first covenant.

## Hebrews 9:16

With a will, the death of the person who made it must be established. 📖

**Help with a word:** A will records who receives someone's property after they die.

## Hebrews 9:17

A will takes effect only after its maker dies. 📖

It cannot take effect while that person lives.

## Hebrews 9:18

That is why even the first covenant was not put into effect without blood. 📖

## Hebrews 9:19

Moses told all the people every command of the law. 📖

He took calves' and goats' blood with water, scarlet wool, and hyssop, and sprinkled the scroll and everyone.

**Help with words:** Scarlet is a bright red colour.

Hyssop is a plant used in sprinkling.

## Hebrews 9:20

He said, “This is the blood of the covenant God commanded you to keep.” 📖

## Hebrews 9:21

He also sprinkled blood on the tabernacle and all the vessels used in worship. 📖

## Hebrews 9:22

According to the law, nearly everything must be cleansed with blood. 📖

Without blood being shed, there is no forgiveness.

## Hebrews 9:23

The copies of heavenly things needed cleansing with these sacrifices. 📖

The heavenly things themselves needed better sacrifices.

## Hebrews 9:24

Christ entered heaven itself, rather than a human-made copy of the true sanctuary. 📖

He now appears before God on our behalf.

## Hebrews 9:25

He did not enter to offer himself repeatedly. 📖

The high priest enters the Most Holy Place yearly with blood that is not his own.

## Hebrews 9:26

Otherwise, Christ would have suffered repeatedly since the world was founded. 📖

But he appeared once for all at the ages' end to remove sin by sacrificing himself.

## Hebrews 9:27

People are appointed to die once and face judgment afterwards. 📖

## Hebrews 9:28

Christ too was offered once to carry the sins of many. 📖

He will appear a second time to bring salvation to those eagerly waiting for him, rather than to bear sin.
