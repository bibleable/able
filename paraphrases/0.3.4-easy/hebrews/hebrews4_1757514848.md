# Hebrews 4 — God's rest and our high priest

**About this chapter**

The writer urges believers to enter God's rest and hold firmly to Jesus.

## Hebrews 4:1

God's promise of entering his rest still stands. 📖

So let us be careful that none of you falls short of it.

## Hebrews 4:2

We received the good news just as they did. 📖

But their message did not benefit them, because they did not share the faith of those who understood it.

## Hebrews 4:3

We who believe enter that rest. 📖

About the others, God said, “In my anger I swore, ‘They will never enter my rest.’”

Yet his works have been finished since the world was founded.

## Hebrews 4:4

Somewhere God spoke about the seventh day this way. 📖

“On the seventh day, God rested from all his works.”

## Hebrews 4:5

Again, the earlier passage says, “They will never enter my rest.” 📖

## Hebrews 4:6

Some can still enter his rest. 📖

Those who first heard the good news did not enter because they disobeyed.

## Hebrews 4:7

So God again named a day, “Today,” speaking through David a long time later. 📖

He said, “Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts.”

## Hebrews 4:8

If Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken later about another day. 📖

## Hebrews 4:9

So a Sabbath rest remains for God's people. 📖

**Help with a word:** The Sabbath is the day of rest in Jewish worship.

## Hebrews 4:10

Anyone entering God's rest also rests from their own work, just as God rested from his. 📖

## Hebrews 4:11

Let us make every effort to enter that rest. 📖

Then no one will fall by following the same pattern of disobedience.

## Hebrews 4:12

God's word is living and active, sharper than any sword with two sharp edges. 📖

It pierces to divide soul and spirit, joints and marrow.

It judges the heart's thoughts and intentions.

**Help with this picture:** The sword pictures God's word reaching the deepest parts of a person.

Marrow is the soft material inside bones.

## Hebrews 4:13

Nothing in creation is hidden from God. 📖

Everything is uncovered and exposed before the One to whom we must give an account.

## Hebrews 4:14

We have a great high priest who passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God. 📖

So let us hold firmly to what we profess.

## Hebrews 4:15

Our high priest understands our weaknesses. 📖

He was tempted in every way we are, yet did not sin.

## Hebrews 4:16

So let us come confidently to the throne of kindness. 📖

Then we can receive mercy and find kindness to help when we need it.
