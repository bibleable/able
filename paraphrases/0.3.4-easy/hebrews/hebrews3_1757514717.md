# Hebrews 3 — Listen to God's voice today

**About this chapter**

The writer compares Jesus with Moses and warns about unbelief.

The chapter recalls the people who rebelled in the desert.

## Hebrews 3:1

Holy brothers and sisters sharing the heavenly call, focus on Jesus, the apostle and high priest we confess. 📖

**Help with words:** Holy means set apart for God.

An apostle is someone sent for a special task.

## Hebrews 3:2

Jesus was faithful to the One who appointed him, as Moses was faithful in all God's house. 📖

## Hebrews 3:3

Jesus deserves greater glory than Moses, just as a house's builder deserves greater honour than the house. 📖

## Hebrews 3:4

Someone builds every house, but God builds everything. 📖

## Hebrews 3:5

Moses faithfully served in all God's house, testifying to things that would be spoken later. 📖

## Hebrews 3:6

Christ is faithful as the Son over God's house. 📖

We are his house if we hold firmly to our confidence and the hope we boast about.

**Help with this picture:** God's house pictures his people.

## Hebrews 3:7

So the Holy Spirit says, “Today, if you hear his voice...” 📖

## Hebrews 3:8

“Do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion, on the day of testing in the desert.” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Hardening hearts pictures stubbornly refusing to listen to God.

## Hebrews 3:9

“Your ancestors tested and tried me there and saw my works for 40 years.” 📖

## Hebrews 3:10

“So I was angry with that generation. 📖

I said, ‘Their hearts always wander, and they have not known my ways.’”

## Hebrews 3:11

“In my anger, I swore on oath, ‘They will never enter my rest.’” 📖

**Help with a word:** An oath is a serious promise or declaration.

## Hebrews 3:12

Brothers and sisters, make sure none of you has an evil, unbelieving heart turning from the living God. 📖

## Hebrews 3:13

Encourage each other daily while it is called today. 📖

Then none of you will be hardened by sin's deceitfulness.

## Hebrews 3:14

We share in Christ if we hold firmly to the end the confidence we had at first. 📖

## Hebrews 3:15

“Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion.” 📖

## Hebrews 3:16

Who heard and rebelled? 📖

Was it not everyone Moses led out of Egypt?

## Hebrews 3:17

Who made God angry for 40 years? 📖

Was it not those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the desert?

## Hebrews 3:18

To whom did God swear they would never enter his rest? 📖

Was it not those who disobeyed?

## Hebrews 3:19

So we see that unbelief kept them from entering. 📖
