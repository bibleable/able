# Hebrews 5 — Jesus is appointed high priest

**About this chapter**

The writer explains a high priest's work and Christ's calling.

He urges the readers to grow beyond the first lessons of faith.

## Hebrews 5:1

Every high priest is chosen from among people to represent them before God and offer gifts and sacrifices for sins. 📖

## Hebrews 5:2

He can deal gently with people who do not know and have gone astray, because he also has weaknesses. 📖

## Hebrews 5:3

That is why he must offer sacrifices for his own sins as well as the people's sins. 📖

## Hebrews 5:4

No one takes this honour for himself. 📖

God must call him, as he called Aaron.

## Hebrews 5:5

Christ did not take the glory of becoming high priest for himself. 📖

The One who called him said, “You are my Son; today I have become your Father.”

## Hebrews 5:6

In another passage, God says, “You are a priest for ever in the order of Melchizedek.” 📖

**Help with these words:** Order means the kind of priesthood associated with Melchizedek.

Chapter 7 explains the comparison.

## Hebrews 5:7

During his earthly life, Jesus offered prayers and requests with loud cries and tears. 📖

He prayed to the One able to save him from death.

He was heard because of his reverence.

**Help with a word:** Reverence means deep respect for God.

## Hebrews 5:8

Although he was a Son, he learned obedience through what he suffered. 📖

## Hebrews 5:9

Made perfect, he became the source of salvation lasting for ever for everyone who obeys him. 📖

## Hebrews 5:10

God appointed him high priest in the order of Melchizedek. 📖

## Hebrews 5:11

We have much to say about this, but it is hard to explain because you have become slow to listen. 📖

## Hebrews 5:12

By now, you should be teachers. 📖

Instead, you need someone to teach you the first lessons of God's word again.

You need milk, rather than solid food!

## Hebrews 5:13

Anyone living on milk is still a baby, inexperienced in the message of righteousness. 📖

## Hebrews 5:14

Solid food is for mature people whose senses are trained through constant use to tell good from evil. 📖

**Help with these pictures:** Milk and solid food picture stages of learning and growing in faith.
