# 2 Corinthians 7 — Sorrow that leads to change

**About this chapter**

Paul describes the comfort Titus brought and the church's response to his painful letter.

## 2 Corinthians 7:1

Dearly loved friends, since we have these promises, let us cleanse ourselves of everything that defiles body and spirit. 📖

Let us bring holiness to completion in the fear of God.

**Help:** Defiles here means makes impure in a moral or religious sense.

Holiness means being set apart for God.

Fear of God includes serious respect for him and his judgment.

## 2 Corinthians 7:2

Make room for us in your hearts. 📖

We have wronged no one, corrupted no one, and taken advantage of no one.

## 2 Corinthians 7:3

I do not say this to condemn you. 📖

As I said before, you are in our hearts, to live and die together with you.

## 2 Corinthians 7:4

I have great confidence in you and great pride in you. 📖

I am filled with encouragement.

In all our troubles, my joy overflows.

## 2 Corinthians 7:5

When we arrived in Macedonia, our bodies had no rest. 📖

We were pressed from every side, with conflicts outside and fears inside.

## 2 Corinthians 7:6

But God, who comforts those weighed down with sadness, comforted us through Titus's arrival. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 7:7

His arrival comforted us, and so did the comfort he received from you. 📖

He told us of your longing, mourning, and eagerness for me, so I rejoiced even more.

## 2 Corinthians 7:8

Even if my letter made you sorrowful, I do not regret it. 📖

I did regret it, because I see it made you sorrowful, but only briefly.

## 2 Corinthians 7:9

Now I rejoice because your sorrow led you to repentance, rather than because you were sorrowful. 📖

You felt the sorrow God intended, so we did not harm you in any way.

**Help:** Repentance means turning from sin towards God.

## 2 Corinthians 7:10

Godly sorrow brings repentance leading to salvation without regret, but worldly sorrow brings death. 📖

**About this distinction:** Paul discusses their response to wrongdoing and his letter.

This is not a diagnosis or a claim that grief or depression is itself a moral failure.

## 2 Corinthians 7:11

Look at what this godly sorrow produced in you. 📖

It brought earnest care, eagerness to clear yourselves, indignation, alarm, longing, zeal, and a desire to see justice done.

You have shown yourselves innocent in this matter in every way.

**Help:** Indignation means strong anger about wrongdoing.

Zeal means strong commitment and eagerness.

## 2 Corinthians 7:12

I wrote neither because of the wrongdoer nor because of the person harmed. 📖

I wrote so your earnest care for us would become clear to you in God's sight.

## 2 Corinthians 7:13

This encourages us. 📖

Beyond our own encouragement, Titus's joy delighted us even more.

You all refreshed his spirit.

## 2 Corinthians 7:14

Nothing I boasted to him about you embarrassed me. 📖

Everything we told you was true, and our boasting to Titus also proved true.

## 2 Corinthians 7:15

His affection grows as he remembers your obedience and how you welcomed him with fear and trembling. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 7:16

I rejoice that I can have complete confidence in you. 📖
