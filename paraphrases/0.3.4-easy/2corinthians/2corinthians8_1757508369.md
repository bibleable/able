# 2 Corinthians 8 — Willing and fair giving

**About this chapter**

Paul encourages the Corinthians to finish their promised gift.

He describes generous giving, fairness, and care in handling the money.

## 2 Corinthians 8:1

Brothers and sisters, we want you to know about God's kindness given to the Macedonian churches. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:2

During terrible suffering, their great joy and deep poverty overflowed into rich generosity. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:3

I testify that they gave as much as they could, and even more, of their own choice. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:4

They earnestly begged us for the privilege of sharing in this service to God's people. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:5

They went beyond what we expected. 📖

By God's will, they gave themselves first to the Lord and then to us.

## 2 Corinthians 8:6

So we urged Titus to help finish your act of kindness, just as he had started it. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:7

You excel in faith, speech, knowledge, complete earnestness, and the love we inspired in you. 📖

Make sure you also excel in this kindness of giving.

**Help:** Excel means do especially well.

Earnestness means serious care and commitment.

## 2 Corinthians 8:8

I am not giving a command. 📖

Through others' earnest care, I am testing whether your love is sincere.

## 2 Corinthians 8:9

You know our Lord Jesus Christ's kindness. 📖

Although rich, he became poor for your sake, so through his poverty you might become rich.

## 2 Corinthians 8:10

Here is my opinion about what helps you in this matter. 📖

Last year, you were first both to give and to desire to give.

## 2 Corinthians 8:11

Now finish the work with the same eager desire, giving according to what you have. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:12

If you are eager, the gift is accepted according to what you have, rather than what you lack. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:13

We do not intend to give others relief while burdening you. 📖

We want equality.

## 2 Corinthians 8:14

Now your extra supplies will meet their need, so later their extra supplies will meet yours. 📖

This will bring equality.

## 2 Corinthians 8:15

The Scriptures say, “The person gathering much had no extra, and the person gathering little had no shortage.” 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:16

Thanks to God, who put the same devoted care for you in Titus's heart as in mine. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:17

He welcomed our appeal and is eagerly coming to you by his own choice. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:18

With Titus, we send the brother all the churches praise for his work in the good news. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:19

The churches also chose this brother to travel with us as we manage this generous offering. 📖

We do this to honour the Lord himself and show our eagerness to help.

## 2 Corinthians 8:20

We want to avoid criticism about how we manage this generous gift. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:21

We take great care to do right in both the Lord's eyes and people's eyes. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 8:22

We also send our brother who has proved his earnest care many times in many ways. 📖

He is even more earnest now because of his great confidence in you.

## 2 Corinthians 8:23

Titus is my partner and fellow worker among you. 📖

Our brothers are the churches' messengers, the glory of Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 8:24

Before all the churches, show these men proof of your love and why we boast about you. 📖
