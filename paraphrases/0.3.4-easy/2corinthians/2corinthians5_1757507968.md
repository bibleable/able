# 2 Corinthians 5 — God makes us new in Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul speaks about life beyond death and being brought back to God.

## 2 Corinthians 5:1

Our body is like a tent on earth. 📖

If it is destroyed, God has an everlasting home for us in heaven.

Human hands did not build it.

## 2 Corinthians 5:2

In this earthly tent, we groan. 📖

We long to put on our heavenly home like clothing.

## 2 Corinthians 5:3

When clothed in it, we will not be naked. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Paul is speaking about the life God gives beyond our present bodies.

## 2 Corinthians 5:4

Life in this tent burdens us, and we groan. 📖

We do not want to be unclothed.

We want new clothing, so life swallows up what can die.

## 2 Corinthians 5:5

God prepared us for this. 📖

He gave us the Spirit as his guarantee of what is coming.

## 2 Corinthians 5:6

So we are always confident. 📖

While we are at home in our bodies, we are away from the Lord.

## 2 Corinthians 5:7

We live by trusting God, rather than by what we can see. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 5:8

We are confident. 📖

We would prefer to leave the body and be at home with the Lord.

## 2 Corinthians 5:9

Whether in this body or away from it, we aim to please him. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 5:10

We must all stand before Christ to be judged. 📖

Each will receive what is due for actions done in the body, good or bad.

## 2 Corinthians 5:11

We know what it means to fear the Lord, so we try to persuade people. 📖

God knows what we are.

I hope your conscience knows too.

**Help with a word:** Conscience is the inner sense of right and wrong.

## 2 Corinthians 5:12

We are not praising ourselves again. 📖

We give you a reason to be proud of us.

You can answer those who boast about appearances instead of the heart.

## 2 Corinthians 5:13

If we seem out of our minds, it is for God. 📖

If we seem sensible, it is for you.

## 2 Corinthians 5:14

Christ's love moves us to act. 📖

We are sure that one died for all.

So all died.

## 2 Corinthians 5:15

He died for all so that those who live would stop living only for themselves. 📖

They should live for him who died and was raised for them.

## 2 Corinthians 5:16

We no longer judge people by ordinary human standards. 📖

We once viewed Christ that way.

We do not do so now.

## 2 Corinthians 5:17

Anyone who is in Christ is a new creation. 📖

The old has gone.

The new has come!

## 2 Corinthians 5:18

All this comes from God. 📖

Through Christ, he brought us back into friendship with himself.

He gave us the work of bringing others back too.

## 2 Corinthians 5:19

In Christ, God was bringing the world back to himself. 📖

He was not counting people's wrongs against them.

He gave us this message to share.

## 2 Corinthians 5:20

We speak as Christ's representatives. 📖

God makes his appeal through us.

On Christ's behalf, we beg you: be brought back to God.

## 2 Corinthians 5:21

Christ had no sin. 📖

God made him to be sin for us.

In him, we could become right with God.

**Help with this verse:** Christ took our sin upon himself so we could be accepted by God.
