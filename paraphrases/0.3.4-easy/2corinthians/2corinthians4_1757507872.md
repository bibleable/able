# 2 Corinthians 4 — Treasure in clay jars

**About this chapter**

Paul explains that his work depends on God's power.

He describes suffering, hope in resurrection, and lasting glory.

## 2 Corinthians 4:1

Since God mercifully gave us this service, we do not lose heart. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 4:2

We have rejected secret, shameful ways. 📖

We do not deceive people or twist God's word.

Instead, openly stating the truth, we recommend ourselves to everyone's conscience in God's sight.

## 2 Corinthians 4:3

Even if our good news is veiled, it is veiled to those who are perishing. 📖

**Help:** Veiled means hidden, like something covered by a cloth.

## 2 Corinthians 4:4

This age's god has blinded unbelievers' minds so they cannot see the light of Christ's glorious good news. 📖

Christ is God's image.

**Help:** This age's god refers to Satan.

Blinded minds pictures not recognising the message, rather than a physical sight condition.

## 2 Corinthians 4:5

We preach Jesus Christ as Lord, rather than ourselves. 📖

We present ourselves as your servants for Jesus' sake.

## 2 Corinthians 4:6

God said, “Let light shine out of darkness.” 📖

He made his light shine in our hearts.

He gives us the light of knowing God's glory in Jesus Christ's face.

## 2 Corinthians 4:7

We have this treasure in clay jars. 📖

This shows that the power, greater than anything else, comes from God rather than us.

**Help:** Clay jars picture the fragile people who carry the treasure of God's message.

## 2 Corinthians 4:8

We are pressed hard on every side, but not crushed. 📖

We are confused, but not without hope.

## 2 Corinthians 4:9

We are persecuted, but not abandoned. 📖

We are struck down, but not destroyed.

**Help:** Persecuted means treated cruelly because of faith.

## 2 Corinthians 4:10

We always carry Jesus' death in our bodies, so his life may also be shown in our bodies. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 4:11

We who live are always handed over to death for Jesus' sake. 📖

Then Jesus' life may also be shown in our bodies that can die.

## 2 Corinthians 4:12

So death works in us, but life works in you. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 4:13

The Scriptures say, “I believed, so I spoke.” 📖

With that same spirit of faith, we also believe and therefore speak,

## 2 Corinthians 4:14

knowing that the one who raised the Lord Jesus will also raise us with Jesus. 📖

He will bring us with you into his presence.

## 2 Corinthians 4:15

All this is for your benefit. 📖

As God's kindness reaches more and more people, thanksgiving can overflow to God's glory.

## 2 Corinthians 4:16

So we do not lose heart. 📖

Though our outer self wastes away, our inner self is being renewed each day.

## 2 Corinthians 4:17

Our light, brief troubles produce an eternal weight of glory for us, far beyond comparison. 📖

**Help:** Weight of glory pictures something lasting and great beyond the suffering Paul describes.

## 2 Corinthians 4:18

We fix our eyes on unseen things rather than seen things. 📖

Seen things last for a time, but unseen things last for ever.
