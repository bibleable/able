# 2 Corinthians 6 — Faithful service and open hearts

**About this chapter**

Paul describes the hardships and qualities of God's servants.

He asks the Corinthians to open their hearts and warns against joining incompatible loyalties.

## 2 Corinthians 6:1

As God's fellow workers, we urge you not to let the kindness you received from God be wasted. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:2

He says, “In the time of favour, I heard you. 📖

On the day of salvation, I helped you.”

Now is the time of favour!

Now is the day of salvation!

## 2 Corinthians 6:3

We put no obstacle in anyone's way, so no one can discredit our service. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:4

As God's servants, we show ourselves faithful in every way: through great endurance, troubles, hardships, and disasters; 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:5

through beatings, prison, riots, hard work, sleepless nights, and hunger; 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:6

through purity, knowledge, patience, kindness, the Holy Spirit, and sincere love; 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:7

through truthful speech and God's power, with righteousness as weapons in both right and left hands; 📖

**Help:** Righteousness means being right before God and doing right.

The weapons picture being equipped for service.

## 2 Corinthians 6:8

through honour and dishonour, slander and praise. 📖

We are seen as frauds, yet we are genuine;

**Help:** Slander means telling lies that harm someone's name.

## 2 Corinthians 6:9

unknown, yet well known; dying, yet still living; punished, yet not killed; 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:10

sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; poor, yet making many rich; having nothing, yet possessing everything. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:11

Corinthians, we have spoken freely to you, and our hearts are wide open. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:12

Your affection is held back, rather than ours. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:13

I speak to you as my children: in a fair exchange, open your hearts wide too. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 6:14

Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. 📖

What partnership can righteousness have with wickedness?

What shared life can light have with darkness?

**Help:** A yoke joins working animals together.

Being unequally yoked pictures being joined in a way that pulls in incompatible directions.

Paul's full meaning and application need further editorial review.

## 2 Corinthians 6:15

What harmony can Christ have with Belial? 📖

What does a believer have in common with an unbeliever?

**Help:** Belial is a name for Satan.

## 2 Corinthians 6:16

What agreement can God's temple have with idols? 📖

We are the living God's temple.

God said, “I will live with them and walk among them.

I will be their God, and they will be my people.”

**Help:** A temple is a place where God lives.

Idols are things worshipped as gods.

## 2 Corinthians 6:17

“So come out from among them and be separate, says the Lord. 📖

Touch nothing unclean, and I will receive you.”

**Help:** Unclean here concerns what is unacceptable to God, rather than physical dirt, illness, or disability.

## 2 Corinthians 6:18

“I will be your Father, and you will be my sons and daughters, says the Lord Almighty.” 📖
