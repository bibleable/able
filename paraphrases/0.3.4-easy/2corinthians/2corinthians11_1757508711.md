# 2 Corinthians 11 — True service and suffering

**About this chapter**

Paul warns about false apostles and responds to boasting by describing his weakness and suffering.

He uses irony to challenge the church's acceptance of mistreatment.

## 2 Corinthians 11:1

I hope you will bear with a little foolishness from me, as you are already doing. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:2

I am jealous for you with God's kind of jealousy. 📖

I promised you to one husband, to present you as a pure virgin to Christ.

**Help:** Paul pictures the church as a bride promised to Christ.

A virgin is someone who has not had sexual relations.

## 2 Corinthians 11:3

The serpent cunningly deceived Eve. 📖

I fear your minds may likewise be led away from simple, pure devotion to Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 11:4

If someone preaches a different Jesus from the one we preached, you bear with it easily. 📖

It is the same if you receive a different spirit from the one you received.

It is also the same if you receive different good news from the one you accepted.

## 2 Corinthians 11:5

I consider myself in no way below those “super-apostles.” 📖

**Help:** Paul uses this title for people claiming superior authority.

## 2 Corinthians 11:6

Although not a polished speaker, I certainly do not lack knowledge. 📖

We have made this clear to you in every possible way.

## 2 Corinthians 11:7

Did I sin by lowering myself to lift you up, preaching God's good news free of charge? 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:8

I robbed other churches by accepting their support to serve you. 📖

**Help:** Robbed is Paul's sharp description of taking support from other churches while serving Corinth without charging them.

## 2 Corinthians 11:9

When I was with you and in need, I burdened no one. 📖

The brothers arriving from Macedonia supplied my needs.

I have avoided burdening you in any way, and I will keep doing so.

## 2 Corinthians 11:10

As surely as Christ's truth is in me, no one in Achaia's regions will silence this boasting. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:11

Why? 📖

Because I do not love you?

God knows I do!

## 2 Corinthians 11:12

I will keep doing this to cut off an opportunity others seek. 📖

They want to be considered our equals in what they boast about.

## 2 Corinthians 11:13

Such men are false apostles, deceitful workers pretending to be Christ's apostles. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:14

No wonder, because Satan himself pretends to be an angel of light. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:15

So it is no surprise if his servants pretend to be servants of righteousness. 📖

Their end will match their actions.

**Help:** Righteousness concerns being right before God and doing right.

## 2 Corinthians 11:16

Again, let no one consider me a fool. 📖

But if you do, receive me as a fool so I too can boast a little.

## 2 Corinthians 11:17

In this confident boasting, I speak as a fool, rather than as the Lord would. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:18

Since many boast according to the flesh, I too will boast. 📖

**Help:** According to the flesh here concerns merely human standards.

## 2 Corinthians 11:19

You gladly bear with fools, since you are so wise! 📖

**Help:** Paul uses irony, saying something sharply to expose the opposite behaviour.

## 2 Corinthians 11:20

You even bear with people who enslave you, exploit you, take advantage of you, exalt themselves, or slap your face. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:21

To my shame, I admit we were too weak for that! 📖

Speaking as a fool, I can match anything others dare to boast about.

## 2 Corinthians 11:22

Are they Hebrews? 📖

So am I.

Are they Israelites?

So am I.

Are they Abraham's descendants?

So am I.

## 2 Corinthians 11:23

Are they Christ's servants? 📖

I speak as though out of my mind: I am much more.

I have worked harder, been imprisoned more, suffered worse beatings, and often faced death.

## 2 Corinthians 11:24

Five times, I received forty lashes minus one from the Jews. 📖

**Help:** Lashes are strikes with a whip.

Forty minus one is thirty-nine.

## 2 Corinthians 11:25

Three times I was beaten with rods, once stoned, and three times shipwrecked. 📖

I spent a night and a day in the open sea.

**Help:** Stoned means attacked with thrown stones.

Shipwrecked means being on a ship that is destroyed or lost at sea.

## 2 Corinthians 11:26

On frequent journeys, I faced danger from rivers, bandits, my own countrymen, and Gentiles. 📖

I faced danger in cities, in the countryside, at sea, and among false believers,

**Help:** Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## 2 Corinthians 11:27

through work and toil, often without sleep, hungry and thirsty, often without food, cold and exposed. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:28

Besides these outward trials, my concern for all the churches presses on me daily. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:29

Who is weak without my being weak too? 📖

Who is led into sin without my burning with grief?

## 2 Corinthians 11:30

If I must boast, I will boast about what shows my weakness. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:31

The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, worthy of praise for ever, knows I am not lying. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:32

In Damascus, the governor under King Aretas guarded the city of the Damascenes to arrest me. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 11:33

But I was lowered in a basket through a window in the wall and escaped his grasp. 📖
