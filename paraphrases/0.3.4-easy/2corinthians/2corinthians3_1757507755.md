# 2 Corinthians 3 — The Spirit and the new covenant

**About this chapter**

Paul calls the believers a letter from Christ.

He compares the old and new covenants through the images of stone, hearts, glory, and a veil.

## 2 Corinthians 3:1

Are we starting to recommend ourselves again? 📖

Do we need letters recommending us to you or from you, as some people do?

## 2 Corinthians 3:2

You yourselves are our letter, written on our hearts, known and read by everyone. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:3

Clearly, you are a letter from Christ, the result of our service. 📖

It is written with the living God's Spirit rather than ink, on human hearts rather than stone tablets.

## 2 Corinthians 3:4

Through Christ, we have this confidence before God. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:5

We are not able by ourselves to claim that anything comes from us. 📖

Our ability comes from God.

## 2 Corinthians 3:6

He made us able to serve a new covenant of the Spirit, rather than the written letter. 📖

The letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.

**Help:** A covenant is a binding agreement or relationship established by God.

The written letter here refers to the law in Paul's covenant comparison.

## 2 Corinthians 3:7

Suppose the service bringing death, carved in letters on stone, came with glory. 📖

The Israelites could not gaze at Moses' face because of its fading glory.

## 2 Corinthians 3:8

Will the service of the Spirit not have even greater glory? 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:9

If the service bringing condemnation had glory, how much greater is the glory of the service bringing righteousness! 📖

**Help:** Condemnation means being judged guilty.

Righteousness means being right before God.

## 2 Corinthians 3:10

What once had glory now has no glory compared with the glory that is so much greater. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:11

If what faded away came with glory, how much greater is the glory of what lasts! 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:12

Because we have this hope, we are very bold. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:13

We are unlike Moses, who covered his face with a veil. 📖

He kept the Israelites from gazing at the end of what was fading away.

**Help:** A veil is a cloth covering.

## 2 Corinthians 3:14

But their minds were closed. 📖

Even today, that same veil remains when the old covenant is read.

It has not been lifted, because only in Christ can it be removed.

## 2 Corinthians 3:15

Even today, a veil covers their hearts when Moses is read. 📖

**Help:** Reading Moses means reading the law associated with Moses.

The veil over hearts pictures not understanding.

Paul's covenant argument should not be expanded into claims about the character or abilities of Jewish people today.

## 2 Corinthians 3:16

Whenever anyone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:17

The Lord is the Spirit, and where the Lord's Spirit is, there is freedom. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 3:18

With unveiled faces, we all reflect the Lord's glory. 📖

We are being changed into his image with ever greater glory.

This comes from the Lord, who is the Spirit.
