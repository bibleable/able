# 2 Corinthians 1 — Comfort and faithful promises

**About this chapter**

Paul and Timothy greet the believers in Corinth and Achaia.

Paul explains God's comfort, his troubles, and his changed travel plans.

## 2 Corinthians 1:1

From Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, and Timothy our brother. 📖

To God's church in Corinth and all God's holy people throughout Achaia.

**Help:** An apostle is someone sent to carry Christ's message.

Achaia was the region that included Corinth.

## 2 Corinthians 1:2

May kindness and peace come to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 1:3

Praise the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the compassionate Father and God of all comfort. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 1:4

He comforts us in all our troubles. 📖

Then we can comfort people in any trouble with the comfort we received from God.

## 2 Corinthians 1:5

Just as Christ's sufferings overflow to us, our comfort also overflows through Christ. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 1:6

If we suffer trouble, it is for your comfort and salvation. 📖

If we receive comfort, it is for your comfort.

This helps you patiently endure the same sufferings we experience.

## 2 Corinthians 1:7

Our hope for you is sure. 📖

We know that, just as you share our sufferings, you will also share our comfort.

## 2 Corinthians 1:8

Brothers and sisters, we want you to know about our hardships in the province of Asia. 📖

The burden was far beyond our strength to endure.

We lost hope of even staying alive.

**Help:** Asia here was a Roman province, rather than the whole continent now called Asia.

## 2 Corinthians 1:9

We felt we had received a death sentence. 📖

This was so we would trust God, who raises the dead, rather than ourselves.

## 2 Corinthians 1:10

He rescued us from such deadly danger, and he will rescue us. 📖

We have placed our hope in him to rescue us again,

## 2 Corinthians 1:11

as you help us through prayer. 📖

Then many will thank God for us, for the favour given us in answer to their prayers.

## 2 Corinthians 1:12

We boast of this: our conscience testifies that we have lived with holiness and sincerity from God. 📖

This is how we acted in the world, especially towards you.

We relied on God's kindness rather than worldly wisdom.

**Help:** Conscience means a person's sense of right and wrong.

Holiness means being set apart for God; sincerity means being honest and genuine.

## 2 Corinthians 1:13

We write nothing beyond your ability to read and understand. 📖

I hope you will understand us fully,

## 2 Corinthians 1:14

as you already partly understand us. 📖

Then you can boast of us on our Lord Jesus' day, just as we will boast of you.

## 2 Corinthians 1:15

Because I was sure of this, I planned to visit you first so you could receive a double blessing. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 1:16

I wanted to visit you on my way to Macedonia, then return to you from Macedonia. 📖

After that, you could help me on my way to Judea.

## 2 Corinthians 1:17

Was I careless when I planned this? 📖

Do I plan by human standards, saying both “Yes, yes” and “No, no”?

## 2 Corinthians 1:18

As surely as God is faithful, our message to you is not both “Yes” and “No.” 📖

## 2 Corinthians 1:19

Silvanus, Timothy, and I preached God's Son, Jesus Christ, among you. 📖

He was not both “Yes” and “No”; in him it has always been “Yes.”

## 2 Corinthians 1:20

All God's promises are “Yes” in Christ. 📖

Through him, we say our “Amen” to give God glory.

**Help:** Amen expresses agreement.

Giving God glory means honouring him.

## 2 Corinthians 1:21

God makes both us and you stand firmly in Christ. 📖

He anointed us,

**Help:** Anointing marks someone as chosen and set apart for a task.

## 2 Corinthians 1:22

placed his seal on us, and put his Spirit in our hearts as a pledge of what will come. 📖

**Help:** A seal marks ownership or confirms something as genuine.

A pledge is a guarantee of what is promised.

## 2 Corinthians 1:23

I call God as my witness: I stayed away from Corinth to spare you. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 1:24

We do not rule over your faith. 📖

We work alongside you for your joy, because you stand firmly by faith.
