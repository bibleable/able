# 2 Corinthians 9 — Cheerful generosity

**About this chapter**

Paul asks the Corinthians to prepare their promised gift.

He explains willing generosity, God's provision, and the thanksgiving their service brings.

## 2 Corinthians 9:1

I do not need to write about this service to God's people. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 9:2

I know your eagerness to help. 📖

I boasted to the Macedonians that you in Achaia were ready to give since last year.

Your eagerness stirred most of them to do the same.

## 2 Corinthians 9:3

I am sending the brothers so our boasting about you in this matter will not prove empty. 📖

You will be ready, just as I said.

## 2 Corinthians 9:4

Otherwise, if Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we would be ashamed of our confidence. 📖

You would be ashamed too.

## 2 Corinthians 9:5

So I thought I must urge the brothers to visit beforehand and arrange the generous gift you promised. 📖

Then it will be ready as a willing gift, rather than a grudging one.

## 2 Corinthians 9:6

Remember: whoever plants little harvests little, and whoever plants generously harvests generously. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 9:7

Each should give what they decided in their heart, without regret or being forced. 📖

God loves a cheerful giver.

## 2 Corinthians 9:8

God can make every kindness overflow to you. 📖

Then, in everything at all times, you can have all you need and overflow in every good work.

## 2 Corinthians 9:9

The Scriptures say, “He has scattered his gifts to the poor. 📖

His righteousness lasts for ever.”

**Help:** Righteousness concerns being right before God and doing right.

## 2 Corinthians 9:10

God supplies seed to the planter and bread for food. 📖

He will supply and multiply your seed and increase the harvest of your righteousness.

**Help:** Paul uses seed and harvest as pictures in his teaching about giving.

## 2 Corinthians 9:11

You will be made rich in every way to be generous on every occasion. 📖

Through us, your generosity will bring thanksgiving to God.

## 2 Corinthians 9:12

This service both meets God's people's needs and overflows in many expressions of thanksgiving to God. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 9:13

This service provides proof that will lead God's people to glorify him. 📖

They will praise him for your obedience in confessing Christ's good news.

They will also praise him for your generous contribution to them and everyone else.

## 2 Corinthians 9:14

Their prayers will express their affection for you because of God's surpassing kindness given to you. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 9:15

Thanks to God for his gift beyond description! 📖
