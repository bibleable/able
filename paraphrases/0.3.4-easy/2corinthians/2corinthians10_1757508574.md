# 2 Corinthians 10 — Authority to build up

**About this chapter**

Paul answers criticism about his presence, letters, and authority.

He explains the limits of his work and the importance of the Lord's approval.

## 2 Corinthians 10:1

I, Paul, appeal to you through Christ's mildness and gentleness. 📖

I am humble when with you, but bold when away.

## 2 Corinthians 10:2

When I come, I beg not to need the boldness I expect towards some people. 📖

They suppose we live according to the flesh.

**Help:** According to the flesh here concerns relying on merely human ways.

## 2 Corinthians 10:3

Although we live in the flesh, we do not fight according to the flesh. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 10:4

Our weapons are not weapons of the flesh. 📖

They have God's power to destroy strongholds.

**Help:** A stronghold is a strongly defended place.

Paul explains his warfare picture through arguments and thoughts in the next verse.

## 2 Corinthians 10:5

We destroy arguments and every proud claim set against knowing God. 📖

We take every thought captive to make it obey Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 10:6

We will be ready to punish every act of disobedience once your obedience is complete. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 10:7

You look at outward appearances. 📖

Anyone sure they belong to Christ should remember that we belong to Christ just as much.

## 2 Corinthians 10:8

Even if I boast somewhat too much about our authority, I will not be ashamed. 📖

The Lord gave it to build you up rather than tear you down.

## 2 Corinthians 10:9

I do not want to seem to frighten you through my letters. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 10:10

Some say, “His letters are weighty and forceful, but his presence is unimpressive, and his speaking counts for nothing.” 📖

## 2 Corinthians 10:11

Such people should realise: what we are in letters while absent, we will be in actions when present. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 10:12

We dare not class or compare ourselves with some who recommend themselves. 📖

They measure themselves by themselves and compare themselves with themselves, showing their ignorance.

## 2 Corinthians 10:13

We will not boast beyond our limits. 📖

We stay within the field of influence God assigned us, which reaches even to you.

## 2 Corinthians 10:14

We are not going beyond our boundaries, as if we had never reached you. 📖

We were the first to reach you with Christ's good news.

## 2 Corinthians 10:15

We do not boast beyond our limits about other people's work. 📖

We hope your growing faith will greatly increase our field of influence among you,

## 2 Corinthians 10:16

so we can preach the good news in regions beyond you. 📖

Then we will not boast about work already done in someone else's territory.

## 2 Corinthians 10:17

Instead, “Anyone who boasts should boast in the Lord.” 📖

## 2 Corinthians 10:18

The person the Lord recommends is approved, rather than someone who recommends themselves. 📖
