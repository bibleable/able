# 2 Corinthians 12 — Power in weakness

**About this chapter**

Paul describes revelations and a painful thorn without identifying exactly what it was.

He explains Christ's power in weakness and his concerns about visiting the church.

## 2 Corinthians 12:1

I must keep boasting, although nothing is gained. 📖

I will turn to visions and revelations from the Lord.

**Help:** Visions and revelations are things shown or made known by God.

## 2 Corinthians 12:2

I know a man in Christ who was caught up to the third heaven fourteen years ago. 📖

Whether in the body or outside it, I do not know; God knows.

## 2 Corinthians 12:3

I know this about that man. 📖

Whether in the body or outside it, I do not know; God knows.

## 2 Corinthians 12:4

He was caught up to Paradise. 📖

He heard things beyond expression, things humans are not permitted to tell.

**Help:** Paradise here names a heavenly place with God.

## 2 Corinthians 12:5

I will boast about such a man, but not about myself, except my weaknesses. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 12:6

Even if I wanted to boast, I would not be foolish, because I would speak truth. 📖

But I hold back so no one credits me beyond what they see in me or hear from me,

## 2 Corinthians 12:7

or because of these extraordinarily great revelations. 📖

To keep me from pride, I was given a thorn in my flesh, a messenger of Satan to torment me.

**About this picture:** The thorn describes something painful that troubled Paul.

The text does not identify a specific illness, disability, or other cause.

## 2 Corinthians 12:8

Three times I begged the Lord to take it away. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 12:9

But he said, “My kindness is enough for you, because my power is made perfect in weakness.” 📖

So I will boast even more gladly in my weaknesses, so Christ's power can rest on me.

## 2 Corinthians 12:10

For Christ's sake, I delight in weaknesses, insults, hardships, persecutions, and difficulties. 📖

When I am weak, then I am strong.

**Help:** Persecutions are cruel treatment because of faith.

## 2 Corinthians 12:11

I have become a fool, but you drove me to it. 📖

You should have recommended me.

I am in no way below those “super-apostles,” even though I am nothing.

## 2 Corinthians 12:12

The marks of a true apostle were performed among you with great endurance: signs, wonders, and miracles. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 12:13

How were you below the other churches, except that I did not burden you? 📖

Forgive me this wrong!

## 2 Corinthians 12:14

I am ready to visit you a third time, and I will not burden you. 📖

I seek you, rather than your possessions.

Children should not have to save for parents; parents should save for children.

## 2 Corinthians 12:15

For your souls, I will gladly spend my money and myself. 📖

If I love you more, will you love me less?

## 2 Corinthians 12:16

Even so, I did not burden you; but crafty as I am, I caught you by trickery! 📖

**About these words:** Read this sharp statement with Paul's questions in verses 17–18 denying exploitation.

## 2 Corinthians 12:17

Did I take advantage of you through anyone I sent? 📖

## 2 Corinthians 12:18

I urged Titus to visit and sent our brother with him. 📖

Did Titus take advantage of you?

Did we not walk in the same Spirit and follow the same footsteps?

## 2 Corinthians 12:19

Have you thought all along that we are defending ourselves to you? 📖

We speak before God in Christ.

Dearly loved friends, everything is to build you up.

## 2 Corinthians 12:20

I fear I may not find you as I wish when I come. 📖

You may not find me as you wish.

I fear quarrels, jealousy, rage, rivalry, slander, gossip, pride, and disorder.

## 2 Corinthians 12:21

I fear that when I return, my God will humble me before you. 📖

I may grieve over many who sinned earlier without repenting of impurity, sexual wrongdoing, and shameless excess.

**Help:** Repenting means turning from sin towards God.
