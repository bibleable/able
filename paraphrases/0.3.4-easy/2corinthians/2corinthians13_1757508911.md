# 2 Corinthians 13 — Examine yourselves and live in peace

**About this chapter**

Paul warns the church before his third visit.

He calls for self-examination and ends with prayer, encouragement, and greetings.

## 2 Corinthians 13:1

This is my third visit to you. 📖

“Every matter must be established through the testimony of two or three witnesses.”

## 2 Corinthians 13:2

I warned you during my second visit. 📖

Now, while absent, I warn those who sinned earlier and everyone else: if I return, I will spare no one,

## 2 Corinthians 13:3

because you demand proof that Christ speaks through me. 📖

He is powerful among you, rather than weak in dealing with you.

## 2 Corinthians 13:4

Christ was crucified in weakness, yet lives by God's power. 📖

We too are weak in him, yet by God's power we will live with him in dealing with you.

**Help:** Crucified means killed on a cross.

## 2 Corinthians 13:5

Examine and test yourselves to see whether you are in the faith. 📖

Do you not recognise Jesus Christ in you, unless you fail the test?

## 2 Corinthians 13:6

I hope you will recognise that we have not failed the test. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 13:7

We pray to God that you will do nothing wrong. 📖

We want you to do right, even if we appear to have failed.

We are not praying merely to appear to have passed the test.

## 2 Corinthians 13:8

We can do nothing against the truth, but only for the truth. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 13:9

We rejoice when we are weak but you are strong. 📖

We pray for you to be made complete.

## 2 Corinthians 13:10

I write while away so I will not need to use my authority severely when present. 📖

The Lord gave me authority to build you up rather than tear you down.

## 2 Corinthians 13:11

Finally, brothers and sisters, rejoice! 📖

Aim for perfect harmony, encourage one another, agree in mind, and live in peace.

The God of love and peace will be with you.

## 2 Corinthians 13:12

Greet one another with a holy kiss. 📖

**Help:** A holy kiss was a greeting expressing believers' shared love and belonging to God.

## 2 Corinthians 13:13

All God's holy people send greetings. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 13:14

May the Lord Jesus Christ's kindness, God's love, and the Holy Spirit's shared fellowship be with you all. 📖

**Help:** Fellowship means shared life and participation together.
