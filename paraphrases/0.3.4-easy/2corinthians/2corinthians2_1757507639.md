# 2 Corinthians 2 — Forgiveness and the fragrance of Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul explains his painful letter and urges forgiveness for someone disciplined by the church.

He describes his travel and his work through the picture of a spreading fragrance.

## 2 Corinthians 2:1

I decided not to make another painful visit to you. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 2:2

If I grieve you, who can cheer me except the people I have grieved? 📖

## 2 Corinthians 2:3

I wrote as I did so those who should give me joy would not grieve me when I arrived. 📖

I trusted you all to share my joy.

## 2 Corinthians 2:4

I wrote with many tears, deep distress, and pain in my heart. 📖

I wanted to show my great love for you, rather than grieve you.

## 2 Corinthians 2:5

If anyone caused grief, he grieved all of you, rather than me. 📖

I mean to some degree, without overstating it.

## 2 Corinthians 2:6

The punishment given him by the majority is enough. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 2:7

Instead, forgive and comfort him, so excessive sorrow will not overwhelm him. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 2:8

So I urge you to show him again that you love him. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 2:9

I wrote to test whether you would obey in everything. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 2:10

If you forgive anyone, I forgive them too. 📖

If I have forgiven anything, I forgave it before Christ for your sake,

## 2 Corinthians 2:11

so Satan would not outwit us. 📖

We know his schemes.

## 2 Corinthians 2:12

When I went to Troas to preach Christ's good news, a door stood open for me in the Lord. 📖

**Help:** An open door pictures an opportunity to do this work.

## 2 Corinthians 2:13

But I had no peace in my spirit because I did not find my brother Titus there. 📖

So I said goodbye and went to Macedonia.

## 2 Corinthians 2:14

Thanks to God, who always leads us as captives in Christ's victory procession. 📖

Through us, he spreads everywhere the fragrance of knowing him.

**Help:** A victory procession displayed the winner and captives before watching crowds.

Fragrance is a smell spreading through the air.

Paul uses it as a picture of spreading the knowledge of Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 2:15

To God, we are Christ's sweet fragrance among those being saved and those perishing. 📖

## 2 Corinthians 2:16

To those perishing, we are a smell bringing death; to those being saved, a fragrance bringing life. 📖

Who is qualified for this task?

## 2 Corinthians 2:17

Unlike so many, we do not sell God's word for profit. 📖

In Christ, we speak sincerely before God as people sent by God.
