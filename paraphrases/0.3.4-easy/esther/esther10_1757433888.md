# Esther 10 — Mordecai’s position

**About this chapter**

The final record describes royal tribute and Mordecai’s rank and work for his people.

## Esther 10:1

Xerxes imposed tribute throughout the land to its farthest shores. 📖

**Help with tribute:** Payments demanded by a ruler.

## Esther 10:2

Mordecai’s powerful, magnificent deeds and the greatness the king granted him are recorded. 📖

They are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Media and Persia.

## Esther 10:3

Mordecai the Jew ranked second to Xerxes, foremost among Jews and favoured by many relatives. 📖

He sought his people’s good and spoke peace to all his countrymen.
