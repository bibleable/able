# Esther 2 — Esther becomes queen and Mordecai uncovers a plot

**About this chapter**

Young women are gathered for the king. | Esther becomes queen while keeping her Jewish identity private. | Mordecai reports a plot against Xerxes.

## Esther 2:1

Later, Xerxes’s anger eased, and he remembered Vashti, her actions, and the decree against her. 📖

## Esther 2:2

His attendants proposed searching for beautiful young virgins for the king. 📖

**Help with virgin:** Someone who has not had sex.

## Esther 2:3

“Appoint officials in every province to gather all beautiful young women to Susa’s harem under Hegai’s care. 📖

He is the king’s eunuch managing the women.

Give them beauty treatments.

**Help with harem and eunuch:** A harem was the royal women’s residence.

Eunuch is a term for a court official, often a man whose testicles had been removed.

## Esther 2:4

Let the woman pleasing the king replace Vashti as queen.” 📖

The king agreed and acted.

## Esther 2:5

Mordecai, a Jew of Benjamin, lived in Susa’s citadel. 📖

He was Jair’s son, Shimei’s grandson, and Kish’s great-grandson.

**Help with citadel:** A strongly defended part of a city.

## Esther 2:6

He had been exiled from Jerusalem with Jeconiah king of Judah by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. 📖

**Help with exile:** Being forced to leave one’s homeland.

## Esther 2:7

Mordecai raised his uncle’s daughter Hadassah, also called Esther, because both parents died. 📖

She was beautiful in form and appearance.

He took her as his own daughter.

## Esther 2:8

The royal decree gathered many young women to Susa under Hegai. 📖

Esther was taken to the palace into his care.

## Esther 2:9

She pleased Hegai, who quickly provided treatments and a special diet. 📖

He gave her 7 chosen palace maidservants and moved them to the harem’s best place.

## Esther 2:10

Esther concealed her people and family background as Mordecai instructed. 📖

## Esther 2:11

Mordecai walked before the harem court daily to learn her wellbeing and circumstances. 📖

## Esther 2:12

Before visiting Xerxes, each woman had 12 months of treatments. 📖

Regulations required 6 months with myrrh oil and 6 with perfumes and cosmetics.

**Help with myrrh:** A fragrant substance used in perfumes.

## Esther 2:13

Each received whatever she requested to take from the harem to the king’s palace. 📖

## Esther 2:14

She went in the evening and returned in the morning to a second harem. 📖

Shaashgaz, the king’s eunuch managing concubines, cared for them.

She could revisit only if the king delighted in her and summoned her by name.

**Help with concubines:** Women in recognised relationships with a man, with lower status than wives.

## Esther 2:15

Esther was Abihail’s daughter; Abihail was Mordecai’s uncle. 📖

Mordecai adopted her as his daughter.

At her turn, she requested only what Hegai, the trusted harem official, advised.

Everyone seeing her favoured her.

## Esther 2:16

Esther entered Xerxes’s royal palace in his year 7, month 10, Tebeth. 📖

## Esther 2:17

He loved and favoured Esther above all other women and virgins. 📖

He crowned her queen instead of Vashti.

## Esther 2:18

He held Esther’s great banquet for all officials and servants, declared a provincial tax holiday, and generously gave royal gifts. 📖

## Esther 2:19

When virgins gathered a second time, Mordecai sat at the king’s gate. 📖

## Esther 2:20

Esther still concealed her people and background, obeying Mordecai as when under his care. 📖

## Esther 2:21

While Mordecai sat at the gate, royal eunuch guards Bigthan and Teresh angrily plotted to assassinate Xerxes. 📖

## Esther 2:22

Mordecai discovered it and told Queen Esther, who informed the king in his name. 📖

## Esther 2:23

Investigation confirmed the report, and both officials were hanged on gallows. 📖

It was recorded in the Book of the Chronicles before the king.

**Help with gallows:** A structure used for execution.
