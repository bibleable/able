# Esther 1 — Queen Vashti refuses the king

**About this chapter**

This chapter describes King Xerxes’ court and the advice his officials give him.

Their claims are part of the story, rather than commands from God.

## Esther 1:1

King Xerxes ruled 127 provinces, from India to Cush. 📖

**Help with a word:** Provinces are regions within an empire.

## Esther 1:2

He ruled from the fortress of Susa. 📖

## Esther 1:3

In year 3 of his rule, he held a feast for officials and servants. 📖

Military leaders from Persia and Media attended.

So did nobles and provincial leaders.

## Esther 1:4

For 180 days, he showed his kingdom's riches and his magnificent royal greatness. 📖

## Esther 1:5

Then the king gave everyone in Susa's fortress a feast lasting 7 days. 📖

People from the least to the greatest attended.

It was in the palace garden court.

## Esther 1:6

White and blue cloths hung from marble pillars. 📖

Fine white and purple cords held them to silver rings.

Gold and silver couches stood on patterned flooring.

The floor used purple stone, marble, mother-of-pearl, and other costly stones.

## Esther 1:7

Drinks came in gold cups, each with a different design. 📖

The king provided plenty of royal wine.

## Esther 1:8

The king ordered no limit on drinking. 📖

His officials were to give each man whatever he wanted.

## Esther 1:9

Queen Vashti gave a feast for the women in Xerxes' palace. 📖

## Esther 1:10

On day 7, wine had made the king cheerful. 📖

He gave an order to his 7 eunuchs.

They were Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carkas.

**Help with a word:** Eunuchs were male court servants whose testicles had been removed.

## Esther 1:11

They were to bring Queen Vashti wearing her crown. 📖

The king wanted the people and officials to see her beauty.

She was very beautiful.

## Esther 1:12

Vashti refused the order delivered by the eunuchs. 📖

The king became very angry.

## Esther 1:13

The king consulted wise men who understood the times. 📖

He usually asked experts in law and justice for advice.

## Esther 1:14

His closest advisers were Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan. 📖

These 7 princes of Persia and Media could meet the king personally.

They held the highest positions.

## Esther 1:15

The king asked, “What does the law say should happen to Vashti? 📖

She refused King Xerxes' command delivered by the eunuchs.”

## Esther 1:16

Memucan said before the king and princes, “Vashti has wronged more than the king. 📖

She has wronged every leader and people in the kingdom.”

## Esther 1:17

“All women will hear what she did. 📖

They will despise their husbands.

They will say Vashti refused when Xerxes called her.”

## Esther 1:18

“Even today, noble women in Persia and Media will speak this way to the officials. 📖

There will be much disrespect and anger.”

## Esther 1:19

“Let the king issue a law that cannot be cancelled under Persian and Median law. 📖

Vashti must never come before Xerxes again.

Give her place as queen to a better woman.”

## Esther 1:20

“Everyone in the vast kingdom will hear the order. 📖

Then all wives, rich or poor, will honour their husbands.”

## Esther 1:21

The king and princes liked this advice. 📖

The king followed Memucan's plan.

## Esther 1:22

He sent letters to every province in its own writing and every people in its own language. 📖

The letters said every man should rule his own household.
