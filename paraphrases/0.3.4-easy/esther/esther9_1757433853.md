# Esther 9 — The fighting and the days of Purim

**About this chapter**

The planned day brings a reversal, and the Jews defeat their enemies. | The account names those killed and repeatedly says no plunder was taken. | Letters establish annual remembrance and celebration.

## Esther 9:1

On month 12, Adar 13, the royal decree took effect. 📖

Enemies expected to overpower Jews, but the opposite happened: Jews overpowered those hating them.

## Esther 9:2

Jews gathered in their cities throughout Xerxes’s provinces to attack those seeking harm. 📖

Nobody could withstand them because all peoples feared them.

## Esther 9:3

Provincial officials, satraps, governors, and royal administrators helped Jews because they feared Mordecai. 📖

## Esther 9:4

Mordecai grew powerful in the palace, his fame spreading through the provinces. 📖

## Esther 9:5

Jews killed and destroyed all their enemies with swords, doing as they pleased to those hating them. 📖

## Esther 9:6

In Susa’s citadel they killed 500 men, 📖

## Esther 9:7

including Parshandatha, Dalphon, Aspatha, 📖

## Esther 9:8

Poratha, Adalia, Aridatha, 📖

## Esther 9:9

Parmashta, Arisai, Aridai, and Vaizatha. 📖

## Esther 9:10

These were 10 sons of Haman son of Hammedatha, the Jews’ enemy. 📖

They killed them but took no plunder.

## Esther 9:11

The king heard Susa’s death count that day. 📖

## Esther 9:12

He told Esther, “Jews killed 500 in Susa’s citadel, including Haman’s 10 sons. 📖

What have they done in other provinces?

What more do you request?

It will be granted.”

## Esther 9:13

Esther asked, “If you please, let Susa’s Jews carry out today’s decree tomorrow too. 📖

Hang Haman’s 10 sons’ bodies on gallows.”

## Esther 9:14

He issued the decree in Susa, and Haman’s 10 sons were hanged. 📖

## Esther 9:15

On Adar 14, Susa’s Jews killed 300 more men but took no plunder. 📖

## Esther 9:16

Other provincial Jews gathered to defend themselves and rid themselves of enemies. 📖

They killed 75,000 who hated them but took no plunder.

## Esther 9:17

This happened on Adar 13. 📖

On day 14 they rested, feasted, and rejoiced.

## Esther 9:18

Susa’s Jews fought on days 13 and 14 and rested with feasting and joy on day 15. 📖

## Esther 9:19

Village Jews therefore celebrate Adar 14 with joy, feasting, and gifts to each other. 📖

## Esther 9:20

Mordecai recorded events and wrote to all Jews in Xerxes’s provinces, near and far, 📖

## Esther 9:21

establishing annual celebration on Adar 14 and 15. 📖

## Esther 9:22

These marked rest from enemies and the month’s change from sorrow and mourning to joy and holiday. 📖

They must feast, rejoice, exchange gifts, and give to the poor.

## Esther 9:23

The Jews agreed to keep the custom they had started, as Mordecai wrote. 📖

## Esther 9:24

Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, enemy of all Jews, had cast Pur to crush and destroy them. 📖

**Help with Pur:** A lot used to choose by drawing or casting an object.

## Esther 9:25

The king heard and wrote that Haman’s evil plan should fall on his own head. 📖

He and his sons must hang on gallows.

## Esther 9:26

They called these days Purim from Pur. 📖

Because of the letter’s instructions and everything they saw and experienced,

## Esther 9:27

Jews bound themselves, descendants, and all joining them to keep both days every year at the appointed time. 📖

## Esther 9:28

Every generation, family, province, and city must remember and celebrate. 📖

Purim must never cease among Jews or its memory fade from descendants.

## Esther 9:29

Esther daughter of Abihail and Mordecai the Jew wrote with full authority confirming this second Purim letter. 📖

## Esther 9:30

Mordecai sent letters with words of peace and truth to all Jews in Xerxes’s 127 provinces, 📖

## Esther 9:31

confirming Purim’s appointed times, as he and Esther established. 📖

They bound themselves and descendants to fasting and lamentation times.

## Esther 9:32

Esther’s decree confirmed Purim’s rules, which were written in the record. 📖
