# Esther 3 — Haman plots against the Jews

**About this chapter**

Xerxes promotes Haman, but Mordecai refuses to bow. | Haman seeks the destruction of all Jews in the kingdom. | His accusation and the royal order retain their full scope.

## Esther 3:1

Xerxes promoted Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, above all his princes. 📖

## Esther 3:2

All royal servants at the king’s gate bowed and honoured Haman as ordered. 📖

Mordecai refused.

## Esther 3:3

They asked Mordecai why he disobeyed the king. 📖

## Esther 3:4

Daily warnings did not change him. 📖

They told Haman to see whether his behaviour would be tolerated.

Mordecai had told them he was a Jew.

## Esther 3:5

Haman saw Mordecai refused to bow or honour him and grew furious. 📖

## Esther 3:6

Knowing Mordecai’s people, Haman scorned attacking only him. 📖

He sought to destroy all Jews throughout Xerxes’s kingdom.

## Esther 3:7

In Xerxes’s year 12, month 1, Nisan, they cast Pur before Haman to choose a day and month. 📖

It fell on month 12, Adar.

**Help with Pur:** A lot used to choose by drawing or casting an object.

## Esther 3:8

Haman told Xerxes, “A people is scattered throughout every province. 📖

Their laws differ from others’, and they disobey royal laws.

It is not in your interest to tolerate them.

**About this accusation:** These are Haman’s claims about the Jews.

## Esther 3:9

If you please, decree their destruction. 📖

I will pay 10,000 silver talents into the treasury for those carrying it out.”

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.

## Esther 3:10

The king gave his signet ring to Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, the Jews’ enemy. 📖

**Help with signet ring:** A ring bearing the royal seal used to authorise documents.

## Esther 3:11

He told Haman to keep his money and do as he pleased with the people. 📖

## Esther 3:12

On month 1, day 13, scribes wrote Haman’s exact orders to satraps, provincial governors, and all peoples’ officials. 📖

Every province’s script and people’s language was used.

It bore Xerxes’s name and royal seal.

**Help with satraps:** Regional rulers in the empire.

## Esther 3:13

Couriers took letters ordering every province to destroy, kill, and annihilate all Jews, young and old, women and children. 📖

The order included plundering their possessions on one day, month 12, Adar 13.

**Help with plunder:** Take possessions by force.

## Esther 3:14

Copies were published in every province for all peoples to prepare for that day. 📖

## Esther 3:15

Royal commands hurried the couriers, and the decree was issued in Susa’s citadel. 📖

The king and Haman drank while Susa’s city was confused.
