# Esther 5 — Esther’s banquet and Haman’s plan

**About this chapter**

Esther approaches Xerxes and invites him and Haman to banquets. | Haman boasts at home but resents Mordecai. | His family and friends propose an execution.

## Esther 5:1

On day 3, Esther wore royal robes in the inner palace court opposite the king’s quarters. 📖

He sat enthroned facing the courtroom entrance.

## Esther 5:2

He saw her and favoured her, extending his gold sceptre. 📖

She approached and touched its tip.

## Esther 5:3

The king asked, “What is it, Queen Esther? 📖

What do you request?

I will grant even half the kingdom.”

## Esther 5:4

She asked, “If it pleases the king, come with Haman today to the banquet I prepared for you.” 📖

## Esther 5:5

He ordered Haman brought quickly to fulfil her request. 📖

Both went to her banquet.

## Esther 5:6

As they drank wine, the king asked Esther, “What is your petition? 📖

I will grant it.

What is your request?

I will fulfil even half the kingdom.”

## Esther 5:7

She answered, “My petition and request is this: 📖

## Esther 5:8

If you favour me and are pleased to grant my request, come with Haman tomorrow to a banquet I prepare. 📖

Then I will answer the king’s question.”

## Esther 5:9

Haman left joyful, but Mordecai at the king’s gate neither rose nor trembled before him. 📖

Haman grew furious.

## Esther 5:10

He restrained himself and went home, calling friends and his wife Zeresh. 📖

## Esther 5:11

He boasted of wealth, many sons, and royal honours placing him above officials and servants. 📖

## Esther 5:12

He said, “Only I joined the king at Esther’s banquet, and she invited me with him tomorrow. 📖

## Esther 5:13

None of this satisfies me while Mordecai the Jew sits at the king’s gate.” 📖

## Esther 5:14

Zeresh and friends advised a gallows 50 cubits high. 📖

“Ask the king tomorrow morning to hang Mordecai.

Then enjoy the royal banquet.”

Pleased, Haman had it built.

**Help with these words:** Gallows were used for execution.

A cubit was roughly a forearm’s length.
