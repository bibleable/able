# Esther 6 — Mordecai is honoured

**About this chapter**

Xerxes cannot sleep and hears the court records. | Haman describes a public honour, expecting it for himself. | The king orders him to honour Mordecai.

## Esther 6:1

The sleepless king ordered the Book of Records, the Chronicles, read to him. 📖

## Esther 6:2

It recorded Mordecai exposing Bigthana and Teresh, eunuch entrance guards plotting Xerxes’s assassination. 📖

## Esther 6:3

He asked what honour Mordecai received. 📖

Attendants answered, “Nothing.”

## Esther 6:4

He asked who was in the court. 📖

Haman had entered the outer palace court to request Mordecai’s hanging on his prepared gallows.

## Esther 6:5

Attendants said Haman was there. 📖

The king called him in.

## Esther 6:6

Haman entered, and the king asked how to honour someone he delighted in. 📖

Haman thought, “Whom would the king delight to honour more than me?”

## Esther 6:7

Haman said, “For this man the king delights to honour, 📖

## Esther 6:8

bring a robe the king wore and a horse he rode, with a royal crest on its head. 📖

**Help with crest:** A symbol of royal authority.

## Esther 6:9

Entrust them to a most noble prince to dress the man and parade him through the city square. 📖

Announce, ‘This is how the king honours a man he delights in!’”

## Esther 6:10

The king told Haman, “Hurry and do all you proposed for Mordecai the Jew at the king’s gate. 📖

Leave nothing out.”

## Esther 6:11

Haman dressed Mordecai and paraded him on the horse through the square. 📖

He proclaimed, “This is how the king honours a man he delights in!”

## Esther 6:12

Mordecai returned to the gate. 📖

Haman hurried home grieving with covered head.

## Esther 6:13

Haman told Zeresh and friends everything. 📖

His advisers and Zeresh said, “Your downfall before Mordecai has begun.

Since he is Jewish, you cannot prevail but will certainly fall before him.”

## Esther 6:14

Royal eunuchs arrived while they spoke and hurried Haman to Esther’s banquet. 📖
