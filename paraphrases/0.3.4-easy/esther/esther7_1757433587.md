# Esther 7 — Esther identifies Haman

**About this chapter**

At the second banquet, Esther asks for her life and her people. | She names Haman as the enemy. | Xerxes orders Haman’s death.

## Esther 7:1

The king and Haman dined with Queen Esther. 📖

## Esther 7:2

As they drank wine on day 2, the king asked again, “Queen Esther, what is your petition? 📖

I will grant it.

What is your request?

I will fulfil even half the kingdom.”

## Esther 7:3

She answered, “If you favour me and it pleases you, grant my life and my people’s lives. 📖

## Esther 7:4

We have been sold for destruction, death, and annihilation. 📖

If sold merely as male and female servants, I would stay silent.

That distress would not justify burdening you.”

## Esther 7:5

Xerxes asked Esther who and where the plotter was. 📖

## Esther 7:6

She identified the wicked adversary as Haman. 📖

He stood terrified before king and queen.

## Esther 7:7

The furious king left his wine for the palace garden. 📖

Haman begged Esther for life, knowing the king planned disaster for him.

## Esther 7:8

The king returned as Haman fell on Esther’s couch. 📖

“Will he assault the queen while I am in the palace?”

At his words, they covered Haman’s face.

**About this accusation:** This is the king’s interpretation of what he sees.

## Esther 7:9

Royal eunuch Harbonah said, “Haman’s house has a 50-cubit gallows he built for Mordecai. 📖

Mordecai’s report saved the king.”

The king ordered Haman hanged on it.

**Help with cubit:** An ancient unit roughly a forearm’s length.

## Esther 7:10

They hanged Haman on the gallows he built for Mordecai. 📖

The king’s fury eased.
