# Esther 8 — A decree permits the Jews to defend themselves

**About this chapter**

Esther asks for Haman’s letters to be revoked. | Xerxes explains the royal seal’s force and permits another decree. | The new order reaches every province.

## Esther 8:1

That day Xerxes gave Esther the estate of Haman, the Jews’ enemy. 📖

Mordecai entered his presence after Esther revealed their relationship.

## Esther 8:2

Xerxes gave Mordecai the signet ring recovered from Haman. 📖

Esther put him over Haman’s estate.

## Esther 8:3

Esther wept at the king’s feet and begged him to revoke Haman the Agagite’s evil plan against the Jews. 📖

## Esther 8:4

He extended the gold sceptre, and she rose before him. 📖

## Esther 8:5

She said, “If it pleases you and I have your favour, I ask for this. 📖

If this seems proper and I please you, write to revoke Haman’s letters.

Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, wrote them to destroy Jews in all your provinces.

## Esther 8:6

How can I bear my people’s disaster or my relatives’ destruction?” 📖

## Esther 8:7

Xerxes told Esther and Mordecai the Jew, “I gave Esther Haman’s estate. 📖

He was hanged for attacking Jews.

## Esther 8:8

Write as you please about the Jews in my name and seal it with my ring. 📖

A decree in my name bearing my seal cannot be revoked.”

## Esther 8:9

On month 3, Sivan 23, scribes wrote all Mordecai’s orders to Jews, satraps, governors, and princes of 127 provinces. 📖

These stretched from India to Cush.

Each province’s script and each people’s language were used, including the Jews’ own script and language.

## Esther 8:10

Mordecai wrote in Xerxes’s name and used the royal seal. 📖

Mounted couriers carried letters on swift horses bred from royal mares.

## Esther 8:11

Every city’s Jews could assemble and defend themselves. 📖

They could destroy, kill, and annihilate all hostile forces of any people or province, including women and children.

They could plunder their possessions.

**Help with plunder:** Take possessions by force.

## Esther 8:12

This was appointed for one day, month 12, Adar 13, throughout Xerxes’s provinces. 📖

## Esther 8:13

Copies were published to everyone in every province so Jews would prepare to avenge themselves on enemies that day. 📖

**Help with avenge:** Bring punishment for wrongs done.

## Esther 8:14

Royal orders hurried the couriers on royal horses, and the decree was issued in Susa’s citadel. 📖

## Esther 8:15

Mordecai left wearing royal blue and white, a large gold crown, and a purple robe of fine linen. 📖

Susa shouted and rejoiced.

## Esther 8:16

Jews had light, gladness, joy, and honour. 📖

**Help with light here:** A picture of relief and hope.

## Esther 8:17

Every city and province receiving the decree saw Jewish feasting, joy, and celebration. 📖

Many of the land’s people became Jews because they feared the Jews.
