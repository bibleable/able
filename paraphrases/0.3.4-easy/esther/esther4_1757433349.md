# Esther 4 — Mordecai appeals to Esther

**About this chapter**

Mordecai mourns and sends Esther the destruction decree. | She explains the danger of approaching Xerxes uninvited. | She asks Susa’s Jews to fast and decides to go.

## Esther 4:1

Mordecai learned everything, tore his clothes, wore sackcloth and ashes, and cried loudly and bitterly in the city. 📖

**Help with sackcloth and ashes:** Signs of grief and humility.

## Esther 4:2

He stopped at the king’s gate because wearing sackcloth inside was forbidden. 📖

## Esther 4:3

Every province receiving the decree saw great Jewish mourning, fasting, weeping, and lament. 📖

Many lay in sackcloth and ashes.

## Esther 4:4

Esther’s maids and eunuchs reported Mordecai’s distress, deeply upsetting her. 📖

She sent clothes to replace his sackcloth, but he refused.

## Esther 4:5

She sent her appointed royal eunuch Hathach to learn what troubled Mordecai and why. 📖

## Esther 4:6

Hathach found him in the city square before the king’s gate. 📖

## Esther 4:7

Mordecai explained everything, including the exact sum Haman promised the treasury for destroying Jews. 📖

## Esther 4:8

He gave Hathach Susa’s written destruction decree to show and explain to Esther. 📖

He urged her to ask the king’s favour and plead for her people.

## Esther 4:9

Hathach returned and reported Mordecai’s response to Esther. 📖

## Esther 4:10

Esther instructed Hathach to tell Mordecai, 📖

## Esther 4:11

“Every royal official and person in the provinces knows this law. 📖

Any man or woman entering the king’s inner court uninvited must die.

Only his extended gold sceptre spares them.

He has not called me for 30 days.”

**Help with sceptre:** A staff showing royal authority.

## Esther 4:12

Mordecai received her words 📖

## Esther 4:13

and replied, “Do not think the palace will spare you alone among Jews. 📖

## Esther 4:14

If you stay silent, Jewish relief and rescue will come elsewhere, but you and your father’s family will perish. 📖

Who knows whether you became queen for this time?”

## Esther 4:15

Esther replied to Mordecai, 📖

## Esther 4:16

“Gather all Susa’s Jews to fast for me. 📖

Do not eat or drink for 3 days, night or day.

My maids and I will fast too.

Then I will go to the king against the law.

If I perish, I perish!”

## Esther 4:17

Mordecai did everything she instructed. 📖
