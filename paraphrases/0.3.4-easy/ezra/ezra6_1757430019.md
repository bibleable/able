# Ezra 6 — Darius confirms the work and the temple is finished

**About this chapter**

A record of Cyrus’s decree is found. | Darius orders support and severe penalties for interference. | The temple is completed and dedicated, and the people keep Passover.

## Ezra 6:1

Darius ordered Babylon’s treasury archives searched. 📖

## Ezra 6:2

A scroll found in Ecbatana’s fortress in Media said, “Memorandum: 📖

**Help with memorandum:** A written record.

## Ezra 6:3

In Cyrus’s 1st year, he ordered God’s house in Jerusalem rebuilt for sacrifice on firm foundations. 📖

It must be 60 cubits high and 60 wide,

**Help with cubit:** An ancient unit roughly a forearm’s length.

## Ezra 6:4

with 3 cut-stone layers and 1 timber layer. 📖

The royal treasury must pay.

## Ezra 6:5

The gold and silver objects Nebuchadnezzar took from Jerusalem’s temple to Babylon must return to God’s house in Jerusalem.” 📖

## Ezra 6:6

Darius ordered Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and fellow regional officials to stay away. 📖

## Ezra 6:7

“Leave this work alone. 📖

Let the Jewish governor and elders rebuild on its original site.

## Ezra 6:8

Pay the elders’ full costs from royal taxes collected west of the Euphrates, so work is not hindered. 📖

## Ezra 6:9

Give daily, without fail, whatever Jerusalem’s priests request. 📖

Supply young bulls, rams, and lambs for burnt offerings to heaven’s God.

Also supply wheat, salt, wine, and oil.

## Ezra 6:10

They can offer sweet-smelling sacrifices to heaven’s God and pray for the king’s and sons’ lives. 📖

## Ezra 6:11

Anyone interfering must have a beam torn from his house and be impaled on it. 📖

His house must become rubble for this offence.

**Help with impaled:** Killed by being pierced on a stake.

This is Darius’s punishment decree.

## Ezra 6:12

May God whose Name dwells there overthrow any king or people altering this decree or destroying his Jerusalem house. 📖

I, Darius, issued it.

Carry it out diligently.”

## Ezra 6:13

Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and associates diligently obeyed Darius. 📖

## Ezra 6:14

Jewish elders built successfully through Haggai’s and Zechariah son of Iddo’s prophecy. 📖

They finished under Israel’s God’s command and decrees of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes, Persia’s kings.

## Ezra 6:15

The temple was completed in Darius’s 6th year, month Adar, day 3. 📖

## Ezra 6:16

Israel’s priests, Levites, and other returned exiles joyfully dedicated God’s house. 📖

## Ezra 6:17

They offered 100 bulls, 200 rams, and 400 lambs. 📖

They offered 12 male goats for all Israel’s sin, one per tribe.

## Ezra 6:18

Priests and Levites were assigned by divisions and groups for God’s Jerusalem service as Moses’s Book required. 📖

## Ezra 6:19

The exiles kept Passover in month 1, day 14. 📖

## Ezra 6:20

All priests and Levites purified themselves and were ceremonially clean. 📖

Levites killed the Passover lamb for all exiles, fellow priests, and themselves.

**Help with ceremonially clean:** Prepared according to religious rules for sacred service.

## Ezra 6:21

Returned Israelites ate with everyone separating from the land’s peoples’ uncleanness to seek the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

**Help with uncleanness here:** Religious practices incompatible with the LORD’s worship, not a statement that ancestry determines human worth.

## Ezra 6:22

They joyfully kept Unleavened Bread for 7 days. 📖

The LORD gave joy and turned Assyria’s king’s heart to support work on Israel’s God’s house.

**Help with Unleavened Bread:** The feast with bread made without yeast.
