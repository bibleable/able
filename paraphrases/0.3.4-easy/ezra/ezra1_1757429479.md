# Ezra 1 — Cyrus permits the return

**About this chapter**

The LORD stirs Cyrus to announce the rebuilding of the temple. | People prepare to return, and neighbours support them. | Cyrus returns objects taken from the LORD’s house.

## Ezra 1:1

In Cyrus king of Persia’s 1st year, the LORD stirred him to fulfil the LORD’s word through Jeremiah. 📖

Cyrus announced this throughout his kingdom and put it in writing:

## Ezra 1:2

“Cyrus king of Persia says, ‘The LORD, heaven’s God, gave me all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

He appointed me to build him a house in Jerusalem, Judah.

## Ezra 1:3

Let anyone among his people go to Jerusalem in Judah to build the LORD, Israel’s God’s house. 📖

May their God be with them.

He is the God in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 1:4

Wherever survivors live, their neighbours must help with silver, gold, goods, and livestock. 📖

They should also give a voluntary offering for God’s house in Jerusalem.’”

## Ezra 1:5

Judah’s and Benjamin’s family heads, priests, and Levites prepared to rebuild the LORD’s house in Jerusalem. 📖

God stirred the spirit of everyone going.

## Ezra 1:6

All their neighbours supported them with silver and gold objects, goods, livestock, and valuables, besides voluntary offerings. 📖

## Ezra 1:7

Cyrus brought out the LORD’s house’s objects that Nebuchadnezzar took from Jerusalem to his gods’ temple. 📖

## Ezra 1:8

Cyrus king of Persia had treasurer Mithredath count them out to Sheshbazzar, Judah’s prince. 📖

## Ezra 1:9

The inventory listed 30 gold dishes, 1,000 silver dishes, and 29 silver utensils, 📖

## Ezra 1:10

30 gold bowls, 410 matching silver bowls, and 1,000 other objects. 📖

## Ezra 1:11

There were 5,400 gold and silver objects altogether. 📖

Sheshbazzar brought them when the exiles returned from Babylon to Jerusalem.

**Help with exiles:** People forced to leave their homeland.
