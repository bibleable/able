# Ezra 4 — Opposition and letters to the kings

**About this chapter**

Opponents hinder rebuilding. | The chapter includes accusations and royal replies from several kings’ reigns. | Claims inside letters remain the words of their writers.

## Ezra 4:1

Judah’s and Benjamin’s enemies heard the returned exiles were building a temple for the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## Ezra 4:2

They told Zerubbabel and family heads, “Let us build with you. 📖

We seek your God and have sacrificed since Esar-haddon king of Assyria brought us here.”

## Ezra 4:3

Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and Israel’s other family heads refused. 📖

“We alone must build our God’s house for the LORD, Israel’s God, as Cyrus king of Persia commanded.

You have no part with us.”

## Ezra 4:4

The land’s people discouraged Judah and frightened them from building. 📖

## Ezra 4:5

They hired advisers to frustrate Judah’s plans throughout Cyrus king of Persia’s reign until Darius king of Persia’s reign. 📖

## Ezra 4:6

An accusation against Judah and Jerusalem was lodged at the start of Xerxes’s reign. 📖

## Ezra 4:7

During Artaxerxes king of Persia’s reign, Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and their associates wrote him a letter. 📖

It was written in Aramaic and translated.

## Ezra 4:8

Commander Rehum and scribe Shimshai wrote against Jerusalem to Artaxerxes: 📖

## Ezra 4:9

“From commander Rehum, scribe Shimshai, and associates, judges and officials over Tripolis, Persia, Erech, Babylon, and Susa’s Elamites, 📖

## Ezra 4:10

and other peoples the great, honourable Ashurbanipal deported to Samaria’s cities and other places west of the Euphrates.” 📖

## Ezra 4:11

Their letter read, “To King Artaxerxes, from your servants west of the Euphrates: 📖

## Ezra 4:12

Jews from you returned here to Jerusalem and rebuild that rebellious, wicked city. 📖

They restore its walls and foundations.

## Ezra 4:13

If the city and walls are rebuilt, they will stop paying tribute, duty, and toll. 📖

The royal treasury will lose money.

**Help with tribute, duty, and toll:** Types of payments to rulers.

## Ezra 4:14

We serve the palace and must not let the king be dishonoured. 📖

We therefore inform you

## Ezra 4:15

you should search your ancestors’ record books. 📖

They will show this city rebels, harms kings and provinces, and has stirred rebellion from ancient times.

That is why it was destroyed.

## Ezra 4:16

If it and its walls are rebuilt, you will lose rule west of the Euphrates.” 📖

## Ezra 4:17

The king replied, “To Rehum, Shimshai, and associates in Samaria and elsewhere west of the Euphrates: greetings. 📖

## Ezra 4:18

Your letter was translated and read before me. 📖

## Ezra 4:19

I ordered a search. 📖

It showed this city revolted against kings and stirred rebellion since ancient times.

## Ezra 4:20

Powerful kings ruled Jerusalem and all territory west of the Euphrates, receiving tribute, duty, and toll. 📖

## Ezra 4:21

Order these men to stop. 📖

The city must not be rebuilt until I order it.

## Ezra 4:22

Do not neglect this. 📖

Why let the threat grow and harm royal interests?”

## Ezra 4:23

Artaxerxes’s letter was read to Rehum, Shimshai the scribe, and associates. 📖

They immediately went to Jerusalem’s Jews and forcibly stopped them.

## Ezra 4:24

Work on God’s house in Jerusalem stopped until Darius king of Persia’s 2nd year. 📖
