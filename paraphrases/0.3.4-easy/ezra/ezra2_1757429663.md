# Ezra 2 — The people who returned

**About this chapter**

This record names returnees and gives their numbers. | Some lists continue across verse headings. | All names, individual counts, and overall totals are kept as this source gives them.

## Ezra 2:1

These provincial people returned from captivity in Babylon, where King Nebuchadnezzar took them. 📖

They returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to their own town.

## Ezra 2:62

These men could not find their family records. 📖

They were excluded from priesthood as unclean.

**Help with unclean here:** They lacked the required proof for priestly status.

This does not mean they had less worth as people.

## Ezra 2:63

The governor forbade them to eat the most holy things until a priest could consult Urim and Thummim. 📖

**Help with Urim and Thummim:** Terms for a priestly way of seeking God’s decision.

## Ezra 2:68

At the LORD’s house in Jerusalem, some family heads gave voluntary offerings to rebuild it on its original site. 📖

## Ezra 2:69

They gave according to their ability: 61,000 gold darics, 5,000 silver minas, and 100 priestly garments. 📖

**Help with these units:** A daric was a gold coin.

A mina measured weight.

## Ezra 2:70

The rest of Israel settled in their towns too.
