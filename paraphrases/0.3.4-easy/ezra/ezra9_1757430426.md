# Ezra 9 — Ezra confesses Israel’s unfaithfulness

**About this chapter**

Leaders report marriages with surrounding peoples and participation in detestable practices. | Ezra mourns and prays. | His prayer describes guilt, grace, and the danger of renewed disobedience.

## Ezra 9:1

After this, leaders told me Israel, priests, and Levites had not kept separate from surrounding peoples. 📖

Their detestable acts were like those of Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians, and Amorites.

## Ezra 9:2

“They married these peoples’ daughters for themselves and their sons. 📖

The holy seed has mixed with the land’s peoples.

Leaders and officials led this unfaithfulness!”

**Help with holy seed:** This refers to the people set apart to belong to God.

It is the report’s language, not a claim that other peoples have less human worth.

## Ezra 9:3

I tore my tunic and cloak, pulled hair from my head and beard, and sat horrified. 📖

**Help with tunic and cloak:** Inner and outer clothing.

These actions express Ezra’s distress.

## Ezra 9:4

Everyone trembling at Israel’s God’s words gathered because of the exiles’ unfaithfulness. 📖

I sat horrified until the evening offering.

## Ezra 9:5

At the evening offering, I rose from humiliation in torn clothes. 📖

I knelt and spread my hands to the LORD my God.

## Ezra 9:6

“My God, I am too ashamed and embarrassed to lift my face to you. 📖

Our sins rise above our heads, and our guilt reaches heaven.

**Help with these images:** Ezra describes overwhelming guilt.

## Ezra 9:7

Our guilt has been great from our ancestors’ time until now. 📖

Our sins brought us, kings, and priests under earthly kings’ power.

We faced swords, captivity, plunder, and humiliation, as now.

## Ezra 9:8

For a brief moment the LORD our God gave grace, preserving survivors and a stake in his holy place. 📖

In slavery he gave new life and light to our eyes.

**Help with these images:** A stake pictures a secure place.

Light to eyes pictures renewed life and hope.

## Ezra 9:9

We are slaves, but God has not abandoned us. 📖

He favoured us before Persia’s kings, giving new life to rebuild his ruined house.

He gave a protective wall in Judah and Jerusalem.

**Help with wall here:** Ezra uses this image for protection and security.

## Ezra 9:10

Our God, what can we say? 📖

We abandoned your commands

## Ezra 9:11

given through your servants the prophets. 📖

‘The land you enter is polluted by its peoples’ impurities and detestable acts, filling it end to end.

## Ezra 9:12

Do not marry your daughters to their sons or their daughters to your sons. 📖

Never seek their peace or prosperity.

Then you may be strong, enjoy the land’s good things, and leave it to your sons forever.’

## Ezra 9:13

All this came on us because of evil deeds and great guilt. 📖

Yet you punished us less than our sins deserved and preserved these survivors.

## Ezra 9:14

Should we again break your commands and marry peoples committing these detestable acts? 📖

Would your anger not destroy us without survivor or remnant?

## Ezra 9:15

LORD, Israel’s God, you are righteous! 📖

We remain as survivors today.

We stand guilty before you, although our guilt means nobody can stand before you.”
