# Ezra 10 — The marriage inquiry

**About this chapter**

Ezra confesses while the people gather and weep. | A proposal calls for foreign wives and children to be sent away. | The account names objections, describes the inquiry, and lists the men involved.

## Ezra 10:1

Ezra prayed, confessed, wept, and fell facedown before God’s house. 📖

A vast gathering of Israelite men, women, and children joined him, weeping bitterly.

## Ezra 10:2

Shecaniah son of Jehiel, an Elamite, told Ezra, “We betrayed God by marrying foreign women from the land’s peoples. 📖

Yet Israel still has hope.

## Ezra 10:3

Let us covenant before God to send away all foreign wives and their children. 📖

Follow my lord’s counsel and those trembling at God’s command.

Let it accord with the Law.

**Help with covenant:** A binding agreement.

The proposal explicitly includes children as well as wives.

## Ezra 10:4

Rise; this is your responsibility. 📖

We support you.

Be strong and act!”

## Ezra 10:5

Ezra rose and made leading priests, Levites, and all Israel swear to do this. 📖

They swore.

## Ezra 10:6

Ezra left God’s house for Jehohanan son of Eliashib’s room. 📖

He neither ate food nor drank water while mourning the exiles’ unfaithfulness.

## Ezra 10:7

An announcement throughout Judah and Jerusalem ordered all exiles to Jerusalem. 📖

## Ezra 10:8

Anyone absent after 3 days would lose all property and be expelled from the exiles’ assembly. 📖

The leaders and elders ordered this.

## Ezra 10:9

Within 3 days Judah’s and Benjamin’s men gathered. 📖

On month 9, day 20, they sat in God’s house’s square, trembling at the matter and heavy rain.

## Ezra 10:10

Ezra the priest said, “You added to Israel’s guilt by marrying foreign women. 📖

## Ezra 10:11

Confess to the LORD, your ancestors’ God, and do his will. 📖

Separate from the land’s peoples and your foreign wives.”

## Ezra 10:12

The assembly loudly answered, “We must do as you say! 📖

## Ezra 10:13

But we are many, and this is rainy season. 📖

We cannot stay outside.

This needs more than 1 or 2 days, because our wrongdoing is great.

## Ezra 10:14

Let leaders represent us. 📖

Each man with a foreign wife can come at an appointed time with his town’s elders and judges.

Let this continue until God’s fierce anger over this turns away.”

## Ezra 10:15

Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah opposed the plan. 📖

Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite supported them.

## Ezra 10:16

The exiles followed the proposal. 📖

Ezra selected named family heads to represent their families.

The inquiry began in month 10, day 1.

## Ezra 10:17

By month 1, day 1, they had dealt with all men who married foreign women. 📖

## Ezra 10:18

Priests involved included descendants of Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers: Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah. 📖

## Ezra 10:19

They pledged to send their wives away and offered a ram for guilt. 📖

## Ezra 10:20

Immer’s descendants included Hanani and Zebadiah, 📖

## Ezra 10:21

## Ezra 10:22

and Pashhur’s, Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Elasah. 📖

## Ezra 10:23

The Levites included Jozabad, Shimei, Kelaiah, also called Kelita, Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer. 📖

## Ezra 10:24

Singer Eliashib and gatekeepers Shallum, Telem, and Uri were included. 📖

## Ezra 10:25

## Ezra 10:26

## Ezra 10:28

## Ezra 10:30
