# Ezra 5 — The rebuilding resumes and officials report

**About this chapter**

Haggai and Zechariah encourage rebuilding. | Officials question the builders and report to Darius. | The letter includes the Jewish elders’ account of the temple’s history.

## Ezra 5:1

Prophets Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo spoke to Judah’s and Jerusalem’s Jews in Israel’s God’s name. 📖

He was over them.

## Ezra 5:2

Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak resumed rebuilding God’s house in Jerusalem. 📖

God’s prophets helped them.

## Ezra 5:3

Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and associates asked who authorised rebuilding this temple and restoring the structure. 📖

## Ezra 5:4

They also asked the builders’ names. 📖

## Ezra 5:5

God’s eye watched the Jewish elders. 📖

Work continued while the report went to Darius and written instructions returned.

**Help with God’s eye:** This pictures God’s watchful care.

## Ezra 5:6

Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and fellow regional officials sent this letter to Darius: 📖

## Ezra 5:7

“To King Darius, all peace. 📖

## Ezra 5:8

We visited Judah’s province and the great God’s house. 📖

They rebuild with large stones and timbers in the walls.

They work diligently and successfully.

## Ezra 5:9

We asked the elders who authorised rebuilding the temple and restoring this structure. 📖

## Ezra 5:10

We requested leaders’ names to report to you. 📖

## Ezra 5:11

They answered, ‘We serve heaven and earth’s God and rebuild the temple a great Israelite king completed many years ago. 📖

## Ezra 5:12

Our ancestors angered heaven’s God. 📖

He handed them to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean who destroyed this temple and exiled its people to Babylon.

## Ezra 5:13

In his 1st year, Cyrus king of Babylon ordered this house rebuilt. 📖

## Ezra 5:14

He returned the temple’s gold and silver objects Nebuchadnezzar carried from Jerusalem to Babylon’s temple. 📖

Cyrus gave them to Sheshbazzar, whom he appointed governor,

## Ezra 5:15

and ordered him to place them in Jerusalem’s temple and rebuild God’s house on its original site. 📖

## Ezra 5:16

Sheshbazzar laid God’s house’s foundation in Jerusalem. 📖

Building has continued from then until now but remains unfinished.’

## Ezra 5:17

If you please, search Babylon’s royal archives to see whether Cyrus ordered God’s Jerusalem house rebuilt. 📖

Send us your decision.”
