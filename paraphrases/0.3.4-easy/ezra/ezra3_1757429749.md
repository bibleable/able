# Ezra 3 — The altar and temple foundation

**About this chapter**

The returned people build the altar and resume offerings. | They prepare the temple foundation. | Some rejoice while others remember the first temple and weep.

## Ezra 3:1

By month 7, Israel had settled in their towns. 📖

They gathered together as one in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 3:2

Jeshua son of Jozadak and fellow priests joined Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his associates to build Israel’s God’s altar. 📖

They would offer burnt offerings as Moses the man of God’s Law required.

## Ezra 3:3

Despite fear of surrounding peoples, they set the altar on its foundation and offered morning and evening burnt offerings. 📖

## Ezra 3:4

They kept Tabernacles as written and offered each day’s required number of burnt offerings. 📖

**Help with Tabernacles:** The feast recalling life in temporary shelters.

## Ezra 3:5

They then offered regular burnt offerings, New Moon and sacred-feast offerings, and every voluntary offering to the LORD. 📖

## Ezra 3:6

On month 7, day 1, they began burnt offerings, although the LORD’s temple foundation was not laid. 📖

## Ezra 3:7

They paid masons and carpenters. 📖

Sidon and Tyre received food, drink, and oil to bring Lebanon cedars by sea to Joppa.

Cyrus king of Persia authorised this.

## Ezra 3:8

Work began in year 2, month 2 after arriving at God’s house in Jerusalem. 📖

Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, and associates began, including priests, Levites, and all returned exiles.

They appointed Levites aged 20 and above to supervise building the LORD’s house.

## Ezra 3:9

Jeshua, his sons and brothers, and Kadmiel and his sons, descendants of Yehudah, supervised together. 📖

Henadad’s sons, their sons, and brothers joined them.

These Levites supervised God’s house’s workers.

## Ezra 3:10

When the foundation was laid, robed priests with trumpets and Asaph’s Levite descendants with cymbals praised the LORD. 📖

They followed David king of Israel’s instructions.

## Ezra 3:11

They sang praise and thanks in response to one another. 📖

“He is good; his faithful love for Israel lasts forever.”

All the people shouted praise because the LORD’s house’s foundation was laid.

## Ezra 3:12

Many older priests, Levites, and family heads who saw the first temple wept loudly at the foundation. 📖

Many others shouted joyfully.

## Ezra 3:13

Nobody could distinguish joyful shouts from weeping amid the noise. 📖

It could be heard far away.
