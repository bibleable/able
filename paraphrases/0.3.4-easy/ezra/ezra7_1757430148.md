# Ezra 7 — Ezra comes with a royal letter

**About this chapter**

Ezra is a priest and skilled teacher of the Law. | Artaxerxes authorises his journey, funding, and judicial work. | The chapter ends in Ezra’s own voice.

## Ezra 7:1

Many years later, under Artaxerxes king of Persia, came Ezra son of Seraiah, grandson of Azariah, great-grandson of Hilkiah, 📖

## Ezra 7:2

son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub, 📖

## Ezra 7:3

son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth, 📖

## Ezra 7:4

son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki, 📖

## Ezra 7:5

son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of chief priest Aaron. 📖

**About this list:** The ancestry continues backwards across these verses.

## Ezra 7:6

Ezra came from Babylon, a scribe skilled in Moses’s Law given by the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

The king granted all his requests because the LORD his God’s hand was on him.

**Help with these words:** A scribe writes and studies texts.

God’s hand pictures his help.

## Ezra 7:7

In Artaxerxes’s 7th year, Ezra went to Jerusalem with Israelites, including priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants. 📖

## Ezra 7:8

He arrived in Jerusalem in the king’s 7th year, month 5. 📖

## Ezra 7:9

He left Babylon on month 1, day 1, and reached Jerusalem on month 5, day 1. 📖

God’s gracious hand was on him.

## Ezra 7:10

Ezra determined to study and practise the LORD’s Law and teach its laws and rules in Israel. 📖

## Ezra 7:11

Artaxerxes gave this letter to Ezra, priest and scribe expert in the LORD’s commands and laws for Israel: 📖

## Ezra 7:12

“Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, scribe of heaven’s God’s Law: greetings. 📖

## Ezra 7:13

Any Israelite volunteers in my kingdom, including priests and Levites, may go with you to Jerusalem. 📖

## Ezra 7:14

The king and his 7 advisers send you to examine Judah and Jerusalem by your God’s Law in your hand. 📖

## Ezra 7:15

Take the silver and gold the king and advisers freely offered Israel’s God, whose dwelling is Jerusalem, 📖

## Ezra 7:16

with all silver and gold found in Babylon’s province and the people’s and priests’ voluntary gifts for God’s Jerusalem house. 📖

## Ezra 7:17

Buy needed bulls, rams, lambs, and their grain and drink offerings with this money. 📖

Offer them on your God’s house’s altar in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 7:18

Use the remaining silver and gold as you and your brothers think best, according to your God’s will. 📖

## Ezra 7:19

Deliver all objects given for your God’s house’s service to Jerusalem’s God. 📖

## Ezra 7:20

Pay any further needs for your God’s house from the royal treasury. 📖

## Ezra 7:21

I, Artaxerxes, order all treasurers west of the Euphrates to promptly supply whatever Ezra requires. 📖

He is priest and scribe of heaven’s God’s Law,

## Ezra 7:22

up to 100 silver talents, 100 wheat cors, 100 wine baths, 100 olive-oil baths, and unlimited salt. 📖

**Help with these units:** Talents measured weight.

Cors and baths measured supplies.

## Ezra 7:23

Do everything heaven’s God commands for his house diligently. 📖

Why bring wrath on the king’s and sons’ realm?

## Ezra 7:24

Do not impose tribute, duty, or toll on priests, Levites, singers, doorkeepers, temple servants, or other servants of God’s house. 📖

**Help with tribute, duty, and toll:** Types of payments to rulers.

## Ezra 7:25

Ezra, use your God-given wisdom to appoint magistrates and judges. 📖

They must judge everyone west of the Euphrates who knows your God’s laws.

Teach anyone who does not know them.

**Help with magistrates:** Officials who judge legal cases.

## Ezra 7:26

Strictly punish anyone breaking your God’s Law and royal law, with death, banishment, confiscated property, or prison.” 📖

**Help with these penalties:** Banishment means being sent away.

Confiscation means property is taken by authority.

These are the king’s orders.

## Ezra 7:27

Bless the LORD, our ancestors’ God, who moved the king to honour the LORD’s Jerusalem house, 📖

## Ezra 7:28

and favoured me before king, advisers, and powerful officials. 📖

The LORD my God’s hand gave me courage, and I gathered Israel’s leaders to return with me.
