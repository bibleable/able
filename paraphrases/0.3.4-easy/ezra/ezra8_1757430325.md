# Ezra 8 — Ezra’s company and the journey

**About this chapter**

Ezra names those travelling with him and seeks Levites to join. | The group fasts and prays for protection. | Gifts are weighed before and after the journey.

## Ezra 8:1

These are family heads and records of those returning with me from Babylon during Artaxerxes’s reign: 📖

## Ezra 8:2

Phinehas’s descendant Gershom, Ithamar’s descendant Daniel, and David’s descendant Hattush, 📖

## Ezra 8:3

from Shecaniah’s descendants. 📖

From Parosh came Zechariah with 150 registered men.

## Ezra 8:4

From Pahath-Moab came Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah with 200 men, 📖

## Ezra 8:5

from Zattu, Shecaniah son of Jahaziel with 300, 📖

## Ezra 8:6

from Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan with 50, 📖

## Ezra 8:7

from Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah with 70, 📖

## Ezra 8:8

from Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael with 80, 📖

## Ezra 8:9

from Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel with 218, 📖

## Ezra 8:10

from Bani, Shelomith son of Josiphiah with 160, 📖

## Ezra 8:11

from Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai with 28, 📖

## Ezra 8:12

from Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan with 110, 📖

## Ezra 8:13

from Adonikam’s later descendants, Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah with 60, 📖

## Ezra 8:14

and from Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur with 70. 📖

## Ezra 8:15

I gathered them at the canal flowing to Ahava and camped 3 days. 📖

I searched the people and priests but found no Levites.

## Ezra 8:16

I summoned leaders Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, and teachers Joiarib and Elnathan. 📖

## Ezra 8:17

I sent them to leader Iddo at Casiphia with a message for him and his temple-servant relatives there. 📖

They must bring servants for our God’s house.

## Ezra 8:18

Our God’s gracious hand brought Sherebiah, an insightful descendant of Mahli son of Levi, son of Israel. 📖

With his sons and brothers, they numbered 18,

## Ezra 8:19

and Hashabiah with Jeshaiah, Merari’s descendants, their brothers, and sons, 20 men. 📖

## Ezra 8:20

They brought 220 named temple servants whom David and officials appointed to help Levites. 📖

## Ezra 8:21

I announced a fast at Ahava Canal to humble ourselves before God. 📖

We sought a safe journey for ourselves, children, and possessions.

**Help with fast:** Going without food as part of seeking God.

## Ezra 8:22

I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen against road enemies. 📖

We told him God’s gracious hand helps all who seek him.

His great anger opposes all who abandon him.

## Ezra 8:23

We fasted and pleaded, and God granted our request. 📖

## Ezra 8:24

I set apart 12 leading priests along with Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and 10 brothers. 📖

## Ezra 8:25

I weighed out gifts for God’s house from king, advisers, leaders, and all Israelites there. 📖

These were silver, gold, and objects.

## Ezra 8:26

I weighed 650 silver talents, silver objects weighing 100 talents, and 100 gold talents, 📖

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.

## Ezra 8:27

20 gold bowls worth 1,000 darics and 2 fine polished bronze objects as precious as gold. 📖

**Help with daric:** A gold coin.

## Ezra 8:28

I told them, “You and these objects are holy to the LORD. 📖

Silver and gold are voluntary gifts to the LORD, your ancestors’ God.

## Ezra 8:29

Guard them until you weigh them in Jerusalem’s temple rooms before chief priests, Levites, and Israelite family heads.” 📖

## Ezra 8:30

Priests and Levites accepted the weighed silver, gold, and sacred objects for our God’s house in Jerusalem. 📖

## Ezra 8:31

On month 1, day 12, we left Ahava Canal for Jerusalem. 📖

God’s hand protected us from enemies and bandits.

## Ezra 8:32

We reached Jerusalem and rested 3 days. 📖

## Ezra 8:33

On day 4, we weighed the silver, gold, and sacred objects in God’s house. 📖

We handed them to priest Meremoth son of Uriah.

Eleazar son of Phinehas and Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui were with him.

## Ezra 8:34

Everything was checked by number and weight, and the full weight recorded. 📖

## Ezra 8:35

Returned exiles offered Israel’s God 12 bulls for all Israel, 96 rams, and 77 lambs. 📖

They offered 12 male goats for sin.

All this was a burnt offering to the LORD.

## Ezra 8:36

They delivered royal orders to satraps and governors west of the Euphrates, who helped the people and God’s house. 📖

**Help with satraps:** Regional rulers in the empire.
