# 1 Peter 3 — Do good and be ready to explain your hope

**About this chapter**

Peter addresses wives and husbands, then teaches all believers about doing good and facing suffering.

He writes about Christ's death, resurrection, and victory.

## 1 Peter 3:1

Wives, in the same way, submit to your husbands. 📖

Do this so that even husbands who refuse the word will be won over without words by their wives' conduct.

## 1 Peter 3:2

This happens when they see your pure and respectful way of life. 📖

## 1 Peter 3:3

Your beauty should not come from outward decorations, such as braided hair, gold jewellery, or fine clothes. 📖

## 1 Peter 3:4

Instead, let it come from your inner heart. 📖

The lasting beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit is precious to God.

## 1 Peter 3:5

This is how the holy women of the past made themselves beautiful. 📖

They hoped in God and submitted to their husbands.

## 1 Peter 3:6

Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him lord. 📖

You are her children if you do right and refuse to give in to fear.

## 1 Peter 3:7

Husbands, in the same way, treat your wives with understanding, as a delicate vessel. 📖

Honour them as fellow heirs of God's gracious gift of life.

Then your prayers will not be hindered.

**Help with these words:** Peter uses a delicate container as a picture for the wife.

He also says husbands and wives share the gift of life together.

An heir receives an inheritance.

Hindered means held back or obstructed.

## 1 Peter 3:8

Finally, all of you, be united in your thinking and care about each other's feelings. 📖

Love as brothers and sisters.

Be tenderhearted and humble.

## 1 Peter 3:9

Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult. 📖

Instead, give a blessing.

God called you to this so that you could inherit a blessing.

## 1 Peter 3:10

“Anyone who wants to love life and see good days must keep their tongue from evil. 📖

They must keep their lips from telling lies.”

## 1 Peter 3:11

“They must turn from evil and do good. 📖

They must seek peace and work for it.”

## 1 Peter 3:12

“The Lord's eyes watch over the righteous, and his ears listen to their prayers. 📖

But the Lord's face is against those who do evil.”

**Help with these pictures:** These words describe the Lord's care, attention, and opposition to evil.

Righteous means right before God.

## 1 Peter 3:13

Who can harm you if you are eager to do good? 📖

## 1 Peter 3:14

But even if you suffer for doing right, you are blessed. 📖

“Do not fear what they fear.

Do not be shaken.”

## 1 Peter 3:15

In your hearts, honour Christ as Lord. 📖

Always be ready to explain your hope to anyone who asks.

Answer with gentleness and respect.

## 1 Peter 3:16

Keep a clear conscience. 📖

Then your good conduct in Christ may put to shame those who speak falsely against you.

## 1 Peter 3:17

If God wills it, it is better to suffer for doing good than for doing evil. 📖

## 1 Peter 3:18

Christ suffered for sins once for all, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring you to God. 📖

He was put to death in the body but made alive in the Spirit.

**Help with these words:** Christ did right, but suffered for those who did wrong.

## 1 Peter 3:19

Through the Spirit, he also went and preached to the spirits in prison. 📖

## 1 Peter 3:20

They disobeyed long ago, when God waited patiently in Noah's days while the ark was being built. 📖

Only a few people, eight in all, were saved through water in the ark.

**Help with a word:** The ark was the large boat Noah built.

## 1 Peter 3:21

This water pictures baptism, which now saves you too. 📖

It is not washing dirt from the body.

It is the pledge of a clear conscience towards God.

This salvation comes through Jesus Christ rising from the dead.

**Help with a word:** A pledge is a serious promise.

## 1 Peter 3:22

Jesus has gone into heaven and is at God's right hand. 📖

Angels, authorities, and powers are under his rule.

**Help with this picture:** The right hand is a place of honour and authority.
