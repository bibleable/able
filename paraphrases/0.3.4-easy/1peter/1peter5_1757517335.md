# 1 Peter 5 — Care for others and trust God

**About this chapter**

Peter writes to church leaders and other believers.

## 1 Peter 5:1

I appeal to your elders as a fellow elder. 📖

I saw Christ suffer.

I will also share in the glory to be shown.

**Help with a word:** Elders are leaders who care for a church.

## 1 Peter 5:2

Care for God's people like shepherds caring for sheep. 📖

Do it willingly, as God wants, rather than because you are forced.

Be eager to serve, not greedy for money.

## 1 Peter 5:3

Do not boss people around. 📖

Be an example to those in your care.

## 1 Peter 5:4

When the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive a crown of glory that never fades. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Jesus is the Chief Shepherd.

The crown pictures lasting honour.

## 1 Peter 5:5

Young men, accept the leadership of the elders. 📖

Everyone, put on humility towards each other like clothing.

God opposes proud people but gives kindness to humble people.

**Help with this picture:** Humility means not putting yourself above others.

Peter pictures it as clothing to wear in relationships with others.

## 1 Peter 5:6

Be humble under God's powerful hand. 📖

At the right time, God will lift you up.

## 1 Peter 5:7

Give all your worries to God. 📖

God cares for you.

## 1 Peter 5:8

Think clearly and stay alert. 📖

Your enemy the devil moves around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to eat.

**Help with this picture:** The lion pictures the devil's desire to destroy people.

## 1 Peter 5:9

Stand against him with firm faith. 📖

Believers throughout the world are facing the same kinds of suffering.

## 1 Peter 5:10

After you have suffered a little while, God himself will restore, secure, strengthen, and establish you. 📖

God, who gives all kindness, called you to his lasting glory in Christ.

**Help with words:** Restore means put right again.

Secure and establish mean set firmly in place.

## 1 Peter 5:11

May power belong to him forever. 📖

Amen.

## 1 Peter 5:12

I wrote this short letter through Silvanus, whom I consider a faithful fellow believer. 📖

I encourage you and tell you this is God's true kindness.

Stand firm in it.

## 1 Peter 5:13

The church in Babylon, chosen by God as you are, sends greetings. 📖

So does Mark, whom I call my son.

## 1 Peter 5:14

Greet each other with a loving kiss. 📖

Peace to all of you who belong to Christ.
