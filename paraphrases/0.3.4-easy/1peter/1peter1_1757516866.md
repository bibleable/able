# 1 Peter 1 — A living hope

**About this chapter**

Peter writes to believers scattered across several regions.

He describes their hope through Jesus and calls them to a holy life.

## 1 Peter 1:1

This letter is from Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ. 📖

It is for God's chosen people, living as scattered exiles in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia.

**Help with words:** An apostle is someone sent out for a special task.

Exiles live away from their homeland.

These names are regions where the believers lived.

## 1 Peter 1:2

God the Father chose you according to what he knew beforehand. 📖

The Spirit set you apart to obey Jesus Christ and be sprinkled with his blood.

May you have kindness and peace in abundance.

**Help with this picture:** Sprinkling with Christ's blood points to his death for his people.

## 1 Peter 1:3

Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! 📖

In his great mercy, he gave us new birth into a living hope.

He did this through Jesus Christ rising from the dead.

**Help with words:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

New birth pictures receiving new life from God.

## 1 Peter 1:4

He also gave us an inheritance kept in heaven for you. 📖

It can never be destroyed or spoiled.

It will never fade.

**Help with a word:** An inheritance is something received as a family member.

## 1 Peter 1:5

Through faith, God's power guards you for the salvation ready to be revealed at the last time. 📖

**Help with words:** Faith means trust in God here.

Salvation means being saved by God.

## 1 Peter 1:6

You greatly rejoice in this. 📖

Yet for a little while now, you may have to suffer grief through different trials.

## 1 Peter 1:7

These trials prove your faith, which is more precious than gold. 📖

Even gold tested by fire can perish.

Your proven faith can bring praise, glory, and honour when Jesus Christ is revealed.

**Help with these words:** Gold is heated in fire to remove what spoils it.

Peter compares tested faith with tested gold.

## 1 Peter 1:8

You love Jesus although you have not seen him. 📖

You do not see him now, but you believe in him.

You rejoice with glorious joy beyond words.

**Help with a word:** Glorious describes wonderful greatness and honour.

## 1 Peter 1:9

You are receiving the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls. 📖

## 1 Peter 1:10

The prophets searched and studied carefully about this salvation. 📖

They had spoken beforehand about the kindness that would come to you.

**Help with a word:** Prophets speak God's message.

## 1 Peter 1:11

They tried to find out the time and situation the Spirit of Christ within them was pointing to. 📖

The Spirit predicted Christ's sufferings and the glories that would follow.

## 1 Peter 1:12

God revealed that they were serving you, rather than themselves. 📖

They spoke about the things now announced by those who told you the good news.

Those messengers spoke by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven.

Even angels long to look into these things.

## 1 Peter 1:13

So prepare your minds for action and think clearly. 📖

Set your hope completely on the kindness you will receive when Jesus Christ is revealed.

## 1 Peter 1:14

Be obedient children. 📖

Do not follow your old desires from the time when you did not know better.

## 1 Peter 1:15

The One who called you is holy. 📖

So be holy in everything you do.

**Help with a word:** Holy means set apart for God.

## 1 Peter 1:16

The Scriptures say, “Be holy, because I am holy.” 📖

**Help with a word:** The Scriptures are the holy writings.

## 1 Peter 1:17

You call on a Father who judges everyone's work without favouring anyone. 📖

Live with deep respect and fear during your time as foreigners here.

## 1 Peter 1:18

You know that silver or gold did not pay for your freedom. 📖

These things can perish.

You were freed from the empty way of life passed down by your ancestors.

## 1 Peter 1:19

Christ's precious blood paid for your freedom. 📖

He is like a lamb without any fault or spot.

**Help with this picture:** Lambs were offered in worship.

Peter connects Christ's death with a perfect offering.

## 1 Peter 1:20

Christ was known before the world was founded. 📖

But he was revealed in these last times for your sake.

## 1 Peter 1:21

Through Christ, you believe in God. 📖

God raised Christ from the dead and gave him glory.

So your faith and hope are in God.

## 1 Peter 1:22

You have purified your souls by obeying the truth, leading to sincere love for fellow believers. 📖

So love each other deeply from a pure heart.

## 1 Peter 1:23

You have been born again through God's living word, which lasts. 📖

This birth comes from seed that cannot perish, rather than seed that perishes.

**Help with this picture:** Seed pictures the source of this new life.

## 1 Peter 1:24

The Scriptures say, “All people are like grass, and all their glory is like flowers in the field. 📖

The grass dries up, and the flowers fall.”

## 1 Peter 1:25

“But the Lord's word stands for ever.” 📖

This is the word told to you in the good news.
