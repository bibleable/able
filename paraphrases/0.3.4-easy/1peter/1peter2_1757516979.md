# 1 Peter 2 — Living stones and Christ's example

**About this chapter**

Peter describes believers as God's building and chosen people.

He teaches them about living among others, authority, and unfair suffering.

## 1 Peter 2:1

So get rid of every wish to harm, every lie, pretence, envy, and harmful false talk. 📖

## 1 Peter 2:2

Like newborn babies, long for pure spiritual milk. 📖

Through it, you can grow up in your salvation.

**Help with this picture:** Milk pictures the nourishment believers need for their new life with God.

## 1 Peter 2:3

You have tasted that the Lord is good. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Tasting pictures experiencing the Lord's goodness.

## 1 Peter 2:4

Come to him, the living stone. 📖

People rejected him, but God chose him and considers him precious.

## 1 Peter 2:5

You too are like living stones being built into a spiritual house. 📖

You are a holy priesthood, offering spiritual sacrifices that God accepts through Jesus Christ.

**Help with these pictures:** Stones form a building, just as believers are joined together as God's people.

A priesthood is a group serving God in worship.

## 1 Peter 2:6

The Scriptures say, “Look, I lay a stone in Zion, a chosen and precious cornerstone. 📖

Anyone who believes in him will never be put to shame.”

**Help with words:** Zion is a name linked with Jerusalem.

A cornerstone is an important stone in a building.

## 1 Peter 2:7

This stone is precious to you who believe. 📖

But for those who do not believe, “The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.”

## 1 Peter 2:8

He is “a stone people stumble over and a rock that offends them.” 📖

They stumble because they disobey the word.

They were appointed to this.

**Help with words:** Stumbling pictures rejecting Christ and his message.

Appointed means assigned or set for something.

## 1 Peter 2:9

But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, and God's own people. 📖

Tell others about the wonderful qualities of the One who called you from darkness into his wonderful light.

**Help with words:** Royal means belonging to a king.

Holy means set apart for God.

Darkness and light picture the change to life among God's people.

## 1 Peter 2:10

Once you were not a people, but now you are God's people. 📖

Once you had not received mercy, but now you have.

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## 1 Peter 2:11

Dear friends, I urge you as foreigners and exiles to stay away from sinful desires. 📖

These desires fight against your soul.

## 1 Peter 2:12

Live honourably among the Gentiles, even when they falsely call you evildoers. 📖

Then they may see your good deeds and praise God on the day he visits us.

**Help with a word:** Gentiles means people who are not Jews.

## 1 Peter 2:13

For the Lord's sake, submit to every human authority. 📖

Submit to the king as the highest authority.

**Help with a word:** Submit means to accept someone's authority.

## 1 Peter 2:14

Also submit to governors sent by the king. 📖

They punish those who do wrong and praise those who do right.

## 1 Peter 2:15

God wants your good actions to silence the ignorant talk of foolish people. 📖

**Help with a word:** Ignorant means not knowing the truth here.

## 1 Peter 2:16

Live as free people. 📖

But do not use freedom to hide evil.

Live as God's servants.

## 1 Peter 2:17

Honour everyone. 📖

Love your fellow believers.

Fear God.

Honour the king.

## 1 Peter 2:18

Servants, submit to your masters with all respect. 📖

Do this for masters who are good and gentle, and even those who are unreasonable.

## 1 Peter 2:19

It is praiseworthy when someone endures the pain of unfair suffering because they are mindful of God. 📖

## 1 Peter 2:20

What credit is there in enduring a beating for doing wrong? 📖

But if you suffer for doing good and endure it, God finds this praiseworthy.

## 1 Peter 2:21

You were called to this, because Christ suffered for you. 📖

He left an example so you could follow in his footsteps.

**Help with this picture:** Following his footsteps means following his example.

## 1 Peter 2:22

“He did nothing wrong, and no lies were found in his mouth.” 📖

## 1 Peter 2:23

When people insulted Christ, he did not insult them back. 📖

When he suffered, he did not threaten them.

He entrusted himself to the One who judges fairly.

## 1 Peter 2:24

He himself carried our sins in his body on the tree. 📖

He did this so we could die to sin and live to do what is right.

“His wounds have healed you.”

**Help with these words:** The tree refers to the cross on which Christ died.

Dying to sin pictures leaving its control.

## 1 Peter 2:25

“You were like sheep wandering away.” 📖

Now you have returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.

**Help with these titles:** Christ is pictured as the one who cares for and watches over his people.
