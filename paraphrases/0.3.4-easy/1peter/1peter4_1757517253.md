# 1 Peter 4 — Serve faithfully through suffering

**About this chapter**

Peter urges believers to leave their old way of life, serve each other, and stay faithful when they suffer.

## 1 Peter 4:1

Christ suffered in his body. 📖

So arm yourselves with the same determination.

Anyone who has suffered in the body is done with sin.

**Help with these words:** Peter connects this determination with living for God's will in the next verse.

## 1 Peter 4:2

That person lives the rest of their earthly life for what God wants, rather than human desires. 📖

## 1 Peter 4:3

You have spent enough time doing what the Gentiles want. 📖

You lived in uncontrolled shameful behaviour, lust, drunkenness, sex parties, wild drinking parties, and hateful idol worship.

**Help with words:** Gentiles means people who are not Jews.

Lust is a strong wrong sexual desire.

An idol is something worshipped in God's place.

## 1 Peter 4:4

They think it strange that you do not plunge with them into the same flood of reckless living. 📖

So they insult you.

**Help with this picture:** A flood pictures being swept into uncontrolled behaviour.

## 1 Peter 4:5

They must give an account to the One ready to judge the living and the dead. 📖

## 1 Peter 4:6

That is why the good news was told even to those who are now dead. 📖

This was so that they might be judged as humans in the body.

Yet they might live according to God in the spirit.

## 1 Peter 4:7

The end of all things is near. 📖

So think clearly and be sober, so that you can pray.

## 1 Peter 4:8

Above all, love each other deeply, because love covers many sins. 📖

## 1 Peter 4:9

Welcome each other into your homes without complaining. 📖

## 1 Peter 4:10

Each of you should use the gift you received to serve others. 📖

Be good managers of God's kindness in its many forms.

## 1 Peter 4:11

Anyone who speaks should speak as someone passing on God's words. 📖

Anyone who serves should use the strength God provides.

Then God may be honoured in everything through Jesus Christ.

May Christ have glory and power for ever and ever.

Amen.

## 1 Peter 4:12

Dear friends, do not be surprised by the fiery trial you are facing. 📖

Do not think something strange is happening to you.

**Help with this picture:** A fiery trial pictures severe testing through suffering.

## 1 Peter 4:13

Instead, rejoice that you share Christ's sufferings. 📖

Then you can be overjoyed when his glory is revealed.

## 1 Peter 4:14

If you are insulted because of Christ's name, you are blessed. 📖

The Spirit of glory and of God rests on you.

## 1 Peter 4:15

None of you should suffer as a murderer, thief, wrongdoer, or someone who meddles in others' affairs. 📖

**Help with a word:** Meddling means interfering in other people's business.

## 1 Peter 4:16

But if you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed. 📖

Honour God because you bear that name.

## 1 Peter 4:17

It is time for judgment to begin with God's family. 📖

If it begins with us, what will happen to those who disobey God's good news?

## 1 Peter 4:18

“If it is hard for the righteous to be saved, what will happen to the ungodly and the sinner?” 📖

## 1 Peter 4:19

So those who suffer according to God's will should entrust their souls to their faithful Creator. 📖

They should keep doing good.

**Help with a word:** Entrust means place in someone's care.
