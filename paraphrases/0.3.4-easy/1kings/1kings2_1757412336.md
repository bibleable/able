# 1 Kings 2 — Solomon’s kingdom is established

**About this chapter**

David gives final instructions and dies.

Solomon orders deaths, removes Abiathar from the priesthood, and appoints officials.

## 1 Kings 2:1

Near death, David instructed Solomon. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:2

“I will soon go the way of all the earth. 📖

Be strong and prove yourself a man.

**Help with way of all the earth:** This means death.

**About prove yourself a man:** David uses this expression to urge strength and responsibility.

## 1 Kings 2:3

Keep the LORD your God’s charge. 📖

Follow his ways and all his laws, commands, rules, and decrees in Moses’ Law.

Then you may prosper in everything you do, wherever you turn.

## 1 Kings 2:4

And so the LORD may fulfil his promise to me. 📖

He said, ‘If your descendants follow me faithfully with all heart and soul, Israel’s throne will never lack your heir.’

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

**About this promise:** Its condition is faithful conduct.

## 1 Kings 2:5

You know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me and to 2 army commanders. 📖

He killed Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether in peacetime to avenge wartime bloodshed.

He stained his belt and sandals with war’s blood.

## 1 Kings 2:6

Use your wisdom. 📖

Do not let his grey head go to Sheol in peace.

**Help with Sheol:** Sheol refers to the place of the dead.

David calls for Joab not to die peacefully.

## 1 Kings 2:7

Show faithful love to Barzillai the Gileadite’s sons and give them places at your table. 📖

They helped me flee your brother Absalom.

## 1 Kings 2:8

Watch Shimei son of Gera, the Benjamite from Bahurim. 📖

He bitterly cursed me when I went to Mahanaim.

At the Jordan, I swore by the LORD not to kill him with the sword.

## 1 Kings 2:9

Do not consider him innocent. 📖

You are wise and know how to bring his grey head to Sheol in blood.”

**Help with Sheol in blood:** David calls for a violent death.

The source’s difficult instruction is retained.

## 1 Kings 2:10

David rested with his ancestors and was buried in the City of David. 📖

**Help with rested:** This means died.

## 1 Kings 2:11

David ruled Israel for 40 years: 7 at Hebron and 33 at Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:12

Solomon sat on David’s throne, and his kingdom was firmly established. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:13

Adonijah son of Haggith visited Solomon’s mother Bathsheba. 📖

She asked, “Do you come in peace?”

He answered, “Yes.”

## 1 Kings 2:14

Adonijah said, “I have something to tell you.” 📖

She answered, “Speak.”

## 1 Kings 2:15

He said, “The kingship was mine, and all Israel expected me to reign. 📖

It went to my brother from the LORD.

**About the kingship:** These are Adonijah’s claims.

## 1 Kings 2:16

I have one request. 📖

Do not refuse.”

She asked him to state it.

## 1 Kings 2:17

He said, “Ask Solomon to give me Abishag the Shunammite as my wife. 📖

He will not refuse you.”

## 1 Kings 2:18

Bathsheba agreed to speak for him. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:19

Bathsheba went to Solomon. 📖

He rose, greeted and bowed to her, and sat on his throne.

He provided a throne for her at his right.

## 1 Kings 2:20

She said, “Do not refuse my small request.” 📖

He answered, “Ask, my mother.

I will not refuse.”

## 1 Kings 2:21

She asked him to give Abishag the Shunammite to Adonijah as his wife. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:22

Solomon answered his mother, “Why request Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? 📖

He is my older brother.

You might as well ask for the kingdom for him, Abiathar the priest, and Joab son of Zeruiah!”

## 1 Kings 2:23

Solomon swore by the LORD, “May God punish me very severely unless this request costs Adonijah his life. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:24

The LORD established me on David’s throne and founded my dynasty as promised. 📖

As surely as he lives, Adonijah will die today!”

**Help with dynasty:** A dynasty is a continuing line of rulers from one family.

## 1 Kings 2:25

Solomon ordered Benaiah son of Jehoiada to strike Adonijah down, and Adonijah died. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:26

Solomon told Abiathar, “Return to your fields at Anathoth. 📖

You deserve death, but I will spare you now.

You carried the ark of the Lord GOD before David and shared all his suffering.”

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Kings 2:27

Solomon removed Abiathar from the LORD’s priesthood. 📖

This fulfilled the LORD’s word at Shiloh against Eli’s family.

## 1 Kings 2:28

Joab heard and fled to the LORD’s tent, holding the altar’s horns. 📖

He had joined Adonijah’s plot, but not Absalom’s.

**Help with horns:** These were projecting corners on the altar.

Holding them was an appeal for protection.

## 1 Kings 2:29

Solomon heard Joab had fled to the LORD’s tent beside the altar. 📖

He sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada to kill him.

## 1 Kings 2:30

Benaiah entered and told Joab the king commanded him to come out. 📖

Joab answered, “No, I will die here.”

Benaiah reported his answer.

## 1 Kings 2:31

The king ordered, “Do as he says. 📖

Kill and bury him to remove his innocent bloodshed from me and my father’s family.

**Help with innocent bloodshed:** This means killing people who should not be killed.

## 1 Kings 2:32

The LORD will bring his bloodshed on his own head. 📖

Without David knowing, he killed 2 better and more righteous men.

They were Abner son of Ner, Israel’s army commander, and Amasa son of Jether, Judah’s army commander.

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means right before God.

## 1 Kings 2:33

Their blood will return on Joab and his descendants forever. 📖

The LORD will give David, his descendants, family, and throne peace forever.”

**Help with blood:** This pictures responsibility and consequences for the killings.

## 1 Kings 2:34

Benaiah son of Jehoiada killed Joab. 📖

Joab was buried at his home in the wilderness.

## 1 Kings 2:35

Solomon appointed Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the army instead of Joab, and Zadok instead of Abiathar. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:36

Solomon told Shimei, “Build a house and stay in Jerusalem. 📖

Go nowhere else.

## 1 Kings 2:37

If you leave and cross the Kidron Valley, you will certainly die. 📖

Your blood will be on your own head.”

**Help with blood on your own head:** Solomon says Shimei will bear responsibility for his death.

## 1 Kings 2:38

Shimei accepted the sentence as fair and promised obedience. 📖

He stayed in Jerusalem a long time.

## 1 Kings 2:39

After 3 years, 2 slaves escaped to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. 📖

Shimei heard they were there.

## 1 Kings 2:40

Shimei saddled his donkey, went to Achish at Gath, and brought the slaves back. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:41

Solomon heard Shimei had left Jerusalem for Gath and returned. 📖

## 1 Kings 2:42

He summoned Shimei. 📖

“Did I not make you swear by the LORD and warn you that leaving meant certain death?

You accepted and promised obedience.

## 1 Kings 2:43

Why break your oath to the LORD and my command?” 📖

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise.

## 1 Kings 2:44

The king added, “You know your evil against David. 📖

The LORD will return it on your own head.

## 1 Kings 2:45

King Solomon will be blessed, and David’s throne will stand secure before the LORD forever.” 📖

## 1 Kings 2:46

Solomon ordered Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who struck Shimei down and killed him. 📖

Solomon’s kingdom was firmly established.
