# 1 Kings 5 — Materials and workers for the temple

**About this chapter**

Solomon asks Hiram for timber.

The chapter records payments, forced labour, and preparation of stones.

## 1 Kings 5:1

Hiram king of Tyre heard Solomon had been anointed king instead of David. 📖

Hiram sent messengers because he had always been David’s friend.

**Help with anointed:** Anointed means marked for a role by the use of oil.

## 1 Kings 5:2

Solomon sent Hiram this message. 📖

## 1 Kings 5:3

“Wars surrounded my father David, preventing him building a house for the LORD his God’s Name. 📖

This lasted until the LORD put his enemies under his feet.

**Help with under his feet:** This pictures enemies brought under control.

## 1 Kings 5:4

The LORD my God has given me rest everywhere, without enemy or crisis. 📖

## 1 Kings 5:5

I plan to build a house for the LORD my God’s Name. 📖

He told David, ‘Your son will sit on your throne and build the house for my Name.’

**Help with house for the Name:** This means a temple for worship of the LORD.

## 1 Kings 5:6

Order Lebanon’s cedars cut for me. 📖

My servants will join yours, and I will pay whatever wages you set.

None of us cuts timber as skilfully as the Sidonians.”

## 1 Kings 5:7

Hiram rejoiced and said, “Blessed be the LORD today! 📖

He gave David a wise son over this great people!”

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed expresses praise to God.

## 1 Kings 5:8

Hiram answered, “I received your message and will supply all the cedar and cypress timber you want. 📖

**Help with cedar and cypress:** These are kinds of trees used for timber.

## 1 Kings 5:9

My servants will bring logs from Lebanon to the sea. 📖

I will float them as rafts to your chosen place and separate them there for you.

In return, provide food for my household.”

## 1 Kings 5:10

Hiram supplied all Solomon’s requested cedar and cypress timber. 📖

## 1 Kings 5:11

Each year, Solomon supplied Hiram’s household with 20,000 cors of wheat and 20,000 baths of pure olive oil. 📖

**Help with cors and baths:** These were measures for grain and liquids.

## 1 Kings 5:12

The LORD gave Solomon wisdom as promised. 📖

Hiram and Solomon had peace and made a treaty.

**Help with treaty:** A treaty is a formal agreement.

## 1 Kings 5:13

Solomon forced 30,000 men from all Israel into labour service. 📖

## 1 Kings 5:14

He sent 10,000 each month to Lebanon. 📖

Each group worked there 1 month and stayed home 2 months.

Adoniram managed the forced labour.

## 1 Kings 5:15

Solomon had 70,000 load carriers and 80,000 stonecutters in the mountains. 📖

## 1 Kings 5:16

There were also 3,300 supervisors overseeing the workers. 📖

## 1 Kings 5:17

The king ordered large, costly stones cut from the quarry and shaped for the temple’s foundations. 📖

**Help with quarry:** A quarry is a place where stone is cut from the ground.

**Help with foundations:** Foundations are the base supporting a building.

## 1 Kings 5:18

Solomon’s and Hiram’s builders and the Gebalites cut stone and prepared timber and stone for the temple. 📖
