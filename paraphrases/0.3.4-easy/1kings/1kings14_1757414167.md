# 1 Kings 14 — Ahijah’s message and Judah’s troubles

**About this chapter**

Jeroboam’s wife asks about her sick son.

Ahijah announces judgment, and the chapter also records Rehoboam’s reign.

## 1 Kings 14:1

Jeroboam’s son Abijah became ill. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:2

Jeroboam told his wife, “Disguise yourself so no one recognises you as my wife. 📖

Go to Shiloh, where Ahijah the prophet lives.

He told me I would rule this people.”

## 1 Kings 14:3

“Take 10 loaves, cakes, and a jar of honey. 📖

He will tell you the boy’s future.”

## 1 Kings 14:4

She went to Ahijah’s house in Shiloh. 📖

Age had dimmed his eyes so he could not see.

## 1 Kings 14:5

The LORD told Ahijah that Jeroboam’s wife would come about her sick son, disguised. 📖

The LORD told him what to say.

## 1 Kings 14:6

Ahijah heard her footsteps at the door. 📖

“Enter, Jeroboam’s wife!

Why disguise yourself?

I bring bad news.”

## 1 Kings 14:7

He said, “Tell Jeroboam the LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I raised you from the people to rule Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:8

I tore David’s kingdom away for you. 📖

You were not like David, who obeyed me wholeheartedly and did only right before me.

## 1 Kings 14:9

You did more evil than all before you. 📖

You made other gods and cast images, angering me and throwing me behind your back.

**Help with behind your back:** This pictures disregarding God.

## 1 Kings 14:10

I will bring disaster on your family, killing every male, slave and free, in Israel. 📖

I will burn Jeroboam’s family away like dung until none remains.

**Help with dung:** Dung means animal waste.

The degrading picture is retained from the source.

## 1 Kings 14:11

Dogs will eat those dying in the city; birds will eat those dying in the field.’ 📖

The LORD has spoken.

## 1 Kings 14:12

Go home. 📖

The child will die when your feet enter the city.

## 1 Kings 14:13

Israel will grieve and bury him. 📖

Only he in Jeroboam’s family will receive proper burial, because only in him did Israel’s God find good.

## 1 Kings 14:14

The LORD will raise a king to destroy Jeroboam’s family. 📖

This is the day, even today!

## 1 Kings 14:15

The LORD will strike Israel like a reed shaking in water. 📖

He will uproot them from their ancestors’ good land and scatter them beyond the Euphrates.

Their Asherah poles provoked his anger.

**Help with Asherah poles:** These were objects used in worship of the goddess Asherah.

## 1 Kings 14:16

He will give Israel over because of Jeroboam’s sins and the sins he caused Israel to commit.” 📖

## 1 Kings 14:17

Jeroboam’s wife returned to Tirzah. 📖

As she crossed her house’s doorway, the boy died.

## 1 Kings 14:18

They buried him, and all Israel grieved, as the LORD said through Ahijah the prophet. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:19

Jeroboam’s other acts, wars, and rule are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:20

Jeroboam ruled 22 years, died, and was succeeded by his son Nadab. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:21

Rehoboam son of Solomon became Judah’s king at 41 and ruled 17 years in Jerusalem. 📖

The LORD chose that city from all Israel’s tribes for his Name.

His mother was Naamah the Ammonite.

## 1 Kings 14:22

Judah did evil, provoking the LORD’s jealous anger with sins greater than their ancestors’. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:23

They built high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree. 📖

**Help with high places:** These were raised places used for worship.

**Help with Asherah poles:** These were objects used in worship of the goddess Asherah.

## 1 Kings 14:24

Male shrine prostitutes lived there too. 📖

Judah copied the hateful practices of nations the LORD drove out before Israel.

**Help with shrine prostitutes:** This describes men providing sexual acts connected with places of worship.

## 1 Kings 14:25

In Rehoboam’s 5th year, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:26

He took all the temple’s and palace’s treasures, including Solomon’s gold shields. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:27

Rehoboam replaced them with bronze shields, entrusted to guard captains at the palace entrance. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:28

Guards carried them whenever the king entered the LORD’s house, then returned them to the guardroom. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:29

Rehoboam’s other deeds are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:30

Rehoboam and Jeroboam fought throughout their lives. 📖

## 1 Kings 14:31

Rehoboam died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. 📖

His mother was Naamah the Ammonite.

His son Abijam succeeded him.
