# 1 Kings 18 — The LORD answers on Mount Carmel

**About this chapter**

Elijah meets Ahab and calls the people to choose whom to follow.

The LORD answers with fire, and rain returns.

## 1 Kings 18:1

In the drought’s 3rd year, after a long time, the LORD told Elijah to meet Ahab. 📖

He would send rain.

**Help with drought:** A drought is a long time with very little rain.

## 1 Kings 18:2

Elijah went. 📖

Samaria’s famine was severe.

**Help with famine:** Famine means a severe shortage of food.

## 1 Kings 18:3

Ahab called palace manager Obadiah, who greatly feared the LORD. 📖

**Help with feared:** Here, feared includes deep respect.

## 1 Kings 18:4

When Jezebel killed the LORD’s prophets, Obadiah hid 100, with 50 in each cave, supplying food and water. 📖

**Help with prophets:** Prophets bring messages from God.

## 1 Kings 18:5

Ahab said, “Search every spring and valley for grass to save our horses and mules, avoiding livestock deaths.” 📖

## 1 Kings 18:6

They divided the search, each going alone in opposite directions. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:7

Obadiah met Elijah, recognised him, and bowed face down. 📖

“Is it you, my lord Elijah?”

## 1 Kings 18:8

Elijah answered, “It is. 📖

Tell your master I am here.”

## 1 Kings 18:9

Obadiah asked, “What sin makes you hand me to Ahab to die? 📖

## 1 Kings 18:10

As surely as the LORD your God lives, Ahab searched every nation and kingdom for you. 📖

He made each swear you were not found.

## 1 Kings 18:11

Now you tell me to report your presence! 📖

## 1 Kings 18:12

The LORD’s Spirit may carry you away after I leave. 📖

Ahab will not find you and will kill me.

I feared the LORD from my youth.

## 1 Kings 18:13

Did you hear what I did when Jezebel killed the LORD’s prophets? 📖

I hid 100 of them, 50 in each cave, and supplied food and water.

## 1 Kings 18:14

Now you tell me to report you! 📖

He will kill me!”

## 1 Kings 18:15

Elijah swore, “As surely as the LORD of Hosts lives, whom I serve, I will meet Ahab today.” 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 1 Kings 18:16

Obadiah told Ahab, who came to meet Elijah. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:17

Ahab asked, “Is that you, Israel’s troubler?” 📖

## 1 Kings 18:18

Elijah answered, “You and your father’s family troubled Israel, not I. 📖

You abandoned the LORD’s commands and followed the Baals.

**Help with Baals:** These were gods worshipped by peoples around Israel.

## 1 Kings 18:19

Gather all Israel on Mount Carmel with Baal’s 450 prophets and Asherah’s 400 prophets who eat at Jezebel’s table.” 📖

## 1 Kings 18:20

Ahab gathered Israel and the prophets on Mount Carmel. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:21

Elijah asked the people, “How long will you waver between two opinions? 📖

If the LORD is God, follow him.

If Baal is God, follow him.”

No one answered.

## 1 Kings 18:22

Elijah said, “I alone remain as the LORD’s prophet, against Baal’s 450. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:23

Bring 2 bulls. 📖

Let Baal’s prophets choose one, cut it up, and place it on wood without lighting a fire.

I will prepare the other bull and place it on wood without lighting a fire.

## 1 Kings 18:24

Call on your god’s name, and I will call on the LORD’s name. 📖

The God who answers by fire is God.”

All the people said, “What you say is good.”

## 1 Kings 18:25

Elijah told Baal’s prophets to choose first because they were many. 📖

They should prepare a bull and call their god without lighting fire.

## 1 Kings 18:26

They prepared their bull and called Baal from morning to noon, leaping around their altar. 📖

“Baal, answer!”

No voice or answer came.

## 1 Kings 18:27

At noon, Elijah mocked, “Shout louder! 📖

He is a god, perhaps thinking, busy, travelling, or sleeping and needing waking!”

## 1 Kings 18:28

They shouted and cut themselves with knives and lances as usual until blood flowed over them. 📖

**Help with lances:** Lances are long spears.

## 1 Kings 18:29

They raved from midday until evening sacrifice. 📖

No one answered or paid attention.

## 1 Kings 18:30

Elijah told all the people, “Come near me.” 📖

All of them came near.

He repaired the LORD’s altar, which had been torn down.

## 1 Kings 18:31

He took 12 stones, one per tribe of Jacob’s sons. 📖

The LORD had told Jacob his name would be Israel.

## 1 Kings 18:32

He built an altar in the LORD’s name and dug a trench holding 2 seahs of seed. 📖

**Help with seahs:** A seah was a measure for dry goods.

## 1 Kings 18:33

He arranged wood and placed the cut bull on it. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:34

He said, “Fill 4 waterpots and pour the water on the offering and wood.” 📖

He told them to do it a 2nd time, and they did.

He told them to do it a 3rd time, and they did.

## 1 Kings 18:35

Water surrounded the altar and filled the trench. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:36

At evening sacrifice, Elijah approached and prayed. 📖

“LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, show today you are Israel’s God.

Show I am your servant and obeyed your command in everything.

## 1 Kings 18:37

Answer me, LORD, answer! 📖

Let them know you are God and have turned their hearts back.”

## 1 Kings 18:38

The LORD’s fire burned the sacrifice, wood, stones, dust, and trench water. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:39

Everyone fell face down. 📖

“The LORD is God!

The LORD is God!”

## 1 Kings 18:40

Elijah ordered every Baal prophet seized, with none escaping. 📖

He brought them to Kishon’s Valley and slaughtered them there.

**About these deaths:** The source’s account of Elijah killing them is retained.

## 1 Kings 18:41

Elijah told Ahab to eat and drink because heavy rain could be heard. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:42

Ahab went, while Elijah climbed Carmel’s summit, bent to the ground, and put his face between his knees. 📖

## 1 Kings 18:43

Elijah sent his servant to look towards the sea. 📖

He reported nothing.

Elijah sent him back 7 times.

## 1 Kings 18:44

On the 7th, he reported a cloud like a man’s hand rising from the sea. 📖

Elijah told him to warn Ahab to prepare his chariot before rain stopped him.

## 1 Kings 18:45

The sky darkened with clouds and wind, and heavy rain fell. 📖

Ahab rode to Jezreel.

## 1 Kings 18:46

The LORD’s hand came on Elijah. 📖

He tucked his cloak into his belt and ran ahead of Ahab to Jezreel.

**Help with hand:** This pictures the LORD’s empowering action.
