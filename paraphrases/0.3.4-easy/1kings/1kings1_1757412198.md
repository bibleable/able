# 1 Kings 1 — Solomon is made king

**About this chapter**

Adonijah claims the kingship while David is old.

David orders Solomon’s anointing, and Adonijah seeks protection.

## 1 Kings 1:1

King David was very old. 📖

Blankets could not keep him warm.

## 1 Kings 1:2

His servants suggested finding a young virgin to attend him, care for him, and lie beside him for warmth. 📖

**Help with virgin:** A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

## 1 Kings 1:3

They searched all Israel for a beautiful young woman and brought Abishag the Shunammite to the king. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:4

Abishag was exceptionally beautiful and served and cared for David. 📖

He did not have sex with her.

## 1 Kings 1:5

Adonijah, David’s son by Haggith, exalted himself, saying, “I will be king!” 📖

He got chariots, horsemen, and 50 men to run ahead.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses.

## 1 Kings 1:6

His father had never challenged his behaviour. 📖

Adonijah was very handsome and was born next after Absalom.

## 1 Kings 1:7

Adonijah consulted Joab son of Zeruiah and Abiathar the priest, who supported him. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:8

Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David’s mighty men did not join. 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## 1 Kings 1:9

Adonijah sacrificed sheep, oxen, and fattened calves by Zoheleth’s stone near En-rogel. 📖

He invited all his royal brothers and all Judah’s men serving the king.

**Help with sacrificed:** Sacrificed means killed animals as offerings to God.

## 1 Kings 1:10

He did not invite Nathan, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:11

Nathan told Solomon’s mother Bathsheba, “Have you heard? 📖

Adonijah son of Haggith became king without David knowing.

## 1 Kings 1:12

Let me advise you to save your life and Solomon’s. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:13

Go to King David and ask about his oath. 📖

Did he not swear that your son Solomon would reign after him on his throne?

Why is Adonijah king instead?

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise.

## 1 Kings 1:14

While you speak, I will come and confirm your words.” 📖

## 1 Kings 1:15

Bathsheba entered David’s bedroom. 📖

Abishag the Shunammite attended the very old king.

## 1 Kings 1:16

Bathsheba bowed in respect. 📖

The king asked what she wanted.

## 1 Kings 1:17

She answered, “My lord, you swore by the LORD your God that my son Solomon would reign on your throne. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:18

Now Adonijah became king without you knowing. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:19

He sacrificed many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep. 📖

He invited your other sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the army commander, but not Solomon.

## 1 Kings 1:20

All Israel looks to you to name your successor on the throne. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:21

Otherwise, when you rest with your ancestors, Solomon and I will be treated as criminals.” 📖

**Help with rest with your ancestors:** This means die.

## 1 Kings 1:22

While Bathsheba still spoke, Nathan the prophet arrived. 📖

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## 1 Kings 1:23

The king heard Nathan was there. 📖

Nathan entered and bowed face down.

## 1 Kings 1:24

Nathan asked, “My lord the king, did you declare Adonijah would reign after you on your throne? 📖

## 1 Kings 1:25

Today he sacrificed many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep. 📖

He invited your sons, army commanders, and Abiathar the priest.

They eat and drink before him, saying, ‘Long live King Adonijah!’

## 1 Kings 1:26

He did not invite me, Zadok, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or Solomon. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:27

Did you allow this without telling your servant who would succeed you?” 📖

## 1 Kings 1:28

David called for Bathsheba. 📖

She came and stood before him.

## 1 Kings 1:29

The king swore, “As surely as the LORD lives, who rescued my life from every trouble, 📖

## 1 Kings 1:30

I will fulfil my oath to you today. 📖

I swore by the LORD, Israel’s God, that Solomon would reign on my throne in my place.”

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise.

## 1 Kings 1:31

Bathsheba bowed face down and said, “May my lord King David live forever!” 📖

## 1 Kings 1:32

David called Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada. 📖

They came before him.

## 1 Kings 1:33

David ordered, “Take my servants, put Solomon on my own mule, and go to Gihon. 📖

**Help with mule:** A mule is an animal with a horse and a donkey as parents.

## 1 Kings 1:34

Zadok and Nathan must anoint him king over Israel there. 📖

Blow the ram’s horn and proclaim, ‘Long live King Solomon!’

**Help with anoint:** Anoint means mark someone for a role by putting oil on them.

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

## 1 Kings 1:35

Follow him up, and he will sit on my throne and reign in my place. 📖

I appointed him ruler over Israel and Judah.”

## 1 Kings 1:36

Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered, “Amen. 📖

May the LORD, your God, declare it too.

**Help with Amen:** Amen expresses agreement.

## 1 Kings 1:37

May the LORD be with Solomon as with you. 📖

May he make Solomon’s throne greater than King David’s.”

## 1 Kings 1:38

Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada went down with the Cherethites and Pelethites. 📖

They put Solomon on King David’s mule and escorted him to Gihon.

## 1 Kings 1:39

Zadok took the oil horn from the tabernacle and anointed Solomon. 📖

They blew the ram’s horn, and everyone proclaimed, “Long live King Solomon!”

**Help with tabernacle:** This was a tent used for worship.

**Help with anointed:** Anointed means marked for a role by the use of oil.

## 1 Kings 1:40

Everyone followed, playing flutes and rejoicing so loudly that the earth split with the sound. 📖

**About the earth:** This strong description of the celebration is retained from the source.

## 1 Kings 1:41

Adonijah and his guests heard the horn as they finished feasting. 📖

Joab asked, “Why is the city in such an uproar?”

## 1 Kings 1:42

Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. 📖

Adonijah said, “Come in, brave man!

You must bring good news.”

## 1 Kings 1:43

Jonathan answered, “No! 📖

King David made Solomon king.

## 1 Kings 1:44

The king sent Zadok, Nathan, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites with Solomon on the king’s mule. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:45

Zadok and Nathan anointed him at Gihon. 📖

They returned rejoicing, and the city rings with the noise you hear.

**Help with anointed:** Anointed means marked for a role by the use of oil.

## 1 Kings 1:46

Solomon has taken the royal throne. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:47

The king’s servants congratulated David. 📖

They said, ‘May your God make Solomon’s name more famous and his throne greater than yours.’

David bowed in worship on his bed.

## 1 Kings 1:48

He said, ‘Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God! 📖

Today he gave someone to sit on my throne, and I saw it.’”

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed expresses praise to God.

## 1 Kings 1:49

Adonijah’s guests rose in terror and scattered. 📖

## 1 Kings 1:50

Adonijah feared Solomon and took hold of the altar’s horns. 📖

**Help with horns:** These were projecting corners on the altar, a place for offerings.

Holding them was an appeal for protection.

## 1 Kings 1:51

Solomon heard, “Adonijah fears you and holds the altar’s horns. 📖

He asks you first to swear not to kill your servant with the sword.”

## 1 Kings 1:52

Solomon answered, “If he shows good character, not one hair will fall. 📖

If evil is found in him, he will die.”

## 1 Kings 1:53

Solomon called Adonijah down from the altar. 📖

Adonijah bowed before Solomon, who told him, “Go home.”
