# 1 Kings 11 — Solomon turns away and faces enemies

**About this chapter**

Solomon follows other gods, and the LORD announces a divided kingdom.

Hadad, Rezon, and Jeroboam oppose Solomon.

## 1 Kings 11:1

Besides Pharaoh’s daughter, Solomon loved many foreign women: Moabite, Ammonite, Edomite, Sidonian, and Hittite. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:2

The LORD had forbidden marriage with these nations because they would turn Israel’s hearts to their gods. 📖

Solomon clung to these women in love.

## 1 Kings 11:3

He had 700 royal wives and 300 concubines. 📖

His wives turned his heart away.

**Help with concubines:** Concubines were women in marriage-like relationships with fewer rights than wives.

## 1 Kings 11:4

In old age, Solomon’s wives turned his heart to other gods. 📖

He was not wholehearted towards the LORD like David.

## 1 Kings 11:5

He followed Ashtoreth, the Sidonians’ goddess, and Milcom, the Ammonites’ abomination. 📖

**Help with abomination:** This means something regarded as deeply hateful.

## 1 Kings 11:6

Solomon did evil before the LORD and did not follow him completely like David. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:7

On a hill east of Jerusalem, Solomon built high places for Moab’s abomination Chemosh and the Ammonites’ abomination Molech. 📖

**Help with high places:** These were raised places used for worship.

## 1 Kings 11:8

He did this for all his foreign wives, who burned incense and sacrificed to their gods. 📖

**Help with incense:** Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

## 1 Kings 11:9

The LORD became angry because Solomon turned from Israel’s God, who appeared twice to him. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:10

The LORD explicitly warned against other gods, but Solomon disobeyed. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:11

The LORD told Solomon, “Because you did this and broke my covenant and laws, I will tear your kingdom away. 📖

I commanded you to keep them.

I will give the kingdom to your servant.

## 1 Kings 11:12

For David’s sake, I will do it in your son’s lifetime, not yours. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:13

I will leave your son 1 tribe for David’s sake and for my chosen Jerusalem.” 📖

**Help with tribe:** A tribe is a large family group.

## 1 Kings 11:14

The LORD raised Hadad, of Edom’s royal family, as Solomon’s enemy. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:15

Earlier, David was in Edom, and Joab went to bury the dead and killed every male there. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:16

Joab and all Israel stayed 6 months until he had killed every male in Edom. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:17

Hadad escaped to Egypt as a young boy, with Edomite servants of his father. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:18

They travelled from Midian to Paran, taking men from Paran to Egypt. 📖

Pharaoh gave Hadad a house, land, and food.

## 1 Kings 11:19

Pharaoh favoured Hadad and married him to Queen Tahpenes’s sister. 📖

Tahpenes was Pharaoh’s wife.

## 1 Kings 11:20

She bore Hadad a son, Genubath. 📖

Tahpenes weaned him in Pharaoh’s palace, and he lived among Pharaoh’s sons.

**Help with weaned:** Weaned means brought from milk feeding to other food.

## 1 Kings 11:21

In Egypt, Hadad heard that David had died, as had Joab, the army commander. 📖

He asked Pharaoh, “Let me return to my own country.”

## 1 Kings 11:22

Pharaoh asked, “What have you lacked to want to leave?” 📖

Hadad answered, “Nothing, but please let me go.”

## 1 Kings 11:23

God raised another enemy, Rezon son of Eliada. 📖

He had fled his master Hadadezer king of Zobah.

## 1 Kings 11:24

When David killed the Zobaites, Rezon gathered raiders and became their leader. 📖

They settled in Damascus and took control.

## 1 Kings 11:25

Rezon opposed Israel throughout Solomon’s reign, adding to Hadad’s trouble. 📖

He ruled Aram with hostility towards Israel.

## 1 Kings 11:26

Jeroboam son of Nebat, an Ephraimite from Zeredah, rebelled against Solomon, whom he served. 📖

His mother was Zeruah, a widow.

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Kings 11:27

This is the account of his rebellion. 📖

Solomon built the supporting terraces and repaired the gap in David’s city wall.

## 1 Kings 11:28

Jeroboam was brave and hardworking. 📖

Solomon made him supervisor of Joseph’s family’s entire labour force.

## 1 Kings 11:29

Ahijah the Shilonite prophet met Jeroboam on the road outside Jerusalem. 📖

Ahijah wore a new cloak, and they were alone in the field.

**Help with prophet:** A prophet brings messages from God.

## 1 Kings 11:30

Ahijah tore his new cloak into 12 pieces. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:31

He told Jeroboam, “Take 10 pieces. 📖

The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I will tear the kingdom from Solomon and give you 10 tribes.

**Help with tribes:** Tribes are large family groups.

## 1 Kings 11:32

1 tribe will remain for David’s sake and Jerusalem, chosen from Israel’s tribes. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:33

They abandoned me to worship Ashtoreth, the Sidonians’ goddess, Chemosh, the Moabites’ god, and Milcom, the Ammonites’ god. 📖

They did not follow my ways, do right in my eyes, or keep my laws and judgments.

Solomon’s father David did these things.

## 1 Kings 11:34

I will not take Solomon’s whole kingdom during his life. 📖

For my chosen servant David, who obeyed me, I will keep Solomon ruler throughout his life.

## 1 Kings 11:35

I will take 10 tribes from his son and give them to you. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:36

I will give his son 1 tribe, keeping David’s lamp before me in Jerusalem, where I chose my Name. 📖

**Help with lamp:** This pictures David’s continuing royal family.

## 1 Kings 11:37

You will reign over everything you desire as Israel’s king. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:38

If you obey all my commands and laws, follow my ways, and do right as David did, I will help. 📖

I will be with you, establish your dynasty like David’s, and give you Israel.

**Help with dynasty:** A dynasty is a continuing line of rulers from one family.

**About this promise:** The promise depends on obedience.

## 1 Kings 11:39

I will humble David’s descendants, but not forever.’” 📖

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

## 1 Kings 11:40

Solomon tried to kill Jeroboam. 📖

Jeroboam fled to Shishak king of Egypt until Solomon died.

## 1 Kings 11:41

Solomon’s other deeds and wisdom are written in the Book of the Acts of Solomon. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:42

Solomon ruled all Israel from Jerusalem for 40 years. 📖

## 1 Kings 11:43

Solomon died and was buried in David’s city. 📖

His son Rehoboam ruled instead.
