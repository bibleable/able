# 1 Kings 10 — The queen of Sheba visits Solomon

**About this chapter**

The queen tests Solomon’s wisdom and exchanges gifts.

The chapter describes Solomon’s wealth, trade, and equipment.

## 1 Kings 10:1

Sheba’s queen heard of Solomon’s fame concerning the LORD’s name and came to test him with difficult questions. 📖

## 1 Kings 10:2

She brought a huge caravan to Jerusalem, with camels carrying spices, much gold, and precious stones. 📖

She told Solomon everything on her mind.

## 1 Kings 10:3

Solomon answered everything. 📖

Nothing was too difficult to explain.

## 1 Kings 10:4

When the queen of Sheba saw all Solomon’s wisdom and the palace he built, 📖

## 1 Kings 10:5

His table’s food, servants’ seating, attendants’ service and clothes, cupbearers, and burnt offerings at the LORD’s house. 📖

She was breathless with wonder.

**Help with cupbearers:** Cupbearers served drinks to the king.

## 1 Kings 10:6

She said, “The report in my country about your words and wisdom was true. 📖

## 1 Kings 10:7

I disbelieved until I saw for myself. 📖

Not even half was told!

Your wisdom and prosperity surpass the report.

## 1 Kings 10:8

Your men and servants are blessed to stand before you continually and hear your wisdom! 📖

## 1 Kings 10:9

Blessed be the LORD your God, who delighted to make you king of Israel! 📖

His eternal love for Israel made you king to carry out justice and righteousness.”

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed expresses praise to God.

**Help with justice and righteousness:** These mean fair and right conduct.

## 1 Kings 10:10

She gave Solomon 120 talents of gold, abundant spices, and precious stones. 📖

Never again were so many spices brought as her gift.

**Help with talent:** A talent was a measure of weight.

## 1 Kings 10:11

Hiram’s fleet brought much almug wood and precious stones from Ophir with the gold. 📖

**Help with almug:** Almug names a kind of valuable timber.

## 1 Kings 10:12

The king used almug wood for temple and palace steps and singers’ lyres and harps. 📖

Such wood had never been brought before or seen again to this day.

**Help with lyres and harps:** These are musical instruments with strings.

## 1 Kings 10:13

Solomon gave the queen everything she asked, besides his generous royal gifts. 📖

She returned home with her servants.

## 1 Kings 10:14

Solomon received 666 talents of gold yearly, 📖

**Help with talent:** A talent was a measure of weight.

## 1 Kings 10:15

Besides income from merchants, traders, all Arabian kings, and the land’s governors. 📖

## 1 Kings 10:16

Solomon made 200 large hammered-gold shields, each using 600 shekels of gold. 📖

**Help with shekels:** A shekel was a measure of weight.

## 1 Kings 10:17

He made 300 small hammered-gold shields, each using 3 minas. 📖

He kept them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.

**Help with minas:** A mina was a measure of weight.

## 1 Kings 10:18

He made a great ivory throne covered with pure gold. 📖

**Help with ivory:** Ivory comes from animals’ tusks.

## 1 Kings 10:19

The throne had 6 steps. 📖

Its back had a rounded top.

It had armrests on both sides, with a lion standing beside each armrest.

## 1 Kings 10:20

12 lions stood on the 6 steps, one at each end. 📖

No kingdom had anything like it.

## 1 Kings 10:21

All Solomon’s drinking cups were gold, and the House of the Forest of Lebanon’s utensils were pure gold. 📖

None was silver, which was considered worthless then.

## 1 Kings 10:22

Tarshish ships sailed with Hiram’s fleet. 📖

Every 3 years, they brought gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks.

## 1 Kings 10:23

Solomon surpassed every earthly king in wealth and wisdom. 📖

## 1 Kings 10:24

The whole world sought to hear the wisdom God put in his heart. 📖

## 1 Kings 10:25

Every year, visitors brought silver and gold objects, clothes, weapons, spices, horses, and mules. 📖

**Help with mules:** Mules are animals with a horse and a donkey as parents.

## 1 Kings 10:26

Solomon gathered 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horses in chariot cities and Jerusalem. 📖

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

## 1 Kings 10:27

Silver became as common as stones in Jerusalem, and cedar as abundant as sycamores in the foothills. 📖

**Help with cedar and sycamores:** These are kinds of trees.

## 1 Kings 10:28

His horses came from Egypt and Kue. 📖

Royal merchants bought them from Kue.

## 1 Kings 10:29

An Egyptian chariot cost 600 silver shekels, and a horse 150. 📖

They also exported them to Hittite and Aramean kings.

**Help with shekels:** A shekel was a measure of weight used in payment.
