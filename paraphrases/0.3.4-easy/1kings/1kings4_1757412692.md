# 1 Kings 4 — Solomon’s officials and wisdom

**About this chapter**

Solomon rules Israel with officials who provide for his household.

God gives him great wisdom.

## 1 Kings 4:1

King Solomon ruled all Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:2

These were his chief officials. 📖

Azariah son of Zadok was the priest.

## 1 Kings 4:3

Elihoreph and Ahijah, sons of Shisha, were secretaries. 📖

Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud kept the records.

## 1 Kings 4:4

Benaiah son of Jehoiada led the army. 📖

Zadok and Abiathar were priests.

## 1 Kings 4:5

Azariah son of Nathan led the governors. 📖

Zabud son of Nathan was a priest and the king's adviser.

## 1 Kings 4:6

Ahishar managed the palace. 📖

Adoniram son of Abda managed forced labour.

**Help with words:** Forced labour means work people are made to do.

## 1 Kings 4:7

Solomon had 12 governors over Israel. 📖

Each provided food for the king and his household for 1 month each year.

## 1 Kings 4:8

The governors included Ben-hur in Ephraim's hill country. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:9

Ben-deker governed Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:10

Ben-hesed governed Arubboth. 📖

His region included Socoh and all the land of Hepher.

## 1 Kings 4:11

Ben-abinadab governed Naphath-dor. 📖

He married Solomon's daughter Taphath.

## 1 Kings 4:12

Baana son of Ahilud governed Taanach, Megiddo, and all Beth-shean. 📖

This included land near Zarethan below Jezreel.

It stretched from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah and beyond Jokmeam.

## 1 Kings 4:13

Ben-geber governed Ramoth-gilead. 📖

His region included Jair son of Manasseh's villages in Gilead.

It also included Argob in Bashan, with 60 large cities protected by walls and bronze bars.

## 1 Kings 4:14

Ahinadab son of Iddo governed Mahanaim. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:15

Ahimaaz governed Naphtali. 📖

He married Solomon's daughter Basemath.

## 1 Kings 4:16

Baana son of Hushai governed Asher and Aloth. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:17

Jehoshaphat son of Paruah governed Issachar. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:18

Shimei son of Ela governed Benjamin. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:19

Geber son of Uri governed Gilead. 📖

This included former lands of Sihon king of the Amorites and Og king of Bashan.

There was also one governor in Judah.

## 1 Kings 4:20

Judah and Israel had as many people as sand beside the sea. 📖

They ate, drank, and were happy.

**Help with this picture:** The sand pictures a very large number of people.

## 1 Kings 4:21

Solomon ruled kingdoms from the Euphrates River to Philistine land and Egypt's border. 📖

They paid him tribute and served him throughout his life.

**Help with a word:** Tribute is payment made to a ruling king.

## 1 Kings 4:22

Each day, Solomon needed 30 cors of fine flour and 60 cors of meal. 📖

**Help with words:** A cor was a large measure for grain.

Meal is ground grain.

## 1 Kings 4:23

He also needed 10 fattened oxen, 20 pasture-fed oxen, and 100 sheep. 📖

There were also deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened birds.

## 1 Kings 4:24

Solomon ruled all kingdoms west of the Euphrates, from Tiphsah to Gaza. 📖

There was peace on every side.

## 1 Kings 4:25

During Solomon's reign, Judah and Israel lived safely from Dan to Beersheba. 📖

Everyone could sit under their own vine and fig tree.

## 1 Kings 4:26

Solomon had 4,000 stalls for chariot horses and 12,000 horses. 📖

**Help with a word:** A chariot was a horse-drawn vehicle used in war.

## 1 Kings 4:27

The governors took turns each month providing food for Solomon and everyone eating with him. 📖

They made sure nothing was missing.

## 1 Kings 4:28

Each governor also supplied the required barley and straw for the horses. 📖

They brought it to the proper place.

## 1 Kings 4:29

God gave Solomon great wisdom and deep understanding. 📖

His understanding was beyond measure, like sand beside the sea.

## 1 Kings 4:30

Solomon was wiser than all the people of the East and all the wise people of Egypt. 📖

## 1 Kings 4:31

He was wiser than everyone.

He was wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite and Mahol's sons Heman, Calcol, and Darda. 📖

His fame spread to the surrounding nations.

## 1 Kings 4:32

Solomon spoke 3,000 proverbs and wrote 1,005 songs. 📖

**Help with a word:** Proverbs are short sayings that teach wisdom.

## 1 Kings 4:33

He taught about trees, from Lebanon's cedars to hyssop growing in walls. 📖

He also taught about animals, birds, reptiles, and fish.

**Help with a word:** Hyssop is a small plant.

## 1 Kings 4:34

People from all nations came to hear Solomon's wisdom. 📖

Kings who had heard about him sent them.
