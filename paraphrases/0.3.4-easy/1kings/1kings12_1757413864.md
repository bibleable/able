# 1 Kings 12 — The kingdom divides

**About this chapter**

Rehoboam refuses the people’s request, and Israel chooses Jeroboam.

Jeroboam establishes worship at Bethel and Dan.

## 1 Kings 12:1

Rehoboam went to Shechem, where all Israel gathered to make him king. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:2

Jeroboam son of Nebat heard while still in Egypt, where he had fled Solomon. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:3

They called Jeroboam, and he and all Israel’s assembly spoke to Rehoboam. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:4

“Your father put a heavy yoke on us. 📖

Lighten his harsh service and heavy yoke, and we will serve you.”

**Help with yoke:** A yoke is a wooden frame for working animals.

Here, it pictures burdens placed on people.

## 1 Kings 12:5

Rehoboam sent them away for 3 days, then told them to return. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:6

Rehoboam consulted the elders who had served his father Solomon during his lifetime. 📖

“How do you advise me to answer these people?” he asked.

## 1 Kings 12:7

They said, “Serve these people today and answer kindly. 📖

If you do, they will serve you forever.”

## 1 Kings 12:8

Rehoboam rejected them and consulted young men who grew up with him and served him. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:9

He asked, “How should we answer these people? 📖

They asked me to lighten the yoke my father put on them.”

## 1 Kings 12:10

The young men who grew up with him answered, “These people want you to lighten your father’s heavy yoke. 📖

Tell them, ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist!

**Help with this picture:** He is boasting that he will be even harsher than his father.

## 1 Kings 12:11

He made your yoke heavy; I will add more. 📖

He whipped you; I will whip you with scorpions.’”

**Help with scorpions:** This threatening picture describes harsher punishment.

## 1 Kings 12:12

After 3 days, Jeroboam and all the people returned to Rehoboam. 📖

They came because the king had told them to return on the 3rd day.

## 1 Kings 12:13

The king answered harshly, rejecting the elders’ advice. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:14

He repeated the young men’s advice. 📖

“I will add to my father’s heavy yoke.

He whipped you; I will whip you with scorpions.”

**Help with yoke and scorpions:** These pictures threaten heavier burdens and harsher punishment.

## 1 Kings 12:15

The king refused to listen. 📖

This turn came from the LORD to fulfil Ahijah the Shilonite’s word to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

## 1 Kings 12:16

Israel replied, “What share do we have in David or inheritance in Jesse’s son? 📖

Israel, go to your tents!

David, look to your own house!”

Israel went home.

## 1 Kings 12:17

Rehoboam still ruled Israelites living in Judah’s cities. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:18

Rehoboam sent Adoram, the forced-labour manager, but all Israel stoned him dead. 📖

Rehoboam hurried into his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem.

**Help with stoned:** Stoned means killed by throwing stones.

## 1 Kings 12:19

Israel has rebelled against David’s family to this day. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:20

Israel heard Jeroboam had returned and made him king at the assembly. 📖

Only Judah’s tribe followed David’s family.

## 1 Kings 12:21

Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem and gathered all Judah and Benjamin, 180,000 chosen warriors. 📖

They would fight Israel and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam son of Solomon.

## 1 Kings 12:22

God’s word came to Shemaiah, the man of God. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:23

“Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon, Judah’s king, all Judah and Benjamin, and the other people: 📖

## 1 Kings 12:24

The LORD forbids fighting your brothers, the Israelites. 📖

Go home, because this is my doing.”

They obeyed and returned.

## 1 Kings 12:25

Jeroboam built Shechem in Ephraim’s hills and lived there. 📖

He also built Penuel.

## 1 Kings 12:26

Jeroboam thought, “The kingdom may return to David’s family. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:27

If they sacrifice at Jerusalem’s temple, their hearts will return to Rehoboam king of Judah. 📖

They will kill me and return to him.”

## 1 Kings 12:28

After advice, Jeroboam made 2 gold calves. 📖

He said, “Jerusalem is too far for you.

Here are your gods, Israel, who brought you from Egypt.”

**About the claim:** These are Jeroboam’s words about the calves.

## 1 Kings 12:29

He put one calf at Bethel, the other at Dan. 📖

## 1 Kings 12:30

This became a sin. 📖

People travelled as far as Dan to worship a calf.

**Help with sin:** Sin means wrongdoing against God.

## 1 Kings 12:31

Jeroboam built high-place shrines and chose priests from every class, though they were not Levites. 📖

**Help with shrines:** Shrines are places of worship.

**Help with Levites:** Levites were members of Levi’s tribe who served in worship.

## 1 Kings 12:32

He established a feast like Judah’s on day 15 of month 8. 📖

At Bethel, he sacrificed to the calves and appointed priests for his high places.

## 1 Kings 12:33

On day 15 of month 8, his own chosen month, he sacrificed at Bethel’s altar. 📖

He established Israel’s feast, sacrificed, and burned incense.

**Help with incense:** Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.
