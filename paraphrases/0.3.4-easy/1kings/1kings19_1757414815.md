# 1 Kings 19 — Elijah at Horeb and Elisha’s call

**About this chapter**

Elijah flees Jezebel and asks to die.

He receives food, hears the LORD at Horeb, and calls Elisha.

## 1 Kings 19:1

Ahab told Jezebel everything Elijah did, including killing the prophets with the sword. 📖

## 1 Kings 19:2

Jezebel sent a threat: “May the gods punish me severely unless you are like those dead prophets by tomorrow!” 📖

## 1 Kings 19:3

Elijah feared and fled to Beersheba in Judah, leaving his servant. 📖

## 1 Kings 19:4

He travelled one day into the wilderness and sat under a broom tree. 📖

He prayed to die.

“Enough, LORD!

Take my life.

I am no better than my ancestors.”

**Help with broom tree:** This is a bush providing shade.

## 1 Kings 19:5

He lay asleep there. 📖

An angel touched him and said, “Rise and eat.”

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

## 1 Kings 19:6

By his head lay bread baked on hot coals and water. 📖

He ate, drank, and lay down.

## 1 Kings 19:7

The LORD’s angel touched him again. 📖

“Eat, or the journey will overwhelm you.”

## 1 Kings 19:8

He ate and drank. 📖

That food strengthened him for 40 days and nights to Horeb, God’s mountain.

## 1 Kings 19:9

He spent the night in a cave. 📖

The LORD asked, “What are you doing here, Elijah?”

## 1 Kings 19:10

Elijah answered, “I served the LORD, God of Hosts, zealously. 📖

Israel abandoned your covenant, destroyed your altars, and killed your prophets.

I alone remain, and they seek my life.”

**Help with zealously:** Zealously means with strong commitment.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Kings 19:11

The LORD told him to stand on the mountain before the passing LORD. 📖

A mighty wind tore mountains and shattered rocks, but the LORD was not in it.

An earthquake followed, but the LORD was not in it.

## 1 Kings 19:12

Fire followed, but the LORD was not in it. 📖

Then came a still, small voice.

## 1 Kings 19:13

Elijah covered his face with his cloak and stood at the cave’s entrance. 📖

A voice asked again what he was doing there.

## 1 Kings 19:14

Elijah repeated, “I zealously served the LORD, God of Hosts. 📖

Israel abandoned your covenant, destroyed altars, and killed prophets.

I alone remain, and they seek my life.”

**Help with God of Hosts:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 1 Kings 19:15

The LORD said, “Return by your route to Damascus’s Desert and anoint Hazael king of Aram. 📖

**Help with anoint:** Anoint means mark someone for a role by putting oil on them.

## 1 Kings 19:16

Anoint Jehu son of Nimshi king of Israel, and Elisha son of Shaphat from Abel-meholah as prophet after you. 📖

## 1 Kings 19:17

Jehu will kill whoever escapes Hazael’s sword; Elisha will kill whoever escapes Jehu’s. 📖

## 1 Kings 19:18

I reserved 7,000 Israelites whose knees never bowed to Baal and mouths never kissed him.” 📖

## 1 Kings 19:19

Elijah found Elisha son of Shaphat ploughing with 12 teams of oxen, himself at the 12th. 📖

Elijah threw his cloak around him.

**Help with oxen:** Oxen are large cattle used for work.

## 1 Kings 19:20

Elisha ran after him, asking to kiss his parents goodbye before following. 📖

Elijah answered, “Go back, for what have I done to you?”

## 1 Kings 19:21

Elisha slaughtered his pair of oxen and cooked their meat using their equipment as fuel. 📖

He fed the people, then followed and served Elijah.
