# 1 Kings 8 — The ark enters the temple and Solomon prays

**About this chapter**

Solomon gathers Israel and dedicates the temple.

His prayer asks the LORD to hear, judge, forgive, and rescue.

## 1 Kings 8:1

Solomon gathered Israel’s elders, tribal heads, and family leaders in Jerusalem. 📖

They would bring the LORD’s covenant ark from Zion, the City of David.

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

## 1 Kings 8:2

All Israel’s men gathered at the feast in Ethanim, the 7th month. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:3

All the elders arrived, and the priests lifted the ark. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:4

Priests and Levites carried the LORD’s ark, Tent of Meeting, and all its sacred furnishings. 📖

**Help with Levites:** Levites were members of Levi’s tribe who served in worship.

**Help with Tent of Meeting:** This was a tent associated with meeting God in worship.

## 1 Kings 8:5

Solomon and all gathered Israel sacrificed countless sheep and oxen before the ark. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:6

The priests placed the LORD’s covenant ark in the inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place, beneath the cherubim’s wings. 📖

**Help with cherubim:** Cherubim are heavenly beings represented by these figures.

## 1 Kings 8:7

The wings spread over and sheltered the ark and its poles. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:8

The long poles’ ends could be seen from the Holy Place before the inner sanctuary, but not outside. 📖

They remain there today.

## 1 Kings 8:9

The ark held only the 2 stone tablets Moses placed there at Horeb. 📖

There, after Israel left Egypt, the LORD made a covenant with them.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Kings 8:10

The priests left the Holy Place, and a cloud filled the LORD’s house. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:11

The cloud prevented the priests from standing to serve. 📖

The LORD’s glory filled the house.

**Help with glory:** Glory means greatness and honour.

## 1 Kings 8:12

Solomon declared, “The LORD said he would dwell in the thick cloud. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:13

I built you an exalted house to live in forever.” 📖

**Help with exalted:** Exalted means raised high in honour.

## 1 Kings 8:14

The king turned and blessed all Israel’s standing assembly. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:15

He said, “Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God! 📖

His own hand fulfilled what he told David.

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed expresses praise to God.

## 1 Kings 8:16

He said, ‘Since bringing Israel from Egypt, I chose no tribal city for a house bearing my Name. 📖

But I chose David over my people Israel.’

## 1 Kings 8:17

David desired to build a house for the LORD, Israel’s God’s Name. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:18

The LORD told him, ‘Your desire to build a house for my Name is good. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:19

But you will not build the house for my Name. 📖

Your own son will build it.’

## 1 Kings 8:20

The LORD fulfilled his word. 📖

I succeeded David on Israel’s throne as promised and built the house for the LORD, Israel’s God’s Name.

## 1 Kings 8:21

I provided a place for the ark containing the LORD’s covenant with our ancestors when he brought them from Egypt.” 📖

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Kings 8:22

Solomon stood before the LORD’s altar facing all Israel and spread his hands towards heaven. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:23

He prayed, “LORD, Israel’s God, none is like you in heaven or earth. 📖

You keep your covenant of faithful love with servants following you wholeheartedly.

**Help with covenant:** A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Kings 8:24

You kept your promise to my father David. 📖

Your hand fulfilled today what your mouth spoke.

## 1 Kings 8:25

Keep your promise to David, LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

You promised an unbroken line on Israel’s throne if his descendants carefully followed you as he did.

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

## 1 Kings 8:26

God of Israel, confirm your promise to my father David. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:27

Will God really dwell on earth? 📖

Even the highest heavens cannot contain you, much less my temple.

## 1 Kings 8:28

Yet hear your servant’s prayer and plea, LORD my God. 📖

Hear my cry and prayer before you today.

## 1 Kings 8:29

Watch this temple night and day, where you promised your Name would be. 📖

Hear my prayer towards this place.

## 1 Kings 8:30

Hear your servant and Israel praying towards this place. 📖

Hear from heaven, your home, and forgive.

## 1 Kings 8:31

When someone sins against a neighbour and must take an oath, he comes before your altar here. 📖

**Help with oath:** An oath is a serious promise to tell the truth.

## 1 Kings 8:32

Then hear from heaven and judge. 📖

Bring the wicked man’s actions on his head, and reward the righteous man according to his righteousness.

**Help with righteous:** Righteous means right before God.

## 1 Kings 8:33

When enemies defeat Israel because they sinned, and they return, confess your name, and pray and plead here, 📖

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong against God.

## 1 Kings 8:34

hear from heaven and forgive. 📖

Restore Israel to the land you gave their ancestors.

## 1 Kings 8:35

When their sin brings closed skies and no rain, they may pray here, confess your name, and turn from sin. 📖

They turn because you afflicted them.

**Help with sin:** Sin means wrongdoing against God.

## 1 Kings 8:36

Then hear from heaven and forgive your servants, Israel. 📖

Teach them the good way to live and send rain on the land you gave as their inheritance.

**Help with inheritance:** Inheritance is a possession given to them.

## 1 Kings 8:37

When famine, plague, blight, mildew, locusts, grasshoppers, enemy sieges, or any sickness strike the land, 📖

**Help with famine:** Famine means a severe shortage of food.

**Help with blight and mildew:** These damage crops.

**Help with siege:** A siege surrounds a city and cuts off supplies.

## 1 Kings 8:38

your people may pray and plead, each knowing their own suffering and spreading hands towards this temple. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:39

Then hear from heaven, your home, forgive, and act. 📖

Repay each person according to their conduct, because only you know every human heart.

## 1 Kings 8:40

Then they may fear you throughout their lives in the land you gave our ancestors. 📖

**Help with fear:** Here, fear includes deep respect for God.

## 1 Kings 8:41

When a foreigner outside Israel comes from a distant land because of your name, 📖

## 1 Kings 8:42

he will have heard of your great name, mighty hand, and outstretched arm. 📖

When he prays towards this temple,

**Help with hand and arm:** These picture God’s power.

## 1 Kings 8:43

hear from heaven, your home, and grant everything he asks. 📖

Then all earth’s peoples will know and fear your name like Israel.

They will know this house bears your Name.

## 1 Kings 8:44

Suppose your people go to war against their enemies, wherever you send them. 📖

Suppose they pray to the LORD towards your chosen city and this house I built for your Name,

## 1 Kings 8:45

Then hear their prayers and pleas from heaven and uphold their cause. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:46

When they sin, as everyone does, you may become angry and hand them to enemies as captives, near or far. 📖

**Help with captives:** Captives are people held under another power.

## 1 Kings 8:47

Suppose they come to their senses in the land where they were taken. 📖

Suppose they turn from sin and plead with you there.

They say, ‘We have sinned and done wrong.

We have acted wickedly.’

## 1 Kings 8:48

Suppose they return to you with all their heart and soul in their enemies’ land.

Suppose they pray towards their ancestors’ land, your chosen city, and this house for your Name. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:49

hear their prayers and pleas from heaven, your home, and uphold their cause. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:50

Forgive all their sins and wrongs against you. 📖

Move their captors to show compassion and mercy.

## 1 Kings 8:51

They are your people and inheritance, whom you brought from Egypt’s iron furnace. 📖

**Help with iron furnace:** This pictures Egypt’s harsh oppression.

## 1 Kings 8:52

Watch for your servant’s and Israel’s pleas. 📖

Listen whenever they call.

## 1 Kings 8:53

Lord GOD, you set them apart from all peoples as your inheritance. 📖

You spoke through Moses when you brought our ancestors from Egypt.”

## 1 Kings 8:54

Solomon finished the whole prayer. 📖

He rose from kneeling before the LORD’s altar, his hands spread towards heaven.

## 1 Kings 8:55

He stood and loudly blessed all Israel’s assembly. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:56

“Blessed be the LORD, who gave Israel rest as promised! 📖

Not one good promise through Moses failed.

**Help with blessed:** Here, blessed expresses praise to God.

## 1 Kings 8:57

May our God be with us as with our ancestors, never leaving or abandoning us. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:58

May he turn our hearts towards himself to follow all his ways and obey all commands given our ancestors. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:59

May my prayers remain near our God day and night. 📖

May he uphold his servant’s and Israel’s cause as each day requires,

## 1 Kings 8:60

so all peoples know the LORD is God. 📖

There is no other!

## 1 Kings 8:61

Be fully devoted to the LORD our God, as today, following his laws and commands.” 📖

## 1 Kings 8:62

The king and all Israel sacrificed before the LORD. 📖

## 1 Kings 8:63

Solomon gave 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep as peace offerings. 📖

The king and Israel dedicated the LORD’s house.

**Help with dedicated:** Dedicated means set apart for God.

## 1 Kings 8:64

The king set apart the middle courtyard for burnt offerings, grain offerings, and peace offerings’ fat. 📖

The bronze altar was too small for them all.

**Help with offerings:** These were gifts given in worship, with burnt offerings burned on an altar.

## 1 Kings 8:65

Solomon and Israel celebrated before the LORD our God for 7 days and 7 more, 14 in all. 📖

The great gathering came from Lebo-hamath to Egypt’s Brook.

## 1 Kings 8:66

On day 15, Solomon sent them home. 📖

They blessed him and left joyful over all the LORD’s goodness to David and Israel.
