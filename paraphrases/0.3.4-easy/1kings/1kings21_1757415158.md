# 1 Kings 21 — Naboth’s vineyard

**About this chapter**

Jezebel arranges false testimony and Naboth’s death.

Elijah confronts Ahab, who then humbles himself.

## 1 Kings 21:1

Naboth the Jezreelite owned a vineyard beside Ahab king of Samaria’s palace in Jezreel. 📖

**Help with vineyard:** A vineyard is a place where grapes grow.

## 1 Kings 21:2

Ahab asked for it as a vegetable garden. 📖

He offered a better vineyard or its value in silver, according to Naboth’s choice.

## 1 Kings 21:3

Naboth answered, “The LORD forbid giving away my ancestors’ inheritance.” 📖

**Help with inheritance:** Inheritance is property passed on in a family.

## 1 Kings 21:4

Ahab returned to his palace, resentful and angry because Naboth the Jezreelite refused him. 📖

Naboth had said, “I will not give you my ancestors’ inheritance.”

Ahab lay on his bed, turned his face away, and refused to eat.

## 1 Kings 21:5

Jezebel asked why he was so sullen he would not eat. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:6

Ahab answered, “I told Naboth the Jezreelite to give me his vineyard for silver. 📖

I offered another vineyard instead if he preferred.

He said, ‘I will not give you my vineyard!’”

## 1 Kings 21:7

Jezebel said, “Do you not rule Israel? 📖

Eat and be cheerful.

I will get Naboth’s vineyard.”

## 1 Kings 21:8

She wrote letters in Ahab’s name, sealed them with his seal, and sent them to Naboth’s city’s elders and nobles. 📖

**Help with seal:** A seal makes a mark showing authority.

## 1 Kings 21:9

She ordered a fast, with Naboth seated in honour. 📖

**Help with fast:** A fast is a time of not eating.

## 1 Kings 21:10

“Put 2 scoundrels opposite him to accuse him of cursing God and the king. 📖

Then take him out and stone him dead.”

**Help with stone:** Stone means kill by throwing stones.

**About the accusation:** Jezebel arranges false witnesses.

## 1 Kings 21:11

The elders and nobles of Naboth’s city did as Jezebel instructed in her letters to them. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:12

They proclaimed the fast and honoured Naboth’s seat. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:13

2 scoundrels sat opposite Naboth and testified against him before the people. 📖

They said, “Naboth cursed both God and the king!”

They took him outside the city and stoned him to death.

**About this accusation:** The preceding verses show that Jezebel arranged this false testimony.

## 1 Kings 21:14

They reported Naboth’s death to Jezebel. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:15

When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned to death, she told Ahab, “Get up! 📖

Take Naboth the Jezreelite’s vineyard, which he refused to give you for silver.

He is no longer alive.

He is dead.”

## 1 Kings 21:16

When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he got up and went down. 📖

He went to take possession of Naboth the Jezreelite’s vineyard.

## 1 Kings 21:17

The LORD’s word came to Elijah the Tishbite. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:18

“Meet Ahab king of Israel, in Samaria, at Naboth’s vineyard, where he has gone to take possession. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:19

Tell Ahab, ‘The LORD asks, “Have you murdered a man and seized his land?”’ 📖

Also tell him, ‘The LORD says dogs will lick your own blood where they licked Naboth’s blood.’”

## 1 Kings 21:20

Ahab told Elijah, “You found me, my enemy!” 📖

Elijah answered, “I have found you because you sold yourself to evil before the LORD.

**Help with sold yourself:** This pictures giving oneself over to wrongdoing.

## 1 Kings 21:21

The LORD will destroy your descendants and every male of Ahab in Israel, slave and free. 📖

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

## 1 Kings 21:22

He will make your family like Jeroboam son of Nebat’s and Baasha son of Ahijah’s. 📖

This is because you angered him and caused Israel to sin.

## 1 Kings 21:23

The LORD says dogs will eat Jezebel by Jezreel’s wall. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:24

Dogs will eat Ahab’s people dying in the city, and birds those dying in the field.” 📖

## 1 Kings 21:25

None matched Ahab in selling himself to evil before the LORD, urged on by Jezebel. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:26

He followed idols in hateful acts like the Amorites whom the LORD drove out before Israel. 📖

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## 1 Kings 21:27

Ahab tore his clothes, wore sackcloth, fasted, lay in sackcloth, and walked humbly. 📖

**Help with sackcloth:** Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## 1 Kings 21:28

The LORD spoke to Elijah the Tishbite. 📖

## 1 Kings 21:29

“Have you seen Ahab humble himself before me? 📖

Because of this, disaster will come to his family in his son’s days, not his own.”
