# 1 Kings 13 — A man of God at Bethel

**About this chapter**

A man of God speaks against Jeroboam’s altar.

An old prophet deceives him, and he later dies on the road.

## 1 Kings 13:1

Jeroboam stood by Bethel’s altar burning incense. 📖

By the LORD’s word, a man of God came from Judah.

**Help with incense:** Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

## 1 Kings 13:2

He cried against the altar, “The LORD says David’s family will have a son named Josiah. 📖

On you he will sacrifice the high-place priests who burn incense, and human bones will burn here.”

## 1 Kings 13:3

He gave a sign: “The LORD says the altar will split, spilling its ashes.” 📖

## 1 Kings 13:4

Jeroboam heard, stretched out his hand, and ordered, “Seize him!” 📖

His hand withered, and he could not draw it back.

**Help with withered:** Here, withered describes the hand becoming unable to work.

## 1 Kings 13:5

The altar split and spilled its ashes as the LORD’s sign said. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:6

The king asked the man to pray to the LORD his God to restore his hand. 📖

The man prayed, and the hand became as before.

## 1 Kings 13:7

The king invited him home for refreshment and a reward. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:8

The man refused. 📖

“Even half your possessions would not make me go or eat bread or drink water here.

## 1 Kings 13:9

The LORD commanded me not to eat, drink, or return by the same way.” 📖

## 1 Kings 13:10

He left Bethel by another road. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:11

An old prophet lived in Bethel. 📖

His sons told him everything the man of God did that day in Bethel.

They also told him what the man said to the king.

## 1 Kings 13:12

The father asked which road he took. 📖

His sons showed the Judahite man of God’s route.

## 1 Kings 13:13

The old prophet ordered his donkey saddled and mounted it. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:14

He followed and found the man under an oak. 📖

He asked, “Are you the man of God from Judah?”

He answered, “I am.”

## 1 Kings 13:15

The prophet invited him home to eat bread. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:16

The man said, “I cannot return or eat bread or drink water with you here. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:17

The LORD told me not to eat, drink, or return by the same road.” 📖

## 1 Kings 13:18

The prophet said, “I too am a prophet. 📖

An angel gave me the LORD’s word to bring you home for bread and water.”

He was lying.

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

**About the claim:** The narrator explicitly identifies this statement as a lie.

## 1 Kings 13:19

The man of God returned and ate bread and drank water in his house. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:20

At the table, the LORD’s word came to the prophet who brought him back. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:21

The prophet cried to the man from Judah, “The LORD says you defied his word and disobeyed his command. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:22

You returned to eat and drink where forbidden. 📖

Your body will never reach your ancestors’ tomb.”

## 1 Kings 13:23

After the man finished eating and drinking, the old prophet saddled the donkey for him. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:24

A lion met and killed him on the road. 📖

His body lay there, with the donkey and lion beside it.

## 1 Kings 13:25

Passers-by saw the body and lion and reported it in the old prophet’s city. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:26

The old prophet heard and said, “The man of God disobeyed the LORD. 📖

The LORD gave him to the lion, which mauled and killed him as the LORD said.”

## 1 Kings 13:27

The old prophet ordered his sons to saddle the donkey, and they did. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:28

He found the body with the donkey and lion beside it. 📖

The lion had neither eaten the body nor mauled the donkey.

## 1 Kings 13:29

He put the body on the donkey and brought it to his city to grieve and bury it. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:30

He placed the man in his own tomb. 📖

They grieved, “My brother!”

## 1 Kings 13:31

After burial, he told his sons, “When I die, bury my bones beside his in this tomb. 📖

## 1 Kings 13:32

His message from the LORD against Bethel’s altar and all the high-place shrines in Samaria’s cities will certainly happen.” 📖

## 1 Kings 13:33

Jeroboam still did not turn from evil. 📖

He again appointed high-place priests from every class, accepting anyone who wanted the role.

## 1 Kings 13:34

This sin brought Jeroboam’s family’s complete destruction from the earth. 📖

**Help with sin:** Sin means wrongdoing against God.
