# 1 Kings 22 — Ahab rejects the warning

**About this chapter**

Ahab, king of Israel, joins Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, in battle.

The prophet Micaiah warns Ahab about what will happen.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## 1 Kings 22:1

Aram and Israel had no war for 3 years. 📖

## 1 Kings 22:2

In year 3, King Jehoshaphat of Judah visited the king of Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 22:3

Israel's king told his servants, “Ramoth-gilead belongs to us. 📖

Why have we not taken it back from Aram's king?”

## 1 Kings 22:4

He asked Jehoshaphat to join him in battle against Ramoth-gilead. 📖

Jehoshaphat said, “I am with you.

My people and horses are yours.”

## 1 Kings 22:5

Jehoshaphat added, “First, please ask for the LORD's message.” 📖

## 1 Kings 22:6

Israel’s king gathered about 400 prophets. 📖

He asked, “Should I attack Ramoth-gilead, or hold back?”

They answered, “Go.

The Lord will give it to the king.”

## 1 Kings 22:7

Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there another prophet of the LORD we can ask?” 📖

## 1 Kings 22:8

Israel's king said, “There is Micaiah son of Imlah. 📖

But I hate him.

He only speaks bad messages about me, never good ones.”

Jehoshaphat said, “The king should not say that!”

## 1 Kings 22:9

Israel's king ordered an official to bring Micaiah son of Imlah immediately. 📖

## 1 Kings 22:10

Both kings sat on thrones in royal robes near Samaria's gate. 📖

They were on a threshing floor.

All the prophets spoke before them.

**Help with words:** A threshing floor was a place for separating grain from stalks.

## 1 Kings 22:11

Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns. 📖

He said, “The LORD says you will gore the Arameans with these until they are destroyed.”

**Help with gore:** This means stab with horns, like an animal attacking.

## 1 Kings 22:12

All the prophets said the same. 📖

“Attack Ramoth-gilead and win.

The LORD will give it to the king.”

## 1 Kings 22:13

The messenger told Micaiah, “All the prophets give the king good news. 📖

Please agree and speak good news too.”

## 1 Kings 22:14

Micaiah said, “As surely as the LORD lives, I will speak only what the LORD tells me.” 📖

## 1 Kings 22:15

The king asked Micaiah whether to attack Ramoth-gilead. 📖

Micaiah answered, “Attack and win.

The LORD will give it to the king.”

## 1 Kings 22:16

The king said, “How often must I make you promise to speak only the LORD's truth?” 📖

## 1 Kings 22:17

Micaiah said, “I saw all Israel scattered on the hills like sheep without a shepherd. 📖

The LORD said they had no master.

Each should return home in peace.”

## 1 Kings 22:18

Israel's king told Jehoshaphat, “I said he only gives me bad messages!” 📖

## 1 Kings 22:19

Micaiah continued, “Hear the LORD's word. 📖

I saw the LORD on his throne.

Heaven's whole army stood on his right and left.”

## 1 Kings 22:20

“The LORD asked who would persuade Ahab to attack Ramoth-gilead and die there. 📖

Different suggestions were made.”

## 1 Kings 22:21

“A spirit stood before the LORD and offered to persuade him. 📖

The LORD asked how.”

## 1 Kings 22:22

“The spirit said, ‘I will go and be a lying spirit in all his prophets’ mouths.’ 📖

The LORD said it would succeed and told it to go.”

## 1 Kings 22:23

“The LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouths of all these prophets of yours. 📖

The LORD has declared disaster for you.”

## 1 Kings 22:24

Zedekiah son of Chenaanah slapped Micaiah's face. 📖

He asked, “Which way did the LORD's Spirit go from me to speak to you?”

## 1 Kings 22:25

Micaiah said, “You will find out when you hide in an inner room.” 📖

## 1 Kings 22:26

Israel's king ordered, “Take Micaiah back to Amon the city governor and Joash the king's son.” 📖

## 1 Kings 22:27

“Put him in prison. 📖

Give him only bread and water until I return safely.”

## 1 Kings 22:28

Micaiah said, “If you return safely, the LORD has not spoken through me.” 📖

He told everyone to take notice.

## 1 Kings 22:29

The kings of Israel and Judah went to Ramoth-gilead. 📖

## 1 Kings 22:30

Israel's king told Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself for battle. 📖

You wear your royal robes.”

He put on a disguise and entered battle.

## 1 Kings 22:31

Aram's king ordered his 32 chariot commanders to fight only Israel's king. 📖

They were to ignore everyone else.

## 1 Kings 22:32

The commanders saw Jehoshaphat and thought he was Israel's king. 📖

They turned to attack him.

Jehoshaphat cried out.

## 1 Kings 22:33

They realised he was not Israel's king. 📖

They stopped chasing him.

## 1 Kings 22:34

A man shot an arrow without aiming at anyone in particular. 📖

It struck Israel's king between pieces of his armour.

The king told his chariot driver to turn around and take him away.

He was badly wounded.

## 1 Kings 22:35

The battle continued all day. 📖

The king was held upright in his chariot, facing the Arameans.

His blood ran onto the chariot floor.

He died that evening.

## 1 Kings 22:36

At sunset, a shout spread through the army. 📖

Everyone was told to return to their own city and land.

## 1 Kings 22:37

The dead king was brought to Samaria and buried there. 📖

## 1 Kings 22:38

They washed the chariot at Samaria's pool, where prostitutes bathed. 📖

Dogs licked Ahab's blood, just as the LORD had said.

**Help with a word:** Prostitutes are people paid for sex.

## 1 Kings 22:39

Ahab's other actions were recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

It told about his ivory palace and the cities he built.

## 1 Kings 22:40

Ahab died and joined his ancestors. 📖

His son Ahaziah became king.

## 1 Kings 22:41

Jehoshaphat son of Asa became Judah's king in year 4 of Ahab's rule over Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 22:42

Jehoshaphat was 35 when he became king. 📖

He ruled in Jerusalem for 25 years.

His mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.

## 1 Kings 22:43

He followed his father Asa's ways and did right before the LORD. 📖

But he did not remove the high places.

People still offered sacrifices and burned incense there.

**Help with words:** High places were local worship sites.

Incense is a substance burned for its sweet smell.

## 1 Kings 22:44

Jehoshaphat also made peace with Israel's king. 📖

## 1 Kings 22:45

Jehoshaphat's other actions, powerful deeds, and wars were recorded in a book. 📖

It was the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah.

## 1 Kings 22:46

He removed the male shrine prostitutes left from his father Asa's time. 📖

**Help with words:** Shrine prostitutes were connected with religious worship sites.

## 1 Kings 22:47

Edom had no king. 📖

An appointed governor ruled it.

## 1 Kings 22:48

Jehoshaphat built ships of Tarshish to collect gold from Ophir. 📖

They never sailed because they were wrecked at Ezion-geber.

## 1 Kings 22:49

Ahaziah son of Ahab offered to send his servants with Jehoshaphat's servants. 📖

Jehoshaphat refused.

## 1 Kings 22:50

Jehoshaphat died and joined his ancestors. 📖

He was buried with them in the city of his father David.

His son Jehoram became king.

## 1 Kings 22:51

Ahaziah son of Ahab became Israel's king in year 17 of Jehoshaphat's rule in Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 2 years.

## 1 Kings 22:52

Ahaziah did evil before the LORD. 📖

He followed his father, mother, and Jeroboam son of Nebat.

Jeroboam had led Israel into sin.

## 1 Kings 22:53

Ahaziah served and worshipped Baal. 📖

Like his father, he made the LORD, Israel's God, angry.

**Help with a name:** Baal was a false god worshipped in the region.
