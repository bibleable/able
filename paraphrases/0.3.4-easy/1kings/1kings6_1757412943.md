# 1 Kings 6 — Building the LORD’s house

**About this chapter**

Solomon builds and decorates the temple.

The LORD’s promise is linked to obedience.

A cubit was a measure of length.

## 1 Kings 6:1

Building began in year 480 after Israel left Egypt. 📖

It was Solomon’s 4th year as king, in Ziv, the 2nd month.

## 1 Kings 6:2

The LORD’s house measured 60 cubits long, 20 wide, and 30 high. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:3

The entrance porch stretched 20 cubits across the main hall’s width and projected 10 cubits in front. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:4

He made narrow, framed windows high in the temple. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:5

Solomon built a structure with side rooms around the temple, against the temple’s and inner sanctuary’s walls. 📖

**Help with sanctuary:** A sanctuary is a holy place of worship.

## 1 Kings 6:6

The lowest floor was 5 cubits wide, the middle 6, and the 3rd 7. 📖

Outside ledges supported it without inserting beams into the temple walls.

## 1 Kings 6:7

Stones were finished at the quarry before construction. 📖

No hammer, chisel, or iron tool sounded in the temple while it was built.

**Help with quarry:** A quarry is a place where stone is cut from the ground.

## 1 Kings 6:8

The lowest floor’s entrance was on the south. 📖

Stairs led to the middle level, then the 3rd.

## 1 Kings 6:9

Solomon finished the temple with cedar beams and roof boards. 📖

**Help with cedar:** Cedar is a tree whose wood is used for building.

## 1 Kings 6:10

Rooms surrounded the temple, each 5 cubits high, attached by cedar beams. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:11

The LORD’s word came to Solomon. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:12

“About this temple you are building, here is my promise. 📖

If you follow my laws, carry out my rules, and keep all my commands, I will fulfil it.

It is the promise I made to your father David, and I will fulfil it through you.

## 1 Kings 6:13

I will live among Israel and not abandon my people.” 📖

## 1 Kings 6:14

Solomon built and finished the temple. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:15

He lined the inside walls with cedar from floor to ceiling and laid cypress floorboards. 📖

**Help with cedar and cypress:** These are kinds of trees used for timber.

## 1 Kings 6:16

He separated the rear 20 cubits with floor-to-ceiling cedar boards. 📖

This formed the inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place.

**Help with Most Holy Place:** This was the temple’s innermost holy room.

## 1 Kings 6:17

The main hall before this room was 40 cubits long. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:18

The cedar panels had carvings of gourds and open flowers. 📖

Cedar covered everything, hiding all stone.

**Help with gourds:** Gourds are fruits with hard outer skins.

## 1 Kings 6:19

Solomon prepared the inner sanctuary for the ark of the LORD’s covenant. 📖

**Help with ark of the covenant:** This sacred chest was associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

A covenant is a firm agreement or promise.

## 1 Kings 6:20

The inner sanctuary measured 20 cubits long, 20 wide, and 20 high. 📖

He covered its inside and the cedar altar with pure gold.

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

## 1 Kings 6:21

Solomon covered the temple’s inside with pure gold. 📖

He stretched gold chains across the front of the gold-covered inner sanctuary.

## 1 Kings 6:22

He covered the entire inside with gold until it was finished. 📖

He also covered the inner sanctuary’s entire altar with gold.

## 1 Kings 6:23

He made 2 olive-wood cherubim in the inner sanctuary, each 10 cubits high. 📖

**Help with cherubim:** Cherubim are heavenly beings represented by these figures.

## 1 Kings 6:24

The first cherub’s wings each measured 5 cubits. 📖

The full wingspan was 10 cubits.

## 1 Kings 6:25

The 2nd cherub also measured 10 cubits. 📖

Both had the same size and shape.

## 1 Kings 6:26

Each cherub was 10 cubits high. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:27

The cherubim stood in the innermost room with wings spread. 📖

Their outer wings touched opposite walls, and their inner wingtips touched in the middle.

## 1 Kings 6:28

He covered the cherubim with gold. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:29

He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers on all the inner and outer sanctuary walls. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:30

He covered the floors of both sanctuaries with gold. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:31

For the inner sanctuary, he made olive-wood doors with 5-sided doorposts. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:32

The double olive-wood doors had cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers carved into them. 📖

Hammered gold covered the cherubim and palms.

## 1 Kings 6:33

He similarly made 4-sided olive-wood doorposts for the sanctuary entrance. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:34

The 2 cypress doors each had 2 folding panels. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:35

He carved cherubim, palms, and open flowers and covered them evenly with hammered gold. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:36

The inner courtyard had 3 rows of shaped stone and 1 row of trimmed cedar beams. 📖

## 1 Kings 6:37

The LORD’s house’s foundation was laid in Solomon’s 4th year, in Ziv. 📖

**Help with foundation:** A foundation is the base supporting a building.

## 1 Kings 6:38

The temple was finished in every detail in year 11, in Bul, the 8th month. 📖

It took 7 years to build.
