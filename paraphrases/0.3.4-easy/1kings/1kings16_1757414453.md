# 1 Kings 16 — Baasha’s family falls and Ahab becomes king

**About this chapter**

Prophetic judgment is followed by killings and changes of ruler.

The chapter records Zimri, Omri, Ahab, and the rebuilding of Jericho.

## 1 Kings 16:1

The LORD’s word against Baasha came to Jehu son of Hanani. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:2

“I raised you from dust to rule Israel. 📖

You followed Jeroboam and made Israel sin, provoking me.

**Help with dust:** This pictures a low starting position.

## 1 Kings 16:3

I will destroy Baasha and his family, making it like Jeroboam son of Nebat’s. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:4

Dogs will eat anyone from Baasha’s family who dies in the city. 📖

Birds will eat anyone from his family who dies in the fields.”

## 1 Kings 16:5

Baasha’s other deeds, achievements, and strength are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:6

Baasha died and was buried at Tirzah. 📖

His son Elah succeeded him.

## 1 Kings 16:7

The LORD spoke through the prophet Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha and his family. 📖

Baasha’s evil work angered the LORD.

He became like Jeroboam’s family, and he also killed them.

## 1 Kings 16:8

Elah son of Baasha became Israel’s king in Asa’s 26th year, ruling Tirzah for 2 years. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:9

Elah drank himself drunk at Arza’s house in Tirzah. 📖

Arza managed his household.

Elah’s servant Zimri, commander of half his chariots, plotted against him.

## 1 Kings 16:10

In Asa’s 27th year as king of Judah, Zimri went in, struck Elah, and killed him. 📖

Zimri became king instead.

## 1 Kings 16:11

On taking the throne, Zimri killed Baasha’s entire family, leaving no male relative or friend. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:12

Zimri destroyed Baasha’s whole family. 📖

This fulfilled the LORD’s word against Baasha through Jehu the prophet.

## 1 Kings 16:13

Baasha and Elah sinned and led Israel astray, provoking the LORD, Israel’s God, with worthless idols. 📖

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## 1 Kings 16:14

Elah’s other achievements are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:15

Zimri ruled Tirzah for 7 days in Asa’s 27th year. 📖

The troops besieged Philistine Gibbethon.

## 1 Kings 16:16

The camp heard Zimri had plotted and killed the king. 📖

That day, all Israel proclaimed army commander Omri king.

## 1 Kings 16:17

Omri and all Israel marched from Gibbethon and surrounded Tirzah. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:18

Zimri saw the city captured and burned the palace’s citadel down on himself. 📖

He died.

**Help with citadel:** A citadel is a protected inner part of a palace or city.

## 1 Kings 16:19

This happened because he did evil before the LORD, following Jeroboam’s sin that led Israel astray. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:20

Zimri’s other acts and treason are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

**Help with treason:** Treason means acting to overthrow one’s ruler.

## 1 Kings 16:21

Israel split equally between Tibni son of Ginath and Omri as king. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:22

Omri’s followers were stronger than those of Tibni son of Ginath. 📖

Tibni died, and Omri became king.

## 1 Kings 16:23

Omri became king in Asa’s 31st year. 📖

He ruled Israel for 12 years, 6 at Tirzah.

## 1 Kings 16:24

He bought Samaria’s hill from Shemer for 2 silver talents and built a city. 📖

He named it Samaria after Shemer.

**Help with talent:** A talent was a measure of weight.

## 1 Kings 16:25

Omri did more evil before the LORD than all before him. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:26

He followed all Jeroboam son of Nebat’s ways and sins, leading Israel astray.

He angered the LORD, Israel’s God, with worthless idols. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:27

Omri’s other achievements and strength are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:28

Omri died and was buried at Samaria. 📖

His son Ahab succeeded him.

## 1 Kings 16:29

Ahab son of Omri became Israel’s king in Asa’s 38th year, ruling Samaria for 22 years. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:30

Ahab did more evil before the LORD than all before him. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:31

Following Jeroboam son of Nebat’s sins was not enough. 📖

He married Jezebel daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians and served and worshipped Baal.

## 1 Kings 16:32

He built Baal’s temple in Samaria and erected an altar there. 📖

## 1 Kings 16:33

He erected an Asherah pole. 📖

Ahab provoked the LORD, Israel’s God, more than every earlier king.

**Help with Asherah pole:** This was an object used in worship of the goddess Asherah.

## 1 Kings 16:34

Hiel the Bethelite rebuilt Jericho in Ahab’s days. 📖

Its foundations cost him Abiram, his firstborn, and its gates cost him Segub, his youngest.

This fulfilled the LORD’s word through Joshua son of Nun.

**About his sons:** The source links the building stages with their loss, without describing the precise events.
