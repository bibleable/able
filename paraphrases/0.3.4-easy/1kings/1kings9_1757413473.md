# 1 Kings 9 — The LORD appears again and Solomon builds

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Solomon promises and warnings.

The chapter records towns, building work, forced labour, worship, and ships.

## 1 Kings 9:1

Solomon finished the LORD’s house, his palace, and all he desired to do. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:2

The LORD appeared a 2nd time, as at Gibeon. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:3

The LORD said, “I heard your prayer and plea. 📖

I set this temple apart by putting my Name here forever.

My eyes and heart will always be here.

## 1 Kings 9:4

If you follow me like David, with integrity and uprightness, obeying all my commands and laws, 📖

**Help with integrity and uprightness:** These mean honesty and right conduct.

## 1 Kings 9:5

I will establish your throne over Israel forever. 📖

I promised David an unbroken line on Israel’s throne.

## 1 Kings 9:6

But if you or your sons turn away, disobey my laws and commands, and worship and serve other gods, 📖

## 1 Kings 9:7

I will remove Israel from the land I gave them. 📖

I will cast this temple, set apart for my Name, from my presence.

All peoples will mock Israel.

## 1 Kings 9:8

The temple will become rubble. 📖

Everyone passing will be appalled and hiss, asking why the LORD did this to the land and temple.

## 1 Kings 9:9

Others will answer, ‘They abandoned the LORD, who brought their ancestors from Egypt. 📖

They embraced, worshipped, and served other gods.

That is why the LORD brought this disaster.’”

## 1 Kings 9:10

Solomon spent 20 years building the LORD’s house and the royal palace. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:11

He gave Hiram king of Tyre 20 towns in Galilee. 📖

Hiram supplied all his desired cedar, cypress, and gold.

## 1 Kings 9:12

Hiram left Tyre to inspect them and was displeased. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:13

Hiram asked, “What towns have you given me, my brother?” 📖

He named them the Land of Cabul, their name to this day.

## 1 Kings 9:14

Hiram had sent Solomon 120 talents of gold. 📖

**Help with talent:** A talent was a measure of weight.

## 1 Kings 9:15

Solomon imposed forced labour to build the LORD’s house, his palace, supporting terraces, and Jerusalem’s wall. 📖

This included Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer.

## 1 Kings 9:16

Pharaoh king of Egypt captured and burned Gezer and killed its Canaanites. 📖

He gave it as a marriage gift to his daughter, Solomon’s wife.

## 1 Kings 9:17

Solomon rebuilt Gezer and Lower Beth-horon, 📖

## 1 Kings 9:18

Baalath and Tamar in Judah’s wilderness, 📖

## 1 Kings 9:19

And all storage cities for his chariots and horses. 📖

He built whatever he desired in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his kingdom.

## 1 Kings 9:20

The remaining Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites were not Israelites. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:21

Israel had been unable to destroy their descendants remaining in the land. 📖

Solomon made these people forced labourers, as they remain today.

**Help with descendants:** Descendants are children and later family.

## 1 Kings 9:22

Solomon did not enslave Israelites. 📖

They served as soldiers, servants, officials, captains, and commanders of chariots and cavalry.

**Help with cavalry:** Cavalry means soldiers using horses.

## 1 Kings 9:23

They included 550 chief supervisors of his building workers. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:24

Pharaoh’s daughter moved from the City of David to her new palace. 📖

Solomon then built the supporting terraces.

## 1 Kings 9:25

3 times yearly, Solomon gave burnt offerings and peace offerings at his altar and burned incense before the LORD. 📖

He completed the temple.

**Help with offerings:** These are gifts given in worship.

**Help with incense:** Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

## 1 Kings 9:26

Solomon built a fleet at Ezion-geber near Eloth in Edom, beside the Red Sea. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:27

Hiram sent experienced sailors to join Solomon’s servants in the fleet. 📖

## 1 Kings 9:28

They sailed to Ophir and brought Solomon 420 talents of gold. 📖

**Help with talent:** A talent was a measure of weight.
