# 1 Kings 3 — Solomon asks for wisdom

**About this chapter**

The LORD appears to Solomon in a dream.

Solomon asks for understanding, then hears a dispute between two women.

## 1 Kings 3:1

Solomon allied with Pharaoh king of Egypt by marrying his daughter. 📖

He brought her to the City of David until his palace, the LORD’s house, and Jerusalem’s wall were finished.

**Help with the LORD’s house:** This means the temple, a place of worship.

## 1 Kings 3:2

People sacrificed at high places because no house for the LORD’s Name had yet been built. 📖

**Help with high places:** These were raised places used for worship.

## 1 Kings 3:3

Solomon loved the LORD and followed David’s laws, except he sacrificed and burned incense at high places. 📖

**Help with incense:** Incense is material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

## 1 Kings 3:4

Solomon went to Gibeon, the great high place, and offered 1,000 burnt offerings at its altar. 📖

**Help with burnt offerings:** These are sacrifices burned on an altar.

## 1 Kings 3:5

At night in Gibeon, the LORD appeared in a dream. 📖

God said, “Ask, and I will give it!”

## 1 Kings 3:6

Solomon answered, “You showed David great faithful love because he lived faithfully, righteously, and uprightly before you. 📖

You continued that love by giving him a son on his throne today.

**Help with righteously and uprightly:** These mean in a way that is right before God.

## 1 Kings 3:7

LORD my God, you made me king instead of David. 📖

I am only a little child, not knowing how to go out or come in.

**Help with little child:** Solomon describes his lack of experience.

The draft does not assign him a literal age here.

## 1 Kings 3:8

I am among your chosen people, too many to count. 📖

## 1 Kings 3:9

Give me an understanding heart to judge your people and tell good from evil. 📖

Who can govern this great people?”

## 1 Kings 3:10

The Lord was pleased with Solomon’s request. 📖

## 1 Kings 3:11

God answered, “You asked for understanding to administer justice, not long life, riches, or your enemies’ deaths. 📖

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.

## 1 Kings 3:12

I will grant it. 📖

You will have a wise, discerning heart unlike anyone before or after you.

**Help with discerning:** Discerning means able to judge wisely.

## 1 Kings 3:13

I will also give you riches and honour you did not request. 📖

No one in any kingdom will equal you throughout your life.

## 1 Kings 3:14

If you follow my ways and keep my laws and commands as David did, I will prolong your life.” 📖

## 1 Kings 3:15

Solomon woke and realised it was a dream. 📖

In Jerusalem, he stood before the Lord’s covenant ark and gave burnt offerings and peace offerings.

He feasted with all his servants.

**Help with ark:** The ark was a sacred chest associated with God’s covenant with Israel.

**Help with offerings:** A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

A peace offering expresses peace and fellowship with God.

## 1 Kings 3:16

2 prostitutes came before the king. 📖

**Help with prostitutes:** Prostitutes are people who sell sexual acts.

## 1 Kings 3:17

One said, “My lord, we share a house, and I gave birth with her there. 📖

## 1 Kings 3:18

3 days later, she gave birth too. 📖

No one else was in the house.

## 1 Kings 3:19

Her son died at night because she rolled onto him. 📖

## 1 Kings 3:20

While I slept, she took my son from beside me. 📖

She held him to her breast and put her dead son at mine.

## 1 Kings 3:21

I rose to feed my son and found him dead. 📖

Looking closely, I knew he was not the child I bore.”

**About this account:** This woman reports these events to the king.

## 1 Kings 3:22

The other said, “No, the living son is mine and the dead one yours!” 📖

The first insisted the opposite.

They argued before the king.

## 1 Kings 3:23

The king repeated their opposing claims. 📖

“One says, ‘Mine lives and yours is dead.’

The other says, ‘Yours is dead and mine lives.’”

## 1 Kings 3:24

The king ordered a sword brought, and they brought one. 📖

## 1 Kings 3:25

He said, “Cut the living child in 2. 📖

Give half to each woman.”

## 1 Kings 3:26

The living boy’s mother pleaded from compassion, “Give her the baby! 📖

Do not kill him!”

The other said, “Neither of us will have him.

Cut him in 2!”

## 1 Kings 3:27

The king ruled, “Give the living baby to the first woman. 📖

Do not kill him.

She is his mother.”

## 1 Kings 3:28

Israel heard and stood in awe of the king. 📖

They saw God’s wisdom in him to administer justice.

**Help with awe:** Awe means wonder and deep respect.

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.
