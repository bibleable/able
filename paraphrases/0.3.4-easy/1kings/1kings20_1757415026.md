# 1 Kings 20 — Ahab fights Aram and releases Ben-hadad

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Israel victory over Aram.

A prophet later confronts Ahab for releasing Ben-hadad.

## 1 Kings 20:1

Ben-hadad king of Aram gathered his entire army with 32 kings, horses, and chariots, surrounding and attacking Samaria. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:2

He sent messengers to Ahab king of Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:3

“Your silver, gold, best wives, and children are mine!” 📖

## 1 Kings 20:4

Ahab answered, “As you say, my lord. 📖

I and everything I have are yours.”

## 1 Kings 20:5

The messengers returned, repeating Ben-hadad’s demand for silver, gold, wives, and children. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:6

“Tomorrow at this time, my servants will search your palace and servants’ houses and take everything precious to you.” 📖

## 1 Kings 20:7

Ahab called all the land’s elders. 📖

“See how this man seeks trouble!

He demanded my wives, children, silver, and gold, and I did not refuse him.”

## 1 Kings 20:8

All elders and people advised refusing the terms. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:9

Ahab sent word accepting the first demand but refusing this one. 📖

The messengers reported to Ben-hadad.

## 1 Kings 20:10

Ben-hadad swore, “May the gods punish me severely unless Samaria has less dust than a handful for each soldier!” 📖

## 1 Kings 20:11

Ahab replied, “One putting on armour should not boast like one taking it off.” 📖

**Help with this saying:** Ahab warns against boasting before winning.

## 1 Kings 20:12

Ben-hadad and the kings drank in their tents. 📖

He ordered his servants into position against the city.

## 1 Kings 20:13

A prophet approached Ahab king of Israel. 📖

“The LORD says, ‘Do you see this entire great army?

I will hand it over to you today.

Then you will know I am the LORD.’”

## 1 Kings 20:14

Ahab asked, “Through whom?” 📖

The prophet answered, “Through the district governors’ young officers.”

Ahab asked who would start.

“You will.”

## 1 Kings 20:15

Ahab gathered 232 young officers, then all Israel’s remaining troops, 7,000 in total. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:16

They marched at noon while Ben-hadad and the 32 allied kings got drunk in their tents. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:17

The young officers went first. 📖

Ben-hadad’s scouts reported men coming from Samaria.

## 1 Kings 20:18

He ordered them taken alive whether coming peacefully or for war. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:19

The young officers left the city with the army behind. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:20

Each killed his opponent. 📖

Arameans fled, Israel pursued, and Ben-hadad escaped on horseback with cavalry.

## 1 Kings 20:21

Ahab attacked the horses and chariots and inflicted great slaughter. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:22

The prophet warned him to strengthen and prepare because Aram’s king would attack in spring. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:23

Aram’s king’s servants said, “Their gods are gods of the hills. 📖

That is why they beat us.

Let us fight Israel on the plains, where we will surely win.

## 1 Kings 20:24

“Replace the kings with officers. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:25

Raise an army like the one you lost, matching every horse and chariot. 📖

Then we can fight Israel on the plain, where we will surely win.”

The king approved their plan and acted on it.

## 1 Kings 20:26

In spring, Ben-hadad gathered Aram’s army at Aphek to fight Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:27

Israel gathered, supplied, and marched out. 📖

They camped like 2 small goat flocks before Arameans covering the countryside.

## 1 Kings 20:28

A man of God approached Israel’s king. 📖

“The LORD says, ‘The Arameans said I am a god of hills, but not valleys.

Because of this, I will hand you this entire great army.

Then you will know I am the LORD.’”

## 1 Kings 20:29

The armies camped opposite each other for 7 days. 📖

On day 7, battle began.

Israel killed 100,000 Aramean foot soldiers that day.

## 1 Kings 20:30

The rest fled into Aphek, where a wall fell on 27,000 of the remaining men. 📖

Ben-hadad also fled there and hid in an inner room.

## 1 Kings 20:31

His servants said, “We hear Israel’s kings are merciful. 📖

Let us go with sackcloth at our waists and ropes on our heads.

Perhaps he will spare you.”

**Help with sackcloth:** Sackcloth is rough cloth worn in grief or distress.

## 1 Kings 20:32

They wrapped sackcloth around their waists and ropes around their heads and went to Israel’s king. 📖

They said, “Your servant Ben-hadad asks you to spare his life.”

Ahab answered, “Is he still alive?

He is my brother!”

**Help with sackcloth:** This was rough cloth worn to express grief or humility.

**Help with brother:** Ahab uses this word for a fellow king.

## 1 Kings 20:33

They seized this hopeful word: “Yes, your brother Ben-hadad.” 📖

Ahab sent for him and invited him onto his chariot.

## 1 Kings 20:34

Ben-hadad promised to return his father’s captured cities and allow markets in Damascus like his father’s in Samaria. 📖

Ahab made a treaty and released him.

**Help with treaty:** A treaty is a formal agreement.

## 1 Kings 20:35

By the LORD’s word, a member of the prophets asked his companion to strike him. 📖

The companion refused.

## 1 Kings 20:36

The prophet said, “A lion will kill you when you leave because you disobeyed the LORD.” 📖

When the man left, a lion found him and killed him.

## 1 Kings 20:37

The prophet asked another man, who struck and wounded him. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:38

He waited beside the king’s road, disguising himself with a bandage over his eyes. 📖

## 1 Kings 20:39

As the king passed, the prophet called out, “I went into the middle of the battle. 📖

A man brought a captive and told me to guard him.

If he went missing for any reason, my life would replace his, or I must pay 1 silver talent.

**Help with talent:** This was a large unit of weight used for silver or gold.

## 1 Kings 20:40

While I was busy here and there, the man vanished.” 📖

Israel’s king answered, “That will be your punishment.

You have pronounced judgment on yourself.”

## 1 Kings 20:41

The prophet quickly removed the bandage from his eyes. 📖

Israel’s king recognised him as one of the prophets.

## 1 Kings 20:42

He said, “The LORD says you released a man marked for destruction. 📖

Your life will replace his, and your people his people.”

## 1 Kings 20:43

Ahab returned to Samaria sullen and angry. 📖

**Help with sullen:** Sullen means resentful and gloomy.
