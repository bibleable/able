# 1 Kings 17 — Elijah, the ravens, and the widow

**About this chapter**

Elijah announces no rain and receives food by the stream.

At Zarephath, a widow shares food, and Elijah prays for her son’s life.

## 1 Kings 17:1

Elijah the Tishbite, a settler in Gilead, told Ahab, “As surely as the LORD, Israel’s God, lives, I serve him. 📖

No dew or rain will come these years except at my word.”

**Help with dew:** Dew is moisture forming on cool surfaces.

## 1 Kings 17:2

The LORD revealed a message to Elijah. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:3

“Go east and hide by Cherith’s stream, east of the Jordan. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:4

Drink there. 📖

I commanded ravens to feed you.”

**Help with ravens:** Ravens are large black birds.

## 1 Kings 17:5

Elijah obeyed and lived beside Cherith’s stream, east of the Jordan. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:6

Ravens brought bread and meat morning and evening, and he drank from the stream. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:7

The stream later dried up because the land had no rain. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:8

The LORD’s word came to Elijah. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:9

“Go and stay at Zarephath of Sidon. 📖

I commanded a widow to feed you.”

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Kings 17:10

Elijah went to Zarephath and reached the city gate. 📖

A widow was gathering sticks.

He called, “Please bring me a little water in a cup to drink.”

## 1 Kings 17:11

As she went, he asked for a piece of bread too. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:12

She answered, “As surely as the LORD your God lives, I have no bread. 📖

I have only a handful of flour and a little oil.

I gather 2 sticks to prepare a last meal for my son and me, then die.”

## 1 Kings 17:13

Elijah said, “Do not fear. 📖

Do as you said, but first make me a small bread cake and bring it.

Then make food for yourself and your son.

## 1 Kings 17:14

The LORD, Israel’s God, says your flour and oil will not run out until he sends rain.” 📖

## 1 Kings 17:15

She obeyed. 📖

Elijah, the woman, and her household ate every day.

## 1 Kings 17:16

The flour and oil did not run out, as the LORD said through Elijah. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:17

Later, her son became increasingly ill until no breath remained. 📖

## 1 Kings 17:18

She asked Elijah, “Man of God, what have you done? 📖

Did you come to recall my wrongdoing and cause my son’s death?”

**About the question:** This is the woman’s explanation of her loss, not a general explanation of children’s deaths.

## 1 Kings 17:19

Elijah said, “Give me your son.” 📖

He took the son from her arms and carried him to the upstairs room where he stayed.

He laid the son on his own bed.

## 1 Kings 17:20

He cried, “LORD my God, did you bring tragedy to this widow hosting me by causing her son’s death?” 📖

## 1 Kings 17:21

He stretched himself over the child 3 times and prayed, “LORD my God, let his life return!” 📖

## 1 Kings 17:22

The LORD heard Elijah, and the child’s life returned. 📖

He lived.

## 1 Kings 17:23

Elijah brought the child downstairs and gave him to his mother. 📖

“Look, your son lives!”

## 1 Kings 17:24

The woman said, “Now I know you are a man of God and speak the LORD’s true word.” 📖
