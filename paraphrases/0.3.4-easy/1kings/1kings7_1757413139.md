# 1 Kings 7 — The palace and temple furnishings

**About this chapter**

Solomon’s palace and the temple’s furnishings are described.

A cubit and a handbreadth measure length.

A bath measures liquid volume.

## 1 Kings 7:1

Solomon took 13 years to finish his whole palace. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:2

The House of the Forest of Lebanon measured 100 cubits long, 50 wide, and 30 high. 📖

It had 4 rows of cedar pillars supporting cedar beams.

## 1 Kings 7:3

The cedar roof covered 45 beams resting on pillars, with 15 beams per row. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:4

High windows faced each other in 3 rows and 3 levels. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:5

All doorways had rectangular frames, with openings facing each other in 3 levels. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:6

The colonnade measured 50 cubits long and 30 wide. 📖

It had a porch in front, with a pillared canopy before the porch.

**Help with colonnade:** A colonnade is a row of pillars.

**Help with canopy:** A canopy is a covering overhead.

## 1 Kings 7:7

He built the throne hall, the Hall of Justice, for judging. 📖

Cedar covered it from floor to ceiling.

## 1 Kings 7:8

His home palace stood further back with similar construction. 📖

He also built a palace like the hall for Pharaoh’s daughter, his wife.

## 1 Kings 7:9

Costly stones were cut and sawn inside and out, from foundations to roof edges. 📖

They extended from outside to the great courtyard.

## 1 Kings 7:10

The foundations used large, costly stones, some 10 cubits long and others 8. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:11

Above them were costly stones cut to size and cedar beams. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:12

The great courtyard had 3 rows of shaped stone and 1 row of trimmed cedar beams. 📖

The LORD’s house’s inner courtyard and porch were similar.

## 1 Kings 7:13

Solomon sent for Huram from Tyre. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:14

Huram’s mother was a widow from Naphtali’s tribe, and his father was a bronze craftsman from Tyre. 📖

Huram had great skill, understanding, and knowledge of all bronze work.

He came to Solomon and did all his work.

**Help with widow:** A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Kings 7:15

He cast 2 bronze pillars, each 18 cubits high and 12 around. 📖

**Help with cast:** Cast means formed by pouring molten metal into a mould.

## 1 Kings 7:16

He cast 2 bronze capitals for the pillars, each 5 cubits high. 📖

**Help with capitals:** Capitals are decorative tops for pillars.

## 1 Kings 7:17

He made lattice networks with chainwork wreaths for the capitals, 7 for each. 📖

**Help with lattice:** Lattice is a pattern of crossing strips.

## 1 Kings 7:18

He made 2 rows of pomegranates around each network covering the capitals. 📖

**Help with pomegranates:** Here, these are decorations shaped like the fruit.

## 1 Kings 7:19

The porch’s pillar capitals were lily-shaped, 4 cubits high. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:20

Each capital had 200 pomegranates in rows, above the rounded projection beside the network. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:21

He erected the porch pillars, naming the south one Jachin and the north one Boaz. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:22

The tops were lily-shaped. 📖

The pillars were finished.

## 1 Kings 7:23

He cast the circular Sea, 10 cubits across, 5 high, and 30 around. 📖

**Help with Sea:** Here, Sea names a large water basin.

## 1 Kings 7:24

Under its rim were 2 rows of buds, 10 per cubit, cast with the Sea itself. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:25

The Sea rested on 12 oxen: 3 facing north, 3 west, 3 south, and 3 east. 📖

All their rear parts faced the centre.

## 1 Kings 7:26

The Sea was 1 handbreadth thick. 📖

Its rim resembled a cup’s brim or lily blossom.

It held 2,000 baths.

## 1 Kings 7:27

He made 10 movable bronze stands, each 4 cubits long, 4 wide, and 3 high. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:28

The stands had side panels fixed to upright supports. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:29

Between the supports, the panels showed lions, oxen, and cherubim. 📖

A platform stood above the supports, and angled wreath decorations lay below the lions and oxen.

**Help with cherubim:** Cherubim are heavenly beings represented by these figures.

## 1 Kings 7:30

Each stand had 4 bronze wheels and axles. 📖

Its basin rested on 4 supports, with wreaths on each side.

## 1 Kings 7:31

The opening inside each top crown was 1 cubit deep. 📖

Its round opening, shaped like a platform, was 1 and a half cubits wide.

Carvings surrounded it, but the side panels were square, not round.

## 1 Kings 7:32

4 wheels lay under the panels, with axles attached to the stand. 📖

Each wheel measured 1 and a half cubits across.

## 1 Kings 7:33

The wheels resembled chariot wheels. 📖

Axles, rims, spokes, and hubs were cast metal.

**Help with wheel parts:** Spokes join the outer rim to the central hub.

## 1 Kings 7:34

Each stand had 4 projecting handles, one at each corner. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:35

A circular band, half a cubit high, topped each stand. 📖

The supports, panels, and top were cast as one piece.

## 1 Kings 7:36

He carved cherubim, lions, and palms wherever there was space on supports and panels, with wreaths around. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:37

All 10 stands had the same casting, size, and shape. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:38

He made 10 bronze basins, each holding 40 baths and measuring 4 cubits across. 📖

Each stand had one basin.

## 1 Kings 7:39

5 stands stood south of the temple and 5 north. 📖

The Sea stood south, at the southeast corner.

## 1 Kings 7:40

Huram also made pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. 📖

This completed his work for Solomon in the LORD’s house:

## 1 Kings 7:41

2 pillars, 2 bowl-shaped capitals, and 2 networks covering the capitals’ bowls; 📖

## 1 Kings 7:42

400 pomegranates for the 2 networks, with 2 rows around each bowl-shaped capital; 📖

## 1 Kings 7:43

10 stands and their 10 basins; 📖

## 1 Kings 7:44

The Sea and its 12 supporting oxen; 📖

## 1 Kings 7:45

And pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. 📖

All Huram’s objects for Solomon in the LORD’s house were polished bronze.

## 1 Kings 7:46

The king had them cast in clay moulds on the Jordan plain between Succoth and Zarethan. 📖

## 1 Kings 7:47

There were too many objects to weigh. 📖

Solomon left their bronze weight unknown.

## 1 Kings 7:48

Solomon made all the LORD’s house’s furnishings, including the gold altar and table for the Bread of the Presence. 📖

**Help with Bread of the Presence:** This bread was placed before the LORD in worship.

## 1 Kings 7:49

Pure gold lampstands stood before the inner sanctuary, 5 on the right and 5 on the left. 📖

Flowers, lamps, and tongs were gold.

## 1 Kings 7:50

Basins, wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, ladles, and censers were pure gold. 📖

Hinges for the Most Holy Place’s doors and main hall’s doors were gold too.

**Help with wick trimmers:** These trim the burning threads in lamps.

**Help with censers:** Censers hold material burned to make a sweet smell in worship.

## 1 Kings 7:51

Solomon finished all the work on the LORD’s house. 📖

He brought in the silver, gold, and furnishings his father David had dedicated.

He put them in the treasuries of the LORD’s house.
