# 1 Kings 15 — Kings of Judah and Israel

**About this chapter**

Abijam and Asa rule Judah.

In Israel, Baasha kills Nadab and destroys Jeroboam’s family.

## 1 Kings 15:1

Abijam became Judah’s king in Jeroboam son of Nebat’s 18th year. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:2

He ruled Jerusalem for 3 years. 📖

His mother was Maacah daughter of Abishalom.

## 1 Kings 15:3

He followed his father’s sins and lacked his ancestor David’s wholehearted devotion to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:4

For David’s sake, the LORD gave a lamp in Jerusalem: a succeeding son and a strong Jerusalem. 📖

**Help with lamp:** This pictures the continuing royal line.

## 1 Kings 15:5

David did right and kept the LORD’s commands throughout his life, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:6

Rehoboam’s and Jeroboam’s families fought throughout Abijam’s life. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:7

Abijam’s other acts are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

Abijam and Jeroboam fought.

## 1 Kings 15:8

Abijam died and was buried in the City of David. 📖

His son Asa succeeded him.

## 1 Kings 15:9

Asa became Judah’s king in Jeroboam’s 20th year over Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:10

He ruled Jerusalem for 41 years. 📖

His grandmother was Maacah daughter of Abishalom.

## 1 Kings 15:11

Asa did right before the LORD, like his father David. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:12

He expelled male shrine prostitutes and removed all his ancestors’ idols. 📖

**Help with shrine prostitutes:** This describes men providing sexual acts connected with places of worship.

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## 1 Kings 15:13

He removed Maacah as queen mother for making a hateful Asherah pole. 📖

He chopped it down and burned it in the Kidron Valley.

**Help with Asherah pole:** This was an object used in worship of the goddess Asherah.

## 1 Kings 15:14

The high places remained, but Asa was wholeheartedly devoted to the LORD throughout his life. 📖

**Help with high places:** These were raised places used for worship.

## 1 Kings 15:15

He brought his and his father’s dedicated silver, gold, and objects into the LORD’s house. 📖

**Help with dedicated:** Dedicated means set apart for God.

## 1 Kings 15:16

Asa and Baasha king of Israel fought throughout their days. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:17

Baasha attacked Judah and fortified Ramah to block movement into and out of Asa’s territory. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:18

Asa took all remaining temple and palace silver and gold and sent it with his servants. 📖

His message went to Ben-hadad son of Tabrimmon, son of Hezion, king of Aram at Damascus.

## 1 Kings 15:19

“Let us make a treaty like our fathers’. 📖

Here is silver and gold.

Break your treaty with Baasha so he withdraws.”

**Help with treaty:** A treaty is a formal agreement.

## 1 Kings 15:20

Ben-hadad agreed and sent commanders against Israel. 📖

They conquered Ijon, Dan, Abel-beth-maacah, and all Naphtali, including Chinnereth’s region.

## 1 Kings 15:21

Baasha stopped fortifying Ramah and withdrew to Tirzah. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:22

Asa called all Judah’s men, without exception, to carry off Ramah’s stones and timber. 📖

He used Baasha’s materials to build Geba of Benjamin and Mizpah.

## 1 Kings 15:23

Asa’s other deeds, strength, achievements, and cities are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

His feet became diseased in old age.

## 1 Kings 15:24

Asa died and was buried with his ancestors in David’s city. 📖

His son Jehoshaphat succeeded him.

## 1 Kings 15:25

Nadab son of Jeroboam became Israel’s king in Asa’s 2nd year and ruled 2 years. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:26

He did evil and followed his father’s example and sin that led Israel astray. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:27

Baasha son of Ahijah, from Issachar’s family, plotted against Nadab. 📖

He killed Nadab while all Israel besieged Philistine Gibbethon.

## 1 Kings 15:28

Baasha killed Nadab in Asa’s 3rd year and became king. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:29

Baasha killed Jeroboam’s entire family, leaving no one breathing. 📖

This fulfilled the LORD’s word through Ahijah the Shilonite.

## 1 Kings 15:30

Jeroboam sinned, led Israel into sin, and provoked the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:31

Nadab’s other achievements are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:32

Asa and Baasha king of Israel fought throughout their days. 📖

## 1 Kings 15:33

In Asa’s 3rd year, Baasha son of Ahijah became king of all Israel. 📖

He ruled Tirzah for 24 years.

## 1 Kings 15:34

Baasha did evil, following Jeroboam’s ways and sin that led Israel astray. 📖
