# Daniel 7 — Four beasts and the heavenly court

**About this chapter**

Daniel records a dream from Belshazzar’s first year.

He sees four beasts, a heavenly court, and One like the Son of Man.

He receives an explanation about kings, kingdoms, and the holy people.

## Daniel 7:1

In Belshazzar’s first year as ruler of Babylon, Daniel had a dream. 📖

Visions passed through his mind as he lay in bed.

He wrote down the dream.

This is the summary of his account.

## Daniel 7:2

Daniel said, “In my vision at night, I looked. 📖

Suddenly, heaven’s four winds stirred up the great sea.

## Daniel 7:3

Four great beasts came up from the sea. 📖

Each was different from the others.

## Daniel 7:4

The first beast was like a lion with an eagle’s wings. 📖

I watched until its wings were torn off.

It was lifted from the ground and made to stand on two feet like a man.

It was given a human mind.

## Daniel 7:5

Suddenly, another beast appeared, looking like a bear. 📖

It was raised on one side.

It had three ribs in its mouth, between its teeth.

It was told, ‘Get up and fill yourself with flesh!’

## Daniel 7:6

As I watched, another beast suddenly appeared, like a leopard. 📖

It had four birdlike wings on its back and four heads.

It was given authority to rule.

## Daniel 7:7

As I watched in my night vision, a fourth beast suddenly appeared. 📖

It was terrifying, dreadful, and extremely strong, with large iron teeth.

It ate and crushed, then trampled whatever remained under its feet.

It was different from all the earlier beasts and had ten horns.

## Daniel 7:8

I was thinking about the horns when a little horn came up among them. 📖

Three of the first horns were pulled out before it.

This horn had humanlike eyes and a mouth that spoke proud words.

## Daniel 7:9

I kept watching as thrones were put in place. 📖

The Ancient of Days sat down.

His clothes were white as snow, and His hair was like pure wool.

His throne burned with fire, and its wheels were all blazing.

**Help with “Ancient of Days”:** This title describes God, who rules over time.

## Daniel 7:10

A river of fire flowed from before Him. 📖

Thousands upon thousands served Him.

Tens of thousands upon tens of thousands stood before Him.

The court met, and the books were opened.

## Daniel 7:11

I kept watching because of the horn’s proud words. 📖

As I watched, the beast was killed.

Its body was destroyed and thrown into the blazing fire.

## Daniel 7:12

The other beasts lost their authority to rule. 📖

But they were allowed to live for a season and a time.

## Daniel 7:13

I kept watching in my night vision. 📖

I saw One like the Son of Man coming with heaven’s clouds.

He approached the Ancient of Days and was led before Him.

**Help with “Son of Man”:** This title uses the picture of a human figure.

## Daniel 7:14

He was given authority to rule, glory, and kingship. 📖

People of every nation and language were to serve Him.

His rule lasts forever and will not pass away.

His kingdom will never be destroyed.

## Daniel 7:15

I, Daniel, was deeply troubled. 📖

The visions in my mind alarmed me.

## Daniel 7:16

I approached one of those standing there and asked the true meaning of all this. 📖

He explained these things to me.

## Daniel 7:17

He said, ‘These four great beasts are four kings who will rise from the earth. 📖

## Daniel 7:18

But the holy people of the Most High will receive the kingdom. 📖

They will own it forever, yes, forever and ever.’

**Help with “holy people”:** BSB calls them “saints.”

They are people set apart for God.

## Daniel 7:19

I wanted to know the true meaning of the fourth beast, which was different from all the others. 📖

It was extremely terrifying.

It ate and crushed with iron teeth and bronze claws, then trampled whatever remained.

## Daniel 7:20

I also wanted to understand the ten horns on its head and the other horn that came up. 📖

Three horns fell before it.

This horn looked more impressive than the others.

It had eyes and a mouth that spoke proud words.

## Daniel 7:21

I watched this horn make war against the holy people and defeat them, 📖

## Daniel 7:22

until the Ancient of Days came and judged in favor of the Most High’s holy people. 📖

The time came for them to own the kingdom.

## Daniel 7:23

He explained, ‘The fourth beast is a fourth kingdom that will appear on earth, different from all other kingdoms. 📖

It will eat the whole earth, trample it down, and crush it.

## Daniel 7:24

The ten horns are ten kings who will rise from this kingdom. 📖

After them, another king will rise, different from the earlier kings.

He will bring three kings under his power.

## Daniel 7:25

He will speak against the Most High and oppress His holy people. 📖

He will try to change the appointed times and laws.

The holy people will be handed over to him for a time, times, and half a time.

**About the time:** The draft keeps this expression without turning it into a calendar date.

## Daniel 7:26

But the court will meet. 📖

His authority to rule will be taken away and completely destroyed forever.

## Daniel 7:27

Then the greatness and ruling power of the kingdoms under all heaven will be given to the people. 📖

These are the holy people of the Most High.

His kingdom will last forever.

All rulers will serve and obey Him.’

## Daniel 7:28

That is the end of the matter. 📖

My thoughts troubled me greatly, and my face turned pale.

But I, Daniel, kept the matter to myself.”
