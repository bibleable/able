# Daniel 2 — The king’s dream and its meaning

**About this chapter**

Nebuchadnezzar demands that his wise men tell him both his dream and its meaning.

God reveals the mystery to Daniel.

Daniel gives God the credit and explains the dream’s kingdoms.

## Daniel 2:1

In the second year of his rule, Nebuchadnezzar had dreams that deeply troubled him. 📖

He could not sleep.

## Daniel 2:2

The king called for the magicians, enchanters, sorcerers, and astrologers to explain his dreams. 📖

They came and stood before him.

**Help with these words:** These people claimed special powers or knowledge.

Astrologers sought messages in the stars.

## Daniel 2:3

The king told them, “I have had a dream. 📖

I am anxious to understand it.”

## Daniel 2:4

The astrologers answered in Aramaic, “King, may you live forever! 📖

Tell us, your servants, the dream.

We will explain its meaning.”

**Help with “Aramaic”:** This is the name of a language.

## Daniel 2:5

The king answered the astrologers, “My decision is final. 📖

If you do not tell me the dream and its meaning, you will be cut into pieces.

Your houses will be reduced to broken ruins.

## Daniel 2:6

But if you tell me the dream and its meaning, I will give you gifts, rewards, and great honor. 📖

So tell me the dream and its meaning.”

## Daniel 2:7

They answered again, “Let the king tell us, his servants, the dream. 📖

We will explain its meaning.”

## Daniel 2:8

The king replied, “I know you are trying to gain time. 📖

You see that my decision is final.

## Daniel 2:9

If you do not tell me the dream, there is only one sentence for you. 📖

You have plotted to tell me false and dishonest words, hoping things will change.

So tell me the dream.

Then I will know you can explain its meaning.”

**Help with “sentence”:** Here this means the king’s order of punishment.

## Daniel 2:10

The astrologers answered, “No one on earth can do what the king asks! 📖

No king, however great or powerful, has asked this of any magician, enchanter, or astrologer.

## Daniel 2:11

The king’s request is so difficult that only the gods can tell him. 📖

They do not live with human beings.”

## Daniel 2:12

This answer made the king furious. 📖

He ordered all the wise men of Babylon to be destroyed.

## Daniel 2:13

The order went out to execute the wise men. 📖

Men searched for Daniel and his friends to execute them too.

**Help with “execute”:** This means killing someone as a punishment.

## Daniel 2:14

Arioch, commander of the king’s guard, went out to execute Babylon’s wise men. 📖

Daniel answered him carefully and wisely.

## Daniel 2:15

Daniel asked, “Why is the king’s order so harsh?” 📖

Arioch explained the situation to him.

## Daniel 2:16

Daniel went to the king and asked for time, so he could explain the dream’s meaning. 📖

## Daniel 2:17

Daniel returned home and explained the matter to his friends Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. 📖

## Daniel 2:18

He urged them to ask the God of heaven for mercy about this mystery. 📖

Then Daniel and his friends would not be killed with the rest of Babylon’s wise men.

## Daniel 2:19

During the night, the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a vision. 📖

He praised the God of heaven.

## Daniel 2:20

Daniel said, “May God’s name be praised forever and ever. 📖

Wisdom and power belong to Him.

## Daniel 2:21

He changes times and seasons. 📖

He removes kings and puts kings in place.

He gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those who understand.

## Daniel 2:22

He reveals deep and hidden things. 📖

He knows what lies in darkness, and light lives with Him.

## Daniel 2:23

God of my ancestors, I thank and praise You. 📖

You have given me wisdom and power.

You have now shown me what we asked for.

You have shown us the king’s dream.”

## Daniel 2:24

Daniel went to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy Babylon’s wise men. 📖

Daniel said, “Do not execute Babylon’s wise men!

Take me before the king, and I will explain the dream’s meaning.”

## Daniel 2:25

Arioch hurried Daniel before the king. 📖

He said, “I have found a man among the people taken from Judah.

He will tell the king the dream’s meaning.”

## Daniel 2:26

The king asked Daniel, also called Belteshazzar, a question. 📖

He said, “Can you tell me what I saw in my dream and what it means?”

## Daniel 2:27

Daniel answered, “No wise man, enchanter, medium, or magician can explain the mystery the king asks about. 📖

**Help with “medium”:** This is someone who claims to receive messages from the dead.

## Daniel 2:28

But there is a God in heaven who reveals mysteries. 📖

He has shown King Nebuchadnezzar what will happen in the later days.

These were your dream and the visions that came as you lay in bed.

## Daniel 2:29

King, as you lay in bed, your thoughts turned to the future. 📖

The Revealer of Mysteries showed you what will happen.

## Daniel 2:30

This mystery was not shown to me because I am wiser than anyone alive. 📖

It was shown so the king could know its meaning and understand his own thoughts.

## Daniel 2:31

King, as you watched, a great statue appeared. 📖

It stood before you, huge and shining.

Its appearance filled you with awe.

## Daniel 2:32

The statue’s head was pure gold. 📖

Its chest and arms were silver.

Its belly and thighs were bronze.

## Daniel 2:33

Its legs were iron. 📖

Its feet were partly iron and partly clay.

## Daniel 2:34

As you watched, a stone was cut out, but not by human hands. 📖

It struck the statue’s iron and clay feet and crushed them.

## Daniel 2:35

Then the iron, clay, bronze, silver, and gold broke into pieces. 📖

They became like chaff on a threshing floor in summer.

The wind blew them away until no trace could be found.

But the stone that struck the statue became a great mountain and filled the whole earth.

**Help with “chaff”:** This is the light waste left when grain is separated on a threshing floor.

## Daniel 2:36

That was the dream. 📖

Now we will tell the king its meaning.

## Daniel 2:37

King, you are the king of kings. 📖

The God of heaven has given you authority to rule, power, strength, and glory.

## Daniel 2:38

He has handed over all people, animals of the field, and birds of the air, wherever they live. 📖

He has made you ruler over them all.

You are that head of gold.

## Daniel 2:39

After you, another kingdom will rise, weaker than yours. 📖

Then a third kingdom, of bronze, will rule the whole earth.

## Daniel 2:40

Finally, a fourth kingdom will come, as strong as iron. 📖

Iron breaks and crushes everything.

Like iron that crushes everything, this kingdom will break and crush all the others.

## Daniel 2:41

You saw feet and toes made partly of baked clay and partly of iron. 📖

In the same way, this will be a divided kingdom.

Yet it will have some of iron’s strength, just as you saw iron mixed with clay.

## Daniel 2:42

The toes were partly iron and partly clay. 📖

In the same way, the kingdom will be partly strong and partly easily broken.

## Daniel 2:43

You saw iron mixed with clay. 📖

In the same way, the peoples will mix with one another.

But they will not hold together, just as iron does not mix with clay.

## Daniel 2:44

In those kings’ days, the God of heaven will establish a kingdom that will never be destroyed. 📖

It will never be handed over to another people.

It will break all these kingdoms and end them.

But it will stand forever.

## Daniel 2:45

You saw a stone cut from a mountain without human hands. 📖

It broke the iron, bronze, clay, silver, and gold.

In this way, the great God has shown the king what will happen in the future.

The dream is true, and its meaning can be trusted.”

## Daniel 2:46

King Nebuchadnezzar fell with his face to the ground and honored Daniel. 📖

He ordered an offering and incense to be presented to Daniel.

**Help with “incense”:** This is something burned to give a sweet smell.

## Daniel 2:47

The king told Daniel, “Your God is truly the God of gods and Lord of kings, the Revealer of Mysteries. 📖

You were able to reveal this mystery.”

## Daniel 2:48

The king promoted Daniel and gave him many generous gifts. 📖

He made Daniel ruler over the whole province of Babylon and chief over all its wise men.

## Daniel 2:49

At Daniel’s request, the king appointed Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to manage Babylon’s province. 📖

Daniel remained in the king’s court.
