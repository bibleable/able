# Daniel 5 — The writing on the wall

**About this chapter**

Belshazzar uses vessels taken from God’s temple at a feast.

A hand writes a message on the wall.

Daniel explains the message and confronts the king’s pride.

## Daniel 5:1

Later, King Belshazzar gave a great feast for a thousand of his nobles. 📖

He drank wine with them.

## Daniel 5:2

Under the influence of wine, Belshazzar ordered the gold and silver vessels brought in. 📖

His father Nebuchadnezzar had taken them from Jerusalem’s temple.

The king, his nobles, wives, and concubines were to drink from them.

**Help with these words:** Vessels are cups and other containers.

Concubines were women who lived with a man as partners but did not have the full status of wives.

## Daniel 5:3

They brought the gold vessels taken from God’s temple in Jerusalem. 📖

The king, his nobles, wives, and concubines drank from them.

## Daniel 5:4

As they drank wine, they praised their gods of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone. 📖

## Daniel 5:5

At that moment, the fingers of a human hand appeared. 📖

They wrote on the plaster wall near the lampstand in the royal palace.

The king watched the writing hand.

## Daniel 5:6

His face turned pale. 📖

His thoughts frightened him so much that his hips gave way and his knees knocked together.

## Daniel 5:7

The king called for the enchanters, astrologers, and diviners. 📖

He told Babylon’s wise men, “Whoever reads this writing and explains it will wear purple and a gold neck chain.

He will become the kingdom’s third highest ruler.”

**Help with these titles:** These people claimed special powers or knowledge about hidden things and the future.

## Daniel 5:8

All the king’s wise men came in. 📖

But they could not read the writing or explain it to him.

## Daniel 5:9

King Belshazzar grew even more terrified and pale. 📖

His nobles were confused.

## Daniel 5:10

The queen heard the king and nobles crying out and entered the feast hall. 📖

She said, “King, may you live forever!

Do not let your thoughts terrify you or your face turn pale.

## Daniel 5:11

There is a man in your kingdom with the spirit of the holy gods in him. 📖

In your father’s days, he showed insight, intelligence, and wisdom like the gods’.

Your father, King Nebuchadnezzar, made him chief of the magicians, enchanters, astrologers, and diviners.

Your own father, the king,

## Daniel 5:12

did this because Daniel, whom he named Belteshazzar, had an extraordinary spirit, knowledge, and understanding. 📖

He could explain dreams and riddles and solve difficult problems.

Call Daniel, and he will explain the writing.”

**Help with “extraordinary”:** This means unusual or outstanding.

## Daniel 5:13

Daniel was brought before the king. 📖

The king asked, “Are you Daniel, one of the people my father the king brought from Judah?

## Daniel 5:14

I have heard that the spirit of the gods is in you. 📖

I hear you have insight, intelligence, and extraordinary wisdom.

## Daniel 5:15

The wise men and enchanters were brought before me to read this writing and explain it. 📖

But they could not explain its meaning.

## Daniel 5:16

I have heard you can explain meanings and solve difficult problems. 📖

If you can read this writing and explain it, you will wear purple and a gold neck chain.

You will become the kingdom’s third highest ruler.”

## Daniel 5:17

Daniel answered, “Keep your gifts and give your rewards to someone else. 📖

Still, I will read the writing for the king and explain it.

## Daniel 5:18

King, the Most High God gave your father Nebuchadnezzar authority to rule, greatness, glory, and honor. 📖

## Daniel 5:19

Because God made him great, people of every nation and language trembled in fear before him. 📖

He killed anyone he wished and kept alive anyone he wished.

He raised up anyone he wished and brought down anyone he wished.

## Daniel 5:20

But his heart became arrogant and hardened with pride. 📖

Then he was removed from his royal throne, and his glory was taken away.

## Daniel 5:21

He was driven away from people, and his mind became like an animal’s. 📖

He lived with wild donkeys and ate grass like an ox.

His body was soaked with heaven’s dew until he acknowledged that the Most High God rules the human kingdom.

God puts anyone He wishes over it.

**Help with “dew”:** This is water that settles on things outdoors.

## Daniel 5:22

But you, his son Belshazzar, have not humbled your heart, even though you knew all this. 📖

## Daniel 5:23

Instead, you have lifted yourself against the Lord of heaven. 📖

His temple’s vessels were brought to you.

You, your nobles, wives, and concubines drank wine from them.

You praised gods of silver, gold, bronze, iron, wood, and stone that cannot see, hear, or understand.

But you have not glorified the God who holds your breath and all your ways in His hand.

## Daniel 5:24

So He sent the hand that wrote the message. 📖

## Daniel 5:25

This is the writing: MENE, MENE, TEKEL, PARSIN. 📖

## Daniel 5:26

This is the message’s meaning. 📖

MENE means God has counted the days of your rule and brought it to an end.

## Daniel 5:27

TEKEL means you have been weighed on the scales and found lacking. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The weighing pictures God’s judgment of the king.

## Daniel 5:28

PERES means your kingdom has been divided and given to the Medes and Persians.” 📖

**About the words:** The writing says “PARSIN.”

Daniel uses “PERES” when explaining this part of the message.

## Daniel 5:29

Belshazzar gave the order to dress Daniel in purple and put a gold chain around his neck. 📖

They announced him as the kingdom’s third highest ruler.

## Daniel 5:30

That very night, Belshazzar, king of the Chaldeans, was killed. 📖

## Daniel 5:31

Darius the Mede received the kingdom when he was sixty-two years old. 📖
