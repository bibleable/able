# Daniel 9 — Daniel’s prayer and Gabriel’s answer

**About this chapter**

Daniel understands Jeremiah’s message about seventy years and prays for Jerusalem.

His prayer confesses sin and appeals to God’s compassion.

Gabriel gives a message about seventy weeks, the Messiah, and coming destruction.

## Daniel 9:1

It was the first year of Darius, son of Xerxes, a Mede by descent. 📖

He had been made ruler over the Chaldeans’ kingdom.

## Daniel 9:2

In that first year, I, Daniel, understood something from the sacred books. 📖

The LORD had spoken through Jeremiah the prophet.

Jerusalem’s ruin would last seventy years.

## Daniel 9:3

I turned to the Lord God, seeking Him with prayer and appeals. 📖

I fasted and used sackcloth and ashes.

**Help with these actions:** Fasting means going without food.

Sackcloth is rough cloth.

Sackcloth and ashes were signs of grief and sorrow for wrongdoing.

## Daniel 9:4

I prayed to the LORD my God and confessed. 📖

I said, “Lord, You are the great and awesome God.

You keep Your covenant of faithful love with those who love You and obey Your commands.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Daniel 9:5

We have sinned and done wrong. 📖

We have acted wickedly and rebelled.

We have turned away from Your commands and rules.

## Daniel 9:6

We have not listened to Your servants the prophets. 📖

They spoke in Your name to our kings, leaders, ancestors, and all the land’s people.

## Daniel 9:7

Lord, You are righteous, but today we are covered with shame. 📖

This includes Judah’s men, Jerusalem’s people, and all Israel, near and far.

We are in all the countries where You drove us because we were unfaithful to You.

**Help with “righteous”:** This means completely right and just.

## Daniel 9:8

LORD, we are covered with shame, including our kings, leaders, and ancestors. 📖

We have sinned against You.

## Daniel 9:9

The Lord our God has compassion and forgiveness, even though we have rebelled against Him 📖

## Daniel 9:10

and have not obeyed the LORD our God. 📖

We have not followed the laws He gave us through His servants the prophets.

## Daniel 9:11

All Israel has broken Your law and turned away, refusing to obey Your voice. 📖

So the curse and sworn warning written in Moses’ Law have been poured over us.

Moses was God’s servant.

This happened because we sinned against You.

## Daniel 9:12

You carried out the words spoken against us and our rulers by bringing great disaster on us. 📖

Nothing under all heaven has ever happened like what happened to Jerusalem.

## Daniel 9:13

All this disaster came on us, just as Moses’ Law says. 📖

Yet we have not sought the LORD our God’s favor.

We have not turned from our sins or paid attention to Your truth.

## Daniel 9:14

So the LORD kept this disaster ready and brought it on us. 📖

The LORD our God is righteous in everything He does.

Yet we have not obeyed His voice.

## Daniel 9:15

Lord our God, You brought Your people out of Egypt with a mighty hand. 📖

You made Your name famous, as it still is today.

We have sinned and acted wickedly.

## Daniel 9:16

Lord, in keeping with all Your righteous acts, turn Your fierce anger away from Jerusalem, Your city and holy mountain. 📖

Our sins and our ancestors’ wrong actions have made Jerusalem and Your people a disgrace to everyone around us.

## Daniel 9:17

Now, our God, hear Your servant’s prayers and appeals. 📖

Lord, for Your sake, let Your face shine on Your ruined holy place.

**Help with “let Your face shine”:** This asks God to show favor.

## Daniel 9:18

My God, listen and hear! 📖

Open Your eyes and see the ruin of the city bearing Your name.

We bring our appeals because of Your great compassion, not because of our own righteous acts.

## Daniel 9:19

Lord, listen! 📖

Lord, forgive!

Lord, hear and act!

My God, for Your sake, do not delay.

Your city and Your people bear Your name.”

## Daniel 9:20

I was speaking and praying, confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel. 📖

I was making my appeal to the LORD my God about His holy mountain.

## Daniel 9:21

While I was still praying, Gabriel came to me in swift flight. 📖

He was the man I had seen in the earlier vision.

It was about the time of the evening sacrifice.

## Daniel 9:22

He instructed me, saying, “Daniel, I have now come to give you insight and understanding. 📖

## Daniel 9:23

When you began your appeals, an answer went out. 📖

I have come to tell you, because you are highly precious.

Consider the message and understand the vision.

## Daniel 9:24

Seventy weeks have been decided for your people and your holy city. 📖

They are to stop their rebellion and end sin.

They are to make atonement for wrongdoing and bring everlasting righteousness.

They are to seal up vision and prophecy and anoint the Most Holy Place.

**Help with these words:** Atonement deals with wrongdoing and makes things right before God.

Righteousness means what is right and just.

Anointing sets something apart for a holy purpose.

**About this prophecy:** The draft keeps “weeks” and the full list of purposes.

It does not turn the periods into calendar dates.

## Daniel 9:25

Know and understand this. 📖

The order to restore and rebuild Jerusalem begins the period.

Until the Messiah, the Prince, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks.

Jerusalem will be rebuilt with streets and a trench, but in troubled times.

**Help with “Messiah”:** This means the Anointed One.

**About the periods:** Both seven weeks and sixty-two weeks are kept as stated.

## Daniel 9:26

After the sixty-two weeks, the Messiah will be cut off and have nothing. 📖

The people of the coming prince will destroy the city and holy place.

The end will come like a flood.

There will be war until the end.

Ruin has been decreed.

**About “cut off”:** The draft keeps this expression from the source.

It does not add details about how this happens.

## Daniel 9:27

He will confirm a covenant with many for one week. 📖

In the middle of the week, he will end sacrifice and offering.

On the temple’s wing will come the horrible thing that causes ruin.

This will continue until the decreed destruction is poured over him.”

**About this difficult passage:** The source uses “he” without naming him here.

The “horrible thing” is BSB’s “abomination that causes desolation.”

The draft keeps the temple-wing picture and does not add an identity or date.
