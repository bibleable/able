# Daniel 11 — Kings of the North and South

**About this chapter**

The speaker continues the message about coming rulers and conflicts.

The passage includes alliances, betrayal, attacks on worship, and suffering among the faithful.

The draft follows its titles and pronouns without adding historical names.

## Daniel 11:1

The speaker continued, “In the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood up to strengthen and protect him. 📖

**About “him”:** The draft keeps the source’s pronoun without adding a name.

## Daniel 11:2

Now I will tell you the truth. 📖

Three more kings will rise in Persia.

A fourth will be far richer than all the others.

By his wealth’s power, he will stir up everyone against Greece’s kingdom.

## Daniel 11:3

Then a mighty king will rise. 📖

He will rule with great authority and do whatever he pleases.

## Daniel 11:4

But as soon as he is established, his kingdom will break apart toward heaven’s four winds. 📖

It will not go to his descendants or have the authority he had.

His kingdom will be uprooted and given to others.

## Daniel 11:5

The king of the South will grow strong. 📖

But one of his commanders will become even stronger.

He will rule his own kingdom with great authority.

## Daniel 11:6

After some years, they will form an alliance. 📖

The king of the South’s daughter will go to the king of the North to confirm the agreement.

But his daughter will not keep her powerful position, and his strength will not last.

She will be given up, with her royal escort, her father, and the one who supported her.

## Daniel 11:7

But someone from her family will rise in his place. 📖

He will attack the king of the North’s army and enter his fortress, fighting and winning.

## Daniel 11:8

He will take their gods captive to Egypt, with their metal images and valuable silver and gold vessels. 📖

For some years, he will stay away from the king of the North.

## Daniel 11:9

The king of the North will invade the king of the South’s kingdom. 📖

Then he will return to his own land.

## Daniel 11:10

His sons will stir up conflict and gather a great army. 📖

It will advance forcefully, sweeping through like a flood.

Again, it will carry the battle as far as his fortress.

## Daniel 11:11

The king of the South will march out in rage to fight the king of the North. 📖

The king of the North will raise a large army.

But it will be handed over to his enemy.

## Daniel 11:12

When the army is carried away, the king of the South will become proud. 📖

He will strike down tens of thousands.

But he will not win in the end.

## Daniel 11:13

The king of the North will raise another army, larger than the first. 📖

After some years, he will advance with a great army and many supplies.

## Daniel 11:14

At that time, many will rise against the king of the South. 📖

Violent people among your own people will raise themselves up in fulfillment of the vision.

But they will fail.

## Daniel 11:15

The king of the North will come, build an attack ramp, and capture a city with strong defenses. 📖

The South’s forces will not stand.

Even their best troops will not be able to resist.

## Daniel 11:16

The attacker will do whatever he pleases. 📖

No one will stand against him.

He will establish himself in the Beautiful Land, with destruction in his hand.

## Daniel 11:17

He will decide to come with his whole kingdom’s strength and reach an agreement with the South’s king. 📖

He will give him a daughter in marriage to overthrow the kingdom.

But his plan will not succeed or help him.

## Daniel 11:18

Then he will turn toward the coastlands and capture many. 📖

But a commander will end the disgrace he causes and turn it back on him.

## Daniel 11:19

Then he will turn toward his own land’s fortresses. 📖

But he will stumble and fall and be no more.

## Daniel 11:20

Someone will rise in his place and send out a tax collector for the kingdom’s glory. 📖

Within a few days, he will be destroyed, but not in anger or battle.

## Daniel 11:21

A hateful person will rise in his place without being given royal honors. 📖

He will come in a time of peace and seize the kingdom through secret plots.

## Daniel 11:22

A flood of forces will be swept away before him and destroyed. 📖

A prince of the covenant will also be destroyed.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Daniel 11:23

After an alliance is made with him, he will act deceitfully. 📖

He will rise to power with only a few people.

## Daniel 11:24

In a time of peace, he will invade the richest provinces. 📖

He will do what his fathers and ancestors never did.

He will give his followers large amounts of stolen goods and wealth.

He will plot against defended places, but only for a time.

## Daniel 11:25

With a large army, he will stir up his power and courage against the king of the South. 📖

The South’s king will gather a very large, powerful army.

But he will not withstand the plots against him.

## Daniel 11:26

Those eating his provisions will try to destroy him. 📖

His army will be swept away, and many will be killed.

## Daniel 11:27

The two kings will sit at the same table and tell lies, their hearts set on evil. 📖

But it will achieve nothing.

The end will still come at the appointed time.

## Daniel 11:28

The king of the North will return to his land with great wealth. 📖

But his heart will be against the holy covenant.

He will do damage and return to his own land.

## Daniel 11:29

At the appointed time, he will invade the South again. 📖

But this time will be different from the first.

## Daniel 11:30

Ships of Kittim will come against him, and he will lose courage. 📖

He will turn back in rage against the holy covenant and do damage.

He will return and favor those who abandon the holy covenant.

## Daniel 11:31

His forces will rise and make the temple fortress unholy. 📖

They will end the daily sacrifice and set up the horrible thing that causes ruin.

**Help with the description:** BSB calls this “the abomination of desolation.”

## Daniel 11:32

He will use flattering words to corrupt those who break the covenant. 📖

But the people who know their God will firmly resist him.

## Daniel 11:33

Those with insight will teach many. 📖

But for a time, they will fall by sword or fire, or be captured or robbed.

## Daniel 11:34

When they fall, they will receive a little help. 📖

But many will join them without being sincere.

## Daniel 11:35

Some of the wise will fall, so they may be refined, purified, and made spotless until the end time. 📖

It will still come at the appointed time.

**Help with the pictures:** Refining removes waste from metal.

Purifying and removing spots picture making people clean before God.

## Daniel 11:36

The king will do whatever he pleases. 📖

He will lift himself above every god and speak monstrous things against the God of gods.

He will succeed until the time of anger is complete.

What has been decreed must happen.

## Daniel 11:37

He will disregard his ancestors’ gods, the one desired by women, and every other god. 📖

He will make himself greater than them all.

**About “the one desired by women”:** The draft keeps this description without assigning a name.

## Daniel 11:38

Instead, he will honor a god of fortresses with gold, silver, precious stones, and riches. 📖

His ancestors did not know this god.

## Daniel 11:39

He will attack the strongest fortresses with a foreign god’s help. 📖

He will greatly honor those who acknowledge him.

He will make them rulers over many and distribute land for a price.

## Daniel 11:40

At the time of the end, the king of the South will fight him. 📖

But the king of the North will storm against him with chariots, horsemen, and many ships.

He will invade many countries and sweep through like a flood.

**Help with “chariots”:** These are vehicles pulled by horses and used in war.

## Daniel 11:41

He will also invade the Beautiful Land, and many countries will fall. 📖

But Edom, Moab, and the leaders of the Ammonites will escape his power.

## Daniel 11:42

He will extend his power over many countries. 📖

Not even Egypt will escape.

## Daniel 11:43

He will control the gold and silver treasures and all Egypt’s riches. 📖

The Libyans and Cushites will also submit to him.

## Daniel 11:44

But news from the east and north will alarm him. 📖

He will go out in great fury to destroy many and devote them to destruction.

## Daniel 11:45

He will set up his royal tents between the sea and the beautiful holy mountain. 📖

But he will reach his end with no one to help him.”
