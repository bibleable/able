# Daniel 1 — Daniel and his friends in Babylon

**About this chapter**

Daniel and three friends from Judah are taken into the king’s training program in Babylon.

They receive new names.

Daniel asks to avoid food and wine that would make him unclean before God.

## Daniel 1:1

In the third year of Jehoiakim’s rule as king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem. 📖

He surrounded the city to attack it.

## Daniel 1:2

The Lord handed Jehoiakim, king of Judah, over to him, with some objects from God’s temple. 📖

Nebuchadnezzar took them to Shinar, to his god’s temple.

He put them in his god’s treasure store.

## Daniel 1:3

The king ordered Ashpenaz, the head of his court officials, to bring some Israelites to him. 📖

They were to come from the royal family and leading families.

## Daniel 1:4

They must be handsome young men without any defect, gifted in all wisdom and knowledge. 📖

They must understand quickly and be qualified to serve in the king’s palace.

They were to learn the Chaldeans’ language and writings.

**About these requirements:** These are the king’s selection rules in the account.

They do not measure a person’s worth.

## Daniel 1:5

The king gave them a daily share of his food and wine. 📖

They were to train for three years and then enter his service.

## Daniel 1:6

Among these young men were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, from Judah. 📖

## Daniel 1:7

The chief official gave them new names. 📖

He named Daniel Belteshazzar and Hananiah Shadrach.

He named Mishael Meshach and Azariah Abednego.

## Daniel 1:8

Daniel decided not to make himself unclean with the king’s food or wine. 📖

He asked the chief official for permission to avoid this.

**About “unclean”:** Here this concerns Daniel’s faithfulness to God.

## Daniel 1:9

God had made the chief official show Daniel kindness and compassion. 📖

## Daniel 1:10

But the official told Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who chose your food and drink. 📖

Why should he see you looking thinner than the other young men your age?

You would put my life in danger before the king!”

## Daniel 1:11

The chief official had appointed a steward over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. 📖

Daniel spoke to this steward.

**Help with “steward”:** This is the man put in charge of their care.

## Daniel 1:12

Daniel said, “Please test us, your servants, for ten days. 📖

Give us only vegetables to eat and water to drink.

## Daniel 1:13

Then compare us with the young men who eat the king’s food. 📖

Deal with us, your servants, according to what you see.”

## Daniel 1:14

The steward agreed and tested them for ten days. 📖

## Daniel 1:15

At the end of ten days, they looked healthier and better fed than all those eating the king’s food. 📖

## Daniel 1:16

So the steward kept withholding their rich food and wine. 📖

He gave them vegetables instead.

## Daniel 1:17

God gave these four young men knowledge and understanding in every kind of writing and wisdom. 📖

Daniel also understood all kinds of visions and dreams.

## Daniel 1:18

When the king’s set time ended, the chief official brought them before Nebuchadnezzar. 📖

## Daniel 1:19

The king spoke with them. 📖

Among all the young men, he found none equal to Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.

So they entered the king’s service.

## Daniel 1:20

The king asked them about every matter of wisdom and understanding. 📖

He found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters in his whole kingdom.

**Help with these words:** Magicians and enchanters claimed special powers and knowledge.

## Daniel 1:21

Daniel remained there until the first year of King Cyrus. 📖
