# Daniel 3 — The blazing furnace

**About this chapter**

Nebuchadnezzar orders everyone to worship his golden statue.

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refuse, even under threat of death.

God rescues them from the furnace.

## Daniel 3:1

King Nebuchadnezzar made a golden statue sixty cubits high and six cubits wide. 📖

He set it up on the plain of Dura in Babylon’s province.

**Help with “cubit”:** This measures from elbow to fingertips.

## Daniel 3:2

King Nebuchadnezzar called the satraps, prefects, governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, magistrates, and all other provincial officials. 📖

They were to attend the ceremony for the statue he had set up.

**Help with the titles:** These are different kinds of officials and rulers.

Treasurers manage money.

Judges and magistrates deal with legal matters.

## Daniel 3:3

The satraps, prefects, governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, magistrates, and all provincial rulers gathered for the statue’s ceremony. 📖

King Nebuchadnezzar had set it up.

They stood before it.

## Daniel 3:4

The announcer called loudly, “People of every nation and language, this is your command. 📖

## Daniel 3:5

When you hear the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and all kinds of music, fall down and worship. 📖

Worship the golden statue King Nebuchadnezzar has set up.

**Help with the instruments:** Horns, flutes, and pipes are blown to make music.

Zithers, lyres, and harps have strings.

## Daniel 3:6

Anyone who does not fall down and worship will immediately be thrown into the blazing furnace.” 📖

**Help with “furnace”:** This is a very hot oven.

## Daniel 3:7

The people heard the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, and all kinds of music. 📖

People of every nation and language fell down and worshiped the golden statue King Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

## Daniel 3:8

At that time, some astrologers came forward and accused the Jews with harmful intent. 📖

**Help with “astrologers”:** These people sought messages in the stars.

## Daniel 3:9

They told King Nebuchadnezzar, “King, may you live forever! 📖

## Daniel 3:10

King, you ordered everyone hearing the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and all kinds of music to worship. 📖

They must fall down and worship the golden statue.

## Daniel 3:11

You said anyone refusing to fall down and worship would be thrown into the blazing furnace. 📖

## Daniel 3:12

But some Jews you put in charge of Babylon’s province have ignored you, king. 📖

They are Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.

They refuse to serve your gods or worship the golden statue you set up.”

## Daniel 3:13

Nebuchadnezzar was furious. 📖

He ordered Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to be brought in.

They were brought before him.

## Daniel 3:14

Nebuchadnezzar said, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, is this true? 📖

Do you refuse to serve my gods or worship the golden statue I set up?

## Daniel 3:15

Now you will hear the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and all kinds of music. 📖

If you are ready to fall down and worship my statue, very good.

But if you refuse, you will immediately be thrown into the blazing furnace.

Then what god can rescue you from my hands?”

## Daniel 3:16

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego replied, “Nebuchadnezzar, we do not need to answer you about this. 📖

## Daniel 3:17

If the God we serve exists, He can rescue us from the blazing furnace and from your hand, king. 📖

## Daniel 3:18

But even if He does not, know this, king. 📖

We will not serve your gods or worship the golden statue you set up.”

**About their answer:** They refuse to worship the statue even if God does not rescue them.

Verse 17 keeps BSB’s wording about God’s existence.

## Daniel 3:19

Nebuchadnezzar became full of rage. 📖

His expression toward Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego changed.

He ordered the furnace heated seven times hotter than usual.

## Daniel 3:20

He ordered some strong, brave men in his army to tie up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. 📖

They were to throw them into the blazing furnace.

## Daniel 3:21

The three men were tied up in their robes, trousers, turbans, and other clothes. 📖

They were thrown into the blazing furnace.

**Help with “turbans”:** These are cloth head coverings.

## Daniel 3:22

The king’s order was urgent, and the furnace was extremely hot. 📖

The flames killed the men carrying Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego up to it.

## Daniel 3:23

The three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell into the blazing furnace, tied tightly. 📖

## Daniel 3:24

King Nebuchadnezzar suddenly jumped up in amazement. 📖

He asked his advisers, “Did we not throw three men, tied tightly, into the fire?”

They replied, “Certainly, king.”

## Daniel 3:25

He exclaimed, “Look! 📖

I see four men walking in the fire, untied and unharmed!

The fourth looks like a son of the gods!”

**About the fourth figure:** These are the king’s words.

The draft does not add another identity.

## Daniel 3:26

Nebuchadnezzar approached the blazing furnace’s door. 📖

He called, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out!”

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego came out of the fire.

## Daniel 3:27

The satraps, prefects, governors, and royal advisers gathered around. 📖

They saw that the fire had not harmed the men’s bodies.

Not a hair on their heads was burned.

Their robes were unharmed, and they did not smell of fire.

## Daniel 3:28

Nebuchadnezzar declared, “Praise the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego! 📖

He sent His angel and rescued His servants who trusted Him.

They disobeyed the king’s order and risked their lives.

They would serve or worship no god except their own God.

## Daniel 3:29

So I give this order for people of every nation and language. 📖

Anyone saying anything offensive against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego will be cut into pieces.

Their houses will become broken ruins.

No other god can rescue in this way.”

## Daniel 3:30

The king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in Babylon’s province. 📖
