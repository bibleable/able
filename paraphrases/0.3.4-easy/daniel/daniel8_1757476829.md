# Daniel 8 — The ram, the goat, and the little horn

**About this chapter**

Daniel sees a ram, a goat, and a horn in a vision.

Gabriel explains the vision.

The draft keeps the nations named in the explanation and does not add names for other figures.

## Daniel 8:1

In King Belshazzar’s third year, another vision appeared to me, Daniel. 📖

It came after the earlier vision.

## Daniel 8:2

In the vision, I saw myself in Susa’s fortress, in the province of Elam. 📖

I was beside the Ulai Canal.

## Daniel 8:3

I looked up and saw a male sheep with two long horns standing beside the canal. 📖

One horn was longer than the other.

The longer horn grew later.

## Daniel 8:4

I saw the male sheep charge west, north, and south. 📖

No animal could stand against him or be rescued from his power.

He did whatever he pleased and became great.

## Daniel 8:5

As I considered this, a goat suddenly came from the west. 📖

He crossed the whole earth without touching the ground.

A large, noticeable horn was between his eyes.

## Daniel 8:6

He came toward the two-horned male sheep I had seen beside the canal. 📖

He rushed at him with furious power.

## Daniel 8:7

I saw the goat approach in rage, strike the male sheep, and break his two horns. 📖

The male sheep had no power to resist.

The goat threw him down and trampled him.

No one could rescue the male sheep from the goat’s power.

## Daniel 8:8

The goat became very great. 📖

But at the height of his power, his large horn broke off.

Four large, noticeable horns grew in its place toward heaven’s four winds.

## Daniel 8:9

A little horn grew from one of these horns. 📖

It grew greatly toward the south, the east, and the Beautiful Land.

**About “the Beautiful Land”:** The draft keeps this title from the vision.

## Daniel 8:10

It grew as high as heaven’s army. 📖

It threw some of that army and some stars to the earth and trampled them.

## Daniel 8:11

It made itself great, even against the army’s Prince. 📖

It removed His daily sacrifice and overthrew His holy place.

## Daniel 8:12

In the rebellion, the army and daily sacrifice were handed over to the horn. 📖

It threw truth to the ground and succeeded in everything it did.

## Daniel 8:13

I heard a holy one speaking. 📖

Another holy one asked him, “How long until this vision is fulfilled?

It concerns the daily sacrifice, the rebellion causing ruin, and the surrender of the holy place and army.

They are handed over to be trampled.”

## Daniel 8:14

He told me, “It will take 2,300 evenings and mornings. 📖

Then the holy place will be properly restored.”

## Daniel 8:15

I, Daniel, watched the vision and tried to understand. 📖

Someone looking like a man stood before me.

## Daniel 8:16

I heard a man’s voice call from between the Ulai’s banks. 📖

It said, “Gabriel, explain the vision to this man.”

## Daniel 8:17

Gabriel came near where I stood. 📖

I was terrified and fell with my face to the ground.

He said, “Son of man, understand that the vision concerns the time of the end.”

## Daniel 8:18

As he spoke, I fell into a deep sleep with my face to the ground. 📖

He touched me and helped me stand.

## Daniel 8:19

He said, “Look! 📖

I will tell you what will happen in the later time of anger.

This concerns the appointed time of the end.

## Daniel 8:20

The male sheep with two horns represents the kings of Media and Persia. 📖

## Daniel 8:21

The shaggy goat represents the king of Greece. 📖

The large horn between his eyes is the first king.

## Daniel 8:22

The four horns that replaced the broken horn represent four kingdoms. 📖

They will rise from that nation but will not have the same power.

## Daniel 8:23

In the later part of their rule, the rebellion will reach its full measure. 📖

Then a boldly disrespectful king, skilled in secret plots, will come to the throne.

## Daniel 8:24

His power will be great, but it will not be his own. 📖

He will cause terrible destruction and succeed in everything he does.

He will destroy mighty men and the holy people.

## Daniel 8:25

By his clever plans and actions, he will make deceit succeed. 📖

In his own mind, he will make himself great.

In a time of peace, he will destroy many.

He will even stand against the Prince of princes.

Yet he will be broken, but not by human hands.

## Daniel 8:26

The vision about evenings and mornings is true. 📖

Seal up the vision, because it concerns the distant future.”

**Help with “seal up”:** This means closing and keeping the vision safely.

## Daniel 8:27

I, Daniel, was exhausted and lay sick for days. 📖

Then I got up and did the king’s work.

The vision confused me.

It was beyond understanding.
