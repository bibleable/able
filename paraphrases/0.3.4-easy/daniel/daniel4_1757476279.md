# Daniel 4 — Nebuchadnezzar’s dream and humbling

**About this chapter**

King Nebuchadnezzar tells what happened to him.

Daniel explains a dream about a great tree.

The king loses his rule and later praises the King of heaven.

## Daniel 4:1

King Nebuchadnezzar sends this message to people of every nation and language throughout the earth. 📖

May your prosperity increase!

## Daniel 4:2

I am pleased to tell you the signs and wonders the Most High God has done for me. 📖

## Daniel 4:3

How great are His signs! 📖

How mighty are His wonders!

His kingdom lasts forever.

His rule continues from generation to generation.

## Daniel 4:4

I, Nebuchadnezzar, was comfortable at home and prospering in my palace. 📖

## Daniel 4:5

I had a dream that frightened me. 📖

As I lay in bed, the pictures and visions in my mind alarmed me.

## Daniel 4:6

I ordered all Babylon’s wise men brought before me to explain the dream’s meaning. 📖

## Daniel 4:7

The magicians, enchanters, astrologers, and diviners came in. 📖

I told them the dream, but they could not explain it.

**Help with these words:** These people claimed special powers or knowledge about hidden things and the future.

## Daniel 4:8

Finally, Daniel came before me. 📖

His name is Belteshazzar, after my god’s name.

The spirit of the holy gods is in him.

I told him the dream.

**About the speaker:** Nebuchadnezzar is describing Daniel using his own beliefs.

## Daniel 4:9

I said, “Belteshazzar, chief of the magicians, I know the spirit of the holy gods is in you. 📖

No mystery confuses you.

Tell me the visions I saw in my dream and what they mean.

## Daniel 4:10

As I lay in bed, I saw this vision in my mind. 📖

A very tall tree stood in the middle of the land.

## Daniel 4:11

The tree grew large and strong. 📖

Its top reached the sky.

It could be seen to the ends of the earth.

## Daniel 4:12

Its leaves were beautiful, and it had much fruit. 📖

It had food for all.

Animals of the field sheltered under it, and birds nested in its branches.

Every creature was fed from it.

## Daniel 4:13

As I lay in bed, I saw a watcher, a holy one, coming down from heaven. 📖

**Help with “watcher”:** This is a heavenly being in the vision.

## Daniel 4:14

He shouted loudly, ‘Cut down the tree and chop off its branches! 📖

Strip off its leaves and scatter its fruit.

Let the animals flee from under it and the birds from its branches.

## Daniel 4:15

But leave the stump and its roots in the ground, with a band of iron and bronze around it. 📖

Leave it in the field’s tender grass.

Let him be soaked with heaven’s dew and eat the earth’s grass with the animals.

**About the picture:** The words change from a tree to a person.

Dew is water that settles on things outdoors.

## Daniel 4:16

Let his mind change from a human mind to an animal’s mind. 📖

Let seven times pass over him.

**About “seven times”:** The draft keeps this expression without changing it to a different time unit.

## Daniel 4:17

This decision is the watchers’ order, the judgment announced by the holy ones. 📖

Then the living will know that the Most High rules the human kingdom.

He gives it to anyone He wishes.

He puts even the lowliest people over it.’

## Daniel 4:18

That is the dream I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw. 📖

Now, Belteshazzar, tell me its meaning.

None of my kingdom’s wise men can explain it.

But you can, because the spirit of the holy gods is in you.”

## Daniel 4:19

Daniel, also called Belteshazzar, was puzzled for a time, and his thoughts alarmed him. 📖

The king said, “Belteshazzar, do not let the dream or its meaning alarm you.”

Belteshazzar replied, “My lord, may the dream concern those who hate you and its meaning concern your enemies!

## Daniel 4:20

You saw a tree grow large and strong. 📖

Its top reached the sky, and it could be seen throughout the earth.

## Daniel 4:21

Its leaves were beautiful, and it had much fruit, giving food for all. 📖

Animals lived beneath it, and birds nested in its branches.

## Daniel 4:22

King, you are that tree! 📖

You have become great and strong.

Your greatness has grown to reach the sky, and your rule reaches the ends of the earth.

## Daniel 4:23

King, you saw a watcher, a holy one, coming down from heaven. 📖

He said, ‘Cut down the tree and destroy it.

But leave the stump and roots in the ground, with a band of iron and bronze around it.

Leave it in the field’s tender grass.

Let him be soaked with heaven’s dew and eat with the field’s animals until seven times pass over him.’

## Daniel 4:24

King, this is the meaning. 📖

This is the order the Most High has given against my lord the king.

## Daniel 4:25

You will be driven away from people to live with the animals of the field. 📖

You will eat grass like an ox and be soaked with heaven’s dew.

Seven times will pass over you until you acknowledge that the Most High rules the human kingdom.

He gives it to anyone He wishes.

## Daniel 4:26

The command left the tree’s stump and roots. 📖

Your kingdom will be restored as soon as you acknowledge that Heaven rules.

## Daniel 4:27

King, please accept my advice. 📖

Break away from your sins by doing right.

Break away from your wrong actions by showing mercy to the oppressed.

Perhaps your prosperity will last longer.”

## Daniel 4:28

All this happened to King Nebuchadnezzar. 📖

## Daniel 4:29

Twelve months later, he was walking on the roof of Babylon’s royal palace. 📖

## Daniel 4:30

The king exclaimed, “Is this not Babylon the Great? 📖

I built it as a royal home by my mighty power, for the glory of my majesty!”

## Daniel 4:31

While the king was still speaking, a voice came from heaven. 📖

It said, “King Nebuchadnezzar, this is the decision about you.

Your kingdom has been taken from you.

## Daniel 4:32

You will be driven away from people to live with the animals of the field. 📖

You will eat grass like an ox.

Seven times will pass over you until you acknowledge that the Most High rules the human kingdom.

He gives it to anyone He wishes.”

## Daniel 4:33

The sentence against Nebuchadnezzar happened at once. 📖

He was driven away from people and ate grass like an ox.

His body was soaked with heaven’s dew.

His hair grew like an eagle’s feathers, and his nails like a bird’s claws.

## Daniel 4:34

At the end of those days, I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked up to heaven, and my reason returned. 📖

I praised, honored, and glorified the Most High, who lives forever.

His rule lasts forever.

His kingdom continues from generation to generation.

**About “my reason returned”:** The king says his ability to think clearly was restored.

The account does not name a medical condition.

## Daniel 4:35

All earth’s peoples count as nothing. 📖

He does as He pleases with heaven’s army and earth’s peoples.

No one can hold back His hand or ask Him, ‘What have You done?’

## Daniel 4:36

When my reason returned, my honor and splendor also returned for my kingdom’s glory. 📖

My advisers and nobles came looking for me.

I was restored to my throne and became even greater.

## Daniel 4:37

Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise, honor, and glorify the King of heaven. 📖

All His works are true, and all His ways are fair.

He can humble those who walk in pride.
