# Daniel 10 — The messenger and the coming conflict

**About this chapter**

Daniel receives a true message about a great conflict.

He describes mourning, a vision, weakness, and renewed strength.

The messenger speaks about opposition from princes and help from Michael.

## Daniel 10:1

In Cyrus’s third year as king of Persia, a message was revealed to Daniel, also called Belteshazzar. 📖

The message was true and concerned a great conflict.

He was given understanding of it in a vision.

## Daniel 10:2

At that time, I, Daniel, mourned for three full weeks. 📖

## Daniel 10:3

I ate no rich food, meat, or wine. 📖

I put no oil on myself until the three weeks ended.

## Daniel 10:4

On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I stood beside the great river, the Tigris. 📖

## Daniel 10:5

I looked up and saw a man dressed in linen. 📖

A belt of fine gold from Uphaz was around his waist.

**Help with “linen”:** This is cloth made from the flax plant.

## Daniel 10:6

His body was like beryl, and his face shone like lightning. 📖

His eyes were like flaming torches.

His arms and legs shone like polished bronze.

His voice sounded like a great crowd.

**Help with “beryl”:** This is a precious stone.

## Daniel 10:7

Only I, Daniel, saw the vision. 📖

The men with me did not see it.

But great terror came over them, and they ran away and hid.

## Daniel 10:8

I was left alone, looking at this great vision. 📖

I had no strength left.

My face turned deathly pale, and I was powerless.

## Daniel 10:9

I heard his words. 📖

As I listened, I fell into a deep sleep with my face to the ground.

## Daniel 10:10

Suddenly, a hand touched me and raised me onto my hands and knees, trembling. 📖

## Daniel 10:11

He told me, “Daniel, you are highly precious. 📖

Carefully consider the words I am about to tell you.

Stand up, because I have now been sent to you.”

When he said this, I stood up trembling.

## Daniel 10:12

He said, “Do not be afraid, Daniel. 📖

From the first day you decided to understand and humble yourself before your God, your words were heard.

I have come in answer to them.

## Daniel 10:13

But the prince of Persia’s kingdom opposed me for twenty-one days. 📖

Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me.

I had been left there with Persia’s kings.

**About the princes:** The passage describes a conflict involving these rulers and the messenger.

The draft does not add names or details about their identity.

## Daniel 10:14

Now I have come to explain what will happen to your people in the later days. 📖

The vision concerns those days.”

## Daniel 10:15

While he spoke, I turned my face toward the ground and could not speak. 📖

## Daniel 10:16

Suddenly, someone looking like a man touched my lips. 📖

I opened my mouth and spoke to the one standing before me.

I said, “My lord, the vision has overcome me with distress, and I have no strength.

## Daniel 10:17

How can I, your servant, speak with you, my lord? 📖

I have no strength now, and no breath is left in me.”

## Daniel 10:18

The one looking like a man touched me again and strengthened me. 📖

## Daniel 10:19

He said, “Do not be afraid, you who are highly precious. 📖

Peace be with you!

Be strong now!

Be very strong!”

As he spoke, I was strengthened.

I said, “Speak, my lord, because you have strengthened me.”

## Daniel 10:20

He asked, “Do you know why I have come? 📖

I must return at once to fight Persia’s prince.

When I have gone out, Greece’s prince will come.

## Daniel 10:21

But first, I will tell you what is written in the Book of Truth. 📖

No one has the courage to support me against these princes except Michael, your prince.”
