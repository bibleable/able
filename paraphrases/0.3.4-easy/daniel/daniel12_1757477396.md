# Daniel 12 — The time of the end

**About this chapter**

The message speaks of great distress, deliverance, and awakening from death.

Daniel hears more about the time of the end.

The stated time periods and the promise to Daniel are kept.

## Daniel 12:1

The speaker said, “At that time, Michael will rise. 📖

He is the great prince who watches over your people.

There will be a time of distress unlike anything since nations began.

But then your people will be rescued, everyone whose name is found written in the book.

## Daniel 12:2

Many sleeping in the earth’s dust will awake. 📖

Some will awake to everlasting life.

Others will awake to shame and everlasting scorn.

**Help with “sleeping”:** This pictures people who have died.

## Daniel 12:3

The wise will shine like the brightness of heaven. 📖

Those who lead many to righteousness will shine like stars forever and ever.

**Help with “righteousness”:** This means what is right and just.

## Daniel 12:4

But Daniel, close up these words and seal the book until the time of the end. 📖

Many will travel back and forth, and knowledge will increase.”

## Daniel 12:5

I, Daniel, looked and saw two others standing there. 📖

One stood on this riverbank and the other on the opposite bank.

## Daniel 12:6

One asked the man dressed in linen, who was above the river’s waters, a question. 📖

He said, “How long until these wonders are fulfilled?”

## Daniel 12:7

The man in linen above the river raised both his right and left hands toward heaven. 📖

I heard him swear by the One who lives forever.

He said, “It will be for a time, times, and half a time.

When the holy people’s power has finally been shattered, all these things will be completed.”

**About the time:** The draft keeps this expression without converting it into a calendar date.

## Daniel 12:8

I heard, but I did not understand. 📖

I asked, “My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?”

## Daniel 12:9

He replied, “Go on your way, Daniel. 📖

The words are closed and sealed until the time of the end.

## Daniel 12:10

Many will be purified, made spotless, and refined. 📖

But the wicked will continue doing wicked things.

None of the wicked will understand.

But the wise will understand.

**Help with these pictures:** Removing spots and refining picture being made clean before God.

## Daniel 12:11

When the daily sacrifice ends and the horrible thing causing ruin is set up, the period begins. 📖

It will last 1,290 days.

**Help with the description:** BSB calls this “the abomination of desolation.”

## Daniel 12:12

Blessed is the one who waits and reaches the end of the 1,335 days. 📖

## Daniel 12:13

But you, go on your way until the end. 📖

You will rest.

Then you will rise to your inheritance at the end of the days.”

**Help with “inheritance”:** This means the share promised to Daniel.
