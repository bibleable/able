# Daniel 6 — Daniel in the lions’ den

**About this chapter**

Officials plot against Daniel’s practice of praying to God.

Darius signs an order that puts Daniel’s life in danger.

God rescues Daniel, and the account also reports the violent punishment of his accusers and their families.

## Daniel 6:1

Darius chose to appoint 120 satraps to rule throughout the kingdom. 📖

**Help with “satraps”:** These are officials who rule parts of the kingdom.

## Daniel 6:2

Three chief officials, including Daniel, were put over them. 📖

The satraps had to answer to these three, so the king would not suffer loss.

## Daniel 6:3

Daniel’s extraordinary spirit soon made him stand out among the chief officials and satraps. 📖

The king planned to put him over the whole kingdom.

## Daniel 6:4

The chief officials and satraps tried to find a charge against Daniel in his work for the kingdom. 📖

But they found no fault or corruption.

He was trustworthy, and they found no carelessness or corruption in him.

## Daniel 6:5

The men said, “We will never find a charge against Daniel unless it concerns his God’s law.” 📖

## Daniel 6:6

The chief officials and satraps went together to the king. 📖

They said, “King Darius, may you live forever!

## Daniel 6:7

All the royal chief officials, prefects, satraps, advisers, and governors have agreed on this. 📖

The king should make and enforce an order for thirty days.

Anyone appealing to any god or person except you, king, must be thrown into the lions’ den.

**Help with “appealing”:** Here this means making a prayer or formal request.

**About their claim:** These are the officials’ words about agreement.

The account has not said that Daniel agreed.

## Daniel 6:8

King, make the order and sign it, so it cannot be changed. 📖

This follows the law of the Medes and Persians, which cannot be canceled.”

## Daniel 6:9

King Darius signed the written order. 📖

## Daniel 6:10

Daniel learned that the order had been signed and went home. 📖

His upstairs room had windows open toward Jerusalem.

Three times a day, he knelt, prayed, and thanked his God, just as before.

## Daniel 6:11

The men went as a group and found Daniel asking and pleading with his God. 📖

## Daniel 6:12

They approached the king and asked about his order. 📖

They said, “Did you not sign a thirty-day order?

Anyone appealing to any god or person except you must be thrown into the lions’ den.”

The king replied, “The order stands under the law of the Medes and Persians.

It cannot be canceled.”

## Daniel 6:13

They told the king, “Daniel, one of the people taken from Judah, shows no respect for you, king. 📖

He ignores the order you signed.

He still prays three times a day.”

## Daniel 6:14

The king was deeply upset when he heard this. 📖

He decided to save Daniel and worked until sunset to rescue him.

## Daniel 6:15

The men approached the king together. 📖

They said, “Remember, king, under the law of the Medes and Persians, no royal order can be changed.”

## Daniel 6:16

The king gave the order. 📖

They brought Daniel and threw him into the lions’ den.

The king told Daniel, “May your God, whom you serve continually, rescue you!”

## Daniel 6:17

A stone was placed over the den’s opening. 📖

The king sealed it with his own signet ring and his nobles’ rings.

Then nothing about Daniel’s situation could be changed.

**Help with “signet ring”:** This ring makes an identifying mark in a seal.

## Daniel 6:18

The king went to his palace and spent the night without eating. 📖

No entertainment was brought to him.

He could not sleep.

## Daniel 6:19

At dawn’s first light, the king got up and hurried to the lions’ den. 📖

## Daniel 6:20

When he reached the den, he cried out in deep distress. 📖

He said, “Daniel, servant of the living God, has your God been able to rescue you from the lions?

You serve Him continually.”

## Daniel 6:21

Daniel replied, “King, may you live forever! 📖

## Daniel 6:22

My God sent His angel and shut the lions’ mouths. 📖

They have not hurt me, because I was found innocent before Him.

I have also done you no wrong, king.”

## Daniel 6:23

The king was overjoyed and ordered Daniel lifted from the den. 📖

When Daniel was lifted out, no wounds at all were found on him.

He had trusted in his God.

## Daniel 6:24

The king ordered the men who falsely accused Daniel brought and thrown into the lions’ den. 📖

Their children and wives were thrown in too.

Before they reached the bottom, the lions overpowered them and crushed all their bones.

**About this punishment:** The account reports the king’s command and its effects on the families too.

## Daniel 6:25

King Darius wrote to people of every nation and language throughout the land. 📖

He said, “May your prosperity increase.

## Daniel 6:26

I order people in every part of my kingdom to tremble in fear before Daniel’s God. 📖

He is the living God and lives forever.

His kingdom will never be destroyed, and His rule will never end.

## Daniel 6:27

He delivers and rescues. 📖

He does signs and wonders in heaven and on earth.

He has rescued Daniel from the lions’ power.”

## Daniel 6:28

Daniel prospered during Darius’s rule and during the rule of Cyrus the Persian. 📖
