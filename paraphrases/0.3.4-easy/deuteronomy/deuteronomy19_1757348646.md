# Deuteronomy 19 — Refuge cities, boundaries, and witnesses

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules for protecting a person who kills accidentally.

He distinguishes that case from planned killing.

He also commands unchanged boundary markers and careful investigation of accusations.

## Deuteronomy 19:1

When the LORD your God destroys the nations and gives you their land, you will drive them out. 📖

When you settle in their cities and houses,

## Deuteronomy 19:2

set apart three cities in the land He gives you to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:3

Build roads and divide the inherited land into three regions so someone who kills another can flee to these cities. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:4

This applies to someone fleeing to save his life after accidentally killing his neighbor without intending harm: 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:5

He enters a forest with his neighbor to cut wood and swings an axe at a tree. 📖

The blade flies off its handle, striking and killing his neighbor.

He may flee to one of these cities to save his life.

## Deuteronomy 19:6

Otherwise, the avenger of blood may chase him in rage and overtake him if the distance is great. 📖

He may kill him, though he deserves no death because he intended no harm.

**Help with “avenger of blood”:** This is the person seeking to avenge the death.

## Deuteronomy 19:7

That is why I command you to set apart three cities. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:8

If the LORD your God enlarges your territory as sworn to your fathers and gives all the land promised them, 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:9

and you carefully keep all commands I give today, loving Him and always following His ways, add three more cities. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:10

Then innocent blood will not be shed in the inherited land the LORD your God gives. 📖

You will not be guilty of bloodshed.

## Deuteronomy 19:11

But suppose a man hates his neighbor, waits to attack him, kills him, and flees to one of these cities. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:12

His city’s elders must send for him, bring him back, and hand him to the avenger of blood to die. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:13

Show no pity. 📖

Remove the guilt of shedding innocent blood from Israel, so you may fare well.

## Deuteronomy 19:14

Do not move your neighbor’s boundary marker, set by your ancestors to mark the inheritance in the land He gives. 📖

**Help with “boundary marker”:** This marks where one person’s land ends and another’s begins.

## Deuteronomy 19:15

One witness cannot establish any wrongdoing or sin against a man, whatever the offense. 📖

A matter requires two or three witnesses’ testimony.

## Deuteronomy 19:16

If a false witness accuses someone of a crime, 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:17

both sides must stand before the LORD, before the priests and judges serving then. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:18

The judges must investigate thoroughly. 📖

If the witness is proved a liar, falsely accusing his brother,

## Deuteronomy 19:19

do to him what he intended for his brother. 📖

Remove the evil from among you.

## Deuteronomy 19:20

The others will hear, fear, and never do such evil among you again. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:21

Show no pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot. 📖
