# Deuteronomy 31 — Joshua’s appointment and the law’s witnesses

**About this chapter**

Moses speaks about his approaching death and encourages Joshua.

He writes the law, commands its public reading, and teaches a song.

The LORD warns of future unfaithfulness and commissions Joshua.

## Deuteronomy 31:1

After speaking these words to all Israel, Moses 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:2

said, “I am a hundred and twenty years old. 📖

I can no longer come and go.

The LORD told me, ‘You will not cross the Jordan.’

## Deuteronomy 31:3

The LORD your God Himself will cross ahead and destroy these nations before you. 📖

You will drive them out.

Joshua will cross ahead, as the LORD said.

## Deuteronomy 31:4

He will do as He did to Sihon and Og, Amorite kings destroyed with their land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:5

He will hand them over. 📖

Do exactly what I commanded.

## Deuteronomy 31:6

Be strong and courageous, not afraid or terrified. 📖

The LORD your God goes with you and will never leave or forsake you.”

## Deuteronomy 31:7

Moses called Joshua before all Israel. 📖

“Be strong and courageous.

You will enter with these people and give them the land sworn to their fathers as an inheritance.

## Deuteronomy 31:8

The LORD Himself goes ahead and will be with you, never leaving or forsaking you. 📖

Do not fear or lose courage.”

## Deuteronomy 31:9

Moses wrote this law and gave it to Levi’s priests, who carried the LORD’s covenant ark, and all Israel’s elders. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:10

He commanded, “At every seven years’ end, the appointed debt-release time during the Feast of Tabernacles, 📖

**Help with “Tabernacles”:** These are temporary shelters used during this feast.

## Deuteronomy 31:11

when all Israel appears before the LORD your God at His chosen place, read this law for all to hear. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:12

Gather men, women, children, and foreigners within your gates. 📖

They must hear, learn to fear the LORD your God, and carefully keep all this law’s words.

## Deuteronomy 31:13

Their children who do not know the law will hear and learn to fear the LORD your God. 📖

They will learn for as long as you live in the land you cross the Jordan to possess.”

## Deuteronomy 31:14

The LORD told Moses, “Your death is near. 📖

Call Joshua and come to the Tent of Meeting so I may appoint him.”

Moses and Joshua presented themselves there.

## Deuteronomy 31:15

The LORD appeared in a cloud pillar standing over the tent’s entrance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:16

He told Moses, “You will soon rest with your fathers. 📖

These people will prostitute themselves with the land’s foreign gods, abandoning Me and breaking My covenant.

**Help with these pictures:** Resting with fathers refers to death.

Prostitution pictures unfaithfulness to God.

## Deuteronomy 31:17

My anger will burn that day. 📖

I will abandon them and hide My face, so they are consumed and suffer many troubles and afflictions.

They will ask, ‘Did these disasters come because our God is no longer with us?’

**Help with “hide My face”:** This pictures withdrawing His favor and protection.

## Deuteronomy 31:18

I will surely hide My face that day because of all their evil in turning to other gods. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:19

Write this song, teach it to Israel, and have them recite it as My witness against them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:20

I will bring them into the land sworn to their fathers, flowing with milk and honey. 📖

They will eat their fill and prosper, then worship other gods, reject Me, and break My covenant.

**Help with “milk and honey”:** These picture plentiful good food.

## Deuteronomy 31:21

When many troubles and afflictions come, this song will testify against them. 📖

Their descendants will remember and recite it.

I know their inclination before bringing them into the land I swore to give.”

**Help with “inclination”:** This means the direction of their desires and choices.

## Deuteronomy 31:22

That very day Moses wrote the song and taught Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:23

The LORD appointed Joshua son of Nun: “Be strong and courageous. 📖

You will bring Israel into the land I swore to give, and I will be with you.”

## Deuteronomy 31:24

Moses finished writing all this law’s words in a book, from beginning to end, 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:25

and commanded the Levites carrying the LORD’s covenant ark, 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:26

“Put this Book of the Law beside the LORD your God’s covenant ark as a lasting witness against you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:27

I know your rebellion and stiff necks. 📖

If you rebel now while I live, how much more after I die!

**Help with “stiff necks”:** This pictures stubborn refusal to obey.

## Deuteronomy 31:28

Gather all tribal elders and officers before me. 📖

I will speak for them to hear and call heaven and earth as witnesses against them.

## Deuteronomy 31:29

After my death, I know you will utterly corrupt yourselves and leave the path I commanded. 📖

Disaster will come because your hands’ work does evil before the LORD and provokes His anger.”
