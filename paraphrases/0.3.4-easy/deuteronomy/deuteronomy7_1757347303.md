# Deuteronomy 7 — Commands and promises about the nations

**About this chapter**

Moses continues speaking to Israel.

He gives commands about the peoples in the land, including complete destruction and no mercy.

The promises of blessing depend on the obedience stated in the passage.

## Deuteronomy 7:1

The LORD your God will bring you into the land to possess and drive out many nations. 📖

These are Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, seven nations larger and stronger than you.

## Deuteronomy 7:2

When He hands them over and you defeat them, devote them to complete destruction. 📖

Make no treaty and show no mercy.

**Help with “devote to complete destruction”:** Here this means setting apart for total destruction.

## Deuteronomy 7:3

Do not marry them. 📖

Do not give your daughters to their sons or take their daughters for your sons,

## Deuteronomy 7:4

because they will turn your sons from following the LORD to other gods. 📖

Then the LORD’s anger will burn against you, and He will destroy you quickly.

## Deuteronomy 7:5

Tear down their altars, smash their sacred pillars, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their idols. 📖

**Help with “Asherah poles”:** These are objects used in worship of Asherah.

## Deuteronomy 7:6

You are holy to the LORD your God. 📖

He chose you from all earth’s peoples as His prized possession.

## Deuteronomy 7:7

He did not love and choose you because you outnumbered other peoples. 📖

You were the fewest of all.

## Deuteronomy 7:8

He loved you and kept His oath to your fathers. 📖

With a mighty hand He redeemed you from slavery, from Pharaoh king of Egypt’s hand.

**Help with these words:** Redeemed means freed from slavery here.

God’s mighty hand pictures His power.

Pharaoh’s hand pictures his control.

## Deuteronomy 7:9

Know that the LORD your God is God, faithful and keeping His covenant of faithful love for a thousand generations. 📖

This is for those loving Him and keeping His commands.

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 7:10

But He repays those hating Him to their faces with destruction. 📖

He will not delay repaying the one hating Him to his face.

## Deuteronomy 7:11

Keep all the commands, laws, and rulings I give you to follow today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:12

If you hear these rulings and carefully keep them, the LORD your God will keep His covenant. 📖

He will keep the faithful love He swore to your fathers.

## Deuteronomy 7:13

He will love, bless, and multiply you. 📖

He will bless your womb’s fruit and your land’s produce: grain, new wine, and oil.

He will bless your herds’ young and your flocks’ lambs in the land He swore to your fathers.

**Help with “womb’s fruit”:** This means children born to you.

## Deuteronomy 7:14

You will be blessed above all peoples. 📖

No man, woman, or livestock among you will be unable to produce offspring.

## Deuteronomy 7:15

The LORD will remove all sickness from you. 📖

He will put none of Egypt’s terrible diseases you knew on you, but put them on all who hate you.

## Deuteronomy 7:16

Destroy all the peoples He hands over to you, without pity. 📖

Do not worship their gods, because that will be a snare.

**Help with “snare”:** This means a trap.

## Deuteronomy 7:17

You may think, “These nations are greater than us. 📖

How can we drive them out?”

## Deuteronomy 7:18

Do not fear them. 📖

Remember what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and all Egypt:

## Deuteronomy 7:19

You saw great trials, signs, wonders, the mighty hand and outstretched arm that brought you out. 📖

He will do the same to all the peoples you fear.

**Help with “hand” and “arm”:** These picture God’s power in action.

## Deuteronomy 7:20

He will send the hornet against them until even the survivors hiding from you perish. 📖

**Help with “hornet”:** This is a stinging insect.

## Deuteronomy 7:21

Do not be terrified of them. 📖

The LORD your God among you is great and awesome.

**Help with “awesome”:** This means inspiring deep awe and reverence.

## Deuteronomy 7:22

He will drive these nations out little by little. 📖

You cannot eliminate them all at once, or wild animals would multiply around you.

## Deuteronomy 7:23

He will hand them over and throw them into great confusion until they are destroyed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:24

He will hand over their kings, and you will wipe their names from under heaven. 📖

No one can stand against you.

You will destroy them completely.

## Deuteronomy 7:25

Burn their gods’ images. 📖

Do not covet or take their silver and gold, or it will trap you.

It is hateful to the LORD your God.

**Help with “covet”:** This means wrongly wanting to have something.

## Deuteronomy 7:26

Bring no such hateful thing into your house, or you will be set apart for destruction like it. 📖

Hate it utterly because it is set apart for destruction.
