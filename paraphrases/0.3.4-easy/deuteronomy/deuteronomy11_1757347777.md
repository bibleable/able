# Deuteronomy 11 — Remember, obey, and choose blessing

**About this chapter**

Moses continues speaking to Israel.

He recalls what the people saw and gives promises and warnings about the land.

The rain and conquest promises retain their stated conditions.

## Deuteronomy 11:1

Love the LORD your God and always keep His charge, laws, rulings, and commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:2

Know today: your children did not experience or see the LORD your God’s discipline. 📖

You saw His greatness, mighty hand, and outstretched arm,

**Help with “hand” and “arm”:** These picture His power in action.

## Deuteronomy 11:3

His signs and works in Egypt against Pharaoh king of Egypt and all his land, 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:4

and His acts against Egypt’s army, horses, and chariots pursuing you. 📖

He made the Red Sea’s waters cover them and destroyed them completely, as remains true today,

## Deuteronomy 11:5

and His acts for you in the wilderness until you reached here, 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:6

and His acts to Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab the Reubenite, before all Israel. 📖

The earth opened its mouth, swallowing them, their households, tents, and every living thing they owned.

## Deuteronomy 11:7

Your own eyes saw every great work the LORD did. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:8

Keep every command I give today, so you may have strength to enter and possess the land across the Jordan, 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:9

and live long in the land He swore to give your fathers and their descendants, flowing with milk and honey. 📖

**Help with “milk and honey”:** These picture plentiful good food.

## Deuteronomy 11:10

The land you enter is unlike Egypt, which you left. 📖

There you planted seed and watered on foot, like a vegetable garden.

## Deuteronomy 11:11

Across the Jordan, the land you will possess has mountains and valleys that drink rain from heaven. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Land drinking rain means land receiving water from rain.

## Deuteronomy 11:12

The LORD your God cares for it. 📖

His eyes are always on it, from the year’s beginning to its end.

**Help with this picture:** This describes God’s constant attention to the land.

## Deuteronomy 11:13

Suppose you carefully obey the commands I give today. 📖

You must love the LORD your God and serve Him with all your heart and all your soul,

## Deuteronomy 11:14

then the LORD will give seasonal autumn and spring rain so you can gather grain, new wine, and oil. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:15

He will give field grass for your livestock, and you will eat and be satisfied. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:16

Be careful not to be drawn away to worship and bow to other gods, 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:17

or the LORD’s anger will burn against you. 📖

He will shut heaven: no rain, no land produce.

You will soon perish from the good land He gives you.

## Deuteronomy 11:18

Fix these words of mine in your hearts and minds. 📖

Tie them as reminders on your hands and bind them on your foreheads.

## Deuteronomy 11:19

Teach them to your children. 📖

Talk about them sitting at home, walking on the road, lying down, and getting up.

## Deuteronomy 11:20

Write them on your houses’ doorposts and your gates, 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:21

Then you and your children may have many days in the land the LORD swore to your fathers. 📖

This may last as long as heaven is above earth.

## Deuteronomy 11:22

Suppose you carefully keep all these commands I give you to follow. 📖

This means loving the LORD your God, following all His ways, and holding fast to Him.

## Deuteronomy 11:23

Then the LORD will drive out all these nations before you. 📖

You will drive out nations greater and stronger than you.

## Deuteronomy 11:24

Every place your foot steps will be yours. 📖

Your territory will reach from the wilderness to Lebanon, from the Euphrates River to the Western Sea.

## Deuteronomy 11:25

No man will stand against you. 📖

The LORD your God will make the whole land fear and dread you wherever you step, as He promised.

## Deuteronomy 11:26

Today I set a blessing and a curse before you: 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:27

a blessing if you obey the LORD your God’s commands I give today, 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:28

a curse if you disobey and leave the path I command today, following other gods you have not known. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:29

When He brings you into the land to possess, proclaim the blessing on Mount Gerizim and curse on Mount Ebal. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:30

Are these mountains not across the Jordan, west of the road toward sunset? 📖

They are in the Arabah’s Canaanite land, opposite Gilgal near the Oak of Moreh.

## Deuteronomy 11:31

You are about to cross the Jordan and possess the land He gives. 📖

When you possess and settle it,

## Deuteronomy 11:32

carefully follow all the laws and rulings I set before you today. 📖
