# Deuteronomy 29 — The covenant in Moab

**About this chapter**

Moses speaks to all Israel about the covenant in Moab, added to the covenant at Horeb.

He warns against a person claiming peace while choosing stubborn disobedience.

The judgments, the nations’ questions, and the reply are retained.

## Deuteronomy 29:1

These are the covenant words the LORD commanded Moses to make with Israel in Moab, besides the covenant at Horeb. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 29:2

Moses called all Israel and said, “Your own eyes saw everything the LORD did in Egypt. 📖

He acted against Pharaoh, all his officials, and all his land.

## Deuteronomy 29:3

Your own eyes saw the great trials, miraculous signs, and wonders. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:4

Yet until today He has not given you an understanding mind, seeing eyes, or hearing ears. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:5

The LORD led you forty years in the wilderness. 📖

Your clothes and sandals never wore out.

## Deuteronomy 29:6

You neither ate bread nor drank wine or strong drink. 📖

This was so you might know He is the LORD your God.

## Deuteronomy 29:7

Here Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came against us in battle. 📖

We defeated them,

## Deuteronomy 29:8

and took their land for Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe as an inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:9

Keep and follow this covenant’s words, so you may prosper in all you do. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:10

All stand today before the LORD your God: tribal leaders, elders, officials, and all Israel’s men, 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:11

your children, wives, and camp foreigners cutting your wood and drawing your water, 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:12

to enter His covenant and oath He makes with you today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:13

This is to establish you today as His people, with Him as your God, as He promised. 📖

He swore this to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

## Deuteronomy 29:14

I make this covenant and oath not only with you, 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:15

but with those here with us today before the LORD our God and those not here today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:16

You know our life in Egypt and our passage through the nations on the way here. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:17

You saw their hateful things and idols of wood, stone, silver, and gold. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:18

Let no man, woman, clan, or tribe turn from the LORD our God today to worship those nations’ gods. 📖

Let no root among you bear poisonous, bitter fruit,

**Help with this picture:** A poisonous root pictures the harmful turning away described here.

## Deuteronomy 29:19

for such a person hears the oath yet blesses himself: ‘I will have peace despite my stubborn heart’s ways.’ 📖

This brings disaster on watered land and dry.

## Deuteronomy 29:20

The LORD will never be willing to forgive him. 📖

His anger and jealousy will burn against him.

Every written curse in this book will fall on him, and He will erase his name from under heaven,

## Deuteronomy 29:21

singling him out from all Israel’s tribes for disaster under every covenant curse in this Book of the Law. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:22

Your sons’ coming generation and foreigners from distant lands will see the land’s plagues and sicknesses He inflicted. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:23

All soil will be burning sulfur and salt waste: unsown, unproductive, growing no plant. 📖

It will resemble Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim, which He overthrew in fierce anger.

## Deuteronomy 29:24

All nations will ask, ‘Why did the LORD do this to the land? 📖

Why such great anger?’

## Deuteronomy 29:25

People will answer, ‘They abandoned the covenant of the LORD, their fathers’ God. 📖

He made it when He brought them from Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 29:26

They served and worshiped other gods they never knew, gods He never gave them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:27

His anger burned against the land, bringing every curse written in this book. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:28

In anger, rage, and great wrath, He uprooted them and threw them into another land, where they are today.’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:29

Secret things belong to the LORD our God. 📖

Revealed things belong to us and our children forever, so we may follow all this law’s words.”
