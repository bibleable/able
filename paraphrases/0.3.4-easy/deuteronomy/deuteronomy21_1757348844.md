# Deuteronomy 21 — An unsolved killing and household judgments

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules about an unsolved killing, a captive woman, inheritance, a rebellious son, and an executed body.

The prayer and the conditions for each ruling are kept.

The deaths and unequal power described in these rules remain explicit.

## Deuteronomy 21:1

Suppose someone is found killed in a field in the land the LORD your God gives you to possess. 📖

The killer is unknown.

## Deuteronomy 21:2

Your elders and judges must measure from the victim to neighboring cities. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:3

The nearest city’s elders must take a heifer never yoked or used for work. 📖

**Help with these words:** A heifer is a young female cow.

A yoke is equipment for making an animal pull a load.

## Deuteronomy 21:4

Bring her to a valley with running water, never plowed or planted. 📖

Break her neck there beside the stream.

## Deuteronomy 21:5

The priests, Levi’s sons, must come forward. 📖

The LORD your God chose them to serve, bless in His name, and rule in every dispute and assault case.

## Deuteronomy 21:6

All the nearest city’s elders must wash their hands by the stream over the heifer with the broken neck, 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:7

and say, “Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes did not see it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:8

Accept this atonement, O LORD, for Your people Israel, whom You redeemed. 📖

Do not hold this innocent blood against them.”

The bloodshed will be atoned for.

**Help with these words:** Atonement concerns putting things right with God.

Redeemed means freed here.

## Deuteronomy 21:9

Remove the guilt of innocent blood from among you by doing right before the LORD. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:10

When you fight enemies and He hands them over, and you take captives, 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:11

if you see a beautiful captive woman, desire her, and want her as your wife, 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:12

bring her home. 📖

She must shave her head, trim her nails,

## Deuteronomy 21:13

and put aside her captive clothing. 📖

She must live in your house a full month and mourn her father and mother.

Then you may have sex with her and become her husband, and she your wife.

## Deuteronomy 21:14

If you are displeased with her, let her go wherever she wishes. 📖

Do not sell her or treat her as a slave, because you dishonored her.

## Deuteronomy 21:15

Suppose a man has two wives, one loved and one unloved. 📖

Both bear sons, but the unloved wife bears the firstborn.

## Deuteronomy 21:16

When dividing his inheritance, he must not favor the loved wife’s son as firstborn over the unloved wife’s son. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:17

Acknowledge the unloved wife’s firstborn. 📖

Give him a double portion of everything the father owns.

He is the firstfruits of his father’s strength, and the firstborn’s right belongs to him.

**Help with these words:** A double portion is twice a share.

“Firstfruits” describes the father’s firstborn son.

## Deuteronomy 21:18

Suppose a man’s son is stubborn and rebellious, disobeying his father and mother even after discipline. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:19

His father and mother must seize him and bring him to his city’s elders at his hometown gate, 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:20

saying, “Our son is stubborn, rebellious, and disobedient. 📖

He is a glutton and drunkard.”

**Help with these words:** A glutton eats to excess.

A drunkard gets drunk habitually.

## Deuteronomy 21:21

All his city’s men must stone him to death. 📖

Remove the evil from among you.

All Israel will hear and fear.

## Deuteronomy 21:22

Suppose a man commits a sin deserving death, is executed, and you hang his body on a tree. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:23

Do not leave it there overnight. 📖

Bury him that day, because anyone hung on a tree is under God’s curse.

Do not defile the land the LORD your God gives as an inheritance.
