# Deuteronomy 24 — Divorce, security for loans, wages, and harvests

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules about divorce, newly married men, loans, workers, and shared harvests.

The conditions for each rule are kept.

The commands repeatedly recall slavery in Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 24:1

Suppose a man marries a woman, then dislikes her because he finds some indecency in her. 📖

He may write a divorce certificate, give it to her, and send her from his house.

**Help with “indecency”:** This is BSB’s term for what he finds objectionable here.

The passage does not define the category further.

## Deuteronomy 24:2

If she leaves and marries another man, 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:3

and he hates her, writes and gives a divorce certificate, and sends her away, or he dies, 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:4

her first husband must not remarry her after she has been defiled. 📖

That is hateful to the LORD.

Do not bring sin on the land He gives as an inheritance.

**About “defiled”:** This is the passage’s description of her status in this ruling.

## Deuteronomy 24:5

A newly married man must not be sent to war or pressed into any duty. 📖

He is free at home one year to bring his wife joy.

## Deuteronomy 24:6

Take no pair of millstones, or even the upper stone, as security for debt. 📖

That would take someone’s livelihood as security.

**Help with these words:** Millstones grind grain for food.

Security is something held to ensure a loan is repaid.

Livelihood means the means of living.

## Deuteronomy 24:7

If someone is caught kidnapping an Israelite brother, treating him as a slave or selling him, he must die. 📖

Remove the evil from among you.

## Deuteronomy 24:8

For infectious skin diseases, carefully follow everything the Levitical priests instruct. 📖

Do as I commanded them.

**Help with “Levitical priests”:** These are priests of Levi’s tribe.

## Deuteronomy 24:9

Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on the journey after you left Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:10

When lending anything to a neighbor, do not enter his house to collect security. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:11

Stand outside while he brings it out. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:12

If he is poor, do not sleep while holding his security. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:13

Return it by sunset so he may sleep in his own cloak and bless you. 📖

This will count as righteousness before the LORD your God.

**Help with “righteousness”:** This means being right before God.

## Deuteronomy 24:14

Do not oppress poor, needy hired workers, whether brothers or foreigners living in your towns. 📖

**Help with “oppress”:** This means treating someone cruelly or unfairly.

## Deuteronomy 24:15

Pay daily wages before sunset, because the worker is poor and depends on them. 📖

Otherwise he may cry to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin.

## Deuteronomy 24:16

Fathers must not be executed for their children, nor children for their fathers. 📖

Each must be executed for his own sin.

## Deuteronomy 24:17

Deny no justice to foreigners or the fatherless. 📖

Take no widow’s cloak as security.

## Deuteronomy 24:18

Remember slavery in Egypt and how the LORD your God redeemed you there. 📖

That is why I command this.

**Help with “redeemed”:** Here this means freed from slavery.

## Deuteronomy 24:19

If you forget a sheaf while harvesting, do not go back for it. 📖

Leave it for foreigners, the fatherless, and widows, so He may bless all your work.

**Help with “sheaf”:** This is a bundle of cut grain stalks.

## Deuteronomy 24:20

After beating olives from the trees, do not go over the branches again. 📖

Leave the rest for foreigners, the fatherless, and widows.

## Deuteronomy 24:21

After gathering grapes, do not go over the vines again. 📖

Leave the rest for foreigners, the fatherless, and widows.

## Deuteronomy 24:22

Remember you were slaves in Egypt. 📖

That is why I command this.
