# Deuteronomy 6 — Love the LORD and remember His commands

**About this chapter**

Moses continues teaching Israel.

He commands wholehearted love and warns against forgetting God when life is plentiful.

He gives the answer parents should teach their sons about the commands.

## Deuteronomy 6:1

These commands, laws, and rulings are what the LORD your God told me to teach you. 📖

Follow them in the land you will enter and possess,

## Deuteronomy 6:2

You, your children, and grandchildren must fear the LORD your God throughout life. 📖

Keep all His laws and commands I give, so your days may be long.

## Deuteronomy 6:3

Israel, hear and carefully obey, so you may prosper and multiply greatly in a land flowing with milk and honey. 📖

The LORD, your fathers’ God, promised this.

**Help with “milk and honey”:** These picture plentiful good food.

## Deuteronomy 6:4

Hear, Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:5

Love the LORD your God with all your heart, all your soul, and all your strength. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:6

Keep these words I command today on your hearts. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:7

Teach them carefully to your children. 📖

Speak of them sitting at home, walking on the road, lying down, and getting up.

## Deuteronomy 6:8

Tie them as reminders on your hands and bind them on your foreheads. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:9

Write them on your houses’ doorposts and on your gates. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:10

The LORD your God will bring you to the land He swore to give your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

It has great, splendid cities you did not build,

## Deuteronomy 6:11

Its houses are full of every good thing you did not supply. 📖

Its wells are ones you did not dig.

Its vineyards and olive groves are ones you did not plant.

When you eat and are satisfied,

## Deuteronomy 6:12

be careful not to forget the LORD who brought you from Egypt, the house of slavery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:13

Fear the LORD your God, serve Him only, and swear your oaths in His name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:14

Follow no other gods, the gods of surrounding peoples. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:15

The LORD your God among you is jealous. 📖

Otherwise His anger will burn against you, and He will wipe you from the earth.

**Help with “jealous”:** Here this concerns God’s claim on the people’s undivided worship.

## Deuteronomy 6:16

Do not test the LORD your God as you did at Massah. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:17

Carefully keep His commands, testimonies, and laws. 📖

**Help with “testimonies”:** These are God’s declared instructions.

## Deuteronomy 6:18

Do what is right and good before the LORD so you may fare well. 📖

Then you may enter and possess the good land He swore to give your fathers,

## Deuteronomy 6:19

driving out all your enemies before you, as the LORD said. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:20

When your son asks in the future, “What do the decrees, laws, and rulings the LORD our God commanded mean?” 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:21

say, “We were Pharaoh’s slaves in Egypt, but the LORD brought us out with a mighty hand. 📖

**Help with “mighty hand”:** This pictures His power in action.

## Deuteronomy 6:22

We saw the LORD bring great, devastating signs and wonders on Egypt, Pharaoh, and all his household. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:23

He brought us out to bring us in and give us the land He swore to our fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:24

The LORD commanded us to follow all these laws and fear the LORD our God. 📖

This is so we always prosper and are preserved, as today.

## Deuteronomy 6:25

If we carefully obey every command before the LORD our God as He commanded, that will be our righteousness.” 📖

**Help with “righteousness”:** This means being right before God.
