# Deuteronomy 17 — Judgments, difficult cases, and kings

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules about offerings, false worship, legal decisions, and a future king.

The rules require investigation and multiple witnesses.

The king must keep and read the law rather than place himself above others.

## Deuteronomy 17:1

Do not sacrifice an ox or sheep with any defect or serious flaw to the LORD your God. 📖

That is hateful to Him.

## Deuteronomy 17:2

If a man or woman in a town He gives you does evil before Him, breaking His covenant, 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 17:3

worshiping other gods, bowing to them or the sun, moon, or heaven’s host, which I forbade, 📖

**Help with “heaven’s host”:** This refers to the things in the sky, including the sun, moon, and stars.

## Deuteronomy 17:4

and you hear a report, investigate thoroughly. 📖

If it is true and this hateful act happened in Israel,

## Deuteronomy 17:5

bring that man or woman to your gates and stone that person to death. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:6

Execution requires two or three witnesses’ testimony. 📖

Do not execute anyone on one witness’s testimony alone.

## Deuteronomy 17:7

The witnesses must be first to put him to death, followed by all the people. 📖

Remove the evil from among you.

## Deuteronomy 17:8

If a dispute about bloodshed, lawsuits, or assaults within your gates is too hard, go up to His chosen place. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:9

Ask the Levitical priests and the judge serving then. 📖

They will give the case’s verdict.

**Help with these words:** Levitical priests are priests of Levi’s tribe.

A verdict is a legal decision.

## Deuteronomy 17:10

Follow their verdict from His chosen place. 📖

Carefully do everything they instruct,

## Deuteronomy 17:11

following the law and verdict they give. 📖

Turn neither right nor left from their decision.

## Deuteronomy 17:12

Anyone arrogantly refusing the priest serving the LORD your God there, or the judge, must die. 📖

Remove the evil from Israel.

**Help with “arrogantly”:** This means acting as if you know better and need not obey.

## Deuteronomy 17:13

All the people will hear, fear, and stop acting arrogantly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:14

When you possess and settle the land He gives, you may say, “Let us have a king like surrounding nations.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:15

Appoint the king the LORD your God chooses, one of your brothers. 📖

Appoint no foreigner who is not your brother.

## Deuteronomy 17:16

The king must not gather many horses or send people back to Egypt for more. 📖

The LORD said, “Never go back that way again.”

## Deuteronomy 17:17

He must not take many wives, lest his heart stray. 📖

He must not gather large amounts of silver and gold.

## Deuteronomy 17:18

On his royal throne, he must write himself a copy of this instruction on a scroll before the Levitical priests. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:19

It must stay with him, and he must read it throughout life. 📖

He must learn to fear the LORD his God, carefully keeping all this instruction’s words and laws.

## Deuteronomy 17:20

Then his heart will not rise above his countrymen or turn right or left from the command. 📖

He and his sons may reign many years over his kingdom in Israel.

**Help with this picture:** A heart rising above others pictures pride.
