# Deuteronomy 32 — Moses’ song and the command to climb Nebo

**About this chapter**

Moses’ song describes the LORD’s care, Israel’s unfaithfulness, judgment, and vindication.

The song’s images, speakers, and violent judgments are retained.

After the song, the LORD tells Moses to view the land and die without entering it.

## Deuteronomy 32:1

Heavens, listen, and I will speak. 📖

Earth, hear my words.

## Deuteronomy 32:2

Let my teaching fall like rain, my speech settle like dew, gentle rain on new grass, showers on tender plants. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The song compares teaching to water nourishing growing plants.

## Deuteronomy 32:3

I proclaim the LORD’s Name. 📖

Declare our God’s greatness!

## Deuteronomy 32:4

He is the Rock, perfect in work and just in every way. 📖

He is faithful, without injustice, righteous and upright.

**Help with “Rock”:** This pictures God’s strength and reliability.

## Deuteronomy 32:5

His people acted corruptly toward Him. 📖

Their blemish is not that of His children, but of a perverse, crooked generation.

**Help with these pictures:** Blemish and crookedness describe corrupt conduct here.

## Deuteronomy 32:6

Foolish, senseless people, is this how you repay the LORD? 📖

Is He not your Father and Creator, who made and established you?

## Deuteronomy 32:7

Remember old days and years long past. 📖

Ask your father and elders; they will tell you.

## Deuteronomy 32:8

The Most High gave nations their inheritance and divided the sons of man. 📖

He fixed peoples’ boundaries by the number of the sons of God.

**About “sons of God”:** This is BSB’s phrase here.

The verse does not identify them further.

## Deuteronomy 32:9

The LORD’s portion is His people, Jacob His allotted inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:10

He found Jacob in desert land, a barren, howling wilderness. 📖

He surrounded, instructed, and guarded him as the apple of His eye.

**Help with these pictures:** Jacob represents the people.

The apple of the eye means the eye’s precious central part, something carefully protected.

## Deuteronomy 32:11

Like an eagle stirring its nest and hovering over its young, He spread wings to catch and carry them. 📖

He carried them on His pinions.

**Help with “pinions”:** These are the outer wing feathers used in flight.

## Deuteronomy 32:12

The LORD alone led him, with no foreign god beside him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:13

He made him ride the land’s heights and fed him field produce. 📖

He nourished him with honey from the rock and oil from a flinty crag,

**Help with these words:** A flinty crag is a hard, rocky cliff.

These lines picture provision in the land.

## Deuteronomy 32:14

curds from the herd, milk from the flock, lambs’ fat, Bashan’s rams, goats, and the finest wheat. 📖

You drank wine from the finest grapes’ juice.

**Help with “curds”:** These are thickened parts of milk.

## Deuteronomy 32:15

Jeshurun grew fat and kicked: fat, bloated, and gorged. 📖

He left the God who made him and scorned the Rock of his salvation.

**Help with these words:** Jeshurun is a name for Israel.

Gorged means filled with food.

The picture connects plentiful provision with rebellion against God.

## Deuteronomy 32:16

They made Him jealous with foreign gods and angry with hateful deeds. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:17

They sacrificed to demons, not God, to unknown, newly arrived gods their fathers never feared. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:18

You ignored the Rock who brought you forth and forgot the God who gave you birth. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The song pictures God bringing the people into being.

## Deuteronomy 32:19

The LORD saw and rejected them, angered by His sons and daughters. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:20

He said, “I will hide My face and see their end. 📖

They are a perverse generation, unfaithful children.

**Help with “hide My face”:** This pictures withdrawing His favor and protection.

## Deuteronomy 32:21

They made Me jealous with what is not God, angry with worthless idols. 📖

I will make them jealous through those not a people, angry through a nation without understanding.

## Deuteronomy 32:22

My anger kindled fire burning to Sheol’s depths, consuming earth and produce, scorching mountains’ foundations. 📖

**Help with “Sheol”:** This is the name used here for the place of the dead.

## Deuteronomy 32:23

I will heap disasters on them and spend My arrows against them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:24

Hunger will waste them; disease and bitter plague will ravage them. 📖

I will send wild beasts’ fangs and the venom of vipers slithering in dust.

## Deuteronomy 32:25

Outside, swords will take their children. 📖

Inside, terror will strike young men, young women, infants, and gray-haired men.

## Deuteronomy 32:26

I would have said I would cut them to pieces and erase their memory from humankind, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:27

if I did not dread enemy taunts. 📖

Their enemies might misunderstand, saying, ‘Our own hand won; the LORD did not do all this.’”

## Deuteronomy 32:28

Israel is a nation without counsel or understanding. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:29

If only they were wise, they would understand their fate. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:30

How could one chase a thousand or two rout ten thousand? 📖

It could happen only if their Rock sold them and the LORD gave them up.

## Deuteronomy 32:31

Their rock is not like our Rock. 📖

Even our enemies admit it.

**Help with “rock”:** The song contrasts their source of security with God, our Rock.

## Deuteronomy 32:32

Their vine comes from Sodom’s vine and Gomorrah’s fields. 📖

Their grapes are poisonous, their clusters bitter.

## Deuteronomy 32:33

Their wine is serpent venom, cobras’ deadly poison. 📖

**About these pictures:** The vine, grapes, and wine describe deadly corruption.

The references in these lines remain for further editorial review.

## Deuteronomy 32:34

“Did I not store these things, sealed in My vaults? 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:35

Vengeance is Mine; I will repay. 📖

Their foot will slip in due time: disaster is near and doom comes quickly.”

**Help with these words:** Vengeance means punishment in return for wrong.

A slipping foot pictures their coming downfall.

## Deuteronomy 32:36

The LORD will vindicate His people and pity His servants when strength is gone and none remain, slave or free. 📖

**Help with “vindicate”:** This means uphold their cause.

## Deuteronomy 32:37

He will say, “Where are their gods, the rock where they sought refuge, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:38

who ate sacrifices’ fat and drank drink-offering wine? 📖

Let them rise, help you, and give shelter!

## Deuteronomy 32:39

I am He; no God is beside Me. 📖

I bring death, give life, wound, and heal.

No one can rescue from My hand.

## Deuteronomy 32:40

I lift My hand to heaven and declare: As surely as I live forever, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:41

when I sharpen My flashing sword and grasp it for judgment, I will avenge Myself on enemies and repay haters. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:42

My arrows will be drunk with blood while My sword devours flesh. 📖

It is the blood of the slain and captives, the heads of enemy leaders.”

**About these images:** The song uses arrows and a sword to picture violent judgment.

## Deuteronomy 32:43

Heavens, rejoice with Him; all God’s angels, worship Him! 📖

Nations, rejoice with His people!

He will avenge His children’s blood, punish enemies, repay haters, and cleanse His land and people.

## Deuteronomy 32:44

Moses came with Joshua son of Nun and recited the whole song for the people to hear. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:45

After reciting all these words to all Israel, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:46

he said, “Take to heart every word I solemnly declared today. 📖

Command your children to carefully follow all this law’s words.

## Deuteronomy 32:47

They are not empty words but your life. 📖

Through them you will live long in the land you cross the Jordan to possess.”

## Deuteronomy 32:48

That same day the LORD told Moses, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:49

“Climb the Abarim Range to Mount Nebo in Moab, across from Jericho. 📖

View Canaan, which I give Israel to possess.

## Deuteronomy 32:50

You will die on that mountain and be gathered to your people, like your brother Aaron at Mount Hor. 📖

**Help with “gathered to your people”:** This is an expression used here for death.

## Deuteronomy 32:51

At Meribah-kadesh’s waters in Zin’s wilderness, you both broke faith with Me among Israel. 📖

You failed to treat Me as holy before them.

## Deuteronomy 32:52

You will see the land I give Israel from afar, but never enter.” 📖
