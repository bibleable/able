# Deuteronomy 3 — Og’s defeat and Moses’ plea

**About this chapter**

Moses recalls Og’s defeat and the land given east of the Jordan.

He repeats the duty to help the other Israelites before returning home.

He also recalls his prayer to enter the land and the LORD’s refusal.

## Deuteronomy 3:1

We turned up the Bashan road. 📖

Og king of Bashan and his whole army came to fight us at Edrei.

## Deuteronomy 3:2

The LORD told me, “Do not fear him. 📖

I handed him, all his people, and his land over to you.

Do as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.”

## Deuteronomy 3:3

The LORD our God handed Og king of Bashan and his whole army over to us. 📖

We struck them down, leaving no survivor.

## Deuteronomy 3:4

We captured all sixty cities, missing none: the whole Argob region, Og’s kingdom in Bashan. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:5

These cities all had high walls, gates, and bars for defense. 📖

There were also many unwalled villages.

## Deuteronomy 3:6

We devoted them to destruction like Sihon king of Heshbon’s cities. 📖

We completely destroyed every city’s men, women, and children.

**Help with “devoted to destruction”:** Here this means set apart for complete destruction.

## Deuteronomy 3:7

We took all the cities’ livestock and plunder for ourselves. 📖

**Help with “plunder”:** This means things taken in war.

## Deuteronomy 3:8

We took the two Amorite kings’ land across the Jordan, from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon— 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:9

called Sirion by Sidonians and Senir by Amorites— 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:10

all the plateau’s cities, all Gilead, and all Bashan to Salecah and Edrei in Og’s kingdom. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:11

Only Og king of Bashan remained of the Rephaim’s remnant. 📖

His iron bed, nine cubits long and four wide, is still in Rabbah of the Ammonites.

**Help with these words:** A remnant is what remains of a group.

A cubit is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Deuteronomy 3:12

We took possession of this land. 📖

I gave Reuben’s and Gad’s people land beyond Aroer along Arnon Valley, half Gilead’s hill country, and its cities.

## Deuteronomy 3:13

I gave Manasseh’s half-tribe the rest of Gilead and all Bashan, Og’s kingdom. 📖

All Argob, the whole Bashan territory, was once called Rephaim land.

## Deuteronomy 3:14

Jair, Manasseh’s descendant, took all Argob to the Geshurite and Maacathite border. 📖

He named Bashan Havvoth-jair after himself, as it is still called.

## Deuteronomy 3:15

I gave Gilead to Machir, 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:16

and gave Reuben’s and Gad’s people land from Gilead to Arnon Valley, whose middle was the border. 📖

It reached the Jabbok River, the Ammonite border.

## Deuteronomy 3:17

The Arabah’s Jordan River bordered it from Chinnereth to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea. 📖

Pisgah’s slopes were to the east.

## Deuteronomy 3:18

I told you, “The LORD your God gave you this land to possess. 📖

All your brave men must cross armed for battle ahead of your brothers, the Israelites.

## Deuteronomy 3:19

Your wives, children, and livestock may stay in the cities I gave you. 📖

I know you have much livestock.

## Deuteronomy 3:20

They may stay until the LORD gives your brothers rest as He gave you. 📖

Your brothers must possess the land the LORD your God gives across the Jordan.

Then each of you may return to the possession I gave you.”

## Deuteronomy 3:21

I told Joshua, “You saw everything the LORD your God did to these two kings. 📖

He will do the same to every kingdom you are about to enter.

## Deuteronomy 3:22

Do not fear them. 📖

The LORD your God Himself will fight for you.”

## Deuteronomy 3:23

I also pleaded with the LORD: 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:24

“O Lord GOD, You began showing Your servant Your greatness and power. 📖

What god in heaven or earth can do works and mighty acts like Yours?

## Deuteronomy 3:25

Please let me cross and see the good land beyond the Jordan, the pleasant hills and Lebanon!” 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:26

The LORD was angry with me because of you and would not listen. 📖

He said, “Enough!

Do not speak to Me about this again.

## Deuteronomy 3:27

Climb Pisgah’s top and look west, north, south, and east. 📖

See the land yourself, because you will not cross this Jordan.

## Deuteronomy 3:28

Appoint Joshua, encourage and strengthen him. 📖

He will cross ahead and enable the people to inherit the land you will see.”

## Deuteronomy 3:29

We stayed in the valley opposite Beth-peor. 📖
