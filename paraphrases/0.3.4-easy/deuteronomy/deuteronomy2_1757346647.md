# Deuteronomy 2 — Passing neighbors and defeating Sihon

**About this chapter**

Moses continues speaking about Israel’s journey.

He recalls lands the LORD forbade Israel to take and the battle with Sihon.

The historical notes and the account of killing women and children are retained.

## Deuteronomy 2:1

We turned toward the wilderness by the Red Sea route, as the LORD told me. 📖

We wandered around Mount Seir many days.

## Deuteronomy 2:2

Then the LORD told me, 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:3

“You wandered these hills long enough. 📖

Turn north

## Deuteronomy 2:4

and tell the people: You will pass through your brothers’ land, Esau’s descendants living in Seir. 📖

They will fear you, so be very careful.

## Deuteronomy 2:5

Do not provoke them. 📖

I will give you none of their land, not even a footprint.

I gave Mount Seir to Esau as his possession.

## Deuteronomy 2:6

Pay them silver for the food you eat and water you drink.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:7

The LORD your God blessed all your work and watched your journey through this vast wilderness. 📖

He was with you forty years, and you lacked nothing.

## Deuteronomy 2:8

We passed our brothers, Esau’s descendants in Seir. 📖

We left the Arabah road from Elath and Ezion-geber and took the road of Moab’s wilderness.

## Deuteronomy 2:9

The LORD told me, “Do not trouble Moab or provoke war. 📖

I will give you none of their land.

I gave Ar to Lot’s descendants to possess.”

## Deuteronomy 2:10

The Emites once lived there, a great, numerous people as tall as the Anakites. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:11

Like the Anakites, they were considered Rephaim, but Moab called them Emites. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:12

Horites once lived in Seir. 📖

Esau’s descendants drove them out, destroyed them, and settled there, as Israel did in the land the LORD gave them.

## Deuteronomy 2:13

The LORD said, “Now rise and cross the Brook of Zered.” 📖

We crossed it.

## Deuteronomy 2:14

Travel from Kadesh-barnea to crossing the Brook of Zered took thirty-eight years. 📖

By then that whole generation of fighting men died from the camp, as the LORD swore.

## Deuteronomy 2:15

The LORD’s hand was against them, removing them from camp until all died. 📖

**Help with “hand”:** This pictures the LORD’s power acting against them.

## Deuteronomy 2:16

When all those fighting men among the people had died, 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:17

the LORD told me, 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:18

“Today you will cross Moab’s border at Ar. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:19

Near the Ammonites, do not trouble or provoke them. 📖

I will give you none of their land.

I gave it to Lot’s descendants to possess.”

## Deuteronomy 2:20

That land too was considered Rephaim land. 📖

They once lived there, but the Ammonites called them Zamzummites.

## Deuteronomy 2:21

They were great and numerous, as tall as the Anakites. 📖

The LORD destroyed them before the Ammonites, who drove them out and settled there.

## Deuteronomy 2:22

He did the same for Esau’s descendants in Seir, destroying the Horites before them. 📖

They drove them out and still live there.

## Deuteronomy 2:23

Avvim lived in villages as far as Gaza. 📖

Caphtorites from Caphtor destroyed them and settled there.

## Deuteronomy 2:24

“Rise, travel, and cross the Arnon Valley. 📖

I handed Sihon the Amorite, Heshbon’s king, and his land over to you.

Begin taking it and fight him.

## Deuteronomy 2:25

Today I will begin making every nation under heaven dread and fear you. 📖

They will hear reports and tremble in distress because of you.”

## Deuteronomy 2:26

From Kedemoth’s wilderness, I sent Sihon king of Heshbon messengers offering peace: 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:27

“Let us cross your land on the main road. 📖

We will turn neither right nor left.

## Deuteronomy 2:28

Sell us food and water for silver. 📖

Let us cross on foot,

## Deuteronomy 2:29

as Esau’s descendants in Seir and Moabites in Ar did for us. 📖

Let us reach the Jordan and cross into the land the LORD our God gives us.”

## Deuteronomy 2:30

Sihon king of Heshbon refused passage. 📖

The LORD your God made his spirit stubborn and his heart unwilling to yield.

God did this to hand him over to you, as has happened today.

## Deuteronomy 2:31

The LORD told me, “I began handing Sihon and his land over to you. 📖

Begin conquering and possessing it.”

## Deuteronomy 2:32

Sihon and his whole army came to fight us at Jahaz. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:33

The LORD our God handed him over, and we defeated him, his sons, and his whole army. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:34

We captured all his cities and devoted every city’s people to destruction, including women and children. 📖

We left no survivors.

**Help with “devoted to destruction”:** Here this means set apart for complete destruction.

## Deuteronomy 2:35

We took only livestock and plunder from the captured cities for ourselves. 📖

**Help with “plunder”:** This means things taken in war.

## Deuteronomy 2:36

From Aroer at Arnon Valley’s edge to Gilead, no city’s walls were too high for us. 📖

This included the city in the valley.

The LORD our God gave us all the cities.

## Deuteronomy 2:37

You avoided Ammonite land, the Jabbok River’s banks, the hill cities, and every place the LORD our God forbade. 📖
