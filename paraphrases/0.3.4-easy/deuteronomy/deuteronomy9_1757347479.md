# Deuteronomy 9 — The land is not a reward for Israel’s goodness

**About this chapter**

Moses tells Israel why the LORD gives them the land.

He recalls their rebellion, the calf, and his prayers for them and Aaron.

The prayer keeps imagined accusations against God distinct from Moses’ own appeal.

## Deuteronomy 9:1

Listen, Israel! 📖

Today you are about to cross the Jordan and drive out greater, stronger nations with large cities fortified to heaven.

**Help with this picture:** Fortified cities have strong defenses.

“To heaven” pictures their great height.

## Deuteronomy 9:2

The people are strong, tall Anakim descendants. 📖

You know them and heard, “Who can stand against Anak’s sons?”

## Deuteronomy 9:3

Today the LORD your God crosses ahead like a consuming fire. 📖

He will destroy and subdue them.

You will drive them out and destroy them quickly, as He promised.

**Help with these words:** Consuming fire pictures His power to destroy.

Subdue means bring under control by force.

## Deuteronomy 9:4

After He drives them out, do not think, “The LORD brought me here because of my righteousness.” 📖

He drives these nations out because of their wickedness.

**Help with “righteousness”:** This means being right before God.

## Deuteronomy 9:5

Your righteousness or upright heart is not why you will possess their land. 📖

He drives them out because of their wickedness and His sworn promise to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

## Deuteronomy 9:6

He does not give this good land because of your righteousness. 📖

You are stiff-necked people.

**Help with “stiff-necked”:** This pictures stubborn refusal to obey.

## Deuteronomy 9:7

Remember and never forget how you angered the LORD your God in the wilderness. 📖

You rebelled against Him from leaving Egypt until arriving here.

## Deuteronomy 9:8

At Horeb you provoked Him, and He was angry enough to destroy you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:9

I climbed the mountain for the stone tablets of His covenant with you. 📖

I stayed forty days and forty nights without eating bread or drinking water.

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 9:10

He gave me two stone tablets written by God’s finger. 📖

Their words exactly matched His words from the mountain’s fire on the assembly day.

## Deuteronomy 9:11

After forty days and forty nights, He gave me the two stone tablets, the covenant tablets. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:12

The LORD told me, “Go down at once! 📖

Your people, whom you brought from Egypt, corrupted themselves and quickly turned from My commanded way.

They made a molten image.”

**Help with “molten image”:** This is an image made by pouring melted material into a mold.

## Deuteronomy 9:13

He also said, “I see these are stiff-necked people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:14

Leave Me alone so I may destroy them and erase their name from under heaven. 📖

I will make you a greater, stronger nation than them.”

## Deuteronomy 9:15

I descended the mountain, still blazing with fire, holding the two covenant tablets. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:16

I saw your sin against the LORD your God: you made a molten calf. 📖

You quickly left His commanded way.

## Deuteronomy 9:17

I threw the two tablets from my hands, shattering them before your eyes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:18

I fell before the LORD forty days and forty nights, as the first time. 📖

I neither ate bread nor drank water.

This was because all your evil sins provoked His anger.

## Deuteronomy 9:19

I feared His anger and wrath against you, enough to destroy you. 📖

But He listened to me this time too.

## Deuteronomy 9:20

He was angry enough to destroy Aaron, and I prayed for Aaron then too. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:21

I burned the sinful calf you made, crushed it, and ground it to powder fine as dust. 📖

I threw it in the stream flowing down the mountain.

## Deuteronomy 9:22

You kept angering the LORD at Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth-hattaavah. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:23

At Kadesh-barnea He sent you, saying, “Go and possess the land I gave you.” 📖

You rebelled against His command, neither believing nor obeying Him.

## Deuteronomy 9:24

You rebelled against the LORD from the day I knew you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:25

I fell before Him forty days and forty nights because He said He would destroy you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:26

I prayed, “O Lord GOD, do not destroy Your people, Your inheritance. 📖

You redeemed them through Your greatness and brought them from Egypt with a mighty hand.

**Help with these words:** Redeemed means freed from slavery here.

Mighty hand pictures God’s power.

## Deuteronomy 9:27

Remember Your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

Overlook this people’s stubbornness, wickedness, and sin.

## Deuteronomy 9:28

Otherwise, people in the land You brought us from will say this. 📖

‘The LORD could not bring them into His promised land.

He hated them and brought them out to kill them in the wilderness.’

## Deuteronomy 9:29

But they are Your people, Your inheritance, whom You brought out with great power and an outstretched arm.” 📖

**Help with “outstretched arm”:** This pictures God’s power in action.
