# Deuteronomy 34 — Moses sees the land and dies

**About this chapter**

The LORD shows Moses the land he will not enter.

Moses dies, Israel mourns, and Joshua leads.

The chapter remembers Moses’ unique relationship with the LORD and the deeds Israel saw.

## Deuteronomy 34:1

Moses climbed from Moab’s plains to Mount Nebo, Pisgah’s top facing Jericho. 📖

The LORD showed all the land, from Gilead to Dan,

## Deuteronomy 34:2

all Naphtali, Ephraim’s and Manasseh’s land, and all Judah to the Western Sea, 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:3

the Negev, and the region from the Valley of Jericho all the way to Zoar. 📖

Jericho is the City of Palms.

## Deuteronomy 34:4

The LORD said, “This is the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, promising it to their descendants. 📖

I let your own eyes see it, but you will not cross.”

## Deuteronomy 34:5

Moses, the LORD’s servant, died there in Moab as He said. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:6

The LORD buried him in Moab’s valley facing Beth-peor. 📖

No one knows his grave’s location to this day.

## Deuteronomy 34:7

Moses died aged a hundred and twenty. 📖

His eyes were not weak, and his strength had not lessened.

**About these statements:** Chapter 31:2 reports Moses saying he could no longer come and go.

Both that statement and this description are retained without supplying a further explanation.

## Deuteronomy 34:8

Israel mourned Moses on Moab’s plains thirty days, until the weeping and mourning ended. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:9

Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses laid hands on him. 📖

Israel obeyed him and did as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Deuteronomy 34:10

No prophet has since risen in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face— 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:11

none doing all the signs and wonders the LORD sent Moses to do in Egypt. 📖

He did them to Pharaoh, all his officials, and all his land,

## Deuteronomy 34:12

none doing all the mighty acts and awesome deeds Moses did before all Israel. 📖
