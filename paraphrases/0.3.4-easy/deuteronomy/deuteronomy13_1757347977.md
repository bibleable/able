# Deuteronomy 13 — Enticement to other gods

**About this chapter**

Moses continues teaching Israel.

He gives death judgments for leading people to other gods, including within a family or city.

The required investigation and conditions are retained.

## Deuteronomy 13:1

If a prophet or dreamer arises among you and announces a sign or wonder, 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:2

and it happens, but he says, “Follow and worship other gods you have not known,” 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:3

do not listen to him. 📖

The LORD your God tests whether you love Him with all your heart and soul.

## Deuteronomy 13:4

Follow and fear the LORD your God. 📖

Keep His commands, hear His voice, serve Him, and hold fast to Him.

## Deuteronomy 13:5

That prophet or dreamer must die for urging rebellion against the LORD your God. 📖

The LORD brought you from Egypt and redeemed you from slavery.

The prophet tried to turn you from His commanded way.

Remove the evil from among you.

**Help with “redeemed”:** Here this means freed from slavery.

## Deuteronomy 13:6

Suppose your own brother, son, daughter, wife you hold close, or closest friend secretly entices you. 📖

This person says, “Let us go and worship other gods.”

Neither you nor your fathers knew these gods,

## Deuteronomy 13:7

from surrounding peoples, near or far, from one end of earth to the other, 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:8

do not yield or listen. 📖

Show no pity.

Do not spare or shield him.

## Deuteronomy 13:9

You must kill him. 📖

Your hand must be first to put him to death, followed by all the people’s hands.

## Deuteronomy 13:10

Stone him to death for trying to turn you from the LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt’s slavery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:11

All Israel will hear and fear and never do such wickedness among you again. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:12

If you hear about one of the cities He gives you to live in 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:13

that wicked men from among you led its people astray, saying, “Serve other gods you have not known,” 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:14

inquire, investigate, and question thoroughly. 📖

If you establish with certainty that this hateful deed happened among you,

## Deuteronomy 13:15

kill that city’s inhabitants with swords. 📖

Devote all its people and livestock to destruction.

**Help with “devote to destruction”:** Here this means setting apart for complete destruction.

## Deuteronomy 13:16

Gather all its plunder in the public square’s middle. 📖

Burn the whole city and all its plunder as a whole burnt offering to the LORD your God.

It must remain a mound of ruins forever, never rebuilt.

**Help with “plunder”:** This means things taken from others in war or conquest.

## Deuteronomy 13:17

Keep nothing set apart for destruction in your hands. 📖

Then He will turn from fierce anger, grant mercy, show compassion, and multiply you as sworn to your fathers,

## Deuteronomy 13:18

because you obey Him, keeping all His commands I give today and doing right before the LORD your God. 📖
