# Deuteronomy 12 — Worship at the place the LORD chooses

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules for worship in the land.

He distinguishes offerings at the chosen place from meat eaten in the towns.

The chapter also forbids blood consumption and copying the nations’ worship, including burning children.

## Deuteronomy 12:1

Carefully keep these laws and rulings throughout life in the land the LORD, your fathers’ God, gave you to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:2

Completely destroy all the nations’ worship places as you drive them out. 📖

These are on high mountains, hills, and under every green tree.

## Deuteronomy 12:3

Tear down their altars, smash sacred pillars, burn Asherah poles, cut down their gods’ idols, and erase their names everywhere. 📖

**Help with “Asherah poles”:** These are objects used in worship of Asherah.

## Deuteronomy 12:4

Do not worship the LORD your God this way. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:5

Seek the place He chooses from all your tribes as a dwelling for His Name. 📖

Go there.

**About this phrase:** The place is associated with God’s Name and worship.

## Deuteronomy 12:6

Bring burnt offerings, sacrifices, tithes, heave offerings, vow offerings, voluntary offerings, and your herds’ and flocks’ firstborn there. 📖

**Help with these offerings:** A tithe is one-tenth.

Heave offerings are contributions presented to the LORD.

Vow offerings fulfill solemn promises.

Voluntary offerings are freely given.

## Deuteronomy 12:7

There before the LORD your God, you and your households must eat and rejoice in all you do. 📖

He blessed you.

## Deuteronomy 12:8

Do not act as we do here today, everyone doing what seems right to him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:9

You have not yet reached the resting place and inheritance the LORD your God gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:10

Suppose you cross the Jordan and live in the land He gives as an inheritance. 📖

He gives rest from all surrounding enemies, and you live safely.

## Deuteronomy 12:11

Then the LORD your God will choose a dwelling for His Name. 📖

Bring everything I command there: burnt offerings, sacrifices, tithes, special gifts, and all your choice vowed offerings to the LORD.

## Deuteronomy 12:12

Rejoice before the LORD your God, with your sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, and the Levite within your gates. 📖

The Levite has no portion or inheritance among you.

## Deuteronomy 12:13

Do not give burnt offerings just anywhere you see. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:14

Offer them only at the place He chooses in one tribal territory. 📖

Do everything I command there.

## Deuteronomy 12:15

Whenever you want, slaughter and eat meat within any of your gates according to His blessing. 📖

Ceremonially clean and unclean people may eat, as with gazelle or deer.

**Help with “clean” and “unclean”:** These describe conditions under worship rules, not a person’s worth.

## Deuteronomy 12:16

But eat no blood. 📖

Pour it on the ground like water.

## Deuteronomy 12:17

Do not eat these within your gates: tithes of grain, new wine, or oil, or your herds’ or flocks’ firstborn. 📖

Nor eat vowed offerings, voluntary offerings, or special gifts there.

## Deuteronomy 12:18

Eat them before the LORD your God at His chosen place. 📖

Include your sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, and the Levite within your gates.

Rejoice before Him in all you do,

## Deuteronomy 12:19

and never neglect the Levites throughout life in your land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:20

When He enlarges your territory as promised, and you crave meat, saying, “I want meat,” eat whenever you want. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:21

If His chosen place for His Name is too far, slaughter from the herd or flock He gave you. 📖

Do as I commanded and eat within your gates whenever you want.

## Deuteronomy 12:22

Eat it like gazelle or deer. 📖

Ceremonially unclean and clean people may both eat.

## Deuteronomy 12:23

Be sure not to eat blood. 📖

Blood is the life, and you must not eat the life with the meat.

## Deuteronomy 12:24

Do not eat blood. 📖

Pour it on the ground like water.

## Deuteronomy 12:25

Do not eat it, so you and your children after you may fare well. 📖

You will be doing what is right before the LORD.

## Deuteronomy 12:26

Take your holy things and vow offerings to the place He chooses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:27

Put your burnt offerings’ meat and blood on the LORD your God’s altar. 📖

Pour other sacrifices’ blood beside His altar, but you may eat their meat.

## Deuteronomy 12:28

Carefully obey everything I command, so you and your children after you may always fare well. 📖

You will do what is good and right before the LORD your God.

## Deuteronomy 12:29

When He destroys the nations before you, and you drive them out and live in their land, 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:30

be careful their ways do not trap you after their destruction. 📖

Do not ask about their gods, saying, “How do they serve their gods?

I will do the same.”

## Deuteronomy 12:31

Do not worship the LORD your God that way. 📖

They do every hateful thing for their gods that the LORD hates.

They even burn their sons and daughters as sacrifices to their gods.

## Deuteronomy 12:32

Do everything I command. 📖

Add nothing and take nothing away.
