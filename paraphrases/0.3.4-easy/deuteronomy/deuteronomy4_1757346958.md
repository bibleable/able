# Deuteronomy 4 — Remember the covenant and avoid idols

**About this chapter**

Moses continues speaking to Israel about the LORD’s commands and what the people saw.

He warns about idols, judgment, and scattering, and speaks of seeking God again.

From verse 41, the account describes Moses setting aside refuge cities and introducing the law.

## Deuteronomy 4:1

Israel, hear the laws and rulings I teach you to follow. 📖

Then you may live and possess the land the LORD, your fathers’ God, gives you.

## Deuteronomy 4:2

Add nothing to my commands and take nothing away. 📖

Keep the LORD your God’s commands I give you.

## Deuteronomy 4:3

You saw what the LORD did at Baal-peor. 📖

He destroyed all among you who followed Baal of Peor.

## Deuteronomy 4:4

But every one of you who held to the LORD your God is alive today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:5

I taught you laws and rulings as the LORD my God commanded me. 📖

Follow them in the land you are entering to possess.

## Deuteronomy 4:6

Keep them carefully to show other peoples your wisdom and understanding. 📖

Hearing all these laws, they will say, “This great nation is wise and understanding!”

## Deuteronomy 4:7

What great nation has a god so near as the LORD our God whenever we call Him? 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:8

What great nation has righteous laws and rulings like this whole law I give you today? 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:9

Guard yourselves carefully, so you never forget what you saw or let it leave your hearts while you live. 📖

Teach your children and grandchildren.

## Deuteronomy 4:10

At Horeb, you stood before the LORD your God. 📖

He told me, “Gather the people to hear My words.

They must learn to fear Me throughout life on earth and teach their children.”

## Deuteronomy 4:11

You stood at the mountain’s base. 📖

It blazed with fire to heaven, with black clouds and deep darkness.

## Deuteronomy 4:12

The LORD spoke from the fire. 📖

You heard words but saw no form, only heard a voice.

## Deuteronomy 4:13

He declared His covenant and commanded you to follow it: the Ten Commandments, written on two stone tablets. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 4:14

The LORD told me to teach you laws and rulings for the land you cross the Jordan to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:15

You saw no form when the LORD spoke from Horeb’s fire. 📖

Be careful

## Deuteronomy 4:16

not to act corruptly by making any shaped idol, whether like a male or female, 📖

**Help with “idol”:** This means an image made for worship.

## Deuteronomy 4:17

any animal on earth or bird flying in the air, 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:18

any creature crawling on the ground or fish in the waters below. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:19

When you see the sun, moon, stars, and all heaven’s host, do not be drawn into bowing or worshiping them. 📖

The LORD your God allotted these to every nation under heaven.

**Help with “heaven’s host”:** This refers to the things in the sky, including the sun, moon, and stars.

## Deuteronomy 4:20

The LORD brought you from Egypt, the iron furnace, to be His inherited people, as you are today. 📖

**Help with “iron furnace”:** This pictures Egypt as a place of severe suffering.

## Deuteronomy 4:21

The LORD was angry with me because of you. 📖

He swore I would not cross the Jordan into the good land He gives you as an inheritance.

## Deuteronomy 4:22

I must die here and will not cross the Jordan. 📖

But you will cross and possess that good land.

## Deuteronomy 4:23

Be careful not to forget the LORD your God’s covenant with you. 📖

Make no idol in any form He forbade.

## Deuteronomy 4:24

The LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God. 📖

**Help with these words:** Consuming fire pictures His power to destroy.

Jealous here concerns His claim on the people’s undivided worship.

## Deuteronomy 4:25

After children, grandchildren, and many years in the land, suppose you act corruptly and make an idol in any form. 📖

This is evil before the LORD your God and provokes His anger.

## Deuteronomy 4:26

Today I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you. 📖

If you do this, you will quickly perish from the land you cross the Jordan to possess.

You will not live long there but be completely destroyed.

## Deuteronomy 4:27

The LORD will scatter you among peoples. 📖

Only a few will survive among the nations where He drives you.

## Deuteronomy 4:28

There you will serve human-made wooden and stone gods that cannot see, hear, eat, or smell. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:29

But from there, you will find the LORD your God if you seek Him with all your heart and soul. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:30

When you suffer all these things, in later days you will return to the LORD your God and obey Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:31

He is merciful. 📖

He will not abandon or destroy you or forget the covenant He swore to your fathers.

## Deuteronomy 4:32

Ask about times long before you, from God’s creation of humans on earth. 📖

Ask from one end of heaven to the other.

Has anything so great happened or been reported?

## Deuteronomy 4:33

Has any people heard God speak from fire, as you did, and lived? 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:34

Has any god tried to take one nation from another as his own? 📖

Has any done so by trials, signs, wonders, war, strong hand, outstretched arm, and great terrors?

The LORD your God did this in Egypt before your eyes.

**Help with “hand” and “arm”:** These picture God’s power in action.

## Deuteronomy 4:35

You saw these things to know the LORD is God and there is no other. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:36

He let you hear His heavenly voice to discipline you and showed His great fire on earth. 📖

You heard His words from that fire.

## Deuteronomy 4:37

He loved your fathers and chose their descendants. 📖

His presence and great power brought you from Egypt,

## Deuteronomy 4:38

driving out greater, stronger nations to bring you in and give their land as your inheritance, as today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:39

Know today and take to heart: the LORD is God in heaven above and earth below. 📖

There is no other.

## Deuteronomy 4:40

Keep His laws and commands I give today, so you and your children after you may prosper. 📖

Then you may live long in the land the LORD your God gives you for all time.

## Deuteronomy 4:41

Moses set aside three cities east across the Jordan, 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:42

where someone who killed a neighbor without intending to, and without earlier malice, could flee to save his life: 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:43

Bezer was in the wilderness plateau, belonging to Reuben. 📖

Ramoth in Gilead belonged to Gad, and Golan in Bashan belonged to Manasseh.

## Deuteronomy 4:44

This is the law Moses set before Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:45

These are the testimonies, laws, and rulings Moses proclaimed after they left Egypt, 📖

**Help with “testimonies”:** These are God’s declared instructions.

## Deuteronomy 4:46

in the valley across the Jordan facing Beth-peor, in Sihon king of the Amorites’ land. 📖

Sihon lived in Heshbon, and Moses and Israel defeated him after leaving Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 4:47

They took Sihon’s land and Og king of Bashan’s land, the two Amorite kings east across the Jordan, 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:48

from Aroer at Arnon Valley’s edge to Mount Siyon, also called Hermon, 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:49

including all the Arabah east of the Jordan to the Sea of the Arabah, below Pisgah’s slopes. 📖
