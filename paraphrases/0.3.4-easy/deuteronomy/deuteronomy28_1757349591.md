# Deuteronomy 28 — Blessings for obedience and curses for disobedience

**About this chapter**

Moses sets out conditional blessings and curses.

The chapter includes disease, defeat, captivity, sexual violence, famine, and eating children during siege.

The text retains these difficult judgments and gives no modern diagnosis or claim about particular people today.

## Deuteronomy 28:1

“Suppose you faithfully obey the LORD your God and carefully keep all His commands I give today. 📖

Then He will set you high above all earth’s nations.

## Deuteronomy 28:2

All these blessings will come upon and overtake you if you obey Him: 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:3

You will be blessed in city and country. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:4

Your womb’s fruit, land’s produce, and livestock’s young, your herds’ calves and flocks’ lambs, will be blessed. 📖

**Help with “womb’s fruit”:** This means children born to you.

## Deuteronomy 28:5

Your basket and kneading bowl will be blessed. 📖

**Help with “kneading bowl”:** This is used for preparing bread dough.

## Deuteronomy 28:6

You will be blessed coming in and going out. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:7

The LORD will defeat enemies rising against you. 📖

They will come one way but flee seven ways.

## Deuteronomy 28:8

He will command blessing on your barns and everything you do, blessing you in the land He gives. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:9

He will establish you as His holy people as sworn, if you keep His commands and follow His ways. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:10

All earth’s peoples will see you bear the LORD’s Name and stand in awe of you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:11

He will greatly prosper your children, livestock’s young, and land’s produce in the land He swore to your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:12

He will open His abundant storehouse, heaven, to give seasonal rain and bless all your work. 📖

You will lend to many nations, but borrow from none.

## Deuteronomy 28:13

He will make you head, not tail, moving only up, never down, if you hear and carefully keep today’s commands. 📖

These are the LORD your God’s commands I give you.

**Help with this picture:** Head and upward movement picture a leading position.

Tail and downward movement picture a lower position.

## Deuteronomy 28:14

Turn neither right nor left from any word I command today. 📖

Do not follow other gods to serve them.

## Deuteronomy 28:15

But if you disobey Him, failing to carefully keep all His commands and laws I give today, these curses follow. 📖

All will come upon and overtake you:

## Deuteronomy 28:16

You will be cursed in city and country. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:17

Your basket and kneading bowl will be cursed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:18

Your womb’s fruit, land’s produce, herds’ calves, and flocks’ lambs will be cursed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:19

You will be cursed coming in and going out. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:20

He will send curses, confusion, and rebuke against all you do until you are destroyed and quickly perish. 📖

This is because you acted wickedly, forsaking Him.

**Help with “rebuke”:** This means strong disapproval or correction.

## Deuteronomy 28:21

He will make plague cling to you until He wipes you from the land you enter to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:22

He will strike you with wasting disease, fever, inflammation, scorching heat, drought, blight, and mildew. 📖

These will pursue you until you perish.

**Help with these words:** Wasting disease weakens the body.

Inflammation is swelling and irritation.

Blight and mildew damage crops here.

## Deuteronomy 28:23

The sky overhead will be bronze and the earth beneath iron. 📖

**Help with this picture:** These images describe hard, unyielding conditions without rain.

## Deuteronomy 28:24

He will make your land’s rain dust and powder falling from the sky until you are destroyed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:25

The LORD will cause your defeat before your enemies. 📖

You will advance one way but flee seven ways, a horror to every earth kingdom.

## Deuteronomy 28:26

Your corpses will feed all birds and beasts, with no one to scare them away. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:27

He will afflict you with Egypt’s boils, tumors, scabs, and itch that cannot be cured. 📖

**Help with these words:** Boils are painful skin swellings.

The source’s disease descriptions are retained without a modern diagnosis.

## Deuteronomy 28:28

He will afflict you with madness, blindness, and confused minds, 📖

**About “madness”:** This is BSB’s term in this judgment.

No modern diagnosis is supplied.

## Deuteronomy 28:29

and at noon you will grope like a blind man in darkness. 📖

You will not prosper.

Daily you will be oppressed and plundered, with no one to save you.

**Help with these words:** Oppressed means treated cruelly or unfairly.

Plundered means having things taken by force.

## Deuteronomy 28:30

You will be pledged to a woman, but another man will violate her. 📖

You will build a house but not live there, and plant a vineyard but not enjoy its fruit.

## Deuteronomy 28:31

Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, but you will eat none. 📖

Your donkey will be taken and never returned.

Your flock will go to enemies, and no one will save you.

## Deuteronomy 28:32

Your sons and daughters will go to another nation. 📖

Your eyes will tire of seeking them daily, with no power in your hand.

**Help with this picture:** No power in your hand means no power to rescue them.

## Deuteronomy 28:33

Unknown people will eat your land’s produce and all your labor’s results. 📖

All your days you will be oppressed and crushed.

## Deuteronomy 28:34

The sights you see will drive you mad. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:35

He will strike your knees and thighs with painful, incurable boils, from feet’s soles to head’s top. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:36

He will take you and your appointed king to a nation neither you nor your fathers knew. 📖

You will worship other gods there, wooden and stone gods.

## Deuteronomy 28:37

You will face horror, scorn, and ridicule among all nations where He drives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:38

You will plant much seed but harvest little, because locusts will eat it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:39

You will plant and tend vineyards but neither drink wine nor gather grapes, because worms will eat them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:40

You will have olive trees throughout your territory, but the olives will fall off. 📖

You will have no oil to anoint yourself.

## Deuteronomy 28:41

You will father sons and daughters, but they will not remain yours: they will go captive. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:42

Locust swarms will eat all your trees and land’s produce. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:43

The foreigner among you will rise higher and higher while you sink lower and lower. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:44

He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. 📖

He will be head, you tail.

## Deuteronomy 28:45

All these curses will pursue and overtake you until destruction, because you disobeyed His voice, commands, and laws. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:46

They will be a sign and wonder on you and your descendants forever. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:47

Because you did not serve the LORD your God joyfully and gladly in all your plenty, 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:48

you will serve enemies He sends, in famine, thirst, nakedness, and utter poverty. 📖

He will put an iron yoke on your neck until He destroys you.

**Help with “iron yoke”:** A yoke is equipment used to make an animal pull a load.

Here it pictures harsh subjection.

## Deuteronomy 28:49

He will bring a nation from far away, earth’s ends, swooping like an eagle. 📖

You will not understand its language,

## Deuteronomy 28:50

a ruthless nation respecting no old person and pitying no young person. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:51

They will eat your livestock’s young and land’s produce until you are destroyed. 📖

They will leave no grain, new wine, oil, calves, or lambs until you perish.

## Deuteronomy 28:52

They will besiege every city until the high, fortified walls you trust fall. 📖

They will besiege all cities throughout the land He gave you.

**Help with “besiege”:** This means surrounding a place to force its surrender.

## Deuteronomy 28:53

During that enemy siege and distress, you will eat your womb’s fruit. 📖

You will eat the flesh of the sons and daughters the LORD your God gave you.

## Deuteronomy 28:54

Even the gentlest, most refined man will resent his brother, the wife he embraces, and his surviving children, 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:55

refusing any share of his children’s flesh he eats. 📖

Nothing else remains in the enemy siege and distress within all your gates.

## Deuteronomy 28:56

Even the gentlest, most refined woman would not venture to set her foot’s sole on the ground. 📖

She will resent the husband she embraces, her son, and her daughter,

## Deuteronomy 28:57

She will refuse them the afterbirth from between her legs and the children she bears. 📖

She will secretly eat these, lacking anything else in the enemy siege and distress within your gates.

**Help with “afterbirth”:** This is the tissue that comes out after a baby is born.

## Deuteronomy 28:58

Suppose you do not carefully follow every word of this law written in this book. 📖

You must follow it to fear this glorious, awesome Name: the LORD your God,

## Deuteronomy 28:59

He will bring extraordinary disasters on you and your descendants, severe, lasting plagues and terrible, lasting sicknesses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:60

He will bring back all Egypt’s diseases you dreaded, and they will cling to you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:61

He will also bring every sickness and plague not written in this Book of the Law until you are destroyed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:62

Once as many as sky’s stars, you will remain few because you would not obey the LORD your God’s voice. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:63

As He delighted to prosper and multiply you, He will delight to annihilate and destroy you. 📖

You will be uprooted from the land you enter to possess.

## Deuteronomy 28:64

He will scatter you among all nations, from one end of earth to the other. 📖

There you will worship wooden and stone gods unknown to you or your fathers.

## Deuteronomy 28:65

There you will find no rest, not even for your foot’s sole. 📖

He will give a trembling heart, failing eyes, and a despairing soul.

## Deuteronomy 28:66

Your life will hang in doubt. 📖

You will fear night and day, never sure of survival.

## Deuteronomy 28:67

In the morning you will say, ‘If only evening came!’ 📖

In evening, ‘If only morning came!’

Your hearts will dread the terrifying sights you see.

## Deuteronomy 28:68

He will return you to Egypt in ships by a route I said you must never see again. 📖

You will sell yourselves as male and female slaves to enemies, but no one will buy you.”
