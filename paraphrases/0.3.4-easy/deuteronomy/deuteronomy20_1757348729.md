# Deuteronomy 20 — Battle rules and different kinds of cities

**About this chapter**

Moses gives battle instructions and reasons for some men to return home.

He distinguishes distant cities from cities in the inherited land.

Forced labor, killing, taking captives, and the command to spare no breathing thing are retained.

## Deuteronomy 20:1

When fighting enemies, do not fear horses, chariots, or a larger army. 📖

The LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt, is with you.

## Deuteronomy 20:2

Before battle, the priest must come forward and address the army, 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:3

saying, “Hear, Israel! 📖

Today you fight your enemies.

Do not lose heart, fear, be alarmed, or be terrified of them.

## Deuteronomy 20:4

The LORD your God goes with you to fight your enemies and give you victory.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:5

The officers must say, “Has anyone built a new house without dedicating it? 📖

Let him return home, lest he die in battle and someone else dedicate it.

## Deuteronomy 20:6

Has anyone planted a vineyard without enjoying its fruit? 📖

Let him return home, lest he die in battle and someone else enjoy it.

## Deuteronomy 20:7

Has anyone pledged to marry a woman but not married her? 📖

Let him return home, lest he die in battle and another man marry her.”

## Deuteronomy 20:8

The officers must also say, “Is anyone afraid or fainthearted? 📖

Let him return home so his brothers’ hearts do not melt like his.”

**Help with “hearts melt”:** This pictures loss of courage.

## Deuteronomy 20:9

After speaking to the army, the officers must appoint commanders to lead it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:10

When approaching a city to fight it, offer peace. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:11

If it accepts and opens its gates, all its people will serve you as forced laborers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:12

If it refuses peace and fights, lay siege to it. 📖

**Help with “siege”:** This means surrounding a place to force it to surrender.

## Deuteronomy 20:13

When the LORD your God hands it over, kill every male with swords. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:14

Take its women, children, livestock, and everything else as plunder. 📖

Use all the enemy’s spoil He gives you.

**Help with “plunder” and “spoil”:** These are people, animals, and things taken in war here.

## Deuteronomy 20:15

Do this to all distant cities that do not belong to the nearby nations. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:16

But in the nations’ cities He gives as your inheritance, leave nothing breathing alive. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:17

Devote them to complete destruction: Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, as the LORD your God commanded. 📖

**Help with “devote to complete destruction”:** Here this means setting apart for total destruction.

## Deuteronomy 20:18

Then they cannot teach you all their hateful practices for their gods. 📖

They cannot cause you to sin against the LORD your God.

## Deuteronomy 20:19

During a long siege to capture a city, do not axe its trees, because you can eat their fruit. 📖

Do not cut them down.

Are field trees human, for you to besiege them?

## Deuteronomy 20:20

You may destroy trees you know bear no fruit. 📖

Use them for siege works against the city fighting you until it falls.

**Help with “siege works”:** These are structures used to attack a surrounded city.
