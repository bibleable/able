# Deuteronomy 16 — Feasts and justice

**About this chapter**

Moses gives the feast times and the people who must share in the rejoicing.

He commands fair judges and forbids bribes.

The chapter also forbids worship objects beside the LORD’s altar.

## Deuteronomy 16:1

Keep the month of Abib and celebrate Passover to the LORD your God. 📖

He brought you from Egypt by night in Abib.

## Deuteronomy 16:2

Offer Him the Passover sacrifice from herd or flock at His chosen dwelling for His Name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:3

Eat no leavened bread with it. 📖

Eat unleavened bread seven days, the bread of affliction, because you left Egypt in haste.

Remember your departure day throughout life.

**Help with these words:** Leavened bread is made with yeast.

Unleavened bread is made without it.

Affliction means suffering.

## Deuteronomy 16:4

No leaven may be found anywhere in your land seven days. 📖

Leave none of the meat sacrificed on the first evening until morning.

## Deuteronomy 16:5

Do not sacrifice the Passover animal in any town He gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:6

Offer the Passover sacrifice only at His chosen dwelling for His Name. 📖

Do this at evening sunset, the time you left Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 16:7

Roast and eat it at His chosen place. 📖

Return to your tents in the morning.

## Deuteronomy 16:8

Eat unleavened bread six days. 📖

On day seven, hold a solemn assembly to the LORD your God and do no work.

## Deuteronomy 16:9

Count seven weeks from first putting the sickle to standing grain. 📖

**Help with “sickle”:** This is a curved tool for cutting crops.

## Deuteronomy 16:10

Celebrate the Feast of Weeks to Him with a voluntary offering in proportion to His blessing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:11

Rejoice before Him at His chosen dwelling for His Name. 📖

Include your sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, the Levite within your gates, foreigners, the fatherless, and widows among you.

## Deuteronomy 16:12

Remember you were slaves in Egypt and carefully obey these laws. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:13

Keep the Feast of Tabernacles seven days after gathering produce from your threshing floor and winepress. 📖

**Help with “Tabernacles”:** These are temporary shelters used during this feast.

A threshing floor is where grain is separated from stalks.

A winepress is where grapes are squeezed for juice.

## Deuteronomy 16:14

Rejoice in the feast with sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, the Levite, foreigners, the fatherless, and widows among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:15

Celebrate to the LORD your God seven days at His chosen place. 📖

He will bless all your produce and work, so your joy will be complete.

## Deuteronomy 16:16

All your men must appear before Him three times yearly at His chosen place. 📖

These are the Feast of Unleavened Bread, Feast of Weeks, and Feast of Tabernacles.

No one may appear before Him empty-handed.

## Deuteronomy 16:17

Each must appear with a gift as able, according to the LORD your God’s blessing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:18

Appoint judges and officials for your tribes in every town He gives you. 📖

They must judge the people righteously.

**Help with “righteously”:** This means rightly and fairly.

## Deuteronomy 16:19

Do not deny justice, favor someone unfairly, or accept bribes. 📖

Bribes blind wise people’s eyes and twist righteous people’s words.

**Help with these words:** Bribes are payments to gain unfair favors.

The blind-eye picture describes judgment distorted by bribery.

## Deuteronomy 16:20

Pursue justice, and justice alone, so you may live and possess the land He gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:21

Put no wooden Asherah pole beside the altar you build for the LORD your God, 📖

**Help with “Asherah pole”:** This is an object used in worship of Asherah.

## Deuteronomy 16:22

and erect no sacred pillar, which He hates. 📖
