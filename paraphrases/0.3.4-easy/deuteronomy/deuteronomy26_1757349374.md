# Deuteronomy 26 — Firstfruits, the tithe, and declarations

**About this chapter**

Moses gives words to say when presenting firstfruits and reporting the tithe’s distribution.

The declarations recall Egypt, deliverance, and the given land.

The chapter ends with Israel’s and the LORD’s declarations.

## Deuteronomy 26:1

When you enter, possess, and settle the land the LORD your God gives as an inheritance, 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:2

put some firstfruits of all your land’s produce in a basket. 📖

Go to His chosen dwelling for His Name,

**Help with “firstfruits”:** These are the first produce given from a harvest.

## Deuteronomy 26:3

to the priest serving then. 📖

Say, “Today I declare to the LORD your God that I entered the land He swore to our fathers.”

## Deuteronomy 26:4

The priest must take the basket from your hands and set it before the LORD your God’s altar. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:5

Say before Him, “My father was a wandering Aramean. 📖

He went to Egypt with few people and lived there.

There he became a great nation, mighty and numerous.

**Help with these words:** Aramean describes someone associated with Aram.

These words describe an ancestor and his people.

## Deuteronomy 26:6

Egypt mistreated and afflicted us, forcing us into hard labor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:7

We cried to the LORD, our fathers’ God. 📖

He heard and saw our suffering, toil, and oppression.

## Deuteronomy 26:8

He brought us from Egypt with mighty hand, outstretched arm, great terror, signs, and wonders. 📖

**Help with “hand” and “arm”:** These picture God’s power in action.

## Deuteronomy 26:9

He brought us here and gave this land flowing with milk and honey. 📖

**Help with “milk and honey”:** These picture plentiful good food.

## Deuteronomy 26:10

Now I bring the firstfruits of the land You, O LORD, gave me.” 📖

Place the basket before Him and bow down.

## Deuteronomy 26:11

Rejoice with the Levite and foreigner among you in all the good things He gave you and your household. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:12

In year three, the tithe year, suppose you have finished setting aside a tenth of all produce. 📖

Give it to Levites, foreigners, the fatherless, and widows, so they eat and are filled within your gates.

## Deuteronomy 26:13

Then say before Him, “I removed the sacred portion from my house. 📖

I gave it to the Levite, foreigner, fatherless, and widow as You commanded.

I neither broke nor forgot Your commands.

## Deuteronomy 26:14

I did not eat it while mourning, remove it while unclean, or offer it for the dead. 📖

I obeyed the LORD my God and did everything You commanded.

**Help with “unclean”:** This describes a condition under worship rules.

## Deuteronomy 26:15

Look down from Your holy dwelling in heaven. 📖

Bless Your people Israel and the land You gave as sworn to our fathers, flowing with milk and honey.”

## Deuteronomy 26:16

Today He commands these laws and rulings. 📖

Carefully follow them with all your heart and all your soul.

## Deuteronomy 26:17

Today you declared that the LORD is your God. 📖

You will follow His ways, keep His laws, commands, and rulings, and hear His voice.

## Deuteronomy 26:18

Today He declared you His people and treasured possession as promised, to keep all His commands, 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:19

and He will set you high in praise, name, and honor above every nation He made. 📖

You will be holy to the LORD your God, as He promised.
