# Deuteronomy 22 — Care for others and marriage judgments

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules about lost property, animals, clothing, farming, and houses.

He then gives marriage and sexual judgments, including death penalties and a forced marriage obligation.

The differing conditions are retained without adding a claim that silence proves consent.

## Deuteronomy 22:1

If your brother’s ox or sheep strays, do not ignore it. 📖

Return it to him.

## Deuteronomy 22:2

If he lives far away or you do not know him, take the animal home. 📖

Keep it until he comes seeking it, then return it.

## Deuteronomy 22:3

Do the same for his donkey, cloak, or anything lost that you find. 📖

Do not ignore it.

## Deuteronomy 22:4

If his donkey or ox falls on the road, do not ignore it. 📖

Help him lift it.

## Deuteronomy 22:5

A woman must not wear men’s clothing, nor a man women’s clothing. 📖

Anyone doing so is hateful to the LORD your God.

## Deuteronomy 22:6

Suppose you find a bird’s nest on the road, in a tree or on the ground, with chicks or eggs. 📖

If the mother sits on them, do not take her with the young.

## Deuteronomy 22:7

You may take the young, but release the mother, so you may fare well and live long. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:8

When building a new house, put a railing around the roof. 📖

Avoid bloodguilt on your house if someone falls.

**Help with “bloodguilt”:** This means guilt for causing someone’s death.

## Deuteronomy 22:9

Plant no two seed types in your vineyard. 📖

Otherwise both the planted crop and vineyard fruit, the whole harvest, will be defiled.

## Deuteronomy 22:10

Do not plow with an ox and donkey yoked together. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:11

Do not wear wool and linen woven together. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:12

Make tassels on your cloak’s four corners. 📖

**Help with “tassels”:** These are hanging bundles of threads.

## Deuteronomy 22:13

Suppose a man marries a woman, has sex with her, then hates her, 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:14

accusing her of shameful conduct and ruining her name. 📖

He says, “I married and had sex with her, but found she was not a virgin.”

**Help with “virgin”:** This means someone who has not had sex.

## Deuteronomy 22:15

Her father and mother must bring proof of her virginity to the city elders at the gate, 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:16

saying, “I gave this man my daughter in marriage, but he hates her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:17

He accused her of shameful conduct, saying, ‘Your daughter was not a virgin.’ 📖

Here is proof of her virginity.”

They must spread the cloth before the elders.

**About the cloth:** The passage calls this evidence of virginity.

No modern medical test is described.

## Deuteronomy 22:18

The city elders must take and punish the man. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:19

Fine him a hundred silver shekels, paid to her father, for giving a virgin of Israel a bad name. 📖

She must remain his wife, and he must never divorce her.

**Help with “shekel”:** This is a measure of weight.

## Deuteronomy 22:20

But if the charge is true and no proof of her virginity is found, 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:21

bring her to her father’s door, where her city’s men will stone her to death. 📖

She committed an outrage in Israel by sexual promiscuity in her father’s house.

Remove the evil from among you.

**Help with “promiscuity”:** This describes sexual conduct condemned by this ruling.

## Deuteronomy 22:22

If a man is found having sex with another man’s wife, both must die. 📖

Remove the evil from Israel.

## Deuteronomy 22:23

Suppose a virgin is pledged to marry a man. 📖

Another man meets her in the city and has sex with her,

## Deuteronomy 22:24

bring both to that city’s gate and stone them to death. 📖

The young woman dies because she did not cry out in the city.

The man dies because he violated his neighbor’s wife.

Remove the evil from among you.

## Deuteronomy 22:25

But if a man meets a pledged woman in open country, overpowers her, and has sex, only he must die. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:26

Do nothing to her. 📖

She committed no sin deserving death.

This is like a man attacking and murdering his neighbor.

## Deuteronomy 22:27

The pledged woman cried out when he found her in the field, but no one could save her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:28

Suppose a man finds a virgin not pledged to marry, seizes her, has sex with her, and they are discovered. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:29

He must pay her father fifty silver shekels. 📖

She must become his wife because he violated her.

He must never divorce her.

## Deuteronomy 22:30

A man must not marry his father’s wife, dishonoring his father’s marriage bed. 📖

**Help with “marriage bed”:** This refers to his father’s marriage relationship.
