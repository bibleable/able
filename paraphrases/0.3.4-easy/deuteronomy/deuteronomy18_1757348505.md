# Deuteronomy 18 — Priests, forbidden practices, and a prophet

**About this chapter**

Moses describes the priests’ portions and a Levite’s service.

He forbids certain spiritual practices and speaks about a prophet like him.

The judgments for speaking falsely in God’s name are retained.

## Deuteronomy 18:1

The Levitical priests, indeed all Levi’s tribe, have no portion or inheritance with Israel. 📖

They eat the LORD’s food offerings as their inheritance.

## Deuteronomy 18:2

They have no inheritance among their brothers, but the LORD is their inheritance, as He promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:3

From a sacrificed bull or sheep, the priests receive the shoulder, jowls, and stomach. 📖

**Help with “jowls”:** These are the cheek parts.

## Deuteronomy 18:4

Give them your grain’s, new wine’s, and oil’s firstfruits, and the first wool sheared from your flock. 📖

**Help with “firstfruits”:** These are the first produce given from a harvest.

## Deuteronomy 18:5

The LORD your God chose Levi and his sons from all tribes to stand and serve in His name forever. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:6

If a Levite leaves any town where he lives in Israel and earnestly comes to His chosen place, 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:7

then he must serve in the LORD his God’s name, like his fellow Levites standing there before the LORD. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:8

They eat equal portions, even though he received money selling his father’s estate. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:9

When you enter the land He gives, do not copy those nations’ hateful ways. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:10

Let no one sacrifice a son or daughter in fire, practice divination or conjury, interpret omens, or practice sorcery, 📖

**Help with these words:** Divination means seeking hidden knowledge or predictions through spiritual practices.

Omens are signs used to predict events.

Conjury and sorcery are forms of magic named here.

## Deuteronomy 18:11

cast spells, consult a medium or spiritist, or inquire of the dead. 📖

**Help with these words:** Spells are magical sayings or actions.

Mediums and spiritists claim to contact spirits or the dead.

## Deuteronomy 18:12

Anyone doing these things is hateful to the LORD. 📖

Because of these hateful things, He drives those nations out before you.

## Deuteronomy 18:13

Be blameless before the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:14

These nations you drive out listen to conjurers and diviners. 📖

The LORD your God has not permitted you to do so.

## Deuteronomy 18:15

He will raise a prophet like me from your brothers. 📖

You must listen to him.

## Deuteronomy 18:16

You asked the LORD your God for this at Horeb on the assembly day. 📖

You said, “Let us no longer hear the LORD our God’s voice.

Let us no longer see this great fire, so we do not die!”

## Deuteronomy 18:17

The LORD told me, “They spoke well. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:18

I will raise a prophet like you from their brothers. 📖

I will put My words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command.

## Deuteronomy 18:19

I will hold accountable anyone not listening to My words spoken by that prophet in My name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:20

Suppose a prophet dares to speak a message in My name I did not command. 📖

Or he speaks in other gods’ names.

That prophet must be put to death.”

## Deuteronomy 18:21

You may wonder, “How do we recognize a message the LORD did not speak?” 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:22

Suppose a prophet speaks in the LORD’s name and the message does not happen or come true. 📖

The LORD did not speak that message.

The prophet spoke presumptuously.

Do not fear him.

**Help with “presumptuously”:** This means claiming authority he was not given.
