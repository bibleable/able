# Deuteronomy 5 — The Ten Commandments and the people’s request

**About this chapter**

Moses speaks to all Israel about the covenant at Horeb.

He repeats the Ten Commandments and recalls the people’s request for him to hear God for them.

The commands and their reasons are retained.

## Deuteronomy 5:1

Moses called all Israel: “Hear the laws and rulings I declare today. 📖

Learn and carefully obey them.

## Deuteronomy 5:2

The LORD our God made a covenant with us at Horeb. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 5:3

He made it with all of us alive here today, not with our fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:4

The LORD spoke with you face to face from fire on the mountain. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:5

I stood between Him and you to declare His word. 📖

You feared the fire and would not climb the mountain.

He said,

## Deuteronomy 5:6

‘I am the LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt, the house of slavery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:7

Have no other gods before Me. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:8

Make no idol shaped like anything in heaven above, earth below, or the waters beneath. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:9

Do not bow to idols or worship them. 📖

I, the LORD your God, am jealous.

I punish fathers’ wrongdoing in their children to the third and fourth generations of those hating Me,

**Help with “jealous”:** Here this concerns God’s claim on the people’s undivided worship.

## Deuteronomy 5:10

but show faithful love to a thousand generations of those loving Me and keeping My commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:11

Do not take the LORD your God’s name in vain. 📖

The LORD will not leave anyone doing so unpunished.

**Help with “in vain”:** This means treating God’s name as worthless or misusing it.

## Deuteronomy 5:12

Keep the Sabbath day holy, as the LORD your God commanded. 📖

**Help with “Sabbath”:** This is the weekly day set apart for rest.

## Deuteronomy 5:13

Work and do all your labor six days, 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:14

but day seven is a Sabbath to the LORD your God. 📖

No one may work: you, your son, daughter, male servant, female servant, ox, donkey, or any livestock.

Nor may the foreigner within your gates work, so your male and female servants rest like you.

## Deuteronomy 5:15

Remember being a slave in Egypt. 📖

The LORD your God brought you out with a mighty hand and outstretched arm.

That is why He commanded you to keep the Sabbath.

**Help with “hand” and “arm”:** These picture His power in action.

## Deuteronomy 5:16

Honor your father and mother, as the LORD your God commanded. 📖

Then you may live long and fare well in the land He gives you.

## Deuteronomy 5:17

Do not murder. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:18

Do not commit adultery. 📖

**Help with “adultery”:** This means sex that breaks a marriage’s faithfulness.

## Deuteronomy 5:19

Do not steal. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:20

Do not give false witness against your neighbor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:21

Do not covet your neighbor’s wife. 📖

Do not covet his house, field, male servant, female servant, ox, donkey, or anything belonging to him.’

**Help with “covet”:** This means wrongly wanting to have what belongs to someone else.

## Deuteronomy 5:22

The LORD spoke these commands loudly to your whole assembly from the mountain’s fire, cloud, and deep darkness. 📖

He added nothing more.

He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me.

## Deuteronomy 5:23

You heard the voice from darkness while the mountain burned. 📖

All your tribal heads and elders approached me,

## Deuteronomy 5:24

saying, ‘The LORD our God showed His glory and greatness. 📖

We heard Him from the fire.

Today we saw a person can live even when God speaks to him.

## Deuteronomy 5:25

Why should we die now? 📖

If we hear the LORD our God’s voice longer, this great fire will consume us and we will die.

## Deuteronomy 5:26

Who among all flesh heard the living God speak from fire, as we did, and survived? 📖

**Help with “all flesh”:** This expression refers to living beings.

## Deuteronomy 5:27

Go near and hear everything the LORD our God says. 📖

Tell us everything He tells you, and we will listen and obey.’

## Deuteronomy 5:28

The LORD heard your words to me. 📖

He told me, ‘I heard what these people said to you.

Everything they said was good.

## Deuteronomy 5:29

If only their hearts would fear Me and always keep all My commands! 📖

Then they and their children might fare well forever.

## Deuteronomy 5:30

Tell them to return to their tents. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:31

But you must stand here with Me. 📖

I will tell you all the commands, laws, and rulings you must teach them.

They must follow these in the land I give them to possess.’

## Deuteronomy 5:32

Carefully do as the LORD your God commanded. 📖

Turn neither right nor left.

## Deuteronomy 5:33

Follow all the ways He commanded, so you may live, prosper, and stay long in the land you will possess.” 📖
