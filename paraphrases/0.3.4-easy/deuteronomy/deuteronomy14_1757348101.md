# Deuteronomy 14 — Food rules and the tithe

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules about mourning, food, and the tenth set aside from produce.

The animal names and descriptions follow BSB.

The chapter includes feasting at the chosen place and providing food for others.

## Deuteronomy 14:1

You are sons of the LORD your God. 📖

Do not cut yourselves or shave your foreheads for the dead.

## Deuteronomy 14:2

You are holy to Him. 📖

He chose you from all earth’s peoples as His prized possession.

## Deuteronomy 14:3

Eat no detestable thing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:4

You may eat oxen, sheep, goats, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:5

deer, gazelles, roe deer, wild goats, ibex, antelopes, and mountain sheep. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:6

You may eat any animal that both has a hoof split in two and chews the cud. 📖

**Help with “chews the cud”:** This means chewing food brought back up from the stomach.

## Deuteronomy 14:7

But do not eat camels, rabbits, or rock badgers among those chewing cud or having a fully divided hoof. 📖

Although they chew cud, they lack divided hooves and are unclean for you.

**About these words:** The animal names and descriptions here follow BSB.

“Unclean” describes the food rules given to Israel.

## Deuteronomy 14:8

Pigs are also unclean: their hooves are divided, but they do not chew cud. 📖

Eat no pig meat or touch its carcass.

**Help with “carcass”:** This means a dead animal’s body.

## Deuteronomy 14:9

Among water creatures, you may eat anything with fins and scales, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:10

but eat nothing lacking either fins or scales. 📖

It is unclean for you.

## Deuteronomy 14:11

You may eat any clean bird, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:12

but not the eagle, bearded vulture, black vulture, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:13

red kite, falcon, any kind of kite, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:14

any kind of raven, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:15

ostrich, screech owl, gull, any kind of hawk, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:16

little owl, great owl, white owl, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:17

desert owl, osprey, cormorant, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:18

stork, any kind of heron, hoopoe, or bat. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:19

All flying insects are unclean for you. 📖

Do not eat them.

## Deuteronomy 14:20

But you may eat any clean bird. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:21

Eat no carcass. 📖

You may give it to a foreigner within your gates to eat or sell it to a foreigner.

You are a holy people belonging to the LORD your God.

Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.

## Deuteronomy 14:22

Set aside one-tenth of all your fields’ produce every year. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:23

Eat the tenth of grain, new wine, and oil, and your herds’ and flocks’ firstborn before the LORD your God. 📖

Eat at His chosen dwelling for His Name, so you learn always to fear Him.

## Deuteronomy 14:24

If His chosen place for His Name is too far to carry what He blessed you with, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:25

exchange it for money, take the money in your hand, and go to His chosen place. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:26

You may spend it on whatever you want: cattle, sheep, wine, strong drink, or anything you wish. 📖

Feast before the LORD your God and rejoice with your household.

## Deuteronomy 14:27

Do not neglect the Levite within your gates. 📖

He has no portion or inheritance among you.

## Deuteronomy 14:28

At the end of every three years, store a tenth of all that year’s produce within your gates. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:29

The Levite, foreigner, fatherless, and widow within your gates may come, eat, and be satisfied. 📖

The Levite has no portion or inheritance among you.

The LORD your God will bless all your work.
