# Deuteronomy 27 — Written law and spoken curses

**About this chapter**

Moses and the elders give instructions for stones and an altar after crossing the Jordan.

Moses assigns tribes to the two mountains.

Each curse and each required “Amen” is included.

## Deuteronomy 27:1

Moses and Israel’s elders commanded, “Keep all the commands I give today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:2

On the day you cross the Jordan into the land the LORD your God gives, set up large stones. 📖

Coat them with plaster.

## Deuteronomy 27:3

After crossing into His given land, write all this law’s words on them. 📖

It flows with milk and honey, as the LORD, your fathers’ God, promised.

**Help with “milk and honey”:** These picture plentiful good food.

## Deuteronomy 27:4

After crossing the Jordan, set these stones on Mount Ebal and plaster them, as I command today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:5

Build a stone altar to the LORD your God there. 📖

Use no iron tool on the stones.

## Deuteronomy 27:6

Build His altar with uncut stones and give burnt offerings to Him on it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:7

Sacrifice peace offerings there, eat them, and rejoice before Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:8

Write all this law’s words clearly on the stones.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:9

Moses and the Levitical priests told all Israel, “Be silent and listen! 📖

Today you became the LORD your God’s people.

**Help with “Levitical priests”:** These are priests of Levi’s tribe.

## Deuteronomy 27:10

Obey His voice and keep His commands and laws I give today.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:11

That day Moses commanded, 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:12

“After crossing the Jordan, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin must stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:13

Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali must stand on Mount Ebal to deliver the curse. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:14

The Levites must proclaim loudly to every Israelite: 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:15

‘Cursed is the man making a carved idol or molten image, hateful to the LORD, a craftsman’s handiwork. 📖

He sets it up secretly.’

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

**Help with these words:** A molten image is made by pouring melted material into a mold.

Amen expresses agreement: let it be so.

## Deuteronomy 27:16

‘Cursed is anyone dishonoring father or mother.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:17

‘Cursed is anyone moving a neighbor’s boundary stone.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:18

‘Cursed is anyone letting a blind man wander in the road.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:19

‘Cursed is anyone denying justice to foreigners, the fatherless, or widows.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:20

‘Cursed is anyone having sex with his father’s wife, violating his father’s marriage bed.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

**Help with “marriage bed”:** This refers to his father’s marriage relationship.

## Deuteronomy 27:21

‘Cursed is anyone having sex with any animal.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:22

‘Cursed is anyone having sex with his sister, his father’s daughter or his mother’s daughter.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:23

‘Cursed is anyone having sex with his mother-in-law.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:24

‘Cursed is anyone striking down his neighbor secretly.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:25

‘Cursed is anyone taking a bribe to kill an innocent person.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’

## Deuteronomy 27:26

‘Cursed is anyone not practicing this law’s words.’ 📖

All the people must say, ‘Amen!’”
