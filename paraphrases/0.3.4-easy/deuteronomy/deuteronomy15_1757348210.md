# Deuteronomy 15 — Debt release, generosity, and servants

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules about debt release and help for poor people.

He gives release rules for a Hebrew man or woman sold into service, and a rule for remaining.

The chapter ends with firstborn animal rules.

## Deuteronomy 15:1

Cancel debts at the end of every seven years. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:2

Every lender must cancel what he lent his neighbor and collect nothing from his neighbor or brother. 📖

The LORD’s release time has been proclaimed.

## Deuteronomy 15:3

You may collect from a foreigner, but forgive whatever your brother owes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:4

There will be no poor among you because the LORD will bless you in the land He gives as inheritance, 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:5

if you obey Him and carefully follow all these commands I give today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:6

When He blesses you as promised, you will lend to many nations but borrow from none. 📖

You will rule many nations but none will rule you.

## Deuteronomy 15:7

Suppose a poor brother lives within any gates in the land the LORD your God gives. 📖

Do not harden your heart or shut your hand to him.

**Help with these pictures:** A hardened heart pictures refusal to care.

A shut hand pictures refusal to give.

## Deuteronomy 15:8

Open your hand and freely lend whatever he needs. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:9

Do not think wickedly, “The seventh year of release is near,” and refuse your poor brother with resentment. 📖

He will cry to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin.

## Deuteronomy 15:10

Give generously without grieving in your heart. 📖

Because of this, the LORD your God will bless all your work and everything you do.

## Deuteronomy 15:11

There will always be poor people in the land. 📖

So I command you to open your hand wide to your brother and the poor and needy there.

## Deuteronomy 15:12

Suppose a fellow Hebrew, man or woman, is sold to you and serves six years. 📖

Then release that person in year seven.

## Deuteronomy 15:13

Do not send the released person away empty-handed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:14

Give generously from your flock, threshing floor, and winepress, as the LORD your God blessed you. 📖

**Help with these places:** A threshing floor is where grain is separated from stalks.

A winepress is where grapes are squeezed for juice.

## Deuteronomy 15:15

Remember you were slaves in Egypt and He redeemed you. 📖

That is why I give this command today.

**Help with “redeemed”:** Here this means freed from slavery.

## Deuteronomy 15:16

But if your servant says, “I do not want to leave,” loving you and your household and faring well there, 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:17

use an awl to pierce his ear into the door. 📖

He will serve you for life.

Treat your female servant the same way.

**Help with “awl”:** This is a pointed tool for making holes.

## Deuteronomy 15:18

Do not think release a hardship. 📖

Six years’ service was worth twice a hired worker’s wages.

The LORD your God will bless all you do.

## Deuteronomy 15:19

Set apart every firstborn male of your herds and flocks to Him. 📖

Do not work firstborn oxen or shear your flock’s firstborn.

## Deuteronomy 15:20

Each year, you and your household must eat it before Him at His chosen place. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:21

Suppose an animal has a defect, is lame or blind, or has any serious flaw. 📖

Do not sacrifice it to the LORD your God.

**Help with “lame”:** Here this describes an animal with difficulty walking.

## Deuteronomy 15:22

Eat it within your gates. 📖

Ceremonially unclean and clean people may eat, as with gazelle or deer.

**Help with “clean” and “unclean”:** These describe conditions under worship rules, not a person’s worth.

## Deuteronomy 15:23

Eat no blood. 📖

Pour it on the ground like water.
