# Deuteronomy 30 — Return to the LORD and choose life

**About this chapter**

Moses speaks of return after scattering and the LORD’s compassion.

The promises retain their conditions of returning and obeying wholeheartedly.

Moses sets life and death before the people and calls them to choose life.

## Deuteronomy 30:1

“Suppose the blessings and curses I set before you come upon you. 📖

You remember them in every nation where the LORD your God banished you,

## Deuteronomy 30:2

and you and your children return and obey the LORD your God with all your heart and soul. 📖

Do this according to everything I give today,

## Deuteronomy 30:3

then He will restore you from captivity, show compassion, and gather you from every nation where He scattered you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:4

Even from the farthest horizon where you were banished, He will gather and return you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:5

He will bring you into your fathers’ land to possess it, prospering and multiplying you more than your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:6

The LORD your God will circumcise your hearts and your descendants’ hearts. 📖

You will love Him with all your heart and all your soul, so you may live.

**Help with this picture:** Circumcision physically removes the foreskin.

Circumcising hearts pictures inward change toward wholehearted love for God.

## Deuteronomy 30:7

He will put all these curses on enemies who hate and persecute you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:8

You will again obey His voice and keep all His commands I give today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:9

He will make your work, children, livestock’s young, and land’s produce abound. 📖

He will again delight in your prosperity, as in your fathers’,

## Deuteronomy 30:10

This requires obeying the LORD your God’s commands and laws written in this Book of the Law. 📖

It also requires turning to Him with all your heart and all your soul.

## Deuteronomy 30:11

Today’s command is not too hard or beyond your reach. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:12

It is not in heaven, requiring you to ask, ‘Who will climb there, get it, and proclaim it for obedience?’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:13

Nor is it beyond the sea, requiring, ‘Who will cross, get it, and proclaim it so we may obey?’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:14

The word is very near, in your mouth and heart, so you may obey. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:15

Today I set life and prosperity before you, and death and disaster. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:16

I command you to love the LORD your God, follow His ways, and keep His commands, laws, and rulings. 📖

Then you may live, increase, and be blessed by Him in the land you enter to possess.

## Deuteronomy 30:17

But if your heart turns away and you refuse to hear, drawn to bow and worship other gods, 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:18

I declare today: you will surely perish. 📖

You will not live long in the land you cross the Jordan to possess.

## Deuteronomy 30:19

I call heaven and earth as witnesses today. 📖

I set before you life and death, blessing and curse.

Choose life, so you and your descendants may live,

## Deuteronomy 30:20

love the LORD your God, obey, and hold fast to Him. 📖

He is your life and will prolong it in the land sworn to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”
