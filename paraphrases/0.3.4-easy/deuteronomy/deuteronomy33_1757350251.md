# Deuteronomy 33 — Moses’ blessing on Israel

**About this chapter**

Before dying, Moses blesses Israel and its tribes.

The names, prayers, images, and violent language are retained.

The blessing does not name Simeon separately.

## Deuteronomy 33:1

This is the blessing Moses, the man of God, gave Israel before dying. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:2

He said, “The LORD came from Sinai, dawning on us from Seir and shining from Mount Paran. 📖

He came with tens of thousands of holy ones, flaming fire at His right hand.

## Deuteronomy 33:3

You love the people. 📖

All holy ones are in Your hand, sitting at Your feet, each receiving Your words—

## Deuteronomy 33:4

Moses’ law, the possession of Jacob’s assembly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:5

The LORD became King in Jeshurun when leaders gathered and Israel’s tribes came together. 📖

**Help with “Jeshurun”:** This is a name for Israel.

## Deuteronomy 33:6

Let Reuben live, not die, and his men not be few.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:7

For Judah he said, “O LORD, hear Judah’s cry and bring him to his people. 📖

His hands defend his cause; may You help against his foes.”

## Deuteronomy 33:8

For Levi he said, “Give Your Thummim to Levi, Your Urim to Your godly one. 📖

You tested him at Massah and contested him at Meribah’s waters.

**Help with “Thummim” and “Urim”:** These name priestly means of seeking God’s guidance.

Their operation is not explained here.

## Deuteronomy 33:9

He said of his parents, ‘I do not consider them,’ disregarding his brothers and not knowing his own sons. 📖

He kept Your word and covenant.

**About these words:** The blessing describes loyalty to God over family ties.

## Deuteronomy 33:10

He will teach Your rulings to Jacob and Your law to Israel. 📖

He will place incense before You and whole burnt offerings on Your altar.

## Deuteronomy 33:11

O LORD, bless his substance and accept his hands’ work. 📖

Smash the loins of those rising against him and his foes, so they never rise again.”

**Help with these words:** Substance concerns Levi’s resources here.

Loins refer to the lower body around the hips.

## Deuteronomy 33:12

For Benjamin he said, “May the LORD’s beloved rest secure in Him. 📖

God shields him all day, and he rests on His shoulders.”

**Help with this picture:** Resting on God’s shoulders pictures safety and care.

## Deuteronomy 33:13

For Joseph he said, “May the LORD bless his land with precious dew from heaven and deep waters below, 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:14

with plentiful harvest from the sun and abundant seasonal yield, 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:15

with the best of ancient mountains and the bounty of everlasting hills, 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:16

with choice gifts of land and all in it, and favor from Him who dwelt in the burning bush. 📖

May these rest on Joseph’s head and crown the brow of his brothers’ prince.

## Deuteronomy 33:17

His majesty is like a firstborn bull, his horns like a wild ox’s. 📖

With them he will gore nations to earth’s ends.

Such are Ephraim’s tens of thousands and Manasseh’s thousands.”

**Help with “gore”:** This means pierce with horns.

## Deuteronomy 33:18

For Zebulun he said, “Rejoice in your journeys, Zebulun, and in your tents, Issachar. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:19

They will call peoples to a mountain and offer righteous sacrifices there. 📖

For they will feast on the seas’ abundance and sand’s hidden treasures.”

## Deuteronomy 33:20

For Gad he said, “Blessed is he who enlarges Gad’s domain! 📖

He lies like a lion, tearing off an arm or head.

## Deuteronomy 33:21

He chose the best land, where a ruler’s portion was reserved for him. 📖

He came with the people’s leaders and carried out the LORD’s justice and rulings for Israel.”

## Deuteronomy 33:22

For Dan he said, “Dan is a lion’s cub, leaping from Bashan.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:23

For Naphtali he said, “Naphtali overflows with favor and the LORD’s blessing. 📖

He will possess the sea and south.”

## Deuteronomy 33:24

For Asher he said, “May Asher be most blessed of sons, most favored among brothers, dipping his foot in oil. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Dipping a foot in oil pictures plentiful oil.

## Deuteronomy 33:25

May your gate bolts be iron and bronze, your strength matching your days.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:26

“No one is like Jeshurun’s God, riding heaven and clouds in majesty to help you. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The image shows God coming powerfully to help His people.

## Deuteronomy 33:27

The eternal God is your dwelling, His everlasting arms underneath. 📖

He drives out enemies, commanding, ‘Destroy him!’

**Help with these pictures:** Dwelling and arms picture God’s shelter and support.

## Deuteronomy 33:28

Israel lives safely, Jacob’s fountain untroubled in grain and new wine land, where heaven drips dew. 📖

**Help with “Jacob’s fountain”:** The fountain image is used for Jacob’s people.

## Deuteronomy 33:29

Blessed Israel, who is like you, a people saved by the LORD? 📖

He is your protecting shield and the sword you boast in.

Enemies will cower, and you will trample their high places.”

**Help with these pictures:** Shield and sword picture God’s protection and power.

High places are worship sites.
