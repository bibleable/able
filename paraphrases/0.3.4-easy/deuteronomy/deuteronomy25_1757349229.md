# Deuteronomy 25 — Punishment, family duties, and honest measures

**About this chapter**

Moses gives rules about beatings, oxen, marriage duties, and fair measures.

The chapter includes a command to cut off a woman’s hand.

It ends by commanding Israel to erase Amalek’s memory.

## Deuteronomy 25:1

Men with a dispute must go to court for judgment, acquitting the innocent and condemning the guilty. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:2

If the guilty man deserves beating, the judge must have him lie down and be whipped before him. 📖

Give the number of lashes the crime deserves.

## Deuteronomy 25:3

Give no more than forty lashes, lest further beating degrade your brother before your eyes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:4

Do not muzzle an ox treading out grain. 📖

**Help with “muzzle”:** This means covering its mouth to stop it eating.

## Deuteronomy 25:5

If brothers live together and one dies without a son, his widow must not marry outside the family. 📖

His brother must marry her and fulfill a brother-in-law’s duty.

## Deuteronomy 25:6

Her first son must carry the dead brother’s name, so it is not erased from Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:7

If he refuses, the widow must go to the elders at the city gate. 📖

She must say, “My husband’s brother refuses to preserve his brother’s name in Israel.

He will not perform a brother-in-law’s duty for me.”

## Deuteronomy 25:8

They must summon and speak with him. 📖

If he persists, saying, “I do not want to marry her,”

## Deuteronomy 25:9

she must approach before the elders, remove his sandal, and spit in his face. 📖

She must say, “This is done to the man refusing to maintain his brother’s line.”

## Deuteronomy 25:10

His family in Israel will be called “The House of the Unsandaled.” 📖

**Help with “Unsandaled”:** This means having a sandal removed.

## Deuteronomy 25:11

Suppose two men fight, and one man’s wife comes to rescue him from the one striking him. 📖

She reaches out and grabs the attacker’s genitals,

**Help with “genitals”:** This means the sex organs.

## Deuteronomy 25:12

cut off her hand. 📖

Show no pity.

## Deuteronomy 25:13

Keep no two different weights in your bag, one heavy and one light. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:14

Keep no two different measures in your house, one large and one small. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:15

Keep accurate, honest weights and measures, so you may live long in the land the LORD your God gives. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:16

Everyone dishonest in these things is hateful to the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:17

Remember what the Amalekites did on your way from Egypt, 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:18

how they met you tired and weary and attacked all who lagged behind. 📖

They did not fear God.

## Deuteronomy 25:19

When He gives rest from surrounding enemies in your inherited land, erase Amalek’s memory from under heaven. 📖

Do not forget!
