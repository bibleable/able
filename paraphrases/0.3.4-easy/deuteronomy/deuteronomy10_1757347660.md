# Deuteronomy 10 — New tablets and the call to love and obey

**About this chapter**

Moses recalls the new tablets and the ark.

The chapter includes a journey note and the Levites’ role.

Moses calls Israel to inward obedience and love for foreigners.

## Deuteronomy 10:1

Then the LORD told me, “Chisel two stone tablets like the first ones. 📖

Come up the mountain to Me and make a wooden ark.

**Help with “ark”:** Here this means a chest.

## Deuteronomy 10:2

I will write the words from the tablets you broke on these. 📖

Put them in the ark.”

## Deuteronomy 10:3

I made an acacia-wood ark, chiseled two tablets like the first, and climbed the mountain holding them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:4

The LORD wrote the same Ten Commandments spoken from the mountain’s fire on the assembly day. 📖

He gave me the tablets,

## Deuteronomy 10:5

and I descended and put them in the ark I made, as commanded. 📖

They remain there.

## Deuteronomy 10:6

Israel traveled from Beeroth Bene-jaakan to Moserah, where Aaron died and was buried. 📖

His son Eleazar became priest after him.

**About the location:** This verse names Moserah.

Numbers 20 and 33 name Mount Hor for Aaron’s death.

The differing descriptions remain for further editorial review.

## Deuteronomy 10:7

They traveled from there to Gudgodah, then from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land with water streams. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:8

The LORD set Levi’s tribe apart to carry His covenant ark, stand before Him serving, and bless in His name. 📖

They still do this.

## Deuteronomy 10:9

Levi therefore has no portion or inheritance among his brothers. 📖

The LORD is his inheritance, as the LORD your God promised.

## Deuteronomy 10:10

I stayed forty days and forty nights on the mountain, like the first time. 📖

The LORD again heard me and agreed not to destroy you.

## Deuteronomy 10:11

He told me, “Rise and travel ahead of the people. 📖

They must enter and possess the land I swore to give their fathers.”

## Deuteronomy 10:12

Israel, what does the LORD your God ask except that you fear Him and follow all His ways? 📖

Love Him and serve Him with all your heart and soul,

## Deuteronomy 10:13

and keep His commands and laws I give today for your good. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:14

The heavens, even the highest heavens, and earth with everything in it belong to Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:15

Yet He loved your fathers and set His affection on them. 📖

He chose you, their descendants, above all peoples, as today.

## Deuteronomy 10:16

Circumcise your hearts and stop stiffening your necks. 📖

**Help with these pictures:** Circumcision physically removes the foreskin.

Here, circumcising hearts calls for inward obedience.

Stiff necks picture stubborn refusal to obey.

## Deuteronomy 10:17

The LORD your God is God of gods and Lord of lords, great, mighty, and awesome. 📖

He shows no favoritism and accepts no bribe.

**Help with these words:** Favoritism means unfair preference for someone.

A bribe is a payment to get an unfair favor.

## Deuteronomy 10:18

He gives justice to the fatherless and widow, and loves foreigners, giving them food and clothing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:19

Love foreigners too, because you were foreigners in Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:20

Fear and serve the LORD your God. 📖

Hold fast to Him and swear your oaths in His name.

## Deuteronomy 10:21

He is your praise and your God. 📖

He did these great, awesome wonders you saw.

## Deuteronomy 10:22

Your fathers went to Egypt, seventy altogether. 📖

Now the LORD your God made you as many as the stars in the sky.
