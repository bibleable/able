# Deuteronomy 23 — Assembly rules, camp rules, and obligations

**About this chapter**

Moses gives assembly restrictions, camp rules, and instructions about slaves, prostitution, loans, vows, and food.

The restrictions and body terms are retained with labeled explanations.

The rules distinguish brothers and foreigners.

## Deuteronomy 23:1

No man with crushed or cut-off genitals may enter the LORD’s assembly. 📖

**Help with “genitals”:** This means the sex organs.

## Deuteronomy 23:2

No one of illegitimate birth, or any descendant even to generation ten, may enter the LORD’s assembly. 📖

**About “illegitimate birth”:** This is BSB’s term for a birth status.

The category is not explained further here.

It does not describe a person’s value.

## Deuteronomy 23:3

No Ammonite, Moabite, or their descendants may enter the LORD’s assembly, even to generation ten. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:4

They did not meet you with food and water when you left Egypt. 📖

They hired Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram-naharaim to curse you.

## Deuteronomy 23:5

The LORD your God refused to hear Balaam and turned the curse into blessing because He loves you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:6

Never seek peace or prosperity from them throughout your life. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:7

Do not despise an Edomite: he is your brother. 📖

Do not despise an Egyptian: you lived as a foreigner in his land.

## Deuteronomy 23:8

The third generation of children born to them may enter the LORD’s assembly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:9

When camped against enemies, keep away from every wicked thing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:10

If a man becomes unclean from a nocturnal emission, he must leave camp and stay outside. 📖

**Help with these words:** A nocturnal emission is a nighttime release of semen.

Unclean refers to the passage’s worship rules.

## Deuteronomy 23:11

Near evening, he must wash in water. 📖

At sunset he may return.

## Deuteronomy 23:12

Have a place outside camp to relieve yourself. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:13

Keep a digging tool with your equipment. 📖

Dig a hole when relieving yourself and cover your excrement.

**Help with “excrement”:** This means solid body waste.

## Deuteronomy 23:14

The LORD your God walks through camp to protect you and hand over enemies. 📖

Keep camp holy, lest He see something unclean and turn away.

## Deuteronomy 23:15

Do not return a slave to his master if he seeks refuge with you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:16

Let him live among you wherever he chooses, in the town he likes. 📖

Do not oppress him.

**Help with “oppress”:** This means treating someone cruelly or unfairly.

## Deuteronomy 23:17

No daughter or son of Israel may be a shrine prostitute. 📖

**Help with “shrine prostitute”:** This describes a person providing sex in connection with shrine worship.

## Deuteronomy 23:18

Do not bring a female or male prostitute’s wages into the LORD your God’s house to fulfill any vow. 📖

Both are hateful to Him.

## Deuteronomy 23:19

Charge your brother no interest on money, food, or any other loan. 📖

**Help with “interest”:** This is extra charged for lending.

## Deuteronomy 23:20

You may charge a foreigner interest, but not your brother. 📖

Then the LORD your God may bless all you do in the land you enter to possess.

## Deuteronomy 23:21

If you vow to the LORD your God, do not delay fulfilling it. 📖

He will surely require it from you.

If you delay, you will be guilty of sin.

## Deuteronomy 23:22

But making no vow is no sin. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:23

Carefully fulfill what you say, because you freely vowed to Him with your own mouth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:24

In your neighbor’s vineyard, you may eat your fill of grapes. 📖

Put none in your basket.

## Deuteronomy 23:25

In his grainfield, you may pick grain heads by hand. 📖

Do not use a sickle on his grain.

**Help with “sickle”:** This is a curved tool for cutting crops.
