# Deuteronomy 8 — Remember the LORD in hardship and plenty

**About this chapter**

Moses recalls the wilderness and warns Israel about pride in the good land.

He connects the people’s survival and future wealth with the LORD’s care and power.

The warning about following other gods remains conditional.

## Deuteronomy 8:1

Carefully keep every command I give today, so you may live, multiply, and possess the land sworn to your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:2

Remember the LORD your God leading you through the wilderness forty years. 📖

He humbled and tested you to know your heart, whether you would keep His commands.

## Deuteronomy 8:3

He humbled you and fed you manna in your hunger, food neither you nor your fathers knew. 📖

This taught that people live on every word from the LORD’s mouth, not bread alone.

**Help with “manna”:** This is the food given during the wilderness journey.

## Deuteronomy 8:4

Your clothes did not wear out or your feet swell in those forty years. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:5

Know in your heart: the LORD your God disciplines you as a man disciplines his son. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:6

Keep His commands, follow His ways, and fear Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:7

He brings you into a good land with brooks, fountains, and springs flowing through valleys and hills, 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:8

a land of wheat, barley, vines, fig trees, pomegranates, olive oil, and honey, 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:9

where food is plentiful and you lack nothing. 📖

Its rocks contain iron, and its hills can be mined for copper.

## Deuteronomy 8:10

When you eat and are satisfied, bless the LORD your God for the good land He gave you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:11

Be careful not to forget Him by failing to keep His commands, rulings, and laws I give today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:12

Otherwise, when you eat your fill and build fine houses to live in, 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:13

when herds and flocks grow and silver, gold, and everything you own multiply, 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:14

your heart will grow proud. 📖

You will forget the LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt, the house of slavery.

## Deuteronomy 8:15

He led you through the vast, terrifying wilderness, with venomous snakes, scorpions, thirst, and no water. 📖

He brought water from flint rock.

**Help with these words:** Venomous snakes have poison in their bites.

Flint is a hard kind of stone.

## Deuteronomy 8:16

He fed you wilderness manna your fathers never knew, humbling and testing you so you might prosper in the end. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:17

You might think, “My own hands’ power and strength made this wealth.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:18

Remember: the LORD your God gives the power to gain wealth, confirming His covenant sworn to your fathers, as today. 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 8:19

If you forget Him and follow other gods, worshiping and bowing to them, I testify today: you will surely perish. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:20

Like the nations the LORD destroyed before you, you will perish if you disobey the LORD your God. 📖
