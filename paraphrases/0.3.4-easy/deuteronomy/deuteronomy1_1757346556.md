# Deuteronomy 1 — Moses recalls the journey and the refusal

**About this chapter**

Moses speaks to all Israel east of the Jordan.

From verse 6, he recalls events using “I,” “we,” and “you.”

He repeats the people’s accusations as their words and the LORD’s replies as His words.

## Deuteronomy 1:1

Moses spoke these words to all Israel in the wilderness east of the Jordan. 📖

They were in the Arabah opposite Suph, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab.

## Deuteronomy 1:2

The journey from Horeb to Kadesh-barnea by Mount Seir takes eleven days. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:3

On day one of month eleven in year forty, Moses told Israel everything the LORD commanded him concerning them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:4

This followed his defeat of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon. 📖

Then at Edrei he defeated Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth.

## Deuteronomy 1:5

East of the Jordan in Moab, Moses began explaining this law: 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:6

The LORD our God told us at Horeb, “You stayed at this mountain long enough. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:7

Resume your journey to the Amorites’ hill country and all the neighboring peoples. 📖

Go to the Arabah, hills, foothills, Negev, and seacoast, to Canaanite land and Lebanon.

Go as far as the great River Euphrates.

## Deuteronomy 1:8

I placed the land before you. 📖

Enter and possess it.

The LORD swore to give it to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob and their descendants after them.”

## Deuteronomy 1:9

Then I told you, “I cannot carry your burden alone. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:10

The LORD your God multiplied you until you are as many as the stars in the sky. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:11

May the LORD, your fathers’ God, multiply you a thousand times and bless you as He promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:12

How can I carry your troubles, burdens, and disputes alone? 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:13

Choose wise, understanding, respected men from each tribe, and I will appoint them as your leaders.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:14

You replied, “What you propose is good.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:15

I took your wise, respected tribal leaders and appointed them over you. 📖

They were commanders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens, and officers for your tribes.

## Deuteronomy 1:16

I told your judges, “Hear disputes between your brothers. 📖

Judge fairly between a man and his brother or a resident foreigner.

## Deuteronomy 1:17

Do not favor anyone in judgment. 📖

Hear small and great alike.

Fear no one, because judgment belongs to God.

Bring cases too hard for you to me, and I will hear them.”

## Deuteronomy 1:18

At that time, I commanded everything you were to do. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:19

We obeyed the LORD our God and left Horeb for the Amorites’ hills. 📖

We crossed the vast, terrifying wilderness you saw.

At Kadesh-barnea,

## Deuteronomy 1:20

I said, “You reached the Amorites’ hill country, which the LORD our God gives us. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:21

The LORD your God put the land before you. 📖

Go and possess it, as the LORD, your fathers’ God, said.

Do not fear or lose courage.”

## Deuteronomy 1:22

You all approached me, saying, “Let us send men ahead to search the land. 📖

They can report the route to follow and cities to enter.”

## Deuteronomy 1:23

I thought the plan good and chose twelve men, one from each tribe. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:24

They entered the hills, reached Eshcol’s valley, and scouted the land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:25

They carried some fruit from the land down to us in their hands. 📖

They said, “The land the LORD our God gives us is good.”

## Deuteronomy 1:26

But you refused to enter and rebelled against the LORD your God’s command. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:27

You complained in your tents: “The LORD hates us. 📖

He brought us from Egypt to hand us to the Amorites to be destroyed.

## Deuteronomy 1:28

Where can we go? 📖

Our brothers melted our hearts, saying the people are bigger and taller than us.

Their cities are large, with walls reaching heaven.

They even saw the Anakim’s descendants!”

**Help with these pictures:** Melting hearts pictures loss of courage.

Walls reaching heaven describes the frightening height claimed in the people’s complaint.

## Deuteronomy 1:29

I told you, “Do not be terrified or afraid of them! 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:30

The LORD your God goes ahead and will fight for you, as you saw Him do in Egypt 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:31

and the wilderness. 📖

There He carried you like a man carries his son, the whole journey until you reached here.”

**Help with this picture:** Carrying a son pictures God’s care for the people.

## Deuteronomy 1:32

Even so, you did not trust the LORD your God, 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:33

who went ahead with fire at night and cloud by day to find campsites and show your road. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:34

The LORD heard your words, became angry, and swore, 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:35

“None of this evil generation’s men will see the good land I swore to your fathers, 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:36

except Caleb son of Jephunneh. 📖

He will see it.

I will give him and his descendants the land he walked on because he followed the LORD wholeheartedly.”

## Deuteronomy 1:37

The LORD was angry with me too because of you. 📖

He said, “Even you will not enter.

## Deuteronomy 1:38

Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, will enter. 📖

Encourage him, because he will enable Israel to inherit it.

## Deuteronomy 1:39

Your little ones, whom you said would be captives, will enter. 📖

Your children did not know good from evil that day.

I will give them the land, and they will possess it.

## Deuteronomy 1:40

But turn back toward the wilderness by the Red Sea route.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:41

You replied, “We sinned against the LORD. 📖

We will go and fight as the LORD our God commanded.”

Everyone put on weapons, thinking entering the hills would be easy.

## Deuteronomy 1:42

The LORD told me, “Tell them not to go and fight. 📖

I am not with you to keep your enemies from defeating you.”

## Deuteronomy 1:43

I told you, but you would not listen. 📖

You rebelled against the LORD’s command and entered the hills with arrogant confidence.

**Help with “arrogant”:** This means wrongly sure of yourself despite the warning.

## Deuteronomy 1:44

The Amorites from the hills attacked and chased you like a swarm of bees. 📖

They defeated you from Seir all the way to Hormah.

## Deuteronomy 1:45

You returned and wept before the LORD, but He would not hear or listen to you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:46

So you stayed in Kadesh a long time, a very long time. 📖
