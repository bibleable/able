# Judges 4 — Deborah, Barak, and Jael

**About this chapter**

Israel cries for help after twenty years under Jabin.

Deborah summons Barak, and the LORD routs Sisera’s army.

Jael kills Sisera with a tent peg; the act and its details are retained.

## Judges 4:1

After Ehud died, Israel again did evil before the LORD. 📖

## Judges 4:2

He sold them to Jabin king of Canaan, reigning in Hazor. 📖

Sisera commanded his forces and lived in Harosheth-hagoyim.

## Judges 4:3

Israel cried to the LORD: Jabin had nine hundred iron chariots and oppressed them harshly twenty years. 📖

## Judges 4:4

Deborah, a woman prophet and wife of Lappidoth, judged Israel then. 📖

## Judges 4:5

She sat under the Palm of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in Ephraim’s hills. 📖

Israel came to her for judgment.

## Judges 4:6

She summoned Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali. 📖

“The LORD, Israel’s God, commands: ‘Take ten thousand men from Naphtali and Zebulun to Mount Tabor.

## Judges 4:7

I will draw Jabin’s commander Sisera, his chariots, and troops to the River Kishon and hand him over.’” 📖

## Judges 4:8

Barak said, “If you come, I will go. 📖

If not, I will not go.”

## Judges 4:9

Deborah replied, “I will certainly come, but this road brings you no honor. 📖

The LORD will sell Sisera into a woman’s hand.”

She went with him to Kedesh,

## Judges 4:10

where he summoned Zebulun and Naphtali. 📖

Ten thousand followed him, and Deborah went too.

## Judges 4:11

Heber the Kenite separated from the Kenites, descendants of Hobab, Moses’ father-in-law. 📖

He pitched his tent near Kedesh by Zaanannim’s great tree.

## Judges 4:12

Sisera heard Barak son of Abinoam climbed Mount Tabor, 📖

## Judges 4:13

and called all nine hundred iron chariots and men from Harosheth-hagoyim to the River Kishon. 📖

## Judges 4:14

Deborah told Barak, “Rise! 📖

Today the LORD hands Sisera over.

Has He not gone ahead?”

Barak descended Tabor with ten thousand following.

## Judges 4:15

The LORD routed Sisera, all charioteers, and all his army by sword before Barak. 📖

Sisera left his chariot and fled on foot.

## Judges 4:16

Barak pursued chariots and army to Harosheth-hagoyim. 📖

Sisera’s whole army fell by sword; no man remained.

## Judges 4:17

Sisera fled on foot to Jael, wife of Heber the Kenite. 📖

Jabin king of Hazor and Heber’s house were at peace.

## Judges 4:18

Jael greeted him: “Come in, my lord. 📖

Do not fear.”

He entered her tent, and she covered him with a blanket.

## Judges 4:19

Sisera asked, “Please give me a little water because I am thirsty.” 📖

She opened a container of milk, gave him a drink, and covered him again.

## Judges 4:20

He said, “Stand at the tent entrance. 📖

If anyone asks, ‘Is a man here?’ say, ‘No.’”

## Judges 4:21

Exhausted, he slept. 📖

Jael, Heber’s wife, quietly approached with a tent peg and hammer.

She drove the peg through his temple into the ground, and he died.

**Help with “temple”:** Here this means the side of the head near the eye.

## Judges 4:22

Barak came pursuing Sisera. 📖

Jael greeted him: “Come see the man you seek.”

He entered with her and saw Sisera dead, the peg through his temple.

## Judges 4:23

God subdued Jabin king of Canaan before Israel that day. 📖

## Judges 4:24

Israel grew stronger and stronger against Jabin king of Canaan until they destroyed him. 📖
