# Judges 10 — Tola, Jair, and Israel’s distress

**About this chapter**

Tola and Jair judge Israel.

Israel serves other gods and is oppressed again.

The people confess and remove their foreign gods while Ammon gathers for war.

## Judges 10:1

After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, son of Dodo, an Issacharite, arose to save Israel. 📖

He lived in Shamir in Ephraim’s hills.

## Judges 10:2

Tola judged Israel twenty-three years. 📖

He died and was buried in Shamir.

## Judges 10:3

Jair the Gileadite followed, judging Israel twenty-two years. 📖

## Judges 10:4

Jair had thirty sons who rode thirty donkeys. 📖

They had thirty towns in Gilead, still called Havvoth-jair today.

## Judges 10:5

Jair died and was buried in Kamon. 📖

## Judges 10:6

The Israelites again did evil in the LORD’s sight. 📖

They served the Baals and Ashtoreths, and the gods of Aram, Sidon, and Moab.

They also served the Ammonites’ and Philistines’ gods.

They abandoned the LORD and did not serve Him.

## Judges 10:7

The LORD’s anger burned against Israel. 📖

He sold them into the hands of the Philistines and Ammonites,

**Help with this picture:** Being sold into their hands describes being handed over to their power.

## Judges 10:9

Ammon crossed the Jordan against Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim’s house. 📖

Israel suffered deeply.

## Judges 10:10

The Israelites cried to the LORD, “We sinned against You. 📖

We abandoned our God and served the Baals.”

## Judges 10:13

## Judges 10:15

Israel replied, “We sinned. 📖

Do as You see fit, but save us today!”

## Judges 10:16

They removed foreign gods and served the LORD. 📖

He could no longer bear Israel’s misery.

## Judges 10:17

The Ammonites were called to arms and camped in Gilead. 📖

The Israelites gathered and camped at Mizpah.

## Judges 10:18

Gilead’s rulers said, “Whoever leads the attack on Ammon will lead all Gilead’s inhabitants.” 📖
