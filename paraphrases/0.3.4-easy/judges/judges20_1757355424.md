# Judges 20 — Israel’s war against Benjamin

**About this chapter**

Israel gathers to hear about the killing at Gibeah.

Benjamin refuses to hand over the men responsible.

The chapter records inquiries to the LORD, Israel’s losses, an ambush, and Benjamin’s defeat.

## Judges 20:1

All Israel came from Dan to Beersheba and Gilead. 📖

They gathered together as one before the LORD at Mizpah.

## Judges 20:2

Leaders of all the people and all Israel’s tribes appeared in the assembly of God’s people. 📖

There were 400,000 men on foot, armed with swords.

## Judges 20:3

The Benjamites heard that the Israelites had gone to Mizpah. 📖

The Israelites asked, “Tell us how this wicked thing happened.”

## Judges 20:4

The Levite, husband of the murdered woman, replied, “My concubine and I came to Gibeah in Benjamin for the night. 📖

**Help with “concubine”:** This means a woman in a marriage relationship with lower status than a wife.

## Judges 20:5

At night Gibeah’s men rose against me and surrounded the house. 📖

They intended to kill me.

They abused my concubine, and she died.

**About this report:** These are the Levite’s words.

Judges 19:25 also records that he sent her outside to the men, who raped and abused her.

## Judges 20:6

I cut my concubine into pieces and sent her throughout the land of Israel’s inheritance. 📖

They had committed a lewd and disgraceful act in Israel.

**Help with these words:** Inheritance here means land received by Israel.

Lewd describes shameful sexual behavior.

## Judges 20:7

All you Israelites, give your advice and decision here and now.” 📖

## Judges 20:8

All the people stood together as one and said, “None of us will return to his tent or house. 📖

## Judges 20:9

This is what we will do to Gibeah. 📖

We will attack as the lot directs.

**Help with “lot”:** A lot is an object used to make a choice.

## Judges 20:10

We will take ten men from each hundred in all Israel’s tribes. 📖

We will take a hundred from each thousand and a thousand from each ten thousand.

These men will supply the army when it goes to Gibeah in Benjamin.

The army will punish them for the terrible act committed in Israel.”

## Judges 20:11

All Israel’s men gathered together as one, united against the city. 📖

## Judges 20:12

Israel’s tribes sent men throughout Benjamin’s tribe, saying, “What is this wickedness among you? 📖

## Judges 20:13

Hand over Gibeah’s wicked men so we can kill them and remove this evil from Israel.” 📖

The Benjamites refused to listen to their fellow Israelites.

## Judges 20:14

They came together from their cities at Gibeah to fight the Israelites. 📖

## Judges 20:15

That day Benjamin gathered 26,000 swordsmen from its cities, besides Gibeah’s 700 chosen men. 📖

## Judges 20:16

Among all these soldiers were 700 chosen left-handed men. 📖

Each could sling a stone at a hair without missing.

**Help with “sling”:** This means throw a stone using a strap made for that purpose.

## Judges 20:17

Israel, apart from Benjamin, gathered 400,000 swordsmen, all experienced warriors. 📖

## Judges 20:18

The Israelites went to Bethel and asked God, “Who should go first to fight Benjamin?” 📖

The LORD replied, “Judah will be first.”

## Judges 20:19

The next morning the Israelites set out and camped near Gibeah. 📖

## Judges 20:20

Israel’s men went to fight Benjamin and took battle positions at Gibeah. 📖

## Judges 20:21

The Benjamites came out of Gibeah and killed 22,000 Israelites on the battlefield that day. 📖

## Judges 20:22

Israel’s army took courage. 📖

They took battle positions again in the same place as on the first day.

## Judges 20:23

They went up and wept before the LORD until evening. 📖

They asked Him, “Should we again go into battle against our brothers, the Benjamites?”

The LORD replied, “Go up against them.”

## Judges 20:24

On the second day, Israel advanced against Benjamin. 📖

## Judges 20:25

That day the Benjamites came out from Gibeah and killed another 18,000 Israelites, all armed with swords. 📖

## Judges 20:26

All the Israelites went to Bethel and sat weeping before the LORD. 📖

They fasted until evening and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings to the LORD.

**Help with these words:** Fasting means going without food.

A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

A peace offering is another kind of sacrifice to God.

## Judges 20:27

The Israelites asked the LORD. 📖

In those days God’s covenant ark was there,

**Help with “covenant ark”:** This was the sacred chest connected with God’s covenant, His binding agreement.

## Judges 20:28

and Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, served before it. 📖

Israel asked, “Should we fight our brothers, the Benjamites, again, or stop?”

The LORD replied, “Fight, for tomorrow I will hand them over to you.”

## Judges 20:29

Israel placed men in hiding around Gibeah for an ambush. 📖

**Help with “ambush”:** This means a surprise attack from a hiding place.

## Judges 20:30

On the third day, Israel advanced against Benjamin and took positions against Gibeah as before. 📖

## Judges 20:31

The Benjamites came out and were drawn away from the city. 📖

They began attacking as before, killing about thirty Israelites in the fields and on the roads.

One road led to Bethel, and the other to Gibeah.

## Judges 20:32

The Benjamites said, “We are defeating them as before!” 📖

But Israel’s men said, “Let us retreat and draw them from the city onto the roads.”

## Judges 20:33

All Israel’s men rose from their places and took positions at Baal-tamar. 📖

Israel’s men in ambush charged from their positions west of Gibeah.

## Judges 20:34

Ten thousand chosen men from all Israel attacked Gibeah from the front. 📖

The battle was fierce, but the Benjamites did not realize disaster was upon them.

## Judges 20:35

The LORD defeated Benjamin before Israel. 📖

That day the Israelites slaughtered 25,100 Benjamites, all armed with swords.

## Judges 20:36

Then the Benjamites realized they were defeated. 📖

Israel’s men had retreated before Benjamin because they relied on the ambush set against Gibeah.

## Judges 20:37

The men in ambush suddenly rushed against Gibeah. 📖

They advanced and killed the whole city with swords.

## Judges 20:38

Israel’s men had arranged a signal with those in ambush. 📖

When they sent up a great cloud of smoke from the city,

## Judges 20:39

Israel’s men would turn in battle. 📖

When the Benjamites began killing about thirty Israelites, they said, “Israel is defeated before us, as in the first battle.”

## Judges 20:40

But when the smoke column began rising, the Benjamites looked behind them. 📖

They saw the whole city going up in smoke.

## Judges 20:41

Then Israel’s men turned back against them. 📖

Benjamin’s men were terrified when they realized disaster had come upon them.

## Judges 20:42

They fled from Israel’s men toward the wilderness, but the battle overtook them. 📖

The men coming from the cities struck them down there.

## Judges 20:43

Israel’s men surrounded and pursued the Benjamites. 📖

They easily caught up with them near Gibeah on the east.

## Judges 20:44

Eighteen thousand Benjamites fell, all brave men. 📖

## Judges 20:45

The Benjamites fled toward the wilderness to Rimmon’s rock. 📖

Israel killed 5,000 on the roads.

They caught up with them at Gidom and killed 2,000 more.

## Judges 20:46

That day 25,000 Benjamite swordsmen fell, all brave men. 📖

**About the numbers:** Verse 35 gives 25,100, and this verse gives 25,000.

Both source numbers are retained.

## Judges 20:47

But 600 men fled into the wilderness to Rimmon’s rock. 📖

They stayed there four months.

## Judges 20:48

Israel’s men turned back against the other Benjamites. 📖

They put all the cities to the sword, including animals and everything else they found.

They burned all cities in their path.

**Help with “put to the sword”:** This means kill with swords.
