# Judges 2 — Unfaithfulness and the judges

**About this chapter**

The angel of the LORD speaks at Bochim.

The account recalls Joshua’s death and describes Israel’s recurring unfaithfulness.

The LORD raises judges to rescue them but leaves nations to test them.

## Judges 2:1

The LORD’s angel went from Gilgal to Bochim. 📖

He said, “I brought you from Egypt into the land promised to your fathers.

I said, ‘I will never break My covenant with you,

**Help with “covenant”:** This means a solemn agreement.

## Judges 2:2

and you must make none with this land’s people, but tear down their altars.’ 📖

You disobeyed My voice.

What have you done?

## Judges 2:3

I will not drive these peoples out. 📖

They will be thorns in your sides, their gods a snare.”

**Help with these pictures:** Thorns picture harm and suffering.

A snare is a trap.

## Judges 2:4

All Israel heard the LORD’s angel and cried aloud, weeping. 📖

## Judges 2:5

They named the place Bochim and sacrificed to the LORD there. 📖

## Judges 2:6

After Joshua dismissed the people, each went to possess his inheritance. 📖

## Judges 2:7

Israel served the LORD throughout Joshua’s life and the lives of elders who outlived him. 📖

Those elders saw all the LORD’s great works for Israel.

## Judges 2:8

Joshua son of Nun, the LORD’s servant, died aged 110. 📖

## Judges 2:9

They buried him in his inheritance at Timnath-heres, Ephraim’s hills north of Mount Gaash. 📖

## Judges 2:10

That generation was gathered to their fathers. 📖

The next did not know the LORD or His works for Israel.

**Help with “gathered to their fathers”:** This is an expression used here for death.

## Judges 2:11

Israel did evil before the LORD and served the Baals. 📖

## Judges 2:12

They abandoned the LORD, their fathers’ God, who brought them from Egypt. 📖

They followed various surrounding peoples’ gods, bowed to them, and angered Him,

## Judges 2:13

abandoning Him for Baal and the Ashtoreths. 📖

## Judges 2:14

His anger burned against Israel. 📖

He handed them to plunderers and sold them to surrounding enemies they could not resist.

**Help with “plunderers”:** These are people who take others’ possessions by force.

## Judges 2:15

Wherever Israel marched, His hand brought disaster as He swore. 📖

They were greatly distressed.

**Help with “hand”:** This pictures God’s power acting against them.

## Judges 2:16

The LORD raised judges who rescued them from plunderers. 📖

**Help with “judges”:** Here these are leaders raised up to rescue Israel.

## Judges 2:17

Israel ignored the judges, prostituting themselves with other gods and bowing to them. 📖

They quickly left their fathers’ way of obeying His commands, not doing as their fathers did.

**Help with this picture:** Prostitution describes unfaithfulness to God here.

## Judges 2:18

Whenever He raised a judge, He was with him and rescued Israel while the judge lived. 📖

He pitied their groaning under those oppressing and afflicting them.

## Judges 2:19

After the judge died, they grew more corrupt than their fathers, serving and bowing to other gods. 📖

They would not give up evil practices and stubborn ways.

## Judges 2:20

The LORD grew angry: “They broke the covenant I commanded their fathers and ignored My voice, 📖

## Judges 2:21

so I will no longer drive out any nations Joshua left at death. 📖

## Judges 2:22

This will test whether Israel follows the LORD’s way as their fathers did.” 📖

## Judges 2:23

That is why He left those nations, not immediately driving them out by handing them to Joshua. 📖
