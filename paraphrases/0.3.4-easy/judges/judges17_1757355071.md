# Judges 17 — Micah makes a place of worship

**About this chapter**

This story describes Micah’s actions and beliefs.

It does not say that God approved them.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## Judges 17:1

A man named Micah lived in the hill country of Ephraim. 📖

## Judges 17:2

He told his mother, “I took your 1,100 shekels of silver. 📖

I heard you curse the thief.

I have the silver.”

His mother said, “May the LORD bless my son!”

**Help with a word:** A shekel was a unit used to weigh silver.

## Judges 17:3

Micah returned the silver. 📖

His mother said, “I set this silver apart for the LORD for my son's benefit.

It will make a carved image and a cast idol.

I will give it back to you.”

**Help with words:** An idol is an object worshipped as a god.

A cast idol is made by pouring melted metal into a shape.

## Judges 17:4

Micah returned the money to his mother. 📖

She gave 200 shekels of silver to a metalworker.

He made the carved image and cast idol.

They were placed in Micah's house.

## Judges 17:5

Micah had a shrine. 📖

He made an ephod and household idols.

He appointed one of his sons as priest.

**Help with words:** A shrine is a place for worship.

An ephod is a garment connected with priestly worship.

## Judges 17:6

Israel had no king then. 📖

Everyone did what seemed right to them.

## Judges 17:7

A young Levite had been living in Bethlehem in Judah, among the people of Judah. 📖

**Help with a word:** Levites belonged to a tribe set apart for religious service.

## Judges 17:8

He left Bethlehem to find somewhere to live. 📖

On his journey, he came to Micah's house in Ephraim's hill country.

## Judges 17:9

Micah asked where he came from. 📖

He answered, “I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah.

I am looking for somewhere to live.”

## Judges 17:10

Micah said, “Stay and be my father and priest. 📖

I will give you 10 shekels of silver each year, clothes, and food.”

The Levite went inside.

**Help with a title:** Father here means a respected religious guide.

## Judges 17:11

The Levite agreed to stay. 📖

Micah treated him like a son.

## Judges 17:12

Micah appointed the Levite as his priest. 📖

The young man lived in Micah's house.

## Judges 17:13

Micah said, “Now I know the LORD will be good to me. 📖

A Levite has become my priest.”
