# Judges 8 — Gideon’s pursuit and what followed

**About this chapter**

Gideon pursues Midian’s kings and punishes Succoth and Penuel.

He kills Zebah and Zalmunna, refuses to rule, and makes an ephod that becomes a snare.

The chapter records his family, death, and Israel’s return to the Baals.

## Judges 8:1

Ephraim’s men demanded, “Why exclude us from the fight against Midian?” 📖

They argued violently with Gideon.

## Judges 8:2

He replied, “What did I do compared with you? 📖

Are Ephraim’s gleanings not better than Abiezer’s grape harvest?

**Help with this picture:** Gleanings are grapes left after harvest.

Gideon compares their accomplishments with his.

## Judges 8:3

God handed you Midian’s two princes Oreb and Zeeb. 📖

What did I do compared to you?”

Their anger cooled.

## Judges 8:4

Gideon and three hundred crossed the Jordan, exhausted but pursuing. 📖

## Judges 8:5

He asked Succoth, “Give my tired troops bread. 📖

I pursue Zebah and Zalmunna, Midian’s kings.”

## Judges 8:6

The leaders asked, “Are Zebah and Zalmunna’s hands already in your possession, that we should feed your army?” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Having them in your possession describes having captured them.

## Judges 8:7

He replied, “When the LORD hands Zebah and Zalmunna over, I will tear your flesh with wilderness thorns and briers!” 📖

## Judges 8:8

He asked Penuel the same, and they answered like Succoth. 📖

## Judges 8:9

He told Penuel, “When I return victorious, I will tear down this tower!” 📖

## Judges 8:10

Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with about fifteen thousand, all remaining eastern troops. 📖

A hundred and twenty thousand swordsmen had fallen.

## Judges 8:11

Gideon took the caravan route east of Nobah and Jogbehah, attacking the army by surprise. 📖

## Judges 8:12

The two Midianite kings fled. 📖

He captured Zebah and Zalmunna and routed their whole army.

## Judges 8:13

Gideon son of Joash returned by the Ascent of Heres. 📖

## Judges 8:14

He captured and questioned a young man of Succoth. 📖

The youth wrote names of its seventy-seven leaders and elders.

## Judges 8:15

Gideon told Succoth, “Here are Zebah and Zalmunna. 📖

You mocked: ‘Are Zebah and Zalmunna’s hands already in your possession, that we should give bread to your weary men?’”

## Judges 8:16

He took the elders and punished Succoth’s men with wilderness thorns and briers. 📖

## Judges 8:17

He tore down Penuel’s tower and killed the city’s men. 📖

## Judges 8:18

Gideon asked Zebah and Zalmunna, “What kind of men did you kill at Tabor?” 📖

They answered, “Men like you, each like a king’s son.”

## Judges 8:19

“My brothers, my mother’s sons!” he replied. 📖

“As the LORD lives, had you spared them, I would not kill you.”

## Judges 8:20

He told his firstborn Jether, “Kill them.” 📖

The youth feared and did not draw his sword because he was young.

## Judges 8:21

The kings said, “Kill us yourself: as the man, so his strength.” 📖

Gideon killed Zebah and Zalmunna and took crescent ornaments from their camels’ necks.

**Help with “crescent”:** This is a curved shape like a partly lit moon.

## Judges 8:22

Israel told Gideon, “Rule us, you, your son, and grandson. 📖

You saved us from Midian.”

## Judges 8:23

He replied, “Neither I nor my son will rule you. 📖

The LORD will.”

## Judges 8:24

He asked each for an earring from their plunder. 📖

Their enemies had gold earrings because they were Ishmaelites.

## Judges 8:25

They gladly agreed, spread a garment, and each threw an earring from his plunder onto it. 📖

## Judges 8:26

The requested gold earrings weighed 1,700 shekels, besides crescents, pendants, Midianite kings’ purple garments, and camels’ neck chains. 📖

**Help with “shekel”:** This is a measure of weight.

## Judges 8:27

From all this Gideon made an ephod and placed it in Ophrah, his hometown. 📖

Soon all Israel prostituted themselves by worshiping it, a snare to Gideon and his household.

**Help with these words:** An ephod is connected with priestly worship.

This verse does not describe its form further.

Prostitution pictures unfaithfulness to God, and a snare is a trap.

## Judges 8:28

Midian was subdued and never raised its head again. 📖

The land rested forty years under Gideon,

## Judges 8:29

who returned home and settled, Jerubbaal son of Joash. 📖

## Judges 8:30

Gideon had seventy sons of his own because he had many wives. 📖

## Judges 8:31

His concubine in Shechem bore another son, whom he named Abimelech. 📖

**Help with “concubine”:** This means a woman in a recognized relationship with a man, with lower status than a wife.

## Judges 8:32

Gideon son of Joash died very old and was buried in his father Joash’s tomb in Ophrah of the Abiezrites. 📖

## Judges 8:33

As soon as Gideon died, Israel turned and prostituted themselves with the Baals, making Baal-berith their god. 📖

## Judges 8:34

They forgot the LORD their God, who rescued them from all surrounding enemies. 📖

## Judges 8:35

They showed no kindness to Jerubbaal’s house, Gideon’s family, for all his good done to Israel. 📖
