# Judges 15 — Samson’s revenge and the jawbone

**About this chapter**

Samson returns at wheat harvest and finds his wife given away.

Revenge brings burning crops, deaths, and a fight at Lehi.

Samson prays for water after killing a thousand men.

## Judges 15:1

At wheat harvest, Samson visited his wife with a young goat. 📖

He said, “I want to go to my wife in her room.”

But her father would not let him enter.

## Judges 15:2

Her father said, “I was sure you thoroughly hated her, so I gave her to your companion. 📖

Is her younger sister not more beautiful?

Please take her instead.”

## Judges 15:3

Samson told them, “This time I will be blameless in harming the Philistines.” 📖

## Judges 15:4

Samson caught three hundred foxes. 📖

He turned them tail-to-tail and fastened a torch between each pair of tails.

## Judges 15:5

He lit the torches and released the foxes into the Philistines’ standing grain. 📖

He burned the piled grain, standing grain, vineyards, and olive groves.

## Judges 15:6

Philistines asked who did it. 📖

They heard, “Samson, the Timnite’s son-in-law; his wife went to his companion.”

They burned her and her father to death.

## Judges 15:7

Samson said, “Because you did this, I will not rest until I take revenge on you.” 📖

## Judges 15:8

He slaughtered them ruthlessly, then stayed in the cave at Etam’s rock. 📖

## Judges 15:9

The Philistines went up, camped in Judah, and spread out near the town of Lehi. 📖

## Judges 15:10

Judah’s men asked, “Why have you attacked us?” 📖

The Philistines replied, “We came to arrest Samson and pay him back for what he did to us.”

## Judges 15:11

Three thousand men from Judah came to the cave at Etam’s rock. 📖

They asked Samson, “Do you not realize the Philistines rule over us?

What have you done to us?”

Samson replied, “I did to them what they did to me.”

## Judges 15:12

They said, “We came to arrest you and hand you to the Philistines.” 📖

Samson replied, “Swear that you yourselves will not kill me.”

## Judges 15:13

They replied, “No, we will not kill you. 📖

We will only bind you securely and hand you over.”

They bound him with two new ropes and led him up from the rock.

## Judges 15:14

When Samson reached Lehi, the Philistines came shouting against him. 📖

The LORD’s Spirit came mightily on him.

His arm ropes became like burnt flax, and his hand bonds broke loose.

**Help with “flax”:** Fibers from this plant can be used to make thread.

## Judges 15:15

Samson found a fresh donkey jawbone. 📖

He reached out, took it, and struck down a thousand men.

## Judges 15:16

Samson said, “With a donkey’s jawbone I piled them in heaps. 📖

With a donkey’s jawbone I killed a thousand men.”

## Judges 15:17

He threw the jawbone away after speaking, naming the place Ramath-lehi. 📖

## Judges 15:18

Very thirsty, Samson cried to the LORD: “You gave this great deliverance through Your servant. 📖

Must I now die of thirst and fall into the hands of the uncircumcised?”

**Help with these words:** Deliverance means rescue.

The uncircumcised here refers to the Philistines.

Falling into their hands describes coming under their power.

## Judges 15:19

God opened Lehi’s hollow place, releasing water. 📖

Samson drank, regained strength, and revived.

He named it En-hakkore, still in Lehi today.

## Judges 15:20

He judged Israel twenty years in Philistine times. 📖
