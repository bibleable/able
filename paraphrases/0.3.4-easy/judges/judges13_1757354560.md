# Judges 13 — Samson’s birth announced

**About this chapter**

The LORD’s angel announces a son to Manoah’s childless wife.

She reports the instructions, and Manoah asks to hear them again.

The chapter records their offering, the angel’s departure, and Samson’s birth.

## Judges 13:1

The Israelites again did evil in the LORD’s sight. 📖

He handed them over to the Philistines for forty years.

## Judges 13:2

Manoah of Zorah, from Dan’s clan, had a barren wife with no children. 📖

**Help with “barren”:** This describes her being unable to have children.

## Judges 13:3

The LORD’s angel said, “You have no children and cannot conceive, but you will conceive and bear a son. 📖

## Judges 13:4

Drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean. 📖

## Judges 13:5

You will conceive and bear a son. 📖

No razor must touch his head because he will be God’s Nazirite from the womb.

He will begin rescuing Israel from the Philistines.”

**Help with “Nazirite”:** This means someone set apart for God.

## Judges 13:6

She told her husband, “A man of God came to me. 📖

He looked extremely awesome, like God’s angel.

I did not ask where he came from, and he gave no name.

**Help with “awesome”:** Here this describes someone inspiring great wonder or awe.

## Judges 13:7

He said, ‘You will conceive and bear a son. 📖

Drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean.

The boy will be God’s Nazirite from the womb until death.’”

## Judges 13:8

Manoah prayed to the LORD, “Please, O Lord, send Your man of God to us again. 📖

Let him teach us how to raise the boy who will be born.”

## Judges 13:9

God heard Manoah’s prayer. 📖

God’s angel returned to the woman as she sat in the field, without her husband Manoah.

## Judges 13:10

She ran to tell him, “The man from the other day returned!” 📖

## Judges 13:11

Manoah got up and followed his wife. 📖

When he reached the man, he asked, “Are you the man who spoke to my wife?”

“I am,” he replied.

## Judges 13:12

Manoah asked, “When your words come true, how must the boy live, and what is his mission?” 📖

## Judges 13:13

The LORD’s angel replied, “Your wife must do everything I told her. 📖

## Judges 13:14

She must eat nothing from the grapevine. 📖

She must drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean.

She must obey everything I commanded her.”

**Help with “unclean”:** This refers to the worship rules given here.

## Judges 13:15

Manoah asked, “Please stay while we prepare a young goat for you.” 📖

## Judges 13:16

The LORD’s angel replied, “Even if you keep Me here, I will not eat your food. 📖

If you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to the LORD.”

Manoah did not know he was the LORD’s angel.

**Help with “burnt offering”:** This is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

## Judges 13:17

Manoah asked, “What is your name, that we may honor you when your word comes true?” 📖

## Judges 13:18

The angel replied, “Why ask My name? 📖

It is beyond understanding.”

## Judges 13:19

Manoah offered a young goat and grain offering on a rock to the LORD. 📖

Manoah and his wife watched the LORD do a marvelous thing.

## Judges 13:20

The flame rose from the altar to the sky, and the LORD’s angel went up in the flame. 📖

Manoah and his wife saw this and fell facedown on the ground.

## Judges 13:21

The LORD’s angel appeared no more to Manoah and his wife. 📖

Then Manoah realized it was the LORD’s angel.

## Judges 13:22

Manoah told his wife, “We will die, for we have seen God!” 📖

## Judges 13:23

She replied, “If the LORD meant to kill us, He would not have accepted our burnt and grain offerings. 📖

Nor would He have shown us these things or spoken to us this way.”

## Judges 13:24

She bore a son and named him Samson. 📖

He grew, blessed by the LORD.

## Judges 13:25

The LORD’s Spirit began to stir Samson at Mahaneh-dan between Zorah and Eshtaol. 📖

**Help with “stir”:** Here this describes moving Samson to act.
