# Judges 9 — Abimelech, Jotham’s fable, and Shechem

**About this chapter**

Abimelech becomes king after killing his brothers.

Jotham tells a fable and pronounces a conditional curse.

The account records betrayal, destruction, and the deaths of Abimelech and Shechem’s people.

## Judges 9:1

Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to his mother’s brothers and whole clan at Shechem, saying, 📖

## Judges 9:2

“Ask all Shechem’s leaders: Is it better for seventy men, all Jerubbaal’s sons, to rule you, or just one? 📖

Remember, I am your flesh and blood.”

**Help with “flesh and blood”:** Abimelech means he is their own relative.

## Judges 9:3

His uncles told all Shechem’s leaders. 📖

Their hearts turned toward him: “He is our brother.”

## Judges 9:4

They gave seventy silver shekels from Baal-berith’s temple. 📖

He hired worthless, reckless followers.

**Help with “shekel”:** This measures weight.

## Judges 9:5

At his father’s house in Ophrah, he murdered his seventy brothers, Jerubbaal’s sons, on one stone. 📖

Jotham, Jerubbaal’s youngest, survived by hiding.

**About the number:** BSB gives seventy and also records Jotham’s survival.

Both statements are retained.

## Judges 9:6

Shechem’s and Beth-millo’s leaders gathered by the oak at Shechem’s pillar and made Abimelech king. 📖

## Judges 9:7

Jotham heard, climbed Mount Gerizim, and shouted, “Listen, Shechem’s leaders, and may God listen to you. 📖

## Judges 9:8

The trees once sought to anoint a king. 📖

They asked the olive, ‘Rule us.’

**About this story:** Jotham tells a fable with speaking trees.

## Judges 9:9

The olive replied, ‘Should I stop giving my oil, which honors God and people, to rule over the trees?’ 📖

## Judges 9:10

They asked the fig, ‘Come rule us.’ 📖

## Judges 9:11

The fig replied, ‘Should I stop giving my sweetness and good fruit to rule over the trees?’ 📖

## Judges 9:12

They asked the grapevine, ‘Come rule us.’ 📖

## Judges 9:13

The grapevine replied, ‘Should I stop giving my wine, which cheers God and people, to rule over the trees?’ 📖

## Judges 9:14

All trees asked the thornbush, ‘Come rule us.’ 📖

## Judges 9:15

It replied, ‘If you truly anoint me king, shelter in my shade. 📖

Otherwise, may fire from me consume Lebanon’s cedars.’

## Judges 9:16

If you faithfully and honestly made Abimelech king, treated Jerubbaal’s family well, and gave him his due, 📖

## Judges 9:17

for my father fought for you, risking his life to rescue you from Midian, 📖

## Judges 9:18

but today you rose against my father’s house and killed his seventy sons on one stone. 📖

You made Abimelech, his maidservant’s son, king over Shechem’s leaders because he is your brother.

**Help with “maidservant”:** This means a woman who serves in a household.

## Judges 9:19

if you acted faithfully and honestly to Jerubbaal’s house today, may you rejoice in Abimelech, and he in you. 📖

## Judges 9:20

Otherwise, may Abimelech’s fire consume Shechem’s and Beth-millo’s leaders, and their fire consume him.” 📖

## Judges 9:21

Jotham escaped to Beer and lived there, fearing his brother Abimelech. 📖

## Judges 9:22

Abimelech ruled Israel three years, 📖

## Judges 9:23

and God sent a spirit of hostility between him and Shechem’s leaders, causing their deceit against him, 📖

**Help with “hostility”:** This means strong opposition or hatred.

## Judges 9:24

so the crime against Jerubbaal’s seventy sons would meet justice. 📖

Their blood would be avenged on their brother Abimelech and Shechem’s leaders, who helped him murder them.

**Help with this picture:** Avenging their blood means punishing those responsible for their deaths.

## Judges 9:25

Shechem’s leaders set hilltop ambushes against Abimelech, robbing all road travelers. 📖

Abimelech heard.

## Judges 9:26

Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers entered Shechem, and its leaders trusted him. 📖

## Judges 9:27

They went into the fields, gathered grapes from their vineyards, and crushed them underfoot. 📖

They held a festival and entered their god’s house.

As they ate and drank, they cursed Abimelech.

## Judges 9:28

Gaal son of Ebed said, “Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? 📖

Is he not Jerubbaal’s son, and is Zebul not his officer?

Serve the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem.

Why should we serve Abimelech?

## Judges 9:29

If I ruled these people, I would remove him, saying, ‘Gather your army and come out!’” 📖

## Judges 9:30

Zebul, city governor, heard Gaal son of Ebed and grew furious. 📖

## Judges 9:31

He secretly messaged Abimelech: “Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers stir Shechem against you. 📖

## Judges 9:32

Tonight hide with your men in the fields. 📖

## Judges 9:33

At sunrise advance on the city. 📖

When Gaal and his men come, do whatever you can.”

## Judges 9:34

Abimelech’s whole force hid against Shechem by night in four groups. 📖

## Judges 9:35

Gaal son of Ebed stood at the city gate’s entrance. 📖

Just then Abimelech and his men came out from hiding.

## Judges 9:36

Gaal told Zebul, “People descend the mountains!” 📖

Zebul replied, “You see mountain shadows as men.”

## Judges 9:37

Gaal said, “People descend from the land’s center, and one group comes by the Diviners’ Oak!” 📖

## Judges 9:38

Zebul replied, “Where is your boasting now? 📖

You said, ‘Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?’

Are these not the people you mocked?

Go out now and fight them!”

## Judges 9:39

Gaal led Shechem’s leaders out to fight Abimelech, 📖

## Judges 9:40

but fled as Abimelech pursued. 📖

Many Shechemites fell wounded to the gate’s entrance.

## Judges 9:41

Abimelech stayed in Arumah. 📖

Zebul expelled Gaal and his brothers from Shechem.

## Judges 9:42

Next day Shechem’s people entered the fields, and Abimelech heard. 📖

## Judges 9:43

He divided his men into three groups, hiding in the fields. 📖

When people left the city, he attacked.

## Judges 9:44

Abimelech and the groups with him rushed forward and took their stand at the city gate’s entrance. 📖

The other two groups rushed against everyone in the fields and struck them down.

## Judges 9:45

Abimelech fought against the city all day until he captured it and killed its people. 📖

Then he demolished the city and scattered salt over it.

## Judges 9:46

All the leaders in Shechem’s tower heard this. 📖

They entered the inner room of El-berith’s temple.

## Judges 9:47

Abimelech heard they had gathered there, 📖

## Judges 9:48

and went with all his men to Mount Zalmon. 📖

He axed a branch, put it on his shoulder, and said, “Quick, copy me!”

## Judges 9:49

Each man cut a branch and followed Abimelech. 📖

They piled branches against the inner room and set it on fire above those inside.

This killed everyone in Shechem’s tower, about a thousand men and women.

## Judges 9:50

He besieged and captured Thebez. 📖

## Judges 9:51

The city had a strong tower, and all its men, women, and leaders fled there. 📖

They locked themselves in and climbed to the roof.

## Judges 9:52

Abimelech approached the tower entrance to burn it, 📖

## Judges 9:53

but a woman dropped an upper millstone on his head, crushing his skull. 📖

**Help with “upper millstone”:** This is the top stone used to grind grain.

## Judges 9:54

He quickly called his armor-bearer: “Draw your sword and kill me. 📖

Otherwise people will say, ‘A woman killed him.’”

His armor-bearer thrust his sword through him, and he died.

**Help with “armor-bearer”:** This is an attendant who carries a warrior’s armor and weapons.

## Judges 9:55

Israel saw he was dead, and everyone went home. 📖

## Judges 9:56

God repaid Abimelech’s evil to his father in murdering his seventy brothers. 📖

## Judges 9:57

God also brought all the wickedness of Shechem’s men back on their own heads. 📖

Jotham son of Jerubbaal’s curse came upon them.

**Help with this picture:** Their wickedness returning on their heads describes their punishment for it.
