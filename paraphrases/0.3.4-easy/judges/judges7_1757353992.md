# Judges 7 — The three hundred and Midian’s flight

**About this chapter**

The LORD reduces Gideon’s army to three hundred.

Gideon hears a dream and its interpretation, then attacks with horns, jars, and torches.

The chapter records Midian’s flight and the deaths of Oreb and Zeeb.

## Judges 7:1

Early, Jerubbaal, Gideon, and all his men camped by Harod’s spring. 📖

Midian camped north in the valley near Moreh’s hill.

## Judges 7:2

The LORD told Gideon, “You have too many men for Me to hand Midian over to them. 📖

Israel might claim glory over Me, saying, ‘My own hand saved me.’

## Judges 7:3

Tell the men, ‘Anyone fearful and trembling may leave Mount Gilead.’” 📖

Twenty-two thousand left; ten thousand stayed.

## Judges 7:4

He said, “Still too many. 📖

Bring them to water, and I will sift them.

If I say, ‘This one goes,’ he goes.

If I say, ‘This one does not,’ he does not.”

**Help with “sift”:** Here this means separate and choose the men.

## Judges 7:5

Gideon brought them to water. 📖

The LORD said, “Separate men lapping water with their tongues like dogs from men kneeling to drink.”

**Help with “lapping”:** This means drinking using the tongue.

## Judges 7:6

Three hundred lapped with hands to mouths. 📖

Everyone else knelt.

## Judges 7:7

The LORD said, “I will save you through the three hundred who lapped and hand Midian over. 📖

Everyone else must go home.”

## Judges 7:8

Gideon sent the others to their tents, keeping three hundred with the others’ supplies and rams’ horns. 📖

Midian camped below in the valley.

## Judges 7:9

That night the LORD said, “Rise and attack the camp; I handed it over. 📖

## Judges 7:10

If afraid, go with your servant Purah 📖

## Judges 7:11

and listen. 📖

Your hands will be strengthened to attack.”

He and Purah went to the outposts where armed men guarded the camp.

**Help with this picture:** Strengthened hands picture renewed courage and readiness.

## Judges 7:12

Midianites, Amalekites, and all eastern peoples filled the valley like locust swarms. 📖

Their camels were countless as seashore sand.

## Judges 7:13

Gideon arrived as a man told his friend a dream. 📖

“A barley loaf tumbled into Midian’s camp and hit the tent so hard it overturned and collapsed.”

## Judges 7:14

His friend said, “This is Gideon son of Joash the Israelite’s sword! 📖

God handed him Midian and the whole camp.”

## Judges 7:15

Hearing the dream and meaning, Gideon worshiped. 📖

He returned to Israel’s camp: “Rise!

The LORD handed Midian’s camp over.”

## Judges 7:16

He divided three hundred into three groups. 📖

Each man had a ram’s horn in one hand and a large jar holding a torch in the other.

## Judges 7:17

He said, “Watch and copy me. 📖

At the camp’s edge, do exactly what I do.

## Judges 7:18

When my men and I blow horns, blow yours all around camp and shout, ‘For the LORD and Gideon!’” 📖

## Judges 7:19

Gideon’s hundred reached the camp’s edge at the middle watch’s beginning, just after the guard changed. 📖

They blew horns and broke their jars.

## Judges 7:20

Three groups blew horns and shattered jars. 📖

Torches in left hands, horns in right hands, they shouted, “A sword for the LORD and Gideon!”

## Judges 7:21

Each stood around camp, and the whole Midianite army fled, crying as they ran. 📖

## Judges 7:22

Three hundred horns sounded, and the LORD turned the camp’s men against each other by sword. 📖

They fled to Beth-shittah toward Zererah, to Abel-meholah’s border near Tabbath.

## Judges 7:23

Israel called Naphtali, Asher, and all Manasseh to pursue Midian. 📖

## Judges 7:24

Gideon sent throughout Ephraim’s hills: “Come against Midian. 📖

Seize the Jordan’s waters ahead of them to Beth-barah.”

Ephraim’s men were called and seized those waters to Beth-barah.

## Judges 7:25

They captured Midian’s two princes Oreb and Zeeb. 📖

They killed Oreb at the rock of Oreb, and Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb.

Pursuing Midian, they brought Oreb’s and Zeeb’s heads to Gideon beyond the Jordan.
