# Judges 14 — Samson’s marriage and riddle

**About this chapter**

Samson seeks a Philistine wife in Timnah.

He kills a lion, finds honey, and poses a riddle.

The feast leads to threats, betrayal, killing, and his wife being given to another man.

## Judges 14:1

Samson went to Timnah and saw a young Philistine woman. 📖

## Judges 14:2

Samson returned and told his father and mother, “I saw a Philistine daughter in Timnah. 📖

Get her for me as my wife.”

## Judges 14:3

His father and mother asked, “Is there no woman among your relatives or all our people? 📖

Must you go to the uncircumcised Philistines for a wife?”

Samson told his father, “Get her for me; she pleases my eyes.”

**Help with these words:** Circumcision means removing the foreskin from the penis.

His parents call the Philistines uncircumcised.

Pleasing his eyes describes how Samson finds the woman attractive.

## Judges 14:4

His father and mother did not know this came from the LORD. 📖

He was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines, who then ruled Israel.

## Judges 14:5

With his parents he reached Timnah’s vineyards. 📖

A young lion roared at him,

## Judges 14:6

and the LORD’s Spirit came powerfully on Samson. 📖

He tore the lion apart with his bare hands as one would tear a young goat.

He did not tell his father or mother what he did.

## Judges 14:7

He went and spoke to the woman, who pleased his eyes. 📖

## Judges 14:8

Returning later to take her, he left the road to inspect the lion’s carcass. 📖

Bees swarmed inside, with honey.

**Help with “carcass”:** This is a dead animal’s body.

## Judges 14:9

Samson scooped honey into his hands and ate as he walked. 📖

He gave some to his father and mother, who ate it.

He did not tell them he took it from the lion’s carcass.

## Judges 14:10

His father went to visit the woman. 📖

Samson held a feast, as bridegrooms customarily did.

## Judges 14:11

When the Philistines saw Samson, they chose thirty men to accompany him. 📖

## Judges 14:12

Samson said, “Let me give you a riddle. 📖

If you solve it within the feast’s seven days, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty clothing sets.

## Judges 14:13

If you cannot solve it, you must give me thirty linen garments and thirty clothing sets.” 📖

“Tell us your riddle,” they replied.

“Let us hear it.”

## Judges 14:14

“From the eater came food; from the strong, sweetness.” 📖

They could not explain it three days.

## Judges 14:15

On day four they threatened his wife, “Get the answer, or we burn you and your father’s household to death. 📖

Did you bring us to rob us?”

## Judges 14:16

Samson’s wife wept before him: “You hate me, and do not love me! 📖

You gave my people a riddle but have not explained it to me.”

He replied, “I have not explained it even to my father or mother.

Why should I explain it to you?”

## Judges 14:17

She wept all seven feast days and pressured him until he told her on day seven. 📖

She told her people.

## Judges 14:18

Before sunset on day seven, the city’s men asked, “What is sweeter than honey? 📖

What is stronger than a lion?”

Samson replied, “If you had not plowed with my heifer, you would not have solved my riddle.”

**Help with this picture:** A heifer is a young female cow.

Samson compares getting the answer through his wife to plowing with his heifer.

## Judges 14:19

The LORD’s Spirit came mightily on Samson. 📖

He went to Ashkelon and killed thirty men.

He took their clothes and gave them to those who solved his riddle.

Burning with anger, he went to his father’s house,

## Judges 14:20

and his wife was given to a companion. 📖
