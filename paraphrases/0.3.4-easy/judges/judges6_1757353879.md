# Judges 6 — Gideon’s call and the fleece

**About this chapter**

Midian impoverishes Israel, and the LORD sends a prophet.

Gideon receives a call, a sign, and commands to destroy Baal’s altar.

The chapter records his fear and both fleece tests.

## Judges 6:1

Israel again did evil before the LORD. 📖

He handed them to Midian seven years,

## Judges 6:2

and Midian overpowered them. 📖

Israel made shelters in mountains, caves, and strongholds because of Midian.

## Judges 6:3

Whenever Israel planted, Midianites, Amalekites, and eastern peoples invaded, 📖

## Judges 6:4

camping as far as Gaza and destroying crops. 📖

They left no food, sheep, oxen, or donkeys for Israel.

## Judges 6:5

Midian came with livestock and tents like locust swarms. 📖

They and their camels were countless, ravaging the land.

## Judges 6:6

Midian made Israel very poor, and they cried to the LORD. 📖

## Judges 6:7

When they cried because of Midian, 📖

## Judges 6:8

He sent a prophet: “The LORD, Israel’s God, says: I brought you from Egypt, the house of slavery. 📖

## Judges 6:9

I rescued you from Egypt and all oppressors, drove them out, and gave their land. 📖

## Judges 6:10

I said, ‘I am the LORD your God. 📖

Do not fear the Amorites’ gods in whose land you live.’

You disobeyed Me.”

## Judges 6:11

The LORD’s angel sat under Joash the Abiezrite’s oak in Ophrah. 📖

Joash’s son Gideon threshed wheat in a winepress, hiding it from Midian.

**Help with these words:** Threshing separates grain from stalks.

A winepress is where grapes are squeezed for juice.

## Judges 6:12

He appeared to Gideon: “The LORD is with you, mighty warrior.” 📖

## Judges 6:13

Gideon replied, “Please, my Lord, if the LORD is with us, why has all this happened? 📖

Where are all His wonders our fathers reported, saying, ‘Has the LORD not brought us from Egypt?’

But now the LORD abandoned us and handed us to Midian.”

## Judges 6:14

The LORD turned: “Go with your strength and save Israel from Midian. 📖

Am I not sending you?”

## Judges 6:15

Gideon replied, “Please, my Lord, how can I save Israel? 📖

My clan is Manasseh’s weakest, and I am the youngest in my father’s house.”

## Judges 6:16

The LORD replied, “I will surely be with you. 📖

You will strike all Midian as one man.”

## Judges 6:17

Gideon said, “If I found Your favor, give a sign that You speak with me. 📖

## Judges 6:18

Wait until I return with an offering to set before You.” 📖

The LORD replied, “I will stay until you return.”

## Judges 6:19

Gideon prepared a young goat, unleavened bread, and an ephah of flour. 📖

Meat went in a basket, broth in a pot.

He presented them under the oak.

**Help with these words:** Unleavened bread is made without yeast.

An ephah measures dry goods.

## Judges 6:20

God’s angel said, “Put meat and unleavened bread on this rock and pour out the broth.” 📖

Gideon obeyed.

## Judges 6:21

The LORD’s angel touched meat and unleavened bread with his staff’s tip. 📖

Fire flared from the rock and consumed both.

The angel vanished.

## Judges 6:22

Gideon realized who it was. 📖

“Oh no, Lord GOD!

I saw the LORD’s angel face to face!”

## Judges 6:23

The LORD replied, “Peace. 📖

Do not fear; you will not die.”

## Judges 6:24

Gideon built an altar to the LORD and named it The LORD Is Peace. 📖

It still stands in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.

## Judges 6:25

That very night the LORD said, “Take your father’s young bull and a second bull, seven years old. 📖

Destroy your father’s Baal altar and cut the Asherah pole beside it.

## Judges 6:26

Build a proper altar to the LORD your God atop this stronghold. 📖

Offer the second bull as a burnt offering, using the cut Asherah wood.”

**Help with “Asherah pole”:** This is an object used in worship of Asherah.

## Judges 6:27

Gideon took ten servants and obeyed. 📖

He feared his father’s household and city’s men, so acted at night, not daytime.

## Judges 6:28

In the morning they saw Baal’s ruined altar, the cut Asherah, and the second bull offered on the new altar. 📖

## Judges 6:29

They asked, “Who did this?” 📖

After thorough investigation, they heard, “Gideon son of Joash.”

## Judges 6:30

The men told Joash, “Bring your son out to die for destroying Baal’s altar and cutting the Asherah beside it.” 📖

## Judges 6:31

Joash answered all those men, “Will you contend for Baal? 📖

Will you save him?

Whoever pleads his case dies by morning!

If Baal is a god, let him contend with his altar’s destroyer.”

**Help with “contend”:** This means argue or fight for a cause.

## Judges 6:32

They named Gideon Jerubbaal that day, meaning “Let Baal contend with him,” because he destroyed Baal’s altar. 📖

## Judges 6:33

All Midianites, Amalekites, and eastern peoples gathered, crossed the Jordan, and camped in the Valley of Jezreel. 📖

## Judges 6:34

The LORD’s Spirit came on Gideon. 📖

He blew the ram’s horn and rallied the Abiezrites behind him.

## Judges 6:35

He sent messengers calling to arms throughout Manasseh, Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali. 📖

They came to meet him.

## Judges 6:36

Gideon told God, “If You will save Israel by my hand as You said, 📖

## Judges 6:37

I will lay wool fleece on the threshing floor. 📖

Dew only on it, with all ground dry, will show You will save Israel through me as said.”

**Help with “fleece”:** This is wool from a sheep.

## Judges 6:38

It happened. 📖

Next morning he squeezed the fleece, wringing out a bowlful of water.

## Judges 6:39

Gideon said to God, “Do not be angry; let me speak once more. 📖

Allow one more fleece test: dry fleece, dew covering the ground.”

## Judges 6:40

God did so that night. 📖

Only the fleece was dry; dew covered the ground.
