# Judges 1 — Conquests and peoples who remain

**About this chapter**

After Joshua dies, Israel asks who should fight first.

The chapter reports victories, mutilation, destruction, and peoples Israel could not or did not drive out.

Every place name and forced-labor report is retained.

## Judges 1:1

After Joshua died, Israel asked the LORD, “Who will first fight the Canaanites for us?” 📖

## Judges 1:2

He answered, “Judah must go. 📖

I handed the land over to them.”

## Judges 1:3

Judah’s men told their brothers, the Simeonites, “Help us fight Canaanites in our allotted land. 📖

We will help in yours too.”

Simeon’s men went.

## Judges 1:4

Judah attacked, and the LORD handed over Canaanites and Perizzites. 📖

They struck ten thousand men at Bezek.

## Judges 1:5

There they found and fought Adoni-bezek, striking Canaanites and Perizzites. 📖

## Judges 1:6

He fled. 📖

They pursued, caught him, and cut off his thumbs and big toes.

## Judges 1:7

Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings with thumbs and big toes cut off gathered scraps under my table. 📖

God repaid what I did.”

They brought him to Jerusalem, where he died.

## Judges 1:8

Judah fought and captured Jerusalem, put it to the sword, and burned it. 📖

**Help with “put it to the sword”:** This describes killing with swords.

## Judges 1:9

Then Judah fought Canaanites in the hills, Negev, and foothills. 📖

## Judges 1:10

Judah attacked Hebron’s Canaanites. 📖

Hebron was formerly Kiriath-arba.

They struck Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

## Judges 1:11

They attacked Debir’s people. 📖

Debir was formerly Kiriath-sepher.

## Judges 1:12

Caleb said, “Whoever defeats and captures Kiriath-sepher will receive my daughter Acsah in marriage.” 📖

## Judges 1:13

Othniel, son of Caleb’s younger brother Kenaz, captured it. 📖

Caleb gave him Acsah in marriage.

## Judges 1:14

One day Acsah urged Othniel to ask her father for a field. 📖

She dismounted her donkey, and Caleb asked, “What do you desire?”

## Judges 1:15

She replied, “Give a blessing. 📖

You gave me Negev land; give springs too.”

Caleb gave both upper and lower springs.

## Judges 1:16

Moses’ father-in-law’s descendants, the Kenite’s family, went with Judah from the City of Palms. 📖

They went to Judah’s wilderness in the Negev near Arad, settling among the people.

## Judges 1:17

Judah and their brothers Simeon attacked Canaanites in Zephath, devoting the city to destruction. 📖

It was called Hormah.

**Help with “devoting to destruction”:** This means setting apart for complete destruction.

## Judges 1:18

Judah captured Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron, each with its territory. 📖

## Judges 1:19

The LORD was with Judah, who took the hills. 📖

But they could not drive out the plains’ inhabitants because of their iron chariots.

## Judges 1:20

As Moses promised, Judah gave Hebron to Caleb. 📖

He drove out the descendants of Anak’s three sons.

## Judges 1:21

Benjamin failed to drive out Jerusalem’s Jebusites, who still live there among Benjamin. 📖

## Judges 1:22

Joseph’s house attacked Bethel too, with the LORD with them. 📖

## Judges 1:23

They sent spies to Bethel, formerly Luz, 📖

## Judges 1:24

who saw a man leaving. 📖

“Show us the entrance, and we will treat you kindly.”

## Judges 1:25

He showed them the entrance. 📖

They put the city to the sword but released him and all his family.

## Judges 1:26

He went to Hittite land, built a city, and named it Luz, still its name today. 📖

## Judges 1:27

Manasseh failed to drive out the people of Beth-shean, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, Megiddo, and their villages. 📖

The Canaanites insisted on staying in that land.

## Judges 1:28

When Israel grew stronger, they made Canaanites forced laborers but never drove them out completely. 📖

## Judges 1:29

Ephraim failed to drive out Gezer’s Canaanites, who stayed among them in Gezer. 📖

## Judges 1:30

Zebulun failed to drive out Kitron and Nahalol’s people. 📖

Canaanites lived among them as forced laborers.

## Judges 1:31

Asher failed to drive out the people of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob. 📖

## Judges 1:32

Asher lived among the land’s Canaanites because they did not drive them out. 📖

## Judges 1:33

Naphtali failed to drive out Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath’s people. 📖

They lived among Canaanites, but Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath’s inhabitants served as forced laborers.

## Judges 1:34

Amorites forced Dan into the hills and would not allow them into the plain. 📖

## Judges 1:35

Amorites insisted on staying at Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. 📖

When Joseph’s house grew stronger, they made Amorites forced laborers.

## Judges 1:36

The Amorite border ran from the Ascent of Akrabbim to Sela and beyond. 📖
