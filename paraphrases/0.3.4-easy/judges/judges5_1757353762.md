# Judges 5 — Deborah and Barak’s song

**About this chapter**

Deborah and Barak sing about the LORD’s victory.

The song praises some, questions others, and blesses Jael.

It also quotes Sisera’s mother and her ladies imagining the spoils of war.

## Judges 5:1

That day Deborah and Barak son of Abinoam sang: 📖

## Judges 5:2

“When Israel’s princes lead and people volunteer, bless the LORD. 📖

## Judges 5:3

Listen, kings and princes! 📖

I will sing to the LORD.

I will sing praise to the LORD, Israel’s God.

## Judges 5:4

O LORD, when You left Seir and marched from Edom, earth trembled. 📖

Heaven poured rain, and clouds poured water.

## Judges 5:5

Mountains shook before the LORD, the One of Sinai, the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## Judges 5:6

In Shamgar son of Anath’s days, in Jael’s days, highways were deserted. 📖

Travelers used byways.

**Help with “byways”:** These are smaller routes away from the main roads.

## Judges 5:7

Village life ceased in Israel until I, Deborah, arose, a mother in Israel. 📖

## Judges 5:8

They chose new gods, and war reached their gates. 📖

No shield or spear was found among forty thousand in Israel.

## Judges 5:9

My heart is with Israel’s princes and the people’s volunteers. 📖

Bless the LORD!

## Judges 5:10

White-donkey riders, those sitting on saddle blankets, and road travelers, consider 📖

## Judges 5:11

singers’ voices at watering places. 📖

They recount the LORD’s righteous acts and His villagers’ righteous deeds in Israel.

Then His people went down to the gates:

## Judges 5:12

‘Awake, awake, Deborah! 📖

Awake, awake, sing!

Rise, Barak son of Abinoam, and take your captives!’

## Judges 5:13

Survivors came down to the nobles. 📖

The LORD’s people came down to me against the mighty.

## Judges 5:14

Some came from Ephraim, rooted in Amalek; Benjamin followed you with your people. 📖

Commanders came from Machir, bearers of the marshal’s staff from Zebulun.

**Help with “marshal”:** This is a military officer.

## Judges 5:15

Issachar’s princes were with Deborah. 📖

Issachar was with Barak, rushing into the valley at his heels.

Reuben’s clans were greatly undecided.

## Judges 5:16

Why sit among sheepfolds, hearing the whistling for the flocks? 📖

Reuben’s clans were greatly undecided.

## Judges 5:17

Gilead stayed beyond the Jordan. 📖

Dan, why stay by ships?

Asher stayed on the coast, in his harbors.

## Judges 5:18

Zebulun risked their lives, and Naphtali too, on battlefield heights. 📖

## Judges 5:19

Kings came and fought. 📖

Canaan’s kings fought at Taanach by Megiddo’s waters but took no silver plunder.

## Judges 5:20

Stars fought from heaven, from their courses against Sisera. 📖

## Judges 5:21

The River Kishon swept them away, the ancient river, the River Kishon. 📖

March strongly, my soul!

## Judges 5:22

Horses’ hooves thundered, his stallions galloping madly. 📖

## Judges 5:23

‘Curse Meroz,’ says the LORD’s angel. 📖

‘Bitterly curse her people because they did not come to help the LORD, to help the LORD against the mighty.’

## Judges 5:24

Most blessed of women is Jael, wife of Heber the Kenite, most blessed of women living in tents. 📖

## Judges 5:25

He asked water; she gave milk. 📖

She brought curds in a magnificent bowl.

**Help with “curds”:** These are thickened parts of milk.

## Judges 5:26

She reached for a tent peg, her right hand for the workman’s hammer. 📖

She struck Sisera, crushed his skull, shattered and pierced his temple.

## Judges 5:27

At her feet he collapsed, fell, and lay still. 📖

At her feet he collapsed and fell.

Where he collapsed, he fell dead.

## Judges 5:28

Sisera’s mother peered through the window’s lattice, lamenting, ‘Why is his chariot so long coming? 📖

What delays the clatter of his chariots?’

**Help with “lattice”:** This is a framework across an opening.

## Judges 5:29

Her wisest ladies answer, and she keeps telling herself, 📖

## Judges 5:30

‘Are they not finding and sharing spoil: a girl or two for each warrior? 📖

Dyed garments for Sisera, embroidered garments for the looter’s neck?’

**About these words:** The mother and her ladies imagine captives and clothing as spoils.

The narrator does not report that these imagined events happened.

## Judges 5:31

So may all Your enemies perish, O LORD! 📖

But may those who love You shine like the sun at its brightest.”

The land rested forty years.
