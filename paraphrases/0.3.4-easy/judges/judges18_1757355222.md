# Judges 18 — Dan takes Micah’s idols and captures Laish

**About this chapter**

Dan’s scouts find land at Laish.

Dan’s armed men take Micah’s priest and idols.

They attack the quiet, unsuspecting people of Laish and settle there.

## Judges 18:1

In those days Israel had no king. 📖

Dan’s tribe sought land to occupy, having not yet received an inheritance among Israel’s tribes.

**Help with “inheritance”:** Here this means land for the tribe.

## Judges 18:2

Dan sent five brave men from their clans in Zorah and Eshtaol to spy out and explore the land. 📖

“Go and explore the land,” they said.

The men reached Micah’s house in Ephraim’s hills and spent the night.

## Judges 18:3

Near Micah’s house, they recognized the young Levite’s voice. 📖

They went over and asked, “Who brought you here?

What are you doing here?

Why are you here?”

**Help with “Levite”:** This means a member of Levi’s tribe.

## Judges 18:4

He replied, “Micah has done this and that for me. 📖

He hired me as his priest.”

## Judges 18:5

They said, “Please ask God whether our journey will succeed.” 📖

## Judges 18:6

The priest told them, “Go in peace. 📖

The LORD is watching over your journey.”

**About the speaker:** This is the priest’s reply to the men.

## Judges 18:7

The five men left and reached Laish. 📖

Its people lived securely like the Sidonians, quiet and unsuspecting.

The land lacked nothing and had no cruel ruler.

They were far from the Sidonians and had no alliance with anyone.

**Help with “alliance”:** This means an agreement to support one another.

## Judges 18:8

They returned to Zorah and Eshtaol. 📖

Their brothers asked, “What did you find?”

## Judges 18:9

They answered, “Come, let us attack them! 📖

We saw the land, and it is very good.

Why fail to act?

Do not hesitate to go and take the land!

## Judges 18:10

You will find an unsuspecting people and a spacious land. 📖

God has handed it over to you.

Nothing on earth is lacking there.”

**About the speaker:** The scouts make this claim about God and the land.

## Judges 18:11

Six hundred Danites left Zorah and Eshtaol, armed with weapons of war. 📖

## Judges 18:12

They went up and camped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. 📖

That is why the place west of Kiriath-jearim is still called Mahaneh-dan today.

## Judges 18:13

From there they traveled to Ephraim’s hills and reached Micah’s house. 📖

## Judges 18:14

The five scouts of Laish’s land told their brothers, “One of these houses has an ephod and household gods. 📖

It also has a carved image and a molten idol.

Did you know that?

Now think about what you should do.”

**Help with these words:** An ephod is connected with priestly worship.

A carved image is shaped by cutting material.

A molten idol is made from melted metal.

These objects were used in worship.

## Judges 18:15

They turned aside and went to the young Levite’s home, Micah’s house, and greeted him. 📖

## Judges 18:16

The six hundred Danites stood at the gate’s entrance with their weapons of war. 📖

## Judges 18:17

The five scouts went inside and took the carved image, ephod, household idols, and molten idol. 📖

The priest stood at the gate’s entrance with the six hundred armed men.

## Judges 18:18

As they entered Micah’s house and took the carved image, ephod, household idols, and molten idol, the priest spoke. 📖

“What are you doing?” he asked.

## Judges 18:19

They replied, “Be quiet! 📖

Put your hand over your mouth and come with us as our father and priest.

Is being priest for one household better than being priest for a tribe and family in Israel?”

**Help with “father”:** Here they use this title for a religious guide.

## Judges 18:20

The priest was glad. 📖

He took the ephod, household idols, and carved image, and went with the people.

## Judges 18:21

They put their small children, livestock, and possessions in front of them, then turned and left. 📖

## Judges 18:22

When they were some distance from Micah’s house, the men in neighboring houses gathered and caught up with the Danites. 📖

## Judges 18:23

They called after the Danites, who turned to face them. 📖

The Danites asked Micah, “What is wrong, that you have called out such a group?”

## Judges 18:24

Micah replied, “You took the gods I made and my priest, then went away. 📖

What else do I have?

How can you ask, ‘What is wrong?’”

## Judges 18:25

The Danites replied, “Do not raise your voice against us. 📖

Otherwise angry men will attack you, and you and your family will lose your lives.”

## Judges 18:26

The Danites went on. 📖

Micah saw they were too strong for him, so he turned and went home.

## Judges 18:27

After taking Micah’s idols and priest, they reached Laish, whose people were quiet and unsuspecting. 📖

They struck them with swords and burned the city.

## Judges 18:28

No one rescued them, because Laish was far from Sidon and had no alliance with anyone. 📖

It was in a valley near Beth-rehob.

The Danites rebuilt the city and lived there.

## Judges 18:29

They named it Dan after their forefather Dan, who was born to Israel. 📖

The city had previously been called Laish.

## Judges 18:30

The Danites set up idols for themselves. 📖

Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Moses, and his sons were Dan’s priests until the land’s captivity.

**Help with “captivity”:** This means people being taken away and held by others.

## Judges 18:31

They set up Micah’s carved image for themselves. 📖

It remained the whole time God’s house was in Shiloh.
