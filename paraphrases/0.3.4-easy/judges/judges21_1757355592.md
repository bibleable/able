# Judges 21 — Wives for Benjamin’s survivors

**About this chapter**

Israel grieves for Benjamin but has sworn not to give daughters as wives to Benjamites.

The chapter records killing at Jabesh-gilead and the taking of women from Shiloh.

The elders’ advice and claim about guilt remain their own words.

## Judges 21:1

Israel’s men had sworn at Mizpah, “None of us will give his daughter in marriage to a Benjamite.” 📖

## Judges 21:2

The people came to Bethel and sat before God until evening. 📖

They raised their voices and wept bitterly.

## Judges 21:3

They cried, “Why, O LORD God of Israel, has this happened in Israel? 📖

Today one tribe is missing from Israel!”

## Judges 21:4

The next day they rose early, built an altar there, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. 📖

**Help with these words:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

A peace offering is another kind of sacrifice to God.

## Judges 21:5

The Israelites asked, “Who from Israel’s tribes did not attend the assembly before the LORD?” 📖

They had sworn a solemn oath.

Anyone who failed to come before the LORD at Mizpah would certainly be put to death.

## Judges 21:6

Israel grieved for their brothers, the Benjamites. 📖

They said, “Today a tribe is cut off from Israel.

## Judges 21:7

What can we do about wives for the survivors? 📖

We swore by the LORD not to give them our daughters in marriage.”

## Judges 21:8

They asked, “Which of Israel’s tribes failed to come before the LORD at Mizpah?” 📖

No one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp for the assembly.

## Judges 21:9

When the people were counted, no residents of Jabesh-gilead were there. 📖

## Judges 21:10

The assembly sent 12,000 of their bravest men. 📖

They commanded, “Go and kill Jabesh-gilead’s residents with swords, including women and children.

## Judges 21:11

This is what you must do: devote every male to destruction. 📖

Also destroy every female who has had sex with a man.”

**Help with “devote to destruction”:** Here the command is to kill them.

The assembly gives this command in the account.

## Judges 21:12

They found four hundred young women in Jabesh-gilead who had never had sex with a man. 📖

They brought them to the camp at Shiloh in Canaan.

## Judges 21:13

The whole assembly sent a message of peace to the Benjamites at Rimmon’s rock. 📖

## Judges 21:14

The Benjamites returned then and were given the women spared from Jabesh-gilead. 📖

But there were not enough for them all.

## Judges 21:15

The people grieved for Benjamin, because the LORD had made a gap among Israel’s tribes. 📖

## Judges 21:16

The assembly’s elders asked, “What can we do about wives for those remaining? 📖

Benjamin’s women have been destroyed.”

## Judges 21:17

They added, “Benjamin’s survivors must have heirs so that a tribe of Israel is not wiped out. 📖

**Help with “heirs”:** This means people who receive family property after others die.

## Judges 21:18

But we cannot give them our daughters as wives.” 📖

The Israelites had sworn, “Cursed is anyone who gives a wife to a Benjamite.”

## Judges 21:19

They said, “There is a yearly feast to the LORD in Shiloh, north of Bethel. 📖

It is east of the road from Bethel to Shechem and south of Lebonah.”

## Judges 21:20

They commanded the Benjamites, “Go and hide in the vineyards 📖

## Judges 21:21

and watch. 📖

When Shiloh’s daughters come out to dance, each of you must leave the vineyards and catch a wife among them.

Then go to Benjamin’s land.

**About this command:** These are the elders’ instructions to the Benjamites to seize the women.

## Judges 21:22

When their fathers or brothers complain to us, we will say, ‘Do us a favor by helping them. 📖

We did not get wives for each man in the war.

Since you did not actually give them your daughters, you have no guilt.’”

**About the claim:** The elders say the families would have no guilt because they had not given their daughters.

This is their proposed answer to the complaints.

## Judges 21:23

The Benjamites followed the instructions, carrying away the number of women they needed from the dancers they caught. 📖

They returned to their own inheritance, rebuilt their cities, and settled there.

**Help with “inheritance”:** Here this means the land belonging to their tribe.

## Judges 21:24

Then each Israelite returned from there to his own tribe and clan, each to his own inheritance. 📖

## Judges 21:25

In those days Israel had no king. 📖

Everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

**Help with “in his own eyes”:** This means according to each person’s own judgment.
