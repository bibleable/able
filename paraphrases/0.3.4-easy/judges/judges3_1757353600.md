# Judges 3 — Othniel, Ehud, and Shamgar

**About this chapter**

The LORD leaves nations to test Israel and teach later generations warfare.

Israel turns to other gods, is oppressed, and cries for help.

The rescue accounts include Ehud killing Eglon and Shamgar using an oxgoad.

## Judges 3:1

The LORD left these nations to test Israel’s people who knew none of Canaan’s wars, 📖

## Judges 3:2

and teach later generations warfare, especially those never knowing it: 📖

## Judges 3:3

five Philistine rulers, all Canaanites, Sidonians, and Hivites in Lebanon’s mountains from Mount Baal-hermon to Lebo-hamath. 📖

## Judges 3:4

They tested whether Israel would keep the LORD’s commands given their fathers through Moses. 📖

## Judges 3:5

Israel lived among Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 📖

## Judges 3:6

They married these peoples’ daughters, gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods. 📖

## Judges 3:7

They did evil before the LORD, forgetting Him and serving the Baals and Asherahs. 📖

## Judges 3:8

His anger burned, and He sold Israel to Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram-naharaim. 📖

They served him eight years.

## Judges 3:9

Israel cried to the LORD. 📖

He raised Othniel, son of Caleb’s younger brother Kenaz, to rescue them.

## Judges 3:10

The LORD’s Spirit came on Othniel, who judged Israel and went to war. 📖

The LORD handed him Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram, and Othniel prevailed.

## Judges 3:11

The land rested forty years, until Othniel son of Kenaz died. 📖

## Judges 3:12

Israel again did evil before the LORD. 📖

Because of this evil, He empowered Eglon king of Moab over Israel.

## Judges 3:13

Eglon joined with Ammonites and Amalekites, attacked and defeated Israel, and seized the City of Palms. 📖

## Judges 3:14

Israel served Eglon king of Moab eighteen years. 📖

## Judges 3:15

They cried to the LORD again. 📖

He raised Ehud son of Gera, a left-handed Benjamite, as deliverer.

They sent him with tribute to Eglon king of Moab.

**Help with “tribute”:** This is a required payment to a ruler.

## Judges 3:16

Ehud made a double-edged sword one cubit long and strapped it under his cloak on his right thigh, 📖

**Help with “cubit”:** This is a length based on elbow to fingertip.

## Judges 3:17

and brought tribute to Eglon king of Moab, a very fat man. 📖

## Judges 3:18

After presenting it, Ehud sent the carriers away. 📖

## Judges 3:19

At the idols near Gilgal, he turned back. 📖

“I have a secret message, O king.”

The king said, “Silence!”

His attendants left.

## Judges 3:20

Ehud approached the king, alone in his cool upper room. 📖

“I have God’s word for you.”

Eglon rose from his seat.

## Judges 3:21

Ehud reached with his left hand, drew the sword from his right thigh, and plunged it into Eglon’s belly. 📖

## Judges 3:22

The handle followed the blade in, and Eglon’s fat closed over it. 📖

Ehud did not withdraw it.

Eglon’s bowels emptied.

**Help with “bowels”:** These are the intestines.

## Judges 3:23

Ehud left through the porch, closing and locking the upper room’s doors. 📖

## Judges 3:24

After Ehud left, Eglon’s servants came in and found the upper room’s doors locked. 📖

“He must be relieving himself in the cool room,” they said.

## Judges 3:25

They waited until worried; the upper room’s doors still had not opened. 📖

They used the key and found their lord dead on the floor.

## Judges 3:26

Ehud escaped while they waited, passing the idols to Seirah. 📖

## Judges 3:27

At Seirah he blew the ram’s horn throughout Ephraim’s hills. 📖

Israel came down with him, and he led them.

## Judges 3:28

“Follow me,” he said. 📖

“The LORD handed your Moabite enemies over.”

They followed, seized the Jordan’s fords toward Moab, and let no one cross.

**Help with “fords”:** These are shallow river crossings.

## Judges 3:29

They killed about ten thousand Moabites, all strong and brave. 📖

Not one escaped.

## Judges 3:30

Moab was subdued under Israel that day. 📖

The land rested eighty years.

**Help with “subdued”:** This means brought under control by force.

## Judges 3:31

After Ehud came Shamgar son of Anath. 📖

He saved Israel too, killing six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad.

**Help with “oxgoad”:** This is a pointed stick used to guide oxen.
