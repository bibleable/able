# Judges 16 — Samson, Delilah, and the temple’s fall

**About this chapter**

Samson’s story moves from Gaza to Delilah in Sorek.

The chapter records deception, capture, blinding, imprisonment, and his final prayer.

The temple collapse and the deaths are retained without adding approval beyond what the account says.

## Judges 16:1

Samson went to Gaza, saw a prostitute, and spent the night with her. 📖

**Help with “prostitute”:** This means someone paid for sex.

## Judges 16:2

Gaza’s people heard Samson was there. 📖

They surrounded the place and waited quietly all night at the city gate.

They said, “We will kill him at dawn.”

## Judges 16:3

Samson lay there only until midnight. 📖

He rose, pulled out the city gate’s doors, both posts, and bar, and put them on his shoulders.

He carried them to the mountain top overlooking Hebron.

## Judges 16:4

Later he loved Delilah in the Valley of Sorek. 📖

## Judges 16:5

The Philistine lords told Delilah, “Entice him to reveal his great strength’s source. 📖

Find how we can overpower him and tie him up to subdue him.

Each of us will give you eleven hundred silver shekels.”

**Help with these words:** Entice means persuade or tempt.

Subdue means bring under control.

A shekel measures weight.

## Judges 16:6

Delilah asked Samson, “Please tell me where your great strength comes from. 📖

How can you be tied up and subdued?”

## Judges 16:7

Samson replied, “If they bind me with seven fresh, undried bowstrings, I will become as weak as any other man.” 📖

**Help with “bowstrings”:** These are strings used on bows.

## Judges 16:8

The lords brought seven fresh, undried bowstrings. 📖

She bound him.

## Judges 16:9

Men were hiding in her room. 📖

Delilah cried, “Samson, the Philistines are here!”

He snapped the bowstrings like yarn burned by flame.

His strength’s source remained unknown.

## Judges 16:10

She said, “You mocked and lied. 📖

Tell how to bind you.”

## Judges 16:11

He replied, “If they bind me with new ropes that have never been used, I will weaken like any man.” 📖

## Judges 16:12

Delilah bound him with new ropes and cried, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” 📖

Men were hiding in her room.

He snapped the ropes off his arms like threads.

## Judges 16:13

Delilah said, “All along you have mocked and lied to me. 📖

Tell me how to bind you.”

He replied, “Weave my seven braids into the loom’s web and pin it tight.

If you do this, I will become as weak as any other man.”

**Help with these words:** Braids are strands of hair woven together.

A loom is equipment for weaving cloth.

The web is the cloth being woven.

## Judges 16:14

While Samson slept, Delilah wove his seven braids into the web and tightened it with the pin. 📖

She cried, “Samson, the Philistines are here!”

He woke and pulled out the pin, with the loom and web.

## Judges 16:15

Delilah asked, “How can you say, ‘I love you,’ when your heart is not with me? 📖

This is the third time you mocked me without revealing your great strength’s source.”

**Help with “heart”:** Here Delilah uses this word to speak about Samson’s feelings and trust.

## Judges 16:16

She pressed him with her words and pleaded daily until he was sick to death, 📖

**Help with “sick to death”:** This describes his extreme distress at her continued pressure.

## Judges 16:17

Samson told her all his heart: “My hair has never been cut. 📖

I have been God’s Nazirite from my mother’s womb.

If I am shaved, my strength will leave, and I will be as weak as any other man.”

**Help with these words:** A Nazirite is someone set apart for God.

Telling all his heart means telling what he had kept within himself.

## Judges 16:18

Delilah realized he had told her all his heart. 📖

She sent word to the Philistine lords: “Come once more, for he has told me all his heart.”

They came with the money in their hands.

## Judges 16:19

Delilah lulled Samson to sleep on her lap. 📖

She called a man to shave off his seven braids.

She began to subdue him, and his strength left.

## Judges 16:20

She cried, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” 📖

He woke and thought, “I will escape as before and shake free.”

He did not know the LORD had left him.

## Judges 16:21

The Philistines seized Samson, gouged out his eyes, and brought him to Gaza. 📖

They bound him in bronze shackles and forced him to grind grain in prison.

**Help with these words:** Gouged out means forcibly removed.

Shackles are restraints used on prisoners.

## Judges 16:22

His shaved hair began growing back. 📖

## Judges 16:23

The lords gathered for a great sacrifice to Dagon their god. 📖

They rejoiced: “Our god handed over Samson our enemy!”

## Judges 16:24

When the people saw Samson, they praised their god. 📖

They said, “Our god handed our enemy over to us!

He destroyed our land and multiplied our dead.”

## Judges 16:25

When their hearts were merry, they said, “Call Samson to entertain us!” 📖

They brought him from prison to entertain them and stood him between pillars.

## Judges 16:26

Samson told the servant holding his hand, “Lead me to feel the pillars supporting the temple. 📖

I want to lean against them.”

## Judges 16:27

The temple was full of men and women, and all the Philistine lords were there. 📖

About three thousand men and women were on the roof, watching Samson entertain them.

## Judges 16:28

Samson called to the LORD: “O Lord GOD, please remember me. 📖

Strengthen me, O God, once more.

Let me repay the Philistines for my two eyes with one vengeful blow.”

## Judges 16:29

He braced against the two central supporting pillars, right hand on one, left on the other, 📖

## Judges 16:30

Samson said, “Let me die with the Philistines.” 📖

He pushed with all his might.

The temple collapsed on the lords and everyone inside.

He killed more people in his death than in his life.

## Judges 16:31

His brothers and father’s family came down, carried him back, and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol. 📖

They buried him in his father Manoah’s tomb.

He had judged Israel twenty years.
