# Judges 12 — Ephraim’s conflict and three later judges

**About this chapter**

Ephraim confronts Jephthah, and fighting follows.

Gilead uses pronunciation at the Jordan crossings to identify and kill fugitives.

The chapter then records Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon.

## Judges 12:1

Ephraim’s men gathered and crossed the Jordan to Zaphon. 📖

They asked Jephthah, “Why did you cross to fight the Ammonites without calling us to go with you?

We will burn your house with you inside!”

## Judges 12:2

Jephthah replied, “My people and I fought Ammon fiercely. 📖

I called, but you did not save me.

## Judges 12:3

When I saw you would not save me, I risked my life and crossed over to the Ammonites. 📖

The LORD handed them over to me.

Why have you come to fight me today?”

## Judges 12:4

Jephthah gathered all Gilead’s men and fought Ephraim. 📖

Gilead’s men struck them down because Ephraim’s men had said, “You Gileadites are fugitives in Ephraim.

You live in Ephraim and Manasseh’s territories.”

**Help with “fugitives”:** This means people who have fled from somewhere.

## Judges 12:5

Gilead seized Jordan’s fords toward Ephraim. 📖

When a fugitive asked to cross, they asked, “Are you an Ephraimite?”

If he said, “No,”

**Help with “fords”:** These are shallow river crossings.

## Judges 12:6

they said, “Say Shibboleth.” 📖

If he said “Sibboleth,” unable to pronounce it correctly, they seized and killed him at the Jordan’s fords.

Forty-two thousand Ephraimites died then.

**About the pronunciation:** The difference is used here to identify people from Ephraim.

## Judges 12:7

Jephthah judged Israel six years, died, and was buried in a Gilead city. 📖

## Judges 12:8

Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel after him. 📖

## Judges 12:9

Ibzan had thirty sons and thirty daughters. 📖

He gave his daughters in marriage outside his clan.

He brought thirty wives from elsewhere for his sons.

Ibzan judged Israel seven years.

## Judges 12:10

Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem. 📖

## Judges 12:11

Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel ten years after Ibzan. 📖

## Judges 12:12

He died and was buried at Aijalon in Zebulun. 📖

## Judges 12:13

Abdon son of Hillel from Pirathon judged Israel after Elon. 📖

## Judges 12:14

His forty sons and thirty grandsons rode seventy donkeys. 📖

He judged eight years.

## Judges 12:15

Abdon son of Hillel from Pirathon died and was buried there in Ephraim, in Amalekite hill country. 📖
