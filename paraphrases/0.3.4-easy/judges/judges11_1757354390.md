# Judges 11 — Jephthah’s negotiations and vow

**About this chapter**

Gilead’s elders bring back Jephthah to lead them.

He disputes the Ammonite king’s land claim and then makes a vow.

The vow’s burnt-offering wording and the account of his only daughter are retained.

## Judges 11:1

Jephthah the Gileadite was a mighty warrior, son of a prostitute. 📖

Gilead was his father.

**Help with “prostitute”:** This means someone paid for sex.

## Judges 11:2

Gilead’s wife bore him sons. 📖

They grew up and drove Jephthah out, saying, “You have no inheritance in our father’s house.

You are another woman’s son.”

**Help with “inheritance”:** This means property passed to family members.

## Judges 11:3

Jephthah fled his brothers and settled in Tob. 📖

Worthless men gathered around him and traveled with him.

## Judges 11:4

Later, Ammon fought Israel, 📖

## Judges 11:5

and Gilead’s elders went to Tob to bring Jephthah. 📖

## Judges 11:6

“Lead us as commander,” they said, “to fight Ammon.” 📖

## Judges 11:7

He replied, “Did you not hate and expel me from my father’s house? 📖

Why seek me in your distress?”

## Judges 11:8

They said, “Come fight Ammon and lead all us Gileadites. 📖

That is why we turn to you.”

## Judges 11:9

He asked, “If you bring me to fight Ammon and the LORD hands them over, will I be your leader?” 📖

## Judges 11:10

They replied, “The LORD witnesses against us if we fail to do as you say.” 📖

## Judges 11:11

Jephthah went with Gilead’s elders, and the people made him leader and commander. 📖

He repeated all his terms before the LORD at Mizpah.

He refused to listen.

They also sent messengers to Moab’s king, but he refused.

So Israel stayed in Kadesh.

## Judges 11:18

They went through wilderness around Edom and Moab, reaching Moab’s east and camping beyond the Arnon. 📖

They entered no Moabite territory: the Arnon was its border.

They asked, ‘Please let us pass through your land to our own place.’

## Judges 11:20

Sihon would not trust Israel to pass through his land. 📖

He gathered all his people, camped at Jahaz, and fought Israel.

## Judges 11:25

Did he contend or fight with Israel?

## Judges 11:26

Why did you not take them back during that time?

## Judges 11:28

The king ignored Jephthah’s message. 📖

## Judges 11:29

The LORD’s Spirit came upon Jephthah. 📖

He passed through Gilead, Manasseh, and Mizpah of Gilead, advancing against Ammon.

## Judges 11:30

He vowed to the LORD, “If You hand Ammon over, 📖

## Judges 11:31

then whatever leaves my house’s door to greet my victorious return from Ammon will belong to the LORD. 📖

And I will offer it up as a burnt offering.”

**About this vow:** This is Jephthah’s vow in the account.

The chapter does not describe a command from the LORD to make it.

A burnt offering is a sacrifice burned on an altar.

## Judges 11:32

He crossed to fight, and the LORD handed Ammon over. 📖

## Judges 11:33

With a great blow he devastated twenty cities, from Aroer to near Minnith, as far as Abel-keramim. 📖

The Ammonites were subdued before Israel.

## Judges 11:34

At home in Mizpah, his daughter met him with tambourines and dancing. 📖

She was his only child, no other son or daughter.

## Judges 11:35

When Jephthah saw her, he tore his clothes and said, “No, not my daughter! 📖

You brought me to my knees and great misery.

I gave the LORD my word and cannot take it back.”

**Help with “brought me to my knees”:** He means being overwhelmed with distress.

These are Jephthah’s words to his daughter.

## Judges 11:36

Do to me as you said, for the LORD avenged you on your enemies, the Ammonites.”

## Judges 11:37

She asked, “Give me two months to wander mountains with friends and mourn my virginity.” 📖

**Help with “virginity”:** This means the state of never having had sex.

## Judges 11:38

“Go,” he said, sending her away for two months. 📖

She left with her friends and mourned her virginity in the mountains.
