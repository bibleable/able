# Judges 19 — The assault at Gibeah

**About this chapter**

A Levite travels with his concubine and servant.

This chapter includes threats of sexual violence, rape, abuse, and the cutting up of the woman’s body.

The account keeps the actions and the speakers’ words, including the disturbing offer made by their host.

## Judges 19:1

In those days Israel had no king. 📖

A Levite living in Ephraim’s remote hills took a concubine from Bethlehem in Judah.

**Help with these words:** A Levite is a member of Levi’s tribe.

A concubine is a woman in a marriage relationship with lower status than a wife.

## Judges 19:2

She was unfaithful to him and returned to her father’s house in Bethlehem in Judah. 📖

After she had been there four months,

## Judges 19:3

her husband went after her to speak kindly and bring her back. 📖

He took his servant and two donkeys.

She brought him into her father’s house.

Her father saw him and gladly welcomed him.

## Judges 19:4

His father-in-law, the woman’s father, persuaded him to stay. 📖

He remained three days, eating, drinking, and sleeping there.

## Judges 19:5

They rose early on day four and prepared to leave. 📖

Her father told his son-in-law, “Refresh your heart with a little bread, and then go.”

**Help with “refresh your heart”:** Here this means feel strengthened and refreshed by eating.

## Judges 19:6

The two men sat down and ate and drank together. 📖

Her father said, “Please stay overnight and let your heart be merry.”

## Judges 19:7

The man got up to leave, but his father-in-law persuaded him to stay that night. 📖

## Judges 19:8

He rose early on day five to leave. 📖

Her father said, “Please refresh your heart.”

They waited until late afternoon, and the two men ate.

## Judges 19:9

The man rose to leave with his concubine and servant. 📖

Her father said, “The day is drawing to a close.

Please spend the night.

See, the day is almost over.

Stay here and let your heart be merry.

Tomorrow rise early for your journey home.”

## Judges 19:10

The man would not stay overnight. 📖

He left and reached a place opposite Jebus, that is, Jerusalem.

His two saddled donkeys and concubine were with him.

## Judges 19:11

Near Jebus, with daylight almost gone, the servant told his master, “Please stop at this Jebusite city for the night.” 📖

## Judges 19:12

His master replied, “We will not enter a city of foreigners with no Israelites. 📖

We will go on to Gibeah.”

## Judges 19:13

He continued, “Come, let us try to reach Gibeah or Ramah to spend the night.” 📖

## Judges 19:14

They continued, and the sun set as they neared Gibeah in Benjamin. 📖

## Judges 19:15

They stopped to spend the night in Gibeah. 📖

The Levite sat in the city square, but no one took them home for the night.

## Judges 19:16

That evening an old man returned from work in the field. 📖

He came from Ephraim’s hills and lived in Gibeah, whose men were Benjamites.

## Judges 19:17

The old man looked up and saw the traveler in the square. 📖

He asked, “Where are you going?

Where have you come from?”

## Judges 19:18

The Levite replied, “We are traveling from Bethlehem in Judah to Ephraim’s remote hills, where I come from. 📖

I went to Bethlehem in Judah and now go to the LORD’s house.

But no one has taken me home,

## Judges 19:19

even though we have straw and feed for our donkeys. 📖

We also have bread and wine for me, the maidservant, and the young man with me.

We, your servants, lack nothing.”

**Help with “maidservant”:** This is the Levite’s description of the woman traveling with him.

## Judges 19:20

The old man said, “Peace to you. 📖

Let me supply everything you need.

Just do not spend the night in the square.”

## Judges 19:21

He took the Levite home and fed his donkeys. 📖

They washed their feet and ate and drank.

## Judges 19:22

While they enjoyed themselves, wicked men from the city suddenly surrounded the house and pounded on the door. 📖

They told the old owner, “Bring out your male guest so we can have sex with him!”

**About this demand:** These men demand that the guest be handed over for sexual violence.

## Judges 19:23

The owner went outside and said, “No, my brothers, do not do this wicked thing! 📖

This man is a guest in my house.

Do not commit this outrage.

## Judges 19:24

Let me bring out my virgin daughter and this man’s concubine. 📖

Use them and do with them as you wish.

But do not do this vile thing to this man.”

**About this offer:** These are the owner’s words, offering the women to the men.

A virgin is someone who has never had sex.

The account reports this offer without a command from God to make it.

## Judges 19:25

The men would not listen. 📖

The Levite took his concubine and sent her outside to them.

They raped and abused her all night, then let her go at dawn.

**Help with “raped”:** This means forced someone to have sex.

## Judges 19:26

Early that morning she returned to the house where her master stayed. 📖

She collapsed at the doorway and lay there until daylight.

## Judges 19:27

In the morning her master rose and opened the doors to leave on his journey. 📖

There lay his concubine, collapsed in the doorway, her hands on the threshold.

**Help with “threshold”:** This means the bottom of a doorway.

## Judges 19:28

He told her, “Get up. 📖

Let us go.”

She did not respond.

He put her on his donkey and set out for home.

**About her condition:** This verse says she did not respond.

Judges 20:4 calls her the murdered woman.

## Judges 19:29

At home he took a knife, took hold of his concubine, and cut her limb by limb into twelve pieces. 📖

He sent her throughout Israel’s territory.

## Judges 19:30

Everyone who saw it said, “Nothing like this has been seen or done since Israel came out of Egypt! 📖

Think it over, take counsel, and speak up!”

**Help with “take counsel”:** This means discuss what to do and seek advice.
