# Zephaniah 1 — The LORD warns of judgment

**About this chapter**

Zephaniah brings the LORD’s message during Josiah’s rule.

The chapter describes severe judgment against wrongdoing and false worship.

## Zephaniah 1:1

The LORD’s word came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah. 📖

This was during the rule of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

## Zephaniah 1:2

The LORD declares, “I will sweep away everything from the face of the earth. 📖

## Zephaniah 1:3

I will sweep away people, animals, birds in the air, and fish in the sea. 📖

I will sweep away idols with their wicked worshippers.

I will cut off people from the face of the earth,” declares the LORD.

**Help with idols:** Idols are objects treated as gods.

## Zephaniah 1:4

“I will stretch out my hand against Judah and everyone living in Jerusalem. 📖

I will remove every trace of Baal from this place.

I will remove the names of the priests who worship idols and other gods.

**Help with Baal:** Baal was a god worshipped by peoples around Israel.

**Help with hand:** Here, God’s hand pictures his power to act.

## Zephaniah 1:5

I will remove those who bow on rooftops to worship the stars and other heavenly bodies. 📖

They bow and swear by the LORD but also swear by Milcom.

**Help with swear by:** This means use a god’s name to make a serious promise.

**Help with Milcom:** Milcom was a god worshipped by the Ammonites.

## Zephaniah 1:6

I will remove those who turn away from following the LORD. 📖

They neither seek the LORD nor ask him for guidance.”

## Zephaniah 1:7

Be silent before the Lord GOD, because the Day of the LORD is near. 📖

The LORD has prepared a sacrifice and set his guests apart.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

**Help with sacrifice and guests:** The passage pictures judgment as a sacrifice with invited guests.

## Zephaniah 1:8

The LORD says, “On the day of my sacrifice, I will punish the princes and the king’s sons. 📖

I will punish everyone dressed in foreign clothing.

**Help with princes:** Princes are royal leaders.

**About foreign clothing:** This is part of the specific accusation in this passage.

## Zephaniah 1:9

On that day, I will punish everyone who leaps over the doorway’s threshold. 📖

They fill their master’s house with violence and lies.

**Help with threshold:** A threshold is the base of a doorway.

The practice of leaping over it needs further editorial explanation.

## Zephaniah 1:10

On that day,” declares the LORD, “a cry will come from the Fish Gate. 📖

Wailing will come from the Second District, and a loud crash from the hills.

## Zephaniah 1:11

Cry, you people living in the Hollow! 📖

All your merchants will be silenced.

Everyone who weighs silver will be cut off.

**Help with merchants:** Merchants buy and sell goods.

Weighing silver was part of trade.

## Zephaniah 1:12

At that time, I will search Jerusalem with lamps. 📖

I will punish men who are settled in their careless confidence.

They say to themselves, ‘The LORD will do nothing, either good or bad.’

## Zephaniah 1:13

Their wealth will be stolen, and their houses ruined. 📖

They will build houses but not live in them.

They will plant vineyards but never drink their wine.

**Help with vineyards:** Vineyards are places where grapes grow.

## Zephaniah 1:14

The great Day of the LORD is near and coming quickly. 📖

Listen!

The Day of the LORD!

The mighty will cry bitterly then.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

## Zephaniah 1:15

That will be a day of anger, trouble, and distress. 📖

It will be a day of destruction and ruin.

It will be a day of darkness, gloom, clouds, and blackness.

## Zephaniah 1:16

It will be a day of horn blasts and battle cries. 📖

They will sound against the protected cities and high corner towers.

## Zephaniah 1:17

I will bring distress on people, so they walk like people who cannot see. 📖

This is because they have sinned against the LORD.

Their blood will be poured out like dust, and their flesh like dung.

**Help with sinned:** Sinned means done wrong against God.

**About the comparison:** The passage pictures distressed people losing their way.

It does not say that physical blindness is caused by sin.

**Help with dung:** Dung means animal waste.

The blood and flesh comparison describes a degrading, violent judgment.

## Zephaniah 1:18

Neither silver nor gold will save them on the day of the LORD’s anger. 📖

The fire of his jealousy will burn up the whole earth.”

He will bring a sudden end to everyone living on the earth.

**Help with jealousy:** Here, jealousy describes the LORD’s demand for loyalty.

Fire pictures destructive judgment.
