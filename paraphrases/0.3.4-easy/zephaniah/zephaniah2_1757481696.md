# Zephaniah 2 — Seek the LORD before the day comes

**About this chapter**

The people are urged to seek the LORD.

The chapter gives judgments against surrounding peoples and Nineveh.

## Zephaniah 2:1

Gather yourselves together, shameful nation! 📖

## Zephaniah 2:2

Gather before the order takes effect and the day passes like chaff. 📖

Gather before the LORD’s burning anger comes upon you.

Gather before the day of the LORD’s anger comes upon you.

**Help with chaff:** Chaff is the light, dry covering of grain.

It pictures something that passes away quickly.

## Zephaniah 2:3

Seek the LORD, all you humble people of the earth who carry out his justice. 📖

Seek what is right, and seek humility.

Perhaps you will be sheltered on the day of the LORD’s anger.

**Help with humble and humility:** These mean not acting as though you are above others.

**Help with justice:** Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.

## Zephaniah 2:4

Gaza will be abandoned, and Ashkelon left in ruins. 📖

Ashdod will be driven out at noon, and Ekron uprooted.

## Zephaniah 2:5

Woe to the people of the seacoast, the nation of the Cherethites! 📖

The LORD’s word is against you, Canaan, land of the Philistines.

“I will destroy you, and no one will be left.”

**Help with woe:** Woe announces disaster or judgment.

## Zephaniah 2:6

The seacoast will become grassland, with wells for shepherds and pens for sheep. 📖

## Zephaniah 2:7

The coast will belong to the remaining people of Judah. 📖

They will find grassland there and lie down among Ashkelon’s houses in the evening.

The LORD their God will care for them and restore their captives.

**Help with captives:** Captives are people held under another power.

## Zephaniah 2:8

The LORD says, “I have heard Moab’s scorn and the Ammonites’ insults. 📖

They mocked my people and threatened their borders.

## Zephaniah 2:9

As surely as I live,” declares the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, “Moab will become like Sodom. 📖

The Ammonites will become like Gomorrah, a place of weeds and salt pits, a wasteland forever.

My remaining people will rob them and take their land.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

**Help with Sodom and Gomorrah:** These cities were destroyed in the Genesis account.

## Zephaniah 2:10

This will be their payment for their pride. 📖

They mocked and insulted the people of the LORD of Hosts.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Zephaniah 2:11

The LORD will terrify them when he starves all the earth’s gods. 📖

Nations from every shore will bow to worship him, each in its own place.

**Help with starves the gods:** This is the source’s picture of bringing other gods to nothing.

## Zephaniah 2:12

The LORD says, “You Cushites too will be killed by my sword.” 📖

## Zephaniah 2:13

The LORD will stretch out his hand against the north and destroy Assyria. 📖

He will make Nineveh deserted and as dry as a desert.

**Help with hand:** Here, hand pictures the LORD’s power to act.

## Zephaniah 2:14

Herds and creatures of every kind will lie down inside Nineveh. 📖

Desert owls and screech owls will sit on top of her pillars.

Their calls will sound from the window, while ruin lies at the doorway.

God will uncover the cedar beams.

**Help with cedar:** Cedar is a tree whose wood is used for building.

## Zephaniah 2:15

This carefree city lived safely and thought, “I am the one, and there is no other.” 📖

What a ruin it has become, a resting place for animals!

Everyone who passes hisses and shakes a fist at it.
