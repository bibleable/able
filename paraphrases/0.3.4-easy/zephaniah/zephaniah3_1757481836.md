# Zephaniah 3 — Judgment and a promise of restoration

**About this chapter**

The message condemns the city’s wrongdoing.

It also promises a humble people, rescue, and the LORD’s presence.

## Zephaniah 3:1

Woe to the city of people who oppress others! 📖

The city is rebellious and defiled.

**Help with woe:** Woe announces disaster or judgment.

**Help with defiled:** Here, defiled means polluted by wrongdoing.

## Zephaniah 3:2

The city listened to no voice and accepted no correction. 📖

She does not trust in the LORD or draw near to her God.

**Help with she:** The city is pictured as a person.

## Zephaniah 3:3

Her princes are roaring lions. 📖

Her judges are evening wolves that leave nothing for morning.

**Help with these pictures:** The leaders are compared to animals that attack and eat their prey.

## Zephaniah 3:4

Her prophets are reckless and break trust. 📖

Her priests dishonour the holy place and do violence to the law.

**Help with prophets:** Prophets are people who speak as messengers from God.

**Help with do violence to the law:** This describes seriously mistreating the law.

## Zephaniah 3:5

The LORD within the city is righteous and does no wrong. 📖

Every morning, he brings his justice.

He does not fail at dawn, yet unjust people know no shame.

**Help with righteous and justice:** Righteous means right before God.

Justice means fairness and putting wrongs right.

## Zephaniah 3:6

The LORD says, “I cut off nations and destroyed their corner towers. 📖

I made their streets empty, with no one passing through.

Their cities are ruined, with no one living in them.

## Zephaniah 3:7

I said, ‘Surely you will fear me and accept correction.’ 📖

Then the city’s home would not be cut off, despite all the punishments I brought on her.

But they got up early to corrupt everything they did.

**Help with fear me:** Here, fear includes respect for God’s authority.

**Help with corrupt:** Corrupt means make wrong or dishonest.

## Zephaniah 3:8

So wait for me,” declares the LORD, “until the day I rise to give testimony. 📖

I have decided to gather nations and kingdoms.

I will pour all my burning anger on them.

The fire of my jealousy will burn up all the earth.

**Help with jealousy:** Here, jealousy describes the LORD’s demand for loyalty.

## Zephaniah 3:9

Then I will restore pure lips to the peoples. 📖

They will all call on the LORD’s name and serve him shoulder to shoulder.

**Help with pure lips:** This pictures speech made fit to honour God.

**Help with shoulder to shoulder:** This means serving together.

## Zephaniah 3:10

From beyond Cush’s rivers, my worshippers, my scattered people, will bring me an offering. 📖

**Help with offering:** An offering is a gift to God in worship.

## Zephaniah 3:11

On that day, you will not be shamed for the deeds by which you rebelled against me. 📖

I will remove those among you who rejoice in their pride.

You will never again be proud on my holy mountain.

**Help with holy:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Zephaniah 3:12

I will leave among you a gentle and humble people. 📖

They will trust in the LORD’s name.

**Help with humble:** Humble means not acting as though you are above others.

## Zephaniah 3:13

Israel’s remaining people will no longer do wrong or tell lies. 📖

No words that deceive will come from their mouths.

They will feed and lie down, and no one will make them afraid.”

## Zephaniah 3:14

Sing for joy, Daughter of Zion! 📖

Shout aloud, Israel!

Daughter of Jerusalem, be glad and rejoice with all your heart!

**Help with Daughter:** This is a way of addressing the city and its people.

## Zephaniah 3:15

The LORD has taken away your punishment and turned back your enemy. 📖

Israel’s King, the LORD, is among you.

You will no longer fear any harm.

## Zephaniah 3:16

On that day, people will tell Jerusalem, “Zion, do not fear. 📖

Do not let your hands hang limp.

**Help with hands hang limp:** This pictures losing courage and giving up.

## Zephaniah 3:17

The LORD your God is among you and is mighty to save. 📖

He will rejoice over you with gladness and quiet you with his love.

He will rejoice over you with singing.”

## Zephaniah 3:18

The LORD says, “I will gather those among you who grieve over the appointed feasts. 📖

Then you will no longer suffer scorn.

**Help with feasts:** These are religious gatherings held at set times.

## Zephaniah 3:19

At that time, I will deal with everyone who makes you suffer. 📖

I will save those who have difficulty walking and gather the scattered.

I will give praise and fame throughout the earth to those who were disgraced.

## Zephaniah 3:20

At that time, I will bring you in and gather you. 📖

I will give you fame and praise among all the earth’s peoples.

This will happen when I restore your captives before your own eyes,” says the LORD.

**Help with captives:** Captives are people held under another power.
