# 1 Chronicles 18 — David’s victories and officials

**About this chapter**

David defeats surrounding peoples and dedicates captured wealth to the LORD. | The record names his officials and their responsibilities.

## 1 Chronicles 18:1

David defeated and subdued the Philistines and took Gath and its villages from them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:2

He defeated Moab, who became subject to him and paid tribute. 📖

**Help with tribute:** Payment demanded by a ruling power.

## 1 Chronicles 18:3

David defeated Hadadezer king of Zobah as far as Hamath. 📖

Hadadezer had marched out to establish his rule along the Euphrates River.

## 1 Chronicles 18:4

David captured 1,000 chariots, 7,000 charioteers, and 20,000 foot soldiers. 📖

He hamstrung all the horses except 100 kept for chariots.

**Help with hamstrung:** Cut the tendons of the legs to disable the horses.

## 1 Chronicles 18:5

Arameans from Damascus came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah. 📖

David killed 22,000 of them.

## 1 Chronicles 18:6

He placed troops in Aram of Damascus. 📖

The Arameans submitted and paid tribute.

The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

## 1 Chronicles 18:7

David took Hadadezer’s officers’ gold shields to Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:8

He took much bronze from Hadadezer’s cities Tibhath and Cun. 📖

Solomon used it for the bronze Sea, pillars, and bronze objects.

**Help with Sea:** A large basin used at the temple.

## 1 Chronicles 18:9

Tou king of Hamath heard David defeated Hadadezer king of Zobah’s whole army. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:10

He sent his son Hadoram to greet David and bless him for defeating Hadadezer, who had fought Tou. 📖

Hadoram brought gold, silver, and bronze objects.

## 1 Chronicles 18:11

David dedicated these to the LORD with silver and gold captured from Edom, Moab, Ammon, the Philistines, and Amalek. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:12

Abishai son of Zeruiah killed 18,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:13

He placed troops in Edom, and all Edom became subject to David. 📖

The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

## 1 Chronicles 18:14

David ruled all Israel, giving all his people justice and righteous government. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:15

Joab son of Zeruiah led the army. 📖

Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud kept the records.

## 1 Chronicles 18:16

Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests. 📖

Shavsha was the scribe.

**Help with scribe:** An official who writes documents.

## 1 Chronicles 18:17

Benaiah son of Jehoiada led the Cherethites and Pelethites. 📖

David’s sons were chief officials beside the king.
