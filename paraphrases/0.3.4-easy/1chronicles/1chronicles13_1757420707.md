# 1 Chronicles 13 — The ark and Uzzah’s death

**About this chapter**

David gathers Israel to bring back God’s ark. | Uzzah dies after touching it, and David becomes afraid. | The ark stays with Obed-edom’s household.

## 1 Chronicles 13:1

David discussed matters with all his leaders, commanders of thousands and hundreds. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:2

He told all Israel, “If you agree and this is from the LORD our God, let us send word everywhere. 📖

Call our brothers throughout Israel and priests and Levites in their cities and pastures to join us.

## 1 Chronicles 13:3

Let us bring back our God’s ark. 📖

We did not seek him during Saul’s reign.”

**Help with ark:** The sacred chest associated with the LORD’s covenant.

## 1 Chronicles 13:4

The whole assembly agreed because everyone thought this was right. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:5

David gathered all Israel, from Egypt’s River Shihor to Lebo-hamath, to bring God’s ark from Kiriath-jearim. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:6

David and all Israel went to Baalah in Judah, also called Kiriath-jearim, for the ark. 📖

It belonged to God the LORD, enthroned between the cherubim, and was called by the Name.

**Help with these words:** Cherubim are heavenly beings represented by winged figures above the ark.

The Name refers to the LORD’s name.

## 1 Chronicles 13:7

They carried God’s ark from Abinadab’s house on a new cart. 📖

Uzzah and Ahio guided it.

## 1 Chronicles 13:8

David and all Israel celebrated before God with all their strength. 📖

They sang and played harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

## 1 Chronicles 13:9

At Chidon’s threshing floor, the oxen stumbled. 📖

Uzzah reached out and held the ark.

**Help with threshing floor:** A place where grain was separated from stalks.

## 1 Chronicles 13:10

The LORD’s anger burned against Uzzah for touching the ark. 📖

He struck Uzzah down, and Uzzah died there before God.

## 1 Chronicles 13:11

David became angry because the LORD burst out against Uzzah. 📖

He called the place Perez-uzzah, its name to this day.

**Help with Perez-uzzah:** The name recalls the outbreak against Uzzah.

“To this day” refers to the account’s time.

## 1 Chronicles 13:12

David feared God that day. 📖

“How can I bring God’s ark to me?”

## 1 Chronicles 13:13

He did not bring it to the City of David. 📖

He took it to Obed-edom the Gittite’s house.

## 1 Chronicles 13:14

God’s ark stayed in Obed-edom’s house with his family for 3 months. 📖

The LORD blessed his household and everything he owned.
