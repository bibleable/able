# 1 Chronicles 20 — Rabbah and battles with Philistine warriors

**About this chapter**

Joab attacks Rabbah while David stays in Jerusalem. | David takes the crown and plunder. | The record then describes battles with descendants of Rapha.

## 1 Chronicles 20:1

In spring, when kings went to war, Joab led the army and devastated Ammon’s land. 📖

He surrounded Rabbah while David stayed in Jerusalem.

Joab attacked and demolished Rabbah.

## 1 Chronicles 20:2

David took their king’s crown, weighing 1 talent of gold and set with precious stones. 📖

It was placed on David’s head.

He took much plunder from the city.

**Help with these words:** A talent was a large unit of weight.

Plunder means goods taken by force.

## 1 Chronicles 20:3

David brought out the people and made them work with saws, iron picks, and axes. 📖

He did this to all Ammonite cities.

David and all his troops returned to Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 20:4

Later, war with the Philistines broke out at Gezer. 📖

Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai, a Rephaim descendant.

The Philistines were subdued.

## 1 Chronicles 20:5

In another Philistine battle, Elhanan son of Jair killed Lahmi, brother of Goliath the Gittite. 📖

His spear’s shaft was like a weaver’s beam.

**Help with weaver’s beam:** A thick bar used in making cloth.

## 1 Chronicles 20:6

In a battle at Gath, a very tall man had 6 fingers on each hand and 6 toes per foot. 📖

There were 24 altogether.

He also descended from Rapha.

## 1 Chronicles 20:7

He taunted Israel. 📖

Jonathan, son of David’s brother Shimei, killed him.

## 1 Chronicles 20:8

These descendants of Rapha in Gath were killed by David and his servants. 📖
