# 1 Chronicles 25 — Musicians and their service order

**About this chapter**

David sets apart musicians from Asaph’s, Heman’s, and Jeduthun’s families. | They serve with music and prophecy. | Lots determine each group’s turn.

## 1 Chronicles 25:1

David and the army commanders set apart some sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun. 📖

They would prophesy with lyres, harps, and cymbals.

These men performed that service:

**Help with prophesy:** Speak or sing messages from God.

## 1 Chronicles 25:2

Asaph’s sons were Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah. 📖

Asaph directed them and prophesied under the king’s direction.

## 1 Chronicles 25:3

Jeduthun’s 6 sons were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Shimei, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah. 📖

Their father directed them, prophesying with the harp and thanking and praising the LORD.

## 1 Chronicles 25:4

Heman’s sons were Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:5

God gave these sons to Heman, the king’s seer, through his promises to exalt him. 📖

God gave him 14 sons and 3 daughters.

**Help with seer:** A prophet who receives God’s messages or visions.

## 1 Chronicles 25:6

Their fathers directed all these musicians in service with cymbals, harps, and lyres at the LORD’s house. 📖

Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman served under the king’s direction.

## 1 Chronicles 25:7

With their relatives, they numbered 288, all trained and skilled in songs to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:8

They cast lots for duties, young and old, teachers and pupils alike. 📖

**Help with lots:** A way of making an allocation by drawing marked objects.
