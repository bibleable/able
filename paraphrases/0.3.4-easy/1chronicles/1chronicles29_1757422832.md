# 1 Chronicles 29 — Gifts, prayer, and Solomon’s kingship

**About this chapter**

David and the leaders give for God’s house. | David thanks God and prays for the people and Solomon. | Solomon is confirmed as king, and the account records David’s death.

## 1 Chronicles 29:1

David told everyone, “God chose my son Solomon, but he is young and inexperienced. 📖

The task is great because this palace is for the LORD God, not for a human.

## 1 Chronicles 29:2

I prepared with all my ability for my God’s house. 📖

Gold, silver, bronze, iron, and wood are ready for objects made from each.

There is abundant onyx for settings, turquoise, coloured and precious stones, and marble slabs.

## 1 Chronicles 29:3

I delight in my God’s house. 📖

I also give my personal gold and silver treasures, beyond all I prepared for this holy temple:

## 1 Chronicles 29:4

3,000 talents of Ophir’s gold and 7,000 talents of refined silver to cover the buildings’ walls, 📖

**Help with talent:** A large unit of weight.

## 1 Chronicles 29:5

for gold work, silver work, and all the craftsmen’s work. 📖

Who will willingly consecrate themselves to the LORD today?”

**Help with consecrate:** Devote oneself to sacred service.

## 1 Chronicles 29:6

Family leaders, tribal officers, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and officials managing the king’s work gave willingly. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:7

They gave God’s house 5,000 talents and 10,000 darics of gold, and 10,000 talents of silver. 📖

They gave 18,000 talents of bronze and 100,000 talents of iron.

**Help with daric:** A gold coin.

## 1 Chronicles 29:8

Those with precious stones gave them to the LORD’s house’s treasury under Jehiel the Gershonite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:9

The people rejoiced because their leaders gave freely and wholeheartedly to the LORD. 📖

David rejoiced greatly too.

## 1 Chronicles 29:10

David blessed the LORD before everyone. 📖

“LORD, God of our father Israel, be blessed from everlasting to everlasting.

## 1 Chronicles 29:11

LORD, greatness, power, glory, splendour, and majesty are yours. 📖

Everything in heaven and earth belongs to you.

Yours is the kingdom, and you are exalted as head over all.

## 1 Chronicles 29:12

Wealth and honour come from you. 📖

You rule all.

Power and might are in your hands to raise up and strengthen everyone.

## 1 Chronicles 29:13

Our God, we thank you and praise your glorious name. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:14

Who am I, and who are my people, that we can give so generously? 📖

Everything comes from you.

We give you what came from your own hand.

## 1 Chronicles 29:15

We are foreigners and strangers before you, like all our ancestors. 📖

Our days on earth are like a shadow, without hope.

**Help with shadow:** This pictures a life that is brief and cannot be held onto.

## 1 Chronicles 29:16

LORD our God, all we provided to build for your holy Name comes from your hand. 📖

It all belongs to you.

## 1 Chronicles 29:17

My God, you test hearts and delight in uprightness. 📖

I gave all this willingly with an upright heart.

I see your people here giving joyfully and willingly.

## 1 Chronicles 29:18

LORD, God of our ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, keep this desire in your people’s hearts forever. 📖

Direct their hearts towards you.

## 1 Chronicles 29:19

Give Solomon my son a whole heart to carry out all your commands and laws. 📖

Let him build your palace, for which I prepared.”

## 1 Chronicles 29:20

David told everyone to bless the LORD their God. 📖

They blessed the LORD, their ancestors’ God, and bowed in homage to the LORD and king.

**Help with homage:** An expression of deep honour.

## 1 Chronicles 29:21

Next day, they offered 1,000 bulls, 1,000 rams, and 1,000 lambs as burnt offerings to the LORD. 📖

They offered their drink offerings and many other sacrifices for all Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 29:22

They ate and drank joyfully before the LORD that day. 📖

For the 2nd time, they made David’s son Solomon king.

They anointed him before the LORD as ruler and Zadok as priest.

## 1 Chronicles 29:23

Solomon sat on the LORD’s throne as king instead of David. 📖

He prospered, and all Israel obeyed.

## 1 Chronicles 29:24

All officials, mighty men, and King David’s sons pledged loyalty to Solomon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:25

The LORD greatly exalted Solomon before all Israel. 📖

He gave him royal majesty greater than any earlier Israelite king.

## 1 Chronicles 29:26

David son of Jesse ruled all Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:27

He ruled Israel for 40 years, 7 in Hebron and 33 in Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:28

He died very old, with years, riches, and honour in abundance. 📖

His son Solomon became king.

## 1 Chronicles 29:29

David’s acts from first to last are recorded in the Chronicles of Samuel the Seer. 📖

They are also in the Chronicles of Nathan the Prophet and the Chronicles of Gad the Seer,

## 1 Chronicles 29:30

with all his reign’s details, power, and events affecting him, Israel, and all the lands’ kingdoms. 📖
