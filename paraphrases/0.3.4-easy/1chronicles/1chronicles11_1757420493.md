# 1 Chronicles 11 — David becomes king and his warriors are named

**About this chapter**

Israel makes David king, and he captures Jerusalem. | The account describes his warriors and their actions. | It then lists their names and family or place connections.

## 1 Chronicles 11:1

All Israel gathered to David at Hebron. 📖

“We are your own flesh and blood.

**Help with flesh and blood:** This means they are his relatives and people.

## 1 Chronicles 11:2

Even under Saul, you led Israel out and back. 📖

The LORD your God said you would shepherd his people Israel and rule them.”

**Help with shepherd:** This pictures leading and caring for people like a shepherd cares for sheep.

## 1 Chronicles 11:3

Israel’s elders came to David at Hebron. 📖

He made a covenant with them before the LORD.

They anointed him Israel’s king, as the LORD said through Samuel.

**Help with these words:** A covenant is a binding agreement.

Anointing involves pouring oil to mark appointment.

## 1 Chronicles 11:4

David and all Israel marched to Jerusalem, also called Jebus. 📖

Jebusites lived there.

## 1 Chronicles 11:5

Jebus’s people said David would never enter. 📖

He captured Zion’s fortress, the City of David.

## 1 Chronicles 11:6

David promised chief command to the first man to strike a Jebusite. 📖

Joab son of Zeruiah went first and became chief.

## 1 Chronicles 11:7

David lived in the fortress, so it was called the City of David. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:8

He built around the city from the supporting terraces to the surrounding wall. 📖

Joab restored the rest.

## 1 Chronicles 11:9

David grew greater because the LORD of Hosts was with him. 📖

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** The LORD who rules heavenly armies.

## 1 Chronicles 11:10

These were David’s chief warriors. 📖

With all Israel, they strengthened his kingship as the LORD promised concerning Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 11:11

David’s warriors included Jashobeam son of Hachmoni, chief of the officers. 📖

He killed 300 men with his spear at one time.

## 1 Chronicles 11:12

Next was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, one of the 3 mighty men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:13

He was with David at Pas-dammim when the Philistines gathered for battle. 📖

The troops fled from the Philistines near a barley field.

## 1 Chronicles 11:14

Eleazar and David stood in the field’s middle and defended it. 📖

They killed Philistines, and the LORD gave a great victory.

## 1 Chronicles 11:15

3 of the 30 chiefs came to David at the rock by Adullam’s cave. 📖

Philistine troops camped in Rephaim Valley.

## 1 Chronicles 11:16

David was in the stronghold, and Philistine troops held Bethlehem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:17

David longed for water from the well by Bethlehem’s gate. 📖

“If only someone would bring me a drink!”

## 1 Chronicles 11:18

The Three broke through the Philistine camp and brought water from Bethlehem’s gate well. 📖

David refused it and poured it out to the LORD.

## 1 Chronicles 11:19

“My God, I must not drink this! 📖

Would I drink the blood of men who risked their lives?”

He refused because they risked their lives to bring it.

These were the 3 mighty men’s deeds.

**Help with blood here:** David compares drinking this costly water to drinking the men’s blood.

## 1 Chronicles 11:20

Abishai, Joab’s brother, led the Three. 📖

He killed 300 men with his spear and won honour alongside the Three.

## 1 Chronicles 11:21

He received double honour above the Three and became their commander, though he was not counted among the Three. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:22

Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a brave man from Kabzeel with many great deeds. 📖

He killed 2 Moabite champions.

On a snowy day, he entered a pit and killed a lion.

## 1 Chronicles 11:23

He killed an Egyptian 5 cubits tall. 📖

The Egyptian held a spear like a weaver’s beam.

Benaiah approached with a club, seized the spear, and killed him with it.

**Help with these words:** A cubit was roughly a forearm’s length.

A weaver’s beam is a thick bar used in making cloth.

## 1 Chronicles 11:24

These deeds brought Benaiah son of Jehoiada honour alongside the 3 mighty men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:25

He was most honoured among the Thirty but was not one of the Three. 📖

David put him over his guard.

## 1 Chronicles 11:26

The mighty men included Asahel, Joab’s brother, and Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:27

Shammoth the Harorite and Helez the Pelonite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:28

Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite and Abiezer the Anathothite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:29

Sibbecai the Hushathite and Ilai the Ahohite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:30

Maharai the Netophathite and Heled son of Baanah the Netophathite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:31

Ithai son of Ribai from Gibeah of Benjamin and Benaiah the Pirathonite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:32

Hurai from Gaash’s brooks and Abiel the Arbathite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:33

Azmaveth the Baharumite and Eliahba the Shaalbonite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:34

Hashem the Gizonite’s sons and Jonathan son of Shagee the Hararite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:35

Ahiam son of Sachar the Hararite and Eliphal son of Ur, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:36

Hepher the Mecherathite and Ahijah the Pelonite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:37

Hezro the Carmelite and Naarai son of Ezbai, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:38

Joel, Nathan’s brother, and Mibhar son of Hagri, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:39

Zelek the Ammonite and Naharai the Beerothite, armour-bearer to Joab son of Zeruiah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:40

Ira the Ithrite and Gareb the Ithrite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:41

Uriah the Hittite and Zabad son of Ahlai, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:42

Adina son of Shiza, the Reubenites’ chief, and the 30 with him, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:43

Hanan son of Maacah and Joshaphat the Mithnite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:44

Uzzia the Ashterathite, and Shama and Jeiel, Hotham the Aroerite’s sons, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:45

Jediael son of Shimri and his brother Joha the Tizite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:46

Eliel the Mahavite, Jeribai and Joshaviah, Elnaam’s sons, and Ithmah the Moabite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:47

Eliel, Obed, and Jaasiel the Mezobaite. 📖
