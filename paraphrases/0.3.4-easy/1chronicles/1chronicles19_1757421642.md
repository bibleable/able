# 1 Chronicles 19 — War with Ammon and Aram

**About this chapter**

David sends comforters to Hanun after his father dies. | They are humiliated, and war follows. | The account describes Joab’s battle plan and David’s later victory.

## 1 Chronicles 19:1

Nahash king of Ammon died, and his son succeeded him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:2

David said, “I will show kindness to Hanun son of Nahash because his father was kind to me.” 📖

He sent messengers to comfort Hanun about his father’s death.

His servants arrived in Ammon to comfort him.

## 1 Chronicles 19:3

Ammon’s princes asked Hanun, “Do you think David sent comforters out of respect for your father? 📖

Did his servants not come to explore, spy, and overthrow the land?”

## 1 Chronicles 19:4

Hanun shaved David’s servants’ beards and cut their clothes off at the hips. 📖

He sent them away.

## 1 Chronicles 19:5

David heard and sent messengers to meet his deeply humiliated men. 📖

“Stay in Jericho until your beards grow back.

Then return.”

## 1 Chronicles 19:6

Ammon knew they had become a stench to David. 📖

Hanun and Ammon sent 1,000 silver talents to hire chariots and horsemen from Aram-naharaim, Aram-maacah, and Zobah.

**Help with these words:** Stench pictures being deeply offensive.

A talent was a large unit of weight.

## 1 Chronicles 19:7

They hired 32,000 chariots and Maacah’s king with his troops, who camped near Medeba. 📖

Ammon gathered from its cities and marched to battle.

## 1 Chronicles 19:8

David heard and sent Joab with the whole army of mighty men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:9

Ammon lined up for battle at the city entrance. 📖

The other kings stayed apart in open country.

## 1 Chronicles 19:10

Joab saw enemies in front and behind. 📖

He chose some of Israel’s best men and placed them against Aram.

## 1 Chronicles 19:11

He put the remaining troops under his brother Abishai against Ammon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:12

Joab said, “If Aram is too strong for me, help me. 📖

If Ammon is too strong for you, I will help you.

## 1 Chronicles 19:13

Be strong and fight bravely for our people and our God’s cities. 📖

May the LORD do what is good in his sight.”

## 1 Chronicles 19:14

Joab and his troops advanced, and the Arameans fled. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:15

Ammon saw Aram flee and fled from Abishai into the city. 📖

Joab returned to Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 19:16

Aram saw Israel had defeated them. 📖

They called more Arameans from beyond the Euphrates, led by Shophach, Hadadezer’s army commander.

## 1 Chronicles 19:17

David heard, gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan, and lined up against Aram. 📖

As he prepared to engage them, they fought him.

## 1 Chronicles 19:18

Aram fled from Israel. 📖

David killed 7,000 charioteers, 40,000 foot soldiers, and Shophach their army commander.

## 1 Chronicles 19:19

Hadadezer’s subjects saw Israel defeated them and made peace with David, becoming his subjects. 📖

Aram would no longer help Ammon.
