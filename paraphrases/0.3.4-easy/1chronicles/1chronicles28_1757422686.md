# 1 Chronicles 28 — David’s charge and temple plans

**About this chapter**

David gathers Israel’s leaders and charges Solomon to serve the LORD. | He gives Solomon detailed temple plans. | The promise and warning concerning obedience remain part of the charge.

## 1 Chronicles 28:1

David gathered all Israel’s leaders in Jerusalem: tribal and royal division leaders, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and court officials. 📖

He called officials managing his and his sons’ property and cattle, and all mighty warriors.

## 1 Chronicles 28:2

David stood and said, “Listen, my brothers and people. 📖

I wanted to build a resting house for the LORD’s covenant ark and a footstool for our God.

I prepared to build.

**Help with footstool:** This image relates the sacred place to God’s royal presence.

## 1 Chronicles 28:3

God told me, ‘You must not build for my Name because you are a warrior who shed blood.’ 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:4

The LORD, Israel’s God, chose me from my father’s family to be Israel’s king forever. 📖

He chose Judah to lead, my father’s household from Judah, and me from my father’s sons.

He was pleased to make me king over all Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 28:5

The LORD gave me many sons. 📖

He chose Solomon to sit on the throne of the LORD’s kingdom over Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 28:6

He said, ‘Solomon will build my house and courts. 📖

I chose him as my son, and I will be his Father.

## 1 Chronicles 28:7

I will establish his kingdom forever if he firmly keeps my commands and rules as he does today.’ 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:8

Before all Israel, the LORD’s assembly, and our God who hears, seek and keep all the LORD your God’s commands. 📖

Do this so you may possess this good land and leave it to your descendants forever.

## 1 Chronicles 28:9

Solomon my son, know your father’s God and serve him wholeheartedly and willingly. 📖

The LORD searches every heart and understands every thought’s purpose.

If you seek him, you will find him.

If you abandon him, he will reject you forever.

## 1 Chronicles 28:10

The LORD chose you to build his sanctuary’s house. 📖

Be strong and do it.”

## 1 Chronicles 28:11

David gave Solomon plans for the temple porch, buildings, storehouses, upstairs rooms, inner rooms, and mercy-seat room. 📖

**Help with mercy seat:** The sacred cover of the covenant ark.

## 1 Chronicles 28:12

The plans included everything David intended for the LORD’s house’s courts and surrounding rooms. 📖

They included God’s house’s treasuries and stores of dedicated things,

## 1 Chronicles 28:13

priestly and Levite divisions, every duty in the LORD’s house, and all its service objects: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:14

gold weights for all gold objects and silver weights for all silver objects, for every kind of service, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:15

each gold lampstand’s and lamps’ weight, and each silver lampstand’s and lamps’ weight according to its use, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:16

gold weight for each showbread table and silver for the silver tables, 📖

**Help with showbread:** Bread set before the LORD.

## 1 Chronicles 28:17

pure-gold weights for forks, sprinkling bowls, and pitchers, each gold dish’s weight, and each silver bowl’s weight, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:18

refined-gold weight for the incense altar, and plans for the gold cherubim’s chariot. 📖

Their wings spread over and sheltered the LORD’s covenant ark.

**Help with cherubim:** Heavenly beings represented by winged figures above the ark.

## 1 Chronicles 28:19

David said, “The LORD made every detail of this plan clear to me in writing by his hand upon me. 📖

**Help with his hand upon me:** This describes the LORD’s guidance.

## 1 Chronicles 28:20

Solomon, be strong and courageous and do it. 📖

Do not fear or lose heart, because the LORD God, my God, is with you.

He will not fail or abandon you before all work for his house’s service is finished.

## 1 Chronicles 28:21

Priestly and Levite divisions are ready for all service in God’s house. 📖

Every willing skilled worker is available.

The officials and all people are fully at your command.”
