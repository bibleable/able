# 1 Chronicles 4 — Judah’s families and Simeon’s settlements

**About this chapter**

The record continues Judah’s families and includes Jabez’s prayer. | It then names Simeon’s families, settlements, and leaders. | It also reports warfare and the destruction of other peoples.

## 1 Chronicles 4:1

Judah’s descendants were Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:2

Reaiah son of Shobal fathered Jahath. 📖

Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad.

These were the Zorathites’ clans.

## 1 Chronicles 4:3

Etam’s sons were Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash. 📖

Their sister was Hazzelelponi.

## 1 Chronicles 4:4

Penuel was Gedor’s father, and Ezer was Hushah’s father. 📖

These descended from Hur, Ephrathah’s firstborn and Bethlehem’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 4:5

Ashhur, Tekoa’s father, had 2 wives, Helah and Naarah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:6

Naarah bore Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. 📖

These were Naarah’s descendants.

## 1 Chronicles 4:7

Helah’s sons were Zereth, Zohar, Ethnan, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:8

and Koz. 📖

Koz fathered Anub, Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel son of Harum.

## 1 Chronicles 4:9

Jabez was more honourable than his brothers. 📖

His mother named him Jabez because she bore him in pain.

**Help with Jabez:** The name is linked here with pain.

## 1 Chronicles 4:10

Jabez prayed to Israel’s God, “Bless me and enlarge my territory! 📖

May your hand be with me.

Keep me from harm so I am free from pain.”

God granted his request.

**Help with your hand:** This pictures God’s help and power.

This account of Jabez’s prayer is not a guarantee that every prayer brings greater territory.

## 1 Chronicles 4:11

Chelub, Shuhah’s brother, fathered Mehir, who fathered Eshton. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:12

Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, Ir-nahash’s father. 📖

These were Recah’s men.

## 1 Chronicles 4:13

Kenaz’s sons were Othniel and Seraiah. 📖

Othniel’s sons were Hathath and Meonothai.

## 1 Chronicles 4:14

Meonothai fathered Ophrah. 📖

Seraiah fathered Joab, father of those living in Ge-harashim.

It received that name because its people were craftsmen.

**Help with craftsmen:** People skilled in making things.

## 1 Chronicles 4:15

Caleb son of Jephunneh had sons Iru, Elah, and Naam. 📖

Elah’s son was Kenaz.

## 1 Chronicles 4:16

Jehallelel’s sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:17

Ezrah’s sons were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. 📖

Mered’s wife Bithiah bore Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah, Eshtemoa’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 4:18

These were the children of Bithiah, Pharaoh’s daughter. 📖

Mered also married a Judean woman.

She bore Jered, Gedor’s father, Heber, Soco’s father, and Jekuthiel, Zanoah’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 4:19

Hodiah’s wife was Naham’s sister. 📖

Her sons were fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

## 1 Chronicles 4:20

Shimon’s sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. 📖

Ishi’s descendants were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

## 1 Chronicles 4:21

Shelah son of Judah had sons Er, Lecah’s father, and Laadah, Mareshah’s father. 📖

His family included linen workers’ clans at Beth-ashbea,

**Help with linen:** Cloth made from flax plants.

## 1 Chronicles 4:22

Jokim, Cozeba’s men, and Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab and Jashubi-lehem. 📖

These names come from ancient records.

## 1 Chronicles 4:23

These potters lived at Netaim and Gederah, serving the king. 📖

**Help with potters:** People who make pots and other objects from clay.

## 1 Chronicles 4:24

Simeon’s descendants were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:25

Shaul’s sons were Shallum, Mibsam, and Mishma. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:26

Mishma’s sons were Hammuel, Zaccur, and Shimei. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:27

Shimei had 16 sons and 6 daughters. 📖

His brothers had few children, so their whole clan did not grow as numerous as Judah’s descendants.

## 1 Chronicles 4:28

They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:29

Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:30

Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:31

Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim. 📖

These were their cities until David’s reign.

## 1 Chronicles 4:32

Their villages included Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan, 5 towns, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:33

and surrounding villages as far as Baal. 📖

These were their settlements.

They kept a family record:

## 1 Chronicles 4:34

Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:35

Joel, and Jehu son of Joshibiah, grandson of Seraiah, great-grandson of Asiel, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:36

Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:37

and Ziza son of Shiphi. 📖

Shiphi was Allon’s son, Allon was Jedaiah’s son, Jedaiah was Shimri’s son, and Shimri was Shemaiah’s son.

## 1 Chronicles 4:38

These named men led their clans. 📖

Their families grew greatly.

## 1 Chronicles 4:39

They travelled to Gedor’s entrance, on the valley’s east side, seeking pasture for their flocks. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:40

They found rich, good pasture and spacious, peaceful, quiet land. 📖

Hamites had lived there earlier.

## 1 Chronicles 4:41

These named men came during Hezekiah king of Judah’s reign. 📖

They attacked the Hamites in their homes and the Meunites there, devoting them to destruction to this day.

They settled there because of its pasture.

**Help with devoted to destruction:** The phrase describes complete destruction under a religious ban.

“To this day” refers to the account’s time.

## 1 Chronicles 4:42

500 Simeonites went to Mount Seir. 📖

Their leaders were Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, Ishi’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 4:43

They killed the Amalekite survivors who had escaped. 📖

They have lived there to this day.
