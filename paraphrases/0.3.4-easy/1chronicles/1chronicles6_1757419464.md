# 1 Chronicles 6 — Levi’s families and their service

**About this chapter**

These records list Levi’s families, priests, musicians, and assigned towns. | Some family lines continue across verse headings. | Gershon and Gershom are kept where each form appears in the source.

## 1 Chronicles 6:1

Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:2

Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:3

Amram’s children were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. 📖

Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

## 1 Chronicles 6:5

## 1 Chronicles 6:7

**Help with tabernacle:** The sacred tent used for worship.
