# 1 Chronicles 5 — Families east of the Jordan

**About this chapter**

The record describes Reuben, Gad, and half of Manasseh. | It tells of birthright, settlements, battle, prayer, and exile. | Family relationships and numbers follow the BSB record.

## 1 Chronicles 5:1

These were the sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn. 📖

Reuben defiled his father’s bed, so his birthright passed to the sons of Joseph son of Israel.

Reuben is therefore not listed according to birthright.

**Help with these words:** Birthright means the firstborn’s special family rights.

Defiling his father’s bed refers to a sexual wrong involving his father’s partner.

## 1 Chronicles 5:2

Judah became stronger than his brothers, and a ruler came from him. 📖

But Joseph held the birthright.

## 1 Chronicles 5:3

Reuben, Israel’s firstborn, had sons Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:4

Joel’s line continued with his son Shemaiah, Shemaiah’s son Gog, and Gog’s son Shimei, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:5

Shimei’s son Micah, Micah’s son Reaiah, and Reaiah’s son Baal, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:6

and Baal’s son Beerah. 📖

Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria exiled Beerah, a Reubenite leader.

**Help with exiled:** Forced to leave his homeland.

## 1 Chronicles 5:7

His relatives are listed by clan in their family record: chief Jeiel, Zechariah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:8

and Bela son of Azaz, grandson of Shema, great-grandson of Joel. 📖

They lived in Aroer as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.

## 1 Chronicles 5:9

They also settled east to the desert’s edge stretching towards the Euphrates River. 📖

Their livestock in Gilead had grown numerous.

## 1 Chronicles 5:10

During Saul’s reign, they defeated the Hagrites in war. 📖

They occupied the Hagrites’ homes throughout the region east of Gilead.

## 1 Chronicles 5:11

Gad’s descendants lived next to Reuben’s in Bashan as far as Salecah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:12

Joel was chief, Shapham was 2nd, followed by Jaanai and Shaphat in Bashan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:13

Their relatives by family were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber, 7 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:14

They were sons of Abihail son of Huri. 📖

Huri was Jaroah’s son, Jaroah was Gilead’s son, and Gilead was Michael’s son.

Michael was Jeshishai’s son, Jeshishai was Jahdo’s son, and Jahdo was Buz’s son.

## 1 Chronicles 5:15

Ahi son of Abdiel, grandson of Guni, led their family. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:16

They lived in Gilead, Bashan and its towns, and all Sharon’s pastures. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:17

All were recorded in family records during the reigns of Jotham king of Judah and Jeroboam king of Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:18

Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe had 44,760 brave warriors trained for battle. 📖

They used shields, swords, and bows.

## 1 Chronicles 5:19

They fought the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:20

They cried to God during battle and received help against their enemies. 📖

The Hagrites and all their allies were handed over to them.

God answered because they trusted him.

## 1 Chronicles 5:21

They took the Hagrites’ 50,000 camels, 250,000 sheep, and 2,000 donkeys, and 100,000 captives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:22

Many others were killed because the battle belonged to God. 📖

They occupied the land until the exile.

## 1 Chronicles 5:23

Manasseh’s half-tribe was numerous. 📖

They settled from Bashan to Baal-hermon, also called Senir or Mount Hermon.

## 1 Chronicles 5:24

Their family heads were Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel. 📖

They were brave, powerful, famous family leaders.

## 1 Chronicles 5:25

They betrayed their ancestors’ God and prostituted themselves with the land’s peoples’ gods. 📖

God had destroyed those peoples before them.

**Help with prostituted themselves:** This image compares religious unfaithfulness to sexual unfaithfulness.

## 1 Chronicles 5:26

Israel’s God stirred Pul king of Assyria, also called Tiglath-pileser, to exile Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe. 📖

He took them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and Gozan’s river, where they remain to this day.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.
