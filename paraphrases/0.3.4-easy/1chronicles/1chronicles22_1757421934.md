# 1 Chronicles 22 — David prepares Solomon to build

**About this chapter**

David prepares materials and workers for the temple. | He tells Solomon why he will build it and urges obedience. | David also commands Israel’s leaders to help.

## 1 Chronicles 22:1

David said, “Here will be the LORD God’s house and Israel’s burnt-offering altar.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:2

He gathered foreigners living in Israel and appointed stonecutters to prepare cut stones for God’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:3

David provided much iron for gateway-door nails and fittings, and more bronze than could be weighed, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:4

and countless cedar logs. 📖

Sidonians and Tyrians brought him large quantities of cedar.

## 1 Chronicles 22:5

David said, “Solomon is young and inexperienced. 📖

The LORD’s house must be magnificent, famous, and glorious in every land.

I must prepare for it.”

He made extensive preparations before his death.

## 1 Chronicles 22:6

He called Solomon his son and ordered him to build the LORD, Israel’s God, a house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:7

David said, “My son, I wanted to build a house for the LORD my God’s Name. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:8

The LORD told me, ‘You shed much blood and fought great wars. 📖

You must not build for my Name because you shed so much blood on the ground before me.

## 1 Chronicles 22:9

You will have a son who is a man of rest. 📖

I will give him rest from all enemies on every side.

His name will be Solomon.

Israel will have peace and quiet during his reign.

## 1 Chronicles 22:10

He will build a house for my Name. 📖

He will be my son, and I his Father.

I will establish his royal throne over Israel forever.’

## 1 Chronicles 22:11

My son, may the LORD be with you. 📖

May you succeed in building his house as he promised.

## 1 Chronicles 22:12

Above all, may the LORD give you insight and understanding when he puts you over Israel. 📖

Then you can keep the LORD your God’s Law.

## 1 Chronicles 22:13

You will succeed if you carefully keep the laws and rules the LORD commanded Moses for Israel. 📖

Be strong and courageous.

Do not fear or lose heart.

## 1 Chronicles 22:14

I worked hard to provide 100,000 talents of gold for the LORD’s house. 📖

I provided 1,000,000 talents of silver, bronze and iron beyond weighing, timber, and stone.

You may add more.

**Help with talent:** This was a large unit of weight.

## 1 Chronicles 22:15

You have many stonecutters, masons, carpenters, and workers skilled in every kind of work, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:16

in gold, silver, bronze, and iron, craftsmen beyond counting. 📖

Begin, and may the LORD be with you.”

## 1 Chronicles 22:17

David ordered all Israel’s leaders to help Solomon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:18

“Is the LORD your God not with you? 📖

Has he not given rest on every side?

He handed the land’s inhabitants to me.

The land is subdued before the LORD and his people.

## 1 Chronicles 22:19

Seek the LORD your God with heart and soul. 📖

Begin his sanctuary so you can bring his covenant ark and sacred objects into it.

The temple will be built for the LORD’s Name.”

**Help with these words:** A sanctuary is a sacred place.

The covenant ark is the sacred chest associated with the LORD’s agreement with Israel.
