# 1 Chronicles 1 — Early family records

**About this chapter**

These records list people and their families from Adam onwards. | Some lists continue across verse headings. | Names and repeated names are kept as the source gives them.

## 1 Chronicles 1:1

Adam, Seth, Enosh, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:2

Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:3

Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, Noah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:4

Noah’s sons were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:5

Japheth’s sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:6

Gomer’s sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:7

Javan’s sons were Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittites, and the Rodanites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:8

Ham’s sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:9

Cush’s sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabteca. 📖

Raamah’s sons were Sheba and Dedan.

## 1 Chronicles 1:10

Cush fathered Nimrod, who began to be a mighty man on earth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:11

Mizraim was father of the Ludites, Anamites, Lehabites, Naphtuhites, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:12

Pathrusites, Casluhites, and Caphtorites. 📖

The Philistines came from the Casluhites.

## 1 Chronicles 1:13

Canaan was father of his firstborn Sidon and of the Hittites, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:14

Jebusites, Amorites, Girgashites, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:15

Hivites, Arkites, Sinites, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:16

Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:17

Shem’s sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. 📖

Aram’s sons were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.

## 1 Chronicles 1:18

Arphaxad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:19

Eber had 2 sons, Peleg and Joktan. 📖

Peleg received his name because the earth was divided in his time.

Joktan was his brother.

## 1 Chronicles 1:20

Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:21

Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:22

Obal, Abimael, Sheba, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:23

Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. 📖

All these were Joktan’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 1:24

From Shem came Arphaxad, Shelah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:25

Eber, Peleg, Reu, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:26

Serug, Nahor, Terah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:27

and Abram, also called Abraham. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:28

Abraham’s sons were Isaac and Ishmael. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:29

This is their family record. 📖

Ishmael’s firstborn was Nebaioth, followed by Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

## 1 Chronicles 1:30

Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:31

Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. 📖

These were Ishmael’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 1:32

Keturah, Abraham’s concubine, bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. 📖

Jokshan’s sons were Sheba and Dedan.

**Help with concubine:** A woman in a recognised relationship with a man, with lower status than a wife.

## 1 Chronicles 1:33

Midian’s sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. 📖

All these were Keturah’s sons.

**Help with sons here:** The family list includes later descendants.

## 1 Chronicles 1:34

Abraham fathered Isaac. 📖

Isaac’s sons were Esau and Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 1:35

Esau’s sons were Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:36

Eliphaz’s sons were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. 📖

Timna bore him Amalek.

## 1 Chronicles 1:37

Reuel’s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:38

Seir’s sons were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:39

Lotan’s sons were Hori and Homam. 📖

Timna was Lotan’s sister.

## 1 Chronicles 1:40

Shobal’s sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. 📖

Zibeon’s sons were Aiah and Anah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:41

Anah’s son was Dishon. 📖

Dishon’s sons were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

## 1 Chronicles 1:42

Ezer’s sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. 📖

Dishan’s sons were Uz and Aran.

## 1 Chronicles 1:43

These kings ruled Edom before any king ruled Israel. 📖

First was Bela son of Beor, whose city was Dinhabah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:44

Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah became king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:45

Jobab died, and Husham from the Temanites’ land became king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:46

Husham died, and Hadad son of Bedad became king. 📖

He defeated Midian in Moab’s country.

His city was Avith.

## 1 Chronicles 1:47

Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah became king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:48

Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth on the Euphrates became king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:49

Shaul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor became king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:50

Baal-hanan died, and Hadad became king. 📖

His city was Pau.

His wife was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-zahab.

## 1 Chronicles 1:51

Hadad died. 📖

Edom’s chiefs were Timna, Alvah, Jetheth,

## 1 Chronicles 1:52

Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:53

Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:54

Magdiel, and Iram. 📖

These were Edom’s chiefs.
