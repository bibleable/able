# 1 Chronicles 21 — The census and the plague

**About this chapter**

Satan urges David to count Israel. | David admits sin, and a plague follows. | David purchases Ornan’s site and offers sacrifices.

## 1 Chronicles 21:1

Satan rose against Israel and urged David to count the people. 📖

**Help with census:** A count of people; this is what David orders next.

## 1 Chronicles 21:2

David told Joab and the commanders, “Count Israel from Beersheba to Dan. 📖

Report their number to me.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:3

Joab answered, “May the LORD multiply his troops 100 times! 📖

My lord the king, are they not all your servants?

Why do this and bring guilt on Israel?”

## 1 Chronicles 21:4

David overruled Joab. 📖

Joab travelled throughout Israel and returned to Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 21:5

Joab reported 1,100,000 men able to draw swords in all Israel, including 470,000 in Judah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:6

Joab left Levi and Benjamin out because he found the king’s command detestable. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:7

God also saw the command as evil and struck Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:8

David told God, “I sinned greatly in doing this. 📖

Take away your servant’s guilt.

I acted very foolishly.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:9

The LORD instructed Gad, David’s seer, 📖

**Help with seer:** A prophet who receives God’s messages or visions.

## 1 Chronicles 21:10

“Tell David the LORD offers 3 choices. 📖

Choose one, and I will carry it out against you.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:11

Gad told David, “The LORD says to choose: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:12

3 years of famine, 3 months fleeing enemies’ swords, or 3 days of the LORD’s sword. 📖

Those 3 days mean plague and the LORD’s angel devastating all Israel.

Decide what answer I should take to the one who sent me.”

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

## 1 Chronicles 21:13

David told Gad, “I am deeply distressed. 📖

Let me fall into the LORD’s hand, because his mercies are great.

Do not let me fall into human hands.”

**Help with hands here:** This means being under someone’s power.

## 1 Chronicles 21:14

The LORD sent plague on Israel, and 70,000 Israelites died. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:15

God sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem. 📖

While the angel destroyed, the LORD saw and relented.

He said, “Enough!

Withdraw your hand!”

The LORD’s angel stood by Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor.

**Help with threshing floor:** A place where grain is separated from stalks.

## 1 Chronicles 21:16

David saw the LORD’s angel between heaven and earth, holding a drawn sword over Jerusalem. 📖

David and the elders, dressed in sackcloth, fell facedown.

**Help with sackcloth:** Rough cloth expressing grief or humility.

## 1 Chronicles 21:17

David told God, “I ordered the count and sinned wickedly. 📖

What have these sheep done?

LORD my God, strike me and my father’s family, but spare your people this plague.”

**Help with sheep:** David compares the people under his care to a shepherd’s flock.

## 1 Chronicles 21:18

The LORD’s angel told Gad to send David to build the LORD an altar on Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:19

David went as Gad instructed in the LORD’s name. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:20

Ornan was threshing wheat. 📖

He turned and saw the angel.

His 4 sons hid.

## 1 Chronicles 21:21

Ornan saw David approaching. 📖

He left the threshing floor and bowed facedown before him.

## 1 Chronicles 21:22

David told Ornan, “Sell me this site at full price. 📖

I will build the LORD an altar so the people’s plague may stop.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:23

Ornan answered, “Take it and do what you think good, my lord the king. 📖

I give oxen for burnt offerings, threshing sledges for wood, and wheat for the grain offering.

I give everything.”

**Help with threshing sledges:** Equipment dragged over grain to separate it from stalks.

## 1 Chronicles 21:24

David answered, “No, I will pay fully. 📖

I will not take your property for the LORD or offer burnt offerings that cost me nothing.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:25

David paid Ornan 600 gold shekels for the site. 📖

**Help with shekel:** A unit of weight used for valuable metals.

## 1 Chronicles 21:26

He built the LORD an altar and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. 📖

He called to the LORD, who answered with fire from heaven on the burnt-offering altar.

## 1 Chronicles 21:27

The LORD spoke to the angel, who put his sword back in its sheath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:28

David saw the LORD had answered at Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor and sacrificed there. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:29

The LORD’s tabernacle Moses made in the wilderness and the burnt-offering altar were at Gibeon’s high place. 📖

**Help with tabernacle:** The sacred tent used for worship.

## 1 Chronicles 21:30

David could not go there to ask God, because he feared the LORD’s angel’s sword. 📖
