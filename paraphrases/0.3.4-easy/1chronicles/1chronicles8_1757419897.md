# 1 Chronicles 8 — Benjamin’s families and Saul’s line

**About this chapter**

These records continue Benjamin’s families and describe Saul’s descendants. | Some lists continue across verse headings. | Similar names remain distinct where the source gives different spellings.

## 1 Chronicles 8:1

Benjamin fathered Bela his firstborn, Ashbel his 2nd, and Aharah his 3rd, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:2

Nohah his 4th and Rapha his 5th. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:3

Bela’s sons were Addar, Gera, Abihud, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:4

Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:5

Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:6

These descendants of Ehud led families living in Geba who were exiled to Manahath: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:7

Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera. 📖

Gera carried them into exile and fathered Uzza and Ahihud.

**Help with exile:** Being forced to leave one’s homeland.

## 1 Chronicles 8:8

Shaharaim had sons in Moab after divorcing his wives Hushim and Baara. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:9

His wife Hodesh bore Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:10

Jeuz, Sachia, and Mirmah. 📖

These sons were family heads.

## 1 Chronicles 8:11

His sons by Hushim were Abitub and Elpaal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:12

Elpaal’s sons included Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod with its villages, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:13

and Beriah and Shema. 📖

They led families in Aijalon and drove out Gath’s inhabitants.

## 1 Chronicles 8:14

Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:15

Zebadiah, Arad, Eder, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:16

Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were Beriah’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:17

Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:18

Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were Elpaal’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:19

Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:20

Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:21

Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were Shimei’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:22

Ishpan, Eber, Eliel, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:23

Abdon, Zichri, Hanan, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:24

Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:25

Iphdeiah, and Penuel were Shashak’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:26

Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:27

Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were Jeroham’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:28

All these were family heads and chiefs listed in their family records. 📖

They lived in Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 8:29

Jeiel, Gibeon’s father, lived in Gibeon. 📖

His wife was Maacah.

## 1 Chronicles 8:30

His firstborn was Abdon, followed by Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:31

Gedor, Ahio, Zecher, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:32

and Mikloth, Shimeah’s father. 📖

They also lived beside their relatives in Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 8:33

Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul. 📖

Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal.

## 1 Chronicles 8:34

Jonathan’s son was Merib-baal, who fathered Micah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:35

Micah’s sons were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:36

Ahaz fathered Jehoaddah. 📖

Jehoaddah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri.

Zimri fathered Moza.

## 1 Chronicles 8:37

Moza fathered Binea. 📖

Binea’s son was Raphah, Raphah’s son Eleasah, and Eleasah’s son Azel.

## 1 Chronicles 8:38

Azel had 6 sons: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. 📖

All were Azel’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 8:39

His brother Eshek’s sons were Ulam his firstborn, Jeush his 2nd, and Eliphelet his 3rd. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:40

Ulam’s sons were brave warriors and archers. 📖

They had many sons and grandsons, 150 altogether.

All these were Benjamin’s descendants.
