# 1 Chronicles 3 — David’s sons and the royal line

**About this chapter**

The record lists David’s sons and the royal line after Solomon. | It continues through the exile and later descendants. | Each verse keeps the source’s names and relationships.

## 1 Chronicles 3:1

David’s sons born in Hebron began with Amnon by Ahinoam of Jezreel. 📖

His 2nd was Daniel by Abigail of Carmel.

## 1 Chronicles 3:2

His 3rd was Absalom by Maacah, daughter of King Talmai of Geshur. 📖

His 4th was Adonijah by Haggith.

## 1 Chronicles 3:3

His 5th was Shephatiah by Abital, and his 6th Ithream by his wife Eglah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:4

These 6 were born in Hebron, where David ruled 7 years and 6 months. 📖

He ruled in Jerusalem for 33 years.

## 1 Chronicles 3:5

His Jerusalem-born sons included Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. 📖

Bathsheba daughter of Ammiel bore these 4.

## 1 Chronicles 3:6

David’s other sons were Ibhar, Elishua, Eliphelet, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:7

Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:8

Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet, 9 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:9

These were David’s sons, besides those born to his concubines. 📖

Tamar was their sister.

**Help with concubines:** Women in recognised relationships with a man, with lower status than wives.

## 1 Chronicles 3:10

Solomon’s son was Rehoboam. 📖

Rehoboam’s son was Abijah, Abijah’s son Asa, and Asa’s son Jehoshaphat.

## 1 Chronicles 3:11

Jehoshaphat’s son was Joram, Joram’s son Ahaziah, and Ahaziah’s son Joash. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:12

Joash’s son was Amaziah, Amaziah’s son Azariah, and Azariah’s son Jotham. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:13

Jotham’s son was Ahaz, Ahaz’s son Hezekiah, and Hezekiah’s son Manasseh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:14

Manasseh’s son was Amon, and Amon’s son Josiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:15

Josiah’s sons were Johanan the firstborn, Jehoiakim the 2nd, Zedekiah the 3rd, and Shallum the 4th. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:16

Jehoiakim’s successors were his son Jeconiah and Zedekiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:17

The descendants of Jeconiah the captive included his son Shealtiel, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:18

Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:19

Pedaiah’s sons were Zerubbabel and Shimei. 📖

Zerubbabel’s children were Meshullam, Hananiah, and their sister Shelomith,

## 1 Chronicles 3:20

and 5 others: Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:21

Hananiah’s descendants were Pelatiah, Jeshaiah, and the sons of Rephaiah, Arnan, Obadiah, and Shecaniah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:22

Shecaniah’s 6 descendants were Shemaiah and Shemaiah’s sons Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:23

Neariah’s sons were Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam, 3 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:24

Elioenai’s sons were Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani, 7 altogether. 📖
