# 1 Chronicles 9 — Returned families and temple duties

**About this chapter**

The record describes Jerusalem’s residents and Levites’ duties after exile. | It then repeats Saul’s family line. | Repeated records and differences of spelling are preserved.

## 1 Chronicles 9:1

All Israel was listed in family records in the Book of the Kings of Israel. 📖

Judah was exiled to Babylon because of unfaithfulness.

**Help with exiled:** Forced to leave their homeland.

## 1 Chronicles 9:2

Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants first resettled their own property in their cities. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:3

Some descendants of Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh lived in Jerusalem: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:4

Uthai son of Ammihud, grandson of Omri. 📖

Omri was Imri’s son, and Imri was Bani’s son.

Uthai descended from Perez son of Judah.

## 1 Chronicles 9:5

From the Shilonites came Asaiah the firstborn and his sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:6

From the Zerahites came Jeuel and 690 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:7

From Benjamin came Sallu son of Meshullam, grandson of Hodaviah, great-grandson of Hassenuah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:8

Ibneiah son of Jeroham, Elah son of Uzzi, grandson of Michri, and Meshullam son of Shephatiah. 📖

Shephatiah was Reuel’s son, and Reuel was Ibnijah’s son.

## 1 Chronicles 9:9

Their records listed 956 relatives. 📖

All these men were family heads.

## 1 Chronicles 9:10

The priests included Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:11

and Azariah son of Hilkiah, grandson of Meshullam. 📖

Meshullam was Zadok’s son, Zadok was Meraioth’s son, and Meraioth was Ahitub’s son.

Azariah was God’s temple’s chief official.

## 1 Chronicles 9:12

They included Adaiah son of Jeroham, grandson of Pashhur, great-grandson of Malchijah, and Maasai son of Adiel. 📖

Adiel was Jahzerah’s son, Jahzerah was Meshullam’s son, Meshullam was Meshillemith’s son, and Meshillemith was Immer’s son.

## 1 Chronicles 9:13

Also included were 1,760 relatives, family heads able to serve in God’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:14

The Levites included Shemaiah son of Hasshub, grandson of Azrikam, great-grandson of Hashabiah, a descendant of Merari, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:15

Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, grandson of Zichri, great-grandson of Asaph, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:16

and Obadiah son of Shemaiah, grandson of Galal, great-grandson of Jeduthun. 📖

Berechiah son of Asa, grandson of Elkanah, lived in the Netophathites’ villages.

## 1 Chronicles 9:17

The gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their relatives. 📖

Shallum was chief.

## 1 Chronicles 9:18

Shallum had previously served at the eastern King’s Gate. 📖

These gatekeepers belonged to the Levites’ camp.

## 1 Chronicles 9:19

Shallum son of Kore, grandson of Ebiasaph, great-grandson of Korah, and his Korahite relatives guarded the Tent’s entrances. 📖

Their ancestors had guarded the entrance to the LORD’s dwelling.

## 1 Chronicles 9:20

Earlier, Phinehas son of Eleazar had led the gatekeepers. 📖

The LORD was with him.

## 1 Chronicles 9:21

Zechariah son of Meshelemiah guarded the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:22

There were 212 chosen gatekeepers, registered by family in their villages. 📖

David and Samuel the seer appointed them to these trusted posts.

**Help with seer:** A prophet who receives God’s messages or visions.

## 1 Chronicles 9:23

They and their descendants guarded the LORD’s house’s gates, the house called the Tent. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:24

Gatekeepers served on 4 sides: east, west, north, and south. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:25

Their relatives came from villages at fixed times to serve 7-day periods with them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:26

The 4 chief gatekeepers were Levites trusted with God’s house’s rooms and treasuries. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:27

They stayed around God’s house at night, responsible for guarding and opening it each morning. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:28

Some managed worship objects and counted them when brought in or taken out. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:29

Others managed sanctuary furnishings and objects, fine flour, wine, oil, frankincense, and spices. 📖

**Help with sanctuary and frankincense:** A sanctuary is a sacred place.

Frankincense is a fragrant substance burned in worship.

## 1 Chronicles 9:30

Some priests’ sons mixed the spices. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:31

Mattithiah, a Levite and Shallum the Korahite’s firstborn, was trusted to bake bread. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:32

Some Kohathite relatives prepared the rows of showbread every Sabbath. 📖

**Help with showbread and Sabbath:** This bread was set before the LORD.

Sabbath was the weekly rest day.

## 1 Chronicles 9:33

Musicians who headed Levite families lived in temple rooms. 📖

They had no other duties because their music duties continued day and night.

## 1 Chronicles 9:34

All these were Levite family heads and chiefs in their records. 📖

They lived in Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 9:35

Jeiel, Gibeon’s father, lived in Gibeon. 📖

His wife was Maacah.

## 1 Chronicles 9:36

His firstborn was Abdon, followed by Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:37

Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:38

Mikloth fathered Shimeam. 📖

They also lived beside their relatives in Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 9:39

Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul. 📖

Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal.

## 1 Chronicles 9:40

Jonathan’s son Merib-baal fathered Micah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:41

Micah’s sons were Pithon, Melech, Tahrea, and Ahaz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:42

Ahaz fathered Jarah. 📖

Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri.

Zimri fathered Moza.

## 1 Chronicles 9:43

Moza fathered Binea. 📖

Binea’s son was Rephaiah, Rephaiah’s son Elasah, and Elasah’s son Azel.

## 1 Chronicles 9:44

Azel’s 6 sons were Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. 📖

These were Azel’s sons.
