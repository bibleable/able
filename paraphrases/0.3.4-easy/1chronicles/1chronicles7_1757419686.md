# 1 Chronicles 7 — Records of several tribes

**About this chapter**

These lists name families of Issachar, Benjamin, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim, and Asher. | They include military totals, settlements, grief, and family growth. | Names and relationships follow this source’s wording.

## 1 Chronicles 7:1

Issachar’s sons were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron, 4 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:2

Tola’s sons were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Shemuel, family leaders. 📖

In David’s time, their family records listed 22,600 brave warriors descended from Tola.

## 1 Chronicles 7:3

Uzzi’s son was Izrahiah. 📖

Izrahiah’s sons were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah.

All 5 were chiefs.

## 1 Chronicles 7:4

Their family record listed another 36,000 troops for battle. 📖

They had many wives and children.

## 1 Chronicles 7:5

All Issachar’s families’ brave warriors numbered 87,000 in their family records. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:6

Benjamin’s 3 sons were Bela, Becher, and Jediael. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:7

Bela’s sons were Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri, 5 family heads. 📖

Their records listed 22,034 brave warriors.

## 1 Chronicles 7:8

Becher’s sons were Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. 📖

All were Becher’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 7:9

Their family records, listed by family heads, included 20,200 brave warriors. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:10

Jediael’s son was Bilhan. 📖

Bilhan’s sons were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar.

## 1 Chronicles 7:11

These descendants of Jediael were family heads and brave warriors. 📖

They had 17,200 men ready for battle.

## 1 Chronicles 7:12

The Shuppites and Huppites descended from Ir. 📖

The Hushites descended from Aher.

## 1 Chronicles 7:13

Naphtali’s sons were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum, descendants of Bilhah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:14

Manasseh’s descendants included Asriel by his Aramean concubine. 📖

She also bore Machir, Gilead’s father.

**Help with concubine:** A woman in a recognised relationship with a man, with lower status than a wife.

## 1 Chronicles 7:15

Machir married a woman from the Huppites and Shuppites. 📖

His sister was Maacah.

Another descendant, Zelophehad, had only daughters.

## 1 Chronicles 7:16

Machir’s wife Maacah bore a son and named him Peresh. 📖

His brother was Sheresh, whose sons were Ulam and Rekem.

## 1 Chronicles 7:17

Ulam’s son was Bedan. 📖

These were descendants of Gilead son of Machir, grandson of Manasseh.

## 1 Chronicles 7:18

His sister Hammolecheth bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:19

Shemida’s sons were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:20

Ephraim’s line continued through Shuthelah, Shuthelah’s son Bered, Bered’s son Tahath, Tahath’s son Eleadah, and Eleadah’s son Tahath, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:21

Tahath’s son Zabad and Zabad’s son Shuthelah. 📖

Ezer and Elead went to steal livestock, and Gath’s inhabitants killed them.

## 1 Chronicles 7:22

Their father Ephraim mourned for many days. 📖

His relatives came to comfort him.

## 1 Chronicles 7:23

He had sex with his wife again. 📖

She became pregnant and bore a son.

He named him Beriah because tragedy had come on his household.

**Help with Beriah:** The account links the name with this family’s misfortune.

## 1 Chronicles 7:24

His daughter Sheerah built Lower Beth-horon, Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:25

His line also included Rephah, Rephah’s son Resheph, Resheph’s son Telah, and Telah’s son Tahan, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:26

Tahan’s son Ladan, Ladan’s son Ammihud, and Ammihud’s son Elishama, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:27

Elishama’s son Nun and Nun’s son Joshua. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:28

Their property and settlements included Bethel and its villages, Naaran eastwards, and Gezer and its villages westwards. 📖

They included Shechem and its villages as far as Ayyah and its villages.

## 1 Chronicles 7:29

Along Manasseh’s borders were Beth-shean, Taanach, Megiddo, and Dor with their villages. 📖

Joseph son of Israel’s descendants lived in these towns.

## 1 Chronicles 7:30

Asher’s children were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:31

Beriah’s sons were Heber and Malchiel, Birzaith’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:32

Heber fathered Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and their sister Shua. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:33

Japhlet’s sons were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. 📖

These were Japhlet’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 7:34

Shemer’s sons were Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:35

His brother Helem’s sons were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:36

Zophah’s sons were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:37

Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:38

Jether’s sons were Jephunneh, Pispa, and Ara. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:39

Ulla’s sons were Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:40

All these were Asher’s descendants, family heads, chosen brave warriors, and chiefs among leaders. 📖

Their records listed 26,000 men ready for battle.
