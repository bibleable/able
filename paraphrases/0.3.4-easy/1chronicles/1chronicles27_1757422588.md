# 1 Chronicles 27 — Military divisions and royal officials

**About this chapter**

Military divisions serve in monthly turns. | The record names commanders, tribal leaders, and officials managing royal property. | It also recalls the unfinished census.

## 1 Chronicles 27:1

These Israelites served the king in matters concerning military divisions on monthly duty throughout the year. 📖

They included family heads, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and officers.

Each division had 24,000 men.

## 1 Chronicles 27:2

Jashobeam son of Zabdiel led the 1st division in month 1. 📖

It had 24,000 men.

## 1 Chronicles 27:3

He descended from Perez and was chief of all army commanders for month 1. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:4

Dodai the Ahohite had charge in month 2, with Mikloth as leader. 📖

His division had 24,000 men.

## 1 Chronicles 27:5

Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest commanded month 3’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:6

This Benaiah was a mighty man among the Thirty and led them. 📖

His son Ammizabad managed his division.

## 1 Chronicles 27:7

Asahel, Joab’s brother, commanded month 4, followed by his son Zebadiah. 📖

His division had 24,000 men.

## 1 Chronicles 27:8

Shamhuth the Izrahite commanded month 5’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:9

Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite commanded month 6’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:10

Helez the Pelonite, an Ephraimite, commanded month 7’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:11

Sibbecai the Hushathite, a Zerahite, commanded month 8’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:12

Abiezer the Anathothite, a Benjamite, commanded month 9’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:13

Maharai the Netophathite, a Zerahite, commanded month 10’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:14

Benaiah the Pirathonite, an Ephraimite, commanded month 11’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:15

Heldai the Netophathite, from Othniel’s family, commanded month 12’s division of 24,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:16

These officials led Israel’s tribes. 📖

Eliezer son of Zichri led Reuben, and Shephatiah son of Maacah led Simeon.

## 1 Chronicles 27:17

Hashabiah son of Kemuel led Levi, and Zadok led Aaron’s family. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:18

Elihu, David’s brother, led Judah, and Omri son of Michael led Issachar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:19

Ishmaiah son of Obadiah led Zebulun, and Jerimoth son of Azriel led Naphtali. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:20

Hoshea son of Azaziah led Ephraim, and Joel son of Pedaiah led one half-tribe of Manasseh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:21

Iddo son of Zechariah led Manasseh’s half-tribe in Gilead, and Jaasiel son of Abner led Benjamin. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:22

Azarel son of Jeroham led Dan. 📖

These were Israel’s tribal leaders.

## 1 Chronicles 27:23

David did not count men aged 20 or younger. 📖

The LORD promised to make Israel as numerous as heaven’s stars.

## 1 Chronicles 27:24

Joab son of Zeruiah began the count but did not finish. 📖

Wrath came on Israel because of this census.

The number was not entered in the Book of the Chronicles of King David.

## 1 Chronicles 27:25

Azmaveth son of Adiel managed royal storehouses. 📖

Jonathan son of Uzziah managed storehouses in the countryside, cities, villages, and fortresses.

## 1 Chronicles 27:26

Ezri son of Chelub managed field workers who worked the soil. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:27

Shimei the Ramathite managed vineyards. 📖

Zabdi the Shiphmite managed vineyard produce for the wine vats.

## 1 Chronicles 27:28

Baal-hanan the Gederite managed olive and sycamore trees in the foothills. 📖

Joash managed olive-oil stores.

## 1 Chronicles 27:29

Shitrai the Sharonite managed herds grazing in Sharon. 📖

Shaphat son of Adlai managed herds in the valleys.

## 1 Chronicles 27:30

Obil the Ishmaelite managed camels. 📖

Jehdeiah the Meronothite managed donkeys.

## 1 Chronicles 27:31

Jaziz the Hagrite managed flocks. 📖

All these officials managed King David’s property.

## 1 Chronicles 27:32

David’s uncle Jonathan was an insightful adviser and scribe. 📖

Jehiel son of Hachmoni attended the king’s sons.

**Help with scribe:** An official who writes documents.

## 1 Chronicles 27:33

Ahithophel advised the king. 📖

Hushai the Archite was the king’s friend.

## 1 Chronicles 27:34

Jehoiada son of Benaiah succeeded Ahithophel, followed by Abiathar. 📖

Joab commanded the king’s army.
