# 1 Chronicles 16 — A song of thanksgiving

**About this chapter**

The ark is placed in its tent. | David appoints worship leaders and gives a song of thanks. | The song remembers the covenant and calls the nations to praise the LORD.

## 1 Chronicles 16:1

They placed God’s ark in David’s tent and presented burnt offerings and peace offerings before God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:2

After the offerings, David blessed the people in the LORD’s name. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:3

He gave every Israelite man and woman a bread loaf, date cake, and raisin cake. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:4

He appointed Levites to serve before the LORD’s ark, celebrating, thanking, and praising the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:5

Asaph was chief and Zechariah 2nd, followed by Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. 📖

They played harps and lyres while Asaph played cymbals.

## 1 Chronicles 16:6

Priests Benaiah and Jahaziel regularly blew trumpets before God’s covenant ark. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:7

That day David first gave Asaph and his companions this song of thanksgiving to the LORD: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:8

“Thank the LORD and call on his name. 📖

Tell the nations his deeds.

## 1 Chronicles 16:9

Sing to him and praise him. 📖

Tell all his wonders.

## 1 Chronicles 16:10

Glory in his holy name. 📖

Let those seeking the LORD rejoice in their hearts.

**Help with glory in:** Rejoice in and honour.

## 1 Chronicles 16:11

Seek the LORD and his strength. 📖

Always seek his face.

**Help with seek his face:** Seek his presence and help.

## 1 Chronicles 16:12

Remember his wonders, marvellous deeds, and spoken judgments, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:13

you descendants of Israel his servant, you sons of Jacob, his chosen ones. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:14

He is the LORD our God. 📖

His judgments extend throughout the earth.

## 1 Chronicles 16:15

Remember his covenant forever, the word he commanded for 1,000 generations, 📖

**Help with covenant:** A binding agreement or promise.

## 1 Chronicles 16:16

the covenant with Abraham and the oath sworn to Isaac. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:17

He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree, to Israel as an everlasting covenant. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:18

‘I will give you Canaan’s land as your share of the inheritance.’ 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:19

They were few, very few, and foreigners in the land. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:20

They wandered between nations and kingdoms. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:21

He let nobody oppress them. 📖

He rebuked kings for their sake.

## 1 Chronicles 16:22

‘Do not touch my anointed ones! 📖

Do not harm my prophets!’

**Help with these words:** Anointed ones were chosen for God’s service.

Prophets bring God’s messages.

## 1 Chronicles 16:23

All the earth, sing to the LORD. 📖

Announce his salvation day after day.

**Help with salvation:** God’s rescue.

## 1 Chronicles 16:24

Tell the nations his glory and all peoples his wonders. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:25

The LORD is great and deserves great praise. 📖

Fear him above all gods.

## 1 Chronicles 16:26

The nations’ gods are idols, but the LORD made heaven. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:27

Splendour and majesty are before him. 📖

Strength and joy fill his dwelling.

**Help with splendour and majesty:** Great beauty and royal greatness.

## 1 Chronicles 16:28

Families of the nations, acknowledge the LORD’s glory and strength. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:29

Give the LORD the glory his name deserves. 📖

Bring an offering and come before him.

Worship the LORD in the splendour of his holiness.

**Help with holiness:** God’s purity and sacred greatness.

## 1 Chronicles 16:30

All the earth, tremble before him. 📖

The world is firmly established and cannot be moved.

## 1 Chronicles 16:31

Let heaven be glad and earth rejoice. 📖

Let the nations hear, ‘The LORD reigns!’

## 1 Chronicles 16:32

Let the sea and everything in it roar. 📖

Let the fields and everything in them rejoice.

## 1 Chronicles 16:33

Let forest trees sing joyfully before the LORD. 📖

He comes to judge the earth.

**Help with singing trees:** The song pictures creation joining in praise.

## 1 Chronicles 16:34

Thank the LORD, because he is good. 📖

His faithful love lasts forever.

## 1 Chronicles 16:35

Cry, ‘Save us, God of our salvation! 📖

Gather and rescue us from the nations so we may thank your holy name and glory in your praise.’

## 1 Chronicles 16:36

Bless the LORD, Israel’s God, from everlasting to everlasting.” 📖

Everyone said, “Amen!” and “Praise the LORD!”

**Help with Amen:** An expression of agreement, meaning “let it be so”.

## 1 Chronicles 16:37

David left Asaph and his companions before the LORD’s covenant ark to serve regularly according to each day’s requirements, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:38

with Obed-edom and his 68 relatives. 📖

Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah served as gatekeepers.

## 1 Chronicles 16:39

David left Zadok and his fellow priests before the LORD’s tabernacle at Gibeon’s high place, 📖

**Help with tabernacle:** The sacred tent used for worship.

## 1 Chronicles 16:40

regularly offering burnt offerings morning and evening on the burnt-offering altar. 📖

They followed all the LORD’s Law written for Israel to obey.

## 1 Chronicles 16:41

Heman, Jeduthun, and the other chosen, named men were with them to thank the LORD. 📖

“His faithful love lasts forever.”

## 1 Chronicles 16:42

Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets, cymbals, and instruments for songs to God. 📖

Jeduthun’s sons served at the gate.

## 1 Chronicles 16:43

Everyone went home. 📖

David returned to bless his household.
