# 1 Chronicles 26 — Gatekeepers, treasuries, and other officials

**About this chapter**

The record lists gatekeeping families and their assignments. | It then describes those managing treasures and other duties for God and the king.

## 1 Chronicles 26:1

The gatekeepers’ divisions included the Korahite Meshelemiah son of Kore, one of Asaph’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:2

Meshelemiah’s sons were Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the 2nd, Zebadiah the 3rd, and Jathniel the 4th, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:3

Elam the 5th, Jehohanan the 6th, and Eliehoenai the 7th. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:4

Obed-edom’s sons were Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the 2nd, Joah the 3rd, Sachar the 4th, and Nethanel the 5th, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:5

Ammiel the 6th, Issachar the 7th, and Peullethai the 8th. 📖

God blessed Obed-edom.

## 1 Chronicles 26:6

His son Shemaiah had sons who led their families because they were strong and capable. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:7

Shemaiah’s sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. 📖

His brothers Elihu and Semachiah were also capable.

## 1 Chronicles 26:8

Obed-edom’s descendants, sons, and brothers were capable and strong for the work, 62 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:9

Meshelemiah’s capable sons and brothers numbered 18. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:10

Hosah the Merarite’s leading son was Shimri. 📖

He was not firstborn, but his father appointed him first.

## 1 Chronicles 26:11

Hilkiah was 2nd, Tebaliah 3rd, and Zechariah 4th. 📖

Hosah’s sons and brothers numbered 13.

## 1 Chronicles 26:12

These gatekeeping divisions served the LORD’s house under their chiefs, just as their relatives did. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:13

They cast lots by family for each gate, young and old alike. 📖

**Help with lots:** A way of making an allocation by drawing marked objects.

## 1 Chronicles 26:14

The East Gate’s lot fell to Shelemiah. 📖

His son Zechariah, a wise adviser, received the North Gate’s lot.

## 1 Chronicles 26:15

Obed-edom received the South Gate’s lot, and his sons received the storehouses. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:16

Shuppim and Hosah received lots for the West Gate and Shallecheth Gate on the uphill road. 📖

Guards served at every watch.

## 1 Chronicles 26:17

Each day, 6 Levites served east, 4 north, 4 south, and 2 pairs at the storehouse. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:18

At the western court, 4 served by the road and 2 at the court. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:19

These gatekeepers’ divisions descended from Korah and Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:20

Their fellow Levites managed God’s house’s treasuries and stores of dedicated things. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:21

Ladan’s descendants were Gershonites through Ladan. 📖

Their family heads included Jehieli,

## 1 Chronicles 26:22

and Jehieli’s sons, Zetham and his brother Joel. 📖

They managed the LORD’s house’s treasuries.

## 1 Chronicles 26:23

From the Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:24

Shebuel, a descendant of Gershom son of Moses, managed the treasuries. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:25

His relatives through Eliezer included Eliezer’s son Rehabiah, Rehabiah’s son Jeshaiah, and Jeshaiah’s son Joram. 📖

Joram’s son was Zichri, and Zichri’s son Shelomith.

## 1 Chronicles 26:26

Shelomith and his brothers managed treasures dedicated by David, family heads commanding thousands and hundreds, and army commanders. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:27

They dedicated battle plunder to repair the LORD’s house. 📖

**Help with plunder:** Goods taken in war.

## 1 Chronicles 26:28

Shelomith and his brothers cared for all dedicated things. 📖

These included gifts from Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah.

## 1 Chronicles 26:29

Chenaniah and his sons, Izharites, handled outside duties as officers and judges over Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:30

The Hebronite Hashabiah and his 1,700 capable relatives managed Israel’s affairs west of the Jordan. 📖

They handled all the LORD’s work and royal service.

## 1 Chronicles 26:31

Jerijah led the Hebronites according to ancestral records. 📖

In David’s 40th year, records were searched.

Strong, capable Hebronites were found at Jazer in Gilead.

## 1 Chronicles 26:32

Jerijah’s relatives included 2,700 capable family heads. 📖

David placed them over Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe for all God’s and the king’s affairs.
