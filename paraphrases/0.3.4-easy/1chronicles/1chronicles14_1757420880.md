# 1 Chronicles 14 — David’s family and victories

**About this chapter**

Hiram helps David build a palace. | David’s family grows. | David seeks God’s direction in two battles against the Philistines.

## 1 Chronicles 14:1

Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers, cedar logs, stonemasons, and carpenters to build David a palace. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:2

David knew the LORD established him as Israel’s king and greatly raised his kingdom for his people Israel’s sake. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:3

David married more wives in Jerusalem and had more sons and daughters. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:4

His Jerusalem-born children were Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:5

Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:6

Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:7

Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:8

All the Philistines heard David was anointed king over all Israel and came seeking him. 📖

David heard and went out to face them.

## 1 Chronicles 14:9

The Philistines raided Rephaim Valley. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:10

David asked God, “Shall I attack the Philistines? 📖

Will you hand them over to me?”

The LORD answered, “Go.

I will hand them over.”

## 1 Chronicles 14:11

David and his men defeated them at Baal-perazim. 📖

He said, “God burst through my enemies by my hand like a bursting flood.”

They named the place Baal-perazim.

**Help with Baal-perazim:** The name recalls breaking through.

## 1 Chronicles 14:12

The Philistines abandoned their gods there. 📖

David ordered them burned.

## 1 Chronicles 14:13

The Philistines raided the valley again. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:14

David asked God again. 📖

God said, “Do not follow straight after them.

Circle around and attack in front of the balsam trees.

## 1 Chronicles 14:15

When you hear marching in the balsam treetops, begin the battle. 📖

God has gone ahead to strike the Philistine camp.”

## 1 Chronicles 14:16

David obeyed God. 📖

They defeated the Philistine army from Gibeon to Gezer.

## 1 Chronicles 14:17

David became famous in every land. 📖

The LORD made all nations fear him.
