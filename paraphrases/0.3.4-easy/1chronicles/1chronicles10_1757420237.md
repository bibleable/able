# 1 Chronicles 10 — Saul’s death

**About this chapter**

Israel is defeated by the Philistines. | Saul and his sons die, and Jabesh-gilead’s men recover their bodies. | The account explains Saul’s death in relation to his unfaithfulness.

## 1 Chronicles 10:1

The Philistines fought Israel. 📖

Israel’s men fled, and many died on Mount Gilboa.

## 1 Chronicles 10:2

The Philistines closely pursued Saul and his sons. 📖

They killed his sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.

## 1 Chronicles 10:3

The battle grew fierce around Saul. 📖

Archers caught up with him and wounded him.

## 1 Chronicles 10:4

Saul told his armour-bearer, “Draw your sword and stab me, or these uncircumcised men will torture me!” 📖

His armour-bearer was terrified and refused.

Saul took his own sword and fell on it.

**Help with these words:** An armour-bearer carried a warrior’s equipment.

Saul calls the Philistines uncircumcised, marking them as outside Israel’s covenant people.

## 1 Chronicles 10:5

His armour-bearer saw Saul was dead. 📖

He also fell on his sword and died.

## 1 Chronicles 10:6

Saul died with his 3 sons and all his house. 📖

**Help with house:** Here this refers to his household.

## 1 Chronicles 10:7

Israelites in the valley saw the army had fled and Saul and his sons were dead. 📖

They abandoned their cities and fled.

The Philistines occupied them.

## 1 Chronicles 10:8

Next day, the Philistines stripped the dead and found Saul and his sons on Mount Gilboa. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:9

They stripped Saul, cut off his head, and took his armour. 📖

They sent messengers throughout Philistine territory to announce this among their people and in their idols’ temple.

## 1 Chronicles 10:10

They put his armour in their gods’ temple and hung his head in Dagon’s temple. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:11

All Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:12

All their brave men brought Saul’s and his sons’ bodies to Jabesh. 📖

They buried the bones under Jabesh’s oak and fasted 7 days.

**Help with fasted:** Went without food.

## 1 Chronicles 10:13

Saul died because he betrayed the LORD and did not keep his word. 📖

He even sought guidance from a medium.

**Help with medium:** Someone claiming contact with spirits of the dead.

## 1 Chronicles 10:14

He failed to ask the LORD. 📖

The LORD put him to death and gave the kingdom to David son of Jesse.
