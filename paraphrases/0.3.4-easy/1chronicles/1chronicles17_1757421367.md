# 1 Chronicles 17 — God’s promise to David

**About this chapter**

David wants to build a house for God.

God instead promises David a lasting royal family.

**Help with a name:** LORD is a way the Bible writes God's name.

## 1 Chronicles 17:1

David settled in his palace. 📖

He told Nathan the prophet, “I live in a cedar house.

The LORD's covenant chest is under a tent.”

**Help with words:** The covenant chest was a sacred chest connected with God's agreement with Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 17:2

Nathan said, “Do what is in your heart. 📖

God is with you.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:3

That night, God gave Nathan a message. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:4

“Tell my servant David what the LORD says. 📖

You are not the one to build me a house to live in.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:5

“Since bringing Israel out of Egypt, I have not lived in a house. 📖

I have moved from tent to tent and dwelling to dwelling.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:6

“I appointed leaders to care for Israel like shepherds. 📖

In all my journeys with Israel, did I ask them for a cedar house?”

## 1 Chronicles 17:7

“Tell my servant David what the LORD of Hosts says. 📖

I took you from watching sheep in the fields.

I made you ruler of my people Israel.”

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title means the LORD who rules heavenly armies.

## 1 Chronicles 17:8

“I have been with you everywhere you went and cut off all your enemies before you. 📖

I will make your name like the greatest names in the land.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:9

“I will give Israel a place of its own. 📖

I will plant my people there so no one troubles them.

Evil people will no longer hurt them as before.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:10

“They oppressed Israel from the time I appointed judges over my people. 📖

I will subdue all your enemies.

I declare that the LORD will build you a house.”

**Help with house:** Here this means a continuing royal family.

## 1 Chronicles 17:11

“When your life ends and you join your ancestors, I will raise up one of your sons. 📖

I will make his kingdom strong.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:12

“He will build a house for me. 📖

I will make his throne last forever.”

**Help with this picture:** Throne pictures his royal rule.

## 1 Chronicles 17:13

“I will be his Father, and he will be my son. 📖

I will never remove my faithful love from him as I did from the king before you.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:14

“I will set him over my house and kingdom forever. 📖

His throne will stand forever.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:15

Nathan told David the whole message God had revealed. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:16

King David sat before the LORD. 📖

He said, “LORD God, who am I and my family that you brought me this far?”

## 1 Chronicles 17:17

“As if this were a small thing, you spoke about my family's future. 📖

LORD God, you treated me as someone of great importance.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:18

“What more can I, David, say about the honour you give me? 📖

You know your servant.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:19

“LORD, you have done this great thing for your servant, as you wished. 📖

You have made these great promises known.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:20

“LORD, no one is like you. 📖

Everything we have heard shows there is no God except you.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:21

“Who is like your people Israel, the one nation on earth God redeemed as his own? 📖

You made your name known through great, awesome wonders.

You drove out nations before your people whom you redeemed from Egypt.”

**Help with redeemed:** Rescued and claimed as one’s own.

## 1 Chronicles 17:22

“You made Israel your own people forever. 📖

You, LORD, became their God.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:23

“LORD, make your promise to your servant and his family stand forever. 📖

Do what you promised.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:24

“May your name stand firm and be greatly honoured forever. 📖

People will say the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, is God over Israel.

May your servant David’s family stand firm before you.”

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** The LORD who rules heavenly armies.

## 1 Chronicles 17:25

“My God, you revealed that you would build a house for me. 📖

That gave me courage to pray.”

**Help with this picture:** House here means a continuing royal family.

## 1 Chronicles 17:26

“LORD, you are God. 📖

You promised this good thing to your servant.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:27

“You chose to bless my family so it may continue before you forever. 📖

You, LORD, have blessed it.

It will be blessed forever.”
