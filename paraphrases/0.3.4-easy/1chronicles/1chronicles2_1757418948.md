# 1 Chronicles 2 — The families of Israel and Judah

**About this chapter**

The record names Israel’s sons and then follows Judah’s families. | Some names describe peoples or places as well as individuals. | The words father and sons can describe family founders and later descendants.

## 1 Chronicles 2:1

Israel’s sons were Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:2

Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:3

Judah’s sons were Er, Onan, and Shelah, born to Bath-shua the Canaanite. 📖

Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked before the LORD.

The LORD put him to death.

## 1 Chronicles 2:4

Tamar, Judah’s daughter-in-law, bore him Perez and Zerah. 📖

Judah had 5 sons altogether.

## 1 Chronicles 2:5

Perez’s sons were Hezron and Hamul. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:6

Zerah’s sons were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara, 5 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:7

Carmi’s son was Achar. 📖

He brought trouble on Israel by breaking the ban concerning devoted things.

**Help with devoted things:** These were set apart for God and forbidden for personal use.

## 1 Chronicles 2:8

Ethan’s son was Azariah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:9

Hezron’s sons were Jerahmeel, Ram, and Caleb. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:10

Ram fathered Amminadab. 📖

Amminadab fathered Nahshon, a leader of Judah’s descendants.

## 1 Chronicles 2:11

Nahshon fathered Salmon, and Salmon fathered Boaz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:12

Boaz fathered Obed, and Obed fathered Jesse. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:13

Jesse’s firstborn was Eliab, his 2nd son Abinadab, and his 3rd Shimea. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:14

His 4th was Nethanel and his 5th Raddai. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:15

His 6th was Ozem and his 7th David. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:16

Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. 📖

Zeruiah’s 3 sons were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel.

## 1 Chronicles 2:17

Abigail bore Amasa, whose father was Jether the Ishmaelite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:18

Caleb son of Hezron had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth. 📖

Azubah’s sons were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.

## 1 Chronicles 2:19

Azubah died, and Caleb married Ephrath, who bore Hur. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:20

Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:21

Later, Hezron had sex with Machir’s daughter. 📖

Machir was Gilead’s father.

Hezron married her when he was 60, and she bore Segub.

## 1 Chronicles 2:22

Segub fathered Jair, who had 23 cities in Gilead. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:23

Geshur and Aram captured Havvoth-jair, Kenath, and its 60 surrounding villages. 📖

All these were descendants of Machir, Gilead’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 2:24

Hezron died in Caleb-ephrathah. 📖

Afterwards, his wife Abijah bore Ashhur, Tekoa’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 2:25

Jerahmeel, Hezron’s firstborn, had these sons: Ram his firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:26

Jerahmeel’s other wife, Atarah, was Onam’s mother. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:27

Ram, Jerahmeel’s firstborn, had sons Maaz, Jamin, and Eker. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:28

Onam’s sons were Shammai and Jada. 📖

Shammai’s sons were Nadab and Abishur.

## 1 Chronicles 2:29

Abishur’s wife Abihail bore him Ahban and Molid. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:30

Nadab’s sons were Seled and Appaim. 📖

Seled died without children.

## 1 Chronicles 2:31

Appaim’s son was Ishi. 📖

Ishi’s son was Sheshan.

Sheshan’s son was Ahlai.

## 1 Chronicles 2:32

Jada, Shammai’s brother, had sons Jether and Jonathan. 📖

Jether died without children.

## 1 Chronicles 2:33

Jonathan’s sons were Peleth and Zaza. 📖

These were Jerahmeel’s descendants.

## 1 Chronicles 2:34

Sheshan had daughters but no sons. 📖

He had an Egyptian servant named Jarha.

## 1 Chronicles 2:35

Sheshan gave his daughter in marriage to Jarha, his servant. 📖

She bore Attai.

## 1 Chronicles 2:36

Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:37

Zabad fathered Ephlal, and Ephlal fathered Obed. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:38

Obed fathered Jehu, and Jehu fathered Azariah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:39

Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Elasah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:40

Elasah fathered Sismai, and Sismai fathered Shallum. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:41

Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:42

Caleb, Jerahmeel’s brother, had firstborn Mesha, Ziph’s father. 📖

His 2nd son Mareshah was Hebron’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 2:43

Hebron’s sons were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:44

Shema fathered Raham, Jorkeam’s father. 📖

Rekem fathered Shammai.

## 1 Chronicles 2:45

Shammai’s son was Maon, and Maon was Beth-zur’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:46

Caleb’s concubine Ephah bore Haran, Moza, and Gazez. 📖

Haran fathered Gazez.

**Help with concubine:** A woman in a recognised relationship with a man, with lower status than a wife.

## 1 Chronicles 2:47

Jahdai’s sons were Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:48

Caleb’s concubine Maacah bore Sheber and Tirhanah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:49

She also bore Shaaph, Madmannah’s father, and Sheva, father of Machbenah and Gibea. 📖

Caleb’s daughter was Acsah.

## 1 Chronicles 2:50

These were Caleb’s descendants. 📖

Hur, Ephrathah’s firstborn, had sons including Shobal, Kiriath-jearim’s father,

## 1 Chronicles 2:51

Salma, Bethlehem’s father, and Hareph, Beth-gader’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:52

Shobal, Kiriath-jearim’s father, had descendants Haroeh and half the Manahathites, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:53

and Kiriath-jearim’s clans: the Ithrites, Puthites, Shumathites, and Mishraites. 📖

The Zorathites and Eshtaolites descended from these.

## 1 Chronicles 2:54

Salma’s descendants were Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, half the Manahathites, the Zorites, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:55

and the scribes’ clans at Jabez: the Tirathites, Shimeathites, and Sucathites. 📖

These Kenites came from Hammath, father of the house of Rechab.

**Help with scribes:** People who wrote and kept records.
