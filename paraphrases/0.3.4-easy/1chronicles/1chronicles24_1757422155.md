# 1 Chronicles 24 — The priestly divisions

**About this chapter**

David divides Aaron’s descendants for service. | Lots determine the order. | Other Levite families are also listed and assigned by lot.

## 1 Chronicles 24:1

These were Aaron’s descendants’ divisions. 📖

Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

## 1 Chronicles 24:2

Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no sons. 📖

Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests.

## 1 Chronicles 24:3

Zadok, Eleazar’s descendant, and Ahimelech, Ithamar’s descendant, helped David divide the priests by their duties. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:4

Eleazar’s descendants had more leaders than Ithamar’s, so there were 16 family heads from Eleazar and 8 from Ithamar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:5

They divided them by lot. 📖

Both Eleazar’s and Ithamar’s descendants included officers of the sanctuary and of God.

**Help with lot:** A way of making an allocation by drawing marked objects.

## 1 Chronicles 24:6

Shemaiah son of Nethanel, a Levite scribe, recorded their names before the king and officials. 📖

Zadok the priest, Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and priestly and Levite family heads were present.

One family came from Eleazar, then one from Ithamar.

## 1 Chronicles 24:7

The 1st lot went to Jehoiarib and the 2nd to Jedaiah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:8

3rd to Harim and 4th to Seorim, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:9

5th to Malchijah and 6th to Mijamin, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:10

7th to Hakkoz and 8th to Abijah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:11

9th to Jeshua and 10th to Shecaniah, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:12

11th to Eliashib and 12th to Jakim, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:13

13th to Huppah and 14th to Jeshebeab, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:14

15th to Bilgah and 16th to Immer, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:15

17th to Hezir and 18th to Happizzez, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:16

19th to Pethahiah and 20th to Jehezkel, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:17

21st to Jachin and 22nd to Gamul, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:18

23rd to Delaiah and 24th to Maaziah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:19

This was their appointed service order in the LORD’s house under their ancestor Aaron’s rules. 📖

The LORD, Israel’s God, commanded him.

## 1 Chronicles 24:20

The remaining Levites included Shubael from Amram’s sons and Jehdeiah from Shubael’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:21

Rehabiah’s leading son was Isshiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:22

The Izharites included Shelomoth, whose son was Jahath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:23

Hebron’s sons were Jeriah the 1st, Amariah the 2nd, Jahaziel the 3rd, and Jekameam the 4th. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:24

Uzziel’s sons included Micah, whose son was Shamir. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:25

Micah’s brother was Isshiah, whose son was Zechariah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:26

Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. 📖

Jaaziah’s son was Beno.

## 1 Chronicles 24:27

Merari’s descendants through Jaaziah were Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:28

From Mahli came Eleazar, who had no sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:29

From Kish came his son Jerahmeel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:30

Mushi’s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. 📖

These were Levites’ descendants by family.

## 1 Chronicles 24:31

Like Aaron’s descendants, they cast lots before King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and priestly and Levite family heads. 📖

Family heads and their younger brothers did this alike.
