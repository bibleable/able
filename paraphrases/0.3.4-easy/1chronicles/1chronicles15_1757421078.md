# 1 Chronicles 15 — The ark is brought to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

David gathers Levites to carry the ark according to the LORD’s command. | Named singers, musicians, priests, and guards take part. | Michal watches David celebrate and despises him.

## 1 Chronicles 15:1

David built buildings in the City of David and prepared a place and tent for God’s ark. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:2

David said, “Only Levites may carry God’s ark. 📖

The LORD chose them to carry his ark and serve before him forever.”

## 1 Chronicles 15:3

David gathered all Israel in Jerusalem to bring the LORD’s ark to its prepared place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:4

He gathered Aaron’s descendants and the Levites: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:5

from Kohath, chief Uriel and 120 relatives, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:6

from Merari, chief Asaiah and 220 relatives, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:7

from Gershom, chief Joel and 130 relatives, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:8

from Elizaphan, chief Shemaiah and 200 relatives, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:9

from Hebron, chief Eliel and 80 relatives, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:10

and from Uzziel, chief Amminadab and 112 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:11

David called priests Zadok and Abiathar and Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:12

He said, “You lead the Levite families. 📖

Consecrate yourselves and your relatives to bring the ark of the LORD, Israel’s God, to its prepared place.

**Help with consecrate:** Prepare and set apart for sacred service.

## 1 Chronicles 15:13

You Levites were absent the first time. 📖

That is why the LORD our God burst out in anger against us.

We did not ask him about the proper order.”

## 1 Chronicles 15:14

The priests and Levites consecrated themselves to bring the ark of the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:15

The Levites carried God’s ark on their shoulders with poles. 📖

Moses commanded this according to the LORD’s word.

## 1 Chronicles 15:16

David told Levite leaders to appoint relatives to sing joyfully with harps, lyres, and cymbals. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:17

They appointed Heman son of Joel, his relative Asaph son of Berechiah, and their Merarite relative Ethan son of Kushaiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:18

Their next-ranking relatives were Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, and Mikneiah. 📖

Gatekeepers Obed-edom and Jeiel joined them.

## 1 Chronicles 15:19

Heman, Asaph, and Ethan played bronze cymbals. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:20

Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah played harps according to Alamoth. 📖

**Help with Alamoth:** A musical term in the source; its precise meaning is uncertain.

## 1 Chronicles 15:21

Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah led with lyres according to Sheminith. 📖

**Help with Sheminith:** A musical term in the source; its precise meaning is uncertain.

## 1 Chronicles 15:22

Chenaniah, head Levite, directed the music because he was highly skilled. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:23

Berechiah and Elkanah guarded the ark. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:24

Priests Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer blew trumpets before God’s ark. 📖

Obed-edom and Jehiah also guarded it.

## 1 Chronicles 15:25

David, Israel’s elders, and commanders of thousands joyfully brought the LORD’s covenant ark from Obed-edom’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:26

God helped the Levites carrying the LORD’s covenant ark. 📖

They sacrificed 7 bulls and 7 rams.

## 1 Chronicles 15:27

David wore a fine linen robe, like the ark-carrying Levites, singers, and Chenaniah, the singers’ music director. 📖

David also wore a linen ephod.

**Help with ephod:** A garment worn in sacred service.

Linen is cloth made from flax.

## 1 Chronicles 15:28

All Israel brought the LORD’s covenant ark with shouting, ram’s horns, trumpets, cymbals, harps, and lyres. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:29

As the LORD’s covenant ark entered the City of David, Saul’s daughter Michal watched from a window. 📖

She saw King David dancing and celebrating and despised him in her heart.
