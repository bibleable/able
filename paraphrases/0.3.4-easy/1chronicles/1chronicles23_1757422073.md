# 1 Chronicles 23 — The Levites’ divisions and duties

**About this chapter**

David installs Solomon as king and organises the Levites. | The record lists family divisions and work. | It gives the ages used in the initial count and David’s final instructions.

## 1 Chronicles 23:1

When David was very old, he made Solomon his son king over Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:2

He gathered Israel’s leaders, priests, and Levites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:3

Levite men aged 30 and above were counted, 38,000 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:4

David said, “24,000 must oversee work in the LORD’s house, and 6,000 serve as officers and judges. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:5

4,000 must guard gates. 📖

4,000 must praise the LORD with instruments I made for praise.”

## 1 Chronicles 23:6

David divided the Levites by Levi’s sons Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:7

Gershon’s family included Ladan and Shimei. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:8

Ladan’s sons were chief Jehiel, Zetham, and Joel, 3 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:9

Shimei’s sons were Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran, 3 altogether. 📖

These led Ladan’s families.

## 1 Chronicles 23:10

Shimei’s sons were Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah, 4 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:11

Jahath was chief and Zizah 2nd. 📖

Jeush and Beriah had few sons, so they were counted as one family with one assignment.

## 1 Chronicles 23:12

Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, 4 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:13

Amram’s sons were Aaron and Moses. 📖

Aaron and his descendants were set apart forever to consecrate the most holy things.

They burned incense, served before the LORD, and blessed in his name forever.

**Help with consecrate:** Set apart for sacred use.

## 1 Chronicles 23:14

Moses the man of God’s sons were counted with Levi’s tribe. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:15

Moses’s sons were Gershom and Eliezer. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:16

Gershom’s leading descendant was Shebuel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:17

Eliezer’s leading descendant was Rehabiah. 📖

Eliezer had no other sons, but Rehabiah had very many sons.

## 1 Chronicles 23:18

Izhar’s leading son was Shelomith. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:19

Hebron’s sons were Jeriah the 1st, Amariah the 2nd, Jahaziel the 3rd, and Jekameam the 4th. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:20

Uzziel’s sons were Micah the 1st and Isshiah the 2nd. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:21

Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. 📖

Mahli’s sons were Eleazar and Kish.

## 1 Chronicles 23:22

Eleazar died with daughters but no sons. 📖

Their cousins, Kish’s sons, married them.

## 1 Chronicles 23:23

Mushi’s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth, 3 altogether. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:24

These were Levi’s descendants by family, individually named and registered with their family heads. 📖

Those aged 20 and above served in the LORD’s house.

## 1 Chronicles 23:25

David said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, gave his people rest and has come to dwell in Jerusalem forever. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:26

The Levites no longer need to carry the tabernacle or its service objects.” 📖

**Help with tabernacle:** The sacred tent used for worship.

## 1 Chronicles 23:27

David’s final instructions counted Levites aged 20 and above. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:28

They assisted Aaron’s descendants in the LORD’s house. 📖

They managed courts and rooms, purification of sacred things, and service in God’s house.

**Help with purification:** Preparing things to meet the law’s requirements for sacred use.

## 1 Chronicles 23:29

They managed the showbread rows and fine flour for grain offerings. 📖

They handled unleavened wafers, baking, mixing, and all measures of quantity and size.

**Help with these foods:** Showbread was set before the LORD.

Unleavened wafers are thin breads made without yeast.

## 1 Chronicles 23:30

They stood to thank and praise the LORD each morning and evening. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:31

They served regularly in the required numbers for burnt offerings on Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts. 📖

**Help with these days:** Sabbath was the weekly rest day.

New Moon marked a month’s beginning.

## 1 Chronicles 23:32

They fulfilled duties for the Tent of Meeting and Holy Place. 📖

Under their relatives, Aaron’s descendants, they served the LORD’s house.
