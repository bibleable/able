# 1 Chronicles 12 — Warriors gather to support David

**About this chapter**

People join David while Saul is king and later gather at Hebron to make David king. | The record keeps each group’s leaders, skills, and numbers.

## 1 Chronicles 12:1

These men came to David at Ziklag while he was banished from Saul son of Kish’s presence. 📖

They were mighty men who helped him fight.

## 1 Chronicles 12:2

They were archers who could sling stones and shoot arrows with either hand. 📖

They were Saul’s relatives from Benjamin.

## 1 Chronicles 12:3

Their chief was Ahiezer, with Joash, both sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite. 📖

Others were Jeziel and Pelet, Azmaveth’s sons, Beracah, and Jehu the Anathothite,

## 1 Chronicles 12:4

Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty man among the Thirty and their leader, Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Jozabad the Gederathite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:5

Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:6

Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam, who were Korahites, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:7

and Joelah and Zebadiah, Jeroham’s sons from Gedor. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:8

Some Gadites joined David at his wilderness stronghold. 📖

They were brave, trained warriors skilled with shield and spear.

Their faces were like lions, and they ran like gazelles on mountains.

**Help with these comparisons:** These images describe fierce courage and speed.

## 1 Chronicles 12:9

Ezer was chief, Obadiah 2nd, and Eliab 3rd, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:10

Mishmannah 4th and Jeremiah 5th, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:11

Attai 6th and Eliel 7th, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:12

Johanan 8th and Elzabad 9th, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:13

Jeremiah 10th and Machbanai 11th. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:14

These Gadite commanders’ least man could match 100, and their greatest could match 1,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:15

They crossed the Jordan in month 1 while it overflowed all its banks. 📖

They drove away everyone in the valleys east and west.

## 1 Chronicles 12:16

Other Benjamites and men from Judah came to David’s stronghold. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:17

David met them. 📖

“If you come peacefully to help me, my heart will be united with yours.

If you betray me to enemies when my hands are free of violence, may our ancestors’ God see and judge.”

**Help with hands free of violence:** David says he is not guilty of violence.

## 1 Chronicles 12:18

The Spirit came on Amasai, chief of the Thirty. 📖

“We are yours, David, son of Jesse!

Peace, peace to you and your helpers, because your God helps you.”

David welcomed them and made them troop leaders.

## 1 Chronicles 12:19

Some Manasseh men joined David as he went with Philistines to fight Saul. 📖

They did not help the Philistines.

Philistine rulers sent David away, fearing he would return to Saul and cost them their heads.

## 1 Chronicles 12:20

As David went to Ziklag, Manasseh’s commanders of thousands joined him. 📖

They were Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai.

## 1 Chronicles 12:21

They helped David against raiders. 📖

All were brave warriors and army commanders.

## 1 Chronicles 12:22

Men came daily to help David until he had a great army, like God’s army. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:23

These armed men came to David at Hebron to transfer Saul’s kingdom to him, according to the LORD’s word: 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:24

Judah sent 6,800 troops with shields and spears. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:25

Simeon sent 7,100 brave men ready for battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:26

Levi sent 4,600, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:27

including Jehoiada, leader of Aaron’s family, with 3,700 men, 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:28

and Zadok, a brave young man, with 22 commanders from his family. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:29

Benjamin, Saul’s relatives, sent 3,000. 📖

Most had remained loyal to Saul’s family until then.

## 1 Chronicles 12:30

Ephraim sent 20,800 brave warriors famous within their clans. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:31

Manasseh’s half-tribe sent 18,000 named men appointed to make David king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:32

Issachar sent men who understood the times and knew what Israel should do. 📖

They had 200 chiefs, with all their relatives under their command.

## 1 Chronicles 12:33

Zebulun sent 50,000 men ready for service and trained with every kind of weapon. 📖

They were united in devotion to David.

## 1 Chronicles 12:34

Naphtali sent 1,000 commanders and 37,000 men with shields and spears. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:35

Dan sent 28,600 men ready for battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:36

Asher sent 40,000 men ready for service and battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:37

From east of the Jordan, Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe sent 120,000 with every kind of weapon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:38

All these prepared warriors came to Hebron determined to make David king over all Israel. 📖

The rest of Israel agreed.

## 1 Chronicles 12:39

They ate and drank with David for 3 days. 📖

Their relatives supplied them.

## 1 Chronicles 12:40

Neighbours from as far as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali brought food on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen. 📖

They brought plentiful flour, fig cakes, raisin cakes, wine, oil, oxen, and sheep.

Israel was joyful.
