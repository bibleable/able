# 2 Kings 4 — Elisha helps families and feeds people

**About this chapter**

A widow receives oil to pay her debt. | A woman of Shunem receives a son, who later dies and is restored to life. | Elisha also helps people during famine.

## 2 Kings 4:1

A prophetic group member’s widow cried to Elisha, “My husband, your servant, died. 📖

You know he feared the LORD.

His creditor is coming to take my 2 children as slaves!”

**Help with creditor:** This is someone who is owed money.

## 2 Kings 4:2

Elisha asked, “How can I help? 📖

What is in your house?”

She answered, “I have nothing but a jar of oil.”

## 2 Kings 4:3

Elisha said, “Borrow empty jars from all your neighbours. 📖

Get many, not just a few.

## 2 Kings 4:4

Go inside with your sons and shut the door. 📖

Pour oil into the jars and set the full ones aside.”

## 2 Kings 4:5

She left and shut the door behind herself and her sons. 📖

They kept bringing jars, and she kept pouring.

## 2 Kings 4:6

When all the jars were full, she asked her son for another. 📖

He said, “There are no more.”

The oil stopped flowing.

## 2 Kings 4:7

She told the man of God. 📖

He said, “Sell the oil and pay your debt.

You and your sons can live on what remains.”

## 2 Kings 4:8

One day, Elisha went to Shunem. 📖

An important woman persuaded him to eat there.

Whenever he passed that way, he stopped to eat.

## 2 Kings 4:9

She told her husband, “I know this man who often visits is a holy man of God. 📖

## 2 Kings 4:10

Let us make a small upstairs room with a bed, table, chair, and lamp. 📖

He can stay there when he visits.”

## 2 Kings 4:11

One day, Elisha visited and lay down in the upstairs room. 📖

## 2 Kings 4:12

He told his servant Gehazi to call the Shunammite woman. 📖

Gehazi called her, and she stood before him.

## 2 Kings 4:13

Elisha told Gehazi, “Tell her, ‘You took all this trouble for us. 📖

What can we do for you?

Shall we speak for you to the king or army commander?’”

She answered, “I have a home among my own people.”

## 2 Kings 4:14

Elisha asked, “What can be done for her?” 📖

Gehazi answered, “She has no son, and her husband is old.”

## 2 Kings 4:15

Elisha said, “Call her.” 📖

Gehazi called her, and she stood in the doorway.

## 2 Kings 4:16

Elisha said, “At this time next year, you will hold a son.” 📖

She answered, “No, my lord!

Man of God, do not lie to your servant!”

## 2 Kings 4:17

She became pregnant and bore a son at that time next year, as Elisha said. 📖

## 2 Kings 4:18

The child grew. 📖

One day, he went to his father, who was with the harvest workers.

## 2 Kings 4:19

He cried to his father, “My head! 📖

My head!”

His father told a servant, “Carry him to his mother.”

## 2 Kings 4:20

The servant carried him to his mother. 📖

He sat on her lap until noon and then died.

## 2 Kings 4:21

She laid him on the man of God’s bed upstairs. 📖

She shut the door and went out.

## 2 Kings 4:22

She asked her husband, “Send me a servant and a donkey. 📖

I want to go quickly to the man of God and return.”

## 2 Kings 4:23

He asked, “Why go today? 📖

It is neither a New Moon nor a Sabbath.”

She answered, “Everything is all right.”

**Help with these days:** New Moon marked a month’s beginning.

Sabbath was the weekly rest day.

## 2 Kings 4:24

She saddled the donkey and told her servant, “Drive on. 📖

Do not slow down for me unless I tell you.”

## 2 Kings 4:25

She travelled to the man of God at Mount Carmel. 📖

He saw her far away and told Gehazi, “There is the Shunammite woman.

## 2 Kings 4:26

Run to meet her. 📖

Ask whether she, her husband, and her child are all right.”

She answered, “Everything is all right.”

## 2 Kings 4:27

She reached the mountain and held Elisha’s feet. 📖

Gehazi tried to push her away.

Elisha said, “Leave her.

She is deeply distressed.

The LORD has hidden this from me and not told me.”

## 2 Kings 4:28

She said, “Did I ask you for a son, my lord? 📖

Did I not say, ‘Do not deceive me’?”

## 2 Kings 4:29

Elisha told Gehazi, “Tie up your clothing, take my staff, and go. 📖

Greet nobody you meet.

Answer nobody’s greeting.

Lay my staff on the boy’s face.”

**Help with staff:** This is a walking stick.

## 2 Kings 4:30

The boy’s mother said, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.” 📖

Elisha rose and followed her.

## 2 Kings 4:31

Gehazi went ahead and laid the staff on the boy’s face. 📖

There was no sound or response.

He returned to meet Elisha and said, “The boy has not awakened.”

## 2 Kings 4:32

Elisha reached the house and found the boy dead on his bed. 📖

## 2 Kings 4:33

He entered, closed the door behind himself and the boy, and prayed to the LORD. 📖

## 2 Kings 4:34

He lay on the boy, mouth to mouth, eyes to eyes, and hands to hands. 📖

As he stretched over him, the boy’s body grew warm.

## 2 Kings 4:35

Elisha walked back and forth in the room, then stretched over the boy again on the bed. 📖

The boy sneezed 7 times and opened his eyes.

## 2 Kings 4:36

Elisha called Gehazi and told him to call the Shunammite woman. 📖

She came, and Elisha said, “Pick up your son.”

## 2 Kings 4:37

She fell at Elisha’s feet and bowed to the ground. 📖

She picked up her son and left.

## 2 Kings 4:38

Elisha returned to Gilgal during famine. 📖

The prophetic group sat at his feet.

He told his attendant, “Put on the large pot and cook stew for the prophetic group.”

**Help with famine:** This is a time when food is very scarce.

## 2 Kings 4:39

One man gathered herbs in a field. 📖

He found a wild vine and filled his clothing with wild gourds.

He cut them into the stew, though nobody knew what they were.

**Help with gourds:** These are fruits related to pumpkins and squashes.

## 2 Kings 4:40

The stew was served. 📖

The men tasted it and cried, “Man of God, there is death in the pot!”

They could not eat it.

## 2 Kings 4:41

Elisha asked for flour and threw it into the pot. 📖

He said, “Serve it for the people to eat.”

Nothing harmful remained.

## 2 Kings 4:42

A man from Baal-shalishah brought the man of God 20 barley loaves in a sack. 📖

They were made from the first ripe grain.

Elisha said, “Give it to the people to eat.”

## 2 Kings 4:43

His servant asked, “How can I serve 20 loaves to 100 men?” 📖

Elisha said, “Give it to them.

The LORD says, ‘They will eat and have some left.’”

## 2 Kings 4:44

The servant served them. 📖

They ate and had some left, as the LORD said.
