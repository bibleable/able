# 2 Kings 18 — Hezekiah trusts the LORD as Assyria threatens

**About this chapter**

Hezekiah removes objects of false worship. | Assyria captures Samaria and later attacks Judah. | Assyria’s spokesman challenges Hezekiah’s trust and addresses Jerusalem’s people.

## 2 Kings 18:1

Hezekiah son of Ahaz became Judah’s king in year 3 of Hoshea son of Elah’s reign over Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 18:2

He became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 29 years. 📖

His mother was Abi daughter of Zechariah.

## 2 Kings 18:3

He did right before the LORD, like his ancestor David. 📖

## 2 Kings 18:4

He removed high places, smashed sacred pillars, and cut down Asherah poles. 📖

He destroyed Moses’s bronze snake, called Nehushtan, because Israel had burned incense to it until then.

**Help with these objects:** High places, pillars, and Asherah poles were used in worship.

The bronze snake had also become an object of worship.

## 2 Kings 18:5

Hezekiah trusted the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

No king of Judah before or after him was like him.

## 2 Kings 18:6

He stayed faithful to the LORD without turning away. 📖

He kept the commands the LORD gave Moses.

## 2 Kings 18:7

The LORD was with Hezekiah, and he succeeded wherever he went. 📖

He rebelled against Assyria’s king and refused to serve him.

## 2 Kings 18:8

He defeated the Philistines to Gaza and its borders, from watchtower to defended city. 📖

## 2 Kings 18:9

In Hezekiah’s 4th year, Shalmaneser king of Assyria surrounded Samaria. 📖

This was year 7 of Hoshea son of Elah’s reign over Israel.

## 2 Kings 18:10

Assyria captured it after 3 years. 📖

Samaria fell in Hezekiah’s 6th year, Hoshea king of Israel’s 9th year.

## 2 Kings 18:11

Assyria’s king exiled Israel to Assyria. 📖

He settled them in Halah, Gozan by the Habor River, and the Medes’ cities.

**Help with exiled:** They were forced to leave their homeland.

## 2 Kings 18:12

This happened because they refused to hear or obey the LORD their God. 📖

They broke his covenant and all Moses, the LORD’s servant, commanded.

## 2 Kings 18:13

In Hezekiah’s 14th year, Sennacherib king of Assyria attacked and captured all Judah’s defended cities. 📖

## 2 Kings 18:14

Hezekiah king of Judah sent word to Assyria’s king at Lachish. 📖

“I did wrong.

Withdraw, and I will pay whatever you demand.”

Assyria’s king demanded 300 talents of silver and 30 talents of gold.

**Help with talent:** This was a large unit of weight.

## 2 Kings 18:15

Hezekiah gave him all silver found in the LORD’s house and palace treasuries. 📖

## 2 Kings 18:16

He stripped the gold he had put on the LORD’s temple’s doors and doorposts. 📖

He gave it to Assyria’s king.

## 2 Kings 18:17

Assyria’s king sent the Tartan, Rabsaris, and Rabshakeh with a great army from Lachish to Hezekiah in Jerusalem. 📖

They stood by the upper pool’s aqueduct on the road to the Launderer’s Field.

**Help with these words:** Tartan, Rabsaris, and Rabshakeh are Assyrian officials’ titles.

An aqueduct carries water.

A launderer washes cloth.

## 2 Kings 18:18

They called for the king. 📖

Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the palace administrator, went out with Shebnah the scribe and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder.

**Help with these roles:** A scribe writes documents.

A recorder keeps official records.

## 2 Kings 18:19

The Rabshakeh said, “Tell Hezekiah that the great king of Assyria asks what supports your confidence. 📖

## 2 Kings 18:20

Your claims of war strategy and strength are empty words. 📖

Whom do you trust, that you rebel against me?

## 2 Kings 18:21

You trust Egypt, a broken reed that pierces the hand leaning on it! 📖

Pharaoh king of Egypt is like that to everyone trusting him.

**Help with this picture:** A reed is a plant stem.

The spokesman says Egypt is an unreliable support.

## 2 Kings 18:22

If you say you trust the LORD your God, did Hezekiah not remove his high places and altars? 📖

Did he not tell Judah and Jerusalem to worship only at this Jerusalem altar?

## 2 Kings 18:23

Make a bargain with my master, Assyria’s king. 📖

I will give you 2,000 horses, if you can find riders!

## 2 Kings 18:24

How can you defeat even one of my master’s weakest officers when you rely on Egypt’s chariots and horsemen? 📖

## 2 Kings 18:25

Did I come to destroy this place without the LORD? 📖

The LORD told me, ‘Attack and destroy this land.’”

**About this claim:** This is what Assyria’s spokesman claims the LORD told him.

## 2 Kings 18:26

Eliakim son of Hilkiah, Shebnah, and Joah told the Rabshakeh, “Speak Aramaic, which we understand. 📖

Do not speak Hebrew where the people on the wall can hear.”

## 2 Kings 18:27

He answered, “Did my master send this message only to you and your master? 📖

Is it not for those on the wall too?

They will eat their own dung and drink their own urine with you.”

**About this threat:** The spokesman threatens the desperate conditions of siege.

Dung means bodily waste.

## 2 Kings 18:28

The Rabshakeh shouted in Hebrew, “Hear the great king of Assyria’s word! 📖

## 2 Kings 18:29

The king says, ‘Do not let Hezekiah deceive you. 📖

He cannot save you from me.

## 2 Kings 18:30

Do not let him persuade you to trust the LORD. 📖

He says the LORD will save you and keep Assyria’s king from taking this city.

## 2 Kings 18:31

Do not listen to Hezekiah. 📖

Assyria’s king says to make peace and come out.

Then everyone can eat from their own vine and fig tree and drink from their own cistern.

**Help with cistern:** This is a place built to store water.

## 2 Kings 18:32

Later I will take you to a land like yours, with grain, new wine, bread, vineyards, olive trees, and honey. 📖

You will live, not die.

Do not listen when Hezekiah misleads you by saying the LORD will save you.

## 2 Kings 18:33

Has any nation’s god saved its land from Assyria’s king? 📖

## 2 Kings 18:34

Where are Hamath’s and Arpad’s gods? 📖

Where are Sepharvaim’s, Hena’s, and Ivvah’s gods?

Did they save Samaria from me?

## 2 Kings 18:35

Which of these lands’ gods saved its land from me? 📖

How could the LORD save Jerusalem from me?’”

## 2 Kings 18:36

The people stayed silent. 📖

Hezekiah had ordered them not to answer.

## 2 Kings 18:37

Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the palace administrator, returned to Hezekiah with Shebna the scribe. 📖

Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, also came.

Their clothes were torn.

They reported the Rabshakeh’s words.

**Help with torn clothes:** This expressed distress.
