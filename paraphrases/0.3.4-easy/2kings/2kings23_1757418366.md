# 2 Kings 23 — Josiah renews the covenant and removes false worship

**About this chapter**

Josiah reads the covenant and removes objects and practices of other worship. | He keeps Passover, but the announced judgment against Judah remains. | The account then describes his death and the kings after him.

## 2 Kings 23:1

The king gathered all Judah’s and Jerusalem’s elders. 📖

## 2 Kings 23:2

He entered the LORD’s house with all Judah and Jerusalem, priests, prophets, and people small and great. 📖

He read aloud every word of the Book of the Covenant found in the LORD’s house.

## 2 Kings 23:3

By the pillar, he made a covenant before the LORD. 📖

He promised to follow the LORD and keep his commands and laws with all his heart and soul.

He would carry out the covenant written in the book.

All the people entered this covenant.

**Help with covenant:** This is a binding agreement.

## 2 Kings 23:4

He gave orders to Hilkiah the high priest, second-ranking priests, and doorkeepers. 📖

Remove all objects made for Baal, Asherah, and heaven’s host from the LORD’s temple.

He burned them in Kidron’s fields outside Jerusalem and took the ashes to Bethel.

**Help with heaven’s host:** Here this refers to heavenly bodies worshipped as gods.

## 2 Kings 23:5

Josiah removed idol priests appointed by Judah’s kings to burn incense at high places throughout Judah and around Jerusalem. 📖

They burned incense to Baal, the sun, moon, constellations, and all heaven’s host.

**Help with high places and constellations:** High places were worship sites.

Constellations are groups of stars.

## 2 Kings 23:6

He took the Asherah pole from the LORD’s house to Kidron Valley outside Jerusalem. 📖

He burned it, ground it to powder, and scattered it on ordinary people’s graves.

## 2 Kings 23:7

He demolished the male shrine prostitutes’ rooms in the LORD’s house, where women wove tapestries for Asherah. 📖

**Help with shrine prostitutes:** These were men involved in sexual practices associated with worship.

Tapestries are woven cloths.

## 2 Kings 23:8

He brought priests from Judah’s cities and made their incense-burning high places unusable for worship, from Geba to Beersheba. 📖

He demolished high places at the gates by city governor Joshua’s gate entrance, left of the city gate.

## 2 Kings 23:9

The high-place priests did not serve at the LORD’s altar in Jerusalem. 📖

They ate unleavened bread with their fellow priests.

**Help with unleavened bread:** Bread made without yeast.

## 2 Kings 23:10

He made Topheth in Ben-hinnom Valley unusable for worship. 📖

Nobody could sacrifice sons or daughters in fire to Molech there.

## 2 Kings 23:11

He removed horses Judah’s kings dedicated to the sun from the LORD’s house’s entrance. 📖

They were in the court near official Nathan-melech’s room.

He burned the sun’s chariots.

## 2 Kings 23:12

He demolished Judah’s kings’ rooftop altars near Ahaz’s upper room. 📖

He demolished Manasseh’s altars in the LORD’s house’s 2 courtyards.

He ground them to dust and threw it into Kidron Valley.

## 2 Kings 23:13

He made high places east of Jerusalem, south of the Mount of Corruption, unusable for worship. 📖

Israel’s King Solomon built them for Ashtoreth, the Sidonians’ detestable goddess.

He also built them for Chemosh, the Moabites’ detestable god, and Milcom, the Ammonites’ detestable god.

## 2 Kings 23:14

He smashed sacred pillars, cut Asherah poles, and covered the sites with human bones. 📖

## 2 Kings 23:15

He demolished Bethel’s altar, the high place Jeroboam son of Nebat built when he caused Israel to sin. 📖

He burned the high place, ground it to powder, and burned the Asherah pole.

## 2 Kings 23:16

Josiah saw hillside tombs. 📖

He had bones taken from them and burned on the altar to make it unfit for worship.

This fulfilled the LORD’s word proclaimed by the man of God who foretold these things.

## 2 Kings 23:17

He asked about a monument. 📖

The city’s men said, “It marks the man of God’s tomb.

He came from Judah and foretold what you did to Bethel’s altar.”

## 2 Kings 23:18

Josiah said, “Leave him alone. 📖

Do not disturb his bones.”

They left his bones and those of the prophet from Samaria undisturbed.

## 2 Kings 23:19

In Samaria’s cities, Josiah removed all high-place shrines Israel’s kings built, as he had done at Bethel. 📖

Those kings had angered the LORD.

## 2 Kings 23:20

He killed all the high-place priests on the altars and burned human bones there. 📖

He returned to Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 23:21

He ordered everyone to keep the LORD their God’s Passover as written in the Book of the Covenant. 📖

**Help with Passover:** This festival remembers the LORD rescuing Israel from Egypt.

## 2 Kings 23:22

No Passover like this had been kept from Israel’s judges’ days through all Israel’s and Judah’s kings’ days. 📖

## 2 Kings 23:23

This Passover was kept for the LORD in Jerusalem in Josiah’s 18th year. 📖

## 2 Kings 23:24

Josiah removed mediums, spiritists, household gods, idols, and all detestable things seen in Judah and Jerusalem. 📖

He fulfilled the law in the book Hilkiah the priest found in the LORD’s house.

**Help with mediums and spiritists:** These claimed contact with spirits of the dead.

## 2 Kings 23:25

No king before or after Josiah turned to the LORD like him. 📖

He turned with all his heart, soul, and strength, following all Moses’s Law.

## 2 Kings 23:26

But the LORD’s fierce anger against Judah did not turn away. 📖

Manasseh’s acts had provoked that anger.

## 2 Kings 23:27

The LORD said, “I will remove Judah as I removed Israel. 📖

I will reject my chosen city Jerusalem and the temple where I said my Name would be.”

## 2 Kings 23:28

Josiah’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 23:29

Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt went to help Assyria’s king at the Euphrates during Josiah’s reign. 📖

Josiah confronted him, and Neco killed him at Megiddo.

## 2 Kings 23:30

His servants brought his body by chariot from Megiddo to Jerusalem and buried him in his own tomb. 📖

The people anointed his son Jehoahaz and made him king instead.

## 2 Kings 23:31

Jehoahaz became king at 23 and ruled in Jerusalem for 3 months. 📖

His mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.

## 2 Kings 23:32

He did evil before the LORD like his ancestors. 📖

## 2 Kings 23:33

Pharaoh Neco imprisoned him at Riblah in Hamath, preventing his rule in Jerusalem. 📖

He demanded 100 talents of silver and 1 talent of gold from Judah.

**Help with talent:** This was a large unit of weight.

## 2 Kings 23:34

Neco made Josiah’s son Eliakim king instead of Josiah and renamed him Jehoiakim. 📖

He took Jehoahaz to Egypt, where Jehoahaz died.

## 2 Kings 23:35

Jehoiakim paid Neco the silver and gold. 📖

To meet Pharaoh’s demand, he taxed the people according to their wealth.

## 2 Kings 23:36

Jehoiakim became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. 📖

His mother was Zebidah daughter of Pedaiah from Rumah.

## 2 Kings 23:37

He did evil before the LORD like his ancestors. 📖
