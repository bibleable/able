# 2 Kings 3 — The war against Moab

**About this chapter**

Three kings march against Moab but run out of water. | They seek the LORD’s word through Elisha. | The chapter ends with destruction and a child sacrifice.

## 2 Kings 3:1

Jehoram son of Ahab became Israel’s king in year 18 of Jehoshaphat’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 12 years.

## 2 Kings 3:2

He did evil before the LORD, though not like his father and mother. 📖

He removed the sacred pillar of Baal that his father made.

**Help with sacred pillar:** This was a standing object used in worship.

## 2 Kings 3:3

But he held to the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit. 📖

He did not turn from them.

## 2 Kings 3:4

Mesha king of Moab bred sheep. 📖

He supplied Israel’s king with 100,000 lambs and the wool of 100,000 rams.

## 2 Kings 3:5

After Ahab died, Moab’s king rebelled against Israel’s king. 📖

## 2 Kings 3:6

King Jehoram left Samaria and gathered all Israel for war. 📖

## 2 Kings 3:7

He asked Jehoshaphat king of Judah, “Moab’s king rebelled against me. 📖

Will you fight Moab with me?”

Jehoshaphat answered, “I will.

I am as you are.

My people and horses are yours.”

## 2 Kings 3:8

He asked, “Which route shall we take?” 📖

Joram answered, “Through Edom’s desert.”

**About Joram:** This is another form of the name Jehoram.

## 2 Kings 3:9

The kings of Israel, Judah, and Edom travelled a roundabout route for 7 days. 📖

They had no water for the army or animals.

## 2 Kings 3:10

Israel’s king said, “Oh no! 📖

The LORD gathered these 3 kings to hand them over to Moab!”

## 2 Kings 3:11

Jehoshaphat asked, “Is a prophet of the LORD here? 📖

Let us ask the LORD through him.”

A servant of Israel’s king answered, “Elisha son of Shaphat is here.

He used to pour water on Elijah’s hands.”

**Help with pouring water:** This describes Elisha’s service to Elijah.

## 2 Kings 3:12

Jehoshaphat said, “The LORD’s word is with him.” 📖

Israel’s king, Jehoshaphat, and Edom’s king went down to Elisha.

## 2 Kings 3:13

Elisha told Israel’s king, “What have we to do with each other? 📖

Go to your father’s and mother’s prophets!”

The king answered, “No!

The LORD gathered these 3 kings to hand them over to Moab.”

## 2 Kings 3:14

Elisha said, “As surely as the LORD of Hosts, whom I serve, lives, I respect Jehoshaphat king of Judah. 📖

Without his presence, I would not look at you or acknowledge you.

**Help with LORD of Hosts:** This title describes the LORD as ruler of heavenly armies.

## 2 Kings 3:15

Now bring me a harp player.” 📖

As the musician played, the LORD’s hand came on Elisha.

**Help with the LORD’s hand:** This describes the LORD’s power working through him.

## 2 Kings 3:16

Elisha said, “The LORD says, ‘Dig ditches throughout this valley.’ 📖

## 2 Kings 3:17

The LORD says, ‘You will see neither wind nor rain, but the valley will fill with water. 📖

You, your cattle, and your animals will drink.’

## 2 Kings 3:18

This is easy for the LORD. 📖

He will also hand Moab over to you.

## 2 Kings 3:19

You will attack every strongly defended city and every important city. 📖

Cut down every good tree, block every spring, and ruin every good field with stones.”

## 2 Kings 3:20

Next morning, at the morning sacrifice, water flowed from Edom’s direction and filled the land. 📖

## 2 Kings 3:21

All Moab heard the kings had come to fight. 📖

Everyone able to carry weapons, young and old, was called to the border.

## 2 Kings 3:22

They rose early. 📖

Sunlight shone on the water, which looked blood-red to the Moabites across the way.

## 2 Kings 3:23

They said, “This is blood! 📖

The kings fought and killed each other.

Moab, go and take their goods!”

## 2 Kings 3:24

The Moabites reached Israel’s camp. 📖

Israel rose and attacked, and Moab fled.

Israel invaded their land and struck them down.

## 2 Kings 3:25

Israel destroyed the cities. 📖

Each man threw stones on every good field until it was covered.

They blocked every spring and cut every good tree.

Only Kir-haraseth’s stones remained in place.

Men with slings surrounded and attacked it too.

**Help with slings:** These are weapons used to throw stones.

## 2 Kings 3:26

Moab’s king saw the battle was too fierce. 📖

He took 700 swordsmen to break through to Edom’s king, but they failed.

## 2 Kings 3:27

He took his firstborn son, who would succeed him, and sacrificed him as a burnt offering on the city wall. 📖

Great fury arose against Israel.

Israel withdrew to their own land.

**About this passage:** The text reports a child sacrifice.

It does not instruct readers to do this.

It does not identify whose fury arose.
