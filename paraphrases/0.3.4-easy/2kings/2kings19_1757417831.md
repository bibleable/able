# 2 Kings 19 — Hezekiah prays and the LORD answers

**About this chapter**

Hezekiah seeks the LORD after the Assyrian threat. | Isaiah brings an answer. | The LORD hears Hezekiah’s prayer and announces Jerusalem’s protection.

## 2 Kings 19:1

Hezekiah heard the report, tore his clothes, wore sackcloth, and entered the LORD’s house. 📖

**Help with sackcloth:** This rough cloth expressed grief or humility.

## 2 Kings 19:2

He sent Eliakim the palace administrator, Shebna the scribe, and leading priests to Isaiah son of Amoz, the prophet. 📖

All wore sackcloth.

## 2 Kings 19:3

They said, “Hezekiah calls today a day of distress, rebuke, and shame. 📖

Children have reached birth, but there is no strength to deliver them.

**Help with this image:** Hezekiah compares their desperate lack of strength to a birth that cannot be completed.

## 2 Kings 19:4

Perhaps the LORD your God will hear the Rabshakeh’s words defying the living God and rebuke him. 📖

Assyria’s king sent him.

Pray for the survivors who remain.”

## 2 Kings 19:5

Hezekiah’s servants came to Isaiah. 📖

## 2 Kings 19:6

Isaiah answered, “Tell your master the LORD says, ‘Do not fear those words. 📖

Assyria’s king’s servants have insulted me.

## 2 Kings 19:7

I will put a spirit in him. 📖

He will hear a rumour and return home, where I will cause him to die by the sword.’”

## 2 Kings 19:8

The Rabshakeh heard Assyria’s king had left Lachish. 📖

He withdrew and found the king fighting Libnah.

## 2 Kings 19:9

Sennacherib heard that Tirhakah king of Cush was coming to fight him. 📖

He sent another message to Hezekiah.

## 2 Kings 19:10

“Tell Judah’s King Hezekiah, ‘Do not let your God deceive you. 📖

You trust him when he says Jerusalem will not fall to Assyria’s king.

## 2 Kings 19:11

You heard how Assyria’s kings completely destroyed other countries. 📖

Will you escape?

## 2 Kings 19:12

Did my ancestors’ defeated nations’ gods save them? 📖

Think of Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and Eden’s people in Telassar.

## 2 Kings 19:13

Where are the kings of Hamath, Arpad, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’” 📖

## 2 Kings 19:14

Hezekiah received and read the letter. 📖

He entered the LORD’s house and spread it before the LORD.

## 2 Kings 19:15

He prayed, “LORD, Israel’s God, enthroned between the cherubim, you alone are God over all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

You made heaven and earth.

**Help with cherubim:** These are heavenly beings, represented by winged figures above the covenant ark.

## 2 Kings 19:16

LORD, turn your ear and hear. 📖

Open your eyes and see.

Hear Sennacherib’s message defying the living God.

## 2 Kings 19:17

LORD, Assyria’s kings truly destroyed these nations and lands. 📖

## 2 Kings 19:18

They burned their gods. 📖

These were no gods, only wood and stone made by human hands.

## 2 Kings 19:19

LORD our God, save us from him. 📖

Let all earth’s kingdoms know that you alone, LORD, are God.”

## 2 Kings 19:20

Isaiah son of Amoz sent word to Hezekiah. 📖

“The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I heard your prayer about Sennacherib king of Assyria.

## 2 Kings 19:21

This is my word against him. 📖

The Virgin Daughter of Zion despises and mocks you.

The Daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head behind you.

**Help with these daughters:** The city and its people are pictured as a young woman.

## 2 Kings 19:22

Whom did you mock and insult? 📖

Against whom did you raise your voice and proud eyes?

Against Israel’s Holy One!

## 2 Kings 19:23

Through your servants you mocked the Lord. 📖

You boasted that your many chariots climbed the highest mountains and remote Lebanon peaks.

You cut its tallest cedars and finest cypresses.

You reached its farthest outposts and densest forests.

## 2 Kings 19:24

You said, “I dug wells and drank foreign water. 📖

My feet dried all Egypt’s streams.”

**Help with this boast:** The king describes conquests as though even great rivers could not stop him.

## 2 Kings 19:25

Have you not heard? 📖

I planned this long ago and have now brought it about.

You crushed defended cities into heaps of rubble.

## 2 Kings 19:26

Their people were powerless, dismayed, and ashamed. 📖

They were like field plants, tender shoots, and rooftop grass scorched before growing.

**Help with these plants:** The image describes people left weak and unable to withstand attack.

## 2 Kings 19:27

I know when you sit, go out, and come in. 📖

I know your rage against me.

## 2 Kings 19:28

Your rage and pride reached my ears. 📖

I will put my hook in your nose and my bit in your mouth.

I will send you back the way you came.’

**Help with hook and bit:** These images describe being restrained and led away under another’s power.

## 2 Kings 19:29

Hezekiah, here is your sign. 📖

This year eat what grows by itself, and next year what springs from that.

In year 3, sow and harvest, plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

## 2 Kings 19:30

Judah’s survivors will again put roots down and bear fruit above. 📖

**Help with this picture:** Like a growing plant, the remaining people will become established again.

## 2 Kings 19:31

Survivors will go out from Jerusalem and Mount Zion. 📖

The zeal of the LORD of Hosts will accomplish this.

**Help with these words:** Zeal means strong commitment.

LORD of Hosts means the LORD who rules heavenly armies.

## 2 Kings 19:32

The LORD says Assyria’s king will not enter this city or shoot an arrow into it. 📖

He will not approach with a shield or build a siege ramp.

**Help with siege ramp:** This is a raised approach used to attack a city wall.

## 2 Kings 19:33

The LORD says he will return the way he came and will not enter this city. 📖

## 2 Kings 19:34

I will defend and save this city for my sake and my servant David’s sake.’” 📖

## 2 Kings 19:35

That night the LORD’s angel struck down 185,000 men in Assyria’s camp. 📖

Next morning, the people saw all the dead bodies.

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

## 2 Kings 19:36

Sennacherib king of Assyria left camp and withdrew to Nineveh, where he stayed. 📖

## 2 Kings 19:37

While he worshipped in his god Nisroch’s temple, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with swords. 📖

They escaped to Ararat.

His son Esar-haddon became king.
