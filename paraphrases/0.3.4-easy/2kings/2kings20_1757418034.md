# 2 Kings 20 — Hezekiah’s healing and Babylon’s visit

**About this chapter**

Hezekiah prays when he is gravely ill. | The LORD grants more years and a sign. | Isaiah later announces that Judah’s treasures and descendants will be taken away.

## 2 Kings 20:1

Hezekiah became deathly ill. 📖

Isaiah son of Amoz, the prophet, told him, “The LORD says to put your house in order.

You will die and not recover.”

**Help with house in order:** This means arrange affairs before death.

## 2 Kings 20:2

Hezekiah turned towards the wall and prayed to the LORD. 📖

## 2 Kings 20:3

“LORD, remember my faithful walk before you with wholehearted devotion. 📖

I did what was good in your sight.”

He wept bitterly.

## 2 Kings 20:4

Before Isaiah left the middle courtyard, the LORD spoke to him. 📖

## 2 Kings 20:5

“Return to Hezekiah, my people’s leader. 📖

The LORD, your ancestor David’s God, says, ‘I heard your prayer and saw your tears.

I will heal you.

On day 3 from now, you will enter the LORD’s house.

## 2 Kings 20:6

I will add 15 years to your life. 📖

I will save you and this city from Assyria’s king.

I will defend this city for my sake and my servant David’s sake.’”

## 2 Kings 20:7

Isaiah ordered a poultice of figs. 📖

They applied it to the boil, and Hezekiah recovered.

**Help with poultice and boil:** A poultice is material placed on skin as treatment.

A boil is a painful swelling.

## 2 Kings 20:8

Hezekiah had asked Isaiah for a sign. 📖

“How will I know the LORD will heal me and I will enter his house on day 3?”

## 2 Kings 20:9

Isaiah answered, “Here is a sign the LORD will keep his promise. 📖

Should the shadow move forward 10 steps or back 10 steps?”

## 2 Kings 20:10

Hezekiah answered, “It is easy for the shadow to lengthen 10 steps. 📖

Let it go back 10 steps instead.”

## 2 Kings 20:11

Isaiah the prophet called to the LORD. 📖

The LORD moved the shadow back the 10 steps it had descended on Ahaz’s stairway.

## 2 Kings 20:12

Merodach-baladan son of Baladan, Babylon’s king, heard of Hezekiah’s illness. 📖

He sent letters and a gift.

## 2 Kings 20:13

Hezekiah welcomed the messengers and showed his entire treasure house: silver, gold, spices, precious oil, and weapons. 📖

He showed everything in his storehouses.

Nothing in his palace or whole kingdom was left unseen.

## 2 Kings 20:14

Isaiah asked Hezekiah, “Where did those men come from, and what did they say?” 📖

He answered, “A distant land, Babylon.”

## 2 Kings 20:15

Isaiah asked, “What did they see in your palace?” 📖

Hezekiah answered, “Everything.

I showed them all my treasures.”

## 2 Kings 20:16

Isaiah told Hezekiah, “Hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## 2 Kings 20:17

The time will come when everything in your palace and everything your ancestors stored until now will go to Babylon. 📖

The LORD says nothing will remain.

## 2 Kings 20:18

Some of your own descendants will be taken away as eunuchs in Babylon’s king’s palace.” 📖

**Help with eunuchs:** These were palace servants.

The word can refer to men who had been castrated.

## 2 Kings 20:19

Hezekiah told Isaiah, “The LORD’s word you spoke is good.” 📖

He thought, “Will there not at least be peace and safety during my lifetime?”

## 2 Kings 20:20

Hezekiah’s other acts, power, and construction of a pool and water tunnel into the city are recorded. 📖

They are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah.

## 2 Kings 20:21

Hezekiah died, and his son Manasseh became king. 📖
