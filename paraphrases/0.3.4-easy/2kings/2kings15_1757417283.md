# 2 Kings 15 — Kings and conspiracies

**About this chapter**

This chapter follows kings of Judah and Israel. | It records short reigns, assassinations, tribute, and Assyrian conquests. | Dates and family names distinguish the rulers.

## 2 Kings 15:1

Azariah son of Amaziah became Judah’s king in year 27 of Jeroboam’s reign over Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:2

He became king at 16 and ruled in Jerusalem for 52 years. 📖

His mother was Jecoliah from Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 15:3

He did right before the LORD, like his father Amaziah. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:4

But the high places remained, and people sacrificed and burned incense there. 📖

**Help with high places:** These were worship sites, often on hills.

## 2 Kings 15:5

The LORD struck the king with leprosy until his death. 📖

He lived in a separate house.

His son Jotham managed the palace and governed the people.

**Help with leprosy:** This Bible term covers serious skin diseases.

The verse describes a particular divine action, not a general explanation for illness.

## 2 Kings 15:6

Azariah’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:7

Azariah died and was buried near his ancestors in the City of David. 📖

His son Jotham became king.

## 2 Kings 15:8

Zechariah son of Jeroboam became Israel’s king in year 38 of Azariah’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 6 months.

## 2 Kings 15:9

He did evil before the LORD like his ancestors. 📖

He continued the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit.

## 2 Kings 15:10

Shallum son of Jabesh plotted against Zechariah and killed him before the people. 📖

Shallum became king.

## 2 Kings 15:11

Zechariah’s other acts are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:12

The LORD’s word to Jehu was fulfilled. 📖

“4 generations of your sons will sit on Israel’s throne.”

## 2 Kings 15:13

Shallum son of Jabesh became king in year 39 of Uzziah’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 1 full month.

**About Uzziah:** This is another name for Azariah.

## 2 Kings 15:14

Menahem son of Gadi came from Tirzah to Samaria. 📖

He killed Shallum son of Jabesh and became king.

## 2 Kings 15:15

Shallum’s other acts and conspiracy are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:16

Menahem attacked Tiphsah and everyone nearby, starting from Tirzah, because they refused to open their gates. 📖

He attacked Tiphsah and ripped open all the pregnant women.

**About this violence:** The account reports an atrocity; it does not instruct readers to do it.

## 2 Kings 15:17

Menahem son of Gadi became Israel’s king in year 39 of Azariah’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 10 years.

## 2 Kings 15:18

He did evil before the LORD. 📖

Throughout his reign, he continued the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit.

## 2 Kings 15:19

Pul king of Assyria invaded. 📖

Menahem gave him 1,000 talents of silver to gain his support and secure his rule.

**Help with talent:** This was a large unit of weight.

## 2 Kings 15:20

Menahem forced each wealthy Israelite to pay 50 silver shekels for Assyria’s king. 📖

Assyria’s king withdrew and did not stay in the land.

**Help with shekel:** This was a unit of weight used for silver.

## 2 Kings 15:21

Menahem’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:22

Menahem died, and his son Pekahiah became king. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:23

Pekahiah son of Menahem became Israel’s king in year 50 of Azariah’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 2 years.

## 2 Kings 15:24

He did evil before the LORD and continued the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:25

His officer Pekah son of Remaliah plotted against him with Argob, Arieh, and 50 Gilead men. 📖

Pekah killed Pekahiah in the Samaria palace’s citadel and became king.

**Help with citadel:** This is a strongly defended part of a building or city.

## 2 Kings 15:26

Pekahiah’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:27

Pekah son of Remaliah became Israel’s king in year 52 of Azariah’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 20 years.

## 2 Kings 15:28

He did evil before the LORD and continued the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:29

During Pekah’s reign, Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria captured Ijon, Abel-beth-maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead, and Galilee. 📖

This included all Naphtali’s land.

He took the people captive to Assyria.

## 2 Kings 15:30

Hoshea son of Elah plotted against Pekah son of Remaliah. 📖

In year 20 of Jotham son of Uzziah, Hoshea attacked and killed Pekah and became king.

## 2 Kings 15:31

Pekah’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:32

Jotham son of Uzziah became Judah’s king in year 2 of Pekah son of Remaliah’s reign over Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:33

He became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years. 📖

His mother was Jerusha daughter of Zadok.

## 2 Kings 15:34

He did right before the LORD, like his father Uzziah. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:35

But the high places remained, and people sacrificed and burned incense there. 📖

Jotham rebuilt the Upper Gate of the LORD’s house.

## 2 Kings 15:36

Jotham’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:37

Then the LORD began sending Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 15:38

Jotham died and was buried with his ancestors in his ancestor David’s city. 📖

His son Ahaz became king.
