# 2 Kings 25 — Jerusalem falls and Judah is exiled

**About this chapter**

Babylon breaks into Jerusalem and destroys its buildings. | Zedekiah is captured, and people and temple objects are taken away. | The account ends with Jehoiachin’s release from prison.

## 2 Kings 25:1

In Zedekiah’s 9th year, month 10, day 10, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came against Jerusalem with his whole army. 📖

They camped outside and built a siege wall around it.

**Help with siege wall:** This surrounds a city to prevent escape and supplies.

## 2 Kings 25:2

The city remained under siege until Zedekiah’s 11th year. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:3

By month 4, day 9, famine was so severe that the people had no food. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:4

The city’s defences were broken. 📖

Though Chaldeans surrounded it, all the soldiers fled at night towards the Arabah.

They used the gate between the 2 walls near the king’s garden.

**Help with Chaldeans:** Here this refers to the Babylonian forces.

## 2 Kings 25:5

The Chaldean army pursued the king and caught him on Jericho’s plains. 📖

His whole army deserted him.

## 2 Kings 25:6

They captured him and brought him to Babylon’s king at Riblah, where judgment was pronounced. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:7

They killed Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes. 📖

Then they put out his eyes, bound him with bronze chains, and took him to Babylon.

## 2 Kings 25:8

Nebuzaradan, guard captain and servant of Babylon’s king, entered Jerusalem in year 19 of Nebuchadnezzar’s reign. 📖

It was month 5, day 7.

## 2 Kings 25:9

He burned the LORD’s house, royal palace, and all Jerusalem’s houses, every important building. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:10

The whole Chaldean army under the guard captain demolished Jerusalem’s surrounding walls. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:11

Nebuzaradan the guard captain exiled the people left in the city and those who deserted to Babylon’s king. 📖

He also exiled the rest of the population.

## 2 Kings 25:12

He left some of the poorest people to tend vineyards and fields. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:13

The Chaldeans broke the bronze pillars, stands, and Sea in the LORD’s house. 📖

They took the bronze to Babylon.

**Help with Sea:** This was the temple’s large basin.

## 2 Kings 25:14

They took pots, shovels, wick trimmers, dishes, and every bronze object used in temple service. 📖

**Help with wick trimmers:** These were tools for trimming lamps’ wicks.

## 2 Kings 25:15

The guard captain took incense burners and sprinkling bowls, everything of pure gold or fine silver. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:16

The bronze of the 2 pillars, Sea, and movable stands Solomon made for the LORD’s house was beyond weighing. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:17

Each pillar was 18 cubits tall. 📖

Its bronze capital was 3 cubits high, with bronze pomegranates in a network around it.

The other pillar and its network were similar.

**Help with these words:** A cubit was roughly a forearm’s length.

A capital is the decorated top of a pillar.

Pomegranates are fruits; here the word describes decorations shaped like them.

## 2 Kings 25:18

The guard captain took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second-ranking priest, and 3 doorkeepers. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:19

From the city, he took the military court official, 5 royal advisers, and 60 other men. 📖

He also took the army commander’s scribe, who enlisted the people.

## 2 Kings 25:20

Nebuzaradan the guard captain brought them to Babylon’s king at Riblah. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:21

Babylon’s king killed them at Riblah in Hamath. 📖

Judah was exiled from its own land.

## 2 Kings 25:22

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, grandson of Shaphan, over the people left in Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:23

The army commanders and men heard that Babylon’s king appointed Gedaliah governor and came to him at Mizpah. 📖

They included Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, and Seraiah son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite.

Jaazaniah son of the Maacathite also came, along with their men.

## 2 Kings 25:24

Gedaliah swore an oath to them and their men. 📖

“Do not fear the Chaldeans’ servants.

Live here and serve Babylon’s king, and all will go well.”

**Help with oath:** This is a serious sworn promise.

## 2 Kings 25:25

In month 7, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, grandson of Elishama, came with 10 men. 📖

He belonged to the royal family.

He killed Gedaliah and the Judeans and Chaldeans with him at Mizpah.

## 2 Kings 25:26

All the people, small and great, and the army commanders fled to Egypt, fearing the Chaldeans. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:27

Evil-merodach became Babylon’s king in year 37 of Judah’s King Jehoiachin’s exile. 📖

That year, on month 12, day 27, he released Jehoiachin from prison.

## 2 Kings 25:28

He spoke kindly to Jehoiachin and placed his throne above those of the other kings with him in Babylon. 📖

## 2 Kings 25:29

Jehoiachin changed out of prison clothes. 📖

He ate regularly at the king’s table for the rest of his life.

## 2 Kings 25:30

The king gave Jehoiachin a daily allowance for the rest of his life. 📖
