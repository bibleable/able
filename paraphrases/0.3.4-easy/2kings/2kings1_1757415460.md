# 2 Kings 1 — Ahaziah and Elijah

**About this chapter**

Ahaziah asks another god about his injury. | Elijah brings the LORD’s answer. | Soldiers are sent to bring Elijah to the king.

## 2 Kings 1:1

After Ahab died, Moab rebelled against Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 1:2

Ahaziah fell through the lattice of his upstairs room in Samaria and was injured. 📖

He sent messengers to Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god.

“Ask whether I will recover from this injury.”

**Help with lattice:** This is a framework of crossed strips.

## 2 Kings 1:3

The LORD’s angel told Elijah the Tishbite to meet the king of Samaria’s messengers. 📖

“Ask whether they seek Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god, because there is no God in Israel.

**Help with angel:** An angel is a messenger from God.

## 2 Kings 1:4

The LORD says, ‘You will not rise from your bed. 📖

You will certainly die.’”

Elijah went.

## 2 Kings 1:5

The messengers returned, and the king asked, “Why have you returned?” 📖

## 2 Kings 1:6

They answered, “A man met us and told us to return to you. 📖

He said, ‘The LORD asks why you send men to Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god.

Is there no God in Israel?

You will not rise from your bed.

You will certainly die.’”

## 2 Kings 1:7

The king asked, “What was the man like who met you and said this?” 📖

## 2 Kings 1:8

They answered, “He was hairy, with a leather belt around his waist.” 📖

The king said, “It was Elijah the Tishbite.”

## 2 Kings 1:9

Ahaziah sent a captain with 50 men to Elijah. 📖

Elijah sat on a hilltop.

The captain said, “Man of God, the king orders you to come down!”

## 2 Kings 1:10

Elijah answered, “If I am a man of God, may fire from heaven burn up you and your 50 men.” 📖

Fire came from heaven and burned up the captain and his 50 men.

## 2 Kings 1:11

The king sent another captain with 50 men. 📖

He said, “Man of God, the king orders you to come down at once!”

## 2 Kings 1:12

Elijah again answered, “If I am a man of God, may fire from heaven burn you and your 50 men.” 📖

God’s fire came from heaven and burned up the captain and his 50 men.

## 2 Kings 1:13

The king sent a 3rd captain with 50 men. 📖

He went up, knelt before Elijah, and begged him.

“Man of God, please value my life and the lives of these 50 servants of yours.

## 2 Kings 1:14

Fire from heaven burned up the first 2 captains and all their men. 📖

But please value my life.”

## 2 Kings 1:15

The LORD’s angel told Elijah, “Go down with him. 📖

Do not fear him.”

Elijah went down with him to the king.

## 2 Kings 1:16

Elijah told Ahaziah, “The LORD asks, ‘Is there no God in Israel whose word you can seek? 📖

Is that why you sent men to Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god?’

You will not rise from your bed.

You will certainly die.”

## 2 Kings 1:17

Ahaziah died as the LORD said through Elijah. 📖

He had no son, so Jehoram succeeded him.

This was year 2 of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat’s reign over Judah.

**About these kings:** The source names two kings Jehoram here, one in Israel and one in Judah.

## 2 Kings 1:18

Ahaziah’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
