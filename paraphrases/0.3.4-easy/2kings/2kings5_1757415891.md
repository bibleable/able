# 2 Kings 5 — Naaman is healed and Gehazi deceives

**About this chapter**

An Israelite captive girl tells Naaman’s wife about Elisha. | Naaman is healed after washing in the Jordan. | Gehazi secretly seeks a gift and lies about it.

## 2 Kings 5:1

Naaman commanded Aram’s king’s army. 📖

His master greatly respected him, because the LORD gave Aram victory through him.

He was a brave warrior, but he had leprosy.

**Help with leprosy:** This Bible word covers serious skin diseases.

It does not mean a person has less worth.

## 2 Kings 5:2

Aramean raiders captured a young girl from Israel. 📖

She served Naaman’s wife.

## 2 Kings 5:3

She told her mistress, “If only my master visited the prophet in Samaria! 📖

He would cure his leprosy.”

## 2 Kings 5:4

Naaman told his master what the girl from Israel said. 📖

## 2 Kings 5:5

Aram’s king said, “Go. 📖

I will send a letter to Israel’s king.”

Naaman took 10 talents of silver, 6,000 shekels of gold, and 10 sets of clothes.

**Help with talents and shekels:** These were units of weight used for valuable metals.

## 2 Kings 5:6

His letter to Israel’s king said, “I send my servant Naaman to you with this letter. 📖

Cure his leprosy.”

## 2 Kings 5:7

Israel’s king read it and tore his clothes. 📖

“Am I God, able to kill and give life?

Why does he expect me to cure someone’s leprosy?

See how he seeks a quarrel!”

**Help with torn clothes:** This expressed distress.

## 2 Kings 5:8

Elisha the man of God heard that Israel’s king tore his clothes. 📖

He sent word, “Why have you torn your clothes?

Let the man come to me.

He will know there is a prophet in Israel.”

## 2 Kings 5:9

Naaman arrived with horses and chariots and stood at Elisha’s door. 📖

## 2 Kings 5:10

Elisha sent a messenger to say, “Wash yourself 7 times in the Jordan. 📖

Your skin will be restored, and you will be clean.”

**Help with clean:** Here this concerns healing and the religious status associated with skin disease.

It does not mean washing away moral guilt.

## 2 Kings 5:11

Naaman left angrily. 📖

“I thought he would come out, stand, and call on the LORD his God.

I thought he would wave his hand over the spot and cure my leprosy.

## 2 Kings 5:12

Are Damascus’s rivers, Abanah and Pharpar, not better than all Israel’s waters? 📖

Could I not wash there and become clean?”

He turned away in a rage.

## 2 Kings 5:13

His servants approached him. 📖

“My father, if the prophet commanded something great, would you not do it?

How much more when he says, ‘Wash and become clean’?”

**Help with my father:** This is a respectful way to address Naaman.

## 2 Kings 5:14

Naaman dipped himself in the Jordan 7 times, as the man of God said. 📖

His skin became like a little child’s skin, and he was clean.

## 2 Kings 5:15

Naaman returned with all his attendants and stood before the man of God. 📖

“Now I know there is no God anywhere except in Israel.

Please accept a gift from your servant.”

## 2 Kings 5:16

Elisha said, “As surely as the LORD, whom I serve, lives, I will not accept it.” 📖

Naaman urged him, but he refused.

## 2 Kings 5:17

Naaman said, “If you refuse, let me take as much soil as 2 mules can carry. 📖

I will never again offer burnt offerings or sacrifices to any god except the LORD.

## 2 Kings 5:18

May the LORD forgive me one thing. 📖

My master leans on my arm when he worships in Rimmon’s temple.

When I bow there with him, may the LORD forgive me.”

## 2 Kings 5:19

Elisha said, “Go in peace.” 📖

Naaman travelled a short distance.

## 2 Kings 5:20

Gehazi, Elisha’s servant, said, “My master let Naaman the Aramean go without accepting his gifts. 📖

As surely as the LORD lives, I will run after him and get something.”

## 2 Kings 5:21

Gehazi ran after Naaman. 📖

Naaman saw him and got out of his chariot to meet him.

“Is everything all right?”

## 2 Kings 5:22

Gehazi answered, “Yes. 📖

My master says 2 young men from the prophetic group just arrived from Ephraim’s hills.

Please give them 1 talent of silver and 2 sets of clothes.”

**About Gehazi’s claim:** Gehazi is making this request falsely, as the later conversation shows.

## 2 Kings 5:23

Naaman urged him, “Take 2 talents.” 📖

He tied the silver in 2 bags with 2 sets of clothes.

He gave them to 2 servants, who carried them ahead of Gehazi.

## 2 Kings 5:24

At the hill, Gehazi took the gifts and stored them in the house. 📖

He sent the servants away.

## 2 Kings 5:25

Gehazi stood before his master. 📖

Elisha asked, “Where have you been?”

He answered, “Your servant went nowhere.”

## 2 Kings 5:26

Elisha said, “Did my spirit not go with you when the man stepped from his chariot to meet you? 📖

Is this the time to accept money, clothes, olive groves, vineyards, sheep, oxen, male servants, or female servants?

## 2 Kings 5:27

Naaman’s leprosy will cling to you and your descendants forever!” 📖

Gehazi left with leprosy, white as snow.

**About this judgment:** The text connects Gehazi’s disease with this particular judgment.

It does not say all illness results from a person’s wrongdoing.
