# 2 Kings 13 — Israel’s kings and Elisha’s final acts

**About this chapter**

Jehoahaz and Jehoash rule Israel while Aram oppresses it. | Elisha gives a sign of victory before his death. | The LORD shows compassion because of his covenant.

## 2 Kings 13:1

Jehoahaz son of Jehu became Israel’s king in year 23 of Joash son of Ahaziah’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 17 years.

## 2 Kings 13:2

He did evil before the LORD. 📖

He continued the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit and did not turn from them.

## 2 Kings 13:3

The LORD’s anger burned against Israel. 📖

He repeatedly handed them over to Hazael king of Aram and his son Ben-hadad.

## 2 Kings 13:4

Jehoahaz sought the LORD’s favour. 📖

The LORD heard him because he saw how Aram’s king oppressed Israel.

## 2 Kings 13:5

The LORD gave Israel a rescuer. 📖

They escaped Aram’s power and lived in their homes as before.

## 2 Kings 13:6

But they kept the sins Jeroboam’s family caused Israel to commit. 📖

Even the Asherah pole remained in Samaria.

**Help with Asherah pole:** This was an object associated with worship of the goddess Asherah.

## 2 Kings 13:7

Jehoahaz had only 50 horsemen, 10 chariots, and 10,000 foot soldiers left. 📖

Aram’s king destroyed the army and made it like dust at threshing.

**Help with this picture:** Threshing separates grain from stalks.

The comparison describes the army’s crushing defeat.

## 2 Kings 13:8

Jehoahaz’s other acts, achievements, and power are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 13:9

Jehoahaz died and was buried in Samaria. 📖

His son Jehoash became king.

## 2 Kings 13:10

Jehoash son of Jehoahaz became Israel’s king in Samaria in year 37 of Joash’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled for 16 years.

## 2 Kings 13:11

He did evil before the LORD and continued all the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit. 📖

## 2 Kings 13:12

Jehoash’s other acts, achievements, power, and war against Judah’s King Amaziah are recorded. 📖

They are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

## 2 Kings 13:13

Jehoash died, and Jeroboam succeeded him. 📖

Jehoash was buried in Samaria with Israel’s kings.

## 2 Kings 13:14

Elisha became ill with the sickness that would kill him. 📖

Jehoash king of Israel visited and wept.

“My father, my father, Israel’s chariots and horsemen!”

**Help with this address:** The king honours Elisha as a protector of Israel.

## 2 Kings 13:15

Elisha said, “Take a bow and arrows.” 📖

Jehoash did.

## 2 Kings 13:16

Elisha told Israel’s king to put his hand on the bow. 📖

The king did, and Elisha put his hands over the king’s.

## 2 Kings 13:17

Elisha told him to open the east window and shoot. 📖

He did both.

Elisha said, “This is the LORD’s arrow of victory over Aram.

You will strike the Arameans at Aphek until you destroy them.”

## 2 Kings 13:18

Elisha told him to take the arrows and strike the ground. 📖

The king struck it 3 times and stopped.

## 2 Kings 13:19

The man of God was angry. 📖

“You should have struck 5 or 6 times.

Then you would have destroyed Aram.

Now you will defeat Aram only 3 times.”

## 2 Kings 13:20

Elisha died and was buried. 📖

Moabite raiders used to enter the land each spring.

## 2 Kings 13:21

Israelites burying a man saw raiders and threw the body into Elisha’s tomb. 📖

The body touched Elisha’s bones.

The man came to life and stood up.

## 2 Kings 13:22

Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel throughout Jehoahaz’s reign. 📖

## 2 Kings 13:23

The LORD showed Israel grace and compassion because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

To this day, he has not wished to destroy them or remove them from his presence.

**Help with covenant:** This is a binding agreement or promise.

“To this day” refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Kings 13:24

Hazael king of Aram died, and his son Ben-hadad became king. 📖

## 2 Kings 13:25

Jehoash son of Jehoahaz recovered cities from Ben-hadad son of Hazael. 📖

Hazael had captured them from Jehoahaz in battle.

Jehoash defeated Ben-hadad 3 times and recovered Israel’s cities.
