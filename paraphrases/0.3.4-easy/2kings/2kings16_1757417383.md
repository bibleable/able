# 2 Kings 16 — Ahaz follows other worship

**About this chapter**

Ahaz seeks Assyria’s help against Aram and Israel. | He copies an altar from Damascus and changes the temple arrangements. | The chapter also records his son’s sacrifice in fire.

## 2 Kings 16:1

Ahaz son of Jotham became Judah’s king in year 17 of Pekah son of Remaliah. 📖

## 2 Kings 16:2

Ahaz became king at 20 and ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years. 📖

Unlike his ancestor David, he did not do right before the LORD his God.

## 2 Kings 16:3

He followed Israel’s kings and even sacrificed his son in fire. 📖

He followed the detestable practices of nations the LORD drove out before Israel.

## 2 Kings 16:4

He sacrificed and burned incense at high places, on hills, and under every green tree. 📖

**Help with high places:** These were sites used for worship.

## 2 Kings 16:5

Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah, Israel’s king, attacked Jerusalem. 📖

They surrounded Ahaz but could not defeat him.

## 2 Kings 16:6

Rezin recovered Elath for Aram and drove out Judah’s men. 📖

He sent Edomites to live there, where they remain to this day.

**Help with to this day:** This means the account’s time.

## 2 Kings 16:7

Ahaz sent word to Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria. 📖

“I am your servant and son.

Save me from Aram’s and Israel’s kings, who attack me.”

**Help with servant and son:** Ahaz uses these words to express submission.

## 2 Kings 16:8

Ahaz took silver and gold from the LORD’s house and palace treasuries. 📖

He sent it as a gift to Assyria’s king.

## 2 Kings 16:9

Assyria’s king responded. 📖

He captured Damascus, took its people captive to Kir, and killed Rezin.

## 2 Kings 16:10

Ahaz visited Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria in Damascus. 📖

He saw its altar and sent Uriah the priest a model and complete building plans.

## 2 Kings 16:11

Uriah built the altar exactly as Ahaz’s instructions from Damascus required. 📖

He finished before Ahaz returned.

## 2 Kings 16:12

Ahaz returned from Damascus, saw the altar, approached it, and offered sacrifices there. 📖

## 2 Kings 16:13

He offered his burnt offering and grain offering and poured his drink offering. 📖

He splashed his peace offerings’ blood on the altar.

**Help with these offerings:** These were different kinds of gifts offered in worship.

## 2 Kings 16:14

He moved the bronze altar from before the LORD. 📖

It had stood between the new altar and the LORD’s house.

He placed it north of the new altar.

## 2 Kings 16:15

Ahaz told Uriah, “Use the great altar for the morning burnt offering and evening grain offering. 📖

Offer the king’s burnt and grain offerings there.

Also offer all the people’s burnt, grain, and drink offerings there.

Splash all burnt offerings’ and sacrifices’ blood on it.

I will use the bronze altar to seek guidance.”

## 2 Kings 16:16

Uriah the priest obeyed King Ahaz completely. 📖

## 2 Kings 16:17

Ahaz cut the movable stands’ frames and removed their bronze basins. 📖

He took the Sea off its bronze oxen and put it on a stone base.

**Help with Sea:** This was the large basin used at the temple.

## 2 Kings 16:18

Because of Assyria’s king, he removed the Sabbath canopy in the temple and closed the outside royal entrance. 📖

**Help with canopy:** This is an overhead covering.

Sabbath was the weekly rest day.

## 2 Kings 16:19

Ahaz’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 16:20

Ahaz died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. 📖

His son Hezekiah became king.
