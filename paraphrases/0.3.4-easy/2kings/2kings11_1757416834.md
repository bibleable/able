# 2 Kings 11 — Joash is protected and made king

**About this chapter**

Athaliah tries to destroy the royal heirs. | Joash is hidden for six years. | Jehoiada then arranges his crowning and renews the covenant.

## 2 Kings 11:1

Athaliah, Ahaziah’s mother, saw her son was dead. 📖

She set about destroying all the royal heirs.

## 2 Kings 11:2

Jehosheba, King Joram’s daughter and Ahaziah’s sister, rescued Joash son of Ahaziah from the king’s sons being murdered. 📖

She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom from Athaliah.

He was not killed.

## 2 Kings 11:3

Joash and his nurse hid in the LORD’s house for 6 years while Athaliah ruled. 📖

## 2 Kings 11:4

In year 7, Jehoiada summoned commanders of hundreds, Carites, and guards to the LORD’s house. 📖

He made a covenant with them and put them under oath.

He showed them the king’s son.

**Help with covenant and oath:** A covenant is a binding agreement.

An oath is a serious sworn promise.

## 2 Kings 11:5

He ordered, “One-third of those starting Sabbath duty must guard the palace. 📖

**Help with Sabbath:** This was the weekly day of rest.

## 2 Kings 11:6

One-third must guard Sur Gate and one-third the gate behind the guards. 📖

Take turns guarding the temple.

## 2 Kings 11:7

The 2 divisions ending Sabbath duty must guard the LORD’s house for the king. 📖

## 2 Kings 11:8

Surround the king with weapons ready. 📖

Kill anyone approaching your ranks.

Stay near the king wherever he goes.”

## 2 Kings 11:9

The commanders of hundreds obeyed Jehoiada the priest. 📖

Each brought his men starting and ending Sabbath duty to him.

## 2 Kings 11:10

The priest gave the commanders King David’s spears and shields kept in the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Kings 11:11

The armed guards surrounded the king by the altar and temple, stretching from the temple’s south side to its north. 📖

## 2 Kings 11:12

Jehoiada brought out the king’s son, crowned him, gave him the Testimony, and proclaimed him king. 📖

They anointed him.

People clapped and shouted, “Long live the king!”

**Help with Testimony:** This refers to the written covenant law.

Anointing involved pouring oil to mark appointment.

## 2 Kings 11:13

Athaliah heard the guards and people. 📖

She went to the people in the LORD’s house.

## 2 Kings 11:14

She saw the king by the pillar, as was customary. 📖

Officers and trumpeters stood beside him.

All the people rejoiced and blew trumpets.

Athaliah tore her clothes and screamed, “Treason!

Treason!”

**Help with treason:** This means betrayal of a ruler or country.

## 2 Kings 11:15

Jehoiada the priest ordered the army’s commanders, “Take her out between the ranks. 📖

Kill anyone following her.”

He had said she must not be killed in the LORD’s house.

## 2 Kings 11:16

They seized Athaliah at the horses’ entrance to the palace grounds and killed her there. 📖

## 2 Kings 11:17

Jehoiada made a covenant between the LORD, the king, and the people: they would be the LORD’s people. 📖

He also made a covenant between king and people.

## 2 Kings 11:18

All the people tore down Baal’s temple and smashed its altars and idols. 📖

They killed Mattan, Baal’s priest, before the altars.

Jehoiada placed guards at the LORD’s house.

## 2 Kings 11:19

He took commanders of hundreds, Carites, guards, and all the people with him. 📖

They brought the king from the LORD’s house into the palace through the Gate of the Guards.

Joash sat on the royal throne.

## 2 Kings 11:20

All the people rejoiced. 📖

The city was quiet because Athaliah had been killed at the palace.

## 2 Kings 11:21

Joash was 7 years old when he became king. 📖
