# 2 Kings 12 — Joash repairs the temple

**About this chapter**

Joash arranges repairs to the LORD’s house. | The chapter describes how money is collected and paid to workers. | Later Joash gives treasures to Hazael and is killed by servants.

## 2 Kings 12:1

Joash became king in Jehu’s 7th year and ruled in Jerusalem for 40 years. 📖

His mother was Zibiah from Beersheba.

## 2 Kings 12:2

Joash did right before the LORD throughout the time Jehoiada the priest instructed him. 📖

## 2 Kings 12:3

But the high places remained. 📖

People still sacrificed and burned incense there.

**Help with high places:** These were worship sites, often on hills.

## 2 Kings 12:4

Joash told the priests, “Collect all money brought as sacred gifts into the LORD’s house. 📖

Include census money, money from vows, and voluntary gifts.

**Help with census and vows:** A census counts people.

Vows are serious promises.

## 2 Kings 12:5

Each priest must receive money from the people he serves. 📖

Use it to repair all damage found in the temple.”

## 2 Kings 12:6

By Joash’s 23rd year, the priests still had not repaired the temple. 📖

## 2 Kings 12:7

Joash called Jehoiada and the other priests. 📖

“Why have you not repaired the temple?

Stop taking money from the people you serve.

Hand it over for temple repairs.”

## 2 Kings 12:8

The priests agreed to stop receiving money from the people and to stop handling repairs themselves. 📖

## 2 Kings 12:9

Jehoiada bored a hole in a chest’s lid. 📖

He placed it beside the altar, on the right when entering the LORD’s house.

The priests guarding the entrance put all the money brought to the LORD’s house there.

## 2 Kings 12:10

Whenever the chest held much money, the royal scribe and high priest came to count it. 📖

They tied the money brought into the LORD’s house in bags.

## 2 Kings 12:11

They gave the counted money to supervisors of work on the LORD’s house. 📖

These paid the carpenters and builders,

## 2 Kings 12:12

masons and stonecutters. 📖

They bought timber and cut stone for repairs and paid the other repair costs.

**Help with masons:** These are workers who build with stone.

## 2 Kings 12:13

They did not use this money for silver basins, wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, trumpets, or gold or silver temple objects. 📖

**Help with wick trimmers:** These were tools for trimming lamps’ wicks.

## 2 Kings 12:14

They paid it to the workers, who repaired the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Kings 12:15

They required no accounts from those receiving money to pay workers, because these men acted honestly. 📖

## 2 Kings 12:16

Money from guilt offerings and sin offerings belonged to the priests. 📖

It was not brought into the LORD’s house.

**Help with these offerings:** These were offerings prescribed in the law for dealing with wrongdoing.

## 2 Kings 12:17

Hazael king of Aram attacked and captured Gath. 📖

Then he decided to attack Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 12:18

Joash king of Judah took sacred objects dedicated by Judah’s kings Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, and his own dedicated objects. 📖

He also took all gold from the LORD’s house’s treasuries and the palace treasuries.

He sent everything to Hazael king of Aram, who withdrew from Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 12:19

Joash’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 12:20

His servants plotted against him and killed him at Beth-millo on the road down to Silla. 📖

## 2 Kings 12:21

His servants Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer struck him, and he died. 📖

They buried him with his ancestors in the City of David.

His son Amaziah became king.
