# 2 Kings 10 — Jehu destroys Ahab’s family and Baal worship

**About this chapter**

Jehu orders killings of Ahab’s sons and others. | He deceives Baal’s worshippers and destroys their temple. | He nevertheless continues the worship associated with Jeroboam.

## 2 Kings 10:1

Ahab had 70 sons in Samaria. 📖

Jehu sent letters there to Jezreel’s officials, elders, and the sons’ guardians.

## 2 Kings 10:2

“Your master’s sons are with you. 📖

You have chariots, horses, weapons, and a defended city.

## 2 Kings 10:3

Choose your master’s best and most worthy son. 📖

Put him on his father’s throne and fight for your master’s family.”

## 2 Kings 10:4

They were terrified. 📖

“If 2 kings could not withstand him, how can we?”

## 2 Kings 10:5

The palace administrator, city overseer, elders, and guardians sent word to Jehu. 📖

“We are your servants and will obey.

We will appoint no king.

Do what you think good.”

## 2 Kings 10:6

Jehu sent a 2nd letter. 📖

“If you support and obey me, bring your master’s sons’ heads to Jezreel by this time tomorrow.”

Leading men of the city were raising the king’s 70 sons.

## 2 Kings 10:7

They received the letter and killed all 70 sons. 📖

They put their heads in baskets and sent them to Jehu at Jezreel.

## 2 Kings 10:8

A messenger told Jehu the heads had arrived. 📖

He ordered, “Put them in 2 piles at the gate entrance until morning.”

## 2 Kings 10:9

Next morning, Jehu told all the people, “You are innocent. 📖

I plotted against my master and killed him.

But who killed all these?

## 2 Kings 10:10

No word the LORD spoke against Ahab’s family will fail. 📖

The LORD has done what he promised through his servant Elijah.”

## 2 Kings 10:11

Jehu killed all Ahab’s remaining family in Jezreel, all his important men, close friends, and priests. 📖

He left no survivor.

## 2 Kings 10:12

Jehu travelled towards Samaria. 📖

At Beth-eked of the Shepherds,

## 2 Kings 10:13

he met relatives of Ahaziah king of Judah and asked who they were. 📖

They answered, “We are Ahaziah’s relatives, visiting the sons of the king and queen mother.”

## 2 Kings 10:14

Jehu ordered their capture alive. 📖

His men captured them, then killed all 42 at Beth-eked’s well.

He spared nobody.

## 2 Kings 10:15

Jehu left and met Jehonadab son of Rechab. 📖

He greeted him and asked, “Is your heart true to me as mine is to you?”

“Yes,” Jehonadab answered.

Jehu said, “Then give me your hand.”

He did, and Jehu helped him into the chariot.

## 2 Kings 10:16

Jehu said, “Come and see my zeal for the LORD!” 📖

Jehonadab rode with him.

**Help with zeal:** This means strong commitment.

## 2 Kings 10:17

In Samaria, Jehu killed everyone remaining who belonged to Ahab. 📖

He destroyed them as the LORD told Elijah.

## 2 Kings 10:18

Jehu gathered all the people. 📖

“Ahab served Baal a little.

Jehu will serve him much!

## 2 Kings 10:19

Call all Baal’s prophets, servants, and priests. 📖

Nobody must be missing, because I have a great sacrifice for Baal.

Anyone absent will die.”

Jehu was deceiving them to destroy Baal’s servants.

## 2 Kings 10:20

Jehu ordered a solemn gathering for Baal, and it was announced. 📖

## 2 Kings 10:21

He sent word throughout Israel. 📖

Every servant of Baal came.

They filled Baal’s temple from end to end.

## 2 Kings 10:22

Jehu told the clothing keeper to bring clothes for all Baal’s servants. 📖

He did.

## 2 Kings 10:23

Jehu and Jehonadab son of Rechab entered Baal’s temple. 📖

Jehu told the worshippers, “Check that only Baal’s servants are here, with no servants of the LORD.”

## 2 Kings 10:24

They entered to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. 📖

Jehu placed 80 men outside.

He warned, “Anyone who lets a man escape will lose his own life instead.”

## 2 Kings 10:25

After the burnt offering, Jehu ordered the guards and officers, “Kill them and let nobody escape.” 📖

They killed them with swords, threw out the bodies, and entered Baal’s temple’s inner room.

## 2 Kings 10:26

They brought out the temple’s sacred pillar and burned it. 📖

## 2 Kings 10:27

They demolished Baal’s sacred pillar and tore down his temple. 📖

They made it a toilet, which it remains to this day.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Kings 10:28

Jehu removed Baal worship from Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 10:29

But he kept the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit. 📖

He continued worship of the gold calves at Bethel and Dan.

## 2 Kings 10:30

The LORD told Jehu, “You did right in my sight and fulfilled my purpose against Ahab’s family. 📖

Because of this, 4 generations of your sons will sit on Israel’s throne.”

## 2 Kings 10:31

Yet Jehu did not carefully follow the LORD, Israel’s God, with all his heart. 📖

He kept the sins Jeroboam caused Israel to commit.

## 2 Kings 10:32

The LORD began reducing Israel’s territory then. 📖

Hazael defeated Israel throughout their territory,

## 2 Kings 10:33

east of the Jordan through Gilead, the land of Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh. 📖

This stretched from Aroer by the Arnon Valley through Gilead to Bashan.

## 2 Kings 10:34

Jehu’s other acts, achievements, and power are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 10:35

Jehu died and was buried in Samaria. 📖

His son Jehoahaz became king.

## 2 Kings 10:36

Jehu ruled Israel in Samaria for 28 years. 📖
