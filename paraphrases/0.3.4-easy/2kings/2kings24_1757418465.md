# 2 Kings 24 — Babylon takes Jerusalem’s king and people

**About this chapter**

Judah comes under Babylon’s power. | Jehoiachin surrenders, and many people and treasures are taken away. | Zedekiah becomes king and rebels.

## 2 Kings 24:1

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon invaded during Jehoiakim’s reign. 📖

Jehoiakim served him for 3 years, then rebelled.

## 2 Kings 24:2

The LORD sent Chaldean, Aramean, Moabite, and Ammonite raiders against Jehoiakim to destroy Judah. 📖

This fulfilled his word through his servants the prophets.

## 2 Kings 24:3

This happened at the LORD’s command to remove Judah from his presence because of Manasseh’s sins and acts, 📖

## 2 Kings 24:4

and the innocent blood he shed. 📖

He filled Jerusalem with innocent blood, and the LORD would not forgive.

## 2 Kings 24:5

Jehoiakim’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:6

Jehoiakim died, and his son Jehoiachin became king. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:7

Egypt’s king no longer left his land with his army. 📖

Babylon’s king took all his territory from the Brook of Egypt to the Euphrates River.

## 2 Kings 24:8

Jehoiachin became king at 18 and ruled in Jerusalem for 3 months. 📖

His mother was Nehushta daughter of Elnathan from Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 24:9

He did evil before the LORD like his father. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:10

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon’s servants surrounded Jerusalem to attack it. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:11

Nebuchadnezzar came while his servants surrounded the city. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:12

Judah’s King Jehoiachin, his mother, servants, commanders, and officials surrendered to Babylon’s king. 📖

Babylon’s king took him captive in the 8th year of his own reign.

## 2 Kings 24:13

As the LORD declared, Nebuchadnezzar took all treasures from the LORD’s house and royal palace. 📖

He cut apart all gold objects Israel’s King Solomon made for the LORD’s temple.

## 2 Kings 24:14

He exiled all Jerusalem: commanders, brave warriors, craftsmen, and metalworkers, 10,000 captives in all. 📖

Only the poorest people remained.

**Help with exiled:** He forced them to leave their homeland.

## 2 Kings 24:15

Nebuchadnezzar took Jehoiachin, his mother, wives, officials, and leading men from Jerusalem into exile in Babylon. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:16

Babylon’s king also exiled 7,000 brave warriors and 1,000 craftsmen and metalworkers to Babylon. 📖

All were strong and fit for battle.

## 2 Kings 24:17

Babylon’s king made Jehoiachin’s uncle Mattaniah king instead and renamed him Zedekiah. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:18

Zedekiah became king at 21 and ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. 📖

His mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.

## 2 Kings 24:19

Zedekiah did evil before the LORD like Jehoiakim. 📖

## 2 Kings 24:20

The LORD’s anger brought these events on Jerusalem and Judah until he banished them from his presence. 📖

Zedekiah rebelled against Babylon’s king.
