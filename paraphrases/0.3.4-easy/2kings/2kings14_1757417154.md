# 2 Kings 14 — Amaziah and Jeroboam

**About this chapter**

Amaziah rules Judah and challenges Israel’s king. | He is defeated and later killed by conspirators. | Jeroboam rules Israel and restores territory.

## 2 Kings 14:1

Amaziah son of Joash became Judah’s king in year 2 of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz’s reign over Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:2

He became king at 25 and ruled in Jerusalem for 29 years. 📖

His mother was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 14:3

He did right before the LORD, though not like his ancestor David. 📖

He followed his father Joash’s example in everything.

## 2 Kings 14:4

The high places remained. 📖

People continued sacrificing and burning incense there.

**Help with high places:** These were worship sites, often on hills.

## 2 Kings 14:5

Once his rule was secure, Amaziah executed the servants who murdered his father the king. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:6

He did not execute the murderers’ sons. 📖

He followed the LORD’s command in the Book of the Law of Moses.

“Do not execute fathers for children or children for fathers.

Each must die for their own sin.”

## 2 Kings 14:7

Amaziah killed 10,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. 📖

He captured Sela and renamed it Joktheel, its name to this day.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Kings 14:8

Amaziah sent messengers to Israel’s king, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, grandson of Jehu. 📖

“Come, let us face each other.”

## 2 Kings 14:9

Jehoash answered Judah’s King Amaziah with a story. 📖

“A Lebanese thistle asked a Lebanese cedar to give his daughter to its son.

A wild animal in Lebanon trampled the thistle.

**Help with this story:** A thistle is a small prickly plant; a cedar is a large tree.

Jehoash warns Amaziah against challenging a stronger opponent.

## 2 Kings 14:10

You defeated Edom and became proud. 📖

Enjoy your glory and stay home.

Why stir up trouble and bring down yourself and Judah?”

## 2 Kings 14:11

Amaziah refused to listen. 📖

Jehoash king of Israel advanced, and they faced each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah.

## 2 Kings 14:12

Israel defeated Judah, and every man fled home. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:13

At Beth-shemesh, Jehoash captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash, grandson of Ahaziah. 📖

Jehoash went to Jerusalem and broke 400 cubits of wall between the Ephraim Gate and Corner Gate.

**Help with cubit:** This was a unit of length, roughly a forearm’s length.

## 2 Kings 14:14

He took all gold, silver, and objects found in the LORD’s house and royal treasuries, and some hostages. 📖

He returned to Samaria.

**Help with hostages:** These are people held to force others to obey.

## 2 Kings 14:15

Jehoash’s other acts, achievements, power, and war against Judah’s King Amaziah are recorded. 📖

They are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

## 2 Kings 14:16

Jehoash died and was buried in Samaria with Israel’s kings. 📖

His son Jeroboam became king.

## 2 Kings 14:17

Amaziah son of Joash, Judah’s king, lived 15 years after Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, Israel’s king, died. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:18

Amaziah’s other acts are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:19

Conspirators plotted against Amaziah in Jerusalem. 📖

He fled to Lachish, but men followed and killed him there.

## 2 Kings 14:20

They carried him back on horses. 📖

They buried him in Jerusalem with his ancestors in the City of David.

## 2 Kings 14:21

All Judah made 16-year-old Azariah king instead of his father Amaziah. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:22

Azariah rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah after King Amaziah died. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:23

Jeroboam son of Jehoash became Israel’s king in year 15 of Amaziah son of Joash’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 41 years.

## 2 Kings 14:24

He did evil before the LORD and continued all the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat caused Israel to commit. 📖

## 2 Kings 14:25

He restored Israel’s border from Lebo-hamath to the Sea of the Arabah. 📖

The LORD, Israel’s God, had promised this through his servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath-hepher.

## 2 Kings 14:26

The LORD saw Israel’s bitter suffering, both slave and free. 📖

Nobody helped Israel.

## 2 Kings 14:27

The LORD had said he would not erase Israel’s name from under heaven. 📖

He saved them through Jeroboam son of Jehoash.

## 2 Kings 14:28

Jeroboam’s other acts, achievements, power, and wars are recorded. 📖

These include recovering Damascus and Hamath for Israel from Judah.

They are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

## 2 Kings 14:29

Jeroboam died and joined his ancestors, Israel’s kings. 📖

His son Zechariah became king.
