# 2 Kings 22 — The Book of the Law is found

**About this chapter**

Josiah orders temple repairs. | Hilkiah finds the Book of the Law, and Josiah hears it read. | Huldah brings the LORD’s answer about judgment and Josiah’s response.

## 2 Kings 22:1

Josiah became king at 8 and ruled in Jerusalem for 31 years. 📖

His mother was Jedidah daughter of Adaiah from Bozkath.

## 2 Kings 22:2

He did right before the LORD and followed all his ancestor David’s ways. 📖

He turned neither right nor left.

**Help with this picture:** He did not stray from the way described.

## 2 Kings 22:3

In his 18th year, Josiah sent Shaphan the scribe, son of Azaliah, grandson of Meshullam, to the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Kings 22:4

“Tell Hilkiah the high priest to count the money brought into the LORD’s house. 📖

The doorkeepers collected it from the people.

## 2 Kings 22:5

Give it to the work supervisors at the LORD’s house. 📖

They must pay the workers repairing the damage,

## 2 Kings 22:6

the carpenters, builders, and masons, and buy timber and cut stone for repairs. 📖

**Help with masons:** These are workers who build with stone.

## 2 Kings 22:7

They need not give accounts of the money, because they work honestly.” 📖

## 2 Kings 22:8

Hilkiah the high priest told Shaphan the scribe, “I found the Book of the Law in the LORD’s house!” 📖

He gave it to Shaphan, who read it.

## 2 Kings 22:9

Shaphan reported to the king, “Your servants paid out the money found in the temple. 📖

They gave it to the workers and supervisors of the LORD’s house.”

## 2 Kings 22:10

Shaphan said Hilkiah the priest had given him a book. 📖

He read it to the king.

## 2 Kings 22:11

The king heard the Book of the Law and tore his clothes. 📖

**Help with torn clothes:** This expressed distress.

## 2 Kings 22:12

He gave orders to Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Achbor son of Micaiah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah. 📖

Asaiah was the king’s servant.

## 2 Kings 22:13

“Ask the LORD about this book for me, the people, and all Judah. 📖

His great anger burns against us.

Our ancestors did not obey this book and do everything written about us.”

## 2 Kings 22:14

Hilkiah, Ahikam, Achbor, Shaphan, and Asaiah visited Huldah the prophetess in Jerusalem’s Second District. 📖

Her husband was Shallum son of Tikvah, grandson of Harhas, the clothing keeper.

**Help with prophetess:** A woman who brings God’s message.

## 2 Kings 22:15

Huldah said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says to tell the man who sent you: 📖

## 2 Kings 22:16

The LORD will bring disaster on this place and its people. 📖

Everything in the book Judah’s king read will happen.

## 2 Kings 22:17

They abandoned me and burned incense to other gods. 📖

Their handiwork angered me.

My anger will burn against this place without being put out.

## 2 Kings 22:18

Tell Judah’s king, who sent you to ask, that the LORD, Israel’s God, says this about what he heard: 📖

## 2 Kings 22:19

Your heart was tender, and you humbled yourself before the LORD. 📖

You heard my warning that this place and its people would become ruined and cursed.

You tore your clothes and wept before me.

I have heard you, declares the LORD.

## 2 Kings 22:20

I will gather you to your ancestors. 📖

You will go to your grave in peace without seeing the disaster I bring on this place.’”

They brought her answer to the king.
