# 2 Kings 9 — Jehu becomes king and kills Joram and Jezebel

**About this chapter**

A prophet anoints Jehu as Israel’s king. | Jehu goes to Jezreel, where Joram is recovering from wounds. | The account describes violent deaths and their connection with earlier prophecy.

## 2 Kings 9:1

Elisha the prophet called a member of the prophetic group. 📖

“Tuck your cloak into your belt, take this flask of oil, and go to Ramoth-gilead.

## 2 Kings 9:2

Find Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, grandson of Nimshi. 📖

Take him away from his companions into an inner room.

## 2 Kings 9:3

Pour the oil on his head. 📖

Say, ‘The LORD anoints you king of Israel.’

Open the door and run without delay!”

**Help with anoints:** Pouring oil on someone marked their appointment to a role.

## 2 Kings 9:4

The young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead. 📖

## 2 Kings 9:5

The army commanders sat there. 📖

He said, “Commander, I have a message for you.”

Jehu asked, “Which of us?”

He answered, “You, commander.”

## 2 Kings 9:6

Jehu entered the house, and the prophet poured oil on his head. 📖

“The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I anoint you king over my people Israel.

## 2 Kings 9:7

Strike down your master Ahab’s family. 📖

I will avenge my prophets and all the LORD’s servants whose blood Jezebel shed.

**Help with avenge:** This means bring punishment for the wrong done.

## 2 Kings 9:8

Ahab’s whole family will perish. 📖

I will remove every male belonging to Ahab in Israel, slave and free.

## 2 Kings 9:9

I will make Ahab’s family like Jeroboam son of Nebat’s and Baasha son of Ahijah’s. 📖

## 2 Kings 9:10

Dogs will eat Jezebel on the ground at Jezreel. 📖

Nobody will bury her.’”

The young prophet opened the door and ran.

## 2 Kings 9:11

Jehu returned to his master’s servants. 📖

They asked, “Is all well?

Why did this madman come?”

He answered, “You know his kind and their chatter.”

**About madman:** This is the servants’ insulting label, not a diagnosis.

## 2 Kings 9:12

They said, “That is a lie! 📖

Tell us!”

Jehu answered, “He said this and that, including, ‘The LORD says I anoint you king over Israel.’”

## 2 Kings 9:13

Each man quickly placed his clothing beneath Jehu on the bare steps. 📖

They blew a ram’s horn and shouted, “Jehu is king!”

## 2 Kings 9:14

Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, grandson of Nimshi, plotted against Joram. 📖

Joram and all Israel had defended Ramoth-gilead against Hazael king of Aram.

## 2 Kings 9:15

Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from Aramean wounds received fighting Hazael. 📖

Jehu told the commanders, “If you want me as king, let nobody escape to report this in Jezreel.”

## 2 Kings 9:16

Jehu rode his chariot to Jezreel, where Joram lay injured. 📖

Ahaziah king of Judah had gone to visit Joram.

## 2 Kings 9:17

A watchman on Jezreel’s tower saw Jehu’s troops. 📖

“Troops are coming!”

Joram ordered a rider sent to ask whether they came in peace.

## 2 Kings 9:18

The rider asked Jehu, “The king asks whether you come in peace.” 📖

Jehu answered, “What do you know about peace?

Follow behind me.”

The watchman reported that the messenger reached them but was not returning.

## 2 Kings 9:19

A 2nd rider asked Jehu, “The king asks whether you come in peace.” 📖

Jehu answered, “What do you know about peace?

Follow behind me.”

## 2 Kings 9:20

The watchman reported, “He reached them but is not returning. 📖

The driver drives like Jehu son of Nimshi, like a madman!”

**About this description:** The watchman is describing Jehu’s wild driving, not diagnosing an illness.

## 2 Kings 9:21

Joram ordered his chariot harnessed. 📖

Israel’s King Joram and Judah’s King Ahaziah each drove out in his own chariot.

They met Jehu on Naboth the Jezreelite’s land.

## 2 Kings 9:22

Joram asked, “Do you come in peace, Jehu?” 📖

Jehu answered, “How can there be peace while your mother Jezebel’s idol worship and witchcraft abound?”

**Help with witchcraft:** This refers to practices seeking supernatural power outside worship of the LORD.

## 2 Kings 9:23

Joram turned and fled, shouting, “Betrayal, Ahaziah!” 📖

## 2 Kings 9:24

Jehu drew his bow and shot Joram between the shoulders. 📖

The arrow pierced his heart, and he collapsed in his chariot.

## 2 Kings 9:25

Jehu told his officer Bidkar, “Throw him into Naboth the Jezreelite’s field. 📖

Remember riding behind his father Ahab with me?

The LORD pronounced this judgment:

## 2 Kings 9:26

‘As surely as I saw Naboth’s blood and his sons’ blood yesterday, I will repay you on this ground. 📖

The LORD declares it.’

Pick him up and throw him on this ground, as the LORD said.”

## 2 Kings 9:27

Ahaziah king of Judah fled towards Beth-haggan. 📖

Jehu pursued and ordered, “Shoot him too!”

They shot him in his chariot on the Ascent of Gur near Ibleam.

He fled to Megiddo and died.

## 2 Kings 9:28

His servants carried him by chariot to Jerusalem. 📖

They buried him with his ancestors in his tomb in the City of David.

## 2 Kings 9:29

Ahaziah had become Judah’s king in year 11 of Joram son of Ahab. 📖

## 2 Kings 9:30

Jezebel heard Jehu had reached Jezreel. 📖

She painted her eyes, decorated her head, and looked from a window.

## 2 Kings 9:31

As he entered the gate, she asked, “Do you come in peace, Zimri, murderer of your master?” 📖

**Help with Zimri:** Jezebel compares Jehu with an earlier man who killed his king.

## 2 Kings 9:32

Jehu looked up and called, “Who is on my side? 📖

Who?”

2 or 3 eunuchs looked down.

**Help with eunuchs:** These were palace officials.

The word can refer to men who had been castrated.

## 2 Kings 9:33

Jehu shouted, “Throw her down!” 📖

They threw her down.

Her blood splashed on the wall and horses as they trampled her.

## 2 Kings 9:34

Jehu went inside and ate and drank. 📖

He said, “Attend to this cursed woman and bury her.

She was a king’s daughter.”

## 2 Kings 9:35

They went to bury her but found only her skull, feet, and palms. 📖

## 2 Kings 9:36

They reported to Jehu. 📖

He said, “The LORD said through his servant Elijah the Tishbite that dogs would eat Jezebel’s flesh at Jezreel.

## 2 Kings 9:37

Her body would lie like dung on Jezreel’s ground. 📖

Nobody could say, ‘This is Jezebel.’”

**Help with dung:** This means animal waste.

The image describes the destruction of her body.
