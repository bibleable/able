# 2 Kings 2 — Elijah is taken up and Elisha continues

**About this chapter**

Elisha stays with Elijah until Elijah is taken into heaven. | Elisha returns across the Jordan and begins his work. | The chapter also describes a curse and a bear attack.

## 2 Kings 2:1

Shortly before the LORD took Elijah to heaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha were travelling from Gilgal. 📖

**Help with whirlwind:** This is a powerful turning wind.

## 2 Kings 2:2

Elijah said, “Stay here, because the LORD sent me to Bethel.” 📖

Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.”

They went to Bethel.

## 2 Kings 2:3

The prophetic group at Bethel asked Elisha, “Do you know the LORD will take your master from you today?” 📖

“Yes,” he answered.

“Do not speak about it.”

**Help with prophetic group:** BSB calls them “sons of the prophets”.

They belong to a group of prophets.

## 2 Kings 2:4

Elijah said, “Stay here, because the LORD sent me to Jericho.” 📖

Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.”

They went to Jericho.

## 2 Kings 2:5

The prophetic group at Jericho asked Elisha, “Do you know the LORD will take your master from you today?” 📖

“Yes,” he answered.

“Do not speak about it.”

## 2 Kings 2:6

Elijah said, “Stay here, because the LORD sent me to the Jordan.” 📖

Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.”

The 2 went on.

## 2 Kings 2:7

50 members of the prophetic group stood some distance away, facing Elijah and Elisha by the Jordan. 📖

## 2 Kings 2:8

Elijah rolled up his cloak and struck the water. 📖

It divided to the right and left, and both crossed on dry ground.

**Help with cloak:** This is an outer piece of clothing.

## 2 Kings 2:9

After crossing, Elijah asked Elisha, “What can I do for you before I am taken away?” 📖

Elisha answered, “Let me inherit a double share of your spirit.”

**Help with double share:** This uses the language of an inheritance.

The request concerns Elijah’s spirit and work.

## 2 Kings 2:10

Elijah said, “You asked something difficult. 📖

If you see me being taken from you, it will be yours.

Otherwise, it will not.”

## 2 Kings 2:11

As they walked and talked, a fiery chariot and fiery horses appeared and separated them. 📖

Elijah went to heaven in a whirlwind.

**Help with chariot:** This is a vehicle pulled by horses.

## 2 Kings 2:12

Elisha watched and cried, “My father, my father, Israel’s chariots and horsemen!” 📖

He saw Elijah no more.

He tore his own clothes in 2.

**Help with torn clothes:** Tearing clothes expressed deep grief.

Elisha calls Elijah “father” as an honoured leader.

## 2 Kings 2:13

Elisha picked up Elijah’s fallen cloak and returned to the Jordan’s bank. 📖

## 2 Kings 2:14

He struck the water with Elijah’s fallen cloak. 📖

“Where now is the LORD, Elijah’s God?” he asked.

The water divided right and left, and Elisha crossed.

## 2 Kings 2:15

The prophetic group watching from Jericho said, “Elijah’s spirit rests on Elisha.” 📖

They met him and bowed to the ground before him.

## 2 Kings 2:16

They said, “We your servants have 50 brave men. 📖

Let them search for your master.

Perhaps the LORD’s Spirit put him on a mountain or in a valley.”

Elisha answered, “Do not send them.”

## 2 Kings 2:17

They pressed him until he felt embarrassed. 📖

He said, “Send them.”

50 men searched for 3 days but did not find Elijah.

## 2 Kings 2:18

They returned to Elisha, who stayed in Jericho. 📖

He said, “Did I not tell you not to go?”

## 2 Kings 2:19

The city’s men told Elisha, “Our lord, you can see the city’s location is good. 📖

But the water is bad, and the land produces no crops.”

## 2 Kings 2:20

Elisha said, “Bring me a new bowl with salt in it.” 📖

They brought it.

## 2 Kings 2:21

Elisha went to the spring and threw the salt in. 📖

“The LORD says, ‘I have healed this water.

It will no longer cause death or unfruitfulness.’”

## 2 Kings 2:22

The water remains healthy to this day, as Elisha said. 📖

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the time when this account was written.

## 2 Kings 2:23

Elisha went towards Bethel. 📖

Boys came from the city and mocked him on the road.

“Go up, baldhead!

Go up, baldhead!”

## 2 Kings 2:24

Elisha turned, looked at them, and called a curse on them in the LORD’s name. 📖

2 female bears came from the woods and mauled 42 of the boys.

**Help with mauled:** This means attacked and badly injured.

The verse gives the number attacked; it does not state that all died.

## 2 Kings 2:25

Elisha went to Mount Carmel and then returned to Samaria. 📖
