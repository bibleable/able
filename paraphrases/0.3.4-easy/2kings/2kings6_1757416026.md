# 2 Kings 6 — Protection and a terrible siege

**About this chapter**

Elisha helps recover a borrowed axe head. | Aram’s troops surround him, but he leads them into Samaria. | A later siege brings terrible hunger and violence.

## 2 Kings 6:1

The prophetic group told Elisha, “The place where we meet with you is too small. 📖

## 2 Kings 6:2

Let us go to the Jordan. 📖

Each can take a log, and we can build a place to live.”

Elisha said, “Go.”

## 2 Kings 6:3

One asked, “Please come with your servants.” 📖

Elisha answered, “I will.”

## 2 Kings 6:4

He went with them to the Jordan, where they began cutting trees. 📖

## 2 Kings 6:5

As one man cut a tree, the iron axe head fell into the water. 📖

He cried, “Oh, master!

It was borrowed!”

## 2 Kings 6:6

The man of God asked where it fell. 📖

The man showed him.

Elisha cut a stick, threw it there, and made the iron float.

## 2 Kings 6:7

“Lift it out,” Elisha said. 📖

The man reached out and took it.

## 2 Kings 6:8

Aram’s king was at war with Israel. 📖

He discussed plans with his servants and chose a place for his camp.

## 2 Kings 6:9

The man of God warned Israel’s king, “Avoid that place. 📖

The Arameans are going there.”

## 2 Kings 6:10

Israel’s king sent word to the place Elisha identified. 📖

Elisha repeatedly warned him, so he guarded against danger in those places.

## 2 Kings 6:11

Aram’s king became furious and demanded of his servants, “Which of us sides with Israel’s king?” 📖

## 2 Kings 6:12

One answered, “Nobody, my lord the king. 📖

Elisha, the prophet in Israel, tells Israel’s king even the words you speak in your bedroom.”

## 2 Kings 6:13

The king said, “Find him so I can capture him.” 📖

He was told, “Elisha is in Dothan.”

## 2 Kings 6:14

Aram’s king sent horses, chariots, and a great army. 📖

They arrived by night and surrounded the city.

## 2 Kings 6:15

Elisha’s servant rose early and saw the army, horses, and chariots surrounding the city. 📖

“Oh, master, what shall we do?”

## 2 Kings 6:16

Elisha answered, “Do not fear. 📖

There are more with us than with them.”

## 2 Kings 6:17

Elisha prayed, “LORD, open his eyes so he can see.” 📖

The LORD opened the young man’s eyes.

He saw the hills full of fiery horses and chariots around Elisha.

## 2 Kings 6:18

As the Arameans approached, Elisha prayed, “LORD, strike these people with blindness.” 📖

The LORD did as Elisha asked.

## 2 Kings 6:19

Elisha told them, “This is not the road or the city. 📖

Follow me to the man you seek.”

He led them to Samaria.

## 2 Kings 6:20

In Samaria, Elisha prayed, “LORD, open their eyes so they can see.” 📖

The LORD opened their eyes.

They saw they were in Samaria.

## 2 Kings 6:21

Israel’s king saw them and asked Elisha, “My father, shall I kill them? 📖

Shall I kill them?”

**Help with my father:** The king addresses Elisha respectfully.

## 2 Kings 6:22

Elisha answered, “Do not kill them. 📖

Would you kill prisoners you captured with your sword or bow?

Give them food and water.

Let them eat and drink, then return to their master.”

## 2 Kings 6:23

The king gave them a great feast. 📖

After they ate and drank, he sent them back to their master.

Aramean raiders no longer entered Israel.

## 2 Kings 6:24

Later, Ben-hadad king of Aram gathered his whole army and surrounded Samaria to attack it. 📖

## 2 Kings 6:25

The long siege caused severe famine. 📖

A donkey’s head cost 80 silver shekels.

A quarter cab of dove’s dung cost 5 silver shekels.

**Help with cab and shekel:** A cab measured volume; a shekel measured weight.

The source calls the substance “dove’s dung”.

## 2 Kings 6:26

A woman cried to Israel’s king as he passed along the wall. 📖

“Help me, my lord the king!”

## 2 Kings 6:27

He answered, “If the LORD does not help you, where can I get help? 📖

From a threshing floor or winepress?”

**Help with these places:** These were used to process grain and grapes.

## 2 Kings 6:28

He asked what was wrong. 📖

She answered, “This woman told me to give up my son for us to eat.

She said we would eat her son tomorrow.

## 2 Kings 6:29

We boiled and ate my son. 📖

Next day, I asked for her son to eat, but she hid him.”

**About this account:** The woman reports a horrifying act during the famine.

The passage does not approve it.

## 2 Kings 6:30

The king tore his clothes when he heard this. 📖

As he passed on the wall, people saw rough sackcloth next to his skin beneath his clothes.

**Help with sackcloth:** This was worn to express grief or humility.

## 2 Kings 6:31

He declared, “May God punish me severely if Elisha son of Shaphat still has his head by day’s end!” 📖

## 2 Kings 6:32

Elisha sat at home with the elders. 📖

The king sent a messenger ahead.

Before he arrived, Elisha said, “This murderer has sent someone to cut off my head.

Shut the door when the messenger comes and keep him out.

Is the sound of his master’s footsteps not behind him?”

## 2 Kings 6:33

While Elisha still spoke, the messenger arrived. 📖

The king said, “This disaster comes from the LORD.

Why wait for the LORD any longer?”
