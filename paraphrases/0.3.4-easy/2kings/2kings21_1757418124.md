# 2 Kings 21 — Manasseh and Amon

**About this chapter**

Manasseh brings false worship into the temple and sheds innocent blood. | The LORD announces judgment against Jerusalem. | Amon follows his father and is killed in a plot.

## 2 Kings 21:1

Manasseh became king at 12 and ruled in Jerusalem for 55 years. 📖

His mother was Hephzibah.

## 2 Kings 21:2

He did evil before the LORD, following detestable practices of nations the LORD drove out before Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:3

He rebuilt high places his father Hezekiah destroyed and built altars for Baal. 📖

He made an Asherah pole like Israel’s King Ahab.

He worshipped and served all heaven’s host.

**Help with these words:** High places and Asherah poles were used in worship.

Heaven’s host here refers to heavenly bodies worshipped as gods.

## 2 Kings 21:4

He built altars in the LORD’s house. 📖

The LORD had said, “I will put my Name in Jerusalem.”

## 2 Kings 21:5

He built altars to all heaven’s host in both courtyards of the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:6

He sacrificed his son in fire, practised sorcery and divination, and consulted mediums and spiritists. 📖

His great evil angered the LORD.

**Help with these practices:** They seek supernatural knowledge or power, including claimed contact with spirits of the dead.

## 2 Kings 21:7

He placed his carved Asherah pole in the temple. 📖

The LORD told David and Solomon, “I chose Jerusalem from all Israel’s tribes.

I will put my Name forever in Jerusalem and this temple.

## 2 Kings 21:8

I will never again make Israel wander from their ancestors’ land if they carefully obey all my commands. 📖

They must keep the whole Law my servant Moses commanded them.”

## 2 Kings 21:9

They would not listen. 📖

Manasseh led them into greater evil than nations the LORD destroyed before Israel.

## 2 Kings 21:10

The LORD spoke through his servants the prophets. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:11

“Manasseh king of Judah committed these detestable acts. 📖

He did worse than the Amorites before him and caused Judah to sin with his idols.

## 2 Kings 21:12

Therefore the LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I will bring disaster on Jerusalem and Judah that makes every hearer’s ears ring. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:13

I will stretch Samaria’s measuring line and Ahab’s family’s plumb line over Jerusalem. 📖

I will wipe Jerusalem like a bowl, wiping it and turning it upside down.

**Help with these images:** Measuring tools picture judgment, like that against Samaria and Ahab’s family.

The emptied bowl pictures thorough removal.

## 2 Kings 21:14

I will abandon the rest of my inheritance and hand them to their enemies. 📖

All their enemies will plunder them.

**Help with inheritance:** Here this refers to the people belonging to the LORD.

## 2 Kings 21:15

They did evil before me and angered me from their ancestors’ departure from Egypt until now.’” 📖

## 2 Kings 21:16

Manasseh also shed so much innocent blood that Jerusalem was filled from end to end. 📖

This was besides the evil he caused Judah to commit before the LORD.

## 2 Kings 21:17

Manasseh’s other acts, achievements, and sin are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:18

Manasseh died and was buried in his palace garden, the garden of Uzza. 📖

His son Amon became king.

## 2 Kings 21:19

Amon became king at 22 and ruled in Jerusalem for 2 years. 📖

His mother was Meshullemeth daughter of Haruz from Jotbah.

## 2 Kings 21:20

He did evil before the LORD like his father Manasseh. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:21

He followed all his father’s ways and served and worshipped his father’s idols. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:22

He abandoned the LORD, his ancestors’ God, and did not follow the LORD’s way. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:23

Amon’s servants plotted against him and killed him in his palace. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:24

The people killed everyone who plotted against Amon. 📖

They made his son Josiah king.

## 2 Kings 21:25

Amon’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 21:26

He was buried in his tomb in Uzza’s garden. 📖

His son Josiah became king.
