# 2 Kings 7 — Food is found outside Samaria

**About this chapter**

Elisha announces food will become affordable. | Four men with leprosy discover Aram’s abandoned camp. | The account repeats the prophecy and describes its fulfilment.

## 2 Kings 7:1

Elisha said, “Hear the LORD’s word. 📖

Tomorrow about this time, at Samaria’s gate, 1 seah of fine flour will cost 1 shekel.

2 seahs of barley will also cost 1 shekel.”

**Help with these units:** A seah measured grain; a shekel measured silver’s weight.

## 2 Kings 7:2

The officer supporting the king answered, “Could this happen even if the LORD made windows in heaven?” 📖

Elisha said, “You will see it, but you will not eat any.”

**Help with windows in heaven:** This picture suggests supplies coming down from above.

## 2 Kings 7:3

4 men with leprosy sat at the city gate. 📖

They asked each other, “Why sit here until we die?

**Help with leprosy:** This Bible term covers serious skin diseases.

## 2 Kings 7:4

If we enter the city, famine will kill us. 📖

If we stay here, we will die too.

Let us go to Aram’s camp.

If they spare us, we live.

If they kill us, we die.”

## 2 Kings 7:5

At twilight, they went to Aram’s camp. 📖

At its edge, they found nobody.

## 2 Kings 7:6

The Lord had made the Arameans hear chariots, horses, and a great army. 📖

They said, “Israel’s king hired Hittite and Egyptian kings to attack us!”

## 2 Kings 7:7

The Arameans fled at twilight to save their lives. 📖

They abandoned tents, horses, and donkeys, leaving the camp intact.

## 2 Kings 7:8

The men with leprosy entered a tent at the camp’s edge and ate and drank. 📖

They carried away silver, gold, and clothes and hid them.

They returned, took things from another tent, and hid those too.

## 2 Kings 7:9

They said, “We are not doing right. 📖

This is a day of good news.

If we stay silent until morning, our sin will catch up with us.

Let us tell the king’s household.”

## 2 Kings 7:10

They called to the city’s gatekeepers. 📖

“We went to Aram’s camp.

Nobody was there, only tied horses and donkeys.

The tents were intact.”

## 2 Kings 7:11

The gatekeepers shouted the news, which reached the king’s household. 📖

## 2 Kings 7:12

The king rose at night and told his servants, “The Arameans know we are starving. 📖

They left camp to hide in the fields.

They plan to capture us alive when we leave, then enter the city.”

**About this explanation:** This is the king’s suspicion, not what the narrator says happened.

## 2 Kings 7:13

A servant said, “Send scouts on 5 horses still here. 📖

Their situation is no worse than that of all Israel remaining here.

All Israel here faces death.

Send them to find out.”

## 2 Kings 7:14

The scouts took 2 chariots with horses. 📖

The king sent them after Aram’s army, saying, “Go and see.”

## 2 Kings 7:15

They followed as far as the Jordan. 📖

Clothes and equipment lay along the whole road, thrown away as the Arameans fled.

The scouts returned and told the king.

## 2 Kings 7:16

The people went out and took goods from Aram’s camp. 📖

1 seah of fine flour cost 1 shekel, and 2 seahs of barley cost 1 shekel.

This happened as the LORD said.

## 2 Kings 7:17

The king put the officer who supported his arm in charge of the gate. 📖

The people trampled him there, and he died.

The man of God had foretold this when the king visited him.

## 2 Kings 7:18

The man of God had told the king what would happen about this time tomorrow at Samaria’s gate. 📖

2 seahs of barley would cost 1 shekel, and 1 seah of fine flour would cost 1 shekel.

## 2 Kings 7:19

The officer had asked whether this could happen even if the LORD made windows in heaven. 📖

Elisha answered, “You will see it, but you will not eat any!”

## 2 Kings 7:20

That happened to him. 📖

The people trampled him in the gateway, and he died.
