# 2 Kings 8 — A home restored and new kings

**About this chapter**

The woman whose son was revived returns after famine. | Elisha speaks with Hazael in Damascus. | The account then describes Judah’s kings Jehoram and Ahaziah.

## 2 Kings 8:1

Elisha had told the woman whose son he revived, “Take your household and live abroad wherever you can. 📖

The LORD has ordered a 7-year famine on this land.”

## 2 Kings 8:2

She obeyed the man of God. 📖

Her household lived as foreigners in the Philistines’ land for 7 years.

## 2 Kings 8:3

After 7 years, she returned from the Philistines’ land. 📖

She appealed to the king for her house and land.

## 2 Kings 8:4

The king was speaking with Gehazi, the man of God’s servant. 📖

“Tell me all Elisha’s great deeds.”

## 2 Kings 8:5

Gehazi was describing how Elisha revived the dead when the woman came to ask for her house and land. 📖

He said, “My lord the king, this is the woman.

This is the son Elisha restored to life.”

## 2 Kings 8:6

The king asked her, and she confirmed it. 📖

He appointed an officer to restore all her property.

He ordered, “Also give her all the field’s proceeds from when she left until now.”

## 2 Kings 8:7

Elisha came to Damascus while Ben-hadad king of Aram was ill. 📖

The king heard the man of God had arrived.

## 2 Kings 8:8

He told Hazael, “Take a gift to the man of God. 📖

Ask the LORD through him whether I will recover.”

## 2 Kings 8:9

Hazael took 40 camel loads of Damascus’s finest goods to Elisha. 📖

He entered and stood before him.

“Your son Ben-hadad king of Aram asks whether he will recover from this illness.”

**Help with your son:** Here the king uses a respectful expression for himself.

## 2 Kings 8:10

Elisha answered, “Tell him, ‘You will certainly recover.’ 📖

But the LORD has shown me he will actually die.”

## 2 Kings 8:11

Elisha stared at Hazael until Hazael felt uncomfortable. 📖

Then Elisha wept.

## 2 Kings 8:12

Hazael asked, “Why are you weeping, my lord?” 📖

Elisha answered, “I know the evil you will do to Israel.

You will burn their fortresses and kill their young men with swords.

You will smash their little children and rip open pregnant women.”

**About this prophecy:** Elisha describes atrocities and weeps.

The passage does not tell readers to do these acts.

## 2 Kings 8:13

Hazael said, “How could your servant, a mere dog, do such a monstrous thing?” 📖

Elisha answered, “The LORD has shown me you will be Aram’s king.”

**Help with dog:** Hazael uses this image to describe himself as insignificant.

## 2 Kings 8:14

Hazael returned to his master, who asked what Elisha said. 📖

Hazael answered, “He told me you would certainly recover.”

## 2 Kings 8:15

Next day, Hazael dipped a thick cloth in water and spread it over the king’s face. 📖

Ben-hadad died, and Hazael became king.

## 2 Kings 8:16

In year 5 of Joram son of Ahab’s reign over Israel, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat succeeded his father in Judah. 📖

**About these names:** Joram and Jehoram are forms of the same name.

Two different kings are named in this verse.

## 2 Kings 8:17

Jehoram became king at 32 and ruled in Jerusalem for 8 years. 📖

## 2 Kings 8:18

He followed Israel’s kings and Ahab’s family. 📖

He married Ahab’s daughter and did evil before the LORD.

## 2 Kings 8:19

For his servant David’s sake, the LORD would not destroy Judah. 📖

He promised to keep a lamp for David and his descendants forever.

**Help with lamp:** This pictures the continuation of David’s royal line.

## 2 Kings 8:20

During Jehoram’s reign, Edom rebelled against Judah and chose its own king. 📖

## 2 Kings 8:21

Jehoram went to Zair with all his chariots. 📖

Edom surrounded him and his chariot commanders.

He attacked by night, but his troops fled home.

## 2 Kings 8:22

Edom remains in rebellion against Judah to this day. 📖

Libnah also rebelled then.

**Help with to this day:** This refers to the account’s own time.

## 2 Kings 8:23

Jehoram’s other acts and achievements are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖

## 2 Kings 8:24

Jehoram died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. 📖

His son Ahaziah became king.

## 2 Kings 8:25

Ahaziah son of Jehoram became Judah’s king in year 12 of Joram son of Ahab’s reign over Israel. 📖

## 2 Kings 8:26

Ahaziah became king at 22 and ruled in Jerusalem for 1 year. 📖

His mother was Athaliah, granddaughter of Omri king of Israel.

## 2 Kings 8:27

Ahaziah followed Ahab’s family and did evil before the LORD like them. 📖

He was a son-in-law of Ahab’s family.

## 2 Kings 8:28

Ahaziah joined Joram son of Ahab in fighting Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead. 📖

The Arameans wounded Joram.

## 2 Kings 8:29

Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from wounds the Arameans inflicted at Ramah while he fought Hazael king of Aram. 📖

Ahaziah son of Jehoram, Judah’s king, visited the wounded Joram son of Ahab in Jezreel.
