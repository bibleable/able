# 2 Kings 17 — Israel is exiled and other peoples settle

**About this chapter**

Assyria captures Samaria and takes Israel away. | The account explains Israel’s disobedience and the LORD’s warnings. | New settlers continue mixed worship despite instruction.

## 2 Kings 17:1

Hoshea son of Elah became Israel’s king in year 12 of Ahaz’s reign over Judah. 📖

He ruled in Samaria for 9 years.

## 2 Kings 17:2

He did evil before the LORD, though not like Israel’s earlier kings. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:3

Shalmaneser king of Assyria attacked. 📖

Hoshea submitted to him and paid tribute.

**Help with tribute:** This is payment demanded by a stronger ruler.

## 2 Kings 17:4

Assyria’s king discovered Hoshea’s plot. 📖

Hoshea sent messengers to King So of Egypt and stopped paying his yearly tribute to Assyria.

Assyria’s king arrested and imprisoned him.

## 2 Kings 17:5

Assyria’s king invaded the whole land and surrounded Samaria for 3 years. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:6

In Hoshea’s 9th year, Assyria’s king captured Samaria and took Israel captive to Assyria. 📖

He settled them in Halah, Gozan by the Habor River, and the Medes’ cities.

## 2 Kings 17:7

This happened because Israel sinned against the LORD their God. 📖

He brought them from Egypt and freed them from Pharaoh king of Egypt’s power.

They worshipped other gods

## 2 Kings 17:8

and followed customs of nations the LORD drove out before them. 📖

They also followed practices introduced by Israel’s kings.

## 2 Kings 17:9

Israel secretly did wrong against the LORD their God. 📖

They built high places in all their cities, from watchtowers to defended cities.

**Help with high places:** These were sites used for worship.

## 2 Kings 17:10

They set up sacred pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree. 📖

**Help with these objects:** These pillars and poles were used in worship of other gods.

## 2 Kings 17:11

They burned incense at all the high places like nations the LORD drove out. 📖

Their wicked acts angered the LORD.

## 2 Kings 17:12

They served idols despite the LORD’s command not to do so. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:13

Through all his prophets and seers, the LORD warned Israel and Judah. 📖

“Turn from evil and keep my commands and laws.

Follow the whole Law I commanded your ancestors and gave you through my servants the prophets.”

**Help with seers:** These are prophets who receive messages or visions from God.

## 2 Kings 17:14

They refused to listen and stiffened their necks like their ancestors, who did not believe the LORD their God. 📖

**Help with stiffened necks:** This pictures stubborn refusal to obey.

## 2 Kings 17:15

They rejected his laws, covenant with their ancestors, and warnings. 📖

They followed worthless idols and became worthless themselves.

They copied surrounding nations the LORD forbade them to imitate.

**About worthless:** This describes the effect of their idol worship in the account’s judgment.

It is not permission to treat people as having no worth.

## 2 Kings 17:16

They abandoned all the LORD their God’s commands. 📖

They cast 2 calf idols and made an Asherah pole.

They bowed to all heaven’s host and served Baal.

**Help with heaven’s host:** Here this refers to heavenly bodies worshipped as gods.

## 2 Kings 17:17

They sacrificed sons and daughters in fire and practised divination and soothsaying. 📖

They gave themselves to evil before the LORD and angered him.

**Help with divination and soothsaying:** These are practices seeking hidden knowledge or predictions by supernatural means.

## 2 Kings 17:18

The LORD became very angry and removed Israel from his presence. 📖

Only Judah’s tribe remained.

## 2 Kings 17:19

Even Judah failed to keep the LORD their God’s commands. 📖

They followed customs introduced by Israel.

## 2 Kings 17:20

The LORD rejected all Israel’s descendants. 📖

He afflicted them and handed them to plunderers until he banished them from his presence.

**Help with plunderers:** These are people who take others’ possessions by force.

## 2 Kings 17:21

When the LORD tore Israel from David’s family, they made Jeroboam son of Nebat king. 📖

Jeroboam led Israel away from the LORD into great sin.

## 2 Kings 17:22

Israel continued all Jeroboam’s sins without turning away. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:23

Finally the LORD removed Israel, as he warned through all his servants the prophets. 📖

Israel was exiled from home to Assyria, where they remain to this day.

**Help with exiled:** They were forced to leave their homeland.

“To this day” refers to the account’s time.

## 2 Kings 17:24

Assyria’s king brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim. 📖

They replaced Israel in Samaria’s towns and took possession of them.

## 2 Kings 17:25

At first, these settlers did not worship the LORD. 📖

He sent lions, which killed some of them.

## 2 Kings 17:26

They told Assyria’s king, “The peoples you settled in Samaria’s cities do not know the land’s God’s requirements. 📖

He sent lions that are killing them.”

## 2 Kings 17:27

Assyria’s king ordered, “Send back one priest taken from Samaria. 📖

He must live there and teach the land’s God’s requirements.”

## 2 Kings 17:28

An exiled priest returned to Bethel and taught them how to worship the LORD. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:29

But each nation kept making its own gods where it settled. 📖

They put them in shrines Samaria’s people made at the high places.

## 2 Kings 17:30

Babylon’s people made Succoth-benoth, Cuth’s people made Nergal, and Hamath’s people made Ashima. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:31

The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. 📖

The Sepharvites burned their children for Adrammelech and Anammelech, Sepharvaim’s gods.

## 2 Kings 17:32

The settlers worshipped the LORD but appointed all sorts of people as priests at the high-place shrines. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:33

They worshipped the LORD and served their own gods according to their former nations’ customs. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:34

To this day, they keep their former customs. 📖

None worship the LORD or follow his rules, laws, and commands given to Jacob’s descendants.

He named Jacob Israel.

## 2 Kings 17:35

The LORD made a covenant with Israel. 📖

“Do not worship other gods, bow to them, serve them, or sacrifice to them.

## 2 Kings 17:36

Worship the LORD who brought you from Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm. 📖

Bow to him and offer him sacrifices.

**Help with outstretched arm:** This pictures God’s mighty action to rescue his people.

## 2 Kings 17:37

Always carefully obey the rules, laws, and commands he wrote for you. 📖

Do not worship other gods.

## 2 Kings 17:38

Do not forget my covenant with you. 📖

Do not worship other gods.

## 2 Kings 17:39

Worship the LORD your God. 📖

He will rescue you from all your enemies.”

## 2 Kings 17:40

They refused to listen and continued their former customs. 📖

## 2 Kings 17:41

These nations worshipped the LORD and served their idols too. 📖

Their children and grandchildren continue their ancestors’ practices to this day.
