# Hosea 11 — God’s love and compassion

**About this chapter**

God speaks of Israel as His child.

He describes their refusal to return and the judgment they face.

He also speaks of compassion and a future return home.

## Hosea 11:1

God says, “When Israel was a child, I loved him. 📖

I called My son out of Egypt.

## Hosea 11:2

But the more I called Israel, the farther they went from Me. 📖

They sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to carved images.

**Help with these words:** The Baals are gods worshiped under the name Baal.

Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

## Hosea 11:3

I taught Ephraim to walk, taking them by the arms. 📖

But they never realized that I was the One who healed them.

## Hosea 11:4

I led them with cords of kindness and ropes of love. 📖

I lifted the yoke from their necks and bent down to feed them.

**Help with “yoke”:** This is a wooden frame used to control working animals.

The pictures describe God’s loving care.

## Hosea 11:5

Will they not return to Egypt and be ruled by Assyria because they refused to repent? 📖

**Help with “repent”:** This means turning away from wrongdoing and returning to God.

## Hosea 11:6

A sword will flash through their cities. 📖

It will destroy their gates’ bars and consume them in their own plans.

## Hosea 11:7

My people insist on turning away from Me. 📖

Though they call to the Most High, He will certainly not raise them up.

## Hosea 11:8

How could I give you up, Ephraim? 📖

How could I surrender you, Israel?

How could I make you like Admah or treat you like Zeboiim?

My heart turns within Me.

My compassion is stirred!

## Hosea 11:9

I will not carry out the full fury of My anger or destroy Ephraim again. 📖

I am God, not a man, the Holy One among you.

I will not come in fierce anger.

## Hosea 11:10

They will follow the LORD. 📖

He will roar like a lion.

When He roars, His children will come trembling from the west.

## Hosea 11:11

They will come trembling like birds from Egypt and like doves from Assyria. 📖

Then I will settle them in their homes, declares the LORD.

## Hosea 11:12

Ephraim surrounds Me with lies, and Israel’s people surround Me with deceit. 📖

But Judah still walks with God and is faithful to the Holy One.”
