# Hosea 6 — Returning and faithfulness

**About this chapter**

The chapter begins with a call to return to the LORD.

God answers with charges about loyalty that disappears, violence, and broken covenant.

## Hosea 6:1

Come, let us return to the LORD. 📖

He has torn us to pieces, but He will heal us.

He has wounded us, but He will bandage our wounds.

## Hosea 6:2

After two days, He will revive us. 📖

On the third day, He will raise us up, so we may live before Him.

## Hosea 6:3

Let us know the LORD. 📖

Let us press on to know Him.

He will appear as surely as the sun rises.

He will come to us like rain, like spring showers watering the earth.

## Hosea 6:4

God says, “What shall I do with you, Ephraim? 📖

What shall I do with you, Judah?

Your loyalty is like morning mist, like early dew that disappears.

**Help with “dew”:** This is water that settles outdoors and dries away.

## Hosea 6:5

So I have cut them down through the prophets. 📖

I have killed them by the words of My mouth.

My judgments go out like lightning.

## Hosea 6:6

I desire mercy rather than sacrifice. 📖

I desire knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings.

**Help with “burnt offerings”:** These are sacrifices burned on an altar for God.

## Hosea 6:7

But like Adam, they have broken the covenant. 📖

There they were unfaithful to Me.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Hosea 6:8

Gilead is a city of evildoers, marked with bloody footprints. 📖

## Hosea 6:9

A band of priests is like raiders waiting in hiding to attack. 📖

They murder on the road to Shechem.

They have certainly done horrible things.

## Hosea 6:10

I have seen a horrible thing among Israel’s people. 📖

Ephraim practices prostitution there, and Israel is unclean.

## Hosea 6:11

Judah, a harvest is also appointed for you when I bring My people back from captivity.” 📖

**Help with “captivity”:** This means being held as prisoners.

**About “harvest”:** The passage uses a harvest picture without explaining its details here.
