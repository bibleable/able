# Hosea 14 — Return and healing

**About this chapter**

Israel is called to return with confession and trust in God.

God promises healing, love, and new growth.

The book ends by calling the wise to understand the LORD’s ways.

## Hosea 14:1

Israel, return to the LORD your God. 📖

You have stumbled because of your sin.

## Hosea 14:2

Bring words of confession and return to the LORD. 📖

Tell Him, “Take away all our wrongdoing and receive us with kindness.

Then we may offer the fruit of our lips.

**Help with “the fruit of our lips”:** This pictures words offered to God.

## Hosea 14:3

Assyria will not save us, and we will not ride horses. 📖

We will never again call things our hands made ‘Our gods!’

In You, children without fathers find compassion.”

## Hosea 14:4

God says, “I will heal their unfaithfulness and love them freely. 📖

My anger has turned away from them.

## Hosea 14:5

I will be like dew to Israel. 📖

He will blossom like a lily and take root like Lebanon’s cedars.

**Help with these pictures:** Dew is water that settles outdoors.

Lilies are flowers, and cedars are trees.

The pictures describe growth and life.

## Hosea 14:6

His shoots will grow. 📖

His beauty will be like an olive tree, and his scent like Lebanon’s cedars.

## Hosea 14:7

They will return and live in his shade. 📖

They will grow grain and blossom like a grapevine.

His fame will be like Lebanon’s wine.

## Hosea 14:8

Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols? 📖

I am the One who answers and watches over him.

I am like a flourishing cypress tree.

Your fruit comes from Me.”

## Hosea 14:9

Let whoever is wise understand these things. 📖

Let whoever has understanding know them.

The LORD’s ways are right.

Those who do right walk in them, but rebels stumble in them.
