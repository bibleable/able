# Hosea 2 — Unfaithfulness, judgment, and renewed love

**About this chapter**

God speaks of His people using the picture of an unfaithful wife.

He announces severe judgment and then promises renewed love, faithfulness, and safety.

The family and marriage language is kept as part of the passage.

## Hosea 2:1

God said, “Call your brothers ‘My people’ and your sisters ‘My loved one.’ 📖

## Hosea 2:2

Rebuke your mother! 📖

Rebuke her, because she is not My wife, and I am not her husband.

Let her remove adultery from her face and unfaithfulness from between her breasts.

**Help with “rebuke”:** This means speaking firmly against wrongdoing.

**About the mother:** In this picture, the mother represents the unfaithful people.

## Hosea 2:3

Otherwise, I will strip her naked, exposing her as on the day she was born. 📖

I will make her like a desert, a dry land, and let her die of thirst.

## Hosea 2:4

I will have no compassion on her children, because they are children of adultery. 📖

## Hosea 2:5

Their mother acted as a prostitute and became pregnant with them in disgrace. 📖

She thought, ‘I will follow my lovers.

They give me bread, water, wool, linen, oil, and drink.’

**Help with “linen”:** This is cloth made from the flax plant.

## Hosea 2:6

So look! 📖

I will block her path with thorns.

I will surround her with a wall, so she cannot find her way.

## Hosea 2:7

She will chase her lovers but not catch them. 📖

She will look for them but not find them.

Then she will say, ‘I will return to my first husband.

I was better off then than now.’

## Hosea 2:8

She does not acknowledge that I gave her grain, new wine, and oil. 📖

I gave her much silver and gold, which they used to make things for Baal.

**Help with “Baal”:** This is the name of a god the people worshiped instead of the LORD.

## Hosea 2:9

So I will take back My grain and new wine in their proper seasons. 📖

I will take away My wool and linen, which covered her naked body.

## Hosea 2:10

Then I will expose her shameful sexual behavior before her lovers. 📖

No one will rescue her from My hands.

## Hosea 2:11

I will end all her celebrations. 📖

I will end her feasts, New Moons, and Sabbaths, all her appointed feasts.

**Help with these days:** New Moons mark the beginning of months.

Sabbaths are the special rest days God commanded.

## Hosea 2:12

I will destroy her grapevines and fig trees, which she thinks are payment from her lovers. 📖

I will turn them into a thick tangle of plants.

Wild animals will eat them.

## Hosea 2:13

I will punish her for the days when she burned incense to the Baals. 📖

She dressed in rings and jewelry and followed her lovers.

But she forgot Me, declares the LORD.

**Help with these words:** Incense is burned to give a sweet smell.

“Baals” refers to the gods worshiped under that name.

## Hosea 2:14

So look! 📖

I will draw her toward Me, lead her into the wilderness, and speak tenderly to her.

## Hosea 2:15

There I will give back her vineyards and make the Valley of Achor a gateway of hope. 📖

There she will respond as in her youth, as when she came up from Egypt.

**Help with “vineyards”:** These are places where grapevines grow.

## Hosea 2:16

On that day, declares the LORD, you will call Me ‘my Husband.’ 📖

You will no longer call Me ‘my Master.’

## Hosea 2:17

I will remove the Baals’ names from her lips. 📖

Their names will no longer be called on.

## Hosea 2:18

On that day, I will make a covenant for them with field animals, birds, and creatures crawling on the ground. 📖

I will end bow, sword, and battle in the land.

I will make them lie down safely.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Hosea 2:19

I will promise Myself to you in marriage forever. 📖

I will do so in righteousness, justice, faithful love, and compassion.

**Help with “righteousness”:** This means what is right and just.

## Hosea 2:20

I will promise Myself to you in faithfulness. 📖

You will know the LORD.

## Hosea 2:21

On that day, I will respond, declares the LORD. 📖

I will respond to the heavens, and they will respond to the earth.

## Hosea 2:22

The earth will respond to the grain, new wine, and oil. 📖

They will respond to Jezreel.

## Hosea 2:23

I will plant her as My own in the land. 📖

I will have compassion on ‘No Compassion.’

I will tell those called ‘Not My People,’ ‘You are My people.’

They will say, ‘You are my God.’”

**About the names:** These words reverse the judgments carried by the children’s names in chapter 1.
