# Hosea 10 — Israel’s altars and coming destruction

**About this chapter**

Israel’s prosperity has supported false worship.

God announces judgment and calls the people to seek Him.

Farming pictures describe both wrongdoing and the right response.

## Hosea 10:1

Israel was a flourishing vine, producing fruit for himself. 📖

As his fruit increased, he made more altars.

As his land produced better crops, he made better sacred pillars.

**Help with “sacred pillars”:** These are standing stones used in worship.

## Hosea 10:2

Their hearts are deceitful. 📖

Now they must bear their guilt.

The LORD will break their altars and destroy their sacred pillars.

## Hosea 10:3

Now they will surely say, “We have no king because we do not honor the LORD. 📖

What can a king do for us?”

## Hosea 10:4

They speak empty words and make agreements with false oaths. 📖

So judgment grows like poisonous weeds in a field’s plowed rows.

## Hosea 10:5

Samaria’s people will fear for Beth-aven’s calf. 📖

Its people and idol priests will mourn for it.

They rejoiced in its glory, but it has been taken away into exile.

**Help with the calf:** This is a calf-shaped idol.

## Hosea 10:6

It will be carried to Assyria as payment to the great king. 📖

Ephraim will be overcome with shame.

Israel will be ashamed of its wooden idols.

## Hosea 10:7

Samaria and her king will be carried away like a twig floating on water. 📖

## Hosea 10:8

Aven’s high places of worship will be destroyed. 📖

They are Israel’s sin.

Thorns and thistles will cover their altars.

People will tell the mountains, “Cover us!”

They will tell the hills, “Fall on us!”

## Hosea 10:9

God says, “Israel, you have sinned since Gibeah’s days and remained there. 📖

Did the battle in Gibeah not overtake those sons of wrongdoing?

## Hosea 10:10

I will punish them when I please. 📖

Nations will gather against them and enslave them because of their double wrongdoing.

## Hosea 10:11

Ephraim is a trained young cow that loves threshing. 📖

But I will put a yoke on her lovely neck.

I will harness Ephraim.

Judah will plow, and Jacob will break the hard ground.

**Help with these words:** Threshing separates grain from its stalks.

A yoke is a wooden frame used to control working animals.

## Hosea 10:12

Plant righteousness for yourselves and harvest the fruit of faithful love. 📖

Break up your unplowed ground.

It is time to seek the LORD until He comes and sends righteousness like rain.

**Help with “righteousness”:** This means what is right and just.

The farming picture calls for a new way of living.

## Hosea 10:13

You have plowed wickedness and harvested injustice. 📖

You have eaten the fruit of lies.

You trusted your own way and your many mighty men.

## Hosea 10:14

So the roar of battle will rise against your people, and all your fortresses will be destroyed. 📖

It will be like Shalman’s destruction of Beth-arbel on the day of battle.

Mothers were smashed to pieces with their children.

## Hosea 10:15

The same will happen to you, Bethel, because of your great wickedness. 📖

At dawn, Israel’s king will be completely cut off.”
