# Hosea 9 — The coming days of punishment

**About this chapter**

Israel’s worship and unfaithfulness bring judgment.

The chapter describes exile, failed harvests, and the loss of children.

Its severe words and the prayer within it are kept.

## Hosea 9:1

Israel, do not celebrate with joy like the nations. 📖

You have acted as a prostitute against your God, offering love for payment on every threshing floor.

**Help with “threshing floor”:** This is where grain is separated from its stalks.

**About the picture:** The passage connects harvest places with Israel’s unfaithfulness.

## Hosea 9:2

The threshing floor and winepress will not feed them. 📖

New wine will fail them.

**Help with “winepress”:** This is where grapes are crushed to make wine.

## Hosea 9:3

They will not remain in the LORD’s land. 📖

Ephraim will return to Egypt and eat unclean food in Assyria.

**Help with “unclean”:** This refers to the worship laws.

## Hosea 9:4

They will not pour wine offerings to the LORD, and their sacrifices will not please Him. 📖

Their sacrifices will be like mourners’ bread, making everyone who eats it unclean.

Their bread will be only for themselves.

It will not enter the LORD’s house.

## Hosea 9:5

What will you do on the appointed day, the day of the LORD’s feast? 📖

## Hosea 9:6

Even if they flee destruction, Egypt will gather them, and Memphis will bury them. 📖

Thistles will take over their precious silver, and thorns will overrun their tents.

## Hosea 9:7

The days of punishment and repayment have arrived. 📖

Let Israel know it!

The prophet is called a fool, and the inspired man is called out of his mind.

This is because of your great sin and hostility.

**About these insults:** The passage reports what people call the prophet and inspired man.

## Hosea 9:8

The prophet is Ephraim’s watchman, along with my God. 📖

Yet a bird-catcher’s trap is on all his paths.

Hostility is in his God’s house!

## Hosea 9:9

They have deeply corrupted themselves, as in the days of Gibeah. 📖

He will remember their guilt and punish their sins.

## Hosea 9:10

God says, “I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness. 📖

I saw your ancestors like the fig tree’s first fruit in its first season.

But they went to Baal-peor and set themselves apart to Shame.

They became as horrible as the thing they loved.

**About “Shame”:** This name is part of the passage’s condemnation of false worship.

## Hosea 9:11

Ephraim’s glory will fly away like a bird. 📖

There will be no birth, no pregnancy, and no conception.

**Help with “conception”:** This is when a pregnancy begins.

## Hosea 9:12

Even if they raise children, I will take every one from them through death. 📖

How terrible for them when I turn away from them!

## Hosea 9:13

I have seen Ephraim planted in a meadow, like Tyre. 📖

But Ephraim will bring his children out to be killed.”

## Hosea 9:14

LORD, give them—what will You give? 📖

Give them wombs that miscarry and breasts that dry up!

**Help with these words:** Miscarriage is the loss of a pregnancy.

Drying breasts here means losing breast milk.

**About this prayer:** The prayer’s harsh request is retained from the source.

## Hosea 9:15

God says, “All their evil appears at Gilgal. 📖

There I hated them.

I will drive them from My house because of their wicked deeds.

I will no longer love them.

All their leaders are rebellious.

## Hosea 9:16

Ephraim is struck down. 📖

Their root is dried up, and they cannot bear fruit.

Even if they bear children, I will kill their beloved children.”

## Hosea 9:17

My God will reject them because they have not obeyed Him. 📖

They will wander among the nations.
