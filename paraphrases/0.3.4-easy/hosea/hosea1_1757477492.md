# Hosea 1 — Hosea’s family and the message to Israel

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Hosea to marry Gomer.

Their children’s names carry messages of judgment.

God also promises a future gathering of Judah and Israel.

## Hosea 1:1

The LORD’s word came to Hosea, son of Beeri. 📖

This was during the rule of Judah’s kings Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah.

It was also during the rule of Israel’s king Jeroboam, son of Jehoash.

## Hosea 1:2

When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, He said, “Marry a prostitute and have children of adultery. 📖

This land is openly acting as a prostitute by leaving the LORD.”

**Help with these words:** A prostitute provides sexual acts in exchange for payment or other benefits.

Adultery means sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

God uses this marriage picture to describe the land’s unfaithfulness to Him.

## Hosea 1:3

Hosea married Gomer, daughter of Diblaim. 📖

She became pregnant and bore him a son.

## Hosea 1:4

The LORD told Hosea, “Name him Jezreel. 📖

Soon I will punish Jehu’s family for the bloodshed at Jezreel.

I will end the kingdom of Israel.

## Hosea 1:5

On that day, I will break Israel’s bow in the Valley of Jezreel.” 📖

**Help with “break Israel’s bow”:** This pictures breaking Israel’s military power.

## Hosea 1:6

Gomer became pregnant again and had a daughter. 📖

The LORD told Hosea, “Name her Lo-ruhamah.

I will no longer have compassion on Israel’s people or ever forgive them.

**Help with the name:** Lo-ruhamah means “No Compassion.”

## Hosea 1:7

Yet I will have compassion on Judah’s people and save them by the LORD their God. 📖

I will not save them by bow, sword, war, horses, or soldiers on horses.”

## Hosea 1:8

After Gomer stopped breastfeeding Lo-ruhamah, she became pregnant and had a son. 📖

## Hosea 1:9

The LORD said, “Name him Lo-ammi. 📖

You are not My people, and I am not your God.

**Help with the name:** Lo-ammi means “Not My People.”

## Hosea 1:10

Yet the Israelites will be as many as the sea’s sand, which cannot be measured or counted. 📖

In the very place where they were called ‘Not My People,’ they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’

## Hosea 1:11

Then Judah’s people and Israel’s people will be gathered together. 📖

They will appoint one leader for themselves and go up out of the land.

The day of Jezreel will be great!”
