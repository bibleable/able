# Hosea 7 — Israel’s sin and failed loyalties

**About this chapter**

God exposes deception, violence, and the failure to seek Him.

Pictures of an oven, an unturned cake, a dove, and a faulty bow describe the people’s condition.

## Hosea 7:1

God says, “When I heal Israel, Ephraim’s sin and Samaria’s crimes will be exposed. 📖

They practice deceit.

Thieves break in, and robbers raid the streets.

## Hosea 7:2

They do not consider that I remember all their evil. 📖

Their deeds surround them and are before My face.

## Hosea 7:3

Their evil delights the king, and their lies delight the princes. 📖

## Hosea 7:4

They are all unfaithful in marriage, like an oven heated by a baker. 📖

He need not stir the fire between kneading the dough and its rising.

**Help with this picture:** The oven stays hot without more attention.

The picture describes their continuing desire for wrongdoing.

## Hosea 7:5

On our king’s day, the princes are inflamed with wine. 📖

The king joins hands with those who mock him.

## Hosea 7:6

They prepare their hearts like an oven while they wait to attack. 📖

Their anger burns slowly all night.

In the morning, it blazes like a flaming fire.

## Hosea 7:7

All are hot as an oven, and they devour their rulers. 📖

All their kings fall.

Not one of them calls on Me.

## Hosea 7:8

Ephraim mixes with the nations. 📖

Ephraim is a cake that has not been turned over.

**Help with the cake picture:** A cake left unturned does not cook properly on both sides.

## Hosea 7:9

Foreigners use up his strength, but he does not notice. 📖

His hair is even streaked with gray, but he does not know.

## Hosea 7:10

Israel’s pride speaks against them. 📖

Yet they do not return to the LORD their God.

Despite all this, they do not seek Him.

## Hosea 7:11

Ephraim has become like a foolish dove without sense, calling to Egypt and then turning to Assyria. 📖

## Hosea 7:12

As they go, I will spread My net over them. 📖

I will bring them down like birds of the air.

I will punish them when I hear them gathering together.

## Hosea 7:13

How terrible for them, because they have strayed from Me! 📖

Destruction is coming because they have rebelled against Me!

Though I would redeem them, they speak lies against Me.

**Help with “redeem”:** This means rescuing or buying back.

## Hosea 7:14

They do not cry to Me from their hearts when they wail on their beds. 📖

They cut themselves for grain and new wine, but turn away from Me.

## Hosea 7:15

Although I trained and strengthened their arms, they plot evil against Me. 📖

## Hosea 7:16

They turn, but not to the Most High. 📖

They are like a faulty bow.

Their leaders will fall by the sword because of the curses they speak.

For this, people in Egypt will mock them.”

**Help with the bow picture:** A faulty bow does not work as it should.
