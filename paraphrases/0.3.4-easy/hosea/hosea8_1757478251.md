# Hosea 8 — They sow the wind

**About this chapter**

God announces judgment for rejected good, false worship, and unapproved rulers.

The people seek foreign help but forget their Maker.

The chapter keeps the pictures of the eagle, whirlwind, vessel, and wild donkey.

## Hosea 8:1

Put the ram’s horn to your lips! 📖

An eagle hangs over the LORD’s house.

The people have broken My covenant and rebelled against My law.

**Help with these words:** A ram’s horn is used as a trumpet.

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Hosea 8:2

Israel cries to Me, “Our God, we know You!” 📖

## Hosea 8:3

But Israel has rejected good. 📖

An enemy will chase him.

## Hosea 8:4

God says, “They appointed kings without Me and princes without My approval. 📖

They made idols from their silver and gold, bringing their own destruction.

## Hosea 8:5

He has rejected your calf, Samaria. 📖

My anger burns against them.

How long will they be unable to be innocent?

**About the calf:** This is a calf-shaped idol, as the next verse explains.

## Hosea 8:6

This thing comes from Israel. 📖

A craftsman made it, and it is not God.

That calf of Samaria will be broken into pieces.

## Hosea 8:7

They plant the wind and will harvest the whirlwind. 📖

There is no standing grain.

What grows produces no flour.

Even if it produced anything, foreigners would swallow it up.

**Help with the wind picture:** Their empty actions bring destructive results.

## Hosea 8:8

Israel is swallowed up! 📖

Now they are among the nations like a worthless container.

## Hosea 8:9

They have gone to Assyria like a wild donkey on its own. 📖

Ephraim has hired lovers.

## Hosea 8:10

Though they hire allies among the nations, I will now gather them up. 📖

They will begin to shrink under the oppression of the king of princes.

## Hosea 8:11

Ephraim made many altars for sin. 📖

But they became altars for sinning.

## Hosea 8:12

Though I wrote the great things of My law for them, they treated them as something strange. 📖

## Hosea 8:13

They offer sacrifices as gifts to Me and eat the meat. 📖

But the LORD does not accept them.

Now He will remember their wrong actions and punish their sins.

They will return to Egypt.

## Hosea 8:14

Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces. 📖

Judah has built many cities with strong defenses.

But I will send fire on their cities.

It will burn up their fortresses.”
