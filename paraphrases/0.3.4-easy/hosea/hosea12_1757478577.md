# Hosea 12 — Jacob’s story and Israel’s deceit

**About this chapter**

The chapter recalls Jacob’s struggle and Israel’s rescue from Egypt.

It calls the people to return to God and condemns dishonest trade, false worship, and pride.

## Hosea 12:1

Ephraim feeds on wind and chases the east wind all day. 📖

He increases lies and violence.

He makes an agreement with Assyria and sends olive oil to Egypt.

**Help with the wind picture:** It describes seeking something that cannot nourish or protect.

## Hosea 12:2

The LORD has a charge against Judah. 📖

He will punish Jacob according to his ways and repay him for his deeds.

## Hosea 12:3

In the womb, Jacob grasped his brother’s heel. 📖

In his strength, he wrestled with God.

## Hosea 12:4

He struggled with the angel and won. 📖

He wept and sought His favor.

He found Him at Bethel and spoke with Him there.

## Hosea 12:5

He is the LORD God of Hosts. 📖

The LORD is His famous name.

**Help with “God of Hosts”:** This title describes God as ruler of heavenly armies.

## Hosea 12:6

You must return to your God. 📖

Keep love and justice, and always wait for your God.

## Hosea 12:7

A merchant loves to cheat, holding dishonest weighing scales. 📖

## Hosea 12:8

Ephraim boasts, “How rich I have become! 📖

I have gained wealth for myself.

In all my work, no one can find any wrong in me that is sinful.”

## Hosea 12:9

God says, “But I have been the LORD your God since Egypt. 📖

I will make you live in tents again, as during the appointed feast.

## Hosea 12:10

I spoke through the prophets and gave them many visions. 📖

I gave stories with a meaning through the prophets.

## Hosea 12:11

Is there wrongdoing in Gilead? 📖

They will certainly come to nothing.

Do they sacrifice bulls in Gilgal?

Their altars will become heaps of stones in the field’s plowed rows.”

## Hosea 12:12

Jacob fled to Aram. 📖

Israel worked for a wife, tending sheep for a wife.

**About the names:** Jacob is also called Israel.

## Hosea 12:13

The LORD brought Israel out of Egypt through a prophet. 📖

Through a prophet, Israel was kept safe.

## Hosea 12:14

Ephraim has caused bitter anger. 📖

So his Lord will leave the guilt of bloodshed on him and repay him for his scorn.
