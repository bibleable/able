# Hosea 13 — Judgment and the power of death

**About this chapter**

Ephraim’s pride and false worship bring judgment.

God describes His past care and the people’s rejection of their helper.

The chapter includes severe images of destruction and difficult words about rescue from death.

## Hosea 13:1

When Ephraim spoke, people trembled. 📖

He held a high position in Israel.

But he became guilty through Baal and died.

## Hosea 13:2

Now they sin more and more. 📖

They make cast images and skillfully made silver idols, all the work of craftsmen.

People say about them, “They offer human sacrifice and kiss the calves!”

**Help with these words:** Cast images are made by pouring melted metal into a mold.

The calves are calf-shaped idols.

## Hosea 13:3

So they will be like morning mist and early dew that disappears. 📖

They will be like chaff blown from a threshing floor and smoke escaping through an open window.

**Help with these pictures:** Dew is water that settles outdoors.

Chaff is light waste separated from grain.

These pictures show how quickly they will disappear.

## Hosea 13:4

God says, “Yet I have been the LORD your God since Egypt. 📖

You know no God except Me.

There is no Savior besides Me.

## Hosea 13:5

I knew you in the wilderness, in the dry land. 📖

## Hosea 13:6

When they had pasture, they became satisfied. 📖

When they were satisfied, their hearts became proud.

So they forgot Me.

## Hosea 13:7

I will pounce on them like a lion and hide beside the path like a leopard. 📖

## Hosea 13:8

I will attack them like a bear robbed of her cubs and tear open their chests. 📖

There I will eat them like a lion, like a wild animal tearing them apart.

## Hosea 13:9

Israel, you are destroyed because you are against Me, your helper. 📖

## Hosea 13:10

Where is your king now, to save you in all your cities? 📖

Where are the rulers you asked, ‘Give me a king and princes’?

## Hosea 13:11

In My anger, I gave you a king. 📖

In My fierce anger, I took him away.

## Hosea 13:12

Ephraim’s wrongdoing is tied up, and his sin is stored away. 📖

**About this picture:** His wrongdoing is kept for judgment rather than forgotten.

## Hosea 13:13

Birth pains come on him, but he is an unwise son. 📖

When the time comes, he does not appear at the womb’s opening.

**About this picture:** Ephraim is pictured as a baby who fails to be born when the time comes.

## Hosea 13:14

I will ransom them from Sheol’s power and redeem them from Death. 📖

Death, where are your plagues?

Sheol, where is your sting?

Compassion is hidden from My eyes.

**Help with these words:** Sheol is the place of the dead.

Ransoming and redeeming mean rescuing or buying back.

Death and Sheol are addressed as if they are people.

**About this difficult verse:** The source includes both rescue words and the statement about hidden compassion.

Both are kept here for further editorial review.

## Hosea 13:15

Though he flourishes among his brothers, an east wind will come from the LORD, rising from the desert. 📖

His fountain will fail, and his spring will dry up.

The wind will take every valuable item from his treasury.

## Hosea 13:16

Samaria will bear her guilt because she rebelled against her God. 📖

They will fall by the sword.

Their little children will be smashed to pieces, and their pregnant women will be ripped open.”
