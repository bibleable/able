# Hosea 3 — Love again

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Hosea to love his wife again.

The passage connects this with God’s love for unfaithful Israel.

It also describes a long period without rulers and worship objects, followed by a return to God.

## Hosea 3:1

The LORD told me, “Go and love your wife again, though another loves her and she is unfaithful in marriage. 📖

Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites.

They turn to other gods and love offering raisin cakes to idols.”

## Hosea 3:2

So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver and a homer and a lethech of barley. 📖

**Help with the measures:** A shekel is a unit of weight.

A homer and a lethech are measures for grain.

The draft keeps the source’s amounts and units.

## Hosea 3:3

I told her, “You must live with me for many days. 📖

You must not be sexually unfaithful or belong to another man.

I will do the same for you.”

## Hosea 3:4

The Israelites must live many days without king or prince, sacrifice or sacred pillar, ephod or idol. 📖

**Help with these objects:** A sacred pillar is a standing stone used in worship.

An ephod is an object or garment connected with worship.

## Hosea 3:5

Afterward, Israel’s people will return and seek the LORD their God and David their king. 📖

In the last days, they will come trembling to the LORD and His goodness.
