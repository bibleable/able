# Hosea 5 — Judgment on priests, people, and rulers

**About this chapter**

God speaks against Israel and Judah.

Their actions and false worship keep them from returning to Him.

Pictures of traps, disease, and a lion describe the coming judgment.

## Hosea 5:1

God said, “Priests, hear this! 📖

People of Israel, pay attention!

Royal family, listen!

This judgment is against you.

You have been a trap at Mizpah and a net spread on Tabor.

## Hosea 5:2

The rebels are deeply involved in killing many people. 📖

But I will punish them all.

## Hosea 5:3

I know everything about Ephraim. 📖

Israel is not hidden from Me.

Ephraim, you have turned to prostitution, and Israel is unclean.

**About this picture:** Prostitution pictures unfaithfulness to God.

## Hosea 5:4

Their deeds do not allow them to return to their God. 📖

A spirit of prostitution is within them, and they do not know the LORD.

## Hosea 5:5

Israel’s pride speaks against them. 📖

Israel and Ephraim stumble in their sin.

Even Judah stumbles with them.

## Hosea 5:6

They go with their flocks and herds to seek the LORD. 📖

But they do not find Him.

He has withdrawn from them.

## Hosea 5:7

They have been unfaithful to the LORD and have borne children outside marriage. 📖

Now the New Moon will devour them and their land.

**About the children:** The accusation concerns the people’s unfaithfulness.

It does not say that a child’s birth determines the child’s worth.

**Help with “New Moon”:** This marks the beginning of a month.

The passage pictures it bringing destruction.

## Hosea 5:8

Blow the ram’s horn in Gibeah and the trumpet in Ramah! 📖

Raise the battle cry in Beth-aven.

Lead on, Benjamin!

**Help with “ram’s horn”:** This is a male sheep’s horn used as a trumpet.

## Hosea 5:9

Ephraim will be ruined on the day of punishment. 📖

Among Israel’s tribes, I announce what is certain.

## Hosea 5:10

Judah’s princes are like people who move boundary stones. 📖

I will pour My fury over them like water.

**Help with “boundary stones”:** These mark the edges of someone’s land.

Moving them can take land from its owner.

## Hosea 5:11

Ephraim is oppressed and crushed in judgment because he insists on following worthless idols. 📖

## Hosea 5:12

So I am like a moth to Ephraim and like decay to Judah’s people. 📖

**Help with these pictures:** Moths damage clothing.

Decay destroys things gradually.

These pictures describe God’s judgment.

## Hosea 5:13

Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound. 📖

Ephraim turned to Assyria and sent to the great king.

But that king cannot cure you or heal your wound.

## Hosea 5:14

I am like a lion to Ephraim and a young lion to Judah’s people. 📖

I Myself will tear them to pieces and go away.

I will carry them off where no one can rescue them.

## Hosea 5:15

Then I will return to My place until they admit their guilt and seek My face. 📖

In their suffering, they will seek Me earnestly.”

**Help with “seek My face”:** This means seeking God Himself and His favor.
