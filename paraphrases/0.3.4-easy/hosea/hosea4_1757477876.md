# Hosea 4 — The LORD’s case against Israel

**About this chapter**

The LORD brings charges against Israel’s people and priests.

He names violence, unfaithfulness, and the rejection of knowledge.

The judgments and the distinction between the men’s and women’s responsibility are kept.

## Hosea 4:1

People of Israel, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

The LORD has a case against the land’s people.

He says, “There is no truth, faithful love, or knowledge of God in the land!

## Hosea 4:2

Cursing, lying, murder, stealing, and adultery spread everywhere. 📖

One act of bloodshed follows another.

## Hosea 4:3

So the land mourns. 📖

Everyone living there will waste away, along with the field animals and birds.

Even the sea’s fish disappear.

## Hosea 4:4

But let no one argue or correct anyone. 📖

Your people are like those who argue with a priest.

## Hosea 4:5

You will stumble by day, and the prophet will stumble with you by night. 📖

So I will destroy your mother.

## Hosea 4:6

My people are destroyed because they lack knowledge. 📖

You have rejected knowledge, so I will reject you as My priests.

You have forgotten your God’s law, so I will forget your children.

## Hosea 4:7

The more they increased, the more they sinned against Me. 📖

They exchanged their Glory for something shameful.

## Hosea 4:8

They feed on My people’s sins and set their hearts on wrongdoing. 📖

**Help with this picture:** The priests benefit from sin instead of opposing it.

## Hosea 4:9

What happens to the people will happen to the priest. 📖

I will punish both for their ways and repay them for their deeds.

## Hosea 4:10

They will eat but not be satisfied. 📖

They will be sexually unfaithful but not increase.

They have abandoned the LORD to give themselves

## Hosea 4:11

to sexual unfaithfulness, wine, and new wine, which take away understanding. 📖

## Hosea 4:12

My people ask their wooden idols for answers. 📖

Their divining rods give them messages.

A spirit of prostitution leads them astray.

They have acted as prostitutes against their God.

**Help with “divining rods”:** These are sticks used to seek hidden answers.

**About the marriage picture:** Prostitution pictures unfaithfulness to God.

## Hosea 4:13

They sacrifice on mountaintops and burn offerings on hills under oak, poplar, and terebinth trees, because the shade is pleasant. 📖

So your daughters become prostitutes, and your sons’ wives commit adultery.

**Help with “adultery”:** This means sexual unfaithfulness in marriage.

## Hosea 4:14

I will not punish your daughters for prostitution or your sons’ wives for adultery. 📖

The men themselves go with prostitutes and offer sacrifices with shrine prostitutes.

So people without understanding will come to ruin.

**Help with “shrine prostitutes”:** These are prostitutes connected with places of worship.

## Hosea 4:15

Israel, though you act as a prostitute, may Judah avoid this guilt! 📖

Do not travel to Gilgal or go up to Beth-aven.

Do not swear, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!’

## Hosea 4:16

Israel is as stubborn as a stubborn young cow. 📖

Can the LORD now shepherd them like lambs in an open meadow?

## Hosea 4:17

Ephraim is joined to idols. 📖

Leave him alone!

## Hosea 4:18

When their alcohol is gone, they turn to prostitution. 📖

Their rulers dearly love disgrace.

## Hosea 4:19

The whirlwind has wrapped them in its wings. 📖

Their sacrifices will bring them shame.”

**Help with the whirlwind picture:** This pictures a powerful storm sweeping them away.
