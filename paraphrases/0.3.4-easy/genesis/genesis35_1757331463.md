# Genesis 35 — Return to Bethel and family deaths

**About this chapter**

Jacob returns to Bethel, removes foreign gods, and receives God’s blessing.

Rachel dies giving birth to Benjamin.

The chapter names Jacob’s sons and records Isaac’s death.

## Genesis 35:1

God told Jacob, “Go to Bethel and settle there. 📖

Build an altar to the God who appeared when you fled from Esau.”

## Genesis 35:2

Jacob told his household and everyone with him, “Remove the foreign gods among you. 📖

Purify yourselves and change your clothes.

**Help with “purify”:** Here this means making themselves clean for worship.

## Genesis 35:3

Let us go to Bethel. 📖

I will build an altar to God, who answered me in my distress.

He has been with me everywhere.”

## Genesis 35:4

They gave Jacob all their foreign gods and earrings. 📖

He buried them under the oak near Shechem.

## Genesis 35:5

As they left, terror from God fell on the surrounding cities. 📖

They did not pursue Jacob’s sons.

## Genesis 35:6

Jacob and everyone with him arrived at Luz, also called Bethel, in Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 35:7

He built an altar and named the place El-bethel. 📖

God had revealed Himself there when Jacob fled his brother.

## Genesis 35:8

Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died. 📖

She was buried under the oak below Bethel.

Jacob named it Allon-bacuth.

## Genesis 35:9

After Jacob returned from Paddan-aram, God appeared again and blessed him. 📖

## Genesis 35:10

God said, “Your name is Jacob, but you will now be called Israel.” 📖

God named him Israel.

## Genesis 35:11

God said, “I am God Almighty. 📖

Be fruitful and increase.

A nation, even a company of nations, will come from you, and kings will descend from you.

## Genesis 35:12

I give you the land I gave Abraham and Isaac. 📖

I will give it to your descendants too.”

## Genesis 35:13

God went up from the place where He spoke with Jacob. 📖

## Genesis 35:14

Jacob stood a stone pillar there. 📖

He poured a drink offering and oil on it.

## Genesis 35:15

He named the place where God spoke with him Bethel. 📖

## Genesis 35:16

They left Bethel. 📖

While still some distance from Ephrath, Rachel began a difficult labor.

## Genesis 35:17

During her severe labor, the midwife said, “Do not fear. 📖

You are having another son.”

**Help with “midwife”:** This is someone who helps a woman give birth.

## Genesis 35:18

As she breathed her last and died, she named him Ben-oni. 📖

His father named him Benjamin.

## Genesis 35:19

Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, also called Bethlehem. 📖

## Genesis 35:20

Jacob stood a pillar on her grave. 📖

It marks Rachel’s tomb to this day.

**About “to this day”:** This refers to the time of the account.

## Genesis 35:21

Israel traveled on and pitched his tent beyond the Tower of Eder. 📖

## Genesis 35:22

While Israel lived there, Reuben had sex with his father’s concubine Bilhah. 📖

Israel heard about it.

Jacob had twelve sons.

**Help with “concubine”:** This was a woman living as a man’s partner without the full status of a wife.

## Genesis 35:23

Leah’s sons were Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. 📖

## Genesis 35:24

Rachel’s sons were Joseph and Benjamin. 📖

## Genesis 35:25

Rachel’s servant Bilhah’s sons were Dan and Naphtali. 📖

## Genesis 35:26

Leah’s servant Zilpah’s sons were Gad and Asher. 📖

These were Jacob’s sons, born to him in Paddan-aram.

## Genesis 35:27

Jacob returned to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron. 📖

Abraham and Isaac had lived there.

## Genesis 35:28

Isaac lived 180 years. 📖

## Genesis 35:29

He breathed his last and died, old and full of years, and was gathered to his people. 📖

His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
