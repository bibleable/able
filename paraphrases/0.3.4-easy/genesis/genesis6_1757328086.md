# Genesis 6 — Human wickedness and God’s command to Noah

**About this chapter**

God sees the earth’s wickedness and violence.

Noah finds favor with Him.

God gives instructions for the ark and promises a covenant with Noah.

## Genesis 6:1

People began to increase on the earth, and daughters were born to them. 📖

## Genesis 6:2

The sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful. 📖

They took any they chose as wives.

**About these titles:** The draft keeps “sons of God” and “daughters of men.”

The passage does not explain their identities here.

## Genesis 6:3

The LORD said, “My Spirit will not contend with man forever, because he is mortal. 📖

His days will be 120 years.”

**Help with these words:** Contend means struggle or strive.

Mortal means subject to death.

The draft keeps the 120 years without adding a date or explanation of the period.

## Genesis 6:4

The Nephilim were on earth then and afterward. 📖

The sons of God had sex with the daughters of men, who bore them children.

These became the mighty men of old, famous men.

**About “Nephilim”:** The source gives this name without explaining it further here.

## Genesis 6:5

The LORD saw that human wickedness was great on earth. 📖

Every intention in the thoughts of the human heart was completely evil all the time.

## Genesis 6:6

The LORD regretted making human beings on earth. 📖

He was grieved in His heart.

## Genesis 6:7

The LORD said, “I will wipe the human beings I created from the earth. 📖

I will wipe out every person, animal, crawling creature, and bird.

I am grieved that I made them.”

## Genesis 6:8

But Noah found favor in the LORD’s eyes. 📖

## Genesis 6:9

This is Noah’s account. 📖

Noah was a righteous man, without blame in his generation.

He walked with God.

**Help with these words:** Righteous means right and just.

Walking with God pictures living in close relationship with Him.

## Genesis 6:10

Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖

## Genesis 6:11

The earth was corrupt before God and full of violence. 📖

## Genesis 6:12

God looked at the earth and saw its corruption. 📖

All living creatures on earth had corrupted their ways.

## Genesis 6:13

God told Noah, “The end of all living creatures has come before Me. 📖

Because of them, the earth is full of violence.

I will destroy them and the earth.

## Genesis 6:14

Make an ark from gopher wood. 📖

Make rooms inside and coat it with pitch inside and outside.

**Help with these words:** An ark is a large boat.

The source calls the wood “gopher wood.”

Pitch is a material used to seal the boat.

## Genesis 6:15

Build it 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high. 📖

**Help with “cubit”:** This measures from elbow to fingertips.

The draft keeps the source’s units.

## Genesis 6:16

Make a roof, finishing the ark’s walls one cubit from the top. 📖

Put a door in its side and make lower, middle, and upper decks.

## Genesis 6:17

I will bring floodwaters on the earth to destroy every creature under heaven that has the breath of life. 📖

Everything on earth will die.

## Genesis 6:18

But I will establish My covenant with you. 📖

You will enter the ark with your sons, your wife, and your sons’ wives.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 6:19

Bring two of every living creature into the ark, male and female, to keep them alive with you. 📖

## Genesis 6:20

Two of every kind of bird, animal, and crawling creature will come to you to be kept alive. 📖

## Genesis 6:21

Take every kind of food that is eaten. 📖

Gather it as food for yourselves and the animals.”

## Genesis 6:22

Noah did everything exactly as God commanded him. 📖
