# Genesis 2 — The seventh day and the garden

**About this chapter**

God completes His work and sets apart the seventh day.

The account describes the man, the garden and its rivers, God’s command, and the making of the woman.

## Genesis 2:1

The heavens and earth were completed, with everything in their vast number. 📖

## Genesis 2:2

By the seventh day, God had finished His work. 📖

On that day, He rested from all His work.

## Genesis 2:3

God blessed the seventh day and made it holy. 📖

On that day, He rested from all His completed work of creation.

**Help with “holy”:** This means set apart for God.

## Genesis 2:4

This is the account of the heavens and earth when they were created. 📖

It tells of the time when the LORD God made them.

## Genesis 2:5

No field shrub had appeared on earth, and no field plant had grown. 📖

The LORD God had not sent rain on the earth.

There was no man to work the ground.

## Genesis 2:6

But springs rose from the earth and watered the whole ground’s surface. 📖

## Genesis 2:7

The LORD God formed the man from the ground’s dust. 📖

He breathed the breath of life into the man’s nostrils.

The man became a living being.

## Genesis 2:8

The LORD God planted a garden in Eden, in the east. 📖

He placed the man He had formed there.

## Genesis 2:9

The LORD God made every tree pleasing to see and good for food grow from the ground. 📖

The tree of life stood in the garden’s middle.

So did the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.

## Genesis 2:10

A river flowed from Eden to water the garden. 📖

From there, it divided into four rivers.

## Genesis 2:11

The first river is named Pishon. 📖

It winds through the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold.

## Genesis 2:12

The gold there is pure. 📖

Bdellium and onyx are also found there.

**About these names:** The source names bdellium and onyx among the land’s valuable materials.

## Genesis 2:13

The second river is named Gihon. 📖

It winds through the whole land of Cush.

## Genesis 2:14

The third river is named Tigris and runs east of Assyria. 📖

The fourth river is the Euphrates.

## Genesis 2:15

The LORD God put the man in the Garden of Eden to work it and care for it. 📖

## Genesis 2:16

The LORD God commanded him, “You may freely eat from every tree in the garden, 📖

## Genesis 2:17

but not from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. 📖

On the day you eat from it, you will certainly die.”

## Genesis 2:18

The LORD God said, “It is not good for the man to be alone. 📖

I will make a suitable helper for him.”

## Genesis 2:19

The LORD God formed every field animal and bird from the ground. 📖

He brought them to the man to see what he would name each one.

Whatever the man called each living creature became its name.

## Genesis 2:20

The man named all the livestock, birds, and field animals. 📖

But no suitable helper was found for Adam.

**Help with “livestock”:** These are farm animals.

## Genesis 2:21

The LORD God made the man fall into a deep sleep. 📖

While he slept, God took one of his ribs and closed the place with flesh.

## Genesis 2:22

The LORD God made a woman from the rib He had taken from the man. 📖

He brought her to the man.

## Genesis 2:23

The man said, “Now this is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh. 📖

She will be called ‘woman,’ because she was taken from man.”

## Genesis 2:24

For this reason, a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife. 📖

They will become one flesh.

**Help with “one flesh”:** This pictures their close union as husband and wife.

## Genesis 2:25

The man and his wife were both naked, and they felt no shame. 📖
