# Genesis 25 — Abraham’s descendants and the twins

**About this chapter**

The chapter records Abraham’s death and descendants through Keturah and Ishmael.

It then tells of Esau and Jacob’s birth and the sale of Esau’s birthright.

## Genesis 25:1

Abraham had taken another wife named Keturah, 📖

## Genesis 25:2

who bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. 📖

## Genesis 25:3

Jokshan was the father of Sheba and Dedan. 📖

Dedan’s sons were the Asshurites, Letushites, and Leummites.

## Genesis 25:4

Midian’s sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. 📖

All these descended from Keturah.

## Genesis 25:5

Abraham left everything he owned to Isaac. 📖

## Genesis 25:6

While still alive, Abraham gave gifts to his concubines’ sons. 📖

He sent them away from Isaac to the eastern land.

**Help with “concubines”:** These were women living as partners without the full status of wives.

## Genesis 25:7

Abraham lived 175 years in total. 📖

## Genesis 25:8

He breathed his last and died at a very old age, old and content. 📖

He was gathered to his people.

**Help with “gathered to his people”:** This is an expression used here about his death.

## Genesis 25:9

Isaac and Ishmael buried him in Machpelah’s cave near Mamre. 📖

It was in the field of Ephron, son of Zohar the Hittite.

## Genesis 25:10

Abraham had bought this field from the Hittites. 📖

He was buried there with his wife Sarah.

## Genesis 25:11

After Abraham died, God blessed Isaac, who lived near Beer-lahai-roi. 📖

## Genesis 25:12

This is the account of Abraham’s son Ishmael. 📖

Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s female servant, bore him to Abraham.

## Genesis 25:13

These are Ishmael’s sons in birth order: Nebaioth his firstborn, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, 📖

## Genesis 25:14

Mishma, Dumah, Massa, 📖

## Genesis 25:15

Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. 📖

## Genesis 25:16

These were Ishmael’s sons, named by their villages and camps, twelve tribal princes. 📖

## Genesis 25:17

Ishmael lived 137 years in total. 📖

He breathed his last, died, and was gathered to his people.

## Genesis 25:18

His descendants settled from Havilah to Shur, near Egypt’s border as you go toward Asshur. 📖

They lived in hostility toward all their brothers.

## Genesis 25:19

This is the account of Abraham’s son Isaac. 📖

Abraham was Isaac’s father,

## Genesis 25:20

and Isaac was forty when he married Rebekah. 📖

She was the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean from Paddan-aram and sister of Laban the Aramean.

## Genesis 25:21

Isaac prayed to the LORD for his wife because she could not have children. 📖

The LORD heard him, and Rebekah became pregnant.

## Genesis 25:22

The children inside her struggled together. 📖

She asked, “Why is this happening to me?”

She went to inquire of the LORD.

## Genesis 25:23

He told her, “Two nations are in your womb. 📖

Two peoples from you will separate.

One will be stronger than the other, and the older will serve the younger.”

## Genesis 25:24

When she gave birth, there were indeed twins. 📖

## Genesis 25:25

The first came out red, covered with hair like a fur coat. 📖

They named him Esau.

## Genesis 25:26

His brother came out holding Esau’s heel, so he was named Jacob. 📖

Isaac was sixty when the twins were born.

## Genesis 25:27

The boys grew. 📖

Esau became a skilled hunter, a man of the field.

Jacob was quiet and stayed at home.

## Genesis 25:28

Isaac loved Esau because he enjoyed wild game. 📖

Rebekah loved Jacob.

**Help with “wild game”:** This is meat from hunted wild animals.

## Genesis 25:29

One day, Jacob cooked stew. 📖

Esau came from the field, very hungry.

## Genesis 25:30

Esau said, “Let me eat some red stew. 📖

I am starving!”

That is why he was also called Edom.

## Genesis 25:31

Jacob replied, “First sell me your birthright.” 📖

**Help with “birthright”:** This means the rights belonging to the firstborn son.

## Genesis 25:32

Esau said, “I am about to die. 📖

What use is a birthright to me?”

## Genesis 25:33

Jacob said, “Swear to me first.” 📖

Esau swore and sold Jacob his birthright.

## Genesis 25:34

Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. 📖

Esau ate, drank, rose, and left.

In this way, he despised his birthright.
