# Genesis 37 — Joseph’s dreams and sale

**About this chapter**

Joseph’s brothers hate him and his dreams.

They plan to kill him, then sell him.

Jacob is deceived by the bloodied robe and mourns.

## Genesis 37:1

Jacob lived in Canaan, where his father had lived. 📖

## Genesis 37:2

This is Jacob’s account. 📖

At seventeen, Joseph tended the flock with his brothers, sons of his father’s wives Bilhah and Zilpah.

He brought their father a bad report about them.

## Genesis 37:3

Israel loved Joseph more than his other sons because Joseph was born in his old age. 📖

He made Joseph a robe of many colors.

## Genesis 37:4

His brothers saw their father loved him most. 📖

They hated Joseph and could not speak kindly to him.

## Genesis 37:5

Joseph dreamed and told his brothers. 📖

They hated him even more.

## Genesis 37:6

He said, “Listen to my dream. 📖

## Genesis 37:7

We tied bundles of grain in a field. 📖

My bundle rose and stood upright.

Your bundles surrounded it and bowed to it.”

## Genesis 37:8

His brothers asked, “Do you intend to reign over us? 📖

Will you really rule us?”

They hated him more because of his dream and words.

**Help with “reign”:** This means rule as a king.

## Genesis 37:9

He dreamed again and told them, “The sun, moon, and eleven stars bowed to me!” 📖

## Genesis 37:10

He told his father and brothers. 📖

His father rebuked him, asking, “What is this dream?

Will your mother, brothers, and I bow to the ground before you?”

**Help with “rebuked”:** This means spoke firmly against him.

## Genesis 37:11

His brothers were jealous, but his father remembered his words. 📖

## Genesis 37:12

Later, his brothers went to pasture their father’s flocks near Shechem. 📖

## Genesis 37:13

Israel told Joseph, “Your brothers are tending the flocks at Shechem. 📖

Get ready.

I am sending you.”

Joseph replied, “I am ready.”

## Genesis 37:14

Israel said, “See how your brothers and flocks are doing and bring me word.” 📖

He sent Joseph from the Valley of Hebron.

Joseph arrived at Shechem,

## Genesis 37:15

where a man found him wandering in the field. 📖

He asked, “What are you looking for?”

## Genesis 37:16

Joseph replied, “My brothers. 📖

Please tell me where they are tending their flocks.”

## Genesis 37:17

The man said, “They left. 📖

I heard them say, ‘Let us go to Dothan.’”

Joseph followed and found them at Dothan.

## Genesis 37:18

They saw Joseph far away and plotted to kill him before he arrived. 📖

## Genesis 37:19

They said, “Here comes that dreamer! 📖

## Genesis 37:20

Let us kill him and throw him into a pit. 📖

We will say a vicious animal ate him.

Then we will see what happens to his dreams!”

## Genesis 37:21

Reuben heard and tried to rescue Joseph. 📖

He said, “Let us not kill him.

## Genesis 37:22

Do not spill his blood. 📖

Throw him into this wilderness pit, but do not harm him.”

Reuben planned to rescue Joseph and return him to their father.

## Genesis 37:23

Joseph arrived, and they stripped off his robe of many colors, 📖

## Genesis 37:24

and threw him into the empty pit. 📖

It had no water.

## Genesis 37:25

They sat to eat and saw Ishmaelites traveling from Gilead toward Egypt. 📖

Their camels carried spices, balm, and myrrh.

**Help with these goods:** Balm and myrrh are valuable plant products.

## Genesis 37:26

Judah asked, “What profit is there in killing our brother and hiding his blood? 📖

## Genesis 37:27

Let us sell him to the Ishmaelites rather than harm him. 📖

He is our brother, our own flesh.”

They agreed.

## Genesis 37:28

Midianite traders passed. 📖

The brothers pulled Joseph from the pit and sold him to the Ishmaelites for twenty shekels of silver.

The Ishmaelites took him to Egypt.

**Help with “shekels”:** These are units of weight.

## Genesis 37:29

Reuben returned and found Joseph gone. 📖

He tore his clothes,

## Genesis 37:30

went to his brothers, and said, “The boy is gone! 📖

What will I do?”

## Genesis 37:31

They killed a young goat and dipped Joseph’s robe in its blood. 📖

## Genesis 37:32

They sent the robe of many colors to their father. 📖

They said, “We found this.

Is it your son’s robe?”

## Genesis 37:33

He recognized it and said, “My son’s robe! 📖

A vicious animal ate him.

Joseph has been torn apart!”

## Genesis 37:34

Jacob tore his clothes, wore sackcloth around his waist, and mourned many days. 📖

**Help with “sackcloth”:** This is rough cloth worn to show grief.

## Genesis 37:35

All his sons and daughters tried to comfort him, but he refused. 📖

He said, “I will go down to Sheol mourning my son.”

He wept for Joseph.

**Help with “Sheol”:** This is the place of the dead.

## Genesis 37:36

The Midianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar, Pharaoh’s officer and captain of the guard. 📖
