# Genesis 8 — The waters recede and Noah leaves the ark

**About this chapter**

God remembers Noah and the animals.

The waters fall, Noah sends birds, and God tells him to leave the ark.

Noah offers sacrifices, and the LORD makes a promise about the earth.

## Genesis 8:1

God remembered Noah and all the wild animals and livestock with him in the ark. 📖

God sent wind over the earth, and the waters began to fall.

## Genesis 8:2

The deep’s springs and heaven’s floodgates closed. 📖

The rain from the sky stopped.

## Genesis 8:3

The waters steadily withdrew from the earth. 📖

After 150 days, they had gone down.

## Genesis 8:4

On the seventeenth day of the seventh month, the ark rested on the mountains of Ararat. 📖

## Genesis 8:5

The waters continued falling until the tenth month. 📖

On that month’s first day, the mountaintops became visible.

## Genesis 8:6

After forty days, Noah opened the ark’s window, which he had made, 📖

## Genesis 8:7

and sent out a raven. 📖

It flew back and forth until the waters had dried from the earth.

## Genesis 8:8

Noah sent a dove to see whether the water had withdrawn from the ground’s surface. 📖

## Genesis 8:9

The dove found no place to rest her foot and returned to Noah in the ark. 📖

Water still covered the whole earth’s surface.

He reached out and brought her back inside.

## Genesis 8:10

Noah waited seven more days and sent the dove out again. 📖

## Genesis 8:11

The dove returned in the evening with a freshly picked olive leaf in her beak. 📖

Noah knew the water had withdrawn from the earth.

## Genesis 8:12

He waited seven more days and sent her out again. 📖

This time, she did not return.

## Genesis 8:13

In Noah’s six hundred and first year, on the first day of the first month, the water had dried up. 📖

Noah removed the ark’s covering and saw that the ground’s surface was dry.

## Genesis 8:14

By the twenty-seventh day of the second month, the earth was completely dry. 📖

## Genesis 8:15

God spoke to Noah. 📖

## Genesis 8:16

He said, “Leave the ark with your wife, your sons, and their wives. 📖

## Genesis 8:17

Bring all the creatures with you: birds, livestock, and everything crawling on the ground. 📖

They can spread over the earth, have young, and increase in number.”

## Genesis 8:18

Noah came out with his sons, his wife, and his sons’ wives. 📖

## Genesis 8:19

Every living creature, crawling creature, and bird, everything moving on earth, left the ark, kind by kind. 📖

## Genesis 8:20

Noah built an altar to the LORD. 📖

He took from every kind of clean animal and clean bird and offered burnt offerings on it.

**Help with these words:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

Burnt offerings are sacrifices burned on it.

Clean is a category used for worship.

## Genesis 8:21

The LORD smelled the pleasing scent. 📖

He said in His heart, “Every intention of the human heart is evil from youth.

Even so, I will never again curse the ground because of humans.

I will never again destroy all living creatures as I have done.

## Genesis 8:22

As long as the earth lasts, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will continue.” 📖
