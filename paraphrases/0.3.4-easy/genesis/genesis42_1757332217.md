# Genesis 42 — Joseph’s brothers come for grain

**About this chapter**

Jacob sends ten sons to Egypt for food.

Joseph recognizes them, but they do not recognize him.

His accusations, their fears, and Jacob’s words are presented as each speaker’s words.

## Genesis 42:1

Jacob learned Egypt had grain. 📖

He asked his sons, “Why are you staring at one another?”

## Genesis 42:2

He added, “I heard Egypt has grain. 📖

Go and buy some for us, so we may live and not die.”

## Genesis 42:3

Ten of Joseph’s brothers went to Egypt to buy grain. 📖

## Genesis 42:4

Jacob kept Joseph’s brother Benjamin home. 📖

He said, “I am afraid he might be harmed.”

## Genesis 42:5

Israel’s sons joined those going to buy grain, because famine had spread to Canaan too. 📖

## Genesis 42:6

Joseph ruled the land and sold grain to all its people. 📖

His brothers came and bowed before him, faces to the ground.

## Genesis 42:7

Joseph recognized his brothers but treated them as strangers and spoke harshly. 📖

He asked, “Where are you from?”

They replied, “Canaan.

We came to buy food.”

## Genesis 42:8

Joseph recognized them, but they did not recognize him. 📖

## Genesis 42:9

He remembered his dreams about them. 📖

He said, “You are spies!

You came to see where our land is open to attack.”

**Help with “spies”:** These are people who secretly gather information about others.

## Genesis 42:10

They replied, “No, my lord. 📖

Your servants came to buy food.

## Genesis 42:11

We are all one man’s sons. 📖

Your servants are honest men, not spies.”

## Genesis 42:12

He said, “No. 📖

You came to see where our land is open to attack.”

## Genesis 42:13

They answered, “Your servants are twelve brothers, one man’s sons in Canaan. 📖

The youngest is with our father now, and one is no more.”

## Genesis 42:14

Joseph said, “As I said, you are spies! 📖

## Genesis 42:15

Here is your test. 📖

As surely as Pharaoh lives, you cannot leave unless your youngest brother comes here.

## Genesis 42:16

Send one of you to get him. 📖

The rest will stay imprisoned while your words are tested.

If they are untrue, then as surely as Pharaoh lives, you are spies!”

## Genesis 42:17

Joseph imprisoned them for three days. 📖

## Genesis 42:18

On the third day, he said, “I fear God. 📖

Do this and you will live.

## Genesis 42:19

If you are honest, leave one brother imprisoned. 📖

The rest may take grain home to feed your hungry households.

## Genesis 42:20

Bring your youngest brother to prove your words true, so you will not die.” 📖

They agreed.

## Genesis 42:21

They told one another, “We are being punished because of our brother. 📖

We saw his distress when he begged us, but we would not listen.

That is why we are suffering now.”

## Genesis 42:22

Reuben answered, “Did I not tell you not to sin against the boy? 📖

You would not listen.

Now we must answer for his blood!”

**Help with “answer for his blood”:** Reuben means being held responsible for his brother’s death.

## Genesis 42:23

They did not know Joseph understood them, because an interpreter stood between them. 📖

**Help with “interpreter”:** This person puts speech into another language.

## Genesis 42:24

Joseph turned away and wept. 📖

He returned and spoke to them.

He took Simeon and had him tied up before their eyes.

## Genesis 42:25

Joseph ordered their bags filled with grain and each man’s silver returned to his sack. 📖

He also ordered supplies for their journey.

This was done,

## Genesis 42:26

and they loaded the grain on their donkeys and left. 📖

## Genesis 42:27

Where they stayed that night, one brother opened his sack for donkey feed. 📖

He saw his silver at the sack’s opening.

## Genesis 42:28

He said, “My silver was returned! 📖

Here it is in my sack.”

They were dismayed and trembled.

They asked one another, “What has God done to us?”

## Genesis 42:29

They reached their father Jacob in Canaan and told him everything that happened. 📖

## Genesis 42:30

They said, “The man ruling the land spoke harshly and accused us of spying. 📖

## Genesis 42:31

We told him, ‘We are honest men, not spies. 📖

## Genesis 42:32

We are twelve brothers, one father’s sons. 📖

One is no more.

The youngest is with our father in Canaan.’

## Genesis 42:33

The ruler said, ‘Here is how I will know you are honest. 📖

Leave one brother with me.

Take food for your hungry households and go.

## Genesis 42:34

Bring your youngest brother back so I know you are honest, not spies. 📖

Then I will release your brother, and you may trade here.’”

## Genesis 42:35

As they emptied their sacks, each found his bag of silver. 📖

They and their father saw the silver and were dismayed.

## Genesis 42:36

Jacob said, “You have taken my sons from me. 📖

Joseph is gone, and Simeon is no more.

Now you want Benjamin.

Everything is against me!”

## Genesis 42:37

Reuben told his father, “You may kill my two sons if I do not bring him back. 📖

Put him in my care, and I will return him.”

## Genesis 42:38

Jacob replied, “My son will not go with you. 📖

His brother is dead, and he alone is left.

If he is harmed on the journey, you will bring my gray hair down to Sheol in sorrow.”

**Help with this picture:** Jacob means that grief would bring his old life to death.

Sheol means the place of the dead.

Jacob speaks from what he believes about Joseph.
