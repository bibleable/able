# Genesis 45 — Joseph makes himself known

**About this chapter**

Joseph reveals his identity and speaks about God’s purpose in sending him to Egypt.

Pharaoh invites the family to move there.

Jacob hears that Joseph lives.

## Genesis 45:1

Joseph could no longer control himself before his attendants. 📖

He cried, “Send everyone away!”

No attendants remained when he made himself known to his brothers.

## Genesis 45:2

He wept so loudly that the Egyptians heard, and Pharaoh’s household soon heard about it. 📖

## Genesis 45:3

He said, “I am Joseph! 📖

Is my father still alive?”

His brothers were too terrified before him to answer.

## Genesis 45:4

Joseph said, “Please come near.” 📖

They did.

He said, “I am your brother Joseph, whom you sold into Egypt!

## Genesis 45:5

Do not be distressed or angry with yourselves for selling me here. 📖

God sent me ahead of you to save lives.

## Genesis 45:6

The famine has lasted two years here. 📖

There will be five more years without plowing or harvesting.

## Genesis 45:7

God sent me ahead to preserve you as a remnant on earth and save your lives through a great rescue. 📖

**Help with “remnant”:** This means a group left alive.

## Genesis 45:8

God sent me here, rather than you. 📖

He made me a father to Pharaoh, master of his whole household, and ruler of all Egypt.

**Help with “father to Pharaoh”:** This pictures Joseph’s position as a trusted guide.

It does not describe biological parenthood.

## Genesis 45:9

Hurry back to my father. 📖

Tell him, ‘Your son Joseph says: God made me master over all Egypt.

Come to me without delay.

## Genesis 45:10

You will live near me in Goshen with your children, grandchildren, flocks, herds, and everything you own. 📖

## Genesis 45:11

I will provide for you there, because five famine years remain. 📖

Otherwise you, your household, and everything you own will be left with nothing.’

## Genesis 45:12

You and my brother Benjamin can see that I, Joseph, am speaking to you. 📖

## Genesis 45:13

Tell my father about all my splendor in Egypt and everything you saw. 📖

Bring him here quickly.”

**Help with “splendor”:** This means Joseph’s great honor and wealth.

## Genesis 45:14

Joseph embraced his brother Benjamin and wept. 📖

Benjamin wept too as they held each other.

## Genesis 45:15

Joseph kissed every brother and wept over them. 📖

Afterward, they talked with him.

## Genesis 45:16

Pharaoh’s household heard that Joseph’s brothers had come. 📖

Pharaoh and his servants were pleased.

## Genesis 45:17

Pharaoh told Joseph, “Tell your brothers, ‘Load your animals and return to Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 45:18

Bring your father and families back to me. 📖

I will give you Egypt’s best land, and you will eat from the land’s rich produce.’

## Genesis 45:19

You must also tell them, ‘Take Egyptian wagons for your young children and wives. 📖

Bring your father and come back.

## Genesis 45:20

Do not worry about your belongings. 📖

The best of all Egypt is yours.’”

## Genesis 45:21

Israel’s sons obeyed. 📖

Joseph gave them wagons as Pharaoh commanded, and supplies for the journey.

## Genesis 45:22

He gave each new clothes. 📖

He gave Benjamin three hundred shekels of silver and five sets of clothes.

**Help with “shekels”:** These are units of weight.

## Genesis 45:23

He sent his father ten donkeys carrying Egypt’s best goods. 📖

Ten female donkeys carried grain, bread, and supplies for his father’s journey.

## Genesis 45:24

Joseph sent his brothers away, saying, “Do not quarrel on the way!” 📖

## Genesis 45:25

The brothers left Egypt and reached their father Jacob in Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 45:26

They said, “Joseph is alive and rules all Egypt!” 📖

Jacob was stunned and did not believe them.

## Genesis 45:27

They told him all Joseph’s words. 📖

He saw the wagons Joseph sent to carry him.

Their father Jacob’s spirit revived.

## Genesis 45:28

Israel said, “Enough! 📖

My son Joseph is alive!

I will see him before I die.”
