# Genesis 19 — Lot’s rescue and Sodom’s destruction

**About this chapter**

Two angels visit Lot, who faces a violent crowd.

Lot’s family is brought out before the cities are destroyed.

The chapter also records the actions of Lot’s daughters and their children’s births.

The sexual violence and incest are retained in plain, non-graphic wording.

## Genesis 19:1

The two angels reached Sodom in the evening. 📖

Lot sat in the city gateway.

He rose to meet them and bowed with his face to the ground.

## Genesis 19:2

He said, “My lords, please come to your servant’s house. 📖

Wash your feet and stay the night.

You can leave early in the morning.”

They answered, “No, we will stay in the square.”

## Genesis 19:3

Lot urged them so strongly that they went to his house. 📖

He made them a feast and baked bread without yeast.

They ate.

## Genesis 19:4

Before they went to bed, all Sodom’s men, young and old, surrounded the house. 📖

## Genesis 19:5

They called, “Lot, where are the men who came to you tonight? 📖

Send them out so we can have sex with them!”

**About the demand:** The crowd is threatening sexual violence against the visitors.

## Genesis 19:6

Lot went outside and shut the door behind him. 📖

## Genesis 19:7

He pleaded, “Please, my brothers, do not do this wicked thing! 📖

## Genesis 19:8

I have two daughters who have never had sex with a man. 📖

Let me bring them out, and you can do whatever you please to them.

But do nothing to these men.

They have come under my roof’s protection.”

**About the speaker:** This is Lot’s offer to the crowd.

The account reports his words.

## Genesis 19:9

They replied, “Get out of the way! 📖

This foreigner came here, and now he acts as a judge!

We will treat you worse than them.”

They pressed against Lot and moved to break down the door.

## Genesis 19:10

The men inside reached out and pulled Lot into the house. 📖

They shut the door.

## Genesis 19:11

They struck the men at the entrance, young and old, with blindness. 📖

The men wore themselves out trying to find the door.

## Genesis 19:12

The two men asked Lot, “Do you have anyone else here? 📖

A son-in-law, sons, daughters, or anyone in the city belonging to you?

Get them out,

## Genesis 19:13

because we are about to destroy this place. 📖

The outcry to the LORD against its people is so great that He sent us to destroy it.”

## Genesis 19:14

Lot spoke to the men pledged to marry his daughters. 📖

He said, “Get up and leave!

The LORD is about to destroy the city!”

But his sons-in-law thought he was joking.

## Genesis 19:15

At dawn, the angels urged Lot, “Get up! 📖

Take your wife and your two daughters here, or you will be swept away in the city’s punishment.”

## Genesis 19:16

Lot hesitated. 📖

The men took his hand and his wife’s and two daughters’ hands.

They brought them safely outside the city because of the LORD’s compassion.

## Genesis 19:17

Once outside, one man said, “Run for your lives! 📖

Do not look back or stop anywhere on the plain.

Escape to the mountains, or you will be swept away!”

## Genesis 19:18

Lot replied, “No, my lords, please! 📖

## Genesis 19:19

I, your servant, have found favor with you. 📖

You have shown great kindness by saving my life.

But I cannot run to the mountains.

Disaster will overtake me, and I will die.

## Genesis 19:20

Look, there is a nearby town where I can escape. 📖

It is a small place.

Please let me flee there.

Is it not a small place?

Then my life will be saved.”

## Genesis 19:21

He answered, “Very well. 📖

I grant this request too and will not destroy that town.

## Genesis 19:22

Hurry and run there! 📖

I cannot do anything until you arrive.”

That is why the town was called Zoar.

## Genesis 19:23

The sun had risen over the land when Lot reached Zoar. 📖

## Genesis 19:24

The LORD rained sulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah, from the LORD out of heaven. 📖

**Help with “sulfur”:** This is a substance that burns.

## Genesis 19:25

He destroyed those cities and the whole plain. 📖

This included all the cities’ people and everything growing on the ground.

## Genesis 19:26

Lot’s wife looked back and became a pillar of salt. 📖

## Genesis 19:27

Early the next morning, Abraham returned to the place where he had stood before the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 19:28

He looked toward Sodom, Gomorrah, and all the plain. 📖

Smoke rose from the land like smoke from a furnace.

## Genesis 19:29

When God destroyed the plain’s cities, He remembered Abraham. 📖

He brought Lot out of the disaster destroying the cities where Lot had lived.

## Genesis 19:30

Lot and his two daughters left Zoar for the mountains because he was afraid to stay there. 📖

They lived in a cave.

## Genesis 19:31

The older daughter told the younger, “Our father is old. 📖

There is no man in the land to have sex with us, as people do everywhere.”

**About this claim:** These are the older daughter’s words.

## Genesis 19:32

“Come, let us get our father drunk with wine and have sex with him to preserve his family line.” 📖

## Genesis 19:33

That night, they got their father drunk with wine. 📖

The older daughter went in and had sex with him.

He did not know when she lay down or got up.

## Genesis 19:34

The next day, the older daughter said, “I had sex with my father last night. 📖

Let us make him drunk with wine again tonight.

Then you can have sex with him and preserve our father’s family line.”

## Genesis 19:35

They made him drunk again that night. 📖

The younger daughter went in and had sex with him.

He did not know when she lay down or got up.

## Genesis 19:36

Both Lot’s daughters became pregnant by their father. 📖

## Genesis 19:37

The older had a son and named him Moab. 📖

He is the father of today’s Moabites.

**About “today”:** This refers to the time of the account, here and in the next verse.

## Genesis 19:38

The younger also had a son and named him Ben-ammi. 📖

He is the father of today’s Ammonites.
