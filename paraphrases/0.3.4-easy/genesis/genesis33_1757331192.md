# Genesis 33 — Jacob and Esau meet

**About this chapter**

Jacob approaches Esau with his family.

The brothers embrace, and Esau accepts Jacob’s gift.

Jacob travels to Succoth and Shechem.

## Genesis 33:1

Jacob saw Esau coming with four hundred men. 📖

He divided the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two female servants.

## Genesis 33:2

He put the servants and their children first, Leah and her children next, and Rachel and Joseph last. 📖

## Genesis 33:3

Jacob went ahead and bowed to the ground seven times as he approached Esau. 📖

## Genesis 33:4

Esau ran to embrace him, threw his arms around his neck, and kissed him. 📖

Both wept.

## Genesis 33:5

Esau saw the women and children and asked who they were. 📖

Jacob answered, “The children God kindly gave your servant.”

## Genesis 33:6

The female servants and their children came and bowed. 📖

## Genesis 33:7

Leah and her children came and bowed. 📖

Then Joseph and Rachel came and bowed.

## Genesis 33:8

Esau asked, “Why did you send this whole company to meet me?” 📖

Jacob answered, “To find favor with you, my lord.”

## Genesis 33:9

Esau replied, “I have plenty, my brother. 📖

Keep what is yours.”

## Genesis 33:10

Jacob insisted, “Please, if I found favor with you, accept my gift. 📖

Seeing your face is like seeing God’s face, because you welcomed me.

## Genesis 33:11

Accept my gift, because God has been gracious and I have everything I need.” 📖

He urged Esau until he accepted.

## Genesis 33:12

Esau said, “Let us travel. 📖

I will go ahead.”

## Genesis 33:13

Jacob replied, “My lord knows the children are frail. 📖

The sheep and cattle with me are nursing young.

If driven hard even one day, all the animals will die.

## Genesis 33:14

Please go ahead of your servant. 📖

I will travel slowly, at the children’s and animals’ pace, until I reach you at Seir.”

## Genesis 33:15

Esau offered to leave some men. 📖

Jacob replied, “Why do that?

Let me find favor with my lord.”

## Genesis 33:16

That day, Esau headed back to Seir, 📖

## Genesis 33:17

but Jacob went to Succoth. 📖

He built himself a house and shelters for his livestock.

That is why the place was called Succoth.

## Genesis 33:18

After returning from Paddan-aram, Jacob reached Shechem in Canaan safely. 📖

He camped outside the city.

## Genesis 33:19

He bought the ground where he pitched his tent from the sons of Hamor, Shechem’s father. 📖

He paid a hundred pieces of silver.

## Genesis 33:20

He built an altar there and named it El-Elohe-Israel. 📖
