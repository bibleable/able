# Genesis 40 — The cupbearer’s and baker’s dreams

**About this chapter**

Two royal officials are imprisoned with Joseph.

Joseph explains their dreams and asks to be remembered.

The different outcomes and the cupbearer’s failure to remember him are kept.

## Genesis 40:1

Later, Egypt’s king’s cupbearer and baker offended their master. 📖

**Help with “cupbearer”:** This is the official who serves the king his drink.

## Genesis 40:2

Pharaoh was angry with his two officers, the chief cupbearer and chief baker, 📖

## Genesis 40:3

and imprisoned them in the guard captain’s house, where Joseph was imprisoned. 📖

## Genesis 40:4

The captain assigned Joseph to serve them personally. 📖

After they had been held for some time,

## Genesis 40:5

both the Egyptian king’s cupbearer and baker dreamed on the same night. 📖

Each dream had its own meaning.

## Genesis 40:6

Joseph saw them in the morning and noticed their distress. 📖

## Genesis 40:7

He asked the royal officers imprisoned with him in his master’s house, “Why do you look so unhappy today?” 📖

## Genesis 40:8

They said, “We both dreamed, but no one can explain our dreams.” 📖

Joseph replied, “Do dream meanings not belong to God?

Tell me.”

## Genesis 40:9

The chief cupbearer said, “In my dream, a grapevine stood before me, 📖

## Genesis 40:10

with three branches. 📖

It budded, its flowers opened, and its grapes ripened.

## Genesis 40:11

I held Pharaoh’s cup. 📖

I squeezed the grapes into it and placed it in his hand.”

## Genesis 40:12

Joseph said, “The three branches mean three days. 📖

## Genesis 40:13

Within three days, Pharaoh will lift up your head and restore your position. 📖

You will put his cup in his hand as before.

**Help with “lift up your head”:** Here it pictures restoring the official to favor.

## Genesis 40:14

When things go well, remember me. 📖

Show kindness and mention me to Pharaoh, so he will bring me out of prison.

## Genesis 40:15

I was kidnapped from the Hebrews’ land. 📖

Here too, I did nothing deserving this dungeon.”

## Genesis 40:16

The chief baker heard the favorable meaning and said, “I dreamed too. 📖

Three baskets of white bread were on my head.

## Genesis 40:17

The top basket held all kinds of baked goods for Pharaoh. 📖

Birds ate them from the basket on my head.”

## Genesis 40:18

Joseph said, “The three baskets mean three days. 📖

## Genesis 40:19

Within three days, Pharaoh will cut off your head and hang you on a tree. 📖

Birds will eat your body’s flesh.”

**About the words:** BSB says Pharaoh will “lift off your head,” contrasting with the cupbearer’s restored position.

## Genesis 40:20

On the third day, Pharaoh’s birthday, he gave a feast for all his officials. 📖

Before them, he lifted up the chief cupbearer’s and chief baker’s heads.

## Genesis 40:21

He restored the chief cupbearer, who again placed the cup in Pharaoh’s hand. 📖

## Genesis 40:22

But he hanged the chief baker, as Joseph had explained. 📖

## Genesis 40:23

The chief cupbearer did not remember Joseph. 📖

He forgot him.
