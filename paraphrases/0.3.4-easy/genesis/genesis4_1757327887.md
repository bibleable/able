# Genesis 4 — Cain, Abel, and their descendants

**About this chapter**

Cain kills his brother Abel and receives judgment and protection.

The chapter records Cain’s descendants, Lamech’s words, and the births of Seth and Enosh.

## Genesis 4:1

Adam had sex with his wife Eve. 📖

She became pregnant and gave birth to Cain.

She said, “With the LORD’s help, I have brought forth a man.”

## Genesis 4:2

Later, she gave birth to Cain’s brother Abel. 📖

Abel kept sheep, and Cain worked the soil.

## Genesis 4:3

In time, Cain brought some produce from the soil as an offering to the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 4:4

Abel brought the best portions from the firstborn of his flock. 📖

The LORD looked with favor on Abel and his offering,

## Genesis 4:5

but He had no regard for Cain and his offering. 📖

Cain became very angry, and his face fell.

**Help with “his face fell”:** This describes his unhappy expression.

## Genesis 4:6

The LORD asked Cain, “Why are you angry? 📖

Why has your face fallen?

## Genesis 4:7

If you do right, will you not be accepted? 📖

But if you refuse to do right, sin crouches at your door.

It desires you, but you must master it.”

**Help with the picture:** Sin is pictured as something waiting to seize Cain.

## Genesis 4:8

Cain told his brother Abel, “Let us go to the field.” 📖

While they were there, Cain attacked his brother Abel and killed him.

## Genesis 4:9

The LORD asked Cain, “Where is your brother Abel?” 📖

Cain answered, “I do not know!

Am I my brother’s keeper?”

## Genesis 4:10

The LORD replied, “What have you done? 📖

Your brother’s blood cries to Me from the ground.

## Genesis 4:11

Now you are cursed and driven from the ground. 📖

It opened its mouth to receive your brother’s blood from your hand.

## Genesis 4:12

When you work the ground, it will no longer give its crops to you. 📖

You will be a fugitive and wanderer on earth.”

**Help with “fugitive”:** This means someone fleeing from danger or pursuit.

## Genesis 4:13

Cain told the LORD, “My punishment is more than I can bear. 📖

## Genesis 4:14

Today You have driven me from the earth’s surface, and I will be hidden from Your face. 📖

I will be a fugitive and wanderer on earth.

Whoever finds me will kill me.”

## Genesis 4:15

The LORD replied, “Not so! 📖

If anyone kills Cain, Cain will be avenged seven times over.”

The LORD put a mark on Cain, so no one finding him would kill him.

**Help with “avenged”:** This means punishment would follow his killing.

**About the mark:** The account does not describe what it looked like.

## Genesis 4:16

Cain left the LORD’s presence and settled in Nod, east of Eden. 📖

## Genesis 4:17

Cain had sex with his wife, and she became pregnant and gave birth to Enoch. 📖

Cain built a city and named it after his son Enoch.

## Genesis 4:18

Enoch had a son named Irad. 📖

Irad was Mehujael’s father.

Mehujael was Methusael’s father, and Methusael was Lamech’s father.

## Genesis 4:19

Lamech married two women, Adah and Zillah. 📖

## Genesis 4:20

Adah gave birth to Jabal. 📖

He was the father of those who live in tents and raise livestock.

**Help with “livestock”:** These are farm animals.

## Genesis 4:21

His brother was named Jubal. 📖

He was the father of all who play the harp and flute.

## Genesis 4:22

Zillah gave birth to Tubal-cain, who made every kind of bronze and iron tool. 📖

Tubal-cain’s sister was Naamah.

## Genesis 4:23

Lamech told his wives, “Adah and Zillah, hear me! 📖

Wives of Lamech, listen to my words.

I killed a man for wounding me, a young man for striking me.

## Genesis 4:24

If Cain is avenged seven times, then Lamech seventy-seven times.” 📖

## Genesis 4:25

Adam had sex with his wife again. 📖

She had a son and named him Seth.

She said, “God has given me another offspring in Abel’s place, because Cain killed him.”

## Genesis 4:26

Seth also had a son and named him Enosh. 📖

At that time, people began to call on the LORD’s name.
