# Genesis 41 — Pharaoh’s dreams and Joseph’s authority

**About this chapter**

Pharaoh dreams twice, and Joseph explains the dreams by God’s help.

He recommends storing food for famine and is appointed over Egypt.

The dreams’ repeated details, seven-year periods, and family names are kept.

## Genesis 41:1

After two full years, Pharaoh dreamed he stood beside the Nile, 📖

## Genesis 41:2

and seven sleek, well-fed cows came from the river and grazed among the reeds. 📖

## Genesis 41:3

Seven sickly, thin cows followed from the Nile and stood beside them on the riverbank. 📖

## Genesis 41:4

The sickly, thin cows ate the seven sleek, well-fed cows. 📖

Pharaoh woke,

## Genesis 41:5

but slept again and dreamed a second time. 📖

Seven plump, ripe heads of grain grew on one stalk.

## Genesis 41:6

Seven thin heads of grain followed, scorched by the east wind. 📖

## Genesis 41:7

The thin heads swallowed the seven plump, ripe heads. 📖

Pharaoh woke and knew it was a dream.

## Genesis 41:8

In the morning, he was troubled and called all Egypt’s magicians and wise men. 📖

He told his dreams, but no one could explain them.

## Genesis 41:9

The chief cupbearer told Pharaoh, “Today I remember my failures. 📖

## Genesis 41:10

Pharaoh was angry and imprisoned me and the chief baker with the captain of the guard. 📖

## Genesis 41:11

One night, we each dreamed, and each dream had its own meaning. 📖

## Genesis 41:12

A young Hebrew, the captain’s servant, was with us. 📖

He explained each dream when we told him.

## Genesis 41:13

Everything happened as he said. 📖

I regained my position, and the other man was hanged.”

## Genesis 41:14

Pharaoh called Joseph, who was brought quickly from the dungeon. 📖

He shaved, changed clothes, and came before Pharaoh.

## Genesis 41:15

Pharaoh said, “I dreamed, but no one can explain it. 📖

I hear you can explain dreams when you hear them.”

## Genesis 41:16

Joseph replied, “I cannot do it myself. 📖

God will give Pharaoh a sound answer.”

## Genesis 41:17

Pharaoh said, “I dreamed I stood beside the Nile, 📖

## Genesis 41:18

and seven sleek, well-fed cows came from it and grazed among the reeds. 📖

## Genesis 41:19

Seven other cows followed, sickly, ugly, and thin. 📖

I never saw cows so ugly anywhere in Egypt!

## Genesis 41:20

The thin, ugly cows ate the first seven, well-fed cows. 📖

## Genesis 41:21

But no one could tell they had eaten them. 📖

They looked as ugly as before.

I woke.

## Genesis 41:22

I also dreamed of seven plump, ripe heads of grain on one stalk. 📖

## Genesis 41:23

Seven others followed, withered, thin, and scorched by the east wind. 📖

## Genesis 41:24

The thin heads swallowed the seven plump ones. 📖

I told the magicians, but none could explain it.”

## Genesis 41:25

Joseph said, “The two dreams are the same. 📖

God showed Pharaoh what He is about to do.

## Genesis 41:26

The seven good cows and seven ripe heads both mean seven years. 📖

The dreams mean the same thing.

## Genesis 41:27

The seven thin, ugly cows that followed also mean seven years. 📖

So do the seven worthless heads of grain scorched by the east wind.

These will be seven years of famine.

**Help with “famine”:** This is a time when there is not enough food.

## Genesis 41:28

God has shown Pharaoh what He will do, just as I said. 📖

## Genesis 41:29

Seven years of great plenty will come throughout Egypt, 📖

## Genesis 41:30

followed by seven famine years. 📖

Egypt’s plenty will be forgotten, and famine will ruin the land.

## Genesis 41:31

The famine will be so severe that people will not remember the plenty before it. 📖

## Genesis 41:32

The dream came in two versions because God has decided this and will do it soon. 📖

## Genesis 41:33

Pharaoh should choose a wise man with understanding and put him over Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 41:34

He should appoint officials to collect a fifth of Egypt’s harvest during the seven plentiful years. 📖

## Genesis 41:35

Under Pharaoh’s authority, they should gather all excess food from the good years. 📖

They should store grain safely for food in the cities.

## Genesis 41:36

It will be a reserve for Egypt’s seven famine years, so the country will not perish.” 📖

## Genesis 41:37

Pharaoh and all his officials liked the plan. 📖

## Genesis 41:38

Pharaoh asked, “Can we find anyone like him, with God’s Spirit in him?” 📖

## Genesis 41:39

He told Joseph, “God showed you all this. 📖

No one has understanding and wisdom like you.

## Genesis 41:40

You will manage my house, and all my people will obey your orders. 📖

Only the throne will make me greater than you.”

## Genesis 41:41

He added, “I put you over all Egypt.” 📖

## Genesis 41:42

Pharaoh took his signet ring off his finger and put it on Joseph’s finger. 📖

He dressed him in fine linen and put a gold chain around his neck.

**Help with these words:** A signet ring makes an identifying mark in a seal.

Linen is cloth made from flax.

## Genesis 41:43

Joseph rode in Pharaoh’s second chariot while men called ahead, “Bow the knee!” 📖

Pharaoh put him over all Egypt.

## Genesis 41:44

Pharaoh told Joseph, “I am Pharaoh, but no one in Egypt may lift hand or foot without your permission.” 📖

**Help with this picture:** Lifting a hand or foot pictures taking action.

Pharaoh is describing Joseph’s authority.

## Genesis 41:45

He named Joseph Zaphenath-paneah and gave him Asenath as his wife. 📖

She was Potiphera’s daughter, priest of On.

Joseph took charge of all Egypt.

## Genesis 41:46

Joseph was thirty when he entered Pharaoh’s service. 📖

He left Pharaoh’s presence and traveled throughout Egypt.

## Genesis 41:47

During the seven plentiful years, the land produced great harvests. 📖

## Genesis 41:48

Joseph collected all Egypt’s excess food during those seven years. 📖

In each city, he stored food from its surrounding fields.

## Genesis 41:49

He stored grain like the sea’s sand, too much to measure. 📖

He stopped keeping count.

## Genesis 41:50

Before the famine, Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, bore Joseph two sons. 📖

## Genesis 41:51

He named the firstborn Manasseh, saying, “God made me forget all my hardship and all my father’s household.” 📖

## Genesis 41:52

He named the second Ephraim, saying, “God made me fruitful in the land of my suffering.” 📖

**Help with “fruitful”:** Joseph pictures his growing family as fruit growing on a plant.

## Genesis 41:53

Egypt’s seven plentiful years ended, 📖

## Genesis 41:54

and the seven famine years began as Joseph said. 📖

Every country had famine, but Egypt had food throughout the land.

## Genesis 41:55

Extreme hunger came throughout Egypt, and people begged Pharaoh for food. 📖

He told all Egyptians, “Go to Joseph and do whatever he tells you.”

## Genesis 41:56

As famine spread everywhere, Joseph opened all the storehouses and sold grain to the Egyptians. 📖

Egypt’s famine was severe.

## Genesis 41:57

Every nation came to buy grain from Joseph in Egypt, because famine was severe over all the earth. 📖
