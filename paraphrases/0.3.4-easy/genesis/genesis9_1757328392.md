# Genesis 9 — God’s covenant and Noah’s family

**About this chapter**

God blesses Noah and his sons and gives commands about life and blood.

He makes a covenant with all living creatures and gives the rainbow as its sign.

The chapter also records Noah’s drunkenness and his words about his descendants.

## Genesis 9:1

God blessed Noah and his sons. 📖

He told them, “Have many children, increase, and fill the earth.

## Genesis 9:2

Every animal on earth, every bird, every crawling creature, and all sea fish will fear and dread you. 📖

They are handed over to you.

## Genesis 9:3

Everything that lives and moves will be food for you. 📖

I give you everything, just as I gave you the green plants.

## Genesis 9:4

But do not eat meat with its lifeblood still in it. 📖

## Genesis 9:5

I will certainly require the life of any person or animal that sheds your lifeblood. 📖

I will demand an account from anyone who takes another person’s life.

## Genesis 9:6

Whoever spills human blood will have his blood spilled by humans. 📖

God made human beings in His own image.

## Genesis 9:7

But you must have young and increase. 📖

Spread over the earth and increase on it.”

## Genesis 9:8

God spoke to Noah and his sons with him. 📖

## Genesis 9:9

He said, “I now establish My covenant with you and your descendants after you, 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 9:10

and with every living creature that was with you. 📖

This includes birds, livestock, and all wild animals, every living thing that left the ark.

## Genesis 9:11

I establish My covenant with you. 📖

Floodwaters will never again cut off all life.

There will never again be a flood to destroy the earth.”

## Genesis 9:12

God said, “This is the sign of My covenant with you and every living creature with you. 📖

It is for all future generations.

## Genesis 9:13

I have put My rainbow in the clouds. 📖

It will be the covenant’s sign between Me and the earth.

## Genesis 9:14

Whenever I bring clouds over the earth and the rainbow appears, 📖

## Genesis 9:15

I will remember My covenant with you and every living creature of every kind. 📖

The waters will never again become a flood to destroy all life.

## Genesis 9:16

Whenever the rainbow appears in the clouds, I will see it. 📖

I will remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of every kind on earth.”

## Genesis 9:17

God told Noah, “This is the sign of the covenant I established between Me and every creature on earth.” 📖

## Genesis 9:18

Noah’s sons who left the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖

Ham was Canaan’s father.

## Genesis 9:19

These three were Noah’s sons. 📖

From them, the whole earth was populated.

## Genesis 9:20

Noah worked the soil and began to plant a vineyard. 📖

## Genesis 9:21

He drank some wine, became drunk, and uncovered himself inside his tent. 📖

## Genesis 9:22

Ham, Canaan’s father, saw his father’s nakedness and told his two brothers outside. 📖

## Genesis 9:23

Shem and Japheth put a garment across their shoulders. 📖

Walking backward, they covered their father’s nakedness.

Their faces turned away, so they did not see him naked.

## Genesis 9:24

Noah woke from his drunkenness and learned what his youngest son had done to him. 📖

## Genesis 9:25

He said, “Cursed be Canaan! 📖

He will be a servant of servants to his brothers.”

**About these words:** Noah speaks the curse, and it names Canaan.

The draft keeps both the speaker and the named person.

## Genesis 9:26

He also said, “Blessed be the LORD, Shem’s God! 📖

May Canaan be Shem’s servant.

## Genesis 9:27

May God enlarge Japheth’s territory. 📖

May he live in Shem’s tents, and may Canaan be his servant.”

## Genesis 9:28

After the flood, Noah lived 350 years. 📖

## Genesis 9:29

Noah lived 950 years in total, and then he died. 📖
