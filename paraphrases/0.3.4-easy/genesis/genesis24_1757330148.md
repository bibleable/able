# Genesis 24 — A wife for Isaac

**About this chapter**

Abraham sends his servant to find a wife for Isaac among his relatives.

The servant prays, meets Rebekah, and tells her family everything.

The full repeated account and Rebekah’s own answer are kept.

## Genesis 24:1

Abraham was old and far along in years. 📖

The LORD had blessed him in every way.

## Genesis 24:2

He instructed his chief household servant, who managed everything he owned. 📖

He said, “Put your hand under my thigh,

**About this action:** It accompanies the oath described in the following verses.

## Genesis 24:3

and swear by the LORD, God of heaven and earth. 📖

You must not take a wife for my son from the Canaanites’ daughters among whom I live,

## Genesis 24:4

but go to my country and relatives to find a wife for my son Isaac.” 📖

## Genesis 24:5

The servant asked, “What if the woman will not follow me here? 📖

Should I take your son back to the land you left?”

## Genesis 24:6

Abraham replied, “Make sure you never take my son back there. 📖

## Genesis 24:7

The LORD, God of heaven, brought me from my father’s household and homeland. 📖

He spoke and swore, ‘I will give this land to your offspring.’

He will send His angel ahead of you, so you can take a wife for my son there.

## Genesis 24:8

If the woman will not follow you, you are released from my oath. 📖

But do not take my son back there.”

## Genesis 24:9

The servant put his hand under Abraham’s thigh and swore about this matter. 📖

## Genesis 24:10

The servant took ten of his master’s camels and many kinds of good things from his master. 📖

He left for Nahor’s hometown in Aram-naharaim.

## Genesis 24:11

Near evening, he made the camels kneel by the well outside town. 📖

It was the time women came to draw water.

## Genesis 24:12

He prayed, “LORD, God of my master Abraham, please give me success today. 📖

Show kindness to my master Abraham.

## Genesis 24:13

I stand beside the spring, and the townspeople’s daughters are coming for water. 📖

## Genesis 24:14

Let me say to a girl, ‘Please lower your jar so I can drink.’ 📖

Let her reply, ‘Drink, and I will water your camels too.’

May she be the one You appointed for Your servant Isaac.

Then I will know You showed kindness to my master.”

## Genesis 24:15

Before he finished praying, Rebekah came with her jar on her shoulder. 📖

Her father was Bethuel, son of Milcah, wife of Abraham’s brother Nahor.

## Genesis 24:16

The girl was very beautiful, a virgin who had never had sex with a man. 📖

She went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came back up.

## Genesis 24:17

The servant ran to meet her. 📖

He said, “Please give me a little water from your jar.”

## Genesis 24:18

She replied, “Drink, my lord.” 📖

She quickly lowered her jar into her hands and gave him a drink.

## Genesis 24:19

Afterward, she said, “I will draw water for your camels until they have enough.” 📖

## Genesis 24:20

She quickly emptied her jar into the trough and ran back to the well. 📖

She drew water for all his camels.

## Genesis 24:21

The man watched silently to see whether the LORD had made his journey succeed. 📖

## Genesis 24:22

After the camels drank, he took a gold ring weighing a beka and two gold bracelets weighing ten shekels. 📖

The bracelets were for her wrists.

**Help with “beka” and “shekels”:** These are units of weight.

## Genesis 24:23

He asked, “Whose daughter are you? 📖

Please tell me, is there room at your father’s house for us tonight?”

## Genesis 24:24

She replied, “I am Bethuel’s daughter. 📖

Milcah bore him to Nahor.”

## Genesis 24:25

She added, “We have plenty of straw and feed and room for you to stay.” 📖

## Genesis 24:26

The man bowed and worshiped the LORD, 📖

## Genesis 24:27

saying, “Praise the LORD, God of my master Abraham! 📖

He has not withheld His kindness and faithfulness from my master.

The LORD has led me to my master’s relatives’ house.”

## Genesis 24:28

The girl ran to tell her mother’s household what happened. 📖

## Genesis 24:29

Rebekah’s brother Laban hurried out to the man at the spring. 📖

## Genesis 24:30

He had seen the ring. 📖

He had also seen the bracelets on his sister’s wrists and heard Rebekah repeat the man’s words.

He went and found the man standing near the spring with the camels.

## Genesis 24:31

Laban said, “Come, you whom the LORD has blessed. 📖

Why stay outside?

I have prepared the house and a place for the camels.”

## Genesis 24:32

The man came to the house, and the camels were unloaded. 📖

They received straw and feed.

Water was brought to wash his feet and his companions’ feet.

## Genesis 24:33

A meal was set before him. 📖

He said, “I will not eat until I tell you why I came.”

Laban replied, “Please speak.”

## Genesis 24:34

He said, “I am Abraham’s servant. 📖

## Genesis 24:35

The LORD has greatly blessed my master and made him rich. 📖

He gave him sheep, cattle, silver, gold, male and female servants, camels, and donkeys.

## Genesis 24:36

My master’s wife Sarah bore him a son in her old age. 📖

My master gave him everything he owns.

## Genesis 24:37

My master made me swear this oath. 📖

He said, ‘Do not take a wife for my son from the Canaanites’ daughters where I live,

## Genesis 24:38

but go to my father’s household and relatives to find my son a wife.’ 📖

## Genesis 24:39

I asked, ‘What if the woman will not come with me?’ 📖

## Genesis 24:40

He answered, ‘The LORD, before whom I have walked, will send His angel with you and make your journey succeed. 📖

You can take a wife for my son from my relatives and father’s household.

## Genesis 24:41

When you reach my relatives, if they refuse to give her to you, you will be released from my oath.’ 📖

## Genesis 24:42

Today, when I came to the spring, I prayed. 📖

I said, ‘LORD, God of my master Abraham, please make my journey succeed.

## Genesis 24:43

I stand beside this spring. 📖

Suppose a young woman comes for water, and I ask, “Please give me water from your jar.”

## Genesis 24:44

If she replies, “Drink, and I will draw water for your camels too,” may she be the appointed woman. 📖

May she be the one the LORD chose for my master’s son.’

## Genesis 24:45

Before I finished praying in my heart, Rebekah came with her jar on her shoulder. 📖

She went to the spring and drew water.

I asked, ‘Please give me a drink.’

## Genesis 24:46

She quickly lowered the jar from her shoulder. 📖

She said, ‘Drink, and I will water your camels too.’

I drank, and she watered the camels.

## Genesis 24:47

I asked whose daughter she was. 📖

She said, ‘Bethuel’s daughter, the son Milcah bore to Nahor.’

I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her wrists.

## Genesis 24:48

I bowed, worshiped, and praised the LORD, God of my master Abraham. 📖

He led me on the right road to take my master’s brother’s granddaughter for his son.

## Genesis 24:49

Tell me if you will show kindness and faithfulness to my master. 📖

If not, tell me, so I can go elsewhere.”

## Genesis 24:50

Laban and Bethuel answered, “This comes from the LORD. 📖

We have no choice in it.

## Genesis 24:51

Here is Rebekah. 📖

Take her, and let her become your master’s son’s wife, as the LORD has decreed.”

## Genesis 24:52

Abraham’s servant heard this and bowed to the ground before the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 24:53

He gave Rebekah silver and gold jewels and clothes. 📖

He gave valuable gifts to her brother and mother too.

## Genesis 24:54

He and his men ate, drank, and stayed the night. 📖

Next morning, he said, “Send me to my master.”

## Genesis 24:55

Her brother and mother said, “Let her stay about ten days. 📖

Afterward, she may go.”

## Genesis 24:56

He replied, “Do not delay me. 📖

The LORD has made my journey succeed.

Send me so I can go to my master.”

## Genesis 24:57

They said, “We will call her and ask what she thinks.” 📖

## Genesis 24:58

They asked Rebekah, “Will you go with this man?” 📖

She replied, “I will go.”

## Genesis 24:59

They sent Rebekah, their sister, with her nurse, Abraham’s servant, and his men. 📖

## Genesis 24:60

They blessed Rebekah, saying, “Our sister, may you become the mother of thousands upon thousands! 📖

May your offspring possess their enemies’ gates.”

## Genesis 24:61

Rebekah and her servant girls rose, mounted the camels, and followed the man. 📖

The servant took Rebekah and left.

## Genesis 24:62

Isaac had just returned from Beer-lahai-roi. 📖

He was living in the Negev.

## Genesis 24:63

Early in the evening, Isaac went to the field to meditate. 📖

He looked up and saw the camels coming.

**Help with “meditate”:** This means thinking deeply.

## Genesis 24:64

Rebekah looked up, saw Isaac, and got down from her camel, 📖

## Genesis 24:65

asking, “Who is that man in the field coming to meet us?” 📖

The servant answered, “My master.”

She took her veil and covered herself.

## Genesis 24:66

The servant told Isaac everything he had done. 📖

## Genesis 24:67

Isaac brought Rebekah into his mother Sarah’s tent and married her. 📖

He loved her and was comforted after his mother’s death.
