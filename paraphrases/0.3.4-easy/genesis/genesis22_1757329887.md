# Genesis 22 — Abraham is tested

**About this chapter**

God tests Abraham concerning Isaac.

Abraham prepares the offering, but the LORD’s angel stops him.

God provides a ram and repeats His promise.

The chapter ends with news of Nahor’s family.

## Genesis 22:1

Some time later, God tested Abraham. 📖

He called, “Abraham!”

Abraham answered, “Here I am.”

## Genesis 22:2

God said, “Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love. 📖

Go to the land of Moriah.

Offer him as a burnt offering on a mountain I will show you.”

**Help with “burnt offering”:** This is a sacrifice burned on an altar for God.

## Genesis 22:3

Abraham rose early the next morning and saddled his donkey. 📖

He took two servants and his son Isaac.

He split wood for the burnt offering and left for the place God chose.

## Genesis 22:4

On the third day, Abraham looked up and saw the place far away. 📖

## Genesis 22:5

Abraham told his servants, “Stay here with the donkey. 📖

The boy and I will go there to worship.

Then we will return.”

## Genesis 22:6

Abraham put the offering’s wood on Isaac. 📖

He carried the fire and sacrificial knife himself.

The two walked on together.

## Genesis 22:7

Isaac said, “My father!” 📖

Abraham replied, “Here I am, my son.”

Isaac asked, “Here are the fire and wood.

Where is the lamb for the burnt offering?”

## Genesis 22:8

Abraham answered, “God Himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering, my son.” 📖

They walked on together.

## Genesis 22:9

At the place God chose, Abraham built an altar and arranged the wood. 📖

He tied Isaac and placed him on the altar, on top of the wood.

## Genesis 22:10

Abraham reached out and took the knife to kill his son. 📖

## Genesis 22:11

The LORD’s angel called from heaven, “Abraham, Abraham!” 📖

He replied, “Here I am.”

## Genesis 22:12

The angel said, “Do not lay a hand on the boy or do anything to him. 📖

Now I know you fear God, because you did not withhold your only son from me.”

## Genesis 22:13

Abraham looked up and saw a male sheep behind him, caught by its horns in a thicket. 📖

He took it and offered it as a burnt offering instead of his son.

## Genesis 22:14

Abraham named the place The LORD Will Provide. 📖

To this day, people say, “On the LORD’s mountain, it will be provided.”

**About “to this day”:** This refers to the time of the account.

## Genesis 22:15

The LORD’s angel called to Abraham from heaven a second time. 📖

## Genesis 22:16

He said, “I have sworn by Myself, declares the LORD. 📖

You have done this and have not withheld your only son.

## Genesis 22:17

So I will certainly bless you. 📖

I will increase your descendants like heaven’s stars and the seashore’s sand.

Your descendants will possess their enemies’ gates.

## Genesis 22:18

All earth’s nations will be blessed through your offspring, because you obeyed My voice.” 📖

## Genesis 22:19

Abraham returned to his servants, and they left together for Beersheba. 📖

Abraham settled there.

## Genesis 22:20

Some time later, Abraham heard, “Milcah has also borne sons to your brother Nahor. 📖

## Genesis 22:21

They are Uz the firstborn, his brother Buz, Kemuel the father of Aram, 📖

## Genesis 22:22

Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.” 📖

## Genesis 22:23

Bethuel became Rebekah’s father. 📖

Milcah bore these eight sons to Abraham’s brother Nahor.

## Genesis 22:24

Nahor’s concubine Reumah bore Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah. 📖

**Help with “concubine”:** This was a woman living as a man’s partner without the full status of a wife.
