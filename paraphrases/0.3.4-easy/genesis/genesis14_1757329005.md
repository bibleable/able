# Genesis 14 — Abram rescues Lot

**About this chapter**

Four kings defeat five kings and take Lot captive.

Abram rescues him.

Melchizedek blesses Abram, who refuses to take the king of Sodom’s possessions for himself.

## Genesis 14:1

In those days, four kings went to war. 📖

They were Amraphel of Shinar, Arioch of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer of Elam, and Tidal of Goiim.

## Genesis 14:2

They fought Bera of Sodom, Birsha of Gomorrah, Shinab of Admah, Shemeber of Zeboiim, and Bela’s king. 📖

Bela is also called Zoar.

## Genesis 14:3

Those five kings joined together in the Valley of Siddim, the Salt Sea. 📖

## Genesis 14:4

They had been under Chedorlaomer’s rule for twelve years. 📖

In the thirteenth year, they rebelled.

## Genesis 14:5

In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and his allied kings attacked. 📖

They defeated the Rephaites at Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzites at Ham, and the Emites at Shaveh-kiriathaim,

## Genesis 14:6

and the Horites around Mount Seir, as far as El-paran near the desert. 📖

## Genesis 14:7

They turned back to attack En-mishpat, also called Kadesh. 📖

They conquered the Amalekites’ whole territory and the Amorites living in Hazazon-tamar.

## Genesis 14:8

The kings of Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, and Bela, also called Zoar, marched out. 📖

They took battle positions in the Valley of Siddim

## Genesis 14:9

against Chedorlaomer of Elam, Tidal of Goiim, Amraphel of Shinar, and Arioch of Ellasar. 📖

It was four kings against five.

## Genesis 14:10

The Valley of Siddim was full of tar pits. 📖

As Sodom’s and Gomorrah’s kings fled, some men fell into the pits.

The survivors fled to the hills.

## Genesis 14:11

The four kings took all Sodom’s and Gomorrah’s goods and food and went away. 📖

## Genesis 14:12

They also took Abram’s nephew Lot and his possessions, because Lot lived in Sodom. 📖

## Genesis 14:13

Someone escaped and told Abram the Hebrew. 📖

Abram lived near the Oaks of Mamre the Amorite, brother of Eshcol and Aner.

All three had a treaty with Abram.

## Genesis 14:14

Abram heard that his relative was captured. 📖

He gathered 318 trained men born in his household and pursued the captors as far as Dan.

## Genesis 14:15

At night, Abram divided his forces and defeated Chedorlaomer’s army. 📖

He chased them to Hobah, north of Damascus.

## Genesis 14:16

He recovered all the goods, his relative Lot and Lot’s possessions, the women, and the other people. 📖

## Genesis 14:17

Abram returned from defeating Chedorlaomer and his allied kings. 📖

Sodom’s king met him in the Valley of Shaveh, also called the King’s Valley.

## Genesis 14:18

Melchizedek, king of Salem, brought bread and wine. 📖

He was priest of God Most High.

## Genesis 14:19

He blessed Abram, saying, “May God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth, bless Abram, 📖

## Genesis 14:20

and may God Most High be blessed! 📖

He handed your enemies over to you.”

Abram gave Melchizedek a tenth of everything.

## Genesis 14:21

Sodom’s king told Abram, “Give me the people, but keep the goods.” 📖

## Genesis 14:22

Abram answered Sodom’s king, “I have raised my hand to the LORD God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth, 📖

## Genesis 14:23

swearing not to accept even a thread, sandal strap, or anything of yours. 📖

Then you cannot say you made Abram rich.

## Genesis 14:24

I will accept only what my men have eaten and the share for those who came with me. 📖

Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre may take their share.”
