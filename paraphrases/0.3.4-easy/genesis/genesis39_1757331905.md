# Genesis 39 — Joseph in Potiphar’s house and prison

**About this chapter**

Joseph serves Potiphar and is put in charge of his household.

He refuses Potiphar’s wife, who then accuses him.

He is imprisoned, but the LORD remains with him.

## Genesis 39:1

Joseph was taken to Egypt. 📖

Potiphar, an Egyptian officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, bought him from the Ishmaelites who brought him.

## Genesis 39:2

The LORD was with Joseph. 📖

He became successful while serving his Egyptian master’s household.

## Genesis 39:3

His master saw that the LORD was with Joseph and made everything he did prosper, 📖

## Genesis 39:4

so Joseph found favor and became his personal attendant. 📖

Potiphar put him over the household and everything he owned.

## Genesis 39:5

From then on, the LORD blessed the Egyptian’s household because of Joseph. 📖

Everything Potiphar owned in house and field was blessed.

## Genesis 39:6

Potiphar left everything to Joseph and worried only about his own food. 📖

Joseph was well-built and handsome,

## Genesis 39:7

and later his master’s wife looked at him and said, “Have sex with me.” 📖

## Genesis 39:8

Joseph refused, saying, “My master worries about nothing in his house with me here. 📖

He entrusted everything to me.

## Genesis 39:9

No one here is greater than I am. 📖

He kept nothing from me except you, his wife.

How could I do this great evil and sin against God?”

## Genesis 39:10

She spoke to him day after day. 📖

He refused to have sex with her or even be near her.

## Genesis 39:11

One day, Joseph entered the house to work. 📖

No other household servant was inside.

## Genesis 39:12

She grabbed his cloak and said, “Have sex with me!” 📖

He left the cloak in her hand and ran outside.

## Genesis 39:13

She saw the cloak in her hand and that he had fled, 📖

## Genesis 39:14

so she called the servants. 📖

She said, “This Hebrew was brought here to humiliate us.

He came to have sex with me, but I screamed loudly.

## Genesis 39:15

When he heard my cry for help, he left his cloak beside me and ran out.” 📖

**About her story:** These are her accusations.

The preceding account describes Joseph refusing her and escaping.

## Genesis 39:16

She kept Joseph’s cloak beside her until his master returned. 📖

## Genesis 39:17

She told him the same story. 📖

She said, “The Hebrew slave you brought us came to humiliate me,

## Genesis 39:18

but when I screamed for help, he left his cloak beside me and ran outside.” 📖

## Genesis 39:19

His master heard his wife say what she claimed his slave had done. 📖

He became furious.

## Genesis 39:20

Joseph’s master had him put in the prison for the king’s prisoners. 📖

While Joseph was there,

## Genesis 39:21

the LORD was with him and showed him kindness. 📖

He gave Joseph favor with the prison warden.

**Help with “warden”:** This is the person in charge of a prison.

## Genesis 39:22

The warden put all prisoners in Joseph’s care. 📖

Joseph was responsible for everything done there.

## Genesis 39:23

The warden worried about nothing Joseph managed, because the LORD was with him. 📖

The LORD made everything Joseph did succeed.
