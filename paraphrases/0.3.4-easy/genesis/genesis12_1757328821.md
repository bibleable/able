# Genesis 12 — Abram is called and travels to Egypt

**About this chapter**

The LORD calls Abram to leave and promises blessing.

Abram travels to Canaan and then Egypt during a famine.

His request that Sarai call herself his sister leads to trouble with Pharaoh.

## Genesis 12:1

The LORD told Abram, “Leave your country, relatives, and father’s household. 📖

Go to the land I will show you.

## Genesis 12:2

I will make you a great nation and bless you. 📖

I will make your name great, so you will be a blessing.

## Genesis 12:3

I will bless those who bless you and curse those who curse you. 📖

All the earth’s families will be blessed through you.”

## Genesis 12:4

Abram left as the LORD directed, and Lot went with him. 📖

Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran.

## Genesis 12:5

Abram took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, and all the possessions and people they acquired in Haran. 📖

They left for Canaan and arrived there.

## Genesis 12:6

Abram traveled through the land as far as the Oak of Moreh at Shechem. 📖

The Canaanites were in the land then.

## Genesis 12:7

The LORD appeared to Abram and said, “I will give this land to your offspring.” 📖

Abram built an altar there to the LORD, who appeared to him.

**Help with these words:** Offspring means descendants.

An altar is a place for offerings to God.

## Genesis 12:8

Abram moved to the hills east of Bethel and pitched his tent. 📖

Bethel was west of it, and Ai was east.

He built an altar to the LORD and called on His name.

## Genesis 12:9

Abram continued toward the Negev. 📖

## Genesis 12:10

A severe famine struck the land. 📖

Abram went to Egypt to live for a while because of it.

**Help with “famine”:** This is a time when there is not enough food.

## Genesis 12:11

As he neared Egypt, Abram told Sarai, “I know you are a beautiful woman. 📖

## Genesis 12:12

When the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘This is his wife.’ 📖

They will kill me but let you live.

## Genesis 12:13

Please say you are my sister. 📖

Then they will treat me well because of you and spare my life.”

## Genesis 12:14

When Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw that Sarai was very beautiful. 📖

## Genesis 12:15

Pharaoh’s officials saw Sarai and praised her to him. 📖

She was taken into Pharaoh’s palace.

## Genesis 12:16

Pharaoh treated Abram well because of her. 📖

Abram gained sheep, cattle, male and female donkeys, male and female servants, and camels.

## Genesis 12:17

But the LORD struck Pharaoh and his household with severe plagues because of Abram’s wife Sarai. 📖

## Genesis 12:18

Pharaoh called Abram and asked, “What have you done to me? 📖

Why did you not tell me she was your wife?

## Genesis 12:19

Why did you say she was your sister, so I took her as my wife? 📖

Here is your wife.

Take her and go!”

## Genesis 12:20

Pharaoh gave his men orders about Abram. 📖

They sent him away with his wife and all his possessions.
