# Genesis 32 — Jacob prepares to meet Esau and wrestles at night

**About this chapter**

Jacob hears that Esau is coming with four hundred men.

He prays and sends gifts ahead.

At night, he wrestles with a man and receives the name Israel.

## Genesis 32:1

Jacob went on his way, and God’s angels met him. 📖

## Genesis 32:2

When he saw them, he said, “This is God’s camp.” 📖

He named the place Mahanaim.

## Genesis 32:3

Jacob sent messengers ahead to Esau, his brother, in Seir, the country of Edom. 📖

## Genesis 32:4

He instructed them, “Tell my master Esau what his servant Jacob says. 📖

I have stayed with Laban until now.

## Genesis 32:5

I have oxen, donkeys, flocks, and male and female servants. 📖

I sent this message to my master to find favor with you.”

## Genesis 32:6

The messengers returned and said, “We went to Esau. 📖

He is coming to meet you with four hundred men.”

## Genesis 32:7

Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed. 📖

He divided his people, flocks, herds, and camels into two camps.

## Genesis 32:8

He thought, “If Esau attacks one camp, the other can escape.” 📖

## Genesis 32:9

Jacob prayed, “God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac! 📖

LORD, You told me to return to my country and relatives, and You would make me prosper.

## Genesis 32:10

I am unworthy of all Your kindness and faithfulness to Your servant. 📖

I crossed the Jordan with only my staff.

Now I have become two camps.

## Genesis 32:11

Please rescue me from my brother Esau. 📖

I fear he will attack me and the mothers and children with me.

## Genesis 32:12

But You said, ‘I will certainly make you prosper. 📖

I will make your offspring like sea sand, too many to count.’”

## Genesis 32:13

Jacob stayed there that night and chose a gift for Esau from what he had brought. 📖

## Genesis 32:14

He chose 200 female goats, 20 male goats, 200 female sheep, and 20 male sheep, 📖

## Genesis 32:15

30 milk camels with their young, 40 cows, 10 bulls, 20 female donkeys, and 10 male donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 32:16

He put servants in charge of separate herds. 📖

He said, “Go ahead and leave space between the herds.”

## Genesis 32:17

He told the first servant, “Esau will ask whose servant you are and where you are going. 📖

He will ask whose animals are in front of you,

## Genesis 32:18

Tell him, ‘They belong to your servant Jacob and are a gift for my lord Esau. 📖

Jacob is behind us.’”

## Genesis 32:19

He told the second, third, and all the servants following the herds, “Say the same when you meet Esau. 📖

## Genesis 32:20

Also tell him, ‘Your servant Jacob is behind us.’” 📖

Jacob thought, “I will calm Esau with the gift ahead.

Then I can face him.

Perhaps he will accept me.”

## Genesis 32:21

The gifts went ahead while Jacob stayed in the camp that night. 📖

## Genesis 32:22

During the night, Jacob took his two wives, two female servants, and eleven sons across the Jabbok’s ford. 📖

**Help with “ford”:** This is a place where people can cross a stream.

## Genesis 32:23

He sent them and all his possessions across the stream. 📖

## Genesis 32:24

Jacob was alone. 📖

A man wrestled with him until dawn.

## Genesis 32:25

The man saw he could not overpower Jacob. 📖

He struck Jacob’s hip socket and knocked it out of joint as they wrestled.

## Genesis 32:26

The man said, “Let me go. 📖

It is daybreak.”

Jacob replied, “Not unless you bless me.”

## Genesis 32:27

The man asked his name. 📖

He answered, “Jacob.”

## Genesis 32:28

The man said, “Your name will now be Israel, not Jacob. 📖

You have struggled with God and men and have prevailed.”

## Genesis 32:29

Jacob asked, “Please tell me your name.” 📖

He replied, “Why do you ask?”

He blessed Jacob there.

## Genesis 32:30

Jacob named the place Peniel. 📖

He said, “I have seen God face to face, yet my life was spared.”

## Genesis 32:31

The sun rose as he passed Penuel, limping because of his hip. 📖

## Genesis 32:32

To this day, Israelites do not eat the tendon attached to the hip socket. 📖

Jacob’s hip socket was struck near that tendon.

**Help with “tendon”:** This connects muscle to bone.

**About “to this day”:** This refers to the time of the account.
