# Genesis 38 — Judah and Tamar

**About this chapter**

The chapter records Judah’s family and Tamar’s treatment.

Tamar conceives by Judah after disguising herself.

Judah recognizes his failure, and Tamar gives birth to twins.

The sexual actions and severe judgments are retained in plain wording.

## Genesis 38:1

About then, Judah left his brothers and settled near Hirah the Adullamite. 📖

## Genesis 38:2

Judah saw the daughter of Shua, a Canaanite, and married her. 📖

He had sex with her.

## Genesis 38:3

She became pregnant and had a son. 📖

Judah named him Er.

## Genesis 38:4

She became pregnant again and named her next son Onan. 📖

## Genesis 38:5

She had another son at Chezib and named him Shelah. 📖

## Genesis 38:6

Judah found Tamar as a wife for his firstborn Er. 📖

## Genesis 38:7

Er was wicked before the LORD, and the LORD put him to death. 📖

## Genesis 38:8

Judah told Onan, “Have sex with your brother’s wife. 📖

Do your duty as her brother-in-law and produce offspring for your brother.”

## Genesis 38:9

Onan knew the offspring would not be his. 📖

Whenever he had sex with his brother’s wife, he spilled his semen on the ground.

He did this to avoid producing offspring for his brother.

**Help with “semen”:** This is fluid released from male sexual organs.

## Genesis 38:10

This was wicked before the LORD, who put Onan to death too. 📖

## Genesis 38:11

Judah told Tamar, “Stay as a widow in your father’s house until Shelah grows up.” 📖

He thought, “He may die like his brothers.”

Tamar went to her father’s house.

## Genesis 38:12

Much later, Judah’s wife, Shua’s daughter, died. 📖

After mourning, Judah went to his sheep-shearers at Timnah with Hirah the Adullamite, his friend.

## Genesis 38:13

Tamar heard that her father-in-law was going to shear sheep at Timnah, 📖

## Genesis 38:14

so she removed her widow’s clothes and covered her face with a veil to disguise herself. 📖

She sat at Enaim’s entrance on the road to Timnah.

Shelah was grown, but she had not been given to him as a wife.

## Genesis 38:15

Judah thought she was a prostitute because her face was covered. 📖

**Help with “prostitute”:** This is someone who provides sexual acts for payment or other benefits.

## Genesis 38:16

He did not know she was his daughter-in-law. 📖

He said, “Let me have sex with you.”

She asked, “What will you give me?”

## Genesis 38:17

Judah answered, “A young goat from my flock.” 📖

She replied, “Only if you leave a pledge until you send it.”

**Help with “pledge”:** This is something kept to guarantee payment.

## Genesis 38:18

He asked what pledge she wanted. 📖

She answered, “Your seal, cord, and the staff in your hand.”

He gave them, had sex with her, and she became pregnant by him.

**Help with “seal”:** This makes an identifying mark.

## Genesis 38:19

Tamar left, removed her veil, and put on her widow’s clothes again. 📖

## Genesis 38:20

Judah sent Hirah the Adullamite with the goat to collect his things. 📖

Hirah could not find her.

## Genesis 38:21

He asked the local men, “Where is the shrine prostitute beside the road at Enaim?” 📖

They answered, “There has been none here.”

**Help with “shrine prostitute”:** This means a prostitute connected with a place of worship.

## Genesis 38:22

Hirah told Judah, “I could not find her. 📖

The men said no shrine prostitute had been there.”

## Genesis 38:23

Judah said, “Let her keep the things, or we will be laughed at. 📖

I sent the goat, but you did not find her.”

## Genesis 38:24

About three months later, Judah heard, “Tamar has acted as a prostitute and is pregnant.” 📖

He said, “Bring her out and burn her to death!”

## Genesis 38:25

As she was brought out, Tamar sent Judah a message. 📖

She said, “The owner of these things made me pregnant.

Please examine whose seal, cord, and staff they are.”

## Genesis 38:26

Judah recognized them and said, “She is more righteous than I am. 📖

I did not give her to Shelah.”

He never had sex with her again.

## Genesis 38:27

When Tamar gave birth, there were twins. 📖

## Genesis 38:28

One put out a hand, and the midwife tied scarlet thread around his wrist. 📖

She said, “This one came first.”

**Help with these words:** A midwife helps women give birth.

Scarlet means bright red.

## Genesis 38:29

He pulled his hand back, and his brother came out. 📖

She said, “You broke out first!”

He was named Perez.

## Genesis 38:30

The brother with scarlet thread on his wrist came out next. 📖

He was named Zerah.
