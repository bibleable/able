# Genesis 10 — The descendants of Noah’s sons

**About this chapter**

This chapter records the descendants of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

It names peoples, cities, and boundaries after the flood.

The names and family relationships follow BSB.

## Genesis 10:1

This is the account of Noah’s sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖

They also had sons after the flood.

## Genesis 10:2

Japheth’s sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. 📖

## Genesis 10:3

Gomer’s sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. 📖

## Genesis 10:4

Javan’s sons were Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittites, and the Rodanites. 📖

## Genesis 10:5

From these, the coastal peoples spread into their territories. 📖

They were divided by languages and by clans within their nations.

## Genesis 10:6

Ham’s sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 10:7

Cush’s sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. 📖

Raamah’s sons were Sheba and Dedan.

## Genesis 10:8

Cush was Nimrod’s father. 📖

Nimrod began to be a mighty one on earth.

## Genesis 10:9

He was a mighty hunter before the LORD. 📖

So people say, “Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before the LORD.”

## Genesis 10:10

His kingdom began in Babylon, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, in Shinar’s land. 📖

## Genesis 10:11

From that land, he went into Assyria. 📖

There he built Nineveh, Rehoboth-Ir, Calah,

## Genesis 10:12

and Resen, between Nineveh and the great city of Calah. 📖

## Genesis 10:13

Mizraim was the father of the Ludites, Anamites, Lehabites, Naphtuhites, 📖

## Genesis 10:14

the Pathrusites, Casluhites, and Caphtorites. 📖

The Philistines came from the Casluhites.

## Genesis 10:15

Canaan was the father of Sidon, his firstborn, and of the Hittites, 📖

## Genesis 10:16

the Jebusites, Amorites, Girgashites, 📖

## Genesis 10:17

the Hivites, Arkites, Sinites, 📖

## Genesis 10:18

the Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites. 📖

Later, the Canaanite clans scattered.

## Genesis 10:19

Canaan’s borders stretched from Sidon toward Gerar as far as Gaza. 📖

They went toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim, as far as Lasha.

## Genesis 10:20

These are Ham’s sons, grouped by clans, languages, lands, and nations. 📖

## Genesis 10:21

Shem, Japheth’s older brother, also had sons. 📖

He was the ancestor of all Eber’s sons.

## Genesis 10:22

Shem’s sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. 📖

## Genesis 10:23

Aram’s sons were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash. 📖

## Genesis 10:24

Arphaxad was Shelah’s father, and Shelah was Eber’s father. 📖

## Genesis 10:25

Eber had two sons. 📖

One was named Peleg because the earth was divided in his days.

His brother was named Joktan.

## Genesis 10:26

Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, 📖

## Genesis 10:27

Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, 📖

## Genesis 10:28

Obal, Abimael, Sheba, 📖

## Genesis 10:29

Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. 📖

All these were Joktan’s sons.

## Genesis 10:30

Their territory stretched from Mesha to Sephar in the eastern hill country. 📖

## Genesis 10:31

These are Shem’s sons, grouped by clans, languages, lands, and nations. 📖

## Genesis 10:32

These are the clans of Noah’s sons, grouped by their generations and nations. 📖

From them, the earth’s nations spread after the flood.
