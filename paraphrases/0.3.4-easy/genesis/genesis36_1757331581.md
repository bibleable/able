# Genesis 36 — Esau’s descendants and Edom’s rulers

**About this chapter**

This chapter records Esau’s family, the Horite families, Edom’s kings, and the chiefs.

Repeated lists and different source spellings are kept.

The account identifies Esau as Edom.

## Genesis 36:1

This is the account of Esau, also called Edom. 📖

## Genesis 36:2

Esau married daughters of Canaan. 📖

They included Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Oholibamah, daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite,

## Genesis 36:3

and Basemath, daughter of Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth. 📖

## Genesis 36:4

Adah bore Esau a son, Eliphaz. 📖

Basemath bore Reuel,

## Genesis 36:5

and Oholibamah bore Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. 📖

These were Esau’s sons born in Canaan.

## Genesis 36:6

Esau took his wives, sons, daughters, and everyone in his household. 📖

He took livestock, all his other animals, and all the property he gained in Canaan.

He moved far from Jacob, his brother.

## Genesis 36:7

They had too many possessions to live together. 📖

The land could not support them because of their livestock.

## Genesis 36:8

Esau, also called Edom, settled around Mount Seir. 📖

## Genesis 36:9

This is the account of Esau, father of the Edomites, around Mount Seir. 📖

## Genesis 36:10

Esau’s sons included Eliphaz, son of his wife Adah, and Reuel, son of his wife Basemath. 📖

## Genesis 36:11

Eliphaz’s sons were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. 📖

## Genesis 36:12

Eliphaz’s concubine Timna bore Amalek. 📖

These were the grandsons of Esau’s wife Adah.

**Help with “concubine”:** This was a woman living as a partner without the full status of a wife.

## Genesis 36:13

Reuel’s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. 📖

They were the grandsons of Esau’s wife Basemath.

## Genesis 36:14

Esau’s wife Oholibamah, daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon, bore him Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. 📖

## Genesis 36:15

These were the chiefs among Esau’s sons. 📖

Eliphaz, Esau’s firstborn, had Chiefs Teman, Omar, Zepho, Kenaz,

## Genesis 36:16

Korah, Gatam, and Amalek. 📖

These were Eliphaz’s chiefs in Edom, Adah’s grandsons.

## Genesis 36:17

Esau’s son Reuel had Chiefs Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. 📖

They were Reuel’s chiefs in Edom and grandsons of Esau’s wife Basemath.

## Genesis 36:18

Esau’s wife Oholibamah had Chiefs Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. 📖

They descended from Oholibamah, Esau’s wife and Anah’s daughter.

## Genesis 36:19

These were Esau’s sons and their chiefs. 📖

Esau is also called Edom.

## Genesis 36:20

These were the sons of Seir the Horite living in the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 📖

## Genesis 36:21

Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. 📖

They were Horite chiefs, descended from Seir in Edom.

## Genesis 36:22

Lotan’s sons were Hori and Hemam. 📖

Timna was Lotan’s sister.

## Genesis 36:23

Shobal’s sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. 📖

## Genesis 36:24

Zibeon’s sons were Aiah and Anah. 📖

This Anah found hot springs in the wilderness while caring for his father Zibeon’s donkeys.

## Genesis 36:25

Anah’s children were Dishon and Oholibamah, Anah’s daughter. 📖

## Genesis 36:26

Dishon’s sons were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. 📖

## Genesis 36:27

Ezer’s sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. 📖

## Genesis 36:28

Dishan’s sons were Uz and Aran. 📖

## Genesis 36:29

The Horite chiefs were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 📖

## Genesis 36:30

Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. 📖

These were the Horite chiefs by their divisions in Seir.

## Genesis 36:31

These kings ruled Edom before any king ruled the Israelites. 📖

## Genesis 36:32

Bela, son of Beor, ruled Edom. 📖

His city was Dinhabah.

## Genesis 36:33

Bela died, and Jobab, son of Zerah from Bozrah, ruled in his place. 📖

## Genesis 36:34

Jobab died, and Husham from the Temanites’ land ruled in his place. 📖

## Genesis 36:35

Husham died, and Hadad, son of Bedad, ruled in his place. 📖

Hadad defeated Midian in Moab’s country.

His city was Avith.

## Genesis 36:36

Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah ruled in his place. 📖

## Genesis 36:37

Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth on the Euphrates ruled in his place. 📖

## Genesis 36:38

Shaul died, and Baal-hanan, son of Achbor, ruled in his place. 📖

## Genesis 36:39

Baal-hanan, son of Achbor, died, and Hadad ruled in his place. 📖

His city was Pau.

His wife was Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, the daughter of Me-zahab.

## Genesis 36:40

These were Esau’s chiefs by their families and regions: Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, 📖

## Genesis 36:41

Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, 📖

## Genesis 36:42

Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, 📖

## Genesis 36:43

Magdiel, and Iram. 📖

These were Edom’s chiefs by their settlements in the land they owned.

Esau was the father of the Edomites.
