# Genesis 30 — More children and Jacob’s growing flocks

**About this chapter**

The chapter records children born to Bilhah, Zilpah, Leah, and Rachel, with the reasons given for their names.

Jacob and Laban agree on wages, and Jacob’s flocks increase.

The account’s breeding details are kept without adding a scientific explanation.

## Genesis 30:1

Rachel saw she had no children for Jacob and envied her sister. 📖

She told Jacob, “Give me children, or I will die!”

## Genesis 30:2

Jacob became angry and said, “Am I in God’s place? 📖

He has withheld children from you.”

## Genesis 30:3

She said, “Here is my servant Bilhah. 📖

Have sex with her, so she can bear children for me.

Then I too can build a family through her.”

## Genesis 30:4

Rachel gave Bilhah to Jacob as a wife, and he had sex with her. 📖

## Genesis 30:5

Bilhah became pregnant and bore him a son. 📖

## Genesis 30:6

Rachel said, “God has vindicated me. 📖

He heard my plea and gave me a son.”

She named him Dan.

**Help with “vindicated”:** This means shown to be in the right.

## Genesis 30:7

Rachel’s servant Bilhah became pregnant again and bore Jacob a second son. 📖

## Genesis 30:8

Rachel said, “In my great struggles, I wrestled with my sister and won.” 📖

She named him Naphtali.

## Genesis 30:9

Leah saw she had stopped having children and gave her servant Zilpah to Jacob as a wife. 📖

## Genesis 30:10

Leah’s servant Zilpah bore Jacob a son. 📖

## Genesis 30:11

Leah said, “How fortunate!” 📖

She named him Gad.

## Genesis 30:12

Leah’s servant Zilpah bore Jacob a second son, 📖

## Genesis 30:13

and Leah said, “How happy I am! 📖

The women call me happy.”

She named him Asher.

## Genesis 30:14

During the wheat harvest, Reuben found mandrakes in a field and brought them to Leah, his mother. 📖

Rachel asked Leah, “Please give me some of your son’s mandrakes.”

**Help with “mandrakes”:** These are a kind of plant.

## Genesis 30:15

Leah replied, “Was taking my husband not enough? 📖

Do you want my son’s mandrakes too?”

Rachel answered, “He may have sex with you tonight in exchange for the mandrakes.”

## Genesis 30:16

Jacob came from the field that evening. 📖

Leah met him and said, “Come with me.

I hired you with my son’s mandrakes.”

He had sex with her that night.

## Genesis 30:17

God listened to Leah. 📖

She became pregnant and bore Jacob a fifth son.

## Genesis 30:18

Leah said, “God rewarded me for giving my servant to my husband.” 📖

She named him Issachar.

## Genesis 30:19

Leah became pregnant again and bore Jacob a sixth son. 📖

## Genesis 30:20

She said, “God gave me a good gift. 📖

Now my husband will honor me, because I bore him six sons.”

She named him Zebulun.

## Genesis 30:21

Afterward, Leah had a daughter and named her Dinah. 📖

## Genesis 30:22

God remembered Rachel, listened to her, and enabled her to have children. 📖

## Genesis 30:23

She became pregnant and had a son. 📖

She said, “God has taken away my shame.”

## Genesis 30:24

She named him Joseph and said, “May the LORD give me another son.” 📖

## Genesis 30:25

After Joseph’s birth, Jacob told Laban, “Send me home to my own land. 📖

## Genesis 30:26

Give me my wives and children, for whom I served you, and let me go. 📖

You know how hard I worked for you.”

## Genesis 30:27

Laban replied, “If I have found favor with you, please stay. 📖

Through divination, I learned that the LORD blessed me because of you.”

**Help with “divination”:** This means seeking hidden messages about events.

## Genesis 30:28

He added, “Name your wages, and I will pay them.” 📖

## Genesis 30:29

Jacob replied, “You know how I served you and how your livestock flourished under my care. 📖

## Genesis 30:30

Before I came, you had little. 📖

Now your wealth has greatly increased.

The LORD blessed you wherever I set foot.

When can I provide for my own household?”

## Genesis 30:31

Laban asked, “What can I give you?” 📖

Jacob replied, “You need not give me anything.

If you do one thing, I will keep shepherding and caring for your flocks.

## Genesis 30:32

Let me remove every speckled or spotted sheep, every dark lamb, and every spotted or speckled goat today. 📖

They will be my wages.

## Genesis 30:33

My honesty will speak for me when you inspect my wages later. 📖

Any goats I have without speckles or spots, or lambs not dark-colored, will count as stolen.”

## Genesis 30:34

Laban said, “Agreed. 📖

Let it be as you said.”

## Genesis 30:35

That day, Laban removed all streaked or spotted male goats and speckled or spotted female goats. 📖

He removed every one with any white and every dark lamb.

He put them in his sons’ care.

## Genesis 30:36

He put three days’ travel between himself and Jacob. 📖

Jacob cared for the rest of Laban’s flocks.

## Genesis 30:37

Jacob took fresh poplar, almond, and plane branches. 📖

He peeled off bark to expose the white wood.

## Genesis 30:38

He put the peeled branches in the drinking troughs before the flocks. 📖

When the flocks were ready to mate and came to drink,

## Genesis 30:39

and they mated before the branches. 📖

They bore streaked, speckled, or spotted young.

## Genesis 30:40

Jacob separated the young and made the others face the streaked, dark-colored sheep in Laban’s flocks. 📖

He kept his own animals apart from Laban’s.

## Genesis 30:41

When stronger females were ready to mate, Jacob put branches in the troughs where they could see them. 📖

They bred before the branches.

## Genesis 30:42

He did not set out branches for weak animals. 📖

The weaker animals became Laban’s, and the stronger became Jacob’s.

## Genesis 30:43

Jacob became extremely prosperous. 📖

He had large flocks, male and female servants, camels, and donkeys.
