# Genesis 50 — Jacob’s burial and Joseph’s last words

**About this chapter**

Jacob is buried in Canaan.

Joseph reassures his frightened brothers.

Before dying, he speaks of God’s promise and asks that his bones be carried from Egypt.

## Genesis 50:1

Joseph fell over his father’s face, wept over him, and kissed him. 📖

## Genesis 50:2

He told the physicians serving him to embalm his father Israel. 📖

They did so,

**Help with these words:** Physicians are people trained to treat illness.

Embalming means treating a dead body to preserve it.

## Genesis 50:3

and took the forty days required for embalming. 📖

The Egyptians mourned him for seventy days.

## Genesis 50:4

After the mourning, Joseph told Pharaoh’s court, “If you favor me, please tell Pharaoh 📖

**Help with “court”:** Here this means the officials around the ruler.

## Genesis 50:5

my father made me swear an oath. 📖

He said, ‘I am about to die.

Bury me in the tomb I dug for myself in Canaan.’

Let me bury my father, and I will return.”

## Genesis 50:6

Pharaoh replied, “Go and bury your father as he made you swear.” 📖

## Genesis 50:7

Joseph went to bury his father. 📖

All Pharaoh’s servants went too: the elders of his household and all Egypt’s elders,

## Genesis 50:8

along with Joseph’s whole household, his brothers, and his father’s household. 📖

Only their children, flocks, and herds stayed in Goshen.

## Genesis 50:9

Chariots and horsemen also went. 📖

The group traveling together was extremely large.

## Genesis 50:10

At Atad’s threshing floor across the Jordan, they wept and cried out loudly in grief. 📖

Joseph mourned his father seven days.

**Help with “threshing floor”:** This is a place where grain is separated from stalks and husks.

## Genesis 50:11

The local Canaanites saw the mourning there. 📖

They said, “This is a solemn Egyptian mourning ceremony.”

So that place across the Jordan is called Abel-mizraim.

## Genesis 50:12

Jacob’s sons obeyed his instructions. 📖

## Genesis 50:13

They carried him to Canaan and buried him in Machpelah’s cave, in the field near Mamre. 📖

Abraham bought it from Ephron the Hittite as a burial place.

## Genesis 50:14

After burying his father, Joseph returned to Egypt. 📖

His brothers and everyone who went with him for the burial returned too.

## Genesis 50:15

Their father was dead, and Joseph’s brothers said, “What if Joseph holds a grudge? 📖

He will repay all the evil we did to him.”

## Genesis 50:16

They sent Joseph this message: “Before he died, your father commanded, 📖

## Genesis 50:17

‘Tell Joseph: Please forgive your brothers’ wrongdoing and sin, for they wronged you.’ 📖

Now please forgive the wrongdoing of your father’s God’s servants.”

Joseph wept when the message reached him.

## Genesis 50:18

His brothers also came, bowed before him, and said, “We are your slaves!” 📖

## Genesis 50:19

Joseph replied, “Do not fear. 📖

Am I in God’s place?

## Genesis 50:20

You intended evil against me, but God intended good. 📖

His purpose was to save many lives, as is happening today.

## Genesis 50:21

Do not fear. 📖

I will provide for you and your little ones.”

He reassured his brothers and spoke kindly.

## Genesis 50:22

Joseph and his father’s household stayed in Egypt. 📖

Joseph lived 110 years.

## Genesis 50:23

He saw Ephraim’s sons to the third generation. 📖

The sons of Manasseh’s son Machir were raised on Joseph’s knees.

**About this picture:** The words describe Joseph’s close care for the children.

## Genesis 50:24

Joseph told his brothers, “I am about to die. 📖

God will surely visit you and bring you from this land.

He will bring you to the land promised by oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”

## Genesis 50:25

Joseph made Israel’s sons swear an oath. 📖

He said, “God will surely attend to you.

Then carry my bones from here.”

## Genesis 50:26

Joseph died at 110. 📖

They embalmed his body and put it in a coffin in Egypt.

**Help with “embalmed”:** They treated his dead body to preserve it.
