# Genesis 1 — God makes the world

**About this chapter**

This chapter tells how God made the world.

## Genesis 1:1

In the beginning, God made the heavens and the earth. 📖

**Help with words:** The heavens are the skies and what is above them.

## Genesis 1:2

The earth had no shape and was empty. 📖

Darkness covered the deep water.

God’s Spirit hovered over the water.

**Help with “hovered”:** This means remaining over the water.

## Genesis 1:3

God said, “Let there be light.” 📖

Then there was light.

## Genesis 1:4

God saw that the light was good. 📖

God separated the light from the darkness.

## Genesis 1:5

God called the light “day” and the darkness “night.” 📖

Evening came, and morning came.

This was day 1.

## Genesis 1:6

God said, “Let there be a space between the waters. 📖

Let it separate the water below from the water above.”

## Genesis 1:7

God made the space. 📖

It separated the water below from the water above.

This happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:8

God called the space “sky.” 📖

Evening came, and morning came.

This was day 2.

## Genesis 1:9

God said, “Let the water under the sky gather in one place. 📖

Let dry land appear.”

This happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:10

God called the dry land “earth.” 📖

God called the gathered water “seas.”

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:11

God said, “Let the earth grow plants with seeds. 📖

Let trees grow fruit with seeds, each of its own kind.”

This happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:12

The earth grew plants with seeds, each of its own kind. 📖

Trees grew fruit with seeds, each of its own kind.

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:13

Evening came, and morning came. 📖

This was day 3.

## Genesis 1:14

God said, “Let there be lights in the sky to separate day from night. 📖

Let them be signs marking seasons, days, and years.”

## Genesis 1:15

“Let these lights in the sky shine on the earth.” 📖

This happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:16

God made 2 great lights. 📖

The greater light would rule the day.

The smaller light would rule the night.

God also made the stars.

## Genesis 1:17

God placed the lights in the sky to shine on the earth. 📖

## Genesis 1:18

The lights ruled the day and night. 📖

They separated light from darkness.

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:19

Evening came, and morning came. 📖

This was day 4.

## Genesis 1:20

God said, “Let the water be filled with living things. 📖

Let birds fly above the earth, across the open sky.”

## Genesis 1:21

God made the great sea creatures. 📖

God made every kind of living thing moving in the water.

God made every kind of bird.

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:22

God blessed them. 📖

God said, “Have many young and fill the seas.

Let the birds also grow in number on the earth.”

## Genesis 1:23

Evening came, and morning came. 📖

This was day 5.

## Genesis 1:24

God said, “Let the earth have every kind of land animal. 📖

Let there be farm animals, crawling creatures, and wild animals.”

This happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:25

God made every kind of wild animal and farm animal. 📖

God made every kind of creature that crawls on the ground.

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:26

God said, “Let us make human beings in our image, to be like us. 📖

Let them rule the fish of the sea and the birds of the air.

Let them rule the livestock, the whole earth, and every creature crawling on it.”

**Help with words:** In God’s image means made to be like God and to represent him.

Livestock means farm animals.

## Genesis 1:27

God made human beings in his own image. 📖

He made them in God's image.

He made them male and female.

## Genesis 1:28

God blessed them. 📖

God said, “Have children and grow in number.

Fill the earth and bring it under your control.

Rule the fish of the sea and the birds of the air.

Rule every creature that crawls on the earth.”

## Genesis 1:29

God said, “I give you every plant with seeds on the whole earth. 📖

I give you every tree with seeds in its fruit.

These will be your food.”

## Genesis 1:30

“I give every green plant as food to all land animals, birds, and crawling creatures. 📖

This is food for every creature that breathes.”

This happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:31

God looked at everything he had made. 📖

It was very good.

Evening came, and morning came.

This was day 6.
