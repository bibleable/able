# Genesis 28 — Jacob’s departure and dream at Bethel

**About this chapter**

Isaac blesses Jacob and sends him to Laban’s family.

Jacob dreams of a ladder and hears the LORD’s promise.

He names the place Bethel and makes a conditional vow.

## Genesis 28:1

Isaac called Jacob, blessed him, and commanded, “Do not marry a Canaanite woman. 📖

## Genesis 28:2

Go now to Paddan-aram, to your mother’s father Bethuel’s house. 📖

Marry one of the daughters of Laban, your mother’s brother.

## Genesis 28:3

May God Almighty bless you, make you fruitful, and increase your descendants into a company of peoples. 📖

## Genesis 28:4

May He give Abraham’s blessing to you and your descendants. 📖

Then you may possess this land where you live as a foreigner, which God gave Abraham.”

## Genesis 28:5

Isaac sent Jacob to Paddan-aram, to Laban, son of Bethuel the Aramean. 📖

Laban was Rebekah’s brother.

Rebekah was Jacob’s and Esau’s mother.

## Genesis 28:6

Esau learned that Isaac blessed Jacob and sent him to find a wife in Paddan-aram. 📖

Isaac commanded him not to marry a Canaanite woman,

## Genesis 28:7

and Jacob obeyed his father and mother and went to Paddan-aram. 📖

## Genesis 28:8

Esau saw that Isaac disapproved of the Canaanite women. 📖

## Genesis 28:9

He went to Ishmael and married Mahalath in addition to his other wives. 📖

She was the daughter of Abraham’s son Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth.

## Genesis 28:10

Jacob left Beersheba for Haran. 📖

## Genesis 28:11

He reached a place and stayed the night because the sun had set. 📖

He put a stone under his head and lay down to sleep.

## Genesis 28:12

He dreamed of a ladder resting on earth, with its top reaching heaven. 📖

God’s angels went up and down it.

## Genesis 28:13

The LORD stood at its top and said, “I am the LORD, God of your father Abraham and of Isaac. 📖

I will give you and your descendants the land where you lie.

## Genesis 28:14

Your descendants will be like earth’s dust. 📖

You will spread west, east, north, and south.

All earth’s families will be blessed through you and your offspring.

## Genesis 28:15

I am with you and will watch over you wherever you go. 📖

I will bring you back here.

I will not leave you until I have done what I promised.”

## Genesis 28:16

Jacob woke and said, “The LORD is surely here, and I did not know it.” 📖

## Genesis 28:17

He was afraid and said, “How awesome this place is! 📖

It is God’s house, the gate of heaven!”

## Genesis 28:18

Early next morning, he stood the stone from under his head upright as a pillar. 📖

He poured oil on its top,

## Genesis 28:19

and named the place Bethel. 📖

The city had previously been called Luz.

## Genesis 28:20

Jacob made a vow. 📖

“If God stays with me, protects me on this journey, and gives me food and clothes,

## Genesis 28:21

so I return safely to my father’s house, then the LORD will be my God. 📖

## Genesis 28:22

This stone pillar will be God’s house. 📖

I will certainly give You a tenth of everything You give me.”
