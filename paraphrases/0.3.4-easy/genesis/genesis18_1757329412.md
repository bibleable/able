# Genesis 18 — The visitors and Abraham’s plea for Sodom

**About this chapter**

The LORD appears to Abraham, and three men receive his hospitality.

Sarah hears a promise about a son.

Abraham pleads for Sodom, and the LORD answers each of his questions.

## Genesis 18:1

The LORD appeared to Abraham by the Oaks of Mamre in the day’s heat. 📖

Abraham sat at his tent’s entrance.

## Genesis 18:2

Abraham looked up and saw three men standing nearby. 📖

He ran from his tent to meet them and bowed low to the ground.

## Genesis 18:3

Abraham said, “My lord, if I have found favor with you, please do not pass your servant by. 📖

## Genesis 18:4

Let some water be brought so you can wash your feet and rest under the tree. 📖

## Genesis 18:5

I will bring some bread to refresh you, since you have come past your servant. 📖

Afterward, you may continue your journey.”

They replied, “Yes, do as you have said.”

## Genesis 18:6

Abraham hurried into the tent and told Sarah, “Quick! 📖

Prepare three seahs of fine flour.

Knead it and bake bread.”

**Help with “seahs”:** These are measures for flour or grain.

## Genesis 18:7

Abraham ran to the herd and chose a tender, fine calf. 📖

He gave it to a servant, who hurried to prepare it.

## Genesis 18:8

Abraham brought curds, milk, and the prepared calf and placed them before the men. 📖

He stood beside them under the tree while they ate.

**Help with “curds”:** These are soft lumps formed from milk.

## Genesis 18:9

They asked, “Where is your wife Sarah?” 📖

He replied, “There, in the tent.”

## Genesis 18:10

The LORD said, “I will certainly return at this time next year. 📖

Your wife Sarah will have a son!”

Sarah was behind him, listening at the tent’s entrance.

## Genesis 18:11

Abraham and Sarah were old and far along in years. 📖

Sarah was past the age of childbearing.

## Genesis 18:12

She laughed to herself and thought, “I am worn out, and my master is old. 📖

Will I now have this pleasure?”

## Genesis 18:13

The LORD asked Abraham, “Why did Sarah laugh and ask, ‘Can I really bear a child when I am old?’ 📖

## Genesis 18:14

Is anything too difficult for the LORD? 📖

I will return at the appointed time, about a year from now.

Sarah will have a son.”

## Genesis 18:15

Sarah was afraid and denied it, saying, “I did not laugh.” 📖

The LORD replied, “No, you did laugh.”

## Genesis 18:16

The men rose to leave and looked toward Sodom. 📖

Abraham walked with them to see them off.

## Genesis 18:17

The LORD said, “Should I hide from Abraham what I am about to do? 📖

## Genesis 18:18

Abraham will certainly become a great, powerful nation. 📖

All earth’s nations will be blessed through him.

## Genesis 18:19

I chose him so he will command his children and household after him to keep the LORD’s way. 📖

They must do what is right and just.

Then the LORD may bring Abraham what He promised.”

## Genesis 18:20

The LORD said, “The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and their sin is very grievous. 📖

## Genesis 18:21

I will go down and see whether their actions fully match the outcry that reached Me. 📖

If not, I will find out.”

## Genesis 18:22

The two men turned toward Sodom, but Abraham stayed before the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 18:23

Abraham stepped forward and asked, “Will You really sweep away the righteous with the wicked? 📖

**Help with “righteous”:** This means those who do what is right.

## Genesis 18:24

Suppose fifty righteous people are in the city. 📖

Will You destroy it rather than spare it for those fifty?

## Genesis 18:25

Surely You would not kill the righteous with the wicked and treat them alike! 📖

Surely not!

Will the Judge of all the earth not do right?”

## Genesis 18:26

The LORD replied, “If I find fifty righteous people in Sodom, I will spare the whole place for them.” 📖

## Genesis 18:27

Abraham answered, “I have dared to speak to the Lord, though I am only dust and ashes. 📖

**Help with “dust and ashes”:** Abraham speaks humbly about his human frailty.

## Genesis 18:28

Suppose the fifty righteous people lack five. 📖

Will You destroy the whole city for the lack of five?”

He replied, “If I find forty-five there, I will not destroy it.”

## Genesis 18:29

Abraham asked again, “Suppose forty are found there?” 📖

The LORD answered, “For those forty, I will not do it.”

## Genesis 18:30

Abraham said, “May the Lord not be angry. 📖

Let me speak further.

Suppose thirty are found there?”

He replied, “If I find thirty, I will not do it.”

## Genesis 18:31

Abraham said, “I have dared to speak to the Lord. 📖

Suppose twenty are found there?”

He answered, “For those twenty, I will not destroy it.”

## Genesis 18:32

Abraham said, “May the Lord not be angry. 📖

Let me speak once more.

Suppose ten are found there?”

He answered, “For those ten, I will not destroy it.”

## Genesis 18:33

The LORD left when He finished speaking with Abraham. 📖

Abraham returned home.
