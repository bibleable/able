# Genesis 11 — Babel and the family line to Abram

**About this chapter**

The people build a city and tower, and the LORD confuses their language.

The chapter then records Shem’s line to Terah and introduces Abram’s family.

## Genesis 11:1

The whole world had one language and the same form of speech. 📖

## Genesis 11:2

As people traveled east, they found a plain in Shinar and settled there. 📖

## Genesis 11:3

They said, “Come, let us make bricks and bake them thoroughly.” 📖

They used brick instead of stone and tar instead of mortar.

**Help with these words:** Mortar holds building materials together.

Tar is a sticky material they used in its place.

## Genesis 11:4

They said, “Come, let us build a city with a tower reaching heaven. 📖

We will make a name for ourselves and avoid being scattered over all the earth.”

## Genesis 11:5

The LORD came down to see the city and tower the people were building. 📖

## Genesis 11:6

The LORD said this. 📖

If they have begun this as one people with one language, nothing they plan will be beyond them.

## Genesis 11:7

Come, let Us go down and confuse their language, so they cannot understand one another.” 📖

## Genesis 11:8

The LORD scattered them from there over all the earth. 📖

They stopped building the city.

## Genesis 11:9

That is why it is called Babel. 📖

There, the LORD confused the whole world’s language.

From there, He scattered them over all the earth.

## Genesis 11:10

This is Shem’s account. 📖

Two years after the flood, when Shem was 100 years old, he became Arphaxad’s father.

## Genesis 11:11

After Arphaxad’s birth, Shem lived 500 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:12

When Arphaxad was 35 years old, he became Shelah’s father. 📖

## Genesis 11:13

After Shelah’s birth, Arphaxad lived 403 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:14

When Shelah was 30 years old, he became Eber’s father. 📖

## Genesis 11:15

After Eber’s birth, Shelah lived 403 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:16

When Eber was 34 years old, he became Peleg’s father. 📖

## Genesis 11:17

After Peleg’s birth, Eber lived 430 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:18

When Peleg was 30 years old, he became Reu’s father. 📖

## Genesis 11:19

After Reu’s birth, Peleg lived 209 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:20

When Reu was 32 years old, he became Serug’s father. 📖

## Genesis 11:21

After Serug’s birth, Reu lived 207 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:22

When Serug was 30 years old, he became Nahor’s father. 📖

## Genesis 11:23

After Nahor’s birth, Serug lived 200 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:24

When Nahor was 29 years old, he became Terah’s father. 📖

## Genesis 11:25

After Terah’s birth, Nahor lived 119 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:26

When Terah was 70 years old, he became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. 📖

## Genesis 11:27

This is Terah’s account. 📖

Terah was the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

Haran was Lot’s father.

## Genesis 11:28

While his father Terah was still alive, Haran died in his homeland, Ur of the Chaldeans. 📖

## Genesis 11:29

Abram married Sarai, and Nahor married Milcah. 📖

Milcah was Haran’s daughter.

Haran was the father of both Milcah and Iscah.

## Genesis 11:30

Sarai could not have children. 📖

She had none.

## Genesis 11:31

Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot, son of Haran, and Abram’s wife Sarai, his daughter-in-law. 📖

They left Ur of the Chaldeans for Canaan.

But they settled in Haran when they arrived there.

**About Haran:** Here Haran is a place name, not the son who died.

## Genesis 11:32

Terah lived 205 years and died in Haran. 📖
