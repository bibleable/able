# Genesis 48 — Jacob blesses Joseph’s sons

**About this chapter**

Jacob receives Joseph and his sons.

He counts Ephraim and Manasseh as his own and gives the younger son first place.

God’s promises, the prayer, and the final land gift are retained.

## Genesis 48:1

Later, Joseph heard his father was ill. 📖

He went with his sons Manasseh and Ephraim.

## Genesis 48:2

Jacob heard, “Your son Joseph has come.” 📖

Israel gathered his strength and sat up in bed.

## Genesis 48:3

Jacob told Joseph, “God Almighty appeared to me at Luz in Canaan. 📖

He blessed me there

## Genesis 48:4

and said, ‘I will make you fruitful and increase your numbers. 📖

You will become many peoples.

I will give this land to your descendants after you as a possession forever.’

**Help with “fruitful”:** Here it pictures having many descendants.

## Genesis 48:5

Your two sons born in Egypt before I came here will count as mine. 📖

Ephraim and Manasseh will be mine, like Reuben and Simeon.

## Genesis 48:6

Any children born to you afterward will be yours. 📖

They will be called by their brothers’ names in the territory they inherit.

## Genesis 48:7

To my sorrow, Rachel died on my return from Paddan. 📖

She died in Canaan, some distance from Ephrath.

I buried her beside the road to Ephrath.”

Ephrath is Bethlehem.

## Genesis 48:8

Israel saw Joseph’s sons and asked, “Who are these?” 📖

## Genesis 48:9

Joseph answered, “The sons God gave me here.” 📖

Jacob said, “Bring them to me so I may bless them.”

## Genesis 48:10

Israel’s eyesight was poor from old age. 📖

He could hardly see.

Joseph brought his sons, and his father kissed and embraced them.

## Genesis 48:11

Israel told Joseph, “I never expected to see your face again. 📖

Now God let me see your children too.”

## Genesis 48:12

Joseph moved his sons away from his father’s knees and bowed facedown. 📖

## Genesis 48:13

Joseph brought both sons near Israel. 📖

Ephraim was in Joseph’s right hand, toward Israel’s left.

Manasseh was in Joseph’s left hand, toward Israel’s right.

## Genesis 48:14

Israel put his right hand on younger Ephraim’s head. 📖

He crossed his hands and placed his left on Manasseh’s head, although Manasseh was firstborn.

## Genesis 48:15

He blessed Joseph, saying, “May the God before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac walked bless these boys. 📖

He is the God who has shepherded me all my life until today,

**Help with these pictures:** Walking before God means living in relationship with Him.

A shepherd cares for sheep.

Jacob pictures God’s care for him.

## Genesis 48:16

the angel who rescued me from all harm. 📖

May He bless these boys.

May they carry my name and my fathers’ names, Abraham and Isaac.

May they become very many on earth.”

**About this prayer:** The opening words continue Jacob’s description from the previous verse.

## Genesis 48:17

Joseph disliked seeing his father’s right hand on Ephraim. 📖

He took it to move it from Ephraim’s head to Manasseh’s.

## Genesis 48:18

He said, “Not so, my father! 📖

This is the firstborn.

Put your right hand on his head.”

## Genesis 48:19

His father refused, saying, “I know, my son, I know! 📖

He too will become a people and be great.

But his younger brother will be greater, and his descendants will become many nations.”

## Genesis 48:20

Jacob blessed them that day. 📖

He said, “Israel will use you when giving a blessing.

They will say, ‘May God make you like Ephraim and Manasseh.’”

He placed Ephraim before Manasseh.

## Genesis 48:21

Israel told Joseph, “I am about to die. 📖

But God will be with you and bring you back to your fathers’ land.

## Genesis 48:22

I give you, above your brothers, the ridge of land I took from the Amorites with my sword and bow.” 📖
