# Genesis 17 — The covenant, new names, and circumcision

**About this chapter**

God gives Abram and Sarai new names and promises a son through Sarah.

He gives circumcision as a covenant sign.

The promises concerning both Ishmael and Isaac are kept distinct.

## Genesis 17:1

When Abram was ninety-nine, the LORD appeared to him. 📖

He said, “I am God Almighty.

Walk before Me and be without blame.

## Genesis 17:2

I will establish My covenant with you and greatly increase your descendants.” 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 17:3

Abram fell with his face to the ground, and God spoke to him. 📖

## Genesis 17:4

He said, “This is My covenant with you. 📖

You will be the father of many nations.

## Genesis 17:5

You will no longer be called Abram. 📖

Your name will be Abraham, because I have made you a father of many nations.

## Genesis 17:6

I will make you greatly fruitful. 📖

I will make nations from you, and kings will descend from you.

## Genesis 17:7

I will establish My covenant with you and your descendants as an everlasting covenant. 📖

I will be your God and your descendants’ God.

## Genesis 17:8

I will give you and your descendants all Canaan, where you now live, as an eternal possession. 📖

I will be their God.”

## Genesis 17:9

God told Abraham, “You and your descendants in every coming generation must keep My covenant. 📖

## Genesis 17:10

This is the covenant you must keep with Me. 📖

Every male among you must be circumcised.

## Genesis 17:11

You must circumcise the flesh of your foreskin. 📖

This will be the covenant’s sign between Me and you.

**Help with “circumcise”:** This means removing the foreskin.

The foreskin is skin covering the end of the penis.

## Genesis 17:12

In every generation, each male must be circumcised at eight days old. 📖

This includes those born in your household and those bought from foreigners, even those not descended from you.

## Genesis 17:13

Whether born in your household or bought, they must be circumcised. 📖

My covenant in your flesh will last forever.

## Genesis 17:14

Any male who is not circumcised will be cut off from his people. 📖

He has broken My covenant.”

## Genesis 17:15

God told Abraham, “Do not call your wife Sarai any longer. 📖

Her name will be Sarah.

## Genesis 17:16

I will bless her and certainly give you a son through her. 📖

I will bless her, and she will be the mother of nations.

Kings of peoples will descend from her.”

## Genesis 17:17

Abraham fell with his face to the ground and laughed. 📖

He thought, “Can a man a hundred years old have a child?

Can Sarah give birth at ninety?”

## Genesis 17:18

Abraham told God, “If only Ishmael could live under Your blessing!” 📖

## Genesis 17:19

God replied, “Your wife Sarah will indeed bear you a son. 📖

Name him Isaac.

I will establish My covenant with him as an everlasting covenant for his descendants.

## Genesis 17:20

I have heard you about Ishmael and will certainly bless him. 📖

I will make him fruitful and greatly increase his descendants.

He will father twelve rulers, and I will make him a great nation.

## Genesis 17:21

But I will establish My covenant with Isaac. 📖

Sarah will bear him to you at this time next year.”

## Genesis 17:22

When God finished speaking with Abraham, He went up from him. 📖

## Genesis 17:23

On that very day, Abraham circumcised his son Ishmael and every male in his household. 📖

They included those born there and those bought with his money.

He did just as God told him.

## Genesis 17:24

Abraham was ninety-nine when he was circumcised, 📖

## Genesis 17:25

and his son Ishmael was thirteen. 📖

## Genesis 17:26

Abraham and Ishmael were circumcised on the same day. 📖

## Genesis 17:27

All Abraham’s household men were circumcised with him. 📖

They included servants born there and those bought from foreigners.
