# Genesis 7 — Entering the ark and the flood

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Noah to enter the ark with his family and the animals.

The chapter gives the animal counts, dates, flood duration, and extent of destruction.

## Genesis 7:1

The LORD told Noah, “Enter the ark with all your family. 📖

I have found you righteous in this generation.

## Genesis 7:2

Take seven pairs of every kind of clean animal, each male with its mate. 📖

Take one pair of every kind of unclean animal, each male with its mate.

**Help with “clean” and “unclean”:** These are categories used in the worship laws.

## Genesis 7:3

Take seven pairs of every kind of bird, male and female. 📖

This will preserve their offspring on all the earth.

## Genesis 7:4

Seven days from now, I will send rain for forty days and forty nights. 📖

I will wipe every living thing I made from the earth.”

## Genesis 7:5

Noah did everything the LORD commanded. 📖

## Genesis 7:6

Noah was 600 years old when the floodwaters came on the earth. 📖

## Genesis 7:7

Noah and his wife, his sons and their wives entered the ark to escape the floodwaters. 📖

## Genesis 7:8

The clean and unclean animals, birds, and everything crawling on the ground 📖

## Genesis 7:9

came to Noah to enter the ark in pairs, male and female, as God commanded him. 📖

## Genesis 7:10

After seven days, floodwaters came on the earth. 📖

## Genesis 7:11

It was Noah’s six hundredth year, the seventeenth day of the second month. 📖

All the great deep’s fountains burst open, and heaven’s floodgates opened.

## Genesis 7:12

Rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights. 📖

## Genesis 7:13

On that very day, Noah entered the ark with his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖

His wife and his sons’ three wives entered with him,

## Genesis 7:14

along with every kind of wild animal, livestock, crawling creature, bird, and winged creature. 📖

## Genesis 7:15

They came to Noah to enter the ark, in pairs of every creature with the breath of life. 📖

## Genesis 7:16

Male and female of every living thing entered, as God commanded Noah. 📖

Then the LORD shut him in.

## Genesis 7:17

The flood kept coming for forty days. 📖

The water rose and lifted the ark high above the earth.

## Genesis 7:18

The waters kept surging and rising greatly on the earth. 📖

The ark floated on their surface.

## Genesis 7:19

The waters completely covered the earth, including all the high mountains under all heaven. 📖

## Genesis 7:20

The water rose fifteen cubits above the mountaintops, covering them. 📖

**Help with “cubit”:** This measures from elbow to fingertips.

## Genesis 7:21

Every living thing moving on earth died. 📖

This included birds, livestock, wild animals, every creature swarming on earth, and all human beings.

## Genesis 7:22

Everything on dry land with the breath of life in its nostrils died. 📖

## Genesis 7:23

Every living thing on earth’s surface was destroyed: people, livestock, crawling creatures, and birds. 📖

They were wiped from the earth.

Only Noah and those with him in the ark remained.

## Genesis 7:24

The waters covered the earth for 150 days. 📖
