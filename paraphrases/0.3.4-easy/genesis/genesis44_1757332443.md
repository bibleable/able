# Genesis 44 — The cup and Judah’s appeal

**About this chapter**

Joseph has his silver cup hidden in Benjamin’s sack.

The brothers return when it is found.

Judah repeats their conversations and offers himself in Benjamin’s place.

## Genesis 44:1

Joseph told his household manager, “Fill their sacks with as much food as they can carry. 📖

Put each man’s silver at his sack’s opening.

## Genesis 44:2

Put my silver cup at the youngest man’s sack opening, along with the silver for his grain.” 📖

The manager obeyed.

## Genesis 44:3

At dawn, the men were sent away with their donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 44:4

They had not gone far from the city. 📖

Joseph told his manager, “Chase them now.

When you catch up, ask, ‘Why did you repay good with evil?

## Genesis 44:5

Is this not the cup my master drinks from and uses for divination? 📖

You have acted wickedly!’”

**Help with “divination”:** This means trying to discover hidden knowledge through special signs or practices.

These words are part of Joseph’s instructions to his manager.

## Genesis 44:6

The manager caught up and said these words. 📖

## Genesis 44:7

They asked, “Why does my lord say this? 📖

Your servants could never do such a thing.

## Genesis 44:8

We brought back from Canaan the silver found in our sacks’ openings. 📖

Why would we steal silver or gold from your master’s house?

## Genesis 44:9

If any servant has it, he must die. 📖

The rest of us will become my lord’s slaves.”

## Genesis 44:10

The manager replied, “As you say. 📖

But only the man found with the cup will be my slave.

The rest will be free of blame.”

## Genesis 44:11

Each quickly lowered his sack to the ground and opened it. 📖

## Genesis 44:12

The manager searched from oldest to youngest. 📖

The cup was found in Benjamin’s sack.

## Genesis 44:13

They all tore their clothes, loaded their donkeys, and returned to the city. 📖

**About torn clothes:** Here this action shows deep distress.

## Genesis 44:14

Judah and his brothers reached Joseph’s house. 📖

He was still there, and they fell to the ground before him.

## Genesis 44:15

Joseph said, “What have you done? 📖

Do you not know a man like me can surely discover truth by divination?”

**About Joseph’s words:** This is Joseph’s claim to his brothers.

Divination means trying to discover hidden knowledge through special signs or practices.

## Genesis 44:16

Judah answered, “What can we say to my lord? 📖

How can we plead or prove ourselves innocent?

God has exposed your servants’ wrongdoing.

We are my lord’s slaves, including the man found with the cup.”

## Genesis 44:17

Joseph replied, “I would never do that. 📖

Only the man found with the cup will be my slave.

The rest may return to your father in peace.”

## Genesis 44:18

Judah approached and said, “Sir, please let your servant speak personally to my lord. 📖

Do not be angry with your servant.

You are equal to Pharaoh himself.

## Genesis 44:19

My lord asked us, ‘Do you have a father or brother?’ 📖

## Genesis 44:20

We answered, ‘We have an elderly father and a young brother, born in his old age. 📖

The boy’s brother is dead.

He alone remains of his mother’s sons, and his father loves him.’

## Genesis 44:21

You told your servants, ‘Bring him here so I can see him myself.’ 📖

## Genesis 44:22

We said, ‘The boy cannot leave his father. 📖

If he leaves, his father will die.’

## Genesis 44:23

But you said, ‘Unless your younger brother comes, you will not see my face again.’ 📖

## Genesis 44:24

We returned to your servant my father and told him your words. 📖

## Genesis 44:25

Our father said, ‘Go back and buy us food.’ 📖

## Genesis 44:26

We answered, ‘We cannot go unless our younger brother goes too. 📖

Without him, we cannot see the man.’

## Genesis 44:27

Your servant my father said, ‘You know my wife bore me two sons. 📖

## Genesis 44:28

One disappeared, and I said, “Surely he was torn to pieces.” 📖

I have not seen him since.

## Genesis 44:29

If you take this one too, and he is harmed, my gray hair will go down to Sheol in sorrow.’ 📖

**Help with this picture:** The father means they would cause him to die of grief in old age.

Sheol means the place of the dead.

## Genesis 44:30

My father’s life is wrapped up in the boy’s life. 📖

If we return without the boy,

**Help with this picture:** Judah describes how deeply his father’s life depends on the boy.

## Genesis 44:31

and my father sees he is absent, my father will die. 📖

Your servants will have brought our father’s gray hair down to Sheol in sorrow.

**Help with this picture:** Judah says they would cause their old father to die of grief.

Sheol means the place of the dead.

## Genesis 44:32

I guaranteed his safety to my father. 📖

I said, ‘If I do not return him, I will bear the guilt before you, my father, all my life.’

## Genesis 44:33

Please let your servant stay as my lord’s slave instead of the boy. 📖

Let him return with his brothers.

## Genesis 44:34

How can I return to my father without him? 📖

I could not bear seeing the misery that would overwhelm my father.”
