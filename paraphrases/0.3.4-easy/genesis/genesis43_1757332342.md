# Genesis 43 — Benjamin goes to Egypt

**About this chapter**

The famine forces another journey for food.

Judah takes responsibility for Benjamin.

Joseph welcomes his brothers to a meal, while they remain unaware of his identity.

## Genesis 43:1

The famine remained severe in the land. 📖

## Genesis 43:2

Jacob’s family ate all the grain brought from Egypt. 📖

Their father said, “Go back and buy us a little more food.”

## Genesis 43:3

Judah replied, “The man firmly warned, ‘You will not see my face again unless your brother comes with you.’ 📖

## Genesis 43:4

If you send our brother, we will go and buy you food. 📖

## Genesis 43:5

If you do not send him, we will not go. 📖

The man said, ‘You will not see my face again unless your brother comes with you.’”

## Genesis 43:6

Israel asked, “Why did you cause me this trouble? 📖

Why tell him you had another brother?”

## Genesis 43:7

They replied, “He asked detailed questions about us and our family. 📖

He asked, ‘Is your father alive?

Do you have another brother?’

We answered his questions.

How could we know he would say, ‘Bring your brother here’?”

## Genesis 43:8

Judah told his father Israel, “Send the boy with me, and we will go now. 📖

Then we, you, and our children may live and not die.

## Genesis 43:9

I will guarantee his safety. 📖

You may hold me responsible.

If I do not bring him back before you, I will bear the guilt before you all my life.

## Genesis 43:10

Without this delay, we could have gone and returned twice by now.” 📖

## Genesis 43:11

Israel said, “If it must be so, do this. 📖

Pack some of our land’s best products as a gift for the man.

Take a little balm and honey, spices and myrrh, pistachios and almonds.

**Help with these goods:** Balm and myrrh are plant products.

Pistachios and almonds are nuts.

## Genesis 43:12

Take twice the silver, so you can return the silver put back in your sacks’ openings. 📖

Perhaps it was a mistake.

## Genesis 43:13

Take your brother too and return to the man at once. 📖

## Genesis 43:14

May God Almighty give you mercy before him, so he releases your other brother and Benjamin. 📖

If I lose my children, I lose them.”

## Genesis 43:15

They took the gifts, double the silver, and Benjamin. 📖

They hurried to Egypt and stood before Joseph.

## Genesis 43:16

Joseph saw Benjamin with them and told his household manager, “Take these men to my house. 📖

Kill an animal and prepare it.

They will eat with me at noon.”

## Genesis 43:17

The man obeyed Joseph and took the brothers to his house. 📖

## Genesis 43:18

They were frightened about being taken there. 📖

They said, “We are here because of the silver returned in our bags the first time.

They plan to overpower us and take us as slaves, along with our donkeys.”

## Genesis 43:19

They approached Joseph’s household manager and spoke at the house entrance. 📖

## Genesis 43:20

They said, “Please, sir, the first time we really came to buy food. 📖

## Genesis 43:21

Where we stayed the night, we opened our sacks. 📖

Each found all his silver at his sack’s opening.

We brought it back.

## Genesis 43:22

We brought more silver to buy food. 📖

We do not know who put our silver in our sacks.”

## Genesis 43:23

The manager said, “It is fine. 📖

Do not fear.

Your God, your father’s God, gave you the treasure in your sacks.

I received your silver.”

He brought Simeon to them.

## Genesis 43:24

The manager took them inside, gave them water to wash their feet, and fed their donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 43:25

They prepared their gift for Joseph’s arrival at noon, because they heard they would eat there. 📖

## Genesis 43:26

Joseph came home. 📖

They gave him their gifts and bowed to the ground before him.

## Genesis 43:27

He asked whether they were well. 📖

Then he asked, “How is the elderly father you told me about?

Is he alive?”

## Genesis 43:28

They replied, “Your servant our father is well and still alive.” 📖

They bowed to honor Joseph.

## Genesis 43:29

Joseph saw Benjamin, his brother by the same mother. 📖

He asked, “Is this the youngest brother you told me about?”

He added, “May God be gracious to you, my son.”

## Genesis 43:30

Joseph hurried to a private room to weep, overcome with feeling for his brother. 📖

## Genesis 43:31

He washed his face and came out. 📖

He controlled his feelings and said, “Serve the meal.”

## Genesis 43:32

Joseph, his brothers, and the Egyptians were served separately. 📖

The Egyptians would not eat with Hebrews, because that disgusted them.

**About this attitude:** The account describes the Egyptians’ attitude toward Hebrews.

## Genesis 43:33

The brothers sat before Joseph in age order, from oldest to youngest. 📖

They looked at one another in amazement.

## Genesis 43:34

Their food came from Joseph’s table. 📖

Benjamin received five times as much as anyone else.

They feasted and drank freely with Joseph.
