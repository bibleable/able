# Genesis 23 — Sarah’s death and the burial field

**About this chapter**

Sarah dies, and Abraham mourns.

He negotiates with the Hittites and Ephron for a burial site.

The witnesses, price, and transfer of the whole property are kept.

## Genesis 23:1

Sarah lived 127 years. 📖

## Genesis 23:2

She died at Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron, in Canaan. 📖

Abraham went to mourn and weep for her.

## Genesis 23:3

Abraham rose from beside his dead wife and spoke to the Hittites. 📖

## Genesis 23:4

He said, “I am a foreigner and outsider among you. 📖

Give me a burial site here, so I can bury my dead.”

## Genesis 23:5

The Hittites answered Abraham. 📖

## Genesis 23:6

They said, “Listen, sir. 📖

You are God’s chosen one among us.

Bury your dead in our finest tombs.

None of us will refuse you his tomb for your burial.”

## Genesis 23:7

Abraham rose and bowed before the land’s people, the Hittites. 📖

## Genesis 23:8

He said, “If you are willing for me to bury my dead, listen. 📖

Ask Ephron, son of Zohar, on my behalf

## Genesis 23:9

to sell me his cave of Machpelah, at the end of his field. 📖

Let him sell it at full price before you, so I can have a burial site.”

## Genesis 23:10

Ephron sat among the sons of Heth. 📖

He answered Abraham before all the Hittites who came to his city gate.

## Genesis 23:11

He said, “No, my lord, listen. 📖

I give you the field and its cave before my people.

Bury your dead.”

## Genesis 23:12

Abraham bowed before the land’s people again 📖

## Genesis 23:13

and told Ephron before them, “Please listen to me. 📖

I will pay the field’s price.

Accept it, so I can bury my dead there.”

## Genesis 23:14

Ephron answered Abraham. 📖

## Genesis 23:15

He said, “Listen, my lord. 📖

The land is worth four hundred shekels of silver.

What is that between us?

Bury your dead.”

**Help with “shekels”:** These are units of weight.

## Genesis 23:16

Abraham agreed to Ephron’s terms. 📖

He weighed the four hundred shekels of silver Ephron named before the Hittites, using the merchants’ standard.

## Genesis 23:17

Ephron’s field at Machpelah near Mamre was legally transferred. 📖

This included its cave and all trees within its boundaries,

## Genesis 23:18

to become Abraham’s property before all the Hittites who came to the city gate. 📖

## Genesis 23:19

Abraham buried Sarah in the field’s cave at Machpelah near Mamre, also called Hebron, in Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 23:20

The Hittites legally transferred the field and cave to Abraham as a burial site. 📖
