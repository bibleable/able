# Genesis 15 — The LORD’s covenant with Abram

**About this chapter**

The LORD promises Abram an heir from his own body and many descendants.

Abram believes Him.

God makes a covenant and names the land and its peoples.

## Genesis 15:1

After these events, the LORD’s word came to Abram in a vision. 📖

He said, “Do not fear, Abram.

I am your shield, your very great reward.”

## Genesis 15:2

Abram replied, “Lord GOD, what can You give me? 📖

I remain without children, and Eliezer of Damascus will inherit my household.”

## Genesis 15:3

Abram added, “You have given me no offspring. 📖

A servant in my household will be my heir.”

**Help with “heir”:** This means someone who receives another person’s property.

## Genesis 15:4

The LORD’s word came to Abram. 📖

He said, “This man will not be your heir.

Someone from your own body will be your heir.”

## Genesis 15:5

The LORD took him outside and said, “Look at the heavens and count the stars, if you can.” 📖

He told him, “Your offspring will be like that.”

## Genesis 15:6

Abram believed the LORD, and this was credited to him as righteousness. 📖

**Help with these words:** Credited means counted or recognized.

Righteousness means what is right before God.

## Genesis 15:7

The LORD told him, “I am the LORD, who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans. 📖

I brought you to give you this land to own.”

## Genesis 15:8

Abram replied, “Lord GOD, how can I know I will own it?” 📖

## Genesis 15:9

The LORD told him, “Bring Me a young cow, a goat, and a male sheep, each three years old. 📖

Bring a turtledove and a young pigeon too.”

## Genesis 15:10

Abram brought them and split each animal down the middle, placing the halves opposite each other. 📖

But he did not cut the birds in half.

## Genesis 15:11

Birds of prey came down on the dead bodies, but Abram drove them away. 📖

## Genesis 15:12

At sunset, Abram fell into a deep sleep. 📖

Great terror and darkness suddenly overwhelmed him.

## Genesis 15:13

The LORD said, “Know this for certain. 📖

Your descendants will be strangers in another land, enslaved and mistreated for four hundred years.

## Genesis 15:14

But I will judge the nation they serve as slaves. 📖

Afterward, they will leave with many possessions.

## Genesis 15:15

You will go to your ancestors in peace and be buried at a very old age. 📖

## Genesis 15:16

In the fourth generation, your descendants will return here. 📖

The Amorites’ wrongdoing is not yet complete.”

## Genesis 15:17

After sunset, when darkness had fallen, a smoking firepot and flaming torch appeared. 📖

They passed between the halves of the dead animals.

## Genesis 15:18

That day, the LORD made a covenant with Abram. 📖

He said, “I have given your descendants this land, from Egypt’s river to the great River Euphrates.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 15:19

It is the land of the Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites, 📖

## Genesis 15:20

Hittites, Perizzites, Rephaites, 📖

## Genesis 15:21

Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.” 📖
