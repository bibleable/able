# Genesis 49 — Jacob’s words to his sons

**About this chapter**

Jacob speaks about his sons and their future.

The strong images, judgments, blessings, and prayer are retained.

Editorial notes offer provisional help with the pictures.

Jacob then gives burial instructions and dies.

## Genesis 49:1

Jacob called his sons and said, “Gather around. 📖

I will tell you what will happen in days to come.

## Genesis 49:2

Come together and listen, sons of Jacob. 📖

Listen to your father Israel.

## Genesis 49:3

Reuben, my firstborn, you are my might and the beginning of my strength. 📖

You excel in honor and power.

## Genesis 49:4

You are uncontrolled like water and will no longer excel. 📖

You went up to your father’s bed, onto my couch, and defiled it.

**Help with these words:** Defiled means made unclean or dishonored.

Jacob refers to Reuben’s sex with his father’s concubine, described in Genesis 35:22.

The water picture describes lack of control.

## Genesis 49:5

Simeon and Levi are brothers. 📖

Their swords are weapons of violence.

## Genesis 49:6

May I never enter their council or join their gathering. 📖

In anger, they kill men and cut oxen’s leg tendons on a whim.

**Help with these words:** A council is a group meeting to discuss or decide things.

Cutting leg tendons cripples the animals.

“On a whim” means doing something just because they feel like it.

## Genesis 49:7

Cursed be their anger, so strong, and their fury, so cruel! 📖

I will scatter them in Jacob and disperse them in Israel.

**Help with these words:** A curse calls for harm or judgment.

Jacob and Israel refer here to the people descended from Jacob.

## Genesis 49:8

Judah, your brothers will praise you. 📖

Your hand will be on your enemies’ necks.

Your father’s sons will bow to you.

**Help with the neck picture:** This pictures power over defeated enemies.

## Genesis 49:9

Judah is a young lion. 📖

My son, you return from the prey.

He crouches and lies down like a lion.

Like a lioness, who dares wake him?

**Help with this picture:** Jacob compares Judah to a powerful lion that has hunted.

Prey means an animal hunted for food.

## Genesis 49:10

The scepter will not leave Judah, nor the staff from between his feet, until Shiloh comes. 📖

The nations’ loyalty will belong to him.

**Help with these words:** A scepter and a ruler’s staff are signs of ruling authority.

“Shiloh” is retained from BSB.

Its interpretation needs further editorial review.

## Genesis 49:11

He ties his donkey to the vine and its young to the best branch. 📖

He washes his clothes in wine, his robes in grape blood.

**Help with this picture:** Grape blood pictures grape juice or wine.

The vines and wine picture great plenty.

## Genesis 49:12

His eyes are darker than wine, and his teeth whiter than milk. 📖

## Genesis 49:13

Zebulun will live by the sea and become a harbor for ships. 📖

His border will reach Sidon.

**Help with “harbor”:** This is a sheltered place where ships stop.

## Genesis 49:14

Issachar is a strong donkey lying between the sheepfolds. 📖

**Help with this picture:** A donkey can carry heavy loads.

Sheepfolds are enclosures for sheep.

## Genesis 49:15

He saw his resting place was good and his land pleasant. 📖

He bent his shoulder to carry the load and submitted to work as a servant.

**About this picture:** The carrying image continues the comparison with a donkey.

## Genesis 49:16

Dan will bring justice for his people as one of Israel’s tribes. 📖

## Genesis 49:17

He will be a snake beside the road, a viper on the path. 📖

It bites a horse’s heels, making the rider fall backward.

**Help with this picture:** A viper is a venomous snake.

The image shows a small attacker bringing down a larger opponent.

## Genesis 49:18

I wait for Your salvation, O LORD. 📖

**Help with “salvation”:** Here this means rescue.

## Genesis 49:19

Raiders will attack Gad, but he will attack their heels. 📖

**Help with “attack their heels”:** This pictures attacking them as they retreat.

## Genesis 49:20

Asher’s food will be rich. 📖

He will provide special foods fit for kings.

## Genesis 49:21

Naphtali is a female deer set free that bears beautiful fawns. 📖

**Help with “fawns”:** These are young deer.

Jacob compares Naphtali to the deer.

## Genesis 49:22

Joseph is a fruitful vine beside a spring. 📖

Its branches climb over the wall.

**Help with this picture:** A spring supplies water.

The growing vine pictures flourishing life.

## Genesis 49:23

Archers bitterly attacked him, aiming at him with hatred. 📖

**Help with “archers”:** These are people who shoot arrows with bows.

## Genesis 49:24

Yet he held his bow steady, and his strong arms were strengthened by the Mighty One of Jacob’s hands. 📖

This was in the name of the Shepherd, the Rock of Israel,

**Help with these pictures:** Shepherd and Rock are titles describing God’s care and strength.

## Genesis 49:25

by your father’s God who helps you, and by the Almighty who blesses you. 📖

He gives blessings from heaven above, the deep waters below, and the breasts and womb.

**Help with the last picture:** Breasts and womb picture nursing and childbirth.

## Genesis 49:26

Your father’s blessings surpass the ancient mountains’ blessings and the everlasting hills’ bounty. 📖

May they rest on Joseph’s head, on the brow of the prince of his brothers.

**Help with these words:** Bounty means plentiful good things.

The picture describes great blessings resting on Joseph.

## Genesis 49:27

Benjamin is a hungry, fierce wolf. 📖

In the morning he devours prey.

In the evening he divides the plunder.”

**Help with this picture:** Jacob compares Benjamin to a wolf that hunts.

Plunder means goods taken after defeating others.

## Genesis 49:28

These are Israel’s twelve tribes. 📖

Their father said these words and blessed each with a suitable blessing.

## Genesis 49:29

Jacob instructed them, “I am about to be gathered to my people. 📖

Bury me with my fathers in the cave in Ephron the Hittite’s field.

**Help with “gathered to my people”:** This refers to dying and joining those who died before him.

## Genesis 49:30

The cave is in Machpelah’s field near Mamre in Canaan. 📖

Abraham bought that field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial place.

## Genesis 49:31

Abraham and his wife Sarah are buried there. 📖

Isaac and his wife Rebekah are there too.

I buried Leah there.

## Genesis 49:32

The field and its cave were bought from the Hittites.” 📖

## Genesis 49:33

Jacob finished instructing his sons and drew his feet into bed. 📖

He breathed his last and was gathered to his people.

**Help with these words:** Jacob died and joined those who died before him.
