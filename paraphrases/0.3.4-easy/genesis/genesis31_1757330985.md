# Genesis 31 — Jacob leaves and makes an agreement with Laban

**About this chapter**

Jacob leaves after God tells him to return home.

Laban pursues him, and they dispute property and treatment.

Rachel’s stolen idols remain hidden.

They make an agreement marked by a stone pillar and mound.

## Genesis 31:1

Jacob heard Laban’s sons say, “Jacob took everything our father owned and gained this wealth at his expense.” 📖

## Genesis 31:2

Jacob saw that Laban’s expression and attitude toward him had changed. 📖

## Genesis 31:3

The LORD told Jacob, “Return to your ancestors’ land and your relatives. 📖

I will be with you.”

## Genesis 31:4

Jacob called Rachel and Leah to the field where his flocks were, 📖

## Genesis 31:5

and said, “Your father’s expression shows his attitude toward me has changed. 📖

But my father’s God has been with me.

## Genesis 31:6

You know I served your father with all my strength. 📖

## Genesis 31:7

He cheated me and changed my wages ten times, but God did not let him harm me. 📖

## Genesis 31:8

If he said, ‘The speckled are your wages,’ the whole flock bore speckled young. 📖

If he said, ‘The streaked are your wages,’ the whole flock bore streaked young.

## Genesis 31:9

God took your father’s livestock and gave them to me. 📖

## Genesis 31:10

When the flocks bred, I dreamed that streaked, spotted, and speckled males mated with the females. 📖

## Genesis 31:11

God’s angel called in the dream, ‘Jacob!’ 📖

I answered, ‘Here I am.’

## Genesis 31:12

He said, ‘Look! 📖

All males mating with the flock are streaked, spotted, or speckled.

I saw everything Laban did to you.

## Genesis 31:13

I am the God of Bethel, where you poured oil on the pillar and made Me a solemn vow. 📖

Get up, leave now, and return to your homeland.’”

## Genesis 31:14

Rachel and Leah replied, “Do we have any share or inheritance left in our father’s house? 📖

## Genesis 31:15

Does he not treat us as outsiders? 📖

He sold us and wasted what was paid for us.

## Genesis 31:16

All the wealth God took from our father belongs to us and our children. 📖

Do everything God told you.”

## Genesis 31:17

Jacob put his children and wives on camels, 📖

## Genesis 31:18

and drove all his livestock ahead. 📖

He took every possession gained in Paddan-aram and went toward his father Isaac in Canaan.

## Genesis 31:19

While Laban sheared his sheep, Rachel stole his household idols. 📖

## Genesis 31:20

Jacob deceived Laban the Aramean by not telling him he was fleeing. 📖

## Genesis 31:21

He fled with all his possessions, crossed the Euphrates, and headed toward Gilead’s hills. 📖

## Genesis 31:22

On the third day, Laban heard Jacob had fled. 📖

## Genesis 31:23

He took his relatives and pursued Jacob for seven days. 📖

He caught up with him in Gilead’s hills.

## Genesis 31:24

That night, God appeared to Laban the Aramean in a dream. 📖

He warned, “Be careful not to say anything to Jacob, either good or bad.”

## Genesis 31:25

Jacob had camped in Gilead’s hills when Laban caught him. 📖

Laban and his relatives camped there too.

## Genesis 31:26

Laban said, “What have you done? 📖

You deceived me and carried my daughters away like war captives!

## Genesis 31:27

Why did you flee secretly and deceive me without telling me? 📖

I would have sent you with joy, songs, tambourines, and harps.

## Genesis 31:28

You did not let me kiss my grandchildren and daughters goodbye. 📖

You acted foolishly.

## Genesis 31:29

I could greatly harm you. 📖

But last night your father’s God warned me not to speak to you, either good or bad.

## Genesis 31:30

You left because you longed for your father’s house. 📖

But why steal my gods?”

## Genesis 31:31

Jacob replied, “I feared you would take your daughters from me by force. 📖

## Genesis 31:32

Anyone found with your gods must not live! 📖

Before our relatives, find anything of yours and take it.”

Jacob did not know Rachel stole the idols.

## Genesis 31:33

Laban searched Jacob’s tent, Leah’s tent, and the two female servants’ tents and found nothing. 📖

He left Leah’s tent and entered Rachel’s.

## Genesis 31:34

Rachel had hidden the household idols in her camel’s saddlebag and sat on them. 📖

Laban searched the whole tent but found nothing.

## Genesis 31:35

Rachel said, “Sir, do not be angry that I cannot stand before you. 📖

I am having my period.”

Laban searched but did not find the idols.

## Genesis 31:36

Jacob became angry and challenged Laban. 📖

He asked, “What is my crime?

What sin made you pursue me so fiercely?

## Genesis 31:37

You searched all my belongings. 📖

Did you find anything of yours?

Put it before my relatives and yours, and let them judge between us.

## Genesis 31:38

I was with you twenty years. 📖

Your sheep and goats did not miscarry, and I did not eat your flock’s male sheep.

## Genesis 31:39

I did not bring you animals torn by wild beasts. 📖

I paid for the loss myself.

You demanded payment for anything stolen by day or night.

## Genesis 31:40

Heat consumed me by day, frost by night, and I could not sleep. 📖

## Genesis 31:41

I served in your household twenty years: fourteen for your two daughters and six for your flocks. 📖

You changed my wages ten times!

## Genesis 31:42

Without my father’s God, Abraham’s God and the Fear of Isaac, you would have sent me away empty-handed. 📖

God saw my suffering and hard work and gave judgment last night.”

**Help with “the Fear of Isaac”:** This title refers to the God whom Isaac feared.

## Genesis 31:43

Laban replied, “These daughters, sons, and flocks are mine! 📖

Everything you see is mine!

But what can I do today about my daughters or their children?

## Genesis 31:44

Come, let us make a covenant as a witness between us.” 📖

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 31:45

Jacob took a stone and stood it upright as a pillar, 📖

## Genesis 31:46

and told his relatives to gather stones. 📖

They made a mound and ate beside it.

## Genesis 31:47

Laban named it Jegar-sahadutha, and Jacob named it Galeed. 📖

## Genesis 31:48

Laban said, “This mound is a witness between us today.” 📖

That is why it was called Galeed.

## Genesis 31:49

It was also called Mizpah. 📖

Laban said, “May the LORD watch between us when we are apart.

## Genesis 31:50

If you mistreat my daughters or take other wives, remember God witnesses between us, even when no one is here.” 📖

## Genesis 31:51

Laban told Jacob, “Here are the mound and pillar I set between us. 📖

## Genesis 31:52

Both witness that I will not pass this mound to harm you. 📖

You will not pass this mound and pillar to harm me.

## Genesis 31:53

May Abraham’s God and Nahor’s God, their father’s God, judge between us.” 📖

Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.

## Genesis 31:54

Jacob offered a sacrifice on the mountain and invited his relatives to eat. 📖

After eating, they stayed the night on the mountain.

## Genesis 31:55

Early next morning, Laban kissed his grandchildren and daughters and blessed them. 📖

He left for home.
