# Genesis 47 — The family settles and the famine continues

**About this chapter**

Jacob’s family settles in Egypt.

The chapter describes the famine, the loss of Egyptian land and freedom, and Joseph’s harvest law.

Jacob asks to be buried with his fathers.

## Genesis 47:1

Joseph told Pharaoh, “My father and brothers came from Canaan with flocks, herds, and everything they own. 📖

They are in Goshen.”

## Genesis 47:2

He chose five brothers and brought them before Pharaoh. 📖

## Genesis 47:3

Pharaoh asked their work. 📖

They replied, “Your servants are shepherds, as our fathers were.”

## Genesis 47:4

They said, “We came to live here for a time. 📖

Canaan’s severe famine has left no pasture for your servants’ flocks.

Please let us settle in Goshen.”

## Genesis 47:5

Pharaoh told Joseph, “Your father and brothers have come to you, 📖

## Genesis 47:6

and Egypt is before you. 📖

Settle them in its best part.

They may live in Goshen.

If any are skilled, put them over my livestock.”

## Genesis 47:7

Joseph brought his father Jacob before Pharaoh. 📖

Jacob blessed Pharaoh.

## Genesis 47:8

Pharaoh asked, “How many years have you lived?” 📖

## Genesis 47:9

Jacob answered, “My travels lasted 130 years. 📖

My years were few and hard.

They have not matched the years of my fathers’ travels.”

## Genesis 47:10

Jacob blessed Pharaoh and left him. 📖

## Genesis 47:11

As Pharaoh commanded, Joseph settled his father and brothers in Egypt. 📖

He gave them property in its best part, the district of Rameses.

## Genesis 47:12

Joseph supplied food for his father, brothers, and all his father’s household, according to their families’ needs. 📖

## Genesis 47:13

But the whole region had no food, because famine was so severe. 📖

The famine exhausted Egypt and Canaan.

## Genesis 47:14

Joseph collected all Egypt’s and Canaan’s money paid for grain. 📖

He brought it to Pharaoh’s palace.

## Genesis 47:15

When Egypt’s and Canaan’s money ran out, all the Egyptians came to Joseph. 📖

They said, “Give us food!

Why should we die before your eyes?

Our money is gone!”

## Genesis 47:16

Joseph said, “Bring your livestock. 📖

Since your money is gone, I will exchange food for livestock.”

## Genesis 47:17

They brought horses, flocks, herds, and donkeys to Joseph for food. 📖

Throughout that year, he supplied food for all their livestock.

## Genesis 47:18

In the second year, they returned and said, “We cannot hide that our money is gone. 📖

All our livestock belongs to you.

Only our bodies and land remain for our lord.

## Genesis 47:19

Why should we and our land perish before you? 📖

Buy us and our land for food, and we and our land will belong to Pharaoh as slaves.

Give us seed so we may live, not die, and the land will not become empty and ruined.”

## Genesis 47:20

Joseph bought all Egypt’s land for Pharaoh. 📖

Every Egyptian sold their fields because the famine was so severe.

The land became Pharaoh’s,

## Genesis 47:21

and Joseph made the people servants across all Egypt. 📖

**About this servitude:** The people lost their freedom and became subject to Pharaoh.

## Genesis 47:22

He did not buy the priests’ land, because Pharaoh had given it to them. 📖

They ate the food Pharaoh supplied and did not sell their land.

## Genesis 47:23

Joseph said, “Today I bought you and your land for Pharaoh. 📖

Here is seed to sow.

## Genesis 47:24

Give Pharaoh a fifth at harvest. 📖

Keep four-fifths for seed and food for yourselves, your households, and children.”

**Help with these amounts:** A fifth is one of five equal parts.

Four-fifths means four of those parts.

## Genesis 47:25

They said, “You saved our lives. 📖

We found favor with our lord, and we will be Pharaoh’s servants.”

## Genesis 47:26

Joseph made it law in Egypt that a fifth of the produce belongs to Pharaoh. 📖

It remains in effect to this day.

Only the priests’ land does not belong to Pharaoh.

**About “to this day”:** This refers to the time of the source account.

## Genesis 47:27

Israel’s people settled in Egypt’s Goshen region and gained property. 📖

They had children and increased greatly in number.

## Genesis 47:28

Jacob lived seventeen years in Egypt. 📖

His whole life lasted 147 years.

## Genesis 47:29

As Israel neared death, he called Joseph. 📖

He said, “If you favor me, put your hand under my thigh and promise kindness and faithfulness.

Do not bury me in Egypt,

**About this action:** Putting a hand under a thigh was part of this solemn promise.

## Genesis 47:30

but when I lie down with my fathers, carry me from Egypt and bury me with them.” 📖

Joseph replied, “I will do as you ask.”

**Help with “lie down with my fathers”:** This means dying, as his ancestors had died.

## Genesis 47:31

Jacob said, “Swear to me.” 📖

Joseph swore, and Israel bowed in worship at the head of his bed.
