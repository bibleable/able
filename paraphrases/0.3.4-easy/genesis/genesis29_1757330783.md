# Genesis 29 — Jacob, Leah, and Rachel

**About this chapter**

Jacob reaches Laban’s family and agrees to work for Rachel.

Laban gives him Leah first.

Jacob marries Rachel too, and the chapter records Leah’s first four sons.

## Genesis 29:1

Jacob continued traveling and reached the land of the eastern people. 📖

## Genesis 29:2

He saw a well in a field with three sheep flocks nearby. 📖

The sheep drank from it.

A large stone covered its opening.

## Genesis 29:3

When all the flocks gathered, the shepherds rolled the stone away and watered the sheep. 📖

Then they put the stone back over the well.

## Genesis 29:4

Jacob asked the shepherds, “My brothers, where are you from?” 📖

They answered, “Haran.”

## Genesis 29:5

He asked, “Do you know Laban, Nahor’s grandson?” 📖

They replied, “We know him.”

## Genesis 29:6

He asked, “Is he well?” 📖

They answered, “Yes.

His daughter Rachel is coming with his sheep.”

## Genesis 29:7

Jacob said, “It is still daylight, not time to gather the livestock. 📖

Water the sheep and take them back to pasture.”

## Genesis 29:8

They replied, “We cannot until all the flocks gather and the stone is rolled away. 📖

Then we will water them.”

## Genesis 29:9

While Jacob spoke, Rachel arrived with her father’s sheep. 📖

She was a shepherdess.

## Genesis 29:10

Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of his mother’s brother Laban, with Laban’s sheep. 📖

He rolled away the stone and watered his uncle’s sheep.

## Genesis 29:11

Jacob kissed Rachel and wept aloud. 📖

## Genesis 29:12

He told her he was Rebekah’s son, her father’s relative. 📖

She ran to tell her father.

## Genesis 29:13

Laban heard about his sister’s son Jacob and ran to meet him. 📖

He embraced and kissed him and brought him home.

Jacob told him everything that happened.

## Genesis 29:14

Laban said, “You are truly my flesh and blood.” 📖

Jacob stayed with him a month.

**Help with “flesh and blood”:** This means they are close relatives.

## Genesis 29:15

Laban asked, “Should you work for nothing because you are my relative? 📖

Tell me what wages you want.”

## Genesis 29:16

Laban had two daughters. 📖

The older was Leah, and the younger was Rachel.

## Genesis 29:17

Leah’s eyes were weak, but Rachel was shapely and beautiful. 📖

**About Leah’s eyes:** The draft keeps BSB’s description without adding a diagnosis.

## Genesis 29:18

Jacob loved Rachel. 📖

He said, “I will serve you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel.”

## Genesis 29:19

Laban replied, “Better to give her to you than another man. 📖

Stay with me.”

## Genesis 29:20

Jacob served seven years for Rachel. 📖

His love made them seem like only a few days.

## Genesis 29:21

Jacob told Laban, “Give me my wife. 📖

My time is complete, and I want to have sex with her.”

## Genesis 29:22

Laban gathered all the local men and made a feast. 📖

## Genesis 29:23

In the evening, he brought his daughter Leah to Jacob. 📖

Jacob had sex with her.

## Genesis 29:24

Laban gave his servant girl Zilpah to Leah as her female servant. 📖

## Genesis 29:25

In the morning, there was Leah! 📖

Jacob asked Laban, “What have you done?

I served you for Rachel.

Why did you deceive me?”

## Genesis 29:26

Laban replied, “Here we do not marry the younger daughter before the older. 📖

## Genesis 29:27

Finish this week’s celebration. 📖

Then we will give you the younger daughter for another seven years’ work.”

## Genesis 29:28

Jacob finished the week’s celebration, and Laban gave him Rachel as his wife. 📖

## Genesis 29:29

Laban gave his servant girl Bilhah to Rachel as her female servant. 📖

## Genesis 29:30

Jacob had sex with Rachel too and loved her more than Leah. 📖

He worked for Laban another seven years.

## Genesis 29:31

The LORD saw that Leah was unloved and enabled her to have children. 📖

But Rachel could not have children.

## Genesis 29:32

Leah became pregnant and had a son. 📖

She named him Reuben, saying, “The LORD has seen my suffering.

Surely my husband will love me now.”

## Genesis 29:33

She became pregnant again and had a son. 📖

She said, “The LORD heard I am unloved and gave me this son too.”

She named him Simeon.

## Genesis 29:34

Leah became pregnant again and had a son. 📖

She said, “Now my husband will become attached to me, because I bore him three sons.”

He was named Levi.

## Genesis 29:35

She became pregnant again and had a son. 📖

She said, “This time I will praise the LORD.”

She named him Judah and stopped having children.
