# Genesis 46 — Jacob’s family moves to Egypt

**About this chapter**

God speaks to Jacob before the journey.

The family names and totals are retained.

Joseph meets his father and prepares his family to speak with Pharaoh.

## Genesis 46:1

Israel set out with everything he had. 📖

At Beersheba, he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac.

## Genesis 46:2

That night, God spoke to Israel in a vision. 📖

He called, “Jacob, Jacob!”

Jacob replied, “Here I am.”

## Genesis 46:3

God said, “I am God, your father’s God. 📖

Do not fear going to Egypt.

I will make you a great nation there.

## Genesis 46:4

I will go with you and surely bring you back. 📖

Joseph’s own hands will close your eyes.”

**About closing his eyes:** This means Joseph will be present when Jacob dies.

## Genesis 46:5

Jacob left Beersheba. 📖

Israel’s sons carried their father Jacob, children, and wives in the wagons Pharaoh sent for him.

## Genesis 46:6

They took livestock and possessions gained in Canaan. 📖

Jacob and all his descendants went to Egypt.

## Genesis 46:7

He took his sons, grandsons, daughters, and granddaughters—all his descendants. 📖

## Genesis 46:8

These are Israel’s family names: Jacob and his descendants who went to Egypt. 📖

Reuben was Jacob’s firstborn.

## Genesis 46:9

Reuben’s sons were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. 📖

## Genesis 46:10

Simeon’s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul. 📖

Shaul’s mother was a Canaanite woman.

## Genesis 46:11

Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## Genesis 46:12

Judah’s sons were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah. 📖

Er and Onan died in Canaan.

Perez’s sons were Hezron and Hamul.

## Genesis 46:13

Issachar’s sons were Tola, Puvah, Job, and Shimron. 📖

## Genesis 46:14

Zebulun’s sons were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel. 📖

## Genesis 46:15

These were Leah’s sons born to Jacob in Paddan-aram, besides his daughter Dinah. 📖

The sons and daughters totaled thirty-three.

## Genesis 46:16

Gad’s sons were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli. 📖

## Genesis 46:17

Asher’s children were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah. 📖

Beriah’s sons were Heber and Malchiel.

## Genesis 46:18

These were Jacob’s sons through Zilpah, sixteen in all. 📖

Laban gave Zilpah to his daughter Leah.

## Genesis 46:19

Jacob’s wife Rachel had Joseph and Benjamin. 📖

## Genesis 46:20

In Egypt, Asenath bore Joseph Manasseh and Ephraim. 📖

She was the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On.

## Genesis 46:21

Benjamin’s sons were Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard. 📖

## Genesis 46:22

These were Rachel’s sons born to Jacob, fourteen in all. 📖

## Genesis 46:23

Dan’s son was Hushim. 📖

## Genesis 46:24

Naphtali’s sons were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem. 📖

## Genesis 46:25

These were Jacob’s sons through Bilhah, seven in all. 📖

Laban gave Bilhah to his daughter Rachel.

## Genesis 46:26

Jacob’s direct descendants who came to Egypt numbered sixty-six, excluding his sons’ wives. 📖

## Genesis 46:27

With Joseph’s two sons born in Egypt, Jacob’s family who went to Egypt numbered seventy in all. 📖

## Genesis 46:28

Jacob sent Judah ahead to Joseph for directions to Goshen. 📖

When the family arrived in Goshen,

## Genesis 46:29

Joseph prepared his chariot and went to meet his father Israel. 📖

He appeared before him, embraced him, and wept greatly.

## Genesis 46:30

Israel said, “Now I can die. 📖

I have seen your face and know you are alive!”

## Genesis 46:31

Joseph told his brothers and his father’s household, “I will tell Pharaoh, ‘My brothers and father’s household came from Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 46:32

They are shepherds and raise livestock. 📖

They brought their flocks, herds, and everything they own.’

## Genesis 46:33

When Pharaoh calls you and asks your work, 📖

## Genesis 46:34

say, ‘Your servants have raised livestock since youth, as our fathers did.’ 📖

Then you may settle in Goshen, because all shepherds disgust the Egyptians.”

**About this attitude:** Joseph describes the Egyptians’ attitude toward shepherds.
