# Genesis 13 — Abram and Lot separate

**About this chapter**

Abram and Lot have too many possessions to remain together.

Lot chooses the Jordan plain.

The LORD promises land and many descendants to Abram.

## Genesis 13:1

Abram left Egypt for the Negev with his wife and all his possessions. 📖

Lot was with him.

## Genesis 13:2

Abram had become very wealthy in livestock, silver, and gold. 📖

## Genesis 13:3

He traveled from place to place from the Negev toward Bethel. 📖

He reached the place between Bethel and Ai where his tent had stood before,

## Genesis 13:4

where he had built the altar. 📖

Abram called on the LORD’s name there.

## Genesis 13:5

Lot, who traveled with Abram, also had flocks, herds, and tents. 📖

## Genesis 13:6

The land could not support both of them together. 📖

They had so many possessions that they could not remain together.

## Genesis 13:7

Abram’s herdsmen and Lot’s herdsmen quarreled. 📖

The Canaanites and Perizzites were also living in the land then.

## Genesis 13:8

Abram told Lot, “Please let there be no quarrel between us or between our herdsmen. 📖

We are relatives.

## Genesis 13:9

The whole land is before you. 📖

Separate from me.

If you go left, I will go right.

If you go right, I will go left.”

## Genesis 13:10

Lot looked at the whole Jordan plain as far as Zoar. 📖

It was well watered, like the LORD’s garden and Egypt’s land.

This was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.

## Genesis 13:11

Lot chose the whole Jordan plain for himself and traveled east. 📖

Abram and Lot separated.

## Genesis 13:12

Abram lived in Canaan. 📖

Lot settled among the plain’s cities and pitched his tent toward Sodom.

## Genesis 13:13

Sodom’s men were wicked and sinned greatly against the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 13:14

After Lot left, the LORD told Abram, “Look from where you are toward the north, south, east, and west. 📖

## Genesis 13:15

I will give all the land you see to you and your offspring forever. 📖

## Genesis 13:16

I will make your offspring like the earth’s dust. 📖

If someone could count the dust, your offspring could also be counted.

## Genesis 13:17

Get up and walk through the land’s length and width. 📖

I will give it to you.”

## Genesis 13:18

Abram moved his tent to the Oaks of Mamre at Hebron and lived there. 📖

He built an altar to the LORD.
