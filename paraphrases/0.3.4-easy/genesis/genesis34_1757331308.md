# Genesis 34 — Dinah’s violation and the brothers’ violence

**About this chapter**

Shechem rapes Dinah and asks to marry her.

Jacob’s sons answer deceitfully, and Simeon and Levi kill the city’s men.

The chapter includes sexual violence, deception, killings, and captives.

## Genesis 34:1

Dinah, Leah’s daughter by Jacob, went to visit the land’s daughters. 📖

## Genesis 34:2

Shechem, son of Hamor the Hivite, ruler of the region, saw her. 📖

He seized her and raped her.

## Genesis 34:3

He was drawn to Dinah, Jacob’s daughter. 📖

He loved the young girl and spoke tenderly to her.

## Genesis 34:4

Shechem told Hamor, “Get me this girl as a wife.” 📖

## Genesis 34:5

Jacob heard that Shechem had violated Dinah. 📖

His sons were in the field with his livestock, so he stayed silent until they returned.

## Genesis 34:6

Shechem’s father Hamor came to speak with Jacob. 📖

## Genesis 34:7

Jacob’s sons heard and returned from the field, filled with grief and fury. 📖

Shechem had committed an outrage in Israel by having sex with Jacob’s daughter.

This should not have been done.

## Genesis 34:8

Hamor said, “My son Shechem longs for your daughter. 📖

Please give her to him as a wife.

## Genesis 34:9

Let our families marry one another. 📖

Give us your daughters, and take our daughters.

## Genesis 34:10

Settle among us. 📖

The land is open to you.

Live here, move freely, and gain property.”

## Genesis 34:11

Shechem told Dinah’s father and brothers, “Grant me favor, and I will give whatever you ask. 📖

## Genesis 34:12

Demand a large marriage payment and expensive gift. 📖

I will give whatever you ask.

Just give me the girl as a wife!”

## Genesis 34:13

Jacob’s sons answered Shechem and Hamor deceitfully because Shechem had violated their sister Dinah. 📖

## Genesis 34:14

They said, “We cannot give our sister to an uncircumcised man. 📖

That would disgrace us.

**Help with “uncircumcised”:** This means the foreskin has not been removed.

Circumcision was the covenant sign described in chapter 17.

## Genesis 34:15

We agree on one condition: every male among you must be circumcised like us. 📖

## Genesis 34:16

Then we will give our daughters and take yours. 📖

We will live among you as one people.

## Genesis 34:17

But if you refuse circumcision, we will take our sister and leave.” 📖

## Genesis 34:18

Hamor and Shechem thought the offer was good. 📖

## Genesis 34:19

The young man acted without delay because he delighted in Jacob’s daughter. 📖

He was the most respected in his father’s household.

## Genesis 34:20

Hamor and Shechem went to the city gate and addressed the men. 📖

## Genesis 34:21

They said, “These men are peaceful toward us. 📖

Let them live and trade here.

The land has enough room.

Let us marry their daughters and give them ours.

## Genesis 34:22

They will join us as one people only if all our men are circumcised like them. 📖

## Genesis 34:23

Will their livestock, possessions, and all their animals not become ours? 📖

Agree, and they will live among us.”

## Genesis 34:24

All the men leaving the city gate listened to Hamor and Shechem. 📖

Every male in the city was circumcised.

## Genesis 34:25

Three days later, while they were still in pain, Dinah’s brothers Simeon and Levi took swords. 📖

They entered the unsuspecting city and killed every male.

## Genesis 34:26

They killed Hamor and Shechem, took Dinah from Shechem’s house, and left. 📖

## Genesis 34:27

Jacob’s other sons came upon the slaughter and robbed the city because their sister had been violated. 📖

## Genesis 34:28

They took flocks, herds, donkeys, and everything in the city and field. 📖

## Genesis 34:29

They took all the possessions, women, and children, and everything in the houses. 📖

## Genesis 34:30

Jacob told Simeon and Levi, “You brought trouble by making me a stench to this land’s Canaanites and Perizzites. 📖

We are few.

If they unite and attack, my household and I will be destroyed.”

**Help with “a stench”:** Jacob pictures himself as something the surrounding peoples cannot bear.

## Genesis 34:31

They replied, “Should he have treated our sister like a prostitute?” 📖

**Help with “prostitute”:** This is someone who provides sexual acts for payment or other benefits.
