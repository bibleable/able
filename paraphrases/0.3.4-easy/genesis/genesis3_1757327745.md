# Genesis 3 — Disobedience and leaving the garden

**About this chapter**

The serpent questions God’s command.

The man and woman eat the forbidden fruit, and God announces the consequences.

They leave the garden, and the way to the tree of life is guarded.

## Genesis 3:1

The serpent was more crafty than any field animal the LORD God made. 📖

He asked the woman, “Did God really say you must not eat from any tree in the garden?”

**Help with “crafty”:** This means clever at deceiving.

## Genesis 3:2

The woman answered, “We may eat fruit from the garden’s trees, 📖

## Genesis 3:3

but God spoke about the tree in the garden’s middle. 📖

He said, ‘You must not eat its fruit or touch it, or you will die.’”

**About her answer:** These are the woman’s words to the serpent.

## Genesis 3:4

The serpent told her, “You certainly will not die. 📖

## Genesis 3:5

God knows that on the day you eat it, your eyes will open. 📖

You will be like God, knowing good and evil.”

## Genesis 3:6

The woman saw that the tree was good for food and pleasing to see. 📖

It was desirable for gaining wisdom.

She took its fruit and ate.

She gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate.

## Genesis 3:7

Both their eyes opened, and they knew they were naked. 📖

They sewed fig leaves together and made coverings for themselves.

## Genesis 3:8

The man and his wife heard the LORD God’s voice as He walked in the garden in the day’s breeze. 📖

They hid from Him among the garden’s trees.

## Genesis 3:9

The LORD God called to the man, “Where are you?” 📖

## Genesis 3:10

The man replied, “I heard Your voice in the garden. 📖

I was afraid because I was naked, so I hid.”

## Genesis 3:11

The LORD God asked, “Who told you that you were naked? 📖

Have you eaten from the tree I commanded you not to eat from?”

## Genesis 3:12

The man answered, “The woman You gave me gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate.” 📖

## Genesis 3:13

The LORD God asked the woman, “What have you done?” 📖

She replied, “The serpent deceived me, and I ate.”

## Genesis 3:14

The LORD God told the serpent, “Because you did this, you are cursed above all livestock and field animals. 📖

You will go on your belly and eat dust all your life.

## Genesis 3:15

I will put hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring and hers. 📖

He will crush your head, and you will strike his heel.”

**Help with “offspring”:** This means descendants.

The draft keeps the change from offspring to “He” without adding another name.

## Genesis 3:16

He told the woman, “I will greatly increase your pain in childbirth. 📖

You will give birth to children in pain.

Your desire will be for your husband, and he will rule over you.”

## Genesis 3:17

He told Adam, “You listened to your wife and ate from the tree I commanded you not to eat from. 📖

So the ground is cursed because of you.

You will eat from it through hard work all your life.

## Genesis 3:18

It will grow thorns and thistles for you, and you will eat the field’s plants. 📖

## Genesis 3:19

You will eat bread by the sweat of your brow until you return to the ground. 📖

You were taken from it.

You are dust, and you will return to dust.”

## Genesis 3:20

Adam named his wife Eve because she would be the mother of all the living. 📖

## Genesis 3:21

The LORD God made clothes of skin for Adam and his wife and dressed them. 📖

## Genesis 3:22

The LORD God said, “The man has become like one of Us, knowing good and evil. 📖

Now he might reach out, take fruit from the tree of life, eat, and live forever...”

## Genesis 3:23

So the LORD God sent him out of the Garden of Eden to work the ground he was taken from. 📖

## Genesis 3:24

God drove the man out. 📖

East of the Garden of Eden, He stationed cherubim and a flaming sword turning in every direction.

They guarded the way to the tree of life.

**Help with “cherubim”:** These are heavenly beings.
