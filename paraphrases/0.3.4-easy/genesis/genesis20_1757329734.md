# Genesis 20 — Abraham, Sarah, and Abimelech

**About this chapter**

Abraham again calls Sarah his sister.

God warns Abimelech in a dream.

Sarah is returned, Abraham explains his actions, and God answers his prayer.

## Genesis 20:1

Abraham traveled to the Negev and settled between Kadesh and Shur. 📖

He stayed in Gerar.

## Genesis 20:2

Abraham said about his wife Sarah, “She is my sister.” 📖

Abimelech, king of Gerar, had Sarah brought to him.

## Genesis 20:3

God came to Abimelech in a dream one night. 📖

He said, “You are as good as dead because of the woman you took.

She is married.”

## Genesis 20:4

Abimelech had not gone near her. 📖

He replied, “Lord, would You destroy an innocent nation?

## Genesis 20:5

Did Abraham not tell me she was his sister? 📖

She herself said he was her brother.

I did this with an honest heart and innocent hands.”

## Genesis 20:6

God answered in the dream, “Yes, I know you did this with a clear conscience. 📖

I kept you from sinning against Me.

That is why I did not let you touch her.

## Genesis 20:7

Return the man’s wife. 📖

He is a prophet and will pray for you, and you will live.

But if you do not return her, you and everyone belonging to you will certainly die.”

## Genesis 20:8

Abimelech rose early the next morning and called all his servants. 📖

He told them everything that happened, and they were terrified.

## Genesis 20:9

Abimelech called Abraham and asked, “What have you done to us? 📖

How did I sin against you, that you brought such great guilt on me and my kingdom?

You have done things to me that should not be done.”

## Genesis 20:10

Abimelech asked, “What made you do this?” 📖

## Genesis 20:11

Abraham replied, “I thought, ‘Surely no one fears God here. 📖

They will kill me because of my wife.’

## Genesis 20:12

She really is my sister, my father’s daughter but not my mother’s daughter. 📖

She became my wife.

## Genesis 20:13

When God sent me traveling from my father’s house, I asked Sarah to show me loyalty. 📖

I said, ‘Wherever we go, say I am your brother.’”

## Genesis 20:14

Abimelech gave Abraham sheep, cattle, and male and female servants. 📖

He returned Abraham’s wife Sarah.

## Genesis 20:15

Abimelech said, “My land is before you. 📖

Settle wherever you please.”

## Genesis 20:16

He told Sarah, “I am giving your brother a thousand pieces of silver. 📖

This clears you before everyone with you.

You are completely cleared.”

## Genesis 20:17

Abraham prayed to God. 📖

God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants, so they could have children again.

## Genesis 20:18

The LORD had completely closed all the wombs in Abimelech’s household because of Abraham’s wife Sarah. 📖
