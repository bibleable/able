# Genesis 27 — Jacob takes Esau’s blessing

**About this chapter**

Isaac intends to bless Esau, but Rebekah and Jacob deceive him.

Both blessings and Esau’s responses are kept.

Rebekah urges Jacob to flee.

## Genesis 27:1

Isaac was old, and his weak eyes could no longer see. 📖

He called his older son Esau, “My son.”

Esau replied, “Here I am.”

## Genesis 27:2

Isaac said, “I am old and do not know when I will die. 📖

## Genesis 27:3

Take your bow and quiver and hunt wild game for me. 📖

**Help with these words:** A quiver holds arrows.

Wild game means animals hunted for food.

## Genesis 27:4

Prepare the tasty food I love and bring it for me to eat. 📖

Then I can bless you before I die.”

## Genesis 27:5

Rebekah heard Isaac speak to Esau. 📖

Esau went to hunt and bring back game.

## Genesis 27:6

Rebekah told Jacob, “I heard your father tell your brother Esau, 📖

## Genesis 27:7

‘Bring me game and prepare tasty food for me. 📖

I will eat and bless you before the LORD before I die.’

## Genesis 27:8

My son, listen and do exactly what I tell you. 📖

## Genesis 27:9

Bring me two fine young goats from the flock. 📖

I will prepare the tasty food your father loves.

## Genesis 27:10

Take it to him to eat, so he can bless you before he dies.” 📖

## Genesis 27:11

Jacob told his mother Rebekah, “Esau is hairy, but my skin is smooth. 📖

## Genesis 27:12

What if my father touches me? 📖

He will discover I am a deceiver.

I will bring a curse on myself instead of a blessing.”

## Genesis 27:13

His mother replied, “Let your curse fall on me, my son. 📖

Just obey and get the goats.”

## Genesis 27:14

Jacob brought two goats to his mother. 📖

She prepared the tasty food his father loved.

## Genesis 27:15

Rebekah took her older son Esau’s finest clothes from the house and dressed her younger son Jacob in them. 📖

## Genesis 27:16

She covered his hands and smooth neck with the young goats’ skins. 📖

## Genesis 27:17

She gave Jacob the tasty food and bread she prepared. 📖

## Genesis 27:18

Jacob went to his father and said, “My father.” 📖

Isaac answered, “Here I am.

Which son are you?”

## Genesis 27:19

Jacob said, “I am Esau, your firstborn. 📖

I did what you told me.

Please sit up and eat my game, so you can bless me.”

## Genesis 27:20

Isaac asked, “How did you find it so quickly, my son?” 📖

Jacob replied, “The LORD your God brought it to me.”

## Genesis 27:21

Isaac said, “Come closer so I can touch you, my son. 📖

Are you really my son Esau?”

## Genesis 27:22

Jacob came close. 📖

Isaac touched him and said, “The voice is Jacob’s, but the hands are Esau’s.”

## Genesis 27:23

Isaac did not recognize him because his hands were hairy like Esau’s. 📖

He blessed him.

## Genesis 27:24

He asked again, “Are you really my son Esau?” 📖

Jacob replied, “I am.”

## Genesis 27:25

Isaac said, “Serve me some of my son’s game, so I can bless you.” 📖

Jacob brought it, and Isaac ate.

Jacob brought wine, and Isaac drank.

## Genesis 27:26

Isaac told him, “Come near and kiss me, my son.” 📖

## Genesis 27:27

Jacob kissed him. 📖

Isaac smelled his clothes, blessed him, and said, “My son smells like a field the LORD blessed.

## Genesis 27:28

May God give you heaven’s dew and earth’s richness, plentiful grain and new wine. 📖

**Help with “dew”:** This is water that settles outdoors.

## Genesis 27:29

May peoples serve you and nations bow to you. 📖

May you be master over your brothers, and may your mother’s sons bow to you.

May those cursing you be cursed and those blessing you be blessed.”

## Genesis 27:30

Isaac finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob left. 📖

Just then, Esau returned from hunting.

## Genesis 27:31

Esau also prepared tasty food and brought it to his father. 📖

He said, “Sit up, my father, and eat my game, so you can bless me.”

## Genesis 27:32

Isaac asked, “Who are you?” 📖

He answered, “Esau, your firstborn son.”

## Genesis 27:33

Isaac trembled violently and asked, “Who hunted and brought me game? 📖

Before you came, I ate it all and blessed him.

He will indeed be blessed!”

## Genesis 27:34

Esau heard this and cried loudly and bitterly. 📖

He said, “Bless me too, my father!”

## Genesis 27:35

Isaac replied, “Your brother deceived me and took your blessing.” 📖

## Genesis 27:36

Esau said, “Is he not rightly named Jacob? 📖

He has cheated me twice.

He took my birthright and now my blessing.”

He asked, “Have you kept a blessing for me?”

**Help with “birthright”:** This means the rights of the firstborn son.

## Genesis 27:37

Isaac answered, “I made him your master and gave him all his relatives as servants. 📖

I supplied him with grain and new wine.

What can I still do for you, my son?”

## Genesis 27:38

Esau asked, “Have you only one blessing, my father? 📖

Bless me too!”

He wept aloud.

## Genesis 27:39

Isaac answered, “Your home will be far from the land’s richness and from heaven’s dew above. 📖

## Genesis 27:40

You will live by the sword and serve your brother. 📖

But when you rebel, you will tear his yoke from your neck.”

**Help with “yoke”:** This is a wooden frame used to control working animals.

Here it pictures being under another’s power.

## Genesis 27:41

Esau hated Jacob because of his father’s blessing. 📖

He thought, “My father’s mourning days are near.

Then I will kill my brother Jacob.”

## Genesis 27:42

Rebekah heard her older son’s words and sent for her younger son Jacob. 📖

She said, “Esau comforts himself by planning to kill you.

## Genesis 27:43

Listen, my son. 📖

Flee now to my brother Laban in Haran.

## Genesis 27:44

Stay a while until your brother’s fury settles, 📖

## Genesis 27:45

until his anger fades and he forgets what you did. 📖

Then I will send for you.

Why should I lose both sons in one day?”

## Genesis 27:46

Rebekah told Isaac, “I am weary of life because of these Hittite women. 📖

If Jacob marries one of them, what good is my life?”
