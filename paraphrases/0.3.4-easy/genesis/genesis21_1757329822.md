# Genesis 21 — Isaac, Ishmael, and the well at Beersheba

**About this chapter**

The LORD fulfills His promise to Sarah, and Isaac is born.

Hagar and Ishmael leave, and God cares for them.

Abraham and Abimelech make an agreement concerning a well.

## Genesis 21:1

The LORD attended to Sarah as He had said. 📖

He did for her what He promised.

## Genesis 21:2

Sarah became pregnant and bore Abraham a son in his old age, at the exact time God promised. 📖

## Genesis 21:3

Abraham named the son Sarah bore him Isaac. 📖

## Genesis 21:4

When Isaac was eight days old, Abraham circumcised him, as God commanded. 📖

**Help with “circumcised”:** This means the foreskin was removed, as the covenant sign described in chapter 17.

## Genesis 21:5

Abraham was a hundred years old when Isaac was born. 📖

## Genesis 21:6

Sarah said, “God has made me laugh. 📖

Everyone hearing this will laugh with me.”

## Genesis 21:7

She added, “Who would have told Abraham that Sarah would breastfeed children? 📖

Yet I have borne him a son in his old age.”

## Genesis 21:8

Isaac grew and stopped breastfeeding. 📖

Abraham held a great feast on the day Isaac was weaned.

**Help with “weaned”:** This means he no longer fed at his mother’s breast.

## Genesis 21:9

Sarah saw the son Hagar the Egyptian bore Abraham mocking her son. 📖

## Genesis 21:10

She told Abraham, “Send the slave woman and her son away! 📖

Her son will never share the inheritance with my son Isaac!”

**Help with “inheritance”:** This is property passed on to a person.

## Genesis 21:11

This greatly distressed Abraham because it concerned his son Ishmael. 📖

## Genesis 21:12

God told Abraham, “Do not be distressed about the boy and your female servant. 📖

Listen to everything Sarah tells you.

Your offspring will be counted through Isaac.

## Genesis 21:13

But I will also make the slave woman’s son a nation, because he is your offspring.” 📖

## Genesis 21:14

Abraham rose early, took bread and a skin of water, and put them on Hagar’s shoulders. 📖

He sent her away with the boy.

She wandered in the Wilderness of Beersheba.

**Help with “skin”:** This is a container made from animal skin.

## Genesis 21:15

When the water was gone, she left the boy under a bush. 📖

## Genesis 21:16

She went about a bowshot away and sat down. 📖

She said, “I cannot bear to watch the boy die!”

Sitting nearby, she cried aloud.

**Help with “bowshot”:** This is the distance an arrow can be shot.

## Genesis 21:17

God heard the boy’s voice. 📖

God’s angel called from heaven, “What is wrong, Hagar?

Do not fear.

God heard the boy’s voice where he lies.

## Genesis 21:18

Get up, lift the boy, and take his hand. 📖

I will make him a great nation.”

## Genesis 21:19

God opened her eyes, and she saw a well. 📖

She filled the skin with water and gave the boy a drink.

## Genesis 21:20

God was with the boy. 📖

He grew, lived in the wilderness, and became a great archer.

## Genesis 21:21

He lived in the Wilderness of Paran. 📖

His mother found him a wife from Egypt.

## Genesis 21:22

At that time, Abimelech and Phicol, his army commander, spoke to Abraham. 📖

They said, “God is with you in everything you do.

## Genesis 21:23

Swear here before God not to deal falsely with me, my children, or my descendants. 📖

Show me and this country where you live the kindness I showed you.”

## Genesis 21:24

Abraham replied, “I swear it.” 📖

## Genesis 21:25

Abraham complained about a well Abimelech’s servants had seized. 📖

## Genesis 21:26

Abimelech replied, “I do not know who did it. 📖

You did not tell me.

I heard nothing about it until today.”

## Genesis 21:27

Abraham gave Abimelech sheep and cattle. 📖

They made a covenant.

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 21:28

Abraham set apart seven female lambs from the flock. 📖

## Genesis 21:29

Abimelech asked, “Why did you set apart these seven female lambs?” 📖

## Genesis 21:30

Abraham replied, “Accept these seven female lambs from me as proof that I dug this well.” 📖

## Genesis 21:31

The place was called Beersheba because they swore an oath there. 📖

## Genesis 21:32

After making the covenant at Beersheba, Abimelech and his army commander Phicol returned to the Philistines’ land. 📖

## Genesis 21:33

Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba. 📖

He called on the LORD’s name there, the Eternal God.

## Genesis 21:34

Abraham stayed in the Philistines’ land for a long time. 📖
