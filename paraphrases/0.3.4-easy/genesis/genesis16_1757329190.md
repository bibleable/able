# Genesis 16 — Hagar and Ishmael

**About this chapter**

Sarai gives Hagar to Abram as a wife.

Hagar becomes pregnant and runs away after harsh treatment.

The LORD’s angel meets her and speaks about her son and descendants.

## Genesis 16:1

Abram’s wife Sarai had no children. 📖

She had an Egyptian female servant named Hagar.

## Genesis 16:2

Sarai told Abram, “The LORD has prevented me from having children. 📖

Please have sex with my servant.

Perhaps I can build a family through her.”

Abram listened to Sarai.

## Genesis 16:3

After Abram had lived in Canaan ten years, Sarai gave her Egyptian servant Hagar to him as a wife. 📖

## Genesis 16:4

Abram had sex with Hagar, and she became pregnant. 📖

When she knew she was pregnant, she began to despise her mistress.

## Genesis 16:5

Sarai told Abram, “May the wrong done to me fall on you! 📖

I gave my servant into your arms.

Since she knew she was pregnant, she has treated me with scorn.

May the LORD judge between us.”

## Genesis 16:6

Abram said, “Your servant is in your hands. 📖

Do whatever you want with her.”

Sarai treated Hagar so harshly that Hagar ran away.

## Genesis 16:7

The LORD’s angel found Hagar beside a desert spring on the road to Shur. 📖

## Genesis 16:8

He said, “Hagar, Sarai’s servant, where have you come from, and where are you going?” 📖

She replied, “I am running away from my mistress Sarai.”

## Genesis 16:9

The LORD’s angel told her, “Return to your mistress and submit to her authority.” 📖

**About this command:** These are the angel’s instructions to Hagar in this account.

## Genesis 16:10

He added, “I will greatly increase your offspring until they are too many to count.” 📖

## Genesis 16:11

The LORD’s angel continued, “You are pregnant and will bear a son. 📖

Name him Ishmael, because the LORD has heard your cry of suffering.

## Genesis 16:12

He will be a wild donkey of a man. 📖

His hand will be against everyone, and everyone’s hand against him.

He will live in hostility toward all his brothers.”

## Genesis 16:13

Hagar named the LORD who spoke to her, “You are the God who sees me.” 📖

She said, “Here I have seen the One who sees me!”

## Genesis 16:14

So the well was called Beer-lahai-roi. 📖

It is between Kadesh and Bered.

## Genesis 16:15

Hagar bore Abram a son. 📖

Abram named the son she bore Ishmael.

## Genesis 16:16

Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to him. 📖
