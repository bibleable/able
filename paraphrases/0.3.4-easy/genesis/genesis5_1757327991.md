# Genesis 5 — The family line from Adam to Noah

**About this chapter**

This chapter records Adam’s descendants through Seth to Noah.

It gives ages at fatherhood, years afterward, and total lifespans.

The ages and repeated statements of death are kept.

## Genesis 5:1

This is the written record of Adam’s generations. 📖

When God created human beings, He made them in His own likeness.

## Genesis 5:2

He created them male and female and blessed them. 📖

On the day they were created, He called them “man.”

## Genesis 5:3

When Adam was 130 years old, he had a son in his own likeness and image. 📖

He named him Seth.

## Genesis 5:4

After Seth’s birth, Adam lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:5

Adam lived 930 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:6

When Seth was 105 years old, he became Enosh’s father. 📖

## Genesis 5:7

After Enosh’s birth, Seth lived 807 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:8

Seth lived 912 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:9

When Enosh was 90 years old, he became Kenan’s father. 📖

## Genesis 5:10

After Kenan’s birth, Enosh lived 815 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:11

Enosh lived 905 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:12

When Kenan was 70 years old, he became Mahalalel’s father. 📖

## Genesis 5:13

After Mahalalel’s birth, Kenan lived 840 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:14

Kenan lived 910 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:15

When Mahalalel was 65 years old, he became Jared’s father. 📖

## Genesis 5:16

After Jared’s birth, Mahalalel lived 830 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:17

Mahalalel lived 895 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:18

When Jared was 162 years old, he became Enoch’s father. 📖

## Genesis 5:19

After Enoch’s birth, Jared lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:20

Jared lived 962 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:21

When Enoch was 65 years old, he became Methuselah’s father. 📖

## Genesis 5:22

After Methuselah’s birth, Enoch walked with God for 300 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

**Help with “walked with God”:** This pictures living in close relationship with God.

## Genesis 5:23

Enoch lived 365 years in total. 📖

## Genesis 5:24

Enoch walked with God and then was no more, because God had taken him away. 📖

## Genesis 5:25

When Methuselah was 187 years old, he became Lamech’s father. 📖

## Genesis 5:26

After Lamech’s birth, Methuselah lived 782 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:27

Methuselah lived 969 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:28

When Lamech was 182 years old, he had a son. 📖

## Genesis 5:29

He named him Noah. 📖

He said, “May this one comfort us in our hands’ hard work caused by the ground the LORD cursed.”

## Genesis 5:30

After Noah’s birth, Lamech lived 595 years and had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:31

Lamech lived 777 years in total, and then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:32

After Noah was 500 years old, he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖
