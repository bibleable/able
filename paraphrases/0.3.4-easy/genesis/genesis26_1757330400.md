# Genesis 26 — Isaac in Gerar and the disputes over wells

**About this chapter**

A famine brings Isaac to Gerar, where the LORD repeats His promises.

Isaac calls Rebekah his sister.

He prospers, faces disputes over wells, and makes an agreement with Abimelech.

## Genesis 26:1

Another famine came after the one in Abraham’s time. 📖

Isaac went to Abimelech, king of the Philistines, at Gerar.

## Genesis 26:2

The LORD appeared to Isaac and said, “Do not go to Egypt. 📖

Settle in the land I tell you.

## Genesis 26:3

Stay here as a foreigner, and I will be with you and bless you. 📖

I will give you and your offspring all these lands.

I will confirm My oath to your father Abraham.

## Genesis 26:4

I will make your descendants as many as heaven’s stars and give them all these lands. 📖

All earth’s nations will be blessed through your offspring,

## Genesis 26:5

because Abraham obeyed My voice and kept My charge, commands, rules, and laws.” 📖

## Genesis 26:6

Isaac settled in Gerar. 📖

## Genesis 26:7

When the men asked about Rebekah, Isaac said she was his sister. 📖

He feared saying she was his wife.

He thought, “They will kill me because Rebekah is beautiful.”

## Genesis 26:8

After Isaac had stayed a long time, Abimelech, king of the Philistines, looked from a window. 📖

He was surprised to see Isaac caressing Rebekah, his wife.

**Help with “caressing”:** This means touching lovingly.

## Genesis 26:9

Abimelech called Isaac and said, “She really is your wife! 📖

How could you say she was your sister?”

Isaac replied, “I thought I might die because of her.”

## Genesis 26:10

Abimelech asked, “What have you done to us? 📖

Someone could easily have had sex with your wife.

You would have brought guilt on us.”

## Genesis 26:11

Abimelech warned everyone, “Anyone harming this man or his wife will certainly be put to death.” 📖

## Genesis 26:12

Isaac planted seed and harvested a hundred times as much that very year. 📖

The LORD blessed him,

## Genesis 26:13

and he grew richer and richer until he was extremely wealthy. 📖

## Genesis 26:14

He had so many flocks, herds, and servants that the Philistines envied him. 📖

## Genesis 26:15

The Philistines filled with dirt all the wells his father’s servants dug in Abraham’s days. 📖

## Genesis 26:16

Abimelech told Isaac, “Leave us. 📖

You are much too powerful for us.”

## Genesis 26:17

Isaac left, camped in the Valley of Gerar, and settled there. 📖

## Genesis 26:18

Isaac reopened Abraham’s wells, which the Philistines blocked after Abraham died. 📖

He gave them the same names his father had.

## Genesis 26:19

His servants dug in the valley and found a well of fresh water. 📖

## Genesis 26:20

Gerar’s herdsmen argued with Isaac’s herdsmen, saying, “The water is ours!” 📖

Isaac named the well Esek because they argued with him.

## Genesis 26:21

They dug another well and argued over it too. 📖

He named it Sitnah.

## Genesis 26:22

He moved and dug another well, and no one argued over it. 📖

He named it Rehoboth.

He said, “At last the LORD made room for us.

We will be fruitful in the land.”

## Genesis 26:23

Isaac went from there to Beersheba. 📖

## Genesis 26:24

That night, the LORD appeared and said, “I am your father Abraham’s God. 📖

Do not fear, because I am with you.

I will bless you and increase your descendants for My servant Abraham’s sake.”

## Genesis 26:25

Isaac built an altar and called on the LORD’s name there. 📖

He pitched his tent, and his servants dug a well.

## Genesis 26:26

Abimelech later came from Gerar with Ahuzzath his adviser and Phicol his army commander. 📖

## Genesis 26:27

Isaac asked, “Why have you come? 📖

You hated me and sent me away.”

## Genesis 26:28

They replied, “We plainly see the LORD has been with you. 📖

Let us swear an oath to one another and make a covenant,

**Help with “covenant”:** This is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 26:29

so you will not harm us. 📖

We did not harm you, but did only good and sent you away in peace.

Now the LORD has blessed you.”

## Genesis 26:30

Isaac made a feast for them. 📖

They ate and drank.

## Genesis 26:31

Early next morning, they swore an oath to one another. 📖

Isaac sent them away, and they left in peace.

## Genesis 26:32

That same day, Isaac’s servants reported about their new well. 📖

They said, “We found water!”

## Genesis 26:33

He named it Shibah. 📖

To this day, the city is called Beersheba.

**About “to this day”:** This refers to the time of the account.

## Genesis 26:34

Esau was forty when he married Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite. 📖

## Genesis 26:35

They brought grief to Isaac and Rebekah. 📖
