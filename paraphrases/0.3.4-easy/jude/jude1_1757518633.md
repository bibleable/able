# Jude 1 — Keep yourselves in God's love

**About this chapter**

Jude warns believers about people who corrupt the faith.

He uses strong examples and pictures, then urges believers to show mercy and stay in God's love.

## Jude 1:1

This letter is from Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and a brother of James. 📖

It is for those called by God, loved by God the Father, and kept in Jesus Christ.

## Jude 1:2

May mercy, peace, and love grow richly among you. 📖

**Help with a word:** Mercy means caring kindness towards someone who needs help or forgiveness.

## Jude 1:3

Dear friends, I was very eager to write about the salvation we share. 📖

But I needed to urge you to fight earnestly for the faith entrusted to God's people once for all.

**Help with words:** Salvation means being saved by God.

Fighting for the faith means holding firmly to the message entrusted to them.

## Jude 1:4

Some ungodly people have slipped in unnoticed. 📖

Their condemnation was marked out long ago.

They turn God's kindness into permission for immoral living.

They deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

## Jude 1:5

You already know this fully, but I want to remind you. 📖

After Jesus rescued his people from Egypt, he destroyed those who did not believe.

**About the source:** The BSB text names Jesus in this verse.

## Jude 1:6

Angels abandoned their proper home and did not stay within their own place of rule. 📖

He has kept them in eternal chains in darkness for judgment on the great day.

**Help with a word:** Eternal means lasting for ever.

## Jude 1:7

Sodom, Gomorrah, and nearby cities acted in a similar way. 📖

They gave themselves to wrong sexual behaviour and pursued strange flesh.

They stand as an example of punishment by eternal fire.

**Help with these words:** Flesh means bodies here.

Jude connects this phrase with sexual wrongdoing.

## Jude 1:8

In the same way, these dreamers defile their bodies, reject authority, and slander glorious beings. 📖

**Help with words:** Defile means spoil or make unclean through wrongdoing.

Slander means speak harmful false accusations.

## Jude 1:9

Even the archangel Michael disputed with the devil about Moses's body. 📖

He did not take it on himself to bring an insulting charge against the devil.

He said, “The Lord rebuke you!”

**Help with words:** An archangel is a chief angel.

Rebuke means correct or speak against someone firmly.

## Jude 1:10

But these people slander what they do not understand. 📖

Like animals without reason, they will be destroyed by the things they do by instinct.

**Help with a word:** Instinct means acting naturally without thinking things through.

## Jude 1:11

Terrible sorrow awaits them! 📖

They followed Cain's path, rushed into Balaam's error, and perished in Korah's rebellion.

## Jude 1:12

They are hidden reefs at your love feasts, eating with you without shame but shepherding only themselves. 📖

They are waterless clouds blown by the wind.

They are fruitless autumn trees, uprooted and twice dead.

**Help with these pictures:** Reefs are rocks beneath the water that can wreck boats.

Love feasts were shared meals among believers.

Jude's pictures describe danger, selfish care, and failure to give good fruit.

## Jude 1:13

They are wild sea waves, foaming up their own shame. 📖

They are wandering stars, with the blackest darkness reserved for them for ever.

## Jude 1:14

Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied about them too. 📖

“Look, the Lord is coming with tens of thousands of his holy ones.”

## Jude 1:15

“He will judge everyone. 📖

He will convict every ungodly person of every wicked deed and every harsh word ungodly sinners spoke against him.”

**Help with a word:** Convict means show someone to be guilty.

## Jude 1:16

These people grumble and are never satisfied. 📖

They follow their own lusts, boast arrogantly, and flatter people for their own benefit.

**Help with words:** Lusts are strong wrong desires.

Flatter means praise someone to gain something from them.

## Jude 1:17

But, dear friends, remember what our Lord Jesus Christ's apostles foretold. 📖

## Jude 1:18

They told you, “In the last times, scoffers will come, following their own ungodly desires.” 📖

**Help with a word:** Scoffers mock what they reject.

## Jude 1:19

These people cause divisions, are worldly, and do not have the Spirit. 📖

## Jude 1:20

But, dear friends, build yourselves up in your most holy faith and pray in the Holy Spirit. 📖

## Jude 1:21

Keep yourselves in God's love. 📖

Wait for our Lord Jesus Christ's mercy to bring you life that lasts for ever.

## Jude 1:22

Show mercy to those who doubt. 📖

## Jude 1:23

Save others by snatching them from the fire. 📖

Show mercy to still others with fear, hating even clothes stained by the flesh.

**Help with these pictures:** Fire pictures danger and judgment.

Stained clothing pictures sin's corrupting influence.

## Jude 1:24

God can keep you from stumbling. 📖

He can present you without fault in his glorious presence with great joy.

## Jude 1:25

To the only God our Saviour belong glory, majesty, rule, and authority through Jesus Christ our Lord. 📖

This is before all time, now, and for all eternity.

Amen.
