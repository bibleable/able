# Joel 2 — The Day of the LORD and a call to return

**About this chapter**

Joel describes an advancing army and calls the people to return to God.

The chapter includes promises of restoration and the gift of God’s Spirit.

## Joel 2:1

Blow the ram’s horn in Zion! 📖

Sound the alarm on the LORD’s holy mountain!

Let everyone in the land tremble.

The Day of the LORD is coming and is near.

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

## Joel 2:2

It is a day of darkness, gloom, clouds, and blackness. 📖

A great, strong army spreads over the mountains like dawn.

Nothing like it has happened before or will happen in the ages to come.

## Joel 2:3

Fire burns up what is before the army, and flames scorch what is behind it. 📖

Ahead, the land is like the Garden of Eden.

Behind, it is like a deserted wasteland.

Nothing will escape them.

**Help with Eden:** Eden was the garden described near the beginning of Genesis.

## Joel 2:4

They look like horses and gallop like swift warhorses. 📖

## Joel 2:5

They leap over the mountaintops with a sound like chariots. 📖

They sound like fire crackling as it burns dry stalks.

They are like a mighty army ready for battle.

**Help with chariots:** Chariots are vehicles pulled by horses, often used in war.

## Joel 2:6

Nations twist in horror before them. 📖

Every face turns pale.

## Joel 2:7

They charge like mighty men and climb walls like soldiers. 📖

Each marches in line, without turning from the course.

## Joel 2:8

They do not push one another. 📖

Each stays on his path.

They burst through the defences without breaking their lines.

## Joel 2:9

They rush into the city and run along the wall. 📖

They climb into houses, entering through windows like thieves.

## Joel 2:10

The earth shakes before them, and the heavens tremble. 📖

The sun and moon grow dark.

The stars lose their brightness.

## Joel 2:11

The LORD raises his voice before his army. 📖

His camp is very large.

Those who obey his command are mighty.

The Day of the LORD is great and very dreadful.

Who can bear it?

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

## Joel 2:12

The LORD declares, “Even now, return to me with all your heart. 📖

Return with fasting, weeping, and grieving.”

**Help with fasting:** Fasting means choosing not to eat for a time.

## Joel 2:13

Tear your hearts, not your clothes, and return to the LORD your God. 📖

He is kind and compassionate, slow to anger, and full of faithful love.

He turns from sending disaster.

**Help with tear your hearts:** Tearing clothes was a sign of grief.

This picture calls for an inward change, beyond an outward sign.

**Help with compassionate:** Compassionate means caring about suffering and wanting to help.

## Joel 2:14

Who knows? 📖

God may turn and decide not to send disaster.

He may leave a blessing: grain and drink offerings for the LORD your God.

**Help with offerings:** These are gifts given to God in worship.

## Joel 2:15

Blow the ram’s horn in Zion. 📖

Set apart a fast and announce a holy gathering.

**Help with ram’s horn:** This is an instrument made from a male sheep’s horn.

**Help with fast:** A fast is a time of not eating.

**Help with holy:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Joel 2:16

Gather the people and set the community apart as holy. 📖

Gather the older people, children, and even babies feeding at the breast.

Let the bridegroom leave his room and the bride leave her room.

**Help with holy:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Joel 2:17

Let the priests who serve before the LORD cry between the entrance porch and the altar. 📖

Let them say, “LORD, spare your people.

Do not make your inheritance an object of scorn among the nations.

Why should the peoples say, ‘Where is their God?’”

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

**Help with inheritance:** Here, God’s inheritance means his people.

## Joel 2:18

Then the LORD became jealous for his land and spared his people. 📖

**Help with jealous:** Here, jealous describes the LORD’s strong concern for what belongs to him.

## Joel 2:19

The LORD answered his people, “I will send you grain, new wine, and oil. 📖

You will have enough of them to satisfy you.

I will never again make you an object of scorn among the nations.

## Joel 2:20

I will drive the northern army away from you into a barren, deserted land. 📖

Its front lines will go into the Eastern Sea, and its rear guard into the Western Sea.

Its stink and foul smell will rise.”

For he has done great things.

**About he:** The source ends this verse with “He has done great things.”

The draft preserves the wording’s reference for further editorial discussion.

## Joel 2:21

Land, do not be afraid! 📖

Rejoice and be glad, because the LORD has done great things.

## Joel 2:22

Wild animals, do not be afraid! 📖

The open grasslands have become green.

The trees bear fruit, and the fig tree and vine give their best.

## Joel 2:23

Children of Zion, be glad and rejoice in the LORD your God. 📖

He has given you the autumn rains for your vindication.

He sends autumn and spring showers as before.

**Help with vindication:** Vindication means showing that someone is in the right.

The wording about rain and vindication needs further editorial discussion.

## Joel 2:24

The threshing floors will be full of grain. 📖

The large containers will overflow with new wine and oil.

**Help with threshing floors:** These are places where grain is separated from its dry covering.

## Joel 2:25

The LORD says, “I will repay you for the years the locusts ate. 📖

There were the swarming locust, young locust, destroying locust, and devouring locust.

They were my great army that I sent against you.

**Help with locusts:** Locusts are insects that can gather in large numbers and eat crops.

## Joel 2:26

You will eat plenty and be satisfied. 📖

You will praise the name of the LORD your God, who has done wonders for you.

My people will never again be put to shame.

## Joel 2:27

You will know that I am present in Israel and am the LORD your God. 📖

There is no other.

My people will never again be put to shame.

## Joel 2:28

Afterward, I will pour out my Spirit on all people. 📖

Your sons and daughters will prophesy.

Your old men will dream dreams, and your young men will see visions.

**Help with prophesy:** Prophesy means speak a message from God.

**Help with visions:** Visions are things God shows a person.

**Help with pour out:** This pictures giving God’s Spirit freely and widely.

## Joel 2:29

Even on my male and female servants, I will pour out my Spirit in those days. 📖

**Help with pour out:** This pictures giving God’s Spirit freely and widely.

## Joel 2:30

I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth. 📖

There will be blood, fire, and columns of smoke.

## Joel 2:31

The sun will turn to darkness and the moon to blood. 📖

This will happen before the great and awesome Day of the LORD comes.

**Help with awesome:** Awesome means causing great wonder and awe.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

## Joel 2:32

Everyone who calls on the LORD’s name will be saved. 📖

There will be rescue on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, as the LORD promised.

It will be among the survivors whom the LORD calls.

**Help with calls on the LORD’s name:** This means turning to the LORD and asking for his help.
