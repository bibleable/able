# Joel 1 — A ruined harvest and a call to pray

**About this chapter**

Joel describes a disaster that destroys food and affects people and animals.

He calls the people to grieve and cry to the LORD.

## Joel 1:1

This is the LORD’s word that came to Joel son of Pethuel. 📖

## Joel 1:2

Listen, elders! 📖

Listen, everyone living in the land!

Has anything like this happened in your days or your ancestors’ days?

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

## Joel 1:3

Tell your children about it. 📖

Let your children tell their children, and let those children tell the next generation.

## Joel 1:4

The swarming locust ate what the devouring locust left. 📖

The young locust ate what the swarming locust left.

The destroying locust ate what the young locust left.

**Help with locusts:** Locusts are insects that can gather in large numbers and eat crops.

The source uses these 4 descriptions of locusts.

## Joel 1:5

Wake up and cry, you people who get drunk! 📖

Wail, all you wine drinkers!

The sweet wine has been taken from your mouths.

## Joel 1:6

A nation has invaded the LORD’s land, powerful and too many to count. 📖

Its teeth are like a lion’s teeth.

Its fangs are like a lioness’s fangs.

**Help with fangs:** Fangs are long, sharp teeth.

These teeth picture the invaders’ destructive power.

## Joel 1:7

It ruined the LORD’s grapevine and broke his fig tree into pieces. 📖

It stripped off the bark and threw it away.

The branches have turned white.

## Joel 1:8

Cry like a virgin wearing sackcloth, grieving for the husband of her youth. 📖

**Help with virgin:** A virgin is someone who has not had sex.

**Help with sackcloth:** Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

**About this comparison:** The source combines these descriptions in its picture of grief.

The draft keeps them without inventing details about a wedding.

## Joel 1:9

Grain offerings and drink offerings have stopped at the LORD’s house. 📖

The priests who serve before the LORD are grieving.

**Help with offerings:** These are gifts given to God in worship.

**Help with the LORD’s house:** This is the temple, a place of worship.

## Joel 1:10

The field is ruined, and the land grieves. 📖

The grain is destroyed, the new wine has dried up, and the oil fails.

## Joel 1:11

Be distressed, farmers! 📖

Cry, you who care for vines, over the wheat and barley!

The harvest in the field has died.

**Help with wheat and barley:** These are grains grown for food.

## Joel 1:12

The grapevine has dried up, and the fig tree has withered. 📖

The pomegranate, palm, apple, and all the orchard’s trees have withered.

Human joy has surely dried up too.

**Help with withered:** Withered means dried up and lost life.

## Joel 1:13

Priests, put on sackcloth and grieve! 📖

Cry, you who serve at the altar!

Servants of my God, come and spend the night in sackcloth!

Grain and drink offerings are held back from your God’s house.

**Help with sackcloth:** Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief.

**Help with altar:** An altar is a place for offerings to God.

**Help with offerings and house:** These gifts were given in worship at God’s temple.

## Joel 1:14

Set apart a fast. 📖

Announce a solemn gathering.

Gather the elders and everyone in the land at the house of the LORD your God.

Cry out to the LORD!

**Help with fast:** A fast is a time of not eating.

**Help with solemn:** Solemn means serious.

**Help with elders:** Elders are community leaders.

**Help with house:** Here, the LORD’s house is the temple, a place of worship.

## Joel 1:15

What a dreadful day! 📖

The Day of the LORD is near.

It will come as destruction from the Almighty.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

**Help with Almighty:** Almighty is a title for God.

## Joel 1:16

Has food not been cut off before our own eyes? 📖

Have joy and gladness not been cut off from our God’s house?

**Help with house:** Here, God’s house is the temple, a place of worship.

## Joel 1:17

The seeds lie shrivelled under lumps of earth. 📖

The storehouses are ruined, and the grain stores are broken down.

The grain has dried up.

## Joel 1:18

How the cattle groan! 📖

The herds wander in confusion because they have no grass to eat.

Even the flocks of sheep suffer.

## Joel 1:19

I call to you, LORD. 📖

Fire has burned up the open grasslands.

Flames have scorched all the trees of the field.

## Joel 1:20

Even the wild animals pant for you. 📖

The streams have dried up, and fire has burned up the open grasslands.
