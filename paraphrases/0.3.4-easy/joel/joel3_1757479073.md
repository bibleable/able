# Joel 3 — The LORD judges the nations

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks of judgment for violence against his people.

The chapter includes slavery, war, and promises for Judah and Jerusalem.

## Joel 3:1

The LORD says, “Yes, in those days and at that time, I will restore Judah and Jerusalem from captivity. 📖

**Help with captivity:** Captivity means being held under another power.

## Joel 3:2

At that time, I will gather all nations and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. 📖

I will judge them there concerning my people, my inheritance Israel.

They scattered my people among the nations and divided my land.

**Help with inheritance:** Here, this means Israel as the people belonging to the LORD.

## Joel 3:3

They cast lots for my people. 📖

They traded a boy for a prostitute and sold a girl for wine to drink.

**Help with cast lots:** People used marked objects to decide how to divide what they had taken.

**Help with prostitute:** A prostitute is someone who sells sexual acts.

**About the children:** The source describes children being traded as property.

This harmful treatment is retained in the draft.

## Joel 3:4

Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia, what do you have against me? 📖

Are you paying me back for something?

If you take revenge on me, I will quickly pay you back on your own heads.

**Help with on your own heads:** This pictures their actions’ consequences falling on them.

## Joel 3:5

You took my silver and gold. 📖

You carried my finest treasures to your temples.

**Help with temples:** Temples are places of worship.

## Joel 3:6

You sold Judah’s and Jerusalem’s people to the Greeks. 📖

You sent them far from their homeland.

## Joel 3:7

I will stir them up from the places where you sold them. 📖

I will pay you back on your own heads.

**Help with on your own heads:** This pictures their actions’ consequences falling on them.

## Joel 3:8

I will sell your sons and daughters into the hands of Judah’s people. 📖

They will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation.”

The LORD has spoken.

**About this judgment:** The passage describes people being sold.

This severe judgment is retained for further editorial discussion.

## Joel 3:9

Announce this among the nations. 📖

“Prepare for war!

Rouse the mighty men!

Let all the soldiers come forward and attack!

## Joel 3:10

Beat your plough blades into swords and your pruning hooks into spears. 📖

Let the weak say, ‘I am strong!’

**Help with these tools:** Plough blades cut soil for planting.

Pruning hooks cut branches.

The verse describes turning farm tools into weapons.

## Joel 3:11

Come quickly and gather, all you surrounding nations.” 📖

LORD, bring down your mighty ones!

## Joel 3:12

The LORD says, “Let the nations rise and come to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. 📖

I will sit there to judge all the nations on every side.

## Joel 3:13

Swing the sickle, because the harvest is ripe. 📖

Come and trample the grapes, because the winepress is full.

The wine containers overflow because their wickedness is great.

**Help with this picture:** A sickle is a curved blade for cutting crops.

A winepress is a place for crushing grapes.

The harvest and overflowing wine picture judgment against great wrongdoing.

## Joel 3:14

Crowds and crowds fill the valley of decision! 📖

The Day of the LORD is near in the valley of decision.

**Help with decision:** Here, the valley is connected with the LORD’s judgment.

**Help with Day of the LORD:** This means a time when the LORD acts in judgment.

## Joel 3:15

The sun and moon will grow dark, and the stars will stop shining. 📖

## Joel 3:16

The LORD will roar from Zion and raise his voice from Jerusalem. 📖

Heaven and earth will tremble.

But the LORD will be a shelter for his people.

He will be a strong place of safety for Israel’s people.

## Joel 3:17

Then you will know that I am the LORD your God. 📖

I live in Zion, my holy mountain.

Jerusalem will be holy, never again overrun by foreigners.

**Help with holy:** Holy means set apart for God.

## Joel 3:18

On that day, the mountains will drip with sweet wine, and the hills will flow with milk. 📖

All Judah’s streams will run with water.

A spring will flow from the LORD’s house to water the Valley of Acacias.

**Help with these pictures:** Wine, milk, and flowing water picture plenty.

**Help with the LORD’s house:** This is the temple, a place of worship.

## Joel 3:19

Egypt will become deserted, and Edom will become a desert wasteland. 📖

This is because of violence against Judah’s people.

They shed innocent blood in Judah’s land.

**Help with innocent blood:** This means killing people who should not be killed.

## Joel 3:20

But Judah will have people living in it forever. 📖

Jerusalem will have people living in it from generation to generation.

## Joel 3:21

I will avenge their blood, which I have not yet avenged.” 📖

The LORD lives in Zion.

**Help with avenge their blood:** This means punish those responsible for their deaths.
