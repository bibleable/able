# John 3 — Jesus tells Nicodemus about new life

**About this chapter**

Nicodemus comes at night to talk with Jesus.

Later, John the Baptist explains why he is glad people are going to Jesus.

## Nicodemus visits Jesus at night

## John 3:1

Nicodemus was a Pharisee.

He was a leader among the Jewish people.

**Help with these words**

The Pharisees were a group of Jewish people who worked hard to keep God’s law.

## John 3:2

One night Nicodemus came to Jesus.

He said, “Rabbi, we know God sent you to teach us.

No one could do the signs you do unless God was with him.”

**Help with these words**

Rabbi means teacher.

The signs Nicodemus saw were things Jesus did that showed God was with him.

## John 3:3

Jesus told Nicodemus, “I tell you the truth.

You must be born again to see God’s kingdom.”

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God ruling as King over his people.

The word Jesus used for *again* can also mean *from above*.

New life from above is new life that comes from God.

## John 3:4

Nicodemus asked, “How can someone be born when they are old?

Can they go back inside their mother and be born a second time?”

**About this verse**

Nicodemus thinks Jesus means being born as a baby a second time.

**Pause and answer**

Who came to Jesus at night?

## God’s Spirit gives new life

## John 3:5

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

To enter God’s kingdom, you must be born of water and the Spirit.

**Help with these words**

The Spirit is God’s Holy Spirit.

People who study the Bible explain *water* here in different ways.

## John 3:6

“Human parents give birth to human life.

God’s Spirit gives birth to spiritual life.

## John 3:7

“So do not be surprised that I said you must be born again.

## John 3:8

“The wind blows where it wants.

You hear the sound of the wind.

But you cannot tell where it came from.

You cannot tell where it is going.

It is the same with everyone born of the Spirit.”

**Help with these words**

In the language Jesus’ words were written in, one word means both *wind* and *Spirit*.

![A tall reed bends to one side.](../pictures/bending-reed.svg)

**Find the bend**

Follow the stem to the place where it bends. You cannot see the wind, but you can see what it does to the reed. Jesus uses the wind to help Nicodemus think about the Spirit.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives people new life?

## Nicodemus asks how this can happen

## John 3:9

Nicodemus asked, “How can this happen?”

## John 3:10

Jesus said, “You are a teacher of the people of Israel.

Do you still not understand these things?

## John 3:11

“I tell you the truth.

We speak about what we know.

We tell you what we have seen.

Yet you people do not accept what we say.

## John 3:12

“I have told you about things on earth.

You do not trust what I say.

So how will you trust me if I tell you about things in heaven?

**Pause and answer**

Did the people trust what Jesus told them?

## The Son of Man will be lifted up

## John 3:13

“No one has gone up to heaven except the Son of Man.

He is the one who came down from heaven.

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

## John 3:14

“Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness.

In the same way, the Son of Man must be lifted up.

**About the picture**

![A bronze-colored snake winds around an upright pole.](../pictures/bronze-snake-on-pole.svg)

Find the snake at the top of the pole. Long ago, snakes bit God’s people in the wilderness. God told Moses to make a bronze snake and lift it up on a pole. People who were bitten looked at it, and they lived. Jesus is speaking about the time when he will be lifted up on a cross.

## John 3:15

“Then everyone who trusts him will have life that lasts forever.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses lift up in the wilderness?

## God gives his Son

## John 3:16

“God loved the world so much that he gave his one and only Son.

Everyone who trusts his Son will not be lost.

Instead, they will have life that lasts forever.

**Help with these words**

To be lost here means to be cut off from God forever.

## John 3:17

“God did not send his Son into the world to judge the world.

He sent his Son so the world could be saved through him.

## John 3:18

“Anyone who trusts the Son is not judged.

Anyone who does not trust him is judged already.

This is because they have not trusted the name of God’s one and only Son.

**Help with these words**

To judge someone here means to decide they are guilty.

The name of the Son means who the Son is.

**Pause and answer**

Why did God send his Son into the world?

## People come into the light

## John 3:19

“This is how people are judged.

The light has come into the world.

But people loved darkness more than the light, because the things they did were evil.

**Help with these words**

Jesus is called the light.

As light shows what is in a room, Jesus shows what people do.

## John 3:20

“Everyone who does evil hates the light.

They stay away from the light.

They do not want the light to show the wrong things they do.

## John 3:21

“But people who do what is true come into the light.

Then everyone can see that they did these things with God.”

**Help with these words**

Doing things with God means living God’s way, close to him.

**Pause and answer**

Why do people who do evil stay away from the light?

## John’s followers bring him news

## John 3:22

After this, Jesus and his followers went into the countryside of Judea.

Jesus stayed there with them and baptized people.

**Help with these words**

To baptize means to put a person in water or pour water on them.

It shows the person is turning to God.

## John 3:23

John the Baptist was also baptizing people at Aenon, near Salim.

There was plenty of water there.

People kept coming to be baptized.

## John 3:24

This happened before John was put in prison.

## John 3:25

Some of John’s followers argued with a Jewish man.

They argued about the washings people did to be clean for worship.

## John 3:26

John’s followers came to John.

They said, “Rabbi, remember the man who was with you across the Jordan River?

You told people about him.

Look, now he is baptizing, and everyone is going to him.”

**Pause and answer**

What news did the followers bring to John?

## John is glad people are going to Jesus

## John 3:27

John answered, “A person can receive only what God gives from heaven.

## John 3:28

“You yourselves heard me say, ‘I am not the Christ.’

I said, ‘God sent me ahead of him.’

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God promised to send.

## John 3:29

“At a wedding, the man who marries the bride is the bridegroom.

The bridegroom’s friend stands and listens for him.

The friend is very glad when he hears the bridegroom’s voice.

I am glad in the same way.

Now my joy is complete.

## John 3:30

“Jesus must become greater.

I must become less important.

**Help with these words**

John compares himself to a friend at a wedding.

Jesus is like the bridegroom.

The friend is glad for the bridegroom.

In the same way, John is glad people are following Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

How did John feel when people went to Jesus?

## The Son speaks God’s words

## John 3:31

“The one who comes from above is greater than all.

Someone from earth belongs to earth.

That person speaks like someone from earth.

The one who comes from heaven is greater than all.

**Help with these words**

*From above* is the same word Jesus used for being born *again* or *from above* in John 3:3.

People who study the Bible are not sure if John is still speaking in John 3:31 to 3:36. The writer of the book may be speaking.

## John 3:32

“He tells people what he has seen and heard.

Yet no one accepts what he says.

## John 3:33

“Anyone who does accept what he says shows for sure that God tells the truth.

## John 3:34

“The one God sent speaks God’s words.

God gives the Spirit without limit.

## John 3:35

“The Father loves the Son.

He has put everything in his Son’s hands.

**Help with these words**

To put everything in his Son’s hands means the Son is in charge of everything.

## John 3:36

“Anyone who trusts the Son has life that lasts forever.

Anyone who refuses to obey the Son will not see that life.

God’s anger stays on that person.”

**Help with these words**

God’s anger is not like a bad temper.

It is God’s fair anger against evil.

**Pause and answer**

What do people who trust the Son receive?
