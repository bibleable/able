# Proverbs 18 — Listen before you answer

**About this chapter**

Solomon describes two people telling their sides of a dispute. He also considers what words can do to a friendship.

## Whose voice matters?

## Proverbs 18:1

Someone who shuts others out goes after what they want (their own desires).

They push back against all good sense (sound judgment).

## Proverbs 18:2

A fool takes no pleasure in learning how things are (understanding).

All he wants is to say what’s on his mind (air his opinions).

## Proverbs 18:3

When a wicked person comes, looking down on others (contempt) comes too.

Shame goes hand in hand with being spoken against (disgrace).

## Proverbs 18:4

A person’s words can be deep like water.

Wisdom is like a spring that feeds a flowing stream.

![Water flows from two springs on a hillside.](../pictures/acsah-springs.svg)

**Follow the water**

Find a spring on the hill. Trace the water flowing out of it. Solomon pictures wisdom flowing into words like water from a spring.

## Words that start trouble

## Proverbs 18:5

It’s wrong to give a guilty person special treatment (show partiality).

It’s wrong to rule against someone who did nothing wrong (deny justice to the innocent).

## Proverbs 18:6

Foolish words start fights.

Someone who speaks that way risks a beating.

## Proverbs 18:7

A fool’s own words bring him down (bring ruin).

What he says is a trap (snare) for his own life (soul).

## Proverbs 18:8

The words of someone who spreads rumors (a gossip) are like tasty bites of food (choice morsels).

They go down deep inside you.

## Where people look for safety

## Proverbs 18:9

Someone who lets their work go undone (neglects it) is like a brother to someone who tears things down.

## Proverbs 18:10

The LORD’s name is like a strong tower.

Those who do right run there for safety.

This means they turn to the LORD for protection.

## Proverbs 18:11

A rich person sees money as a city with strong walls.

He imagines his wealth is a high wall around him.

## Proverbs 18:12

Before someone falls (meets their downfall), they think too highly of themselves (are proud).

Before others honor them, they take a low place (show humility).

## Listen to the whole matter

## Proverbs 18:13

Answering before you’ve heard the whole matter is foolish (folly) and brings you shame (disgrace).

## Proverbs 18:14

When you’re sick, keeping your courage up (a strong spirit) helps you get through it.

But if you lose heart (have a broken spirit), who can bear it?

## Proverbs 18:15

People who think things through (the discerning) take in what they learn.

Wise people listen for what they can learn (seek knowledge).

## Proverbs 18:16

A gift can open a door for the giver.

It can help someone meet a powerful person.

## Two sides of a quarrel

## Proverbs 18:17

The first person to tell their side seems right.

Then the other person comes and asks questions to test what was said (cross-examines them).

## Proverbs 18:18

Drawing lots (casting the lot) settles fights and separates strong opponents.

To draw lots, people can pick from marked objects without looking.

## Proverbs 18:19

A brother you have hurt (an offended brother) is harder to win back than a city with strong walls (a fortified city).

Fights (disputes) keep people apart like bars across a castle gate.

## What our words produce

## Proverbs 18:20

What your words produce (the fruit of your mouth) fills your belly.

It’s like eating your fill from the crop you’ve grown (the harvest from your lips).

## Proverbs 18:21

What you say (the tongue) can bring life or death.

If you love to use it, you’ll live with what your words bring (eat their fruit).

## Proverbs 18:22

A man who finds a wife finds something good.

The LORD has been good to him (shown him favor).

## Proverbs 18:23

A poor person begs to be treated kindly (pleads for mercy).

A rich person answers with hard words (harshly).

## Proverbs 18:24

Someone with many people around them (companions) can still end up ruined (come to ruin).

Yet one friend can stick closer than a brother.

**Pause and answer**

What should you do before you answer someone?
