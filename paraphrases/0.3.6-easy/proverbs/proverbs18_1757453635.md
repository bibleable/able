# Proverbs 18 — Listen before you answer

**About this chapter**

Solomon describes two people telling their sides of a dispute. He also considers what words can do to a friendship.

## Whose voice matters?

## Proverbs 18:1

Someone who shuts others out to get his own way refuses good advice.

## Proverbs 18:2

A foolish person has little interest in understanding.

All he wants is to say what he thinks.

## Proverbs 18:3

Wicked actions bring contempt.

When someone acts shamefully, people lose respect for that person.

## Proverbs 18:4

A person’s words can be deep like water.

Wisdom is like a spring that feeds a flowing stream.

![Water flows from two springs on a hillside.](../pictures/acsah-springs.svg)

**Follow the water**

Find a spring on the hill. Trace the water flowing out of it. Solomon pictures wisdom flowing into words like water from a spring.

## Words that start trouble

## Proverbs 18:5

It is wrong to take the side of a guilty person.

It is wrong to deny an innocent person a fair hearing.

## Proverbs 18:6

Foolish words start fights.

Someone who speaks that way risks a beating.

## Proverbs 18:7

A foolish person’s own words bring ruin.

Those words catch the speaker like a trap.

## Proverbs 18:8

Gossip is like a bite of delicious food.

We take it in eagerly.

What we hear stays deep inside us.

## Where people look for safety

## Proverbs 18:9

Someone who neglects their work is like someone who destroys it.

Both can spoil what others have built.

## Proverbs 18:10

The LORD’s name is like a strong tower.

Those who do right run there for safety.

This means they turn to the LORD for protection.

## Proverbs 18:11

A rich person sees money as a city with strong walls.

He imagines his wealth is a high wall around him.

## Proverbs 18:12

Pride comes before a fall.

Humility comes before honor.

## Listen to the whole matter

## Proverbs 18:13

Listen to the whole matter before giving an answer.

Answering too soon is foolish and brings shame.

## Proverbs 18:14

Courage can help a sick person endure illness.

But when someone loses heart, how can they bear it?

## Proverbs 18:15

People who think things through (the discerning) keep learning.

Wise people listen for things they can learn.

## Proverbs 18:16

A gift can open a door for the giver.

It can help someone meet a powerful person.

## Two sides of a quarrel

## Proverbs 18:17

The first person to speak in a dispute may sound right.

Then the other person comes and asks questions.

## Proverbs 18:18

People sometimes drew lots to settle a quarrel.

They used marked objects to decide between two sides.

This could end a fight between powerful people.

## Proverbs 18:19

A brother you have hurt can be harder to win back than a walled city.

Quarrels keep people apart like heavy bars across a gate.

## What our words produce

## Proverbs 18:20

What you say can bring you a harvest.

Just as farmers eat what they grow, you live on what your words produce.

## Proverbs 18:21

Words have power to bring life or death.

People who love to talk must live with what their words bring (eat their fruit).

## Proverbs 18:22

A man who finds a wife receives a good gift.

The LORD has shown him kindness.

## Proverbs 18:23

A poor person begs for mercy.

A rich person answers with harsh words.

## Proverbs 18:24

Someone with many companions can still come to ruin.

Yet one friend may stay closer than a brother.

**Pause and answer**

What should you do before you answer someone?
