## Daniel 9:1
Darius son of Xerxes was a Mede who became ruler of the Chaldeans. This happened in his first year. 👑
## Daniel 9:2
In that year, I understood from the sacred books that Jerusalem’s ruin would last seventy years. The LORD had spoken this through Jeremiah the prophet. 📖
## Daniel 9:3
I turned to the Lord God in prayer and requests, with fasting, sackcloth, and ashes. 🙏
## Daniel 9:4
I prayed to the LORD my God and confessed. “Lord, great and awesome God, You keep Your covenant of faithful love with those who love You and obey Your commands. ❤️
## Daniel 9:5
We sinned, did wrong, acted wickedly, and rebelled. We turned from Your commands and rules. 💔
## Daniel 9:6
We ignored Your prophets, who spoke in Your name to our kings, leaders, ancestors, and all the land’s people. ⚠️
## Daniel 9:7
Lord, righteousness belongs to You, but shame covers us today. It covers Judah, Jerusalem, and all Israel, near and far. You scattered us to other countries because we were unfaithful to You. 💔
## Daniel 9:8
LORD, shame covers us, our kings, leaders, and ancestors, because we sinned against You. 🙏
## Daniel 9:9
Mercy and forgiveness belong to the Lord our God, though we rebelled against Him. ❤️
## Daniel 9:10
We disobeyed the LORD our God’s voice and laws, which He gave through His prophets. ⚠️
## Daniel 9:11
All Israel broke Your law and refused to obey You. So the curse and oath in Moses’ law fell on us. Moses was God’s servant, and we sinned against You. ⚖️
## Daniel 9:12
You fulfilled Your words against us and our rulers with a great disaster. Nothing under heaven has happened like what happened to Jerusalem. 💔
## Daniel 9:13
This disaster came as Moses’ law said. Yet we did not seek the LORD our God’s favor by turning from sin and attending to Your truth. ⚠️
## Daniel 9:14
The LORD kept this disaster ready and brought it upon us. He is righteous in all He does, but we disobeyed Him. ⚖️
## Daniel 9:15
Lord our God, You brought Your people from Egypt with a mighty hand and made Your name famous to this day. We sinned and acted wickedly. 💔
## Daniel 9:16
Lord, according to Your righteous acts, turn Your anger from Jerusalem, Your city and holy mountain. Our sins and our ancestors’ evil made Jerusalem and Your people objects of scorn to our neighbors. 🙏
## Daniel 9:17
Our God, hear Your servant’s prayers and requests. For Your sake, let Your face shine on Your ruined holy place. ✨
## Daniel 9:18
My God, listen and look at the ruin of the city that bears Your name. We ask because of Your great mercy, rather than our righteous acts. 🙏
## Daniel 9:19
Lord, listen and forgive! Hear and act! Do not delay, my God, for Your own sake. Your city and people bear Your name.” 🙏
## Daniel 9:20
I continued speaking, praying, and confessing my sin and Israel’s sin. I made my request before the LORD my God about His holy mountain. 🙏
## Daniel 9:21
While I prayed, Gabriel flew quickly to me about the evening sacrifice’s time. He was the man from my earlier vision. ✨
## Daniel 9:22
He instructed me, “Daniel, I came to give you insight and understanding. 📖
## Daniel 9:23
An answer went out when you began your requests. I came to tell you because you are highly precious. Consider the message and understand the vision. 💭
## Daniel 9:24
Seventy weeks are decreed for your people and holy city. This is to stop rebellion, end sin, and make atonement for evil. It is to bring everlasting righteousness, seal vision and prophecy, and anoint the Most Holy Place. ⏳
## Daniel 9:25
Understand this: seven weeks and sixty-two weeks will pass from the order to restore Jerusalem until the Messiah, the Prince. Jerusalem will be rebuilt with streets and a trench during troubled times. 🏘️
## Daniel 9:26
After the sixty-two weeks, the Messiah will be cut off and have nothing. A coming prince’s people will destroy the city and holy place. The end will come like a flood. War will continue to the end, and ruins are decreed. ⚖️
## Daniel 9:27
He will confirm a covenant with many for one week. In its middle, he will end sacrifice and offering. The abomination that brings ruin will come on the temple’s wing until the decreed destruction is poured upon him.” ⚠️
