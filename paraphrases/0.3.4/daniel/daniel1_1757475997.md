## Daniel 1:1
In Jehoiakim king of Judah’s third year, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came and surrounded Jerusalem with his army. ⚔️
## Daniel 1:2
The Lord handed Jehoiakim and some objects from God’s house to him. Nebuchadnezzar took the objects to Shinar and placed them in his god’s temple treasury. 🏺
## Daniel 1:3
The king ordered Ashpenaz, his chief court official, to bring some Israelites from the royal family and nobles. 👥
## Daniel 1:4
They must be handsome young men without defects, wise, knowledgeable, quick to understand, and fit to serve in the palace. They were to learn the Chaldeans’ language and literature. 📖
## Daniel 1:5
The king assigned them daily royal food and wine. They would train for three years and then serve the king. 🍞
## Daniel 1:6
Among them were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah from Judah. 👥
## Daniel 1:7
The chief official renamed Daniel Belteshazzar, Hananiah Shadrach, Mishael Meshach, and Azariah Abednego. 📋
## Daniel 1:8
Daniel decided not to make himself unclean with the king’s food or wine. He asked the chief official for permission to avoid this uncleanness. 🙏
## Daniel 1:9
God made the chief official favor Daniel and show him mercy. ❤️
## Daniel 1:10
But the official said, “I fear my lord the king, who assigned your food and drink. What if he sees you thinner than other young men your age? You would put my head at risk before him!” ⚠️
## Daniel 1:11
Daniel spoke to the steward appointed over him, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. 💬
## Daniel 1:12
He said, “Please test your servants for ten days. Give us only vegetables and water. 🥗
## Daniel 1:13
Compare us with the young men who eat the royal food. Then treat us according to what you see.” 👀
## Daniel 1:14
The steward agreed and tested them for ten days. 📅
## Daniel 1:15
After ten days, they looked healthier and better fed than all the young men who ate the king’s food. 💪
## Daniel 1:16
The steward kept giving them vegetables instead of their rich food and wine. 🥗
## Daniel 1:17
God gave these four men knowledge and understanding in every kind of literature and wisdom. Daniel understood all kinds of visions and dreams. 📖
## Daniel 1:18
At the end of the king’s appointed time, the chief official presented them to Nebuchadnezzar. 👑
## Daniel 1:19
The king spoke with them and found none of the other young men equal to Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. They entered his service. 👥
## Daniel 1:20
In every question of wisdom and understanding, the king found them ten times better than all his kingdom’s magicians and enchanters. 💭
## Daniel 1:21
Daniel remained there until King Cyrus’s first year. ⏳
