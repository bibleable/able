## Daniel 2:1
In his second year, Nebuchadnezzar had dreams that troubled his spirit. He could not sleep. 🌙
## Daniel 2:2
He called the magicians, enchanters, sorcerers, and astrologers to explain his dreams. They came and stood before him. 👥
## Daniel 2:3
He said, “I had a dream, and my spirit longs to understand it.” 💭
## Daniel 2:4
The astrologers answered in Aramaic, “King, may you live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will explain it.” 💬
## Daniel 2:5
The king replied, “My word is final. Tell me the dream and its meaning, or you will be cut apart and your houses made rubble. ⚠️
## Daniel 2:6
If you tell me, I will give you gifts, rewards, and great honor. Tell me the dream and its meaning.” 🎁
## Daniel 2:7
They answered again, “Tell your servants the dream, and we will explain it.” 💬
## Daniel 2:8
The king said, “I know you delay because you see my word is final. ⚠️
## Daniel 2:9
If you do not tell me the dream, one judgment awaits you. You agreed to deceive me with false words, hoping circumstances would change. Tell me the dream so I can know you can explain it.” ⚖️
## Daniel 2:10
The astrologers said, “No one on earth can do this! No king, however powerful, has ever asked a magician, enchanter, or astrologer for this. 💬
## Daniel 2:11
Only the gods can answer such a difficult request. They do not live among mortal people.” ⚠️
## Daniel 2:12
The king became furious and ordered all Babylon’s wise men killed. 😠
## Daniel 2:13
The execution order went out. Men searched for Daniel and his friends to kill them. ⚔️
## Daniel 2:14
Arioch, commander of the royal guard, went to kill Babylon’s wise men. Daniel answered him wisely and carefully. 💬
## Daniel 2:15
He asked, “Why is the king’s order so harsh?” Arioch explained the matter. ⚠️
## Daniel 2:16
Daniel asked the king for time so that he could explain the dream. ⏳
## Daniel 2:17
Daniel returned home and told Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah what happened. 🏠
## Daniel 2:18
He urged them to ask heaven’s God for mercy about this mystery. Then they would not be killed with Babylon’s other wise men. 🙏
## Daniel 2:19
That night, the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a vision. He praised heaven’s God. ✨
## Daniel 2:20
He said, “Praise God’s name forever and ever! Wisdom and power belong to Him. 🙏
## Daniel 2:21
He changes times and seasons, removes kings, and appoints kings. He gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those with understanding. 👑
## Daniel 2:22
He reveals deep and hidden things. He knows what lies in darkness, and light lives with Him. ✨
## Daniel 2:23
My ancestors’ God, I thank and praise You for giving me wisdom and power. You answered our request and revealed the king’s dream to us.” 🙏
## Daniel 2:24
Daniel went to Arioch, who was appointed to kill Babylon’s wise men. He said, “Do not kill them! Bring me to the king, and I will explain the dream.” 🛡️
## Daniel 2:25
Arioch quickly brought Daniel to the king. He said, “I found a man among Judah’s exiles who can explain the dream.” 👑
## Daniel 2:26
The king asked Daniel, also called Belteshazzar, “Can you tell me my dream and its meaning?” 💬
## Daniel 2:27
Daniel answered, “No wise man, enchanter, medium, or magician can explain the mystery you ask about. ⚠️
## Daniel 2:28
But God in heaven reveals mysteries. He showed Nebuchadnezzar what will happen in the last days. These were your dream and visions as you lay in bed. 🌙
## Daniel 2:29
King, you thought about the future in bed. The Revealer of Mysteries showed you what will happen. 💭
## Daniel 2:30
This mystery was not revealed because I am wiser than everyone alive. It was revealed so that you could know its meaning and understand your thoughts. 📖
## Daniel 2:31
King, you saw a great, dazzling statue before you. Its appearance was awesome. ✨
## Daniel 2:32
Its head was pure gold, its chest and arms silver, and its belly and thighs bronze. 🗿
## Daniel 2:33
Its legs were iron, and its feet were part iron and part clay. 🗿
## Daniel 2:34
You watched a stone cut out without human hands. It struck and crushed the statue’s iron and clay feet. 🪨
## Daniel 2:35
The iron, clay, bronze, silver, and gold broke into pieces like chaff on a summer threshing floor. Wind carried them away without a trace. The stone became a great mountain that filled the whole earth. ⛰️
## Daniel 2:36
This was your dream. Now we will explain it to you. 💬
## Daniel 2:37
King, you are king of kings. Heaven’s God gave you rule, power, strength, and glory. 👑
## Daniel 2:38
He gave you authority over people, wild animals, and birds wherever they live. You are the gold head. 🗿
## Daniel 2:39
Another kingdom, less than yours, will follow. A third kingdom of bronze will then rule the whole earth. 👑
## Daniel 2:40
A fourth kingdom will be strong as iron. Like iron that shatters and crushes everything, it will shatter and crush all the others. ⚔️
## Daniel 2:41
The feet and toes of iron and fired clay show a divided kingdom. It will have some iron strength, just as you saw iron mixed with clay. 🗿
## Daniel 2:42
The toes of iron and clay show that the kingdom will be partly strong and partly fragile. 🗿
## Daniel 2:43
The mixed iron and clay show peoples who mix but do not hold together. Iron does not mix with clay. 💭
## Daniel 2:44
During those kings’ days, heaven’s God will establish a kingdom that will never be destroyed or given to another people. It will crush and end all these kingdoms, but it will stand forever. 👑
## Daniel 2:45
You saw a stone cut from a mountain without human hands. It shattered iron, bronze, clay, silver, and gold. The great God showed you the future. The dream is true, and its meaning is trustworthy.” 🪨
## Daniel 2:46
Nebuchadnezzar fell facedown and honored Daniel. He ordered an offering and incense presented to him. 🙏
## Daniel 2:47
He said, “Your God truly is God of gods, Lord of kings, and Revealer of Mysteries. You were able to reveal this mystery.” ✨
## Daniel 2:48
He promoted Daniel and gave him generous gifts. He made him ruler of Babylon’s whole province and chief administrator over all its wise men. 👑
## Daniel 2:49
At Daniel’s request, the king appointed Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to manage Babylon’s province. Daniel remained in the royal court. 👥
