## Daniel 4:1
“From King Nebuchadnezzar to all peoples, nations, and languages throughout the earth. May your prosperity increase! 👑
## Daniel 4:2
I gladly announce the signs and wonders the Most High God performed for me. ✨
## Daniel 4:3
His signs are great, and His wonders are mighty! His kingdom is eternal, and His rule lasts through every generation. 👑
## Daniel 4:4
I, Nebuchadnezzar, rested at ease in my house and prospered in my palace. 🏰
## Daniel 4:5
A dream frightened me. The images and visions in my mind alarmed me as I lay in bed. 🌙
## Daniel 4:6
I ordered all Babylon’s wise men brought to explain the dream. 📣
## Daniel 4:7
The magicians, enchanters, astrologers, and diviners came. I told them the dream, but they could not explain it. 💭
## Daniel 4:8
Finally, Daniel came, named Belteshazzar after my god. The spirit of the holy gods is in him. I told him my dream. 💬
## Daniel 4:9
‘Belteshazzar, chief magician, I know the spirit of the holy gods is in you. No mystery confuses you. Explain my dream’s visions and meaning. 💭
## Daniel 4:10
As I lay in bed, I saw a very tall tree in the middle of the land. 🌳
## Daniel 4:11
It grew large and strong. Its top reached the sky, and everyone to the earth’s ends could see it. 🌳
## Daniel 4:12
Its leaves were beautiful, and its fruit was plentiful, with food for everyone. Animals sheltered under it, birds nested in its branches, and every creature fed from it. 🐦
## Daniel 4:13
I saw a watcher, a holy one, come down from heaven in my visions. ✨
## Daniel 4:14
He called loudly, “Cut down the tree and its branches. Strip its leaves and scatter its fruit. Let animals flee from beneath it and birds from its branches. 🌳
## Daniel 4:15
Leave its stump and roots in the ground, with an iron and bronze band around it, in the field’s tender grass. Let heaven’s dew soak him, and let him feed with animals on the earth’s grass. 💧
## Daniel 4:16
Change his human mind to an animal’s mind until seven times pass. ⏳
## Daniel 4:17
The watchers made this decision, and the holy ones declared this judgment. The living must know that the Most High rules human kingdoms. He gives rule to anyone He wishes, even the lowliest people.” 👑
## Daniel 4:18
This was my dream, Belteshazzar. None of my wise men can explain it. You can, because the spirit of the holy gods is in you.’ 💬
## Daniel 4:19
Daniel, also called Belteshazzar, was troubled and alarmed for a time. I told him, ‘Do not be alarmed by the dream or its meaning.’ He replied, ‘My lord, may the dream and its meaning be for those who hate you, your enemies! 💭
## Daniel 4:20
You saw a large, strong tree that reached the sky and was visible to all the earth. 🌳
## Daniel 4:21
It had beautiful leaves and abundant fruit, with food for everyone. Animals lived beneath it, and birds nested in its branches. 🐦
## Daniel 4:22
King, you are that tree! You became great and strong. Your greatness reaches the sky, and your rule extends to the earth’s ends. 👑
## Daniel 4:23
You saw a holy watcher come from heaven. He said, “Destroy the tree, but leave its stump and roots, with an iron and bronze band, in the field’s tender grass. Let heaven’s dew soak him. Let him feed with animals until seven times pass.” 🌳
## Daniel 4:24
This is the meaning and the Most High’s judgment against you, my lord the king. ⚖️
## Daniel 4:25
You will be driven from people to live with animals. You will eat grass like an ox, and heaven’s dew will soak you. Seven times will pass until you acknowledge the Most High’s rule over human kingdoms. He gives them to anyone He wishes. ⏳
## Daniel 4:26
The stump and roots were left because your kingdom will return when you acknowledge that Heaven rules. 🌳
## Daniel 4:27
King, please accept my advice. Stop sinning and do right. Turn from evil and show mercy to oppressed people. Perhaps your prosperity will last longer.’” 🙏
## Daniel 4:28
All this happened to Nebuchadnezzar. ⚖️
## Daniel 4:29
Twelve months later, he walked on the roof of Babylon’s royal palace. 🏰
## Daniel 4:30
He exclaimed, “Is this not great Babylon? I built it as a royal home through my power and for my majesty’s glory!” 👑
## Daniel 4:31
While he still spoke, a voice came from heaven. “Nebuchadnezzar, this is the judgment: Your kingdom has left you. ⚖️
## Daniel 4:32
You will be driven from people to live with animals and eat grass like an ox. Seven times will pass until you acknowledge the Most High’s rule over human kingdoms. He gives them to anyone He wishes.” ⏳
## Daniel 4:33
The judgment happened immediately. Nebuchadnezzar was driven from people and ate grass like an ox. Dew soaked his body until his hair grew like eagle feathers and his nails like birds’ claws. 💧
## Daniel 4:34
“At the end of those days, I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked to heaven, and my sanity returned. I praised, honored, and glorified the One who lives forever. His rule lasts forever, and His kingdom lasts through every generation. 🙏
## Daniel 4:35
The earth’s peoples count as nothing. He does as He pleases with heaven’s army and earth’s peoples. No one can restrain His hand or ask, ‘What have You done?’ ✨
## Daniel 4:36
When my sanity returned, my royal honor and splendor returned too. My advisers and nobles sought me, and I regained my throne with even greater power. 👑
## Daniel 4:37
I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise, exalt, and glorify heaven’s King. All His works are true, and His ways are just. He can humble those who live proudly.” 🙏
