## Daniel 10:1
In Cyrus king of Persia’s third year, a message was revealed to Daniel, also called Belteshazzar. It was true and concerned a great conflict. A vision gave him understanding. 👀
## Daniel 10:2
I, Daniel, mourned for three full weeks. 😢
## Daniel 10:3
I ate no rich food or meat, drank no wine, and used no oil on myself until those three weeks ended. 🙏
## Daniel 10:4
On the first month’s twenty-fourth day, I stood beside the great Tigris River. 🌊
## Daniel 10:5
I looked up and saw a man in linen, with a belt of fine gold from Uphaz around his waist. ✨
## Daniel 10:6
His body was like beryl, his face like lightning, and his eyes like burning torches. His arms and legs gleamed like polished bronze. His voice sounded like a crowd. ✨
## Daniel 10:7
Only I saw the vision. The men with me saw nothing, but great fear struck them, and they ran and hid. 😨
## Daniel 10:8
I was alone before the great vision. My strength left, my face became deathly pale, and I was powerless. 💔
## Daniel 10:9
I heard his words and fell deeply asleep, facedown on the ground. 🌙
## Daniel 10:10
A hand touched me and raised me trembling onto my hands and knees. 🛡️
## Daniel 10:11
He said, “Daniel, you are highly precious. Consider my words carefully and stand, because I was sent to you.” I stood trembling. 💬
## Daniel 10:12
He said, “Do not fear, Daniel. Your words were heard from the first day you sought understanding and humbled yourself before your God. I came in response. 🙏
## Daniel 10:13
Persia’s prince opposed me for twenty-one days. Michael, one of the chief princes, helped me, because I had been left with Persia’s kings. ⚔️
## Daniel 10:14
I came to explain your people’s future in the last days. The vision concerns those days.” ⏳
## Daniel 10:15
As he spoke, I looked at the ground and could not speak. 💭
## Daniel 10:16
Someone who looked like a man touched my lips. I said to the one before me, “My lord, the vision fills me with anguish. I have no strength. 💔
## Daniel 10:17
How can your servant speak to you? I have no strength or breath left.” 💬
## Daniel 10:18
The one who looked like a man touched me again and strengthened me. 💪
## Daniel 10:19
He said, “Do not fear, highly precious one. Peace to you! Be strong, very strong!” His words strengthened me, and I said, “Speak, my lord. You strengthened me.” 🕊️
## Daniel 10:20
He said, “Do you know why I came? I must return at once to fight Persia’s prince. When I go, Greece’s prince will come. ⚔️
## Daniel 10:21
First, I will tell you what the Book of Truth records. Only Michael, your prince, has courage to support me against these.” 📖
