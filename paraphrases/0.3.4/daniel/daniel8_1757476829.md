## Daniel 8:1
In King Belshazzar’s third year, I, Daniel, saw another vision after my earlier one. 👀
## Daniel 8:2
In the vision, I was in Susa’s fortress in Elam’s province, beside the Ulai Canal. 🏰
## Daniel 8:3
I saw a ram beside the canal with two long horns. One was longer and grew later than the other. 🐏
## Daniel 8:4
The ram charged west, north, and south. No animal could resist him, and no one could rescue from his power. He did what he pleased and became great. 🐏
## Daniel 8:5
As I considered this, a goat came from the west across the whole earth without touching the ground. He had a prominent horn between his eyes. 🐐
## Daniel 8:6
He rushed furiously at the ram with two horns beside the canal. ⚔️
## Daniel 8:7
He struck the ram in rage and broke its two horns. The ram could not resist. The goat threw him down and trampled him, and no one could rescue him. ⚠️
## Daniel 8:8
The goat became very great. At his greatest power, his large horn broke. Four prominent horns replaced it, pointing toward heaven’s four winds. 🐐
## Daniel 8:9
A small horn came from one of these horns. It grew greatly south, east, and toward the Beautiful Land. 👀
## Daniel 8:10
It grew as high as heaven’s army. It threw some of the army and stars to the earth and trampled them. ⭐
## Daniel 8:11
It made itself great, even to the army’s Prince. It removed His daily sacrifice and overthrew His holy place. ⛔
## Daniel 8:12
In the rebellion, the army and daily sacrifice were handed to the horn. It threw truth to the ground and prospered in everything it did. ⚠️
## Daniel 8:13
I heard one holy one speak, and another asked, “How long will this vision take to be fulfilled? It concerns the daily sacrifice, the rebellion that brings ruin, and the holy place and army handed over for trampling.” 💬
## Daniel 8:14
He told me, “It will take 2,300 evenings and mornings. Then the holy place will be properly restored.” ⏳
## Daniel 8:15
I watched and tried to understand. Someone who looked like a man stood before me. 👀
## Daniel 8:16
A man’s voice called from between the Ulai’s banks, “Gabriel, explain this vision to him.” 📣
## Daniel 8:17
Gabriel approached, and I fell facedown in fear. He said, “Son of man, understand that this vision concerns the time of the end.” ⚠️
## Daniel 8:18
While he spoke, I fell deeply asleep, facedown on the ground. He touched me and helped me stand. 🛡️
## Daniel 8:19
He said, “I will show you what happens in the later time of anger. This concerns the appointed time of the end. ⏳
## Daniel 8:20
The ram with two horns represents Media’s and Persia’s kings. 🐏
## Daniel 8:21
The shaggy goat represents Greece’s king. Its large horn is the first king. 🐐
## Daniel 8:22
The four horns that replaced it represent four kingdoms from that nation. They will not have the same power. 👑
## Daniel 8:23
Late in their reign, rebellion will reach its full measure. An arrogant king skilled in secret plots will take the throne. ⚠️
## Daniel 8:24
His power will be great, but not his own. He will bring terrible destruction and succeed in everything he does. He will destroy mighty men and the holy people. ⚔️
## Daniel 8:25
His skill and actions will make deceit prosper, and he will think himself great. He will destroy many during peace and oppose the Prince of princes. He will be broken, but not by human hands. ⚖️
## Daniel 8:26
The vision of evenings and mornings is true. Seal it up, because it concerns the distant future.” 📜
## Daniel 8:27
I was exhausted and ill for days. Then I rose and returned to the king’s business. The vision confused me and was beyond understanding. 💭
