## Daniel 6:1
Darius appointed 120 satraps to rule throughout the kingdom. 👥
## Daniel 6:2
Three administrators, including Daniel, supervised them. The satraps answered to these men so the king would suffer no loss. 👥
## Daniel 6:3
Daniel’s extraordinary spirit made him stand out among the administrators and satraps. The king planned to put him over the whole kingdom. 👑
## Daniel 6:4
The administrators and satraps sought a charge against Daniel’s public service. They found no corruption or neglect because he was trustworthy. ⚖️
## Daniel 6:5
They said, “We can find a charge against Daniel only through his God’s law.” ⚠️
## Daniel 6:6
The administrators and satraps went together to the king. They said, “Darius, live forever! 👑
## Daniel 6:7
All administrators, prefects, satraps, advisers, and governors agree on this order. For thirty days, anyone who petitions any god or person except you must be thrown into the lions’ den. ⚠️
## Daniel 6:8
Sign this order so it cannot change. The Medes’ and Persians’ law cannot be canceled.” 📜
## Daniel 6:9
Darius signed the written order. ✍️
## Daniel 6:10
Daniel heard it was signed and went home to his upstairs room, whose windows opened toward Jerusalem. Three times daily, he knelt, prayed, and thanked God as before. 🙏
## Daniel 6:11
The men came together and found Daniel pleading and asking his God for help. 👀
## Daniel 6:12
They asked, “Did you not sign this order for thirty days? Anyone who petitions any god or person except you must be thrown into the lions’ den.” The king replied, “The Medes’ and Persians’ law makes the order stand. It cannot be canceled.” 📜
## Daniel 6:13
They said, “Daniel, Judah’s exile, ignores you and your signed order. He still prays three times daily.” ⚠️
## Daniel 6:14
The king became deeply distressed. He worked until sunset to find a way to rescue Daniel. 🌅
## Daniel 6:15
The men returned together. They said, “Remember, the Medes’ and Persians’ law forbids changing any order the king establishes.” 📜
## Daniel 6:16
The king ordered Daniel brought and thrown into the lions’ den. He told Daniel, “May your God, whom you always serve, rescue you!” 🦁
## Daniel 6:17
A stone covered the den’s opening. The king sealed it with his ring and his nobles’ rings, so Daniel’s situation could not change. 🪨
## Daniel 6:18
He returned to the palace and fasted that night. He had no entertainment and could not sleep. 🌙
## Daniel 6:19
At dawn, he hurried to the lions’ den. 🌅
## Daniel 6:20
He cried in distress, “Daniel, servant of the living God! Has the God you always serve rescued you from the lions?” 🦁
## Daniel 6:21
Daniel replied, “King, live forever! 👑
## Daniel 6:22
My God sent His angel and shut the lions’ mouths. They did not hurt me because He found me innocent. I also did no wrong against you.” 🛡️
## Daniel 6:23
The king rejoiced and ordered Daniel lifted out. They found no wound on him because he trusted his God. ❤️
## Daniel 6:24
The king ordered Daniel’s false accusers, their children, and their wives thrown into the den. Before they reached the bottom, the lions overpowered them and crushed all their bones. ⚖️
## Daniel 6:25
Darius wrote to all peoples, nations, and languages throughout the land. “May your prosperity increase! ✉️
## Daniel 6:26
I order everyone in my kingdom to tremble before Daniel’s God. He is the living God and lasts forever. His kingdom will never be destroyed, and His rule will never end. 👑
## Daniel 6:27
He rescues and saves, and performs signs and wonders in heaven and on earth. He rescued Daniel from the lions’ power.” 🛡️
## Daniel 6:28
Daniel prospered during Darius’s reign and Cyrus the Persian’s reign. 🌅
