## Daniel 5:1
King Belshazzar held a great feast for a thousand nobles and drank wine with them. 🍷
## Daniel 5:2
Under wine’s influence, he ordered the gold and silver vessels his father Nebuchadnezzar took from Jerusalem’s temple. He, his nobles, wives, and concubines would drink from them. 🏺
## Daniel 5:3
They brought the gold vessels from God’s temple in Jerusalem. The king, his nobles, wives, and concubines drank from them. 🍷
## Daniel 5:4
They drank wine and praised gods of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone. ⛔
## Daniel 5:5
Suddenly, human fingers appeared and wrote on the palace wall’s plaster near the lampstand. The king watched the hand write. ✍️
## Daniel 5:6
His face turned pale, his thoughts terrified him, his hips gave way, and his knees knocked together. 😨
## Daniel 5:7
He called the enchanters, astrologers, and diviners. He told Babylon’s wise men, “Anyone who reads and explains this writing will wear purple and a gold chain. He will become the kingdom’s third highest ruler.” 👑
## Daniel 5:8
All his wise men came, but they could not read or explain the writing. 💭
## Daniel 5:9
Belshazzar grew more terrified and pale, and his nobles were confused. 😨
## Daniel 5:10
The queen heard their cries and entered the feast hall. She said, “King, live forever! Do not be terrified or pale. 👑
## Daniel 5:11
A man in your kingdom has the spirit of the holy gods. In your father’s days, he showed insight, intelligence, and wisdom like the gods. Your father Nebuchadnezzar appointed him chief of the magicians, enchanters, astrologers, and diviners. 📖
## Daniel 5:12
Daniel, whom he named Belteshazzar, has an extraordinary spirit, knowledge, and understanding. He explains dreams and riddles and solves difficult problems. Call him, and he will explain this writing.” 💬
## Daniel 5:13
Daniel was brought before the king. The king asked, “Are you Daniel, one of the exiles my father brought from Judah? 💬
## Daniel 5:14
I heard the spirit of the gods is in you. You have insight, intelligence, and extraordinary wisdom. 💭
## Daniel 5:15
The wise men and enchanters came to read and explain this writing, but they could not. ⚠️
## Daniel 5:16
I heard you can explain things and solve difficult problems. If you read and explain this writing, you will wear purple and a gold chain. You will become the kingdom’s third highest ruler.” 👑
## Daniel 5:17
Daniel replied, “Keep your gifts and give your rewards to someone else. I will still read and explain the writing. 💬
## Daniel 5:18
King, the Most High God gave your father Nebuchadnezzar rule, greatness, glory, and honor. 👑
## Daniel 5:19
All peoples, nations, and languages feared him because of that greatness. He killed or spared anyone he wished. He raised or humbled anyone he wished. ⚖️
## Daniel 5:20
His heart became arrogant, and pride hardened his spirit. He lost his throne and glory. ⚠️
## Daniel 5:21
He was driven from people, and his mind became like an animal’s. He lived with wild donkeys, ate grass like an ox, and was soaked with dew. This lasted until he acknowledged the Most High God’s rule over human kingdoms. God appoints anyone He wishes. 💧
## Daniel 5:22
You, his son Belshazzar, knew all this but did not humble your heart. ⚠️
## Daniel 5:23
You raised yourself against heaven’s Lord and drank from His vessels with your nobles, wives, and concubines. You praised gods of silver, gold, bronze, iron, wood, and stone, which cannot see, hear, or understand. You did not glorify God, who holds your breath and all your ways in His hand. ⛔
## Daniel 5:24
So He sent the hand that wrote this message. ✍️
## Daniel 5:25
The writing is MENE, MENE, TEKEL, PARSIN. 📜
## Daniel 5:26
MENE means God numbered your reign’s days and ended it. ⚖️
## Daniel 5:27
TEKEL means you were weighed on scales and found lacking. ⚖️
## Daniel 5:28
PERES means your kingdom is divided and given to the Medes and Persians.” 👑
## Daniel 5:29
Belshazzar ordered Daniel dressed in purple with a gold chain around his neck. He announced him as the kingdom’s third highest ruler. 👑
## Daniel 5:30
That night, Belshazzar king of the Chaldeans was killed. ⚔️
## Daniel 5:31
Darius the Mede received the kingdom at sixty-two years old. 👑
