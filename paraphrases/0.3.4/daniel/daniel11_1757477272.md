## Daniel 11:1
“In Darius the Mede’s first year, I stood to strengthen and protect him. 🛡️
## Daniel 11:2
I will tell you the truth. Three more kings will rise in Persia, then a fourth richer than all the others. His wealth will give him power to stir everyone against Greece. 👑
## Daniel 11:3
A mighty king will rise, rule with great authority, and do as he pleases. 👑
## Daniel 11:4
Once he is established, his kingdom will break apart toward heaven’s four winds. It will go to others, rather than his descendants. It will not have his authority, because his kingdom will be uprooted. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:5
The king of the South will grow strong. One of his commanders will grow stronger and rule his own kingdom with great authority. 👑
## Daniel 11:6
After years, they will form an alliance. The daughter of the king of the South will go to the king of the North to confirm it. She will not keep her power, and his strength will not last. She, her royal escort, her father, and her supporter will be given up then. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:7
One from her family line will rise in his place. He will attack the army of the king of the North and enter his fortress, fighting and prevailing. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:8
He will take their gods to Egypt, with their metal images and precious silver and gold vessels. For years, he will stay away from the king of the North. 🏺
## Daniel 11:9
The king of the North will invade the realm of the king of the South, then return home. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:10
His sons will stir conflict and gather a great army. It will advance forcefully like a flood and carry battle to his fortress again. 🌊
## Daniel 11:11
The angry king of the South will fight the king of the North. The king of the North will raise a large army, but his enemy will receive power over it. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:12
When the army is taken, the king of the South will become proud. He will cast down tens of thousands but will not triumph. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:13
The king of the North will raise a larger army. After some years, he will advance with a great army and many supplies. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:14
Many will rise against the king of the South then. Violent people among your own people will raise themselves to fulfill the vision, but they will fail. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:15
The king of the North will build a siege ramp and capture a defended city. The South’s forces cannot stand, not even their best troops. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:16
The invader will do as he pleases, with no one to resist. He will establish himself in the Beautiful Land, holding destruction in his hand. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:17
He will plan to come with his whole kingdom’s strength and make an agreement with the king of the South. He will give a daughter in marriage to overthrow the kingdom. His plan will not succeed or help him. 💬
## Daniel 11:18
He will turn to the coastlands and capture many. A commander will end his insults and turn them back on him. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:19
He will return toward his own fortresses, but stumble, fall, and be no more. ⚖️
## Daniel 11:20
His successor will send a tax collector for the kingdom’s glory. Within a few days, he will be destroyed, though not in anger or battle. 👑
## Daniel 11:21
A despicable person will replace him without receiving royal honors. He will come during peace and seize the kingdom through secret plots. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:22
A flood of forces will be swept away and destroyed before him, with a prince of the covenant. 🌊
## Daniel 11:23
After an alliance with him, he will act deceitfully. He will gain power with only a few people. ⛔
## Daniel 11:24
During peace, he will invade the richest provinces and do what his ancestors never did. He will lavish stolen goods, plunder, and wealth on followers. He will plan against strongholds, but only for a time. 💰
## Daniel 11:25
With a large army, he will stir his power and courage against the king of the South. That king will gather a very large, powerful army but cannot resist the plots against him. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:26
Those who eat his provisions will seek to destroy him. His army will be swept away, and many will be killed. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:27
Both kings will plan evil and lie at one table, but achieve nothing. The end will still come at the appointed time. ⏳
## Daniel 11:28
The king of the North will return with great wealth. His heart will oppose the holy covenant. He will cause harm and return to his land. ⛔
## Daniel 11:29
At the appointed time, he will invade the South again. This will differ from the first time. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:30
Kittim’s ships will oppose him, and he will lose courage. He will return and rage against the holy covenant, causing harm. He will favor those who abandon it. 🚢
## Daniel 11:31
His forces will rise and make the temple fortress unholy. They will end the daily sacrifice and set up the abomination that brings ruin. ⛔
## Daniel 11:32
He will corrupt covenant breakers with flattery. But those who know their God will resist firmly. 🛡️
## Daniel 11:33
Those with insight will teach many. For a time, they will fall by sword or fire, or be captured or plundered. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:34
When they fall, they will receive a little help. Many will join them without sincerity. 🤝
## Daniel 11:35
Some wise people will fall so they can be refined, purified, and made spotless until the end. The end still comes at its appointed time. ⏳
## Daniel 11:36
The king will do as he pleases and raise himself above every god. He will speak monstrous things against the God of gods. He will succeed until the time of anger ends. What is decreed must happen. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:37
He will disregard his ancestors’ gods, the one desired by women, and every other god. He will raise himself above all of them. ⛔
## Daniel 11:38
Instead, he will honor a god of fortresses unknown to his ancestors. He will offer gold, silver, precious stones, and riches. 💎
## Daniel 11:39
He will attack the strongest fortresses with a foreign god’s help. He will honor those who acknowledge him, making them rulers over many. He will divide land for a price. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:40
At the end, the king of the South will fight him. The king of the North will storm against him with chariots, riders, and many ships. He will invade many lands like a flood. 🌊
## Daniel 11:41
He will invade the Beautiful Land, and many countries will fall. Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites’ leaders will escape his hand. 🛡️
## Daniel 11:42
His power will reach many countries. Even Egypt will not escape. ⚔️
## Daniel 11:43
He will control Egypt’s gold, silver, and all its riches. Libyans and Cushites will submit to him. 💰
## Daniel 11:44
News from the east and north will alarm him. He will go out furiously to destroy many and devote them to destruction. ⚠️
## Daniel 11:45
He will place his royal tents between the sea and the beautiful holy mountain. His end will come, with no one to help.” ⚖️
