## Daniel 12:1
“Michael, the great prince who guards your people, will rise then. Distress unlike anything since nations began will come. Your people, everyone whose name is written in the book, will be rescued. 🛡️
## Daniel 12:2
Many who sleep in earth’s dust will awake. Some will receive everlasting life, and others shame and everlasting contempt. 🌅
## Daniel 12:3
Wise people will shine like heaven’s brightness. Those who lead many to righteousness will shine like stars forever and ever. ⭐
## Daniel 12:4
Daniel, close these words and seal the book until the end’s time. Many will roam back and forth, and knowledge will grow.” 📖
## Daniel 12:5
I saw two others, one on each bank of the river. 👀
## Daniel 12:6
One asked the man in linen above the river, “How long until these wonders are fulfilled?” 💬
## Daniel 12:7
The man raised his right and left hands to heaven and swore by the One who lives forever. He said, “A time, times, and half a time. All will be completed when the holy people’s power is finally shattered.” ⏳
## Daniel 12:8
I heard but did not understand. I asked, “My lord, what will be the outcome?” 💭
## Daniel 12:9
He replied, “Go your way, Daniel. These words are closed and sealed until the time of the end. 📜
## Daniel 12:10
Many will be purified, made spotless, and refined. Wicked people will continue doing evil and will not understand. Wise people will understand. ✨
## Daniel 12:11
From the removal of the daily sacrifice and the setting up of the abomination that brings ruin, there will be 1,290 days. 📅
## Daniel 12:12
The person who waits and reaches the end of the 1,335 days is blessed. 🌅
## Daniel 12:13
Go your way until the end. You will rest, then rise to receive your inheritance at the end of the days.” 🙏
