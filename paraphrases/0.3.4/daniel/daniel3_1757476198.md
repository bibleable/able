## Daniel 3:1
Nebuchadnezzar made a gold statue sixty cubits high and six cubits wide. He placed it on Dura’s plain in Babylon’s province. 🗿
## Daniel 3:2
He called the satraps, prefects, governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, magistrates, and all other provincial officials. They were to attend the statue’s dedication. 👥
## Daniel 3:3
These satraps, prefects, governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, magistrates, and provincial rulers gathered for the dedication. They stood before Nebuchadnezzar’s statue. 🗿
## Daniel 3:4
A herald called loudly, “People of every nation and language, you are commanded to do this. 📣
## Daniel 3:5
When you hear the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and all kinds of music, fall down and worship Nebuchadnezzar’s gold statue. 🎵
## Daniel 3:6
Anyone who refuses will immediately be thrown into the blazing furnace.” 🔥
## Daniel 3:7
The people heard the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, and all kinds of music. People of every nation and language fell down and worshiped Nebuchadnezzar’s gold statue. 🗿
## Daniel 3:8
Some astrologers came forward and maliciously accused the Jews. ⚠️
## Daniel 3:9
They told Nebuchadnezzar, “King, may you live forever! 👑
## Daniel 3:10
You ordered everyone to fall down and worship the gold statue when the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and all kinds of music sound. 🎵
## Daniel 3:11
Anyone who refuses must be thrown into the blazing furnace. 🔥
## Daniel 3:12
But Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego ignore you. You appointed these Jews to manage Babylon’s province, but they refuse to serve your gods or worship your gold statue.” ⚠️
## Daniel 3:13
Nebuchadnezzar furiously called Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. They were brought before him. 😠
## Daniel 3:14
He asked, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, is it true you refuse to serve my gods or worship my gold statue? 💬
## Daniel 3:15
When the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and all kinds of music sound, be ready to worship my statue. If you do, good. If you refuse, you will immediately be thrown into the blazing furnace. What god can then rescue you from my hands?” 🔥
## Daniel 3:16
They replied, “Nebuchadnezzar, we do not need to answer you about this. 💬
## Daniel 3:17
If the God we serve exists, He can rescue us from the blazing furnace and your hand, king. 🛡️
## Daniel 3:18
Even if He does not, know that we will not serve your gods or worship your gold statue.” 🙏
## Daniel 3:19
Nebuchadnezzar became furious, and his expression changed toward the three men. He ordered the furnace heated seven times hotter than usual. 😠
## Daniel 3:20
He ordered mighty warriors to tie up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego and throw them into the blazing furnace. ⚔️
## Daniel 3:21
They were tied in their robes, trousers, turbans, and other clothes and thrown into the furnace. 🔥
## Daniel 3:22
The order was urgent, and the furnace was so hot that flames killed the men carrying Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. 🔥
## Daniel 3:23
The three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell firmly tied into the blazing furnace. 🔥
## Daniel 3:24
Nebuchadnezzar suddenly jumped up in amazement. He asked, “Did we not throw three firmly tied men into the fire?” His advisers replied, “Yes, king.” 👀
## Daniel 3:25
He exclaimed, “Look! Four men walk freely and unharmed in the fire. The fourth looks like a son of the gods!” ✨
## Daniel 3:26
He approached the furnace door and called, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out!” They came out of the fire. 🛡️
## Daniel 3:27
The satraps, prefects, governors, and royal advisers gathered and saw no effect of fire on their bodies. No hair was burned, their robes were unchanged, and they did not smell of fire. 👀
## Daniel 3:28
Nebuchadnezzar declared, “Praise their God! He sent His angel and rescued Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, His servants who trusted Him. They disobeyed my command and risked their lives rather than serve or worship any god except their own. 🙏
## Daniel 3:29
I order that anyone of any nation or language who insults their God be cut apart, and his house made rubble. No other god can rescue like this.” ⚖️
## Daniel 3:30
The king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in Babylon’s province. 👑
