## Daniel 7:1
In Belshazzar’s first year as Babylon’s king, Daniel dreamed and saw visions in bed. He wrote the dream down. This is his account’s summary. 🌙
## Daniel 7:2
Daniel said, “At night, I saw heaven’s four winds stir up the great sea. 🌊
## Daniel 7:3
Four great beasts came from the sea, each different. 🐾
## Daniel 7:4
The first was like a lion with eagle’s wings. Its wings were torn off. It was lifted from the ground to stand on two feet like a man. It received a human mind. 🦁
## Daniel 7:5
Another beast appeared, like a bear raised on one side. It held three ribs between its teeth. It was told, ‘Get up and eat much flesh!’ 🐻
## Daniel 7:6
Another beast appeared, like a leopard with four birdlike wings on its back. It had four heads and received authority to rule. 🐆
## Daniel 7:7
A fourth beast appeared in my night vision, terrifying and extremely strong, with large iron teeth. It devoured, crushed, and trampled what remained. It differed from all the others and had ten horns. ⚠️
## Daniel 7:8
I considered the horns. A small horn rose among them, and three earlier horns were uprooted before it. It had humanlike eyes and a mouth that spoke proudly. 👀
## Daniel 7:9
Thrones were placed, and the Ancient of Days sat down. His clothes were white as snow, and His hair was like pure wool. His throne burned with fire, and its wheels blazed. 🔥
## Daniel 7:10
A river of fire flowed from His presence. Thousands upon thousands served Him. Tens of thousands upon tens of thousands stood before Him. The court sat, and the books opened. ⚖️
## Daniel 7:11
I watched because of the horn’s proud words. The beast was killed, and its body was destroyed and thrown into blazing fire. 🔥
## Daniel 7:12
The other beasts lost their rule. They were allowed to live longer, for a season and a time. ⏳
## Daniel 7:13
In my night vision, One like the Son of Man came with heaven’s clouds. He approached the Ancient of Days and was brought before Him. ☁️
## Daniel 7:14
He received rule, glory, and kingship, so all peoples, nations, and languages would serve Him. His rule lasts forever, and His kingdom will never be destroyed. 👑
## Daniel 7:15
My spirit grieved, and the visions alarmed me. 💔
## Daniel 7:16
I approached someone standing there and asked the true meaning. He explained it. 💬
## Daniel 7:17
‘These four great beasts are four kings who will rise from the earth. 👑
## Daniel 7:18
But the Most High’s holy people will receive the kingdom and possess it forever and ever.’ ✨
## Daniel 7:19
I wanted to understand the fourth beast, different and more terrifying than the others. Its iron teeth and bronze claws devoured and crushed, and it trampled what remained. ⚠️
## Daniel 7:20
I asked about its ten horns and the new horn before which three fell. This horn looked more imposing than the others. It had eyes and a mouth that spoke proudly. 👀
## Daniel 7:21
This horn fought against the holy people and was winning. ⚔️
## Daniel 7:22
This continued until the Ancient of Days came and judged for the Most High’s holy people. The time came for them to possess the kingdom. ⚖️
## Daniel 7:23
He said, ‘The fourth beast is a fourth kingdom, different from all others. It will devour the whole earth, trample it, and crush it. ⚠️
## Daniel 7:24
Its ten horns are ten kings who will rise from that kingdom. Another king will follow, different from them, and subdue three kings. 👑
## Daniel 7:25
He will speak against the Most High, oppress His holy people, and intend to change appointed times and laws. They will be handed to him for a time, times, and half a time. ⏳
## Daniel 7:26
The court will sit, remove his rule, and destroy it completely forever. ⚖️
## Daniel 7:27
The rule, authority, and greatness of kingdoms under all heaven will be given to the Most High’s holy people. His kingdom will last forever. All rulers will serve and obey Him.’ 👑
## Daniel 7:28
This ends the matter. My thoughts deeply troubled me, and my face turned pale. I kept it to myself.” 💭
