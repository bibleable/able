## Psalms 85:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song for the music leader. LORD, You showed kindness to Your land. You brought Jacob’s people back from captivity. 🎵
## Psalms 85:2
You forgave Your people’s wrong acts. You covered all their sins. *Song direction: Selah.* 🤲
## Psalms 85:3
You held back all Your fierce anger. You turned away from Your great anger. ❤️
## Psalms 85:4
God who saves us, restore us. Do not remain angry with us. 🙏
## Psalms 85:5
Will You be angry with us forever? Will Your anger last through all generations? ❓
## Psalms 85:6
Will You not give us new life again? Then Your people can be glad in You. ❤️😊
## Psalms 85:7
LORD, show us Your faithful love. Save us. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 85:8
I will listen to what God the LORD says. He will speak peace to His people, His holy ones. He will not let them return to foolish ways. 👂🤝
## Psalms 85:9
His rescue is surely near to people who fear Him. Then His glory can live in our land. ✨
## Psalms 85:10
Faithful love and truth meet together. Righteousness and peace kiss each other. 🤝
## Psalms 85:11
Faithfulness grows from the earth like a plant. Righteousness looks down from heaven. 🌱
## Psalms 85:12
The LORD will give what is good. Our land will produce its crops. 🌾
## Psalms 85:13
Righteousness will go ahead of Him. It will prepare a path for His steps. 🛤️
