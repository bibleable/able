## Psalms 83:1
Asaph wrote this song. God, do not stay silent. Do not hold back Your words or Your actions. 🙏
## Psalms 83:2
See how Your enemies rage. They raise their heads against You. 👀
## Psalms 83:3
They make clever, secret plans against Your people. They plan together against the people You love. 💭❌
## Psalms 83:4
They say, “Come, let us destroy them as a nation. Let no one remember the name of Israel again.” 🗣️
## Psalms 83:5
They agree in their plans. They join forces against You. 👥
## Psalms 83:6
They include the people in Edom’s tents and the Ishmaelites. They include Moab and the Hagrites. ⛺
## Psalms 83:7
They include Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek. They include Philistia and the people of Tyre. 👥
## Psalms 83:8
Even Assyria joins them. Assyria gives strength to Lot’s descendants. *Song direction: Selah.* 💪
## Psalms 83:9
Treat them as You treated Midian. Treat them as You treated Sisera and Jabin at the River Kishon. ⚖️
## Psalms 83:10
They died at Endor. They became like animal waste on the ground. ⚖️
## Psalms 83:11
Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeeb. Make all their rulers like Zebah and Zalmunna. ⚖️
## Psalms 83:12
Those rulers said, “Let us take God’s fields for ourselves.” 🌿
## Psalms 83:13
My God, make them like tumbleweed. Make them like dry grain husks in the wind. 🌬️
## Psalms 83:14
Fire burns up a forest. Flames set the mountains on fire. 🔥
## Psalms 83:15
Chase them like that with Your fierce wind. Fill them with fear through Your storm. ⛈️
## Psalms 83:16
LORD, cover their faces with shame. Then they can seek Your name. 🙏
## Psalms 83:17
Let shame and fear stay with them forever. Let them die in disgrace. ⚖️
## Psalms 83:18
Let them know that You alone are Most High over all the earth. Your name is the LORD. 🌍
