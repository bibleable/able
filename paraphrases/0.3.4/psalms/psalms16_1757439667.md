## Psalms 16:1
David wrote this song, called a Miktam. God, keep me safe. I come to You for protection. 🛡️🙏
## Psalms 16:2
I said to the LORD, “You are my Lord. Without You, I have nothing good.” ❤️
## Psalms 16:3
The holy people in the land are wonderful. They give me great joy. 👥😊
## Psalms 16:4
People who follow other gods will have more and more sorrow. I will not pour out blood as gifts to those gods. I will not speak their names. ❌
## Psalms 16:5
LORD, You are my share and my cup. You keep my future safe. 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 16:6
The land within my boundaries is pleasant. The share You give me is wonderful. 🌳🎁
## Psalms 16:7
I will praise the LORD who gives me advice. Even at night, my thoughts teach me. 🙌🌙
## Psalms 16:8
I keep the LORD before me always. He is at my right side. So I will stand firm. 🛡️
## Psalms 16:9
That makes my heart glad. I speak with joy. My body will also rest in safety. 😊🛌
## Psalms 16:10
You will not leave me in the place of the dead. You will not let the body of Your Holy One rot. ❤️
## Psalms 16:11
You show me the path of life. With You, I have complete joy. At Your right side, there is pleasure forever. 🛤️😊
