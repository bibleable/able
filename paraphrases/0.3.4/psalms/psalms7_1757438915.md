## Psalms 7:1
David sang this song to the LORD about the words of Cush, a man from Benjamin. The song is a Shiggaion. LORD my God, I come to You for safety. Save me from everyone who chases me. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 7:2
Otherwise, they will tear me apart like a lion. No one will rescue me. 🦁🆘
## Psalms 7:3
LORD my God, suppose I did what they accuse me of. Suppose I treated someone unfairly. 💭⚖️
## Psalms 7:4
Suppose I hurt a friend who helped me. Suppose I robbed an enemy without a reason. 💭
## Psalms 7:5
If I did those things, let my enemy chase me and catch me. Let him crush me on the ground. Let him leave my honor in the dust. *Song direction: Selah.* ⚖️
## Psalms 7:6
LORD, act in Your anger! Stand against my angry enemies. My God, act now and order a fair judgment. ⚖️🆘
## Psalms 7:7
Let the people gather around You. Take Your seat high above them. 👑👥
## Psalms 7:8
The LORD judges the people. LORD, show that I do right. Judge me by my honest life. ⚖️
## Psalms 7:9
Stop the evil that bad people do. Make people who do right secure. God, You are fair. You examine hearts and minds. 🛡️💭
## Psalms 7:10
God protects me like a shield. He saves people whose hearts want to do right. 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 7:11
God is a fair judge. He is angry with evil every day. ⚖️
## Psalms 7:12
If a person does not turn away from sin, God will sharpen His sword. He bends His bow and makes it ready. ⚔️🏹
## Psalms 7:13
He prepares weapons that kill. He makes His arrows burn with fire. 🏹🔥
## Psalms 7:14
The person who does evil is like a mother who gives birth. He forms evil plans inside himself. He brings trouble and lies into the world. 💭❌
## Psalms 7:15
He digs a deep hole. Then he falls into his own hole. 🕳️
## Psalms 7:16
The trouble he causes comes back on him. His own violence hurts him. ⚖️
## Psalms 7:17
I will thank the LORD because He does right. I will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High. 🙌🎵
