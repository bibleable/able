## Psalms 26:1
David wrote this song. LORD, show that I am right. I live honestly. I trust in the LORD without doubt. ⚖️🙏
## Psalms 26:2
LORD, test me and examine me. Look closely at my heart and mind. 🔍💭
## Psalms 26:3
I keep Your faithful love before my eyes. I live by Your truth. ❤️✅
## Psalms 26:4
I do not sit with people who deceive others. I do not join people who pretend to be good. ❌
## Psalms 26:5
I hate the crowd of people who do evil. I refuse to sit with them. ✋
## Psalms 26:6
I wash my hands to show that I am innocent. Then I walk around Your altar, LORD. An altar is a place for sacrifices to God. 🤲
## Psalms 26:7
I raise my voice to thank You. I tell about all Your wonderful acts. 🙌📣
## Psalms 26:8
LORD, I love the house where You live. I love the place where Your glory is present. 🏠❤️
## Psalms 26:9
Do not take my life away with people who sin. Do not let me die with people who murder. 🆘
## Psalms 26:10
Their hands carry out evil plans. Their right hands accept money to do wrong. 💰❌
## Psalms 26:11
But I will live honestly. Rescue me and be kind to me. ✅🙏
## Psalms 26:12
My feet stand on level ground. I will praise the LORD when the people gather. 🧍🙌
