## Psalms 63:1
David wrote this song in the Desert of Judah. God, You are my God. I seek You with all my heart. I thirst for You. My body longs for You in a dry, tired land without water. 🏜️💧
## Psalms 63:2
I saw You in Your holy place. I saw Your power and glory. ✨
## Psalms 63:3
Your faithful love is better than life. So my lips will praise You. ❤️🙌
## Psalms 63:4
I will praise You as long as I live. I will lift my hands in Your name. 🤲
## Psalms 63:5
You satisfy me like the richest food. With joy, I will speak Your praise. 🍽️😊
## Psalms 63:6
I remember You in my bed. I think about You through the hours of the night. 🛌🌙
## Psalms 63:7
You help me. I will sing with joy in the shadow of Your wings. 🎵🪽
## Psalms 63:8
I hold firmly to You. Your right hand supports me. 🤝
## Psalms 63:9
But people who want to destroy my life will go deep into the earth. ⚖️
## Psalms 63:10
They will die by the sword. Foxes will eat their bodies. ⚔️
## Psalms 63:11
But the king will be glad in God. Everyone who makes promises in God’s name will celebrate. People who tell lies will fall silent. 👑😊
