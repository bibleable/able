## Psalms 127:1
This is a song of ascent of Solomon. Without the LORD’s work, builders work for nothing on a house. Without the LORD’s protection, guards watch a city for nothing. 🏠🛡️
## Psalms 127:2
You rise early and stay up late to work hard for food. Without His help, this is useless. He gives sleep to people He loves. 🌅🛌
## Psalms 127:3
Children are a share given by the LORD. Babies are His reward. 👶🎁
## Psalms 127:4
Children born during a person’s youth are like arrows in a soldier’s hand. 🏹
## Psalms 127:5
The man whose arrow bag is full of them receives good from God. He will not face shame when he meets enemies at the city gate. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦🛡️
