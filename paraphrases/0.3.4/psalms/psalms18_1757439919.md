## Psalms 18:1
David, the LORD’s servant, wrote this song for the music leader. He sang it when the LORD rescued him from all his enemies and from Saul. He said, “LORD, You are my strength. I love You.” 🎵❤️
## Psalms 18:2
The LORD is my rock, my fort, and my rescuer. My God is a rock where I find safety. He is my shield and my strong protection. He is the power that saves me. 🪨🛡️
## Psalms 18:3
I will call to the LORD, who deserves praise. He will save me from my enemies. 🙏🙌
## Psalms 18:4
Death surrounded me like ropes. Destruction rushed over me like a flood. 🌊🆘
## Psalms 18:5
The place of the dead held me like ropes. Death set traps in front of me. 🆘
## Psalms 18:6
In my trouble, I called to the LORD. I cried to my God for help. He heard my voice from His temple. My cry reached His ears. 🙏👂
## Psalms 18:7
Then the earth shook. Even the bases of the mountains shook. They shook because God was very angry. 🌍⛰️
## Psalms 18:8
Smoke came from His nose. Fire from His mouth burned everything in its way. Hot coals blazed out. 🔥
## Psalms 18:9
He opened the heavens and came down. Dark clouds were under His feet. ☁️
## Psalms 18:10
He rode on a cherub, a heavenly being, and flew. He flew on the wings of the wind. 🪽🌬️
## Psalms 18:11
He hid Himself in darkness. Storm clouds surrounded Him like a tent. 🌧️⛺
## Psalms 18:12
Bright light shone from Him. His clouds moved forward with hail and hot coals of fire. ✨🔥
## Psalms 18:13
The LORD sent thunder from heaven. The voice of the Most High sounded with hail and hot coals of fire. ⛈️🔥
## Psalms 18:14
He shot His arrows and scattered the enemies. He threw lightning and made them run away. 🏹⚡
## Psalms 18:15
LORD, You spoke against them. The breath from Your nose blew strongly. The sea floor appeared. The bases of the earth lay open. 🌊🌬️
## Psalms 18:16
He reached down from high above and took hold of me. He pulled me out of deep water. ✋🌊
## Psalms 18:17
He rescued me from my powerful enemy. My enemies were too strong for me. 🛡️
## Psalms 18:18
They attacked me on my day of disaster. But the LORD supported me. 🤲
## Psalms 18:19
He brought me into an open place. He rescued me because He took pleasure in me. ❤️
## Psalms 18:20
The LORD rewarded me because I did right. He repaid me because my acts were clean. ⚖️
## Psalms 18:21
I followed the LORD’s ways. I did not turn away from my God to do evil. 🛤️✅
## Psalms 18:22
I kept all His rules before me. I did not ignore His commands. 📖
## Psalms 18:23
I lived before Him without blame. I kept myself from sin. ✅
## Psalms 18:24
So the LORD rewarded me because I did right. He saw that my acts were clean. ⚖️
## Psalms 18:25
You are faithful to people who are faithful. You show that You are without fault to people who live without blame. 🤝
## Psalms 18:26
You show Yourself pure to people who are pure. But You outwit people who are dishonest. 🔍
## Psalms 18:27
You save people who suffer. But You bring proud people low. 🤲
## Psalms 18:28
LORD, You light my lamp. My God turns my darkness into light. 🪔✨
## Psalms 18:29
With You, I can attack an army. With my God, I can climb a wall. 🧱
## Psalms 18:30
God’s way is perfect. The LORD’s word has no fault. He is a shield for everyone who comes to Him for safety. 📖🛡️
## Psalms 18:31
Who is God except the LORD? Who is a rock of safety except our God? 🪨
## Psalms 18:32
God gives me strength. He makes my way clear. 💪🛤️
## Psalms 18:33
He makes my feet steady like a deer’s feet. He helps me stand on high places. 🦌⛰️
## Psalms 18:34
He trains my hands to fight. My arms can bend a bow of bronze. 🏹
## Psalms 18:35
You give me Your shield to save me. Your right hand supports me. Your gentle care makes me great. 🛡️✋
## Psalms 18:36
You make the path wide under me. So my feet do not slip. 🛤️
## Psalms 18:37
I chased my enemies and caught them. I did not turn back until I destroyed them. ⚔️
## Psalms 18:38
I crushed them. They were unable to get up. They fell under my feet. ⚔️
## Psalms 18:39
You gave me strength for battle. You put my enemies under my power. 💪
## Psalms 18:40
You made my enemies run away from me. I destroyed the people who hated me. ⚔️
## Psalms 18:41
They cried for help, but no one saved them. They cried to the LORD, but He did not answer. 🆘
## Psalms 18:42
I crushed them like dust that blows in the wind. I stepped on them like mud in the streets. 🌬️
## Psalms 18:43
You rescued me from fights among the people. You made me the leader of nations. People I did not know will serve me. 👑🌍
## Psalms 18:44
As soon as they hear me, they obey me. People from other countries bow before me in fear. 👂
## Psalms 18:45
People from other countries lose courage. They come out of their forts in fear. 🏰
## Psalms 18:46
The LORD lives! Praise my Rock! Give honor to the God who saves me. 🙌🪨
## Psalms 18:47
God punishes those who hurt me. He puts nations under my power. ⚖️🌍
## Psalms 18:48
He rescues me from my enemies. You lift me above those who attack me. You save me from violent people. 🛡️
## Psalms 18:49
So, LORD, I will praise You among the nations. I will sing praise to Your name. 🎵🌍
## Psalms 18:50
He gives His king great victories. He shows faithful love to the king He chose. He shows this love to David and his descendants forever. 👑❤️
