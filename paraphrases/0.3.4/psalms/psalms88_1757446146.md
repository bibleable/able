## Psalms 88:1
This song of the sons of Korah is for the music leader. Its music direction is Mahalath Leannoth. It is a Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite. LORD, God who saves me, I cry before You day and night. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 88:2
Let my prayer come before You. Turn Your ear toward my cry. 👂
## Psalms 88:3
I am full of troubles inside. My life is near the place of the dead. 💔
## Psalms 88:4
People count me among those who go down to the grave. I am like a person with no strength. 😢
## Psalms 88:5
I am alone among the dead. I am like people who die by violence and lie in the grave. You no longer remember them. They are outside Your care. 💔
## Psalms 88:6
You put me in the deepest pit. You put me in the darkest depths. 🌑
## Psalms 88:7
Your anger presses heavily on me. All Your waves cover me. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌊
## Psalms 88:8
You take my friends away from me. You make them find me disgusting. I am shut in and cannot escape. 💔
## Psalms 88:9
Grief makes my eyes dim. LORD, I call to You every day. I spread my hands toward You. 🤲😢
## Psalms 88:10
Do You do wonderful things for the dead? Do spirits of dead people rise to praise You? *Song direction: Selah.* ❓
## Psalms 88:11
Can anyone tell of Your faithful love in the grave? Can anyone tell of Your faithfulness in Abaddon, the place of destruction? ❓
## Psalms 88:12
Will anyone know Your wonderful acts in the darkness? Will anyone know Your righteousness in the land where all is forgotten? 🌑
## Psalms 88:13
But LORD, I cry to You for help. In the morning, my prayer comes before You. 🌅🙏
## Psalms 88:14
LORD, why do You reject me? Why do You hide Your face from me? ❓
## Psalms 88:15
I suffer and face death from my youth. I bear the terrors You send. I lose hope. 💔
## Psalms 88:16
Your fierce anger sweeps over me. Your terrors destroy me. 🌊
## Psalms 88:17
They cover me like water all day. They surround me on every side. 🌊
## Psalms 88:18
You take my loved one and friend away. Darkness is my closest companion. 🌑💔
