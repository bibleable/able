## Psalms 143:1
David wrote this song. LORD, hear my prayer. Listen to my request because You are faithful. Answer me because You are righteous. 🙏👂
## Psalms 143:2
Do not put me, Your servant, on trial. No person alive is righteous before You. ⚖️
## Psalms 143:3
The enemy chases my life and crushes me to the ground. He makes me live in darkness like people dead long ago. 🌑
## Psalms 143:4
My spirit grows weak inside me. My heart is full of fear. 💔
## Psalms 143:5
I remember the days long ago. I think deeply about all Your works. I consider the work of Your hands. 💭
## Psalms 143:6
I stretch my hands toward You. I thirst for You like dry land. *Song direction: Selah.* 🤲💧
## Psalms 143:7
LORD, answer quickly because my spirit fails. Do not hide Your face. Otherwise, I will be like people who go down to the grave. 🆘
## Psalms 143:8
Let me hear of Your faithful love in the morning. I trust You. Teach me the way to walk. I lift my whole self to You. 🌅🛤️
## Psalms 143:9
LORD, rescue me from my enemies. I run to You for safety. 🛡️
## Psalms 143:10
Teach me to do what You want because You are my God. Let Your good Spirit lead me on level ground. 🛤️
## Psalms 143:11
LORD, give me life again to honor Your name. Because of Your righteousness, bring me out of trouble. 🙏
## Psalms 143:12
Because of Your faithful love, destroy my enemies. Destroy everyone who hurts me. I am Your servant. ⚖️
