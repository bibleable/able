## Psalms 99:1
The LORD rules! Let the nations tremble. He sits as King above the cherubim, heavenly beings. Let the earth shake! 👑🌍
## Psalms 99:2
The LORD is great in Zion. He is high above all peoples. ⛰️
## Psalms 99:3
Let them praise Your great name, which fills them with wonder and fear. He is holy! 🙌
## Psalms 99:4
The powerful King loves fairness. You set up fair rule. You do what is fair and right among Jacob’s people. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 99:5
Honor the LORD our God. Worship at His footstool, the place under His feet. He is holy! 🙏
## Psalms 99:6
Moses and Aaron were among His priests. Samuel was among those who called on His name. They called to the LORD, and He answered. 🙏
## Psalms 99:7
He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud. They kept His instructions and the laws He gave them. ☁️📖
## Psalms 99:8
LORD our God, You answered them. You forgave them. But You also punished their wrong acts. 🤲⚖️
## Psalms 99:9
Honor the LORD our God. Worship at His holy mountain because the LORD our God is holy. ⛰️🙏
