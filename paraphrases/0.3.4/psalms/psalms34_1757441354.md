## Psalms 34:1
David wrote this song after he pretended to have an illness of the mind before Abimelech. The king sent him away. I will praise the LORD at all times. I will always speak His praise. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 34:2
I boast about the LORD. Let people who suffer hear and be glad. 📣😊
## Psalms 34:3
Tell of the LORD’s greatness with me. Let us honor His name together. 🙌
## Psalms 34:4
I sought the LORD, and He answered me. He rescued me from all my fears. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 34:5
People who look to Him shine with joy. They will never lower their faces in shame. 😊✨
## Psalms 34:6
This poor man called out, and the LORD heard him. He saved him from all his troubles. 🆘👂
## Psalms 34:7
The LORD’s angel camps around people who respect Him. The angel rescues them. 🛡️
## Psalms 34:8
Taste and see that the LORD is good. The person who comes to Him for safety receives good from Him. ❤️
## Psalms 34:9
His holy people, respect the LORD. People who respect Him lack nothing. 🙏
## Psalms 34:10
Young lions lack food and grow hungry. But people who seek the LORD lack no good thing. 🦁🍞
## Psalms 34:11
Children, come and listen to me. I will teach you to respect the LORD. 🧒👂
## Psalms 34:12
Who loves life? Who wants to enjoy good days? ❤️❓
## Psalms 34:13
Keep your tongue from evil words. Do not let your lips tell lies. 🗣️✅
## Psalms 34:14
Turn away from evil and do good. Look for peace and work toward it. 🤝
## Psalms 34:15
The LORD watches people who do right. His ears hear their cries. 👀👂
## Psalms 34:16
But the LORD stands against people who do evil. He will remove all memory of them from the earth. ⚖️
## Psalms 34:17
People who do right cry out, and the LORD hears. He rescues them from all their troubles. 🆘🛡️
## Psalms 34:18
The LORD is near to people whose hearts are broken. He saves people whose spirits are crushed. 💔🤲
## Psalms 34:19
A person who does right has many troubles. But the LORD rescues that person from them all. 🛡️
## Psalms 34:20
He protects all that person’s bones. Not one bone will break. 🛡️
## Psalms 34:21
Evil will kill people who do evil. People who hate those who do right will face a guilty judgment. ⚖️
## Psalms 34:22
The LORD rescues His servants. No one who comes to Him for safety will face a guilty judgment. 🛡️❤️
