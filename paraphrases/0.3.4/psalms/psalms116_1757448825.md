## Psalms 116:1
I love the LORD. He hears my voice and my cry for kindness. ❤️👂
## Psalms 116:2
He turns His ear toward me. So I will call to Him as long as I live. 🙏
## Psalms 116:3
Death held me like ropes. Pain from the place of the dead came over me. Trouble and sorrow faced me. 🆘
## Psalms 116:4
Then I called on the LORD’s name. I said, “LORD, rescue my life!” 🙏
## Psalms 116:5
The LORD is kind and right. Our God is full of care. ❤️
## Psalms 116:6
The LORD protects people with simple hearts. I was helpless, and He saved me. 🛡️
## Psalms 116:7
My soul, return to rest. The LORD treats you well. ❤️
## Psalms 116:8
You rescue my life from death. You keep my eyes from tears and my feet from a fall. 🛡️
## Psalms 116:9
I will walk before the LORD among people who are alive. 🚶
## Psalms 116:10
I believed, so I said, “I suffer greatly.” 🙏
## Psalms 116:11
In fear, I said, “All people tell lies!” 🗣️
## Psalms 116:12
How can I repay the LORD for all His goodness to me? 🎁❓
## Psalms 116:13
I will lift the cup of rescue. I will call on the LORD’s name. 🙏
## Psalms 116:14
I will keep my promises to the LORD before all His people. ✅
## Psalms 116:15
The death of His holy people is precious to the LORD. ❤️
## Psalms 116:16
LORD, I truly am Your servant. I am Your servant, the son of Your woman servant. You break my chains. ⛓️
## Psalms 116:17
I will offer You a sacrifice of thanks. I will call on the LORD’s name. 🙏
## Psalms 116:18
I will keep my promises to the LORD before all His people. ✅
## Psalms 116:19
I will do this in the LORD’s courts, in the middle of Jerusalem. Praise the LORD! 🏙️🙌
