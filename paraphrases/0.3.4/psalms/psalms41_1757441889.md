## Psalms 41:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. The person who cares for poor people receives good from God. The LORD will rescue that person when trouble comes. 🤲🛡️
## Psalms 41:2
The LORD will protect that person and keep him alive. He will give him good things in the land. He will not let enemies do whatever they want to him. 🛡️
## Psalms 41:3
The LORD will support that person on his sickbed. He will help him recover from his illness. 🛌🤲
## Psalms 41:4
I said, “LORD, be kind to me. Heal me, because I sinned against You.” 🙏
## Psalms 41:5
My enemies want to hurt me. They say, “When will he die? When will people forget him?” 🗣️
## Psalms 41:6
A person who visits me tells lies. He collects harmful stories about me. Then he goes out and spreads them. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 41:7
Everyone who hates me whispers against me. They imagine the worst for me. 👥
## Psalms 41:8
They say, “A terrible disease fills him. He will never get out of his bed again!” 🛌
## Psalms 41:9
Even my close friend turns against me. I trusted him. He shared my bread. But he acts against me like someone who raises a heel to strike. 🍞💔
## Psalms 41:10
But LORD, be kind and help me get up. Then I can repay them. 🙏
## Psalms 41:11
My enemy does not defeat me. This tells me that You take pleasure in me. 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 41:12
You support me because I live honestly. You keep me with You forever. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 41:13
Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, forever and ever. Amen and amen. Let it be so. 🙌
