## Psalms 60:1
David wrote this teaching Miktam for the music leader, to the tune “The Lily of the Covenant.” He fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah. Joab returned and killed 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. God, You rejected us and broke us in anger. Restore us! 🎵🆘
## Psalms 60:2
You shook the land and split it open. Heal its cracks because it still shakes. 🌍
## Psalms 60:3
You made Your people suffer hardship. We stumble from the wine You made us drink. 😢
## Psalms 60:4
You raise a flag for people who fear You. They can escape from the enemy’s bow. *Song direction: Selah.* 🚩🏹
## Psalms 60:5
Answer us and save us with Your right hand. Rescue the people You love. ✋❤️
## Psalms 60:6
God speaks from His holy place. He says, “I will win! I will divide Shechem. I will share out the Valley of Succoth. 👑
## Psalms 60:7
Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine. Ephraim is like My helmet. Judah is like My royal rod. 👑
## Psalms 60:8
Moab is like My bowl for washing. I throw My sandal on Edom. I shout in victory over Philistia.” 👑
## Psalms 60:9
Who will bring me to the city with strong walls? Who will lead me to Edom? 🧱❓
## Psalms 60:10
God, did You not reject us? Will You no longer go out with our armies? ❓
## Psalms 60:11
Help us against the enemy. Human help is useless. 🆘
## Psalms 60:12
With God, we will act bravely. He will step on our enemies. 💪
