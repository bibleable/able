## Psalms 103:1
David wrote this song. My soul, praise the LORD. Everything inside me, praise His holy name. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 103:2
My soul, praise the LORD. Do not forget all His kind acts. 💭🙌
## Psalms 103:3
He forgives all your sins. He heals all your diseases. 🤲
## Psalms 103:4
He rescues your life from the grave. He crowns you with faithful love and care. 👑❤️
## Psalms 103:5
He satisfies you with good things. Your youth becomes new, like an eagle’s. 🦅
## Psalms 103:6
The LORD acts rightly and fairly for everyone who suffers cruelty. ⚖️
## Psalms 103:7
He showed His ways to Moses. He showed His acts to Israel’s people. 📖
## Psalms 103:8
The LORD is kind and caring. He does not become angry quickly. His faithful love is great. ❤️
## Psalms 103:9
He will not always accuse us. He will not keep His anger forever. 🤲
## Psalms 103:10
He does not treat us as our sins deserve. He does not repay us for all our wrong acts. ❤️
## Psalms 103:11
The heavens are high above the earth. His faithful love for people who fear Him is just as great. ☁️❤️
## Psalms 103:12
The east is far from the west. He removes our sins just as far from us. 🌅🌇
## Psalms 103:13
A father cares for his children. In the same way, the LORD cares for people who fear Him. 👨‍👧‍👦❤️
## Psalms 103:14
He knows how we are made. He remembers that we are dust. 🌬️
## Psalms 103:15
A person’s days are like grass. A person grows like a flower in a field. 🌼
## Psalms 103:16
The wind passes over the flower, and it disappears. Its place remembers it no more. 🌬️
## Psalms 103:17
But the LORD’s faithful love lasts forever for people who fear Him. His righteousness reaches their grandchildren. ❤️👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 103:18
These people keep His covenant, the agreement He made. They remember to obey His commands. 🤝📖
## Psalms 103:19
The LORD sets His throne firm in heaven. His kingdom rules over everything. 👑
## Psalms 103:20
All His powerful angels, praise the LORD. You carry out His word. You listen to His command. 🙌
## Psalms 103:21
All the LORD’s armies, praise Him. All His servants who do what He wants, praise Him. 🙌
## Psalms 103:22
Everything He made, praise the LORD everywhere He rules. My soul, praise the LORD! 🌍🙌
