## Psalms 79:1
Asaph wrote this song. God, the nations invade the land that belongs to You. They make Your holy temple unclean. They turn Jerusalem into piles of broken stones. 🏚️
## Psalms 79:2
They give Your servants’ dead bodies as food to birds. They give the flesh of Your holy people to wild animals. 💔
## Psalms 79:3
They pour out their blood like water all around Jerusalem. No one is left to bury the dead. 💔
## Psalms 79:4
Our neighbors insult us. People around us mock us and laugh at us. 🗣️
## Psalms 79:5
LORD, how long? Will You be angry forever? Will Your jealousy burn like fire? 🆘🔥
## Psalms 79:6
Pour Your anger on nations that do not acknowledge You. Pour it on kingdoms that refuse to call on Your name. ⚖️
## Psalms 79:7
They destroy the people of Jacob. They ruin their homeland. 🏚️
## Psalms 79:8
Do not count past sins against us. Hurry to show us Your care. We are very low. 🙏
## Psalms 79:9
God who saves us, help us for the honor of Your name. Rescue us and forgive our sins for Your name’s sake. 🆘🙏
## Psalms 79:10
Why must the nations ask, “Where is their God?” Before our eyes, show the nations Your punishment for the bloodshed of Your servants. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 79:11
Hear the prisoners’ cries of pain. With Your strong arm, keep people alive who face a death sentence. 👂🛡️
## Psalms 79:12
Lord, repay our neighbors seven times for the insults they throw at You. ⚖️
## Psalms 79:13
Then we, Your people, will thank You forever. We are the sheep in Your field. Through every generation, we will tell Your praise. 🐑🙌
