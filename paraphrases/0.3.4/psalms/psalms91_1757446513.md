## Psalms 91:1
The person who lives in the Most High’s shelter will stay in the Almighty’s shadow. 🛡️
## Psalms 91:2
I will say to the LORD, “You are my safe place and my strong fort. You are my God, and I trust You.” 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 91:3
He will surely rescue you from the bird catcher’s trap. He will rescue you from deadly disease. 🛡️
## Psalms 91:4
He will cover you with His feathers. You will find safety under His wings. His faithfulness protects you like a shield and a wall. 🪽🛡️
## Psalms 91:5
You will not fear terror at night. You will not fear arrows that fly during the day. 🌙🏹
## Psalms 91:6
You will not fear disease that moves in the dark. You will not fear disaster that destroys at noon. 🛡️
## Psalms 91:7
A thousand can fall beside you and ten thousand at your right side. But harm will not come near you. 🛡️
## Psalms 91:8
You will only watch with your eyes. You will see the punishment of people who do evil. 👀⚖️
## Psalms 91:9
You make the LORD your home. The Most High is my safe place. 🏠
## Psalms 91:10
Because of this, no evil will happen to you. No deadly disease will come near your tent. ⛺🛡️
## Psalms 91:11
He will command His angels to protect you in all your ways. 🛡️
## Psalms 91:12
They will lift you in their hands. Then your foot will not hit a stone. 🤲🪨
## Psalms 91:13
You will step on the lion and cobra. You will crush the young lion and snake under your feet. 🦁🐍
## Psalms 91:14
God says, “He loves Me, so I will rescue him. He knows My name, so I will protect him. ❤️🛡️
## Psalms 91:15
When he calls to Me, I will answer. I will be with him in trouble. I will rescue him and give him honor. 🙏
## Psalms 91:16
I will satisfy him with a long life. I will show him My power to save.” ❤️
