## Psalms 62:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. Its music direction is Jeduthun. I find rest in God alone. He is the One who saves me. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 62:2
He alone is my rock and my rescuer. He is my strong fort. I will never fall from safety. 🪨🛡️
## Psalms 62:3
How long will you all threaten one man? Will you throw him down like a wall that leans or a fence about to fall? ❓
## Psalms 62:4
They plan to pull him down from his high place. They enjoy lies. Their mouths wish him good, but their hearts wish him harm. *Song direction: Selah.* 🗣️❌
## Psalms 62:5
My soul, rest in God alone. My hope comes from Him. ❤️
## Psalms 62:6
He alone is my rock and my rescuer. He is my strong fort. I will stand firm. 🪨🛡️
## Psalms 62:7
My rescue and my honor depend on God. He is my strong rock. God is my safe place. 🪨
## Psalms 62:8
People, trust Him at all times. Tell Him everything in your hearts. God is our safe place. *Song direction: Selah.* 🙏❤️
## Psalms 62:9
People of low status are only a breath. The greatness of people with high status is false. Together they weigh less than a breath on a scale. 🌬️⚖️
## Psalms 62:10
Do not trust money you force from people. Do not hope in things you steal. If your wealth grows, do not give it your heart. 💰❌
## Psalms 62:11
God speaks once, and I hear this twice: power belongs to God. 💪
## Psalms 62:12
Lord, faithful love belongs to You too. You repay each person for what that person does. ❤️⚖️
