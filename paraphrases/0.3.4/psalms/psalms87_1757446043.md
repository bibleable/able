## Psalms 87:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song. God built His city on the holy mountains. ⛰️🏙️
## Psalms 87:2
The LORD loves Zion’s gates more than all the homes of Jacob. 🚪❤️
## Psalms 87:3
City of God, people tell glorious things about you. *Song direction: Selah.* 🏙️✨
## Psalms 87:4
God says, “I will name Rahab and Babylon among the people who know Me. I will include Philistia, Tyre, and Cush. I will say, ‘This person was born in Zion.’” 🌍
## Psalms 87:5
People will say about Zion, “This person and that person were born here. The Most High Himself will make the city stand firm.” 🏙️
## Psalms 87:6
The LORD will write in His list of the peoples, “This person was born in Zion.” *Song direction: Selah.* 📖
## Psalms 87:7
Singers and people with pipes will say, “All my springs of joy are in You.” 🎵💧
