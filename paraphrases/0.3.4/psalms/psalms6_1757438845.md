## Psalms 6:1
David wrote this song for the music leader, with instruments that have strings. Its music direction is Sheminith. LORD, do not correct me in Your anger. Do not punish me in Your great anger. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 6:2
LORD, be kind to me because I am weak. LORD, heal me because my bones hurt badly. 🤲
## Psalms 6:3
I feel great distress inside me. LORD, how long will this last? 😢⏳
## Psalms 6:4
LORD, turn to me and rescue me. Save my life because of Your faithful love. 🆘❤️
## Psalms 6:5
Dead people do not speak about You. Who praises You in the place of the dead? ❓
## Psalms 6:6
My cries of pain make me tired. All night my tears soak my bed. My tears make my couch wet. 😢🛌
## Psalms 6:7
My sorrow makes my eyes weak. My eyes grow dim because of all my enemies. 😢
## Psalms 6:8
All of you who do evil, leave me! The LORD heard my tears. ✋🙏
## Psalms 6:9
The LORD heard my cry for kindness. The LORD accepts my prayer. 👂🙏
## Psalms 6:10
All my enemies will feel shame and fear. They will turn back in sudden shame. ⚖️
