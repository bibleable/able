## Psalms 140:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. LORD, rescue me from evil people. Protect me from violent people. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 140:2
They make evil plans in their hearts. They cause war all day. 💭❌
## Psalms 140:3
Their sharp tongues are like snakes. Their lips carry poison like a viper’s. *Song direction: Selah.* 🐍
## Psalms 140:4
LORD, guard me from the power of people who do evil. Keep me safe from violent people who plan to make me fall. 🛡️
## Psalms 140:5
Proud people hide a trap for me. They spread the ropes of their net along the path. They set bait for me. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛤️
## Psalms 140:6
I say to the LORD, “You are my God.” LORD, hear my cry for help. 🙏
## Psalms 140:7
GOD the Lord, You are the strength that saves me. You shield my head on the day of battle. 🛡️
## Psalms 140:8
LORD, do not give evil people what they want. Do not help their evil plans succeed. Do not let them become great. *Song direction: Selah.* ✋
## Psalms 140:9
Let the trouble from their own lips cover the heads of people who surround me. ⚖️
## Psalms 140:10
Let hot coals fall on them. Let them fall into fire and muddy pits, never to rise again. 🔥
## Psalms 140:11
Let people who tell harmful lies find no secure place in the land. Let disaster chase violent people. ⚖️
## Psalms 140:12
I know the LORD gives fairness to poor people. He defends people in need. ⚖️🤲
## Psalms 140:13
People who do right will surely praise Your name. Honest people will live with You. 🙌❤️
