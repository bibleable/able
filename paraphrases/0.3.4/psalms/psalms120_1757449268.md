## Psalms 120:1
This is a song of ascent. In trouble, I cried to the LORD. He answered me. 🙏
## Psalms 120:2
LORD, rescue my life from lips that lie. Rescue me from a tongue that deceives. 🛡️
## Psalms 120:3
Dishonest tongue, what will God do to you? What more will He give you? ⚖️❓
## Psalms 120:4
A soldier’s sharp arrows will come. Hot coals from the broom tree will come too. 🏹🔥
## Psalms 120:5
I suffer because I live in Meshech! I live among Kedar’s tents! ⛺
## Psalms 120:6
I live too long among people who hate peace. 💔
## Psalms 120:7
I want peace. But when I speak, they want war. 🤝
