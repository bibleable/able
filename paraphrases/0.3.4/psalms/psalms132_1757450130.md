## Psalms 132:1
This is a song of ascent. LORD, remember David and all the hardship he bore. 🙏
## Psalms 132:2
He made a firm promise to the LORD. He gave this promise to the Mighty One of Jacob. 🤝
## Psalms 132:3
He said, “I will not enter my house. I will not get into my bed. 🏠🛌
## Psalms 132:4
I will not let my eyes sleep. I will not close them for rest. 👀
## Psalms 132:5
I will do this until I find a place for the LORD. I will find a home for the Mighty One of Jacob.” 🏠
## Psalms 132:6
We heard that the ark was in Ephrathah. We found it in the fields of Jaar. The ark is God’s sacred chest. 📦
## Psalms 132:7
Let us go to His home. Let us worship at His footstool, the place under His feet. 🙏
## Psalms 132:8
LORD, come to Your resting place. Come with the ark of Your strength. 📦
## Psalms 132:9
Let Your priests wear righteousness like clothes. Let Your holy people shout with joy. 😊
## Psalms 132:10
For the sake of Your servant David, do not reject the king You chose. 👑
## Psalms 132:11
The LORD made a firm promise to David. He will not take it back. He said, “I will put one of your descendants on your throne. 👑🤝
## Psalms 132:12
If your sons keep My covenant and the instructions I teach them, their sons will sit on your throne forever.” *Word help: A covenant is a binding agreement.* 🤝
## Psalms 132:13
The LORD chooses Zion. He wants it for His home. ⛰️🏠
## Psalms 132:14
He says, “This is My resting place forever. I will live here because I want this home. 🏠
## Psalms 132:15
I will give Zion plenty of supplies. I will give its poor people enough bread. 🍞
## Psalms 132:16
I will dress its priests in rescue like clothes. Its holy people will sing with joy. 🎵😊
## Psalms 132:17
There I will make a horn of power grow for David. I prepare a lamp for the king I chose. 🪔👑
## Psalms 132:18
I will cover his enemies with shame. But his crown will shine.” 👑✨
