## Psalms 122:1
David wrote this song of ascent. I was glad when people said, “Let us go to the LORD’s house.” 🏠😊
## Psalms 122:2
Jerusalem, our feet stand inside your gates. 🚪
## Psalms 122:3
Jerusalem is a city with its parts joined firmly together. 🏙️
## Psalms 122:4
The LORD’s tribes go up there. Israel follows this instruction to thank the LORD’s name. 👥🙌
## Psalms 122:5
The thrones for judgment stand there. They are the thrones of David’s family. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 122:6
Pray for Jerusalem’s peace. Say, “Let people who love you prosper. 🙏🤝
## Psalms 122:7
Let peace be inside your walls. Let your strong forts have plenty.” 🧱
## Psalms 122:8
For my brothers and friends, I will say, “Let peace be inside you.” 🤝
## Psalms 122:9
Because of the LORD our God’s house, I will seek your good. 🏠❤️
