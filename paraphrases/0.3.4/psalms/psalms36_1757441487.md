## Psalms 36:1
David, the LORD’s servant, wrote this song for the music leader. I have a message in my heart about the sin of an evil person. This person has no fear of God. 🎵💭
## Psalms 36:2
He is too proud to recognize his own sin. He does not hate it. ❌
## Psalms 36:3
His words are evil and dishonest. He no longer acts wisely. He no longer does good. 🗣️
## Psalms 36:4
Even in bed, he makes evil plans. He chooses a path that is not good. He does not reject evil. 🛌💭
## Psalms 36:5
LORD, Your faithful love reaches to the heavens. Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ☁️❤️
## Psalms 36:6
Your right acts are like the highest mountains. Your judgments are like the deepest sea. LORD, You keep people and animals alive. ⛰️🌊
## Psalms 36:7
God, Your faithful love is precious! People find safety in the shadow of Your wings. ❤️🪽
## Psalms 36:8
They eat the rich food of Your house. You give them drink from Your river of pleasures. 🍞💧
## Psalms 36:9
Life flows from You like water from a spring. In Your light, we see light. 💧✨
## Psalms 36:10
Keep Your faithful love with people who know You. Keep Your right care with people whose hearts are honest. ❤️
## Psalms 36:11
Do not let proud people step on me. Do not let people who do evil drive me away. 🛡️
## Psalms 36:12
There the people who do evil lie on the ground. They fall down and cannot get up. ⚖️
