## Psalms 109:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. God whom I praise, do not stay silent. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 109:2
People who do evil and deceive others speak against me. They tell lies about me. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 109:3
They surround me with hateful words. They attack me without a cause. 💔
## Psalms 109:4
I love them, but they accuse me. Yet I continue to pray. 🙏
## Psalms 109:5
I do good, but they repay me with evil. I give love, but they repay me with hate. 💔
## Psalms 109:6
Put an evil man in charge of him. Let someone stand at his right side to accuse him. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:7
At his trial, let the judge find him guilty. Let his prayer count as sin. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:8
Let his days be few. Let another person take his position. ⏳
## Psalms 109:9
Let his children have no father. Let his wife have no husband. 💔
## Psalms 109:10
Let his children wander and beg for food. Let them search for food far from their ruined homes. 🏚️
## Psalms 109:11
Let the person he owes money to take everything he owns. Let strangers steal what he worked to earn. 💰
## Psalms 109:12
Let no one show him kindness. Let no one care for his children who have no father. 💔
## Psalms 109:13
Let his descendants die out. Let the next generation forget their name. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:14
Let the LORD remember his ancestors’ sins. Never let the record of his mother’s sin disappear. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:15
Let their sins always remain before the LORD. Let Him remove their memory from the earth. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:16
He never thought to show kindness. He chased poor people, people in need, and people with broken hearts. He chased them even to death. 💔
## Psalms 109:17
He loved to curse others. Let that curse fall on him. He did not enjoy good wishes for others. Let good wishes stay far from him. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:18
He wore curses like a coat. Let those curses soak into his body like water. Let them soak into his bones like oil. 💧
## Psalms 109:19
Let the curse wrap around him like a robe. Let it stay around him forever like a belt. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:20
Let the LORD repay my accusers in this way. They speak evil against me. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:21
But GOD, the Lord, treat me kindly to honor Your name. Rescue me because Your faithful love is good. ❤️🙏
## Psalms 109:22
I am poor and need help. My heart hurts inside me. 💔
## Psalms 109:23
I fade like a shadow that grows longer. I am like a locust that someone shakes off. 🌬️
## Psalms 109:24
My knees are weak because I go without food. My body grows thin and weak. 🆘
## Psalms 109:25
My accusers treat me with scorn. When they see me, they shake their heads. 🗣️
## Psalms 109:26
LORD my God, help me. Save me because of Your faithful love. 🙏❤️
## Psalms 109:27
Let them know that Your hand does this. LORD, let them know that You act. ✋
## Psalms 109:28
They curse, but You will give good. When they attack, they will face shame. But I, Your servant, will be glad. 😊
## Psalms 109:29
Let my accusers wear disgrace like clothes. Let shame cover them like a robe. ⚖️
## Psalms 109:30
I will thank the LORD with many words. I will praise Him before many people. 🗣️🙌
## Psalms 109:31
He stands at the right side of the person in need. He saves that person from people who condemn him. 🛡️
