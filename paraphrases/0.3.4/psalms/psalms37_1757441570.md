## Psalms 37:1
David wrote this song. Do not worry about people who do evil. Do not envy people who do wrong. ✋
## Psalms 37:2
They soon dry up like grass. They fade like young plants. 🌿
## Psalms 37:3
Trust the LORD and do good. Live in the land and practice faithfulness. 🤝✅
## Psalms 37:4
Find joy in the LORD. He will give you what your heart wants. 😊❤️
## Psalms 37:5
Put your way of life in the LORD’s hands. Trust Him, and He will act. 🤲
## Psalms 37:6
He will make your right conduct clear like the dawn. He will make your fair cause shine like the sun at noon. 🌅☀️
## Psalms 37:7
Be quiet before the LORD and wait patiently for Him. Do not worry when people succeed through evil plans. ⏳
## Psalms 37:8
Let go of anger. Give up fierce anger. Do not worry, because it only causes harm. ✋
## Psalms 37:9
People who do evil will face destruction. But people who hope in the LORD will receive the land. 🌳
## Psalms 37:10
Soon people who do evil will be gone. You will look for them, but you will not find them. 👀
## Psalms 37:11
But gentle people will receive the land. They will enjoy great peace and plenty. 🌳❤️
## Psalms 37:12
People who do evil make plans against people who do right. They grind their teeth at them in anger. 😠
## Psalms 37:13
But the Lord laughs at them. He sees that their day of judgment is near. ⚖️
## Psalms 37:14
People who do evil draw swords and prepare their bows. They want to bring down poor people and people in need. They want to kill people who live rightly. ⚔️🏹
## Psalms 37:15
But their swords will go through their own hearts. Their bows will break. ⚖️
## Psalms 37:16
The little that a person who does right owns is better than the wealth of many evil people. 💰
## Psalms 37:17
The power of people who do evil will break like broken arms. But the LORD supports people who do right. 🤲
## Psalms 37:18
The LORD knows the days of people who live without blame. The share He gives them will last forever. ❤️
## Psalms 37:19
They will not face shame when trouble comes. When there is not enough food, they will have enough to eat. 🍞
## Psalms 37:20
But people who do evil will die. The LORD’s enemies will fade like the beauty of the fields. They will disappear like smoke. 🌬️
## Psalms 37:21
People who do evil borrow and do not pay back. But people who do right are kind and give freely. 🤲💰
## Psalms 37:22
People who receive good from God will receive the land. People under His curse will face destruction. ⚖️
## Psalms 37:23
The LORD directs a person’s steps. He takes pleasure in that person’s path. 🛤️
## Psalms 37:24
Even if that person falls, the fall will not destroy him. The LORD holds his hand. 🤝
## Psalms 37:25
I was young, and now I am old. I never saw God leave a person who does right without help. I never saw that person’s children beg for bread. 👴🍞
## Psalms 37:26
People who do right are always generous and willing to lend. Their children bring good to others. 🤲👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 37:27
Turn away from evil and do good. Then you will live securely forever. ✅
## Psalms 37:28
The LORD loves fairness. He will not leave His holy people. He keeps them safe forever. But the descendants of people who do evil will face destruction. ⚖️🛡️
## Psalms 37:29
People who do right will receive the land. They will live in it forever. 🌳
## Psalms 37:30
The person who does right speaks wise words. This person speaks about fairness. 🗣️⚖️
## Psalms 37:31
God’s law is in this person’s heart. This person’s steps do not slip. 📖❤️
## Psalms 37:32
People who do evil hide and wait for the person who does right. They want to kill that person. 👀
## Psalms 37:33
But the LORD will not leave that person in their power. He will not allow a guilty judgment against that person. 🛡️⚖️
## Psalms 37:34
Wait for the LORD and follow His way. He will lift you up to receive the land. You will see people who do evil disappear. ⏳🛤️
## Psalms 37:35
I saw a cruel person who did evil. He grew strong like a native tree with deep roots. 🌳
## Psalms 37:36
Then he passed away and was gone. I looked for him, but I did not find him. 👀
## Psalms 37:37
Look at the person who lives without blame. Watch the person who does right. The person who lives in peace has a future with descendants. 🤝👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 37:38
But all people who choose sin will face destruction. People who do evil will have no future. ⚖️
## Psalms 37:39
The LORD saves people who do right. He is their strong fort when trouble comes. 🛡️
## Psalms 37:40
The LORD helps and rescues them. He saves them from people who do evil. He does this because they come to Him for safety. 🛡️❤️
