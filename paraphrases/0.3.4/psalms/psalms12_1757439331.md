## Psalms 12:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. Its music direction is Sheminith. LORD, help! People who love You are gone. People who are faithful are no longer among us. 🎵🆘
## Psalms 12:2
People lie to one another. They say pleasant words that they do not mean. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 12:3
LORD, silence everyone who gives false praise. Silence everyone who boasts. ✋
## Psalms 12:4
They say, “Our words will help us win. We control our own lips. Who can rule over us?” 🗣️
## Psalms 12:5
The LORD says, “People suffer from cruelty. People in need cry out. So I will act now. I will give safety to the person who longs for it.” 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 12:6
The LORD’s words have no fault. They are like silver that fire makes pure. They are like gold that goes through this process seven times. 🔥✨
## Psalms 12:7
LORD, You will keep us safe. You will protect us from these people forever. 🛡️
## Psalms 12:8
People who do evil walk around freely. This happens when people honor things that are very wrong. 🚶❌
