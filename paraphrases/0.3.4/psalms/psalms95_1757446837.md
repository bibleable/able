## Psalms 95:1
Come, let us sing with joy to the LORD. Let us shout to the Rock who saves us. 🎵🪨
## Psalms 95:2
Let us come before Him with thanks. Let us make a joyful sound with songs. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 95:3
The LORD is a great God. He is a great King above all gods. 👑
## Psalms 95:4
He holds the depths of the earth in His hand. The mountain peaks belong to Him. 🌍⛰️
## Psalms 95:5
The sea belongs to Him because He made it. His hands formed the dry land. 🌊🌍
## Psalms 95:6
Come, let us worship and bow down. Let us kneel before the LORD who made us. 🙏
## Psalms 95:7
He is our God. We are the people in His field, the sheep He cares for. If you hear His voice today, pay attention. 🐑👂
## Psalms 95:8
God says, “Do not make your hearts hard as you did at Meribah. Do not be like that day at Massah in the desert. 🏜️
## Psalms 95:9
There your ancestors tested Me. They tried Me even though they saw My work. ❌
## Psalms 95:10
For forty years, I was angry with that generation. I said, ‘Their hearts go the wrong way. They do not know My ways.’ ⚖️
## Psalms 95:11
So in My anger I made this firm promise: ‘They will never enter My rest.’” ⚖️
