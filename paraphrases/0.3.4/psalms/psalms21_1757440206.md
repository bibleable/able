## Psalms 21:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. LORD, the king is glad because of Your strength. He has great joy because You save him. 👑😊
## Psalms 21:2
You gave him what his heart wanted. You did not refuse his request. *Song direction: Selah.* ❤️🎁
## Psalms 21:3
You welcomed him with many good gifts. You put a crown of pure gold on his head. 👑✨
## Psalms 21:4
He asked You for life. You gave him a long life that lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 21:5
Your help gives him great honor. You give him beauty and royal greatness. 👑
## Psalms 21:6
You give him good things forever. Your presence fills him with joy. 😊❤️
## Psalms 21:7
The king trusts in the LORD. The faithful love of the Most High keeps him secure. 🛡️
## Psalms 21:8
Your hand will catch all Your enemies. Your right hand will seize those who hate You. ✋
## Psalms 21:9
When You appear, You will put them in a hot furnace. The LORD will swallow them up in His anger. Fire will destroy them. 🔥
## Psalms 21:10
You will remove their descendants from the earth. Their children will no longer live among people. ⚖️
## Psalms 21:11
They plan to hurt You. But their plans will not succeed. ❌
## Psalms 21:12
You will aim Your bow at them. You will make them run away. 🏹
## Psalms 21:13
LORD, show Your great strength. We will sing and praise Your power. 🎵🙌
