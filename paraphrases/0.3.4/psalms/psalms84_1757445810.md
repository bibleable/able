## Psalms 84:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song for the music leader, with the music direction Gittith. LORD of Hosts, Your home is lovely! 🎵🏠
## Psalms 84:2
I long so much for the LORD’s courts that I grow faint. My heart and body cry out for the living God. ❤️🙏
## Psalms 84:3
LORD of Hosts, my King and God, even a sparrow finds a home near Your altars. A swallow makes a nest there for her young. 🐦
## Psalms 84:4
People who live in Your house receive good from You. They always praise You. *Song direction: Selah.* 🏠🙌
## Psalms 84:5
People who find their strength in You receive good from You. Their hearts long for the journey to worship You. 🛤️
## Psalms 84:6
As they pass through the Valley of Baca, they make it a place of springs. Autumn rain covers it with pools too. 💧🌧️
## Psalms 84:7
They grow stronger and stronger. Each one comes before God in Zion. 💪⛰️
## Psalms 84:8
LORD God of Hosts, hear my prayer. God of Jacob, listen to me. *Song direction: Selah.* 🙏👂
## Psalms 84:9
God, look at our shield. Look with kindness at the king You chose. 👑
## Psalms 84:10
One day in Your courts is better than a thousand days elsewhere. I prefer to guard the door of God’s house rather than live in the tents of evil people. 🏠❤️
## Psalms 84:11
The LORD God is like a sun and a shield. He gives kindness and glory. He holds no good thing back from people who live honestly. ☀️🛡️
## Psalms 84:12
LORD of Hosts, the person who trusts You receives good from You! 🙏❤️
