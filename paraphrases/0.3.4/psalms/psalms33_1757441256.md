## Psalms 33:1
People who do right, be glad in the LORD. It is right for honest people to praise Him. 😊🙌
## Psalms 33:2
Praise the LORD with the harp. Make music to Him on an instrument with ten strings. 🎵
## Psalms 33:3
Sing a new song to Him. Play well and shout with joy. 🎵😊
## Psalms 33:4
The LORD’s word is right. Everything He does deserves our trust. 📖✅
## Psalms 33:5
The LORD loves right conduct and fairness. His faithful love fills the earth. 🌍❤️
## Psalms 33:6
The LORD made the heavens by His word. He made all the stars by the breath of His mouth. 🌌⭐
## Psalms 33:7
He gathers the sea waters into a heap. He puts the deep waters in places like storehouses. 🌊
## Psalms 33:8
Let all the earth fear the LORD. Let everyone in the world show Him deep respect. 🌍🙏
## Psalms 33:9
He spoke, and the world came to be. He gave a command, and it stood firm. 🌍
## Psalms 33:10
The LORD stops the plans of the nations. He defeats the plans of the people. ✋🌍
## Psalms 33:11
The LORD’s plans stand forever. His purposes last through all generations. ✅
## Psalms 33:12
The nation whose God is the LORD receives good from Him. He chooses its people to belong to Him. 👥❤️
## Psalms 33:13
The LORD looks down from heaven. He sees all human beings. 👀🌍
## Psalms 33:14
From His home, He watches everyone who lives on earth. 👀
## Psalms 33:15
He forms each person’s heart. He considers everything they do. ❤️🔍
## Psalms 33:16
A large army cannot save a king by itself. A soldier’s great strength cannot rescue him by itself. 👑
## Psalms 33:17
It is useless to depend on a horse for safety. Even its great strength cannot save you. 🐎
## Psalms 33:18
The LORD watches people who respect Him. He watches those who hope in His faithful love. 👀❤️
## Psalms 33:19
He rescues them from death. He keeps them alive when there is not enough food. 🍞🛡️
## Psalms 33:20
We wait for the LORD. He is our help and our shield. ⏳🛡️
## Psalms 33:21
We are glad because of Him. We trust in His holy name. 😊
## Psalms 33:22
LORD, let Your faithful love rest on us. We put our hope in You. ❤️🙏
