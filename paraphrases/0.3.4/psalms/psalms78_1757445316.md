## Psalms 78:1
Asaph wrote this Maskil. My people, hear my instructions. Listen to my words. 🎵👂
## Psalms 78:2
I will tell stories that teach. I will speak about things hidden from the beginning. 📣
## Psalms 78:3
We heard and learned these things. Our ancestors told them to us. 👂
## Psalms 78:4
We will not hide them from their children. We will tell the next generation why the LORD deserves praise. We will tell of His strength and wonderful acts. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦📣
## Psalms 78:5
He gave instructions to Jacob and a law to Israel. He commanded our ancestors to teach them to their children. 📖🧒
## Psalms 78:6
Then the next generation will know them, even children not yet born. Those children will grow up and tell their own children. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 78:7
Then they can trust God. They must not forget His works. They must keep His commands. 📖✅
## Psalms 78:8
They must not be like their stubborn ancestors who refused to obey. Their ancestors’ hearts were not loyal. Their spirits were not faithful to God. ❌
## Psalms 78:9
The soldiers of Ephraim carried bows. But they turned back on the day of battle. 🏹
## Psalms 78:10
They did not keep God’s covenant, the agreement He made with them. They refused to live by His law. 🤝❌
## Psalms 78:11
They forgot His acts. They forgot the wonderful things He showed them. 💭
## Psalms 78:12
He did wonderful things before their ancestors in Egypt. This happened in the region of Zoan. ✨
## Psalms 78:13
He split the sea and led them through it. He made the waters stand like a wall. 🌊🧱
## Psalms 78:14
He led them with a cloud during the day. He led them with the light of fire all night. ☁️🔥
## Psalms 78:15
He split rocks in the desert. He gave them as much water as the seas. 🪨💧
## Psalms 78:16
He brought streams out of stone. He made water flow down like rivers. 🪨💧
## Psalms 78:17
But they continued to sin against Him. In the desert, they refused to obey the Most High. 🏜️❌
## Psalms 78:18
They chose to test God. They demanded the food they wanted. 🍽️
## Psalms 78:19
They spoke against God. They said, “Can God really prepare a meal in the desert? 🏜️❓
## Psalms 78:20
He struck the rock, and water rushed out. Great streams flowed. But can He also give bread? Can He give His people meat?” 🍞
## Psalms 78:21
The LORD heard this and became very angry. Fire burned against Jacob. His anger rose against Israel. 🔥
## Psalms 78:22
They did not believe God. They did not depend on Him to save them. ❌
## Psalms 78:23
Yet He commanded the clouds above. He opened the doors of heaven. ☁️
## Psalms 78:24
He sent down manna for them to eat, like rain. He gave them grain from heaven. 🌾
## Psalms 78:25
People ate the bread of angels. God sent plenty of food. 🍞
## Psalms 78:26
He sent the east wind from heaven. By His power, He drove the south wind. 🌬️
## Psalms 78:27
He sent meat down on them like dust. He sent birds as many as the grains of sand by the sea. 🐦
## Psalms 78:28
He made the birds fall inside their camp. The birds fell all around their tents. ⛺🐦
## Psalms 78:29
They ate until they were full. He gave them the food they wanted. 🍽️
## Psalms 78:30
But they still wanted more. The food was still in their mouths. 🍽️
## Psalms 78:31
Then God’s anger rose against them. He killed their strongest people. He brought down Israel’s young men. ⚖️
## Psalms 78:32
Even after all this, they continued to sin. They did not believe despite His wonderful acts. ❌
## Psalms 78:33
So He ended their days with nothing achieved. He ended their years in sudden terror. ⚖️
## Psalms 78:34
When He killed some of them, the others sought Him. They turned from sin and searched for God. 🙏
## Psalms 78:35
They remembered that God was their Rock. God Most High was their Rescuer. 🪨🛡️
## Psalms 78:36
But they used their mouths to deceive Him. They told Him lies with their tongues. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 78:37
Their hearts were not loyal to Him. They were not faithful to His covenant. 🤝❌
## Psalms 78:38
Yet He showed kindness and forgave their sins. He did not destroy them. Often He held back His anger. He did not let all His fierce anger loose. 🤲
## Psalms 78:39
He remembered that they were only human flesh. They were like a breeze that passes and never returns. 🌬️
## Psalms 78:40
How often they disobeyed Him in the wilderness! How often they caused Him grief in the desert! 🏜️
## Psalms 78:41
Again and again, they tested God. They made the Holy One of Israel angry. ❌
## Psalms 78:42
They did not remember His power. They forgot the day He rescued them from the enemy. 💭
## Psalms 78:43
He showed His signs in Egypt. He did wonderful things in the fields of Zoan. ✨
## Psalms 78:44
He turned their rivers into blood. They had no water to drink from their streams. 💧
## Psalms 78:45
He sent great crowds of flies that ate at them. He sent frogs that caused destruction. 🪰🐸
## Psalms 78:46
He gave their crops to grasshoppers. Locusts ate the food they worked to grow. 🌾
## Psalms 78:47
He killed their vines with hail. He killed their sycamore-fig trees with icy rain. 🌳
## Psalms 78:48
He gave their cattle over to hail. He gave their farm animals over to lightning. ⚡
## Psalms 78:49
He sent His fierce anger against them. He sent wrath, anger, and disaster. He sent a band of angels to destroy. ⚖️
## Psalms 78:50
He cleared a path for His anger. He did not keep them from death. He gave their lives over to a deadly disease. ⚖️
## Psalms 78:51
He struck down all the firstborn of Egypt. They were the first strength of the families in the tents of Ham. 💔
## Psalms 78:52
He led His own people out like sheep. He guided them like a flock through the desert. 🐑🏜️
## Psalms 78:53
He led them safely, so they were not afraid. But the sea covered their enemies. 🌊🛡️
## Psalms 78:54
He brought them to His holy land. He brought them to the mountain His right hand gained. ⛰️
## Psalms 78:55
He drove the nations out before them. He divided the land for them to receive. He settled Israel’s tribes in the other nations’ tents. ⛺
## Psalms 78:56
But they tested God Most High and disobeyed Him. They did not keep His commands. ❌
## Psalms 78:57
They turned away and were unfaithful like their ancestors. They were like a bent bow that does not work properly. 🏹
## Psalms 78:58
Their places for worship on the hills made Him angry. Their idols made Him jealous. ❌
## Psalms 78:59
God heard this and became very angry. He rejected Israel completely. ⚖️
## Psalms 78:60
He left the holy tent at Shiloh. He set that tent among people, but now He left it. ⛺
## Psalms 78:61
He let enemies capture His strength. He gave His splendor into the enemy’s hands. 💔
## Psalms 78:62
He let the sword kill His people. He was very angry with the people who belonged to Him. ⚔️
## Psalms 78:63
Fire destroyed His young men. The young women had no wedding songs. 🔥💔
## Psalms 78:64
His priests died by the sword. But their widows were unable to cry out in grief. ⚔️
## Psalms 78:65
Then the Lord woke as if from sleep. He was like a powerful soldier overcome by wine. 💪
## Psalms 78:66
He drove His enemies back. He gave them shame that lasts forever. ⚖️
## Psalms 78:67
He rejected Joseph’s tent. He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim. ⛺
## Psalms 78:68
But He chose the tribe of Judah. He chose Mount Zion, which He loved. ⛰️❤️
## Psalms 78:69
He built His holy place like the heights of heaven. He made it like the earth, which He set firm forever. 🏠🌍
## Psalms 78:70
He chose His servant David. He took him from the sheep pens. 🐑
## Psalms 78:71
David cared for mother sheep. God brought him to care for His people Jacob, His own people Israel, like a shepherd. 🐑👥
## Psalms 78:72
David cared for them with an honest heart. He guided them with skilled hands. ❤️🤲
