## Psalms 142:1
David prayed this Maskil when he was in a cave. I cry aloud to the LORD. I raise my voice to ask the LORD for kindness. 🙏
## Psalms 142:2
I pour out my complaint before Him. I tell Him my trouble. 🗣️
## Psalms 142:3
My spirit grows weak inside me, but You know my way. People hide a trap along the path I travel. 🛤️
## Psalms 142:4
Look at my right side. No one pays attention to me. I have no safe place. No one cares for my life. 💔
## Psalms 142:5
LORD, I cry to You. I say, “You are my safe place. You are my share among people who are alive.” 🛡️
## Psalms 142:6
Listen to my cry because I am very low. Rescue me from people who chase me. They are too strong for me. 🆘
## Psalms 142:7
Free my life from prison so that I can praise Your name. People who do right will gather around me because You treat me well. 🙌🤲
