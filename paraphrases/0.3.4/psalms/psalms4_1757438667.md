## Psalms 4:1
David wrote this song for the music leader, with instruments that have strings. God, You defend me when I do right. Answer me when I call. You gave me relief from trouble. Be kind to me and hear my prayer. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 4:2
People, how long will you turn my honor into shame? How long will you love worthless things? How long will you search for lies? *Song direction: Selah.* 🗣️❓
## Psalms 4:3
Know that the LORD chose people who are faithful to Him as His own. The LORD hears me when I call to Him. 👂❤️
## Psalms 4:4
When you are angry, do not sin. Think carefully in your bed. Examine your heart and be quiet. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛌💭
## Psalms 4:5
Give the LORD the right sacrifices. Trust in the LORD. 🙏
## Psalms 4:6
Many people ask, “Who will show us something good?” LORD, look on us with kindness, like light from Your face. ✨
## Psalms 4:7
You make me very glad. You give me more joy than people feel when they have much grain and new wine. 😊❤️
## Psalms 4:8
I will lie down and sleep in peace. LORD, You alone let me live in safety. 🛌🛡️
