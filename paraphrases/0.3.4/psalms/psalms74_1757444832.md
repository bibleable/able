## Psalms 74:1
Asaph wrote this Maskil. God, why do You reject us forever? Why does Your anger burn against Your people, the sheep in Your field? 🐑🆘
## Psalms 74:2
Remember the people You bought long ago. You rescued them to be Your own tribe. Remember Mount Zion, where You live. ⛰️❤️
## Psalms 74:3
Come to the ruins that remain. See everything the enemy destroyed in Your holy place. 🏚️
## Psalms 74:4
Your enemies roar in Your meeting place. They raise their own flags as signs. 🚩
## Psalms 74:5
They act like people who swing axes in a thick group of trees. 🪓🌳
## Psalms 74:6
They smash all the carved wood with axes and picks. 🪓
## Psalms 74:7
They burn Your holy place to the ground. They make the home of Your Name unclean. 🔥
## Psalms 74:8
They think, “We will crush them completely.” They burn every place in the land where God meets us. 🔥
## Psalms 74:9
We see no signs. There are no prophets left. None of us knows how long this will last. ❓
## Psalms 74:10
God, how long will the enemy mock You? Will the enemy insult Your name forever? 🆘
## Psalms 74:11
Why do You hold back Your strong right hand? Stretch it out to destroy them! ✋⚖️
## Psalms 74:12
But God is my King from long ago. He saves people on the earth. 👑🌍
## Psalms 74:13
You divided the sea by Your strength. You smashed the heads of the sea dragons. 🌊
## Psalms 74:14
You crushed Leviathan’s heads. You gave him as food to creatures of the desert. ⚖️
## Psalms 74:15
You opened springs and floods. You dried up rivers that always flowed. 💧
## Psalms 74:16
The day belongs to You, and so does the night. You put the moon and sun in place. ☀️🌙
## Psalms 74:17
You set all the earth’s boundaries. You made summer and winter. 🌍
## Psalms 74:18
LORD, remember how the enemy mocks You. Remember how foolish people reject Your name. 🗣️
## Psalms 74:19
Do not give Your dove’s life to wild animals. Never forget the lives of Your people who suffer. 🕊️🛡️
## Psalms 74:20
Remember Your covenant, the agreement You made. The dark places of the land are full of homes of violence. 🤝
## Psalms 74:21
Do not let people who suffer cruelty turn back in shame. Let poor people and people in need praise Your name. 🙌
## Psalms 74:22
God, act now and defend Your cause! Remember how foolish people mock You all day. 🆘
## Psalms 74:23
Do not ignore the noise of people who oppose You. Your enemies make more and more noise without end. 👂
