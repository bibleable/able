## Psalms 115:1
LORD, give glory to Your name, not to us. Your faithful love and truth deserve the glory. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 115:2
Why must the nations say, “Where is their God?” 🌍❓
## Psalms 115:3
Our God is in heaven. He does whatever He chooses. ☁️
## Psalms 115:4
Their idols are silver and gold. Human hands make them. ❌
## Psalms 115:5
They have mouths but cannot speak. They have eyes but cannot see. 👄👀
## Psalms 115:6
They have ears but cannot hear. They have noses but cannot smell. 👂
## Psalms 115:7
They have hands but cannot feel. They have feet but cannot walk. They cannot even clear their throats. ❌
## Psalms 115:8
People who make them become like them. So does everyone who trusts them. ❌
## Psalms 115:9
Israel, trust the LORD! He helps and shields His people. 🛡️
## Psalms 115:10
Aaron’s family, trust the LORD! He helps and shields them. 🛡️
## Psalms 115:11
People who fear the LORD, trust Him! He helps and shields them. 🛡️
## Psalms 115:12
The LORD remembers us and will give us good things. He will give good things to Israel’s family. He will give good things to Aaron’s family. ❤️
## Psalms 115:13
He will give good things to people who fear Him. This includes small and great people alike. 👥
## Psalms 115:14
Let the LORD make you and your children grow in number. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 115:15
Let the LORD give you good things. He made heaven and earth. 🌍🎁
## Psalms 115:16
The highest heavens belong to the LORD. But He gives the earth to human beings. ☁️🌍
## Psalms 115:17
Dead people do not praise the LORD. People who go down into silence do not praise Him. 🤫
## Psalms 115:18
But we will praise the LORD now and forever. Praise the LORD! 🙌
