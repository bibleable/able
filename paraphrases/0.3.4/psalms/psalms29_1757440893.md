## Psalms 29:1
David wrote this song. Heavenly beings, honor the LORD for His glory and strength. 🙌✨
## Psalms 29:2
Give the LORD the honor that His name deserves. Worship the LORD in the beauty of His holiness. 🙏✨
## Psalms 29:3
The LORD’s voice sounds over the waters. The God of glory sends thunder. The LORD speaks over the great waters. 🌊⛈️
## Psalms 29:4
The LORD’s voice is powerful. The LORD’s voice is great and wonderful. 📣
## Psalms 29:5
The LORD’s voice breaks the cedar trees. The LORD smashes the cedar trees of Lebanon. 🌲
## Psalms 29:6
He makes Lebanon jump like a calf. He makes Sirion jump like a young wild ox. 🐂⛰️
## Psalms 29:7
The LORD’s voice strikes with flames of fire. 🔥
## Psalms 29:8
The LORD’s voice shakes the desert. The LORD shakes the Desert of Kadesh. 🏜️
## Psalms 29:9
The LORD’s voice twists the oak trees. It strips the forests bare. In His temple, everyone shouts, “Glory!” 🌳🙌
## Psalms 29:10
The LORD sits as King over the flood. The LORD rules as King forever. 🌊👑
## Psalms 29:11
The LORD gives His people strength. The LORD gives His people peace. 💪❤️
