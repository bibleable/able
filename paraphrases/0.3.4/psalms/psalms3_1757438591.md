## Psalms 3:1
David wrote this song when he ran away from his son Absalom. LORD, I have so many enemies! So many people attack me! 🆘
## Psalms 3:2
Many people say about me, “God will not save him. *Song direction: Selah.*” 🗣️
## Psalms 3:3
But LORD, You protect me like a shield all around me. You give me honor. You help me lift my head again. 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 3:4
I call loudly to the LORD. He answers me from His holy mountain. *Song direction: Selah.* 🙏⛰️
## Psalms 3:5
I lie down and sleep. I wake up again because the LORD keeps me alive. 🛌☀️
## Psalms 3:6
Thousands of enemies surround me. But I will not be afraid of them. 🛡️
## Psalms 3:7
LORD, act now! My God, save me! Hit all my enemies on the jaw. Break the teeth of people who do evil. 🆘⚖️
## Psalms 3:8
The LORD is the One who saves. LORD, give good things to Your people. *Song direction: Selah.* 🙏❤️
