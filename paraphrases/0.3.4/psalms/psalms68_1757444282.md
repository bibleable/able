## Psalms 68:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. God acts, and His enemies scatter. People who hate Him run away from Him. 🎵
## Psalms 68:2
You drive them away like smoke in the wind. People who do evil will die before God, like wax that melts by a fire. 🌬️🔥
## Psalms 68:3
But people who do right will be glad before God. They will celebrate with joy. 😊🙌
## Psalms 68:4
Sing to God! Sing praise to His name. Honor the One who rides on the clouds. His name is the LORD. Be glad before Him. 🎵☁️
## Psalms 68:5
God lives in His holy home. He is a father to children who have no father. He defends women whose husbands died. 🧒🛡️
## Psalms 68:6
God gives families to lonely people. He leads prisoners out to a good life. But people who refuse to obey live in a land that the sun dries up. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦☀️
## Psalms 68:7
God, You went ahead of Your people. You marched through the desert. *Song direction: Selah.* 🏜️
## Psalms 68:8
The earth shook, and rain poured from the sky before You. You are the God of Sinai, the God of Israel. 🌧️🌍
## Psalms 68:9
God, You sent plenty of rain. You gave new strength to Your tired land. 🌧️🌱
## Psalms 68:10
Your people settled there like a flock. God, from Your plenty, You provided for poor people. 🐑🤲
## Psalms 68:11
The Lord gives the command. A great crowd of women tells the news. 📣👥
## Psalms 68:12
They say, “Kings and their armies run away quickly! The woman who stays at home divides the goods taken in battle. 👑
## Psalms 68:13
Even while you rest among the sheep pens, the dove’s wings shine with silver. Its feathers shine with gold.” 🕊️✨
## Psalms 68:14
When the Almighty scattered the kings there, it was like snow on Zalmon. ⛰️
## Psalms 68:15
Mount Bashan is a great mountain of God. Mount Bashan has many peaks. ⛰️
## Psalms 68:16
Mountains with many peaks, why do you look with envy? This is the mountain God chose for His home. The LORD will live there forever. ⛰️🏠
## Psalms 68:17
God has tens of thousands of chariots, thousands upon thousands. The Lord is in His holy place, as He was at Sinai. 👑
## Psalms 68:18
You went up on high and led prisoners away. You received gifts from people, even from those who refused to obey. So the LORD God can live there. 🎁
## Psalms 68:19
Praise the Lord! Each day He carries our heavy loads. He is the God who saves us. *Song direction: Selah.* 🤲🙌
## Psalms 68:20
Our God is the God who rescues. The Lord GOD saves us from death. 🛡️
## Psalms 68:21
God will crush His enemies’ heads. He will crush the hairy heads of people who continue in sin. ⚖️
## Psalms 68:22
The Lord said, “I will bring them back from Bashan. I will bring them up from the depths of the sea. 🌊
## Psalms 68:23
Then you can dip your feet in your enemies’ blood. Your dogs can lick that blood too.” ⚖️
## Psalms 68:24
God, people see Your great march. They see my God and King go into His holy place. 👑
## Psalms 68:25
The singers go first, and the musicians follow. Young women play tambourines among them. 🎵
## Psalms 68:26
Praise God in the great crowd. People who come from Israel, praise the LORD. 👥🙌
## Psalms 68:27
Benjamin, the youngest, leads them. The leaders of Judah are there with their group. The leaders of Zebulun and Naphtali are there too. 👥
## Psalms 68:28
God, call on Your power. Show the strength You use for us. 💪🙏
## Psalms 68:29
Because of Your temple in Jerusalem, kings will bring You gifts. 👑🎁
## Psalms 68:30
Oppose the beast in the reeds and the herd of bulls among the calves of the nations. Make it submit and bring bars of silver. Scatter the nations that enjoy war. ⚖️
## Psalms 68:31
Messengers will come from Egypt. Cush will stretch out its hands to God. 🤲
## Psalms 68:32
Kingdoms of the earth, sing to God. Sing praise to the Lord. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌍🎵
## Psalms 68:33
He rides through the highest heavens from long ago. Listen! His powerful voice sounds out. ☁️📣
## Psalms 68:34
Honor God for His power. His greatness is over Israel. His strength is in the skies. 💪
## Psalms 68:35
God, You fill us with wonder and fear in Your holy place. The God of Israel gives strength and power to His people. Praise God! 🙌💪
