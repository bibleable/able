## Psalms 66:1
This song is for the music leader. All the earth, shout to God with joy! 🎵🌍
## Psalms 66:2
Sing about the glory of His name. Give Him glorious praise. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 66:3
Say to God, “Your acts fill us with wonder and fear! Your power is so great that Your enemies bow low in fear. 💪
## Psalms 66:4
All the earth bows before You. People sing praise to You. They sing praise to Your name.” *Song direction: Selah.* 🌍🎵
## Psalms 66:5
Come and see God’s works. His acts for human beings fill us with wonder and fear. 👀
## Psalms 66:6
He turned the sea into dry land. The people walked through the waters. There we were glad in Him. 🌊🚶
## Psalms 66:7
He rules forever by His power. He watches the nations. People who refuse His rule must not make themselves great. *Song direction: Selah.* 👑🌍
## Psalms 66:8
People, praise our God. Let everyone hear the sound of His praise. 🙌📣
## Psalms 66:9
He keeps us alive. He keeps our feet from a fall. 🛡️
## Psalms 66:10
God, You tested us. You made us pure like silver in a hot furnace. 🔥
## Psalms 66:11
You led us into a net. You put heavy loads on our backs. 😢
## Psalms 66:12
You let people ride over our heads. We went through fire and water. But You brought us to a place of plenty. 🔥🌊
## Psalms 66:13
I will enter Your house with burnt offerings. I will keep my promises to You. 🙏
## Psalms 66:14
I made those promises with my own lips. I spoke them when I was in trouble. 🗣️
## Psalms 66:15
I will offer fat animals as burnt offerings to You. Smoke from the rams will give a pleasant smell. I will offer bulls and goats. *Song direction: Selah.* 🐂🐐
## Psalms 66:16
Everyone who fears God, come and listen. I will tell you what He did for me. 👂📣
## Psalms 66:17
I cried to Him with my mouth. I praised Him with my tongue. 🙏🙌
## Psalms 66:18
If I held on to sin in my heart, the Lord would not listen. ❤️
## Psalms 66:19
But God surely heard me. He listened to the sound of my prayer. 👂🙏
## Psalms 66:20
Praise God! He does not reject my prayer. He does not hold His faithful love back from me. 🙌❤️
