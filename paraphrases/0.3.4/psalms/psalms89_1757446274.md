## Psalms 89:1
Ethan the Ezrahite wrote this Maskil. I will sing of the LORD’s faithful love forever. I will tell every generation about Your faithfulness. 🎵❤️
## Psalms 89:2
I say, “Faithful love stands firm forever. You make Your faithfulness stand firm in the heavens.” ❤️
## Psalms 89:3
You said, “I made a covenant with My chosen one. I made this firm promise to My servant David. *Word help: A covenant is a binding agreement.* 🤝
## Psalms 89:4
‘I will make your family line last forever. I will build up your throne through all generations.’” *Song direction: Selah.* 👑
## Psalms 89:5
LORD, the heavens praise Your wonderful acts. The gathering of holy ones praises Your faithfulness. 🙌
## Psalms 89:6
Who in the skies can compare with the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like Him? ❓
## Psalms 89:7
The holy ones who gather greatly fear God. He fills them with more wonder than anyone around Him. 🙏
## Psalms 89:8
LORD God of Hosts, who is like You? Mighty LORD, Your faithfulness surrounds You. ❤️💪
## Psalms 89:9
You rule the wild sea. When its waves rise high, You quiet them. 🌊
## Psalms 89:10
You crushed Rahab like a dead body. Your strong arm scattered Your enemies. 💪
## Psalms 89:11
The heavens and the earth belong to You. You made the world and everything in it. 🌍
## Psalms 89:12
You created the north and the south. Tabor and Hermon shout with joy at Your name. ⛰️😊
## Psalms 89:13
Your arm is powerful. Your hand is strong. Your right hand is high above all. ✋💪
## Psalms 89:14
Righteousness and fairness are the base of Your throne. Faithful love and truth go ahead of You. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 89:15
People who know the joyful sound receive good from You. LORD, they walk in the light of Your presence. 🎵✨
## Psalms 89:16
They are glad in Your name all day. They celebrate Your righteousness. 😊
## Psalms 89:17
You are the glory of their strength. Your kindness raises our horn, the sign of our power. 💪
## Psalms 89:18
Our shield belongs to the LORD. Our king belongs to the Holy One of Israel. 🛡️👑
## Psalms 89:19
You once spoke to Your faithful people in a vision. You said, “I gave help to a soldier. I lifted up a person chosen from the people. 👑
## Psalms 89:20
I found My servant David. I poured My holy oil on him to choose him. 🫙
## Psalms 89:21
My hand will support him. My arm will make him strong. ✋💪
## Psalms 89:22
No enemy will force money from him. No person who does evil will treat him cruelly. 🛡️
## Psalms 89:23
I will crush his enemies before him. I will strike down those who hate him. ⚖️
## Psalms 89:24
My faithfulness and faithful love will stay with him. Through My name, his horn of power will rise high. ❤️💪
## Psalms 89:25
I will put the sea under his hand. I will put the rivers under his right hand. 🌊
## Psalms 89:26
He will call to Me, ‘You are my Father and my God. You are the Rock who saves me.’ 🪨
## Psalms 89:27
I will give him the place of My firstborn son. I will make him the highest of the earth’s kings. 👑
## Psalms 89:28
I will keep My faithful love for him forever. My covenant with him will stand firm. 🤝❤️
## Psalms 89:29
I will make his family line last forever. His throne will last as long as the heavens. 👑
## Psalms 89:30
Suppose his sons leave My law and do not live by My judgments. 📖
## Psalms 89:31
Suppose they break My rules and do not keep My commands. ❌
## Psalms 89:32
then I will punish their sin with a rod. I will punish their wrong acts with blows. ⚖️
## Psalms 89:33
But I will not take My faithful love away from him. I will never fail to be faithful. ❤️
## Psalms 89:34
I will not break My covenant. I will not change the words from My lips. 🤝
## Psalms 89:35
I swore by My holiness once for all. I will not lie to David. ✅
## Psalms 89:36
His family line will last forever. His throne will remain before Me like the sun. ☀️👑
## Psalms 89:37
It will be like the moon, which stands firm forever. The moon is a faithful witness in the sky.” *Song direction: Selah.* 🌙
## Psalms 89:38
But now You reject him and turn him away. You are very angry with the king You chose. 💔
## Psalms 89:39
You reject the covenant with Your servant. You make his crown dirty in the dust. 👑
## Psalms 89:40
You break down all his walls. You turn his strong forts into piles of broken stones. 🏚️
## Psalms 89:41
Everyone who passes steals from him. His neighbors insult him. 💔
## Psalms 89:42
You raise the power of his enemies. You make all his enemies glad. ❌
## Psalms 89:43
You bend his sword’s edge. You do not support him in battle. ⚔️
## Psalms 89:44
You end his royal greatness. You throw his throne to the ground. 👑
## Psalms 89:45
You cut short his youth. You cover him with shame. *Song direction: Selah.* 💔
## Psalms 89:46
LORD, how long? Will You hide Yourself forever? Will Your anger burn on like fire? 🆘🔥
## Psalms 89:47
Remember how short my life is! Why did You create all people for such a brief, empty life? ⏳
## Psalms 89:48
Who can live and never see death? Can anyone rescue his own life from the place of the dead? *Song direction: Selah.* ❓
## Psalms 89:49
Lord, where is Your faithful love from long ago? Where is the love You faithfully promised to David? ❤️❓
## Psalms 89:50
Lord, remember the insults against Your servants. I carry in my heart the insults from so many people. 💔
## Psalms 89:51
LORD, Your enemies speak insults. They mock every step of the king You chose! 🗣️
## Psalms 89:52
Praise the LORD forever! Amen and amen. Let it be so. 🙌
