## Psalms 135:1
Praise the LORD! Praise His name. Servants of the LORD, praise Him. 🙌
## Psalms 135:2
You stand in the LORD’s house. You stand in the courts of our God’s house. 🏠
## Psalms 135:3
Praise the LORD because He is good. Sing praise to His name because it is lovely. 🎵❤️
## Psalms 135:4
The LORD chooses Jacob as His own. Israel is His precious possession. ❤️
## Psalms 135:5
I know that the LORD is great. Our Lord is above all gods. 🙌
## Psalms 135:6
The LORD does whatever He chooses in heaven and on earth. He does this in the seas and all their depths. 🌍🌊
## Psalms 135:7
He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth. He sends lightning with rain. He brings wind from His storehouses. ☁️⚡🌬️
## Psalms 135:8
He killed Egypt’s firstborn, both people and animals. ⚖️
## Psalms 135:9
Egypt, He sent signs and wonderful acts among you. He acted against Pharaoh and all his servants. ✨
## Psalms 135:10
He struck down many nations. He killed powerful kings. ⚖️
## Psalms 135:11
These included Sihon, king of the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan. They included all Canaan’s kings. 👑
## Psalms 135:12
He gave their land to His people Israel. It became the share His people received. 🌳🎁
## Psalms 135:13
LORD, Your name lasts forever. LORD, people know Your greatness through all generations. 🙌
## Psalms 135:14
The LORD will defend His people’s cause. He will show care for His servants. ⚖️❤️
## Psalms 135:15
The nations’ idols are silver and gold. Human hands make them. ❌
## Psalms 135:16
They have mouths but cannot speak. They have eyes but cannot see. 👄👀
## Psalms 135:17
They have ears but cannot hear. Their mouths have no breath. 👂
## Psalms 135:18
People who make them become like them. So does everyone who trusts them. ❌
## Psalms 135:19
Israel’s family, praise the LORD! Aaron’s family, praise the LORD! 🙌
## Psalms 135:20
Levi’s family, praise the LORD! Everyone who fears the LORD, praise Him! 🙌
## Psalms 135:21
Praise the LORD from Zion. He lives in Jerusalem. Praise the LORD! ⛰️🙌
