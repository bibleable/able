## Psalms 102:1
This is the prayer of a person who suffers and grows weak. This person pours out sorrow before the LORD. LORD, hear my prayer. Let my cry for help come before You. 🙏😢
## Psalms 102:2
Do not hide Your face when I am in trouble. Turn Your ear toward me. Answer quickly when I call. 🆘👂
## Psalms 102:3
My days disappear like smoke. My bones burn like hot coals. 🌬️🔥
## Psalms 102:4
My heart suffers and dries up like grass. I even forget to eat my bread. 💔
## Psalms 102:5
I cry loudly in pain. My skin hangs on my bones. 😢
## Psalms 102:6
I am like an owl in the desert. I am like an owl among broken buildings. 🦉🏚️
## Psalms 102:7
I lie awake. I am like a lonely bird on a roof. 🐦
## Psalms 102:8
My enemies mock me all day. They laugh at me and curse me. 🗣️
## Psalms 102:9
I eat ashes like bread. My tears mix with my drink. 😢
## Psalms 102:10
This happens because of Your fierce anger. You lift me up and throw me aside. 💔
## Psalms 102:11
My days are like shadows that grow longer. I dry up like grass. 🌿
## Psalms 102:12
But LORD, You sit as King forever. People remember Your name through all generations. 👑
## Psalms 102:13
You will act and show care for Zion. The time to show kindness has come. This is the time You appointed. ⛰️❤️
## Psalms 102:14
Your servants love Zion’s stones. They feel sorrow for its dust. 🪨
## Psalms 102:15
The nations will fear the LORD’s name. All the earth’s kings will fear Your glory. 🌍👑
## Psalms 102:16
The LORD will rebuild Zion. He appears in His glory. 🏙️✨
## Psalms 102:17
He will turn toward the prayer of people who have nothing. He will not reject their prayer. 🙏👂
## Psalms 102:18
Write this for the next generation. Then people not yet born can praise the LORD. 📖👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 102:19
He looked down from His high, holy place. The LORD looked from heaven to earth. 👀🌍
## Psalms 102:20
He listened to the prisoner’s cries of pain. He acted to free people under a death sentence. 👂🛡️
## Psalms 102:21
Then they can tell the LORD’s name in Zion. They can praise Him in Jerusalem. 📣🙌
## Psalms 102:22
People and kingdoms will gather to serve the LORD. 👥
## Psalms 102:23
He broke my strength along the way. He cut my days short. 💔
## Psalms 102:24
I say, “My God, do not take my life in the middle of my days! Your years last through all generations. 🙏
## Psalms 102:25
In the beginning, You laid the earth’s foundations. Your hands made the heavens. 🌍
## Psalms 102:26
They will die away, but You will remain. They will wear out like clothes. You will change them like clothes, and they will pass away. 👕
## Psalms 102:27
But You stay the same. Your years will never end. ❤️
## Psalms 102:28
Your servants’ children will live safely. Their descendants will stand firm before You.” 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦🛡️
