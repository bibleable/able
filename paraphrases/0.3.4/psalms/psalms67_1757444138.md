## Psalms 67:1
This song is for the music leader, with instruments that have strings. God, be kind to us and give us good things. Let Your face shine on us. *Song direction: Selah.* 🎵✨
## Psalms 67:2
Then people on earth will know Your ways. All nations will know how You save. 🌍🛡️
## Psalms 67:3
God, let the people praise You. Let all the people praise You. 🙌
## Psalms 67:4
Let the nations be glad and sing with joy. You judge the people fairly. You lead the nations of the earth. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 67:5
God, let the people praise You. Let all the people praise You. 🙌
## Psalms 67:6
The earth gives its harvest. God, our God, gives us good things. 🌾🎁
## Psalms 67:7
God gives us good things. So people at every end of the earth will fear Him. 🌍🙏
