## Psalms 77:1
Asaph wrote this song for the music leader, with the music direction Jeduthun. I cried to God. I cried loudly for God to hear me. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 77:2
When trouble came, I sought the Lord. I stretched out my hands all night and did not grow tired. I refused comfort. 🤲🌙
## Psalms 77:3
God, I remembered You and cried in pain. I thought deeply, and my spirit grew weak. *Song direction: Selah.* 💭😢
## Psalms 77:4
You keep me awake. I am too troubled to speak. 🛌
## Psalms 77:5
I thought about days long ago. I thought about years far in the past. 💭
## Psalms 77:6
At night, I remembered my song. I thought deeply in my heart. I asked myself these questions. 🌙💭
## Psalms 77:7
“Will the Lord reject us forever? Will He never show kindness again? ❓
## Psalms 77:8
Is His faithful love gone forever? Will His promise never come true? ❓
## Psalms 77:9
Does God forget to be kind? Does His anger stop His care?” *Song direction: Selah.* 🙏
## Psalms 77:10
Then I said, “It hurts me that the right hand of the Most High is different now.” 💔
## Psalms 77:11
I will remember the LORD’s works. Yes, I will remember Your wonderful acts from long ago. 💭
## Psalms 77:12
I will think about everything You do. I will consider Your powerful acts. 💭💪
## Psalms 77:13
God, Your way is holy. What god is as great as our God? 🛤️
## Psalms 77:14
You are the God who does wonderful things. You show Your strength among the people. 💪
## Psalms 77:15
With Your power, You rescued Your people. They are the descendants of Jacob and Joseph. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛡️
## Psalms 77:16
God, the waters saw You and moved in great circles. Even the deepest waters shook. 🌊
## Psalms 77:17
Rain poured from the clouds. Thunder sounded in the sky. Your arrows flashed back and forth. 🌧️⚡
## Psalms 77:18
Your thunder sounded in the whirlwind. Lightning lit up the world. The earth shook. ⛈️🌍
## Psalms 77:19
Your path went through the sea. You went through the mighty waters. But no one found Your footprints. 🌊🛤️
## Psalms 77:20
You used Moses and Aaron to lead Your people. You led them like a flock of sheep. 🐑
