## Psalms 86:1
This is David’s prayer. LORD, turn Your ear toward me and answer. I am poor and need help. 🙏👂
## Psalms 86:2
Protect my life because I am faithful to You. You are my God. Save me, Your servant who trusts You. 🛡️
## Psalms 86:3
Lord, be kind to me. I call to You all day. 🙏
## Psalms 86:4
Give me, Your servant, joy. Lord, I lift up my whole self to You. ❤️😊
## Psalms 86:5
Lord, You are kind and ready to forgive. Your faithful love is great toward everyone who calls to You. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 86:6
LORD, hear my prayer. Pay attention to my cry for kindness. 👂🙏
## Psalms 86:7
When trouble comes, I call to You. You answer me. 🆘
## Psalms 86:8
Lord, none of the gods are like You. No works compare to Yours. ✨
## Psalms 86:9
Lord, You made all the nations. They will come and bow before You. They will honor Your name. 🌍🙏
## Psalms 86:10
You are great and do wonderful things. You alone are God. 🙌
## Psalms 86:11
LORD, teach me Your way so that I can live by Your truth. Give me a heart that is not divided. Then I can fear Your name. 🛤️❤️
## Psalms 86:12
Lord my God, I will praise You with all my heart. I will honor Your name forever. ❤️🙌
## Psalms 86:13
Your faithful love for me is great. You rescued me from the depths of the place of the dead. 🛡️
## Psalms 86:14
God, proud people attack me. A group of cruel people wants to kill me. They have no respect for You. 🆘
## Psalms 86:15
But Lord, You are a God of kindness and care. You do not become angry quickly. You have great faithful love and truth. ❤️
## Psalms 86:16
Turn toward me and be kind. Give Your strength to me, Your servant. Save me, the son of Your woman servant. 🤲
## Psalms 86:17
Show me a sign of Your goodness. Let my enemies see it and feel shame. LORD, You help and comfort me. 👀❤️
