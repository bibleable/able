## Psalms 94:1
LORD, You are the God who punishes people for the wrong things they do. God, show Your power. ⚖️✨
## Psalms 94:2
God, You judge everyone on earth. Act now. Give proud people the punishment they deserve. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 94:3
LORD, how long will people who do evil celebrate their success? How long will they be happy about it? ⏳😟
## Psalms 94:4
They speak as if they are better than other people. All these people who do evil boast about themselves. 🗣️😠
## Psalms 94:5
LORD, they hurt Your people badly. They treat the people who belong to You with cruelty. 😢
## Psalms 94:6
They kill women whose husbands died. They kill people from other countries. They murder children who have no father. 👩🧒💔
## Psalms 94:7
They say, “The LORD does not see us. The God of Jacob does not pay attention.” 🗣️👀
## Psalms 94:8
You people who do not understand, pay attention! When will you learn to make wise choices? 👂💭
## Psalms 94:9
God made the ear. Surely He can hear. God made the eye. Surely He can see. 👂👀
## Psalms 94:10
God corrects nations when they do wrong. Surely He can punish them. God teaches people. Surely He knows what they do. 📖⚖️
## Psalms 94:11
The LORD knows what people think. He knows that their thoughts achieve nothing. 💭
## Psalms 94:12
LORD, the person You correct receives good from You. You teach that person from Your law. 📖❤️
## Psalms 94:13
You give that person rest from days of trouble. That rest lasts until a pit is ready for people who do evil. 😌🕳️
## Psalms 94:14
The LORD will not reject His people. He will never leave the people who belong to Him. 🤝❤️
## Psalms 94:15
Judges will make fair decisions again. All people who want to do right will accept those decisions. ⚖️✅
## Psalms 94:16
Who will help me against people who do evil? Who will stand with me against people who do wrong? 🆘🤝
## Psalms 94:17
The LORD helped me. His help kept me from an early death in the silent place of the dead. 🆘
## Psalms 94:18
When I say, “My foot slips,” Your love supports me, LORD. Your love does not fail. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 94:19
When I have too many worries, You comfort me. You make me glad again. 💭❤️😊
## Psalms 94:20
Can a ruler who does evil work with You? This ruler makes laws to hurt people. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 94:21
These rulers join together against people who do right. They order the death of people who did nothing wrong. 👥💔
## Psalms 94:22
But the LORD protects me like a strong fort. My God is like a solid rock where I am safe. 🛡️🪨
## Psalms 94:23
God will make these rulers suffer for the evil they do. He will destroy them because they do evil. The LORD our God will destroy them. ⚖️
