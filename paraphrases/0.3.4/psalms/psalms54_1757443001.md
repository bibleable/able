## Psalms 54:1
David wrote this Maskil for the music leader, with instruments that have strings. The Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Does David not hide among us?” God, save me by Your name. Use Your power to show that I am right. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 54:2
God, hear my prayer. Listen to my words. 🙏👂
## Psalms 54:3
Strangers attack me. Cruel people want to kill me. They have no respect for God. *Song direction: Selah.* 🆘
## Psalms 54:4
God helps me. The Lord keeps me alive. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 54:5
He will repay my enemies with harm. God, be faithful and destroy them. ⚖️
## Psalms 54:6
I will freely offer a sacrifice to You. LORD, I will praise Your name because it is good. 🙌
## Psalms 54:7
He rescues me from every trouble. I see my enemies’ defeat. 🛡️
