## Psalms 136:1
Thank the LORD because He is good. His faithful love lasts forever. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 136:2
Thank the God of gods. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:3
Thank the Lord of lords. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:4
He alone does great, wonderful things. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:5
He made the heavens with understanding. His faithful love lasts forever. ☁️❤️
## Psalms 136:6
He spread the earth over the waters. His faithful love lasts forever. 🌍❤️
## Psalms 136:7
He made the great lights. His faithful love lasts forever. ✨❤️
## Psalms 136:8
He made the sun to rule the day. His faithful love lasts forever. ☀️❤️
## Psalms 136:9
He made the moon and stars to rule the night. His faithful love lasts forever. 🌙⭐❤️
## Psalms 136:10
He struck down Egypt’s firstborn. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:11
He brought Israel out from among them. His faithful love lasts forever. 🚶❤️
## Psalms 136:12
He used His powerful hand and outstretched arm. His faithful love lasts forever. ✋❤️
## Psalms 136:13
He split the Red Sea in two. His faithful love lasts forever. 🌊❤️
## Psalms 136:14
He led Israel through the middle of it. His faithful love lasts forever. 🚶❤️
## Psalms 136:15
He swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea. His faithful love lasts forever. 🌊❤️
## Psalms 136:16
He led His people through the wilderness. His faithful love lasts forever. 🏜️❤️
## Psalms 136:17
He struck down great kings. His faithful love lasts forever. 👑❤️
## Psalms 136:18
He killed powerful kings. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:19
He killed Sihon, king of the Amorites. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:20
He killed Og, king of Bashan. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:21
He gave their land as a share to receive. His faithful love lasts forever. 🌳❤️
## Psalms 136:22
He gave that share to His servant Israel. His faithful love lasts forever. 🎁❤️
## Psalms 136:23
He remembered us when we were low. His faithful love lasts forever. ❤️
## Psalms 136:24
He freed us from our enemies. His faithful love lasts forever. 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 136:25
He gives food to every creature. His faithful love lasts forever. 🍞❤️
## Psalms 136:26
Thank the God of heaven! His faithful love lasts forever. 🙌❤️
