## Psalms 44:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song, called a Maskil, for the music leader. God, we heard what our ancestors told us. They told us about Your work in their days long ago. 🎵👂
## Psalms 44:2
Your hand drove out the nations. You settled our ancestors in their land like plants with roots. You crushed the other people and sent them away. 🌳
## Psalms 44:3
Our ancestors did not win the land by their own swords. Their own strength did not give them victory. Your right hand and arm gave them victory. Your face shone on them because You loved them. ✋❤️
## Psalms 44:4
God, You are my King. You order victories for the people of Jacob. 👑
## Psalms 44:5
Through You, we push our enemies back. Through Your name, we step on the people who attack us. 🛡️
## Psalms 44:6
I do not trust my bow. My sword does not save me. 🏹⚔️
## Psalms 44:7
You save us from our enemies. You bring shame on the people who hate us. 🛡️
## Psalms 44:8
We boast about God all day. We will praise Your name forever. *Song direction: Selah.* 🙌
## Psalms 44:9
But now You reject us and bring us low. You no longer go out with our armies. 😢
## Psalms 44:10
You make us run away from the enemy. People who hate us steal our goods. 💔
## Psalms 44:11
You hand us over like sheep for people to eat. You scatter us among the nations. 🐑🌍
## Psalms 44:12
You sell Your people for nothing. You gain no money from the sale. 💔
## Psalms 44:13
You let our neighbors insult us. People around us mock us and laugh at us. 🗣️
## Psalms 44:14
You make our name a word of scorn among the nations. Other people laugh at us. 🌍
## Psalms 44:15
My disgrace is before me all day. Shame covers my face. 😢
## Psalms 44:16
I hear people mock and insult me. The enemy wants revenge. 🗣️
## Psalms 44:17
All this happens even though we do not forget You. We do not betray Your covenant, the agreement You made with us. 🤝
## Psalms 44:18
Our hearts do not turn away. Our steps do not leave Your path. 🛤️❤️
## Psalms 44:19
Yet You crush us where jackals live. You cover us with the deepest darkness. 🌑
## Psalms 44:20
Suppose we forgot the name of our God. Suppose we lifted our hands in prayer to a foreign god. 💭
## Psalms 44:21
Surely God will discover such acts. He knows the secrets of the heart. 🔍❤️
## Psalms 44:22
But because of You, we face death all day. People treat us like sheep that they plan to kill. 🐑💔
## Psalms 44:23
Lord, wake up! Why do You sleep? Act now! Do not reject us forever. 🆘
## Psalms 44:24
Why do You hide Your face? Why do You forget our pain and the cruelty we suffer? ❓😢
## Psalms 44:25
We sink down into the dust. Our bodies lie flat on the ground. 💔
## Psalms 44:26
Act now and help us! Rescue us because of Your faithful love. 🆘❤️
