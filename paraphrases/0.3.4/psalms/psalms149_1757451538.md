## Psalms 149:1
Praise the LORD! Sing Him a new song. Sing His praise when faithful people gather. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 149:2
Let Israel be glad in the One who made them. Let Zion’s children be glad in their King. 👑😊
## Psalms 149:3
Let them dance to praise His name. Let them make music to Him with tambourine and harp. 🎵
## Psalms 149:4
The LORD takes pleasure in His people. He makes people who suffer beautiful through His rescue. ❤️🛡️
## Psalms 149:5
Let His holy people celebrate in glory. Let them shout with joy on their beds. 😊
## Psalms 149:6
Let their mouths be full of great praise to God. Let their hands hold swords with two sharp edges. 🙌⚔️
## Psalms 149:7
They will carry out revenge on the nations. They will punish the peoples. ⚖️
## Psalms 149:8
They will tie the kings with chains. They will tie the leaders with iron chains. 👑⛓️
## Psalms 149:9
They will carry out the judgment written against those people. All His holy people have this honor. Praise the LORD! ⚖️🙌
