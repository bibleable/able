## Psalms 81:1
Asaph wrote this song for the music leader, with the music direction Gittith. Sing with joy to God, our strength. Shout with joy to the God of Jacob. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 81:2
Begin a song. Strike the tambourine. Play sweet music on the harp and lyre. 🎵
## Psalms 81:3
Blow the ram’s horn at the New Moon. Blow it at the full moon on the day of our feast. 📯🌙
## Psalms 81:4
This is a law for Israel. The God of Jacob gives this command. 📖
## Psalms 81:5
God made it a witness for Joseph at the time of the journey over Egypt. There I heard a language I did not know. 👂
## Psalms 81:6
God says, “I took the heavy load from his shoulder. I freed his hands from the basket. 🤲
## Psalms 81:7
You called in trouble, and I rescued you. I answered you from the cloud of thunder. I tested you at the waters of Meribah. *Song direction: Selah.* ⛈️🛡️
## Psalms 81:8
My people, hear My warning. Israel, I long for you to listen to Me! 👂
## Psalms 81:9
There must be no other god among you. You must not bow to a foreign god. ❌
## Psalms 81:10
I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it. 🍞
## Psalms 81:11
But My people refused to listen. Israel refused to obey Me. ❌
## Psalms 81:12
So I let them follow their stubborn hearts. I let them carry out their own plans. 💭
## Psalms 81:13
If only My people would listen to Me! If only Israel would follow My ways! 🛤️
## Psalms 81:14
Then I would quickly defeat their enemies. I would turn My hand against the people who fight them. ✋
## Psalms 81:15
People who hate the LORD would pretend to obey Him. Their punishment would last forever. ⚖️
## Psalms 81:16
But I would feed you the finest wheat. I would satisfy you with honey from the rock.” 🌾🍯
