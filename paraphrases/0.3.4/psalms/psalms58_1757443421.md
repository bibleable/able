## Psalms 58:1
David wrote this Miktam for the music leader. Its tune is “Do Not Destroy.” Rulers, do you really speak fairly? Do you judge people rightly? 🎵⚖️
## Psalms 58:2
No! In your hearts, you plan unfair acts. Your hands spread violence on the earth. ❌
## Psalms 58:3
People who do evil turn away from birth. People who tell lies go wrong from the time they are born. 👶
## Psalms 58:4
Their poison is like a snake’s poison. They are like a cobra that closes its ears. 🐍
## Psalms 58:5
It refuses to hear the skilled snake charmer’s music and spell. 🐍🎵
## Psalms 58:6
God, break their teeth in their mouths. LORD, tear out these lions’ sharp teeth. ⚖️🦁
## Psalms 58:7
Let them disappear like water that flows away. When they aim their bows, let their arrows lose their sharp points. 💧🏹
## Psalms 58:8
Let them be like a slug that melts away in its slime. Let them never see the sun, like a baby who dies before birth. ⚖️
## Psalms 58:9
Before thorn branches heat your pots, God will sweep those people away. He will act whether the branches are green or dry. 🔥
## Psalms 58:10
People who do right will be glad when they see punishment for those who hurt them. They will wash their feet in the blood of people who do evil. ⚖️
## Psalms 58:11
Then people will say, “People who do right surely receive a reward! There is surely a God who judges the earth!” 🌍⚖️
