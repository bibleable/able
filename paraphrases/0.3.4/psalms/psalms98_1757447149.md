## Psalms 98:1
Sing a new song to the LORD because He does wonderful things. His right hand and holy arm won Him victory. 🎵💪
## Psalms 98:2
The LORD announces His rescue. He shows His righteousness to the nations. 🌍
## Psalms 98:3
He remembers His love and faithfulness to Israel’s people. People at every end of the earth see our God’s rescue. 🌍❤️
## Psalms 98:4
All the earth, shout to the LORD with joy. Let your cry sound out! Sing praise! 🌍🎵
## Psalms 98:5
Sing praise to the LORD with the lyre. Sing a sweet song with the harp. 🎵
## Psalms 98:6
Sound trumpets and the ram’s horn. Shout with joy before the LORD, the King. 📯👑
## Psalms 98:7
Let the sea and everything in it make a great sound. Let the world and everyone in it join in. 🌊🌍
## Psalms 98:8
Let the rivers clap their hands. Let the mountains sing together with joy. 💧⛰️
## Psalms 98:9
Let them sing before the LORD because He comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world rightly. He will judge the people fairly. 🌍⚖️
