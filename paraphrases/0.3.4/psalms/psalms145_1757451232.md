## Psalms 145:1
David wrote this song of praise. My God and King, I will honor You. I will praise Your name forever. 👑🙌
## Psalms 145:2
I will praise You every day. I will praise Your name forever. 🙌
## Psalms 145:3
The LORD is great and deserves great praise. No one can fully discover His greatness. ✨
## Psalms 145:4
One generation will praise Your works to the next. They will tell about Your powerful acts. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦📣
## Psalms 145:5
They will tell of the glorious beauty of Your royal greatness. I will think deeply about Your wonderful works. ✨💭
## Psalms 145:6
They will tell of Your powerful acts that fill them with wonder and fear. I will declare Your greatness. 📣
## Psalms 145:7
They will praise Your great goodness that everyone knows. They will sing with joy about Your righteousness. 🎵😊
## Psalms 145:8
The LORD is kind and caring. He does not become angry quickly. His faithful love is great. ❤️
## Psalms 145:9
The LORD is good to everyone. He cares for everything He makes. 🌍❤️
## Psalms 145:10
LORD, everything You make will thank You. Your holy people will praise You. 🙌
## Psalms 145:11
They will tell of Your kingdom’s glory. They will speak of Your power. 👑💪
## Psalms 145:12
Then people will know Your powerful acts. They will know the glorious beauty of Your kingdom. 👑✨
## Psalms 145:13
Your kingdom lasts forever. Your rule lasts through all generations. The LORD is faithful in every word. He is kind in every act. 👑❤️
## Psalms 145:14
The LORD supports everyone who falls. He lifts everyone who bends low. 🤲
## Psalms 145:15
All eyes look to You. You give them food at the right time. 👀🍞
## Psalms 145:16
You open Your hand. You satisfy the desires of every living thing. 🤲
## Psalms 145:17
The LORD is righteous in all His ways. He is kind in all His acts. ❤️
## Psalms 145:18
The LORD is near everyone who calls to Him. He is near everyone who calls to Him in truth. 🙏
## Psalms 145:19
He gives people who fear Him what they desire. He hears their cry and saves them. 👂🛡️
## Psalms 145:20
The LORD protects everyone who loves Him. But He will destroy all people who do evil. 🛡️⚖️
## Psalms 145:21
My mouth will speak the LORD’s praise. Let every creature praise His holy name forever. 🗣️🙌
