## Psalms 131:1
David wrote this song of ascent. LORD, my heart and eyes are not proud. I do not chase great things or matters too high for me. ❤️
## Psalms 131:2
I make my inner self still and quiet. I am like a child beside his mother after he no longer needs her milk. I am quiet like that child. 👩‍👦
## Psalms 131:3
Israel, put your hope in the LORD now and forever. 🙏❤️
