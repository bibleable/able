## Psalms 137:1
We sat by Babylon’s rivers and cried. We remembered Zion. 🌊😢
## Psalms 137:2
We hung our harps on the willow trees there. 🌳
## Psalms 137:3
The people who held us as prisoners asked for a song. People who caused us pain demanded joyful songs. They said, “Sing us a song of Zion.” 🎵💔
## Psalms 137:4
How can we sing the LORD’s song in a foreign land? ❓
## Psalms 137:5
Jerusalem, if I forget you, let my right hand stop its work. ✋
## Psalms 137:6
Let my tongue stick to the top of my mouth if I forget you. Let this happen if Jerusalem is not my greatest joy. ❤️
## Psalms 137:7
LORD, remember what Edom’s descendants said on the day Jerusalem fell. They said, “Destroy it! Tear it down to its foundations!” 🏚️
## Psalms 137:8
Babylon, destruction waits for you. The person who repays you for what you do to us will receive good. ⚖️
## Psalms 137:9
The person who seizes your babies and smashes them against the rocks will receive good. 💔
