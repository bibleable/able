## Psalms 100:1
This is a song of thanks. All the earth, shout to the LORD with joy! 🌍😊
## Psalms 100:2
Serve the LORD gladly. Come before Him with joyful songs. 🎵
## Psalms 100:3
Know that the LORD is God. He made us, and we belong to Him. We are His people and the sheep in His field. 🐑❤️
## Psalms 100:4
Enter His gates with thanks. Enter His courts with praise. Thank Him and praise His name. 🙌
## Psalms 100:5
The LORD is good. His faithful love lasts forever. His faithfulness lasts through all generations. ❤️
