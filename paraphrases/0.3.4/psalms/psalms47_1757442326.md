## Psalms 47:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song for the music leader. All people, clap your hands! Shout to God with joy because of victory! 👏😊
## Psalms 47:2
The LORD Most High fills us with wonder and fear. He is the great King over all the earth. 👑🌍
## Psalms 47:3
He puts nations under our power. He places people under our feet. 🌍
## Psalms 47:4
He chooses our share of land for us. It is the pride of Jacob, whom He loves. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌳❤️
## Psalms 47:5
God goes up as people shout with joy. The LORD goes up with the sound of a horn. 📯😊
## Psalms 47:6
Sing praise to God! Sing praise! Sing praise to our King! Sing praise! 🎵👑
## Psalms 47:7
God is King over all the earth. Sing Him a song of praise. 🌍🎵
## Psalms 47:8
God rules the nations. God sits on His holy throne. 👑
## Psalms 47:9
The leaders of the nations gather as the people of Abraham’s God. All the earth’s shields belong to God. He is very great. 🌍🙌
