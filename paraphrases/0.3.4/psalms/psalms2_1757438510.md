## Psalms 2:1
Why are the nations so angry? Why do the people make plans that cannot succeed? 🌍😠
## Psalms 2:2
The kings of the earth prepare to fight. The rulers join together against the LORD and the King He chose. 👑⚔️
## Psalms 2:3
The rulers say, “Let us break Their chains. Let us throw away Their ropes.” They want to escape Their rule. ⛓️
## Psalms 2:4
God sits on His throne in heaven and laughs. The Lord mocks these rulers. 👑
## Psalms 2:5
Then He speaks to them in anger. His great anger fills them with fear. 😠
## Psalms 2:6
God says, “I put My King on Zion, My holy mountain.” 👑⛰️
## Psalms 2:7
The King says, “I will tell you what the LORD declared to Me. He said, ‘You are My Son. Today I became Your Father. 📣
## Psalms 2:8
Ask Me. I will give You the nations. The whole earth will belong to You. 🌍🎁
## Psalms 2:9
You will break them with an iron rod. You will smash them like pots of clay.’” ⚖️
## Psalms 2:10
So, kings, be wise. Rulers of the earth, accept this warning. 👑👂
## Psalms 2:11
Serve the LORD with deep respect and fear. Be glad before Him, but tremble at His power. 🙌
## Psalms 2:12
Kiss the Son to show Him respect. Otherwise, His anger can begin suddenly. You will die because you refuse His rule. All who go to Him for safety receive good from Him. 🛡️
