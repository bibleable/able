## Psalms 71:1
LORD, I come to You for safety. Never let me suffer shame. 🛡️🙏
## Psalms 71:2
Rescue me because You are fair. Turn Your ear toward me and save me. ⚖️👂
## Psalms 71:3
Be my rock of safety, where I can always go. Give the command to save me. You are my rock and my strong fort. 🪨🛡️
## Psalms 71:4
My God, rescue me from people who do evil. Free me from the grip of unfair and cruel people. 🆘
## Psalms 71:5
Lord GOD, You are my hope. I trust You from the days of my youth. ❤️
## Psalms 71:6
I depend on You from birth. You pulled me from my mother’s womb. My praise is always for You. 👶🙌
## Psalms 71:7
Many people see me as a sign. But You are my strong, safe place. 🛡️
## Psalms 71:8
My mouth is full of Your praise. All day I tell of Your greatness. 🗣️🙌
## Psalms 71:9
Do not throw me away when I am old. Do not leave me when my strength fails. 👴🙏
## Psalms 71:10
My enemies speak against me. People who wait to kill me plan together. 💭❌
## Psalms 71:11
They say, “God left him. Chase him and seize him. No one will rescue him.” 🗣️
## Psalms 71:12
God, do not stay far from me. My God, hurry to help me. 🆘🙏
## Psalms 71:13
Let people who accuse me face shame and destruction. Let people who want to hurt me face scorn and disgrace. ⚖️
## Psalms 71:14
But I will always hope. I will praise You more and more. ❤️🙌
## Psalms 71:15
All day I will tell of Your right acts and Your rescue. They are more than I can fully know. 📣
## Psalms 71:16
I will come with the strength of the Lord GOD. I will tell of Your righteousness, Yours alone. 💪
## Psalms 71:17
God, You taught me from my youth. Even now, I tell about Your wonderful acts. 📣
## Psalms 71:18
God, do not leave me even when I am old and gray. Let me tell the next generation about Your power. Let me tell everyone still to come about Your strength. 👴👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 71:19
God, Your righteousness reaches to the heavens. You do great things. God, who is like You? ✨
## Psalms 71:20
You let me experience many troubles and disasters. But You will give me life again. You will bring me back up even from deep in the earth. 🤲
## Psalms 71:21
You will give me greater honor. You will comfort me again. ❤️
## Psalms 71:22
My God, I will praise Your faithfulness with the harp. Holy One of Israel, I will sing praise to You with the lyre. 🎵
## Psalms 71:23
My lips will shout with joy when I sing praise to You. My whole self will be glad because You rescued me. 😊🎵
## Psalms 71:24
All day I will tell of Your right acts. People who want to hurt me face shame and confusion. 📣⚖️
