## Psalms 14:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. A foolish person thinks, “There is no God.” These people are evil. They do terrible things. No one does good. 💭❌
## Psalms 14:2
The LORD looks down from heaven at human beings. He looks for anyone who understands. He looks for anyone who seeks God. 👀🌍
## Psalms 14:3
All of them turn away. Together they become evil. No one does good, not even one. ❌
## Psalms 14:4
Will people who do evil never learn? They destroy my people as easily as they eat bread. They refuse to call on the LORD. 💔
## Psalms 14:5
But great fear comes over them. God is with the people who do right. 🛡️
## Psalms 14:6
You who sin try to ruin the plans of people who suffer. But the LORD is their safe place. 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 14:7
I long for God to save Israel from Zion! When the LORD brings His captive people back, let the people of Jacob be glad. Let Israel celebrate! 🙌😊
