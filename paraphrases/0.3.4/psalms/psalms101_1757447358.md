## Psalms 101:1
David wrote this song. I will sing of Your faithful love and fairness. LORD, I will sing praise to You. 🎵❤️
## Psalms 101:2
I will think about the way to live without blame. When will You come to me? I will live in my house with an honest heart. 🏠❤️
## Psalms 101:3
I will put nothing worthless before my eyes. I hate the acts of people who turn away from what is right. Their acts will not become part of my life. 👀❌
## Psalms 101:4
I will put a dishonest heart far from me. I will have nothing to do with evil. ❌
## Psalms 101:5
I will silence anyone who secretly tells harmful lies about a neighbor. I will not accept a person with proud eyes and a proud heart. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 101:6
I will favor faithful people in the land so that they can live with me. A person who lives honestly will serve me. 🤝✅
## Psalms 101:7
No person who deceives others will live in my house. No person who tells lies will stand before me. 🏠
## Psalms 101:8
Every morning, I will remove all people who do evil from the land. I will remove everyone who does evil from the LORD’s city. ⚖️
