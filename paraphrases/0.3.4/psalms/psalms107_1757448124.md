## Psalms 107:1
Thank the LORD because He is good. His faithful love lasts forever. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 107:2
Let the people the LORD rescues say this. He rescues them from the enemy’s power. 🛡️📣
## Psalms 107:3
He gathers them from many lands. They come from east and west, north and south. 🌍
## Psalms 107:4
Some wandered in empty deserts. They found no path to a city where they could live. 🏜️
## Psalms 107:5
They were hungry and thirsty. Their strength inside them failed. 🆘
## Psalms 107:6
Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. He rescued them from their distress. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 107:7
He led them along a straight path. They reached a city where they could live. 🛤️🏙️
## Psalms 107:8
Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love. Let them thank Him for His wonderful acts for people. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 107:9
He satisfies people who are thirsty. He fills hungry people with good things. 🍞💧
## Psalms 107:10
Some sat in darkness and the shadow of death. They were prisoners in pain and chains. 🌑⛓️
## Psalms 107:11
They refused to obey God’s words. They rejected the Most High’s advice. ❌
## Psalms 107:12
He humbled their hearts with hard work. They fell, and no one helped them. 💔
## Psalms 107:13
Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. He saved them from their distress. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 107:14
He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death. He broke their chains. ⛓️🛡️
## Psalms 107:15
Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love. Let them thank Him for His wonderful acts for people. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 107:16
He breaks gates of bronze. He cuts through bars of iron. 🛡️
## Psalms 107:17
Foolish people suffered because they refused to obey. Their own sins caused their pain. 💔
## Psalms 107:18
They hated all food. They came near the gates of death. 🆘
## Psalms 107:19
Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. He saved them from their distress. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 107:20
He sent His word and healed them. He rescued them from the grave. 📖🛡️
## Psalms 107:21
Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love. Let them thank Him for His wonderful acts for people. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 107:22
Let them offer sacrifices of thanks. Let them tell His works with joy. 😊📣
## Psalms 107:23
Others traveled on ships at sea. They traded goods on the great waters. ⛵🌊
## Psalms 107:24
They saw the LORD’s works. They saw His wonderful acts in the deep sea. 👀🌊
## Psalms 107:25
He spoke and sent a fierce storm. It raised the sea’s waves high. ⛈️
## Psalms 107:26
The waves lifted them toward heaven and dropped them into the depths. Their distress took away their courage. 🌊
## Psalms 107:27
They stumbled like people who drink too much wine. All their skill was useless. ⛵
## Psalms 107:28
Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble. He brought them out of their distress. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 107:29
He made the storm as quiet as a whisper. He quieted the sea’s waves. 🌊
## Psalms 107:30
They were glad that the storm was quiet. He guided them to the harbor they wanted. ⛵😊
## Psalms 107:31
Let them thank the LORD for His faithful love. Let them thank Him for His wonderful acts for people. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 107:32
Let them honor Him when the people gather. Let them praise Him when the elders meet. 👥🙌
## Psalms 107:33
He turns rivers into deserts. He turns springs into dry ground. 💧🏜️
## Psalms 107:34
He turns good farmland into fields of salt. This happens because the people who live there do evil. 🌾
## Psalms 107:35
He turns deserts into pools of water. He turns dry ground into springs. 🏜️💧
## Psalms 107:36
He lets hungry people settle there. They build a city to live in. 🏙️
## Psalms 107:37
They sow fields and plant vines. These give a good harvest. 🌾
## Psalms 107:38
He gives them good things, and they grow greatly in number. He does not let their herds grow smaller. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 107:39
Cruelty, evil, and sorrow can reduce their numbers. These troubles bring them low. 💔
## Psalms 107:40
Then He pours scorn on the rulers. He makes them wander in an empty land without paths. 🏜️
## Psalms 107:41
But He lifts people in need out of suffering. He makes their families grow like flocks. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦🐑
## Psalms 107:42
People who do right see this and are glad. All evil shuts its mouth. 😊
## Psalms 107:43
Let wise people pay attention to these things. Let them think about the LORD’s faithful love. 💭❤️
