## Psalms 80:1
Asaph wrote this song for the music leader, to the tune “The Lilies of the Covenant.” Shepherd of Israel, hear us. You lead Joseph like a flock. You sit as King between the cherubim, heavenly beings. Shine out! 🎵🐑
## Psalms 80:2
Show Your light before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh. Call on Your great power. Come to save us. ✨🆘
## Psalms 80:3
God, restore us. Let Your face shine on us so that we can be safe. ✨🙏
## Psalms 80:4
LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your anger burn against Your people’s prayers? 🆘
## Psalms 80:5
You feed them tears like bread. You give them a full measure of tears to drink. 😢
## Psalms 80:6
You make us fight with our neighbors. Our enemies mock us. 🗣️
## Psalms 80:7
God of Hosts, restore us. Let Your face shine on us so that we can be safe. ✨🙏
## Psalms 80:8
You brought a vine out of Egypt. You drove out the nations and planted it. 🌱
## Psalms 80:9
You cleared the ground for it. Its roots grew, and it filled the land. 🌱
## Psalms 80:10
Its shade covered the mountains. Its branches covered the great cedar trees. 🌳⛰️
## Psalms 80:11
Its branches reached the Sea. Its new shoots reached the River. 🌿🌊
## Psalms 80:12
Why do You break down its walls? Everyone who passes picks its fruit. 🧱❓
## Psalms 80:13
A wild pig from the forest tears it apart. Animals in the fields eat it. 🐗
## Psalms 80:14
God of Hosts, return, we pray! Look down from heaven and see. Care for this vine. 👀🌱
## Psalms 80:15
Care for the root Your right hand planted. Care for the son You raised for Yourself. ✋
## Psalms 80:16
Enemies cut down and burn Your vine. Your people die when You look at them in anger. 🔥
## Psalms 80:17
Let Your hand rest on the man at Your right side. Support the son of man You raised for Yourself. ✋
## Psalms 80:18
Then we will not turn away from You. Give us new life, and we will call on Your name. 🙏❤️
## Psalms 80:19
LORD God of Hosts, restore us. Let Your face shine on us so that we can be safe. ✨🙏
