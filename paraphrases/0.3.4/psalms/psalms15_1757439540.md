## Psalms 15:1
David wrote this song. LORD, who can stay in Your tent? Who can live on Your holy mountain? ⛺⛰️
## Psalms 15:2
The person who lives honestly can stay there. This person does what is right. This person speaks the truth from the heart. ✅❤️
## Psalms 15:3
This person does not tell harmful lies about others. This person does not hurt a neighbor. This person does not insult a friend. 🤝
## Psalms 15:4
This person rejects people who do terrible things. This person honors people who respect the LORD. This person keeps a promise even when it causes personal loss. ✅
## Psalms 15:5
This person lends money without extra charges. This person refuses money offered to harm someone who did nothing wrong. A person who does these things will stand firm forever. 💰⚖️
