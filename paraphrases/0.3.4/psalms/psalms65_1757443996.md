## Psalms 65:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. God, praise waits for You in Zion. We will keep our promises to You. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 65:2
You listen to prayer. All people will come to You. 🙏👂
## Psalms 65:3
When my sins are too strong for me, You forgive our wrong acts. 🤲
## Psalms 65:4
The person You choose and bring near receives good from You. That person lives in Your courts. The goodness of Your house satisfies us. The holiness of Your temple satisfies us too. 🏠❤️
## Psalms 65:5
God who saves us, You answer us with wonderful, right acts. People at every end of the earth hope in You. People beyond the farthest seas hope in You. 🌍🌊
## Psalms 65:6
You formed the mountains by Your power. Strength surrounds You like a belt. ⛰️💪
## Psalms 65:7
You quieted the roar of the seas and their great waves. You quieted the noise of the nations. 🌊
## Psalms 65:8
People far away fear Your wonderful acts. You make dawn and sunset shout with joy. 🌅🌇
## Psalms 65:9
You care for the earth and water it. You make it rich with crops. Your streams are full of water. You supply the land with what it needs and prepare grain for us. 🌾💧
## Psalms 65:10
You soak the rows of soil and level the raised ground. Rain softens the earth. You make its crops grow well. 🌧️🌱
## Psalms 65:11
You fill the year with good things like a crown. Wherever You go, there is plenty. 👑🌾
## Psalms 65:12
Grasslands in the desert overflow with plenty. The hills look as if they wear clothes of joy. 🌿😊
## Psalms 65:13
Flocks cover the fields like clothes. Grain covers the valleys. They shout for joy and sing. 🐑🌾🎵
