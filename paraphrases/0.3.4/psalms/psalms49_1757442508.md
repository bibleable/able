## Psalms 49:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song for the music leader. All people, hear this. Everyone in the world, listen. 🎵👂
## Psalms 49:2
Listen, people with little status and people with high status. Listen, rich people and poor people. 👥
## Psalms 49:3
I will speak wise words. The thoughts of my heart will help you understand. 🗣️💭
## Psalms 49:4
I will listen to a wise saying. I will explain my riddle with harp music. 🎵
## Psalms 49:5
Why must I fear when trouble comes? Why must I fear when people who do evil surround me to take my place? ❓
## Psalms 49:6
They trust their wealth. They boast about their great riches. 💰
## Psalms 49:7
No person can buy a brother’s life back from death. No person can pay God the price for that life. 💰❌
## Psalms 49:8
The price to rescue a life is too high. No payment is ever enough. ❌
## Psalms 49:9
No payment can let a person live forever and keep the body from decay. ⏳
## Psalms 49:10
Everyone can see that wise people die. Foolish people and people without understanding die too. They leave their wealth to others. 💰
## Psalms 49:11
Their graves become their homes forever. They stay there through all generations. This happens even though they give their own names to their lands. 🪦
## Psalms 49:12
Even a rich person cannot live forever. People are like animals that die. ⏳
## Psalms 49:13
This is the end of foolish people. It is also the end of those who follow them and approve their words. *Song direction: Selah.* ⚖️
## Psalms 49:14
They go to the place of the dead like sheep. Death will be their shepherd. People who do right will rule over them in the morning. Their bodies will rot in the place of the dead, far from their grand homes. 🐑
## Psalms 49:15
But God will rescue my life from the place of the dead. He will take me to Himself. *Song direction: Selah.* 🤲❤️
## Psalms 49:16
Do not be afraid when someone grows rich. Do not fear when that person’s house becomes grand. 🏠💰
## Psalms 49:17
When that person dies, he will take nothing with him. His wealth will not follow him down. 💰❌
## Psalms 49:18
During his life, he tells himself that he is happy. People praise you when you succeed. 🗣️
## Psalms 49:19
But he will join his ancestors. They will never see daylight again. 🌑
## Psalms 49:20
A rich person who does not understand is like an animal that dies. 💰
