## Psalms 90:1
Moses, the man of God, prayed this prayer. Lord, You are our home through all generations. 🏠🙏
## Psalms 90:2
Before the mountains came to be, before You made the earth and the world, You were God. You are God forever, without beginning or end. 🌍⛰️
## Psalms 90:3
You return people to dust. You say, “Human beings, return.” 🌬️
## Psalms 90:4
To You, a thousand years are like a day that passes. They are like a few hours of the night. ⏳🌙
## Psalms 90:5
You sweep people away in their sleep. They are like new grass in the morning. 🌿
## Psalms 90:6
In the morning, the grass grows fresh. By evening, it fades and dries up. ☀️🌿
## Psalms 90:7
Your anger destroys us. Your fierce anger fills us with fear. ⚖️
## Psalms 90:8
You put our sins before You. The light of Your presence shows our secret sins. ✨
## Psalms 90:9
All our days pass under Your anger. Our years end with a sigh. ⏳
## Psalms 90:10
Our lives last seventy years, or eighty if we are strong. Yet even their best part is hard work and sorrow. They pass quickly, and we fly away. ⏳
## Psalms 90:11
Who knows the power of Your anger? Your anger is as great as the fear You deserve. ⚖️
## Psalms 90:12
Teach us to count our days. Then we can gain a wise heart. ⏳❤️
## Psalms 90:13
LORD, return! How long will it take? Show care for Your servants. 🆘🙏
## Psalms 90:14
In the morning, satisfy us with Your faithful love. Then we can sing with joy and be glad all our days. 🌅🎵
## Psalms 90:15
Give us as many days of joy as the days You made us suffer. Give us as many years of joy as our years of trouble. 😊
## Psalms 90:16
Show Your work to Your servants. Show Your greatness to their children. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦✨
## Psalms 90:17
Lord our God, let Your kindness rest on us. Make the work of our hands last. Yes, make the work of our hands last! 🤲✅
