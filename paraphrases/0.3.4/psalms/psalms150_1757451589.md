## Psalms 150:1
Praise the LORD! Praise God in His holy place. Praise Him in His mighty heavens. 🙌
## Psalms 150:2
Praise Him for His powerful acts. Praise Him for His excellent greatness. 💪🙌
## Psalms 150:3
Praise Him with the sound of a horn. Praise Him with the harp and lyre. 📯🎵
## Psalms 150:4
Praise Him with tambourine and dance. Praise Him with string instruments and flute. 🎵
## Psalms 150:5
Praise Him with cymbals that clash loudly. Praise Him with cymbals that ring out. 🎵
## Psalms 150:6
Let everything that breathes praise the LORD! Praise the LORD! 🙌
