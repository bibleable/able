## Psalms 92:1
This song is for the Sabbath day. It is good to praise the LORD. Most High, it is good to sing praise to Your name. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 92:2
It is good to tell of Your faithful love in the morning. It is good to tell of Your faithfulness at night. 🌅🌙
## Psalms 92:3
Make music with the harp of ten strings. Play a tune on the lyre. 🎵
## Psalms 92:4
LORD, Your acts make me glad. I sing with joy at the work of Your hands. 😊🎵
## Psalms 92:5
LORD, Your works are so great! Your thoughts are so deep! 💭✨
## Psalms 92:6
A person without sense does not know this. A foolish person does not understand. 💭
## Psalms 92:7
People who do evil grow like grass. They seem to thrive. But they will face destruction forever. 🌿⚖️
## Psalms 92:8
But LORD, You are high above all forever! 🙌
## Psalms 92:9
LORD, Your enemies will surely die. All people who do evil will scatter. ⚖️
## Psalms 92:10
You raise my horn of strength like a wild ox’s horn. You pour fine oil on me. 🐂🫙
## Psalms 92:11
I see my enemies fall. I hear my evil enemies cry in pain. 👀👂
## Psalms 92:12
People who do right will thrive like palm trees. They will grow like cedar trees in Lebanon. 🌴🌲
## Psalms 92:13
They are like trees planted in the LORD’s house. They will grow well in our God’s courts. 🌳🏠
## Psalms 92:14
Even when old, they will still give fruit. They will stay healthy and green. 🌳
## Psalms 92:15
They will say, “The LORD does right. He is my Rock. There is no wrong in Him.” 🪨✅
