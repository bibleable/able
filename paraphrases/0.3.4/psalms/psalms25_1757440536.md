## Psalms 25:1
David wrote this song. LORD, I lift up my whole self to You. 🙏❤️
## Psalms 25:2
My God, I trust You. Do not let me suffer shame. Do not let my enemies celebrate their victory over me. 🛡️
## Psalms 25:3
No one who waits for You will suffer shame. But people who betray others without a reason will face shame. ⚖️
## Psalms 25:4
LORD, show me Your ways. Teach me Your paths. 🛤️
## Psalms 25:5
Guide me in Your truth and teach me. You are the God who saves me. I wait for You all day. 📖🙏
## Psalms 25:6
LORD, remember Your kindness and faithful love. They exist from long ago. ❤️
## Psalms 25:7
Do not remember the sins of my youth or the times I refused to obey. LORD, remember me with Your faithful love. Do this because You are good. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 25:8
The LORD is good and right. So He shows people who sin the right way. 🛤️
## Psalms 25:9
He guides humble people to do right. He teaches them His way. ✅
## Psalms 25:10
The LORD shows love and faithfulness in all His ways. He does this for people who keep His covenant and commands. A covenant is a binding agreement. 🤝📖
## Psalms 25:11
LORD, forgive my great sin to honor Your name. 🙏
## Psalms 25:12
Who respects the LORD? The LORD will teach that person the path to choose. 🛤️
## Psalms 25:13
That person will live well. That person’s descendants will receive the land. 🌳👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 25:14
The LORD shares His secrets with people who respect Him. He makes His covenant known to them. 🤝
## Psalms 25:15
I always look to the LORD. He will free my feet from the net. 👀🛡️
## Psalms 25:16
Turn toward me and be kind. I am lonely and in pain. 🤲😢
## Psalms 25:17
My troubles grow greater inside me. Free me from my distress. 🆘
## Psalms 25:18
Look at my pain and trouble. Forgive all my sins. 🙏
## Psalms 25:19
See how many enemies I have. See how cruelly they hate me. 👀
## Psalms 25:20
Protect my life and rescue me. Do not let me suffer shame. I come to You for safety. 🛡️
## Psalms 25:21
Let honesty and right conduct protect me. I wait for You. ✅
## Psalms 25:22
God, rescue Israel from all its troubles. 🆘🙏
