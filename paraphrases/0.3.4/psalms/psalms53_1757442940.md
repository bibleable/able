## Psalms 53:1
David wrote this Maskil for the music leader, with the music direction Mahalath. A foolish person thinks, “There is no God.” These people are evil. Their ways are terrible. No one does good. 🎵💭
## Psalms 53:2
God looks down from heaven at human beings. He looks for anyone who understands. He looks for anyone who seeks God. 👀🌍
## Psalms 53:3
All of them turn away. Together they become evil. No one does good, not even one. ❌
## Psalms 53:4
Will people who do evil never learn? They destroy my people as easily as they eat bread. They refuse to call on God. 💔
## Psalms 53:5
Great fear comes over them even where there is nothing to fear. God scatters the bones of those who surround you to attack. You bring shame on them because God rejects them. ⚖️
## Psalms 53:6
I long for God to save Israel from Zion! When God brings His captive people back, let the people of Jacob be glad. Let Israel celebrate! 🙌😊
