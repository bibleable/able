## Psalms 9:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. Its tune is “The Death of the Son.” I will thank the LORD with all my heart. I will tell about all Your wonderful acts. 🎵❤️
## Psalms 9:2
I will be glad because of You. God Most High, I will sing praise to Your name. 😊🎵
## Psalms 9:3
When my enemies turn back, they fall and die before You. ⚖️
## Psalms 9:4
You defended me because my cause was right. You sit on Your throne and judge fairly. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 9:5
You corrected the nations. You destroyed people who do evil. You removed their names forever. ⚖️
## Psalms 9:6
The enemy faces ruin forever. You destroyed their cities. People do not even remember them. 🏚️
## Psalms 9:7
But the LORD lives forever. He set up His throne to judge. 👑
## Psalms 9:8
He judges the world fairly. He rules all people with fairness. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 9:9
The LORD is a safe place for people who suffer from cruelty. He protects them like a strong fort in times of trouble. 🛡️
## Psalms 9:10
People who know Your name trust You. LORD, You do not leave people who seek You. 🤝❤️
## Psalms 9:11
Sing praise to the LORD, who lives in Zion. Tell the nations what He does. 🎵📣
## Psalms 9:12
God remembers people who suffer murder. He punishes those who kill them. He does not ignore the cries of people who suffer. 👂⚖️
## Psalms 9:13
LORD, be kind to me. See how my enemies hurt me. Rescue me from the edge of death. 🆘
## Psalms 9:14
Then I can tell everyone why You deserve praise. At the gates of Zion, I will be glad because You saved me. 🙌😊
## Psalms 9:15
The nations fall into the pit they dug. Their feet catch in the net they hid. 🕳️
## Psalms 9:16
The LORD shows who He is through His fair judgments. People who do evil trap themselves by their own acts. *Song direction: Higgaion and Selah.* ⚖️
## Psalms 9:17
People who do evil will return to the place of the dead. This includes all the nations that forget God. ⚖️
## Psalms 9:18
God will not always leave people in need without help. People who suffer from cruelty will not lose their hope forever. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 9:19
LORD, act now! Do not let human beings win against You. Judge the nations before You. ⚖️🌍
## Psalms 9:20
LORD, fill them with fear. Let the nations know that they are only human beings. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌍
