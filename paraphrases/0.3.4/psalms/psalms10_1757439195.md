## Psalms 10:1
LORD, why do You stand far away? Why do You hide when trouble comes? 🆘❓
## Psalms 10:2
Proud people who do evil chase people who need help. Let their own plans trap them. ⚖️
## Psalms 10:3
The person who does evil boasts about what he wants. He praises greedy people. He insults the LORD. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 10:4
He is too proud to seek God. None of his plans include God. 💭❌
## Psalms 10:5
His ways always seem successful. Your judgments are far beyond his concern. He laughs at all his enemies. 😠
## Psalms 10:6
He tells himself, “Nothing will shake me. Trouble will never come to me or my family.” 🗣️
## Psalms 10:7
He curses people, tells lies, and threatens violence. His words hide trouble and a wish to hurt others. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 10:8
He hides near villages and waits. He murders people who do nothing wrong. He secretly watches for people who cannot protect themselves. 👀
## Psalms 10:9
He waits like a lion in thick bushes. He hides to catch people who suffer. He catches them like animals in a net. 🦁
## Psalms 10:10
He crushes his victims. People who cannot protect themselves fall under his strength. 💔
## Psalms 10:11
He tells himself, “God forgot. He hides His face. He never sees.” 🗣️
## Psalms 10:12
LORD, act now! God, raise Your hand to help. Do not forget people who cannot protect themselves. 🆘✋
## Psalms 10:13
Why does the person who does evil reject God? He tells himself, “You will never make me answer for my acts.” ❓
## Psalms 10:14
But You see trouble and sorrow. You act to deal with them. The victim trusts You. You help children who have no father. 👀🤲
## Psalms 10:15
Break the power of the person who does evil, like an arm that breaks. Make him answer for every evil act. Leave none without judgment. ⚖️
## Psalms 10:16
The LORD is King forever. The nations will disappear from His land. 👑🌍
## Psalms 10:17
LORD, You hear what humble people want. You will give them courage. You will listen to them. 👂❤️
## Psalms 10:18
You will defend children who have no father. You will defend people who suffer from cruelty. Then human beings will no longer fill them with fear. 🛡️🧒
