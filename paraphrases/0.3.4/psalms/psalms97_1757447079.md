## Psalms 97:1
The LORD rules! Let the earth celebrate. Let the distant shores be glad. 👑🌍
## Psalms 97:2
Clouds and darkness surround Him. Righteousness and fairness are the base of His throne. ☁️⚖️
## Psalms 97:3
Fire goes before Him. It destroys His enemies on every side. 🔥
## Psalms 97:4
His lightning lights up the world. The earth sees it and shakes. ⚡🌍
## Psalms 97:5
The mountains melt like wax before the LORD. He is the Lord of all the earth. ⛰️
## Psalms 97:6
The heavens declare His righteousness. All people see His glory. ✨
## Psalms 97:7
Everyone who worships images will face shame. They boast about idols. All you gods, worship Him! ❌🙏
## Psalms 97:8
LORD, Zion hears and is glad. Judah’s towns celebrate because of Your judgments. 😊⚖️
## Psalms 97:9
LORD, You are Most High over all the earth. You are far above all gods. 🌍
## Psalms 97:10
People who love the LORD, hate evil! He protects the lives of His holy people. He rescues them from the power of people who do evil. ❤️🛡️
## Psalms 97:11
Light shines on people who do right. Joy comes to people whose hearts are honest. ✨😊
## Psalms 97:12
People who do right, be glad in the LORD. Praise His holy name. 😊🙌
