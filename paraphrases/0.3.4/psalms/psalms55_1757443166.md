## Psalms 55:1
David wrote this Maskil for the music leader, with instruments that have strings. God, listen to my prayer. Do not ignore my request. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 55:2
Pay attention to me and answer. My complaint gives me no rest. I feel deep distress. 😢
## Psalms 55:3
The enemy’s voice and the pressure of evil people trouble me. They bring disaster on me. In anger, they hate me. 🆘
## Psalms 55:4
My heart beats hard inside me. The fear of death attacks me. 💔
## Psalms 55:5
Fear grips me, and I shake. Great terror covers me. 😨
## Psalms 55:6
I said, “I wish I had wings like a dove! Then I can fly away and rest. 🕊️
## Psalms 55:7
I want to escape far away. I want to stay in the desert. *Song direction: Selah.* 🏜️
## Psalms 55:8
I want to hurry to a shelter, far from this fierce storm.” ⛈️
## Psalms 55:9
Lord, confuse their words and divide them. I see violence and fights in the city. 🏙️
## Psalms 55:10
Day and night, they go around its walls. Inside the city there is trouble and a wish to hurt others. 🧱
## Psalms 55:11
Destruction fills the city. Cruelty and lies never leave its streets. 💔
## Psalms 55:12
If an enemy insults me, I can bear it. If an enemy attacks me, I can hide from him. 🛡️
## Psalms 55:13
But you are the one who hurts me. You are a man like me, my companion and close friend. 💔
## Psalms 55:14
We enjoyed time together. We walked with the crowd into God’s house. 👥🏠
## Psalms 55:15
Let death surprise them. Let them go alive into the place of the dead. Evil lives with them in their homes. ⚖️
## Psalms 55:16
But I call to God. The LORD saves me. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 55:17
Morning, noon, and night, I cry out in distress. He hears my voice. ☀️🌙👂
## Psalms 55:18
Many people fight against me. But He rescues me safely from the battle. 🛡️
## Psalms 55:19
God rules from His throne through all ages. He will hear and bring shame on them. They do not change, and they do not fear God. *Song direction: Selah.* 👑⚖️
## Psalms 55:20
My companion attacks his friends. He breaks his covenant, the agreement he made. 🤝❌
## Psalms 55:21
His words are smooth like butter, but his heart wants war. His words are softer than oil, but they are like swords ready to strike. 🗣️⚔️
## Psalms 55:22
Give your heavy load to the LORD. He will support you. He will never let a person who does right fall from His care. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 55:23
But God, You will bring those people down to the pit of destruction. People who murder and deceive will not live even half their days. But I will trust You. ⚖️🙏
