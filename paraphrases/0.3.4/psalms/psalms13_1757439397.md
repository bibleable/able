## Psalms 13:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. LORD, how long? Will You forget me forever? How long will You hide Your face from me? 🆘⏳
## Psalms 13:2
How long must I struggle with my thoughts? How long must I feel sorrow each day? How long will my enemy have power over me? 💭😢
## Psalms 13:3
LORD my God, look at me and answer. Give light to my eyes. Do not let me sleep in death. 🙏✨
## Psalms 13:4
Do not let my enemy say, “I defeated him.” Do not let my enemies celebrate my fall. 🆘
## Psalms 13:5
But I trust Your faithful love. I will be glad because You save me. ❤️😊
## Psalms 13:6
I will sing to the LORD because He treats me well. 🎵🙌
