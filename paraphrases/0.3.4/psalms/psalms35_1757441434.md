## Psalms 35:1
David wrote this song. LORD, oppose the people who oppose me. Fight those who fight me. 🆘
## Psalms 35:2
Take Your large shield and Your small shield. Come now to help me. 🛡️
## Psalms 35:3
Take Your spear and javelin against those who chase me. Tell me, “I am the One who saves you.” 🛡️
## Psalms 35:4
Let the people who want to kill me face shame. Let the people who plan to hurt me turn back in confusion. ⚖️
## Psalms 35:5
Let them be like dry grain husks in the wind. Let the LORD’s angel drive them away. 🌬️
## Psalms 35:6
Let their path be dark and slippery. Let the LORD’s angel chase them. 🌑
## Psalms 35:7
They hid a net to trap me without a cause. They dug a pit to kill me without a reason. 🕳️
## Psalms 35:8
Let sudden ruin come on them. Let their hidden net trap them. Let them fall into the danger they made. ⚖️
## Psalms 35:9
Then I will be glad in the LORD. I will celebrate because He saves me. 😊🙌
## Psalms 35:10
My whole body will say, “LORD, who is like You? You rescue people who suffer from those who attack them. You rescue poor people and people in need from robbers.” 🛡️
## Psalms 35:11
People who want to hurt me come as witnesses. They accuse me of things I know nothing about. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 35:12
I do good to them, but they do evil to me. Their acts leave me deeply alone and sad. 💔
## Psalms 35:13
When they were ill, I wore rough clothes to show grief. I humbled myself and went without food. But my prayers received no answer. 🙏
## Psalms 35:14
I walked about in sorrow as if they were my friend or brother. I bowed in grief as if my mother died. 😢
## Psalms 35:15
But when I fell, they gathered with joy against me. People I did not know attacked me. They never stopped their harmful lies about me. 🗣️
## Psalms 35:16
They mock me like people at a feast who have no respect for God. They grind their teeth at me in anger. 😠
## Psalms 35:17
Lord, how long will You watch? Rescue my precious life from their attacks. They are like lions. 🦁🆘
## Psalms 35:18
Then I will thank You when the great crowd gathers. I will praise You among many people. 👥🙌
## Psalms 35:19
Do not let my enemies celebrate over me without a cause. Do not let people who hate me without reason signal their cruel pleasure. ✋
## Psalms 35:20
They do not speak of peace. They make dishonest plans against people who live quietly in the land. 💭❌
## Psalms 35:21
They open their mouths wide against me. They say, “Aha! We saw it ourselves!” 🗣️
## Psalms 35:22
LORD, You see it. Do not stay silent. Lord, do not stay far from me. 👀🙏
## Psalms 35:23
My God and my Lord, act now! Stand up to defend me and my cause. ⚖️🆘
## Psalms 35:24
LORD my God, judge fairly and show that I am right. Do not let them celebrate my defeat. ⚖️
## Psalms 35:25
Do not let them think, “Aha! This is what we wanted!” Do not let them say, “We swallowed him up!” ✋
## Psalms 35:26
Let people who enjoy my pain face shame and confusion. Let people who make themselves great at my expense face shame and dishonor. ⚖️
## Psalms 35:27
Let the people who want a fair judgment for me shout with joy. Let them always say, “Honor the LORD! He enjoys the well-being of His servant.” 😊🙌
## Psalms 35:28
Then I will tell of Your right acts. I will speak Your praise all day long. 📣🙌
