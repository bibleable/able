## Psalms 129:1
This is a song of ascent. Let Israel say, “They attacked me many times from my youth. 🆘
## Psalms 129:2
They attacked me many times from my youth. But they did not defeat me. 🛡️
## Psalms 129:3
People plow across my back. They make long rows in it. 💔
## Psalms 129:4
The LORD does right. He cuts me free from the ropes of people who do evil.” ⛓️
## Psalms 129:5
Let everyone who hates Zion turn back in shame. ⚖️
## Psalms 129:6
Let them be like grass on a roof. It dries up before it grows. 🌿
## Psalms 129:7
It cannot fill a crop gatherer’s hands. It cannot fill the arms of someone who ties bundles of grain. 🌾
## Psalms 129:8
Let no one who passes say, “Let the LORD give you good. We give you good wishes in the LORD’s name.” ⚖️
