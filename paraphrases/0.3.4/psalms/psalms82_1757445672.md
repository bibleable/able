## Psalms 82:1
Asaph wrote this song. God leads the divine assembly. He judges among the gods. ⚖️
## Psalms 82:2
He says, “How long will you judge unfairly? How long will you favor people who do evil? *Song direction: Selah.* ⚖️
## Psalms 82:3
Defend weak people and children who have no father. Protect the rights of people who suffer pain and cruelty. 🧒🛡️
## Psalms 82:4
Rescue weak people and people in need. Save them from the power of people who do evil. 🛡️
## Psalms 82:5
They do not know or understand. They walk in darkness. All the earth’s foundations shake. 🌍
## Psalms 82:6
I said, ‘You are gods. All of you are sons of the Most High.’ 👑
## Psalms 82:7
But you will die like human beings. You will fall like other rulers.” ⚖️
## Psalms 82:8
God, act now and judge the earth. All the nations belong to You. 🌍⚖️
