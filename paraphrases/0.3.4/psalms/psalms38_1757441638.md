## Psalms 38:1
David wrote this song as a reminder. LORD, do not correct me in Your anger. Do not punish me in Your great anger. 🙏
## Psalms 38:2
Your arrows go deep into me. Your hand presses down on me. 🏹✋
## Psalms 38:3
My whole body is sick because of Your anger. My bones have no rest because of my sin. 😢
## Psalms 38:4
My sins rise over me. They are a load too heavy for me to carry. 💔
## Psalms 38:5
My wounds smell bad and fill with pus. This happens because of my foolish sin. 😢
## Psalms 38:6
I bend over and hang my head. I go around in sorrow all day. 😢
## Psalms 38:7
My lower body is full of pain like fire. No part of my body is healthy. 🔥
## Psalms 38:8
I feel numb and badly crushed. Deep pain in my heart makes me cry out. 💔
## Psalms 38:9
Lord, You see everything I want. You hear my cries of pain. 👀👂
## Psalms 38:10
My heart beats hard. My strength fails. Even the light in my eyes grows dim. 😢
## Psalms 38:11
My loved ones and friends stay away from my disease. My relatives stand far away. 💔
## Psalms 38:12
People who want to kill me set traps. People who want to hurt me speak about my destruction. They plan lies all day. 💭❌
## Psalms 38:13
But I am like a person who cannot hear. I am like a person who cannot speak. I do not open my mouth. 🤫
## Psalms 38:14
I am like a person who cannot hear. I give no reply. 🤫
## Psalms 38:15
LORD, I wait for You. Lord my God, You will answer. ⏳🙏
## Psalms 38:16
I said, “Do not let them celebrate over me. They mock me when my foot slips.” 🆘
## Psalms 38:17
I am close to a fall. My pain is always with me. 😢
## Psalms 38:18
I admit my sin. My sin troubles me. 🙏
## Psalms 38:19
Many people are my enemies without a cause. Many people hate me without a reason. 💔
## Psalms 38:20
I do good to them, but they do evil to me. They attack me because I try to do good. ❌
## Psalms 38:21
LORD, do not leave me. My God, do not stay far from me. 🙏
## Psalms 38:22
Lord, come quickly to help me. You are the One who saves me. 🆘
