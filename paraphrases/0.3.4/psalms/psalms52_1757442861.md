## Psalms 52:1
David wrote this Maskil for the music leader after Doeg the Edomite reported to Saul. Doeg said, “David went to Ahimelech’s house.” Powerful man, why do you boast about evil? God’s faithful love lasts all day. 🎵❤️
## Psalms 52:2
You use your tongue to plan destruction. Your lies cut like a sharp razor. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 52:3
You love evil more than good. You love lies more than true words. *Song direction: Selah.* ❌
## Psalms 52:4
Your dishonest tongue loves every word that destroys people. 🗣️
## Psalms 52:5
But God will bring you to ruin forever. He will seize you and pull you out of your tent. He will remove you from the land of people who are alive. *Song direction: Selah.* ⚖️
## Psalms 52:6
People who do right will see this and feel fear. They will mock the person who does evil. 👀
## Psalms 52:7
They will say, “Look at the man who did not go to God for safety. He trusted his great wealth. He made himself strong by destroying others.” 💰❌
## Psalms 52:8
But I am like a healthy olive tree in God’s house. I trust God’s faithful love forever. 🌳❤️
## Psalms 52:9
I will praise You forever because You acted. Your name is good. I will wait for You among Your holy people. 🙌⏳
