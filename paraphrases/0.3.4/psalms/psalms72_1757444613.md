## Psalms 72:1
This is a song of Solomon. God, give the king Your fairness. Give the king’s son Your right judgment. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 72:2
Let him judge Your people rightly. Let him treat people who suffer fairly. ⚖️
## Psalms 72:3
Let the mountains bring peace to the people. Let the hills bring righteousness. ⛰️🤝
## Psalms 72:4
Let him defend people who suffer. Let him save the children of people in need. Let him crush the person who treats them cruelly. 🧒🛡️
## Psalms 72:5
Let people fear him as long as the sun shines. Let them fear him as long as the moon remains, through all generations. ☀️🌙
## Psalms 72:6
Let him be like rain on fresh-cut grass. Let him be like spring rain that waters the earth. 🌧️🌿
## Psalms 72:7
Let people who do right thrive during his rule. Let there be plenty until the moon is no more. 🌙🌾
## Psalms 72:8
Let him rule from sea to sea. Let him rule from the Euphrates River to the ends of the earth. 🌊🌍
## Psalms 72:9
Let people who move from place to place bow before him. Let his enemies lick the dust. 👑
## Psalms 72:10
Let the kings of Tarshish and distant shores bring gifts to honor him. Let the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts. 👑🎁
## Psalms 72:11
Let all kings bow before him. Let all nations serve him. 🌍👑
## Psalms 72:12
He will rescue people in need who cry for help. He will rescue people who suffer and have no helper. 🆘🛡️
## Psalms 72:13
He will show kindness to poor people and people in need. He will save the lives of people who suffer cruelty. 🤲
## Psalms 72:14
He will rescue them from cruelty and violence. Their lives are precious to him. ❤️
## Psalms 72:15
Let him live long! Let people give him gold from Sheba. Let people always pray for him. Let them speak good of him all day. 👑🙏
## Psalms 72:16
Let the land have plenty of grain, even on the hilltops. Let its fruit trees grow like the forests of Lebanon. Let people in its cities grow in number like grass in a field. 🌾🌳
## Psalms 72:17
Let his name last forever, as long as the sun shines. Let all nations receive good through him. Let them call him blessed, a person who receives good from God. ☀️🌍
## Psalms 72:18
Praise the LORD God, the God of Israel. He alone does wonderful things. 🙌
## Psalms 72:19
Praise His glorious name forever. Let His glory fill all the earth. Amen and amen. Let it be so. 🌍✨
## Psalms 72:20
The prayers of David, Jesse’s son, end here. 🙏
