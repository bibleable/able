## Psalms 133:1
David wrote this song of ascent. It is good and pleasant when brothers live together in peace. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦🤝😊
## Psalms 133:2
This peace is like fine oil on Aaron’s head. The oil flows down his beard. Then the oil flows over the collar of his robe. 🫙🧔👕
## Psalms 133:3
This peace is like dew from Mount Hermon on the mountains of Zion. Dew is small drops of water on the ground. There the LORD gives His gift of life that lasts forever. ⛰️💧🎁
