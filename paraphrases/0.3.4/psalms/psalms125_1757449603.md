## Psalms 125:1
This is a song of ascent. People who trust the LORD are like Mount Zion. It stands firm and lasts forever. ⛰️
## Psalms 125:2
Mountains surround Jerusalem. In the same way, the LORD surrounds His people now and forever. ⛰️🛡️
## Psalms 125:3
Evil rulers will not keep control of the land given to people who do right. Then people who do right will not turn to unfair acts. ⚖️
## Psalms 125:4
LORD, do good to people who are good. Do good to people whose hearts are honest. ❤️
## Psalms 125:5
The LORD will send away people who turn to crooked ways. He will send them away with people who do evil. Let Israel have peace. ⚖️🤝
