## Psalms 139:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. LORD, You examined me. You know me. 🔍❤️
## Psalms 139:2
You know when I sit down and when I stand up. You understand my thoughts from far away. 🪑🧍💭
## Psalms 139:3
You examine where I go and where I rest. You know everything I do. 🚶🛌
## Psalms 139:4
LORD, You know every word before I say it. 🗣️💭
## Psalms 139:5
You surround me from behind and from the front. You put Your hand on me. ✋
## Psalms 139:6
What You know is too wonderful for me to understand. It is beyond what I can understand. 💭✨
## Psalms 139:7
Where can I go to escape Your Spirit? Where can I run to get away from You? 🕊️
## Psalms 139:8
If I go up to heaven, You are there. If I lie down in the place of the dead, You are there. ⬆️⬇️
## Psalms 139:9
If I fly like the first light of dawn, You are there. If I live beyond the farthest sea, You are there. 🌅🌊
## Psalms 139:10
Even there, Your hand will guide me. Your right hand will hold me firmly. ✋🤝
## Psalms 139:11
I can say, “Surely the dark will hide me. The light around me will turn into night.” 🌑
## Psalms 139:12
But even the dark is not dark to You. The night shines like the day to You. To You, darkness is like light. 🌙☀️
## Psalms 139:13
You made every part inside me. You put me together inside my mother’s womb, where a baby grows before birth. 👶❤️
## Psalms 139:14
I praise You. The way You made me fills me with wonder and deep respect. Your works are wonderful. I know this very well. 🙌✨
## Psalms 139:15
You saw my bones as You put me together in secret. You formed me in a hidden place, like the deep parts of the earth. 🔍🌎
## Psalms 139:16
You saw my body before it was complete. You wrote all my days in Your book. You planned those days before my first day began. 📖🗓️
## Psalms 139:17
God, Your thoughts are precious to me. There are so many of them! 💭❤️
## Psalms 139:18
Your thoughts are more than all the grains of sand. I cannot count them all. When I wake up, I am still with You. 🏖️❤️
## Psalms 139:19
God, kill the people who do evil! You people who want to kill others, go away from me! ✋⚖️
## Psalms 139:20
They tell lies about You. Your enemies use Your name without respect. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 139:21
LORD, I hate the people who hate You. I hate the people who act against You. 😠
## Psalms 139:22
I hate them completely. I see them as my enemies. 😠
## Psalms 139:23
God, examine me and know my heart. Test me and know my worries. 🔍❤️
## Psalms 139:24
See if I do anything that offends You. Guide me on the path that lasts forever. 🛤️➡️
