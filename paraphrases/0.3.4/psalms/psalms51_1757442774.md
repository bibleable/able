## Psalms 51:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. Nathan the prophet came to David after David had sex with Bathsheba, another man’s wife. God, be kind to me because of Your faithful love. Because of Your great care, remove my sins. 🙏❤️
## Psalms 51:2
Wash away the wrong I did. Make me clean from my sin. 💧
## Psalms 51:3
I know the wrong I did. My sin is always before me. 💭
## Psalms 51:4
I sinned against You, against You alone. I did what You call evil. So Your words are right. Your judgment has no fault. ⚖️
## Psalms 51:5
Sin was with me when I was born. Sin was with me from the start of my life inside my mother. 👶
## Psalms 51:6
You want truth deep inside me. You teach me wisdom in that hidden place. ❤️
## Psalms 51:7
Make me pure with the hyssop plant. Then I will be clean. Wash me, and I will be whiter than snow. 🌿💧
## Psalms 51:8
Let me hear sounds of joy again. Let the bones You crushed be glad. 😊
## Psalms 51:9
Turn Your face away from my sins. Remove all the wrong I did. 🙏
## Psalms 51:10
God, create a clean heart in me. Give me a right spirit again. ❤️
## Psalms 51:11
Do not send me away from You. Do not take Your Holy Spirit from me. 🙏
## Psalms 51:12
Give me back the joy of Your rescue. Give me a spirit that wants to obey, and keep me strong. 😊🤲
## Psalms 51:13
Then I will teach Your ways to people who do wrong. People who sin will return to You. 🛤️
## Psalms 51:14
God who saves me, rescue me from the guilt of bloodshed. Then I will sing about Your right acts. 🙏🎵
## Psalms 51:15
Lord, open my lips. I will speak Your praise. 🗣️🙌
## Psalms 51:16
I am ready to bring a sacrifice, but it does not give You pleasure. You take no pleasure in burnt offerings. 🙏
## Psalms 51:17
The sacrifice You want is a broken spirit. God, You will not reject a heart that is broken and sorry for sin. 💔❤️
## Psalms 51:18
Choose to make Zion prosper. Build up Jerusalem’s walls. 🧱
## Psalms 51:19
Then right sacrifices will give You pleasure. You will welcome whole burnt offerings. People will offer bulls on Your altar. An altar is a place for sacrifices to God. 🙏
