## Psalms 113:1
Praise the LORD! All of you who serve the LORD, praise Him. Praise the name of the LORD. 🙌🎵
## Psalms 113:2
Praise the name of the LORD now and forever. ⏰❤️
## Psalms 113:3
People praise the name of the LORD from the east to the west. The sun rises in the east and sets in the west. 🌅🌇🙌
## Psalms 113:4
The LORD is above all nations. His greatness is above the heavens. 🌍✨
## Psalms 113:5
Who is like the LORD our God? He rules from His seat high above us. 👑⬆️
## Psalms 113:6
He lowers Himself to look at the heavens and the earth. 👀🌎
## Psalms 113:7
He lifts poor people from the dust. He lifts people who need help from the piles of waste. 🤝⬆️
## Psalms 113:8
He gives these people seats with leaders. They sit with the leaders of His people. 👑🪑
## Psalms 113:9
He gives a home and children to a woman who cannot have children. He makes her a happy mother. Praise the LORD! 🏠👶😊
