## Psalms 147:1
Praise the LORD! It is good to sing praise to our God. Praise for Him is pleasant and lovely. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 147:2
The LORD builds up Jerusalem. He gathers Israel’s people who live far from home. 🏙️
## Psalms 147:3
He heals people with broken hearts. He wraps their wounds. 💔🤲
## Psalms 147:4
He counts the stars. He calls each one by name. ⭐
## Psalms 147:5
Our Lord is great and very powerful. His understanding has no limit. 💪
## Psalms 147:6
The LORD supports humble people. But He throws people who do evil to the ground. 🤲⚖️
## Psalms 147:7
Sing to the LORD with thanks. Make music to our God on the harp. 🎵
## Psalms 147:8
He covers the sky with clouds. He prepares rain for the earth. He makes grass grow on the hills. ☁️🌧️🌿
## Psalms 147:9
He gives food to animals. He gives food to young ravens when they call. 🐦
## Psalms 147:10
The strength of a horse does not give Him pleasure. He does not take pleasure in a person’s legs. 🐎
## Psalms 147:11
The LORD takes pleasure in people who fear Him. They hope in His faithful love. ❤️
## Psalms 147:12
Jerusalem, honor the LORD! Zion, praise your God! ⛰️🙌
## Psalms 147:13
He makes the bars of your gates strong. He gives good things to the children inside you. 🚪🧒
## Psalms 147:14
He gives peace at your borders. He fills you with the finest wheat. 🤝🌾
## Psalms 147:15
He sends His command to the earth. His word runs quickly. 🌍
## Psalms 147:16
He spreads snow like wool. He scatters frost like ashes. ❄️
## Psalms 147:17
He throws hail like small stones. Who can stand against His icy wind? ❄️🌬️
## Psalms 147:18
He sends His word and melts the ice. He releases His winds, and the waters flow. 💧🌬️
## Psalms 147:19
He tells His word to Jacob. He gives His rules and judgments to Israel. 📖
## Psalms 147:20
He does not do this for any other nation. They do not know His judgments. Praise the LORD! 🌍🙌
