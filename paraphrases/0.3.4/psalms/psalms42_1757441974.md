## Psalms 42:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song, called a Maskil, for the music leader. A deer longs for streams of water. God, I long for You in the same way. 🦌💧
## Psalms 42:2
I thirst for God, the living God. When can I come before Him? 🙏❓
## Psalms 42:3
My tears are like my food day and night. All day people ask me, “Where is your God?” 😢
## Psalms 42:4
I pour out my feelings as I remember these things. I walked with the crowd to God’s house. I led the joyful group. We shouted with joy and praise. 👥🎵
## Psalms 42:5
Why am I so sad inside? Why am I so troubled? Put your hope in God. I will praise Him again for the help of His presence. ❤️🙏
## Psalms 42:6
My God, I feel deep sadness inside. So I remember You from the land of Jordan. I remember You from the peaks of Hermon and from Mount Mizar. ⛰️🙏
## Psalms 42:7
Deep water calls to deep water through the roar of Your waterfalls. All Your waves roll over me. 🌊
## Psalms 42:8
By day, the LORD sends His faithful love. At night, His song is with me. It is a prayer to the God who gives me life. ☀️🌙🎵
## Psalms 42:9
I say to God my Rock, “Why do You forget me? Why must I live in sorrow because my enemy treats me cruelly?” 🪨😢
## Psalms 42:10
My enemies’ insults feel like my bones break. All day they say to me, “Where is your God?” 🗣️💔
## Psalms 42:11
Why am I so sad inside? Why am I so troubled? Put your hope in God. I will praise Him again. He is my Savior and my God. 🙏❤️
