## Psalms 24:1
David wrote this song. The earth and everything in it belong to the LORD. The world and everyone who lives in it belong to Him. 🌍
## Psalms 24:2
He set the earth on the seas. He made it firm over the waters. 🌊🌍
## Psalms 24:3
Who can go up the LORD’s hill? Who can stand in His holy place? ⛰️❓
## Psalms 24:4
The person whose acts are clean and whose heart is pure can stand there. This person does not worship an idol. This person does not make false promises. ✅❤️
## Psalms 24:5
This person will receive good from the LORD. God, who saves this person, will declare this person right. 🎁⚖️
## Psalms 24:6
These are the people who seek Him. God of Jacob, these people seek Your face. *Song direction: Selah.* 🙏
## Psalms 24:7
Gates, lift up your heads! Ancient doors, open high! Let the King of Glory enter! 🚪👑
## Psalms 24:8
Who is this King of Glory? He is the LORD, strong and powerful. He is the LORD, powerful in battle. 👑💪
## Psalms 24:9
Gates, lift up your heads! Ancient doors, open high! Let the King of Glory enter! 🚪👑
## Psalms 24:10
Who is this King of Glory? He is the LORD of Hosts, the Lord of armies. He is the King of Glory. *Song direction: Selah.* 👑
