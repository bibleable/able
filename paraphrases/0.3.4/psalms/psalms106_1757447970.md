## Psalms 106:1
Praise the LORD! Thank the LORD because He is good. His faithful love lasts forever. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 106:2
Who can describe all the LORD’s powerful acts? Who can fully tell His praise? 💪❓
## Psalms 106:3
People who act fairly receive good from God. They do what is right at all times. ⚖️
## Psalms 106:4
LORD, remember me when You show kindness to Your people. Come and save me. 🙏
## Psalms 106:5
Then I can see Your chosen people prosper. I can share Your nation’s joy. I can praise You with the people who belong to You. 😊🙌
## Psalms 106:6
We sin like our ancestors. We do wrong and act with evil. ❌
## Psalms 106:7
Our ancestors in Egypt did not understand Your wonderful acts. They did not remember Your great kindness. They refused to obey by the sea, at the Red Sea. 🌊
## Psalms 106:8
Yet He saved them to honor His name. He made His power known. 🛡️💪
## Psalms 106:9
He commanded the Red Sea, and it dried up. He led them through the deep water as through a desert. 🌊🏜️
## Psalms 106:10
He saved them from the power of people who hated them. He rescued them from the enemy. 🛡️
## Psalms 106:11
The water covered their enemies. Not one enemy remained. 🌊
## Psalms 106:12
Then they believed His promises. They sang His praise. 🎵
## Psalms 106:13
But they soon forgot His works. They did not wait for His advice. 💭❌
## Psalms 106:14
In the wilderness, they wanted food very badly. They tested God in the desert. 🏜️
## Psalms 106:15
He gave them what they asked for. But He sent a disease that made them waste away. ⚖️
## Psalms 106:16
In the camp, they envied Moses. They also envied Aaron, the LORD’s holy one. ⛺
## Psalms 106:17
The earth opened and swallowed Dathan. It covered Abiram’s group. 🌍
## Psalms 106:18
Fire burned through their group. Flames destroyed the people who did evil. 🔥
## Psalms 106:19
At Horeb, they made an image of a calf from melted metal. They worshiped it. ❌
## Psalms 106:20
They traded their glorious God for the image of an ox that eats grass. 🐂
## Psalms 106:21
They forgot God who saved them. He did great things in Egypt. 💭
## Psalms 106:22
He did wonderful works in the land of Ham. He did acts at the Red Sea that filled them with wonder and fear. 🌊
## Psalms 106:23
God said He would destroy them. But Moses, whom God chose, stood before Him like someone who guards a gap in a wall. Moses turned His anger away, so He did not destroy them. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 106:24
They rejected the pleasant land. They did not believe His promise. ❌
## Psalms 106:25
They complained in their tents. They did not listen to the LORD’s voice. ⛺
## Psalms 106:26
So He raised His hand and made a firm promise. He would make them fall in the wilderness. ⚖️
## Psalms 106:27
He would scatter their descendants among the nations. He would spread them through other lands. 🌍
## Psalms 106:28
They joined in worship of Baal of Peor. They ate sacrifices offered to gods with no life. ❌
## Psalms 106:29
Their acts made the LORD angry. A deadly disease spread among them. ⚖️
## Psalms 106:30
But Phinehas stood up and acted. The deadly disease stopped. 🛑
## Psalms 106:31
God counted this as a right act for Phinehas. This judgment lasts through all future generations. ✅
## Psalms 106:32
They made the LORD angry at the waters of Meribah. Moses suffered trouble because of them. 💧
## Psalms 106:33
They refused to obey His Spirit. Moses spoke without enough care. 🗣️
## Psalms 106:34
They did not destroy the peoples as the LORD commanded. ❌
## Psalms 106:35
Instead, they mixed with the nations. They learned those nations’ customs. 🌍
## Psalms 106:36
They worshiped their idols. The idols became a trap for them. ❌
## Psalms 106:37
They sacrificed their sons and daughters to demons. 💔
## Psalms 106:38
They shed the innocent blood of their sons and daughters. They sacrificed these children to Canaan’s idols. The blood made the land unclean. 💔
## Psalms 106:39
Their acts made them unclean. They were unfaithful like people who sell themselves for sex. ❌
## Psalms 106:40
So the LORD’s anger burned against His people. He hated the people who belonged to Him. ⚖️
## Psalms 106:41
He gave them into the nations’ power. People who hated them ruled them. 💔
## Psalms 106:42
Their enemies treated them cruelly. They put them under their power. 💔
## Psalms 106:43
He rescued them many times. But they were determined to refuse to obey. They sank down in their sin. 🆘
## Psalms 106:44
Yet He heard their cry. He saw their distress. 👂❤️
## Psalms 106:45
He remembered His covenant with them. His great, faithful love moved Him to hold back punishment. A covenant is a binding agreement. 🤝❤️
## Psalms 106:46
He made all the people who held them as prisoners show them care. 🤲
## Psalms 106:47
LORD our God, save us. Gather us from the nations. Then we can thank Your holy name and find joy in Your praise. 🌍🙏
## Psalms 106:48
Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, forever and ever! Let all the people say, “Amen!” Praise the LORD! 🙌
