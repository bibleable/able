## Psalms 126:1
This is a song of ascent. When the LORD brought Zion’s prisoners back, we were like people in a dream. 💭😊
## Psalms 126:2
Our mouths laughed. Our tongues shouted with joy. The nations said, “The LORD does great things for them.” 😊🌍
## Psalms 126:3
The LORD does great things for us. We are full of joy. 😊🙌
## Psalms 126:4
LORD, bring our prisoners back. Restore them like streams that flow in the Negev. 💧🙏
## Psalms 126:5
People who sow seeds with tears will gather crops with shouts of joy. 😢🌾😊
## Psalms 126:6
A person goes out with tears and a trail of seed. That person will surely return with shouts of joy and bundles of grain. 🌾😊
