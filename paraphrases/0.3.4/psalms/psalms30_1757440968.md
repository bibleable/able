## Psalms 30:1
David wrote this song to dedicate the temple to God. LORD, I will honor You because You lifted me up. You did not let my enemies celebrate my defeat. 🏠🙌
## Psalms 30:2
LORD my God, I cried to You for help. You healed me. 🙏❤️
## Psalms 30:3
LORD, You pulled me up from the place of the dead. You kept me from the grave. 🛡️
## Psalms 30:4
People who belong to the LORD, sing to Him. Praise His holy name. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 30:5
His anger lasts only a short time. His kindness lasts a lifetime. Tears can stay through the night. But joy comes in the morning. 🌙🌅😊
## Psalms 30:6
When life went well, I said, “Nothing will ever shake me.” 🗣️
## Psalms 30:7
LORD, Your kindness made my mountain stand strong. But when You hid Your face, I felt great fear. ⛰️
## Psalms 30:8
LORD, I called to You. I begged my Lord for kindness. 🙏
## Psalms 30:9
I said, “What good comes from my death? What good comes if I go to the grave? Will the dust praise You? Will it tell about Your faithfulness? ❓
## Psalms 30:10
LORD, hear me and be kind. LORD, help me.” 🆘🙏
## Psalms 30:11
You changed my sorrow into a joyful dance. You removed my rough clothes of grief. You dressed me in joy. 😊
## Psalms 30:12
So my heart will sing praise to You. I will not stay silent. LORD my God, I will thank You forever. 🎵🙌
