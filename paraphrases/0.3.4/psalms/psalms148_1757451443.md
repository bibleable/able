## Psalms 148:1
Praise the LORD! Praise Him from heaven. Praise Him in the highest places. ☁️🙌
## Psalms 148:2
All His angels, praise Him. All His heavenly armies, praise Him. 🙌
## Psalms 148:3
Sun and moon, praise Him. All bright stars, praise Him. ☀️🌙⭐
## Psalms 148:4
Highest heavens, praise Him. Waters above the skies, praise Him. ☁️💧
## Psalms 148:5
Let them praise the LORD’s name. He gave a command and created them. 🙌
## Psalms 148:6
He set them firm forever. He gave a law that will never pass away. ✅
## Psalms 148:7
Great sea creatures and ocean depths, praise the LORD from the earth. 🌊
## Psalms 148:8
Lightning, hail, snow, and clouds, praise Him. Powerful winds that obey His word, praise Him. ⚡❄️🌬️
## Psalms 148:9
Mountains and hills, praise Him. Fruit trees and cedars, praise Him. ⛰️🌳
## Psalms 148:10
Wild animals and cattle, praise Him. Small creatures on the ground and birds in flight, praise Him. 🐂🐦
## Psalms 148:11
Earth’s kings and all peoples, praise Him. All leaders and rulers, praise Him. 👑🌍
## Psalms 148:12
Young men and young women, praise Him. Old people and young people together, praise Him. 👥
## Psalms 148:13
Let them praise the LORD’s name. His name alone is high above all. His beauty is above earth and heaven. 🌍✨
## Psalms 148:14
He raises a horn of power for His people. This brings praise to all His holy people, Israel, who are near Him. Praise the LORD! 💪🙌
