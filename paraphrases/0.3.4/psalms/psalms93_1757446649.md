## Psalms 93:1
The LORD rules! He wears greatness like a robe. He wears strength like clothes and armor. The world stands firm. Nothing can move it. 👑🌍
## Psalms 93:2
Your throne stands firm from long ago. You exist from eternity, without a beginning. 👑
## Psalms 93:3
LORD, the floodwaters rise. The rivers raise their voices. The seas lift their great waves. 🌊
## Psalms 93:4
The LORD on high is greater than the roar of many waters. He is greater than the sea’s mighty waves. 🌊💪
## Psalms 93:5
Your instructions are completely sure. LORD, holiness makes Your house beautiful forever. 📖🏠
