## Psalms 20:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. I pray that the LORD answers you when trouble comes. I pray that the God of Jacob protects you. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 20:2
I pray that He sends you help from His holy place. I pray that He supports you from Zion. 🤲⛰️
## Psalms 20:3
I pray that He remembers all your gifts. I pray that He accepts the offerings you burn for Him. *Song direction: Selah.* 🎁
## Psalms 20:4
I pray that He gives you what your heart wants. I pray that He makes all your plans succeed. ❤️
## Psalms 20:5
Let us shout with joy when you win. Let us raise a flag in the name of our God. I pray that the LORD answers all your requests. 🚩😊
## Psalms 20:6
Now I know that the LORD saves the king He chose. He answers him from His holy heaven. He uses His strong right hand to save him. 👑✋
## Psalms 20:7
Some people trust in chariots. Others trust in horses. But we trust in the name of the LORD our God. 🐎🛡️
## Psalms 20:8
They collapse and fall. But we rise and stand firm. 🧍
## Psalms 20:9
LORD, save the king. Answer us when we call. 👑🙏
