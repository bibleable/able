## Psalms 76:1
Asaph wrote this song for the music leader, with instruments that have strings. People know God in Judah. His name is great in Israel. 🎵
## Psalms 76:2
His tent is in Salem. His home is in Zion. ⛺
## Psalms 76:3
There He broke the arrows of fire. He broke shields, swords, and other weapons of war. *Song direction: Selah.* 🏹⚔️
## Psalms 76:4
You shine with light. You are greater than mountains full of wild animals. ✨⛰️
## Psalms 76:5
Brave soldiers lie dead, and people take their goods. They sleep their last sleep. No strong soldier can lift a hand. ⚔️
## Psalms 76:6
God of Jacob, You spoke against them. Both horses and riders lie still in shock. 🐎
## Psalms 76:7
You alone deserve our fear. When You are angry, who can stand before You? ⚖️
## Psalms 76:8
You announced judgment from heaven. The earth felt fear and became still. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 76:9
God acted to judge. He acted to save all the humble people on earth. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛡️
## Psalms 76:10
Even human anger will bring You praise. You will wear the survivors of that anger like clothes. 🙌
## Psalms 76:11
Make promises to the LORD your God and keep them. Let all the nearby lands bring gifts to the One they must fear. 🎁✅
## Psalms 76:12
He breaks the spirits of rulers. The kings of the earth fear Him. 👑
