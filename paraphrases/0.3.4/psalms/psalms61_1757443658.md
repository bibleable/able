## Psalms 61:1
David wrote this song for the music leader, with instruments that have strings. God, hear my cry. Pay attention to my prayer. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 61:2
From the ends of the earth, I call to You when my heart grows weak. Lead me to the rock higher than I am. 🪨🆘
## Psalms 61:3
You are my safe place. You are a strong tower against the enemy. 🏰🛡️
## Psalms 61:4
Let me live in Your tent forever. Let me find safety under Your wings. *Song direction: Selah.* ⛺🪽
## Psalms 61:5
God, You heard my promises. You gave me the share kept for people who respect Your name. 🎁
## Psalms 61:6
Add days to the king’s life. Let his years stretch through many generations. 👑⏳
## Psalms 61:7
Let him rule before God forever. Send Your faithful love and truth to protect him. 👑🛡️
## Psalms 61:8
Then I will always sing praise to Your name. I will keep my promises day by day. 🎵✅
