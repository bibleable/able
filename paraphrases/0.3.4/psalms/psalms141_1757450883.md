## Psalms 141:1
David wrote this song. LORD, I call to You. Come quickly to me. Hear my voice when I call. 🙏👂
## Psalms 141:2
Let my prayer rise before You like incense. Let my raised hands be like the evening offering. *Word help: Incense gives sweet smoke when it burns.* 🤲
## Psalms 141:3
LORD, put a guard over my mouth. Watch the door of my lips. 🗣️🛡️
## Psalms 141:4
Do not let my heart turn toward evil. Do not let me join evil acts with people who do wrong. Do not let me eat their rich food. ✋
## Psalms 141:5
Let a person who does right strike and correct me with faithful love. This is like oil for my head. Do not let me refuse it. I always pray against the acts of people who do evil. 🤲
## Psalms 141:6
When their rulers are thrown down from the cliffs, people will listen to my words. My words are pleasant. 👂
## Psalms 141:7
Our bones lie scattered at the mouth of the place of the dead. They are like soil that someone plows and breaks apart. 💔
## Psalms 141:8
But GOD the Lord, my eyes look firmly at You. I come to You for safety. Do not leave my life without protection. 👀🛡️
## Psalms 141:9
Keep me from the traps they set. Keep me from the bait of people who do evil. 🛡️
## Psalms 141:10
Let people who do evil fall into their own nets. Let me pass safely by. 🛡️
