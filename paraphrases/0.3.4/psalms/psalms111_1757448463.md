## Psalms 111:1
Praise the LORD! I will thank Him with all my heart. I will thank Him when people who do right meet together. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 111:2
The LORD’s works are great. Everyone who enjoys them thinks deeply about them. 💭
## Psalms 111:3
His work is beautiful and great. His righteousness lasts forever. ✨
## Psalms 111:4
He makes people remember His wonderful acts. The LORD is kind and caring. ❤️
## Psalms 111:5
He gives food to people who fear Him. He remembers His covenant forever. A covenant is a binding agreement. 🍞🤝
## Psalms 111:6
He shows His people the power of His works. He gives them lands that belonged to the nations. 🌍
## Psalms 111:7
His hands act with truth and fairness. All His commands deserve trust. ⚖️📖
## Psalms 111:8
His commands stand firm forever. He gives them in truth and rightness. 📖✅
## Psalms 111:9
He sends rescue to His people. He sets His covenant to last forever. His name is holy and fills us with wonder and fear. 🤝🛡️
## Psalms 111:10
Wisdom begins with fear of the LORD. Everyone who obeys His commands gains deep understanding. His praise lasts forever! 📖🙌
