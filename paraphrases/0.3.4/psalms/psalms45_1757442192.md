## Psalms 45:1
The sons of Korah wrote this love song, called a Maskil, for the music leader. Its tune is “The Lilies.” A noble subject fills my heart as I speak my verses to the king. My tongue is like the pen of a skilled writer. 🎵👑
## Psalms 45:2
You are the most handsome of men. Kind words flow from your lips. So God gives you good things forever. 👑
## Psalms 45:3
Powerful soldier, put your sword at your side. Show your royal greatness and beauty. ⚔️👑
## Psalms 45:4
Ride out in your greatness and win. Stand for truth, humility, and fairness. Let your right hand show your wonderful acts. ⚖️
## Psalms 45:5
Your arrows go through the hearts of the king’s enemies. The nations fall under your feet. 🏹
## Psalms 45:6
God, Your throne lasts forever. You rule Your kingdom fairly, as a king holds his royal rod. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 45:7
You love what is right and hate evil. So God, your God, chooses you above your companions. He pours the oil of joy on you. 🫙😊
## Psalms 45:8
Your clothes smell of myrrh, aloes, and cassia, which give pleasant scents. Harp music from palaces of ivory makes you glad. 🎵
## Psalms 45:9
Kings’ daughters are among your honored women. The queen stands at your right side. She wears gold from Ophir. 👑✨
## Psalms 45:10
Daughter, listen carefully. Think about my words. Forget your own people and your father’s house. 👂
## Psalms 45:11
Then the king will desire your beauty. Bow to him because he is your lord. 👑
## Psalms 45:12
The people of Tyre will come with a gift. Rich people will seek your kindness. 🎁
## Psalms 45:13
The princess looks beautiful in her room. Threads of gold decorate her dress. 👗✨
## Psalms 45:14
People lead her to the king in colorful clothes. They bring her companions, young women who never had sex, before you. 👗👑
## Psalms 45:15
People lead them in with joy. They enter the king’s palace. 🏰😊
## Psalms 45:16
Your sons will take the place of your ancestors. You will make them princes through all the land. 👑
## Psalms 45:17
I will make people remember your name through all generations. So the nations will praise you forever. 🌍📣
