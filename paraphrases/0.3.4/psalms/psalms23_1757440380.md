## Psalms 23:1
David wrote this song. The LORD cares for me like a shepherd cares for sheep. I have everything I need. 🐑❤️
## Psalms 23:2
He lets me lie down in fields of green grass. He leads me beside quiet water. 🌿💧
## Psalms 23:3
He gives me new life inside. He guides me on the right paths to honor His name. 🛤️❤️
## Psalms 23:4
Even in a valley dark with the shadow of death, I will not fear evil. You are with me. Your rod and Your staff comfort me. They are the tools of a shepherd. 🛡️
## Psalms 23:5
You prepare a meal for me in front of my enemies. You put oil on my head. You fill my cup until it flows over. 🍽️🫙
## Psalms 23:6
Your goodness and kindness will follow me every day of my life. I will live in the LORD’s house forever. 🏠❤️
