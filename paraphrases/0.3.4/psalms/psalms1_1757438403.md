## Psalms 1:1
The person who refuses advice from people who do evil receives good from God. This person does not join people who sin. This person does not sit with people who mock others. 🚶✅
## Psalms 1:2
Instead, this person loves the LORD’s law. This person thinks about His law day and night. 📖☀️🌙
## Psalms 1:3
This person is like a tree beside streams of water. The tree gives fruit at the right time. Its leaves do not dry up. The person succeeds in all that he does. 🌳💧🍎
## Psalms 1:4
People who do evil are different. They are like the dry husks of grain that the wind blows away. 🌬️
## Psalms 1:5
When God judges, people who do evil will not stand before Him successfully. People who sin will not belong among people who do right. ⚖️
## Psalms 1:6
The LORD protects the path of people who do right. But the path of people who do evil leads to their destruction. 🛤️🛡️
