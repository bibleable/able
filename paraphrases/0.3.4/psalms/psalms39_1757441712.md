## Psalms 39:1
David wrote this song for the music leader, for Jeduthun. I said, “I will watch my conduct so that my words do not cause sin. I will keep my mouth shut like an animal with a muzzle while people who do evil are near.” 🎵🤫
## Psalms 39:2
I stayed quiet and still. I did not even speak good words. But my sorrow grew. 😢
## Psalms 39:3
My heart grew hot inside me. As I thought, a fire seemed to burn. Then I spoke. 💭🔥
## Psalms 39:4
I said, “LORD, show me the end of my life. Show me how many days I have. Help me know how short my life is. ⏳
## Psalms 39:5
You made my days as short as the width of a hand. My whole lifetime is almost nothing before You. Even at our best, we are only a breath. *Song direction: Selah.* ✋🌬️
## Psalms 39:6
People move about like shadows. They rush around for nothing. They store up wealth, but do not know who will take it away. 💰
## Psalms 39:7
So now, Lord, what do I wait for? My hope is in You. 🙏
## Psalms 39:8
Rescue me from all my sins. Do not let foolish people mock me. 🛡️
## Psalms 39:9
I stay silent. I do not open my mouth because You are the One who acts. 🤫
## Psalms 39:10
Take Your punishment away from me. The force of Your hand destroys me. 🆘
## Psalms 39:11
You correct and punish people for their sin. You destroy what they treasure, like a moth that eats cloth. Every person is only a breath. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌬️
## Psalms 39:12
LORD, hear my prayer and my cry for help. Do not ignore my tears. I live with You as a foreigner and a visitor. All my ancestors lived this way too. 🙏😢
## Psalms 39:13
Turn Your gaze away from me so that I can be glad again. Let this happen before I leave this life and am gone.” 🙏
