## Psalms 32:1
David wrote this song, called a Maskil. The person whose sins God forgives receives good from God. God covers that person’s sins. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 32:2
The person whose sin the LORD does not count against him receives good from Him. That person does not hide lies inside. ❤️
## Psalms 32:3
When I kept silent about my sin, my bones grew weak. I cried in pain all day. 😢
## Psalms 32:4
Your hand pressed heavily on me day and night. My strength dried up like water in the summer heat. *Song direction: Selah.* ☀️
## Psalms 32:5
Then I admitted my sin to You. I did not hide the wrong I did. I said, “I will tell the LORD my sins.” You forgave my sin and its guilt. *Song direction: Selah.* 🙏❤️
## Psalms 32:6
So let all faithful people pray to You while they can find You. When great floods rise, the waters will not reach them. 🙏🌊
## Psalms 32:7
You are my place to hide. You protect me from trouble. You surround me with songs about rescue. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛡️🎵
## Psalms 32:8
I will teach you the way to go. I will give you advice and watch over you. 🛤️👀
## Psalms 32:9
Do not be like a horse or mule that does not understand. You must use a bit and bridle to bring these animals near. 🐎
## Psalms 32:10
People who do evil have many sorrows. But faithful love surrounds the person who trusts in the LORD. ❤️
## Psalms 32:11
People who do right, be glad in the LORD! Everyone whose heart is honest, shout with joy! 😊🙌
