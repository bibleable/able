## Psalms 57:1
David wrote this Miktam for the music leader when he escaped from Saul into a cave. Its tune is “Do Not Destroy.” God, be kind to me! I come to You for safety. I will hide under Your wings until the danger passes. 🎵🪽
## Psalms 57:2
I cry to God Most High. He carries out His purpose for me. 🙏
## Psalms 57:3
He reaches down from heaven and saves me. He corrects the people who step on me. *Song direction: Selah.* God sends His faithful love and truth. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 57:4
I live among people who are like hungry lions. Their teeth are like spears and arrows. Their tongues are like sharp swords. 🦁⚔️
## Psalms 57:5
God, show Your greatness above the heavens. Let Your glory cover all the earth. 🌍✨
## Psalms 57:6
They set a net to catch my feet. I felt deep sadness. They dug a pit in front of me, but they fell into it themselves! *Song direction: Selah.* 🕳️
## Psalms 57:7
God, my heart stands firm. My heart stands firm. I will sing and make music. ❤️🎵
## Psalms 57:8
Wake up, my glory! Harp and lyre, wake up! I will wake the dawn with music. 🎵🌅
## Psalms 57:9
Lord, I will praise You among the nations. I will sing Your praise among the people. 🌍🎵
## Psalms 57:10
Your faithful love reaches to the heavens. Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ☁️❤️
## Psalms 57:11
God, show Your greatness above the heavens. Let Your glory cover all the earth. 🌍✨
