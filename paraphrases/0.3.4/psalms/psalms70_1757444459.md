## Psalms 70:1
David wrote this song for the music leader as a reminder. God, hurry to rescue me! LORD, hurry to help me! 🎵🆘
## Psalms 70:2
Let people who want to kill me face shame and confusion. Let people who wish me harm turn back in shame. ⚖️
## Psalms 70:3
Let people who mock me with “Aha!” turn back because of their shame. 🗣️
## Psalms 70:4
Let everyone who seeks You be glad in You. Let people who love Your rescue always say, “God is great!” 😊🙌
## Psalms 70:5
But I am poor and need help. God, hurry to me. You are my Helper and Rescuer. LORD, do not delay. 🆘🙏
