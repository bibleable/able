## Psalms 73:1
Asaph wrote this song. God is surely good to Israel. He is good to people whose hearts are pure. ❤️
## Psalms 73:2
But my feet almost fell. My steps nearly slipped. 🆘
## Psalms 73:3
I envied proud people. I saw how well people who do evil lived. 👀
## Psalms 73:4
They do not struggle in death. Their bodies have plenty of food. 🍽️
## Psalms 73:5
They do not carry other people’s heavy loads. They do not suffer like others. 💭
## Psalms 73:6
So they wear pride like a necklace. Violence covers them like clothes. ❌
## Psalms 73:7
Their wealth leads to sin. The wishes of their hearts have no limits. 💰
## Psalms 73:8
They mock people and speak to hurt them. In pride, they threaten to treat people cruelly. 🗣️
## Psalms 73:9
Their mouths speak against heaven. Their tongues speak proudly through all the earth. 🗣️🌍
## Psalms 73:10
So their people return to this place. They drink plenty of water there. 💧
## Psalms 73:11
People who do evil say, “How can God know? Does the Most High know anything?” 🗣️
## Psalms 73:12
Look at these people who do evil! They seem free from care. Their wealth keeps growing. 💰
## Psalms 73:13
I think, “I keep my heart pure for nothing. I wash my hands to show innocence, but it seems useless. 💭
## Psalms 73:14
I suffer all day. Every morning brings punishment.” 😢
## Psalms 73:15
If I chose to speak that way, I would betray Your children. 💭
## Psalms 73:16
I tried to understand all this. But it troubled me deeply. 💭
## Psalms 73:17
Then I entered God’s holy place. There I understood how those people will end. 🏠
## Psalms 73:18
You put them on slippery ground. You throw them down into ruin. ⚖️
## Psalms 73:19
Destruction comes on them so suddenly! Terrors sweep them away completely. ⚖️
## Psalms 73:20
Lord, when You act, You reject their image. They are like a dream that disappears when a person wakes. 💭
## Psalms 73:21
My heart was full of grief. Pain pierced me deep inside. 💔
## Psalms 73:22
I did not understand and knew nothing. Before You, I was like an animal without understanding. 💭
## Psalms 73:23
Yet I am always with You. You hold my right hand. 🤝
## Psalms 73:24
You guide me with Your advice. Later, You will welcome me in glory. 🛤️✨
## Psalms 73:25
Who do I have in heaven except You? On earth, I want no one except You. ❤️
## Psalms 73:26
My body and heart can fail. But God gives my heart strength. He is my share forever. ❤️💪
## Psalms 73:27
People far from You will die. You destroy everyone who is unfaithful to You. ⚖️
## Psalms 73:28
But it is good for me to be near God. I make the Lord GOD my safe place. Then I can tell about all Your works. 🛡️📣
