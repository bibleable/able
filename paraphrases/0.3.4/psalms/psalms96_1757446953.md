## Psalms 96:1
Sing a new song to the LORD. All the earth, sing to the LORD. 🌍🎵
## Psalms 96:2
Sing to the LORD and praise His name. Tell about His rescue day after day. 🎵📣
## Psalms 96:3
Tell the nations about His glory. Tell all people about His wonderful acts. 🌍📣
## Psalms 96:4
The LORD is great and deserves great praise. People must fear Him more than all gods. 🙌
## Psalms 96:5
All the gods of the nations are idols. But the LORD made the heavens. ☁️
## Psalms 96:6
Beauty and royal greatness stand before Him. Strength and beauty fill His holy place. ✨
## Psalms 96:7
Families of the nations, honor the LORD. Honor the LORD for His glory and strength. 🌍🙌
## Psalms 96:8
Give the LORD the glory that His name deserves. Bring an offering and enter His courts. 🎁
## Psalms 96:9
Worship the LORD in the beauty of His holiness. All the earth, tremble before Him. 🌍🙏
## Psalms 96:10
Tell the nations, “The LORD rules!” The world stands firm, and nothing can move it. He will judge the people fairly. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 96:11
Let the heavens be glad and the earth celebrate. Let the sea and everything in it make a great sound. 🌍🌊
## Psalms 96:12
Let the fields and everything in them celebrate. Then all the forest’s trees will sing with joy. 🌳🎵
## Psalms 96:13
They will sing before the LORD because He comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world rightly. He will judge the people faithfully. 🌍⚖️
