## Psalms 146:1
Praise the LORD! My soul, praise the LORD. 🙌
## Psalms 146:2
I will praise the LORD all my life. I will sing praise to my God as long as I exist. 🎵
## Psalms 146:3
Do not trust rulers. Do not trust human beings who die and cannot save. 👑
## Psalms 146:4
When a person’s spirit leaves, that person returns to the ground. On that day, the person’s plans end. 🌬️
## Psalms 146:5
The person whose help comes from Jacob’s God receives good from Him. This person hopes in the LORD his God. ❤️
## Psalms 146:6
He made heaven, earth, the sea, and everything in them. He stays faithful forever. 🌍🌊
## Psalms 146:7
He gives fairness to people who suffer cruelty. He gives food to hungry people. The LORD sets prisoners free. ⚖️🍞
## Psalms 146:8
The LORD opens blind people’s eyes. He lifts people who carry heavy loads. He loves people who do right. 👀🤲
## Psalms 146:9
The LORD protects people from other countries. He supports children who have no father and women whose husbands died. But He ruins the ways of people who do evil. 🛡️
## Psalms 146:10
Zion, the LORD your God rules forever. He rules through all generations. Praise the LORD! 👑🙌
