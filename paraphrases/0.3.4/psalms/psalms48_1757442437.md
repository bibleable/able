## Psalms 48:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song. The LORD is great. He deserves great praise in His city, on His holy mountain. ⛰️🙌
## Psalms 48:2
Mount Zion is high and beautiful, like the peaks of Zaphon. It gives joy to the whole earth. It is the city of the great King. ⛰️👑
## Psalms 48:3
God is in the city’s strong forts. He shows that He is its safe place. 🏰🛡️
## Psalms 48:4
Look! The kings gathered. They moved forward together. 👑
## Psalms 48:5
They saw the city and felt great shock. They ran away in fear. 👀
## Psalms 48:6
They shook with fear. Their pain was like a woman’s pain when she gives birth. 😨
## Psalms 48:7
You sent a wind from the east. It wrecked the ships of Tarshish. 🌬️⛵
## Psalms 48:8
We heard about these things, and now we see them in our God’s city. It is the city of the LORD of Hosts. God will make it stand forever. *Song direction: Selah.* 🏙️🛡️
## Psalms 48:9
God, inside Your temple, we think about Your faithful love. 🏠❤️
## Psalms 48:10
God, Your name reaches the ends of the earth. Your praise reaches there too. Your right hand acts with complete fairness. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 48:11
Mount Zion is glad because of Your judgments. The towns of Judah celebrate. ⛰️😊
## Psalms 48:12
Walk all around Zion. Count its towers. 🚶🏰
## Psalms 48:13
Look carefully at its walls. Walk through its forts. Then tell the next generation about them. 🧱📣
## Psalms 48:14
This God is our God forever. He will guide us until death. 🛤️❤️
