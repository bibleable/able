## Psalms 112:1
Praise the LORD! The person who fears the LORD receives good from Him. This person greatly enjoys His commands. 🙌📖
## Psalms 112:2
This person’s descendants will be powerful in the land. The children of people who do right will receive good from God. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 112:3
Wealth and riches fill this person’s house. This person’s righteousness lasts forever. 🏠
## Psalms 112:4
Light rises in the darkness for people who do right. They are kind, caring, and fair. ✨
## Psalms 112:5
Things go well for the person who gives and lends freely. This person deals fairly in business. 🤲⚖️
## Psalms 112:6
This person will stand firm forever. People will remember the person who does right forever. 🛡️
## Psalms 112:7
This person does not fear bad news. This person’s heart stands firm in trust of the LORD. ❤️
## Psalms 112:8
This person’s heart is secure and has no fear. At last, this person will see the defeat of enemies. 🛡️
## Psalms 112:9
This person gives freely to poor people. This person’s righteousness lasts forever. This person’s horn of power will rise high in honor. 🤲
## Psalms 112:10
The person who does evil will see this and feel grief. He will grind his teeth and waste away. The wishes of people who do evil will fail. ⚖️
