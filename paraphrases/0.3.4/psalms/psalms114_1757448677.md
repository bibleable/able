## Psalms 114:1
Israel left Egypt. Jacob’s family left people who spoke a foreign language. 🚶
## Psalms 114:2
Judah became God’s holy place. Israel became His kingdom. 👑
## Psalms 114:3
The sea saw this and fled. The Jordan River turned back. 🌊
## Psalms 114:4
The mountains jumped like rams. The hills jumped like lambs. ⛰️🐑
## Psalms 114:5
Sea, why did you flee? Jordan, why did you turn back? 🌊❓
## Psalms 114:6
Mountains, why did you jump like rams? Hills, why did you jump like lambs? ⛰️❓
## Psalms 114:7
Earth, tremble before the Lord. Tremble before the God of Jacob. 🌍
## Psalms 114:8
He turned rock into a pool of water. He turned hard flint into a spring. 🪨💧
