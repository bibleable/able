## Psalms 108:1
David wrote this song. God, my heart stands firm. I will sing and make music with my whole self. ❤️🎵
## Psalms 108:2
Harp and lyre, wake up! I will wake the dawn with music. 🎵🌅
## Psalms 108:3
LORD, I will praise You among the nations. I will sing Your praise among the people. 🌍🎵
## Psalms 108:4
Your faithful love reaches beyond the heavens. Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ☁️❤️
## Psalms 108:5
God, show Your greatness above the heavens. Let Your glory cover all the earth. 🌍✨
## Psalms 108:6
Answer us and save us with Your right hand. Rescue the people You love. ✋❤️
## Psalms 108:7
God speaks from His holy place. He says, “I will win! I will divide Shechem. I will share out the Valley of Succoth. 👑
## Psalms 108:8
Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine. Ephraim is like My helmet. Judah is like My royal rod. 👑
## Psalms 108:9
Moab is like My bowl for washing. I throw My sandal on Edom. I shout in victory over Philistia.” 👑
## Psalms 108:10
Who will bring me to the city with strong walls? Who will lead me to Edom? 🧱❓
## Psalms 108:11
God, did You not reject us? Will You no longer go out with our armies? ❓
## Psalms 108:12
Help us against the enemy. Human help is useless. 🆘
## Psalms 108:13
With God, we will act bravely. He will step on our enemies. 💪
