## Psalms 27:1
David wrote this song. The LORD is my light and the One who saves me. Who will I fear? The LORD protects my life like a strong fort. Who will frighten me? ✨🛡️
## Psalms 27:2
People who do evil came to tear me apart. But these enemies fell down. 🛡️
## Psalms 27:3
Even if an army surrounds me, I will not be afraid. Even if war begins against me, I will still trust God. 🛡️
## Psalms 27:4
I ask the LORD for one thing. I want to live in His house every day of my life. I want to look at the LORD’s beauty. I want to seek Him in His temple. 🏠❤️
## Psalms 27:5
When trouble comes, He will hide me in His shelter. He will keep me safe under His tent. He will put me high on a rock. ⛺🪨
## Psalms 27:6
Then I will lift my head above the enemies around me. At His tent, I will offer sacrifices with shouts of joy. I will sing and make music to the LORD. 🎵😊
## Psalms 27:7
LORD, hear me when I call. Be kind and answer me. 🙏👂
## Psalms 27:8
My heart says, “Seek His face.” LORD, I will seek Your face. ❤️🙏
## Psalms 27:9
Do not hide Your face from me. Do not turn me, Your servant, away in anger. You help me. God who saves me, do not leave me or reject me. 🆘
## Psalms 27:10
Even if my father and mother leave me, the LORD will welcome me. 🤲❤️
## Psalms 27:11
LORD, teach me Your way. My enemies treat me cruelly. Lead me on a level path. 🛤️
## Psalms 27:12
Do not let my enemies do what they want to me. People tell lies against me. They threaten violence. 🛡️
## Psalms 27:13
But I am sure that I will see the LORD’s goodness while I am alive. ❤️
## Psalms 27:14
Wait patiently for the LORD. Be strong and have courage. Wait patiently for the LORD! ⏳💪
