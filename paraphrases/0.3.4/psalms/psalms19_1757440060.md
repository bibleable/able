## Psalms 19:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. The heavens tell us about God’s greatness. The skies show the work of His hands. 🌌🙌
## Psalms 19:2
Day after day, they tell their message. Night after night, they make knowledge clear. ☀️🌙
## Psalms 19:3
They do not use speech or language. No one hears a sound from them. 🤫
## Psalms 19:4
Yet their message reaches all the earth. Their words reach the ends of the world. God sets up a tent for the sun in the heavens. 🌍☀️
## Psalms 19:5
The sun comes out like a bridegroom who leaves his room. It is like a champion eager to run a race. ☀️🏃
## Psalms 19:6
The sun rises at one end of the heavens. It travels to the other end. Nothing escapes its heat. ☀️
## Psalms 19:7
The LORD’s law is perfect and gives new life inside us. The LORD’s instructions deserve our trust. They make people with little knowledge wise. 📖❤️
## Psalms 19:8
The LORD’s rules are right and give joy to the heart. The LORD’s commands shine brightly and help our eyes see. 📖✨
## Psalms 19:9
Respect for the LORD is pure and lasts forever. The LORD’s judgments are true. All of them are right. ⚖️
## Psalms 19:10
They are worth more than gold, even much pure gold. They are sweeter than honey, even fresh honey from the honeycomb. ✨🍯
## Psalms 19:11
Your words warn me, Your servant. People who obey them receive a great reward. 📖🎁
## Psalms 19:12
Who can recognize all their own mistakes? Make me clean from faults that I do not know about. 🔍
## Psalms 19:13
Keep me, Your servant, from sins that I choose on purpose. Do not let these sins control me. Then I will be without blame and free from great sin. 🛡️
## Psalms 19:14
LORD, accept my words and my thoughts with pleasure. You are my Rock and my Rescuer. 🪨❤️
