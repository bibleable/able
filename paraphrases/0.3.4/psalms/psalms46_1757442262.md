## Psalms 46:1
The sons of Korah wrote this song for the music leader. Its music direction is Alamoth. God is our safe place and our strength. He is always here to help when trouble comes. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 46:2
So we will not be afraid, even if the earth changes. We will not fear if mountains fall into the deep sea. ⛰️🌊
## Psalms 46:3
We will not fear if the waters roar and foam. We will not fear if the mountains shake as the waters rise. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌊⛰️
## Psalms 46:4
A river flows through the city of God and gives it joy. This is the holy home of the Most High. 💧🏙️
## Psalms 46:5
God is in the city, so it will stand firm. God will help it at dawn. 🌅🛡️
## Psalms 46:6
Nations rage, and kingdoms fall. When God raises His voice, the earth melts. 🌍
## Psalms 46:7
The LORD of Hosts, the Lord of armies, is with us. The God of Jacob is our strong fort. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛡️
## Psalms 46:8
Come and see the LORD’s work. He brings destruction on the earth. 👀
## Psalms 46:9
He stops wars through all the earth. He breaks bows and spears. He burns shields in the fire. 🏹🔥
## Psalms 46:10
God says, “Be still and know that I am God. The nations will honor Me. All the earth will honor Me.” 🌍🙌
## Psalms 46:11
The LORD of Hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our strong fort. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛡️
