## Psalms 124:1
David wrote this song of ascent. Let Israel declare what follows. Think of our lives without the LORD on our side. 🛡️
## Psalms 124:2
Suppose the LORD was not on our side when people attacked us. 🆘
## Psalms 124:3
Their anger burned against us. Then they would swallow us alive. 💔
## Psalms 124:4
The floods would cover us. The rushing stream would flow over us. 🌊
## Psalms 124:5
The fierce waters would sweep us away. 🌊
## Psalms 124:6
Praise the LORD! He does not give us to their teeth as food. 🙌
## Psalms 124:7
We escape like a bird from a bird catcher’s trap. The net breaks, and we get free. 🐦
## Psalms 124:8
Our help is in the LORD’s name. He made heaven and earth. 🌍🛡️
