## Psalms 110:1
David wrote this song. The LORD said to my Lord, “Sit at My right side. Stay there until I make Your enemies a place to rest Your feet.” 👑
## Psalms 110:2
The LORD sends Your powerful royal rod out from Zion. He says, “Rule among Your enemies.” 👑
## Psalms 110:3
Your people will gladly serve You on Your day of battle. They will wear holy beauty. Your youth is like dew from the birth of dawn. 🌅💧
## Psalms 110:4
The LORD makes a firm promise and will not change His mind. He says, “You are a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.” ✅
## Psalms 110:5
The Lord is at Your right side. He will crush kings on the day of His anger. 👑⚖️
## Psalms 110:6
He will judge the nations and pile up the dead. He will crush rulers across many lands. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 110:7
He will drink from a stream beside the road. Then He will lift His head high. 💧
