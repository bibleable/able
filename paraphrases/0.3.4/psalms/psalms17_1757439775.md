## Psalms 17:1
This is David’s prayer. LORD, hear my request for what is right. Listen to my cry. Hear my prayer. I do not tell lies. 🙏👂
## Psalms 17:2
Show that I am right. Look at the facts fairly. ⚖️
## Psalms 17:3
You examined my heart. You came to me at night. You tested me and found no evil. I decided not to sin with my words. 🔍💭
## Psalms 17:4
Your words help me avoid the violent acts of other people. 📖✅
## Psalms 17:5
I stay on Your paths. My feet do not slip. 🛤️
## Psalms 17:6
God, I call to You because You will answer me. Turn Your ear toward me. Hear my words. 🙏👂
## Psalms 17:7
Show Your wonderful, faithful love. Your right hand saves people who come to You for safety from their enemies. ✋❤️
## Psalms 17:8
Protect me as carefully as the center of Your eye. Hide me under Your wings, like a bird that protects its young. 👁️🪽
## Psalms 17:9
Protect me from people who do evil and attack me. Protect me from enemies who surround me and want to kill me. 🛡️
## Psalms 17:10
They close their hearts to kindness. They speak as if they are better than everyone else. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 17:11
They follow our tracks and now surround us. They watch for a chance to throw us to the ground. 👀
## Psalms 17:12
They are like a lion eager to catch an animal. They are like a young lion that hides and waits to attack. 🦁
## Psalms 17:13
LORD, act now and face them! Bring them down. Use Your sword to rescue me from people who do evil. 🆘⚔️
## Psalms 17:14
LORD, use Your hand to save me from people whose only share is in this life. Give Your precious people enough food. Give their children enough too. Let them leave extra food for the next generation. 🤲👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 17:15
But I will do right and see Your face. When I wake, Your presence will satisfy me. ❤️😊
