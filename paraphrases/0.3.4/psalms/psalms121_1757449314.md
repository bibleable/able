## Psalms 121:1
This is a song of ascent. I lift my eyes to the hills. Where does my help come from? 👀⛰️
## Psalms 121:2
My help comes from the LORD. He made heaven and earth. 🌍🛡️
## Psalms 121:3
He will not let your foot slip. The One who protects you will not fall asleep. 🛡️
## Psalms 121:4
Look! The One who protects Israel will never fall asleep. He will not sleep. 👀
## Psalms 121:5
The LORD keeps you safe. The LORD is the shade at your right side. 🛡️
## Psalms 121:6
The sun will not hurt you during the day. The moon will not hurt you at night. ☀️🌙
## Psalms 121:7
The LORD will guard you from all evil. He will protect your life. 🛡️
## Psalms 121:8
The LORD will watch over you when you go out and come in. He will do this now and forever. 🚶❤️
