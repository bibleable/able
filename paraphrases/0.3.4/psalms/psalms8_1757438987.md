## Psalms 8:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. Its music direction is Gittith. LORD, our Lord, Your name is great through all the earth! Your greatness is above the heavens. 🎵🌍
## Psalms 8:2
You use praise from children and babies against Your enemies. Their praise stops the enemy who wants revenge. 👶🙌
## Psalms 8:3
I look at Your heavens, the work of Your fingers. I see the moon and the stars that You put in place. 🌙⭐
## Psalms 8:4
Why do You think about human beings? Why do You care for people? 💭❤️
## Psalms 8:5
You made people a little lower than the angels. You gave people greatness and honor, like a crown. 👑
## Psalms 8:6
You gave people authority over the things You made. You put everything under their control. 🌍
## Psalms 8:7
This includes all sheep and cattle. It also includes wild animals. 🐑🐂
## Psalms 8:8
It includes the birds in the sky and the fish in the sea. It includes everything that swims in the seas. 🐦🐟🌊
## Psalms 8:9
LORD, our Lord, Your name is great through all the earth! 🌍🙌
