## Psalms 40:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. I waited patiently for the LORD. He turned toward me and heard my cry. ⏳👂
## Psalms 40:2
He lifted me out of a pit of despair and sticky mud. He put my feet on a rock. He made my steps firm. 🪨🛡️
## Psalms 40:3
He gave me a new song to praise our God. Many people will see this and fear Him. They will trust the LORD. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 40:4
The person who trusts the LORD receives good from Him. This person does not turn to proud people. This person does not join those who turn to lies. ✅
## Psalms 40:5
LORD my God, You do many wonderful things. You make many plans for us. No one compares to You. I want to tell about Your acts and plans, but they are too many to count. 💭🙌
## Psalms 40:6
You did not want sacrifices and gifts. Instead, You opened my ears. You did not require burnt offerings or offerings for sin. 👂
## Psalms 40:7
Then I said, “Here I am. I come to You. The scroll, a rolled sheet for writing, contains words about me. 📜
## Psalms 40:8
My God, I love to do what You want. Your law is in my heart.” 📖❤️
## Psalms 40:9
I tell about right conduct when the great crowd gathers. LORD, You know that I do not keep my lips closed. 📣
## Psalms 40:10
I do not hide Your right acts in my heart. I tell of Your faithfulness and how You save. I do not hide Your faithful love and truth from the great crowd. 📣❤️
## Psalms 40:11
LORD, do not hold back Your kindness from me. Your faithful love and truth will always protect me. 🛡️❤️
## Psalms 40:12
Troubles too many to count surround me. My sins catch up with me, and I cannot see. They are more than the hairs on my head. My courage fails. 🆘
## Psalms 40:13
LORD, choose to rescue me. LORD, hurry to help me. 🙏
## Psalms 40:14
Let people who want to kill me face shame and confusion. Let people who wish me harm turn back in shame. ⚖️
## Psalms 40:15
Let people who say, “Aha!” to mock me face shock at their own shame. ⚖️
## Psalms 40:16
Let everyone who seeks You be glad in You. Let people who love Your rescue always say, “The LORD is great!” 😊🙌
## Psalms 40:17
But I am poor and need help. Lord, think of me. You are my Helper and Rescuer. My God, do not delay. 🆘🙏
