## Psalms 123:1
This is a song of ascent. I lift my eyes to You. You sit as King in heaven. 👀👑
## Psalms 123:2
Servants watch their master’s hand. A woman servant watches her mistress’s hand. In the same way, we watch the LORD our God until He shows us kindness. 👀🤲
## Psalms 123:3
LORD, be kind to us! Be kind! People treat us with much scorn. 🙏
## Psalms 123:4
Proud people mock us so much. They treat us as worthless. 💔
