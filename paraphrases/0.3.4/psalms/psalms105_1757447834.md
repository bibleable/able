## Psalms 105:1
Thank the LORD and call on His name. Tell the nations about His acts. 🙌🌍
## Psalms 105:2
Sing to Him. Sing His praise. Tell of all His wonderful acts. 🎵📣
## Psalms 105:3
Be proud of His holy name. Let people who seek the LORD be glad. 😊
## Psalms 105:4
Seek the LORD and His strength. Always seek His face. 🙏
## Psalms 105:5
Remember His wonderful acts. Remember His great works and the judgments He announced. 💭
## Psalms 105:6
You are descendants of His servant Abraham. You are children of Jacob, the people He chose. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 105:7
He is the LORD our God. His judgments reach all the earth. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 105:8
He remembers His covenant forever. This is the promise He commanded for a thousand generations. A covenant is a binding agreement. 🤝
## Psalms 105:9
He made that covenant with Abraham. He made a firm promise to Isaac. 🤝
## Psalms 105:10
He confirmed it as a law for Jacob. He made it a covenant that lasts forever for Israel. 📖
## Psalms 105:11
He said, “I will give you the land of Canaan. It will be the share you receive.” 🌳🎁
## Psalms 105:12
At that time, they were very few. They were strangers in the land. 👥
## Psalms 105:13
They wandered from nation to nation. They moved from one kingdom to another. 🚶
## Psalms 105:14
He let no one treat them cruelly. He warned kings for their sake. 👑🛡️
## Psalms 105:15
He said, “Do not touch the people I chose! Do not hurt My prophets!” ✋
## Psalms 105:16
He sent a time with no food on the land. He cut off all their food supplies. 🌾
## Psalms 105:17
He sent Joseph ahead of them. People sold Joseph as a slave. 🚶
## Psalms 105:18
They hurt his feet with metal chains. They put an iron collar around his neck. ⛓️
## Psalms 105:19
Then the event Joseph predicted happened. The LORD’s word proved Joseph right. ✅
## Psalms 105:20
The king sent an order to release him. The ruler of the people set him free. 👑
## Psalms 105:21
He put Joseph in charge of his house. He made him ruler over everything he owned. 🏠
## Psalms 105:22
Joseph had authority to instruct the king’s rulers as he wished and teach wisdom to the king’s elders. 📖
## Psalms 105:23
Then Israel came into Egypt. Jacob lived in the land of Ham. 🚶
## Psalms 105:24
The LORD gave His people many children. They became more numerous than their enemies. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 105:25
He turned their enemies’ hearts to hate His people. They made secret plans against His servants. 💭❌
## Psalms 105:26
He sent His servant Moses. He sent Aaron, whom He chose. 🚶
## Psalms 105:27
They showed His miraculous signs among those people. They did wonderful things in the land of Ham. ✨
## Psalms 105:28
He sent darkness, and the land became dark. Yet they refused His words. 🌑
## Psalms 105:29
He turned their water into blood. He made their fish die. 🐟
## Psalms 105:30
Frogs filled their land. Frogs even entered the kings’ rooms. 🐸
## Psalms 105:31
He spoke, and crowds of insects came. Gnats spread through their country. 🪰
## Psalms 105:32
He sent hail instead of rain. Lightning flashed through their land. ⚡
## Psalms 105:33
He struck their vines and fig trees. He broke the trees in their country. 🌳
## Psalms 105:34
He spoke, and locusts came. Young locusts came in numbers too great to count. 🌾
## Psalms 105:35
They ate every plant in the land. They ate everything that grew from the soil. 🌱
## Psalms 105:36
He struck down all the firstborn in their land. They were the first fruits of their parents’ strength. 💔
## Psalms 105:37
He brought Israel out with silver and gold. No one among His tribes stumbled. 💰
## Psalms 105:38
Egypt was glad when they left. The people of Egypt were afraid of Israel. 🛡️
## Psalms 105:39
He spread a cloud to cover them. He gave fire to light the night. ☁️🔥
## Psalms 105:40
They asked, and He brought quail for food. He satisfied them with bread from heaven. 🐦🍞
## Psalms 105:41
He opened a rock, and water rushed out. It flowed like a river through the desert. 🪨💧
## Psalms 105:42
He remembered His holy promise to His servant Abraham. 🤝
## Psalms 105:43
He brought His people out with joy. His chosen people shouted with joy. 😊
## Psalms 105:44
He gave them the lands of other nations. They received the crops that other people worked to grow. 🌾
## Psalms 105:45
He did this to let them keep His rules and obey His laws. Praise the LORD! 📖🙌
