## Psalms 43:1
God, show that I am right. Defend my cause against a nation that does not honor You. Rescue me from people who are dishonest and unfair. ⚖️🛡️
## Psalms 43:2
You are the God who gives me safety. Why do You reject me? Why must I live in sorrow because my enemy treats me cruelly? 🆘😢
## Psalms 43:3
Send Your light and Your truth to guide me. Let them bring me to Your holy mountain. Let them bring me to Your home. ✨⛰️
## Psalms 43:4
Then I will go to God’s altar. God is my greatest joy. My God, I will praise You with the harp. 🎵😊
## Psalms 43:5
Why am I so sad inside? Why am I so troubled? Put your hope in God. I will praise Him again. He is my Savior and my God. 🙏❤️
