## Psalms 31:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. LORD, I come to You for safety. Never let me suffer shame. Save me because You do right. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 31:2
Turn Your ear toward me. Come quickly to rescue me. Be my rock of safety and the strong fort that saves me. 🪨🏰
## Psalms 31:3
You are my rock and my fort. Lead me and guide me to honor Your name. 🪨🛤️
## Psalms 31:4
Free me from the net they set for me. You are my safe place. 🛡️
## Psalms 31:5
I put my spirit in Your hands. LORD, God of truth, You rescued me. 🤲
## Psalms 31:6
I hate people who hold firmly to worthless idols. But I trust in the LORD. ❌🙏
## Psalms 31:7
I will be glad because of Your faithful love. You see my pain. You know the deep distress inside me. ❤️
## Psalms 31:8
You do not give me to the enemy. You put my feet in an open place. 🛡️
## Psalms 31:9
LORD, be kind to me because I am in trouble. Sorrow makes my eyes weak. My body and my inner life grow weak too. 😢
## Psalms 31:10
Grief fills my life. Cries of pain fill my years. My sin drains my strength. My bones grow weak. 💔
## Psalms 31:11
My enemies treat me with shame. My neighbors do so even more. My friends are afraid of me. They run away when they see me in the street. 😢
## Psalms 31:12
People forget me like someone who is dead. I am like a broken pot. 💔
## Psalms 31:13
I hear many people tell harmful lies about me. Fear is all around me. They join together and plan to kill me. 🗣️
## Psalms 31:14
But LORD, I trust You. I say, “You are my God.” 🙏❤️
## Psalms 31:15
My days are in Your hands. Rescue me from my enemies and the people who chase me. 🤲🛡️
## Psalms 31:16
Look with kindness on me, Your servant. Save me by Your faithful love. ❤️
## Psalms 31:17
LORD, I call to You. Do not let me suffer shame. Let people who do evil face shame. Let them lie silent in the place of the dead. ⚖️
## Psalms 31:18
Silence the lips that tell lies. These proud people insult those who do right. They treat them as worthless. ✋
## Psalms 31:19
Your goodness is so great! You store it for people who respect You. Before other people, You give it to those who come to You for safety. 🎁❤️
## Psalms 31:20
You hide them safely with You from the plans of other people. Your shelter protects them from words that accuse them. 🛡️
## Psalms 31:21
Praise the LORD! He showed me His faithful love in a city that enemies surrounded. 🏙️❤️
## Psalms 31:22
In fear I said, “You can no longer see me!” But You heard my cry for kindness. You heard me when I called for help. 👂🙏
## Psalms 31:23
All His holy people, love the LORD. The LORD protects people who are faithful. But He fully repays people who are proud. ❤️⚖️
## Psalms 31:24
Everyone who hopes in the LORD, be strong. Have courage! 💪
