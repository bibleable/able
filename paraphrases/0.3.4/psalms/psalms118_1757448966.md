## Psalms 118:1
Thank the LORD because He is good. His faithful love lasts forever. 🙌❤️
## Psalms 118:2
Let Israel say, “His faithful love lasts forever.” ❤️
## Psalms 118:3
Let Aaron’s family say, “His faithful love lasts forever.” ❤️
## Psalms 118:4
Let people who fear the LORD say, “His faithful love lasts forever.” ❤️
## Psalms 118:5
In trouble, I called to the LORD. He answered and set me free. 🙏🛡️
## Psalms 118:6
The LORD is on my side. I will not fear. What can a human being do to me? 🛡️
## Psalms 118:7
The LORD is on my side and helps me. So I will see the defeat of people who hate me. 🛡️
## Psalms 118:8
It is better to find safety in the LORD than to trust a human being. 🛡️
## Psalms 118:9
It is better to find safety in the LORD than to trust rulers. 👑🛡️
## Psalms 118:10
All the nations surrounded me. But in the LORD’s name, I cut them off. 🌍
## Psalms 118:11
They surrounded me on every side. But in the LORD’s name, I cut them off. 🛡️
## Psalms 118:12
They swarmed around me like bees. But they died out like thorn branches in a fire. In the LORD’s name, I cut them off. 🐝🔥
## Psalms 118:13
Someone pushed me hard, and I began to fall. But the LORD helped me. 🤲
## Psalms 118:14
The LORD is my strength and my song. He becomes the One who saves me. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 118:15
People who do right shout with joy in their tents because God saves them. They say, “The LORD’s right hand acts bravely! ⛺😊
## Psalms 118:16
The LORD’s right hand is high above all! The LORD’s right hand acts bravely!” ✋💪
## Psalms 118:17
I will not die. I will live and tell what the LORD does. 📣
## Psalms 118:18
The LORD corrected me with severe punishment. But He did not let death take me. ⚖️
## Psalms 118:19
Open the gates of righteousness for me. I will enter and thank the LORD. 🚪🙌
## Psalms 118:20
This is the LORD’s gate. People who do right will enter through it. 🚪
## Psalms 118:21
I will thank You because You answer me. You are the One who saves me. 🙏
## Psalms 118:22
The builders rejected a stone. That stone became the cornerstone, the main stone at the corner. 🪨
## Psalms 118:23
The LORD does this. It is wonderful to our eyes. 👀✨
## Psalms 118:24
This is the day the LORD makes. We will celebrate and be glad in it. 😊
## Psalms 118:25
LORD, save us, we pray. LORD, please make us prosper! 🙏
## Psalms 118:26
The person who comes in the LORD’s name receives good from God. From the LORD’s house, we speak good wishes for you. 🏠❤️
## Psalms 118:27
The LORD is God. He makes His light shine on us. Tie the feast’s sacrifice with ropes to the horns of the altar. ✨
## Psalms 118:28
You are my God, and I will thank You. You are my God, and I will honor You. 🙌
## Psalms 118:29
Thank the LORD because He is good. His faithful love lasts forever. 🙌❤️
