## Psalms 28:1
David wrote this song. LORD, I call to You. My Rock, do not ignore me. If You stay silent, I will be like people who go down to the grave. 🙏🪨
## Psalms 28:2
Hear my cry for kindness when I call for help. I lift my hands toward Your holy place. 🤲🙏
## Psalms 28:3
Do not drag me away with people who do evil. They speak of peace to their neighbors. But they want to hurt them. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 28:4
Repay them for their acts and the evil they do. Give them what their own hands deserve. ⚖️
## Psalms 28:5
They pay no attention to the LORD’s acts or the work of His hands. So He will tear them down. He will never build them up again. ⚖️
## Psalms 28:6
Praise the LORD! He heard my cry for kindness. 🙌👂
## Psalms 28:7
The LORD is my strength and my shield. I trust Him with my heart, and He helps me. So my heart is glad. I thank Him with my song. 🛡️🎵
## Psalms 28:8
The LORD gives His people strength. He protects and saves the king He chose. 👑🛡️
## Psalms 28:9
Save Your people. Give good things to the people who belong to You. Care for them like a shepherd. Carry them forever. 🐑❤️
