## Psalms 128:1
This is a song of ascent. Everyone who fears the LORD receives good from Him. These people follow His ways. 🛤️
## Psalms 128:2
You will eat the food you work to grow. You will receive good things and prosper. 🌾
## Psalms 128:3
Your wife will be like a vine with much fruit in your house. Your sons around the table will be like young olive plants. 🌿👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Psalms 128:4
This is how the man who fears the LORD will receive good from Him. 🎁
## Psalms 128:5
Let the LORD give you good things from Zion. Let you see Jerusalem prosper every day of your life. ⛰️🏙️
## Psalms 128:6
Let you see your grandchildren. Let Israel have peace! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦🤝
