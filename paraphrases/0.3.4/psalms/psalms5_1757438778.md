## Psalms 5:1
David wrote this song for the music leader, with flutes. LORD, listen to my words. Pay attention to my cries of pain. 🎵🙏
## Psalms 5:2
My King and my God, listen when I cry for help. I pray to You. 👑🙏
## Psalms 5:3
LORD, You hear my voice in the morning. At dawn I bring my requests to You. Then I wait for Your answer. 🌅🙏
## Psalms 5:4
You do not enjoy evil. Evil cannot live with You. ❌
## Psalms 5:5
People who boast cannot stand before You. You hate all people who do evil. ⚖️
## Psalms 5:6
You destroy people who tell lies. The LORD hates people who murder and deceive others. ⚖️
## Psalms 5:7
But Your great love lets me enter Your house. I will bow toward Your holy temple with deep respect. 🏠🙏
## Psalms 5:8
LORD, my enemies surround me. Lead me to do what is right. Make Your path clear before me. 🛤️
## Psalms 5:9
I cannot trust any of their words. They plan to destroy people. Their throats are like open graves. Their words deceive others. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 5:10
God, declare that they are guilty. Let their own plans cause their defeat. Send them away because of their many sins. They refuse to obey You. ⚖️
## Psalms 5:11
Let all who come to You for safety be glad. Let them always shout with joy. Protect them. Let everyone who loves Your name be glad in You. 🛡️😊
## Psalms 5:12
LORD, You give good things to people who do right. Your kindness surrounds them like a shield. 🛡️❤️
