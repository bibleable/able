## Psalms 144:1
David wrote this song. Praise the LORD, my Rock! He trains my hands for war and my fingers for battle. 🪨🙌
## Psalms 144:2
He is my faithful love, my fort, and my rescuer. He is my shield and my safe place. He puts peoples under my power. 🛡️
## Psalms 144:3
LORD, why do You pay attention to human beings? Why do You think about people? ❓❤️
## Psalms 144:4
A person is like a breath. A person’s days are like a shadow that passes. 🌬️
## Psalms 144:5
LORD, open Your heavens and come down. Touch the mountains so that they smoke. ☁️⛰️
## Psalms 144:6
Send Your lightning and scatter them. Shoot Your arrows and make them run away. ⚡🏹
## Psalms 144:7
Reach down from high above. Free me from deep water. Rescue me from the grip of people from other countries. ✋🌊
## Psalms 144:8
Their mouths tell lies. Their right hands act dishonestly. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 144:9
God, I will sing You a new song. I will make music to You on a harp with ten strings. 🎵
## Psalms 144:10
You give victory to kings. You free Your servant David from the deadly sword. 👑🛡️
## Psalms 144:11
Free me from the grip of people from other countries. Their mouths tell lies. Their right hands act dishonestly. 🛡️
## Psalms 144:12
Then our sons will be like plants that grow well in their youth. Our daughters will be like beautiful corner pillars carved for a palace. 🌱
## Psalms 144:13
Our storehouses will be full of every kind of crop. Our flocks will grow by thousands and tens of thousands in our fields. 🌾🐑
## Psalms 144:14
Our oxen will carry heavy loads. No enemy will break through our walls or take us as prisoners. No cries of sorrow will fill our streets. 🧱🛡️
## Psalms 144:15
The people who have this receive good from God. The people whose God is the LORD receive good from Him. ❤️
