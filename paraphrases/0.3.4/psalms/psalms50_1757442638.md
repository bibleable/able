## Psalms 50:1
Asaph wrote this song. The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks. He calls the whole earth, from the east to the west. 🌍📣
## Psalms 50:2
God shines from Zion, a place of perfect beauty. ⛰️✨
## Psalms 50:3
Our God comes and will not stay silent. Fire burns everything in its path before Him. A fierce storm surrounds Him. 🔥⛈️
## Psalms 50:4
He calls the heavens above and the earth below. He will judge His people. 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 50:5
He says, “Gather My holy people to Me. They made a covenant with Me through sacrifice.” A covenant is a binding agreement. 🤝
## Psalms 50:6
The heavens declare that God does right. God Himself is the Judge. *Song direction: Selah.* ⚖️
## Psalms 50:7
God says, “My people, listen, and I will speak. Israel, I will give evidence against you. I am God, your God. 👂
## Psalms 50:8
I do not correct you because you fail to bring sacrifices. Your burnt offerings are always before Me. 🔥
## Psalms 50:9
I do not need a bull from your stall. I do not need goats from your pens. 🐂🐐
## Psalms 50:10
Every animal in the forest belongs to Me. The cattle on a thousand hills belong to Me. 🌳🐂
## Psalms 50:11
I know every bird in the mountains. All the creatures of the fields belong to Me. 🐦
## Psalms 50:12
Suppose I am hungry. I will not tell you. The world and everything in it belong to Me. 🌍
## Psalms 50:13
Do I eat the meat of bulls? Do I drink the blood of goats? ❓
## Psalms 50:14
Bring God a sacrifice to thank Him. Keep your promises to the Most High. 🙏
## Psalms 50:15
Call to Me when trouble comes. I will rescue you. You will honor Me.” 🆘🛡️
## Psalms 50:16
But God says to people who do evil, “What right do you have to repeat My laws? What right do you have to speak about My covenant? ⚖️
## Psalms 50:17
You hate My instructions. You throw My words behind you. 📖❌
## Psalms 50:18
When you see a thief, you make friends with him. You join people who have sex outside their marriages. ❌
## Psalms 50:19
You let your mouth speak evil freely. You use your tongue to tell lies. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 50:20
You sit and speak against your brother. You tell harmful lies about your own mother’s son. 🗣️
## Psalms 50:21
You did these things, and I stayed silent. You thought I was just like you. But now I correct you. I accuse you face to face. ⚖️
## Psalms 50:22
You who forget God, think about this. Otherwise, I will tear you apart. No one will rescue you. ⚖️
## Psalms 50:23
The person who brings a sacrifice of thanks honors Me. I will show My power to save to the person who lives rightly.” 🙏❤️
