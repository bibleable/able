## Psalms 69:1
David wrote this song for the music leader, to the tune “Lilies.” God, save me! The water reaches my neck. 🎵🌊
## Psalms 69:2
I sink into deep mud with no firm place for my feet. I drift into deep water. The flood covers me. 🌊🆘
## Psalms 69:3
My cries make me tired. My throat is dry. My eyes grow weak as I watch for my God. 😢
## Psalms 69:4
People who hate me without a cause outnumber the hairs on my head. Many enemies want to destroy me for no reason. I did not steal, but I must pay back what is missing. 🆘
## Psalms 69:5
God, You know my foolish acts. I cannot hide my guilt from You. 🔍
## Psalms 69:6
Lord GOD of Hosts, do not let people who hope in You face shame because of me. God of Israel, do not let people who seek You face dishonor because of me. 🙏
## Psalms 69:7
People insult me because of You. Shame covers my face. 😢
## Psalms 69:8
My brothers treat me like a stranger. My own mother’s sons treat me like a foreigner. 💔
## Psalms 69:9
My strong love for Your house fills me like a fire. The insults of people who insult You fall on me. 🏠🔥
## Psalms 69:10
I cried and went without food. But people insulted me for this. 😢
## Psalms 69:11
I wore rough clothes to show grief. People made fun of me. 💔
## Psalms 69:12
People who sit at the gate mock me. People who drink too much wine sing songs about me. 🗣️
## Psalms 69:13
LORD, I pray to You for a time of kindness. God, answer me because of Your great, faithful love. Save me with Your sure help. 🙏❤️
## Psalms 69:14
Pull me out of the mud. Do not let me sink. Rescue me from my enemies and from deep water. 🌊🆘
## Psalms 69:15
Do not let the flood cover me. Do not let the deep water swallow me. Do not let the grave close its mouth over me. 🆘
## Psalms 69:16
LORD, answer me because Your faithful love is good. Turn toward me because of Your great care. ❤️
## Psalms 69:17
Do not hide Your face from me, Your servant. I am in trouble. Answer me quickly! 🆘🙏
## Psalms 69:18
Come near and rescue me. Set me free because of my enemies. 🤲
## Psalms 69:19
You know my insults, shame, and dishonor. You see all my enemies. 👀
## Psalms 69:20
Insults break my heart. I lose hope. I look for someone to care, but no one does. I look for someone to comfort me, but I find no one. 💔
## Psalms 69:21
They poison my food with bitter gall. When I am thirsty, they give me vinegar. 🍽️
## Psalms 69:22
Let their table become a trap. Let it bring punishment and catch them. ⚖️
## Psalms 69:23
Make their eyes dark so that they cannot see. Keep their backs bent forever. ⚖️
## Psalms 69:24
Pour Your anger on them. Let Your fierce anger catch them. ⚖️🔥
## Psalms 69:25
Let their homes stand empty. Let no one live in their tents. ⛺
## Psalms 69:26
They attack the person You strike. They talk about the pain of people You wound. 💔
## Psalms 69:27
Add their sins to the sins already against them. Do not let them share in Your righteousness. ⚖️
## Psalms 69:28
Remove their names from the Book of Life. Do not list them with people who do right. 📖⚖️
## Psalms 69:29
But I am in pain and distress. God, protect me with Your power to save. 🆘🛡️
## Psalms 69:30
I will praise God’s name with a song. I will honor Him with thanks. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 69:31
This will please the LORD more than an ox. It will please Him more than a bull with horns and hooves. 🐂
## Psalms 69:32
Humble people will see and be glad. You who seek God, let your hearts find new life! 😊❤️
## Psalms 69:33
The LORD listens to people who need help. He does not reject His people who are prisoners. 👂
## Psalms 69:34
Let heaven and earth praise Him. Let the seas and everything in them praise Him. 🌍🌊🙌
## Psalms 69:35
God will save Zion and rebuild Judah’s cities. His people will live there and own the land. 🏙️
## Psalms 69:36
His servants’ descendants will receive that land. People who love His name will settle there. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦❤️
