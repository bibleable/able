## Psalms 64:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. God, hear my complaint. Protect my life from fear of the enemy. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 64:2
Hide me from the secret plans of people who do evil. Hide me from their crowd. 🛡️
## Psalms 64:3
They sharpen their tongues like swords. They aim their cruel words like arrows. 🗣️🏹
## Psalms 64:4
They hide to attack an innocent person alone. They shoot suddenly, without fear. 🏹
## Psalms 64:5
They hold firmly to their evil plan. They talk about hidden traps. They say, “Who will see them?” 💭❌
## Psalms 64:6
They plan unfair acts and say, “Our secret plan is perfect.” What lies deep in people’s hearts is hard to understand. 💭
## Psalms 64:7
But God will shoot arrows at them. His arrows will wound them suddenly. 🏹⚖️
## Psalms 64:8
Their own words will turn against them and cause their fall. Everyone who sees this will shake their heads. 🗣️
## Psalms 64:9
Then all people will fear. They will tell about God’s work. They will think about what He does. 📣💭
## Psalms 64:10
Let people who do right be glad in the LORD and find safety in Him. Let everyone whose heart is honest celebrate. 😊🛡️
