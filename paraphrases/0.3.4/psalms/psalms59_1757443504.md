## Psalms 59:1
David wrote this Miktam for the music leader. Its tune is “Do Not Destroy.” Saul sent men to watch David’s house and kill him. My God, rescue me from my enemies. Protect me from people who attack me. 🎵🛡️
## Psalms 59:2
Rescue me from people who do evil. Save me from people who murder. 🆘
## Psalms 59:3
See how they hide and wait for me. Fierce people plan together against me. LORD, I did no wrong or sin to cause this. 👀
## Psalms 59:4
They hurry to attack me though I did nothing wrong. Act now to help me. Look at what happens. 🆘
## Psalms 59:5
LORD God of Hosts, God of Israel, act now to punish all the nations. Show no kindness to evil people who betray others. *Song direction: Selah.* ⚖️
## Psalms 59:6
They return in the evening. They growl like dogs and roam around the city. 🐕🌙
## Psalms 59:7
Look at what comes from their mouths! Their words cut like swords. They say, “Who can hear us?” 🗣️⚔️
## Psalms 59:8
But LORD, You laugh at them. You mock all the nations. 🌍
## Psalms 59:9
You are my strength. I will watch for You because You, God, are my strong fort. 👀🛡️
## Psalms 59:10
My God of faithful love will come to meet me. He will let me look at my defeated enemies. ❤️
## Psalms 59:11
Do not kill them, or my people will forget. Lord, our shield, scatter them by Your power. Bring them down. 🛡️
## Psalms 59:12
Let their sinful words trap them in their pride. Let the curses and lies from their lips trap them. 🗣️⚖️
## Psalms 59:13
Destroy them in Your anger until none are left. Then the whole earth will know that God rules over Jacob. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌍⚖️
## Psalms 59:14
They return in the evening. They growl like dogs and roam around the city. 🐕🌙
## Psalms 59:15
They search for scraps of food. They growl if they do not have enough. 🐕
## Psalms 59:16
But I will sing about Your strength. In the morning, I will tell of Your faithful love. You are my strong fort and my safe place when trouble comes. 🎵🌅
## Psalms 59:17
My Strength, I sing praise to You. God, You are my strong fort. You are my God of faithful love. 🎵❤️
