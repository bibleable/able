## Psalms 75:1
Asaph wrote this song for the music leader, to the tune “Do Not Destroy.” God, we thank You. We thank You because Your Name is near. People tell of Your wonderful acts. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 75:2
God says, “At the time I choose, I will judge fairly. ⚖️
## Psalms 75:3
When the earth and everyone on it shake, I hold its pillars firm. *Song direction: Selah.* 🌍
## Psalms 75:4
I tell proud people, ‘Do not boast.’ I tell people who do evil, ‘Do not raise your horns in pride. 🗣️
## Psalms 75:5
Do not raise your horns against heaven. Do not stretch your necks out and speak proudly.’” ❌
## Psalms 75:6
Honor does not come from the east or west. It does not come from the desert. 🌍
## Psalms 75:7
God is the Judge. He brings one person down and lifts another up. ⚖️
## Psalms 75:8
The LORD holds a cup of wine with foam and spices. He pours it out. All people on earth who do evil must drink it to the last drops. 🍷⚖️
## Psalms 75:9
But I will tell of Him forever. I will sing praise to the God of Jacob. 🎵🙌
## Psalms 75:10
“I will cut off all the horns of people who do evil. But the horns of people who do right will rise high.” ⚖️
