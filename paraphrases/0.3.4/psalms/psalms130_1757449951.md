## Psalms 130:1
This is a song of ascent. LORD, I cry to You from the depths! 🆘🙏
## Psalms 130:2
Lord, hear my voice. Pay attention to my cry for kindness. 👂
## Psalms 130:3
LORD, if You kept a record of sins, who could stand before You? ⚖️❓
## Psalms 130:4
But You forgive. So people can fear You. 🤲
## Psalms 130:5
I wait for the LORD with my whole self. I put my hope in His word. ⏳📖
## Psalms 130:6
I wait for the Lord more than guards wait for morning. Yes, more than guards wait for morning. 🌅⏳
## Psalms 130:7
Israel, put your hope in the LORD. His love is faithful. He has more than enough power to rescue. ❤️🛡️
## Psalms 130:8
He will rescue Israel from all its sins. 🛡️
