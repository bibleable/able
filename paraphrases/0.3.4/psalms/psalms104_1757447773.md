## Psalms 104:1
My soul, praise the LORD! LORD my God, You are very great. You wear beauty and royal greatness like clothes. 🙌👑
## Psalms 104:2
He wraps Himself in light like a robe. He stretches out the heavens like a tent. ✨⛺
## Psalms 104:3
He lays the beams of His rooms in the waters above. He uses clouds as His chariot. He walks on the wings of the wind. ☁️🌬️
## Psalms 104:4
He makes winds His messengers. He makes flames of fire His servants. 🌬️🔥
## Psalms 104:5
He set the earth on its foundations. It will never move from them. 🌍
## Psalms 104:6
You covered the earth with deep water like a robe. The water stood above the mountains. 🌊⛰️
## Psalms 104:7
You spoke against the waters, and they fled. At the sound of Your thunder, they hurried away. ⛈️
## Psalms 104:8
The mountains rose, and the valleys sank. They went to the places You chose for them. ⛰️
## Psalms 104:9
You set a boundary that the waters cannot cross. They can never cover the earth again. 🌊
## Psalms 104:10
He sends springs into the valleys. They flow between the mountains. 💧⛰️
## Psalms 104:11
They give water to every animal in the fields. Wild donkeys drink until they are not thirsty. 🫏💧
## Psalms 104:12
Birds make nests beside the springs. They sing among the branches. 🐦🎵
## Psalms 104:13
He waters the mountains from His rooms above. His works give the earth everything it needs. 🌧️⛰️
## Psalms 104:14
He makes grass grow for farm animals. He gives people crops to grow. They bring food from the earth. 🌿🌾
## Psalms 104:15
Wine makes people glad. Oil makes their faces shine. Bread gives their hearts strength. 🍞
## Psalms 104:16
The LORD’s trees have plenty. These include the cedars of Lebanon that He planted. 🌲
## Psalms 104:17
Birds build nests there. The stork makes her home in the cypress trees. 🐦🌲
## Psalms 104:18
The high mountains are home to wild goats. Rock badgers find safety in the cliffs. ⛰️🐐
## Psalms 104:19
He made the moon to mark the seasons. The sun knows when to set. 🌙☀️
## Psalms 104:20
You bring darkness, and night comes. Then the forest’s animals roam. 🌙
## Psalms 104:21
Young lions roar for animals to eat. They seek their food from God. 🦁
## Psalms 104:22
The sun rises, and they return to their dens. They lie down there. 🌅
## Psalms 104:23
People go out to work. They continue their work until evening. 🚶
## Psalms 104:24
LORD, Your works are so many! You made them all with wisdom. The earth is full of Your creatures. 🌍
## Psalms 104:25
The sea is vast and wide. It is full of more creatures than anyone can count, both large and small. 🌊🐟
## Psalms 104:26
Ships travel there. Leviathan is there too. You made him to play in the sea. ⛵
## Psalms 104:27
All creatures look to You for food at the right time. 🍽️
## Psalms 104:28
When You give food, they gather it. When You open Your hand, good things satisfy them. 🤲
## Psalms 104:29
When You hide Your face, they feel terror. When You take away their breath, they die and return to dust. 🌬️
## Psalms 104:30
When You send Your Spirit, You create them. You make the face of the earth new again. 🌍🌱
## Psalms 104:31
Let the LORD’s glory last forever. Let the LORD be glad in His works. ✨😊
## Psalms 104:32
He looks at the earth, and it shakes. He touches mountains, and they smoke. 🌍⛰️
## Psalms 104:33
I will sing to the LORD all my life. I will sing praise to my God as long as I exist. 🎵
## Psalms 104:34
Let my thoughts give Him pleasure. I am glad in the LORD. 💭😊
## Psalms 104:35
Let people who sin disappear from the earth. Let people who do evil be no more. My soul, praise the LORD. Praise the LORD! 🌍🙌
