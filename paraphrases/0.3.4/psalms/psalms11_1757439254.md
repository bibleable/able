## Psalms 11:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. I come to the LORD for safety. So why do you tell me, “Fly like a bird to your mountain! 🛡️🐦
## Psalms 11:2
Look! People who do evil prepare their bows. They put arrows on the strings. They hide in the dark to shoot people who do right. 🏹
## Psalms 11:3
When the foundations of a fair society collapse, what can people who do right do?” ❓⚖️
## Psalms 11:4
The LORD is in His holy temple. The LORD sits on His throne in heaven. He watches people closely. He examines them. 👑👀
## Psalms 11:5
The LORD tests people who do right and people who do evil. He hates people who love violence. ⚖️
## Psalms 11:6
He will send hot coals and sulfur down on people who do evil. He will give them a very hot wind. 🔥
## Psalms 11:7
The LORD does right. He loves fairness. People who do right will see His face. ⚖️❤️
