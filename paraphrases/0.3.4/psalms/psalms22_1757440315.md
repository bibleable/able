## Psalms 22:1
David wrote this song for the music leader. Its tune is “The Doe of the Dawn.” My God, my God, why did You leave me? Why are You so far away instead of here to save me? Why are You so far from my cries of pain? 🎵🆘
## Psalms 22:2
My God, I cry to You during the day. But You do not answer. I cry at night, but I find no rest. ☀️🌙😢
## Psalms 22:3
Yet You are holy. You sit as King among the praises of Israel. 👑🙌
## Psalms 22:4
Our ancestors trusted You. They trusted You, and You rescued them. 🤝
## Psalms 22:5
They cried to You, and You set them free. They trusted You, and You did not let them down. 🛡️
## Psalms 22:6
But I feel like a worm, not a person. People insult me and treat me as worthless. 😢
## Psalms 22:7
Everyone who sees me mocks me. They make faces and shake their heads. 🗣️
## Psalms 22:8
They say, “He trusts the LORD. Let the LORD save him! Let the LORD rescue him, since He takes pleasure in him!” 🗣️
## Psalms 22:9
But You brought me out of my mother’s womb. You kept me safe at my mother’s breast. 👶❤️
## Psalms 22:10
From birth, I depended on You. You were my God from the time I was in my mother’s womb. 👶🙏
## Psalms 22:11
Do not stay far from me. Trouble is near. No one is here to help. 🆘
## Psalms 22:12
My enemies surround me like many bulls. They are like the strong bulls of Bashan. 🐂
## Psalms 22:13
They open their mouths against me. They are like lions that roar and tear their victims apart. 🦁
## Psalms 22:14
I feel like water that spills out. All my bones are out of joint. My heart is like wax that melts inside me. 💔
## Psalms 22:15
My strength dries up like a piece of broken pottery. My tongue sticks to the top of my mouth. You lay me in the dust of death. 🆘
## Psalms 22:16
People who do evil surround me like dogs. They put holes through my hands and feet. 💔
## Psalms 22:17
I can count all my bones. My enemies stare at me. They enjoy my pain. 👀
## Psalms 22:18
They divide my clothes among themselves. They cast lots, a way to choose by chance, to decide who gets my clothes. 👕
## Psalms 22:19
But LORD, do not stay far away. You are my strength. Come quickly to help me. 🆘🙏
## Psalms 22:20
Save my life from the sword. Rescue my precious life from the power of wild dogs. ⚔️🛡️
## Psalms 22:21
Save me from the lion’s mouth. You answered me when the horns of wild oxen threatened me! 🦁🐂
## Psalms 22:22
I will tell my brothers about Your name. I will praise You when the people gather. 📣🙌
## Psalms 22:23
Everyone who respects the LORD, praise Him! All descendants of Jacob, honor Him! All descendants of Israel, show Him deep respect! 🙌
## Psalms 22:24
He does not treat a person’s suffering as worthless. He does not turn His face away from that person. He hears the cry for help. 👂❤️
## Psalms 22:25
I will praise You when the great crowd gathers. I will keep my promises before the people who respect You. 👥🙌
## Psalms 22:26
Poor people will eat enough. People who seek the LORD will praise Him. Let your hearts live forever! 🍞❤️
## Psalms 22:27
People at every end of the earth will remember and turn to the LORD. All the families of the nations will bow before Him. 🌍🙏
## Psalms 22:28
The LORD has authority over all. He rules the nations. 👑🌍
## Psalms 22:29
All rich people on earth will eat and worship. Everyone who goes down to the dust of death will kneel before Him. This includes people who cannot keep themselves alive. 🙏
## Psalms 22:30
Future generations will serve Him. They will tell the next generation about the Lord. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦📣
## Psalms 22:31
They will tell people not yet born about His right acts. They will tell all that He did. 📣✅
