## Psalms 56:1
David wrote this Miktam for the music leader when the Philistines seized him in Gath. Its tune is “A Dove on Distant Oaks.” God, be kind to me. People chase me and attack me all day. 🎵🆘
## Psalms 56:2
My enemies chase me all day. Many proud people attack me. 🆘
## Psalms 56:3
When I am afraid, I trust You. 🙏❤️
## Psalms 56:4
I praise God’s word. I trust God and will not be afraid. What can a human being do to me? 📖🛡️
## Psalms 56:5
All day they twist my words. All their thoughts are about my destruction. 🗣️❌
## Psalms 56:6
They plan together and hide. They watch my steps as they wait to kill me. 👀
## Psalms 56:7
Will they escape after such sin? God, in Your anger, bring down the nations. ⚖️
## Psalms 56:8
You count the times I wander. Put my tears in Your bottle. Surely You record them in Your book. 😢📖
## Psalms 56:9
When I cry for help, my enemies will turn back. Then I will know that God is on my side. 🆘🛡️
## Psalms 56:10
I praise God’s word. I praise the LORD’s word. 📖🙌
## Psalms 56:11
I trust God and will not be afraid. What can a human being do to me? 🛡️
## Psalms 56:12
God, I must keep my promises to You. I will bring offerings to thank You. 🎁🙏
## Psalms 56:13
You rescue my life from death. You keep my feet from a fall. So I can walk before God in the light of life. 🛤️✨
