## 2 Peter 3:1
Dear friends, this is my second letter to you. Both letters remind you of the truth to encourage pure thoughts. ✉️
## 2 Peter 3:2
Remember what the holy prophets foretold. Remember also the commands of our Lord and Savior that your apostles gave you. 📖
## 2 Peter 3:3
Above all, understand that people will come in the last days to mock the truth. They will follow their own evil desires. ⚠️
## 2 Peter 3:4
They will ask, “Where is His promised return? Ever since our ancestors died, everything has continued as it did from the beginning of creation.” 💬
## 2 Peter 3:5
They deliberately ignore that the heavens existed long ago by God’s word. By His word, the earth formed out of water and by water. 🌍
## 2 Peter 3:6
Through that water, the world of that time perished in the flood. 🌊
## 2 Peter 3:7
By the same word, the present heavens and earth are kept for fire. They await the day of judgment, when people who dishonor God will be destroyed. 🔥
## 2 Peter 3:8
Dear friends, do not forget this: To the Lord, one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like one day. ⏳
## 2 Peter 3:9
Some people think the Lord is slow to keep His promise, but He is patient with you. He does not want anyone to perish. He wants everyone to turn from sin. ❤️
## 2 Peter 3:10
The Day of the Lord will come like a thief. The heavens will disappear with a roar. Fire will destroy the elements, and the earth and its works will be exposed. 🔥
## 2 Peter 3:11
Since everything will be destroyed in this way, what kind of people must you be? You must live in a holy way that honors God. 👣
## 2 Peter 3:12
Wait for the day of God and work to make it come sooner. On that day, fire will destroy the heavens, and the elements will melt in the heat. 🔥
## 2 Peter 3:13
But we look forward to the new heaven and new earth that God promised. Righteousness will live there. 🌅
## 2 Peter 3:14
So, dear friends, as you wait for these things, make every effort to be at peace. Be free from stain and blame in His sight. 🕊️
## 2 Peter 3:15
Remember that our Lord’s patience brings salvation. Our dear brother Paul also wrote this to you with the wisdom God gave him. 📜
## 2 Peter 3:16
Paul writes about these matters in all his letters. Some parts are hard to understand. Ignorant and unstable people twist them, as they do the other Scriptures. This brings their own destruction. ⚠️
## 2 Peter 3:17
Dear friends, you already know these things. Be on guard, so that the errors of lawless people do not carry you away from your secure position. 🛡️
## 2 Peter 3:18
But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. May He receive glory now and forever. Amen. ✨
