## 2 Peter 2:1
False prophets lived among the people, and false teachers will also appear among you. They will secretly bring in destructive false beliefs. They will even deny the Master who bought them. They will bring quick destruction on themselves. ⚠️
## 2 Peter 2:2
Many will follow their evil behavior. Because of them, people will speak against the way of truth. ⛔
## 2 Peter 2:3
These false teachers are greedy. They will use deceptive words to take advantage of you. The judgment against them was declared long ago and still stands. Their destruction does not sleep. ⚖️
## 2 Peter 2:4
God did not spare the angels when they sinned. He threw them deep into hell and placed them in chains of darkness to await judgment. ⚖️
## 2 Peter 2:5
God did not spare the ancient world. He sent a flood on its people who dishonored Him. But He kept Noah, a preacher of righteousness, safe among eight people. 🌊
## 2 Peter 2:6
God condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah and turned them into ashes. He made them an example of what will happen to those who dishonor Him. 🔥
## 2 Peter 2:7
God rescued Lot, a righteous man. The evil behavior of those who rejected the law caused Lot great distress. 🛡️
## 2 Peter 2:8
Day after day, this righteous man lived among them. The lawless acts he saw and heard caused deep pain in his righteous soul. 💔
## 2 Peter 2:9
All this shows that the Lord knows how to rescue those who honor Him from trials. He also knows how to hold the unrighteous for punishment on the day of judgment. ⚖️
## 2 Peter 2:10
This punishment is especially for those who follow corrupt physical desires and despise authority. They are bold and insist on their own way. They are not afraid to insult glorious beings. ⚠️
## 2 Peter 2:11
But angels, who have greater strength and power, do not dare to bring such insulting charges against them before the Lord. 👼
## 2 Peter 2:12
These people are like animals without reason. They follow instinct and are born to be caught and destroyed. They insult things they do not understand. Like those animals, they too will be destroyed. ⚖️
## 2 Peter 2:13
The harm they suffer will be the payment for their evil. They enjoy wild parties in broad daylight. They are like stains and defects. They enjoy their deception as they feast with you. ⛔
## 2 Peter 2:14
Their eyes are full of adultery, and they never have enough sin. They lure unstable people. They have trained their hearts in greed. They are children under a curse. ⚠️
## 2 Peter 2:15
They left the straight path and wandered away. They followed Balaam son of Beor, who loved the payment he received for evil. 🛤️
## 2 Peter 2:16
But a donkey corrected him for his wrongdoing. This animal, which could not normally speak, spoke with a human voice and stopped the prophet’s madness. 🫏
## 2 Peter 2:17
These people are springs without water and mists that a storm drives away. The deepest darkness is kept for them. 🌑
## 2 Peter 2:18
They speak proud but empty words. They use sinful physical desires to lure those who have just begun to escape from people who live in error. ⚠️
## 2 Peter 2:19
They promise freedom, but they themselves are slaves to corruption. A person is a slave to whatever controls him. ⛓️
## 2 Peter 2:20
Suppose they escaped the world’s corruption through knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. If it traps and defeats them again, their final condition is worse than their first. ⚠️
## 2 Peter 2:21
It would have been better for them never to know the right path. Instead, they knew it and then turned away from the holy command passed on to them. ⛔
## 2 Peter 2:22
These proverbs describe them truly: “A dog returns to its vomit.” And, “A washed female pig returns to roll in the mud.” 🐕
