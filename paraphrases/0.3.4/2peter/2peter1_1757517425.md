## 2 Peter 1:1
This letter is from Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ. I write to those who received faith as precious as ours through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ. ✉️
## 2 Peter 1:2
May your knowledge of God and Jesus our Lord bring you more and more grace and peace. 🕊️
## 2 Peter 1:3
God’s power has given us everything we need for life and to honor Him. This comes through our knowledge of Him who called us by His own glory and excellence. ✨
## 2 Peter 1:4
Through His glory and excellence, He gave us precious and great promises. Through these promises, you can share in God’s nature. You have escaped the corruption that evil desires cause in the world. 🌅
## 2 Peter 1:5
For this reason, make every effort to add good character to your faith. Add knowledge to good character. 📖
## 2 Peter 1:6
Add control of yourself to knowledge. Add patience to control of yourself. Add a life that honors God to patience. 👣
## 2 Peter 1:7
Add kindness toward your brothers to a life that honors God. Add love to this kindness. ❤️
## 2 Peter 1:8
If you have these qualities and continue to grow in them, your knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ will produce useful results. 🌱
## 2 Peter 1:9
But anyone without these qualities cannot see far and is like a blind person. He has forgotten that he was cleansed from his past sins. 💭
## 2 Peter 1:10
So, brothers, make every effort to confirm that God called and chose you. If you practice these things, you will never stumble. 👣
## 2 Peter 1:11
You will receive a rich welcome into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. 👑
## 2 Peter 1:12
So I will always remind you of these things. You already know them and stand firmly in the truth you now have. 📖
## 2 Peter 1:13
My body is like a tent I live in. As long as I live in it, I think it is right to remind you of these things. ⛺
## 2 Peter 1:14
I know I must soon leave this tent behind. Our Lord Jesus Christ has made this clear to me. ⏳
## 2 Peter 1:15
I will make every effort to help you remember these things at all times after I leave. 💭
## 2 Peter 1:16
We told you about the power and return of our Lord Jesus Christ. We did not follow clever, invented stories. We saw His majesty with our own eyes. 👀
## 2 Peter 1:17
He received honor and glory from God the Father. A voice came to Him from the Majestic Glory: “This is My beloved Son. He gives Me great pleasure.” ✨
## 2 Peter 1:18
We heard this voice from heaven ourselves when we were with Him on the holy mountain. ⛰️
## 2 Peter 1:19
The words of the prophets are also confirmed beyond doubt. Pay attention to them as you would to a lamp in a dark place. Continue until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts. 🪔
## 2 Peter 1:20
Above all, understand this: No prophecy in Scripture comes from a person’s own interpretation. 📖
## 2 Peter 1:21
No prophecy came from human will. People spoke from God as the Holy Spirit carried them along. 🕊️
