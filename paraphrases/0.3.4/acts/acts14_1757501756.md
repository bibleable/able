## Acts 14:1
At Iconium, Paul and Barnabas entered the Jewish synagogue as usual. They spoke so well that many Jews and Greeks believed. 📖

## Acts 14:2
The Jews who did not believe stirred up the Gentiles. They poisoned their minds against the brothers. 📖

## Acts 14:3
Paul and Barnabas stayed a long time and spoke boldly for the Lord. He confirmed the message of His grace by enabling signs and wonders through them. 📖

## Acts 14:4
The city’s people divided. Some supported the Jews, and others supported the apostles. 📖

## Acts 14:5
Gentiles and Jews, together with their rulers, planned to mistreat the apostles and throw stones at them. 📖

## Acts 14:6
Paul and Barnabas learned of this. They fled to Lystra and Derbe, cities in Lycaonia, and the surrounding region. 📖

## Acts 14:7
They continued preaching the good news there. 📖

## Acts 14:8
In Lystra sat a man with crippled feet. He had been unable to walk since birth and had never walked. 📖

## Acts 14:9
He listened to Paul. Paul looked intently at him and saw that he had faith to be healed. 📖

## Acts 14:10
Paul called loudly, “Stand up on your feet!” The man jumped up and began walking. 📖

## Acts 14:11
The crowds saw what Paul did. They shouted in the Lycaonian language, “The gods have come down to us in human form!” 📖

## Acts 14:12
They called Barnabas Zeus. They called Paul Hermes because he was the main speaker. 📖

## Acts 14:13
Zeus’ temple stood just outside the city. His priest brought bulls and wreaths to the city gates. He and the crowds wanted to offer a sacrifice. 📖

## Acts 14:14
When the apostles Barnabas and Paul learned this, they tore their clothes. They rushed into the crowd and shouted. 📖

## Acts 14:15
“Men, why do this? We are human like you. We bring good news so you turn from these worthless things to the living God. He made heaven, earth, sea, and everything in them. 📖

## Acts 14:16
In past generations, He let all nations follow their own ways. 📖

## Acts 14:17
Yet He gave evidence of His goodness. He gives you rain from heaven and fruitful seasons. He fills your hearts with food and joy.” 📖

## Acts 14:18
Even with these words, Paul and Barnabas could barely stop the crowds from sacrificing to them. 📖

## Acts 14:19
Some Jews arrived from Antioch and Iconium and won over the crowds. They threw stones at Paul and dragged him outside the city, thinking he was dead. 📖

## Acts 14:20
The disciples gathered around him. Then he got up and went back into the city. The next day, he left with Barnabas for Derbe. 📖

## Acts 14:21
They preached the good news in Derbe and made many disciples. Then they returned to Lystra, Iconium, and Antioch. 📖

## Acts 14:22
They strengthened the disciples’ souls and encouraged them to continue in the faith. They said, “We must endure many hardships to enter God’s kingdom.” 📖

## Acts 14:23
Paul and Barnabas appointed elders in each church. With prayer and fasting, they entrusted them to the Lord, in whom they had believed. 📖

## Acts 14:24
They passed through Pisidia and came to Pamphylia. 📖

## Acts 14:25
They spoke the word in Perga. Then they went down to Attalia. 📖

## Acts 14:26
They sailed from Attalia to Antioch. There they had been entrusted to God’s grace for the work they had now completed. 📖

## Acts 14:27
They arrived and gathered the church. They reported everything God did through them and how He opened the door of faith to the Gentiles. 📖

## Acts 14:28
They stayed there with the disciples a long time. 📖
