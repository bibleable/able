## Acts 3:1
Peter and John went to the temple for prayer at the ninth hour that afternoon. 📖
## Acts 3:2
A man lame from birth was carried daily to the Beautiful Gate to beg from those entering the temple courts. 📖
## Acts 3:3
He asked Peter and John for money as they entered. 📖
## Acts 3:4
Peter and John looked directly at him. Peter said, “Look at us.” 📖
## Acts 3:5
He watched, expecting to receive something. 📖
## Acts 3:6
Peter said, “I have no silver or gold. I give what I have. In Jesus Christ of Nazareth’s name, rise and walk!” 📖
## Acts 3:7
Peter lifted him by his right hand. His feet and ankles immediately became strong. 📖
## Acts 3:8
He sprang up and walked. He entered the temple courts with them, walking, leaping, and praising God. 🙏
## Acts 3:9
Everyone saw him walking and praising God. 📖
## Acts 3:10
They recognized him as the beggar who sat at the temple’s Beautiful Gate. They marveled at what happened to him. 📖
## Acts 3:11
He clung to Peter and John. The astonished people ran to them at Solomon’s Colonnade. 📖
## Acts 3:12
Peter said, “Israelites, why marvel and stare as if our power or godliness made him walk? ❓
## Acts 3:13
Our fathers’ God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, glorified His servant Jesus. You surrendered and rejected Him before Pilate despite Pilate’s decision to release Him. 📖
## Acts 3:14
You rejected the Holy and Righteous One and demanded a murderer’s release. 📖
## Acts 3:15
You killed life’s Author. God raised Him from death, and we are witnesses. 📖
## Acts 3:16
Faith in Jesus’ name strengthened this man you know and see. His name and faith coming through Him completely healed the man before you. 📖
## Acts 3:17
Brothers, you and your leaders acted ignorantly. 📖
## Acts 3:18
In this way, God fulfilled what He foretold through all the prophets: His Christ would suffer. 📖
## Acts 3:19
Repent and turn back so your sins may be wiped away. 📖
## Acts 3:20
Then refreshing times may come from the Lord’s presence, and He may send Jesus, your appointed Christ. 📖
## Acts 3:21
Heaven must receive Him until the restoration of all things God announced long ago through His holy prophets. 📖
## Acts 3:22
Moses said, ‘The Lord your God will raise a prophet like me from your brothers. Listen to everything He tells you. 📖
## Acts 3:23
Whoever refuses Him will be completely cut off from his people.’ 📖
## Acts 3:24
All the prophets who spoke, from Samuel onward, proclaimed these days. 📖
## Acts 3:25
You are sons of the prophets and God’s covenant with your fathers. He told Abraham, ‘Through your offspring all earth’s families will be blessed.’ 📖
## Acts 3:26
God raised up His Servant and sent Him first to you, to bless you by turning each from wickedness.” 📖
