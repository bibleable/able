## Acts 18:1
Paul then left Athens and went to Corinth. 📖

## Acts 18:2
He found Aquila, a Jew born in Pontus. Aquila and his wife Priscilla had recently come from Italy because Claudius ordered all Jews to leave Rome. Paul visited them. 📖

## Acts 18:3
He stayed and worked with them. They made tents, as he did. 📖

## Acts 18:4
Every Sabbath, he reasoned in the synagogue. He tried to persuade both Jews and Greeks. 📖

## Acts 18:5
Silas and Timothy came down from Macedonia. Paul then devoted himself fully to the word, testifying to the Jews that Jesus is the Christ. 📖

## Acts 18:6
They opposed and insulted him. He shook out his garments and said, “Your blood be on your own heads! I am innocent of it. From now on, I will go to the Gentiles.” 📖

## Acts 18:7
Paul left the synagogue. He went next door to Titus Justus’ house. Titus Justus worshiped God. 📖

## Acts 18:8
Crispus, the synagogue leader, and his whole household believed in the Lord. Many Corinthians who heard the message also believed and were baptized. 📖

## Acts 18:9
One night, the Lord spoke to Paul in a vision. “Do not fear. Keep speaking. Do not be silent. 📖

## Acts 18:10
I am with you, and no one will lay a hand on you. I have many people in this city.” 📖

## Acts 18:11
Paul stayed a year and a half. He taught God’s word among the Corinthians. 📖

## Acts 18:12
When Gallio was Achaia’s proconsul, the Jews organized an attack on Paul. They brought him before the judgment seat. 📖

## Acts 18:13
They said, “This man persuades people to worship God in ways against the law.” 📖

## Acts 18:14
Paul was about to speak. Gallio told the Jews, “If this concerned wrongdoing or a vicious crime, it would be reasonable to hear your complaint. 📖

## Acts 18:15
But this dispute concerns words, names, and your own law. Settle it yourselves. I refuse to judge such matters.” 📖

## Acts 18:16
He drove them away from the judgment seat. 📖

## Acts 18:17
The crowd seized Sosthenes, the synagogue leader. They beat him before the judgment seat. Gallio showed no concern about any of this. 📖

## Acts 18:18
Paul stayed in Corinth quite some time, then said goodbye to the brothers. At Cenchrea, he had his head shaved to keep a vow. He sailed to Syria with Priscilla and Aquila. 📖

## Acts 18:19
They reached Ephesus. Paul left Priscilla and Aquila there. He entered the synagogue and reasoned with the Jews. 📖

## Acts 18:20
They asked him to stay longer, but he declined. 📖

## Acts 18:21
As he left, he said, “I will return if God is willing.” He sailed from Ephesus. 📖

## Acts 18:22
Paul landed at Caesarea. He went up to greet the Jerusalem church, then went down to Antioch. 📖

## Acts 18:23
He stayed in Antioch for some time. Then he traveled through Galatia and Phrygia, strengthening all the disciples. 📖

## Acts 18:24
Meanwhile, Apollos, a Jew born in Alexandria, came to Ephesus. He spoke eloquently and knew Scripture well. 📖

## Acts 18:25
He had learned the Lord’s way and was fervent in spirit. He spoke and taught accurately about Jesus. But he knew only John’s baptism. 📖

## Acts 18:26
He began speaking boldly in the synagogue. Priscilla and Aquila heard him. They took him aside and explained God’s way more accurately. 📖

## Acts 18:27
Apollos decided to cross to Achaia. The brothers encouraged him and wrote to the disciples there to welcome him. He arrived and greatly helped those who had believed by grace. 📖

## Acts 18:28
He powerfully refuted the Jews in public debate. He proved from Scripture that Jesus is the Christ. 📖
