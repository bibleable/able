## Acts 1:1
Theophilus, my first book described all Jesus began doing and teaching. 📖
## Acts 1:2
It covered the time until He was taken to heaven. Before that, He instructed His chosen apostles through the Holy Spirit. 📖
## Acts 1:3
After suffering, He gave many convincing proofs He was alive. He appeared over forty days and spoke about God’s kingdom. 📖
## Acts 1:4
Gathered with them, He commanded, “Stay in Jerusalem. Wait for the Father’s promised gift that I told you about. 📖
## Acts 1:5
John baptized with water. In a few days you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.” 📖
## Acts 1:6
They asked, “Lord, will You restore Israel’s kingdom now?” ❓
## Acts 1:7
He answered, “The Father fixed times and seasons by His authority. Knowing them is not for you. 📖
## Acts 1:8
You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you. You will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, all Judea and Samaria, and to earth’s ends.” 📖
## Acts 1:9
They watched Him being taken up until a cloud hid Him. 📖
## Acts 1:10
As they watched the sky intently, two men in white stood beside them. 📖
## Acts 1:11
“Galileans, why stand looking into the sky? This same Jesus, taken to heaven, will return the same way you saw Him go.” 📖
## Acts 1:12
They returned to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, a Sabbath day’s journey from the city. 📖
## Acts 1:13
They entered their upper room: Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James. 📖
## Acts 1:14
They continued united in prayer with the women, Mary the mother of Jesus, and His brothers. 🙏
## Acts 1:15
Peter stood among about a hundred and twenty brothers. 📖
## Acts 1:16
“Scripture concerning Judas had to be fulfilled. The Holy Spirit foretold it through David. Judas guided those who arrested Jesus. 📖
## Acts 1:17
He belonged to our number and shared our ministry.” 📖
## Acts 1:18
Judas bought a field with the payment for his wickedness. He fell headlong there, burst open in the middle, and his intestines spilled out. 📖
## Acts 1:19
All Jerusalem heard. In their language they named it Akeldama, meaning Field of Blood. 📖
## Acts 1:20
“Psalms says, ‘May his place be deserted, with no inhabitant,’ and, ‘May another take his position.’ 📖
## Acts 1:21
We must choose a man who accompanied us throughout the Lord Jesus’ time among us, 📖
## Acts 1:22
from John’s baptism until Jesus was taken up. He must join us as a witness of His resurrection.” 📖
## Acts 1:23
They proposed Joseph called Barsabbas, also known as Justus, and Matthias. 📖
## Acts 1:24
They prayed, “Lord, You know every heart. Show which of these two You chose, 🙏
## Acts 1:25
for this ministry and apostleship that Judas abandoned to go to his rightful place.” 🙏
## Acts 1:26
They cast lots. Matthias was chosen by lot and added to the eleven apostles. 📖
