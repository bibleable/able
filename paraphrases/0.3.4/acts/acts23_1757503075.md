## Acts 23:1
Paul looked straight at the Sanhedrin. He said, “Brothers, I have lived before God with a completely good conscience to this day.” 📖

## Acts 23:2
The high priest Ananias ordered those near Paul to strike his mouth. 📖

## Acts 23:3
Paul told him, “God will strike you, you whitewashed wall! You sit to judge me by the law. Yet you break it by ordering me struck.” 📖

## Acts 23:4
Those nearby said, “How dare you insult God’s high priest!” 📖

## Acts 23:5
Paul replied, “Brothers, I did not know he was the high priest. Scripture says, ‘Do not speak evil of your people’s ruler.’” 📖

## Acts 23:6
Paul knew some were Sadducees and others Pharisees. He called out, “Brothers, I am a Pharisee, a Pharisee’s son. I stand trial because I hope in the resurrection of the dead.” 📖

## Acts 23:7
His words caused a dispute between Pharisees and Sadducees. The assembly divided. 📖

## Acts 23:8
Sadducees deny resurrection, angels, and spirits. Pharisees acknowledge them all. 📖

## Acts 23:9
A loud uproar began. Some Pharisee scribes stood and argued strongly. They said, “We find nothing wrong with this man. What if a spirit or angel spoke to him?” 📖

## Acts 23:10
The dispute became violent. The commander feared they would tear Paul apart. He ordered soldiers to go down, forcibly remove Paul, and bring him into the barracks. 📖

## Acts 23:11
The next night, the Lord stood near Paul. He said, “Take courage! You testified about Me in Jerusalem. You must also testify in Rome.” 📖

## Acts 23:12
At daylight, the Jews formed a plot. They swore not to eat or drink until they killed Paul. 📖

## Acts 23:13
More than forty men joined this plot. 📖

## Acts 23:14
They went to the chief priests and elders. They said, “We solemnly swore not to eat anything until we kill Paul. 📖

## Acts 23:15
You and the Sanhedrin must ask the commander to bring him down. Pretend you want to examine his case more carefully. We are ready to kill him on the way.” 📖

## Acts 23:16
Paul’s sister’s son heard about the plot. He went into the barracks and told Paul. 📖

## Acts 23:17
Paul called a centurion. He said, “Take this young man to the commander. He has something to tell him.” 📖

## Acts 23:18
The centurion brought him to the commander. He said, “Paul the prisoner asked me to bring this young man. He has something to tell you.” 📖

## Acts 23:19
The commander took the young man by the hand and drew him aside. He asked, “What do you need to tell me?” 📖

## Acts 23:20
He answered, “The Jews agreed to ask you to bring Paul before the Sanhedrin tomorrow. They pretend they want more information about him. 📖

## Acts 23:21
Do not agree. More than forty men wait to ambush him. They swore not to eat or drink until they kill him. They are ready and await your consent.” 📖

## Acts 23:22
The commander dismissed him with this instruction: “Tell no one you reported this to me.” 📖

## Acts 23:23
He called two centurions. He said, “Prepare two hundred soldiers, seventy horsemen, and two hundred spearmen. They must leave for Caesarea at the third hour of the night. 📖

## Acts 23:24
Provide animals for Paul to ride. Take him safely to Governor Felix.” 📖

## Acts 23:25
He wrote this letter: 📖

## Acts 23:26
“From Claudius Lysias. To His Excellency, Governor Felix: greetings. 📖

## Acts 23:27
The Jews seized this man and were about to kill him. I came with my troops to rescue him because I learned he was a Roman citizen. 📖

## Acts 23:28
I wanted to understand their charges. So I brought him before their Sanhedrin. 📖

## Acts 23:29
I found the accusation concerned their own law. There was no charge deserving death or imprisonment. 📖

## Acts 23:30
I learned of a plot against him and immediately sent him to you. I also ordered his accusers to present their case before you.” 📖

## Acts 23:31
The soldiers obeyed. They took Paul to Antipatris during the night. 📖

## Acts 23:32
The next day, they returned to the barracks. The horsemen continued with Paul. 📖

## Acts 23:33
The horsemen reached Caesarea. They delivered the letter to the governor and presented Paul to him. 📖

## Acts 23:34
The governor read the letter and asked which province Paul came from. He learned Paul was from Cilicia. 📖

## Acts 23:35
He said, “I will hear your case when your accusers arrive.” He ordered Paul guarded in Herod’s Praetorium. 📖
