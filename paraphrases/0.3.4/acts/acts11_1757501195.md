## Acts 11:1
The apostles and brothers throughout Judea soon heard that Gentiles had also accepted God’s word. 📖

## Acts 11:2
Peter went up to Jerusalem. The circumcised believers criticized him. 📖

## Acts 11:3
They said, “You visited uncircumcised men and ate with them.” 📖

## Acts 11:4
Peter explained all the events in order. 📖

## Acts 11:5
He said, “I was praying in Joppa. In a trance, I saw a vision. Something like a large sheet was lowered from heaven by its four corners. It came down to me. 📖

## Acts 11:6
I looked closely. I saw animals with four feet, wild animals, reptiles, and birds. 📖

## Acts 11:7
I heard a voice say, ‘Get up, Peter. Kill and eat.’ 📖

## Acts 11:8
I answered, ‘No, Lord. Nothing impure or unclean has ever entered my mouth.’ 📖

## Acts 11:9
The voice spoke from heaven a second time: ‘Do not call impure anything God has made clean.’ 📖

## Acts 11:10
This happened three times. Then everything was drawn back into heaven. 📖

## Acts 11:11
Just then, three men sent to me from Caesarea arrived at the house where I was staying. 📖

## Acts 11:12
The Spirit told me to go with them without hesitation. These six brothers also went with me. We entered the man’s home. 📖

## Acts 11:13
He told us he had seen an angel standing in his house. The angel said, ‘Send to Joppa for Simon, called Peter. 📖

## Acts 11:14
He will bring a message by which you and your whole household will be saved.’ 📖

## Acts 11:15
As I began speaking, the Holy Spirit came upon them, just as He came upon us at the beginning. 📖

## Acts 11:16
I remembered the Lord’s words: ‘John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.’ 📖

## Acts 11:17
God gave them the same gift He gave us who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ. Who was I to hinder God’s work?” 📖

## Acts 11:18
When they heard this, they stopped objecting and glorified God. They said, “God has granted even Gentiles repentance that leads to life.” 📖

## Acts 11:19
The persecution that began with Stephen had scattered believers. They traveled as far as Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch. They spoke the message only to Jews. 📖

## Acts 11:20
Some were men from Cyprus and Cyrene. They went to Antioch and also spoke to Greeks. They proclaimed the good news about the Lord Jesus. 📖

## Acts 11:21
The Lord’s hand was with them. Many people believed and turned to the Lord. 📖

## Acts 11:22
The Jerusalem church heard the news and sent Barnabas to Antioch. 📖

## Acts 11:23
He arrived and saw God’s grace. He rejoiced and encouraged everyone to remain in the Lord with all their hearts. 📖

## Acts 11:24
Barnabas was a good man, full of the Holy Spirit and faith. Many people were brought to the Lord. 📖

## Acts 11:25
Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul. 📖

## Acts 11:26
He found Saul and brought him to Antioch. For a full year, they met with the church and taught many people. The disciples were first called Christians in Antioch. 📖

## Acts 11:27
During those days, prophets came down from Jerusalem to Antioch. 📖

## Acts 11:28
One named Agabus stood and predicted through the Spirit that a severe famine would spread across the whole world. This happened during Claudius’ reign. 📖

## Acts 11:29
Each disciple decided to give what he could to help the brothers living in Judea. 📖

## Acts 11:30
They sent their gifts to the elders with Barnabas and Saul. 📖
