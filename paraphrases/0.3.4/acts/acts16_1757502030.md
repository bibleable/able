## Acts 16:1
Paul came to Derbe, then Lystra. There he found a disciple named Timothy. His mother was a Jewish believer, and his father was Greek. 📖

## Acts 16:2
The brothers in Lystra and Iconium spoke well of Timothy. 📖

## Acts 16:3
Paul wanted Timothy to travel with him. He circumcised Timothy because of the Jews in that area. They all knew his father was Greek. 📖

## Acts 16:4
As they traveled from town to town, they delivered the decisions from Jerusalem’s apostles and elders. The people were to obey these decisions. 📖

## Acts 16:5
The churches grew stronger in faith. Their numbers increased daily. 📖

## Acts 16:6
The Holy Spirit prevented them from speaking the word in the province of Asia. They traveled through Phrygia and Galatia. 📖

## Acts 16:7
At Mysia’s border, they tried to enter Bithynia. But the Spirit of Jesus did not permit them. 📖

## Acts 16:8
They passed by Mysia and went down to Troas. 📖

## Acts 16:9
During the night, Paul saw a vision. A Macedonian man stood and pleaded, “Come over to Macedonia and help us.” 📖

## Acts 16:10
After Paul saw the vision, we immediately prepared to leave for Macedonia. We concluded God had called us to preach the good news there. 📖

## Acts 16:11
We sailed straight from Troas to Samothrace. The next day, we sailed to Neapolis. 📖

## Acts 16:12
From there, we went to Philippi, a Roman colony and the leading city in that Macedonian district. We stayed several days. 📖

## Acts 16:13
On the Sabbath, we went outside the city gate beside the river. A place of prayer was customary there. We sat and spoke to the women gathered there. 📖

## Acts 16:14
Lydia, a seller of purple cloth from Thyatira, was listening. She worshiped God. The Lord opened her heart to respond to Paul’s message. 📖

## Acts 16:15
Lydia and her household were baptized. She urged us, “If you consider me a believer in the Lord, come stay at my house.” She persuaded us. 📖

## Acts 16:16
One day, we met a slave girl while going to the place of prayer. She had a spirit of divination. Her predictions earned much money for her masters. 📖

## Acts 16:17
She followed Paul and us, shouting, “These men are servants of the Most High God! They proclaim the way of salvation to you!” 📖

## Acts 16:18
She continued for many days. Paul became greatly annoyed. He turned and told the spirit, “In Jesus Christ’s name, I command you to leave her!” The spirit left at that moment. 📖

## Acts 16:19
Her owners saw that their hope of profit was gone. They seized Paul and Silas and dragged them before the authorities in the marketplace. 📖

## Acts 16:20
They brought them before the magistrates and said, “These men are Jews who disturb our city. 📖

## Acts 16:21
They promote customs that we Romans cannot lawfully adopt or practice.” 📖

## Acts 16:22
The crowd joined the attack on Paul and Silas. The magistrates ordered them stripped and beaten with rods. 📖

## Acts 16:23
After many blows, they were thrown into prison. The jailer was ordered to guard them securely. 📖

## Acts 16:24
He obeyed by putting them in the inner cell and fastening their feet in stocks. 📖

## Acts 16:25
About midnight, Paul and Silas prayed and sang hymns to God. The other prisoners listened. 📖

## Acts 16:26
Suddenly, a strong earthquake shook the prison’s foundations. All the doors immediately opened, and everyone’s chains came loose. 📖

## Acts 16:27
The jailer woke and saw the open doors. He thought the prisoners had escaped. He drew his sword and was about to kill himself. 📖

## Acts 16:28
Paul shouted, “Do not harm yourself! We are all here!” 📖

## Acts 16:29
The jailer called for lights and rushed in. Trembling, he fell before Paul and Silas. 📖

## Acts 16:30
He brought them out and asked, “Sirs, what must I do to be saved?” 📖

## Acts 16:31
They answered, “Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved, you and your household.” 📖

## Acts 16:32
They spoke the Lord’s word to him and everyone in his house. 📖

## Acts 16:33
At that hour of the night, he took them and washed their wounds. He and his whole household were baptized without delay. 📖

## Acts 16:34
He brought them into his home and gave them a meal. He and his entire household rejoiced because they had come to believe in God. 📖

## Acts 16:35
At daylight, the magistrates sent officers with this order: “Release those men.” 📖

## Acts 16:36
The jailer told Paul, “The magistrates ordered your release. Now you can leave in peace.” 📖

## Acts 16:37
Paul told the officers, “We are Roman citizens. They beat us publicly without trial and imprisoned us. Now they want to send us away secretly? Absolutely not! They must come themselves and escort us out!” 📖

## Acts 16:38
The officers told the magistrates. They were alarmed to learn Paul and Silas were Roman citizens. 📖

## Acts 16:39
The magistrates came to appease them. They escorted them out and asked them to leave the city. 📖

## Acts 16:40
Paul and Silas left prison and went to Lydia’s house. They saw the brothers and encouraged them. Then they left the city. 📖
