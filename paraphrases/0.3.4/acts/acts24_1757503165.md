## Acts 24:1
Five days later, the high priest Ananias arrived with elders and a lawyer named Tertullus. They presented their case against Paul to the governor. 📖

## Acts 24:2
Paul was called in. Tertullus began the prosecution: “You have given us lasting peace. Your foresight has brought improvements to this nation. 📖

## Acts 24:3
Most excellent Felix, we acknowledge this everywhere and in every way with complete gratitude. 📖

## Acts 24:4
I do not want to delay you further. Please be kind enough to hear us briefly. 📖

## Acts 24:5
We found this man to be a plague. He stirs up disputes among Jews worldwide. He leads the Nazarene sect. 📖

## Acts 24:6
He even tried to defile the temple. So we seized him. 📖

## Acts 24:8
Question him yourself. You can learn the truth about all our charges against him.” 📖

## Acts 24:9
The Jews agreed and said the charges were true. 📖

## Acts 24:10
The governor signaled for Paul to speak. Paul answered, “I know you have judged this nation for many years. So I gladly present my defense. 📖

## Acts 24:11
You can confirm that I went to Jerusalem to worship no more than twelve days ago. 📖

## Acts 24:12
My accusers found me debating with no one in the temple. I stirred up no crowd in the synagogues or city. 📖

## Acts 24:13
They cannot prove any of their charges against me. 📖

## Acts 24:14
But I admit I worship our fathers’ God according to the Way, which they call a sect. I believe everything laid down in the Law and written in the Prophets. 📖

## Acts 24:15
I share their hope in God. Both the righteous and the wicked will rise from death. 📖

## Acts 24:16
Because of this hope, I always strive for a clear conscience before God and people. 📖

## Acts 24:17
After several years, I returned to Jerusalem. I brought gifts for my people’s poor and came to present offerings. 📖

## Acts 24:18
They found me ceremonially clean in the temple. I was not stirring up a crowd or uproar. But some Jews from the province of Asia were there. 📖

## Acts 24:19
They should appear before you and bring charges if they have anything against me. 📖

## Acts 24:20
Otherwise, let these men name any crime they found when I stood before the Sanhedrin. 📖

## Acts 24:21
Perhaps it was this one statement I made among them: ‘I stand trial before you today concerning the resurrection of the dead.’” 📖

## Acts 24:22
Felix knew the Way well. He postponed the hearing, saying, “I will decide your case when Commander Lysias arrives.” 📖

## Acts 24:23
He ordered the centurion to guard Paul but allow him some freedom. Paul’s friends could attend to his needs. 📖

## Acts 24:24
Several days later, Felix returned with his Jewish wife, Drusilla. He sent for Paul and heard him speak about faith in Christ Jesus. 📖

## Acts 24:25
Paul explained righteousness, self-control, and the coming judgment. Felix became frightened and said, “Go for now. I will call you when I have time.” 📖

## Acts 24:26
Felix also hoped Paul would offer him a bribe. So he often sent for Paul and talked with him. 📖

## Acts 24:27
Two years later, Porcius Festus replaced Felix. Felix wanted to favor the Jews, so he left Paul imprisoned. 📖
