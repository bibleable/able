## Acts 19:1
Apollos was in Corinth. Paul traveled through the interior and reached Ephesus. He found some disciples there. 📖

## Acts 19:2
He asked, “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?” They answered, “No. We have not even heard that there is a Holy Spirit.” 📖

## Acts 19:3
Paul asked, “Into what were you baptized?” They replied, “John’s baptism.” 📖

## Acts 19:4
Paul explained, “John’s baptism was a baptism of repentance. He told people to believe in the One coming after him, Jesus.” 📖

## Acts 19:5
They heard this and were baptized into the Lord Jesus’ name. 📖

## Acts 19:6
Paul put his hands on them. The Holy Spirit came upon them, and they spoke in tongues and prophesied. 📖

## Acts 19:7
There were about twelve men in all. 📖

## Acts 19:8
Paul entered the synagogue and spoke boldly for three months. He reasoned persuasively about God’s kingdom. 📖

## Acts 19:9
Some stubbornly refused to believe and publicly slandered the Way. Paul left the synagogue with his disciples. He held daily discussions in Tyrannus’ lecture hall. 📖

## Acts 19:10
This continued for two years. Everyone living in the province of Asia, both Jews and Greeks, heard the Lord’s word. 📖

## Acts 19:11
God performed extraordinary miracles through Paul’s hands. 📖

## Acts 19:12
Even handkerchiefs and aprons that touched Paul were taken to sick people. Their diseases and evil spirits left them. 📖

## Acts 19:13
Some traveling Jewish exorcists tried using the Lord Jesus’ name over people with evil spirits. They said, “I command you by Jesus, whom Paul proclaims.” 📖

## Acts 19:14
Seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, did this. 📖

## Acts 19:15
One day, the evil spirit answered, “I know Jesus, and I know about Paul. But who are you?” 📖

## Acts 19:16
The man with the evil spirit jumped on them and overpowered them all. The attack was so violent that they fled the house naked and wounded. 📖

## Acts 19:17
All the Jews and Greeks living in Ephesus heard about this. Fear came over everyone. The Lord Jesus’ name was greatly honored. 📖

## Acts 19:18
Many believers now came forward. They confessed and disclosed their deeds. 📖

## Acts 19:19
Many who practiced magic brought their books and burned them publicly. The books’ total value was calculated at fifty thousand drachmas. 📖

## Acts 19:20
The Lord’s word continued to spread and prevail powerfully. 📖

## Acts 19:21
After these events, Paul resolved in the Spirit to travel through Macedonia and Achaia to Jerusalem. He said, “After that, I must also see Rome.” 📖

## Acts 19:22
He sent two helpers, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia. He stayed in the province of Asia for a time. 📖

## Acts 19:23
About then, a great disturbance arose over the Way. 📖

## Acts 19:24
It began with Demetrius, a silversmith who made silver shrines of Artemis. This brought much business to the craftsmen. 📖

## Acts 19:25
He gathered the craftsmen and workers in related trades. He said, “Men, you know our prosperity comes from this business. 📖

## Acts 19:26
You see and hear how Paul has persuaded many to turn away. This happens in Ephesus and nearly all the province of Asia. He says gods made by people are no gods at all. 📖

## Acts 19:27
Our business risks losing its reputation. The great goddess Artemis’ temple may also lose honor, and her majesty may fall. All the province of Asia and the whole world worship her.” 📖

## Acts 19:28
The men heard this and became furious. They shouted, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” 📖

## Acts 19:29
The whole city soon became confused. The people rushed into the theatre together. They dragged Gaius and Aristarchus with them, Paul’s Macedonian traveling companions. 📖

## Acts 19:30
Paul wanted to go before the assembly. But the disciples would not let him. 📖

## Acts 19:31
Some provincial officials in Asia were Paul’s friends. They also sent messages, begging him not to enter the theatre. 📖

## Acts 19:32
The assembly was in confusion. Some shouted one thing, and others another. Most did not even know why they were there. 📖

## Acts 19:33
The Jews in the crowd pushed Alexander forward to explain himself. He signaled for silence to present his defense to the people. 📖

## Acts 19:34
They realized he was a Jew. For about two hours, everyone shouted together, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” 📖

## Acts 19:35
The city clerk finally quieted them. He said, “Men of Ephesus, everyone knows our city guards the great Artemis’ temple and her image that fell from heaven. 📖

## Acts 19:36
These things cannot be denied. Be calm and do nothing rash. 📖

## Acts 19:37
You brought these men here. Yet they have neither robbed our temple nor blasphemed our goddess. 📖

## Acts 19:38
If Demetrius and his fellow craftsmen have a complaint, the courts are open and proconsuls are available. They should bring charges there. 📖

## Acts 19:39
Anything beyond this must be settled in a legal assembly. 📖

## Acts 19:40
Today’s events put us at risk of a charge of rioting. We have no justification for this disturbance.” 📖

## Acts 19:41
After saying this, he dismissed the assembly. 📖
