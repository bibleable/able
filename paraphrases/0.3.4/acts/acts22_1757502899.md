## Acts 22:1
“Brothers and fathers, listen now to my defense before you.” 📖

## Acts 22:2
They heard Paul speak Hebrew and became even quieter. Then he said, 📖

## Acts 22:3
“I am a Jew, born in Tarsus in Cilicia and raised in this city. Gamaliel taught me strict obedience to our fathers’ law. I was as zealous for God as any of you today. 📖

## Acts 22:4
I persecuted this Way even to death. I arrested men and women and imprisoned them. 📖

## Acts 22:5
The high priest and whole Council can confirm this. I obtained their letters to their brothers in Damascus. I traveled there to arrest these people and bring them to Jerusalem for punishment. 📖

## Acts 22:6
About noon, as I approached Damascus, a bright light from heaven suddenly flashed around me. 📖

## Acts 22:7
I fell to the ground. I heard a voice say, ‘Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me?’ 📖

## Acts 22:8
I asked, ‘Who are You, Lord?’ He answered, ‘I am Jesus of Nazareth, whom you persecute.’ 📖

## Acts 22:9
My companions saw the light. But they could not understand the voice speaking to me. 📖

## Acts 22:10
I asked, ‘What should I do, Lord?’ He answered, ‘Get up and go into Damascus. There you will learn everything you have been appointed to do.’ 📖

## Acts 22:11
The light’s brightness blinded me. My companions led me by the hand into Damascus. 📖

## Acts 22:12
A man named Ananias lived there. He faithfully obeyed the law. All the Jews living there respected him. 📖

## Acts 22:13
He came and stood beside me. He said, ‘Brother Saul, receive your sight.’ At that moment, I could see him. 📖

## Acts 22:14
He said, ‘Our fathers’ God appointed you to know His will, see the Righteous One, and hear His voice. 📖

## Acts 22:15
You will witness to everyone about what you have seen and heard. 📖

## Acts 22:16
What are you waiting for? Get up, be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling on His name.’ 📖

## Acts 22:17
Later, I returned to Jerusalem. While praying at the temple, I fell into a trance. 📖

## Acts 22:18
I saw the Lord say, ‘Hurry! Leave Jerusalem quickly. The people here will not accept your testimony about Me.’ 📖

## Acts 22:19
I answered, ‘Lord, they know I went from synagogue to synagogue, imprisoning and beating those who believed in You. 📖

## Acts 22:20
When Your witness Stephen’s blood was shed, I stood there approving. I guarded the garments of those who killed him.’ 📖

## Acts 22:21
He told me, ‘Go! I will send you far away to the Gentiles.’” 📖

## Acts 22:22
The crowd listened until Paul said this. Then they shouted, “Remove him from the earth! He is not fit to live!” 📖

## Acts 22:23
They shouted, threw off their cloaks, and tossed dust into the air. 📖

## Acts 22:24
The commander ordered Paul taken into the barracks. He ordered him flogged and questioned to discover why the people shouted against him. 📖

## Acts 22:25
They stretched Paul out to strap him down. He asked the centurion standing there, “Can you lawfully flog a Roman citizen without trial?” 📖

## Acts 22:26
The centurion heard this and reported to the commander. He asked, “What are you going to do? This man is a Roman citizen.” 📖

## Acts 22:27
The commander went to Paul and asked, “Tell me, are you a Roman citizen?” Paul answered, “Yes.” 📖

## Acts 22:28
The commander said, “I paid much for my citizenship.” Paul replied, “I was born a citizen.” 📖

## Acts 22:29
Those about to question Paul immediately stepped back. The commander was alarmed when he realized he had chained a Roman citizen. 📖

## Acts 22:30
The next day, the commander wanted to learn the Jews’ real accusation against Paul. He released Paul and ordered the chief priests and whole Sanhedrin to gather. He brought Paul down to stand before them. 📖
