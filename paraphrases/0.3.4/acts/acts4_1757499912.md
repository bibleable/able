## Acts 4:1
While Peter and John spoke to the people, priests, the temple guard’s captain, and Sadducees approached them. 📖
## Acts 4:2
They were greatly disturbed by the apostles teaching the people and proclaiming the resurrection of the dead in Jesus. 📖
## Acts 4:3
They arrested Peter and John. Since evening had come, they kept them in custody until the next day. 📖
## Acts 4:4
Many listeners believed. The number of men reached about five thousand. 📖
## Acts 4:5
The next day, rulers, elders, and scribes assembled in Jerusalem. 📖
## Acts 4:6
High priest Annas, Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and many others from the high priest’s family also gathered. 📖
## Acts 4:7
They asked Peter and John by what power or name they acted. ❓
## Acts 4:8
Filled with the Holy Spirit, Peter answered, “Rulers and elders, 📖
## Acts 4:9
if you question our kindness to a lame man and how he was healed, 📖
## Acts 4:10
know, all of you and all Israel, that he stands healed in Jesus Christ of Nazareth’s name. You crucified Him, but God raised Him from death. 📖
## Acts 4:11
Jesus is the stone you builders rejected that became the cornerstone. 📖
## Acts 4:12
Salvation comes from no one else. No other name under heaven was given to people by which we must be saved.” 📖
## Acts 4:13
They saw Peter’s and John’s boldness and realized they were unschooled, ordinary men. They marveled and recognized their association with Jesus. 📖
## Acts 4:14
The healed man stood beside them, leaving no answer. 📖
## Acts 4:15
They ordered them outside the Sanhedrin and conferred. 📖
## Acts 4:16
“What shall we do? All Jerusalem knows a remarkable miracle occurred through them. We cannot deny it. 📖
## Acts 4:17
To stop this message spreading further among the people, warn them never to speak to anyone in this name.” 📖
## Acts 4:18
They called them back and forbade all speaking or teaching in Jesus’ name. 📖
## Acts 4:19
Peter and John answered, “Judge whether God considers obeying you rather than Him right. 📖
## Acts 4:20
We cannot stop telling what we saw and heard.” 📖
## Acts 4:21
They threatened them further and released them. Everyone glorified God, leaving no way to punish them. 📖
## Acts 4:22
The miraculously healed man was over forty years old. 📖
## Acts 4:23
After their release, Peter and John returned to their own people. They reported everything the chief priests and elders said to them. 📖
## Acts 4:24
The believers prayed united: “Sovereign Lord, You made heaven, earth, sea, and everything in them. 🙏
## Acts 4:25
Through our father David, Your servant, You spoke by the Holy Spirit. ‘Why do nations rage and peoples plot vainly? 🙏
## Acts 4:26
Earth’s kings and rulers gather against the Lord and His Anointed One.’ 🙏
## Acts 4:27
Here Herod, Pontius Pilate, Gentiles, and Israel conspired against Jesus, Your holy servant whom You anointed. 🙏
## Acts 4:28
They fulfilled what Your hand and will decided beforehand. 🙏
## Acts 4:29
Lord, consider their threats. Give Your servants complete boldness to speak Your word, 🙏
## Acts 4:30
while You stretch Your hand to heal and perform signs and wonders through Your holy servant Jesus’ name.” 🙏
## Acts 4:31
Their meeting place shook after prayer. All were filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke God’s word boldly. 📖
## Acts 4:32
The believers were one in heart and soul. None claimed his possessions as his own. They shared everything. ❤️
## Acts 4:33
The apostles powerfully testified to the Lord Jesus’ resurrection. Abundant grace rested on all. 📖
## Acts 4:34
None lacked necessities because landowners and homeowners sold property and brought the proceeds, 📖
## Acts 4:35
laying them at the apostles’ feet for distribution according to need. 📖
## Acts 4:36
Joseph was a Levite from Cyprus. The apostles called him Barnabas, meaning Son of Encouragement. 📖
## Acts 4:37
He sold a field he owned and brought the money. He laid it at the apostles’ feet. 📖
