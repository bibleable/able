## Acts 8:1
Saul approved of Stephen’s death. That day, severe persecution began against the Jerusalem church. All except the apostles scattered throughout Judea and Samaria. 📖

## Acts 8:2
Men who feared God buried Stephen and mourned deeply for him. 📖

## Acts 8:3
Saul began destroying the church. He went from house to house, dragged men and women away, and put them in prison. 📖

## Acts 8:4
Those who scattered preached the word wherever they went. 📖

## Acts 8:5
Philip went down to a city in Samaria. He proclaimed the Christ to its people. 📖

## Acts 8:6
The crowds all listened closely to Philip’s message. They also paid attention to the signs they saw him perform. 📖

## Acts 8:7
Unclean spirits came out of many possessed people with loud cries. Many paralyzed people and people unable to walk were healed. 📖

## Acts 8:8
This brought great joy to that city. 📖

## Acts 8:9
Previously, a man named Simon practiced sorcery in the city. He amazed Samaria’s people and claimed to be someone great. 📖

## Acts 8:10
Everyone, from the least to the greatest, listened to him. They said, “This man is the divine power called the Great Power.” 📖

## Acts 8:11
They listened closely because his sorcery had amazed them for a long time. 📖

## Acts 8:12
Philip preached the good news about God’s kingdom and Jesus Christ’s name. When people believed him, both men and women were baptized. 📖

## Acts 8:13
Simon also believed and was baptized. He followed Philip closely. The great signs and miracles he saw amazed him. 📖

## Acts 8:14
The apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had accepted God’s word. They sent Peter and John there. 📖

## Acts 8:15
When they arrived, they prayed that the people would receive the Holy Spirit. 📖

## Acts 8:16
The Holy Spirit had not yet come upon any of them. They had only been baptized into the Lord Jesus’ name. 📖

## Acts 8:17
Peter and John put their hands on them. Then they received the Holy Spirit. 📖

## Acts 8:18
Simon saw that people received the Spirit through the apostles placing their hands on them. He offered the apostles money. 📖

## Acts 8:19
He said, “Give me this power too. Then everyone I put my hands on may receive the Holy Spirit.” 📖

## Acts 8:20
Peter answered, “May your silver perish with you! You thought you could buy God’s gift with money. 📖

## Acts 8:21
You have no part or share in our ministry. Your heart is not right before God. 📖

## Acts 8:22
Therefore, repent of your wickedness and pray to the Lord. Perhaps He will forgive the intent of your heart. 📖

## Acts 8:23
I see that bitterness poisons you and sin holds you captive.” 📖

## Acts 8:24
Simon answered, “Pray to the Lord for me. Ask that none of these things you have said happen to me.” 📖

## Acts 8:25
Peter and John testified and spoke the Lord’s word. Then they returned to Jerusalem. On their way, they preached the good news in many Samaritan villages. 📖

## Acts 8:26
An angel of the Lord told Philip, “Get up and go south. Take the desert road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza.” 📖

## Acts 8:27
Philip went and met an Ethiopian eunuch on the way. This court official managed the entire treasury of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians. He had gone to Jerusalem to worship. 📖

## Acts 8:28
He was returning home. He sat in his chariot and read Isaiah the prophet. 📖

## Acts 8:29
The Spirit told Philip, “Go over to that chariot and stay beside it.” 📖

## Acts 8:30
Philip ran over and heard the man reading Isaiah the prophet. He asked, “Do you understand what you are reading?” 📖

## Acts 8:31
The man replied, “How can I, unless someone guides me?” He invited Philip to get in and sit with him. 📖

## Acts 8:32
The eunuch was reading this Scripture: “He was led like a sheep to slaughter. Like a lamb silent before its shearer, He did not open His mouth. 📖

## Acts 8:33
In His humiliation, He was denied justice. Who can recount His descendants? His life was taken from the earth.” 📖

## Acts 8:34
The eunuch asked Philip, “Tell me who the prophet means. Is he talking about himself or someone else?” 📖

## Acts 8:35
Philip began with that very Scripture. He told the man the good news about Jesus. 📖

## Acts 8:36
They traveled along the road and came to some water. The eunuch said, “Look, here is water! What prevents me from being baptized?” 📖

## Acts 8:37

## Acts 8:38
He ordered the chariot to stop. Philip and the eunuch both went down into the water. Philip baptized him. 📖

## Acts 8:39
When they came up from the water, the Lord’s Spirit carried Philip away. The eunuch did not see him again. He continued his journey with joy. 📖

## Acts 8:40
Philip appeared at Azotus. He traveled through the region, preaching the good news in every town until he reached Caesarea. 📖
