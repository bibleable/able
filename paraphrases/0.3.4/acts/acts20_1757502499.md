## Acts 20:1
After the uproar ended, Paul called the disciples together and encouraged them. He said goodbye and left for Macedonia. 📖

## Acts 20:2
He traveled through that region and spoke many encouraging words. Then he reached Greece. 📖

## Acts 20:3
He stayed three months. As he prepared to sail to Syria, the Jews plotted against him. He decided to return through Macedonia. 📖

## Acts 20:4
His companions were Sopater, Pyrrhus’ son from Berea, Aristarchus and Secundus from Thessalonica, Gaius from Derbe, Timothy, and Tychicus and Trophimus from Asia. 📖

## Acts 20:5
These men went ahead and waited for us in Troas. 📖

## Acts 20:6
After the Feast of Unleavened Bread, we sailed from Philippi. Five days later, we joined them in Troas. We stayed seven days. 📖

## Acts 20:7
On the week’s first day, we gathered to break bread. Paul planned to leave the next day. He spoke to the people until midnight. 📖

## Acts 20:8
Many lamps burned in the upstairs room where we gathered. 📖

## Acts 20:9
A young man named Eutychus sat in the window. He fell deeply asleep as Paul continued speaking. He fell from the third floor and was picked up dead. 📖

## Acts 20:10
Paul went down, threw himself on the young man, and embraced him. He said, “Do not be alarmed! He is still alive!” 📖

## Acts 20:11
Paul went back upstairs, broke bread, and ate. He spoke until dawn, then left. 📖

## Acts 20:12
The people took the young man home alive. They were greatly relieved. 📖

## Acts 20:13
We went ahead to the ship and sailed to Assos to take Paul aboard. He had arranged this because he would travel there on foot. 📖

## Acts 20:14
He met us at Assos. We took him aboard and sailed to Mitylene. 📖

## Acts 20:15
The next day, we sailed to a point opposite Chios. The following day, we reached Samos. The day after that, we came to Miletus. 📖

## Acts 20:16
Paul decided to sail past Ephesus to avoid spending time in the province of Asia. He hurried to reach Jerusalem by Pentecost, if possible. 📖

## Acts 20:17
From Miletus, Paul sent for the Ephesian church’s elders. 📖

## Acts 20:18
When they arrived, he said, “You know how I lived throughout my time with you. You saw this from my first day in Asia. 📖

## Acts 20:19
I served the Lord with great humility and tears. This was especially so during the trials caused by the Jews’ plots. 📖

## Acts 20:20
I held back nothing helpful to you. I taught you publicly and from house to house. 📖

## Acts 20:21
I testified to both Jews and Greeks about repentance toward God and faith in our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## Acts 20:22
Now the Spirit compels me to go to Jerusalem. I do not know what will happen there. 📖

## Acts 20:23
I only know the Holy Spirit warns me in town after town. Chains and suffering await me. 📖

## Acts 20:24
I count my life as worth nothing to me, if only I finish my course and ministry from the Lord Jesus. That ministry is to testify to the good news of God’s grace. 📖

## Acts 20:25
I preached the kingdom among you. Now I know none of you will see my face again. 📖

## Acts 20:26
Therefore, I testify today that I am innocent of everyone’s blood. 📖

## Acts 20:27
I did not hold back from declaring God’s entire will to you. 📖

## Acts 20:28
Watch yourselves and the whole flock the Holy Spirit made you oversee. Shepherd God’s church, which He bought with His own blood. 📖

## Acts 20:29
I know savage wolves will enter among you after I leave. They will not spare the flock. 📖

## Acts 20:30
Even men from your own number will rise and twist the truth. They will draw disciples away to follow them. 📖

## Acts 20:31
So be alert. Remember how I warned each of you with tears, night and day, without stopping for three years. 📖

## Acts 20:32
I now entrust you to God and the word of His grace. It can strengthen you and give you an inheritance among everyone made holy. 📖

## Acts 20:33
I have not desired anyone’s silver, gold, or clothing. 📖

## Acts 20:34
You know my own hands provided for my needs and those of my companions. 📖

## Acts 20:35
In everything, I showed you that we must work hard like this to help the weak. Remember the Lord Jesus’ own words: ‘Giving is more blessed than receiving.’” 📖

## Acts 20:36
After saying this, Paul knelt with all of them and prayed. 📖

## Acts 20:37
They all wept openly, embraced Paul, and kissed him. 📖

## Acts 20:38
They grieved especially because he said they would never see his face again. They accompanied him to the ship. 📖
