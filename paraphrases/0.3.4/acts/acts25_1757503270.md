## Acts 25:1
Three days after arriving in the province, Festus went up from Caesarea to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 25:2
The chief priests and Jewish leaders presented their case against Paul. They urged Festus to grant their request. 📖

## Acts 25:3
They wanted him to summon Paul to Jerusalem as a favor to them against Paul. They planned an ambush to kill Paul on the way. 📖

## Acts 25:4
Festus answered, “Paul is held in Caesarea. I will go there soon myself. 📖

## Acts 25:5
Let some of your leaders come with me. If he has done anything wrong, accuse him there.” 📖

## Acts 25:6
Festus stayed with them no more than eight or ten days. He returned to Caesarea. The next day, he sat on the judgment seat and ordered Paul brought in. 📖

## Acts 25:7
Paul arrived. The Jews from Jerusalem stood around him. They brought many serious charges they could not prove. 📖

## Acts 25:8
Paul defended himself: “I committed no offense against the Jews’ law, the temple, or Caesar.” 📖

## Acts 25:9
Festus wanted to favor the Jews. He asked Paul, “Will you go to Jerusalem and stand trial before me there on these charges?” 📖

## Acts 25:10
Paul replied, “I stand before Caesar’s judgment seat, where I should be tried. I have done the Jews no wrong, as you know very well. 📖

## Acts 25:11
If I committed a crime deserving death, I do not refuse to die. But if their accusations are false, no one may hand me over to them. I appeal to Caesar!” 📖

## Acts 25:12
Festus consulted his council. He answered, “You appealed to Caesar. You will go to Caesar!” 📖

## Acts 25:13
Several days later, King Agrippa and Bernice came to Caesarea to pay their respects to Festus. 📖

## Acts 25:14
They stayed several days. Festus explained Paul’s case to the king. He said, “Felix left a man in prison. 📖

## Acts 25:15
In Jerusalem, the chief priests and Jewish elders presented their case against him. They requested a judgment against him. 📖

## Acts 25:16
I told them the Roman custom. A man must face his accusers and have a chance to defend himself before being handed over. 📖

## Acts 25:17
They came here with me. I did not delay. The next day, I sat on the judgment seat and ordered the man brought in. 📖

## Acts 25:18
His accusers stood to speak. But they accused him of none of the crimes I expected. 📖

## Acts 25:19
They disputed matters of their religion and a man named Jesus. Jesus had died, but Paul insisted He was alive. 📖

## Acts 25:20
I did not know how to investigate these matters. I asked whether he would go to Jerusalem for trial on these charges. 📖

## Acts 25:21
Paul appealed to remain in custody for the Emperor’s decision. I ordered him held until I could send him to Caesar.” 📖

## Acts 25:22
Agrippa told Festus, “I would like to hear this man myself.” Festus replied, “You will hear him tomorrow.” 📖

## Acts 25:23
The next day, Agrippa and Bernice entered the auditorium with great ceremony. Commanders and the city’s leading men accompanied them. Festus ordered Paul brought in. 📖

## Acts 25:24
Festus said, “King Agrippa and everyone here, you see this man. The whole Jewish community petitioned me here and in Jerusalem. They shouted that he should live no longer. 📖

## Acts 25:25
I found he did nothing deserving death. But he appealed to the Emperor, so I decided to send him. 📖

## Acts 25:26
I have nothing definite to write to our sovereign about him. I brought him before you all, especially King Agrippa. After this inquiry, I may have something to write. 📖

## Acts 25:27
Sending a prisoner without specifying the charges seems unreasonable to me.” 📖
