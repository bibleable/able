## Acts 13:1
Antioch’s church had prophets and teachers. They were Barnabas, Simeon called Niger, Lucius of Cyrene, Manaen, and Saul. Manaen had been raised with Herod the tetrarch. 📖

## Acts 13:2
They worshiped the Lord and fasted. The Holy Spirit said, “Set Barnabas and Saul apart for Me. I have called them to a particular work.” 📖

## Acts 13:3
After fasting and praying, they put their hands on Barnabas and Saul and sent them out. 📖

## Acts 13:4
The Holy Spirit sent Barnabas and Saul. They went down to Seleucia and sailed from there to Cyprus. 📖

## Acts 13:5
At Salamis, they proclaimed God’s word in the Jewish synagogues. John was with them as their helper. 📖

## Acts 13:6
They traveled across the whole island to Paphos. There they found Bar-Jesus, a Jewish sorcerer and false prophet. 📖

## Acts 13:7
He attended Sergius Paulus, the proconsul. The proconsul was intelligent. He called for Barnabas and Saul because he wanted to hear God’s word. 📖

## Acts 13:8
The sorcerer Elymas opposed them. His name means sorcerer. He tried to turn the proconsul away from the faith. 📖

## Acts 13:9
Saul, also called Paul, was filled with the Holy Spirit. He looked straight at Elymas. 📖

## Acts 13:10
He said, “You are a child of the devil and an enemy of all righteousness! You are full of every kind of deceit and trickery. Will you never stop twisting the Lord’s straight ways? 📖

## Acts 13:11
Look, the Lord’s hand is against you. For a time, you will be blind and unable to see sunlight.” Immediately, mist and darkness covered Elymas. He felt around, seeking someone to lead him by the hand. 📖

## Acts 13:12
The proconsul saw what happened and believed. The teaching about the Lord amazed him. 📖

## Acts 13:13
Paul and his companions sailed from Paphos to Perga in Pamphylia. John left them there and returned to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 13:14
They traveled inland from Perga to Pisidian Antioch. On the Sabbath, they entered the synagogue and sat down. 📖

## Acts 13:15
The Law and the Prophets were read. Then the synagogue leaders sent this message: “Brothers, please speak if you have an encouraging word for the people.” 📖

## Acts 13:16
Paul stood, signaled with his hand, and spoke. “Men of Israel and Gentiles who fear God, listen! 📖

## Acts 13:17
The God of Israel chose our fathers. He made them a great people while they lived in Egypt. With an uplifted arm, He led them out. 📖

## Acts 13:18
He endured their conduct in the wilderness for about forty years. 📖

## Acts 13:19
He defeated seven nations in Canaan. He gave their land to His people as an inheritance. 📖

## Acts 13:20
All this took about 450 years. Then God gave them judges until the time of Samuel the prophet. 📖

## Acts 13:21
They asked for a king. God gave them Saul, Kish’s son, from Benjamin’s tribe. He ruled forty years. 📖

## Acts 13:22
God removed Saul and raised up David as king. He testified, ‘I found David, Jesse’s son, a man after My own heart. He will carry out My entire will.’ 📖

## Acts 13:23
As promised, God brought Israel the Savior Jesus from David’s descendants. 📖

## Acts 13:24
Before Jesus came, John preached a baptism of repentance to all Israel’s people. 📖

## Acts 13:25
As John finished his course, he said, ‘Who do you think I am? I am not that One. Someone comes after me whose sandals I am unworthy to untie.’ 📖

## Acts 13:26
Brothers, Abraham’s children, and Gentiles who fear God, this message of salvation has been sent to us. 📖

## Acts 13:27
Jerusalem’s people and rulers did not recognize Jesus. By condemning Him, they fulfilled the prophets’ words that are read every Sabbath. 📖

## Acts 13:28
They found no reason for a death sentence. Yet they asked Pilate to execute Him. 📖

## Acts 13:29
They carried out everything written about Him. Then they took Him down from the tree and put Him in a tomb. 📖

## Acts 13:30
But God raised Him from death. 📖

## Acts 13:31
For many days, He appeared to those who had traveled with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem. They are now His witnesses to our people. 📖

## Acts 13:32
We now proclaim the good news to you. God made a promise to our fathers. 📖

## Acts 13:33
He fulfilled it for us, their children, by raising up Jesus. The second Psalm says, ‘You are My Son. Today I have become Your Father.’ 📖

## Acts 13:34
God raised Him from death, never to decay. God said, ‘I will give you the holy and certain blessings promised to David.’ 📖

## Acts 13:35
Another Psalm says, ‘You will not let Your Holy One decay.’ 📖

## Acts 13:36
David served God’s purpose in his own generation. Then he fell asleep in death. He was buried with his fathers, and his body decayed. 📖

## Acts 13:37
But the One God raised from death did not decay. 📖

## Acts 13:38
Therefore, brothers, know this: forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you through Jesus. 📖

## Acts 13:39
Through Him, everyone who believes is justified from everything from which Moses’ law could not justify you. 📖

## Acts 13:40
Be careful that the prophets’ words do not happen to you: 📖

## Acts 13:41
‘Look, you scoffers! Wonder and perish! I am doing a work in your days that you would never believe, even if someone told you.’” 📖

## Acts 13:42
As Paul and Barnabas left the synagogue, the people urged them to continue this message the next Sabbath. 📖

## Acts 13:43
After the meeting ended, many Jews and devout converts to Judaism followed Paul and Barnabas. They spoke to them and urged them to continue in God’s grace. 📖

## Acts 13:44
The next Sabbath, almost the whole city gathered to hear the Lord’s word. 📖

## Acts 13:45
The Jews saw the crowds and became jealous. They contradicted Paul’s words with blasphemy. 📖

## Acts 13:46
Paul and Barnabas answered boldly, “God’s word had to be spoken to you first. You reject it and judge yourselves unworthy of eternal life. So we now turn to the Gentiles. 📖

## Acts 13:47
The Lord commanded us, ‘I made you a light for the Gentiles to bring salvation to earth’s ends.’” 📖

## Acts 13:48
The Gentiles heard this, rejoiced, and glorified the Lord’s word. Everyone appointed to eternal life believed. 📖

## Acts 13:49
The Lord’s word spread throughout the region. 📖

## Acts 13:50
The Jews stirred up prominent religious women and the city’s leading men. They provoked persecution against Paul and Barnabas and drove them out of their district. 📖

## Acts 13:51
Paul and Barnabas shook the dust from their feet in protest against them. They went to Iconium. 📖

## Acts 13:52
The disciples were filled with joy and the Holy Spirit. 📖
