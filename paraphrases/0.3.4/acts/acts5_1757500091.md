## Acts 5:1
Ananias and his wife Sapphira sold property. 📖
## Acts 5:2
With her full knowledge, he kept some proceeds and laid only part at the apostles’ feet. 📖
## Acts 5:3
Peter said, “Ananias, why did Satan fill your heart to lie to the Holy Spirit and withhold land proceeds? 📖
## Acts 5:4
The land was yours, and its sale money remained yours to use. Why plan this? You lied to God, not men.” 📖
## Acts 5:5
Ananias heard, fell, and died. Everyone hearing the news was greatly afraid. 📖
## Acts 5:6
Young men wrapped his body, carried it out, and buried it. 📖
## Acts 5:7
About three hours later, his wife came in. She did not know what had happened. 📖
## Acts 5:8
Peter asked, “Is this the price you and your husband received for the land?” She answered, “Yes, that is the price.” 📖
## Acts 5:9
“Why agree to test the Lord’s Spirit? The men who buried your husband are at the door. They will carry you out too.” 📖
## Acts 5:10
She immediately fell at his feet and died. The young men came in and found her dead. They carried her out and buried her beside her husband. 📖
## Acts 5:11
Great fear gripped the whole church and everyone hearing of these events. 📖
## Acts 5:12
The apostles performed many signs and wonders among the people. The believers gathered united in Solomon’s Colonnade. 📖
## Acts 5:13
People highly respected them, but no one else dared join them. 📖
## Acts 5:14
Increasing numbers of men and women believed in the Lord. 📖
## Acts 5:15
As a result, people laid the sick on cots and mats in the streets. They hoped Peter’s shadow might fall on some as he passed. 📖
## Acts 5:16
Crowds from nearby towns brought the sick and those tormented by unclean spirits to Jerusalem. All were healed. ❤️
## Acts 5:17
The high priest and all his associates, who belonged to the Sadducees’ party, became jealous. They went out. 📖
## Acts 5:18
They arrested the apostles and put them in the public jail. 📖
## Acts 5:19
An angel of the Lord opened the jail that night and led them out. 📖
## Acts 5:20
The angel said, “Go and stand in the temple courts. Tell the people the full message of this new life.” 📖
## Acts 5:21
At daybreak they obeyed and taught there. The high priest and associates assembled Israel’s elders, the full Sanhedrin, and sent for the apostles. 📖
## Acts 5:22
The officers arrived at the jail but did not find the apostles. They returned to report. 📖
## Acts 5:23
“The jail was locked, with guards at the doors. We opened it and found no one.” 📖
## Acts 5:24
The temple guard’s captain and chief priests heard this account. They were perplexed about what was happening. 📖
## Acts 5:25
Someone reported the jailed men teaching in the temple courts. 📖
## Acts 5:26
The captain and officers brought them without force, fearing people would stone them. 📖
## Acts 5:27
The high priest questioned them before the Sanhedrin. 📖
## Acts 5:28
“We strictly forbade teaching in this name. Yet you filled Jerusalem with your teaching and want us held responsible for this man’s blood.” 📖
## Acts 5:29
Peter and the apostles answered, “We must obey God rather than men. 📖
## Acts 5:30
Our fathers’ God raised Jesus, whom you killed by hanging on a tree. 📖
## Acts 5:31
God exalted Him to His right hand as Prince and Savior to grant Israel repentance and forgiveness of sins. 📖
## Acts 5:32
We are witnesses of these things, as is the Holy Spirit God gave to those obeying Him.” 📖
## Acts 5:33
The Council was furious and resolved to kill them. 📖
## Acts 5:34
Gamaliel was a Pharisee and law teacher respected by everyone. He stood in the Sanhedrin and ordered the men outside for a short time. 📖
## Acts 5:35
He said, “Israelites, consider carefully what you are about to do to these men. 📖
## Acts 5:36
Theudas claimed to be somebody and gained about four hundred followers. He was killed, his followers scattered, and his movement failed. 📖
## Acts 5:37
Then Judas the Galilean rose during the census and gained followers. He died, and they scattered. 📖
## Acts 5:38
In this case, I advise you to leave these men alone. Let them go. If their purpose or work comes from people, it will fail. 📖
## Acts 5:39
If it comes from God, you cannot stop them. You may even find yourselves fighting God.” 📖
## Acts 5:40
They yielded to Gamaliel. They called the apostles in and had them flogged. They forbade them to speak in Jesus’ name and released them. 📖
## Acts 5:41
The apostles left the Sanhedrin rejoicing. They had been counted worthy of suffering disgrace for the Name. 📖
## Acts 5:42
Daily, in the temple courts and houses, they kept teaching and proclaiming Jesus as the Christ. 📖
