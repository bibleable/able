## Acts 2:1
At Pentecost they gathered together in one place. 📖
## Acts 2:2
A sound like a mighty rushing wind came from heaven and filled the house where they sat. 📖
## Acts 2:3
Tongues like flames separated and rested on each of them. ✨
## Acts 2:4
All were filled with the Holy Spirit. They spoke in other tongues as the Spirit enabled them. 📖
## Acts 2:5
God-fearing Jews from every nation lived in Jerusalem. 📖
## Acts 2:6
The sound drew a bewildered crowd. Each heard his own language spoken. 📖
## Acts 2:7
They marveled, “Are these speakers not all Galileans? ❓
## Acts 2:8
How do we each hear our native language? ❓
## Acts 2:9
We are Parthians, Medes, Elamites, and residents of Mesopotamia, Judea, Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia, 📖
## Acts 2:10
Phrygia, Pamphylia, Egypt, and Libya near Cyrene, and visitors from Rome, 📖
## Acts 2:11
Jews and converts to Judaism, Cretans and Arabs. We hear God’s wonders in our own tongues!” 📖
## Acts 2:12
Amazed and puzzled, they asked what this meant. ❓
## Acts 2:13
Others mocked, “They are drunk on new wine!” 📖
## Acts 2:14
Peter stood with the Eleven and spoke loudly. “Men of Judea and Jerusalem’s residents, listen carefully. Understand this. 📖
## Acts 2:15
They are not drunk. It is only the third hour of the day. 📖
## Acts 2:16
This fulfills Joel’s prophecy: 📖
## Acts 2:17
‘In the last days I will pour My Spirit on all people, God says. Sons and daughters will prophesy. Young men will see visions, and old men dream dreams. 📖
## Acts 2:18
I will pour My Spirit on My male and female servants then, and they will prophesy. 📖
## Acts 2:19
I will show heavenly wonders and earthly signs, blood, fire, and billows of smoke. 📖
## Acts 2:20
The sun will become darkness and the moon blood before the great and glorious Day of the Lord. 📖
## Acts 2:21
Everyone calling on the Lord’s name will be saved.’ 📖
## Acts 2:22
Israelites, God certified Jesus of Nazareth to you by miracles, wonders, and signs. You know God performed these among you through Him. 📖
## Acts 2:23
God’s fixed plan and foreknowledge delivered Him up. You used lawless hands to nail Him to the cross and kill Him. 📖
## Acts 2:24
God raised Him, freeing Him from death’s agony. Death could not possibly hold Him. 📖
## Acts 2:25
David says about Him, ‘The Lord is always before me. He is at my right hand, so I will not be shaken. 📖
## Acts 2:26
My heart is therefore glad, my tongue rejoices, and my body dwells in hope. 📖
## Acts 2:27
This is because You will not abandon my soul to Hades or let Your Holy One decay. 📖
## Acts 2:28
You showed me life’s paths. Your presence will fill me with joy.’ 📖
## Acts 2:29
Brothers, David died and was buried. His tomb remains with us today. 📖
## Acts 2:30
As a prophet, he knew God swore to put his descendant on his throne. 📖
## Acts 2:31
David foresaw and spoke of Christ’s resurrection. Christ was not abandoned to Hades, and His body did not decay. 📖
## Acts 2:32
God raised this Jesus to life. We are all witnesses of this. 📖
## Acts 2:33
Exalted to God’s right hand, He received the Father’s promised Holy Spirit. He poured out what you see and hear. 📖
## Acts 2:34
David did not ascend to heaven. He says, ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand, 📖
## Acts 2:35
until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”’ 📖
## Acts 2:36
All Israel must know: God made Jesus, whom you crucified, both Lord and Christ!” 📖
## Acts 2:37
Deeply pierced in heart, the people asked Peter and the other apostles, “Brothers, what must we do?” ❓
## Acts 2:38
Peter said, “Repent and be baptized, every one, in Jesus Christ’s name for forgiveness of sins. You will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. 📖
## Acts 2:39
This promise is for you, your children, and all far away whom the Lord our God calls to Himself.” 📖
## Acts 2:40
He testified with many other words and urged them to be saved from that corrupt generation. 📖
## Acts 2:41
Those accepting his message were baptized. About three thousand joined the believers that day. 📖
## Acts 2:42
They devoted themselves to apostolic teaching, fellowship, breaking bread, and prayer. 🙏
## Acts 2:43
Everyone felt awe. The apostles performed many wonders and signs. 📖
## Acts 2:44
All believers stayed together and shared everything. 📖
## Acts 2:45
They sold possessions and goods and shared with anyone in need. ❤️
## Acts 2:46
They met united daily in the temple courts and broke bread from house to house. They ate with glad and sincere hearts. 📖
## Acts 2:47
They praised God and enjoyed everyone’s favor. The Lord added daily those being saved. 📖
