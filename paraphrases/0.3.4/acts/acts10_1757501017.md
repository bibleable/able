## Acts 10:1
Cornelius lived in Caesarea. He was a centurion in the Italian Regiment. 📖

## Acts 10:2
He and his entire household were devoted to God and feared Him. Cornelius gave generously to the people and prayed to God regularly. 📖

## Acts 10:3
One day, about the ninth hour, he clearly saw an angel of God in a vision. The angel came to him and called, “Cornelius!” 📖

## Acts 10:4
Cornelius stared in fear and asked, “What is it, Lord?” The angel answered, “Your prayers and gifts to the poor have risen as a memorial offering before God. 📖

## Acts 10:5
Send men to Joppa to call Simon, who is called Peter. 📖

## Acts 10:6
He is staying with Simon the tanner, whose house is beside the sea.” 📖

## Acts 10:7
After the angel left, Cornelius called two servants and a soldier who was devoted to God. The soldier was one of his attendants. 📖

## Acts 10:8
Cornelius explained what had happened and sent them to Joppa. 📖

## Acts 10:9
The next day, about the sixth hour, the men approached the city. Peter went up to the roof to pray. 📖

## Acts 10:10
He became hungry and wanted food. While the meal was being prepared, he fell into a trance. 📖

## Acts 10:11
He saw heaven open. Something like a large sheet was lowered to earth by its four corners. 📖

## Acts 10:12
It held all kinds of animals with four feet, reptiles, and birds. 📖

## Acts 10:13
A voice said, “Get up, Peter. Kill and eat!” 📖

## Acts 10:14
Peter answered, “No, Lord! I have never eaten anything impure or unclean.” 📖

## Acts 10:15
The voice spoke a second time: “Do not call impure anything God has made clean.” 📖

## Acts 10:16
This happened three times. Then the sheet was suddenly taken back into heaven. 📖

## Acts 10:17
Peter wondered what the vision meant. Meanwhile, Cornelius’ messengers found Simon’s house and came to the gate. 📖

## Acts 10:18
They called out and asked whether Simon, called Peter, was staying there. 📖

## Acts 10:19
Peter was still thinking about the vision. The Spirit told him, “Look, three men are seeking you. 📖

## Acts 10:20
Get up, go downstairs, and go with them without hesitation. I have sent them.” 📖

## Acts 10:21
Peter went down to the men. He said, “I am the one you are seeking. Why have you come?” 📖

## Acts 10:22
They answered, “Cornelius the centurion sent us. He is righteous and fears God. The whole Jewish nation respects him. A holy angel told him to invite you to his home to hear your message.” 📖

## Acts 10:23
Peter invited them inside as guests. The next day, he prepared and went with them. Some brothers from Joppa accompanied him. 📖

## Acts 10:24
The following day, Peter reached Caesarea. Cornelius expected them and had gathered his relatives and close friends. 📖

## Acts 10:25
As Peter was about to enter, Cornelius met him. He fell at Peter’s feet to worship him. 📖

## Acts 10:26
Peter helped him up and said, “Stand up. I am only a man myself.” 📖

## Acts 10:27
Peter talked with him as he went inside. He found many people gathered there. 📖

## Acts 10:28
He said, “You know it is unlawful for a Jew to associate with or visit a foreigner. But God showed me I must not call any person impure or unclean. 📖

## Acts 10:29
So I came without objection when invited. Why did you send for me?” 📖

## Acts 10:30
Cornelius answered, “Four days ago, I was praying in my house at this ninth hour. Suddenly, a man in shining clothes stood before me. 📖

## Acts 10:31
He said, ‘Cornelius, your prayer has been heard. God has remembered your gifts to the poor. 📖

## Acts 10:32
Send to Joppa for Simon, called Peter. He is staying at Simon the tanner’s house beside the sea.’ 📖

## Acts 10:33
I sent for you immediately. You were kind to come. Now we are all here before God to hear everything the Lord instructed you to tell us.” 📖

## Acts 10:34
Peter began speaking: “Now I truly understand that God shows no favoritism. 📖

## Acts 10:35
He welcomes people from every nation who fear Him and do what is right. 📖

## Acts 10:36
He sent His message to Israel’s people. He proclaimed the good news of peace through Jesus Christ, who is Lord of all. 📖

## Acts 10:37
You know what happened throughout Judea. It began in Galilee with the baptism John proclaimed. 📖

## Acts 10:38
God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and power. Jesus went around doing good and healing everyone the devil oppressed. God was with Him. 📖

## Acts 10:39
We witnessed everything He did in the land of the Jews and in Jerusalem. They killed Him by hanging Him on a tree. 📖

## Acts 10:40
But God raised Him on the third day and caused Him to be seen. 📖

## Acts 10:41
He appeared to witnesses God had chosen beforehand, rather than to everyone. We are those witnesses. We ate and drank with Him after He rose from death. 📖

## Acts 10:42
He commanded us to preach to the people and testify about Him. God appointed Him to judge the living and the dead. 📖

## Acts 10:43
All the prophets testify about Him. Everyone who believes in Him receives forgiveness of sins through His name.” 📖

## Acts 10:44
While Peter still spoke these words, the Holy Spirit came upon everyone hearing his message. 📖

## Acts 10:45
The circumcised believers who came with Peter were all amazed. The gift of the Holy Spirit had been poured out even on Gentiles. 📖

## Acts 10:46
They heard them speak in tongues and praise God. Then Peter said, 📖

## Acts 10:47
“Can anyone withhold water for these people’s baptism? They have received the Holy Spirit just as we have!” 📖

## Acts 10:48
He ordered their baptism in Jesus Christ’s name. Then they asked him to stay a few days. 📖
