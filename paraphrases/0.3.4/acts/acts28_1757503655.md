## Acts 28:1
After reaching shore safely, we learned the island was Malta. 📖

## Acts 28:2
The islanders showed extraordinary kindness. They lit a fire and welcomed us all because it was rainy and cold. 📖

## Acts 28:3
Paul gathered sticks and put them on the fire. The heat drove out a viper. It fastened onto his hand. 📖

## Acts 28:4
The islanders saw it hanging from his hand. They said, “This man must be a murderer. He escaped the sea, but Justice will not let him live.” 📖

## Acts 28:5
Paul shook the creature into the fire. He suffered no harm. 📖

## Acts 28:6
They expected him to swell or suddenly fall dead. They waited a long time and saw nothing unusual happen. They changed their minds and said he was a god. 📖

## Acts 28:7
Publius, the island’s chief official, owned an estate nearby. He welcomed us and treated us hospitably for three days. 📖

## Acts 28:8
Publius’ father lay sick with fever and dysentery. Paul visited him. He prayed, placed his hands on the man, and healed him. 📖

## Acts 28:9
Then the island’s other sick people came and were healed too. 📖

## Acts 28:10
The islanders honored us in many ways. When we prepared to sail, they supplied what we needed. 📖

## Acts 28:11
Three months later, we sailed in an Alexandrian ship that had spent winter at the island. Its figurehead was the Twin Brothers. 📖

## Acts 28:12
We landed at Syracuse and stayed three days. 📖

## Acts 28:13
We raised anchor and sailed to Rhegium. After one day, a south wind began blowing. On the second day, we reached Puteoli. 📖

## Acts 28:14
We found brothers there who invited us to stay a week. And so we came to Rome. 📖

## Acts 28:15
The brothers there heard about us. They traveled to the Forum of Appius and the Three Taverns to meet us. Paul saw them, took courage, and thanked God. 📖

## Acts 28:16
In Rome, Paul was allowed to stay by himself with a soldier guarding him. 📖

## Acts 28:17
Three days later, he called the Jewish leaders together. He said, “Brothers, I did nothing against our people or our fathers’ customs. Yet I was taken prisoner in Jerusalem and handed to the Romans. 📖

## Acts 28:18
They questioned me and wanted to release me. They found no reason to sentence me to death. 📖

## Acts 28:19
The Jews objected, so I had to appeal to Caesar. But I have no charge against my nation. 📖

## Acts 28:20
That is why I asked to see and speak to you. I wear this chain because of Israel’s hope.” 📖

## Acts 28:21
They replied, “We received no letters about you from Judea. No brothers from there have reported or mentioned anything bad about you. 📖

## Acts 28:22
We think your views are worth hearing. We know people everywhere speak against this sect.” 📖

## Acts 28:23
They arranged a day, and many came to Paul’s lodging. From morning until evening, he explained and testified about God’s kingdom. He used Moses’ Law and the Prophets to persuade them about Jesus. 📖

## Acts 28:24
Some were convinced by his words. Others refused to believe. 📖

## Acts 28:25
They disagreed and began leaving after Paul’s final statement. He said, “The Holy Spirit spoke rightly to your fathers through Isaiah the prophet: 📖

## Acts 28:26
‘Go and tell these people, “You will keep hearing but never understand. You will keep seeing but never perceive.” 📖

## Acts 28:27
These people’s hearts have become hard. They barely hear, and they have closed their eyes. Otherwise, they might see, hear, understand with their hearts, and turn. Then I would heal them.’ 📖

## Acts 28:28
Therefore, know that God’s salvation has been sent to the Gentiles. They will listen!” 📖

Paul stayed two full years in his own rented house. He welcomed everyone who visited him. 📖

## Acts 28:31
He boldly and freely proclaimed God’s kingdom and taught about the Lord Jesus Christ. 📖
