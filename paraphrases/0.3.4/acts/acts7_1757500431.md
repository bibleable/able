## Acts 7:1
The high priest asked Stephen, “Are these charges true?” 📖

## Acts 7:2
Stephen answered, “Brothers and fathers, listen. The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham in Mesopotamia, before he lived in Haran. 📖

## Acts 7:3
God said, ‘Leave your country and relatives. Go to the land I will show you.’ 📖

## Acts 7:4
Abraham left the land of the Chaldeans and settled in Haran. After his father died, God brought him here, to the land where you live. 📖

## Acts 7:5
God gave him no inheritance here, not even a foot of ground. Abraham had no child yet. But God promised this land to him and his descendants. 📖

## Acts 7:6
God said his descendants would live as foreigners in another land. They would be slaves and suffer mistreatment for four hundred years. 📖

## Acts 7:7
God said, ‘I will punish the nation that enslaves them. Then they will come out and worship Me in this place.’ 📖

## Acts 7:8
God gave Abraham the covenant of circumcision. Abraham became Isaac’s father and circumcised him on the eighth day. Isaac became Jacob’s father, and Jacob became the father of the twelve patriarchs. 📖

## Acts 7:9
The patriarchs were jealous of Joseph. They sold him as a slave into Egypt. But God was with him. 📖

## Acts 7:10
God rescued Joseph from all his troubles. He gave him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt. Pharaoh made Joseph ruler over Egypt and his entire household. 📖

## Acts 7:11
Famine and great suffering spread across Egypt and Canaan. Our fathers could not find food. 📖

## Acts 7:12
Jacob heard that Egypt had grain. He sent our fathers there on their first visit. 📖

## Acts 7:13
On their second visit, Joseph told his brothers who he was. Pharaoh also learned about Joseph’s family. 📖

## Acts 7:14
Joseph sent for his father Jacob and all his relatives. There were seventy-five people in all. 📖

## Acts 7:15
Jacob went down to Egypt. He and our fathers died there. 📖

## Acts 7:16
Their bones were brought back to Shechem and put in the tomb there. Abraham had bought it from Hamor’s sons and paid in silver. 📖

## Acts 7:17
The time for God to fulfill His promise to Abraham approached. Our people in Egypt greatly increased in number. 📖

## Acts 7:18
Then a different king ruled Egypt. He knew nothing about Joseph. 📖

## Acts 7:19
He exploited our people and oppressed our fathers. He forced them to abandon their infants so they would die. 📖

## Acts 7:20
Moses was born at that time. He was beautiful in God’s sight. His family cared for him in his father’s house for three months. 📖

## Acts 7:21
When Moses was put outside, Pharaoh’s daughter took him. She raised him as her own son. 📖

## Acts 7:22
Moses learned all the wisdom of the Egyptians. He was powerful in speech and action. 📖

## Acts 7:23
At forty years old, Moses decided to visit his brothers, the children of Israel. 📖

## Acts 7:24
He saw an Egyptian mistreating one of them. Moses defended the man and avenged him by killing the Egyptian who oppressed him. 📖

## Acts 7:25
Moses thought his brothers would understand that God was using him to rescue them. But they did not understand. 📖

## Acts 7:26
The next day, he found two Israelites fighting. He tried to bring peace between them. He said, ‘Men, you are brothers. Why do you mistreat each other?’ 📖

## Acts 7:27
The man who mistreated his neighbor pushed Moses aside. He asked, ‘Who made you our ruler and judge? 📖

## Acts 7:28
Do you want to kill me as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’ 📖

## Acts 7:29
When Moses heard this, he fled to Midian. He lived there as a foreigner and had two sons. 📖

## Acts 7:30
Forty years later, an angel appeared to Moses. The angel appeared in the flames of a burning bush in the desert near Mount Sinai. 📖

## Acts 7:31
Moses marveled at the sight. He approached to look more closely. Then he heard the Lord’s voice. 📖

## Acts 7:32
The Lord said, ‘I am your fathers’ God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’ Moses shook with fear and did not dare look. 📖

## Acts 7:33
The Lord said, ‘Remove your sandals. The place where you stand is holy ground. 📖

## Acts 7:34
I have seen My people’s oppression in Egypt. I have heard their groans and have come down to rescue them. Come now. I will send you back to Egypt.’ 📖

## Acts 7:35
They rejected Moses, asking, ‘Who made you ruler and judge?’ But God sent this same Moses as their ruler and redeemer. God did this through the angel who appeared in the bush. 📖

## Acts 7:36
Moses led them out. He performed wonders and signs in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and during forty years in the wilderness. 📖

## Acts 7:37
This same Moses told the Israelites, ‘God will raise up a prophet like me for you from among your brothers.’ 📖

## Acts 7:38
Moses was in the wilderness assembly with our fathers and the angel who spoke to him on Mount Sinai. He received living words to give us. 📖

## Acts 7:39
Our fathers refused to obey Moses. They rejected him. In their hearts, they turned back to Egypt. 📖

## Acts 7:40
They told Aaron, ‘Make us gods to go before us. This Moses led us out of Egypt. We do not know what happened to him.’ 📖

## Acts 7:41
They made a calf then and offered a sacrifice to this idol. They rejoiced in what their own hands had made. 📖

## Acts 7:42
God turned away and gave them over to worship the heavenly host. The book of the prophets says, ‘Israel, did you bring Me sacrifices and offerings during forty years in the wilderness? 📖

## Acts 7:43
You carried Molech’s tabernacle and the star of your god Rephan. You made these idols to worship them. Therefore, I will send you into exile beyond Babylon.’ 📖

## Acts 7:44
Our fathers had the tabernacle of the Testimony in the wilderness. It followed God’s exact instructions to Moses and the pattern Moses had seen. 📖

## Acts 7:45
Our fathers received it and brought it with Joshua when they took the nations’ land. God drove these nations out before them. The tabernacle remained until David’s time. 📖

## Acts 7:46
David found favor with God. He asked to provide a dwelling place for the God of Jacob. 📖

## Acts 7:47
But Solomon built the house for Him. 📖

## Acts 7:48
The Most High does not live in houses that human hands make. The prophet says, 📖

## Acts 7:49
‘Heaven is My throne, and earth is My footstool. What kind of house will you build for Me, says the Lord? Where will I rest? 📖

## Acts 7:50
Did My hand not make all these things?’ 📖

## Acts 7:51
You stubborn people have uncircumcised hearts and ears! You always resist the Holy Spirit, just as your fathers did. 📖

## Acts 7:52
Which prophet did your fathers not persecute? They even killed those who foretold the Righteous One’s coming. Now you have betrayed and murdered Him. 📖

## Acts 7:53
You received the law ordained by angels, but you have not obeyed it.” 📖

## Acts 7:54
The Sanhedrin members became furious when they heard this. They ground their teeth at Stephen. 📖

## Acts 7:55
Stephen was full of the Holy Spirit. He looked intently into heaven. He saw God’s glory and Jesus standing at God’s right hand. 📖

## Acts 7:56
He said, “Look! I see heaven open and the Son of Man standing at God’s right hand.” 📖

## Acts 7:57
They covered their ears, shouted loudly, and rushed together at him. 📖

## Acts 7:58
They dragged him outside the city and began throwing stones at him. The witnesses put their garments at the feet of a young man named Saul. 📖

## Acts 7:59
While they threw stones at him, Stephen appealed, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.” 📖

## Acts 7:60
He fell to his knees and shouted, “Lord, do not hold this sin against them.” After saying this, he fell asleep in death. 📖
