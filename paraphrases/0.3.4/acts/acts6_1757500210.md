## Acts 6:1
As disciples multiplied, the Grecian Jews among them complained against the Hebraic Jews. The Grecian Jews’ widows were overlooked in daily food distribution. 📖
## Acts 6:2
The Twelve gathered all disciples. “We should not neglect God’s word to serve tables. 📖
## Acts 6:3
Choose seven men from among you confirmed full of the Spirit and wisdom. We will assign this task to them. 📖
## Acts 6:4
We will devote ourselves to prayer and the ministry of the word.” 🙏
## Acts 6:5
Everyone approved. They chose Stephen, full of faith and the Holy Spirit, Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolas from Antioch, a convert to Judaism. 📖
## Acts 6:6
They presented the seven to the apostles, who prayed and laid hands on them. 📖
## Acts 6:7
God’s word spread. Jerusalem’s disciples multiplied rapidly, and many priests obeyed the faith. 📖
## Acts 6:8
Stephen, full of grace and power, performed great wonders and signs among the people. 📖
## Acts 6:9
Members of the Synagogue of the Freedmen disputed with him. They included Cyrenians, Alexandrians, and men from Cilicia and Asia. 📖
## Acts 6:10
They could not withstand his wisdom or the Spirit by whom he spoke. 📖
## Acts 6:11
They prompted men to claim Stephen blasphemed Moses and God. 📖
## Acts 6:12
They stirred the people, elders, and scribes, seized Stephen, and brought him to the Sanhedrin. 📖
## Acts 6:13
False witnesses said, “He never stops speaking against this holy place and the law. 📖
## Acts 6:14
We heard him say Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place and change the customs Moses handed down to us.” 📖
## Acts 6:15
Everyone seated in the Sanhedrin looked intently at Stephen. They saw his face was like an angel’s face. 📖
