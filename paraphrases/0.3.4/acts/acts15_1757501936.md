## Acts 15:1
Some men came down from Judea and taught the brothers. They said, “You cannot be saved unless you are circumcised according to Moses’ custom.” 📖

## Acts 15:2
Paul and Barnabas debated sharply with them. Paul, Barnabas, and some other believers were appointed to go to Jerusalem. They would discuss this question with the apostles and elders. 📖

## Acts 15:3
The church sent them on their way. They passed through Phoenicia and Samaria, describing the Gentiles’ conversion. This brought all the brothers great joy. 📖

## Acts 15:4
The Jerusalem church, apostles, and elders welcomed them. They reported everything God had done through them. 📖

## Acts 15:5
Some believers from the Pharisees’ party stood and said, “The Gentiles must be circumcised and required to obey Moses’ law.” 📖

## Acts 15:6
The apostles and elders met to examine this matter. 📖

## Acts 15:7
After much discussion, Peter stood and spoke. “Brothers, you know God chose among you in the early days. Through my lips, the Gentiles would hear the good news and believe. 📖

## Acts 15:8
God knows the heart. He showed His approval by giving them the Holy Spirit, just as He gave Him to us. 📖

## Acts 15:9
He made no distinction between us and them. He cleansed their hearts by faith. 📖

## Acts 15:10
Why test God now? You put a yoke on the disciples’ necks that neither we nor our fathers could bear. 📖

## Acts 15:11
We believe we are saved through the Lord Jesus’ grace, just as they are.” 📖

## Acts 15:12
The whole assembly became silent. They listened to Barnabas and Paul describe the signs and wonders God did through them among Gentiles. 📖

## Acts 15:13
After they finished, James said, “Brothers, listen to me! 📖

## Acts 15:14
Simon told us how God first visited the Gentiles to take a people from them for Himself. 📖

## Acts 15:15
The prophets’ words agree with this. Scripture says, 📖

## Acts 15:16
‘After this, I will return and rebuild David’s fallen tent. I will rebuild its ruins and restore it. 📖

## Acts 15:17
Then the remaining people and all Gentiles called by My name may seek the Lord. The Lord says this. He does these things. 📖

## Acts 15:18
They have been known for ages.’ 📖

## Acts 15:19
Therefore, I judge that we should not trouble the Gentiles who are turning to God. 📖

## Acts 15:20
We should write to them instead. Tell them to avoid food polluted by idols, sexual immorality, meat from strangled animals, and blood. 📖

## Acts 15:21
Moses has been proclaimed in every city since ancient times. His writings are read in the synagogues every Sabbath.” 📖

## Acts 15:22
The apostles, elders, and whole church decided to send chosen men to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas. They chose Judas, called Barsabbas, and Silas. These men were leaders among the brothers. 📖

## Acts 15:23
They sent this letter with them: “From the apostles and elders, your brothers. To the Gentile brothers in Antioch, Syria, and Cilicia: greetings. 📖

## Acts 15:24
We heard that some people left us without our authorization. Their words unsettled you and troubled your minds. 📖

## Acts 15:25
We all agreed to choose men to send with our beloved Barnabas and Paul. 📖

## Acts 15:26
Barnabas and Paul have risked their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. 📖

## Acts 15:27
We are sending Judas and Silas to tell you personally what we write here. 📖

## Acts 15:28
The Holy Spirit and we thought it good to give you only these essential requirements. 📖

## Acts 15:29
Avoid food sacrificed to idols, blood, meat from strangled animals, and sexual immorality. You will do well to avoid these things. Farewell.” 📖

## Acts 15:30
The men were sent off and went down to Antioch. They gathered the congregation and delivered the letter. 📖

## Acts 15:31
The people read it and rejoiced at its encouraging message. 📖

## Acts 15:32
Judas and Silas were also prophets. They spoke at length to encourage and strengthen the brothers. 📖

## Acts 15:33
After they stayed some time, the brothers sent them away in peace to those who had sent them. 📖

## Acts 15:35
Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch. With many others, they taught and preached the Lord’s word. 📖

## Acts 15:36
Later, Paul told Barnabas, “Let us return to every town where we proclaimed the Lord’s word. We can visit the brothers and see how they are doing.” 📖

## Acts 15:37
Barnabas wanted to take John, also called Mark. 📖

## Acts 15:38
Paul thought they should not take him. John had deserted them in Pamphylia and had not continued with them in the work. 📖

## Acts 15:39
They disagreed so sharply that they separated. Barnabas took Mark and sailed to Cyprus. 📖

## Acts 15:40
Paul chose Silas and left. The brothers entrusted him to the Lord’s grace. 📖

## Acts 15:41
He traveled through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches. 📖
