## Acts 21:1
We tore ourselves away from them and sailed straight to Cos. The next day, we reached Rhodes. From there, we went to Patara. 📖

## Acts 21:2
We found a ship crossing to Phoenicia. We boarded it and sailed. 📖

## Acts 21:3
We sighted Cyprus and passed south of it. We sailed to Syria and landed at Tyre, where the ship would unload its cargo. 📖

## Acts 21:4
We found the disciples in Tyre and stayed seven days. Through the Spirit, they repeatedly told Paul not to go up to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 21:5
When our time there ended, we continued our journey. All the disciples, with their wives and children, accompanied us outside the city. They knelt with us on the beach to pray. 📖

## Acts 21:6
We said goodbye and boarded the ship. They returned home. 📖

## Acts 21:7
We finished our voyage from Tyre at Ptolemais. We greeted the brothers and stayed one day. 📖

## Acts 21:8
The next day, we left for Caesarea. We stayed with Philip the evangelist. He was one of the Seven. 📖

## Acts 21:9
Philip had four unmarried daughters who prophesied. 📖

## Acts 21:10
After we stayed several days, a prophet named Agabus came down from Judea. 📖

## Acts 21:11
He came to us, took Paul’s belt, and bound his own hands and feet. He said, “The Holy Spirit says, ‘Jerusalem’s Jews will bind this belt’s owner like this and hand him over to Gentiles.’” 📖

## Acts 21:12
We and the people there heard this. We pleaded with Paul not to go up to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 21:13
Paul answered, “Why weep and break my heart? I am ready for imprisonment and even death in Jerusalem for the Lord Jesus’ name.” 📖

## Acts 21:14
He would not be persuaded. We stopped and said, “May the Lord’s will be done.” 📖

## Acts 21:15
After those days, we packed and went to Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 21:16
Some Caesarean disciples accompanied us. They took us to stay with Mnason from Cyprus, an early disciple. 📖

## Acts 21:17
The brothers welcomed us joyfully when we reached Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 21:18
The next day, Paul went with us to see James. All the elders were present. 📖

## Acts 21:19
Paul greeted them. He described, one by one, what God had done among the Gentiles through his ministry. 📖

## Acts 21:20
They heard this and glorified God. They told Paul, “Brother, you see how many thousands of Jews have believed. They are all zealous for the law. 📖

## Acts 21:21
They believe you teach Jews living among Gentiles to abandon Moses. They think you tell them not to circumcise their children or follow our customs. 📖

## Acts 21:22
What should we do? They will certainly hear you have come. 📖

## Acts 21:23
Do what we advise. Four men here have taken a vow. 📖

## Acts 21:24
Take them, purify yourself with them, and pay their expenses so they can shave their heads. Everyone will know the rumors about you are false. They will see you also obey the law. 📖

## Acts 21:25
We wrote our decision to Gentile believers. They must avoid food sacrificed to idols, blood, meat from strangled animals, and sexual immorality.” 📖

## Acts 21:26
The next day, Paul took the men and purified himself with them. He entered the temple to announce when their purification would end and an offering would be made for each man. 📖

## Acts 21:27
The seven days were almost over. Some Jews from Asia saw Paul at the temple. They stirred up the whole crowd and seized him. 📖

## Acts 21:28
They shouted, “Men of Israel, help! This man teaches everyone everywhere against our people, law, and this place. He even brought Greeks into the temple and defiled this holy place.” 📖

## Acts 21:29
They had earlier seen Trophimus the Ephesian with Paul in the city. They assumed Paul had brought him into the temple. 📖

## Acts 21:30
The whole city was stirred up, and people rushed together. They seized Paul and dragged him from the temple. The gates were immediately shut. 📖

## Acts 21:31
They tried to kill him. The Roman regiment’s commander heard a report that all Jerusalem was in turmoil. 📖

## Acts 21:32
He immediately took soldiers and centurions and ran down to the crowd. The people saw them and stopped beating Paul. 📖

## Acts 21:33
The commander arrested Paul and ordered him bound with two chains. He asked who Paul was and what he had done. 📖

## Acts 21:34
Some shouted one thing, and others another. The uproar prevented the commander from learning the truth. He ordered Paul taken into the barracks. 📖

## Acts 21:35
At the steps, the soldiers had to carry Paul because of the mob’s violence. 📖

## Acts 21:36
The crowd followed, repeatedly shouting, “Away with him!” 📖

## Acts 21:37
As they were about to enter the barracks, Paul asked the commander, “May I speak to you?” He replied, “Do you speak Greek? 📖

## Acts 21:38
Are you not the Egyptian who recently started a rebellion and led four thousand Assassins into the wilderness?” 📖

## Acts 21:39
Paul answered, “I am a Jew from Tarsus in Cilicia, a citizen of no ordinary city. Please let me speak to the people.” 📖

## Acts 21:40
The commander permitted him. Paul stood on the steps and signaled to the crowd. They became very quiet. He spoke to them in Hebrew. 📖
