## Acts 12:1
About that time, King Herod began harming some church members. 📖

## Acts 12:2
He had James, John’s brother, killed with the sword. 📖

## Acts 12:3
Herod saw that this pleased the Jews. During the Feast of Unleavened Bread, he also seized Peter. 📖

## Acts 12:4
He imprisoned Peter under the guard of four squads, each with four soldiers. He planned to bring Peter before the people after Passover. 📖

## Acts 12:5
Peter remained in prison. But the church prayed earnestly to God for him. 📖

## Acts 12:6
The night before Herod planned to bring him to trial, Peter slept between two soldiers. Two chains bound him. Guards stood at the prison entrance. 📖

## Acts 12:7
Suddenly, an angel of the Lord appeared, and light shone in the cell. He tapped Peter’s side and woke him, saying, “Get up quickly.” The chains fell from Peter’s wrists. 📖

## Acts 12:8
The angel said, “Get dressed and put on your sandals.” Peter obeyed. The angel said, “Wrap your cloak around you and follow me.” 📖

## Acts 12:9
Peter followed him out. He did not realize the angel’s actions were real. He thought he was seeing a vision. 📖

## Acts 12:10
They passed the first and second guards. The iron gate leading into the city opened by itself. They went outside and walked one block. Suddenly, the angel left Peter. 📖

## Acts 12:11
Peter came to his senses. He said, “Now I know the Lord sent His angel. He rescued me from Herod’s power and everything the Jewish people expected.” 📖

## Acts 12:12
After realizing this, he went to Mary’s house. She was the mother of John, also called Mark. Many people had gathered there to pray. 📖

## Acts 12:13
Peter knocked at the outer gate. A servant girl named Rhoda came to answer. 📖

## Acts 12:14
She recognized Peter’s voice and was overjoyed. She forgot to open the gate and ran inside. She announced, “Peter is standing at the gate!” 📖

## Acts 12:15
They told her, “You are out of your mind.” She kept insisting. They said, “It must be his angel.” 📖

## Acts 12:16
Peter kept knocking. They opened the door and saw him. They were amazed. 📖

## Acts 12:17
Peter signaled with his hand for silence. He described how the Lord brought him out of prison. He said, “Tell James and the brothers.” Then he left for another place. 📖

## Acts 12:18
At dawn, the soldiers were greatly disturbed about what had happened to Peter. 📖

## Acts 12:19
Herod searched unsuccessfully for Peter. He questioned the guards and ordered their execution. Then he went down from Judea to Caesarea and stayed there for some time. 📖

## Acts 12:20
Herod was in a furious dispute with Tyre’s and Sidon’s people. They gathered before him after gaining support from Blastus, the king’s chamberlain. They asked for peace because their region depended on the king’s country for food. 📖

## Acts 12:21
On the appointed day, Herod put on his royal robes. He sat on his throne and addressed the people. 📖

## Acts 12:22
They shouted, “This is a god’s voice, not a man’s!” 📖

## Acts 12:23
Herod did not give glory to God. Because of this, an angel of the Lord immediately struck him down. Worms ate him, and he died. 📖

## Acts 12:24
But God’s word continued to spread and multiply. 📖

## Acts 12:25
Barnabas and Saul completed their mission to Jerusalem and returned. They brought John, also called Mark, with them. 📖
