## Acts 26:1
Agrippa told Paul, “You may speak for yourself.” Paul stretched out his hand and began his defense. 📖

## Acts 26:2
“King Agrippa, I am fortunate to stand before you today. I can defend myself against all the Jews’ accusations. 📖

## Acts 26:3
You know all Jewish customs and disputes. So please listen patiently. 📖

## Acts 26:4
All the Jews know how I lived from early childhood among my people and in Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 26:5
They have known me a long time. If willing, they can testify I lived as a Pharisee. I followed our religion’s strictest sect. 📖

## Acts 26:6
Now I stand trial because I hope in God’s promise to our fathers. 📖

## Acts 26:7
Our twelve tribes hope for that promise’s fulfillment as they earnestly serve God day and night. King, the Jews accuse me because of this hope. 📖

## Acts 26:8
Why should any of you find it unbelievable that God raises the dead? 📖

## Acts 26:9
I too believed I should do everything possible to oppose Jesus of Nazareth’s name. 📖

## Acts 26:10
I did this in Jerusalem. With the chief priests’ authority, I imprisoned many saints. When they were sentenced to death, I voted against them. 📖

## Acts 26:11
I often had them punished in synagogues and tried to make them blaspheme. I was so furious that I went even to foreign cities to persecute them. 📖

## Acts 26:12
For this purpose, I traveled to Damascus with the chief priests’ authority and commission. 📖

## Acts 26:13
King, about noon on the road, I saw a heavenly light brighter than the sun. It shone around me and my companions. 📖

## Acts 26:14
We all fell to the ground. I heard a voice in Hebrew: ‘Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me? Kicking against the goads is hard for you.’ 📖

## Acts 26:15
I asked, ‘Who are You, Lord?’ The Lord answered, ‘I am Jesus, whom you persecute. 📖

## Acts 26:16
Get up and stand on your feet. I appeared to appoint you as a servant and witness. You will witness what you have seen from Me and what I will show you. 📖

## Acts 26:17
I will rescue you from your people and the Gentiles. I am sending you to them. 📖

## Acts 26:18
You will open their eyes so they turn from darkness to light and from Satan’s power to God. Then they may receive forgiveness of sins and an inheritance among those made holy by faith in Me.’ 📖

## Acts 26:19
King Agrippa, I obeyed the heavenly vision. 📖

## Acts 26:20
I first proclaimed in Damascus and Jerusalem, then throughout Judea, and then to Gentiles. I told them to repent and turn to God. They should perform deeds worthy of their repentance. 📖

## Acts 26:21
For this reason, the Jews seized me in the temple courts and tried to kill me. 📖

## Acts 26:22
God has helped me to this day. I stand here to testify to small and great alike. I say only what Moses and the prophets said would happen. 📖

## Acts 26:23
The Christ would suffer. As the first to rise from death, He would proclaim light to our people and the Gentiles.” 📖

## Acts 26:24
At this point, Festus shouted, “Paul, you are insane! Your great learning is making you mad!” 📖

## Acts 26:25
Paul answered, “Most excellent Festus, I am not insane. I speak truthful, sensible words. 📖

## Acts 26:26
The king knows these matters, and I speak freely to him. I am sure none escaped his notice. These events did not happen in a corner. 📖

## Acts 26:27
King Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? I know you do.” 📖

## Acts 26:28
Agrippa asked Paul, “Can you persuade me to become a Christian in such a short time?” 📖

## Acts 26:29
Paul replied, “Short or long, I pray to God for you and everyone hearing me today. May you become what I am, except for these chains.” 📖

## Acts 26:30
The king, governor, Bernice, and those seated with them rose. 📖

## Acts 26:31
As they left, they told one another, “This man did nothing deserving death or imprisonment.” 📖

## Acts 26:32
Agrippa told Festus, “He could have been released if he had not appealed to Caesar.” 📖
