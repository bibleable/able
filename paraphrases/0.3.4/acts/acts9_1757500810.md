## Acts 9:1
Saul still spoke murderous threats against the Lord’s disciples. He went to the high priest. 📖

## Acts 9:2
He requested letters to the Damascus synagogues. These would let him bring any men or women of the Way to Jerusalem as prisoners. 📖

## Acts 9:3
As Saul approached Damascus on his journey, a light from heaven suddenly flashed around him. 📖

## Acts 9:4
He fell to the ground. He heard a voice say, “Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me?” 📖

## Acts 9:5
Saul asked, “Who are You, Lord?” The answer came, “I am Jesus, whom you persecute. 📖

## Acts 9:6
Get up and go into the city. You will be told what you must do.” 📖

## Acts 9:7
Saul’s traveling companions stood speechless. They heard the voice but saw no one. 📖

## Acts 9:8
Saul got up, but he could see nothing when he opened his eyes. His companions led him by the hand into Damascus. 📖

## Acts 9:9
He could not see for three days. He ate and drank nothing. 📖

## Acts 9:10
A disciple named Ananias lived in Damascus. In a vision, the Lord called, “Ananias!” He answered, “Here I am, Lord.” 📖

## Acts 9:11
The Lord said, “Get up! Go to Judas’ house on Straight Street. Ask for Saul, a man from Tarsus. He is praying. 📖

## Acts 9:12
In a vision, he saw a man named Ananias come and place his hands on him to restore his sight.” 📖

## Acts 9:13
Ananias answered, “Lord, many people have told me about this man. He has done much harm to Your saints in Jerusalem. 📖

## Acts 9:14
Now he has come here with the chief priests’ authority to arrest everyone who calls on Your name.” 📖

## Acts 9:15
The Lord said, “Go! I chose this man as My instrument. He will carry My name before Gentiles, their kings, and Israel’s people. 📖

## Acts 9:16
I will show him how much he must suffer for My name.” 📖

## Acts 9:17
Ananias went to the house and placed his hands on Saul. He said, “Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus appeared to you on your way here. He sent me so you may see again and be filled with the Holy Spirit.” 📖

## Acts 9:18
Immediately, something like scales fell from Saul’s eyes, and he could see again. He got up and was baptized. 📖

## Acts 9:19
He ate some food and regained strength. He spent several days with the disciples in Damascus. 📖

## Acts 9:20
Saul immediately began proclaiming Jesus in the synagogues. He said, “He is the Son of God.” 📖

## Acts 9:21
Everyone who heard him was amazed. They asked, “Is this not the man who attacked people calling on this name in Jerusalem? Did he not come here to take them as prisoners to the chief priests?” 📖

## Acts 9:22
Saul became more powerful. He proved that Jesus is the Christ and confounded the Jews living in Damascus. 📖

## Acts 9:23
After many days, the Jews plotted to kill him. 📖

## Acts 9:24
Saul learned of their plot. They watched the city gates day and night to kill him. 📖

## Acts 9:25
One night, his disciples lowered him in a basket through a window in the wall. 📖

## Acts 9:26
Saul reached Jerusalem and tried to join the disciples. They all feared him because they did not believe he was a disciple. 📖

## Acts 9:27
Barnabas brought Saul to the apostles. He described how Saul saw the Lord on the Damascus road and how the Lord spoke to him. He also described Saul speaking boldly in Jesus’ name in Damascus. 📖

## Acts 9:28
Saul stayed with them. He moved freely through Jerusalem and spoke boldly in the Lord’s name. 📖

## Acts 9:29
He spoke and debated with the Grecian Jews. But they tried to kill him. 📖

## Acts 9:30
When the brothers learned this, they took Saul down to Caesarea and sent him to Tarsus. 📖

## Acts 9:31
The church throughout Judea, Galilee, and Samaria then had peace. It grew stronger and increased in number. It lived in fear of the Lord and with the Holy Spirit’s encouragement. 📖

## Acts 9:32
Peter traveled through the area. He went to visit the saints in Lydda. 📖

## Acts 9:33
There he found a man named Aeneas. He had been paralyzed and confined to bed for eight years. 📖

## Acts 9:34
Peter said, “Aeneas, Jesus Christ heals you! Get up and put away your mat.” Aeneas immediately got up. 📖

## Acts 9:35
Everyone living in Lydda and Sharon saw him and turned to the Lord. 📖

## Acts 9:36
In Joppa, a disciple named Tabitha always did kind and charitable works. Her name is translated as Dorcas. 📖

## Acts 9:37
At that time, she became sick and died. Her body was washed and put in an upstairs room. 📖

## Acts 9:38
Lydda was near Joppa. The disciples heard Peter was there. They sent two men to urge him, “Come to us without delay.” 📖

## Acts 9:39
Peter got up and went with them. They took him to the upstairs room. All the widows stood around him, crying. They showed him tunics and other clothes Dorcas had made while she was with them. 📖

## Acts 9:40
Peter sent everyone out. He knelt and prayed. Then he turned toward her body and said, “Tabitha, get up!” She opened her eyes, saw Peter, and sat up. 📖

## Acts 9:41
Peter took her hand and helped her stand. He called the saints and widows and presented her alive to them. 📖

## Acts 9:42
People throughout Joppa heard about this. Many believed in the Lord. 📖

## Acts 9:43
Peter stayed in Joppa for several days with Simon, a tanner. 📖
