## Song of Songs 6:1
Most beautiful of women, where did your beloved go? Which way did he turn? We will search with you. 💬
## Song of Songs 6:2
My beloved went down to his garden, to the spice beds. He went to feed his flock in the gardens and gather lilies. 🌸
## Song of Songs 6:3
I belong to my beloved, and he belongs to me. He feeds his flock among the lilies. ❤️
## Song of Songs 6:4
My darling, you are beautiful as Tirzah, lovely as Jerusalem, and majestic as troops with banners. ✨
## Song of Songs 6:5
Turn your eyes from me, because they overwhelm me. Your hair is like a flock of goats that streams down from Gilead. 🐐
## Song of Songs 6:6
Your teeth are like a flock of sheep coming up from the wash. Each has its twin, and none is missing. 🐑
## Song of Songs 6:7
Your brow behind your veil is like a slice of pomegranate. 🌸
## Song of Songs 6:8
There are sixty queens, eighty concubines, and countless young women. 👑
## Song of Songs 6:9
But my dove, my perfect one, is unique. She is the favorite of the mother who bore her. Young women see her and call her blessed. Queens and concubines praise her. ❤️
## Song of Songs 6:10
Who shines like dawn, fair as the moon, bright as the sun, and majestic as stars in procession? 🌅
## Song of Songs 6:11
I went to the walnut grove to see the valley’s flowers. I wanted to see if the vines had buds and the pomegranates had flowers. 🌿
## Song of Songs 6:12
Before I knew it, my desire placed me among my people’s royal chariots. 👑
## Song of Songs 6:13
Come back, come back, Shulammite! Come back so we can look at you. Why do you look at the Shulammite as at the dance of Mahanaim? 💬
