## Song of Songs 3:1
At night on my bed, I searched for the one I love. I searched but did not find him. 🌙
## Song of Songs 3:2
I will get up and go through the city’s streets and squares. I will search for the one I love. I searched but did not find him. 👣
## Song of Songs 3:3
I met the watchmen as they patrolled the city. I asked, “Have you seen the one I love?” 💬
## Song of Songs 3:4
Just after I passed them, I found the one I love. I held him and would not let go. I brought him to my mother’s house, to the room of the woman who conceived me. ❤️
## Song of Songs 3:5
Daughters of Jerusalem, I make this solemn appeal by the gazelles and female deer of the field. Do not stir or wake love before the right time. 🦌
## Song of Songs 3:6
Who comes from the wilderness like a column of smoke? The scent is myrrh and frankincense, from all the merchant’s spices. 🌸
## Song of Songs 3:7
Look, it is Solomon’s carriage! Sixty of Israel’s mightiest men guard it. 🛡️
## Song of Songs 3:8
All are skilled with swords and experienced in war. Each has a sword at his side, ready for the terrors of night. ⚔️
## Song of Songs 3:9
King Solomon made his carriage from Lebanon’s wood. 🌲
## Song of Songs 3:10
He made its posts of silver, its base of gold, and its seat of purple cloth. The daughters of Jerusalem inlaid its inside with love. 💎
## Song of Songs 3:11
Daughters of Zion, come out and look at King Solomon. He wears the crown his mother gave him on his wedding day, the day his heart rejoiced. 👑
