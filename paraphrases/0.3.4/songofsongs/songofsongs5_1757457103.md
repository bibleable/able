## Song of Songs 5:1
I came to my garden, my sister, my bride. I gathered my myrrh and spice. I ate my honeycomb and honey. I drank my wine and milk. Friends, eat and drink! Beloved, drink freely! 🍯
## Song of Songs 5:2
I sleep, but my heart is awake. Listen, my beloved knocks: “Open for me, my sister, my darling, my dove, my flawless one. Dew drenches my head, and the night’s dampness wets my hair.” 🌙
## Song of Songs 5:3
I took off my robe. Must I put it on again? I washed my feet. Must I make them dirty again? 💬
## Song of Songs 5:4
My beloved put his hand to the latch. My heart pounded for him. ❤️
## Song of Songs 5:5
I got up to open for my beloved. My hands and fingers dripped with flowing myrrh on the bolt’s handles. 🌸
## Song of Songs 5:6
I opened for my beloved, but he had turned away and gone. My heart sank when he left. I searched but did not find him. I called, but he did not answer. 💔
## Song of Songs 5:7
I met the watchmen as they patrolled the city. The guards of the walls beat and bruised me, and took my cloak. 💔
## Song of Songs 5:8
Daughters of Jerusalem, I solemnly appeal to you. If you find my beloved, tell him I am sick with love. ❤️
## Song of Songs 5:9
Most beautiful of women, how is your beloved better than others? How is he better than another, that you give us this charge? 💬
## Song of Songs 5:10
My beloved is radiant and rosy, outstanding among ten thousand. ✨
## Song of Songs 5:11
His head is like the purest gold. His hair is wavy and black as a raven. 💎
## Song of Songs 5:12
His eyes are like doves beside streams, washed in milk and set like jewels. 🕊️
## Song of Songs 5:13
His cheeks are like spice beds and towers of perfume. His lips are like lilies that drip with flowing myrrh. 🌸
## Song of Songs 5:14
His arms are like gold rods set with beryl. His body is like polished ivory decorated with sapphires. 💎
## Song of Songs 5:15
His legs are like marble pillars on pure gold bases. He looks like Lebanon, majestic as its cedars. 🌲
## Song of Songs 5:16
His mouth is very sweet, and he is lovely in every way. Daughters of Jerusalem, this is my beloved and my friend. ❤️
