## Song of Songs 7:1
Prince’s daughter, your feet in sandals are beautiful! The curves of your thighs are like jewels made by a master craftsman. 💎
## Song of Songs 7:2
Your navel is like a round cup that never lacks blended wine. Your waist is like a mound of wheat surrounded by lilies. 🌾
## Song of Songs 7:3
Your breasts are like two fawns, a gazelle’s twins. 🦌
## Song of Songs 7:4
Your neck is like an ivory tower. Your eyes are like Heshbon’s pools by the gate of Bath-rabbim. Your nose is like Lebanon’s tower that faces Damascus. 💎
## Song of Songs 7:5
Your head crowns you like Mount Carmel, and your hair is like purple threads. The king is captured in your hair. 👑
## Song of Songs 7:6
Love, you are beautiful and pleasant, full of delights! ❤️
## Song of Songs 7:7
Your figure is like a palm tree, and your breasts are like clusters of fruit. 🌴
## Song of Songs 7:8
I said, “I will climb the palm tree and take its fruit.” May your breasts be like grape clusters, and your breath smell like apples. 🍎
## Song of Songs 7:9
May your mouth be like the finest wine. May it flow smoothly to my beloved, gently over lips and teeth. 🍷
## Song of Songs 7:10
I belong to my beloved, and he desires me. ❤️
## Song of Songs 7:11
Come, my beloved, let us go to the countryside. Let us spend the night among the wildflowers. 🌸
## Song of Songs 7:12
Let us go early to the vineyards. Let us see if the vines have buds, the flowers have opened, and the pomegranates are in bloom. There I will give you my love. ❤️
## Song of Songs 7:13
The mandrakes spread their scent. Every delicacy waits at our door, new and old. I stored them for you, my beloved. 🌸
