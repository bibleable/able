## Song of Songs 2:1
I am a rose of Sharon, a lily of the valley. 🌸
## Song of Songs 2:2
My darling among the young women is like a lily among thorns. 🌸
## Song of Songs 2:3
My beloved among the young men is like an apple tree among forest trees. I delight to sit in his shade. His fruit tastes sweet to me. 🌳
## Song of Songs 2:4
He brought me to the house of wine. His banner over me is love. ❤️
## Song of Songs 2:5
Give me strength with raisins and refresh me with apples. I am faint with love. 🍎
## Song of Songs 2:6
His left hand is under my head. His right arm holds me. ❤️
## Song of Songs 2:7
Daughters of Jerusalem, I make this solemn appeal by the gazelles and female deer of the field. Do not stir or wake love before the right time. 🦌
## Song of Songs 2:8
Listen! My beloved comes! Look, he leaps across the mountains and over the hills. ⛰️
## Song of Songs 2:9
My beloved is like a gazelle or a young stag. He stands behind our wall and looks through the windows and lattice. 🦌
## Song of Songs 2:10
My beloved calls, “Get up, my darling. Come away with me, my beautiful one. ❤️
## Song of Songs 2:11
Winter has passed, and the rain is over. 🌤️
## Song of Songs 2:12
Flowers appear in the countryside. The season for songs has come, and turtledoves coo in our land. 🌸
## Song of Songs 2:13
The fig tree ripens its figs, and the vines’ flowers spread their scent. Get up, my darling. Come away with me, my beautiful one.” 🌿
## Song of Songs 2:14
My dove in the cracks of the rock and cliff, let me see your face and hear your voice. Your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely. 🕊️
## Song of Songs 2:15
Catch the foxes for us, the little foxes that ruin vineyards. Our vineyards are in bloom. 🦊
## Song of Songs 2:16
My beloved is mine, and I am his. He feeds his flock among the lilies. ❤️
## Song of Songs 2:17
Before daybreak and before the shadows flee, turn, my beloved. Be like a gazelle or young stag on Bether’s mountains. 🦌
