## Song of Songs 4:1
My darling, you are so beautiful! Your eyes behind your veil are like doves. Your hair is like a flock of goats that streams down Mount Gilead. 🕊️
## Song of Songs 4:2
Your teeth are like a flock of freshly shorn sheep coming up from the wash. Each has its twin, and none is missing. 🐑
## Song of Songs 4:3
Your lips are like a bright red ribbon, and your mouth is lovely. Your brow behind your veil is like a slice of pomegranate. 🌸
## Song of Songs 4:4
Your neck is like David’s tower, built with rows of stones. A thousand shields hang on it, all warriors’ shields. 🛡️
## Song of Songs 4:5
Your breasts are like two fawns, a gazelle’s twins that feed among the lilies. 🦌
## Song of Songs 4:6
Before daybreak and before shadows flee, I will go to the mountain of myrrh and the hill of frankincense. ⛰️
## Song of Songs 4:7
My darling, you are beautiful in every way. There is no flaw in you. ❤️
## Song of Songs 4:8
Come with me from Lebanon, my bride! Come down from Amana’s peak and the summits of Senir and Hermon. Come from lions’ dens and leopards’ mountains. ⛰️
## Song of Songs 4:9
My sister, my bride, you captured my heart. One glance from your eyes and one jewel on your neck stole my heart. ❤️
## Song of Songs 4:10
My sister, my bride, your love is delightful! It is much better than wine, and your perfume’s scent is better than all spices. 🌸
## Song of Songs 4:11
My bride, your lips drip sweetness like a honeycomb. Honey and milk are under your tongue. Your clothes smell like Lebanon. 🍯
## Song of Songs 4:12
My sister, my bride, you are a locked garden, an enclosed spring, and a sealed fountain. 🌿
## Song of Songs 4:13
Your branches are an orchard of pomegranates with the finest fruit, with henna and nard. 🌳
## Song of Songs 4:14
They have nard, saffron, calamus, cinnamon, every kind of frankincense tree, myrrh, aloes, and all the finest spices. 🌸
## Song of Songs 4:15
You are a garden spring, a well of fresh water that flows from Lebanon. 💧
## Song of Songs 4:16
Wake, north wind, and come, south wind! Blow on my garden and spread its spices’ scent. Let my beloved enter his garden and taste its finest fruit. 🌿
