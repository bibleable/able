## Song of Songs 1:1
This is Solomon’s Song of Songs. 🎵
## Song of Songs 1:2
Let him kiss me with his mouth! Your love delights me more than wine. ❤️
## Song of Songs 1:3
Your perfume smells pleasant. Your name is like perfume poured out. No wonder the young women adore you. 🌸
## Song of Songs 1:4
Take me with you, and let us hurry! May the king bring me into his rooms. We will rejoice and delight in you. We will praise your love more than wine. It is right that they adore you. ❤️
## Song of Songs 1:5
Daughters of Jerusalem, I am dark and lovely. I am like Kedar’s tents and Solomon’s curtains. ✨
## Song of Songs 1:6
Do not stare because I am dark. The sun has looked on me. My mother’s sons were angry with me and made me care for vineyards. But I neglected my own vineyard. ☀️
## Song of Songs 1:7
You whom I love, tell me where you feed your sheep. Where do you let them rest at midday? Why should I be like a veiled woman beside your companions’ flocks? 🐑
## Song of Songs 1:8
Most beautiful of women, if you do not know, follow the flock’s tracks. Feed your young goats near the shepherds’ tents. 🐐
## Song of Songs 1:9
My darling, I compare you to a mare among Pharaoh’s chariots. 🐎
## Song of Songs 1:10
Ornaments make your cheeks beautiful. Strings of jewels make your neck beautiful. 💎
## Song of Songs 1:11
We will make gold ornaments for you, set with silver beads. 💎
## Song of Songs 1:12
While the king sat at his table, my perfume spread its scent. 🌸
## Song of Songs 1:13
My beloved is like a small bag of myrrh that rests between my breasts. ❤️
## Song of Songs 1:14
My beloved is like a cluster of henna flowers in En-gedi’s vineyards. 🌸
## Song of Songs 1:15
My darling, you are so beautiful! Your eyes are like doves. 🕊️
## Song of Songs 1:16
My beloved, you are handsome and delightful! The soft grass is our bed. 🌿
## Song of Songs 1:17
Cedars are the beams of our house. Fragrant firs are its rafters. 🌲
