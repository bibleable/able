## Song of Songs 8:1
I wish you were like a brother who nursed at my mother’s breasts! Then I could kiss you outdoors, and no one would despise me. ❤️
## Song of Songs 8:2
I would lead you to the house of my mother, who taught me. I would give you spiced wine and my pomegranates’ nectar. 🍷
## Song of Songs 8:3
His left hand is under my head. His right arm holds me. ❤️
## Song of Songs 8:4
Daughters of Jerusalem, I solemnly appeal to you. Do not stir or wake love before the right time. ❤️
## Song of Songs 8:5
Who comes from the wilderness, leaning on her beloved? I woke you under the apple tree. There your mother conceived you, felt birth pains, and gave birth to you. 🌳
## Song of Songs 8:6
Place me like a seal over your heart and on your arm. Love is strong as death, and its jealousy is unyielding as Sheol. Its sparks are flames, the fiercest fire. 🔥

*Word help: Sheol is the place of the dead.*
## Song of Songs 8:7
Great waters cannot put out love, and rivers cannot sweep it away. If a man offered all his household’s wealth for love, people would utterly despise the offer. ❤️
## Song of Songs 8:8
We have a little sister whose breasts have not yet grown. What will we do for her when someone asks to marry her? 💬
## Song of Songs 8:9
If she is a wall, we will build a silver tower on her. If she is a door, we will enclose her with cedar panels. 🏰
## Song of Songs 8:10
I am a wall, and my breasts are like towers. In his eyes, I became like someone who brings peace. 🕊️
## Song of Songs 8:11
Solomon had a vineyard at Baal-hamon and rented it to tenants. Each had to bring a thousand silver shekels for its fruit. 🌾
## Song of Songs 8:12
My own vineyard is mine to give. Solomon, the thousand shekels are yours, and two hundred belong to those who tend its fruit. 🌿
## Song of Songs 8:13
You who live in the gardens, my companions listen for your voice. Let me hear it! 👂
## Song of Songs 8:14
Come away, my beloved. Be like a gazelle or young stag on the mountains of spices. 🦌
