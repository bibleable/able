## Nahum 3:1
Great trouble waits for the city of bloodshed! It is full of lies and stolen goods. It always has victims. 💔
## Nahum 3:2
Whips crack, and wheels rumble. Horses race, and chariots bounce! 🐎
## Nahum 3:3
Riders charge. Swords flash, and spears shine. Dead bodies form endless piles. People stumble over the dead. ⚔️
## Nahum 3:4
The city is like a prostitute who tempts others with magic. She betrays nations through prostitution and families through witchcraft. *Word help: Prostitution is the sale of sex.* ❌
## Nahum 3:5
The LORD of Hosts says, “I am against you. I will lift your skirts over your face. I will show your nakedness to the nations and your shame to the kingdoms. ⚖️
## Nahum 3:6
I will throw filth at you and treat you with scorn. I will make people stare at you. ⚖️
## Nahum 3:7
Everyone who sees you will draw back. They will say, ‘Nineveh is ruined! Who will grieve for her?’ Where can I find someone to comfort you?” 💔
## Nahum 3:8
Are you better than Thebes beside the Nile? Water surrounded her. The sea was her defense, and water was her wall. 🌊
## Nahum 3:9
Cush and Egypt gave her strength without limit. Put and Libya were her allies. 🤝
## Nahum 3:10
Yet enemies carried her people into exile. They smashed her babies to pieces at the start of every street. They cast lots for her important people. They chained all her leaders. *Word help: People cast lots to choose by chance.* 💔⛓️
## Nahum 3:11
You too will become drunk. You will hide and seek safety from the enemy. 🆘
## Nahum 3:12
All your forts are like fig trees with their first ripe fruit. When someone shakes them, the fruit falls into the eater’s mouth! 🌳
## Nahum 3:13
Look at your troops. They are like the women among you. Your land’s gates stand wide open to enemies. Fire burns the gates’ bars. 🔥
## Nahum 3:14
Store water for the attack. Strengthen your forts. Work clay and step on mortar. Repair the brick kiln. 🧱
## Nahum 3:15
Fire will burn you there. The sword will kill you and consume you like a young locust. Grow in number like young locusts! Grow in number like swarming locusts! 🔥⚔️
## Nahum 3:16
You make your traders more numerous than the stars. But the young locust strips the land and flies away. ⭐
## Nahum 3:17
Your guards are like swarming locusts. Your scribes are like crowds of locusts on walls on a cold day. When the sun rises, they fly away. No one knows where they go. 🌅
## Nahum 3:18
King of Assyria, your shepherds sleep. Your officers rest. Your people scatter on the mountains, and no one gathers them. ⛰️
## Nahum 3:19
Your injury has no cure. Your wound is severe. Everyone who hears the news claps at your defeat. Who did not experience your constant cruelty? 👏⚖️
