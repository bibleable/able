## Nahum 2:1
Nineveh, a destroyer advances against you. Guard the fort! Watch the road! Prepare yourselves and gather all your strength! 🛡️
## Nahum 2:2
The LORD will restore Jacob’s glory like Israel’s glory. Destroyers ruined them and broke their vine’s branches. 🌿
## Nahum 2:3
The soldiers’ shields are red, and their clothes are bright red. The chariots’ metal parts flash like fire on the day they prepare. The soldiers raise their cypress spears. ⚔️🔥
## Nahum 2:4
Chariots rush through streets and open squares. They look like torches. They move like lightning. ⚡
## Nahum 2:5
The commander calls his leaders. They stumble as they advance. They hurry to the wall. Someone sets the protective shield in place. 🧱
## Nahum 2:6
The river gates open. The palace falls apart. 🌊🏚️
## Nahum 2:7
The decree orders the city’s people into exile and captivity. Her women servants cry like doves. They beat their chests in grief. 💔
## Nahum 2:8
Nineveh is like a pool that always held water. But now the water flows away. People cry, “Stop! Stop!” Yet no one turns back. 🌊
## Nahum 2:9
“Take the silver! Take the gold!” The treasure seems endless. There are great amounts of every precious thing. 💰
## Nahum 2:10
The city is empty and ruined! Hearts lose courage. Knees knock together, bodies shake, and every face turns pale. 🏚️
## Nahum 2:11
Where is the lions’ den and the young lions’ place for food? Lions, lionesses, and cubs once roamed there without fear. 🦁
## Nahum 2:12
The lion tore enough animals apart for its cubs. It killed animals for the lioness. It filled its dens with dead prey. 🦁
## Nahum 2:13
The LORD of Hosts says, “I am against you. I will burn your chariots to ashes. The sword will kill your young lions. I will remove your prey from the earth. No one will hear your messengers again.” 🔥⚖️
