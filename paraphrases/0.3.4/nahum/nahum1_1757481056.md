## Nahum 1:1
This message is against Nineveh. It is the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite. 📣
## Nahum 1:2
The LORD is a jealous God who punishes His enemies. He is full of fierce anger. He takes revenge on those who oppose Him. He keeps anger for His enemies. ⚖️
## Nahum 1:3
The LORD does not become angry quickly, and His power is great. He will surely punish the guilty. His path is in the whirlwind and storm. Clouds are like dust under His feet. ⛈️
## Nahum 1:4
He commands the sea, and it dries up. He makes all rivers dry. Bashan and Carmel fade. Lebanon’s flowers dry up. 🌊🌿
## Nahum 1:5
Mountains shake before Him, and hills melt. The earth and everyone in the world tremble before Him. ⛰️🌍
## Nahum 1:6
Who can stand against His anger? Who can bear His fierce anger? His anger pours out like fire. Even rocks break apart before Him. 🔥🪨
## Nahum 1:7
The LORD is good. He is a strong fort when trouble comes. He cares for people who trust Him. 🛡️❤️
## Nahum 1:8
But He will end Nineveh with a great flood. He will chase His enemies into darkness. 🌊🌑
## Nahum 1:9
Whatever you plan against the LORD, He will end it. Trouble will not rise a second time. ⚖️
## Nahum 1:10
They will be tangled like thorn branches. Destruction will consume them like a drunk person’s drink, like dry stalks. 🔥
## Nahum 1:11
Nineveh, someone comes from you who plans evil against the LORD. He gives evil advice. 💭❌
## Nahum 1:12
The LORD says, “They join forces and are many. But destruction will cut them down. Judah, I made you suffer, but I will make you suffer no longer. 🛡️
## Nahum 1:13
I will now break their yoke from your neck. I will tear your chains away.” ⛓️
## Nahum 1:14
The LORD gives this command about Nineveh. “No descendants will carry on your name. I will remove the carved and cast idols from your gods’ house. I will prepare your grave because you are contemptible.” ⚖️
## Nahum 1:15
Look at the mountains! A messenger brings good news and announces peace. Judah, celebrate your feasts and keep your promises. Evil people will never march through you again. Destruction will end them completely. ⛰️🤝
