## Numbers 25:1
While Israel stayed at Shittim, the men began sexual immorality with Moabite women. 📖

## Numbers 25:2
These women invited them to sacrifices for their gods. The people ate and bowed to those gods. 📖

## Numbers 25:3
Israel joined in worshiping Baal of Peor. The LORD’s anger burned against them. 📖

## Numbers 25:4
The LORD told Moses, “Take all the people’s leaders and execute them before the LORD in broad daylight. Then His fierce anger can turn from Israel.” 📖

## Numbers 25:5
Moses told Israel’s judges, “Each must kill all his men who joined in worshiping Baal of Peor.” 📖

## Numbers 25:6
An Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his family before Moses and the whole congregation. They were weeping at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 25:7
Phinehas saw this, rose from the assembly, and took a spear. He was Eleazar’s son and Aaron the priest’s grandson. 📖

## Numbers 25:8
He followed the Israelite into the tent. He drove the spear through the man and through the woman’s belly. The plague against Israel stopped. 📖

## Numbers 25:9
But the plague killed 24,000. 📖

## Numbers 25:10
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 25:11
“Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, turned My wrath from Israel. He acted zealously for Me among them. Therefore, I did not consume Israel in My zeal. 📖

## Numbers 25:12
Declare that I give him My covenant of peace. 📖

## Numbers 25:13
It is a covenant of permanent priesthood for him and his descendants. He was zealous for his God and made atonement for Israel.” 📖

## Numbers 25:14
The slain Israelite was Zimri son of Salu, leader of a Simeonite family. He died with the Midianite woman. 📖

## Numbers 25:15
The slain woman was Cozbi, daughter of Zur, a tribal chief of a Midianite family. 📖

## Numbers 25:16
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 25:17
“Attack the Midianites and kill them. 📖

## Numbers 25:18
They attacked you deceitfully by seducing you in the matter of Peor and their sister Cozbi. She was the Midianite leader’s daughter. She died on the day the plague came because of Peor.” 📖
