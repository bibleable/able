## Numbers 2:1
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 2:2
“Israel must camp around the Tent of Meeting, some distance away. Each man must be under his standard with his family’s banners. 📖

## Numbers 2:3
Judah’s divisions must camp under their standard on the east, toward sunrise. Their leader is Nahshon son of Amminadab. 📖

## Numbers 2:4
His division has 74,600 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:5
Issachar’s tribe must camp beside Judah. Its leader is Nethanel son of Zuar. 📖

## Numbers 2:6
His division has 54,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:7
Zebulun’s tribe must camp next. Its leader is Eliab son of Helon. 📖

## Numbers 2:8
His division has 57,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:9
Judah’s camp has 186,400 men in its divisions. They must leave first. 📖

## Numbers 2:10
Reuben’s divisions must camp under their standard on the south. Their leader is Elizur son of Shedeur. 📖

## Numbers 2:11
His division has 46,500 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:12
Simeon’s tribe must camp beside Reuben. Its leader is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai. 📖

## Numbers 2:13
His division has 59,300 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:14
Gad’s tribe must camp next. Its leader is Eliasaph son of Deuel. 📖

## Numbers 2:15
His division has 45,650 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:16
Reuben’s camp has 151,450 men in its divisions. They must leave second. 📖

## Numbers 2:17
The Tent of Meeting must travel with the Levites’ camp in the middle of the camps. Everyone must leave in their camping order, each in his place under his standard. 📖

## Numbers 2:18
Ephraim’s divisions must camp under their standard on the west. Their leader is Elishama son of Ammihud. 📖

## Numbers 2:19
His division has 40,500 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:20
Manasseh’s tribe must camp beside Ephraim. Its leader is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur. 📖

## Numbers 2:21
His division has 32,200 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:22
Benjamin’s tribe must camp next. Its leader is Abidan son of Gideoni. 📖

## Numbers 2:23
His division has 35,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:24
Ephraim’s camp has 108,100 men in its divisions. They must leave third. 📖

## Numbers 2:25
Dan’s divisions must camp under their standard on the north. Their leader is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai. 📖

## Numbers 2:26
His division has 62,700 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:27
Asher’s tribe must camp beside Dan. Its leader is Pagiel son of Ocran. 📖

## Numbers 2:28
His division has 41,500 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:29
Naphtali’s tribe must camp next. Its leader is Ahira son of Enan. 📖

## Numbers 2:30
His division has 53,400 men. 📖

## Numbers 2:31
Dan’s camp has 157,600 men. They must leave last, under their standards.” 📖

## Numbers 2:32
These Israelites were counted by families. The total across the camps’ divisions was 603,550. 📖

## Numbers 2:33
The Levites were not counted with the other Israelites, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 2:34
Israel did everything the LORD commanded Moses. They camped under their standards and traveled in the same order, each man with his clan and family. 📖
