## Numbers 32:1
The Reubenites and Gadites had many herds and flocks. They examined Jazer and Gilead and saw land suitable for livestock. 📖

## Numbers 32:2
They approached Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the congregation’s leaders. 📖

## Numbers 32:3
They said, “Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon, 📖

## Numbers 32:4
the places the LORD conquered before Israel’s congregation, are good for livestock. We, your servants, have livestock.” 📖

## Numbers 32:5
They added, “If we have your favor, give us this land to possess. Do not make us cross the Jordan.” 📖

## Numbers 32:6
Moses asked them, “Will your brothers fight while you stay here? 📖

## Numbers 32:7
Why discourage Israel from entering the land the LORD gave them? 📖

## Numbers 32:8
Your fathers did this when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to examine the land. 📖

## Numbers 32:9
They reached the Valley of Eshcol and saw the land. Then they discouraged Israel from entering the land the LORD gave them. 📖

## Numbers 32:10
His anger burned that day. He swore, 📖

## Numbers 32:11
‘No man aged twenty or older who left Egypt will see the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. They did not follow Me wholeheartedly. 📖

## Numbers 32:12
Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun are the exceptions. They followed the LORD wholeheartedly.’ 📖

## Numbers 32:13
His anger burned against Israel. He made them wander in the wilderness forty years, until the whole generation that did evil before Him died. 📖

## Numbers 32:14
Now you sinners replace your fathers and increase the LORD’s burning anger against Israel. 📖

## Numbers 32:15
If you turn from following Him, He will leave these people in the wilderness again. You will cause their destruction.” 📖

## Numbers 32:16
They approached Moses, saying, “We want to build pens here for our livestock and towns for our children. 📖

## Numbers 32:17
We will arm ourselves and lead Israel until we bring them to their place. Our children will stay in fortified towns, safe from the land’s inhabitants. 📖

## Numbers 32:18
We will not return home until every Israelite possesses his inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 32:19
We will receive no inheritance with them beyond the Jordan. Ours is on its eastern side.” 📖

## Numbers 32:20
Moses replied, “This applies if you arm yourselves before the LORD for battle, 📖

## Numbers 32:21
and every armed man crosses the Jordan before Him until He drives out His enemies. 📖

## Numbers 32:22
When the land is subdued before Him, you may return, free of obligation to the LORD and Israel. This land will be yours to possess before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 32:23
If you fail to do this, you will sin against Him. Be certain your sin will find you out. 📖

## Numbers 32:24
Build towns for your children and pens for your flocks. But fulfill your promise.” 📖

## Numbers 32:25
The Gadites and Reubenites told Moses, “We, your servants, will do exactly as our lord commands. 📖

## Numbers 32:26
Our children, wives, livestock, and all our animals will stay in Gilead’s towns. 📖

## Numbers 32:27
All your servants, armed for war, will cross to fight before the LORD, as our lord says.” 📖

## Numbers 32:28
Moses gave instructions about them to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and Israel’s tribal family leaders. 📖

## Numbers 32:29
He said, “If the Gadites and Reubenites cross the Jordan with you, every man armed for battle before the LORD, give them Gilead to possess. Do this when the land is subdued before you. 📖

## Numbers 32:30
If they do not arm themselves and cross with you, they must accept their possession among you in Canaan.” 📖

## Numbers 32:31
They replied, “We will do what the LORD told His servants. 📖

## Numbers 32:32
We will cross into Canaan armed before Him, so our inheritance can remain on this side of the Jordan.” 📖

## Numbers 32:33
Moses gave land to Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph. It included Sihon the Amorite king’s kingdom and Og king of Bashan’s kingdom, with their towns and surrounding territory. 📖

## Numbers 32:34
The Gadites rebuilt Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer, 📖

## Numbers 32:35
Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah, 📖

## Numbers 32:36
Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran as fortified towns. They also built pens for their flocks. 📖

## Numbers 32:37
The Reubenites rebuilt Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim, 📖

## Numbers 32:38
Nebo, Baal-meon, and Sibmah. Nebo’s and Baal-meon’s names were changed. They renamed the towns they rebuilt. 📖

## Numbers 32:39
Machir son of Manasseh’s descendants went to Gilead. They captured it and drove out its Amorites. 📖

## Numbers 32:40
Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh’s clan. They settled there. 📖

## Numbers 32:41
Jair, Manasseh’s descendant, captured their villages and named them Havvoth-jair. 📖

## Numbers 32:42
Nobah captured Kenath and its villages. He renamed it Nobah after himself. 📖
