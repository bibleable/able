## Numbers 18:1
The LORD told Aaron, “You, your sons, and your father’s family must bear guilt for offenses involving the sanctuary. You and your sons alone must bear guilt for offenses involving your priesthood. 📖

## Numbers 18:2
Bring your brothers from your father’s tribe, Levi. They must join and help you and your sons before the Tent of the Testimony. 📖

## Numbers 18:3
They must attend to your duties and all the Tent’s duties. They must not approach the sanctuary’s furnishings or altar, or both they and you will die. 📖

## Numbers 18:4
They must join you for duties at the Tent of Meeting and all its work. No outsider may approach you. 📖

## Numbers 18:5
You must attend to sanctuary and altar duties. Then wrath will not fall on Israel again. 📖

## Numbers 18:6
I chose your fellow Levites from Israel as a gift to you. They are dedicated to the LORD for service at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 18:7
Only you and your sons must perform priestly service concerning the altar and everything inside the veil. I give you this priestly work as a gift. Put any outsider who approaches the sanctuary to death.” 📖

## Numbers 18:8
The LORD told Aaron, “I put you in charge of My offerings. I give all Israel’s sacred offerings to you and your sons as your portion, by permanent statute. 📖

## Numbers 18:9
You and your sons receive a portion of the most holy offerings kept from the fire. This includes portions from grain, sin, and guilt offerings they present to Me as most holy. 📖

## Numbers 18:10
Eat it as a most holy offering. Every male may eat it. Regard it as holy. 📖

## Numbers 18:11
Israel’s offered gifts and wave offerings also belong to you. I give them to you, your sons, and your daughters by permanent statute. Every ceremonially clean person in your household may eat them. 📖

## Numbers 18:12
I give you all the freshest olive oil and finest new wine and grain that Israel gives the LORD as first produce. 📖

## Numbers 18:13
The first produce of everything in their land brought to the LORD belongs to you. Every ceremonially clean household member may eat it. 📖

## Numbers 18:14
Every devoted thing in Israel belongs to you. 📖

## Numbers 18:15
Every firstborn human or animal offered to the LORD belongs to you. But redeem every firstborn son and every firstborn male from unclean animals. 📖

## Numbers 18:16
Pay the assessed redemption price for a male aged one month: five silver sanctuary shekels. Each shekel equals twenty gerahs. 📖

## Numbers 18:17
Do not redeem firstborn oxen, sheep, or goats. They are holy. Splash their blood on the altar. Burn their fat as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 18:18
Their meat belongs to you, like the breast and right thigh of the wave offering. 📖

## Numbers 18:19
I give you, your sons, and your daughters all Israel’s holy offerings to the LORD by permanent statute. This is a permanent covenant of salt before Him for you and your descendants.” 📖

## Numbers 18:20
The LORD told Aaron, “You will have no inheritance in their land or share among them. I am your share and inheritance among Israel. 📖

## Numbers 18:21
I give the Levites all Israel’s tithes as their inheritance. This pays for their service at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 18:22
Israelites must no longer approach the Tent of Meeting, or they will become guilty and die. 📖

## Numbers 18:23
The Levites must do its work and bear their guilt. This is a permanent statute for future generations. They will receive no inheritance among Israel. 📖

## Numbers 18:24
I give them Israel’s tithe, presented as a contribution to the LORD, as their inheritance. Therefore, I said they would receive no inheritance among Israel.” 📖

## Numbers 18:25
The LORD instructed Moses, 📖

## Numbers 18:26
“Tell the Levites: ‘When you receive Israel’s tithe, the inheritance I give you, offer a tenth of that tithe to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 18:27
Your offering will count as grain from a threshing floor or juice from a winepress. 📖

## Numbers 18:28
Present the LORD’s offering from all the tithes you receive from Israel. Give it to Aaron the priest. 📖

## Numbers 18:29
Give the LORD His offering from the best of every gift, its holiest part.’ 📖

## Numbers 18:30
Tell the Levites: ‘After you present the best part, it will count as produce from a threshing floor or winepress. 📖

## Numbers 18:31
You and your households may eat the rest anywhere. It pays for your work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 18:32
After presenting the best part, you will bear no guilt because of it. Do not defile Israel’s sacred offerings, or you will die.’” 📖
