## Numbers 8:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 8:2
“Tell Aaron: ‘Position the seven lamps to light the area in front of the lampstand.’” 📖

## Numbers 8:3
Aaron placed the lamps facing forward, exactly as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 8:4
The lampstand was hammered gold from its base to its blossoms. It was made according to the pattern the LORD showed Moses. 📖

## Numbers 8:5
The LORD spoke to Moses again. 📖

## Numbers 8:6
“Take the Levites from Israel and make them ceremonially clean. 📖

## Numbers 8:7
Sprinkle them with purification water. They must shave their whole bodies and wash their clothes. Thus they must purify themselves. 📖

## Numbers 8:8
Have them bring a young bull with a grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil. Take a second young bull as a sin offering. 📖

## Numbers 8:9
Bring the Levites before the Tent of Meeting. Gather Israel’s whole congregation. 📖

## Numbers 8:10
Present the Levites before the LORD. Have Israel lay their hands on them. 📖

## Numbers 8:11
Aaron must present them before the LORD as a wave offering from Israel. Then they can serve the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 8:12
The Levites must lay their hands on the bulls’ heads. Offer one to the LORD as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. This makes atonement for the Levites. 📖

## Numbers 8:13
Place the Levites before Aaron and his sons. Then present them to the LORD as a wave offering. 📖

## Numbers 8:14
Thus set the Levites apart from the rest of Israel. They will belong to Me. 📖

## Numbers 8:15
After you cleanse them and present them as a wave offering, they may serve at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 8:16
The Levites are given entirely to Me from Israel. I took them for Myself instead of every firstborn Israelite, those first from the womb. 📖

## Numbers 8:17
Every firstborn male in Israel, human or animal, belongs to Me. I set them apart for Myself when I killed all Egypt’s firstborn. 📖

## Numbers 8:18
I took the Levites instead of all Israel’s firstborn. 📖

## Numbers 8:19
I gave the Levites from Israel to Aaron and his sons. They must serve for Israel at the Tent of Meeting and make atonement for them. Then no plague will strike Israel when they approach the sanctuary.” 📖

## Numbers 8:20
Moses, Aaron, and Israel’s whole congregation did everything with the Levites that the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 8:21
The Levites purified themselves and washed their clothes. Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD. He made atonement to cleanse them. 📖

## Numbers 8:22
Then they served at the Tent of Meeting before Aaron and his sons. Everything done with the Levites followed the LORD’s command to Moses. 📖

## Numbers 8:23
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 8:24
“This applies to the Levites: men aged twenty-five or older must enter service at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 8:25
At fifty, they must retire from the work and no longer serve. 📖

## Numbers 8:26
They may help their brothers with duties at the Tent of Meeting, but must not do the work themselves. Assign the Levites’ responsibilities this way.” 📖
