## Numbers 28:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 28:2
“Command Israel to present My food offerings at their appointed times, as a pleasing aroma to Me. 📖

## Numbers 28:3
Tell them to present two male lambs aged one year without defect each day. This is the regular burnt food offering to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 28:4
Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight. 📖

## Numbers 28:5
Include a grain offering of one tenth of an ephah of fine flour. Mix it with one quarter hin of oil from pressed olives. 📖

## Numbers 28:6
This regular burnt offering was established at Mount Sinai. It is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 28:7
Each lamb’s drink offering must be one quarter hin. Pour this fermented drink offering to the LORD in the sanctuary area. 📖

## Numbers 28:8
Offer the second lamb at twilight with the same grain and drink offerings as in the morning. It is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 28:9
On the Sabbath, present two male lambs aged one year without defect. Include two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and a drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:10
This Sabbath burnt offering is additional to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:11
At each month’s beginning, offer two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs aged one year to the LORD as a burnt offering. All must be without defect. 📖

## Numbers 28:12
With each bull, bring three tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering. With the ram, bring two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil. 📖

## Numbers 28:13
With each lamb, bring one tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil. This burnt offering is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 28:14
Bring wine as drink offerings: half a hin per bull, one third hin for the ram, and one quarter hin per lamb. This monthly burnt offering is for each new moon throughout the year. 📖

## Numbers 28:15
Also present one male goat to the LORD as a sin offering. This is additional to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:16
The first month’s fourteenth day is the LORD’s Passover. 📖

## Numbers 28:17
Begin a feast on that month’s fifteenth day. Eat bread without yeast for seven days. 📖

## Numbers 28:18
Hold a sacred assembly on the first day. Do no regular work. 📖

## Numbers 28:19
Present two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs aged one year to the LORD as a burnt food offering. All must be without defect. 📖

## Numbers 28:20
Bring fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering. Use three tenths of an ephah per bull and two tenths for the ram. 📖

## Numbers 28:21
Use one tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 📖

## Numbers 28:22
Include one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you. 📖

## Numbers 28:23
Present these in addition to the regular morning burnt offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:24
Offer this same food daily for seven days, as a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. Include its drink offering and the regular burnt offering. 📖

## Numbers 28:25
Hold a sacred assembly on the seventh day. Do no regular work. 📖

## Numbers 28:26
On the day of first produce, during the Feast of Weeks, present new grain to the LORD. Hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work. 📖

## Numbers 28:27
Offer two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs aged one year as a burnt offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 28:28
Include grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil. Use three tenths of an ephah per bull and two tenths for the ram. 📖

## Numbers 28:29
Use one tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 📖

## Numbers 28:30
Include one male goat to make atonement for you. 📖

## Numbers 28:31
Offer these with their drink offerings, besides the regular burnt offering and its grain offering. The animals must be without defect. 📖
