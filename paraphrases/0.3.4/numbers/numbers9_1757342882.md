## Numbers 9:1
In the first month of the second year after Israel left Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses in the Wilderness of Sinai. 📖

## Numbers 9:2
“Israel must observe Passover at its appointed time. 📖

## Numbers 9:3
Observe it at twilight on this month’s fourteenth day, according to its statutes and ordinances.” 📖

## Numbers 9:4
Moses told Israel to observe Passover. 📖

## Numbers 9:5
They observed it in the Wilderness of Sinai, at twilight on the first month’s fourteenth day. They did everything exactly as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 9:6
Some men were unclean because of a dead body and could not observe Passover that day. They came before Moses and Aaron the same day. 📖

## Numbers 9:7
They told Moses, “We are unclean because of a dead body. Why should we be excluded from presenting the LORD’s offering with Israel at the appointed time?” 📖

## Numbers 9:8
Moses replied, “Wait here until I learn the LORD’s command concerning you.” 📖

## Numbers 9:9
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 9:10
“Tell Israel: ‘You or your descendants may be unclean because of a dead body, or away traveling. You may still observe Passover to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 9:11
Such people must observe it at twilight on the second month’s fourteenth day. Eat the lamb with bread without yeast and bitter herbs. 📖

## Numbers 9:12
Leave none until morning and break none of its bones. Observe Passover according to all its statutes. 📖

## Numbers 9:13
But someone ceremonially clean and not traveling may fail to observe Passover. Cut that person off from the people for failing to present the LORD’s offering at its appointed time. That person must bear the consequences of the sin. 📖

## Numbers 9:14
A foreigner living among you may wish to observe Passover to the LORD. Observe it according to Passover’s statute and ordinances. Apply the same statute to foreigners and native people.’” 📖

## Numbers 9:15
The cloud covered the tabernacle, the Tent of the Testimony, on the day it was set up. From evening to morning, it appeared like fire above the tabernacle. 📖

## Numbers 9:16
This continued: the cloud covered the tabernacle by day and appeared like fire at night. 📖

## Numbers 9:17
When the cloud rose from the Tent, Israel traveled. Wherever it settled, they camped. 📖

## Numbers 9:18
They traveled and camped at the LORD’s command. They stayed in camp as long as the cloud remained over the tabernacle. 📖

## Numbers 9:19
Even if it stayed many days, they obeyed the LORD’s instructions and did not travel. 📖

## Numbers 9:20
Sometimes the cloud stayed only a few days. They camped and traveled at the LORD’s command. 📖

## Numbers 9:21
Sometimes it stayed only from evening to morning. When it rose in the morning, they traveled. Whether day or night, they traveled whenever it rose. 📖

## Numbers 9:22
Whether it stayed two days, a month, or longer, they remained in camp while it was over the tabernacle. When it rose, they traveled. 📖

## Numbers 9:23
They camped and traveled at the LORD’s command. They followed His instructions given through Moses. 📖
