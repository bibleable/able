## Numbers 20:1
In the first month, Israel’s whole congregation entered the Wilderness of Zin and stayed at Kadesh. Miriam died and was buried there. 📖

## Numbers 20:2
There was no water. The congregation gathered against Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Numbers 20:3
They argued with Moses. “If only we had died with our brothers before the LORD! 📖

## Numbers 20:4
Why bring His assembly into this wilderness, where we and our livestock will die? 📖

## Numbers 20:5
Why bring us from Egypt to this miserable place? There is no grain, figs, vines, or pomegranates. There is no drinking water!” 📖

## Numbers 20:6
Moses and Aaron left the assembly and went to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. They fell facedown. The LORD’s glory appeared to them. 📖

## Numbers 20:7
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 20:8
“Take the staff and gather the congregation. You and your brother Aaron must speak to the rock before their eyes. It will give water. Thus you will bring water from the rock for the congregation and their livestock to drink.” 📖

## Numbers 20:9
Moses took the staff from the LORD’s presence, as commanded. 📖

## Numbers 20:10
Moses and Aaron gathered everyone before the rock. Moses said, “Listen, rebels! Must we bring water from this rock for you?” 📖

## Numbers 20:11
Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff. Much water poured out. The congregation and their livestock drank. 📖

## Numbers 20:12
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, “You did not trust Me to show My holiness before Israel. Therefore, you will not lead this assembly into the land I gave them.” 📖

## Numbers 20:13
These were Meribah’s waters, where Israel argued with the LORD. He showed His holiness among them. 📖

## Numbers 20:14
Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to Edom’s king. “Your brother Israel says, ‘You know all our hardships. 📖

## Numbers 20:15
Our fathers went to Egypt, and we lived there many years. The Egyptians mistreated us and our fathers. 📖

## Numbers 20:16
We cried to the LORD. He heard, sent an angel, and brought us from Egypt. Now we are at Kadesh, a town on your border. 📖

## Numbers 20:17
Please let us cross your land. We will avoid fields and vineyards and drink no well water. We will follow the King’s Highway without turning right or left until we cross your territory.’” 📖

## Numbers 20:18
Edom replied, “You must not cross our land. Otherwise, we will confront you with the sword.” 📖

## Numbers 20:19
Israel replied, “We will stay on the main road. If we or our herds drink water, we will pay. There will be no problem. Just let us cross on foot.” 📖

## Numbers 20:20
Edom insisted, “You may not cross.” They confronted Israel with a large army and a strong hand. 📖

## Numbers 20:21
Edom refused passage, and Israel turned away. 📖

## Numbers 20:22
The whole congregation left Kadesh and reached Mount Hor. 📖

## Numbers 20:23
At Mount Hor, near Edom’s border, the LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 20:24
“Aaron will be gathered to his people. He will not enter the land I gave Israel. You both rebelled against My command at Meribah’s waters. 📖

## Numbers 20:25
Bring Aaron and his son Eleazar up Mount Hor. 📖

## Numbers 20:26
Remove Aaron’s priestly clothes and put them on Eleazar. Aaron will be gathered to his people and die there.” 📖

## Numbers 20:27
Moses obeyed. They climbed Mount Hor before the whole congregation. 📖

## Numbers 20:28
Moses removed Aaron’s clothes and put them on Eleazar. Aaron died on the mountaintop. Moses and Eleazar came down. 📖

## Numbers 20:29
Everyone saw that Aaron died. All Israel mourned him thirty days. 📖
