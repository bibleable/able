## Numbers 27:1
Zelophehad was the son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh. His family belonged to the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph. His daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 📖

## Numbers 27:2
They approached the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. They stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the leaders, and the whole congregation, saying, 📖

## Numbers 27:3
“Our father died in the wilderness for his own sin. He did not join Korah’s followers who gathered against the LORD. He had no sons. 📖

## Numbers 27:4
Why should his name disappear from his clan because he had no sons? Give us property among our father’s brothers.” 📖

## Numbers 27:5
Moses brought their case before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 27:6
The LORD answered, 📖

## Numbers 27:7
“Zelophehad’s daughters are right. Give them property as an inheritance among their father’s brothers. Transfer their father’s inheritance to them. 📖

## Numbers 27:8
Tell Israel: ‘If a man dies without a son, transfer his inheritance to his daughter. 📖

## Numbers 27:9
If he has no daughter, give it to his brothers. 📖

## Numbers 27:10
If he has no brothers, give it to his father’s brothers. 📖

## Numbers 27:11
If his father has no brothers, give it to his nearest relative in his clan to possess. This is a statutory ordinance for Israel, as the LORD commanded Moses.’” 📖

## Numbers 27:12
The LORD told Moses, “Climb this mountain of the Abarim range. See the land I gave Israel. 📖

## Numbers 27:13
After seeing it, you too will be gathered to your people, like your brother Aaron. 📖

## Numbers 27:14
When the congregation argued in the Wilderness of Zin, you both rebelled against My command. You failed to show My holiness before them regarding the waters.” These were Meribah’s waters at Kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin. 📖

## Numbers 27:15
Moses appealed to the LORD, 📖

## Numbers 27:16
“LORD, God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation. 🙏

## Numbers 27:17
Let him go out and come in before them, leading them out and bringing them in. Then Your congregation will not be like sheep without a shepherd.” 🙏

## Numbers 27:18
The LORD replied, “Take Joshua son of Nun, who has the Spirit in him. Lay your hands on him. 📖

## Numbers 27:19
Place him before Eleazar the priest and the whole congregation. Commission him before their eyes. 📖

## Numbers 27:20
Give him some of your authority, so all Israel’s congregation obeys him. 📖

## Numbers 27:21
He must stand before Eleazar the priest. Eleazar will seek the LORD’s counsel for him through the Urim’s judgment. At his command, Joshua and all Israel, the whole congregation, will go out and come in.” 📖

## Numbers 27:22
Moses obeyed the LORD. He brought Joshua before Eleazar the priest and the whole congregation. 📖

## Numbers 27:23
He laid his hands on Joshua and commissioned him, as the LORD instructed through Moses. 📖
