## Numbers 29:1
“Hold a sacred assembly on the first day of the seventh month. Do no regular work. This is a day to sound trumpets. 📖

## Numbers 29:2
Present one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs aged one year as a burnt offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. All must be without defect. 📖

## Numbers 29:3
Include grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil. Use three tenths of an ephah for the bull and two tenths for the ram. 📖

## Numbers 29:4
Use one tenth of an ephah for each of the seven male lambs. 📖

## Numbers 29:5
Include one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you. 📖

## Numbers 29:6
These are additional to the monthly and daily burnt offerings, with their prescribed grain and drink offerings. They are a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 29:7
Hold a sacred assembly on this seventh month’s tenth day. Humble yourselves and do no work. 📖

## Numbers 29:8
Present one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs aged one year as a burnt offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. All must be without defect. 📖

## Numbers 29:9
Include grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil. Use three tenths of an ephah for the bull and two tenths for the ram. 📖

## Numbers 29:10
Use one tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 📖

## Numbers 29:11
Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the sin offering of atonement. Also include the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 29:12
Hold a sacred assembly on the seventh month’s fifteenth day. Do no regular work. Celebrate a feast to the LORD for seven days. 📖

## Numbers 29:13
Present thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs aged one year as a burnt food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. All must be without defect. 📖

## Numbers 29:14
Bring grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil. Use three tenths of an ephah for each of the thirteen bulls. Use two tenths for each of the two rams. 📖

## Numbers 29:15
Use one tenth of an ephah for each of the fourteen lambs. 📖

## Numbers 29:16
Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 29:17
On the second day, present twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs aged one year. All must be without defect. 📖

## Numbers 29:18
Include the prescribed grain and drink offerings according to the number of bulls, rams, and lambs. 📖

## Numbers 29:19
Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 29:20

## Numbers 29:21

Include one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offering. 📖
