## Numbers 35:1
The LORD spoke to Moses again on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, opposite Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 35:2
“Command Israel to give the Levites towns from their inheritance to live in. Include pasturelands around the towns. 📖

## Numbers 35:3
The towns are their homes. The pastures are for their herds, flocks, and other livestock. 📖

## Numbers 35:4
The Levites’ pastures must extend a thousand cubits from each town’s wall on every side. 📖

## Numbers 35:5
Also measure two thousand cubits outside on the east, south, west, and north, with the town in the center. These form the towns’ larger pasturelands. 📖

## Numbers 35:6
Choose six of the Levites’ towns as refuge towns, where someone who kills another may flee. Give them forty-two additional towns. 📖

## Numbers 35:7
Give the Levites forty-eight towns in total, with their pasturelands. 📖

## Numbers 35:8
Each tribe must give towns from its territory in proportion to its inheritance. Large tribes must give more, and small tribes less.” 📖

## Numbers 35:9
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 35:10
“Tell Israel: when you cross the Jordan into Canaan, 📖

## Numbers 35:11
designate refuge towns for anyone who kills someone unintentionally to flee to. 📖

## Numbers 35:12
These towns offer refuge from the avenger. The person who killed another must not die before standing trial before the assembly. 📖

## Numbers 35:13
Select six refuge towns. 📖

## Numbers 35:14
Choose three across the Jordan and three in Canaan. 📖

## Numbers 35:15
These six towns offer refuge to Israelites, foreigners, and strangers among them. Anyone who kills someone unintentionally may flee there. 📖

## Numbers 35:16
If someone strikes and kills another with an iron object, that person is a murderer. The murderer must die. 📖

## Numbers 35:17
If someone holds a stone large enough to kill and strikes another dead with it, that person is a murderer. The murderer must die. 📖

## Numbers 35:18
If someone strikes and kills another with a deadly wooden object held in the hand, that person is a murderer. The murderer must die. 📖

## Numbers 35:19
The avenger of blood must kill the murderer upon finding him. 📖

## Numbers 35:20
Someone may maliciously push another, or deliberately throw an object at him, and kill him. 📖

## Numbers 35:21
Or someone may strike another by hand in hostility and kill him. In these cases, the killer is a murderer and must die. The avenger of blood must kill him upon finding him. 📖

## Numbers 35:22
But someone may suddenly push another without hostility or throw an object unintentionally. 📖

## Numbers 35:23
Or, without looking, he may drop a heavy stone and kill someone. The dead person was not his enemy, and he intended no harm. 📖

## Numbers 35:24
Then the congregation must judge between the killer and the avenger of blood according to these ordinances. 📖

## Numbers 35:25
The assembly must protect the killer from the avenger and return him to the refuge town where he fled. He must live there until the high priest, anointed with holy oil, dies. 📖

## Numbers 35:26
But the killer may leave his refuge town’s boundaries. 📖

## Numbers 35:27
If the avenger finds and kills him outside, the avenger bears no bloodguilt. 📖

## Numbers 35:28
The killer must stay in his refuge town until the high priest dies. Only then may he return to his own land. 📖

## Numbers 35:29
This is a statutory ordinance for you and future generations, wherever you live. 📖

## Numbers 35:30
A murderer must be executed on witnesses’ testimony. No one may be executed on a single witness’s testimony. 📖

## Numbers 35:31
Accept no ransom to spare a murderer who deserves death. He must die. 📖

## Numbers 35:32
Accept no ransom to let someone who fled to refuge return to his land before the high priest dies. 📖

## Numbers 35:33
Do not pollute your land. Bloodshed pollutes it. Only the blood of the person who shed blood can make atonement for that land. 📖

## Numbers 35:34
Do not defile the land where you live and I dwell. I, the LORD, dwell among Israel.” 📖
