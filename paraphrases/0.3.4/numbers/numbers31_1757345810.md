## Numbers 31:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 31:2
“Take vengeance on Midian for Israel. Afterward, you will be gathered to your people.” 📖

## Numbers 31:3
Moses told the people, “Arm some men to fight Midian and carry out the LORD’s vengeance. 📖

## Numbers 31:4
Send a thousand men from each Israelite tribe into battle.” 📖

## Numbers 31:5
A thousand were recruited from each tribe, twelve thousand armed men. 📖

## Numbers 31:6
Moses sent the thousand from each tribe with Phinehas, son of Eleazar the priest. Phinehas carried sanctuary vessels and trumpets for signals. 📖

## Numbers 31:7
They fought Midian as the LORD commanded Moses. They killed every male. 📖

## Numbers 31:8
They killed Midian’s five kings: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. They also killed Balaam son of Beor with the sword. 📖

## Numbers 31:9
Israel captured Midian’s women and children. They plundered all their herds, flocks, and goods. 📖

## Numbers 31:10
They burned all Midian’s inhabited towns and camps. 📖

## Numbers 31:11
They took all the plunder and spoils, including people and animals. 📖

## Numbers 31:12
They brought the captives and plunder to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and Israel’s congregation. The camp was on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, opposite Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 31:13
Moses, Eleazar, and all the congregation’s leaders met them outside camp. 📖

## Numbers 31:14
Moses was angry with the army officers returning from battle, the commanders of thousands and hundreds. 📖

## Numbers 31:15
He asked, “Did you spare all the women? 📖

## Numbers 31:16
Following Balaam’s advice, these women led Israel into unfaithfulness against the LORD at Peor. That caused plague to strike His congregation. 📖

## Numbers 31:17
Kill all the boys and every woman who has had sexual relations with a man. 📖

## Numbers 31:18
But keep alive for yourselves every girl who has never had sexual relations with a man. 📖

## Numbers 31:19
Everyone who killed someone or touched a dead person must stay outside camp seven days. Purify yourselves and your captives on the third and seventh days. 📖

## Numbers 31:20
Purify all clothing, leather goods, everything made from goat hair, and all wooden articles.” 📖

## Numbers 31:21
Eleazar the priest told the returning soldiers, “This is the law the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 31:22
Gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead, 📖

## Numbers 31:23
everything that withstands fire, must pass through fire to become clean. Also purify it with purification water. Everything that cannot withstand fire must pass through water. 📖

## Numbers 31:24
Wash your clothes on the seventh day. Then you will be clean and may enter camp.” 📖

## Numbers 31:25
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 31:26
“You, Eleazar the priest, and the congregation’s family heads must count all captured people and animals. 📖

## Numbers 31:27
Divide the captives between the soldiers who fought and the rest of the congregation. 📖

## Numbers 31:28
From the soldiers’ share, set aside tribute for the LORD: one of every five hundred people, cattle, donkeys, and sheep. 📖

## Numbers 31:29
Take this from their half. Give it to Eleazar the priest as the LORD’s offering. 📖

## Numbers 31:30
From Israel’s half, take one of every fifty people, cattle, donkeys, sheep, and other animals. Give them to the Levites who care for the LORD’s tabernacle.” 📖

## Numbers 31:31
Moses and Eleazar obeyed the LORD’s command to Moses. 📖

## Numbers 31:32
This plunder remained from the soldiers’ spoils: 675,000 sheep, 📖

## Numbers 31:33
72,000 cattle, 📖

## Numbers 31:34
61,000 donkeys, 📖

## Numbers 31:35
and 32,000 women who had never slept with a man. 📖

## Numbers 31:36
The soldiers’ half included 337,500 sheep. 📖

## Numbers 31:37
The LORD’s tribute from these was 675. 📖

## Numbers 31:38
Their share included 36,000 cattle, with 72 as the LORD’s tribute, 📖

## Numbers 31:39
30,500 donkeys, with 61 as His tribute, 📖

## Numbers 31:40
and 16,000 people, with 32 as His tribute. 📖

## Numbers 31:41
Moses gave the tribute to Eleazar the priest as the LORD’s offering, exactly as commanded. 📖

## Numbers 31:42
Moses separated Israel’s half from the soldiers’ share. 📖

## Numbers 31:43
The congregation’s half included 337,500 sheep, 📖

## Numbers 31:44
36,000 cattle, 📖

## Numbers 31:45
30,500 donkeys, 📖

## Numbers 31:46
and 16,000 people. 📖

## Numbers 31:47
Moses took one of every fifty people and animals from Israel’s half. He gave them to the Levites who cared for the LORD’s tabernacle, as commanded. 📖

## Numbers 31:48
The army’s officers, commanders of thousands and hundreds, approached Moses. 📖

## Numbers 31:49
They said, “We, your servants, counted our soldiers. Not one is missing. 📖

## Numbers 31:50
We bring the LORD the gold articles each acquired: armlets, bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces. This is to make atonement for us before Him.” 📖

## Numbers 31:51
Moses and Eleazar received all these gold articles. 📖

## Numbers 31:52
The gold that the commanders of thousands and hundreds offered to the LORD weighed 16,750 shekels. 📖

## Numbers 31:53
Each soldier had taken plunder for himself. 📖

## Numbers 31:54
Moses and Eleazar received the commanders’ gold and brought it into the Tent of Meeting. It served as a memorial for Israel before the LORD. 📖
