## Numbers 4:1
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 4:2
“Count the Kohathites among the Levites by clans and families. 📖

## Numbers 4:3
Count men aged thirty to fifty who qualify to work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:4
The Kohathites’ service at the Tent of Meeting concerns the most holy things. 📖

## Numbers 4:5
When the camp prepares to leave, Aaron and his sons must enter. They must take down the curtain’s veil and cover the ark of the Testimony with it. 📖

## Numbers 4:6
Put fine leather over it, then a cloth entirely blue. Insert the ark’s poles. 📖

## Numbers 4:7
Spread a blue cloth over the table of the Presence. Put its plates, cups, bowls, and drink offering pitchers on it. Leave the regular bread offering there. 📖

## Numbers 4:8
Spread a scarlet cloth over them, cover them with fine leather, and insert the poles. 📖

## Numbers 4:9
Cover the lampstand used for light with a blue cloth. Include its lamps, wick trimmers, trays, and jars of oil for its supply. 📖

## Numbers 4:10
Wrap it and all its utensils in fine leather. Put it on the carrying frame. 📖

## Numbers 4:11
Spread a blue cloth over the gold altar. Cover it with fine leather and insert the poles. 📖

## Numbers 4:12
Put all utensils used for sanctuary service in a blue cloth. Cover them with fine leather and put them on the carrying frame. 📖

## Numbers 4:13
Remove ashes from the bronze altar. Spread a purple cloth over it. 📖

## Numbers 4:14
Put all its equipment on it: firepans, meat forks, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. Cover it with fine leather and insert the poles. 📖

## Numbers 4:15
Aaron and his sons must finish covering the holy objects and their equipment. When the camp is ready to move, the Kohathites must carry them. They must not touch the holy objects, or they will die. These are their transport duties for the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:16
Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, must supervise the lamp oil, fragrant incense, daily grain offering, and anointing oil. He supervises the whole tabernacle and everything in it, including holy objects and utensils.” 📖

## Numbers 4:17
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 4:18
“Do not let the Kohathite clans be cut off from the Levites. 📖

## Numbers 4:19
Do this so they can approach the most holy things and live: Aaron and his sons must enter first. They must assign each man his task and load. 📖

## Numbers 4:20
The Kohathites must not enter and look at the holy objects, even briefly. Otherwise, they will die.” 📖

## Numbers 4:21
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 4:22
“Also count the Gershonites by families and clans. 📖

## Numbers 4:23
Count everyone aged thirty to fifty who comes to work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:24
These are the Gershonite clans’ duties for work and transport. 📖

## Numbers 4:25
They must carry the tabernacle’s curtains and the Tent of Meeting with its fine leather covering. They must carry the Tent of Meeting’s entrance curtains, 📖

## Numbers 4:26
the courtyard curtains, and the entrance curtains for the courtyard around the tabernacle and altar. Include their ropes and all service equipment. The Gershonites must perform all necessary work for these items. 📖

## Numbers 4:27
Aaron and his sons must direct all their transport duties and other work. Assign everything they are responsible to carry. 📖

## Numbers 4:28
This is the Gershonite clans’ service at the Tent of Meeting. Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest, must direct their duties. 📖

## Numbers 4:29
Count Merari’s descendants by clans and families. 📖

## Numbers 4:30
Count everyone aged thirty to fifty who comes to work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:31
Their service at the Tent of Meeting is to carry the tabernacle’s frames, crossbars, posts, and bases. 📖

## Numbers 4:32
They must carry the surrounding courtyard’s posts, bases, tent pegs, ropes, and all related equipment. Assign by name the items each must carry. 📖

## Numbers 4:33
This is all the Merarite clans’ work at the Tent of Meeting. Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest, must direct it.” 📖

## Numbers 4:34
Moses, Aaron, and the congregation’s leaders counted the Kohathites by clans and families. 📖

## Numbers 4:35
They counted everyone aged thirty to fifty who came to work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:36
The total counted by clans was 2,750. 📖

## Numbers 4:37
These Kohathites could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron counted them as the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖

## Numbers 4:38
The Gershonites were counted by clans and families. 📖

## Numbers 4:39
They counted everyone aged thirty to fifty who came to work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:40
The total counted by clans and families was 2,630. 📖

## Numbers 4:41
These Gershonites served at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron counted them at the LORD’s command. 📖

## Numbers 4:42
The Merarites were counted by clans and families. 📖

## Numbers 4:43
They counted everyone aged thirty to fifty who came to work at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:44
The men registered by clans totaled 3,200. 📖

## Numbers 4:45
These Merarites were counted by Moses and Aaron at the LORD’s command through Moses. 📖

## Numbers 4:46
Moses, Aaron, and Israel’s leaders counted all the Levites by clans and families. 📖

## Numbers 4:47
They counted everyone aged thirty to fifty who came to serve and carry the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 4:48
The men totaled 8,580. 📖

## Numbers 4:49
At the LORD’s command through Moses, they were counted. Each received his work and load, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖
