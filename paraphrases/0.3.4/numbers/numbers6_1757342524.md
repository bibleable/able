## Numbers 6:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 6:2
“Tell Israel: a man or woman may make a special Nazirite vow to be set apart for the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 6:3
Then the Nazirite must avoid wine and strong drink, and vinegar made from either. Do not drink grape juice or eat fresh grapes or raisins. 📖

## Numbers 6:4
Throughout the time set apart, eat nothing from grapevines, not even seeds or skins. 📖

## Numbers 6:5
No razor may touch the Nazirite’s head throughout the vow. Remain holy until the time set apart for the LORD ends. Let the hair grow long. 📖

## Numbers 6:6
Do not approach a dead body throughout this time set apart for the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 6:7
Do not become defiled even when a father, mother, brother, or sister dies. The symbol of dedication to God is on the Nazirite’s head. 📖

## Numbers 6:8
Throughout this time, the Nazirite is holy to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 6:9
Someone may suddenly die nearby and defile the Nazirite’s dedicated hair. If so, shave the head on the day of cleansing, the seventh day. 📖

## Numbers 6:10
On the eighth day, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 6:11
The priest must offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. This makes atonement for the sin of being near the dead body. The Nazirite must dedicate the head again that day. 📖

## Numbers 6:12
Dedicate the time set apart to the LORD again. Bring a male lamb aged one year as a guilt offering. The previous days do not count, because the time set apart was defiled. 📖

## Numbers 6:13
This is the law when a Nazirite’s time set apart ends. Bring the Nazirite to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 6:14
Present a male lamb aged one year without defect to the LORD as a burnt offering. Present a female lamb aged one year without defect as a sin offering. Also bring a ram without defect as a peace offering. 📖

## Numbers 6:15
Include their grain and drink offerings. Bring a basket of cakes without yeast, made from fine flour mixed with oil. Include wafers without yeast, coated with oil. 📖

## Numbers 6:16
The priest must present these before the LORD. He must offer the sin offering and burnt offering. 📖

## Numbers 6:17
He must offer the ram to the LORD as a peace offering with the basket of bread without yeast. He must also offer the accompanying grain and drink offerings. 📖

## Numbers 6:18
At the Tent of Meeting’s entrance, the Nazirite must shave the dedicated head. Put the hair in the fire under the peace offering. 📖

## Numbers 6:19
The priest must take the ram’s boiled shoulder, one cake without yeast from the basket, and one wafer without yeast. Put them in the hands of the Nazirite who just shaved the dedicated hair. 📖

## Numbers 6:20
The priest must wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. This holy portion belongs to the priest, besides the breast of the wave offering and the thigh presented. Afterward, the Nazirite may drink wine. 📖

## Numbers 6:21
This is the law for a Nazirite’s offering vowed to the LORD for the time set apart. It is additional to whatever else the Nazirite can afford. Fulfill every vow according to this law.” 📖

## Numbers 6:22
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 6:23
“Tell Aaron and his sons to bless Israel this way: 📖

## Numbers 6:24
‘May the LORD bless and protect you. 🙏

## Numbers 6:25
May He make His face shine on you and treat you with grace. 🙏

## Numbers 6:26
May He lift His face toward you and give you peace.’ 🙏

## Numbers 6:27
Thus they will put My name on Israel, and I will bless them.” 📖
