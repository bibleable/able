## Numbers 36:1
Gilead’s clan descended from Gilead son of Machir son of Manasseh, one of Joseph’s clans. Its family heads approached Moses and Israel’s family leaders. 📖

## Numbers 36:2
They said, “The LORD commanded you, our lord, to divide Israel’s land by lot as an inheritance. He also commanded you to give our brother Zelophehad’s inheritance to his daughters. 📖

## Numbers 36:3
If they marry men from other Israelite tribes, their land will leave our fathers’ portion. It will join the tribes they marry into. We will lose our allotted inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 36:4
At Israel’s Jubilee, their inheritance will belong to the tribes they marry into. Our fathers’ tribe will lose it.” 📖

## Numbers 36:5
At the LORD’s word, Moses commanded Israel, “Joseph’s tribe is right. 📖

## Numbers 36:6
The LORD commands this about Zelophehad’s daughters: they may marry whoever they want, but only within a clan of their father’s tribe. 📖

## Numbers 36:7
No Israelite inheritance may pass between tribes. Each Israelite must keep his ancestral tribe’s inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 36:8
Every daughter inheriting land from an Israelite tribe must marry within a clan of her father’s tribe. Then each Israelite will possess his fathers’ inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 36:9
No inheritance may pass between tribes. Each tribe must keep its own inheritance.” 📖

## Numbers 36:10
Zelophehad’s daughters obeyed the LORD’s command to Moses. 📖

## Numbers 36:11
Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah married cousins on their father’s side. They were Zelophehad’s daughters. 📖

## Numbers 36:12
They married within the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph’s descendants. Their inheritance stayed within their father’s clan’s tribe. 📖

## Numbers 36:13
The LORD gave Israel these commands and ordinances through Moses on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, opposite Jericho. 📖
