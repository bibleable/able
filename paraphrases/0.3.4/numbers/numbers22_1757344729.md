## Numbers 22:1
Israel traveled and camped on Moab’s plains near the Jordan, opposite Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 22:2
Balak son of Zippor saw everything Israel did to the Amorites. 📖

## Numbers 22:3
Moab feared Israel greatly because they were so numerous. The Moabites dreaded them. 📖

## Numbers 22:4
They told Midian’s elders, “This horde will consume everything around us like an ox eating field grass.” Balak son of Zippor was then Moab’s king. 📖

## Numbers 22:5
He sent messengers to call Balaam son of Beor from Pethor by the Euphrates, in his people’s land. Balak said, “People from Egypt cover the land. They have settled beside me. 📖

## Numbers 22:6
Please come and curse them. They are too powerful for me. Perhaps I can then defeat them and drive them away. I know that those you bless are blessed, and those you curse are cursed.” 📖

## Numbers 22:7
Moab’s and Midian’s elders traveled with payment for divination. They reached Balaam and gave him Balak’s message. 📖

## Numbers 22:8
He replied, “Stay overnight. I will tell you the LORD’s answer.” Moab’s princes stayed with him. 📖

## Numbers 22:9
God came to Balaam and asked, “Who are these men with you?” 📖

## Numbers 22:10
Balaam replied, “Balak son of Zippor, Moab’s king, sent this message: 📖

## Numbers 22:11
‘People came from Egypt and cover the land. Come and curse them for me. Perhaps I can fight and drive them away.’” 📖

## Numbers 22:12
God told Balaam, “Do not go with them. Do not curse these people. They are blessed.” 📖

## Numbers 22:13
Next morning, Balaam told Balak’s princes, “Return home. The LORD refuses to let me go with you.” 📖

## Numbers 22:14
Moab’s princes returned to Balak, saying, “Balaam refused to come.” 📖

## Numbers 22:15
Balak sent more princes, greater in number and more distinguished than the first messengers. 📖

## Numbers 22:16
They told Balaam, “Balak son of Zippor says, ‘Let nothing stop you coming to me. 📖

## Numbers 22:17
I will honor you richly and do whatever you say. Please come and curse these people!’” 📖

## Numbers 22:18
Balaam answered Balak’s servants, “Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not exceed my God’s command. I could do nothing small or great beyond what the LORD commands. 📖

## Numbers 22:19
Stay overnight like the others. I will learn what else the LORD tells me.” 📖

## Numbers 22:20
God came to Balaam that night. “These men came to call you, so go with them. But do only what I tell you.” 📖

## Numbers 22:21
In the morning, Balaam saddled his donkey and went with Moab’s princes. 📖

## Numbers 22:22
God became angry because Balaam was going. The LORD’s angel stood in the road to oppose him. Balaam rode his donkey with two servants. 📖

## Numbers 22:23
The donkey saw the angel in the road holding a drawn sword. She left the road for a field. Balaam beat her to make her return. 📖

## Numbers 22:24
The LORD’s angel stood in a narrow passage between two vineyards, with walls on both sides. 📖

## Numbers 22:25
The donkey saw him and pressed against a wall, crushing Balaam’s foot. Balaam beat her again. 📖

## Numbers 22:26
The angel moved ahead to a narrow place. There was no room to turn right or left. 📖

## Numbers 22:27
The donkey saw the angel and lay down beneath Balaam. He became furious and beat her with his staff. 📖

## Numbers 22:28
The LORD opened her mouth. She asked Balaam, “What did I do to deserve these three beatings?” 📖

## Numbers 22:29
He replied, “You made a fool of me! If I had a sword, I would kill you now!” 📖

## Numbers 22:30
She asked, “Am I not the donkey you have ridden all your life until today? Have I ever treated you this way?” He answered, “No.” 📖

## Numbers 22:31
The LORD opened Balaam’s eyes. He saw the LORD’s angel in the road holding a drawn sword. Balaam bowed and fell facedown. 📖

## Numbers 22:32
The angel asked, “Why beat your donkey these three times? I came to oppose you because your way is perverse in my sight. 📖

## Numbers 22:33
She saw me and turned away three times. Otherwise, I would have killed you and spared her.” 📖

## Numbers 22:34
Balaam replied, “I sinned. I did not realize you stood in the road to confront me. If this displeases you, I will return home.” 📖

## Numbers 22:35
The angel said, “Go with the men, but speak only what I tell you.” Balaam went with Balak’s princes. 📖

## Numbers 22:36
Balak heard he was coming. He met Balaam at the Moabite town on the Arnon border, at his territory’s edge. 📖

## Numbers 22:37
He asked, “Did I not urgently call you? Why did you not come? Can I not reward you richly?” 📖

## Numbers 22:38
Balaam replied, “I have come. But can I say anything I wish? I must speak only the word God puts in my mouth.” 📖

## Numbers 22:39
Balaam accompanied Balak to Kiriath-huzoth. 📖

## Numbers 22:40
Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep. He gave portions to Balaam and the princes with him. 📖

## Numbers 22:41
Next morning, Balak brought Balaam up to Bamoth-baal. He could see the people’s camp outskirts from there. 📖
