## Numbers 16:1
Korah was the son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi. He joined the Reubenites Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth. 📖

## Numbers 16:2
They rebelled against Moses with 250 Israelite men. These were well-known congregational leaders and assembly representatives. 📖

## Numbers 16:3
They gathered against Moses and Aaron, saying, “You have taken too much authority! Everyone in the congregation is holy, and the LORD is among them. Why place yourselves above His assembly?” 📖

## Numbers 16:4
Moses heard and fell facedown. 📖

## Numbers 16:5
He told Korah and his followers, “Tomorrow morning, the LORD will show who belongs to Him and who is holy. He will bring His chosen person near Him. 📖

## Numbers 16:6
Korah, you and all your followers must take censers. 📖

## Numbers 16:7
Tomorrow, put fire and incense in them before the LORD. The man He chooses will be holy. You sons of Levi have taken too much upon yourselves!” 📖

## Numbers 16:8
Moses also told Korah, “Listen, sons of Levi! 📖

## Numbers 16:9
Israel’s God set you apart from the congregation and brought you near Him. You work at His tabernacle and stand before the congregation to serve them. Is that not enough? 📖

## Numbers 16:10
He brought you and all your fellow Levites near Him. But you seek the priesthood too. 📖

## Numbers 16:11
You and your followers have conspired against the LORD! Who is Aaron, that you should complain against him?” 📖

## Numbers 16:12
Moses summoned Dathan and Abiram, Eliab’s sons. They replied, “We will not come! 📖

## Numbers 16:13
You brought us from a land flowing with milk and honey to kill us in the wilderness. Is that not enough? Must you rule us too? 📖

## Numbers 16:14
You have not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey or given us fields and vineyards as an inheritance. Will you gouge out these men’s eyes? We will not come!” 📖

## Numbers 16:15
Moses became very angry and told the LORD, “Do not accept their offering. I took no donkey from them and mistreated none of them.” 📖

## Numbers 16:16
He told Korah, “You and all your followers must appear before the LORD tomorrow with Aaron. 📖

## Numbers 16:17
Each man must bring his censer with incense before the LORD, 250 censers. You and Aaron must bring yours too.” 📖

## Numbers 16:18
Each took a censer with fire and incense. They stood with Moses and Aaron at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 16:19
Korah gathered his whole assembly against them at the entrance. The LORD’s glory appeared to the whole congregation. 📖

## Numbers 16:20
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 16:21
“Separate yourselves from this congregation, so I can consume them instantly.” 📖

## Numbers 16:22
They fell facedown and said, “God, God of the spirits of all flesh, will You be angry with everyone when one man sins?” 🙏

## Numbers 16:23
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 16:24
“Tell the congregation to move from Korah’s, Dathan’s, and Abiram’s homes.” 📖

## Numbers 16:25
Moses rose and went to Dathan and Abiram. Israel’s elders followed. 📖

## Numbers 16:26
He warned everyone, “Move away from these wicked men’s tents. Touch nothing they own, or you will be swept away because of all their sins.” 📖

## Numbers 16:27
They moved from Korah’s, Dathan’s, and Abiram’s homes. Dathan and Abiram stood at their tent entrances with their wives, children, and infants. 📖

## Numbers 16:28
Moses said, “This will show that the LORD sent me to do everything. It was not my own doing. 📖

## Numbers 16:29
If these men die naturally, sharing everyone’s usual fate, the LORD did not send me. 📖

## Numbers 16:30
But He may do something never done before. The earth may open its mouth and swallow them with all they own, taking them alive into Sheol. If so, you will know they despised the LORD.” 📖

## Numbers 16:31
Immediately after Moses finished speaking, the ground beneath them split. 📖

## Numbers 16:32
The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and their households. It swallowed all Korah’s men and their possessions. 📖

## Numbers 16:33
They descended alive into Sheol with everything they owned. The earth closed over them, and they vanished from the assembly. 📖

## Numbers 16:34
Israelites around them heard their cries and fled. They said, “The earth may swallow us too!” 📖

## Numbers 16:35
Fire came from the LORD and consumed the 250 men offering incense. 📖

## Numbers 16:36
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 16:37
“Tell Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, to remove the censers from the fire. Scatter the coals far away, because the censers are holy. 📖

## Numbers 16:38
Their owners sinned at the cost of their lives. Hammer their censers into sheets to cover the altar. They became holy because they were presented before the LORD. They will be a sign to Israel.” 📖

## Numbers 16:39
Eleazar took the bronze censers from those burned to death. He had them hammered into an altar covering. 📖

## Numbers 16:40
This followed the LORD’s command through Moses. It reminded Israel that no outsider, anyone not descended from Aaron, may approach to offer incense before the LORD. Otherwise, he might become like Korah and his followers. 📖

## Numbers 16:41
Next day, Israel’s whole congregation complained against Moses and Aaron. “You killed the LORD’s people!” 📖

## Numbers 16:42
Everyone gathered against them. Moses and Aaron turned toward the Tent of Meeting. Suddenly the cloud covered it, and the LORD’s glory appeared. 📖

## Numbers 16:43
Moses and Aaron went to the Tent of Meeting’s front. 📖

## Numbers 16:44
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 16:45
“Move from this congregation, so I can consume them instantly.” Moses and Aaron fell facedown. 📖

## Numbers 16:46
Moses told Aaron, “Put fire from the altar in your censer and add incense. Hurry to the congregation and make atonement. Wrath has come from the LORD. The plague has begun.” 📖

## Numbers 16:47
Aaron took the censer as ordered and ran among the assembly. He saw the plague had begun. He offered incense and made atonement for the people. 📖

## Numbers 16:48
He stood between the living and dead, and the plague stopped. 📖

## Numbers 16:49
The plague killed 14,700, besides those who died because of Korah. 📖

## Numbers 16:50
Aaron returned to Moses at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance because the plague had stopped. 📖
