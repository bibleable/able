## Numbers 23:1
Balaam told Balak, “Build seven altars here for me. Prepare seven bulls and seven rams.” 📖

## Numbers 23:2
Balak obeyed. He and Balaam offered one bull and one ram on each altar. 📖

## Numbers 23:3
Balaam told him, “Stay by your burnt offering while I leave. Perhaps the LORD will meet me. I will tell you whatever He reveals.” Balaam went to a barren height. 📖

## Numbers 23:4
God met him. Balaam said, “I built seven altars and offered a bull and ram on each.” 📖

## Numbers 23:5
The LORD put a message in his mouth. “Return to Balak and give this message.” 📖

## Numbers 23:6
Balaam returned. Balak stood beside his burnt offering with all Moab’s princes. 📖

## Numbers 23:7
Balaam proclaimed this oracle: “Moab’s king Balak brought me from Aram, from the eastern mountains. He said, ‘Come, curse Jacob and denounce Israel!’ 📖

## Numbers 23:8
How can I curse those God has not cursed? How can I denounce those the LORD has not denounced? 📖

## Numbers 23:9
I see them from rocky cliffs and watch them from the hills. They live apart, not counting themselves among the nations. 📖

## Numbers 23:10
Who can count Jacob’s dust or even a fourth of Israel? Let me die like the righteous. Let my end be like theirs!” 📖

## Numbers 23:11
Balak said, “What have you done to me? I brought you to curse my enemies, but you only blessed them!” 📖

## Numbers 23:12
Balaam replied, “Must I not say exactly what the LORD puts in my mouth?” 📖

## Numbers 23:13
Balak said, “Come to another place where you can see them. You will see only the camp’s outskirts. Curse them for me there.” 📖

## Numbers 23:14
Balak brought him to Zophim’s field on Pisgah’s summit. He built seven altars and offered a bull and ram on each. 📖

## Numbers 23:15
Balaam said, “Stay beside your burnt offering while I meet the LORD over there.” 📖

## Numbers 23:16
The LORD met Balaam and put a message in his mouth. “Return to Balak and say what I tell you.” 📖

## Numbers 23:17
He returned to Balak, who stood by his burnt offering with Moab’s princes. Balak asked, “What did the LORD say?” 📖

## Numbers 23:18
Balaam proclaimed this oracle: “Rise and listen, Balak! Son of Zippor, hear me. 📖

## Numbers 23:19
God is not a man who lies or a son of man who changes his mind. Does He speak without acting? Does He promise without fulfilling? 📖

## Numbers 23:20
I received a command to bless. He blessed, and I cannot change it. 📖

## Numbers 23:21
He considers no disaster for Jacob and sees no trouble for Israel. The LORD their God is with them. The King’s shout is among them. 📖

## Numbers 23:22
God brought them from Egypt with strength like a wild ox. 📖

## Numbers 23:23
No spell stands against Jacob, and no divination stands against Israel. People will now say of them, ‘What great things God has done!’ 📖

## Numbers 23:24
The people rise like a lioness and rouse themselves like a lion. They will not rest until they devour prey and drink the slain’s blood.” 📖

## Numbers 23:25
Balak said, “Then neither curse nor bless them!” 📖

## Numbers 23:26
Balaam replied, “Did I not tell you I must do whatever the LORD says?” 📖

## Numbers 23:27
Balak said, “Come to another place. Perhaps God will be pleased if you curse them for me there.” 📖

## Numbers 23:28
He took Balaam to Peor’s summit, overlooking the wasteland. 📖

## Numbers 23:29
Balaam said, “Build seven altars here for me. Prepare seven bulls and seven rams.” 📖

## Numbers 23:30
Balak obeyed. He offered a bull and ram on each altar. 📖
