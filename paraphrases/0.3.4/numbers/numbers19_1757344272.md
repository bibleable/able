## Numbers 19:1
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 19:2
“This is the law the LORD commands. Tell Israel to bring a red heifer without any defect that has never worn a yoke. 📖

## Numbers 19:3
Give it to Eleazar the priest. He must have it brought outside camp and killed before him. 📖

## Numbers 19:4
He must take some blood on his finger. Sprinkle it seven times toward the Tent of Meeting’s front. 📖

## Numbers 19:5
Burn the heifer before him. Burn its hide, flesh, blood, and dung. 📖

## Numbers 19:6
The priest must throw cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet wool onto the burning heifer. 📖

## Numbers 19:7
He must wash his clothes and bathe in water. Then he may enter camp, but remains ceremonially unclean until evening. 📖

## Numbers 19:8
The person who burned the heifer must also wash his clothes and bathe in water. He too remains ceremonially unclean until evening. 📖

## Numbers 19:9
A ceremonially clean man must collect the ashes and store them in a ceremonially clean place outside camp. Israel’s congregation must keep them for preparing purification water. This purifies from sin. 📖

## Numbers 19:10
The man collecting the ashes must wash his clothes and remains ceremonially unclean until evening. This is a permanent statute for Israel and foreign residents. 📖

## Numbers 19:11
Anyone touching a dead body is unclean for seven days. 📖

## Numbers 19:12
Purify yourself with the water on the third and seventh days. Then you will be clean. Without purification on both days, you remain unclean. 📖

## Numbers 19:13
Anyone touching a human corpse who fails to become purified defiles the LORD’s tabernacle. Cut that person off from Israel. Without purification water being sprinkled on that person, the uncleanness remains. 📖

## Numbers 19:14
This is the law when someone dies in a tent. Everyone there and everyone entering becomes unclean for seven days. 📖

## Numbers 19:15
Every open container without a lid fastened on it becomes unclean. 📖

## Numbers 19:16
In an open field, touching someone killed by a sword or dead naturally makes a person unclean for seven days. Touching a human bone or grave does the same. 📖

## Numbers 19:17
To purify an unclean person, put ashes from the burnt sin offering in a jar. Pour fresh water over them. 📖

## Numbers 19:18
A ceremonially clean man must dip hyssop in the water. Sprinkle the tent, all its furnishings, and everyone who was there. Also sprinkle whoever touched a bone, grave, or someone dead naturally or killed. 📖

## Numbers 19:19
He must sprinkle the unclean person on the third and seventh days. After purification on the seventh day, the person must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That evening, he will be clean. 📖

## Numbers 19:20
Cut anyone unclean who fails to become purified off from the assembly. That person defiled the LORD’s sanctuary. Without purification water sprinkled on that person, the uncleanness remains. 📖

## Numbers 19:21
This is a permanent statute: the person sprinkling purification water must wash his clothes. Anyone touching that water is unclean until evening. 📖

## Numbers 19:22
Everything an unclean person touches becomes unclean. Anyone touching it is unclean until evening.” 📖
