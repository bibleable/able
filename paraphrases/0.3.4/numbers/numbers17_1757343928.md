## Numbers 17:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 17:2
“Tell Israel to bring twelve staffs, one from each tribal leader. Write each man’s name on his staff. 📖

## Numbers 17:3
Write Aaron’s name on Levi’s staff. There must be one staff for each tribe’s head. 📖

## Numbers 17:4
Put them in the Tent of Meeting before the Testimony, where I meet you. 📖

## Numbers 17:5
My chosen man’s staff will sprout. I will end Israel’s constant complaints against you.” 📖

## Numbers 17:6
Moses spoke to Israel. Each tribal leader gave him a staff, twelve in total. Aaron’s staff was among them. 📖

## Numbers 17:7
Moses placed them before the LORD in the Tent of the Testimony. 📖

## Numbers 17:8
Next day, he entered the Tent. Aaron’s staff for Levi’s family had sprouted, budded, blossomed, and produced almonds. 📖

## Numbers 17:9
Moses brought every staff from the LORD’s presence to Israel. They saw them, and each man took his own. 📖

## Numbers 17:10
The LORD told Moses, “Put Aaron’s staff back before the Testimony. Keep it as a sign for rebels, to end their complaints against Me so they do not die.” 📖

## Numbers 17:11
Moses did as commanded. 📖

## Numbers 17:12
Israel told Moses, “Look, we are perishing! We are lost, all of us! 📖

## Numbers 17:13
Anyone approaching the LORD’s tabernacle dies. Will we all perish?” 📖
