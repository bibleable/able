## Numbers 12:1
Miriam and Aaron criticized Moses because he married a Cushite woman. His wife was a Cushite. 📖

## Numbers 12:2
They said, “Does the LORD speak only through Moses? Does He not speak through us too?” The LORD heard this. 📖

## Numbers 12:3
Moses was very humble, more than any other man on earth. 📖

## Numbers 12:4
Suddenly the LORD told Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, “You three, come to the Tent of Meeting.” They went. 📖

## Numbers 12:5
He descended in a pillar of cloud and stood at the Tent’s entrance. He called Aaron and Miriam forward. They both came. 📖

## Numbers 12:6
He said, “Hear My words. If there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, reveal Myself through visions and speak through dreams. 📖

## Numbers 12:7
This differs with My servant Moses. He is faithful in all My house. 📖

## Numbers 12:8
I speak with him face to face, clearly, without riddles. He sees the LORD’s form. Why were you unafraid to criticize My servant Moses?” 📖

## Numbers 12:9
The LORD’s anger burned against them, and He left. 📖

## Numbers 12:10
When the cloud rose above the Tent, Miriam suddenly became leprous, white as snow. Aaron turned and saw her leprosy. 📖

## Numbers 12:11
He told Moses, “My lord, please do not hold this foolish sin against us. 📖

## Numbers 12:12
Do not let her be like a stillborn infant, half its flesh consumed when it leaves its mother’s womb.” 📖

## Numbers 12:13
Moses cried to the LORD, “God, please heal her!” 🙏

## Numbers 12:14
The LORD answered, “If her father spat in her face, would she not suffer disgrace for seven days? Confine her outside camp for seven days. Then bring her back.” 📖

## Numbers 12:15
Miriam stayed outside camp for seven days. The people did not travel until she returned. 📖

## Numbers 12:16
Then they left Hazeroth and camped in the Wilderness of Paran. 📖
