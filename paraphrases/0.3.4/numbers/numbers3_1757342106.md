## Numbers 3:1
This is the account of Aaron and Moses when the LORD spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai. 📖

## Numbers 3:2
Aaron’s sons were Nadab, the firstborn, then Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 📖

## Numbers 3:3
These sons were anointed and appointed to serve as priests. 📖

## Numbers 3:4
Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD after offering unauthorized fire in the Wilderness of Sinai. They had no sons. Only Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests while their father Aaron lived. 📖

## Numbers 3:5
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 3:6
“Bring Levi’s tribe before Aaron the priest to help him. 📖

## Numbers 3:7
They must perform duties for him and the whole congregation before the Tent of Meeting. They must serve at the tabernacle. 📖

## Numbers 3:8
They must care for all the Tent of Meeting’s furnishings. They must fulfill their obligations for Israel by serving at the tabernacle. 📖

## Numbers 3:9
Assign the Levites to Aaron and his sons. From among Israel, they are given exclusively to him. 📖

## Numbers 3:10
Appoint Aaron and his sons to perform priestly duties. Put any outsider who approaches the tabernacle to death.” 📖

## Numbers 3:11
The LORD spoke to Moses again. 📖

## Numbers 3:12
“I have taken the Levites from Israel in place of every firstborn Israelite. The Levites belong to Me. 📖

## Numbers 3:13
All firstborn belong to Me. When I killed Egypt’s firstborn, I set apart Israel’s firstborn people and animals for Myself. They are Mine. I am the LORD.” 📖

## Numbers 3:14
The LORD told Moses in the Wilderness of Sinai, 📖

## Numbers 3:15
“Count the Levites by families and clans. Count every male aged one month or older.” 📖

## Numbers 3:16
Moses counted them according to the LORD’s word, as commanded. 📖

## Numbers 3:17
Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## Numbers 3:18
Gershon’s sons, by clans, were Libni and Shimei. 📖

## Numbers 3:19
Kohath’s sons, by clans, were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 📖

## Numbers 3:20
Merari’s sons, by clans, were Mahli and Mushi. These were Levi’s clans by families. 📖

## Numbers 3:21
The Libnite and Shimeite clans came from Gershon. They were the Gershonite clans. 📖

## Numbers 3:22
Their males aged one month or older totaled 7,500. 📖

## Numbers 3:23
The Gershonite clans had to camp west of the tabernacle, behind it. 📖

## Numbers 3:24
Their family leader was Eliasaph son of Lael. 📖

## Numbers 3:25
At the Tent of Meeting, they cared for the tabernacle and tent, its covering, and the entrance curtain. 📖

## Numbers 3:26
They also cared for the courtyard curtains and the courtyard entrance curtain around the tabernacle and altar. They were responsible for the cords and all service for these items. 📖

## Numbers 3:27
The Amramite, Izharite, Hebronite, and Uzzielite clans came from Kohath. They were the Kohathite clans. 📖

## Numbers 3:28
Their males aged one month or older totaled 8,600. They were responsible for sanctuary duties. 📖

## Numbers 3:29
The Kohathite clans had to camp south of the tabernacle. 📖

## Numbers 3:30
Their family leader was Elizaphan son of Uzziel. 📖

## Numbers 3:31
They cared for the ark, table, lampstand, altars, sanctuary articles used with them, and curtain. They performed all service for these items. 📖

## Numbers 3:32
Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, was chief over the Levite leaders. He supervised those responsible for sanctuary duties. 📖

## Numbers 3:33
The Mahlite and Mushite clans came from Merari. They were the Merarite clans. 📖

## Numbers 3:34
Their males aged one month or older totaled 6,200. 📖

## Numbers 3:35
Their family leader was Zuriel son of Abihail. They had to camp north of the tabernacle. 📖

## Numbers 3:36
Merari’s descendants cared for the tabernacle’s frames, crossbars, posts, bases, and all its equipment. They performed all service for these items. 📖

## Numbers 3:37
They also cared for the surrounding courtyard’s posts, bases, tent pegs, and ropes. 📖

## Numbers 3:38
Moses, Aaron, and Aaron’s sons had to camp east of the tabernacle, toward sunrise, before the Tent of Meeting. They performed sanctuary duties for Israel. Put any outsider who approached the sanctuary to death. 📖

## Numbers 3:39
Moses and Aaron counted the Levites by clans at the LORD’s command. All males aged one month or older totaled 22,000. 📖

## Numbers 3:40
The LORD told Moses, “Count every firstborn Israelite male aged one month or older. List their names. 📖

## Numbers 3:41
Take the Levites for Me instead of Israel’s firstborn. Take their livestock instead of Israel’s firstborn livestock. I am the LORD.” 📖

## Numbers 3:42
Moses counted all Israel’s firstborn, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Numbers 3:43
The firstborn males aged one month or older, listed by name, totaled 22,273. 📖

## Numbers 3:44
The LORD spoke to Moses again. 📖

## Numbers 3:45
“Take the Levites instead of Israel’s firstborn, and their livestock instead of Israel’s livestock. The Levites belong to Me. I am the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 3:46
There are 273 more firstborn Israelites than Levites. To redeem these, 📖

## Numbers 3:47
collect five shekels per person, using the sanctuary shekel. Each shekel equals twenty gerahs. 📖

## Numbers 3:48
Give the money to Aaron and his sons as the redemption price for the extra Israelites.” 📖

## Numbers 3:49
Moses collected redemption money from those beyond the number redeemed by the Levites. 📖

## Numbers 3:50
He collected 1,365 shekels from Israel’s firstborn, using the sanctuary shekel. 📖

## Numbers 3:51
He gave the money to Aaron and his sons, obeying the LORD’s word exactly as commanded. 📖
