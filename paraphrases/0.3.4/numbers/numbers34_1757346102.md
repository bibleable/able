## Numbers 34:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 34:2
“Command Israel: when you enter Canaan, you will receive it as an inheritance within these boundaries. 📖

## Numbers 34:3
Your southern border will extend from the Wilderness of Zin along Edom’s border. On the east, it begins at the Salt Sea’s end. 📖

## Numbers 34:4
It crosses south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, continues to Zin, and passes south of Kadesh-barnea. It continues to Hazar-addar and Azmon. 📖

## Numbers 34:5
It turns from Azmon, joins the Brook of Egypt, and ends at the Sea. 📖

## Numbers 34:6
Your western boundary is the Great Sea’s coast. 📖

## Numbers 34:7
Your northern border runs directly from the Great Sea to Mount Hor. 📖

## Numbers 34:8
It continues from Mount Hor to Lebo-hamath, then to Zedad. 📖

## Numbers 34:9
It continues to Ziphron and ends at Hazar-enan. This is your northern boundary. 📖

## Numbers 34:10
Your eastern border runs directly from Hazar-enan to Shepham. 📖

## Numbers 34:11
It descends from Shepham to Riblah, east of Ain. It continues along the slopes east of the Sea of Chinnereth. 📖

## Numbers 34:12
It descends along the Jordan and ends at the Salt Sea. These are your land’s boundaries on every side.” 📖

## Numbers 34:13
Moses commanded Israel, “Divide this land by lot as an inheritance. The LORD assigned it to the nine and a half tribes. 📖

## Numbers 34:14
Reuben’s and Gad’s tribes, with Manasseh’s half-tribe, already received their inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 34:15
These two and a half tribes received land across the Jordan from Jericho, toward sunrise.” 📖

## Numbers 34:16
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 34:17
“Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun must assign your land as an inheritance. 📖

## Numbers 34:18
Appoint one leader from each tribe to distribute it. 📖

## Numbers 34:19
Their names are: from Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh, 📖

## Numbers 34:20
from Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud, 📖

## Numbers 34:21
from Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon, 📖

## Numbers 34:22
from Dan, the leader Bukki son of Jogli, 📖

## Numbers 34:23
from Manasseh son of Joseph, the leader Hanniel son of Ephod, 📖

## Numbers 34:24
from Ephraim, the leader Kemuel son of Shiphtan, 📖

## Numbers 34:25
from Zebulun, the leader Eli-zaphan son of Parnach, 📖

## Numbers 34:26
from Issachar, the leader Paltiel son of Azzan, 📖

## Numbers 34:27
from Asher, the leader Ahihud son of Shelomi, 📖

## Numbers 34:28
and from Naphtali, the leader Pedahel son of Ammihud.” 📖

## Numbers 34:29
The LORD commanded these men to divide Israel’s inheritance in Canaan. 📖
