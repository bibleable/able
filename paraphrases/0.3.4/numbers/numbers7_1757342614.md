## Numbers 7:1
On the day Moses finished setting up the tabernacle, he anointed and dedicated it, all its furnishings, the altar, and its utensils. 📖

## Numbers 7:2
Israel’s leaders, the heads of their families, brought an offering. These tribal leaders had supervised the registration. 📖

## Numbers 7:3
They brought six covered carts and twelve oxen before the LORD at the tabernacle. Each leader gave one ox, and each pair of leaders gave one cart. 📖

## Numbers 7:4
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 7:5
“Accept these gifts for work at the Tent of Meeting. Give them to the Levites according to each man’s service.” 📖

## Numbers 7:6
Moses took the carts and oxen and gave them to the Levites. 📖

## Numbers 7:7
He gave the Gershonites two carts and four oxen, as their service required. 📖

## Numbers 7:8
He gave the Merarites four carts and eight oxen, as their service required. Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest, directed them. 📖

## Numbers 7:9
He gave none to the Kohathites. They had to carry the holy objects on their shoulders. 📖

## Numbers 7:10
When the altar was anointed, the leaders brought their offerings for its dedication. They presented them before the altar. 📖

## Numbers 7:11
The LORD told Moses, “Each day, one leader must present his offering for the altar’s dedication.” 📖

## Numbers 7:12
On the first day, Nahshon son of Amminadab, from Judah’s tribe, brought his offering. 📖

## Numbers 7:13
His offering included one silver platter weighing 130 shekels and one silver bowl weighing seventy shekels. Both used the sanctuary shekel. Both held fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering. 📖

## Numbers 7:14
He brought one gold dish weighing ten shekels, filled with incense. 📖

## Numbers 7:15
For a burnt offering, he brought one young bull, one ram, and one male lamb aged one year. 📖

## Numbers 7:16
He brought one male goat as a sin offering. 📖

## Numbers 7:17
For a peace offering, he brought two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five male lambs aged one year. This was Nahshon son of Amminadab’s offering. 📖

## Numbers 7:84
Israel’s leaders brought these offerings to dedicate the altar when it was anointed: twelve silver platters, twelve silver bowls, and twelve gold dishes. 📖

## Numbers 7:85
Each silver platter weighed 130 shekels, and each silver bowl weighed seventy shekels. The silver articles totaled 2,400 sanctuary shekels. 📖

## Numbers 7:86
The twelve gold dishes filled with incense each weighed ten sanctuary shekels. Their total weight was 120 shekels. 📖

## Numbers 7:87
The burnt offerings totaled twelve bulls, twelve rams, and twelve male lambs aged one year, with their grain offerings. There were twelve male goats for sin offerings. 📖

## Numbers 7:88
The peace offerings totaled twenty-four bulls, sixty rams, sixty male goats, and sixty male lambs aged one year. This was the altar’s dedication offering after its anointing. 📖

## Numbers 7:89
Moses entered the Tent of Meeting to speak with the LORD. He heard the voice from between the two cherubim above the mercy seat on the ark of the Testimony. Thus the LORD spoke to him. 📖
