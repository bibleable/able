## Numbers 10:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 10:2
“Make two trumpets from hammered silver. Use them to call the congregation and signal the camps to leave. 📖

## Numbers 10:3
When both sound, the whole congregation must gather before you at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Numbers 10:4
When only one sounds, Israel’s clan leaders must gather before you. 📖

## Numbers 10:5
Sound short blasts to signal the eastern camps to leave. 📖

## Numbers 10:6
Sound short blasts a second time to signal the southern camps to leave. These blasts signal departure. 📖

## Numbers 10:7
Use long blasts to gather the assembly, rather than short blasts. 📖

## Numbers 10:8
Aaron’s sons, the priests, must sound the trumpets. This is a permanent statute for you and future generations. 📖

## Numbers 10:9
When an enemy attacks and you fight in your land, sound short trumpet blasts. The LORD your God will remember you and save you from your enemies. 📖

## Numbers 10:10
Blow the trumpets over burnt and peace offerings on joyful occasions, appointed feasts, and at each month’s beginning. They will serve as a reminder for you before your God. I am the LORD your God.” 📖

## Numbers 10:11
On the twentieth day of the second month in the second year, the cloud rose above the tabernacle of the Testimony. 📖

## Numbers 10:12
Israel left the Wilderness of Sinai. They traveled from place to place until the cloud settled in the Wilderness of Paran. 📖

## Numbers 10:13
This first departure followed the LORD’s command through Moses. 📖

## Numbers 10:14
Judah’s camp divisions left first under their standard, led by Nahshon son of Amminadab. 📖

## Numbers 10:15
Nethanel son of Zuar led Issachar’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:16
Eliab son of Helon led Zebulun’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:17
The tabernacle was taken down. The Gershonites and Merarites left, carrying it. 📖

## Numbers 10:18
Reuben’s camp divisions left next under their standard, led by Elizur son of Shedeur. 📖

## Numbers 10:19
Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai led Simeon’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:20
Eliasaph son of Deuel led Gad’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:21
The Kohathites left next, carrying the holy objects. The tabernacle had to be set up before they arrived. 📖

## Numbers 10:22
Ephraim’s camp divisions left next under their standard, led by Elishama son of Ammihud. 📖

## Numbers 10:23
Gamaliel son of Pedahzur led Manasseh’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:24
Abidan son of Gideoni led Benjamin’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:25
Dan’s camp divisions left last under their standard, led by Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai. They guarded the rear of all units. 📖

## Numbers 10:26
Pagiel son of Ocran led Asher’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:27
Ahira son of Enan led Naphtali’s tribal division. 📖

## Numbers 10:28
This was the marching order of Israel’s divisions when they traveled. 📖

## Numbers 10:29
Moses spoke to Hobab, whose father was Reuel the Midianite. Reuel was Moses’ father-in-law. “We travel to the place the LORD promised us. Come with us, and we will treat you well. The LORD promised good things to Israel.” 📖

## Numbers 10:30
Hobab replied, “I will not go. I will return to my own land and people.” 📖

## Numbers 10:31
Moses said, “Please stay. You know where we should camp in the wilderness. You can be our eyes. 📖

## Numbers 10:32
If you come, we will share whatever good things the LORD gives us.” 📖

## Numbers 10:33
They traveled for three days from the LORD’s mountain. His covenant ark went ahead those three days to find a resting place for them. 📖

## Numbers 10:34
The LORD’s cloud was over them by day when they left camp. 📖

## Numbers 10:35
Whenever the ark left, Moses said, “Rise, LORD! Scatter Your enemies. Let those who hate You flee before You.” 🙏

## Numbers 10:36
When it rested, he said, “Return, LORD, to Israel’s countless thousands.” 🙏
