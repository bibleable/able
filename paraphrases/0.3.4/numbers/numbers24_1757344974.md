## Numbers 24:1
Balaam saw that blessing Israel pleased the LORD. Unlike before, he sought no omens. He turned toward the wilderness. 📖

## Numbers 24:2
He looked up and saw Israel camped by tribes. God’s Spirit came on him. 📖

## Numbers 24:3
He proclaimed this oracle: “This is Balaam son of Beor’s prophecy, the prophecy of a man with open eyes. 📖

## Numbers 24:4
He hears God’s words and sees a vision from the Almighty. He bows with eyes wide open. 📖

## Numbers 24:5
Jacob, how lovely are your tents! Israel, how lovely are your homes! 📖

## Numbers 24:6
They spread like palm groves, gardens beside a stream, aloes the LORD planted, and cedars beside water. 📖

## Numbers 24:7
Water will flow from his buckets, and his seed will have abundant water. His king will be greater than Agag. His kingdom will be raised high. 📖

## Numbers 24:8
God brought him from Egypt with strength like a wild ox. Israel will devour hostile nations, crush their bones, and pierce them with arrows. 📖

## Numbers 24:9
He crouches and lies like a lion or lioness. Who dares wake him? Blessed are those who bless you. Cursed are those who curse you.” 📖

## Numbers 24:10
Balak became angry and struck his hands together. “I called you to curse my enemies! You have kept blessing them these three times. 📖

## Numbers 24:11
Flee home now! I promised you a rich reward, but the LORD denied it to you.” 📖

## Numbers 24:12
Balaam answered, “Did I not tell your messengers this? 📖

## Numbers 24:13
Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not exceed the LORD’s command. I could do nothing good or bad of my own will. I must say whatever He says. 📖

## Numbers 24:14
I will return to my people. But come, let me warn you what these people will do to yours in later days.” 📖

## Numbers 24:15
He proclaimed this oracle: “This is Balaam son of Beor’s prophecy, the prophecy of a man with open eyes. 📖

## Numbers 24:16
He hears God’s words, receives knowledge from the Most High, and sees a vision from the Almighty. He bows with eyes wide open. 📖

## Numbers 24:17
I see him, but not now or nearby. A star will come from Jacob, and a scepter will rise from Israel. He will crush Moab’s skulls and strike all Sheth’s sons. 📖

## Numbers 24:18
Edom and Seir, his enemy, will become possessions. Israel will act with courage. 📖

## Numbers 24:19
A ruler will come from Jacob. He will destroy the city’s survivors.” 📖

## Numbers 24:20
Balaam saw Amalek and proclaimed, “Amalek was first among nations, but destruction will be his end.” 📖

## Numbers 24:21
He saw the Kenites and proclaimed, “Your home is secure. Your nest stands in a cliff. 📖

## Numbers 24:22
Yet Kain will be destroyed when Asshur takes you captive.” 📖

## Numbers 24:23
He proclaimed another oracle: “Who can live unless God ordains it? 📖

## Numbers 24:24
Ships will come from Cyprus’s coasts. They will subdue Asshur and Eber, but they too will perish forever.” 📖

## Numbers 24:25
Balaam rose and returned home. Balak also went his way. 📖
