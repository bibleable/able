## Numbers 30:1
Moses told Israel’s tribal heads, “This is the LORD’s command. 📖

## Numbers 30:2
If a man vows to the LORD or swears an oath binding himself to a pledge, he must keep his word. He must do everything promised. 📖

## Numbers 30:3
A young woman living in her father’s house may vow to the LORD or bind herself with a pledge. 📖

## Numbers 30:4
If her father hears and says nothing, all her vows and pledges remain binding. 📖

## Numbers 30:5
If he forbids them on the day he hears, none remain binding. The LORD will release her because her father forbade them. 📖

## Numbers 30:6
A woman may marry while bound by a vow or rash promise. 📖

## Numbers 30:7
If her husband hears and says nothing that day, her vows and pledges remain binding. 📖

## Numbers 30:8
If he forbids them when he hears, he cancels her binding vow or rash promise. The LORD will release her. 📖

## Numbers 30:9
Every vow or pledge a widow or divorced woman makes remains binding on her. 📖

## Numbers 30:10
A woman living in her husband’s house may make a vow or binding oath. 📖

## Numbers 30:11
If he hears and says nothing, without forbidding it, all her vows and pledges remain binding. 📖

## Numbers 30:12
If he cancels them on the day he hears, none of her spoken vows or pledges remain binding. Her husband canceled them, and the LORD will release her. 📖

## Numbers 30:13
Her husband may confirm or cancel any vow or sworn pledge to deny herself something. 📖

## Numbers 30:14
If he says nothing from day to day, he confirms every binding vow and pledge. He confirmed them by remaining silent on the day he heard. 📖

## Numbers 30:15
But if he later cancels them after hearing, he must bear her guilt.” 📖

## Numbers 30:16
The LORD gave Moses these statutes about a man and his wife, and a father and his young daughter still at home. 📖
