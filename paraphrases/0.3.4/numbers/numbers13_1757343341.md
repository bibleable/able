## Numbers 13:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 13:2
“Send men to explore Canaan, the land I give Israel. Send one leader from each ancestral tribe.” 📖

## Numbers 13:3
At the LORD’s command, Moses sent them from the Wilderness of Paran. All were leaders of Israel. 📖

## Numbers 13:4
Their names were: from Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur, 📖

## Numbers 13:5
from Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori, 📖

## Numbers 13:6
from Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh, 📖

## Numbers 13:7
from Issachar, Igal son of Joseph, 📖

## Numbers 13:8
from Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun, 📖

## Numbers 13:9
from Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu, 📖

## Numbers 13:10
from Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi, 📖

## Numbers 13:11
from Manasseh, a tribe of Joseph, Gaddi son of Susi, 📖

## Numbers 13:12
from Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli, 📖

## Numbers 13:13
from Asher, Sethur son of Michael, 📖

## Numbers 13:14
from Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi, 📖

## Numbers 13:15
and from Gad, Geuel son of Machi. 📖

## Numbers 13:16
Moses sent these men to explore the land. He gave Hoshea son of Nun the name Joshua. 📖

## Numbers 13:17
He sent them to explore Canaan, saying, “Go through the Negev into the hill country. 📖

## Numbers 13:18
Examine the land. See whether the people are strong or weak, few or many. 📖

## Numbers 13:19
Is their land good or bad? Are their towns open camps or fortified places? 📖

## Numbers 13:20
Is the soil fertile or unproductive? Are there trees? Be courageous and bring some fruit back.” It was the season of the first ripe grapes. 📖

## Numbers 13:21
They explored from the Wilderness of Zin to Rehob, toward Lebo-hamath. 📖

## Numbers 13:22
They passed through the Negev to Hebron. Anak’s descendants Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai lived there. Hebron was built seven years before Zoan in Egypt. 📖

## Numbers 13:23
In the Valley of Eshcol, they cut a branch with one grape cluster. Two men carried it on a pole between them. They also took pomegranates and figs. 📖

## Numbers 13:24
The place was named the Valley of Eshcol because of the grape cluster Israel cut there. 📖

## Numbers 13:25
After forty days exploring the land, the men returned. 📖

## Numbers 13:26
They returned to Moses, Aaron, and Israel’s whole congregation at Kadesh in the Wilderness of Paran. They reported to everyone and showed the land’s fruit. 📖

## Numbers 13:27
They told Moses, “We entered the land you sent us to explore. It flows with milk and honey. Here is its fruit! 📖

## Numbers 13:28
But its people are strong, and its towns are large and fortified. We even saw Anak’s descendants there. 📖

## Numbers 13:29
Amalekites live in the Negev. Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites live in the hills. Canaanites live beside the sea and along the Jordan.” 📖

## Numbers 13:30
Caleb quieted the people before Moses. He said, “We must go and possess the land. We can certainly conquer it!” 📖

## Numbers 13:31
The other explorers replied, “We cannot fight those people. They are stronger than us!” 📖

## Numbers 13:32
They gave Israel a bad report about the explored land. “It devours its inhabitants. Everyone we saw was very tall. 📖

## Numbers 13:33
We even saw Nephilim, Anak’s descendants who come from the Nephilim! We felt like grasshoppers. We must have looked the same to them!” 📖
