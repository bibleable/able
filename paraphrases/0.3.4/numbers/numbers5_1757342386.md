## Numbers 5:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 5:2
“Command Israel to send these people out of camp: anyone with skin disease, bodily discharge, or uncleanness from a dead body. 📖

## Numbers 5:3
Send males and females alike outside. They must not defile the camp where I live among them.” 📖

## Numbers 5:4
Israel sent these people outside the camp. They did exactly what the LORD instructed Moses. 📖

## Numbers 5:5
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 5:6
“Tell Israel: a man or woman who sins against another person acts unfaithfully against the LORD. That person is guilty. 📖

## Numbers 5:7
The offender must confess the sin and fully repay the loss. Add one fifth of its value and give everything to the wronged person. 📖

## Numbers 5:8
If the wronged person has no relative to receive repayment, it belongs to the LORD. Give it to the priest with the ram that makes atonement for the offender. 📖

## Numbers 5:9
Every sacred contribution Israel brings to the priest belongs to him. 📖

## Numbers 5:10
Each person’s sacred gifts are his own. But whatever he gives the priest belongs to the priest.” 📖

## Numbers 5:11
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 5:12
“Tell Israel: a man’s wife may stray and be unfaithful to him. 📖

## Numbers 5:13
She may sleep with another man without her husband knowing. Her impurity may remain undetected, with no witness and without her being caught. 📖

## Numbers 5:14
Her husband may become jealous and suspect her, whether she has defiled herself or remained undefiled. 📖

## Numbers 5:15
Then he must bring her to the priest with one tenth of an ephah of barley flour as her offering. Put no oil or frankincense on it. It is a grain offering for jealousy, a memorial offering that recalls sin. 📖

## Numbers 5:16
The priest must bring her forward to stand before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 5:17
He must put holy water in a clay jar. Add dust from the tabernacle floor. 📖

## Numbers 5:18
After placing her before the LORD, he must let down her hair. Put the memorial grain offering for jealousy in her hands. The priest must hold the bitter water that brings a curse. 📖

## Numbers 5:19
He must put her under oath and say, ‘Suppose no other man has slept with you. Suppose you have not strayed or become defiled under your husband’s authority. Then may this bitter water that brings a curse leave you unharmed. 📖

## Numbers 5:20
But suppose you have strayed under your husband’s authority and defiled yourself by sleeping with another man. 📖

## Numbers 5:21
Then this curse applies.’ The priest must place her under the curse’s oath, saying, ‘May the LORD make you a known curse among your people. May He make your thigh shrivel and your belly swell. 📖

## Numbers 5:22
May this water that brings a curse enter your stomach, swell your belly, and shrivel your thigh.’ She must say, ‘Amen, Amen.’ 📖

## Numbers 5:23
The priest must write the curses on a scroll. Wash them off into the bitter water. 📖

## Numbers 5:24
He must make her drink the bitter water that brings a curse. It will enter her and may cause bitter suffering. 📖

## Numbers 5:25
He must take the grain offering for jealousy from her hand. Wave it before the LORD and bring it to the altar. 📖

## Numbers 5:26
He must burn a handful on the altar as its memorial portion. Afterward, he must make her drink the water. 📖

## Numbers 5:27
If she has defiled herself and been unfaithful to her husband, the water will enter her and cause bitter suffering. Her belly will swell and her thigh shrivel. She will become a curse among her people. 📖

## Numbers 5:28
But if she has not defiled herself and is clean, she will remain unharmed and able to conceive children. 📖

## Numbers 5:29
This is the law of jealousy when a wife strays and defiles herself under her husband’s authority, 📖

## Numbers 5:30
or when her husband becomes jealous and suspects her. He must place her before the LORD. The priest must apply this whole law to her. 📖

## Numbers 5:31
The husband will be free of guilt, but the woman must bear her sin.” 📖
