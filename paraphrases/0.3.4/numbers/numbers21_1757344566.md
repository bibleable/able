## Numbers 21:1
Arad’s Canaanite king lived in the Negev. He heard Israel was coming along the Atharim road. He attacked Israel and took prisoners. 📖

## Numbers 21:2
Israel vowed to the LORD, “If You give these people into our hands, we will set their towns apart for destruction.” 📖

## Numbers 21:3
The LORD heard and gave them the Canaanites. Israel destroyed them and their towns. They named the place Hormah. 📖

## Numbers 21:4
They left Mount Hor on the Red Sea route to avoid Edom. They grew impatient on the journey. 📖

## Numbers 21:5
They criticized God and Moses. “Why bring us from Egypt to die in the wilderness? There is no bread or water. We hate this miserable food!” 📖

## Numbers 21:6
The LORD sent venomous snakes among them. Many Israelites were bitten and died. 📖

## Numbers 21:7
They told Moses, “We sinned by speaking against the LORD and you. Pray that He takes the snakes away.” Moses prayed for them. 📖

## Numbers 21:8
The LORD told Moses, “Make a fiery snake and put it on a pole. Anyone bitten who looks at it will live.” 📖

## Numbers 21:9
Moses made a bronze snake and put it on a pole. Anyone bitten who looked at it lived. 📖

## Numbers 21:10
Israel traveled and camped at Oboth. 📖

## Numbers 21:11
They left Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim, in the wilderness opposite Moab on the east. 📖

## Numbers 21:12
They left there and camped in the Valley of Zered. 📖

## Numbers 21:13
They moved and camped across the Arnon, in the wilderness extending into Amorite territory. The Arnon separates Moabite and Amorite land. 📖

## Numbers 21:14
Thus the Book of the Wars of the LORD says, “Waheb in Suphah and the Arnon’s wadis, 📖

## Numbers 21:15
the wadis’ slopes extending to Ar and lying along Moab’s border.” 📖

## Numbers 21:16
They went to Beer, the well where the LORD told Moses, “Gather the people, so I can give them water.” 📖

## Numbers 21:17
Israel sang, “Spring up, well! Everyone, sing to it! 📖

## Numbers 21:18
Princes dug the well. The people’s nobles hollowed it with their scepters and staffs.” From the wilderness, Israel went to Mattanah. 📖

## Numbers 21:19
From Mattanah they went to Nahaliel, then to Bamoth. 📖

## Numbers 21:20
From Bamoth they went to Moab’s valley, where Pisgah’s summit overlooks the wasteland. 📖

## Numbers 21:21
Israel sent messengers to Sihon, the Amorites’ king. 📖

## Numbers 21:22
“Let us cross your land. We will avoid fields and vineyards and drink no well water. We will follow the King’s Highway until we cross your territory.” 📖

## Numbers 21:23
Sihon refused passage. He gathered his whole army and confronted Israel in the wilderness. He reached Jahaz and fought them. 📖

## Numbers 21:24
Israel killed him with the sword. They took his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, stopping at Ammon’s border because it was fortified. 📖

## Numbers 21:25
They captured and occupied all the Amorite towns, including Heshbon and its villages. 📖

## Numbers 21:26
Heshbon was Sihon the Amorite king’s town. He fought Moab’s former king and took all his land to the Arnon. 📖

## Numbers 21:27
Therefore, poets say, “Come to Heshbon! Rebuild and restore Sihon’s town. 📖

## Numbers 21:28
Fire came from Heshbon, a blaze from Sihon’s town. It consumed Ar of Moab and the rulers of Arnon’s heights. 📖

## Numbers 21:29
Woe to you, Moab! Chemosh’s people, you are destroyed! He gave his sons as refugees and his daughters as captives to Sihon, the Amorites’ king. 📖

## Numbers 21:30
We overthrew them. Heshbon is destroyed to Dibon. We demolished them to Nophah, which reaches Medeba.” 📖

## Numbers 21:31
Israel lived in Amorite land. 📖

## Numbers 21:32
Moses sent spies to Jazer. Israel captured its villages and drove out the Amorites there. 📖

## Numbers 21:33
They turned onto Bashan’s road. Og, Bashan’s king, and his whole army met them in battle at Edrei. 📖

## Numbers 21:34
The LORD told Moses, “Do not fear him. I gave him, his people, and his land into your hands. Treat him like Sihon, the Amorites’ king in Heshbon.” 📖

## Numbers 21:35
They killed Og, his sons, and his whole army. No survivor remained. They possessed his land. 📖
