## Numbers 11:1
The people complained about their hardships within the LORD’s hearing. He heard and became angry. His fire burned among them and consumed the camp’s outskirts. 📖

## Numbers 11:2
They cried to Moses. He prayed to the LORD, and the fire died down. 📖

## Numbers 11:3
They named the place Taberah because the LORD’s fire burned among them. 📖

## Numbers 11:4
The rabble among them strongly craved other food. Israel wept again, saying, “Who will give us meat? 📖

## Numbers 11:5
We remember Egypt’s fish, which we ate freely, and cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic. 📖

## Numbers 11:6
Now we have no appetite. We see nothing but manna!” 📖

## Numbers 11:7
Manna resembled coriander seed and looked like gum resin. 📖

## Numbers 11:8
People walked around gathering it. They ground it with a handmill or crushed it in a mortar, then boiled it or formed cakes. It tasted like pastry baked with fine oil. 📖

## Numbers 11:9
Manna fell with the dew on the camp at night. 📖

## Numbers 11:10
Moses heard family after family weeping at their tent entrances. The LORD was very angry, and Moses was displeased. 📖

## Numbers 11:11
He asked the LORD, “Why bring this trouble on Your servant? Why have I not found Your favor? Why put the burden of all these people on me? 📖

## Numbers 11:12
Did I conceive or give birth to all these people? Why tell me, ‘Carry them at your breast like a nurse carries an infant’? Why must I carry them to the land You swore to their fathers? 📖

## Numbers 11:13
Where can I get meat for everyone? They keep crying, ‘Give us meat!’ 📖

## Numbers 11:14
I cannot carry all these people alone. The burden is too heavy. 📖

## Numbers 11:15
If You will treat me this way, please kill me now, if I have Your favor. Do not let me see my own misery.” 📖

## Numbers 11:16
The LORD told Moses, “Bring seventy Israelite elders whom you know as leaders and officers. Have them stand with you at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 11:17
I will descend and speak with you there. I will take some of the Spirit on you and place that Spirit on them. They will help carry the people’s burden, so you need not carry it alone. 📖

## Numbers 11:18
Tell the people, ‘Dedicate yourselves for tomorrow, when you will eat meat. The LORD heard you crying, “Who will give us meat? We were better off in Egypt!” Therefore, He will give you meat to eat. 📖

## Numbers 11:19
You will eat it for more than one or two days. It will be more than five, ten, or twenty days. 📖

## Numbers 11:20
You will eat it for a whole month, until it comes from your nostrils and makes you nauseous. You rejected the LORD who is among you. You cried before Him, “Why did we leave Egypt?”’” 📖

## Numbers 11:21
Moses replied, “I am among 600,000 men on foot. Yet You promise them meat to eat for a month. 📖

## Numbers 11:22
Would killing all our flocks and herds provide enough? Would catching all the sea’s fish provide enough?” 📖

## Numbers 11:23
The LORD answered, “Is My arm too short? You will see whether My word comes true.” 📖

## Numbers 11:24
Moses told the people the LORD’s words. He gathered seventy elders and placed them around the tent. 📖

## Numbers 11:25
The LORD descended in the cloud and spoke with Moses. He took some of the Spirit on Moses and placed that Spirit on the seventy elders. When the Spirit rested on them, they prophesied. They never did so again. 📖

## Numbers 11:26
Two men, Eldad and Medad, stayed in camp. They were listed but had not gone to the tent. The Spirit rested on them, and they prophesied in camp. 📖

## Numbers 11:27
A young man ran to tell Moses, “Eldad and Medad are prophesying in camp!” 📖

## Numbers 11:28
Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ attendant since youth, said, “Moses, my lord, stop them!” 📖

## Numbers 11:29
Moses replied, “Are you jealous for me? I wish all the LORD’s people were prophets and He would place His Spirit on them!” 📖

## Numbers 11:30
Moses and Israel’s elders returned to camp. 📖

## Numbers 11:31
The LORD sent a wind that brought quail from the sea near the camp. They were about two cubits above the ground, extending a day’s journey in every direction around camp. 📖

## Numbers 11:32
People gathered quail all that day, that night, and the next day. Everyone gathered at least ten homers. They spread them around the camp. 📖

## Numbers 11:33
Before they chewed the meat, while it remained between their teeth, the LORD’s anger burned against them. He struck them with a severe plague. 📖

## Numbers 11:34
They named the place Kibroth-hattaavah. There they buried those who craved other food. 📖

## Numbers 11:35
They moved from Kibroth-hattaavah to Hazeroth and stayed there for some time. 📖
