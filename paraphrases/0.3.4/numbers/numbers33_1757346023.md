## Numbers 33:1
These are Israel’s journeys out of Egypt, by divisions under Moses and Aaron’s leadership. 📖

## Numbers 33:2
At the LORD’s command, Moses recorded the journey’s stages. These are listed by their starting places. 📖

## Numbers 33:3
Israel left Rameses on the first month’s fifteenth day, the day after Passover. They marched defiantly before all the Egyptians. 📖

## Numbers 33:4
The Egyptians were burying all their firstborn, whom the LORD killed. He executed judgment against their gods. 📖

## Numbers 33:5
They left Rameses and camped at Succoth. 📖

## Numbers 33:6
They left Succoth and camped at Etham, at the wilderness edge. 📖

## Numbers 33:7
They left Etham and turned back to Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon. They camped near Migdol. 📖

## Numbers 33:8
They left Pi-hahiroth and crossed the sea into the wilderness. They traveled three days into the Wilderness of Etham and camped at Marah. 📖

## Numbers 33:9
They left Marah and camped at Elim. It had twelve springs and seventy palm trees. 📖

## Numbers 33:10
They left Elim and camped beside the Red Sea. 📖

## Numbers 33:11
They left the Red Sea and camped in the Desert of Sin. 📖

## Numbers 33:12
They left the Desert of Sin and camped at Dophkah. 📖

## Numbers 33:13
They left Dophkah and camped at Alush. 📖

## Numbers 33:14
They left Alush and camped at Rephidim, where there was no drinking water. 📖

## Numbers 33:15
They left Rephidim and camped in the Wilderness of Sinai. 📖

## Numbers 33:16
They left the Wilderness of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-hattaavah. 📖

## Numbers 33:17
They left Kibroth-hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth. 📖

## Numbers 33:18
They left Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah. 📖

## Numbers 33:19
They left Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez. 📖

## Numbers 33:20
They left Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah. 📖

## Numbers 33:21
They left Libnah and camped at Rissah. 📖

## Numbers 33:22
They left Rissah and camped at Kehelathah. 📖

## Numbers 33:23
They left Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shepher. 📖

## Numbers 33:24
They left Mount Shepher and camped at Haradah. 📖

## Numbers 33:25
They left Haradah and camped at Makheloth. 📖

## Numbers 33:26
They left Makheloth and camped at Tahath. 📖

## Numbers 33:27
They left Tahath and camped at Terah. 📖

## Numbers 33:28
They left Terah and camped at Mithkah. 📖

## Numbers 33:29
They left Mithkah and camped at Hashmonah. 📖

## Numbers 33:30
They left Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth. 📖

## Numbers 33:31
They left Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan. 📖

## Numbers 33:32
They left Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-haggidgad. 📖

## Numbers 33:33
They left Hor-haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah. 📖

## Numbers 33:34
They left Jotbathah and camped at Abronah. 📖

## Numbers 33:35
They left Abronah and camped at Ezion-geber. 📖

## Numbers 33:36
They left Ezion-geber and camped at Kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin. 📖

## Numbers 33:37
They left Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor, at Edom’s edge. 📖

## Numbers 33:38
At the LORD’s command, Aaron the priest climbed Mount Hor and died. This was the first day of the fifth month, in the fortieth year after Israel left Egypt. 📖

## Numbers 33:39
Aaron was 123 years old when he died on Mount Hor. 📖

## Numbers 33:40
Arad’s Canaanite king lived in the Negev in Canaan. He heard Israel was coming. 📖

## Numbers 33:41
They left Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah. 📖

## Numbers 33:42
They left Zalmonah and camped at Punon. 📖

## Numbers 33:43
They left Punon and camped at Oboth. 📖

## Numbers 33:44
They left Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim on Moab’s border. 📖

## Numbers 33:45
They left Iyim and camped at Dibon-gad. 📖

## Numbers 33:46
They left Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim. 📖

## Numbers 33:47
They left Almon-diblathaim and camped in the Abarim mountains facing Nebo. 📖

## Numbers 33:48
They left the Abarim mountains and camped on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, opposite Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 33:49
They camped along the Jordan on Moab’s plains, from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim. 📖

## Numbers 33:50
On Moab’s plains by the Jordan, opposite Jericho, the LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 33:51
“Tell Israel: when you cross the Jordan into Canaan, 📖

## Numbers 33:52
drive out all the land’s inhabitants. Destroy their carved images and cast idols, and demolish every high place. 📖

## Numbers 33:53
Possess the land and settle there. I gave it to you to possess. 📖

## Numbers 33:54
Divide it by lot according to your clans. Give large clans larger inheritances and small clans smaller ones. Each receives what falls to him by lot. Receive your inheritance according to your ancestral tribes. 📖

## Numbers 33:55
If you leave inhabitants in the land, they will become barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides. They will harass you where you settle. 📖

## Numbers 33:56
Then I will do to you what I planned to do to them.” 📖
