## Numbers 1:1
On the first day of the second month, in the second year after Israel left Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses. They were at the Tent of Meeting in the Wilderness of Sinai. He said, 📖

## Numbers 1:2
“Count Israel’s whole congregation by clans and families. List every man by name, one at a time. 📖

## Numbers 1:3
You and Aaron must count everyone aged twenty or older who can serve in Israel’s army. Count them by divisions. 📖

## Numbers 1:4
One man from each tribe must help you. Each must be the head of his family. 📖

## Numbers 1:5
These men must help: from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur, 📖

## Numbers 1:6
from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai, 📖

## Numbers 1:7
from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab, 📖

## Numbers 1:8
from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar, 📖

## Numbers 1:9
from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon, 📖

## Numbers 1:10
from Joseph’s sons: from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud, and from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur, 📖

## Numbers 1:11
from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni, 📖

## Numbers 1:12
from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai, 📖

## Numbers 1:13
from Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran, 📖

## Numbers 1:14
from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel, 📖

## Numbers 1:15
and from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.” 📖

## Numbers 1:16
These men were appointed from the congregation. They led their ancestral tribes and headed Israel’s clans. 📖

## Numbers 1:17
Moses and Aaron took these men who had been named. 📖

## Numbers 1:18
They gathered the whole congregation on the first day of the second month. They recorded ancestry by clans and families. They listed everyone aged twenty or older by name, one at a time. 📖

## Numbers 1:19
Moses counted them in the Wilderness of Sinai, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Numbers 1:20
Reuben was Israel’s firstborn. His descendants were recorded by clans and families. Every male aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name, one at a time. 📖

## Numbers 1:21
Reuben’s tribe had 46,500 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:22
Simeon’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Every male aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name, one at a time. 📖

## Numbers 1:23
Simeon’s tribe had 59,300 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:24
Gad’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:25
Gad’s tribe had 45,650 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:26
Judah’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:27
Judah’s tribe had 74,600 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:28
Issachar’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:29
Issachar’s tribe had 54,400 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:30
Zebulun’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:31
Zebulun’s tribe had 57,400 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:32
Among Joseph’s sons, Ephraim’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:33
Ephraim’s tribe had 40,500 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:34
Manasseh’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:35
Manasseh’s tribe had 32,200 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:36
Benjamin’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:37
Benjamin’s tribe had 35,400 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:38
Dan’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:39
Dan’s tribe had 62,700 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:40
Asher’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:41
Asher’s tribe had 41,500 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:42
Naphtali’s descendants were recorded by clans and families. Everyone aged twenty or older who could serve in the army was listed by name. 📖

## Numbers 1:43
Naphtali’s tribe had 53,400 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 1:44
Moses and Aaron counted these men with help from Israel’s twelve leaders. Each leader represented his family. 📖

## Numbers 1:45
All Israelites aged twenty or older who could serve in Israel’s army were counted by families. 📖

## Numbers 1:46
The total was 603,550. 📖

## Numbers 1:47
The Levites were not counted with them by their ancestral tribe. 📖

## Numbers 1:48
The LORD had told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 1:49
“Do not count Levi’s tribe with the other Israelites. 📖

## Numbers 1:50
Put the Levites in charge of the tabernacle of the Testimony, its furnishings, and everything in it. They must carry the tabernacle and its articles, care for it, and camp around it. 📖

## Numbers 1:51
When the tabernacle moves, the Levites must take it down. When it is pitched, they must set it up. Put any outsider who approaches it to death. 📖

## Numbers 1:52
Israel must camp by divisions. Each man must stay in his own camp under his own standard. 📖

## Numbers 1:53
The Levites must camp around the tabernacle of the Testimony and guard it. Then wrath will not fall on Israel’s congregation. They are responsible for the tabernacle of the Testimony.” 📖

## Numbers 1:54
Israel did everything exactly as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖
