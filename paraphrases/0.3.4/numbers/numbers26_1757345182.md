## Numbers 26:1
After the plague ended, the LORD told Moses and Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, 📖

## Numbers 26:2
“Count Israel’s whole congregation by ancestral families. Count everyone aged twenty or older who can serve in Israel’s army.” 📖

## Numbers 26:3
Moses and Eleazar the priest gave this instruction on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, opposite Jericho: 📖

## Numbers 26:4
“Count men aged twenty or older, as the LORD commanded Moses.” These were the Israelites who came from Egypt. 📖

## Numbers 26:5
Reuben was Israel’s firstborn. His descendants included the Hanochite clan from Hanoch and the Palluite clan from Pallu, 📖

## Numbers 26:6
the Hezronite clan from Hezron and the Carmite clan from Carmi. 📖

## Numbers 26:7
Reuben’s clans had 43,730 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:8
Pallu’s son was Eliab. 📖

## Numbers 26:9
Eliab’s sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram were chosen by the congregation. They joined Korah’s followers in rebelling against Moses and Aaron, and against the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 26:10
The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them with Korah. His followers died when fire consumed 250 men. They serve as a warning sign. 📖

## Numbers 26:11
But Korah’s family line did not die out. 📖

## Numbers 26:12
Simeon’s descendants included the Nemuelite clan from Nemuel, the Jaminite clan from Jamin, and the Jachinite clan from Jachin, 📖

## Numbers 26:13
the Zerahite clan from Zerah and the Shaulite clan from Shaul. 📖

## Numbers 26:14
Simeon’s clans had 22,200 men. 📖

## Numbers 26:15
Gad’s descendants included the Zephonite clan from Zephon, the Haggite clan from Haggi, and the Shunite clan from Shuni, 📖

## Numbers 26:16
the Oznite clan from Ozni and the Erite clan from Eri, 📖

## Numbers 26:17
the Arodite clan from Arod and the Arelite clan from Areli. 📖

## Numbers 26:18
Gad’s clans had 40,500 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:19
Judah’s sons Er and Onan died in Canaan. 📖

## Numbers 26:20
Judah’s descendants included the Shelanite clan from Shelah, the Perezite clan from Perez, and the Zerahite clan from Zerah. 📖

## Numbers 26:21
Perez’s descendants included the Hezronite clan from Hezron and the Hamulite clan from Hamul. 📖

## Numbers 26:22
Judah’s clans had 76,500 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:23
Issachar’s descendants included the Tolaite clan from Tola and the Punite clan from Puvah, 📖

## Numbers 26:24
the Jashubite clan from Jashub and the Shimronite clan from Shimron. 📖

## Numbers 26:25
Issachar’s clans had 64,300 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:26
Zebulun’s descendants included the Seredite clan from Sered, the Elonite clan from Elon, and the Jahleelite clan from Jahleel. 📖

## Numbers 26:27
Zebulun’s clans had 60,500 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:28
Joseph’s descendants included Manasseh’s and Ephraim’s clans. 📖

## Numbers 26:29
Manasseh’s descendants included the Machirite clan from Machir, Gilead’s father, and the Gileadite clan from Gilead. 📖

## Numbers 26:30
Gilead’s descendants included the Iezerite clan from Iezer and the Helekite clan from Helek, 📖

## Numbers 26:31
the Asrielite clan from Asriel and the Shechemite clan from Shechem, 📖

## Numbers 26:32
the Shemidaite clan from Shemida and the Hepherite clan from Hepher. 📖

## Numbers 26:33
Zelophehad son of Hepher had daughters but no sons. They were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 📖

## Numbers 26:34
Manasseh’s clans had 52,700 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:35
Ephraim’s descendants included the Shuthelahite clan from Shuthelah, the Becherite clan from Becher, and the Tahanite clan from Tahan. 📖

## Numbers 26:36
Shuthelah’s descendants included the Eranite clan from Eran. 📖

## Numbers 26:37
Ephraim’s clans had 32,500 registered men. These clans descended from Joseph. 📖

## Numbers 26:38
Benjamin’s descendants included the Belaite clan from Bela, the Ashbelite clan from Ashbel, and the Ahiramite clan from Ahiram, 📖

## Numbers 26:39
the Shuphamite clan from Shupham and the Huphamite clan from Hupham. 📖

## Numbers 26:40
Bela’s descendants through Ard and Naaman included the Ardite clan from Ard and the Naamite clan from Naaman. 📖

## Numbers 26:41
Benjamin’s clans had 45,600 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:42
Dan’s descendants included the Shuhamite clan from Shuham. These were Dan’s clans. 📖

## Numbers 26:43
All were Shuhamite clans. They had 64,400 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:44
Asher’s descendants included the Imnite clan from Imnah, the Ishvite clan from Ishvi, and the Beriite clan from Beriah. 📖

## Numbers 26:45
Beriah’s descendants included the Heberite clan from Heber and the Malchielite clan from Malchiel. 📖

## Numbers 26:46
Asher’s daughter was Serah. 📖

## Numbers 26:47
Asher’s clans had 53,400 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:48
Naphtali’s descendants included the Jahzeelite clan from Jahzeel and the Gunite clan from Guni, 📖

## Numbers 26:49
the Jezerite clan from Jezer and the Shillemite clan from Shillem. 📖

## Numbers 26:50
Naphtali’s clans had 45,400 registered men. 📖

## Numbers 26:51
Israel’s men totaled 601,730. 📖

## Numbers 26:52
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 26:53
“Divide the land among the tribes as an inheritance according to the number of registered names. 📖

## Numbers 26:54
Give large tribes more land and small tribes less. Each tribe’s inheritance must match its registered population. 📖

## Numbers 26:55
Divide the land by lot, according to their ancestral tribes’ names. 📖

## Numbers 26:56
Divide each inheritance by lot among larger and smaller tribes.” 📖

## Numbers 26:57
The Levites were counted by clans. These included the Gershonite clan from Gershon, the Kohathite clan from Kohath, and the Merarite clan from Merari. 📖

## Numbers 26:58
Levi’s families included the Libnite, Hebronite, Mahlite, Mushite, and Korahite clans. Kohath was Amram’s father. 📖

## Numbers 26:59
Amram’s wife Jochebed was Levi’s daughter, born to him in Egypt. She bore Amram’s children Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam. 📖

## Numbers 26:60
Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 📖

## Numbers 26:61
Nadab and Abihu died after offering unauthorized fire before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 26:62
The Levites totaled 23,000 males aged one month or older. They were not counted with the other Israelites because they received no inheritance among them. 📖

## Numbers 26:63
Moses and Eleazar the priest counted these Israelites on Moab’s plains by the Jordan, opposite Jericho. 📖

## Numbers 26:64
None had been counted by Moses and Aaron the priest in the Wilderness of Sinai. 📖

## Numbers 26:65
The LORD said they would die in the wilderness. None remained except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun. 📖
