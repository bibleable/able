## Numbers 15:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 15:2
“Tell Israel: after you enter the land I give you as a home, 📖

## Numbers 15:3
you may present food offerings to the LORD from herds or flocks, giving a pleasing aroma. These may be burnt offerings or sacrifices for special vows, freewill offerings, or appointed feasts. 📖

## Numbers 15:4
Then also present a grain offering of one tenth of an ephah of fine flour. Mix it with one quarter hin of olive oil. 📖

## Numbers 15:5
With each lamb’s burnt offering or sacrifice, prepare one quarter hin of wine as a drink offering. 📖

## Numbers 15:6
With a ram, prepare a grain offering of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour. Mix it with one third hin of olive oil. 📖

## Numbers 15:7
Also bring one third hin of wine as a drink offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:8
You may prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or sacrifice to fulfill a vow, or as a peace offering to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:9
Then present three tenths of an ephah of fine flour as its grain offering. Mix it with half a hin of olive oil. 📖

## Numbers 15:10
Present half a hin of wine as a drink offering. It is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:11
Do this for each bull, ram, lamb, or goat. 📖

## Numbers 15:12
Prepare each this way, however many you offer. 📖

## Numbers 15:13
Every native Israelite must prepare food offerings this way, as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:14
Throughout future generations, a foreign resident or anyone among you may prepare such an offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. They must do exactly as you do. 📖

## Numbers 15:15
The assembly must have one statute for you and foreign residents. It is permanent for future generations. You and foreigners are equal before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:16
The same law and ordinance apply to you and the foreigner living among you.” 📖

## Numbers 15:17
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 15:18
“Tell Israel: when you enter the land I bring you to, 📖

## Numbers 15:19
and eat its food, lift up an offering to the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 15:20
Lift up a cake from your first dough as a contribution. Offer it like an offering from the threshing floor. 📖

## Numbers 15:21
Throughout your generations, give the LORD an offering from your first dough. 📖

## Numbers 15:22
You may unintentionally stray and fail to obey these commands the LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

## Numbers 15:23
These include everything He commanded through Moses, from the day He gave the commands through future generations. 📖

## Numbers 15:24
If the congregation sins unintentionally without knowing, it must offer one young bull as a burnt offering. This gives the LORD a pleasing aroma. Include its grain and drink offerings according to the rule. Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. 📖

## Numbers 15:25
The priest must make atonement for Israel’s whole congregation, so they can be forgiven. Their sin was unintentional. They brought food and sin offerings before the LORD for that unintentional sin. 📖

## Numbers 15:26
The whole congregation and the foreigners among them will be forgiven. All the people sinned unintentionally. 📖

## Numbers 15:27
If one person sins unintentionally, present a female goat aged one year as a sin offering. 📖

## Numbers 15:28
The priest must make atonement before the LORD for that person’s unintentional sin. Then that person will be forgiven. 📖

## Numbers 15:29
Use the same law for anyone who sins unintentionally, whether a native Israelite or a foreign resident. 📖

## Numbers 15:30
Anyone who sins defiantly blasphemes the LORD, whether a native or foreigner. Cut that person off from the people. 📖

## Numbers 15:31
That person despised the LORD’s word and broke His command. The person must certainly be cut off and remains guilty.” 📖

## Numbers 15:32
While Israel was in the wilderness, a man was found gathering wood on the Sabbath. 📖

## Numbers 15:33
Those who found him brought him to Moses, Aaron, and the whole congregation. 📖

## Numbers 15:34
They held him in custody, because what to do with him had not been declared. 📖

## Numbers 15:35
The LORD told Moses, “The man must die. The whole congregation must stone him outside camp.” 📖

## Numbers 15:36
They took him outside and stoned him to death, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Numbers 15:37
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Numbers 15:38
“Tell Israel to make tassels on their garments’ corners throughout future generations. Put a blue cord on each tassel. 📖

## Numbers 15:39
Look at these tassels to remember and obey all the LORD’s commands. Do not prostitute yourselves by following your own hearts and eyes. 📖

## Numbers 15:40
Then you will remember and obey all My commands and be holy to your God. 📖

## Numbers 15:41
I am the LORD your God. I brought you from Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD your God.” 📖
