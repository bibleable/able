## Numbers 14:1
The whole congregation cried aloud. The people wept that night. 📖

## Numbers 14:2
Everyone complained against Moses and Aaron. “If only we had died in Egypt or this wilderness! 📖

## Numbers 14:3
Why does the LORD bring us here to die by the sword? Our wives and children will become plunder. Would returning to Egypt be better?” 📖

## Numbers 14:4
They told each other, “Let us appoint a leader and return to Egypt.” 📖

## Numbers 14:5
Moses and Aaron fell facedown before Israel’s whole assembly. 📖

## Numbers 14:6
Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, two of the explorers, tore their clothes. 📖

## Numbers 14:7
They told the whole congregation, “The land we traveled through and explored is extremely good. 📖

## Numbers 14:8
If the LORD delights in us, He will bring us into that land flowing with milk and honey. He will give it to us. 📖

## Numbers 14:9
Do not rebel against Him or fear the land’s people. They will be like bread for us. Their protection is gone, and the LORD is with us. Do not fear them!” 📖

## Numbers 14:10
The whole congregation threatened to stone Joshua and Caleb. Then the LORD’s glory appeared to all Israel at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Numbers 14:11
The LORD told Moses, “How long will these people despise Me? How long will they refuse to believe Me despite all My signs among them? 📖

## Numbers 14:12
I will strike them with plague and destroy them. I will make you a greater, stronger nation than them.” 📖

## Numbers 14:13
Moses replied, “The Egyptians will hear. You brought these people from among them by Your strength. 📖

## Numbers 14:14
They will tell this land’s inhabitants. These inhabitants already heard that You, LORD, are among this people and have been seen face to face. Your cloud stands over them. You lead them in a pillar of cloud by day and fire by night. 📖

## Numbers 14:15
If You kill these people as one man, nations that heard Your fame will say, 📖

## Numbers 14:16
‘The LORD could not bring them into the land He swore to give them. Therefore, He slaughtered them in the wilderness.’ 📖

## Numbers 14:17
Now, please show my Lord’s great power, as You declared: 🙏

## Numbers 14:18
‘The LORD is slow to anger and full of loving devotion. He forgives sin and rebellion but never leaves the guilty unpunished. He visits fathers’ sins on their children to the third and fourth generations.’ 🙏

## Numbers 14:19
Please forgive these people’s sin according to Your great loving devotion. You have forgiven them ever since they left Egypt.” 🙏

## Numbers 14:20
The LORD replied, “I forgave them as you asked. 📖

## Numbers 14:21
But as surely as I live and the whole earth is filled with the LORD’s glory, 📖

## Numbers 14:22
this judgment applies to men who saw My glory and signs in Egypt and the wilderness, yet tested and disobeyed Me these ten times. 📖

## Numbers 14:23
None will see the land I swore to give their fathers. No one who despised Me will see it. 📖

## Numbers 14:24
My servant Caleb has a different spirit and followed Me wholeheartedly. Therefore, I will bring him into the land he entered. His descendants will inherit it. 📖

## Numbers 14:25
Amalekites and Canaanites live in the valleys. Tomorrow, turn toward the wilderness along the Red Sea route.” 📖

## Numbers 14:26
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Numbers 14:27
“How long will this wicked congregation complain against Me? I have heard Israel’s complaints against Me. 📖

## Numbers 14:28
Tell them, ‘As surely as I live, declares the LORD, I will do exactly what I heard you say. 📖

## Numbers 14:29
Your bodies will fall in this wilderness because you complained against Me. This includes everyone aged twenty or older registered in the census. 📖

## Numbers 14:30
None of you will enter the land where I swore to settle you, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun. 📖

## Numbers 14:31
I will bring your children into the land you rejected. You said they would become plunder, but they will enjoy it. 📖

## Numbers 14:32
Your bodies, however, will fall in this wilderness. 📖

## Numbers 14:33
Your children will be shepherds here for forty years. They will suffer for your unfaithfulness until the last of your bodies lies in the wilderness. 📖

## Numbers 14:34
You explored the land forty days. You will bear your guilt forty years, one year per day. You will experience being estranged from Me. 📖

## Numbers 14:35
I, the LORD, have spoken. I will certainly do this to the whole wicked congregation that conspired against Me. They will meet their end and die in this wilderness.’” 📖

## Numbers 14:36
The explorers Moses sent returned with a bad report. They caused the whole congregation to complain against him. 📖

## Numbers 14:37
Those men who gave the bad report died from a plague before the LORD. 📖

## Numbers 14:38
Only Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh survived among the explorers. 📖

## Numbers 14:39
Moses told all Israel these words. The people mourned bitterly. 📖

## Numbers 14:40
Early next morning, they went toward the hill country’s ridge. “We sinned,” they said, “but we will go to the place the LORD promised.” 📖

## Numbers 14:41
Moses said, “Why break the LORD’s command? This will fail! 📖

## Numbers 14:42
Do not go, or your enemies may strike you down. The LORD is not among you. 📖

## Numbers 14:43
Amalekites and Canaanites will face you there, and you will die by the sword. The LORD will not be with you because you turned from Him.” 📖

## Numbers 14:44
They dared to climb toward the hill country’s ridge. But neither Moses nor the LORD’s covenant ark left camp. 📖

## Numbers 14:45
The Amalekites and Canaanites living in those hills came down and attacked. They defeated Israel all the way to Hormah. 📖
