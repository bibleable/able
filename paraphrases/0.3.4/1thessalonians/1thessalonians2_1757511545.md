## 1 Thessalonians 2:1
Brothers, you know that our visit to you was not useless. 🤝
## 1 Thessalonians 2:2
You know that we had suffered and faced shameful treatment in Philippi. But God gave us courage to tell you His good news, despite strong opposition. 💪
## 1 Thessalonians 2:3
Our appeal does not come from deceit, hidden motives, or tricks. 📣
## 1 Thessalonians 2:4
God approved us and trusted us with the good news. We speak to please God, who examines our hearts, rather than to please people. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:5
You know that we never used flattery or hid greed behind an excuse. God is our witness! ⚖️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:6
We did not seek praise from you or anyone else. As Christ’s apostles, we had authority to demand it. 📣
## 1 Thessalonians 2:7
Instead, we were gentle among you, like a mother who nurses and cares for her children. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:8
We loved you so much that we gladly shared God’s good news and our own lives with you. You had become very dear to us. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:9
Brothers, you remember our hard work. We worked night and day while we told you God’s good news, so we would not burden anyone. 🛠️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:10
You and God are witnesses. We behaved in a holy, righteous, and blameless way among you who believed. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 2:11
You know we treated each of you as a father treats his children. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:12
We encouraged, comforted, and urged you to live in a way worthy of God. He calls you into His own kingdom and glory. 👑
## 1 Thessalonians 2:13
We continually thank God because you accepted the message you heard from us as His word. It truly is God’s word, rather than a human message. It now works in you who believe. 📖
## 1 Thessalonians 2:14
Brothers, you followed the example of God’s churches in Christ Jesus in Judea. You suffered from your own countrymen as those churches suffered from the Jews. ✝️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:15
These opponents killed the Lord Jesus and their own prophets, and drove us out too. They do not please God and are hostile to all people. ⛔
## 1 Thessalonians 2:16
They try to stop us from telling the Gentiles how to be saved. So they continue to fill up the measure of their sins. The fullest anger has come upon them. ⚖️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:17
Brothers, we were separated from you for a short time in body, though you remained in our hearts. We longed even more to see you again. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 2:18
We wanted to visit you. I, Paul, tried again and again, but Satan stopped us. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 2:19
Who is our hope, joy, and crown of pride before our Lord Jesus when He returns? Is it not you? 👑
## 1 Thessalonians 2:20
You are our glory and joy. 😊
