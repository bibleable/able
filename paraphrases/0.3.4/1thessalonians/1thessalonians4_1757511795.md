## 1 Thessalonians 4:1
Brothers, in the Lord Jesus, we ask and encourage you to live in a way that pleases God, as we taught you. You already live this way. Continue to do so more and more. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 4:2
You know the instructions we gave by the Lord Jesus’s authority. 📖
## 1 Thessalonians 4:3
God wants you to be holy. Stay away from sexual immorality. ⛔
## 1 Thessalonians 4:4
Each of you must learn to control his own body in holiness and honor. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 4:5
Do not act from lustful passion, like the Gentiles who do not know God. ⚠️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:6
No one must wrong or take advantage of his brother in these matters. The Lord will punish all such acts. We already told you this and warned you solemnly. ⚖️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:7
God called us to holiness rather than impurity. ✨
## 1 Thessalonians 4:8
Anyone who rejects this command rejects God, rather than a person. God is the One who gives you His Holy Spirit. 🕊️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:9
We do not need to write to you about love for your brothers. God Himself taught you to love each other. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:10
You show this love to all the brothers throughout Macedonia. But we urge you to love more and more. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:11
Make it your aim to live quietly, attend to your own affairs, and work with your hands, as we instructed you. 🛠️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:12
Then you will behave properly toward people outside the faith and will not depend on anyone. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 4:13
Brothers, we want you to understand about those who sleep in death. Then you will not grieve like others who have no hope. 🌅
## 1 Thessalonians 4:14
We believe that Jesus died and rose again. So we also believe that God will bring with Jesus those who died in Him. ✝️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:15
We tell you this by the Lord’s word. Those of us still alive when He comes will not go ahead of those who have died. 📖
## 1 Thessalonians 4:16
The Lord Himself will come down from heaven with a loud command, an archangel’s voice, and God’s trumpet. Those who died in Christ will rise first. 📯
## 1 Thessalonians 4:17
Then those of us still alive will be taken up with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. We will always be with the Lord. ☁️
## 1 Thessalonians 4:18
Encourage each other with these words. ❤️
