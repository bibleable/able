## 1 Thessalonians 3:1
When we could bear it no longer, we agreed to stay alone in Athens. ⏳
## 1 Thessalonians 3:2
We sent Timothy, our brother and fellow worker for God in Christ’s good news. We sent him to strengthen and encourage your faith. 💪
## 1 Thessalonians 3:3
We did not want these trials to shake any of you. You know that we were appointed to face such trials. 🛡️
## 1 Thessalonians 3:4
When we were with you, we kept warning that we would face persecution. You know that this has happened. ✝️
## 1 Thessalonians 3:5
When I could bear it no longer, I sent to learn about your faith. I feared that the tempter had tempted you and our work had been useless. 💭
## 1 Thessalonians 3:6
Timothy has just returned from you with good news about your faith and love. He says you always remember us with affection. You long to see us, just as we long to see you. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 3:7
Brothers, your faith has encouraged us through all our distress and persecution. 💪
## 1 Thessalonians 3:8
Now we can go on living, as long as you stand firm in the Lord. 🌱
## 1 Thessalonians 3:9
How can we thank God enough for you? You give us such great joy before Him! 🙏
## 1 Thessalonians 3:10
Night and day, we pray earnestly to see you again. We want to supply what your faith still lacks. 🙏
## 1 Thessalonians 3:11
May our God and Father Himself and our Lord Jesus guide our way to you. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 3:12
May the Lord make your love grow and overflow for each other and everyone else. Our love for you overflows in this way. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 3:13
May He make your hearts holy and free from blame before our God and Father when our Lord Jesus comes with all His holy people. Amen. ✨
