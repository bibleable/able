## 1 Thessalonians 5:1
Brothers, we do not need to write about the times and seasons. ⏳
## 1 Thessalonians 5:2
You know very well that the Day of the Lord will come like a thief at night. 🌙
## 1 Thessalonians 5:3
People will say, “Peace and safety.” Then destruction will come suddenly, like birth pains on a pregnant woman. They will not escape. ⚠️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:4
But, brothers, you are not in darkness, so that this day should catch you like a thief. 🛡️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:5
You are all children of light and of the day. We do not belong to night or darkness. ☀️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:6
So let us stay awake and clearheaded instead of sleeping like the others. 👀
## 1 Thessalonians 5:7
Those who sleep do so at night. Those who get drunk do so at night. 🌙
## 1 Thessalonians 5:8
We belong to the day, so let us keep clear heads. Put on faith and love like armor over your chest. Put on the hope of salvation like a helmet. 🛡️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:9
God appointed us to receive salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ, rather than suffer His anger. ✝️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:10
Christ died for us so that we can live together with Him, whether we are awake or asleep. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:11
Encourage and strengthen each other, as you already do. 🤝
## 1 Thessalonians 5:12
Brothers, respect those who work hard among you, care for you in the Lord, and warn you. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:13
Honor them highly in love because of their work. Live at peace with each other. 🕊️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:14
Brothers, warn those who reject discipline. Encourage those who lose heart, help the weak, and be patient with everyone. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:15
Make sure no one pays back evil with evil. Always seek what is good for each other and everyone. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 5:16
Rejoice always. 😊
## 1 Thessalonians 5:17
Continue to pray without stopping. 🙏
## 1 Thessalonians 5:18
Give thanks in every situation. This is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus. 🙏
## 1 Thessalonians 5:19
Do not put out the Spirit’s fire. 🔥
## 1 Thessalonians 5:20
Do not treat prophecies with contempt. 📣
## 1 Thessalonians 5:21
Test everything. Hold firmly to what is good. 📖
## 1 Thessalonians 5:22
Stay away from every kind of evil. ⛔
## 1 Thessalonians 5:23
May the God of peace Himself make you completely holy. May your whole spirit, soul, and body be kept free from blame when our Lord Jesus Christ comes. 🕊️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:24
The One who calls you is faithful. He will do this. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 5:25
Brothers, pray for us too. 🙏
## 1 Thessalonians 5:26
Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss. 🤝
## 1 Thessalonians 5:27
I command you before the Lord to have this letter read to all the brothers. 📜
## 1 Thessalonians 5:28
May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. 🕊️
