## 1 Thessalonians 1:1
This letter is from Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy. We write to the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. May you have grace and peace. ✉️
## 1 Thessalonians 1:2
We always thank God for all of you and remember you in our prayers. 🙏
## 1 Thessalonians 1:3
Before our God and Father, we constantly remember your work that comes from faith and your hard service that comes from love. We also remember your lasting hope in our Lord Jesus Christ. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 1:4
Brothers whom God loves, we know He has chosen you. ❤️
## 1 Thessalonians 1:5
Our good news reached you with words, power, the Holy Spirit, and deep conviction. You know how we lived among you for your sake. 🕊️
## 1 Thessalonians 1:6
You followed our example and the Lord’s example. Though you suffered greatly, you welcomed the message with joy from the Holy Spirit. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 1:7
So you became an example to all believers in Macedonia and Achaia. 🌱
## 1 Thessalonians 1:8
The Lord’s message sounded out from you to Macedonia and Achaia. Your faith in God became known everywhere. We do not need to say anything more. 📣
## 1 Thessalonians 1:9
People tell us how you welcomed us. They tell how you turned from idols to serve the living and true God. 👣
## 1 Thessalonians 1:10
You also wait for His Son from heaven. God raised Him from the dead. He is Jesus, who rescues us from the coming anger. 🌅
