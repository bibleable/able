## 2 Chronicles 17:1
Asa’s son Jehoshaphat became king and strengthened himself against Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:2
He stationed troops in every fortified town of Judah. He placed garrisons throughout Judah and in the Ephraimite towns his father Asa captured. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:3
The LORD was with him because he followed his father David’s earlier ways. He did not seek the Baals. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:4
He sought his father’s God and obeyed His commands, instead of Israel’s practices. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:5
The LORD secured his kingdom. All Judah brought tribute, giving him abundant wealth and honor. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:6
His heart delighted in the LORD’s ways. He removed high places and Asherah poles from Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:7
In his third year, he sent Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah, his officials, to teach in Judah’s towns. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:8
Levites accompanied them: Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah. The priests Elishama and Jehoram also went. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:9
They carried the Book of the Law of the LORD. They traveled throughout Judah’s towns and taught the people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:10
The surrounding kingdoms dreaded the LORD. They did not fight Jehoshaphat. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:11
Some Philistines brought gifts and silver tribute. Arabs brought 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats from their flocks. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:12
Jehoshaphat grew stronger and built fortresses and storage towns in Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:13
He kept abundant supplies in Judah’s towns. Mighty warriors served him in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:14
These were their numbers by ancestral families. Judah’s commanders of thousands included Adnah, with 300,000 mighty warriors, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:15
Jehohanan, with 280,000 beside him, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:16
and Amasiah son of Zichri, who volunteered for the LORD, with 200,000 mighty warriors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:17
From Benjamin, the mighty warrior Eliada had 200,000 men with bows and shields. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:18
Beside him, Jehozabad had 180,000 armed men. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 17:19
These served the king, besides those stationed in Judah’s fortified towns. 📖
