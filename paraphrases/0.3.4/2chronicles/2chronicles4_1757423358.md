## 2 Chronicles 4:1
He made a bronze altar twenty cubits long, twenty wide, and ten high. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:2
He made the round Sea from cast metal. It measured ten cubits across, five high, and thirty around its circumference. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:3
Figures of oxen circled below its rim, ten per cubit. They were cast in two rows as part of the Sea. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:4
It stood on twelve oxen: three facing north, three west, three south, and three east. Their hindquarters faced the center beneath the Sea. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:5
The Sea was a handbreadth thick. Its rim resembled a cup’s brim or a lily blossom. It held three thousand baths. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:6
He made ten washing basins, five south and five north. They were for rinsing burnt offering parts. The priests washed in the Sea. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:7
He made ten gold lampstands according to their specifications. He placed five south and five north in the temple. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:8
He placed ten tables in the temple, five south and five north. He also made a hundred gold bowls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:9
He made the priests’ courtyard and the large court with doors. He covered the doors with bronze. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:10
He placed the Sea on the south, at the southeast corner. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:11
Huram made the pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. Thus he finished his work for King Solomon in God’s house. These were his completed items: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:12
two pillars, two capitals shaped like bowls on top, and two sets of network covering those capital bowls, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:13
four hundred pomegranates for the two sets of network. Each network had two rows of pomegranates covering the capitals shaped like bowls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:14
He made the stands and their basins, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:15
the Sea and its twelve supporting oxen, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:16
pots, shovels, meat forks, and all the other articles. Huram-abi made all these from polished bronze for King Solomon and the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:17
The king had them cast in clay molds on the Jordan plain between Succoth and Zeredah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:18
Solomon made so many articles that the bronze’s weight could not be determined. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:19
He made all God’s house furnishings, including the gold altar and the tables for the Bread of the Presence, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:20
the pure gold lampstands and lamps to burn before the inner sanctuary as prescribed, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:21
the flowers, lamps, and tongs of purest gold, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 4:22
and the pure gold wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, ladles, and censers. He made the temple’s gold doors, both the inner doors to the Most Holy Place and the main hall’s doors. 📖
