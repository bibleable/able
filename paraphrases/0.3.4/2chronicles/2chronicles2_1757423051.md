## 2 Chronicles 2:1
Solomon decided to build a house for the LORD’s Name and a royal palace for himself. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:2
He forced 70,000 men to carry loads and 80,000 to cut stone in the mountains. He appointed 3,600 supervisors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:3
He sent Hiram king of Tyre this message: “Send cedar for me, as you did for my father David’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:4
I will build and dedicate a house for the LORD my God’s Name. There we will burn fragrant incense, continually display showbread, and offer burnt offerings each morning and evening. We will also offer them on Sabbaths, New Moons, and His appointed feasts. This is Israel’s permanent duty. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:5
The house will be great, because our God is greater than all gods. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:6
Who can build Him a house when heaven, even the highest heavens, cannot contain Him? Who am I to build it, except for burning sacrifices before Him? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:7
Send a craftsman skilled in engraving, gold, silver, bronze, iron, and purple, crimson, and blue yarn. He will work with the craftsmen my father David provided in Judah and Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:8
Send cedar, cypress, and algum logs from Lebanon. Your servants know how to cut timber there. Mine will work with yours, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:9
preparing abundant timber for the great, wonderful temple I will build. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:10
I will pay your woodcutters 20,000 cors of ground wheat and 20,000 cors of barley. I will also give 20,000 baths of wine and 20,000 baths of olive oil.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:11
Hiram king of Tyre replied by letter, “The LORD made you king because He loves His people.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:12
He added, “Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God, who made heaven and earth! He gave King David a wise son with insight and understanding. This son will build the LORD’s temple and his own royal palace. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:13
I send Huram-abi, a skilled, creative man. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:14
His mother is from Dan, and his father is from Tyre. He works with gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, wood, purple, blue, and crimson yarn, and fine linen. He can do every kind of engraving and any assigned design. He will work with your craftsmen and those of my lord, your father David. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:15
Let my lord send his servants the promised wheat, barley, olive oil, and wine. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:16
We will cut all the Lebanon logs you need and float them as sea rafts to Joppa. You can take them up to Jerusalem.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:17
Solomon counted Israel’s foreign men after his father David’s census. They totaled 153,600. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 2:18
He assigned 70,000 to carry loads, 80,000 to cut mountain stone, and 3,600 to supervise. 📖
