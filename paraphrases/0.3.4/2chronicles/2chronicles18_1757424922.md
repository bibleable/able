## 2 Chronicles 18:1
Jehoshaphat had great riches and honor. He formed a marriage alliance with Ahab. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:2
Years later, he visited Ahab in Samaria. Ahab sacrificed many sheep and cattle for him and his companions. He urged Jehoshaphat to attack Ramoth-gilead. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:3
Ahab king of Israel asked Jehoshaphat king of Judah, “Will you join me against Ramoth-gilead?” He replied, “I am as you are. My people are your people. We will join the war.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:4
Jehoshaphat added, “First, please seek the LORD’s word.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:5
Israel’s king gathered four hundred prophets. “Should we fight Ramoth-gilead or refrain?” They replied, “Go! God will give it to the king.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:6
Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there another prophet of the LORD we can consult?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:7
Israel’s king replied, “Micaiah son of Imlah remains. But I hate him. He prophesies only bad for me, never good.” Jehoshaphat said, “The king should not say that!” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:8
Israel’s king ordered an official, “Bring Micaiah son of Imlah immediately.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:9
Both kings sat on thrones in royal clothes at the threshing floor by Samaria’s gate. All the prophets prophesied before them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:10
Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns. He declared, “The LORD says, ‘With these, you will gore the Arameans until they are destroyed.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:11
All the prophets agreed. “Attack Ramoth-gilead and win! The LORD will give it to the king.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:12
The messenger told Micaiah, “All the prophets speak favorably to the king. Please agree and speak favorably too.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:13
Micaiah replied, “As surely as the LORD lives, I will say whatever my God tells me.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:14
The king asked Micaiah, “Should we fight Ramoth-gilead or refrain?” He answered, “Go and win. They will be given into your hand.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:15
The king said, “How often must I make you swear to tell only the truth in the LORD’s name?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:16
Micaiah said, “I saw all Israel scattered on hills like sheep without a shepherd. The LORD said, ‘They have no master. Let each return home peacefully.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:17
Israel’s king told Jehoshaphat, “Did I not say he prophesies only bad for me?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:18
Micaiah continued, “Hear the LORD’s word. I saw Him on His throne. Heaven’s whole host stood on His right and left. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:19
He asked, ‘Who will entice Ahab king of Israel to attack Ramoth-gilead and fall there?’ They offered various suggestions. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:20
A spirit stood before Him. ‘I will entice him.’ The LORD asked, ‘How?’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:21
The spirit replied, ‘I will be a lying spirit in all his prophets’ mouths.’ The LORD said, ‘You will entice him successfully. Go and do it.’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:22
The LORD put a lying spirit in your prophets’ mouths. He pronounced disaster against you.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:23
Zedekiah son of Chenaanah struck Micaiah’s face. “Which way did the LORD’s Spirit leave me to speak to you?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:24
Micaiah replied, “You will soon see, on the day you hide in an inner room.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:25
Israel’s king ordered, “Return Micaiah to Amon, the town’s governor, and Joash, the king’s son. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:26
Tell them, ‘The king commands you to imprison him. Give him only bread and water until I return safely.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:27
Micaiah replied, “If you return safely, the LORD did not speak through me.” He added, “Everyone, take heed!” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:28
Israel’s king and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went to Ramoth-gilead. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:29
Israel’s king told Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself for battle. You wear royal robes.” He disguised himself and fought. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:30
Aram’s king ordered his chariot commanders, “Fight no one, small or great, except Israel’s king.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:31
They saw Jehoshaphat and mistook him for Israel’s king. They attacked, but he cried out. The LORD helped him, and God drew them away. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:32
They realized he was not Israel’s king and stopped chasing him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:33
A man shot an arrow without special aim. It struck Israel’s king between his armor’s joints. The king told his charioteer, “Turn around and take me from battle! I am badly wounded.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 18:34
Battle raged all day. Israel’s king propped himself in his chariot facing the Arameans until evening. He died at sunset. 📖
