## 2 Chronicles 9:1
Sheba’s queen heard Solomon’s fame and came to test him with hard questions. Her huge caravan reached Jerusalem with camels carrying spices, abundant gold, and precious stones. She told Solomon everything on her mind. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:2
He answered every question. Nothing was too difficult to explain. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:3
Sheba’s queen saw his wisdom and palace. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:4
She saw his table’s food, his servants’ seating, his attendants’ service and clothing, and his cupbearers’ clothing. She saw his burnt offerings at the LORD’s house. These took her breath away. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:5
She told him, “The report in my country about your words and wisdom was true. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:6
I did not believe until I saw for myself. They told me less than half your wisdom’s greatness. You far exceed the reports. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:7
Your men are blessed! These servants who continually stand before you and hear your wisdom are blessed! 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:8
Blessed be the LORD your God, who delighted to place you on His throne as king for Him. He loved Israel enough to establish them forever. He made you king to carry out justice and righteousness.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:9
She gave him 120 talents of gold, abundant spices, and precious stones. No spices had ever been like those Sheba’s queen gave Solomon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:10
Hiram’s and Solomon’s servants brought gold from Ophir, also algum wood and precious stones. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:11
Solomon used the algum wood for steps in the LORD’s house and his palace, and lyres and harps for singers. Judah had never seen anything like them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:12
He gave Sheba’s queen everything she desired and asked for, far more than she brought him. She and her servants returned home. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:13
Solomon received 666 talents of gold each year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:14
This excluded merchants’ and traders’ revenue. All Arabian kings and the land’s governors also brought him gold and silver. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:15
He made two hundred large shields of hammered gold. Each used six hundred shekels of hammered gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:16
He made three hundred small shields of hammered gold, each using three hundred shekels. He placed them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:17
He made a great ivory throne and covered it with pure gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:18
It had six steps and an attached gold footstool. Armrests stood on each side, with a lion beside each. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:19
Twelve lions stood on the six steps, one at each end of every step. No kingdom had anything like it. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:20
All his drinking cups were gold. All utensils in the House of the Forest of Lebanon were pure gold. There was no silver, considered worthless in Solomon’s time. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:21
The king had ships of Tarshish that sailed with Hiram’s servants. Every three years, they arrived with gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:22
Solomon surpassed all earth’s kings in riches and wisdom. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:23
All earth’s kings sought to meet him and hear the wisdom God placed in his heart. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:24
Each visitor brought annual tribute: silver and gold articles, clothing, weapons, spices, horses, and mules. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:25
Solomon had 4,000 stalls for horses and chariots, and 12,000 horses. He stationed them in chariot towns and with him in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:26
He ruled all kings from the Euphrates to Philistine land and Egypt’s border. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:27
He made silver as common as stones in Jerusalem, and cedars as abundant as foothill sycamores. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:28
His horses were imported from Egypt and all lands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:29
His other deeds, from beginning to end, are recorded in the Records of Nathan the Prophet and the Prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite. They are also in the Visions of Iddo the Seer concerning Jeroboam son of Nebat. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:30
He reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 9:31
He rested with his fathers and was buried in his father David’s city. His son Rehoboam became king. 📖
