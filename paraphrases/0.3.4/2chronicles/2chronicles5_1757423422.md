## 2 Chronicles 5:1
Solomon finished all work on the LORD’s house. He brought in David’s dedicated silver, gold, and furnishings. He placed them in God’s house treasuries. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:2
He gathered Israel’s elders, tribal heads, and family leaders in Jerusalem. They would bring the LORD’s covenant ark from Zion, the City of David. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:3
All Israel’s men gathered before the king at the feast in the seventh month. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:4
When all the elders arrived, the Levites lifted the ark. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:5
The Levitical priests carried up the ark, the Tent of Meeting, and all its sacred furnishings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:6
Solomon and Israel’s assembled congregation sacrificed sheep and oxen before the ark. There were too many to count. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:7
The priests brought the LORD’s covenant ark into the temple’s inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place. They placed it beneath the cherubim’s wings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:8
The cherubim’s wings spread over the ark’s place. They covered the ark and its poles. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:9
The poles extended so their ends were visible from in front of the inner sanctuary. They were not visible from outside the Holy Place. They remain there to this day. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:10
The ark held only the two tablets Moses placed there at Horeb. There the LORD made a covenant with Israel after they left Egypt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:11
All priests present had dedicated themselves, regardless of their divisions. The priests came from the Holy Place. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:12
All Levitical singers stood east of the altar in fine linen. They were Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and relatives, playing cymbals, harps, and lyres. They were accompanied by 120 priests sounding trumpets. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:13
Trumpeters and singers praised and thanked the LORD as one voice. Trumpets, cymbals, and instruments accompanied their praise: “He is good. His loving devotion lasts forever.” A cloud filled the LORD’s temple. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 5:14
The priests could not stand to serve because of the cloud. The LORD’s glory filled God’s house. 📖
