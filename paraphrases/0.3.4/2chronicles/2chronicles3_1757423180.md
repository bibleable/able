## 2 Chronicles 3:1
Solomon began the LORD’s house in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the LORD appeared to his father David. David prepared the site at Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:2
Construction began on the second day of the second month, in Solomon’s fourth year as king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:3
The foundation measured sixty cubits long and twenty wide, using the old standard. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:4
The front portico spanned the temple’s width, twenty cubits long and twenty high. He covered its interior with pure gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:5
He lined the main room with cypress, covered it with fine gold, and decorated it with palm trees and chains. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:6
He decorated the temple with precious stones for beauty. Its gold came from Parvaim. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:7
He covered its beams, thresholds, walls, and doors with gold. He carved cherubim on the walls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:8
He made the Most Holy Place twenty cubits long and twenty wide, matching the temple’s width. He covered its interior with six hundred talents of fine gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:9
The gold nails weighed fifty shekels. He also covered the upper rooms with gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:10
He made two sculpted cherubim in the Most Holy Place and covered them with gold. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:11
Their combined wingspan was twenty cubits. The first cherub’s wing measured five cubits and touched the temple wall. Its other wing measured five cubits and touched the other cherub’s wing. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:12
The second cherub’s wing measured five cubits and touched the wall. Its other wing measured five cubits and touched the first cherub’s wing. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:13
Their combined wingspan was twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, facing the main room. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:14
He made the veil from blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine linen. Cherubim were woven into it. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:15
He made two pillars before the temple, together thirty-five cubits high. Each had a capital five cubits high on top. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:16
He put interwoven chains on the pillars’ tops. He fastened a hundred pomegranates into the chainwork. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 3:17
He erected the pillars before the temple, one south and one north. He named the southern pillar Jachin and the northern one Boaz. 📖
