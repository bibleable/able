## 2 Chronicles 14:1
Abijah rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. His son Asa became king. The land had peace for ten years in his days. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:2
Asa did what the LORD his God saw as good and right. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:3
He removed foreign altars and high places. He smashed sacred pillars and cut down Asherah poles. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:4
He commanded Judah to seek the LORD, their fathers’ God, and obey His law and commands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:5
He removed high places and incense altars from all Judah’s towns. His kingdom had peace. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:6
He built fortified towns in Judah during that peace. No one fought him because the LORD gave him rest. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:7
He told Judah, “Let us build towns with walls, towers, doors, and bars. The land remains ours because we sought the LORD our God. He gave us rest on every side.” They built and prospered. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:8
His army had 300,000 from Judah with large shields and spears. Another 280,000 from Benjamin carried small shields and bows. All were mighty warriors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:9
Zerah the Cushite attacked with 1,000,000 men and 300 chariots. They advanced to Mareshah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:10
Asa faced him in battle formation in the Valley of Zephathah near Mareshah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:11
Asa cried to the LORD his God, “Only You can help the powerless against the mighty. Help us, LORD our God. We rely on You and confront this army in Your name. You are our God. Do not let a mere mortal defeat You.” 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 14:12
The LORD struck the Cushites before Asa and Judah. The Cushites fled. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:13
Asa’s army chased them to Gerar. They fell and could not recover, crushed before the LORD and His army. Judah took much plunder. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:14
They attacked all towns around Gerar, where the LORD’s terror had fallen. They plundered them because they held abundant spoils. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 14:15
They attacked herdsmen’s tents and took many sheep and camels. Then they returned to Jerusalem. 📖
