## 2 Chronicles 23:1
In the seventh year, Jehoiada strengthened himself and covenanted with commanders of hundreds. They were Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, and Azariah son of Obed. Maaseiah son of Adaiah and Elishaphat son of Zichri also joined. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:2
They traveled throughout Judah, gathering Levites from all its towns and Israel’s family heads. They came to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:3
The whole assembly covenanted with the king in God’s house. Jehoiada said, “Here is the king’s son! He must reign, as the LORD promised David’s descendants. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:4
Do this: one third of the priests and Levites beginning Sabbath duty must guard the doors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:5
One third must guard the palace, and one third the Foundation Gate. Everyone else must stay in the LORD’s house courtyards. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:6
Only priests and serving Levites may enter the LORD’s house because they are dedicated. Everyone must obey His requirement. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:7
Levites must surround the king, weapons in hand. Put anyone entering the temple to death. Stay close wherever the king goes.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:8
The Levites and all Judah obeyed Jehoiada the priest. Each leader took his men coming on and going off Sabbath duty. Jehoiada had released no divisions. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:9
He gave the commanders of hundreds David’s spears and large and small shields from God’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:10
He stationed all armed troops around the king by the altar and temple, from the temple’s south side to its north. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:11
Jehoiada and his sons brought out the king’s son. They crowned him, gave him the Testimony, and proclaimed him king. They anointed him and shouted, “Long live the king!” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:12
Athaliah heard people running and cheering the king. She went to them in the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:13
She saw the king by his pillar at the entrance, with officers and trumpeters beside him. Everyone rejoiced and sounded trumpets while singers led praise with instruments. She tore her clothes and screamed, “Treason, treason!” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:14
Jehoiada ordered the army’s commanders of hundreds, “Bring her out between the ranks. Use the sword to kill anyone who follows her.” He had said, “Do not kill her in the LORD’s house.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:15
They seized her at the Horse Gate entrance on the palace grounds. They executed her there. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:16
Jehoiada covenanted with the king and people that they would be the LORD’s people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:17
Everyone demolished Baal’s temple. They smashed its altars and idols. They killed Mattan, Baal’s priest, before the altars. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:18
Jehoiada put Levitical priests in charge of the LORD’s house, as David appointed. They must offer burnt offerings according to Moses’ Law, with joy and song as David ordained. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:19
He stationed gatekeepers so no one unclean in any way could enter the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:20
He gathered commanders of hundreds, nobles, rulers, and all the people. They brought the king from the LORD’s house through the Upper Gate into the palace. They seated Joash on the royal throne. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 23:21
Everyone rejoiced. The town was quiet because Athaliah died by the sword. 📖
