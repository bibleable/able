## 2 Chronicles 26:1
Judah made sixteen-year-old Uzziah king instead of his father Amaziah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:2
He rebuilt Eloth and restored it to Judah after King Amaziah rested with his fathers. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:3
He became king at sixteen and reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jecoliah from Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:4
He did what the LORD saw as right, like his father Amaziah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:5
He sought God throughout Zechariah’s days. Zechariah taught him to fear God. God gave him success as long as he sought the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:6
He fought the Philistines and demolished Gath’s, Jabneh’s, and Ashdod’s walls. He built towns near Ashdod and among the Philistines. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:7
God helped him against Philistines, Arabs in Gur-baal, and Meunites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:8
Ammonites brought tribute. His fame reached Egypt’s border because he became extremely powerful. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:9
He built and fortified Jerusalem’s towers at the Corner Gate, Valley Gate, and the wall’s angle. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:10
He built desert towers and dug many cisterns for his abundant livestock in the foothills and plain. He loved the soil. He employed farmers and vineyard workers in the hills and fertile fields. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:11
His army was ready for battle and fought in assigned divisions. Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer recorded them under the royal officer Hananiah’s direction. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:12
The mighty warriors’ family leaders totaled 2,600. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:13
They commanded 307,500 trained soldiers, a powerful force supporting the king against enemies. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:14
He supplied the entire army with shields, spears, helmets, armor, bows, and slingstones. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:15
In Jerusalem, he made skillfully designed devices to shoot arrows and throw large stones from towers and corners. His fame spread widely. He received tremendous help until he became powerful. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:16
His power made him arrogant, leading to destruction. He was unfaithful to the LORD his God. He entered the temple to burn incense on its incense altar. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:17
Azariah the priest and eighty brave priests of the LORD followed him inside. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:18
They opposed him. “Uzziah, you have no right to offer incense. Only Aaron’s priestly descendants are dedicated for this. Leave the sanctuary! Your unfaithfulness will bring no honor from the LORD God.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:19
Holding a censer to offer incense, he became furious. As he raged at the priests before the incense altar in the LORD’s house, leprosy appeared on his forehead. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:20
Chief priest Azariah and the others saw his leprous forehead and rushed him out. He hurried out himself because the LORD afflicted him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:21
Uzziah remained leprous until death. He lived isolated and excluded from the LORD’s house. His son Jotham managed the palace and governed the people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:22
Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet recorded his other deeds, from beginning to end. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 26:23
Uzziah rested with his fathers. They buried him near them in the kings’ burial field, saying, “He was a leper.” His son Jotham became king. 📖
