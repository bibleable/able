## 2 Chronicles 22:1
Jerusalem made Jehoram’s youngest son Ahaziah king of Judah. Raiders who entered the camp with the Arabs killed all the older sons. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:2
Ahaziah became king at twenty-two and reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother was Athaliah, Omri’s granddaughter. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:3
He followed Ahab’s house because his mother advised him in wickedness. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:4
He did evil before the LORD like Ahab’s house. After his father died, they advised him to his destruction. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:5
Following their advice, he joined Joram son of Ahab, Israel’s king, against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead. The Arameans wounded Joram. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:6
Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from wounds received at Ramah fighting Hazael king of Aram. Ahaziah son of Jehoram, Judah’s king, visited Joram son of Ahab there because of his wounds. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:7
God brought Ahaziah’s downfall through this visit. He and Joram went to meet Jehu son of Nimshi. The LORD anointed Jehu to destroy Ahab’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:8
While judging Ahab’s house, Jehu found Judah’s rulers and Ahaziah’s brothers’ sons serving Ahaziah. He killed them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:9
Jehu searched for Ahaziah. His soldiers captured him hiding in Samaria and brought him for execution. They buried him, saying, “He is Jehoshaphat’s grandson. Jehoshaphat sought the LORD wholeheartedly.” No one in Ahaziah’s house remained strong enough to rule. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:10
Athaliah saw her son Ahaziah was dead. She began destroying all Judah’s royal heirs. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:11
Jehoshabeath, King Jehoram’s daughter, rescued Joash son of Ahaziah from the royal sons being murdered. She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom. She was Ahaziah’s sister and Jehoiada the priest’s wife. She hid Joash from Athaliah so she could not kill him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 22:12
Joash stayed hidden with them in God’s house six years while Athaliah ruled. 📖
