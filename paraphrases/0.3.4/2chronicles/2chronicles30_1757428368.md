## 2 Chronicles 30:1
Hezekiah invited all Israel and Judah to Jerusalem to observe Passover to the LORD, Israel’s God, at His house. He also wrote to Ephraim and Manasseh. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:2
He, his officials, and Jerusalem’s whole assembly decided to observe Passover in the second month. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:3
They could not observe it at the regular time. Too few priests had dedicated themselves, and the people had not gathered in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:4
The king and whole assembly approved the plan. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:5
They decreed a proclamation throughout Israel, from Beersheba to Dan. Everyone should observe Passover to the LORD, Israel’s God, in Jerusalem. They had not observed it in great numbers as prescribed. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:6
The king’s couriers carried his and his officials’ letters throughout Israel and Judah. “Israel, return to the LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel. Then He can return to those remaining who escaped Assyria’s kings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:7
Do not imitate your fathers’ and brothers’ unfaithfulness to the LORD, their fathers’ God. He made them objects of horror, as you see. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:8
Do not be stubborn like your fathers. Submit to the LORD and enter His sanctuary, dedicated forever. Serve Him so His fierce anger turns away. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:9
If you return to Him, your brothers and sons will receive mercy from their captors and return here. He is gracious and merciful. He will not turn His face away if you return.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:10
Couriers visited Ephraim’s and Manasseh’s towns as far as Zebulun. People mocked and scorned them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:11
But some from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:12
God’s power united Judah’s heart to obey the king and officials according to the LORD’s word. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:13
A huge assembly gathered in Jerusalem in the second month for the Feast of Unleavened Bread. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:14
They removed Jerusalem’s altars and incense altars and threw them into the Kidron Valley. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:15
They killed the Passover lamb on the second month’s fourteenth day. Ashamed, the priests and Levites dedicated themselves and brought burnt offerings to the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:16
They stood at their prescribed posts according to the Law of Moses, the man of God. Priests splashed the blood they received from Levites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:17
Many had not dedicated themselves. Levites killed Passover lambs for each unclean person, dedicating the lambs to the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:18
Many from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun ate Passover without purification, against what was written. Hezekiah prayed for them, “May the good LORD provide atonement for everyone 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 30:19
who determines to seek God, the LORD, his fathers’ God, even without the sanctuary’s prescribed purification.” 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 30:20
The LORD heard and healed the people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:21
Israelites in Jerusalem celebrated the Feast of Unleavened Bread joyfully for seven days. Levites and priests praised the LORD daily with loud instruments. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:22
Hezekiah encouraged the Levites serving skillfully before the LORD. For seven days, they ate their portions, sacrificed peace offerings, and thanked the LORD, their fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:23
Everyone agreed to celebrate seven more days. They did so joyfully. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:24
Hezekiah supplied a thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep. Officials supplied a thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep for the assembly. Many priests dedicated themselves. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:25
Judah’s assembly, priests, Levites, and Israel’s visiting assembly rejoiced. This included foreigners from Israel and those living in Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:26
Jerusalem had great joy. Nothing like it happened there since Solomon son of David, Israel’s king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 30:27
Priests and Levites stood to bless the people. God heard their voice. Their prayer reached His holy home in heaven. 📖
