## 2 Chronicles 32:1
After these faithful acts, Sennacherib king of Assyria invaded Judah. He besieged its fortified towns, intending to conquer them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:2
Hezekiah saw he intended to fight Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:3
He consulted leaders and warriors about blocking the springs outside town. They helped him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:4
Many gathered and blocked all the springs and the stream through the land. They said, “Why let Assyria’s kings find abundant water?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:5
He rebuilt all broken wall sections and raised towers. He built an outer wall, strengthened the City of David’s supporting terraces, and made many weapons and shields. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:6
He appointed military commanders and gathered everyone in the gate square. He encouraged them, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:7
“Be strong and courageous! Do not fear Assyria’s king and his huge army or lose courage. There is a greater One with us than with him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:8
He has only the arm of flesh. We have the LORD our God to help and fight for us.” His words strengthened them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:9
While besieging Lachish with all his forces, Sennacherib sent servants to Jerusalem. They brought a message for Hezekiah king of Judah and Judah’s people there. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:10
“Sennacherib king of Assyria asks, ‘What confidence keeps you in besieged Jerusalem? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:11
Hezekiah says your God will save you from Assyria’s king. Is he not misleading you to die from hunger and thirst? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:12
Did he not remove His high places and altars? Did he not tell Judah and Jerusalem to worship and sacrifice at only one altar? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:13
Do you not know what my fathers and I did to every land’s people? Did their gods ever save their lands from me? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:14
Which god saved the people my fathers devoted to destruction from my hand? How can your God save you? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:15
Do not let Hezekiah deceive or mislead you. Do not believe him. No nation’s or kingdom’s god saved its people from me or my fathers. How much less can your God save you!’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:16
His servants spoke further against the LORD God and His servant Hezekiah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:17
Sennacherib wrote letters mocking the LORD, Israel’s God. “Nations’ gods did not save their people from me. Neither will Hezekiah’s God save His people.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:18
Assyrians shouted in Hebrew to Jerusalem’s people on the wall. They intended to terrify them and capture the town. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:19
They spoke against Jerusalem’s God as against the nations’ gods, made by human hands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:20
Hezekiah and Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet cried to heaven in prayer. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:21
The LORD sent an angel who destroyed every mighty warrior, leader, and commander in Assyria’s camp. Sennacherib returned home disgraced. In his god’s temple, some of his sons killed him with the sword. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:22
The LORD saved Hezekiah and Jerusalem from Sennacherib king of Assyria and all others. He gave them rest on every side. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:23
Many brought the LORD offerings and Hezekiah valuable gifts in Jerusalem. All nations honored him from then on. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:24
Hezekiah became mortally ill. He prayed, and the LORD spoke and gave him a sign. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:25
Pride kept him from repaying the favor shown him. Wrath came on him, Judah, and Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:26
He and Jerusalem’s people humbled their pride. The LORD’s wrath did not fall during Hezekiah’s days. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:27
He had great wealth and honor. He made treasuries for silver, gold, precious stones, spices, shields, and every kind of valuable article. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:28
He made stores for grain, new wine, and oil harvests, stalls for livestock, and pens for flocks. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:29
He built towns and acquired abundant sheep and cattle. God gave him great wealth. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:30
He blocked Gihon’s upper spring outlet and directed water to the west side of the City of David. He prospered in everything. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:31
Babylon’s rulers sent ambassadors to ask about the land’s wonder. God left him alone to test him and know everything in his heart. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:32
His other deeds and loving devotion are in Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet’s vision in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 32:33
He rested with his fathers and was buried in David’s descendants’ upper tombs. All Judah and Jerusalem honored his death. His son Manasseh became king. 📖
