## 2 Chronicles 24:1
Joash became king at seven and reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother was Zibiah from Beersheba. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:2
He did what the LORD saw as right throughout Jehoiada the priest’s life. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:3
Jehoiada arranged two wives for him. Joash had sons and daughters. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:4
Later, Joash determined to repair the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:5
He told the priests and Levites, “Collect Israel’s annual dues in Judah’s towns to repair your God’s house. Hurry!” But the Levites delayed. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:6
He summoned high priest Jehoiada. “Why have you not required the Levites to collect Judah’s and Jerusalem’s tax? Moses, the LORD’s servant, and Israel’s assembly imposed it for the Tent of the Testimony.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:7
Wicked Athaliah’s sons broke into God’s house. They used its sacred objects for the Baals. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:8
At the king’s command, a chest was placed outside at the LORD’s house gate. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:9
Judah and Jerusalem were commanded to bring the LORD’s tax. God’s servant Moses imposed it on Israel in the wilderness. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:10
All the officials and people rejoiced. They filled the chest with contributions. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:11
The Levites brought the chest to the king’s overseers. When they saw much money, the royal scribe and high priest’s officer emptied it and returned it to its place. They did this daily and gathered abundant money. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:12
The king and Jehoiada gave the money to work supervisors. They hired stonecutters, carpenters, and iron and bronze workers to restore the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:13
The repairs progressed. Workers restored God’s house to its specifications and reinforced it. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:14
They returned the remaining money to the king and Jehoiada. It funded service utensils, burnt offering utensils, dishes, and gold and silver objects for the LORD’s house. Burnt offerings continued regularly throughout Jehoiada’s days. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:15
Jehoiada died at the great age of 130. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:16
He was buried with the kings in the City of David. He did good in Israel for God and His temple. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:17
After he died, Judah’s officials paid homage to Joash. The king listened to them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:18
They abandoned the house of the LORD, their fathers’ God, and served Asherah poles and idols. Wrath fell on Judah and Jerusalem because of their guilt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:19
The LORD sent prophets to bring them back and testify against them. They refused to listen. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:20
God’s Spirit came on Zechariah son of Jehoiada the priest. He stood before them, saying, “God says, ‘Why break the LORD’s commands and prevent your prosperity? You abandoned Him, so He abandoned you.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:21
They conspired against him. At the king’s order, they stoned him in the LORD’s house courtyard. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:22
Joash forgot Jehoiada’s kindness and killed his son. Dying, Zechariah said, “May the LORD see and hold you accountable.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:23
In spring, Aram’s army attacked Joash. They invaded Judah and Jerusalem, killed all the people’s leaders, and sent all plunder to their king in Damascus. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:24
Few Arameans came, but the LORD gave them victory over a huge army. Judah abandoned the LORD, their fathers’ God. Thus judgment fell on Joash. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:25
They withdrew, leaving him badly wounded. His servants conspired against him because he shed the blood of Jehoiada the priest’s son. They killed him on his bed. He was buried in the City of David, outside the kings’ tombs. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:26
The conspirators were Zabad son of Shimeath, an Ammonite woman, and Jehozabad son of Shimrith, a Moabite woman. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 24:27
His sons’ accounts, the many pronouncements about him, and God’s house restoration are in the Treatise of the Book of the Kings. His son Amaziah became king. 📖
