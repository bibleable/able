## 2 Chronicles 35:1
Josiah observed Passover to the LORD in Jerusalem. They killed the Passover lamb on the first month’s fourteenth day. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:2
He assigned priests their duties and encouraged their service in the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:3
He spoke to the Levites who taught all Israel and were holy to the LORD. “Put the holy ark in the temple built by Solomon son of David, king of Israel. Do not carry it on your shoulders. Serve the LORD your God and His people Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:4
Prepare by families and divisions according to the written instructions of David king of Israel and his son Solomon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:5
Stand in the Holy Place by family divisions of your fellow lay people and the Levites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:6
Kill the Passover lambs and dedicate yourselves. Prepare for your countrymen to fulfill the LORD’s word through Moses.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:7
Josiah gave all those present a total of 30,000 lambs and goats, plus 3,000 bulls, from his flocks and herds for Passover offerings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:8
Officials gave willingly to people, priests, and Levites. Temple chief officials Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel gave priests 2,600 Passover offerings and 300 bulls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:9
Levitical officers Conaniah, his brothers Shemaiah and Nethanel, Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad gave Levites 5,000 Passover offerings and 500 bulls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:10
They prepared the service. Priests stood at their posts and Levites in their divisions, as the king commanded. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:11
They killed the Passover lambs. Priests splashed the blood handed to them, and Levites skinned the animals. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:12
They reserved burnt offerings for the people’s family divisions to offer the LORD according to the Book of Moses. They did the same with bulls. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:13
They roasted Passover animals as prescribed. They boiled other holy offerings in pots, kettles, and bowls and quickly served everyone. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:14
Afterward, Levites prepared for themselves and the priests, Aaron’s descendants. The priests had offered burnt offerings and fat until nightfall. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:15
According to the command of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun the king’s seer, Asaph’s singers kept their posts. Gatekeepers stayed at their gates because fellow Levites prepared for them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:16
The entire service was completed that day: Passover and burnt offerings on the LORD’s altar, according to Josiah’s command. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:17
The Israelites present observed Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:18
No Passover like it occurred since Samuel the prophet. No Israelite king observed one like Josiah with priests, Levites, all Judah, visiting Israelites, and Jerusalem’s people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:19
This Passover was in Josiah’s eighteenth year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:20
After Josiah restored the temple, Neco king of Egypt marched to fight at Carchemish beside the Euphrates. Josiah confronted him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:21
Neco sent messengers. “What dispute have we, Judah’s king? I fight another dynasty, not you. God told me to hurry. Stop opposing God, who is with me, or He will destroy you!” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:22
Josiah refused to withdraw and disguised himself for battle. He ignored Neco’s words from God’s mouth and fought on the Plain of Megiddo. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:23
Archers shot him. He told his servants, “Take me away! I am badly wounded.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:24
They moved him from his chariot to his second chariot and brought him to Jerusalem. He died and was buried in his fathers’ tomb. All Judah and Jerusalem mourned. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:25
Jeremiah lamented him. Male and female singers still recite Josiah’s laments today. They established them as Israel’s statute. They are written in the Book of Laments. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:26
His other deeds and loving devotion according to the LORD’s Law, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 35:27
from beginning to end, are in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. 📖
