## 2 Chronicles 12:1
After Rehoboam secured his kingdom and power, he and all Israel with him abandoned the LORD’s Law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:2
In his fifth year, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem because they had been unfaithful to the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:3
He brought 1,200 chariots, 60,000 horsemen, and countless troops from Egypt: Libyans, Sukkites, and Cushites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:4
He captured Judah’s fortified towns and reached Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:5
Shemaiah the prophet came to Rehoboam and Judah’s leaders, gathered in Jerusalem because of Shishak. He said, “The LORD says, ‘You abandoned Me. Therefore, I abandoned you to Shishak.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:6
Israel’s leaders and the king humbled themselves. “The LORD is righteous,” they said. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:7
The LORD saw their humility and told Shemaiah, “They humbled themselves. I will not destroy them. I will soon deliver them. I will not pour out My wrath on Jerusalem through Shishak. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:8
But they will serve him. They will learn the difference between serving Me and serving other lands’ kings.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:9
Shishak attacked Jerusalem and took the LORD’s house treasures and the palace treasures. He took everything, including Solomon’s gold shields. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:10
Rehoboam replaced them with bronze shields. He entrusted them to the guard captains serving at the palace entrance. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:11
Whenever he entered the LORD’s house, guards accompanied him carrying the shields. Afterward, they returned them to the guardroom. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:12
Because Rehoboam humbled himself, the LORD’s anger turned from him. He did not destroy him completely. Conditions in Judah were good. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:13
Rehoboam established himself as king in Jerusalem. He became king at forty-one and reigned seventeen years there. The LORD chose Jerusalem from Israel’s tribes for His Name. His mother was Naamah the Ammonite. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:14
He did evil because he did not determine in his heart to seek the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:15
His deeds, from first to last, are in Shemaiah the Prophet’s and Iddo the Seer’s records concerning genealogies. Rehoboam and Jeroboam fought throughout their days. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 12:16
Rehoboam rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. His son Abijah became king. 📖
