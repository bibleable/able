## 2 Chronicles 6:1
Solomon declared, “The LORD said He would live in the thick cloud. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:2
I have built You a magnificent house, a home forever.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:3
The king turned and blessed all Israel’s assembly while they stood. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:4
He said, “Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God! His hand fulfilled what His mouth told my father David. He said, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:5
‘Since bringing My people from Egypt, I chose no Israelite tribal city for a house bearing My Name. I chose no ruler for My people Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:6
Now I choose Jerusalem for My Name and David to rule My people Israel.’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:7
My father David wanted to build a house for the Name of the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:8
The LORD told him, ‘You did well to desire a house for My Name. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:9
But you will not build it. Your own son will build it for My Name.’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:10
The LORD fulfilled His word. I succeeded my father David on Israel’s throne, as promised. I built the house for the Name of the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:11
I placed the ark there. It contains the LORD’s covenant with Israel.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:12
Solomon stood before the LORD’s altar in front of Israel’s whole assembly. He spread his hands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:13
He made a bronze platform five cubits long, five wide, and three high in the courtyard’s center. He stood on it, then knelt before everyone. He spread his hands toward heaven. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 6:14
He said, “LORD, Israel’s God, no God in heaven or earth is like You. You keep Your covenant of loving devotion with servants who walk before You wholeheartedly. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:15
You kept Your promise to Your servant, my father David. Today Your hand fulfilled what Your mouth spoke. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:16
LORD, Israel’s God, keep Your promise to David. You said, ‘Your descendants must carefully follow My law as you walked before Me. If they do, you will always have a man on Israel’s throne before Me.’ 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:17
LORD, Israel’s God, confirm Your promise to Your servant David. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:18
Will God truly live with people on earth? Even the highest heavens cannot contain You. Much less can this temple I built. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:19
Yet regard Your servant’s prayer and plea, LORD my God. Hear the cry and prayer I offer before You. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:20
Keep Your eyes on this temple day and night, the place You promised to put Your Name. Hear Your servant’s prayer toward this place. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:21
Hear Your servant and Your people Israel when they plead toward this place. Hear from heaven, Your home, and forgive. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:22
A man may sin against his neighbor and be required to swear an oath. He may come before Your altar in this temple to swear it. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:23
Then hear from heaven and act. Judge Your servants. Condemn the wicked by bringing their deeds on their own heads. Declare the righteous innocent and reward their righteousness. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:24
Your people Israel may suffer defeat because they sinned against You. They may return, confess Your name, and pray and plead before You in this temple. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:25
Then hear from heaven and forgive their sin. Restore them to the land You gave them and their fathers. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:26
The sky may close without rain because Your people sinned against You. They may pray toward this place and confess Your name. They may turn from sin because You afflicted them. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:27
Then hear from heaven and forgive Your servants, Your people Israel. Teach them the good path to walk. Send rain on the land You gave them as an inheritance. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:28
Famine, plague, blight, mildew, locusts, or grasshoppers may strike the land. Enemies may besiege their towns. Whatever plague or sickness comes, 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:29
Your people Israel may pray or plead. Each knows his own suffering and spreads his hands toward this temple. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:30
Hear them from heaven, Your home. Forgive and repay each according to all his ways. You know his heart. You alone know human hearts. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:31
Then they can fear You and walk in Your ways throughout life in the land You gave our fathers. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:32
A foreigner outside Israel may come from far away because of Your great name, mighty hand, and outstretched arm. He may pray toward this temple. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:33
Then hear from heaven, Your home, and grant all he asks. All earth’s peoples can know Your name and fear You like Israel. They can know this house I built bears Your Name. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:34
Your people may fight enemies wherever You send them. They may pray toward Your chosen city and the house I built for Your Name. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:35
Then hear their prayer and plea from heaven. Uphold their cause. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:36
They may sin against You, because everyone sins. You may become angry and hand them to an enemy who takes them captive, far or near. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:37
They may come to their senses and repent in their captors’ land. They may plead, ‘We sinned and did wrong. We acted wickedly.’ 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:38
They may return to You with their whole heart and soul in their enemies’ land. They may pray toward the land You gave their fathers, Your chosen city, and the house I built for Your Name. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:39
Then hear their prayer and pleas from heaven, Your home, and uphold their cause. Forgive Your people’s sin against You. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:40
My God, open Your eyes and listen attentively to prayer in this place. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:41
Rise, LORD God, and enter Your resting place with the ark of Your might. Clothe Your priests with salvation. Let Your godly ones rejoice in goodness. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 6:42
LORD God, do not reject Your anointed one. Remember Your loving devotion to Your servant David.” 🙏
