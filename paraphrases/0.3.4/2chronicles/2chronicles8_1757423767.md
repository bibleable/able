## 2 Chronicles 8:1
Solomon spent twenty years building the LORD’s house and his own palace. Afterward, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:2
he rebuilt the towns Hiram gave him and settled Israelites there. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:3
He went to Hamath-zobah and captured it. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:4
He built Tadmor in the wilderness and all the storage towns in Hamath. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:5
He rebuilt Upper and Lower Beth-horon as fortified towns with walls, gates, and bars. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:6
He rebuilt Baalath and all his storage towns and towns for chariots and horses. He built whatever he desired in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his territory. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:7
Some Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites remained. They were not Israelites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:8
Solomon forced their descendants, whom Israel had not destroyed, into labor. They remain forced laborers to this day. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:9
He did not force Israelites into slave labor. They served as soldiers, leaders of his captains, and commanders of chariots and cavalry. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:10
They were also Solomon’s chief officers, 250 supervisors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:11
Solomon moved Pharaoh’s daughter from the City of David to the palace he built for her. He said, “My wife must not live in David king of Israel’s house. The places the LORD’s ark entered are holy.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:12
He offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar he built before the portico. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:13
He fulfilled the daily offering requirements Moses commanded for Sabbaths, New Moons, and three annual feasts. These were the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Tabernacles. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:14
Following his father David’s ordinances, he assigned priestly divisions to service. He assigned Levites to praise and serve before the priests according to daily requirements. He assigned gatekeeper divisions at each gate, as David the man of God commanded. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:15
They obeyed every royal command concerning priests and Levites, including treasury matters. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:16
All Solomon’s work was completed, from laying the LORD’s house foundation until finishing it. Thus the LORD’s house was complete. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:17
Solomon went to Ezion-geber and Eloth on Edom’s coast. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 8:18
Hiram sent ships captained by his servants with experienced crews. They sailed with Solomon’s servants to Ophir. They brought King Solomon 450 talents of gold from there. 📖
