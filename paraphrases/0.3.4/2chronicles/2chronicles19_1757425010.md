## 2 Chronicles 19:1
Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned safely to his Jerusalem home. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:2
Jehu, son of Hanani the seer, confronted him. “Should you help the wicked and love those hating the LORD? His wrath rests on you because of this. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:3
But there is good in you. You removed the Asherah poles and determined to seek God.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:4
Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem. Again he visited his people, from Beersheba to Ephraim’s hills, and brought them back to the LORD, their fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:5
He appointed judges in every fortified town of Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:6
He told them, “Consider your actions carefully. You judge for the LORD, rather than people. He is with you in judgment. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:7
Fear Him and act carefully. The LORD our God is never unjust or partial and accepts no bribes.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:8
In Jerusalem, he appointed Levites, priests, and Israelite family heads to judge for the LORD and settle disputes. They lived in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:9
He commanded, “Serve faithfully and wholeheartedly, fearing the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:10
Warn your brothers in every dispute brought from their towns. Cases may involve bloodshed or violations of law, commands, statutes, or ordinances. Warn them so they avoid guilt before the LORD and wrath does not fall on you and them. Do this, and you will bear no guilt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 19:11
Chief priest Amariah supervises you in the LORD’s matters. Zebadiah son of Ishmael, Judah’s ruler, supervises the king’s matters. Levites serve as officers before you. Act firmly. May the LORD be with the upright!” 📖
