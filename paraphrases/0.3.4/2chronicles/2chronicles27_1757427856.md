## 2 Chronicles 27:1
Jotham became king at twenty-five and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jerushah daughter of Zadok. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:2
He did what the LORD saw as right, like his father Uzziah. He did not enter the LORD’s temple. But the people remained corrupt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:3
He rebuilt the Upper Gate of the LORD’s house and worked extensively on Ophel’s wall. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:4
He built towns in Judah’s hills and fortresses and towers in the forests. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:5
He defeated the Ammonite king. That year, they paid a hundred silver talents, ten thousand cors of wheat, and ten thousand cors of barley. They paid the same in the second and third years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:6
He grew powerful because he ordered his ways before the LORD his God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:7
His other deeds, wars, and ways are in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:8
He became king at twenty-five and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 27:9
He rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. His son Ahaz became king. 📖
