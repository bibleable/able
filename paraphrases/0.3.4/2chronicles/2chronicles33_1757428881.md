## 2 Chronicles 33:1
Manasseh became king at twelve and reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:2
He did evil before the LORD, copying the detestable nations He drove out before Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:3
He rebuilt the high places his father Hezekiah demolished. He built altars for the Baals and Asherah poles. He worshiped and served heaven’s whole host. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:4
He built altars in the LORD’s house. The LORD had said, “My Name will remain in Jerusalem forever.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:5
He built altars for heaven’s whole host in both temple courtyards. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:6
He sacrificed his sons in fire in the Valley of Ben-hinnom. He practiced sorcery, divination, and witchcraft and consulted mediums and spiritists. His great evil angered the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:7
He placed his carved idol in God’s house. God told David and Solomon, “I establish My Name forever in this temple and Jerusalem, chosen from all Israel’s tribes. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:8
If Israel carefully obeys everything I commanded through Moses, all laws, statutes, and judgments, I will not uproot them again. They can remain in the land I assigned your fathers.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:9
Manasseh misled Judah and Jerusalem. They did worse evil than the nations the LORD destroyed before Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:10
He spoke to Manasseh and his people. They refused to listen. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:11
The LORD sent Assyria’s military commanders against them. They captured Manasseh, put a hook in his nose, and bound him with bronze shackles. They took him to Babylon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:12
In distress, he sought favor from the LORD his God. He humbled himself earnestly before his fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:13
The LORD heard his prayer and accepted his plea. He restored him to Jerusalem and his kingdom. Manasseh knew the LORD is God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:14
He rebuilt the City of David’s outer wall from west of Gihon in the valley to the Fish Gate. He extended it around Ophel and raised it greatly. He stationed commanders in all Judah’s fortified towns. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:15
He removed foreign gods and the idol from the LORD’s house. He removed his altars from the temple mount and Jerusalem, dumping them outside town. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:16
He restored the LORD’s altar and offered peace and thank offerings. He told Judah to serve the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:17
People still sacrificed at high places, but only to the LORD their God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:18
His other deeds and prayer, and the seers’ words to him in the name of the LORD, Israel’s God, are recorded. They are in the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:19
His prayer, God’s acceptance, his sins, and his unfaithfulness are in the Records of the Seers. The record lists where he built high places and set up Asherah poles and idols before he humbled himself. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:20
He rested with his fathers and was buried at his palace. His son Amon became king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:21
Amon became king at twenty-two and reigned two years in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:22
He did evil before the LORD like Manasseh. He served and sacrificed to all his father’s idols. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:23
Unlike his father, he did not humble himself before the LORD. He increased his guilt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:24
His servants conspired and killed him in his palace. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 33:25
The people killed all King Amon’s conspirators. They made his son Josiah king. 📖
