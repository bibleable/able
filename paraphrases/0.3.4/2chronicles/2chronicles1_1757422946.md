## 2 Chronicles 1:1
Solomon son of David secured his kingdom. The LORD his God was with him and made him very great. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:2
He spoke to all Israel: commanders of thousands and hundreds, judges, and every leader, the heads of families. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:3
He and the whole assembly went to Gibeon’s high place, where God’s Tent of Meeting stood. Moses, the LORD’s servant, made it in the wilderness. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:4
David had brought God’s ark from Kiriath-jearim to a place he prepared in Jerusalem. He pitched a tent for it there. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:5
The bronze altar made by Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur stood before the LORD’s tabernacle in Gibeon. Solomon and the assembly sought Him there. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:6
Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings before the LORD on the bronze altar at the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:7
God appeared to him that night. “Ask, and I will give it to you!” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:8
Solomon replied, “You showed great loving devotion to my father David. You made me king in his place. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:9
LORD God, fulfill Your promise to my father David. You made me king over people as numerous as earth’s dust. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 1:10
Give me wisdom and knowledge to lead them. Who can govern this great people of Yours?” 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 1:11
God said, “Your heart sought wisdom and knowledge to govern My people, whom I made you king over. You did not ask for riches, wealth, honor, your enemies’ deaths, or even long life. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:12
Therefore, I grant you wisdom and knowledge. I will also give riches, wealth, and honor unlike those of any king before or after you.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:13
Solomon left the high place at Gibeon, before the Tent of Meeting, and returned to Jerusalem. He reigned over Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:14
He accumulated 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horses. He stationed them in chariot towns and with him in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:15
He made silver and gold as common as stones in Jerusalem. Cedars became as abundant as sycamores in the foothills. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:16
His horses were imported from Egypt and Kue. Royal merchants bought them from Kue. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 1:17
A chariot imported from Egypt cost six hundred silver shekels, and a horse cost one hundred and fifty. They also exported them to all the Hittite kings and Aram’s kings. 📖
