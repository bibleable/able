## 2 Chronicles 36:1
The people made Jehoahaz son of Josiah king in Jerusalem instead of his father. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:2
He became king at twenty-three and reigned three months in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:3
Egypt’s king dethroned him in Jerusalem and imposed a hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold on Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:4
Neco made Jehoahaz’s brother Eliakim king of Judah and Jerusalem and renamed him Jehoiakim. He took Jehoahaz to Egypt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:5
Jehoiakim became king at twenty-five and reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. He did evil before the LORD his God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:6
Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon attacked and shackled him in bronze to take him to Babylon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:7
He took some of the LORD’s house articles to his own temple in Babylon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:8
Jehoiakim’s other deeds, detestable acts, and charges against him are in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. His son Jehoiachin became king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:9
Jehoiachin became king at eighteen and reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem. He did evil before the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:10
In spring, Nebuchadnezzar summoned Jehoiachin and brought him and the LORD’s house valuables to Babylon. He made Jehoiachin’s relative Zedekiah king of Judah and Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:11
Zedekiah became king at twenty-one and reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:12
He did evil before the LORD his God. He refused humility before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke for the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:13
He rebelled against Nebuchadnezzar, who made him swear by God. He became stubborn and hardened his heart against returning to the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:14
All the leaders of the priests and the people multiplied unfaithful acts. They copied all the nations’ detestable practices and defiled the LORD’s house, which He dedicated in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:15
The LORD, their fathers’ God, repeatedly sent messengers because He had compassion on His people and His home. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:16
They mocked God’s messengers, despised His words, and ridiculed His prophets. His wrath against His people grew beyond remedy. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:17
He brought the Chaldean king against them. The king killed young men with the sword in the sanctuary. He spared neither young men nor young women, neither old nor infirm. God handed everyone to Nebuchadnezzar. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:18
He took everything to Babylon: all God’s house articles, large and small, and the treasures of the temple, king, and officials. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:19
Chaldeans burned God’s house and broke Jerusalem’s wall. They burned all palaces and destroyed every valuable article. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:20
Nebuchadnezzar exiled those spared by the sword to Babylon. They served him and his sons until Persia’s kingdom rose. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:21
The land enjoyed Sabbath rest throughout its desolation until seventy years ended. This fulfilled the LORD’s word through Jeremiah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:22
In Cyrus king of Persia’s first year, the LORD stirred his spirit to fulfill His word through Jeremiah. Cyrus sent a written proclamation throughout his kingdom: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 36:23
“Cyrus king of Persia says, ‘The LORD, heaven’s God, gave me all earth’s kingdoms. He appointed me to build His house in Jerusalem in Judah. Whoever belongs to His people, may the LORD his God be with him. Let him go up.’” 📖
