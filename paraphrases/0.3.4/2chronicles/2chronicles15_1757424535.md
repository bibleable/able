## 2 Chronicles 15:1
God’s Spirit came on Azariah son of Oded. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:2
He met Asa, saying, “Listen, Asa, Judah, and Benjamin. The LORD is with you when you are with Him. If you seek Him, you will find Him. If you abandon Him, He will abandon you. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:3
Israel spent many years without the true God, a teaching priest, or the law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:4
In distress, they returned to the LORD, Israel’s God. They sought and found Him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:5
Travelers were unsafe then, because the lands’ inhabitants had many conflicts. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:6
Nation crushed nation, and town crushed town. God afflicted them with every kind of trouble. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:7
Be strong and do not lose courage. Your work will be rewarded.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:8
Asa heard these words and Azariah son of Oded the prophet’s message and took courage. He removed detestable idols from all Judah, Benjamin, and the towns he captured in Ephraim’s hills. He restored the LORD’s altar before the temple’s portico. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:9
He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, including Ephraimites, Manassites, and Simeonites settled among them. Many came from Israel because they saw the LORD his God was with him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:10
They gathered in Jerusalem in the third month of Asa’s fifteenth year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:11
They sacrificed seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep to the LORD from their plunder. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:12
They covenanted to seek the LORD, their fathers’ God, with their whole heart and soul. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:13
Anyone refusing to seek the LORD, Israel’s God, would be put to death, whether young or old, male or female. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:14
They swore to Him loudly, with shouts, trumpets, and rams’ horns. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:15
All Judah rejoiced because they swore wholeheartedly. They sought Him earnestly and found Him. The LORD gave them rest on every side. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:16
Asa removed his grandmother Maacah as queen mother for making a detestable Asherah pole. He cut it down, crushed it, and burned it in the Kidron Valley. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:17
Israel’s high places remained. But Asa’s heart was fully devoted all his life. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:18
He brought into God’s house the silver, gold, and articles he and his father dedicated. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 15:19
There was no war until Asa’s thirty-fifth year. 📖
