## 2 Chronicles 16:1
In Asa’s thirty-sixth year, Baasha king of Israel attacked Judah. He fortified Ramah to block anyone entering or leaving Asa king of Judah’s territory. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:2
Asa took silver and gold from the LORD’s house and palace treasuries. He sent them to Ben-hadad king of Aram in Damascus with this message: 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:3
“Let us make a treaty like our fathers had. I send silver and gold. Break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, so he withdraws from me.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:4
Ben-hadad agreed. He sent army commanders against Israel’s towns. They captured Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all Naphtali’s storage towns. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:5
Baasha heard and stopped fortifying Ramah. He abandoned the work. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:6
Asa gathered all Judah’s men. They took Ramah’s stones and Baasha’s building timber. Asa used them to strengthen Geba and Mizpah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:7
Hanani the seer told King Asa of Judah, “You relied on Aram’s king instead of the LORD your God. Therefore, Aram’s army escaped your hand. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:8
Did the Cushites and Libyans not have a huge army with many chariots and horsemen? You relied on the LORD, and He gave them into your hand. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:9
His eyes search all earth to show His strength for those wholeheartedly devoted to Him. You acted foolishly. From now on, you will have war.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:10
Asa became furious and imprisoned the seer. He also oppressed some people then. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:11
His deeds, from beginning to end, are in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:12
In his thirty-ninth year, Asa’s feet became diseased. The disease grew severe. Yet he sought only physicians, never the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:13
He died in his forty-first year and rested with his fathers. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 16:14
He was buried in his own prepared tomb in the City of David. They laid him on a bier filled with spices and blended perfumes. They made a great fire to honor him. 📖
