## 2 Chronicles 20:1
Moabites, Ammonites, and some Meunites came to fight Jehoshaphat. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:2
Men told him, “A huge army comes from Edom beyond the Sea. They have reached Hazazon-tamar,” which is En-gedi. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:3
Alarmed, he determined to seek the LORD. He proclaimed a fast throughout Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:4
Judah gathered to seek Him. People came from all Judah’s towns. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:5
Jehoshaphat stood before Judah’s and Jerusalem’s assembly in the LORD’s house, in front of the new courtyard. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:6
He said, “LORD, our fathers’ God, are You not heaven’s God and ruler of every nation’s kingdom? Your hand holds power and might. No one can withstand You. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 20:7
Did You not drive out this land’s inhabitants before Israel? You gave it forever to the descendants of Your friend Abraham. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 20:8
They lived here and built a sanctuary for Your Name. They said, 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 20:9
‘If disaster comes, whether sword, judgment, plague, or famine, we will stand before this temple and You. Your Name is here. We will cry in distress, and You will hear and save us.’ 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 20:10
Now Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir attack. You forbade Israel to invade them when leaving Egypt. Israel turned away and did not destroy them. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 20:11
They repay us by trying to drive us from the inheritance You gave us. 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 20:12
Our God, will You not judge them? We are powerless against this huge army. We do not know what to do. Our eyes are on You.” 🙏

## 2 Chronicles 20:13
All Judah’s men stood before the LORD with their wives, children, and little ones. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:14
Jahaziel was the son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah. He was a Levite descended from Asaph. The LORD’s Spirit came on him as he stood among the assembly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:15
He said, “Listen, Judah, Jerusalem, and King Jehoshaphat! The LORD says, ‘Do not fear or lose courage before this army. The battle belongs to God, rather than you. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:16
March against them tomorrow. They will come up the Ascent of Ziz. Find them at the valley’s end facing the Wilderness of Jeruel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:17
You need not fight. Stand in position and watch the LORD save you, Judah and Jerusalem. Do not fear or lose courage. Face them tomorrow. The LORD is with you.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:18
Jehoshaphat bowed facedown. All Judah and Jerusalem bowed before the LORD and worshiped. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:19
Kohathite and Korahite Levites stood and praised the LORD, Israel’s God, shouting loudly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:20
Early next morning, they left for the Wilderness of Tekoa. Jehoshaphat said, “Judah and Jerusalem, listen! Trust in the LORD your God, and you will be upheld. Believe His prophets, and you will succeed.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:21
He consulted the people and appointed singers to praise the LORD’s splendid holiness ahead of the army. They sang, “Thank the LORD! His loving devotion lasts forever.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:22
As soon as they began shouting praise, the LORD set ambushes against Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir’s men attacking Judah. They were defeated. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:23
The Ammonites and Moabites destroyed Mount Seir’s inhabitants. Afterward, they helped destroy one another. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:24
Judah reached a place overlooking the wilderness and looked for the army. Only dead bodies remained. No one escaped. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:25
Jehoshaphat and his people found more goods and valuables on the bodies than they could carry. They gathered plunder for three days because there was so much. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:26
On the fourth day, they gathered in the Valley of Beracah and blessed the LORD. Therefore, it is called the Valley of Beracah to this day. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:27
Jehoshaphat led Judah’s and Jerusalem’s men home joyfully. The LORD made them rejoice over their enemies. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:28
They entered Jerusalem and the LORD’s house with harps, lyres, and trumpets. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:29
All the kingdoms feared God when they heard He fought Israel’s enemies. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:30
Jehoshaphat’s kingdom had peace. His God gave him rest on every side. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:31
He became Judah’s king at thirty-five and reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:32
He followed his father Asa’s way without turning aside. He did what the LORD saw as right. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:33
The high places remained. The people had not yet determined to seek their fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:34
His other deeds, from beginning to end, are in the Chronicles of Jehu son of Hanani. These are recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:35
Later, he allied with Ahaziah king of Israel, who acted wickedly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:36
They agreed to build ships for Tarshish. They built them at Ezion-geber. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 20:37
Eliezer son of Dodavahu, from Mareshah, prophesied, “Because you allied with Ahaziah, the LORD destroyed your works.” The ships were wrecked and could not sail to Tarshish. 📖
