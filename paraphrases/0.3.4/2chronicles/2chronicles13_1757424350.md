## 2 Chronicles 13:1
Abijah became Judah’s king in Jeroboam’s eighteenth year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:2
He reigned three years in Jerusalem. His mother was Micaiah daughter of Uriel, from Gibeah. Abijah and Jeroboam were at war. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:3
Abijah marched with 400,000 chosen soldiers. Jeroboam faced him with 800,000 chosen, mighty warriors. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:4
Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim in Ephraim’s hills. He said, “Jeroboam and all Israel, listen! 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:5
Do you not know Israel’s God, the LORD, gave David and his descendants Israel’s kingship forever through a covenant of salt? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:6
Yet Jeroboam son of Nebat, servant of Solomon son of David, rebelled against his master. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:7
Worthless, wicked men joined him against Rehoboam son of Solomon. Rehoboam was young, inexperienced, and unable to resist. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:8
Now you think you can resist the LORD’s kingdom, held by David’s descendants. You have a huge army and Jeroboam’s gold calves as your gods. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:9
Did you not drive out the LORD’s priests, Aaron’s sons, and the Levites? You made priests like other nations do. Anyone bringing a young bull and seven rams for dedication can become a priest of things that are not gods. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:10
The LORD is our God, and we have not abandoned Him. His serving priests are Aaron’s sons. The Levites perform their duties. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:11
They offer burnt offerings and fragrant incense each morning and evening. They place showbread in rows on the ceremonially clean table. Each evening, they light the gold lampstand’s lamps. We fulfill our God’s requirements, but you abandoned Him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:12
God Himself leads us. His priests sound their trumpets’ battle call against you. Israel, do not fight the LORD, your fathers’ God. You will fail.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:13
Jeroboam sent troops to ambush Judah from behind. His army faced them in front, with the ambush behind. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:14
Judah turned and saw enemies before and behind them. They cried to the LORD. The priests sounded trumpets. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:15
Judah raised the battle cry. God defeated Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:16
Israel fled. God gave them into Judah’s hands. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:17
Abijah and his people struck a great blow. They killed 500,000 chosen Israelite men. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:18
Israel was subdued. Judah won because they relied on the LORD, their fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:19
Abijah chased Jeroboam and captured Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron with their villages. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:20
Jeroboam never regained power during Abijah’s days. The LORD struck him, and he died. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:21
Abijah grew strong. He married fourteen wives and fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 13:22
His other deeds, ways, and words are in the Treatise of the Prophet Iddo. 📖
