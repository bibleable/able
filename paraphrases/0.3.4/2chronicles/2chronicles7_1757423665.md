## 2 Chronicles 7:1
When Solomon finished praying, fire descended from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and sacrifices. The LORD’s glory filled the temple. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:2
The priests could not enter the LORD’s house because His glory filled it. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:3
Israel saw the descending fire and the LORD’s glory above the temple. They bowed facedown on the pavement, worshiped, and thanked Him. “He is good. His loving devotion lasts forever.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:4
The king and everyone offered sacrifices before the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:5
Solomon sacrificed 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep. Thus the king and all the people dedicated God’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:6
Priests and Levites stood at their posts. The Levites used the LORD’s instruments that David made and used for thankful praise: “His loving devotion lasts forever.” Opposite them, priests sounded trumpets while all Israel stood. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:7
Solomon dedicated the courtyard’s center before the LORD’s house. He offered burnt offerings and peace offerings’ fat there. His bronze altar could not hold all those offerings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:8
Solomon and all Israel celebrated the feast seven days. This huge assembly came from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:9
They held a solemn assembly on the eighth day. The altar’s dedication lasted seven days, followed by seven days of feasting. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:10
Solomon sent everyone home on the seventh month’s twenty-third day. They rejoiced in all the good the LORD did for David, Solomon, and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:11
Solomon finished the LORD’s house and his palace. He successfully completed everything he desired for both. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:12
The LORD appeared to him at night. “I heard your prayer and chose this place as My house for sacrifices. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:13
I may close the sky without rain, command locusts to devour the land, or send plague among My people. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:14
If this happens, this promise applies to My people who bear My name. If they humble themselves, pray, seek My face, and turn from wicked ways, I will hear from heaven. I will forgive their sin and heal their land. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:15
My eyes will be open, and My ears will attend to prayers offered here. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:16
I chose and dedicated this temple for My Name to remain forever. My eyes and heart will be here always. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:17
If you walk before Me like your father David, doing all I command and keeping My statutes and ordinances, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:18
I will establish your throne. I covenanted with your father David, saying, ‘You will always have a man to rule Israel.’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:19
But you may turn away, abandon My statutes and commands, and serve and worship other gods. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:20
If so, I will uproot Israel from the land I gave them. I will banish this temple, dedicated for My Name, from My presence. I will make it an object of scorn and ridicule among all peoples. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:21
This temple will become rubble. Everyone passing will be horrified and ask, ‘Why did the LORD do this to the land and temple?’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 7:22
Others will answer, ‘They abandoned the LORD, their fathers’ God, who brought them from Egypt. They embraced, worshiped, and served other gods. Therefore, He brought all this disaster.’” 📖
