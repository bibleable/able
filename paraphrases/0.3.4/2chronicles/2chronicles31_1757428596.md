## 2 Chronicles 31:1
Afterward, the Israelites present visited Judah’s towns. They smashed sacred pillars, cut Asherah poles, and destroyed all high places and altars throughout Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh. Then each returned home to his property. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:2
Hezekiah restored priestly and Levitical divisions according to their duties. They offered burnt and peace offerings, ministered, gave thanks, and sang praises at the LORD’s home gates. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:3
He provided from his property for regular morning and evening burnt offerings. He also provided for Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts according to the LORD’s Law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:4
He commanded Jerusalem to support priests and Levites so they could devote themselves to the LORD’s Law. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:5
Israel immediately brought generous first produce of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and everything from the fields. They brought abundant tithes of everything. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:6
Israelites and Judahites in Judah’s towns brought tithes of herds, flocks, and holy things dedicated to the LORD their God. They piled them in great heaps. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:7
They began the heaps in the third month and finished in the seventh. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:8
Hezekiah and his officials saw the heaps and blessed the LORD and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:9
He asked priests and Levites about them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:10
Chief priest Azariah, from Zadok’s house, replied, “Since contributions began, we have eaten enough with plenty remaining. The LORD blessed His people. This abundance is left over.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:11
He ordered storerooms in the LORD’s house. They prepared them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:12
They faithfully brought contributions, tithes, and dedicated gifts. Conaniah the Levite supervised, with his brother Shimei second. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:13
Overseers were Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah. They served under Conaniah and Shimei. King Hezekiah and Azariah, chief official of God’s house, appointed them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:14
Kore son of Imnah, the Levite guarding the East Gate, supervised God’s freewill offerings. He distributed the LORD’s contributions and dedicated gifts. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:15
Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah served under him. They faithfully distributed portions in priestly towns by divisions, to old and young alike. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:16
They also gave portions to registered males aged three or older. This included all entering the LORD’s house for daily service according to their divisions’ duties. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:17
They supplied priests registered by ancestral families and Levites aged twenty or older according to their duties and divisions. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:18
Genealogies included all little ones, wives, sons, and daughters of the whole assembly. They faithfully dedicated themselves as holy. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:19
Named men distributed portions to Aaron’s priestly descendants living on farmland around their towns or in other towns. They supplied every male priest and every genealogically registered Levite. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:20
Hezekiah did this throughout Judah. He did what was good, upright, and true before the LORD his God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 31:21
He worked wholeheartedly in God’s house service, law, and commands to seek his God. He prospered. 📖
