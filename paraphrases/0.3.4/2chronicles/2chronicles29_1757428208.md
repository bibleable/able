## 2 Chronicles 29:1
Hezekiah became king at twenty-five and reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother was Abijah daughter of Zechariah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:2
He did what the LORD saw as right, like his father David. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:3
He opened and repaired the LORD’s house doors in his reign’s first month and year. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:4
He gathered priests and Levites in the eastern square. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:5
“Listen, Levites. Dedicate yourselves and the house of the LORD, your fathers’ God. Remove every impurity from the Holy Place. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:6
Our fathers were unfaithful and did evil before Him. They abandoned Him, turned their faces from His dwelling, and turned their backs on Him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:7
They shut the portico doors and extinguished the lamps. They offered no incense or burnt offerings in the Holy Place of Israel’s God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:8
His wrath fell on Judah and Jerusalem. He made them objects of terror, horror, and scorn, as you see. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:9
Therefore, our fathers died by the sword. Our sons, daughters, and wives are captive. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:10
I intend to covenant with the LORD, Israel’s God, so His fierce anger turns from us. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:11
My sons, do not neglect your duties. He chose you to stand before Him, serve Him, minister, and burn incense.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:12
The Levites began work. Kohathites included Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah. Merarites included Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel. Gershonites included Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:13
Elizaphanites Shimri and Jeuel joined, along with Asaphites Zechariah and Mattaniah, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:14
Hemanites Jehiel and Shimei, and Jeduthunites Shemaiah and Uzziel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:15
They gathered their brothers, dedicated themselves, and entered to cleanse the LORD’s house. They obeyed the king’s command according to the LORD’s words. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:16
Priests cleansed the temple’s interior and brought every unclean thing into the courtyard. Levites carried these to the Kidron Valley. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:17
They began on the first day of the first month and reached the portico on the eighth day. They dedicated the house for eight more days. They finished on the first month’s sixteenth day. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:18
They told Hezekiah, “We cleansed the whole house of the LORD, the burnt offering altar and its utensils, and the showbread table and its utensils. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:19
We prepared and dedicated every article Ahaz discarded in his unfaithfulness while king. They stand before the LORD’s altar.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:20
Early next morning, Hezekiah gathered the town officials and entered the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:21
They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats as a sin offering for the kingdom, sanctuary, and Judah. He ordered Aaron’s priestly descendants to offer them on the LORD’s altar. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:22
They killed the bulls, and priests splashed their blood on the altar. They killed the rams and did the same. They killed the lambs and splashed their blood on the altar. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:23
They brought the sin offering goats to the king and assembly. They laid their hands on them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:24
Priests killed the goats and put their blood on the altar as a sin offering to make atonement for all Israel. The king ordered both burnt and sin offerings for all Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:25
He stationed Levites with cymbals, harps, and lyres in the LORD’s house. David, Gad the king’s seer, and Nathan the prophet commanded this. The command came from the LORD through His prophets. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:26
Levites held David’s instruments. Priests held trumpets. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:27
He ordered the burnt offering on the altar. As it began, the LORD’s song and trumpets sounded with David king of Israel’s instruments. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:28
Everyone worshiped while singers sang and trumpeters played. This continued until the burnt offering ended. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:29
When the offerings ended, the king and all with him bowed and worshiped. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:30
Hezekiah and his officials ordered Levites to praise the LORD using David’s and Asaph the seer’s words. They sang joyfully, bowed, and worshiped. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:31
Hezekiah said, “You are dedicated to the LORD. Bring sacrifices and thank offerings to His house.” The assembly did so. All willing hearts brought burnt offerings. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:32
They brought seventy bulls, a hundred rams, and two hundred lambs as burnt offerings to the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:33
The dedicated offerings totaled six hundred bulls and three thousand sheep. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:34
Too few priests were available to skin all the burnt offerings. Levites helped until the work ended and the priests dedicated themselves. Levites were more diligent in dedicating themselves than the priests. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:35
Burnt offerings were abundant, with peace offerings’ fat and accompanying drink offerings. Thus service in the LORD’s house was established. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 29:36
Hezekiah and everyone rejoiced at what God prepared for them. Everything happened so quickly. 📖
