## 2 Chronicles 21:1
Jehoshaphat rested with his fathers and was buried with them in the City of David. His son Jehoram became king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:2
Jehoram’s brothers were Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah. All were sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:3
Their father gave them abundant silver, gold, precious gifts, and fortified towns in Judah. He gave Jehoram the kingdom because he was firstborn. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:4
Jehoram secured his father’s kingdom. He strengthened himself by killing all his brothers and some Israelite princes with the sword. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:5
He became king at thirty-two and reigned eight years in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:6
He followed Israel’s kings and Ahab’s house. He married Ahab’s daughter and did evil before the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:7
The LORD refused to destroy David’s house because of His covenant with David. He promised to keep a lamp for David and his descendants forever. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:8
Edom rebelled against Judah’s rule during Jehoram’s reign. They appointed their own king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:9
Jehoram entered Edom with his officers and all his chariots. Edomites surrounded him and his chariot commanders. He rose and attacked by night. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:10
Edom remains in rebellion against Judah to this day. Libnah also rebelled then because Jehoram abandoned the LORD, his fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:11
He built high places in Judah’s hills. He caused Jerusalem’s people to prostitute themselves and led Judah astray. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:12
Elijah the prophet sent him a letter. “The LORD, your father David’s God, says, ‘You did not follow your father Jehoshaphat or Asa king of Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:13
You followed Israel’s kings. Like Ahab’s house, you made Judah and Jerusalem prostitute themselves. You killed your brothers, your father’s family, who were better than you. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:14
The LORD will strike your people, sons, wives, and all your possessions with a serious blow. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:15
You will suffer severe bowel disease day after day until your bowels come out.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:16
The LORD stirred the Philistines and Arabs living near the Cushites against Jehoram. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:17
They invaded Judah and took all the palace possessions, his sons, and his wives. Only Jehoahaz, his youngest son, remained. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:18
Afterward, the LORD afflicted him with incurable bowel disease. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:19
It continued daily for two full years. His intestines came out, and he died in severe pain. His people made no fire to honor him, unlike his fathers. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 21:20
He became king at thirty-two and reigned eight years in Jerusalem. No one regretted his death. He was buried in the City of David, outside the kings’ tombs. 📖
