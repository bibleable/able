## 2 Chronicles 10:1
Rehoboam went to Shechem. All Israel had gathered there to make him king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:2
Jeroboam son of Nebat heard and returned from Egypt, where he fled from King Solomon. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:3
They sent for Jeroboam. He and all Israel came to Rehoboam, saying, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:4
“Your father placed a heavy yoke on us. Lighten the hard service and heavy burden he imposed. Then we will serve you.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:5
Rehoboam replied, “Return in three days.” The people left. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:6
He consulted the elders who served his father Solomon during his life. “How should I answer these people?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:7
They replied, “Be kind and please them with kind words. If you do, they will serve you forever.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:8
He rejected their advice. He consulted the young men who grew up with him and served him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:9
He asked, “How should we answer those asking me to lighten my father’s yoke?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:10
They replied, “Tell the people asking you to lighten your father’s heavy yoke, ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist! 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:11
He gave you a heavy yoke. I will make it heavier. He whipped you with whips. I will whip you with scorpions.’” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:12
After three days, Jeroboam and everyone returned. The king had told them to return on the third day. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:13
The king answered harshly. Rehoboam rejected the elders’ advice. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:14
He followed the young men’s advice. “My father made your yoke heavy. I will make it heavier. He whipped you with whips. I will whip you with scorpions.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:15
He refused to listen. God brought this about to fulfill the LORD’s word through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:16
Israel saw the king would not listen. They answered, “What share have we in David, or inheritance in Jesse’s son? Israel, return to your tents! David, look after your own house!” Everyone went home. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:17
Rehoboam still ruled Israelites in Judah’s towns. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:18
Rehoboam sent Hadoram, the forced labor supervisor. Israel stoned him to death. Rehoboam hurried into his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 10:19
Israel remains in rebellion against David’s house to this day. 📖
