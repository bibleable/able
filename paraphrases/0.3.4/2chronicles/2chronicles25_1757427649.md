## 2 Chronicles 25:1
Amaziah became king at twenty-five and reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:2
He did what the LORD saw as right, but not wholeheartedly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:3
Once his kingdom was secure, he executed the servants who murdered his father the king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:4
He spared their sons, obeying the Law in the Book of Moses. The LORD commanded, “Do not execute fathers for children or children for fathers. Each must die for his own sin.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:5
He gathered Judah by families under commanders of thousands and hundreds. He counted Judah’s and Benjamin’s men aged twenty or older. He found 300,000 chosen men fit for military service and armed with spear and shield. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:6
He hired 100,000 mighty Israelite warriors for a hundred talents of silver. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:7
A man of God told him, “King, do not take Israel’s army. The LORD is not with Israel or any Ephraimites. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:8
Even if you fight bravely, God will overthrow you before your enemies. God can help or overthrow.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:9
Amaziah asked, “What about my hundred talents paid to Israel’s troops?” The man replied, “The LORD can give you much more.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:10
He dismissed Ephraim’s troops. Furious with Judah, they returned home very angry. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:11
Amaziah strengthened himself and led his army to the Valley of Salt. He killed 10,000 men of Seir. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:12
Judah captured another 10,000 alive. They took them to a cliff and threw them down. All were dashed to pieces. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:13
The dismissed troops raided Judah’s towns from Samaria to Beth-horon. They killed 3,000 people and took much plunder. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:14
Returning from slaughtering the Edomites, Amaziah brought the Seirites’ gods. He made them his gods, bowed to them, and burned sacrifices. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:15
The LORD became angry and sent a prophet. “Why seek gods that could not save their people from you?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:16
The king interrupted, “Did we appoint you as royal adviser? Stop! Why should you be killed?” The prophet stopped, saying, “God determined to destroy you. You did this and ignored my advice.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:17
After taking counsel, Amaziah king of Judah sent Jehoash king of Israel a message. Jehoash was the son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu. “Come, let us meet face to face.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:18
Jehoash replied, “A Lebanon thistle asked a Lebanon cedar, ‘Give your daughter to my son in marriage.’ A wild beast came through Lebanon and trampled the thistle. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:19
You boast of defeating Edom and have become proud. Stay home. Why invite trouble that destroys you and Judah?” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:20
Amaziah refused to listen. God brought this about to give them into Jehoash’s hands because they sought Edom’s gods. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:21
Jehoash king of Israel advanced against Amaziah king of Judah. They met at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:22
Israel defeated Judah. Each man fled home. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:23
Jehoash captured Amaziah, son of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh and took him to Jerusalem. He demolished four hundred cubits of Jerusalem’s wall, from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:24
He took all temple gold, silver, and articles kept by Obed-edom, and the palace treasures. He took hostages and returned to Samaria. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:25
Amaziah son of Joash, Judah’s king, outlived Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, Israel’s king, by fifteen years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:26
His other deeds, from beginning to end, are in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:27
After Amaziah turned from the LORD, conspirators rose against him in Jerusalem. He fled to Lachish. They sent men after him and killed him there. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 25:28
They brought him back on horses. He was buried with his fathers in the City of Judah. 📖
