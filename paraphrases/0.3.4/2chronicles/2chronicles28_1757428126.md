## 2 Chronicles 28:1
Ahaz became king at twenty and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. Unlike his father David, he did not do what the LORD saw as right. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:2
He followed Israel’s kings and made cast images of the Baals. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:3
He burned incense in the Valley of Ben-hinnom and sacrificed his sons in fire. He copied the detestable nations the LORD drove out before Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:4
He sacrificed and burned incense at high places, on hills, and under every green tree. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:5
The LORD his God handed him to Aram’s king. Aram attacked and took many captives to Damascus. The LORD also handed him to Israel’s king, who struck him hard. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:6
Pekah son of Remaliah killed 120,000 brave men of Judah in one day. This happened because they abandoned the LORD, their fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:7
Zichri, Ephraim’s mighty warrior, killed the king’s son Maaseiah, palace governor Azrikam, and Elkanah, second to the king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:8
Israel took 200,000 captive women, sons, and daughters from their own kin. They brought much plunder to Samaria. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:9
The LORD’s prophet Oded met the returning army at Samaria. “Your fathers’ God handed Judah to you in His wrath. But your murderous rage reaches heaven. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:10
Now you plan to enslave Judah’s and Jerusalem’s men and women. Are you not guilty before the LORD your God too? 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:11
Listen and return your captive kin. The LORD’s fierce anger rests on you.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:12
Ephraimite leaders opposed the returning soldiers. They were Azariah son of Jehohanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:13
They said, “Do not bring captives here. You would add guilt before the LORD to our sins. Our guilt is already great, and fierce anger rests on Israel.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:14
The soldiers left the captives and plunder before the leaders and assembly. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:15
The named men cared for the captives. They used plunder to clothe the naked and provided sandals, food, and drink. They anointed wounds and put the weak on donkeys. They brought them to their brothers at Jericho, the City of Palms. Then they returned to Samaria. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:16
Ahaz requested help from Assyria’s king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:17
Edomites had attacked Judah again and taken captives. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:18
Philistines raided Judah’s foothills and Negev. They captured and occupied Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Soco, Timnah, and Gimzo with their villages. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:19
The LORD humbled Judah because Ahaz king of Israel cast off restraint in Judah and was deeply unfaithful to Him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:20
Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria came but afflicted Ahaz instead of strengthening him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:21
Ahaz gave him a portion from the LORD’s house, the palace, and the princes. It did not help. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:22
In distress, Ahaz became more unfaithful to the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:23
He sacrificed to Damascus’s gods, who defeated him. “Aram’s kings’ gods helped them,” he said. “I will sacrifice so they help me.” These gods ruined him and all Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:24
He gathered God’s house articles and cut them apart. He shut the LORD’s house doors and built altars on every Jerusalem street corner. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:25
He built high places in every Judahite town for incense to other gods. He angered the LORD, his fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:26
His other deeds and ways, from beginning to end, are in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 28:27
He rested with his fathers and was buried in Jerusalem, outside Israel’s kings’ tombs. His son Hezekiah became king. 📖
