## 2 Chronicles 34:1
Josiah became king at eight and reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:2
He did what the LORD saw as right and followed his father David’s ways. He turned neither right nor left. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:3
Still young, he began seeking his father David’s God in his eighth year as king. In his twelfth year, he began cleansing Judah and Jerusalem of high places, Asherah poles, carved idols, and cast images. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:4
Altars of the Baals were demolished before him. He cut apart the incense altars above them. He smashed the Asherah poles, carved idols, and cast images into dust. He scattered it on the graves of those who sacrificed to them. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:5
He burned the priests’ bones on their altars. He cleansed Judah and Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:6
He did the same in Manasseh’s, Ephraim’s, and Simeon’s towns, as far as Naphtali, and surrounding ruins. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:7
Throughout Israel, he demolished altars and Asherah poles, crushed idols to powder, and cut apart incense altars. Then he returned to Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:8
In his eighteenth year, he sent men to repair the house of the LORD his God to cleanse the land and temple. They were Shaphan son of Azaliah, town governor Maaseiah, and recorder Joah son of Joahaz. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:9
They gave high priest Hilkiah the temple money collected by Levitical doorkeepers. It came from Manasseh, Ephraim, all Israel’s remnant, Judah, Benjamin, and Jerusalem. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:10
They gave it to supervisors, who paid the workers repairing the LORD’s house. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:11
They paid carpenters and builders for dressed stone and timber couplings and beams. These repaired buildings Judah’s kings allowed to deteriorate. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:12
Workers served faithfully. Merarites Jahath and Obadiah and Kohathites Zechariah and Meshullam supervised. Other Levites were skilled musicians, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:13
supervising laborers and every task. Some Levites served as secretaries, officers, and gatekeepers. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:14
While they brought out the temple money, Hilkiah the priest found the Book of the Law of the LORD given through Moses. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:15
He told Shaphan the scribe, “I found the Book of the Law in the LORD’s house!” He gave it to him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:16
Shaphan brought it to the king. “Your servants do everything assigned. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:17
They paid the supervisors and workers the money found in the LORD’s house.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:18
He added, “Hilkiah the priest gave me a book.” He read it before the king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:19
The king heard the Law and tore his clothes. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:20
He commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah his servant, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:21
“Consult the LORD for me and Israel’s and Judah’s remnant about this book. His great wrath fell on us because our fathers failed to obey everything written here.” 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:22
Hilkiah and the appointed men consulted Huldah the prophetess in Jerusalem’s Second District. Her husband was Shallum, wardrobe keeper, son of Tokhath son of Hasrah. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:23
She said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘Tell the man who sent you, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:24
I will bring disaster on this place and people according to all the curses read before Judah’s king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:25
They abandoned Me and burned incense to other gods, angering Me with their hands’ work. My wrath will pour on this place and not be quenched.’ 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:26
Tell Judah’s king, who sent you to consult Him, ‘The LORD, Israel’s God, says about the words you heard, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:27
your heart was tender. You humbled yourself before God at His words against this place and people. You humbled yourself before Me, tore your clothes, and wept. Therefore, I heard you, declares the LORD. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:28
I will gather you to your fathers and your grave in peace. You will not see the disaster I bring on this place and people.’” They gave the king her answer. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:29
He summoned all Judah’s and Jerusalem’s elders. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:30
He entered the LORD’s house with Judah, Jerusalem, priests, and Levites, everyone great and small. He read all the Book of the Covenant found there for them to hear. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:31
Standing by the pillar, he covenanted before the LORD to follow Him wholeheartedly and with his whole soul. He promised to keep His commands, decrees, and statutes and fulfill this book’s covenant. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:32
He required everyone in Jerusalem and Benjamin to agree. Jerusalem fulfilled the covenant of their fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 34:33
He removed every detestable thing from Israel’s lands and required everyone to serve the LORD their God. Throughout his reign, they followed the LORD, their fathers’ God. 📖
