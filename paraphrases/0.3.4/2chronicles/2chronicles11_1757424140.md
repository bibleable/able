## 2 Chronicles 11:1
In Jerusalem, Rehoboam gathered 180,000 chosen warriors from Judah and Benjamin. They would fight Israel and restore his kingdom. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:2
The LORD’s word came to Shemaiah the man of God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:3
“Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon, Judah’s king, and all Israelites in Judah and Benjamin, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:4
‘The LORD says, do not fight your brothers. Return home, because this is My doing.’” They obeyed and stopped their advance against Jeroboam. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:5
Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and strengthened Judah’s towns for defense. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:6
He fortified Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:7
Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:8
Gath, Mareshah, Ziph, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:9
Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah, 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:10
Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, fortified towns in Judah and Benjamin. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:11
He strengthened their defenses and stationed officers with food, oil, and wine supplies. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:12
He put shields and spears in every town and strengthened them greatly. Judah and Benjamin belonged to him. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:13
Priests and Levites throughout Israel’s districts supported Rehoboam. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:14
The Levites left their pastures and property for Judah and Jerusalem. Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them as the LORD’s priests. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:15
Jeroboam appointed his own priests for high places, goat demons, and the calf idols he made. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:16
People from every Israelite tribe determined to seek the LORD their God followed the Levites to Jerusalem. They sacrificed to the LORD, their fathers’ God. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:17
They strengthened Judah’s kingdom and supported Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years. They followed David’s and Solomon’s way those three years. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:18
Rehoboam married Mahalath, daughter of Jerimoth son of David and Abihail daughter of Eliab son of Jesse. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:19
She bore him Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:20
Later, he married Maacah daughter of Absalom. She bore Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:21
He loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and concubines. He had eighteen wives, sixty concubines, twenty-eight sons, and sixty daughters. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:22
He made Maacah’s son Abijah chief prince among his brothers. He intended to make him king. 📖

## 2 Chronicles 11:23
He wisely sent some sons throughout Judah’s and Benjamin’s districts and fortified towns. He gave them abundant supplies and sought many wives for them. 📖
