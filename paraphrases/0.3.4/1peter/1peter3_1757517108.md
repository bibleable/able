## 1 Peter 3:1
Wives, in the same way, submit to your husbands. Even if they refuse to believe the word, your behavior can win them over without words. ❤️
## 1 Peter 3:2
They will see your pure behavior and deep respect. 👣
## 1 Peter 3:3
Your beauty must not depend on outward things, such as braided hair, gold jewelry, or fine clothes. ✨
## 1 Peter 3:4
Instead, let it come from your heart’s inner character. The lasting beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit is precious to God. ❤️
## 1 Peter 3:5
The holy women of the past made themselves beautiful this way. They hoped in God and submitted to their husbands. 👣
## 1 Peter 3:6
Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him lord. You are her children if you do right and refuse to give in to fear. 🛡️
## 1 Peter 3:7
Husbands, treat your wives with understanding, like delicate vessels. Honor them as fellow heirs of God’s gracious gift of life. Then nothing will hinder your prayers. ❤️
## 1 Peter 3:8
All of you must think alike and care about each other. Love as brothers, with tender hearts and humility. 🤝
## 1 Peter 3:9
Do not repay evil with evil or insults with insults. Instead, bless others, because God called you to this so that you could inherit a blessing. ❤️
## 1 Peter 3:10
Scripture says, “Anyone who wants to love life and see good days must keep his tongue from evil and his lips from deceit. 🗣️
## 1 Peter 3:11
He must turn from evil and do good. He must seek peace and pursue it. 🕊️
## 1 Peter 3:12
The Lord watches righteous people and listens to their prayers. But He turns His face against those who do evil.” 🙏
## 1 Peter 3:13
Who can harm you if you eagerly do good? 🛡️
## 1 Peter 3:14
Even if you suffer for doing right, you are blessed. “Do not fear what they fear, and do not be shaken.” 💪
## 1 Peter 3:15
In your hearts, honor Christ as holy and as Lord. Always be ready to explain the reason for your hope to anyone who asks. Answer gently and with respect. 🗣️
## 1 Peter 3:16
Keep a clear conscience. Then your good behavior in Christ can put those who slander you to shame. 👣
## 1 Peter 3:17
If God wills it, suffering for doing good is better than suffering for doing evil. ✝️
## 1 Peter 3:18
Christ suffered for sins once for all. He was righteous and suffered for the unrighteous to bring you to God. He was killed in the body but made alive in the Spirit. ✝️
## 1 Peter 3:19
In the Spirit, He also went and preached to the spirits in prison. 📣
## 1 Peter 3:20
They disobeyed long ago, while God waited patiently in Noah’s time as the ark was built. Only a few people, eight souls, were saved through water in the ark. 🌊
## 1 Peter 3:21
This water pictures baptism, which now saves you too through Jesus Christ’s resurrection. Baptism is a pledge of a clear conscience toward God, rather than a removal of dirt from the body. 💧
## 1 Peter 3:22
Jesus Christ has gone into heaven and sits at God’s right hand. Angels, authorities, and powers are under His rule. 👑
