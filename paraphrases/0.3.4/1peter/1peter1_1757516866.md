## 1 Peter 1:1
This letter is from Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ. I write to God’s chosen people, scattered exiles in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia. ✉️
## 1 Peter 1:2
God the Father chose you according to His knowledge beforehand. The Spirit made you holy to obey Jesus Christ and receive the sprinkling of His blood. May grace and peace be yours in abundance. 🕊️
## 1 Peter 1:3
Praise the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! Through His great mercy, He gave us new birth into a living hope. This came through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead. 🌅
## 1 Peter 1:4
He also gave us an inheritance that cannot perish, become unclean, or fade. It is kept in heaven for you. ✨
## 1 Peter 1:5
Through faith, God’s power protects you for salvation. This salvation is ready to be revealed at the last time. 🛡️
## 1 Peter 1:6
You rejoice greatly in this. Yet for a little while, you may now need to suffer grief through various trials. 🌱
## 1 Peter 1:7
These trials test your faith, which is more precious than gold. Even gold purified by fire can perish. Your tested faith can bring praise, glory, and honor when Jesus Christ appears. 🔥
## 1 Peter 1:8
You love Him, though you have not seen Him. You believe in Him, though you do not see Him now. You rejoice with glorious joy that words cannot express. ❤️
## 1 Peter 1:9
You are receiving the goal of your faith: the salvation of your souls. 🌅
## 1 Peter 1:10
The prophets carefully searched and investigated this salvation. They foretold the grace that would come to you. 📖
## 1 Peter 1:11
The Spirit of Christ in them predicted Christ’s sufferings and the glories afterward. They tried to discover the time and circumstances He meant. 💭
## 1 Peter 1:12
They learned that these predictions served you, rather than themselves. Those who preached the good news now announced these things to you through the Holy Spirit sent from heaven. Even angels long to look into them. 🕊️
## 1 Peter 1:13
Prepare your minds for action and keep clear heads. Put all your hope in the grace you will receive when Jesus Christ appears. 🌅
## 1 Peter 1:14
Live as obedient children. Do not follow the desires you had when you did not know the truth. 👣
## 1 Peter 1:15
The One who called you is holy. So be holy in everything you do. ✨
## 1 Peter 1:16
Scripture says, “Be holy, because I am holy.” 📖
## 1 Peter 1:17
You call on a Father who judges everyone’s work fairly. Live with deep respect and fear during your time as foreigners. 🙏
## 1 Peter 1:18
You know that silver, gold, and other things that perish did not buy your freedom. You were freed from the empty way of life passed down by your ancestors. ⛓️
## 1 Peter 1:19
Christ’s precious blood bought your freedom. He was like a lamb without defect or stain. ✝️
## 1 Peter 1:20
He was known before the world’s foundation. He was revealed in these last times for your sake. ✨
## 1 Peter 1:21
Through Him, you believe in God, who raised Him from the dead and gave Him glory. So your faith and hope are in God. 🌅
## 1 Peter 1:22
You purified your souls by obeying the truth, so that you sincerely love your brothers. Love each other deeply from a pure heart. ❤️
## 1 Peter 1:23
You have been born again through seed that cannot perish. This came through God’s living and lasting word, rather than seed that perishes. 🌱
## 1 Peter 1:24
Scripture says, “All people are like grass, and all their glory is like flowers in a field. The grass dries up, and the flowers fall. 🌾
## 1 Peter 1:25
But the Lord’s word stands forever.” This is the word announced to you. 📖
