## 1 Peter 2:1
Get rid of all malice, deceit, hypocrisy, envy, and slander. ⛔
## 1 Peter 2:2
Like newborn babies, desire pure spiritual milk. Through it, you can grow in your salvation. 🌱
## 1 Peter 2:3
You have now tasted that the Lord is good. ❤️
## 1 Peter 2:4
Come to Him, the living stone. People rejected Him, but God chose Him and considers Him precious. 🪨
## 1 Peter 2:5
You too are like living stones, built into a spiritual house. You are to be holy priests who offer spiritual sacrifices that God accepts through Jesus Christ. 🏠
## 1 Peter 2:6
Scripture says, “Look, I place a chosen and precious cornerstone in Zion. Anyone who believes in Him will never be put to shame.” 🪨
## 1 Peter 2:7
This stone is precious to you who believe. But for those who do not believe, “The stone the builders rejected became the cornerstone.” 🪨
## 1 Peter 2:8
He is also “a stone that makes people stumble and a rock that makes them fall.” They stumble because they disobey the word. This is what they were appointed to. ⚠️
## 1 Peter 2:9
But you are a chosen people, royal priests, a holy nation, and God’s own possession. Announce the excellence of Him who called you out of darkness into His wonderful light. ✨
## 1 Peter 2:10
Once you were not a people, but now you are God’s people. Once you had not received mercy, but now you have received it. ❤️
## 1 Peter 2:11
Dear friends, you are foreigners and exiles. I urge you to avoid sinful physical desires, which fight against your soul. 🛡️
## 1 Peter 2:12
Live honorably among the Gentiles. They may accuse you of evil, but they can see your good actions and glorify God on the day He visits us. 👣
## 1 Peter 2:13
For the Lord’s sake, submit to every human institution. Submit to the king as the highest authority. 👑
## 1 Peter 2:14
Also submit to governors whom the king sends to punish wrongdoers and praise those who do right. ⚖️
## 1 Peter 2:15
God wants your good actions to silence the ignorant talk of foolish people. 👣
## 1 Peter 2:16
Live as free people, but do not use freedom to hide evil. Live as God’s servants. 🛡️
## 1 Peter 2:17
Respect everyone. Love the family of believers, fear God, and honor the king. ❤️
## 1 Peter 2:18
Servants, submit to your masters with full respect. Submit to good and gentle masters and also to unreasonable ones. 🛠️
## 1 Peter 2:19
It deserves praise when someone endures the pain of unfair suffering because he is mindful of God. ✝️
## 1 Peter 2:20
What credit do you deserve if you endure a beating for doing wrong? But God approves when you endure suffering for doing good. ⚖️
## 1 Peter 2:21
God called you to this because Christ suffered for you. He left an example for you to follow in His footsteps. 👣
## 1 Peter 2:22
“He committed no sin and spoke no deceit.” 📖
## 1 Peter 2:23
When people insulted Him, He did not insult them back. When He suffered, He made no threats. He entrusted Himself to the One who judges fairly. ✝️
## 1 Peter 2:24
He carried our sins in His body on the tree, so that we could die to sin and live for righteousness. “His wounds have healed you.” ✝️
## 1 Peter 2:25
“You were like sheep that wandered away.” Now you have returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls. 🐑
