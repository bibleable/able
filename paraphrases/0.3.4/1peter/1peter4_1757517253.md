## 1 Peter 4:1
Christ suffered in His body, so arm yourselves with the same resolve. Anyone who has suffered in his body is done with sin. 🛡️
## 1 Peter 4:2
He lives his remaining time on earth for God’s will instead of human passions. 👣
## 1 Peter 4:3
You spent enough time in the past following the Gentiles’ desires. You lived in wild immorality, lust, drunkenness, orgies, wild parties, and detestable idol worship. ⛔
## 1 Peter 4:4
They think it strange that you do not join their flood of reckless behavior. They heap insults on you. ⚠️
## 1 Peter 4:5
But they must answer to the One who is ready to judge the living and the dead. ⚖️
## 1 Peter 4:6
That is why the good news was preached even to those now dead. This was so they could be judged as humans in the body but live according to God in the spirit. 📣
## 1 Peter 4:7
The end of all things is near. Keep clear heads and control yourselves, so that you can pray. 🙏
## 1 Peter 4:8
Above all, love each other deeply. Love covers many sins. ❤️
## 1 Peter 4:9
Welcome each other into your homes without complaints. 🏠
## 1 Peter 4:10
Each of you received a gift. Use it to serve each other, as good managers of God’s grace in its many forms. 🎁
## 1 Peter 4:11
Anyone who speaks must speak as one who delivers God’s words. Anyone who serves must use the strength God supplies. Then God can receive glory in everything through Jesus Christ. May Christ receive glory and power forever and ever. Amen. ✨
## 1 Peter 4:12
Dear friends, do not be surprised by the fiery trial that has come to test you. Do not treat it as something strange. 🔥
## 1 Peter 4:13
Rejoice that you share Christ’s sufferings. Then you can have overflowing joy when His glory appears. 🌅
## 1 Peter 4:14
If people insult you because of Christ’s name, you are blessed. The Spirit of glory and of God rests on you. 🕊️
## 1 Peter 4:15
None of you must suffer as a murderer, thief, wrongdoer, or someone who interferes in other people’s affairs. ⛔
## 1 Peter 4:16
If you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed. Glorify God because you bear that name. ✝️
## 1 Peter 4:17
The time has come for judgment to begin with God’s family. If it begins with us, what will happen to those who disobey God’s good news? ⚖️
## 1 Peter 4:18
Scripture says, “If righteous people are saved with difficulty, what will happen to those who dishonor God and to sinners?” 📖
## 1 Peter 4:19
Those who suffer according to God’s will must entrust their souls to their faithful Creator. They must continue to do good. 🛡️
