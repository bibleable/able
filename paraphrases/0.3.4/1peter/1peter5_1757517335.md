## 1 Peter 5:1
I appeal to the elders among you as a fellow elder and a witness of Christ’s sufferings. I will also share in the glory that will appear. 👥
## 1 Peter 5:2
Be shepherds of God’s flock among you. Watch over them willingly, as God wants, rather than because you are forced. Serve eagerly, rather than from greed. 🐑
## 1 Peter 5:3
Do not dominate those entrusted to you. Be examples to the flock. 👣
## 1 Peter 5:4
When the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive a crown of glory that never fades. 👑
## 1 Peter 5:5
Young men, submit to your elders in the same way. All of you must clothe yourselves with humility toward each other. “God opposes proud people but gives grace to humble people.” ❤️
## 1 Peter 5:6
Humble yourselves under God’s mighty hand. Then He can lift you up at the proper time. 🙏
## 1 Peter 5:7
Give all your worries to Him, because He cares for you. ❤️
## 1 Peter 5:8
Keep a clear head and stay alert. Your enemy the devil walks around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to devour. 🦁
## 1 Peter 5:9
Resist him and stand firm in faith. Remember that your brothers throughout the world face the same kinds of suffering. 🛡️
## 1 Peter 5:10
God, who gives all grace, called you to His eternal glory in Christ. After you suffer for a little while, He Himself will restore you. He will make you secure, strong, and firm. 💪
## 1 Peter 5:11
May He have power forever and ever. Amen. ✨
## 1 Peter 5:12
I wrote this brief letter through Silvanus, whom I consider a faithful brother. I encouraged you and testified that this is God’s true grace. Stand firm in it. 📜
## 1 Peter 5:13
The church in Babylon, chosen together with you, sends greetings. My son Mark also sends greetings. 👋
## 1 Peter 5:14
Greet each other with a kiss of love. May all of you in Christ have peace. 🕊️
