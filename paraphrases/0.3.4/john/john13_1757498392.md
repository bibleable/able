## John 13:1
Before Passover, Jesus knew His hour to return from this world to the Father had come. He loved His own in the world to the end. ❤️
## John 13:2
During supper, the devil had already placed the intent to betray Jesus in the heart of Judas son of Simon Iscariot. ⚠️
## John 13:3
Jesus knew the Father gave everything into His hands. He came from God and was returning to God. 🙏
## John 13:4
He rose, removed His outer clothes, and tied a towel around His waist. 📖
## John 13:5
He poured water into a basin, washed the disciples’ feet, and dried them with His towel. 💧
## John 13:6
Simon Peter asked, “Lord, will You wash my feet?” ❓
## John 13:7
Jesus answered, “You do not understand now, but will later.” 💬
## John 13:8
Peter refused. Jesus said, “Unless I wash you, you have no part with Me.” 💧
## John 13:9
Simon Peter answered, “Lord, wash my hands and head too!” 🙏
## John 13:10
Jesus said, “Someone bathed needs only his feet washed to be completely clean. You are clean, but not all of you.” 💧
## John 13:11
He knew His betrayer, so He said not all were clean. ⚠️
## John 13:12
Jesus dressed again and reclined after washing their feet. He asked, “Do you understand what I did? ❓
## John 13:13
You rightly call Me Teacher and Lord. I am both. 🙏
## John 13:14
If your Lord and Teacher washed your feet, wash each other’s feet. 🤝
## John 13:15
Follow My example and do what I did for you. ❤️
## John 13:16
I tell you the truth. No servant exceeds his master, and no messenger exceeds his sender. 📖
## John 13:17
If you know these things, you will be blessed if you do them. 🙏
## John 13:18
I do not mean everyone. I know those I chose. Scripture must be fulfilled: ‘My bread sharer raised his heel against Me.’ 📖
## John 13:19
I tell you beforehand so you will believe I am He when it happens. 🙏
## John 13:20
I tell you the truth. Whoever receives the one I send receives Me. Receiving Me means receiving the One who sent Me.” 🤝
## John 13:21
Troubled in spirit, Jesus testified, “I tell you the truth. One of you will betray Me.” 💔
## John 13:22
The disciples looked at each other, unsure whom He meant. 👥
## John 13:23
The disciple Jesus loved reclined beside Him. ❤️
## John 13:24
Simon Peter signaled him to ask who Jesus meant. 💬
## John 13:25
Leaning against Jesus, he asked who it was. ❓
## John 13:26
Jesus said, “The one receiving this dipped morsel.” He dipped it and gave it to Judas son of Simon Iscariot. 🍞
## John 13:27
Satan entered Judas after he received it. Jesus said, “Do what you intend quickly.” ⚠️
## John 13:28
Nobody at the table understood this command. 💭
## John 13:29
Judas kept the money bag. Some thought Jesus sent him to buy feast supplies or help the poor. 🪙
## John 13:30
Judas left immediately into the night after receiving the morsel. 🌌
## John 13:31
When Judas had gone out, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man is glorified, and God is glorified in Him. ✨
## John 13:32
If God is glorified in Him, God will glorify the Son in Himself at once. 🙏
## John 13:33
Little children, I remain briefly. You will seek Me. As I told the Jews, you cannot come where I go. 💬
## John 13:34
My new command is to love each other as I loved you. ❤️
## John 13:35
If you love each other, everyone will know you are My disciples.” 🤝
## John 13:36
Simon Peter asked where He was going. Jesus answered, “You cannot follow now, but will later.” 🚶
## John 13:37
Peter asked, “Why not now? I will die for You.” 💬
## John 13:38
Jesus asked, “Will you? I tell you the truth: you will deny Me three times before the rooster crows. 🐓
