## John 10:1
“I tell you the truth. Anyone entering the sheepfold without the gate, climbing another way, is a thief and robber. 🐑
## John 10:2
The shepherd enters through the gate. 🚪
## John 10:3
The gatekeeper opens for him. His sheep hear his voice. He calls each by name and leads them out. 🐑
## John 10:4
He leads after bringing all his sheep out. They follow, recognizing his voice. 🚶
## John 10:5
They flee strangers, whose voices they do not recognize. They never follow them.” 🐑
## John 10:6
The people did not understand Jesus’ illustration. 💭
## John 10:7
Jesus said, “I tell you the truth: I am the sheep’s gate. 🚪
## John 10:8
Those before Me were thieves and robbers. The sheep ignored them. ⚠️
## John 10:9
I am the gate. If anyone enters through Me, he will be saved. He will come, go, and find pasture. 🌾
## John 10:10
The thief only steals, kills, and destroys. I came so they may have life in its fullness. ❤️
## John 10:11
I am the good shepherd, who gives His life for the sheep. 🐑
## John 10:12
A hired worker does not own the sheep. Seeing a wolf, he flees and abandons them. The wolf attacks and scatters them. ⚠️
## John 10:13
He flees because he is hired and does not care about the sheep. 💔
## John 10:14
I am the good shepherd. My sheep and I know each other, 🐑
## John 10:15
as the Father and I know each other. I give My life for the sheep. ✝️
## John 10:16
I must bring other sheep outside this fold. They will hear Me. There will be one flock and one shepherd. 🤝
## John 10:17
The Father loves Me because I give My life to take it again. ❤️
## John 10:18
I lay down My life freely. No one takes it from Me. I have authority to lay it down and take it up again. My Father gave this command.” 🙏
## John 10:19
His message again divided the Jews. 💬
## John 10:20
Many said, “He has a demon and is insane. Why listen?” ❓
## John 10:21
Others answered, “A demon-possessed man would not speak like this. Can demons open blind eyes?” 👁️
## John 10:22
Jerusalem’s Feast of Dedication came in winter. 📅
## John 10:23
Jesus walked in Solomon’s Colonnade in the temple courts. 🏛️
## John 10:24
The Jews asked, “Why keep us in suspense? Tell us plainly if You are Christ.” ❓
## John 10:25
Jesus answered, “I told you, but you disbelieved. My works in My Father’s name testify for Me. ✨
## John 10:26
You disbelieve because you are not My sheep. 🐑
## John 10:27
My sheep hear My voice and follow. I know them. ❤️
## John 10:28
I give eternal life. They never perish. Nobody can seize them from My hand. 🛡️
## John 10:29
My Father gave them to Me and is greater than all. Nobody can seize them from His hand. 🙏
## John 10:30
The Father and I are one.” ✨
## John 10:31
The Jews again took stones against Him. 🪨
## John 10:32
Jesus asked, “I showed good works from My Father. Which deserves stoning?” ❓
## John 10:33
They answered, “Your blasphemy deserves it, rather than good works. You are human, but claim to be God.” ⚠️
## John 10:34
Jesus answered, “Your Law says, ‘I said you are gods.’ 📖
## John 10:35
If He called those receiving God’s word gods, and Scripture cannot be broken, 📖
## John 10:36
why accuse the One the Father sanctified and sent into the world of blasphemy for claiming to be God’s Son? 🙏
## John 10:37
If I do not do My Father’s works, do not believe Me. ❓
## John 10:38
If I do, believe the works even if you disbelieve Me. Then you may understand the Father is in Me, and I in Him.” ✨
## John 10:39
They tried to seize Him, but He escaped. 🚶
## John 10:40
Jesus returned across the Jordan, where John first baptized, and stayed there. 🌊
## John 10:41
Many said, “John performed no signs. Everything he said about Him was true.” 📖
## John 10:42
Many there believed in Jesus. 🙏
