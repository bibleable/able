## John 19:1
Pilate had Jesus whipped. 💔
## John 19:2
Soldiers crowned Him with woven thorns and dressed Him in purple. 👑
## John 19:3
They repeatedly approached, saluted Him as the Jews’ King, and slapped His face. ⚠️
## John 19:4
Pilate told the Jews, “I bring Him out so you know I find no basis for a charge against Him.” ⚖️
## John 19:5
Jesus emerged in thorns and purple. Pilate said, “Here is the man!” 👑
## John 19:6
Chief priests and officers shouted for crucifixion. Pilate answered, “You crucify Him. I find no basis for a charge.” ⚠️
## John 19:7
The Jews answered, “Our law requires His death because He declared Himself God’s Son.” 💬
## John 19:8
Pilate grew more afraid, 😨
## John 19:9
returned inside the Praetorium, and asked Jesus His origin. Jesus did not answer. ❓
## John 19:10
Pilate said, “Why refuse to speak? I have authority to release or crucify You.” ⚖️
## John 19:11
Jesus answered, “You have authority over Me only because it came from above. The one surrendering Me to you therefore has greater sin.” 🙏
## John 19:12
Pilate tried to release Him. The Jews shouted, “If you release Him, you are no friend of Caesar. Anyone claiming kingship defies Caesar.” ⚠️
## John 19:13
Pilate brought Jesus out and sat at the judgment seat on the Stone Pavement, called Gabbatha in Hebrew. ⚖️
## John 19:14
About the sixth hour on Passover’s Preparation Day, Pilate told the Jews, “Your King!” 👑
## John 19:15
They demanded crucifixion. Pilate asked, “Your King?” Chief priests replied, “Only Caesar is our king.” ⚠️
## John 19:16
Pilate surrendered Jesus for crucifixion, and soldiers took Him. ✝️
## John 19:17
Jesus carried His cross to the Place of the Skull, called Golgotha in Hebrew. ✝️
## John 19:18
They crucified Him between two others. 💔
## John 19:19
Pilate posted: JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS. 📜
## John 19:20
Many Jews read it, since Jesus’ crucifixion site was near the city. It was written in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek. 📖
## John 19:21
Chief priests told Pilate, “Write that He claimed kingship, rather than that He is the Jews’ King.” 💬
## John 19:22
Pilate answered, “I wrote what I wrote.” 📜
## John 19:23
Soldiers divided Jesus’ clothes into four portions, one per soldier. The tunic was seamless, woven in one piece top to bottom. 👕
## John 19:24
They cast lots instead of tearing it, fulfilling Scripture: “They divided My garments and cast lots for My clothing.” The soldiers did this. 📖
## John 19:25
Jesus’ mother, her sister, Mary wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene stood by His cross. 👥
## John 19:26
Seeing His mother and His beloved disciple, Jesus said, “Woman, here is your son.” ❤️
## John 19:27
He told the disciple, “Your mother.” The disciple took her home from that hour. 🏠
## John 19:28
Knowing everything was accomplished, Jesus said, “I thirst,” to fulfill Scripture. 📖
## John 19:29
They soaked a sponge in sour wine, put it on a hyssop stalk, and raised it to His mouth. 🍷
## John 19:30
Jesus received it and said, “It is finished.” He bowed His head and yielded His spirit. ✝️
## John 19:31
It was Preparation Day before a High Sabbath. The Jews asked Pilate to have the legs broken and bodies removed before the Sabbath. 📅
## John 19:32
Soldiers broke the legs of both men beside Jesus. ⚠️
## John 19:33
Jesus was already dead, so they left His legs unbroken. ✝️
## John 19:34
One pierced His side with a spear. Blood and water immediately flowed. 💔
## John 19:35
The eyewitness testifies truthfully and knows his testimony is true, so you may believe. 📖
## John 19:36
This fulfilled Scripture: “None of His bones will break.” 🙏
## John 19:37
Another Scripture says, “They will look on the One they pierced.” 📖
## John 19:38
Joseph of Arimathea secretly followed Jesus, fearing the Jews. He received Pilate’s permission and removed Jesus’ body. 🪦
## John 19:39
Nicodemus, His earlier nighttime visitor, brought about seventy-five pounds of myrrh and aloes. 🏺
## John 19:40
They wrapped Jesus’ body with linen and spices, following Jewish burial customs. 🪦
## John 19:41
At the crucifixion site was a garden with a new, unused tomb. 🌳
## John 19:42
They laid Jesus in this tomb because it was nearby and was the Jewish Preparation Day. 🪦
