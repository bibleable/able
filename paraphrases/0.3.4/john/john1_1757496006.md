## John 1:1
The Word was in the beginning. The Word was with God, and the Word was God. 📖
## John 1:2
He was with God in the beginning. 🙏
## John 1:3
Everything was made through Him. Nothing made was made without Him. 🌍
## John 1:4
Life was in Him, and that life was humanity’s light. ✨
## John 1:5
The Light shines in darkness, which has not overcome it. 🕯️
## John 1:6
God sent a man named John. 📣
## John 1:7
He came to testify about the Light so everyone might believe through him. 🙏
## John 1:8
He was not the Light. He came to testify about it. 📖
## John 1:9
The true Light was coming into the world, giving light to everyone. ✨
## John 1:10
He was in the world made through Him, but it did not recognize Him. 🌍
## John 1:11
He came to His own, who did not receive Him. 💔
## John 1:12
He gave everyone receiving Him and believing in His name the right to become God’s children. ❤️
## John 1:13
They were born of God, rather than blood, human desire, or human will. 🙏
## John 1:14
The Word became flesh and lived among us. We saw His glory, the glory of the Father’s one and only Son, full of grace and truth. ✨
## John 1:15
John testified, “The One coming after me surpassed me because He was before me. This is the One I spoke about.” 📣
## John 1:16
From His fullness, we all received grace upon grace. 🎁
## John 1:17
The law came through Moses. Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ. 📖
## John 1:18
No one ever saw God. The one and only Son, Himself God and at the Father’s side, made Him known. 🙏
## John 1:19
Jerusalem’s Jews sent priests and Levites to ask John who he was. This was his testimony: 💬
## John 1:20
He openly confessed, “I am not the Christ.” 📣
## John 1:21
They asked, “Who are you? Are you Elijah?” He answered, “I am not.” “Are you the Prophet?” He answered, “No.” ❓
## John 1:22
They said, “Who are you? Give us an answer for those sending us. What do you say about yourself?” 💬
## John 1:23
John quoted Isaiah: “I am a voice calling in the wilderness, ‘Make the Lord’s way straight.’” 🏜️
## John 1:24
The Pharisees who had been sent 👥
## John 1:25
asked, “Why baptize if you are neither Christ, Elijah, nor the Prophet?” ❓
## John 1:26
John answered, “I baptize with water. Someone you do not know stands among you. 💧
## John 1:27
He comes after me. I am not worthy to untie His sandal straps.” 🙏
## John 1:28
This happened at Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John baptized. 🌊
## John 1:29
Next day, John saw Jesus approaching and said, “Look, God’s Lamb, taking away the world’s sin! ✝️
## John 1:30
He is the man I described: the One coming after me surpassed me because He was before me. 📣
## John 1:31
I did not know Him. I came baptizing with water so He might be revealed to Israel.” 💧
## John 1:32
John testified, “I saw the Spirit descend from heaven like a dove and rest on Him. 🕊️
## John 1:33
I did not know Him. The One sending me to baptize with water gave this message. ‘The man on whom you see the Spirit descend and rest will baptize with the Holy Spirit.’ 🕊️
## John 1:34
I saw and testified that He is God’s Son.” 🙏
## John 1:35
Next day, John stood with two disciples. 👥
## John 1:36
He saw Jesus passing and said, “Look, God’s Lamb!” ✝️
## John 1:37
The two heard and followed Jesus. 🚶
## John 1:38
Jesus turned and asked what they wanted. They said, “Rabbi, where do You stay?” Rabbi means Teacher. ❓
## John 1:39
He answered, “Come see.” They spent the day where He stayed. It was about the tenth hour. 🏠
## John 1:40
Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, was one of the two following Jesus after hearing John. 👥
## John 1:41
He first told his brother Simon, “We found the Messiah.” Messiah is translated as Christ. 🙏
## John 1:42
Andrew brought Simon to Jesus. Jesus said, “You are Simon son of John. You will be called Cephas,” translated as Peter. 👥
## John 1:43
Next day, Jesus decided to go to Galilee. He found Philip and said, “Follow Me.” 🚶
## John 1:44
Philip came from Bethsaida, Andrew and Peter’s town. 🏙️
## John 1:45
Philip told Nathanael, “We found the One Moses wrote about in the Law and the prophets foretold. He is Jesus of Nazareth, Joseph’s son.” 📖
## John 1:46
Nathanael asked, “Can Nazareth produce anything good?” Philip answered, “Come see.” ❓
## John 1:47
Jesus saw Nathanael approach and said, “A true Israelite without deceit!” 🙏
## John 1:48
Nathanael asked how He knew him. Jesus said, “I saw you under the fig tree before Philip called you.” 👁️
## John 1:49
Nathanael answered, “Rabbi, You are God’s Son and Israel’s King!” 👑
## John 1:50
Jesus asked, “Do you believe because I told you I saw you under the fig tree? You will see greater things.” ✨
## John 1:51
He said, “I tell you the truth. You will all see heaven open and God’s angels going up and down on the Son of Man.” 👼
