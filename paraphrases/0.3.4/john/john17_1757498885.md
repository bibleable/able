## John 17:1
Jesus looked heavenward and prayed, “Father, the hour has come. Glorify Your Son so He may glorify You. 🙏
## John 17:2
You gave Him authority over everyone, to give eternal life to all You gave Him. 🌅
## John 17:3
Eternal life is knowing You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom You sent. 🙏
## John 17:4
I glorified You on earth, finishing Your assigned work. 📖
## John 17:5
Father, glorify Me beside You with My glory from before the world existed. ✨
## John 17:6
I revealed Your name to those You gave Me from the world. They were Yours and kept Your word. 🙏
## John 17:7
They now know everything You gave Me comes from You. 🎁
## John 17:8
I gave Your words, and they accepted them. They knew certainly I came from You and believed You sent Me. 📖
## John 17:9
I pray for those You gave Me, rather than the world. They are Yours. 🙏
## John 17:10
All Mine is Yours, and all Yours is Mine. I am glorified in them. ✨
## John 17:11
I leave the world, where they remain, and come to You. Holy Father, protect them by Your name, which You gave Me, so they are one like Us. 🛡️
## John 17:12
While I was with them, I protected and preserved them by Your name, which You gave Me. None was lost except the son of destruction, fulfilling Scripture. 📖
## John 17:13
I come to You, speaking now in the world so they have My joy fulfilled within. ❤️
## John 17:14
I gave Your word. The world hated them because they do not belong to it, as I do not. 🛡️
## John 17:15
Keep them from the evil one, rather than removing them from the world. 🙏
## John 17:16
Neither they nor I belong to the world. 🌍
## John 17:17
Sanctify them through truth. Your word is truth. 📖
## John 17:18
I sent them into the world as You sent Me. 🚶
## John 17:19
I sanctify Myself for them, so they may be sanctified through truth. 🙏
## John 17:20
I also pray for those who will believe in Me through their message, 🙏
## John 17:21
so all may be one, as You and I are in each other. May they be in Us, so the world may believe You sent Me. 🤝
## John 17:22
I gave them Your glory, so they may be one as We are, ✨
## John 17:23
I am in them and You are in Me. May they be perfectly united, so the world may know You sent Me and loved them as You loved Me. ❤️
## John 17:24
Father, I want those You gave Me to be with Me where I am and see My glory. You gave it because You loved Me before the world’s foundation. ✨
## John 17:25
Righteous Father, the world did not know You. I know You, and they know You sent Me. 🙏
## John 17:26
I revealed Your name and will keep revealing it. May Your love for Me be in them, and I in them.” ❤️
