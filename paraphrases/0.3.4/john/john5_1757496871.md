## John 5:1
Later, Jesus went to Jerusalem for a Jewish feast. 🚶
## John 5:2
Near Jerusalem’s Sheep Gate was Bethesda, a pool with five covered colonnades. Bethesda was its Hebrew name. 💧
## John 5:3
Many sick, blind, lame, and paralyzed people lay there. 💔
## John 5:4
*BSB source note: The local BSB has no text at this verse address.* 📖
## John 5:5
A man there had been disabled thirty-eight years. 💔
## John 5:6
Jesus saw him and knew his long suffering. He asked, “Do you want to get well?” ❓
## John 5:7
He answered, “Sir, nobody helps me into the pool when water stirs. As I try to enter, someone else goes before me.” 💬
## John 5:8
Jesus said, “Rise, take your mat, and walk.” 🚶
## John 5:9
He immediately recovered, took his mat, and walked. It was the Sabbath. ❤️
## John 5:10
The Jews told him, “Carrying your mat on the Sabbath is unlawful.” ⚠️
## John 5:11
He answered, “My healer told me to take it and walk.” 💬
## John 5:12
They asked who told him that. ❓
## John 5:13
He did not know. Jesus slipped away among the crowd. 👥
## John 5:14
Jesus found him in the temple and said, “You are well. Stop sinning, or worse may happen.” ⚠️
## John 5:15
He told the Jews Jesus healed him. 📣
## John 5:16
They persecuted Jesus for doing this on the Sabbath. ⚠️
## John 5:17
Jesus answered, “My Father works even now. I work too.” 🙏
## John 5:18
They tried harder to kill Him. He broke the Sabbath and called God His own Father, making Himself equal with God. ⚠️
## John 5:19
Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. The Son can do nothing by Himself unless He sees the Father doing it. Whatever the Father does, the Son does too. 🙏
## John 5:20
The Father loves the Son and shows everything He does. He will show greater works, amazing you. ✨
## John 5:21
The Father raises and gives life to the dead. The Son likewise gives life to whomever He wishes. 🌅
## John 5:22
The Father judges no one. He gave all judgment to the Son, ⚖️
## John 5:23
so all may honor the Son as the Father. Dishonoring the Son dishonors the Father who sent Him. 🙏
## John 5:24
Whoever hears My word and believes the One who sent Me has eternal life and will not face judgment. He has crossed from death to life. I tell you the truth. 🌅
## John 5:25
The hour is coming and is now here when the dead hear God’s Son. Those hearing will live. I tell you the truth. 🌅
## John 5:26
The Father has life in Himself. He granted the Son life in Himself too. 🙏
## John 5:27
He gave the Son authority to judge because He is the Son of Man. ⚖️
## John 5:28
Do not be amazed. A time comes when everyone in graves hears His voice 👂
## John 5:29
and comes out. Those doing good rise to life. Those doing evil rise to judgment. 🌅
## John 5:30
I can do nothing by Myself. I judge as I hear. My judgment is just because I seek the will of the One who sent Me, rather than My own. ⚖️
## John 5:31
If I testify about Myself, My testimony is invalid. 💬
## John 5:32
Another testifies about Me. I know His testimony is valid. 🙏
## John 5:33
You sent to John, who testified to truth. 📖
## John 5:34
I do not accept human testimony, but say this so you may be saved. 🛡️
## John 5:35
John was a burning, shining lamp. You were willing to enjoy his light for a time. 🕯️
## John 5:36
I have testimony greater than John’s. The works My Father gave Me, which I perform, testify that He sent Me. ✨
## John 5:37
My Father also testified. You never heard His voice or saw His form. 👁️
## John 5:38
His word does not remain in you because you disbelieve the One He sent. ⚠️
## John 5:39
You examine Scripture, presuming it gives you eternal life. These words testify about Me, 📖
## John 5:40
but you refuse to come for life. 💔
## John 5:41
I accept no human glory. 🙏
## John 5:42
I know you lack God’s love within. 💔
## John 5:43
I came in My Father’s name, and you rejected Me. If another comes in his own name, you will receive him. ⚠️
## John 5:44
How can you believe, accepting glory from each other instead of seeking glory from the only God? ❓
## John 5:45
Do not think I will accuse you before the Father. Moses, your hope, accuses you. ⚖️
## John 5:46
If you had believed Moses, you would believe Me because he wrote about Me. 📖
## John 5:47
Since you disbelieve his writings, how will you believe My words?” ❓
