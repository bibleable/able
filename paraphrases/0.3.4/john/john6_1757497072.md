## John 6:1
Jesus crossed the Sea of Galilee, also called the Sea of Tiberias. 🌊
## John 6:2
A large crowd followed because they saw His signs healing the sick. 👥
## John 6:3
Jesus sat with His disciples on a mountain. ⛰️
## John 6:4
The Jewish Passover Feast was near. 📅
## John 6:5
Seeing the crowd, Jesus asked Philip, “Where can we buy bread for them?” 🍞
## John 6:6
He tested Philip, already knowing what He would do. 💭
## John 6:7
Philip answered, “Two hundred denarii cannot buy even a little bread for everyone.” 🪙
## John 6:8
Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother and one disciple, said: 👥
## John 6:9
“A boy has five barley loaves and two small fish. How can these serve so many?” 🍞
## John 6:10
Jesus told them to seat the people. About five thousand men sat on the abundant grass. 👥
## John 6:11
Jesus gave thanks and distributed the loaves and fish. Everyone seated received as much as they wanted. 🙏
## John 6:12
After everyone ate fully, He said, “Collect leftovers so nothing is wasted.” 🧺
## John 6:13
They filled twelve baskets with pieces left from the five barley loaves. 🍞
## John 6:14
Seeing Jesus’ sign, the people said, “He truly is the Prophet coming into the world.” 🙏
## John 6:15
Jesus knew they intended to force Him to be king. He withdrew alone to a mountain. ⛰️
## John 6:16
At evening, His disciples went to the sea, 🌆
## John 6:17
boarded a boat, and headed for Capernaum. Darkness came, but Jesus had not joined them. 🚤
## John 6:18
A strong wind stirred the sea. 🌊
## John 6:19
After rowing about three or four miles, they saw Jesus walking on the sea toward them. They were terrified. 😨
## John 6:20
Jesus said, “It is I. Do not fear.” 🛡️
## John 6:21
They were willing to receive Him into the boat. It immediately reached the shore where they were going. 🚤
## John 6:22
Next day, the remaining crowd realized only one boat had been there. Jesus had not boarded it, but His disciples left alone. 👥
## John 6:23
Boats from Tiberias arrived near where people ate bread after the Lord thanked God. 🚤
## John 6:24
The crowd saw Jesus and His disciples were absent. They sailed to Capernaum to find Him. 🔎
## John 6:25
They asked, “Rabbi, when did You arrive?” ❓
## John 6:26
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. You seek Me because you ate fully, rather than because you saw signs. 🍞
## John 6:27
Work for food lasting to eternal life, rather than food that perishes. The Son of Man will give it. God the Father placed His seal of approval on Him. 🙏
## John 6:28
They asked, “How do we perform God’s works?” ❓
## John 6:29
Jesus answered, “God’s work is believing in the One He sent.” 🙏
## John 6:30
They asked, “What sign will You perform so we can believe? What will You do? ❓
## John 6:31
Our fathers ate wilderness manna. Scripture says, ‘He gave them heaven’s bread to eat.’” 🍞
## John 6:32
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. Moses did not give you heaven’s bread. My Father gives the true bread from heaven. 🙏
## John 6:33
God’s bread is the One descending from heaven to give the world life.” 🌅
## John 6:34
They said, “Sir, always give us this bread.” 🍞
## John 6:35
Jesus answered, “I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to Me never hungers. Whoever believes in Me never thirsts. 🙏
## John 6:36
You saw Me, as I said, but still disbelieve. 💔
## John 6:37
Everyone My Father gives Me will come. I will never drive away anyone coming to Me. ❤️
## John 6:38
I descended from heaven to do the will of the One who sent Me, rather than My own. 🙏
## John 6:39
He wills that I lose none He gave Me, but raise them on the last day. 🌅
## John 6:40
My Father wills that everyone looking to the Son and believing in Him has eternal life. I will raise him on the last day.” 🌅
## John 6:41
The Jews grumbled because Jesus called Himself the bread descending from heaven. 💬
## John 6:42
They asked, “Is this not Jesus, Joseph’s son? We know His parents. How can He claim to descend from heaven?” ❓
## John 6:43
Jesus said, “Stop grumbling. 💬
## John 6:44
No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him. I will raise him on the last day. 🌅
## John 6:45
The Prophets say, ‘God will teach them all.’ Everyone hearing and learning from the Father comes to Me. 📖
## John 6:46
Only the One from God has seen the Father. No one else has seen Him. 👁️
## John 6:47
I tell you the truth: whoever believes has eternal life. 🙏
## John 6:48
I am the bread of life. 🍞
## John 6:49
Your fathers ate wilderness manna and died. 💔
## John 6:50
This bread descends from heaven so anyone may eat and not die. 🌅
## John 6:51
I am living bread from heaven. Anyone eating this bread lives forever. I will give My flesh, this bread, for the world’s life.” ✝️
## John 6:52
The Jews argued, “How can He give us His flesh to eat?” ❓
## John 6:53
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. Without eating the Son of Man’s flesh and drinking His blood, you have no life. ✝️
## John 6:54
Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life. I will raise him on the last day. 🌅
## John 6:55
My flesh is real food. My blood is real drink. 🍞
## John 6:56
Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood remains in Me, and I in him. 🙏
## John 6:57
The living Father sent Me, and I live because of Him. Whoever feeds on Me likewise lives because of Me. 🌅
## John 6:58
This bread descended from heaven. Your fathers ate manna and died, but whoever eats this bread lives forever.” 🍞
## John 6:59
Jesus taught this in Capernaum’s synagogue. 📖
## John 6:60
Many disciples said, “This teaching is difficult. Who can accept it?” ❓
## John 6:61
Jesus knew they grumbled and asked, “Does this offend you? 💬
## John 6:62
What if you see the Son of Man ascend where He was before? ☁️
## John 6:63
The Spirit gives life. The flesh benefits nothing. My words are spirit and life. 🕊️
## John 6:64
Some of you do not believe.” Jesus knew from the beginning who disbelieved and who would betray Him. 💔
## John 6:65
Jesus said, “No one comes to Me unless the Father grants it. That is why I told you this.” 🙏
## John 6:66
Many disciples then left and stopped walking with Him. 💔
## John 6:67
Jesus asked the Twelve, “Will you leave too?” ❓
## John 6:68
Simon Peter answered, “Lord, who else can we go to? You have the words of eternal life. 🙏
## John 6:69
We believe and know You are God’s Holy One.” ❤️
## John 6:70
Jesus answered, “I chose you Twelve. Yet one is a devil!” ⚠️
## John 6:71
He meant Judas, Simon Iscariot’s son, one of the Twelve, who would betray Jesus. 💔
