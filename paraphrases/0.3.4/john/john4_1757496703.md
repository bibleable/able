## John 4:1
Jesus learned the Pharisees knew He gained and baptized more disciples than John. 📖
## John 4:2
His disciples actually baptized, rather than Jesus Himself. 💧
## John 4:3
He left Judea and returned to Galilee. 🚶
## John 4:4
He had to pass through Samaria. 🌍
## John 4:5
He reached Sychar in Samaria, near the land Jacob gave his son Joseph. 🏙️
## John 4:6
Tired from traveling, Jesus sat by Jacob’s well there. It was about the sixth hour. 💧
## John 4:7
A Samaritan woman came for water. Jesus asked, “Give Me a drink.” 💧
## John 4:8
His disciples had gone to buy food in town. 🍞
## John 4:9
She asked, “You are a Jew. Why ask a Samaritan woman for water?” Jews do not associate with Samaritans. ❓
## John 4:10
Jesus answered, “If you knew God’s gift and who asks you for water, you would have asked Him. He would have given you living water.” 💧
## John 4:11
She said, “Sir, the well is deep, and You have nothing for drawing water. Where will You get living water? ❓
## John 4:12
Are You greater than Jacob, our father? He gave this well and drank from it with his sons and livestock.” 💧
## John 4:13
Jesus answered, “Everyone drinking this water thirsts again. 💧
## John 4:14
Whoever drinks My water will never thirst. It will become a fountain within him, springing to eternal life.” 🌅
## John 4:15
She said, “Sir, give me this water so I avoid thirst and repeated trips here.” 🙏
## John 4:16
Jesus said, “Call your husband and return.” 💬
## John 4:17
She answered, “I have no husband.” Jesus said, “You spoke correctly. 💬
## John 4:18
You had five husbands, and your present man is not your husband. You spoke truthfully.” 📖
## John 4:19
She said, “Sir, I see You are a prophet. 🙏
## John 4:20
Our fathers worshiped on this mountain. You Jews say worship must take place in Jerusalem.” ⛰️
## John 4:21
Jesus answered, “Believe Me, woman. A time comes when you will worship the Father neither here nor in Jerusalem. 🙏
## John 4:22
You worship what you do not know. We worship what we know, because salvation comes from the Jews. 📖
## John 4:23
The time is coming and is now here for true worshipers to worship the Father in spirit and truth. He seeks such worshipers. 🙏
## John 4:24
God is Spirit. His worshipers must worship in spirit and truth.” 🕊️
## John 4:25
She said, “I know Messiah, called Christ, is coming. He will explain everything.” 🙏
## John 4:26
Jesus answered, “I who speak to you am He.” ✨
## John 4:27
The disciples returned, surprised He spoke with a woman. None asked what He wanted from her or why He spoke with her. 👥
## John 4:28
She left her water jar, returned to town, and said: 🏺
## John 4:29
“See a man who told everything I ever did. Could He be the Christ?” 📣
## John 4:30
The people came out toward Jesus. 🚶
## John 4:31
The disciples urged, “Rabbi, eat.” 🍞
## John 4:32
He answered, “I have food you know nothing about.” 💬
## John 4:33
They asked each other, “Did someone bring Him food?” ❓
## John 4:34
Jesus said, “My food is to do the will of the One who sent Me and finish His work. 🙏
## John 4:35
You say harvest is four months away. Look up at the fields! They are ripe now. 🌾
## John 4:36
The reaper already earns wages and gathers a crop for eternal life. Sower and reaper may rejoice together. 🌾
## John 4:37
The saying applies: one sows, another reaps. 🌱
## John 4:38
I sent you to reap what others worked for. You have entered their hard labor.” 🤝
## John 4:39
Many Samaritans from that town believed in Jesus because she testified, “He told everything I ever did.” 🙏
## John 4:40
The Samaritans asked Him to stay, and He stayed two days. 🏠
## John 4:41
Many more believed His message. 📖
## John 4:42
They told her, “We now believe because of more than your words. We heard for ourselves and know this man truly is the Savior of the world.” 🙏
## John 4:43
After two days, Jesus left for Galilee. 🚶
## John 4:44
Jesus Himself testified that a prophet lacks honor in his hometown. 📖
## John 4:45
Galileans welcomed Him. They had attended Jerusalem’s feast and seen all He did there. 🤝
## John 4:46
He returned to Cana in Galilee, where He changed water to wine. A royal official’s son was sick in Capernaum. 🍷
## John 4:47
The official heard Jesus returned from Judea to Galilee. He begged Him to heal his dying son. 🙏
## John 4:48
Jesus said, “Without signs and wonders, you people will never believe.” 💬
## John 4:49
The official pleaded, “Sir, come before my child dies.” 💔
## John 4:50
Jesus said, “Go. Your son will live.” He believed Jesus’ word and left. 🙏
## John 4:51
His servants met him on the way, saying his son lived. ❤️
## John 4:52
He asked when the boy recovered. They answered, “Yesterday at the seventh hour, the fever left.” 🌅
## John 4:53
The father recognized the hour Jesus promised his son would live. He and his whole household believed. 🙏
## John 4:54
This was Jesus’ second sign after returning from Judea to Galilee. ✨
