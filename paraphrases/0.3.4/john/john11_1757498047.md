## John 11:1
Lazarus was sick in Bethany, Mary and her sister Martha’s village. 💔
## John 11:2
Mary would anoint the Lord with perfume and wipe His feet with her hair. Her brother Lazarus was sick. 🏺
## John 11:3
The sisters told Jesus, “Lord, the one You love is sick.” 🙏
## John 11:4
Jesus said, “This sickness will not end in death. It serves God’s glory, so God’s Son may be glorified through it.” ✨
## John 11:5
Jesus loved Martha, her sister, and Lazarus. ❤️
## John 11:6
Hearing Lazarus was sick, He stayed two more days, ⏳
## John 11:7
then told His disciples to return to Judea. 🚶
## John 11:8
They asked, “Rabbi, the Jews just tried to stone You. Will You return?” ❓
## John 11:9
Jesus answered, “Daylight lasts twelve hours. Whoever walks then sees this world’s light and does not stumble. ☀️
## John 11:10
Whoever walks at night stumbles because he has no light.” 🌌
## John 11:11
He said, “Our friend Lazarus sleeps. I go to wake him.” 💬
## John 11:12
They answered, “Lord, if he sleeps, he will recover.” 🙏
## John 11:13
Jesus meant Lazarus’s death. They thought He meant ordinary sleep. 💭
## John 11:14
He plainly said, “Lazarus died. 💔
## John 11:15
For your sake, I am glad I was absent, so you may believe. Let us go to him.” 🙏
## John 11:16
Thomas, called Didymus, told the others, “Let us go and die with Him.” 👥
## John 11:17
Jesus arrived after Lazarus spent four days in the tomb. 🪦
## John 11:18
Bethany was a little less than two miles from Jerusalem. 🌍
## John 11:19
Many Jews consoled Martha and Mary about their brother. 💧
## John 11:20
Martha met the arriving Jesus. Mary stayed home. 🚶
## John 11:21
Martha said, “Lord, my brother would not have died if You were here. 💔
## John 11:22
Even now, God will give whatever You ask.” 🙏
## John 11:23
Jesus said her brother would rise. 🌅
## John 11:24
Martha answered, “I know he will rise at the last day’s resurrection.” 🙏
## John 11:25
Jesus said, “I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in Me will live, though he dies. 🌅
## John 11:26
Everyone living and believing in Me will never die. Do you believe?” ❓
## John 11:27
She answered, “Yes, Lord. You are Christ, God’s Son coming into the world.” 🙏
## John 11:28
Martha privately called Mary, “The Teacher asks for you.” 💬
## John 11:29
Mary quickly went to Him. 🚶
## John 11:30
Jesus remained where Martha met Him, outside the village. 🏠
## John 11:31
Jews comforting Mary followed, thinking she went to mourn at the tomb. 💧
## John 11:32
Mary fell at Jesus’ feet, saying, “Lord, my brother would not have died if You were here.” 🙏
## John 11:33
Jesus saw her and the Jews weeping. Deeply moved in spirit, He was troubled. 💔
## John 11:34
He asked where they placed him. They answered, “Come see, Lord.” 🪦
## John 11:35
Jesus wept. 💧
## John 11:36
The Jews said, “He loved him so much!” ❤️
## John 11:37
Others asked, “He healed blind eyes. Could He not prevent Lazarus’s death?” ❓
## John 11:38
Deeply moved again, Jesus reached the cave tomb, sealed by a stone. 🪨
## John 11:39
Jesus asked them to remove it. Martha said, “Lord, he stinks after four days!” 💬
## John 11:40
Jesus answered, “Did I not say that if you believed, you would see God’s glory?” ✨
## John 11:41
They removed the stone. Jesus looked upward: “Father, thank You for hearing Me. 🙏
## John 11:42
I know You always hear. I say this so those standing here may believe You sent Me.” 🙏
## John 11:43
He shouted, “Lazarus, come out!” 📣
## John 11:44
The dead man emerged, hands and feet bound in linen strips, face wrapped in cloth. Jesus said, “Unwrap him and let him go.” 🌅
## John 11:45
Many Jews visiting Mary believed after seeing Jesus’ work. 🙏
## John 11:46
Some reported it to the Pharisees. 💬
## John 11:47
Chief priests and Pharisees gathered the Sanhedrin. They asked, “What can we do? He performs many signs. ❓
## John 11:48
If we let Him continue, everyone will believe. Romans will take our place and nation.” ⚠️
## John 11:49
Caiaphas, that year’s high priest, said, “You understand nothing! 💬
## John 11:50
One man dying for the people is better than the whole nation perishing.” ⚖️
## John 11:51
Caiaphas did not say this on his own. As high priest that year, he prophesied Jesus would die for the nation, 📖
## John 11:52
and not only for the nation. He would also die for God’s scattered children to gather them into one. 🤝
## John 11:53
They began plotting His death that day. ⚠️
## John 11:54
Jesus stopped appearing publicly among the Jews. He stayed with His disciples in Ephraim, near the wilderness. 🚶
## John 11:55
Near Passover, many came from the countryside to Jerusalem for purification before the feast. 📅
## John 11:56
In the temple courts, they looked for Jesus and wondered whether He would attend. ❓
## John 11:57
Chief priests and Pharisees ordered anyone knowing His location to report it for His arrest. ⚠️
