## John 16:1
“I say this so you will not fall away. 🛡️
## John 16:2
They will expel you from synagogues. Your killers will think they serve God. ⚠️
## John 16:3
They do this because they know neither My Father nor Me. 💔
## John 16:4
I warned you so you remember when the time comes. I did not tell you originally because I was with you. 📖
## John 16:5
Now I go to the One who sent Me. None asks where I go. 💬
## John 16:6
My words fill your hearts with sorrow. 💔
## John 16:7
I tell you the truth. Leaving benefits you. Unless I leave, the Advocate will not come. If I leave, I will send Him. 🕊️
## John 16:8
He will convict the world regarding sin, righteousness, and judgment: ⚖️
## John 16:9
sin, because they disbelieve Me, 💔
## John 16:10
righteousness, because I go to the Father and you no longer see Me, 🙏
## John 16:11
and judgment, because this world’s prince has been condemned. ⚖️
## John 16:12
I have much more to tell, but you cannot bear it yet. 💬
## John 16:13
The Spirit of truth will guide you into all truth. He speaks what He hears, rather than independently, and declares what will come. 🕊️
## John 16:14
He glorifies Me by revealing what is Mine to you. ✨
## John 16:15
Everything My Father has is Mine. This is why the Spirit takes from Mine and reveals it to you. 🙏
## John 16:16
Soon you will not see Me. Shortly afterward, you will see Me.” 👁️
## John 16:17
Disciples asked each other why He spoke of briefly disappearing, reappearing, and going to the Father. ❓
## John 16:18
They asked what “a little while” meant, not understanding. 💭
## John 16:19
Jesus knew their questions: “Do you ask why I said you would stop seeing Me briefly, then see Me again? 💬
## John 16:20
I tell you the truth. You will weep while the world rejoices. Your grief will turn to joy. ❤️
## John 16:21
Labor pains hurt a woman, but her newborn child’s arrival brings joy that makes her forget the anguish. 👶
## John 16:22
You grieve now. I will see you again, and your hearts will rejoice. Nobody will take your joy. ❤️
## John 16:23
You will no longer question Me that day. I tell you the truth: whatever you ask the Father in My name, He will give you. 🙏
## John 16:24
You have not asked in My name yet. Ask and you will receive, so your joy may be complete. ❤️
## John 16:25
I used figures of speech. Soon I will speak plainly about the Father. 📖
## John 16:26
You will ask in My name then. I do not say I will ask the Father for you. 🙏
## John 16:27
The Father loves you because you loved Me and believed I came from God. ❤️
## John 16:28
I entered the world from the Father. I leave it to return to Him.” 🙏
## John 16:29
They answered, “Now You speak plainly. 💬
## John 16:30
You know everything and need no questions. We therefore believe You came from God.” 🙏
## John 16:31
Jesus answered, “Do you finally believe? ❓
## John 16:32
You will scatter to your own homes and leave Me alone. This hour is coming and is already here. Yet I am not alone, because My Father is with Me. 🛡️
## John 16:33
I say this so you may find peace in Me. This world brings suffering. Take courage! I have overcome the world.” 🕊️
