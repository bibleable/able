## John 20:1
Early on the week’s first day, still dark, Mary Magdalene found the tomb’s stone removed. 🌅
## John 20:2
She ran to Simon Peter and Jesus’ beloved disciple, saying, “They have taken the Lord out of the tomb! We do not know where they put Him.” 💔
## John 20:3
Peter and the other disciple went to the tomb. 🚶
## John 20:4
The two ran together, but the other reached it before Peter. 🏃
## John 20:5
He looked at the linen cloths without entering. 👁️
## John 20:6
Simon Peter arrived, entered, and saw them. 🪦
## John 20:7
The cloth from Jesus’ head lay rolled up, separate from the linen cloths. 📖
## John 20:8
The other disciple entered, saw, and believed. 🙏
## John 20:9
They still did not understand Scripture’s requirement that Jesus rise from death. 📖
## John 20:10
They returned home. 🏠
## John 20:11
Mary wept outside and looked inside, 💧
## John 20:12
seeing two angels in white where Jesus’ body had lain, one at the head and one at the feet. 👼
## John 20:13
They asked why she wept. She answered, “They took my Lord. I do not know where.” 💔
## John 20:14
She turned and saw Jesus, without recognizing Him. 👁️
## John 20:15
Jesus asked why she wept and whom she sought. Thinking Him the gardener, she asked, “Sir, if You took Him, tell me where, and I will get Him.” ❓
## John 20:16
Jesus said, “Mary.” She answered in Hebrew, “Rabboni,” meaning Teacher. ❤️
## John 20:17
Jesus said, “Do not cling to Me. I have not ascended to the Father. Tell My brothers I ascend to My Father and yours, My God and yours.” 🙏
## John 20:18
Mary Magdalene told the disciples she saw the Lord and repeated His words. 📣
## John 20:19
That evening, the week’s first day, they locked the doors for fear of the Jews. Jesus stood among them, saying, “Peace.” 🕊️
## John 20:20
He showed His hands and side. They rejoiced to see the Lord. ❤️
## John 20:21
Jesus said, “Peace to you. I send you as My Father sent Me.” 🚶
## John 20:22
He breathed on them: “Receive the Holy Spirit. 🕊️
## John 20:23
If you forgive anyone’s sins, they are forgiven. If you withhold forgiveness from anyone, it is withheld.” 🙏
## John 20:24
Thomas, called Didymus, one of the Twelve, was absent. 👥
## John 20:25
They told him they saw the Lord. Thomas answered, “I will never believe unless I see His hands’ nail marks. I must put my finger there and my hand into His side.” ❓
## John 20:26
Eight days later, Thomas was with them behind locked doors. Jesus stood among them, saying, “Peace.” 🕊️
## John 20:27
He told Thomas, “See My hands and put your finger here. Put your hand into My side. Stop doubting and believe.” 🙏
## John 20:28
Thomas answered, “My Lord and my God!” ❤️
## John 20:29
Jesus said, “You believed because you saw. Blessed are those believing without seeing.” 🙏
## John 20:30
Many other signs Jesus performed before His disciples are absent from this book. 📖
## John 20:31
These are recorded so you may believe Jesus is Christ, God’s Son, and have life in His name through believing. 🌅
