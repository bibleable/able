## John 7:1
Jesus traveled Galilee, avoiding Judea because its Jews sought to kill Him. 🚶
## John 7:2
The Jewish Feast of Tabernacles was near. 📅
## John 7:3
His brothers said, “Go to Judea so Your disciples see Your works. 💬
## John 7:4
Anyone seeking public recognition avoids secrecy. Show Yourself to the world.” 📣
## John 7:5
Even His brothers did not believe in Him. 💔
## John 7:6
Jesus answered, “Your time is always ready. Mine has not come. ⏳
## John 7:7
The world cannot hate you. It hates Me because I testify its deeds are evil. ⚠️
## John 7:8
Go to the feast. I am not going because My time has not come.” 📅
## John 7:9
Jesus remained in Galilee. 🏠
## John 7:10
After His brothers left, Jesus secretly went to the feast. 🚶
## John 7:11
The Jews sought Him there, asking where He was. 🔎
## John 7:12
People whispered, “He is good.” Others said, “He deceives people.” 💬
## John 7:13
Fear of the Jews kept them from speaking publicly. ⚠️
## John 7:14
Halfway through the feast, Jesus taught in the temple courts. 📖
## John 7:15
The Jews marveled, “How did He learn without studying?” ❓
## John 7:16
Jesus answered, “My teaching comes from the One who sent Me, rather than Myself. 🙏
## John 7:17
Anyone wishing to do His will knows whether My teaching is God’s or My own. 📖
## John 7:18
Someone speaking on his own authority seeks personal glory. The One seeking the glory of Him who sent Him is truthful and has no falsehood. 🙏
## John 7:19
Moses gave the law, but none of you keeps it. Why seek to kill Me?” ❓
## John 7:20
The crowd replied, “You have a demon! Who seeks to kill You?” 💬
## John 7:21
Jesus answered, “I did one miracle, and all marvel. ✨
## John 7:22
Moses gave circumcision, originally from the patriarchs. You circumcise boys on the Sabbath. 📖
## John 7:23
You circumcise on the Sabbath to keep Moses’ law. Why resent Me making a whole man well on the Sabbath? ❤️
## John 7:24
Judge justly, rather than by appearances.” ⚖️
## John 7:25
Jerusalem’s people asked, “Is He not the man they seek to kill? ❓
## John 7:26
He speaks publicly, and they say nothing. Do the rulers recognize Him as Christ? 💬
## John 7:27
We know His origin. Nobody will know Christ’s origin when He comes.” ❓
## John 7:28
Jesus cried from the temple courts, “You know Me and My origin. I came from the true One who sent Me, rather than of Myself. You do not know Him. 🙏
## John 7:29
I know Him because I am from Him and He sent Me.” 🙏
## John 7:30
They tried to seize Him, but nobody touched Him. His hour had not come. ⏳
## John 7:31
Many believed, asking, “Will Christ perform more signs than this man when He comes?” ✨
## John 7:32
Pharisees heard the whispers about Jesus. They and chief priests sent officers to arrest Him. ⚠️
## John 7:33
Jesus said, “Soon I return to the One who sent Me. 💬
## John 7:34
You will search without finding Me. You cannot come where I am.” ❓
## John 7:35
The Jews asked, “Where will He go? To the Jews among Greeks, to teach Greeks? 💬
## John 7:36
Why say we cannot find Him or go where He is?” ❓
## John 7:37
On the feast’s last, greatest day, Jesus shouted, “Whoever thirsts, come drink from Me! 💧
## John 7:38
Scripture says streams of living water will flow from within whoever believes in Me.” 🌊
## John 7:39
He meant the Spirit believers would receive. The Spirit was not yet given because Jesus was not yet glorified. 🕊️
## John 7:40
Some said, “He truly is the Prophet.” 🙏
## John 7:41
Others said, “He is Christ.” Others asked, “Can Christ come from Galilee? ❓
## John 7:42
Does Scripture not say the Christ comes from David’s line and Bethlehem, David’s village?” 📖
## John 7:43
The crowd was divided because of Jesus. 💬
## John 7:44
Some wanted to seize Him, but nobody touched Him. ⚠️
## John 7:45
Officers returned to the chief priests and Pharisees. They asked why they brought no prisoner. ❓
## John 7:46
The officers answered, “Nobody ever spoke like Him!” 💬
## John 7:47
Pharisees asked, “Are you deceived too? ⚠️
## John 7:48
Did any rulers or Pharisees believe? ❓
## John 7:49
This crowd does not know the law and is cursed.” ⚠️
## John 7:50
Nicodemus, one of them who previously visited Jesus, asked: 💬
## John 7:51
“Does our law condemn a man without first hearing from him and learning what he did?” ⚖️
## John 7:52
They answered, “Are you from Galilee too? Investigate. No prophet comes from Galilee.” 💬
## John 7:53
Each went home. 🏠
