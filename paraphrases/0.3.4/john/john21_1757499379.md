## John 21:1
Jesus revealed Himself again beside the Sea of Tiberias: 🌊
## John 21:2
Simon Peter, Thomas called Didymus, Nathanael from Cana in Galilee, Zebedee’s sons, and two others were together. 👥
## John 21:3
Simon Peter went fishing, and they joined. They caught nothing that night. 🚤
## John 21:4
Jesus stood ashore early. They did not recognize Him. 🌅
## John 21:5
He asked, “Children, any fish?” They answered, “No.” ❓
## John 21:6
He said, “Cast the net on the boat’s right side, and you will find fish.” They cast it and caught too many fish to haul in the net. 🐟
## John 21:7
Jesus’ beloved disciple told Peter, “The Lord!” Simon Peter put his removed outer garment back on and jumped into the sea. 🌊
## John 21:8
The other disciples came ashore in the boat, dragging the full net from about a hundred yards offshore. 🚤
## John 21:9
They landed near a charcoal fire with fish and bread. 🔥
## John 21:10
Jesus asked them to bring freshly caught fish. 🐟
## John 21:11
Simon Peter dragged 153 large fish ashore in an unbroken net. 🧺
## John 21:12
Jesus invited breakfast. None dared ask who He was, knowing He was the Lord. 🍞
## John 21:13
Jesus served bread and fish. 🐟
## John 21:14
This was His third appearance to His disciples after rising from death. 🌅
## John 21:15
Jesus asked Simon Peter, “Simon son of John, do you love Me more than these?” He answered, “Yes, Lord. You know I love You.” Jesus said, “Feed My lambs.” 🐑
## John 21:16
Jesus asked a second time, “Simon son of John, do you love Me?” He answered, “Yes, Lord. You know I love You.” Jesus said, “Shepherd My sheep.” 🐑
## John 21:17
Jesus asked a third time, “Simon son of John, do you love Me?” Hurt by the third question, Peter answered, “You know everything and know I love You.” Jesus said, “Feed My sheep. ❤️
## John 21:18
When young, you dressed and walked freely. When old, you will stretch out your hands. Another will dress you and lead you where you do not want to go.” 💬
## John 21:19
Jesus indicated Peter’s death that would glorify God. He said, “Follow Me.” ✝️
## John 21:20
Peter saw Jesus’ beloved disciple following. He had leaned against Jesus at supper and asked, “Lord, who will betray You?” 👥
## John 21:21
Peter asked, “Lord, what about him?” ❓
## John 21:22
Jesus answered, “If I want him to remain until I return, what is that to you? You follow Me.” 🚶
## John 21:23
The rumor spread among the brothers that this disciple would not die. Jesus did not say that, only, “If I want him to remain until I return, what is that to you?” 💬
## John 21:24
He is the disciple writing and testifying to these things. We know his testimony is true. 📖
## John 21:25
Jesus did much more. I suppose writing everything would require more books than the world could hold. 📚
