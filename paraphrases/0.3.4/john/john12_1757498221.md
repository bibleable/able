## John 12:1
Six days before Passover, Jesus came to Bethany, where Lazarus lived. Jesus had raised him from the dead. 🚶
## John 12:2
They prepared dinner. Martha served, and Lazarus ate with Jesus. 🍽️
## John 12:3
Mary poured about a pint of costly pure nard perfume on Jesus’ feet and wiped them with her hair. Its fragrance filled the house. 🏺
## John 12:4
Judas Iscariot, His disciple and coming betrayer, asked: 💬
## John 12:5
“Why not sell this for three hundred denarii and give the poor the money?” 🪙
## John 12:6
Judas was a thief, rather than concerned for the poor. He kept the money bag and stole from it. ⚠️
## John 12:7
Jesus said, “Leave her alone. She kept it for My burial. 🏺
## John 12:8
You always have the poor, but will not always have Me.” 💬
## John 12:9
Many Jews came to see Jesus and Lazarus, whom He raised from death. 👥
## John 12:10
Chief priests planned to kill Lazarus too, ⚠️
## John 12:11
because many Jews left them and believed in Jesus on account of Lazarus. 🙏
## John 12:12
Next day, the feast’s crowd heard Jesus was coming to Jerusalem. 👥
## John 12:13
They met Him with palm branches, shouting, “Hosanna! Blessed is He coming in the Lord’s name! Blessed is Israel’s King!” 👑
## John 12:14
Jesus sat on a young donkey, fulfilling Scripture: 🫏
## John 12:15
“Daughter of Zion, do not fear. Your King comes on a donkey’s colt.” 👑
## John 12:16
The disciples understood later, after Jesus was glorified. They remembered His treatment and the writings about Him. 📖
## John 12:17
Those witnessing Jesus raise Lazarus from the tomb kept testifying. 📣
## John 12:18
The crowd met Him because they heard this sign. 🙏
## John 12:19
Pharisees said, “Our efforts accomplish nothing. The whole world follows Him!” 💬
## John 12:20
Some Greeks came to worship at the feast. 👥
## John 12:21
They asked Philip of Bethsaida in Galilee, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.” 💬
## John 12:22
Philip told Andrew. Both told Jesus. 👥
## John 12:23
Jesus answered, “The Son of Man’s hour of glory has come. ✨
## John 12:24
I tell you the truth. A wheat seed must fall to the ground and die to produce much fruit. Otherwise, it remains only a seed. 🌾
## John 12:25
Loving your life loses it. Hating your life in this world keeps it for eternal life. 🌅
## John 12:26
My servants must follow Me and be where I am. The Father honors whoever serves Me. 🙏
## John 12:27
My soul is troubled. Should I say, ‘Father, save Me from this hour’? No. I came to this hour for this purpose. 💔
## John 12:28
Father, glorify Your name!” Heaven’s voice answered, “I glorified it and will again.” 📣
## John 12:29
Some heard thunder. Others said an angel spoke to Him. 💬
## John 12:30
Jesus said, “This voice is for you, rather than Me. 🙏
## John 12:31
This world is judged now. Its prince will be cast out. ⚖️
## John 12:32
When lifted from earth, I will draw everyone to Myself.” ✝️
## John 12:33
He said this to show how He would die. 📖
## John 12:34
They asked, “The Law says Christ remains forever. Why must the Son of Man be lifted? Who is He?” ❓
## John 12:35
Jesus answered, “Light remains briefly. Walk while you have it, so darkness does not overtake you. Someone walking in darkness cannot tell his destination. ✨
## John 12:36
Believe in the Light while you have it, to become sons of light.” He left and was hidden from them. 🙏
## John 12:37
Despite His many signs, they disbelieved. 💔
## John 12:38
This fulfilled Isaiah: “Lord, who believed our message? To whom was Your arm revealed?” 📖
## John 12:39
For this reason, they could not believe. Isaiah also said: 📖
## John 12:40
“He blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts. They cannot see with their eyes, understand with their hearts, and turn so I would heal them.” ⚖️
## John 12:41
Isaiah saw Jesus’ glory and spoke about Him. ✨
## John 12:42
Many leaders believed but hid it, fearing Pharisees would expel them from the synagogue. ⚠️
## John 12:43
They preferred human praise to God’s praise. 💔
## John 12:44
Jesus cried, “Believing Me also means believing the One who sent Me. 🙏
## John 12:45
Seeing Me means seeing the One who sent Me. 👁️
## John 12:46
I came as light so believers in Me would not remain in darkness. ✨
## John 12:47
I do not judge those hearing My words but not keeping them. I came to save the world, rather than judge it. ❤️
## John 12:48
My spoken word will judge whoever rejects Me and My words on the last day. ⚖️
## John 12:49
The Father sent Me and commanded what to say and how. I speak nothing independently. 🙏
## John 12:50
His command leads to eternal life. I speak exactly as My Father told Me.” 🌅
