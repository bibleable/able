## John 3:1
Nicodemus was a Pharisee and Jewish leader. 👥
## John 3:2
He visited Jesus at night. “Rabbi, we know God sent You to teach. No one could perform Your signs without God’s presence.” 🌌
## John 3:3
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. No one can see God’s kingdom unless born again.” 🙏
## John 3:4
Nicodemus asked, “How can an old man be born? Can he enter his mother’s womb a second time and be born?” ❓
## John 3:5
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. No one enters God’s kingdom unless born of water and the Spirit. 🕊️
## John 3:6
Flesh is born of flesh, but spirit is born of the Spirit. 🙏
## John 3:7
Do not be amazed that I said you must be born again. 💬
## John 3:8
Wind blows where it wishes. You hear it, but cannot tell its origin or destination. Everyone born of the Spirit is like this.” 🕊️
## John 3:9
Nicodemus asked, “How can this happen?” ❓
## John 3:10
Jesus answered, “You teach Israel, but do not understand? 📖
## John 3:11
I tell you the truth. We speak what we know and testify about what we saw. You do not accept our testimony. 💬
## John 3:12
If you disbelieve earthly things I tell you, how will you believe heavenly things? ❓
## John 3:13
No one has ascended to heaven except the Son of Man, who descended from heaven. ☁️
## John 3:14
As Moses lifted the snake in the wilderness, the Son of Man must be lifted, ✝️
## John 3:15
so everyone believing in Him may have eternal life. 🌅
## John 3:16
God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son. This was so everyone believing in Him would not perish but have eternal life. ❤️
## John 3:17
God sent His Son to save the world through Him, rather than condemn it. 🛡️
## John 3:18
Whoever believes in Him is not condemned. Whoever does not believe is already condemned for not believing in the name of God’s one and only Son. ⚖️
## John 3:19
The verdict is this: Light entered the world, but people loved darkness rather than Light because their actions were evil. ⚠️
## John 3:20
Evildoers hate the Light and avoid it, fearing their actions will be exposed. 💔
## John 3:21
Whoever practices truth enters the Light, so his actions may be clearly seen as accomplished in God.” ✨
## John 3:22
Jesus and His disciples went to Judea’s countryside. He spent time with them and baptized. 💧
## John 3:23
John baptized at Aenon near Salim because water was plentiful. People kept coming for baptism. 🌊
## John 3:24
John was not yet imprisoned. 📖
## John 3:25
John’s disciples disputed with a Jew over ceremonial washing. 💬
## John 3:26
They told John, “Rabbi, the One with you beyond the Jordan, whom you testified about, baptizes now. Everyone goes to Him!” 💬
## John 3:27
John answered, “A person can receive only what is given him from heaven. 🙏
## John 3:28
You can testify I said, ‘I am not the Christ. I am sent ahead of Him.’ 📖
## John 3:29
The bride belongs to the bridegroom. His friend stands and listens, overjoyed to hear his voice. That joy is mine, now complete. 💍
## John 3:30
He must increase. I must decrease. 🙏
## John 3:31
The One from above is above all. Someone from earth belongs to earth and speaks from earth. The One from heaven is above all. ☁️
## John 3:32
He testifies about what He saw and heard, but no one accepts it. 💬
## John 3:33
Whoever accepts His testimony certifies God’s truthfulness. 🙏
## John 3:34
The One God sent speaks God’s words. God gives the Spirit without limit. 🕊️
## John 3:35
The Father loves the Son and put everything in His hands. ❤️
## John 3:36
Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life. Whoever rejects Him will not see life. God’s wrath remains on him.” ⚖️
