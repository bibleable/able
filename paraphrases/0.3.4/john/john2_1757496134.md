## John 2:1
On the third day, a wedding took place at Cana in Galilee. Jesus’ mother was there. 💍
## John 2:2
Jesus and His disciples were invited too. 👥
## John 2:3
The wine ran out. Jesus’ mother told Him, “They have no wine.” 🍷
## John 2:4
Jesus answered, “Woman, what is that to you and Me? My hour has not come.” 💬
## John 2:5
She told the servants, “Do whatever He says.” 👂
## John 2:6
Six stone jars stood for Jewish purification rites. Each could hold twenty to thirty gallons. 🏺
## John 2:7
Jesus told the servants to fill them with water. They filled them to the brim. 💧
## John 2:8
He said, “Draw some and take it to the banquet’s master.” They obeyed, 🍷
## John 2:9
and he tasted water turned to wine. He did not know its source, but the servants knew. He called the bridegroom 💍
## John 2:10
and said, “People serve fine wine first and cheap wine after guests become drunk. You kept fine wine until now!” 🍷
## John 2:11
Jesus gave His first sign at Cana in Galilee, revealing His glory. His disciples believed in Him. ✨
## John 2:12
He went to Capernaum with His mother, brothers, and disciples. They stayed a few days. 🏠
## John 2:13
Near the Jewish Passover, Jesus went to Jerusalem. 🚶
## John 2:14
In the temple courts, He found cattle, sheep, and dove sellers and seated money changers. 🏛️
## John 2:15
He made a cord whip and drove all from the courts, both sheep and cattle. He poured out money changers’ coins and overturned their tables. ⚖️
## John 2:16
He told dove sellers, “Remove these! How dare you make My Father’s house a marketplace!” ⚠️
## John 2:17
His disciples remembered Scripture: “Zeal for Your house will consume Me.” 📖
## John 2:18
The Jews demanded, “What sign proves Your authority to do this?” ❓
## John 2:19
Jesus answered, “Destroy this temple, and I will raise it in three days.” 🏛️
## John 2:20
They replied, “Building it took forty-six years! Will You raise it in three days?” ❓
## John 2:21
Jesus meant His body when He spoke of the temple. ✝️
## John 2:22
After He rose from the dead, His disciples remembered His words. They believed Scripture and Jesus’ message. 🌅
## John 2:23
At Jerusalem’s Passover Feast, many saw His signs and believed in His name. 🙏
## John 2:24
Jesus did not entrust Himself to them because He knew everyone. 👁️
## John 2:25
He needed no testimony about people because He knew what was within a person. 💭
