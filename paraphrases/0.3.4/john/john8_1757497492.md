## John 8:1
Jesus went to the Mount of Olives. ⛰️
## John 8:2
Early, He returned to the temple courts. Everyone gathered, and He sat to teach. 📖
## John 8:3
Scribes and Pharisees brought a woman caught in adultery and stood her before them. ⚠️
## John 8:4
They said, “Teacher, she was caught committing adultery. 💬
## John 8:5
Moses’ Law commands stoning such women. What do You say?” ❓
## John 8:6
They tested Him to find grounds for accusation. Jesus bent and wrote on the ground with His finger. ⚖️
## John 8:7
They kept questioning. He stood and said, “Whoever among you has no sin should throw the first stone.” 🪨
## John 8:8
He bent and wrote again. 📖
## John 8:9
They left one by one, the older first. Only Jesus and the woman remained. 👥
## John 8:10
Jesus stood and asked, “Woman, where are your accusers? Did nobody condemn you?” ❓
## John 8:11
She answered, “Nobody, Lord.” Jesus said, “Neither do I. Go, and stop sinning.” ❤️
## John 8:12
Jesus said, “I am the world’s light. Followers of Me never walk in darkness, but have the light of life.” ✨
## John 8:13
Pharisees replied, “Your testimony about Yourself is invalid.” 💬
## John 8:14
Jesus said, “Even if I testify about Myself, My testimony is valid. I know where I came from and where I go. You know neither. 🙏
## John 8:15
You judge according to the flesh. I judge no one. ⚖️
## John 8:16
Even if I judge, My judgment is true. I am not alone, but with My Father who sent Me. 🙏
## John 8:17
Your Law validates two men’s testimony. 📖
## John 8:18
I testify about Myself. My Father, who sent Me, testifies too.” 🙏
## John 8:19
They asked where His Father was. Jesus answered, “You know neither Me nor My Father. Knowing Me would mean knowing Him too.” ❓
## John 8:20
He taught near the treasury in the temple courts. Nobody seized Him because His hour had not come. ⏳
## John 8:21
He said, “I go away. You will seek Me, but die in your sin. You cannot come where I go.” 💔
## John 8:22
The Jews asked, “Will He kill Himself? Is that why He says we cannot follow?” ❓
## John 8:23
Jesus answered, “You are from below, of this world. I am from above, not of this world. ☁️
## John 8:24
Unless you believe I am He, you will die in your sins. That is why I told you this.” ⚠️
## John 8:25
They asked who He was. Jesus answered, “What I have told you from the beginning. 💬
## John 8:26
I have much to say and judge about you. The One who sent Me is truthful. I tell the world what I heard from Him.” 📖
## John 8:27
They did not understand He meant the Father. 💭
## John 8:28
Jesus said, “When you lift the Son of Man, you will know I am He. I do nothing independently, speaking exactly what My Father taught. ✝️
## John 8:29
The One who sent Me remains with Me. He has not left Me alone because I always please Him.” 🙏
## John 8:30
Many believed as Jesus spoke. 🙏
## John 8:31
He told believing Jews, “If you remain in My word, you truly are My disciples. 📖
## John 8:32
You will know truth, and truth will free you.” 🛡️
## John 8:33
They answered, “We descend from Abraham and never were slaves. How will we be freed?” ❓
## John 8:34
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. Everyone sinning is sin’s slave. ⛓️
## John 8:35
A slave does not stay forever in the house. A son does. 🏠
## John 8:36
If the Son frees you, you are truly free. 🛡️
## John 8:37
I know your descent from Abraham. You seek to kill Me because My word has no place in you. ⚠️
## John 8:38
I speak what I saw with the Father. You do what you heard from your father.” 💬
## John 8:39
They answered, “Abraham is our father.” Jesus said, “If you were Abraham’s children, you would do his works. 📖
## John 8:40
You seek to kill Me, a man telling God’s truth. Abraham never acted like this. ⚠️
## John 8:41
You do your father’s works.” They answered, “We are not illegitimate. God is our only Father.” 💬
## John 8:42
Jesus said, “If God were your Father, you would love Me. I came from God because He sent Me, rather than independently. 🙏
## John 8:43
You cannot understand because you cannot accept My message. 💔
## John 8:44
Your father is the devil, whose desires you want to fulfill. He murdered from the beginning and rejected truth because none is in him. Lies are his native language. He is a liar and the father of lies. ⚠️
## John 8:45
You disbelieve Me because I tell truth! 💬
## John 8:46
Who can prove Me sinful? If I speak truth, why disbelieve? ❓
## John 8:47
God’s people hear His words. You do not hear because you do not belong to Him.” 🙏
## John 8:48
The Jews answered, “Are we not right? You are a Samaritan with a demon!” 💬
## John 8:49
Jesus said, “I have no demon. I honor My Father, and you dishonor Me. 🙏
## John 8:50
I do not seek My glory. One seeks it and judges. ⚖️
## John 8:51
I tell you the truth: whoever keeps My word never sees death.” 🌅
## John 8:52
The Jews answered, “You have a demon! Abraham and the prophets died. You claim whoever keeps Your word never tastes death. ❓
## John 8:53
Are You greater than Abraham, our father? He and the prophets died. Who do You claim to be?” ❓
## John 8:54
Jesus answered, “If I glorify Myself, My glory means nothing. My Father glorifies Me, and you call Him your God. 🙏
## John 8:55
You do not know Him. I know Him and keep His word. If I said I did not know Him, I would be a liar like you. 📖
## John 8:56
Your father Abraham rejoiced to see My day. He saw and rejoiced.” 🌅
## John 8:57
The Jews asked, “You are not yet fifty years old. Have You seen Abraham?” ❓
## John 8:58
Jesus declared, “I tell you the truth: before Abraham was born, I am!” ✨
## John 8:59
They took stones to throw. Jesus was hidden and left the temple area. 🪨
