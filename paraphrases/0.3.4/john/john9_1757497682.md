## John 9:1
Jesus saw a man blind from birth. 👁️
## John 9:2
His disciples asked, “Rabbi, did his sin or his parents’ sin cause his blindness from birth?” ❓
## John 9:3
Jesus answered, “Neither he nor his parents sinned. This happened to display God’s works in him. 🙏
## John 9:4
We must do the works of the One who sent Me while it is day. Night comes, when nobody can work. ⏳
## John 9:5
While here, I am the world’s light.” ✨
## John 9:6
Jesus spat on the ground, made mud, and put it on his eyes. 👁️
## John 9:7
He told him to wash in Siloam’s pool. Siloam means Sent. He washed and returned seeing. 💧
## John 9:8
Neighbors and those knowing him as a beggar asked, “Is he the man who sat begging?” ❓
## John 9:9
Some agreed. Others thought he only looked similar. He insisted, “I am the one.” 💬
## John 9:10
They asked how his eyes opened. ❓
## John 9:11
He answered, “Jesus put mud on my eyes and told me to wash at Siloam. I washed and saw.” 👁️
## John 9:12
They asked where Jesus was. He did not know. ❓
## John 9:13
They took him to the Pharisees. 👥
## John 9:14
Jesus made the mud and healed his eyes on the Sabbath. 📅
## John 9:15
Pharisees asked how he saw. He answered, “He put mud on my eyes. I washed and now see.” 💬
## John 9:16
Some Pharisees said, “He cannot be from God. He breaks the Sabbath.” Others asked, “Can a sinner do such signs?” They were divided. ❓
## John 9:17
They asked his opinion of his healer. He answered, “He is a prophet.” 🙏
## John 9:18
The Jews disbelieved his former blindness and healing until they summoned his parents 👥
## John 9:19
and asked, “Is he your son, born blind? How does he see now?” ❓
## John 9:20
They answered, “He is our son and was born blind. 💬
## John 9:21
We do not know his healer or how he sees. He is old enough. Ask him.” ❓
## John 9:22
They feared the Jews, who decided to expel anyone confessing Jesus as Christ from the synagogue. ⚠️
## John 9:23
That is why his parents said, “He is old enough. Ask him.” 💬
## John 9:24
They summoned him a second time: “Give God glory! We know this man sins.” ⚠️
## John 9:25
He answered, “I do not know whether He sins. I know I was blind, but now see!” 👁️
## John 9:26
They asked what He did to open his eyes. ❓
## John 9:27
He said, “I told you already, and you ignored me. Why hear again? Do you want to follow Him too?” 💬
## John 9:28
They insulted him: “You follow Him. We follow Moses. 📖
## John 9:29
God spoke to Moses. We do not know this man’s origin.” ❓
## John 9:30
He answered, “Remarkable! You cannot tell His origin, yet He opened my eyes. 👁️
## John 9:31
God does not hear sinners. He hears whoever worships Him and does His will. 🙏
## John 9:32
Nobody ever heard of opening the eyes of someone born blind. 👁️
## John 9:33
If He were not from God, He could do nothing like this.” 🙏
## John 9:34
They said, “You were born entirely sinful. Do you teach us?” They expelled him. ⚠️
## John 9:35
Jesus heard and found him. He asked, “Do you believe in the Son of Man?” ❓
## John 9:36
He asked, “Who is He, Sir? Tell me so I can believe.” 🙏
## John 9:37
Jesus answered, “You saw Him. He is speaking with you.” ✨
## John 9:38
He said, “Lord, I believe,” and worshiped Jesus. 🙏
## John 9:39
Jesus said, “I came for judgment, so the blind may see and those seeing may become blind.” ⚖️
## John 9:40
Pharisees nearby asked, “Are we blind too?” ❓
## John 9:41
Jesus answered, “If blind, you would have no guilt. You claim sight, so guilt remains.” ⚠️
