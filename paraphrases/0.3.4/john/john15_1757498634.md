## John 15:1
“I am the true vine. My Father keeps the vineyard. 🌱
## John 15:2
He cuts off fruitless branches in Me and prunes fruitful ones to produce more. 🌿
## John 15:3
My word already made you clean. 📖
## John 15:4
Remain in Me, and I will remain in you. A branch cannot bear fruit apart from the vine. Neither can you, apart from Me. 🌱
## John 15:5
I am the vine, and you are branches. Remaining in Me, and I in you, brings much fruit. Without Me, you can do nothing. 🌾
## John 15:6
Whoever does not remain in Me is like a discarded branch that withers. These are gathered, thrown into fire, and burned. 🔥
## John 15:7
If you remain in Me and My words in you, ask whatever you wish. It will be done for you. 🙏
## John 15:8
Much fruit glorifies My Father and proves your discipleship. 🌾
## John 15:9
I loved you as My Father loved Me. Remain in My love. ❤️
## John 15:10
If you keep My commands, you will remain in My love. I have kept My Father’s commands and remain in His love. 🙏
## John 15:11
I tell you this so My joy is in you and yours is complete. ❤️
## John 15:12
Love each other as I loved you. This is My command. 🤝
## John 15:13
Giving your life for friends is the greatest love. ❤️
## John 15:14
You are My friends if you obey My commands. 🤝
## John 15:15
I call you friends, rather than servants who do not know their master’s business. I revealed everything My Father taught Me. 📖
## John 15:16
You did not choose Me, but I chose you. I appointed you to go and bear lasting fruit. This was so the Father would give whatever you ask in My name. 🌾
## John 15:17
My command is to love each other. ❤️
## John 15:18
If the world hates you, remember it hated Me first. 🛡️
## John 15:19
The world would love you as its own if you belonged to it. It hates you because I chose you out of it. ⚠️
## John 15:20
No servant exceeds his master. Those persecuting Me will persecute you. Those keeping My word will keep yours. 📖
## John 15:21
They treat you this way for My name because they do not know the One who sent Me. 💔
## John 15:22
Without My coming and speaking, they would have no guilt of sin. Now they have no excuse. ⚖️
## John 15:23
Hating Me means hating My Father. 💔
## John 15:24
Without My works, unlike anyone else’s, they would have no guilt. Now they saw and hated both Me and My Father. ⚠️
## John 15:25
Their Law is fulfilled: ‘They hated Me without reason.’ 📖
## John 15:26
I will send the Advocate from the Father, the Spirit of truth proceeding from Him. He will testify about Me. 🕊️
## John 15:27
You must testify too, having been with Me from the beginning. 📣
