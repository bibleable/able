## John 14:1
“Do not be troubled. You believe in God. Believe in Me too. ❤️
## John 14:2
My Father’s house has many rooms. Would I tell you I go to prepare a place if this were untrue? 🏠
## John 14:3
If I go and prepare a place for you, I will return and welcome you into My presence. This is so you may also be where I am. ❤️
## John 14:4
You know My destination’s way.” 🚶
## John 14:5
Thomas answered, “Lord, we do not know Your destination. How can we know the way?” ❓
## John 14:6
Jesus said, “I am the way, truth, and life. Nobody comes to the Father except through Me. 🙏
## John 14:7
If you knew Me, you would know My Father. Now you know and have seen Him.” 👁️
## John 14:8
Philip said, “Lord, show the Father. That suffices.” ❓
## John 14:9
Jesus answered, “Philip, after so long, do you not know Me? Seeing Me is seeing the Father. Why ask to see Him? 👁️
## John 14:10
I am in the Father, and He in Me. Do you not believe? My words are not independent. My Father dwelling in Me does His works. 🙏
## John 14:11
Believe We are in each other, or believe because of the works. ✨
## John 14:12
I tell you the truth. Believers in Me will do My works and greater ones because I go to the Father. 🙏
## John 14:13
I will do whatever you ask in My name, so the Father is glorified in the Son. 🙏
## John 14:14
If you ask Me anything in My name, I will do it. 🙏
## John 14:15
If you love Me, you will keep My commands. ❤️
## John 14:16
I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Advocate to be with you forever, 🕊️
## John 14:17
the Spirit of truth. The world cannot receive Him, not seeing or knowing Him. You know Him because He remains with you and will be in you. 🕊️
## John 14:18
I will not leave you orphaned. I will come. ❤️
## John 14:19
The world soon stops seeing Me, but you will see Me. My life means you will live too. 🌅
## John 14:20
Then you will know I am in My Father, you in Me, and I in you. 🙏
## John 14:21
Whoever has and keeps My commands loves Me. My Father and I will love him, and I will reveal Myself.” ❤️
## John 14:22
Judas, not Iscariot, asked, “Lord, why reveal Yourself to us instead of the world?” ❓
## John 14:23
Jesus answered, “If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word. My Father will love him, and We will come and make Our home with him. 🏠
## John 14:24
Those not loving Me do not keep My words. My words come from the Father who sent Me. 📖
## John 14:25
I said this while still with you. 💬
## John 14:26
The Father will send the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, in My name. He will teach everything and remind you of all I said. 🕊️
## John 14:27
I give My peace, unlike the world’s giving. Do not be troubled or afraid. 🕊️
## John 14:28
I told you I go and return. Loving Me would mean rejoicing that I go to the Father, who is greater than I. 🙏
## John 14:29
I tell you beforehand so you will believe when it happens. 📖
## John 14:30
Our conversation soon ends. This world’s prince comes, with no claim on Me. ⚠️
## John 14:31
I obey My Father’s commands exactly so the world knows I love Him. Rise. Let us leave. ❤️
