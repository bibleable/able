## John 18:1
Jesus crossed the Kidron Valley with His disciples and entered a garden. 🌳
## John 18:2
Judas knew it, since Jesus often met His disciples there. ⚠️
## John 18:3
Judas brought soldiers and officers from the chief priests and Pharisees, carrying lanterns, torches, and weapons. 🔥
## John 18:4
Knowing everything ahead, Jesus asked whom they sought. ❓
## John 18:5
They answered, “Jesus of Nazareth.” He said, “I am He.” Judas stood with them. 💬
## John 18:6
Hearing “I am He,” they stepped back and fell. ⚠️
## John 18:7
He asked again. They answered, “Jesus of Nazareth.” 💬
## John 18:8
Jesus said, “I told you I am He. If you seek Me, let these men go.” 🛡️
## John 18:9
This fulfilled His words: “I lost none You gave Me.” 📖
## John 18:10
Simon Peter drew his sword and cut off the high priest’s servant Malchus’s right ear. ⚔️
## John 18:11
Jesus said, “Peter, sheath your sword! Should I not drink My Father’s cup?” 🍷
## John 18:12
Soldiers, their commander, and Jewish officers arrested and bound Jesus. ⛓️
## John 18:13
They first took Him to Annas, Caiaphas’s father-in-law. Caiaphas was high priest that year. 👥
## John 18:14
Caiaphas had advised that one man should die for the people. 💬
## John 18:15
Simon Peter and another disciple followed. The other, known to the high priest, entered the courtyard with Jesus. 👥
## John 18:16
Peter waited outside. The other disciple spoke to the doorkeeper and brought him in. 🚪
## John 18:17
The servant girl asked Peter, “Are you His disciple too?” He denied it. 💬
## John 18:18
Cold servants and officers gathered at a charcoal fire. Peter warmed himself there too. 🔥
## John 18:19
The high priest questioned Jesus about His teaching and disciples. ❓
## John 18:20
Jesus answered, “I taught publicly in synagogues and the temple where all Jews gather. I spoke nothing secretly. 📖
## John 18:21
Ask those hearing Me. They know My message. Why question Me?” ❓
## John 18:22
An officer slapped Him, asking, “Is that how You answer the high priest?” ⚠️
## John 18:23
Jesus answered, “Show My wrong words if I spoke wrongly. If rightly, why hit Me?” ⚖️
## John 18:24
Annas sent Him bound to Caiaphas, the high priest. ⛓️
## John 18:25
They asked Simon Peter, still warming himself, whether he followed Him. He denied it. 💬
## John 18:26
Malchus’s relative, a high priest’s servant, asked Peter, “Did I not see you in the garden with Him?” ❓
## John 18:27
Peter denied again. A rooster immediately crowed. 🐓
## John 18:28
Early, they took Jesus from Caiaphas to the Praetorium. The Jews stayed outside to remain clean for Passover. 📅
## John 18:29
Pilate asked outside, “What charge do you bring?” ⚖️
## John 18:30
They answered, “Would we surrender Him if He were no criminal?” 💬
## John 18:31
Pilate told them to judge Him by their law. They replied, “We are not permitted to execute anyone.” ⚖️
## John 18:32
This fulfilled Jesus’ words about how He would die. ✝️
## John 18:33
Pilate summoned Jesus inside the Praetorium: “Are You the Jews’ King?” 👑
## John 18:34
Jesus asked, “Your own question, or did others tell you about Me?” ❓
## John 18:35
Pilate answered, “Am I a Jew? Your people and chief priests surrendered You. What did You do?” 💬
## John 18:36
Jesus said, “My kingdom is not of this world. Otherwise, My servants would fight to prevent My arrest by the Jews. It is not of this realm.” 👑
## John 18:37
Pilate called Him king. Jesus answered, “You say I am king. I was born and came to testify to truth. Everyone belonging to truth hears Me.” 🙏
## John 18:38
Pilate asked, “What is truth?” Outside, he told the Jews, “I find no basis for a charge against Him. ⚖️
## John 18:39
By your Passover custom, I release one prisoner. Should I release the Jews’ King?” ❓
## John 18:40
They shouted for Barabbas instead. Barabbas took part in an uprising. ⚠️
