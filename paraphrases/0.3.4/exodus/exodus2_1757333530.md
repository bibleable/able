## Exodus 2:1
A man from Levi’s house married a Levite woman. 📖

## Exodus 2:2
She conceived and bore a son. Seeing he was beautiful, she hid him three months. 📖

## Exodus 2:3
She could no longer hide him. She coated a papyrus basket with tar and pitch and placed him inside. She set it among reeds at the Nile’s bank. 📖

## Exodus 2:4
His sister watched from a distance to see what would happen. 📖

## Exodus 2:5
Pharaoh’s daughter came to bathe in the Nile while her attendants walked along its bank. She saw the basket among reeds and sent her female servant to fetch it. 📖

## Exodus 2:6
She opened it and saw the crying boy. She pitied him. “This is a Hebrew child.” 📖

## Exodus 2:7
His sister asked, “Shall I find a Hebrew woman to nurse him for you?” 📖

## Exodus 2:8
Pharaoh’s daughter agreed. The girl fetched his mother. 📖

## Exodus 2:9
Pharaoh’s daughter said, “Nurse this child for me. I will pay you.” His mother took and nursed him. 📖

## Exodus 2:10
When he grew older, she brought him to Pharaoh’s daughter. He became her son. She named him Moses, saying, “I drew him from the water.” 📖

## Exodus 2:11
As an adult, Moses visited his own people and saw their hard labor. He saw an Egyptian beating one of his people, a Hebrew. 📖

## Exodus 2:12
He looked around and saw no one. He killed the Egyptian and hid his body in the sand. 📖

## Exodus 2:13
Next day, he saw two Hebrews fighting. He asked the one at fault, “Why attack your companion?” 📖

## Exodus 2:14
The man replied, “Who made you our ruler and judge? Are you planning to kill me like the Egyptian?” Moses feared and thought, “People know what I did.” 📖

## Exodus 2:15
Pharaoh heard and sought to kill Moses. Moses fled to Midian and settled there. He sat beside a well. 📖

## Exodus 2:16
Midian’s priest had seven daughters. They drew water and filled troughs for their father’s flock. 📖

## Exodus 2:17
Shepherds drove them away. Moses rose to help them and watered their flock. 📖

## Exodus 2:18
Their father Reuel asked, “Why return so early today?” 📖

## Exodus 2:19
They replied, “An Egyptian rescued us from shepherds. He drew water and watered our flock.” 📖

## Exodus 2:20
He asked, “Where is he? Why leave him behind? Invite him to eat.” 📖

## Exodus 2:21
Moses agreed to stay. Reuel gave him his daughter Zipporah in marriage. 📖

## Exodus 2:22
She bore a son. Moses named him Gershom, saying, “I became a foreigner in a foreign land.” 📖

## Exodus 2:23
Much later, Egypt’s king died. Israel groaned and cried under slavery. Their cry for release reached God. 📖

## Exodus 2:24
He heard their groaning and remembered His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

## Exodus 2:25
God saw Israel and took notice. 📖
