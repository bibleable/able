## Exodus 12:1
In Egypt, the LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Exodus 12:2
“This month will be your year’s first month. 📖

## Exodus 12:3
Tell Israel’s whole congregation: on this month’s tenth day, each man must choose one lamb per family household. 📖

## Exodus 12:4
If a household is too small, share with the nearest neighbor. Divide the lamb according to the number eating it. 📖

## Exodus 12:5
Choose a male aged one year without defect, from sheep or goats. 📖

## Exodus 12:6
Keep it until the fourteenth day. Israel’s whole assembly must kill the animals at twilight. 📖

## Exodus 12:7
Put some blood on the doorframes’ sides and tops where you eat the lambs. 📖

## Exodus 12:8
Eat the meat that night, roasted over fire, with bread without yeast and bitter herbs. 📖

## Exodus 12:9
Eat none raw or boiled. Roast its head, legs, and inner parts over fire. 📖

## Exodus 12:10
Leave none until morning. Burn leftovers before morning. 📖

## Exodus 12:11
Eat dressed for travel, sandals on your feet and staff in hand. Eat hurriedly. It is the LORD’s Passover. 📖

## Exodus 12:12
That night, I will pass through Egypt and kill every firstborn male, human and animal. I will judge all Egypt’s gods. I am the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 12:13
Blood on your houses will be a sign. Seeing it, I will pass over you. No destroying plague will strike you when I strike Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 12:14
Remember this day and celebrate the LORD’s feast. This is a permanent statute for future generations. 📖

## Exodus 12:15
Eat bread without yeast seven days. Remove yeast from your houses on the first day. Cut anyone eating food with yeast from the first through seventh day off from Israel. 📖

## Exodus 12:16
Hold sacred assemblies on the first and seventh days. Do no work except preparing meals. That is all you may do. 📖

## Exodus 12:17
Observe the Feast of Unleavened Bread. I brought your divisions from Egypt on this very day. Keep it as a permanent statute for future generations. 📖

## Exodus 12:18
Eat bread without yeast from the first month’s fourteenth evening until its twenty-first evening. 📖

## Exodus 12:19
Keep no yeast in your houses seven days. Cut anyone eating food with yeast off from Israel’s congregation, whether foreigner or native. 📖

## Exodus 12:20
Eat nothing with yeast. Eat bread without yeast in all your homes.” 📖

## Exodus 12:21
Moses told all Israel’s elders, “Select a lamb for each family immediately. Kill the Passover lamb. 📖

## Exodus 12:22
Dip a hyssop cluster in the basin’s blood. Brush the doorframe’s top and sides. Stay inside until morning. 📖

## Exodus 12:23
The LORD will pass through to strike Egypt. Seeing blood on the doorframe’s top and sides, He will pass over that doorway. He will not let the destroyer enter and strike you. 📖

## Exodus 12:24
Keep this command permanently, you and your descendants. 📖

## Exodus 12:25
Observe this service in the land the LORD promised to give you. 📖

## Exodus 12:26
Your children will ask, ‘What does this service mean to you?’ 📖

## Exodus 12:27
Answer, ‘It is the LORD’s Passover sacrifice. He passed over Israel’s houses when striking Egypt and spared our homes.’” The people bowed and worshiped. 📖

## Exodus 12:28
Israel obeyed everything the LORD commanded Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Exodus 12:29
At midnight, the LORD killed every firstborn male in Egypt. Pharaoh, who sat on the throne, and the prisoner in the dungeon both lost their firstborn. All firstborn livestock died too. 📖

## Exodus 12:30
Pharaoh, his officials, and all Egypt rose that night. Loud wailing filled Egypt. Every house had someone dead. 📖

## Exodus 12:31
He summoned Moses and Aaron that night. “Leave my people, you and Israel! Worship the LORD as requested. 📖

## Exodus 12:32
Take your flocks and herds as you said. Leave! Bless me too.” 📖

## Exodus 12:33
Egypt urged them to leave quickly. “Otherwise, we will all die!” 📖

## Exodus 12:34
They took dough before it was leavened. They carried kneading bowls on their shoulders, wrapped in clothing. 📖

## Exodus 12:35
Following Moses’ instructions, Israel asked Egypt for silver and gold articles and clothing. 📖

## Exodus 12:36
The LORD gave Israel favor, so Egypt granted their requests. Thus they plundered Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 12:37
Israel traveled from Rameses to Succoth. About 600,000 men walked, besides women and children. 📖

## Exodus 12:38
A mixed multitude went with them, and vast flocks and herds. 📖

## Exodus 12:39
They baked their Egyptian dough into loaves without yeast. Driven out, they could not delay or prepare provisions. 📖

## Exodus 12:40
Israel stayed in Egypt 430 years. 📖

## Exodus 12:41
Exactly 430 years later, to the day, all the LORD’s divisions left Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 12:42
The LORD kept watch that night to bring them from Egypt. All Israel must keep that night as a vigil to Him throughout future generations. 📖

## Exodus 12:43
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, “This is Passover’s statute. No foreigner may eat it. 📖

## Exodus 12:44
A purchased slave may eat after you circumcise him. 📖

## Exodus 12:45
No temporary resident or hired worker may eat Passover. 📖

## Exodus 12:46
Eat it inside one house. Take no meat outside. Break no bones. 📖

## Exodus 12:47
Israel’s whole congregation must celebrate it. 📖

## Exodus 12:48
If a foreign resident wants to celebrate the LORD’s Passover, circumcise every male in his household first. Then he may celebrate like a native. No uncircumcised man may eat it. 📖

## Exodus 12:49
The same law applies to natives and foreign residents.” 📖

## Exodus 12:50
All Israel did exactly as the LORD commanded Moses and Aaron. 📖

## Exodus 12:51
That very day, the LORD brought Israel from Egypt by their divisions. 📖
