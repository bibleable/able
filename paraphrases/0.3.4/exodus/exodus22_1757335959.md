## Exodus 22:1
“If a man steals an ox or sheep and kills or sells it, he must repay five oxen per ox or four sheep per sheep. 📖

## Exodus 22:2
If a thief is caught breaking in and beaten to death, no one bears bloodguilt. 📖

## Exodus 22:3
After sunrise, however, there is bloodguilt. A thief must repay fully. If he has nothing, sell him for his theft. 📖

## Exodus 22:4
If the stolen ox, donkey, or sheep is found alive with him, he must repay double. 📖

## Exodus 22:5
If someone lets his grazing livestock stray into another’s field, he must compensate from the best of his field or vineyard. 📖

## Exodus 22:6
If fire spreads through thornbushes and consumes stacked grain, standing grain, or a whole field, its starter must repay fully. 📖

## Exodus 22:7
Money or goods given to a neighbor for safekeeping may be stolen from his house. If caught, the thief must repay double. 📖

## Exodus 22:8
If the thief is not found, the householder must appear before judges. They must determine whether he took his neighbor’s property. 📖

## Exodus 22:9
In disputes over illegal possession of an ox, donkey, sheep, clothing, or lost item claimed as one’s own, bring both parties before judges. The guilty party must repay his neighbor double. 📖

## Exodus 22:10
Someone may leave an ox, donkey, sheep, or other animal with a neighbor for care. It may die, suffer injury, or be stolen without witnesses. 📖

## Exodus 22:11
Make an oath before the LORD between the parties to determine whether the caretaker took his neighbor’s property. The owner must accept it without demanding compensation. 📖

## Exodus 22:12
If the animal was actually stolen from the caretaker, he must compensate its owner. 📖

## Exodus 22:13
If it was torn apart, bring its remains as evidence. No compensation is required. 📖

## Exodus 22:14
If a borrowed animal dies or is injured without its owner present, the borrower must repay fully. 📖

## Exodus 22:15
No repayment is required if its owner was present. For a rented animal, the fee covers the loss. 📖

## Exodus 22:16
If a man seduces and sleeps with an unmarried virgin not pledged in marriage, he must pay the full dowry to marry her. 📖

## Exodus 22:17
If her father refuses absolutely, he must still pay the normal bridal price for a virgin. 📖

## Exodus 22:18
Do not let a sorceress live. 📖

## Exodus 22:19
Execute anyone having sexual relations with an animal. 📖

## Exodus 22:20
Anyone sacrificing to a god other than the LORD alone must be set apart for destruction. 📖

## Exodus 22:21
Do not exploit or oppress foreign residents. You were foreigners in Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 22:22
Mistreat no widow or orphan. 📖

## Exodus 22:23
If you mistreat them and they cry to Me in distress, I will hear. 📖

## Exodus 22:24
My anger will burn. I will kill you with the sword, making your wives widows and your children fatherless. 📖

## Exodus 22:25
If you lend money to one of My poor people among you, do not act as a creditor or charge interest. 📖

## Exodus 22:26
If you take your neighbor’s cloak as security, return it by sunset. 📖

## Exodus 22:27
It is his only covering. What else can he sleep in? I am compassionate and will hear his cry. 📖

## Exodus 22:28
Do not blaspheme God or curse your people’s ruler. 📖

## Exodus 22:29
Do not withhold offerings from your grain stores or vats. Give Me your firstborn sons. 📖

## Exodus 22:30
Do likewise with cattle and sheep. Let the young stay with their mothers seven days. Give them to Me on the eighth. 📖

## Exodus 22:31
Be My holy people. Do not eat meat from an animal mauled in the field. Throw it to dogs. 📖
