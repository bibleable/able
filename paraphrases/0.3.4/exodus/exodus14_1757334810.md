## Exodus 14:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 14:2
“Tell Israel to turn back. Camp by the sea before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, opposite Baal-zephon. 📖

## Exodus 14:3
Pharaoh will think you wander in confusion, trapped by the wilderness. 📖

## Exodus 14:4
I will harden his heart so he pursues you. I will gain honor through Pharaoh and his whole army. Egypt will know I am the LORD.” Israel obeyed. 📖

## Exodus 14:5
Pharaoh heard Israel fled. He and his officials changed their minds. “What have we done, releasing Israel from our service?” 📖

## Exodus 14:6
He prepared his chariot and took his army. 📖

## Exodus 14:7
He took 600 best chariots and all Egypt’s other chariots, with officers over them all. 📖

## Exodus 14:8
The LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt. Pharaoh chased Israel, who marched out defiantly. 📖

## Exodus 14:9
All Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, horsemen, and troops caught Israel camping by the sea near Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon. 📖

## Exodus 14:10
Israel saw Pharaoh and Egypt’s army approaching. Terrified, they cried to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 14:11
They told Moses, “Did Egypt lack graves, so you brought us here to die? Why bring us from Egypt? 📖

## Exodus 14:12
We told you there to leave us serving Egypt. Slavery would be better than dying in the wilderness.” 📖

## Exodus 14:13
Moses said, “Do not fear. Stand firm and see the LORD save you today. You will never again see these Egyptians you see today. 📖

## Exodus 14:14
He will fight for you. You need only be still.” 📖

## Exodus 14:15
The LORD told Moses, “Why cry to Me? Tell Israel to advance. 📖

## Exodus 14:16
Raise your staff and stretch your hand over the sea. Divide it so Israel walks through on dry ground. 📖

## Exodus 14:17
I will harden Egypt’s hearts so they follow. I will gain honor through Pharaoh, his whole army, chariots, and horsemen. 📖

## Exodus 14:18
Egypt will know I am the LORD when I gain honor through Pharaoh, his chariots, and horsemen.” 📖

## Exodus 14:19
God’s angel moved from Israel’s front to their rear. The cloud pillar moved behind them too. 📖

## Exodus 14:20
It stood between Egypt’s and Israel’s camps. The cloud was in darkness but lit the night. Neither camp approached the other all night. 📖

## Exodus 14:21
Moses stretched his hand over the sea. The LORD drove it back all night with a strong east wind. Dry ground appeared, and the waters divided. 📖

## Exodus 14:22
Israel walked through on dry ground. Water stood as walls on their right and left. 📖

## Exodus 14:23
All Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, and horsemen chased them into the sea. 📖

## Exodus 14:24
During morning watch, the LORD looked from the fire and cloud pillar at Egypt’s army. He threw their camp into confusion. 📖

## Exodus 14:25
He made chariot wheels wobble, making the chariots difficult to drive. Egypt said, “Flee! The LORD fights for Israel against Egypt!” 📖

## Exodus 14:26
The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand over the sea. Water will return over the Egyptians, their chariots, and their horsemen.” 📖

## Exodus 14:27
Moses stretched his hand. At daybreak, the sea returned to normal. Egypt retreated, but the LORD swept them into the sea. 📖

## Exodus 14:28
Water covered the chariots and horsemen, Pharaoh’s entire pursuing army in the sea. No one survived. 📖

## Exodus 14:29
Israel walked on dry ground with water walls to their right and left. 📖

## Exodus 14:30
The LORD saved Israel from Egypt that day. Israel saw Egyptians dead on the shore. 📖

## Exodus 14:31
Seeing His great power against Egypt, Israel feared the LORD and believed in Him and His servant Moses. 📖
