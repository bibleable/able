## Exodus 6:1
The LORD said, “Now see what I will do to Pharaoh. My mighty hand will make him release the people. My strong hand will make him drive them from his land.” 📖

## Exodus 6:2
God told Moses, “I am the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 6:3
I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty. I did not make Myself known to them by My name the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 6:4
I covenanted to give them Canaan, where they lived as foreigners. 📖

## Exodus 6:5
I heard Israel groaning under Egyptian slavery and remembered My covenant. 📖

## Exodus 6:6
Tell Israel, ‘I am the LORD. I will remove Egypt’s yoke and free you from bondage. I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and mighty judgments. 📖

## Exodus 6:7
I will take you as My people and be your God. You will know I am the LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt’s yoke. 📖

## Exodus 6:8
I will bring you into the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I will give it to you to possess. I am the LORD.’” 📖

## Exodus 6:9
Moses told Israel, but their broken spirits and cruel slavery kept them from listening. 📖

## Exodus 6:10
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 6:11
“Tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to release Israel from his land.” 📖

## Exodus 6:12
Moses replied before the LORD, “Israel will not listen. Why would Pharaoh listen when my speech is unskilled?” 📖

## Exodus 6:13
The LORD commanded Moses and Aaron regarding Israel and Pharaoh king of Egypt. They must bring Israel from Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 6:14
These were their ancestral family heads. Reuben, Israel’s firstborn, had sons Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. These were Reuben’s clans. 📖

## Exodus 6:15
Simeon’s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mother was Canaanite. These were Simeon’s clans. 📖

## Exodus 6:16
Levi’s sons in the records were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Levi lived 137 years. 📖

## Exodus 6:17
Gershon’s sons by clans were Libni and Shimei. 📖

## Exodus 6:18
Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. Kohath lived 133 years. 📖

## Exodus 6:19
Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. These were the recorded Levite clans. 📖

## Exodus 6:20
Amram married his father’s sister Jochebed. She bore Aaron and Moses. Amram lived 137 years. 📖

## Exodus 6:21
Izhar’s sons were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri. 📖

## Exodus 6:22
Uzziel’s sons were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri. 📖

## Exodus 6:23
Aaron married Elisheba, Amminadab’s daughter and Nahshon’s sister. She bore Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 📖

## Exodus 6:24
Korah’s sons were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. These were the Korahite clans. 📖

## Exodus 6:25
Aaron’s son Eleazar married a daughter of Putiel. She bore Phinehas. These were the Levite family heads by clans. 📖

## Exodus 6:26
The LORD told this Aaron and Moses, “Bring Israel from Egypt by their divisions.” 📖

## Exodus 6:27
This Moses and Aaron spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt to bring Israel from Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 6:28
On the day the LORD spoke to Moses in Egypt, 📖

## Exodus 6:29
He said, “I am the LORD. Tell Pharaoh king of Egypt everything I tell you.” 📖

## Exodus 6:30
Moses replied before the LORD, “Why would Pharaoh listen to my unskilled speech?” 📖
