## Exodus 20:1
God spoke these words. 📖

## Exodus 20:2
“I am the LORD your God. I brought you from Egypt’s slavery. 📖

## Exodus 20:3
Have no other gods before Me. 📖

## Exodus 20:4
Make no idol resembling anything in heaven above, earth below, or waters beneath. 📖

## Exodus 20:5
Do not bow to or worship them. I, the LORD your God, am jealous. I visit fathers’ sins on children to the third and fourth generations of those hating Me. 📖

## Exodus 20:6
I show loving devotion to a thousand generations loving Me and keeping My commands. 📖

## Exodus 20:7
Do not take the LORD your God’s name in vain. He will punish anyone doing so. 📖

## Exodus 20:8
Remember the Sabbath and keep it holy. 📖

## Exodus 20:9
Work six days and do all your labor. 📖

## Exodus 20:10
The seventh is the LORD your God’s Sabbath. Neither you, your sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, livestock, nor foreigners within your gates may work. 📖

## Exodus 20:11
He made heaven, earth, sea, and everything in them in six days. He rested on the seventh. Therefore, He blessed the Sabbath and made it holy. 📖

## Exodus 20:12
Honor your father and mother to live long in the land the LORD your God gives you. 📖

## Exodus 20:13
Do not murder. 📖

## Exodus 20:14
Do not commit adultery. 📖

## Exodus 20:15
Do not steal. 📖

## Exodus 20:16
Do not testify falsely against your neighbor. 📖

## Exodus 20:17
Do not covet your neighbor’s house, wife, male servant, female servant, ox, donkey, or anything belonging to him.” 📖

## Exodus 20:18
Everyone heard thunder and the ram’s horn and saw lightning and mountain smoke. They trembled and stood far away. 📖

## Exodus 20:19
They told Moses, “Speak yourself, and we will listen. But do not let God speak to us, or we will die.” 📖

## Exodus 20:20
He replied, “Do not fear. God came to test you, so you fear Him and avoid sin.” 📖

## Exodus 20:21
They stood far away. Moses approached the thick darkness where God was. 📖

## Exodus 20:22
The LORD said, “Tell Israel, ‘You saw Me speak from heaven. 📖

## Exodus 20:23
Make no gods alongside Me. Make no silver or gold gods. 📖

## Exodus 20:24
Make an earthen altar for Me. Sacrifice burnt and peace offerings there, your sheep, goats, and cattle. Wherever I cause My name to be remembered, I will come and bless you. 📖

## Exodus 20:25
If you build Me a stone altar, use no stones shaped by tools. Using a chisel on it defiles it. 📖

## Exodus 20:26
Do not climb steps to My altar, exposing your nakedness.’ 📖
