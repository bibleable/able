## Exodus 5:1
Moses and Aaron told Pharaoh, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘Release My people to celebrate My feast in the wilderness.’” 📖

## Exodus 5:2
He replied, “Who is the LORD, that I should obey and release Israel? I do not know Him. I will not release Israel.” 📖

## Exodus 5:3
They answered, “The Hebrews’ God met us. Permit a three-day wilderness journey to sacrifice to the LORD our God. Otherwise, He may strike us with plague or sword.” 📖

## Exodus 5:4
Egypt’s king said, “Moses and Aaron, why distract people from work? Return to your labor!” 📖

## Exodus 5:5
He added, “The land’s people are numerous. You would stop them working.” 📖

## Exodus 5:6
That day, he commanded the taskmasters and foremen, 📖

## Exodus 5:7
“Stop supplying brickmaking straw. They must collect their own. 📖

## Exodus 5:8
Demand the same number of bricks. Do not reduce it. They are lazy. That is why they ask to sacrifice to their God. 📖

## Exodus 5:9
Make their work harder, keeping them occupied so they ignore these lies.” 📖

## Exodus 5:10
The taskmasters and foremen told them, “Pharaoh says, ‘I will supply no straw. 📖

## Exodus 5:11
Find your own wherever you can. Your workload will not decrease.’” 📖

## Exodus 5:12
They scattered throughout Egypt, gathering stubble for straw. 📖

## Exodus 5:13
The taskmasters insisted, “Make your required number daily, as when straw was supplied.” 📖

## Exodus 5:14
Pharaoh’s taskmasters beat the Israelite foremen they appointed. They asked, “Why did you miss the required brick totals yesterday and today?” 📖

## Exodus 5:15
The foremen appealed to Pharaoh, “Why treat your servants this way? 📖

## Exodus 5:16
You give no straw but demand bricks. We are beaten, though your own people are at fault.” 📖

## Exodus 5:17
He replied, “You are slackers! Slackers! That is why you ask to sacrifice to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 5:18
Work! You will receive no straw but must deliver the full brick total.” 📖

## Exodus 5:19
The Israelite foremen knew they were in trouble when ordered not to reduce daily brick totals. 📖

## Exodus 5:20
Leaving Pharaoh, they met Moses and Aaron waiting for them. 📖

## Exodus 5:21
They said, “May the LORD see and judge you! You made us a stench before Pharaoh and his officials. You put a sword in their hands to kill us!” 📖

## Exodus 5:22
Moses returned to the LORD. “Lord, why bring trouble on these people? Why send me? 🙏

## Exodus 5:23
Since I spoke to Pharaoh in Your name, he has oppressed them. You have not delivered Your people at all.” 🙏
