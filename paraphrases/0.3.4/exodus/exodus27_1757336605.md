## Exodus 27:1
“Build an acacia altar, square, five cubits long and five wide. Make it three cubits high. 📖

## Exodus 27:2
Make horns at its four corners as one piece with the altar. Cover it with bronze. 📖

## Exodus 27:3
Make bronze utensils: ash pots, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans. 📖

## Exodus 27:4
Make a bronze mesh grate with a bronze ring at each of its four corners. 📖

## Exodus 27:5
Place it beneath the altar’s ledge, reaching halfway up the altar. 📖

## Exodus 27:6
Make acacia carrying poles covered with bronze. 📖

## Exodus 27:7
Insert them through rings on two sides of the altar for carrying. 📖

## Exodus 27:8
Make it hollow from boards, as shown on the mountain. 📖

## Exodus 27:9
Make a courtyard for the tabernacle. Its south side must have finely spun linen curtains a hundred cubits long, 📖

## Exodus 27:10
with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. Make silver hooks and bands for the posts. 📖

## Exodus 27:11
On the north, make curtains a hundred cubits long, twenty posts, and twenty bronze bases. Add silver hooks and bands. 📖

## Exodus 27:12
Make the western curtains fifty cubits wide, with ten posts and ten bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:13
The eastern side, toward sunrise, must be fifty cubits wide. 📖

## Exodus 27:14
Make curtains fifteen cubits long on one side, with three posts and three bases, 📖

## Exodus 27:15
and fifteen cubits on the other, with three posts and three bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:16
Make the courtyard gate twenty cubits long. Its embroidered curtain must use blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. Give it four posts and four bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:17
All courtyard posts must have silver bands, silver hooks, and bronze bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:18
Make the whole courtyard a hundred cubits long and fifty wide. Its finely spun linen curtains must be five cubits high, with bronze bases. 📖

## Exodus 27:19
Make every tabernacle utensil, all its tent pegs, and all courtyard tent pegs from bronze. 📖

## Exodus 27:20
Command Israel to bring pure pressed olive oil for lighting. Keep the lamps burning continually. 📖

## Exodus 27:21
Aaron and his sons must tend the lamps before the LORD from evening to morning. Do this in the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil before the Testimony. This is a permanent statute for Israel’s future generations. 📖
