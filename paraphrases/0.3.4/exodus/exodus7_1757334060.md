## Exodus 7:1
The LORD said, “I made you like God to Pharaoh. Your brother Aaron will be your prophet. 📖

## Exodus 7:2
Speak everything I command. Aaron must tell Pharaoh to release Israel from his land. 📖

## Exodus 7:3
I will harden Pharaoh’s heart. I will multiply signs and wonders in Egypt, 📖

## Exodus 7:4
but he will not listen. I will lay My hand on Egypt. Through mighty judgments, I will bring the divisions of My people Israel out of Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 7:5
When I stretch My hand against Egypt and bring Israel out, Egypt will know I am the LORD.” 📖

## Exodus 7:6
Moses and Aaron obeyed exactly. 📖

## Exodus 7:7
Moses was eighty and Aaron eighty-three when they spoke to Pharaoh. 📖

## Exodus 7:8
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖

## Exodus 7:9
“When Pharaoh demands a miracle, tell Aaron to throw his staff before him. It will become a serpent.” 📖

## Exodus 7:10
They obeyed before Pharaoh. Aaron threw his staff before him and his officials. It became a serpent. 📖

## Exodus 7:11
Pharaoh called Egypt’s wise men, sorcerers, and magicians. They did the same through magic. 📖

## Exodus 7:12
Each threw down a staff, which became a serpent. Aaron’s staff swallowed their staffs. 📖

## Exodus 7:13
Pharaoh’s heart remained hard. He refused to listen, as the LORD said. 📖

## Exodus 7:14
The LORD told Moses, “Pharaoh’s heart is stubborn. He refuses to release the people. 📖

## Exodus 7:15
Meet him in the morning as he goes to the water. Wait at the Nile’s bank with the staff that became a snake. 📖

## Exodus 7:16
Tell him, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, sent me to demand His people’s release to worship in the wilderness. You have refused until now. 📖

## Exodus 7:17
The LORD says, this will show that I am the LORD. With the staff in my hand, I will strike the Nile’s water. It will become blood. 📖

## Exodus 7:18
Its fish will die. The Nile will stink, and Egypt will be unable to drink its water.’” 📖

## Exodus 7:19
The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to stretch his staff and hand over Egypt’s rivers, canals, ponds, and reservoirs. The water will become blood throughout Egypt, even in wooden and stone vessels.” 📖

## Exodus 7:20
They obeyed. Before Pharaoh and his officials, Aaron raised the staff and struck the Nile. All its water became blood. 📖

## Exodus 7:21
Its fish died, and its stench prevented Egypt from drinking the water. Blood was throughout Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 7:22
Egypt’s magicians did the same through magic. Pharaoh’s heart remained hard. He refused to listen, as the LORD said. 📖

## Exodus 7:23
He returned to his palace without taking any of this to heart. 📖

## Exodus 7:24
All Egypt dug around the Nile for drinking water. They could not drink from the river. 📖

## Exodus 7:25
Seven full days passed after the LORD struck the Nile. 📖
