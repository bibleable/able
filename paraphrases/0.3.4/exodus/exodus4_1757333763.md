## Exodus 4:1
Moses asked, “What if they do not believe or listen? They may say, ‘The LORD did not appear to you.’” 📖

## Exodus 4:2
The LORD asked, “What is in your hand?” He replied, “A staff.” 📖

## Exodus 4:3
The LORD said, “Throw it down.” Moses did, and it became a snake. He ran away. 📖

## Exodus 4:4
The LORD said, “Grab its tail.” Moses reached and caught it. It became a staff in his hand. 📖

## Exodus 4:5
“This will help them believe their fathers’ God, the LORD, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, appeared to you.” 📖

## Exodus 4:6
The LORD told Moses, “Put your hand inside your cloak.” He did and withdrew it. His hand was leprous, white as snow. 📖

## Exodus 4:7
He said, “Put it back.” Moses did and withdrew it again. It was restored like his other skin. 📖

## Exodus 4:8
The LORD said, “If they refuse to believe you or heed the first sign’s witness, they may believe the second. 📖

## Exodus 4:9
If they reject both signs and refuse to listen, take Nile water and pour it on dry ground. It will become blood there.” 📖

## Exodus 4:10
Moses replied, “Please, Lord, I have never spoken eloquently, before or since You spoke to me, Your servant. My speech and tongue are slow.” 📖

## Exodus 4:11
The LORD asked, “Who made human mouths? Who makes people mute, deaf, sighted, or blind? Is it not I, the LORD? 📖

## Exodus 4:12
Go! I will help you speak and teach you what to say.” 📖

## Exodus 4:13
Moses replied, “Please, Lord, send someone else.” 📖

## Exodus 4:14
The LORD became angry. “Your brother Aaron the Levite speaks well. He comes to meet you and will rejoice to see you. 📖

## Exodus 4:15
Tell him the words to say. I will help you both speak and teach you what to do. 📖

## Exodus 4:16
He will speak for you to the people. He will be your spokesman, and you will be as God to him. 📖

## Exodus 4:17
Take this staff to perform the signs.” 📖

## Exodus 4:18
Moses told his father-in-law Jethro, “Let me visit my brothers in Egypt to see whether they live.” Jethro replied, “Go peacefully.” 📖

## Exodus 4:19
The LORD had told Moses in Midian, “Return to Egypt. Everyone who sought to kill you is dead.” 📖

## Exodus 4:20
Moses put his wife and sons on a donkey and returned toward Egypt. He carried God’s staff. 📖

## Exodus 4:21
The LORD said, “Perform before Pharaoh all the wonders I enabled you to do. I will harden his heart, so he refuses to release the people. 📖

## Exodus 4:22
Tell him, ‘The LORD says, Israel is My firstborn son. 📖

## Exodus 4:23
I told you to release My son to worship Me. You refused. Therefore, I will kill your firstborn son.’” 📖

## Exodus 4:24
At a lodging place on the way, the LORD met Moses and was about to kill him. 📖

## Exodus 4:25
Zipporah used a flint knife to remove her son’s foreskin and touched it to Moses’ feet. She said, “You are a bridegroom of blood to me.” 📖

## Exodus 4:26
The LORD left him alone. Her words “bridegroom of blood” referred to circumcision. 📖

## Exodus 4:27
The LORD told Aaron to meet Moses in the wilderness. He met him at God’s mountain and kissed him. 📖

## Exodus 4:28
Moses told Aaron everything the LORD sent him to say and all the signs He commanded him to perform. 📖

## Exodus 4:29
Moses and Aaron gathered all Israel’s elders. 📖

## Exodus 4:30
Aaron told them everything the LORD told Moses. Moses performed the signs before the people. 📖

## Exodus 4:31
They believed. Hearing the LORD attended to Israel and saw their suffering, they bowed and worshiped. 📖
