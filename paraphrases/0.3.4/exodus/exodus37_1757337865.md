## Exodus 37:1
Bezalel made the ark from acacia wood. It was two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one and a half cubits high. 📖

## Exodus 37:2
He covered the inside and outside with pure gold. He made a gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 37:3
He cast four gold rings for its four feet. Two rings were on each side. 📖

## Exodus 37:4
He made acacia wood poles and covered them with gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:5
He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the ark to carry it. 📖

## Exodus 37:6
He made a mercy seat from pure gold. It was two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. 📖

## Exodus 37:7
He made two cherubim from hammered gold at the ends of the mercy seat. 📖

## Exodus 37:8
One cherub was at each end. He made everything from one piece of gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:9
The cherubim spread their wings upward to shade the mercy seat. They faced each other and looked toward the mercy seat. 📖

## Exodus 37:10
He made an acacia wood table. It was two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high. 📖

## Exodus 37:11
He covered it with pure gold and made a gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 37:12
He made a rim around it, one handbreadth wide. He put a gold molding on the rim. 📖

## Exodus 37:13
He cast four gold rings for the table. He attached them at its four corners, at the four legs. 📖

## Exodus 37:14
The rings were close to the rim. They held the poles used to carry the table. 📖

## Exodus 37:15
He made acacia wood poles to carry the table. He covered them with gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:16
He made all the table utensils from pure gold. These were its plates, dishes, and the bowls and pitchers for pouring drink offerings. 📖

## Exodus 37:17
He made the lampstand from pure hammered gold. Its base, shaft, cups, buds, and petals were all one piece. 📖

## Exodus 37:18
Six branches extended from its sides, three on each side. 📖

## Exodus 37:19
Each of the six branches had three cups shaped like almond blossoms. Each cup had buds and petals. 📖

## Exodus 37:20
The lampstand had four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with buds and petals. 📖

## Exodus 37:21
A bud was below the first pair of branches. A bud was below the second pair, and another was below the third pair. 📖

## Exodus 37:22
The buds and branches were one piece with the lampstand. He hammered the whole object from pure gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:23
He also made its seven lamps, wick trimmers, and trays from pure gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:24
He used one talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all its utensils. 📖

## Exodus 37:25
He made the incense altar from acacia wood. It was square, one cubit long and one cubit wide. It was two cubits high. Its horns formed one piece with it. 📖

## Exodus 37:26
He covered the top, all the sides, and the horns with pure gold. He made a gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 37:27
He made two gold rings below the molding on opposite sides. They held the poles used to carry it. 📖

## Exodus 37:28
He made the poles from acacia wood and covered them with gold. 📖

## Exodus 37:29
He also made the sacred anointing oil and the pure, fragrant incense with the skill of a perfumer. 📖
