## Exodus 23:1
“Spread no false report. Do not help the wicked as a malicious witness. 📖

## Exodus 23:2
Do not follow crowds in wrongdoing. Do not twist justice by siding with the crowd when testifying in a lawsuit. 📖

## Exodus 23:3
Do not favor a poor man in his lawsuit. 📖

## Exodus 23:4
Return your enemy’s stray ox or donkey. 📖

## Exodus 23:5
If someone who hates you has a donkey fallen under its load, do not abandon it. Help him with it. 📖

## Exodus 23:6
Do not deny poor people justice in lawsuits. 📖

## Exodus 23:7
Avoid false accusations. Kill neither innocent nor righteous people. I will not acquit the guilty. 📖

## Exodus 23:8
Accept no bribe. Bribes blind those who see and twist righteous people’s words. 📖

## Exodus 23:9
Do not oppress foreign residents. You know their feelings because you were foreigners in Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 23:10
Plant and harvest your land six years. 📖

## Exodus 23:11
Let it rest unplanted in the seventh. Poor people may eat its produce, and wild animals what remains. Do the same with vineyards and olive groves. 📖

## Exodus 23:12
Work six days and rest on the seventh. Your ox and donkey can rest, and your female servant’s son and foreign residents can be refreshed. 📖

## Exodus 23:13
Attend to everything I said. Do not invoke other gods’ names. Let none be heard on your lips. 📖

## Exodus 23:14
Celebrate My feast three times yearly. 📖

## Exodus 23:15
Observe the Feast of Unleavened Bread as commanded. Eat bread without yeast seven days in Abib, at the appointed time. You left Egypt in that month. Do not appear before Me with nothing to offer. 📖

## Exodus 23:16
Celebrate the Feast of Harvest with your fields’ first produce. Celebrate the Feast of Ingathering at year’s end when gathering your fields’ produce. 📖

## Exodus 23:17
All your males must appear before the Lord GOD three times yearly. 📖

## Exodus 23:18
Offer none of My sacrificial blood with anything containing yeast. Leave none of My feast’s fat until morning. 📖

## Exodus 23:19
Bring your soil’s best first produce to the LORD your God’s house. Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk. 📖

## Exodus 23:20
I send an angel ahead to protect you along the way and bring you to the place I prepared. 📖

## Exodus 23:21
Attend to him, obey his voice, and do not defy him. He will not forgive rebellion because My Name is in him. 📖

## Exodus 23:22
If you carefully obey him and do all I say, I will oppose your enemies and foes. 📖

## Exodus 23:23
My angel will go ahead of you into the land of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites. He will bring you there, and I will annihilate them. 📖

## Exodus 23:24
Do not bow to their gods, serve them, or copy their practices. Demolish them and smash their sacred stones. 📖

## Exodus 23:25
Serve the LORD your God. He will bless your bread and water. I will remove sickness from you. 📖

## Exodus 23:26
No woman in your land will miscarry or be barren. I will give you your full lifespan. 📖

## Exodus 23:27
I will send My terror ahead. I will confuse every nation you encounter and make all your enemies flee. 📖

## Exodus 23:28
I will send the hornet ahead to drive away Hivites, Canaanites, and Hittites. 📖

## Exodus 23:29
I will not drive them out in one year. The land would become deserted, and wild animals would multiply against you. 📖

## Exodus 23:30
I will drive them out gradually until you increase and possess the land. 📖

## Exodus 23:31
I will establish your borders from the Red Sea to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the desert to the Euphrates. I will deliver the inhabitants to you. You will drive them out. 📖

## Exodus 23:32
Make no covenant with them or their gods. 📖

## Exodus 23:33
They must not stay in your land and lead you to sin against Me. Serving their gods would trap you.” 📖
