## Exodus 17:1
Israel’s whole congregation left the Desert of Sin, traveling in stages as the LORD commanded. They camped at Rephidim without drinking water. 📖

## Exodus 17:2
They demanded water from Moses. He replied, “Why quarrel with me? Why test the LORD?” 📖

## Exodus 17:3
Thirsty, they complained, “Why bring us from Egypt to kill us, our children, and livestock with thirst?” 📖

## Exodus 17:4
Moses cried to the LORD, “What should I do? A little more, and they will stone me!” 🙏

## Exodus 17:5
The LORD said, “Go ahead with some Israelite elders. Carry the staff you used to strike the Nile. 📖

## Exodus 17:6
I will stand before you by Horeb’s rock. Strike it, and water will flow for the people.” Moses obeyed before Israel’s elders. 📖

## Exodus 17:7
He named the place Massah and Meribah. Israel quarreled and tested the LORD, asking, “Is He among us?” 📖

## Exodus 17:8
Amalekites attacked Israel at Rephidim. 📖

## Exodus 17:9
Moses told Joshua, “Choose men to fight Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on the hill with God’s staff in my hand.” 📖

## Exodus 17:10
Joshua obeyed and fought. Moses, Aaron, and Hur climbed the hill. 📖

## Exodus 17:11
Israel prevailed while Moses raised his hands. Amalek prevailed when he lowered them. 📖

## Exodus 17:12
His hands grew heavy. They placed a stone under him, and he sat on it. Aaron and Hur supported his hands, one on either side, keeping them steady until sunset. 📖

## Exodus 17:13
Joshua defeated Amalek and his army with the sword. 📖

## Exodus 17:14
The LORD told Moses, “Record this on a scroll as a reminder and recite it to Joshua. I will erase Amalek’s memory from under heaven.” 📖

## Exodus 17:15
Moses built an altar named The LORD Is My Banner. 📖

## Exodus 17:16
He said, “A hand was raised toward the LORD’s throne. He will fight Amalek from generation to generation.” 📖
