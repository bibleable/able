## Exodus 35:1
Moses gathered the whole community of Israel. He said, “These are the things the LORD commands you to do. 📖

## Exodus 35:2
You may work for six days. The seventh day must be holy to you, a Sabbath of complete rest to the LORD. Anyone who works that day must be put to death. 📖

## Exodus 35:3
Do not light a fire in any of your homes on the Sabbath day.” 📖

## Exodus 35:4
Moses also told the whole community of Israel, “This is what the LORD commands. 📖

## Exodus 35:5
Collect an offering to the LORD from among yourselves. Everyone who is willing should bring an offering to the LORD. Bring gold, silver, and bronze. 📖

## Exodus 35:6
Bring blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, fine linen, and goat hair. 📖

## Exodus 35:7
Bring ram skins dyed red, fine leather, and acacia wood. 📖

## Exodus 35:8
Bring olive oil for the light. Bring spices for the anointing oil and fragrant incense. 📖

## Exodus 35:9
Bring onyx stones and other gemstones to mount on the ephod and breastpiece. 📖

## Exodus 35:10
Every skilled craftsman among you should come and make everything the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 35:11
Make the tabernacle with its tent and covering. Make its clasps, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases. 📖

## Exodus 35:12
Make the ark with its poles and mercy seat. Make the veil to shield it. 📖

## Exodus 35:13
Make the table with its poles and all its utensils. Prepare the Bread of the Presence. 📖

## Exodus 35:14
Make the lampstand for light with its accessories and lamps. Prepare oil for the light. 📖

## Exodus 35:15
Make the incense altar with its poles. Prepare the anointing oil and fragrant incense. Make the curtain for the doorway at the tabernacle entrance. 📖

## Exodus 35:16
Make the altar for burnt offerings with its bronze grate, poles, and all its utensils. Make the basin with its stand. 📖

## Exodus 35:17
Make the courtyard curtains with their posts and bases. Make the curtain for the courtyard gate. 📖

## Exodus 35:18
Make the tent pegs for the tabernacle and courtyard, with their ropes. 📖

## Exodus 35:19
Make the woven garments for service in the holy place. These include the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments for his sons to serve as priests.” 📖

## Exodus 35:20
Then the whole community of Israel left Moses. 📖

## Exodus 35:21
Everyone whose heart and spirit moved him brought an offering to the LORD. These offerings were for work on the Tent of Meeting, all its services, and the holy garments. 📖

## Exodus 35:22
All the willing men and women came. They brought brooches, earrings, rings, necklaces, and every kind of gold jewelry. They presented their gold as a wave offering to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 35:23
Everyone with blue, purple, or scarlet yarn, fine linen, or goat hair brought it. They also brought ram skins dyed red and articles of fine leather. 📖

## Exodus 35:24
Everyone who could give silver or bronze brought it as an offering to the LORD. Everyone with acacia wood for any part of the service brought it. 📖

## Exodus 35:25
Every skilled woman spun yarn by hand. They brought the blue, purple, or scarlet yarn, or fine linen they had spun. 📖

## Exodus 35:26
All the skilled women who were moved to help spun goat hair. 📖

## Exodus 35:27
The leaders brought onyx stones and other gemstones to mount on the ephod and breastpiece. 📖

## Exodus 35:28
They also brought spices and olive oil for the light, anointing oil, and fragrant incense. 📖

## Exodus 35:29
All the willing Israelite men and women brought voluntary offerings to the LORD. These were for all the work the LORD had commanded through Moses. 📖

## Exodus 35:30
Moses told the Israelites, “The LORD has called Bezalel by name. He is the son of Uri and grandson of Hur, from the tribe of Judah. 📖

## Exodus 35:31
The LORD has filled him with the Spirit of God. He has given him skill, ability, and knowledge in every kind of craft. 📖

## Exodus 35:32
He can design artistic objects made from gold, silver, and bronze. 📖

## Exodus 35:33
He can cut gemstones for settings and carve wood. He can master every artistic craft. 📖

## Exodus 35:34
The LORD has also given him and Oholiab the ability to teach others. Oholiab is the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan. 📖

## Exodus 35:35
He has given them skill for every kind of work. They can engrave, design, and weave. They can embroider with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen. They are artistic designers in every kind of craft.” 📖
