## Exodus 16:1
On the second month’s fifteenth day after leaving Egypt, Israel’s whole congregation left Elim. They reached the Desert of Sin between Elim and Sinai. 📖

## Exodus 16:2
Everyone complained against Moses and Aaron in the desert. 📖

## Exodus 16:3
“If only the LORD killed us in Egypt! There we sat by meat pots and ate our fill of bread. You brought us here to starve everyone!” 📖

## Exodus 16:4
The LORD told Moses, “I will rain bread from heaven. Gather enough each day for that day. This tests whether you follow My instructions. 📖

## Exodus 16:5
On the sixth day, prepare twice the amount gathered on other days.” 📖

## Exodus 16:6
Moses and Aaron told Israel, “Tonight you will know the LORD brought you from Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 16:7
In the morning, you will see His glory because He heard your complaints against Him. Who are we, that you complain against us?” 📖

## Exodus 16:8
Moses added, “He will give meat tonight and bread to fill you tomorrow morning. He heard your complaints. Who are we? You complain against Him, rather than us.” 📖

## Exodus 16:9
Moses told Aaron, “Tell everyone to come before the LORD. He heard their complaints.” 📖

## Exodus 16:10
As Aaron spoke, Israel looked toward the desert. The LORD’s glory appeared in a cloud. 📖

## Exodus 16:11
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 16:12
“I heard Israel’s complaints. Tell them, ‘At twilight you will eat meat. In the morning, you will have your fill of bread. You will know I am the LORD your God.’” 📖

## Exodus 16:13
Quail covered the camp that evening. Dew lay around the camp in the morning. 📖

## Exodus 16:14
After the dew evaporated, thin flakes covered the desert floor, fine as frost. 📖

## Exodus 16:15
Israel asked, “What is it?” They did not know. Moses replied, “It is the bread the LORD gives you. 📖

## Exodus 16:16
He commands each to gather what he needs. Take an omer for each person in your tent.” 📖

## Exodus 16:17
They obeyed. Some gathered more and others less. 📖

## Exodus 16:18
They measured by the omer. Those gathering much had no excess, and those gathering little had no shortage. Each gathered as much as he needed to eat. 📖

## Exodus 16:19
Moses said, “Keep none until morning.” 📖

## Exodus 16:20
Some disobeyed and kept it overnight. Maggots infested it, and it smelled. Moses became angry. 📖

## Exodus 16:21
Each morning, everyone gathered enough. It melted when the sun grew hot. 📖

## Exodus 16:22
On the sixth day, they gathered double, two omers each. All the congregation’s leaders reported to Moses. 📖

## Exodus 16:23
He replied, “The LORD says, ‘Tomorrow is complete rest, His holy Sabbath. Bake and boil what you want. Keep the remainder until morning.’” 📖

## Exodus 16:24
They kept it overnight as commanded. It had no smell or maggots. 📖

## Exodus 16:25
Moses said, “Eat it today, the LORD’s Sabbath. You will find none in the fields today. 📖

## Exodus 16:26
Gather six days. None will be there on the seventh, the Sabbath.” 📖

## Exodus 16:27
Some went to gather on the seventh day but found nothing. 📖

## Exodus 16:28
The LORD told Moses, “How long will you refuse My commands and instructions? 📖

## Exodus 16:29
The LORD gave you the Sabbath. Therefore, He gives bread for two days on the sixth day. Everyone must remain in his place on the seventh. No one may leave.” 📖

## Exodus 16:30
They rested on the seventh day. 📖

## Exodus 16:31
Israel named it manna. It was white, resembling coriander seed, and tasted like honey wafers. 📖

## Exodus 16:32
Moses said, “The LORD commands you to preserve an omer for future generations. They must see the bread He fed you in the wilderness after bringing you from Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 16:33
He told Aaron, “Put an omer of manna in a jar before the LORD. Preserve it for future generations.” 📖

## Exodus 16:34
Aaron placed it before the Testimony for preservation, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 16:35
Israel ate manna forty years until they reached land to settle in. They ate it until reaching Canaan’s border. 📖

## Exodus 16:36
An omer is one tenth of an ephah. 📖
