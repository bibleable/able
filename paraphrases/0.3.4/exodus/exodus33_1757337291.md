## Exodus 33:1
The LORD told Moses, “Leave this place with the people you brought out of Egypt. Go to the land I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I promised to give it to their descendants. 📖

## Exodus 33:2
I will send an angel before you. I will force out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 📖

## Exodus 33:3
Go to the land flowing with milk and honey. But I will not go with you because you are stubborn. Otherwise, I might destroy you on the way.” 📖

## Exodus 33:4
The people mourned when they heard this bad news. No one put on any jewelry. 📖

## Exodus 33:5
The LORD had told Moses to tell the Israelites, “You are stubborn. If I went with you for even a moment, I would destroy you. Remove your jewelry. Then I will decide what to do with you.” 📖

## Exodus 33:6
From Mount Horeb onward, the Israelites wore no jewelry. 📖

## Exodus 33:7
Moses used to set up the tent some distance outside the camp. He called it the Tent of Meeting. Anyone who wanted to ask the LORD something went to this tent outside the camp. 📖

## Exodus 33:8
Whenever Moses went to the tent, all the people stood at the entrances of their own tents. They watched Moses until he entered the tent. 📖

## Exodus 33:9
When Moses entered the tent, the pillar of cloud came down and stayed at its entrance. The LORD spoke with Moses. 📖

## Exodus 33:10
All the people saw the pillar of cloud at the tent entrance. Each person stood and worshiped at the entrance of his own tent. 📖

## Exodus 33:11
The LORD spoke to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend. Moses then returned to the camp. But his young assistant, Joshua son of Nun, stayed in the tent. 📖

## Exodus 33:12
Moses told the LORD, “You tell me to lead these people onward. But You have not told me whom You will send with me. Yet You say You know me by name and are pleased with me. 📖

## Exodus 33:13
If You are pleased with me, please teach me Your ways. Then I can know You and continue to please You. Remember that this nation is Your people.” 📖

## Exodus 33:14
The LORD answered, “My Presence will go with you. I will give you rest.” 📖

## Exodus 33:15
Moses replied, “If Your Presence will not go with us, do not lead us away from here. 📖

## Exodus 33:16
Unless You go with us, how can anyone know that You are pleased with me and Your people? How else will we be distinct from all the other peoples on earth?” 📖

## Exodus 33:17
The LORD told Moses, “I will do what you have asked. I am pleased with you, and I know you by name.” 📖

## Exodus 33:18
Moses said, “Please show me Your glory.” 📖

## Exodus 33:19
The LORD replied, “I will make all My goodness pass before you. I will announce My name, the LORD, in your presence. I will show mercy to those I choose. I will show compassion to those I choose. 📖

## Exodus 33:20
But you cannot see My face. No one can see Me and live.” 📖

## Exodus 33:21
The LORD continued, “Stand on a rock at a place near Me. 📖

## Exodus 33:22
When My glory passes, I will put you in a gap in the rock. I will cover you with My hand until I pass. 📖

## Exodus 33:23
Then I will remove My hand, and you will see My back. But you must not see My face.” 📖
