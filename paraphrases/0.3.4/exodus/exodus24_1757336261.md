## Exodus 24:1
The LORD told Moses, “Come up to the LORD with Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy Israelite elders. Worship at a distance. 📖

## Exodus 24:2
Only Moses may approach. The others must stay away. The people may not climb with him.” 📖

## Exodus 24:3
Moses told everyone all the LORD’s words and ordinances. They answered together, “We will do everything He said.” 📖

## Exodus 24:4
He wrote all the LORD’s words. Early next morning, he built an altar at the mountain’s foot and twelve pillars for Israel’s twelve tribes. 📖

## Exodus 24:5
He sent young Israelite men to offer burnt offerings and sacrifice young bulls as peace offerings to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 24:6
He put half the blood in bowls and splashed half on the altar. 📖

## Exodus 24:7
He read the Book of the Covenant aloud. They replied, “We will do all He said and obey.” 📖

## Exodus 24:8
He splashed blood on the people. “This is the blood of the LORD’s covenant with you according to all these words.” 📖

## Exodus 24:9
Moses climbed with Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy Israelite elders. 📖

## Exodus 24:10
They saw Israel’s God. Beneath His feet was something like sapphire pavement, clear as the sky. 📖

## Exodus 24:11
God did not strike Israel’s nobles. They saw Him and ate and drank. 📖

## Exodus 24:12
The LORD told Moses, “Come up and remain with Me. I will give you stone tablets bearing My written law and commands to teach them.” 📖

## Exodus 24:13
Moses left with his attendant Joshua and climbed God’s mountain. 📖

## Exodus 24:14
He told the elders, “Wait for us to return. Aaron and Hur are with you. Bring disputes to them.” 📖

## Exodus 24:15
Moses climbed, and the cloud covered the mountain. 📖

## Exodus 24:16
The LORD’s glory settled on Sinai. The cloud covered it six days. On the seventh, He called Moses from the cloud. 📖

## Exodus 24:17
Israel saw His glory like consuming fire on the summit. 📖

## Exodus 24:18
Moses entered the cloud and climbed. He remained forty days and forty nights. 📖
