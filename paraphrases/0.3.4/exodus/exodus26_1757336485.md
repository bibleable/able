## Exodus 26:1
“Make ten tabernacle curtains of finely spun linen with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. Work cherubim skillfully into them. 📖

## Exodus 26:2
Make each twenty-eight cubits long and four wide. All must match in size. 📖

## Exodus 26:3
Join five curtains as one set and the other five as another. 📖

## Exodus 26:4
Make blue loops on the end curtain’s edge in each set. 📖

## Exodus 26:5
Make fifty loops on each set’s end curtain. Line the loops up opposite each other. 📖

## Exodus 26:6
Make fifty gold clasps. Join the curtains with them to form one tabernacle unit. 📖

## Exodus 26:7
Make eleven goat hair curtains for the tent over the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 26:8
Make all eleven thirty cubits long and four wide. 📖

## Exodus 26:9
Join five as one set and six as another. Fold the sixth curtain double at the tent’s front. 📖

## Exodus 26:10
Put fifty loops along the end curtain’s edge in each set. 📖

## Exodus 26:11
Make fifty bronze clasps and join the loops with them. The tent will form one unit. 📖

## Exodus 26:12
Let the remaining half curtain hang over the tabernacle’s back. 📖

## Exodus 26:13
The tent curtains extend one cubit beyond each side. Let the excess hang down to cover the tabernacle’s sides. 📖

## Exodus 26:14
Cover the tent with ram skins dyed red. Put a fine leather covering over that. 📖

## Exodus 26:15
Make upright tabernacle frames from acacia wood. 📖

## Exodus 26:16
Make each frame ten cubits long and one and a half wide. 📖

## Exodus 26:17
Each frame must have two connected tenons. Make every frame this way. 📖

## Exodus 26:18
Make twenty frames for the south side. 📖

## Exodus 26:19
Put forty silver bases beneath them, two per frame, one under each tenon. 📖

## Exodus 26:20
Make twenty frames for the second side, the north, 📖

## Exodus 26:21
with forty silver bases, two under each frame. 📖

## Exodus 26:22
Make six frames for the rear, the west side, 📖

## Exodus 26:23
and two frames for the two rear corners. 📖

## Exodus 26:24
Join the corner frames from bottom to top, fitting them into one ring. Make both corners this way. 📖

## Exodus 26:25
There will be eight frames and sixteen silver bases, two per frame. 📖

## Exodus 26:26
Make five acacia crossbars for one side’s frames, 📖

## Exodus 26:27
five for the other side, and five for the rear, the west side. 📖

## Exodus 26:28
The central crossbar must run through the frames’ middle from end to end. 📖

## Exodus 26:29
Cover the frames and crossbars with gold. Make gold rings to hold the crossbars. 📖

## Exodus 26:30
Erect the tabernacle according to the pattern shown on the mountain. 📖

## Exodus 26:31
Make a veil from blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. Work cherubim skillfully into it. 📖

## Exodus 26:32
Hang it with gold hooks on four gold-covered acacia posts. Stand them on four silver bases. 📖

## Exodus 26:33
Hang the veil from the clasps. Place the ark of the Testimony behind it. The veil separates the Holy Place and Most Holy Place. 📖

## Exodus 26:34
Put the mercy seat on the ark of the Testimony in the Most Holy Place. 📖

## Exodus 26:35
Place the table outside the veil on the north. Put the lampstand opposite it on the south. 📖

## Exodus 26:36
Make an embroidered entrance curtain from blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 26:37
Make five acacia posts for the curtain, overlay them with gold hooks, and cast five bronze bases. 📖
