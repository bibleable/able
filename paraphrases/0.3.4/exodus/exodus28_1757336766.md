## Exodus 28:1
“Bring your brother Aaron and his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar from Israel to serve Me as priests. 📖

## Exodus 28:2
Make holy clothes for Aaron’s glory and splendor. 📖

## Exodus 28:3
I filled skilled craftsmen with a spirit of wisdom. Instruct them to make clothes for Aaron’s dedication as My priest. 📖

## Exodus 28:4
Make a breastpiece, ephod, robe, woven tunic, turban, and sash. These holy garments are for Aaron and his sons to serve Me as priests. 📖

## Exodus 28:5
Use gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine linen. 📖

## Exodus 28:6
Make the ephod from finely spun linen embroidered with gold and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 📖

## Exodus 28:7
Attach two shoulder pieces at two corners to fasten it. 📖

## Exodus 28:8
Its skillfully woven waistband must be one piece with it, using the same workmanship. Use gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 28:9
Engrave Israel’s sons’ names on two onyx stones. 📖

## Exodus 28:10
Put six names on each, ordered by birth. 📖

## Exodus 28:11
Engrave the two stones like a gem cutter engraves a seal. Mount them in gold filigree settings. 📖

## Exodus 28:12
Attach these memorial stones to the ephod’s shoulder pieces. Aaron must carry Israel’s sons’ names on his two shoulders as a memorial before the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 28:13
Make gold filigree settings, 📖

## Exodus 28:14
and two braided chains of pure gold. Attach them to the settings. 📖

## Exodus 28:15
Make a breastpiece of judgment with the ephod’s workmanship. Use gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 28:16
Fold it double into a square, one span long and one wide. 📖

## Exodus 28:17
Set gemstones in four rows. In the first, put a ruby, topaz, and emerald, 📖

## Exodus 28:18
in the second, turquoise, sapphire, and diamond, 📖

## Exodus 28:19
in the third, jacinth, agate, and amethyst, 📖

## Exodus 28:20
and in the fourth, beryl, onyx, and jasper. Use gold filigree settings. 📖

## Exodus 28:21
The twelve stones represent Israel’s sons’ names. Engrave each like a seal with one of the twelve tribes’ names. 📖

## Exodus 28:22
Make pure gold braided chains like cords for the breastpiece. 📖

## Exodus 28:23
Attach two gold rings to its two corners. 📖

## Exodus 28:24
Fasten the two gold chains to these two rings. 📖

## Exodus 28:25
Fasten their other ends to the two filigree settings on the front of the ephod’s shoulder pieces. 📖

## Exodus 28:26
Put two more gold rings on the breastpiece’s other corners, on the inner edge next to the ephod. 📖

## Exodus 28:27
Attach two additional gold rings to the bottom of the ephod’s two shoulder pieces. Place them in front, near the seam just above the woven waistband. 📖

## Exodus 28:28
Tie the breastpiece’s rings to the ephod’s rings with blue cord. The breastpiece must stay above the waistband without swinging away. 📖

## Exodus 28:29
Aaron must carry Israel’s sons’ names over his heart on the judgment breastpiece when entering the Holy Place. This is a continual reminder before the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 28:30
Put Urim and Thummim in the judgment breastpiece, over Aaron’s heart whenever he comes before the LORD. He must continually carry Israel’s judgment over his heart before Him. 📖

## Exodus 28:31
Make the ephod’s robe entirely blue. 📖

## Exodus 28:32
Put an opening at its top center. Weave a collar around it, with an opening like a garment’s, to prevent tearing. 📖

## Exodus 28:33
Make blue, purple, and scarlet yarn pomegranates around the lower hem. Put gold bells between them, 📖

## Exodus 28:34
alternating bells and pomegranates around the robe’s lower hem. 📖

## Exodus 28:35
Aaron must wear it while ministering. Its sound must be heard when he enters or leaves the sanctuary before the LORD, so he does not die. 📖

## Exodus 28:36
Make a pure gold plate engraved like a seal: HOLY TO THE LORD. 📖

## Exodus 28:37
Fasten it with blue cord to the turban’s front. 📖

## Exodus 28:38
Aaron must wear it on his forehead and bear guilt involving all Israel’s dedicated holy gifts. Keep it always on his forehead so they may be acceptable before the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 28:39
Weave the tunic from fine linen. Make a fine linen turban and embroidered sash. 📖

## Exodus 28:40
Make tunics, sashes, and headbands for Aaron’s sons, for glory and splendor. 📖

## Exodus 28:41
Dress Aaron and his sons. Anoint, ordain, and dedicate them to serve Me as priests. 📖

## Exodus 28:42
Make linen undergarments covering their bare bodies from waist to thigh. 📖

## Exodus 28:43
They must wear them when entering the Tent of Meeting or approaching the altar to serve in the Holy Place. Then they will avoid guilt and death. This is a permanent statute for Aaron and his descendants. 📖
