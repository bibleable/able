## Exodus 15:1
Moses and Israel sang to the LORD, “I will sing because He is highly exalted. He threw horse and rider into the sea. 📖

## Exodus 15:2
The LORD is my strength, song, and salvation. He is my God, and I praise Him. He is my father’s God, and I exalt Him. 📖

## Exodus 15:3
The LORD is a warrior. The LORD is His name. 📖

## Exodus 15:4
He threw Pharaoh’s chariots and army into the sea. Pharaoh’s finest officers drowned in the Red Sea. 📖

## Exodus 15:5
The depths covered them. They sank like stone. 📖

## Exodus 15:6
LORD, Your right hand is majestic in power. It shattered the enemy. 📖

## Exodus 15:7
Your great majesty overthrew Your enemies. Your burning wrath consumed them like stubble. 📖

## Exodus 15:8
Your nostrils’ blast piled up waters. Currents stood as a wall. The depths congealed in the sea’s heart. 📖

## Exodus 15:9
The enemy said, ‘I will chase and catch them. I will divide plunder and gorge myself. I will draw my sword and destroy them with my hand.’ 📖

## Exodus 15:10
You blew Your breath, and the sea covered them. They sank like lead in mighty waters. 📖

## Exodus 15:11
LORD, which god is like You, majestic in holiness, revered in praise, and doing wonders? 📖

## Exodus 15:12
You stretched Your right hand. Earth swallowed them. 📖

## Exodus 15:13
Your loving devotion will lead those You redeemed. Your strength will guide them to Your holy home. 📖

## Exodus 15:14
Nations will hear and tremble. Anguish will grip Philistia’s inhabitants. 📖

## Exodus 15:15
Edom’s chiefs will be dismayed. Moab’s leaders will tremble. Canaan’s inhabitants will melt away. 📖

## Exodus 15:16
Terror and dread will fall on them. Your mighty arm will hold them still as stone until Your people pass, LORD, until those You bought pass. 📖

## Exodus 15:17
You will bring and plant them on Your inheritance mountain, LORD, the home You prepared. It is the sanctuary, Lord, Your hands established. 📖

## Exodus 15:18
The LORD will reign forever and ever!” 📖

## Exodus 15:19
Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, and horsemen entered the sea. The LORD brought its waters over them. Israel crossed on dry ground. 📖

## Exodus 15:20
Miriam the prophetess, Aaron’s sister, took a tambourine. All the women followed with tambourines and dancing. 📖

## Exodus 15:21
She sang back, “Sing to the LORD, highly exalted! He threw horse and rider into the sea.” 📖

## Exodus 15:22
Moses led Israel from the Red Sea into the Desert of Shur. They traveled three days without finding water. 📖

## Exodus 15:23
Marah’s water was too bitter to drink. This is why it was named Marah. 📖

## Exodus 15:24
They complained to Moses, “What can we drink?” 📖

## Exodus 15:25
Moses cried to the LORD. The LORD showed him a log. Moses threw it in the water, sweetening it. The LORD gave a statute and ordinance there and tested them. 📖

## Exodus 15:26
He said, “Listen carefully to the LORD your God, do right before Him, attend to His commands, and keep all His statutes. If you do, I will bring none of the diseases I inflicted on Egypt upon you. I am the LORD who heals you.” 📖

## Exodus 15:27
They camped by Elim’s waters. It had twelve springs and seventy palm trees. 📖
