## Exodus 3:1
Moses tended his father-in-law Jethro’s flock. Jethro was Midian’s priest. Moses led the flock beyond the wilderness to Horeb, God’s mountain. 📖

## Exodus 3:2
The LORD’s angel appeared in fire within a bush. Moses saw it burning without being consumed. 📖

## Exodus 3:3
He thought, “I must inspect this marvelous sight. Why does the bush not burn up?” 📖

## Exodus 3:4
The LORD saw him approach. God called from the bush, “Moses, Moses!” He answered, “Here I am.” 📖

## Exodus 3:5
God said, “Come no closer. Remove your sandals. This ground is holy. 📖

## Exodus 3:6
I am your father’s God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.” Moses hid his face, afraid to look at God. 📖

## Exodus 3:7
The LORD said, “I saw My people’s suffering in Egypt. I heard their cries because of their oppressors. I know their pain. 📖

## Exodus 3:8
I came down to rescue them from Egypt and bring them into a good, spacious land flowing with milk and honey. Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites live there. 📖

## Exodus 3:9
Israel’s cry reached Me. I saw Egypt’s severe oppression. 📖

## Exodus 3:10
Go! I send you to Pharaoh to bring My people Israel from Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 3:11
Moses asked, “Who am I to approach Pharaoh and bring Israel from Egypt?” 📖

## Exodus 3:12
God replied, “I will be with you. This will prove I sent you: after bringing them from Egypt, you will all worship God on this mountain.” 📖

## Exodus 3:13
Moses asked, “Suppose I tell Israel their fathers’ God sent me. If they ask His name, what should I say?” 📖

## Exodus 3:14
God said, “I AM WHO I AM. Tell Israel, ‘I AM sent me to you.’” 📖

## Exodus 3:15
He added, “Tell Israel, ‘The LORD, your fathers’ God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, sent me to you.’ This is My name forever. Remember Me this way in every generation. 📖

## Exodus 3:16
Gather Israel’s elders. Tell them, ‘The LORD, your fathers’ God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, appeared to me. He said He attended to you and saw what Egypt did to you. 📖

## Exodus 3:17
He promised to bring you from Egypt’s suffering into a land flowing with milk and honey. It belongs to Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.’ 📖

## Exodus 3:18
The elders will listen. Take them to Egypt’s king and say, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, met us. Permit a three-day wilderness journey to sacrifice to the LORD our God.’ 📖

## Exodus 3:19
I know Egypt’s king will refuse unless a mighty hand forces him. 📖

## Exodus 3:20
I will stretch My hand and strike Egypt with all My wonders. Then he will release you. 📖

## Exodus 3:21
I will give you favor with Egypt. You will not leave with nothing. 📖

## Exodus 3:22
Every woman must ask her neighbor and any woman staying in her house for silver and gold jewelry and clothing. Put these on your sons and daughters. Thus you will plunder Egypt.” 📖
