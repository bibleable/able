## Exodus 25:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 25:2
“Tell Israel to bring Me an offering. Receive it from everyone whose heart moves him to give. 📖

## Exodus 25:3
Accept gold, silver, bronze, 📖

## Exodus 25:4
blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, fine linen, goat hair, 📖

## Exodus 25:5
ram skins dyed red, fine leather, acacia wood, 📖

## Exodus 25:6
olive oil for lighting, spices for anointing oil and fragrant incense, 📖

## Exodus 25:7
onyx stones, and gemstones for the ephod and breastpiece. 📖

## Exodus 25:8
Make Me a sanctuary so I can dwell among them. 📖

## Exodus 25:9
Make the tabernacle and all its furnishings according to the pattern I show you. 📖

## Exodus 25:10
Build an acacia wood ark two and a half cubits long, one and a half wide, and one and a half high. 📖

## Exodus 25:11
Cover it inside and outside with pure gold. Add gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 25:12
Cast four gold rings and attach them to its four feet. Put two on each side. 📖

## Exodus 25:13
Make acacia poles covered with gold. 📖

## Exodus 25:14
Insert them through the ark’s side rings for carrying. 📖

## Exodus 25:15
Leave the poles in the rings. Never remove them. 📖

## Exodus 25:16
Put the Testimony I give you inside the ark. 📖

## Exodus 25:17
Make a pure gold mercy seat two and a half cubits long and one and a half wide. 📖

## Exodus 25:18
Make two hammered gold cherubim at the mercy seat’s ends. 📖

## Exodus 25:19
Put one at each end, all made from one piece of gold. 📖

## Exodus 25:20
Their wings must spread upward over the mercy seat. They must face each other and look toward it. 📖

## Exodus 25:21
Place the mercy seat on the ark. Put My Testimony inside. 📖

## Exodus 25:22
I will meet you above the mercy seat, between the two cherubim over the ark of the Testimony. I will give you all My commands for Israel there. 📖

## Exodus 25:23
Make an acacia table two cubits long, one wide, and one and a half high. 📖

## Exodus 25:24
Cover it with pure gold and add gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 25:25
Make a rim one handbreadth wide around it. Add gold molding to the rim. 📖

## Exodus 25:26
Attach four gold rings at the four corners by its four legs. 📖

## Exodus 25:27
Keep the rings close to the rim. They hold the carrying poles. 📖

## Exodus 25:28
Make acacia carrying poles covered with gold. 📖

## Exodus 25:29
Make pure gold plates and dishes. Also make pure gold pitchers and bowls for pouring drink offerings. 📖

## Exodus 25:30
Keep the Bread of the Presence on the table before Me always. 📖

## Exodus 25:31
Make a lampstand from pure hammered gold as one piece. Include its base, shaft, cups, buds, and petals. 📖

## Exodus 25:32
Extend six branches from its sides, three on each side. 📖

## Exodus 25:33
Each of the six branches must have three cups shaped like almond blossoms, with buds and petals. 📖

## Exodus 25:34
The lampstand must have four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with buds and petals. 📖

## Exodus 25:35
Put a bud under each of the three pairs of branches, six branches in all. 📖

## Exodus 25:36
Hammer the buds and branches from pure gold as one piece with the lampstand. 📖

## Exodus 25:37
Make seven lamps. Set them on the lampstand to light the area in front. 📖

## Exodus 25:38
Make its wick trimmers and trays from pure gold. 📖

## Exodus 25:39
Use one talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all these utensils. 📖

## Exodus 25:40
Make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain. 📖
