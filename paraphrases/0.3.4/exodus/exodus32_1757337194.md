## Exodus 32:1
The people saw that Moses was taking a long time to come down the mountain. They gathered around Aaron. They said, “Make gods to go before us. Moses brought us out of Egypt, but we do not know what happened to him.” 📖

## Exodus 32:2
Aaron said, “Remove the gold earrings from your wives, sons, and daughters. Bring them to me.” 📖

## Exodus 32:3
All the people removed their gold earrings and brought them to Aaron. 📖

## Exodus 32:4
Aaron took their gold. He used an engraving tool to shape it into a cast calf. The people said, “Israel, these are your gods who brought you out of Egypt!” 📖

## Exodus 32:5
When Aaron saw this, he built an altar before the calf. He announced, “Tomorrow we will hold a feast for the LORD.” 📖

## Exodus 32:6
The next day, they got up and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. They sat down to eat and drink. Then they got up to take part in wild celebrations. 📖

## Exodus 32:7
The LORD said to Moses, “Go down at once. Your people, whom you brought out of Egypt, have become corrupt. 📖

## Exodus 32:8
They have quickly left the way I commanded them to follow. They made a cast calf and bowed before it. They sacrificed to it. They said, ‘Israel, these are your gods who brought you out of Egypt.’” 📖

## Exodus 32:9
The LORD also said to Moses, “I have seen these people. They are very stubborn. 📖

## Exodus 32:10
Now leave Me alone. My anger will burn against them and destroy them. Then I will make you into a great nation.” 📖

## Exodus 32:11
Moses sought the favor of the LORD his God. He said, “LORD, why does Your anger burn against Your people? You brought them out of Egypt with great power and a mighty hand. 📖

## Exodus 32:12
Why should the Egyptians say You brought them out with evil intent, to kill them in the mountains and remove them from the earth? Turn from Your fierce anger. Do not bring harm on Your people. 📖

## Exodus 32:13
Remember Your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Israel. You swore by Yourself to make their descendants as numerous as the stars in the sky. You promised their descendants all this land as their inheritance forever.” 📖

## Exodus 32:14
The LORD did not bring the disaster He had threatened against His people. 📖

## Exodus 32:15
Moses turned and went down the mountain. He carried the two tablets of the Testimony in his hands. They had writing on both sides, front and back. 📖

## Exodus 32:16
God had made the tablets. God had also engraved the writing on them. 📖

## Exodus 32:17
Joshua heard the people shouting. He told Moses, “The sound of war is in the camp.” 📖

## Exodus 32:18
Moses replied, “That is not a cry of victory or defeat. I hear singing!” 📖

## Exodus 32:19
Moses approached the camp and saw the calf and the dancing. He became very angry. He threw the tablets from his hands. They broke at the foot of the mountain. 📖

## Exodus 32:20
He took the calf they had made and burned it. He ground it into powder and scattered the powder on the water. Then he forced the Israelites to drink it. 📖

## Exodus 32:21
Moses asked Aaron, “What did these people do to you? Why did you lead them into such a great sin?” 📖

## Exodus 32:22
Aaron replied, “My lord, do not be angry. You know that these people want to do evil. 📖

## Exodus 32:23
They told me, ‘Make gods to go before us. Moses brought us out of Egypt, but we do not know what happened to him.’ 📖

## Exodus 32:24
I told anyone with gold to remove it. They gave it to me. I threw it into the fire, and this calf came out!” 📖

## Exodus 32:25
Moses saw that the people were out of control. Aaron had let them run wild. Their enemies laughed at them. 📖

## Exodus 32:26
Moses stood at the camp entrance. He said, “Everyone who is for the LORD, come to me.” All the Levites gathered around him. 📖

## Exodus 32:27
He told them, “The LORD, the God of Israel, commands each man to fasten his sword at his side. Go through the camp from gate to gate and back again. Kill your brother, your friend, and your neighbor.” 📖

## Exodus 32:28
The Levites obeyed Moses. About three thousand people died that day. 📖

## Exodus 32:29
Moses then said, “Today you have been ordained to serve the LORD. Each man went against his son and brother. Therefore, the LORD has blessed you today.” 📖

## Exodus 32:30
The next day, Moses told the people, “You have committed a great sin. I will now go up to the LORD. Perhaps I can make atonement for your sin.” 📖

## Exodus 32:31
Moses returned to the LORD. He said, “These people have committed a terrible sin! They made gold gods for themselves. 📖

## Exodus 32:32
Please forgive their sin. If You will not, please remove my name from the book You have written.” 📖

## Exodus 32:33
The LORD replied to Moses, “I will remove from My book anyone who has sinned against Me. 📖

## Exodus 32:34
Now lead the people to the place I described. My angel will go before you. But when the day of judgment comes, I will punish them for their sin.” 📖

## Exodus 32:35
The LORD sent a plague on the people because of what they had done with the calf Aaron made. 📖
