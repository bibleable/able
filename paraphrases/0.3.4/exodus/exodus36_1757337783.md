## Exodus 36:1
Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person must carry out all the LORD's commands. The LORD has given them skill and ability to know how to do all the work of building the sanctuary. 📖

## Exodus 36:2
Moses called Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person the LORD had gifted. These were all the people moved to come and do the work. 📖

## Exodus 36:3
They received from Moses all the Israelites' contributions for building the sanctuary. The people continued to bring voluntary offerings every morning. 📖

## Exodus 36:4
All the skilled craftsmen working on the sanctuary left their work. 📖

## Exodus 36:5
They told Moses, “The people are bringing more than we need for the work the LORD commanded us to do.” 📖

## Exodus 36:6
Moses gave an order, and an announcement went throughout the camp. No man or woman was to make anything more as an offering for the sanctuary. This stopped the people from bringing more. 📖

## Exodus 36:7
They already had more than enough materials to complete all the work. 📖

## Exodus 36:8
The skilled craftsmen among the workers made ten curtains for the tabernacle. They used finely spun linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. They skillfully worked cherubim into the curtains. 📖

## Exodus 36:9
Each curtain was twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide. All the curtains were the same size. 📖

## Exodus 36:10
He joined five curtains together. He also joined the other five together. 📖

## Exodus 36:11
He made blue loops on the edge of the end curtain in the first set. He did the same on the end curtain in the second set. 📖

## Exodus 36:12
He made fifty loops on one curtain and fifty on the end curtain of the second set. The loops lined up opposite each other. 📖

## Exodus 36:13
He made fifty gold clasps to join the curtains together. This made the tabernacle one unit. 📖

## Exodus 36:14
He made eleven goat hair curtains for the tent over the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 36:15
All eleven curtains were the same size. Each was thirty cubits long and four cubits wide. 📖

## Exodus 36:16
He joined five curtains into one set and the other six into another set. 📖

## Exodus 36:17
He made fifty loops on the edge of the end curtain in the first set. He made fifty loops on the corresponding curtain in the second set. 📖

## Exodus 36:18
He made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent together as one unit. 📖

## Exodus 36:19
He made a covering of ram skins dyed red for the tent. He put a covering of fine leather over it. 📖

## Exodus 36:20
Next, he made upright acacia wood frames for the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 36:21
Each frame was ten cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. 📖

## Exodus 36:22
Each frame had two tenons connected to each other. He made all the tabernacle frames this way. 📖

## Exodus 36:23
He made twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 36:24
He made forty silver bases to put under the twenty frames. Each frame had two bases, one under each tenon. 📖

## Exodus 36:25
He made twenty frames for the second side of the tabernacle, the north side. 📖

## Exodus 36:26
He made forty silver bases for them, two under each frame. 📖

## Exodus 36:27
He made six frames for the rear of the tabernacle, the west side. 📖

## Exodus 36:28
He made two frames for the two rear corners of the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 36:29
The corner frames were joined from bottom to top and fitted into one ring. He made both corners this way. 📖

## Exodus 36:30
There were eight frames with sixteen silver bases, two under each frame. 📖

## Exodus 36:31
He made five acacia wood crossbars for the frames on one side of the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 36:32
He made five crossbars for the other side. He made five more for the rear, the west side. 📖

## Exodus 36:33
He made the central crossbar pass through the middle of the frames, from one end to the other. 📖

## Exodus 36:34
He covered the frames with gold. He made gold rings to hold the crossbars. He also covered the crossbars with gold. 📖

## Exodus 36:35
He made the veil from blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. He skillfully worked cherubim into it. 📖

## Exodus 36:36
He made four acacia wood posts for the veil and covered them with gold. He made gold hooks and cast four silver bases for the posts. 📖

## Exodus 36:37
He made a curtain for the tent entrance. He embroidered it with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 36:38
He made five posts with their hooks. He covered the tops of the posts and their bands with gold. Their five bases were bronze. 📖
