## Exodus 34:1
The LORD told Moses, “Chisel two stone tablets like the first ones. I will write on them the words from the tablets you broke. 📖

## Exodus 34:2
Be ready in the morning. Come up Mount Sinai and present yourself before Me on the summit. 📖

## Exodus 34:3
No one may come with you. No one may be seen anywhere on the mountain. Even the flocks and herds must not graze in front of it.” 📖

## Exodus 34:4
Moses chiseled two stone tablets like the first ones. He got up early and carried the two tablets up Mount Sinai. He did as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Exodus 34:5
The LORD came down in a cloud and stood there with Moses. He announced His name, the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 34:6
The LORD passed in front of Moses and called out, “The LORD, the LORD God, is compassionate and gracious. He is slow to become angry. He is full of faithful love and faithfulness. 📖

## Exodus 34:7
He maintains faithful love for a thousand generations. He forgives wrongdoing, rebellion, and sin. But He never leaves the guilty unpunished. He punishes the children and grandchildren for their fathers' wrongdoing, to the third and fourth generations.” 📖

## Exodus 34:8
Moses immediately bowed to the ground and worshiped. 📖

## Exodus 34:9
He said, “Lord, if You are pleased with me, please go with us. These people are stubborn. But forgive our wrongdoing and sin. Take us as Your inheritance.” 📖

## Exodus 34:10
The LORD said, “I am making a covenant. Before all your people, I will do wonders never done in any nation anywhere in the world. Everyone among whom you live will see the LORD's work. What I do with you will cause awe. 📖

## Exodus 34:11
Obey what I command you today. I will force out the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites before you. 📖

## Exodus 34:12
Do not make a treaty with the people in the land you are entering. They could become a trap among you. 📖

## Exodus 34:13
Tear down their altars. Break their sacred stones. Cut down their Asherah poles. 📖

## Exodus 34:14
Do not worship any other god. The LORD's name is Jealous. He is a jealous God. 📖

## Exodus 34:15
Do not make a covenant with the people of that land. When they give themselves to their gods and sacrifice, they will invite you. Then you will eat their sacrifices. 📖

## Exodus 34:16
You might take their daughters as wives for your sons. These daughters will give themselves to their gods. They will cause your sons to do the same. 📖

## Exodus 34:17
Do not make cast gods for yourselves. 📖

## Exodus 34:18
Keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. Eat unleavened bread for seven days at the appointed time in the month of Abib, as I commanded. You came out of Egypt in that month. 📖

## Exodus 34:19
The first offspring from every womb belongs to Me. This includes every firstborn male among your cattle and sheep. 📖

## Exodus 34:20
Redeem a firstborn donkey with a lamb. If you do not redeem it, break its neck. Redeem all your firstborn sons. No one may appear before Me with empty hands. 📖

## Exodus 34:21
Work for six days, but rest on the seventh day. You must rest even during plowing and harvest. 📖

## Exodus 34:22
Celebrate the Feast of Weeks with the firstfruits of your wheat harvest. Celebrate the Feast of Ingathering at the turn of the year. 📖

## Exodus 34:23
All your males must appear before the Lord GOD, the God of Israel, three times each year. 📖

## Exodus 34:24
I will force out the nations before you and enlarge your borders. No one will covet your land when you appear before the LORD your God three times each year. 📖

## Exodus 34:25
Do not offer sacrificial blood to Me with anything containing leaven. Do not keep any Passover sacrifice until morning. 📖

## Exodus 34:26
Bring the best firstfruits from your soil to the house of the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in its mother's milk.” 📖

## Exodus 34:27
The LORD also told Moses, “Write these words down. I have made a covenant with you and Israel according to these words.” 📖

## Exodus 34:28
Moses stayed there with the LORD for forty days and forty nights. He ate no bread and drank no water. He wrote the covenant words, the Ten Commandments, on the tablets. 📖

## Exodus 34:29
Moses came down Mount Sinai with the two tablets of the Testimony in his hands. His face shone because he had spoken with the LORD. Moses did not know this. 📖

## Exodus 34:30
Aaron and all the Israelites saw that Moses' face shone. They were afraid to approach him. 📖

## Exodus 34:31
Moses called to them. Aaron and all the community leaders returned to him, and he spoke to them. 📖

## Exodus 34:32
Then all the Israelites came near. Moses commanded them to do everything the LORD had told him on Mount Sinai. 📖

## Exodus 34:33
When Moses finished speaking to them, he covered his face with a veil. 📖

## Exodus 34:34
Whenever Moses went before the LORD to speak with Him, he removed the veil until he came out. Afterward, he told the Israelites what the LORD had commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 34:35
The Israelites saw that Moses' face shone. Then he covered his face with the veil again until he went to speak with the LORD. 📖
