## Exodus 13:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖

## Exodus 13:2
“Dedicate every firstborn male to Me. Every firstborn from Israelite wombs belongs to Me, human and animal.” 📖

## Exodus 13:3
Moses said, “Remember this day of leaving Egypt’s slavery. The LORD’s strong hand brought you out. Eat nothing with yeast. 📖

## Exodus 13:4
You leave today, in Abib. 📖

## Exodus 13:5
He will bring you to the Canaanites’, Hittites’, Amorites’, Hivites’, and Jebusites’ land, flowing with milk and honey. He swore it to your fathers. Observe this service in this month there. 📖

## Exodus 13:6
Eat bread without yeast seven days. On the seventh day, hold the LORD’s feast. 📖

## Exodus 13:7
Eat bread without yeast those seven days. Let no food with yeast or any yeast be found anywhere within your borders. 📖

## Exodus 13:8
Tell your son that day, ‘This is because of what the LORD did for me when I left Egypt.’ 📖

## Exodus 13:9
It will be a sign on your hand and reminder on your forehead to keep His Law on your lips. His mighty hand brought you from Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 13:10
Keep this statute at its appointed time every year. 📖

## Exodus 13:11
The LORD will give you Canaan’s land as He swore to you and your fathers. After He brings you there, 📖

## Exodus 13:12
present each womb’s firstborn male to Him. All your livestock’s firstborn males belong to Him. 📖

## Exodus 13:13
Redeem every firstborn donkey with a lamb. Otherwise, break the donkey’s neck. Redeem every firstborn son. 📖

## Exodus 13:14
When your son asks its meaning, say, ‘The LORD’s mighty hand brought us from Egypt’s slavery. 📖

## Exodus 13:15
Pharaoh stubbornly refused release. The LORD killed Egypt’s firstborn, human and animal. Therefore, I sacrifice each womb’s firstborn male to Him but redeem every firstborn son.’ 📖

## Exodus 13:16
It will be a hand sign and forehead symbol. His mighty hand brought us from Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 13:17
God avoided the shorter route through Philistine land when Pharaoh released them. He said, “War might change their minds and make them return to Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 13:18
He led them through the wilderness toward the Red Sea. Israel left Egypt ready for battle. 📖

## Exodus 13:19
Moses carried Joseph’s bones. Joseph made Israel swear, “God will attend to you. Then carry my bones from here with you.” 📖

## Exodus 13:20
They left Succoth and camped at Etham, on the wilderness edge. 📖

## Exodus 13:21
The LORD led them by a cloud pillar during the day and a fire pillar at night. The fire gave light so they could travel day or night. 📖

## Exodus 13:22
The cloud pillar by day and fire pillar by night never left their place before the people. 📖
