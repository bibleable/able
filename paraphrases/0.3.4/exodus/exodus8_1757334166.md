## Exodus 8:1
The LORD told Moses, “Tell Pharaoh, ‘The LORD says, release My people to worship Me. 📖

## Exodus 8:2
If you refuse, I will plague your whole country with frogs. 📖

## Exodus 8:3
The Nile will fill with frogs. They will enter your palace, bedroom, and bed, your officials’ and people’s homes, your ovens, and your kneading bowls. 📖

## Exodus 8:4
They will come on you, your people, and all your officials.’” 📖

## Exodus 8:5
The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to stretch his staff and hand over rivers, canals, and ponds. Bring frogs onto Egypt’s land.” 📖

## Exodus 8:6
Aaron stretched his hand over Egypt’s waters. Frogs covered Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 8:7
Magicians did the same through magic and brought frogs onto Egypt’s land. 📖

## Exodus 8:8
Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron. “Pray that the LORD removes frogs from me and my people. Then I will release your people to sacrifice to Him.” 📖

## Exodus 8:9
Moses said, “You may have the honor of choosing. When should I pray for you, your officials, and your people? Frogs will leave you and your houses, remaining only in the Nile.” 📖

## Exodus 8:10
Pharaoh answered, “Tomorrow.” Moses said, “As you say, so you know no one is like the LORD our God. 📖

## Exodus 8:11
Frogs will leave you, your homes, your officials, and your people. Only those in the Nile will remain.” 📖

## Exodus 8:12
Moses and Aaron left Pharaoh. Moses cried to the LORD about the frogs He brought against Pharaoh. 📖

## Exodus 8:13
The LORD answered his request. Frogs in the houses, courtyards, and fields died. 📖

## Exodus 8:14
People piled them into countless heaps. A terrible stench filled the land. 📖

## Exodus 8:15
Pharaoh saw relief and hardened his heart. He refused to listen, as the LORD said. 📖

## Exodus 8:16
The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to stretch his staff and strike the ground’s dust. It will become swarms of gnats throughout Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 8:17
They obeyed. Aaron struck the dust with his staff. Gnats covered people and animals. All Egypt’s dust became gnats. 📖

## Exodus 8:18
Magicians tried to make gnats through magic but failed. Gnats remained on people and animals. 📖

## Exodus 8:19
They told Pharaoh, “This is God’s finger.” His heart remained hard. He refused to listen, as the LORD said. 📖

## Exodus 8:20
The LORD told Moses, “Rise early and confront Pharaoh as he goes to the water. Tell him, ‘The LORD says, release My people to worship Me. 📖

## Exodus 8:21
Otherwise, I will send flies on you, your officials, your people, and your houses. Egypt’s homes and the ground beneath them will fill with flies. 📖

## Exodus 8:22
I will spare Goshen, My people’s home, that day. No flies will swarm there. You will know I, the LORD, am in the land. 📖

## Exodus 8:23
I will distinguish My people from yours. This sign will happen tomorrow.’” 📖

## Exodus 8:24
The LORD did so. Thick swarms filled Pharaoh’s palace and his officials’ homes. Flies ruined the land throughout Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 8:25
Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron. “Sacrifice to your God within this land.” 📖

## Exodus 8:26
Moses replied, “That would be wrong. Egypt detests our sacrifices to the LORD our God. Would they not stone us for offering such sacrifices before them? 📖

## Exodus 8:27
We must travel three days into the wilderness and sacrifice as the LORD our God commands.” 📖

## Exodus 8:28
Pharaoh replied, “I will release you to sacrifice to Him in the wilderness. Do not go far. Pray for me.” 📖

## Exodus 8:29
Moses said, “After leaving, I will pray to the LORD that tomorrow flies leave Pharaoh, his officials, and his people. Do not deceive again by refusing the people’s release to sacrifice to the LORD.” 📖

## Exodus 8:30
Moses left Pharaoh and prayed. 📖

## Exodus 8:31
The LORD did as requested. He removed flies from Pharaoh, his officials, and his people. Not one remained. 📖

## Exodus 8:32
Pharaoh hardened his heart again. He refused to release the people. 📖
