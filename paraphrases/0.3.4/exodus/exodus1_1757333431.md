## Exodus 1:1
These sons of Israel went to Egypt with Jacob, each with his family: 📖

## Exodus 1:2
Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, 📖

## Exodus 1:3
Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin, 📖

## Exodus 1:4
Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. 📖

## Exodus 1:5
Jacob’s descendants totaled seventy, including Joseph, already in Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 1:6
Joseph, all his brothers, and their whole generation died. 📖

## Exodus 1:7
Israel had many children and multiplied rapidly. They became so numerous that they filled the land. 📖

## Exodus 1:8
A new king who did not know Joseph ruled Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 1:9
He told his people, “Israel has become too numerous and powerful for us. 📖

## Exodus 1:10
Let us deal shrewdly with them, or they will multiply further. If war comes, they may join our enemies, fight us, and leave the country.” 📖

## Exodus 1:11
Egypt appointed taskmasters to oppress Israel with forced labor. Israel built Pharaoh’s storage towns, Pithom and Rameses. 📖

## Exodus 1:12
The more Egypt oppressed them, the more they multiplied and flourished. Egypt grew afraid of Israel. 📖

## Exodus 1:13
They forced Israel to work ruthlessly. 📖

## Exodus 1:14
They made Israel’s life bitter through hard work with brick and mortar and every kind of field labor. All their imposed service was harsh. 📖

## Exodus 1:15
Egypt’s king spoke to the Hebrew midwives Shiphrah and Puah. 📖

## Exodus 1:16
“Watch the birthstools when helping Hebrew women give birth. Kill every son, but let daughters live.” 📖

## Exodus 1:17
The midwives feared God. They disobeyed Egypt’s king and let boys live. 📖

## Exodus 1:18
He summoned them. “Why did you do this and let boys live?” 📖

## Exodus 1:19
They answered Pharaoh, “Hebrew women differ from Egyptian women. They are vigorous and give birth before midwives arrive.” 📖

## Exodus 1:20
God treated the midwives well. The people multiplied and became more numerous. 📖

## Exodus 1:21
Because the midwives feared God, He gave them their own families. 📖

## Exodus 1:22
Pharaoh commanded all his people, “Throw every Hebrew newborn son into the Nile. You may let every daughter live.” 📖
