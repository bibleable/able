## Exodus 9:1
The LORD told Moses, “Tell Pharaoh, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, says, release My people to worship Me. 📖

## Exodus 9:2
If you keep restraining them and refuse, 📖

## Exodus 9:3
His hand will bring severe plague on your field livestock: horses, donkeys, camels, herds, and flocks. 📖

## Exodus 9:4
The LORD will distinguish Israel’s livestock from Egypt’s. No Israelite animal will die.’” 📖

## Exodus 9:5
He set the time. “The LORD will do this tomorrow.” 📖

## Exodus 9:6
Next day, He did. All Egypt’s livestock died. Not one Israelite animal died. 📖

## Exodus 9:7
Pharaoh sent officials and learned that no Israelite livestock died. His heart remained hard. He refused to release the people. 📖

## Exodus 9:8
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, “Take handfuls of furnace soot. Moses must throw it into the air before Pharaoh. 📖

## Exodus 9:9
It will become fine dust throughout Egypt. Festering boils will appear on people and animals everywhere.” 📖

## Exodus 9:10
They stood before Pharaoh with furnace soot. Moses threw it into the air. Festering boils appeared on people and animals. 📖

## Exodus 9:11
Magicians could not stand before Moses. Boils covered them and all Egypt’s people. 📖

## Exodus 9:12
The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart. He refused to listen, as the LORD told Moses. 📖

## Exodus 9:13
The LORD told Moses, “Rise early and tell Pharaoh, ‘The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, says, release My people to worship Me. 📖

## Exodus 9:14
Otherwise, I will send all My plagues against you, your officials, and your people. You will know no one on earth is like Me. 📖

## Exodus 9:15
I could already have stretched My hand to wipe you and your people from earth with plague. 📖

## Exodus 9:16
I raised you up to show you My power and have My name proclaimed throughout earth. 📖

## Exodus 9:17
Yet you dominate My people and refuse to release them. 📖

## Exodus 9:18
At this time tomorrow, I will send Egypt’s worst hail since its founding. 📖

## Exodus 9:19
Order shelter for your livestock and everything in the fields. Every person or animal left outside in the fields will die under the hail.’” 📖

## Exodus 9:20
Officials who feared the LORD’s word hurried their servants and livestock into shelter. 📖

## Exodus 9:21
Those ignoring His word left them in the fields. 📖

## Exodus 9:22
The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand toward heaven. Hail will fall throughout Egypt on people, animals, and every field plant.” 📖

## Exodus 9:23
Moses raised his staff toward heaven. The LORD sent thunder and hail, with lightning striking the ground. He rained hail on Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 9:24
Lightning flashed continuously through the hail. No hail like it had fallen in Egypt since it became a nation. 📖

## Exodus 9:25
It struck everything in Egypt’s fields, people and animals. It beat down every plant and stripped every tree. 📖

## Exodus 9:26
Only Goshen, Israel’s home, had no hail. 📖

## Exodus 9:27
Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron. “I sinned this time. The LORD is righteous. My people and I are wicked. 📖

## Exodus 9:28
Pray to Him. We have had enough thunder and hail. I will release you. You need not stay.” 📖

## Exodus 9:29
Moses replied, “After leaving town, I will spread my hands to the LORD. Thunder and hail will stop. You will know earth belongs to Him. 📖

## Exodus 9:30
But I know you and your officials still do not fear the LORD our God.” 📖

## Exodus 9:31
Flax and barley were destroyed because the barley was ripe and flax flowering. 📖

## Exodus 9:32
Wheat and spelt survived because they are late crops. 📖

## Exodus 9:33
Moses left Pharaoh, went outside town, and spread his hands to the LORD. Thunder, hail, and rain stopped. 📖

## Exodus 9:34
Pharaoh saw the storm stop and sinned again. He and his officials hardened their hearts. 📖

## Exodus 9:35
He refused to release Israel, as the LORD said through Moses. 📖
