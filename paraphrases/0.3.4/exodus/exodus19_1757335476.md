## Exodus 19:1
In the third month, on the same day of the month they left Egypt, Israel reached the Wilderness of Sinai. 📖

## Exodus 19:2
They left Rephidim and camped in Sinai’s wilderness before the mountain. 📖

## Exodus 19:3
Moses went to God. The LORD called from the mountain, “Tell Jacob’s house and Israel’s sons, 📖

## Exodus 19:4
‘You saw what I did to Egypt. I carried you on eagles’ wings and brought you to Myself. 📖

## Exodus 19:5
If you obey My voice and keep My covenant, you will be My treasured possession among all nations. All earth belongs to Me. 📖

## Exodus 19:6
You will be My kingdom of priests and holy nation.’ Tell Israel these words.” 📖

## Exodus 19:7
Moses summoned the elders and gave all the LORD’s commanded words. 📖

## Exodus 19:8
Everyone answered together, “We will do everything He said.” Moses reported their answer to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 19:9
The LORD said, “I will come in a dense cloud. They will hear Me speak with you and trust you always.” Moses reported what they said. 📖

## Exodus 19:10
He said, “Dedicate the people today and tomorrow. They must wash their clothes. 📖

## Exodus 19:11
Prepare for the third day. Then the LORD will descend on Mount Sinai before everyone’s eyes. 📖

## Exodus 19:12
Set a boundary around the mountain. Warn them not to climb it or touch its base. Put anyone touching it to death. 📖

## Exodus 19:13
Do not touch the offender by hand. Stone him or shoot him with arrows. No offender, human or animal, may live. Approach the mountain only at the ram’s horn’s long blast.” 📖

## Exodus 19:14
Moses descended and dedicated the people. They washed their clothes. 📖

## Exodus 19:15
He said, “Prepare for the third day. Do not have sexual relations with a woman.” 📖

## Exodus 19:16
That morning, the third day, thunder, lightning, and a thick cloud covered the mountain. A very loud ram’s horn sounded. Everyone in camp trembled. 📖

## Exodus 19:17
Moses led them from camp to meet God at the mountain’s foot. 📖

## Exodus 19:18
Smoke covered Mount Sinai because the LORD descended in fire. It rose like furnace smoke. The whole mountain shook violently. 📖

## Exodus 19:19
The ram’s horn grew louder. Moses spoke, and God answered in thunder. 📖

## Exodus 19:20
The LORD descended to Sinai’s summit and called Moses up. Moses climbed. 📖

## Exodus 19:21
The LORD said, “Go down and warn them not to break through to see Me. Many could perish. 📖

## Exodus 19:22
Even priests approaching Me must dedicate themselves, or I will break out against them.” 📖

## Exodus 19:23
Moses replied, “They cannot climb Sinai. You solemnly ordered a boundary and commanded us to set the mountain apart as holy.” 📖

## Exodus 19:24
The LORD said, “Go down and bring Aaron. Priests and people must not break through to approach Me, or I will break out against them.” 📖

## Exodus 19:25
Moses descended and spoke to the people. 📖
