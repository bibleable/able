## Exodus 18:1
Jethro, Moses’ father-in-law and Midian’s priest, heard everything God did for Moses and Israel. He heard the LORD brought Israel from Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 18:2
Jethro received Zipporah, Moses’ wife, after Moses sent her back. 📖

## Exodus 18:3
He also received her two sons. Moses named one Gershom, saying, “I was a foreigner in a foreign land.” 📖

## Exodus 18:4
He named the other Eliezer, saying, “My father’s God helped me and rescued me from Pharaoh’s sword.” 📖

## Exodus 18:5
Jethro came with Moses’ wife and sons to his desert camp at God’s mountain. 📖

## Exodus 18:6
He sent word, “Your father-in-law Jethro comes with your wife and her two sons.” 📖

## Exodus 18:7
Moses met him, bowed, and kissed him. They greeted one another and entered the tent. 📖

## Exodus 18:8
Moses told him everything the LORD did to Pharaoh and Egypt for Israel. He described their journey’s hardships and the LORD’s deliverance. 📖

## Exodus 18:9
Jethro rejoiced at the LORD’s goodness in rescuing Israel from Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 18:10
He said, “Blessed be the LORD, who rescued you from Pharaoh and Egypt. He rescued His people from Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 18:11
I know He is greater than all gods. He acted when Egypt treated Israel arrogantly.” 📖

## Exodus 18:12
Jethro brought God a burnt offering and sacrifices. Aaron and all Israel’s elders ate bread with him before God. 📖

## Exodus 18:13
Next day, Moses judged the people. They stood around him from morning to evening. 📖

## Exodus 18:14
Jethro asked, “What are you doing? Why judge alone while everyone waits from morning to evening?” 📖

## Exodus 18:15
Moses replied, “They come to seek God’s counsel. 📖

## Exodus 18:16
They bring disputes for me to judge. I teach God’s statutes and laws.” 📖

## Exodus 18:17
Jethro replied, “This is not good. 📖

## Exodus 18:18
You and these people will wear out. The task is too heavy to handle alone. 📖

## Exodus 18:19
Listen to my advice, and may God be with you. Represent the people before God and bring their cases to Him. 📖

## Exodus 18:20
Teach statutes and laws. Show them how to live and what work to do. 📖

## Exodus 18:21
Choose capable men who fear God, are trustworthy, and hate dishonest gain. Appoint leaders over thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. 📖

## Exodus 18:22
They must judge continually, bringing you major cases and handling minor ones. Sharing the load will ease your burden. 📖

## Exodus 18:23
If you follow this advice and God directs it, you can endure. Everyone can return home peacefully.” 📖

## Exodus 18:24
Moses obeyed all his father-in-law’s advice. 📖

## Exodus 18:25
He chose capable men from all Israel to lead thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. 📖

## Exodus 18:26
They judged continually. They brought difficult cases to Moses and judged minor ones themselves. 📖

## Exodus 18:27
Moses sent Jethro on his way. He returned to his own land. 📖
