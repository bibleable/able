## Exodus 30:1
Make an altar from acacia wood to burn incense. 📖

## Exodus 30:2
Make it square, one cubit long and one cubit wide. Make it two cubits high. Its horns must form one piece with it. 📖

## Exodus 30:3
Cover its top, sides, and horns with pure gold. Make a gold molding around it. 📖

## Exodus 30:4
Make two gold rings below the molding on opposite sides. They will hold the poles used to carry the altar. 📖

## Exodus 30:5
Make the poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold. 📖

## Exodus 30:6
Put the altar in front of the veil before the ark of the Testimony. Put it before the mercy seat over the Testimony. I will meet with you there. 📖

## Exodus 30:7
Aaron must burn fragrant incense on it every morning when he tends the lamps. 📖

## Exodus 30:8
Aaron must also burn incense when he sets up the lamps at twilight. This incense must burn regularly before the LORD through future generations. 📖

## Exodus 30:9
Do not offer unauthorized incense, burnt offerings, or grain offerings on this altar. Do not pour drink offerings on it. 📖

## Exodus 30:10
Once each year, Aaron must make atonement on the horns of the altar. He must use blood from the sin offering for atonement. Do this each year through your generations. The altar is most holy to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 30:11
Then the LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

## Exodus 30:12
When you count the Israelites in a census, each man must pay the LORD a ransom for his life. Then no plague will strike them when you count them. 📖

## Exodus 30:13
Everyone who crosses over to join those counted must pay half a sanctuary shekel. A sanctuary shekel weighs twenty gerahs. This half shekel is an offering to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 30:14
Everyone aged twenty years or older who crosses over must give this offering to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 30:15
Give this offering to the LORD to make atonement for your lives. The rich must not give more than half a shekel. The poor must not give less. 📖

## Exodus 30:16
Take this atonement money from the Israelites. Use it for service in the Tent of Meeting. It will be a memorial for the Israelites before the LORD, to make atonement for your lives. 📖

## Exodus 30:17
The LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

## Exodus 30:18
Make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing. Put it between the Tent of Meeting and the altar. Fill it with water. 📖

## Exodus 30:19
Aaron and his sons must use this water to wash their hands and feet. 📖

## Exodus 30:20
They must wash with water whenever they enter the Tent of Meeting. They must also wash before approaching the altar to present a food offering to the LORD. They must do this so they will not die. 📖

## Exodus 30:21
They must wash their hands and feet so they will not die. This is a permanent rule for Aaron and his descendants through future generations. 📖

## Exodus 30:22
Then the LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

## Exodus 30:23
Take the finest spices. Use 500 shekels of liquid myrrh. Use half that amount, 250 shekels, of fragrant cinnamon. Also use 250 shekels of fragrant cane. 📖

## Exodus 30:24
Add 500 shekels of cassia. Measure all these spices by the sanctuary shekel. Also take one hin of olive oil. 📖

## Exodus 30:25
Use these ingredients to prepare sacred anointing oil. Blend it with the skill of a perfumer to give it fragrance. This will be sacred anointing oil. 📖

## Exodus 30:26
Use the oil to anoint the Tent of Meeting and the ark of the Testimony. 📖

## Exodus 30:27
Also anoint the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, and the incense altar. 📖

## Exodus 30:28
Also anoint the altar for burnt offerings and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand. 📖

## Exodus 30:29
Dedicate them so they will be most holy. Whatever touches them will be holy. 📖

## Exodus 30:30
Anoint Aaron and his sons. Dedicate them to serve Me as priests. 📖

## Exodus 30:31
Tell the Israelites that this will be My sacred anointing oil through future generations. 📖

## Exodus 30:32
Do not use it to anoint an ordinary man. Do not make another mixture with the same formula. It is holy. You must regard it as holy. 📖

## Exodus 30:33
Anyone who makes a perfume like it or puts it on an outsider must be cut off from his people. 📖

## Exodus 30:34
The LORD also spoke to Moses. Take equal amounts of these fragrant spices: gum resin, onycha, galbanum, and pure frankincense. 📖

## Exodus 30:35
Blend them into fragrant incense with the skill of a perfumer. Season it with salt. It must be pure and holy. 📖

## Exodus 30:36
Grind some into fine powder. Put it before the Testimony in the Tent of Meeting, where I will meet with you. You must regard it as most holy. 📖

## Exodus 30:37
Never use this formula to make incense for yourselves. Regard it as holy to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 30:38
Anyone who makes incense like it to enjoy its fragrance must be cut off from his people. 📖
