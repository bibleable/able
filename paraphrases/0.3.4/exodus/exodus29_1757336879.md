## Exodus 29:1
“Dedicate Aaron and his sons as My priests this way. Take a young bull and two rams without defect, 📖

## Exodus 29:2
bread without yeast, cakes without yeast mixed with oil, and wafers without yeast coated with oil. Use fine wheat flour. 📖

## Exodus 29:3
Put these in a basket. Present them with the bull and two rams. 📖

## Exodus 29:4
Bring Aaron and his sons to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. Wash them with water. 📖

## Exodus 29:5
Dress Aaron in the tunic, ephod robe, ephod, and breastpiece. Fasten the ephod’s woven waistband. 📖

## Exodus 29:6
Put on his turban and attach the holy diadem. 📖

## Exodus 29:7
Anoint him by pouring anointing oil on his head. 📖

## Exodus 29:8
Bring his sons and dress them in tunics. 📖

## Exodus 29:9
Wrap sashes around Aaron and his sons and tie headbands on them. The priesthood belongs to them by permanent statute. Ordain them this way. 📖

## Exodus 29:10
Present the bull before the Tent of Meeting. Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head. 📖

## Exodus 29:11
Kill it before the LORD at the Tent’s entrance. 📖

## Exodus 29:12
Put some bull’s blood on the altar’s horns with your finger. Pour the rest at its base. 📖

## Exodus 29:13
Burn all the fat covering the entrails, the liver’s lobe, and both kidneys with their fat on the altar. 📖

## Exodus 29:14
Burn the bull’s meat, hide, and dung outside camp. It is a sin offering. 📖

## Exodus 29:15
Take one ram. Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head. 📖

## Exodus 29:16
Kill it and splash its blood on every side of the altar. 📖

## Exodus 29:17
Cut it apart. Wash its entrails and legs and put them with its head and other pieces. 📖

## Exodus 29:18
Burn the whole ram on the altar. It is the LORD’s burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma. 📖

## Exodus 29:19
Take the second ram. Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head. 📖

## Exodus 29:20
Kill it. Put blood on Aaron’s and his sons’ right earlobes, right thumbs, and right big toes. Splash the rest on all altar sides. 📖

## Exodus 29:21
Sprinkle altar blood and anointing oil on Aaron, his sons, and their garments. They and their garments will become dedicated. 📖

## Exodus 29:22
Take the ram’s fat, fat tail, entrails’ covering fat, liver’s lobe, both kidneys with their fat, and right thigh. This ram is for ordination. 📖

## Exodus 29:23
Take one loaf, one cake made with oil, and one wafer from the basket of bread without yeast before the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 29:24
Put these in Aaron’s and his sons’ hands. Wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖

## Exodus 29:25
Take them and burn them on the altar atop the burnt offering. It is a food offering with a pleasing aroma before the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 29:26
Wave the breast from Aaron’s ordination ram before the LORD. This wave offering is your portion. 📖

## Exodus 29:27
Dedicate the waved breast and lifted thigh from the ordination ram for Aaron and his sons. These are the wave offering’s breast and heave offering’s thigh. 📖

## Exodus 29:28
They receive this regular portion from Israel. It is the heave offering Israel gives the LORD from peace offerings. 📖

## Exodus 29:29
Aaron’s holy garments pass to his sons for their anointing and ordination. 📖

## Exodus 29:30
His successor entering the Tent of Meeting to serve in the Holy Place must wear them seven days. 📖

## Exodus 29:31
Boil the ordination ram’s meat in a holy place. 📖

## Exodus 29:32
Aaron and his sons must eat it and the basket’s bread at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## Exodus 29:33
Aaron and his sons must eat what made atonement for their ordination and dedication. No outsider may eat it, because it is sacred. 📖

## Exodus 29:34
Burn any ordination meat or bread remaining until morning. Eat none of the leftovers, because they are sacred. 📖

## Exodus 29:35
Do everything I commanded for Aaron and his sons. Ordain them over seven days. 📖

## Exodus 29:36
Offer a bull daily as a sin offering for atonement. Purify the altar by making atonement for it. Anoint it for dedication. 📖

## Exodus 29:37
Make atonement for and dedicate the altar seven days. It will become most holy. Whatever touches it will be holy. 📖

## Exodus 29:38
Offer two lambs aged one year on the altar daily. 📖

## Exodus 29:39
Offer one in the morning and one at twilight. 📖

## Exodus 29:40
With the first, offer one tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with one quarter hin of pressed olive oil. Add one quarter hin of wine as a drink offering. 📖

## Exodus 29:41
Offer the second lamb at twilight with the same grain and drink offerings. It is the LORD’s food offering with a pleasing aroma. 📖

## Exodus 29:42
Make this regular burnt offering throughout future generations at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance before the LORD. I will meet and speak with you there. 📖

## Exodus 29:43
I will meet Israel there. My glory will dedicate that place. 📖

## Exodus 29:44
I will dedicate the Tent of Meeting and altar. I will also dedicate Aaron and his sons to serve Me as priests. 📖

## Exodus 29:45
I will dwell among Israel and be their God. 📖

## Exodus 29:46
They will know I am the LORD their God. I brought them from Egypt to dwell among them. I am the LORD their God. 📖
