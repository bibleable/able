## Exodus 40:1
Then the LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

## Exodus 40:2
Set up the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting, on the first day of the first month. 📖

## Exodus 40:3
Put the ark of the Testimony inside. Screen it off with the veil. 📖

## Exodus 40:4
Bring in the table and arrange the things on it. Bring in the lampstand and set up its lamps. 📖

## Exodus 40:5
Put the gold incense altar in front of the ark of the Testimony. Hang the curtain at the tabernacle entrance. 📖

## Exodus 40:6
Put the altar for burnt offerings in front of the entrance to the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Exodus 40:7
Put the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar. Fill it with water. 📖

## Exodus 40:8
Set up the surrounding courtyard. Hang the curtain for its entrance. 📖

## Exodus 40:9
Anoint the tabernacle and everything inside with the anointing oil. Dedicate the tabernacle and all its furnishings. It will be holy. 📖

## Exodus 40:10
Anoint the altar for burnt offerings and all its utensils. Dedicate the altar. It will be most holy. 📖

## Exodus 40:11
Anoint the basin and its stand. Dedicate them. 📖

## Exodus 40:12
Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. Wash them with water. 📖

## Exodus 40:13
Dress Aaron in the holy garments. Anoint and dedicate him to serve Me as a priest. 📖

## Exodus 40:14
Bring his sons forward and dress them in tunics. 📖

## Exodus 40:15
Anoint them as you anointed their father so they can also serve Me as priests. This anointing will qualify them for a permanent priesthood through future generations. 📖

## Exodus 40:16
Moses did everything as the LORD commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 40:17
The tabernacle was set up on the first day of the first month in the second year. 📖

## Exodus 40:18
Moses set up the tabernacle. He laid its bases, positioned its frames, inserted its crossbars, and set up its posts. 📖

## Exodus 40:19
He spread the tent over the tabernacle and put the covering over the tent. He did this as the LORD commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 40:20
Moses put the Testimony inside the ark. He attached the poles to the ark and put the mercy seat on top. 📖

## Exodus 40:21
He brought the ark into the tabernacle. He hung the veil to screen off the ark of the Testimony. He did this as the LORD commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 40:22
Moses put the table in the Tent of Meeting, on the north side of the tabernacle outside the veil. 📖

## Exodus 40:23
He arranged the bread on it before the LORD, as the LORD commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 40:24
He put the lampstand in the Tent of Meeting opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 40:25
He set up the lamps before the LORD, as the LORD commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 40:26
Moses put the gold altar in the Tent of Meeting, in front of the veil. 📖

## Exodus 40:27
He burned fragrant incense on it, as the LORD commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 40:28
He hung the curtain at the tabernacle entrance. 📖

## Exodus 40:29
He put the altar for burnt offerings near the entrance to the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. He offered the burnt offering and grain offering on it, as the LORD commanded him. 📖

## Exodus 40:30
He put the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar. He filled it with water for washing. 📖

## Exodus 40:31
Moses, Aaron, and Aaron's sons used this water to wash their hands and feet. 📖

## Exodus 40:32
They washed whenever they entered the Tent of Meeting or approached the altar. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 40:33
Moses set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and altar. He hung the courtyard entrance curtain. Then his work was complete. 📖

## Exodus 40:34
The cloud covered the Tent of Meeting. The glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 40:35
Moses could not enter the Tent of Meeting. The cloud had settled on it, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 40:36
Throughout their journey, the Israelites moved onward whenever the cloud rose from above the tabernacle. 📖

## Exodus 40:37
If the cloud did not rise, they stayed where they were until it rose. 📖

## Exodus 40:38
The LORD's cloud was over the tabernacle by day. Fire was in the cloud by night. All the people of Israel saw this throughout their journeys. 📖
