## Exodus 31:1
Then the LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

## Exodus 31:2
I have called Bezalel by name. He is the son of Uri and grandson of Hur, from the tribe of Judah. 📖

## Exodus 31:3
I have filled him with the Spirit of God. I have given him skill, ability, and knowledge in every kind of craft. 📖

## Exodus 31:4
He can design artistic objects made from gold, silver, and bronze. 📖

## Exodus 31:5
He can cut gemstones for settings and carve wood. He can master every craft. 📖

## Exodus 31:6
I have chosen Oholiab as his assistant. He is the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan. I have also given skill to all the craftsmen. They can make everything I commanded you to make. 📖

## Exodus 31:7
This includes the Tent of Meeting, the ark of the Testimony with its mercy seat, and all the other tent furnishings. 📖

## Exodus 31:8
It includes the table and its utensils, the pure gold lampstand and all its utensils, and the incense altar. 📖

## Exodus 31:9
It includes the altar for burnt offerings and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand. 📖

## Exodus 31:10
It also includes the woven garments. These are the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments for his sons to serve as priests. 📖

## Exodus 31:11
They must also make the anointing oil and fragrant incense for the Holy Place. They must make everything as I commanded you. 📖

## Exodus 31:12
The LORD spoke to Moses. 📖

## Exodus 31:13
Tell the Israelites to keep My Sabbaths. This will be a sign between Me and you through future generations. Then you will know that I am the LORD who makes you holy. 📖

## Exodus 31:14
Keep the Sabbath because it is holy to you. Anyone who treats it as unholy must be put to death. Anyone who works on that day must be cut off from his people. 📖

## Exodus 31:15
You may work for six days. The seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, holy to the LORD. Anyone who works on the Sabbath day must be put to death. 📖

## Exodus 31:16
The Israelites must keep and celebrate the Sabbath as a permanent covenant through future generations. 📖

## Exodus 31:17
It is a sign between Me and the Israelites forever. The LORD made the heavens and the earth in six days. On the seventh day, He rested and was refreshed. 📖

## Exodus 31:18
The LORD finished speaking to Moses on Mount Sinai. Then He gave Moses the two tablets of the Testimony. These stone tablets had writing made by the finger of God. 📖
