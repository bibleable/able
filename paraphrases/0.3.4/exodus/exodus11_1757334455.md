## Exodus 11:1
The LORD told Moses, “One more plague will strike Pharaoh and Egypt. Then he will release you. He will drive you out completely. 📖

## Exodus 11:2
Tell men and women alike to ask their neighbors for silver and gold articles.” 📖

## Exodus 11:3
The LORD gave Israel favor with Egypt. Pharaoh’s officials and Egypt’s people greatly respected Moses. 📖

## Exodus 11:4
Moses declared, “The LORD says, ‘About midnight, I will pass throughout Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 11:5
Every firstborn son in Egypt will die. This includes the firstborn of Pharaoh, seated on his throne, and of the servant girl behind the handmill. All firstborn cattle will die too. 📖

## Exodus 11:6
Egypt will cry as never before and never again. 📖

## Exodus 11:7
No dog will snarl at any Israelite person or animal.’ You will know the LORD distinguishes Egypt from Israel. 📖

## Exodus 11:8
All your officials will bow before me, saying, ‘Leave with all your followers!’ Then I will leave.” Moses departed from Pharaoh in fierce anger. 📖

## Exodus 11:9
The LORD told Moses, “Pharaoh will refuse to listen, so My wonders multiply in Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 11:10
Moses and Aaron performed all these wonders. The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, preventing him from releasing Israel. 📖
