## Exodus 10:1
The LORD told Moses, “Go to Pharaoh. I hardened his and his officials’ hearts to perform My signs among them. 📖

## Exodus 10:2
Tell your children and grandchildren how severely I treated Egypt and the miraculous signs I performed there. Then all of you will know I am the LORD.” 📖

## Exodus 10:3
Moses and Aaron told Pharaoh, “The LORD, the Hebrews’ God, says, ‘How long will you refuse humility before Me? Release My people to worship Me. 📖

## Exodus 10:4
If you refuse, I will bring locusts tomorrow. 📖

## Exodus 10:5
They will hide the ground from sight. They will eat everything left by hail and every tree growing in your fields. 📖

## Exodus 10:6
They will fill your homes, your officials’ homes, and every Egyptian home. Your fathers and grandfathers never saw anything like it since coming here.’” Moses left Pharaoh. 📖

## Exodus 10:7
His officials asked, “How long will this man trap us? Release them to worship the LORD their God. Do you not see Egypt is ruined?” 📖

## Exodus 10:8
Moses and Aaron returned at Pharaoh’s command. “Go and worship Him. But who will go?” 📖

## Exodus 10:9
Moses replied, “Young and old, sons and daughters, flocks and herds. We must celebrate the LORD’s feast.” 📖

## Exodus 10:10
Pharaoh replied, “May the LORD be with you if I ever release you and your little ones! Clearly, you intend evil. 📖

## Exodus 10:11
Only the men may worship Him. That is what you requested.” Moses and Aaron were driven from him. 📖

## Exodus 10:12
The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand over Egypt. Locusts will eat every plant, everything left by hail.” 📖

## Exodus 10:13
Moses stretched his staff over Egypt. The LORD sent an east wind all day and night. By morning, it brought locusts. 📖

## Exodus 10:14
They filled Egypt’s entire territory. Never before were there so many, and never again will there be. 📖

## Exodus 10:15
They covered the ground until it was black. They ate every plant and all tree fruit left by hail. Nothing green remained on any tree or plant in Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 10:16
Pharaoh hurriedly summoned Moses and Aaron. “I sinned against the LORD your God and you. 📖

## Exodus 10:17
Forgive me once more. Ask Him to remove this death from me.” 📖

## Exodus 10:18
Moses left and appealed to the LORD. 📖

## Exodus 10:19
The LORD changed the wind to a very strong west wind. It carried locusts into the Red Sea. Not one remained in Egypt. 📖

## Exodus 10:20
The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart. He refused to release Israel. 📖

## Exodus 10:21
The LORD told Moses, “Stretch your hand toward heaven. Darkness you can feel will cover Egypt.” 📖

## Exodus 10:22
He stretched his hand. Total darkness covered Egypt three days. 📖

## Exodus 10:23
No one saw anyone else or left his place for three days. Israel had light in their homes. 📖

## Exodus 10:24
Pharaoh summoned Moses. “Worship the LORD. Your little ones may go too. Leave only your flocks and herds.” 📖

## Exodus 10:25
Moses replied, “You must also provide sacrifices and burnt offerings for the LORD our God. 📖

## Exodus 10:26
All our livestock must go. Not a hoof will remain. We need some to worship Him, and will not know how until we arrive.” 📖

## Exodus 10:27
The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart. He refused to release them. 📖

## Exodus 10:28
Pharaoh said, “Leave! Never see my face again. If you do, you will die that day.” 📖

## Exodus 10:29
Moses replied, “As you say, I will not see your face again.” 📖
