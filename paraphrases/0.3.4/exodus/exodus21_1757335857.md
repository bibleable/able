## Exodus 21:1
“Give them these ordinances. 📖

## Exodus 21:2
A purchased Hebrew servant must serve six years. In the seventh, release him without payment. 📖

## Exodus 21:3
If he came alone, he leaves alone. If he arrived with a wife, she leaves with him. 📖

## Exodus 21:4
If his master gives him a wife who bears children, she and the children remain the master’s. Only the man goes free. 📖

## Exodus 21:5
The servant may say, ‘I love my master, wife, and children. I do not want freedom.’ 📖

## Exodus 21:6
Then his master must bring him before judges. At the door or doorpost, pierce his ear with an awl. He will serve for life. 📖

## Exodus 21:7
If a man sells his daughter as a servant, she is not released like male servants. 📖

## Exodus 21:8
If she displeases the master who chose her for himself, he must allow her redemption. He cannot sell her to foreigners because he broke faith with her. 📖

## Exodus 21:9
If he chooses her for his son, treat her as a daughter. 📖

## Exodus 21:10
If he takes another wife, he must maintain the first wife’s food, clothing, and marital rights. 📖

## Exodus 21:11
If he fails to provide these three things, she leaves free without payment. 📖

## Exodus 21:12
Anyone striking and killing a man must be executed. 📖

## Exodus 21:13
If he did not lie in wait and God allowed it, I will appoint a place for him to flee. 📖

## Exodus 21:14
If he plans and deliberately kills his neighbor, take him from My altar for execution. 📖

## Exodus 21:15
Execute anyone striking his father or mother. 📖

## Exodus 21:16
Execute a kidnapper, whether he sells his victim or keeps him. 📖

## Exodus 21:17
Execute anyone cursing his father or mother. 📖

## Exodus 21:18
Men may quarrel, and one may strike the other with a stone or fist. The victim may survive but remain in bed. 📖

## Exodus 21:19
If he gets up and walks outside with his staff, the attacker escapes punishment. But he must compensate for lost work and ensure full healing. 📖

## Exodus 21:20
If a man beats a male or female servant with a rod and the servant dies, punish him. 📖

## Exodus 21:21
If the servant gets up after a day or two, do not punish the owner. The servant is his property. 📖

## Exodus 21:22
Fighting men may strike a pregnant woman and cause premature birth without further injury. Fine the responsible man as her husband demands and the court allows. 📖

## Exodus 21:23
If serious injury results, require life for life, 📖

## Exodus 21:24
eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot, 📖

## Exodus 21:25
burn for burn, wound for wound, lash mark for lash mark. 📖

## Exodus 21:26
If a man strikes and blinds his male or female servant’s eye, free the servant as compensation. 📖

## Exodus 21:27
If he knocks out the servant’s tooth, free the servant as compensation. 📖

## Exodus 21:28
If an ox kills a man or woman by goring, stone it and eat none of its meat. Its owner bears no responsibility. 📖

## Exodus 21:29
Suppose an ox habitually gores, and its owner is warned but fails to restrain it. If it kills a man or woman, stone it and execute its owner. 📖

## Exodus 21:30
If payment is demanded instead, he may redeem his life by paying the full amount. 📖

## Exodus 21:31
Apply the same rule if it gores a son or daughter. 📖

## Exodus 21:32
If it gores a male or female servant, pay the servant’s master thirty silver shekels. Stone the ox. 📖

## Exodus 21:33
A man may open or dig a pit without covering it. An ox or donkey may fall inside. 📖

## Exodus 21:34
The pit’s owner must pay the animal’s owner. The dead animal then belongs to the pit’s owner. 📖

## Exodus 21:35
If one ox kills a neighbor’s ox, sell the live one and divide the proceeds. Divide the dead animal too. 📖

## Exodus 21:36
If its owner knew its goring habit and failed to restrain it, pay full compensation, ox for ox. The dead animal belongs to that owner.” 📖
