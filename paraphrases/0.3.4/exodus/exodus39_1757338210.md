## Exodus 39:1
They used blue, purple, and scarlet yarn to make specially woven garments for service in the sanctuary. They also made Aaron's holy garments. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:2
Bezalel made the ephod from finely spun linen. He embroidered it with gold and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 📖

## Exodus 39:3
They hammered gold into thin sheets and cut threads from them. Skilled craftsmen wove these threads with the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen. 📖

## Exodus 39:4
They made shoulder pieces for the ephod. They attached these at two corners so the ephod could be fastened. 📖

## Exodus 39:5
The skillfully woven waistband formed one piece with the ephod and had the same workmanship. It contained gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:6
They mounted onyx stones in gold filigree settings. The names of Israel's sons were engraved on the stones like a seal. 📖

## Exodus 39:7
They attached these to the ephod's shoulder pieces as memorial stones for Israel's sons. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:8
He made the breastpiece with the same workmanship as the ephod. He used gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 39:9
When folded double, it was square, one span long and one span wide. 📖

## Exodus 39:10
They mounted four rows of gemstones on it. The first row contained a ruby, a topaz, and an emerald. 📖

## Exodus 39:11
The second row contained a turquoise, a sapphire, and a diamond. 📖

## Exodus 39:12
The third row contained a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst. 📖

## Exodus 39:13
The fourth row contained a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper. The stones were mounted in gold filigree settings. 📖

## Exodus 39:14
The twelve stones represented the names of Israel's sons. Each stone was engraved like a seal with the name of one of the twelve tribes. 📖

## Exodus 39:15
They made braided chains like cords of pure gold for the breastpiece. 📖

## Exodus 39:16
They made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings. They attached the two rings at the two corners of the breastpiece. 📖

## Exodus 39:17
They attached the two gold chains to the two gold rings at the breastpiece corners. 📖

## Exodus 39:18
They attached the other ends of both chains to the two filigree settings. They attached the settings to the ephod's shoulder pieces at the front. 📖

## Exodus 39:19
They made two more gold rings for the other two breastpiece corners. They attached them on its inside edge next to the ephod. 📖

## Exodus 39:20
They made two additional gold rings for the bottoms of the ephod's two shoulder pieces. They attached them at the front, near the seam just above the woven waistband. 📖

## Exodus 39:21
They tied the breastpiece rings to the ephod rings with a blue yarn cord. This kept the breastpiece above the waistband and stopped it from swinging away from the ephod. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:22
They wove the ephod's robe entirely from blue cloth. 📖

## Exodus 39:23
The robe had an opening in its center like the opening in a garment. A collar surrounded the opening to prevent tearing. 📖

## Exodus 39:24
They made pomegranates from blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. They put these around the robe's lower hem. 📖

## Exodus 39:25
They made bells from pure gold. They attached them around the hem between the pomegranates. 📖

## Exodus 39:26
Bells and pomegranates alternated around the lower hem of the robe worn for ministry. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:27
They wove fine linen tunics for Aaron and his sons. 📖

## Exodus 39:28
They also made the turban from fine linen. They made the ornate headbands and undergarments from finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 39:29
They made the sash from finely spun linen, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:30
They made the plate of the holy crown from pure gold. They engraved these words on it like a seal inscription: HOLY TO THE LORD. 📖

## Exodus 39:31
They attached a blue cord to it to mount it on the turban. They did this as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:32
All the work for the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting, was complete. The Israelites did everything as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:33
They brought the tabernacle to Moses. They brought the tent with all its furnishings, clasps, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases. 📖

## Exodus 39:34
They brought the covering of ram skins dyed red, the fine leather covering, and the covering veil. 📖

## Exodus 39:35
They brought the ark of the Testimony with its poles and mercy seat. 📖

## Exodus 39:36
They brought the table with all its utensils and the Bread of the Presence. 📖

## Exodus 39:37
They brought the pure gold lampstand with its row of lamps and all its utensils. They also brought oil for the light. 📖

## Exodus 39:38
They brought the gold altar, anointing oil, fragrant incense, and the curtain for the tent entrance. 📖

## Exodus 39:39
They brought the bronze altar with its bronze grate, poles, and all its utensils. They also brought the basin with its stand. 📖

## Exodus 39:40
They brought the courtyard curtains with their posts and bases, and the curtain for the courtyard gate. They brought its ropes and tent pegs. They brought all the equipment for service in the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Exodus 39:41
They brought the woven garments for service in the sanctuary. These included the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments for his sons to serve as priests. 📖

## Exodus 39:42
The Israelites had done all the work as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Exodus 39:43
Moses inspected all the work. He saw that they had done it as the LORD commanded. Then Moses blessed them. 📖
