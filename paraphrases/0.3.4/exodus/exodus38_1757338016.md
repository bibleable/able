## Exodus 38:1
Bezalel made the altar for burnt offerings from acacia wood. It was square, five cubits long and five cubits wide. It was three cubits high. 📖

## Exodus 38:2
He made a horn at each of its four corners. The horns and altar formed one piece. He covered the altar with bronze. 📖

## Exodus 38:3
He made all the altar utensils from bronze. These were its pots, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans. 📖

## Exodus 38:4
He made a bronze mesh grate for the altar. He put it under the ledge, halfway up from the bottom. 📖

## Exodus 38:5
He cast four rings at the four corners of the bronze grate. These held the poles. 📖

## Exodus 38:6
He made acacia wood poles and covered them with bronze. 📖

## Exodus 38:7
He put the poles through the rings on the altar sides to carry it. He made the altar hollow, using boards. 📖

## Exodus 38:8
He made the bronze basin and its stand from mirrors. The mirrors came from the women who served at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## Exodus 38:9
Then he made the courtyard. Its south side was one hundred cubits long, with curtains of finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 38:10
This side had twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The posts had silver hooks and bands. 📖

## Exodus 38:11
The north side was also one hundred cubits long. It had twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The posts had silver hooks and bands. 📖

## Exodus 38:12
The west side was fifty cubits long. It had curtains, ten posts, and ten bases. The posts had silver hooks and bands. 📖

## Exodus 38:13
The east side, toward the sunrise, was also fifty cubits long. 📖

## Exodus 38:14
The curtains on one side of the entrance were fifteen cubits long. They had three posts and three bases. 📖

## Exodus 38:15
The curtains on the other side were also fifteen cubits long. They also had three posts and three bases. 📖

## Exodus 38:16
All the curtains around the courtyard were made from finely spun linen. 📖

## Exodus 38:17
The posts had bronze bases, silver hooks, and silver bands. Their tops had silver plating. All the courtyard posts had silver bands. 📖

## Exodus 38:18
The courtyard entrance curtain was embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen. It was twenty cubits long. It was five cubits high, like the other courtyard curtains. 📖

## Exodus 38:19
It had four posts and four bronze bases. The hooks, bands, and plating on the tops were silver. 📖

## Exodus 38:20
All the tent pegs for the tabernacle and the surrounding courtyard were bronze. 📖

## Exodus 38:21
This is the inventory of the tabernacle of the Testimony. Moses commanded the Levites to record it. Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest, directed them. 📖

## Exodus 38:22
Bezalel made everything the LORD commanded Moses. Bezalel was the son of Uri and grandson of Hur, from the tribe of Judah. 📖

## Exodus 38:23
Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, worked with him. He was an engraver and designer. He embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen. 📖

## Exodus 38:24
All the gold from the wave offering used for the sanctuary work totaled 29 talents and 730 shekels. The shekels were measured by the sanctuary shekel. 📖

## Exodus 38:25
Silver from the people counted in the community totaled 100 talents and 1,775 shekels. The shekels were measured by the sanctuary shekel. 📖

## Exodus 38:26
Each person gave one beka, or half a sanctuary shekel. This came from everyone aged twenty years or older who crossed over to be counted. There were 603,550 men. 📖

## Exodus 38:27
The hundred talents of silver were used to cast the bases for the sanctuary and veil. There were 100 bases from 100 talents, one talent for each base. 📖

## Exodus 38:28
He used the 1,775 shekels of silver to make the post hooks. He also covered the post tops and supplied their bands with this silver. 📖

## Exodus 38:29
The bronze from the wave offering totaled 70 talents and 2,400 shekels. 📖

## Exodus 38:30
He used it for the bases at the Tent of Meeting entrance. He also made the bronze altar, its bronze grate, and all its utensils from it. 📖

## Exodus 38:31
He also used it for the bases of the surrounding courtyard and its gate. He made all the tent pegs for the tabernacle and its surrounding courtyard from it. 📖
