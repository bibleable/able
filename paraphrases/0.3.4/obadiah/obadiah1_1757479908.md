## Obadiah 1:1
This is Obadiah’s vision. The Lord GOD gives this message about Edom. We heard a message from the LORD. A messenger went among the nations. He said, “Rise, and let us fight Edom!” 📣
## Obadiah 1:2
The LORD says, “I will make you small among the nations. People will deeply despise you. ⚖️
## Obadiah 1:3
Your proud heart deceives you. You live in cracks in the rocks, high above the ground. You think, ‘Who can bring me down?’ ⛰️
## Obadiah 1:4
You can fly high like an eagle and make your nest among the stars. But even from there, I will bring you down.” The LORD declares this. 🦅⚖️
## Obadiah 1:5
“If thieves or night robbers came, would they not steal only what they wanted? How great your ruin will be! If people gathered grapes, would they not leave some fruit? 🍇
## Obadiah 1:6
But enemies will take everything from Esau. They will search out his hidden treasures. 💰
## Obadiah 1:7
All your allies will drive you to the border. Your peaceful friends will deceive you and defeat you. People who eat your bread will set a trap that you do not recognize. 🤝❌
## Obadiah 1:8
On that day, I will surely destroy Edom’s wise people. I will destroy people with understanding in Esau’s mountains.” The LORD declares this. ⚖️
## Obadiah 1:9
“Teman, your powerful soldiers will feel terror. Everyone in Esau’s mountains will die in the slaughter. ⚖️
## Obadiah 1:10
You used violence against your brother Jacob. So shame will cover you, and destruction will end you forever. 💔
## Obadiah 1:11
You stood apart while strangers carried away his wealth. Foreigners entered his gate and cast lots for Jerusalem. You were just like them. *Word help: People cast lots to choose by chance.* ❌
## Obadiah 1:12
Do not enjoy your brother’s day of trouble. Do not celebrate Judah’s destruction. Do not boast proudly when they suffer. ✋
## Obadiah 1:13
Do not enter My people’s gate on their day of disaster. Do not enjoy their pain. Do not steal their wealth during that disaster. ✋
## Obadiah 1:14
Do not stand at the crossroads to stop people who escape. Do not hand over the survivors when they are in trouble. 🛤️✋
## Obadiah 1:15
The Day of the LORD is near for all nations. What you do to others will happen to you. Your own acts will bring punishment on your head. 🌍⚖️
## Obadiah 1:16
You drank on My holy mountain. In the same way, all nations will keep drinking. They will gulp it down. They will become as if they never existed. ⚖️
## Obadiah 1:17
But there will be rescue on Mount Zion. It will be holy. Jacob’s family will take back their land. ⛰️🛡️
## Obadiah 1:18
Jacob’s family will be a great fire, and Joseph’s family will be a flame. Esau’s family will be like dry stalks. Jacob will burn them up completely. No survivor will remain from Esau’s family.” The LORD speaks this. 🔥
## Obadiah 1:19
People from the Negev will own Esau’s mountains. People from the foothills will own the Philistines’ land. They will take Ephraim’s and Samaria’s fields. Benjamin will own Gilead. 🌍
## Obadiah 1:20
Israelites from this group of exiles will own Canaan’s land as far as Zarephath. Exiles from Jerusalem who live in Sepharad will own the Negev’s cities. 🏙️
## Obadiah 1:21
Rescuers will go up Mount Zion to rule Esau’s mountains. The kingdom will belong to the LORD. ⛰️👑
