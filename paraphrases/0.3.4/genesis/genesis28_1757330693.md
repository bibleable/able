## Genesis 28:1
Isaac called Jacob and blessed him. He commanded, “Do not marry a Canaanite woman. 📖

## Genesis 28:2
Go now to Paddan-aram, to your mother's father Bethuel's house. Marry a daughter of Laban, your mother's brother. 📖

## Genesis 28:3
May God Almighty bless you, make you fruitful, and multiply you. May you become a company of peoples. 📖

## Genesis 28:4
May He give Abraham's blessing to you and your descendants. May you possess the land where you live as a foreigner, the land God gave Abraham.” 📖

## Genesis 28:5
Isaac sent Jacob to Laban in Paddan-aram. Laban was Bethuel the Aramean's son and Rebekah's brother. Rebekah was Jacob's and Esau's mother. 📖

## Genesis 28:6
Esau heard that Isaac blessed Jacob and sent him to Paddan-aram to find a wife. Isaac had forbidden him to marry a Canaanite woman. 📖

## Genesis 28:7
Jacob obeyed his father and mother and went to Paddan-aram. 📖

## Genesis 28:8
Esau saw that Isaac disapproved of Canaanite women. 📖

## Genesis 28:9
He went to Ishmael and married Mahalath, daughter of Abraham's son Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth. He added her to his other wives. 📖

## Genesis 28:10
Jacob left Beersheba for Haran. 📖

## Genesis 28:11
He reached a place and stayed overnight because the sun had set. He put a stone from there under his head. He lay down to sleep. 📖

## Genesis 28:12
He dreamed of a ladder standing on earth, its top reaching heaven. God's angels climbed up and down it. 📖

## Genesis 28:13
The LORD stood at the top. He said, “I am the LORD, your father Abraham's God and Isaac's God. I will give you and your descendants the land where you lie. 📖

## Genesis 28:14
Your descendants will be like the earth's dust. You will spread west, east, north, and south. All the earth's families will be blessed through you and your offspring. 📖

## Genesis 28:15
I am with you. I will watch over you wherever you go and bring you back here. I will not leave you until I fulfill My promise.” 📖

## Genesis 28:16
Jacob woke and said, “The LORD is in this place! I did not know it.” 📖

## Genesis 28:17
He was afraid. He said, “This place inspires awe! This is God's house and heaven's gate!” 📖

## Genesis 28:18
Early the next morning, Jacob took the stone he had put under his head and set it up as a pillar. He poured oil on its top. 📖

## Genesis 28:19
He named the place Bethel. The city's former name was Luz. 📖

## Genesis 28:20
Jacob made a vow. “If God stays with me and watches over me on this journey, and if He gives me food and clothes, 📖

## Genesis 28:21
so I return safely to my father's house, then the LORD will be my God. 📖

## Genesis 28:22
This stone I set up as a pillar will be God's house. I will give You a tenth of everything You give me.” 📖
