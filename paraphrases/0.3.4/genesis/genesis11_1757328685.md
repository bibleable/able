## Genesis 11:1
The whole world had one language and a common way of speaking. 📖

## Genesis 11:2
People traveled eastward. They found a plain in Shinar's land and settled there. 📖

## Genesis 11:3
They told each other, “Let us make bricks and bake them thoroughly.” They used brick instead of stone and tar instead of mortar. 📖

## Genesis 11:4
They said, “Let us build a city with a tower reaching heaven. We will make a name for ourselves. Then we will not scatter across the whole earth.” 📖

## Genesis 11:5
The LORD came down to see the city and tower people were building. 📖

## Genesis 11:6
The LORD said, “If one people speaking one language have begun this, nothing they plan will be beyond them. 📖

## Genesis 11:7
Come, let Us go down and confuse their language. They will not understand each other's speech.” 📖

## Genesis 11:8
The LORD scattered them from there across the whole earth. They stopped building the city. 📖

## Genesis 11:9
The place is called Babel because the LORD confused the whole world's language there. From there, He scattered them across the whole earth. 📖

## Genesis 11:10
This is Shem's account. Two years after the flood, at 100 years old, Shem became Arphaxad's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:11
After Arphaxad's birth, Shem lived 500 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:12
At 35 years old, Arphaxad became Shelah's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:13
After Shelah's birth, Arphaxad lived 403 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:14
At 30 years old, Shelah became Eber's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:15
After Eber's birth, Shelah lived 403 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:16
At 34 years old, Eber became Peleg's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:17
After Peleg's birth, Eber lived 430 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:18
At 30 years old, Peleg became Reu's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:19
After Reu's birth, Peleg lived 209 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:20
At 32 years old, Reu became Serug's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:21
After Serug's birth, Reu lived 207 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:22
At 30 years old, Serug became Nahor's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:23
After Nahor's birth, Serug lived 200 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:24
At 29 years old, Nahor became Terah's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:25
After Terah's birth, Nahor lived 119 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 11:26
At 70 years old, Terah became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. 📖

## Genesis 11:27
This is Terah's account. He became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. Haran became Lot's father. 📖

## Genesis 11:28
Haran died while his father Terah was alive. He died in his homeland, Ur of the Chaldeans. 📖

## Genesis 11:29
Abram married Sarai, and Nahor married Milcah. Milcah was Haran's daughter. Haran was the father of Milcah and Iscah. 📖

## Genesis 11:30
Sarai was barren. She had no children. 📖

## Genesis 11:31
Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot son of Haran, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, Abram's wife. They left Ur of the Chaldeans for Canaan's land. When they reached Haran, they settled there. 📖

## Genesis 11:32
Terah lived 205 years. He died in Haran. 📖
