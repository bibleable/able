## Genesis 19:1
The two angels reached Sodom that evening. Lot sat in the city gateway. He saw them, rose to meet them, and bowed facedown. 📖

## Genesis 19:2
He said, “My lords, please come to your servant's house. Wash your feet and spend the night. You can leave early tomorrow.” They replied, “No, we will stay in the square.” 📖

## Genesis 19:3
Lot insisted until they followed him home. He prepared a feast and baked unleavened bread. They ate. 📖

## Genesis 19:4
Before they went to bed, all Sodom's men, young and old, surrounded the house. 📖

## Genesis 19:5
They called to Lot, “Where are the men who came tonight? Bring them out so we can have relations with them!” 📖

## Genesis 19:6
Lot went outside to them. He shut the door behind him. 📖

## Genesis 19:7
He pleaded, “Please, my brothers, do not do this wicked thing! 📖

## Genesis 19:8
I have two daughters who have never slept with a man. Let me bring them out. Do to them what you want. But do nothing to these men. They are under my roof's protection.” 📖

## Genesis 19:9
They replied, “Move aside! This foreigner came here and already acts as a judge! We will treat you worse than them.” They pushed against Lot and approached to break the door. 📖

## Genesis 19:10
The men inside reached out and pulled Lot into the house. They shut the door. 📖

## Genesis 19:11
They blinded the men at the entrance, young and old. The men exhausted themselves searching for the door. 📖

## Genesis 19:12
The two men asked Lot, “Who else do you have here? Get your son-in-law, sons, daughters, and anyone else belonging to you out of the city. 📖

## Genesis 19:13
We will destroy this place. The outcry against its people is so great that the LORD sent us to destroy it.” 📖

## Genesis 19:14
Lot spoke to the sons-in-law engaged to his daughters. He said, “Get up! Leave this place! The LORD will destroy the city!” They thought he was joking. 📖

## Genesis 19:15
At dawn, the angels urged Lot, “Get up! Take your wife and your two daughters here. Otherwise, you will be swept away in the city's punishment.” 📖

## Genesis 19:16
Lot hesitated. The men took his hand and the hands of his wife and two daughters. They led them safely outside the city because the LORD had compassion on them. 📖

## Genesis 19:17
After bringing them out, one said, “Run for your lives! Do not look back or stop anywhere on the plain! Flee to the mountains, or you will be swept away!” 📖

## Genesis 19:18
Lot replied, “No, my lords, please! 📖

## Genesis 19:19
You favored your servant and showed great kindness by saving my life. But I cannot run to the mountains. The disaster will catch me, and I will die. 📖

## Genesis 19:20
A small town nearby is close enough to flee to. Please let me escape there. Is it not small? Then my life will be saved.” 📖

## Genesis 19:21
He answered, “I will grant this request too. I will not destroy that town. 📖

## Genesis 19:22
Hurry there! I cannot do anything until you arrive.” That is why the town was called Zoar. 📖

## Genesis 19:23
By sunrise, Lot reached Zoar. 📖

## Genesis 19:24
The LORD rained sulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah. It came from the LORD out of heaven. 📖

## Genesis 19:25
He destroyed the cities, the whole plain, all the cities' inhabitants, and everything growing on the ground. 📖

## Genesis 19:26
Lot's wife looked back. She became a pillar of salt. 📖

## Genesis 19:27
Early the next morning, Abraham returned to the place where he stood before the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 19:28
He looked toward Sodom, Gomorrah, and the whole plain. Smoke rose from the land like smoke from a furnace. 📖

## Genesis 19:29
When God destroyed the plain's cities, He remembered Abraham. He rescued Lot from the disaster that destroyed the cities where Lot lived. 📖

## Genesis 19:30
Lot feared staying in Zoar. He and his two daughters moved to the mountains. They lived in a cave. 📖

## Genesis 19:31
The older daughter told the younger, “Our father is old. There is no man in the land to sleep with us, as is customary throughout the earth. 📖

## Genesis 19:32
Let us give our father wine until he is drunk. We can sleep with him and preserve his family line.” 📖

## Genesis 19:33
That night, they made their father drunk with wine. The firstborn went in and slept with him. He did not know when she lay down or got up. 📖

## Genesis 19:34
The next day, the older daughter said, “I slept with my father last night. Let us make him drunk with wine tonight. You can sleep with him. We can preserve our father's line.” 📖

## Genesis 19:35
Again that night, they made their father drunk with wine. The younger daughter went in and slept with him. He did not know when she lay down or got up. 📖

## Genesis 19:36
Both Lot's daughters became pregnant by their father. 📖

## Genesis 19:37
The older daughter bore a son and named him Moab. He is the father of today's Moabites. 📖

## Genesis 19:38
The younger daughter bore a son and named him Ben-ammi. He is the father of today's Ammonites. 📖
