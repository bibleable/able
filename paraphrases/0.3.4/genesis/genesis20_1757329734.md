## Genesis 20:1
Abraham traveled to the Negev region. He settled between Kadesh and Shur. He stayed in Gerar. 📖

## Genesis 20:2
Abraham said about his wife Sarah, “She is my sister.” Abimelech, Gerar's king, had Sarah brought to him. 📖

## Genesis 20:3
One night, God came to Abimelech in a dream. He said, “You are as good as dead because the woman you took is married.” 📖

## Genesis 20:4
Abimelech had not approached her. He asked, “Lord, would You destroy an innocent nation? 📖

## Genesis 20:5
Abraham told me she was his sister. She said he was her brother. I acted with an honest heart and innocent hands.” 📖

## Genesis 20:6
God answered in the dream, “I know you acted with a clear conscience. I kept you from sinning against Me. That is why I did not let you touch her. 📖

## Genesis 20:7
Return the man's wife. He is a prophet. He will pray for you, and you will live. If you do not return her, you and everyone belonging to you will certainly die.” 📖

## Genesis 20:8
Abimelech rose early and called all his servants. He told them everything that happened. They were terrified. 📖

## Genesis 20:9
Abimelech called Abraham. He asked, “What have you done to us? How did I sin against you? You brought great guilt on me and my kingdom. You did things to me that should never be done.” 📖

## Genesis 20:10
He also asked, “Why did you do this?” 📖

## Genesis 20:11
Abraham replied, “I thought, ‘People here do not fear God. They will kill me because of my wife.’ 📖

## Genesis 20:12
She really is my sister, my father's daughter. She is not my mother's daughter. She became my wife. 📖

## Genesis 20:13
When God made me leave my father's house, I told Sarah how to show loyalty to me. I said, ‘Wherever we go, call me your brother.’” 📖

## Genesis 20:14
Abimelech gave Abraham sheep, cattle, male servants, and female servants. He returned Sarah, Abraham's wife. 📖

## Genesis 20:15
Abimelech said, “My land is before you. Settle wherever you wish.” 📖

## Genesis 20:16
He told Sarah, “I give your brother a thousand silver pieces. This clears you before everyone with you. You are completely cleared.” 📖

## Genesis 20:17
Abraham prayed to God. God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants. They could have children again. 📖

## Genesis 20:18
The LORD had closed every womb in Abimelech's household because of Sarah, Abraham's wife. 📖
