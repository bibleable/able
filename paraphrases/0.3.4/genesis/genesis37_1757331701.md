## Genesis 37:1
Jacob lived in Canaan, the land where his father stayed. 📖

## Genesis 37:2
This is Jacob's account. At seventeen, Joseph tended the flock with his brothers, the sons of his father's wives Bilhah and Zilpah. He brought their father a bad report about them. 📖

## Genesis 37:3
Israel loved Joseph more than his other sons because Joseph was born in his old age. He made him a robe of many colors. 📖

## Genesis 37:4
His brothers saw their father's greater love for Joseph. They hated him and could not speak kindly to him. 📖

## Genesis 37:5
Joseph dreamed and told his brothers. They hated him more. 📖

## Genesis 37:6
He said, “Listen to my dream. 📖

## Genesis 37:7
We tied grain bundles in the field. My bundle suddenly stood upright. Your bundles gathered around and bowed to mine.” 📖

## Genesis 37:8
They asked, “Will you reign over us? Will you rule us?” His dream and words made them hate him more. 📖

## Genesis 37:9
Joseph told his brothers another dream. “The sun, moon, and eleven stars bowed to me.” 📖

## Genesis 37:10
He told his father and brothers. His father rebuked him. “What is this dream? Will your mother, brothers, and I bow to the ground before you?” 📖

## Genesis 37:11
His brothers envied him. His father remembered his words. 📖

## Genesis 37:12
Later, his brothers took their father's flocks to pasture near Shechem. 📖

## Genesis 37:13
Israel said, “Your brothers are tending flocks at Shechem. Get ready. I am sending you.” Joseph answered, “I am ready.” 📖

## Genesis 37:14
Israel said, “See how your brothers and the flocks are doing. Bring me news.” He sent Joseph from Hebron's valley. Joseph reached Shechem. 📖

## Genesis 37:15
A man found him wandering in the field. He asked, “What are you seeking?” 📖

## Genesis 37:16
Joseph replied, “My brothers. Where are they tending their flocks?” 📖

## Genesis 37:17
The man said, “They moved on. I heard them mention Dothan.” Joseph followed and found them at Dothan. 📖

## Genesis 37:18
They saw him far away. Before he arrived, they plotted to kill him. 📖

## Genesis 37:19
They said, “Here comes the dreamer! 📖

## Genesis 37:20
Let us kill him and throw him in a pit. We will say a vicious animal devoured him. Then we will see what happens to his dreams!” 📖

## Genesis 37:21
Reuben heard and tried to rescue Joseph. He said, “Do not kill him. 📖

## Genesis 37:22
Do not shed his blood. Throw him in this wilderness pit, but do not touch him.” Reuben planned to rescue Joseph and return him to his father. 📖

## Genesis 37:23
Joseph reached them. They stripped off his robe of many colors. 📖

## Genesis 37:24
They threw him into the pit. It was empty and had no water. 📖

## Genesis 37:25
As they ate, they saw Ishmaelites traveling from Gilead. Their camels carried spices, balm, and myrrh to Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 37:26
Judah asked, “What profit is there in killing our brother and hiding his blood? 📖

## Genesis 37:27
Let us sell him to the Ishmaelites instead of harming him. He is our brother, our own flesh.” They agreed. 📖

## Genesis 37:28
Midianite traders passed. The brothers pulled Joseph from the pit and sold him to the Ishmaelites for twenty silver shekels. They took him to Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 37:29
Reuben returned and found Joseph missing. He tore his clothes. 📖

## Genesis 37:30
He told his brothers, “The boy is gone! What will I do?” 📖

## Genesis 37:31
They killed a young goat and dipped Joseph's robe in its blood. 📖

## Genesis 37:32
They sent the colorful robe to their father. They said, “We found this. Is it your son's robe?” 📖

## Genesis 37:33
He recognized it. “My son's robe! A vicious animal devoured him. Joseph was torn to pieces!” 📖

## Genesis 37:34
Jacob tore his clothes and wrapped sackcloth around his waist. He mourned his son many days. 📖

## Genesis 37:35
All his sons and daughters tried to comfort him. He refused. “I will go to Sheol mourning my son.” His father wept for him. 📖

## Genesis 37:36
The Midianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar, Pharaoh's officer and captain of the guard. 📖
