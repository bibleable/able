## Genesis 45:1
Joseph could no longer control himself before his attendants. He ordered everyone out. None stayed when he revealed himself to his brothers. 📖

## Genesis 45:2
He wept so loudly that Egyptians heard. Pharaoh's household heard the news. 📖

## Genesis 45:3
Joseph said, “I am Joseph! Is my father alive?” They were too terrified to answer. 📖

## Genesis 45:4
He called them closer. They approached. “I am your brother Joseph, whom you sold into Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 45:5
Do not be distressed or angry with yourselves because you sold me here. God sent me ahead to save lives. 📖

## Genesis 45:6
Famine has covered the land two years. Five years remain without plowing or harvesting. 📖

## Genesis 45:7
God sent me ahead to preserve you as a remnant on earth. He would save your lives by a great deliverance. 📖

## Genesis 45:8
God sent me, not you. He made me a father to Pharaoh, lord of his whole household, and ruler over all Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 45:9
Hurry to my father. Say, ‘Your son Joseph says God made him lord of all Egypt. Come without delay. 📖

## Genesis 45:10
Live in Goshen near me, with your children, grandchildren, flocks, herds, and everything you own. 📖

## Genesis 45:11
I will provide for you. Five famine years remain. Otherwise, you, your household, and everything you own will become destitute.’ 📖

## Genesis 45:12
You and my brother Benjamin can see that I, Joseph, am speaking to you. 📖

## Genesis 45:13
Tell my father about my splendor in Egypt and everything you saw. Bring him quickly.” 📖

## Genesis 45:14
Joseph embraced Benjamin and wept. Benjamin wept as they embraced. 📖

## Genesis 45:15
He kissed and wept over all his brothers. Afterward, they spoke with him. 📖

## Genesis 45:16
Pharaoh and his servants were pleased to hear Joseph's brothers had arrived. 📖

## Genesis 45:17
Pharaoh told Joseph, “Tell them to load their animals and return to Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 45:18
Bring your father and families to me. I will give you Egypt's best land. You will eat from the land's richness. 📖

## Genesis 45:19
Tell them to take Egyptian wagons for their young children and wives. Bring your father and return. 📖

## Genesis 45:20
Do not worry about belongings. The best of all Egypt is yours.” 📖

## Genesis 45:21
Israel's sons obeyed. Joseph gave wagons as Pharaoh ordered and provisions for their journey. 📖

## Genesis 45:22
He gave each new garments. Benjamin received three hundred silver shekels and five sets of clothes. 📖

## Genesis 45:23
He sent his father ten donkeys carrying Egypt's best goods. Ten female donkeys carried grain, bread, and provisions for his father's journey. 📖

## Genesis 45:24
Joseph sent his brothers away. He said, “Do not quarrel on the way!” 📖

## Genesis 45:25
They left Egypt and reached Jacob in Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 45:26
They said, “Joseph lives! He rules all Egypt!” Jacob was stunned and did not believe them. 📖

## Genesis 45:27
They repeated Joseph's words. Jacob saw the wagons sent to carry him. His spirit revived. 📖

## Genesis 45:28
Israel said, “Enough! Joseph lives! I will see my son before I die.” 📖
