## Genesis 32:1
Jacob continued his journey. God's angels met him. 📖

## Genesis 32:2
He saw them and said, “This is God's camp.” He named the place Mahanaim. 📖

## Genesis 32:3
Jacob sent messengers ahead to Esau in Seir's land, Edom's country. 📖

## Genesis 32:4
He instructed them, “Tell my master Esau, ‘Your servant Jacob stayed with Laban until now. 📖

## Genesis 32:5
I have oxen, donkeys, flocks, male servants, and female servants. I send my master this message to find favor with you.’” 📖

## Genesis 32:6
The messengers returned and said, “We met your brother Esau. He is coming with four hundred men.” 📖

## Genesis 32:7
Jacob was very afraid and distressed. He divided his people, flocks, herds, and camels into two camps. 📖

## Genesis 32:8
He thought, “If Esau attacks one camp, the other can escape.” 📖

## Genesis 32:9
Jacob prayed, “God of my father Abraham and father Isaac, LORD, You told me to return to my country and relatives. You promised to make me prosper. 📖

## Genesis 32:10
I am unworthy of all Your kindness and faithfulness to Your servant. I crossed the Jordan with only my staff. Now I have become two camps. 📖

## Genesis 32:11
Please save me from my brother Esau's hand. I fear he will attack me and the mothers and children with me. 📖

## Genesis 32:12
You promised to make me prosper. You promised offspring like the sea's sand, too many to count.” 📖

## Genesis 32:13
Jacob stayed there that night. He chose a gift for Esau from his possessions. 📖

## Genesis 32:14
He chose 200 female goats, 20 male goats, 200 female sheep, and 20 rams, 📖

## Genesis 32:15
30 milk camels with their young, 40 cows, 10 bulls, 20 female donkeys, and 10 male donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 32:16
He gave the separate herds to his servants. He said, “Go ahead. Leave space between the herds.” 📖

## Genesis 32:17
He told the leader, “Esau may ask whose servant you are, where you are going, and who owns these animals. 📖

## Genesis 32:18
Say, ‘They belong to your servant Jacob. They are a gift to my lord Esau. Jacob is behind us.’” 📖

## Genesis 32:19
He told the second, third, and all following the herds, “Say the same when you meet Esau. 📖

## Genesis 32:20
Tell him his servant Jacob is behind you.” He thought, “The gift going ahead will calm Esau. Then perhaps he will accept me when we meet.” 📖

## Genesis 32:21
The gifts went ahead. Jacob spent the night in camp. 📖

## Genesis 32:22
That night, Jacob took his two wives, two female servants, and eleven sons. He crossed the Jabbok's ford. 📖

## Genesis 32:23
He sent them across the stream with all his possessions. 📖

## Genesis 32:24
Jacob was alone. A man wrestled with him until dawn. 📖

## Genesis 32:25
The man saw he could not overpower Jacob. He struck Jacob's hip socket and dislocated it as they wrestled. 📖

## Genesis 32:26
The man said, “Release me. Dawn has come.” Jacob replied, “I will not release you unless you bless me.” 📖

## Genesis 32:27
The man asked his name. He answered, “Jacob.” 📖

## Genesis 32:28
The man said, “Your name will be Israel, no longer Jacob. You struggled with God and men and prevailed.” 📖

## Genesis 32:29
Jacob asked, “Please tell me your name.” He replied, “Why ask my name?” He blessed Jacob there. 📖

## Genesis 32:30
Jacob named the place Peniel. He said, “I saw God face to face. Yet my life was spared.” 📖

## Genesis 32:31
The sun rose as he passed Penuel. He limped because of his hip. 📖

## Genesis 32:32
Israelites still do not eat the tendon attached to the hip socket. Jacob's hip socket was struck near that tendon. 📖
