## Genesis 14:1
In those days, Amraphel was Shinar's king, and Arioch was Ellasar's king. Chedorlaomer was Elam's king, and Tidal was Goiim's king. 📖

## Genesis 14:2
They fought Bera, Sodom's king, and Birsha, Gomorrah's king. They also fought Shinab, Admah's king, Shemeber, Zeboiim's king, and Bela's king. Bela is also called Zoar. 📖

## Genesis 14:3
These five kings joined as allies in the Valley of Siddim, also called the Salt Sea. 📖

## Genesis 14:4
They served Chedorlaomer for twelve years. In the thirteenth year, they rebelled. 📖

## Genesis 14:5
In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and his allied kings attacked. They defeated the Rephaites in Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzites in Ham, and the Emites in Shaveh-kiriathaim. 📖

## Genesis 14:6
They also defeated the Horites around Mount Seir, as far as El-paran near the desert. 📖

## Genesis 14:7
They turned back and invaded En-mishpat, also called Kadesh. They conquered all Amalekite territory and the Amorites living in Hazazon-tamar. 📖

## Genesis 14:8
The kings of Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, and Bela, also called Zoar, marched out. They formed battle lines in the Valley of Siddim. 📖

## Genesis 14:9
They faced Chedorlaomer, Elam's king, Tidal, Goiim's king, Amraphel, Shinar's king, and Arioch, Ellasar's king. Four kings fought five. 📖

## Genesis 14:10
The Valley of Siddim was full of tar pits. As Sodom's and Gomorrah's kings fled, some men fell into the pits. Survivors fled to the hill country. 📖

## Genesis 14:11
The four kings seized all Sodom's and Gomorrah's goods and food. They left. 📖

## Genesis 14:12
They also captured Abram's nephew Lot and took his possessions. Lot was living in Sodom. 📖

## Genesis 14:13
An escapee told Abram the Hebrew. Abram lived near the Oaks of Mamre the Amorite. Mamre's brothers were Eshcol and Aner. All three had a treaty with Abram. 📖

## Genesis 14:14
Abram heard his relative was captured. He called out the 318 trained men born in his household. They pursued the captors as far as Dan. 📖

## Genesis 14:15
At night, Abram divided his forces and defeated Chedorlaomer's army. He pursued them to Hobah, north of Damascus. 📖

## Genesis 14:16
He recovered all the goods, his relative Lot and Lot's possessions, the women, and the rest of the people. 📖

## Genesis 14:17
Abram returned after defeating Chedorlaomer and his allied kings. Sodom's king met him in the Valley of Shaveh, also called the King's Valley. 📖

## Genesis 14:18
Melchizedek, Salem's king, brought bread and wine. He was God Most High's priest. 📖

## Genesis 14:19
He blessed Abram. He said, “May God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth, bless Abram. 📖

## Genesis 14:20
May God Most High be blessed! He gave your enemies into your hand.” Abram gave Melchizedek one tenth of everything. 📖

## Genesis 14:21
Sodom's king told Abram, “Give me the people. Keep the goods.” 📖

## Genesis 14:22
Abram answered Sodom's king, “I raised my hand to the LORD God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth. 📖

## Genesis 14:23
I will accept no thread, sandal strap, or anything belonging to you. Then you cannot say, ‘I made Abram rich.’ 📖

## Genesis 14:24
I will accept only what my men ate and the share for Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre, who went with me. They may take their portion.” 📖
