## Genesis 27:1
Isaac was old. His eyes were so weak that he could no longer see. He called his older son Esau. “My son.” Esau answered, “Here I am.” 📖

## Genesis 27:2
Isaac said, “I am old. I do not know when I will die. 📖

## Genesis 27:3
Take your weapons, your quiver and bow. Hunt some game in the field for me. 📖

## Genesis 27:4
Prepare the tasty food I love. Bring it for me to eat so I can bless you before I die.” 📖

## Genesis 27:5
Rebekah heard Isaac speaking to Esau. Esau went to hunt game and bring it back. 📖

## Genesis 27:6
Rebekah told her son Jacob, “I heard your father tell your brother Esau, 📖

## Genesis 27:7
‘Bring game and prepare tasty food for me. I will eat and bless you before the LORD before I die.’ 📖

## Genesis 27:8
My son, obey me. Do exactly what I tell you. 📖

## Genesis 27:9
Bring me two choice young goats from the flock. I will prepare your father's favorite food. 📖

## Genesis 27:10
Take it to him to eat. He can bless you before he dies.” 📖

## Genesis 27:11
Jacob told his mother Rebekah, “Esau is hairy, but my skin is smooth. 📖

## Genesis 27:12
What if my father touches me? He would discover I am a deceiver. I would receive a curse instead of a blessing.” 📖

## Genesis 27:13
His mother replied, “Let your curse fall on me, my son. Obey me and fetch the goats.” 📖

## Genesis 27:14
Jacob brought two goats to his mother. She prepared the tasty food his father loved. 📖

## Genesis 27:15
Rebekah took her older son Esau's finest clothes from the house. She dressed her younger son Jacob in them. 📖

## Genesis 27:16
She put the young goats' skins on his hands and smooth neck. 📖

## Genesis 27:17
She gave Jacob the tasty food and bread she prepared. 📖

## Genesis 27:18
Jacob called to his father, “My father.” He answered, “Here I am. Which son are you?” 📖

## Genesis 27:19
Jacob replied, “I am Esau, your firstborn. I did as you asked. Please sit up and eat my game so you can bless me.” 📖

## Genesis 27:20
Isaac asked, “How did you find it so quickly, my son?” He replied, “The LORD your God brought it to me.” 📖

## Genesis 27:21
Isaac told Jacob, “Come closer so I can touch you, my son. Are you really Esau?” 📖

## Genesis 27:22
Jacob approached Isaac. His father touched him and said, “The voice is Jacob's. The hands are Esau's.” 📖

## Genesis 27:23
Isaac did not recognize him because his hands were hairy like Esau's. He blessed him. 📖

## Genesis 27:24
He asked again, “Are you really my son Esau?” Jacob answered, “I am.” 📖

## Genesis 27:25
Isaac said, “Serve me my son's game so I can eat and bless you.” Jacob brought it, and Isaac ate. Jacob brought wine, and Isaac drank. 📖

## Genesis 27:26
Isaac said, “Come near and kiss me, my son.” 📖

## Genesis 27:27
Jacob approached and kissed him. Isaac smelled his clothes. He blessed him, saying, “My son's smell is like a field the LORD blessed. 📖

## Genesis 27:28
May God give you heaven's dew and the earth's richness. May He give abundant grain and new wine. 📖

## Genesis 27:29
May peoples serve you and nations bow to you. May you master your brothers. May your mother's sons bow to you. May those cursing you be cursed. May those blessing you be blessed.” 📖

## Genesis 27:30
Isaac finished blessing Jacob. Jacob had just left his father when Esau returned from hunting. 📖

## Genesis 27:31
Esau prepared tasty food and brought it to his father. He said, “Sit up and eat your son's game, my father. Then you can bless me.” 📖

## Genesis 27:32
Isaac asked, “Who are you?” He replied, “Esau, your firstborn son.” 📖

## Genesis 27:33
Isaac trembled violently. He said, “Who brought me hunted game? I ate it all before you came and blessed him. He will certainly be blessed!” 📖

## Genesis 27:34
Esau heard his father and cried loudly and bitterly. “Bless me too, my father!” 📖

## Genesis 27:35
Isaac replied, “Your brother deceived me and took your blessing.” 📖

## Genesis 27:36
Esau said, “Jacob is rightly named! He cheated me twice. He took my birthright and now my blessing.” He asked, “Did you save no blessing for me?” 📖

## Genesis 27:37
Isaac answered, “I made him your master. I gave him all his relatives as servants. I sustained him with grain and new wine. What is left for me to do for you, my son?” 📖

## Genesis 27:38
Esau asked, “Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me too!” He wept aloud. 📖

## Genesis 27:39
Isaac answered, “Your home will be away from the land's richness. It will be away from heaven's dew above. 📖

## Genesis 27:40
You will live by the sword and serve your brother. When you rebel, you will tear his yoke from your neck.” 📖

## Genesis 27:41
Esau resented Jacob because of his father's blessing. He thought, “The days of mourning for my father are near. Then I will kill Jacob.” 📖

## Genesis 27:42
Rebekah heard what Esau said. She called Jacob, her younger son. “Your brother Esau comforts himself by planning to kill you. 📖

## Genesis 27:43
My son, obey me. Flee now to my brother Laban in Haran. 📖

## Genesis 27:44
Stay with him for a while, until your brother's fury ends. 📖

## Genesis 27:45
Wait until his anger fades and he forgets what you did. Then I will send for you and bring you back. Why should I lose you both in one day?” 📖

## Genesis 27:46
Rebekah told Isaac, “These Hittite women make me weary of life. If Jacob marries a Hittite woman here, what good is my life?” 📖
