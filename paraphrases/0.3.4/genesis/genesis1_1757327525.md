## Genesis 1:1
God created the heavens and the earth in the beginning. 📖

## Genesis 1:2
The earth had no form and was empty. Darkness covered the deep's surface. God's Spirit hovered over the water's surface. 📖

## Genesis 1:3
God said, “Let there be light.” Light appeared. 📖

## Genesis 1:4
God saw that the light was good. He separated light from darkness. 📖

## Genesis 1:5
God called the light “day” and the darkness “night.” Evening came, then morning. This was the first day. 📖

## Genesis 1:6
God said, “Let there be an expanse between the waters. Let it separate water from water.” 📖

## Genesis 1:7
God made the expanse. He separated the water below it from the water above it. This happened as He said. 📖

## Genesis 1:8
God called the expanse “sky.” Evening came, then morning. This was the second day. 📖

## Genesis 1:9
God said, “Let the water under the sky gather in one place. Let dry land appear.” This happened as He said. 📖

## Genesis 1:10
God called the dry land “earth” and the gathered waters “seas.” He saw that it was good. 📖

## Genesis 1:11
God said, “Let the earth grow plants that bear seed and fruit trees. Each tree must bear fruit with seed according to its kind.” This happened as He said. 📖

## Genesis 1:12
The earth grew plants that bore seed according to their kinds. Trees bore fruit with seed according to their kinds. God saw that it was good. 📖

## Genesis 1:13
Evening came, then morning. This was the third day. 📖

## Genesis 1:14
God said, “Let lights appear in the sky's expanse to separate day from night. Let them be signs marking seasons, days, and years. 📖

## Genesis 1:15
Let them shine from the sky's expanse onto the earth.” This happened as He said. 📖

## Genesis 1:16
God made two great lights. The greater light ruled the day, and the lesser light ruled the night. He also made the stars. 📖

## Genesis 1:17
God put these lights in the sky's expanse to shine on the earth. 📖

## Genesis 1:18
They were to rule day and night and separate light from darkness. God saw that it was good. 📖

## Genesis 1:19
Evening came, then morning. This was the fourth day. 📖

## Genesis 1:20
God said, “Let the waters fill with living creatures. Let birds fly above the earth in the sky's open expanse.” 📖

## Genesis 1:21
God created the great sea creatures and every moving creature filling the waters, according to their kinds. He created every winged bird according to its kind. He saw that it was good. 📖

## Genesis 1:22
God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and multiply. Fill the seas' waters. Let birds multiply on earth.” 📖

## Genesis 1:23
Evening came, then morning. This was the fifth day. 📖

## Genesis 1:24
God said, “Let the earth produce living creatures according to their kinds. Let there be livestock, crawling creatures, and wild animals according to their kinds.” This happened as He said. 📖

## Genesis 1:25
God made wild animals, livestock, and everything crawling on earth according to their kinds. He saw that it was good. 📖

## Genesis 1:26
God said, “Let Us make mankind in Our image and likeness. They will rule the sea's fish, the air's birds, and the livestock. They will rule all the earth and every creature crawling on it.” 📖

## Genesis 1:27
God created mankind in His own image. He created them in God's image. He created them male and female. 📖

## Genesis 1:28
God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and multiply. Fill the earth and bring it under your control. Rule the sea's fish, the air's birds, and every creature crawling on earth.” 📖

## Genesis 1:29
Then God said, “I have given you every plant bearing seed on the whole earth. I have given you every tree whose fruit contains seed. These will be your food. 📖

## Genesis 1:30
I have given every green plant as food to every land animal, bird, and crawling creature. This is for everything that has life's breath.” This happened as He said. 📖

## Genesis 1:31
God looked at everything He made. It was very good. Evening came, then morning. This was the sixth day. 📖
