## Genesis 39:1
Joseph was taken to Egypt. Potiphar, an Egyptian and Pharaoh's officer, bought him from the Ishmaelites who brought him. Potiphar was captain of the guard. 📖

## Genesis 39:2
The LORD was with Joseph. He succeeded while serving his Egyptian master's household. 📖

## Genesis 39:3
His master saw that the LORD was with him. The LORD made everything he did succeed. 📖

## Genesis 39:4
Joseph pleased him and became his personal attendant. Potiphar put him in charge of his household and everything he owned. 📖

## Genesis 39:5
From then on, the LORD blessed the Egyptian's household because of Joseph. Everything in his house and field received the LORD's blessing. 📖

## Genesis 39:6
Potiphar left all his possessions in Joseph's care. He concerned himself only with his food. Joseph was well-built and handsome. 📖

## Genesis 39:7
Later, his master's wife looked at Joseph and said, “Sleep with me.” 📖

## Genesis 39:8
He refused. He said, “My master worries about nothing in his house with me here. He entrusted everything to me. 📖

## Genesis 39:9
No one here is greater than me. He withheld only you because you are his wife. How could I do this great evil and sin against God?” 📖

## Genesis 39:10
She spoke to him daily. He refused to sleep with her or even be near her. 📖

## Genesis 39:11
One day, Joseph entered the house for work. No household servant was inside. 📖

## Genesis 39:12
She grabbed his cloak. “Sleep with me!” He left the cloak in her hand and ran outside. 📖

## Genesis 39:13
She saw the cloak left in her hand and Joseph fleeing outside. 📖

## Genesis 39:14
She called the servants. “This Hebrew was brought here to mock us! He came to sleep with me. I screamed loudly. 📖

## Genesis 39:15
When he heard my cry for help, he left his cloak beside me and fled outside.” 📖

## Genesis 39:16
She kept the cloak beside her until Joseph's master came home. 📖

## Genesis 39:17
She repeated her story. “The Hebrew slave you brought came to mock me. 📖

## Genesis 39:18
I screamed for help. He left his cloak beside me and fled outside.” 📖

## Genesis 39:19
His master heard his wife's story about what his slave had done to her. He became very angry. 📖

## Genesis 39:20
Joseph's master put him in the prison holding the king's prisoners. Joseph remained there. 📖

## Genesis 39:21
The LORD was with him and showed him kindness. He gave him favor with the prison warden. 📖

## Genesis 39:22
The warden put every prisoner in Joseph's care. Joseph was responsible for everything done in the prison. 📖

## Genesis 39:23
The warden did not worry about anything Joseph managed. The LORD was with Joseph and made everything he did succeed. 📖
