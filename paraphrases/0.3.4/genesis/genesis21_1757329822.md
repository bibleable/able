## Genesis 21:1
The LORD attended to Sarah as He said. He did for her what He promised. 📖

## Genesis 21:2
Sarah conceived and bore Abraham a son in his old age. He was born at the time God promised. 📖

## Genesis 21:3
Abraham named the son Sarah bore him Isaac. 📖

## Genesis 21:4
Abraham circumcised Isaac at eight days old, as God commanded. 📖

## Genesis 21:5
Abraham was a hundred years old when Isaac was born. 📖

## Genesis 21:6
Sarah said, “God made me laugh. Everyone hearing this will laugh with me.” 📖

## Genesis 21:7
She said, “Who would have told Abraham that Sarah would nurse children? Yet I bore him a son in his old age.” 📖

## Genesis 21:8
The child grew and was weaned. Abraham held a great feast on the day Isaac was weaned. 📖

## Genesis 21:9
Sarah saw the son Hagar the Egyptian bore to Abraham mocking her son. 📖

## Genesis 21:10
She told Abraham, “Send away the slave woman and her son. Her son will never share the inheritance with my son Isaac!” 📖

## Genesis 21:11
This greatly distressed Abraham because it concerned his son Ishmael. 📖

## Genesis 21:12
God told Abraham, “Do not be distressed about the boy or your female servant. Listen to everything Sarah says. Your offspring will be reckoned through Isaac. 📖

## Genesis 21:13
I will also make the slave woman's son a nation. He is your offspring.” 📖

## Genesis 21:14
Abraham rose early. He put bread and a skin of water on Hagar's shoulders. He sent her away with the boy. She wandered in the Wilderness of Beersheba. 📖

## Genesis 21:15
The water in the skin ran out. She left the boy under one of the bushes. 📖

## Genesis 21:16
She sat nearby, about a bowshot away. She said, “I cannot watch the boy die!” She sat there and wept aloud. 📖

## Genesis 21:17
God heard the boy's voice. God's angel called to Hagar from heaven, “What is wrong, Hagar? Do not fear. God heard the boy where he lies. 📖

## Genesis 21:18
Get up and lift the boy. Take him by the hand. I will make him a great nation.” 📖

## Genesis 21:19
God opened her eyes, and she saw a well. She filled the skin with water and gave the boy a drink. 📖

## Genesis 21:20
God was with the boy. He grew, settled in the wilderness, and became a great archer. 📖

## Genesis 21:21
He lived in the Wilderness of Paran. His mother found him a wife from Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 21:22
Abimelech and his army commander Phicol told Abraham, “God is with you in everything you do. 📖

## Genesis 21:23
Swear to me here before God that you will not deceive me, my children, or my descendants. Show me and this country where you live the kindness I showed you.” 📖

## Genesis 21:24
Abraham replied, “I swear it.” 📖

## Genesis 21:25
Abraham complained to Abimelech about a well Abimelech's servants had seized. 📖

## Genesis 21:26
Abimelech replied, “I do not know who did this. You did not tell me. I heard nothing about it until today.” 📖

## Genesis 21:27
Abraham gave Abimelech sheep and cattle. The two men made a covenant. 📖

## Genesis 21:28
Abraham separated seven female lambs from the flock. 📖

## Genesis 21:29
Abimelech asked, “Why did you set apart these seven female lambs?” 📖

## Genesis 21:30
Abraham replied, “Accept these seven female lambs from my hand. They are my witness that I dug this well.” 📖

## Genesis 21:31
The place was called Beersheba because the two men swore an oath there. 📖

## Genesis 21:32
After the covenant at Beersheba, Abimelech and his army commander Phicol returned to the Philistines' land. 📖

## Genesis 21:33
Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba. He called on the name of the LORD, the Eternal God, there. 📖

## Genesis 21:34
Abraham stayed in the Philistines' land for a long time. 📖
