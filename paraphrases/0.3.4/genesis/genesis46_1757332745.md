## Genesis 46:1
Israel left with everything he had. At Beersheba, he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. 📖

## Genesis 46:2
That night God spoke to Israel in a vision. He called, “Jacob, Jacob!” Jacob answered, “Here I am.” 📖

## Genesis 46:3
God said, “I am God, the God of your father. Do not be afraid to go to Egypt. I will make you a great nation there. 📖

## Genesis 46:4
“I will go with you to Egypt. I will certainly bring you back. Joseph will close your eyes with his own hands.” 📖

## Genesis 46:5
Jacob left Beersheba. Israel’s sons carried their father Jacob in the wagons Pharaoh had sent for him. They also took their children and wives. 📖

## Genesis 46:6
They took the livestock and possessions they had gained in Canaan. Jacob and all his descendants went to Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 46:7
Jacob took all his descendants to Egypt. These included his sons, grandsons, daughters, and granddaughters. 📖

## Genesis 46:8
These are the names of Israel’s sons who went to Egypt, including Jacob and his descendants. Reuben was Jacob’s firstborn son. 📖

## Genesis 46:9
Reuben’s sons were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. 📖

## Genesis 46:10
Simeon’s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul. Shaul’s mother was a Canaanite woman. 📖

## Genesis 46:11
Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## Genesis 46:12
Judah’s sons were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah. Er and Onan died in Canaan. Perez’s sons were Hezron and Hamul. 📖

## Genesis 46:13
Issachar’s sons were Tola, Puvah, Job, and Shimron. 📖

## Genesis 46:14
Zebulun’s sons were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel. 📖

## Genesis 46:15
These were Leah’s sons born to Jacob in Paddan-aram, besides his daughter Dinah. The total number of sons and daughters was thirty-three. 📖

## Genesis 46:16
Gad’s sons were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli. 📖

## Genesis 46:17
Asher’s children were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah. Beriah’s sons were Heber and Malchiel. 📖

## Genesis 46:18
These were Jacob’s sons born to Zilpah, sixteen in all. Laban had given Zilpah to his daughter Leah. 📖

## Genesis 46:19
Joseph and Benjamin were the sons of Jacob’s wife Rachel. 📖

## Genesis 46:20
Manasseh and Ephraim were born to Joseph in Egypt. Their mother was Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera, the priest of On. 📖

## Genesis 46:21
Benjamin’s sons were Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard. 📖

## Genesis 46:22
These were Rachel’s sons born to Jacob, fourteen in all. 📖

## Genesis 46:23
Dan’s son was Hushim. 📖

## Genesis 46:24
Naphtali’s sons were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem. 📖

## Genesis 46:25
These were Jacob’s sons born to Bilhah, seven in all. Laban had given Bilhah to his daughter Rachel. 📖

## Genesis 46:26
Jacob’s direct descendants who came to Egypt numbered sixty-six. This count did not include the wives of Jacob’s sons. 📖

## Genesis 46:27
Joseph had two sons born in Egypt. Including them, the members of Jacob’s family who went to Egypt numbered seventy. 📖

## Genesis 46:28
Jacob had sent Judah ahead to Joseph to get directions to Goshen. Jacob’s family arrived in Goshen. 📖

## Genesis 46:29
Joseph prepared his chariot and went to meet his father Israel in Goshen. He presented himself to his father, embraced him, and wept greatly. 📖

## Genesis 46:30
Israel said to Joseph, “Now I can die. I have seen your face and know that you are still alive!” 📖

## Genesis 46:31
Joseph told his brothers and his father’s household, “I will go and tell Pharaoh about you. I will say, ‘My brothers and my father’s household have come to me from Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 46:32
“‘They are shepherds who raise livestock. They have brought their flocks, herds, and everything they own.’ 📖

## Genesis 46:33
“Pharaoh will call for you and ask, ‘What work do you do?’ 📖

## Genesis 46:34
“Tell him, ‘Your servants have raised livestock since our youth, as our fathers did.’ Then you can settle in Goshen, because Egyptians detest all shepherds.” 📖
