## Genesis 49:1
Jacob called his sons together. He said, “Gather around me. I will tell you what will happen to you in the days ahead. 📖

## Genesis 49:2
“Come together and listen, sons of Jacob. Listen to your father Israel. 📖

## Genesis 49:3
“Reuben, you are my firstborn, my might, and the beginning of my strength. You excel in honor and power. 📖

## Genesis 49:4
“You are uncontrolled like water. You will no longer excel, because you went to your father’s bed. You went onto my couch and defiled it. 📖

## Genesis 49:5
“Simeon and Levi are brothers. Their swords are weapons of violence. 📖

## Genesis 49:6
“May I never enter their council or join their assembly. In their anger, they kill men. On a whim, they cut the tendons of oxen. 📖

## Genesis 49:7
“A curse on their strong anger and cruel wrath! I will disperse them among Jacob’s people and scatter them in Israel. 📖

## Genesis 49:8
“Judah, your brothers will praise you. Your hand will be on your enemies’ necks. Your father’s sons will bow to you. 📖

## Genesis 49:9
“Judah is a young lion. My son, you return from the prey. He crouches and lies down like a lion. Like a lioness, who dares to wake him? 📖

## Genesis 49:10
“The scepter will not leave Judah. The staff will remain between his feet until Shiloh comes. The nations will give him their allegiance. 📖

## Genesis 49:11
“He ties his donkey to the vine and his colt to the best branch. He washes his clothes in wine and his robes in the blood of grapes. 📖

## Genesis 49:12
“His eyes are darker than wine. His teeth are whiter than milk. 📖

## Genesis 49:13
“Zebulun will live by the seashore and become a harbor for ships. His border will reach Sidon. 📖

## Genesis 49:14
“Issachar is a strong donkey that lies between the sheepfolds. 📖

## Genesis 49:15
“He saw that his resting place was good and his land was pleasant. So he bent his shoulder to carry the burden. He accepted work as a servant. 📖

## Genesis 49:16
“Dan will provide justice for his people as one of Israel’s tribes. 📖

## Genesis 49:17
“Dan will be a snake beside the road, a viper in the path. It bites a horse’s heels and makes the rider fall backward. 📖

## Genesis 49:18
“LORD, I wait for Your salvation. 📖

## Genesis 49:19
“Raiders will attack Gad. But he will attack their heels. 📖

## Genesis 49:20
“Asher’s food will be rich. He will provide fine foods for kings. 📖

## Genesis 49:21
“Naphtali is a female deer set free. She gives birth to beautiful fawns. 📖

## Genesis 49:22
“Joseph is a fruitful vine by a spring. Its branches climb over the wall. 📖

## Genesis 49:23
“Archers attacked him with bitterness. They aimed at him with hostility. 📖

## Genesis 49:24
“But he held his bow steady. His strong arms were strengthened by the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob. This was in the name of the Shepherd, the Rock of Israel. 📖

## Genesis 49:25
“Your father’s God helps you. The Almighty blesses you with blessings from the heavens above and the depths below. He gives you blessings of the breasts and womb. 📖

## Genesis 49:26
“Your father’s blessings exceed the blessings of the ancient mountains and the abundance of the everlasting hills. May these blessings rest on Joseph’s head. May they rest on the brow of the prince among his brothers. 📖

## Genesis 49:27
“Benjamin is a hungry wolf. In the morning he devours his prey. In the evening he divides the plunder.” 📖

## Genesis 49:28
These are the twelve tribes of Israel. Their father said these things to them as he blessed them. He gave each one a suitable blessing. 📖

## Genesis 49:29
Jacob instructed them, “I am about to be gathered to my people. Bury me with my fathers in the cave in Ephron the Hittite’s field. 📖

## Genesis 49:30
“The cave is in the field of Machpelah, near Mamre in Canaan. Abraham bought this field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial place. 📖

## Genesis 49:31
“Abraham and his wife Sarah are buried there. Isaac and his wife Rebekah are buried there. I buried Leah there too. 📖

## Genesis 49:32
“The field and its cave were bought from the Hittites.” 📖

## Genesis 49:33
Jacob finished instructing his sons. He pulled his feet into the bed and breathed his last. He was gathered to his people. 📖
