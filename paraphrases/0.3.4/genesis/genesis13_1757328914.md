## Genesis 13:1
Abram went from Egypt up to the Negev with his wife and all his possessions. Lot went with him. 📖

## Genesis 13:2
Abram was extremely rich in livestock, silver, and gold. 📖

## Genesis 13:3
He traveled from place to place from the Negev toward Bethel. He reached his former campsite between Bethel and Ai. 📖

## Genesis 13:4
He returned to the place where he built the altar. Abram called on the LORD's name there. 📖

## Genesis 13:5
Lot traveled with Abram. He also had flocks, herds, and tents. 📖

## Genesis 13:6
The land could not support them both living together. Their possessions were too many for them to stay together. 📖

## Genesis 13:7
Abram's and Lot's herdsmen quarreled. The Canaanites and Perizzites also lived in the land then. 📖

## Genesis 13:8
Abram told Lot, “Please let there be no quarrel between us or our herdsmen. We are relatives. 📖

## Genesis 13:9
The whole land is before you. Separate from me. If you go left, I will go right. If you go right, I will go left.” 📖

## Genesis 13:10
Lot saw the whole Jordan plain as far as Zoar. It had plentiful water, like the LORD's garden and Egypt's land. This was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. 📖

## Genesis 13:11
Lot chose the whole Jordan plain and traveled east. Abram and Lot separated. 📖

## Genesis 13:12
Abram lived in Canaan's land. Lot settled in the plain's cities and pitched his tent toward Sodom. 📖

## Genesis 13:13
Sodom's men were wicked. They sinned greatly against the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 13:14
After Lot left, the LORD told Abram, “Look from where you are. Look north, south, east, and west. 📖

## Genesis 13:15
I will give all the land you see to you and your offspring forever. 📖

## Genesis 13:16
I will make your offspring like the earth's dust. If someone could count the dust, your offspring could be counted. 📖

## Genesis 13:17
Get up and walk through the land's length and width. I will give it to you.” 📖

## Genesis 13:18
Abram moved his tent and lived near the Oaks of Mamre at Hebron. He built an altar to the LORD there. 📖
