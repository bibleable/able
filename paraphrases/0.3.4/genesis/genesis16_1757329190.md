## Genesis 16:1
Abram's wife Sarai had no children by him. She had an Egyptian female servant named Hagar. 📖

## Genesis 16:2
Sarai told Abram, “The LORD has kept me from bearing children. Please go to my servant. Perhaps I can build a family through her.” Abram listened to Sarai. 📖

## Genesis 16:3
Abram had lived in Canaan for ten years. Sarai took her Egyptian servant Hagar and gave her to Abram as his wife. 📖

## Genesis 16:4
He slept with Hagar, and she conceived. When she knew she was pregnant, she despised her mistress. 📖

## Genesis 16:5
Sarai told Abram, “May you bear responsibility for the wrong done to me! I put my servant in your arms. Since she knew she was pregnant, she has treated me with contempt. May the LORD judge between us.” 📖

## Genesis 16:6
Abram replied, “Your servant is in your hands. Do what you want with her.” Sarai treated Hagar so harshly that Hagar fled. 📖

## Genesis 16:7
The LORD's angel found Hagar beside a spring in the desert. It was the spring on the road to Shur. 📖

## Genesis 16:8
He asked, “Hagar, Sarai's servant, where have you come from? Where are you going?” She replied, “I am fleeing from my mistress Sarai.” 📖

## Genesis 16:9
The LORD's angel told her, “Return to your mistress. Submit to her authority.” 📖

## Genesis 16:10
The angel added, “I will greatly multiply your offspring. They will be too many to count.” 📖

## Genesis 16:11
The LORD's angel said, “You have conceived and will bear a son. Name him Ishmael because the LORD heard your cry of suffering. 📖

## Genesis 16:12
He will be a wild donkey of a man. His hand will oppose everyone, and everyone's hand will oppose him. He will live in hostility toward all his brothers.” 📖

## Genesis 16:13
Hagar named the LORD who spoke to her, “You are the God who sees me.” She said, “Here I have seen the One who sees me!” 📖

## Genesis 16:14
The well was therefore named Beer-lahai-roi. It lies between Kadesh and Bered. 📖

## Genesis 16:15
Hagar bore Abram a son. Abram named the son she bore Ishmael. 📖

## Genesis 16:16
Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore him Ishmael. 📖
