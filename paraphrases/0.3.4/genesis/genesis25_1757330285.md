## Genesis 25:1
Abraham had taken another wife named Keturah. 📖

## Genesis 25:2
She bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah to him. 📖

## Genesis 25:3
Jokshan fathered Sheba and Dedan. Dedan's sons were the Asshurites, Letushites, and Leummites. 📖

## Genesis 25:4
Midian's sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these descended from Keturah. 📖

## Genesis 25:5
Abraham left everything he owned to Isaac. 📖

## Genesis 25:6
While alive, Abraham gave gifts to his concubines' sons. He sent them away from Isaac to the eastern land. 📖

## Genesis 25:7
Abraham lived 175 years in total. 📖

## Genesis 25:8
He breathed his last at a ripe old age. He died old and contented. He was gathered to his people. 📖

## Genesis 25:9
Isaac and Ishmael buried their father in Machpelah's cave near Mamre. It was in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite. 📖

## Genesis 25:10
Abraham had bought this field from the Hittites. He was buried there with his wife Sarah. 📖

## Genesis 25:11
After Abraham died, God blessed his son Isaac. Isaac lived near Beer-lahai-roi. 📖

## Genesis 25:12
This is the account of Abraham's son Ishmael. Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's female servant, bore him to Abraham. 📖

## Genesis 25:13
Ishmael's sons, in birth order, were his firstborn Nebaioth, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, 📖

## Genesis 25:14
Mishma, Dumah, Massa, 📖

## Genesis 25:15
Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. 📖

## Genesis 25:16
These were Ishmael's sons, named by their villages and camps. They were twelve princes of their tribes. 📖

## Genesis 25:17
Ishmael lived 137 years in total. He breathed his last and died. He was gathered to his people. 📖

## Genesis 25:18
His descendants settled from Havilah to Shur, near Egypt's border toward Asshur. They lived in hostility toward all their brothers. 📖

## Genesis 25:19
This is the account of Abraham's son Isaac. Abraham became Isaac's father. 📖

## Genesis 25:20
Isaac was forty when he married Rebekah. She was Bethuel the Aramean's daughter from Paddan-aram. She was Laban the Aramean's sister. 📖

## Genesis 25:21
Isaac prayed to the LORD for his wife because she was barren. The LORD heard him. Rebekah conceived. 📖

## Genesis 25:22
The children struggled inside her. She asked, “Why is this happening to me?” She went to inquire of the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 25:23
He said, “Two nations are in your womb. Two peoples from you will separate. One will be stronger than the other. The older will serve the younger.” 📖

## Genesis 25:24
When she gave birth, she had twins. 📖

## Genesis 25:25
The first came out red, covered in hair like a fur coat. They named him Esau. 📖

## Genesis 25:26
His brother followed, grasping Esau's heel. He was named Jacob. Isaac was sixty when the twins were born. 📖

## Genesis 25:27
The boys grew. Esau became a skilled hunter, a man of the field. Jacob was quiet and stayed home. 📖

## Genesis 25:28
Isaac loved Esau because he liked wild game. Rebekah loved Jacob. 📖

## Genesis 25:29
Jacob was cooking stew. Esau came from the field, very hungry. 📖

## Genesis 25:30
Esau said, “Give me some red stew. I am very hungry.” That is why he was also called Edom. 📖

## Genesis 25:31
Jacob replied, “First sell me your birthright.” 📖

## Genesis 25:32
Esau said, “I am about to die. What good is a birthright?” 📖

## Genesis 25:33
Jacob said, “Swear to me first.” Esau swore and sold Jacob his birthright. 📖

## Genesis 25:34
Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. Esau ate, drank, rose, and left. He despised his birthright. 📖
