## Genesis 47:1
Joseph told Pharaoh, “My father and brothers have come from Canaan with their flocks, herds, and everything they own. They are now in Goshen.” 📖

## Genesis 47:2
Joseph chose five of his brothers and presented them to Pharaoh. 📖

## Genesis 47:3
Pharaoh asked Joseph’s brothers, “What work do you do?” They answered, “Your servants are shepherds, as our fathers were.” 📖

## Genesis 47:4
They told Pharaoh, “We have come to live here for a time. The severe famine in Canaan has left no pasture for your servants’ flocks. Please let your servants settle in Goshen.” 📖

## Genesis 47:5
Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Your father and brothers have now come to you. 📖

## Genesis 47:6
“Egypt is before you. Settle your father and brothers in the best part of the land. They may live in Goshen. If you know skilled men among them, put them in charge of my livestock.” 📖

## Genesis 47:7
Joseph brought his father Jacob in and presented him to Pharaoh. Jacob blessed Pharaoh. 📖

## Genesis 47:8
Pharaoh asked Jacob, “How many years have you lived?” 📖

## Genesis 47:9
Jacob answered, “My travels have lasted 130 years. My years have been few and hard. They have not reached the years of my fathers’ travels.” 📖

## Genesis 47:10
Jacob blessed Pharaoh and left him. 📖

## Genesis 47:11
Joseph settled his father and brothers in Egypt. He gave them property in Rameses, the best district in the land, as Pharaoh had commanded. 📖

## Genesis 47:12
Joseph supplied food for his father, his brothers, and his father’s entire household, to feed their families. 📖

## Genesis 47:13
There was no food in the whole region because the famine was very severe. The famine had exhausted Egypt and Canaan. 📖

## Genesis 47:14
Joseph collected all the money in Egypt and Canaan in payment for the grain the people bought. He brought the money into Pharaoh’s palace. 📖

## Genesis 47:15
When the money in Egypt and Canaan was gone, all the Egyptians came to Joseph. They said, “Give us food! Why should we die before your eyes? Our money has run out!” 📖

## Genesis 47:16
Joseph answered, “Bring me your livestock. Since your money is gone, I will sell you food in exchange for your livestock.” 📖

## Genesis 47:17
They brought their livestock to Joseph. He gave them food in exchange for their horses, flocks, herds, and donkeys. Throughout that year, he supplied food in exchange for all their livestock. 📖

## Genesis 47:18
The next year they came to Joseph again. They said, “We cannot hide this from our lord. Our money is gone, and all our livestock belongs to you. We have only our bodies and land left for our lord. 📖

## Genesis 47:19
“Why should we and our land perish before your eyes? Buy us and our land in exchange for food. We and our land will belong to Pharaoh as slaves. Give us seed so that we can live instead of dying. Then the land will not become empty and ruined.” 📖

## Genesis 47:20
Joseph bought all the land in Egypt for Pharaoh. Every Egyptian sold his fields because the famine was so severe. The land became Pharaoh’s property. 📖

## Genesis 47:21
Joseph made the people slaves throughout Egypt, from one end to the other. 📖

## Genesis 47:22
He did not buy the priests’ land. Pharaoh had given it to them. The priests ate the food rations Pharaoh supplied, so they did not sell their land. 📖

## Genesis 47:23
Joseph told the people, “Today I have bought you and your land for Pharaoh. Here is seed for you to plant in the land. 📖

## Genesis 47:24
“At harvest, give one-fifth to Pharaoh. Keep four-fifths as seed for your fields and food for yourselves, your households, and your children.” 📖

## Genesis 47:25
They said, “You have saved our lives! We have found favor with our lord. We will be Pharaoh’s servants.” 📖

## Genesis 47:26
Joseph made a law that one-fifth of the produce belongs to Pharaoh. This law remains in Egypt to this day. Only the priests’ land does not belong to Pharaoh. 📖

## Genesis 47:27
The Israelites settled in Goshen, in Egypt. They gained property there. They became fruitful and greatly increased in number. 📖

## Genesis 47:28
Jacob lived in Egypt for seventeen years. His whole life lasted 147 years. 📖

## Genesis 47:29
As Israel’s death came near, he called his son Joseph. He said, “If I have found favor with you, put your hand under my thigh. Promise to show me kindness and faithfulness. Do not bury me in Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 47:30
“When I lie down with my fathers, carry me out of Egypt. Bury me with them.” Joseph answered, “I will do as you ask.” 📖

## Genesis 47:31
Jacob said, “Swear to me.” Joseph swore to him. Israel bowed in worship at the head of his bed. 📖
