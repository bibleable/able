## Genesis 2:1
The heavens and earth were complete, with all their vast array. 📖

## Genesis 2:2
By the seventh day, God finished His work. He rested from all His work that day. 📖

## Genesis 2:3
God blessed the seventh day and made it holy. He rested that day from all the work of creation He had completed. 📖

## Genesis 2:4
This is the account of the heavens and earth's creation, when the LORD God made them. 📖

## Genesis 2:5
No field shrub had appeared, and no field plant had sprouted on earth. The LORD God had not sent rain. No man was there to cultivate the ground. 📖

## Genesis 2:6
But springs rose from the earth. They watered the ground's whole surface. 📖

## Genesis 2:7
The LORD God formed man from the ground's dust. He breathed life's breath into his nostrils. The man became a living being. 📖

## Genesis 2:8
The LORD God planted a garden in Eden, in the east. He put the man He had formed there. 📖

## Genesis 2:9
The LORD God made every tree pleasing to the eye and good for food grow from the ground. The tree of life stood in the garden's middle. The tree of the knowledge of good and evil was also there. 📖

## Genesis 2:10
A river flowed from Eden to water the garden. From there, it divided into four headwaters. 📖

## Genesis 2:11
The first river is the Pishon. It winds through all Havilah, where there is gold. 📖

## Genesis 2:12
That land's gold is pure. Bdellium and onyx are also found there. 📖

## Genesis 2:13
The second river is the Gihon. It winds through all Cush. 📖

## Genesis 2:14
The third river is the Tigris. It runs along Assyria's east side. The fourth river is the Euphrates. 📖

## Genesis 2:15
The LORD God placed the man in Eden's garden to cultivate and care for it. 📖

## Genesis 2:16
The LORD God commanded him, “You may freely eat from every tree in the garden. 📖

## Genesis 2:17
But do not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. On the day you eat from it, you will certainly die.” 📖

## Genesis 2:18
The LORD God said, “Being alone is not good for the man. I will make a suitable helper for him.” 📖

## Genesis 2:19
The LORD God formed every field animal and bird of the air from the ground. He brought them to the man to see what he would name each. Each living creature received whatever name the man gave it. 📖

## Genesis 2:20
The man named all the livestock, birds of the air, and field animals. But no suitable helper was found for Adam. 📖

## Genesis 2:21
The LORD God made the man fall into deep sleep. While he slept, God took one rib. He closed the place with flesh. 📖

## Genesis 2:22
The LORD God made a woman from the rib He took from the man. He brought her to him. 📖

## Genesis 2:23
The man said, “She is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh. She will be called ‘woman’ because she was taken from man.” 📖

## Genesis 2:24
For this reason, a man will leave his father and mother. He will be united with his wife. They will become one flesh. 📖

## Genesis 2:25
The man and his wife were both naked. They felt no shame. 📖
