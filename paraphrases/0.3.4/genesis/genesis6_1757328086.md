## Genesis 6:1
People began multiplying across the earth. Daughters were born to them. 📖

## Genesis 6:2
The sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful. They married whomever they chose. 📖

## Genesis 6:3
The LORD said, “My Spirit will not contend with man forever because he is mortal. His days will be 120 years.” 📖

## Genesis 6:4
The Nephilim were on earth in those days and afterward. The sons of God had relations with the daughters of men. The women bore children who became mighty men of old, men of renown. 📖

## Genesis 6:5
The LORD saw great human wickedness on earth. Every inclination of the thoughts of man's heart was completely evil all the time. 📖

## Genesis 6:6
The LORD regretted making man on earth. His heart was grieved. 📖

## Genesis 6:7
The LORD said, “I will wipe mankind, whom I created, from the earth's surface. I will wipe away every man, animal, crawling creature, and bird of the air. I am grieved that I made them.” 📖

## Genesis 6:8
But the LORD looked favorably on Noah. 📖

## Genesis 6:9
This is Noah's account. He was righteous and blameless in his generation. He walked with God. 📖

## Genesis 6:10
Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖

## Genesis 6:11
The earth was corrupt before God. It was full of violence. 📖

## Genesis 6:12
God saw the earth's corruption. All living creatures on earth had corrupted their ways. 📖

## Genesis 6:13
God told Noah, “The end of all living creatures has come before Me. They have filled the earth with violence. I will destroy them and the earth. 📖

## Genesis 6:14
Build an ark from gopher wood. Make rooms inside it. Cover it inside and outside with pitch. 📖

## Genesis 6:15
Build the ark 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high. 📖

## Genesis 6:16
Make a roof. Finish the walls one cubit from the top. Put a door in the ark's side. Build lower, middle, and upper decks. 📖

## Genesis 6:17
I will bring floodwaters on earth to destroy every creature under heaven that has life's breath. Everything on earth will die. 📖

## Genesis 6:18
But I will establish My covenant with you. You, your sons, your wife, and your sons' wives will enter the ark together. 📖

## Genesis 6:19
Bring two of every living creature into the ark, male and female. Keep them alive with you. 📖

## Genesis 6:20
Two of every kind of bird, animal, and crawling creature will come to you to be kept alive. 📖

## Genesis 6:21
Gather every kind of food that is eaten. Store it as food for yourselves and the animals.” 📖

## Genesis 6:22
Noah did everything exactly as God commanded. 📖
