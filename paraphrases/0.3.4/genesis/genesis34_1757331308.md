## Genesis 34:1
Dinah, Leah's daughter by Jacob, went to visit the land's daughters. 📖

## Genesis 34:2
Shechem saw her. He was the son of Hamor the Hivite, the region's prince. He took her and forced her to sleep with him. 📖

## Genesis 34:3
He was drawn to Jacob's daughter Dinah. He loved the young girl and spoke tenderly to her. 📖

## Genesis 34:4
Shechem told his father Hamor, “Get me this girl as my wife.” 📖

## Genesis 34:5
Jacob heard that Shechem defiled Dinah. His sons were in the field with the livestock. He stayed silent until they returned. 📖

## Genesis 34:6
Shechem's father Hamor came to speak with Jacob. 📖

## Genesis 34:7
Jacob's sons heard and returned from the field. They were grieving and furious. Shechem committed an outrage in Israel by sleeping with Jacob's daughter. This should never have been done. 📖

## Genesis 34:8
Hamor said, “My son Shechem longs for your daughter. Please give her to him as his wife. 📖

## Genesis 34:9
Marry into our families. Give us your daughters and marry ours. 📖

## Genesis 34:10
Settle among us. The land is open to you. Live here, move freely, and acquire property.” 📖

## Genesis 34:11
Shechem told Dinah's father and brothers, “Grant me this favor. I will give whatever you ask. 📖

## Genesis 34:12
Ask a high dowry and costly gift. I will pay whatever you ask. Just give me the girl as my wife!” 📖

## Genesis 34:13
Jacob's sons answered Shechem and Hamor deceitfully because Shechem defiled their sister Dinah. 📖

## Genesis 34:14
They said, “We cannot give our sister to an uncircumcised man. That would disgrace us. 📖

## Genesis 34:15
We agree on one condition. Every male among you must be circumcised like us. 📖

## Genesis 34:16
Then we will give you our daughters and marry yours. We will live among you as one people. 📖

## Genesis 34:17
If you refuse circumcision, we will take our sister and leave.” 📖

## Genesis 34:18
Hamor and Shechem liked the offer. 📖

## Genesis 34:19
Shechem was the most respected in his father's household. He did not delay because Jacob's daughter delighted him. 📖

## Genesis 34:20
Hamor and Shechem went to their city gate. They spoke to the city's men. 📖

## Genesis 34:21
“These men are peaceful toward us. Let them live and trade here. The land is large enough. Let us marry their daughters and give them ours. 📖

## Genesis 34:22
They will live with us as one people only if all our males are circumcised like them. 📖

## Genesis 34:23
Will their livestock, possessions, and all their animals not become ours? Agree with them so they live among us.” 📖

## Genesis 34:24
All the men going through the city gate listened to Hamor and Shechem. Every male in the city was circumcised. 📖

## Genesis 34:25
Three days later, the men were still in pain. Dinah's brothers Simeon and Levi, two of Jacob's sons, took swords. They entered the unsuspecting city and killed every male. 📖

## Genesis 34:26
They killed Hamor and Shechem with their swords. They took Dinah from Shechem's house and left. 📖

## Genesis 34:27
Jacob's other sons came upon the slaughter. They looted the city because their sister was defiled. 📖

## Genesis 34:28
They took flocks, herds, donkeys, and everything else in the city and field. 📖

## Genesis 34:29
They captured all the women and children. They carried off all the possessions and plundered everything in the houses. 📖

## Genesis 34:30
Jacob told Simeon and Levi, “You made me a stench to the Canaanites and Perizzites in this land. You brought trouble on me. We are few. If they unite and attack, my household and I will be destroyed.” 📖

## Genesis 34:31
They replied, “Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?” 📖
