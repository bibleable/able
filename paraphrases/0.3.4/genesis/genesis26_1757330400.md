## Genesis 26:1
Another famine struck after the one in Abraham's time. Isaac went to Abimelech, the Philistines' king, at Gerar. 📖

## Genesis 26:2
The LORD appeared to Isaac. He said, “Do not go to Egypt. Settle where I tell you. 📖

## Genesis 26:3
Stay here as a foreigner. I will be with you and bless you. I will give all these lands to you and your offspring. I will confirm My oath to your father Abraham. 📖

## Genesis 26:4
I will make your descendants as many as the sky's stars. I will give them all these lands. All the earth's nations will be blessed through your offspring. 📖

## Genesis 26:5
This is because Abraham listened to My voice. He kept My charge, commandments, statutes, and laws.” 📖

## Genesis 26:6
Isaac settled in Gerar. 📖

## Genesis 26:7
The local men asked about his wife. He said, “She is my sister.” He feared calling her his wife. He thought, “They will kill me because Rebekah is beautiful.” 📖

## Genesis 26:8
After Isaac lived there a long time, Abimelech, the Philistines' king, looked down from a window. He was surprised to see Isaac caressing his wife Rebekah. 📖

## Genesis 26:9
Abimelech called Isaac. “She really is your wife! Why did you call her your sister?” Isaac replied, “I thought I might die because of her.” 📖

## Genesis 26:10
Abimelech asked, “What have you done to us? Someone could easily have slept with your wife. You would have brought guilt on us.” 📖

## Genesis 26:11
Abimelech warned everyone, “Anyone harming this man or his wife will certainly be put to death.” 📖

## Genesis 26:12
Isaac planted seed in the land. That year, he harvested a hundred times what he planted. The LORD blessed him. 📖

## Genesis 26:13
He grew richer and richer until he was extremely wealthy. 📖

## Genesis 26:14
He had so many flocks, herds, and servants that the Philistines envied him. 📖

## Genesis 26:15
The Philistines filled all the wells with dirt. His father's servants had dug them during Abraham's lifetime. 📖

## Genesis 26:16
Abimelech told Isaac, “Leave us. You are far too powerful for us.” 📖

## Genesis 26:17
Isaac left and camped in the Valley of Gerar. He settled there. 📖

## Genesis 26:18
Isaac reopened the wells dug during Abraham's lifetime. The Philistines had blocked them after Abraham died. Isaac used the names his father gave them. 📖

## Genesis 26:19
Isaac's servants dug in the valley. They found a well of fresh water. 📖

## Genesis 26:20
Gerar's herdsmen quarreled with Isaac's herdsmen. They said, “The water is ours!” He named the well Esek because they disputed with him. 📖

## Genesis 26:21
They dug another well and quarreled over it too. He named it Sitnah. 📖

## Genesis 26:22
He moved and dug another well. No one quarreled over it. He named it Rehoboth. He said, “The LORD has finally made room for us. We will be fruitful in the land.” 📖

## Genesis 26:23
Isaac went from there to Beersheba. 📖

## Genesis 26:24
That night, the LORD appeared to him. “I am your father Abraham's God. Do not fear, because I am with you. I will bless you and multiply your descendants for My servant Abraham's sake.” 📖

## Genesis 26:25
Isaac built an altar and called on the LORD's name there. He pitched his tent. His servants dug a well there. 📖

## Genesis 26:26
Abimelech came from Gerar with his adviser Ahuzzath and army commander Phicol. 📖

## Genesis 26:27
Isaac asked, “Why did you come? You hated me and sent me away.” 📖

## Genesis 26:28
They replied, “We plainly see that the LORD has been with you. Let there be an oath between us. Let us make a covenant with you. 📖

## Genesis 26:29
Do not harm us, as we did not harm you. We did only good to you and sent you away peacefully. Now the LORD has blessed you.” 📖

## Genesis 26:30
Isaac prepared a feast. They ate and drank. 📖

## Genesis 26:31
Early the next morning, they swore an oath to each other. Isaac sent them away. They left peacefully. 📖

## Genesis 26:32
That day, his servants told him about the well they dug. They said, “We found water!” 📖

## Genesis 26:33
He named it Shibah. The city is still called Beersheba today. 📖

## Genesis 26:34
At forty, Esau married Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite. He also married Basemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite. 📖

## Genesis 26:35
They brought grief to Isaac and Rebekah. 📖
