## Genesis 4:1
Adam had relations with his wife Eve. She conceived and gave birth to Cain. She said, “With the LORD's help, I have brought forth a man.” 📖

## Genesis 4:2
Later, she gave birth to Cain's brother Abel. Abel tended sheep. Cain cultivated the ground. 📖

## Genesis 4:3
After some time, Cain brought an offering to the LORD from the ground's produce. 📖

## Genesis 4:4
Abel brought the best portions from his flock's firstborn. The LORD was pleased with Abel and his offering. 📖

## Genesis 4:5
But He had no regard for Cain and his offering. Cain became very angry. His face fell. 📖

## Genesis 4:6
The LORD asked Cain, “Why are you angry? Why has your face fallen? 📖

## Genesis 4:7
If you do right, will you not be accepted? If you refuse to do right, sin crouches at your door. It desires you. But you must master it.” 📖

## Genesis 4:8
Cain told his brother Abel, “Let us go to the field.” While they were there, Cain attacked his brother and killed him. 📖

## Genesis 4:9
The LORD asked Cain, “Where is your brother Abel?” Cain answered, “I do not know! Am I my brother's keeper?” 📖

## Genesis 4:10
The LORD replied, “What have you done? Your brother's blood cries to Me from the ground. 📖

## Genesis 4:11
You are now cursed and banished from the ground. It opened its mouth to receive your brother's blood from your hand. 📖

## Genesis 4:12
When you cultivate the ground, it will no longer produce crops for you. You will be a fugitive and wanderer on earth.” 📖

## Genesis 4:13
Cain told the LORD, “My punishment is more than I can bear. 📖

## Genesis 4:14
Today You have driven me from the earth's surface. I will be hidden from Your face. I will be a fugitive and wanderer on earth. Whoever finds me will kill me.” 📖

## Genesis 4:15
The LORD replied, “No! If anyone kills Cain, Cain will be avenged seven times.” He marked Cain so no one finding him would kill him. 📖

## Genesis 4:16
Cain left the LORD's presence. He settled in the land of Nod, east of Eden. 📖

## Genesis 4:17
Cain had relations with his wife. She conceived and gave birth to Enoch. Cain built a city and named it after his son Enoch. 📖

## Genesis 4:18
Enoch became Irad's father. Irad became Mehujael's father. Mehujael became Methusael's father. Methusael became Lamech's father. 📖

## Genesis 4:19
Lamech married two women. Their names were Adah and Zillah. 📖

## Genesis 4:20
Adah gave birth to Jabal. He was the father of those who live in tents and raise livestock. 📖

## Genesis 4:21
His brother was named Jubal. He was the father of everyone who plays the harp and flute. 📖

## Genesis 4:22
Zillah gave birth to Tubal-cain. He forged every kind of bronze and iron tool. His sister was Naamah. 📖

## Genesis 4:23
Lamech told his wives, “Adah and Zillah, hear my voice. Wives of Lamech, listen to my words. I killed a man for wounding me, a young man for striking me. 📖

## Genesis 4:24
If Cain is avenged seven times, Lamech will be avenged seventy-seven times.” 📖

## Genesis 4:25
Adam again had relations with his wife. She bore a son and named him Seth. She said, “God gave me another offspring in Abel's place, because Cain killed him.” 📖

## Genesis 4:26
Seth also had a son. He named him Enosh. At that time, people began calling on the LORD's name. 📖
