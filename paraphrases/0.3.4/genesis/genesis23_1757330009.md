## Genesis 23:1
Sarah lived 127 years. 📖

## Genesis 23:2
She died at Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron, in Canaan's land. Abraham went to mourn and weep for her. 📖

## Genesis 23:3
He rose from beside his dead wife. He spoke to the Hittites. 📖

## Genesis 23:4
“I am a foreigner and outsider among you. Give me a burial site so I can bury my dead.” 📖

## Genesis 23:5
The Hittites answered Abraham. 📖

## Genesis 23:6
“Listen, sir. You are God's chosen one among us. Bury your dead in our finest tomb. None of us will refuse you his tomb for burial.” 📖

## Genesis 23:7
Abraham rose and bowed before the Hittites, the land's people. 📖

## Genesis 23:8
He said, “If you are willing for me to bury my dead, listen. Speak to Ephron son of Zohar for me. 📖

## Genesis 23:9
Ask him to sell me Machpelah's cave at the end of his field. Let him sell it at full price before you. I will have a burial site.” 📖

## Genesis 23:10
Ephron sat among Heth's sons. Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham before all the Hittites gathered at his city gate. 📖

## Genesis 23:11
“No, my lord, listen. I give you the field and the cave in it. I give them before my people. Bury your dead.” 📖

## Genesis 23:12
Abraham bowed again before the land's people. 📖

## Genesis 23:13
He told Ephron before them, “Please listen. I will pay the field's price. Accept it so I can bury my dead there.” 📖

## Genesis 23:14
Ephron answered Abraham. 📖

## Genesis 23:15
“Listen, my lord. The land is worth four hundred silver shekels. What is that between us? Bury your dead.” 📖

## Genesis 23:16
Abraham accepted Ephron's terms. He weighed four hundred silver shekels by the merchants' standard. Ephron had named this price before the Hittites. 📖

## Genesis 23:17
Ephron's field at Machpelah near Mamre was deeded over, with its cave and every tree within its boundaries. 📖

## Genesis 23:18
They became Abraham's possession before all the Hittites gathered at Ephron's city gate. 📖

## Genesis 23:19
Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the field's cave at Machpelah near Mamre, also called Hebron, in Canaan's land. 📖

## Genesis 23:20
The Hittites deeded the field and cave to Abraham as a burial site. 📖
