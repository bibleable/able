## Genesis 48:1
Some time later, Joseph heard that his father was ill. He went to him with his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim. 📖

## Genesis 48:2
Jacob heard that his son Joseph had come. Israel gathered his strength and sat up in bed. 📖

## Genesis 48:3
Jacob told Joseph, “God Almighty appeared to me at Luz in Canaan. He blessed me there. 📖

## Genesis 48:4
“He said, ‘I will make you fruitful and increase your numbers. I will make you a multitude of peoples. I will give this land to your descendants after you as a possession forever.’ 📖

## Genesis 48:5
“Your two sons born in Egypt before I came to you here will now count as mine. Ephraim and Manasseh will be mine, just as Reuben and Simeon are mine. 📖

## Genesis 48:6
“Any children born to you after them will be yours. They will bear their brothers’ names in the territory they inherit. 📖

## Genesis 48:7
“On my return from Paddan, Rachel died in Canaan, some distance from Ephrath. This brought me sorrow. I buried her beside the road to Ephrath.” Ephrath is Bethlehem. 📖

## Genesis 48:8
Israel saw Joseph’s sons and asked, “Who are these?” 📖

## Genesis 48:9
Joseph answered his father, “They are the sons God has given me here.” Jacob said, “Please bring them to me so that I can bless them.” 📖

## Genesis 48:10
Israel could hardly see because his eyes were weak from old age. Joseph brought his sons to him. His father kissed and embraced them. 📖

## Genesis 48:11
Israel told Joseph, “I never expected to see your face again. Now God has let me see your children too.” 📖

## Genesis 48:12
Joseph moved his sons away from his father’s knees. He bowed with his face to the ground. 📖

## Genesis 48:13
Joseph took Ephraim in his right hand, toward Israel’s left hand. He took Manasseh in his left hand, toward Israel’s right hand. He brought both sons close to his father. 📖

## Genesis 48:14
Israel put his right hand on the head of Ephraim, the younger son. He crossed his hands and put his left hand on Manasseh’s head, although Manasseh was the firstborn. 📖

## Genesis 48:15
He blessed Joseph and said, “My fathers Abraham and Isaac walked before God. God has been my shepherd throughout my life until this day. 📖

## Genesis 48:16
“The angel has saved me from all harm. May He bless these boys. May they bear my name and the names of my fathers Abraham and Isaac. May they become a multitude on the earth.” 📖

## Genesis 48:17
Joseph saw his father’s right hand on Ephraim’s head and was displeased. He took his father’s hand to move it from Ephraim’s head to Manasseh’s. 📖

## Genesis 48:18
Joseph said, “Not that way, my father! This one is the firstborn. Put your right hand on his head.” 📖

## Genesis 48:19
His father refused and said, “I know, my son. He too will become a people and will be great. But his younger brother will be greater than he is. His younger brother’s descendants will become a multitude of nations.” 📖

## Genesis 48:20
That day Jacob blessed them. He said, “Israel will use your names in this blessing: ‘May God make you like Ephraim and Manasseh.’” Thus he put Ephraim before Manasseh. 📖

## Genesis 48:21
Israel told Joseph, “I am about to die. But God will be with you. He will bring you back to your fathers’ land. 📖

## Genesis 48:22
“I give you the ridge of land I took from the Amorites with my sword and bow. I give this to you as one above your brothers.” 📖
