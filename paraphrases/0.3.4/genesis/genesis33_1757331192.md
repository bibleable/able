## Genesis 33:1
Jacob saw Esau coming with four hundred men. He divided the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two female servants. 📖

## Genesis 33:2
He put the servants and their children first. Leah and her children came next. Rachel and Joseph were last. 📖

## Genesis 33:3
Jacob went ahead. He bowed to the ground seven times as he approached his brother. 📖

## Genesis 33:4
Esau ran to embrace him. He threw his arms around Jacob's neck and kissed him. They both wept. 📖

## Genesis 33:5
Esau saw the women and children. He asked, “Who are these?” Jacob answered, “The children God graciously gave your servant.” 📖

## Genesis 33:6
The female servants and their children approached and bowed. 📖

## Genesis 33:7
Leah and her children approached and bowed. Joseph and Rachel followed and bowed. 📖

## Genesis 33:8
Esau asked, “Why did you send this whole company to meet me?” Jacob replied, “To find favor with you, my lord.” 📖

## Genesis 33:9
Esau said, “I have plenty, my brother. Keep what is yours.” 📖

## Genesis 33:10
Jacob insisted, “Please accept my gift if you favor me. Seeing your face is like seeing God's face. You received me favorably. 📖

## Genesis 33:11
Accept the gift brought to you. God has been gracious, and I have everything I need.” Jacob urged him until he accepted. 📖

## Genesis 33:12
Esau said, “Let us travel. I will go ahead.” 📖

## Genesis 33:13
Jacob replied, “My lord knows the children are frail. The sheep and cattle nurse their young. If driven hard for one day, all the animals will die. 📖

## Genesis 33:14
Go ahead of your servant. I will move slowly at a comfortable pace for the livestock and children until I reach you in Seir.” 📖

## Genesis 33:15
Esau offered, “Let me leave some people with you.” Jacob replied, “Why do that? Let me find favor with my lord.” 📖

## Genesis 33:16
Esau returned toward Seir that day. 📖

## Genesis 33:17
Jacob went to Succoth. He built a house and shelters for his livestock. That is why the place was called Succoth. 📖

## Genesis 33:18
Jacob arrived safely at Shechem in Canaan after coming from Paddan-aram. He camped outside the city. 📖

## Genesis 33:19
He bought his campsite from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for a hundred silver pieces. 📖

## Genesis 33:20
He built an altar there and named it El-Elohe-Israel. 📖
