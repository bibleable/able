## Genesis 17:1
When Abram was ninety-nine, the LORD appeared to him. He said, “I am God Almighty. Walk before Me and be blameless. 📖

## Genesis 17:2
I will establish My covenant between us. I will multiply you greatly.” 📖

## Genesis 17:3
Abram fell facedown. God spoke to him. 📖

## Genesis 17:4
“This is My covenant with you. You will father many nations. 📖

## Genesis 17:5
Your name will no longer be Abram. It will be Abraham because I have made you a father of many nations. 📖

## Genesis 17:6
I will make you extremely fruitful. I will make nations from you. Kings will descend from you. 📖

## Genesis 17:7
I will establish My everlasting covenant between Me, you, and your descendants after you. I will be your God and your descendants' God. 📖

## Genesis 17:8
I will give you and your descendants all Canaan's land, where you live now. It will be your eternal possession. I will be their God.” 📖

## Genesis 17:9
God told Abraham, “You and your descendants in future generations must keep My covenant. 📖

## Genesis 17:10
This is My covenant for you and your descendants to keep. Every male among you must be circumcised. 📖

## Genesis 17:11
Circumcise the flesh of your foreskin. This will be the covenant's sign between Me and you. 📖

## Genesis 17:12
In every generation, circumcise every male at eight days old. This includes those born in your household and those bought from foreigners. It includes those who are not your offspring. 📖

## Genesis 17:13
Those born in your household or bought must be circumcised. My covenant in your flesh will be everlasting. 📖

## Genesis 17:14
Any male who is not circumcised will be cut off from his people. He has broken My covenant.” 📖

## Genesis 17:15
God told Abraham, “Do not call your wife Sarai anymore. Her name will be Sarah. 📖

## Genesis 17:16
I will bless her and give you a son through her. I will bless her, and she will be a mother of nations. Kings of peoples will descend from her.” 📖

## Genesis 17:17
Abraham fell facedown and laughed. He thought, “Can a hundred-year-old man have a child? Can Sarah give birth at ninety?” 📖

## Genesis 17:18
Abraham told God, “If only Ishmael could live under Your blessing!” 📖

## Genesis 17:19
God replied, “Your wife Sarah will bear you a son. Name him Isaac. I will establish My covenant with him as an everlasting covenant for his descendants. 📖

## Genesis 17:20
I have heard you about Ishmael. I will bless him, make him fruitful, and multiply him greatly. He will father twelve rulers. I will make him a great nation. 📖

## Genesis 17:21
But I will establish My covenant with Isaac. Sarah will bear him to you at this time next year.” 📖

## Genesis 17:22
God finished speaking with Abraham. He went up from him. 📖

## Genesis 17:23
That very day, Abraham circumcised his son Ishmael and every male in his household. This included all those born there and those bought with his money. He did as God told him. 📖

## Genesis 17:24
Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he was circumcised. 📖

## Genesis 17:25
His son Ishmael was thirteen. 📖

## Genesis 17:26
Abraham and Ishmael were circumcised on the same day. 📖

## Genesis 17:27
All Abraham's household men were circumcised with him. This included servants born in his household and those bought from foreigners. 📖
