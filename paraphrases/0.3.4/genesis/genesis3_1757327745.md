## Genesis 3:1
The serpent was craftier than every field animal the LORD God made. He asked the woman, “Did God really forbid you from eating from any garden tree?” 📖

## Genesis 3:2
The woman told the serpent, “We may eat fruit from the garden's trees. 📖

## Genesis 3:3
But God spoke about the fruit of the tree in the garden's middle. He said, ‘Do not eat it or touch it, or you will die.’” 📖

## Genesis 3:4
The serpent told her, “You will certainly not die. 📖

## Genesis 3:5
God knows your eyes will open when you eat it. You will be like God, knowing good and evil.” 📖

## Genesis 3:6
The woman saw that the tree was good for food and pleasing to the eye. She wanted it to gain wisdom. She took fruit and ate it. She gave some to her husband, who was with her. He ate it too. 📖

## Genesis 3:7
Both their eyes opened. They knew they were naked. They sewed fig leaves together to make coverings. 📖

## Genesis 3:8
They heard the LORD God's voice as He walked in the garden in the day's breeze. They hid from His presence among the garden's trees. 📖

## Genesis 3:9
The LORD God called the man, “Where are you?” 📖

## Genesis 3:10
He replied, “I heard Your voice in the garden. I was afraid because I was naked. I hid.” 📖

## Genesis 3:11
The LORD God asked, “Who told you that you were naked? Did you eat from the tree I forbade you to eat from?” 📖

## Genesis 3:12
The man answered, “The woman You gave me gave me fruit from the tree. I ate it.” 📖

## Genesis 3:13
The LORD God asked the woman, “What have you done?” She replied, “The serpent deceived me. I ate.” 📖

## Genesis 3:14
The LORD God told the serpent, “You did this. Therefore, you are cursed above all livestock and field animals. You will move on your belly and eat dust all your life. 📖

## Genesis 3:15
I will put hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring and hers. He will crush your head. You will strike his heel.” 📖

## Genesis 3:16
He told the woman, “I will greatly increase your childbirth pain. You will give birth in pain. Your desire will be for your husband. He will rule over you.” 📖

## Genesis 3:17
He told Adam, “You listened to your wife and ate from the tree I forbade. Therefore, the ground is cursed because of you. You will toil to eat from it all your life. 📖

## Genesis 3:18
It will grow thorns and thistles for you. You will eat the field's plants. 📖

## Genesis 3:19
You will eat bread by the sweat of your brow until you return to the ground. You were taken from it. You are dust. You will return to dust.” 📖

## Genesis 3:20
Adam named his wife Eve because she would be the mother of all the living. 📖

## Genesis 3:21
The LORD God made skin garments for Adam and his wife. He clothed them. 📖

## Genesis 3:22
The LORD God said, “The man has become like one of Us, knowing good and evil. Now, to keep him from reaching out, taking from the tree of life, eating, and living forever...” 📖

## Genesis 3:23
The LORD God banished him from Eden's garden to work the ground he was taken from. 📖

## Genesis 3:24
God drove the man out. He placed cherubim and a whirling sword of flame on the east side of the Garden of Eden. They guarded the way to the tree of life. 📖
