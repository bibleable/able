## Genesis 41:1
Two full years later, Pharaoh dreamed he stood beside the Nile. 📖

## Genesis 41:2
Seven sleek, well-fed cows came from the river and grazed among the reeds. 📖

## Genesis 41:3
Seven sickly, thin cows followed from the Nile. They stood beside the fat cows on the riverbank. 📖

## Genesis 41:4
The sickly, thin cows devoured the seven sleek, well-fed cows. Pharaoh woke. 📖

## Genesis 41:5
He slept again and dreamed a second time. Seven plump, ripe grain heads grew on one stalk. 📖

## Genesis 41:6
Seven thin grain heads followed, scorched by the east wind. 📖

## Genesis 41:7
The thin heads swallowed the seven plump, ripe heads. Pharaoh woke and realized he dreamed. 📖

## Genesis 41:8
He was troubled that morning. He called all Egypt's magicians and wise men. He told his dreams, but none could interpret them. 📖

## Genesis 41:9
The chief cupbearer told Pharaoh, “Today I remember my failures. 📖

## Genesis 41:10
You were angry with your servants. You imprisoned me and the chief baker with the captain of the guard. 📖

## Genesis 41:11
We dreamed on the same night. Each dream had its meaning. 📖

## Genesis 41:12
A young Hebrew, the captain's servant, was with us. He interpreted our dreams individually. 📖

## Genesis 41:13
Everything happened as he said. I was restored. The other man was hanged.” 📖

## Genesis 41:14
Pharaoh called Joseph. They quickly brought him from the dungeon. He shaved, changed clothes, and came before Pharaoh. 📖

## Genesis 41:15
Pharaoh said, “No one can interpret my dream. I heard you can interpret dreams you hear.” 📖

## Genesis 41:16
Joseph replied, “I cannot do it myself. God will give Pharaoh a sound answer.” 📖

## Genesis 41:17
Pharaoh said, “I dreamed I stood on the Nile's bank. 📖

## Genesis 41:18
Seven well-fed, sleek cows came from the river and grazed among reeds. 📖

## Genesis 41:19
Seven sickly, ugly, thin cows followed. I never saw such ugly cows anywhere in Egypt! 📖

## Genesis 41:20
The thin, ugly cows devoured the first seven fat cows. 📖

## Genesis 41:21
No one could tell they ate them. They looked just as ugly as before. I woke. 📖

## Genesis 41:22
I also dreamed of seven plump, ripe grain heads on one stalk. 📖

## Genesis 41:23
Seven withered, thin grain heads followed, scorched by the east wind. 📖

## Genesis 41:24
The thin heads swallowed the seven plump ones. I told the magicians, but none could explain.” 📖

## Genesis 41:25
Joseph said, “Pharaoh's dreams have one meaning. God showed Pharaoh what He will do. 📖

## Genesis 41:26
The seven good cows mean seven years. The seven ripe grain heads mean seven years. Both dreams mean the same thing. 📖

## Genesis 41:27
The seven thin, ugly cows mean seven years. So do the seven worthless grain heads scorched by the east wind. They mean seven famine years. 📖

## Genesis 41:28
As I said, God showed Pharaoh what He will do. 📖

## Genesis 41:29
Seven years of great abundance will come throughout Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 41:30
Seven famine years will follow. Egypt's abundance will be forgotten. Famine will ruin the land. 📖

## Genesis 41:31
The following famine will be so severe that no one will remember the abundance. 📖

## Genesis 41:32
The dream came in two versions because God decreed it. He will do it soon. 📖

## Genesis 41:33
Pharaoh should appoint a discerning, wise man over Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 41:34
Appoint commissioners to collect a fifth of Egypt's harvest during the seven abundant years. 📖

## Genesis 41:35
Under Pharaoh's authority, collect all the excess food during the good years. Store grain as food in the cities. 📖

## Genesis 41:36
This reserve will feed the land during Egypt's seven famine years. The country will not perish.” 📖

## Genesis 41:37
Pharaoh and all his officials liked the proposal. 📖

## Genesis 41:38
Pharaoh asked, “Can we find anyone like him, with God's Spirit living in him?” 📖

## Genesis 41:39
He told Joseph, “God made all this known to you. No one is as discerning and wise. 📖

## Genesis 41:40
You will manage my house. All my people must obey you. Only the throne will make me greater than you.” 📖

## Genesis 41:41
Pharaoh said, “I appoint you over all Egypt.” 📖

## Genesis 41:42
He put his own signet ring on Joseph's finger. He dressed him in fine linen and put a gold chain around his neck. 📖

## Genesis 41:43
He had Joseph ride his second chariot. Men called ahead, “Bow the knee!” He placed Joseph over all Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 41:44
Pharaoh declared, “I am Pharaoh. But no one in Egypt may lift a hand or foot without your permission.” 📖

## Genesis 41:45
He named Joseph Zaphenath-paneah. He gave him Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, On's priest, as his wife. Joseph took charge of all Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 41:46
Joseph was thirty when he began serving Pharaoh, king of Egypt. He left Pharaoh and traveled throughout Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 41:47
The land produced plentifully during the seven abundant years. 📖

## Genesis 41:48
Joseph collected all Egypt's excess food during those seven years. In each city, he stored food from its surrounding fields. 📖

## Genesis 41:49
He stored grain like the sea's sand. He stopped counting because it was beyond measure. 📖

## Genesis 41:50
Before the famine, Asenath bore Joseph two sons. Her father was Potiphera, the priest of On. 📖

## Genesis 41:51
Joseph named his firstborn Manasseh. “God made me forget all my hardship and all my father's household.” 📖

## Genesis 41:52
He named the second Ephraim. “God made me fruitful in the land of my suffering.” 📖

## Genesis 41:53
Egypt's seven years of abundance ended. 📖

## Genesis 41:54
The seven famine years began as Joseph said. Every country had famine, but all Egypt had food. 📖

## Genesis 41:55
Egypt grew extremely hungry. People asked Pharaoh for food. He told all the Egyptians, “Go to Joseph. Do everything he tells you.” 📖

## Genesis 41:56
Famine spread over the whole land. Joseph opened all the storehouses and sold grain to the Egyptians. Egypt's famine was severe. 📖

## Genesis 41:57
Every nation came to buy grain from Joseph in Egypt. Famine was severe throughout the earth. 📖
