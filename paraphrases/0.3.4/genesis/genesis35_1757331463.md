## Genesis 35:1
God told Jacob, “Go to Bethel and settle there. Build an altar to the God who appeared when you fled Esau.” 📖

## Genesis 35:2
Jacob told his household and everyone with him, “Remove the foreign gods among you. Purify yourselves and change your clothes. 📖

## Genesis 35:3
Let us go to Bethel. I will build an altar to God there. He answered me in distress and stayed with me wherever I went.” 📖

## Genesis 35:4
They gave Jacob all their foreign gods and earrings. He buried them beneath the oak near Shechem. 📖

## Genesis 35:5
As they traveled, God sent terror on the surrounding cities. They did not pursue Jacob's sons. 📖

## Genesis 35:6
Jacob and everyone with him reached Luz, also called Bethel, in Canaan's land. 📖

## Genesis 35:7
He built an altar and named the place El-bethel. God revealed Himself there when Jacob fled his brother. 📖

## Genesis 35:8
Rebekah's nurse Deborah died. She was buried under the oak below Bethel. Jacob named it Allon-bacuth. 📖

## Genesis 35:9
After Jacob returned from Paddan-aram, God appeared again and blessed him. 📖

## Genesis 35:10
God said, “Your name is Jacob. You will no longer be called Jacob. Your name will be Israel.” God named him Israel. 📖

## Genesis 35:11
God said, “I am God Almighty. Be fruitful and multiply. A nation, even a company of nations, will come from you. Kings will descend from you. 📖

## Genesis 35:12
I will give you the land I gave Abraham and Isaac. I will give it to your descendants after you.” 📖

## Genesis 35:13
God went up from the place where He spoke with Jacob. 📖

## Genesis 35:14
Jacob set up a stone pillar where God spoke with him. He poured a drink offering on it and anointed it with oil. 📖

## Genesis 35:15
He named the place where God spoke with him Bethel. 📖

## Genesis 35:16
They left Bethel. Some distance from Ephrath, Rachel went into difficult labor. 📖

## Genesis 35:17
During her severe labor, the midwife said, “Do not fear. You are having another son.” 📖

## Genesis 35:18
With her last breath, as she died, Rachel named him Ben-oni. His father named him Benjamin. 📖

## Genesis 35:19
Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, also called Bethlehem. 📖

## Genesis 35:20
Jacob put a pillar on her grave. It still marks Rachel's tomb. 📖

## Genesis 35:21
Israel traveled again and pitched his tent beyond the Tower of Eder. 📖

## Genesis 35:22
While Israel lived there, Reuben slept with his father's concubine Bilhah. Israel heard about it. Jacob had twelve sons. 📖

## Genesis 35:23
Leah's sons were Jacob's firstborn Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. 📖

## Genesis 35:24
Rachel's sons were Joseph and Benjamin. 📖

## Genesis 35:25
Rachel's female servant Bilhah's sons were Dan and Naphtali. 📖

## Genesis 35:26
Leah's female servant Zilpah's sons were Gad and Asher. These are Jacob's sons born to him in Paddan-aram. 📖

## Genesis 35:27
Jacob returned to Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron. Abraham and Isaac had stayed there. 📖

## Genesis 35:28
Isaac lived 180 years. 📖

## Genesis 35:29
He breathed his last and died, old and full of years. He was gathered to his people. His sons Esau and Jacob buried him. 📖
