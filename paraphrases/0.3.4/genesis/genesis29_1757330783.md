## Genesis 29:1
Jacob continued to the eastern people's land. 📖

## Genesis 29:2
He saw a well in a field. Three sheep flocks lay nearby because they drank from it. A large stone covered the well's mouth. 📖

## Genesis 29:3
When all the flocks gathered, the shepherds rolled away the stone and watered the sheep. They then put the stone back over the well's mouth. 📖

## Genesis 29:4
Jacob asked, “My brothers, where are you from?” The shepherds answered, “Haran.” 📖

## Genesis 29:5
He asked, “Do you know Laban, Nahor's grandson?” They replied, “We know him.” 📖

## Genesis 29:6
Jacob asked, “Is he well?” They answered, “Yes. His daughter Rachel is coming with his sheep.” 📖

## Genesis 29:7
Jacob said, “It is still broad daylight. It is not time to gather livestock. Water the sheep and take them to pasture.” 📖

## Genesis 29:8
They replied, “We cannot until all the flocks gather and the stone is rolled from the well's mouth. Then we will water them.” 📖

## Genesis 29:9
While he spoke, Rachel arrived with her father's sheep. She was a shepherdess. 📖

## Genesis 29:10
Jacob saw Rachel, daughter of his mother's brother Laban, with Laban's sheep. He rolled the stone from the well's mouth. He watered his uncle's sheep. 📖

## Genesis 29:11
Jacob kissed Rachel and wept aloud. 📖

## Genesis 29:12
He told her he was Rebekah's son, her father's relative. She ran to tell her father. 📖

## Genesis 29:13
Laban heard about his sister's son Jacob and ran to meet him. He hugged and kissed him and brought him home. Jacob told him everything that happened. 📖

## Genesis 29:14
Laban said, “You are my own flesh and blood.” Jacob stayed with him a month. 📖

## Genesis 29:15
Laban said, “Should you work without pay because you are my relative? Tell me your wages.” 📖

## Genesis 29:16
Laban had two daughters. Leah was older, and Rachel was younger. 📖

## Genesis 29:17
Leah had weak eyes. Rachel had a shapely body and was beautiful. 📖

## Genesis 29:18
Jacob loved Rachel. He said, “I will work seven years for your younger daughter Rachel.” 📖

## Genesis 29:19
Laban replied, “Better to give her to you than another man. Stay with me.” 📖

## Genesis 29:20
Jacob served seven years for Rachel. His love made them seem like a few days. 📖

## Genesis 29:21
Jacob told Laban, “Give me my wife. My time is complete. I want to sleep with her.” 📖

## Genesis 29:22
Laban invited all the local men and prepared a feast. 📖

## Genesis 29:23
That evening, Laban gave his daughter Leah to Jacob. Jacob slept with her. 📖

## Genesis 29:24
Laban gave his servant girl Zilpah to Leah as her female servant. 📖

## Genesis 29:25
Morning came, and there was Leah! Jacob asked Laban, “What have you done? I served you for Rachel. Why did you deceive me?” 📖

## Genesis 29:26
Laban replied, “Our custom is not to marry the younger daughter before the older. 📖

## Genesis 29:27
Finish this week's celebration. We will give you the younger daughter for another seven years of work.” 📖

## Genesis 29:28
Jacob completed the week's celebration. Laban gave him Rachel as his wife. 📖

## Genesis 29:29
Laban gave his servant girl Bilhah to Rachel as her female servant. 📖

## Genesis 29:30
Jacob slept with Rachel too. He loved Rachel more than Leah. He worked another seven years for Laban. 📖

## Genesis 29:31
The LORD saw that Leah was unloved. He opened her womb. Rachel was barren. 📖

## Genesis 29:32
Leah conceived and bore a son. She named him Reuben. She said, “The LORD saw my suffering. My husband will surely love me now.” 📖

## Genesis 29:33
She conceived again and bore a son. She said, “The LORD heard that I am unloved. He gave me this son too.” She named him Simeon. 📖

## Genesis 29:34
Leah conceived again and bore a son. She said, “Now my husband will be attached to me. I have borne him three sons.” He was named Levi. 📖

## Genesis 29:35
She conceived again and bore a son. She said, “This time I will praise the LORD.” She named him Judah. Then Leah stopped having children. 📖
