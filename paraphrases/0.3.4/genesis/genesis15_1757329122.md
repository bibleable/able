## Genesis 15:1
After this, the LORD's word came to Abram in a vision. “Do not fear, Abram. I am your shield, your very great reward.” 📖

## Genesis 15:2
Abram replied, “Lord GOD, what can You give me? I am childless. Eliezer of Damascus will inherit my household.” 📖

## Genesis 15:3
Abram said, “You gave me no offspring. A servant in my household will inherit from me.” 📖

## Genesis 15:4
The LORD's word came to Abram. “This man will not be your heir. Someone from your own body will inherit from you.” 📖

## Genesis 15:5
The LORD took him outside. He said, “Look at the heavens. Count the stars, if you can. Your offspring will be like this.” 📖

## Genesis 15:6
Abram believed the LORD. This was credited to him as righteousness. 📖

## Genesis 15:7
The LORD told him, “I am the LORD who brought you from Ur of the Chaldeans. I brought you to possess this land.” 📖

## Genesis 15:8
Abram replied, “Lord GOD, how can I know I will possess it?” 📖

## Genesis 15:9
The LORD said, “Bring Me a heifer, goat, and ram, each three years old. Also bring a turtledove and young pigeon.” 📖

## Genesis 15:10
Abram brought them all to Him. He split the animals down the middle and placed the halves opposite each other. He did not split the birds. 📖

## Genesis 15:11
Birds of prey came down on the carcasses. Abram drove them away. 📖

## Genesis 15:12
As the sun set, Abram fell into deep sleep. Great terror and darkness suddenly overwhelmed him. 📖

## Genesis 15:13
The LORD told Abram, “Know for certain that your descendants will be strangers in a land belonging to others. They will be enslaved and mistreated for four hundred years. 📖

## Genesis 15:14
I will judge the nation they serve as slaves. Afterward, they will leave with many possessions. 📖

## Genesis 15:15
You will go to your fathers in peace. You will be buried at a ripe old age. 📖

## Genesis 15:16
Your descendants will return here in the fourth generation. The Amorites' wrongdoing is not yet complete.” 📖

## Genesis 15:17
After sunset, when darkness fell, a smoking firepot and flaming torch appeared. They passed between the carcasses' halves. 📖

## Genesis 15:18
That day, the LORD made a covenant with Abram. He said, “I have given your descendants this land, from Egypt's river to the great River Euphrates. 📖

## Genesis 15:19
It is the land of the Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites, 📖

## Genesis 15:20
Hittites, Perizzites, Rephaites, 📖

## Genesis 15:21
Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.” 📖
