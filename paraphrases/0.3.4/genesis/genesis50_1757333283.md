## Genesis 50:1
Joseph fell upon his father’s face. He wept over him and kissed him. 📖

## Genesis 50:2
Joseph told the physicians who served him to embalm his father Israel. They embalmed him. 📖

## Genesis 50:3
The embalming took the required forty days. The Egyptians mourned for Israel for seventy days. 📖

## Genesis 50:4
After the days of mourning, Joseph spoke to Pharaoh’s court. He said, “If I have found favor with you, please give Pharaoh this message. 📖

## Genesis 50:5
“My father made me swear an oath. He said, ‘I am about to die. Bury me in the tomb I dug for myself in Canaan.’ Please let me go to bury my father. Then I will return.” 📖

## Genesis 50:6
Pharaoh answered, “Go and bury your father, as he made you swear to do.” 📖

## Genesis 50:7
Joseph went to bury his father. All Pharaoh’s servants went with him. These included the elders of Pharaoh’s household and all the elders of Egypt. 📖

## Genesis 50:8
Joseph’s entire household, his brothers, and his father’s household also went. Only their children, flocks, and herds stayed in Goshen. 📖

## Genesis 50:9
Chariots and horsemen went with Joseph. The procession was very large. 📖

## Genesis 50:10
They reached Atad’s threshing floor across the Jordan. They mourned and cried out loudly there. Joseph mourned for his father for seven days. 📖

## Genesis 50:11
The Canaanites saw the mourning at Atad’s threshing floor. They said, “This is a solemn ceremony of mourning by the Egyptians.” That is why the place across the Jordan is called Abel-mizraim. 📖

## Genesis 50:12
Jacob’s sons did as he had instructed them. 📖

## Genesis 50:13
They carried him to Canaan and buried him in the cave in the field of Machpelah near Mamre. Abraham had bought this field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial place. 📖

## Genesis 50:14
After burying his father, Joseph returned to Egypt. His brothers and everyone who had gone with him for the burial also returned. 📖

## Genesis 50:15
Joseph’s brothers saw that their father was dead. They said, “What if Joseph holds a grudge against us? He will certainly repay us for all the evil we did to him.” 📖

## Genesis 50:16
They sent Joseph this message: “Before your father died, he gave this command. 📖

## Genesis 50:17
“He said, ‘Tell Joseph: Please forgive your brothers’ transgression and sin, because they wronged you.’ Now please forgive the transgression of the servants of your father’s God.” Joseph wept when he received their message. 📖

## Genesis 50:18
His brothers also came and bowed before him. They said, “We are your slaves!” 📖

## Genesis 50:19
Joseph answered, “Do not be afraid. Am I in God’s place? 📖

## Genesis 50:20
“You intended evil against me. God intended it for good, to bring about what has happened today. He intended to preserve many people’s lives. 📖

## Genesis 50:21
“Do not be afraid. I will provide for you and your little ones.” Joseph reassured his brothers and spoke kindly to them. 📖

## Genesis 50:22
Joseph and his father’s household stayed in Egypt. Joseph lived to the age of 110. 📖

## Genesis 50:23
Joseph saw Ephraim’s sons to the third generation. Manasseh’s son Machir had sons, and they were brought up on Joseph’s knees. 📖

## Genesis 50:24
Joseph told his brothers, “I am about to die. But God will certainly visit you and bring you out of this land. He will bring you to the land He promised on oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.” 📖

## Genesis 50:25
Joseph made Israel’s sons swear an oath. He said, “God will certainly attend to you. Then you must carry my bones out of this place.” 📖

## Genesis 50:26
Joseph died at the age of 110. They embalmed his body and put it in a coffin in Egypt. 📖
