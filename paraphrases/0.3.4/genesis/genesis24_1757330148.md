## Genesis 24:1
Abraham was very old. The LORD had blessed him in every way. 📖

## Genesis 24:2
He instructed his household's chief servant, who managed all his possessions. “Put your hand under my thigh. 📖

## Genesis 24:3
Swear by the LORD, God of heaven and earth. You must not choose my son's wife from the Canaanites' daughters among whom I live. 📖

## Genesis 24:4
Go to my country and relatives. Find a wife for my son Isaac there.” 📖

## Genesis 24:5
The servant asked, “Suppose the woman will not follow me here. Should I take your son to the land you left?” 📖

## Genesis 24:6
Abraham replied, “Do not take my son back there. 📖

## Genesis 24:7
The LORD, heaven's God, brought me from my father's house and homeland. He swore to give this land to my offspring. He will send His angel before you. You can find my son's wife there. 📖

## Genesis 24:8
If the woman will not follow you, you are released from my oath. But do not take my son back there.” 📖

## Genesis 24:9
The servant put his hand under his master Abraham's thigh. He swore the oath about this matter. 📖

## Genesis 24:10
The servant took ten of his master's camels and many kinds of good things from his master. He left for Nahor's hometown in Aram-naharaim. 📖

## Genesis 24:11
Near evening, he made the camels kneel beside the well outside town. It was the time women came to draw water. 📖

## Genesis 24:12
He prayed, “LORD, God of my master Abraham, give me success today. Show kindness to my master Abraham. 📖

## Genesis 24:13
I stand beside the spring. The townspeople's daughters are coming to draw water. 📖

## Genesis 24:14
I will ask a girl to lower her jar so I can drink. Suppose she replies, ‘Drink, and I will water your camels too.’ Let her be the one You appointed for Your servant Isaac. Then I will know You showed kindness to my master.” 📖

## Genesis 24:15
Before he finished praying, Rebekah came with a jar on her shoulder. She was Bethuel's daughter. Bethuel was the son of Milcah, wife of Abraham's brother Nahor. 📖

## Genesis 24:16
She was very beautiful, a virgin who had never had relations with a man. She went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came up. 📖

## Genesis 24:17
The servant ran to her. He said, “Please give me a little water from your jar.” 📖

## Genesis 24:18
She replied, “Drink, my lord.” She quickly lowered the jar to her hands and gave him a drink. 📖

## Genesis 24:19
Afterward, she said, “I will draw water for your camels until they have enough.” 📖

## Genesis 24:20
She quickly emptied her jar into the trough. She ran to the well and kept drawing water for all his camels. 📖

## Genesis 24:21
The man watched silently. He wanted to know whether the LORD had made his journey successful. 📖

## Genesis 24:22
After the camels drank, he took out a gold ring weighing one beka. He took two gold bracelets for her wrists, weighing ten shekels together. 📖

## Genesis 24:23
He asked, “Whose daughter are you? Is there room in your father's house for us to spend the night?” 📖

## Genesis 24:24
She replied, “I am Bethuel's daughter. He is the son Milcah bore to Nahor.” 📖

## Genesis 24:25
She added, “We have plenty of straw and feed. We also have a place to spend the night.” 📖

## Genesis 24:26
The man bowed and worshiped the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 24:27
He said, “May the LORD, my master Abraham's God, be blessed! He has not withheld kindness and faithfulness from my master. The LORD led me to my master's relatives' house.” 📖

## Genesis 24:28
The girl ran to tell her mother's household what happened. 📖

## Genesis 24:29
Rebekah's brother was Laban. He rushed out to the man beside the spring. 📖

## Genesis 24:30
Laban saw the ring. He saw the bracelets on his sister's wrists. He heard Rebekah describe the man's words. He went and found the man standing beside the camels near the spring. 📖

## Genesis 24:31
Laban said, “Come, you who are blessed by the LORD. Why stand outside? I prepared the house and a place for the camels.” 📖

## Genesis 24:32
The man entered the house. The camels were unloaded and given straw and feed. Water was brought to wash his feet and his companions' feet. 📖

## Genesis 24:33
Food was set before him. He said, “I will not eat until I tell you my message.” Laban replied, “Please speak.” 📖

## Genesis 24:34
He said, “I am Abraham's servant. 📖

## Genesis 24:35
The LORD greatly blessed my master and made him rich. He gave him sheep, cattle, silver, gold, male and female servants, camels, and donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 24:36
My master's wife Sarah bore a son in her old age. My master gave him everything he owns. 📖

## Genesis 24:37
My master made me swear an oath. He said, ‘Do not choose my son's wife from the Canaanites' daughters in whose land I live. 📖

## Genesis 24:38
Go to my father's house and relatives to find my son's wife.’ 📖

## Genesis 24:39
I asked, ‘Suppose the woman will not come with me?’ 📖

## Genesis 24:40
He replied, ‘The LORD, before whom I walked, will send His angel with you. He will make your journey successful. You will find my son's wife from my relatives and father's house. 📖

## Genesis 24:41
When you reach my relatives, you are released from my oath if they refuse to give her to you.’ 📖

## Genesis 24:42
Today I came to the spring and prayed, ‘LORD, God of my master Abraham, please make my journey successful. 📖

## Genesis 24:43
I stand beside this spring. Suppose a maiden comes to draw water, and I ask for a little drink from her jar. 📖

## Genesis 24:44
If she says, “Drink, and I will draw water for your camels too,” may she be the LORD's chosen wife for my master's son.’ 📖

## Genesis 24:45
Before I finished praying in my heart, Rebekah came with her jar on her shoulder. She went down to the spring and drew water. I asked her for a drink. 📖

## Genesis 24:46
She quickly lowered her jar from her shoulder. She said, ‘Drink, and I will water your camels too.’ I drank. She also watered the camels. 📖

## Genesis 24:47
I asked whose daughter she was. She answered, ‘I am Bethuel's daughter. Milcah bore him to Nahor.’ I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her wrists. 📖

## Genesis 24:48
I bowed and worshiped the LORD. I blessed the LORD, my master Abraham's God. He led me on the right road to choose my master's brother's granddaughter for his son. 📖

## Genesis 24:49
Tell me if you will show kindness and faithfulness to my master. If not, tell me, so I can go elsewhere.” 📖

## Genesis 24:50
Laban and Bethuel answered, “This comes from the LORD. We have no choice. 📖

## Genesis 24:51
Rebekah is here. Take her and go. Let her become your master's son's wife, as the LORD decreed.” 📖

## Genesis 24:52
Abraham's servant heard them and bowed to the ground before the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 24:53
He gave Rebekah silver and gold jewels and clothing. He gave precious gifts to her brother and mother. 📖

## Genesis 24:54
He and his men ate, drank, and stayed the night. The next morning, he said, “Send me to my master.” 📖

## Genesis 24:55
Her brother and mother replied, “Let her stay about ten days. Then she may go.” 📖

## Genesis 24:56
He said, “Do not delay me. The LORD made my journey successful. Send me to my master.” 📖

## Genesis 24:57
They said, “We will call the girl and ask her opinion.” 📖

## Genesis 24:58
They asked Rebekah, “Will you go with this man?” She replied, “I will go.” 📖

## Genesis 24:59
They sent their sister Rebekah with her nurse, Abraham's servant, and his men. 📖

## Genesis 24:60
They blessed Rebekah. “Our sister, may you become the mother of thousands upon thousands. May your offspring possess their enemies' gates.” 📖

## Genesis 24:61
Rebekah and her servant girls rose, mounted the camels, and followed the man. The servant took Rebekah and left. 📖

## Genesis 24:62
Isaac had just returned from Beer-lahai-roi. He lived in the Negev. 📖

## Genesis 24:63
Early in the evening, Isaac went to the field to meditate. He looked up and saw camels approaching. 📖

## Genesis 24:64
Rebekah looked up and saw Isaac. She got down from her camel. 📖

## Genesis 24:65
She asked the servant, “Who is that man in the field coming to meet us?” He replied, “My master.” She took her veil and covered herself. 📖

## Genesis 24:66
The servant told Isaac everything he had done. 📖

## Genesis 24:67
Isaac brought Rebekah into his mother Sarah's tent and married her. He loved her. He was comforted after his mother's death. 📖
