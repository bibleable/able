## Genesis 43:1
The land's famine remained severe. 📖

## Genesis 43:2
They ate all the grain from Egypt. Jacob said, “Return and buy a little more food.” 📖

## Genesis 43:3
Judah replied, “The man strictly warned that we could not see him without our brother. 📖

## Genesis 43:4
If you send him, we will buy food. 📖

## Genesis 43:5
If you refuse, we will not go. The man forbade us to return without our brother.” 📖

## Genesis 43:6
Israel asked, “Why trouble me by telling him you had another brother?” 📖

## Genesis 43:7
They replied, “He asked about our family. He asked whether our father lived and whether we had another brother. We answered. How could we know he would demand our brother?” 📖

## Genesis 43:8
Judah told Israel, “Send the boy with me. We will leave now so we, you, and our children live rather than die. 📖

## Genesis 43:9
I guarantee his safety. Hold me responsible. If I do not return him to you, let me bear the guilt before you all my life. 📖

## Genesis 43:10
Without this delay, we could have returned twice already.” 📖

## Genesis 43:11
Israel said, “If this must happen, take the land's best products as gifts. Take a little balm, honey, spices, myrrh, pistachios, and almonds. 📖

## Genesis 43:12
Take twice the silver. Return the silver put in your sacks' mouths. Perhaps it was a mistake. 📖

## Genesis 43:13
Take your brother. Return to the man now. 📖

## Genesis 43:14
May God Almighty make him merciful so he releases your other brother and Benjamin. If I lose my children, I lose them.” 📖

## Genesis 43:15
They took the gifts, double silver, and Benjamin. They hurried to Egypt and stood before Joseph. 📖

## Genesis 43:16
Joseph saw Benjamin and told his steward, “Bring these men home. Kill an animal and prepare it. They will eat with me at noon.” 📖

## Genesis 43:17
The steward obeyed and brought them to Joseph's house. 📖

## Genesis 43:18
They feared being taken there. They said, “This is because our silver was returned before. They will overpower us and take us as slaves with our donkeys.” 📖

## Genesis 43:19
They approached the steward at the house entrance. 📖

## Genesis 43:20
They said, “Sir, we first came to buy food. 📖

## Genesis 43:21
At our overnight lodging, we opened the sacks. Each found his full silver amount at the sack's mouth. We brought it back. 📖

## Genesis 43:22
We brought more silver to buy food. We do not know who returned our silver.” 📖

## Genesis 43:23
He said, “Do not fear. Your God, your father's God, gave you treasure in your sacks. I received your silver.” He brought Simeon to them. 📖

## Genesis 43:24
The steward brought them inside. He gave water to wash their feet and feed for their donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 43:25
They heard they would eat there. They prepared their gifts for Joseph's return at noon. 📖

## Genesis 43:26
Joseph came home. They gave their gifts and bowed to the ground. 📖

## Genesis 43:27
He asked about their welfare. “Is your elderly father well? Is he still alive?” 📖

## Genesis 43:28
They answered, “Our father, your servant, is well and alive.” They bowed to honor him. 📖

## Genesis 43:29
Joseph saw Benjamin, his own mother's son. He asked, “Is this your youngest brother?” He said, “May God be gracious to you, my son.” 📖

## Genesis 43:30
Joseph hurried to a private room. His feelings for his brother moved him to tears. He wept there. 📖

## Genesis 43:31
He washed his face and returned. He controlled himself and said, “Serve the food.” 📖

## Genesis 43:32
Joseph, his brothers, and the Egyptians were served separately. Egyptians would not eat with Hebrews. They found it detestable. 📖

## Genesis 43:33
The brothers sat before Joseph by age, oldest to youngest. They looked at each other in amazement. 📖

## Genesis 43:34
They received portions from Joseph's table. Benjamin's was five times larger than anyone else's. They feasted and drank freely with Joseph. 📖
