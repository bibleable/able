## Genesis 44:1
Joseph told his steward, “Fill their sacks with as much food as they can carry. Put each man's silver at his sack's mouth. 📖

## Genesis 44:2
Put my silver cup in the youngest's sack with his grain money.” The steward obeyed. 📖

## Genesis 44:3
At dawn, the men left with their donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 44:4
They had gone only a little way. Joseph told his steward, “Pursue them. Ask why they repaid good with evil. 📖

## Genesis 44:5
Say, ‘My master drinks from this cup and uses it for divination. You acted wickedly!’” 📖

## Genesis 44:6
The steward caught them and repeated the message. 📖

## Genesis 44:7
They asked, “Why say this, my lord? We could never do that. 📖

## Genesis 44:8
We brought back the silver from Canaan. Why would we steal silver or gold from your master's house? 📖

## Genesis 44:9
If any servant has it, let him die. The rest will be your slaves.” 📖

## Genesis 44:10
The steward replied, “As you say. But only the one with the cup will be my slave. The others will be free of blame.” 📖

## Genesis 44:11
Each quickly lowered his sack and opened it. 📖

## Genesis 44:12
The steward searched from oldest to youngest. He found the cup in Benjamin's sack. 📖

## Genesis 44:13
They tore their clothes, loaded the donkeys, and returned to the city. 📖

## Genesis 44:14
Judah and his brothers found Joseph still at home. They fell to the ground before him. 📖

## Genesis 44:15
Joseph asked, “What have you done? Did you not know a man like me can discover truth by divination?” 📖

## Genesis 44:16
Judah replied, “What can we say? How can we plead or justify ourselves? God exposed our wrongdoing. We are your slaves, including the one with the cup.” 📖

## Genesis 44:17
Joseph said, “I would never do that. Only the man with the cup will be my slave. Return peacefully to your father.” 📖

## Genesis 44:18
Judah approached. “Sir, let your servant speak personally. Do not be angry. You are equal to Pharaoh. 📖

## Genesis 44:19
You asked whether we had a father or brother. 📖

## Genesis 44:20
We said, ‘Our father is elderly. He has a young son born in his old age. The boy's brother is dead. He alone remains of his mother's sons. His father loves him.’ 📖

## Genesis 44:21
You said to bring him so you could see him. 📖

## Genesis 44:22
We said he could not leave his father. His father would die if he left. 📖

## Genesis 44:23
You said we could not see you again without our younger brother. 📖

## Genesis 44:24
We returned and told our father, your servant. 📖

## Genesis 44:25
He told us to return and buy food. 📖

## Genesis 44:26
We said we could not return without our younger brother. We could not see the man without him. 📖

## Genesis 44:27
My father said, ‘My wife bore me two sons. 📖

## Genesis 44:28
One disappeared. I said he was torn to pieces. I have not seen him since. 📖

## Genesis 44:29
If you take this one and harm comes, you will bring my gray hair to Sheol in sorrow.’ 📖

## Genesis 44:30
My father's life is wrapped in the boy's life. If I return without the boy 📖

## Genesis 44:31
and he sees the boy missing, he will die. We will bring our father's gray hair to Sheol in sorrow. 📖

## Genesis 44:32
I guaranteed him to my father. If I do not return him, I promised to bear the guilt before my father all my life. 📖

## Genesis 44:33
Please let me remain as your slave instead of the boy. Let him return with his brothers. 📖

## Genesis 44:34
How can I return without him? I cannot bear to see my father's overwhelming misery.” 📖
