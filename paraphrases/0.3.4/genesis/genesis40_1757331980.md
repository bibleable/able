## Genesis 40:1
Later, the cupbearer and baker of Egypt's king offended their master. 📖

## Genesis 40:2
Pharaoh was angry with his two officers, the chief cupbearer and chief baker. 📖

## Genesis 40:3
He imprisoned them in the captain of the guard's house, where Joseph was confined. 📖

## Genesis 40:4
The captain assigned Joseph to attend them. They remained in custody for some time. 📖

## Genesis 40:5
The imprisoned cupbearer and baker each dreamed on the same night. Each dream had its own meaning. 📖

## Genesis 40:6
Joseph came that morning and saw their distress. 📖

## Genesis 40:7
He asked Pharaoh's officers imprisoned with him in his master's house, “Why do you look sad today?” 📖

## Genesis 40:8
They said, “We dreamed, but no one can interpret our dreams.” Joseph replied, “Interpretations belong to God. Tell me your dreams.” 📖

## Genesis 40:9
The chief cupbearer said, “I dreamed of a vine before me. 📖

## Genesis 40:10
It had three branches. It budded, blossomed, and produced ripe grape clusters. 📖

## Genesis 40:11
I held Pharaoh's cup. I squeezed grapes into it and placed it in his hand.” 📖

## Genesis 40:12
Joseph said, “The three branches mean three days. 📖

## Genesis 40:13
Within three days, Pharaoh will lift your head and restore your position. You will put his cup in his hand as before. 📖

## Genesis 40:14
When things go well, remember me. Show kindness by mentioning me to Pharaoh so he releases me from prison. 📖

## Genesis 40:15
I was kidnapped from the Hebrews' land. I did nothing here deserving this dungeon.” 📖

## Genesis 40:16
The chief baker saw that the interpretation was favorable. He said, “I dreamed of three baskets of white bread on my head. 📖

## Genesis 40:17
The top basket held many baked foods for Pharaoh. Birds ate them from the basket on my head.” 📖

## Genesis 40:18
Joseph said, “The three baskets mean three days. 📖

## Genesis 40:19
Within three days, Pharaoh will lift off your head and hang you on a tree. Birds will eat your flesh.” 📖

## Genesis 40:20
The third day was Pharaoh's birthday. He feasted with all his officials. Before them, he lifted the chief cupbearer's and chief baker's heads. 📖

## Genesis 40:21
He restored the chief cupbearer. He again put Pharaoh's cup in his hand. 📖

## Genesis 40:22
Pharaoh hanged the chief baker, as Joseph interpreted. 📖

## Genesis 40:23
The chief cupbearer forgot Joseph. He did not remember him. 📖
