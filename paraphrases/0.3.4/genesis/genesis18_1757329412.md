## Genesis 18:1
The LORD appeared to Abraham beside the Oaks of Mamre. Abraham sat at his tent entrance in the day's heat. 📖

## Genesis 18:2
Abraham looked up and saw three men nearby. He ran from his tent entrance to meet them. He bowed low to the ground. 📖

## Genesis 18:3
Abraham said, “My lord, if you are pleased with me, please do not pass your servant by. 📖

## Genesis 18:4
Let me bring a little water so you can wash your feet. Rest under the tree. 📖

## Genesis 18:5
I will bring some bread to refresh you. This is why you have come your servant's way. Then you may continue.” They replied, “Do as you said.” 📖

## Genesis 18:6
Abraham hurried into the tent. He told Sarah, “Quick! Take three seahs of fine flour. Knead it and bake bread.” 📖

## Genesis 18:7
Abraham ran to the herd and chose a tender, choice calf. He gave it to a servant, who hurried to prepare it. 📖

## Genesis 18:8
Abraham brought curds, milk, and the prepared calf. He set them before the men. He stood beside them under the tree while they ate. 📖

## Genesis 18:9
They asked, “Where is your wife Sarah?” He replied, “In the tent.” 📖

## Genesis 18:10
The LORD said, “I will return at this time next year. Your wife Sarah will have a son!” Sarah was behind him, listening at the tent entrance. 📖

## Genesis 18:11
Abraham and Sarah were very old. Sarah had passed childbearing age. 📖

## Genesis 18:12
She laughed to herself. “I am worn out, and my master is old. Will I now have this pleasure?” 📖

## Genesis 18:13
The LORD asked Abraham, “Why did Sarah laugh? Why did she ask whether she could bear a child when old? 📖

## Genesis 18:14
Is anything too difficult for the LORD? At the appointed time, in about a year, I will return. Sarah will have a son.” 📖

## Genesis 18:15
Sarah was afraid. She denied it, saying, “I did not laugh.” The LORD replied, “No, you did laugh.” 📖

## Genesis 18:16
The men rose to leave and looked toward Sodom. Abraham walked with them to see them off. 📖

## Genesis 18:17
The LORD said, “Should I hide from Abraham what I will do? 📖

## Genesis 18:18
Abraham will become a great, powerful nation. All the earth's nations will be blessed through him. 📖

## Genesis 18:19
I chose him to command his children and household after him to keep the LORD's way. They must do what is right and just. Then the LORD may bring Abraham what He promised.” 📖

## Genesis 18:20
The LORD said, “The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great. Their sin is very serious. 📖

## Genesis 18:21
I will go down to see whether their actions fully match the outcry reaching Me. If not, I will find out.” 📖

## Genesis 18:22
The two men turned toward Sodom. Abraham remained standing before the LORD. 📖

## Genesis 18:23
Abraham stepped forward. He asked, “Will You destroy righteous people with wicked people? 📖

## Genesis 18:24
Suppose fifty righteous people live in the city. Will You destroy it? Will You not spare the place for those fifty? 📖

## Genesis 18:25
You would not kill righteous people with wicked people and treat them alike! You would never do that! Will the whole earth's Judge not do right?” 📖

## Genesis 18:26
The LORD replied, “If I find fifty righteous people in Sodom, I will spare the whole place because of them.” 📖

## Genesis 18:27
Abraham answered, “I have dared to speak to the Lord, although I am dust and ashes. 📖

## Genesis 18:28
Suppose the fifty lack five. Will You destroy the whole city for lack of five?” He replied, “If I find forty-five, I will not destroy it.” 📖

## Genesis 18:29
Abraham asked again, “Suppose forty are there?” The LORD answered, “I will not do it because of the forty.” 📖

## Genesis 18:30
Abraham said, “May the Lord not be angry. Let me speak again. Suppose thirty are there?” He replied, “If I find thirty, I will not do it.” 📖

## Genesis 18:31
Abraham said, “I have dared to speak to the Lord. Suppose twenty are there?” He answered, “I will not destroy it because of the twenty.” 📖

## Genesis 18:32
Abraham said, “May the Lord not be angry. Let me speak once more. Suppose ten are there?” He answered, “I will not destroy it because of the ten.” 📖

## Genesis 18:33
The LORD finished speaking with Abraham and left. Abraham returned home. 📖
