## Genesis 12:1
The LORD told Abram, “Leave your country, relatives, and father's household. Go to the land I will show you. 📖

## Genesis 12:2
I will make you a great nation and bless you. I will make your name great so you will be a blessing. 📖

## Genesis 12:3
I will bless those who bless you. I will curse those who curse you. All the earth's families will be blessed through you.” 📖

## Genesis 12:4
Abram left as the LORD directed. Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran. 📖

## Genesis 12:5
Abram took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, and all the possessions and people they acquired in Haran. They traveled to Canaan's land and arrived there. 📖

## Genesis 12:6
Abram traveled through the land to the Oak of Moreh at Shechem. The Canaanites lived in the land then. 📖

## Genesis 12:7
The LORD appeared to Abram and said, “I will give this land to your offspring.” Abram built an altar there to the LORD who appeared to him. 📖

## Genesis 12:8
Abram moved to the hill country east of Bethel. He pitched his tent with Bethel west of him and Ai east of him. He built an altar to the LORD there. He called on the LORD's name. 📖

## Genesis 12:9
Abram continued toward the Negev. 📖

## Genesis 12:10
A famine struck the land. Abram went down to Egypt to live for a while because the famine was severe. 📖

## Genesis 12:11
As he neared Egypt, Abram told his wife Sarai, “I know you are beautiful. 📖

## Genesis 12:12
When the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘This is his wife.’ They will kill me but let you live. 📖

## Genesis 12:13
Please say you are my sister. Then they will treat me well because of you. My life will be spared on your account.” 📖

## Genesis 12:14
When Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw that Sarai was very beautiful. 📖

## Genesis 12:15
Pharaoh's officials saw her and praised her to him. She was taken into Pharaoh's palace. 📖

## Genesis 12:16
Pharaoh treated Abram well because of her. Abram gained sheep, cattle, male and female donkeys, male and female servants, and camels. 📖

## Genesis 12:17
But the LORD struck Pharaoh and his household with severe plagues because of Sarai, Abram's wife. 📖

## Genesis 12:18
Pharaoh called Abram and asked, “What have you done to me? Why did you not tell me she was your wife? 📖

## Genesis 12:19
Why did you call her your sister, so I took her as my wife? Here is your wife. Take her and leave!” 📖

## Genesis 12:20
Pharaoh gave his men orders about Abram. They sent him away with his wife and all his possessions. 📖
