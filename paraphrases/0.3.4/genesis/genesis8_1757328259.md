## Genesis 8:1
God remembered Noah and all the animals and livestock with him in the ark. God sent wind over the earth. The water began to fall. 📖

## Genesis 8:2
The deep's springs and heaven's floodgates closed. Rain from the sky stopped. 📖

## Genesis 8:3
The water steadily withdrew from the earth. After 150 days, it had gone down. 📖

## Genesis 8:4
On the seventeenth day of the seventh month, the ark rested on Ararat's mountains. 📖

## Genesis 8:5
The water kept falling until the tenth month. The mountaintops became visible on the first day of that month. 📖

## Genesis 8:6
After forty days, Noah opened the window he made in the ark. 📖

## Genesis 8:7
He sent out a raven. It flew back and forth until the water dried from the earth. 📖

## Genesis 8:8
Noah sent out a dove to see whether the water had withdrawn from the ground's surface. 📖

## Genesis 8:9
The dove found nowhere to rest her foot. Water still covered the earth's whole surface. She returned to Noah in the ark. He reached out his hand and brought her inside. 📖

## Genesis 8:10
Noah waited seven more days. He sent the dove out of the ark again. 📖

## Genesis 8:11
That evening, she returned with a freshly picked olive leaf in her beak. Noah knew the water had withdrawn from the earth. 📖

## Genesis 8:12
He waited seven more days and sent her out again. This time, she did not return. 📖

## Genesis 8:13
In Noah's six hundred and first year, on the first day of the first month, the water had dried from the earth. Noah removed the ark's covering. He saw that the ground's surface was dry. 📖

## Genesis 8:14
By the twenty-seventh day of the second month, the earth was completely dry. 📖

## Genesis 8:15
God spoke to Noah. 📖

## Genesis 8:16
“Leave the ark with your wife, sons, and their wives. 📖

## Genesis 8:17
Bring out all the living creatures with you: birds, livestock, and everything crawling on the ground. Let them spread across the earth, be fruitful, and multiply.” 📖

## Genesis 8:18
Noah came out with his sons, wife, and sons' wives. 📖

## Genesis 8:19
Every living creature, crawling thing, and bird came out of the ark, kind by kind. Everything moving on earth came out. 📖

## Genesis 8:20
Noah built an altar to the LORD. He took from every kind of clean animal and clean bird. He offered burnt offerings on the altar. 📖

## Genesis 8:21
The LORD smelled the pleasing aroma and spoke in His heart. “I will never again curse the ground because of man, even though every inclination of his heart is evil from his youth. I will never again destroy all living creatures as I have done. 📖

## Genesis 8:22
As long as the earth remains, seedtime and harvest will continue. Cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will never stop.” 📖
