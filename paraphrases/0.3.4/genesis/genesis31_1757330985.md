## Genesis 31:1
Jacob heard Laban's sons saying, “Jacob took everything our father owned. He gained all this wealth at our father's expense.” 📖

## Genesis 31:2
Jacob saw that Laban's expression and attitude toward him had changed. 📖

## Genesis 31:3
The LORD told Jacob, “Return to your fathers' land and your relatives. I will be with you.” 📖

## Genesis 31:4
Jacob called Rachel and Leah to the field where his flocks were. 📖

## Genesis 31:5
He said, “Your father's face shows his changed attitude toward me. But my father's God has been with me. 📖

## Genesis 31:6
You know I served your father with all my strength. 📖

## Genesis 31:7
He cheated me and changed my wages ten times. But God did not let him harm me. 📖

## Genesis 31:8
If he made speckled animals my wages, the whole flock bore speckled young. If he made streaked animals my wages, the whole flock bore streaked young. 📖

## Genesis 31:9
God took your father's livestock and gave them to me. 📖

## Genesis 31:10
During the breeding season, I dreamed that streaked, spotted, and speckled males mated with the females. 📖

## Genesis 31:11
In the dream, God's angel called, ‘Jacob!’ I replied, ‘Here I am.’ 📖

## Genesis 31:12
He said, ‘Look up. All the males mating with the flock are streaked, spotted, or speckled. I have seen everything Laban did to you. 📖

## Genesis 31:13
I am Bethel's God. You anointed the pillar and made a solemn vow to Me there. Leave this land now. Return to your homeland.’” 📖

## Genesis 31:14
Rachel and Leah answered, “Is any portion or inheritance left for us in our father's house? 📖

## Genesis 31:15
Does he not treat us as outsiders? He sold us and wasted what was paid for us. 📖

## Genesis 31:16
All the wealth God took from our father belongs to us and our children. Do everything God told you.” 📖

## Genesis 31:17
Jacob rose and put his children and wives on camels. 📖

## Genesis 31:18
He drove all his livestock ahead, with all his possessions acquired in Paddan-aram. He left for his father Isaac in Canaan's land. 📖

## Genesis 31:19
Laban was shearing sheep. Rachel stole her father's household idols. 📖

## Genesis 31:20
Jacob deceived Laban the Aramean. He did not tell him he was fleeing. 📖

## Genesis 31:21
He fled with all his possessions, crossed the Euphrates, and headed toward Gilead's hill country. 📖

## Genesis 31:22
On the third day, Laban heard that Jacob fled. 📖

## Genesis 31:23
Laban took his relatives and pursued Jacob for seven days. He caught him in Gilead's hill country. 📖

## Genesis 31:24
That night, God warned Laban the Aramean in a dream. “Be careful not to say anything to Jacob, good or bad.” 📖

## Genesis 31:25
Jacob had pitched his tent in Gilead's hill country when Laban caught him. Laban and his relatives camped there too. 📖

## Genesis 31:26
Laban asked Jacob, “What have you done? You deceived me and carried my daughters away like war captives! 📖

## Genesis 31:27
Why did you flee secretly and deceive me without telling me? I would have sent you away with joy, songs, tambourines, and harps. 📖

## Genesis 31:28
You did not let me kiss my grandchildren and daughters goodbye. You acted foolishly. 📖

## Genesis 31:29
I can harm you greatly. But last night, your father's God warned me not to say anything to Jacob, good or bad. 📖

## Genesis 31:30
You left because you longed for your father's house. But why did you steal my gods?” 📖

## Genesis 31:31
Jacob replied, “I feared you would take your daughters from me by force. 📖

## Genesis 31:32
Whoever has your gods must not live! Search before our relatives. Take anything belonging to you.” Jacob did not know Rachel stole the idols. 📖

## Genesis 31:33
Laban searched Jacob's tent, Leah's tent, and the two female servants' tents. He found nothing. He left Leah's tent and entered Rachel's. 📖

## Genesis 31:34
Rachel had put the household idols in her camel's saddlebag and sat on them. Laban searched the whole tent but found nothing. 📖

## Genesis 31:35
Rachel told her father, “Sir, do not be angry that I cannot stand before you. I am having my period.” He searched but found no idols. 📖

## Genesis 31:36
Jacob became angry and challenged Laban. “What is my crime? What sin made you pursue me so fiercely? 📖

## Genesis 31:37
You searched all my goods. Did you find anything yours? Put it before my brothers and yours. Let them judge between us. 📖

## Genesis 31:38
I stayed with you twenty years. Your sheep and goats did not miscarry. I did not eat your flock's rams. 📖

## Genesis 31:39
I did not bring you animals torn by wild beasts. I bore the loss. You demanded payment for anything stolen by day or night. 📖

## Genesis 31:40
Daytime heat consumed me, and frost consumed me at night. Sleep fled from my eyes. 📖

## Genesis 31:41
I served your household twenty years. Fourteen were for your two daughters, and six were for your flocks. You changed my wages ten times! 📖

## Genesis 31:42
My father's God, Abraham's God and the Fear of Isaac, was with me. Otherwise, you would have sent me away with nothing. God saw my suffering and my hands' toil. Last night, He gave judgment.” 📖

## Genesis 31:43
Laban replied, “These daughters, sons, and flocks are mine! Everything you see is mine! But what can I do today about my daughters or their children? 📖

## Genesis 31:44
Let us make a covenant. Let it witness between us.” 📖

## Genesis 31:45
Jacob chose a stone and set it up as a pillar. 📖

## Genesis 31:46
He told his relatives, “Gather stones.” They made a mound of stones and ate beside it. 📖

## Genesis 31:47
Laban named it Jegar-sahadutha. Jacob named it Galeed. 📖

## Genesis 31:48
Laban said, “This mound witnesses between us today.” Therefore, it was named Galeed. 📖

## Genesis 31:49
It was also named Mizpah. Laban said, “May the LORD watch between us when we are apart. 📖

## Genesis 31:50
If you mistreat my daughters or marry other wives, remember that God witnesses between us. This is true even if no one is with us.” 📖

## Genesis 31:51
Laban told Jacob, “Here are the mound and pillar I set between us. 📖

## Genesis 31:52
They witness that I will not pass this mound to harm you. You will not pass this mound and pillar to harm me. 📖

## Genesis 31:53
May Abraham's God and Nahor's God, their father's God, judge between us.” Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac. 📖

## Genesis 31:54
Jacob sacrificed on the mountain and invited his relatives to eat. After the meal, they spent the night on the mountain. 📖

## Genesis 31:55
Early the next morning, Laban kissed his grandchildren and daughters and blessed them. He returned home. 📖
