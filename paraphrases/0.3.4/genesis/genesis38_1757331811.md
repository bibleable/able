## Genesis 38:1
Around that time, Judah left his brothers. He settled near Hirah, an Adullamite. 📖

## Genesis 38:2
Judah saw Shua the Canaanite's daughter. He married her and slept with her. 📖

## Genesis 38:3
She conceived and bore a son. Judah named him Er. 📖

## Genesis 38:4
She conceived again and bore a son. She named him Onan. 📖

## Genesis 38:5
She bore another son and named him Shelah. He was born at Chezib. 📖

## Genesis 38:6
Judah found a wife named Tamar for his firstborn Er. 📖

## Genesis 38:7
Er was wicked before the LORD. The LORD put Judah's firstborn to death. 📖

## Genesis 38:8
Judah told Onan, “Sleep with your brother's wife. Fulfill your duty as her brother-in-law. Raise offspring for your brother.” 📖

## Genesis 38:9
Onan knew the offspring would not be his. Whenever he slept with his brother's wife, he spilled his seed on the ground. He avoided producing offspring for his brother. 📖

## Genesis 38:10
His actions were wicked before the LORD. The LORD put Onan to death too. 📖

## Genesis 38:11
Judah told Tamar, “Stay as a widow in your father's house until Shelah grows.” He feared Shelah might die like his brothers. Tamar lived in her father's house. 📖

## Genesis 38:12
After a long time, Judah's wife, Shua's daughter, died. After mourning, Judah went with his friend Hirah the Adullamite to his sheepshearers at Timnah. 📖

## Genesis 38:13
Tamar heard that her father-in-law was going to shear sheep at Timnah. 📖

## Genesis 38:14
She removed her widow's clothes and disguised herself with a veil over her face. She sat at Enaim's entrance, on the Timnah road. Shelah was grown, but she had not been given to him as a wife. 📖

## Genesis 38:15
Judah saw her covered face and thought she was a prostitute. 📖

## Genesis 38:16
He did not recognize his daughter-in-law. He approached and said, “Let me sleep with you.” She asked, “What will you give me?” 📖

## Genesis 38:17
He replied, “A young goat from my flock.” She said, “Leave a pledge until you send it.” 📖

## Genesis 38:18
He asked what pledge she wanted. She requested his seal, cord, and the staff in his hand. He gave them and slept with her. She became pregnant by him. 📖

## Genesis 38:19
Tamar left. She removed her veil and wore her widow's clothes again. 📖

## Genesis 38:20
Judah sent Hirah the Adullamite with the young goat to collect his pledged items. His friend could not find her. 📖

## Genesis 38:21
He asked the local men, “Where is the shrine prostitute beside Enaim's road?” They replied, “No shrine prostitute was here.” 📖

## Genesis 38:22
Hirah told Judah, “I could not find her. The local men said no shrine prostitute was there.” 📖

## Genesis 38:23
Judah said, “Let her keep the items, or people will laugh at us. I sent the goat, but you could not find her.” 📖

## Genesis 38:24
About three months later, Judah heard Tamar had prostituted herself and was pregnant. He ordered, “Bring her out. Burn her to death!” 📖

## Genesis 38:25
As she was brought out, Tamar sent her father-in-law a message. “The owner of these items made me pregnant. Examine them. Whose seal, cord, and staff are they?” 📖

## Genesis 38:26
Judah recognized them. “She is more righteous than I, because I did not give her to Shelah.” He never had relations with her again. 📖

## Genesis 38:27
When Tamar gave birth, twins were in her womb. 📖

## Genesis 38:28
One put out his hand during birth. The midwife tied scarlet thread around his wrist. She said, “This one came out first.” 📖

## Genesis 38:29
He drew his hand back. His brother came out. She said, “You broke out first!” He was named Perez. 📖

## Genesis 38:30
His brother followed with the scarlet thread around his wrist. He was named Zerah. 📖
