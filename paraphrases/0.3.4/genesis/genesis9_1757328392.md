## Genesis 9:1
God blessed Noah and his sons. He told them, “Be fruitful and multiply. Fill the earth. 📖

## Genesis 9:2
Every land animal, bird, crawling creature, and fish of the sea will fear and dread you. They are given into your hand. 📖

## Genesis 9:3
Everything living and moving will be your food. I gave you green plants. Now I give you everything. 📖

## Genesis 9:4
But do not eat meat that still has its lifeblood in it. 📖

## Genesis 9:5
I will demand the life of any person or animal that sheds your lifeblood. I will demand an accounting from anyone who takes another person's life. 📖

## Genesis 9:6
Whoever sheds human blood will have his blood shed by humans. This is because God made mankind in His own image. 📖

## Genesis 9:7
Be fruitful and multiply. Spread across the earth and multiply on it.” 📖

## Genesis 9:8
God spoke to Noah and his sons with him. 📖

## Genesis 9:9
“I now establish My covenant with you and your descendants after you. 📖

## Genesis 9:10
It includes every living creature with you: birds, livestock, and every land animal. It includes everything that came out of the ark. 📖

## Genesis 9:11
I establish My covenant with you. Floodwaters will never again destroy all life. A flood will never again destroy the earth.” 📖

## Genesis 9:12
God said, “This is the sign of My covenant with you and every living creature with you. This covenant is for all future generations. 📖

## Genesis 9:13
I have placed My rainbow in the clouds. It will be a sign of the covenant between Me and the earth. 📖

## Genesis 9:14
Whenever I bring clouds over the earth and the rainbow appears in them, 📖

## Genesis 9:15
I will remember My covenant with you and every living creature of every kind. Water will never again become a flood that destroys all life. 📖

## Genesis 9:16
Whenever the rainbow appears in the clouds, I will see it. I will remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of every kind on earth.” 📖

## Genesis 9:17
God told Noah, “This is the sign of the covenant I established between Me and every creature on earth.” 📖

## Genesis 9:18
Noah's sons who left the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham was Canaan's father. 📖

## Genesis 9:19
These were Noah's three sons. The whole earth was populated from them. 📖

## Genesis 9:20
Noah worked the soil. He planted a vineyard. 📖

## Genesis 9:21
He drank its wine and became drunk. He uncovered himself inside his tent. 📖

## Genesis 9:22
Ham, Canaan's father, saw his father's nakedness. He told his two brothers outside. 📖

## Genesis 9:23
Shem and Japheth put a garment over their shoulders. They walked backward and covered their father's nakedness. Their faces turned away so they did not see it. 📖

## Genesis 9:24
Noah woke from his drunkenness. He learned what his youngest son had done to him. 📖

## Genesis 9:25
He said, “May Canaan be cursed! He will be a servant of servants to his brothers.” 📖

## Genesis 9:26
He said, “May the LORD, Shem's God, be blessed! May Canaan be Shem's servant. 📖

## Genesis 9:27
May God expand Japheth's territory. May he live in Shem's tents. May Canaan be his servant.” 📖

## Genesis 9:28
Noah lived 350 years after the flood. 📖

## Genesis 9:29
Noah lived 950 years in total. Then he died. 📖
