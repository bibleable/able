## Genesis 42:1
Jacob heard Egypt had grain. He asked his sons, “Why stare at each other?” 📖

## Genesis 42:2
He said, “Egypt has grain. Buy some there so we live instead of dying.” 📖

## Genesis 42:3
Ten of Joseph's brothers went to Egypt to buy grain. 📖

## Genesis 42:4
Jacob kept Joseph's brother Benjamin home. He feared harm would come to him. 📖

## Genesis 42:5
Israel's sons joined the grain buyers because Canaan also suffered famine. 📖

## Genesis 42:6
Joseph ruled the land and sold grain to all its people. His brothers arrived and bowed facedown before him. 📖

## Genesis 42:7
Joseph recognized them but acted as if they were strangers. He spoke harshly. “Where are you from?” They answered, “Canaan. We came for food.” 📖

## Genesis 42:8
Joseph knew them. They did not recognize him. 📖

## Genesis 42:9
He remembered his dreams. He said, “You are spies seeking our land's weaknesses!” 📖

## Genesis 42:10
They answered, “No, my lord. Your servants came to buy food. 📖

## Genesis 42:11
We are one man's sons. We are honest, not spies.” 📖

## Genesis 42:12
He said, “No. You seek our land's weaknesses.” 📖

## Genesis 42:13
They replied, “We are twelve brothers, sons of one man in Canaan. The youngest is with our father. One is no more.” 📖

## Genesis 42:14
Joseph said, “As I said, you are spies! 📖

## Genesis 42:15
This will test you. As surely as Pharaoh lives, you cannot leave until your youngest brother comes. 📖

## Genesis 42:16
Send one man to bring him. The rest will remain imprisoned while your words are tested. If untrue, as surely as Pharaoh lives, you are spies!” 📖

## Genesis 42:17
Joseph imprisoned them three days. 📖

## Genesis 42:18
On the third day, he said, “I fear God. Do this and live. 📖

## Genesis 42:19
If honest, leave one brother here in custody. The rest may take grain home for your hungry households. 📖

## Genesis 42:20
Bring your youngest brother so your words are verified and you do not die.” They agreed. 📖

## Genesis 42:21
They said, “Our brother's suffering brings this punishment. We saw his distress and heard his pleas but would not listen. That is why we suffer now.” 📖

## Genesis 42:22
Reuben replied, “I warned you not to sin against the boy. You would not listen. We must now account for his blood!” 📖

## Genesis 42:23
They did not know Joseph understood. He used an interpreter. 📖

## Genesis 42:24
He turned away and wept. He returned and spoke to them. He took Simeon and had him bound before them. 📖

## Genesis 42:25
Joseph ordered their bags filled with grain. Each man's silver was returned to his sack. They received provisions for the journey. His orders were obeyed. 📖

## Genesis 42:26
They loaded their donkeys and left with the grain. 📖

## Genesis 42:27
At their overnight lodging, one opened his sack for donkey feed. His silver was at the sack's mouth. 📖

## Genesis 42:28
He said, “My silver is back in my sack!” They trembled, and their hearts sank. They asked, “What has God done to us?” 📖

## Genesis 42:29
They reached Jacob in Canaan and told him everything. 📖

## Genesis 42:30
“The land's lord spoke harshly and accused us of spying. 📖

## Genesis 42:31
We told him, ‘We are honest, not spies. 📖

## Genesis 42:32
We are twelve brothers by one father. One is no more. Our youngest is with our father in Canaan.’ 📖

## Genesis 42:33
He said, ‘Leave one brother with me. Take food to your hungry households and go. This will show your honesty. 📖

## Genesis 42:34
Bring your youngest brother so I know you are honest, not spies. I will return your brother. You may trade here.’” 📖

## Genesis 42:35
They emptied their sacks. Each found his silver bag. They and their father saw the silver and were dismayed. 📖

## Genesis 42:36
Jacob said, “You deprived me of sons. Joseph is gone. Simeon is no more. Now you want Benjamin. Everything is against me!” 📖

## Genesis 42:37
Reuben said, “Kill my two sons if I do not bring him back. Entrust him to me. I will return him.” 📖

## Genesis 42:38
Jacob replied, “He will not go. His brother is dead, and he alone remains. If harm comes on your journey, you will bring my gray hair to Sheol in sorrow.” 📖
