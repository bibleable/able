## Genesis 7:1
The LORD told Noah, “Enter the ark with your whole family. I have found you righteous in this generation. 📖

## Genesis 7:2
Take seven pairs of every kind of clean animal, each a male and its mate. Take one pair of every kind of unclean animal, a male and its mate. 📖

## Genesis 7:3
Take seven pairs of every kind of bird, male and female. This will preserve their offspring across the whole earth. 📖

## Genesis 7:4
In seven days, I will send rain on earth for forty days and forty nights. I will wipe every living thing I made from the earth's surface.” 📖

## Genesis 7:5
Noah did everything the LORD commanded. 📖

## Genesis 7:6
Noah was 600 years old when the floodwaters came on earth. 📖

## Genesis 7:7
Noah entered the ark with his wife, sons, and their wives. They went in to escape the floodwaters. 📖

## Genesis 7:8
The clean and unclean animals, birds, and everything crawling on the ground 📖

## Genesis 7:9
came to Noah to enter the ark. They came two by two, male and female, as God commanded Noah. 📖

## Genesis 7:10
After seven days, the floodwaters came on earth. 📖

## Genesis 7:11
In Noah's six hundredth year, on the seventeenth day of the second month, all the great deep's springs burst open. Heaven's floodgates opened. 📖

## Genesis 7:12
Rain fell on earth for forty days and forty nights. 📖

## Genesis 7:13
That very day, Noah entered the ark. His sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth entered with him. His wife and his sons' three wives also entered. 📖

## Genesis 7:14
They entered with every kind of wild animal, livestock, crawling creature, bird, and winged creature. 📖

## Genesis 7:15
Every kind of creature with life's breath came to Noah to enter the ark, two by two. 📖

## Genesis 7:16
Male and female of every living thing entered as God commanded Noah. Then the LORD shut him in. 📖

## Genesis 7:17
The flood continued on earth for forty days. Rising water lifted the ark high above the earth. 📖

## Genesis 7:18
The water surged and rose greatly over the earth. The ark floated on its surface. 📖

## Genesis 7:19
The water completely covered the earth. It covered all the high mountains under all the heavens. 📖

## Genesis 7:20
The water rose fifteen cubits above the mountaintops. 📖

## Genesis 7:21
Every living thing moving on earth died. Birds, livestock, wild animals, swarming creatures, and all mankind died. 📖

## Genesis 7:22
Everything on dry land with life's breath in its nostrils died. 📖

## Genesis 7:23
Every living thing on the earth's surface was destroyed. People, livestock, crawling creatures, and birds of the air were wiped away. Only Noah and those with him in the ark remained. 📖

## Genesis 7:24
The water covered the earth for 150 days. 📖
