## Genesis 5:1
This is the book of Adam's generations. When God created mankind, He made them in His own likeness. 📖

## Genesis 5:2
God created them male and female. He blessed them. On the day He created them, He called them “man.” 📖

## Genesis 5:3
At 130 years old, Adam had a son in his own image and likeness. He named him Seth. 📖

## Genesis 5:4
After Seth's birth, Adam lived 800 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:5
Adam lived 930 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:6
At 105 years old, Seth became Enosh's father. 📖

## Genesis 5:7
After Enosh's birth, Seth lived 807 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:8
Seth lived 912 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:9
At 90 years old, Enosh became Kenan's father. 📖

## Genesis 5:10
After Kenan's birth, Enosh lived 815 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:11
Enosh lived 905 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:12
At 70 years old, Kenan became Mahalalel's father. 📖

## Genesis 5:13
After Mahalalel's birth, Kenan lived 840 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:14
Kenan lived 910 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:15
At 65 years old, Mahalalel became Jared's father. 📖

## Genesis 5:16
After Jared's birth, Mahalalel lived 830 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:17
Mahalalel lived 895 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:18
At 162 years old, Jared became Enoch's father. 📖

## Genesis 5:19
After Enoch's birth, Jared lived 800 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:20
Jared lived 962 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:21
At 65 years old, Enoch became Methuselah's father. 📖

## Genesis 5:22
After Methuselah's birth, Enoch walked with God for 300 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:23
Enoch lived 365 years in total. 📖

## Genesis 5:24
Enoch walked with God. Then he was gone, because God took him away. 📖

## Genesis 5:25
At 187 years old, Methuselah became Lamech's father. 📖

## Genesis 5:26
After Lamech's birth, Methuselah lived 782 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:27
Methuselah lived 969 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:28
At 182 years old, Lamech had a son. 📖

## Genesis 5:29
He named him Noah. He said, “May he comfort us in our hands' labor and toil caused by the ground the LORD cursed.” 📖

## Genesis 5:30
After Noah's birth, Lamech lived 595 years. He had other sons and daughters. 📖

## Genesis 5:31
Lamech lived 777 years in total. Then he died. 📖

## Genesis 5:32
After Noah was 500 years old, he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖
