## Genesis 22:1
Later, God tested Abraham. He called, “Abraham!” Abraham answered, “Here I am.” 📖

## Genesis 22:2
God said, “Take Isaac, your only son whom you love. Go to Moriah's land. Offer him as a burnt offering on a mountain I will show you.” 📖

## Genesis 22:3
Abraham rose early the next morning and saddled his donkey. He took two servants and his son Isaac. He split wood for the burnt offering. He left for the place God designated. 📖

## Genesis 22:4
On the third day, Abraham looked up and saw the place far away. 📖

## Genesis 22:5
He told his servants, “Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go there to worship. Then we will return to you.” 📖

## Genesis 22:6
Abraham put the burnt offering's wood on Isaac. He carried the fire and sacrificial knife himself. The two walked together. 📖

## Genesis 22:7
Isaac called, “My father!” Abraham replied, “Here I am, my son.” Isaac said, “We have fire and wood. Where is the lamb for the burnt offering?” 📖

## Genesis 22:8
Abraham answered, “God Himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering, my son.” They walked together. 📖

## Genesis 22:9
They reached the place God designated. Abraham built an altar and arranged the wood. He bound Isaac and put him on the altar, above the wood. 📖

## Genesis 22:10
Abraham reached out and took the knife to kill his son. 📖

## Genesis 22:11
The LORD's angel called from heaven, “Abraham, Abraham!” He replied, “Here I am.” 📖

## Genesis 22:12
The angel said, “Do not touch the boy or do anything to him. Now I know you fear God. You have not withheld your only son from me.” 📖

## Genesis 22:13
Abraham looked up and saw a ram behind him, its horns caught in a thicket. He took the ram and offered it as a burnt offering instead of his son. 📖

## Genesis 22:14
Abraham named the place The LORD Will Provide. People still say, “On the LORD's mountain, it will be provided.” 📖

## Genesis 22:15
The LORD's angel called to Abraham from heaven a second time. 📖

## Genesis 22:16
He said, “I have sworn by Myself, declares the LORD. You have done this and have not withheld your only son. 📖

## Genesis 22:17
Therefore, I will certainly bless you. I will multiply your descendants like the sky's stars and the seashore's sand. Your descendants will possess their enemies' gates. 📖

## Genesis 22:18
All the earth's nations will be blessed through your offspring because you obeyed My voice.” 📖

## Genesis 22:19
Abraham returned to his servants. They left together for Beersheba. Abraham settled there. 📖

## Genesis 22:20
Later, Abraham was told, “Milcah has also borne sons to your brother Nahor. 📖

## Genesis 22:21
They are Uz the firstborn, his brother Buz, Kemuel, Aram's father, 📖

## Genesis 22:22
Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.” 📖

## Genesis 22:23
Bethuel became Rebekah's father. Milcah bore these eight sons to Abraham's brother Nahor. 📖

## Genesis 22:24
Nahor's concubine Reumah bore Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah. 📖
