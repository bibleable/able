## Genesis 30:1
Rachel had no children for Jacob and envied her sister. She told Jacob, “Give me children, or I will die!” 📖

## Genesis 30:2
Jacob became angry. He asked, “Am I in God's place? He has kept children from you.” 📖

## Genesis 30:3
She said, “Sleep with my servant Bilhah. She can bear children for me. I can build a family through her.” 📖

## Genesis 30:4
Rachel gave Bilhah to Jacob as a wife. He slept with her. 📖

## Genesis 30:5
Bilhah conceived and bore him a son. 📖

## Genesis 30:6
Rachel said, “God vindicated me. He heard my plea and gave me a son.” She named him Dan. 📖

## Genesis 30:7
Rachel's servant Bilhah conceived again. She bore Jacob a second son. 📖

## Genesis 30:8
Rachel said, “I struggled greatly and wrestled with my sister. I won.” She named him Naphtali. 📖

## Genesis 30:9
Leah saw that she stopped having children. She gave her servant Zilpah to Jacob as a wife. 📖

## Genesis 30:10
Leah's servant Zilpah bore Jacob a son. 📖

## Genesis 30:11
Leah said, “How fortunate!” She named him Gad. 📖

## Genesis 30:12
Zilpah, Leah's servant, bore Jacob a second son. 📖

## Genesis 30:13
Leah said, “I am so happy! The women call me happy.” She named him Asher. 📖

## Genesis 30:14
During wheat harvest, Reuben found mandrakes in the field. He brought them to his mother Leah. Rachel asked her, “Please give me some of your son's mandrakes.” 📖

## Genesis 30:15
Leah replied, “You took my husband. Was that not enough? Do you want my son's mandrakes too?” Rachel answered, “He can sleep with you tonight for your son's mandrakes.” 📖

## Genesis 30:16
Jacob returned from the field that evening. Leah met him and said, “Come with me. I hired you with my son's mandrakes.” He slept with her that night. 📖

## Genesis 30:17
God listened to Leah. She conceived and bore Jacob a fifth son. 📖

## Genesis 30:18
Leah said, “God rewarded me for giving my servant to my husband.” She named him Issachar. 📖

## Genesis 30:19
Leah conceived again. She bore Jacob a sixth son. 📖

## Genesis 30:20
She said, “God gave me a good gift. Now my husband will honor me because I bore him six sons.” She named him Zebulun. 📖

## Genesis 30:21
Leah later bore a daughter. She named her Dinah. 📖

## Genesis 30:22
God remembered Rachel. He listened to her and opened her womb. 📖

## Genesis 30:23
She conceived and bore a son. She said, “God took away my shame.” 📖

## Genesis 30:24
She named him Joseph. She said, “May the LORD add another son for me.” 📖

## Genesis 30:25
After Joseph's birth, Jacob told Laban, “Send me home to my country. 📖

## Genesis 30:26
Give me the wives and children I served you for. Let me leave. You know how hard I worked for you.” 📖

## Genesis 30:27
Laban replied, “Please stay if you are pleased with me. Through divination, I learned that the LORD blessed me because of you. 📖

## Genesis 30:28
Name your wages. I will pay them.” 📖

## Genesis 30:29
Jacob answered, “You know how I served you. Your livestock thrived in my care. 📖

## Genesis 30:30
You had little before I came. Now your wealth has multiplied. The LORD blessed you wherever I set foot. When can I provide for my own household? 📖

## Genesis 30:31
Laban asked, “What can I give you?” Jacob answered, “Give me nothing. If you do this one thing, I will keep tending your flocks. 📖

## Genesis 30:32
Let me remove every speckled or spotted sheep, every dark lamb, and every spotted or speckled goat from your flocks today. These will be my wages. 📖

## Genesis 30:33
My honesty will testify when you check my wages later. Any goat not speckled or spotted, or lamb not dark, in my possession will count as stolen.” 📖

## Genesis 30:34
Laban said, “Agreed. Let it be as you said.” 📖

## Genesis 30:35
That day, Laban removed all streaked or spotted male goats and speckled or spotted female goats, every one with white on it. He removed every dark lamb. He put them in his sons' care. 📖

## Genesis 30:36
He put a three-day journey between himself and Jacob. Jacob tended Laban's remaining flocks. 📖

## Genesis 30:37
Jacob took fresh poplar, almond, and plane branches. He peeled their bark to expose the white inner wood. 📖

## Genesis 30:38
He put the peeled branches in the watering troughs before the flocks coming to drink. The flocks came to drink while in heat. 📖

## Genesis 30:39
They mated before the branches. Their young were streaked, speckled, or spotted. 📖

## Genesis 30:40
Jacob separated the young. He made the rest face the streaked dark sheep in Laban's flocks. He kept his own stock separate from Laban's animals. 📖

## Genesis 30:41
When stronger females were in heat, Jacob put branches in the troughs where the animals could see them. He did this so they would breed before the branches. 📖

## Genesis 30:42
He did not put branches out for weak animals. The weaker animals went to Laban. The stronger ones went to Jacob. 📖

## Genesis 30:43
Jacob became extremely prosperous. He owned large flocks, female and male servants, camels, and donkeys. 📖
