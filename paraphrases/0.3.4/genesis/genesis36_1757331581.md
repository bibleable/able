## Genesis 36:1
This is the account of Esau, also called Edom. 📖

## Genesis 36:2
Esau married Canaanite women. They were Adah, Elon the Hittite's daughter, and Oholibamah, Anah's daughter and Zibeon the Hivite's granddaughter, 📖

## Genesis 36:3
and Basemath, Ishmael's daughter and Nebaioth's sister. 📖

## Genesis 36:4
Adah bore Eliphaz to Esau. Basemath bore Reuel. 📖

## Genesis 36:5
Oholibamah bore Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These were Esau's sons born in Canaan's land. 📖

## Genesis 36:6
Esau took his wives, sons, daughters, and whole household. He took his livestock, other animals, and all his property acquired in Canaan. He moved far from Jacob. 📖

## Genesis 36:7
Their possessions were too great to live together. The land could not support their livestock. 📖

## Genesis 36:8
Esau, also called Edom, settled around Mount Seir. 📖

## Genesis 36:9
This is Esau's account in Mount Seir's region. He was the Edomites' father. 📖

## Genesis 36:10
Esau's sons included Eliphaz, by his wife Adah, and Reuel, by his wife Basemath. 📖

## Genesis 36:11
Eliphaz's sons were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. 📖

## Genesis 36:12
Timna, Esau's son Eliphaz's concubine, bore Amalek. These are the grandsons of Esau's wife Adah. 📖

## Genesis 36:13
Reuel's sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. They were the grandsons of Esau's wife Basemath. 📖

## Genesis 36:14
Esau's wife Oholibamah, Anah's daughter and Zibeon's granddaughter, bore Jeush, Jalam, and Korah to him. 📖

## Genesis 36:15
These are the chiefs among Esau's sons. Esau's firstborn Eliphaz's sons included Chiefs Teman, Omar, Zepho, Kenaz, 📖

## Genesis 36:16
Korah, Gatam, and Amalek. These were Eliphaz's chiefs in Edom's land, Adah's grandsons. 📖

## Genesis 36:17
Esau's son Reuel's sons were Chiefs Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. They were Reuel's chiefs in Edom's land and Basemath's grandsons. Basemath was Esau's wife. 📖

## Genesis 36:18
Oholibamah's sons were Chiefs Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These chiefs descended from Esau's wife Oholibamah, Anah's daughter. 📖

## Genesis 36:19
These were Esau's sons and their chiefs. Esau is also called Edom. 📖

## Genesis 36:20
Seir the Horite's sons lived in the land. They were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 📖

## Genesis 36:21
Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. They were Horite chiefs descended from Seir in Edom's land. 📖

## Genesis 36:22
Lotan's sons were Hori and Hemam. Timna was Lotan's sister. 📖

## Genesis 36:23
Shobal's sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. 📖

## Genesis 36:24
Zibeon's sons were Aiah and Anah. This Anah found hot springs in the wilderness while tending his father Zibeon's donkeys. 📖

## Genesis 36:25
Anah's children were Dishon and his daughter Oholibamah. 📖

## Genesis 36:26
Dishon's sons were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. 📖

## Genesis 36:27
Ezer's sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. 📖

## Genesis 36:28
Dishan's sons were Uz and Aran. 📖

## Genesis 36:29
The Horite chiefs were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 📖

## Genesis 36:30
Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These were their chiefs by divisions in Seir's land. 📖

## Genesis 36:31
These kings ruled Edom before any king ruled the Israelites. 📖

## Genesis 36:32
Bela son of Beor ruled Edom. His city was Dinhabah. 📖

## Genesis 36:33
Bela died. Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah succeeded him. 📖

## Genesis 36:34
Jobab died. Husham from the Temanites' land succeeded him. 📖

## Genesis 36:35
Husham died. Hadad son of Bedad succeeded him. Hadad defeated Midian in Moab's country. His city was Avith. 📖

## Genesis 36:36
Hadad died. Samlah from Masrekah succeeded him. 📖

## Genesis 36:37
Samlah died. Shaul from Rehoboth on the Euphrates succeeded him. 📖

## Genesis 36:38
Shaul died. Baal-hanan son of Achbor succeeded him. 📖

## Genesis 36:39
Baal-hanan son of Achbor died. Hadad succeeded him. His city was Pau. His wife was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, the daughter of Me-zahab. 📖

## Genesis 36:40
Esau's chiefs by their families, regions, and names were Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, 📖

## Genesis 36:41
Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, 📖

## Genesis 36:42
Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, 📖

## Genesis 36:43
Magdiel, and Iram. These were Edom's chiefs by their settlements in the land they possessed. Esau was the Edomites' father. 📖
