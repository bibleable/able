## Mark 6:1
Jesus left there and went to His hometown with His disciples. 🚶
## Mark 6:2
On the Sabbath, He taught in the synagogue, and many listeners were amazed. They asked, “Where did He get these ideas? What wisdom is this? How does He do such miracles? ❓
## Mark 6:3
Is He not the carpenter, Mary’s son, and brother of James, Joses, Judas, and Simon? Are His sisters not here?” They took offense at Him. ⚠️
## Mark 6:4
Jesus said, “A prophet lacks honor only in his hometown, among his relatives, and in his own home.” 🏠
## Mark 6:5
He could do no miracles there, except to place His hands on a few sick people and heal them. ❤️
## Mark 6:6
Their unbelief amazed Him. He went through the villages and taught. 📖
## Mark 6:7
Jesus called the Twelve and sent them out in pairs. He gave them authority over unclean spirits. 🛡️
## Mark 6:8
He told them to take only a staff for the journey. They should take no bread, bag, or money in their belts. 🚶
## Mark 6:9
They should wear sandals, but take no second tunic. 👕
## Mark 6:10
He said, “When you enter a house, stay there until you leave the area. 🏠
## Mark 6:11
If people will not welcome or hear you, shake dust from your feet when leaving, as testimony against them.” 👣
## Mark 6:12
They went out and preached that people should repent. 📣
## Mark 6:13
They drove out many demons. They put oil on many sick people and healed them. ❤️
## Mark 6:14
King Herod heard, because Jesus’ name was well known. People said, “John the Baptist rose from the dead! That explains his miraculous powers.” 👑
## Mark 6:15
Others said, “He is Elijah.” Still others said, “He is a prophet like those of old.” 💬
## Mark 6:16
Herod heard and said, “John, whom I beheaded, rose from the dead!” ⚠️
## Mark 6:17
Herod had ordered John arrested, bound, and imprisoned because of Herodias. Herod married her, although she was his brother Philip’s wife. ⛓️
## Mark 6:18
John had told Herod, “You cannot lawfully have your brother’s wife!” ⚠️
## Mark 6:19
Herodias hated John and wanted him killed, but could not do it. 💔
## Mark 6:20
Herod feared and protected John, knowing he was righteous and holy. John’s words deeply puzzled him, yet he gladly listened. 👂
## Mark 6:21
Herod’s birthday gave her an opportunity. He held a feast for his nobles, military commanders, and Galilee’s leading men. 🍽️
## Mark 6:22
Herodias’s daughter danced and pleased Herod and his guests. The king said, “Ask for anything you want. I will give it to you.” 👑
## Mark 6:23
He swore, “I will give you whatever you ask, up to half my kingdom!” 💬
## Mark 6:24
She went out and asked her mother, “What should I ask for?” Her mother answered, “John the Baptist’s head.” ⚠️
## Mark 6:25
She hurried back and said, “Give me John the Baptist’s head on a platter immediately.” 💔
## Mark 6:26
The king was deeply sorrowful. Because of his oaths and guests, he did not want to refuse her. 👑
## Mark 6:27
The king immediately ordered John’s head brought in. He sent an executioner, who beheaded John in prison. ⚔️
## Mark 6:28
The executioner brought John’s head on a platter and gave it to the girl. She gave it to her mother. 💔
## Mark 6:29
John’s disciples heard, took his body, and laid it in a tomb. 🪦
## Mark 6:30
The apostles gathered around Jesus and reported everything they did and taught. 💬
## Mark 6:31
He said, “Come with Me privately to a place alone and rest awhile.” Many people came and went, leaving them no time to eat. 🚶
## Mark 6:32
They left by boat for a place by themselves. 🚤
## Mark 6:33
Many saw and recognized them leaving. People ran from all the towns and arrived ahead of them. 👥
## Mark 6:34
Jesus came ashore and saw the large crowd. He felt compassion because they were like sheep without a shepherd. He began teaching them many things. ❤️
## Mark 6:35
It grew late. His disciples said, “This place is deserted, and it is late. 🌆
## Mark 6:36
Send the people to nearby countryside and villages to buy food.” 🍞
## Mark 6:37
Jesus said, “Give them food yourselves.” They asked, “Should we spend two hundred denarii on bread for everyone?” ❓
## Mark 6:38
He said, “Go and see how many loaves you have.” They checked and answered, “Five, and two fish.” 🐟
## Mark 6:39
Jesus told them to seat everyone in groups on the green grass. 🌱
## Mark 6:40
They sat in groups of hundreds and fifties. 👥
## Mark 6:41
Jesus took the five loaves and two fish, looked toward heaven, and spoke a blessing. He broke the loaves and gave them to His disciples to distribute. He divided the two fish among everyone. 🍞
## Mark 6:42
Everyone ate and was satisfied. ❤️
## Mark 6:43
The disciples collected twelve baskets of bread and fish pieces. 🧺
## Mark 6:44
Five thousand men ate the loaves. 👥
## Mark 6:45
Jesus immediately made His disciples enter the boat and go ahead to Bethsaida. He stayed to dismiss the crowd. 🚤
## Mark 6:46
After saying goodbye, He went up the mountain to pray. 🙏
## Mark 6:47
At evening, the boat was in the sea’s middle. Jesus was alone on land. 🌊
## Mark 6:48
He saw them struggling to row against the wind. Around the fourth watch of the night, He walked toward them on the sea. He intended to pass them. 🌌
## Mark 6:49
They saw Him walking on the sea and cried out, thinking He was a ghost. 😨
## Mark 6:50
Everyone saw Him and was terrified. Jesus immediately said, “Take courage! It is I. Do not fear.” 🛡️
## Mark 6:51
He entered the boat, and the wind stopped. The disciples were completely amazed. 🕊️
## Mark 6:52
They had not understood about the loaves, because their hearts were hard. ⚠️
## Mark 6:53
They crossed to Gennesaret, landed, and secured the boat. 🚤
## Mark 6:54
As soon as they left the boat, people recognized Jesus. 👁️
## Mark 6:55
They ran through the region, carrying sick people on mats wherever they heard He was. 🛏️
## Mark 6:56
In villages, towns, and countryside, they laid sick people in marketplaces. They begged to touch just His cloak’s edge. Everyone who touched Him was healed. ❤️
