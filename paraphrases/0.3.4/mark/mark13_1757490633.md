## Mark 13:1
As Jesus left the temple, a disciple said, “Teacher, look at these magnificent stones and buildings!” 🏛️
## Mark 13:2
Jesus answered, “Do you see these great buildings? No stone will remain on another. Every stone will be thrown down.” ⚠️
## Mark 13:3
Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple. Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked privately: ⛰️
## Mark 13:4
“When will this happen? What sign will show that these things are about to be fulfilled?” ❓
## Mark 13:5
Jesus began, “Make sure no one deceives you. ⚠️
## Mark 13:6
Many will come in My name, claiming, ‘I am He.’ They will deceive many. 💬
## Mark 13:7
Do not fear reports or rumors of wars. These must happen, but the end is still ahead. ⚔️
## Mark 13:8
Nations and kingdoms will fight each other. Earthquakes and famines will occur in different places. These begin the birth pains. 🌍
## Mark 13:9
Be on guard. You will be handed to councils and beaten in synagogues. Because of Me, you will stand before governors and kings as witnesses. ⚖️
## Mark 13:10
The good news must first be announced to all nations. 🌍
## Mark 13:11
When you are arrested and handed over, do not worry beforehand about your words. Say what you receive then. The Holy Spirit will speak, rather than you. 🕊️
## Mark 13:12
Brothers will betray brothers to death, and fathers their children. Children will oppose their parents and have them killed. 💔
## Mark 13:13
Everyone will hate you because of My name. Whoever endures to the end will be saved. 🛡️
## Mark 13:14
When you see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not be, those in Judea must flee to the mountains. Let the reader understand. ⛰️
## Mark 13:15
Anyone on a roof must not enter the house to collect anything. 🏠
## Mark 13:16
Anyone in a field must not return for his cloak. 🌾
## Mark 13:17
Those days will be miserable for pregnant and nursing mothers! 💔
## Mark 13:18
Pray that this will not happen in winter. 🙏
## Mark 13:19
Those days will bring suffering unlike anything since God’s creation began. Nothing like it will ever happen again. ⚠️
## Mark 13:20
If the Lord had not shortened those days, no one would be saved. He shortened them for His elect, those He chose. 🛡️
## Mark 13:21
If anyone then says, ‘Here is the Christ!’ or, ‘There He is!’ do not believe it. ⚠️
## Mark 13:22
False Christs and prophets will show signs and wonders. They will try to deceive even the elect, if possible. 👁️
## Mark 13:23
Be on guard. I told you everything beforehand. ⚠️
## Mark 13:24
After that suffering, the sun will grow dark, and the moon will give no light. 🌌
## Mark 13:25
Stars will fall from the sky, and heaven’s powers will be shaken. ✨
## Mark 13:26
They will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory. ☁️
## Mark 13:27
He will send angels to gather His elect from the four winds, from earth’s ends to heaven’s ends. 👼
## Mark 13:28
Learn from the fig tree. When its branches soften and leaves grow, you know summer is near. 🌳
## Mark 13:29
Likewise, when these things happen, know He is near, at the door. 🚪
## Mark 13:30
I tell you the truth: this generation will not pass away before all these things happen. ⏳
## Mark 13:31
Heaven and earth will pass away. My words will never pass away. 📖
## Mark 13:32
Only the Father knows that day or hour. Neither heaven’s angels nor the Son knows. 🙏
## Mark 13:33
Be on guard and stay alert. You do not know when the appointed time comes. 👁️
## Mark 13:34
It is like a man leaving on a journey. He left his house, assigned each servant a task, and told the doorkeeper to watch. 🏠
## Mark 13:35
Keep watch. You do not know when the house’s master returns: evening, midnight, rooster’s crow, or morning. 🌌
## Mark 13:36
Otherwise, he could arrive suddenly and find you asleep. ⚠️
## Mark 13:37
I tell everyone what I tell you: keep watch!” 👁️
