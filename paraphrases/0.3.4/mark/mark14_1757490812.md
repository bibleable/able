## Mark 14:1
The Passover and Feast of Unleavened Bread were two days away. The chief priests and scribes sought a secret way to arrest and kill Jesus. ⚠️
## Mark 14:2
They said, “Avoid the feast, or the people might riot.” 👥
## Mark 14:3
Jesus reclined at table in Simon the Leper’s home in Bethany. A woman brought an alabaster jar of costly perfume made from pure nard. She broke it open and poured the perfume on Jesus’ head. 🏺
## Mark 14:4
Some became angry and asked each other, “Why waste this perfume? ❓
## Mark 14:5
It could sell for over three hundred denarii to help the poor.” They scolded her. 💰
## Mark 14:6
Jesus said, “Leave her alone. Why trouble her? She did something beautiful for Me. ❤️
## Mark 14:7
You will always have poor people with you and can help them whenever you want. You will not always have Me. 🤝
## Mark 14:8
She did what she could, anointing My body before My burial. 🏺
## Mark 14:9
I tell you the truth: wherever the good news is preached worldwide, her deed will also be told in her memory.” 📣
## Mark 14:10
Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, went to the chief priests to betray Jesus. 💔
## Mark 14:11
They were delighted and promised him money. Judas sought an opportunity to betray Jesus. 🪙
## Mark 14:12
On the Feast of Unleavened Bread’s first day, the Passover lamb was to be sacrificed. His disciples asked Jesus, “Where should we prepare Your Passover meal?” 🍞
## Mark 14:13
He sent two disciples, saying, “Go into the city. A man carrying a water jug will meet you. Follow him. 🏺
## Mark 14:14
At the house he enters, ask the owner, ‘Where is the Teacher’s guest room? He wants to eat the Passover there with His disciples.’ 🏠
## Mark 14:15
He will show you a large, furnished upper room, ready for use. Prepare for us there.” 🍽️
## Mark 14:16
They went into the city and found everything as Jesus said. They prepared the Passover. 🍞
## Mark 14:17
At evening, Jesus arrived with the Twelve. 🌆
## Mark 14:18
As they reclined and ate, Jesus said, “I tell you the truth: someone eating with Me will betray Me.” 💔
## Mark 14:19
They became sorrowful. One after another asked, “Surely it is not me?” ❓
## Mark 14:20
He answered, “One of the Twelve, dipping his hand into the bowl with Me. 🍽️
## Mark 14:21
The Son of Man will go as Scripture says. Woe to His betrayer! It would be better if that man had never been born.” ⚠️
## Mark 14:22
While they ate, Jesus took bread, blessed it, broke it, and gave it to the disciples. He said, “Take this. It is My body.” 🍞
## Mark 14:23
He took the cup, thanked God, and gave it to them. They all drank. 🍷
## Mark 14:24
He said, “This is My covenant blood, poured out for many. ❤️
## Mark 14:25
I tell you the truth: I will not drink the vine’s fruit again until I drink it anew in God’s kingdom.” 👑
## Mark 14:26
They sang a hymn and went to the Mount of Olives. 🎵
## Mark 14:27
Jesus said, “You will all fall away. Scripture says, ‘I will strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will scatter.’ 🐑
## Mark 14:28
After I rise, I will go ahead of you to Galilee.” 🌅
## Mark 14:29
Peter said, “Even if everyone falls away, I never will.” 💬
## Mark 14:30
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth: tonight, before the rooster crows twice, you will deny Me three times.” ⚠️
## Mark 14:31
Peter insisted, “Even if I must die with You, I will never deny You.” Everyone else said the same. 💬
## Mark 14:32
At Gethsemane, Jesus told His disciples, “Sit here while I pray.” 🙏
## Mark 14:33
He took Peter, James, and John with Him. He became deeply troubled and distressed. 💔
## Mark 14:34
He said, “My soul is filled with sorrow to the point of death. Stay here and watch.” 💔
## Mark 14:35
He went farther, fell to the ground, and prayed that the hour might pass from Him, if possible. 🙏
## Mark 14:36
He said, “Abba, Father, everything is possible for You. Take this cup away. Yet do what You will, rather than what I will.” 🙏
## Mark 14:37
Jesus returned and found them asleep. He asked, “Simon, are you asleep? Could you not watch for one hour? 👁️
## Mark 14:38
Watch and pray, so you will not enter temptation. The spirit is willing, but the body is weak.” 🙏
## Mark 14:39
He left again and prayed the same words. 🙏
## Mark 14:40
Jesus returned and again found them asleep because their eyes were heavy. They did not know how to answer. 😴
## Mark 14:41
On His third return, Jesus asked, “Still sleeping and resting? Enough! The hour has come. The Son of Man is betrayed into sinners’ hands. ⚠️
## Mark 14:42
Rise. Let us go. My betrayer approaches!” 🚶
## Mark 14:43
While Jesus spoke, Judas, one of the Twelve, arrived with a crowd carrying swords and clubs. The chief priests, scribes, and elders sent them. ⚔️
## Mark 14:44
The betrayer arranged a signal: “Arrest the One I kiss and lead Him away securely.” ⚠️
## Mark 14:45
He went straight to Jesus, said, “Rabbi!” and kissed Him. 💔
## Mark 14:46
The men seized and arrested Jesus. ⛓️
## Mark 14:47
A bystander drew his sword and cut off the high priest’s servant’s ear. ⚔️
## Mark 14:48
Jesus asked, “Did you bring swords and clubs to arrest Me like an outlaw? ❓
## Mark 14:49
I taught among you daily in the temple courts, and you never arrested Me. This happened to fulfill Scripture.” 📖
## Mark 14:50
Everyone abandoned Him and fled. 💔
## Mark 14:51
A young man followed Jesus wearing only a linen cloth. They seized him, 👕
## Mark 14:52
but he escaped the cloth and ran away naked. 🚶
## Mark 14:53
They led Jesus to the high priest. All the chief priests, elders, and scribes gathered. 👥
## Mark 14:54
Peter followed at a distance into the high priest’s courtyard. He sat with the officers and warmed himself by the fire. 🔥
## Mark 14:55
The chief priests and entire Sanhedrin sought testimony to put Jesus to death. They found none. ⚖️
## Mark 14:56
Many testified falsely against Jesus, but their statements disagreed. ⚠️
## Mark 14:57
Some stood and falsely testified: 💬
## Mark 14:58
“We heard Him say, ‘I will destroy this temple made by human hands. In three days, I will build another made without hands.’” 🏛️
## Mark 14:59
Even these statements disagreed. ⚠️
## Mark 14:60
The high priest stood before them and asked Jesus, “Will You not answer? What testimony do these men give against You?” ❓
## Mark 14:61
Jesus remained silent. The high priest asked again, “Are You the Christ, the Blessed One’s Son?” ❓
## Mark 14:62
Jesus answered, “I am. You will see the Son of Man seated at Power’s right hand and coming with heaven’s clouds.” ☁️
## Mark 14:63
The high priest tore his clothes and said, “Why do we need more witnesses? ⚠️
## Mark 14:64
You heard the blasphemy. What is your verdict?” Everyone condemned Him as deserving death. ⚖️
## Mark 14:65
Some spat on Him, covered His eyes, punched Him, and said, “Prophesy!” The officers received Him with slaps to His face. 💔
## Mark 14:66
Peter was below in the courtyard when a high priest’s servant girl came down. 🏠
## Mark 14:67
She saw him warming himself, looked closely at Peter, and said, “You were with Jesus the Nazarene.” 👁️
## Mark 14:68
He denied it, saying, “I do not know or understand your words.” He went to the gateway, and the rooster crowed. 🐓
## Mark 14:69
The servant girl saw him there and again told those nearby, “This man is one of them.” 💬
## Mark 14:70
He denied it again. Later, those nearby told Peter, “You must be one of them because you are a Galilean.” ⚠️
## Mark 14:71
He began cursing and swearing, “I do not know this man you mention!” 💬
## Mark 14:72
The rooster immediately crowed a second time. Peter remembered Jesus’ words: “Before the rooster crows twice, you will deny Me three times.” He broke down and wept. 💧
