## Mark 9:1
Jesus said, “I tell you the truth: some standing here will not die before seeing God’s kingdom arrive with power.” 👑
## Mark 9:2
Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John up a high mountain by themselves. There His appearance changed before them. ⛰️
## Mark 9:3
His clothes became shining white. No launderer on earth could bleach clothes that white. ✨
## Mark 9:4
Elijah and Moses appeared and spoke with Jesus. 👥
## Mark 9:5
Peter told Jesus, “Rabbi, it is good to be here. Let us build three shelters: one for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.” ⛺
## Mark 9:6
They were so afraid that Peter did not know what else to say. 😨
## Mark 9:7
A cloud surrounded them. A voice from it said, “This is My beloved Son. Listen to Him!” ☁️
## Mark 9:8
They suddenly looked around and saw only Jesus with them. 👁️
## Mark 9:9
As they descended, Jesus warned them to tell no one what they saw until the Son of Man rose from the dead. ⚠️
## Mark 9:10
They kept this private and discussed what rising from the dead meant. 💬
## Mark 9:11
They asked Jesus, “Why do the scribes say Elijah must come first?” ❓
## Mark 9:12
He answered, “Elijah does come first and restores everything. Why then does Scripture say the Son of Man must suffer greatly and be rejected? 📖
## Mark 9:13
I tell you, Elijah already came. They did whatever they wanted to him, just as Scripture says about him.” ⚠️
## Mark 9:14
They returned to the other disciples and saw a large crowd around them. Scribes were arguing with the disciples. 👥
## Mark 9:15
Everyone saw Jesus, was filled with awe, and ran to greet Him. 🚶
## Mark 9:16
He asked, “What are you arguing about with them?” ❓
## Mark 9:17
A man in the crowd answered, “Teacher, I brought my son. A spirit makes him unable to speak. 💔
## Mark 9:18
It seizes him and throws him down. He foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and becomes stiff. I asked Your disciples to drive it out, but they could not.” ⚠️
## Mark 9:19
Jesus answered, “Unbelieving generation! How long must I stay with you? How long must I bear with you? Bring the boy to Me.” 🙏
## Mark 9:20
They brought him. When the spirit saw Jesus, it immediately made the boy shake violently. He fell and rolled on the ground, foaming at the mouth. 💔
## Mark 9:21
Jesus asked his father, “How long has this happened?” He answered, “Since childhood. ❓
## Mark 9:22
It often throws him into fire or water to kill him. If You can do anything, have compassion and help us.” 🙏
## Mark 9:23
Jesus repeated, “‘If You can’? Everything is possible for someone who believes!” 🙏
## Mark 9:24
The father immediately cried, “I believe! Help my unbelief!” 💔
## Mark 9:25
Jesus saw the crowd running up and rebuked the unclean spirit. He said, “Deaf and mute spirit, I command you to leave him and never return!” 🛡️
## Mark 9:26
The spirit screamed, shook the boy violently, and left. The boy looked like a corpse, and many said, “He is dead.” ⚠️
## Mark 9:27
Jesus took his hand and helped him rise. He stood up. ❤️
## Mark 9:28
Inside the house, His disciples privately asked Jesus, “Why could we not drive it out?” ❓
## Mark 9:29
Jesus answered, “This kind can only come out through prayer.” 🙏
## Mark 9:30
They left and traveled through Galilee. Jesus wanted no one to know, 🚶
## Mark 9:31
because He was teaching His disciples. He said, “The Son of Man will be handed over to people who will kill Him. After three days, He will rise.” ✝️
## Mark 9:32
They did not understand and feared asking Him about it. ❓
## Mark 9:33
They came to Capernaum. In the house, Jesus asked, “What did you discuss on the way?” 🏠
## Mark 9:34
They remained silent. On the way, they argued about who was greatest. ⚠️
## Mark 9:35
Jesus sat, called the Twelve, and said, “Anyone wanting to be first must be last of all and serve everyone.” 🤝
## Mark 9:36
He placed a little child among them. Holding the child, He said: 👶
## Mark 9:37
“Anyone welcoming such a child in My name welcomes Me. Anyone welcoming Me also welcomes the One who sent Me.” ❤️
## Mark 9:38
John said, “Teacher, we saw someone driving out demons in Your name. We tried to stop him because he does not accompany us.” 💬
## Mark 9:39
Jesus answered, “Do not stop him. No one doing a miracle in My name can immediately speak evil of Me. 🛡️
## Mark 9:40
Anyone not against us is for us. 🤝
## Mark 9:41
If anyone gives you a cup of water because you bear Christ’s name, he will never lose his reward. I tell you the truth. 💧
## Mark 9:42
If anyone causes a little one who believes in Me to stumble, being thrown into the sea would be better for him. This would be with a large millstone hung around his neck. ⚠️
## Mark 9:43
If your hand causes you to sin, cut it off. Entering life crippled is better than having two hands and entering hell’s fire that cannot be put out. 🔥
