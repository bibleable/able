## Mark 7:1
Pharisees and some scribes from Jerusalem gathered around Jesus. 👥
## Mark 7:2
They saw some disciples eating with unclean hands, meaning unwashed hands. 👐
## Mark 7:3
The Pharisees and all the Jews follow the elders’ tradition. They do not eat before washing their hands ceremonially. 💧
## Mark 7:4
They wash before eating after returning from the market. They follow many other traditions, including washing cups, pitchers, kettles, and dining couches. 🏺
## Mark 7:5
The Pharisees and scribes asked Jesus, “Why do Your disciples ignore the elders’ tradition and eat with unclean hands?” ❓
## Mark 7:6
Jesus answered, “Isaiah correctly prophesied about you hypocrites. He wrote, ‘These people honor Me with their lips, but their hearts are far from Me. 📖
## Mark 7:7
Their worship is useless. They teach human rules as doctrine.’ ⚠️
## Mark 7:8
You ignore God’s command to keep human tradition.” ⚖️
## Mark 7:9
He continued, “You neatly reject God’s command to maintain your tradition. ⚠️
## Mark 7:10
Moses said, ‘Honor your father and mother,’ and, ‘Anyone cursing his father or mother must be put to death.’ 📖
## Mark 7:11
But you say that if a man tells his parents, ‘What you would receive from me is Corban’... Corban means a gift dedicated to God. 🎁
## Mark 7:12
You then no longer allow him to do anything for his father or mother. 💔
## Mark 7:13
Your inherited tradition cancels God’s word. You do this in many other matters.” ⚠️
## Mark 7:14
Jesus called the crowd again and said, “Everyone, listen and understand. 👂
## Mark 7:15
Nothing entering a person from outside makes him unclean. What comes out of him makes him unclean.” ⚖️
## Mark 7:16
*BSB source note: The local BSB has no text at this verse address.* 📖
## Mark 7:17
Jesus left the crowd and entered the house. His disciples asked about the parable. 🏠
## Mark 7:18
He asked, “Are you still so dull? Do you not understand? Nothing entering a person from outside makes him unclean. ❓
## Mark 7:19
It enters his stomach, rather than his heart, and then leaves his body.” Thus, all foods are clean. 🍞
## Mark 7:20
He continued, “What comes out of a person makes him unclean. ⚖️
## Mark 7:21
From within people’s hearts come evil thoughts, sexual immorality, theft, murder, adultery, 💔
## Mark 7:22
greed, wickedness, deceit, wild immoral behavior, envy, slander, pride, and foolishness. ⚠️
## Mark 7:23
All these evils come from within and make a person unclean.” ⚖️
## Mark 7:24
Jesus went to Tyre’s region and entered a house. He wanted no one to know He was there, but could not remain unnoticed. 🏠
## Mark 7:25
A woman whose little daughter had an unclean spirit soon heard about Him. She came and fell at His feet. 🙏
## Mark 7:26
She was Greek, of Syrophoenician origin. She kept asking Jesus to drive the demon from her daughter. 💔
## Mark 7:27
He said, “Let the children eat their fill first. It is not right to take their bread and throw it to dogs.” 🍞
## Mark 7:28
She answered, “Yes, Lord. Even dogs under the table eat the children’s crumbs.” 🙏
## Mark 7:29
Jesus said, “Because of your answer, you may go. The demon left your daughter.” ❤️
## Mark 7:30
She went home and found her child on the bed. The demon was gone. 🏠
## Mark 7:31
Jesus left Tyre’s region, traveled through Sidon, and reached the Sea of Galilee in the Decapolis region. 🚶
## Mark 7:32
People brought Him a deaf man who could barely speak. They begged Jesus to put His hand on him. 🙏
## Mark 7:33
Jesus took him aside, away from the crowd. He put His fingers in the man’s ears, then spat and touched his tongue. ❤️
## Mark 7:34
Looking toward heaven, He sighed deeply and said, “Ephphatha!” This means, “Be opened!” 🙏
## Mark 7:35
The man’s ears immediately opened, and his tongue was released. He began speaking clearly. ✨
## Mark 7:36
Jesus told them to tell no one. The more He ordered this, the more widely they spread the news. 📣
## Mark 7:37
They were completely amazed and said, “He does everything well! He makes deaf people hear and those unable to speak talk!” ❤️
