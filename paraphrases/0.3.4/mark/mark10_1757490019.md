## Mark 10:1
Jesus left and went to Judea’s region beyond the Jordan. Crowds gathered again, and He taught as usual. 📖
## Mark 10:2
Pharisees tested Him by asking, “Can a man lawfully divorce his wife?” ❓
## Mark 10:3
He answered, “What did Moses command?” 📖
## Mark 10:4
They said, “Moses allowed a man to write a divorce certificate and send his wife away.” 📜
## Mark 10:5
Jesus said, “Moses wrote this command because of your hard hearts. ⚠️
## Mark 10:6
From creation’s beginning, God made them male and female. 🌍
## Mark 10:7
For this reason, a man will leave his father and mother and unite with his wife. 💍
## Mark 10:8
The two will become one flesh. They are no longer two, but one flesh. 🤝
## Mark 10:9
No one should separate what God joined.” 💍
## Mark 10:10
Back inside the house, the disciples asked Jesus about this. 🏠
## Mark 10:11
He answered, “A man who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery against her. ⚠️
## Mark 10:12
A woman who divorces her husband and marries another man commits adultery.” ⚠️
## Mark 10:13
People brought little children for Jesus to place His hands on them. The disciples rebuked those bringing them. 👶
## Mark 10:14
Jesus saw and became angry. He said, “Let the little children come to Me. Do not stop them. God’s kingdom belongs to such as these. ❤️
## Mark 10:15
I tell you the truth: anyone who does not receive God’s kingdom like a little child will never enter it.” 🙏
## Mark 10:16
He held the children, placed His hands on them, and blessed them. ❤️
## Mark 10:17
As Jesus left, a man ran up and knelt. He asked, “Good Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?” 🙏
## Mark 10:18
Jesus answered, “Why call Me good? Only God is good. ❓
## Mark 10:19
You know the commands: do not murder, commit adultery, steal, give false testimony, or cheat others. Honor your father and mother.” 📖
## Mark 10:20
He answered, “Teacher, I kept all these from my youth.” 💬
## Mark 10:21
Jesus looked at him with love. He said, “You lack one thing. Sell everything and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then follow Me.” ❤️
## Mark 10:22
The words saddened the man. He left sorrowfully because he had great wealth. 💔
## Mark 10:23
Jesus looked around and told His disciples, “Entering God’s kingdom is hard for rich people!” 💰
## Mark 10:24
The disciples were amazed. Jesus continued, “Children, entering God’s kingdom is hard! ❓
## Mark 10:25
A camel passes through a needle’s eye more easily than a rich man enters God’s kingdom.” 🐪
## Mark 10:26
Even more amazed, they asked each other, “Who then can be saved?” ❓
## Mark 10:27
Jesus looked at them and answered, “This is impossible for people, but everything is possible for God.” 🙏
## Mark 10:28
Peter said, “We left everything and followed You.” 🚶
## Mark 10:29
Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth. This concerns anyone who left home, brothers, sisters, mother, father, children, or fields for Me and the good news. 🏠
## Mark 10:30
He will receive a hundred times as much now: houses, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and fields, along with persecution. In the coming age, he will receive eternal life. 🌅
## Mark 10:31
Many who are first will be last, and the last will be first.” 🔄
## Mark 10:32
Jesus walked ahead on the road to Jerusalem. The disciples were amazed, and those following were afraid. He took the Twelve aside again and told what would happen: 🚶
## Mark 10:33
“We are going to Jerusalem. The Son of Man will be handed to the chief priests and scribes. They will sentence Him to death and hand Him to the Gentiles. ⚖️
## Mark 10:34
They will mock Him, spit on Him, whip Him, and kill Him. After three days, He will rise.” ✝️
## Mark 10:35
James and John, Zebedee’s sons, came to Jesus. They said, “Teacher, do whatever we ask.” 💬
## Mark 10:36
He asked, “What do you want Me to do?” ❓
## Mark 10:37
They answered, “In Your glory, let one of us sit at Your right and the other at Your left.” 👑
## Mark 10:38
Jesus answered, “You do not know what you ask. Can you drink My cup or receive the baptism I will receive?” ❓
## Mark 10:39
They answered, “We can.” Jesus said, “You will drink My cup and receive My baptism. 💬
## Mark 10:40
But seats at My right or left are not Mine to grant. They belong to those for whom they were prepared.” 👑
## Mark 10:41
The other ten heard and became angry with James and John. ⚠️
## Mark 10:42
Jesus gathered them and said, “The Gentiles’ recognized rulers dominate them, and their superiors exercise authority over them. 👑
## Mark 10:43
It must be different among you. Anyone wanting greatness among you must serve you. 🤝
## Mark 10:44
Anyone wanting first place must be everyone’s slave. 🙏
## Mark 10:45
Even the Son of Man came to serve rather than be served. He came to give His life as a ransom for many.” ✝️
## Mark 10:46
They reached Jericho. As Jesus, His disciples, and a large crowd left, the blind beggar Bartimaeus sat beside the road. He was Timaeus’s son. 🚶
## Mark 10:47
He heard Jesus of Nazareth was passing and shouted, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” 🙏
## Mark 10:48
Many told him to be silent. He shouted louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!” 📣
## Mark 10:49
Jesus stopped and said, “Call him.” They called the blind man, saying, “Take courage! Stand up! He calls you.” ❤️
## Mark 10:50
Bartimaeus threw off his cloak, jumped up, and came to Jesus. 🚶
## Mark 10:51
Jesus asked, “What do you want Me to do?” He answered, “Rabboni, let me see again.” 🙏
## Mark 10:52
Jesus said, “Go. Your faith healed you.” He immediately saw again and followed Jesus on the road. ❤️
