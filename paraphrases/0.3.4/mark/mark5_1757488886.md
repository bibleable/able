## Mark 5:1
They crossed the sea and reached the Gerasenes’ region. 🌊
## Mark 5:2
As Jesus left the boat, a man with an unclean spirit came from the tombs to meet Him. ⚠️
## Mark 5:3
The man lived in the tombs. No one could restrain him, even with chains. ⛓️
## Mark 5:4
People often chained and shackled him, but he broke the chains and shattered the shackles. No one was strong enough to control him. ⚠️
## Mark 5:5
Night and day, he cried out among the tombs and mountains and cut himself with stones. 💔
## Mark 5:6
He saw Jesus from far away, ran to Him, and fell on his knees. 🙏
## Mark 5:7
He shouted, “Jesus, Son of the Most High God, what do You want with me? Before God, I beg You not to torture me!” 😨
## Mark 5:8
Jesus had already said, “Unclean spirit, come out of this man!” 🛡️
## Mark 5:9
Jesus asked, “What is your name?” He answered, “Legion, because we are many.” ❓
## Mark 5:10
He repeatedly begged Jesus not to send them out of the region. 🙏
## Mark 5:11
A large herd of pigs fed on the nearby hillside. 🐖
## Mark 5:12
The demons begged Jesus, “Send us into the pigs.” ⚠️
## Mark 5:13
Jesus allowed it. The unclean spirits entered the pigs. About two thousand pigs rushed down the steep bank into the sea and drowned. 🌊
## Mark 5:14
The herders ran to report this in town and countryside. People came to see what happened. 📣
## Mark 5:15
They came to Jesus and saw the man formerly possessed by the legion of demons. He sat dressed and thinking clearly. They were afraid. 😨
## Mark 5:16
Witnesses described what happened to the man and the pigs. 💬
## Mark 5:17
The people began begging Jesus to leave their region. 🚤
## Mark 5:18
As Jesus entered the boat, the man freed from demons begged to accompany Him. 🙏
## Mark 5:19
Jesus refused and said, “Go home to your people. Tell them how much the Lord did for you and how He showed you mercy.” ❤️
## Mark 5:20
The man went throughout the Decapolis, announcing how much Jesus did for him. Everyone was amazed. 📣
## Mark 5:21
Jesus crossed back by boat. A large crowd gathered around Him beside the sea. 🌊
## Mark 5:22
Jairus, a synagogue leader, came and fell at Jesus’ feet. 🙏
## Mark 5:23
He pleaded urgently, “My little daughter is near death. Please put Your hands on her, so she will be healed and live.” 💔
## Mark 5:24
Jesus went with him. A large crowd followed and pressed around Him. 👥
## Mark 5:25
A woman there had suffered bleeding for twelve years. 💔
## Mark 5:26
She suffered greatly under many doctors and spent everything she had. Nothing helped, and she only became worse. ⚠️
## Mark 5:27
She heard about Jesus, came behind Him through the crowd, and touched His cloak. 👕
## Mark 5:28
She kept saying, “If I only touch His clothes, I will be healed.” 🙏
## Mark 5:29
Her bleeding immediately stopped. She felt in her body that she was healed. ❤️
## Mark 5:30
Jesus immediately knew power had gone out from Him. He turned and asked the crowd, “Who touched My clothes?” ❓
## Mark 5:31
His disciples answered, “The crowd presses against You, yet You ask, ‘Who touched Me?’” 👥
## Mark 5:32
He kept looking around for the person who did it. 👁️
## Mark 5:33
The woman knew what happened to her. Trembling with fear, she fell before Him and told the whole truth. 🙏
## Mark 5:34
Jesus said, “Daughter, your faith healed you. Go in peace, free from your suffering.” ❤️
## Mark 5:35
While He spoke, messengers arrived from Jairus’s house. They said, “Your daughter died. Why bother the Teacher anymore?” 💔
## Mark 5:36
Jesus heard and told Jairus, “Do not fear. Only believe.” 🙏
## Mark 5:37
He allowed only Peter, James, and James’s brother John to accompany Him. 👥
## Mark 5:38
At the synagogue leader’s house, Jesus saw confusion and people loudly weeping and wailing. 💧
## Mark 5:39
He entered and asked, “Why this confusion and weeping? The child is asleep, rather than dead.” 💬
## Mark 5:40
They laughed at Him. He put everyone outside, then took the child’s parents and His companions into her room. 🏠
## Mark 5:41
Jesus took her hand and said, “Talitha koum!” This means, “Little girl, I tell you, get up!” ❤️
## Mark 5:42
The twelve-year-old girl immediately stood and walked around. They were completely amazed. ✨
## Mark 5:43
Jesus strictly ordered them to tell no one. He told them to give her food. 🍞
