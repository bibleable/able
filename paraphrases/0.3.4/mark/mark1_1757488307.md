## Mark 1:1
This begins the good news of Jesus Christ, God’s Son. 📖
## Mark 1:2
The prophet Isaiah wrote, “I will send My messenger ahead of You to prepare Your way.” 📜
## Mark 1:3
“A voice calls in the wilderness, ‘Prepare the Lord’s way. Make straight paths for Him.’” 🏜️
## Mark 1:4
John the Baptist appeared in the wilderness. He preached a baptism of repentance for forgiveness of sins. 💧
## Mark 1:5
People from all Jerusalem and Judea’s countryside came to him. They confessed their sins, and he baptized them in the Jordan River. 🌊
## Mark 1:6
John wore camel’s hair and a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey. 🍯
## Mark 1:7
He announced, “Someone stronger than me will come after me. I am not worthy to bend down and untie His sandal straps. 🙏
## Mark 1:8
I baptize you with water. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.” 🕊️
## Mark 1:9
During those days, Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee. John baptized Him in the Jordan. 💧
## Mark 1:10
Immediately after coming out of the water, Jesus saw the heavens break open. The Spirit came down on Him like a dove. 🕊️
## Mark 1:11
A voice from heaven said, “You are My beloved Son. You greatly please Me.” ❤️
## Mark 1:12
The Spirit immediately drove Jesus into the wilderness. 🏜️
## Mark 1:13
He stayed there forty days, tempted by Satan. He was with wild animals, and angels served Him. 👼
## Mark 1:14
After John’s arrest, Jesus went to Galilee and announced God’s good news. 📣
## Mark 1:15
He said, “The time is fulfilled, and God’s kingdom is near. Repent and believe the good news!” 🙏
## Mark 1:16
Jesus walked beside the Sea of Galilee and saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were fishermen casting a net into the sea. 🎣
## Mark 1:17
Jesus said, “Follow Me. I will make you fishers of people.” 🎣
## Mark 1:18
They immediately left their nets and followed Him. 🚶
## Mark 1:19
A little farther on, He saw James son of Zebedee and his brother John repairing nets in a boat. 🚤
## Mark 1:20
Jesus immediately called them. They left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired workers and followed Him. 🚶
## Mark 1:21
Jesus and His companions went to Capernaum. On the Sabbath, Jesus immediately entered the synagogue and taught. 📖
## Mark 1:22
His teaching amazed the people. He taught with authority, unlike the scribes. 👂
## Mark 1:23
Suddenly, a man with an unclean spirit shouted in the synagogue: ⚠️
## Mark 1:24
“Jesus of Nazareth, what do You want with us? Did You come to destroy us? I know You are God’s Holy One!” 😨
## Mark 1:25
Jesus rebuked the spirit and said, “Be silent! Come out of him!” 🛡️
## Mark 1:26
The unclean spirit made the man shake violently and came out with a loud scream. ⚠️
## Mark 1:27
Everyone was amazed and asked each other, “What is this? A new teaching with authority! Even unclean spirits obey His commands!” ❓
## Mark 1:28
News about Jesus quickly spread throughout Galilee. 📣
## Mark 1:29
Immediately after leaving the synagogue, Jesus and His companions went with James and John to Simon and Andrew’s home. 🏠
## Mark 1:30
Simon’s mother-in-law lay sick with a fever. They immediately told Jesus. 🌡️
## Mark 1:31
He took her hand and helped her stand. The fever left, and she began serving them. ❤️
## Mark 1:32
After sunset that evening, people brought Jesus all the sick and those possessed by demons. 🌆
## Mark 1:33
The whole town gathered at the door. 🏠
## Mark 1:34
He healed many people with different diseases and drove out many demons. He would not let the demons speak because they knew Him. 🛡️
## Mark 1:35
Early the next morning, while still dark, Jesus rose and went to a place alone to pray. 🙏
## Mark 1:36
Simon and his companions searched for Him. 🔎
## Mark 1:37
When they found Him, they said, “Everyone is looking for You!” 📣
## Mark 1:38
Jesus answered, “Let us go to nearby towns so I can preach there too. That is why I came.” 🚶
## Mark 1:39
He traveled throughout Galilee, preaching in their synagogues and driving out demons. 📖
## Mark 1:40
A man with leprosy knelt before Jesus and begged, “If You are willing, You can make me clean.” 🙏
## Mark 1:41
With compassion, Jesus reached out and touched him. He said, “I am willing. Be clean!” ❤️
## Mark 1:42
The leprosy immediately left, and the man was clean. ✨
## Mark 1:43
Jesus immediately sent him away with a stern warning: ⚠️
## Mark 1:44
“Tell no one. Show yourself to the priest and offer what Moses commanded for your cleansing, as a testimony to them.” 🙏
## Mark 1:45
Instead, the man openly spread the news. Jesus could no longer enter towns openly and stayed in places away from people. Yet people came from everywhere to Him. 📣
