## Mark 12:1
Jesus told them parables: “A man planted a vineyard, surrounded it with a wall, dug a wine vat, and built a watchtower. He rented it to tenants and went on a journey. 🍇
## Mark 12:2
At harvest, he sent a servant to collect his share of fruit from the tenants. 🌾
## Mark 12:3
They seized and beat the servant, then sent him away with nothing. ⚠️
## Mark 12:4
He sent another servant. They hit his head and treated him shamefully. 💔
## Mark 12:5
He sent another, whom they killed. He sent many more. They beat some and killed others. ⚔️
## Mark 12:6
He had one beloved son left and finally sent him. He said, ‘They will respect my son.’ ❤️
## Mark 12:7
The tenants said, ‘This is the heir. Let us kill him and take the inheritance.’ ⚠️
## Mark 12:8
They seized and killed the son and threw him outside the vineyard. 💔
## Mark 12:9
What will the owner do? He will come, kill those tenants, and give the vineyard to others. ⚖️
## Mark 12:10
Have you never read this Scripture? ‘The stone the builders rejected became the cornerstone. 🪨
## Mark 12:11
The Lord did this, and it is wonderful in our eyes.’” ✨
## Mark 12:12
The leaders wanted to arrest Jesus because they knew the parable concerned them. They feared the crowd, so they left Him. ⚠️
## Mark 12:13
They later sent Pharisees and Herodians to trap Jesus through His words. 💬
## Mark 12:14
They said, “Teacher, You speak truth, seek no favor, and show no partiality. You teach God’s way truthfully. Is paying Caesar’s taxes lawful? Should we pay?” ❓
## Mark 12:15
Jesus recognized their hypocrisy and asked, “Why test Me? Bring Me a denarius to examine.” 🪙
## Mark 12:16
They brought one. He asked, “Whose image and inscription are these?” They answered, “Caesar’s.” ❓
## Mark 12:17
Jesus said, “Give Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and God what belongs to God.” They were amazed. ⚖️
## Mark 12:18
Sadducees, who deny resurrection, came and asked Jesus: ❓
## Mark 12:19
“Teacher, Moses wrote about a man whose brother dies with a wife but no children. The man must marry his brother’s widow and have children for him. 📖
## Mark 12:20
There were seven brothers. The first married and died without children. 👥
## Mark 12:21
The second married the widow and died without children. The third did the same. 💔
## Mark 12:22
None of the seven left children. Finally, the woman died. 🪦
## Mark 12:23
At the resurrection, whose wife will she be? All seven married her.” ❓
## Mark 12:24
Jesus answered, “Are you not mistaken because you know neither Scripture nor God’s power? 📖
## Mark 12:25
When the dead rise, they will neither marry nor be given in marriage. They will be like heaven’s angels. 👼
## Mark 12:26
Concerning the dead rising, did you not read the burning bush account in Moses’ book? God said, ‘I am Abraham’s God, Isaac’s God, and Jacob’s God.’ 📖
## Mark 12:27
He is the God of the living, rather than the dead. You are badly mistaken!” ⚠️
## Mark 12:28
A scribe heard their discussion and noticed Jesus answered well. He asked, “Which command is most important?” ❓
## Mark 12:29
Jesus answered, “The most important is, ‘Israel, listen. The Lord our God, the Lord is One. 🙏
## Mark 12:30
Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength.’ ❤️
## Mark 12:31
The second is, ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ No command is greater than these.” 🤝
## Mark 12:32
The scribe answered, “Correct, Teacher. God is One, and there is no other. 🙏
## Mark 12:33
Love Him with all your heart, understanding, and strength. Love your neighbor as yourself. This matters more than all burnt offerings and sacrifices.” ❤️
## Mark 12:34
Jesus saw his wise answer and said, “You are not far from God’s kingdom.” No one dared ask Him more questions. 👑
## Mark 12:35
Teaching in the temple courts, Jesus asked, “How can scribes say the Christ is David’s Son? ❓
## Mark 12:36
David spoke through the Holy Spirit: ‘The Lord told my Lord, “Sit at My right until I put Your enemies under Your feet.”’ 🕊️
## Mark 12:37
David calls Him Lord. How then is He David’s son?” The large crowd listened gladly. 👂
## Mark 12:38
Jesus taught, “Beware of scribes. They like long robes and marketplace greetings. ⚠️
## Mark 12:39
They want the best synagogue seats and places of honor at feasts. 🍽️
## Mark 12:40
They cheat widows out of their homes and make long prayers for show. They will receive greater condemnation.” ⚖️
## Mark 12:41
Jesus sat opposite the treasury and watched people contribute. Many rich people gave large amounts. 🪙
## Mark 12:42
A poor widow gave two small copper coins, worth a small part of a denarius. 🪙
## Mark 12:43
Jesus called His disciples and said, “I tell you the truth: this poor widow gave more than everyone else. ❤️
## Mark 12:44
They gave from their extra wealth. In her poverty, she gave everything she had to live on.” 🙏
