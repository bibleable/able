## Mark 4:1
Jesus taught beside the sea again. A large crowd gathered, so He sat in a boat while the people stood along the shore. 🚤
## Mark 4:2
He taught many things through parables and said: 📖
## Mark 4:3
“Listen! A farmer went out to sow seed. 🌱
## Mark 4:4
Some seed fell on the path as he sowed. Birds came and ate it. 🐦
## Mark 4:5
Some fell on rocky ground with little soil. It grew quickly because the soil was shallow. 🪨
## Mark 4:6
The rising sun scorched the seedlings. They dried up because they had no root. ☀️
## Mark 4:7
Other seed fell among thorns. The thorns grew and choked the seedlings, which produced no crop. 🌵
## Mark 4:8
Other seed fell on good soil and grew. It produced a crop thirty, sixty, or a hundred times the seed sown.” 🌾
## Mark 4:9
Jesus said, “Anyone with ears to hear should listen.” 👂
## Mark 4:10
When Jesus was alone with the Twelve and those around Him, they asked about the parable. ❓
## Mark 4:11
He answered, “You received the mystery of God’s kingdom. Everything is expressed in parables to those outside. 📖
## Mark 4:12
This is so that ‘they may keep seeing without perceiving and keep hearing without understanding. Otherwise, they might turn and be forgiven.’” ⚠️
## Mark 4:13
Jesus asked, “Do you not understand this parable? How will you understand any parable? ❓
## Mark 4:14
The farmer sows the word. 🌱
## Mark 4:15
Some people are like seed on the path where the word is sown. As soon as they hear, Satan takes the word sown in them. ⚠️
## Mark 4:16
Some are like seed on rocky ground. They hear the word and immediately receive it with joy. 🪨
## Mark 4:17
They have no root and last only a short time. Trouble or persecution because of the word quickly makes them fall away. ⚠️
## Mark 4:18
Others are like seed among thorns. They hear the word, 🌵
## Mark 4:19
but life’s worries, wealth’s deceit, and desires for other things choke it. It produces no fruit. 💔
## Mark 4:20
Others are like seed on good soil. They hear, receive the word, and produce a crop thirty, sixty, or a hundred times what was sown.” 🌾
## Mark 4:21
Jesus said, “Is a lamp brought in to be put under a basket or bed? Is it not put on a stand? 🕯️
## Mark 4:22
Everything hidden will be revealed. Everything concealed will be brought to light. ✨
## Mark 4:23
Anyone with ears to hear should listen.” 👂
## Mark 4:24
He continued, “Pay attention to what you hear. The measure you use will be used for you, with more added. ⚖️
## Mark 4:25
Those who have will receive more. Those who do not have will lose even what they have.” ⚠️
## Mark 4:26
Jesus said, “God’s kingdom is like a man scattering seed on the ground. 🌱
## Mark 4:27
Night and day, he sleeps and wakes. The seed sprouts and grows, although he does not know how. 🌾
## Mark 4:28
The earth produces the crop by itself: first the stalk, then the head, then ripe grain. 🌱
## Mark 4:29
When the grain ripens, he immediately uses the sickle because harvest time has come.” 🌾
## Mark 4:30
He asked, “What can we compare God’s kingdom to? What parable can describe it? ❓
## Mark 4:31
It is like a mustard seed, the smallest of all seeds sown on earth. 🌱
## Mark 4:32
After planting, it becomes the largest garden plant. It grows large branches, so birds can nest in its shade.” 🐦
## Mark 4:33
Jesus spoke the word through many such parables, as much as they could understand. 📖
## Mark 4:34
He always used parables when speaking to them. In private, He explained everything to His disciples. 👂
## Mark 4:35
That evening, He told His disciples, “Let us cross to the other side.” 🌆
## Mark 4:36
They dismissed the crowd and took Him in the boat where He already sat. Other boats went with Him. 🚤
## Mark 4:37
A fierce windstorm rose. Waves broke over the boat and began filling it. 🌊
## Mark 4:38
Jesus slept on a cushion in the boat’s stern. They woke Him and said, “Teacher, do You not care that we are dying?” 😨
## Mark 4:39
Jesus rose and rebuked the wind and sea. He said, “Silence! Be still!” The wind stopped, and everything became calm. 🕊️
## Mark 4:40
He asked, “Why are you so afraid? Do you still have no faith?” 🙏
## Mark 4:41
Filled with fear, they asked each other, “Who is this? Even the wind and sea obey Him!” ❓
