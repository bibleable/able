## Mark 3:1
Jesus entered the synagogue again. A man with a withered hand was there. 🏠
## Mark 3:2
They watched to see whether Jesus would heal on the Sabbath, so they could accuse Him. 👁️
## Mark 3:3
Jesus told the man with the withered hand, “Stand among us.” 🚶
## Mark 3:4
He asked, “Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or evil, to save life or destroy it?” They remained silent. ❓
## Mark 3:5
Jesus looked at them with anger and sorrow over their hard hearts. He told the man, “Stretch out your hand.” The man obeyed, and his hand was restored. ❤️
## Mark 3:6
The Pharisees left and began planning with the Herodians how to kill Jesus. ⚠️
## Mark 3:7
Jesus withdrew to the sea with His disciples. A large crowd followed from Galilee, Judea, 🌊
## Mark 3:8
Jerusalem, Idumea, beyond the Jordan, and around Tyre and Sidon. They came because they heard about His great works. 👥
## Mark 3:9
Jesus told His disciples to prepare a boat, so the crowd would not crush Him. 🚤
## Mark 3:10
He had healed many, so everyone with diseases pressed forward to touch Him. ❤️
## Mark 3:11
When unclean spirits saw Him, they fell before Him and shouted, “You are God’s Son!” ⚠️
## Mark 3:12
He sternly warned them not to reveal Him. 🛡️
## Mark 3:13
Jesus went up the mountain and called those He wanted. They came to Him. ⛰️
## Mark 3:14
He appointed twelve, naming them apostles. They would accompany Him and be sent to preach, 📖
## Mark 3:15
with authority to drive out demons. 🛡️
## Mark 3:16
He appointed these twelve: Simon, whom He named Peter, 👥
## Mark 3:17
James son of Zebedee and his brother John, whom He named Boanerges, meaning “Sons of Thunder,” ⚡
## Mark 3:18
Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot, 👥
## Mark 3:19
and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus. 💔
## Mark 3:20
Jesus went home, and another crowd gathered. He and His disciples could not even eat. 🏠
## Mark 3:21
His family heard and went to take charge of Him. They said, “He is out of His mind.” ⚠️
## Mark 3:22
Scribes from Jerusalem said, “Beelzebul possesses Him. He drives out demons through the demons’ prince.” 💬
## Mark 3:23
Jesus called them together and spoke in parables: “How can Satan drive out Satan? ❓
## Mark 3:24
A kingdom divided against itself cannot stand. 👑
## Mark 3:25
A house divided against itself cannot stand. 🏠
## Mark 3:26
If Satan rises against himself and is divided, he cannot stand. His end has come. ⚠️
## Mark 3:27
No one can rob a strong man’s house without first tying him up. Then the house can be robbed. ⛓️
## Mark 3:28
I tell you the truth: people will be forgiven all their sins and blasphemies, however many they speak. 🙏
## Mark 3:29
Whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven. He is guilty of eternal sin.” ⚠️
## Mark 3:30
Jesus said this because they claimed, “He has an unclean spirit.” 💬
## Mark 3:31
Jesus’ mother and brothers stood outside and sent someone to call Him. 🏠
## Mark 3:32
A crowd sat around Him. They told Him, “Your mother and brothers are outside asking for You.” 👥
## Mark 3:33
Jesus answered, “Who are My mother and brothers?” ❓
## Mark 3:34
He looked around at those seated with Him and said, “Here are My mother and brothers! ❤️
## Mark 3:35
Anyone who does God’s will is My brother, sister, and mother.” 🤝
