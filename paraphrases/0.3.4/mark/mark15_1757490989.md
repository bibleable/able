## Mark 15:1
Early in the morning, the chief priests, elders, scribes, and whole Sanhedrin made a plan. They bound Jesus and handed Him to Pilate. ⛓️
## Mark 15:2
Pilate asked, “Are You the Jews’ King?” Jesus answered, “You said so.” 👑
## Mark 15:3
The chief priests accused Him of many things. ⚖️
## Mark 15:4
Pilate asked again, “Will You not answer? Look at their many charges!” ❓
## Mark 15:5
Jesus gave no further answer, which amazed Pilate. 💬
## Mark 15:6
At the feast, Pilate usually released a prisoner chosen by the people. ⛓️
## Mark 15:7
Barabbas was imprisoned with rebels who murdered during the uprising. ⚠️
## Mark 15:8
The crowd came and asked Pilate to follow this custom. 👥
## Mark 15:9
Pilate asked, “Should I release the Jews’ King?” ❓
## Mark 15:10
He knew the chief priests handed Jesus over because of envy. ⚠️
## Mark 15:11
The chief priests stirred the crowd to request Barabbas instead. 👥
## Mark 15:12
Pilate asked, “What should I do with the One you call the Jews’ King?” ❓
## Mark 15:13
They shouted, “Crucify Him!” 📣
## Mark 15:14
Pilate asked, “Why? What evil did He do?” They shouted louder, “Crucify Him!” ⚠️
## Mark 15:15
Pilate wanted to satisfy the crowd and released Barabbas. He had Jesus whipped and handed Him over for crucifixion. 💔
## Mark 15:16
The soldiers led Jesus into the palace, the Praetorium, and called the whole company together. 🏛️
## Mark 15:17
They dressed Him in purple and put a crown woven from thorns on His head. 👑
## Mark 15:18
They saluted Him, “Hail, King of the Jews!” 💬
## Mark 15:19
They repeatedly hit His head with a staff and spat on Him. They knelt and bowed before Him. 💔
## Mark 15:20
After mocking Him, they removed the purple robe and dressed Him in His clothes. They led Him out for crucifixion. ✝️
## Mark 15:21
Simon of Cyrene, Alexander and Rufus’s father, passed by from the countryside. The soldiers forced him to carry Jesus’ cross. 🚶
## Mark 15:22
They brought Jesus to Golgotha, meaning “The Place of the Skull.” ✝️
## Mark 15:23
They offered wine mixed with myrrh, but He refused it. 🍷
## Mark 15:24
They crucified Him and cast lots to decide how to divide His clothes. ✝️
## Mark 15:25
They crucified Him at the third hour. ⏳
## Mark 15:26
The written charge read: THE KING OF THE JEWS. 📜
## Mark 15:27
They crucified two robbers with Jesus, one on His right and one on His left. ✝️
## Mark 15:28

## Mark 15:29
Passersby insulted Him and shook their heads. They said, “You who would destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days, ⚠️
## Mark 15:30
come down from the cross and save Yourself!” 💬
## Mark 15:31
The chief priests and scribes likewise mocked Him among themselves. They said, “He saved others, but cannot save Himself! ⚠️
## Mark 15:32
Let the Christ, Israel’s King, come down from the cross so we can see and believe!” Those crucified with Him also insulted Him. 💔
## Mark 15:33
Darkness covered the whole land from the sixth to the ninth hour. 🌌
## Mark 15:34
At the ninth hour, Jesus loudly cried, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” This means, “My God, My God, why have You abandoned Me?” 💔
## Mark 15:35
Some nearby heard and said, “He calls Elijah.” 💬
## Mark 15:36
Someone ran, filled a sponge with sour wine, and raised it on a reed for Jesus to drink. He said, “Leave Him alone. Let us see whether Elijah comes to take Him down.” 🍷
## Mark 15:37
Jesus cried loudly and breathed His last. ✝️
## Mark 15:38
The temple curtain tore in two from top to bottom. 🏛️
## Mark 15:39
The centurion facing Jesus saw how He died. He said, “This man truly was God’s Son!” 🙏
## Mark 15:40
Women watched from far away, including Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and Joses, and Salome. 👥
## Mark 15:41
These women followed and served Jesus in Galilee. Many other women came with Him to Jerusalem. 🚶
## Mark 15:42
Evening had come on Preparation Day, the day before the Sabbath. 🌆
## Mark 15:43
Joseph of Arimathea, a prominent Council member awaiting God’s kingdom, boldly went to Pilate. He asked for Jesus’ body. 🪦
## Mark 15:44
Pilate was surprised Jesus had already died. He called the centurion to confirm it. ❓
## Mark 15:45
After confirmation, Pilate gave Joseph permission to take the body. 🪦
## Mark 15:46
Joseph bought linen, took Jesus’ body down, and wrapped it. He placed it in a tomb cut from rock and rolled a stone across the entrance. 🪨
## Mark 15:47
Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joseph saw where His body was placed. 👁️
