## Mark 16:1
After the Sabbath, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices to anoint Jesus’ body. 🏺
## Mark 16:2
They went to the tomb very early on the week’s first day, just after sunrise. 🌅
## Mark 16:3
They asked each other, “Who will roll the stone away from the tomb’s entrance?” ❓
## Mark 16:4
Looking up, they saw the very large stone already rolled away. 🪨
## Mark 16:5
They entered and saw a young man wearing a white robe, seated on the right. They became alarmed. 😨
## Mark 16:6
He said, “Do not be alarmed. You seek Jesus the Nazarene, who was crucified. He rose! He is not here. Look where they placed Him. 🌅
## Mark 16:7
Tell His disciples and Peter, ‘He goes ahead to Galilee. You will see Him there, as He told you.’” 📣
## Mark 16:8
The women ran from the tomb, trembling and confused. Fear kept them from saying anything to anyone. 😨
## Mark 16:9
Early on the week’s first day, the risen Jesus first appeared to Mary Magdalene. He had driven seven demons from her. 🌅
## Mark 16:10
She told those who had accompanied Him. They were mourning and weeping. 💧
## Mark 16:11
They heard Jesus lived and she had seen Him, but did not believe. 💬
## Mark 16:12
Later, Jesus appeared in a different form to two followers walking in the countryside. 🚶
## Mark 16:13
They returned and told the others, who did not believe them either. 💬
## Mark 16:14
Jesus later appeared to the Eleven as they ate. He rebuked their unbelief and hard hearts. They had not believed those who saw Him after He rose. ⚠️
## Mark 16:15
He said, “Go throughout the world and preach the good news to every creature. 🌍
## Mark 16:16
Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved. Whoever does not believe will be condemned. ⚖️
## Mark 16:17
These signs will accompany believers. In My name, they will drive out demons and speak new languages. 🛡️
## Mark 16:18
They will pick up snakes with their hands. Deadly poison will not harm them if they drink it. They will place their hands on sick people, who will become well.” ❤️
## Mark 16:19
After speaking, the Lord Jesus was taken into heaven. He sat at God’s right hand. 👑
## Mark 16:20
They preached everywhere. The Lord worked through them and confirmed His word with accompanying signs. 📣
