## Mark 8:1
During those days, another large crowd gathered without food. Jesus called His disciples and said: 👥
## Mark 8:2
“I feel compassion for these people. They have been with Me three days and have no food. ❤️
## Mark 8:3
If I send them home hungry, they will faint on the way. Some came from far away.” 🚶
## Mark 8:4
His disciples asked, “Where could anyone find enough bread for everyone in this deserted place?” ❓
## Mark 8:5
Jesus asked, “How many loaves do you have?” They answered, “Seven.” 🍞
## Mark 8:6
He told the crowd to sit on the ground. He took the seven loaves, gave thanks, broke them, and gave them to His disciples. They distributed the bread. 🙏
## Mark 8:7
They also had a few small fish. Jesus blessed them and told the disciples to serve them too. 🐟
## Mark 8:8
Everyone ate and was satisfied. The disciples collected seven baskets of leftover pieces. 🧺
## Mark 8:9
About four thousand men were there. Jesus dismissed the crowd. 👥
## Mark 8:10
He immediately entered the boat with His disciples and went to Dalmanutha’s district. 🚤
## Mark 8:11
Pharisees came and argued with Jesus. To test Him, they demanded a sign from heaven. ⚠️
## Mark 8:12
Jesus sighed deeply in His spirit and asked, “Why does this generation demand a sign? I tell you the truth: this generation will receive no sign.” 💬
## Mark 8:13
He left them, entered the boat again, and crossed to the other side. 🚤
## Mark 8:14
The disciples forgot to bring bread. They had only one loaf in the boat. 🍞
## Mark 8:15
He warned, “Watch out! Beware of the Pharisees’ leaven and Herod’s leaven.” ⚠️
## Mark 8:16
They discussed their lack of bread with each other. 💬
## Mark 8:17
Jesus knew and asked, “Why discuss having no bread? Do you still not see or understand? Are your hearts so hard? ❓
## Mark 8:18
You have eyes, but do you not see? You have ears, but do you not hear? Do you not remember? 👂
## Mark 8:19
When I broke five loaves for five thousand, how many baskets of pieces did you collect?” They answered, “Twelve.” 🧺
## Mark 8:20
“When I broke seven loaves for four thousand, how many baskets did you collect?” They answered, “Seven.” 🧺
## Mark 8:21
He asked, “Do you still not understand?” ❓
## Mark 8:22
At Bethsaida, people brought a blind man and begged Jesus to touch him. 🙏
## Mark 8:23
Jesus took his hand and led him outside the village. He spat on the man’s eyes and placed His hands on him. He asked, “Can you see anything?” 👁️
## Mark 8:24
The man looked up and said, “I see people, but they look like trees walking.” 🌳
## Mark 8:25
Jesus put His hands on the man’s eyes again. The man opened them, and his sight was restored. He saw everything clearly. ✨
## Mark 8:26
Jesus sent him home and said, “Do not enter the village again.” 🏠
## Mark 8:27
Jesus and His disciples traveled to the villages around Caesarea Philippi. On the way, He asked, “Who do people say I am?” ❓
## Mark 8:28
They answered, “John the Baptist, Elijah, or one of the prophets.” 💬
## Mark 8:29
He asked, “Who do you say I am?” Peter answered, “You are the Christ.” 🙏
## Mark 8:30
Jesus warned them to tell no one about Him. ⚠️
## Mark 8:31
He taught that the Son of Man must suffer greatly and be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and scribes. He must be killed and rise after three days. ✝️
## Mark 8:32
Jesus spoke openly. Peter took Him aside and began rebuking Him. 💬
## Mark 8:33
Jesus turned, looked at His disciples, and rebuked Peter. He said, “Get behind Me, Satan! You think about human things instead of God’s things.” ⚠️
## Mark 8:34
Jesus called the crowd and His disciples. He said, “Anyone wanting to follow Me must deny himself, take up his cross, and follow Me. ✝️
## Mark 8:35
Whoever wants to save his life will lose it. Whoever loses his life for Me and the good news will save it. 🙏
## Mark 8:36
What benefit is gaining the whole world and losing your soul? 🌍
## Mark 8:37
What can anyone give in exchange for his soul? ❓
## Mark 8:38
If anyone is ashamed of Me and My words in this adulterous, sinful generation, the Son of Man will also be ashamed of him. This will happen when He comes in His Father’s glory with the holy angels.” ✨
