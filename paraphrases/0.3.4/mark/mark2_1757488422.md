## Mark 2:1
A few days later, Jesus returned to Capernaum. People heard He was home. 🏠
## Mark 2:2
So many gathered that no space remained, even outside the door. Jesus spoke the word to them. 👥
## Mark 2:3
Four men brought Him a paralyzed man. 🛏️
## Mark 2:4
They could not reach Jesus through the crowd. They opened the roof above Him and lowered the paralyzed man on his mat. 🏠
## Mark 2:5
Jesus saw their faith and told the paralyzed man, “Son, your sins are forgiven.” ❤️
## Mark 2:6
Some scribes sitting there thought to themselves: 💭
## Mark 2:7
“Why does this man speak this way? He blasphemes! Only God can forgive sins!” ⚠️
## Mark 2:8
Jesus immediately knew in His spirit what they thought. He asked, “Why do you think these things in your hearts? 👁️
## Mark 2:9
Which is easier: telling a paralyzed man, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or, ‘Get up, take your mat, and walk’? ❓
## Mark 2:10
But so you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins...” He told the paralyzed man: 📖
## Mark 2:11
“I tell you, get up, take your mat, and go home.” 🚶
## Mark 2:12
The man immediately stood, took his mat, and walked out before everyone. All were amazed and glorified God, saying, “We never saw anything like this!” 🙏
## Mark 2:13
Jesus went beside the sea again. All the people came, and He taught them. 🌊
## Mark 2:14
As He walked, Jesus saw Levi son of Alphaeus at the tax booth. He said, “Follow Me.” Levi stood and followed Him. 🚶
## Mark 2:15
Jesus ate at Levi’s house with His disciples and many tax collectors and sinners. Many of these people followed Him. 🍞
## Mark 2:16
The Pharisee scribes saw Him eating with them. They asked His disciples, “Why does He eat with tax collectors and sinners?” ❓
## Mark 2:17
Jesus heard and said, “Sick people need a doctor. Healthy people do not. I came to call sinners, rather than righteous people.” ❤️
## Mark 2:18
John’s disciples and the Pharisees often fasted. People asked Jesus, “Why do Your disciples not fast like John’s disciples and the Pharisees’ disciples?” ❓
## Mark 2:19
Jesus answered, “Can wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them? They cannot fast while He is there. 💍
## Mark 2:20
The time will come when the bridegroom is taken away. Then they will fast. ⏳
## Mark 2:21
No one sews cloth that has not shrunk onto an old garment. The new patch would pull away and make the tear worse. 👕
## Mark 2:22
No one puts new wine into old wineskins. The wine would burst the skins, ruining both. New wine goes into new wineskins.” 🍷
## Mark 2:23
One Sabbath, Jesus crossed grainfields. His disciples picked grain heads as they walked. 🌾
## Mark 2:24
The Pharisees asked Him, “Why do they do what is unlawful on the Sabbath?” ❓
## Mark 2:25
Jesus answered, “Did you never read what David did when he and his companions were hungry and in need? 📖
## Mark 2:26
During Abiathar’s high priesthood, David entered God’s house and ate holy bread reserved by law for priests. He also gave some to his companions.” 🍞
## Mark 2:27
Jesus said, “The Sabbath was made for people. People were not made for the Sabbath. 📅
## Mark 2:28
The Son of Man is therefore Lord even of the Sabbath.” 👑
