## Mark 11:1
Near Jerusalem, they reached Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives. Jesus sent two disciples. ⛰️
## Mark 11:2
He said, “Enter the village ahead. You will immediately find a tied colt that no one ever rode. Untie it and bring it. 🫏
## Mark 11:3
If anyone asks why, say, ‘The Lord needs it and will return it soon.’” 💬
## Mark 11:4
They found the colt in the street, tied outside a doorway, and untied it. 🫏
## Mark 11:5
Some people nearby asked, “Why untie the colt?” ❓
## Mark 11:6
The disciples answered as Jesus instructed. The people gave permission. 🤝
## Mark 11:7
They brought the colt to Jesus and put their cloaks over it. He sat on it. 🫏
## Mark 11:8
Many spread cloaks on the road. Others spread branches cut from the fields. 🌿
## Mark 11:9
Those ahead and behind shouted, “Hosanna! Blessed is He who comes in the Lord’s name!” 📣
## Mark 11:10
“Blessed is our father David’s coming kingdom! Hosanna in the highest!” 👑
## Mark 11:11
Jesus entered Jerusalem’s temple courts and looked at everything. It was late, so He went to Bethany with the Twelve. 🏠
## Mark 11:12
The next day, Jesus was hungry as they left Bethany. 🚶
## Mark 11:13
He saw a leafy fig tree far away and went to look for fruit. He found only leaves because it was not fig season. 🌳
## Mark 11:14
He told the tree, “May no one eat your fruit again.” His disciples heard. ⚠️
## Mark 11:15
In Jerusalem, Jesus entered the temple courts and drove out buyers and sellers. He overturned the money changers’ tables and dove sellers’ seats. ⚖️
## Mark 11:16
He allowed no one to carry goods through the temple courts. 🏠
## Mark 11:17
He taught, “Does Scripture not say, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations’? You made it a robbers’ den.” 🙏
## Mark 11:18
The chief priests and scribes heard and sought a way to kill Him. They feared Him because His teaching amazed the entire crowd. ⚠️
## Mark 11:19
At evening, Jesus and His disciples left the city. 🌆
## Mark 11:20
Returning in the morning, they saw the fig tree dried up from its roots. 🌳
## Mark 11:21
Peter remembered and said, “Rabbi, look! The fig tree You cursed dried up.” 👁️
## Mark 11:22
Jesus said, “Have faith in God. 🙏
## Mark 11:23
I tell you the truth. If anyone tells this mountain, ‘Rise and be thrown into the sea,’ it will be done for him. He must believe it will happen and have no doubt in his heart. ⛰️
## Mark 11:24
Whatever you request in prayer, believe you received it, and it will be yours. 🙏
## Mark 11:25
When you stand praying, forgive anything you hold against another. Do this so your Father in heaven will also forgive your offenses.” ❤️

## Mark 11:27
They returned to Jerusalem. As Jesus walked in the temple courts, the chief priests, scribes, and elders approached. 👥
## Mark 11:28
They asked, “What authority lets You do these things? Who gave You this authority?” ❓
## Mark 11:29
Jesus answered, “Answer one question, and I will tell you My authority for these things. 💬
## Mark 11:30
Did John’s baptism come from heaven or people? Answer Me!” ❓
## Mark 11:31
They discussed their answer: “If we say, ‘Heaven,’ He will ask, ‘Why did you not believe him?’ 💬
## Mark 11:32
But if we say, ‘People’...” They feared the people because everyone considered John a true prophet. ⚠️
## Mark 11:33
They answered, “We do not know.” Jesus said, “Then I will not tell you My authority for these things.” 💬
