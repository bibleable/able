## Lamentations 3:1
I am the man who has suffered under the rod of God’s anger. 💔
## Lamentations 3:2
He drove me away and made me walk in darkness instead of light. 🌑
## Lamentations 3:3
He keeps turning His hand against me all day. ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:4
He wore away my flesh and skin. He shattered my bones. 💔
## Lamentations 3:5
He surrounded me like an army, with bitterness and hardship. ⛓️
## Lamentations 3:6
He made me live in darkness like those who died long ago. 🌑
## Lamentations 3:7
He walled me in so I cannot escape. He weighed me down with chains. ⛓️
## Lamentations 3:8
Even when I cry and plead for help, He shuts out my prayer. 🙏
## Lamentations 3:9
He blocked my roads with cut stones. He made my paths crooked. 🪨
## Lamentations 3:10
He is like a bear waiting for me or a lion hidden for an attack. 🦁
## Lamentations 3:11
He forced me off my path and tore me apart. He left me without help. 💔
## Lamentations 3:12
He bent His bow and made me the target for His arrow. 🏹
## Lamentations 3:13
He pierced my kidneys with His arrows. 🏹
## Lamentations 3:14
All my people laugh at me. They mock me in songs all day. 😢
## Lamentations 3:15
He filled me with bitterness. He made me drunk with wormwood. 💔

*Word help: Wormwood is a bitter plant.*
## Lamentations 3:16
He ground my teeth with gravel and trampled me in the dust. 🪨
## Lamentations 3:17
My soul has no peace. I have forgotten what it means to prosper. 💔
## Lamentations 3:18
So I say, “My strength is gone, and so is my hope from the LORD.” 😢
## Lamentations 3:19
Remember my suffering and wandering, the wormwood and gall. 🙏

*Word help: Gall is a bitter substance.*
## Lamentations 3:20
My soul remembers these things and is humbled within me. 💭
## Lamentations 3:21
But I remember this, and it gives me hope: 🌅
## Lamentations 3:22
The LORD’s faithful love keeps us from being consumed. His mercies never fail. ❤️
## Lamentations 3:23
They are new every morning. Your faithfulness is great! 🌅
## Lamentations 3:24
My soul says, “The LORD is my portion. So I will put my hope in Him.” 🙏
## Lamentations 3:25
The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul that seeks Him. ❤️
## Lamentations 3:26
It is good to wait quietly for the LORD’s salvation. 🕊️
## Lamentations 3:27
It is good for a man to carry the yoke while he is still young. 👣
## Lamentations 3:28
Let him sit alone in silence, because the LORD placed it on him. 🤫
## Lamentations 3:29
Let him put his face in the dust. Perhaps there is still hope. 🙏
## Lamentations 3:30
Let him offer his cheek to someone who strikes him. Let him bear all their insults. 💔
## Lamentations 3:31
The Lord will not reject us forever. 🌅
## Lamentations 3:32
Even if He causes grief, He will show mercy through His great faithful love. ❤️
## Lamentations 3:33
He does not willingly cause people suffering or grief. 💔
## Lamentations 3:34
The Lord does not approve of crushing all the land’s prisoners underfoot. ⛓️
## Lamentations 3:35
He does not approve of denying a person justice before the Most High. ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:36
He does not approve of cheating a person in a lawsuit. ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:37
Who can speak and make something happen unless the Lord commands it? 💬
## Lamentations 3:38
Do both hardship and good not come from the mouth of the Most High? ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:39
Why should a mortal person complain when he considers his sins? 💭
## Lamentations 3:40
Let us examine and test our ways. Let us turn back to the LORD. 👣
## Lamentations 3:41
Let us lift our hearts and hands to God in heaven. 🙏
## Lamentations 3:42
We have sinned and rebelled. You have not forgiven us. 💔
## Lamentations 3:43
You covered Yourself with anger and pursued us. You killed without pity. ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:44
You covered Yourself with a cloud that no prayer can pass through. ☁️
## Lamentations 3:45
You made us scum and rubbish among the nations. 💔
## Lamentations 3:46
All our enemies open their mouths against us. ⛔
## Lamentations 3:47
Panic and traps came upon us, with ruin and destruction. 😨
## Lamentations 3:48
Streams of tears flow from my eyes over my people’s destruction. 😢
## Lamentations 3:49
My tears flow without pause or relief. 😢
## Lamentations 3:50
They will continue until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees. 🙏
## Lamentations 3:51
What my eyes see brings grief to my soul because of all the daughters of my city. 💔
## Lamentations 3:52
Without reason, my enemies hunted me like a bird. 🐦
## Lamentations 3:53
They dropped me alive into a pit and threw stones on me. 🪨
## Lamentations 3:54
Water flowed over my head, and I thought I would die. 🌊
## Lamentations 3:55
LORD, I called on Your name from deep in the Pit. 🙏
## Lamentations 3:56
You heard my plea: “Do not ignore my cry for relief.” 🙏
## Lamentations 3:57
You came near when I called. You said, “Do not be afraid.” 🛡️
## Lamentations 3:58
Lord, You defend my case and redeem my life. 🛡️
## Lamentations 3:59
LORD, You have seen the wrong done to me. Give me justice! ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:60
You have seen all their malice and all their plans against me. 👀
## Lamentations 3:61
LORD, You heard their insults and all their plans against me. 🙏
## Lamentations 3:62
You heard my attackers’ slander and whispers against me all day. ⛔
## Lamentations 3:63
Watch them when they sit and when they rise. See how they mock me in songs. 👀
## Lamentations 3:64
LORD, You will repay them as their actions deserve. ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:65
Put a veil of anguish over their hearts. May Your curse fall on them! ⚖️
## Lamentations 3:66
LORD, You will pursue them in anger and destroy them from under Your heavens. ⚖️
