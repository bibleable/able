## Lamentations 1:1
How lonely the city is, though it was once full of people! She was great among the nations, but now she is like a widow. She was a princess among the provinces, but now she is a slave. 🏚️

*Word help: This poem speaks of Jerusalem as a woman.*
## Lamentations 1:2
She cries aloud at night, with tears on her cheeks. None of her lovers comfort her. All her friends betrayed her and became her enemies. 😢
## Lamentations 1:3
Judah has gone into exile, with suffering and harsh slavery. She lives among the nations but finds no rest. Her pursuers caught her in the middle of her distress. ⛓️
## Lamentations 1:4
The roads to Zion mourn because no one comes to her appointed feasts. Her gates are empty, her priests groan, and her young women grieve. She is full of bitter pain. 💔
## Lamentations 1:5
Her enemies have become her masters and live at ease. The LORD brought her grief because of her many sins. Her children went away as prisoners before the enemy. ⛓️
## Lamentations 1:6
All beauty and glory have left the Daughter of Zion. Her princes are like deer without pasture. They have no strength to escape the hunter. 🦌
## Lamentations 1:7
In her suffering and wandering, Jerusalem remembers all the treasures she once had. When her people fell into enemy hands, no one helped her. Her enemies watched and laughed at her fall. 💔
## Lamentations 1:8
Jerusalem sinned greatly and became an object of contempt. All who honored her now despise her, because they have seen her nakedness. She groans and turns away. 😢
## Lamentations 1:9
Her uncleanness stains her skirts. She did not think about her end. Her fall was shocking, and no one comforted her. Look at my suffering, LORD, because the enemy has won! 🙏
## Lamentations 1:10
The enemy took all her treasures. She saw foreign nations enter her holy place. You had forbidden them to enter Your assembly. ⛔
## Lamentations 1:11
All her people groan as they search for bread. They trade their treasures for food to stay alive. Look, LORD, and see how despised I have become. 🍞
## Lamentations 1:12
All you who pass by, does this mean nothing to you? Look and see! Is there any sorrow like mine? The LORD brought this suffering on me on the day of His fierce anger. 💔
## Lamentations 1:13
He sent fire from above, and it overcame my bones. He spread a net for my feet and turned me back. He left me ruined and faint all day. 🔥
## Lamentations 1:14
His hand tied my sins together into a yoke around my neck. The Lord broke my strength. He gave me to people I cannot resist. ⛓️

*Word help: A yoke is a frame placed on the neck to carry a load or pull something.*
## Lamentations 1:15
The Lord rejected all the mighty men among me. He called an army to crush my young warriors. He trampled the Virgin Daughter of Judah like grapes in a winepress. ⚖️
## Lamentations 1:16
I cry because of these things, and tears flow from my eyes. No one is near to comfort me or restore my soul. My children have nothing, because the enemy has won. 😢
## Lamentations 1:17
Zion stretches out her hands, but no one comforts her. The LORD commanded that Jacob’s neighbors become his enemies. Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them. 🙏
## Lamentations 1:18
The LORD is righteous, but I rebelled against His command. All you people, listen and see my suffering. My young men and women have gone into captivity. ⛓️
## Lamentations 1:19
I called to my lovers, but they betrayed me. My priests and elders died in the city as they searched for food to stay alive. 🍞
## Lamentations 1:20
LORD, see my distress! My insides churn, and my heart pounds, because I have rebelled greatly. Outside, the sword takes loved ones away. Inside, there is death. 💔
## Lamentations 1:21
People heard my groans, but no one comforted me. My enemies heard of my trouble and rejoiced because You caused it. Bring the day You announced, so that they become like me. 🙏
## Lamentations 1:22
Let all their evil come before You. Deal with them as You dealt with me for all my sins. My groans are many, and my heart is weak. ⚖️
