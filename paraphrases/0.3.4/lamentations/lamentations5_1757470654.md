## Lamentations 5:1
LORD, remember what happened to us. Look and see our disgrace! 🙏
## Lamentations 5:2
Our inheritance was given to strangers, and our houses to foreigners. 🏚️
## Lamentations 5:3
We have become orphans without fathers. Our mothers are widows. 💔
## Lamentations 5:4
We must pay for our water and wood. 💧
## Lamentations 5:5
Our pursuers are close behind us. We are tired and find no rest. 👣
## Lamentations 5:6
We submitted to Egypt and Assyria to get enough bread. 🍞
## Lamentations 5:7
Our ancestors sinned and are dead. But we carry their punishment. ⚖️
## Lamentations 5:8
Slaves rule over us. No one rescues us from their hands. ⛓️
## Lamentations 5:9
We risk our lives to get bread because of the sword in the wilderness. 🍞
## Lamentations 5:10
Hunger gives us fever, and our skin is hot as an oven. 💔
## Lamentations 5:11
Women have been raped in Zion, and virgins have been raped in Judah’s cities. 💔
## Lamentations 5:12
Princes have been hung up by their hands. Elders receive no respect. ⛓️
## Lamentations 5:13
Young men work hard at millstones. Boys stumble under loads of wood. 🛠️
## Lamentations 5:14
The elders have left the city gate. The young men have stopped their music. 🎵
## Lamentations 5:15
Joy has left our hearts. Our dancing has become mourning. 😢
## Lamentations 5:16
The crown has fallen from our head. How terrible for us, because we have sinned! 👑
## Lamentations 5:17
Because of this, our hearts are weak and our eyes grow dim. 💔
## Lamentations 5:18
Mount Zion lies ruined, and foxes walk over it. 🦊
## Lamentations 5:19
LORD, You rule forever. Your throne lasts from one generation to the next. 👑
## Lamentations 5:20
Why have You forgotten us forever? Why have You abandoned us for so long? 🙏
## Lamentations 5:21
LORD, bring us back to Yourself, so we may return. Renew our days as they once were. 🙏
## Lamentations 5:22
Do this unless You have completely rejected us and remain angry with us beyond measure. 💔
