## Lamentations 2:1
The Lord has covered the Daughter of Zion with the cloud of His anger! He threw Israel’s glory from heaven to earth. On the day of His anger, He abandoned His footstool. ☁️
## Lamentations 2:2
Without pity, the Lord swallowed all Jacob’s homes. In His anger, He destroyed Judah’s defended cities. He brought her kingdom and princes to the ground and made them unclean. 🏚️
## Lamentations 2:3
In fierce anger, He cut off every horn of Israel. He withdrew His right hand when the enemy approached. He burned in Jacob like a fire that consumes everything around it. 🔥

*Word help: A horn can be an image of strength.*
## Lamentations 2:4
He bent His bow like an enemy and held His right hand ready. Like an enemy, He killed everyone who pleased the eye. He poured His anger like fire on the tent of the Daughter of Zion. 🏹
## Lamentations 2:5
The Lord is like an enemy. He swallowed Israel and all her palaces, and destroyed her strongholds. He greatly increased Judah’s grief and cries of sorrow. 💔
## Lamentations 2:6
He destroyed His sacred tent like a shelter in a garden. He ruined His meeting place. The LORD made Zion forget her appointed feasts and Sabbaths. In fierce anger, He despised both king and priest. 🏚️
## Lamentations 2:7
The Lord rejected His altar and abandoned His holy place. He gave her palace walls to the enemy. They shouted in the LORD’s house as on the day of an appointed feast. 📣
## Lamentations 2:8
The LORD decided to destroy Zion’s wall. He stretched out a line to measure it and did not stop His hand from destroying it. He made the defensive banks and walls mourn. Together, they waste away. 🏚️
## Lamentations 2:9
Her gates sank into the ground, and He broke their bars. Her king and princes are exiled among the nations. The law is no more. Even her prophets receive no vision from the LORD. ⛓️
## Lamentations 2:10
Zion’s elders sit silently on the ground. They threw dust on their heads and put on sackcloth. Jerusalem’s young women bow their heads to the ground. 😢

*Word help: Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief or sorrow for sin.*
## Lamentations 2:11
My eyes fail from tears, and my insides churn. My heart pours out in grief over my people’s destruction. Children and babies faint in the city streets. 💔
## Lamentations 2:12
They cry to their mothers, “Where is the grain and wine?” They faint like wounded people in the city streets. Their lives fade in their mothers’ arms. 🍞
## Lamentations 2:13
What can I say for you, Daughter of Jerusalem? What can I compare you with? What can I say to comfort you, Virgin Daughter of Zion? Your wound is as deep as the sea. Who can heal you? 💔
## Lamentations 2:14
Your prophets’ visions were empty and deceptive. They did not expose your guilt to prevent your captivity. The messages they saw for you were empty and misleading. ⚠️
## Lamentations 2:15
Everyone who passes claps at you in contempt. They hiss and shake their heads at Jerusalem. They ask, “Is this the city called perfect in beauty, the joy of all the earth?” 🏚️
## Lamentations 2:16
All your enemies speak against you. They hiss and grind their teeth. They say, “We swallowed her! This is the day we waited for. We have lived to see it!” ⛔
## Lamentations 2:17
The LORD did what He planned. He fulfilled the command He gave long ago. He overthrew you without pity. He let your enemy rejoice over you and raised the horn of your foes. ⚖️
## Lamentations 2:18
The people’s hearts cry to the Lord. Wall of the Daughter of Zion, let your tears flow like a river, day and night. Give yourself no relief and your eyes no rest. 😢
## Lamentations 2:19
Get up and cry out at night, from the first watch. Pour out your heart like water before the Lord. Lift your hands to Him for your children’s lives. They faint from hunger at every street corner. 🙏
## Lamentations 2:20
LORD, look and consider! Whom have You treated like this? Should women eat their own children, the babies they cared for? Should priests and prophets be killed in the Lord’s holy place? 💔
## Lamentations 2:21
Young and old lie together in the dust of the streets. My young men and women fell by the sword. You killed them on the day of Your anger, without mercy. ⚖️
## Lamentations 2:22
You called terrors around me as if for an appointed feast. On the day of the LORD’s anger, no one escaped or survived. My enemy destroyed those I cared for and raised. 💔
