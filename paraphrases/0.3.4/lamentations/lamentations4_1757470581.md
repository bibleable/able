## Lamentations 4:1
The gold has lost its shine. The pure gold has become dull! The temple’s precious stones lie scattered at every street corner. 🪨
## Lamentations 4:2
Zion’s precious sons were once worth their weight in pure gold. Now people value them as clay jars made by a potter. 🏺
## Lamentations 4:3
Even jackals feed their young from their breasts. But my people have become cruel, like ostriches in the wilderness. 💔
## Lamentations 4:4
The thirsty baby’s tongue sticks to the roof of its mouth. Children beg for bread, but no one gives them any. 🍞
## Lamentations 4:5
Those who once ate fine food now have nothing in the streets. Those raised in rich red clothes huddle in ash heaps. 🏚️
## Lamentations 4:6
My people’s punishment is greater than Sodom’s punishment. Sodom was overthrown in an instant, with no hand turned to help her. ⚖️
## Lamentations 4:7
Her leaders were brighter than snow and whiter than milk. Their bodies were redder than rubies, and their appearance was like sapphires. ✨
## Lamentations 4:8
Now they look blacker than soot, and no one recognizes them in the streets. Their skin shriveled on their bones and became dry as a stick. 💔
## Lamentations 4:9
Those killed by the sword are better off than those who die of hunger. The hungry waste away in sharp pain because the fields produce no food. 🍞
## Lamentations 4:10
Women who were full of mercy cooked their own children. The children became their food during my people’s destruction. 💔
## Lamentations 4:11
The LORD poured out all His fierce anger. He lit a fire in Zion, and it consumed her foundations. 🔥
## Lamentations 4:12
The kings of the earth and all the world’s people did not believe that an enemy could enter Jerusalem’s gates. 🏘️
## Lamentations 4:13
But this happened because of her prophets’ sins and her priests’ guilt. They shed the blood of righteous people within her. ⚖️
## Lamentations 4:14
They wandered blindly in the streets, made unclean by that blood. No one dared to touch their clothes. ⛔
## Lamentations 4:15
People shouted, “Go away! Unclean! Away, away! Do not touch us!” They fled and wandered. Among the nations, people said, “They cannot stay here any longer.” ⛔
## Lamentations 4:16
The LORD’s presence scattered them. He no longer pays attention to them. No one honors the priests or shows kindness to the elders. 💔
## Lamentations 4:17
Our eyes grew weak as we watched in vain for help. From our towers, we watched for a nation that could not save us. 👀
## Lamentations 4:18
They followed our every step, so we could not walk in our streets. Our end drew near, our time ran out, and our end came! ⛓️
## Lamentations 4:19
Our pursuers were faster than eagles in the sky. They chased us over the mountains and hid to attack us in the wilderness. 🦅
## Lamentations 4:20
The LORD’s anointed one, the breath of our life, was caught in their pits. We had said, “We will live among the nations under his shadow.” ⛓️
## Lamentations 4:21
Rejoice and be glad, Daughter of Edom, who lives in Uz. But the cup will come to you too. You will become drunk and expose yourself. ⚖️
## Lamentations 4:22
Daughter of Zion, your punishment is complete. He will not prolong your exile. But, Daughter of Edom, He will punish your evil and expose your sins. ⚖️
