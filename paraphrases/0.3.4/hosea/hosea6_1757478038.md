## Hosea 6:1
Come, let us return to the LORD. He tore us apart, but will heal us. He wounded us, but will bandage our wounds. ❤️
## Hosea 6:2
After two days, He will revive us. On the third day, He will raise us, so we may live in His presence. 🌅
## Hosea 6:3
Let us press on to know the LORD. He will appear as surely as the sunrise. He will come like rain, like spring showers watering the earth. 🌧️
## Hosea 6:4
“Ephraim, what should I do with you? Judah, what should I do with you? Your loyalty is like morning mist and early dew that disappears. 💧
## Hosea 6:5
I therefore cut them down through the prophets and killed them with My words. My judgments go out like lightning. ⚡
## Hosea 6:6
I desire mercy rather than sacrifice, and knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings. ❤️
## Hosea 6:7
Like Adam, they broke the covenant. There they were unfaithful to Me. ⚠️
## Hosea 6:8
Gilead is a city of evildoers, marked with bloody footprints. 👣
## Hosea 6:9
A band of priests acts like raiders waiting in ambush. They murder on the road to Shechem. They have committed terrible crimes. ⚔️
## Hosea 6:10
I saw something horrible in Israel’s house. Ephraim practices prostitution there, and Israel is unclean. 💔
## Hosea 6:11
Judah, a harvest is appointed for you too, when I restore My people from captivity.” 🌾
