## Hosea 4:1
Israel’s children, hear the LORD’s word. He has a case against the land’s people: “This land has no truth, faithful love, or knowledge of God. ⚖️
## Hosea 4:2
Cursing, lying, murder, theft, and adultery spread everywhere. One act of bloodshed follows another. 💔
## Hosea 4:3
The land therefore mourns. Everyone living there wastes away, along with wild animals and birds. Even the sea’s fish disappear. 🌍
## Hosea 4:4
Let no one argue or rebuke another. Your people are like those who argue with a priest. ⚠️
## Hosea 4:5
You will stumble by day, and the prophet will stumble with you by night. I will destroy your mother. ⚖️
## Hosea 4:6
Lack of knowledge destroys My people. You rejected knowledge, so I reject you as My priests. You forgot your God’s law, so I will forget your children. 📖
## Hosea 4:7
The more they increased, the more they sinned against Me. They exchanged their Glory for something disgraceful. ⚠️
## Hosea 4:8
They feed on My people’s sins and desire wrongdoing. 💔
## Hosea 4:9
The people and priests will share the same fate. I will punish both for their ways and repay their actions. ⚖️
## Hosea 4:10
They will eat without satisfaction and act as prostitutes without increasing in number. They abandoned the LORD to give themselves 🌾
## Hosea 4:11
to prostitution, wine, and new wine. These take away understanding. ⚠️
## Hosea 4:12
My people consult wooden idols and receive answers from their divining rods. A spirit of prostitution leads them astray. They act as prostitutes against their God. 🪵
## Hosea 4:13
They sacrifice on mountaintops and burn offerings on hills, under oak, poplar, and terebinth trees, whose shade is pleasant. Your daughters therefore become prostitutes, and your sons’ wives commit adultery. 🌳
## Hosea 4:14
I will not punish your daughters for prostitution or your sons’ wives for adultery. The men themselves visit prostitutes and sacrifice with shrine prostitutes. People without understanding will be ruined. ⚠️
## Hosea 4:15
Israel, although you act as a prostitute, may Judah avoid this guilt. Do not travel to Gilgal or Beth-aven. Do not swear, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!’ ⚠️
## Hosea 4:16
Israel is like a stubborn young cow. Can the LORD now shepherd them like lambs in an open meadow? 🐑
## Hosea 4:17
Ephraim is joined to idols. Leave him alone! ⚠️
## Hosea 4:18
When their liquor ends, they turn to prostitution. Their rulers dearly love disgrace. 💔
## Hosea 4:19
A whirlwind wraps them in its wings. Their sacrifices will bring them shame.” 🌪️
