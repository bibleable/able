## Hosea 3:1
The LORD told me, “Love your wife again, although another loves her and she commits adultery. Love her as the LORD loves Israel. They turn to other gods and love offering raisin cakes to idols.” ❤️
## Hosea 3:2
I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver, a homer of barley, and a lethech of barley. ⚖️
## Hosea 3:3
I told her, “Live with me for many days. Do not act as a prostitute or belong to another man. I will do the same for you.” 🤝
## Hosea 3:4
Israel must live many days without king, prince, sacrifice, sacred pillar, ephod, or idol. ⏳
## Hosea 3:5
Afterward, Israel will return and seek the LORD their God and David their king. In the last days, they will come trembling to the LORD and His goodness. 🙏
