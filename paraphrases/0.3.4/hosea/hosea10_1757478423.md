## Hosea 10:1
Israel was a flourishing vine that produced fruit for himself. As his fruit increased, he increased his altars. As his land improved, he improved his sacred pillars. 🍇
## Hosea 10:2
Their hearts deceive, and now they must bear their guilt. The LORD will destroy their altars and sacred pillars. ⚖️
## Hosea 10:3
Now they will say, “We have no king because we do not deeply respect the LORD. What could a king do for us?” 👑
## Hosea 10:4
They speak empty words and make covenants with false oaths. Judgment grows like poisonous weeds in a field’s furrows. 🌱
## Hosea 10:5
Samaria’s people will fear for Beth-aven’s calf. Its people and idol priests, who celebrated its glory, will mourn because it was taken into exile. 💔
## Hosea 10:6
It will be carried to Assyria as payment to the great king. Shame will seize Ephraim, and Israel will be ashamed of its wooden idols. ⚠️
## Hosea 10:7
Samaria and her king will be carried away like a twig floating on water. 🌊
## Hosea 10:8
Aven’s high places, Israel’s sin, will be destroyed. Thorns and thistles will cover their altars. They will tell the mountains, “Cover us!” and the hills, “Fall on us!” ⛰️
## Hosea 10:9
“Israel, you sinned from Gibeah’s days and have stayed there. Did the battle in Gibeah not overtake those wicked people? ⚠️
## Hosea 10:10
I will punish them when I please. Nations will gather against them and enslave them for their double offense. ⛓️
## Hosea 10:11
Ephraim is a trained young cow that loves threshing. I will put a yoke on her beautiful neck and harness Ephraim. Judah will plow, and Jacob will break hard ground. 🌾
## Hosea 10:12
Sow righteousness and harvest the fruit of faithful love. Break your unplowed ground. It is time to seek the LORD until He comes and sends righteousness on you like rain. 🌧️
## Hosea 10:13
You plowed wickedness, harvested injustice, and ate the fruit of lies. You trusted your own way and your many mighty men. ⚠️
## Hosea 10:14
Therefore, battle will roar against your people, and all your fortresses will fall. Shalman likewise destroyed Beth-arbel in battle, when mothers and children were smashed to pieces together. ⚔️
## Hosea 10:15
Bethel, this will happen to you because of your great wickedness. At dawn, Israel’s king will be completely cut off.” ⚖️
