## Hosea 7:1
“When I heal Israel, Ephraim’s wrongdoing and Samaria’s crimes will be exposed. They deceive, thieves break in, and bandits raid the streets. ⚠️
## Hosea 7:2
They do not consider that I remember all their evil. Their actions surround them and are before My face. 👁️
## Hosea 7:3
Their evil delights the king, and their lies delight the princes. 👑
## Hosea 7:4
They are all adulterers, like an oven heated by a baker. He need not tend the fire between kneading the dough and its rising. 🔥
## Hosea 7:5
On our king’s day, wine inflames the princes. He joins hands with those who mock him. 🍷
## Hosea 7:6
As they wait in ambush, they prepare their hearts like an oven. Their anger burns slowly all night, then blazes like fire in the morning. 🔥
## Hosea 7:7
They all burn like an oven and devour their rulers. All their kings fall. None calls on Me. 👑
## Hosea 7:8
Ephraim mixes with the nations. Ephraim is like a cake that has not been turned. 🍞
## Hosea 7:9
Foreigners consume his strength, but he does not notice. His hair turns gray, but he does not know. ⚠️
## Hosea 7:10
Israel’s pride testifies against them. Despite everything, they neither return to the LORD their God nor seek Him. 💔
## Hosea 7:11
Ephraim became like a foolish dove without sense. He calls to Egypt, then turns to Assyria. 🕊️
## Hosea 7:12
When they go, I will spread My net over them and bring them down like birds. When I hear them gather, I will punish them. 🪤
## Hosea 7:13
Woe to them because they strayed from Me! Destruction comes because they rebelled against Me! I would redeem them, but they speak lies against Me. ⚠️
## Hosea 7:14
They wail on their beds, but do not cry to Me from their hearts. They slash themselves for grain and new wine, but turn away from Me. 💔
## Hosea 7:15
I trained and strengthened their arms, yet they plan evil against Me. 💪
## Hosea 7:16
They turn, but not to the Most High. They are like a faulty bow. Their leaders will die by the sword for their cursing, and Egypt will mock them for this.” 🏹
