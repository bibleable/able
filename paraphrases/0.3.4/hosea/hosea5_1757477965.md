## Hosea 5:1
“Priests, Israel’s house, and the royal house, listen! This judgment concerns you. You became a trap at Mizpah and a net spread on Tabor. ⚖️
## Hosea 5:2
The rebels are deep in slaughter. I will punish them all. ⚠️
## Hosea 5:3
I know everything about Ephraim. Israel is not hidden from Me. Ephraim, you turned to prostitution, and Israel became unclean. 👁️
## Hosea 5:4
Their actions prevent them from returning to their God. A spirit of prostitution is within them, and they do not know the LORD. 💔
## Hosea 5:5
Israel’s pride testifies against them. Israel and Ephraim stumble in wrongdoing, and Judah stumbles with them. ⚠️
## Hosea 5:6
They bring flocks and herds to seek the LORD, but cannot find Him. He has withdrawn from them. 🐑
## Hosea 5:7
They were unfaithful to the LORD and bore illegitimate children. Now the New Moon will devour them and their land. ⚖️
## Hosea 5:8
Blow the ram’s horn in Gibeah and the trumpet in Ramah. Raise Beth-aven’s battle cry: Lead on, Benjamin! 📯
## Hosea 5:9
Ephraim will become a wasteland on the day of rebuke. I announce what is certain among Israel’s tribes. ⚠️
## Hosea 5:10
Judah’s princes act like people who move boundary stones. I will pour My fury over them like water. 🌊
## Hosea 5:11
Ephraim suffers oppression and crushing judgment because he insists on following worthless idols. ⚖️
## Hosea 5:12
I am like a moth to Ephraim and like decay to Judah’s house. 🦋
## Hosea 5:13
Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound. Ephraim turned to Assyria and sent to the great king. But he cannot cure you or heal your wound. 💔
## Hosea 5:14
I am like a lion to Ephraim and a young lion to Judah’s house. I Myself will tear them apart and leave. I will carry them away where no one can rescue them. 🦁
## Hosea 5:15
I will return to My place until they admit their guilt and seek My face. In their suffering, they will earnestly seek Me.” 🙏
