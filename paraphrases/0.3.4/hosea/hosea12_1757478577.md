## Hosea 12:1
Ephraim feeds on wind and chases the east wind all day. He increases lies and violence. He makes a covenant with Assyria and sends olive oil to Egypt. 🌬️
## Hosea 12:2
The LORD has a charge against Judah. He will punish Jacob for his ways and repay his actions. ⚖️
## Hosea 12:3
In the womb, he grasped his brother’s heel. In his strength, he wrestled with God. 👣
## Hosea 12:4
He struggled with the angel and won. He wept and sought His favor. He found Him at Bethel and spoke with Him there. 🙏
## Hosea 12:5
He is the LORD God of Hosts. The LORD is His renowned name. 📖
## Hosea 12:6
Return to your God. Maintain love and justice, and always wait on your God. ❤️
## Hosea 12:7
A merchant holds dishonest scales and loves to cheat. ⚖️
## Hosea 12:8
Ephraim boasts, “I became rich and gained wealth for myself! In all my work, no one can find sinful wrongdoing in me.” 💰
## Hosea 12:9
“I have been the LORD your God since Egypt. I will make you live in tents again, as during the appointed feast. ⛺
## Hosea 12:10
I spoke through the prophets and gave them many visions. I gave parables through them.” 📖
## Hosea 12:11
Is there wrongdoing in Gilead? They will surely become nothing. Do they sacrifice bulls in Gilgal? Their altars will become stone heaps in the fields’ furrows. 🪨
## Hosea 12:12
Jacob fled to Aram’s land. Israel worked for a wife, tending sheep for her. 🐑
## Hosea 12:13
The LORD brought Israel out of Egypt through a prophet. Through a prophet, Israel was kept safe. 🛡️
## Hosea 12:14
Ephraim caused bitter anger. His Lord will leave his guilt for bloodshed on him and repay his contempt. ⚖️
