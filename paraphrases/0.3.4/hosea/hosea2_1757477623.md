## Hosea 2:1
“Call your brothers ‘My people’ and your sisters ‘My loved one.’ ❤️
## Hosea 2:2
Rebuke your mother. Rebuke her, for she is not My wife, and I am not her husband. Tell her to remove adultery from her face and unfaithfulness from between her breasts. ⚠️
## Hosea 2:3
Otherwise, I will strip her naked, as on her birth’s day. I will make her like a dry desert and let her die of thirst. 🏜️
## Hosea 2:4
I will show her children no compassion because they are children of adultery. 💔
## Hosea 2:5
Their mother acted as a prostitute and conceived them in disgrace. She thought, ‘I will follow my lovers. They give me bread, water, wool, linen, oil, and drink.’ ⚠️
## Hosea 2:6
I will block her path with thorns and enclose her with a wall, so she cannot find her way. 🌵
## Hosea 2:7
She will chase her lovers without catching them and search without finding them. Then she will say, ‘I will return to my first husband. My life was better then.’ 🚶
## Hosea 2:8
She does not admit that I gave her grain, new wine, and oil. I generously gave her silver and gold, which they made into objects for Baal. ⚠️
## Hosea 2:9
I will take back My grain and new wine in their seasons. I will remove My wool and linen, which covered her nakedness. 🌾
## Hosea 2:10
I will expose her shameful behavior before her lovers. No one will rescue her from My hands. ⚖️
## Hosea 2:11
I will end all her celebrations: festivals, New Moons, Sabbaths, and every appointed feast. 📅
## Hosea 2:12
I will destroy her vines and fig trees, which she considers payment from her lovers. I will make them a thicket, and wild animals will eat them. 🌳
## Hosea 2:13
I will punish her for the days she burned incense to the Baals. She wore rings and jewelry and followed her lovers. She forgot Me,” declares the LORD. ⚖️
## Hosea 2:14
“Therefore, I will draw her to Me, lead her into the wilderness, and speak tenderly to her. ❤️
## Hosea 2:15
There I will restore her vineyards and make the Valley of Achor a gateway of hope. She will respond as in her youth, when she left Egypt. 🌅
## Hosea 2:16
“On that day,” declares the LORD, “you will call Me ‘my Husband’ instead of ‘my Master.’ 💍
## Hosea 2:17
I will remove the Baals’ names from her lips. No one will call on their names again. 🙏
## Hosea 2:18
On that day, I will make a covenant for them with wild animals, birds, and creatures that crawl. I will remove bows, swords, and war from the land. I will let them lie down safely. 🕊️
## Hosea 2:19
I will promise to marry you forever, in righteousness, justice, faithful love, and compassion. 💍
## Hosea 2:20
I will promise to marry you in faithfulness. You will know the LORD.” ❤️
## Hosea 2:21
“On that day, I will respond,” declares the LORD. “I will respond to the heavens, and they will respond to the earth. 🌧️
## Hosea 2:22
The earth will respond to grain, new wine, and oil. They will respond to Jezreel. 🌾
## Hosea 2:23
I will sow her as My own in the land and show compassion to ‘No Compassion.’ I will tell ‘Not My People,’ ‘You are My people.’ They will say, ‘You are my God.’” ❤️
