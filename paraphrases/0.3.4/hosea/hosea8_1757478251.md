## Hosea 8:1
“Put the ram’s horn to your lips! An eagle hangs over the LORD’s house because the people broke My covenant and rebelled against My law. 📯
## Hosea 8:2
Israel cries to Me, ‘Our God, we know You!’ 🙏
## Hosea 8:3
But Israel rejected good. An enemy will chase him. ⚠️
## Hosea 8:4
They appoint kings without Me and princes without My approval. They make silver and gold idols, bringing their own destruction. 👑
## Hosea 8:5
Samaria, He rejected your calf. My anger burns against them. How long will they remain unable to be innocent? ⚖️
## Hosea 8:6
This thing came from Israel. A craftsman made it, and it is not God. Samaria’s calf will be broken into pieces. 🔨
## Hosea 8:7
They sow wind and will harvest a whirlwind. No grain stands, and the sprouts produce no flour. Even if they produced grain, foreigners would consume it. 🌪️
## Hosea 8:8
Israel is swallowed up! Among the nations, they are like a worthless vessel. 🏺
## Hosea 8:9
They went to Assyria like a wild donkey wandering alone. Ephraim hired lovers. 🫏
## Hosea 8:10
Although they hire foreign allies, I will gather them up. They will begin to decrease under the oppression of the king of princes. ⚠️
## Hosea 8:11
Ephraim increased his altars for sin, but they became altars for sinning. 💔
## Hosea 8:12
I wrote the great things of My law for them, but they considered them strange. 📖
## Hosea 8:13
They offer Me sacrifices and eat the meat, but the LORD does not accept them. He will remember their wrongdoing and punish their sins. They will return to Egypt. ⚖️
## Hosea 8:14
Israel forgot his Maker and built palaces. Judah increased its fortified cities. I will send fire against their cities to consume their strongholds.” 🔥
