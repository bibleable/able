## Hosea 9:1
Israel, do not celebrate like the nations. You acted as a prostitute against your God. You made love for payment on every threshing floor. ⚠️
## Hosea 9:2
The threshing floor and winepress will not feed them. The new wine will fail them. 🌾
## Hosea 9:3
They will not remain in the LORD’s land. Ephraim will return to Egypt and eat unclean food in Assyria. 🚶
## Hosea 9:4
They will offer no wine to the LORD, and their sacrifices will not please Him. Those sacrifices will be like mourners’ bread, making everyone who eats them unclean. Their bread is only for themselves and will not enter the LORD’s house. 🍞
## Hosea 9:5
What will you do on the appointed day, the LORD’s feast day? 📅
## Hosea 9:6
Even if they escape destruction, Egypt will gather them, and Memphis will bury them. Thistles will cover their precious silver, and thorns will overrun their tents. 🌵
## Hosea 9:7
The days of punishment and repayment have arrived. Let Israel know this. People call the prophet foolish and the inspired man insane because of your great wrongdoing and hostility. ⚖️
## Hosea 9:8
The prophet is Ephraim’s watchman, along with my God. Yet a birdcatcher’s trap lies on every path he takes. There is hostility in his God’s house! 🪤
## Hosea 9:9
They deeply corrupted themselves, as in Gibeah’s days. He will remember their guilt and punish their sins. ⚠️
## Hosea 9:10
“I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness and saw your fathers like a fig tree’s first fruit in its first season. But they went to Baal-peor and dedicated themselves to Shame. They became as disgusting as what they loved. 🍇
## Hosea 9:11
Ephraim’s glory will fly away like a bird. There will be no birth, pregnancy, or conception. 🕊️
## Hosea 9:12
Even if they raise children, I will take every child from them. Woe to them when I turn away! 💔
## Hosea 9:13
I saw Ephraim planted in a meadow, like Tyre. But Ephraim will bring his children out for slaughter.” ⚠️
## Hosea 9:14
LORD, give them. What will You give? Give them wombs that miscarry and breasts that dry up! 🙏
## Hosea 9:15
“All their evil appears at Gilgal, where I hated them. I will drive them from My house for their wicked actions. I will no longer love them. All their leaders rebel. ⚖️
## Hosea 9:16
Ephraim is struck down. Their root is dry, and they cannot bear fruit. Even if they bear children, I will kill the cherished children of their wombs.” 💔
## Hosea 9:17
My God will reject them because they did not obey Him. They will wander among the nations. 🚶
