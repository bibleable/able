## Hosea 13:1
When Ephraim spoke, people trembled. He was exalted in Israel, but became guilty through Baal and died. ⚠️
## Hosea 13:2
They sin more and more. Craftsmen skillfully make cast images and silver idols for them. People say, “They offer human sacrifices and kiss the calves!” 💔
## Hosea 13:3
They will become like morning mist and early dew that disappears. They will be like chaff blown from a threshing floor and smoke through an open window. 🌬️
## Hosea 13:4
“I have been the LORD your God since Egypt. You know no God except Me. There is no other Savior. 🙏
## Hosea 13:5
I knew you in the wilderness, in the dry land. 🏜️
## Hosea 13:6
When they had pasture, they became satisfied. Satisfaction made them proud, so they forgot Me. ⚠️
## Hosea 13:7
I will attack them like a lion and wait by the path like a leopard. 🦁
## Hosea 13:8
I will attack like a bear robbed of her cubs and tear open their chests. There I will devour them like a lion, like a wild animal tearing them apart. 🐻
## Hosea 13:9
Israel, you are destroyed because you oppose Me, your helper. ⚖️
## Hosea 13:10
Where is your king to save you in all your cities? Where are the rulers you asked, ‘Give me a king and princes’? 👑
## Hosea 13:11
In anger, I gave you a king. In fury, I took him away. ⚖️
## Hosea 13:12
Ephraim’s wrongdoing is bound up, and his sin is stored away. 📜
## Hosea 13:13
Birth pains come on him, but he is an unwise son. At the proper time, he fails to appear at the womb’s opening. ⚠️
## Hosea 13:14
I will ransom them from Sheol’s power and redeem them from Death. Death, where are your plagues? Sheol, where is your sting? Compassion is hidden from My eyes. ⚖️
## Hosea 13:15
Although he flourishes among his brothers, an east wind from the LORD will rise from the desert. His fountain will fail, and his spring will dry up. The wind will take every precious item from his treasury. 🌬️
## Hosea 13:16
Samaria will bear guilt for rebelling against her God. They will die by the sword. Their little children will be smashed to pieces, and their pregnant women torn open.” 💔
