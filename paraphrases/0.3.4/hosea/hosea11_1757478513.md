## Hosea 11:1
“When Israel was a child, I loved him. I called My son out of Egypt. ❤️
## Hosea 11:2
The more I called Israel, the farther they went from Me. They sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to carved images. 💔
## Hosea 11:3
I taught Ephraim to walk and held their arms. They did not realize that I healed them. 👣
## Hosea 11:4
I led them with cords of kindness and ropes of love. I lifted the yoke from their necks and bent down to feed them. ❤️
## Hosea 11:5
Will they not return to Egypt and be ruled by Assyria because they refused to repent? ⚠️
## Hosea 11:6
A sword will flash through their cities and destroy their gate bars. It will consume them in their own plans. ⚔️
## Hosea 11:7
My people insist on turning away from Me. Although they call to the Most High, He will certainly not exalt them. 💔
## Hosea 11:8
Ephraim, how could I give you up? Israel, how could I surrender you? How could I make you like Admah or treat you like Zeboiim? My heart turns within Me. My compassion rises! ❤️
## Hosea 11:9
I will not release My full fury or destroy Ephraim again. I am God, the Holy One among you, rather than a man. I will not come in anger. 🕊️
## Hosea 11:10
They will follow the LORD, who will roar like a lion. When He roars, His children will come trembling from the west. 🦁
## Hosea 11:11
They will come trembling like birds from Egypt and like doves from Assyria. I will settle them in their homes,” declares the LORD. 🏠
## Hosea 11:12
“Ephraim surrounds Me with lies, and Israel’s house with deceit. Judah still walks with God and remains faithful to the Holy One. 🙏
