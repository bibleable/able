## Hosea 14:1
Israel, return to the LORD your God. You stumbled because of your wrongdoing. 🙏
## Hosea 14:2
Bring your confessions and return to the LORD. Tell Him, “Remove all our wrongdoing and receive us graciously, so we may offer the fruit of our lips. 🙏
## Hosea 14:3
Assyria will not save us, and we will not ride horses. We will never again call things we made ‘Our gods!’ In You, children without fathers find compassion.” ❤️
## Hosea 14:4
“I will heal their turning away from Me and love them freely. My anger has turned away from them. ❤️
## Hosea 14:5
I will be like dew to Israel. He will blossom like a lily and take root like Lebanon’s cedars. 🌸
## Hosea 14:6
His shoots will grow. His beauty will be like an olive tree, and his fragrance like Lebanon’s cedars. 🌳
## Hosea 14:7
They will return and live in his shade. They will grow grain and blossom like a vine. His fame will be like Lebanon’s wine. 🌾
## Hosea 14:8
Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols? I answer and watch over him. I am like a flourishing cypress, and your fruit comes from Me.” 🌲
## Hosea 14:9
Let the wise understand these things and those with insight know them. The LORD’s ways are right. Righteous people walk in them, but rebels stumble in them. 🚶
