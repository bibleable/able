## Hosea 1:1
The LORD’s word came to Hosea son of Beeri. Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah ruled Judah during this time. Jeroboam son of Jehoash ruled Israel. 📖
## Hosea 1:2
When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, He said, “Marry a prostitute and have children of adultery. This land openly acts like a prostitute by leaving the LORD.” ⚠️
## Hosea 1:3
Hosea married Gomer daughter of Diblaim. She became pregnant and bore him a son. 👶
## Hosea 1:4
The LORD told Hosea, “Name him Jezreel. Soon I will punish Jehu’s house for the bloodshed at Jezreel. I will end Israel’s kingdom. ⚖️
## Hosea 1:5
On that day, I will break Israel’s bow in the Valley of Jezreel.” 🏹
## Hosea 1:6
Gomer became pregnant again and bore a daughter. The LORD told Hosea, “Name her Lo-ruhamah. I will no longer show compassion to Israel’s house or forgive them. ⚠️
## Hosea 1:7
But I will show compassion to Judah’s house and save them through the LORD their God. Bows, swords, war, horses, and cavalry will not save them.” 🛡️
## Hosea 1:8
After Gomer stopped nursing Lo-ruhamah, she became pregnant and bore a son. 👶
## Hosea 1:9
The LORD said, “Name him Lo-ammi. You are not My people, and I am not your God. ⚠️
## Hosea 1:10
Yet the Israelites will be as numerous as the sea’s sand, beyond measurement or counting. Where they heard, ‘You are not My people,’ they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’ 🌅
## Hosea 1:11
Judah and Israel will gather together and appoint one leader. They will go up out of the land. The day of Jezreel will be great.” 🤝
