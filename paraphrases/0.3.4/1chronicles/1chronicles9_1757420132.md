## 1 Chronicles 9:1
All Israel was recorded in genealogies in the Book of the Kings of Israel. Judah was exiled to Babylon because of its unfaithfulness. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:2
Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants were the first to resettle their own property in their cities. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:3
Some descendants of Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh lived in Jerusalem. They are listed next. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:4
Uthai was Ammihud’s son. Ammihud was Omri’s son, Omri was Imri’s son, and Imri was Bani’s son. Bani descended from Perez, Judah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:5
From the Shilonites came Asaiah the firstborn and his sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:6
From the Zerahites came Jeuel and 690 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:7
From the Benjamites came Sallu, Meshullam’s son. Meshullam was Hodaviah’s son, and Hodaviah was Hassenuah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:8
Ibneiah was Jeroham’s son. Elah was Uzzi’s son, and Uzzi was Michri’s son. Meshullam was Shephatiah’s son, Shephatiah was Reuel’s son, and Reuel was Ibnijah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:9
Their genealogy listed 956 relatives. All these men were family heads. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:10
The priests included Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:11
Azariah, God’s temple’s chief official, was Hilkiah’s son. Hilkiah was Meshullam’s son, and Meshullam was Zadok’s son. Zadok was Meraioth’s son, and Meraioth was Ahitub’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:12
Adaiah was Jeroham’s son, Jeroham was Pashhur’s son, and Pashhur was Malchijah’s son. Maasai was Adiel’s son, Adiel was Jahzerah’s son, and Jahzerah was Meshullam’s son. Meshullam was Meshillemith’s son, and Meshillemith was Immer’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:13
They had 1,760 relatives, family heads who were capable of serving in God’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:14
The Levites included Shemaiah, Hasshub’s son. Hasshub was Azrikam’s son, and Azrikam was Hashabiah’s son. Hashabiah descended from Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:15
They also included Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah. Mattaniah was Mica’s son, Mica was Zichri’s son, and Zichri was Asaph’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:16
Obadiah was Shemaiah’s son, Shemaiah was Galal’s son, and Galal was Jeduthun’s son. Berechiah was Asa’s son, and Asa was Elkanah’s son. Berechiah lived in the Netophathites’ villages. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:17
The gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their relatives. Shallum was chief. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:18
He previously served at the King’s Gate on the east side. These were the gatekeepers from the Levites’ camp. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:19
Shallum was Kore’s son, Kore was Ebiasaph’s son, and Ebiasaph was Korah’s son. Shallum and his Korahite relatives guarded the Tent’s thresholds. Their fathers had guarded the entrance to the LORD’s dwelling. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:20
Earlier, Phinehas, Eleazar’s son, supervised the gatekeepers. The LORD was with him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:21
Zechariah, Meshelemiah’s son, guarded the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:22
There were 212 chosen gatekeepers at the thresholds. Their villages registered them by genealogy. David and Samuel the seer appointed them to these trusted positions. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:23
They and their descendants guarded the gates of the LORD’s house, called the Tent. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:24
The gatekeepers served on all four sides: east, west, north, and south. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:25
Their relatives came from their villages at fixed times. They served with them for seven days at a time. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:26
The four chief gatekeepers were Levites. They were entrusted with the rooms and treasuries of God’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:27
They stayed around God’s house overnight. They were responsible for guarding it and opening it every morning. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:28
Some supervised the articles used in worship. They counted them whenever they were brought in or taken out. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:29
Others supervised the sanctuary’s furnishings and articles. They also supervised the fine flour, wine, oil, frankincense, and spices. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:30
Some priests’ sons mixed the spices. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:31
The Levite Mattithiah supervised bread baking. He was the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:32
Some Kohathite relatives prepared the rows of showbread every Sabbath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:33
The musicians were Levite family heads. They stayed in the temple chambers. They had no other duties because they served day and night. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:34
All these were Levite family heads, chiefs according to their genealogies. They lived in Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:35
Jeiel, Gibeon’s father, lived in Gibeon. His wife was Maacah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:36
His firstborn was Abdon. Then came Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, and Nadab. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:37
His sons also included Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:38
Mikloth was Shimeam’s father. They too lived alongside their relatives in Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:39
Ner was Kish’s father. Kish was Saul’s father. Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:40
Jonathan’s son was Merib-baal. Merib-baal was Micah’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:41
Micah’s sons were Pithon, Melech, Tahrea, and Ahaz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:42
Ahaz was Jarah’s father. Jarah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri was Moza’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:43
Moza was Binea’s father. Binea’s son was Rephaiah. Rephaiah’s son was Elasah. Elasah’s son was Azel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 9:44
Azel’s six sons were Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were Azel’s sons. 📖
