## 1 Chronicles 5:1
These were the sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn. Reuben defiled his father’s bed. So his birthright passed to the sons of Joseph, Israel’s son. Reuben is therefore not listed according to birthright. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:2
Judah prevailed over his brothers, and a ruler came from him. But Joseph held the birthright. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:3
Reuben, Israel’s firstborn, had these sons: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:4
Joel’s son was Shemaiah. Shemaiah’s son was Gog. Gog’s son was Shimei. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:5
Shimei’s son was Micah. Micah’s son was Reaiah. Reaiah’s son was Baal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:6
Baal’s son was Beerah, a Reubenite leader. Tiglath-pileser, Assyria’s king, took Beerah into exile. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:7
His relatives were recorded by clan in their genealogy. They included Jeiel the chief and Zechariah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:8
They also included Bela, Azaz’s son. Azaz was Shema’s son, and Shema was Joel’s son. They settled in Aroer and as far as Nebo and Baal-meon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:9
Their livestock increased in Gilead. So they also settled eastward, as far as the edge of the desert extending to the Euphrates River. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:10
During Saul’s days, they fought and defeated the Hagrites. They occupied the Hagrites’ homes throughout the region east of Gilead. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:11
Gad’s descendants lived beside the Reubenites in Bashan’s land, as far as Salecah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:12
Joel was chief, and Shapham was second. Next were Jaanai and Shaphat, who lived in Bashan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:13
Their relatives by family were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber, seven in all. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:14
These were Abihail’s sons. Abihail’s father was Huri, whose father was Jaroah, whose father was Gilead. Gilead’s father was Michael, whose father was Jeshishai, whose father was Jahdo, whose father was Buz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:15
Ahi led their family. He was Abdiel’s son, and Abdiel was Guni’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:16
They lived in Gilead, Bashan and its towns, and throughout Sharon’s pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:17
All were recorded in genealogies during the reigns of Jotham, Judah’s king, and Jeroboam, Israel’s king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:18
The Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh had 44,760 warriors. These brave men carried shields and swords, drew bows, and were trained for battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:19
They fought the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:20
They cried to God in battle. He helped them and delivered the Hagrites and all their allies into their hands. God answered their prayers because they trusted Him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:21
They seized the Hagrites’ livestock: 50,000 camels, 250,000 sheep, and 2,000 donkeys. They also took 100,000 captives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:22
Many others were killed because the battle belonged to God. They occupied the land until the exile. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:23
The half-tribe of Manasseh was numerous. They settled from Bashan to Baal-hermon, also called Senir and Mount Hermon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:24
Their family heads were Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel. They were famous, mighty warriors and heads of their families. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:25
They were unfaithful to their fathers’ God. They prostituted themselves with the gods of the land’s peoples, whom God had destroyed before them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 5:26
Israel’s God stirred the spirit of Pul, Assyria’s king, also called Tiglath-pileser. He exiled the Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh. He brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river of Gozan. They remain there to this day. 📖
