## 1 Chronicles 12:1
The following men joined David at Ziklag while he was banished from Saul, Kish’s son. They were mighty men who helped him in battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:2
They were archers, able to sling stones and shoot arrows with either hand. They were Saul’s relatives from Benjamin. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:3
They included their chief Ahiezer and Joash, Shemaah the Gibeathite’s sons. They also included Jeziel and Pelet, Azmaveth’s sons, Beracah, and Jehu the Anathothite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:4
Ishmaiah the Gibeonite was a mighty man among the Thirty and a leader over them. Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Jozabad the Gederathite also came. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:5
Others were Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:6
Others were Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam, who were Korahites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:7
Others were Joelah and Zebadiah, Jeroham’s sons from Gedor. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:8
Some Gadites defected to David at his wilderness stronghold. These mighty warriors were trained for battle and skilled with shield and spear. Their faces were like lions’ faces. They were as swift as gazelles on the mountains. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:9
Ezer was chief, Obadiah second in command, and Eliab third. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:10
Mishmannah was fourth, and Jeremiah was fifth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:11
Attai was sixth, and Eliel was seventh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:12
Johanan was eighth, and Elzabad was ninth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:13
Jeremiah was tenth, and Machbanai was eleventh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:14
These Gadites commanded armies. The least was a match for a hundred, and the greatest for a thousand. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:15
They crossed the Jordan in the first month while it overflowed all its banks. They made everyone in the valleys flee, both east and west. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:16
Other Benjamites and some men from Judah came to David’s stronghold. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:17
David met them and said, “If you come peacefully to help me, my heart will unite with you. If you come to betray me to my enemies, though my hands are free of violence, may our fathers’ God see and judge you.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:18
The Spirit came upon Amasai, chief of the Thirty. He said, “We are yours, David! We are with you, Jesse’s son! Peace, peace to you and your helpers, because your God helps you.” David accepted them and appointed them as troop leaders. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:19
Some from Manasseh defected to David when he went with the Philistines to fight Saul. They did not help the Philistines. The Philistine rulers consulted and sent David away. They said, “We will lose our heads if he returns to his master Saul.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:20
When David went to Ziklag, men from Manasseh defected to him. They were Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai, commanders of thousands in Manasseh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:21
They helped David against the raiders. They were all mighty warriors and army commanders. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:22
Men came daily to help David then. His army became great, like God’s army. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:23
The following numbers record armed men who came to David at Hebron. They came to give him Saul’s kingdom according to the LORD’s word. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:24
Judah sent 6,800 armed troops with shields and spears. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:25
Simeon sent 7,100 mighty warriors ready for battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:26
Levi sent 4,600 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:27
These included Jehoiada, leader of Aaron’s house, with 3,700 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:28
They also included Zadok, a mighty young warrior, with 22 commanders from his own family. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:29
Benjamin, Saul’s relatives, sent 3,000 men. Most had remained loyal to Saul’s house until then. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:30
Ephraim sent 20,800 mighty warriors, famous in their clans. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:31
Manasseh’s half-tribe sent 18,000 men. They were designated by name to come and make David king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:32
Issachar sent 200 chiefs with all their relatives under their command. These men understood the times and knew what Israel should do. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:33
Zebulun sent 50,000 men fit for service and trained with every kind of weapon. They were united in devotion to David. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:34
Naphtali sent 1,000 commanders with 37,000 men carrying shields and spears. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:35
Dan sent 28,600 men prepared for battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:36
Asher sent 40,000 men fit for service and prepared for battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:37
Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh’s half-tribe east of the Jordan sent 120,000 men armed with every kind of weapon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:38
These warriors came to Hebron ready for battle and determined to make David king over all Israel. All the other Israelites agreed to make him king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:39
They ate and drank with David there for three days. Their relatives had provided for them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 12:40
Neighbors as far away as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali brought food on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen. They brought abundant flour, fig cakes, raisin cakes, wine, oil, oxen, and sheep. Israel was joyful. 📖
