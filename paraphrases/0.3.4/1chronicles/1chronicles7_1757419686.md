## 1 Chronicles 7:1
Issachar’s four sons were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:2
Tola’s sons were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Shemuel, heads of their families. During David’s days, their genealogies listed 22,600 descendants of Tola as mighty warriors. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:3
Uzzi’s son was Izrahiah. Izrahiah’s sons were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah. All five were chiefs. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:4
Their genealogy listed another 36,000 troops for battle because they had many wives and children. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:5
Their relatives from all Issachar’s families included 87,000 mighty warriors. Their genealogies recorded this total. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:6
Benjamin’s three sons were Bela, Becher, and Jediael. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:7
Bela’s five sons were Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri, heads of their families. Their genealogies listed 22,034 mighty warriors. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:8
Becher’s sons were Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. All these were Becher’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:9
Their genealogies were recorded by family heads. They listed 20,200 mighty warriors. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:10
Jediael’s son was Bilhan. Bilhan’s sons were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:11
All these sons of Jediael were family heads and mighty warriors. There were 17,200 men fit for battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:12
The Shuppites and Huppites descended from Ir. The Hushites descended from Aher. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:13
Naphtali’s sons were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum. They were Bilhah’s descendants. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:14
Manasseh’s descendants included Asriel, through his Aramean concubine. She also bore Machir, Gilead’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:15
Machir married a woman from the Huppites and Shuppites. His sister was Maacah. Another descendant, Zelophehad, had only daughters. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:16
Machir’s wife Maacah bore a son and named him Peresh. His brother was Sheresh. His sons were Ulam and Rekem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:17
Ulam’s son was Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead, Machir’s son. Machir was Manasseh’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:18
His sister Hammolecheth bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:19
Shemida’s sons were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:20
Ephraim’s descendants included Shuthelah. Shuthelah’s son was Bered, whose son was Tahath. Tahath’s son was Eleadah, whose son was Tahath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:21
Tahath’s son was Zabad, whose son was Shuthelah. Gath’s natives killed Ezer and Elead because they went down to steal their livestock. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:22
Their father Ephraim mourned many days. His relatives came to comfort him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:23
He slept with his wife again. She conceived and bore a son. He named him Beriah because tragedy had struck his household. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:24
His daughter Sheerah built Lower and Upper Beth-horon and Uzzen-sheerah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:25
His son was also Rephah. Rephah’s son was Resheph, whose son was Telah, whose son was Tahan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:26
Tahan’s son was Ladan. Ladan’s son was Ammihud. Ammihud’s son was Elishama. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:27
Elishama’s son was Nun. Nun’s son was Joshua. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:28
Their holdings and settlements included Bethel and its villages, Naaran eastward, and Gezer and its villages westward. They also included Shechem and its villages as far as Ayyah and its villages. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:29
Along Manasseh’s borders were Beth-shean, Taanach, Megiddo, and Dor, with their villages. Descendants of Joseph, Israel’s son, lived in these towns. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:30
Asher’s children were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:31
Beriah’s sons were Heber and Malchiel. Malchiel was Birzaith’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:32
Heber was the father of Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and their sister Shua. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:33
Japhlet’s sons were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were Japhlet’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:34
Shemer’s sons were Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:35
His brother Helem’s sons were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:36
Zophah’s sons included Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, and Imrah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:37
They also included Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:38
Jether’s sons were Jephunneh, Pispa, and Ara. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:39
Ulla’s sons were Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 7:40
All these were Asher’s descendants, family heads, choice and mighty warriors, and chiefs among leaders. Their genealogies listed 26,000 men fit for battle. 📖
