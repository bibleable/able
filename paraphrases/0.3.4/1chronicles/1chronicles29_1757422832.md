## 1 Chronicles 29:1
David told the whole assembly, “God chose my son Solomon, who is young and inexperienced. The task is great. This palace is for the LORD God, not for a person. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:2
With all my ability, I supplied materials for my God’s house. I provided gold, silver, bronze, iron, and wood for articles of each material. I also supplied abundant onyx for settings, turquoise, stones of varied colors, every kind of precious stone, and marble slabs. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:3
I delight in my God’s house. So I give my personal gold and silver treasures beyond everything I supplied for this holy temple. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:4
I give three thousand talents of Ophir’s gold and seven thousand talents of refined silver to cover the buildings’ walls. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:5
These are for gold work, silver work, and all the craftsmen’s work. Who will willingly consecrate himself to the LORD today?” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:6
Family leaders, Israel’s tribal officers, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and officials supervising the king’s work gave willingly. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:7
For service in God’s house, they gave 5,000 talents and 10,000 darics of gold. They gave 10,000 talents of silver, 18,000 talents of bronze, and 100,000 talents of iron. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:8
Anyone with precious stones gave them to the LORD’s house’s treasury. Jehiel the Gershonite supervised it. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:9
The people rejoiced because their leaders gave freely and wholeheartedly to the LORD. King David also rejoiced greatly. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:10
David blessed the LORD before the whole assembly. He said, “Blessed be You, LORD, God of our father Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:11
LORD, greatness, power, glory, splendor, and majesty are Yours. Everything in heaven and earth belongs to You. The kingdom is Yours, LORD. You are exalted as head over everything. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:12
Riches and honor come from You. You rule everything. Your hands hold power and might to exalt and strengthen everyone. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:13
Our God, we thank You and praise Your glorious name. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:14
Who am I, and who are my people, to give so generously? Everything comes from You. We give You what came from Your own hand. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:15
We are foreigners and strangers before You, as all our forefathers were. Our earthly days are like a shadow, without hope. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:16
LORD our God, all this abundance for building a house for Your holy name comes from Your hand. It all belongs to You. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:17
My God, I know You test hearts and delight in uprightness. I willingly gave these things with an upright heart. I now see Your people here giving joyfully and willingly to You. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:18
LORD, God of our fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, keep this desire in Your people’s hearts forever. Direct their hearts toward You. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:19
Give my son Solomon a whole heart to obey all Your commands, decrees, and statutes. May he build Your palace, for which I prepared.” 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 29:20
David told the assembly, “Bless the LORD your God.” They all blessed the LORD, their fathers’ God. They bowed and paid homage to the LORD and the king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:21
The next day, they sacrificed and presented burnt offerings to the LORD. These included a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings. They offered many other sacrifices for all Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:22
They ate and drank joyfully before the LORD that day. They designated David’s son Solomon king a second time, anointing him before the LORD as ruler. They anointed Zadok as priest. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:23
Solomon sat on the LORD’s throne as king in his father David’s place. He prospered, and all Israel obeyed him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:24
All officials, mighty men, and King David’s sons pledged allegiance to King Solomon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:25
The LORD greatly exalted Solomon before all Israel. He gave him royal majesty greater than any previous king in Israel had received. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:26
David, Jesse’s son, was king over all Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:27
He ruled Israel forty years: seven in Hebron and thirty-three in Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:28
He died at a ripe old age, full of years, riches, and honor. His son Solomon ruled in his place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:29
King David’s deeds, from first to last, are written in three records. They are the Chronicles of Samuel the Seer, Nathan the Prophet, and Gad the Seer. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 29:30
They record all his reign’s details, his might, and the circumstances affecting him, Israel, and all the lands’ kingdoms. 📖
