## 1 Chronicles 1:1
The lineage begins with Adam, Seth, and Enosh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:2
It continues with Kenan, Mahalalel, and Jared. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:3
Next come Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, and Noah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:4
Noah’s sons were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:5
Japheth’s sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:6
Gomer’s sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:7
Javan’s sons were Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittites, and the Rodanites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:8
Ham’s sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:9
Cush’s sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabteca. Raamah’s sons were Sheba and Dedan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:10
Cush was Nimrod’s father. Nimrod began to be a mighty one on earth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:11
Mizraim was the father of the Ludites, Anamites, Lehabites, and Naphtuhites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:12
He was also the father of the Pathrusites, Casluhites, and Caphtorites. The Philistines came from the Casluhites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:13
Canaan was the father of Sidon, his firstborn, and the Hittites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:14
He was also the father of the Jebusites, Amorites, and Girgashites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:15
He was also the father of the Hivites, Arkites, and Sinites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:16
He was also the father of the Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:17
Shem’s sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. Aram’s sons were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:18
Arphaxad was Shelah’s father. Shelah was Eber’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:19
Eber had two sons. One was Peleg, because the earth was divided in his days. His brother was Joktan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:20
Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:21
He was also the father of Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:22
He was also the father of Obal, Abimael, and Sheba. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:23
He was also the father of Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were Joktan’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:24
The lineage from Shem continues with Arphaxad and Shelah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:25
Next come Eber, Peleg, and Reu. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:26
Next come Serug, Nahor, and Terah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:27
Then comes Abram, also called Abraham. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:28
Abraham’s sons were Isaac and Ishmael. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:29
Their genealogies follow. Ishmael’s firstborn was Nebaioth, followed by Kedar, Adbeel, and Mibsam. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:30
His sons also included Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:31
His sons also included Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These were Ishmael’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:32
Abraham’s concubine Keturah bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. Jokshan’s sons were Sheba and Dedan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:33
Midian’s sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were Keturah’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:34
Abraham was Isaac’s father. Isaac’s sons were Esau and Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:35
Esau’s sons were Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:36
Eliphaz’s sons were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. He also had Amalek by Timna. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:37
Reuel’s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:38
Seir’s sons were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:39
Lotan’s sons were Hori and Homam. Timna was Lotan’s sister. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:40
Shobal’s sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. Zibeon’s sons were Aiah and Anah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:41
Anah’s son was Dishon. Dishon’s sons were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:42
Ezer’s sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. Dishan’s sons were Uz and Aran. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:43
These kings ruled Edom before any king ruled Israel. Bela, Beor’s son, was king. His city was Dinhabah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:44
Bela died. Jobab, Zerah’s son from Bozrah, became king in his place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:45
Jobab died. Husham from the Temanites’ land became king in his place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:46
Husham died. Hadad, Bedad’s son, became king in his place. Hadad defeated Midian in Moab’s country. His city was Avith. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:47
Hadad died. Samlah from Masrekah became king in his place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:48
Samlah died. Shaul from Rehoboth on the Euphrates became king in his place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:49
Shaul died. Baal-hanan, Achbor’s son, became king in his place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:50
Baal-hanan died. Hadad became king in his place. His city was Pau. His wife was Mehetabel, Matred’s daughter and Me-zahab’s granddaughter. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:51
Then Hadad died. Edom’s chiefs included Timna, Alvah, and Jetheth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:52
The chiefs also included Oholibamah, Elah, and Pinon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:53
The chiefs also included Kenaz, Teman, and Mibzar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 1:54
The chiefs also included Magdiel and Iram. These were Edom’s chiefs. 📖
