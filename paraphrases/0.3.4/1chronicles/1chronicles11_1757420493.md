## 1 Chronicles 11:1
All Israel gathered to David in Hebron. They said, “We are your own flesh and blood. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:2
Even when Saul was king, you led Israel out and brought them back. The LORD your God said, ‘You will shepherd My people Israel. You will rule them.’” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:3
All Israel’s elders came to the king at Hebron. David made a covenant with them before the LORD. They anointed him king over Israel, as the LORD said through Samuel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:4
David and all the Israelites marched to Jerusalem, also called Jebus. Jebusites lived there. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:5
Jebus’ people told David, “You will never enter here.” But David captured Zion’s fortress, called the City of David. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:6
David had said, “The first to strike down a Jebusite will become chief commander.” Joab, Zeruiah’s son, went up first and became chief. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:7
David lived in the fortress. That is why it was called the City of David. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:8
He built up the city around it, from the supporting terraces to the surrounding wall. Joab restored the rest of the city. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:9
David became increasingly great because the LORD of Hosts was with him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:10
The following men were chiefs among David’s mighty men. Together with all Israel, they strengthened his kingdom according to the LORD’s word concerning Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:11
David’s mighty men are listed here. Jashobeam, Hachmoni’s son, was chief of the officers. He used his spear to kill three hundred men at one time. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:12
Eleazar, Dodo the Ahohite’s son, was next in command. He was one of the three mighty men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:13
He was with David at Pas-dammim when the Philistines gathered for battle. At a field full of barley, the troops fled from the Philistines. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:14
Eleazar and David stood in the field’s middle and defended it. They killed the Philistines. The LORD gave a great victory. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:15
Three of the thirty chief men went down to David at the rock by Adullam’s cave. A Philistine company camped in the Valley of Rephaim. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:16
David was in the stronghold then. The Philistine garrison was at Bethlehem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:17
David longed for water. He said, “If only someone would bring me water from the well by Bethlehem’s gate!” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:18
The Three broke through the Philistine camp. They drew water from the well at Bethlehem’s gate and brought it to David. He refused to drink it and poured it out to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:19
He said, “My God, may I never do this! How can I drink the blood of men who risked their lives?” He refused to drink because they risked their lives to bring it. Such were the three mighty men’s deeds. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:20
Abishai, Joab’s brother, was chief of the Three. He killed three hundred men with his spear. He became renowned along with the Three. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:21
He received twice the honor of the Three and became their commander. But he was not included among the Three. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:22
Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son from Kabzeel, was a brave man with many great deeds. He killed two champions of Moab. On a snowy day, he entered a pit and killed a lion. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:23
He killed an Egyptian five cubits tall. The Egyptian carried a spear like a weaver’s beam. Benaiah approached with a club and seized the spear. He killed the Egyptian with that man’s own spear. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:24
These were Benaiah’s deeds. He was Jehoiada’s son and became renowned along with the three mighty men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:25
He was most honored among the Thirty but did not join the Three. David appointed him over his guard. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:26
The mighty men included Asahel, Joab’s brother, and Elhanan, Dodo’s son from Bethlehem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:27
They included Shammoth the Harorite and Helez the Pelonite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:28
They included Ira, Ikkesh the Tekoite’s son, and Abiezer the Anathothite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:29
They included Sibbecai the Hushathite and Ilai the Ahohite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:30
They included Maharai the Netophathite and Heled, Baanah the Netophathite’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:31
They included Ithai, Ribai’s son from Gibeah of the Benjamites, and Benaiah the Pirathonite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:32
They included Hurai from Gaash’s brooks and Abiel the Arbathite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:33
They included Azmaveth the Baharumite and Eliahba the Shaalbonite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:34
They included Hashem the Gizonite’s sons and Jonathan, Shagee the Hararite’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:35
They included Ahiam, Sachar the Hararite’s son, and Eliphal, Ur’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:36
They included Hepher the Mecherathite and Ahijah the Pelonite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:37
They included Hezro the Carmelite and Naarai, Ezbai’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:38
They included Joel, Nathan’s brother, and Mibhar, Hagri’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:39
They included Zelek the Ammonite and Naharai the Beerothite. Naharai carried armor for Joab, Zeruiah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:40
They included Ira the Ithrite and Gareb the Ithrite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:41
They included Uriah the Hittite and Zabad, Ahlai’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:42
They included Adina, Shiza the Reubenite’s son, a Reubenite chief, and thirty men with him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:43
They included Hanan, Maacah’s son, and Joshaphat the Mithnite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:44
They included Uzzia the Ashterathite, and Shama and Jeiel, Hotham the Aroerite’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:45
They included Jediael, Shimri’s son, and his brother Joha the Tizite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:46
They included Eliel the Mahavite, Jeribai and Joshaviah, Elnaam’s sons, and Ithmah the Moabite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 11:47
They included Eliel, Obed, and Jaasiel the Mezobaite. 📖
