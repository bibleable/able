## 1 Chronicles 13:1
David consulted all his leaders, the commanders of thousands and hundreds. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:2
He told all Israel’s assembly, “If you agree and this comes from the LORD our God, let us send word throughout Israel. Invite our remaining brothers and the priests and Levites in their cities and pasturelands to join us. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:3
Let us bring back our God’s ark. We did not inquire of Him during Saul’s days.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:4
The whole assembly agreed. All the people thought the proposal was right. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:5
David gathered all Israel, from Egypt’s River Shihor to Lebo-hamath. They would bring God’s ark from Kiriath-jearim. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:6
David and all Israel went up to Baalah of Judah, also called Kiriath-jearim, to bring back the ark of God the LORD. He is enthroned between the cherubim. The ark was called by the Name. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:7
They carried God’s ark from Abinadab’s house on a new cart. Uzzah and Ahio guided the cart. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:8
David and all Israel celebrated before God with all their might. They sang and played harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:9
They reached Chidon’s threshing floor. The oxen stumbled, so Uzzah reached out and held the ark. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:10
The LORD’s anger burned against Uzzah. He struck him down because he touched the ark. Uzzah died there before God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:11
David became angry because the LORD burst out against Uzzah. He named the place Perez-uzzah. It retains that name to this day. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:12
David feared God that day. He asked, “How can I bring God’s ark to me?” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:13
He did not bring the ark to the City of David. He took it instead to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 13:14
God’s ark stayed with Obed-edom’s family in his house for three months. The LORD blessed his household and everything he owned. 📖
