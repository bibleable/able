## 1 Chronicles 23:1
David was old and full of years. He made his son Solomon king over Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:2
He gathered all Israel’s leaders, priests, and Levites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:3
The Levite men aged thirty or older were counted. There were 38,000. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:4
David said, “24,000 will oversee work in the LORD’s house. 6,000 will serve as officers and judges. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:5
4,000 will guard the gates. 4,000 will praise the LORD with the instruments I made for praise.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:6
David divided the Levites according to Levi’s sons: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:7
The Gershonites included Ladan and Shimei. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:8
Ladan’s three sons were Jehiel, the first, Zetham, and Joel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:9
Shimei’s three sons were Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran. These were Ladan’s family heads. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:10
Shimei’s four sons were Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:11
Jahath was first, and Zizah was second. Jeush and Beriah had few sons. They were counted as one family with one assignment. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:12
Kohath’s four sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:13
Amram’s sons were Aaron and Moses. Aaron and his descendants were set apart forever to consecrate the most holy things. They would burn incense before the LORD, serve Him, and pronounce blessings in His name forever. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:14
Moses was the man of God. His sons were listed among Levi’s tribe. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:15
Moses’ sons were Gershom and Eliezer. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:16
Shebuel was first among Gershom’s descendants. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:17
Rehabiah was first among Eliezer’s descendants. Eliezer had no other sons. Rehabiah’s sons were very numerous. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:18
Shelomith was first among Izhar’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:19
Hebron’s first son was Jeriah. Amariah was second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:20
Uzziel’s first son was Micah. Isshiah was second. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:21
Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. Mahli’s sons were Eleazar and Kish. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:22
Eleazar died with daughters but no sons. Kish’s sons, their cousins, married them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:23
Mushi’s three sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:24
These were Levi’s descendants by family, the family heads registered individually by name. Those aged twenty or older served in the LORD’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:25
David said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, gave His people rest. He has come to live in Jerusalem forever. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:26
The Levites no longer need to carry the tabernacle or any articles used in its service.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:27
David’s final instructions required counting Levites aged twenty or older. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:28
Their duty was to help Aaron’s descendants serve in the LORD’s house. They supervised the courts and chambers, the purification of all holy things, and service work in God’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:29
They prepared the showbread rows, fine flour for grain offerings, and unleavened bread wafers. They supervised baking, mixing, and all measurements of quantity and size. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:30
They stood every morning and evening to thank and praise the LORD. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:31
They served regularly before the LORD in prescribed numbers when burnt offerings were presented. This included Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 23:32
The Levites carried out duties for the Tent of Meeting and the Holy Place. Under their relatives, Aaron’s descendants, they served in the LORD’s house. 📖
