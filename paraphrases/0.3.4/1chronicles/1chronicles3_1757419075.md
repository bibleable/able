## 1 Chronicles 3:1
David’s sons born in Hebron follow. Amnon, by Ahinoam of Jezreel, was firstborn. Daniel, by Abigail of Carmel, was second. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:2
Absalom was third. His mother, Maacah, was King Talmai of Geshur’s daughter. Adonijah, Haggith’s son, was fourth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:3
Shephatiah, by Abital, was fifth. Ithream, by David’s wife Eglah, was sixth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:4
These six sons were born in Hebron. David ruled there seven years and six months. He ruled in Jerusalem thirty-three years. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:5
His sons born in Jerusalem were Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. Bathsheba, Ammiel’s daughter, bore these four to him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:6
David’s other sons included Ibhar, Elishua, and Eliphelet. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:7
They also included Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:8
They also included Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet, making nine in all. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:9
These were all David’s sons, apart from his concubines’ sons. Tamar was their sister. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:10
Solomon’s son was Rehoboam. Rehoboam’s son was Abijah. Abijah’s son was Asa. Asa’s son was Jehoshaphat. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:11
Jehoshaphat’s son was Joram. Joram’s son was Ahaziah. Ahaziah’s son was Joash. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:12
Joash’s son was Amaziah. Amaziah’s son was Azariah. Azariah’s son was Jotham. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:13
Jotham’s son was Ahaz. Ahaz’s son was Hezekiah. Hezekiah’s son was Manasseh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:14
Manasseh’s son was Amon. Amon’s son was Josiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:15
Josiah’s firstborn was Johanan. Jehoiakim was second, Zedekiah third, and Shallum fourth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:16
Jehoiakim’s successors were his son Jeconiah and Zedekiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:17
Jeconiah was a captive. His descendants included his son Shealtiel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:18
They also included Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:19
Pedaiah’s sons were Zerubbabel and Shimei. Zerubbabel’s children included Meshullam, Hananiah, and their sister Shelomith. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:20
His children also included five others: Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:21
Hananiah’s descendants were Pelatiah, Jeshaiah, and the sons of Rephaiah, Arnan, Obadiah, and Shecaniah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:22
Shecaniah’s six descendants were Shemaiah and Shemaiah’s sons: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:23
Neariah’s three sons were Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 3:24
Elioenai’s seven sons were Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani. 📖
