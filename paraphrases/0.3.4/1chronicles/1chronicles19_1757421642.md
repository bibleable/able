## 1 Chronicles 19:1
Later, Nahash, the Ammonites’ king, died. His son became king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:2
David said, “I will show kindness to Hanun, Nahash’s son. His father showed me kindness.” He sent messengers to comfort Hanun about his father. David’s servants arrived in Ammonite land to comfort him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:3
The Ammonite princes told Hanun, “Do David’s comforters really show respect for your father? Have his servants not come to explore, spy out, and overthrow the land?” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:4
Hanun seized David’s servants. He shaved their beards, cut their garments off at the hips, and sent them away. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:5
David heard what happened and sent messengers to meet his deeply humiliated men. He told them, “Stay in Jericho until your beards grow back. Then return.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:6
The Ammonites realized they had become a stench to David. Hanun and the Ammonites sent a thousand talents of silver. They hired chariots and horsemen from Aram-naharaim, Aram-maacah, and Zobah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:7
They hired thirty-two thousand chariots and Maacah’s king with his troops. These forces camped near Medeba. The Ammonites gathered from their cities and marched into battle. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:8
David heard this and sent Joab with the entire army of mighty men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:9
The Ammonites formed their battle line at the city entrance. The kings who came stayed separately in the open country. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:10
Joab saw enemy lines ahead and behind. He chose some of Israel’s best men and positioned them against the Arameans. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:11
He gave the remaining troops to his brother Abishai. Abishai positioned them against the Ammonites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:12
Joab said, “If the Arameans overpower me, rescue me. If the Ammonites overpower you, I will rescue you. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:13
Be strong. Let us fight bravely for our people and our God’s cities. May the LORD do what He sees as good.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:14
Joab and his troops advanced against the Arameans. The Arameans fled. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:15
The Ammonites saw the Arameans flee. They fled from Joab’s brother Abishai into the city. Joab returned to Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:16
The Arameans saw Israel had defeated them. They sent messengers for more Arameans beyond the Euphrates. Shophach, commander of Hadadezer’s army, led them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:17
David heard the report and gathered all Israel. He crossed the Jordan, approached the Arameans, and formed his battle line. They fought against him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:18
The Arameans fled from Israel. David killed seven thousand charioteers and forty thousand foot soldiers. He also killed Shophach, their army commander. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 19:19
Hadadezer’s subjects saw Israel had defeated them. They made peace with David and became his subjects. The Arameans no longer wanted to help the Ammonites. 📖
