## 1 Chronicles 4:1
Judah’s descendants were Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:2
Reaiah, Shobal’s son, was Jahath’s father. Jahath was Ahumai and Lahad’s father. These were the Zorathites’ clans. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:3
Etam’s sons were Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash. Their sister was Hazzelelponi. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:4
Penuel was Gedor’s father. Ezer was Hushah’s father. These were Hur’s descendants. Hur was Ephrathah’s firstborn and Bethlehem’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:5
Ashhur, Tekoa’s father, had two wives: Helah and Naarah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:6
Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were Naarah’s descendants. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:7
Helah’s sons included Zereth, Zohar, and Ethnan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:8
They also included Koz. He was Anub and Zobebah’s father, and the father of the clans of Aharhel, Harum’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:9
Jabez was more honorable than his brothers. His mother named him Jabez because she bore him in pain. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:10
Jabez prayed to Israel’s God, “Please bless me and enlarge my territory! May Your hand be with me. Keep me from harm so I am free from pain.” God granted his request. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 4:11
Chelub was Shuhah’s brother and Mehir’s father. Mehir was Eshton’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:12
Eshton was the father of Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah. Tehinnah was Ir-nahash’s father. These were Recah’s men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:13
Kenaz’s sons were Othniel and Seraiah. Othniel’s sons were Hathath and Meonothai. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:14
Meonothai was Ophrah’s father. Seraiah was Joab’s father. Joab was the father of those living in Ge-harashim. That place received its name because its people were craftsmen. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:15
Caleb was Jephunneh’s son. Caleb’s sons were Iru, Elah, and Naam. Elah’s son was Kenaz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:16
Jehallelel’s sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:17
Ezrah’s sons were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. Mered’s wife Bithiah bore Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah. Ishbah was Eshtemoa’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:18
These were the children of Bithiah, Pharaoh’s daughter. Mered also married a Judean woman. She bore Jered, Gedor’s father, Heber, Soco’s father, and Jekuthiel, Zanoah’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:19
Hodiah’s wife was Naham’s sister. Her sons were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:20
Shimon’s sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. Ishi’s descendants were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:21
Shelah was Judah’s son. Shelah’s sons included Er, Lecah’s father, Laadah, Mareshah’s father, and the linen workers’ clans at Beth-ashbea. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:22
They also included Jokim, Cozeba’s men, and Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab and Jashubi-lehem. These names come from ancient records. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:23
These were the potters living at Netaim and Gederah. They lived there serving the king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:24
Simeon’s descendants were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:25
Shaul’s sons were Shallum, Mibsam, and Mishma. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:26
Mishma’s sons were Hammuel, Zaccur, and Shimei. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:27
Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters. His brothers had few children. So their whole clan remained smaller than Judah’s descendants. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:28
They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, and Hazar-shual. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:29
They also lived in Bilhah, Ezem, and Tolad. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:30
They also lived in Bethuel, Hormah, and Ziklag. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:31
They also lived in Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim. These were their cities until David’s reign. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:32
Their villages included five towns: Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:33
They also had all the surrounding villages as far as Baal. These were their settlements. They kept a genealogical record. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:34
It lists Meshobab, Jamlech, and Joshah, Amaziah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:35
It also lists Joel and Jehu. Jehu was Joshibiah’s son, Seraiah’s grandson, and Asiel’s great-grandson. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:36
It also lists Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, and Benaiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:37
It also lists Ziza, Shiphi’s son. Shiphi was Allon’s son, Allon was Jedaiah’s son, Jedaiah was Shimri’s son, and Shimri was Shemaiah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:38
These named men led their clans. Their families increased greatly. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:39
They traveled to Gedor’s entrance, east of the valley. They sought pasture for their flocks. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:40
They found rich, good pasture there. The land was spacious, peaceful, and quiet. Some Hamites had lived there previously. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:41
The named men came during Hezekiah’s reign over Judah. They attacked the Hamites in their homes and the Meunites there, devoting them to destruction that remains to this day. They settled in their place because it had pasture for their flocks. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:42
Five hundred of these Simeonites went to Mount Seir. Ishi’s sons Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel led them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 4:43
They killed the remaining Amalekites who had escaped. They have lived there to this day. 📖
