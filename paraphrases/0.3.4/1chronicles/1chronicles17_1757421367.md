## 1 Chronicles 17:1
David settled in his palace. He told Nathan the prophet, “I live in a cedar house. But the ark of the LORD’s covenant is under a tent.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:2
Nathan answered, “Do everything in your heart. God is with you.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:3
That night, God’s word came to Nathan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:4
“Tell My servant David, ‘The LORD says: You will not build a house for Me to live in. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:5
I have not lived in a house since I brought Israel from Egypt. I have moved from tent to tent and dwelling to dwelling. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:6
I traveled with all Israel and appointed leaders to shepherd My people. Did I ever ask any leader, “Why have you not built Me a cedar house?”’ 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:7
Tell My servant David, ‘The LORD of Hosts says: I took you from the pasture, from following sheep, to rule My people Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:8
I have been with you wherever you went and cut off all your enemies before you. I will make your name like the greatest in the land. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:9
I will provide a place for My people Israel and plant them there. They may live in their own place without further disturbance. Wicked people will no longer oppress them as at the beginning. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:10
That oppression continued since I appointed judges over My people Israel. I will subdue all your enemies. I declare that the LORD will build a house for you. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:11
When your days end and you join your fathers, I will raise up your descendant, one of your own sons. I will establish his kingdom. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:12
He will build a house for Me. I will establish his throne forever. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:13
I will be his Father, and he will be My son. I will never withdraw My loving devotion from him as I withdrew it from your predecessor. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:14
I will set him over My house and kingdom forever. His throne will be established forever.’” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:15
Nathan told David every word of this entire revelation. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 17:16
King David went in and sat before the LORD. He said, “LORD God, who am I, and what is my house, that You brought me this far? 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:17
God, this seemed small to You. You also spoke about Your servant’s household in the future. LORD God, You regarded me as a man of great distinction. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:18
What more can David say for honoring Your servant? You know Your servant. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:19
LORD, You did this great thing for Your servant’s sake and according to Your heart. You made all these great promises known. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:20
LORD, no one is like You. There is no God except You, according to everything we have heard ourselves. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:21
Who is like Israel, the one nation God went to redeem for Himself? You made Your name great through awesome wonders, driving out nations before Your people. You redeemed them from Egypt. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:22
You made Israel Your own people forever. You, LORD, became their God. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:23
LORD, establish forever Your word about Your servant and his house. Do what You promised. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:24
Then Your name will be established and exalted forever. People will say, ‘The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, is God over Israel.’ May Your servant David’s house be established before You. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:25
My God, You revealed that You will build a house for Your servant. Therefore, Your servant found courage to pray before You. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:26
LORD, You are God! You promised this goodness to Your servant. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 17:27
You were pleased to bless Your servant’s house so it continues before You forever. LORD, You blessed it. It will be blessed forever.” 🙏
