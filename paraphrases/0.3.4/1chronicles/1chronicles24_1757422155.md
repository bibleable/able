## 1 Chronicles 24:1
These were the divisions of Aaron’s descendants. Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:2
Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:3
David divided them by their service offices. Zadok, Eleazar’s descendant, and Ahimelech, Ithamar’s descendant, helped him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:4
Eleazar’s descendants had more leaders than Ithamar’s. The divisions reflected this. Eleazar’s descendants had sixteen family heads, and Ithamar’s had eight. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:5
They divided them by lot. Both Eleazar’s and Ithamar’s descendants had officers of the sanctuary and officers of God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:6
Shemaiah, Nethanel’s son, a Levite scribe, recorded their names. The king, officers, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech, Abiathar’s son, and priestly and Levite family heads were present. They took one family from Eleazar, then one from Ithamar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:7
Jehoiarib received the first lot. Jedaiah received the second. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:8
Harim received the third lot. Seorim received the fourth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:9
Malchijah received the fifth lot. Mijamin received the sixth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:10
Hakkoz received the seventh lot. Abijah received the eighth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:11
Jeshua received the ninth lot. Shecaniah received the tenth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:12
Eliashib received the eleventh lot. Jakim received the twelfth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:13
Huppah received the thirteenth lot. Jeshebeab received the fourteenth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:14
Bilgah received the fifteenth lot. Immer received the sixteenth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:15
Hezir received the seventeenth lot. Happizzez received the eighteenth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:16
Pethahiah received the nineteenth lot. Jehezkel received the twentieth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:17
Jachin received the twenty-first lot. Gamul received the twenty-second. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:18
Delaiah received the twenty-third lot. Maaziah received the twenty-fourth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:19
This was their appointed service order when entering the LORD’s house. They followed their forefather Aaron’s regulations, as the LORD, Israel’s God, commanded him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:20
The remaining descendants of Levi follow. Amram’s sons included Shubael. Shubael’s sons included Jehdeiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:21
Rehabiah’s first son was Isshiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:22
The Izharites included Shelomoth. Shelomoth’s sons included Jahath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:23
Hebron’s first son was Jeriah. Amariah was second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:24
Uzziel’s sons included Micah. Micah’s sons included Shamir. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:25
Micah’s brother was Isshiah. Isshiah’s sons included Zechariah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:26
Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. Jaaziah’s son was Beno. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:27
Merari’s descendants through Jaaziah were Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:28
Mahli’s descendants included Eleazar, who had no sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:29
Kish’s descendants included his son Jerahmeel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:30
Mushi’s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the Levites’ sons by family. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 24:31
They also cast lots, as their relatives, Aaron’s descendants, did. King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and priestly and Levite family heads were present. Family heads and their younger brothers participated alike. 📖
