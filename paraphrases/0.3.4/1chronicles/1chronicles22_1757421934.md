## 1 Chronicles 22:1
David said, “The LORD God’s house will be here. Israel’s burnt offering altar will also be here.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:2
David ordered Israel’s resident foreigners gathered. He appointed stonecutters from them to prepare finished stones for building God’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:3
David supplied much iron for gateway door nails and fittings. He also supplied more bronze than could be weighed. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:4
He supplied more cedar logs than could be counted. The Sidonians and Tyrians had brought him many cedar logs. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:5
David said, “My son Solomon is young and inexperienced. The LORD’s house must be exceedingly magnificent, famous and glorious throughout all lands. I must prepare for it.” David made lavish preparations before dying. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:6
David called Solomon and instructed him to build a house for the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:7
He said, “My son, I wanted to build a house for the name of the LORD my God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:8
But the LORD told me, ‘You shed much blood and fought great wars. You must not build a house for My name. You shed much blood on the ground before Me. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:9
A son will be born to you, a man of rest. I will give him rest from enemies on every side. His name will be Solomon. I will give Israel peace and quiet during his reign. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:10
He will build a house for My name. He will be My son, and I will be his Father. I will establish his kingdom’s throne over Israel forever.’ 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:11
My son, may the LORD be with you. May you succeed in building His house, as the LORD your God said you would. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:12
Above all, may the LORD give you insight and understanding when He puts you over Israel. Then you may obey the LORD your God’s Law. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:13
You will succeed if you carefully obey the statutes and ordinances the LORD gave Moses for Israel. Be strong and courageous. Do not fear or be discouraged. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:14
I took great pains to prepare for the LORD’s house. I supplied 100,000 talents of gold, 1,000,000 talents of silver, and bronze and iron beyond weighing. I also supplied timber and stone. You may add to them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:15
You have many workers: stonecutters, masons, carpenters, and men skilled in every kind of work. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:16
You have countless craftsmen skilled in gold, silver, bronze, and iron. Begin the work. May the LORD be with you.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:17
David ordered all Israel’s leaders to help his son Solomon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:18
“Is the LORD your God not with you? Has He not given you rest everywhere? He delivered the land’s inhabitants into my hand. The land is subdued before the LORD and His people. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 22:19
Set your heart and soul to seek the LORD your God. Begin building His sanctuary. Then bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant and God’s holy articles into the temple built for His name.” 📖
