## 1 Chronicles 21:1
Satan rose against Israel. He prompted David to take a census of Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:2
David told Joab and the troop commanders, “Count the Israelites from Beersheba to Dan. Report to me so I know their number.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:3
Joab answered, “May the LORD increase His troops a hundred times. My lord the king, are they not all your servants? Why do you want this? Why bring guilt on Israel?” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:4
The king overruled Joab. Joab traveled throughout Israel and returned to Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:5
Joab reported the troops’ total to David. All Israel had 1,100,000 men who drew swords, including 470,000 in Judah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:6
Joab excluded Levi and Benjamin from the count. He detested the king’s command. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:7
God also saw the command as evil. So He struck Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:8
David told God, “I sinned greatly by doing this. Please remove Your servant’s sin. I acted very foolishly.” 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 21:9
The LORD instructed Gad, David’s seer. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:10
“Tell David, ‘The LORD says: I offer you three choices. Choose one, and I will carry it out against you.’” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:11
Gad went to David and said, “The LORD says you must choose. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:12
Choose three years of famine, or three months fleeing enemies who overtake you with swords. Or choose three days of the LORD’s sword, a plague throughout the land, with His angel ravaging every part of Israel. Decide what answer I should give Him who sent me.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:13
David told Gad, “I am deeply distressed. Let me fall into the LORD’s hand because His mercies are very great. Do not let me fall into human hands.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:14
The LORD sent a plague on Israel. Seventy thousand Israelite men died. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:15
God sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem. During the destruction, the LORD saw and relented from the disaster. He told the destroying angel, “Enough! Withdraw your hand!” The LORD’s angel stood beside Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor then. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:16
David looked up and saw the LORD’s angel between heaven and earth. His drawn sword stretched over Jerusalem. David and the elders wore sackcloth and fell facedown. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:17
David told God, “I ordered the count. I sinned and acted wickedly. What have these sheep done? LORD my God, let Your hand fall on me and my father’s house. Do not let this plague remain on Your people.” 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 21:18
The LORD’s angel told Gad to instruct David. David must build an altar to the LORD on Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:19
David went up, obeying Gad’s message in the LORD’s name. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:20
Ornan was threshing wheat. He turned and saw the angel. His four sons with him hid. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:21
David came to Ornan. Ornan looked and saw David. He left the threshing floor and bowed facedown before him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:22
David said, “Give me this threshing floor’s site to build an altar to the LORD. Sell it at full price so the people’s plague may stop.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:23
Ornan answered, “Take it! My lord the king may do whatever he thinks good. I give the oxen for burnt offerings, threshing sledges for wood, and wheat for the grain offering. I give everything.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:24
King David answered, “No. I must pay full price. I will not take your property for the LORD or offer burnt offerings that cost me nothing.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:25
David paid Ornan six hundred shekels of gold for the site. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:26
He built an altar to the LORD there and presented burnt offerings and peace offerings. He called on the LORD. The LORD answered with fire from heaven on the burnt offering altar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:27
The LORD spoke to the angel. The angel sheathed his sword. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:28
David saw the LORD had answered him at Ornan the Jebusite’s threshing floor. He offered sacrifices there. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:29
The LORD’s tabernacle, which Moses made in the wilderness, and the burnt offering altar were at Gibeon’s high place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 21:30
David could not go there to inquire of God. He feared the LORD’s angel’s sword. 📖
