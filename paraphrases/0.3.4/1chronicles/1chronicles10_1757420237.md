## 1 Chronicles 10:1
The Philistines fought Israel. Israel’s men fled, and many were killed on Mount Gilboa. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:2
The Philistines closely pursued Saul and his sons. They killed his sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:3
The battle intensified against Saul. Archers overtook him and wounded him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:4
Saul told his armor-bearer, “Draw your sword and kill me with it. Otherwise, these uncircumcised men will come and torture me!” His armor-bearer was terrified and refused. Saul took his own sword and fell on it. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:5
His armor-bearer saw Saul was dead. He also fell on his own sword and died. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:6
Saul, his three sons, and all his household died together. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:7
All the Israelites in the valley saw their army flee and learned Saul and his sons had died. They abandoned their cities and fled. The Philistines occupied those cities. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:8
The next day, the Philistines came to strip the dead. They found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:9
They stripped Saul, cut off his head, and took his armor. They sent messengers throughout Philistine land. The messengers announced the news in their idols’ temple and among their people. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:10
They put Saul’s armor in their gods’ temple. They hung his head in Dagon’s temple. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:11
All Jabesh-gilead’s people heard everything the Philistines did to Saul. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:12
All their brave men retrieved Saul’s and his sons’ bodies and brought them to Jabesh. They buried the bones under the oak there and fasted seven days. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:13
Saul died because he was unfaithful to the LORD. He disobeyed the LORD’s word and even sought guidance from a medium. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 10:14
He failed to inquire of the LORD. So the LORD killed him and gave the kingdom to David, Jesse’s son. 📖
