## 1 Chronicles 25:1
David and the army commanders set apart sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun. They would prophesy with lyres, harps, and cymbals. The men who served are listed next. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:2
Asaph’s sons were Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah. Asaph directed them. He prophesied under the king’s direction. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:3
Jeduthun’s six sons were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Shimei, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah. Their father Jeduthun directed them. He prophesied with the harp, thanking and praising the LORD. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:4
Heman’s sons were Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:5
Heman was the king’s seer. God gave him all these sons through His promises to exalt him. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:6
Their fathers directed all these men in music for service in the LORD’s house, God’s house. They used cymbals, harps, and lyres. Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman served under the king’s direction. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:7
They and their relatives totaled 288. All were trained and skilled in songs to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 25:8
They cast lots for duties. Young and old, teacher and pupil, participated alike. 📖
