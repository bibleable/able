## 1 Chronicles 27:1
The Israelites serving the king included family heads, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and their officers. They handled divisions rotating military duty each month throughout the year. Each division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:2
Jashobeam, Zabdiel’s son, commanded the first division for the first month. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:3
He descended from Perez. He was chief of all army commanders for the first month. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:4
Dodai the Ahohite supervised the second month’s division, with Mikloth as leader. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:5
The third commander, chief for the third month, was Benaiah, the priest Jehoiada’s son. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:6
Benaiah was mighty among the Thirty and led them. His son Ammizabad supervised his division. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:7
The fourth commander, for the fourth month, was Asahel, Joab’s brother. His son Zebadiah commanded after him. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:8
The fifth commander, for the fifth month, was Shamhuth the Izrahite. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:9
The sixth commander, for the sixth month, was Ira, Ikkesh the Tekoite’s son. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:10
The seventh commander, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, an Ephraimite. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:11
The eighth commander, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, a Zerahite. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:12
The ninth commander, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, a Benjamite. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:13
The tenth commander, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, a Zerahite. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:14
The eleventh commander, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, an Ephraimite. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:15
The twelfth commander, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, from Othniel’s family. His division had 24,000 men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:16
These officers supervised Israel’s tribes. Eliezer, Zichri’s son, supervised the Reubenites. Shephatiah, Maacah’s son, supervised the Simeonites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:17
Hashabiah, Kemuel’s son, supervised Levi. Zadok supervised Aaron. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:18
Elihu, David’s brother, supervised Judah. Omri, Michael’s son, supervised Issachar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:19
Ishmaiah, Obadiah’s son, supervised Zebulun. Jerimoth, Azriel’s son, supervised Naphtali. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:20
Hoshea, Azaziah’s son, supervised the Ephraimites. Joel, Pedaiah’s son, supervised one half-tribe of Manasseh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:21
Iddo, Zechariah’s son, supervised Manasseh’s half-tribe in Gilead. Jaasiel, Abner’s son, supervised Benjamin. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:22
Azarel, Jeroham’s son, supervised Dan. These were Israel’s tribal leaders. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:23
David did not count men aged twenty or younger. The LORD had promised to make Israel as numerous as the sky’s stars. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:24
Joab, Zeruiah’s son, started counting but did not finish. This census brought wrath on Israel. Its number was not entered in the Book of the Chronicles of King David. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:25
Azmaveth, Adiel’s son, supervised the royal storehouses. Jonathan, Uzziah’s son, supervised storehouses in the country, cities, villages, and fortresses. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:26
Ezri, Chelub’s son, supervised field workers who tilled the soil. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:27
Shimei the Ramathite supervised vineyards. Zabdi the Shiphmite supervised the vineyard produce for the wine vats. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:28
Baal-hanan the Gederite supervised olive and sycamore trees in the foothills. Joash supervised olive oil stores. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:29
Shitrai the Sharonite supervised the herds grazing in Sharon. Shaphat, Adlai’s son, supervised valley herds. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:30
Obil the Ishmaelite supervised camels. Jehdeiah the Meronothite supervised donkeys. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:31
Jaziz the Hagrite supervised flocks. All these officials supervised King David’s property. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:32
David’s uncle Jonathan was a counselor, a man of insight, and a scribe. Jehiel, Hachmoni’s son, attended to the king’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:33
Ahithophel was the king’s counselor. Hushai the Archite was the king’s friend. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 27:34
Jehoiada, Benaiah’s son, succeeded Ahithophel. Abiathar followed him. Joab commanded the king’s army. 📖
