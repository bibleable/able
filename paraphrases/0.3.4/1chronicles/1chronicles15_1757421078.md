## 1 Chronicles 15:1
David built buildings for himself in the City of David. He prepared a place for God’s ark and pitched a tent for it. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:2
David said, “Only Levites may carry God’s ark. The LORD chose them to carry His ark and serve before Him forever.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:3
David gathered all Israel in Jerusalem. They would bring the LORD’s ark to its prepared place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:4
He gathered Aaron’s descendants and the Levites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:5
From the Kohathites came Uriel the chief and 120 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:6
From the Merarites came Asaiah the chief and 220 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:7
From the Gershomites came Joel the chief and 130 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:8
From the Elizaphanites came Shemaiah the chief and 200 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:9
From the Hebronites came Eliel the chief and 80 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:10
From the Uzzielites came Amminadab the chief and 112 relatives. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:11
David called the priests Zadok and Abiathar. He also called the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:12
He said, “You lead the Levite families. You and your relatives must consecrate yourselves. Then bring the ark of the LORD, Israel’s God, to its prepared place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:13
You Levites were absent the first time. Because of this, the LORD our God burst out against us in anger. We did not consult Him about the proper order.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:14
The priests and Levites consecrated themselves to bring the ark of the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:15
The Levites carried God’s ark on their shoulders with poles. They obeyed Moses’ command, which followed the LORD’s word. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:16
David told the Levite leaders to appoint their relatives as singers. They would sing joyfully, accompanied by harps, lyres, and cymbals. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:17
The Levites appointed Heman, Joel’s son, and his relative Asaph, Berechiah’s son. From their Merarite relatives, they appointed Ethan, Kushaiah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:18
Their relatives next in rank served with them. They were Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, and gatekeepers Obed-edom and Jeiel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:19
The musicians Heman, Asaph, and Ethan would play bronze cymbals. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:20
Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah would play harps. Musical direction: Alamoth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:21
Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah would lead the music with lyres. Musical direction: Sheminith. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:22
Chenaniah, the head Levite, directed the music because he was highly skilled. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:23
Berechiah and Elkanah would guard the ark. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:24
The priests Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer would blow trumpets before God’s ark. Obed-edom and Jehiah would also guard the ark. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:25
David, Israel’s elders, and commanders of thousands went joyfully to bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant from Obed-edom’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:26
God helped the Levites carrying the ark of the LORD’s covenant. So they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:27
David wore a fine linen robe. So did the Levites carrying the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah, who directed the singers’ music. David also wore a linen ephod. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:28
All Israel brought up the ark of the LORD’s covenant with shouting. They sounded rams’ horns, trumpets, and cymbals, and played harps and lyres. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 15:29
The ark of the LORD’s covenant entered the City of David. Saul’s daughter Michal watched from a window. She saw King David dancing and celebrating, and despised him in her heart. 📖
