## 1 Chronicles 28:1
David summoned all Israel’s leaders to Jerusalem. They included tribal and service division leaders, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and officials overseeing royal and princely property and cattle. Court officials and all mighty warriors also came. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:2
King David stood and spoke. “My brothers and people, listen. I wanted to build a resting place for the ark of the LORD’s covenant, a footstool for our God. I prepared to build it. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:3
But God told me, ‘You must not build a house for My name. You are a man of war who shed blood.’ 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:4
The LORD, Israel’s God, chose me from my father’s house to be Israel’s king forever. He chose Judah to lead, and my father’s household from Judah. From my father’s sons, He was pleased to make me king over all Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:5
The LORD gave me many sons. From them, He chose Solomon to sit on the throne of the LORD’s kingdom over Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:6
He told me, ‘Your son Solomon will build My house and courts. I chose him as My son, and I will be his Father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:7
I will establish his kingdom forever if he firmly obeys My commands and ordinances, as he does today.’ 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:8
Before all Israel, the LORD’s assembly, and in our God’s hearing, seek and obey all the LORD your God’s commands. Then you may possess this good land and leave it to your descendants as an inheritance forever. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:9
Solomon, my son, know your father’s God. Serve Him wholeheartedly and willingly. The LORD searches every heart and understands every thought’s intent. Seek Him, and you will find Him. Abandon Him, and He will reject you forever. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:10
Remember, the LORD chose you to build a house for the sanctuary. Be strong and do it.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:11
David gave Solomon plans for the temple’s portico, buildings, storehouses, upper rooms, inner rooms, and mercy seat room. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:12
The plans contained everything David had in mind for the LORD’s house’s courts and surrounding rooms. They covered the treasuries of God’s house and dedicated things. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:13
They covered priestly and Levite divisions, every service task in the LORD’s house, and all its service articles. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:14
They specified the weight of gold articles for every kind of service. They also specified the weight of silver articles for every kind of service. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:15
They specified each gold lampstand’s weight and its lamps’ weight. They also specified each silver lampstand’s and its lamps’ weight, according to its use. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:16
They specified the gold weight for each showbread table and the silver weight for silver tables. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:17
They specified pure gold weights for forks, sprinkling bowls, and pitchers. They also specified each gold dish’s weight and each silver bowl’s weight. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:18
They specified refined gold weight for the incense altar. They included plans for the chariot of gold cherubim. Their outspread wings overshadowed the ark of the LORD’s covenant. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:19
David said, “The LORD made all these plan details clear to me in writing, by His hand upon me.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:20
David told Solomon, “Be strong and courageous, and do it. Do not fear or be discouraged. The LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not fail or abandon you before all the work for service in His house is finished. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 28:21
The priestly and Levite divisions are ready for every service in God’s house. Every willing person with any skill is available for the work. The officials and all the people are fully at your command.” 📖
