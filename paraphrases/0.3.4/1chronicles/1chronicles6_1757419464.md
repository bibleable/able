## 1 Chronicles 6:1
Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:2
Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:3
Amram’s children were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:4
Eleazar was Phinehas’ father. Phinehas was Abishua’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:5
Abishua was Bukki’s father. Bukki was Uzzi’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:6
Uzzi was Zerahiah’s father. Zerahiah was Meraioth’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:7
Meraioth was Amariah’s father. Amariah was Ahitub’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:8
Ahitub was Zadok’s father. Zadok was Ahimaaz’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:9
Ahimaaz was Azariah’s father. Azariah was Johanan’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:10
Johanan was Azariah’s father. This Azariah served as priest in the temple Solomon built in Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:11
Azariah was Amariah’s father. Amariah was Ahitub’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:12
Ahitub was Zadok’s father. Zadok was Shallum’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:13
Shallum was Hilkiah’s father. Hilkiah was Azariah’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:14
Azariah was Seraiah’s father. Seraiah was Jehozadak’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:15
Jehozadak went into captivity when the LORD exiled Judah and Jerusalem through Nebuchadnezzar. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:16
Levi’s sons were Gershom, Kohath, and Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:17
Gershom’s sons were Libni and Shimei. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:18
Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:19
Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. The Levite clans are listed next according to their fathers. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:20
Gershom’s son was Libni. Libni’s son was Jahath. Jahath’s son was Zimmah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:21
Zimmah’s son was Joah. Joah’s son was Iddo. Iddo’s son was Zerah. Zerah’s son was Jeatherai. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:22
Kohath’s son was Amminadab. Amminadab’s son was Korah. Korah’s son was Assir. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:23
Assir’s son was Elkanah. Elkanah’s son was Ebiasaph. Ebiasaph’s son was Assir. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:24
Assir’s son was Tahath. Tahath’s son was Uriel. Uriel’s son was Uzziah. Uzziah’s son was Shaul. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:25
Elkanah’s descendants included Amasai and Ahimoth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:26
Ahimoth’s son was Elkanah. Elkanah’s son was Zophai. Zophai’s son was Nahath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:27
Nahath’s son was Eliab. Eliab’s son was Jeroham. Jeroham’s son was Elkanah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:28
Samuel’s firstborn was Joel. His second son was Abijah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:29
Merari’s descendants included Mahli. Mahli’s son was Libni. Libni’s son was Shimei. Shimei’s son was Uzzah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:30
Uzzah’s son was Shimea. Shimea’s son was Haggiah. Haggiah’s son was Asaiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:31
The following men supervised music in the LORD’s house by David’s appointment, after the ark rested there. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:32
They served with songs before the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. They continued until Solomon built the LORD’s house in Jerusalem. They performed their duties according to their regulations. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:33
These men served with their sons. From the Kohathites came Heman the singer. Heman was Joel’s son, and Joel was Samuel’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:34
Samuel was Elkanah’s son. Elkanah was Jeroham’s son. Jeroham was Eliel’s son. Eliel was Toah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:35
Toah was Zuph’s son. Zuph was Elkanah’s son. Elkanah was Mahath’s son. Mahath was Amasai’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:36
Amasai was Elkanah’s son. Elkanah was Joel’s son. Joel was Azariah’s son. Azariah was Zephaniah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:37
Zephaniah was Tahath’s son. Tahath was Assir’s son. Assir was Ebiasaph’s son. Ebiasaph was Korah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:38
Korah was Izhar’s son. Izhar was Kohath’s son. Kohath was Levi’s son. Levi was Israel’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:39
Heman’s relative Asaph served at his right hand. Asaph was Berechiah’s son. Berechiah was Shimea’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:40
Shimea was Michael’s son. Michael was Baaseiah’s son. Baaseiah was Malchijah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:41
Malchijah was Ethni’s son. Ethni was Zerah’s son. Zerah was Adaiah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:42
Adaiah was Ethan’s son. Ethan was Zimmah’s son. Zimmah was Shimei’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:43
Shimei was Jahath’s son. Jahath was Gershom’s son. Gershom was Levi’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:44
Their relatives, Merari’s sons, served on the left. Ethan was Kishi’s son. Kishi was Abdi’s son. Abdi was Malluch’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:45
Malluch was Hashabiah’s son. Hashabiah was Amaziah’s son. Amaziah was Hilkiah’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:46
Hilkiah was Amzi’s son. Amzi was Bani’s son. Bani was Shemer’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:47
Shemer was Mahli’s son. Mahli was Mushi’s son. Mushi was Merari’s son. Merari was Levi’s son. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:48
Their fellow Levites received every kind of service assignment for the tabernacle, God’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:49
Aaron and his sons did all the work of the Most Holy Place. They presented offerings on the burnt offering altar and incense altar to make atonement for Israel. They obeyed everything Moses, God’s servant, commanded. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:50
Aaron’s son was Eleazar. Eleazar’s son was Phinehas. Phinehas’ son was Abishua. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:51
Abishua’s son was Bukki. Bukki’s son was Uzzi. Uzzi’s son was Zerahiah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:52
Zerahiah’s son was Meraioth. Meraioth’s son was Amariah. Amariah’s son was Ahitub. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:53
Ahitub’s son was Zadok. Zadok’s son was Ahimaaz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:54
The following territories were assigned for Aaron’s descendants from the Kohathite clan to settle. The first lot fell to them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:55
They received Hebron in Judah’s land and its surrounding pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:56
But Caleb, Jephunneh’s son, received the fields and villages around the city. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:57
Aaron’s descendants received Hebron, a refuge city, and Libnah, Jattir, and Eshtemoa. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:58
They also received Hilen and Debir. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:59
They also received Ashan, Juttah, and Beth-shemesh, together with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:60
From Benjamin’s tribe, they received Gibeon, Geba, Alemeth, and Anathoth, with their pasturelands. Their families had thirteen cities in all. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:61
The remaining Kohathites received ten cities by lot from Manasseh’s half-tribe. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:62
The Gershomite clans received thirteen cities by lot from the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Manasseh in Bashan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:63
The Merarite clans received twelve cities by lot from Reuben’s, Gad’s, and Zebulun’s tribes. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:64
The Israelites gave these cities and their pasturelands to the Levites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:65
They assigned the named cities by lot from Judah’s, Simeon’s, and Benjamin’s tribes. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:66
Some Kohathite clans received cities from Ephraim’s tribe as their territory. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:67
They received Shechem, a refuge city, and its pasturelands in Ephraim’s hill country. They also received Gezer. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:68
They also received Jokmeam and Beth-horon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:69
They also received Aijalon and Gath-rimmon, together with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:70
From Manasseh’s half-tribe, the remaining Kohathite clans received Aner and Bileam with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:71
The Gershomites received towns from Manasseh’s half-tribe clan. These were Golan in Bashan and Ashtaroth, with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:72
From Issachar’s tribe, they received Kedesh and Daberath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:73
They also received Ramoth and Anem, together with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:74
From Asher’s tribe, they received Mashal and Abdon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:75
They also received Hukok and Rehob, together with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:76
From Naphtali’s tribe, they received Kedesh in Galilee, Hammon, and Kiriathaim, with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:77
The Merarites were the remaining Levites. From Zebulun’s tribe, they received Rimmono and Tabor with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:78
From Reuben’s tribe east of the Jordan, opposite Jericho, they received Bezer in the wilderness and Jahzah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:79
They also received Kedemoth and Mephaath, together with their pasturelands. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:80
From Gad’s tribe, they received Ramoth in Gilead and Mahanaim. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 6:81
They also received Heshbon and Jazer, together with their pasturelands. 📖
