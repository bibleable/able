## 1 Chronicles 16:1
They brought God’s ark into David’s tent and set it there. They presented burnt offerings and peace offerings before God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:2
David finished sacrificing the burnt offerings and peace offerings. Then he blessed the people in the LORD’s name. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:3
He gave every Israelite man and woman a loaf of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:4
David appointed Levites to serve before the LORD’s ark. They would celebrate, give thanks, and praise the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:5
Asaph was chief, and Zechariah was second. Next were Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. They would play harps and lyres. Asaph would play cymbals. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:6
The priests Benaiah and Jahaziel regularly blew trumpets before the ark of God’s covenant. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:7
That day, David first entrusted this thanksgiving song to Asaph and his associates. It was a song to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:8
“Thank the LORD and call on His name. Tell the nations His deeds. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:9
Sing to Him and praise Him in song. Tell of all His wonders. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:10
Glory in His holy name. Let those seeking the LORD rejoice in their hearts. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:11
Seek the LORD and His strength. Always seek His face. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:12
Remember His wonders, marvelous deeds, and the judgments He pronounced. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:13
Remember these, descendants of His servant Israel, sons of Jacob, His chosen ones. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:14
He is the LORD our God. His judgments reach throughout the earth. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:15
Remember His covenant forever, the word He commanded for a thousand generations. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:16
This was His covenant with Abraham and His oath to Isaac. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:17
He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree and to Israel as an everlasting covenant. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:18
He said, ‘I will give you Canaan’s land as your inheritance.’ 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:19
They were very few in number and foreigners in the land. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:20
They wandered from nation to nation and kingdom to kingdom. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:21
He let no one oppress them. He rebuked kings for their sake. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:22
He said, ‘Do not touch My anointed ones! Do not harm My prophets!’ 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:23
All the earth, sing to the LORD! Proclaim His salvation daily. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:24
Declare His glory among the nations and His wonders among all peoples. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:25
The LORD is great and deserves great praise. He is to be feared above all gods. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:26
All the nations’ gods are idols. But the LORD made the heavens. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:27
Splendor and majesty are before Him. Strength and joy fill His dwelling. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:28
Families of the nations, attribute glory and strength to the LORD. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:29
Give the LORD the glory His name deserves. Bring an offering and come before Him. Worship Him in the splendor of His holiness. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:30
All the earth, tremble before Him. The world is firmly established and cannot be moved. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:31
Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice. Let them say among the nations, ‘The LORD reigns!’ 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:32
Let the sea and everything in it resound. Let the fields and everything in them rejoice. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:33
The forest’s trees will sing joyfully before the LORD. He is coming to judge the earth. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:34
Thank the LORD because He is good. His loving devotion lasts forever. 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:35
Cry out, ‘Save us, God of our salvation. Gather and rescue us from the nations. Then we may thank Your holy name and glory in praising You.’ 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:36
Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God, from everlasting to everlasting.” All the people said, “Amen!” and “Praise the LORD!” 🙏

## 1 Chronicles 16:37
David left Asaph and his associates before the ark of the LORD’s covenant. They would serve regularly, according to each day’s requirements. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:38
Obed-edom and his sixty-eight relatives served with them. Obed-edom, Jeduthun’s son, and Hosah were gatekeepers. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:39
David left Zadok the priest and his fellow priests before the LORD’s tabernacle at Gibeon’s high place. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:40
They would present burnt offerings to the LORD on the burnt offering altar every morning and evening. They followed everything written in the LORD’s Law, which He commanded Israel to obey. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:41
Heman, Jeduthun, and the others chosen and designated by name were with them. They thanked the LORD because “His loving devotion lasts forever.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:42
Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments for music and songs to God. Jeduthun’s sons served at the gate. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 16:43
Everyone went home. David returned home to bless his household. 📖
