## 1 Chronicles 26:1
The gatekeepers’ divisions follow. From the Korahites came Meshelemiah, Kore’s son, one of Asaph’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:2
Meshelemiah’s firstborn was Zechariah. Jediael was second, Zebadiah third, and Jathniel fourth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:3
Elam was fifth, Jehohanan sixth, and Eliehoenai seventh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:4
Obed-edom’s firstborn was Shemaiah. Jehozabad was second, Joah third, Sachar fourth, and Nethanel fifth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:5
Ammiel was sixth, Issachar seventh, and Peullethai eighth. God had blessed Obed-edom. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:6
Obed-edom’s son Shemaiah had sons who ruled their families. They were strong, capable men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:7
Shemaiah’s sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. His brothers, Elihu and Semachiah, were also capable men. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:8
All these were Obed-edom’s descendants. They, their sons, and their brothers were capable and strong enough for the work. There were 62 from Obed-edom in all. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:9
Meshelemiah had sons and brothers who were capable men, 18 in all. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:10
Hosah the Merarite’s first son was Shimri. Shimri was not firstborn, but his father appointed him first. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:11
Hilkiah was second, Tebaliah third, and Zechariah fourth. Hosah’s sons and brothers totaled 13. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:12
These gatekeeper divisions, through their chiefs, had duties in the LORD’s house, as their relatives did. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:13
They cast lots for each gate by family. Young and old participated alike. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:14
Shelemiah received the East Gate’s lot. Lots were cast for his son Zechariah, a wise counselor. Zechariah received the North Gate’s lot. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:15
Obed-edom received the South Gate’s lot. His sons received the storehouses’ lot. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:16
Shuppim and Hosah received lots for the West Gate and Shallecheth Gate on the ascending highway. Guards served at every watch. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:17
Each day, six Levites served eastward, four northward, four southward, and two pairs at the storehouse. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:18
For the western court, four served at the highway and two at the court. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:19
These gatekeeper divisions descended from Korah and Merari. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:20
Their fellow Levites supervised the treasuries of God’s house and the treasuries of dedicated things. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:21
Ladan’s Gershonite descendants included Jehieli, a head of Ladan the Gershonite’s families. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:22
Jehieli’s sons, Zetham and his brother Joel, supervised the treasuries of the LORD’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:23
The following came from the Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:24
Shebuel, a descendant of Gershom, Moses’ son, was the officer supervising the treasuries. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:25
His relatives through Eliezer included Eliezer’s son Rehabiah. Rehabiah’s son was Jeshaiah, whose son was Joram. Joram’s son was Zichri, whose son was Shelomith. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:26
Shelomith and his brothers supervised all treasuries of dedicated things. King David, family heads commanding thousands and hundreds, and army commanders had dedicated these things. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:27
They dedicated some battle plunder to repair the LORD’s house. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:28
Samuel the seer, Saul, Kish’s son, Abner, Ner’s son, and Joab, Zeruiah’s son, had dedicated things. Shelomith and his brothers cared for these and everything else dedicated. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:29
From the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons handled outside duties as officers and judges over Israel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:30
Hashabiah and his Hebronite relatives numbered 1,700 capable men. They supervised Israel’s affairs west of the Jordan for all the LORD’s work and the king’s service. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:31
Jerijah led the Hebronites according to his ancestors’ genealogies. During David’s fortieth year as king, the records were searched. Strong, capable Hebronites were found at Jazer in Gilead. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 26:32
Jerijah’s relatives included 2,700 capable family heads. David appointed them over the Reubenites, Gadites, and Manasseh’s half-tribe. They handled every matter concerning God and the king. 📖
