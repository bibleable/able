## 1 Chronicles 18:1
Later, David defeated and subdued the Philistines. He took Gath and its villages from them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:2
David defeated the Moabites. They became his subjects and paid tribute. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:3
David defeated Hadadezer, Zobah’s king, as far as Hamath. Hadadezer had marched out to establish his rule along the Euphrates River. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:4
David captured a thousand chariots, seven thousand charioteers, and twenty thousand foot soldiers. He hamstrung all the horses except a hundred kept for chariots. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:5
Damascus’ Arameans came to help Hadadezer, Zobah’s king. David killed twenty-two thousand of them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:6
David placed garrisons in Aram of Damascus. The Arameans became his subjects and paid tribute. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:7
David took the gold shields of Hadadezer’s officers. He brought them to Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:8
David took much bronze from Hadadezer’s cities, Tibhath and Cun. Solomon used it to make the bronze Sea, pillars, and various bronze articles. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:9
Tou, Hamath’s king, heard David defeated the entire army of Hadadezer, Zobah’s king. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:10
Tou sent his son Hadoram to greet and bless King David for defeating Hadadezer. Hadadezer had fought Tou. Hadoram brought all kinds of gold, silver, and bronze articles. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:11
David dedicated these to the LORD. He also dedicated silver and gold taken from Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, Philistines, and Amalekites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:12
Abishai, Zeruiah’s son, killed eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:13
He placed garrisons in Edom. All the Edomites became David’s subjects. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:14
David ruled all Israel. He administered justice and righteousness for all his people. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:15
Joab, Zeruiah’s son, commanded the army. Jehoshaphat, Ahilud’s son, was recorder. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:16
Zadok, Ahitub’s son, and Ahimelech, Abiathar’s son, were priests. Shavsha was scribe. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 18:17
Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son, commanded the Cherethites and Pelethites. David’s sons were chief officials at the king’s side. 📖
