## 1 Chronicles 8:1
Benjamin’s firstborn was Bela. Ashbel was second, and Aharah was third. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:2
Nohah was fourth, and Rapha was fifth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:3
Bela’s sons included Addar, Gera, and Abihud. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:4
They also included Abishua, Naaman, and Ahoah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:5
They also included Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:6
The following descendants of Ehud led families living in Geba. They were exiled to Manahath. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:7
They were Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera. Gera carried them into exile. He was Uzza and Ahihud’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:8
Shaharaim divorced his wives Hushim and Baara. Afterward, he had sons in Moab’s country. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:9
His sons by his wife Hodesh included Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, and Malcam. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:10
They also included Jeuz, Sachia, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of families. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:11
His sons by Hushim were Abitub and Elpaal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:12
Elpaal’s sons included Eber, Misham, and Shemed. Shemed built Ono and Lod with its villages. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:13
They also included Beriah and Shema. These men led families in Aijalon and drove out Gath’s inhabitants. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:14
Beriah’s sons included Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:15
They also included Zebadiah, Arad, and Eder. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:16
They also included Michael, Ishpah, and Joha. These were Beriah’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:17
Elpaal’s sons included Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, and Heber. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:18
They also included Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab. These were Elpaal’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:19
Shimei’s sons included Jakim, Zichri, and Zabdi. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:20
They also included Elienai, Zillethai, and Eliel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:21
They also included Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath. These were Shimei’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:22
Shashak’s sons included Ishpan, Eber, and Eliel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:23
They also included Abdon, Zichri, and Hanan. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:24
They also included Hananiah, Elam, and Anthothijah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:25
They also included Iphdeiah and Penuel. These were Shashak’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:26
Jeroham’s sons included Shamsherai, Shehariah, and Athaliah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:27
They also included Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri. These were Jeroham’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:28
All these were family heads, chiefs according to their genealogies. They lived in Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:29
Jeiel, Gibeon’s father, lived in Gibeon. His wife was Maacah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:30
His firstborn was Abdon. Then came Zur, Kish, Baal, and Nadab. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:31
His sons also included Gedor, Ahio, and Zecher. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:32
They also included Mikloth, Shimeah’s father. They too lived alongside their relatives in Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:33
Ner was Kish’s father. Kish was Saul’s father. Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:34
Jonathan’s son was Merib-baal. Merib-baal was Micah’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:35
Micah’s sons were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:36
Ahaz was Jehoaddah’s father. Jehoaddah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri was Moza’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:37
Moza was Binea’s father. Binea’s son was Raphah. Raphah’s son was Eleasah. Eleasah’s son was Azel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:38
Azel’s six sons were Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were Azel’s sons. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:39
His brother Eshek’s firstborn was Ulam. Jeush was second, and Eliphelet was third. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 8:40
Ulam’s sons were mighty warriors and archers. They had many sons and grandsons, 150 in all. All these were Benjamin’s descendants. 📖
