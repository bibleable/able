## 1 Chronicles 2:1
Israel’s sons included Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:2
They also included Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:3
Judah’s sons were Er, Onan, and Shelah. Bath-shua the Canaanite bore these three to him. Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked in the LORD’s sight. So the LORD killed him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:4
Tamar, Judah’s daughter-in-law, bore him Perez and Zerah. Judah had five sons in all. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:5
Perez’s sons were Hezron and Hamul. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:6
Zerah’s five sons were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:7
Carmi’s son was Achar. He brought trouble on Israel by violating the ban on devoted things. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:8
Ethan’s son was Azariah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:9
Hezron’s sons were Jerahmeel, Ram, and Caleb. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:10
Ram was Amminadab’s father. Amminadab was Nahshon’s father. Nahshon led descendants of Judah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:11
Nahshon was Salmon’s father. Salmon was Boaz’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:12
Boaz was Obed’s father. Obed was Jesse’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:13
Jesse’s firstborn was Eliab. Abinadab was second, and Shimea was third. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:14
Nethanel was fourth, and Raddai was fifth. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:15
Ozem was sixth, and David was seventh. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:16
Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah’s three sons were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:17
Abigail was Amasa’s mother. His father was Jether the Ishmaelite. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:18
Caleb, Hezron’s son, had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth. Azubah’s sons were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:19
Azubah died. Caleb married Ephrath, who bore him Hur. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:20
Hur was Uri’s father. Uri was Bezalel’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:21
Later, Hezron slept with Machir’s daughter. Machir was Gilead’s father. Hezron married her at sixty years old. She bore him Segub. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:22
Segub was Jair’s father. Jair had twenty-three cities in Gilead’s land. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:23
Geshur and Aram captured Havvoth-jair, Kenath, and Kenath’s sixty surrounding villages. All these were descendants of Machir, Gilead’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:24
Hezron died in Caleb-ephrathah. Afterward, his wife Abijah bore him Ashhur, Tekoa’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:25
Jerahmeel was Hezron’s firstborn. Jerahmeel’s sons were Ram, his firstborn, and Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:26
Jerahmeel had another wife, Atarah. She was Onam’s mother. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:27
Ram was Jerahmeel’s firstborn. Ram’s sons were Maaz, Jamin, and Eker. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:28
Onam’s sons were Shammai and Jada. Shammai’s sons were Nadab and Abishur. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:29
Abishur’s wife was Abihail. She bore him Ahban and Molid. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:30
Nadab’s sons were Seled and Appaim. Seled died without children. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:31
Appaim’s son was Ishi. Ishi’s son was Sheshan. Sheshan’s son was Ahlai. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:32
Jada was Shammai’s brother. Jada’s sons were Jether and Jonathan. Jether died without children. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:33
Jonathan’s sons were Peleth and Zaza. These were Jerahmeel’s descendants. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:34
Sheshan had daughters but no sons. He had an Egyptian servant named Jarha. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:35
Sheshan gave his daughter to his servant Jarha in marriage. She bore Jarha a son, Attai. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:36
Attai was Nathan’s father. Nathan was Zabad’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:37
Zabad was Ephlal’s father. Ephlal was Obed’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:38
Obed was Jehu’s father. Jehu was Azariah’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:39
Azariah was Helez’s father. Helez was Elasah’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:40
Elasah was Sismai’s father. Sismai was Shallum’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:41
Shallum was Jekamiah’s father. Jekamiah was Elishama’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:42
Caleb was Jerahmeel’s brother. Caleb’s firstborn, Mesha, was Ziph’s father. His second son, Mareshah, was Hebron’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:43
Hebron’s sons were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:44
Shema was Raham’s father. Raham was Jorkeam’s father. Rekem was Shammai’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:45
Shammai’s son was Maon. Maon was Beth-zur’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:46
Caleb’s concubine Ephah was the mother of Haran, Moza, and Gazez. Haran was Gazez’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:47
Jahdai’s sons were Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:48
Caleb’s concubine Maacah was Sheber and Tirhanah’s mother. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:49
She was also the mother of Shaaph, Madmannah’s father, and Sheva, the father of Machbenah and Gibea. Caleb’s daughter was Acsah. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:50
These were Caleb’s descendants. Hur was Ephrathah’s firstborn. His sons included Shobal, Kiriath-jearim’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:51
Hur’s sons also included Salma, Bethlehem’s father, and Hareph, Beth-gader’s father. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:52
Shobal was Kiriath-jearim’s father. His descendants included Haroeh and half the Manahathites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:53
They also included Kiriath-jearim’s clans: the Ithrites, Puthites, Shumathites, and Mishraites. The Zorathites and Eshtaolites descended from these. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:54
Salma’s descendants included Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, half the Manahathites, and the Zorites. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 2:55
They also included the scribes’ clans living at Jabez: the Tirathites, Shimeathites, and Sucathites. These Kenites came from Hammath, father of the house of Rechab. 📖
