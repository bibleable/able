## 1 Chronicles 20:1
In spring, when kings go to war, Joab led the army and ravaged Ammonite land. He besieged Rabbah while David stayed in Jerusalem. Joab attacked Rabbah and demolished it. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 20:2
David took the crown from their king’s head. It weighed a talent of gold and held precious stones. It was placed on David’s head. David took much plunder from the city. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 20:3
David brought out the people and put them to work with saws, iron picks, and axes. He did this to all Ammonite cities. Then he and all his troops returned to Jerusalem. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 20:4
Later, war with the Philistines began at Gezer. Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai, a descendant of the Rephaim. The Philistines were subdued. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 20:5
In another Philistine battle, Elhanan, Jair’s son, killed Lahmi. Lahmi was Goliath the Gittite’s brother. His spear shaft was like a weaver’s beam. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 20:6
Another battle took place at Gath. A very tall man there had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot, twenty-four in all. He too descended from Rapha. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 20:7
He mocked Israel. Jonathan, the son of David’s brother Shimei, killed him. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 20:8
These descendants of Rapha in Gath fell to David and his servants. 📖
