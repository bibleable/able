## 1 Chronicles 14:1
Hiram, Tyre’s king, sent messengers, cedar logs, stonemasons, and carpenters to David. They would build him a palace. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:2
David realized the LORD had established him as Israel’s king. He had greatly exalted David’s kingdom for His people Israel’s sake. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:3
David married more wives in Jerusalem. He had more sons and daughters. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:4
His children born in Jerusalem included Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:5
They also included Ibhar, Elishua, and Elpelet. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:6
They also included Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:7
They also included Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:8
The Philistines heard David had been anointed king over all Israel. They all went to seek him. David heard this and went to face them. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:9
The Philistines had raided the Valley of Rephaim. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:10
David asked God, “Should I attack the Philistines? Will You deliver them into my hand?” The LORD answered, “Go. I will deliver them into your hand.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:11
David and his men went to Baal-perazim and defeated the Philistines there. David said, “Like a bursting flood, God broke through my enemies by my hand.” So they called that place Baal-perazim. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:12
The Philistines abandoned their gods there. David ordered them burned. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:13
The Philistines raided the valley again. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:14
David again asked God. He answered, “Do not follow them directly. Circle around and attack in front of the balsam trees. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:15
When you hear marching in the balsam treetops, move into battle immediately. This means God has gone ahead to strike the Philistine camp.” 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:16
David obeyed God. They struck down the Philistine army from Gibeon to Gezer. 📖

## 1 Chronicles 14:17
David’s fame spread to every land. The LORD made all nations fear him. 📖
