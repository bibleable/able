## Ecclesiastes 12:1
Remember your Creator while you are young, before hard days and years come. You will say, “I find no pleasure in them.” 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 12:2
Remember Him before the sun, moon, and stars grow dark, and clouds return after rain. 🌑
## Ecclesiastes 12:3
The house’s keepers will tremble, and strong men will bend down. Those who grind will stop because they are few. Those who look through windows will see dimly. 🏠
## Ecclesiastes 12:4
The street doors will close, and the mill’s sound will fade. Someone will rise at a bird’s sound, and all the daughters of song will grow faint. 🐦
## Ecclesiastes 12:5
People will fear heights and road dangers. The almond tree will bloom, the grasshopper will lose its spring, and the caper berry will shrivel. People will go to their eternal home, while mourners walk the streets. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 12:6
Remember Him before the silver cord snaps and the gold bowl breaks. Remember Him before the pitcher shatters at the spring and the wheel breaks at the well. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 12:7
Remember Him before dust returns to the ground it came from and the spirit returns to God, who gave it. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 12:8
The Teacher says, “Completely pointless! Everything is pointless!” 💭
## Ecclesiastes 12:9
The Teacher was wise and taught people knowledge. He considered, searched out, and arranged many proverbs. 📖
## Ecclesiastes 12:10
He searched for delightful sayings and recorded accurate words of truth. ✍️
## Ecclesiastes 12:11
Wise words are like pointed sticks that guide animals. The masters’ collected sayings are like firmly fixed nails, driven by one Shepherd. 📖
## Ecclesiastes 12:12
My son, take this further warning. Books are made without end, and much study tires the body. 📚
## Ecclesiastes 12:13
After everything is heard, this is the conclusion: Fear God and obey His commands. This is everyone’s whole duty. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 12:14
God will judge every action and every hidden thing, whether good or evil. ⚖️
