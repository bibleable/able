## Ecclesiastes 1:1
These are the Teacher’s words. He was David’s son and king in Jerusalem. 📖
## Ecclesiastes 1:2
The Teacher says, “Completely pointless! Completely pointless! Everything is pointless!” 💭
## Ecclesiastes 1:3
What does anyone gain from all his hard work under the sun? ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 1:4
Generations come and go, but the earth remains forever. 🌍
## Ecclesiastes 1:5
The sun rises and sets. It hurries back to where it rises. 🌅
## Ecclesiastes 1:6
The wind blows south and turns north. It circles again and again, always returning on its course. 🌬️
## Ecclesiastes 1:7
All rivers flow into the sea, but the sea never fills. The streams flow again to the place they came from. 🌊
## Ecclesiastes 1:8
Everything is tiring beyond description. The eye never sees enough, and the ear never hears enough. 👀
## Ecclesiastes 1:9
What happened will happen again. What was done will be done again. Nothing is new under the sun. ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 1:10
Can anyone say, “Look, this is new”? It already existed in ages before us. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 1:11
People do not remember those who came before. Later generations will not remember those yet to come. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 1:12
I, the Teacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem. 👑
## Ecclesiastes 1:13
I used wisdom to search and explore everything done under heaven. God gave people this miserable task to occupy them. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 1:14
I saw everything done under the sun. It was all pointless, like chasing the wind. 🌬️
## Ecclesiastes 1:15
What is crooked cannot be straightened. What is missing cannot be counted. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 1:16
I told myself, “I gained more wisdom than all who ruled Jerusalem before me. My mind has seen much wisdom and knowledge.” 📖
## Ecclesiastes 1:17
I set my mind to understand wisdom, madness, and foolishness. I learned that this too is like chasing the wind. 🌬️
## Ecclesiastes 1:18
Much wisdom brings much sorrow. More knowledge brings more grief. 💔
