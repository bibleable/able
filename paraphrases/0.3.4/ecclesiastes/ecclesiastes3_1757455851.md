## Ecclesiastes 3:1
Everything has a season, and every purpose under heaven has a time. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 3:2
There is a time to be born and to die, to plant and to uproot. 🌱
## Ecclesiastes 3:3
There is a time to kill and to heal, to tear down and to build. 🛠️
## Ecclesiastes 3:4
There is a time to cry and to laugh, to mourn and to dance. 😢
## Ecclesiastes 3:5
There is a time to throw stones away and to gather them. There is a time to embrace and to avoid embracing. 🤝
## Ecclesiastes 3:6
There is a time to search and to count something lost, to keep and to throw away. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 3:7
There is a time to tear and to mend, to stay silent and to speak. 🗣️
## Ecclesiastes 3:8
There is a time to love and to hate, for war and for peace. 🕊️
## Ecclesiastes 3:9
What does a worker gain from his labor? 🛠️
## Ecclesiastes 3:10
I saw the burden God gave people to occupy them. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 3:11
God made everything beautiful in its time and set eternity in human hearts. Yet people cannot understand all His work from beginning to end. ✨
## Ecclesiastes 3:12
I know nothing is better for them than to rejoice and do good while they live. ❤️
## Ecclesiastes 3:13
Everyone should eat, drink, and enjoy all his work. This is God’s gift. 🎁
## Ecclesiastes 3:14
Everything God does lasts forever. Nothing can be added or removed. God does this so people fear Him. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 3:15
What exists and what will come have already been. God will call to account what has passed. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 3:16
I saw evil under the sun in the place of judgment and in the place of righteousness. ⚖️
## Ecclesiastes 3:17
I told myself, “God will judge righteous and wicked people. There is a time for every activity and action.” ⚖️
## Ecclesiastes 3:18
I told myself, “God tests people so that they can see they are like animals.” 💭
## Ecclesiastes 3:19
People and animals share one fate. Both die and have the same breath. People have no advantage over animals, because everything is pointless. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 3:20
All go to one place. All come from dust and return to dust. 🌍
## Ecclesiastes 3:21
Who knows if a person’s spirit rises and an animal’s spirit goes down into the earth? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 3:22
I saw nothing better than to enjoy your work, because this is your portion. Who can bring you to see what comes after you? 🛠️
