## Ecclesiastes 9:1
I took this to heart: righteous and wise people, with their actions, are in God’s hands. People do not know whether love or hate lies ahead. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 9:2
One fate comes to righteous and wicked people, good and bad people, and clean and unclean people. It comes to those who sacrifice and those who do not. It comes to good people and sinners, those who make vows and those who refuse. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 9:3
This is an evil under the sun: everyone shares one fate. People’s hearts are full of evil and madness while they live. Then they join the dead. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 9:4
Anyone still alive has hope. A living dog is better than a dead lion. 🌅
## Ecclesiastes 9:5
The living know they will die, but the dead know nothing. They have no more reward, because their memory is forgotten. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 9:6
Their love, hate, and envy are gone. They will never again share in anything done under the sun. ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 9:7
Eat your bread joyfully and drink your wine with a cheerful heart. God already approved your work. 🍞
## Ecclesiastes 9:8
Always wear white clothes, and never lack oil for your head. ✨
## Ecclesiastes 9:9
Enjoy life with the wife you love during all the brief days God gives you under the sun. This is your portion in life and work under the sun. ❤️
## Ecclesiastes 9:10
Do whatever your hands find to do with all your strength. You are going to Sheol, where there is no work, planning, knowledge, or wisdom. 🛠️

*Word help: Sheol is the place of the dead.*
## Ecclesiastes 9:11
I saw this under the sun: fast runners do not always win races, and strong warriors do not always win battles. Wisdom does not always bring bread, intelligence wealth, or skill favor. Time and chance happen to everyone. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 9:12
No one knows his time. Like fish caught in a cruel net or birds in a trap, people are caught by sudden evil times. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 9:13
I also saw this wisdom under the sun, and it impressed me greatly. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 9:14
A small city had few men. A powerful king attacked it, surrounded it, and built large siege ramps against it. ⚔️
## Ecclesiastes 9:15
A poor, wise man lived there and saved the city through wisdom. Yet no one remembered him. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 9:16
I said, “Wisdom is better than strength. But people despise a poor man’s wisdom and ignore his words.” 📖
## Ecclesiastes 9:17
People listen to calm, wise words more than a ruler’s shouts among fools. 🗣️
## Ecclesiastes 9:18
Wisdom is better than weapons, but one sinner destroys much good. ⚠️
