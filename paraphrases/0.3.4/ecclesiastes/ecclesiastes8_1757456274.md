## Ecclesiastes 8:1
Who is like the wise person? Who can explain a matter? Wisdom brightens a person’s face and changes its stern look. ✨
## Ecclesiastes 8:2
Obey the king because of your oath before God. 👑
## Ecclesiastes 8:3
Do not hurry away from him or persist in a bad cause. He will do whatever he pleases. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 8:4
The king’s word has the highest authority. Who can ask him, “What are you doing?” 👑
## Ecclesiastes 8:5
Anyone who obeys his command will suffer no harm. A wise heart knows the proper time and procedure. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 8:6
Every purpose has a proper time and procedure, though a person’s misery weighs heavily on him. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 8:7
No one knows what will happen. Who can tell him what comes next? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 8:8
No one can contain the wind or control his death’s day. No one is released from service in wartime. Evil will not release those who practice it. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 8:9
I considered every action under the sun and saw all this. Sometimes one person rules another to his own harm. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 8:10
I saw wicked people buried. They used to enter and leave the holy place, and people praised them in the city where they did this. This too is pointless. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 8:11
When punishment for crime is delayed, people’s hearts become fully determined to do evil. ⚖️
## Ecclesiastes 8:12
A sinner may do evil a hundred times and live long. Yet I know things will go well for those who fear God and respect Him. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 8:13
Things will not go well for wicked people because they do not fear God. Their days will not grow long like a shadow. ⚖️
## Ecclesiastes 8:14
Something pointless happens on earth. Righteous people receive what wicked actions deserve, and wicked people receive what righteous actions deserve. I say this too is pointless. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 8:15
I praised enjoyment of life. Nothing is better under the sun than to eat, drink, and rejoice. This joy stays with people in their work during the days God gives them under the sun. 🍞
## Ecclesiastes 8:16
I sought wisdom and observed the task a person performs on earth, though his eyes see no sleep day or night. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 8:17
I saw God’s work and that people cannot understand what happens under the sun. They search hard but cannot find its meaning. Even a wise person who claims to know cannot understand it. 📖
