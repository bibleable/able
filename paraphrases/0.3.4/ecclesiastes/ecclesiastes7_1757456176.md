## Ecclesiastes 7:1
A good name is better than fine perfume. The day of death is better than the day of birth. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:2
A house of mourning is better than a house of feasting. Death comes to everyone, and the living must take this to heart. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 7:3
Sorrow is better than laughter, because a sad face benefits the heart. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 7:4
Wise hearts are in the house of mourning. Fools’ hearts are in the house of pleasure. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:5
Listen to a wise person’s correction rather than a fool’s song. 📖
## Ecclesiastes 7:6
A fool’s laughter is like thorns that crackle under a pot. This too is pointless. 🔥
## Ecclesiastes 7:7
Extortion makes a wise person foolish, and a bribe corrupts the heart. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 7:8
The end of something is better than its beginning. A patient spirit is better than a proud one. 🕊️
## Ecclesiastes 7:9
Do not become angry quickly. Anger settles in a fool’s lap. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 7:10
Do not ask, “Why were the old days better?” That question is unwise. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:11
Wisdom is good, like an inheritance. It benefits those who see the sun. ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 7:12
Wisdom provides shelter, like money. Knowledge has this advantage: wisdom preserves its owner’s life. 🛡️
## Ecclesiastes 7:13
Consider God’s work. Who can straighten what He bent? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:14
Rejoice when you prosper. In hardship, remember that God made both, so that people cannot discover what comes afterward. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 7:15
In my pointless life, I saw righteous people perish in their righteousness and wicked people live long in their wickedness. ⚖️
## Ecclesiastes 7:16
Do not be too righteous or make yourself too wise. Why destroy yourself? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:17
Do not be too wicked or foolish. Why die before your time? ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 7:18
Hold one warning without letting the other slip. Anyone who fears God will follow both. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 7:19
Wisdom makes a wise person stronger than ten city rulers. 💪
## Ecclesiastes 7:20
No righteous person on earth does good and never sins. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 7:21
Do not listen to every spoken word. You might hear your servant curse you. 🗣️
## Ecclesiastes 7:22
You know in your heart that you often cursed others yourself. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:23
I tested all this with wisdom and said, “I will be wise.” But wisdom was beyond me. 📖
## Ecclesiastes 7:24
What exists is beyond reach and very deep. Who can understand it? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:25
I sought to understand and explore wisdom and explanations. I wanted to understand the stupidity of evil and the foolishness of madness. 📖
## Ecclesiastes 7:26
I find the woman who is a trap more bitter than death. Her heart is a net, and her hands are chains. Anyone who pleases God escapes her, but the sinner is trapped. ⛓️
## Ecclesiastes 7:27
The Teacher says, “I found this by adding one thing to another to seek an explanation. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 7:28
While I still searched without finding, I found one upright man among a thousand. But among all these, I found no such woman. 👥
## Ecclesiastes 7:29
I found only this: God made people upright, but they sought many schemes.” ⚠️
