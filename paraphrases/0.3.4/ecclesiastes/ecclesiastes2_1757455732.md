## Ecclesiastes 2:1
I told myself, “I will test you with pleasure. Enjoy what is good!” But this proved pointless. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 2:2
I called laughter foolish and asked pleasure, “What do you achieve?” 💬
## Ecclesiastes 2:3
I tried to cheer my body with wine and embrace foolishness, while wisdom still guided my mind. I wanted to learn what people should do under heaven during their few days of life. 🍷
## Ecclesiastes 2:4
I expanded my work. I built houses and planted vineyards for myself. 🏠
## Ecclesiastes 2:5
I made gardens and parks and planted every kind of fruit tree. 🌳
## Ecclesiastes 2:6
I built reservoirs to water my groves of growing trees. 💧
## Ecclesiastes 2:7
I acquired male and female servants, and servants were born in my house. I owned more herds and flocks than anyone in Jerusalem before me. 🐑
## Ecclesiastes 2:8
I gathered silver, gold, and treasures from kings and provinces. I gathered male and female singers, and many concubines, the delights of men. 💎
## Ecclesiastes 2:9
I became greater than all who came before me in Jerusalem. My wisdom stayed with me. 👑
## Ecclesiastes 2:10
I denied myself nothing my eyes desired and no pleasure my heart wanted. My heart enjoyed all my work. This was my reward for my labor. ❤️
## Ecclesiastes 2:11
I considered everything my hands made and all my hard work. It was all pointless, like chasing the wind. Nothing was gained under the sun. 🌬️
## Ecclesiastes 2:12
I turned to consider wisdom, madness, and foolishness. What more can the king’s successor do beyond what was already done? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 2:13
I saw that wisdom is better than foolishness, as light is better than darkness. ✨
## Ecclesiastes 2:14
The wise person has eyes in his head, while the fool walks in darkness. Yet the same fate comes to both. 🌑
## Ecclesiastes 2:15
I told myself, “The fool’s fate will happen to me too. What did I gain from wisdom?” I said that this too is pointless. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 2:16
Neither wise people nor fools are remembered forever. Both will be forgotten in the days ahead. Sadly, the wise die just like fools! ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 2:17
I hated life because the work done under the sun caused me grief. Everything is pointless, like chasing the wind. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 2:18
I hated all my work under the sun because I must leave it to someone after me. 🛠️
## Ecclesiastes 2:19
Who knows if he will be wise or foolish? He will control everything I worked skillfully to gain under the sun. This too is pointless. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 2:20
My heart began to despair over all my work under the sun. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 2:21
Someone works with wisdom, knowledge, and skill but must leave his portion to someone who did not work for it. This too is pointless and a great evil. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 2:22
What does someone gain from all his hard work and striving under the sun? ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 2:23
His days are full of grief, and his work brings sorrow. Even at night, his mind cannot rest. This too is pointless. 🌙
## Ecclesiastes 2:24
Nothing is better than to eat, drink, and enjoy work. I saw that this comes from God’s hand. 🍞
## Ecclesiastes 2:25
Apart from Him, who can eat or find enjoyment? 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 2:26
God gives wisdom, knowledge, and joy to the person who pleases Him. He gives sinners the task of gathering things to hand to someone who pleases God. This too is pointless, like chasing the wind. 🌬️
