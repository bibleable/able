## Ecclesiastes 5:1
Watch your steps when you enter God’s house. Come to listen instead of offering the sacrifice of fools, who do not know they do wrong. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 5:2
Do not speak quickly or rush to say something before God. He is in heaven, and you are on earth. Use few words. 🗣️
## Ecclesiastes 5:3
Many worries bring dreams, and many words bring a fool’s speech. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 5:4
When you promise God something, fulfill it without delay. He takes no pleasure in fools. Keep your promise. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 5:5
Not making a promise is better than making one and breaking it. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 5:6
Do not let your mouth cause you to sin. Do not tell the messenger your promise was a mistake. Why make God angry at your words and destroy your work? 🗣️
## Ecclesiastes 5:7
Many dreams and many words bring pointlessness. Fear God. 🙏
## Ecclesiastes 5:8
Do not be surprised if poor people are oppressed and denied justice and righteousness in a province. One official is watched by a superior, and still higher officials are above them. ⚖️
## Ecclesiastes 5:9
Everyone takes the earth’s produce. The king himself profits from the fields. 🌾
## Ecclesiastes 5:10
Those who love money never have enough money. Those who love wealth never have enough income. This too is pointless. 💰
## Ecclesiastes 5:11
As goods increase, so do the people who consume them. What does the owner gain except to look at them? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 5:12
A worker sleeps sweetly, whether he eats little or much. A rich person’s abundance prevents sleep. 🌙
## Ecclesiastes 5:13
I saw a terrible evil under the sun: wealth stored up that harms its owner. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 5:14
Wealth can be lost in a failed venture. When the man has a son, he has nothing to pass on. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 5:15
He leaves naked, as he came from his mother’s womb. He carries away none of the gain from his work. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 5:16
This too is a terrible affliction. A person leaves exactly as he came. What does he gain by working for the wind? 🌬️
## Ecclesiastes 5:17
All his days, he eats in darkness with much sorrow, sickness, and anger. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 5:18
I saw what is good and proper: eat, drink, and enjoy your work under the sun during the few days God gives you. This is your portion. 🍞
## Ecclesiastes 5:19
God gave everyone riches and wealth, and enabled him to enjoy them, accept his portion, and rejoice in his work. This is God’s gift. 🎁
## Ecclesiastes 5:20
He seldom thinks about his life’s days because God occupies him with joy in his heart. ❤️
