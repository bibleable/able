## Ecclesiastes 11:1
Throw your bread on the waters. After many days, you will find it again. 🌊
## Ecclesiastes 11:2
Divide your portion among seven or even eight. You do not know what disaster may strike the land. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 11:3
Full clouds pour rain on the earth. A tree stays where it falls, whether south or north. 🌧️
## Ecclesiastes 11:4
Anyone who watches the wind will not plant. Anyone who watches the clouds will not harvest. 🌾
## Ecclesiastes 11:5
You do not know the wind’s path or how bones form in a mother’s womb. You cannot understand the work of God, who makes everything. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 11:6
Plant seed in the morning, and keep your hands at work in the evening. You do not know which will succeed, or whether both will prosper equally. 🌱
## Ecclesiastes 11:7
Light is sweet, and seeing the sun pleases the eyes. ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 11:8
If someone lives many years, let him enjoy them all. But remember the many days of darkness. Everything to come is pointless. ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 11:9
Young man, rejoice in your youth and let your heart be glad. Follow your heart’s ways and what your eyes see. But know that God will judge all these things. ⚖️
## Ecclesiastes 11:10
Remove sorrow from your heart and pain from your body. Youth and strength quickly pass. 🌱
