## Ecclesiastes 10:1
Dead flies make perfume oil stink. A little foolishness outweighs wisdom and honor. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 10:2
A wise person’s heart turns right, but a fool’s turns left. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 10:3
Even while walking on a road, a fool lacks sense and shows everyone his foolishness. 👣
## Ecclesiastes 10:4
If a ruler becomes angry with you, do not leave your post. Calmness puts great offenses to rest. 🕊️
## Ecclesiastes 10:5
I saw an evil under the sun, an error that comes from the ruler. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 10:6
Foolishness receives high positions, while rich people sit in low positions. 👑
## Ecclesiastes 10:7
I saw slaves on horses and princes walking like slaves. 🐎
## Ecclesiastes 10:8
Anyone who digs a pit may fall in. Anyone who breaks through a wall may be bitten by a snake. 🐍
## Ecclesiastes 10:9
Anyone who cuts stone may be hurt by it. Anyone who splits logs risks injury. 🛠️
## Ecclesiastes 10:10
A dull, unsharpened axe requires more strength. Skill brings success. 🪓
## Ecclesiastes 10:11
If a snake bites before it is charmed, the charmer gains nothing. 🐍
## Ecclesiastes 10:12
Wise words are gracious, but a fool’s lips consume him. 🗣️
## Ecclesiastes 10:13
His speech begins with foolishness and ends in evil madness. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 10:14
The fool keeps talking. No one knows what comes next. Who can tell him what happens after him? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 10:15
A fool’s work tires him, because he does not know the way to the city. 👣
## Ecclesiastes 10:16
How terrible for a land whose king is young and whose princes feast in the morning! ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 10:17
Blessed is a land whose king is a noble’s son and whose princes feast at the proper time. They eat for strength, rather than drunkenness. 👑
## Ecclesiastes 10:18
Laziness makes a roof fall in. Idle hands let a house leak. 🏚️
## Ecclesiastes 10:19
A feast brings laughter, and wine makes life cheerful. Money answers everything. 🍽️
## Ecclesiastes 10:20
Do not curse the king in your thoughts or rich people in your bedroom. A bird may carry your words, and a winged creature may report them. 🐦
