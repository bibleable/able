## Ecclesiastes 4:1
I again considered all the oppression under the sun. Oppressed people cried, with no one to comfort them. Their oppressors had power, and they had no comforter. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 4:2
I admired those already dead more than those still alive. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 4:3
Someone who has not yet existed is better off than both. He has not seen the evil done under the sun. ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 4:4
I saw that all work and success come from envy of a neighbor. This too is pointless, like chasing the wind. 🌬️
## Ecclesiastes 4:5
The fool folds his hands and consumes his own flesh. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 4:6
One handful with peace is better than two handfuls with hard work and chasing the wind. 🕊️
## Ecclesiastes 4:7
Again, I saw something pointless under the sun. ☀️
## Ecclesiastes 4:8
A man is alone, without son or brother. His work never ends, and his wealth never satisfies his eyes. He asks, “For whom do I work and deny myself enjoyment?” This too is pointless and miserable work. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 4:9
Two are better than one because their work brings a good return. 🤝
## Ecclesiastes 4:10
If one falls, the other can lift him. Pity anyone who falls without someone to help! ❤️
## Ecclesiastes 4:11
Two who lie together keep warm. How can one person keep warm alone? 🤝
## Ecclesiastes 4:12
One person can be overpowered, but two can resist. A cord with three strands does not break quickly. 💪
## Ecclesiastes 4:13
A poor, wise young person is better than an old, foolish king who no longer accepts warnings. 👑
## Ecclesiastes 4:14
The young person rose from prison to be king, though he was born poor in his kingdom. 🌅
## Ecclesiastes 4:15
I saw everyone living under the sun follow this second person, the young man who replaced the king. 👥
## Ecclesiastes 4:16
There is no end to all the people before them. Yet later generations will not celebrate the successor. This too is pointless, like chasing the wind. 🌬️
