## Ecclesiastes 6:1
I saw another evil under the sun that weighs heavily on people. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 6:2
God gives someone riches, wealth, and honor, with everything his heart desires. But God does not let him enjoy it. A stranger enjoys it instead. This is pointless and a terrible affliction. ⚠️
## Ecclesiastes 6:3
A man may father a hundred children and live many years. If he never enjoys his prosperity or receives a proper burial, a stillborn child is better off. 💔
## Ecclesiastes 6:4
The stillborn child comes in pointlessness and leaves in darkness. His name is hidden in obscurity. 🌑
## Ecclesiastes 6:5
Though the child never sees the sun or knows anything, he has more rest than that man. 🕊️
## Ecclesiastes 6:6
This is true even if the man lives a thousand years twice over without enjoying his prosperity. Do all not go to the same place? ⏳
## Ecclesiastes 6:7
All a person’s work is for his mouth, but his appetite is never satisfied. 🍞
## Ecclesiastes 6:8
What advantage does the wise person have over the fool? What does a poor person gain by knowing how to behave before others? 💭
## Ecclesiastes 6:9
What the eye sees is better than wandering desire. This too is pointless, like chasing the wind. 🌬️
## Ecclesiastes 6:10
Everything that exists was named long ago. It is known what a person is. But he cannot argue with someone stronger than himself. 💭
## Ecclesiastes 6:11
More words bring more pointlessness. How does that help anyone? 🗣️
## Ecclesiastes 6:12
Who knows what is good during a person’s few days of life, which pass like a shadow? Who can tell him what comes afterward under the sun? ⏳
