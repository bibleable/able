## Matthew 11:1
Jesus finished instructing the Twelve and went to teach and preach in their cities. 📖
## Matthew 11:2
John heard Christ’s works from prison and sent his disciples, 📖
## Matthew 11:3
asking, “Are You the One to come, or should we expect another?” ❓
## Matthew 11:4
Jesus answered, “Tell John what you hear and see. 📖
## Matthew 11:5
The blind see, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, and the dead rise. Good news is preached to the poor. 📖
## Matthew 11:6
Blessed is whoever does not fall away because of Me.” 📖
## Matthew 11:7
As they left, Jesus asked the crowds about John. “What did you seek in the wilderness? A windblown reed? ❓
## Matthew 11:8
A man in fine clothes? Such people live in kings’ palaces. 📖
## Matthew 11:9
A prophet? Yes, and more than a prophet. 📖
## Matthew 11:10
Scripture says about him, ‘I will send My messenger before You to prepare Your way.’ 📖
## Matthew 11:11
Truly, none born of women is greater than John the Baptist. Yet the least in the kingdom of heaven is greater. 📖
## Matthew 11:12
Since John’s days until now, the kingdom of heaven has suffered violence. The violent lay claim to it. 📖
## Matthew 11:13
All the Prophets and Law prophesied until John. 📖
## Matthew 11:14
If you accept it, he is the Elijah who was to come. 📖
## Matthew 11:15
Let those with ears hear. 📖
## Matthew 11:16
What resembles this generation? Children calling in marketplaces, ❓
## Matthew 11:17
‘We played the flute, but you did not dance. We sang a dirge, but you did not mourn.’ 📖
## Matthew 11:18
John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say he has a demon. 📖
## Matthew 11:19
The Son of Man came eating and drinking. They call Him a glutton, drunkard, and friend of tax collectors and sinners. Wisdom is vindicated by her actions.” 📖
## Matthew 11:20
Jesus denounced the cities with most of His miracles because they did not repent. 📖
## Matthew 11:21
“Woe to Chorazin and Bethsaida! Had your miracles occurred in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes. 📖
## Matthew 11:22
Tyre and Sidon will find judgment day more bearable than you. 📖
## Matthew 11:23
Capernaum, will you rise to heaven? No, you will be brought down to Hades! Had your miracles occurred in Sodom, it would still remain. 📖
## Matthew 11:24
Sodom will find judgment day more bearable than you.” 📖
## Matthew 11:25
Jesus said, “Father, Lord of heaven and earth, I praise You because You hid these things from the wise and learned. You also revealed them to little children. 🙏
## Matthew 11:26
Yes, Father, this pleased You. 🙏
## Matthew 11:27
My Father entrusted everything to Me. Only the Father knows the Son. Only the Son and those to whom He chooses to reveal the Father know the Father. 📖
## Matthew 11:28
Come to Me, all weary and burdened people. I will give you rest. ❤️
## Matthew 11:29
Take My yoke and learn from Me because I am gentle and humble in heart. You will find rest for your souls. ❤️
## Matthew 11:30
My yoke is easy, and My burden is light.” ❤️
