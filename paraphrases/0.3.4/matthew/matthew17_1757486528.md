## Matthew 17:1
After six days Jesus took Peter, James, and his brother John up a high mountain alone. 📖
## Matthew 17:2
He was transfigured before them. His face shone like the sun and clothes became white as light. ✨
## Matthew 17:3
Moses and Elijah appeared, talking with Him. 📖
## Matthew 17:4
Peter said, “Lord, it is good to be here. If You wish, I will build three shelters, one each for You, Moses, and Elijah.” 📖
## Matthew 17:5
A bright cloud surrounded them as he spoke. A voice from the cloud said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to Him!” 📖
## Matthew 17:6
They fell facedown in terror. 📖
## Matthew 17:7
Jesus touched them. “Rise. Do not fear.” 📖
## Matthew 17:8
They looked up and saw only Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 17:9
Descending, Jesus forbade telling the vision until the Son of Man was raised from death. 📖
## Matthew 17:10
They asked why the scribes said Elijah must come first. ❓
## Matthew 17:11
He answered, “Elijah comes and will restore everything. 📖
## Matthew 17:12
He already came, but they failed to recognize him and did whatever they wished. The Son of Man will likewise suffer at their hands.” 📖
## Matthew 17:13
They understood He meant John the Baptist. 📖
## Matthew 17:14
At the crowd, a man knelt before Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 17:15
“Lord, have mercy on my son. His seizures cause terrible suffering. He often falls into fire or water. 🙏
## Matthew 17:16
Your disciples could not heal him when I brought him.” 📖
## Matthew 17:17
Jesus answered, “Unbelieving, perverse generation! How long must I stay and endure you? Bring the boy to Me.” 📖
## Matthew 17:18
He rebuked the demon, which left. The boy was healed from that moment. ❤️
## Matthew 17:19
The disciples asked privately why they could not expel it. ❓
## Matthew 17:20
“Your faith is so little. With faith the size of a mustard seed, you can tell this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will. Nothing will be impossible for you.” 📖

## Matthew 17:22
In Galilee, Jesus said, “The Son of Man will soon be delivered into men’s hands. 📖
## Matthew 17:23
They will kill Him. On day three He will be raised to life.” The disciples grieved deeply. 💔
## Matthew 17:24
In Capernaum, collectors asked Peter whether Jesus paid the two-drachma tax. 📖
## Matthew 17:25
He said yes. When Peter entered the house, Jesus spoke first. “Simon, do earthly kings collect customs and taxes from their sons or others?” ❓
## Matthew 17:26
“Others.” Jesus said, “Then sons are exempt. 📖
## Matthew 17:27
To avoid offending them, cast a hook into the sea and take the first fish. Open its mouth, and you will find a four-drachma coin. Take it and give it to them for My tax and yours.” 📖
