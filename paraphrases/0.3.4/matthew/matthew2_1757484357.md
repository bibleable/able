## Matthew 2:1
Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea during King Herod’s time. Magi from the east came to Jerusalem. 📖
## Matthew 2:2
They asked, “Where is the newborn King of the Jews? We saw His star in the east and came to worship Him.” ❓
## Matthew 2:3
Herod and all Jerusalem were disturbed. 📖
## Matthew 2:4
He gathered all the chief priests and scribes and asked where the Christ would be born. 📖
## Matthew 2:5
They answered, “Bethlehem in Judea. The prophet wrote: 📖
## Matthew 2:6
‘Bethlehem in Judah, you are not least among Judah’s rulers. A ruler will come from you to shepherd My people Israel.’” 📖
## Matthew 2:7
Herod secretly asked the Magi when the star appeared and learned the exact time. 📖
## Matthew 2:8
He sent them to Bethlehem. “Search carefully for the Child. When you find Him, tell me so I can worship Him too.” 📖
## Matthew 2:9
They left after hearing the king. The star seen in the east went before them and stopped over the Child’s place. 📖
## Matthew 2:10
They saw the star and rejoiced greatly. ✨
## Matthew 2:11
At the house, they saw the Child with His mother Mary and fell down to worship Him. They opened their treasures and gave gold, frankincense, and myrrh. 🙏
## Matthew 2:12
A dream warned them not to return to Herod. They went home by another route. 📖
## Matthew 2:13
After they left, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream. “Get up! Flee to Egypt with the Child and His mother. Stay until I tell you. Herod will seek to kill Him.” 📖
## Matthew 2:14
Joseph rose and took the Child and His mother to Egypt by night. 📖
## Matthew 2:15
He stayed until Herod died, fulfilling the Lord’s word through the prophet: “Out of Egypt I called My Son.” 📖
## Matthew 2:16
Herod was furious that the Magi outwitted him. By his orders, all boys aged two and under in Bethlehem and its vicinity were killed, based on the time the Magi reported. 💔
## Matthew 2:17
This fulfilled Jeremiah’s prophecy: 📖
## Matthew 2:18
“A voice in Ramah weeps and mourns greatly. Rachel weeps for her children and refuses comfort because they are no more.” 💔
## Matthew 2:19
After Herod died, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream in Egypt. 📖
## Matthew 2:20
“Get up! Take the Child and His mother to Israel. Those seeking His life are dead.” 📖
## Matthew 2:21
Joseph rose and took the Child and His mother to Israel. 📖
## Matthew 2:22
He feared going to Judea because Archelaus ruled after his father Herod. Warned in a dream, he went to Galilee, 📖
## Matthew 2:23
and settled in Nazareth. This fulfilled the prophets’ words: “He will be called a Nazarene.” 📖
