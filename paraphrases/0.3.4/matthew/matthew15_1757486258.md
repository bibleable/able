## Matthew 15:1
Pharisees and scribes came from Jerusalem and asked Jesus, 📖
## Matthew 15:2
“Why do Your disciples break the elders’ tradition by eating without washing their hands?” ❓
## Matthew 15:3
He answered, “Why break God’s command for your tradition? ❓
## Matthew 15:4
God said, ‘Honor your father and mother. Anyone cursing either must be put to death.’ 📖
## Matthew 15:5
You say someone may tell his parents, ‘What you would receive from me is devoted to God.’ 📖
## Matthew 15:6
Then he need not honor them with it. Your tradition nullifies God’s word. 📖
## Matthew 15:7
Hypocrites! Isaiah rightly prophesied about you: 📖
## Matthew 15:8
‘They honor Me with their lips, but their hearts are far away. 📖
## Matthew 15:9
They worship Me in vain, teaching human rules as doctrine.’” 📖
## Matthew 15:10
He told the crowd, “Listen and understand. 📖
## Matthew 15:11
What enters the mouth does not defile a man. What comes out does.” 📖
## Matthew 15:12
The disciples asked whether He knew the Pharisees were offended. 📖
## Matthew 15:13
He replied, “Every plant My heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted. 📖
## Matthew 15:14
Disregard them. They are blind guides. Both blind leader and follower fall into a pit.” 📖
## Matthew 15:15
Peter asked Him to explain the parable. ❓
## Matthew 15:16
“Do you still not understand? ❓
## Matthew 15:17
Food entering the mouth goes to the stomach and is eliminated. 📖
## Matthew 15:18
Things from the mouth come from the heart and defile a man. 📖
## Matthew 15:19
The heart produces evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false testimony, and slander. 📖
## Matthew 15:20
These defile a man. Eating with unwashed hands does not.” 📖
## Matthew 15:21
Jesus withdrew to Tyre and Sidon’s district. 📖
## Matthew 15:22
A Canaanite woman cried, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy! My daughter is terribly possessed by a demon.” 🙏
## Matthew 15:23
He said nothing. His disciples urged, “Send her away. She keeps crying after us.” 📖
## Matthew 15:24
He said, “I was sent only to Israel’s lost sheep.” 📖
## Matthew 15:25
She knelt and said, “Lord, help me!” 🙏
## Matthew 15:26
He answered, “Taking children’s bread and throwing it to dogs is not right.” 📖
## Matthew 15:27
She said, “Yes, Lord. Even dogs eat crumbs from their masters’ table.” 📖
## Matthew 15:28
He answered, “Woman, your faith is great! Let it be as you desire.” Her daughter was healed that very hour. ❤️
## Matthew 15:29
Jesus went along the Sea of Galilee and sat on a mountain. 📖
## Matthew 15:30
Crowds laid the lame, blind, crippled, mute, and many others at His feet. He healed them. ❤️
## Matthew 15:31
They saw the mute speak, crippled restored, lame walk, and blind see. Amazed, they glorified Israel’s God. 🙏
## Matthew 15:32
Jesus said, “I feel compassion for this crowd because they stayed with Me three days and have no food. I do not want to send them away hungry, or they may faint along the way.” ❤️
## Matthew 15:33
The disciples asked where enough bread could be found for so many in that deserted place. ❓
## Matthew 15:34
He asked how many loaves they had. “Seven, and a few small fish.” 📖
## Matthew 15:35
He told the crowd to sit on the ground. 📖
## Matthew 15:36
He took seven loaves and fish, gave thanks, broke them, and gave them to His disciples. They gave them to the people. 📖
## Matthew 15:37
Everyone ate and was satisfied. His disciples collected seven baskets of leftover pieces. 📖
## Matthew 15:38
Four thousand men ate, besides women and children. 👥
## Matthew 15:39
Jesus dismissed the crowds, entered the boat, and went to Magadan’s region. 📖
