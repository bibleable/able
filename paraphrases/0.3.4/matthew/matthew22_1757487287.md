## Matthew 22:1
Jesus spoke another parable. 📖
## Matthew 22:2
“The kingdom of heaven resembles a king preparing his son’s wedding feast. 📖
## Matthew 22:3
His servants summoned invited guests, who refused. 📖
## Matthew 22:4
He sent others: ‘Dinner is ready. My oxen and fattened cattle are killed. Come to the wedding feast.’ 📖
## Matthew 22:5
They ignored it and went to fields or business. 📖
## Matthew 22:6
Others mistreated and killed his servants. 📖
## Matthew 22:7
The enraged king sent soldiers to destroy the murderers and burn their city. 📖
## Matthew 22:8
He said, ‘The feast is ready, but the invited guests were unworthy. 📖
## Matthew 22:9
Invite everyone you find at the crossroads.’ 📖
## Matthew 22:10
Servants gathered everyone, evil and good, filling the hall. 📖
## Matthew 22:11
The king saw a man without wedding clothes. 📖
## Matthew 22:12
‘Friend, how did you enter without wedding clothes?’ He was speechless. 📖
## Matthew 22:13
The king ordered, ‘Bind his hands and feet and throw him into outer darkness, with weeping and grinding of teeth.’ 📖
## Matthew 22:14
Many are called, but few chosen.” 📖
## Matthew 22:15
The Pharisees plotted to trap Jesus with His words. 📖
## Matthew 22:16
They sent disciples and Herodians. “Teacher, You are honest and teach God’s way truthfully. You seek no favor because You ignore outward appearance. 📖
## Matthew 22:17
Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar?” ❓
## Matthew 22:18
Jesus knew their evil intent. “Hypocrites, why test Me? ❓
## Matthew 22:19
Show the tax coin.” They brought a denarius. 📖
## Matthew 22:20
He asked whose image and inscription it bore. ❓
## Matthew 22:21
“Caesar’s.” He answered, “Give Caesar what is Caesar’s, and God what is God’s.” 📖
## Matthew 22:22
They marveled and left. 📖
## Matthew 22:23
Sadducees denying resurrection questioned Him that day. 📖
## Matthew 22:24
“Moses said that if a man dies childless, his brother must marry the widow and raise offspring for him. 📖
## Matthew 22:25
Seven brothers lived among us. The first married, died childless, and left his wife to his brother. 📖
## Matthew 22:26
The same happened to the second and third, through the seventh. 📖
## Matthew 22:27
She died last. 📖
## Matthew 22:28
Whose wife will she be in the resurrection? All seven married her.” ❓
## Matthew 22:29
He answered, “You err because you know neither Scripture nor God’s power. 📖
## Matthew 22:30
Resurrected people neither marry nor are given in marriage. They are like heaven’s angels. 📖
## Matthew 22:31
Concerning resurrection, did you not read God’s words: 📖
## Matthew 22:32
‘I am Abraham’s, Isaac’s, and Jacob’s God’? He is God of the living, not the dead.” 📖
## Matthew 22:33
The crowds marveled at His teaching. 📖
## Matthew 22:34
The Pharisees gathered after hearing He silenced the Sadducees. 📖
## Matthew 22:35
A law expert among them tested Him, 📖
## Matthew 22:36
“Teacher, which Law command is greatest?” ❓
## Matthew 22:37
He answered, “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and mind. ❤️
## Matthew 22:38
This is the first and greatest command. 📖
## Matthew 22:39
The second is similar: Love your neighbor as yourself. ❤️
## Matthew 22:40
All the Law and Prophets hang on these two commands.” 📖
## Matthew 22:41
He questioned the gathered Pharisees, 📖
## Matthew 22:42
“Whose son is the Christ?” They answered, “David’s.” 📖
## Matthew 22:43
“Why does David call Him ‘Lord’ in the Spirit? He says, 📖
## Matthew 22:44
‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand until I put Your enemies under Your feet.”’ 📖
## Matthew 22:45
If David calls Him Lord, how is He David’s son?” ❓
## Matthew 22:46
No one could answer. From that day no one dared question Him further. 📖
