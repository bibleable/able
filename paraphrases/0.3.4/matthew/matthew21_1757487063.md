## Matthew 21:1
Near Jerusalem, at Bethphage on the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two disciples. 📖
## Matthew 21:2
“In the next village, you will find a tied donkey with her colt. Untie and bring them. 📖
## Matthew 21:3
If questioned, say the Lord needs them. He will send them immediately.” 📖
## Matthew 21:4
This fulfilled the prophet’s words: 📖
## Matthew 21:5
“Tell Zion’s Daughter, ‘Your King comes gently, riding a donkey, a colt, a donkey’s foal.’” 📖
## Matthew 21:6
The disciples obeyed. 📖
## Matthew 21:7
They brought the donkey and colt, laid cloaks on them, and Jesus sat on them. 📖
## Matthew 21:8
Crowds spread cloaks on the road. Others cut tree branches and spread them. 📖
## Matthew 21:9
Those ahead and behind shouted, “Hosanna to David’s Son! Blessed is He coming in the Lord’s name! Hosanna in the highest!” 🙏
## Matthew 21:10
All Jerusalem stirred, asking who He was. ❓
## Matthew 21:11
The crowd answered, “Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.” 📖
## Matthew 21:12
Jesus expelled the temple courts’ buyers and sellers. He overturned money changers’ tables and dove sellers’ seats. 📖
## Matthew 21:13
He said, “It is written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer.’ You make it ‘a den of robbers.’” 📖
## Matthew 21:14
He healed the blind and lame who came to the temple. ❤️
## Matthew 21:15
The chief priests and scribes resented His wonders and the children crying, “Hosanna to David’s Son!” 📖
## Matthew 21:16
They asked whether He heard them. “Yes. Did you not read, ‘From children’s and infants’ mouths You ordained praise’?” 📖
## Matthew 21:17
He left for Bethany and stayed the night. 📖
## Matthew 21:18
He returned to the city hungry in the morning. 📖
## Matthew 21:19
A roadside fig tree had only leaves. He said, “Never bear fruit again!” It withered immediately. 📖
## Matthew 21:20
The disciples marveled and asked how it withered so quickly. ❓
## Matthew 21:21
He answered, “With faith and no doubt, you can do what was done to the fig tree. Tell this mountain to be lifted and thrown into the sea, and it will happen. 📖
## Matthew 21:22
Believing, you will receive whatever you ask in prayer.” 📖
## Matthew 21:23
Teaching in the temple courts, He was asked by chief priests and elders what authority He had and who gave it. ❓
## Matthew 21:24
He replied, “Answer one question, and I will tell you My authority. 📖
## Matthew 21:25
Was John’s baptism from heaven or people?” They discussed: “If heaven, He will ask why we disbelieved John. 📖
## Matthew 21:26
If people, we fear the crowds because all consider John a prophet.” 📖
## Matthew 21:27
They said, “We do not know.” He answered, “Then I will not tell you My authority. 📖
## Matthew 21:28
What do you think? A man told his first of two sons to work in the vineyard that day. 📖
## Matthew 21:29
He refused but changed his mind and went. 📖
## Matthew 21:30
The second agreed to the same request but never went. 📖
## Matthew 21:31
Which obeyed?” They answered, “The first.” Jesus said, “Tax collectors and prostitutes enter God’s kingdom before you. 📖
## Matthew 21:32
John came in righteousness, and they believed him. You refused, even after seeing them believe. You did not repent and believe. 📖
## Matthew 21:33
A landowner planted a vineyard, surrounded it with a wall, dug a winepress, and built a tower. He rented it to tenants and traveled away. 📖
## Matthew 21:34
Near harvest, he sent servants to collect his share. 📖
## Matthew 21:35
The tenants beat one, killed another, and stoned a third. 📖
## Matthew 21:36
He sent a larger group, and they treated them the same. 📖
## Matthew 21:37
He finally sent his son, expecting respect. 📖
## Matthew 21:38
They said, ‘Kill the heir and take his inheritance.’ 📖
## Matthew 21:39
They seized him, threw him outside the vineyard, and killed him. 📖
## Matthew 21:40
What will the returning owner do?” ❓
## Matthew 21:41
They answered, “Destroy those wretches and rent to tenants paying his harvest share.” 📖
## Matthew 21:42
He said, “Did you not read, ‘The stone the builders rejected became the cornerstone. The Lord did this, marvelous in our eyes’? 📖
## Matthew 21:43
God’s kingdom will be taken from you and given to a people producing its fruit. 📖
## Matthew 21:44
Falling on this stone breaks a person to pieces. Whoever it falls on is crushed.” 📖
## Matthew 21:45
The chief priests and Pharisees realized His parables referred to them. 📖
## Matthew 21:46
They wanted to arrest Him but feared the crowds, who considered Him a prophet. 📖
