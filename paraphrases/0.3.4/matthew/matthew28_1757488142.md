## Matthew 28:1
At dawn after the Sabbath, on the week’s first day, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary visited the tomb. 📖
## Matthew 28:2
A great earthquake occurred because an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, rolled the stone away, and sat on it. ✨
## Matthew 28:3
He appeared like lightning in clothes white as snow. 📖
## Matthew 28:4
The guards trembled and became like dead men from fear. 📖
## Matthew 28:5
He told the women, “Do not fear. I know you seek the crucified Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 28:6
He rose as He said. He is not here. See where He lay. 📖
## Matthew 28:7
Quickly tell His disciples He rose and precedes you to Galilee. You will see Him there. I have told you.” 📖
## Matthew 28:8
They ran to tell the disciples, fearful and greatly joyful. 📖
## Matthew 28:9
Jesus met and greeted them. They grasped His feet and worshiped Him. 🙏
## Matthew 28:10
He said, “Do not fear. Tell My brothers to go to Galilee, where they will see Me.” 📖
## Matthew 28:11
Some guards reported everything to the chief priests while the women traveled. 📖
## Matthew 28:12
The priests and elders planned and paid the soldiers a large sum, 📖
## Matthew 28:13
telling them to claim His disciples stole Him at night while they slept. 📖
## Matthew 28:14
“We will satisfy the governor and protect you if he hears this.” 📖
## Matthew 28:15
The guards accepted the money and complied. This account circulates among the Jews to this day. 📖
## Matthew 28:16
The Eleven went to Galilee’s mountain designated by Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 28:17
They worshiped Him on seeing Him, but some doubted. 📖
## Matthew 28:18
He said, “All authority in heaven and earth has been given to Me. 📖
## Matthew 28:19
Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, 📖
## Matthew 28:20
and teaching them to obey all My commands. I am always with you, even to the end of the age.” ❤️
