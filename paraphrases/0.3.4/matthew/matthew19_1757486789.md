## Matthew 19:1
Jesus finished and left Galilee for Judea beyond the Jordan. 📖
## Matthew 19:2
Large crowds followed, and He healed them. ❤️
## Matthew 19:3
Pharisees tested Him: “May a man lawfully divorce his wife for any reason?” ❓
## Matthew 19:4
He answered, “Have you not read that the Creator made them male and female from the beginning, 📖
## Matthew 19:5
and said a man leaves his parents and joins his wife, and the two become one flesh? 📖
## Matthew 19:6
They are one flesh, no longer two. Do not separate what God joined.” 📖
## Matthew 19:7
They asked why Moses ordered a divorce certificate and dismissal. ❓
## Matthew 19:8
He answered, “Moses permitted divorce because your hearts were hard. It was not this way originally. 📖
## Matthew 19:9
Whoever divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, and marries another woman commits adultery.” 📖
## Matthew 19:10
The disciples said, “If so, it is better not to marry.” 📖
## Matthew 19:11
He replied, “Only those given this word can accept it. 📖
## Matthew 19:12
Some eunuchs were born that way, others made so by men. Others live like eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven. Whoever can accept it should.” 📖
## Matthew 19:13
People brought children for Jesus to lay hands and pray. His disciples rebuked those bringing them. 📖
## Matthew 19:14
He said, “Let children come to Me. Do not hinder them. The kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these.” ❤️
## Matthew 19:15
He laid hands on them and left. 📖
## Matthew 19:16
A man asked, “Teacher, what good thing must I do to obtain eternal life?” ❓
## Matthew 19:17
Jesus answered, “Why ask Me about good? Only One is good. Keep the commands to enter life.” 📖
## Matthew 19:18
“Which?” He answered, “Do not murder, commit adultery, steal, or bear false witness. 📖
## Matthew 19:19
Honor your parents and love your neighbor as yourself.” 📖
## Matthew 19:20
The young man said, “I kept these. What do I lack?” ❓
## Matthew 19:21
Jesus said, “If you want to be perfect, sell your possessions and give to the poor. You will have heavenly treasure. Then follow Me.” 📖
## Matthew 19:22
He left sadly because he was very wealthy. 📖
## Matthew 19:23
Jesus said, “Truly, entering the kingdom of heaven is hard for the rich. 📖
## Matthew 19:24
A camel passes through a needle’s eye more easily than a rich man enters God’s kingdom.” 📖
## Matthew 19:25
The astonished disciples asked who could be saved. ❓
## Matthew 19:26
He answered, “This is impossible with man. With God everything is possible.” 📖
## Matthew 19:27
Peter said, “We left everything to follow You. What will we receive?” ❓
## Matthew 19:28
Jesus answered, “At the renewal of all things, the Son of Man will sit on His glorious throne. You who followed Me will sit on twelve thrones judging Israel’s twelve tribes. 📖
## Matthew 19:29
Anyone leaving houses, brothers, sisters, father, mother, wife, children, or fields for My name will receive a hundredfold. He will inherit eternal life. 📖
## Matthew 19:30
Many who are first will be last, and the last will be first.” 📖
