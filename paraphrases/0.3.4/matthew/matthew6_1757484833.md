## Matthew 6:1
“Do not perform righteous acts before people to be seen. Otherwise your Father in heaven will give no reward. 📖
## Matthew 6:2
When giving to the needy, do not sound a trumpet before you as hypocrites do in synagogues and streets to seek honor. They already received their full reward. 📖
## Matthew 6:3
When giving, do not let your left hand know what your right does. 📖
## Matthew 6:4
Give secretly. Your Father sees what is secret and will reward you. 📖
## Matthew 6:5
Do not pray like hypocrites who love praying while standing in synagogues and on street corners to be seen. They already received their full reward. 📖
## Matthew 6:6
Pray in your inner room with the door shut to your unseen Father. He sees what is secret and will reward you. 🙏
## Matthew 6:7
Do not babble like pagans who think many words make their prayers heard. 📖
## Matthew 6:8
Do not imitate them. Your Father knows your needs before you ask. 📖
## Matthew 6:9
Pray this way: ‘Our Father in heaven, may Your name be honored as holy. 🙏
## Matthew 6:10
May Your kingdom come and Your will be done on earth as in heaven. 🙏
## Matthew 6:11
Give us our daily bread today. 🙏
## Matthew 6:12
Forgive our debts as we have forgiven our debtors. 🙏
## Matthew 6:13
Do not lead us into temptation. Rescue us from the evil one.’ 🙏
## Matthew 6:14
If you forgive people’s trespasses, your heavenly Father will forgive yours. 📖
## Matthew 6:15
If you do not forgive theirs, your Father will not forgive yours. 📖
## Matthew 6:16
When fasting, do not look gloomy like hypocrites who disfigure their faces to show their fast. They already received their full reward. 📖
## Matthew 6:17
Anoint your head and wash your face when fasting, 📖
## Matthew 6:18
so people do not see your fasting, only your unseen Father. He sees what is secret and will reward you. 📖
## Matthew 6:19
Do not store earthly treasures, where moths and rust destroy and thieves break in and steal. 📖
## Matthew 6:20
Store heavenly treasures, where moths and rust do not destroy and thieves do not break in or steal. 📖
## Matthew 6:21
Your heart will be where your treasure is. ❤️
## Matthew 6:22
Your eye is your body’s lamp. If your eyes are good, your whole body will be full of light. 📖
## Matthew 6:23
If your eyes are bad, your whole body will be full of darkness. If your inner light is darkness, how great that darkness is! 📖
## Matthew 6:24
No one can serve two masters. He will hate one and love the other, or be devoted to one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and money. 📖
## Matthew 6:25
Therefore do not worry about food or drink for your life or clothes for your body. Is life not more than food, and your body more than clothes? ❓
## Matthew 6:26
Birds do not sow, reap, or store in barns, yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable? ❓
## Matthew 6:27
Can worrying add one hour to your life? ❓
## Matthew 6:28
Why worry about clothes? Field lilies grow without working or spinning thread. 🌸
## Matthew 6:29
Even Solomon in all his glory was not dressed like one of them. 📖
## Matthew 6:30
God clothes grass here today and thrown into the furnace tomorrow. Will He not clothe you much more, people of little faith? ❓
## Matthew 6:31
Do not worry about what to eat, drink, or wear. 📖
## Matthew 6:32
Gentiles pursue these things. Your heavenly Father knows you need them. 📖
## Matthew 6:33
Seek God’s kingdom and His righteousness first. You will receive all these things too. 📖
## Matthew 6:34
Do not worry about tomorrow. Tomorrow will worry about itself. Today has enough trouble. 📖
