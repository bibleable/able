## Matthew 23:1
Jesus told the crowds and disciples, 📖
## Matthew 23:2
“The scribes and Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat. 📖
## Matthew 23:3
Obey their instructions, but not their example. They do not practice their preaching. 📖
## Matthew 23:4
They burden people with heavy loads but will not lift a finger to move them. 📖
## Matthew 23:5
They do everything to be seen. They widen phylacteries and lengthen tassels. 📖
## Matthew 23:6
They love places of honor at banquets and chief synagogue seats, 📖
## Matthew 23:7
marketplace greetings, and being called ‘Rabbi.’ 📖
## Matthew 23:8
Do not be called ‘Rabbi.’ You have one Teacher and are all brothers. 📖
## Matthew 23:9
Call no earthly person your father. Your one Father is in heaven. 📖
## Matthew 23:10
Do not be called instructors. Your one Instructor is the Christ. 📖
## Matthew 23:11
Your greatest must be your servant. 📖
## Matthew 23:12
Whoever exalts himself will be humbled. Whoever humbles himself will be exalted. 📖
## Matthew 23:13
Woe to you, hypocritical scribes and Pharisees! You shut the kingdom of heaven in people’s faces, neither entering nor allowing others to enter. 📖
## Matthew 23:14

## Matthew 23:15
Woe to you, hypocritical scribes and Pharisees! You cross land and sea for one convert. You make him twice as much a son of hell as yourselves. 📖
## Matthew 23:16
Woe, blind guides! You say temple oaths mean nothing, but temple gold oaths bind the speaker. 📖
## Matthew 23:17
Blind fools! Is the gold greater or the temple making it sacred? ❓
## Matthew 23:18
You say altar oaths mean nothing, but oaths by its gift bind the speaker. 📖
## Matthew 23:19
Blind men! Is the gift greater or the altar making it sacred? ❓
## Matthew 23:20
An altar oath includes everything on it. 📖
## Matthew 23:21
Whoever swears by the temple swears by it and the One dwelling there. 📖
## Matthew 23:22
Whoever swears by heaven swears by God’s throne and the One sitting on it. 📖
## Matthew 23:23
Woe to you, hypocritical scribes and Pharisees! You tithe mint, dill, and cumin but neglect justice, mercy, and faithfulness, the Law’s weightier matters. Practice these without neglecting tithes. 📖
## Matthew 23:24
Blind guides, you strain gnats but swallow camels! 📖
## Matthew 23:25
Woe to you, hypocritical scribes and Pharisees! You clean cups and dishes outside. Inside they are full of greed and self-indulgence. 📖
## Matthew 23:26
Blind Pharisee, clean inside first so the outside becomes clean too. 📖
## Matthew 23:27
Woe to you, hypocritical scribes and Pharisees! You resemble beautiful whitewashed tombs full of dead men’s bones and every impurity. 📖
## Matthew 23:28
You look righteous outside but are full of hypocrisy and wickedness inside. 📖
## Matthew 23:29
Woe to you, hypocritical scribes and Pharisees! You build prophets’ tombs and decorate righteous people’s monuments. 📖
## Matthew 23:30
You claim you would not have joined your fathers in shedding prophets’ blood. 📖
## Matthew 23:31
You testify you are sons of prophets’ murderers. 📖
## Matthew 23:32
Fill your fathers’ measure of sin! 📖
## Matthew 23:33
Snakes, brood of vipers! How will you escape hell’s sentence? ❓
## Matthew 23:34
Therefore I send you prophets, wise men, and teachers. You will kill and crucify some, flog others in synagogues, and persecute them between towns. 📖
## Matthew 23:35
All righteous blood shed on earth will come upon you, from righteous Abel to Zechariah son of Berechiah. You murdered him between temple and altar. 📖
## Matthew 23:36
Truly, all this will come on this generation. 📖
## Matthew 23:37
Jerusalem, you kill prophets and stone those sent to you. I longed often to gather your children as a hen gathers chicks under her wings. You refused! 💔
## Matthew 23:38
Your house is left desolate. 📖
## Matthew 23:39
You will not see Me again until you say, ‘Blessed is He coming in the Lord’s name.’” 📖
