## Matthew 14:1
Herod the tetrarch heard reports about Jesus, 📖
## Matthew 14:2
and told his servants, “John the Baptist rose from death. That explains his miraculous powers.” 📖
## Matthew 14:3
Herod had bound and imprisoned John because of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife. 📖
## Matthew 14:4
John had told him, “Having her is unlawful.” 📖
## Matthew 14:5
Herod wanted John dead but feared the people, who considered him a prophet. 📖
## Matthew 14:6
Herodias’s daughter danced at Herod’s birthday and pleased him. 📖
## Matthew 14:7
He swore to give her whatever she asked. 📖
## Matthew 14:8
Her mother prompted her: “Give me John the Baptist’s head here on a platter.” 📖
## Matthew 14:9
Herod grieved but granted it because of his oaths and guests. 📖
## Matthew 14:10
He sent orders to behead John in prison. 📖
## Matthew 14:11
His head was brought on a platter to the girl, who took it to her mother. 📖
## Matthew 14:12
John’s disciples buried his body and told Jesus. 💔
## Matthew 14:13
Jesus heard and withdrew privately by boat to a solitary place. Crowds learned this and followed on foot from the towns. 📖
## Matthew 14:14
He stepped ashore, felt compassion for the large crowd, and healed their sick. ❤️
## Matthew 14:15
At evening, His disciples said, “This place is deserted and it is late. Send them to buy food in villages.” 📖
## Matthew 14:16
He answered, “They need not leave. You give them food.” 📖
## Matthew 14:17
They said, “We have only five loaves and two fish.” 📖
## Matthew 14:18
He said, “Bring them to Me.” 📖
## Matthew 14:19
He told the crowd to sit on grass, took the five loaves and two fish, looked to heaven, and blessed them. He broke the loaves, gave them to His disciples, and they served the people. 📖
## Matthew 14:20
Everyone ate and was satisfied. The disciples collected twelve baskets of leftover pieces. 📖
## Matthew 14:21
About five thousand men ate, besides women and children. 👥
## Matthew 14:22
Jesus immediately sent the disciples ahead by boat to the other side while dismissing the crowd. 📖
## Matthew 14:23
He went up the mountain alone to pray after dismissing them. Evening came, and He remained alone. 🙏
## Matthew 14:24
Far from land, waves battered the boat because the wind opposed it. 🌊
## Matthew 14:25
In the fourth night watch, Jesus walked across the sea to them. 📖
## Matthew 14:26
They saw Him and cried in terror, “A ghost!” 📖
## Matthew 14:27
He immediately said, “Take courage. It is I. Do not fear.” 📖
## Matthew 14:28
Peter said, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come on the water.” 📖
## Matthew 14:29
He said, “Come.” Peter left the boat and walked on the water toward Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 14:30
The strong wind frightened him. Beginning to sink, he cried, “Lord, save me!” 🙏
## Matthew 14:31
Jesus immediately reached out His hand and caught him. “Why doubt, you of little faith?” 📖
## Matthew 14:32
They entered the boat, and the wind ceased. 📖
## Matthew 14:33
Those aboard worshiped Him: “Truly, You are God’s Son!” 🙏
## Matthew 14:34
They crossed and landed at Gennesaret. 📖
## Matthew 14:35
The local men recognized Jesus and notified the surrounding region. People brought all the sick, 📖
## Matthew 14:36
begging to touch His cloak’s fringe. All who touched Him were healed. ❤️
