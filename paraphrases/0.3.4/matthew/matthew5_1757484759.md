## Matthew 5:1
Jesus saw the crowds, went up the mountain, and sat. His disciples came to Him. 📖
## Matthew 5:2
He began teaching them. 📖
## Matthew 5:3
“Blessed are the poor in spirit because the kingdom of heaven is theirs. 📖
## Matthew 5:4
Blessed are mourners because they will be comforted. ❤️
## Matthew 5:5
Blessed are the meek because they will inherit the earth. 📖
## Matthew 5:6
Blessed are those hungering and thirsting for righteousness because they will be filled. 📖
## Matthew 5:7
Blessed are the merciful because they will receive mercy. ❤️
## Matthew 5:8
Blessed are the pure in heart because they will see God. 📖
## Matthew 5:9
Blessed are peacemakers because they will be called God’s sons. ❤️
## Matthew 5:10
Blessed are those persecuted for righteousness because the kingdom of heaven is theirs. 📖
## Matthew 5:11
Blessed are you when people insult and persecute you and falsely accuse you of every kind of evil because of Me. 📖
## Matthew 5:12
Rejoice because your heavenly reward is great. They persecuted the prophets before you the same way. 📖
## Matthew 5:13
You are earth’s salt. If salt loses flavor, how can it become salty again? It is useful only for throwing out and trampling. 📖
## Matthew 5:14
You are the world’s light. A hilltop city cannot be hidden. ✨
## Matthew 5:15
People put a lit lamp on a stand, not under a basket. It lights everyone in the house. ✨
## Matthew 5:16
Let your light shine before people so they see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven. ✨
## Matthew 5:17
Do not think I came to abolish the Law or Prophets. I came to fulfill them. 📖
## Matthew 5:18
Truly, until heaven and earth pass away, no jot or pen stroke will disappear from the Law until everything is accomplished. 📖
## Matthew 5:19
Whoever breaks one of these least commands and teaches others likewise will be called least in the kingdom of heaven. Whoever practices and teaches them will be called great there. 📖
## Matthew 5:20
Unless your righteousness exceeds the scribes’ and Pharisees’, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven. 📖
## Matthew 5:21
You heard the ancients were told, ‘Do not murder. Murderers are subject to judgment.’ 📖
## Matthew 5:22
I say anyone angry with his brother faces judgment. Anyone calling his brother ‘Raca’ faces the Sanhedrin. Anyone saying ‘You fool!’ faces hell’s fire. 📖
## Matthew 5:23
If you bring an altar gift and remember your brother has something against you, 📖
## Matthew 5:24
leave it before the altar. First reconcile with your brother, then return and offer it. 📖
## Matthew 5:25
Settle quickly with your adversary on the way to court. Otherwise he may hand you to the judge, who may hand you to the officer. You may be imprisoned. 📖
## Matthew 5:26
You will not leave until you pay the last penny. 📖
## Matthew 5:27
You heard, ‘Do not commit adultery.’ 📖
## Matthew 5:28
I say anyone looking at a woman to lust after her already committed adultery with her in his heart. 📖
## Matthew 5:29
If your right eye causes sin, gouge it out and discard it. Losing one body part is better than your whole body being thrown into hell. 📖
## Matthew 5:30
If your right hand causes sin, cut it off and discard it. Losing one body part is better than your whole body going into hell. 📖
## Matthew 5:31
It was said, ‘Give your wife a divorce certificate if you divorce her.’ 📖
## Matthew 5:32
I say whoever divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, brings adultery upon her. Marrying a divorced woman is adultery. 📖
## Matthew 5:33
The ancients were told, ‘Keep your oaths and fulfill your vows to the Lord.’ 📖
## Matthew 5:34
I say do not swear at all. Do not swear by heaven because it is God’s throne, 📖
## Matthew 5:35
or earth because it is His footstool, or Jerusalem because it is the great King’s city. 📖
## Matthew 5:36
Do not swear by your head. You cannot make one hair white or black. 📖
## Matthew 5:37
Let your ‘Yes’ be ‘Yes’ and ‘No’ be ‘No.’ Anything more comes from the evil one. 📖
## Matthew 5:38
You heard, ‘Eye for eye and tooth for tooth.’ 📖
## Matthew 5:39
I say do not resist an evil person. Turn your other cheek to someone slapping your right cheek. 📖
## Matthew 5:40
If someone sues for your tunic, give your cloak too. 📖
## Matthew 5:41
If someone forces you one mile, go two miles with him. 📖
## Matthew 5:42
Give to whoever asks. Do not turn away someone wanting to borrow. 📖
## Matthew 5:43
You heard, ‘Love your neighbor and hate your enemy.’ 📖
## Matthew 5:44
I say love your enemies and pray for your persecutors, ❤️
## Matthew 5:45
so you may be sons of your Father in heaven. He makes His sun rise on evil and good alike. He sends rain on righteous and unrighteous alike. 📖
## Matthew 5:46
What reward comes from loving those who love you? Even tax collectors do this. ❓
## Matthew 5:47
What extra do you do by greeting only your brothers? Even Gentiles do this. ❓
## Matthew 5:48
Be perfect as your heavenly Father is perfect. 📖
