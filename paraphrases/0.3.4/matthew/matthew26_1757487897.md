## Matthew 26:1
Jesus finished and told His disciples, 📖
## Matthew 26:2
“Passover is two days away. The Son of Man will be handed over for crucifixion.” 📖
## Matthew 26:3
Chief priests and elders gathered in high priest Caiaphas’s courtyard, 📖
## Matthew 26:4
plotting Jesus’ secret arrest and death. 📖
## Matthew 26:5
They feared a riot during the feast and decided against that time. 📖
## Matthew 26:6
Jesus was at Simon the Leper’s house in Bethany. 📖
## Matthew 26:7
A woman poured expensive perfume from an alabaster jar on His head as He reclined. 📖
## Matthew 26:8
The disciples were indignant and asked, “Why this waste? 📖
## Matthew 26:9
It could have been sold for much and the money given to the poor.” 📖
## Matthew 26:10
He said, “Why bother her? She did something beautiful for Me. 📖
## Matthew 26:11
The poor are always with you, but I will not be. 📖
## Matthew 26:12
She prepared My body for burial with this perfume. 📖
## Matthew 26:13
Wherever this gospel is preached worldwide, her deed will also be told in her memory.” 📖
## Matthew 26:14
Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, went to the chief priests, 📖
## Matthew 26:15
asking payment for handing Jesus over. They set out thirty silver pieces. 📖
## Matthew 26:16
He began seeking a betrayal opportunity. 📖
## Matthew 26:17
On Unleavened Bread’s first day, the disciples asked where to prepare His Passover meal. 📖
## Matthew 26:18
He sent them to a certain man in the city. “Tell him the Teacher says, ‘My time is near. I will keep Passover with My disciples at your house.’” 📖
## Matthew 26:19
They obeyed and prepared it. 📖
## Matthew 26:20
At evening He reclined with the Twelve. 📖
## Matthew 26:21
While eating He said, “Truly, one of you will betray Me.” 📖
## Matthew 26:22
Deeply grieved, each asked, “Surely not I, Lord?” 📖
## Matthew 26:23
“The one who dipped his hand in the bowl with Me will betray Me. 📖
## Matthew 26:24
The Son of Man goes as written. Woe to His betrayer! Never being born would have been better for him.” 📖
## Matthew 26:25
Judas asked, “Surely not I, Rabbi?” Jesus answered, “You said it yourself.” 📖
## Matthew 26:26
Jesus blessed and broke bread, giving it to His disciples. “Take and eat. This is My body.” 📖
## Matthew 26:27
He took the cup, gave thanks, and gave it to them. “Drink, all of you. 📖
## Matthew 26:28
This is My covenant blood, poured out for many for forgiveness of sins. 📖
## Matthew 26:29
I will not drink this vine’s fruit until drinking it anew with you in My Father’s kingdom.” 📖
## Matthew 26:30
They sang a hymn and went to the Mount of Olives. 📖
## Matthew 26:31
“Tonight all will fall away because of Me. Scripture says, ‘I will strike the Shepherd, scattering the flock.’ 📖
## Matthew 26:32
After rising, I will precede you to Galilee.” 📖
## Matthew 26:33
Peter said, “Others may fall away because of You. I never will.” 📖
## Matthew 26:34
Jesus said, “Tonight, before the rooster crows, you will deny Me three times.” 📖
## Matthew 26:35
Peter said, “Even if I must die with You, I will never deny You.” All the disciples said the same. 📖
## Matthew 26:36
At Gethsemane He told the disciples to sit while He prayed. 📖
## Matthew 26:37
He took Peter and Zebedee’s two sons and grew deeply distressed and sorrowful. 📖
## Matthew 26:38
“My soul is sorrowful to death. Stay and watch with Me.” 💔
## Matthew 26:39
He went farther, fell facedown, and prayed, “My Father, if possible, let this cup pass from Me. Your will, not Mine, be done.” 🙏
## Matthew 26:40
He found them sleeping. He asked Peter, “Could you not watch with Me one hour? 📖
## Matthew 26:41
Watch and pray so you do not enter temptation. The spirit is willing, but the body weak.” 🙏
## Matthew 26:42
He prayed a second time: “My Father, if this cup cannot pass without My drinking it, Your will be done.” 🙏
## Matthew 26:43
He returned and found them sleeping again because their eyes were heavy. 📖
## Matthew 26:44
He left and prayed the same a third time. 🙏
## Matthew 26:45
He returned: “Still sleeping and resting? The hour nears. The Son of Man is betrayed into sinners’ hands. 📖
## Matthew 26:46
Rise, let us go. My betrayer approaches.” 📖
## Matthew 26:47
While Jesus spoke, Judas, one of the Twelve, arrived with a large crowd armed with swords and clubs, sent by the chief priests and elders. 📖
## Matthew 26:48
He arranged a kiss as the arrest signal. 📖
## Matthew 26:49
He greeted Jesus as Rabbi and kissed Him. 📖
## Matthew 26:50
Jesus said, “Friend, do what you came for.” They seized and arrested Him. 📖
## Matthew 26:51
A companion drew his sword and cut off the high priest’s servant’s ear. 📖
## Matthew 26:52
Jesus said, “Put it away. All drawing swords will die by swords. 📖
## Matthew 26:53
My Father could immediately give Me more than twelve legions of angels if I asked. 📖
## Matthew 26:54
But how would Scripture requiring this be fulfilled?” 📖
## Matthew 26:55
He asked, “Why arrest Me like an outlaw with swords and clubs? I taught daily in the temple courts without arrest. 📖
## Matthew 26:56
This fulfills the prophets’ writings.” All the disciples deserted Him and fled. 📖
## Matthew 26:57
They took Him to Caiaphas’s house, where scribes and elders gathered. 📖
## Matthew 26:58
Peter followed at a distance and sat with guards in the high priest’s courtyard to see the outcome. 📖
## Matthew 26:59
The chief priests and whole Sanhedrin sought false testimony to execute Him. 📖
## Matthew 26:60
They found no false testimony, though many false witnesses came. Finally two came forward, 📖
## Matthew 26:61
“He claimed He could destroy God’s temple and rebuild it in three days.” 📖
## Matthew 26:62
The high priest demanded an answer to the accusations. 📖
## Matthew 26:63
Jesus was silent. The high priest charged Him under oath by the living God to say whether He was the Christ, God’s Son. 📖
## Matthew 26:64
He answered, “You said it yourself. From now on you all will see the Son of Man sitting at Power’s right hand and coming on heaven’s clouds.” 📖
## Matthew 26:65
The high priest tore his clothes. “He blasphemed! We need no more witnesses. You heard the blasphemy. 📖
## Matthew 26:66
What is your verdict?” They said, “He deserves death.” 📖
## Matthew 26:67
They spat in His face, struck, and slapped Him, 📖
## Matthew 26:68
asking the Christ to prophesy who hit Him. 📖
## Matthew 26:69
A servant girl told Peter in the courtyard he was with Jesus the Galilean. 📖
## Matthew 26:70
He publicly denied knowing what she meant. 📖
## Matthew 26:71
At the gateway, another servant girl identified him with Jesus of Nazareth. 📖
## Matthew 26:72
He swore he did not know the man. 📖
## Matthew 26:73
Others said his accent proved he was one of them. 📖
## Matthew 26:74
He cursed and swore he did not know the man. A rooster immediately crowed. 📖
## Matthew 26:75
He remembered Jesus’ prediction of three denials before the rooster crowed. He went outside and wept bitterly. 💔
