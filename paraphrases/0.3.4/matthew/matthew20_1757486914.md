## Matthew 20:1
“The kingdom of heaven resembles a landowner hiring vineyard workers early in the morning. 📖
## Matthew 20:2
He agreed on a denarius for the day and sent them to work. 📖
## Matthew 20:3
Around the third hour, he saw others idle in the marketplace. 📖
## Matthew 20:4
He promised them fair pay for vineyard work. 📖
## Matthew 20:5
They went. He did the same about the sixth and ninth hours. 📖
## Matthew 20:6
About the eleventh hour he asked others why they stood idle all day. 📖
## Matthew 20:7
They said no one hired them. He sent them to his vineyard. 📖
## Matthew 20:8
At evening, he told his foreman to pay the last hired first, ending with the first hired. 📖
## Matthew 20:9
Those hired about the eleventh hour each received a denarius. 📖
## Matthew 20:10
The original workers expected more but received a denarius each. 📖
## Matthew 20:11
They complained against the owner, 📖
## Matthew 20:12
‘They worked one hour. You made them equal to us, who endured the day’s burden and scorching heat!’ 📖
## Matthew 20:13
He replied, ‘Friend, I am fair. You agreed on a denarius. 📖
## Matthew 20:14
Take your pay and go. I want to give this last man the same as you. 📖
## Matthew 20:15
Can I not do what I want with my own property? Are you envious because I am generous?’ 📖
## Matthew 20:16
The last will be first, and first last.” 📖
## Matthew 20:17
Jesus took the Twelve aside while going to Jerusalem. 📖
## Matthew 20:18
“The Son of Man will be handed to the chief priests and scribes in Jerusalem. They will condemn Him to death, 📖
## Matthew 20:19
and hand Him to Gentiles for mocking, flogging, and crucifixion. On day three He will be raised to life.” 📖
## Matthew 20:20
The mother of Zebedee’s sons came with them and knelt to make a request. 📖
## Matthew 20:21
He asked what she wanted. “Let my two sons sit at Your right and left in Your kingdom.” 📖
## Matthew 20:22
He said, “You do not know what you ask. Can you drink the cup I am going to drink?” They answered, “We can.” 📖
## Matthew 20:23
“You will drink My cup. Seats at My right or left are not Mine to grant. My Father prepared them for their recipients.” 📖
## Matthew 20:24
The other ten were indignant with the brothers. 📖
## Matthew 20:25
Jesus said, “Gentile rulers lord it over them, and their superiors exercise authority over them. 📖
## Matthew 20:26
You must be different. Whoever wants greatness must be your servant. 📖
## Matthew 20:27
Whoever wants first place must be your slave. 📖
## Matthew 20:28
The Son of Man came to serve, not be served, and to give His life as a ransom for many.” 📖
## Matthew 20:29
Large crowds followed as they left Jericho. 👥
## Matthew 20:30
Two roadside blind men heard Jesus pass and cried, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy!” 🙏
## Matthew 20:31
The crowd told them to be silent. They cried louder, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy!” 🙏
## Matthew 20:32
He stopped, called them, and asked what they wanted Him to do. 📖
## Matthew 20:33
“Lord, open our eyes.” 🙏
## Matthew 20:34
He compassionately touched their eyes. They immediately saw and followed Him. ❤️
