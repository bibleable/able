## Matthew 10:1
Jesus gathered His twelve disciples and gave authority to expel unclean spirits and heal every disease and sickness. 📖
## Matthew 10:2
The twelve apostles were first Simon called Peter and his brother Andrew, James son of Zebedee and his brother John, 📖
## Matthew 10:3
Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew the tax collector, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, 📖
## Matthew 10:4
Simon the Zealot, and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 10:5
Jesus sent these twelve, saying, “Do not take the Gentiles’ road or enter Samaritan towns. 📖
## Matthew 10:6
Go to Israel’s lost sheep instead. 📖
## Matthew 10:7
Preach, ‘The kingdom of heaven is near.’ 📖
## Matthew 10:8
Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, and expel demons. Give freely as you received freely. 📖
## Matthew 10:9
Take no gold, silver, or copper in your belts. 📖
## Matthew 10:10
Take no bag, second tunic, sandals, or staff. The worker deserves provisions. 📖
## Matthew 10:11
Find a worthy person in each town or village and stay at his house until leaving. 📖
## Matthew 10:12
Greet the occupants when entering. 📖
## Matthew 10:13
Let your peace rest on a worthy home. If unworthy, let your peace return. 📖
## Matthew 10:14
If people will not welcome you or heed your words, shake dust from your feet when leaving that home or town. 📖
## Matthew 10:15
Truly, Sodom and Gomorrah will find judgment day more bearable than that town. 📖
## Matthew 10:16
I send you like sheep among wolves. Be shrewd as snakes and innocent as doves. 📖
## Matthew 10:17
Beware of people who will hand you to councils and flog you in synagogues. 📖
## Matthew 10:18
Because of Me, you will face governors and kings as witnesses to them and the Gentiles. 📖
## Matthew 10:19
Do not worry about your response when handed over. You will receive what to say that hour. 📖
## Matthew 10:20
Your Father’s Spirit will speak through you, not you yourselves. 📖
## Matthew 10:21
Brothers will betray brothers to death, and fathers their children. Children will rise against parents and have them executed. 📖
## Matthew 10:22
Everyone will hate you because of My name. Whoever endures to the end will be saved. 📖
## Matthew 10:23
Flee to another town when persecuted. Truly, you will not reach all Israel’s towns before the Son of Man comes. 📖
## Matthew 10:24
A disciple is not above his teacher, nor a servant above his master. 📖
## Matthew 10:25
It is enough for a disciple to be like his teacher and a servant like his master. If they call the household’s head Beelzebul, how much more its members! 📖
## Matthew 10:26
Do not fear them. All concealed and hidden things will become known. 📖
## Matthew 10:27
Speak in daylight what I tell you in darkness. Proclaim from rooftops what is whispered in your ear. 📖
## Matthew 10:28
Do not fear those killing the body but unable to kill the soul. Fear the One who can destroy soul and body in hell. 📖
## Matthew 10:29
Two sparrows cost a penny. Not one falls to the ground without your Father’s will. 📖
## Matthew 10:30
Even your head’s hairs are all numbered. 📖
## Matthew 10:31
Do not fear. You are worth more than many sparrows. ❤️
## Matthew 10:32
I will acknowledge before My heavenly Father anyone acknowledging Me before people. 📖
## Matthew 10:33
I will deny before My heavenly Father anyone denying Me before people. 📖
## Matthew 10:34
Do not assume I came to bring earth peace. I came to bring a sword. 📖
## Matthew 10:35
I came to turn a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law. 📖
## Matthew 10:36
A man’s own household will be his enemies. 📖
## Matthew 10:37
Anyone loving father or mother more than Me is unworthy of Me. Anyone loving son or daughter more than Me is unworthy of Me. 📖
## Matthew 10:38
Anyone who does not take up his cross and follow Me is unworthy of Me. 📖
## Matthew 10:39
Whoever finds his life will lose it. Whoever loses his life for My sake will find it. 📖
## Matthew 10:40
Whoever receives you receives Me. Whoever receives Me receives the One who sent Me. 📖
## Matthew 10:41
Whoever receives a prophet because he is a prophet receives a prophet’s reward. Whoever receives a righteous man because he is righteous receives a righteous man’s reward. 📖
## Matthew 10:42
Truly, anyone giving one of these little ones even a cup of cold water because that one is My disciple will never lose his reward.” 📖
