## Matthew 3:1
John the Baptist came preaching in Judea’s wilderness. 📖
## Matthew 3:2
“Repent because the kingdom of heaven is near.” 📖
## Matthew 3:3
Isaiah spoke of him: “A voice calls in the wilderness, ‘Prepare the Lord’s way. Make His paths straight.’” 📖
## Matthew 3:4
John wore camel’s hair clothing and a leather belt. He ate locusts and wild honey. 📖
## Matthew 3:5
People came from Jerusalem, all Judea, and the whole Jordan region. 👥
## Matthew 3:6
They confessed their sins, and he baptized them in the Jordan River. 📖
## Matthew 3:7
Many Pharisees and Sadducees came to his baptism place. He asked, “Brood of vipers, who warned you to flee the coming wrath? ❓
## Matthew 3:8
Produce fruit consistent with repentance. 🌱
## Matthew 3:9
Do not assume you can say, ‘Abraham is our father.’ God can raise children for Abraham from these stones. 📖
## Matthew 3:10
The axe is ready at the trees’ root. Every tree lacking good fruit will be cut down and thrown into fire. 📖
## Matthew 3:11
I baptize with water for repentance. One more powerful comes after me. I am unworthy to carry His sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire. 📖
## Matthew 3:12
He holds His winnowing fork to clear His threshing floor and gather wheat into His barn. He will burn chaff with fire that cannot be extinguished.” 📖
## Matthew 3:13
Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan for John to baptize Him. 📖
## Matthew 3:14
John tried to stop Him: “I need to be baptized by You, yet You come to me?” ❓
## Matthew 3:15
Jesus answered, “Allow it now. This is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness.” John allowed it. 📖
## Matthew 3:16
Immediately after baptism, Jesus came up out of the water. Heaven opened, and He saw God’s Spirit descend like a dove and rest on Him. ✨
## Matthew 3:17
A heavenly voice said, “This is My beloved Son. I am well pleased with Him!” 📖
