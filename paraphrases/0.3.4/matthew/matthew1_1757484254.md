## Matthew 1:1
This records the genealogy of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham. 📖
## Matthew 1:2
Abraham was Isaac’s father. Isaac was Jacob’s father. Jacob was the father of Judah and his brothers. 📖
## Matthew 1:3
Judah was the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar. Perez was Hezron’s father, and Hezron was Ram’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:4
Ram was Amminadab’s father. Amminadab was Nahshon’s father, and Nahshon was Salmon’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:5
Salmon was the father of Boaz by Rahab. Boaz was the father of Obed by Ruth. Obed was Jesse’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:6
Jesse was King David’s father. David was the father of Solomon by Uriah’s wife. 📖
## Matthew 1:7
Solomon was Rehoboam’s father. Rehoboam was Abijah’s father, and Abijah was Asa’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:8
Asa was Jehoshaphat’s father. Jehoshaphat was Joram’s father, and Joram was Uzziah’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:9
Uzziah was Jotham’s father. Jotham was Ahaz’s father, and Ahaz was Hezekiah’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:10
Hezekiah was Manasseh’s father. Manasseh was Amon’s father, and Amon was Josiah’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:11
Josiah was the father of Jeconiah and his brothers at the exile to Babylon. 📖
## Matthew 1:12
After the exile to Babylon, Jeconiah was Shealtiel’s father. Shealtiel was Zerubbabel’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:13
Zerubbabel was Abiud’s father. Abiud was Eliakim’s father, and Eliakim was Azor’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:14
Azor was Zadok’s father. Zadok was Achim’s father, and Achim was Eliud’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:15
Eliud was Eleazar’s father. Eleazar was Matthan’s father, and Matthan was Jacob’s father. 📖
## Matthew 1:16
Jacob was Joseph’s father. Joseph was Mary’s husband. Mary gave birth to Jesus, who is called Christ. 📖
## Matthew 1:17
There were fourteen generations from Abraham to David. There were fourteen from David to the exile to Babylon and fourteen from that exile to the Christ. 📖
## Matthew 1:18
This is how Jesus Christ’s birth happened. Mary was pledged in marriage to Joseph. Before they came together, she was found pregnant through the Holy Spirit. 📖
## Matthew 1:19
Joseph, her husband, was righteous and unwilling to disgrace her publicly. Therefore he decided to divorce her quietly. 📖
## Matthew 1:20
After he considered this, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream. “Joseph, son of David, do not fear taking Mary as your wife. The One conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. 📖
## Matthew 1:21
She will bear a Son. Name Him Jesus because He will save His people from their sins.” 📖
## Matthew 1:22
This fulfilled the Lord’s word through the prophet: 📖
## Matthew 1:23
“The virgin will conceive and bear a son. They will call Him Immanuel,” meaning “God with us.” 📖
## Matthew 1:24
Joseph woke and obeyed the Lord’s angel, taking Mary as his wife. 📖
## Matthew 1:25
He had no union with her until she bore a Son. He named Him Jesus. 📖
