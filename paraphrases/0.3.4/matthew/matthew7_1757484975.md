## Matthew 7:1
“Do not judge, or you will be judged. 📖
## Matthew 7:2
The judgment you give will be given to you. The measure you use will be used for you. 📖
## Matthew 7:3
Why see your brother’s eye speck but ignore your own eye’s beam? ❓
## Matthew 7:4
How can you offer to remove his speck while a beam remains in your eye? ❓
## Matthew 7:5
Hypocrite! Remove your own beam first. Then you can see clearly to remove his speck. 📖
## Matthew 7:6
Do not give dogs holy things or throw pearls before pigs. They may trample them, then turn and tear you apart. 📖
## Matthew 7:7
Ask, and you will receive. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and the door will be opened. 📖
## Matthew 7:8
Everyone asking receives. Everyone seeking finds. The door opens to whoever knocks. 📖
## Matthew 7:9
Would you give your son a stone when he asks for bread? ❓
## Matthew 7:10
Would you give him a snake when he asks for fish? ❓
## Matthew 7:11
You are evil but know how to give your children good gifts. How much more will your heavenly Father give good things to those asking Him! 📖
## Matthew 7:12
In everything, treat others as you want them to treat you. This is the essence of the Law and Prophets. 📖
## Matthew 7:13
Enter the narrow gate. The wide gate and broad road lead to destruction, and many enter. 📖
## Matthew 7:14
The small gate and narrow road lead to life, and few find it. 📖
## Matthew 7:15
Beware of false prophets in sheep’s clothing. Inside they are ravenous wolves. 📖
## Matthew 7:16
You will recognize them by their fruit. Do grapes grow on thornbushes or figs on thistles? ❓
## Matthew 7:17
Good trees bear good fruit, and bad trees bear bad fruit. 🌳
## Matthew 7:18
A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, nor a bad tree good fruit. 🌳
## Matthew 7:19
Trees not bearing good fruit are cut down and thrown into fire. 📖
## Matthew 7:20
You will recognize them by their fruit. 📖
## Matthew 7:21
Not everyone calling Me ‘Lord, Lord’ enters the kingdom of heaven. Only those doing My heavenly Father’s will enter. 📖
## Matthew 7:22
Many will ask that day, ‘Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy, drive out demons, and perform many miracles, all in Your name?’ ❓
## Matthew 7:23
I will plainly answer, ‘I never knew you. Leave Me, you workers of lawlessness!’ 📖
## Matthew 7:24
Whoever hears these words of Mine and acts on them is like a wise man building his house on rock. 📖
## Matthew 7:25
Rain, raging torrents, and winds struck the house. It stood because its foundation was on rock. 📖
## Matthew 7:26
Whoever hears these words of Mine without acting on them is like a foolish man building his house on sand. 📖
## Matthew 7:27
Rain, raging torrents, and winds struck it. It fell with a great collapse!” 📖
## Matthew 7:28
Jesus finished, and the crowds marveled at His teaching, 📖
## Matthew 7:29
because He taught with authority, unlike their scribes. 📖
