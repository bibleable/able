## Matthew 12:1
Jesus crossed grainfields on the Sabbath. His hungry disciples picked grain heads and ate. 📖
## Matthew 12:2
The Pharisees said, “Your disciples break Sabbath law.” 📖
## Matthew 12:3
He answered, “Did you not read what David did when he and his companions were hungry? ❓
## Matthew 12:4
David entered God’s house. He and his companions ate the consecrated bread, permitted only to priests. 📖
## Matthew 12:5
Did you not read that temple priests break the Sabbath yet are innocent? ❓
## Matthew 12:6
One greater than the temple is here. 📖
## Matthew 12:7
Knowing ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice’ would have kept you from condemning the innocent. 📖
## Matthew 12:8
The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.” 📖
## Matthew 12:9
He entered their synagogue. 📖
## Matthew 12:10
A man had a withered hand. They asked whether Sabbath healing was lawful, seeking grounds to accuse Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 12:11
He answered, “Would you not lift your sheep from a pit on the Sabbath? ❓
## Matthew 12:12
A man is worth much more than a sheep. Therefore doing good on the Sabbath is lawful.” 📖
## Matthew 12:13
He told the man to stretch his hand. He did, and it was restored to full use like the other. ❤️
## Matthew 12:14
The Pharisees left and plotted to kill Him. 📖
## Matthew 12:15
Jesus knew and withdrew. Large crowds followed, and He healed them all, 📖
## Matthew 12:16
warning them not to make Him known. 📖
## Matthew 12:17
This fulfilled Isaiah’s prophecy: 📖
## Matthew 12:18
“My chosen Servant is My beloved, in whom My soul delights. I will put My Spirit on Him. He will proclaim justice to the nations. 📖
## Matthew 12:19
He will not quarrel or cry out. No one will hear His voice in the streets. 📖
## Matthew 12:20
He will not break a bruised reed or extinguish a smoldering wick until He leads justice to victory. 📖
## Matthew 12:21
The nations will hope in His name.” 📖
## Matthew 12:22
A blind, mute man possessed by a demon was brought. Jesus healed him to see and speak. ❤️
## Matthew 12:23
The crowds marveled: “Could this be David’s Son?” ❓
## Matthew 12:24
The Pharisees said, “He expels demons only by Beelzebul, their prince.” 📖
## Matthew 12:25
Jesus knew their thoughts. “Every kingdom divided against itself is destroyed. Every city or household divided against itself cannot stand. 📖
## Matthew 12:26
If Satan expels Satan, he is divided against himself. How can his kingdom stand? ❓
## Matthew 12:27
If I expel demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons expel them? They will be your judges. 📖
## Matthew 12:28
If I expel demons by God’s Spirit, His kingdom has come upon you. 📖
## Matthew 12:29
How can someone plunder a strong man’s house without first tying him up? Then he can steal his possessions. ❓
## Matthew 12:30
Whoever is not with Me is against Me. Whoever does not gather with Me scatters. 📖
## Matthew 12:31
Every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people except blasphemy against the Spirit. 📖
## Matthew 12:32
Speaking against the Son of Man will be forgiven. Speaking against the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven, in this age or the coming one. 📖
## Matthew 12:33
Make a tree good and its fruit good, or bad and its fruit bad. A tree is known by its fruit. 🌳
## Matthew 12:34
Brood of vipers, how can evil people speak good? The mouth speaks from the heart’s overflow. 📖
## Matthew 12:35
A good man draws good things from his good treasure store. An evil man draws evil from his evil store. 📖
## Matthew 12:36
People must account for every careless word on judgment day. 📖
## Matthew 12:37
Your words will acquit or condemn you.” 📖
## Matthew 12:38
Some scribes and Pharisees said, “Teacher, show us a sign.” 📖
## Matthew 12:39
Jesus answered, “A wicked, adulterous generation demands a sign. It will receive only the prophet Jonah’s sign. 📖
## Matthew 12:40
Jonah spent three days and three nights in the great fish’s belly. The Son of Man will spend three days and three nights in earth’s heart. 📖
## Matthew 12:41
Nineveh’s men will rise at judgment with this generation and condemn it. They repented at Jonah’s preaching. One greater than Jonah is here. 📖
## Matthew 12:42
The Queen of the South will rise at judgment and condemn this generation. She came from earth’s ends to hear Solomon’s wisdom. One greater than Solomon is here. 📖
## Matthew 12:43
An unclean spirit leaves a man and seeks rest in dry places without finding it. 📖
## Matthew 12:44
It says, ‘I will return to the house I left.’ It finds it vacant, swept clean, and ordered. 📖
## Matthew 12:45
It brings seven other spirits more wicked than itself, and they live there. The man’s final condition is worse than his first. This wicked generation will be like that.” 📖
## Matthew 12:46
His mother and brothers waited outside to speak to Him while He addressed the crowds. 👥
## Matthew 12:47
Someone told Him His mother and brothers stood outside wanting to speak. 📖
## Matthew 12:48
He asked, “Who are My mother and brothers?” ❓
## Matthew 12:49
Pointing to His disciples, He said, “Here are My mother and brothers. 📖
## Matthew 12:50
Whoever does My heavenly Father’s will is My brother, sister, and mother.” 📖
