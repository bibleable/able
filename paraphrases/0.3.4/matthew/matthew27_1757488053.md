## Matthew 27:1
At morning all chief priests and elders plotted Jesus’ death. 📖
## Matthew 27:2
They bound Him and delivered Him to Governor Pilate. 📖
## Matthew 27:3
Judas saw His condemnation and felt remorse. He returned thirty silver pieces to the chief priests and elders. 📖
## Matthew 27:4
“I sinned by betraying innocent blood.” They answered, “That is your responsibility.” 📖
## Matthew 27:5
He threw the silver into the temple, left, and hanged himself. 💔
## Matthew 27:6
The priests collected it. “Blood money cannot lawfully enter the treasury.” 📖
## Matthew 27:7
They agreed to buy the potter’s field to bury foreigners. 📖
## Matthew 27:8
It is called the Field of Blood to this day. 📖
## Matthew 27:9
This fulfilled Jeremiah’s prophecy: “They took thirty silver pieces, His price set by Israel’s people, 📖
## Matthew 27:10
and paid for the potter’s field as the Lord commanded me.” 📖
## Matthew 27:11
Pilate asked Jesus whether He was the Jews’ King. “You have said so.” 📖
## Matthew 27:12
He did not answer His accusers, the chief priests and elders. 📖
## Matthew 27:13
Pilate asked whether He heard their many charges. ❓
## Matthew 27:14
His complete silence amazed Pilate. 📖
## Matthew 27:15
At the feast, the governor customarily released the crowd’s chosen prisoner. 📖
## Matthew 27:16
Barabbas was a notorious prisoner. 📖
## Matthew 27:17
Pilate asked whether to release Barabbas or Jesus called Christ. ❓
## Matthew 27:18
He knew envy caused them to surrender Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 27:19
His wife sent word while he sat judging: “Avoid that innocent man. I suffered terribly in a dream today because of Him.” 📖
## Matthew 27:20
The priests and elders persuaded the crowd to demand Barabbas’s release and Jesus’ death. 📖
## Matthew 27:21
Pilate asked which to release. “Barabbas.” 📖
## Matthew 27:22
He asked what to do with Jesus called Christ. Everyone cried, “Crucify Him!” 📖
## Matthew 27:23
He asked what evil Jesus did. They shouted louder for crucifixion. 📖
## Matthew 27:24
A riot began, and Pilate saw no progress. He washed his hands with water before them: “I am innocent of His blood. You bear responsibility.” 📖
## Matthew 27:25
The whole crowd answered, “His blood be on us and our children!” 📖
## Matthew 27:26
He released Barabbas, had Jesus flogged, and handed Him over for crucifixion. 📖
## Matthew 27:27
The soldiers took Him into the Praetorium and gathered their whole company. 📖
## Matthew 27:28
They stripped Him and dressed Him in a scarlet robe. 📖
## Matthew 27:29
They crowned Him with twisted thorns and put a staff in His right hand. Kneeling mockingly, they hailed the Jews’ King. 📖
## Matthew 27:30
They spat on Him and repeatedly struck His head with the staff. 📖
## Matthew 27:31
They removed the robe, restored His own clothes, and led Him to crucifixion. 📖
## Matthew 27:32
They forced Simon of Cyrene to carry His cross. 📖
## Matthew 27:33
They reached Golgotha, meaning The Place of the Skull. 📖
## Matthew 27:34
He tasted wine mixed with gall but refused to drink. 📖
## Matthew 27:35
They crucified Him and cast lots for His clothes. 📖
## Matthew 27:36
They sat watching Him. 📖
## Matthew 27:37
They posted the charge above His head: “THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS.” 📖
## Matthew 27:38
Two robbers were crucified beside Him, one right and one left. 📖
## Matthew 27:39
Passersby shook their heads and abused Him, 📖
## Matthew 27:40
“You destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days? Save Yourself! If God’s Son, come down!” 📖
## Matthew 27:41
The chief priests, scribes, and elders mocked, 📖
## Matthew 27:42
“He saved others but cannot save Himself. Let Israel’s King descend the cross, and we will believe. 📖
## Matthew 27:43
He trusts God, who should rescue Him if He wants Him. He claimed to be God’s Son.” 📖
## Matthew 27:44
The crucified robbers also berated Him. 📖
## Matthew 27:45
Darkness covered all the land from the sixth to ninth hours. 📖
## Matthew 27:46
About the ninth hour He cried loudly, “Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?” This means, “My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?” 💔
## Matthew 27:47
Some thought He called Elijah. 📖
## Matthew 27:48
One quickly ran for a sponge, filled it with sour wine, and put it on a reed. He held it up for Jesus to drink. 📖
## Matthew 27:49
Others said, “Leave Him. Let us see whether Elijah comes to save Him.” 📖
## Matthew 27:50
Jesus cried loudly again and yielded His spirit. 💔
## Matthew 27:51
The temple veil tore in two from top to bottom. Earth shook, and rocks split. 📖
## Matthew 27:52
Tombs opened, and the bodies of many saints who had died were raised. 📖
## Matthew 27:53
After Jesus’ resurrection, they left the tombs, entered the holy city, and appeared to many. 📖
## Matthew 27:54
The centurion and those guarding Jesus saw the earthquake and all that happened. Terrified, they said, “Truly, He was God’s Son.” 📖
## Matthew 27:55
Many women watched from afar. They had followed from Galilee to minister to Him. 📖
## Matthew 27:56
Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary mother of James and Joseph, and Zebedee’s sons’ mother. 📖
## Matthew 27:57
At evening, wealthy Joseph of Arimathea came, a disciple of Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 27:58
He asked Pilate for the body, and Pilate ordered its release. 📖
## Matthew 27:59
Joseph wrapped it in clean linen, 📖
## Matthew 27:60
and placed it in his own new rock-cut tomb. He rolled a large stone across the entrance and left. 📖
## Matthew 27:61
Mary Magdalene and the other Mary sat opposite. 📖
## Matthew 27:62
The day after Preparation Day, chief priests and Pharisees met Pilate. 📖
## Matthew 27:63
“That deceiver said He would rise after three days. 📖
## Matthew 27:64
Secure the tomb until day three so His disciples cannot steal Him and claim resurrection. That deception would be worse than the first.” 📖
## Matthew 27:65
Pilate said, “You have a guard. Secure it as best you can.” 📖
## Matthew 27:66
They sealed the stone and posted guards to secure it. 📖
