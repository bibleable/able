## Matthew 13:1
That day Jesus left the house and sat beside the sea. 📖
## Matthew 13:2
Large crowds gathered, so He sat in a boat while they stood ashore. 👥
## Matthew 13:3
He taught many parables. “A farmer sowed seed. 🌱
## Matthew 13:4
Some fell on the path and birds ate it. 🌱
## Matthew 13:5
Some fell on rocky ground with little soil. It sprouted quickly because the soil was shallow. 🌱
## Matthew 13:6
Sun scorched the seedlings. They withered without roots. 🌱
## Matthew 13:7
Some fell among thorns, which grew and choked the seedlings. 🌱
## Matthew 13:8
Some fell on good soil, yielding a hundredfold, sixtyfold, or thirtyfold. 🌱
## Matthew 13:9
Let those with ears hear.” 📖
## Matthew 13:10
The disciples asked why He used parables. ❓
## Matthew 13:11
He answered, “You received knowledge of the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven. They did not. 📖
## Matthew 13:12
Whoever has will receive more in abundance. Whoever lacks will lose even what he has. 📖
## Matthew 13:13
I use parables because they see without seeing and hear without hearing or understanding. 📖
## Matthew 13:14
Isaiah’s prophecy is fulfilled in them: ‘You will keep hearing without understanding and seeing without perceiving. 📖
## Matthew 13:15
Their hearts grew callous, their ears barely hear, and they closed their eyes. Otherwise they might see, hear, understand in their hearts, and turn. I would heal them.’ 📖
## Matthew 13:16
Your eyes are blessed because they see, and your ears because they hear. 📖
## Matthew 13:17
Truly, many prophets and righteous men longed to see and hear what you do. They did not see or hear it. 📖
## Matthew 13:18
Consider the sower’s parable. 📖
## Matthew 13:19
The path seed represents one hearing the kingdom’s message without understanding. The evil one snatches what was sown in his heart. 📖
## Matthew 13:20
Rocky ground represents one hearing the word and immediately receiving it joyfully. 📖
## Matthew 13:21
He has no root and lasts only a season. Trouble or persecution because of the word makes him quickly fall away. 📖
## Matthew 13:22
Thorny ground represents one hearing the word. Life’s worries and wealth’s deceit choke it, making it unfruitful. 📖
## Matthew 13:23
Good soil represents one hearing and understanding the word. He bears a crop of a hundredfold, sixtyfold, or thirtyfold.” 🌱
## Matthew 13:24
Jesus said, “The kingdom of heaven resembles a man sowing good seed in his field. 🌱
## Matthew 13:25
While everyone slept, his enemy sowed weeds among the wheat and left. 📖
## Matthew 13:26
The wheat sprouted and bore grain, and the weeds appeared. 🌱
## Matthew 13:27
His servants asked, ‘Sir, did you not sow good seed? Where did the weeds come from?’ ❓
## Matthew 13:28
He answered, ‘An enemy did it.’ They asked whether to pull them. 📖
## Matthew 13:29
He said, ‘No. You might uproot the wheat too. 📖
## Matthew 13:30
Let both grow until harvest. Then I will tell the harvesters to bundle weeds for burning first and gather wheat into my barn.’” 📖
## Matthew 13:31
He said, “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed planted in a field. 🌱
## Matthew 13:32
It is the smallest seed but becomes the largest garden plant, a tree where birds nest in its branches.” 🌳
## Matthew 13:33
He said, “The kingdom of heaven is like leaven a woman mixes into three measures of flour until all is leavened.” 📖
## Matthew 13:34
Jesus taught the crowds these things only in parables. 📖
## Matthew 13:35
This fulfilled the prophet’s words: “I will speak in parables of things hidden since the world’s foundation.” 📖
## Matthew 13:36
He dismissed the crowds and entered the house. His disciples asked Him to explain the weeds parable. 📖
## Matthew 13:37
“The good seed’s Sower is the Son of Man. 📖
## Matthew 13:38
The field is the world. Good seed represents the kingdom’s sons, and weeds the evil one’s sons. 📖
## Matthew 13:39
The sowing enemy is the devil. Harvest is the age’s end, and harvesters are angels. 📖
## Matthew 13:40
Weeds are collected and burned. So it will be at the age’s end. 📖
## Matthew 13:41
The Son of Man will send His angels to remove every cause of sin and all lawless people from His kingdom. 📖
## Matthew 13:42
They will throw them into the fiery furnace, with weeping and grinding of teeth. 📖
## Matthew 13:43
The righteous will shine like the sun in their Father’s kingdom. Let those with ears hear. ✨
## Matthew 13:44
The kingdom of heaven resembles hidden field treasure. A man finds and hides it again, then joyfully sells everything and buys the field. 📖
## Matthew 13:45
It also resembles a merchant seeking fine pearls. 📖
## Matthew 13:46
He finds one very precious pearl, sells everything, and buys it. 📖
## Matthew 13:47
It also resembles a net cast into the sea that catches every kind of fish. 📖
## Matthew 13:48
When full, men pull it ashore, sit, put good fish in containers, and discard bad fish. 📖
## Matthew 13:49
At the age’s end, angels will separate wicked people from the righteous, 📖
## Matthew 13:50
and throw them into the fiery furnace, with weeping and grinding of teeth. 📖
## Matthew 13:51
Do you understand all this?” They answered, “Yes.” 📖
## Matthew 13:52
He said, “Therefore every scribe trained in the kingdom of heaven is like a homeowner bringing new and old treasures from storage.” 📖
## Matthew 13:53
Jesus finished these parables and left. 📖
## Matthew 13:54
He taught in His hometown synagogue. The people marveled at His wisdom and miraculous powers. They asked where these came from. ❓
## Matthew 13:55
“Is He not the carpenter’s son? Is His mother not Mary, and His brothers James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas? ❓
## Matthew 13:56
Are His sisters not all here? Where did He get these things?” ❓
## Matthew 13:57
They took offense. Jesus said, “A prophet lacks honor only in his hometown and household.” 📖
## Matthew 13:58
He did not perform many miracles there because of their unbelief. 📖
