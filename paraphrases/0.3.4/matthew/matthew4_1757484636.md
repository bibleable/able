## Matthew 4:1
The Spirit led Jesus into the wilderness for the devil to tempt Him. 📖
## Matthew 4:2
He fasted forty days and forty nights and was hungry. 📖
## Matthew 4:3
The tempter said, “If You are God’s Son, command these stones to become bread.” 📖
## Matthew 4:4
Jesus answered, “It is written, ‘Man does not live on bread alone but on every word from God’s mouth.’” 📖
## Matthew 4:5
The devil took Him to the holy city and placed Him on the temple’s pinnacle. 📖
## Matthew 4:6
He said, “If You are God’s Son, throw Yourself down. It is written, ‘He will command His angels about You. They will lift You in their hands so Your foot does not strike a stone.’” 📖
## Matthew 4:7
Jesus answered, “It is also written, ‘Do not test the Lord your God.’” 📖
## Matthew 4:8
The devil took Him to a very high mountain and showed all the world’s kingdoms and their glory. 📖
## Matthew 4:9
“I will give You all this if You fall down and worship me.” 📖
## Matthew 4:10
Jesus said, “Away, Satan! It is written, ‘Worship the Lord your God and serve Him alone.’” 📖
## Matthew 4:11
The devil left, and angels came and ministered to Him. 📖
## Matthew 4:12
Jesus heard John was imprisoned and withdrew to Galilee. 📖
## Matthew 4:13
He left Nazareth and lived in Capernaum by the sea, in Zebulun and Naphtali’s region. 📖
## Matthew 4:14
This fulfilled Isaiah’s prophecy: 📖
## Matthew 4:15
“Zebulun’s and Naphtali’s lands, the Way of the Sea beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles, 📖
## Matthew 4:16
people living in darkness saw a great light. Light dawned on those in the land of death’s shadow.” ✨
## Matthew 4:17
Jesus began preaching, “Repent because the kingdom of heaven is near.” 📖
## Matthew 4:18
Beside the Sea of Galilee, Jesus saw Simon called Peter and his brother Andrew. They cast a net into the sea because they were fishermen. 📖
## Matthew 4:19
He said, “Follow Me. I will make you fishers of men.” 📖
## Matthew 4:20
They immediately left their nets and followed Him. 📖
## Matthew 4:21
He saw James son of Zebedee and his brother John repairing nets in a boat with their father. He called them, 📖
## Matthew 4:22
and they immediately left their boat and father and followed Him. 📖
## Matthew 4:23
Jesus taught in synagogues throughout Galilee, preached the gospel of the kingdom, and healed every disease and sickness among the people. 📖
## Matthew 4:24
News spread throughout Syria. People brought Him everyone suffering diseases, acute pain, demon possession, seizures, or paralysis. He healed them. ❤️
## Matthew 4:25
Large crowds followed from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond the Jordan. 👥
