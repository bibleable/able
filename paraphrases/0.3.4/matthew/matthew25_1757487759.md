## Matthew 25:1
“Then heaven’s kingdom will resemble ten virgins carrying lamps to meet the bridegroom. 📖
## Matthew 25:2
Five were foolish and five wise. 📖
## Matthew 25:3
The foolish took no extra oil. 📖
## Matthew 25:4
The wise took oil flasks with their lamps. 📖
## Matthew 25:5
The bridegroom delayed, and all fell asleep. 📖
## Matthew 25:6
At midnight a cry announced him and called them to meet him. 📖
## Matthew 25:7
All woke and trimmed their lamps. 📖
## Matthew 25:8
The foolish asked for oil because their lamps were going out. 📖
## Matthew 25:9
The wise refused, fearing too little for both groups, and told them to buy oil. 📖
## Matthew 25:10
While they went, the bridegroom arrived. Those ready entered the feast with him, and the door shut. 📖
## Matthew 25:11
The others arrived, crying, ‘Lord, lord, open for us!’ 📖
## Matthew 25:12
He answered, ‘Truly, I do not know you.’ 📖
## Matthew 25:13
Keep watch because you know neither day nor hour. 📖
## Matthew 25:14
A traveling man entrusted his servants with possessions. 📖
## Matthew 25:15
He assigned five talents, two, and one, each according to the servant’s ability, and left. 📖
## Matthew 25:16
The five-talent servant immediately put them to work and gained five more. 📖
## Matthew 25:17
The two-talent servant gained two more. 📖
## Matthew 25:18
The one-talent servant buried his master’s money. 📖
## Matthew 25:19
After a long time, the master returned to settle accounts. 📖
## Matthew 25:20
The first presented five more talents, saying he doubled the entrusted five. 📖
## Matthew 25:21
The master said, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant. You were faithful in little and will oversee much. Enter your master’s joy!’ 📖
## Matthew 25:22
The second presented two more talents, saying he doubled the entrusted two. 📖
## Matthew 25:23
The master said, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant. You were faithful in little and will oversee much. Enter your master’s joy!’ 📖
## Matthew 25:24
The last said, ‘Master, you are hard, reaping where you never sowed and gathering where you scattered no seed. 📖
## Matthew 25:25
I feared you and hid your talent in the ground. Here is yours.’ 📖
## Matthew 25:26
He replied, ‘Wicked, lazy servant! You knew I reap where I never sowed and gather where I scattered no seed. 📖
## Matthew 25:27
You should have deposited my money with bankers so I would receive it back with interest. 📖
## Matthew 25:28
Give his talent to the one with ten. 📖
## Matthew 25:29
Whoever has will receive more in abundance. Whoever lacks will lose even what he has. 📖
## Matthew 25:30
Throw the worthless servant into outer darkness, with weeping and grinding of teeth.’ 📖
## Matthew 25:31
The Son of Man will come in glory with all angels and sit on His glorious throne. 📖
## Matthew 25:32
All nations will be gathered before Him. He will separate the people like a shepherd separating sheep and goats. 📖
## Matthew 25:33
He will place the sheep on His right and the goats on His left. 📖
## Matthew 25:34
The King will tell those on His right, ‘Come, blessed by My Father. Inherit the kingdom prepared for you since the world’s foundation. 📖
## Matthew 25:35
I was hungry, and you fed Me. I was thirsty, and you gave Me drink. I was a stranger, and you welcomed Me. 📖
## Matthew 25:36
I was naked, and you clothed Me. I was sick, and you cared for Me. I was imprisoned, and you visited Me.’ 📖
## Matthew 25:37
The righteous will ask, ‘Lord, when did we see You hungry and feed You, or thirsty and give You drink? ❓
## Matthew 25:38
When did we see You a stranger and welcome You, or naked and clothe You? ❓
## Matthew 25:39
When did we see You sick or imprisoned and visit You?’ ❓
## Matthew 25:40
He will answer, ‘Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of Mine, you did for Me.’ ❤️
## Matthew 25:41
He will tell those on His left, ‘Depart from Me, cursed ones, to eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels. 📖
## Matthew 25:42
I was hungry, and you gave Me no food. I was thirsty, and you gave Me no drink. 📖
## Matthew 25:43
I was a stranger, and you did not welcome Me. I was naked, and you did not clothe Me. I was sick and imprisoned, and you did not care for Me.’ 📖
## Matthew 25:44
They will ask, ‘Lord, when did we see You hungry, thirsty, a stranger, naked, sick, or imprisoned and fail to serve You?’ ❓
## Matthew 25:45
He will answer, ‘Whatever you did not do for one of the least of these, you did not do for Me.’ 📖
## Matthew 25:46
They will enter eternal punishment, and the righteous eternal life.” 📖
