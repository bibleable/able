## Matthew 9:1
Jesus crossed by boat to His own town. 📖
## Matthew 9:2
Men brought a paralyzed man on a mat. Jesus saw their faith and said, “Take courage, son. Your sins are forgiven.” ❤️
## Matthew 9:3
Some scribes thought, “He blasphemes!” 📖
## Matthew 9:4
Jesus knew their thoughts. “Why hold evil in your hearts? ❓
## Matthew 9:5
Which is easier to say: ‘Your sins are forgiven’ or ‘Get up and walk’? ❓
## Matthew 9:6
But so you may know the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins...” He told the paralyzed man, “Get up, take your mat, and go home.” 📖
## Matthew 9:7
He rose and went home. 📖
## Matthew 9:8
The crowds saw this and glorified God in awe for giving such authority to men. 🙏
## Matthew 9:9
Jesus saw Matthew at the tax booth. “Follow Me.” Matthew rose and followed Him. 📖
## Matthew 9:10
At Matthew’s house, many tax collectors and sinners ate with Jesus and His disciples. 👥
## Matthew 9:11
The Pharisees asked His disciples why their Teacher ate with tax collectors and sinners. ❓
## Matthew 9:12
Jesus heard and said, “The sick need a doctor, not the healthy. 📖
## Matthew 9:13
Learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice.’ I came to call sinners, not the righteous.” 📖
## Matthew 9:14
John’s disciples asked why they and the Pharisees fasted often but Jesus’ disciples did not. ❓
## Matthew 9:15
He answered, “Can wedding guests mourn while the bridegroom is with them? When He is taken away, they will fast. 📖
## Matthew 9:16
No one patches old clothing with unshrunk cloth. The patch pulls away and worsens the tear. 📖
## Matthew 9:17
People do not put new wine in old wineskins. It bursts them, spilling wine and ruining the skins. New wine in new wineskins preserves both.” 📖
## Matthew 9:18
A synagogue leader knelt before Him as He spoke. “My daughter just died. Lay Your hand on her, and she will live.” 🙏
## Matthew 9:19
Jesus and His disciples went with him. 📖
## Matthew 9:20
A woman bleeding for twelve years touched His cloak’s fringe from behind. 📖
## Matthew 9:21
She thought, “Touching His cloak will heal me.” 📖
## Matthew 9:22
Jesus turned and saw her. “Take courage, daughter. Your faith healed you.” She was healed from that very hour. ❤️
## Matthew 9:23
At the leader’s house, Jesus saw flute players and a noisy crowd. 📖
## Matthew 9:24
He said, “Leave. The girl is not dead but asleep.” They laughed. 📖
## Matthew 9:25
After the crowd was put outside, He entered and took her hand. She rose. ❤️
## Matthew 9:26
News spread throughout the region. 📖
## Matthew 9:27
Two blind men followed Jesus, crying, “Son of David, have mercy!” 🙏
## Matthew 9:28
They approached Him inside the house. He asked, “Do you believe I can do this?” They answered, “Yes, Lord.” 📖
## Matthew 9:29
He touched their eyes. “It will be done according to your faith.” 📖
## Matthew 9:30
Their eyes opened. Jesus sternly warned, “Let no one find out!” 📖
## Matthew 9:31
They spread news of Him throughout the land. 📖
## Matthew 9:32
As they left, a mute man possessed by a demon was brought to Jesus. 📖
## Matthew 9:33
He spoke after the demon was expelled. The crowds marveled: “Israel never saw anything like this!” 📖
## Matthew 9:34
The Pharisees said, “He expels demons by their prince.” 📖
## Matthew 9:35
Jesus taught in synagogues throughout the towns and villages, preached the gospel of the kingdom, and healed every disease and sickness. 📖
## Matthew 9:36
He felt compassion for the crowds because they were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd. ❤️
## Matthew 9:37
He told His disciples, “The harvest is plentiful, but workers are few. 📖
## Matthew 9:38
Ask the Lord of the harvest to send workers into His harvest.” 🙏
