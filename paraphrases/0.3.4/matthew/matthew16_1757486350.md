## Matthew 16:1
Pharisees and Sadducees tested Jesus by requesting a sign from heaven. 📖
## Matthew 16:2
He replied, “At evening you predict fair weather from a red sky. 📖
## Matthew 16:3
In the morning you predict storms from a red, overcast sky. You interpret the sky but not the times’ signs. 📖
## Matthew 16:4
A wicked, adulterous generation demands a sign. It will receive only Jonah’s sign.” He left them. 📖
## Matthew 16:5
The disciples crossed without remembering bread. 📖
## Matthew 16:6
Jesus warned, “Beware of the Pharisees’ and Sadducees’ leaven.” 📖
## Matthew 16:7
They concluded He meant their lack of bread. 📖
## Matthew 16:8
He knew their discussion. “Why debate lacking bread, you of little faith? ❓
## Matthew 16:9
Do you not understand? Do you not remember the five loaves for five thousand and how many baskets you collected? ❓
## Matthew 16:10
Or seven loaves for four thousand and how many baskets you collected? ❓
## Matthew 16:11
Why do you not understand I was not speaking about bread? Beware of the Pharisees’ and Sadducees’ leaven.” 📖
## Matthew 16:12
They understood His warning concerned the Pharisees’ and Sadducees’ teaching, not bread leaven. 📖
## Matthew 16:13
Near Caesarea Philippi, Jesus asked, “Who do people say the Son of Man is?” ❓
## Matthew 16:14
They answered, “John the Baptist, Elijah, Jeremiah, or another prophet.” 📖
## Matthew 16:15
He asked, “What about you? Who do you say I am?” ❓
## Matthew 16:16
Simon Peter answered, “You are the Christ, the living God’s Son.” 📖
## Matthew 16:17
Jesus said, “Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah! My heavenly Father revealed this, not flesh and blood. 📖
## Matthew 16:18
You are Peter. On this rock I will build My church. Hades’ gates will not prevail against it. 📖
## Matthew 16:19
I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. What you bind on earth will be bound in heaven. What you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven.” 📖
## Matthew 16:20
He warned them not to tell anyone He was the Christ. 📖
## Matthew 16:21
Jesus began explaining He must go to Jerusalem and suffer at the hands of elders, chief priests, and scribes. He must be killed and raised to life on day three. 📖
## Matthew 16:22
Peter rebuked Him privately: “Never, Lord! This must never happen to You!” 📖
## Matthew 16:23
Jesus turned: “Get behind Me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to Me. You think of human things instead of God’s.” 📖
## Matthew 16:24
He told the disciples, “Anyone wanting to follow Me must deny himself, take his cross, and follow Me. 📖
## Matthew 16:25
Whoever wants to save his life will lose it. Whoever loses it for My sake will find it. 📖
## Matthew 16:26
What profit is gaining the world while losing your soul? What can a man give in exchange for his soul? ❓
## Matthew 16:27
The Son of Man will come in His Father’s glory with His angels and repay each according to his deeds. 📖
## Matthew 16:28
Truly, some standing here will not die before seeing the Son of Man coming in His kingdom.” 📖
