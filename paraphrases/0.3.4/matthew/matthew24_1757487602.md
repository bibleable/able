## Matthew 24:1
As Jesus left the temple, His disciples came and pointed out its buildings. 📖
## Matthew 24:2
He said, “Do you see this? No stone here will remain on another. All will be thrown down.” 📖
## Matthew 24:3
On the Mount of Olives, they privately asked when this would happen and the sign of His coming and the age’s end. ❓
## Matthew 24:4
“Let no one deceive you. 📖
## Matthew 24:5
Many will claim to be the Christ in My name and deceive many. 📖
## Matthew 24:6
Do not fear wars or war rumors. These must happen, but the end is still coming. 📖
## Matthew 24:7
Nations and kingdoms will fight each other. Famines and earthquakes will strike various places. 📖
## Matthew 24:8
These begin the birth pains. 📖
## Matthew 24:9
You will face persecution and death. All nations will hate you because of My name. 📖
## Matthew 24:10
Many will fall away, betray, and hate each other. 📖
## Matthew 24:11
Many false prophets will deceive many. 📖
## Matthew 24:12
Increasing wickedness will chill most people’s love. 📖
## Matthew 24:13
Whoever endures to the end will be saved. 📖
## Matthew 24:14
The kingdom’s gospel will be preached worldwide as testimony to every nation. Then the end comes. 📖
## Matthew 24:15
When you see Daniel’s ‘abomination of desolation’ standing in the holy place, let the reader understand, 📖
## Matthew 24:16
those in Judea must flee to the mountains. 📖
## Matthew 24:17
Do not descend from your rooftop to collect household possessions. 📖
## Matthew 24:18
Do not return from the field for your cloak. 📖
## Matthew 24:19
How miserable for pregnant and nursing mothers then! 📖
## Matthew 24:20
Pray your flight is neither in winter nor on the Sabbath. 🙏
## Matthew 24:21
Great tribulation will occur, unmatched since the world began and never to be repeated. 📖
## Matthew 24:22
Without shortened days, no one would be saved. They will be shortened for the elect’s sake. 📖
## Matthew 24:23
Do not believe claims that the Christ is here or there. 📖
## Matthew 24:24
False Christs and prophets will perform great signs and wonders to deceive even the elect, if possible. 📖
## Matthew 24:25
I warned you beforehand. 📖
## Matthew 24:26
Do not go looking in the wilderness or believe He is in inner rooms. 📖
## Matthew 24:27
The Son of Man’s coming will be like lightning flashing east to west. 📖
## Matthew 24:28
Vultures gather wherever the carcass is. 📖
## Matthew 24:29
Immediately after that tribulation, sun and moon will be darkened. Stars will fall, and heaven’s powers will be shaken. 📖
## Matthew 24:30
The Son of Man’s sign will appear in heaven. All earth’s tribes will mourn. They will see Him coming on heavenly clouds with power and great glory. ✨
## Matthew 24:31
He will send His angels with a loud trumpet call. They will gather His elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other. 📖
## Matthew 24:32
Tender fig branches sprouting leaves tell you summer is near. 🌳
## Matthew 24:33
These things likewise tell you He is near, at the door. 📖
## Matthew 24:34
Truly, this generation will not pass away until all these things happen. 📖
## Matthew 24:35
Heaven and earth will pass away. My words never will. 📖
## Matthew 24:36
Only the Father knows the day and hour, not heaven’s angels or the Son. 📖
## Matthew 24:37
The Son of Man’s coming will be like Noah’s days. 📖
## Matthew 24:38
People ate, drank, married, and gave in marriage until Noah entered the ark. 📖
## Matthew 24:39
They knew nothing until the flood swept them all away. So will the Son of Man’s coming be. 📖
## Matthew 24:40
Two men will be in a field. One will be taken, the other left. 📖
## Matthew 24:41
Two women will grind at a mill. One will be taken, the other left. 📖
## Matthew 24:42
Keep watch because you do not know your Lord’s coming day. 📖
## Matthew 24:43
If the homeowner knew in which night watch the thief would come, he would watch and prevent his house being broken into. 📖
## Matthew 24:44
Be ready. The Son of Man comes at an unexpected hour. 📖
## Matthew 24:45
Who is the faithful, wise servant appointed to feed the household at the proper time? ❓
## Matthew 24:46
Blessed is the servant found doing this when his master returns. 📖
## Matthew 24:47
The master will put him over all his possessions. 📖
## Matthew 24:48
Suppose a wicked servant thinks his master will be away a long time, 📖
## Matthew 24:49
and beats fellow servants and eats and drinks with drunkards. 📖
## Matthew 24:50
His master will return on an unexpected day and hour. 📖
## Matthew 24:51
He will cut him to pieces and place him with hypocrites, with weeping and grinding of teeth. 📖
