## Matthew 8:1
Jesus descended the mountain, and large crowds followed. 👥
## Matthew 8:2
A leper knelt before Him. “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean.” 📖
## Matthew 8:3
Jesus reached out His hand and touched him. “I am willing. Be clean!” His leprosy was immediately cleansed. ❤️
## Matthew 8:4
He said, “Tell no one. Show yourself to the priest and offer Moses’ prescribed gift as a testimony to them.” 📖
## Matthew 8:5
A centurion approached Jesus in Capernaum and pleaded, 📖
## Matthew 8:6
“Lord, my servant lies paralyzed at home in terrible agony.” 🙏
## Matthew 8:7
Jesus said, “I will go and heal him.” 📖
## Matthew 8:8
The centurion answered, “Lord, I am unworthy to receive You under my roof. Say the word, and my servant will be healed. 📖
## Matthew 8:9
I am under authority and have soldiers under me. I tell one to go, and he goes, or another to come, and he comes. My servant does what I tell him.” 📖
## Matthew 8:10
Jesus marveled and told His followers, “Truly, I have not found anyone in Israel with such great faith. 📖
## Matthew 8:11
Many will come from east and west to feast with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven. 📖
## Matthew 8:12
The kingdom’s sons will be thrown into outer darkness, with weeping and grinding of teeth.” 📖
## Matthew 8:13
He told the centurion, “Go! It will be done for you as you believed.” His servant was healed that very hour. ❤️
## Matthew 8:14
At Peter’s house, Jesus found Peter’s mother-in-law sick in bed with fever. 📖
## Matthew 8:15
He touched her hand, and the fever left. She rose and served Him. ❤️
## Matthew 8:16
At evening, many possessed by demons were brought. He expelled the spirits with a word and healed all the sick. 📖
## Matthew 8:17
This fulfilled Isaiah’s prophecy: “He took our infirmities and carried our diseases.” 📖
## Matthew 8:18
Seeing a large crowd, Jesus ordered a crossing to the sea’s other side. 📖
## Matthew 8:19
A scribe said, “Teacher, I will follow You anywhere.” 📖
## Matthew 8:20
Jesus replied, “Foxes have dens and birds have nests. The Son of Man has nowhere to lay His head.” 📖
## Matthew 8:21
Another disciple asked, “Lord, first let me bury my father.” 📖
## Matthew 8:22
Jesus said, “Follow Me. Let the dead bury their own dead.” 📖
## Matthew 8:23
His disciples followed Him into the boat. 📖
## Matthew 8:24
A violent sea storm engulfed the boat with waves. Jesus slept. 🌊
## Matthew 8:25
They woke Him: “Lord, save us! We are perishing!” 🙏
## Matthew 8:26
He answered, “Why fear, people of little faith?” He rose and rebuked the winds and sea. Perfect calm followed. 📖
## Matthew 8:27
They marveled: “What kind of man is this? Even wind and sea obey Him!” ❓
## Matthew 8:28
In the Gadarenes’ region, two demon-possessed men met Him from the tombs. They were so violent no one could pass that way. 📖
## Matthew 8:29
They shouted, “What do You want with us, Son of God? Did You come to torture us before the appointed time?” 📖
## Matthew 8:30
A large pig herd fed in the distance. 📖
## Matthew 8:31
The demons begged, “If You expel us, send us into the pigs.” 📖
## Matthew 8:32
He said, “Go!” They came out and entered the pigs. The whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea and died in the water. 📖
## Matthew 8:33
The keepers fled to town and reported everything, including what happened to the demon-possessed men. 📖
## Matthew 8:34
The whole town met Jesus. They saw Him and begged Him to leave their region. 📖
