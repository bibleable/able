## Matthew 18:1
The disciples asked who was greatest in the kingdom of heaven. ❓
## Matthew 18:2
Jesus placed a little child among them. 📖
## Matthew 18:3
“Truly, unless you change and become like children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven. 📖
## Matthew 18:4
Whoever humbles himself like this child is greatest there. 📖
## Matthew 18:5
Welcoming such a child in My name welcomes Me. ❤️
## Matthew 18:6
For someone causing a little one believing in Me to stumble, drowning in the deep sea with a large millstone around his neck is better. 📖
## Matthew 18:7
Woe to the world for sin’s causes! Stumbling blocks must come, but woe to whoever brings them. 📖
## Matthew 18:8
Cut off and discard a hand or foot causing you to sin. Entering life crippled or lame is better than having two hands and feet and being thrown into eternal fire. 📖
## Matthew 18:9
Gouge out and discard an eye causing you to sin. Entering life with one eye is better than having two and being thrown into hell’s fire. 📖
## Matthew 18:10
Do not despise these little ones. Their angels always see My heavenly Father’s face. 📖
## Matthew 18:11

## Matthew 18:12
Would a man with a hundred sheep not leave ninety-nine on the hills to seek one lost sheep? ❓
## Matthew 18:13
If he finds it, he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine that stayed. 📖
## Matthew 18:14
Likewise, your heavenly Father does not want any little one to perish. ❤️
## Matthew 18:15
Confront your brother privately if he sins against you. If he listens, you won him over. 📖
## Matthew 18:16
If he refuses, take one or two others so two or three witnesses’ testimony establishes every matter. 📖
## Matthew 18:17
If he refuses them, tell the church. If he refuses the church, regard him as a pagan or tax collector. 📖
## Matthew 18:18
What you bind on earth will be bound in heaven. What you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven. 📖
## Matthew 18:19
If two of you on earth agree about anything you ask, My heavenly Father will do it for you. 📖
## Matthew 18:20
I am with two or three gathered in My name.” 📖
## Matthew 18:21
Peter asked, “Lord, should I forgive my brother’s sins against me up to seven times?” ❓
## Matthew 18:22
“Not just seven, but seventy-seven times! 📖
## Matthew 18:23
The kingdom of heaven is like a king settling his servants’ accounts. 📖
## Matthew 18:24
A debtor owing ten thousand talents was brought. 📖
## Matthew 18:25
He could not pay. The master ordered him, his wife, children, and possessions sold to pay the debt. 📖
## Matthew 18:26
He knelt and begged for patience, promising to repay everything. 📖
## Matthew 18:27
His master compassionately forgave the debt and released him. ❤️
## Matthew 18:28
He found another servant owing him a hundred denarii, grabbed him, and began choking him. ‘Pay what you owe!’ 📖
## Matthew 18:29
The other fell and begged for patience, promising repayment. 📖
## Matthew 18:30
He refused and imprisoned him until he could repay. 📖
## Matthew 18:31
Their fellow servants were deeply distressed and reported everything to the master. 📖
## Matthew 18:32
He summoned him: ‘Wicked servant! I forgave all your debt because you begged. 📖
## Matthew 18:33
Should you not show the mercy I showed you?’ ❓
## Matthew 18:34
Angrily, he handed him to jailers for torture until he repaid all he owed. 📖
## Matthew 18:35
My heavenly Father will treat you like this unless you forgive your brother from your heart.” 📖
