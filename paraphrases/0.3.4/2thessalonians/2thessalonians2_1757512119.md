## 2 Thessalonians 2:1
Brothers, we ask you about the return of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together with Him. 🙏
## 2 Thessalonians 2:2
Do not quickly become upset or afraid because of a spirit, message, or letter that seems to come from us. Such a message may claim that the Day of the Lord has already come. 📜
## 2 Thessalonians 2:3
Do not let anyone deceive you in any way. That day will not come until the rebellion occurs and the man of lawlessness appears. He is the son of destruction. ⚠️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:4
He will oppose every so-called god and every object of worship. He will place himself above them all. He will sit in God’s temple and declare that he is God. ⛔
## 2 Thessalonians 2:5
Do you remember that I told you these things when I was still with you? 💭
## 2 Thessalonians 2:6
You know what now holds him back. This allows him to appear at the proper time. ⏳
## 2 Thessalonians 2:7
The hidden power of lawlessness already acts. But the one who now holds it back will continue until he is taken out of the way. ⚠️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:8
Then the lawless man will appear. The Lord Jesus will kill him with the breath of His mouth. The glory of His arrival will destroy him completely. ⚖️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:9
Satan will work through the arrival of the lawless man. He will use every kind of power, sign, and false wonder. ⚠️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:10
He will use every evil deception against those who perish. They refused to love the truth that would have saved them. ⛔
## 2 Thessalonians 2:11
For this reason, God will send them a powerful deception, so they believe the lie. ⚖️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:12
Then judgment will come on all who rejected the truth and took pleasure in evil. ⚖️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:13
Brothers whom the Lord loves, we must always thank God for you. From the beginning, God chose to save you through the Spirit who makes you holy and through your faith in the truth. ❤️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:14
God called you to this through the good news we told you. He called you to share the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ. ✨
## 2 Thessalonians 2:15
So, brothers, stand firm. Hold to the teachings we passed on to you, whether we spoke them or wrote them in a letter. 📜
## 2 Thessalonians 2:16
Our Lord Jesus Christ Himself and God our Father loved us. Through grace, they gave us comfort that lasts forever and good hope. ❤️
## 2 Thessalonians 2:17
May they encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good word and act. 🙏
