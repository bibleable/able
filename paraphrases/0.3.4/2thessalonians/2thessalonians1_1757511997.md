## 2 Thessalonians 1:1
This letter is from Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy. We write to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ✉️
## 2 Thessalonians 1:2
May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace. 🕊️
## 2 Thessalonians 1:3
Brothers, we must always thank God for you. This is right because your faith continues to grow. Your love for each other also increases. ❤️
## 2 Thessalonians 1:4
So we tell God’s churches with pride about your faith and patience. You continue to trust Him through all the trouble and attacks you face. 💪
## 2 Thessalonians 1:5
All this clearly shows that God judges rightly. He will count you worthy of His kingdom, for which you now suffer. 👑
## 2 Thessalonians 1:6
It is right for God to bring trouble on those who cause you trouble. ⚖️
## 2 Thessalonians 1:7
He will also give relief to you who suffer and to us. This will happen when the Lord Jesus appears from heaven with His powerful angels. 👼
## 2 Thessalonians 1:8
He will appear in flames of fire. He will punish those who do not know God and do not obey the good news of our Lord Jesus. 🔥
## 2 Thessalonians 1:9
Their punishment will be eternal destruction. They will be separated from the Lord’s presence and the glory of His power. ⚖️
## 2 Thessalonians 1:10
This will happen on the day He comes to receive glory among His holy people. All who believe will look at Him with wonder. This includes you, because you believed what we told you. ✨
## 2 Thessalonians 1:11
So we always pray that our God will count you worthy of His call. We ask Him to use His power to fulfill every good desire and every act that comes from your faith. 🙏
## 2 Thessalonians 1:12
Then you will bring glory to the name of our Lord Jesus, and you will share His glory. This comes through the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ. ✨
