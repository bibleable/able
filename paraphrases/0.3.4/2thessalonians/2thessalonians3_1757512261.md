## 2 Thessalonians 3:1
Finally, brothers, pray for us. Pray that the Lord’s word will spread quickly and receive honor, as it did among you. 🙏
## 2 Thessalonians 3:2
Pray also that God will rescue us from wicked and evil people. Not everyone has faith. 🛡️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:3
But the Lord is faithful. He will strengthen you and protect you from the evil one. 🛡️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:4
We trust in the Lord that you do what we command. We also trust that you will continue to do it. 💪
## 2 Thessalonians 3:5
May the Lord guide your hearts into God’s love and the patience of Christ. ❤️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:6
Brothers, we command you in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. Keep away from any brother who lives without discipline and rejects the teachings you received from us. ⚠️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:7
You know how you must follow our example. We did not live without discipline among you. 👣
## 2 Thessalonians 3:8
We did not eat anyone’s food without paying for it. We worked hard, night and day, so we would not be a burden to any of you. 🛠️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:9
We had the right to receive support. But we wanted to give you an example to follow. 👣
## 2 Thessalonians 3:10
Even when we were with you, we gave you this command: “If anyone is unwilling to work, he must not eat.” 🛠️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:11
We hear that some of you live without discipline. They do no work, but they interfere in other people’s affairs. ⚠️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:12
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, we command and urge such people to work quietly and earn what they need to live. 🛠️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:13
But you, brothers, must not become tired of doing good. 💪
## 2 Thessalonians 3:14
Take note of anyone who does not obey the instructions in this letter. Do not associate with him, so that he may feel ashamed. ⚠️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:15
But do not treat him as an enemy. Warn him as a brother. ❤️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:16
May the Lord of peace Himself give you peace at all times and in every way. May the Lord be with all of you. 🕊️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:17
I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. This is my mark in every letter. This is how I write. ✍️
## 2 Thessalonians 3:18
May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. 🕊️
