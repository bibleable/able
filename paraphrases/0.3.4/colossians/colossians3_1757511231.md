## Colossians 3:1
You have been raised with Christ. So seek the things above, where Christ sits at God’s right hand. 👑
## Colossians 3:2
Set your minds on things above instead of earthly things. 💭
## Colossians 3:3
You died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God. ✝️
## Colossians 3:4
Christ is your life. When He appears, you too will appear with Him in glory. ✨
## Colossians 3:5
Put to death the sinful parts of your earthly nature. These include sexual immorality, impurity, lust, evil desires, and greed. Greed is idol worship. ⛔
## Colossians 3:6
Because of these things, God’s anger is coming on those who disobey Him. ⚖️
## Colossians 3:7
You too lived in these ways when you lived among them. 💭
## Colossians 3:8
But now put these things away: anger, rage, malice, lies against others, and filthy speech. ⚠️
## Colossians 3:9
Do not lie to each other. You have taken off your old self and its practices. 👣
## Colossians 3:10
You have put on the new self. It is renewed in knowledge to become like its Creator. 🌱
## Colossians 3:11
Here there is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, barbarian or Scythian, slave or free. Christ is everything and is in everyone. 🤝
## Colossians 3:12
God chose you as His holy and beloved people. Clothe yourselves with mercy, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience. ❤️
## Colossians 3:13
Be patient with each other. Forgive any complaint you have against someone. Forgive as the Lord forgave you. ❤️
## Colossians 3:14
Above all these qualities, put on love. It joins everything in perfect unity. 🤝
## Colossians 3:15
Let Christ’s peace rule in your hearts. You were called to this peace as parts of one body. Be thankful. 🕊️
## Colossians 3:16
Let Christ’s word live richly in you. Teach and warn each other with all wisdom. Sing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs with thanks to God in your hearts. 🎵
## Colossians 3:17
Whatever you say or do, do it all in the name of the Lord Jesus. Through Him, thank God the Father. 🙏
## Colossians 3:18
Wives, submit to your husbands, as is right in the Lord. 🤝
## Colossians 3:19
Husbands, love your wives and do not treat them harshly. ❤️
## Colossians 3:20
Children, obey your parents in everything. This pleases the Lord. 👣
## Colossians 3:21
Fathers, do not provoke your children, so that they do not lose heart. ❤️
## Colossians 3:22
Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything. Do this with sincere hearts and fear of the Lord, rather than only to please your masters when they watch. 🛠️
## Colossians 3:23
Whatever you do, work with your whole self, as for the Lord rather than for people. 🛠️
## Colossians 3:24
You know that the Lord will give you an inheritance as your reward. You serve the Lord Christ. 👑
## Colossians 3:25
Anyone who does wrong will receive payment for that wrong. There is no favoritism. ⚖️
