## Colossians 4:1
Masters, give your slaves what is right and fair. Remember that you too have a Master in heaven. ⚖️
## Colossians 4:2
Give yourselves to prayer. Stay watchful and thankful. 🙏
## Colossians 4:3
Pray for us too. Ask God to open a door for our message, so we can announce the mystery of Christ. I am in chains for this message. 📣
## Colossians 4:4
Pray that I can explain it clearly, as I should. 🙏
## Colossians 4:5
Act wisely toward those outside the faith. Make the most of your time. 👣
## Colossians 4:6
Always speak with grace, as if your words were seasoned with salt. Then you will know how to answer each person. 🗣️
## Colossians 4:7
Tychicus will tell you all the news about me. He is a dear brother, a faithful minister, and a fellow servant in the Lord. ✉️
## Colossians 4:8
I sent him so that you can know about us and receive encouragement in your hearts. ❤️
## Colossians 4:9
Onesimus goes with him. He is our faithful and dear brother, and one of you. They will tell you everything that happens here. 🤝
## Colossians 4:10
Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, sends greetings. Mark, Barnabas’s cousin, also sends greetings. You already received instructions about Mark. If he comes to you, welcome him. 👋
## Colossians 4:11
Jesus, who is called Justus, also sends greetings. These men are the only Jews among my fellow workers for God’s kingdom. They have comforted me. ❤️
## Colossians 4:12
Epaphras, one of you and a servant of Christ Jesus, sends greetings. He always struggles in prayer for you. He prays that you stand mature and fully sure in all God’s will. 🙏
## Colossians 4:13
I can testify that he works hard for you and for those at Laodicea and Hierapolis. 🛠️
## Colossians 4:14
Luke, our dear doctor, and Demas send greetings. 👋
## Colossians 4:15
Greet the brothers in Laodicea, Nympha, and the church that meets in her house. 🏠
## Colossians 4:16
After you read this letter together, make sure the church at Laodicea reads it too. Also read the letter from Laodicea yourselves. 📜
## Colossians 4:17
Tell Archippus, “Make sure you complete the ministry you received in the Lord.” 📣
## Colossians 4:18
I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. May grace be with you. ✍️
