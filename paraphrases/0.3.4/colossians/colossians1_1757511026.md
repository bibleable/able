## Colossians 1:1
This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God’s will, and Timothy our brother. ✉️
## Colossians 1:2
We write to God’s holy people and faithful brothers in Christ at Colossae. May God our Father give you grace and peace. 🕊️
## Colossians 1:3
When we pray for you, we always thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. 🙏
## Colossians 1:4
We have heard about your faith in Christ Jesus and your love for all God’s holy people. ❤️
## Colossians 1:5
Your faith and love come from the hope kept for you in heaven. You heard about this hope in the true message, the good news. 🌅
## Colossians 1:6
This good news has reached you. Throughout the world, it grows and produces fruit. It has done this among you since you heard it and truly understood God’s grace. 🌱
## Colossians 1:7
You learned it from Epaphras, our dear fellow servant. He faithfully serves Christ on our behalf. 🤝
## Colossians 1:8
He also told us about your love in the Spirit. ❤️
## Colossians 1:9
Since we heard about you, we have continued to pray for you. We ask God to fill you with knowledge of His will, with all spiritual wisdom and understanding. 🙏
## Colossians 1:10
Then you can live in a way worthy of the Lord and please Him in everything. You can produce fruit in every good task and grow in your knowledge of God. 🌱
## Colossians 1:11
May His glorious might give you all the power you need. May you have full endurance and patience. 💪
## Colossians 1:12
Give joyful thanks to the Father. He has made you fit to share the inheritance of His holy people in the light. 🙏
## Colossians 1:13
He rescued us from the rule of darkness. He brought us into the kingdom of His beloved Son. 👑
## Colossians 1:14
In the Son, we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins. ✝️
## Colossians 1:15
The Son is the image of God, whom no one can see. He is the firstborn over all creation. ✨
## Colossians 1:16
In Him, all things were created, in heaven and on earth, seen and unseen. This includes thrones, powers, rulers, and authorities. Everything was created through Him and for Him. 🌍
## Colossians 1:17
He exists before all things. In Him, all things hold together. ✨
## Colossians 1:18
He is the head of the body, which is the church. He is the beginning and the firstborn from the dead. So He has first place in everything. 👑
## Colossians 1:19
God was pleased to have all His fullness live in the Son. ✨
## Colossians 1:20
Through the Son, God chose to bring all things back to Himself, whether on earth or in heaven. He made peace through the blood of Christ’s cross. ✝️
## Colossians 1:21
Once you were separated from God. Your minds were hostile toward Him, and you did evil things. 💔
## Colossians 1:22
But now God has brought you back to Himself through the death of Christ’s physical body. His purpose is to present you holy, without stain or blame, before Him. ✝️
## Colossians 1:23
This requires you to continue firmly in your faith. Do not move away from the hope of the good news you heard. This good news has been announced to every creature under heaven. I, Paul, have become its servant. 📣
## Colossians 1:24
Now I rejoice in what I suffer for you. In my body, I complete what is still lacking in Christ’s afflictions for His body, the church. ✝️
## Colossians 1:25
I became the church’s servant through the task God gave me. He sent me to announce His word fully to you. 📖
## Colossians 1:26
This message is the mystery hidden for ages and generations. Now God has made it known to His holy people. ✨
## Colossians 1:27
God chose to show them the glorious riches of this mystery among the Gentiles. The mystery is Christ in you, the hope of glory. 🌅
## Colossians 1:28
We announce Christ. We warn and teach everyone with all wisdom, so that we can present everyone perfect in Christ. 📣
## Colossians 1:29
I work hard toward this goal. I strive with all Christ’s energy, which works powerfully in me. 💪
