## Colossians 2:1
I want you to know how hard I struggle for you, for those at Laodicea, and for everyone who has not met me. ❤️
## Colossians 2:2
I want their hearts to be encouraged and united in love. I want them to have the full riches of complete understanding. Then they can know God’s mystery, which is Christ. ✨
## Colossians 2:3
All the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden in Christ. 📖
## Colossians 2:4
I say this so that no one deceives you with persuasive words. ⚠️
## Colossians 2:5
Though I am away in body, I am with you in spirit. I rejoice to see your good order and firm faith in Christ. ❤️
## Colossians 2:6
You received Christ Jesus as Lord. So continue to live in Him. 👣
## Colossians 2:7
Be rooted and built up in Him. Be firm in the faith you were taught, and overflow with thanks. 🌱
## Colossians 2:8
Do not let anyone capture you through philosophy and empty deception. These depend on human traditions and the spiritual forces of the world, instead of Christ. ⚠️
## Colossians 2:9
All the fullness of God lives in Christ in bodily form. ✨
## Colossians 2:10
You have been made complete in Christ. He is the head over every ruler and authority. 👑
## Colossians 2:11
In Him, you also received circumcision when your sinful nature was removed. Christ performed this circumcision, rather than human hands. ✝️
## Colossians 2:12
You were buried with Him in baptism. You were also raised with Him through faith in God’s power. God raised Him from the dead. 🌅
## Colossians 2:13
You were dead in your sins and in the uncircumcision of your sinful nature. God made you alive with Christ. He forgave all our sins. ❤️
## Colossians 2:14
God canceled the debt recorded against us in the rules that condemned us. He removed it and nailed it to the cross. ✝️
## Colossians 2:15
He took power away from the rulers and authorities. Through the cross, He defeated them and displayed them publicly. 🛡️
## Colossians 2:16
Do not let anyone judge you about food, drink, a feast, a New Moon, or a Sabbath. 📜
## Colossians 2:17
These things are a shadow of what was to come. But the body that casts the shadow belongs to Christ. ✝️
## Colossians 2:18
Do not let anyone who enjoys false humility and angel worship disqualify you with claims about what he has seen. Such a person is full of empty pride from his unspiritual mind. ⚠️
## Colossians 2:19
They have lost their connection to the head. From the head, the whole body receives support and is joined together through joints and ligaments. It grows as God makes it grow. 🌱
## Colossians 2:20
If you died with Christ to the spiritual forces of the world, why do you submit to the world’s rules? Why act as if you still belong to it? 💬
## Colossians 2:21
These rules say, “Do not handle! Do not taste! Do not touch!” ⛔
## Colossians 2:22
The things these rules concern all perish with use. The rules depend on human commands and teachings. 📜
## Colossians 2:23
These rules seem wise, with their invented worship, false humility, and harsh treatment of the body. But they have no power to stop sinful physical desires. ⚠️
