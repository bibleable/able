## Haggai 1:1
This happened in King Darius’s second year, on the first day of the sixth month. The LORD spoke through the prophet Haggai. He spoke to Zerubbabel, Shealtiel’s son and Judah’s governor. He also spoke to Joshua, Jehozadak’s son and the high priest. 📣
## Haggai 1:2
The LORD of Hosts says, “These people say, ‘It is not yet time to rebuild the LORD’s house.’” 🏠
## Haggai 1:3
Then the LORD spoke through the prophet Haggai again. 📣
## Haggai 1:4
He said, “Is this the time to live in your houses with beautiful wood panels? My house lies in ruins!” 🏚️
## Haggai 1:5
Now the LORD of Hosts says, “Think carefully about your ways. 💭
## Haggai 1:6
You plant much but gather little. You eat but never have enough. You drink but remain thirsty. You wear clothes but do not get warm. You earn money and put it in a bag with holes.” 🌾💰
## Haggai 1:7
The LORD of Hosts says, “Think carefully about your ways. 💭
## Haggai 1:8
Go to the hills and bring wood. Build My house. Then I can enjoy it and receive honor.” The LORD says this. ⛰️🏠
## Haggai 1:9
The LORD of Hosts says, “You expected much but received little. I blew away what you brought home. Why? My house lies in ruins while each of you works on your own house. 🏚️
## Haggai 1:10
Because of you, the sky gives no dew. The earth gives no crops. 🌾
## Haggai 1:11
I called for dry weather on the fields and mountains. It affects grain, new wine, oil, and everything the ground grows. It affects people, animals, and all your work.” ☀️
## Haggai 1:12
Zerubbabel, Shealtiel’s son, and Joshua, Jehozadak’s son and the high priest, obeyed the LORD their God. All the remaining people obeyed too. They obeyed Haggai’s words because the LORD their God sent him. The people feared the LORD. ✅
## Haggai 1:13
Haggai, the LORD’s messenger, gave the people His message. The LORD declared, “I am with you.” ❤️
## Haggai 1:14
The LORD stirred the spirit of Zerubbabel, Shealtiel’s son and Judah’s governor. He stirred Joshua’s spirit too. Joshua was Jehozadak’s son and the high priest. The LORD also stirred the spirits of all the remaining people. They came and began work on the house of the LORD of Hosts, their God. 🏠
## Haggai 1:15
They began on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in King Darius’s second year. 📅
