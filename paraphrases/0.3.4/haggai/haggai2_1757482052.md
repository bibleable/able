## Haggai 2:1
On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the LORD spoke through the prophet Haggai. 📣📅
## Haggai 2:2
He said, “Speak to Zerubbabel, Shealtiel’s son and Judah’s governor. Speak to Joshua, Jehozadak’s son and the high priest. Speak to the remaining people too. Ask them these questions. 👥
## Haggai 2:3
‘Who among you saw this house in its earlier glory? How does it look now? Does it not seem like nothing beside the old house?’ 🏠❓
## Haggai 2:4
But now, Zerubbabel, be strong. Joshua, Jehozadak’s son and the high priest, be strong. All people of the land, be strong. Work, because I am with you.” The LORD of Hosts declares this. 💪
## Haggai 2:5
He says, “This is the promise I made when you left Egypt. My Spirit stays among you. Do not be afraid.” ❤️
## Haggai 2:6
The LORD of Hosts says, “Once more, in a little while, I will shake heaven and earth. I will shake the sea and dry land. 🌍🌊
## Haggai 2:7
I will shake all the nations. They will come with all their treasures. I will fill this house with glory. 🌍✨
## Haggai 2:8
The silver belongs to Me. The gold belongs to Me. 💰
## Haggai 2:9
This house’s later glory will be greater than its earlier glory. I will give peace in this place.” The LORD of Hosts declares this. 🏠🤝
## Haggai 2:10
In Darius’s second year, on the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, the LORD spoke to the prophet Haggai. 📅
## Haggai 2:11
The LORD of Hosts said, “Ask the priests to decide this question. ❓
## Haggai 2:12
Suppose someone carries holy meat in a fold of his clothes. If that fold touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or other food, does the food become holy?” The priests answered, “No.” ❓
## Haggai 2:13
Haggai asked, “Suppose a person becomes unclean after contact with a dead body. If that person touches any of these foods, does the food become unclean?” The priests answered, “Yes, it becomes unclean.” ❓
## Haggai 2:14
Haggai gave the LORD’s reply: “These people and this nation are like that before Me. So is all the work of their hands. Everything they offer there is unclean. ❌
## Haggai 2:15
From today onward, think carefully about this. Remember the time before people put one stone on another in the LORD’s temple. 💭
## Haggai 2:16
You expected a pile of twenty ephahs of grain, but found only ten. You expected fifty baths from the winepress, but found only twenty. *Word help: Ephahs measure grain, and baths measure liquids.* 🌾
## Haggai 2:17
I struck all your work with crop disease, mildew, and hail. But you did not turn to Me.” The LORD declares this. ⚖️
## Haggai 2:18
“Think carefully from today onward, the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month. This is the day people laid the LORD’s temple foundation. Think carefully. 📅💭
## Haggai 2:19
Is there still seed in the barn? The vine, fig, pomegranate, and olive trees give no fruit yet. But from today onward, I will give you good things.” 🌱
## Haggai 2:20
The LORD spoke to Haggai a second time that day, the twenty-fourth day of the month. 📣
## Haggai 2:21
He said, “Tell Zerubbabel, Judah’s governor, that I am about to shake heaven and earth. 🌍
## Haggai 2:22
I will overturn royal thrones and destroy the nations’ power. I will overturn chariots and their riders. Horses and riders will fall by one another’s swords. 👑⚔️
## Haggai 2:23
On that day, I will take you, My servant Zerubbabel, Shealtiel’s son. I will make you like My signet ring because I choose you.” The LORD of Hosts declares this. *Word help: A signet ring carries its owner’s seal.* 💍
