## Revelation 4:1
After this, I saw an open door in heaven. The voice I heard earlier, like a trumpet, said, “Come up here. I will show you what must happen after these things.” 🚪
## Revelation 4:2
Immediately I was in the Spirit. I saw a throne in heaven, with someone seated on it. 👑
## Revelation 4:3
The One seated there looked like jasper and carnelian. A rainbow shining like an emerald surrounded the throne. 🌈
## Revelation 4:4
Twenty-four other thrones surrounded it. Twenty-four elders sat on them, dressed in white, with gold crowns on their heads. 👑
## Revelation 4:5
Lightning, rumblings, and thunder came from the throne. Seven fiery torches burned before it. These are God’s seven spirits. 🔥
## Revelation 4:6
Before the throne was something like a sea of glass, clear as crystal. In the center, around the throne, were four living creatures. Eyes covered their fronts and backs. 📖
## Revelation 4:7
The first creature was like a lion, and the second like a calf. The third had a face like a man. The fourth was like an eagle flying. 🦅
## Revelation 4:8
Each of the four creatures had six wings and eyes all around and within. Day and night they never stop saying, “Holy, Holy, Holy is the Lord God Almighty, who was, is, and is to come!” 🙏
## Revelation 4:9
Whenever the creatures give glory, honor, and thanks to the One on the throne, who lives forever and ever, 🙏
## Revelation 4:10
the twenty-four elders fall before Him and worship Him who lives forever and ever. They throw their crowns before the throne, saying, 👑
## Revelation 4:11
“Our Lord and God, You deserve glory, honor, and power because You created everything. By Your will all things exist and were created.” 🙏
