## Revelation 17:1
One of the seven angels with seven bowls told me, “Come. I will show you the punishment of the great prostitute seated on many waters. ⚖️
## Revelation 17:2
Earth’s kings committed immorality with her. Earth’s inhabitants became drunk on the wine of her immorality.” ⚠️
## Revelation 17:3
In the Spirit, the angel carried me to a wilderness. A woman sat on a scarlet beast covered with blasphemous names. It had seven heads and ten horns. 📖
## Revelation 17:4
She wore purple and scarlet, gold, precious stones, and pearls. She held a gold cup full of detestable things and the impurities of her sexual immorality. ⚠️
## Revelation 17:5
A mysterious name was written on her forehead: BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF PROSTITUTES AND OF THE ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH. 📖
## Revelation 17:6
She was drunk with the blood of the saints and witnesses for Jesus. I was utterly amazed when I saw her. ⚠️
## Revelation 17:7
The angel asked, “Why are you amazed? I will explain the mystery of the woman and the beast carrying her, with its seven heads and ten horns. 📖
## Revelation 17:8
The beast you saw was, but now is not. It will soon rise from the Abyss and go to destruction. Earth’s inhabitants whose names were not written in the Book of Life from the world’s foundation will marvel when they see it. The beast was, is not, and yet will be. 📖
## Revelation 17:9
This calls for a wise mind. The seven heads are seven mountains where the woman sits. 📖
## Revelation 17:10
There are also seven kings. Five fell, one is, and the other has not yet come. When he comes, he must stay only a little while. 👑
## Revelation 17:11
The beast that was and now is not is an eighth king. He belongs to the seven and goes to destruction. 👑
## Revelation 17:12
The ten horns are ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom. They will receive authority as kings with the beast for one hour. 👑
## Revelation 17:13
Their one purpose is to give their power and authority to the beast. ⚠️
## Revelation 17:14
They will fight the Lamb. He will triumph because He is Lord of lords and King of kings. His called, chosen, and faithful ones will be with Him.” 👑
## Revelation 17:15
The angel told me, “The waters where the prostitute sat are peoples, multitudes, nations, and languages. 📖
## Revelation 17:16
The ten horns and the beast will hate the prostitute. They will leave her desolate and naked, eat her flesh, and burn her with fire. ⚖️
## Revelation 17:17
God put it in their hearts to carry out His purpose. They will unite to give their kingdom to the beast until God’s words are fulfilled. 📖
## Revelation 17:18
The woman is the great city that rules earth’s kings.” 🏙️
