## Revelation 8:1
The Lamb opened the seventh seal. Heaven was silent for about half an hour. 📜
## Revelation 8:2
I saw the seven angels who stand before God. They received seven trumpets. 📯
## Revelation 8:3
Another angel with a gold censer stood at the altar. He received much incense to offer with all the saints’ prayers on the gold altar before the throne. 🙏
## Revelation 8:4
The incense smoke and the saints’ prayers rose before God from the angel’s hand. 🙏
## Revelation 8:5
The angel filled the censer with fire from the altar and threw it to earth. Thunder, rumblings, lightning, and an earthquake followed. 🔥
## Revelation 8:6
The seven angels with seven trumpets prepared to blow them. 📯
## Revelation 8:7
The first angel blew his trumpet. Hail and fire mixed with blood were thrown onto the earth. A third of the earth and trees burned, along with all the green grass. 🔥
## Revelation 8:8
The second angel blew his trumpet. Something like a great burning mountain was thrown into the sea. A third of the sea turned to blood, 🌊
## Revelation 8:9
a third of its living creatures died, and a third of the ships were destroyed. ⛵
## Revelation 8:10
The third angel blew his trumpet. A great star burning like a torch fell from heaven onto a third of the rivers and onto the springs. ✨
## Revelation 8:11
The star’s name is Wormwood. A third of the waters became bitter like wormwood oil. Many people died from the bitter water. 💧
## Revelation 8:12
The fourth angel blew his trumpet. A third of the sun, moon, and stars were struck. A third of the stars became dark. A third of the day and night had no light. 🌑
## Revelation 8:13
I heard an eagle flying overhead cry loudly, “Woe! Woe! Woe to those living on earth! The remaining three angels are about to blow their trumpets!” 🦅
