## Revelation 12:1
A great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed in the sun. The moon was under her feet, and twelve stars crowned her head. ✨
## Revelation 12:2
She was pregnant and cried in the pain and agony of childbirth. 📖
## Revelation 12:3
Another sign appeared in heaven: a huge red dragon. He had seven heads, ten horns, and seven royal crowns on his heads. 📖
## Revelation 12:4
His tail swept a third of the stars from the sky and threw them to earth. He stood before the woman, ready to eat her child as soon as she gave birth. ⚠️
## Revelation 12:5
She bore a son, a male child who will rule all nations with an iron scepter. Her child was caught up to God and His throne. 👑
## Revelation 12:6
She fled into the wilderness. God had prepared a place there for her to receive food for 1,260 days. 📖
## Revelation 12:7
War broke out in heaven. Michael and his angels fought the dragon, and the dragon and his angels fought back. ⚔️
## Revelation 12:8
The dragon was not strong enough. He and his angels no longer had a place in heaven. ⚖️
## Revelation 12:9
The great dragon was thrown down. He is the ancient serpent called the devil and Satan, who deceives the whole world. He and his angels were thrown to earth. ⚖️
## Revelation 12:10
I heard a loud voice in heaven: “Our God’s salvation, power, and kingdom have now come, with His Christ’s authority. Our brothers’ accuser, who accused them before God day and night, has been thrown down. 📖
## Revelation 12:11
They conquered him through the Lamb’s blood and the word of their testimony. They did not love their lives enough to turn away from death. 📖
## Revelation 12:12
Rejoice, heavens and all who live there! Woe to earth and sea! The devil has come to you in great fury, knowing his time is short.” ⚠️
## Revelation 12:13
The dragon saw he had been thrown to earth. He pursued the woman who bore the male child. ⚠️
## Revelation 12:14
She received two wings of a great eagle to fly away from the serpent to her wilderness place. There she was fed for a time, times, and half a time. 🦅
## Revelation 12:15
The serpent poured water from his mouth like a river to overtake the woman and sweep her away. 🌊
## Revelation 12:16
The earth helped her. It opened its mouth and swallowed the river the dragon poured from his mouth. 📖
## Revelation 12:17
The dragon became angry with the woman and went to fight her other children. They keep God’s commandments and hold to Jesus’ testimony. The dragon stood on the seashore. ⚔️
