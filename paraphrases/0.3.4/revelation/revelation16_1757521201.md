## Revelation 16:1
I heard a loud voice from the temple tell the seven angels, “Pour the seven bowls of God’s wrath onto the earth.” ⚖️
## Revelation 16:2
The first angel poured his bowl onto the earth. Horrible, malignant sores broke out on those bearing the beast’s mark and worshiping its image. ⚖️
## Revelation 16:3
The second angel poured his bowl into the sea. It became blood like a dead person’s blood. Everything living in the sea died. 🌊
## Revelation 16:4
The third angel poured his bowl into the rivers and springs. They became blood. 💧
## Revelation 16:5
I heard the waters’ angel say, “Holy One, who is and was, You are righteous because You brought these judgments. ⚖️
## Revelation 16:6
They spilled the blood of saints and prophets. You gave them blood to drink, as they deserve.” ⚖️
## Revelation 16:7
I heard the altar answer, “Yes, Lord God Almighty, Your judgments are true and just.” 🙏
## Revelation 16:8
The fourth angel poured his bowl onto the sun. It received power to scorch people with fire. ☀️
## Revelation 16:9
Intense heat scorched the people. They cursed the name of God, who had authority over these plagues. They did not repent and give Him glory. ⚠️
## Revelation 16:10
The fifth angel poured his bowl onto the beast’s throne. Darkness covered its kingdom. People gnawed their tongues in anguish, 🌑
## Revelation 16:11
and cursed heaven’s God because of their pain and sores. They did not repent of their deeds. ⚠️
## Revelation 16:12
The sixth angel poured his bowl onto the great river Euphrates. Its water dried up to prepare the way for the kings of the East. 📖
## Revelation 16:13
I saw three unclean spirits like frogs come from the mouths of the dragon, beast, and false prophet. 📖
## Revelation 16:14
They are demonic spirits that perform signs. They go to all earth’s kings to gather them for battle on God the Almighty’s great day. ⚔️
## Revelation 16:15
“I am coming like a thief. Blessed is the one who stays awake and clothed, so he will not walk naked and expose his shame.” 📖
## Revelation 16:16
They gathered the kings at the place called Armageddon in Hebrew. ⚔️
## Revelation 16:17
The seventh angel poured his bowl into the air. A loud voice from the throne in the temple said, “It is done!” ⚖️
## Revelation 16:18
Lightning, rumblings, thunder, and a great earthquake followed. No earthquake so powerful had occurred since people lived on earth. 📖
## Revelation 16:19
The great city split into three parts, and the nations’ cities fell. God remembered Babylon the great. He gave her the cup of wine of His furious wrath. ⚖️
## Revelation 16:20
Every island fled, and no mountain could be found. 📖
## Revelation 16:21
Huge hailstones, each weighing almost a hundred pounds, fell on the people. They cursed God because the hail plague was so terrible. ⚠️
