## Revelation 18:1
Another angel descended from heaven with great authority. His glory lit the earth. 👼
## Revelation 18:2
He cried powerfully, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She became a home for demons, every unclean spirit and bird, and every detestable beast. ⚖️
## Revelation 18:3
All nations drank the wine of the passion of her immorality. Earth’s kings committed immorality with her. Earth’s merchants became rich from her extravagant luxury.” ⚠️
## Revelation 18:4
I heard another voice from heaven. It said, “Come out of her, My people, so you will not share her sins or suffer her plagues. 📖
## Revelation 18:5
Her sins pile up to heaven. God remembered her evil deeds. ⚖️
## Revelation 18:6
Repay her for what she did to others. Pay her back double for her deeds. Mix a double portion in her own cup. ⚖️
## Revelation 18:7
Give her as much torment and grief as the glory and luxury she gave herself. She says in her heart, ‘I sit as queen. I am no widow and will never know grief.’ 👑
## Revelation 18:8
Her plagues will come in one day: death, grief, and famine. Fire will consume her, because the Lord God who judges her is mighty.” ⚖️
## Revelation 18:9
Earth’s kings who committed sexual immorality and lived luxuriously with her will weep and wail. They will see smoke from the fire consuming her. 🔥
## Revelation 18:10
Afraid of her torment, they will stand far away. They will cry, “Woe, woe to Babylon, the great and mighty city! Your judgment came in a single hour.” ⚖️
## Revelation 18:11
Earth’s merchants will weep and mourn her. No one remains to buy their cargo: 📖
## Revelation 18:12
gold, silver, precious stones, pearls, fine linen, purple, silk, and scarlet. Also every kind of citron wood and articles of ivory, precious wood, bronze, iron, and marble, 📖
## Revelation 18:13
cinnamon, spice, incense, myrrh, frankincense, wine, olive oil, fine flour, and wheat. Also cattle, sheep, horses, carriages, and the bodies and souls of slaves. 📖
## Revelation 18:14
They will say, “The fruit your soul desired has left you. All your luxury and splendor vanished, never to be seen again.” ⚖️
## Revelation 18:15
The merchants who sold these things and grew rich from her will stand far away, afraid of her torment. They will weep and mourn, 📖
## Revelation 18:16
saying, “Woe, woe to the great city! You wore fine linen, purple, and scarlet, and gold, precious stones, and pearls. ⚖️
## Revelation 18:17
Such great wealth was destroyed in a single hour!” Every shipmaster, passenger, sailor, and person earning a living from the sea will stand far away, ⛵
## Revelation 18:18
and cry when they see the smoke from the fire consuming her. They will ask, “What city was ever like this great city?” 🔥
## Revelation 18:19
They will throw dust on their heads, weep, and mourn. They will cry, “Woe, woe to the great city! Her wealth made everyone with ships at sea rich. She was destroyed in a single hour!” ⚖️
## Revelation 18:20
Rejoice over her, heaven, saints, apostles, and prophets! God has pronounced His judgment against her for you. 🙏
## Revelation 18:21
A mighty angel threw a stone the size of a great millstone into the sea. He said, “Babylon the great city will be thrown down this violently, never to be seen again. 🪨
## Revelation 18:22
Harpists, musicians, flute players, and trumpeters will never be heard in you again. No craftsman of any trade will be found in you again. No millstone will be heard in you again. 🎶
## Revelation 18:23
No lamp will shine in you again. No bride or bridegroom’s voice will be heard in you again. Your merchants were earth’s great ones, because your sorcery deceived all nations.” ⚖️
## Revelation 18:24
In her was found the blood of prophets, saints, and everyone slain on earth. ⚖️
