## Revelation 1:1
This is the revelation of Jesus Christ. God gave it to Him to show His servants what must happen soon. He made it known by sending His angel to His servant John, 📖
## Revelation 1:2
who testifies to everything he saw. This is God’s word and the testimony of Jesus Christ. 📖
## Revelation 1:3
Blessed is the person who reads this prophecy aloud. Blessed are those who hear and obey its words, because the time is near. 📖
## Revelation 1:4
From John to the seven churches in Asia’s province. Grace and peace to you from Him who is, was, and is to come. Grace and peace also come from the seven spirits before His throne, 🕊️
## Revelation 1:5
and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, firstborn from the dead, and ruler of the earth’s kings. He loves us and freed us from our sins by His blood, ❤️
## Revelation 1:6
and made us a kingdom, priests to His God and Father. To Him belong glory and power forever and ever! Amen. 🙏
## Revelation 1:7
Look! He comes with the clouds, and every eye will see Him, even those who pierced Him. All the earth’s tribes will mourn because of Him. This will happen! Amen. ☁️
## Revelation 1:8
The Lord God says, “I am the Alpha and the Omega.” He is, was, and is to come, the Almighty. 👑
## Revelation 1:9
I, John, am your brother. We share in the tribulation, kingdom, and perseverance that are in Jesus. I was on Patmos island because of God’s word and my testimony about Jesus. 📖
## Revelation 1:10
On the Lord’s day, I was in the Spirit. Behind me I heard a loud voice like a trumpet, 📯
## Revelation 1:11
saying, “Write what you see on a scroll and send it to the seven churches. Send it to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.” 📜
## Revelation 1:12
I turned toward the voice speaking to me. I saw seven gold lampstands, ✨
## Revelation 1:13
and among them, One like the Son of Man. He wore a long robe and a gold sash around His chest. 👑
## Revelation 1:14
His hair was white like wool, white as snow. His eyes were like blazing fire. 🔥
## Revelation 1:15
His feet were like polished bronze refined in a furnace. His voice was like the roar of many waters. 🌊
## Revelation 1:16
He held seven stars in His right hand. A sharp sword with two edges came from His mouth. His face was like the sun at its brightest. ✨
## Revelation 1:17
I saw Him and fell at His feet like a dead man. He placed His right hand on me and said, “Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last, 🙏
## Revelation 1:18
the Living One. I was dead, but now I live forever and ever! I hold the keys of Death and Hades. 👑
## Revelation 1:19
Write what you have seen, what is now, and what will happen afterward. 📜
## Revelation 1:20
Here is the mystery of the seven stars in My right hand and the seven gold lampstands. The seven stars are the seven churches’ angels. The seven lampstands are the seven churches. ✨
