## Revelation 20:1
I saw an angel descend from heaven with the Abyss’s key and a great chain in his hand. 👼
## Revelation 20:2
He seized the dragon, the ancient serpent who is the devil and Satan. He bound him for a thousand years. ⚖️
## Revelation 20:3
He threw him into the Abyss, shut it, and sealed it over him. This stopped him from deceiving the nations until the thousand years ended. Afterward he must be released briefly. ⚖️
## Revelation 20:4
I saw thrones and people on them who had received authority to judge. I saw the souls of those beheaded for their testimony of Jesus and God’s word. I also saw those who had not worshiped the beast or its image or received its mark on their foreheads or hands. They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years. 👑
## Revelation 20:5
The other dead did not return to life until the thousand years ended. This is the first resurrection. 📖
## Revelation 20:6
Blessed and holy are those sharing in the first resurrection! The second death has no power over them. They will be priests of God and Christ and reign with Him for a thousand years. 👑
## Revelation 20:7
When the thousand years end, Satan will be released from prison, 📖
## Revelation 20:8
and deceive the nations at earth’s four corners, Gog and Magog. He will gather them for battle. They number like the sand on the seashore. ⚔️
## Revelation 20:9
They marched across the earth’s broad expanse and surrounded the saints’ camp and beloved city. Fire descended from heaven and consumed them. 🔥
## Revelation 20:10
The devil who deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur. The beast and false prophet had already been thrown there. They will suffer torment day and night forever and ever. ⚖️
## Revelation 20:11
I saw a great white throne and the One seated on it. Earth and heaven fled His presence, and no place remained for them. 👑
## Revelation 20:12
I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne. Books were opened, including the Book of Life. The dead were judged by their deeds recorded in the books. 📖
## Revelation 20:13
The sea gave up its dead. Death and Hades gave up their dead. Each person was judged by his deeds. ⚖️
## Revelation 20:14
Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. The lake of fire is the second death. ⚖️
## Revelation 20:15
Anyone whose name was not found in the Book of Life was thrown into the lake of fire. ⚖️
