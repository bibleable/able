## Revelation 9:1
The fifth angel blew his trumpet. I saw a star that had fallen from heaven to earth. It received the key to the Abyss’s pit. 🔑
## Revelation 9:2
The star opened the pit of the Abyss. Smoke rose like smoke from a great furnace. The pit’s smoke darkened the sun and air. 🌫️
## Revelation 9:3
Locusts came from the smoke onto the earth. They received power like the earth’s scorpions. ⚠️
## Revelation 9:4
They were told not to harm grass, plants, or trees. They could harm only people without God’s seal on their foreheads. ⚠️
## Revelation 9:5
The locusts could not kill them, but could torment them for five months. The torment was like a scorpion’s sting. ⚠️
## Revelation 9:6
In those days people will seek death but not find it. They will long to die, but death will escape them. ⚖️
## Revelation 9:7
The locusts looked like horses ready for battle. On their heads was something like gold crowns. Their faces were like human faces. 📖
## Revelation 9:8
They had hair like women’s hair and teeth like lions’ teeth. 📖
## Revelation 9:9
Their breastplates were like iron breastplates. Their wings sounded like the roar of many horses and chariots rushing to battle. 📖
## Revelation 9:10
Their tails had stingers like scorpions. With these they could injure people for five months. ⚠️
## Revelation 9:11
Their king was the angel of the Abyss. His Hebrew name is Abaddon. His Greek name is Apollyon. 📖
## Revelation 9:12
The first woe passed. Two more woes will follow. ⚠️
## Revelation 9:13
The sixth angel blew his trumpet. I heard a voice from the four horns of the gold altar before God, 📯
## Revelation 9:14
saying to the sixth angel with the trumpet, “Release the four angels bound at the great river Euphrates.” 👼
## Revelation 9:15
The four angels were released to kill a third of mankind. They had been prepared for this hour, day, month, and year. ⚖️
## Revelation 9:16
The mounted troops numbered two hundred million. I heard their number. 👥
## Revelation 9:17
In my vision, the riders wore breastplates colored like fire, sapphire, and sulfur. The horses’ heads were like lions’ heads. Fire, smoke, and sulfur came from their mouths. 🔥
## Revelation 9:18
These three plagues, fire, smoke, and sulfur from their mouths, killed a third of mankind. ⚖️
## Revelation 9:19
The horses’ power was in their mouths and tails. Their tails were like snakes with heads that could inflict harm. ⚠️
## Revelation 9:20
The people who survived the plagues did not repent of the works of their hands. They kept worshiping demons and idols made of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood. These idols cannot see, hear, or walk. ⚠️
## Revelation 9:21
They also refused to repent of murder, sorcery, sexual immorality, and theft. ⚠️
