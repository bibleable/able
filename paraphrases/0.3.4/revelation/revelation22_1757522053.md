## Revelation 22:1
The angel showed me a river of the water of life, clear as crystal. It flowed from the throne of God and the Lamb, 💧
## Revelation 22:2
down the city’s main street. On either side stood a tree of life, bearing twelve kinds of fruit with a fresh crop each month. Its leaves are for the nations’ healing. 🌳
## Revelation 22:3
No curse will remain. The throne of God and the Lamb will be in the city. His servants will worship Him. 🙏
## Revelation 22:4
They will see His face. His name will be on their foreheads. ❤️
## Revelation 22:5
There will be no more night. They will need no lamp or sunlight, because the Lord God will shine on them. They will reign forever and ever. 👑
## Revelation 22:6
The angel told me, “These words are faithful and true. The Lord is the God of the prophets’ spirits. He sent His angel to show His servants what must happen soon.” 📖
## Revelation 22:7
“I am coming soon. Blessed is the one who keeps this book’s prophetic words.” 📖
## Revelation 22:8
I, John, heard and saw these things. Afterward I fell to worship at the feet of the angel who showed them to me. 🙇
## Revelation 22:9
He said, “Do not do that! I am a fellow servant with you, your brothers the prophets, and those who keep this book’s words. Worship God!” 🙏
## Revelation 22:10
He told me, “Do not seal this book’s prophetic words. The time is near. 📖
## Revelation 22:11
Let the unrighteous remain unrighteous and the vile remain vile. Let the righteous keep practicing righteousness and the holy remain holy.” 📖
## Revelation 22:12
“I am coming soon. My reward is with Me, to give each person according to his deeds. 📖
## Revelation 22:13
I am the Alpha and Omega, the First and Last, the Beginning and End.” 👑
## Revelation 22:14
Blessed are those who wash their robes, so they may have the right to the tree of life and enter through the city’s gates. 🌳
## Revelation 22:15
Outside are dogs, sorcerers, the sexually immoral, murderers, idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices falsehood. ⚖️
## Revelation 22:16
“I, Jesus, sent My angel to give you this testimony for the churches. I am David’s Root and Offspring, the bright Morning Star.” ✨
## Revelation 22:17
The Spirit and bride say, “Come!” Let whoever hears say, “Come!” Let the thirsty come. Let anyone who desires the water of life drink freely. 💧
## Revelation 22:18
I testify to everyone hearing this book’s prophetic words. If anyone adds to them, God will add to him the plagues this book describes. ⚖️
## Revelation 22:19
If anyone removes words from this prophetic book, God will remove his share in the tree of life and holy city. This book describes both. ⚖️
## Revelation 22:20
The One testifying to these things says, “Yes, I am coming soon.” Amen. Come, Lord Jesus! 🙏
## Revelation 22:21
May the Lord Jesus’ grace be with all the saints. Amen. 🙏
