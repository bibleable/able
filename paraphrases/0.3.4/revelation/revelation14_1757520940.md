## Revelation 14:1
I saw the Lamb on Mount Zion with 144,000. His name and His Father’s name were written on their foreheads. 🐑
## Revelation 14:2
A sound from heaven was like roaring waters and loud thunder. It also sounded like harpists playing their harps. 🎶
## Revelation 14:3
They sang a new song before the throne, the four creatures, and the elders. Only the 144,000 redeemed from the earth could learn it. 🎶
## Revelation 14:4
They are virgins who have not been defiled with women. They follow the Lamb wherever He goes. They were redeemed from among people as firstfruits to God and the Lamb. 📖
## Revelation 14:5
No lie was found in their mouths. They are blameless. 📖
## Revelation 14:6
Another angel flew overhead with the eternal gospel. He would proclaim it to earth’s inhabitants, to every nation, tribe, language, and people. 👼
## Revelation 14:7
He said loudly, “Fear God and glorify Him, because His judgment’s hour has come. Worship Him who made heaven, earth, sea, and springs of water.” 🙏
## Revelation 14:8
A second angel followed, saying, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She made all nations drink the wine of the passion of her immorality.” ⚖️
## Revelation 14:9
A third angel followed and cried loudly, “If anyone worships the beast and its image and receives its mark on his forehead or hand, ⚠️
## Revelation 14:10
he will drink the wine of God’s anger, poured full strength into His cup of wrath. He will suffer torment in fire and sulfur before the holy angels and the Lamb. ⚖️
## Revelation 14:11
The smoke of their torment rises forever and ever. There is no rest, day or night, for those worshiping the beast and its image, or anyone receiving its name’s mark.” ⚖️
## Revelation 14:12
This calls for the saints’ perseverance. They keep God’s commandments and the faith of Jesus. 📖
## Revelation 14:13
A voice from heaven told me to write, “Blessed are those who die in the Lord from now on.” The Spirit says, “Yes, they will rest from their labors, because their deeds will follow them.” 📖
## Revelation 14:14
I saw a white cloud. One like the Son of Man sat on it with a gold crown on His head and a sharp sickle in His hand. 👑
## Revelation 14:15
An angel left the temple and called loudly to the One on the cloud. “Swing Your sickle and harvest,” he said. “The time has come, because the earth’s crop is ripe.” 🌾
## Revelation 14:16
The One on the cloud swung His sickle over the earth. The earth was harvested. 🌾
## Revelation 14:17
Another angel came from heaven’s temple, also holding a sharp sickle. 👼
## Revelation 14:18
Another angel, with authority over fire, came from the altar. He called loudly to the angel with the sharp sickle, “Swing your sickle and gather the grapes from earth’s vine. Its grapes are ripe.” 🍇
## Revelation 14:19
The angel swung his sickle over the earth and gathered its grapes. He threw them into the great winepress of God’s wrath. ⚖️
## Revelation 14:20
The winepress was trampled outside the city. Blood from it flowed as high as the horses’ bridles for a distance of 1,600 stadia. ⚖️
