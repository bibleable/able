## Revelation 5:1
I saw a scroll in the right hand of the One on the throne. Writing covered both sides, and seven seals closed it. 📜
## Revelation 5:2
A mighty angel proclaimed loudly, “Who is worthy to break the seals and open the scroll?” 👼
## Revelation 5:3
No one in heaven, on earth, or under the earth could open the scroll or look inside. 📜
## Revelation 5:4
I wept bitterly because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or look inside. 💔
## Revelation 5:5
An elder told me, “Do not weep! The Lion of Judah’s tribe, the Root of David, has triumphed. He can open the scroll and its seven seals.” 📜
## Revelation 5:6
I saw a Lamb who looked as if He had been slain, standing in the throne’s center, surrounded by the four creatures and elders. He had seven horns and seven eyes, which represent God’s seven spirits sent into all the earth. 🐑
## Revelation 5:7
He came and took the scroll from the right hand of the One seated on the throne. 📜
## Revelation 5:8
After He took the scroll, the four creatures and twenty-four elders fell before the Lamb. Each had a harp, and they held gold bowls of incense. These are the saints’ prayers. 🙏
## Revelation 5:9
They sang a new song: “You are worthy to take the scroll and open its seals, because You were slain. By Your blood You purchased people for God from every tribe, language, people, and nation. 🎶
## Revelation 5:10
You made them a kingdom and priests to serve our God. They will reign on the earth.” 👑
## Revelation 5:11
I looked and heard many angels around the throne, the living creatures, and the elders. They numbered myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands. 👼
## Revelation 5:12
They said loudly, “The slain Lamb is worthy to receive power, riches, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and blessing!” 🙏
## Revelation 5:13
I heard every creature in heaven, on earth, under the earth, and in the sea, and everything in them. They said, “Praise, honor, glory, and power to the One on the throne and to the Lamb forever and ever!” 🙏
## Revelation 5:14
The four creatures said, “Amen.” The elders fell down and worshiped. 🙇
