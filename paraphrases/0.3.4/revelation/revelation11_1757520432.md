## Revelation 11:1
I received a measuring rod like a staff. I was told, “Measure God’s temple and the altar. Count the worshipers there. 📏
## Revelation 11:2
Do not measure the outside courtyard. It has been given to the nations, who will trample the holy city for 42 months. 📖
## Revelation 11:3
I will empower my two witnesses. They will prophesy for 1,260 days, wearing sackcloth.” 👥
## Revelation 11:4
These witnesses are the two olive trees and two lampstands standing before the Lord of the earth. 📖
## Revelation 11:5
If anyone wants to harm them, fire comes from their mouths and consumes their enemies. Anyone who wants to harm them must die this way. 🔥
## Revelation 11:6
They can shut the sky to stop rain during their prophecy. They can turn water to blood and strike the earth with every kind of plague as often as they wish. ⚖️
## Revelation 11:7
When the two witnesses finish their testimony, the beast from the Abyss will fight them. It will overpower and kill them. ⚔️
## Revelation 11:8
Their bodies will lie in the great city’s street, where their Lord was crucified. The city is figuratively called Sodom and Egypt. 📖
## Revelation 11:9
For three and a half days, all peoples, tribes, languages, and nations will look at their bodies. They will not allow burial in a tomb. 📖
## Revelation 11:10
Earth’s inhabitants will celebrate their deaths and exchange gifts. These two prophets had tormented them. 🎁
## Revelation 11:11
After three and a half days, God’s breath of life entered the two witnesses. They stood on their feet. Everyone watching was greatly afraid. 📖
## Revelation 11:12
They heard a loud voice from heaven say, “Come up here.” They rose to heaven in a cloud while their enemies watched. ☁️
## Revelation 11:13
That hour a great earthquake collapsed a tenth of the city and killed seven thousand. The survivors were terrified and gave glory to heaven’s God. ⚖️
## Revelation 11:14
The second woe passed. The third is coming soon. ⚠️
## Revelation 11:15
The seventh angel blew his trumpet. Loud voices in heaven said, “The world’s kingdom has become the kingdom of our Lord and His Christ. He will reign forever and ever.” 👑
## Revelation 11:16
The twenty-four elders seated on thrones before God fell facedown and worshiped Him, 🙇
## Revelation 11:17
saying, “We thank You, Lord God Almighty, who is and was. You have taken Your great power and begun to reign. 🙏
## Revelation 11:18
The nations were angry, and Your wrath has come. It is time to judge the dead and reward Your servants the prophets, the saints, and those who fear Your name, small and great. It is time to destroy those who destroy the earth.” ⚖️
## Revelation 11:19
God’s temple in heaven opened, and the ark of His covenant appeared inside. Lightning, rumblings, thunder, an earthquake, and a great hailstorm followed. 🏛️
