## Revelation 7:1
I saw four angels at the earth’s four corners, holding back its four winds. No wind could blow on land, sea, or any tree. 👼
## Revelation 7:2
Another angel rose from the east with the living God’s seal. He called loudly to the four angels given power to harm land and sea, 👼
## Revelation 7:3
“Do not harm the land, sea, or trees until we seal our God’s servants on their foreheads.” 📖
## Revelation 7:4
I heard the number sealed: 144,000 from all Israel’s tribes. 👥
## Revelation 7:5
From Judah’s tribe, 12,000 were sealed. From Reuben’s tribe, 12,000. From Gad’s tribe, 12,000. 👥
## Revelation 7:6
From Asher’s tribe, 12,000. From Naphtali’s tribe, 12,000. From Manasseh’s tribe, 12,000. 👥
## Revelation 7:7
From Simeon’s tribe, 12,000. From Levi’s tribe, 12,000. From Issachar’s tribe, 12,000. 👥
## Revelation 7:8
From Zebulun’s tribe, 12,000. From Joseph’s tribe, 12,000. From Benjamin’s tribe, 12,000. 👥
## Revelation 7:9
I saw a crowd too large to count from every nation, tribe, people, and language. They stood before the throne and Lamb, wearing white robes and holding palm branches. 🤍
## Revelation 7:10
They cried loudly, “Salvation belongs to our God, who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!” 🙏
## Revelation 7:11
All the angels stood around the throne, elders, and four living creatures. They fell facedown before the throne and worshiped God, 🙇
## Revelation 7:12
saying, “Amen! Blessing, glory, wisdom, thanks, honor, power, and strength to our God forever and ever! Amen.” 🙏
## Revelation 7:13
An elder asked me, “Who are these people in white robes? Where did they come from?” ❓
## Revelation 7:14
I answered, “Sir, you know.” He said, “They came out of the great tribulation. They washed their robes and made them white in the Lamb’s blood. 🤍
## Revelation 7:15
That is why they stand before God’s throne and serve Him day and night in His temple. The One on the throne will spread His tabernacle over them. 🏛️
## Revelation 7:16
They will never hunger or thirst again. The sun will not beat on them, nor will any scorching heat. ☀️
## Revelation 7:17
The Lamb in the throne’s center will be their shepherd. He will lead them to springs of living water. God will wipe every tear from their eyes.” ❤️
