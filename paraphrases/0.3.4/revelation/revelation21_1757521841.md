## Revelation 21:1
I saw a new heaven and new earth. The first heaven and earth had passed away, and the sea was gone. 📖
## Revelation 21:2
I saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, descending from God out of heaven. It was prepared like a bride adorned for her husband. 🏙️
## Revelation 21:3
A loud voice from the throne said, “God’s dwelling place is with people. He will live with them. They will be His people, and God Himself will be with them as their God. ❤️
## Revelation 21:4
He will wipe every tear from their eyes. Death, mourning, crying, and pain will be gone, because the former things passed away.” ❤️
## Revelation 21:5
The One on the throne said, “I make everything new.” Then He said, “Write this, because these words are faithful and true.” 📜
## Revelation 21:6
He told me, “It is done! I am the Alpha and Omega, the Beginning and End. To the thirsty I will freely give from the spring of the water of life. 💧
## Revelation 21:7
The one who overcomes will inherit everything. I will be his God, and he will be My son. ❤️
## Revelation 21:8
The cowardly, unbelieving, detestable, murderers, sexually immoral, sorcerers, idolaters, and all liars will have their place in the lake burning with fire and sulfur. This is the second death.” ⚖️
## Revelation 21:9
One of the seven angels with seven bowls full of the seven final plagues approached. He said, “Come. I will show you the bride, the Lamb’s wife.” 👼
## Revelation 21:10
In the Spirit, he carried me to a great, high mountain. He showed me the holy city Jerusalem descending from God out of heaven, 🏙️
## Revelation 21:11
shining with God’s glory. Its brilliance was like a very precious jewel, like jasper clear as crystal. ✨
## Revelation 21:12
The city had a great, high wall with twelve gates and twelve angels at them. The gates bore the names of Israel’s twelve tribes. 🏙️
## Revelation 21:13
Three gates faced east, three north, three south, and three west. 🏙️
## Revelation 21:14
The city wall had twelve foundations bearing the names of the Lamb’s twelve apostles. 🏙️
## Revelation 21:15
The angel speaking to me had a gold measuring rod to measure the city, its gates, and walls. 📏
## Revelation 21:16
The city was square, its width equal to its length. He measured it with the rod: 12,000 stadia in length, width, and height, all equal. 📏
## Revelation 21:17
He measured its wall at 144 cubits, using human measurement. 📏
## Revelation 21:18
The wall was jasper. The city was pure gold, as pure as glass. ✨
## Revelation 21:19
Every kind of precious stone adorned the wall’s foundations. The first was jasper, the second sapphire, the third chalcedony, and the fourth emerald, ✨
## Revelation 21:20
the fifth sardonyx, sixth carnelian, seventh chrysolite, eighth beryl, ninth topaz, tenth chrysoprase, eleventh jacinth, and twelfth amethyst. ✨
## Revelation 21:21
The twelve gates were twelve pearls, each gate a single pearl. The city’s main street was pure gold, clear as glass. ✨
## Revelation 21:22
I saw no temple there. The Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple. 🏛️
## Revelation 21:23
The city needs no sun or moon. God’s glory lights it, and the Lamb is its lamp. ✨
## Revelation 21:24
The nations will walk by its light. Earth’s kings will bring their glory into it. 👑
## Revelation 21:25
Its gates will never close at day’s end, because there will be no night. 🏙️
## Revelation 21:26
The nations’ glory and honor will be brought into the city. 👑
## Revelation 21:27
Nothing unclean will ever enter, nor anyone practicing detestable things or lies. Only those named in the Lamb’s Book of Life may enter. 📖
