## Revelation 6:1
I watched the Lamb open one of the seven seals. One of the four living creatures said with a voice like thunder, “Come!” 📜
## Revelation 6:2
I saw a white horse. Its rider held a bow and received a crown. He rode out to overcome and conquer. 🐎
## Revelation 6:3
The Lamb opened the second seal. I heard the second creature say, “Come!” 📜
## Revelation 6:4
Another horse appeared, bright red. Its rider received permission to take peace from the earth and make people kill each other. He received a great sword. ⚔️
## Revelation 6:5
The Lamb opened the third seal. The third creature said, “Come!” I saw a black horse whose rider held a pair of scales. ⚖️
## Revelation 6:6
I heard what sounded like a voice among the four creatures. It said, “A quart of wheat for a denarius, and three quarts of barley for a denarius. Do not harm the oil and wine.” 🌾
## Revelation 6:7
The Lamb opened the fourth seal. The fourth creature said, “Come!” 📜
## Revelation 6:8
I saw a pale green horse. Its rider was named Death, and Hades followed closely. They received authority over a fourth of the earth to kill by sword, famine, plague, and wild beasts. 🐎
## Revelation 6:9
The Lamb opened the fifth seal. Under the altar, I saw the souls of those slain for God’s word and the testimony they upheld. 📖
## Revelation 6:10
They cried loudly, “Holy and true Lord, how long until You judge those living on earth and avenge our blood?” 🙏
## Revelation 6:11
Each received a white robe. They were told to rest a little longer until the full number of their fellow servants, their brothers, were killed as they were. 🤍
## Revelation 6:12
I watched the Lamb open the sixth seal, and a great earthquake came. The sun became black like sackcloth made of goat hair. The whole moon turned blood red, 🌑
## Revelation 6:13
and the stars fell to earth like unripe figs dropping from a tree shaken by strong wind. ✨
## Revelation 6:14
The sky receded like a scroll rolling up. Every mountain and island moved from its place. 📜
## Revelation 6:15
The earth’s kings, nobles, commanders, rich, mighty, and every slave and free person hid in caves and among mountain rocks. 🪨
## Revelation 6:16
They told the mountains and rocks, “Fall on us! Hide us from the face of the One on the throne and from the Lamb’s wrath. ⚖️
## Revelation 6:17
The great day of Their wrath has come. Who can withstand it?” ⚖️
