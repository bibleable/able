## Revelation 19:1
I heard a sound like a great crowd roaring in heaven. They shouted, “Hallelujah! Salvation, glory, and power belong to our God! 🙏
## Revelation 19:2
His judgments are true and just. He judged the great prostitute who corrupted the earth through her immorality. He avenged His servants’ blood, which she spilled.” ⚖️
## Revelation 19:3
They called a second time, “Hallelujah! Her smoke rises forever and ever.” 🙏
## Revelation 19:4
The twenty-four elders and four creatures fell and worshiped God seated on the throne. They said, “Amen, Hallelujah!” 🙇
## Revelation 19:5
A voice from the throne said, “Praise our God, all His servants and everyone who fears Him, small and great!” 🙏
## Revelation 19:6
I heard a sound like a great crowd, rushing waters, and mighty thunder. It cried, “Hallelujah! The Lord our God the Almighty reigns. 👑
## Revelation 19:7
Let us rejoice, be glad, and glorify Him. The Lamb’s marriage has come, and His bride made herself ready. 🎶
## Revelation 19:8
She received fine linen, bright and pure, to wear.” The fine linen is the saints’ righteous acts. 🤍
## Revelation 19:9
The angel told me to write, “Blessed are those invited to the Lamb’s marriage supper.” He said, “These are God’s true words.” 📖
## Revelation 19:10
I fell at his feet to worship him. He said, “Do not do that! I am a fellow servant with you and your brothers who rely on Jesus’ testimony. Worship God! The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.” 🙏
## Revelation 19:11
I saw heaven open and a white horse before me. Its rider is called Faithful and True. He judges and fights with righteousness. 🐎
## Revelation 19:12
His eyes are like blazing fire, and many royal crowns are on His head. A name is written on Him that only He knows. 👑
## Revelation 19:13
He wears a robe dipped in blood. His name is The Word of God. 📖
## Revelation 19:14
Heaven’s armies follow Him on white horses. They wear fine linen, white and pure. 🐎
## Revelation 19:15
A sharp sword comes from His mouth to strike the nations. He will rule them with an iron scepter. He treads the winepress of God the Almighty’s furious wrath. ⚔️
## Revelation 19:16
On His robe and thigh is written this name: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS. 👑
## Revelation 19:17
An angel stood in the sun and called loudly to all birds overhead. “Gather for God’s great supper, 📖
## Revelation 19:18
to eat the flesh of kings, commanders, mighty men, horses, and riders, and of everyone, slave and free, small and great.” 📖
## Revelation 19:19
I saw the beast, earth’s kings, and their armies gathered to fight the One on the horse and His army. ⚔️
## Revelation 19:20
The beast and false prophet were captured. The false prophet’s signs for the beast deceived those bearing its mark and worshiping its image. Both were thrown alive into the fiery lake of burning sulfur. ⚖️
## Revelation 19:21
The sword from the rider’s mouth killed the rest. All the birds ate their flesh until full. ⚖️
