## Revelation 3:1
“Write to the angel of Sardis’s church. These are the words of the One who holds God’s seven spirits and the seven stars. I know your deeds. People say you are alive, but you are dead. ⚠️
## Revelation 3:2
Wake up and strengthen what remains, which is about to die. I have found your deeds incomplete before My God. ⚠️
## Revelation 3:3
Remember what you received and heard. Keep it and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief at an hour you do not know. ⚠️
## Revelation 3:4
A few in Sardis have not soiled their garments. They will walk with Me in white because they are worthy. 🤍
## Revelation 3:5
The one who overcomes will likewise wear white. I will never erase his name from the Book of Life. I will acknowledge his name before My Father and His angels. 📖
## Revelation 3:6
Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖
## Revelation 3:7
Write to the angel of Philadelphia’s church. These are the words of the holy and true One who holds David’s key. No one can shut what He opens or open what He shuts. 🔑
## Revelation 3:8
I know your deeds. I placed an open door before you that no one can shut. You have little strength, but you kept My word and did not deny My name. 🚪
## Revelation 3:9
Some belong to Satan’s synagogue and falsely claim to be Jews. I will make them come and bow at your feet. They will know I love you. ❤️
## Revelation 3:10
Because you kept My command to persevere, I will keep you from the hour of testing. It will soon come on the whole world to test those living on earth. 📖
## Revelation 3:11
I am coming soon. Hold firmly to what you have so no one takes your crown. 👑
## Revelation 3:12
I will make the one who overcomes a pillar in My God’s temple. He will never leave it again. I will write My God’s name on him and the name of My God’s city, the new Jerusalem. That city comes down from My God out of heaven. I will also write My new name on him. 🏛️
## Revelation 3:13
Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖
## Revelation 3:14
Write to the angel of Laodicea’s church. These are the words of the Amen, the faithful and true Witness, the Originator of God’s creation. 📖
## Revelation 3:15
I know your deeds. You are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were one or the other! ⚠️
## Revelation 3:16
Because you are lukewarm, neither hot nor cold, I am about to vomit you from My mouth! ⚠️
## Revelation 3:17
You say, ‘I am rich and wealthy. I need nothing.’ You do not realize you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind, and naked. ⚠️
## Revelation 3:18
I advise you to buy gold from Me, refined by fire, so you may become rich. Buy white garments to cover yourself and hide your shameful nakedness. Buy salve to put on your eyes so you may see. 📖
## Revelation 3:19
I rebuke and discipline those I love. Be earnest and repent. ❤️
## Revelation 3:20
I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in and eat with him, and he with Me. 🚪
## Revelation 3:21
I will let the one who overcomes sit with Me on My throne. I also overcame and sat with My Father on His throne. 👑
## Revelation 3:22
Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.” 📖
