## Revelation 2:1
“Write to the angel of Ephesus’s church. These are the words of Him who holds the seven stars in His right hand. He walks among the seven gold lampstands. ✨
## Revelation 2:2
I know your deeds, hard work, and perseverance. You cannot tolerate evil people. You tested those who falsely claimed to be apostles and exposed their lies. 📖
## Revelation 2:3
You persevered and endured many things for My name without growing weary. 💪
## Revelation 2:4
But I have this against you: You abandoned your first love. ⚠️
## Revelation 2:5
Remember how far you have fallen. Repent and do what you did at first. If you do not repent, I will come and remove your lampstand from its place. ⚖️
## Revelation 2:6
But this is to your credit: You hate the Nicolaitans’ works, which I also hate. 📖
## Revelation 2:7
Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. I will give the one who overcomes the right to eat from the tree of life in God’s Paradise. 🌳
## Revelation 2:8
Write to the angel of Smyrna’s church. These are the words of the First and the Last, who died and returned to life. 📖
## Revelation 2:9
I know your suffering and poverty, though you are rich! I know the slander of those who falsely claim to be Jews. They are a synagogue of Satan. ⚠️
## Revelation 2:10
Do not fear what you will suffer. The devil will soon imprison some of you to test you. You will suffer tribulation for ten days. Be faithful even to death, and I will give you the crown of life. 👑
## Revelation 2:11
Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The second death will not harm the one who overcomes. 🙏
## Revelation 2:12
Write to the angel of Pergamum’s church. These are the words of the One who holds the sharp sword with two edges. ⚔️
## Revelation 2:13
I know where you live, where Satan’s throne is. Yet you hold firmly to My name. You did not deny your faith in Me, even in the days of My faithful witness Antipas. He was killed among you, where Satan lives. 📖
## Revelation 2:14
I have a few things against you. Some follow Balaam’s teaching. He taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the Israelites so they would eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual immorality. ⚠️
## Revelation 2:15
Likewise, some of you follow the Nicolaitans’ teaching. ⚠️
## Revelation 2:16
Repent! Otherwise I will come soon and fight against them with the sword of My mouth. ⚔️
## Revelation 2:17
Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. I will give the one who overcomes hidden manna. I will also give him a white stone bearing a new name, known only to its recipient. 📖
## Revelation 2:18
Write to the angel of Thyatira’s church. These are the Son of God’s words. His eyes are like blazing fire, and His feet are like polished bronze. 🔥
## Revelation 2:19
I know your deeds: your love, faith, service, and perseverance. Your latest deeds are greater than your first. ❤️
## Revelation 2:20
But I have this against you: You tolerate the woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess. Her teaching misleads My servants to commit sexual immorality and eat food sacrificed to idols. ⚠️
## Revelation 2:21
I gave her time to repent of her immorality, but she refuses. ⚠️
## Revelation 2:22
I will throw her onto a bed of sickness. Those who commit adultery with her will suffer great tribulation unless they repent of her deeds. ⚖️
## Revelation 2:23
I will strike her children dead. All the churches will know I search minds and hearts. I will repay each of you according to your deeds. ⚖️
## Revelation 2:24
The rest of you in Thyatira do not follow her teaching and have not learned the so-called deep things of Satan. I will put no further burden on you, 📖
## Revelation 2:25
except to hold firmly to what you have until I come. 💪
## Revelation 2:26
I will give authority over the nations to the one who overcomes and continues in My work to the end. 👑
## Revelation 2:27
He will rule them with an iron scepter and shatter them like pottery, just as I received authority from My Father. ⚖️
## Revelation 2:28
I will give him the morning star. ✨
## Revelation 2:29
Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. 📖
