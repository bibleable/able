## Revelation 10:1
I saw another mighty angel descending from heaven, wrapped in a cloud, with a rainbow above his head. His face was like the sun. His legs were like pillars of fire. 🌈
## Revelation 10:2
He held a small open scroll. His right foot stood on the sea, and his left foot on the land. 📜
## Revelation 10:3
He cried loudly like a roaring lion. The seven thunders answered with their voices. 📖
## Revelation 10:4
I was about to write what the seven thunders said. A voice from heaven told me, “Seal up their words. Do not write them.” 📜
## Revelation 10:5
The angel standing on sea and land raised his right hand toward heaven. 👼
## Revelation 10:6
He swore by Him who lives forever and ever, who created heaven, earth, sea, and everything in them. He said, “There will be no more delay! 📖
## Revelation 10:7
When the seventh angel begins to blow his trumpet, God’s mystery will be fulfilled. It will happen as He announced to His servants the prophets.” 📯
## Revelation 10:8
The voice from heaven spoke to me again. “Take the small open scroll from the angel standing on sea and land,” it said. 📜
## Revelation 10:9
I asked the angel, “Give me the small scroll.” He said, “Take it and eat it. It will make your stomach bitter, but taste sweet as honey in your mouth.” 📜
## Revelation 10:10
I took the scroll from his hand and ate it. It tasted sweet as honey in my mouth. After I ate it, my stomach turned bitter. 📖
## Revelation 10:11
They told me, “You must prophesy again about many peoples, nations, languages, and kings.” 📖
