## Revelation 13:1
I saw a beast rise from the sea with ten horns and seven heads. Ten royal crowns were on its horns, and blasphemous names on its heads. 📖
## Revelation 13:2
The beast was like a leopard, with a bear’s feet and a lion’s mouth. The dragon gave it his power, throne, and great authority. ⚠️
## Revelation 13:3
One head seemed to have a fatal wound. The fatal wound healed, and the whole world marveled and followed the beast. ⚠️
## Revelation 13:4
They worshiped the dragon who gave the beast authority. They worshiped the beast, saying, “Who is like it? Who can fight it?” ⚠️
## Revelation 13:5
The beast received a mouth to speak arrogant and blasphemous words. It received authority to act for 42 months. ⚠️
## Revelation 13:6
It spoke blasphemies against God. It slandered His name and His tabernacle, those who live in heaven. ⚠️
## Revelation 13:7
The beast received permission to fight and conquer the saints. It received authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation. ⚔️
## Revelation 13:8
All earth’s inhabitants whose names have not been written from the world’s foundation in the slain Lamb’s Book of Life will worship the beast. 📖
## Revelation 13:9
Let anyone with an ear hear: 📖
## Revelation 13:10
“If anyone is destined for captivity, he will go into captivity. If anyone is to die by the sword, he must be killed by the sword.” This calls for the saints’ perseverance and faith. 📖
## Revelation 13:11
I saw another beast rise from the earth. It had two horns like a lamb but spoke like a dragon. 📖
## Revelation 13:12
It exercised all the first beast’s authority. It made the earth and its inhabitants worship the first beast, whose fatal wound healed. ⚠️
## Revelation 13:13
The second beast performed great signs. It even made fire fall from heaven to earth before the people. 🔥
## Revelation 13:14
It deceived earth’s inhabitants through signs it was given to perform for the first beast. It told them to make an image of the beast that survived a sword wound. ⚠️
## Revelation 13:15
The second beast received permission to give the first beast’s image breath. The image could speak and cause everyone who refused to worship it to be killed. ⚠️
## Revelation 13:16
The second beast required everyone, small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, to receive a mark on their right hand or forehead, ⚠️
## Revelation 13:17
so no one could buy or sell without it. The mark was the beast’s name or the number of its name. 📖
## Revelation 13:18
This calls for wisdom. Let the person with insight calculate the beast’s number. It is the number of a man, and that number is 666. 📖
