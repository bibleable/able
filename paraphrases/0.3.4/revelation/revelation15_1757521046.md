## Revelation 15:1
I saw another great and marvelous sign in heaven. Seven angels had the seven final plagues, which complete God’s wrath. 👼
## Revelation 15:2
I saw something like a sea of glass mixed with fire. Beside it stood those who conquered the beast, its image, and its name’s number. They held harps from God, 🎶
## Revelation 15:3
and sang the song of God’s servant Moses and of the Lamb. “Lord God Almighty, Your works are great and wonderful! King of the nations, Your ways are just and true! 🎶
## Revelation 15:4
Who will not fear You, Lord, and glorify Your name? You alone are holy. All nations will come and worship before You, because Your righteous acts have been revealed.” 🙏
## Revelation 15:5
I looked, and the temple in heaven opened. It is the tabernacle of the Testimony. 🏛️
## Revelation 15:6
The seven angels with seven plagues came from the temple. They wore clean, bright linen and gold sashes around their chests. 👼
## Revelation 15:7
One of the four creatures gave the seven angels seven gold bowls. The bowls were full of the wrath of God, who lives forever and ever. ⚖️
## Revelation 15:8
God’s glory and power filled the temple with smoke. No one could enter until the seven angels’ seven plagues were completed. 🏛️
