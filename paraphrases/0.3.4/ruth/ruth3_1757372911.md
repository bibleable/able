## Ruth 3:1
One day, Naomi said to Ruth, “My daughter, should I not find a home where you can rest and live well? 🏠
## Ruth 3:2
Boaz, whose servant girls you worked with, is our relative. Tonight, he will separate barley from the chaff on the threshing floor. 🌾
## Ruth 3:3
Wash yourself, put on perfume, and wear your best clothes. Go down to the threshing floor. Do not let Boaz know you are there until he finishes his food and drink. 👣
## Ruth 3:4
When he lies down, note the place. Go there, uncover his feet, and lie down. He will tell you what to do.” 💬
## Ruth 3:5
Ruth replied, “I will do everything you say.” 🤝
## Ruth 3:6
She went down to the threshing floor and did everything her mother-in-law had instructed. 👣
## Ruth 3:7
Boaz finished his food and drink and felt happy. He lay down at the end of the grain pile. Ruth went in secretly, uncovered his feet, and lay down. 🌙
## Ruth 3:8
At midnight, Boaz woke with a start and turned over. A woman lay at his feet! 🌙
## Ruth 3:9
He asked, “Who are you?” She replied, “I am your servant Ruth. Spread the corner of your garment over me, because you are a family redeemer.” 🤝
## Ruth 3:10
Boaz said, “May the LORD bless you, my daughter. You have shown more kindness now than before. You did not pursue younger men, whether rich or poor. ❤️
## Ruth 3:11
Do not be afraid, my daughter. I will do everything you ask. Everyone in my town knows that you are a woman of good character. 🛡️
## Ruth 3:12
It is true that I am a family redeemer. But another redeemer is a closer relative than I am. 👤
## Ruth 3:13
Stay here tonight. If he wants to redeem you in the morning, good. Let him do so. But if he refuses, I will redeem you, as surely as the LORD lives. Lie here until morning.” 🌙
## Ruth 3:14
Ruth lay at his feet until morning. She got up before anyone could recognize her. Boaz said, “Do not let anyone know that a woman came to the threshing floor.” 🌅
## Ruth 3:15
He said, “Bring your shawl and hold it out.” She did so. He poured six measures of barley into it and placed the load on her. Then he went into the city. 🌾
## Ruth 3:16
Ruth returned to her mother-in-law. Naomi asked, “How did it go, my daughter?” Ruth told her everything Boaz had done for her. 💬
## Ruth 3:17
She said, “He gave me these six measures of barley. He said, ‘Do not return to your mother-in-law empty-handed.’” 🌾
## Ruth 3:18
Naomi said, “Wait, my daughter, until you learn what happens. He will not rest until he settles this matter today.” ⏳
