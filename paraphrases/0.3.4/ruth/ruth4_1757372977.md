## Ruth 4:1
Boaz went to the town gate and sat down. Soon the family redeemer he had mentioned came past. Boaz said, “Come here, my friend, and sit down.” The man came and sat down. 🏘️
## Ruth 4:2
Boaz called ten town elders and said, “Sit here.” They sat down. 👥
## Ruth 4:3
Boaz said to the family redeemer, “Naomi has returned from Moab. She is selling the land that belonged to our brother Elimelech. 🌾
## Ruth 4:4
I thought I should tell you. You can buy it back before these people and the elders of my people. If you want to redeem it, do so. If you will not, tell me. You have the first right, and I am next.” The man replied, “I will redeem it.” 🤝
## Ruth 4:5
Boaz said, “When you buy the land from Naomi and Ruth from Moab, you must also take the dead man’s widow. This will preserve the dead man’s name through his inheritance.” 💍
## Ruth 4:6
The family redeemer replied, “I cannot redeem it, because I could put my own inheritance at risk. Take my right to redeem it. I cannot do it.” 💬
## Ruth 4:7
In earlier times in Israel, a man removed his sandal and gave it to the other person to confirm a property agreement. This made an agreement to redeem or exchange property legally binding. 👡
## Ruth 4:8
So the family redeemer removed his sandal. He told Boaz, “Buy it for yourself.” 👡
## Ruth 4:9
Boaz said to the elders and all the people, “You are witnesses today. I am buying from Naomi everything that belonged to Elimelech, Chilion, and Mahlon. 👥
## Ruth 4:10
I have also taken Ruth from Moab, Mahlon’s widow, as my wife. This will preserve the dead man’s name through his inheritance. His name will not disappear from his relatives or the gate of his hometown. You are witnesses today.” 💍
## Ruth 4:11
The elders and everyone at the gate said, “We are witnesses. May the LORD make this woman like Rachel and Leah, who together built Israel’s family. May you prosper in Ephrathah and become famous in Bethlehem. 🙏
## Ruth 4:12
May your family become like the family of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah. May this happen through the children the LORD gives you by this young woman.” 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Ruth 4:13
Boaz took Ruth as his wife. He had sexual relations with her, and the LORD enabled her to become pregnant. She gave birth to a son. 👶
## Ruth 4:14
The women said to Naomi, “Praise the LORD. He has not left you today without a family redeemer. May this child become famous in Israel. 🙏
## Ruth 4:15
He will renew your life and support you in your old age. Your daughter-in-law, who loves you and is better to you than seven sons, has given birth to him.” ❤️
## Ruth 4:16
Naomi took the child, held him on her lap, and cared for him. 👶
## Ruth 4:17
The neighbor women said, “A son has been born to Naomi.” They named him Obed. He became the father of Jesse, who became the father of David. 👶
## Ruth 4:18
This is the family line of Perez: Perez was the father of Hezron. 🌳
## Ruth 4:19
Hezron was the father of Ram. Ram was the father of Amminadab. 🌳
## Ruth 4:20
Amminadab was the father of Nahshon. Nahshon was the father of Salmon. 🌳
## Ruth 4:21
Salmon was the father of Boaz. Boaz was the father of Obed. 🌳
## Ruth 4:22
Obed was the father of Jesse. Jesse was the father of David. 🌳
