## Ruth 1:1
When the judges ruled, a famine struck the land. A man from Bethlehem in Judah went to live in Moab with his wife and two sons. 🌾
## Ruth 1:2
His name was Elimelech, and his wife was Naomi. Their sons were Mahlon and Chilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah. They went to Moab and settled there. 🏠
## Ruth 1:3
Naomi’s husband Elimelech died. She was left with her two sons. 😢
## Ruth 1:4
Her sons married women from Moab. One woman was Orpah, and the other was Ruth. They lived in Moab for about ten years. 💍
## Ruth 1:5
Then Mahlon and Chilion also died. Naomi was left without her two sons and her husband. 💔
## Ruth 1:6
In Moab, Naomi heard that the LORD had cared for His people by giving them food. She and her daughters-in-law prepared to leave Moab. 🍞
## Ruth 1:7
Naomi left the place where she had lived. Her two daughters-in-law went with her on the road back to Judah. 👣
## Ruth 1:8
Naomi said to them, “Each of you, go back to your mother’s home. May the LORD show you faithful love, as you showed to your dead husbands and to me. ❤️
## Ruth 1:9
May the LORD give each of you rest in the home of a new husband.” Then she kissed them, and they cried aloud. 😢
## Ruth 1:10
They said, “We will surely go back with you to your people.” 🤝
## Ruth 1:11
But Naomi replied, “Go home, my daughters. Why would you come with me? Do I still have sons in my womb who could become your husbands? 💬
## Ruth 1:12
Go home, my daughters. I am too old to have another husband. Suppose I still had hope and could have a husband tonight and give birth to sons. ⏳
## Ruth 1:13
Would you wait for them to grow up? Would you remain without husbands? No, my daughters. My pain is much more bitter than yours, because the LORD’s hand has turned against me.” 💔
## Ruth 1:14
They cried aloud again. Orpah kissed her mother-in-law goodbye, but Ruth held tightly to her. 🤝
## Ruth 1:15
Naomi said, “Look, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and her gods. Follow her home.” 👣
## Ruth 1:16
But Ruth replied, “Do not urge me to leave you or turn away from you. Where you go, I will go. Where you live, I will live. Your people will be my people, and your God will be my God. ❤️
## Ruth 1:17
Where you die, I will die, and I will be buried there. May the LORD punish me very severely if anything except death separates us.” 🤝
## Ruth 1:18
Naomi saw that Ruth was determined to go with her. So she stopped trying to persuade her. 👣
## Ruth 1:19
Naomi and Ruth traveled to Bethlehem. Their arrival stirred the whole town. The women asked, “Can this be Naomi?” 🏘️
## Ruth 1:20
She replied, “Do not call me Naomi. Call me Mara, because the Almighty has made my life very bitter. 💔
## Ruth 1:21
I left full, but the LORD brought me back empty. Why call me Naomi? The LORD has testified against me, and the Almighty has brought trouble on me.” 😢
## Ruth 1:22
So Naomi returned from Moab with her daughter-in-law Ruth, a woman from Moab. They arrived in Bethlehem at the start of the barley harvest. 🌾
