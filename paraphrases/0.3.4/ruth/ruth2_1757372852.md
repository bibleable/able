## Ruth 2:1
Naomi had a relative on her husband’s side named Boaz. He was an important man of good character from Elimelech’s family group. 👤
## Ruth 2:2
Ruth from Moab said to Naomi, “Please let me go to the fields to collect grain left after the harvest. Perhaps someone will allow me to do this.” Naomi replied, “Go, my daughter.” 🌾
## Ruth 2:3
Ruth went to a field and collected grain behind the harvest workers. She happened to come to the part that belonged to Boaz, who was from Elimelech’s family group. 🌾
## Ruth 2:4
Just then, Boaz arrived from Bethlehem. He said to the harvest workers, “May the LORD be with you.” They replied, “May the LORD bless you.” 🙏
## Ruth 2:5
Boaz asked the leader of his harvest workers, “Whose young woman is this?” 💬
## Ruth 2:6
The leader replied, “She is the woman from Moab who returned with Naomi from that land. 👣
## Ruth 2:7
She asked, ‘Please let me collect grain among the bundles behind the harvest workers.’ She has worked from morning until now, with only a short rest in the shelter.” 🌾
## Ruth 2:8
Boaz said to Ruth, “Listen, my daughter. Do not collect grain in another field. Stay here, close to my servant girls. 🌾
## Ruth 2:9
Watch the field where they harvest, and follow the girls. I have ordered the young men not to touch you. When you are thirsty, drink from the jars the young men have filled.” 💧
## Ruth 2:10
Ruth bowed with her face to the ground. She asked, “Why have you shown me such kindness and noticed me, even though I am a foreigner?” 🙏
## Ruth 2:11
Boaz replied, “I have heard all you have done for your mother-in-law since your husband died. You left your father, mother, and homeland. You came to live among people you did not know before. ❤️
## Ruth 2:12
May the LORD repay your work. May you receive a rich reward from the LORD, the God of Israel. You have come for shelter under His wings.” 🛡️
## Ruth 2:13
She said, “My lord, may you continue to show me kindness. You have comforted me and spoken kindly to your servant, though I am not like one of your servant girls.” ❤️
## Ruth 2:14
At mealtime, Boaz said, “Come here. Eat some bread and dip it in the vinegar sauce.” Ruth sat beside the harvest workers, and he gave her roasted grain. She ate until she had enough, with some left over. 🍞
## Ruth 2:15
When Ruth stood up to collect grain, Boaz ordered his young men, “Do not insult her, even if she collects grain among the bundles. 🌾
## Ruth 2:16
Pull some stalks out of the bundles and leave them for her to collect. Do not scold her.” 🌾
## Ruth 2:17
Ruth collected grain in the field until evening. She beat the grain out of the stalks. She had about an ephah of barley. 🌾
## Ruth 2:18
Ruth carried the grain into town, and her mother-in-law saw what she had collected. Ruth also gave Naomi the food left from her meal. 🍞
## Ruth 2:19
Naomi asked, “Where did you collect grain and work today? May the man who noticed you be blessed.” Ruth told her where she had worked. She said, “The man’s name is Boaz.” 💬
## Ruth 2:20
Naomi said, “May the LORD bless him. The LORD has not stopped showing kindness to the living or the dead.” She added, “This man is a close relative. He is one of our family redeemers.” ❤️

*Word help: A family redeemer is a relative with the right to recover family property and help preserve the family line.*
## Ruth 2:21
Ruth from Moab said, “He also told me, ‘Stay with my young men until they finish my whole harvest.’” 🌾
## Ruth 2:22
Naomi said to Ruth, “My daughter, it is good for you to work with his young women. Then you will not face danger in another field.” 🛡️
## Ruth 2:23
Ruth stayed close to Boaz’s servant girls to collect grain until the barley and wheat harvests ended. She lived with her mother-in-law. 🌾
