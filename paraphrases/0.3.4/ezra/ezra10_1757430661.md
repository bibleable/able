## Ezra 10:1
Ezra prayed, confessed, cried, and fell facedown before God’s house. A very large crowd of Israelite men, women, and children gathered around him. They also cried bitterly. 😢
## Ezra 10:2
Shecaniah son of Jehiel, an Elamite, said, “We were unfaithful to our God by marrying foreign women from this land’s peoples. Yet Israel still has hope. 🌅
## Ezra 10:3
Let us make a covenant before God to send away all foreign wives and their children. Let us follow my lord’s advice and those who tremble at our God’s command. Let this be done according to the law. 📜
## Ezra 10:4
Get up! This is your responsibility, and we will support you. Be strong and act!” 💪
## Ezra 10:5
Ezra rose and made the leading priests, Levites, and all Israel swear to do this. They took the oath. 👥
## Ezra 10:6
Ezra left God’s house and went to the room of Jehohanan son of Eliashib. He ate no food and drank no water there because he mourned the exiles’ unfaithfulness. 😢
## Ezra 10:7
An announcement went throughout Judah and Jerusalem. All the returned exiles were to gather in Jerusalem. 📣
## Ezra 10:8
The leaders and elders ordered that anyone absent after three days would lose all his property. He would also be expelled from the exiles’ assembly. ⚖️
## Ezra 10:9
All Judah’s and Benjamin’s men gathered in Jerusalem within three days. On the twentieth day of the ninth month, they sat in the square at God’s house. They trembled because of this matter and the heavy rain. 🌧️
## Ezra 10:10
Ezra the priest stood and said, “You were unfaithful by marrying foreign women. You added to Israel’s guilt. ⚠️
## Ezra 10:11
Confess to the LORD, your ancestors’ God, and do His will. Separate from this land’s peoples and your foreign wives.” 📜
## Ezra 10:12
The whole assembly answered loudly, “We must do as you say! 👥
## Ezra 10:13
But many people are here, and it is the rainy season. We cannot remain outdoors. This cannot be done in one or two days, because we sinned greatly in this matter. 🌧️
## Ezra 10:14
Let our leaders represent the assembly. Those who married foreign women should come at appointed times with their towns’ elders and judges. Continue until our God’s fierce anger about this turns away from us.” 👥
## Ezra 10:15
Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah opposed this plan. Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite supported them. 👥
## Ezra 10:16
The exiles followed the proposal. Ezra selected named family heads to represent their families. They began the investigation on the first day of the tenth month. 📋
## Ezra 10:17
By the first day of the first month, they finished dealing with all the men who married foreign women. 📋
## Ezra 10:18
Some priests who married foreign women descended from Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers. They were Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah. 👥
## Ezra 10:19
They promised to send their wives away and offered a ram from the flock for their guilt. 🐏
## Ezra 10:20
Immer’s descendants included Hanani and Zebadiah. 👥
## Ezra 10:21
Harim’s descendants included Maaseiah, Elijah, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Uzziah. 👥
## Ezra 10:22
Pashhur’s descendants included Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Elasah. 👥
## Ezra 10:23
The Levites included Jozabad, Shimei, Kelaiah, also called Kelita, Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer. 👥
## Ezra 10:24
Eliashib was among the singers. Shallum, Telem, and Uri were among the gatekeepers. 👥
## Ezra 10:25
Among the other Israelites, Parosh’s descendants included Ramiah, Izziah, Malchijah, Mijamin, Eleazar, Malchijah, and Benaiah. 👥
## Ezra 10:26
Elam’s descendants included Mattaniah, Zechariah, Jehiel, Abdi, Jeremoth, and Elijah. 👥
## Ezra 10:27
Zattu’s descendants included Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattaniah, Jeremoth, Zabad, and Aziza. 👥
## Ezra 10:28
Bebai’s descendants included Jehohanan, Hananiah, Zabbai, and Athlai. 👥
## Ezra 10:29
Bani’s descendants included Meshullam, Malluch, Adaiah, Jashub, Sheal, and Jeremoth. 👥
## Ezra 10:30
Pahath-moab’s descendants included Adna, Chelal, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezalel, Binnui, and Manasseh. 👥
## Ezra 10:31
Harim’s descendants included Eliezer, Isshijah, Malchijah, Shemaiah, and Shimeon. 👥
## Ezra 10:32
They also included Benjamin, Malluch, and Shemariah. 👥
## Ezra 10:33
Hashum’s descendants included Mattenai, Mattattah, Zabad, Eliphelet, Jeremai, Manasseh, and Shimei. 👥
## Ezra 10:34
Bani’s descendants included Maadai, Amram, and Uel. 👥
## Ezra 10:35
They also included Benaiah, Bedeiah, and Cheluhi. 👥
## Ezra 10:36
They also included Vaniah, Meremoth, and Eliashib. 👥
## Ezra 10:37
They also included Mattaniah, Mattenai, and Jaasu. 👥
## Ezra 10:38
Binnui’s descendants included Shimei. 👥
## Ezra 10:39
They also included Shelemiah, Nathan, and Adaiah. 👥
## Ezra 10:40
They also included Machnadebai, Shashai, and Sharai. 👥
## Ezra 10:41
They also included Azarel, Shelemiah, and Shemariah. 👥
## Ezra 10:42
They also included Shallum, Amariah, and Joseph. 👥
## Ezra 10:43
Nebo’s descendants included Jeiel, Mattithiah, Zabad, Zebina, Jaddai, Joel, and Benaiah. 👥
## Ezra 10:44
All these men married foreign women. Some had children by these wives. 👥
