## Ezra 8:1
These are the family heads and family records of those who returned with me from Babylon during King Artaxerxes’ reign. 📋
## Ezra 8:2
Gershom was from Phinehas’s descendants, Daniel from Ithamar’s descendants, and Hattush from David’s descendants. 🌳
## Ezra 8:3
Hattush also belonged to Shecaniah’s descendants. Zechariah came from Parosh’s descendants, with 150 registered men. 👥
## Ezra 8:4
Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah came from Pahath-Moab’s descendants, with 200 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:5
Shecaniah son of Jahaziel came from Zattu’s descendants, with 300 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:6
Ebed son of Jonathan came from Adin’s descendants, with 50 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:7
Jeshaiah son of Athaliah came from Elam’s descendants, with 70 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:8
Zebadiah son of Michael came from Shephatiah’s descendants, with 80 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:9
Obadiah son of Jehiel came from Joab’s descendants, with 218 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:10
Shelomith son of Josiphiah came from Bani’s descendants, with 160 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:11
Zechariah son of Bebai came from Bebai’s descendants, with 28 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:12
Johanan son of Hakkatan came from Azgad’s descendants, with 110 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:13
Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah came from Adonikam’s later descendants, with 60 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:14
Uthai and Zaccur came from Bigvai’s descendants, with 70 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:15
I gathered the exiles at the canal that flows to Ahava. We camped there for three days. I examined the people and priests but found no Levites. 👥
## Ezra 8:16
I called the leaders Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam. I also called the teachers Joiarib and Elnathan. 👥
## Ezra 8:17
I sent them to Iddo, Casiphia’s leader. I gave a message for him and his relatives, the temple servants there. They were to bring servants for our God’s house. 📣
## Ezra 8:18
Our God’s gracious hand was on us, so they brought Sherebiah, a man of understanding. He descended from Mahli son of Levi, the son of Israel. His sons and brothers came with him, 18 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:19
They brought Hashabiah and Jeshaiah from Merari’s descendants, with his brothers and their sons, 20 men. 👥
## Ezra 8:20
They also brought 220 temple servants, each named. David and the officials had appointed temple servants to help the Levites. 👥
## Ezra 8:21
At the Ahava Canal, I declared a fast to humble ourselves before our God. We asked Him for a safe journey for us, our children, and all our possessions. 🙏
## Ezra 8:22
I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and riders to guard us from enemies on the road. We had told him, “Our God’s hand is gracious to everyone who seeks Him. His great anger opposes everyone who abandons Him.” 🛡️
## Ezra 8:23
We fasted and asked our God about this. He granted our request. 🙏
## Ezra 8:24
I set apart twelve leading priests, with Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brothers. 👥
## Ezra 8:25
I weighed the silver, gold, and objects offered for our God’s house and gave them to these men. The king, his advisers, his leaders, and all the Israelites there had offered them. 🎁
## Ezra 8:26
I weighed 650 talents of silver, silver objects weighing 100 talents, and 100 talents of gold into their hands. ⚖️
## Ezra 8:27
I also gave them 20 gold bowls worth 1,000 darics. Two fine polished bronze objects were as precious as gold. 🏺
## Ezra 8:28
I said, “You and these objects are holy to the LORD. The silver and gold are a voluntary offering to the LORD, your ancestors’ God. 🙏
## Ezra 8:29
Guard them carefully until you weigh them in the rooms of the LORD’s house in Jerusalem. Do this before the leading priests, Levites, and heads of Israel’s families.” 🛡️
## Ezra 8:30
The priests and Levites accepted the weighed silver, gold, and holy objects to take to our God’s house in Jerusalem. 🏺
## Ezra 8:31
We left the Ahava Canal for Jerusalem on the twelfth day of the first month. Our God’s hand protected us from enemies and bandits along the way. 👣
## Ezra 8:32
We reached Jerusalem and rested for three days. 🏘️
## Ezra 8:33
On the fourth day, we weighed the silver, gold, and holy objects in our God’s house. We gave them to Meremoth son of Uriah, the priest. Eleazar son of Phinehas was with him. The Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui were also there. ⚖️
## Ezra 8:34
They checked every item’s number and weight and recorded the total weight then. 📋
## Ezra 8:35
The returned exiles offered Israel’s God 12 bulls for all Israel, 96 rams, and 77 lambs. They also offered 12 male goats as a sin offering. All this was a burnt offering to the LORD. 🙏
## Ezra 8:36
They delivered the king’s orders to the royal satraps and governors west of the Euphrates. These officials helped the people and God’s house. 📜
