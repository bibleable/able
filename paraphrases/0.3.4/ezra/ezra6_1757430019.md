## Ezra 6:1
King Darius ordered a search of the records kept in Babylon’s treasury. 📜
## Ezra 6:2
A scroll was found in Ecbatana’s fortress in Media’s province. This record was written on it. 📜
## Ezra 6:3
“In his first year, King Cyrus ordered God’s house in Jerusalem rebuilt for sacrifices, with firm foundations. It must be sixty cubits high and sixty cubits wide. 🏠
## Ezra 6:4
Use three layers of cut stones and one of wooden beams. The royal treasury must pay the costs. 🛠️
## Ezra 6:5
Return the gold and silver objects Nebuchadnezzar took from Jerusalem’s temple to Babylon. Put them back in God’s house in Jerusalem.” 🏺
## Ezra 6:6
Darius ordered, “Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and your associates and regional officials, stay away from that place! ⛔
## Ezra 6:7
Do not interfere with the construction of God’s house. Let the Jewish governor and elders rebuild it on its original site. 🛠️
## Ezra 6:8
I order you to pay these elders’ full costs for rebuilding God’s house. Use the royal treasury’s taxes from the provinces west of the Euphrates, so the work is not hindered. 🎁
## Ezra 6:9
Give whatever Jerusalem’s priests request each day without fail. Supply young bulls, rams, and lambs for burnt offerings to heaven’s God, and wheat, salt, wine, and oil. 🙏
## Ezra 6:10
Then they can offer sweet-smelling sacrifices to heaven’s God and pray for the king’s and his sons’ lives. 🙏
## Ezra 6:11
If anyone interferes with this order, remove a beam from his house and raise it up. Impale him on it. Turn his house into rubble for this offense. ⚖️
## Ezra 6:12
May God, whose Name lives there, overthrow any king or people who change this order or destroy His house in Jerusalem. I, Darius, issue this order. Carry it out carefully.” 📜
## Ezra 6:13
Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and their associates carefully carried out King Darius’s order. 👣
## Ezra 6:14
The Jewish elders built and prospered through the prophecies of Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo. They completed the work according to the command of Israel’s God. They also followed the orders of Persia’s kings Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes. 🏠
## Ezra 6:15
The temple was completed on Adar’s third day, in King Darius’s sixth year. 🎉
## Ezra 6:16
Israel’s priests, Levites, and other returned exiles joyfully celebrated the dedication of God’s house. 🎉
## Ezra 6:17
They offered a hundred bulls, two hundred rams, and four hundred lambs for the dedication. They offered twelve male goats for all Israel’s sin, one for each tribe. 🙏
## Ezra 6:18
They appointed priests by their divisions and Levites by their groups to serve God in Jerusalem. They followed the Book of Moses. 📖
## Ezra 6:19
The returned exiles kept Passover on the first month’s fourteenth day. 🙏
## Ezra 6:20
All priests and Levites had purified themselves and were ceremonially clean. The Levites killed the Passover lamb for all the exiles, their fellow priests, and themselves. 🐑
## Ezra 6:21
The returned Israelites ate it with those who left the surrounding peoples’ uncleanness to seek the LORD, the God of Israel. 🤝
## Ezra 6:22
They joyfully celebrated the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days. The LORD gave them joy and turned the heart of Assyria’s king toward them. The king strengthened them in the work on the house of Israel’s God. 🎉
