## Ezra 5:1
Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo prophesied to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem. They spoke in the name of Israel’s God, who was over them. 📣
## Ezra 5:2
Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak began rebuilding God’s house in Jerusalem. God’s prophets stayed with them and helped them. 🛠️
## Ezra 5:3
Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and their associates came to the Jews. They asked, “Who authorized you to rebuild this temple and restore this structure?” 💬
## Ezra 5:4
They also asked, “What are the names of the men who build this house?” 📋
## Ezra 5:5
Their God watched over the Jewish elders, so work did not stop while a report went to Darius. They waited for his written instructions. 🛡️
## Ezra 5:6
Tattenai, governor west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and their associates, the regional officials, sent this letter to King Darius. ✉️
## Ezra 5:7
Their report said, “To King Darius: May you have full peace. 🕊️
## Ezra 5:8
We went to Judah’s province and the house of the great God. The people rebuild it with large stones and put wooden beams in the walls. They work carefully and make good progress. 🛠️
## Ezra 5:9
We asked the elders, ‘Who authorized you to rebuild this temple and restore this structure?’ 💬
## Ezra 5:10
We asked their names so that we could record their leaders’ names for you. 📋
## Ezra 5:11
They answered, ‘We serve the God of heaven and earth. We rebuild the temple that a great king of Israel built and completed many years ago. 🏠
## Ezra 5:12
Our ancestors angered the God of heaven. He handed them to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean. He destroyed this temple and took the people to Babylon. ⛓️
## Ezra 5:13
In his first year, Cyrus king of Babylon ordered this house of God rebuilt. 📜
## Ezra 5:14
Nebuchadnezzar had taken gold and silver objects from Jerusalem’s temple to Babylon’s temple. Cyrus removed them and gave them to Sheshbazzar, whom he appointed governor. 🏺
## Ezra 5:15
He said, “Take these objects to Jerusalem’s temple. Let God’s house be rebuilt on its original site.” 🏠
## Ezra 5:16
Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundation of God’s house in Jerusalem. Construction has continued from then until now, but it is not finished.’ 🛠️
## Ezra 5:17
If it pleases the king, search Babylon’s royal records. See whether Cyrus ordered God’s house rebuilt in Jerusalem. Then send us your decision.” 📜
