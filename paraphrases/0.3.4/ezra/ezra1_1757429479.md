## Ezra 1:1
In Cyrus king of Persia’s first year, the LORD fulfilled His word spoken through Jeremiah. He stirred Cyrus’s spirit to announce a message throughout his kingdom and write it down. 📜
## Ezra 1:2
“Cyrus king of Persia says, ‘The LORD, the God of heaven, gave me all the earth’s kingdoms. He appointed me to build Him a house in Jerusalem in Judah. 👑
## Ezra 1:3
Anyone among you who belongs to His people may go to Jerusalem in Judah. May his God be with him. Let him build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel, who is in Jerusalem. 🏠
## Ezra 1:4
Wherever a survivor lives, the people there must help him with silver, gold, goods, and livestock. They must also give a voluntary offering for God’s house in Jerusalem.’” 🎁
## Ezra 1:5
The family heads of Judah and Benjamin, priests, and Levites prepared to rebuild the LORD’s house in Jerusalem. God had stirred the spirit of everyone who prepared to go. 👣
## Ezra 1:6
Their neighbors gave them silver and gold objects, goods, livestock, and valuables. They also gave voluntary offerings. 🎁
## Ezra 1:7
King Cyrus brought out the objects from the LORD’s house. Nebuchadnezzar had taken them from Jerusalem and put them in his gods’ temple. 🏺
## Ezra 1:8
Cyrus had Mithredath the treasurer bring them out. Mithredath counted them and gave them to Sheshbazzar, Judah’s prince. 📋
## Ezra 1:9
The list contained 30 gold dishes, 1,000 silver dishes, and 29 silver utensils. 🏺
## Ezra 1:10
It also contained 30 gold bowls, 410 matching silver bowls, and 1,000 other objects. 🏺
## Ezra 1:11
There were 5,400 gold and silver objects in all. Sheshbazzar brought them when the exiles returned from Babylon to Jerusalem. 👣
