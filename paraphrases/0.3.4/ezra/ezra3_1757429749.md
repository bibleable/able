## Ezra 3:1
By the seventh month, the Israelites had settled in their towns. They gathered together as one in Jerusalem. 👥
## Ezra 3:2
Jeshua son of Jozadak and his fellow priests began to build the altar of Israel’s God. Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his associates joined them. They would offer burnt sacrifices as Moses, the man of God, instructed in the law. 🔥
## Ezra 3:3
They built the altar on its foundation, though they feared the land’s people. They offered morning and evening burnt offerings to the LORD. 🙏
## Ezra 3:4
They celebrated the Feast of Tabernacles according to the written instructions. Each day, they offered the required number of burnt offerings. 🎉
## Ezra 3:5
Then they offered regular burnt offerings and offerings for New Moons and all the LORD’s appointed holy feasts. They also presented all the voluntary offerings brought to the LORD. 🙏
## Ezra 3:6
They began burnt offerings to the LORD on the first day of the seventh month. The foundation of His temple was not yet laid. 🔥
## Ezra 3:7
They paid the stoneworkers and carpenters. They gave food, drink, and oil to Sidon’s and Tyre’s people to ship Lebanon’s cedar logs to Joppa by sea. Cyrus king of Persia had authorized this. 🌲
## Ezra 3:8
Work began in the second month of the second year after they arrived at God’s house in Jerusalem. Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak began it with their associates. These included priests, Levites, and all who returned from exile. They appointed Levites at least twenty years old to supervise the construction of the LORD’s house. 🛠️
## Ezra 3:9
Jeshua and his sons and brothers joined Kadmiel and his sons, descendants of Yehudah. Henadad’s sons and their sons and brothers joined them. All were Levites who together supervised the workers on God’s house. 👥
## Ezra 3:10
The builders laid the foundation of the LORD’s temple. Priests in their robes stood with trumpets, and Levites descended from Asaph stood with cymbals. They praised the LORD as David king of Israel instructed. 🎵
## Ezra 3:11
They answered each other in songs of praise and thanks to the LORD. They sang, “He is good, and His faithful love for Israel lasts forever.” Everyone shouted great praise because the foundation of the LORD’s house was laid. 🙏
## Ezra 3:12
Many older priests, Levites, and family heads had seen the first temple. They cried loudly when they saw this foundation, while many others shouted with joy. 😢
## Ezra 3:13
People could not distinguish the joyful shouts from the cries because the noise was so loud. The sound carried far away. 📣
