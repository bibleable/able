## Ezra 7:1
Many years later, Artaxerxes was king of Persia. Ezra was Seraiah’s son, Seraiah was Azariah’s son, and Azariah was Hilkiah’s son. 🌳
## Ezra 7:2
Hilkiah was Shallum’s son, Shallum was Zadok’s son, and Zadok was Ahitub’s son. 🌳
## Ezra 7:3
Ahitub was Amariah’s son, Amariah was Azariah’s son, and Azariah was Meraioth’s son. 🌳
## Ezra 7:4
Meraioth was Zerahiah’s son, Zerahiah was Uzzi’s son, and Uzzi was Bukki’s son. 🌳
## Ezra 7:5
Bukki was Abishua’s son, and Abishua was Phinehas’s son. Phinehas was Eleazar’s son, and Eleazar was the chief priest Aaron’s son. 🌳
## Ezra 7:6
This Ezra came from Babylon. He was a scribe skilled in Moses’ law, which the LORD, the God of Israel, gave. The king granted all his requests because the hand of the LORD his God was on him. 📖
## Ezra 7:7
In King Artaxerxes’ seventh year, Ezra went to Jerusalem with some Israelites. These included priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants. 👣
## Ezra 7:8
Ezra reached Jerusalem in the fifth month of the king’s seventh year. 🏘️
## Ezra 7:9
He left Babylon on the first day of the first month and reached Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month. His God’s gracious hand was on him. 👣
## Ezra 7:10
Ezra set his heart to study and practice the LORD’s law. He also wanted to teach its rules and commands in Israel. 📖
## Ezra 7:11
King Artaxerxes gave this letter to Ezra, the priest and scribe who knew the LORD’s commands and rules for Israel. ✉️
## Ezra 7:12
“From Artaxerxes, king of kings. Greetings to Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven. 👑
## Ezra 7:13
I order that any Israelites in my kingdom who volunteer may go with you to Jerusalem. This includes priests and Levites. 👣
## Ezra 7:14
The king and his seven advisers send you to examine Judah and Jerusalem according to your God’s law, which you hold. 📜
## Ezra 7:15
Take the silver and gold that the king and his advisers freely offered to Israel’s God, who lives in Jerusalem. 🎁
## Ezra 7:16
Take all the silver and gold you find in Babylon’s province. Also take the people’s and priests’ voluntary offerings for their God’s house in Jerusalem. 🎁
## Ezra 7:17
Use this money to buy the needed bulls, rams, lambs, grain offerings, and drink offerings. Offer them on the altar at your God’s house in Jerusalem. 🙏
## Ezra 7:18
Use the remaining silver and gold as you and your brothers think best, according to your God’s will. 🎁
## Ezra 7:19
Deliver to Jerusalem’s God all the objects given to you for service in your God’s house. 🏺
## Ezra 7:20
Pay from the royal treasury for anything else you need to supply for your God’s house. 👑
## Ezra 7:21
I, King Artaxerxes, order every treasurer west of the Euphrates to supply promptly whatever Ezra requires. He is the priest and scribe of the law of the God of heaven. 📜
## Ezra 7:22
Supply up to a hundred talents of silver and a hundred cors of wheat. Supply a hundred baths each of wine and olive oil, and unlimited salt. 🎁
## Ezra 7:23
Carefully do everything heaven’s God commands for His house. Why should anger fall on the kingdom of the king and his sons? 🙏
## Ezra 7:24
You must not impose tribute, duty, or toll on priests, Levites, singers, doorkeepers, temple servants, or other servants of this house. ⚖️
## Ezra 7:25
Ezra, use your God’s wisdom to appoint officials and judges for everyone west of the Euphrates who knows your God’s laws. Teach anyone who does not know them. 📖
## Ezra 7:26
Strictly punish anyone who disobeys your God’s law and the king’s law. Use death, exile, loss of property, or imprisonment. ⚖️
## Ezra 7:27
Praise the LORD, our ancestors’ God! He put it in the king’s heart to honor the LORD’s house in Jerusalem. 🙏
## Ezra 7:28
He showed me kindness before the king, his advisers, and powerful officials. I took courage because the hand of the LORD my God was on me. I gathered Israel’s leaders to return with me. 👣
