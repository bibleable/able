## Ezra 2:68

## Ezra 2:69
According to their means, they gave the treasury 61,000 gold darics, 5,000 silver minas, and 100 priestly garments for the work. 🎁
## Ezra 2:70
The priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, and some of the people settled in their own towns. The other Israelites also settled in their towns. 🏘️
