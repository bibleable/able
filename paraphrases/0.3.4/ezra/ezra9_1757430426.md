## Ezra 9:1
Afterward, the leaders came to me. They said, “Israel’s people, priests, and Levites have not separated from surrounding peoples and their detestable acts. These acts are like those of the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians, and Amorites. ⚠️
## Ezra 9:2
Israelites married these peoples’ daughters and took them as wives for their sons. The holy offspring mixed with the land’s peoples. Leaders and officials led this unfaithfulness!” ⛔
## Ezra 9:3
I tore my tunic and cloak when I heard this. I pulled hair from my head and beard and sat in horror. 😢
## Ezra 9:4
Everyone who trembled at the words of Israel’s God gathered around me because of the exiles’ unfaithfulness. I sat in horror until the evening offering. 🙏
## Ezra 9:5
At the evening offering, I rose from my humiliation, with my tunic and cloak torn. I knelt and stretched my hands to the LORD my God. 🙏
## Ezra 9:6
I said, “My God, I am too ashamed and embarrassed to lift my face to You. Our sins rise above our heads, and our guilt reaches heaven. 💔
## Ezra 9:7
Our guilt has been great from our ancestors’ days until now. Our sins caused us, our kings, and our priests to be handed to the earth’s kings. We faced the sword, captivity, plunder, and shame, as we do today. ⚖️
## Ezra 9:8
Now, for a brief moment, the LORD our God showed us grace. He preserved a surviving group and gave us a stake in His holy place. Even in slavery, He gave us new life and light for our eyes. 🌅
## Ezra 9:9
We are slaves, but God did not abandon us. He showed us grace before Persia’s kings, giving us new life to rebuild His house and repair its ruins. He gave us a wall of protection in Judah and Jerusalem. 🛡️
## Ezra 9:10
Our God, what can we say now? We abandoned Your commands. 💔
## Ezra 9:11
You gave them through Your servants the prophets. You said, ‘The land you enter to possess is polluted by its peoples’ impurity. Their detestable acts fill it from end to end. ⚠️
## Ezra 9:12
Do not marry your daughters to their sons or take their daughters for your sons. Never seek their peace or prosperity. Then you can be strong, eat the land’s good things, and leave it to your sons forever.’ 📜
## Ezra 9:13
All this happened because of our evil actions and great guilt. Yet You punished us less than our sins deserved and left us this surviving group. ❤️
## Ezra 9:14
Should we again break Your commands and marry peoples who commit these detestable acts? Would You not become angry enough to destroy us, with no survivor left? ⚖️
## Ezra 9:15
LORD, Israel’s God, You are righteous! We remain today as a surviving group. We stand guilty before You, though our guilt means no one can stand before You.” 🙏
