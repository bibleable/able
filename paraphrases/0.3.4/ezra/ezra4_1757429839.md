## Ezra 4:1
Judah’s and Benjamin’s enemies heard that the returned exiles were building a temple for the LORD, the God of Israel. 🏠
## Ezra 4:2
They approached Zerubbabel and the family heads. They said, “Let us build with you, because we seek your God as you do. We have sacrificed to Him since King Esar-haddon of Assyria brought us here.” 💬
## Ezra 4:3
Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and Israel’s other family heads replied, “You have no part with us in building our God’s house. We alone must build it for the LORD, the God of Israel, as Cyrus king of Persia commanded.” 🛠️
## Ezra 4:4
The land’s people discouraged Judah’s people and made them afraid to build. ⚠️
## Ezra 4:5
They hired advisers to oppose and frustrate their plans. This continued throughout Cyrus king of Persia’s reign until Darius king of Persia’s reign. ⛔
## Ezra 4:6
At the start of Xerxes’ reign, an accusation was made against Judah’s and Jerusalem’s people. 📜
## Ezra 4:7
During Artaxerxes king of Persia’s reign, Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and their associates wrote to him. The letter was written in Aramaic and translated. ✉️
## Ezra 4:8
Rehum the commander and Shimshai the scribe wrote this letter against Jerusalem to King Artaxerxes. 📜
## Ezra 4:9
It came from Rehum, Shimshai, and their associates. These included judges and officials over Tripolis, Persia, Erech, and Babylon, and the Elamites of Susa. 👥
## Ezra 4:10
It also came from the other peoples whom the great and honorable Ashurbanipal deported. He settled them in Samaria’s cities and elsewhere west of the Euphrates. 🏘️
## Ezra 4:11
This is the letter they sent: “To King Artaxerxes, from your servants, the men west of the Euphrates. ✉️
## Ezra 4:12
The king should know that the Jews who came from you to us returned to Jerusalem. They rebuild that rebellious and wicked city. They restore its walls and repair its foundations. 🏘️
## Ezra 4:13
If they rebuild the city and walls, they will not pay tribute, duty, or toll. The royal treasury will suffer. ⚠️
## Ezra 4:14
We serve the palace and should not let the king be dishonored. So we send this message. 👑
## Ezra 4:15
Search your ancestors’ record books. They will show that this city rebelled, harmed kings and provinces, and stirred rebellion from ancient times. That is why the city was destroyed. 📜
## Ezra 4:16
If they rebuild the city and walls, you will have no rule west of the Euphrates.” ⚠️
## Ezra 4:17
The king replied, “Greetings to Rehum the commander, Shimshai the scribe, and your associates in Samaria and elsewhere west of the Euphrates. ✉️
## Ezra 4:18
Your letter was translated and read before me. 📜
## Ezra 4:19
I ordered a search. It showed that this city rebelled against kings from ancient times and stirred revolt. ⚖️
## Ezra 4:20
Mighty kings ruled Jerusalem and the whole region west of the Euphrates. They received tribute, duty, and toll. 👑
## Ezra 4:21
Order these men to stop. The city must not be rebuilt until I command it. ⛔
## Ezra 4:22
Do not neglect this. Why let the threat grow and harm the king’s interests?” ⚠️
## Ezra 4:23
The king’s letter was read to Rehum, Shimshai the scribe, and their associates. They immediately went to Jerusalem’s Jews and forced them to stop. ⛔
## Ezra 4:24
Construction of God’s house in Jerusalem stopped. It remained stopped until the second year of Darius king of Persia’s reign. 🏚️
