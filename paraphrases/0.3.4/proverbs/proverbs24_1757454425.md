## Proverbs 24:1
Do not envy wicked people or desire their company. 📖

## Proverbs 24:2
Their hearts plan violence. Their lips speak trouble. 📖

## Proverbs 24:3
Wisdom builds a house. Understanding establishes it. 📖

## Proverbs 24:4
Knowledge fills its rooms with precious, beautiful treasures. 📖

## Proverbs 24:5
A wise person is strong. Knowledge increases strength. 📖

## Proverbs 24:6
Wage war only with sound guidance. Many counselors bring victory. 📖

## Proverbs 24:7
Wisdom is too high for fools. They stay silent in the meeting place. 📖

## Proverbs 24:8
Evil planners are called schemers. 📖

## Proverbs 24:9
A foolish scheme is sin. People detest a mocker. 📖

## Proverbs 24:10
Fainting under distress shows small strength. 📖

## Proverbs 24:11
Rescue those led to death. Hold back those stumbling toward slaughter. 📖

## Proverbs 24:12
You may claim you did not know. Does He who weighs hearts not consider this? Does your life’s Guardian not know? Will He not repay each person’s deeds? 📖

## Proverbs 24:13
My son, eat good honey. Honeycomb tastes sweet. 📖

## Proverbs 24:14
Wisdom likewise sweetens your soul. Find it, and you have a future. Your hope will never be cut off. 📖

## Proverbs 24:15
Wicked person, do not ambush a righteous person’s home or destroy his resting place. 📖

## Proverbs 24:16
A righteous person may fall seven times but rises again. Wicked people stumble in bad times. 📖

## Proverbs 24:17
Do not gloat over your enemy’s fall or rejoice when he stumbles. 📖

## Proverbs 24:18
Otherwise, the LORD will see, disapprove, and turn His wrath from him. 📖

## Proverbs 24:19
Do not fret over evildoers or envy wicked people. 📖

## Proverbs 24:20
An evil person has no future. Wicked people’s lamps go out. 📖

## Proverbs 24:21
My son, fear the LORD and the king. Avoid rebellious people. 📖

## Proverbs 24:22
They bring sudden destruction. Who knows the ruin they can bring? 📖

## Proverbs 24:23
These are additional wise sayings. Partiality in judgment is wrong. 📖

## Proverbs 24:24
People and nations curse and denounce those declaring guilty people innocent. 📖

## Proverbs 24:25
Those convicting guilty people will fare well and receive rich blessing. 📖

## Proverbs 24:26
An honest answer resembles a kiss on the lips. 📖

## Proverbs 24:27
Finish outdoor work and prepare your field. Then build your house. 📖

## Proverbs 24:28
Do not testify against your neighbor without cause or deceive with your lips. 📖

## Proverbs 24:29
Do not say, “I will treat him as he treated me. I will repay him for his deeds.” 📖

## Proverbs 24:30
I passed a lazy person’s field and a vineyard belonging to someone without judgment. 📖

## Proverbs 24:31
Thorns and thistles covered the ground. The stone wall had collapsed. 📖

## Proverbs 24:32
I observed, took it to heart, and learned this lesson. 📖

## Proverbs 24:33
A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of hands to rest. 📖

## Proverbs 24:34
Then poverty comes like a robber and need like a bandit. 📖
