## Proverbs 29:1
Remaining stubborn after repeated correction brings sudden destruction beyond recovery. 📖

## Proverbs 29:2
People rejoice when righteous people flourish. They groan when wicked people rule. 📖

## Proverbs 29:3
Loving wisdom delights your father. Keeping prostitutes’ company wastes your wealth. 📖

## Proverbs 29:4
A just king stabilizes the land. Someone exacting tribute destroys it. 📖

## Proverbs 29:5
Flattering your neighbor spreads a net for his feet. 📖

## Proverbs 29:6
An evil person’s sin traps him. A righteous person sings and rejoices. 📖

## Proverbs 29:7
Righteous people consider poor people’s cause. Wicked people disregard it. 📖

## Proverbs 29:8
Mockers inflame a city. Wise people turn away anger. 📖

## Proverbs 29:9
A wise person going to court with a fool meets raving and laughter without resolution. 📖

## Proverbs 29:10
Bloodthirsty people hate a blameless person. Upright people care for his life. 📖

## Proverbs 29:11
A fool releases all his anger. A wise person restrains it. 📖

## Proverbs 29:12
A ruler listening to lies will have wicked officials throughout. 📖

## Proverbs 29:13
Poor people and oppressors share this: the LORD gives both light for their eyes. 📖

## Proverbs 29:14
A king judging poor people fairly will have his throne established forever. 📖

## Proverbs 29:15
A corrective rod teaches wisdom. A child left to himself disgraces his mother. 📖

## Proverbs 29:16
Wicked people’s prosperity increases rebellion. Righteous people will witness their downfall. 📖

## Proverbs 29:17
Discipline your son. He will give you rest and delight your soul. 📖

## Proverbs 29:18
Without vision, people abandon restraint. Keeping the Law brings blessing. 📖

## Proverbs 29:19
Words alone cannot correct a servant. He understands but will not respond. 📖

## Proverbs 29:20
See someone speaking hastily? A fool has more hope than he does. 📖

## Proverbs 29:21
Pampering a servant from youth brings grief eventually. 📖

## Proverbs 29:22
An angry person starts conflict. A person quick to anger commits many offenses. 📖

## Proverbs 29:23
Pride brings a person low. A humble spirit receives honor. 📖

## Proverbs 29:24
A thief’s partner hates his own soul. He receives the oath but refuses to testify. 📖

## Proverbs 29:25
Fear of people is a trap. Trusting the LORD places you securely on high. 📖

## Proverbs 29:26
Many seek a ruler’s favor. Justice comes from the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 29:27
Righteous people detest unjust people. Wicked people detest upright people. 📖
