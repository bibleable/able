## Proverbs 28:1
Wicked people flee without pursuit. Righteous people are bold as lions. 📖

## Proverbs 28:2
A rebellious land has many rulers. A knowledgeable person with understanding maintains order. 📖

## Proverbs 28:3
A destitute leader oppressing poor people resembles driving rain that leaves no food. 📖

## Proverbs 28:4
Those abandoning the law praise wicked people. Those keeping it resist them. 📖

## Proverbs 28:5
Evil people do not understand justice. Those seeking the LORD understand it fully. 📖

## Proverbs 28:6
Better poor with integrity than rich with perverse ways. 📖

## Proverbs 28:7
A discerning son keeps the law. A companion of gluttons disgraces his father. 📖

## Proverbs 28:8
Wealth increased through interest and usury is stored for someone kind to poor people. 📖

## Proverbs 28:9
Even the prayer of someone refusing to hear the law is detestable. 📖

## Proverbs 28:10
Leading upright people toward evil makes you fall into your own pit. Blameless people inherit good. 📖

## Proverbs 28:11
A rich person considers himself wise. A discerning poor person sees through him. 📖

## Proverbs 28:12
Righteous people’s triumph brings great glory. Wicked people’s rise makes people hide. 📖

## Proverbs 28:13
Concealing sins prevents prosperity. Confessing and renouncing them brings mercy. 📖

## Proverbs 28:14
Continual reverence brings blessing. Hardening your heart brings trouble. 📖

## Proverbs 28:15
A wicked ruler over helpless people resembles a roaring lion or charging bear. 📖

## Proverbs 28:16
A leader without judgment greatly oppresses. Hating dishonest profit lengthens life. 📖

## Proverbs 28:17
Someone burdened by bloodguilt flees into the Pit. Let no one support him. 📖

## Proverbs 28:18
Integrity brings safety. Perverse ways bring sudden downfall. 📖

## Proverbs 28:19
Working your land brings plenty of food. Chasing fantasies brings plenty of poverty. 📖

## Proverbs 28:20
A faithful person receives abundant blessings. Eagerness for riches will bring punishment. 📖

## Proverbs 28:21
Partiality is wrong. Yet someone will do wrong for a piece of bread. 📖

## Proverbs 28:22
A stingy person rushes after wealth, unaware poverty awaits. 📖

## Proverbs 28:23
Whoever rebukes someone will later find more favor than a flatterer. 📖

## Proverbs 28:24
Robbing your parents and denying wrongdoing makes you a destroyer’s companion. 📖

## Proverbs 28:25
Greed starts conflict. Trusting the LORD brings prosperity. 📖

## Proverbs 28:26
Trusting yourself is foolish. Living wisely brings safety. 📖

## Proverbs 28:27
Giving to poor people prevents need. Closing your eyes to them brings many curses. 📖

## Proverbs 28:28
People hide when wicked people gain power. Righteous people flourish when the wicked perish. 📖
