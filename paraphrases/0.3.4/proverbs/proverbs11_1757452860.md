## Proverbs 11:1
The LORD detests dishonest scales. Accurate weights delight Him. 📖

## Proverbs 11:2
Pride brings disgrace. Humility brings wisdom. 📖

## Proverbs 11:3
Integrity guides the upright. Unfaithful people’s perversity destroys them. 📖

## Proverbs 11:4
Riches are worthless on wrath’s day. Righteousness rescues from death. 📖

## Proverbs 11:5
Blameless people’s righteousness guides their paths. Wicked people fall through their own wickedness. 📖

## Proverbs 11:6
Upright people’s righteousness rescues them. Unfaithful people are trapped by their desires. 📖

## Proverbs 11:7
A wicked person’s hope dies with him. Hope in his strength vanishes. 📖

## Proverbs 11:8
Righteous people escape trouble. Wicked people enter it instead. 📖

## Proverbs 11:9
Ungodly mouths destroy neighbors. Knowledge rescues righteous people. 📖

## Proverbs 11:10
The city rejoices when righteous people thrive. Wicked people’s deaths bring joyful shouts. 📖

## Proverbs 11:11
The upright people’s blessing builds a city. Wicked mouths tear it down. 📖

## Proverbs 11:12
Despising your neighbor shows poor judgment. Understanding keeps a person silent. 📖

## Proverbs 11:13
A gossip exposes secrets. A trustworthy person keeps confidences. 📖

## Proverbs 11:14
A nation falls without guidance. Many counselors bring deliverance. 📖

## Proverbs 11:15
Guaranteeing a stranger’s debt brings certain suffering. Hating indebtedness brings security. 📖

## Proverbs 11:16
A gracious woman gains honor. Ruthless men gain only wealth. 📖

## Proverbs 11:17
Kind people benefit themselves. Cruel people trouble themselves. 📖

## Proverbs 11:18
Wicked people earn empty wages. Those sowing righteousness reap a true reward. 📖

## Proverbs 11:19
True righteousness leads to life. Pursuing evil brings death. 📖

## Proverbs 11:20
The LORD detests people with perverse hearts. He delights in people who live blamelessly. 📖

## Proverbs 11:21
Wicked people will certainly face punishment. Righteous people’s offspring will escape. 📖

## Proverbs 11:22
A beautiful woman without discretion resembles a gold ring in a pig’s snout. 📖

## Proverbs 11:23
Righteous people’s desires lead only to good. Wicked people’s hopes bring wrath. 📖

## Proverbs 11:24
One gives freely and gains more. Another withholds what is right and becomes poor. 📖

## Proverbs 11:25
Generous people prosper. Those refreshing others will be refreshed. 📖

## Proverbs 11:26
People curse grain hoarders. Blessing crowns those selling it. 📖

## Proverbs 11:27
Seeking good brings favor. Seeking evil brings evil upon you. 📖

## Proverbs 11:28
Those trusting riches fall. Righteous people thrive like foliage. 📖

## Proverbs 11:29
Those troubling their household inherit wind. Fools serve the wise of heart. 📖

## Proverbs 11:30
Righteous people’s fruit is a tree of life. Whoever wins souls is wise. 📖

## Proverbs 11:31
Righteous people receive their due on earth. How much more will ungodly people and sinners! 📖
