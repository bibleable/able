## Proverbs 14:1
A wise woman builds her house. A foolish woman tears hers down with her own hands. 📖

## Proverbs 14:2
Upright living shows fear of the LORD. Deceitful ways show contempt for Him. 📖

## Proverbs 14:3
A fool’s proud speech brings a rod to his back. Wise lips protect their speakers. 📖

## Proverbs 14:4
Without oxen, the manger is empty. An ox’s strength brings abundant harvest. 📖

## Proverbs 14:5
An honest witness never deceives. A dishonest witness pours out lies. 📖

## Proverbs 14:6
A mocker seeks wisdom without finding it. Discerning people gain knowledge easily. 📖

## Proverbs 14:7
Avoid foolish people. Their speech teaches you no knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 14:8
Prudent people wisely discern their way. Fools are deceived by their foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 14:9
Fools mock making amends. Upright people share goodwill. 📖

## Proverbs 14:10
The heart knows its own bitterness. No stranger shares its joy. 📖

## Proverbs 14:11
Wicked people’s house will be destroyed. Upright people’s tent will flourish. 📖

## Proverbs 14:12
A way may seem right but end in death. 📖

## Proverbs 14:13
A laughing heart may ache. Joy may end in sorrow. 📖

## Proverbs 14:14
Someone turning away in heart receives the full results of his own ways. A good person receives reward for his ways. 📖

## Proverbs 14:15
Inexperienced people believe every word. Prudent people watch their steps. 📖

## Proverbs 14:16
A wise person fears and turns from evil. A fool is careless and reckless. 📖

## Proverbs 14:17
A person quick to anger acts foolishly. A deceitful person is hated. 📖

## Proverbs 14:18
Inexperienced people inherit foolishness. Prudent people are crowned with knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 14:19
Evil people bow before good people. Wicked people bow at righteous people’s gates. 📖

## Proverbs 14:20
Even neighbors hate poor people. Rich people have many who love them. 📖

## Proverbs 14:21
Despising your neighbor is sinful. Kindness to poor people brings blessing. 📖

## Proverbs 14:22
Do evil planners not go astray? Those planning good find loving devotion and faithfulness. 📖

## Proverbs 14:23
All labor brings profit. Mere talk brings only poverty. 📖

## Proverbs 14:24
Wise people’s wealth crowns them. Fools’ efforts remain foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 14:25
A truthful witness saves lives. A lying witness is deceitful. 📖

## Proverbs 14:26
Someone fearing the LORD has confident security. His children have refuge. 📖

## Proverbs 14:27
Fear of the LORD is a fountain of life. It turns people from death’s traps. 📖

## Proverbs 14:28
A large population gives a king splendor. A shortage of subjects ruins a prince. 📖

## Proverbs 14:29
Patience shows great understanding. Quick anger promotes foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 14:30
A peaceful heart gives the body life. Envy rots the bones. 📖

## Proverbs 14:31
Oppressing poor people insults their Maker. Kindness to needy people honors Him. 📖

## Proverbs 14:32
A wicked person’s sin brings him down. A righteous person finds refuge even in death. 📖

## Proverbs 14:33
Wisdom rests in discerning hearts. She is known even among fools. 📖

## Proverbs 14:34
Righteousness exalts a nation. Sin disgraces any people. 📖

## Proverbs 14:35
A wise servant delights the king. Shameful conduct brings his anger. 📖
