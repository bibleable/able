## Proverbs 2:1
My son, accept my words and store my commands within you. 📖

## Proverbs 2:2
Listen carefully to wisdom. Direct your heart toward understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 2:3
Truly call for insight. Raise your voice for understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 2:4
Seek it like silver. Search for it like hidden treasure. 📖

## Proverbs 2:5
Then you will understand fear of the LORD and discover knowledge of God. 📖

## Proverbs 2:6
The LORD gives wisdom. Knowledge and understanding come from His mouth. 📖

## Proverbs 2:7
He stores sound wisdom for the upright. He shields people who live with integrity. 📖

## Proverbs 2:8
He guards justice’s paths and protects His saints’ way. 📖

## Proverbs 2:9
Then you will understand righteousness, justice, fairness, and every good path. 📖

## Proverbs 2:10
Wisdom will enter your heart. Knowledge will delight your soul. 📖

## Proverbs 2:11
Discretion will watch over you. Understanding will guard you. 📖

## Proverbs 2:12
They will rescue you from evil’s way and the man who speaks perversely. 📖

## Proverbs 2:13
They will rescue you from people who abandon straight paths for dark ways. 📖

## Proverbs 2:14
These people enjoy evil and rejoice in its twistedness. 📖

## Proverbs 2:15
Their paths are crooked and their ways deceitful. 📖

## Proverbs 2:16
Wisdom will rescue you from the forbidden woman, the stranger whose words seduce. 📖

## Proverbs 2:17
She abandons her youthful partner and forgets her God’s covenant. 📖

## Proverbs 2:18
Her house sinks toward death. Her tracks lead to departed spirits. 📖

## Proverbs 2:19
Those visiting her never return or walk life’s paths. 📖

## Proverbs 2:20
You will follow good people’s ways and stay on righteous paths. 📖

## Proverbs 2:21
The upright will live in the land. The blameless will remain there. 📖

## Proverbs 2:22
The wicked will be cut off from the land. The unfaithful will be uprooted. 📖
