## Proverbs 5:1
My son, attend to my wisdom and listen to my insight. 📖

## Proverbs 5:2
Then you can maintain discretion and preserve knowledge with your lips. 📖

## Proverbs 5:3
The forbidden woman’s lips drip honey. Her speech is smoother than oil. 📖

## Proverbs 5:4
Yet she ends bitter as wormwood and sharp as a sword with two edges. 📖

## Proverbs 5:5
Her feet descend to death. Her steps lead straight to Sheol. 📖

## Proverbs 5:6
She ignores life’s path. She does not know her ways are unstable. 📖

## Proverbs 5:7
My sons, listen. Never turn from my words. 📖

## Proverbs 5:8
Stay far from her. Avoid her house’s door. 📖

## Proverbs 5:9
Otherwise, you may give your vigor to others and your years to someone cruel. 📖

## Proverbs 5:10
Strangers may feast on your wealth. Your labor may enrich a foreigner’s house. 📖

## Proverbs 5:11
At life’s end, you will groan as your flesh and body waste away. 📖

## Proverbs 5:12
You will say, “I hated discipline and despised correction! 📖

## Proverbs 5:13
I ignored my teachers and refused my mentors’ instruction. 📖

## Proverbs 5:14
I stand near complete ruin before the whole assembly.” 📖

## Proverbs 5:15
Drink from your own cistern and the running water of your own well. 📖

## Proverbs 5:16
Why let your springs flow into streets and your streams into public squares? 📖

## Proverbs 5:17
Keep them for yourself alone. Never share them with strangers. 📖

## Proverbs 5:18
May your fountain be blessed. Rejoice in the wife of your youth. 📖

## Proverbs 5:19
She is a loving doe and graceful fawn. May her breasts always satisfy you and her love captivate you forever. 📖

## Proverbs 5:20
My son, why be captivated by an adulteress or embrace a stranger’s bosom? 📖

## Proverbs 5:21
The LORD sees people’s ways and examines all their paths. 📖

## Proverbs 5:22
Wicked people are trapped by their wrongdoing and tangled in their sin’s cords. 📖

## Proverbs 5:23
They die from lack of discipline, led astray by their great foolishness. 📖
