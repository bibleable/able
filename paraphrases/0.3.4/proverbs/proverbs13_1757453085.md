## Proverbs 13:1
A wise son accepts his father’s discipline. A mocker ignores rebuke. 📖

## Proverbs 13:2
A person enjoys good from his lips’ fruit. Unfaithful people desire violence. 📖

## Proverbs 13:3
Guarding your mouth protects your life. Opening your lips invites your ruin. 📖

## Proverbs 13:4
Lazy people crave but have nothing. Diligent souls are fully satisfied. 📖

## Proverbs 13:5
Righteous people hate falsehood. Wicked people bring shame and disgrace. 📖

## Proverbs 13:6
Righteousness guards a person of integrity. Wickedness undermines sinners. 📖

## Proverbs 13:7
One pretends wealth but has nothing. Another pretends poverty but owns great riches. 📖

## Proverbs 13:8
Riches may ransom a person’s life. A poor person hears no threat. 📖

## Proverbs 13:9
Righteous people’s light shines brightly. Wicked people’s lamp goes out. 📖

## Proverbs 13:10
Arrogance brings only conflict. Good counsel brings wisdom. 📖

## Proverbs 13:11
Dishonest wealth shrinks. Wealth earned through hard work increases. 📖

## Proverbs 13:12
Delayed hope sickens the heart. Fulfilled desire is a tree of life. 📖

## Proverbs 13:13
Despising instruction brings punishment. Respecting commands brings reward. 📖

## Proverbs 13:14
Wise teaching is a fountain of life. It turns people from death’s traps. 📖

## Proverbs 13:15
Good understanding wins favor. Unfaithful people’s way is difficult. 📖

## Proverbs 13:16
Prudent people act with knowledge. Fools display foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 13:17
A wicked messenger meets trouble. A faithful envoy brings healing. 📖

## Proverbs 13:18
Ignoring discipline brings poverty and shame. Accepting correction brings honor. 📖

## Proverbs 13:19
Fulfilled desire is sweet to the soul. Fools hate turning from evil. 📖

## Proverbs 13:20
Walk with wise people and become wise. Companions of fools will be destroyed. 📖

## Proverbs 13:21
Disaster pursues sinners. Prosperity rewards righteous people. 📖

## Proverbs 13:22
A good person leaves an inheritance for grandchildren. A sinner’s wealth passes to righteous people. 📖

## Proverbs 13:23
Poor people’s uncultivated ground holds abundant food. Injustice sweeps it away. 📖

## Proverbs 13:24
Whoever spares the rod hates his son. Whoever loves him disciplines him diligently. 📖

## Proverbs 13:25
A righteous person eats to satisfaction. Wicked people’s stomachs are empty. 📖
