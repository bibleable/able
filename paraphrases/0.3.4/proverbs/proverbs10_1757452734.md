## Proverbs 10:1
Solomon’s proverbs follow. A wise son brings his father joy. A foolish son brings his mother grief. 📖

## Proverbs 10:2
Dishonest treasure brings no benefit. Righteousness rescues from death. 📖

## Proverbs 10:3
The LORD keeps righteous people from hunger but denies wicked people’s cravings. 📖

## Proverbs 10:4
Idle hands bring poverty. Diligent hands bring wealth. 📖

## Proverbs 10:5
A son gathering in summer is wise. A son sleeping through harvest brings disgrace. 📖

## Proverbs 10:6
Blessings rest on righteous people’s heads. Wicked people’s mouths conceal violence. 📖

## Proverbs 10:7
Remembering the righteous brings blessing. Wicked people’s names will rot. 📖

## Proverbs 10:8
A wise heart accepts commands. Foolish lips come to ruin. 📖

## Proverbs 10:9
Someone walking with integrity walks safely. Those twisting their ways will be exposed. 📖

## Proverbs 10:10
Winking causes grief. Foolish lips come to ruin. 📖

## Proverbs 10:11
A righteous person’s mouth is life’s fountain. A wicked person’s mouth conceals violence. 📖

## Proverbs 10:12
Hatred stirs conflict. Love covers every offense. 📖

## Proverbs 10:13
Discerning lips hold wisdom. A rod awaits the back of someone lacking judgment. 📖

## Proverbs 10:14
The wise store knowledge. A fool’s mouth invites destruction. 📖

## Proverbs 10:15
A rich person’s wealth is his fortified city. Poverty ruins poor people. 📖

## Proverbs 10:16
Righteous people’s labor leads to life. Wicked people’s gain brings punishment. 📖

## Proverbs 10:17
Following instruction puts you on life’s path. Ignoring correction leads you astray. 📖

## Proverbs 10:18
Someone hiding hatred has lying lips. Whoever spreads slander is foolish. 📖

## Proverbs 10:19
Many words make sin unavoidable. Restraining your lips shows wisdom. 📖

## Proverbs 10:20
A righteous person’s tongue is choice silver. A wicked person’s heart has little value. 📖

## Proverbs 10:21
Righteous lips feed many. Fools die through poor judgment. 📖

## Proverbs 10:22
The LORD’s blessing brings riches. He adds no sorrow to it. 📖

## Proverbs 10:23
A fool enjoys shameful conduct. A person of understanding has wisdom. 📖

## Proverbs 10:24
Wicked people’s fears will overtake them. Righteous people’s desires will be granted. 📖

## Proverbs 10:25
After the whirlwind, wicked people are gone. Righteous people remain secure forever. 📖

## Proverbs 10:26
A lazy messenger is like vinegar to teeth or smoke to eyes for those sending him. 📖

## Proverbs 10:27
Fear of the LORD lengthens life. Wicked people’s years are cut short. 📖

## Proverbs 10:28
Righteous people’s hope brings joy. Wicked people’s expectations perish. 📖

## Proverbs 10:29
The LORD’s way shelters the upright. Destruction awaits evildoers. 📖

## Proverbs 10:30
Righteous people will never be shaken. Wicked people will not inhabit the land. 📖

## Proverbs 10:31
A righteous mouth produces wisdom. A perverse tongue will be cut out. 📖

## Proverbs 10:32
Righteous lips know fitting words. Wicked mouths speak perversely. 📖
