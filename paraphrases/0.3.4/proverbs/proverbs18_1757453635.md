## Proverbs 18:1
Someone isolating himself pursues selfish desires and rebels against all sound judgment. 📖

## Proverbs 18:2
A fool enjoys expressing opinions rather than understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 18:3
Contempt accompanies a wicked person. Shame comes with disgrace. 📖

## Proverbs 18:4
A person’s words are deep waters. Wisdom’s fountain is a bubbling brook. 📖

## Proverbs 18:5
Favoring wicked people is wrong. So is denying innocent people justice. 📖

## Proverbs 18:6
A fool’s lips cause conflict. His mouth invites a beating. 📖

## Proverbs 18:7
A fool’s mouth ruins him. His lips trap his soul. 📖

## Proverbs 18:8
Gossip resembles choice morsels that enter the deepest being. 📖

## Proverbs 18:9
A lazy worker is a destroyer’s brother. 📖

## Proverbs 18:10
The LORD’s name is a strong tower. Righteous people run to it and are safe. 📖

## Proverbs 18:11
A rich person’s wealth is his fortified city. He imagines it as a high wall. 📖

## Proverbs 18:12
A person’s heart is proud before his downfall. Humility precedes honor. 📖

## Proverbs 18:13
Answering before listening brings foolishness and disgrace. 📖

## Proverbs 18:14
A person’s spirit can endure sickness. Who can survive a broken spirit? 📖

## Proverbs 18:15
A discerning heart gains knowledge. A wise ear seeks it. 📖

## Proverbs 18:16
A gift opens doors and brings its giver before great people. 📖

## Proverbs 18:17
The first person’s case seems right until another questions him closely. 📖

## Proverbs 18:18
Casting lots ends disputes and separates strong opponents. 📖

## Proverbs 18:19
Winning an offended brother is harder than taking a fortified city. Disputes resemble castle bars. 📖

## Proverbs 18:20
A person’s belly fills with his mouth’s fruit. His lips’ harvest satisfies him. 📖

## Proverbs 18:21
The tongue holds power over life and death. Those loving it eat its fruit. 📖

## Proverbs 18:22
Finding a wife means finding something good and receiving the LORD’s favor. 📖

## Proverbs 18:23
A poor person pleads for mercy. A rich person answers harshly. 📖

## Proverbs 18:24
Many companions may bring ruin. Yet there is a friend who stays closer than a brother. 📖
