## Proverbs 23:1
When dining with a ruler, carefully consider what lies before you. 📖

## Proverbs 23:2
Put a knife to your throat if your appetite is great. 📖

## Proverbs 23:3
Do not crave his delicacies. The food deceives. 📖

## Proverbs 23:4
Do not exhaust yourself pursuing riches. Wisely restrain yourself. 📖

## Proverbs 23:5
Wealth vanishes when you glance at it. It grows wings and flies skyward like an eagle. 📖

## Proverbs 23:6
Do not eat a stingy person’s bread or crave his delicacies. 📖

## Proverbs 23:7
He counts the cost inwardly. He invites you to eat and drink, but his heart is not with you. 📖

## Proverbs 23:8
You will vomit your small meal and waste your pleasant words. 📖

## Proverbs 23:9
Do not speak to a fool. He will despise your wise words. 📖

## Proverbs 23:10
Do not move ancient boundary stones or encroach on fields belonging to the fatherless. 📖

## Proverbs 23:11
Their Redeemer is strong. He will plead their case against you. 📖

## Proverbs 23:12
Apply your heart to instruction and listen to words of knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 23:13
Do not withhold discipline from a child. Even if you strike him with a rod, he will not die. 📖

## Proverbs 23:14
Strike him with a rod, and you will rescue his soul from Sheol. 📖

## Proverbs 23:15
My son, if your heart is wise, my heart will rejoice. 📖

## Proverbs 23:16
Your upright words will delight my deepest being. 📖

## Proverbs 23:17
Do not envy sinners in your heart. Always fear the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 23:18
A future certainly awaits. Your hope will not be cut off. 📖

## Proverbs 23:19
My son, listen and be wise. Guide your heart along the right course. 📖

## Proverbs 23:20
Avoid those drinking excessive wine or gorging on meat. 📖

## Proverbs 23:21
Drunkards and gluttons become poor. Drowsiness clothes them in rags. 📖

## Proverbs 23:22
Listen to the father who gave you life. Do not despise your aging mother. 📖

## Proverbs 23:23
Invest in truth, wisdom, instruction, and understanding. Never sell them. 📖

## Proverbs 23:24
A righteous person’s father greatly rejoices. Fathering a wise son brings delight. 📖

## Proverbs 23:25
May your parents be glad! May the mother who bore you rejoice! 📖

## Proverbs 23:26
My son, give me your heart. Let your eyes delight in my ways. 📖

## Proverbs 23:27
A prostitute is a deep pit. An adulteress is a narrow well. 📖

## Proverbs 23:28
She waits like a robber and increases unfaithfulness among men. 📖

## Proverbs 23:29
Who has woe, sorrow, conflict, complaints, needless wounds, and bloodshot eyes? 📖

## Proverbs 23:30
Those lingering over wine and tasting mixed drinks. 📖

## Proverbs 23:31
Do not gaze at red wine sparkling in the cup and going down smoothly. 📖

## Proverbs 23:32
It ends by biting like a snake and stinging like a viper. 📖

## Proverbs 23:33
Your eyes see strange things. Your mind speaks perversities. 📖

## Proverbs 23:34
You resemble someone sleeping at sea or lying atop a mast. 📖

## Proverbs 23:35
“They struck me without pain! They beat me without my knowing! When can I wake and seek another drink?” 📖
