## Proverbs 8:1
Does wisdom not call, and understanding raise her voice? 📖

## Proverbs 8:2
She stands on heights above the road and at crossroads. 📖

## Proverbs 8:3
She cries beside the city gates and entrances. 📖

## Proverbs 8:4
“I call to people everywhere, to the sons of men. 📖

## Proverbs 8:5
Inexperienced people, learn sound judgment. Fools, gain understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 8:6
Listen. I speak noble things and reveal what is right. 📖

## Proverbs 8:7
My mouth speaks truth. My lips hate wickedness. 📖

## Proverbs 8:8
Every word I speak is righteous. None is crooked or perverse. 📖

## Proverbs 8:9
Discerning people find them clear. Those finding knowledge find them upright. 📖

## Proverbs 8:10
Choose my instruction over silver and knowledge over pure gold. 📖

## Proverbs 8:11
Wisdom is more precious than rubies. Nothing you desire compares with her. 📖

## Proverbs 8:12
I am wisdom. I live with prudence and find knowledge and discretion. 📖

## Proverbs 8:13
Fearing the LORD means hating evil. I hate arrogant pride, evil conduct, and perverse speech. 📖

## Proverbs 8:14
I possess counsel, sound judgment, insight, and strength. 📖

## Proverbs 8:15
Through me, kings reign and rulers make just laws. 📖

## Proverbs 8:16
Through me, princes rule, as do all nobles governing justly. 📖

## Proverbs 8:17
I love those loving me. Those seeking me early will find me. 📖

## Proverbs 8:18
I have riches, honor, lasting wealth, and righteousness. 📖

## Proverbs 8:19
My fruit surpasses pure gold. My harvest surpasses choice silver. 📖

## Proverbs 8:20
I walk the way of righteousness and the paths of justice. 📖

## Proverbs 8:21
I give wealth to those loving me and fill their treasuries. 📖

## Proverbs 8:22
The LORD created me as His first course, before His ancient works. 📖

## Proverbs 8:23
I was established from everlasting, from the beginning, before earth began. 📖

## Proverbs 8:24
I was brought forth before watery depths existed or springs overflowed. 📖

## Proverbs 8:25
I was brought forth before mountains were settled or hills existed. 📖

## Proverbs 8:26
This was before He made land, fields, or any earthly dust. 📖

## Proverbs 8:27
I was there when He established heaven and drew a circle across the deep’s surface. 📖

## Proverbs 8:28
I was there when He established the clouds and the deep’s fountains gushed. 📖

## Proverbs 8:29
He set the sea’s boundary so water would obey His command. He marked earth’s foundations. I was there. 📖

## Proverbs 8:30
I worked beside Him as a skilled craftsman. I was His daily delight, always rejoicing before Him. 📖

## Proverbs 8:31
I rejoiced in His whole world and delighted in people. 📖

## Proverbs 8:32
My sons, listen. Those keeping my ways are blessed. 📖

## Proverbs 8:33
Listen to instruction and be wise. Never ignore it. 📖

## Proverbs 8:34
Blessed is whoever listens, watches daily at my doors, and waits by my doorposts. 📖

## Proverbs 8:35
Whoever finds me finds life and receives the LORD’s favor. 📖

## Proverbs 8:36
Whoever fails to find me harms himself. Those hating me love death.” 📖
