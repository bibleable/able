## Proverbs 6:1
My son, have you guaranteed your neighbor’s debt or shaken hands in a pledge with a stranger? 📖

## Proverbs 6:2
Have your own words trapped and ensnared you? 📖

## Proverbs 6:3
If so, free yourself from your neighbor’s power. Humble yourself and urgently plead with your neighbor. 📖

## Proverbs 6:4
Let neither your eyes nor eyelids sleep. 📖

## Proverbs 6:5
Escape like a gazelle from a hunter or a bird from a birdcatcher’s trap. 📖

## Proverbs 6:6
Lazy person, observe and follow the ant’s ways. Become wise. 📖

## Proverbs 6:7
The ant has no commander, supervisor, or ruler. 📖

## Proverbs 6:8
Yet it prepares supplies in summer and gathers food at harvest. 📖

## Proverbs 6:9
Lazy person, how long will you lie there? When will you rise from sleep? 📖

## Proverbs 6:10
A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of hands to rest. 📖

## Proverbs 6:11
Then poverty arrives like a robber and need like a bandit. 📖

## Proverbs 6:12
A worthless, wicked person goes about speaking perversely. 📖

## Proverbs 6:13
He winks, signals with his feet, and points with his fingers. 📖

## Proverbs 6:14
He plans evil with a deceitful heart and constantly spreads discord. 📖

## Proverbs 6:15
Disaster will suddenly strike him. In an instant, he will be shattered beyond recovery. 📖

## Proverbs 6:16
The LORD hates six things. Seven things are detestable to Him. 📖

## Proverbs 6:17
These include proud eyes, a lying tongue, and hands shedding innocent blood. 📖

## Proverbs 6:18
They include a heart planning wicked schemes and feet hurrying toward evil. 📖

## Proverbs 6:19
They include a lying witness and someone spreading discord among brothers. 📖

## Proverbs 6:20
My son, keep your father’s command. Never abandon your mother’s teaching. 📖

## Proverbs 6:21
Always bind them to your heart and tie them around your neck. 📖

## Proverbs 6:22
They guide you when walking, guard you when lying down, and speak when you wake. 📖

## Proverbs 6:23
The command is a lamp. The teaching is light. Disciplinary correction is life’s way. 📖

## Proverbs 6:24
It protects you from the evil woman and the adulteress’s smooth tongue. 📖

## Proverbs 6:25
Do not lust for her beauty in your heart or let her eyes captivate you. 📖

## Proverbs 6:26
A prostitute’s cost is poverty. An adulteress hunts your very life. 📖

## Proverbs 6:27
Can someone embrace fire without burning his clothes? 📖

## Proverbs 6:28
Can someone walk on hot coals without scorching his feet? 📖

## Proverbs 6:29
Likewise, anyone sleeping with another man’s wife will suffer punishment. No one touching her escapes it. 📖

## Proverbs 6:30
People do not despise a thief who steals to satisfy hunger. 📖

## Proverbs 6:31
If caught, he must repay seven times, surrendering all his household’s wealth. 📖

## Proverbs 6:32
Adultery shows a lack of judgment. Those committing it destroy themselves. 📖

## Proverbs 6:33
Wounds and dishonor await them. Their disgrace will never disappear. 📖

## Proverbs 6:34
Jealousy enrages a husband. He shows no mercy when taking vengeance. 📖

## Proverbs 6:35
No ransom appeases him. Even lavish gifts cannot persuade him. 📖
