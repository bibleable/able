## Proverbs 30:1
Agur, Jakeh’s son, declared this burden to Ithiel. “God, I am weary and worn out. 📖

## Proverbs 30:2
I am the most ignorant of people. I lack human understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 30:3
I have not learned wisdom. I have no knowledge of the Holy One. 📖

## Proverbs 30:4
Who ascended heaven and descended? Who gathered wind in His hands and wrapped waters in His cloak? Who established all earth’s ends? What are His name and His Son’s name? Surely you know! 📖

## Proverbs 30:5
Every word of God is flawless. He shields those seeking refuge in Him. 📖

## Proverbs 30:6
Never add to His words, or He may rebuke you and prove you a liar. 📖

## Proverbs 30:7
I ask You two things. Do not refuse before I die. 📖

## Proverbs 30:8
Keep falsehood and deceitful words far away. Give neither poverty nor riches. Feed me my allotted bread. 📖

## Proverbs 30:9
Otherwise, abundance may make me deny You and ask, ‘Who is the LORD?’ Poverty may make me steal and profane my God’s name. 📖

## Proverbs 30:10
Do not slander a servant to his master. Otherwise, he will curse you, and you will bear guilt. 📖

## Proverbs 30:11
A generation curses fathers and never blesses mothers. 📖

## Proverbs 30:12
A generation thinks itself pure while remaining filthy. 📖

## Proverbs 30:13
A generation has proud eyes and pretentious glances. 📖

## Proverbs 30:14
A generation has swords for teeth and knives for jaws. They devour oppressed and needy people from the earth and humanity. 📖

## Proverbs 30:15
The leech has two daughters, Give and Give. Three things are never satisfied. Four never say, ‘Enough!’ 📖

## Proverbs 30:16
They are Sheol, the barren womb, land never satisfied with water, and fire never saying, ‘Enough!’ 📖

## Proverbs 30:17
May valley ravens pluck out an eye mocking a father and scorning obedience to a mother. May young vultures devour it. 📖

## Proverbs 30:18
Three things are too wonderful for me. Four escape my understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 30:19
They are an eagle’s way in the sky, a snake’s on rock, a ship’s at sea, and a man’s with a maiden. 📖

## Proverbs 30:20
An adulteress eats, wipes her mouth, and claims, ‘I did nothing wrong.’ 📖

## Proverbs 30:21
Three things make earth tremble. Four are unbearable to it. 📖

## Proverbs 30:22
These include a servant becoming king and a fool filled with food. 📖

## Proverbs 30:23
They include an unloved woman marrying and a maidservant replacing her mistress. 📖

## Proverbs 30:24
Four earthly creatures are small but exceedingly wise. 📖

## Proverbs 30:25
Ants have little strength but store food in summer. 📖

## Proverbs 30:26
Rock badgers have little power but build homes among rocks. 📖

## Proverbs 30:27
Locusts have no king but advance together in formation. 📖

## Proverbs 30:28
A lizard can be caught in your hands, yet lives in kings’ palaces. 📖

## Proverbs 30:29
Three things have a stately stride. Four walk impressively. 📖

## Proverbs 30:30
These include a lion, mighty among beasts, retreating from nothing. 📖

## Proverbs 30:31
They include a strutting rooster, a male goat, and a king surrounded by his army. 📖

## Proverbs 30:32
If you foolishly exalted yourself or planned evil, cover your mouth with your hand. 📖

## Proverbs 30:33
Churning milk makes butter. Twisting a nose draws blood. Stirring anger likewise produces conflict.” 📖
