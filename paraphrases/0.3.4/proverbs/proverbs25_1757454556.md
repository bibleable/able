## Proverbs 25:1
These are more of Solomon’s proverbs. Men serving Hezekiah, Judah’s king, copied them. 📖

## Proverbs 25:2
God’s glory is to conceal a matter. Kings’ glory is to investigate it. 📖

## Proverbs 25:3
Like heaven’s height and earth’s depth, kings’ hearts cannot be searched. 📖

## Proverbs 25:4
Remove impurities from silver, and a vessel emerges for the silversmith. 📖

## Proverbs 25:5
Remove wicked people from the king’s presence. His throne will be established in righteousness. 📖

## Proverbs 25:6
Do not exalt yourself before the king or claim great people’s place. 📖

## Proverbs 25:7
Better an invitation upward than demotion before a prince. Even concerning what you personally saw, 📖

## Proverbs 25:8
do not rush to court. What will you do afterward when your neighbor shames you? 📖

## Proverbs 25:9
Argue your case with your neighbor without revealing someone else’s secret. 📖

## Proverbs 25:10
Otherwise, the hearer may shame you, and your disgrace may never disappear. 📖

## Proverbs 25:11
A fitting word resembles gold apples in silver settings. 📖

## Proverbs 25:12
A wise rebuke to a listening ear resembles a gold earring or fine gold ornament. 📖

## Proverbs 25:13
A trustworthy messenger refreshes his masters’ souls like snow’s cold at harvest. 📖

## Proverbs 25:14
Boasting about gifts never given resembles clouds and wind without rain. 📖

## Proverbs 25:15
Patience can persuade a ruler. A gentle tongue can break a bone. 📖

## Proverbs 25:16
Find honey? Eat only what you need, or excess will make you vomit. 📖

## Proverbs 25:17
Visit your neighbor rarely, or he may tire of you and hate you. 📖

## Proverbs 25:18
A false witness against his neighbor resembles a club, sword, or sharp arrow. 📖

## Proverbs 25:19
Trusting an unfaithful person during trouble resembles a broken tooth or dislocated foot. 📖

## Proverbs 25:20
Singing to a heavy heart resembles removing a garment on a cold day or pouring vinegar on a wound. 📖

## Proverbs 25:21
Feed your hungry enemy. Give him water when thirsty. 📖

## Proverbs 25:22
You will heap burning coals on his head. The LORD will reward you. 📖

## Proverbs 25:23
The north wind brings rain. A backbiting tongue brings angry looks. 📖

## Proverbs 25:24
Better a roof’s corner than a shared house with a quarrelsome wife. 📖

## Proverbs 25:25
Good news from distant lands resembles cold water to a weary soul. 📖

## Proverbs 25:26
A righteous person yielding to wicked people resembles a muddied spring or polluted well. 📖

## Proverbs 25:27
Eating excessive honey is no good. Neither is seeking your own glory. 📖

## Proverbs 25:28
A person who cannot control his temper resembles a city with broken walls. 📖
