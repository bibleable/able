## Proverbs 1:1
These are the proverbs of Solomon, David’s son and Israel’s king. 📖

## Proverbs 1:2
They teach wisdom and discipline and help people understand insightful words. 📖

## Proverbs 1:3
They instruct people in wise living, righteousness, justice, and fairness. 📖

## Proverbs 1:4
They give sound judgment to the inexperienced, and knowledge and discretion to the young. 📖

## Proverbs 1:5
Let wise people listen and learn more. Let discerning people gain wise counsel. 📖

## Proverbs 1:6
They can understand proverbs, parables, and wise people’s sayings and riddles. 📖

## Proverbs 1:7
Fear of the LORD begins knowledge. Fools despise wisdom and discipline. 📖

## Proverbs 1:8
My son, listen to your father’s instruction. Do not abandon your mother’s teaching. 📖

## Proverbs 1:9
They are a graceful garland on your head and a pendant around your neck. 📖

## Proverbs 1:10
My son, do not yield when sinners tempt you. 📖

## Proverbs 1:11
They may say, “Join us. Let us ambush innocent people without cause and shed their blood. 📖

## Proverbs 1:12
Let us swallow them alive like Sheol, whole like people descending into the Pit. 📖

## Proverbs 1:13
We will find precious goods and fill our houses with plunder. 📖

## Proverbs 1:14
Join us, and we will share one purse.” 📖

## Proverbs 1:15
My son, do not take their road or step onto their path. 📖

## Proverbs 1:16
Their feet run toward evil. They hurry to shed blood. 📖

## Proverbs 1:17
Spreading a net where a bird sees it is useless. 📖

## Proverbs 1:18
Their ambush sheds their own blood and takes their own lives. 📖

## Proverbs 1:19
Everyone greedy for unjust gain meets this fate. Their gain takes their own lives. 📖

## Proverbs 1:20
Wisdom calls in the street. She raises her voice in the square. 📖

## Proverbs 1:21
She cries in the main gathering place and speaks at the city gates. 📖

## Proverbs 1:22
“How long will inexperienced people love their simple ways? How long will mockers enjoy mockery and fools hate knowledge? 📖

## Proverbs 1:23
If you had repented at my rebuke, I would have poured my spirit upon you. I would have made my words known. 📖

## Proverbs 1:24
You refused my call. No one took my outstretched hand. 📖

## Proverbs 1:25
You ignored all my counsel and rejected my correction. 📖

## Proverbs 1:26
Because of this, I will mock your disaster and sneer when terror strikes. 📖

## Proverbs 1:27
Your dread will come like a storm and destruction like a whirlwind. Distress and anguish will overwhelm you. 📖

## Proverbs 1:28
They will call me, but I will not answer. They will seek earnestly but never find me. 📖

## Proverbs 1:29
They hated knowledge and refused to fear the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 1:30
They accepted none of my counsel and despised every rebuke. 📖

## Proverbs 1:31
So they will eat the fruit of their own ways and be filled with their own schemes. 📖

## Proverbs 1:32
The inexperienced die through their waywardness. Fools are destroyed by their complacency. 📖

## Proverbs 1:33
Whoever listens to me will live safely, free from fear of evil.” 📖
