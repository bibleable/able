## Proverbs 22:1
A good name surpasses great riches. Favor is better than silver and gold. 📖

## Proverbs 22:2
Rich and poor people share one Maker, the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 22:3
Prudent people see danger and hide. Inexperienced people continue and suffer the consequences. 📖

## Proverbs 22:4
Humility and fear of the LORD bring wealth, honor, and life. 📖

## Proverbs 22:5
Perverse paths hold thorns and traps. Those guarding their souls stay far away. 📖

## Proverbs 22:6
Train a child in the right way. Even in old age, he will not leave it. 📖

## Proverbs 22:7
Rich people rule poor people. Borrowers serve lenders as slaves. 📖

## Proverbs 22:8
Sowing injustice reaps disaster. The rod of that person’s fury will be destroyed. 📖

## Proverbs 22:9
A generous person is blessed because he shares bread with poor people. 📖

## Proverbs 22:10
Expel the mocker, and conflict leaves. Quarrels and insults stop. 📖

## Proverbs 22:11
Loving a pure heart and gracious lips wins the king’s friendship. 📖

## Proverbs 22:12
The LORD’s eyes guard knowledge. He frustrates unfaithful people’s words. 📖

## Proverbs 22:13
A lazy person says, “A lion is outside! I will be killed in the streets!” 📖

## Proverbs 22:14
An adulteress’s mouth is a deep pit. Anyone under the LORD’s wrath falls into it. 📖

## Proverbs 22:15
Foolishness is bound up in a child’s heart. The rod of discipline drives it far away. 📖

## Proverbs 22:16
Oppressing poor people to gain wealth or giving gifts to rich people certainly brings poverty. 📖

## Proverbs 22:17
Listen carefully to wise words. Apply your mind to my knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 22:18
Keeping them within you and always on your lips is pleasing. 📖

## Proverbs 22:19
I instruct you today, yes, you, so you may trust the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 22:20
Have I not written you thirty sayings of counsel and knowledge? 📖

## Proverbs 22:21
They teach true, reliable words so you can give sound answers to those who sent you. 📖

## Proverbs 22:22
Do not rob someone because he is poor. Do not crush afflicted people at the gate. 📖

## Proverbs 22:23
The LORD will plead their case and plunder their robbers. 📖

## Proverbs 22:24
Do not befriend angry people or associate with those quick to anger. 📖

## Proverbs 22:25
You might learn their ways and trap yourself. 📖

## Proverbs 22:26
Do not pledge yourself as security for debts. 📖

## Proverbs 22:27
If you cannot pay, why let your bed be taken from beneath you? 📖

## Proverbs 22:28
Do not move ancient boundary stones your fathers placed. 📖

## Proverbs 22:29
See someone skilled in his work? He will serve before kings rather than obscure people. 📖
