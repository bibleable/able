## Proverbs 9:1
Wisdom built her house and carved its seven pillars. 📖

## Proverbs 9:2
She prepared meat, mixed wine, and set her table. 📖

## Proverbs 9:3
She sent her maidservants and calls from the city’s heights. 📖

## Proverbs 9:4
She invites inexperienced people lacking judgment, “Come inside! 📖

## Proverbs 9:5
Eat my bread and drink my mixed wine. 📖

## Proverbs 9:6
Leave foolishness and live. Follow understanding’s way.” 📖

## Proverbs 9:7
Correcting a mocker brings shame upon yourself. Rebuking a wicked person taints yourself. 📖

## Proverbs 9:8
Do not rebuke a mocker, or he will hate you. Rebuke a wise person, and he will love you. 📖

## Proverbs 9:9
Instruct the wise, and they become wiser. Teach the righteous, and they learn more. 📖

## Proverbs 9:10
Fear of the LORD begins wisdom. Knowing the Holy One is understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 9:11
Wisdom multiplies your days and adds years to your life. 📖

## Proverbs 9:12
If you are wise, your wisdom benefits you. If you mock, you alone bear the consequences. 📖

## Proverbs 9:13
The woman named Folly is loud, naive, and ignorant. 📖

## Proverbs 9:14
She sits at her house’s door on the city’s heights. 📖

## Proverbs 9:15
She calls to passersby who follow straight paths. 📖

## Proverbs 9:16
She tells inexperienced people lacking judgment, “Come inside! 📖

## Proverbs 9:17
Stolen water is sweet! Secretly eaten bread tastes good!” 📖

## Proverbs 9:18
They do not know the dead are there. Her guests lie in Sheol’s depths. 📖
