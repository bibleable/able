## Proverbs 20:1
Wine mocks, and strong drink brawls. Those they lead astray are unwise. 📖

## Proverbs 20:2
A king’s terror resembles a lion’s roar. Provoking him forfeits your life. 📖

## Proverbs 20:3
Resolving disputes brings honor. Any fool will quarrel. 📖

## Proverbs 20:4
A lazy person avoids plowing in season. At harvest, he looks but finds nothing. 📖

## Proverbs 20:5
A heart’s intentions are deep waters. Understanding draws them out. 📖

## Proverbs 20:6
Many proclaim their loving devotion. Who can find someone trustworthy? 📖

## Proverbs 20:7
A righteous person lives with integrity. His children after him are blessed. 📖

## Proverbs 20:8
A king judging from his throne sifts all evil with his eyes. 📖

## Proverbs 20:9
Who can say, “I kept my heart pure and am cleansed from sin”? 📖

## Proverbs 20:10
The LORD detests both unequal weights and unequal measures. 📖

## Proverbs 20:11
Even a young man’s actions show whether his conduct is pure and upright. 📖

## Proverbs 20:12
The LORD made both hearing ears and seeing eyes. 📖

## Proverbs 20:13
Do not love sleep, or poverty will follow. Open your eyes, and food will be abundant. 📖

## Proverbs 20:14
The buyer calls it worthless. Then he leaves boasting. 📖

## Proverbs 20:15
Gold and rubies are abundant. Knowledgeable lips are rare treasure. 📖

## Proverbs 20:16
Take the garment of someone guaranteeing a stranger’s debt. Obtain collateral when the pledge is for a foreigner. 📖

## Proverbs 20:17
Food gained by fraud tastes sweet. Later it fills the mouth with gravel. 📖

## Proverbs 20:18
Make plans through consultation. Wage war with sound guidance. 📖

## Proverbs 20:19
A constant gossip reveals secrets. Avoid someone who babbles. 📖

## Proverbs 20:20
Whoever curses a parent will have his lamp extinguished in deepest darkness. 📖

## Proverbs 20:21
An inheritance gained quickly ends without blessing. 📖

## Proverbs 20:22
Do not promise revenge for evil. Wait for the LORD. He will save you. 📖

## Proverbs 20:23
The LORD detests unequal weights. Dishonest scales are no good. 📖

## Proverbs 20:24
The LORD directs a person’s steps. How can anyone understand his own way? 📖

## Proverbs 20:25
Rashly dedicating something, then reconsidering the vow, traps a person. 📖

## Proverbs 20:26
A wise king separates wicked people and drives the threshing wheel over them. 📖

## Proverbs 20:27
A person’s spirit is the LORD’s lamp, searching his deepest being. 📖

## Proverbs 20:28
Loving devotion and faithfulness preserve a king. Through them he maintains his throne. 📖

## Proverbs 20:29
Young men’s glory is their strength. Old people’s splendor is gray hair. 📖

## Proverbs 20:30
Lashes and wounds scour away evil. Beatings cleanse the deepest parts. 📖
