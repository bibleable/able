## Proverbs 19:1
Better poor with integrity than foolish with perverse lips. 📖

## Proverbs 19:2
Zeal without knowledge is no good. Hurrying feet miss the mark. 📖

## Proverbs 19:3
A person’s foolishness ruins his way. Yet his heart rages against the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 19:4
Wealth draws many friends. A poor person’s friend abandons him. 📖

## Proverbs 19:5
A false witness will face punishment. A liar will not escape. 📖

## Proverbs 19:6
Many seek a prince’s favor. Everyone befriends a gift giver. 📖

## Proverbs 19:7
A poor person’s brothers all hate him. How much more do friends avoid him! He pursues them pleading, but they cannot be found. 📖

## Proverbs 19:8
Gaining wisdom shows love for yourself. Guarding understanding brings success. 📖

## Proverbs 19:9
A false witness will face punishment. Whoever pours out lies will perish. 📖

## Proverbs 19:10
Luxury does not suit a fool. Even worse is a slave ruling over princes! 📖

## Proverbs 19:11
Insight brings patience. Overlooking an offense shows virtue. 📖

## Proverbs 19:12
A king’s rage resembles a lion’s roar. His favor resembles dew on grass. 📖

## Proverbs 19:13
A foolish son ruins his father. A quarrelsome wife resembles constant dripping. 📖

## Proverbs 19:14
Fathers leave houses and wealth as inheritance. The LORD gives a prudent wife. 📖

## Proverbs 19:15
Laziness brings deep sleep. An idle soul suffers hunger. 📖

## Proverbs 19:16
Keeping a command preserves your soul. Carelessness in your ways brings death. 📖

## Proverbs 19:17
Kindness to poor people lends to the LORD. He repays the lender. 📖

## Proverbs 19:18
Discipline your son, because this offers hope. Do not contribute to his death. 📖

## Proverbs 19:19
A very angry person must pay the penalty. Rescue him, and you will need to repeat it. 📖

## Proverbs 19:20
Listen to counsel and accept discipline. Then you can be wise throughout your remaining days. 📖

## Proverbs 19:21
A heart holds many plans. The LORD’s purpose prevails. 📖

## Proverbs 19:22
People desire loving devotion. Better poor than a liar. 📖

## Proverbs 19:23
Fear of the LORD leads to life, contented rest, and freedom from harm. 📖

## Proverbs 19:24
A lazy person buries his hand in the dish. He will not even raise it to his mouth. 📖

## Proverbs 19:25
Strike a mocker, and inexperienced people learn caution. Rebuke a discerning person, and he gains knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 19:26
A son attacking his father or evicting his mother brings shame and disgrace. 📖

## Proverbs 19:27
My son, if you stop listening to instruction, you will stray from words of knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 19:28
A corrupt witness mocks justice. A wicked mouth swallows sin. 📖

## Proverbs 19:29
Judgments await mockers. Beatings await fools’ backs. 📖
