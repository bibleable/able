## Proverbs 16:1
People plan in their hearts. The tongue’s reply comes from the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 16:2
People consider all their ways pure. The LORD weighs their motives. 📖

## Proverbs 16:3
Entrust your work to the LORD. Your plans will succeed. 📖

## Proverbs 16:4
The LORD made everything for His purpose, even wicked people for disaster’s day. 📖

## Proverbs 16:5
The LORD detests everyone proud in heart. They will certainly face punishment. 📖

## Proverbs 16:6
Sin is atoned for through loving devotion and faithfulness. Fear of the LORD turns people from evil. 📖

## Proverbs 16:7
When someone’s ways please the LORD, He makes even enemies live peacefully with him. 📖

## Proverbs 16:8
Better little wealth with righteousness than great gain with injustice. 📖

## Proverbs 16:9
A person’s heart plans his course. The LORD determines his steps. 📖

## Proverbs 16:10
A divine verdict is on a king’s lips. His mouth must never betray justice. 📖

## Proverbs 16:11
Honest scales and balances come from the LORD. Every weight in the bag concerns Him. 📖

## Proverbs 16:12
Wicked conduct is detestable for kings. Righteousness establishes a throne. 📖

## Proverbs 16:13
Righteous lips delight a king. Honest speakers are loved. 📖

## Proverbs 16:14
A king’s wrath is death’s messenger. A wise person calms it. 📖

## Proverbs 16:15
A king’s brightened face brings life. His favor resembles a spring rain cloud. 📖

## Proverbs 16:16
Wisdom is much better than gold! Understanding is more desirable than silver. 📖

## Proverbs 16:17
The upright people’s highway avoids evil. Guarding your way protects your life. 📖

## Proverbs 16:18
Pride precedes destruction. A proud spirit precedes a fall. 📖

## Proverbs 16:19
Better a humble spirit among humble people than sharing plunder with proud people. 📖

## Proverbs 16:20
Heeding instruction brings success. Trusting the LORD brings blessing. 📖

## Proverbs 16:21
Wise hearts are called discerning. Pleasant speech promotes instruction. 📖

## Proverbs 16:22
Understanding is life’s fountain for its possessor. Fools’ discipline is foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 16:23
A wise heart teaches the mouth and makes its words persuasive. 📖

## Proverbs 16:24
Pleasant words are honeycomb, sweet to the soul and healing to bones. 📖

## Proverbs 16:25
A way may seem right but end in death. 📖

## Proverbs 16:26
A worker’s appetite serves him. Hunger drives him onward. 📖

## Proverbs 16:27
A worthless person digs up evil. His speech resembles scorching fire. 📖

## Proverbs 16:28
A perverse person spreads conflict. Gossip separates close friends. 📖

## Proverbs 16:29
A violent person entices his neighbor onto a bad path. 📖

## Proverbs 16:30
Someone winking plans perversity. Someone pursing his lips is intent on evil. 📖

## Proverbs 16:31
Gray hair is a glorious crown, gained along the path of righteousness. 📖

## Proverbs 16:32
A patient person is better than a warrior. Controlling your temper is greater than capturing a city. 📖

## Proverbs 16:33
The lot is cast into the lap. Every decision comes from the LORD. 📖
