## Proverbs 27:1
Do not boast about tomorrow. You cannot know what a day brings. 📖

## Proverbs 27:2
Let someone else praise you, even a stranger. Never let your own lips do it. 📖

## Proverbs 27:3
Stone is heavy and sand burdensome. A fool’s aggravation outweighs both. 📖

## Proverbs 27:4
Wrath is cruel, and anger floods. Who can withstand jealousy? 📖

## Proverbs 27:5
Open rebuke is better than hidden love. 📖

## Proverbs 27:6
A friend’s wounds are faithful. An enemy’s kisses deceive. 📖

## Proverbs 27:7
A satisfied soul hates honey. A hungry soul finds even bitterness sweet. 📖

## Proverbs 27:8
A person wandering from home resembles a bird straying from its nest. 📖

## Proverbs 27:9
Oil and incense delight the heart. A friend’s counsel sweetens the soul. 📖

## Proverbs 27:10
Do not abandon your friend or your father’s friend. Do not visit your brother’s house during your disaster. A nearby neighbor is better than a distant brother. 📖

## Proverbs 27:11
My son, be wise and delight my heart. Then I can answer those taunting me. 📖

## Proverbs 27:12
Prudent people see danger and hide. Inexperienced people continue and pay the penalty. 📖

## Proverbs 27:13
Take the garment of someone guaranteeing a stranger’s debt. Obtain collateral when the pledge is for a foreigner. 📖

## Proverbs 27:14
Loudly blessing your neighbor early in the morning counts as a curse. 📖

## Proverbs 27:15
A contentious woman resembles constant dripping on a rainy day. 📖

## Proverbs 27:16
Restraining her resembles holding wind back or grasping oil with your right hand. 📖

## Proverbs 27:17
People sharpen one another as iron sharpens iron. 📖

## Proverbs 27:18
Tend a fig tree and eat its fruit. Care for your master and receive honor. 📖

## Proverbs 27:19
Water reflects a face. The heart reflects the true person. 📖

## Proverbs 27:20
Sheol and Abaddon never have enough. Human eyes are likewise never satisfied. 📖

## Proverbs 27:21
A crucible tests silver, and a furnace tests gold. Praise tests a person. 📖

## Proverbs 27:22
Even if you grind a fool like grain with mortar and pestle, his foolishness will remain. 📖

## Proverbs 27:23
Know your flocks’ condition. Watch your herds carefully. 📖

## Proverbs 27:24
Riches do not last forever. A crown does not endure every generation. 📖

## Proverbs 27:25
When hay is removed, new growth appears, and hill grass is gathered, 📖

## Proverbs 27:26
lambs provide clothing and goats pay for a field. 📖

## Proverbs 27:27
Goats’ milk will abundantly feed you, your household, and your maidservants. 📖
