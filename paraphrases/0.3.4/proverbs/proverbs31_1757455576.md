## Proverbs 31:1
These are King Lemuel’s words, the burden his mother taught him. 📖

## Proverbs 31:2
“What can I say, my son, son of my womb and vows? 📖

## Proverbs 31:3
Do not spend your strength on women or your vigor on those ruining kings. 📖

## Proverbs 31:4
Lemuel, kings should not drink wine, and rulers should not crave strong drink. 📖

## Proverbs 31:5
They may drink and forget decrees, denying oppressed people justice. 📖

## Proverbs 31:6
Give strong drink to someone perishing and wine to those bitter in soul. 📖

## Proverbs 31:7
Let him drink and forget poverty and misery. 📖

## Proverbs 31:8
Speak for people without a voice and defend dispossessed people’s cause. 📖

## Proverbs 31:9
Speak, judge righteously, and defend poor and needy people.” 📖

## Proverbs 31:10
Who can find a wife of noble character? She is far more precious than rubies. 📖

## Proverbs 31:11
Her husband trusts her wholeheartedly. He lacks nothing valuable. 📖

## Proverbs 31:12
She brings him good, never harm, throughout her life. 📖

## Proverbs 31:13
She chooses wool and flax and works eagerly with her hands. 📖

## Proverbs 31:14
Like merchant ships, she brings food from afar. 📖

## Proverbs 31:15
She rises before daylight to feed her household and provide portions for her maidservants. 📖

## Proverbs 31:16
She evaluates and buys a field. She uses her earnings to plant a vineyard. 📖

## Proverbs 31:17
She equips herself with strength and shows that her arms are strong. 📖

## Proverbs 31:18
She sees her gain is good. Her lamp stays lit at night. 📖

## Proverbs 31:19
Her hands reach for the distaff. Her fingers grasp the spindle. 📖

## Proverbs 31:20
She opens her arms to poor people and her hands to needy people. 📖

## Proverbs 31:21
She does not fear snow for her household, because everyone wears scarlet. 📖

## Proverbs 31:22
She makes bed coverings. She wears fine linen and purple. 📖

## Proverbs 31:23
Her husband is known at the city gates, sitting with the land’s elders. 📖

## Proverbs 31:24
She makes and sells linen garments. She supplies merchants with sashes. 📖

## Proverbs 31:25
Strength and honor clothe her. She can laugh at coming days. 📖

## Proverbs 31:26
She speaks wisely and gives faithful instruction. 📖

## Proverbs 31:27
She watches her household’s affairs and never eats the bread of idleness. 📖

## Proverbs 31:28
Her children rise and call her blessed. Her husband also praises her. 📖

## Proverbs 31:29
“Many daughters have done noble things. You surpass them all!” 📖

## Proverbs 31:30
Charm deceives, and beauty fades. A woman fearing the LORD deserves praise. 📖

## Proverbs 31:31
Give her the fruit of her hands. Let her works praise her at the gates. 📖
