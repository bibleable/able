## Proverbs 12:1
Loving discipline means loving knowledge. Hating correction is stupid. 📖

## Proverbs 12:2
The LORD favors good people but condemns those planning evil. 📖

## Proverbs 12:3
Wickedness cannot establish anyone. Righteous people cannot be uprooted. 📖

## Proverbs 12:4
A wife of noble character crowns her husband. A shameful wife resembles decay in his bones. 📖

## Proverbs 12:5
Righteous people plan justly. Wicked counsel leads to deceit. 📖

## Proverbs 12:6
Wicked words wait in ambush to shed blood. Upright speech rescues the upright. 📖

## Proverbs 12:7
Wicked people are overthrown and perish. Righteous households stand. 📖

## Proverbs 12:8
Wisdom brings praise. A twisted mind brings contempt. 📖

## Proverbs 12:9
Better to have little respect and a servant than self-importance without food. 📖

## Proverbs 12:10
A righteous person cares about his animal’s life. Even wicked people’s tender mercy is cruel. 📖

## Proverbs 12:11
Working your land brings abundant food. Chasing fantasies shows poor judgment. 📖

## Proverbs 12:12
Wicked people desire evildoers’ plunder. Righteous people’s roots flourish. 📖

## Proverbs 12:13
Rebellious speech traps an evil person. A righteous person escapes trouble. 📖

## Proverbs 12:14
Fruitful speech fills a person with good. His hands’ work returns to him. 📖

## Proverbs 12:15
A fool thinks his way is right. A wise person listens to counsel. 📖

## Proverbs 12:16
A fool immediately shows anger. A prudent person overlooks insults. 📖

## Proverbs 12:17
Truthful speech declares what is right. A false witness speaks deceitfully. 📖

## Proverbs 12:18
Rash speech resembles a piercing sword. Wise tongues bring healing. 📖

## Proverbs 12:19
Truthful lips last forever. A lying tongue lasts a moment. 📖

## Proverbs 12:20
Evil planners’ hearts hold deceit. Counselors of peace have joy. 📖

## Proverbs 12:21
Righteous people suffer no harm. Wicked people are filled with trouble. 📖

## Proverbs 12:22
The LORD detests lying lips. Faithful dealings delight Him. 📖

## Proverbs 12:23
A shrewd person keeps knowledge private. A foolish heart announces its foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 12:24
Diligent hands rule. Laziness ends in forced labor. 📖

## Proverbs 12:25
Anxiety weighs down a heart. A good word cheers it. 📖

## Proverbs 12:26
A righteous person chooses friendships cautiously. Wicked people’s ways lead them astray. 📖

## Proverbs 12:27
A lazy person does not roast his game. A diligent person values his possession. 📖

## Proverbs 12:28
The path of righteousness holds life. Another path leads to death. 📖
