## Proverbs 26:1
Honor does not suit a fool, like summer snow or harvest rain. 📖

## Proverbs 26:2
An undeserved curse never rests, like a fluttering sparrow or darting swallow. 📖

## Proverbs 26:3
A whip suits a horse, a bridle a donkey, and a rod fools’ backs. 📖

## Proverbs 26:4
Do not answer a fool according to his foolishness, or you will resemble him. 📖

## Proverbs 26:5
Answer a fool according to his foolishness so he does not think himself wise. 📖

## Proverbs 26:6
Sending a message through a fool resembles cutting off your feet or drinking violence. 📖

## Proverbs 26:7
A proverb in a fool’s mouth resembles disabled legs hanging limp. 📖

## Proverbs 26:8
Honoring a fool resembles binding a stone into a sling. 📖

## Proverbs 26:9
A proverb in a fool’s mouth resembles a thorn entering a drunkard’s hand. 📖

## Proverbs 26:10
Hiring a fool or passerby resembles an archer wounding at random. 📖

## Proverbs 26:11
A fool repeats foolishness like a dog returning to vomit. 📖

## Proverbs 26:12
See someone considering himself wise? A fool has more hope than he does. 📖

## Proverbs 26:13
A lazy person says, “A lion is in the road! A fierce lion roams the square!” 📖

## Proverbs 26:14
A lazy person turns in bed like a door on hinges. 📖

## Proverbs 26:15
He buries his hand in the dish. Returning it to his mouth exhausts him. 📖

## Proverbs 26:16
He thinks himself wiser than seven people giving discreet answers. 📖

## Proverbs 26:17
Meddling in another’s quarrel resembles grabbing a dog’s ears while passing. 📖

## Proverbs 26:18
A madman shoots firebrands and deadly arrows. 📖

## Proverbs 26:19
Someone deceiving his neighbor and claiming he was joking resembles that man. 📖

## Proverbs 26:20
Without wood, fire dies. Without gossip, conflict ends. 📖

## Proverbs 26:21
A quarrelsome person fuels conflict like charcoal fuels embers and wood fuels fire. 📖

## Proverbs 26:22
Gossip resembles choice morsels entering the deepest being. 📖

## Proverbs 26:23
Burning lips and a wicked heart resemble glaze covering an earthen vessel. 📖

## Proverbs 26:24
A hateful person disguises himself with speech while storing deceit in his heart. 📖

## Proverbs 26:25
Distrust his gracious speech. Seven abominations fill his heart. 📖

## Proverbs 26:26
Deception may hide his hatred. His wickedness will be exposed before the assembly. 📖

## Proverbs 26:27
Dig a pit, and you will fall in. Roll a stone, and it will roll back on you. 📖

## Proverbs 26:28
A lying tongue hates its victims. A flattering mouth brings ruin. 📖
