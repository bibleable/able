## Proverbs 17:1
Better a dry morsel in peace than a house of feasting with conflict. 📖

## Proverbs 17:2
A wise servant rules a disgraceful son and shares the inheritance as one of the brothers. 📖

## Proverbs 17:3
A crucible tests silver, and a furnace tests gold. The LORD tests hearts. 📖

## Proverbs 17:4
A wicked person listens to evil lips. A liar listens to destructive tongues. 📖

## Proverbs 17:5
Mocking poor people insults their Maker. Gloating over disaster will bring punishment. 📖

## Proverbs 17:6
Grandchildren crown old people. A son’s glory is his father. 📖

## Proverbs 17:7
Eloquent words do not suit a fool. Lying lips suit a ruler even less! 📖

## Proverbs 17:8
A bribe is a charm to its giver. Wherever he turns, he succeeds. 📖

## Proverbs 17:9
Concealing an offense promotes love. Repeating it separates friends. 📖

## Proverbs 17:10
A discerning person feels rebuke more deeply than a fool feels a hundred lashes. 📖

## Proverbs 17:11
An evil person seeks only rebellion. A cruel messenger will be sent against him. 📖

## Proverbs 17:12
Better meet a bear robbed of her cubs than a fool in his foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 17:13
Repay good with evil, and evil will never leave your house. 📖

## Proverbs 17:14
Starting a quarrel releases a flood. Leave the dispute before it breaks out. 📖

## Proverbs 17:15
The LORD detests both acquitting guilty people and condemning righteous people. 📖

## Proverbs 17:16
Why should a fool have money without intending to buy wisdom? 📖

## Proverbs 17:17
A friend always loves. A brother is born for adversity. 📖

## Proverbs 17:18
Someone lacking judgment shakes hands in a pledge and guarantees his neighbor’s debt. 📖

## Proverbs 17:19
Loving sin means loving conflict. Building your gate high invites destruction. 📖

## Proverbs 17:20
A person with a perverse heart finds no good. Someone with a deceitful tongue meets trouble. 📖

## Proverbs 17:21
Fathering a fool brings grief. A fool’s father has no joy. 📖

## Proverbs 17:22
A joyful heart is good medicine. A broken spirit dries the bones. 📖

## Proverbs 17:23
A wicked person accepts a secret bribe to twist justice. 📖

## Proverbs 17:24
Discerning people focus on wisdom. A fool’s eyes wander to earth’s ends. 📖

## Proverbs 17:25
A foolish son brings his father grief and his mother bitterness. 📖

## Proverbs 17:26
Punishing innocent people is wrong. So is flogging a noble for honesty. 📖

## Proverbs 17:27
Someone with knowledge restrains his words. Someone with understanding keeps a calm spirit. 📖

## Proverbs 17:28
Even fools seem wise when silent and discerning when holding their tongues. 📖
