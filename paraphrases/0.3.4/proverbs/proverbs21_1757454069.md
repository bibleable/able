## Proverbs 21:1
A king’s heart is a waterway in the LORD’s hand. He directs it wherever He pleases. 📖

## Proverbs 21:2
People think all their ways are right. The LORD weighs the heart. 📖

## Proverbs 21:3
Righteousness and justice please the LORD more than sacrifice. 📖

## Proverbs 21:4
Proud eyes and hearts guide wicked people. They are sin. 📖

## Proverbs 21:5
Diligent plans bring plenty. Haste certainly brings poverty. 📖

## Proverbs 21:6
Wealth gained through lies is vanishing mist and a deadly pursuit. 📖

## Proverbs 21:7
Wicked people’s violence sweeps them away because they refuse justice. 📖

## Proverbs 21:8
A guilty person’s way is crooked. Innocent conduct is upright. 📖

## Proverbs 21:9
Better a roof’s corner than a shared house with a quarrelsome wife. 📖

## Proverbs 21:10
A wicked soul craves evil. His neighbor receives no favor. 📖

## Proverbs 21:11
Punishing a mocker teaches inexperienced people wisdom. Instructing a wise person increases knowledge. 📖

## Proverbs 21:12
The Righteous One examines wicked people’s house and brings them to ruin. 📖

## Proverbs 21:13
Ignore poor people’s cries, and your own cries will go unanswered. 📖

## Proverbs 21:14
A secret gift soothes anger. A concealed bribe calms great wrath. 📖

## Proverbs 21:15
Carrying out justice delights righteous people and terrifies evildoers. 📖

## Proverbs 21:16
Someone leaving understanding’s path will rest among the assembled dead. 📖

## Proverbs 21:17
Loving pleasure brings poverty. Loving wine and oil prevents riches. 📖

## Proverbs 21:18
Wicked people become a ransom for righteous people. Unfaithful people become a ransom for the upright. 📖

## Proverbs 21:19
Better desert living than life with a contentious wife quick to anger. 📖

## Proverbs 21:20
Wise homes hold precious treasure and oil. A fool consumes them. 📖

## Proverbs 21:21
Pursuing righteousness and loving devotion brings life, righteousness, and honor. 📖

## Proverbs 21:22
A wise person scales mighty people’s city and destroys their trusted stronghold. 📖

## Proverbs 21:23
Guarding your mouth and tongue protects your soul from distress. 📖

## Proverbs 21:24
“Mocker” is the name of a proud, arrogant person who acts with excessive pride. 📖

## Proverbs 21:25
A lazy person’s craving kills him. His hands refuse work. 📖

## Proverbs 21:26
He craves more all day. Righteous people give freely. 📖

## Proverbs 21:27
Wicked people’s sacrifice is detestable. How much more when offered with evil intent! 📖

## Proverbs 21:28
A lying witness perishes. A person listening to truth speaks forever. 📖

## Proverbs 21:29
A wicked person hardens his face. An upright person secures his way. 📖

## Proverbs 21:30
No wisdom, understanding, or counsel can prevail against the LORD. 📖

## Proverbs 21:31
A horse is prepared for battle day. Victory comes from the LORD. 📖
