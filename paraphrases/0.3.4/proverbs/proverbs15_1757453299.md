## Proverbs 15:1
A gentle answer turns away wrath. Harsh words stir anger. 📖

## Proverbs 15:2
Wise tongues commend knowledge. Foolish mouths pour out foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 15:3
The LORD’s eyes are everywhere. He watches evil and good people. 📖

## Proverbs 15:4
A soothing tongue is a tree of life. A perverse tongue crushes the spirit. 📖

## Proverbs 15:5
A fool rejects his father’s discipline. Accepting correction shows prudence. 📖

## Proverbs 15:6
Righteous homes hold great treasure. Wicked people’s income brings trouble. 📖

## Proverbs 15:7
Wise lips spread knowledge. Foolish hearts do not. 📖

## Proverbs 15:8
The LORD detests wicked people’s sacrifices. Upright people’s prayers delight Him. 📖

## Proverbs 15:9
The LORD detests wicked ways. He loves those pursuing righteousness. 📖

## Proverbs 15:10
Leaving the path brings harsh discipline. Hating correction brings death. 📖

## Proverbs 15:11
Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD. How much more do human hearts! 📖

## Proverbs 15:12
A mocker dislikes correction and avoids consulting wise people. 📖

## Proverbs 15:13
A joyful heart brightens the face. Heartfelt sorrow crushes the spirit. 📖

## Proverbs 15:14
A discerning heart seeks knowledge. A fool’s mouth feeds on foolishness. 📖

## Proverbs 15:15
Oppressed people’s days are all bad. A cheerful heart has a continual feast. 📖

## Proverbs 15:16
Better little wealth with fear of the LORD than great treasure with turmoil. 📖

## Proverbs 15:17
Better vegetables with love than a fattened ox with hatred. 📖

## Proverbs 15:18
A person quick to anger starts conflict. A patient person calms disputes. 📖

## Proverbs 15:19
A lazy person’s way resembles a thorn hedge. An upright person’s path is a highway. 📖

## Proverbs 15:20
A wise son brings his father joy. A foolish man despises his mother. 📖

## Proverbs 15:21
Those lacking judgment enjoy foolishness. Understanding leads a person along a straight path. 📖

## Proverbs 15:22
Plans fail without counsel. Many advisers bring success. 📖

## Proverbs 15:23
A fitting reply brings joy. A timely word is good! 📖

## Proverbs 15:24
Wise people follow life’s upward path, avoiding the descent to Sheol. 📖

## Proverbs 15:25
The LORD destroys proud people’s houses but protects widows’ boundaries. 📖

## Proverbs 15:26
The LORD detests wicked people’s thoughts. Pure people’s words please Him. 📖

## Proverbs 15:27
Greed for unjust gain troubles your household. Hating bribes brings life. 📖

## Proverbs 15:28
A righteous heart considers its answer. A wicked mouth blurts evil. 📖

## Proverbs 15:29
The LORD is far from wicked people. He hears righteous people’s prayers. 📖

## Proverbs 15:30
Bright eyes cheer the heart. Good news nourishes the bones. 📖

## Proverbs 15:31
Listening to correction that gives life places you among the wise. 📖

## Proverbs 15:32
Ignoring discipline shows self-contempt. Accepting correction brings understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 15:33
Fear of the LORD teaches wisdom. Humility precedes honor. 📖
