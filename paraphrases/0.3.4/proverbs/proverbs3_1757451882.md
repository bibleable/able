## Proverbs 3:1
My son, remember my teaching. Keep my commands in your heart. 📖

## Proverbs 3:2
They will lengthen your days and add years and peace to your life. 📖

## Proverbs 3:3
Keep loving devotion and faithfulness. Bind them around your neck. Write them on your heart’s tablet. 📖

## Proverbs 3:4
Then God and people will favor and respect you. 📖

## Proverbs 3:5
Trust the LORD wholeheartedly. Do not rely on your own understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 3:6
Acknowledge Him in every way. He will straighten your paths. 📖

## Proverbs 3:7
Do not consider yourself wise. Fear the LORD and turn from evil. 📖

## Proverbs 3:8
This brings healing to your body and refreshment to your bones. 📖

## Proverbs 3:9
Honor the LORD with your wealth and the firstfruits of every crop. 📖

## Proverbs 3:10
Then your barns will fill with abundance. Your vats will overflow with new wine. 📖

## Proverbs 3:11
My son, do not reject the LORD’s discipline or hate His rebuke. 📖

## Proverbs 3:12
The LORD disciplines those He loves, like a father disciplines the son who delights him. 📖

## Proverbs 3:13
Blessed is the person who finds wisdom and gains understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 3:14
Wisdom profits more than silver. Her gain exceeds fine gold. 📖

## Proverbs 3:15
She is more precious than rubies. Nothing you desire compares with her. 📖

## Proverbs 3:16
Her right hand holds long life. Her left holds riches and honor. 📖

## Proverbs 3:17
All her ways are pleasant. All her paths are peaceful. 📖

## Proverbs 3:18
She is a tree of life to those embracing her. Those holding her are blessed. 📖

## Proverbs 3:19
The LORD founded earth through wisdom and established heaven through understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 3:20
His knowledge broke open the watery depths and made the clouds drip dew. 📖

## Proverbs 3:21
My son, preserve sound judgment and discernment. Never lose sight of them. 📖

## Proverbs 3:22
They bring life to your soul and adorn your neck. 📖

## Proverbs 3:23
Then you will travel safely without stumbling. 📖

## Proverbs 3:24
You will lie down without fear and rest in sweet sleep. 📖

## Proverbs 3:25
Do not fear sudden danger or the wicked people’s ruin. 📖

## Proverbs 3:26
The LORD will be your confidence. He will keep your foot from the trap. 📖

## Proverbs 3:27
Do not withhold good from deserving people when you can act. 📖

## Proverbs 3:28
Do not promise your neighbor help tomorrow when you can provide it now. 📖

## Proverbs 3:29
Do not plan evil against your neighbor, who trusts you and lives beside you. 📖

## Proverbs 3:30
Do not accuse someone without cause when he has not harmed you. 📖

## Proverbs 3:31
Do not envy violent people or choose their ways. 📖

## Proverbs 3:32
The LORD detests perverse people but befriends upright people. 📖

## Proverbs 3:33
The LORD curses wicked people’s houses but blesses righteous people’s homes. 📖

## Proverbs 3:34
He mocks mockers but gives grace to humble people. 📖

## Proverbs 3:35
The wise inherit honor. Fools are exposed to shame. 📖
