## Proverbs 4:1
My sons, listen to a father’s instruction. Pay attention and gain understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 4:2
I give sound teaching. Do not abandon my instructions. 📖

## Proverbs 4:3
I was once my father’s young son and my mother’s only child. 📖

## Proverbs 4:4
He taught me, “Hold my words in your heart. Keep my commands, and you will live. 📖

## Proverbs 4:5
Gain wisdom and understanding. Remember my words and never turn from them. 📖

## Proverbs 4:6
Keep wisdom, and she will preserve you. Love her, and she will guard you. 📖

## Proverbs 4:7
Wisdom is supreme, so acquire it. Whatever else you acquire, gain understanding. 📖

## Proverbs 4:8
Value her, and she will exalt you. Embrace her, and she will honor you. 📖

## Proverbs 4:9
She will place a graceful garland on your head and give you a beautiful crown.” 📖

## Proverbs 4:10
My son, listen and accept my words. Your years will be many. 📖

## Proverbs 4:11
I will guide you in wisdom’s way and lead you on straight paths. 📖

## Proverbs 4:12
Nothing will hinder your steps when you walk. You will run without stumbling. 📖

## Proverbs 4:13
Hold instruction firmly. Guard it and never release it, because it is your life. 📖

## Proverbs 4:14
Do not enter wicked people’s paths or follow evildoers’ ways. 📖

## Proverbs 4:15
Avoid that road. Turn away and pass it by. 📖

## Proverbs 4:16
They cannot sleep without doing evil. They lose sleep until they make someone fall. 📖

## Proverbs 4:17
They eat wickedness’s bread and drink violence’s wine. 📖

## Proverbs 4:18
The righteous people’s path resembles dawn’s first light. It shines increasingly brightly until midday. 📖

## Proverbs 4:19
Wicked people’s way resembles deepest darkness. They do not know what makes them stumble. 📖

## Proverbs 4:20
My son, attend to my words and listen to my sayings. 📖

## Proverbs 4:21
Keep them in view and within your heart. 📖

## Proverbs 4:22
They bring life to those finding them and health to the whole body. 📖

## Proverbs 4:23
Guard your heart diligently. Life’s springs flow from it. 📖

## Proverbs 4:24
Remove deception from your mouth. Keep perverse speech from your lips. 📖

## Proverbs 4:25
Look forward. Fix your eyes straight ahead. 📖

## Proverbs 4:26
Level the path for your feet. All your ways will be secure. 📖

## Proverbs 4:27
Turn neither right nor left. Keep your feet from evil. 📖
