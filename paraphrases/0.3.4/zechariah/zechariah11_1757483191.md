## Zechariah 11:1
Lebanon, open your doors so fire may consume your cedars! 🔥
## Zechariah 11:2
Cypress, wail because the cedar fell and the majestic trees are ruined! Bashan’s oaks, wail because the thick forest was cut down! 🌳
## Zechariah 11:3
Listen to the shepherds wailing because their glory is ruined. Listen to young lions roaring because the Jordan’s thickets are destroyed. 🦁
## Zechariah 11:4
The LORD my God says, “Feed the flock marked for slaughter. 🐑
## Zechariah 11:5
Its buyers kill without regret, and its sellers say, ‘Bless the LORD! I am rich!’ Even its own shepherds show no compassion. 💔
## Zechariah 11:6
I will no longer show compassion to this land’s people,” declares the LORD. “I will hand each over to his neighbor and king. They will devastate the land, and I will not rescue it from them.” ⚖️
## Zechariah 11:7
I fed the flock marked for slaughter, especially its suffering sheep. I took two staffs, naming one Favor and the other Union, and tended the flock. 🐑
## Zechariah 11:8
In one month, I dismissed three shepherds. I became impatient with the flock, and they hated me. ⚠️
## Zechariah 11:9
I said, “I will no longer shepherd you. Let the dying die and the perishing perish. Let the survivors eat each other’s flesh.” 💔
## Zechariah 11:10
I cut my staff Favor in two. This canceled the covenant I made with all the nations. 🪵
## Zechariah 11:11
It was canceled that day. The suffering sheep watching me knew this was the LORD’s word. 📖
## Zechariah 11:12
I said, “If you think it right, give me my wages. Otherwise, keep them.” They weighed out thirty pieces of silver as my wages. ⚖️
## Zechariah 11:13
The LORD said, “Throw it to the potter.” That was the magnificent price they valued me at. I took the thirty silver pieces and threw them to the potter in the LORD’s house. 🏺
## Zechariah 11:14
I cut my second staff, Union, in two. This broke the brotherhood between Judah and Israel. 🪵
## Zechariah 11:15
The LORD said, “Take a foolish shepherd’s equipment again. 🐑
## Zechariah 11:16
I will raise a shepherd in the land. He will not care for lost sheep, seek the young, heal the injured, or feed the healthy. He will eat the best sheep’s flesh and tear off their hooves. ⚠️
## Zechariah 11:17
Woe to the worthless shepherd who abandons the flock! May a sword strike his arm and right eye! May his arm completely wither and his right eye become completely blind!” ⚔️
