## Zechariah 7:1
The LORD’s word came to Zechariah in King Darius’s fourth year, on Chislev’s fourth day. Chislev was the ninth month. 📅
## Zechariah 7:2
Bethel’s people sent Sharezer, Regem-melech, and their men to plead before the LORD. 🙏
## Zechariah 7:3
They asked the LORD of Hosts’ temple priests and the prophets, “Should I weep and fast in the fifth month, as for these many years?” ❓
## Zechariah 7:4
The LORD of Hosts’ word came to me: 📖
## Zechariah 7:5
“Ask all the land’s people and priests this: ‘You fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months for seventy years. Did you really fast for Me? ⏳
## Zechariah 7:6
When you ate and drank, was it not simply for yourselves? 🍞
## Zechariah 7:7
Are these not the LORD’s words through the earlier prophets? Jerusalem and nearby towns then had many people and prosperity. People lived in the Negev and foothills.’” 🏙️
## Zechariah 7:8
The LORD’s word came to Zechariah: 📖
## Zechariah 7:9
“The LORD of Hosts says, ‘Carry out true justice. Show faithful love and compassion to each other. ❤️
## Zechariah 7:10
Do not oppress widows, children without fathers, foreigners, or poor people. Do not plan evil against each other in your hearts.’ ⚖️
## Zechariah 7:11
They refused to listen, stubbornly turned away, and blocked their ears. ⚠️
## Zechariah 7:12
They made their hearts like flint and refused to hear the law. They rejected the LORD of Hosts’ words sent by His Spirit through earlier prophets. The LORD of Hosts therefore became very angry. 🪨
## Zechariah 7:13
I called, but they would not listen. When they called, I would not listen,” says the LORD of Hosts. 💔
## Zechariah 7:14
“I scattered them with a whirlwind among unfamiliar nations. The land behind them became deserted, with no one coming or going. They turned the pleasant land into a wasteland.” 🌪️
