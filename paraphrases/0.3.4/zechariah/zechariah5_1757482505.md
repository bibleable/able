## Zechariah 5:1
I looked up again and saw a flying scroll. 📜
## Zechariah 5:2
The angel asked, “What do you see?” I answered, “A flying scroll, twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide.” 📏
## Zechariah 5:3
He said, “This curse goes over the whole land. According to one side of the scroll, every thief will be removed. According to the other, everyone who swears falsely will be removed. ⚖️
## Zechariah 5:4
The LORD of Hosts declares, ‘I will send it into every thief’s house and every house of those swearing falsely by My name. It will stay there and destroy the house, including its wood and stones.’” 🏠
## Zechariah 5:5
The angel speaking with me came forward and said, “Look up and see what approaches.” 👁️
## Zechariah 5:6
I asked, “What is it?” He answered, “A measuring basket is coming.” He added, “This is their wrongdoing throughout the land.” 🧺
## Zechariah 5:7
The lead cover was lifted. A woman sat inside the basket. 👩
## Zechariah 5:8
He said, “This is Wickedness.” He pushed her into the basket and pressed its lead cover over the opening. ⚠️
## Zechariah 5:9
I looked up and saw two women approaching, with wind in their wings. Their wings were like a stork’s. They lifted the basket between heaven and earth. 🪽
## Zechariah 5:10
I asked the angel speaking with me, “Where are they taking the basket?” ❓
## Zechariah 5:11
He answered, “To build a house for it in Shinar. When the house is ready, the basket will stand there on its pedestal.” 🏠
