## Zechariah 14:1
A day of the LORD is coming when your stolen goods will be divided before you. ⚠️
## Zechariah 14:2
“I will gather all nations to fight Jerusalem. The city will be captured, houses robbed, and women raped. Half the city will go into exile. The remaining people will stay in the city.” ⚔️
## Zechariah 14:3
The LORD will go out and fight those nations, as He fights on a battle day. 🛡️
## Zechariah 14:4
On that day, His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem. The mountain will split east to west, making a great valley. Half will move north, and half south. ⛰️
## Zechariah 14:5
You will escape through My mountain valley, which will reach Azal. You will flee as during the earthquake in Uzziah king of Judah’s days. The LORD my God will come with all the holy ones. 🚶
## Zechariah 14:6
On that day, there will be no light, cold, or frost. 🌌
## Zechariah 14:7
This unique day, known only to the LORD, will have neither day nor night. Yet light will appear at evening. ✨
## Zechariah 14:8
On that day, living water will flow from Jerusalem. Half will flow to the Eastern Sea and half to the Western Sea, in both summer and winter. 💧
## Zechariah 14:9
The LORD will become King over all the earth that day. The LORD alone, and His name alone! 👑
## Zechariah 14:10
The land from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem, will become a plain. Jerusalem will rise and remain in place. It will extend from the Benjamin Gate through the First Gate’s site to the Corner Gate. It will reach from the Tower of Hananel to the royal winepresses. 🏙️
## Zechariah 14:11
People will live there, safe from complete destruction ever again. Jerusalem will be secure. 🛡️
## Zechariah 14:12
The LORD will strike everyone who fought Jerusalem with this plague. Their flesh will rot as they stand. Their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues in their mouths. ⚠️
## Zechariah 14:13
On that day, the LORD will cause great panic among them. Each will grab another’s hand and attack the other. ⚔️
## Zechariah 14:14
Judah will also fight at Jerusalem. Great amounts of gold, silver, and clothing from all surrounding nations will be gathered. 💰
## Zechariah 14:15
A similar plague will strike the horses, mules, camels, donkeys, and every animal in those camps. 🐎
## Zechariah 14:16
Survivors from all nations that attacked Jerusalem will come each year to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts. They will celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles. 🙏
## Zechariah 14:17
If any of earth’s families do not come to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts, they will receive no rain. 🌧️
## Zechariah 14:18
If Egypt’s people do not come and enter, they too will receive no rain. This is the plague with which the LORD will strike nations that do not come to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles. ⚖️
## Zechariah 14:19
This will punish Egypt and every nation that does not come to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles. ⚠️
## Zechariah 14:20
On that day, the horses’ bells will bear the words HOLY TO THE LORD. Cooking pots in the LORD’s house will be like sprinkling bowls before the altar. 🔔
## Zechariah 14:21
Every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to the LORD of Hosts. All who sacrifice will come, take pots, and cook in them. On that day, no Canaanite will remain in the LORD of Hosts’ house. 🙏
