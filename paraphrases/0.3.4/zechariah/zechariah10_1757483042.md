## Zechariah 10:1
Ask the LORD for spring rain. He makes storm clouds and will give everyone rain showers and crops in the fields. 🌧️
## Zechariah 10:2
Idols deceive, and diviners see illusions. They tell false dreams and give empty comfort. The people therefore wander like sheep, oppressed without a shepherd. 🐑
## Zechariah 10:3
“My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the leaders. The LORD of Hosts cares for Judah’s house, His flock. He will make them like His royal battle horse. 🐎
## Zechariah 10:4
The cornerstone, tent peg, battle bow, and every ruler will come from Judah. 🏹
## Zechariah 10:5
They will fight like mighty men, trampling enemies in the streets’ mud. They will fight because the LORD is with them and put horsemen to shame. 🛡️
## Zechariah 10:6
I will strengthen Judah’s house and save Joseph’s house. My compassion will restore them as though I had never rejected them. I am the LORD their God and will answer them. ❤️
## Zechariah 10:7
Ephraim will be like a mighty man, with hearts glad as with wine. Their children will see and rejoice. Their hearts will rejoice in the LORD. 🎉
## Zechariah 10:8
I will whistle to gather them because I redeemed them. They will be as numerous as before. 📣
## Zechariah 10:9
Although I sow them among nations, they will remember Me in distant lands. They and their children will live and return. 🌍
## Zechariah 10:10
I will bring them back from Egypt and gather them from Assyria. I will bring them to Gilead and Lebanon until no more room remains. 🏠
## Zechariah 10:11
They will cross the sea of distress and strike its waves. The Nile’s depths will dry up. Assyria’s pride will fall, and Egypt’s scepter will depart. 🌊
## Zechariah 10:12
I will strengthen them in the LORD. They will walk in His name,” declares the LORD. 🚶
