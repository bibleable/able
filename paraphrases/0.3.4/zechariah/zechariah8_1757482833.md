## Zechariah 8:1
The LORD of Hosts’ word came to me again: 📖
## Zechariah 8:2
“The LORD of Hosts says, ‘I am intensely jealous for Zion. My jealousy for her burns deeply.’ ❤️
## Zechariah 8:3
The LORD says, ‘I will return to Zion and live in Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be called the City of Truth, and the LORD of Hosts’ mountain will be called the Holy Mountain.’ 🏙️
## Zechariah 8:4
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘Old men and women will sit along Jerusalem’s streets again. Each will hold a staff because of great age. 👴
## Zechariah 8:5
The city’s streets will be full of boys and girls playing.’ 👧
## Zechariah 8:6
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘If this seems impossible to this people’s remnant now, should it seem impossible to Me?’ declares the LORD of Hosts. 🙏
## Zechariah 8:7
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘I will save My people from the eastern and western lands. 🛡️
## Zechariah 8:8
I will bring them back to live in Jerusalem. They will be My people, and I will be their faithful, righteous God.’ ❤️
## Zechariah 8:9
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘Strengthen your hands, you who hear the prophets’ words now. These prophets were present when foundations were laid to rebuild the LORD of Hosts’ temple. 🏗️
## Zechariah 8:10
Before then, neither people nor animals received wages. Enemies made travel unsafe. I had turned everyone against their neighbor. ⚠️
## Zechariah 8:11
Now I will treat this people’s remnant differently from before,’ declares the LORD of Hosts. 🌅
## Zechariah 8:12
‘The seed will prosper, vines will bear fruit, the ground will produce crops, and the skies will give dew. I will give this people’s remnant all these things as an inheritance. 🌾
## Zechariah 8:13
Judah’s house and Israel’s house, you were a curse among the nations. I will save you, and you will become a blessing. Do not fear. Strengthen your hands.’ 💪
## Zechariah 8:14
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘When your fathers angered Me, I resolved to bring disaster on you. I did not change My decision,’ says the LORD of Hosts. ⚖️
## Zechariah 8:15
‘Now I have resolved to do good to Jerusalem and Judah again. Do not fear. 🌅
## Zechariah 8:16
Do these things: speak truth to each other and give true, sound judgments at your gates. ⚖️
## Zechariah 8:17
Do not plan evil against your neighbor in your hearts or love swearing falsely. I hate all these things,’ declares the LORD.” ⚠️
## Zechariah 8:18
The LORD of Hosts’ word came to me: 📖
## Zechariah 8:19
“The LORD of Hosts says, ‘The fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months’ fasts will become joyful, cheerful feasts for Judah’s house. Therefore, love truth and peace.’ 🕊️
## Zechariah 8:20
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘People from many cities will yet come. 🏙️
## Zechariah 8:21
One city’s people will go to another and say, “Let us go now to plead before the LORD and seek the LORD of Hosts. I am going myself.” 🙏
## Zechariah 8:22
Many peoples and powerful nations will come to Jerusalem to seek the LORD of Hosts and plead before the LORD.’ 🌍
## Zechariah 8:23
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘In those days, ten men from nations of every language will tightly grasp a Jew’s robe. They will say, “Let us go with you. We heard that God is with you.”’” 🤝
