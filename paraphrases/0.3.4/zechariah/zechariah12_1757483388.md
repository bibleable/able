## Zechariah 12:1
This is the LORD’s message concerning Israel. He stretches out the heavens, lays earth’s foundation, and forms each person’s spirit within them. He declares: 📖
## Zechariah 12:2
“I will make Jerusalem a cup that makes all nearby peoples drunk. Judah and Jerusalem will be surrounded in war. 🍷
## Zechariah 12:3
On that day, all earth’s nations will gather against Jerusalem. I will make her a heavy stone for all peoples. Anyone trying to lift her away will suffer severe injury. 🪨
## Zechariah 12:4
On that day,” declares the LORD, “I will strike every horse with panic and every rider with madness. I will watch over Judah’s house, but blind all the nations’ horses. 🐎
## Zechariah 12:5
Judah’s leaders will think, ‘Jerusalem’s people are my strength because the LORD of Hosts is their God.’ 🛡️
## Zechariah 12:6
On that day, I will make Judah’s clans like a firepot among wood and a burning torch among grain bundles. They will consume all surrounding peoples to their right and left. Jerusalem’s people will remain safe there. 🔥
## Zechariah 12:7
The LORD will save Judah’s tents first. This is so David’s house and Jerusalem’s people will not have greater glory than Judah. ⛺
## Zechariah 12:8
On that day, the LORD will defend Jerusalem’s people, so even the weakest will be like David. David’s house will be like God, like the LORD’s angel going before them. 🛡️
## Zechariah 12:9
On that day, I will set out to destroy every nation coming against Jerusalem. ⚖️
## Zechariah 12:10
I will pour a spirit of grace and prayer on David’s house and Jerusalem’s people. They will look on Me, the One they pierced. They will mourn for Him as for an only child and grieve bitterly as for a firstborn son. 💔
## Zechariah 12:11
Jerusalem’s wailing that day will be as great as Hadad-rimmon’s wailing on Megiddo’s plain. 💧
## Zechariah 12:12
The land will mourn, each clan on its own: David’s house and their wives, Nathan’s house and their wives, 💔
## Zechariah 12:13
Levi’s house and their wives, Shimei’s clan and their wives, 💔
## Zechariah 12:14
and every remaining clan and their wives. 💔
