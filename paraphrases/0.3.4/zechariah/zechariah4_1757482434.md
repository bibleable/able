## Zechariah 4:1
The angel speaking with me returned and woke me, as someone wakes a sleeping man. 👼
## Zechariah 4:2
He asked, “What do you see?” I answered, “A solid gold lampstand with a bowl on top, seven lamps, and seven spouts to the lamps. 🕯️
## Zechariah 4:3
Two olive trees stand beside it, one on each side of the bowl.” 🌳
## Zechariah 4:4
I asked the angel speaking with me, “My lord, what are these?” ❓
## Zechariah 4:5
The angel asked, “Do you not know?” I answered, “No, my lord.” 👼
## Zechariah 4:6
He said, “The LORD tells Zerubbabel, ‘This comes through My Spirit, rather than might or power,’ says the LORD of Hosts. 🕊️
## Zechariah 4:7
Great mountain, what are you? You will become level ground before Zerubbabel. He will bring out the capstone as people shout, ‘Grace, grace to it!’” ⛰️
## Zechariah 4:8
The LORD’s word came to me: 📖
## Zechariah 4:9
“Zerubbabel’s hands laid this house’s foundation, and his hands will finish it. You will know that the LORD of Hosts sent me to you. 🏗️
## Zechariah 4:10
Who has despised the day of small things? The LORD’s seven eyes, which scan the whole earth, will rejoice at the plumb line in Zerubbabel’s hand.” 👁️
## Zechariah 4:11
I asked the angel, “What are the two olive trees on the lampstand’s right and left?” 🌳
## Zechariah 4:12
I also asked, “What are the two olive branches beside the two gold pipes from which golden oil pours?” 🫒
## Zechariah 4:13
He asked, “Do you not know?” I answered, “No, my lord.” ❓
## Zechariah 4:14
He said, “These are the two anointed ones standing beside the Lord of all the earth.” 🙏
