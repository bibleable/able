## Zechariah 3:1
The angel showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the LORD’s angel. Satan stood at Joshua’s right hand to accuse him. ⚖️
## Zechariah 3:2
The LORD said, “Satan, the LORD rebukes you! The LORD who chose Jerusalem rebukes you! Is this man not a burning stick rescued from fire?” 🔥
## Zechariah 3:3
Joshua stood before the angel in filthy clothes. 👕
## Zechariah 3:4
The angel told those nearby, “Remove his filthy clothes!” He told Joshua, “I removed your wrongdoing. I will dress you in splendid robes.” ✨
## Zechariah 3:5
I said, “Put a clean turban on his head.” They put it on and dressed him while the LORD’s angel stood nearby. 👳
## Zechariah 3:6
The LORD’s angel gave Joshua this charge: 📖
## Zechariah 3:7
“The LORD of Hosts says, ‘If you follow My ways and instructions, you will govern My house and oversee My courts. I will give you a place among those standing here. 🙏
## Zechariah 3:8
High priest Joshua, listen with your companions seated before you. You are a sign. I will bring My servant, the Branch. 🌱
## Zechariah 3:9
Look at the stone I placed before Joshua. That one stone has seven eyes. I will engrave an inscription on it,’ declares the LORD of Hosts. ‘I will remove this land’s wrongdoing in one day. 🪨
## Zechariah 3:10
On that day, each of you will invite your neighbor to sit under your own vine and fig tree,’ declares the LORD of Hosts.” 🌳
