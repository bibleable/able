## Zechariah 2:1
I looked up and saw a man holding a measuring line. 📏
## Zechariah 2:2
I asked, “Where are you going?” He answered, “To measure Jerusalem’s width and length.” 🏙️
## Zechariah 2:3
The angel speaking with me went forward. Another angel came to meet him 👼
## Zechariah 2:4
and said, “Run and tell that young man, ‘Jerusalem will have no walls because of its many people and animals. 🏙️
## Zechariah 2:5
I will be a wall of fire around it and the glory within it,’ declares the LORD.” 🔥
## Zechariah 2:6
“Get up! Get up! Escape from the northern land,” declares the LORD. “I scattered you like heaven’s four winds,” declares the LORD. 🌬️
## Zechariah 2:7
“Get up, Zion! You who live with the Daughter of Babylon, escape!” 🚶
## Zechariah 2:8
The LORD of Hosts says, “His Glory has sent Me against the nations that robbed you. Whoever touches you touches the apple of His eye. 🛡️
## Zechariah 2:9
After this, I will surely wave My hand over them. Their own servants will rob them. You will know that the LORD of Hosts sent Me.” ⚖️
## Zechariah 2:10
“Daughter of Zion, shout joyfully and be glad! I am coming to live among you,” declares the LORD. 🎉
## Zechariah 2:11
“On that day, many nations will join the LORD and become My people. I will live among you. You will know that the LORD of Hosts sent Me to you. 🤝
## Zechariah 2:12
The LORD will possess Judah as His portion in the Holy Land. He will choose Jerusalem again. 🏙️
## Zechariah 2:13
Everyone, be silent before the LORD! He has risen from His holy dwelling.” 🙏
