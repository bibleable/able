## Zechariah 9:1
This is the LORD’s message against Hadrach’s land, with Damascus as its resting place. People and all Israel’s tribes look to the LORD. 📖
## Zechariah 9:2
The message is also against neighboring Hamath, and Tyre and Sidon, although they are very shrewd. 🏙️
## Zechariah 9:3
Tyre built a fortress and piled up silver like dust and gold like street dirt. 💰
## Zechariah 9:4
The Lord will make her poor and throw her wealth into the sea. Fire will consume her. 🔥
## Zechariah 9:5
Ashkelon will see and fear. Gaza and Ekron will twist in pain because Ekron’s hope fades. Gaza will have no king, and Ashkelon will have no inhabitants. ⚠️
## Zechariah 9:6
“People of mixed ancestry will occupy Ashdod. I will remove the Philistines’ pride. ⚖️
## Zechariah 9:7
I will remove blood from their mouths and disgusting things from between their teeth. They too will become a remnant for our God, like a clan in Judah. Ekron will be like the Jebusites. 🙏
## Zechariah 9:8
I will camp around My house against armies marching back and forth. No oppressor will overrun My people again. I now watch with My own eyes.” 🛡️
## Zechariah 9:9
Daughter of Zion, rejoice greatly! Daughter of Jerusalem, shout in victory! Your King comes, righteous and victorious. He is humble and rides a donkey, a colt born to a donkey. 👑
## Zechariah 9:10
“I will remove Ephraim’s chariots and Jerusalem’s horses. The battle bow will be broken. He will announce peace to the nations. His rule will reach from sea to sea, from the Euphrates to earth’s ends. 🕊️
## Zechariah 9:11
Because of My covenant’s blood, I will release your prisoners from the pit without water. ⛓️
## Zechariah 9:12
Prisoners of hope, return to your stronghold. Today I declare that I will restore double to you. 🌅
## Zechariah 9:13
I will bend Judah as My bow and fit it with Ephraim. Zion, I will rouse your sons against Greece’s sons. I will make you like a mighty man’s sword.” 🏹
## Zechariah 9:14
The LORD will appear over them, and His arrow will flash like lightning. The Lord GOD will blow the ram’s horn and advance in southern whirlwinds. ⚡
## Zechariah 9:15
The LORD of Hosts will shield them. They will destroy and conquer with slingstones and drink and roar as with wine. They will be filled like sprinkling bowls and drenched like the altar’s corners. 🛡️
## Zechariah 9:16
On that day, the LORD their God will save them as His people’s flock. They will sparkle over His land like jewels in a crown. 💎
## Zechariah 9:17
How lovely and beautiful they will be! Grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine will make the young women flourish. 🌾
