## Zechariah 1:1
In Darius’s second year, during the eighth month, the LORD’s word came to the prophet Zechariah. He was Berechiah’s son and Iddo’s grandson. 📖
## Zechariah 1:2
“The LORD was very angry with your fathers. ⚠️
## Zechariah 1:3
Tell the people what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Return to Me, and I will return to you,’ declares the LORD of Hosts. 🙏
## Zechariah 1:4
Do not be like your fathers. Earlier prophets told them the LORD of Hosts said, ‘Turn from your evil ways and actions.’ But they did not listen or pay attention to Me,” declares the LORD. ⚠️
## Zechariah 1:5
“Where are your fathers now? Do the prophets live forever? ⏳
## Zechariah 1:6
Did My words and laws, which I commanded My servants the prophets, not overtake your fathers? They repented and said, ‘The LORD of Hosts did what He planned for us according to our ways and actions.’” ⚖️
## Zechariah 1:7
The LORD’s word came to the prophet Zechariah on Shebat’s twenty-fourth day, in Darius’s second year. Shebat was the eleventh month. Zechariah was Berechiah’s son and Iddo’s grandson. 📅
## Zechariah 1:8
At night, I saw a man riding a red horse among myrtle trees in a hollow. Behind him were red, sorrel, and white horses. 🐎
## Zechariah 1:9
I asked, “My lord, what are these?” The angel speaking with me answered, “I will show you.” ❓
## Zechariah 1:10
The man among the myrtle trees said, “These are the ones the LORD sent to patrol the earth.” 🌍
## Zechariah 1:11
The riders answered the LORD’s angel among the myrtle trees, “We patrolled the earth. The whole earth is quiet and at rest.” 🕊️
## Zechariah 1:12
The LORD’s angel asked, “LORD of Hosts, how long will You withhold mercy from Jerusalem and Judah’s cities? You have been angry with them for seventy years.” 🙏
## Zechariah 1:13
The LORD spoke kind, comforting words to the angel speaking with me. ❤️
## Zechariah 1:14
The angel told me, “Announce what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘I am very jealous for Jerusalem and Zion. 📣
## Zechariah 1:15
I am fiercely angry with the nations at ease. I was a little angry, but they increased the disaster.’ ⚖️
## Zechariah 1:16
Therefore, the LORD says, ‘I will return to Jerusalem with mercy. My house will be rebuilt there,’ declares the LORD of Hosts. ‘A measuring line will be stretched over Jerusalem.’ 🏗️
## Zechariah 1:17
Also announce what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘My cities will overflow with prosperity again. The LORD will comfort Zion and choose Jerusalem again.’” 🌅
## Zechariah 1:18
I looked up and saw four horns. 👁️
## Zechariah 1:19
I asked the angel speaking with me, “What are these?” He answered, “The horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.” ❓
## Zechariah 1:20
The LORD showed me four craftsmen. 🔨
## Zechariah 1:21
I asked, “What will they do?” He answered, “These horns scattered Judah until no one could lift his head. The craftsmen came to frighten and overthrow the nations’ horns that rose against Judah to scatter it.” ⚖️
