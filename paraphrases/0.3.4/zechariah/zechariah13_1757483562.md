## Zechariah 13:1
“On that day, a fountain will open for David’s house and Jerusalem’s people to cleanse them from sin and impurity. 💧
## Zechariah 13:2
On that day,” declares the LORD of Hosts, “I will erase the idols’ names from the land. They will no longer be remembered. I will remove the prophets and the spirit of impurity from the land. ⚖️
## Zechariah 13:3
If anyone still prophesies, his own parents will say, ‘You must die because you spoke falsely in the LORD’s name.’ When he prophesies, his own father and mother will pierce him through. ⚠️
## Zechariah 13:4
On that day, every prophet will be ashamed of his vision when he prophesies. He will no longer wear a hairy cloak to deceive. 👕
## Zechariah 13:5
He will say, ‘I am not a prophet. I work the land because I was bought as a servant in my youth.’ 🌾
## Zechariah 13:6
If someone asks, ‘What are these wounds on your chest?’ he will answer, ‘I received them in my friends’ house.’ ❓
## Zechariah 13:7
Sword, wake against My Shepherd, the man who is My Companion,” declares the LORD of Hosts. “Strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will scatter. I will turn My hand against the little ones. ⚔️
## Zechariah 13:8
“Throughout the land,” declares the LORD, “two-thirds will be cut off and die. One-third will remain. ⚖️
## Zechariah 13:9
I will bring this third through fire, refine them like silver, and test them like gold. They will call My name, and I will answer. I will say, ‘They are My people.’ They will say, ‘The LORD is our God.’” 🔥
