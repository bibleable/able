## Zechariah 6:1
I looked up again and saw four chariots coming from between two bronze mountains. ⛰️
## Zechariah 6:2
The first chariot had red horses, and the second had black horses. 🐎
## Zechariah 6:3
The third had white horses, and the fourth had dappled horses. All the horses were strong. 🐎
## Zechariah 6:4
I asked the angel speaking with me, “My lord, what are these?” ❓
## Zechariah 6:5
He answered, “These are heaven’s four spirits. They go from their place before the Lord of all the earth. 🌍
## Zechariah 6:6
The black horses go north, the white horses west, and the dappled horses south.” 🧭
## Zechariah 6:7
The strong horses went out, eager to patrol the earth. The LORD said, “Go and patrol the earth.” They did so. 🐎
## Zechariah 6:8
The LORD called me and said, “Those going north have given My Spirit rest in the northern land.” 🕊️
## Zechariah 6:9
The LORD’s word came to me: 📖
## Zechariah 6:10
“Take an offering from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, the exiles who arrived from Babylon. That same day, go to Josiah son of Zephaniah’s house. 🏠
## Zechariah 6:11
Take silver and gold and make a decorated crown. Put it on high priest Joshua son of Jehozadak’s head. 👑
## Zechariah 6:12
Tell him what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Here is the man named the Branch. He will branch out from His place and build the LORD’s temple. 🌱
## Zechariah 6:13
He will build the LORD’s temple, wear splendor, sit on His throne, and rule. He will be a priest on His throne. There will be peaceful counsel between the two.’ 👑
## Zechariah 6:14
The crown will remain in the LORD’s temple. It will be a memorial to Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Zephaniah’s gracious son. 🙏
## Zechariah 6:15
Even those far away will come and build the LORD’s temple. You will know that the LORD of Hosts sent Me to you. This will happen if you carefully obey the LORD your God.” 🏗️
