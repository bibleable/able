## Nehemiah 4:1
Sanballat heard we rebuilt the wall and became furious. He mocked the Jews. 😠
## Nehemiah 4:2
Before his associates and Samaria’s army, he said, “What do these weak Jews do? Can they restore it themselves? Will they sacrifice? Will they finish in one day? Can they revive burned stones from rubble?” ⚠️
## Nehemiah 4:3
Tobiah the Ammonite stood beside him. He said, “Even a fox on their wall would break it down!” 🦊
## Nehemiah 4:4
Our God, hear us, because we are despised. Turn their scorn back on them. Let them be taken as plunder to a land of captivity. 🙏
## Nehemiah 4:5
Do not cover their guilt or erase their sin from Your sight. They provoked the builders. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 4:6
We joined the whole wall to half its height because the people wanted to work. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 4:7
Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabs, Ammonites, and Ashdodites heard that repairs progressed and gaps closed. They were furious. 😠
## Nehemiah 4:8
They plotted together to attack Jerusalem and hinder us. ⚔️
## Nehemiah 4:9
We prayed and posted guards against them day and night. 🛡️
## Nehemiah 4:10
Judah’s people said, “The workers’ strength fails. There is too much rubble. We can never rebuild the wall.” 💔
## Nehemiah 4:11
Our enemies said, “Before they know or see anything, we will enter among them, kill them, and stop the work.” ⚠️
## Nehemiah 4:12
Nearby Jews warned us ten times, “Wherever you turn, they will attack us.” 📣
## Nehemiah 4:13
I placed men by families behind the wall’s lowest, weakest places. They held swords, spears, and bows. 🛡️
## Nehemiah 4:14
I inspected and told the nobles, officials, and people, “Do not fear them. Remember the great and awesome Lord. Fight for your brothers, sons, daughters, wives, and homes.” 💪
## Nehemiah 4:15
Our enemies heard we knew their plan and God defeated it. We returned to our own wall work. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 4:16
From then on, half my servants worked while half held spears, shields, bows, and armor. Officers stood behind Judah’s people. 🛡️
## Nehemiah 4:17
These people rebuilt the wall. Those carrying materials worked with one hand and held a weapon with the other. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 4:18
Every builder wore his sword at his side. The trumpeter stayed beside me. ⚔️
## Nehemiah 4:19
I told the nobles, officials, and people, “The work is large and spread out. We are far apart along the wall. 🧱
## Nehemiah 4:20
Gather where you hear the horn. Our God will fight for us!” 📯
## Nehemiah 4:21
We continued working, while half the men held spears from dawn until the stars appeared. 🌅
## Nehemiah 4:22
I said, “Let everyone and his servant stay in Jerusalem at night. They can guard by night and work by day.” 🛡️
## Nehemiah 4:23
My brothers, servants, guards, and I did not change our clothes. Each carried his weapon, even when getting water. ⚔️
