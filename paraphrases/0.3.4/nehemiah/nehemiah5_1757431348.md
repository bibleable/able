## Nehemiah 5:1
The people and their wives raised a great complaint against their fellow Jews. 📣
## Nehemiah 5:2
Some said, “We and our sons and daughters are many. We need grain to eat and live.” 🍞
## Nehemiah 5:3
Others said, “We mortgage our fields, vineyards, and houses to get grain during the famine.” 💔
## Nehemiah 5:4
Others said, “We borrowed money to pay the king’s tax on our fields and vineyards. 💰
## Nehemiah 5:5
We and our children are like our countrymen and their children, but we make our sons and daughters slaves. Some daughters are already enslaved. We cannot redeem them, because others own our fields and vineyards.” ⛓️
## Nehemiah 5:6
Their complaints made me very angry. 😠
## Nehemiah 5:7
After serious thought, I corrected the nobles and officials. I said, “You demand interest from your own brothers!” I called a large assembly against them. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 5:8
I said, “We worked to buy back Jews sold to foreigners. Now you sell your brothers so we must buy them back!” They were silent, with no answer. ⛓️
## Nehemiah 5:9
I continued, “This is wrong. Should you not fear our God to avoid our foreign enemies’ scorn? ⚠️
## Nehemiah 5:10
My brothers, servants, and I also lend money and grain. Let us stop charging interest. 🤝
## Nehemiah 5:11
Return their fields, vineyards, olive groves, and houses immediately. Also return the percentage of money, grain, new wine, and oil you charged.” ⚖️
## Nehemiah 5:12
They replied, “We will return it and demand nothing more. We will do as you say.” I called the priests and made the nobles and officials swear to keep their promise. 👥
## Nehemiah 5:13
I shook my robe’s folds and said, “May God shake out anyone who breaks this promise from his house and possessions. May he be shaken out with nothing!” Everyone said, “Amen,” and praised the LORD. They kept their promise. 🙏
## Nehemiah 5:14
Artaxerxes appointed me governor in Judah from his twentieth to thirty-second year, twelve years in all. My brothers and I did not eat the governor’s allotted food. 👑
## Nehemiah 5:15
Earlier governors burdened people with bread, wine, and forty silver shekels. Their servants oppressed people too. I did not do this because I feared God. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 5:16
I devoted myself to wall construction, and all my servants worked there. We acquired no land. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 5:17
My table fed 150 Jews and officials, besides guests from surrounding nations. 🍽️
## Nehemiah 5:18
Each day, one ox, six fine sheep, and birds were prepared for me. Every ten days, plentiful wine of every kind arrived. I did not demand the governor’s food because the people’s burden was heavy. 🍞
## Nehemiah 5:19
My God, remember me favorably for everything I did for these people. 🙏
