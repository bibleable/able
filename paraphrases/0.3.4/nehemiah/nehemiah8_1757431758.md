## Nehemiah 8:1
Everyone gathered in the square before the Water Gate. They asked Ezra the scribe to bring Moses’ Book of the Law, which the LORD commanded for Israel. 📖
## Nehemiah 8:2
On the seventh month’s first day, Ezra brought the law before the assembly. Men, women, and everyone who could listen and understand were there. 👥
## Nehemiah 8:3
Facing the Water Gate’s square, he read from dawn until noon to everyone who could understand. They listened carefully to the Book of the Law. 📖
## Nehemiah 8:4
Ezra stood on a high wooden platform built for this. Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah stood on his right. Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hash-baddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam stood on his left. 👥
## Nehemiah 8:5
Ezra stood above everyone and opened the book where all could see. Everyone stood as he opened it. 📖
## Nehemiah 8:6
He praised the LORD, the great God. Everyone raised their hands and said, “Amen, Amen!” They bowed facedown and worshiped the LORD. 🙏
## Nehemiah 8:7
The Levites taught the law while the people stood in place. These were Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, and Pelaiah. 👥
## Nehemiah 8:8
They read God’s Book of the Law and explained it. They gave understanding so people knew what was read. 📖
## Nehemiah 8:9
Nehemiah the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the teaching Levites said, “Today is holy to the LORD your God. Do not mourn or cry.” Everyone had cried while hearing the law. 😢
## Nehemiah 8:10
Nehemiah said, “Eat rich food, drink sweet drinks, and share with those who have nothing prepared. Today is holy to our Lord. Do not grieve. The LORD’s joy is your strength.” 🎉
## Nehemiah 8:11
The Levites calmed them, saying, “Be still. Today is holy. Do not grieve.” 🕊️
## Nehemiah 8:12
Everyone ate, drank, shared portions, and rejoiced greatly. They understood the words explained to them. 🎉
## Nehemiah 8:13
On the second day, the family heads, priests, and Levites gathered around Ezra to study the law. 📖
## Nehemiah 8:14
They found the LORD’s command through Moses in the law. Israelites must live in shelters during the seventh month’s feast. 🌿
## Nehemiah 8:15
They were to announce in their towns and Jerusalem, “Bring branches from olive, wild olive, myrtle, palm, and other leafy trees in the hills. Build shelters as the law says.” 🌳
## Nehemiah 8:16
They brought branches and built shelters on roofs and in courtyards. They also built them in the courts of God’s house and squares by the Water Gate and Gate of Ephraim. 🌿
## Nehemiah 8:17
All the returned exiles made shelters and lived in them. Israel had not celebrated this way since the days of Joshua son of Nun. They rejoiced greatly. 🎉
## Nehemiah 8:18
Ezra read God’s Book of the Law every day, from first to last. They celebrated seven days and held an assembly on the eighth, as required. 📖
