## Nehemiah 3:1
Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests rebuilt the Sheep Gate. They dedicated it and installed its doors. They built to the Tower of the Hundred and Tower of Hananel, and dedicated the wall. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:2
Jericho’s men built next to Eliashib. Zaccur son of Imri built next to them. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:3
Hassenaah’s sons rebuilt the Fish Gate. They laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. 🚪
## Nehemiah 3:4
Meremoth son of Uriah, grandson of Hakkoz, repaired next to them. Meshullam son of Berechiah, grandson of Meshezabel, repaired next to him. Zadok son of Baana repaired next. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:5
The Tekoites repaired next, but their nobles would not work under their supervisors. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 3:6
Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah repaired the Jeshanah Gate. They laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. 🚪
## Nehemiah 3:7
Melatiah the Gibeonite, Jadon the Meronothite, and men from Gibeon and Mizpah repaired next. They were under the governor west of the Euphrates. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:8
Uzziel son of Harhaiah, a goldsmith, repaired next. Hananiah, a perfumer, repaired beside him. They strengthened Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:9
Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of half Jerusalem’s district, repaired next. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:10
Jedaiah son of Harumaph repaired opposite his house. Hattush son of Hashabneiah repaired next to him. 🏠
## Nehemiah 3:11
Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired another section and the Tower of the Ovens. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:12
Shallum son of Hallohesh repaired next with his daughters’ help. He ruled the other half of Jerusalem’s district. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:13
Hanun and Zanoah’s people repaired the Valley Gate and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. They repaired a thousand cubits of wall to the Dung Gate. 🚪
## Nehemiah 3:14
Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of Beth-haccherem’s district, repaired the Dung Gate. He installed its doors, bolts, and bars. 🚪
## Nehemiah 3:15
Shallun son of Col-hozeh, ruler of Mizpah’s district, repaired and roofed the Fountain Gate. He installed doors, bolts, and bars. He repaired the wall of the Pool of Shelah near the king’s garden, to the stairs descending from the City of David. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:16
Nehemiah son of Azbuk ruled half Beth-zur’s district. He repaired opposite David’s tombs, to the artificial pool and House of the Mighty. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:17
Levites under Rehum son of Bani repaired next. Hashabiah, ruler of half Keilah’s district, repaired next for his district. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:18
Their countrymen repaired next under Binnui son of Henadad, ruler of Keilah’s other half-district. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:19
Ezer son of Jeshua, Mizpah’s ruler, repaired another section. It was opposite the ascent to the armory, near the wall’s angle. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:20
Baruch son of Zabbai carefully repaired the next section, from the angle to the doorway of Eliashib the high priest’s house. 🏠
## Nehemiah 3:21
Meremoth son of Uriah, grandson of Hakkoz, repaired next. His section ran from Eliashib’s doorway to the house’s end. 🏠
## Nehemiah 3:22
Priests from the surrounding area repaired next. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:23
Benjamin and Hasshub repaired in front of their house. Azariah son of Maaseiah, grandson of Ananiah, repaired beside his house next. 🏠
## Nehemiah 3:24
Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section, from Azariah’s house to the angle and corner. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:25
Palal son of Uzai repaired opposite the angle and the tower projecting from the king’s upper palace near the guard’s courtyard. Pedaiah son of Parosh repaired next. 🏰
## Nehemiah 3:26
Pedaiah and the temple servants on Ophel’s hill repaired opposite the Water Gate toward the east and the projecting tower. 🚪
## Nehemiah 3:27
The Tekoites repaired another section next. It ran from opposite the great projecting tower to Ophel’s wall. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:28
Above the Horse Gate, each priest repaired in front of his house. 🏠
## Nehemiah 3:29
Zadok son of Immer repaired opposite his house. Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, the East Gate’s guard, repaired next. 🚪
## Nehemiah 3:30
Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, Zalaph’s sixth son, repaired another section. Meshullam son of Berechiah repaired opposite his quarters next. 👥
## Nehemiah 3:31
Malchijah the goldsmith repaired to the house of the temple servants and merchants, opposite the Inspection Gate. His section reached the upper room above the corner. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 3:32
The goldsmiths and merchants repaired between that upper room and the Sheep Gate. 👥
