## Nehemiah 10:28
“The other people include priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and those who left the land’s peoples to obey God’s law. Their wives and sons and daughters who can understand also join. 👥
## Nehemiah 10:29
They join their noble brothers and swear to follow God’s law through Moses. They will carefully obey every command, rule, and law of the LORD our Lord. 📜
## Nehemiah 10:30
We will not marry our daughters to the land’s peoples or take their daughters for our sons. 👥
## Nehemiah 10:31
We will not buy goods or grain they bring on Sabbaths or holy days. Every seventh year, we will leave fields unplanted and cancel every debt. 📅
## Nehemiah 10:32
We commit to give a third of a shekel yearly for service in our God’s house. 🎁
## Nehemiah 10:33
This supplies showbread, regular grain and burnt offerings, Sabbath offerings, New Moons, appointed feasts, and holy offerings. It supplies sin offerings for Israel’s atonement and every duty of our God’s house. 🙏
## Nehemiah 10:34
Priests, Levites, and people cast lots for our families’ wood donations at appointed yearly times. They must bring wood to our God’s house to burn on the LORD’s altar, as the law says. 🌲
## Nehemiah 10:35
Every year, we will bring the firstfruits of our land and every fruit tree to the LORD’s house. 🌾
## Nehemiah 10:36
We will bring our firstborn sons and livestock as the law says. We will bring the firstborn of herds and flocks to the priests serving in our God’s house. 🙏
## Nehemiah 10:37
We will bring the priests the firstfruits of dough, grain offerings, tree fruit, new wine, and oil in our God’s storerooms. The Levites receive a tenth of our produce in every town where we work. 🎁
## Nehemiah 10:38
A priest from Aaron’s line must accompany the Levites collecting the tenth. They must bring a tenth of these tithes to the treasury storerooms in our God’s house. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 10:39
Israelites and Levites bring grain, new wine, and oil to the storerooms holding holy objects. Serving priests, gatekeepers, and singers stay there. We will not neglect our God’s house.” 🏠
