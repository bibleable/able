## Nehemiah 1:1
These are the words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah. In Chislev, in the twentieth year, I was in Susa’s fortress. 📖
## Nehemiah 1:2
My brother Hanani arrived with men from Judah. I asked about the Jews who survived exile and about Jerusalem. 💬
## Nehemiah 1:3
They said, “The survivors in the province face great trouble and disgrace. Jerusalem’s wall is broken, and its gates burned.” 💔
## Nehemiah 1:4
I sat and cried when I heard this. For days, I mourned, fasted, and prayed to heaven’s God. 🙏
## Nehemiah 1:5
I said, “LORD, God of heaven, You are great and awesome. You keep Your covenant of faithful love with those who love You and obey Your commands. ❤️
## Nehemiah 1:6
Open Your eyes and hear Your servant’s prayer for the Israelites, Your servants, day and night. I confess our sins against You. My father’s house and I have sinned. 🙏
## Nehemiah 1:7
We acted corruptly and disobeyed the commands, rules, and laws You gave Moses, Your servant. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 1:8
Remember Your word to Moses: ‘If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the nations. 🌍
## Nehemiah 1:9
If you return and obey My commands, I will gather your exiles, even from the farthest horizon. I will bring them to the place I chose for My Name to live.’ 👣
## Nehemiah 1:10
They are Your servants and people. You redeemed them with Your great power and mighty hand. 🛡️
## Nehemiah 1:11
Lord, hear my prayer and Your servants’ prayers. We delight to honor Your name. Give Your servant success today and mercy before this man.” At that time, I was the king’s cupbearer. 🙏
