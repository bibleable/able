## Nehemiah 12:1
These priests and Levites returned with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua. They included Seraiah, Jeremiah, and Ezra. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:2
They included Amariah, Malluch, and Hattush. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:3
They included Shecaniah, Rehum, and Meremoth. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:4
They included Iddo, Ginnethon, and Abijah. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:5
They included Mijamin, Maadiah, and Bilgah. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:6
They included Shemaiah, Joiarib, and Jedaiah. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:7
They included Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, and Jedaiah. These led the priests and their associates in Jeshua’s days. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:8
The Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah. Mattaniah and his associates led songs of thanks. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:9
Their associates Bakbukiah and Unni stood opposite them during services. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:10
Jeshua fathered Joiakim, Joiakim fathered Eliashib, and Eliashib fathered Joiada. 🌳
## Nehemiah 12:11
Joiada fathered Jonathan, and Jonathan fathered Jaddua. 🌳
## Nehemiah 12:12
In Joiakim’s days, priestly family heads included Meraiah for Seraiah’s family and Hananiah for Jeremiah’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:13
Meshullam led Ezra’s family, and Jehohanan led Amariah’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:14
Jonathan led Malluchi’s family, and Joseph led Shebaniah’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:15
Adna led Harim’s family, and Helkai led Meraioth’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:16
Zechariah led Iddo’s family, and Meshullam led Ginnethon’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:17
Zichri led Abijah’s family, and Piltai led Miniamin’s and Moadiah’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:18
Shammua led Bilgah’s family, and Jonathan led Shemaiah’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:19
Mattenai led Joiarib’s family, and Uzzi led Jedaiah’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:20
Kallai led Sallai’s family, and Eber led Amok’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:21
Hashabiah led Hilkiah’s family, and Nethanel led Jedaiah’s. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:22
Levite and priestly family heads were recorded in the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, during Darius the Persian’s reign. 📜
## Nehemiah 12:23
The family heads descended from Levi were recorded in the Book of the Chronicles through the days of Johanan son of Eliashib. 📖
## Nehemiah 12:24
Levite leaders were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Jeshua son of Kadmiel. Their associates stood opposite them, alternating praise and thanks as David, God’s man, instructed. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:25
Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub guarded the storerooms at the gates. 🚪
## Nehemiah 12:26
They served in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua, grandson of Jozadak. They also served in the days of Nehemiah the governor and Ezra the priest and scribe. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:27
Levites came from all their homes to Jerusalem for the wall’s joyful dedication. They celebrated with thanks, songs, cymbals, harps, and lyres. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:28
Singers gathered from Jerusalem’s surrounding region and the Netophathites’ villages. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:29
They also came from Beth-gilgal and Geba’s and Azmaveth’s fields. They had built villages around Jerusalem. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 12:30
The priests and Levites purified themselves, the people, the gates, and the wall. 🙏
## Nehemiah 12:31
I brought Judah’s leaders onto the wall and appointed two large thanksgiving choirs. One went right along the wall toward the Dung Gate. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:32
Hoshaiah and half Judah’s leaders followed. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:33
Azariah, Ezra, and Meshullam followed with them. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:34
Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah also followed. 👥
## Nehemiah 12:35
Some priests had trumpets. Zechariah was Jonathan’s son, and Jonathan was Shemaiah’s son. Shemaiah was Mattaniah’s son, and Mattaniah was Micaiah’s son. Micaiah was Zaccur’s son, and Zaccur was Asaph’s son. 📯
## Nehemiah 12:36
His associates were Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani. They carried David’s prescribed instruments, and Ezra the scribe led them. David was the man of God. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:37
At the Fountain Gate, they climbed the City of David’s stairs up to the wall. They passed above David’s house to the Water Gate in the east. 👣
## Nehemiah 12:38
The second thanksgiving choir went left, followed by me and half the people. We went along the wall past the Tower of the Ovens to the Broad Wall. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:39
We crossed the Gate of Ephraim, Jeshanah Gate, Fish Gate, Tower of Hananel, and Tower of the Hundred to the Sheep Gate. We stopped at the Gate of the Guard. 👣
## Nehemiah 12:40
Both choirs stood in God’s house, with me and half the officials who accompanied me. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:41
Priests with trumpets stood there: Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah. 📯
## Nehemiah 12:42
Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer also stood there. Jezrahiah directed the choirs’ loud songs. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:43
They offered great sacrifices and rejoiced because God gave them great joy. Women and children rejoiced too. Jerusalem’s joy was heard far away. 🎉
## Nehemiah 12:44
Men were appointed over storerooms for supplies, contributions, firstfruits, and tithes. They gathered the law’s portions for priests and Levites from village fields. Judah rejoiced over the serving priests and Levites. 🎁
## Nehemiah 12:45
They served God and performed purification, with singers and gatekeepers, as David and Solomon his son instructed. 🙏
## Nehemiah 12:46
In David’s and Asaph’s days, directors led the singers and songs of praise and thanks to God. 🎵
## Nehemiah 12:47
In Zerubbabel’s and Nehemiah’s days, all Israel gave daily portions for singers and gatekeepers. They set aside daily portions for Levites, who set aside daily portions for Aaron’s descendants. 🎁
