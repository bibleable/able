## Nehemiah 6:1
Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and our other enemies heard the wall was rebuilt without gaps. I had not yet installed the gate doors. 🧱
## Nehemiah 6:2
Sanballat and Geshem sent, “Meet us in a village on Ono’s plain.” They planned to harm me. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 6:3
I sent messengers: “I do great work and cannot come down. Why stop the work while I leave to meet you?” 🛠️
## Nehemiah 6:4
They sent the same request four times. I gave the same answer each time. 📣
## Nehemiah 6:5
The fifth time, Sanballat sent a young servant with the same message in an unsealed letter. ✉️
## Nehemiah 6:6
It said, “The nations report, and Geshem agrees, that you and the Jews plan rebellion. This is why you build the wall. They say you will be king. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 6:7
You appointed prophets to announce in Jerusalem, ‘Judah has a king.’ These reports will reach the king. Come and discuss this with us.” 💬
## Nehemiah 6:8
I replied, “None of this is true. You invent these reports in your mind.” ⛔
## Nehemiah 6:9
They tried to frighten us, saying, “Their hands will weaken, and the work will never finish.” My God, strengthen my hands now. 🙏
## Nehemiah 6:10
I visited Shemaiah son of Delaiah, grandson of Mehetabel, who was confined at home. He said, “Meet me inside God’s temple and shut its doors. They come tonight to kill you!” ⚠️
## Nehemiah 6:11
I replied, “Should someone like me flee? Should I enter the temple to save my life? I will not go!” 🛡️
## Nehemiah 6:12
I realized God did not send him. Tobiah and Sanballat hired him to prophesy against me. ⛔
## Nehemiah 6:13
They hired him to frighten me into sin by obeying his suggestion. They wanted to give me a bad name and discredit me. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 6:14
My God, remember Tobiah, Sanballat, Noadiah the prophetess, and the other prophets for trying to frighten me. 🙏
## Nehemiah 6:15
The wall was finished in fifty-two days, on Elul’s twenty-fifth day. 🎉
## Nehemiah 6:16
Our enemies heard, and surrounding nations became afraid and discouraged. They realized our God completed this work. 🛡️
## Nehemiah 6:17
Judah’s nobles sent many letters to Tobiah, and his letters kept coming to them. ✉️
## Nehemiah 6:18
Many in Judah were bound to him by oath. Tobiah was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah. His son Jehohanan married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah. 👥
## Nehemiah 6:19
The nobles told me Tobiah’s good deeds and told him my words. Tobiah sent letters to frighten me. ✉️
