## Nehemiah 11:1
The leaders settled in Jerusalem. The others cast lots for one in ten to live in the holy city of Jerusalem. The other nine stayed in their towns. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:2
The people blessed everyone who volunteered to live in Jerusalem. ❤️
## Nehemiah 11:3
These provincial heads settled in Jerusalem. In Judah’s villages, Israelites, priests, Levites, temple servants, and descendants of Solomon’s servants lived on their own property. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:4
Some descendants of Judah and Benjamin lived in Jerusalem. Judah’s descendants included Athaiah son of Uzziah. Uzziah was Zechariah’s son, and Zechariah was Amariah’s son. Amariah was Shephatiah’s son, and Shephatiah was Mahalalel’s son. Mahalalel descended from Perez. 🌳
## Nehemiah 11:5
Maaseiah was Baruch’s son, and Baruch was Col-hozeh’s son. Col-hozeh was Hazaiah’s son, and Hazaiah was Adaiah’s son. Adaiah was Joiarib’s son, and Joiarib was Zechariah’s son. Zechariah descended from Shelah. 🌳
## Nehemiah 11:6
Perez’s descendants in Jerusalem numbered 468 brave men. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:7
Benjamin’s descendants included Sallu son of Meshullam. Meshullam was Joed’s son, Joed was Pedaiah’s son, and Pedaiah was Kolaiah’s son. Kolaiah was Maaseiah’s son, Maaseiah was Ithiel’s son, and Ithiel was Jeshaiah’s son. 🌳
## Nehemiah 11:8
His followers Gabbai and Sallai were with him, 928 men. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:9
Joel son of Zichri supervised them. Judah son of Hassenuah supervised the city’s Second District. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:10
The priests included Jedaiah son of Joiarib and Jachin. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:11
Seraiah, chief official of God’s house, was Hilkiah’s son. Hilkiah was Meshullam’s son, and Meshullam was Zadok’s son. Zadok was Meraioth’s son, and Meraioth was Ahitub’s son. 🌳
## Nehemiah 11:12
Their temple workers numbered 822. Adaiah was Jeroham’s son, and Jeroham was Pelaliah’s son. Pelaliah was Amzi’s son, and Amzi was Zechariah’s son. Zechariah was Pashhur’s son, and Pashhur was Malchijah’s son. 🌳
## Nehemiah 11:13
His associates, family leaders, numbered 242. Amashai was Azarel’s son, and Azarel was Ahzai’s son. Ahzai was Meshillemoth’s son, and Meshillemoth was Immer’s son. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:14
His associates numbered 128 mighty, brave men. Zabdiel son of Haggedolim supervised them. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:15
The Levites included Shemaiah son of Hasshub. Hasshub was Azrikam’s son, Azrikam was Hashabiah’s son, and Hashabiah was Bunni’s son. 🌳
## Nehemiah 11:16
Shabbethai and Jozabad were two Levite leaders who supervised work outside God’s house. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 11:17
Mattaniah son of Mica led thanks and prayer. Mica was Zabdi’s son, and Zabdi was Asaph’s son. Bakbukiah ranked second among his associates. Abda was Shammua’s son, Shammua was Galal’s son, and Galal was Jeduthun’s son. 🙏
## Nehemiah 11:18
The holy city’s Levites numbered 284. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:19
Gatekeepers Akkub, Talmon, and their associates numbered 172 men who guarded the gates. 🚪
## Nehemiah 11:20
The other Israelites, priests, and Levites lived in Judah’s villages, each on his inheritance. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:21
Temple servants lived on Ophel’s hill, supervised by Ziha and Gishpa. 👥
## Nehemiah 11:22
Uzzi son of Bani supervised Jerusalem’s Levites. Bani was Hashabiah’s son, Hashabiah was Mattaniah’s son, and Mattaniah was Mica’s son. Uzzi belonged to Asaph’s descendants, the singers supervising service in God’s house. 🎵
## Nehemiah 11:23
The king had issued an order regulating the singers’ daily work. 📜
## Nehemiah 11:24
Pethahiah son of Meshezabel acted for the king in every matter concerning the people. He descended from Zerah son of Judah. 👑
## Nehemiah 11:25
Some of Judah’s people lived in Kiriath-arba, Dibon, Jekabzeel, and their villages and fields. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:26
They lived in Jeshua, Moladah, and Beth-pelet. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:27
They lived in Hazar-shual and Beersheba and its villages. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:28
They lived in Ziklag and Meconah and its villages. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:29
They lived in En-rimmon, Zorah, and Jarmuth. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:30
They lived in Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages, Lachish and its fields, and Azekah and its villages. They settled from Beersheba to the Valley of Hinnom. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:31
Benjamin’s descendants from Geba lived in Michmash, Aija, and Bethel and its villages. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:32
They lived in Anathoth, Nob, and Ananiah. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:33
They lived in Hazor, Ramah, and Gittaim. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:34
They lived in Hadid, Zeboim, and Neballat. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:35
They lived in Lod, Ono, and the Valley of the Craftsmen. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 11:36
Some divisions of Judah’s Levites settled in Benjamin. 👥
