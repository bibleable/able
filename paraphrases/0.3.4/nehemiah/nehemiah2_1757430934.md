## Nehemiah 2:1
In Nisan, in Artaxerxes’ twentieth year, I gave the king his wine. I had never looked sad before him. 🍷
## Nehemiah 2:2
He asked, “Why do you look sad when you are not ill? Your heart must be sad.” I was very afraid. 😨
## Nehemiah 2:3
I replied, “King, live forever! Why should I not be sad? The city where my ancestors are buried lies ruined, and its gates burned.” 💔
## Nehemiah 2:4
He asked, “What do you request?” I prayed to heaven’s God. 🙏
## Nehemiah 2:5
I said, “If it pleases you and you favor your servant, send me to Judah. Let me rebuild the city where my ancestors are buried.” 🏘️
## Nehemiah 2:6
The queen sat beside him. He asked, “How long will you travel, and when will you return?” He gladly sent me, and I set a time. 👑
## Nehemiah 2:7
I asked, “Please give me letters for the governors west of the Euphrates. Let them allow me safe passage to Judah. ✉️
## Nehemiah 2:8
Give me a letter to Asaph, keeper of your forest, for wood. I need beams for the temple fortress’s gates, the city wall, and my future house.” The king granted this because my God’s gracious hand was on me. 🌲
## Nehemiah 2:9
I gave the governors west of the Euphrates the king’s letters. The king sent army officers and cavalry with me. 👣
## Nehemiah 2:10
Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite official heard this. They were deeply troubled that someone sought Israel’s good. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 2:11
I reached Jerusalem and stayed three days. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 2:12
At night, I went out with a few men. I told no one what God put in my heart for Jerusalem. The only animal was the one I rode. 🌙
## Nehemiah 2:13
I went through the Valley Gate toward the Well of the Serpent and the Dung Gate. I inspected Jerusalem’s broken walls and burned gates. 👀
## Nehemiah 2:14
I continued to the Fountain Gate and King’s Pool. There was no room for my animal to pass. 👣
## Nehemiah 2:15
I went up the valley at night and inspected the wall. I returned through the Valley Gate. 🌙
## Nehemiah 2:16
The officials did not know where I went or what I did. I had not told the Jews, priests, nobles, officials, or other workers. 🤫
## Nehemiah 2:17
I told them, “You see our trouble. Jerusalem is ruined, and its gates burned. Let us rebuild the wall so we are no longer a disgrace.” 🛠️
## Nehemiah 2:18
I told them about my God’s gracious hand and the king’s words. They said, “Let us rebuild!” They began this good work. 💪
## Nehemiah 2:19
Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite official, and Geshem the Arab mocked us. They asked, “What are you doing? Do you rebel against the king?” ⚠️
## Nehemiah 2:20
I replied, “Heaven’s God will give us success. We, His servants, will rebuild. You have no portion, right, or claim in Jerusalem.” 🛡️
