## Nehemiah 9:1
On the same month’s twenty-fourth day, Israelites gathered to fast in sackcloth, with dust on their heads. 🙏
## Nehemiah 9:2
Those of Israelite descent separated from foreigners. They stood and confessed their sins and their ancestors’ sins. 💔
## Nehemiah 9:3
They stood and read the law of the LORD their God for a quarter of the day. For another quarter, they confessed and worshiped Him. 📖
## Nehemiah 9:4
The Levites Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani stood on the platform. They cried loudly to the LORD their God. 📣
## Nehemiah 9:5
The Levites Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah said, “Stand and praise the LORD your God forever. Praise Your glorious name! May it rise above every blessing and praise. 🙏
## Nehemiah 9:6
You alone are the LORD. You made the heavens, the highest heavens and all their host, the earth, the seas, and everything in them. You give everything life, and heaven’s host worships You. 🌍
## Nehemiah 9:7
You chose Abram, brought him from Ur of the Chaldeans, and named him Abraham. 👣
## Nehemiah 9:8
You found his heart faithful and promised his descendants the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Jebusites, and Girgashites. You kept Your covenant promise because You are righteous. 📜
## Nehemiah 9:9
You saw our ancestors’ suffering in Egypt and heard their cry at the Red Sea. 🌊
## Nehemiah 9:10
You performed signs and wonders against Pharaoh, his officials, and his people because they treated our ancestors arrogantly. Your name became famous and remains so today. ✨
## Nehemiah 9:11
You divided the sea, and they crossed on dry ground. You threw their pursuers into raging waters like a stone into the depths. 🌊
## Nehemiah 9:12
You led them with a cloud pillar by day and a fire pillar by night to light their path. 🔥
## Nehemiah 9:13
You came down on Sinai and spoke from heaven. You gave just rules, true laws, and good commands. ⛰️
## Nehemiah 9:14
You revealed Your holy Sabbath and gave commands, rules, and laws through Moses. 📖
## Nehemiah 9:15
You fed their hunger with heaven’s bread and their thirst with water from a rock. You told them to possess the land You swore to give. 🍞
## Nehemiah 9:16
They and our ancestors became proud, stiffened their necks, and disobeyed Your commands. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 9:17
They refused to listen or remember Your wonders. They stiffened their necks and chose a leader to return them to Egyptian slavery. But You forgive and show grace and mercy. You are slow to anger and rich in faithful love, and You did not abandon them. ❤️
## Nehemiah 9:18
They cast a calf image and said, ‘This is your God who brought you from Egypt.’ They committed terrible insults against You. ⛔
## Nehemiah 9:19
Yet Your great mercy did not abandon them in the wilderness. The cloud pillar guided them by day, and the fire pillar lit their path at night. 🛡️
## Nehemiah 9:20
You gave Your good Spirit to teach them. You gave manna for their mouths and water for their thirst. 🍞
## Nehemiah 9:21
For forty years, You sustained them in the wilderness, and they lacked nothing. Their clothes did not wear out, and their feet did not swell. 👣
## Nehemiah 9:22
You gave them kingdoms, peoples, and every corner of the land. They took the lands of Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan. 🏘️
## Nehemiah 9:23
You multiplied their descendants like heaven’s stars. You brought them to the land their ancestors were told to possess. ⭐
## Nehemiah 9:24
Their descendants entered and took the land. You subdued the Canaanites and handed them the land’s kings and peoples to treat as they wished. ⚔️
## Nehemiah 9:25
They captured defended cities, fertile land, houses full of goods, dug wells, vineyards, olive groves, and many fruit trees. They ate, grew full and fat, and delighted in Your great goodness. 🌾
## Nehemiah 9:26
They disobeyed and rebelled, throwing Your law behind their backs. They killed prophets who warned them to return and terribly insulted You. ⛔
## Nehemiah 9:27
You handed them to enemies who oppressed them. In distress, they cried to You. You heard from heaven and mercifully sent rescuers who saved them from their enemies. 🛡️
## Nehemiah 9:28
When they rested, they did evil again. You abandoned them to their enemies’ rule. They cried again, and You heard from heaven and mercifully rescued them many times. ❤️
## Nehemiah 9:29
You warned them to return, but they proudly disobeyed. They sinned against Your rules, which give life to those who practice them. They turned stubborn shoulders, stiffened necks, and refused obedience. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 9:30
You were patient for years, and Your Spirit warned them through prophets. They refused to listen, so You handed them to neighboring peoples. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 9:31
Your great mercy did not destroy or abandon them. You are gracious and full of mercy. ❤️
## Nehemiah 9:32
Our God, You are great, mighty, and awesome, and keep Your gracious covenant. Do not treat our hardship lightly. It came on us, our kings, leaders, priests, prophets, ancestors, and all Your people from Assyria’s kings’ days until now. 🙏
## Nehemiah 9:33
You were just in everything that happened. You acted faithfully, and we acted wickedly. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 9:34
Our kings, leaders, priests, and ancestors disobeyed Your law, commands, and warnings. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 9:35
They would not serve You or turn from evil, even in their kingdom and the spacious, fertile land You gave. They had Your abundant goodness. 🌾
## Nehemiah 9:36
Today we are slaves in the land You gave our ancestors for its fruit and goodness. We are slaves here! ⛓️
## Nehemiah 9:37
Its rich harvest goes to kings You placed over us because of our sins. They rule our bodies and livestock as they please. Our distress is great. 💔
## Nehemiah 9:38
Because of this, we make a binding written agreement. We seal it with our leaders’, Levites’, and priests’ names.” 📜
