## Nehemiah 13:1
Moses’ book was read aloud. The people found that Ammonites and Moabites must never enter God’s assembly. 📖
## Nehemiah 13:2
They had not met Israel with food and water. They hired Balaam to curse Israel, but our God turned the curse into blessing. ❤️
## Nehemiah 13:3
When they heard the law, they excluded everyone of foreign descent from Israel. 👥
## Nehemiah 13:4
Earlier, Eliashib the priest, Tobiah’s relative, supervised our God’s storerooms. 🏠
## Nehemiah 13:5
He prepared Tobiah a large room formerly used for grain offerings, frankincense, and temple objects. It held the prescribed tithes of grain, new wine, and oil for Levites, singers, and gatekeepers, and priests’ contributions. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 13:6
I was away then. In Artaxerxes king of Babylon’s thirty-second year, I had returned to him. Later, I obtained his permission to leave. 👑
## Nehemiah 13:7
I returned to Jerusalem and discovered Eliashib’s evil in giving Tobiah a room in God’s courts. ⛔
## Nehemiah 13:8
I was deeply displeased and threw Tobiah’s household goods out. 😠
## Nehemiah 13:9
I ordered the rooms purified and returned the objects of God’s house, grain offerings, and frankincense. 🏺
## Nehemiah 13:10
Levites had not received their portions. The Levites and singers who served had returned to their fields. 🌾
## Nehemiah 13:11
I corrected the officials, asking, “Why is God’s house neglected?” I gathered the Levites and singers and restored their posts. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 13:12
All Judah brought a tenth of grain, new wine, and oil to the storerooms. 🎁
## Nehemiah 13:13
I appointed Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah the Levite as treasurers. Hanan son of Zaccur, grandson of Mattaniah, helped them. They were trustworthy and distributed supplies to fellow Levites. 👥
## Nehemiah 13:14
My God, remember this. Do not erase my faithful deeds for Your house and its services. 🙏
## Nehemiah 13:15
I saw Judahites press wine and load donkeys with grain, wine, grapes, and figs on the Sabbath. They brought all kinds of goods into Jerusalem. I warned them against selling food that day. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 13:16
Tyre’s men who lived there imported fish and goods. They sold them to Judah’s people in Jerusalem on the Sabbath. ⛔
## Nehemiah 13:17
I corrected Judah’s nobles. “Why do you do this evil and make the Sabbath unholy? ⚖️
## Nehemiah 13:18
Your ancestors did this, and God brought disaster on us and this city. You stir His anger against Israel again by making the Sabbath unholy!” ⚠️
## Nehemiah 13:19
As evening shadows fell before the Sabbath, I ordered Jerusalem’s gates shut until the Sabbath ended. I posted servants so no load could enter that day. 🚪
## Nehemiah 13:20
Merchants selling all kinds of goods camped outside Jerusalem once or twice. 👥
## Nehemiah 13:21
I warned, “Why camp before the wall? If you do it again, I will lay hands on you.” They no longer came on the Sabbath. ⚠️
## Nehemiah 13:22
I told Levites to purify themselves and guard the gates to keep the Sabbath holy. My God, remember this too. Show mercy according to Your abundant faithful love. 🙏
## Nehemiah 13:23
I saw Jews married to women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab. 👥
## Nehemiah 13:24
Half their children spoke Ashdod’s or other peoples’ languages but could not speak Judah’s language. 🗣️
## Nehemiah 13:25
I corrected them and called curses on them. I beat some men and pulled their hair. I made them swear before God. I said, “Do not marry your daughters to their sons or take their daughters for yourselves or your sons! ⚠️
## Nehemiah 13:26
Did Solomon king of Israel not sin like this? No king among many nations was like him. God loved him and made him Israel’s king, yet foreign women led him to sin. 👑
## Nehemiah 13:27
Must we hear that you do this terrible evil and betray our God by marrying foreign women?” ⛔
## Nehemiah 13:28
One son of Jehoiada son of Eliashib the high priest married the daughter of Sanballat the Horonite. I drove him away. 👥
## Nehemiah 13:29
My God, remember them for making the priesthood unclean and breaking the covenant of the priests and Levites. ⚖️
## Nehemiah 13:30
I purified the priests and Levites from everything foreign and assigned each specific duties. 🛠️
## Nehemiah 13:31
I arranged wood contributions at appointed times and the firstfruits. My God, remember me with favor. 🙏
