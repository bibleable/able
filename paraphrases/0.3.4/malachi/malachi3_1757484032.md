## Malachi 3:1
The LORD of Hosts says, “I will send My messenger to prepare the way before Me. Then the Lord you seek will suddenly come to His temple. He is the Messenger of the covenant, whom you delight in. Look, He is coming!” 📣
## Malachi 3:2
Who can endure the day He comes? Who can stand when He appears? He will be like fire that purifies metal and soap that cleans clothes. 🔥
## Malachi 3:3
He will sit to refine and purify silver. He will purify Levi’s sons and refine them like gold and silver. Then they will offer sacrifices to the LORD in righteousness. ✨
## Malachi 3:4
The offerings of Judah and Jerusalem will please the LORD again, as they did in earlier days and years. 🙏
## Malachi 3:5
The LORD of Hosts says, “I will come near to judge you. I will quickly testify against sorcerers, adulterers, and those who lie under oath. I will testify against those who oppress widows and children without fathers, and those who cheat workers of their pay. I will also testify against those who deny foreigners justice and do not fear Me. ⚖️
## Malachi 3:6
I, the LORD, do not change. That is why you descendants of Jacob have not been destroyed. 🛡️
## Malachi 3:7
Since your ancestors’ days, you have turned from My commands and disobeyed them. Return to Me, and I will return to you,” says the LORD of Hosts. “But you ask, ‘How can we return?’ 👣
## Malachi 3:8
Will a person rob God? Yet you rob Me! You ask, ‘How do we rob You?’ You withhold tithes and offerings. ⚠️

*Word help: A tithe is a tenth given to God.*
## Malachi 3:9
The whole nation is under a curse, yet you still rob Me. ⚖️
## Malachi 3:10
Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, so that My house has food. Test Me in this,” says the LORD of Hosts. “See if I will open heaven’s windows and pour out blessings beyond measure for you. 🌧️
## Malachi 3:11
I will stop the devourer from destroying your crops. Your vines will not fail to produce fruit,” says the LORD of Hosts. 🌾
## Malachi 3:12
“All nations will call you blessed, because your land will be a delight,” says the LORD of Hosts. 🌍
## Malachi 3:13
The LORD says, “You have spoken harsh words against Me. Yet you ask, ‘What have we said against You?’ ⚠️
## Malachi 3:14
You said, ‘It is useless to serve God. What do we gain by obeying His requirements and showing sorrow before the LORD of Hosts? 💬
## Malachi 3:15
Now we call proud people blessed. Those who do evil prosper. They even test God and escape punishment.’” ⚠️
## Malachi 3:16
Then those who feared the LORD spoke to each other, and the LORD listened. A scroll was written before Him to remember those who feared Him and honored His name. 📜
## Malachi 3:17
The LORD of Hosts says, “They will be Mine on the day I prepare My treasured possession. I will spare them as a man spares his own son who serves him. ❤️
## Malachi 3:18
Then you will again see the difference between the righteous and the wicked. You will see the difference between those who serve God and those who do not.” ⚖️
