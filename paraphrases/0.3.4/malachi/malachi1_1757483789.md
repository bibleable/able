## Malachi 1:1
This is the LORD’s solemn message to Israel through Malachi. 📣
## Malachi 1:2
The LORD says, “I have loved you.” But you ask, “How have You loved us?” The LORD declares, “Was Esau not Jacob’s brother? Yet I loved Jacob. ❤️
## Malachi 1:3
But I hated Esau. I made his mountains a wasteland and left his inheritance to the desert jackals.” 🏜️
## Malachi 1:4
Edom may say, “We are ruined, but we will rebuild.” The LORD of Hosts says, “They may build, but I will tear it down. People will call them the Land of Wickedness. They will be a people with whom the LORD is angry forever. ⚖️
## Malachi 1:5
You will see this yourselves. You will say, ‘The LORD is great, even beyond Israel’s borders.’” 🌍
## Malachi 1:6
The LORD of Hosts says to the priests who despise His name, “A son honors his father, and a servant honors his master. If I am a father, where is My honor? If I am a master, where is your fear of Me? But you ask, ‘How have we despised Your name?’ ⚠️
## Malachi 1:7
You put unclean food on My altar. You ask, ‘How have we made You unclean?’ You say that the LORD’s table deserves contempt. ⛔
## Malachi 1:8
Is it not wrong to sacrifice blind animals? Is it not wrong to offer animals that are lame or sick? Try giving them to your governor! Would he be pleased or show you kindness?” asks the LORD of Hosts. ⚖️
## Malachi 1:9
The LORD of Hosts says, “Now ask God to show you kindness. Will He be gracious? You brought these offerings. Will He accept you? 🙏
## Malachi 1:10
I wish one of you would shut the temple doors! Then you would stop lighting useless fires on My altar. I take no pleasure in you,” says the LORD of Hosts. “I will accept no offering from your hands. ⛔
## Malachi 1:11
My name will be great among the nations, from sunrise to sunset. In every place, people will offer incense and pure offerings in My name. My name will be great among the nations,” says the LORD of Hosts. 🌍
## Malachi 1:12
“But you dishonor My name when you say, ‘The Lord’s table is unclean. Its produce and food deserve contempt.’ ⛔
## Malachi 1:13
You say, ‘What a nuisance!’ You turn up your noses at it,” says the LORD of Hosts. “You bring stolen animals and animals that are lame or sick. Should I accept these from you?” asks the LORD. ⚠️
## Malachi 1:14
“A deceiver has an acceptable male animal in his flock and promises to give it. But he sacrifices a defective animal to the Lord. A curse is on him. I am a great King,” says the LORD of Hosts. “The nations must fear My name. 👑
