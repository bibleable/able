## Malachi 2:1
“Now, priests, this command is for you. 📣
## Malachi 2:2
Listen and take seriously your duty to honor My name,” says the LORD of Hosts. “If you do not, I will send a curse and curse your blessings. I have already begun to curse them, because you do not take this seriously. ⚖️
## Malachi 2:3
I will rebuke your descendants. I will spread animal waste on your faces, the waste from your feasts. You will be carried away with it. ⚖️
## Malachi 2:4
Then you will know that I sent this command so that My covenant with Levi may continue,” says the LORD of Hosts. 📜
## Malachi 2:5
“My covenant with him gave life and peace. It required deep respect, and he respected Me. He stood in awe of My name. 🕊️
## Malachi 2:6
He taught the truth and spoke nothing false. He walked with Me in peace and did what was right. He turned many people away from sin. 📖
## Malachi 2:7
A priest’s words should preserve knowledge. People should seek instruction from him, because he is the messenger of the LORD of Hosts. 📣
## Malachi 2:8
But you left the right path. Your teaching caused many to stumble. You broke the covenant with Levi,” says the LORD of Hosts. ⚠️
## Malachi 2:9
“So I made you despised and ashamed before all the people. You did not follow My ways. You favored some people over others in matters of the law.” ⚖️
## Malachi 2:10
Do we not all have one Father? Did one God not create us? Why do we betray each other and dishonor the covenant of our ancestors? 💬
## Malachi 2:11
Judah has been unfaithful. A terrible act has been committed in Israel and Jerusalem. Judah has dishonored the LORD’s beloved holy place by marrying the daughter of a foreign god. ⛔
## Malachi 2:12
As for the man who does this, may the LORD remove everyone awake and aware from Jacob’s tents. May He do so even if that man brings an offering to the LORD of Hosts. ⚖️
## Malachi 2:13
You also cover the LORD’s altar with tears, cries, and groans. You do this because He no longer pays attention to your offerings or gladly accepts them. 😢
## Malachi 2:14
You ask, “Why?” The LORD witnessed the covenant between you and the wife you married when you were young. You betrayed her, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant. 💔
## Malachi 2:15
Did the LORD not make them one, with a share of the Spirit? Why one? Because He seeks children who honor God. So guard your spirit. Do not betray the wife you married when you were young. ❤️
## Malachi 2:16
The LORD, the God of Israel, says, “I hate divorce.” The LORD of Hosts says, “A man who divorces his wife covers his garment with violence.” So guard your spirit and do not be unfaithful. ⚠️
## Malachi 2:17
You have tired the LORD with your words. You ask, “How have we tired Him?” You say, “Everyone who does evil is good in the LORD’s sight, and He delights in them.” Or you ask, “Where is the God of justice?” ⚠️
