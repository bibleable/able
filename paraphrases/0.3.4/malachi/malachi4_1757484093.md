## Malachi 4:1
The LORD of Hosts says, “The day is coming, hot as a furnace. All proud people and those who do evil will be like dry stalks. I will set them on fire that day. No root or branch will remain.” 🔥
## Malachi 4:2
“But for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings. You will go out and leap like calves released from a stall. ☀️
## Malachi 4:3
You will trample the wicked. They will be ashes under your feet on the day I am preparing,” says the LORD of Hosts. ⚖️
## Malachi 4:4
“Remember the law of My servant Moses. Remember the rules and commands I gave him at Horeb for all Israel. 📜
## Malachi 4:5
I will send you the prophet Elijah before the great and awesome Day of the LORD comes. 📣
## Malachi 4:6
He will turn fathers’ hearts toward their children and children’s hearts toward their fathers. Otherwise, I will come and strike the land with a curse.” ❤️
