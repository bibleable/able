## 2 Corinthians 12:1
I must continue boasting, though it gains nothing. I will turn to visions and revelations from the Lord. 👀
## 2 Corinthians 12:2
I know a man in Christ who was taken to the third heaven fourteen years ago. I do not know whether in or outside the body. God knows. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 12:3
I know this man, though only God knows whether he was in or outside the body. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 12:4
He was taken to Paradise. He heard things beyond expression, which people are not permitted to tell. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 12:5
I will boast about that man. I will boast about myself only in weakness. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 12:6
If I wanted to boast, I would speak truth rather than foolishness. I hold back so no one credits me beyond what he sees or hears from me. 👀
## 2 Corinthians 12:7
Nor should anyone credit me for these very great revelations. To keep me from pride, I was given a thorn in my flesh, Satan’s messenger, to torment me. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 12:8
Three times, I begged the Lord to remove it. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 12:9
He said, “My grace is enough for you. My power is made perfect in weakness.” I gladly boast in weakness, so Christ’s power rests on me. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 12:10
For Christ’s sake, I delight in weakness, insults, hardships, persecution, and difficulties. When I am weak, I am strong. ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 12:11
You drove me to become a fool. You should have praised me. Though I am nothing, I am not below those “super-apostles.” 💬
## 2 Corinthians 12:12
The signs of a true apostle were performed among you with great endurance: signs, wonders, and miracles. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 12:13
How were you below other churches, except that I did not burden you? Forgive this wrong! 💬
## 2 Corinthians 12:14
I am ready to visit a third time and will not burden you. I seek you, rather than your possessions. Parents should save for children, rather than children for parents. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 12:15
For your souls, I gladly spend my money and myself. Will you love me less if I love you more? ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 12:16
I did not burden you. Yet, crafty as I am, I caught you by trickery. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 12:17
Did anyone I sent exploit you? 💭
## 2 Corinthians 12:18
I urged Titus to visit and sent our brother with him. Did Titus exploit you? Did we not walk in the same Spirit and footsteps? 👣
## 2 Corinthians 12:19
Do you think we defend ourselves to you? We speak before God in Christ. Dear friends, everything is to build you up. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 12:20
I fear we may disappoint each other when I come. I fear quarrels, jealousy, rage, rivalry, slander, gossip, pride, and disorder. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 12:21
I fear God will humble me before you at my return. I fear that I will grieve over many who sinned before and did not repent of impurity, sexual immorality, and wild immoral behavior. 💔
