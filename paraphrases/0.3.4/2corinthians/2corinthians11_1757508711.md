## 2 Corinthians 11:1
Bear with a little foolishness from me. You already do. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 11:2
I am jealous for you with godly jealousy. I promised you to one husband, to present you as a pure virgin to Christ. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 11:3
The serpent cunningly deceived Eve. I fear your minds may likewise leave simple, pure devotion to Christ. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 11:4
You easily tolerate someone preaching a different Jesus from the One we preached. You easily receive a different spirit or good news from those you accepted. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 11:5
I am in no way below those “super-apostles.” 💬
## 2 Corinthians 11:6
My speech may lack polish, but my knowledge does not. We made this clear to you in every possible way. 📖
## 2 Corinthians 11:7
Did I sin by humbling myself to raise you up and preaching God’s good news without charge? 📣
## 2 Corinthians 11:8
I robbed other churches by taking their support to serve you. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 11:9
I burdened no one while with you in need. Brothers from Macedonia supplied me. I avoided burdening you in any way and will continue. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 11:10
As surely as Christ’s truth is in me, no one in Achaia will silence this boast. 📣
## 2 Corinthians 11:11
Is this because I do not love you? God knows I do! ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 11:12
I will continue, to deny my opponents their chance to claim equality with us in what they boast about. 🛡️
## 2 Corinthians 11:13
They are false apostles and deceptive workers, disguised as Christ’s apostles. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 11:14
This is no surprise. Satan disguises himself as an angel of light. ⛔
## 2 Corinthians 11:15
His servants may likewise disguise themselves as servants of righteousness. Their end will match their actions. ⚖️
## 2 Corinthians 11:16
Do not think me a fool. If you do, receive me as a fool so I too can boast a little. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 11:17
In this confident boasting, I speak as a fool rather than as the Lord would. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 11:18
Many boast by human standards, so I will boast too. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 11:19
You gladly tolerate fools, since you are so wise! ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 11:20
You tolerate anyone who enslaves, exploits, or takes advantage of you, raises himself up, or strikes your face. ⛔
## 2 Corinthians 11:21
I admit with shame that we were too weak for that! Speaking as a fool, I can match anyone else’s daring boast. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 11:22
Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they Abraham’s descendants? So am I. 🌳
## 2 Corinthians 11:23
Are they Christ’s servants? I speak as if mad. I am far more, with harder work, more prison, worse beatings, and frequent danger of death. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 11:24
Five times, the Jews gave me forty lashes minus one. ⚖️
## 2 Corinthians 11:25
Three times I was beaten with rods, once stoned, and three times shipwrecked. I spent a night and day in the open sea. 🌊
## 2 Corinthians 11:26
I often traveled in danger from rivers, bandits, countrymen, and Gentiles. I faced danger in cities, countryside, sea, and among false brothers. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 11:27
I faced hard work, sleeplessness, hunger, thirst, often no food, cold, and exposure. 💔
## 2 Corinthians 11:28
Besides these outward trials, concern for all churches presses on me daily. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 11:29
Who is weak without me sharing his weakness? Who is led to sin without me burning with grief? 💔
## 2 Corinthians 11:30
If I must boast, I will boast about my weakness. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 11:31
The God and Father of the Lord Jesus knows I do not lie. He deserves praise forever. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 11:32
In Damascus, King Aretas’s governor guarded the city to arrest me. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 11:33
I escaped through a window in the wall, lowered in a basket. 🧺
