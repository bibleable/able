## 2 Corinthians 13:1
I come to you a third time. “Every matter requires testimony from two or three witnesses.” 📖
## 2 Corinthians 13:2
I warned you on my second visit. Now, while absent, I warn earlier sinners and everyone else: If I return, I will spare no one. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 13:3
You demand proof that Christ speaks through me. He is powerful among you, rather than weak toward you. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 13:4
He was crucified in weakness but lives by God’s power. We are weak in Him, but God’s power will make us live with Him in dealing with you. ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 13:5
Examine and test yourselves to see whether you are in the faith. Do you not know Jesus Christ is in you, unless you fail the test? 💭
## 2 Corinthians 13:6
I hope you realize we have not failed. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 13:7
We pray you do nothing wrong. We want you to do right, even if we seem to fail, rather than make us appear approved. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 13:8
We can act only for truth, never against it. 📖
## 2 Corinthians 13:9
We rejoice when you are strong, though we are weak. We pray for your perfection. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 13:10
I write while away so I need not act severely when present. The Lord gave me authority to build you up rather than destroy you. ✉️
## 2 Corinthians 13:11
Brothers, rejoice! Seek perfect harmony, encourage each other, think alike, and live peacefully. The God of love and peace will be with you. 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 13:12
Greet each other with a holy kiss. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 13:13
All God’s holy people send greetings. 👋
## 2 Corinthians 13:14
May the Lord Jesus Christ’s grace, God’s love, and the Holy Spirit’s fellowship be with you all. 🕊️
