## 2 Corinthians 2:1
I decided not to make another painful visit to you. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 2:2
If I grieve you, who can cheer me except those I grieved? ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 2:3
I wrote so that those who should make me rejoice would not grieve me when I came. I trusted all of you to share my joy. ✉️
## 2 Corinthians 2:4
I wrote with many tears, deep distress, and a troubled heart. I wanted you to know my great love, rather than cause you grief. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 2:5
If someone caused grief, he grieved all of you to some degree, rather than me alone. I do not want to overstate this. 💔
## 2 Corinthians 2:6
The punishment most of you gave him is enough. ⚖️
## 2 Corinthians 2:7
Forgive and comfort him now, so excessive sorrow does not overwhelm him. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 2:8
I urge you to confirm your love for him. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 2:9
I wrote to test whether you would obey in everything. 📜
## 2 Corinthians 2:10
If you forgive anyone, I forgive him too. If I forgave anything, I did it before Christ for your sake. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 2:11
We must not let Satan outwit us. We know his plans. 🛡️
## 2 Corinthians 2:12
I went to Troas to preach Christ’s good news. The Lord opened a door for me there. 📣
## 2 Corinthians 2:13
My spirit had no peace because my brother Titus was not there. I said goodbye and went to Macedonia. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 2:14
Thank God! He always leads us as captives in Christ’s victory procession. Through us, He spreads the fragrance of knowing Him everywhere. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 2:15
To God, we are Christ’s sweet scent among those being saved and those perishing. 🌸
## 2 Corinthians 2:16
To one group, we are a smell that brings death. To the other, we are a fragrance that brings life. Who is qualified for this task? 💭
## 2 Corinthians 2:17
Many sell God’s word for profit, but we do not. In Christ, we speak sincerely before God as people He sent. 📖
