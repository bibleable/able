## 2 Corinthians 3:1
Do we praise ourselves again? Do we need letters of recommendation to you or from you, as some people do? ✉️
## 2 Corinthians 3:2
You are our letter, written on our hearts and known and read by everyone. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 3:3
Clearly, you are Christ’s letter, the result of our service. It is written with the living God’s Spirit rather than ink. It is on human hearts rather than stone tablets. 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 3:4
Through Christ, we have this confidence before God. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 3:5
We cannot claim that anything comes from our own ability. Our ability comes from God. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 3:6
He qualified us to serve a new covenant of the Spirit, rather than the written letter. The letter kills, but the Spirit gives life. 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 3:7
The ministry of death was engraved in letters on stone. It came with glory, and Israelites could not look at Moses’ face because of its fading glory. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 3:8
Will the Spirit’s ministry not have even greater glory? 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 3:9
If the ministry of condemnation had glory, the ministry of righteousness has far more glory. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 3:10
The greater glory makes what was once glorious seem without glory by comparison. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 3:11
If what faded came with glory, what lasts has much greater glory. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 3:12
This hope makes us very bold. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 3:13
We differ from Moses. He veiled his face so Israelites could not see the end of what faded. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 3:14
Their minds were closed. Even today, that veil remains when the old covenant is read. Only Christ can remove it. 📖
## 2 Corinthians 3:15
Even today, a veil covers their hearts when Moses is read. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 3:16
Whenever anyone turns to the Lord, the veil is removed. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 3:17
The Lord is the Spirit. Where the Lord’s Spirit is, there is freedom. 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 3:18
With unveiled faces, we all reflect the Lord’s glory. We are changed into His image with increasing glory. This comes from the Lord, who is the Spirit. ✨
