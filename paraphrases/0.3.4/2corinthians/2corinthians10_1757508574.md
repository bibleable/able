## 2 Corinthians 10:1
I, Paul, appeal through Christ’s mildness and gentleness. I am humble with you in person but bold when away. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 10:2
Please do not make me use the boldness I expect toward those who think we live by human standards. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 10:3
We live in human bodies, but we do not wage war by human methods. ⚔️
## 2 Corinthians 10:4
Our weapons are not human weapons. They have God’s power to destroy strongholds. 🛡️
## 2 Corinthians 10:5
We destroy arguments and every proud claim against knowing God. We take every thought captive to obey Christ. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 10:6
Once your obedience is complete, we are ready to punish all disobedience. ⚖️
## 2 Corinthians 10:7
You look at outward appearances. Anyone sure he belongs to Christ must remember that we belong to Him too. 👀
## 2 Corinthians 10:8
I may boast too much about our authority, but I will not be ashamed. The Lord gave it to build you up rather than destroy you. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 10:9
I do not want my letters to seem intended to frighten you. ✉️
## 2 Corinthians 10:10
Some say, “His letters are weighty and forceful. His presence is unimpressive, and his speech worthless.” 💬
## 2 Corinthians 10:11
They should know our actions when present will match our letters when absent. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 10:12
We dare not compare or group ourselves with those who praise themselves. They measure and compare themselves by themselves, showing their ignorance. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 10:13
We boast only within the limits of the field God assigned us. That field reaches you. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 10:14
We do not cross our limits as though we never reached you. We were first to reach you with Christ’s good news. 📣
## 2 Corinthians 10:15
We do not boast beyond our limits in others’ work. We hope your growing faith greatly expands our influence among you. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 10:16
Then we can preach beyond you without boasting about work already done in someone else’s territory. 📣
## 2 Corinthians 10:17
“Anyone who boasts must boast in the Lord.” 📖
## 2 Corinthians 10:18
The Lord’s praise gives approval, rather than someone’s praise of himself. 🙏
