## 2 Corinthians 5:1
If our earthly tent is taken down, we have a building from God. It is an eternal heavenly house, not made by human hands. 🏠
## 2 Corinthians 5:2
We groan in this tent and long to be clothed with our heavenly home. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 5:3
When we are clothed, we will not be found naked. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 5:4
We groan under burdens in this tent. We want to be clothed rather than unclothed, so that life can swallow up what is mortal. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 5:5
God prepared us for this and gave His Spirit as a pledge of what will come. 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 5:6
We are always confident. We know that at home in the body, we are away from the Lord. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 5:7
We walk by faith rather than sight. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 5:8
We are confident and prefer to leave the body and be at home with the Lord. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 5:9
We aim to please Him, whether in this body or away from it. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 5:10
We must all appear before Christ’s judgment seat. Each will receive what he deserves for bodily actions, good or bad. ⚖️
## 2 Corinthians 5:11
We know what it means to fear the Lord, so we try to persuade people. God knows what we are, and I hope your conscience knows too. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 5:12
We do not praise ourselves again. We give you reason to be proud of us. You can answer those proud of outward appearances rather than the heart. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 5:13
If we are out of our minds, it is for God. If we are sensible, it is for you. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 5:14
Christ’s love compels us. We are sure that One died for all, and therefore all died. ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 5:15
He died for all so the living would no longer live for themselves. They should live for the One who died and rose for them. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 5:16
We no longer view anyone by human standards. We once viewed Christ this way, but no longer do. 💭
## 2 Corinthians 5:17
Anyone in Christ is a new creation. The old passed away, and the new has come! 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 5:18
God brought us back to Himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 5:19
In Christ, God was reconciling the world to Himself, without counting people’s sins against them. He entrusted the message of reconciliation to us. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 5:20
We are Christ’s ambassadors, as though God appeals through us. On Christ’s behalf, we beg you to be reconciled to God. 📣
## 2 Corinthians 5:21
God made the One who knew no sin to be sin for us. In Him, we could become God’s righteousness. ✝️
