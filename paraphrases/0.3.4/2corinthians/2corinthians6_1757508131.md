## 2 Corinthians 6:1
As God’s fellow workers, we urge you not to receive His grace for nothing. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 6:2
He says, “In the time of favor, I heard you. On salvation’s day, I helped you.” Now is the time of favor and the day of salvation! 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 6:3
We place no obstacle before anyone, so no one can discredit our ministry. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 6:4
We show ourselves as God’s servants in every way. We endure greatly through troubles, hardships, and disasters. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 6:5
We endure beatings, prison, riots, hard work, sleepless nights, and hunger. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 6:6
We serve in purity, knowledge, patience, kindness, the Holy Spirit, and sincere love. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 6:7
We serve with truthful words and God’s power. We hold weapons of righteousness in our right and left hands. 🛡️
## 2 Corinthians 6:8
We face glory and dishonor, slander and praise. People call us imposters, but we are genuine. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 6:9
We are unknown yet well known, dying yet alive, punished yet not killed. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 6:10
We are sorrowful yet always joyful, poor yet enriching many, with nothing yet possessing everything. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 6:11
Corinthians, we spoke freely, and our hearts are wide open. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 6:12
We do not hold back our love. You hold back yours. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 6:13
I speak as to my children. Respond fairly and open your hearts too. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 6:14
Do not share an unequal yoke with unbelievers. What partnership can righteousness have with evil? What fellowship can light have with darkness? ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 6:15
What harmony can Christ have with Belial? What does a believer share with an unbeliever? 💭
## 2 Corinthians 6:16
What agreement can God’s temple have with idols? We are the living God’s temple. He said, “I will live with them and walk among them. I will be their God, and they will be My people.” 🏠
## 2 Corinthians 6:17
The Lord says, “Come out from them and be separate. Touch nothing unclean, and I will welcome you.” 👣
## 2 Corinthians 6:18
The Lord Almighty says, “I will be your Father, and you will be My sons and daughters.” ❤️
