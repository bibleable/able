## 2 Corinthians 1:1
This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God’s will, and Timothy our brother. We write to God’s church in Corinth and all His holy people throughout Achaia. ✉️
## 2 Corinthians 1:2
May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace. 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 1:3
Praise the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! He is the Father of mercy and the God of all comfort. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 1:4
He comforts us in every trouble. We can then comfort others in any trouble with the comfort God gave us. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 1:5
Christ’s sufferings overflow to us, and our comfort also overflows through Christ. ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 1:6
If we suffer, it is for your comfort and salvation. If we receive comfort, it comforts you too. It helps you patiently endure the same sufferings we face. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 1:7
Our hope for you is firm. You share our sufferings and will also share our comfort. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 1:8
Brothers, know about our troubles in Asia’s province. The burden was far beyond our strength, and we despaired of life itself. 💔
## 2 Corinthians 1:9
We felt sentenced to death. This was so we would trust God, who raises the dead, rather than ourselves. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 1:10
He rescued us from deadly danger and will rescue us. We hope in Him to rescue us again. 🛡️
## 2 Corinthians 1:11
You help us through prayer. Many will then thank God for the favor shown us in answer to their prayers. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 1:12
Our conscience gives us this reason for pride. We lived in God’s holiness and sincerity, especially toward you. We depended on His grace rather than worldly wisdom. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 1:13
We write only what you can read and understand. I hope you will understand us fully. 📜
## 2 Corinthians 1:14
You already understand us partly. On our Lord Jesus’ day, you can be proud of us as we will be proud of you. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 1:15
With this confidence, I planned to visit you first so you could receive a double blessing. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 1:16
I wanted to visit on my way to Macedonia and return to you afterward. Then you could help me travel to Judea. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 1:17
Was I careless in this plan? Do I plan by human standards, saying both “Yes, yes” and “No, no”? 💬
## 2 Corinthians 1:18
As surely as God is faithful, our message to you is not both “Yes” and “No.” 📖
## 2 Corinthians 1:19
Silvanus, Timothy, and I announced God’s Son, Jesus Christ, among you. He was not both “Yes” and “No.” In Him, it has always been “Yes.” ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 1:20
All God’s promises are “Yes” in Christ. Through Him, we say “Amen” to God’s glory. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 1:21
God makes us and you firm in Christ. He anointed us. 🕊️
## 2 Corinthians 1:22
He placed His seal on us and His Spirit in our hearts as a pledge of what will come. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 1:23
God is my witness: I did not return to Corinth because I wanted to spare you. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 1:24
We do not rule over your faith. We work with you for your joy, because you stand firm by faith. 🤝
