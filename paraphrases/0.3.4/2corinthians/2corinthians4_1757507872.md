## 2 Corinthians 4:1
God mercifully gave us this ministry, so we do not lose heart. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 4:2
We rejected secret, shameful ways. We neither deceive nor twist God’s word. We openly announce truth and commend ourselves to everyone’s conscience before God. 📖
## 2 Corinthians 4:3
If our good news is veiled, it is veiled to those perishing. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 4:4
The god of this age blinded unbelievers’ minds. They cannot see the light of the good news about Christ’s glory. Christ is God’s image. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 4:5
We announce Jesus Christ as Lord, rather than ourselves. We are your servants for Jesus’ sake. ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 4:6
God said, “Let light shine from darkness.” He shone in our hearts so we could know His glory in Jesus Christ’s face. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 4:7
We have this treasure in clay jars. This shows that the very great power comes from God rather than us. 🏺
## 2 Corinthians 4:8
We are pressed on every side but not crushed. We are confused but do not despair. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 4:9
We face persecution but are not abandoned. We are struck down but not destroyed. 🛡️
## 2 Corinthians 4:10
We always carry Jesus’ death in our bodies, so His life can also appear in our bodies. ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 4:11
We who live are always handed over to death for Jesus’ sake. His life can then appear in our mortal bodies. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 4:12
Death works in us, but life works in you. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 4:13
Scripture says, “I believed, so I spoke.” We have that same spirit of faith. We believe and therefore speak. 📖
## 2 Corinthians 4:14
The One who raised the Lord Jesus will raise us with Him. He will present us with you before Him. 🌅
## 2 Corinthians 4:15
All this benefits you. Grace reaches more people, so thanks can overflow to God’s glory. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 4:16
We do not lose heart. Our outer self wastes away, but our inner self is renewed daily. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 4:17
Our light, brief trouble produces an eternal weight of glory beyond all comparison. ✨
## 2 Corinthians 4:18
We fix our eyes on unseen things rather than visible things. Visible things last temporarily, but unseen things last forever. 👀
