## 2 Corinthians 7:1
Dear friends, these promises are ours. Let us cleanse body and spirit from all uncleanness and complete our holiness in the fear of God. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 7:2
Make room for us in your hearts. We wronged, corrupted, and exploited no one. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 7:3
I do not condemn you. I already said you are in our hearts, so we live and die together. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 7:4
I have great confidence and pride in you. I am encouraged, and my joy overflows through all our troubles. 😊
## 2 Corinthians 7:5
In Macedonia, our bodies had no rest. Troubles pressed from every side, with conflict outside and fear within. 💔
## 2 Corinthians 7:6
God comforts discouraged people. He comforted us through Titus’s arrival. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 7:7
Your comfort to Titus also comforted us. He described your longing, mourning, and eager concern for me. I rejoiced even more. 😊
## 2 Corinthians 7:8
I do not regret my letter, even if it grieved you. I did regret it before. I see it caused sorrow, but only briefly. ✉️
## 2 Corinthians 7:9
I now rejoice because your sorrow led to repentance, rather than because you were sad. You felt the sorrow God intended, so we harmed you in no way. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 7:10
Godly sorrow brings repentance leading to salvation without regret. Worldly sorrow brings death. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 7:11
This godly sorrow brought earnestness, eagerness to clear yourselves, anger, alarm, longing, zeal, and vindication. In every way, you proved yourselves innocent in this matter. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 7:12
I wrote so you could see your earnest care for us before God. My purpose was not centered on the wrongdoer or the injured person. ✉️
## 2 Corinthians 7:13
This encourages us. Titus’s joy delighted us even more, because all of you refreshed his spirit. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 7:14
My boasts to Titus about you did not embarrass me. Everything we told you was true, and our boasts about you proved true too. 💬
## 2 Corinthians 7:15
He loves you even more when he remembers your obedience and how you welcomed him with fear and trembling. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 7:16
I rejoice that I can trust you completely. 😊
