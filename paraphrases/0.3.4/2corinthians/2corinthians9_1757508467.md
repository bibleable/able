## 2 Corinthians 9:1
I do not need to write about service to God’s holy people. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 9:2
I know your eagerness and tell the Macedonians proudly that Achaia was ready to give last year. Your zeal inspired most of them. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 9:3
I send the brothers so our boasts about you are not empty. Be ready, as I said you would. 👥
## 2 Corinthians 9:4
If Macedonians come with me and find you unready, we would be ashamed of our confidence. You would be ashamed too. ⚠️
## 2 Corinthians 9:5
I urged the brothers to visit first and prepare your promised generous gift. Then it will be ready, freely given rather than unwillingly. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 9:6
Anyone who plants little will harvest little. Anyone who plants generously will harvest generously. 🌾
## 2 Corinthians 9:7
Give what you decided in your heart, without regret or force. God loves a cheerful giver. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 9:8
God can make all grace overflow to you. You can always have everything you need and abound in every good task. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 9:9
Scripture says, “He scattered His gifts to poor people. His righteousness lasts forever.” 📖
## 2 Corinthians 9:10
He supplies seed to plant and bread to eat. He will supply and multiply your seed and increase your harvest of righteousness. 🌾
## 2 Corinthians 9:11
You will be enriched in every way to be generous on every occasion. Through us, your generosity will bring thanks to God. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 9:12
This service meets the needs of God’s holy people and overflows in many expressions of thanks to God. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 9:13
This service proves your obedient confession of Christ’s good news and your generosity to them and everyone else. God’s holy people will glorify Him for this. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 9:14
They will pray with affection for you because of God’s very great grace to you. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 9:15
Thank God for His gift beyond description! 🎁
