## 2 Corinthians 8:1
Brothers, we want you to know the grace God gave Macedonia’s churches. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 8:2
They suffered severely, yet their great joy and deep poverty overflowed in rich generosity. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 8:3
I testify that they willingly gave according to their ability and even beyond it. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 8:4
They earnestly begged for the privilege of sharing this service to God’s holy people. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 8:5
They exceeded our expectations. According to God’s will, they first gave themselves to the Lord, then to us. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 8:6
We urged Titus to complete this gracious act among you, as he began it. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 8:7
You excel in faith, speech, knowledge, full earnestness, and the love we inspired. Excel in this grace of giving too. 🌱
## 2 Corinthians 8:8
I do not command you. Through others’ earnestness, I test whether your love is sincere. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 8:9
You know our Lord Jesus Christ’s grace. He was rich but became poor for you. Through His poverty, you could become rich. ✝️
## 2 Corinthians 8:10
Here is my advice to help you. Last year, you were first to give and first to want to give. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 8:11
Finish now with the same eagerness, according to your means. 💪
## 2 Corinthians 8:12
A willing gift is accepted according to what someone has, rather than what he lacks. ❤️
## 2 Corinthians 8:13
We want equality, rather than relief for others and a burden for you. ⚖️
## 2 Corinthians 8:14
Your present abundance meets their need. In turn, their abundance will meet yours, so there is equality. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 8:15
Scripture says, “Anyone who gathered much had no excess. Anyone who gathered little lacked nothing.” 📖
## 2 Corinthians 8:16
Thank God for giving Titus the same devotion to you that I have. 🙏
## 2 Corinthians 8:17
He welcomed our appeal and eagerly comes by his own choice. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 8:18
We send with him the brother all the churches praise for his work in the good news. 📣
## 2 Corinthians 8:19
The churches chose this brother to travel with us and this gracious offering. We manage it to honor the Lord and show our eagerness to help. 🎁
## 2 Corinthians 8:20
We want no criticism of how we manage this generous gift. ⚖️
## 2 Corinthians 8:21
We carefully do what is right before people and the Lord. 👣
## 2 Corinthians 8:22
We also send our brother, who repeatedly proved his earnestness in many ways. His great confidence in you makes him even more eager. 👥
## 2 Corinthians 8:23
Titus is my partner and fellow worker among you. Our brothers are the churches’ messengers, the glory of Christ. 🤝
## 2 Corinthians 8:24
Show these men proof of your love and our reason for boasting about you, before all the churches. ❤️
