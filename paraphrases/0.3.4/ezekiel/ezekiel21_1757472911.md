## Ezekiel 21:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:2
“Son of man, face Jerusalem and preach against the sanctuaries. Prophesy against Israel's land. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:3
Tell her the LORD says, ‘I am against you. I will draw My sword from its sheath. I will cut off both righteous and wicked people from you. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:4
I will cut off both righteous and wicked people. Therefore, My sword will be drawn against everyone from south to north. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:5
Everyone will know that I, the LORD, drew My sword from its sheath. I will not put it back.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 21:6
Son of man, groan before them. Groan with a broken heart and bitter grief. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:7
When they ask why you groan, tell them, ‘News is coming. Every heart will melt, and every hand will become limp. Every spirit will faint, and every knee will turn to water.’ It is coming and will certainly happen. The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖

## Ezekiel 21:8
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:9
“Son of man, prophesy. Tell them the Lord says, ‘A sword! A sword is sharpened and polished. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:10
It is sharpened for slaughter and polished to flash like lightning. Should we rejoice in My son's scepter? The sword despises every such stick. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:11
The sword is to be polished and held in the hand. It is sharpened and polished for the killer's hand. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:12
Son of man, cry and wail! The sword is against My people and all Israel's princes. They are given to the sword with My people. Therefore, strike your thigh. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:13
Testing will certainly come! What if even the scepter the sword despises does not remain?’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:14
‘Son of man, prophesy and strike your hands together. Let the sword strike two times, even three. It is a sword that kills, a sword of great slaughter. It closes in on every side! 📖

## Ezekiel 21:15
I have placed a sword for slaughter at all their gates. Their hearts will melt, and many will stumble. The sword is ready to flash like lightning. It is drawn for slaughter. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:16
Slash right. Turn your blade left. Go wherever your blade is directed. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:17
I will also strike My hands together. I will satisfy My wrath.’ I, the LORD, have spoken.” 📖

## Ezekiel 21:18
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:19
“Son of man, mark two roads for the sword of Babylon's king to take. Both must start in the same land. Make a signpost where the road divides toward each city. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:20
Mark one road for the sword against Rabbah of the Ammonites. Mark the other against Judah, into fortified Jerusalem. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:21
Babylon's king stands where the two roads divide to seek an omen. He shakes arrows, consults idols, and examines a liver. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:22
His right hand receives the omen for Jerusalem. There he must set up battering rams and call for slaughter. He must raise a battle cry and direct battering rams against the gates. He must build a ramp and siege wall. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:23
Those who swore loyalty to him will consider it a false omen. But it will expose their guilt and take them captive. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:24
The Lord GOD says, ‘You have drawn attention to your guilt and exposed your rebellion. Your sins are revealed in all your deeds. You have been brought to remembrance. Therefore, you will be seized. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:25
Profane and wicked prince of Israel, your day has come. It is time for your final punishment.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 21:26
The Lord GOD says, ‘Remove the turban and take off the crown. Things will change. Raise the lowly and bring the exalted low. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:27
A ruin! A ruin! I will make it a ruin! It will not be restored until He comes to whom it belongs. I have given Him the right of judgment.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 21:28
Son of man, prophesy the Lord GOD's words about the Ammonites and their contempt. ‘A sword! A sword is drawn for slaughter. It is polished to consume and flash like lightning. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:29
They offer false visions and lying divinations for you. The sword is to be placed on the necks of the wicked who are slain. Their day has come, the time of their final punishment. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:30
Return the sword to its sheath! I will judge you where you were created, in your land of origin. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:31
I will pour out My anger on you. I will breathe My fury's fire against you. I will hand you over to brutal men skilled in destruction. 📖

## Ezekiel 21:32
You will be fuel for fire. Your blood will stain your own land. You will not be remembered. I, the LORD, have spoken.’” 📖
