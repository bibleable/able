## Ezekiel 15:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 15:2
“Son of man, how is vine wood better than any other branch among the forest's trees? 📖

## Ezekiel 15:3
Can its wood make anything useful? Can it make a peg to hang utensils on? 📖

## Ezekiel 15:4
No, it is thrown into the fire as fuel. Fire consumes both ends and chars the middle. Is it useful for anything? 📖

## Ezekiel 15:5
It could not be made useful even when it was whole. How much less useful is it after fire consumes and chars it! 📖

## Ezekiel 15:6
The Lord GOD says, ‘I gave vine wood from among the forest's trees to the fire as fuel. In the same way, I will give up Jerusalem's people. 📖

## Ezekiel 15:7
I will set My face against them. They may have escaped the fire, but another fire will consume them. When I set My face against them, you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 15:8
I will make the land desolate because they were unfaithful.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖
