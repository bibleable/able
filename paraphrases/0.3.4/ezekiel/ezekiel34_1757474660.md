## Ezekiel 34:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:2
“Son of man, prophesy against Israel's shepherds. Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘Woe to Israel's shepherds who feed only themselves! Should shepherds not feed their flock? 📖

## Ezekiel 34:3
You eat the fat and wear the wool. You kill the fattened sheep. But you do not feed the flock. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:4
You have not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, or bandaged the injured. You have not brought back strays or searched for the lost. You ruled them with violence and cruelty. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:5
They scattered because they had no shepherd. They became food for all the wild animals when they scattered. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:6
My flock wandered on all the mountains and every high hill. They scattered across the whole earth. No one searched for them or sought them out.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 34:7
Therefore, shepherds, hear the LORD's word. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:8
‘As surely as I live,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘My flock has no shepherd and has become prey and food for every wild animal. My shepherds did not search for My flock. They fed themselves instead. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:9
Therefore, shepherds, hear the LORD's word!’ 📖

## Ezekiel 34:10
The Lord GOD says, ‘I am against the shepherds. I will demand My flock from them. I will stop them tending the flock so they can no longer feed themselves. I will rescue My flock from their mouths. It will no longer be their food.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 34:11
The Lord GOD says, ‘I Myself will search for My flock and seek them out. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:12
A shepherd among scattered sheep searches for them. In the same way, I will search for My flock. I will rescue them from every place where they scattered on a day of clouds and darkness. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:13
I will bring them from the peoples, gather them from the countries, and return them to their land. I will feed them on Israel's mountains, in the ravines, and in all the land's settlements. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:14
I will feed them in good pasture. Israel's high mountains will be their grazing land. They will lie down in good grazing land. They will feed in rich pasture on Israel's mountains. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:15
I will tend My flock and make them lie down,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:16
‘I will seek the lost and bring back strays. I will bandage the injured and strengthen the weak. But I will destroy the sleek and strong. I will shepherd them with justice.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 34:17
The Lord GOD tells My flock, ‘I will judge between one sheep and another, between rams and goats. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:18
Is eating the good pasture not enough? Must your feet trample the rest? Is drinking clear water not enough? Must your feet muddy the rest? 📖

## Ezekiel 34:19
Why must My flock eat what your feet trampled? Why must they drink what your feet muddied?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 34:20
The Lord GOD tells them, ‘I Myself will judge between fat sheep and lean sheep. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:21
You push with your sides and shoulders. You strike all the weak ones with your horns until you scatter them abroad. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:22
Therefore, I will save My flock. They will no longer be prey. I will judge between one sheep and another. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:23
I will appoint one shepherd over them, My servant David. He will feed them. He will feed them and be their shepherd. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:24
I, the LORD, will be their God. My servant David will be a prince among them. I, the LORD, have spoken. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:25
I will make a covenant of peace with them. I will remove wild animals from the land. They can live securely in the wilderness and sleep in the forest. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:26
I will make them and the places around My hill a blessing. I will send rain in its season, showers of blessing. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:27
The field's trees will bear fruit. The land will yield produce. My flock will be secure in their land. I will break their yoke's bars and rescue them from those who enslaved them. Then they will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:28
The nations will no longer prey on them. The earth's animals will not consume them. They will live securely. No one will frighten them. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:29
I will raise a famous garden for them. Famine in the land will no longer harm them. They will no longer bear the nations' scorn. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:30
They will know that I, the LORD their God, am with them. They, the house of Israel, are My people.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 34:31
‘You are My flock, the sheep of My pasture, My people. I am your God,’ declares the Lord GOD.” 📖
