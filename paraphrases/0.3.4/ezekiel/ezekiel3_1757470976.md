## Ezekiel 3:1
He told me, “Son of man, eat what you find here. Eat this scroll. Then go and speak to the house of Israel.” 📖

## Ezekiel 3:2
I opened my mouth, and He fed me the scroll. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:3
He said, “Son of man, fill your stomach with the scroll I give you.” I ate it. It tasted as sweet as honey in my mouth. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:4
He said, “Son of man, go to the house of Israel now. Speak My words to them. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:5
I am sending you to the house of Israel. I am not sending you to people whose speech is unfamiliar or whose language is difficult. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:6
I am not sending you to many peoples whose unfamiliar speech and difficult languages you cannot understand. If I had sent you to them, they would have listened. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:7
But the house of Israel will not listen to you because they will not listen to Me. The whole house of Israel has stubborn heads and hearts. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:8
I will make your face as hard as their faces. I will make your forehead as hard as theirs. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:9
I will make your forehead like a diamond, harder than flint. Do not fear them or lose courage in their presence. They are a rebellious house.” 📖

## Ezekiel 3:10
He added, “Son of man, listen carefully to every word I speak. Take these words to heart. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:11
Go to your people, the exiles. Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’ Do this whether they listen or refuse.” 📖

## Ezekiel 3:12
The Spirit lifted me. I heard a great rumbling sound behind me. It said, “May the LORD's glory be blessed in His dwelling place!” 📖

## Ezekiel 3:13
The creatures' wings brushed against each other. Their wings and the wheels beside them made a great rumbling sound. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:14
The Spirit lifted me and carried me away. I went with bitterness and anger in my spirit. The LORD's strong hand was on me. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:15
I came to the exiles at Tel-abib beside the River Kebar. I sat where they sat. I stayed among them for seven days, overwhelmed. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:16
After seven days, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:17
“Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel. Whenever you hear a word from My mouth, warn them for Me. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:18
Suppose I tell a wicked man, ‘You will certainly die.’ You must warn him to leave his wicked way and save his life. If you do not warn him, he will die in his wrongdoing. I will hold you responsible for his blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:19
Suppose you warn a wicked man, but he does not leave his wickedness and wicked way. He will die in his wrongdoing. But you will have saved yourself. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:20
Suppose a righteous man leaves his righteousness and does wrong, and I put a stumbling block before him. He will die. If you did not warn him, he will die in his sin. His righteous acts will not be remembered. I will hold you responsible for his blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:21
Suppose you warn a righteous man not to sin, and he does not sin. He will live because he obeyed your warning. You will have saved yourself.” 📖

## Ezekiel 3:22
The LORD's hand was on me there. He said, “Get up and go to the plain. I will speak with you there.” 📖

## Ezekiel 3:23
I got up and went to the plain. The LORD's glory was there, like the glory I had seen beside the River Kebar. I fell facedown. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:24
The Spirit entered me and made me stand on my feet. He told me, “Go and shut yourself inside your house. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:25
Son of man, they will tie you with ropes. You will be bound and unable to go among the people. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:26
I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth. You will be silent and unable to rebuke them, although they are a rebellious house. 📖

## Ezekiel 3:27
When I speak with you, I will open your mouth. Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’ Let those who listen, listen. Let those who refuse, refuse. They are a rebellious house. 📖
