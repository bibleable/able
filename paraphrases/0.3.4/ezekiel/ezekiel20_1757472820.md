## Ezekiel 20:1
In the seventh year, on the tenth day of the fifth month, some of Israel's elders came to inquire of the LORD. They sat before me. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:2
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:3
“Son of man, tell Israel's elders the Lord GOD says, ‘Have you come to inquire of Me? As surely as I live, I will not let you consult Me.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:4
Will you judge them? Will you judge them, son of man? Confront them with their fathers' detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:5
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘When I chose Israel, I swore an oath to the house of Jacob's descendants. I made Myself known to them in Egypt. I raised My hand and said, “I am the LORD your God.” 📖

## Ezekiel 20:6
That day, I swore to bring them from Egypt to a land I had found for them. It flowed with milk and honey. It was the glory of all lands. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:7
I told them, “Each must throw away the detestable things before his eyes. Do not defile yourselves with Egypt's idols. I am the LORD your God.” 📖

## Ezekiel 20:8
But they rebelled and refused to listen. None threw away the detestable things before their eyes. They did not abandon Egypt's idols. I decided to pour out My wrath and anger on them in Egypt. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:9
But I acted for My name's sake. I kept it from being dishonored before the nations among whom they lived. Those nations had seen Me reveal Myself to Israel by bringing them from Egypt. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:10
I brought them from Egypt. I led them into the wilderness. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:11
I gave them My statutes and taught them My ordinances. The man who does these things will live by them. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:12
I also gave them My Sabbaths as a sign between us. They would know that I am the LORD who makes them holy. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:13
The house of Israel rebelled against Me in the wilderness. They did not follow My statutes. They rejected My ordinances, although a man who does these things will live by them. They completely defiled My Sabbaths. I decided to pour out My wrath and destroy them in the wilderness. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:14
But I acted for My name's sake. I kept it from being dishonored before the nations who had seen Me bring them out. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:15
In the wilderness, I raised My hand and swore not to bring them into the land I had given them. It flowed with milk and honey. It was the glory of all lands. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:16
They kept rejecting My ordinances, refusing My statutes, and defiling My Sabbaths. Their hearts continually pursued their idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:17
Yet I pitied them. I did not destroy them or bring them to an end in the wilderness. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:18
I told their children in the wilderness, “Do not follow your fathers' statutes or keep their ordinances. Do not defile yourselves with their idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:19
I am the LORD your God. Follow My statutes. Keep My ordinances and practice them. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:20
Keep My Sabbaths holy as a sign between us. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God.” 📖

## Ezekiel 20:21
But the children rebelled against Me. They did not follow My statutes or carefully keep My ordinances. Yet a man who does these things will live by them. They defiled My Sabbaths. I decided to pour out My wrath and anger on them in the wilderness. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:22
But I held back My hand and acted for My name's sake. I kept it from being dishonored before the nations who had seen Me bring them out. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:23
Yet in the wilderness, I raised My hand and swore to scatter them among nations and throughout the lands. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:24
They did not practice My ordinances. They rejected My statutes and defiled My Sabbaths. They fixed their eyes on their fathers' idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:25
I also handed them over to statutes that were not good. I handed them over to ordinances by which they could not live. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:26
I pronounced them unclean through their gifts, the sacrifice of every firstborn in fire. I did this to devastate them. Then they would know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:27
Son of man, tell the house of Israel the Lord GOD says, “Your fathers also blasphemed Me in this way. They were unfaithful to Me. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:28
I brought them into the land I swore to give them. Whenever they saw a high hill or leafy tree, they offered sacrifices there. They presented offerings that provoked Me. They sent up fragrant incense and poured out drink offerings. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:29
I asked, ‘What is this high place you go to?’ It is still called Bamah today. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:30
Tell the house of Israel the Lord GOD says, ‘Will you defile yourselves like your fathers? Will you prostitute yourselves with their detestable things? 📖

## Ezekiel 20:31
You offer gifts and sacrifice your sons in fire. You still defile yourselves with all your idols today. House of Israel, should I let you consult Me? As surely as I live, I will not let you consult Me.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:32
You say, ‘Let us be like the nations and peoples of the lands. Let us serve wood and stone.’ What you intend will never happen. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:33
The Lord GOD declares, ‘As surely as I live, I will rule over you. I will use a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and wrath poured out. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:34
I will bring you from the peoples and gather you from the lands where you were scattered. I will use a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and wrath poured out. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:35
I will bring you into the nations' wilderness. I will judge you face to face there. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:36
I will judge you as I judged your fathers in the wilderness of Egypt's land.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:37
‘I will make you pass under the rod. I will bring you into the covenant's bond. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:38
I will remove those who rebel and transgress against Me from among you. I will bring them out of the land where they live. But they will not enter Israel's land. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:39
House of Israel, the Lord GOD says, “Go and serve your idols, every one of you. But afterward, you will listen to Me. Your gifts and idols will no longer defile My holy name. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:40
The whole house of Israel will serve Me in the land on My holy mountain, Israel's high mountain,” declares the Lord GOD. “I will accept them there. I will require your offerings, best gifts, and all your holy sacrifices. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:41
I will bring you from the peoples and gather you from the lands where you were scattered. Then I will accept you as a pleasing aroma. I will show My holiness through you before the nations. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:42
I will bring you into Israel's land, which I swore to give your fathers. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:43
You will remember your ways and all the acts that defiled you there. You will hate yourselves for all the evil you did. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:44
House of Israel, I will treat you for My name's sake. I will not treat you according to your wicked ways and corrupt acts. Then you will know that I am the LORD.” The Lord GOD declares this.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 20:45
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:46
“Son of man, face south and preach against it. Prophesy against the Negev's forest. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:47
Tell the Negev's forest to hear the LORD's word. The Lord GOD says, ‘I will start a fire in you. It will consume all your trees, both green and dry. The blazing flame will not be put out. It will scorch every face from south to north. 📖

## Ezekiel 20:48
Everyone will see that I, the LORD, started the fire. It will not be put out.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 20:49
I said, “Lord GOD, they say about me, ‘Is he not just telling parables?’” 📖
