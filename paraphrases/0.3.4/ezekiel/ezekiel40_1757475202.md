## Ezekiel 40:1
It was the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the year's beginning, on the tenth day of the month. Fourteen years had passed since Jerusalem fell. That very day, the LORD's hand was on me. He took me there. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:2
In visions of God, He brought me to Israel's land. He placed me on a very high mountain. A structure like a city stood on its southern slope. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:3
He took me there. I saw a man who looked like bronze. He stood in the gateway with a linen cord and measuring rod in his hand. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:4
He said, “Son of man, look with your eyes and listen with your ears. Pay attention to everything I show you. That is why you were brought here. Tell the house of Israel everything you see.” 📖

## Ezekiel 40:5
I saw a wall around the temple area. The man's measuring rod was six long cubits. Each long cubit measured one cubit and one handbreadth. He measured the wall as one rod thick and one rod high. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:6
He went to the east gate and climbed its steps. He measured the gate threshold as one rod deep. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:7
Each gate chamber was one rod long and one rod wide. Five cubits separated the chambers. The inner threshold beside the inward-facing portico was one rod deep. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:8
He measured the gateway's inner portico. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:9
It was eight cubits deep. Its jambs were two cubits thick. The gateway's portico faced the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:10
The east gate had three gate chambers on each side. All had the same measurements. The gateposts on both sides also matched in size. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:11
He measured the gateway entrance as ten cubits wide and thirteen cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:12
A wall one cubit high stood before each gate chamber. Each chamber was six cubits square. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:13
He measured from one gate chamber's roof to the opposite chamber's roof. The distance from doorway to doorway was twenty-five cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:14
He measured the gateposts as sixty cubits high. The gateway extended around to the courtyard's gatepost. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:15
The distance from the gateway entrance to the far end of its inner portico was fifty cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:16
The gate chambers and side pillars had beveled windows around the gateway's inside. The porticos also had windows all around inside. Palm trees decorated each side pillar. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:17
He brought me into the outer court. Chambers and pavement surrounded the court. Thirty chambers faced the pavement. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:18
The pavement ran beside the gateways and matched the gates' length. This was the lower pavement. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:19
He measured from the front of the lower gateway to the inner court's outside. The distance was one hundred cubits on both the east and north sides. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:20
He measured the length and width of the outer court's north gateway. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:21
It had three gate chambers on each side. Its chambers, side pillars, and portico matched the first gate's measurements. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:22
Its windows, portico, and palm trees matched those of the east gate. Seven steps led up to it. Its portico was opposite the steps. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:23
A gate to the inner court faced the north gate, as on the east. He measured one hundred cubits between the gateways. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:24
He led me south. I saw a gateway facing south. He measured its side pillars and portico. Their measurements matched the others. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:25
The gateway and portico had windows all around, like the other windows. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:26
Seven steps led up to it. Its portico was opposite them. A palm tree decorated each side pillar. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:27
The inner court also had a south gate. He measured one hundred cubits between the gateways toward the south. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:28
He brought me into the inner court through the south gate. He measured this gate. Its measurements matched the others. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:29
Its gate chambers, side pillars, and portico matched the others' measurements. The gateway and portico had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:30
The porticoes around the inner court were twenty-five cubits long and five cubits deep. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:31
Its portico faced the outer court. Palm trees decorated its side pillars. Eight steps led up to it. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:32
He brought me to the inner court's east side. He measured the gateway. Its measurements matched the others. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:33
Its gate chambers, side pillars, and portico matched the others' measurements. The gateway and portico had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:34
Its portico faced the outer court. Palm trees decorated the side pillars on both sides. Eight steps led up to it. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:35
He brought me to the north gate and measured it. Its measurements matched the others. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:36
Its gate chambers, side pillars, and portico also matched the others. It had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:37
Its portico faced the outer court. Palm trees decorated the side pillars on both sides. Eight steps led up to it. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:38
Each inner gateway had a chamber with a doorway beside the portico. The burnt offering was washed there. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:39
Two tables stood on each side inside the gateway's portico. Burnt offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings were killed on them. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:40
Outside, approaching the north gateway entrance, two tables stood on one side. Two more stood on the other side of the gate's portico. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:41
Four tables were inside the gateway, and four were outside. These eight tables were used to kill the sacrifices. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:42
Four more tables for the burnt offering were made from dressed stone. Each was one and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one cubit high. They held the utensils used to kill burnt offerings and other sacrifices. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:43
Hooks with two prongs were fastened around the room's inside. Each was one handbreadth long. The offering's flesh was placed on the tables. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:44
Two chambers stood outside the inner gate, within the inner court. One stood beside the north gate and faced south. The other stood beside the south gate and faced north. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:45
The man told me, “The south-facing chamber is for priests responsible for the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:46
The north-facing chamber is for priests responsible for the altar. These are Zadok's sons, the only Levites who may approach the LORD to serve before Him.” 📖

## Ezekiel 40:47
He measured the court. It was square, one hundred cubits long and one hundred cubits wide. The altar stood before the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:48
He brought me to the temple's portico. He measured its side pillars as five cubits on each side. The gateway was fourteen cubits wide. Its sidewalls were three cubits on each side. 📖

## Ezekiel 40:49
The portico was twenty cubits wide and twelve cubits deep. Ten steps led up to it. A column stood beside each side pillar. 📖
