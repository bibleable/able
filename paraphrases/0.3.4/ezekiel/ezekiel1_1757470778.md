## Ezekiel 1:1
In the thirtieth year, on the fifth day of the fourth month, I was among the exiles beside the River Kebar. The heavens opened, and I saw visions of God. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:2
It was the fifth day of the month. It was also the fifth year of King Jehoiachin's exile. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:3
The LORD's word came directly to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi. He was in the Chaldeans' land beside the River Kebar. The LORD's hand was on him there. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:4
I saw a whirlwind coming from the north. A great cloud had fire flashing back and forth, with bright light around it. A gleam like amber was in the fire's center. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:5
Within it, I saw the forms of four living creatures. Their forms looked human. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:6
But each creature had four faces and four wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:7
Their legs were straight. The soles of their feet looked like calves' hooves. They shone like polished bronze. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:8
They had human hands under their wings on their four sides. All four living creatures had faces and wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:9
Their wings touched each other. The creatures did not turn when they moved. Each went straight ahead. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:10
Each of the four had a human face. Each also had a lion's face on the right, an ox's face on the left, and an eagle's face. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:11
These were their faces. Their wings spread upward. Each had two wings touching the wings of the creatures on either side. Two wings covered each creature's body. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:12
Each creature went straight ahead. They went wherever the spirit wanted to go. They did not turn while moving. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:13
Among the creatures, I saw what looked like glowing coals or torches. Bright fire moved back and forth between them. Lightning flashed from it. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:14
The creatures moved quickly back and forth like flashes of lightning. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:15
I looked at the creatures. I saw a wheel on the ground beside each creature with its four faces. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:16
All four wheels looked alike and shone like beryl. Each looked like a wheel inside another wheel. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:17
They could move in any of the four directions. They did not turn as they moved. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:18
Their rims were high and inspired awe. Eyes covered all four rims, all the way around. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:19
When the creatures moved, the wheels moved beside them. When the creatures rose from the ground, the wheels also rose. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:20
They went wherever the spirit wanted to go. The wheels rose beside them because the creatures' spirit was in the wheels. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:21
When the creatures moved, the wheels moved. When they stood still, the wheels stood still. When they rose from the ground, the wheels rose beside them. The creatures' spirit was in the wheels. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:22
Above the creatures' heads was something like a vast expanse. It shone like crystal and inspired awe. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:23
Under this expanse, their wings stretched toward each other. Each creature also had two wings covering its body. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:24
When the creatures moved, their wings sounded like roaring waters, the Almighty's voice, and an army's noise. When they stood still, they lowered their wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:25
They stood still with their wings lowered. A voice came from above the expanse over their heads. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:26
Above the expanse over their heads was something like a sapphire throne. High on the throne was a figure that looked like a man. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:27
Above what seemed to be His waist, I saw an amber gleam with what looked like fire inside, all around. Below what seemed to be His waist, I saw what looked like fire. Bright light surrounded Him. 📖

## Ezekiel 1:28
The bright light around Him looked like a rainbow in a cloud on a rainy day. This was the appearance of the likeness of the LORD's glory. When I saw it, I fell facedown. I heard a voice speaking. 📖
