## Ezekiel 35:1
The LORD's word also came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:2
“Son of man, face Mount Seir. Prophesy against it. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:3
Announce the Lord GOD's words. ‘Mount Seir, I am against you. I will reach out My hand against you and make you a desolate wasteland. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:4
I will ruin your cities. You will become desolate. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:5
You held an ancient hatred. You handed the Israelites over to the sword during their disaster, at the final stage of their punishment. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:6
Therefore, as surely as I live, I will hand you over to bloodshed,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘It will pursue you. You did not hate bloodshed. Therefore, it will pursue you. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:7
I will make Mount Seir a desolate wasteland. I will cut off everyone who comes and goes there. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:8
I will fill its mountains with the dead. Those killed by the sword will fall on your hills, in your valleys, and in all your ravines. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:9
I will make you desolate forever. Your cities will be uninhabited. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:10
You said, “These two nations and countries will be ours. We will possess them.” Yet the LORD was there. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:11
As surely as I live, I will treat you according to the anger and jealousy you showed in hatred against them,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘When I judge you, I will make Myself known among them. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:12
You will know that I, the LORD, heard every contemptuous word you spoke against Israel's mountains. You said, “They are desolate. They are ours to devour!” 📖

## Ezekiel 35:13
Your mouth boasted against Me. You added more and more words against Me. I heard it Myself! 📖

## Ezekiel 35:14
The Lord GOD says, “While the whole earth rejoices, I will make you desolate. 📖

## Ezekiel 35:15
You rejoiced when the house of Israel's inheritance became desolate. I will do the same to you. Mount Seir and all Edom will become desolate. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”’ 📖
