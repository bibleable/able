## Ezekiel 29:1
In the tenth year, on the twelfth day of the tenth month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:2
“Son of man, face Pharaoh, Egypt's king. Prophesy against him and all Egypt. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:3
Tell him the Lord GOD says, ‘Pharaoh, Egypt's king, I am against you. You are a great monster lying among your rivers. You say, “The Nile is mine. I made it myself.” 📖

## Ezekiel 29:4
I will put hooks in your jaws. I will make your streams' fish cling to your scales. I will pull you from your rivers. All your streams' fish will cling to your scales. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:5
I will leave you and all your streams' fish in the desert. You will fall in the open field. No one will take you away or gather you for burial. I have given you as food to the earth's animals and the birds of the air. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:6
All Egypt's people will know that I am the LORD. You were only a staff made of reeds for the house of Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:7
When Israel held you, you splintered and tore all their shoulders. When they leaned on you, you broke and twisted their backs. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:8
The Lord GOD says, “I will bring a sword against you. I will destroy your people and animals. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:9
Egypt's land will become a desolate wasteland. They will know that I am the LORD. You said, ‘The Nile is mine. I made it.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 29:10
Therefore, I am against you and your rivers. I will make Egypt's land a desolate ruin, from Migdol to Syene and as far as Cush's border. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:11
No human or animal foot will pass through it. It will be uninhabited for forty years. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:12
I will make Egypt's land desolate among desolate lands. Its cities will lie desolate for forty years among ruined cities. I will scatter the Egyptians among nations and throughout countries. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:13
The Lord GOD says, ‘After forty years, I will gather the Egyptians from the nations where they were scattered. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:14
I will restore Egypt from captivity. I will bring them back to Pathros, their land of origin. They will be a lowly kingdom there. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:15
Egypt will be the lowest of kingdoms. It will never rise above the nations again. I will weaken Egypt so it never rules the nations again. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:16
The house of Israel will never trust Egypt again. Egypt will remind them of their wrongdoing in turning to the Egyptians. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 29:17
In the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:18
“Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon's king, made his army work very hard against Tyre. Every head became bald, and every shoulder became raw. But he and his army received no wages from Tyre for their work. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:19
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will give Egypt's land to Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon's king. He will carry away its wealth, spoil, and plunder. This will pay his army's wages. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:20
I have given him Egypt's land as a reward for his work. He did it for Me,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 29:21
‘That day, I will make a horn sprout for the house of Israel. I will open your mouth to speak among them. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’” 📖
