## Ezekiel 48:1
“These are the tribes' names. Dan will have one portion at the northern frontier, from east to west. It will border the Hethlon road to Lebo-hamath, continuing to Hazar-enan on Damascus's border. Hamath will be to the north. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:2
Asher will have one portion beside Dan's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:3
Naphtali will have one portion beside Asher's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:4
Manasseh will have one portion beside Naphtali's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:5
Ephraim will have one portion beside Manasseh's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:6
Reuben will have one portion beside Ephraim's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:7
Judah will have one portion beside Reuben's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:8
Set apart a portion beside Judah's territory, from east to west. It will be 25,000 cubits wide. Its length will match a tribal portion from east to west. The sanctuary will be in its center. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:9
The special portion for the LORD must be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:10
This will be the priests' holy portion. Its north and south sides will each be 25,000 cubits long. Its east and west sides will each be 10,000 cubits wide. The LORD's sanctuary will be in its center. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:11
It will belong to the dedicated priests descended from Zadok. They kept My charge when the Israelites strayed. They did not stray as the Levites did. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:12
This will be their special portion set apart from the land. It will be most holy, beside the Levites' territory. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:13
The Levites' area will border the priests' territory. It will be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. Its full length will be 25,000 cubits, and its width 10,000 cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:14
They must not sell or exchange any of it. They must not transfer this best part of the land. It is holy to the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:15
The remaining area, 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long, will serve the city's common use, houses, and pastureland. The city will be in its center. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:16
The city's north, south, east, and west sides will each measure 4,500 cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:17
The city's pastureland will extend 250 cubits in each direction: north, south, east, and west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:18
The remaining length beside the holy portion will be 10,000 cubits on the east and 10,000 cubits on the west. Its produce will feed the city's workers. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:19
The city workers cultivating it will come from all Israel's tribes. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:20
The whole portion will be a square, 25,000 cubits by 25,000 cubits. Set apart the holy portion with the city property. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:21
The remaining area on both sides of the holy portion and city property will belong to the prince. His land will adjoin the tribal portions. It will extend east from the holy district's 25,000 cubits to the eastern border. It will extend west from the 25,000 cubits to the western border. The holy portion and temple sanctuary will be in the center. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:22
The Levites' property and city property will lie in the center of the prince's area. This area is between Judah's and Benjamin's borders. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:23
For the remaining tribes, Benjamin will have one portion from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:24
Simeon will have one portion beside Benjamin's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:25
Issachar will have one portion beside Simeon's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:26
Zebulun will have one portion beside Issachar's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:27
Gad will have one portion beside Zebulun's territory, from east to west. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:28
Gad's southern border will run from Tamar to Meribath-kadesh's waters. It will follow the Brook of Egypt to the Great Sea. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:29
Allot this land to Israel's tribes as their inheritance. These will be their portions,” declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:30
“These are the city's exits. The north side will be 4,500 cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:31
The city gates will be named for Israel's tribes. Three northern gates will be named Reuben, Judah, and Levi. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:32
The east side will be 4,500 cubits long. Its three gates will be named Joseph, Benjamin, and Dan. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:33
The south side will be 4,500 cubits long. Its three gates will be named Simeon, Issachar, and Zebulun. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:34
The west side will be 4,500 cubits long. Its three gates will be named Gad, Asher, and Naphtali. 📖

## Ezekiel 48:35
The city's perimeter will be 18,000 cubits. From that day, its name will be THE LORD IS THERE.” 📖
