## Ezekiel 4:1
“Son of man, take a brick and put it in front of you. Draw the city of Jerusalem on it. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:2
Lay siege to it. Build a siege wall and a ramp. Set up camps against it. Put battering rams around it on every side. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:3
Put an iron plate between yourself and the city as an iron wall. Face the city and besiege it. This will be a sign to the house of Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:4
Lie on your left side. Put the house of Israel's wrongdoing on yourself. Bear their wrongdoing for all the days you lie on your side. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:5
I have assigned you 390 days, corresponding to the years of their wrongdoing. You must bear the house of Israel's wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:6
After those days, lie on your right side. Bear the house of Judah's wrongdoing. I have assigned you 40 days, one day for each year. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:7
Face Jerusalem's siege with your arm bare. Prophesy against the city. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:8
I will tie you with ropes. You will not be able to turn from side to side until your siege days end. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:9
Put wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt in one container. Make bread from them. Eat this during the 390 days you lie on your side. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:10
Weigh out twenty shekels of food each day. Eat it at set times. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:11
Measure out one sixth of a hin of water. Drink it at set times. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:12
Eat the food like a barley cake. Bake it over dried human excrement where the people can see you.” 📖

## Ezekiel 4:13
The LORD said, “This is how the Israelites will eat their defiled bread among the nations where I will banish them.” 📖

## Ezekiel 4:14
I said, “Lord GOD, I have never defiled myself. Since my youth, I have not eaten anything found dead or torn by wild animals. No unclean meat has ever entered my mouth.” 📖

## Ezekiel 4:15
He replied, “I will let you use cow dung instead of human excrement. You may bake your bread over it.” 📖

## Ezekiel 4:16
He told me, “Son of man, I will cut off Jerusalem's food supply. People will eat bread anxiously, rationed by weight. They will drink measured water in despair. 📖

## Ezekiel 4:17
They will lack food and water. They will be horrified as they see each other wasting away in their wrongdoing. 📖
