## Ezekiel 33:1
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:2
“Son of man, tell your people, ‘Suppose I bring the sword against a land. Its people choose one of their men as a watchman. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:3
He sees the sword coming against the land. He blows the ram's horn to warn the people. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:4
Suppose someone hears the horn but ignores the warning. The sword comes and takes his life. His blood will be on his own head. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:5
He heard the horn but ignored the warning. Therefore, his blood will be on his own head. If he had obeyed the warning, he would have saved his life. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:6
Suppose the watchman sees the sword coming but does not blow the horn to warn the people. The sword comes and takes someone's life. That person will die in his wrongdoing. But I will hold the watchman responsible for his blood.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:7
Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel. Hear the word from My mouth. Warn them for Me. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:8
Suppose I tell a wicked man, ‘You will certainly die.’ If you do not speak to turn him from his way, he will die in his wrongdoing. But I will hold you responsible for his blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:9
Suppose you warn a wicked man to leave his way, but he does not turn. He will die in his wrongdoing. But you will have saved your life. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:10
Son of man, tell the house of Israel what they have said. ‘Our rebellions and sins weigh heavily on us. They make us waste away. How can we live?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:11
Tell them, ‘As surely as I live, I take no pleasure in wicked people's death,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘I prefer them to leave their ways and live. Turn! Turn from your evil ways! House of Israel, why should you die?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:12
Son of man, tell your people, ‘A righteous man's righteousness will not save him when he rebels. A wicked man's wickedness will not make him fall when he turns from it. A righteous man cannot survive by his righteousness when he sins.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:13
Suppose I tell a righteous man he will certainly live. But he trusts his righteousness and does wrong. None of his righteous works will be remembered. He will die because of his wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:14
Suppose I tell a wicked man, ‘You will certainly die.’ He turns from sin and does what is just and right. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:15
He returns a pledge and repays what he stole. He follows the statutes of life without doing wrong. Then he will certainly live. He will not die. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:16
None of his sins will be held against him. He has done what is just and right. He will certainly live. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:17
Yet your people say, ‘The Lord's way is unjust.’ But their way is unjust. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:18
If a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does wrong, he will die for it. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:19
If a wicked man leaves his wickedness and does what is just and right, he will live because of it. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:20
Yet you say, ‘The Lord's way is unjust.’ House of Israel, I will judge each of you according to his ways.” 📖

## Ezekiel 33:21
In the twelfth year of our exile, on the fifth day of the tenth month, a fugitive from Jerusalem came. He told me, “The city has been captured!” 📖

## Ezekiel 33:22
The LORD's hand was on me the evening before the fugitive arrived. He opened my mouth before the man came that morning. I could speak and was no longer mute. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:23
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:24
“Son of man, people living in Israel's ruins say, ‘Abraham was only one man, but he possessed the land. We are many. Surely the land has been given to us to possess.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:25
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘You eat meat with blood in it. You look to idols and shed blood. Should you possess the land? 📖

## Ezekiel 33:26
You rely on swords and commit detestable acts. Each of you defiles his neighbor's wife. Should you possess the land?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:27
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘As surely as I live, those in the ruins will die by the sword. I will give those in open fields to wild animals to devour. Those in strongholds and caves will die from plague. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:28
I will make the land a desolate wasteland. Her proud strength will end. Israel's mountains will be desolate, and no one will pass through. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:29
I will make the land desolate because of all their detestable acts. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:30
Son of man, your people discuss you beside city walls and in their doorways. They tell each other, ‘Come and hear the LORD's message!’ 📖

## Ezekiel 33:31
My people come as usual and sit before you. They hear your words but do not practice them. Their mouths express love, but their hearts pursue dishonest gain. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:32
You are like a singer of love songs to them. Your voice is beautiful, and you play an instrument skillfully. They hear your words but do not practice them. 📖

## Ezekiel 33:33
It will certainly happen. When it does, they will know a prophet has been among them.” 📖
