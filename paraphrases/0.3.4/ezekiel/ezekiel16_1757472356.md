## Ezekiel 16:1
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:2
“Son of man, confront Jerusalem about her detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:3
Tell Jerusalem the Lord GOD says, ‘You originated and were born in the Canaanites' land. Your father was an Amorite. Your mother was a Hittite. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:4
On the day you were born, no one cut your cord or washed you with water. No one rubbed you with salt or wrapped you in cloths. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:5
No one had enough compassion to do even one of these things for you. They threw you into the open field. You were despised on the day you were born. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:6
I passed and saw you struggling in your blood. As you lay in your blood, I told you, “Live!” I told you there, “Live!” 📖

## Ezekiel 16:7
I made you thrive like a field plant. You grew, matured, and became very beautiful. Your breasts formed, and your hair grew. But you were naked and bare. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:8
I passed and saw you were old enough for love. I spread My cloak over you and covered your nakedness. I pledged Myself to you and entered a covenant with you. You became Mine,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:9
I bathed you with water and washed away your blood. I anointed you with oil. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:10
I clothed you with embroidered cloth and gave you fine leather sandals. I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you with silk. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:11
I decorated you with jewelry. I put bracelets on your wrists and a chain around your neck. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:12
I put a ring in your nose and earrings on your ears. I put a beautiful crown on your head. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:13
Gold and silver decorated you. You wore fine linen, silk, and embroidered cloth. You ate fine flour, honey, and oil. You became very beautiful and rose to be queen. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:14
The Lord GOD declares, ‘Your beauty made you famous among the nations. It was perfect because of the splendor I gave you. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:15
But your fame made you trust your beauty and act as a prostitute. You gave your favors to everyone passing by. Your beauty was theirs whenever they asked. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:16
You used some garments to make colorful high places. You prostituted yourself on them. Such things should never have happened! 📖

## Ezekiel 16:17
You took the fine gold and silver jewelry I gave you. You made male idols and prostituted yourself with them. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:18
You covered them with your embroidered garments. You put My oil and incense before them. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:19
You put the food I gave you before them as a pleasing aroma. It was the fine flour, oil, and honey I fed you. That is what happened,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:20
‘You even took the sons and daughters you bore to Me. You sacrificed them as food for idols. Was your prostitution not enough? 📖

## Ezekiel 16:21
You killed My children. You gave them to idols through the fire. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:22
Through all your detestable acts and prostitution, you forgot your youth. You had been naked and bare, struggling in your own blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:23
Woe! Woe to you!’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘You added to all your other wickedness. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:24
You built yourself a mound. You made a high shrine in every public square. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:25
You built high shrines at the head of every street. You degraded your beauty. You spread your legs to everyone passing by and increased your promiscuity. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:26
You prostituted yourself with your lustful Egyptian neighbors. You increased your promiscuity to make Me angry. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:27
I reached out My hand against you and reduced your portion. I handed you over to the desires of those who hate you, the Philistines' daughters. They were ashamed of your lewd behavior. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:28
You prostituted yourself with the Assyrians because you were not satisfied. Even then, you were still not satisfied. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:29
You extended your promiscuity to Chaldea, the merchants' land. Even this did not satisfy you! 📖

## Ezekiel 16:30
How weak the will of your heart is!’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘You do all these things, the acts of a shameless prostitute. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:31
You built mounds at the head of every street and high shrines in every public square. But you were not even like a prostitute. You despised payment. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:32
You adulterous wife! You receive strangers instead of your husband! 📖

## Ezekiel 16:33
Men give gifts to all their prostitutes. But you gave gifts to all your lovers. You bribed them to come from everywhere for your illicit favors. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:34
Your prostitution is the opposite of other women's prostitution. No one asked for your favors. You paid instead of receiving payment. You are the opposite! 📖

## Ezekiel 16:35
Therefore, prostitute, hear the LORD's word! 📖

## Ezekiel 16:36
The Lord GOD says, “You poured out your wealth and exposed your nakedness in promiscuity with your lovers and all your detestable idols. You gave them your children's blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:37
Therefore, I will gather all the lovers you enjoyed, everyone you loved and everyone you hated. I will gather them against you from every side. I will expose you before them. They will see you completely naked. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:38
I will sentence you like women who commit adultery or shed blood. I will bring on you the wrath of bloodshed and jealousy. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:39
I will hand you over to your lovers. They will level your mounds and tear down your high shrines. They will strip off your clothes and take your fine jewelry. They will leave you naked and bare. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:40
They will bring a mob against you. The mob will stone you and cut you to pieces with swords. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:41
They will burn your houses and carry out judgment against you before many women. I will end your prostitution. You will never pay your lovers again. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:42
My wrath against you will end. My jealousy will turn away from you. I will become calm and no longer angry. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:43
You forgot your youth and made Me angry with all these things. Therefore, I will bring your deeds back on your own head,” declares the Lord GOD. “Did you not commit this lewdness in addition to all your other detestable acts? 📖

## Ezekiel 16:44
Everyone who uses proverbs will say this about you: ‘Like mother, like daughter.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 16:45
You are your mother's daughter. She despised her husband and children. You are your sisters' sister. They despised their husbands and children. Your mother was a Hittite, and your father was an Amorite. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:46
Your older sister was Samaria. She and her daughters lived north of you. Your younger sister was Sodom. She and her daughters lived south of you. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:47
You followed their ways and practiced their detestable acts. Soon you became even more corrupt than they were. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:48
As surely as I live, your sister Sodom and her daughters never did what you and your daughters have done.” The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:49
“This was your sister Sodom's wrongdoing. She and her daughters were proud, overfed, and complacent. They did not help poor and needy people. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:50
They were proud and committed detestable acts before Me. Therefore, I removed them, as you saw. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:51
Samaria did not commit half as many sins as you. Your detestable acts exceeded theirs. All your detestable acts made your sisters appear righteous. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:52
Bear your disgrace because you have made your sisters appear justified. Your sins were worse than theirs, so they appear more righteous than you. Bear your shame and disgrace. You have made your sisters appear righteous. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:53
But I will restore Sodom and her daughters from captivity. I will also restore Samaria and her daughters. I will restore you with them. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:54
Then you will bear your disgrace. You will be ashamed of everything you did that comforted them. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:55
Your sisters Sodom and Samaria, with their daughters, will return to their former state. You and your daughters will also return to your former state. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:56
Did you not mock your sister Sodom in the days of your pride? 📖

## Ezekiel 16:57
That was before your wickedness was exposed. Now Edom's daughters and everyone around her mock you. The Philistines' daughters and everyone around you despise you. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:58
You will bear the consequences of your lewdness and detestable acts,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:59
The Lord GOD says, “I will treat you according to your deeds. You despised the oath by breaking the covenant. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:60
But I will remember My covenant with you from your youth. I will establish an everlasting covenant with you. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:61
You will remember your ways and be ashamed when you receive your older and younger sisters. I will give them to you as daughters. I will not do this because of My covenant with you. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:62
I will establish My covenant with you. You will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 16:63
When I make atonement for everything you did, you will remember and be ashamed. Your disgrace will keep you from opening your mouth again.” The Lord GOD declares this.’” 📖
