## Ezekiel 36:1
“Son of man, prophesy to Israel's mountains. Tell them to hear the LORD's word. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:2
The Lord GOD says, ‘Your enemy said, “Aha! The ancient heights have become ours!” 📖

## Ezekiel 36:3
Therefore, prophesy the Lord GOD's words. They made you desolate and trampled you on every side. The remaining nations took possession of you. Their talkers slandered you. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:4
Therefore, Israel's mountains must hear the Lord GOD's word. He speaks to the mountains, hills, ravines, valleys, desolate ruins, and abandoned cities. The remaining nations around you plundered and mocked them. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:5
The Lord GOD says, “I spoke in burning zeal against the remaining nations and all Edom. They claimed My land with wholehearted joy and complete contempt. They plundered its pastureland. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:6
Prophesy about Israel's land. Tell the mountains, hills, ravines, and valleys the Lord GOD says, ‘I spoke in burning zeal because you bore the nations' reproach. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:7
The Lord GOD says, “I raised My hand and swore that the nations around you will bear their own reproach. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:8
But Israel's mountains will produce branches and fruit for My people Israel. They will come home soon. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:9
I am on your side. I will turn toward you. You will be cultivated and planted. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:10
I will multiply your people, the whole house of Israel. People will live in the cities. The ruins will be rebuilt. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:11
I will fill you with people and animals. They will multiply and be fruitful. You will be inhabited as before. I will make you more prosperous than before. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:12
I will make My people Israel walk on you. They will possess you as their inheritance. You will no longer take their children from them. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:13
The Lord GOD says, ‘People tell you, “You devour people and take your nation's children.” 📖

## Ezekiel 36:14
Therefore, you will no longer devour people or take your nation's children,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:15
‘I will no longer let the nations' taunts be heard against you. You will not bear the peoples' reproach or cause your nation to stumble.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖

## Ezekiel 36:16
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:17
“Son of man, Israel's people defiled their land with their ways and deeds when they lived there. Their behavior before Me was like a woman's impurity. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:18
I poured out My wrath on them because they shed blood on the land. They also defiled it with idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:19
I scattered them among nations and throughout the lands. I judged them according to their ways and deeds. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:20
They dishonored My holy name wherever they went among the nations. People said, “These are the LORD's people, but they had to leave His land.” 📖

## Ezekiel 36:21
But I cared about My holy name. The house of Israel dishonored it among the nations where they went. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:22
Tell the house of Israel the Lord GOD says, ‘I will act for My holy name, which you dishonored among the nations. House of Israel, I will not act for your sake. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:23
I will show the holiness of My great name, which you dishonored among the nations. I will show My holiness in you before their eyes. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:24
‘I will take you from the nations. I will gather you from all the countries. I will bring you back to your own land. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:25
I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you will be clean. I will cleanse you from all your impurities and idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:26
I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit in you. I will remove your stone heart. I will give you a heart of flesh. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:27
I will put My Spirit in you. I will cause you to follow My statutes and carefully keep My ordinances. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:28
You will live in the land I gave your forefathers. You will be My people. I will be your God. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:29
I will save you from all your uncleanness. I will summon grain and make it plentiful. I will not bring famine on you. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:30
I will make the trees' fruit and the fields' crops plentiful. The nations will no longer reproach you because of famine. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:31
You will remember your evil ways and wicked deeds. You will hate yourselves for your wrongdoing and detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:32
Know that I will not act for your sake,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘House of Israel, be ashamed and disgraced because of your ways! 📖

## Ezekiel 36:33
The Lord GOD says, “When I cleanse you from all your wrongdoing, I will have the cities resettled. I will have the ruins rebuilt. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:34
People will cultivate the desolate land. It will no longer lie desolate before everyone passing through. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:35
They will say, ‘This desolate land has become like Eden's garden. The ruined, desolate, destroyed cities are now fortified and inhabited.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 36:36
The nations remaining around you will know that I, the LORD, rebuilt what was destroyed. I replanted what was desolate. I, the LORD, have spoken. I will do it. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:37
The Lord GOD says, ‘Once again, I will hear the house of Israel's plea and do this for them. I will multiply their people like a flock. 📖

## Ezekiel 36:38
Jerusalem has many sacrificial flocks during its appointed feasts. In the same way, the ruined cities will be filled with flocks of people. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’” 📖
