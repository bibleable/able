## Ezekiel 37:1
The LORD's hand was on me. He brought me out by His Spirit. He placed me in a valley full of bones. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:2
He led me around among them. Many bones covered the valley floor. They were very dry. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:3
He asked, “Son of man, can these bones live?” I answered, “Lord GOD, only You know.” 📖

## Ezekiel 37:4
He told me, “Prophesy about these bones. Tell them, ‘Dry bones, hear the LORD's word! 📖

## Ezekiel 37:5
The Lord GOD tells these bones, “I will put breath in you. You will live. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:6
I will attach tendons and make flesh grow on you. I will cover you with skin. I will put breath in you so you live. Then you will know that I am the LORD.”’” 📖

## Ezekiel 37:7
I prophesied as commanded. As I spoke, I suddenly heard a rattling noise. The bones joined together, bone to bone. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:8
I watched tendons appear, flesh grow, and skin cover them. But they had no breath. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:9
He said, “Son of man, prophesy to the breath. Tell it the Lord GOD says, ‘Breath, come from the four winds. Breathe into those who were killed so they live!’” 📖

## Ezekiel 37:10
I prophesied as He commanded. Breath entered them. They lived and stood on their feet, a vast army. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:11
He said, “Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel. They say, ‘Our bones are dry, and our hope is dead. We are cut off.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 37:12
Prophesy and tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘My people, I will open your graves and bring you out. I will bring you back to Israel's land. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:13
When I open your graves and bring you out, My people, you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:14
I will put My Spirit in you, and you will live. I will settle you in your land. You will know that I, the LORD, have spoken. I will do it,’ declares the LORD.” 📖

## Ezekiel 37:15
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:16
“Son of man, take one stick. Write, ‘Belonging to Judah and the Israelites joined with him.’ Take another stick. Write, ‘Belonging to Joseph, Ephraim's stick, and all the house of Israel joined with him.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 37:17
Join them into one stick. They will become one in your hand. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:18
Your people will ask you to explain what you mean by these sticks. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:19
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘I will take Joseph's stick from Ephraim's hand, with the tribes of Israel joined with him. I will join them with Judah's stick. I will make them one stick. They will become one in My hand.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 37:20
Hold the sticks you wrote on in your hand, where the people can clearly see them. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:21
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘I will take the Israelites from the nations where they went. I will gather them from every side and bring them to their land. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:22
I will make them one nation in the land, on Israel's mountains. One king will rule them all. They will no longer be two nations. They will never again be divided into two kingdoms. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:23
They will no longer defile themselves with idols, detestable images, or rebellions. I will save them from all their unfaithfulness through which they sinned. I will cleanse them. They will be My people, and I will be their God. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:24
My servant David will be their king. They will all have one shepherd. They will follow My ordinances and keep and practice My statutes. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:25
They will live in the land I gave My servant Jacob, where your fathers lived. They, their children, and their grandchildren will live there forever. My servant David will be their prince forever. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:26
I will make an everlasting covenant of peace with them. I will establish and multiply them. I will put My sanctuary among them forever. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:27
My dwelling place will be with them. I will be their God. They will be My people. 📖

## Ezekiel 37:28
My sanctuary will be among them forever. Then the nations will know that I, the LORD, make Israel holy.’” 📖
