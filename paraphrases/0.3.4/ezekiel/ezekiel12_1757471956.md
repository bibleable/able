## Ezekiel 12:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:2
“Son of man, you live in a rebellious house. They have eyes but do not see. They have ears but do not hear. They are a rebellious house. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:3
Son of man, pack your bags for exile. Move from your home to another place in daylight while they watch. Perhaps they will understand, although they are a rebellious house. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:4
Bring out your exile baggage during the day while they watch. In the evening, leave like someone going into exile while they watch. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:5
Dig through the wall while they watch. Carry your belongings out through it. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:6
At dusk, lift your bags onto your shoulder and take them out while they watch. Cover your face so you cannot see the land. I have made you a sign to the house of Israel.” 📖

## Ezekiel 12:7
I did as commanded. I brought out my exile bags during the day. In the evening, I dug through the wall by hand. At dusk, I carried my belongings out on my shoulder while they watched. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:8
In the morning, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:9
“Son of man, has the rebellious house of Israel not asked what you are doing? 📖

## Ezekiel 12:10
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘This burden concerns Jerusalem's prince and the whole house of Israel there.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 12:11
Tell them, ‘I am a sign to you.’ What happened here will happen to them. They will go into exile as captives. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:12
At dusk, the prince among them will lift his bags onto his shoulder and leave. They will dig through the wall to bring him out. He will cover his face so he cannot see the land. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:13
I will spread My net over him. He will be caught in My snare. I will bring him to Babylon, in the Chaldeans' land. He will not see it, but he will die there. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:14
I will scatter all his attendants and troops to every wind. I will draw a sword to chase them. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:15
When I scatter them among nations and countries, they will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:16
I will spare a few from sword, famine, and plague. They can tell of all their detestable acts in the nations where they go. Then they will know that I am the LORD.” 📖

## Ezekiel 12:17
The LORD's word also came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:18
“Son of man, tremble as you eat your bread. Shake with anxiety as you drink your water. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:19
Tell the land's people the Lord GOD's words about those living in Jerusalem and Israel's land. ‘They will eat bread anxiously and drink water in dread. Their land will be stripped of everything because all its inhabitants are violent. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:20
The inhabited cities will be ruined. The land will become desolate. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 12:21
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:22
“Son of man, what is this proverb in Israel's land? You say, ‘Days pass, and every vision fails.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 12:23
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘I will end this proverb. Israel will no longer recite it.’ Tell them, ‘The days are near when every vision will be fulfilled. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:24
There will be no more false visions or flattering divinations in the house of Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:25
I, the LORD, will speak, and My word will be fulfilled without delay. Rebellious house, I will speak a message and fulfill it in your days.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖

## Ezekiel 12:26
The LORD's word also came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 12:27
“Son of man, the house of Israel says, ‘His vision is for many years from now. He prophesies about the distant future.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 12:28
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘None of My words will be delayed any longer. The message I speak will be fulfilled.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖
