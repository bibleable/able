## Ezekiel 25:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:2
“Son of man, face the Ammonites. Prophesy against them. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:3
Tell the Ammonites to hear the Lord GOD's word. The Lord GOD says, ‘You shouted, “Aha!” when My sanctuary was defiled. You did this when Israel's land was ruined and the house of Judah went into exile. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:4
Therefore, I will give you to the people of the East to possess. They will set up camps and pitch tents among you. They will eat your fruit and drink your milk. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:5
I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels. I will make Ammon a resting place for sheep. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 25:6
The Lord GOD says, ‘You clapped your hands and stamped your feet. Your heart was full of contempt as you rejoiced over Israel's land. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:7
Therefore, I will reach out My hand against you. I will give you as plunder to the nations. I will cut you off from the peoples and wipe you out from the countries. I will destroy you. You will know that I am the LORD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 25:8
The Lord GOD says, ‘Moab and Seir said, “The house of Judah is like all the other nations.” 📖

## Ezekiel 25:9
Therefore, I will expose Moab's side, starting with its frontier cities. These are Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim, the land's glory. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:10
I will give it with the Ammonites to the people of the East to possess. The nations will no longer remember the Ammonites. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:11
I will carry out judgments against Moab. They will know that I am the LORD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 25:12
The Lord GOD says, ‘Edom took revenge on the house of Judah. This made Edom terribly guilty. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:13
Therefore, the Lord GOD says, “I will reach out My hand against Edom. I will destroy its people and animals. I will make it a wasteland. From Teman to Dedan, they will die by the sword. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:14
I will use My people Israel to take My revenge on Edom. They will treat Edom according to My anger and wrath. Then they will know My vengeance.” The Lord GOD declares this.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 25:15
The Lord GOD says, ‘The Philistines took revenge with malice in their souls. Their ancient hostility drove them to destroy Judah. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:16
Therefore, the Lord GOD says, “I will reach out My hand against the Philistines. I will cut off the Cherethites. I will destroy the survivors along the coast. 📖

## Ezekiel 25:17
I will take great revenge on them with furious rebukes. When I take My revenge, they will know that I am the LORD.”’” 📖
