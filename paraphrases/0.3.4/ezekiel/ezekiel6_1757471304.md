## Ezekiel 6:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:2
“Son of man, face Israel's mountains. Prophesy against them. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:3
Tell Israel's mountains to hear the Lord GOD's word. The Lord GOD speaks to the mountains, hills, ravines, and valleys. He says, ‘I will bring a sword against you. I will destroy your high places. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:4
Your altars will be demolished. Your incense altars will be smashed. I will throw your dead bodies before your idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:5
I will put the Israelites' corpses before their idols. I will scatter your bones around your altars. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:6
Wherever you live, the cities will be ruined and the high places demolished. Your altars will be ruined and defiled. Your idols will be smashed and destroyed. Your incense altars will be cut down. Your works will be erased. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:7
People will fall dead among you. You will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:8
Yet I will leave survivors. Some of you will escape the sword when you are scattered among the nations and throughout the lands. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:9
Your captive survivors will remember Me among the nations. Their adulterous hearts turned from Me, and their eyes lusted after idols. This grieved Me. They will hate themselves for their evil and all their detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:10
They will know that I am the LORD. My declaration that I would bring this disaster was not empty. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:11
The Lord GOD says, “Clap your hands and stamp your feet. Cry, ‘Alas!’ because of all the house of Israel's wicked, detestable acts. They will die by sword, famine, and plague. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:12
Those far away will die from plague. Those nearby will die by the sword. Those who remain will die from famine. I will pour out My fury on them. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:13
Then you will know that I am the LORD. Their dead will lie among their idols around their altars. They will lie on every high hill and mountaintop, and under every green tree and leafy oak. They offered fragrant incense to all their idols in these places. 📖

## Ezekiel 6:14
I will reach out My hand against them. Wherever they live, I will make the land a desolate wasteland, from the wilderness to Diblah. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”’ 📖
