## Ezekiel 46:1
“The Lord GOD says, ‘The inner court's east gate must remain shut during the six working days. It must open on Sabbaths and New Moons. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:2
The prince must enter from outside through the gateway's portico. He must stand beside the gatepost while priests sacrifice his burnt offerings and peace offerings. He must bow in worship at the threshold and leave. The gate must remain open until evening. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:3
On Sabbaths and New Moons, the land's people must also bow before the LORD at that gateway's entrance. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:4
The prince's burnt offering to the LORD on the Sabbath must be six male lambs and a ram. All must have no defect. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:5
The grain offering with the ram must be one ephah. With the lambs, he must give as much as he can. He must give one hin of oil for each ephah. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:6
On the New Moon, he must offer a young bull without defect, six lambs, and a ram without defect. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:7
He must give a grain offering of one ephah with the bull and one with the ram. With the lambs, he must give as much as he can. He must give one hin of oil for each ephah. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:8
The prince must enter through the gateway's portico. He must leave the same way. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:9
When the land's people worship before the LORD at appointed feasts, those entering the north gate must leave through the south. Those entering the south gate must leave through the north. No one may return through the gate they entered. Each must leave through the opposite gate. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:10
The prince must enter with the people. He must leave when they leave. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:11
At festivals and appointed feasts, give a grain offering of one ephah with a bull and one with a ram. With lambs, give as much as you can. Give one hin of oil for each ephah. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:12
When the prince gives the LORD a voluntary burnt offering or peace offering, open the east gate for him. He must offer it as on the Sabbath. Then he must leave. Close the gate after he leaves. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:13
Provide a lamb aged one year without defect as a daily burnt offering to the LORD. Offer it every morning. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:14
Each morning, provide a grain offering of one sixth of an ephah with it. Add one third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour. This grain offering to the LORD is a permanent statute. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:15
They must provide the lamb, grain offering, and oil every morning as a regular burnt offering.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 46:16
The Lord GOD says, ‘If the prince gives an inheritance gift to a son, it will belong to his descendants. It will become their inherited property. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:17
If he gives part of his inheritance to a servant, it belongs to that servant until the year of freedom. Then it returns to the prince. His inheritance belongs only to his sons. It will be theirs. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:18
The prince must not take the people's inheritance by evicting them. He must give his sons an inheritance from his own property. None of My people must be displaced from their property.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 46:19
The man led me through the entrance beside the gate into the priests' holy chambers facing north. He showed me a place at the far western end. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:20
He said, “Here the priests must boil guilt offerings and sin offerings and bake grain offerings. They must not bring these into the outer court and transfer holiness to the people.” 📖

## Ezekiel 46:21
He brought me to the outer court's four corners. Each corner had a separate court. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:22
These four enclosed courts were each forty cubits long and thirty cubits wide. All four corner areas had the same dimensions. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:23
A row of masonry surrounded the inside of each of the four courts. Ovens were built at the base of the walls on every side. 📖

## Ezekiel 46:24
He said, “These are the kitchens where the temple's servants will cook the people's sacrifices.” 📖
