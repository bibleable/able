## Ezekiel 7:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:2
“Son of man, the Lord GOD says to Israel's land, ‘The end! The end has reached the land's four corners. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:3
Your end has come. I will release My anger against you. I will judge you according to your ways and repay you for all your detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:4
I will not pity you or spare you. I will punish you for your ways and the detestable acts among you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 7:5
The Lord GOD says, ‘Disaster! A disaster unlike any before is coming! 📖

## Ezekiel 7:6
The end has come! The end has come! It has risen against you. It has arrived! 📖

## Ezekiel 7:7
People of the land, doom has come to you. The time has come. The day is near. The mountains have panic instead of joyful shouts. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:8
Very soon, I will pour out My wrath and anger on you. I will judge you according to your ways. I will repay you for all your detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:9
I will not pity you or spare you. I will punish you for your ways and the detestable acts among you. Then you will know that I, the LORD, strike the blow. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:10
The day is here! It has come! Doom has gone out. The rod has budded. Pride has bloomed. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:11
Their violence has grown into a rod to punish their wickedness. None of them will remain. Their multitude, wealth, and everything of value will be gone. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:12
The time has come. The day has arrived. The buyer must not rejoice, and the seller must not mourn. Wrath is on the whole multitude. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:13
The seller will not recover what he sold while both buyer and seller are alive. The vision about the whole multitude will not be canceled. Because of their wrongdoing, none will preserve his life. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:14
They have blown the trumpet and prepared everything. But no one goes to war. My wrath is on the whole multitude. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:15
The sword is outside. Plague and famine are inside. People in the countryside will die by the sword. Famine and plague will consume those in the city. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:16
Survivors will escape to the mountains. They will moan like valley doves, each for his own wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:17
Every hand will become limp. Every knee will turn to water. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:18
They will wear sackcloth. Terror will overwhelm them. Shame will cover every face. Every head will be shaved. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:19
They will throw silver into the streets and regard their gold as unclean. Silver and gold cannot save them on the day of the LORD's wrath. Wealth cannot satisfy their appetites or fill their stomachs. It became the stumbling block that brought their wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:20
They turned His beautiful ornaments into a source of pride. They used them to make vile images and detestable idols. Therefore, I will make these things unclean for them. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:21
I will give these things to foreigners as plunder. I will give them as loot to wicked people on earth. They will defile them. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:22
I will turn My face away from them. They will defile My treasured place. Violent men will enter and defile it. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:23
Forge the chain. The land is full of crimes involving bloodshed. The city is full of violence. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:24
I will bring the most wicked nations to take their houses. I will end the mighty people's pride. Their holy places will be defiled. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:25
Anguish is coming! They will look for peace but find none. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:26
Disaster will follow disaster, and rumor will follow rumor. They will seek a vision from a prophet. But the priests' instruction and elders' counsel will disappear. 📖

## Ezekiel 7:27
The king will mourn. Despair will clothe the prince. The hands of the land's people will tremble. I will treat them according to their conduct and judge them by their own standards. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’” 📖
