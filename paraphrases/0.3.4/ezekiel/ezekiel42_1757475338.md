## Ezekiel 42:1
The man led me north into the outer court. He brought me to the chambers opposite the temple courtyard and outer wall on the north. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:2
The building with the north-facing door was one hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:3
Galleries faced each other on three levels. They stood opposite the inner court's twenty-cubit area and the outer court's pavement. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:4
An inner walkway stood before the chambers. It was ten cubits wide and one hundred cubits long. Their doors faced north. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:5
The upper chambers were smaller because the galleries took more space from the building's lower and middle chambers. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:6
They had three stories and no pillars, unlike the courts. Therefore, the upper chambers were set back farther than the lower and middle floors. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:7
An outer wall before the chambers was fifty cubits long. It ran parallel to the chambers and outer court. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:8
The chambers on the outer court were fifty cubits long. Those facing the temple were one hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:9
The entrance from the outer court was below these chambers on the east. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:10
Chambers stood along the outer court wall on the south side. They adjoined the courtyard and stood opposite the building. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:11
A passage stood before them, like the northern chambers' passage. They matched the northern chambers' length and width. Their exits and dimensions were similar. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:12
A door stood before the walkway, corresponding to the south-facing chambers' doors. The walkway ran parallel to the wall extending eastward. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:13
The man said, “The northern and southern chambers facing the temple courtyard are holy chambers. Priests who approach the LORD will eat the most holy offerings there. They will place the most holy grain offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings there. The place is holy. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:14
Priests entering the holy area must leave their service garments behind before going to the outer court. The garments are holy. They must put on other clothes before approaching the people's areas.” 📖

## Ezekiel 42:15
The man finished measuring the temple area's interior. He led me out through the east gate and measured the surrounding area. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:16
He used the measuring rod to measure the east side. It was five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:17
He measured the north side as five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:18
He measured the south side as five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:19
He went around and measured the west side as five hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 42:20
He measured all four sides. A wall surrounded the area, five hundred cubits long and five hundred cubits wide. It separated the holy from the common. 📖
