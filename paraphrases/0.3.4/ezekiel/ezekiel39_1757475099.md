## Ezekiel 39:1
“Son of man, prophesy against Gog. Announce the Lord GOD's words. ‘Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, I am against you. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:2
I will turn you around and drive you along. I will bring you from the far north. I will send you against Israel's mountains. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:3
I will strike the bow from your left hand. I will knock the arrows from your right hand. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:4
You, all your troops, and the nations with you will fall on Israel's mountains. I will give you as food to every kind of hungry bird and wild animal. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:5
You will fall in the open field. I have spoken,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:6
‘I will send fire on Magog and those living securely in the coastlands. They will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:7
I will make My holy name known among My people Israel. I will no longer let it be dishonored. The nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:8
It is coming and will certainly happen,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘This is the day I spoke about. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:9
People in Israel's cities will go out and light fires. They will burn the weapons: bucklers, shields, bows, arrows, clubs, and spears. They will use them as fuel for seven years. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:10
They will not gather wood from the countryside or cut forest wood. They will use weapons as fuel. They will loot those who looted them and plunder those who plundered them.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:11
‘That day, I will give Gog a burial place in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers, east of the Sea. It will block travelers because Gog and all his hordes will be buried there. It will be called the Valley of Hamon-gog. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:12
The house of Israel will bury them for seven months to cleanse the land. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:13
All the land's people will bury them. This will make them renowned on the day I show My glory,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:14
‘Men will be employed to pass through the land continually. They will cleanse it by burying invaders left on the ground. They will begin their search after seven months. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:15
Anyone traveling through who sees a human bone will put a pillar beside it. It will remain until the gravediggers bury the bone in the Valley of Hamon-gog. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:16
The city will also be named Hamonah. They will cleanse the land this way. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:17
Son of man, the Lord GOD says, “Call every kind of bird and every field animal. Tell them, ‘Gather from every side for the sacrificial feast I prepare for you. It is a great feast on Israel's mountains. You will eat flesh and drink blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:18
You will eat mighty men's flesh and drink the earth's princes' blood. They will be like rams, lambs, goats, and bulls, all Bashan's fattened animals. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:19
At the sacrifice I prepare, you will eat fat until full. You will drink blood until drunk. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:20
At My table, you will eat your fill of horses, riders, mighty men, and every kind of warrior.’” The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:21
I will show My glory among the nations. All nations will see the judgment I carry out and the hand I lay on them. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:22
From that day onward, the house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:23
The nations will know Israel's people went into exile because of their wrongdoing. They were unfaithful to Me. I hid My face and handed them over to their enemies. They all fell by the sword. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:24
I treated them according to their uncleanness and rebellions. I hid My face from them. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:25
The Lord GOD says, ‘Now I will restore Jacob from captivity. I will show compassion to the whole house of Israel. I will be jealous for My holy name. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:26
They will live securely in their land, with no one to frighten them. Then they will forget their disgrace and all their treachery against Me. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:27
I will bring them from the peoples and gather them from their enemies' lands. I will show My holiness in them before many nations. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:28
I will gather them to their land after their exile among the nations. I will leave none behind. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God. 📖

## Ezekiel 39:29
I will no longer hide My face from them. I will pour out My Spirit on the house of Israel.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖
