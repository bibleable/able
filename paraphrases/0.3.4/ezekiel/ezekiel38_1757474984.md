## Ezekiel 38:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:2
“Son of man, face Gog from Magog's land, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal. Prophesy against him. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:3
Announce the Lord GOD's words. ‘Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, I am against you. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:4
I will turn you around and put hooks in your jaws. I will bring you out with your whole army. Your horses and fully armored horsemen will come. A great company will carry shields and bucklers. All will wave their swords. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:5
Persia, Cush, and Put will join them. All will have shields and helmets. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:6
Gomer and all its troops will come. Beth-togarmah from the far north will come with all its troops. Many nations will be with you. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:7
Get ready. Prepare yourself and your whole company gathered around you. You will guard them. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:8
After a long time, you will be summoned. In the latter years, you will enter a land recovered from war. Its people were gathered from many nations to Israel's mountains, which were desolate for a long time. They were brought out from the nations. Now they all live securely. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:9
You, all your troops, and many peoples with you will advance like a thunderstorm. You will be like a cloud covering the land. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:10
The Lord GOD says, “That day, thoughts will rise in your mind. You will make an evil plan. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:11
You will say, ‘I will attack a land of villages without walls. I will attack quiet people living securely. None have walls, bars, or gates. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:12
I will seize spoil and carry off plunder. I will reach against desolate places that are inhabited again. I will attack people gathered from the nations. They have gained livestock and possessions. They live at the land's center.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 38:13
Sheba, Dedan, and Tarshish's merchants with all its villages will ask, ‘Did you come to seize plunder? Did you gather your hordes to carry away loot? Will you take silver, gold, cattle, and goods? Will you seize great spoil?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 38:14
Son of man, prophesy and tell Gog the Lord GOD says, ‘When My people Israel live securely, will you not notice? 📖

## Ezekiel 38:15
You will come from your place in the far north. Many peoples will come with you, all riding horses. You will be a mighty horde, a huge army. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:16
You will advance against My people Israel like a cloud covering the land. Gog, I will bring you against My land in the latter days. The nations will know Me when I show Myself holy in you before their eyes. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:17
The Lord GOD asks, “Are you the one I spoke of long ago through My servants, Israel's prophets? They prophesied for years that I would bring you against them.” 📖

## Ezekiel 38:18
The Lord GOD declares, “On the day Gog attacks Israel's land, My wrath will flare. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:19
In My zeal and fiery rage, I announce a great earthquake in Israel's land that day. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:20
Sea fish, birds of the air, field animals, and every crawling creature will tremble before Me. All mankind on earth will tremble. Mountains will fall, cliffs will collapse, and every wall will fall to the ground. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:21
I will summon a sword against Gog on all My mountains,” declares the Lord GOD. “Every man's sword will turn against his brother. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:22
I will judge him with plague and bloodshed. I will pour heavy rain, hailstones, fire, and sulfur on him. They will fall on his troops and the many nations with him. 📖

## Ezekiel 38:23
I will show My greatness and holiness. I will reveal Myself before many nations. Then they will know that I am the LORD. 📖
