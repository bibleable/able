## Ezekiel 19:1
“Sing a lament for Israel's princes. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:2
Say, ‘What was your mother? She was a lioness among lions. She lay among young lions and raised her cubs. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:3
She raised one cub until he became a young lion. He learned to tear his prey. He devoured people. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:4
The nations heard about him. He was caught in their pit. They led him to Egypt with hooks. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:5
She saw that her waiting was useless and her hope was lost. She took another cub and raised him into a young lion. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:6
He prowled among lions and became a young lion. He learned to tear his prey. He devoured people. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:7
He broke their strongholds and ruined their cities. The land and everything in it shook at his roar. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:8
Nations from the surrounding provinces came against him. They spread their net over him. He was caught in their pit. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:9
They caged him with hooks and brought him to Babylon's king. They made him a captive so his roar was no longer heard on Israel's mountains. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:10
Your mother was like a vine in your vineyard, planted beside water. Plentiful water made it fruitful and full of branches. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:11
Its strong branches were fit for a ruler's scepter. It rose high above the thick branches. Its height and dense leaves made it stand out. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:12
In fury, it was pulled up and thrown down. The east wind dried its fruit. Its strong branches were stripped off and withered. Fire consumed them. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:13
Now it is planted in the wilderness, in dry and thirsty land. 📖

## Ezekiel 19:14
Fire came from its main branch and consumed its fruit. No strong branch remains fit for a ruler's scepter.’ This is a lament. It must be used as a lament.” 📖
