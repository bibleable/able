## Ezekiel 47:1
The man brought me back to the temple entrance. Water flowed east from beneath its threshold, because the temple faced east. It flowed down from under the temple's south side, south of the altar. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:2
He took me out through the north gate. He led me around outside to the outer east gate. Water trickled from the south side. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:3
He went east with a measuring line in his hand. He measured one thousand cubits and led me through water up to my ankles. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:4
He measured another thousand cubits and led me through water up to my knees. He measured another thousand cubits and led me through water up to my waist. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:5
He measured another thousand cubits. Now the river was too deep to cross on foot. The water had risen high enough to swim in. I could not cross it on foot. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:6
He asked, “Son of man, do you see this?” Then he led me back to the riverbank. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:7
When I arrived, I saw many trees on both riverbanks. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:8
He said, “This water flows east and down into the Arabah. It enters the Dead Sea. The sea's water becomes fresh. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:9
Wherever the river flows, living creatures will swarm and fish will be plentiful. The river makes the water fresh. Everything will thrive wherever it flows. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:10
Fishermen will stand on the shore. They will spread nets from En-gedi to En-eglaim. They will catch many kinds of fish, like those in the Great Sea. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:11
But swamps and marshes will not become fresh. They will remain for salt. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:12
Every kind of fruit tree will grow on both riverbanks. Their leaves will not wither, and their fruit will not fail. They will bear fruit each month because sanctuary water flows to them. Their fruit will provide food. Their leaves will provide healing.” 📖

## Ezekiel 47:13
The Lord GOD says, “Divide the land as an inheritance among Israel's twelve tribes according to these boundaries. Joseph must receive two portions. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:14
Divide it equally among them. I raised My hand and swore to give it to your forefathers. It will be your inheritance. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:15
The northern boundary will run from the Great Sea by Hethlon, through Lebo-hamath to Zedad, 📖

## Ezekiel 47:16
Berothah, and Sibraim, on the border between Damascus and Hamath. It will reach Hazer-hatticon, on Hauran's border. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:17
The border will run from the Sea to Hazar-enan along Damascus's northern border. Hamath's territory will be to the north. This will be the northern boundary. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:18
The eastern border will run between Hauran and Damascus. It will follow the Jordan between Gilead and Israel's land, to the Eastern Sea and as far as Tamar. This will be the eastern boundary. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:19
The southern border will run from Tamar to Meribath-kadesh's waters. It will follow the Brook of Egypt to the Great Sea. This will be the southern boundary. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:20
The Great Sea will be the western border, to a point opposite Lebo-hamath. This will be the western boundary. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:21
Divide this land among yourselves according to Israel's tribes. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:22
Allot it as an inheritance for yourselves and foreigners living among you who have children. Treat them as native Israelites. They must receive an inheritance with you among Israel's tribes. 📖

## Ezekiel 47:23
Give each foreigner his inheritance in the tribe where he lives,” declares the Lord GOD. 📖
