## Ezekiel 44:1
The man brought me back to the sanctuary's outer east gate. It was shut. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:2
The LORD told me, “This gate must remain shut. No one may open it or enter through it. The LORD, the God of Israel, entered through it. Therefore, it must remain shut. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:3
Only the prince himself may sit inside the gateway to eat before the LORD. He must enter through the gateway's portico. He must leave the same way.” 📖

## Ezekiel 44:4
The man brought me through the north gate to the temple's front. I saw the LORD's glory filling His temple. I fell facedown. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:5
The LORD said, “Son of man, pay attention. Look carefully and listen closely to everything I say about all the statutes and laws of the LORD's house. Note the temple entrance and all the sanctuary's exits. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:6
Tell the rebellious house of Israel the Lord GOD says, ‘House of Israel, I have had enough of all your detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:7
You added to your detestable acts by bringing foreigners uncircumcised in heart and flesh to occupy My sanctuary. You defiled My temple while offering My food, the fat and blood. You broke My covenant. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:8
You did not take responsibility for My holy things. You appointed others to take responsibility for My sanctuary for you.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 44:9
The Lord GOD says, ‘No foreigner uncircumcised in heart and flesh may enter My sanctuary. This includes foreigners living among the Israelites. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:10
Levites who left Me when Israel strayed, following their idols, will bear the consequences of their wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:11
Yet they will serve in My sanctuary. They will tend the temple gates and serve there. They will kill the people's burnt offerings and other sacrifices. They will stand before the people to serve them. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:12
They served before their idols and became a stumbling block that brought wrongdoing to the house of Israel. Therefore, I raised My hand and swore that they would bear their wrongdoing's consequences.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:13
‘They must not approach Me to serve as priests. They must not approach any of My holy or most holy things. They will bear the shame of their detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:14
Yet I will put them in charge of all the temple's work and everything done there. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:15
The Levitical priests descended from Zadok kept charge of My sanctuary when Israel strayed from Me. They must approach Me to serve before Me. They will stand before Me to offer fat and blood,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:16
‘Only they may enter My sanctuary and approach My table to serve before Me. They will keep My charge. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:17
They must wear linen when entering the inner court's gates. They must wear no wool when serving at those gates or inside the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:18
They must wear linen turbans on their heads and linen undergarments around their waists. They must wear nothing that makes them sweat. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:19
Before going to the people in the outer court, they must remove their service garments. They must leave them in the holy chambers and put on other clothes. They must not transfer holiness to the people through their garments. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:20
They must not shave their heads or let their hair grow long. They must carefully trim their hair. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:21
No priest may drink wine before entering the inner court. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:22
They must marry either a virgin descended from the house of Israel or a priest's widow. They must not marry any other widow or a divorced woman. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:23
They must teach My people the difference between holy and common. They must show them how to distinguish clean from unclean. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:24
They must judge disputes according to My ordinances. They must keep My laws and statutes for all My appointed feasts. They must keep My Sabbaths holy. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:25
A priest must not defile himself by approaching a dead person. He may do so for his father, mother, son, daughter, brother, or unmarried sister. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:26
After he is cleansed, he must count seven days for himself. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:27
When he enters the sanctuary's inner court to serve in the sanctuary, he must present his sin offering. The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:28
‘I am their inheritance. Give them no possession in Israel. I am their possession. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:29
They must eat the grain offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings. Everything in Israel devoted to the LORD belongs to them. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:30
The best of all firstfruits and every contribution from all your offerings belongs to the priests. Give your first batch of dough to the priest. Then a blessing may rest on your homes. 📖

## Ezekiel 44:31
Priests may not eat any bird or animal found dead or torn by wild animals. 📖
