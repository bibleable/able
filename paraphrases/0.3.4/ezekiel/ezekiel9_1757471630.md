## Ezekiel 9:1
I heard Him call loudly, “City executioners, come near. Each must carry a weapon of destruction.” 📖

## Ezekiel 9:2
I saw six men come from the Upper Gate, which faces north. Each carried a weapon for killing. Another man with them wore linen and had a writing kit at his side. They came in and stood beside the bronze altar. 📖

## Ezekiel 9:3
The God of Israel's glory rose from above the cherubim and moved to the temple threshold. He called the man wearing linen, who had the writing kit at his side. 📖

## Ezekiel 9:4
The LORD said, “Go throughout Jerusalem. Mark the foreheads of the men sighing and groaning over all the detestable acts committed there.” 📖

## Ezekiel 9:5
I heard Him tell the others, “Follow him through the city and begin killing. Do not pity or spare anyone! 📖

## Ezekiel 9:6
Kill the old men, young men, maidens, women, and children. But do not approach anyone with the mark. Begin at My sanctuary.” They began with the elders before the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 9:7
He told them, “Defile the temple. Fill the courts with dead bodies. Go!” They went out and began killing throughout the city. 📖

## Ezekiel 9:8
While they killed, I was left alone. I fell facedown and cried, “Lord GOD, will You destroy all Israel's survivors as You pour out wrath on Jerusalem?” 📖

## Ezekiel 9:9
He replied, “The house of Israel and Judah's wrongdoing is extremely great. The land is full of bloodshed, and the city is full of corruption. They say, ‘The LORD has abandoned the land. The LORD does not see.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 9:10
I will not pity them or spare them. I will bring their deeds back on their own heads.” 📖

## Ezekiel 9:11
The man wearing linen, with the writing kit at his side, returned and reported. He said, “I have done what You commanded.” 📖
