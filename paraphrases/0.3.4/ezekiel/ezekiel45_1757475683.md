## Ezekiel 45:1
“When you divide the land by lot as an inheritance, set aside a holy portion for the LORD. It must be 25,000 cubits long and 20,000 cubits wide. The whole tract will be holy. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:2
Within it, set aside a sanctuary area 500 cubits square. Leave 50 cubits of open land around it. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:3
Measure an area 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide from this holy portion. It will contain the sanctuary, the Most Holy Place. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:4
This holy land will be for priests who serve in the sanctuary and approach the LORD to serve before Him. It will have space for their houses. It will also have a holy area for the sanctuary. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:5
The adjoining area, 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide, will belong to the Levites serving in the temple. They will possess it for towns to live in. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:6
Set aside city property beside the holy district, 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long. It will belong to the whole house of Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:7
The prince's land will border both sides of the holy district and city property. It will extend west from the western side and east from the eastern side. It will run lengthwise from the western boundary to the eastern boundary, parallel to a tribal portion. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:8
This land will be his possession in Israel. My princes will no longer oppress My people. They will give the remaining land to the house of Israel according to their tribes. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:9
The Lord GOD says, ‘Enough, Israel's princes! Stop your violence and oppression. Do what is just and right. Stop taking My people's property from them.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:10
Use honest scales, an honest ephah, and an honest bath. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:11
The ephah and bath must measure the same amount. A bath must hold one tenth of a homer. An ephah must also hold one tenth of a homer. The homer will be the standard for both. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:12
One shekel will be twenty gerahs. Twenty shekels plus twenty-five shekels plus fifteen shekels will equal one mina. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:13
Offer this contribution: one sixth of an ephah from each homer of wheat. Also offer one sixth of an ephah from each homer of barley. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:14
Measure oil by the bath. Give one tenth of a bath from each cor. One cor is ten baths, or one homer. Ten baths equal one homer. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:15
Give one sheep from every flock of two hundred in Israel's pastures with plentiful water. These are for grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings to make atonement for the people. The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:16
All the land's people must give this contribution for Israel's prince. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:17
The prince must provide burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings for the feasts, New Moons, and Sabbaths. This includes all the house of Israel's appointed feasts. He must provide sin offerings, grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings to make atonement for the house of Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:18
The Lord GOD says, ‘On the first day of the first month, take a young bull without defect. Purify the sanctuary. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:19
The priest must take some blood from the sin offering. He must put it on the temple doorposts and the altar ledge's four corners. He must also put it on the inner court's gateposts. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:20
Do the same on the seventh day of the month for anyone who strays unintentionally or through ignorance. This will make atonement for the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:21
Observe Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month. It is a seven-day feast. Eat unleavened bread during it. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:22
That day, the prince must provide a bull as a sin offering for himself and all the land's people. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:23
Each day during the feast's seven days, he must provide seven bulls and seven rams without defect as a burnt offering to the LORD. He must also provide a male goat as a sin offering each day. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:24
He must provide one ephah as a grain offering for each bull and each ram. He must provide one hin of olive oil for each ephah of grain. 📖

## Ezekiel 45:25
The feast beginning on the fifteenth day of the seventh month lasts seven days. He must provide the same sin offerings, burnt offerings, grain offerings, and oil then.’ 📖
