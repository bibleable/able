## Ezekiel 18:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:2
“What do you mean by using this proverb about Israel's land? You say, ‘Fathers ate sour grapes, and their children's teeth are set on edge.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 18:3
The Lord GOD declares, ‘As surely as I live, you will no longer use this proverb in Israel.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 18:4
Every soul belongs to Me. Both father and son are Mine. The soul who sins will die. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:5
Suppose a man is righteous and does what is just and right. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:6
He does not eat at the mountain or look to the house of Israel's idols. He does not defile his neighbor's wife. He does not approach a woman during her menstrual period. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:7
He does not oppress anyone. He returns the debtor's pledge. He does not rob. He gives bread to hungry people and clothes to naked people. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:8
He does not practice usury or charge excessive interest. He keeps his hand from wrongdoing. He gives true justice between people. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:9
He follows My statutes and faithfully keeps My ordinances. That man is righteous. He will certainly live. The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:10
Suppose that man has a violent son. The son sheds blood or does any of these things. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:11
His father did none of them. But the son eats at the mountain and defiles his neighbor's wife. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:12
He oppresses poor and needy people. He robs and does not return a pledge. He looks to idols. He commits detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:13
He practices usury and charges excessive interest. Will he live? No! He will certainly die because he committed all these detestable acts. His blood will be on his own head. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:14
Suppose this son has a son. This grandson sees all his father's sins, considers them, and does not act the same way. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:15
He does not eat at the mountain or look to the house of Israel's idols. He does not defile his neighbor's wife. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:16
He does not oppress, keep a pledge, or rob. He gives bread to hungry people. He clothes naked people. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:17
He keeps his hand from harming poor people. He takes no interest or usury. He keeps My ordinances and follows My statutes. He will not die for his father's wrongdoing. He will certainly live. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:18
His father will die for his own wrongdoing. He practiced extortion, robbed his brother, and did wrong among his people. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:19
You may ask, ‘Why should the son not bear his father's wrongdoing?’ The son did what was just and right. He carefully obeyed all My statutes. He will certainly live. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:20
The soul who sins will die. A son will not bear his father's wrongdoing. A father will not bear his son's wrongdoing. The righteous man's righteousness will rest on him. The wicked man's wickedness will rest on him. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:21
Suppose a wicked man turns from all his sins, keeps all My statutes, and does what is just and right. He will certainly live. He will not die. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:22
None of his rebellions will be held against him. He will live because he practiced righteousness. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:23
The Lord GOD asks, ‘Do I take any pleasure in a wicked person's death? Would I not prefer him to leave his ways and live?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 18:24
Suppose a righteous man leaves his righteousness and does wrong, committing the same detestable acts as wicked people. Will he live? None of his righteous acts will be remembered. He will die because of his unfaithfulness and sin. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:25
Yet you say, ‘The Lord's way is unjust.’ House of Israel, listen. Is My way unjust? Are your ways not unjust? 📖

## Ezekiel 18:26
If a righteous man leaves his righteousness and does wrong, he will die for it. He will die because of his wrongdoing. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:27
If a wicked man leaves his wickedness and does what is just and right, he will save his life. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:28
He considered and turned from all his rebellions. Therefore, he will certainly live. He will not die. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:29
Yet the house of Israel says, ‘The Lord's way is unjust.’ House of Israel, are My ways unjust? Are your ways not unjust? 📖

## Ezekiel 18:30
The Lord GOD declares, ‘House of Israel, I will judge each of you according to his ways. Repent and turn from all your rebellions. Then wrongdoing will not cause your downfall. 📖

## Ezekiel 18:31
Throw away all your rebellions. Make yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. House of Israel, why should you die? 📖

## Ezekiel 18:32
I take no pleasure in anyone's death,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘Repent and live!’” 📖
