## Ezekiel 27:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:2
“Son of man, sing a lament for Tyre. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:3
Tell Tyre the Lord GOD's words. Tyre lives at the sea's gateway and trades with peoples on many coasts. ‘Tyre, you said, “My beauty is perfect.” 📖

## Ezekiel 27:4
Your borders are in the seas' heart. Your builders made your beauty perfect. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:5
They made all your planks from Senir's cypress. They used a cedar from Lebanon for your mast. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:6
They made your oars from Bashan's oaks. They made your deck from wood from Cyprus's coasts. They set ivory into it. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:7
They made your sail from Egypt's embroidered fine linen. It served as your banner. Your awning was blue and purple material from Elishah's coasts. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:8
Men from Sidon and Arvad rowed your oars. Tyre, your skilled men were your captains. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:9
Gebal's elders were aboard as shipwrights, repairing leaks. All the sea's ships and their sailors came alongside to trade for your merchandise. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:10
Men from Persia, Lydia, and Put served as warriors in your army. They hung shields and helmets on your walls. They gave you splendor. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:11
Men from Arvad and Helech guarded your walls all around. Men from Gammad were in your towers. They hung shields around your walls. They made your beauty perfect. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:12
Tarshish traded with you because of your great wealth of goods. They exchanged silver, iron, tin, and lead for your wares. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:13
Javan, Tubal, and Meshech traded with you. They exchanged slaves and bronze utensils for your merchandise. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:14
Men from Beth-togarmah exchanged horses, war horses, and mules for your wares. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:15
Men from Dedan were your clients. Many coastlands were your market. They paid you with ivory tusks and ebony. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:16
Aram bought from you because of your many products. They exchanged turquoise, purple material, embroidery, fine linen, coral, and rubies for your wares. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:17
Judah and Israel's land traded with you. They exchanged Minnith's wheat, cakes, honey, oil, and balm for your merchandise. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:18
Damascus traded with you because of your many products and great wealth of goods. They offered wine from Helbon and wool from Zahar. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:19
They exchanged casks of wine from Izal for your wares. Wrought iron, cassia, and sweet cane were exchanged for your merchandise. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:20
Dedan traded riding saddlecloths with you. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:21
Arabia and all Kedar's princes were your customers. They traded lambs, rams, and goats. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:22
Sheba's and Raamah's merchants traded with you. They exchanged gold, the finest spices, and precious stones for your wares. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:23
Haran, Canneh, and Eden traded with you. So did merchants from Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:24
They traded fine blue garments, embroidery, and multicolored rugs in your marketplace. The rugs had tightly twisted and knotted cords. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:25
Tarshish's ships carried your merchandise. Heavy cargo filled you in the sea's heart. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:26
Your oarsmen brought you onto the high seas. But the east wind will break you in the sea's heart. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:27
On the day you fall, your wealth, wares, and merchandise will sink into the sea's heart. Your sailors, captains, shipwrights, merchants, and all your warriors will sink too. So will everyone else aboard. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:28
The countryside will shake when your sailors cry out. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:29
Everyone who handles oars will abandon their ships. Sailors and all the sea's captains will stand on the shore. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:30
They will raise their voices and cry bitterly for you. They will throw dust on their heads and roll in ashes. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:31
They will shave their heads and wear sackcloth for you. They will weep over you with anguish in their souls and bitter mourning. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:32
They will wail, mourn, and sing a lament for you. ‘Who was ever like Tyre, silenced in the sea's middle? 📖

## Ezekiel 27:33
Your wares went out to sea and satisfied many nations. Your abundant wealth and merchandise enriched the earth's kings. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:34
Now the seas have broken you in the deep waters. Your merchandise and people have sunk with you. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:35
All the coastlands' people are horrified about you. Their kings shake with fear. Their faces twist. 📖

## Ezekiel 27:36
Those trading among the nations hiss at you. You have met a horrible end. You will be no more.’” 📖
