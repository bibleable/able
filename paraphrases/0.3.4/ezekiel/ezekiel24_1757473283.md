## Ezekiel 24:1
In the ninth year, on the tenth day of the tenth month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:2
“Son of man, record today's date. On this very day, Babylon's king has laid siege to Jerusalem. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:3
Tell this rebellious house a parable. The Lord GOD says, ‘Put the pot on the fire. Put it on and pour in water. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:4
Put in pieces of meat, every good piece of thigh and shoulder. Fill it with the best bones. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:5
Take the best of the flock. Pile fuel beneath the pot. Bring it to a boil and cook the bones inside.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 24:6
The Lord GOD says, ‘Woe to the city of bloodshed! Woe to the rusty pot whose rust will not come off! Empty it piece by piece. Do not cast lots for its contents. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:7
The blood she shed remains inside her. She poured it onto bare rock. She did not pour it on the ground to cover it with dust. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:8
I placed her blood on bare rock so it would remain uncovered. I did this to stir wrath and take vengeance.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 24:9
The Lord GOD says, ‘Woe to the city of bloodshed! I will also pile the kindling high. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:10
Pile the logs and light the fire. Cook the meat well and add spices. Let the bones burn. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:11
Put the empty pot on its coals until it becomes hot and its copper glows. Its impurity will melt inside. Its rust will be consumed. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:12
It has defeated every effort. Even fire has not removed its thick rust. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:13
I tried to cleanse you because of your indecent uncleanness. But you would not be purified from your filth. You will not be pure again until My wrath against you ends. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:14
I, the LORD, have spoken. The time is coming, and I will act. I will not hold back, show pity, or change My decision. I will judge you according to your ways and deeds.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖

## Ezekiel 24:15
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:16
“Son of man, I will take away the desire of your eyes with a fatal blow. You must not mourn, weep, or let tears flow. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:17
Groan quietly. Do not mourn for the dead. Put on your turban and fasten your sandals. Do not cover your lips or eat mourners' bread.” 📖

## Ezekiel 24:18
I spoke to the people in the morning. My wife died that evening. The next morning, I did as commanded. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:19
The people asked, “Will you not tell us what your actions mean for us?” 📖

## Ezekiel 24:20
I answered, “The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:21
He told me to tell the house of Israel the Lord GOD says, ‘I will defile My sanctuary. It is the pride of your power, the desire of your eyes, and your soul's delight. The sons and daughters you left behind will die by the sword.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 24:22
You will do as I have done. You will not cover your lips or eat mourners' bread. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:23
Your turbans will stay on your heads, and your sandals will stay on your feet. You will not mourn or weep. You will waste away because of your sins. You will groan among yourselves. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:24
‘Ezekiel will be a sign to you. You will do everything he has done. When this happens, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 24:25
Son of man, I will take away their stronghold, pride, and joy. It is the desire of their eyes that lifted their souls. I will also take their sons and daughters. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:26
On that day, a fugitive will come and tell you the news. 📖

## Ezekiel 24:27
That day, your mouth will open to the escaped man. You will speak and no longer be mute. You will be a sign to them. They will know that I am the LORD.” 📖
