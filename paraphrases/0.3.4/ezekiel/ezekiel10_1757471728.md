## Ezekiel 10:1
I looked above the expanse over the cherubim's heads. I saw something like a sapphire throne. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:2
The LORD told the man wearing linen, “Go inside the wheelwork beneath the cherubim. Fill your hands with burning coals from among the cherubim. Scatter the coals over the city.” He went in as I watched. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:3
When he entered, the cherubim stood on the temple's south side. A cloud filled the inner court. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:4
The LORD's glory rose from above the cherubim and stood over the temple threshold. The cloud filled the temple. The brightness of the LORD's glory filled the court. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:5
The cherubim's wings could be heard as far as the outer court. They sounded like God Almighty's voice when He speaks. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:6
The LORD told the man wearing linen to take fire from the wheelwork, from among the cherubim. The man went in and stood beside a wheel. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:7
One cherub reached out his hand and took fire from among the cherubim. He put it into the hands of the man wearing linen. The man received it and went out. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:8
The cherubim appeared to have human hands under their wings. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:9
I saw four wheels beside the cherubim, one beside each cherub. The wheels shone like beryl. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:10
All four looked alike. Each looked like a wheel inside another wheel. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:11
They moved in any of the four directions without turning. The cherubim went wherever the head faced. They did not turn as they moved. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:12
Eyes covered their whole bodies, including their backs, hands, and wings. Eyes also covered their four wheels, all the way around. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:13
I heard the wheels called “the whirling wheels.” 📖

## Ezekiel 10:14
Each cherub had four faces. The first was a cherub's face. The second was a man's face. The third was a lion's face. The fourth was an eagle's face. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:15
The cherubim rose upward. They were the living creatures I had seen beside the River Kebar. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:16
When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved beside them. When they spread their wings to rise, the wheels did not move away from their sides. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:17
When the cherubim stood still, the wheels stood still. When they rose, the wheels rose with them. The living creatures' spirit was in the wheels. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:18
The LORD's glory left the temple threshold. It stood above the cherubim. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:19
As I watched, the cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the ground. The wheels stayed beside them as they moved. They stopped at the east gate entrance of the LORD's house. The God of Israel's glory was above them. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:20
These were the living creatures I had seen beneath the God of Israel beside the River Kebar. I knew they were cherubim. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:21
Each had four faces and four wings. Under their wings were what looked like human hands. 📖

## Ezekiel 10:22
Their faces looked like those I had seen beside the River Kebar. Each creature went straight ahead. 📖
