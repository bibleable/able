## Ezekiel 28:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:2
“Son of man, tell Tyre's ruler the Lord GOD says, ‘Your heart is proud. You say, “I am a god. I sit in the gods' seat in the sea's heart.” But you are a man, not a god. Yet you regard your heart as a god's heart. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:3
You are wiser than Daniel. No secret is hidden from you! 📖

## Ezekiel 28:4
Your wisdom and understanding gained your wealth. You gathered gold and silver for your treasuries. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:5
Your great trading skill increased your wealth. But your wealth made your heart proud. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:6
Therefore, the Lord GOD says, “You regard your heart as a god's heart. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:7
Therefore, I will bring foreigners against you, the most ruthless nations. They will draw swords against your wisdom's beauty. They will defile your splendor. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:8
They will bring you down to the Pit. You will die violently in the seas' heart. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:9
Will you still say you are a god before those who kill you? You will be a man, not a god, in the hands of those who wound you. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:10
Foreigners will make you die the death of the uncircumcised. I have spoken,” declares the Lord GOD.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 28:11
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:12
“Son of man, sing a lament for Tyre's king. Tell him the Lord GOD says, ‘You were the seal of perfection. You were full of wisdom and perfect in beauty. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:13
You were in Eden, God's garden. Every kind of precious stone decorated you: ruby, topaz, diamond, beryl, onyx, jasper, sapphire, turquoise, and emerald. Your mountings and settings were made from gold. They were prepared on the day you were created. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:14
You were anointed as a guardian cherub because I ordained you. You were on God's holy mountain. You walked among fiery stones. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:15
Your ways were blameless from the day you were created. Then wickedness was found in you. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:16
Your vast trade filled you with violence, and you sinned. I drove you in disgrace from God's mountain. Guardian cherub, I banished you from among the fiery stones. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:17
Your beauty made your heart proud. Your splendor made you corrupt your wisdom. I threw you to the earth. I made you a spectacle before kings. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:18
Your many wrongdoings and dishonest trade defiled your sanctuaries. I made fire come from inside you, and it consumed you. I reduced you to ashes on the ground before everyone watching. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:19
All the nations that know you are horrified about you. You have met a horrible end. You will be no more.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 28:20
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:21
“Son of man, face Sidon. Prophesy against her. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:22
Announce the Lord GOD's words. ‘Sidon, I am against you. I will be glorified within you. I will judge her and show My holiness through her. Then they will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:23
I will send a plague against her and shed blood in her streets. People will fall dead within her as the sword attacks her from every side. Then they will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:24
Israel's people will no longer face a pricking brier or painful thorn from surrounding people who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 28:25
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they were scattered. I will show Myself holy among them before the nations. They will live in their own land, which I gave My servant Jacob. 📖

## Ezekiel 28:26
They will live securely there, build houses, and plant vineyards. They will live securely when I judge everyone around them who treats them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.’” 📖
