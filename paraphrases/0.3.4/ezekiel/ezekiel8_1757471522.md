## Ezekiel 8:1
In the sixth year, on the fifth day of the sixth month, I sat in my house. Judah's elders sat before me. The Lord GOD's hand came on me there. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:2
I saw a figure that looked like a man. Below His waist, He looked like fire. Above His waist, He shone like amber. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:3
He reached out what looked like a hand and took hold of the hair on my head. The Spirit lifted me between earth and heaven. He carried me in visions of God to Jerusalem's inner court, to the north gate entrance. The idol that provokes jealousy sat there. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:4
I saw the God of Israel's glory there. It was like the vision I had seen on the plain. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:5
He said, “Son of man, look north.” I looked north. In the entrance north of the Altar Gate, I saw this idol of jealousy. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:6
He asked, “Son of man, do you see what they are doing? The house of Israel's great detestable acts drive Me far from My sanctuary. Yet you will see even greater detestable acts.” 📖

## Ezekiel 8:7
He brought me to the court entrance. I saw a hole in the wall. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:8
He told me, “Son of man, dig through the wall.” I dug through it and found a doorway. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:9
He said, “Go inside. See the wicked, detestable acts they commit here.” 📖

## Ezekiel 8:10
I went in and looked. Every kind of crawling creature and detestable beast was engraved around the wall. All the house of Israel's idols were there too. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:11
Seventy elders of the house of Israel stood before them. Jaazaniah son of Shaphan stood among the elders. Each held a censer. A fragrant cloud of incense rose. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:12
He asked, “Son of man, do you see what Israel's elders do in darkness, each at his own idol's shrine? They say, ‘The LORD does not see us. The LORD has abandoned the land.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 8:13
He told me again, “You will see them commit even greater detestable acts.” 📖

## Ezekiel 8:14
He brought me to the north gate entrance of the LORD's house. Women sat there, weeping for Tammuz. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:15
He asked, “Son of man, do you see this? You will see even greater detestable acts.” 📖

## Ezekiel 8:16
He brought me into the inner court of the LORD's house. About twenty-five men stood at the LORD's temple entrance, between the portico and altar. Their backs faced the temple, and their faces turned east. They bowed eastward to worship the sun. 📖

## Ezekiel 8:17
He asked, “Son of man, do you see this? Are the house of Judah's detestable acts here not enough? Must they also fill the land with violence and keep making Me angry? They even put the branch to their nose! 📖

## Ezekiel 8:18
Therefore, I will respond with wrath. I will not pity them or spare them. Even if they shout loudly in My ears, I will not listen.” 📖
