## Ezekiel 14:1
Some of Israel's elders came and sat before me. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:2
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:3
“Son of man, these men have set up idols in their hearts. They have put wicked stumbling blocks before their faces. Should I consult with them at all? 📖

## Ezekiel 14:4
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘Suppose an Israelite sets up idols in his heart and a wicked stumbling block before his face. Then he comes to a prophet. I, the LORD, will answer him according to his great idolatry. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:5
I will do this to take hold of Israel's people's hearts. Their idols have separated all of them from Me.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 14:6
Tell the house of Israel the Lord GOD says, ‘Repent and turn from your idols. Turn your faces away from all your detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:7
Suppose an Israelite or foreigner living in Israel separates himself from Me. He sets up idols in his heart and a wicked stumbling block before his face. Then he comes to a prophet to inquire of Me. I, the LORD, will answer him Myself. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:8
I will set My face against that man. I will make him a sign and proverb. I will cut him off from My people. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:9
If a prophet is enticed to speak a message, I, the LORD, enticed him. I will reach out My hand against him. I will destroy him from among My people Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:10
They will bear their punishment. The person who inquires and the prophet will receive the same punishment. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:11
This will keep the house of Israel from leaving Me or defiling themselves with all their rebellions. They will be My people. I will be their God.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖

## Ezekiel 14:12
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:13
“Son of man, suppose a land sins against Me by being unfaithful. Suppose I reach out My hand, cut off its food supply, and send famine to destroy its people and animals. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:14
Even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were there, these three men's righteousness could save only themselves. The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:15
Suppose I send wild animals through the land, leaving it childless and desolate. No one passes through because they fear the animals. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:16
The Lord GOD declares, ‘As surely as I live, even if those three men were there, they could not save their sons or daughters. Only they would be saved. The land would be desolate.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 14:17
Suppose I bring a sword against the land and command it to pass through, destroying its people and animals. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:18
The Lord GOD declares, ‘As surely as I live, even if those three men were there, they could not save their sons or daughters. Only they would be saved.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 14:19
Suppose I send a plague into the land. Suppose I pour out My wrath through bloodshed, destroying its people and animals. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:20
The Lord GOD declares, ‘As surely as I live, even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were there, they could not save their sons or daughters. Their righteousness could save only themselves.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 14:21
The Lord GOD says, ‘How much worse will it be when I send My four terrible judgments against Jerusalem? I will send sword, famine, wild animals, and plague to destroy its people and animals. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:22
Yet some survivors will remain, sons and daughters who will be brought out. They will come to you. Their conduct and actions will comfort you about all the disaster I brought on Jerusalem. 📖

## Ezekiel 14:23
Seeing their conduct and actions will console you. You will know I had cause to do all these things in Jerusalem.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖
