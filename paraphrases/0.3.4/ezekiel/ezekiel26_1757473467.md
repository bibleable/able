## Ezekiel 26:1
In the eleventh month of the twelfth year, on the first day of the month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:2
“Son of man, Tyre has said about Jerusalem, ‘Aha! The gate to the nations is broken. It has opened to me. Now she is ruined, and I will be filled.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 26:3
Therefore, the Lord GOD says, ‘Tyre, I am against you. I will bring many nations against you, like waves rising from the sea. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:4
They will destroy Tyre's walls and demolish her towers. I will scrape off her soil. I will make her a bare rock. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:5
She will become a place to spread nets in the sea. I have spoken,’ declares the Lord GOD. ‘The nations will plunder her. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:6
People in her mainland villages will be killed by the sword. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 26:7
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will bring Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon's king and king of kings, from the north against Tyre. He will come with horses, chariots, cavalry, and many troops. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:8
He will kill the people in your mainland villages with the sword. He will build siege works and a ramp to your walls. He will raise shields against you. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:9
He will strike your walls with battering rams. He will tear down your towers with axes. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:10
Dust from his many horses will cover you. He will enter your gates like an army entering a city with broken walls. The noise of cavalry, wagons, and chariots will shake your walls. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:11
His horses' hooves will trample all your streets. He will kill your people with the sword. Your mighty pillars will fall to the ground. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:12
They will plunder your wealth and merchandise. They will demolish your walls and tear down your beautiful houses. They will throw your stones, timber, and soil into the water. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:13
I will silence your songs. Your lyres' music will no longer be heard. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:14
I will make you a bare rock, a place to spread fishing nets. You will never be rebuilt. I, the LORD, have spoken.’ The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:15
The Lord GOD asks Tyre, ‘Will the coastlands not shake at the sound of your fall? Wounded people will groan during the slaughter among you. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:16
All the sea's princes will leave their thrones. They will remove their robes and embroidered garments. Terror will clothe them. They will sit on the ground, trembling every moment and horrified about you. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:17
They will lament, “How you have perished, famous city where seafaring people lived! You and your people were powerful on the sea. You brought terror on all peoples. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:18
The coastlands tremble now on the day you fall. The sea's islands are dismayed at your destruction.”’ 📖

## Ezekiel 26:19
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will make you desolate like other abandoned cities. I will raise the deep against you, and mighty waters will cover you. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:20
Then I will bring you down with those descending to the Pit, to ancient people. I will make you live in the earth below, like ancient ruins. You will be with those descending to the Pit. You will no longer be inhabited or set in splendor in the land of the living. 📖

## Ezekiel 26:21
I will make you a horror, and you will be gone. People will search for you but never find you.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖
