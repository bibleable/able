## Ezekiel 32:1
In the twelfth year, on the first day of the twelfth month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:2
“Son of man, sing a lament for Pharaoh, Egypt's king. Tell him, ‘You are like a lion among nations and a monster in the seas. You thrash in your rivers. Your feet stir the water and muddy the streams.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 32:3
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will spread My net over you with many peoples. They will pull you up in My net. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:4
I will abandon you on the land and throw you into the open field. I will make all the birds of the air settle on you. All the earth's animals will eat their fill of you. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:5
I will put your flesh on the mountains. I will fill the valleys with your remains. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:6
I will soak the land with your flowing blood, all the way to the mountains. The ravines will be filled. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:7
When I put out your light, I will cover the heavens and darken their stars. I will cover the sun with a cloud. The moon will not give light. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:8
I will darken all heaven's shining lights over you. I will bring darkness on your land,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:9
‘I will bring your destruction among the nations, to countries you do not know. This will trouble many peoples' hearts. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:10
I will make many peoples horrified about you. When I wave My sword before their kings, they will shake in horror because of you. On the day you fall, each will tremble every moment for his life.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 32:11
The Lord GOD says, ‘The sword of Babylon's king will come against you! 📖

## Ezekiel 32:12
I will make your hordes fall by mighty warriors' swords. These are the most ruthless nations. They will ravage Egypt's pride. All her multitudes will be destroyed. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:13
I will kill all her cattle beside the plentiful waters. No human foot will muddy the waters again. No cattle hooves will disturb them. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:14
Then I will let her waters settle. I will make her rivers flow like oil,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:15
‘I will make Egypt's land desolate and empty it of everything that filled it. I will strike down all its inhabitants. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 32:16
The nations' daughters will chant this lament for her. They will chant it over Egypt and all her multitudes. The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖

## Ezekiel 32:17
In the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:18
“Son of man, wail for Egypt's multitudes. Send her and the mighty nations' daughters to the earth's depths with those descending to the Pit. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:19
Whom do you surpass in beauty? Go down and lie with the uncircumcised! 📖

## Ezekiel 32:20
They will fall among those killed by the sword. The sword is appointed! Let them drag her away with all her multitudes. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:21
Mighty chiefs in Sheol will speak about Egypt and her allies. ‘They have come down. They lie with the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 32:22
Assyria is there with her whole company. Her graves surround her. They were all killed by the sword. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:23
Her graves lie in the Pit's depths. Her company surrounds her grave. They were all killed by the sword. They once spread terror in the land of the living. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:24
Elam is there with all her multitudes around her grave. They were all killed by the sword. They went down uncircumcised to the earth below. They once spread terror in the land of the living. They bear their disgrace with those descending to the Pit. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:25
They prepare a resting place for Elam and all her hordes among the dead. Her graves surround her. They are all uncircumcised, killed by the sword. They once spread terror in the land of the living. They bear their disgrace with those descending to the Pit. They are placed among the dead. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:26
Meshech and Tubal are there with all their multitudes and surrounding graves. They are all uncircumcised, killed by the sword. They spread terror in the land of the living. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:27
They do not lie with the fallen warriors of old who went to Sheol with their weapons. Those warriors' swords were placed under their heads. Their shields rested on their bones. The mighty once spread terror in the land of the living. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:28
You will also be shattered. You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:29
Edom is there with all her kings and princes. Despite their might, they lie among those killed by the sword. They lie with the uncircumcised and those descending to the Pit. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:30
All the northern leaders and Sidonians are there. Despite their terrifying might, they went down in disgrace with the dead. They lie uncircumcised with those killed by the sword. They bear their shame with those descending to the Pit. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:31
Pharaoh will see them and be comforted about all his multitude. Pharaoh and his whole army were killed by the sword. The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 32:32
I will spread My terror in the land of the living. Pharaoh and all his multitude will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword. The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖
