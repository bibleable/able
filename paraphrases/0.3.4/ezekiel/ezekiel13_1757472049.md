## Ezekiel 13:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:2
“Son of man, prophesy against Israel's prophets who are prophesying now. Tell those speaking from their own imagination to hear the LORD's word. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:3
The Lord GOD says, ‘Woe to foolish prophets! They follow their own spirit but have seen nothing. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:4
Israel, your prophets are like foxes among ruins. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:5
You did not go to the gaps or repair the wall around the house of Israel. You did not prepare it to stand in battle on the Day of the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:6
They see false visions and speak lying divinations. They say, “The LORD declares this,” although the LORD did not send them. Yet they wait for their message to be fulfilled. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:7
Have you not seen a false vision and spoken a lying divination? You announce, “The LORD declares this,” although I have not spoken. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:8
The Lord GOD says, “I am against you because you spoke empty words and saw false visions.” The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:9
My hand will oppose prophets who see false visions and speak lying divinations. They will not belong to My people's council. Their names will not be recorded in the house of Israel's register. They will not enter Israel's land. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:10
They misled My people by saying, “Peace,” when there was no peace. They whitewash every flimsy wall people build. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:11
Therefore, tell those whitewashing the wall that it will fall. Heavy rain will come. I will send hailstones down. A windstorm will break out. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:12
When the wall falls, you will not be asked, “Where is the whitewash you put on it?” 📖

## Ezekiel 13:13
The Lord GOD says, “In My wrath, I will release a windstorm. In My anger, heavy rain and hail will fall with destructive fury. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:14
I will tear down your whitewashed wall to the ground and expose its foundation. The city will fall. You will be destroyed inside it. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:15
I will pour out My wrath on the wall and those who whitewashed it. Then I will tell you the wall and its whitewashers are gone. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:16
These were Israel's prophets who prophesied to Jerusalem. They saw a vision of peace for her, although there was no peace.” The Lord GOD declares this.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 13:17
Son of man, face your people's daughters who prophesy from their own imagination. Prophesy against them. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:18
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘Woe to women who sew magic charms on their wrists! They make head veils for people of every height to trap their souls. Will you trap My people's souls but preserve your own? 📖

## Ezekiel 13:19
You dishonored Me among My people for handfuls of barley and scraps of bread. You lied to My people who listened. You killed those who should not have died and spared those who should not have lived. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:20
The Lord GOD says, “I oppose the magic charms you use to trap souls like birds. I will tear them from your arms. I will free the souls you trapped like birds. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:21
I will tear off your veils. I will rescue My people from your hands so they will no longer be your prey. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:22
Your lies discouraged righteous people, although I gave them no grief. You encouraged wicked people not to leave their evil ways and save their lives. 📖

## Ezekiel 13:23
Therefore, you will no longer see false visions or practice divination. I will rescue My people from your hands. Then you will know that I am the LORD.”’” 📖
