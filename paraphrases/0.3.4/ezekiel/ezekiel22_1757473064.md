## Ezekiel 22:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:2
“Son of man, will you judge her? Will you judge the city of bloodshed? Confront her with all her detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:3
Tell her the Lord GOD says, ‘City, you bring your own doom by shedding blood within your walls. You defile yourself by making idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:4
The blood you shed makes you guilty. The idols you made defile you. You have ended your days and reached the end of your years. Therefore, I have made you a reproach to the nations. All the lands mock you. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:5
Infamous city full of turmoil, those near and far will mock you. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:6
Every prince of Israel in you has used his power to shed blood. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:7
People treat fathers and mothers with contempt. Within your walls, they exploit foreign residents. They oppress fatherless children and widows. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:8
You despised My holy things. You defiled My Sabbaths. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:9
Slanderous men among you are intent on shedding blood. People among you eat at mountain shrines and commit indecent acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:10
They expose their fathers' nakedness within you. They violate women during their menstrual impurity. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:11
One man commits a detestable act with his neighbor's wife. Another wickedly defiles his daughter-in-law. Another violates his sister, his own father's daughter. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:12
People among you take bribes to shed blood. You practice usury, charge excessive interest, and extort your neighbors. But you have forgotten Me,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:13
‘I strike My hands together against your dishonest gains. I oppose the blood you shed among you. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:14
Will your courage last or your hands remain strong when I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken. I will act. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:15
I will scatter you among nations and throughout the lands. I will remove your uncleanness. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:16
When you defile yourself before the nations, you will know that I am the LORD.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 22:17
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:18
“Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to Me. All of them are copper, tin, iron, and lead inside the furnace. They are only the dross of silver. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:19
The Lord GOD says, ‘You have all become dross. Therefore, I will gather you into Jerusalem. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:20
People gather silver, copper, iron, lead, and tin into a furnace. They melt them with a blast of fire. In the same way, I will gather you in My anger and wrath. I will leave you there and melt you. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:21
I will gather you. I will blow the fire of My wrath on you. You will melt inside the city. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:22
You will melt inside the city like silver in a furnace. Then you will know that I, the LORD, poured out My wrath on you.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 22:23
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:24
“Son of man, tell her, ‘On the day of indignation, you are a land that has not been cleansed. No rain has fallen on you.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 22:25
The princes' conspiracy among her is like a roaring lion tearing its prey. They devour people and seize treasures and precious things. They increase the widows among her. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:26
Her priests violate My law and defile My holy things. They do not distinguish holy things from common things. They do not distinguish clean things from unclean things. They disregard My Sabbaths. Thus I am dishonored among them. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:27
Her officials are like wolves tearing their prey. They shed blood and destroy lives for dishonest gain. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:28
Her prophets whitewash these deeds with false visions and lying divinations. They say, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says,’ although the LORD has not spoken. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:29
The land's people practice extortion and robbery. They oppress poor and needy people. They exploit foreign residents without justice. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:30
I searched among them for a man to repair the wall. He would stand in the gap before Me for the land so I would not destroy it. But I found no one. 📖

## Ezekiel 22:31
I poured out My indignation on them. I consumed them with My fury's fire. I brought their ways back on their own heads.” The Lord GOD declares this. 📖
