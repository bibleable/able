## Ezekiel 30:1
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:2
“Son of man, prophesy the Lord GOD's words. ‘Wail, “Alas for that day!” 📖

## Ezekiel 30:3
The day is near. The Day of the LORD is near. It will be a cloudy day, a time of doom for the nations. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:4
A sword will come against Egypt. Cush will suffer anguish when people fall dead in Egypt. Egypt's wealth will be taken, and its foundations will be torn down. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:5
Cush, Put, Lud, and all the various peoples will fall with Egypt by the sword. Libya and the men of the covenant land will also fall. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:6
The LORD says, “Egypt's allies will fall. Her proud strength will collapse. From Migdol to Syene, they will die by the sword within her.” The Lord GOD declares this. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:7
They will be desolate among desolate lands. Their cities will lie among ruined cities. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:8
I will set Egypt on fire, and all her helpers will be broken. Then they will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:9
That day, messengers will leave Me in ships to frighten Cush from its complacency. Anguish will come on them on Egypt's day of doom. It is coming. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:10
The Lord GOD says, “I will use Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon's king, to end Egypt's hordes. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:11
He and his people, the most ruthless nations, will be brought to destroy the land. They will draw swords against Egypt. They will fill the land with dead bodies. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:12
I will dry the streams and sell the land to wicked people. I will use foreigners to ruin the land and everything in it. I, the LORD, have spoken. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:13
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will destroy Memphis's idols and images. Egypt will no longer have a prince. I will put fear in that land. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:14
I will ruin Pathros and set Zoan on fire. I will judge Thebes. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:15
I will pour My wrath on Pelusium, Egypt's stronghold. I will cut off Thebes's crowds. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:16
I will set Egypt on fire. Pelusium will twist in anguish. Thebes will be split open. Memphis will suffer distress daily. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:17
The young men of On and Pi-beseth will die by the sword. Those cities will go into captivity. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:18
Day will become dark in Tahpanhes when I break Egypt's yoke. Her proud strength will end. A cloud will cover her. Her daughters will go into captivity. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:19
I will judge Egypt. They will know that I am the LORD.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 30:20
In the eleventh year, on the seventh day of the first month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:21
“Son of man, I have broken Pharaoh's arm. He is Egypt's king. His arm has not been bandaged to heal or splinted to make it strong enough to hold a sword. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:22
The Lord GOD says, ‘I am against Pharaoh, Egypt's king. I will break both his strong arm and his already broken arm. I will make the sword fall from his hand. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:23
I will scatter the Egyptians among nations and throughout the lands. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:24
I will strengthen Babylon's king's arms and put My sword in his hand. I will break Pharaoh's arms. He will groan before Babylon's king like a man with a fatal wound. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:25
I will strengthen Babylon's king's arms, but Pharaoh's arms will become limp. I will put My sword in Babylon's king's hand. He will use it against Egypt's land. Then they will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 30:26
I will scatter the Egyptians among nations and throughout the lands. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’” 📖
