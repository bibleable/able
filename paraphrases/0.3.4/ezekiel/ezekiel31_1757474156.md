## Ezekiel 31:1
In the eleventh year, on the first day of the third month, the LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:2
“Son of man, tell Pharaoh, Egypt's king, and his multitude, ‘Who can match your greatness? 📖

## Ezekiel 31:3
Look at Assyria, a cedar in Lebanon. Its beautiful branches shaded the forest. It stood tall, with its top among the clouds. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:4
Water made it grow. Deep springs made it tall. Their streams flowed around its base, and their channels reached all the field's trees. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:5
It grew taller than all the field's trees. Its branches multiplied and grew long. Plentiful water helped it spread them. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:6
All the birds of the air nested in its branches. All the field's animals gave birth beneath them. All the great nations lived in its shade. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:7
Its greatness and long limbs were beautiful. Its roots reached plentiful water. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:8
The cedars in God's garden could not rival it. The cypresses could not match its branches. The plane trees could not match its boughs. No tree in God's garden could match its beauty. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:9
I made it beautiful with many branches. All Eden's trees in God's garden envied it.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 31:10
The Lord GOD says, ‘It grew tall and set its top among the clouds. Its height made it proud. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:11
Therefore, I handed it over to the nations' ruler. He would treat it according to its wickedness. I have banished it. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:12
Foreigners, the most ruthless nations, cut it down and left it. Its branches fell on the mountains and in every valley. Its boughs lay broken in all the earth's ravines. All the earth's peoples left its shade and abandoned it. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:13
All the birds of the air nested on its fallen trunk. All the field's animals lived among its boughs. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:14
This happened so no other trees beside water would grow tall and place their tops among the clouds. No other trees with plentiful water would reach that height. They have all been assigned to death, to the earth's depths. They will be among mortals descending to the Pit.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 31:15
The Lord GOD says, ‘I caused mourning on the day it went down to Sheol. I covered the deep because of it. I held back its rivers and plentiful waters. I made Lebanon mourn for it. All the field's trees fainted because of it. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:16
I threw it down to Sheol with those descending to the Pit. The sound of its fall made the nations shake. All Eden's trees were comforted in the earth below. These were Lebanon's best trees, all the trees with plentiful water. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:17
They also went down with it to Sheol, to those killed by the sword. They had been its allies, living in its shade among the nations. 📖

## Ezekiel 31:18
Who among Eden's trees matches your glory and greatness? You will also be brought to the earth's depths with Eden's trees. You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his multitude.’ The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖
