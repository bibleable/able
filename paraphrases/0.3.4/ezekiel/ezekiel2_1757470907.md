## Ezekiel 2:1
He told me, “Son of man, stand on your feet. I will speak to you.” 📖

## Ezekiel 2:2
As He spoke, the Spirit entered me and made me stand on my feet. I heard Him speaking to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:3
He said, “Son of man, I am sending you to the Israelites. They are a rebellious nation that has rebelled against Me. They and their fathers have rebelled against Me until this very day. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:4
They are stubborn children who refuse to change. I am sending you to them. Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 2:5
They are a rebellious house. Whether they listen or refuse, they will know a prophet has been among them. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:6
Son of man, do not fear them or their words. Do not fear, even if briers and thorns surround you and you live among scorpions. Do not fear their words or lose courage in their presence. They are a rebellious house. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:7
Speak My words to them whether they listen or refuse. They are rebellious. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:8
Son of man, listen to what I tell you. Do not rebel like that rebellious house. Open your mouth and eat what I give you.” 📖

## Ezekiel 2:9
I looked and saw a hand reaching toward me. It held a scroll. 📖

## Ezekiel 2:10
He unrolled it before me. Words of lamentation, mourning, and woe were written on its front and back. 📖
