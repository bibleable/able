## Ezekiel 11:1
The Spirit lifted me and brought me to the east gate of the LORD's house. Twenty-five men stood at the entrance. Among them were Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, leaders of the people. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:2
The LORD said, “Son of man, these men plan evil. They give wicked advice in this city. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:3
They say, ‘Is the time to build houses not near? The city is the cooking pot, and we are the meat.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 11:4
Therefore, prophesy against them. Prophesy, son of man!” 📖

## Ezekiel 11:5
The LORD's Spirit came on me. He told me to announce the LORD's words. “House of Israel, that is what you think. I know the thoughts that rise in your minds. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:6
You have killed many people in this city. You have filled its streets with dead bodies. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:7
The Lord GOD says, ‘The people you killed and left in the city are the meat. The city is the pot. But I will remove you from it. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:8
You fear the sword. Therefore, I will bring the sword against you,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:9
I will bring you out of the city and hand you over to foreigners. I will carry out judgments against you. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:10
You will die by the sword. I will judge you even to Israel's borders. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:11
The city will not be your pot. You will not be the meat inside it. I will judge you even to Israel's borders. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:12
Then you will know that I am the LORD. You have not followed My statutes or practiced My ordinances. You have followed the ordinances of the nations around you.” 📖

## Ezekiel 11:13
While I prophesied, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. I fell facedown and cried loudly, “Lord GOD, will You completely destroy Israel's survivors?” 📖

## Ezekiel 11:14
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:15
“Son of man, consider your brothers, relatives, fellow exiles, and the whole house of Israel. Jerusalem's people say about them, ‘They are far from the LORD. This land has been given to us to possess.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 11:16
Announce the Lord GOD's words. ‘I sent them far away among the nations and scattered them among countries. Yet for a little while, I have been their sanctuary in the countries where they went.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 11:17
Announce the Lord GOD's words. ‘I will gather you from the peoples and countries where you were scattered. I will give you back Israel's land.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 11:18
When they return, they will remove all its detestable things and acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:19
I will give them an undivided heart. I will put a new spirit in them. I will remove their stone heart and give them a heart of flesh. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:20
Then they can follow My statutes, keep My ordinances, and practice them. They will be My people. I will be their God. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:21
But I will bring people's conduct back on their own heads if their hearts pursue detestable things and acts. The Lord GOD declares this.” 📖

## Ezekiel 11:22
The cherubim spread their wings, with the wheels beside them. The God of Israel's glory was above them. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:23
The LORD's glory rose from inside the city. It stood over the mountain east of the city. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:24
In the vision given by God's Spirit, the Spirit lifted me and carried me back to the exiles in Chaldea. Then the vision left me. 📖

## Ezekiel 11:25
I told the exiles everything the LORD had shown me. 📖
