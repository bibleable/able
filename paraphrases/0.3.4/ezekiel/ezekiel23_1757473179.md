## Ezekiel 23:1
The LORD's word came to me again. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:2
“Son of man, there were two women. They had the same mother. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:3
They prostituted themselves in Egypt from their youth. Their breasts were fondled there. Their virgin bosoms were caressed. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:4

## Ezekiel 23:5
Oholah prostituted herself while she was Mine. She lusted after her lovers, the Assyrian warriors. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:6
They wore blue. They were governors and commanders, all desirable young men riding horses. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:7
She gave sexual favors to all Assyria's elite. She defiled herself with all the idols of those she lusted after. 📖

They exposed her nakedness and seized her sons and daughters. They killed her with the sword. She became a byword among women. They carried out judgment against her. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:11
Her sister Oholibah saw this. Yet her lust and prostitution became more corrupt than her sister's. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:12
She also lusted after the Assyrians. They were governors, commanders, splendidly dressed warriors, and horsemen. All were desirable young men. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:13
I saw that she had also defiled herself. Both had followed the same path. 📖

Oholibah took her prostitution further. She saw pictures of men on the wall. They were Chaldean images engraved in vermilion. 📖

Seeing them made her lust for them. She sent messengers to them in Chaldea. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:17
The Babylonians came to her bed of love. Their lust defiled her. After they defiled her, she turned from them in disgust. 📖

Oholibah openly prostituted herself and exposed her nakedness. I turned from her in disgust, as I had turned from her sister. 📖

Yet she increased her promiscuity. She remembered her youth, when she prostituted herself in Egypt. 📖

She lusted after their lovers. Their genitals were like donkeys' genitals. Their emission was like stallions' emission. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:23
They include the Babylonians, all the Chaldeans, and men from Pekod, Shoa, and Koa. All the Assyrians will join them. All are desirable young men, governors, commanders, officers, and renowned men riding horses. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:24
They will come against you with many peoples, weapons, chariots, and wagons. They will surround you with bucklers, shields, and helmets. I will give them authority to judge. They will punish you by their own standards. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:25
I will direct My jealous rage against you. They will treat you with fury. They will cut off your noses and ears. Your survivors will die by the sword. They will seize your sons and daughters. Fire will consume those who remain. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:26
They will strip off your clothes. They will take your fine jewelry. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:27
I will end your indecency and prostitution that began in Egypt. You will not look to them or remember Egypt anymore.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 23:28
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will hand you over to those you hate. These are the ones you turned from in disgust. 📖

They will treat you with hatred and take everything you worked for. They will leave you naked and bare. The shame of your prostitution will be exposed. Your indecency and promiscuity 📖

## Ezekiel 23:31

## Ezekiel 23:32
The Lord GOD says, ‘You will drink your sister's deep, wide cup. It holds so much that it will bring scorn and mockery. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:33
You will be filled with drunkenness and grief. It is a cup of devastation and desolation, your sister Samaria's cup. 📖

The Lord GOD says, ‘You forgot Me and threw Me behind your back. Therefore, you must bear the consequences of your indecency and prostitution.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 23:36
The LORD told me, “Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Tell them their detestable acts. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:37
They committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. They committed adultery with their idols. They sacrificed the children they bore to Me in fire as food for their idols. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:38
They also did this to Me. On that same day, they defiled My sanctuary and My Sabbaths. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:39

## Ezekiel 23:40

## Ezekiel 23:41
You sat on a luxurious couch. A table was set before it. You had put My incense and oil on it. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:42
A carefree crowd made noise there. Drunkards from the desert joined men from the rabble. They put bracelets on your wrists and beautiful crowns on your head. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:43
I said about her who had grown old in adultery, ‘Now let them use her as a prostitute. That is all she is!’ 📖

## Ezekiel 23:44
They slept with her as with a prostitute. They slept with Oholah and Oholibah, those lewd women. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:45
Righteous men will sentence them like women who commit adultery and bloodshed. They are adulteresses with blood on their hands. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:46
The Lord GOD says, ‘Bring a mob against them. Give them over to terror and plunder. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:47
The mob will stone them and cut them down with swords. They will kill their sons and daughters. They will burn their houses. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:48
I will end indecency in the land. All the women will be warned not to copy your behavior. 📖

## Ezekiel 23:49
They will repay you for your indecency. You will bear the consequences of your idolatry's sins. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD.’” 📖
