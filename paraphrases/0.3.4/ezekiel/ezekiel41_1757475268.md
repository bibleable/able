## Ezekiel 41:1
The man brought me into the outer sanctuary. He measured the side pillars as six cubits wide on each side. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:2
The entrance was ten cubits wide. Its sides were five cubits on each side. He measured the outer sanctuary as forty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:3
He entered the inner sanctuary. He measured the entrance's side pillars as two cubits wide. The entrance was six cubits wide. The walls on each side were seven cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:4
He measured the room beside the inner sanctuary as twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. He told me, “This is the Most Holy Place.” 📖

## Ezekiel 41:5
He measured the temple wall as six cubits thick. Each side room around the temple was four cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:6
The side rooms were stacked in three levels, with thirty rooms on each level. Ledges around the temple wall supported them. These supports were not fastened into the temple wall itself. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:7
The side rooms became wider on each higher level. The surrounding structure rose in stages as the temple wall narrowed upward. A stairway led from the lowest level through the middle to the highest. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:8
I saw a raised base around the temple. It formed the side rooms' foundation. It measured one full rod, six long cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:9
The side rooms' outer wall was five cubits thick. An open area separated the temple's side rooms 📖

## Ezekiel 41:10
from the outer chambers. It was twenty cubits wide all around the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:11
The side rooms opened into this area. One entrance faced north, and another faced south. The open area was five cubits wide all around. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:12
The building facing the temple courtyard on the west was seventy cubits wide and ninety cubits long. Its wall was five cubits thick all around. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:13
He measured the temple as one hundred cubits long. The temple courtyard and the building with its walls also measured one hundred cubits long. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:14
The temple courtyard's width on the east, including the temple's front, was one hundred cubits. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:15
He measured the building facing the courtyard behind the temple. It was one hundred cubits long, including its galleries on both sides. The outer sanctuary and inner sanctuary stood there, with the porticoes facing the court. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:16
These, with the thresholds, beveled windows, and galleries around all three levels opposite the threshold, had wood covering all sides. Wood panels extended from the ground to the windows. The windows were covered. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:17
Evenly spaced decorations covered the walls around the inner and outer sanctuaries. They were above the outside of the inner sanctuary's entrance. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:18
Carved cherubim and palm trees alternated. Each cherub had two faces. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:19
A human face looked toward the palm tree on one side. A young lion's face looked toward the palm tree on the other. They were carved all around the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:20
Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the outer sanctuary's wall. They extended from the floor to the space above the entrance. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:21
The outer sanctuary's doorframe was rectangular. The sanctuary's doorframe was similar. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:22
A wooden altar was three cubits high and two cubits square. Its corners, base, and sides were wood. The man said, “This is the table before the LORD.” 📖

## Ezekiel 41:23
The outer sanctuary and inner sanctuary both had double doors. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:24
Each door had two swinging panels. One door had two panels, and the other also had two. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:25
Cherubim and palm trees like those on the walls were carved on the outer sanctuary's doors. A wooden canopy stood outside, on the portico's front. 📖

## Ezekiel 41:26
The portico's sidewalls had beveled windows and palm trees. The temple's side rooms also had canopies. 📖
