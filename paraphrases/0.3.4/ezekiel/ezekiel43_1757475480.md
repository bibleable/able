## Ezekiel 43:1
The man brought me back to the east gate. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:2
I saw the God of Israel's glory coming from the east. His voice sounded like roaring waters. The earth shone with His glory. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:3
This vision was like the one I saw when He came to destroy the city. It was like the visions beside the River Kebar. I fell facedown. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:4
The LORD's glory entered the temple through the east gate. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:5
The Spirit lifted me and brought me into the inner court. The LORD's glory filled the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:6
The man stood beside me. I heard someone speaking to me from inside the temple. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:7
He said, “Son of man, this is My throne's place and the place for the soles of My feet. I will live among the Israelites here forever. The house of Israel and their kings will never again defile My holy name. They will not do this through prostitution or funeral offerings for their kings at their deaths. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:8
They put their threshold beside Mine and their doorposts beside Mine. Only a wall separated Me from them. Their detestable acts defiled My holy name. Therefore, I consumed them in My anger. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:9
Let them take their prostitution and funeral offerings for kings far from Me. Then I will live among them forever. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:10
Son of man, describe the temple to Israel's people so they become ashamed of their wrongdoing. Let them measure its plan. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:11
If they are ashamed of everything they did, show them the temple's design, arrangement, exits, and entrances. Show its complete design, statutes, forms, and laws. Write it before their eyes. Then they can keep and carry out its whole design and all its statutes. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:12
This is the temple's law. All its surrounding territory on the mountain's top will be most holy. This is the temple's law. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:13
These are the altar's measurements in long cubits. Each long cubit is one cubit and one handbreadth. Its gutter is one cubit deep and one cubit wide. A rim measuring one span surrounds its edge. Here is the altar's height. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:14
The distance from the gutter on the ground to the lower ledge is two cubits. The ledge is one cubit wide. The distance from the smaller ledge to the larger is four cubits. That ledge is one cubit wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:15
The altar hearth is four cubits high. Four horns extend upward from it. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:16
The hearth is square at its four corners. It is twelve cubits long and twelve cubits wide. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:17
The ledge is square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide. A rim of half a cubit and a gutter of one cubit surround it. The altar's steps face east.” 📖

## Ezekiel 43:18
He told me, “Son of man, the Lord GOD says, ‘These are the altar's statutes when it is built. Burnt offerings can be sacrificed on it, and blood can be splashed on it. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:19
Give a young bull from the herd as a sin offering to the Levitical priests from Zadok's family. They approach Me to serve before Me,’ declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:20
‘Put some bull's blood on the altar's four horns, the ledge's four corners, and all around the rim. This will cleanse the altar and make atonement for it. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:21
Take away the sin offering bull. Burn it in the appointed part of the temple area outside the sanctuary. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:22
On the second day, present a male goat without defect as a sin offering. Cleanse the altar as you did with the bull. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:23
After the purification, present a young bull and a ram from the flock. Both must have no defect. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:24
Present them before the LORD. The priests must sprinkle salt on them. They must sacrifice them as a burnt offering to the LORD. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:25
For seven days, provide a male goat each day as a sin offering. Also provide a young bull and a ram from the flock, both without defect. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:26
For seven days, the priests must make atonement for the altar and cleanse it. They will dedicate it this way. 📖

## Ezekiel 43:27
After these days, from the eighth day onward, the priests must present your burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar. Then I will accept you,’ declares the Lord GOD.” 📖
