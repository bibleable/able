## Ezekiel 5:1
“Son of man, take a sharp sword. Use it like a barber's razor to shave your head and beard. Use scales to divide the hair. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:2
When the siege days end, burn one third of the hair inside the city. Cut one third with the sword around the city. Scatter one third to the wind. I will send a sword after them. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:3
But keep a few hairs in the folds of your garment. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:4
Take a few of these hairs and burn them in the fire. From there, fire will spread to the whole house of Israel. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:5
The Lord GOD says, ‘This is Jerusalem. I placed her in the center of the nations, with countries around her. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:6
She rebelled against My ordinances more wickedly than the nations. She rebelled against My statutes worse than the countries around her. Her people rejected My ordinances and did not follow My statutes.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 5:7
The Lord GOD says, ‘You have been more rebellious than the nations around you. You have not followed My statutes or kept My ordinances. You have not even followed the surrounding nations' ordinances.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 5:8
The Lord GOD says, ‘Jerusalem, I Myself am against you. I will carry out judgments among you where the nations can see. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:9
Because of all your detestable acts, I will do something to you that I have never done before. I will never do it again. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:10
Fathers among you will eat their sons. Sons will eat their fathers. I will carry out judgments against you. I will scatter all your survivors to every wind.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 5:11
The Lord GOD declares, “As surely as I live, I Myself will withdraw My favor. You have defiled My sanctuary with all your detestable idols and acts. That is why I will not pity you or spare you. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:12
One third of your people will die from plague or famine inside the city. One third will die by the sword outside your walls. I will scatter one third to every wind and send a sword after them. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:13
When I finish pouring out My anger and wrath on them, I will be satisfied. Then they will know that I, the LORD, spoke in My zeal. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:14
I will make you a ruin and a disgrace among the surrounding nations. Everyone who passes will see it. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:15
I will judge you with anger, wrath, and raging fury. The surrounding nations will reproach and mock you. You will be a warning and a horror to them. I, the LORD, have spoken. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:16
I will shower you with deadly arrows of famine and destruction to destroy you. I will make the famine worse. I will cut off your food supply. 📖

## Ezekiel 5:17
I will send famine and wild animals against you. They will leave you childless. Plague and bloodshed will sweep through you. I will bring a sword against you. I, the LORD, have spoken.” 📖
