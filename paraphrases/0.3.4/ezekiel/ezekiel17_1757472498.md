## Ezekiel 17:1
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:2
“Son of man, give the house of Israel a riddle. Tell them a parable. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:3
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘A great eagle came to Lebanon. It had great wings, long flight feathers, and feathers of many colors. It took the cedar's top. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:4
It plucked the highest shoot and carried it to the merchants' land. It planted the shoot in a city of traders. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:5
It took some seed from the land and planted it in fertile soil. It placed it beside plentiful water, like a willow. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:6
The seed grew into a low, spreading vine. Its branches turned toward the eagle. Its roots stayed where it stood. The vine produced branches and shoots. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:7
Another great eagle had great wings and many feathers. The vine bent its roots toward this eagle. It stretched its branches toward him from its planting bed so he might water it. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:8
It had been planted in good soil beside plentiful water. It was meant to produce branches and fruit and become a splendid vine.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 17:9
Tell them the Lord GOD says, ‘Will it thrive? Will it not be pulled up and stripped of fruit until it shrivels? All its leaves will wither! Pulling it up by the roots will not need a strong arm or many people. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:10
Even if transplanted, will it thrive? Will it not completely wither when the east wind strikes? It will wither in the bed where it grew.’” 📖

## Ezekiel 17:11
The LORD's word came to me. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:12
“Ask this rebellious house, ‘Do you not know what this means?’ Tell them, ‘Babylon's king came to Jerusalem. He took its king and officials back to Babylon with him. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:13
He made a covenant with a royal family member and put him under oath. He carried away the land's leading men. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:14
This would bring the kingdom low and prevent it from rising. It could survive only by keeping his covenant. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:15
But this king rebelled against Babylon. He sent messengers to Egypt to request horses and a large army. Will he thrive? Will someone who does this escape? Can he break a covenant and escape?’ 📖

## Ezekiel 17:16
The Lord GOD declares, ‘As surely as I live, he will die in Babylon. This is the land of the king who put him on the throne. He despised that king's oath and broke his covenant. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:17
Pharaoh's mighty army and vast horde will not help him in battle. Siege ramps and walls will be built to destroy many lives. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:18
He despised the oath by breaking the covenant. He gave his hand as a pledge, yet did all these things. He will not escape!’ 📖

## Ezekiel 17:19
The Lord GOD says, ‘As surely as I live, I will bring consequences on his head for despising My oath and breaking My covenant. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:20
I will spread My net over him and catch him in My snare. I will bring him to Babylon. I will judge him there for his treason against Me. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:21
All his best troops will die by the sword. Survivors will be scattered to every wind. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken.’ 📖

## Ezekiel 17:22
The Lord GOD says, ‘I will take a shoot from the cedar's high top and plant it. I will pluck a tender twig from its highest shoots. I will plant it on a high mountain. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:23
I will plant it on Israel's mountain heights to produce branches. It will bear fruit and become a majestic cedar. Birds of every kind will nest beneath it. They will shelter in its branches' shade. 📖

## Ezekiel 17:24
All the field's trees will know that I am the LORD. I bring the tall tree down and make the low tree tall. I dry the green tree and make the withered tree thrive. I, the LORD, have spoken and done it.’” 📖
