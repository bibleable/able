## Esther 10:1
Xerxes imposed tribute throughout the land, even to its farthest shores. 👑
## Esther 10:2
Mordecai’s powerful and magnificent achievements were written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Media and Persia. It included the full account of the greatness the king gave him. 📜
## Esther 10:3
Mordecai the Jew ranked second only to Xerxes. He was prominent among the Jews, and many relatives favored him greatly. He sought his people’s good and spoke peace to all his countrymen. 🕊️
