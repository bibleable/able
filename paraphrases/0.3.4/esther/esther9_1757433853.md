## Esther 9:1
The king’s order took effect on the thirteenth day of Adar, the twelfth month. The Jews’ enemies hoped to overpower them. Instead, the Jews overpowered those who hated them. ⚔️
## Esther 9:2
Throughout Xerxes’ provinces, Jews gathered in their cities to attack those who sought to harm them. No one could resist, because all peoples feared them. 🛡️
## Esther 9:3
Provincial officials, satraps, governors, and royal administrators helped the Jews because they feared Mordecai. 👥
## Esther 9:4
Mordecai held great power in the palace. His fame spread through the provinces as his power grew. 👑
## Esther 9:5
The Jews killed and destroyed their enemies with swords. They did as they pleased to those who hated them. ⚔️
## Esther 9:6
In Susa’s fortress, they killed and destroyed five hundred men. ⚖️
## Esther 9:7
These included Parshandatha, Dalphon, and Aspatha. 👥
## Esther 9:8
They included Poratha, Adalia, and Aridatha. 👥
## Esther 9:9
They included Parmashta, Arisai, Aridai, and Vaizatha. 👥
## Esther 9:10
They killed these ten sons of Haman son of Hammedatha, the Jews’ enemy. But they took no plunder. ⚖️
## Esther 9:11
That day, the king received the number killed in Susa’s fortress. 📋
## Esther 9:12
He told Esther, “The Jews killed five hundred men in Susa, including Haman’s ten sons. What did they do in the other provinces? What do you ask now? I will grant it. What else do you request? I will fulfill it.” 👑
## Esther 9:13
Esther replied, “If it pleases you, let Susa’s Jews carry out today’s order tomorrow too. Hang the bodies of Haman’s ten sons on the gallows.” 📜
## Esther 9:14
The king ordered this, and an order was issued in Susa. Haman’s ten sons were hanged. ⚖️
## Esther 9:15
On Adar’s fourteenth day, Susa’s Jews gathered again and killed three hundred men. They took no plunder. ⚔️
## Esther 9:16
The other Jews throughout the provinces gathered to defend themselves and rid themselves of enemies. They killed 75,000 who hated them but took no plunder. 🛡️
## Esther 9:17
They did this on Adar’s thirteenth day. On the fourteenth, they rested, feasted, and rejoiced. 🎉
## Esther 9:18
Susa’s Jews gathered on both the thirteenth and fourteenth days. They rested, feasted, and rejoiced on the fifteenth. 🎉
## Esther 9:19
This is why Jews in villages celebrate Adar’s fourteenth day with joy and feasting. They send gifts to each other on this holiday. 🎁
## Esther 9:20
Mordecai recorded these events and sent letters to all Jews in every province of Xerxes’ kingdom, near and far. ✉️
## Esther 9:21
He established a yearly celebration on Adar’s fourteenth and fifteenth days. 📅
## Esther 9:22
These marked their rest from enemies and the month when sorrow became joy and mourning became a holiday. He instructed them to feast, rejoice, and send gifts to each other and poor people. 🎁
## Esther 9:23
The Jews agreed to continue the custom they began, as Mordecai wrote. 📜
## Esther 9:24
Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, was the enemy of all Jews. He plotted to destroy them and cast the Pur, or lot, to crush and destroy them. ⚠️
## Esther 9:25
The matter came before the king. He ordered in writing that Haman’s evil plan return upon his own head. Haman and his sons were to be hanged on the gallows. ⚖️
## Esther 9:26
They called these days Purim, from the word Pur. They considered the letter’s instructions and all they saw and experienced. 📜
## Esther 9:27
The Jews committed themselves, their descendants, and everyone who joined them to this custom. They must celebrate both days every year at the appointed time, according to the rule. 📅
## Esther 9:28
Every generation, family, province, and city must remember and celebrate these days. The Jews must never stop observing Purim, and their descendants must never forget it. 🎉
## Esther 9:29
Queen Esther daughter of Abihail and Mordecai the Jew wrote with full authority to confirm this second letter about Purim. ✉️
## Esther 9:30
Mordecai sent letters with words of peace and truth to all Jews in Xerxes’ 127 provinces. 🕊️
## Esther 9:31
They confirmed Purim’s appointed times as Mordecai and Esther established them. The Jews also committed themselves and their descendants to times of fasting and mourning. 📅
## Esther 9:32
Esther’s order confirmed these Purim rules, which were written in the record. 📜
