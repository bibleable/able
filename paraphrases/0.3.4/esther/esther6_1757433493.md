## Esther 6:1
That night, the king could not sleep. He ordered the Book of Records, the Chronicles, brought and read to him. 📖
## Esther 6:2
It recorded how Mordecai exposed Bigthana and Teresh, two eunuchs who guarded the king’s entrance. They had plotted to kill Xerxes. 📜
## Esther 6:3
The king asked, “What honor or dignity did Mordecai receive for this?” His attendants replied, “Nothing was done for him.” 💬
## Esther 6:4
The king asked, “Who is in the court?” Haman had just entered the outer court to request Mordecai’s hanging on the gallows he prepared. ⚠️
## Esther 6:5
The attendants said, “Haman stands in the court.” The king ordered, “Bring him in.” 👣
## Esther 6:6
Haman entered, and the king asked, “What should be done for a man the king delights to honor?” Haman thought, “Whom would he honor more than me?” 💭
## Esther 6:7
Haman answered, “For a man the king delights to honor, do this. 💬
## Esther 6:8
Bring a royal robe the king wore and a horse he rode. The horse must have a royal crest on its head. 👑
## Esther 6:9
Give them to one of the noblest princes. Let him dress the honored man and lead him on the horse through the city square. Announce before him, ‘This is done for the man the king delights to honor!’” 🐎
## Esther 6:10
The king told Haman, “Hurry and do this for Mordecai the Jew at the king’s gate. Take the robe and horse. Do everything you proposed.” 👑
## Esther 6:11
Haman dressed Mordecai in the robe and led him on the horse through the square. He called, “This is done for the man the king delights to honor!” 🐎
## Esther 6:12
Mordecai returned to the king’s gate. Haman hurried home, covering his head in grief. 😢
## Esther 6:13
Haman told Zeresh and his friends everything. His advisers and Zeresh said, “Your fall before Mordecai has begun. Because he is Jewish, you cannot defeat him. You will surely fall before him.” ⚠️
## Esther 6:14
While they spoke, the king’s eunuchs arrived and hurried Haman to Esther’s feast. 👣
