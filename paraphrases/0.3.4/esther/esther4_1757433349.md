## Esther 4:1
Mordecai heard what happened, tore his clothes, and put on sackcloth and ashes. He went into the city and cried loudly and bitterly. 😢
## Esther 4:2
He stopped at the king’s gate. The law forbade anyone in sackcloth from entering it. 🚪
## Esther 4:3
Jews mourned greatly in every province that received the king’s order. They fasted, cried, and lamented. Many lay in sackcloth and ashes. 💔
## Esther 4:4
Esther’s maidservants and eunuchs told her about Mordecai, and she became deeply distressed. She sent clothes to replace his sackcloth, but he refused them. 😢
## Esther 4:5
Esther called Hathach, a royal eunuch assigned to her. She sent him to learn what troubled Mordecai and why. 👣
## Esther 4:6
Hathach went to Mordecai in the city square before the king’s gate. 🚪
## Esther 4:7
Mordecai told him everything that happened. He told the exact amount Haman promised the royal treasury to destroy the Jews. 💬
## Esther 4:8
He gave Hathach a copy of Susa’s order to destroy the Jews, to show and explain to Esther. He urged her to approach the king, seek his favor, and plead for her people. 📜
## Esther 4:9
Hathach returned and told Esther what Mordecai said. 📣
## Esther 4:10
Esther instructed Hathach to tell Mordecai this message. 💬
## Esther 4:11
“Everyone in the royal court and provinces knows this law: Anyone who enters the king’s inner court uninvited must die. The person can live only if the king extends his gold scepter. He has not called me for thirty days.” ⚠️
## Esther 4:12
They delivered Esther’s words to Mordecai. 📣
## Esther 4:13
He replied, “Do not think you alone among the Jews will escape because you live in the palace. ⚠️
## Esther 4:14
If you stay silent now, help and rescue will come for the Jews from elsewhere. But you and your father’s house will perish. Who knows whether you became queen for a time like this?” 💭
## Esther 4:15
Esther sent this reply to Mordecai. 📣
## Esther 4:16
“Gather all Jews in Susa and fast for me. Do not eat or drink for three days, night or day. My maidservants and I will do the same. Then I will go to the king against the law. If I perish, I perish!” 💪
## Esther 4:17
Mordecai did everything Esther instructed. 👣
