## Esther 1:1
These events happened when Xerxes ruled 127 provinces from India to Cush. 👑
## Esther 1:2
King Xerxes sat on his royal throne in Susa’s fortress. 🏰
## Esther 1:3
In his third year, he held a feast for all his officials and servants. Persia’s and Media’s military leaders attended, with the provinces’ nobles and princes. 🍽️
## Esther 1:4
For 180 days, he displayed his kingdom’s glorious riches and the magnificent splendor of his greatness. 💎
## Esther 1:5
Then he held a seven-day feast in the palace garden court. Everyone in Susa’s fortress attended, from the least to the greatest. 🎉
## Esther 1:6
White and blue linen curtains hung from silver rings on marble pillars by cords of fine white and purple cloth. Gold and silver couches stood on a mosaic floor of porphyry, marble, mother-of-pearl, and other costly stones. 💎
## Esther 1:7
Drinks came in gold cups, each with a different design. The king supplied royal wine freely and generously. 🍷
## Esther 1:8
The king ordered no limit on drinking. Every household official was to serve each man what he wanted. 🍷
## Esther 1:9
Queen Vashti also held a feast for women in King Xerxes’ royal palace. 👑
## Esther 1:10
On the seventh day, wine made the king cheerful. He commanded his seven eunuchs: Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carkas. 📣
## Esther 1:11
They were to bring Queen Vashti before him in her royal crown. He wanted to show her beauty to the people and officials, because she was beautiful. 👑
## Esther 1:12
The eunuchs delivered his command, but Vashti refused to come. The king became furious, and anger burned within him. 😠
## Esther 1:13
The king consulted wise men who understood the times. He usually sought advice from experts in law and justice. ⚖️
## Esther 1:14
His closest advisers were Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan. These seven princes of Persia and Media could meet the king personally and held the kingdom’s highest ranks. 👥
## Esther 1:15
He asked, “What does the law say we should do to Queen Vashti? She disobeyed King Xerxes’ command delivered by the eunuchs.” 💬
## Esther 1:16
Before the king and princes, Memucan replied, “Vashti wronged the king, all the princes, and all the peoples in his provinces. ⚠️
## Esther 1:17
Every woman will hear what the queen did and despise her husband. They will say, ‘Xerxes ordered Vashti to come before him, but she refused.’ 💬
## Esther 1:18
Today, Persia’s and Media’s noble women will hear about this. They will speak the same way to the king’s officials, causing much contempt and anger. ⚠️
## Esther 1:19
If it pleases the king, issue an order and record it in Persia’s and Media’s laws, so that it cannot be canceled. Vashti must never enter Xerxes’ presence again. Give her royal position to a better woman. 📜
## Esther 1:20
Your order will be heard throughout your vast kingdom. All women, from the least to the greatest, will honor their husbands.” 👑
## Esther 1:21
The king and princes liked this advice. The king did what Memucan proposed. 👥
## Esther 1:22
He sent letters to every province in its own script and every people in its own language. They announced that every man should rule his own household. ✉️
