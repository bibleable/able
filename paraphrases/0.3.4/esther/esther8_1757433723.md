## Esther 8:1
That day, Xerxes gave Esther the estate of Haman, the Jews’ enemy. Esther explained her relationship to Mordecai, so he entered the king’s presence. 👑
## Esther 8:2
The king gave Mordecai the signet ring he recovered from Haman. Esther placed Mordecai over Haman’s estate. 💍
## Esther 8:3
Esther spoke to the king again. She fell at his feet, cried, and begged him to cancel Haman the Agagite’s evil plan against the Jews. 🙏
## Esther 8:4
The king extended his gold scepter. Esther rose and stood before him. 👑
## Esther 8:5
She said, “If it pleases you, if you favor me, if this seems right, and if I please you, issue an order. Cancel the letters Haman son of Hammedatha, the scheming Agagite, wrote to destroy Jews in all your provinces. 📜
## Esther 8:6
How could I bear to see disaster fall on my people? How could I bear my relatives’ destruction?” 💔
## Esther 8:7
Xerxes told Esther and Mordecai, “I gave Haman’s estate to Esther. He was hanged because he attacked the Jews. ⚖️
## Esther 8:8
Write what you please about the Jews in the king’s name and seal it with the royal signet ring. An order written in his name and sealed with his ring cannot be canceled.” 📜
## Esther 8:9
On Sivan’s twenty-third day, the third month, royal scribes wrote Mordecai’s orders. They addressed the Jews, satraps, governors, and princes of the 127 provinces from India to Cush. Each province and people received its own script and language, including the Jews. ✍️
## Esther 8:10
Mordecai wrote in Xerxes’ name and sealed the documents with the royal signet ring. Couriers carried them on swift horses bred from royal mares. 🐎
## Esther 8:11
The letters permitted Jews in every city to gather and defend themselves. They could kill and completely destroy the forces of any hostile people or province, including women and children. They could plunder their possessions. ⚔️
## Esther 8:12
This was allowed on one day throughout Xerxes’ provinces: the thirteenth day of Adar, the twelfth month. 📅
## Esther 8:13
Copies were to be published in every province for everyone. The Jews were to prepare to take revenge on their enemies that day. 📜
## Esther 8:14
The king’s command sent the couriers riding quickly on royal horses. The order was also issued in Susa’s fortress. 🐎
## Esther 8:15
Mordecai left the king wearing blue and white royal clothes, a large gold crown, and a purple robe of fine linen. Susa’s people shouted and rejoiced. 👑
## Esther 8:16
The Jews had light, gladness, joy, and honor. ✨
## Esther 8:17
Jews in every city and province that received the king’s order rejoiced, feasted, and celebrated. Many people of the land became Jews because they feared the Jews. 🎉
