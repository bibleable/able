## Esther 5:1
On the third day, Esther wore her royal robes and stood in the palace’s inner court, opposite the king’s rooms. The king sat on his throne in the royal courtroom, facing the entrance. 👑
## Esther 5:2
The king saw Esther and favored her. He extended his gold scepter, and she approached and touched its tip. ❤️
## Esther 5:3
He asked, “What is it, Queen Esther? What do you request? I will give it to you, even half the kingdom.” 👑
## Esther 5:4
Esther replied, “If it pleases the king, come with Haman today to the feast I prepared for you.” 🍽️
## Esther 5:5
The king ordered, “Bring Haman quickly so we can do as Esther asks.” They went to her feast. 👣
## Esther 5:6
As they drank wine, the king asked, “What do you ask? I will grant it. What do you request? Even half the kingdom will be yours.” 🍷
## Esther 5:7
Esther replied, “This is what I ask and request. 💬
## Esther 5:8
If you favor me and wish to grant my request, come with Haman tomorrow to a feast I will prepare. Then I will answer your question.” 🍽️
## Esther 5:9
Haman left happily that day. At the king’s gate, Mordecai neither stood nor trembled before him. Haman became furious with Mordecai. 😠
## Esther 5:10
Haman controlled himself and went home. He called his friends and his wife Zeresh. 🏠
## Esther 5:11
He told them about his glorious wealth, many sons, and all the king’s honors. He described his promotion above the other officials and servants. 💎
## Esther 5:12
Haman said, “Esther invited only me to feast with the king. I am invited with the king again tomorrow. 🍽️
## Esther 5:13
But none of this satisfies me while I see Mordecai the Jew at the king’s gate.” 😠
## Esther 5:14
Zeresh and his friends said, “Build gallows fifty cubits high. In the morning, ask the king to hang Mordecai on it. Then enjoy the feast with the king.” Haman liked this and had the gallows built. ⚠️
