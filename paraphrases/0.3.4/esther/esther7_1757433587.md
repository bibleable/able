## Esther 7:1
The king and Haman went to dine with Queen Esther. 🍽️
## Esther 7:2
As they drank wine on the second day, he again asked, “Queen Esther, what do you ask? I will grant it. What do you request? Even half the kingdom will be yours.” 👑
## Esther 7:3
Esther replied, “If you favor me and it pleases you, grant my life and my people’s lives as my request. 🙏
## Esther 7:4
My people and I were sold for destruction, death, and complete ruin. If we were only sold as male and female servants, I would stay silent. Such distress would not justify troubling the king.” 💔
## Esther 7:5
Xerxes asked, “Who is he? Where is the man who planned this?” 😠
## Esther 7:6
Esther replied, “The enemy and opponent is this wicked Haman!” Haman stood terrified before the king and queen. ⚠️
## Esther 7:7
The furious king left his wine and went to the palace garden. Haman stayed to beg Esther for his life. He knew the king planned a terrible fate for him. 😨
## Esther 7:8
The king returned as Haman fell on Esther’s couch. He exclaimed, “Will he assault the queen while I am in the palace?” Immediately, they covered Haman’s face. ⚠️
## Esther 7:9
Harbonah, a royal eunuch, said, “Haman built gallows fifty cubits high at his house for Mordecai, whose report saved the king.” The king ordered, “Hang him on it!” ⚖️
## Esther 7:10
They hanged Haman on the gallows he prepared for Mordecai. The king’s anger faded. ⚖️
