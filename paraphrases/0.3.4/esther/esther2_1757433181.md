## Esther 2:1
Later, Xerxes’ anger faded. He remembered Vashti, her actions, and the order made against her. 💭
## Esther 2:2
His attendants proposed, “Search for beautiful young virgins for the king. 👥
## Esther 2:3
Appoint officials in every province to gather the beautiful young women into the harem at Susa’s fortress. Put them under Hegai, the king’s eunuch who cares for the women. Give them beauty treatments. 🏰
## Esther 2:4
Let the woman who pleases the king replace Vashti as queen.” The king liked this and acted on it. 👑
## Esther 2:5
A Jew named Mordecai lived in Susa’s fortress and belonged to Benjamin’s tribe. He was Jair’s son, Jair was Shimei’s son, and Shimei was Kish’s son. 🌳
## Esther 2:6
He had been taken from Jerusalem into exile by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. He was among the captives taken with Jeconiah king of Judah. ⛓️
## Esther 2:7
Mordecai raised his uncle’s daughter Hadassah, also called Esther, because her parents were dead. She was a young woman with a beautiful figure and appearance. After her parents died, Mordecai took her as his own daughter. ❤️
## Esther 2:8
The king’s order was announced, and many young women gathered in Susa’s fortress under Hegai’s care. Esther was taken to the palace and placed under Hegai, the women’s guardian. 🏰
## Esther 2:9
Esther pleased him and gained his favor. He quickly supplied her beauty treatments and special diet. He gave her seven chosen palace maidservants and moved them with her to the harem’s best place. 🎁
## Esther 2:10
Esther did not reveal her people or family background, as Mordecai instructed. 🤫
## Esther 2:11
Every day, Mordecai walked in front of the harem court to learn how Esther was and what happened to her. 👣
## Esther 2:12
Before each woman’s turn with King Xerxes, the harem required twelve months of beauty treatments. She received six months with myrrh oil and six months with perfumes and cosmetics. 🌸
## Esther 2:13
When she went to the king, she could take whatever she requested from the harem to his palace. 🎁
## Esther 2:14
She went in the evening and returned in the morning to a second harem. Shaashgaz, the king’s eunuch who cared for the concubines, supervised it. She returned to the king only if he delighted in her and called her by name. 🌙
## Esther 2:15
Esther was the daughter of Abihail, Mordecai’s uncle, and Mordecai adopted her. At her turn, she asked only for what Hegai, the king’s trusted harem official, advised. Everyone who saw her favored her. ❤️
## Esther 2:16
Esther was taken to King Xerxes in the palace in Tebeth, the tenth month, in his seventh year. 👑
## Esther 2:17
The king loved Esther more than all other women and favored her more than all other virgins. He put the royal crown on her head and made her queen instead of Vashti. 👑
## Esther 2:18
He held Esther’s great feast for all his officials and servants. He declared a tax holiday in the provinces and gave generous royal gifts. 🎉
## Esther 2:19
When the virgins gathered a second time, Mordecai sat at the king’s gate. 🚪
## Esther 2:20
Esther still hid her family background and people, as Mordecai instructed. She obeyed him as she did while in his care. 🤫
## Esther 2:21
While Mordecai sat at the king’s gate, two eunuchs who guarded the entrance became angry. Bigthan and Teresh plotted to kill King Xerxes. ⚠️
## Esther 2:22
Mordecai learned of the plot and told Queen Esther. She informed the king and credited Mordecai. 📣
## Esther 2:23
An investigation confirmed the report, and both officials were hanged on the gallows. This was written in the Book of the Chronicles before the king. 📜
