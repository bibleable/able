## Esther 3:1
Later, Xerxes honored Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite. He raised him above all his other princes. 👑
## Esther 3:2
The king commanded all royal servants at his gate to bow and honor Haman. They obeyed, but Mordecai would not bow or honor him. ⛔
## Esther 3:3
The royal servants asked Mordecai, “Why do you disobey the king’s command?” 💬
## Esther 3:4
They warned him daily, but he refused. He had told them he was a Jew. They reported him to Haman to see whether his behavior would be allowed. ⚠️
## Esther 3:5
Haman saw that Mordecai refused to bow or honor him. He became furious. 😠
## Esther 3:6
Haman learned who Mordecai’s people were and rejected the idea of harming only Mordecai. He planned to destroy all Jews, Mordecai’s people, throughout Xerxes’ kingdom. ⛔
## Esther 3:7
In Nisan, the first month of Xerxes’ twelfth year, the Pur, or lot, was cast before Haman to choose a day and month. It selected Adar, the twelfth month. 📅
## Esther 3:8
Haman told Xerxes, “A people is scattered among the peoples of every province. Their laws differ from others’, and they disobey your laws. It does not serve your interests to tolerate them. ⚠️
## Esther 3:9
If it pleases the king, order their destruction. I will put ten thousand talents of silver in the royal treasury to pay those who carry it out.” ⛔
## Esther 3:10
The king removed his signet ring and gave it to Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, the Jews’ enemy. 💍
## Esther 3:11
He said, “Keep your money. Do what you please with these people.” 👑
## Esther 3:12
On the first month’s thirteenth day, the royal scribes wrote exactly what Haman ordered the satraps, provincial governors, and peoples’ officials. They used each province’s script and each people’s language. The order bore Xerxes’ name and the seal of his signet ring. 📜
## Esther 3:13
Couriers took letters to every royal province. They ordered all Jews killed and completely destroyed, young and old, women and children, and their property plundered. This would happen on one day, Adar’s thirteenth day, in the twelfth month. ⚠️
## Esther 3:14
A copy of the order was to be published in every province for everyone. They were to be ready that day. 📜
## Esther 3:15
The couriers hurried away at the king’s command, and the order was issued in Susa’s fortress. The king and Haman sat to drink, but Susa’s people were confused. 🍷
