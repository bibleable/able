## 3 John 1:1
I am the elder. I write to dear Gaius, whom I love in the truth. ✉️❤️
## 3 John 1:2
Dear friend, I pray that all goes well for you. I pray that your body is healthy, just as your soul does well. 🙏❤️
## 3 John 1:3
Some brothers came and told me how faithful you are to the truth. You continue to live by it. This gave me great joy. 😊
## 3 John 1:4
Nothing gives me greater joy than news that my children live by the truth. ❤️
## 3 John 1:5
Dear friend, you are faithful in your help for the brothers. You help them even though they are strangers to you. 🤲
## 3 John 1:6
They told the church about your love. Do well by helping them continue their journey in a way that honors God. 🛤️❤️
## 3 John 1:7
They went out for the sake of the Name. They accepted nothing from the Gentiles. 🚶
## 3 John 1:8
So we must support people like them. Then we can work together for the truth. 🤝
## 3 John 1:9
I wrote to the church about this. But Diotrephes loves to be first. He refuses our instructions. ❌
## 3 John 1:10
If I come, I will point out his harmful lies about us. He also refuses to welcome the brothers. He stops people who want to welcome them. He even puts those people out of the church. 🗣️❌
## 3 John 1:11
Dear friend, copy what is good, not what is evil. A person who does good belongs to God. A person who does evil never saw God. ✅
## 3 John 1:12
Everyone speaks well of Demetrius. The truth itself is a witness for him. We also speak well of him. You know that our witness is true. 🗣️✅
## 3 John 1:13
I have much to write to you. But I prefer not to use pen and ink. ✍️
## 3 John 1:14
Instead, I hope to see you soon and talk face to face. Let peace be with you. Our friends here send greetings. Greet each friend there by name. 🤝👋
