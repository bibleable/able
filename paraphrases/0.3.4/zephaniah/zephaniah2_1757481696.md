## Zephaniah 2:1
Shameful nation, gather together! Gather yourselves! 👥
## Zephaniah 2:2
Gather before the decree takes effect and the day passes like dry grain husks. Gather before the LORD’s fierce anger reaches you. Gather before His day of anger comes. ⏳
## Zephaniah 2:3
All humble people who carry out His fairness, seek the LORD. Seek righteousness and humility. Perhaps He will shelter you on His day of anger. 🙏🛡️
## Zephaniah 2:4
People will leave Gaza, and Ashkelon will lie in ruins. Enemies will drive Ashdod’s people out at noon. Ekron will be like a plant pulled up by its roots. 🏚️
## Zephaniah 2:5
Great trouble waits for the Cherethites on the coast! Canaan, land of the Philistines, the LORD’s word is against you. He says, “I will destroy you. No one will remain.” ⚖️
## Zephaniah 2:6
The coast will become grassland. Shepherds will have wells, and sheep will have pens. 🐑
## Zephaniah 2:7
The coast will belong to Judah’s surviving people. They will find grass there. In the evening, they will rest in Ashkelon’s houses. The LORD their God will care for them and bring their captives back. 🏠❤️
## Zephaniah 2:8
God says, “I hear Moab’s scorn and the Ammonites’ insults. They mock My people and threaten their borders. 🗣️
## Zephaniah 2:9
As surely as I live, Moab will become like Sodom. The Ammonites will become like Gomorrah. Weeds and salt pits will fill their land, an empty place forever. My surviving people will take their goods and their land.” The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, declares this. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 2:10
This will repay their pride. They mock and insult the LORD of Hosts’ people. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 2:11
The LORD will fill them with terror when He starves all the earth’s gods. Then nations on every shore will worship Him, each in its own place. 🌍🙏
## Zephaniah 2:12
God says, “Cushites, My sword will kill you too.” ⚔️
## Zephaniah 2:13
He will stretch His hand north and destroy Assyria. He will make Nineveh empty and dry like a desert. 🏜️
## Zephaniah 2:14
Herds and creatures of every kind will rest there. Desert owls and screech owls will sit on the pillars. Their calls will sound from windows. The doorways will lie ruined, and He will expose the cedar beams. 🦉🏚️
## Zephaniah 2:15
This city lives without care and feels secure. It thinks, “I am the only one. No one is like me.” But it becomes a ruin where wild animals rest! Everyone who passes hisses and shakes a fist. 🏚️
