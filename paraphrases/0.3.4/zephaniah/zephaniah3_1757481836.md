## Zephaniah 3:1
Great trouble waits for the city that refuses to obey! It is unclean and treats people cruelly. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 3:2
The city listens to no voice and accepts no correction. It does not trust the LORD. It does not come near its God. ❌
## Zephaniah 3:3
Its rulers are like lions that roar. Its judges are like wolves at evening. They leave nothing for morning. 🦁
## Zephaniah 3:4
Its prophets are careless and unfaithful. Its priests make the holy place unclean. They do violence to the law. ❌
## Zephaniah 3:5
The LORD within the city is righteous and does no wrong. Every morning, He gives fair judgments. He never fails at dawn. But unfair people feel no shame. ⚖️🌅
## Zephaniah 3:6
God says, “I destroy nations and their corner towers. I empty their streets, and no one passes through. Their cities lie ruined, without a single person. 🏚️
## Zephaniah 3:7
I said, ‘Surely you will fear Me and accept correction.’ Then your home would not face destruction despite My punishments. But they rose early to make all their acts evil. ❌
## Zephaniah 3:8
So wait for Me until the day I rise to give evidence. I decide to gather nations and kingdoms. I will pour all My fierce anger on them. The fire of My jealousy will burn all the earth. 🔥
## Zephaniah 3:9
Then I will give the peoples pure lips again. They will all call on the LORD’s name and serve Him side by side. 🤝
## Zephaniah 3:10
My worshipers, My scattered people, will come from beyond Cush’s rivers. They will bring Me an offering. 🎁
## Zephaniah 3:11
On that day, your sins against Me will no longer bring you shame. I will remove the people who enjoy their pride. You will never again be proud on My holy mountain. ⛰️
## Zephaniah 3:12
I will leave gentle and humble people among you. They will trust the LORD’s name. ❤️
## Zephaniah 3:13
Israel’s survivors will do no wrong or tell lies. Their tongues will not deceive. They will eat and lie down. No one will make them afraid.” 🛡️
## Zephaniah 3:14
Zion’s people, sing with joy! Israel, shout aloud! Jerusalem’s people, be glad with all your heart! 🎵😊
## Zephaniah 3:15
The LORD removes your punishment and turns your enemy back. Israel’s King, the LORD, is among you. You will no longer fear harm. 👑🛡️
## Zephaniah 3:16
On that day, people will say to Jerusalem, “Zion, do not fear. Do not let your hands hang weakly. ✋
## Zephaniah 3:17
The LORD your God is among you. He is powerful to save. He will be glad over you. He will quiet you with His love. He will sing with joy over you.” ❤️🎵
## Zephaniah 3:18
God says, “I will gather those who grieve over the appointed feasts. You will no longer suffer insults. 🤲
## Zephaniah 3:19
At that time, I will deal with everyone who hurts you. I will save people who cannot walk well and gather those scattered far away. I will give praise and honor throughout the earth to people who face disgrace. 🛡️
## Zephaniah 3:20
At that time, I will bring you home and gather you. I will give you honor and praise among all the earth’s peoples. You will see Me bring your captives back.” The LORD says this. 🌍❤️
