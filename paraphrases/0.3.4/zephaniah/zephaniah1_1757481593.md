## Zephaniah 1:1
The LORD spoke to Zephaniah, Cushi’s son. Cushi was Gedaliah’s son. Gedaliah was Amariah’s son. Amariah was Hezekiah’s son. This happened while King Josiah, Amon’s son, ruled Judah. 📣
## Zephaniah 1:2
The LORD declares, “I will sweep everything away from the earth’s surface. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 1:3
I will sweep away people, animals, birds, and fish. I will sweep away idols and the evil people who worship them. I will remove human beings from the earth.” The LORD declares this. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 1:4
“I will stretch My hand against Judah and everyone in Jerusalem. I will remove every trace of Baal from this place. I will remove the names of priests who worship idols and other gods. ✋
## Zephaniah 1:5
I will remove people who bow on roofs to worship the stars of heaven. I will remove people who make promises by the LORD but also by Milcom. ❌
## Zephaniah 1:6
I will remove people who turn away from the LORD. They do not seek Him or ask Him for guidance.” ❌
## Zephaniah 1:7
Be silent before the Lord GOD. The Day of the LORD is near. He prepares a sacrifice and sets His guests apart as holy. 🤫
## Zephaniah 1:8
God says, “On the day of My sacrifice, I will punish rulers and the king’s sons. I will punish everyone who wears foreign clothes. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 1:9
That day, I will punish everyone who jumps over the doorway’s threshold. They fill their master’s house with violence and lies. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 1:10
On that day, a cry will rise from the Fish Gate. Loud cries will come from the Second District. A great crash will sound from the hills.” The LORD declares this. 🏙️
## Zephaniah 1:11
“People in the Hollow, cry loudly! All your traders will fall silent. Everyone who weighs silver will face destruction. 💰
## Zephaniah 1:12
I will search Jerusalem with lamps. I will punish people who settle into careless ease. They think, ‘The LORD will do nothing good or bad.’ 🪔
## Zephaniah 1:13
Enemies will steal their wealth and ruin their houses. They will build houses but not live in them. They will plant vines but never drink the wine. 🏚️
## Zephaniah 1:14
The LORD’s great day is near and comes quickly! Listen to that day’s sound. Powerful soldiers will cry bitterly. 🆘
## Zephaniah 1:15
That day brings fierce anger, trouble, and distress. It brings destruction and emptiness. It brings darkness, gloom, and black clouds. 🌑
## Zephaniah 1:16
Horns and battle cries will sound against strong cities. They will sound against the high corner towers. 📯
## Zephaniah 1:17
I will bring people such distress that they walk like blind people. This happens because they sinned against the LORD. Their blood will pour out like dust, and their flesh will lie like animal waste. ⚖️
## Zephaniah 1:18
Their silver and gold cannot save them on the day of the LORD’s anger. The fire of His jealousy will burn all the earth.” He will make a sudden end of everyone on earth. 🔥
