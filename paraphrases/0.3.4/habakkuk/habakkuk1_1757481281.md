## Habakkuk 1:1
The prophet Habakkuk received this message in a vision. 📣
## Habakkuk 1:2
LORD, how long must I call for help while You do not hear? I cry, “Violence!” But You do not save. 🆘
## Habakkuk 1:3
Why do You make me see evil? Why do You allow wrong acts? Destruction and violence are before me. Arguments continue, and fights are everywhere. 💔
## Habakkuk 1:4
So the law cannot act, and fairness never comes. People who do evil surround people who do right. They turn justice into something wrong. ⚖️
## Habakkuk 1:5
The LORD says, “Look at the nations and watch. Feel great wonder! I do a work in your days that you would not believe even if someone told you. 👀
## Habakkuk 1:6
I raise up the Chaldeans. This cruel nation acts quickly and marches across the earth. It takes homes that belong to others. 🌍
## Habakkuk 1:7
People dread them and fear them. They make their own rules of justice and authority. ⚖️
## Habakkuk 1:8
Their horses are faster than leopards and fiercer than wolves at night. Their riders charge from far away. They fly like a vulture that rushes down to eat. 🐎
## Habakkuk 1:9
They all come to do violence. Their crowds advance like the east wind. They gather prisoners as many as grains of sand. ⛓️
## Habakkuk 1:10
They mock kings and laugh at rulers. They laugh at every fort. They build ramps against it and capture it. 👑
## Habakkuk 1:11
They sweep past like the wind. They are guilty. Their own strength is their god.” 🌬️❌
## Habakkuk 1:12
LORD my God, my Holy One, do You not exist forever? We will not die. LORD, You appoint them to carry out judgment. My Rock, You establish them to correct us. 🪨⚖️
## Habakkuk 1:13
Your eyes are too pure to look at evil. You cannot accept wrong acts. Why then do You allow unfaithful people to act? Why stay silent while evil people swallow people more righteous than themselves? ❓
## Habakkuk 1:14
You make people like fish in the sea. They are like small creatures without a ruler. 🐟
## Habakkuk 1:15
The enemy pulls them up with a hook. He catches and gathers them in his nets. So he is glad. 🪝
## Habakkuk 1:16
He sacrifices to his net and burns incense to it. These nets give him rich food and plenty. *Word help: Incense gives sweet smoke when it burns.* ❌
## Habakkuk 1:17
Will he empty his net again and again? Will he continue to kill nations without kindness? ❓
