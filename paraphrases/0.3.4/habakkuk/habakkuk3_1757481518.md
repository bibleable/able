## Habakkuk 3:1
This is the prophet Habakkuk’s prayer. Its music direction is Shigionoth. 🎵🙏
## Habakkuk 3:2
LORD, I hear the report about You. Your acts fill me with wonder and fear. Do those works again in these years. Make them known in these years. In Your anger, remember kindness! 🙏
## Habakkuk 3:3
God came from Teman, and the Holy One came from Mount Paran. *Song direction: Selah.* His glory covered heaven. His praise filled the earth. ⛰️✨
## Habakkuk 3:4
His bright light was like sunlight. Rays flashed from His hand, where His power lies hidden. ✨✋
## Habakkuk 3:5
Deadly disease went before Him. Fever followed His steps. ⚖️
## Habakkuk 3:6
He stood and measured the earth. His look startled the nations. Ancient mountains broke apart, and lasting hills fell. His ways last forever. ⛰️🌍
## Habakkuk 3:7
I saw Cushan’s tents in distress. Midian’s tent curtains shook. ⛺
## Habakkuk 3:8
LORD, were You angry with the rivers and streams? Did You rage against the sea when You rode Your horses and chariots of rescue? 🌊❓
## Habakkuk 3:9
You raised Your bow and called for many arrows. *Song direction: Selah.* You split the earth with rivers. 🏹💧
## Habakkuk 3:10
The mountains saw You and shook. Great streams swept past. The deep water roared and lifted its hands high. ⛰️🌊
## Habakkuk 3:11
Sun and moon stood still in their places when Your arrows flashed and Your spear shone brightly. ☀️🌙
## Habakkuk 3:12
You marched across the earth with fierce anger. You crushed nations like grain on a threshing floor. 🌍⚖️
## Habakkuk 3:13
You went out to save Your people and the one You chose. You crushed the head of the evil family. You stripped him from head to foot. *Song direction: Selah.* 🛡️⚖️
## Habakkuk 3:14
You pierced his head with his own spear. His soldiers rushed out to scatter us. They were glad, like people ready to eat the weak in secret. ⚔️
## Habakkuk 3:15
You rode Your horses through the sea. They stirred the great waters. 🌊🐎
## Habakkuk 3:16
I heard and shook inside. My lips shook at the sound. My bones felt decay, and my body trembled. But I must wait patiently for trouble to come on the people who invade us. ⏳
## Habakkuk 3:17
The fig tree has no buds, and vines have no fruit. The olive crop fails, and fields give no food. Sheep are gone from their pens, and stalls have no cattle. 🌾
## Habakkuk 3:18
Yet I will celebrate in the LORD! I will be glad in the God who saves me. 😊🙌
## Habakkuk 3:19
GOD the Lord is my strength. He makes my feet like a deer’s. He makes me walk on high places! *Music directions: For the music leader, with instruments that have strings.* 🦌⛰️
