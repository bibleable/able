## Habakkuk 2:1
I will stand at my guard post on the strong wall. I will watch for what God says to me. I will consider how to answer His correction. 👀
## Habakkuk 2:2
The LORD answered, “Write this vision clearly on tablets. Then a messenger can run with it. ✍️
## Habakkuk 2:3
The vision waits for the appointed time. It tells about the end and does not lie. Even if it seems slow, wait for it. It will surely come at its proper time. ⏳
## Habakkuk 2:4
Look at the proud person. His inner life is not right. But the person who does right will live by faith. ❤️
## Habakkuk 2:5
Wealth betrays him. He is proud and never at rest. He wants more, like the place of the dead. Like Death, he never has enough. He gathers all nations and peoples as his own. 💰
## Habakkuk 2:6
All these people will surely mock him. They will say, ‘Great trouble waits for the person who piles up things that are not his! He becomes rich through many loans. How long will this continue?’ ⚖️
## Habakkuk 2:7
Will the people you owe not rise suddenly? Will the people who disturb you not wake? Then you will become their prey. ⚖️
## Habakkuk 2:8
You stole from many nations. So their surviving people will steal from you. You shed blood and used violence against the land, the city, and everyone there. 💔
## Habakkuk 2:9
Great trouble waits for the person who builds his house with unfair gain! He puts his nest high to escape disaster. 🏠
## Habakkuk 2:10
You plan shame for your house. You destroy many peoples and lose your own life. ⚖️
## Habakkuk 2:11
The stones will cry from the wall. The wooden beams will answer them. 🧱
## Habakkuk 2:12
Great trouble waits for the person who builds a city with bloodshed! He builds a town through sin. 💔
## Habakkuk 2:13
The LORD of Hosts makes people’s hard work feed only fire. The nations make themselves tired for nothing. Is this not His work? 🔥
## Habakkuk 2:14
People will know the LORD’s glory through all the earth. This knowledge will fill it as water covers the sea. 🌍🌊
## Habakkuk 2:15
Great trouble waits for the person who gives neighbors wine until they are drunk! He does this to look at their naked bodies. ❌
## Habakkuk 2:16
Shame will fill you instead of glory. You too must drink and show your uncircumcision. The cup in the LORD’s right hand will come to you. Complete disgrace will cover your glory. ⚖️
## Habakkuk 2:17
Your violence against Lebanon will cover you. The destruction of animals will fill you with terror. You shed blood and used violence against the land, the city, and everyone there. 💔
## Habakkuk 2:18
What use is an idol that a worker carves? It is an image that teaches lies. Its maker trusts what he makes. He makes idols that cannot speak. ❌
## Habakkuk 2:19
Great trouble waits for the person who tells wood, ‘Wake up!’ He tells silent stone, ‘Rise!’ Can it guide him? Gold and silver cover it, but it has no breath.” ❌
## Habakkuk 2:20
But the LORD is in His holy temple. Let all the earth be silent before Him. 🏠🤫
