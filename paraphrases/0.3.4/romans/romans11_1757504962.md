## Romans 11:1
Did God reject His people? Certainly not! I am an Israelite, Abraham’s descendant from Benjamin’s tribe. 🙏
## Romans 11:2
God did not reject the people He foreknew. Remember how Scripture describes Elijah appealing to God against Israel: 📖
## Romans 11:3
“Lord, they killed Your prophets and destroyed Your altars. Only I remain, and they seek my life too.” 💔
## Romans 11:4
God answered, “I kept seven thousand men for Myself who never knelt to Baal.” 🛡️
## Romans 11:5
Likewise, a remnant chosen by grace remains now. 🎁
## Romans 11:6
If it is through grace, it is no longer through works. Otherwise, grace would no longer be grace. 🙏
## Romans 11:7
Israel did not obtain what it sought, but the elect did. The others were hardened. ⚠️
## Romans 11:8
Scripture says, “God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes unable to see, and ears unable to hear, even today.” 📖
## Romans 11:9
David says, “May their table become a snare, trap, stumbling block, and punishment for them. ⚖️
## Romans 11:10
May their eyes grow dark and blind, and their backs remain bent forever.” ⚠️
## Romans 11:11
Did they stumble beyond recovery? Certainly not! Their offense brought salvation to Gentiles to make Israel jealous. 🌍
## Romans 11:12
If their offense brings riches to the world and their failure to Gentiles, how much greater riches will their fullness bring! 🎁
## Romans 11:13
I speak to you Gentiles. As your apostle, I honor my ministry, 📖
## Romans 11:14
hoping to make my own people jealous and save some. 🙏
## Romans 11:15
If their rejection reconciles the world, will their acceptance not mean life from the dead? 🌅
## Romans 11:16
If the dough’s first portion is holy, the whole batch is holy. If the root is holy, the branches are holy. 🌳
## Romans 11:17
If some branches were broken off and you, a wild olive shoot, were grafted among others to share the olive root’s nourishment, 🌳
## Romans 11:18
do not boast over those branches. Remember, the root supports you. You do not support it. ⚠️
## Romans 11:19
You will say, “Branches were broken off so I could be grafted in.” 💬
## Romans 11:20
Correct. Unbelief broke them off, but faith keeps you standing. Do not be proud. Be afraid. ⚠️
## Romans 11:21
If God did not spare natural branches, He will certainly not spare you. ⚖️
## Romans 11:22
Notice God’s kindness and severity. Severity comes to those who fell, but kindness to you if you remain in His kindness. Otherwise, you too will be cut off. ⚠️
## Romans 11:23
If they stop persisting in unbelief, they will be grafted in. God can graft them in again. 🌳
## Romans 11:24
You were cut from a wild olive tree and grafted against nature into a cultivated tree. If so, how much more readily will natural branches be grafted into their own tree! 🌳
## Romans 11:25
Brothers, know this mystery so you will not become conceited. Israel is partly hardened until the Gentiles’ full number comes in. 📖
## Romans 11:26
Thus, all Israel will be saved. Scripture says, “The Deliverer will come from Zion and remove godlessness from Jacob. 🛡️
## Romans 11:27
This is My covenant with them when I remove their sins.” ❤️
## Romans 11:28
Regarding the good news, they are enemies for your sake. Regarding election, they are loved because of the patriarchs. 🙏
## Romans 11:29
God’s gifts and calling cannot be revoked. 🎁
## Romans 11:30
You once disobeyed God, but received mercy through their disobedience. ❤️
## Romans 11:31
They too now disobey, so they may now receive mercy through the mercy shown to you. ❤️
## Romans 11:32
God handed everyone over to disobedience so He may show mercy to everyone. 🙏
## Romans 11:33
God’s wisdom and knowledge have deep riches! No one can fully understand His judgments or trace His ways. ✨
## Romans 11:34
“Who knew the Lord’s mind or advised Him?” ❓
## Romans 11:35
“Who first gave God something, so God should repay him?” ❓
## Romans 11:36
Everything comes from Him, through Him, and to Him. Glory to Him forever! Amen. 🙏
