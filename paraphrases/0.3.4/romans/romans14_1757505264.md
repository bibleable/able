## Romans 14:1
Accept those weak in faith without judging their opinions. 🤝
## Romans 14:2
One person has faith to eat anything. Another, weak in faith, eats only vegetables. 🥬
## Romans 14:3
Those eating everything must not belittle those who abstain. Those abstaining must not judge those eating, because God accepted them. ❤️
## Romans 14:4
Who are you to judge another’s servant? He stands or falls before his own master. He will stand because the Lord can make him stand. ⚖️
## Romans 14:5
One person values a day above others. Another regards every day alike. Each should be fully convinced in his own mind. 📅
## Romans 14:6
Whoever observes a special day does so for the Lord. Whoever eats does so for the Lord and thanks God. Whoever abstains also does so for the Lord and thanks God. 🙏
## Romans 14:7
None of us lives or dies for himself alone. 🤝
## Romans 14:8
If we live, we live to the Lord. If we die, we die to Him. Whether living or dying, we belong to Him. 🙏
## Romans 14:9
Christ died and returned to life to be Lord of both the dead and living. ✝️
## Romans 14:10
Why judge or belittle your brother? We will all stand before God’s judgment seat. ⚖️
## Romans 14:11
Scripture says, “As surely as I live,” says the Lord, “every knee will bow before Me. Every tongue will confess to God.” 📖
## Romans 14:12
Each of us will give God an account of himself. ⚖️
## Romans 14:13
Stop judging each other. Decide instead to put no stumbling block or obstacle in your brother’s way. 🤝
## Romans 14:14
I am fully convinced in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in itself. If someone considers something unclean, it is unclean for him. 🙏
## Romans 14:15
If your food distresses your brother, you no longer act in love. Do not destroy him through your eating. Christ died for him. ❤️
## Romans 14:16
Do not let what you consider good be called evil. ⚠️
## Romans 14:17
God’s kingdom concerns righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit, rather than eating and drinking. 🕊️
## Romans 14:18
Serving Christ this way pleases God and receives people’s approval. 🙏
## Romans 14:19
Seek what brings peace and builds each other up. 🤝
## Romans 14:20
Do not destroy God’s work for food. All food is clean, but eating in a way that makes another stumble is wrong. ⚠️
## Romans 14:21
If eating meat, drinking wine, or doing anything makes your brother stumble, it is better to avoid it. ❤️
## Romans 14:22
Keep your belief about these matters between yourself and God. Blessed is whoever does not condemn himself by what he approves. 🙏
## Romans 14:23
If someone doubts yet eats, he is condemned because his eating does not come from faith. Everything not from faith is sin. ⚠️
