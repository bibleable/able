## Romans 9:1
I speak truth in Christ, without lying. My conscience confirms this in the Holy Spirit. 🙏
## Romans 9:2
My heart has deep sorrow and constant anguish. 💔
## Romans 9:3
For my brothers, my own flesh and blood, I could wish myself cursed and cut off from Christ. 💔
## Romans 9:4
They are Israel’s people. They have adoption as sons, divine glory, covenants, the giving of the law, temple worship, and promises. 📖
## Romans 9:5
The patriarchs belong to them, and Christ descended from them as a human. He is God over all and deserves praise forever. Amen. 🙏
## Romans 9:6
God’s word did not fail. Not everyone descended from Israel is Israel. 📖
## Romans 9:7
Not all Abraham’s descendants are his children. Instead, “Your descendants will be counted through Isaac.” 👶
## Romans 9:8
Children of the promise, rather than children of the flesh, are counted as God’s children and descendants. 🙏
## Romans 9:9
The promise said, “I will return at the appointed time, and Sarah will have a son.” 👶
## Romans 9:10
Rebecca’s children also came from one man, our father Isaac. 👥
## Romans 9:11
Before the twins’ birth or any good or bad action, God acted so His plan of election might stand. 🙏
## Romans 9:12
It depended on the One calling, rather than works. She was told, “The older will serve the younger.” 📖
## Romans 9:13
Scripture says, “I loved Jacob, but hated Esau.” 📖
## Romans 9:14
Is God unjust? Certainly not! ⚖️
## Romans 9:15
He tells Moses, “I will have mercy on whom I have mercy and compassion on whom I have compassion.” ❤️
## Romans 9:16
This depends on God’s mercy, rather than human desire or effort. 🙏
## Romans 9:17
Scripture tells Pharaoh, “I raised you up to display My power in you and have My name announced throughout the earth.” 🌍
## Romans 9:18
God shows mercy to whomever He wants and hardens whomever He wants. ⚖️
## Romans 9:19
One of you will ask, “Why does God still find fault? Who can resist His will?” ❓
## Romans 9:20
Who are you to talk back to God? Should something formed ask its Maker, “Why did You make me this way?” 🏺
## Romans 9:21
Does a potter not have the right to make vessels from the same lump of clay? One is for special occasions, another for ordinary use. 🏺
## Romans 9:22
What if God, wanting to show wrath and power, endured with great patience vessels of wrath prepared for destruction? ⚖️
## Romans 9:23
What if He did this to reveal His glory’s riches to vessels of mercy, whom He prepared beforehand for glory? ✨
## Romans 9:24
These include us, called from both Jews and Gentiles. 🤝
## Romans 9:25
In Hosea, He says, “I will call those not My people ‘My People.’ I will call her who is not My beloved ‘My Beloved.’” ❤️
## Romans 9:26
“Where they heard, ‘You are not My people,’ they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’” 🙏
## Romans 9:27
Isaiah cries about Israel, “Though Israelites number like the sea’s sand, only the remnant will be saved. 🌊
## Romans 9:28
The Lord will carry out His sentence on earth completely and decisively.” ⚖️
## Romans 9:29
Isaiah foretold, “If the Lord of Hosts had left us no descendants, we would have become like Sodom and resembled Gomorrah.” ⚠️
## Romans 9:30
Gentiles did not seek righteousness, but obtained it through faith. 🙏
## Romans 9:31
Israel sought a law of righteousness, but did not attain it. 📖
## Romans 9:32
Why? They sought it as though through works, rather than faith. They stumbled over the stumbling stone. 🪨
## Romans 9:33
Scripture says, “I place in Zion a stumbling stone and a rock of offense. Whoever believes in Him will never be put to shame.” 🛡️
