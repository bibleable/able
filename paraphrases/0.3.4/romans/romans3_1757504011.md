## Romans 3:1
What advantage does a Jew have? What value does circumcision have? ❓
## Romans 3:2
Much, in every way. First, God entrusted them with His own words. 📖
## Romans 3:3
What if some lacked faith? Does their lack of faith cancel God’s faithfulness? ❓
## Romans 3:4
Certainly not! Let God be true and every person a liar. Scripture says, “May You be proved right when You speak and victorious when You judge.” 📖
## Romans 3:5
If our unrighteousness highlights God’s righteousness, what should we say? Is God unjust to bring His anger on us? I speak in human terms. ❓
## Romans 3:6
Certainly not! Otherwise, how could God judge the world? ⚖️
## Romans 3:7
If my lie highlights God’s truth and increases His glory, why am I still condemned as a sinner? ❓
## Romans 3:8
Why not say, “Let us do evil to bring good”? Some falsely claim we teach this. They deserve condemnation. ⚠️
## Romans 3:9
Are we better? Not at all. We already charged Jews and Greeks alike with being under sin. ⚖️
## Romans 3:10
Scripture says, “No one is righteous, not even one. 📖
## Romans 3:11
No one understands or seeks God. 💔
## Romans 3:12
Everyone turned away and together became worthless. No one does good, not even one.” ⚠️
## Romans 3:13
“Their throats are open graves. Their tongues deceive.” “Vipers’ poison is on their lips.” 🐍
## Romans 3:14
“Their mouths are full of curses and bitterness.” 💬
## Romans 3:15
“Their feet hurry to shed blood. ⚔️
## Romans 3:16
They leave ruin and misery behind them. 💔
## Romans 3:17
They do not know the way of peace.” 🕊️
## Romans 3:18
“They have no fear of God before their eyes.” ⚠️
## Romans 3:19
The law speaks to those under it, so every mouth may be silenced. This is so the whole world may answer to God. ⚖️
## Romans 3:20
No one will be declared righteous before God through works of the law. The law makes people aware of sin. 📖
## Romans 3:21
God’s righteousness has now been revealed apart from the law. The Law and Prophets testify to it. 📖
## Romans 3:22
This righteousness from God comes through faith in Jesus Christ to everyone believing. There is no distinction. 🙏
## Romans 3:23
Everyone sinned and falls short of God’s glory. 💔
## Romans 3:24
They are freely declared righteous by His grace through the redemption in Christ Jesus. ✝️
## Romans 3:25
God presented Him as an atoning sacrifice through His blood, received through faith. This demonstrated God’s righteousness because, in His patience, He passed over earlier sins. ✝️
## Romans 3:26
He did this to show His righteousness now, so He would be just and declare righteous whoever has faith in Jesus. ⚖️
## Romans 3:27
Where is boasting? It is excluded. By a principle of works? No, by a principle of faith. 🙏
## Romans 3:28
We maintain that people are declared righteous through faith, apart from works of the law. 🙏
## Romans 3:29
Is God only the Jews’ God? Is He not also the Gentiles’ God? Yes, He is. 🌍
## Romans 3:30
There is one God. He will declare circumcised and uncircumcised people righteous through the same faith. ⚖️
## Romans 3:31
Does this faith cancel the law? Certainly not! We uphold it. 📖
