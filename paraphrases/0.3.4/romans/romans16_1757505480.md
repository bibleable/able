## Romans 16:1
I recommend our sister Phoebe, a servant of Cenchrea’s church. 🤝
## Romans 16:2
Welcome her in the Lord in a way worthy of saints. Help with anything she needs. She greatly helped many people, including me. ❤️
## Romans 16:3
Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus. 👥
## Romans 16:4
They risked their lives for me. All Gentile churches join me in thanking them. 🙏
## Romans 16:5
Greet the church meeting in their house. Greet my beloved Epenetus, the first convert to Christ in the province of Asia. 🏠
## Romans 16:6
Greet Mary, who worked very hard for you. 🤝
## Romans 16:7
Greet Andronicus and Junia, my fellow countrymen and fellow prisoners. They are outstanding among the apostles and were in Christ before me. 👥
## Romans 16:8
Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord. ❤️
## Romans 16:9
Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my beloved Stachys. 👥
## Romans 16:10
Greet Apelles, approved in Christ. Greet those in Aristobulus’s household. 🤝
## Romans 16:11
Greet Herodion, my fellow countryman. Greet those in Narcissus’s household who belong to the Lord. 👥
## Romans 16:12
Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, women working hard in the Lord. Greet my beloved Persis, who worked very hard in the Lord. ❤️
## Romans 16:13
Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother. She was a mother to me too. ❤️
## Romans 16:14
Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them. 👥
## Romans 16:15
Greet Philologus, Julia, Nereus and his sister, Olympas, and all the saints with them. 👥
## Romans 16:16
Greet each other with a holy kiss. All Christ’s churches greet you. ❤️
## Romans 16:17
Brothers, watch for those creating divisions and obstacles against the teaching you learned. Turn away from them. ⚠️
## Romans 16:18
They serve their own appetites, rather than our Lord Christ. They deceive the naive with smooth words and flattery. ⚠️
## Romans 16:19
Everyone heard your obedience, so I rejoice over you. I want you to be wise about good and innocent about evil. 🙏
## Romans 16:20
The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. May our Lord Jesus Christ’s grace be with you. 🛡️
## Romans 16:21
Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you. So do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my fellow countrymen. 👥
## Romans 16:22
I am Tertius, who wrote down this letter. I greet you in the Lord. 📜
## Romans 16:23
Gaius, host to me and the whole church, greets you. So do Erastus, the city treasurer, and our brother Quartus. 🤝

## Romans 16:26
but now revealed through the prophets’ writings by the eternal God’s command. It was made known to lead all nations to the obedience that comes through faith. 🌍
