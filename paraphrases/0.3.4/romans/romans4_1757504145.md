## Romans 4:1
What did Abraham, our ancestor according to the flesh, discover? ❓
## Romans 4:2
If Abraham was declared righteous through works, he could boast, but not before God. ⚖️
## Romans 4:3
Scripture says, “Abraham believed God, and this was counted as righteousness for him.” 📖
## Romans 4:4
A worker’s wages are a debt owed, rather than a gift. 💰
## Romans 4:5
The one who does not work, but believes in Him who declares ungodly people righteous, has his faith counted as righteousness. 🙏
## Romans 4:6
David likewise describes the blessing of someone whom God counts righteous apart from works: 📖
## Romans 4:7
“Blessed are those whose lawless actions are forgiven and whose sins are covered. ❤️
## Romans 4:8
Blessed is the man whose sin the Lord will never count against him.” 🙏
## Romans 4:9
Does this blessing concern only circumcised people, or also uncircumcised people? We say Abraham’s faith was counted as righteousness. ❓
## Romans 4:10
When was it counted: before or after circumcision? Before, not after. ⏳
## Romans 4:11
He received circumcision as a sign and seal of the righteousness he had by faith while uncircumcised. He is therefore the father of uncircumcised believers, so righteousness may also be counted for them. 🙏
## Romans 4:12
He is also father of those who are circumcised and follow the faith our father Abraham had before his circumcision. 🚶
## Romans 4:13
Abraham and his descendants received the promise that he would inherit the world. It came through righteousness by faith, rather than the law. 🌍
## Romans 4:14
If those living by the law are heirs, faith is useless and the promise worthless. ⚖️
## Romans 4:15
The law brings anger. Where there is no law, there is no violation. 📖
## Romans 4:16
The promise comes through faith to rest on grace and be guaranteed to all Abraham’s descendants. This includes those of the law and those sharing Abraham’s faith. He is father of us all. 🤝
## Romans 4:17
Scripture says, “I made you father of many nations.” Abraham is our father before the God he believed. God gives life to the dead and calls into being things that do not exist. 🌅
## Romans 4:18
Against all hope, Abraham believed with hope and became father of many nations. God had told him, “Your descendants will be like this.” 🙏
## Romans 4:19
His faith did not weaken. He recognized his worn-out body, about a hundred years old, and Sarah’s lifeless womb. ⏳
## Romans 4:20
He did not doubt God’s promise through unbelief. His faith grew stronger, and he glorified God. 🙏
## Romans 4:21
He was fully convinced God could do what He promised. 🛡️
## Romans 4:22
This is why it was counted as righteousness for him. ⚖️
## Romans 4:23
The words “counted as righteousness for him” were written for more than Abraham alone. 📖
## Romans 4:24
They were also written for us who believe in the One who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead. Righteousness will be counted for us. 🌅
## Romans 4:25
Jesus was handed over to death for our offenses and raised to life for our justification. ✝️
