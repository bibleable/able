## Romans 5:1
We have been declared righteous through faith. Therefore, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. 🕊️
## Romans 5:2
Through Him, faith gives us access to the grace in which we stand. We rejoice in hope of God’s glory. 🙏
## Romans 5:3
We also rejoice in suffering because suffering produces endurance. 💪
## Romans 5:4
Endurance produces character, and character produces hope. 🌅
## Romans 5:5
Hope does not disappoint us. God poured His love into our hearts through the Holy Spirit, whom He gave us. ❤️
## Romans 5:6
At the right time, while we were powerless, Christ died for ungodly people. ✝️
## Romans 5:7
Rarely will anyone die for a righteous person. Someone might dare to die for a good person. 💔
## Romans 5:8
God proves His love this way: Christ died for us while we were sinners. ❤️
## Romans 5:9
We have now been declared righteous by His blood. How much more will He save us from wrath! 🛡️
## Romans 5:10
Even as God’s enemies, we were reconciled to Him through His Son’s death. If so, how much more will His life save us now that we are reconciled! ✝️
## Romans 5:11
We also rejoice in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we received reconciliation. 🙏
## Romans 5:12
Sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin. Death spread to everyone because everyone sinned. ⚠️
## Romans 5:13
Sin existed before the law was given. Without law, sin is not counted. 📖
## Romans 5:14
Yet death ruled from Adam to Moses, even over people who did not sin as Adam did. Adam is a pattern of the One to come. ⏳
## Romans 5:15
The gift differs from the offense. If many died through one man’s offense, how much more did God’s grace and gift overflow to many! The gift came through the grace of one man, Jesus Christ. 🎁
## Romans 5:16
The gift also differs from the result of one man’s sin. Judgment after one sin brought condemnation. The gift after many offenses brought justification. ⚖️
## Romans 5:17
If death ruled through one man’s offense, how much more will these people rule in life? They receive abundant grace and the gift of righteousness and rule through one man, Jesus Christ. 👑
## Romans 5:18
One offense brought condemnation for everyone. Likewise, one righteous act brought justification and life for everyone. ✝️
## Romans 5:19
One man’s disobedience made many sinners. Likewise, one man’s obedience will make many righteous. 🙏
## Romans 5:20
The law came so the offense would increase. Where sin increased, grace increased even more. 🎁
## Romans 5:21
This was so grace might rule through righteousness, as sin ruled in death. Grace brings eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord. 🌅
