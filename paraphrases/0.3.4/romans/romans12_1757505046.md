## Romans 12:1
Brothers, because of God’s mercy, I urge you to offer your bodies as living sacrifices, holy and pleasing to God. This is your spiritual service of worship. 🙏
## Romans 12:2
Do not conform to this world. Be transformed through your mind’s renewal. Then you can test and approve God’s good, pleasing, and perfect will. 💭
## Romans 12:3
Through the grace given me, I tell each of you: do not think too highly of yourself. Use sober judgment, according to the measure of faith God gave you. 🙏
## Romans 12:4
Each of us has one body with many parts. The parts have different functions. 👥
## Romans 12:5
Likewise, in Christ, we are many but form one body. Each part belongs to the others. 🤝
## Romans 12:6
Our gifts differ according to the grace given us. If your gift is prophecy, use it in proportion to your faith. 🎁
## Romans 12:7
If it is service, serve. If it is teaching, teach. 🤝
## Romans 12:8
If it is encouragement, encourage. If it is giving, give generously. If it is leading, lead carefully and diligently. If it is mercy, show it cheerfully. ❤️
## Romans 12:9
Love sincerely. Hate evil and hold firmly to good. ❤️
## Romans 12:10
Devote yourselves to each other in brotherly love. Excel in honoring each other. 🤝
## Romans 12:11
Do not lose your eagerness. Keep your spiritual zeal and serve the Lord. 🔥
## Romans 12:12
Rejoice in hope. Be patient in suffering and persistent in prayer. 🙏
## Romans 12:13
Share with saints in need and welcome guests. 🏠
## Romans 12:14
Bless your persecutors. Bless them instead of cursing. ❤️
## Romans 12:15
Rejoice with those rejoicing. Weep with those weeping. 💧
## Romans 12:16
Live harmoniously. Avoid pride and associate with the lowly. Do not be conceited. 🤝
## Romans 12:17
Repay no one evil for evil. Carefully consider what is right in everyone’s eyes. ⚖️
## Romans 12:18
As far as possible on your part, live peacefully with everyone. 🕊️
## Romans 12:19
Beloved, do not take revenge. Leave room for God’s wrath. Scripture says, “Revenge is Mine. I will repay,” says the Lord. ⚖️
## Romans 12:20
“If your enemy is hungry, feed him. If thirsty, give him a drink. This will heap burning coals on his head.” 🍞
## Romans 12:21
Do not let evil defeat you. Defeat evil with good. 🛡️
