## Romans 13:1
Everyone must submit to governing authorities. All authority comes from God, who appointed the existing authorities. ⚖️
## Romans 13:2
Resisting authority opposes what God established. Those resisting bring judgment on themselves. ⚠️
## Romans 13:3
Rulers are a terror to those doing wrong, rather than those doing good. Do you want to live without fear of authority? Do right, and you will receive approval. ⚖️
## Romans 13:4
The ruler is God’s servant for your good. Fear if you do wrong, because he does not carry a sword uselessly. He serves God by punishing wrongdoers. ⚔️
## Romans 13:5
Submit to authority both to avoid punishment and for conscience’s sake. 🙏
## Romans 13:6
This is also why you pay taxes. Authorities serve God and devote themselves to their work. 🪙
## Romans 13:7
Pay everyone what you owe: taxes, revenue, respect, and honor, each to those entitled to it. ⚖️
## Romans 13:8
Owe no one anything except love for each other. Anyone loving his neighbor fulfills the law. ❤️
## Romans 13:9
The commands forbid adultery, murder, theft, and coveting. These and all other commands are summed up in, “Love your neighbor as yourself.” 📖
## Romans 13:10
Love does no wrong to a neighbor. Love therefore fulfills the law. ❤️
## Romans 13:11
Do this knowing the time. Wake from sleep, because salvation is nearer now than when we first believed. 🌅
## Romans 13:12
Night is nearly over, and day is near. Remove deeds of darkness and put on light’s armor. 🛡️
## Romans 13:13
Behave decently, as in daylight. Avoid wild parties, drunkenness, sexual immorality, wild immoral behavior, conflict, and jealousy. ⚠️
## Romans 13:14
Clothe yourselves with the Lord Jesus Christ. Make no provision for the flesh’s desires. 🙏
