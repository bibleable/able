## Romans 2:1
You who judge others have no excuse. Your judgment condemns yourself because you do the same things. ⚖️
## Romans 2:2
We know God judges those doing such things according to truth. ⚖️
## Romans 2:3
You judge others but do the same things. Do you think you will escape God’s judgment? ❓
## Romans 2:4
Do you disregard His abundant kindness, tolerance, and patience? Do you not realize His kindness leads you to repentance? ❤️
## Romans 2:5
Your hard, unrepentant heart stores up anger against yourself. On the day of anger, God’s righteous judgment will be revealed. ⚠️
## Romans 2:6
God will repay everyone according to their actions. ⚖️
## Romans 2:7
He will give eternal life to those who persist in doing good and seek glory, honor, and life that never ends. 🌅
## Romans 2:8
Anger and wrath await selfish people who reject truth and follow wickedness. ⚠️
## Romans 2:9
Trouble and distress await everyone doing evil, first the Jew, then the Greek. 💔
## Romans 2:10
Glory, honor, and peace await everyone doing good, first the Jew, then the Greek. 🕊️
## Romans 2:11
God shows no favoritism. ⚖️
## Romans 2:12
Those sinning without the law will perish without it. Those sinning under the law will be judged by it. 📖
## Romans 2:13
Hearing the law does not make people righteous before God. Those doing what it requires will be declared righteous. ⚖️
## Romans 2:14
Gentiles do not have the law. When they naturally do what it requires, they are a law to themselves despite not having it. 📖
## Romans 2:15
They show that the law’s work is written in their hearts. Their consciences testify, and their thoughts accuse or defend them. ❤️
## Romans 2:16
This concerns the day God judges people’s secrets through Christ Jesus, as my good news announces. ⚖️
## Romans 2:17
Suppose you call yourself a Jew, rely on the law, and boast in God. 📖
## Romans 2:18
Suppose the law teaches you His will, so you know it and approve of better things. 👂
## Romans 2:19
Suppose you are convinced you guide the blind and bring light to those in darkness. 🕯️
## Romans 2:20
Suppose you instruct fools and teach infants because the law gives you knowledge and truth in concrete form. 📖
## Romans 2:21
Then, while teaching others, do you not teach yourself? You preach against theft, but do you steal? ❓
## Romans 2:22
You forbid adultery, but do you commit it? You hate idols, but do you rob temples? ❓
## Romans 2:23
You boast in the law, but do you dishonor God by breaking it? ⚠️
## Romans 2:24
Scripture says, “God’s name is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you.” 📖
## Romans 2:25
Circumcision has value if you keep the law. Breaking the law makes your circumcision count as uncircumcision. ⚖️
## Romans 2:26
If an uncircumcised man keeps the law’s requirements, will he not be considered circumcised? ❓
## Romans 2:27
A physically uncircumcised man who obeys the law will condemn you. You break the law despite having its written code and circumcision. ⚠️
## Romans 2:28
Outward appearance alone does not make a man a Jew. Circumcision is not merely outward and physical. 👁️
## Romans 2:29
A man is a Jew inwardly. Circumcision concerns the heart, through the Spirit rather than the written code. Such a man receives praise from God rather than people. ❤️
