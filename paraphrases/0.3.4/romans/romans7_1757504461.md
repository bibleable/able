## Romans 7:1
Brothers, I speak to those who know the law. Do you not know it rules a person only while he lives? 📖
## Romans 7:2
The law binds a married woman to her husband while he lives. His death releases her from marriage’s law. 💍
## Romans 7:3
If she joins another man while her husband lives, she is called an adulteress. If her husband dies, she is free from that law. Marrying another man then does not make her an adulteress. ⚖️
## Romans 7:4
Brothers, you died to the law through Christ’s body to belong to another, the One raised from the dead. This was so we might bear fruit for God. ✝️
## Romans 7:5
When we lived according to the flesh, the law stirred sinful desires in our bodies. These bore fruit for death. ⚠️
## Romans 7:6
We died to what bound us and were released from the law. This was so we could serve in the Spirit’s new way, rather than the written code’s old way. 🕊️
## Romans 7:7
Is the law sin? Certainly not! Without it, I would not know sin. I would not know coveting if the law had not said, “Do not covet.” 📖
## Romans 7:8
Sin used the command’s opportunity to produce every kind of covetous desire in me. Apart from the law, sin is dead. ⚠️
## Romans 7:9
I once lived apart from the law. When the command came, sin came alive, and I died. 💔
## Romans 7:10
I found that the command meant to bring life brought death. ⚖️
## Romans 7:11
Sin used the opportunity through the command to deceive and kill me through that command. ⚠️
## Romans 7:12
The law is holy. The command is holy, righteous, and good. 📖
## Romans 7:13
Did something good become death for me? Certainly not! Sin produced death in me through what was good to be exposed as sin. This was so sin might become utterly sinful through the command. ⚠️
## Romans 7:14
The law is spiritual. I am unspiritual, sold as sin’s slave. ⛓️
## Romans 7:15
I do not understand my actions. I do not do what I want. I do what I hate. 💔
## Romans 7:16
If I do what I do not want, I admit the law is good. 📖
## Romans 7:17
Then it is no longer I doing it, but sin living in me. ⚠️
## Romans 7:18
Nothing good lives in me, meaning my flesh. I want to do good, but cannot carry it out. 💔
## Romans 7:19
I do not do the good I want. I keep doing the evil I do not want. ⚠️
## Romans 7:20
If I do what I do not want, it is no longer I doing it, but sin living in me. 💔
## Romans 7:21
I found this principle: when I want to do good, evil is beside me. ⚠️
## Romans 7:22
In my inner being, I delight in God’s law. 📖
## Romans 7:23
I see another law in my body fighting my mind’s law. It holds me captive to sin’s law within me. ⛓️
## Romans 7:24
I am a miserable man! Who will rescue me from this body of death? 🙏
## Romans 7:25
Thanks to God through Jesus Christ our Lord! My mind serves God’s law, but my flesh serves sin’s law. 🙏
