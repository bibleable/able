## Romans 6:1
Should we keep sinning so grace increases? ❓
## Romans 6:2
Certainly not! We died to sin. How can we keep living in it? ⚠️
## Romans 6:3
Do you not know that everyone baptized into Christ Jesus was baptized into His death? 💧
## Romans 6:4
Baptism buried us with Him into death. Christ rose from the dead through the Father’s glory. This was so we too might walk in new life. 🌅
## Romans 6:5
If we were united with Him in His death like this, we will certainly be united with Him in His resurrection. ✝️
## Romans 6:6
Our old self was crucified with Him so the body of sin might become powerless. This was so we would no longer be sin’s slaves. ⛓️
## Romans 6:7
Anyone who died has been freed from sin. 🛡️
## Romans 6:8
If we died with Christ, we believe we will live with Him. 🌅
## Romans 6:9
The risen Christ cannot die again. Death no longer rules Him. 👑
## Romans 6:10
He died to sin once for all. He lives His life to God. ✝️
## Romans 6:11
Count yourselves dead to sin, but alive to God in Christ Jesus. 🙏
## Romans 6:12
Do not let sin rule your mortal body and make you obey its desires. ⚠️
## Romans 6:13
Do not offer your body’s parts to sin as tools of wickedness. Offer yourselves to God as people brought from death to life. Offer Him your body’s parts as tools of righteousness. 🙏
## Romans 6:14
Sin will not master you because you are under grace rather than law. 🛡️
## Romans 6:15
Should we sin because we are under grace rather than law? Certainly not! ⚠️
## Romans 6:16
When you offer yourselves as obedient slaves, you become slaves to whoever you obey. Slavery to sin leads to death. Obedience leads to righteousness. ⛓️
## Romans 6:17
Thank God! You were sin’s slaves, but wholeheartedly obeyed the form of teaching to which you were committed. 🙏
## Romans 6:18
You were freed from sin and became righteousness’s slaves. 🛡️
## Romans 6:19
I speak in human terms because your flesh is weak. You once offered your body’s parts as slaves to impurity and increasing wickedness. Now offer them as slaves to righteousness, which leads to holiness. 🙏
## Romans 6:20
When you were sin’s slaves, you were free from obligation to righteousness. ⛓️
## Romans 6:21
What fruit came from the things that now shame you? Their result is death. 💔
## Romans 6:22
You are now freed from sin and enslaved to God. Your fruit leads to holiness, and the result is eternal life. 🌅
## Romans 6:23
Sin’s wages are death. God’s gift is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord. 🎁
