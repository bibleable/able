## Romans 1:1
I am Paul, a servant of Christ Jesus. I was called to be an apostle and set apart for God’s good news. 📖
## Romans 1:2
God promised this good news beforehand through His prophets in the Holy Scriptures. 📜
## Romans 1:3
It concerns His Son, who descended from David according to the flesh. 👑
## Romans 1:4
Through the Spirit of holiness, His resurrection from the dead powerfully declared Him God’s Son. He is Jesus Christ our Lord. 🌅
## Romans 1:5
Through Him, for His name’s sake, we received grace and apostleship to call all Gentiles to obedience that comes from faith. 🙏
## Romans 1:6
You too are called to belong to Jesus Christ. ❤️
## Romans 1:7
To everyone in Rome whom God loves and calls to be saints: grace and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 🕊️
## Romans 1:8
First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you. People announce your faith throughout the world. 🙏
## Romans 1:9
God witnesses how constantly I remember you. I serve Him with my spirit by preaching His Son’s good news. 📖
## Romans 1:10
I always remember you in prayer. I ask that, by God’s will, I may finally succeed in visiting you. 🙏
## Romans 1:11
I long to see you and share a spiritual gift to strengthen you. 🎁
## Romans 1:12
I mean that we may encourage each other through our faith. 🤝
## Romans 1:13
Brothers, know that I often planned to visit, but was prevented until now. I wanted a harvest among you, as among other Gentiles. 🌾
## Romans 1:14
I have an obligation to Greeks and non-Greeks, to wise and foolish people. 🤝
## Romans 1:15
That is why I eagerly want to preach the good news to you in Rome. 📣
## Romans 1:16
I am not ashamed of the good news. It is God’s power for salvation to everyone believing, first the Jew, then the Greek. 🛡️
## Romans 1:17
The good news reveals God’s righteousness, which comes through faith from beginning to end. Scripture says, “The righteous will live by faith.” 📖
## Romans 1:18
God’s anger is revealed from heaven against all human godlessness and wickedness. These people suppress truth through wickedness. ⚖️
## Romans 1:19
What can be known about God is clear to them because God made it clear. 👁️
## Romans 1:20
Since creation, God’s work clearly reveals His invisible qualities: eternal power and divine nature. People understand these through what He made and therefore have no excuse. 🌍
## Romans 1:21
They knew God, but neither glorified Him as God nor thanked Him. Their thinking became useless, and their foolish hearts became dark. ⚠️
## Romans 1:22
They claimed wisdom, but became fools. 💬
## Romans 1:23
They exchanged the glory of God, who cannot die, for images of people who die, birds, animals, and reptiles. 🪵
## Romans 1:24
God therefore gave them over to their hearts’ impure desires. They dishonored their bodies with each other. ⚠️
## Romans 1:25
They exchanged God’s truth for a lie. They worshiped and served created things instead of the Creator, who deserves praise forever. Amen. 🙏
## Romans 1:26
God therefore gave them over to shameful desires. Their women exchanged natural relations for unnatural ones. ⚠️
## Romans 1:27
The men likewise abandoned natural relations with women and burned with desire for each other. Men did indecent acts with men and received the due penalty for their error within themselves. ⚖️
## Romans 1:28
They refused to acknowledge God. He gave them over to corrupt minds, to do what should not be done. ⚠️
## Romans 1:29
They are filled with every kind of wickedness, evil, greed, and moral corruption. They are full of envy, murder, conflict, deceit, and malice. They gossip, 💔
## Romans 1:30
slander, and hate God. They are rude, proud, and boastful. They invent evil and disobey their parents. ⚠️
## Romans 1:31
They lack understanding, faithfulness, love, and mercy. 💔
## Romans 1:32
They know God’s righteous decree: people doing these things deserve death. Yet they continue and approve of others doing the same. ⚖️
