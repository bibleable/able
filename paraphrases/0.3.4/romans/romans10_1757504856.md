## Romans 10:1
Brothers, I desire and pray to God for Israel’s salvation. 🙏
## Romans 10:2
I testify that they are eager for God, but without knowledge as their basis. 💬
## Romans 10:3
They did not know God’s righteousness and tried to establish their own. They did not submit to His righteousness. ⚠️
## Romans 10:4
Christ is the law’s end, bringing righteousness to everyone believing. ✝️
## Romans 10:5
Moses writes about righteousness through the law: “Whoever does these things will live by them.” 📖
## Romans 10:6
Righteousness through faith says, “Do not ask in your heart, ‘Who will go up to heaven?’” That means bringing Christ down. ☁️
## Romans 10:7
“Do not ask, ‘Who will go down into the Abyss?’” That means bringing Christ up from the dead. ✝️
## Romans 10:8
It says, “The word is near, in your mouth and heart.” This is the word of faith we announce. 📖
## Romans 10:9
If you confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved. 🙏
## Romans 10:10
You believe with your heart and are declared righteous. You confess with your mouth and are saved. ❤️
## Romans 10:11
Scripture says, “Anyone believing in Him will never be put to shame.” 🛡️
## Romans 10:12
Jew and Greek have no difference here. The same Lord rules everyone and gives richly to all who call on Him. 🤝
## Romans 10:13
“Everyone calling on the Lord’s name will be saved.” 🙏
## Romans 10:14
How can they call on Him without believing? How can they believe without hearing about Him? How can they hear without a preacher? ❓
## Romans 10:15
How can people preach unless sent? Scripture says, “How beautiful are the feet of those bringing good news!” 👣
## Romans 10:16
Not everyone welcomed the good news. Isaiah says, “Lord, who believed our message?” 📖
## Romans 10:17
Faith comes through hearing, and hearing through Christ’s word. 👂
## Romans 10:18
Did they not hear? Yes, they did. “Their voice went throughout the earth, their words to the world’s ends.” 🌍
## Romans 10:19
Did Israel not understand? Moses first says, “I will make you jealous through those who are no nation. I will make you angry through a nation without understanding.” 📖
## Romans 10:20
Isaiah boldly says, “Those not seeking Me found Me. I revealed Myself to those not asking for Me.” 🙏
## Romans 10:21
About Israel, he says, “All day, I held out My hands to disobedient, stubborn people.” 💔
