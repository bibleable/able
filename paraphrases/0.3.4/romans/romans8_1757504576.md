## Romans 8:1
There is now no condemnation for those in Christ Jesus. 🛡️
## Romans 8:2
In Christ Jesus, the law of the Spirit of life freed you from the law of sin and death. 🕊️
## Romans 8:3
God did what the law could not do because the flesh weakened it. He sent His Son in sinful man’s likeness as a sin offering. He condemned sin in the flesh. ✝️
## Romans 8:4
This was so the law’s righteous standard might be fulfilled in us. We walk according to the Spirit rather than the flesh. 🕊️
## Romans 8:5
Those living according to the flesh think about its things. Those living according to the Spirit think about the Spirit’s things. 💭
## Romans 8:6
The mind of the flesh is death. The mind of the Spirit is life and peace. 🕊️
## Romans 8:7
The mind of the flesh opposes God. It does not submit to God’s law and cannot do so. ⚠️
## Romans 8:8
Those controlled by the flesh cannot please God. ⚖️
## Romans 8:9
If God’s Spirit lives in you, the Spirit controls you rather than the flesh. Anyone without Christ’s Spirit does not belong to Christ. 🕊️
## Romans 8:10
If Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin. Yet your spirit lives because of righteousness. 🌅
## Romans 8:11
If the Spirit of the One who raised Jesus from the dead lives in you, that One will give your mortal bodies life. He will do this through His Spirit, who lives in you. 🌅
## Romans 8:12
Brothers, we have an obligation, but not to live according to the flesh. 🙏
## Romans 8:13
Living according to the flesh brings death. If you put the body’s actions to death through the Spirit, you will live. 🛡️
## Romans 8:14
Everyone led by God’s Spirit is a son of God. ❤️
## Romans 8:15
You received the Spirit of adoption as sons, rather than a spirit of slavery returning you to fear. Through Him, we cry, “Abba! Father!” ❤️
## Romans 8:16
The Spirit Himself testifies with our spirit that we are God’s children. 🕊️
## Romans 8:17
If we are children, we are God’s heirs and share Christ’s inheritance, if indeed we suffer with Him. This is so we may be glorified with Him. 👑
## Romans 8:18
Our present sufferings cannot compare with the glory to be revealed in us. 🌅
## Romans 8:19
Creation eagerly waits for God’s sons to be revealed. 🌍
## Romans 8:20
Creation was subjected to futility against its own will. The One who subjected it did so in hope 🌍
## Romans 8:21
that creation itself will be freed from slavery to decay and share God’s children’s glorious freedom. 🌅
## Romans 8:22
All creation groans together in childbirth pains until now. 💔
## Romans 8:23
We too, with the Spirit’s firstfruits, groan inwardly. We eagerly await adoption as sons, the redemption of our bodies. 🙏
## Romans 8:24
We were saved in this hope. Visible hope is no hope. Who hopes for what he already sees? 👁️
## Romans 8:25
If we hope for what we cannot yet see, we wait patiently. 🙏
## Romans 8:26
The Spirit helps our weakness. We do not know how to pray as we should. The Spirit Himself pleads for us with groans too deep for words. 🕊️
## Romans 8:27
The One searching our hearts knows the Spirit’s mind. The Spirit pleads for the saints according to God’s will. 🙏
## Romans 8:28
God works everything together for the good of those who love Him and are called according to His purpose. ❤️
## Romans 8:29
God predestined those He foreknew to be shaped like His Son’s image. This was so His Son would be firstborn among many brothers. 🙏
## Romans 8:30
Those He predestined, He called. Those He called, He declared righteous. Those He declared righteous, He glorified. 🌅
## Romans 8:31
What can we say? If God is for us, who can oppose us? 🛡️
## Romans 8:32
God gave His own Son for us all instead of sparing Him. How will He not freely give us everything with Him? ❤️
## Romans 8:33
Who will accuse God’s elect? God declares them righteous. ⚖️
## Romans 8:34
Who can condemn us? Christ Jesus died and rose to life. He is at God’s right hand and pleads for us. ✝️
## Romans 8:35
What will separate us from Christ’s love? Trouble, distress, persecution, famine, nakedness, danger, or sword? 🛡️
## Romans 8:36
Scripture says, “For Your sake, we face death all day. We are considered sheep for slaughter.” 📖
## Romans 8:37
Through the One who loved us, we are more than conquerors in all these things. 🛡️
## Romans 8:38
I am convinced: neither death nor life, neither angels nor principalities, neither present nor future, nor any powers, 🛡️
## Romans 8:39
neither height nor depth nor anything else in all creation can separate us from God’s love in Christ Jesus our Lord. ❤️
