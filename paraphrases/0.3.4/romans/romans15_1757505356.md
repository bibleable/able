## Romans 15:1
We who are strong should bear with the shortcomings of the weak, rather than please ourselves. 🤝
## Romans 15:2
Each of us should please his neighbor for his good, to build him up. ❤️
## Romans 15:3
Christ did not please Himself. Scripture says, “The insults of those insulting You fell on Me.” ✝️
## Romans 15:4
Everything written in the past was for our instruction. Through endurance and Scripture’s encouragement, we may have hope. 📖
## Romans 15:5
May God, who gives endurance and encouragement, give you harmony with each other in Christ Jesus. 🤝
## Romans 15:6
Then, with one mind and voice, you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. 🙏
## Romans 15:7
Accept each other as Christ accepted you, to bring God glory. ❤️
## Romans 15:8
Christ became the circumcised people’s servant for God’s truth, to confirm the promises made to the patriarchs. 📖
## Romans 15:9
This was so Gentiles might glorify God for His mercy. Scripture says, “I will praise You among Gentiles and sing hymns to Your name.” 🎵
## Romans 15:10
It also says, “Gentiles, rejoice with His people.” 🎉
## Romans 15:11
“All Gentiles, praise the Lord! All peoples, praise Him highly!” 🙏
## Romans 15:12
Isaiah says, “The Root of Jesse will appear and rise to rule Gentiles. They will put their hope in Him.” 🌱
## Romans 15:13
May the God of hope fill you with joy and peace as you believe. Through the Holy Spirit’s power, may you overflow with hope. 🕊️
## Romans 15:14
Brothers, I am convinced you are full of goodness and knowledge and can instruct each other. 🤝
## Romans 15:15
I wrote boldly to remind you about some points because of the grace God gave me. 📜
## Romans 15:16
I serve Christ Jesus among Gentiles in the priestly service of God’s good news. This is so Gentiles might become an offering acceptable to God, made holy by the Holy Spirit. 🕊️
## Romans 15:17
I therefore rejoice in Christ Jesus in my service to God. 🙏
## Romans 15:18
I will speak only of what Christ accomplished through me. He led Gentiles to obedience through word and action, 📣
## Romans 15:19
through the power of signs and wonders and the power of God’s Spirit. From Jerusalem all the way around to Illyricum, I fully announced Christ’s good news. 🌍
## Romans 15:20
I aimed to preach where Christ was unknown, so I would not build on another’s foundation. 🏗️
## Romans 15:21
Scripture says, “Those never told about Him will see. Those who never heard will understand.” 📖
## Romans 15:22
That is why I was often prevented from visiting you. 🚶
## Romans 15:23
No further opportunities remain in these regions. For many years, I longed to visit you. 🌍
## Romans 15:24
I hope to see you on my way to Spain. After enjoying your company awhile, you can equip me for the journey. 🤝
## Romans 15:25
Now I travel to Jerusalem to serve the saints there. 🚶
## Romans 15:26
Macedonia and Achaia gladly contributed to Jerusalem’s poor saints. 🎁
## Romans 15:27
They were pleased and owed this help. If Gentiles shared in their spiritual blessings, they owe them service through material blessings. 🤝
## Romans 15:28
After completing this service and delivering the gift safely, I will go to Spain by way of you. 🚶
## Romans 15:29
I know I will come with Christ’s full blessing when I visit. ❤️
## Romans 15:30
Brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and the Spirit’s love, I urge you to join my struggle through prayer to God for me. 🙏
## Romans 15:31
Pray for my rescue from Judea’s unbelievers and for Jerusalem’s saints to accept my service. 🙏
## Romans 15:32
Then, by God’s will, I may come joyfully and be refreshed with you. 🤝
## Romans 15:33
May the God of peace be with you all. Amen. 🕊️
