## Jeremiah 41:1
In the seventh month, Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son and Elishama’s grandson, came to Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son, at Mizpah with ten men. Ishmael belonged to the royal family and was one of the king’s chief officers. They ate together. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:2
Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, and his ten men rose and killed Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson, with the sword. Babylon’s king had appointed Gedaliah to govern the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:3
Ishmael also killed all the Jews with Gedaliah at Mizpah and the Chaldean soldiers there. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:4
On the second day after Gedaliah’s murder, no one else knew about it yet. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:5
Eighty men came from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria. They had shaved their beards, torn their clothes, and cut themselves. They carried grain offerings and frankincense for the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:6
Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, left Mizpah to meet them. He wept as he went. He told them, “Come to Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son.” 📖

## Jeremiah 41:7
When they entered the city, Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, and his men slaughtered them. They threw them into a cistern. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:8
Ten men said, “Do not kill us! We have hidden wheat, barley, oil, and honey in the field!” Ishmael did not kill them with the others. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:9
Ishmael threw the bodies of the men he killed along with Gedaliah into a large cistern. King Asa had made it because he feared Baasha, Israel’s king. Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, filled it with the dead. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:10
Ishmael captured everyone remaining in Mizpah, including the king’s daughters. Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, had put Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son, over them. Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, took them captive and started toward the Ammonites. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:11
Johanan, Kareah’s son, and all his army commanders heard about all the crimes of Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:12
They took all their men to fight Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son. They found him near Gibeon’s great pool. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:13
Everyone with Ishmael saw Johanan, Kareah’s son, and all his army commanders. They rejoiced. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:14
All those Ishmael captured at Mizpah turned and went to Johanan, Kareah’s son. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:15
Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, escaped Johanan with eight men. They went to the Ammonites. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:16
Johanan, Kareah’s son, and all his army commanders took everyone he recovered from Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son. Ishmael had captured them at Mizpah after killing Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son. They included soldiers, women, children, and court officials brought back from Gibeon. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:17
They went to Geruth Chimham near Bethlehem. They stayed there to prepare to go to Egypt. 📖

## Jeremiah 41:18
They wanted to escape the Chaldeans because they feared them. Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, had killed Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son, whom Babylon’s king appointed over the land. 📖
