## Jeremiah 5:1
“Go through Jerusalem’s streets. Look carefully and search her squares. If you find even one person who acts justly and seeks truth, I will forgive the city. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:2
“They say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives.’ But their oaths are false.” 📖

## Jeremiah 5:3
LORD, do Your eyes not seek truth? You struck them, but they felt no pain. You finished them off, but they refused discipline. They made their faces harder than stone and refused to repent. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:4
I thought, “These are only poor people. They act foolishly because they do not know the LORD’s way or their God’s justice. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:5
“I will speak to the powerful. Surely they know the LORD’s way and their God’s justice.” But they too had all broken the yoke and torn off the chains. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:6
So a forest lion will strike them down, and a desert wolf will ravage them. A leopard will wait near their cities. It will tear apart everyone who goes out. Their rebellious acts and unfaithful deeds are many. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:7
“Why should I forgive you? Your children have abandoned Me and sworn by gods that are not gods. I met their needs. But they committed adultery and gathered at prostitutes’ houses. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:8
“They are well-fed stallions full of desire. Each neighs after his neighbor’s wife. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:9
“Should I not punish them for this?” declares the LORD. “Should I not take vengeance on a nation like this? 📖

## Jeremiah 5:10
“Go through her vineyards and ravage them. But do not destroy them completely. Strip off her branches, because they do not belong to the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:11
“The houses of Israel and Judah have been completely unfaithful to Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:12
They have lied about the LORD. They say, “He will do nothing. No harm will come to us. We will face neither sword nor famine. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:13
“The prophets are only wind. The word is not in them. Let their own predictions happen to them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 5:14
So the LORD God of Hosts says, “Because you said this, I will make My words a fire in your mouth. These people will be the wood it consumes. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:15
“House of Israel, I am bringing a distant nation against you,” declares the LORD. “It is an established, ancient nation. You do not know its language or understand its speech. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:16
“Their quivers are like open graves. All their men are mighty. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:17
“They will devour your harvest and food, your sons and daughters. They will eat your flocks, herds, vines, and fig trees. With the sword, they will destroy the fortified cities you trust.” 📖

## Jeremiah 5:18
“Even in those days,” declares the LORD, “I will not destroy you completely. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:19
“The people will ask, ‘What offense made the LORD our God do all this to us?’ Tell them, ‘You abandoned Me and served foreign gods in your land. In the same way, you will serve foreigners in a land that is not yours.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 5:20
Announce this to Jacob’s house. Proclaim it in Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:21
“Listen, foolish and senseless people. You have eyes but do not see. You have ears but do not hear. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:22
“Do you not fear Me or tremble before Me?” declares the LORD. “I made the sand the sea’s boundary, a lasting barrier it cannot cross. The waves surge but cannot overcome it. They roar but cannot cross it. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:23
“But these people have stubborn, rebellious hearts. They have turned aside and gone away. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:24
“They do not say in their hearts, ‘Let us fear the LORD our God. He gives autumn and spring rains at the proper times. He preserves the appointed harvest weeks for us.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 5:25
“Your wrongdoing has turned these things away from you. Your sins have kept My abundance from you. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:26
“Wicked men live among My people. They watch like bird catchers waiting in hiding. They set traps to catch people. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:27
“Their houses are full of deceit, like cages full of birds. So they have become rich and powerful. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:28
“They have grown fat and sleek. They excel in wicked deeds. They do not defend the fatherless so that they may prosper. They do not defend the rights of the needy. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:29
“Should I not punish them for this?” declares the LORD. “Should I not take vengeance on a nation like this? 📖

## Jeremiah 5:30
“Something horrible and shocking has happened in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 5:31
“The prophets prophesy falsely. The priests rule by their own authority. My people love this. But what will you do in the end?” 📖
