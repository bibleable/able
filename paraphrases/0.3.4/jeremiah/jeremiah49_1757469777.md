## Jeremiah 49:1
The LORD says about the Ammonites, “Does Israel have no sons or heir? Why has Milcom taken Gad’s land? Why do his people live in their cities? 📖

## Jeremiah 49:2
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will sound the battle cry against Ammon’s Rabbah. It will become a pile of ruins. Its villages will burn. Israel will drive out those who took their land,” says the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:3
“Heshbon, wail, because Ai is destroyed! Daughters of Rabbah, cry out! Wear sackcloth and mourn. Run back and forth inside your walls. Milcom will go into exile with his priests and officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:4
“Faithless daughter, why boast about your fruitful valleys? You trust your riches and ask, ‘Who can attack me?’ 📖

## Jeremiah 49:5
“I will bring terror from everyone around you,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts. “Each of you will be driven away headlong. No one will gather the fugitives. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:6
“But afterward, I will restore the Ammonites from captivity,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:7
The LORD of Hosts says about Edom, “Is there no wisdom left in Teman? Have the prudent lost their counsel? Has their wisdom decayed? 📖

## Jeremiah 49:8
“People of Dedan, turn and flee! Lie low. I will bring disaster on Esau when I punish him. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:9
“If grape gatherers came, would they not leave some grapes? If thieves came at night, would they not take only what they wanted? 📖

## Jeremiah 49:10
“But I will strip Esau bare and uncover his hiding places. He will be unable to hide. His descendants, relatives, and neighbors will be destroyed. He will be no more. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:11
“Leave your orphans. I will keep them alive. Let your widows trust Me.” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:12
The LORD says, “If those who do not deserve the cup must drink it, can you escape punishment? You will not escape. You must drink it too. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:13
“I swear by Myself,” declares the LORD, “that Bozrah will become desolate, disgraced, ruined, and cursed. All her cities will lie ruined forever.” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:14
I have heard a message from the LORD. An envoy has gone to the nations. “Gather to march against her! Rise for battle!” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:15
“I will make you small among nations and despised among people. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:16
“You live in rock crevices and occupy the mountain’s summit. The terror you cause and your proud heart have deceived you. Even if you raise your nest like an eagle, I will bring you down,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:17
“Edom will become an object of horror. Everyone passing will be horrified and mock all her wounds. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:18
“As Sodom, Gomorrah, and their neighbors were overthrown,” says the LORD, “no one will live or stay there. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:19
“Someone will come like a lion from the Jordan’s thickets to the watered pasture. I will drive Edom from her land in an instant. Whom will I choose for this? Who is like Me or can challenge Me? What shepherd can oppose Me?” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:20
Hear the LORD’s plans against Edom and His strategies against Teman’s people. The flock’s little ones will certainly be dragged away. Their pasture will certainly be made desolate because of them. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:21
The sound of their fall will shake the earth. Their cry will reach the Red Sea. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:22
Look! An eagle will soar and swoop down, spreading its wings over Bozrah. On that day, Edom’s mighty men will have hearts like a woman giving birth. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:23
About Damascus: “Hamath and Arpad are shamed because they heard bad news. They are troubled like the sea. Their anxiety cannot be calmed. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:24
“Damascus has become weak and turned to flee. Panic grips her. Anguish and pain seize her like a woman giving birth. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:25
“How is the city of praise not abandoned, the town that gives Me joy? 📖

## Jeremiah 49:26
“Her young men will fall in the streets. All her warriors will fall silent on that day,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:27
“I will light a fire in Damascus’s walls. It will consume Ben-hadad’s fortresses.” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:28
The LORD speaks about Kedar and Hazor’s kingdoms, which Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, defeated. “Rise and advance against Kedar! Destroy the people of the east! 📖

## Jeremiah 49:29
“They will take their tents, flocks, tent curtains, goods, and camels. They will shout to them, ‘Terror is everywhere!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 49:30
“People of Hazor, run and escape quickly! Lie low,” declares the LORD. “Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, has made plans and strategies against you. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:31
“Rise and attack a nation living at ease and in security,” declares the LORD. “They live alone without gates or bars. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:32
“Their camels and large herds will become plunder. I will scatter those who shave their temples to the wind in every direction. I will bring disaster on them from every side,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:33
“Hazor will become a home for jackals, desolate forever. No one will live or stay there.” 📖

## Jeremiah 49:34
This word about Elam came from the LORD to the prophet Jeremiah early in the reign of Zedekiah, Judah’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:35
The LORD of Hosts says, “I will break Elam’s bow, the main support of their strength. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:36
“I will bring four winds against Elam from heaven’s four corners. I will scatter them to all those winds. Elam’s exiles will go to every nation. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:37
“I will shatter Elam before enemies who want to kill them. I will bring disaster, My fierce anger, on them,” declares the LORD. “I will send the sword after them until I finish them off. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:38
“I will set My throne in Elam and destroy its king and officials,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 49:39
“But in the last days, I will restore Elam from captivity,” declares the LORD. 📖
