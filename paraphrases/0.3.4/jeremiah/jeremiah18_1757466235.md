## Jeremiah 18:1
This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:2
“Go to the potter’s house at once. I will give you My message there.” 📖

## Jeremiah 18:3
I went to the potter’s house. He was working at the wheel. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:4
The clay vessel he was shaping became flawed in his hand. So he made it into another vessel, as he thought best. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:5
The LORD’s word came to me. He said, 📖

## Jeremiah 18:6
“House of Israel, can I not treat you as this potter treats his clay?” declares the LORD. “You are in My hand like clay in the potter’s hand, house of Israel. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:7
“At any time, I may announce that I will uproot, tear down, and destroy a nation or kingdom. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:8
“But if the nation I warned turns from evil, I will change My mind about the disaster I planned. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:9
“At another time, I may announce that I will build up and establish a nation or kingdom. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:10
“But if it does evil before Me and disobeys My voice, I will change My mind about the good I intended for it. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:11
“Tell Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s people what the LORD says. ‘I am preparing disaster and making plans against you. Each of you must turn from your evil ways. Correct your ways and deeds.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 18:12
“But they will answer, ‘There is no hope. We will follow our own plans. Each of us will follow his stubborn, evil heart.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 18:13
So the LORD says, “Ask among the nations. Who has heard anything like this? Virgin Israel has done something terrible. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:14
“Does Lebanon’s snow ever leave its rocky slopes? Do its cool waters flowing from a distance ever dry up? 📖

## Jeremiah 18:15
“Yet My people have forgotten Me. They burn incense to worthless idols that make them stumble in their ways. They leave the ancient roads. They walk on rutted side paths instead of the highway. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:16
“They have made their land desolate and an everlasting object of scorn. Everyone who passes will be horrified and shake his head. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:17
“I will scatter them before the enemy like the east wind. On their day of disaster, I will show them My back instead of My face.” 📖

## Jeremiah 18:18
Some people said, “Let us plan against Jeremiah. Priests will still have the law, wise men will still have counsel, and prophets will still have oracles. Let us denounce him and ignore everything he says.” 📖

## Jeremiah 18:19
LORD, pay attention to me. Hear what my accusers say! 📖

## Jeremiah 18:20
Should evil repay good? They have dug a pit for me. Remember how I stood before You and spoke for their good. I wanted to turn Your wrath away from them. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:21
Hand their children over to famine. Pour the sword’s power on them. Let their wives lose their children and husbands. Let disease kill their husbands. Let the sword strike down their young men in battle. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:22
Let cries come from their houses when You suddenly bring raiders against them. They dug a pit to catch me. They hid snares for my feet. 📖

## Jeremiah 18:23
LORD, You know all their plots to kill me. Do not wipe away their guilt or remove their sin from Your sight. Let them be overthrown before You. Deal with them in Your time of anger. 📖
