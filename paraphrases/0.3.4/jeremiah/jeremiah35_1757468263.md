## Jeremiah 35:1
This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD during the reign of Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:2
“Speak to the Rechabites at their house. Bring them to a room in the LORD’s house and offer them wine.” 📖

## Jeremiah 35:3
I took Jaazaniah, Jeremiah’s son and Habazziniah’s grandson, with his brothers and all his sons. This was the entire Rechabite house. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:4
I brought them to the LORD’s house, to the room of the sons of Hanan, Igdaliah’s son, a man of God. It was near the officials’ room. That room was above the room of Maaseiah, Shallum’s son, the doorkeeper. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:5
I put pitchers full of wine and some cups before the Rechabite men. I said, “Drink some wine.” 📖

## Jeremiah 35:6
They answered, “We do not drink wine. Our forefather Jonadab, Rechab’s son, commanded, ‘Neither you nor your descendants must ever drink wine. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:7
“‘Never build houses, sow seed, or plant vineyards. These things are not for you. Live in tents throughout your lives. Then you may live long in the land where you wander.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 35:8
“We obeyed everything our forefather Jonadab, Rechab’s son, commanded. Neither we, our wives, our sons, nor our daughters have ever drunk wine. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:9
“We have built no houses to live in. We have owned no vineyards, fields, or crops. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:10
“We have lived in tents. We have obeyed our forefather Jonadab’s commands exactly. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:11
“When Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, entered the land, we said, ‘Let us go to Jerusalem to escape the Chaldean and Aramean armies.’ We have stayed in Jerusalem.” 📖

## Jeremiah 35:12
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:13
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. Tell Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s people, ‘Will you not accept discipline and obey My words?’ declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:14
“Jonadab, Rechab’s son, commanded his sons not to drink wine. They have obeyed their forefather and have not drunk it to this day. But I have spoken to you repeatedly, and you disobeyed Me! 📖

## Jeremiah 35:15
“I repeatedly sent all My servants the prophets. They said, ‘Each must turn from his wicked ways and correct his actions. Do not serve other gods. Live in the land I gave you and your fathers.’ But you refused to pay attention or listen to Me. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:16
“The sons of Jonadab, Rechab’s son, obeyed their forefather’s command. But these people have not listened to Me. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:17
“So the LORD God of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. ‘I will bring all the disaster I announced on Judah and everyone in Jerusalem. I spoke, but they disobeyed. I called, but they did not answer.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 35:18
Jeremiah told the Rechabites, “The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. ‘You obeyed your forefather Jonadab. You kept all his commands and did everything he ordered. 📖

## Jeremiah 35:19
“‘So the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says that Jonadab, Rechab’s son, will always have a man to stand before Me.’” 📖
