## Jeremiah 45:1
The prophet Jeremiah spoke this word to Baruch, Neriah’s son, while Baruch wrote these words on a scroll at Jeremiah’s dictation. It was the fourth year of Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 45:2
“Baruch, the LORD, Israel’s God, says this to you. 📖

## Jeremiah 45:3
“You said, ‘How terrible for me! The LORD has added sorrow to my pain. Groaning has exhausted me, and I have found no rest.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 45:4
Jeremiah was to tell Baruch, “The LORD says, ‘Throughout the land, I will tear down what I built and uproot what I planted. 📖

## Jeremiah 45:5
“‘Do you seek great things for yourself? Stop seeking them! I will bring disaster on every living creature,’ declares the LORD. ‘But wherever you go, I will give you your life like plunder from war.’” 📖
