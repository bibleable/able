## Jeremiah 16:1
The LORD’s word came to me. He said, 📖

## Jeremiah 16:2
“Do not marry or have sons or daughters in this place.” 📖

## Jeremiah 16:3
The LORD speaks about the sons and daughters born here. He also speaks about the mothers and fathers who had them in this land. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:4
“They will die from deadly diseases. No one will mourn or bury them. They will lie like dung on the ground. Sword and famine will finish them off. Their corpses will feed the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.” 📖

## Jeremiah 16:5
The LORD says, “Do not enter a house with a funeral meal. Do not go to mourn or show sympathy. I have taken My peace, loving devotion, and compassion from these people,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:6
“Great and small alike will die in this land. No one will bury or mourn them. No one will cut himself or shave his head for them. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:7
“No one will offer food to comfort those who mourn the dead. No one will give a cup of consolation, even for a father or mother. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:8
“Do not enter a house with feasting to sit, eat, and drink with them. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:9
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. In your lifetime, before your eyes, I will remove joyful, glad sounds from this place. I will remove the voices of brides and bridegrooms. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:10
“When you tell these people all this, they will question you. ‘Why has the LORD announced such great disaster against us? What wrong have we done? What sin have we committed against the LORD our God?’ 📖

## Jeremiah 16:11
“Answer them, ‘Your fathers abandoned Me,’ declares the LORD. ‘They followed other gods, which they served and worshiped. They abandoned Me and did not keep My instruction. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:12
“‘You have done more evil than your fathers. Each of you follows his stubborn, evil heart instead of obeying Me. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:13
“‘I will throw you out of this land into a land neither you nor your fathers have known. There you will serve other gods day and night. I will show you no favor.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 16:14
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when people will no longer use this oath. ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites out of Egypt.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 16:15
“Instead, they will say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites from the northern land and every land where He banished them.’ I will return them to their land, which I gave their forefathers. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:16
“For now, I will send for many fishermen,” declares the LORD. “They will catch them. Afterward, I will send many hunters. They will hunt them on every mountain and hill, even in cracks in the rocks. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:17
“My eyes see all their ways. They cannot hide from My face. Their guilt cannot hide from My eyes. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:18
“First, I will repay them double for their wrongdoing and sin. They defiled My land with the carcasses of their detestable idols. They filled My inheritance with abominations.” 📖

## Jeremiah 16:19
LORD, You are my strength, fortress, and shelter in times of distress. Nations will come to You from the ends of the earth. They will say, “Our fathers inherited only lies and worthless idols that help no one. 📖

## Jeremiah 16:20
“Can people make gods for themselves? These are not gods!” 📖

## Jeremiah 16:21
“So I will teach them. This time, I will make them know My power and might. They will know that My name is the LORD. 📖
