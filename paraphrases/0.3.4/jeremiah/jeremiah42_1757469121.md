## Jeremiah 42:1
All the army commanders, Johanan, Kareah’s son, Jezaniah, Hoshaiah’s son, and all the people, from least to greatest, approached. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:2
They told the prophet Jeremiah, “Please hear our request. Pray to the LORD your God for all of us who remain. You can see that only a few remain from the many. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:3
“Pray that the LORD your God will tell us where to go and what to do.” 📖

## Jeremiah 42:4
The prophet Jeremiah answered, “I have heard you. I will certainly pray to the LORD your God as you ask. I will tell you everything He answers. I will hide nothing.” 📖

## Jeremiah 42:5
They told Jeremiah, “May the LORD be a true and faithful witness against us if we disobey any word He sends through you. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:6
“Whether pleasant or unpleasant, we will obey the LORD our God, to whom we send you. Then things will go well for us as we obey His voice!” 📖

## Jeremiah 42:7
After ten days, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:8
He called Johanan, Kareah’s son, all his army commanders, and all the people, from least to greatest. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:9
Jeremiah told them, “You sent me to present your request to the LORD, Israel’s God. He says, 📖

## Jeremiah 42:10
“‘If you stay in this land, I will build you up instead of tearing you down. I will plant you instead of uprooting you. I will change My mind about the disaster I brought on you. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:11
“‘Do not fear Babylon’s king, whom you now fear,’ declares the LORD. ‘I am with you to save you and rescue you from him. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:12
“‘I will show you compassion. He will have compassion on you and restore you to your own land.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:13
“But suppose you say, ‘We will not stay here,’ and disobey the LORD your God. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:14
“Suppose you say, ‘We will live in Egypt instead. There we will see no war, hear no ram’s horn, and have no hunger for bread.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:15
“Then hear the LORD’s word, Judah’s survivors! The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘If you insist on going to Egypt to live, 📖

## Jeremiah 42:16
“‘then the sword you fear will catch you there. The famine you dread will follow close behind you into Egypt. You will die there. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:17
“‘Everyone determined to settle in Egypt will die by sword, famine, and plague. None will survive or escape the disaster I bring.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:18
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. ‘I poured My anger and wrath on Jerusalem’s people. In the same way, I will pour My wrath on you if you go to Egypt. You will become an object of curses, horror, insults, and disgrace. You will never see this place again.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:19
“Judah’s survivors, the LORD told you, ‘Do not go to Egypt.’ Be certain that I have warned you today! 📖

## Jeremiah 42:20
“You deceived yourselves when you sent me to the LORD your God. You said, ‘Pray to the LORD our God for us. Tell us everything He says, and we will do it.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 42:21
“I have told you today. But you have not obeyed the LORD your God in everything He sent me to tell you. 📖

## Jeremiah 42:22
“Be certain of this. Sword, famine, and plague will kill you in the place where you want to settle.” 📖
