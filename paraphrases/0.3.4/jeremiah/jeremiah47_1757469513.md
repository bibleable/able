## Jeremiah 47:1
This word about the Philistines came from the LORD to the prophet Jeremiah before Pharaoh attacked Gaza. 📖

## Jeremiah 47:2
The LORD says, “Waters rise from the north and become an overflowing flood. They will cover the land and everything in it, the cities and their people. Everyone in the land will cry and wail. 📖

## Jeremiah 47:3
“They will hear galloping stallions’ hooves, rumbling chariots, and clattering wheels. Fathers will not turn back for their sons. Their hands will hang limp. 📖

## Jeremiah 47:4
“The day has come to destroy all the Philistines. Every remaining ally will be cut off from Tyre and Sidon. The LORD will destroy the Philistines, those remaining from Caphtor’s coasts. 📖

## Jeremiah 47:5
“Gaza’s people will shave their heads in mourning. Ashkelon will fall silent. You who remain in their valley, how long will you cut yourselves? 📖

## Jeremiah 47:6
“‘Alas, sword of the LORD! How long until you rest? Return to your sheath. Stop and be still!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 47:7
“How can it rest? The LORD has commanded it. He appointed it against Ashkelon and its coast.” 📖
