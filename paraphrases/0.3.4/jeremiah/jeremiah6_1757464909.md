## Jeremiah 6:1
“Sons of Benjamin, flee Jerusalem and run for shelter! Blow the ram’s horn in Tekoa. Raise a signal over Beth-haccherem. Disaster and great destruction threaten from the north. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:2
“I will destroy the Daughter of Zion, although she is beautiful and delicate. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:3
“Shepherds and their flocks will come against her. They will set their tents all around her. Each will tend his own portion. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:4
“They say, ‘Prepare for battle against her! Get up and attack at noon! How terrible for us! Daylight is fading, and the evening shadows grow long. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:5
“‘Get up! Let us attack at night and destroy her fortresses!’” 📖

## Jeremiah 6:6
The LORD of Hosts says, “Cut down trees and build a siege ramp against Jerusalem. This city must be punished. It is full of oppression. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:7
“She pours out evil as a well pours out water. Violence and destruction sound within her. Sickness and wounds are always before Me. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:8
“Jerusalem, accept this warning, or I will turn away from you. I will make you a desolate land where no one lives.” 📖

## Jeremiah 6:9
The LORD of Hosts says, “Glean what remains of Israel as thoroughly as a vine. Pass your hand over the branches again, like a grape gatherer.” 📖

## Jeremiah 6:10
Whom can I warn? Who will listen to me? Their ears are closed, and they cannot hear. The LORD’s word offends them. They take no pleasure in it. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:11
I am full of the LORD’s wrath. I am tired of holding it back. “Pour it over the children in the street and the young men gathered together. Husband and wife will be captured, along with the old and very old. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:12
“Others will take their houses, fields, and wives. I will stretch out My hand against those who live in the land,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:13
“From the least to the greatest, all are greedy for gain. From prophet to priest, all practice deceit. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:14
“They treat My people’s wound with little care. They say, ‘Peace, peace,’ although there is no peace. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:15
“Are they ashamed of the abomination they committed? No, they have no shame and do not know how to blush. They will fall among the fallen. When I punish them, they will collapse,” says the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:16
The LORD says, “Stand at the crossroads and look. Ask for the ancient paths and the good way. Walk there, and you will find rest for your souls. But they said, ‘We will not walk there!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 6:17
“I appointed watchmen over you. I said, ‘Listen for the ram’s horn.’ But they answered, ‘We will not listen!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 6:18
“So listen, nations. Learn, congregations, what will happen to them. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:19
“Listen, earth! I am bringing disaster on these people as the fruit of their own schemes. They ignored My word and rejected My instruction. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:20
“What use is frankincense from Sheba to Me? What use is sweet cane from a distant land? I do not accept your burnt offerings. Your sacrifices do not please Me.” 📖

## Jeremiah 6:21
So the LORD says, “I will put stumbling blocks before these people. Fathers and sons alike will stumble. Friends and neighbors will perish.” 📖

## Jeremiah 6:22
The LORD says, “An army comes from the northern land. A great nation rises from the ends of the earth. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:23
“They carry bows and spears. They are cruel and show no mercy. Their voices roar like the sea, and they ride horses. Daughter of Zion, they form battle lines against you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 6:24
We have heard the report. Our hands hang limp. Anguish grips us, with pain like a woman giving birth. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:25
Do not go to the fields or walk along the road. The enemy has a sword. Terror is on every side. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:26
Daughter of my people, put on sackcloth and roll in ashes. Mourn with bitter cries, as for an only son. The destroyer will suddenly come against us. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:27
“I have appointed you to examine My people like ore. You will know and test their ways. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:28
“They are all hardened rebels who go about slandering others. They are bronze and iron. All are corrupt. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:29
“The bellows blow fiercely. Fire blasts away the lead. The refining achieves nothing, because the wicked are not removed. 📖

## Jeremiah 6:30
“They are called rejected silver, because the LORD has rejected them.” 📖
