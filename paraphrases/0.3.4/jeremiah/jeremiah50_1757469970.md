## Jeremiah 50:1
The LORD spoke this word through the prophet Jeremiah about Babylon and the Chaldeans’ land. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:2
“Announce it among the nations. Raise a banner and hide nothing. Say, ‘Babylon is captured. Bel is shamed, and Marduk is shattered. Her images are disgraced, and her idols are broken.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 50:3
“A northern nation will attack her and make her land desolate. No one will live there. People and animals will flee.” 📖

## Jeremiah 50:4
“In those days, at that time,” declares the LORD, “Israel’s children and Judah’s children will come together. They will weep as they come and seek the LORD their God. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:5
“They will ask the way to Zion and turn toward it. They will join themselves to the LORD in an everlasting covenant that will never be forgotten. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:6
“My people are lost sheep. Their shepherds led them astray and made them roam the mountains. They wandered from mountain to hill and forgot their resting place. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:7
“Everyone who found them devoured them. Their enemies said, ‘We are not guilty. They sinned against the LORD, their true pasture and their fathers’ hope.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 50:8
“Flee Babylon and leave the Chaldeans’ land. Be like male goats leading the flock. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:9
“I will stir up a gathering of great northern nations and bring them against Babylon. They will form battle lines against her. She will be captured from the north. Their arrows will be like skilled warriors who never return empty-handed. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:10
“Chaldea will be plundered. All who plunder her will take their fill,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:11
“You plunder My inheritance and rejoice in triumph. You play like a heifer treading grain and neigh like stallions. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:12
“Because of this, your mother who bore you will suffer great shame and disgrace. She will become the least nation, a dry wilderness and desert. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:13
“The LORD’s wrath will make her completely desolate, with no one living there. Everyone passing through Babylon will be horrified and hiss at all her wounds. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:14
“Archers, form battle lines around Babylon! Shoot at her and spare no arrows. She has sinned against the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:15
“Shout against her from every side! She has raised her hands to surrender. Her towers have fallen, and her walls are torn down. This is the LORD’s vengeance. Take vengeance and do to her what she did to others. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:16
“Cut off Babylon’s sowers and harvesters who use sickles. Before the oppressor’s sword, everyone will flee to his own people and native land. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:17
“Israel is a scattered flock driven away by lions. Assyria’s king devoured him first. Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, crushed his bones last.” 📖

## Jeremiah 50:18
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “I will punish Babylon’s king and his land as I punished Assyria’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:19
“I will return Israel to his pasture. He will graze on Carmel and Bashan. His soul will be satisfied on Ephraim and Gilead’s hills. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:20
“In those days, at that time,” declares the LORD, “people will search for Israel’s guilt and Judah’s sins. They will find none. I will forgive the survivors I preserve. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:21
“Attack Merathaim’s land and Pekod’s people. Kill them and devote them to destruction. Do everything I command,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:22
“The noise of battle and great destruction fills the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:23
“The hammer of the whole earth lies broken and shattered! Babylon has become a horror among the nations! 📖

## Jeremiah 50:24
“Babylon, I set a trap for you. You were caught before you knew it. You were found and captured because you challenged the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:25
“The LORD opened His armory and brought out His weapons of wrath. The Lord GOD of Hosts has work in the Chaldeans’ land. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:26
“Come against her from the farthest border. Break open her grain stores. Pile her up like heaps of grain. Devote her to destruction and leave no survivors. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:27
“Kill all her young bulls. Let them go to slaughter. How terrible for them! Their day of punishment has come. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:28
Listen to fugitives and refugees from Babylon’s land. In Zion, they announce the LORD our God’s vengeance, vengeance for His temple. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:29
“Call all archers against Babylon. Camp all around her and let no one escape. Repay her for her deeds. Do to her what she did to others. She defied the LORD, Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:30
“So her young men will fall in the streets. All her warriors will fall silent on that day,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:31
“Arrogant one, I am against you,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts. “Your day has come, the time for My punishment. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:32
“The arrogant one will stumble and fall. No one will lift him. I will light a fire in his cities to consume everyone around him.” 📖

## Jeremiah 50:33
The LORD of Hosts says, “Israel’s sons and Judah’s sons are oppressed. All their captors hold them tightly and refuse to free them. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:34
“Their Redeemer is strong. His name is the LORD of Hosts. He will plead their case earnestly. He will bring rest to the earth but turmoil to Babylon’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:35
“A sword comes against the Chaldeans,” declares the LORD. “It comes against Babylon’s people, officials, and wise men. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:36
“A sword comes against her false prophets. They will become fools. A sword comes against her warriors. They will be terrified. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:37
“A sword comes against her horses, chariots, and all the foreigners among her. They will become like women. A sword comes against her treasures. They will be plundered. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:38
“Drought comes on her waters. They will dry up. This land is full of carved images. Its people go mad over idols. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:39
“Desert creatures, hyenas, and ostriches will live there. People will never live there again, from generation to generation. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:40
“As God overthrew Sodom, Gomorrah, and their neighbors,” declares the LORD, “no one will live or stay there. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:41
“An army comes from the north. A great nation and many kings rise from earth’s ends. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:42
“They carry bows and spears. They are cruel and show no mercy. Their voices roar like the sea. They ride horses and form battle lines against you, Daughter of Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:43
“Babylon’s king heard the report. His hands hang limp. Anguish grips him, with pain like a woman giving birth. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:44
“Someone will come like a lion from the Jordan’s thickets to the watered pasture. I will drive Babylon from her land in an instant. Whom will I choose for this? Who is like Me or can challenge Me? What shepherd can oppose Me?” 📖

## Jeremiah 50:45
Hear the LORD’s plans against Babylon and His strategies against the Chaldeans’ land. The flock’s little ones will certainly be dragged away. Their pasture will certainly become desolate because of them. 📖

## Jeremiah 50:46
The sound of Babylon’s capture will shake the earth. A cry will be heard among the nations. 📖
