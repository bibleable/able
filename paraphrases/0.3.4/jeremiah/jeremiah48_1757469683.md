## Jeremiah 48:1
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says about Moab, “How terrible for Nebo! It will be devastated. Kiriathaim will be captured and shamed. The fortress will be broken and torn apart. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:2
“No one praises Moab anymore. In Heshbon, people plot against her. ‘Let us destroy her as a nation.’ People of Madmen, you too will fall silent. The sword will pursue you. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:3
“A voice cries from Horonaim, ‘Devastation and great destruction!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 48:4
“Moab will be shattered. Her little ones will cry. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:5
“People weep bitterly as they climb toward Luhith. As they descend toward Horonaim, cries of distress sound over the destruction. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:6
“‘Flee! Run for your lives! Become like a juniper in the desert.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 48:7
“You trust your works and treasures, so you too will be captured. Chemosh will go into exile with his priests and officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:8
“The destroyer will attack every city. No town will escape. The valley and high plain will be ruined, as the LORD said. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:9
“Put salt on Moab, because she will be laid waste. Her cities will become desolate, with no one living there. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:10
“A curse is on anyone careless in doing the LORD’s work. A curse is on anyone who keeps his sword from bloodshed. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:11
“Moab has lived at ease since youth, like wine settled on its dregs. He has not been poured from one vessel to another or gone into exile. His flavor and smell have stayed the same. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:12
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will send wanderers to pour him out. They will empty his vessels and break his jars. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:13
“Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh. Israel’s house was ashamed in the same way when they trusted Bethel. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:14
“How can you say, ‘We are warriors, mighty men ready for battle’? 📖

## Jeremiah 48:15
“Moab is destroyed, and its towns are invaded. Its best young men have gone down to slaughter,” declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:16
“Moab’s disaster is near. His suffering rushes toward him. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:17
“Everyone around him, everyone who knows his name, mourn for him. Say, ‘The mighty scepter, the glorious staff, is broken!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 48:18
“Daughter living in Dibon, come down from your glory. Sit on dry ground. Moab’s destroyer has attacked you and destroyed your fortresses. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:19
“You who live in Aroer, stand beside the road and watch. Ask fleeing men and escaping women, ‘What happened?’ 📖

## Jeremiah 48:20
“Moab is shamed because it is shattered. Wail and cry! Announce beside the Arnon that Moab is destroyed. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:21
“Judgment has come to the high plain, to Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:22
“It has come to Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:23
“It has come to Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:24
“It has come to Kerioth, Bozrah, and all Moab’s towns, far and near. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:25
“Moab’s horn is cut off, and his arm is broken,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:26
“Make him drunk, because he made himself great against the LORD. Moab will roll in his own vomit. He too will become a laughingstock. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:27
“Did you not ridicule Israel? Was he ever found among thieves? You shake your head whenever you speak about him. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:28
“People of Moab, leave the towns and settle among rocks. Be like a dove nesting at a cave’s entrance. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:29
“We have heard of Moab’s great pride and conceit. We have heard of his arrogance and proud heart. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:30
“I know his insolence,” declares the LORD, “but it achieves nothing. His boasting is as empty as his deeds. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:31
“I will wail and cry for all Moab. I will moan for the men of Kir-heres. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:32
“Vine of Sibmah, I will weep for you more than for Jazer. Your tendrils stretch to the sea and reach Jazer. The destroyer has attacked your summer fruit and grape harvest. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:33
“Joy and gladness have left Moab’s orchard and fields. I stopped the wine flowing from the presses. No one treads them with joyful shouts. Their shouts are not joyful. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:34
“Cries sound from Heshbon to Elealeh and Jahaz. Voices rise from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah. Even Nimrim’s waters have dried up. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:35
“I will end those in Moab who offer sacrifices on high places and burn incense to their gods,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:36
“My heart laments like a flute for Moab and the men of Kir-heres. The wealth they gained has perished. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:37
“Every head is shaved, and every beard is clipped. Every hand has a cut. Every waist has sackcloth. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:38
“Everyone mourns on all Moab’s rooftops and in its public squares. I shattered Moab like an unwanted jar,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:39
“How shattered it is! How they wail! Moab has turned his back in shame. He has become an object of ridicule and horror to everyone around him.” 📖

## Jeremiah 48:40
The LORD says, “An eagle swoops down. It spreads its wings against Moab. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:41
“Kirioth is taken, and the strongholds are seized. On that day, Moab’s warriors will have hearts like a woman giving birth. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:42
“Moab will be destroyed as a nation because he made himself great against the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:43
“People of Moab, terror, pit, and snare await you,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:44
“Whoever flees panic will fall into the pit. Whoever climbs out of the pit will be caught in the snare. I will bring Moab’s year of punishment,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:45
“Fugitives stand helpless in Heshbon’s shadow. Fire has come from Heshbon, a flame from within Sihon. It devours Moab’s foreheads and the skulls of the sons of tumult. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:46
“How terrible for you, Moab! Chemosh’s people have perished. Your sons and daughters have gone into exile and captivity. 📖

## Jeremiah 48:47
“But in the latter days, I will restore Moab from captivity,” declares the LORD. Here ends the judgment on Moab. 📖
