## Jeremiah 3:1
“If a man divorces his wife and she marries another man, can he return to her? Would that not completely defile the land? But you have acted as a prostitute with many lovers. Would you return to Me?” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:2
“Look up at the bare heights. Is there any place where you have not been violated? You waited for lovers beside the highways, like a desert nomad. Your prostitution and wickedness have defiled the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:3
“That is why the showers have been withheld and no spring rains have fallen. Yet you have a prostitute’s bold look. You refuse to feel shame. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:4
“Have you not just called to Me, ‘My Father, You have been my friend since youth. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:5
“‘Will He stay angry forever? Will His anger last to the end?’ You have said this, but you keep doing all the evil you can.” 📖

## Jeremiah 3:6
During King Josiah’s reign, the LORD told me, “Have you seen what faithless Israel has done? She has prostituted herself on every high hill and under every green tree. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:7
“I thought she would return to Me after doing all this. But she did not return. Her unfaithful sister Judah saw it. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:8
“Judah saw that I gave faithless Israel a divorce certificate and sent her away because she committed adultery. But her unfaithful sister Judah had no fear. She also prostituted herself. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:9
“Israel did not care about her unfaithfulness. She defiled the land and committed adultery with stones and trees. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:10
“Despite all this, her unfaithful sister Judah did not return to Me with her whole heart. She only pretended,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:11
The LORD told me, “Faithless Israel has shown herself more righteous than unfaithful Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:12
“Go and announce this message toward the north. ‘Return, faithless Israel,’ declares the LORD. ‘I will no longer look at you with anger. I am merciful,’ declares the LORD. ‘I will not stay angry forever. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:13
“‘Only admit your guilt. You have rebelled against the LORD your God. You have spread your favors among foreign gods under every green tree. You have not obeyed My voice,’” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:14
“Return, faithless children,” declares the LORD. “I am your master. I will take one from a city and two from a family. I will bring you to Zion. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:15
“I will give you shepherds who follow My heart. They will feed you with knowledge and understanding.” 📖

## Jeremiah 3:16
“In those days you will multiply and increase in the land,” declares the LORD. “People will no longer discuss the ark of the LORD’s covenant. It will not come to mind. No one will remember or miss it. No one will make another one. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:17
“Then they will call Jerusalem ‘The Throne of the LORD.’ All nations will gather there to honor the LORD’s name. They will no longer follow their stubborn, evil hearts. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:18
“In those days, Judah’s house will walk with Israel’s house. Together they will come from the northern land. They will return to the land I gave your fathers as an inheritance. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:19
“I said, ‘I long to make you My sons. I want to give you a desirable land, the most beautiful inheritance among all nations!’ I thought you would call Me ‘Father’ and never stop following Me. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:20
“But you have betrayed Me, house of Israel, as a woman betrays her husband,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:21
A voice comes from the bare heights. Israel’s children weep and beg for mercy. They have twisted their ways and forgotten the LORD their God. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:22
“Return, faithless children. I will heal your faithlessness.” “Here we are. We come to You because You are the LORD our God. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:23
“Deception comes from the hills and commotion from the mountains. Israel’s salvation is surely in the LORD our God. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:24
“Since our youth, that shameful god has consumed the products of our fathers’ work. He has consumed their flocks, herds, sons, and daughters. 📖

## Jeremiah 3:25
“Let us lie in our shame and let disgrace cover us. We and our fathers have sinned against the LORD our God. From our youth until today, we have not obeyed His voice.” 📖
