## Jeremiah 52:1
Zedekiah became king at twenty-one. He ruled in Jerusalem for eleven years. His mother was Hamutal, Jeremiah’s daughter, from Libnah. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:2
Zedekiah did evil before the LORD, as Jehoiakim had done. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:3
All this happened in Jerusalem and Judah because of the LORD’s anger. Finally, He banished them from His presence. Zedekiah also rebelled against Babylon’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:4
On the tenth day of the tenth month of Zedekiah’s ninth year, Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, marched against Jerusalem with his entire army. They camped outside and built a siege wall around it. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:5
The siege continued until King Zedekiah’s eleventh year. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:6
By the ninth day of the fourth month, famine was so severe that the land’s people had no food. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:7
The city’s defenses were breached. The Chaldeans surrounded it, but all the soldiers fled at night. They used the gate between the two walls near the king’s garden. They headed toward the Arabah. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:8
The Chaldean army pursued the king and caught Zedekiah on Jericho’s plains. His entire army deserted him. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:9
The Chaldeans captured the king. They brought him to Babylon’s king at Riblah in Hamath’s land. Babylon’s king pronounced judgment on Zedekiah there. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:10
At Riblah, Babylon’s king killed Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes. He also killed all Judah’s officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:11
He put out Zedekiah’s eyes and bound him with bronze shackles. He took him to Babylon and kept him imprisoned until his death. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:12
Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard and servant of Babylon’s king, entered Jerusalem on the tenth day of the fifth month. It was the nineteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar’s reign over Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:13
He burned the LORD’s house, the royal palace, and all Jerusalem’s houses. He burned every significant building. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:14
The entire Chaldean army under the guard captain broke down all the walls around Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:15
Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, exiled some of the poorest people and those remaining in the city. He also took those who deserted to Babylon’s king and the remaining craftsmen. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:16
Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, left some of the land’s poorest people to tend vineyards and fields. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:17
The Chaldeans broke up the bronze pillars, stands, and bronze Sea in the LORD’s house. They took all the bronze to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:18
They took the pots, shovels, wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, dishes, and every bronze article used in temple service. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:19
The guard captain also took basins, censers, sprinkling bowls, pots, lampstands, pans, and drink offering bowls. He took everything made of pure gold or fine silver. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:20
King Solomon made two pillars, the Sea, twelve bronze bulls beneath it, and movable stands for the LORD’s house. The weight of all this bronze was beyond measure. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:21
Each pillar was eighteen cubits high and twelve cubits around. Each was hollow, with walls four fingers thick. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:22
One pillar had a bronze capital five cubits high. A network of bronze pomegranates surrounded it. The second pillar and its pomegranates were similar. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:23
Each capital had ninety-six pomegranates on its sides. A total of one hundred pomegranates were above the surrounding network. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:24
The guard captain also took Seraiah, the chief priest, Zephaniah, the second-ranking priest, and the three doorkeepers. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:25
From those remaining in the city, he took a court official appointed over the soldiers and seven trusted royal advisers. He took the army captain’s scribe, who enlisted the land’s people. He also took sixty men found in the city. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:26
Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, took them to Babylon’s king at Riblah. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:27
There at Riblah in Hamath’s land, Babylon’s king killed them. Judah went into exile, away from its own land. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:28
Nebuchadnezzar exiled these people. In his seventh year, he took 3,023 Jews. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:29
In Nebuchadnezzar’s eighteenth year, he took 832 people from Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:30
In Nebuchadnezzar’s twenty-third year, Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, exiled 745 Jews. The total taken was 4,600 people. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:31
Evil-merodach, Babylon’s king, pardoned Judah’s king Jehoiachin and freed him from prison in the first year of his reign. This happened on the twenty-fifth day of the twelfth month of Jehoiachin’s thirty-seventh year in exile. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:32
He spoke kindly to Jehoiachin. He put his throne above the thrones of the other kings with him in Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:33
Jehoiachin changed out of his prison clothes. He ate regularly at the king’s table for the rest of his life. 📖

## Jeremiah 52:34
Babylon’s king gave Jehoiachin a daily portion throughout the rest of his life, until his death. 📖
