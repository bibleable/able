## Jeremiah 44:1
This word came to Jeremiah about all the Jews living in Egypt. They lived in Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis, and Pathros’s land. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:2
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. You saw all the disaster I brought on Jerusalem and all Judah’s cities. Today they lie ruined and desolate. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:3
“This is because of their evil deeds. They made Me angry by continuing to burn incense and serve other gods. Neither they, you, nor your fathers ever knew those gods. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:4
“I repeatedly sent you all My servants the prophets. I said, ‘Do not do this detestable thing that I hate.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 44:5
“But they refused to listen or pay attention. They did not turn from wickedness or stop burning incense to other gods. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:6
“So My wrath and anger poured out and burned in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. They became the desolate ruins you see today. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:7
“Now the LORD God of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. Why do you harm yourselves so greatly? You cut off men, women, children, and infants from Judah. You leave yourselves no survivors. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:8
“Why do you make Me angry with the work of your hands? You burn incense to other gods in Egypt, where you went to live. You will be cut off. You will become an object of curses and reproach among all earth’s nations. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:9
“Have you forgotten your fathers’ wickedness and the wickedness of Judah’s kings and their wives? Have you forgotten your own wickedness and your wives’ wickedness in Judah and Jerusalem’s streets? 📖

## Jeremiah 44:10
“To this day, they have not humbled themselves or shown reverence. They have not followed My instruction or the statutes I set before you and your fathers. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:11
“So the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. I will turn against all Judah to bring disaster and cut them off. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:12
“I will remove Judah’s survivors who insisted on settling in Egypt. They will meet their end. From least to greatest, all will die by sword or famine. They will become an object of curses, horror, insults, and reproach. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:13
“I will punish those living in Egypt by sword, famine, and plague, as I punished Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:14
“None of Judah’s survivors who settled in Egypt will escape to return and live in Judah, where they long to go. None will return except a few fugitives.” 📖

## Jeremiah 44:15
A great assembly answered Jeremiah. It included all the men who knew their wives burned incense to other gods and all the women standing there. All the people living in Egypt and Pathros joined them. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:16
“We will not listen to the word you spoke to us in the LORD’s name! 📖

## Jeremiah 44:17
“We will do everything we vowed. We will burn incense and pour drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven. We, our fathers, our kings, and our officials did this in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. Then we had abundant food and good things. We saw no disaster. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:18
“Since we stopped burning incense and pouring drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven, we have lacked everything. Sword and famine have killed us.” 📖

## Jeremiah 44:19
The women added, “We burned incense and poured drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven. We made sacrificial cakes in her image. Did we do these things without our husbands’ knowledge?” 📖

## Jeremiah 44:20
Jeremiah answered all the men and women who had spoken. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:21
“You, your fathers, kings, officials, and the land’s people burned incense in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. Did the LORD not remember this and bring it to mind? 📖

## Jeremiah 44:22
“The LORD could no longer bear your evil deeds and detestable acts. Your land became desolate, a horror and an object of curses, with no one living there. It remains that way today. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:23
“This disaster came because you burned incense and sinned against the LORD. You disobeyed His voice and did not follow His instruction, statutes, and testimonies. You see the result today.” 📖

## Jeremiah 44:24
Jeremiah told everyone, including all the women, “All Judah’s people in Egypt, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:25
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. ‘You and your wives spoke and acted on your words. You vowed to burn incense and pour drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven. Go ahead and keep your promises and vows! 📖

## Jeremiah 44:26
“‘But all Judah’s people living in Egypt must hear the LORD’s word. I swear by My great name,’ says the LORD. ‘No man of Judah living in Egypt will ever invoke My name again. He will not say, “As surely as the Lord GOD lives.” 📖

## Jeremiah 44:27
“‘I watch over them for harm instead of good. Every man of Judah in Egypt will die by sword or famine until they are finished off. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:28
“‘A few will escape the sword and return from Egypt to Judah. Everyone remaining from Judah who settled in Egypt will know whose word stands, Mine or theirs! 📖

## Jeremiah 44:29
“‘This will be your sign that I will punish you here,’ declares the LORD. ‘You will know that My threats of harm will certainly stand. 📖

## Jeremiah 44:30
“‘The LORD says this. I will hand Egypt’s king Pharaoh Hophra to enemies who want to kill him. I handed Judah’s king Zedekiah to Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, in the same way. Nebuchadnezzar was his enemy who wanted to kill him.’” 📖
