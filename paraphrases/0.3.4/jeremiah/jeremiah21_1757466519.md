## Jeremiah 21:1
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah when King Zedekiah sent Pashhur, Malchijah’s son, and the priest Zephaniah, Maaseiah’s son, to him. They said, 📖

## Jeremiah 21:2
“Please ask the LORD for us. Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, is attacking us. Perhaps the LORD will do wonders for us as He did before. Then Nebuchadnezzar may withdraw.” 📖

## Jeremiah 21:3
Jeremiah answered, “Tell Zedekiah this. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:4
“The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘You fight Babylon’s king and the Chaldeans besieging you outside the wall. I will turn your weapons against you. I will gather their forces in the city’s center. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:5
“‘I Myself will fight you with an outstretched hand and a mighty arm. I will fight with anger, fury, and great wrath. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:6
“‘I will strike down this city’s people and animals. They will die in a terrible plague.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 21:7
“‘Afterward,’ declares the LORD, ‘I will hand Judah’s king Zedekiah and his officers to Babylon’s king Nebuchadnezzar. I will also hand him this city’s people who survive plague, sword, and famine. I will hand them to enemies who want to kill them. He will kill them with the sword. He will not spare them or show pity or compassion.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 21:8
“Tell these people what the LORD says. ‘I set the way of life and the way of death before you. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:9
“‘Anyone who stays in this city will die by sword, famine, and plague. Anyone who leaves and surrenders to the Chaldeans besieging you will live. He will keep his life like plunder from war. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:10
“‘I have turned against this city to bring disaster instead of good,’ declares the LORD. ‘Babylon’s king will receive it and burn it down.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 21:11
“Tell Judah’s royal house to hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:12
“The LORD tells David’s house, ‘Give justice every morning. Rescue robbery victims from their oppressors. Otherwise, My wrath will burn like fire because of their evil deeds. No one will put it out. 📖

## Jeremiah 21:13
“‘I am against you who live above the valley on the rocky plateau,’ declares the LORD. ‘You say, “Who can attack us? Who can enter our homes?” 📖

## Jeremiah 21:14
“‘I will punish you as your deeds deserve,’ declares the LORD. ‘I will light a fire in your forest. It will consume everything around you.’” 📖
