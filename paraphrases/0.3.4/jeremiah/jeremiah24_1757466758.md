## Jeremiah 24:1
Babylon’s king Nebuchadnezzar had taken Judah’s king Jeconiah, Jehoiakim’s son, to Babylon. He also took Judah’s officials and Jerusalem’s craftsmen and metalsmiths. Afterward, the LORD showed me two baskets of figs in front of His temple. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:2
One basket held very good figs, like early ripe ones. The other held very bad figs that no one could eat. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:3
The LORD asked, “Jeremiah, what do you see?” I answered, “Figs! The good figs are very good. The bad figs are so bad that no one can eat them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 24:4
The LORD’s word came to me. He said, 📖

## Jeremiah 24:5
“The LORD, Israel’s God, says this. I regard Judah’s exiles as good, like these good figs. I sent them from this place to the Chaldeans’ land. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:6
“I will watch over them for their good and return them to this land. I will build them up instead of tearing them down. I will plant them instead of uprooting them. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:7
“I will give them a heart to know Me as the LORD. They will be My people, and I will be their God. They will return to Me with their whole heart. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:8
“But I will treat Judah’s king Zedekiah, his officials, and Jerusalem’s remaining people like figs too bad to eat,” says the LORD. “This includes those remaining here and those living in Egypt. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:9
“I will make them a horror and an offense to all earth’s kingdoms. Wherever I banish them, they will face disgrace, scorn, ridicule, and curses. 📖

## Jeremiah 24:10
“I will send sword, famine, and plague against them. They will perish from the land I gave them and their fathers.” 📖
