## Jeremiah 28:1
This happened in the fifth month of King Zedekiah’s fourth year, early in his reign over Judah. The prophet Hananiah, Azzur’s son from Gibeon, spoke to me in the LORD’s house. The priests and all the people were present. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:2
He said, “The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘I have broken the yoke of Babylon’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:3
“‘Within two years, I will bring back all the articles from the LORD’s house. Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, took them from here to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:4
“‘I will bring back Jeconiah, Jehoiakim’s son, king of Judah, and all Judah’s exiles who went to Babylon,’ declares the LORD. ‘I will break the yoke of Babylon’s king.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 28:5
The prophet Jeremiah answered the prophet Hananiah. The priests and all the people standing in the LORD’s house heard him. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:6
Jeremiah said, “Amen! May the LORD do this and fulfill your prophecy! May He bring the articles from His house and all the exiles back here from Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:7
“But now hear this message I speak to you and all the people. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:8
“Long before you and me, prophets prophesied war, disaster, and plague against many lands and great kingdoms. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:9
“If a prophet predicts peace, people will recognize that the LORD truly sent him only if his words come true.” 📖

## Jeremiah 28:10
The prophet Hananiah took the yoke from the prophet Jeremiah’s neck and broke it. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:11
Hananiah announced before everyone, “The LORD says, ‘Within two years, I will break Nebuchadnezzar’s yoke off all nations’ necks, just like this.’” Nebuchadnezzar was Babylon’s king. Then the prophet Jeremiah went away. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:12
Soon after the prophet Hananiah broke the yoke from Jeremiah’s neck, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:13
“Tell Hananiah what the LORD says. ‘You have broken a wooden yoke. But you have made an iron yoke in its place.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 28:14
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. ‘I have put an iron yoke on all these nations’ necks to make them serve Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king. They will serve him. I have even given him control over the field’s animals.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 28:15
The prophet Jeremiah told the prophet Hananiah, “Listen, Hananiah! The LORD did not send you. But you made these people trust a lie. 📖

## Jeremiah 28:16
“So the LORD says, ‘I will remove you from the earth. You will die this year because you preached rebellion against the LORD.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 28:17
The prophet Hananiah died in the seventh month of that same year. 📖
