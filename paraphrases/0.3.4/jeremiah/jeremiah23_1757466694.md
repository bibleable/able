## Jeremiah 23:1
“How terrible for the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of My pasture!” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:2
The LORD, Israel’s God, tells the shepherds who tend His people, “You scattered My flock and drove them away. You did not care for them. I will deal with your evil deeds,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:3
“I Myself will gather My flock’s survivors from every land where I banished them. I will return them to their pasture. They will be fruitful and multiply there. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:4
“I will appoint shepherds to care for them. They will no longer be afraid or dismayed. None will go missing,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:5
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will raise a righteous Branch for David. He will reign wisely as King. He will act with justice and righteousness in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:6
“In His days, Judah will be saved, and Israel will live securely. His name will be ‘The LORD Our Righteousness.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 23:7
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when people will no longer say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites out of Egypt.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 23:8
“Instead, they will speak of the LORD who brought and led Israel’s descendants from the northern land and every land where He banished them. They will say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who did this.’ They will live in their own land again.” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:9
About the prophets, my heart is broken, and all my bones tremble. I am like a drunkard, like a man overcome by wine. This is because of the LORD and His holy words. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:10
The land is full of adulterers. Because of the curse, the land mourns, and the wilderness pastures have dried up. Their way is evil, and they misuse their power. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:11
“Both prophet and priest are ungodly. I have found their wickedness even in My house,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:12
“Their path will become slippery. They will be driven into darkness and fall there. I will bring disaster on them in the year of their punishment,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:13
“I saw something offensive among Samaria’s prophets. They prophesied by Baal and led My people Israel astray. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:14
“I have seen something horrible among Jerusalem’s prophets. They commit adultery and live by lies. They support evildoers, so no one turns from wickedness. They are all like Sodom to Me. Jerusalem’s people are like Gomorrah.” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:15
The LORD of Hosts says about the prophets, “I will feed them wormwood and give them poisoned water. Ungodliness has spread from Jerusalem’s prophets throughout the land.” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:16
The LORD of Hosts says, “Do not listen to the prophets who prophesy to you. They fill you with false hopes. Their visions come from their own minds instead of the LORD’s mouth. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:17
“They keep telling those who despise Me, ‘The LORD says you will have peace.’ They tell everyone who follows his stubborn heart, ‘No harm will come to you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 23:18
“Which of them has stood in the LORD’s council to see and hear His word? Who has paid attention to His word and obeyed it? 📖

## Jeremiah 23:19
“The LORD’s storm has gone out in fury. A whirlwind swirls down on the heads of the wicked. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:20
“The LORD’s anger will not turn back until He fulfills all His heart’s purposes. In the days ahead, you will understand this clearly. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:21
“I did not send these prophets, but they ran with their message. I did not speak to them, but they prophesied. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:22
“If they had stood in My council, they would have announced My words to My people. They would have turned them from their evil ways and deeds.” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:23
“Am I only a God nearby?” declares the LORD. “Am I not also a God far away?” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:24
“Can anyone hide where I cannot see him?” declares the LORD. “Do I not fill the heavens and earth?” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:25
“I have heard the prophets who prophesy lies in My name. They say, ‘I had a dream! I had a dream!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 23:26
“How long will these prophets keep falsehood in their hearts? They prophesy the delusions of their own minds. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:27
“They think the dreams they tell one another will make My people forget My name. Their fathers forgot My name by worshiping Baal. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:28
“Let a prophet with a dream tell it. But let the one who has My word speak it truthfully. What is straw compared with grain?” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:29
“Is My word not like fire?” declares the LORD. “Is it not like a hammer that breaks rock?” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:30
The LORD declares, “I oppose the prophets who steal one another’s words and claim they come from Me.” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:31
The LORD declares, “I oppose the prophets who use their own tongues and say, ‘The LORD declares it.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 23:32
The LORD declares, “I oppose those who prophesy false dreams. They tell them and lead My people astray with reckless lies. I did not send or command them. They do these people no good,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:33
“These people, a prophet, or a priest may ask you, ‘What is the LORD’s burden?’ Answer, ‘What burden? I will abandon you,’ declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:34
“If a prophet, priest, or anyone claims, ‘This is the LORD’s burden,’ I will punish him and his household. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:35
“Each person must ask his friend and brother, ‘What has the LORD answered?’ or ‘What has the LORD spoken?’ 📖

## Jeremiah 23:36
“Do not refer to the LORD’s burden again. Each person’s own word becomes his burden. You twist the words of the living God, the LORD of Hosts, our God. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:37
“Ask the prophet, ‘What has the LORD answered you?’ and ‘What has the LORD spoken?’ 📖

## Jeremiah 23:38
“But suppose you claim, ‘This is the LORD’s burden.’ The LORD says this because you made that claim despite My specific command against it. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:39
“I will certainly forget you. I will throw you and the city I gave you and your fathers out of My presence. 📖

## Jeremiah 23:40
“I will bring everlasting shame and lasting humiliation on you. It will never be forgotten.” 📖
