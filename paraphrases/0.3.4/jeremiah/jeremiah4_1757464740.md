## Jeremiah 4:1
“Israel, if you return, return to Me,” declares the LORD. “If you remove your detestable idols from My sight and no longer waver, 📖

## Jeremiah 4:2
“and if you swear truthfully, justly, and righteously, ‘As surely as the LORD lives,’ then the nations will receive blessings through Him. They will glory in Him.” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:3
The LORD tells the men of Judah and Jerusalem, “Break up your unplowed ground. Do not plant among thorns. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:4
“Men of Judah and people of Jerusalem, circumcise yourselves to the LORD. Remove the foreskins of your hearts. Otherwise, My wrath will burn like fire because of your evil deeds. No one will put it out.” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:5
Announce this in Judah and Jerusalem. Say, “Blow the ram’s horn throughout the land. Shout, ‘Gather together! Let us flee to the fortified cities!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 4:6
“Raise a signal flag toward Zion. Seek shelter without delay! I am bringing disaster and terrible destruction from the north. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:7
“A lion has left his thicket. A destroyer of nations has set out from his den to ruin your land. Your cities will become ruins, with no one living there. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:8
“Put on sackcloth. Mourn and cry out. The LORD’s fierce anger has not turned away from us.” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:9
The LORD declares, “On that day, the king and officials will lose courage. The priests will tremble in fear. The prophets will be astonished.” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:10
I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! You have completely deceived these people and Jerusalem. You said, ‘You will have peace.’ Yet a sword is at our throats!” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:11
At that time, this message will come to these people and Jerusalem. “A scorching wind blows from the desert’s bare heights toward the daughter of My people. It does not come to winnow or sift. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:12
“This wind from Me is too strong for that. Now I also pronounce judgments against them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:13
Look! He advances like clouds. His chariots are like a whirlwind, and his horses are faster than eagles. How terrible for us! We are ruined! 📖

## Jeremiah 4:14
Jerusalem, wash evil from your heart so that you can be saved. How long will you keep wicked thoughts inside you? 📖

## Jeremiah 4:15
A voice sounds from Dan. It announces disaster from Ephraim’s hills. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:16
Warn the nations! Announce this to Jerusalem. “An army comes from a distant land to surround you. They shout against Judah’s cities. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:17
“They surround her like men guarding a field, because she has rebelled against Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:18
“Your ways and deeds have brought this on you. This is your punishment. It is bitter and pierces your heart!” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:19
My anguish! I twist in pain, and my chest hurts! My heart pounds, and I cannot be silent. I have heard the horn, the alarm of battle. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:20
Reports tell of one disaster after another. The whole land lies ruined. My tents are destroyed in an instant, and my curtains in a moment. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:21
How long must I see the signal flag and hear the horn? 📖

## Jeremiah 4:22
“My people are fools who do not know Me. They are foolish children without understanding. They know how to do evil well. But they do not know how to do good.” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:23
I looked at the earth. It had no form and was empty. I looked at the heavens, and they had no light. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:24
I looked at the mountains. They shook, and all the hills swayed. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:25
I looked, and no person remained. All the birds had fled from the sky. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:26
I looked, and the fruitful land had become a desert. All its cities lay torn down before the LORD and His fierce anger. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:27
The LORD says, “The whole land will become desolate. But I will not destroy it completely. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:28
“So the earth will mourn, and the heavens above will become dark. I have spoken and planned this. I will not change My mind or turn back.” 📖

## Jeremiah 4:29
At the sound of horsemen and archers, the people of every city flee. They enter thickets and climb among rocks. Every city is abandoned. No one remains there. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:30
You devastated one, what will you do? You dress in scarlet, wear gold jewelry, and make your eyes look larger with paint. You make yourself beautiful without success. Your lovers despise you and want to kill you. 📖

## Jeremiah 4:31
I hear the Daughter of Zion cry like a woman giving birth to her first child. She gasps for breath and stretches out her hands. She says, “How terrible for me! My soul faints before the murderers!” 📖
