## Jeremiah 15:1
The LORD told me, “Even if Moses and Samuel stood before Me, My heart would not turn toward these people. Send them out of My presence. Let them go! 📖

## Jeremiah 15:2
“If they ask, ‘Where should we go?’ tell them what the LORD says. ‘Those destined for death will go to death. Those destined for the sword will go to the sword. Those destined for famine will go to famine. Those destined for captivity will go to captivity.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 15:3
“I will appoint four kinds of destroyers over them,” declares the LORD. “The sword will kill, and dogs will drag away. Birds of the sky and animals of the earth will devour and destroy. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:4
“I will make them a horror to all earth’s kingdoms. This is because of what Manasseh, Hezekiah’s son, king of Judah, did in Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:5
“Jerusalem, who will pity you? Who will mourn for you? Who will turn aside to ask how you are? 📖

## Jeremiah 15:6
“You have abandoned Me and turned your back,” declares the LORD. “So I will stretch out My hand against you and destroy you. I am tired of showing compassion. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:7
“I will scatter them with a winnowing fork at the land’s gates. I will take away My people’s children and destroy My people. They have not turned from their ways. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:8
“I will make their widows more numerous than the sea’s sand. At noon, I will bring a destroyer against the mothers of young men. I will suddenly bring anguish and dismay on them. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:9
“The mother of seven will grow faint and breathe her last. Her sun will set while it is still day. She will suffer disgrace and humiliation. I will put the rest to the sword before their enemies,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:10
How terrible for me, my mother! You bore me to be a man of conflict and strife throughout the land. I have neither lent nor borrowed. Yet everyone curses me. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:11
The LORD said, “I will certainly rescue you for a good purpose. I will certainly plead with your enemy for you in times of trouble and distress. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:12
“Can anyone smash iron from the north or bronze? 📖

## Jeremiah 15:13
“I will give your wealth and treasures as plunder, without payment. This is for all your sins throughout your territory. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:14
“I will enslave you to your enemies in an unknown land. My anger will light a fire that burns against you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 15:15
LORD, You understand. Remember me and care for me. Take vengeance on my persecutors. In Your patience, do not take me away. Know that I bear insults for Your honor. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:16
I found Your words and ate them. They became my joy and my heart’s delight. I bear Your name, LORD God of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:17
I never sat or celebrated with the revelers. Your hand was on me, so I sat alone. You filled me with indignation. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:18
Why does my pain never end? Why is my wound incurable and unwilling to heal? You have become like a mirage to me, like water that is not there. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:19
The LORD says, “If you return, I will restore you to stand before Me. If you speak noble words instead of worthless ones, you will be My spokesman. They must turn to you. You must not turn to them. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:20
“I will make you a fortified bronze wall to these people. They will fight you, but they will not overcome you. I am with you to save and rescue you,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 15:21
“I will rescue you from the wicked. I will redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless.” 📖
