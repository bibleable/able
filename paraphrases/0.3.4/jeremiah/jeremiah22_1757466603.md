## Jeremiah 22:1
The LORD says, “Go to the palace of Judah’s king. Announce this message there. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:2
“Say, ‘King of Judah on David’s throne, hear the LORD’s word. You, your officials, and your people who enter these gates must listen. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:3
“‘The LORD says, “Act with justice and righteousness. Rescue robbery victims from their oppressors. Do no wrong or violence to foreigners, the fatherless, or widows. Do not shed innocent blood here. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:4
“‘“If you truly obey these commands, kings on David’s throne will enter this palace’s gates. They, their officials, and their people will ride chariots and horses. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:5
“‘“But if you disobey these words, this house will become a pile of rubble. I swear this by Myself,” declares the LORD.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:6
The LORD says about Judah’s royal house, “You are like Gilead to Me, like Lebanon’s summit. But I will certainly turn you into a desert, like cities where no one lives. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:7
“I will send destroyers against you, each with his weapons. They will cut down your best cedars and throw them into the fire. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:8
“Many nations will pass this city. They will ask one another, ‘Why has the LORD done this to such a great city?’ 📖

## Jeremiah 22:9
“People will answer, ‘They abandoned the covenant of the LORD their God. They worshiped and served other gods.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:10
Do not weep for the dead man or mourn his loss. Weep bitterly for the exiled man. He will never return to see his native land. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:11
The LORD speaks about Shallum, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. He succeeded his father Josiah but has left this place. “He will never return. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:12
“He will die in the place of his exile. He will never see this land again.” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:13
“How terrible for the man who builds his palace through wrongdoing and his upper rooms without justice! He makes his countrymen work without paying their wages. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:14
“He says, ‘I will build a great palace with spacious upper rooms.’ He cuts windows, covers the walls with cedar, and paints them vermilion. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:15
“Does having the best cedar make you a king? Your father had food and drink, did he not? He acted with justice and righteousness, so things went well for him. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:16
“He defended the poor and needy, so things went well for him. Is that not what knowing Me means?” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:17
“But your eyes and heart seek only dishonest gain, the shedding of innocent blood, extortion, and oppression. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:18
The LORD says about Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah, “They will not mourn him by crying, ‘Alas, my brother! Alas, my sister!’ They will not mourn him by crying, ‘Alas, my master! Alas, his splendor!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 22:19
“He will have a donkey’s burial. They will drag him away and throw him outside Jerusalem’s gates. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:20
“Go to Lebanon and cry out. Shout in Bashan. Cry out from Abarim, because all your lovers have been crushed. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:21
“I warned you when you were secure. You said, ‘I will not listen.’ Since your youth, you have refused to obey My voice. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:22
“Wind will drive all your shepherds away. Your lovers will go into captivity. Then all your wickedness will bring you shame and humiliation. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:23
“You who live in Lebanon, nestled among cedars, will groan in anguish! Your pain will be like a woman giving birth.” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:24
The LORD declares, “As surely as I live, I would pull you off even if you were a signet ring on My right hand. I speak to you, Coniah, Jehoiakim’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:25
“I will hand you to those you fear, who want to kill you. I will hand you to Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, and the Chaldeans. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:26
“I will throw you and the mother who bore you into another land. Neither of you was born there. Both of you will die there. 📖

## Jeremiah 22:27
“You will never return to the land you long for.” 📖

## Jeremiah 22:28
Is Coniah a despised, broken pot, a jar no one wants? Why are he and his descendants thrown into an unknown land? 📖

## Jeremiah 22:29
Land, land, land! Hear the LORD’s word! 📖

## Jeremiah 22:30
The LORD says, “Record this man as childless, a man who will not prosper during his life. None of his descendants will prosper by sitting on David’s throne or ruling Judah again.” 📖
