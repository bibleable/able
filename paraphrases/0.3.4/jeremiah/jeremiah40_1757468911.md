## Jeremiah 40:1
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah after Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, freed him at Ramah. Nebuzaradan had found him chained among Jerusalem and Judah’s captives going into exile in Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:2
The guard captain found Jeremiah and said, “The LORD your God decreed this disaster on this place. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:3
“Now the LORD has done exactly what He said. This happened because you people sinned against Him and disobeyed His voice. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:4
“Today I free your wrists from their chains. If you want to come with me to Babylon, come. I will care for you. If you do not want to come, go no farther. The whole land is before you. Go wherever you think is good and right.” 📖

## Jeremiah 40:5
Before Jeremiah turned to leave, Nebuzaradan added, “Return to Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson. Babylon’s king appointed him over Judah’s cities. Stay with him among the people, or go wherever you wish.” The guard captain gave Jeremiah food rations and a gift. Then he released him. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:6
Jeremiah went to Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son, at Mizpah. He stayed with him among those remaining in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:7
All the army commanders and men in the field heard that Babylon’s king had appointed Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son, over the land. He put Gedaliah in charge of its poorest men, women, and children. These had not been exiled to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:8
They and their men came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. These included Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, Johanan and Jonathan, Kareah’s sons, and Seraiah, Tanhumeth’s son. They also included the sons of Ephai the Netophathite and Jezaniah, the Maacathite’s son. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:9
Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson, swore to them and their men. He said, “Do not fear serving the Chaldeans. Live in the land and serve Babylon’s king. Then things will go well for you. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:10
“I will stay in Mizpah to represent you before the Chaldeans who come to us. Gather wine grapes, summer fruit, and oil. Put them in storage jars. Live in the cities you have taken.” 📖

## Jeremiah 40:11
Jews in Moab, Ammon, Edom, and all other lands heard that Babylon’s king had left survivors in Judah. They heard he appointed Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson, over them. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:12
They all returned from every place where they had been banished. They came to Judah’s land, to Gedaliah at Mizpah. They gathered abundant wine grapes and summer fruit. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:13
Johanan, Kareah’s son, and all the army commanders in the field came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:14
They asked, “Do you know that Baalis, the Ammonites’ king, sent Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, to kill you?” Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son, did not believe them. 📖

## Jeremiah 40:15
Johanan, Kareah’s son, spoke privately to Gedaliah at Mizpah. “Let me kill Ishmael, Nethaniah’s son, secretly. Why should he kill you and scatter Judah’s people who gathered to you? Why should Judah’s survivors perish?” 📖

## Jeremiah 40:16
Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son, told Johanan, Kareah’s son, “Do not do that! Your claim about Ishmael is a lie.” 📖
