## Jeremiah 10:1
House of Israel, hear the word the LORD speaks to you. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:2
The LORD says, “Do not learn the nations’ ways. Do not fear signs in the heavens, although the nations fear them. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:3
“The peoples’ customs are worthless. They cut a tree from the forest. A craftsman shapes it with a chisel. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:4
“They decorate it with silver and gold. They use a hammer and nails to fasten it so that it will not fall over. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:5
“Their idols cannot speak, like scarecrows in a cucumber patch. People must carry them because they cannot walk. Do not fear them. They can do neither harm nor good.” 📖

## Jeremiah 10:6
LORD, no one is like You. You are great, and Your name is mighty in power. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:7
King of nations, who would not fear You? You deserve this. No one among the nations’ wise men or in all their kingdoms is like You. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:8
They are all senseless and foolish. Worthless wooden idols instruct them! 📖

## Jeremiah 10:9
People bring hammered silver from Tarshish and gold from Uphaz. Craftsmen and goldsmiths make the idols. Skilled workers make all their blue and purple clothes. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:10
The LORD is the true God, the living God, and the eternal King. The earth shakes at His wrath. Nations cannot endure His anger. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:11
Tell them, “These gods made neither the heavens nor the earth. They will perish from the earth and from under these heavens.” 📖

## Jeremiah 10:12
The LORD made the earth by His power. He established the world by His wisdom. He stretched out the heavens by His understanding. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:13
When He thunders, the waters in the heavens roar. He raises clouds from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning with the rain. He brings wind from His storehouses. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:14
Everyone is senseless and without knowledge. Every goldsmith’s idols bring him shame. His cast images are false and have no breath. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:15
They are worthless things to mock. They will perish when punishment comes. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:16
The Portion of Jacob is different from these idols. He made everything. Israel is the tribe of His inheritance. His name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:17
You who live under siege, gather your belongings from this land. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:18
The LORD says, “This time, I will sling the land’s inhabitants out. I will bring distress on them so that they can be captured.” 📖

## Jeremiah 10:19
How terrible my brokenness is! My wound is severe! But I said, “This is truly my sickness. I must bear it.” 📖

## Jeremiah 10:20
My tent is destroyed, and all its ropes have snapped. My sons have left me and are no more. No one remains to pitch my tent or hang my curtains. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:21
The shepherds have become senseless. They do not seek the LORD. So they have not prospered, and their entire flock is scattered. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:22
Listen! A report comes with a great commotion from the northern land. It will make Judah’s cities desolate, a home for jackals. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:23
LORD, I know that a person’s way is not his own. No one who walks directs his own steps. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:24
LORD, correct me with justice. Do not correct me in anger, or You will reduce me to nothing. 📖

## Jeremiah 10:25
Pour Your wrath on nations that do not acknowledge You and families that do not call on Your name. They have devoured Jacob and finished him off. They have ruined his homeland. 📖
