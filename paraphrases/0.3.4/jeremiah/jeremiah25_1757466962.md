## Jeremiah 25:1
This word about all Judah’s people came to Jeremiah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. It was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:2
The prophet Jeremiah spoke these words to all Judah’s people and everyone living in Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:3
“The LORD’s word has come to me for twenty-three years, from the thirteenth year of Josiah, Amon’s son, king of Judah, until today. I have spoken to you repeatedly, but you have not listened. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:4
“The LORD repeatedly sent all His servants the prophets to you. But you refused to listen or pay attention. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:5
“The prophets said, ‘Each of you must turn from your evil ways and deeds. Then you can live in the land the LORD gave you and your fathers forever. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:6
“‘Do not follow other gods to serve or worship them. Do not make Me angry with things your hands make. Then I will not harm you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 25:7
“‘But you have harmed yourselves by refusing to listen to Me,’ declares the LORD. ‘You have made Me angry with things your hands make.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 25:8
“So the LORD of Hosts says, ‘You have not obeyed My words. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:9
“‘So I will call all the northern families,’ declares the LORD. ‘I will send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king. I will bring him against this land, its people, and all surrounding nations. I will devote them to destruction. They will become an object of horror and contempt, an everlasting desolation. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:10
“‘I will remove joyful, glad sounds and the voices of brides and bridegrooms from them. I will remove the sound of millstones and the light of lamps. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:11
“‘This whole land will become a desolate wasteland. These nations will serve Babylon’s king for seventy years. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:12
“‘After seventy years, I will punish Babylon’s king and that nation, the Chaldeans’ land, for their guilt,’ declares the LORD. ‘I will make it desolate forever. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:13
“‘I will bring everything I announced against that land upon it. This includes everything written in this book that Jeremiah prophesied against all nations. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:14
“‘Many nations and great kings will enslave them. I will repay them according to their deeds and the work of their hands.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 25:15
The LORD, Israel’s God, told me, “Take this cup of the wine of wrath from My hand. Make every nation I send you to drink it. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:16
“They will drink, stagger, and lose their minds because of the sword I send among them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 25:17
I took the cup from the LORD’s hand. I made every nation He sent me to drink from it. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:18
These included Jerusalem, Judah’s cities, and their kings and officials. They would become ruined, an object of horror, contempt, and curses, as they are today. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:19
They included Pharaoh, Egypt’s king, his officials, his leaders, and all his people. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:20
They included all the mixed tribes and all Uz’s kings. They included all the Philistine kings: Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and what remained of Ashdod. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:21
They included Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:22
They included all the kings of Tyre and Sidon and the kings of the coastlands across the sea. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:23
They included Dedan, Tema, Buz, and everyone who cuts the corners of his hair. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:24
They included all Arabia’s kings and all the kings of the mixed tribes living in the desert. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:25
They included all the kings of Zimri, Elam, and Media. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:26
They included all the northern kings, near and far, one after another. They included all earth’s kingdoms. After them all, Sheshach’s king will drink too. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:27
“Tell them what the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says. ‘Drink, become drunk, and vomit. Fall and never rise again because of the sword I send among you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 25:28
“If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink, tell them what the LORD of Hosts says. ‘You must certainly drink it! 📖

## Jeremiah 25:29
“‘I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that bears My Name. How can you escape punishment? You will not escape. I am calling a sword against everyone living on earth,’ declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:30
“Prophesy all these words against them. Say, ‘The LORD will roar from above and raise His voice from His holy dwelling. He will roar loudly over His pasture. Like those treading grapes, He will shout against all earth’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:31
“‘The noise will reach earth’s ends because the LORD brings a charge against the nations. He judges all people and puts the wicked to the sword,’” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:32
The LORD of Hosts says, “Disaster spreads from nation to nation. A mighty storm rises from earth’s ends.” 📖

## Jeremiah 25:33
On that day, those the LORD kills will lie from one end of the earth to the other. No one will mourn, gather, or bury them. They will lie like dung on the ground. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:34
Shepherds, weep and cry out! Leaders of the flock, roll in dust! The days of your slaughter have come. You will fall and break like fine pottery. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:35
The shepherds and leaders of the flock will find no way to flee or escape. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:36
Hear the shepherds’ cries and the flock leaders’ wailing. The LORD is destroying their pasture. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:37
The LORD’s burning anger has silenced the peaceful meadows. 📖

## Jeremiah 25:38
He has left His den like a lion. The oppressor’s sword and the LORD’s fierce anger have made their land desolate. 📖
