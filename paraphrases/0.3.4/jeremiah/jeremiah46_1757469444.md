## Jeremiah 46:1
This is the LORD’s word about the nations. It came to the prophet Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:2
It concerns Egypt and the army of Pharaoh Neco, Egypt’s king. Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, defeated that army at Carchemish on the Euphrates River. This happened in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:3
“Prepare small and large shields. Advance to battle! 📖

## Jeremiah 46:4
“Harness the horses and mount them! Take your positions with helmets on. Polish spears and put on armor! 📖

## Jeremiah 46:5
“Why do I see this? They are terrified and retreating. Their warriors are defeated. They flee quickly without looking back. Terror is everywhere!” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:6
“The swift cannot flee, and the warrior cannot escape. They stumble and fall in the north beside the Euphrates River. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:7
“Who rises like the Nile, like rivers with churning waters? 📖

## Jeremiah 46:8
“Egypt rises like the Nile. Its waters churn like rivers. It boasts, ‘I will rise and cover the earth. I will destroy cities and their people.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 46:9
“Horses, advance! Chariots, race fiercely! Let warriors come forward. Cush and Put carry shields. Lydia’s men draw bows. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:10
“That day belongs to the Lord GOD of Hosts. It is His day of vengeance against His enemies. The sword will devour until satisfied and drink its fill of their blood. The Lord GOD of Hosts will hold a sacrifice in the northern land beside the Euphrates River. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:11
“Virgin Daughter of Egypt, go to Gilead for balm. Your many remedies achieve nothing. There is no healing for you. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:12
“Nations have heard of your shame. Your cries fill the earth. One warrior stumbles over another, and both fall together.” 📖

## Jeremiah 46:13
The LORD spoke this word to the prophet Jeremiah about Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, coming to attack Egypt. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:14
“Announce this in Egypt, Migdol, Memphis, and Tahpanhes. ‘Take your positions and prepare! The sword devours those around you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 46:15
“Why have your warriors fallen? They cannot stand, because the LORD pushed them down. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:16
“They keep stumbling and falling over one another. They say, ‘Get up! Let us return to our people and native land. Let us escape the oppressor’s sword.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 46:17
“There they will shout, ‘Pharaoh, Egypt’s king, was only noise. He let the appointed time pass.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 46:18
The King declares this. His name is the LORD of Hosts. “As surely as I live, someone will come like Tabor among the mountains and like Carmel beside the sea. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:19
“Daughter living in Egypt, pack for exile. Memphis will be ruined and destroyed. No one will live there. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:20
“Egypt is a beautiful heifer. But a gadfly from the north comes against her. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:21
“Her hired soldiers are like fattened calves. They too will turn and flee together. They will not stand their ground. Their day of disaster, the time of their punishment, is coming. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:22
“Egypt will hiss like a fleeing snake. The enemy will advance in strength with axes, like woodsmen cutting trees. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:23
“They will cut down her forest, however dense it is,” declares the LORD. “They are more numerous than locusts. No one can count them. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:24
“The Daughter of Egypt will be shamed. She will be handed to the northern people.” 📖

## Jeremiah 46:25
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “I will punish Amon, Thebes’s god, and Pharaoh. I will punish Egypt with its gods and kings, and those who trust Pharaoh. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:26
“I will hand them to Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, and his officers, who want to kill them. But afterward, people will live in Egypt as they did long ago,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:27
“My servant Jacob, do not fear. Israel, do not be dismayed. I will certainly save you from a distant place and your descendants from their captivity. Jacob will return to peace and ease. No one will frighten him. 📖

## Jeremiah 46:28
“My servant Jacob, do not fear,” declares the LORD. “I am with you. I will completely destroy all nations where I banished you. But I will not completely destroy you. I will discipline you justly and certainly punish you.” 📖
