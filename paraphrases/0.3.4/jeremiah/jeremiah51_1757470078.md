## Jeremiah 51:1
The LORD says, “I will stir up a destroyer’s spirit against Babylon and Leb-kamai’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:2
“I will send strangers to winnow Babylon and empty her land. They will attack from every side on her day of disaster. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:3
“Do not let the archer bend his bow or put on armor. Spare none of her young men. Devote her entire army to destruction! 📖

## Jeremiah 51:4
“They will fall dead in the Chaldeans’ land, pierced through in her streets. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:5
“Israel and Judah’s God, the LORD of Hosts, has not abandoned them. Their land is full of guilt before Israel’s Holy One, but He has not abandoned them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:6
Flee Babylon! Escape with your lives! Do not perish in her punishment. This is the time of the LORD’s vengeance. He will pay her what she deserves. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:7
Babylon was a gold cup in the LORD’s hand that made the whole earth drunk. The nations drank her wine and went mad. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:8
Babylon has suddenly fallen and shattered. Wail for her. Bring balm for her pain. Perhaps she can be healed. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:9
“We tried to heal Babylon, but she could not be healed. Leave her! Let each return to his own land. Her judgment reaches the sky and the clouds.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:10
“The LORD has proved us right. Come, let us tell Zion what the LORD our God has done.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:11
Sharpen arrows and fill quivers! The LORD has stirred the spirit of the Median kings. He plans to destroy Babylon. This is the LORD’s vengeance for His temple. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:12
Raise a banner against Babylon’s walls! Post guards and watchmen. Prepare an ambush. The LORD has planned and fulfilled His words against Babylon’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:13
You live beside many waters and have rich treasures. Your end has come. Your life’s thread is cut. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:14
The LORD of Hosts swore by Himself. “I will certainly fill you with men like locusts. They will shout in triumph over you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:15
The LORD made the earth by His power. He established the world by His wisdom. He stretched out the heavens by His understanding. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:16
When He thunders, the waters in the heavens roar. He raises clouds from earth’s ends. He makes lightning with the rain. He brings wind from His storehouses. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:17
Everyone is senseless and without knowledge. Every goldsmith’s idols bring him shame. His cast images are false and have no breath. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:18
They are worthless things to mock. They will perish when punishment comes. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:19
The Portion of Jacob is different from these idols. He made everything, including the tribe of His inheritance. His name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:20
“You are My war club, My battle weapon. With you I shatter nations and ruin kingdoms. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:21
“With you I shatter horses and riders, chariots and drivers. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:22
“With you I shatter men and women, the old and young, young men and maidens. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:23
“With you I shatter shepherds and flocks, farmers and oxen, governors and officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:24
“Before your eyes, I will repay Babylon and all Chaldea’s people for all their evil in Zion,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:25
“Destroying mountain, you ruin the whole earth. I am against you,” declares the LORD. “I will stretch out My hand against you. I will roll you over the cliffs and make you a burned mountain. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:26
“No one will take a cornerstone or foundation stone from you. You will be desolate forever,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:27
“Raise a banner in the land! Blow the ram’s horn among the nations! Prepare them against her. Call the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz. Appoint a captain against her and bring horses like swarming locusts. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:28
“Prepare the nations for battle against her. Prepare the Median kings, their governors, all their officials, and all the lands they rule. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:29
“The earth shakes and twists. The LORD’s plans against Babylon stand. He will make Babylon’s land desolate, with no one living there. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:30
“Babylon’s warriors have stopped fighting and sit in their strongholds. Their strength is exhausted, and they have become like women. Babylon’s homes burn. Her gate bars are broken. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:31
“Couriers race to meet one another. Messenger follows messenger to tell Babylon’s king that his city is captured from end to end. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:32
“The fords are seized, the marshes burn, and the soldiers are terrified.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:33
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “The Daughter of Babylon is like a threshing floor being trampled. Her harvest time will come soon.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:34
“Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, has devoured and crushed me. He set me aside like an empty vessel. He swallowed me like a monster, filled his belly with my fine foods, and vomited me out. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:35
“May Babylon bear the violence done to me and my flesh,” says Zion’s inhabitant. Jerusalem says, “May Chaldea’s people bear my blood.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:36
So the LORD says, “I will plead your case and take vengeance for you. I will dry her sea and springs. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:37
“Babylon will become a pile of rubble, a home for jackals. She will be an object of horror and scorn, with no one living there. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:38
“They will roar together like young lions and growl like lion cubs. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:39
“While they are hot and flushed, I will serve them a feast. I will make them drunk so they can revel. Then they will sleep forever and never wake,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:40
“I will bring them to slaughter like lambs, like rams with male goats. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:41
“Sheshach is captured! The praise of the whole earth is seized. Babylon has become a horror among the nations! 📖

## Jeremiah 51:42
“The sea has risen over Babylon. Churning waves cover her. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:43
“Her cities have become desolate, a dry, arid land. No one lives or passes through there. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:44
“I will punish Bel in Babylon and make him vomit what he swallowed. Nations will no longer stream to him. Even Babylon’s wall will fall. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:45
“My people, come out of her! Each must save his life from the LORD’s fierce anger. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:46
“Do not lose heart or fear rumors in the land. One rumor will come one year, and another the next. They will tell of violence in the land and ruler fighting ruler. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:47
“Days are coming when I will punish Babylon’s idols. Her entire land will be shamed. All her dead will lie fallen within her. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:48
“Heaven, earth, and everything in them will shout joyfully over Babylon. Northern destroyers will attack her,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:49
“Babylon must fall because of Israel’s slain. In the same way, the slain of all the earth fell because of Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:50
“You who escaped the sword, leave without delay! Remember the LORD from far away. Bring Jerusalem to mind.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:51
“We are ashamed because we heard reproach. Disgrace covers our faces because foreigners entered the holy places of the LORD’s house.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:52
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will punish her idols. The wounded will groan throughout her land. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:53
“Even if Babylon climbs to heaven and strengthens her high stronghold, the destroyers I send will attack her,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:54
“Cries come from Babylon. The noise of great destruction comes from the Chaldeans’ land. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:55
“The LORD will destroy Babylon and silence her mighty voice. Waves will roar like great waters. The noise of their voices will resound. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:56
“A destroyer comes against Babylon. Her warriors will be captured, and their bows broken. The LORD is a God of retribution. He will repay fully. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:57
“I will make her princes, wise men, governors, officials, and warriors drunk. They will sleep forever and never wake,” declares the King. His name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:58
The LORD of Hosts says, “Babylon’s thick walls will be leveled, and fire will consume her high gates. The peoples’ work will achieve nothing. The nations will exhaust themselves to feed the flames.” 📖

## Jeremiah 51:59
The prophet Jeremiah gave this message to the quartermaster Seraiah, Neriah’s son and Mahseiah’s grandson. Seraiah went to Babylon with Judah’s king Zedekiah in the fourth year of Zedekiah’s reign. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:60
Jeremiah had written on one scroll all the disaster that would come on Babylon. It contained all these words written about Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:61
Jeremiah told Seraiah, “When you reach Babylon, read all these words aloud. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:62
“Say, ‘LORD, You promised to destroy this place so that neither people nor animals remain. It will be desolate forever.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 51:63
“After reading this scroll, tie a stone to it. Throw it into the Euphrates. 📖

## Jeremiah 51:64
“Say, ‘Babylon will sink like this and never rise again because of the disaster I bring on her. Her people will grow weary.’” Here end Jeremiah’s words. 📖
