## Jeremiah 33:1
Jeremiah was still confined in the guard’s courtyard. The LORD’s word came to him a second time. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:2
The LORD made, formed, and established the earth. His name is the LORD. He says, 📖

## Jeremiah 33:3
“Call to Me, and I will answer. I will show you great things beyond your searching, things you do not know. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:4
“The LORD, Israel’s God, speaks about this city’s houses and the palaces of Judah’s kings. They were torn down for defense against siege ramps and the sword. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:5
“The Chaldeans come to fight. They will fill those places with the corpses of men I strike down in My anger and wrath. I have hidden My face from this city because of all its wickedness. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:6
“But I will bring health and healing to it. I will heal its people. I will reveal abundant peace and truth to them. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:7
“I will restore Judah and Israel from captivity. I will rebuild them as they were before. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:8
“I will cleanse them from all their wrongdoing against Me. I will forgive all their sins of rebellion against Me. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:9
“This city will bring Me renown, joy, praise, and glory before all earth’s nations. They will hear all the good I do for it. They will tremble in awe at all the goodness and prosperity I give it. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:10
“The LORD says this about this place. You say it is a wasteland without people or animals. Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets are deserted, with neither people nor animals. But sounds will be heard there again. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:11
“Joyful, glad sounds and the voices of brides and bridegrooms will return. People bringing thank offerings to the LORD’s house will say, ‘Give thanks to the LORD of Hosts. The LORD is good. His loving devotion lasts forever.’ I will restore the land from captivity as before,” says the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:12
The LORD of Hosts says, “This desolate place has neither people nor animals. Yet here and in all its cities, shepherds will again have pastures to rest their flocks. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:13
“Flocks will again pass under the hands of the one counting them,” says the LORD. “This will happen in the cities of the hill country, foothills, and Negev. It will happen in Benjamin’s land, the cities around Jerusalem, and Judah’s cities. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:14
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will fulfill My gracious promise to Israel’s house and Judah’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:15
“In those days, at that time, I will make a righteous Branch sprout for David. He will act with justice and righteousness in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:16
“In those days, Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will live securely. The city will be called ‘The LORD Our Righteousness.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 33:17
“The LORD says this. David will always have a man to sit on the throne of Israel’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:18
“The Levite priests will always have a man before Me to offer burnt offerings, burn grain offerings, and present sacrifices.” 📖

## Jeremiah 33:19
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:20
The LORD says, “Suppose you could break My covenant with day and night, so they no longer came at their appointed times. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:21
“Only then could My covenant with My servant David and My ministers, the Levite priests, be broken. Only then would David have no son to reign on his throne. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:22
“Heaven’s host cannot be counted, and the seashore’s sand cannot be measured. In the same way, I will increase My servant David’s descendants and the Levites who serve before Me.” 📖

## Jeremiah 33:23
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah again. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:24
“Have you noticed what these people say? ‘The LORD rejected the two families He chose.’ So they despise My people and no longer consider them a nation. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:25
“The LORD says this. Suppose I had not established My covenant with day and night and the fixed order of heaven and earth. 📖

## Jeremiah 33:26
“Only then would I reject Jacob’s descendants and My servant David’s descendants. Only then would I stop choosing his descendants to rule Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob’s descendants. I will restore them from captivity and have compassion on them.” 📖
