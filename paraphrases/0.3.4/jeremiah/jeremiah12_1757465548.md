## Jeremiah 12:1
LORD, You are righteous when I plead before You. But I want to question You about Your judgments. Why do the wicked prosper? Why do all the faithless live at ease? 📖

## Jeremiah 12:2
You planted them, and they took root. They grew and produced fruit. You are always on their lips but far from their hearts. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:3
LORD, You know and see me. You test my heart toward You. Drag the wicked away like sheep to slaughter. Set them apart for the day of carnage. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:4
How long will the land mourn and every field’s grass stay withered? The people’s evil has swept away animals and birds. The people say, “He cannot see what our end will be.” 📖

## Jeremiah 12:5
“If men running on foot have exhausted you, how can you race horses? If you stumble in a peaceful land, how will you manage in the Jordan’s thickets? 📖

## Jeremiah 12:6
“Even your brothers, your own father’s household, have betrayed you. They too have cried out against you. Do not trust them, even if they speak kindly to you. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:7
“I have abandoned My house and My inheritance. I have handed the beloved of My soul over to her enemies. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:8
“My inheritance has become like a forest lion to Me. She has roared against Me. That is why I hate her. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:9
“Is My inheritance not like a speckled bird of prey to Me, with other birds of prey circling against her? Gather all the field’s animals. Bring them to devour her. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:10
“Many shepherds have destroyed My vineyard and trampled My plot of ground. They have turned My pleasant field into a desolate wasteland. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:11
“They have made it desolate. It mourns before Me in its desolation. The whole land lies ruined, but no one takes it to heart. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:12
“Destroyers have come over all the wilderness’s bare heights. The LORD’s sword devours from one end of the earth to the other. No one has peace. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:13
“They planted wheat but harvested thorns. They exhausted themselves without success. Be ashamed of your harvest because of the LORD’s fierce anger.” 📖

## Jeremiah 12:14
The LORD says, “My evil neighbors attack the inheritance I gave My people Israel. I will uproot them from their land. I will uproot Judah’s house from among them. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:15
“After uprooting them, I will have compassion on them again. I will return each to his inheritance and land. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:16
“If they carefully learn My people’s ways and swear by My name, they will be established among My people. They must say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives.’ They once taught My people to swear by Baal. 📖

## Jeremiah 12:17
“But if they refuse to obey, I will uproot that nation. I will uproot and destroy it,” declares the LORD. 📖
