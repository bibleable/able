## Jeremiah 38:1
Shephatiah, Mattan’s son, Gedaliah, Pashhur’s son, Jucal, Shelemiah’s son, and Pashhur, Malchijah’s son, heard Jeremiah’s message to all the people. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:2
“The LORD says, ‘Anyone who stays in this city will die by sword, famine, and plague. Anyone who surrenders to the Chaldeans will live. He will keep his life like plunder from war. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:3
“‘The LORD says this city will certainly be handed to Babylon’s royal army. Babylon’s king will capture it.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:4
The officials told the king, “This man deserves death. His words discourage the warriors remaining here and all the people. He seeks their ruin instead of their well-being.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:5
King Zedekiah answered, “He is in your hands. The king can do nothing to stop you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:6
They took Jeremiah to the cistern of Malchiah, the king’s son, in the guard’s courtyard. They lowered him with ropes. The cistern held mud but no water. Jeremiah sank into the mud. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:7
Ebed-melech the Cushite, a palace court official, heard that Jeremiah was in the cistern. The king was sitting at Benjamin’s Gate. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:8
Ebed-melech left the palace and spoke to the king. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:9
“My lord the king, these men have treated the prophet Jeremiah wickedly. They put him in the cistern. He will starve there because no bread remains in the city.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:10
The king commanded Ebed-melech the Cushite, “Take thirty men from here. Pull the prophet Jeremiah out of the cistern before he dies.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:11
Ebed-melech took the men to a place below the palace storehouse. He took old rags and worn clothes from there. He lowered them to Jeremiah with ropes. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:12
Ebed-melech the Cushite called, “Put these old rags and clothes under your arms to pad the ropes.” Jeremiah did this. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:13
They pulled him up with the ropes and lifted him out of the cistern. Jeremiah stayed in the guard’s courtyard. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:14
King Zedekiah sent for the prophet Jeremiah. He met him at the third entrance to the LORD’s house. The king said, “I will ask you something. Hide nothing from me.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:15
Jeremiah answered, “If I tell you, you will certainly kill me. Even if I give you advice, you will not listen.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:16
King Zedekiah secretly swore to Jeremiah, “As surely as the LORD lives, who gave us this life, I will not kill you. I will not hand you to these men who want to kill you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:17
Jeremiah told Zedekiah, “The LORD God of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. ‘If you surrender to the officers of Babylon’s king, you will live. This city will not be burned. You and your household will survive. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:18
“‘If you refuse to surrender to those officers, the Chaldeans will receive this city. They will burn it. You will not escape them.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:19
King Zedekiah told Jeremiah, “I fear the Jews who deserted to the Chaldeans. The Chaldeans might hand me to them for abuse.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:20
Jeremiah answered, “They will not hand you over. Obey the LORD’s voice in what I tell you. Then things will go well for you, and you will live. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:21
“But if you refuse to surrender, the LORD has shown me this. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:22
“All the women remaining in Judah’s royal palace will be brought to Babylon’s royal officials. They will say, ‘Your trusted friends misled and overpowered you. Your feet sank in the mud, and they abandoned you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 38:23
“All your wives and children will be brought to the Chaldeans. You will not escape them. Babylon’s king will seize you, and this city will be burned.” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:24
Zedekiah warned Jeremiah, “Tell no one about this conversation, or you will die. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:25
“The officials may hear that I spoke with you. They may demand, ‘Tell us what you said to the king and what he said to you. Hide nothing, or we will kill you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 38:26
“Then answer, ‘I asked the king not to send me back to Jonathan’s house to die there.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 38:27
All the officials came and questioned Jeremiah. He told them exactly what the king commanded. They said no more, because no one had overheard the conversation. 📖

## Jeremiah 38:28
Jeremiah stayed in the guard’s courtyard until Jerusalem was captured. 📖
