## Jeremiah 20:1
Pashhur the priest heard Jeremiah prophesy these things. He was Immer’s son and the chief official in the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:2
Pashhur had the prophet Jeremiah beaten. He put him in stocks at Benjamin’s Upper Gate, beside the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:3
The next day, Pashhur released Jeremiah from the stocks. Jeremiah told him, “The LORD calls you Magor-missabib instead of Pashhur. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:4
“The LORD says, ‘I will make you a terror to yourself and all your friends. You will watch their enemies kill them with the sword. I will hand Judah over to Babylon’s king. He will take them to Babylon and kill them with the sword. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:5
“‘I will give their enemies all this city’s wealth, products, and valuables, and all the treasures of Judah’s kings. They will seize and plunder them and carry them to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:6
“‘Pashhur, you and everyone in your house will go into captivity in Babylon. You will die and be buried there. So will all your friends to whom you prophesied these lies.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 20:7
LORD, You deceived me, and I was deceived. You overpowered me and won. I am a laughingstock all day. Everyone mocks me. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:8
Whenever I speak, I cry out about violence and destruction. The LORD’s word has brought me insults and mockery all day. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:9
I may say, “I will not mention Him or speak in His name again.” But His message becomes a fire in my heart, trapped in my bones. Holding it in exhausts me. I cannot do it. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:10
I hear many whisper, “Terror is everywhere! Report him!” All my trusted friends watch for my fall. They say, “Perhaps he will be deceived. Then we can overcome him and take revenge.” 📖

## Jeremiah 20:11
The LORD is with me like a terrifying warrior. So my persecutors will stumble and fail. Their failure will bring complete shame. Their everlasting disgrace will never be forgotten. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:12
LORD of Hosts, You examine the righteous and see hearts and minds. Let me see Your vengeance on them. I have entrusted my case to You. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:13
Sing to the LORD! Praise the LORD! He rescues the needy from evildoers. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:14
A curse on the day of my birth! May the day my mother bore me never receive a blessing. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:15
A curse on the man who brought my father this news: “You have a son!” That news brought him great joy. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:16
May that man become like the cities the LORD destroyed without compassion. May he hear an outcry in the morning and a battle cry at noon. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:17
This is because he did not kill me in the womb. Then my mother would have been my grave, and her womb would have remained enlarged forever. 📖

## Jeremiah 20:18
Why did I leave the womb to see only trouble and sorrow, and end my days in shame? 📖
