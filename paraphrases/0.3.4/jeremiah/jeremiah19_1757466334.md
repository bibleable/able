## Jeremiah 19:1
The LORD says, “Buy a clay jar from a potter. Take some elders of the people and leaders of the priests. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:2
“Go to the Valley of Ben-hinnom near the Potsherd Gate’s entrance. Announce the words I give you there. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:3
“Say, ‘Judah’s kings and Jerusalem’s people, hear the LORD’s word.’ The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘I will bring disaster on this place. Everyone who hears about it will have ringing ears. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:4
“‘They abandoned Me and made this a foreign place. Here they burned incense to gods neither they, their fathers, nor Judah’s kings ever knew. They filled this place with innocent blood. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:5
“‘They built Baal’s high places to burn their children as offerings to Baal. I never commanded or mentioned this. It never entered My mind. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:6
“‘Days are coming,’ declares the LORD, ‘when people will no longer call this place Topheth or the Valley of Ben-hinnom. They will call it the Valley of Slaughter. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:7
“‘Here I will ruin Judah and Jerusalem’s plans. I will make them fall by the sword before enemies who want to kill them. Their corpses will feed the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:8
“‘I will make this city desolate and an object of scorn. Everyone passing by will be horrified and mock all her wounds. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:9
“‘I will make them eat their sons’ and daughters’ flesh. They will eat one another’s flesh during the siege and distress their enemies bring. These enemies want to kill them.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 19:10
“Break the jar in front of the men with you. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:11
“Tell them what the LORD of Hosts says. ‘I will shatter this nation and city like a potter’s jar that can never be repaired. They will bury the dead in Topheth until no burial space remains. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:12
“‘This is what I will do to this place and its people,’ declares the LORD. ‘I will make this city like Topheth. 📖

## Jeremiah 19:13
“‘Jerusalem’s houses and the houses of Judah’s kings will become defiled like Topheth. On all those rooftops, people burned incense to heaven’s entire host and poured drink offerings to other gods.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 19:14
Jeremiah returned from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy. He stood in the courtyard of the LORD’s house. He announced to all the people, 📖

## Jeremiah 19:15
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, ‘I will bring every disaster I announced against this city and all its surrounding villages. They have stubbornly refused to listen to My words.’” 📖
