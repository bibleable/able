## Jeremiah 13:1
The LORD told me, “Buy a linen loincloth and wear it around your waist. Do not let it touch water.” 📖

## Jeremiah 13:2
I bought a loincloth as the LORD commanded. I put it around my waist. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:3
The LORD’s word came to me a second time. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:4
“Take the loincloth you bought and are wearing. Go to Perath at once. Hide it in a crack in the rocks there.” 📖

## Jeremiah 13:5
I went and hid it at Perath, as the LORD commanded. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:6
Many days later, the LORD told me, “Go to Perath. Get the loincloth I commanded you to hide there.” 📖

## Jeremiah 13:7
I went to Perath and dug up the loincloth. I took it from its hiding place. It was ruined and completely useless. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:8
The LORD’s word came to me. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:9
The LORD said, “In the same way, I will ruin Judah’s pride and Jerusalem’s great pride. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:10
“These evil people refuse to listen to My words. They follow their stubborn hearts and other gods, which they serve and worship. They will become completely useless, like this loincloth. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:11
“A loincloth clings to a man’s waist. In the same way, I made the entire houses of Israel and Judah cling to Me,” declares the LORD. “They were to be My people, for My renown, praise, and glory. But they did not listen. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:12
“Tell them what the LORD, Israel’s God, says: ‘Every wineskin will be filled with wine.’ They will answer, ‘We certainly know every wineskin should be filled with wine!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 13:13
“Then tell them what the LORD says. ‘I will fill everyone in this land with drunkenness. This includes the kings on David’s throne, the priests, the prophets, and all Jerusalem’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:14
“‘I will smash them against one another, fathers and sons alike,’ declares the LORD. ‘No mercy, pity, or compassion will stop Me from destroying them.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 13:15
Listen and pay attention. Do not be arrogant, because the LORD has spoken. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:16
Give glory to the LORD your God before He brings darkness. Do this before your feet stumble on the mountains at dusk. You wait for light, but He turns it to deep gloom and thick darkness. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:17
If you refuse to listen, I will weep secretly because of your pride. My eyes will overflow with tears, because the LORD’s flock has been captured. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:18
Tell the king and queen mother, “Take a lowly seat. Your glorious crowns have fallen from your heads.” 📖

## Jeremiah 13:19
The Negev’s cities are shut tight. No one can open them. All Judah has gone into exile, completely taken captive. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:20
Look up and see those coming from the north. Where is the flock entrusted to you? Where are the sheep you took pride in? 📖

## Jeremiah 13:21
What will you say when He puts your close allies over you, the ones you trained? Will anguish not grip you like a woman giving birth? 📖

## Jeremiah 13:22
You may ask yourself, “Why has this happened to me?” Your great wrongdoing is the reason your skirts were stripped off and your body exposed. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:23
Can an Ethiopian change his skin? Can a leopard change its spots? Neither can you do good when you are accustomed to evil. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:24
“I will scatter you like chaff blown by the desert wind. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:25
“This is your lot, the portion I have measured for you,” declares the LORD. “You have forgotten Me and trusted lies. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:26
“So I will pull your skirts over your face to expose your shame. 📖

## Jeremiah 13:27
“I have seen your detestable deeds: your adultery, lustful neighing, and shameless prostitution on the hills and in the fields. How terrible for you, Jerusalem! How long will you remain unclean?” 📖
