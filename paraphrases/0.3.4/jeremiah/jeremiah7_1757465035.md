## Jeremiah 7:1
This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:2
“Stand at the gate of the LORD’s house and announce this message. People of Judah who enter these gates to worship the LORD, hear His word. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:3
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Correct your ways and deeds. Then I will let you live here. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:4
“Do not trust these deceptive words: ‘This is the LORD’s temple, the LORD’s temple, the LORD’s temple!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 7:5
“If you truly correct your ways and deeds, and if you treat one another justly, 📖

## Jeremiah 7:6
“if you stop oppressing foreigners, the fatherless, and widows, and stop shedding innocent blood here or following other gods to your own harm, 📖

## Jeremiah 7:7
“then I will let you live here. This is the land I gave your fathers forever. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:8
“But you keep trusting deceptive words that cannot help you. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:9
“Will you steal, murder, commit adultery, and swear falsely? Will you burn incense to Baal and follow gods you have not known, 📖

## Jeremiah 7:10
“and then stand before Me in this house that bears My Name? Will you say, ‘We are saved, so we can keep doing all these abominations’? 📖

## Jeremiah 7:11
“Do you see this house that bears My Name as a robbers’ den? I too have seen it,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:12
“Go to Shiloh, where I first made a dwelling for My Name. See what I did there because of My people Israel’s wickedness. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:13
“You have done all these things,” declares the LORD. “I spoke to you repeatedly, but you refused to listen. I called, but you refused to answer. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:14
“So I will do to this house what I did to Shiloh. This house bears My Name, and you trust in it. I gave this place to you and your fathers. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:15
“I will throw you out of My presence. I threw out all your brothers, all Ephraim’s descendants, in the same way. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:16
“Do not pray for these people. Do not plead or make requests for them. Do not beg Me, because I will not listen to you. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:17
“Do you not see what they do in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets? 📖

## Jeremiah 7:18
“Sons gather wood, fathers light fires, and women knead dough to make cakes for the Queen of Heaven. They pour drink offerings to other gods to make Me angry. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:19
“But is it Me they provoke?” declares the LORD. “Are they not harming themselves and bringing shame on themselves? 📖

## Jeremiah 7:20
The Lord GOD says, “I will pour My anger and fury over this place. It will fall on people, animals, field trees, and the land’s produce. It will burn, and no one will put it out. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:21
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Add your burnt offerings to your other sacrifices. Eat the meat yourselves! 📖

## Jeremiah 7:22
“When I brought your fathers out of Egypt, My commands were not merely about burnt offerings and sacrifices. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:23
“I commanded them, ‘Obey Me. I will be your God, and you will be My people. Follow every way I command you, so that things may go well for you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 7:24
“But they refused to listen or pay attention. They followed the stubborn desires of their evil hearts. They went backward instead of forward. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:25
“From the day your fathers left Egypt until today, I repeatedly sent you all My servants the prophets. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:26
“But they refused to listen to Me or pay attention. They stubbornly resisted and did more evil than their fathers. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:27
“You will tell them all this, but they will not listen. You will call to them, but they will not answer. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:28
“Tell them, ‘This nation refused to obey the LORD their God or accept correction. Truth has perished and disappeared from their lips. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:29
“‘Cut off your hair and throw it away. Sing a lament on the bare heights. The LORD has rejected and abandoned the generation under His wrath.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 7:30
“Judah’s people have done evil before Me,” declares the LORD. “They have put their abominations in the house that bears My Name. They have defiled it. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:31
“They built Topheth’s high places in the Valley of Ben-hinnom to burn their sons and daughters in the fire. I never commanded this. It never entered My mind. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:32
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when people will no longer call this place Topheth or the Valley of Ben-hinnom. They will call it the Valley of Slaughter. They will bury the dead in Topheth until no space remains. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:33
“These people’s corpses will feed the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth. No one will frighten them away. 📖

## Jeremiah 7:34
“I will remove joyful, glad sounds and the voices of brides and bridegrooms from Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. The land will become a wasteland.” 📖
