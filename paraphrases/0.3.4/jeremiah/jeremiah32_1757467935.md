## Jeremiah 32:1
This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah, Judah’s king. It was Nebuchadnezzar’s eighteenth year. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:2
Babylon’s royal army was besieging Jerusalem. The prophet Jeremiah was imprisoned in the guard’s courtyard in the palace of Judah’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:3
Zedekiah, Judah’s king, had imprisoned him. He asked, “Why do you prophesy like this? You claim the LORD says, ‘I will hand this city to Babylon’s king. He will capture it. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:4
“‘Judah’s king Zedekiah will not escape the Chaldeans. He will certainly be handed to Babylon’s king. He will speak with him face to face and see him eye to eye. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:5
“‘He will take Zedekiah to Babylon. Zedekiah will stay there until I attend to him,’ declares the LORD. ‘If you fight the Chaldeans, you will fail.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 32:6
Jeremiah answered, “The LORD’s word came to me. He said, 📖

## Jeremiah 32:7
“‘Your uncle Shallum’s son Hanamel will come to you. He will say, “Buy my field in Anathoth. You have the right of redemption to buy it.”’ 📖

## Jeremiah 32:8
“My cousin Hanamel came to the guard’s courtyard, as the LORD said. He urged me, ‘Buy my field in Anathoth, in Benjamin’s land. You have the rights of inheritance and redemption. Buy it for yourself.’” Then I knew this was the LORD’s word. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:9
I bought the field in Anathoth from my cousin Hanamel. I weighed out seventeen silver shekels. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:10
I signed and sealed the deed. I called witnesses and weighed the silver on scales. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:11
I took both copies of the purchase deed. One was sealed and held the terms and conditions. The other was open. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:12
I gave the deed to Baruch, Neriah’s son and Mahseiah’s grandson. My cousin Hanamel, the witnesses signing the purchase agreement, and all the Jews sitting in the guard’s courtyard saw this. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:13
Before them, I instructed Baruch, 📖

## Jeremiah 32:14
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says this. Put both purchase deeds, the sealed copy and the open copy, in a clay jar. Preserve them for a long time. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:15
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says that people will buy houses, fields, and vineyards in this land again.” 📖

## Jeremiah 32:16
After giving the purchase deed to Baruch, Neriah’s son, I prayed to the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:17
“Oh, Lord GOD! You made the heavens and earth by Your great power and outstretched arm. Nothing is too difficult for You! 📖

## Jeremiah 32:18
“You show loving devotion to thousands. But You put the fathers’ wrongdoing into their children’s laps after them. You are the great and mighty God, whose name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:19
“You are great in counsel and mighty in deeds. Your eyes see everyone’s ways. You reward each according to his ways and the fruit of his deeds. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:20
“You performed signs and wonders in Egypt. You still do them today in Israel and among all people. You have made a name for Yourself that remains today. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:21
“You brought Your people Israel out of Egypt with signs and wonders. You used a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and great terror. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:22
“You gave them this land, flowing with milk and honey. You had sworn to give it to their fathers. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:23
“They entered and possessed it. But they disobeyed Your voice and did not follow Your law. They failed to do everything You commanded. So You brought all this disaster on them. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:24
“Look at the siege ramps raised to capture the city. Sword, famine, and plague have handed the city to the attacking Chaldeans. Your words have come true, as You see! 📖

## Jeremiah 32:25
“Yet You told me, Lord GOD, ‘Buy the field with silver and call witnesses, although the city has been handed to the Chaldeans!’” 📖

## Jeremiah 32:26
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:27
“I am the LORD, the God of all flesh. Is anything too difficult for Me? 📖

## Jeremiah 32:28
“The LORD says this. I will hand this city to the Chaldeans and Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king. He will capture it. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:29
“The attacking Chaldeans will enter and burn this city. They will burn the houses where people made Me angry. On those rooftops, they burned incense to Baal and poured drink offerings to other gods. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:30
“Since their youth, Israel’s children and Judah’s children have done only evil before Me. They have done nothing but make Me angry with the work of their hands,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:31
“This city has stirred My wrath and fury from its first day until now. So I will remove it from My presence. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:32
“Israel’s children and Judah’s children have made Me angry with all their evil. This includes their kings, officials, priests, prophets, Judah’s men, and Jerusalem’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:33
“They turned their backs to Me instead of their faces. I taught them repeatedly. But they refused to listen or accept discipline. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:34
“They put their abominations in the house that bears My Name. They defiled it. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:35
“They built Baal’s high places in the Valley of Ben-hinnom. They made their sons and daughters pass through fire to Molech. I never commanded this. It never entered My mind that they should commit this abomination and make Judah sin. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:36
“You say sword, famine, and plague will hand this city to Babylon’s king. Now the LORD, Israel’s God, says this about the city. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:37
“I will certainly gather My people from every land where I banished them in furious anger and great wrath. I will bring them back here and let them live safely. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:38
“They will be My people, and I will be their God. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:39
“I will give them one heart and one way. They will always fear Me, for their own good and their children’s good after them. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:40
“I will make an everlasting covenant with them. I will never stop doing good to them. I will put fear of Me in their hearts so that they never turn away from Me. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:41
“I will rejoice in doing them good. I will faithfully plant them in this land with My whole heart and soul. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:42
“The LORD says this. I brought all this great disaster on these people. In the same way, I will bring all the good I promised them. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:43
“People will buy fields in this land again. You say it is desolate, without people or animals, and handed over to the Chaldeans. 📖

## Jeremiah 32:44
“People will buy fields with silver and sign, seal, and witness deeds. This will happen in Benjamin’s land, around Jerusalem, and in Judah’s cities. These include cities in the hill country, foothills, and Negev. I will restore them from captivity,” declares the LORD. 📖
