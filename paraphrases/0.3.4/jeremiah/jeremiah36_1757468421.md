## Jeremiah 36:1
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:2
“Take a scroll. Write all My words about Israel, Judah, and all nations on it. Include everything from My first words to you during Josiah’s reign until today. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:3
“Perhaps Judah’s people will hear about all the disaster I plan and each will turn from his wicked way. Then I will forgive their wrongdoing and sin.” 📖

## Jeremiah 36:4
Jeremiah called Baruch, Neriah’s son. Jeremiah dictated all the words the LORD had spoken to him. Baruch wrote them on a scroll. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:5
Jeremiah commanded Baruch, “I am restricted and cannot enter the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:6
“Go there on a day of fasting. Read the LORD’s words from the scroll you wrote at my dictation. Let everyone hear, including all Judah’s people coming from their cities. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:7
“Perhaps they will plead before the LORD, and each will turn from his wicked way. The anger and fury the LORD announced against these people are great.” 📖

## Jeremiah 36:8
Baruch, Neriah’s son, obeyed everything the prophet Jeremiah commanded. He read the LORD’s words from the scroll in the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:9
In the ninth month of Jehoiakim’s fifth year, everyone in Jerusalem and those coming from Judah’s cities received a proclamation to fast before the LORD. Jehoiakim was Josiah’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:10
Baruch read Jeremiah’s words from the scroll for everyone to hear. He read from the room of Gemariah, Shaphan the scribe’s son. It was in the upper courtyard at the New Gate’s entrance in the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:11
Micaiah, Gemariah’s son and Shaphan’s grandson, heard all the LORD’s words from the scroll. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:12
He went to the scribe’s room in the king’s palace, where all the officials sat. These included Elishama the scribe, Delaiah, Shemaiah’s son, and Elnathan, Achbor’s son. Gemariah, Shaphan’s son, Zedekiah, Hananiah’s son, and all the other officials were there too. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:13
Micaiah told them every word he had heard Baruch read from the scroll before the people. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:14
The officials sent Jehudi to Baruch. Jehudi was Nethaniah’s son, Shelemiah’s grandson, and Cushi’s great-grandson. Their message said, “Bring the scroll you read to the people and come here.” Baruch, Neriah’s son, took the scroll and went to them. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:15
They said, “Please sit and read it to us.” Baruch read it for them to hear. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:16
After hearing everything, they looked at one another in fear. They told Baruch, “We must report all these words to the king.” 📖

## Jeremiah 36:17
They asked Baruch, “How did you write all these words? Did Jeremiah dictate them?” 📖

## Jeremiah 36:18
Baruch answered, “He dictated every word to me. I wrote them in ink on the scroll.” 📖

## Jeremiah 36:19
The officials told Baruch, “You and Jeremiah must hide. Tell no one where you are.” 📖

## Jeremiah 36:20
They stored the scroll in the room of Elishama the scribe. Then they went to the king in the courtyard and reported everything. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:21
The king sent Jehudi for the scroll. Jehudi took it from the room of Elishama the scribe. He read it for the king and all the officials standing beside him to hear. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:22
It was the ninth month. The king sat in his winter quarters with a fire burning before him. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:23
Whenever Jehudi read three or four columns, Jehoiakim cut them off with a scribe’s knife. He threw them into the firepot. He continued until fire consumed the entire scroll. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:24
The king and his servants heard all these words. But they did not become frightened or tear their clothes. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:25
Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah urged the king not to burn the scroll. But he refused to listen. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:26
The king ordered Jerahmeel, a son of the king, Seraiah, Azriel’s son, and Shelemiah, Abdeel’s son, to seize Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet. But the LORD had hidden them. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:27
The king burned the scroll Baruch had written at Jeremiah’s dictation. Afterward, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:28
“Take another scroll. Write the same words that were on the first scroll, which Jehoiakim, Judah’s king, burned. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:29
“Announce what the LORD says about Jehoiakim, Judah’s king. ‘You burned the scroll. You asked why it said Babylon’s king would certainly destroy this land and leave it without people or animals. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:30
“‘So the LORD says this about Jehoiakim, Judah’s king. He will have no one to sit on David’s throne. His body will be thrown out into the heat by day and frost by night. 📖

## Jeremiah 36:31
“‘I will punish him, his descendants, and his servants for their wrongdoing. I will bring every disaster I warned about on them, Jerusalem’s people, and Judah’s men. They refused to listen.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 36:32
Jeremiah gave another scroll to the scribe Baruch, Neriah’s son. Jeremiah dictated every word from the scroll Jehoiakim, Judah’s king, had burned. Baruch wrote them on the new scroll. Many similar words were added. 📖
