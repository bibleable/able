## Jeremiah 37:1
Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, made Zedekiah, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. He ruled in place of Coniah, Jehoiakim’s son. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:2
Zedekiah, his officers, and the land’s people refused to obey the LORD’s words through the prophet Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:3
Yet King Zedekiah sent Jehucal, Shelemiah’s son, and the priest Zephaniah, Maaseiah’s son, to the prophet Jeremiah. They said, “Please pray to the LORD our God for us!” 📖

## Jeremiah 37:4
Jeremiah could still move freely among the people. They had not yet imprisoned him. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:5
Pharaoh’s army left Egypt. When the Chaldeans besieging Jerusalem heard this, they withdrew from Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:6
The LORD’s word came to the prophet Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:7
“Tell Judah’s king, who sent you to Me, what the LORD, Israel’s God, says. Pharaoh’s army came to help you. But it will return to Egypt, its own land. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:8
“Then the Chaldeans will return and attack this city. They will capture and burn it. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:9
“The LORD says, ‘Do not deceive yourselves by saying the Chaldeans will leave forever. They will not! 📖

## Jeremiah 37:10
“‘Even if you defeated their entire attacking army and only wounded men remained in their tents, those men would get up and burn this city.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 37:11
The Chaldean army withdrew from Jerusalem because it feared Pharaoh’s army. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:12
Then Jeremiah started to leave Jerusalem for Benjamin’s land. He intended to claim his portion there among the people. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:13
At Benjamin’s Gate, the guard captain Irijah seized him. Irijah was Shelemiah’s son and Hananiah’s grandson. He said, “You are deserting to the Chaldeans!” 📖

## Jeremiah 37:14
Jeremiah answered, “That is a lie! I am not deserting to the Chaldeans!” Irijah refused to listen. He arrested Jeremiah and took him to the officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:15
The officials were angry with Jeremiah. They beat him and imprisoned him in the house of Jonathan the scribe. They had made that house a prison. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:16
Jeremiah went into a dungeon cell. He stayed there a long time. 📖

## Jeremiah 37:17
Later, King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah and met him privately in the palace. He asked, “Is there a word from the LORD?” Jeremiah answered, “Yes. You will be handed to Babylon’s king.” 📖

## Jeremiah 37:18
Jeremiah asked King Zedekiah, “How have I sinned against you, your servants, or these people? Why have you imprisoned me? 📖

## Jeremiah 37:19
“Where are your prophets who said Babylon’s king would not attack you or this land? 📖

## Jeremiah 37:20
“My lord the king, please listen to my request. Do not send me back to the house of Jonathan the scribe. I will die there.” 📖

## Jeremiah 37:21
King Zedekiah ordered Jeremiah placed in the guard’s courtyard. He ordered a daily loaf from the bakers’ street until the city’s bread ran out. Jeremiah stayed in the guard’s courtyard. 📖
