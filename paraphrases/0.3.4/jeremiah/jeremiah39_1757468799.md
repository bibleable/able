## Jeremiah 39:1
In the tenth month of Zedekiah’s ninth year as Judah’s king, Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, marched against Jerusalem with his entire army. He besieged the city. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:2
The city’s defenses were breached on the ninth day of the fourth month of Zedekiah’s eleventh year. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:3
All Babylon’s royal officials entered and sat in the Middle Gate. These included Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all the other royal officials. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:4
Zedekiah, Judah’s king, and all his soldiers saw them and fled. They left the city at night through the king’s garden and the gate between the two walls. They followed the route to the Arabah. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:5
The Chaldean army pursued them and caught Zedekiah on Jericho’s plains. They took him to Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, at Riblah in Hamath’s land. Nebuchadnezzar pronounced judgment on him there. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:6
At Riblah, Babylon’s king killed Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes. He also killed all Judah’s nobles. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:7
Then he put out Zedekiah’s eyes. He bound him with bronze chains to take him to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:8
The Chaldeans burned the royal palace and the people’s houses. They broke down Jerusalem’s walls. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:9
Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, exiled the people remaining in the city to Babylon. He also took those who deserted to him. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:10
Nebuzaradan left some poor people without property in Judah’s land. At that time, he gave them vineyards and fields. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:11
Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, had given Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, these orders about Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:12
“Take him and care for him. Let no harm come to him. Do whatever he asks.” 📖

## Jeremiah 39:13
Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, Nebushazban the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all Babylon’s royal captains acted. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:14
They had Jeremiah brought from the guard’s courtyard. They handed him to Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson, to take him home. Jeremiah stayed among his own people. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:15
While Jeremiah was confined in the guard’s courtyard, the LORD’s word had come to him. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:16
“Tell Ebed-melech the Cushite what the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says. ‘I will fulfill My words against this city for harm instead of good. You will see them fulfilled on that day. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:17
“‘But I will rescue you on that day,’ declares the LORD. ‘You will not be handed to the men you fear. 📖

## Jeremiah 39:18
“‘I will certainly rescue you from death by the sword. You trusted Me, so you will escape with your life like plunder from war,’ declares the LORD.” 📖
