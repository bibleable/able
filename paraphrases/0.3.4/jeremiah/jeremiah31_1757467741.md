## Jeremiah 31:1
“At that time,” declares the LORD, “I will be God to all Israel’s families. They will be My people.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:2
The LORD says, “The people who survived the sword found favor in the wilderness when Israel sought rest.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:3
The LORD appeared to us in the past. He said, “I have loved you with everlasting love. So I have drawn you with loving devotion. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:4
“Virgin Israel, I will build you again, and you will be rebuilt. Again you will take your tambourines and go out dancing joyfully. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:5
“Again you will plant vineyards on Samaria’s hills. The farmers will plant and enjoy the fruit. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:6
“A day will come when watchmen on Ephraim’s hills will call, ‘Get up! Let us go to Zion, to the LORD our God!’” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:7
The LORD says, “Sing joyfully for Jacob. Shout for the foremost nation! Let your praises be heard. Say, ‘LORD, save Your people, Israel’s survivors!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 31:8
“I will bring them from the northern land and gather them from earth’s farthest parts. They will include the blind, the lame, pregnant women, and women giving birth. They will return as a great assembly! 📖

## Jeremiah 31:9
“They will come weeping, and I will lead them as they plead. I will guide them beside streams on a level path where they will not stumble. I am Israel’s Father. Ephraim is My firstborn.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:10
Nations, hear the LORD’s word. Announce it in distant coastlands. “The One who scattered Israel will gather them. He will guard them as a shepherd guards his flock. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:11
“The LORD has ransomed Jacob. He has redeemed him from the hand that overpowered him. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:12
“They will come and shout joyfully on Zion’s heights. They will shine with joy over the LORD’s abundance: grain, new wine, oil, and young animals from the flocks and herds. Their life will be like a well-watered garden. They will never languish again. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:13
“Young women will dance joyfully, and young and old men will rejoice too. I will turn their mourning into joy. I will give them comfort and joy in place of sorrow. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:14
“I will fill the priests’ souls abundantly. I will satisfy My people with My goodness,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:15
The LORD says, “A voice is heard in Ramah, with mourning and great weeping. Rachel weeps for her children. She refuses comfort because they are no more.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:16
The LORD says, “Stop weeping and keep tears from your eyes. Your work will receive a reward,” declares the LORD. “Your children will return from the enemy’s land. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:17
“There is hope for your future,” declares the LORD. “Your children will return to their own land. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:18
“I have certainly heard Ephraim moan. ‘You disciplined me severely, like an untrained calf. Restore me so that I can return. You are the LORD my God. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:19
“‘After returning, I repented. After receiving instruction, I struck my thigh in grief. I was ashamed and humiliated because I bore the disgrace of my youth.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 31:20
“Is Ephraim not My precious son, a delightful child? I often speak against him, but I still remember him. My heart longs for him. I have great compassion for him,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:21
“Set up road markers and signposts. Remember the highway you traveled. Virgin Israel, return to these cities of yours. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:22
“Faithless daughter, how long will you wander? The LORD has created something new in the land. A woman will shelter a man.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:23
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “When I restore them from captivity, people will again say this throughout Judah and its cities. ‘May the LORD bless you, righteous dwelling place and holy mountain.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 31:24
“Judah and all its cities will live together in the land. Farmers and those who move with flocks will live there. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:25
“I will refresh every weary soul and restore all who are weak.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:26
Then I awoke and looked around. My sleep had been very pleasant. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:27
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will sow Israel’s house and Judah’s house with the seed of people and animals. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:28
“I watched over them to uproot, tear down, demolish, destroy, and bring disaster. In the same way, I will watch over them to build and plant,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:29
“In those days, people will stop saying, ‘The fathers ate sour grapes, and their children’s teeth were set on edge.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 31:30
“Each person will die for his own wrongdoing. Whoever eats sour grapes will have his own teeth set on edge. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:31
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will make a new covenant with Israel’s house and Judah’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:32
“It will differ from My covenant with their fathers. I took them by the hand and led them out of Egypt. They broke that covenant, although I was their husband,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:33
“After those days, I will make this covenant with Israel’s house,” declares the LORD. “I will put My law in their minds and write it on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be My people. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:34
“No one will need to tell his neighbor or brother, ‘Know the LORD.’ They will all know Me, from least to greatest,” declares the LORD. “I will forgive their wrongdoing. I will never again remember their sins.” 📖

## Jeremiah 31:35
The LORD gives the sun for daylight and orders the moon and stars for light at night. He stirs the sea so that its waves roar. His name is the LORD of Hosts. He says, 📖

## Jeremiah 31:36
“Only if this fixed order left My presence would Israel’s descendants stop being a nation before Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:37
The LORD says, “Only if someone could measure the heavens above and search out earth’s foundations below would I reject all Israel’s descendants. Only then would I reject them for everything they have done,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:38
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when this city will be rebuilt for Me from Hananel’s tower to the Corner Gate. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:39
“The measuring line will stretch straight to Gareb’s hill again. Then it will turn toward Goah. 📖

## Jeremiah 31:40
“The entire valley of corpses and ashes will be holy to the LORD. So will all fields to the Kidron Valley and the corner of the Horse Gate toward the east. It will never be uprooted or demolished again.” 📖
