## Jeremiah 1:1
These are the words of Jeremiah, Hilkiah’s son. He was one of the priests in Anathoth, in Benjamin’s territory. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:2
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah in the thirteenth year of Josiah’s reign. Josiah, Amon’s son, was king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:3
The word continued through the reign of Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. It continued until the fifth month of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. Then Jerusalem’s people went into exile. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:4
The LORD’s word came to me. He said, 📖

## Jeremiah 1:5
“I knew you before I formed you in the womb. Before your birth, I set you apart. I appointed you as a prophet to the nations.” 📖

## Jeremiah 1:6
I answered, “Ah, Lord GOD! I do not know how to speak. I am only a child!” 📖

## Jeremiah 1:7
The LORD told me, “Do not say, ‘I am only a child.’ Go to everyone I send you to. Say everything I command you to say. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:8
“Do not fear them. I am with you to rescue you,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:9
The LORD reached out His hand and touched my mouth. He told me, “I have put My words in your mouth. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:10
“Today I appoint you over nations and kingdoms. You will uproot and tear down, destroy and overthrow, build and plant.” 📖

## Jeremiah 1:11
The LORD’s word came to me. He asked, “Jeremiah, what do you see?” I answered, “I see an almond branch.” 📖

## Jeremiah 1:12
The LORD said, “You have seen correctly. I am watching over My word to fulfill it.” 📖

## Jeremiah 1:13
The LORD’s word came to me again. He asked, “What do you see?” I answered, “I see a boiling pot. It tilts toward us from the north.” 📖

## Jeremiah 1:14
The LORD told me, “Disaster from the north will pour over everyone who lives in the land. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:15
“I am about to call all the clans and kingdoms of the north,” declares the LORD. “Their kings will set their thrones at Jerusalem’s gates. They will attack all its surrounding walls and all Judah’s other cities. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:16
“I will pronounce My judgments against them for all their wickedness. They have abandoned Me, burned incense to other gods, and worshiped things their own hands made. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:17
“Get ready. Stand and tell them everything I command you. Do not let them frighten you, or I will terrify you before them. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:18
“Today I have made you like a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the whole land. You stand against Judah’s kings, officials, priests, and people. 📖

## Jeremiah 1:19
“They will fight you, but they will never overcome you. I am with you to rescue you,” declares the LORD. 📖
