## Jeremiah 26:1
This word came from the LORD early in the reign of Jehoiakim, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:2
The LORD says, “Stand in the courtyard of My house. Speak to people from all Judah’s cities who come to worship there. Say every word I commanded you. Leave out nothing. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:3
“Perhaps they will listen, and each will turn from his evil way. Then I may change My mind about the disaster I planned because of their evil deeds. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:4
“Tell them what the LORD says. ‘If you refuse to listen to Me and follow the law I put before you, 📖

## Jeremiah 26:5
“‘and refuse to listen to My servants the prophets, whom I sent repeatedly despite your refusal to listen, 📖

## Jeremiah 26:6
“‘then I will make this house like Shiloh. I will make this city an object of cursing among all earth’s nations.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 26:7
The priests, prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah speak these words in the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:8
Jeremiah finished telling everyone all the LORD had commanded. Then the priests, prophets, and all the people seized him. They shouted, “You must die! 📖

## Jeremiah 26:9
“How dare you prophesy in the LORD’s name that this house will become like Shiloh? How dare you say this city will become desolate and deserted?” All the people gathered against Jeremiah in the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:10
Judah’s officials heard about this. They went from the king’s palace to the LORD’s house. They sat at the entrance of the New Gate. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:11
The priests and prophets told the officials and everyone else, “This man deserves death. You heard him prophesy against this city with your own ears!” 📖

## Jeremiah 26:12
Jeremiah answered all the officials and people. “The LORD sent me to speak every word you heard against this house and city. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:13
“Correct your ways and deeds. Obey the LORD your God. Then He may change His mind about the disaster He announced against you. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:14
“I am in your hands. Do to me what you think is good and right. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:15
“But know this for certain. If you kill me, you will bring innocent blood on yourselves, this city, and its people. The LORD truly sent me to speak all these words for you to hear.” 📖

## Jeremiah 26:16
The officials and everyone else told the priests and prophets, “This man does not deserve death. He spoke to us in the LORD our God’s name!” 📖

## Jeremiah 26:17
Some elders of the land stood and spoke to the whole assembly. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:18
“Micah the Moreshite prophesied during the reign of Hezekiah, Judah’s king. He told all Judah’s people what the LORD of Hosts said. ‘Zion will be plowed like a field. Jerusalem will become a pile of rubble. The temple mountain will become a wooded ridge.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 26:19
“Did Hezekiah, Judah’s king, or anyone in Judah kill him? Did Hezekiah not fear the LORD and seek His favor? Did the LORD not change His mind about the disaster He announced? We are about to bring great harm on ourselves!” 📖

## Jeremiah 26:20
Another man prophesied in the LORD’s name: Uriah, Shemaiah’s son, from Kiriath-jearim. He prophesied the same things as Jeremiah against this city and land. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:21
King Jehoiakim and all his mighty men and officials heard Uriah’s words. The king wanted to kill him. Uriah learned this and fled to Egypt in fear. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:22
King Jehoiakim sent Elnathan, Achbor’s son, and some other men to Egypt. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:23
They brought Uriah from Egypt to King Jehoiakim. The king had him killed with the sword. He had Uriah’s body thrown into the common people’s burial place. 📖

## Jeremiah 26:24
But Ahikam, Shaphan’s son, supported Jeremiah. So Jeremiah was not handed to the people to be killed. 📖
