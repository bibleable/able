## Jeremiah 11:1
This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:2
“Listen to this covenant’s words. Tell them to Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:3
“Tell them what the LORD, Israel’s God, says. ‘A curse is on anyone who does not obey this covenant’s words. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:4
“‘I commanded your forefathers to obey it when I brought them out of Egypt, the iron furnace. I said, “Obey Me and do everything I command. You will be My people, and I will be your God.” 📖

## Jeremiah 11:5
“‘This would fulfill My oath to your forefathers. I swore to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is today.’” I answered, “Amen, LORD.” 📖

## Jeremiah 11:6
The LORD told me, “Announce all these words in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets. Say, ‘Hear this covenant’s words and obey them. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:7
“‘From the time I brought your fathers out of Egypt until today, I warned them strongly and repeatedly. I said, “Obey My voice.” 📖

## Jeremiah 11:8
“‘But they would not obey or listen. Each followed his stubborn, evil heart. So I brought all this covenant’s curses on them. I had commanded them to keep the covenant, but they did not.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 11:9
The LORD told me, “Judah’s men and Jerusalem’s people are conspiring. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:10
“They have returned to their forefathers’ sins. Their forefathers refused to obey My words. They have followed other gods to serve them. The houses of Israel and Judah have broken My covenant with their fathers. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:11
“So the LORD says, ‘I will bring a disaster they cannot escape. They will cry to Me, but I will not listen. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:12
“‘Then Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s people will cry to the gods to which they burned incense. But those gods will certainly not save them during their disaster. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:13
“‘Judah, you have as many gods as cities. You have set up as many shameful altars as Jerusalem has streets. These are altars for burning incense to Baal.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 11:14
“Do not pray for these people. Do not cry out or pray for them. I will not listen when they call to Me during their disaster. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:15
“What right has My beloved to be in My house after carrying out so many evil schemes? Can consecrated meat prevent your doom? You rejoice when you do evil. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:16
“The LORD once called you a flourishing olive tree, beautiful with well-formed fruit. But with a mighty roar, He will set it on fire. Its branches will be consumed. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:17
“The LORD of Hosts planted you. He has decreed disaster because of the evil the houses of Israel and Judah brought on themselves. They made Me angry by burning incense to Baal.” 📖

## Jeremiah 11:18
The LORD informed me, so I knew. You showed me their deeds. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:19
I was like a gentle lamb led to slaughter. I did not know their plot against me. They said, “Destroy the tree with its fruit. Cut him off from the land of the living. Then no one will remember his name.” 📖

## Jeremiah 11:20
LORD of Hosts, You judge righteously and examine hearts and minds. Let me see Your vengeance on them. I have entrusted my case to You. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:21
The LORD speaks about Anathoth’s people who want to kill you. They say, “Do not prophesy in the LORD’s name, or we will kill you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 11:22
The LORD of Hosts says, “I will punish them. Their young men will die by the sword. Their sons and daughters will die from famine. 📖

## Jeremiah 11:23
“No survivors will remain. I will bring disaster on Anathoth’s people in the year of their punishment.” 📖
