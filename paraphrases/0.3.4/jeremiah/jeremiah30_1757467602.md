## Jeremiah 30:1
This word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:2
The LORD, Israel’s God, says, “Write all the words I have spoken to you in a book. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:3
“Days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will restore My people Israel and Judah from captivity. I will return them to the land I gave their fathers. They will possess it,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:4
These are the LORD’s words about Israel and Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:5
The LORD says, “A cry of panic can be heard. It is a cry of terror instead of peace. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:6
“Ask and see whether a male can give birth. Why do all the men hold their stomachs like women giving birth? Why has every face turned pale? 📖

## Jeremiah 30:7
“That day will be terrible! No other day will be like it. It is the time of Jacob’s distress, but he will be saved from it. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:8
“On that day,” declares the LORD of Hosts, “I will break the yoke from their necks and tear off their bonds. Foreigners will no longer enslave them. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:9
“They will serve the LORD their God and David their king. I will raise him up for them. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:10
“My servant Jacob, do not fear,” declares the LORD. “Israel, do not be dismayed. I will certainly save you from a distant place and your descendants from their captivity. Jacob will return to peace and ease. No one will frighten him. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:11
“I am with you to save you,” declares the LORD. “I will completely destroy all the nations where I scattered you. But I will not completely destroy you. I will discipline you justly. I will certainly punish you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 30:12
The LORD says, “Your injury is incurable. Your wound is severe. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:13
“No one pleads your case. There is no remedy for your sores and no recovery for you. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:14
“All your lovers have forgotten you and no longer seek you. I struck you like an enemy and disciplined you like someone cruel. This is because of your great wrongdoing and many sins. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:15
“Why do you cry over your wound? Your pain cannot be cured! I did these things because of your great wrongdoing and many sins. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:16
“But all who devour you will be devoured. Every enemy will go into exile. Those who plunder you will be plundered. Those who raid you will be raided. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:17
“I will restore your health and heal your wounds,” declares the LORD. “They call you an outcast, Zion, whom no one cares about.” 📖

## Jeremiah 30:18
The LORD says, “I will restore the fortunes of Jacob’s tents and have compassion on his homes. The city will be rebuilt on its own ruins. The palace will stand in its proper place. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:19
“Thanksgiving and sounds of celebration will come from them. I will increase their numbers, and they will not decrease. I will honor them, and no one will belittle them. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:20
“Their children will be as they were long ago. Their congregation will be established before Me. I will punish all their oppressors. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:21
“Their leader will be one of their own. Their ruler will come from among them. I will bring him near, and he will approach Me. Who would dare approach Me on his own?” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:22
“You will be My people, and I will be your God.” 📖

## Jeremiah 30:23
The LORD’s storm has gone out in fury. A whirlwind swirls down on the heads of the wicked. 📖

## Jeremiah 30:24
The LORD’s fierce anger will not turn back until He fulfills all His heart’s purposes. In the days ahead, you will understand this. 📖
