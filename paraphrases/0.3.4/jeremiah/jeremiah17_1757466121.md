## Jeremiah 17:1
“Judah’s sin is written with an iron stylus. A diamond point engraves it on the tablets of their hearts and the horns of their altars. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:2
“Even their children remember their altars and Asherah poles beside green trees and on high hills. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:3
“My mountain in the countryside, I will give your wealth and all your treasures as plunder. This is because of the sin of your high places throughout your territory. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:4
“You yourself will give up the inheritance I gave you. I will enslave you to your enemies in an unknown land. You have lit My anger, and it will burn forever.” 📖

## Jeremiah 17:5
The LORD says, “A curse is on anyone who trusts people, makes mere flesh his strength, and turns his heart from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:6
“He will be like a desert shrub. He will not see prosperity when it comes. He will live in dry desert places, in a salt land where no one lives. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:7
“But a blessing is on anyone who trusts the LORD and puts his confidence in Him. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:8
“He is like a tree planted by water that sends roots toward the stream. It does not fear heat, and its leaves stay green. It does not worry in a year of drought. It never stops producing fruit. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:9
“The heart is more deceitful than anything else and cannot be cured. Who can understand it? 📖

## Jeremiah 17:10
“I, the LORD, search hearts and examine minds. I reward each person according to his ways and what his deeds deserve. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:11
“Someone who gains riches unjustly is like a partridge hatching eggs it did not lay. His riches will leave him in the middle of his life. In the end, he will be a fool.” 📖

## Jeremiah 17:12
Our sanctuary is a glorious throne, raised high from the beginning. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:13
LORD, Israel’s hope, everyone who abandons You will be shamed. Those who turn away will be written in the dust. They have abandoned the LORD, the fountain of living water. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:14
LORD, heal me, and I will be healed. Save me, and I will be saved. You are my praise. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:15
They keep asking me, “Where is the LORD’s word? Let it come now!” 📖

## Jeremiah 17:16
I have not run away from being Your shepherd. I have not wanted the day of despair. You know that I spoke my words in Your presence. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:17
Do not become a terror to me. You are my shelter in the day of disaster. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:18
Let my persecutors be shamed and terrified. But do not let me be shamed or terrified. Bring the day of disaster on them. Shatter them with double destruction. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:19
The LORD told me, “Stand at the people’s gate, where Judah’s kings enter and leave. Stand at all Jerusalem’s other gates too. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:20
“Say to Judah’s kings and all people of Judah and Jerusalem who enter these gates, ‘Hear the LORD’s word.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 17:21
The LORD says, “Take care. Do not carry a load or bring one through Jerusalem’s gates on the Sabbath day. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:22
“Do not carry loads out of your houses or do any work on the Sabbath day. Keep it holy, as I commanded your forefathers. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:23
“But they refused to listen or pay attention. They stubbornly resisted, refused to listen, and rejected My discipline. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:24
“If you listen carefully to Me and keep the Sabbath holy,” says the LORD, “this is what will happen. This means bringing no loads through this city’s gates and doing no work on the Sabbath. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:25
“Then kings and princes who sit on David’s throne will enter this city’s gates. They will ride chariots and horses with their officials, Judah’s men, and Jerusalem’s people. People will live in this city forever. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:26
“People will come from Judah’s cities, Jerusalem’s surroundings, Benjamin’s land, the foothills, the hill country, and the Negev. They will bring burnt offerings, sacrifices, grain offerings, frankincense, and thank offerings to the LORD’s house. 📖

## Jeremiah 17:27
“But if you refuse to obey Me and keep the Sabbath holy by bringing no loads through Jerusalem’s gates, I will light a fire there. No one will put it out. It will consume Jerusalem’s citadels.” 📖
