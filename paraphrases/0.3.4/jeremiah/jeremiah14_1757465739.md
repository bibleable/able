## Jeremiah 14:1
This word about the drought came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:2
“Judah mourns, and her gates languish. Her people cry out for the land. A cry rises from Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:3
“Nobles send their servants to the cisterns for water. But the servants find none and return with empty jars. They cover their heads in shame and humiliation. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:4
“The ground is cracked because no rain has fallen on the land. The farmers cover their heads in shame. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:5
“Even a female deer in the field abandons her newborn fawn because there is no grass. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:6
“Wild donkeys stand on bare heights. They pant for air like jackals. Their eyes fail because there is no pasture.” 📖

## Jeremiah 14:7
LORD, our wrongdoing testifies against us. But act for Your name’s sake. We have rebelled many times. We have sinned against You. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:8
Hope of Israel, its Savior in times of distress, why are You like a stranger in the land? Why are You like a traveler who stays only one night? 📖

## Jeremiah 14:9
Why are You like a surprised man or a warrior unable to save? Yet You are among us, LORD, and we bear Your name. Do not abandon us! 📖

## Jeremiah 14:10
The LORD says about these people, “They truly love to wander. They have not restrained their feet. So the LORD does not accept them. He will now remember their wrongdoing and punish their sins.” 📖

## Jeremiah 14:11
The LORD told me, “Do not pray for these people’s well-being. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:12
“Even if they fast, I will not hear their cries. Even if they offer burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. I will finish them off by sword, famine, and plague.” 📖

## Jeremiah 14:13
I answered, “Ah, Lord GOD! The prophets tell them, ‘You will face neither sword nor famine. I will give you lasting peace here.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 14:14
The LORD answered, “The prophets speak lies in My name. I did not send or appoint them. I did not speak to them. Their prophecies are false visions, worthless divination, and the empty delusions of their own minds. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:15
“The LORD says this about the prophets who prophesy in My name. I did not send them. Yet they say, ‘Neither sword nor famine will touch this land.’ These same prophets will die by sword and famine! 📖

## Jeremiah 14:16
“Their hearers will be thrown into Jerusalem’s streets because of famine and sword. No one will bury them, their wives, their sons, or their daughters. I will pour their own evil over them. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:17
“Tell them, ‘My eyes overflow with tears that never stop, day or night. The virgin daughter of my people has suffered a crushing blow and a very severe wound. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:18
“‘In the country, I see people killed by the sword. In the city, I see people ravaged by famine. Both prophet and priest travel to an unknown land.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 14:19
Have You completely rejected Judah? Do You despise Zion? Why have You struck us beyond healing? We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for healing, but only terror came. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:20
LORD, we admit our wickedness and our fathers’ guilt. We have sinned against You. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:21
For Your name’s sake, do not despise us. Do not disgrace Your glorious throne. Remember Your covenant with us. Do not break it. 📖

## Jeremiah 14:22
Can the nations’ worthless idols bring rain? Can the skies send showers by themselves? LORD our God, are You not the One who does this? We put our hope in You, because You have done all these things. 📖
