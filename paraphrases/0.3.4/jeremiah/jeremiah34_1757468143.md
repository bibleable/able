## Jeremiah 34:1
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah while Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, fought Jerusalem and its surrounding cities. His entire army, all earth’s kingdoms under his rule, and all the other nations fought with him. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:2
The LORD, Israel’s God, told Jeremiah to speak to Zedekiah, Judah’s king. “Tell him what the LORD says. I will hand this city to Babylon’s king. He will burn it down. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:3
“You will not escape him. You will certainly be captured and handed to him. You will see Babylon’s king eye to eye and speak face to face. You will go to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:4
“But hear the LORD’s word, Zedekiah, Judah’s king. The LORD says you will not die by the sword. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:5
“You will die in peace. People burned spices for your fathers, the kings before you. They will burn spices for you and mourn, ‘Alas, master!’ I Myself have spoken this word,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:6
The prophet Jeremiah told Zedekiah, Judah’s king, all these words in Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:7
Babylon’s royal army was fighting Jerusalem and Judah’s remaining cities, Lachish and Azekah. These were Judah’s only remaining fortified cities. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:8
King Zedekiah made a covenant with all Jerusalem’s people to proclaim freedom. Afterward, the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:9
Each person was to free his Hebrew slaves, male and female. No one was to keep a fellow Jew in slavery. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:10
All the officials and people who joined the covenant agreed to free their male and female slaves. They would no longer keep them in slavery. They obeyed and released them. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:11
Later, they changed their minds. They took back the male and female slaves they had freed. They forced them into slavery again. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:12
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. He said, 📖

## Jeremiah 34:13
“The LORD, Israel’s God, says this. I made a covenant with your forefathers when I brought them out of Egypt, the house of slavery. I said, 📖

## Jeremiah 34:14
“‘Every seventh year, each of you must free his Hebrew brother who sold himself to you. He may serve six years. Then you must free him.’ But your fathers refused to listen or pay attention. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:15
“Recently you repented and pleased Me by proclaiming freedom for your neighbors. You made a covenant before Me in the house that bears My Name. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:16
“Now you have changed your minds and profaned My name. You freed your male and female slaves to go wherever they wished. But each of you took them back and forced them to serve as slaves again. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:17
“So the LORD says this. You disobeyed Me and did not proclaim freedom for your brothers and neighbors. Now I proclaim freedom for you,” declares the LORD. “You are free to fall by sword, plague, and famine! I will make you a horror to all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:18
“You broke My covenant and failed to keep its terms before Me. You cut a calf in two and passed between its pieces. I will treat you like that calf. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:19
“Judah and Jerusalem’s officials, the court officials, the priests, and all the land’s people passed between the calf’s pieces. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:20
“I will hand them to enemies who want to kill them. Their corpses will feed the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:21
“I will hand Judah’s king Zedekiah and his officials to enemies who want to kill them. I will hand them to Babylon’s royal army, which withdrew from you. 📖

## Jeremiah 34:22
“I will give the command,” declares the LORD, “and bring them back to this city. They will attack, capture, and burn it. I will make Judah’s cities desolate, with no one living there.” 📖
