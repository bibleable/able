## Jeremiah 29:1
The prophet Jeremiah sent a letter from Jerusalem to the surviving elders among the exiles, the priests, the prophets, and all the others. Nebuchadnezzar had exiled them from Jerusalem to Babylon. The letter’s text follows. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:2
This was after King Jeconiah, the queen mother, court officials, Judah and Jerusalem’s officials, craftsmen, and metalsmiths had been exiled from Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:3
Elasah, Shaphan’s son, and Gemariah, Hilkiah’s son, carried the letter. Zedekiah, Judah’s king, had sent them to King Nebuchadnezzar in Babylon. The letter said, 📖

## Jeremiah 29:4
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, speaks to all the exiles taken from Jerusalem to Babylon. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:5
“Build houses and settle down. Plant gardens and eat their produce. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:6
“Marry and have sons and daughters. Find wives for your sons and husbands for your daughters, so they too can have sons and daughters. Increase there. Do not decrease. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:7
“Seek the prosperity of the city where I sent you into exile. Pray to the LORD for it. If it prospers, you will prosper too.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:8
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “Do not let the prophets and diviners among you deceive you. Do not listen to the dreams you draw from them. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:9
“They prophesy falsely to you in My name. I did not send them,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:10
The LORD says, “When Babylon’s seventy years end, I will attend to you. I will confirm My promise to bring you back here. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:11
“I know My plans for you,” declares the LORD. “They are plans to prosper you instead of harming you. They will give you a future and hope. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:12
“You will call to Me. You will come and pray, and I will listen. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:13
“You will seek and find Me when you search with your whole heart. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:14
“You will find Me,” declares the LORD. “I will restore you from captivity. I will gather you from every nation and place where I banished you,” declares the LORD. “I will bring you back to the place from which I exiled you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:15
You may say, “The LORD has raised prophets for us in Babylon.” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:16
So the LORD speaks about the king on David’s throne and everyone remaining in this city. These are your brothers who did not go into exile with you. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:17
The LORD of Hosts says, “I will send sword, famine, and plague against them. I will make them like rotten figs that no one can eat. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:18
“I will pursue them with sword, famine, and plague. I will make them a horror to all earth’s kingdoms. In every nation where I banish them, they will be a curse, a desolation, and an object of scorn and reproach. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:19
“They did not listen to My words,” declares the LORD. “I sent those words repeatedly through My servants the prophets. You exiles have not listened either,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:20
All you exiles I sent from Jerusalem to Babylon, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:21
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, speaks about Ahab, Kolaiah’s son, and Zedekiah, Maaseiah’s son. They prophesy lies to you in My name. “I will hand them to Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king. He will kill them before your eyes. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:22
“All Judah’s exiles in Babylon will use this curse because of them. ‘May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and Ahab, whom Babylon’s king roasted in fire!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 29:23
“They committed an outrage in Israel. They committed adultery with their neighbors’ wives and spoke lies in My name, which I never commanded. I know, and I am a witness,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:24
Tell Shemaiah the Nehelamite this. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:25
The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “You sent letters in your own name to all Jerusalem’s people. You also sent them to the priest Zephaniah, Maaseiah’s son, and all the priests. You told Zephaniah, 📖

## Jeremiah 29:26
“‘The LORD made you priest in Jehoiada’s place as chief officer in His house. You are responsible for every madman who acts as a prophet. Put him in stocks and neck irons. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:27
“‘Why have you not rebuked Jeremiah of Anathoth? He poses as a prophet among you. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:28
“‘He sent us this message in Babylon: “The exile will last a long time. Build houses and settle down. Plant gardens and eat their produce.”’” 📖

## Jeremiah 29:29
But the priest Zephaniah had read this letter to the prophet Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:30
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:31
“Send a message to all the exiles about Shemaiah the Nehelamite. Tell them what the LORD says. Shemaiah prophesied to you although I did not send him. He made you trust a lie. 📖

## Jeremiah 29:32
“So the LORD says, ‘I will certainly punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his descendants. He will have no one left among these people. He will not see the good I bring My people,’ declares the LORD. ‘He preached rebellion against the LORD.’” 📖
