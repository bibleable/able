## Jeremiah 9:1
If only my head were a spring of water and my eyes a fountain of tears! I would weep day and night for the slain daughter of my people. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:2
If only I had a traveler’s lodge in the wilderness! I would leave my people and go away. They are all adulterers, a crowd of faithless people. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:3
“They bend their tongues like bows. Lies defeat truth in the land. They go from one evil to another and disregard Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:4
“Everyone must guard against his neighbor. Do not trust any brother. Every brother acts deceitfully, and every friend spreads slander. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:5
“Each betrays his friend. No one speaks truth. They have trained their tongues to lie. They exhaust themselves with wrongdoing. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:6
“You live surrounded by deception. In their deceit, they refuse to know Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:7
The LORD of Hosts says, “I will refine and test them. What else can I do because of the daughter of My people? 📖

## Jeremiah 9:8
“Their tongues are deadly arrows that speak deceit. A man speaks peacefully to his neighbor. But in his heart, he sets a trap for him. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:9
“Should I not punish them for this?” declares the LORD. “Should I not take vengeance on a nation like this?” 📖

## Jeremiah 9:10
I will weep and cry out for the mountains. I will sing a funeral song for the wilderness pastures. They are scorched, so no one passes through, and no cattle can be heard. Both birds and animals have fled. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:11
“I will make Jerusalem a pile of rubble and a home for jackals. I will make Judah’s cities desolate, with no one living there.” 📖

## Jeremiah 9:12
Who is wise enough to understand this? To whom has the LORD spoken, so that he can explain it? Why is the land destroyed and scorched like a desert, with no one passing through? 📖

## Jeremiah 9:13
The LORD answered, “They have abandoned My law, which I put before them. They have not followed it or obeyed My voice. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:14
“Instead, they have followed their stubborn hearts. They have followed the Baals, as their fathers taught them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 9:15
So the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says, “I will feed these people wormwood. I will give them poisoned water to drink. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:16
“I will scatter them among nations that neither they nor their fathers have known. I will send a sword after them until I finish them off.” 📖

## Jeremiah 9:17
The LORD of Hosts says, “Pay attention. Call the women who wail. Send for the most skilled among them. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:18
“Let them come quickly and sing a lament over us. Let our eyes overflow with tears and our eyelids pour out water. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:19
“Cries of mourning come from Zion. ‘We are devastated! Our shame is great! We have left the land because our homes were torn down.’” 📖

## Jeremiah 9:20
Women, hear the LORD’s word. Open your ears to His words. Teach your daughters to wail. Teach one another to lament. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:21
Death has climbed through our windows and entered our fortresses. It cuts off children from the streets and young men from the town squares. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:22
Announce what the LORD says. “People’s corpses will fall like dung in an open field. They will lie like newly cut grain behind a reaper, with no one to gather it.” 📖

## Jeremiah 9:23
The LORD says, “The wise must not boast of their wisdom. The strong must not boast of their strength. The wealthy must not boast of their riches. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:24
“Let those who boast boast that they understand and know Me. I am the LORD, who acts with loving devotion, justice, and righteousness on earth. I delight in these things,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:25
The LORD declares, “Days are coming when I will punish everyone circumcised only in the flesh. 📖

## Jeremiah 9:26
“These include Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon, Moab, and all desert inhabitants who clip the hair at their temples. All these nations are uncircumcised. Israel’s entire house is uncircumcised in heart.” 📖
