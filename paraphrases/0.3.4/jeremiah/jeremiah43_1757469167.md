## Jeremiah 43:1
Jeremiah finished telling everyone all the LORD their God’s words. He said everything the LORD sent him to say. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:2
Azariah, Hoshaiah’s son, Johanan, Kareah’s son, and all the arrogant men answered Jeremiah. “You lie! The LORD our God did not send you to say, ‘Do not settle in Egypt.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 43:3
“Baruch, Neriah’s son, turns you against us. He wants to hand us to the Chaldeans so they will kill us or exile us to Babylon!” 📖

## Jeremiah 43:4
Johanan, Kareah’s son, and all the army commanders disobeyed the LORD’s command to stay in Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:5
Instead, Johanan, Kareah’s son, and all the army commanders took everyone remaining in Judah. These people had returned to Judah from every nation where they had been scattered. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:6
They took the men, women, children, and king’s daughters. They took everyone Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, had left with Gedaliah, Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson. They also took the prophet Jeremiah and Baruch, Neriah’s son. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:7
They entered Egypt because they disobeyed the LORD’s voice. They went as far as Tahpanhes. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:8
The LORD’s word came to Jeremiah at Tahpanhes. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:9
“While the Jews watch, take large stones. Bury them in the clay of the brick pavement at the entrance to Pharaoh’s palace in Tahpanhes. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:10
“Tell them what the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, says. ‘I will send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king. I will put his throne over these stones I embedded. He will spread his royal pavilion over them. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:11
“‘He will attack Egypt. He will bring death to those destined for death, captivity to those destined for captivity, and the sword to those destined for the sword. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:12
“‘I will light a fire in the temples of Egypt’s gods. Nebuchadnezzar will burn the temples and capture their gods. He will wrap himself in Egypt’s land as a shepherd wraps himself in his garment. He will leave unharmed. 📖

## Jeremiah 43:13
“‘He will destroy the sacred pillars of the sun’s temple in Egypt. He will burn the temples of Egypt’s gods.’” 📖
