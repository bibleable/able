## Jeremiah 8:1
The LORD declares, “At that time, bones will be taken from the graves. They will include Judah’s kings, officials, priests, prophets, and Jerusalem’s people. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:2
“Their bones will lie exposed to the sun, moon, and all heaven’s host. These are the things they loved, served, followed, consulted, and worshiped. No one will gather or bury their bones. They will lie like dung on the ground. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:3
“The survivors of this evil family will prefer death to life, wherever I have banished them,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:4
Tell them what the LORD says. “Do people fall and never get up? Does someone turn away and never return? 📖

## Jeremiah 8:5
“Why have these people turned away? Why does Jerusalem always turn away? They hold tightly to deceit and refuse to return. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:6
“I have listened and heard. They do not speak rightly. No one repents of his wickedness or asks, ‘What have I done?’ Each pursues his own course like a horse charging into battle. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:7
“The stork in the sky knows her appointed seasons. The turtledove, swift, and thrush migrate at their appointed times. But My people do not know the LORD’s requirements. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:8
“How can you say, ‘We are wise and have the LORD’s Law’? The scribes’ lying pens have produced deception. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:9
“The wise will be shamed, dismayed, and trapped. They have rejected the LORD’s word. What wisdom do they really have? 📖

## Jeremiah 8:10
“I will give their wives to other men and their fields to new owners. From the least to the greatest, all are greedy for gain. From prophet to priest, all practice deceit. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:11
“They treat the wound of the daughter of My people with little care. They say, ‘Peace, peace,’ although there is no peace. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:12
“Are they ashamed of the abomination they committed? No, they have no shame and do not know how to blush. They will fall among the fallen. When I punish them, they will collapse,” says the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:13
“I will take away their harvest,” declares the LORD. “No grapes will remain on the vine and no figs on the tree. Even the leaf will wither. They will lose everything I gave them.” 📖

## Jeremiah 8:14
Why do we sit here? Gather together and flee to the fortified cities. Let us perish there, because the LORD our God has doomed us. He gave us poisoned water to drink because we sinned against Him. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:15
We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for a time of healing, but only terror came. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:16
Enemy horses snort in Dan. Their mighty neighing makes the whole land shake. They come to devour the land and everything in it. They will devour the city and everyone who lives there. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:17
“I will send snakes among you,” declares the LORD. “These vipers cannot be charmed. They will bite you.” 📖

## Jeremiah 8:18
My sorrow is beyond healing. My heart is faint within me. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:19
Hear the daughter of my people cry from a distant land. “Is the LORD no longer in Zion? Is her King no longer there?” “Why have their carved images and worthless foreign idols made Me angry?” 📖

## Jeremiah 8:20
“The harvest has passed, and summer has ended. But we have not been saved.” 📖

## Jeremiah 8:21
The brokenness of the daughter of my people crushes me. I mourn, and horror grips me. 📖

## Jeremiah 8:22
Is there no balm in Gilead? Is there no physician there? Why has the daughter of my people not regained her health? 📖
