## Jeremiah 27:1
This word came from the LORD to Jeremiah early in the reign of Zedekiah, Josiah’s son, king of Judah. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:2
The LORD told me, “Make a yoke with leather straps. Put it on your neck. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:3
“Send word to the kings of Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, and Sidon. Use the envoys who came to Jerusalem to see Zedekiah, Judah’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:4
“Give them a message from the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, to take to their masters. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:5
“By My great power and outstretched arm, I made the earth and its people and animals. I give it to anyone I choose. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:6
“Now I have placed all these lands under My servant Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king. I have even put the field’s animals under him. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:7
“All nations will serve him, his son, and his grandson until his own land’s time comes. Then many nations and great kings will enslave him. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:8
“If a nation or kingdom refuses to serve Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, and put its neck under his yoke, I will punish it,” declares the LORD. “Sword, famine, and plague will strike it until I destroy it through him. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:9
“Do not listen to your prophets, diviners, dream interpreters, mediums, or sorcerers who say, ‘You will not serve Babylon’s king.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 27:10
“They prophesy lies that will remove you from your land. I will banish you, and you will perish. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:11
“If a nation puts its neck under the yoke of Babylon’s king and serves him, I will leave it in its land. It can cultivate the land and live there,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:12
I gave Zedekiah, Judah’s king, the same message. “Put your necks under the yoke of Babylon’s king. Serve him and his people, and live! 📖

## Jeremiah 27:13
“Why should you and your people die by sword, famine, and plague? The LORD decreed this against every nation that refuses to serve Babylon’s king. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:14
“Do not listen to the prophets who say, ‘You must not serve Babylon’s king.’ They prophesy lies to you. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:15
“I did not send them,” declares the LORD. “They prophesy falsely in My name. So I will banish you. You and the prophets who prophesy to you will perish.” 📖

## Jeremiah 27:16
I told the priests and all these people, “The LORD says, ‘Do not listen to your prophets. They say the articles from the LORD’s house will soon return from Babylon. They prophesy lies to you.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 27:17
“Do not listen to them. Serve Babylon’s king and live! Why should this city become a ruin? 📖

## Jeremiah 27:18
“If they are true prophets with the LORD’s word, let them plead with the LORD of Hosts. Let them ask that the remaining articles not go to Babylon. These remain in the LORD’s house, Judah’s royal palace, and Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:19
“The LORD of Hosts speaks about the pillars, the sea, the bases, and the other articles remaining in this city. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:20
“Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king, did not take these when he exiled Jeconiah, Jehoiakim’s son, king of Judah, from Jerusalem to Babylon. He also exiled all Judah and Jerusalem’s nobles. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:21
“The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, speaks about the articles remaining in His house, Judah’s royal palace, and Jerusalem. 📖

## Jeremiah 27:22
“‘They will go to Babylon,’ declares the LORD. ‘They will stay until the day I attend to them again. Then I will bring them back and restore them here.’” 📖
