## Jeremiah 2:1
The LORD’s word came to me. He said, 📖

## Jeremiah 2:2
“Go and announce this for Jerusalem to hear. The LORD says, ‘I remember your devotion in your youth and your love as a bride. You followed Me through the wilderness, through land where no one planted crops. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:3
“‘Israel was holy to the LORD, the firstfruits of His harvest. Everyone who devoured her was guilty. Disaster came on them,’” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:4
House of Jacob, hear the LORD’s word. All families of the house of Israel, listen. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:5
The LORD says, “What fault did your fathers find in Me that made them go so far away? They followed worthless idols and became worthless themselves. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:6
“They did not ask, ‘Where is the LORD who brought us out of Egypt? He led us through the wilderness, through deserts and pits. He led us through a land of drought and darkness. No one travels or lives there.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 2:7
“I brought you into fertile land to eat its fruit and rich produce. But you defiled My land and made My inheritance detestable. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:8
“The priests did not ask, ‘Where is the LORD?’ The law’s experts no longer knew Me. The leaders rebelled against Me. The prophets prophesied by Baal and followed useless idols. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:9
“So I will bring a case against you again,” declares the LORD. “I will also bring a case against your grandchildren. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:10
“Cross to Cyprus’s coasts and look. Send people to Kedar and examine carefully. See whether anything like this has ever happened. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:11
“Has any nation changed its gods, although they are not gods at all? Yet My people have exchanged their Glory for useless idols. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:12
“Heavens, be stunned at this! Be shocked and completely horrified,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:13
“My people have done two evil things. They have abandoned Me, the fountain of living water. They have dug their own cisterns. These cisterns are broken and cannot hold water. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:14
“Is Israel a slave? Was he born into slavery? Why has he become prey? 📖

## Jeremiah 2:15
“Young lions have roared loudly at him. They have ruined his land. His cities lie in ruins, with no one living there. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:16
“The men of Memphis and Tahpanhes have shaved the crown of your head. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:17
“You brought this on yourself by abandoning the LORD your God when He led you along the way, did you not? 📖

## Jeremiah 2:18
“What will you gain by going to Egypt to drink the Nile’s waters? What will you gain by going to Assyria to drink the Euphrates’ waters? 📖

## Jeremiah 2:19
“Your evil will discipline you. Your acts of turning away from Me will rebuke you. Consider how evil and bitter it is to abandon the LORD your God. Realize how evil it is to have no fear of Me,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:20
“Long ago, you broke your yoke and tore off your chains. You said, ‘I will not serve!’ On every high hill and under every green tree, you lay as a prostitute. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:21
“I planted you like a choice vine from the best seed. How could you turn into a rotten, wild vine before Me? 📖

## Jeremiah 2:22
“You may wash with lye and use much soap. But the stain of your guilt remains before Me,” declares the Lord GOD. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:23
“How can you say, ‘I am not defiled, and I have not followed the Baals’? Look at your actions in the valley. Admit what you have done. You are a swift young female camel that runs here and there. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:24
“You are a wild donkey at home in the wilderness. She sniffs the wind with intense desire. Who can restrain her passion? Those who seek her need not tire themselves. They will find her in mating season. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:25
“You should have kept your feet from becoming bare and your throat from becoming thirsty. But you said, ‘There is no hope! I love foreign gods. I must follow them.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 2:26
“A thief feels shame when caught. In the same way, Israel’s house is disgraced. This includes its kings, officials, priests, and prophets. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:27
“They tell a tree, ‘You are my father.’ They tell a stone, ‘You gave me birth.’ They turn their backs to Me instead of their faces. But in trouble they say, ‘Rise and save us!’ 📖

## Jeremiah 2:28
“Where are the gods you made for yourselves? Let them rise and save you in your trouble, if they can. Judah, you have as many gods as cities. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:29
“Why do you bring a case against Me? All of you have rebelled against Me,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:30
“I struck your sons without success. They accepted no discipline. Your own sword devoured your prophets like a hungry lion. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:31
People of this generation, consider the LORD’s word. “Have I been a wilderness to Israel or a land of deep darkness? Why do My people say, ‘We are free to roam. We will never come to You again’? 📖

## Jeremiah 2:32
“Does a young woman forget her jewelry? Does a bride forget her wedding sash? Yet My people have forgotten Me for more days than anyone can count. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:33
“You pursue love with such skill! Even the most immoral women could learn from your ways. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:34
“Your skirts also bear the blood of the innocent poor. You did not catch them breaking in. Yet despite all these things, 📖

## Jeremiah 2:35
“you say, ‘I am innocent. Surely His anger will turn away from me.’ I will judge you because you say, ‘I have not sinned.’ 📖

## Jeremiah 2:36
“You act impulsively and keep changing your ways! Egypt will disappoint you, just as Assyria did. 📖

## Jeremiah 2:37
“You will leave that place with your hands on your head. The LORD has rejected those you trust. Their help will not make you prosper.” 📖
