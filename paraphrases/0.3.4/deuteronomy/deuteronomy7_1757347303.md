## Deuteronomy 7:1
The LORD your God will bring you into the land and drive out seven larger, stronger nations. They are Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:2
When He delivers them to you for defeat, devote them to complete destruction. Make no treaty and show no mercy. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:3
Do not intermarry. Neither give your daughters to their sons nor take their daughters for yours. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:4
They will turn your sons from following Me toward other gods. The LORD’s anger will burn, and He will quickly destroy you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:5
Destroy their altars, smash their sacred pillars, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their idols. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:6
You are holy to the LORD your God. He chose you from all earthly peoples as His prized possession. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:7
He did not choose and love you because you outnumbered others. You were the fewest of all peoples. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:8
He loved you and kept His oath to your fathers. His mighty hand redeemed you from slavery under Pharaoh, Egypt’s king. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:9
Know that the LORD your God is God. He faithfully keeps His covenant of loving devotion for a thousand generations of those loving Him and obeying His commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:10
He repays those hating Him with destruction to their faces. He does not delay. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:11
Keep the commands, statutes, and ordinances I give today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:12
Listen to these ordinances and obey carefully. Then the LORD your God will keep His covenant and loving devotion sworn to your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:13
He will love, bless, and multiply you in the land promised to your fathers. He will bless your children, crops, grain, new wine, oil, young cattle, and lambs. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:14
You will be blessed above all peoples. Neither your men, women, nor livestock will be barren. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:15
He will remove all sickness from you. He will not give you any terrible disease you knew in Egypt. He will inflict those diseases on all who hate you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:16
Destroy all peoples He delivers to you without pity. Never worship their gods, which would trap you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:17
You may wonder how to drive out nations greater than yourselves. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:18
Do not fear them. Remember what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:19
You saw great trials, signs, wonders, His mighty hand, and His outstretched arm bringing you out. He will do likewise to every people you fear. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:20
He will send the hornet until even survivors hiding from you perish. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:21
Do not fear them. The LORD your God among you is great and awesome. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:22
He will drive nations out gradually. You cannot eliminate them at once, because wild animals would multiply around you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:23
He will deliver them to you and throw them into great confusion until they are destroyed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:24
He will hand over their kings. You will erase their names from under heaven. No one will withstand you. You will annihilate them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:25
Burn their gods’ images. Neither covet nor take their silver and gold, which could trap you. It is detestable to the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 7:26
Bring nothing detestable home, or you too will be devoted to destruction. Utterly hate and abhor it, because it is set apart for destruction. 📖
