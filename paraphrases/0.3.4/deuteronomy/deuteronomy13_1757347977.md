## Deuteronomy 13:1
A prophet or dreamer may arise among you and announce a sign or wonder. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:2
The sign or wonder may happen. But he may say, “Let us follow and worship other gods,” gods you have not known. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:3
If so, do not listen to that prophet or dreamer. The LORD your God is testing whether you love Him with your whole heart and soul. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:4
Follow the LORD your God and fear Him. Keep His commands and listen to His voice. Serve Him and hold firmly to Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:5
Put that prophet or dreamer to death for urging rebellion against the LORD your God. He brought you from Egypt and freed you from slavery. The prophet tried to turn you from the path He commanded. Thus remove evil from among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:6
Your own brother, son, daughter, beloved wife, or closest friend may secretly urge you to worship other gods. Neither you nor your fathers knew those gods. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:7
They may be gods of peoples near or far, from one end of earth to the other. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:8
If this happens, do not agree or listen. Do not pity, spare, or protect that person. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:9
You must put that person to death. Your hand must strike first, and then the hands of all the people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:10
Stone that person to death for trying to turn you from the LORD your God. He brought you from Egypt, out of slavery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:11
All Israel will hear and fear. They will never again do such evil among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:12
You may hear a report about one of the towns the LORD your God gives you to live in. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:13
Wicked men from among you may have misled its people. They may have said, “Let us serve other gods,” gods you have not known. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:14
If so, question people and investigate thoroughly. Establish with certainty whether this hateful act happened among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:15
If it did, kill that town’s inhabitants with the sword. Set apart all its people and livestock for destruction. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:16
Gather all its plunder in the public square. Burn the whole town and all its plunder as a whole burnt offering to the LORD your God. It must remain a mound of ruins forever, never rebuilt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:17
Keep nothing set apart for destruction. Then the LORD can turn from His fierce anger, give you mercy and compassion, and multiply you as He swore to your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 13:18
This is because you obey Him, keep every command I give today, and do what He sees as right. 📖
