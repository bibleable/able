## Deuteronomy 2:1
We turned toward the wilderness by the Red Sea route, as the LORD instructed me. We wandered around Mount Seir for many days. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:2
The LORD then told me, 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:3
“You wandered these hills long enough. Turn north. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:4
Tell the people, ‘You will pass through your brothers’ territory in Seir. They descend from Esau. They will fear you, so be very careful. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:5
Do not provoke them. I will give you none of their land, not even a footprint. I gave Mount Seir to Esau. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:6
Pay them silver for your food and water.’” 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:7
The LORD your God blessed all your work and watched your journey through this vast wilderness. He stayed with you forty years. You lacked nothing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:8
We passed our brothers, Esau’s descendants in Seir. We left the Arabah road from Elath and Ezion-geber and took Moab’s wilderness road. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:9
The LORD said, “Do not harass Moab or provoke war. I will give you none of their land. I gave Ar to Lot’s descendants.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:10
The Emites formerly lived there. They were great and numerous, as tall as Anakites. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:11
They were regarded as Rephaim, like the Anakites. Moabites called them Emites. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:12
Horites formerly lived in Seir. Esau’s descendants destroyed and drove them out, settling in their place. Israel similarly occupied the land the LORD gave them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:13
“Cross the Brook of Zered now.” We crossed it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:14
Thirty-eight years passed between leaving Kadesh-barnea and crossing the Brook of Zered. The entire generation of fighting men died from the camp, as the LORD swore. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:15
The LORD’s hand opposed them until He eliminated them all from the camp. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:16
All those fighting men had died. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:17
Then the LORD told me, 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:18
“Today you will cross Moab’s border at Ar. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:19
Do not harass or provoke the Ammonites when approaching them. I will give you none of their land. I gave it to Lot’s descendants.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:20
That land also belonged formerly to the Rephaim. The Ammonites called them Zamzummites. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:21
They were great and numerous, as tall as Anakites. The LORD destroyed them before the Ammonites, who drove them out and settled there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:22
He likewise destroyed the Horites before Esau’s descendants in Seir. They drove the Horites out and live there to this day. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:23
Avvim lived in villages as far as Gaza. Caphtorites from Caphtor destroyed them and settled in their place. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:24
“Cross the Arnon Valley. I delivered Sihon the Amorite, Heshbon’s king, and his land into your hand. Begin taking his land and fight him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:25
Today I will begin making all nations under heaven dread and fear you. Reports of you will make them tremble in anguish.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:26
From Kedemoth’s wilderness, I offered peace through messengers to Sihon, Heshbon’s king. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:27
“Let us cross your land on the main road. We will turn neither right nor left. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:28
Sell us food and water for silver. Let us pass on foot. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:29
Esau’s descendants in Seir and Moab’s people in Ar did this for us. Let us cross the Jordan to the land the LORD our God gives us.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:30
Sihon refused passage. The LORD your God made his spirit stubborn and his heart obstinate to deliver him into your hand, as happened today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:31
The LORD told me, “I began delivering Sihon and his land to you. Begin conquering and possessing it.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:32
Sihon and his whole army fought us at Jahaz. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:33
The LORD our God delivered them to us. We defeated Sihon, his sons, and his entire army. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:34
We captured all his cities and devoted their people to destruction, including women and children. We left no survivors. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:35
We kept only livestock and plunder from the captured cities. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:36
We took every city from Aroer on the Arnon Valley’s rim, including the valley city, as far as Gilead. No walls were too high. The LORD our God gave us every city. 📖

## Deuteronomy 2:37
You avoided Ammonite land, the Jabbok River’s banks, hill country cities, and every place the LORD our God forbade. 📖
