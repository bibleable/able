## Deuteronomy 26:1
You will enter, possess, and settle in the land the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:2
Then put some first produce from every crop grown in the land He gives you into a basket. Go to the place He chooses as a home for His Name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:3
Tell the priest serving then, “Today I declare to the LORD your God that I have entered the land He swore to give our fathers.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:4
The priest must take the basket from you and place it before the LORD your God’s altar. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:5
Declare before Him, “My father was a wandering Aramean. He went to Egypt with few people and lived there. There he became a great, powerful, numerous nation. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:6
But the Egyptians mistreated and oppressed us. They forced us into hard labor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:7
We cried to the LORD, our fathers’ God. He heard us and saw our suffering, toil, and oppression. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:8
He brought us from Egypt with a mighty hand, an outstretched arm, great terror, signs, and wonders. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:9
He brought us here and gave us this land flowing with milk and honey. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:10
Now, LORD, I bring the first produce from the land You gave me.” Place the basket before the LORD your God and bow before Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:11
Rejoice in all the good things He gave you and your household. Include the Levite and the foreigner living among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:12
In the third year, the year of the tithe, set aside a tenth of all your produce. Give it to Levites, foreigners, fatherless people, and widows, so they can eat in your towns and be satisfied. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:13
Then declare before Him, “I removed the sacred portion from my house. I gave it to Levites, foreigners, fatherless people, and widows according to all Your commands. I did not break or forget Your commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:14
I ate none while mourning, removed none while unclean, and offered none for the dead. I obeyed the LORD my God and did everything You commanded. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:15
Look down from Your holy home in heaven. Bless Your people Israel and this land flowing with milk and honey, which You gave us as You swore to our fathers.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:16
Today the LORD your God commands you to follow these statutes and ordinances. Carefully follow them with your whole heart and soul. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:17
Today you declared that the LORD is your God. You promised to walk in His ways, keep His statutes, commands, and ordinances, and listen to His voice. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:18
Today the LORD declared you His people and treasured possession, as promised. You must keep all His commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 26:19
He will set you above all nations He made, in praise, name, and honor. You will be a holy people to the LORD your God, as promised. 📖
