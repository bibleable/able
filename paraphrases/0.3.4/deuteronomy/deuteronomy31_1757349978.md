## Deuteronomy 31:1
After Moses finished speaking these words to all Israel, 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:2
he said, “I am now one hundred and twenty years old. I can no longer come and go. The LORD told me, ‘You will not cross the Jordan.’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:3
The LORD your God Himself will cross ahead of you. He will destroy these nations, and you will take their land. Joshua will cross ahead of you, as the LORD said. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:4
He will do to them what He did to the Amorite kings Sihon and Og. He destroyed them and their land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:5
He will give them into your hands. Do to them exactly as I commanded. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:6
Be strong and courageous. Do not fear them or be terrified. The LORD your God goes with you. He will never leave or abandon you.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:7
Moses called Joshua and spoke before all Israel. “Be strong and courageous. You will go with this people into the land He swore to give their fathers. You will give it to them as an inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:8
The LORD Himself goes ahead of you and will be with you. He will never leave or abandon you. Do not be afraid or discouraged.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:9
Moses wrote this law and gave it to the priests, Levi’s sons, who carried the LORD’s covenant ark. He also gave it to all Israel’s elders. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:10
He commanded them, “At the end of every seven years, during the Feast of Tabernacles in the appointed year for canceling debts, 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:11
read this law aloud to all Israel. Do this when everyone comes before the LORD your God at the place He chooses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:12
Gather men, women, children, and foreigners from your towns. They must listen, learn to fear Him, and carefully obey every word of this law. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:13
Then their children who do not know the law will listen and learn to fear Him. This must continue while you live in the land across the Jordan that you will possess.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:14
The LORD told Moses, “Your death is near. Call Joshua and appear at the Tent of Meeting, so I can commission him.” Moses and Joshua went and appeared there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:15
The LORD appeared at the tent in a pillar of cloud. The cloud stood over the tent’s entrance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:16
He told Moses, “You will soon rest with your fathers. These people will prostitute themselves with the foreign gods of the land they enter. They will abandon Me and break My covenant with them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:17
My anger will burn against them that day. I will abandon them and hide My face, so they will be consumed. Many troubles and sufferings will fall on them. They will say, ‘Did these disasters come because our God is no longer with us?’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:18
I will certainly hide My face that day. They have done evil by turning to other gods. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:19
Therefore, write this song for yourselves and teach it to Israel. Have them recite it, so it can witness for Me against them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:20
I will bring them into the land flowing with milk and honey that I swore to their fathers. They will eat until satisfied and prosper. Then they will worship other gods, reject Me, and break My covenant. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:21
When many troubles and sufferings come, this song will testify against them. Their descendants will not forget its words. I know their inclinations even before bringing them into the land I swore to give them.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:22
That very day, Moses wrote the song and taught it to Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:23
The LORD commissioned Joshua son of Nun. He said, “Be strong and courageous. You will bring Israel into the land I swore to give them. I will be with you.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:24
Moses finished writing every word of this law in a book, from beginning to end. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:25
He commanded the Levites who carried the LORD’s covenant ark, 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:26
“Place this Book of the Law beside the LORD your God’s covenant ark. It must remain there as a witness against you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:27
I know how rebellious and stubborn you are. You already rebel against the LORD while I live. How much more will you rebel after I die! 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:28
Gather all your tribal elders and officers before me. I will speak these words for them to hear and call heaven and earth as witnesses against them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:29
I know that after my death you will become completely corrupt and leave the path I commanded. Disaster will come in later days. You will do evil before the LORD and anger Him with your hands’ work.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 31:30
Moses recited every word of this song aloud to Israel’s whole assembly, from beginning to end. 📖
