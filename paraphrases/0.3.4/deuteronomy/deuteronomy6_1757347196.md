## Deuteronomy 6:1
The LORD your God commanded me to teach these commands, statutes, and ordinances. Obey them in the land you will possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:2
You, your children, and grandchildren must fear Him throughout your lives by obeying all His statutes and commands I give. Then your days may lengthen. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:3
Israel, listen and obey carefully. Then you may prosper and multiply greatly in land flowing with milk and honey, as your fathers’ God promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:4
Hear, Israel: the LORD our God, the LORD is One. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:5
Love the LORD your God with your whole heart, soul, and strength. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:6
Keep today’s commands upon your hearts. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:7
Teach them diligently to your children. Discuss them at home, on the road, when lying down, and when rising. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:8
Tie them as reminders on your hands and bind them on your foreheads. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:9
Write them on your houses’ doorposts and your gates. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:10
The LORD your God will bring you into the land sworn to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. It has great, splendid cities you never built. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:11
You will receive houses stocked with goods you never supplied and wells you never dug. You will have vineyards and olive groves you never planted. You will eat and be satisfied. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:12
Then remember the LORD who brought you from Egypt’s house of slavery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:13
Fear the LORD your God. Serve Him alone and swear your oaths in His name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:14
Never follow neighboring peoples’ gods. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:15
The LORD your God among you is jealous. Otherwise, His anger will burn, and He will wipe you from the earth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:16
Do not test Him as you did at Massah. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:17
Diligently keep the LORD your God’s commands, testimonies, and statutes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:18
Do what He sees as right and good. Then you may prosper and possess the good land He swore to your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:19
He will drive all your enemies out before you, as He said. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:20
Your son may someday ask what the LORD our God’s decrees, statutes, and ordinances mean. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:21
Tell him, ‘We were Pharaoh’s slaves in Egypt. The LORD rescued us with a mighty hand. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:22
We saw Him inflict great, devastating signs and wonders upon Egypt, Pharaoh, and his entire household. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:23
He brought us out to give us the land He swore to our fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:24
He commanded these statutes and fear of the LORD our God for our lasting prosperity and preservation, as today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 6:25
Carefully obeying every command before the LORD our God, as He commanded, will be our righteousness.’” 📖
