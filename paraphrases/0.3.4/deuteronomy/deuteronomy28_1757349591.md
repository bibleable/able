## Deuteronomy 28:1
“Faithfully obey the LORD your God’s voice and carefully follow every command I give today. If you do, He will set you above all earth’s nations. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:2
All these blessings will come upon you and overtake you if you obey His voice. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:3
You will be blessed in town and in the countryside. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:4
Your children, your land’s produce, and your livestock’s young will be blessed. This includes your herds’ calves and your flocks’ lambs. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:5
Your basket and kneading bowl will be blessed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:6
You will be blessed when you come in and go out. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:7
The LORD will defeat enemies who rise against you. They will attack from one direction but flee from you in seven. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:8
He will command a blessing on your barns and everything you do. The LORD your God will bless you in the land He gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:9
He will establish you as His holy people, as He swore. This applies if you keep His commands and walk in His ways. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:10
All earth’s peoples will see that you bear the LORD’s name. They will stand in awe of you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:11
He will give you abundant children, young livestock, and produce. This will be in the land He swore to your fathers to give you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:12
He will open heaven, His abundant storehouse, to rain on your land in season and bless all your work. You will lend to many nations but borrow from none. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:13
The LORD will make you the head, never the tail. You will move upward, never downward. This applies if you listen to and carefully follow His commands I give today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:14
Do not turn right or left from any word I command today. Do not follow other gods to serve them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:15
But if you fail to obey Him by carefully following all His commands and statutes I give today, these curses will come. They will come upon you and overtake you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:16
You will be cursed in town and in the countryside. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:17
Your basket and kneading bowl will be cursed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:18
Your children, your land’s produce, your herds’ calves, and your flocks’ lambs will be cursed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:19
You will be cursed when you come in and go out. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:20
The LORD will send curses, confusion, and rebuke on everything you do. You will quickly perish and be destroyed because of your evil in abandoning Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:21
He will make plague cling to you until He removes you completely from the land you enter to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:22
He will strike you with wasting disease, fever, inflammation, scorching heat, drought, blight, and mildew. These will pursue you until you perish. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:23
The sky above your head will be bronze, and the earth beneath you will be iron. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:24
The LORD will turn your land’s rain into dust and powder. It will fall from the sky until you are destroyed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:25
He will make your enemies defeat you. You will attack in one direction but flee in seven. All earth’s kingdoms will view you with horror. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:26
Your dead bodies will feed all the birds and wild animals. No one will frighten them away. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:27
The LORD will strike you with Egypt’s boils, tumors, scabs, and itching. You will not be cured. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:28
He will strike you with madness, blindness, and a confused mind. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:29
At noon, you will feel your way like a blind man in darkness. Your ways will not prosper. Day after day, people will oppress and plunder you. No one will save you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:30
You will be pledged to marry a woman, but another man will violate her. You will build a house but never live in it. You will plant a vineyard but never enjoy its fruit. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:31
Your ox will be killed before your eyes, but you will eat none of it. Your donkey will be taken and never returned. Your flock will be given to your enemies. No one will save you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:32
Your sons and daughters will be given to another nation. Day after day, your eyes will grow tired looking for them. Your hands will have no power. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:33
An unknown people will eat your land’s produce and everything your work produces. You will be oppressed and crushed all your days. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:34
The sights you see will drive you mad. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:35
The LORD will strike you with painful, incurable boils on your knees and thighs. They will cover you from the soles of your feet to the top of your head. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:36
He will take you and your appointed king to a nation neither you nor your fathers knew. There you will worship other gods made of wood and stone. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:37
You will become an object of horror, scorn, and ridicule among every nation where the LORD drives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:38
You will sow much seed but harvest little. Locusts will consume it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:39
You will plant and tend vineyards but neither drink the wine nor gather the grapes. Worms will eat them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:40
You will have olive trees throughout your land but never put their oil on yourselves. The olives will fall off. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:41
You will have sons and daughters, but they will not remain yours. They will go into captivity. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:42
Swarms of locusts will consume all your trees and your land’s produce. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:43
The foreigner among you will rise higher and higher above you. You will sink lower and lower. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:44
He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:45
All these curses will come upon you, pursue you, and overtake you until you are destroyed. You did not obey the LORD your God or keep His commands and statutes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:46
These curses will remain a sign and wonder on you and your descendants forever. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:47
You did not serve the LORD your God with joy and a glad heart when you had abundance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:48
Therefore, you will serve enemies He sends against you, in hunger, thirst, nakedness, and lack of everything. He will put an iron yoke on your neck until He destroys you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:49
The LORD will bring a nation from far away, from earth’s ends. It will swoop on you like an eagle. You will not understand its language. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:50
It will be a ruthless nation that neither respects the old nor pities the young. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:51
They will eat your livestock’s young and your land’s produce until you are destroyed. They will leave no grain, new wine, oil, calves, or lambs until you perish. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:52
They will besiege every town throughout your land until the high, fortified walls you trust fall. They will besiege all your towns in the land the LORD your God gave you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:53
During the siege and distress your enemy inflicts, you will eat your own children’s flesh. These are the sons and daughters the LORD your God gave you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:54
Even the gentlest, most refined man among you will resent sharing with his brother, beloved wife, and surviving children. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:55
He will refuse them any of his children’s flesh that he eats. He will have nothing else during the siege and distress your enemy inflicts in all your towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:56
Even the gentlest, most refined woman would not let her foot touch the ground. Yet she will resent sharing with her beloved husband, son, and daughter. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:57
She will not share the afterbirth from between her legs or the children she bears. She will secretly eat them because nothing else remains during the siege and distress your enemy inflicts in your towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:58
You may fail to carefully obey every word of this law written in this book. You may fail to fear the glorious, awesome name: the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:59
If so, He will bring extraordinary disasters on you and your descendants. These will include severe, lasting plagues and terrible, chronic sicknesses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:60
He will again afflict you with all the diseases you feared in Egypt. They will cling to you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:61
He will also bring every sickness and plague not written in this Book of the Law until you are destroyed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:62
You were as numerous as the stars, but few will remain. This is because you refused to obey the LORD your God’s voice. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:63
The LORD delighted in making you prosper and multiply. He will likewise delight in destroying and annihilating you. You will be uprooted from the land you enter to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:64
He will scatter you among all nations, from one end of earth to the other. There you will worship other gods of wood and stone, which neither you nor your fathers knew. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:65
There you will find no rest, not even a place for your foot to rest. The LORD will give you a trembling heart, failing eyes, and a despairing soul. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:66
Your life will hang in doubt. You will fear night and day, never sure you will survive. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:67
In the morning, you will say, ‘If only it were evening!’ In the evening, you will say, ‘If only it were morning!’ Your hearts will dread the terrifying sights you see. 📖

## Deuteronomy 28:68
The LORD will send you to Egypt in ships along a route I said you would never see again. There you will offer yourselves to your enemies as male and female slaves. But no one will buy you.” 📖
