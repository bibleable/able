## Deuteronomy 18:1
The Levitical priests and the whole tribe of Levi have no share or inheritance with Israel. They must eat the food offerings to the LORD as their inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:2
They have no inheritance among their brothers. The LORD is their inheritance, as He promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:3
People who sacrifice a bull or sheep must give the priests the shoulder, jowls, and stomach as their share. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:4
Give them the first produce of your grain, new wine, and oil, and the first wool cut from your flock. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:5
The LORD your God chose Levi and his sons from all your tribes to stand and serve in His name forever. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:6
A Levite may leave any town where he lives in Israel. He may come with an earnest desire to the place the LORD chooses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:7
Then he may serve in the LORD his God’s name like his fellow Levites who stand before the LORD there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:8
They must receive equal food portions. This applies even if he received money from selling his father’s estate. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:9
When you enter the land the LORD your God gives you, do not copy the nations’ detestable practices there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:10
No one among you may sacrifice a son or daughter in fire. No one may practice divination, conjury, or sorcery, or interpret omens, 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:11
cast spells, consult mediums or spiritists, or question the dead. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:12
The LORD finds everyone who does these things detestable. These detestable practices are why He drives the nations out before you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:13
Be blameless before the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:14
The nations you will drive out listen to conjurers and diviners. The LORD your God does not permit you to do this. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:15
He will raise up a prophet like me for you from among your brothers. Listen to him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:16
You asked Him for this at Horeb on the assembly day. You said, “Let us no longer hear His voice or see this great fire, so we do not die!” 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:17
The LORD told me, “They have spoken well. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:18
I will raise up a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put My words in his mouth. He will tell them everything I command. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:19
I will hold accountable anyone who refuses to listen to My words that he speaks in My name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:20
A prophet may dare to speak in My name a message I did not command. Or he may speak in other gods’ names. If so, put that prophet to death.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:21
You may ask yourselves, “How can we recognize a message the LORD has not spoken?” 📖

## Deuteronomy 18:22
A prophet may speak in His name, but the message fails to happen or come true. Then the LORD did not speak that message. The prophet spoke arrogantly. Do not fear him. 📖
