## Deuteronomy 16:1
Observe the month of Abib and celebrate Passover to the LORD your God. He brought you from Egypt at night in that month. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:2
Offer the Passover sacrifice to Him from your herd or flock. Do this at the place He chooses as a home for His Name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:3
Do not eat bread made with yeast with it. For seven days, eat bread without yeast, the bread of suffering, because you left Egypt hurriedly. Thus remember the day you left Egypt throughout your life. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:4
There must be no yeast anywhere in your land for seven days. None of the meat sacrificed on the first evening may remain until morning. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:5
Do not sacrifice the Passover animal in any of the towns the LORD your God gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:6
Offer it only where He chooses a home for His Name. Do this at sunset, at the time you left Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:7
Roast and eat it at the place He chooses. Return to your tents in the morning. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:8
Eat bread without yeast for six days. On the seventh day, hold a solemn assembly to the LORD your God. Do no work. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:9
Count seven weeks from when you first cut the standing grain with a sickle. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:10
Celebrate the Feast of Weeks to the LORD your God. Give a freewill offering in proportion to His blessing to you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:11
Rejoice before Him at the place He chooses as a home for His Name. Include your sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, and the Levite in your town. Also include foreigners, fatherless people, and widows among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:12
Remember that you were slaves in Egypt. Carefully follow these statutes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:13
Celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles for seven days after gathering produce from your threshing floor and winepress. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:14
Rejoice at your feast with your sons, daughters, male servants, and female servants. Include Levites, foreigners, fatherless people, and widows among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:15
Celebrate a feast to Him for seven days at His chosen place. He will bless all your produce and work, so your joy will be complete. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:16
All your men must appear before Him at His chosen place three times a year. These are the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Tabernacles. No one may appear before the LORD with nothing to give. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:17
Each must bring a gift he can afford, according to the LORD your God’s blessing to you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:18
Appoint judges and officials for your tribes in every town He gives you. They must judge the people justly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:19
Do not deny justice or favor anyone unfairly. Do not accept bribes. Bribes blind wise eyes and twist righteous people’s words. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:20
Pursue justice alone, so you can live and possess the land the LORD your God gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:21
Do not set up a wooden Asherah pole beside the altar you build for the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 16:22
Do not set up a sacred pillar for yourselves. He hates such pillars. 📖
