## Deuteronomy 21:1
A slain person may be found in a field in the land He gives you to possess. You may not know who killed that person. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:2
If so, your elders and judges must measure the distances from the victim to nearby towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:3
The nearest town’s elders must take a heifer that has never worked or worn a yoke. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:4
Bring it to a valley with running water, where no one has plowed or planted. Break its neck beside the stream. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:5
The priests, Levi’s sons, must come forward. The LORD your God chose them to serve Him, pronounce blessings in His name, and decide every dispute and assault case. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:6
All the nearest town’s elders must wash their hands over the heifer with the broken neck beside the stream. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:7
They must declare, “Our hands did not shed this blood. Our eyes did not see it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:8
LORD, accept this atonement for Your people Israel, whom You freed. Do not hold this innocent bloodshed against them.” Then the bloodshed will be atoned for. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:9
Thus remove the guilt of innocent bloodshed from among you by doing what the LORD sees as right. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:10
You may go to war against your enemies, and the LORD your God may give them into your hands as captives. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:11
Among them, you may see a beautiful woman whom you desire to marry. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:12
If so, bring her into your house. She must shave her head and trim her nails. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:13
She must put aside her captive clothing. Let her live in your house and mourn her father and mother for a full month. Then you may have sexual relations with her and become her husband. She will be your wife. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:14
If she does not please you, let her go wherever she wishes. Do not sell her or treat her as a slave, because you have dishonored her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:15
A man may have two wives, one loved and one unloved. Both may bear sons, but the unloved wife bears the firstborn. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:16
When assigning his sons’ inheritance, he must not give the loved wife’s son the firstborn’s place over the unloved wife’s son. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:17
He must recognize the unloved wife’s son as firstborn by giving him a double share of all he owns. That son is the first fruit of his father’s strength. The firstborn’s right belongs to him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:18
A man may have a stubborn, rebellious son who disobeys his father and mother. He may refuse to listen even when they discipline him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:19
If so, both parents must take hold of him and bring him to his hometown’s elders at the gate. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:20
They must say, “Our son is stubborn and rebellious. He disobeys us. He is a glutton and a drunkard.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:21
Then all his town’s men must stone him to death. Thus remove evil from among you. All Israel will hear and fear. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:22
A man may commit a sin deserving death, be executed, and have his body hung on a tree. 📖

## Deuteronomy 21:23
Do not leave his body there overnight. Bury him that day, because anyone hung on a tree is under God’s curse. Do not defile the land the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance. 📖
