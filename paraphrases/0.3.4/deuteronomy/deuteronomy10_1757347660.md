## Deuteronomy 10:1
The LORD told me, “Chisel two stone tablets like the first ones. Make a wooden ark and come up the mountain to Me. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:2
I will write the words from the tablets you broke. Put the new tablets in the ark.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:3
I made an acacia wood ark and two tablets like the originals. I climbed the mountain carrying them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:4
The LORD rewrote the Ten Commandments He spoke from the fire on the assembly day. He gave me the tablets. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:5
I descended and placed them in my ark, obeying Him. They remain there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:6
Israel traveled from Beeroth Bene-jaakan to Moserah. Aaron died and was buried there. His son Eleazar became priest in his place. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:7
They traveled to Gudgodah, then Jotbathah, a land of streams. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:8
The LORD set Levi apart to carry His covenant ark, serve before Him, and pronounce blessings in His name. They continue these duties today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:9
Levi therefore has no portion or inheritance among his brothers. The LORD is his inheritance, as the LORD your God promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:10
I stayed forty days and forty nights, like before. Again the LORD listened and agreed to spare you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:11
He said, “Continue the journey ahead of the people. Let them possess the land I swore to their fathers.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:12
Israel, what does He require? Fear the LORD your God, walk in every way of His, and love Him. Serve Him with your whole heart and soul. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:13
Keep His commands and statutes I give today for your own good. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:14
Heaven, the highest heavens, earth, and everything in it belong to the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:15
Yet He loved your fathers and chose you, their descendants, above all other peoples, as today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:16
Circumcise your hearts. Stop being stubborn. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:17
He is God of gods and Lord of lords, great, mighty, and awesome. He shows no partiality and accepts no bribe. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:18
He gives justice to fatherless people and widows. He loves foreigners and provides food and clothing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:19
Love foreigners too. You were foreigners in Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:20
Fear and serve the LORD your God. Hold firmly to Him and swear oaths in His name. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:21
He is your praise and your God. You saw His great, awesome wonders for you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 10:22
Your fathers entered Egypt as seventy people. Now He made you as numerous as the sky’s stars. 📖
