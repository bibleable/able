## Deuteronomy 5:1
Moses gathered all Israel. “Hear the statutes and ordinances I declare today. Learn them and obey carefully. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:2
The LORD our God made a covenant with us at Horeb. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:3
He made it with us, all alive here today, rather than our fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:4
The LORD spoke to you face to face from the mountain’s fire. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:5
I stood between you and Him to declare His word. You feared the fire and refused to climb the mountain. He said, 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:6
‘I am the LORD your God. I brought you from Egypt, the house of slavery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:7
Have no other gods before Me. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:8
Make no idol resembling anything in heaven above, earth below, or the waters beneath. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:9
Neither bow to nor worship them. I, the LORD your God, am jealous. I visit fathers’ sins upon their children to the third and fourth generations of those hating Me. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:10
I show loving devotion to a thousand generations of those loving Me and keeping My commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:11
Never take the LORD your God’s name in vain. He will punish anyone doing so. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:12
Keep the Sabbath holy, as the LORD your God commanded. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:13
Work for six days and complete all your labor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:14
The seventh is the LORD your God’s Sabbath. No one may work: you, your children, your male or female servants, or foreigners within your gates. Neither may your ox, donkey, or other livestock. Your servants must rest as you do. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:15
Remember your slavery in Egypt. The LORD your God brought you out with a mighty hand and outstretched arm. That is why He commanded the Sabbath. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:16
Honor your parents, as the LORD your God commanded. Then you may live long and prosper in the land He gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:17
Do not murder. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:18
Do not commit adultery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:19
Do not steal. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:20
Do not give false testimony against your neighbor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:21
Do not covet your neighbor’s wife. Do not covet his house, field, male or female servant, ox, donkey, or anything he owns.’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:22
The LORD loudly declared these commands to your whole assembly from the mountain’s fire, cloud, and deep darkness. He added nothing. He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:23
You heard His voice from the darkness while the mountain burned. Your tribal heads and elders approached me. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:24
You said, ‘The LORD our God showed His glory and greatness. We heard Him from the fire. Today we saw humans can survive God speaking to them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:25
Why should we die now? This great fire will consume us. Hearing the LORD our God’s voice any longer will kill us. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:26
Who else heard the living God speak from fire and survived? 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:27
Go and hear everything the LORD our God says. Tell us His words. We will listen and obey.’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:28
The LORD heard and told me, ‘I heard their words to you. Everything they said is good. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:29
If only their hearts always feared Me and kept all My commands! Then they and their children might prosper forever. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:30
Tell them to return to their tents. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:31
You stay here with Me. I will give all commands, statutes, and ordinances for you to teach them to obey in their promised land.’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:32
Carefully obey the LORD your God. Turn neither right nor left. 📖

## Deuteronomy 5:33
Follow every way He commanded. Then you may live, prosper, and remain long in the land you will possess. 📖
