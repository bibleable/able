## Deuteronomy 32:1
Heavens, listen as I speak. Earth, hear the words from my mouth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:2
Let my teaching fall like rain and my speech settle like dew. Let it be like gentle rain on new grass and showers on tender plants. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:3
I will proclaim the LORD’s name. Declare our God’s greatness! 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:4
He is the Rock, and His work is perfect. All His ways are just. He is faithful, righteous, and upright, without injustice. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:5
His people acted corruptly toward Him. Their blemish marks a perverse, crooked generation, rather than His children. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:6
Foolish, senseless people, is this how you repay the LORD? Is He not your Father and Creator? Did He not make and establish you? 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:7
Remember ancient days and consider years long past. Ask your father and elders. They will tell you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:8
The Most High gave the nations their inheritance and divided mankind. He set the peoples’ boundaries according to the number of the sons of God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:9
The LORD’s share is His people. Jacob is His assigned inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:10
He found Jacob in a desert, a barren, howling wilderness. He surrounded and instructed him. He guarded him as the apple of His eye. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:11
Like an eagle stirring its nest and hovering over its young, He spread His wings to catch them. He carried them on His wing feathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:12
The LORD alone led him. No foreign god was with him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:13
He made him ride on the land’s heights and fed him the fields’ produce. He nourished him with honey from rock and oil from the flinty cliff. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:14
He gave curds from herds, milk from flocks, lamb fat, rams from Bashan, goats, and the finest wheat. You drank wine from the finest grapes’ juice. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:15
But Jeshurun grew fat and kicked. He became fat, bloated, and overfed. He abandoned the God who made him and scorned the Rock of his salvation. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:16
They made Him jealous with foreign gods. Their detestable acts angered Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:17
They sacrificed to demons, not God. These were unknown gods, newly arrived gods their fathers did not fear. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:18
You ignored the Rock who brought you forth. You forgot the God who gave you birth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:19
The LORD saw and rejected them. His sons and daughters provoked His anger. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:20
He said, “I will hide My face from them and see their end. They are a perverse generation, unfaithful children. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:21
They made Me jealous with what is not God and angered Me with worthless idols. I will make them jealous through those who are not a people. I will anger them through a nation without understanding. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:22
My anger has lit a fire that burns to Sheol’s depths. It consumes earth and its produce and scorches the mountains’ foundations. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:23
I will heap disasters on them. I will use all My arrows against them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:24
Hunger will waste them, and pestilence and bitter plague will ravage them. I will send wild animals’ fangs against them and venom from vipers sliding in the dust. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:25
Outside, the sword will take their children. Inside, terror will strike young men, young women, infants, and men with gray hair. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:26
I would have said, ‘I will cut them to pieces and erase their memory from mankind.’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:27
But I dreaded the enemy’s taunts. Their enemies might misunderstand and say, ‘Our own hand won. The LORD did not do all this.’” 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:28
Israel is a nation without counsel or understanding. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:29
If only they were wise, they would understand this. They would recognize their fate. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:30
How could one man chase a thousand, or two chase ten thousand? This could happen only if their Rock sold them and the LORD surrendered them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:31
Their rock is unlike our Rock. Even our enemies admit this. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:32
Their vine comes from Sodom’s vine and Gomorrah’s fields. Their grapes are poisonous, and their clusters are bitter. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:33
Their wine is serpent venom, the deadly poison of cobras. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:34
“Have I not stored these things, sealed inside My vaults? 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:35
Vengeance belongs to Me. I will repay. Their foot will slip at the appointed time. Their day of disaster is near, and their doom comes quickly.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:36
The LORD will vindicate His people and have compassion on His servants. This happens when He sees their strength is gone and no one remains, slave or free. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:37
He will say, “Where are their gods, the rock where they took refuge? 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:38
Those gods ate their sacrifices’ fat and drank their drink offerings’ wine. Let them rise and help you. Let them shelter you! 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:39
See that I am He. There is no God besides Me. I kill and give life. I wound and heal. No one can rescue from My hand. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:40
I raise My hand to heaven and declare, ‘As surely as I live forever, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:41
when I sharpen My flashing sword and grasp it in judgment, I will take vengeance on My enemies. I will repay those who hate Me. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:42
I will make My arrows drunk with the blood of the slain and captives. My sword will devour flesh, including the heads of enemy leaders.’” 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:43
Heavens, rejoice with Him, and let all God’s angels worship Him. Nations, rejoice with His people. He will avenge His children’s blood. He will take vengeance on His enemies and repay those who hate Him. He will cleanse His land and people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:44
Moses came with Joshua son of Nun and recited every word of this song for the people to hear. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:45
When he finished reciting all these words to all Israel, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:46
he said, “Take to heart every word I solemnly declare today. Then command your children to carefully obey every word of this law. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:47
These are no empty words. They are your life. Through them you will live long in the land across the Jordan that you will possess.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:48
That same day, the LORD told Moses, 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:49
“Climb the Abarim Range to Mount Nebo in Moab, opposite Jericho. View Canaan, the land I give Israel to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:50
You will die on that mountain and be gathered to your people. This is like your brother Aaron, who died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:51
At Meribah-kadesh’s waters in the Wilderness of Zin, you both broke faith with Me among Israel. You failed to treat Me as holy before them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 32:52
You will see the land I give Israel from a distance. But you will not enter it.” 📖
