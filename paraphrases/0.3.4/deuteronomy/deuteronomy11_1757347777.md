## Deuteronomy 11:1
Therefore, love the LORD your God. Always keep His instructions, statutes, ordinances, and commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:2
Understand today: your children did not experience or see His discipline. They did not see His greatness, mighty hand, or outstretched arm. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:3
They did not see His signs and works against Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and his whole land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:4
He made the Red Sea cover the Egyptian army, horses, and chariots as they chased you. He destroyed them completely, as remains clear today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:5
Your children did not experience all He did for you in the wilderness until you reached this place. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:6
He acted among all Israel against Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab the Reubenite. The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them. It swallowed their households, tents, and every living thing they owned. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:7
Your own eyes saw every great work the LORD did. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:8
Therefore, keep every command I give you today. Then you can have strength to cross the Jordan and take possession of the land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:9
Then you can live long in the land flowing with milk and honey. The LORD swore to give it to your fathers and their descendants. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:10
This land you will possess differs from Egypt, which you left. There you planted seeds and watered the ground on foot, like a vegetable garden. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:11
The land across the Jordan that you will possess has mountains and valleys. It drinks rain from heaven. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:12
The LORD your God cares for it. His eyes are always on it, from the beginning of the year to its end. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:13
This promise applies if you carefully obey the commands I give today. Love the LORD your God and serve Him with your whole heart and soul. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:14
Then I will give your land rain in season, in autumn and spring. You can gather your grain, new wine, and oil. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:15
I will give grass in your fields for your livestock. You will eat and be satisfied. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:16
Be careful. Do not let anyone attract you away to worship and bow to other gods. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:17
Otherwise, the LORD will become angry with you. He will close heaven, so no rain falls and the land produces nothing. You will soon perish from the good land He gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:18
Keep these words of mine in your hearts and minds. Tie them on your hands as reminders and bind them on your foreheads. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:19
Teach them to your children. Speak about them at home and along the road, when you lie down and when you get up. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:20
Write them on your houses’ doorposts and on your gates. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:21
This is so you and your children can live many days in the land He swore to give your fathers. These days can continue as long as heaven remains above earth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:22
Carefully keep all these commands I give you to follow. Love the LORD your God, walk in all His ways, and hold firmly to Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:23
If you do this, He will drive out all these nations before you. You will take land from nations greater and stronger than you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:24
Every place your foot touches will be yours. Your territory will reach from the wilderness to Lebanon, and from the Euphrates River to the Western Sea. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:25
No one will withstand you. As promised, the LORD your God will make the whole land fear and dread you wherever you set foot. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:26
Look, today I set a blessing and a curse before you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:27
You receive the blessing if you obey the LORD your God’s commands that I give you today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:28
You receive the curse if you disobey His commands and leave the path I command today. This happens by following other gods you have not known. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:29
When He brings you into the land you will possess, proclaim the blessing on Mount Gerizim. Proclaim the curse on Mount Ebal. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:30
These mountains are across the Jordan, west of the road toward sunset. They are in Canaanite land, in the Arabah opposite Gilgal, near the Oak of Moreh. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:31
You are about to cross the Jordan and possess the land the LORD your God gives you. You will take possession and settle there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 11:32
Then carefully follow all the statutes and ordinances I set before you today. 📖
