## Deuteronomy 29:1
These are the covenant’s words that the LORD commanded Moses to make with Israel in Moab. This covenant was additional to the one He made with them at Horeb. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:2
Moses called all Israel and said, “Your own eyes saw everything the LORD did in Egypt to Pharaoh, all his officials, and his whole land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:3
Your own eyes saw the great trials, miraculous signs, and wonders. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:4
Yet until today, He has not given you a mind to understand, eyes to see, or ears to hear. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:5
I led you in the wilderness for forty years. Your clothes and sandals did not wear out. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:6
You ate no bread and drank no wine or strong drink. This was so you could know that I am the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:7
Here Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan attacked us, but we defeated them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:8
We took their land and gave it to the Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh as an inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:9
Keep and obey this covenant’s words, so you can prosper in everything you do. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:10
Today you all stand before the LORD your God: tribal leaders, elders, officials, and all Israel’s men, 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:11
also your children, wives, and the foreigners in your camps who cut your wood and draw your water. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:12
You stand here to enter His covenant and oath, which He makes with you today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:13
Thus He can establish you today as His people and be your God. He promised you this and swore it to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:14
I make this covenant and oath with more than you alone. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:15
It includes those standing here with us today before the LORD our God, and those absent today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:16
You know how we lived in Egypt and passed through the nations on our way here. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:17
You saw their detestable objects and idols made of wood, stone, silver, and gold. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:18
Let no man, woman, clan, or tribe among you turn from the LORD our God today to worship those nations’ gods. Let no root among you bear poisonous, bitter fruit like this. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:19
Such a person hears this oath and blesses himself, saying, ‘I will have peace, though I follow my stubborn heart.’ This will bring disaster on the watered land and the dry land alike. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:20
The LORD will refuse to forgive him. His anger and jealousy will burn against that man. Every curse in this book will fall on him. The LORD will erase his name from under heaven. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:21
He will single him out from all Israel’s tribes for disaster. This will follow every covenant curse written in this Book of the Law. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:22
The next generation, your sons after you, and foreigners from distant lands will see the land’s plagues. They will see the sicknesses the LORD inflicted on it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:23
All its soil will be burning sulfur and salt, unplanted and unproductive, with nothing growing. It will resemble Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim, which He destroyed in fierce anger. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:24
All nations will ask, ‘Why did the LORD do this to the land? Why this great anger?’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:25
People will answer, ‘They abandoned the covenant of the LORD, their fathers’ God. He made it with them when He brought them from Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:26
They served and worshiped other gods they had not known. These were gods the LORD had not given them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:27
Therefore, His anger burned against the land. He brought every curse written in this book upon it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:28
In anger, rage, and great wrath, He uprooted them from their land. He threw them into another land, where they remain today.’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 29:29
Secret things belong to the LORD our God. Revealed things belong to us and our children forever, so we can obey every word of this law. 📖
