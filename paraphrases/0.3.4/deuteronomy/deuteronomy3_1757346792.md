## Deuteronomy 3:1
We took Bashan’s road. Og, Bashan’s king, and his entire army came to fight us at Edrei. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:2
The LORD said, “Do not fear him. I delivered him, his people, and his land into your hand. Treat him like Sihon, the Amorites’ king in Heshbon.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:3
The LORD our God delivered Og and his whole army to us. We killed them and left no survivor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:4
We captured all sixty cities without exception. We took the entire Argob region, Og’s kingdom in Bashan. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:5
These cities had high walls, gates, and bars. There were also many unwalled villages. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:6
We devoted them to destruction, as with Sihon of Heshbon. We completely destroyed every city’s men, women, and children. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:7
We kept all livestock and city plunder. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:8
We took both Amorite kings’ land across the Jordan, from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:9
Sidonians call that mountain Sirion. Amorites call it Senir. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:10
We took all plateau cities, Gilead, and Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei in Og’s kingdom. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:11
Og was the last remaining Rephaim. His iron bed remains at Rabbah of the Ammonites. It measures nine cubits long and four wide. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:12
We possessed this land then. I gave Reuben and Gad the land beyond Aroer along the Arnon Valley and half Gilead’s hill country with its cities. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:13
I gave Manasseh’s half-tribe the remaining Gilead and all Bashan, Og’s kingdom. The entire Argob region and Bashan were formerly called the Rephaim’s land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:14
Jair, Manasseh’s descendant, took all Argob to the Geshurites’ and Maacathites’ border. He named Bashan after himself, Havvoth-jair. That name remains today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:15
I gave Gilead to Machir. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:16
I gave Reuben and Gad territory from Gilead to the Arnon Valley, whose middle formed the border. It extended to the Jabbok River, Ammon’s border. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:17
The Jordan in the Arabah bordered it from Chinnereth to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea. Pisgah’s slopes lay eastward. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:18
I commanded, “The LORD your God gave you this land. All your brave men must cross armed for battle before your Israelite brothers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:19
Your wives, children, and abundant livestock may stay in the cities I gave you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:20
Wait until the LORD gives your brothers rest and possession across the Jordan as He gave you. Then each may return to his assigned possession.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:21
I told Joshua, “You saw everything the LORD your God did to these two kings. He will do likewise to every kingdom you enter. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:22
Do not fear them. The LORD your God Himself fights for you.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:23
I also pleaded with the LORD. 🙏

## Deuteronomy 3:24
“Lord GOD, You began showing Your servant Your greatness and power. What god in heaven or earth can match Your mighty works? 🙏

## Deuteronomy 3:25
Please let me cross the Jordan to see the good land, its pleasant hills, and Lebanon!” 🙏

## Deuteronomy 3:26
The LORD was angry with me because of you and refused to listen. “Enough! Never speak to Me about this again. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:27
Climb Pisgah and look west, north, south, and east. See the land yourself. You will not cross the Jordan. 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:28
Commission Joshua, encourage him, and strengthen him. He will cross ahead of the people and enable them to inherit the land you see.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 3:29
We stayed in the valley opposite Beth-peor. 📖
