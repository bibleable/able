## Deuteronomy 25:1
If men have a dispute, they must go to court for judgment. The innocent must be cleared and the guilty condemned. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:2
If the guilty man deserves flogging, the judge must make him lie down and receive it before him. Give the number of lashes his crime deserves. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:3
Do not give more than forty lashes. More would degrade your brother before your eyes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:4
Do not muzzle an ox while it treads grain. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:5
If brothers live together and one dies without a son, his widow must not marry outside the family. His brother must marry her and perform a brother-in-law’s duty for her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:6
Her first son must carry the dead brother’s name. Then his name will not disappear from Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:7
If the man refuses to marry her, she must tell the elders at the town gate. She must say, “My husband’s brother refuses to preserve his brother’s name in Israel. He will not perform a brother-in-law’s duty for me.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:8
The town’s elders must summon and question him. He may persist, saying, “I do not want to marry her.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:9
If so, his brother’s widow must approach him before the elders. She must remove his sandal, spit in his face, and declare, “This happens to a man who refuses to continue his brother’s family line.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:10
His family in Israel will be named “The House of the Unsandaled.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:11
Two men may fight, and one man’s wife may come to rescue him from the attacker. She may reach out and grab the attacker’s genitals. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:12
If so, cut off her hand. Show her no pity. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:13
Do not keep two different weights in your bag, one heavy and one light. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:14
Do not keep two different measures in your house, one large and one small. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:15
Keep accurate, honest weights and measures. Then you can live long in the land the LORD your God gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:16
He finds everyone who acts dishonestly in these matters detestable. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:17
Remember what the Amalekites did as you traveled from Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:18
They met you when you were tired and weary and attacked everyone trailing behind. They did not fear God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 25:19
The LORD your God will give you rest from surrounding enemies in the land He gives you to possess as an inheritance. Then erase Amalek’s memory from under heaven. Do not forget! 📖
