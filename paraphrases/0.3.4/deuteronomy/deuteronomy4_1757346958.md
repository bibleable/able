## Deuteronomy 4:1
Israel, listen to the statutes and ordinances I teach you to obey. Then you may live and possess the land the LORD, your fathers’ God, gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:2
Neither add to nor remove anything from my commands. Keep the LORD your God’s commands that I give you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:3
You saw the LORD your God destroy everyone among you who followed Baal of Peor at Baal-peor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:4
Every one of you who held firmly to the LORD your God remains alive today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:5
I taught statutes and ordinances as the LORD my God commanded. Follow them in the land you will enter and possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:6
Obey carefully, showing other peoples your wisdom and understanding. They will hear these statutes and call this great nation wise and understanding. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:7
What great nation has a god as near as the LORD our God whenever we call Him? 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:8
What great nation has righteous statutes and ordinances like this entire law I give today? 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:9
Guard yourselves diligently. Remember everything you saw throughout your lives, keeping it in your hearts. Teach your children and grandchildren. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:10
At Horeb, you stood before the LORD your God. He told me, “Gather them to hear My words. Let them learn lifelong fear of Me on earth and teach their children.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:11
You stood at the mountain’s base. Fire blazed toward heaven amid black clouds and deep darkness. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:12
The LORD spoke from the fire. You heard His words and voice but saw no form. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:13
He declared His covenant and commanded obedience. He wrote its Ten Commandments on two stone tablets. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:14
The LORD commanded me to teach the statutes and ordinances you must obey in the land across the Jordan. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:15
You saw no form when the LORD spoke from Horeb’s fire. Be very careful. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:16
Do not corrupt yourselves by making any idol shaped like a male or female. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:17
Do not make idols resembling earthly beasts or flying birds. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:18
Do not make idols resembling crawling creatures or fish in the waters below. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:19
Do not worship the sun, moon, stars, or heavenly host when you see them. The LORD your God apportioned them to every nation under heaven. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:20
The LORD took you from Egypt, the iron furnace. You became the people of His inheritance, as you are today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:21
The LORD was angry with me because of you. He swore I would not cross the Jordan to the good land He gives you as inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:22
I must die here without crossing the Jordan. You will cross and possess the good land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:23
Remember the LORD your God’s covenant with you. Make no idol in any form He forbade. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:24
The LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:25
After children, grandchildren, and long settlement, you may corrupt yourselves by making idols of any form. That is evil before the LORD your God and provokes His anger. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:26
If so, I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you today. You will quickly perish from the land across the Jordan. You will not live there long but be utterly destroyed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:27
The LORD will scatter you among peoples. Few will survive in the nations where He drives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:28
There you will serve human-made wooden and stone gods that cannot see, hear, eat, or smell. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:29
Seek the LORD your God from there with your whole heart and soul. If you do, you will find Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:30
After this distress comes upon you, in later days you will return to the LORD your God and obey His voice. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:31
He is merciful. He will neither abandon nor destroy you nor forget His sworn covenant with your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:32
Ask about ancient days, since God created humans on earth, from one end of heaven to the other. Has anything this great happened or been reported? 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:33
Has another people heard God’s voice from fire and lived, as you did? 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:34
Has any god taken a nation from another nation through trials, signs, wonders, war, great terrors, a strong hand, and an outstretched arm? You saw the LORD your God do this in Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:35
He showed you these things so you would know the LORD is God. No other god exists besides Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:36
He spoke from heaven to discipline you. He showed great fire on earth, and you heard His words from it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:37
He loved your fathers and chose their descendants. His presence and great power brought you from Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:38
He drove out greater, stronger nations to bring you into their land and give it as inheritance, as today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:39
Know and take to heart today that the LORD is God in heaven and earth. There is no other. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:40
Keep His statutes and commands I give today. Then you and your children may prosper and live long in the land He gives forever. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:41
Moses set aside three cities east of the Jordan. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:42
Someone killing a neighbor unintentionally, without prior malice, could flee there and save his life. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:43
They were Bezer on the Reubenite wilderness plateau, Ramoth in Gilead for Gad, and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:44
This is the law Moses gave Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:45
He proclaimed these testimonies, statutes, and ordinances after Israel left Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:46
They were in the valley across the Jordan opposite Beth-peor, in Sihon the Amorite king’s land. He lived in Heshbon. Moses and Israel defeated him after leaving Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:47
They possessed Sihon’s land and Og, Bashan’s king’s land. These two Amorite kings ruled east of the Jordan. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:48
The land extended from Aroer on the Arnon Valley’s rim to Mount Siyon, also called Hermon. 📖

## Deuteronomy 4:49
It included all the Arabah east of the Jordan to the Sea of the Arabah, below Pisgah’s slopes. 📖
