## Deuteronomy 30:1
“The blessings and curses I set before you will come upon you. You will remember them among all the nations where the LORD your God banished you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:2
You and your children will return to Him. You will obey His voice with your whole heart and soul, following everything I give you today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:3
When this happens, He will restore you from captivity and have compassion on you. He will gather you from every nation where He scattered you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:4
Even if banished to the farthest horizon, you will be gathered and brought back by Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:5
He will bring you into your fathers’ land, and you will possess it. He will make you more prosperous and numerous than your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:6
He will circumcise your hearts and your descendants’ hearts. Then you will love Him with your whole heart and soul, so you can live. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:7
He will put all these curses on your enemies who hate and persecute you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:8
You will again obey the LORD’s voice and follow every command I give you today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:9
He will give abundance in all your work, your children, your livestock’s young, and your land’s produce. He will again delight in your prosperity, as He delighted in your fathers’ prosperity. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:10
This applies if you obey Him by keeping His commands and statutes written in this Book of the Law. Return to Him with your whole heart and soul. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:11
This command I give you today is neither too difficult nor beyond your reach. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:12
It is not in heaven. You need not ask, ‘Who will climb to heaven, get it, and proclaim it so we can obey?’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:13
It is not across the sea. You need not ask, ‘Who will cross the sea, get it, and proclaim it so we can obey?’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:14
The word is very near you, in your mouth and heart, so you can obey it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:15
Look, today I set life and prosperity before you, and also death and disaster. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:16
Today I command you to love the LORD your God and walk in His ways. Keep His commands, statutes, and ordinances, so you can live and increase. Then He can bless you in the land you enter to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:17
But your heart may turn away, and you may refuse to listen. You may be drawn away to bow to and worship other gods. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:18
If so, I declare today that you will surely perish. You will not live long in the land across the Jordan that you will possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:19
Today I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you. I set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Choose life, so you and your descendants can live. 📖

## Deuteronomy 30:20
Love and obey the LORD your God, and hold firmly to Him. He is your life. He will prolong your life in the land He swore to give your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.” 📖
