## Deuteronomy 9:1
Hear, Israel. Today you are about to cross the Jordan and dispossess greater, stronger nations. Their large cities have walls reaching heaven. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:2
The Anakim are strong and tall. You know them and heard people ask, “Who can withstand Anak’s sons?” 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:3
The LORD your God goes ahead today as a consuming fire. He will destroy and subdue them. You will drive them out and quickly annihilate them, as He promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:4
After His victory, do not credit your righteousness for possessing the land. He drives these nations out because of their wickedness. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:5
Your righteousness and upright heart do not gain this land. The LORD drives these nations out because of their wickedness. He does this to keep His promise sworn to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:6
He does not give this good land because you are righteous. You are stubborn people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:7
Never forget how you provoked Him in the wilderness. You rebelled from the day you left Egypt until reaching here. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:8
At Horeb, you angered Him enough to destroy you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:9
I climbed the mountain for the stone tablets of His covenant with you. I stayed forty days and forty nights, eating no bread and drinking no water. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:10
The LORD gave two stone tablets written by God’s finger. They contained His exact words spoken from the mountain’s fire on the assembly day. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:11
After forty days and forty nights, He gave me the two stone covenant tablets. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:12
He said, “Go down immediately. Your people, whom you brought from Egypt, corrupted themselves. They quickly abandoned My commanded way and made a molten image.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:13
He also said, “I see these people are stubborn. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:14
Leave Me alone to destroy them and erase their name from under heaven. I will make you a greater, stronger nation.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:15
I descended the burning mountain carrying both covenant tablets. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:16
I saw your sin against the LORD your God. You made a molten calf and quickly abandoned His commanded way. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:17
I threw both tablets down and shattered them before your eyes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:18
I again fell before the LORD for forty days and forty nights. I ate no bread and drank no water. Your many sins and evil had provoked His anger. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:19
I feared His wrath, which could destroy you. He listened to me this time too. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:20
He was angry enough to destroy Aaron. I prayed for Aaron then too. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:21
I burned your sinful calf, crushed it, and ground it into fine dust. I threw the powder into the stream descending the mountain. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:22
You provoked the LORD at Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth-hattaavah too. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:23
He sent you from Kadesh-barnea to possess His land. You rebelled against His command, neither trusting nor obeying Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:24
You rebelled against Him from the day I knew you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:25
I fell before the LORD forty days and forty nights because He threatened to destroy you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 9:26
I prayed, “Lord GOD, spare Your people, Your inheritance. You redeemed them through Your greatness and rescued them from Egypt with a mighty hand. 🙏

## Deuteronomy 9:27
Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, Your servants. Overlook this people’s stubbornness and wicked sin. 🙏

## Deuteronomy 9:28
Otherwise, Egypt’s people will claim You could not give the promised land. They will say You hated us and brought us to die in the wilderness. 🙏

## Deuteronomy 9:29
They are Your people and inheritance. Your great power and outstretched arm brought them out.” 🙏
