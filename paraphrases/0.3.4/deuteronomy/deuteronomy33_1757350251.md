## Deuteronomy 33:1
Before his death, Moses the man of God pronounced this blessing on Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:2
He said, “The LORD came from Sinai and dawned on us from Seir. He shone from Mount Paran and came with myriads of holy ones. Flaming fire was at His right hand. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:3
Surely You love the people. All the holy ones are in Your hand. They sit at Your feet and each receives Your words. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:4
These are the law Moses gave us, the possession of Jacob’s assembly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:5
The LORD became King in Jeshurun when the people’s leaders gathered and Israel’s tribes came together. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:6
Let Reuben live and not die. Let his men not be few.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:7
About Judah he said, “LORD, hear Judah’s cry and bring him to his people. He defends his cause with his own hands. Help him against his enemies.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:8
About Levi he said, “Give Your Thummim to Levi and Your Urim to Your godly one. You tested him at Massah and contested with him at Meribah’s waters. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:9
He said of his parents, ‘I do not consider them.’ He disregarded his brothers and did not know his sons. He kept Your word and maintained Your covenant. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:10
He will teach Your ordinances to Jacob and Your law to Israel. He will place incense before You and whole burnt offerings on Your altar. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:11
LORD, bless his possessions and accept his hands’ work. Crush the loins of those who rise against him, his enemies, so they never rise again.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:12
About Benjamin he said, “Let the LORD’s beloved rest safely in Him. God shields him all day. He rests on His shoulders.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:13
About Joseph he said, “Let the LORD bless his land with precious dew from heaven and deep waters beneath, 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:14
with abundant harvests from the sun and abundant produce from the seasons, 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:15
with the best of ancient mountains and the abundance of everlasting hills, 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:16
with the land’s choice gifts and everything in it. May he have the favor of Him who lived in the burning bush. Let these rest on Joseph’s head and crown the brow of the prince among his brothers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:17
His majesty is like a firstborn bull, his horns like a wild ox’s horns. With them he will gore nations to earth’s ends. Such are Ephraim’s myriads and Manasseh’s thousands.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:18
About Zebulun he said, “Zebulun, rejoice in your journeys. Issachar, rejoice in your tents. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:19
They will call peoples to a mountain and offer righteous sacrifices there. They will feast on the seas’ abundance and treasures hidden in sand.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:20
About Gad he said, “Blessed is he who enlarges Gad’s territory. He lies like a lion and tears off an arm or head. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:21
He chose the best land because a ruler’s share was reserved for him there. He came with the people’s leaders. He administered the LORD’s justice and ordinances for Israel.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:22
About Dan he said, “Dan is a lion’s cub leaping from Bashan.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:23
About Naphtali he said, “Naphtali abounds in favor and is full of the LORD’s blessing. He will possess the sea and the south.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:24
About Asher he said, “Let Asher be the most blessed son and the most favored among his brothers. Let him dip his foot in oil. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:25
Let your gate’s bolts be iron and bronze. Let your strength match your days.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:26
“No one is like Jeshurun’s God. He rides the heavens to help you, and the clouds in His majesty. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:27
The eternal God is your home. His everlasting arms are underneath. He drives out your enemy and commands, ‘Destroy him!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:28
Israel lives safely. Jacob’s fountain lives untroubled in a land of grain and new wine. Even the heavens drip dew there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 33:29
Israel, you are blessed! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD? He is your protecting shield and the sword you boast in. Your enemies will cower before you. You will trample their high places.” 📖
