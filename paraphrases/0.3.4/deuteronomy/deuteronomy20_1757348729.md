## Deuteronomy 20:1
When you fight enemies with horses, chariots, and a larger army, do not fear them. The LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt, is with you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:2
Before battle, the priest must come forward and speak to the army. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:3
He must say, “Listen, Israel. Today you will fight your enemies. Do not lose courage or fear them. Do not be alarmed or terrified. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:4
The LORD your God goes with you to fight your enemies for you and give you victory.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:5
The officers must say, “Has anyone built a new house but not dedicated it? Let him go home. Otherwise, he may die in battle, and another man may dedicate it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:6
Has anyone planted a vineyard but not enjoyed its fruit? Let him go home. Otherwise, he may die in battle, and another man may enjoy its fruit. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:7
Is anyone pledged to marry a woman but has not married her? Let him go home. Otherwise, he may die in battle, and another man may marry her.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:8
The officers must also say, “Is anyone afraid or without courage? Let him go home, so his brothers’ hearts do not melt like his.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:9
After speaking to the army, the officers must appoint commanders to lead it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:10
When you approach a town to fight it, offer peace. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:11
If its people accept and open their gates, they must all serve you as forced laborers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:12
If they refuse peace and fight you, surround the town in a siege. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:13
When the LORD your God gives it into your hands, kill every male with the sword. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:14
But you may take the women, children, livestock, and everything else in the town as plunder. Use the plunder from your enemies that He gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:15
Treat all distant towns this way, those that do not belong to nearby nations. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:16
But in the towns of the nations He gives you as an inheritance, leave nothing breathing alive. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:17
Completely destroy the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, as the LORD your God commanded. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:18
Then they cannot teach you their detestable practices for their gods. They cannot thus cause you to sin against the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:19
During a long siege to capture a town, do not cut down its fruit trees with an axe. You can eat their fruit. Do not cut them down. Are field trees human, that you should besiege them? 📖

## Deuteronomy 20:20
You may cut down trees you know produce no fruit. Use them to build siege works against the town fighting you until it falls. 📖
