## Deuteronomy 15:1
Cancel debts at the end of every seven years. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:2
Every lender must cancel loans made to a neighbor. Do not collect from your neighbor or brother, because the LORD’s time of release has been proclaimed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:3
You may collect from a foreigner. But cancel whatever your brother owes you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:4
There will be no poor among you. The LORD will surely bless you in the land He gives you to possess as an inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:5
This applies if you obey Him and carefully follow all the commands I give you today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:6
He will bless you as promised. You will lend to many nations but borrow from none. You will rule many nations, but none will rule you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:7
A brother may be poor in any town in the land He gives you. If so, do not harden your heart or close your hand against him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:8
Open your hand to him. Freely lend whatever he needs. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:9
Do not entertain this evil thought: “The seventh year, the year of release, is near.” Do not resent your poor brother and give him nothing because of it. He will cry to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:10
Give generously without a sorrowful heart. Because of this, the LORD your God will bless all your work and everything you do. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:11
There will always be poor people in the land. Therefore, I command you to give freely to your brother and to poor and needy people in your land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:12
A fellow Hebrew, male or female, may be sold to you and serve six years. In the seventh year, set that person free. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:13
Do not send that person away with nothing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:14
Give generously from your flock, threshing floor, and winepress. Give according to the LORD your God’s blessing to you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:15
Remember that you were slaves in Egypt, and He freed you. That is why I give you this command today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:16
Your servant may say, “I do not want to leave.” He may love you and your household and live well with you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:17
If so, use an awl to pierce his ear into the door. He will become your servant for life. Treat a female servant the same way. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:18
Do not consider freeing your servant a hardship. Six years of his service were worth twice a hired worker’s wages. The LORD your God will bless everything you do. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:19
Set apart every firstborn male from your herds and flocks for the LORD your God. Do not work firstborn oxen or shear firstborn sheep. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:20
Each year, you and your household must eat them before Him at the place He chooses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:21
Do not sacrifice a defective animal to Him. This includes lame or blind animals and those with any serious flaw. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:22
Eat them in your towns. People who are ceremonially unclean or clean may eat them, as they would gazelle or deer. 📖

## Deuteronomy 15:23
But do not eat the blood. Pour it on the ground like water. 📖
