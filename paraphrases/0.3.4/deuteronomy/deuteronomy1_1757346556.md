## Deuteronomy 1:1
Moses spoke these words to all Israel in the wilderness east of the Jordan. They were in the Arabah opposite Suph, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:2
The journey from Horeb to Kadesh-barnea takes eleven days by Mount Seir’s route. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:3
In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses spoke to Israel. He told them everything the LORD commanded concerning them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:4
This followed his defeat of Sihon, the Amorites’ king, who lived in Heshbon. He also defeated Og, Bashan’s king, at Edrei. Og lived in Ashtaroth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:5
East of the Jordan in Moab, Moses began explaining this law. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:6
The LORD our God told us at Horeb, “You stayed at this mountain long enough. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:7
Continue to the Amorites’ hill country and neighboring peoples. Go through the Arabah, hills, foothills, Negev, and coast to Canaanite land and Lebanon. Go as far as the great Euphrates River. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:8
I placed the land before you. Enter and possess it. The LORD swore to give it to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob and their descendants.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:9
I told you then, “I cannot bear your burden alone. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:10
The LORD your God multiplied you. Today you are as numerous as the sky’s stars. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:11
May the LORD, your fathers’ God, increase you a thousand times and bless you as promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:12
How can I alone bear your troubles, burdens, and disputes? 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:13
Choose wise, understanding, respected men from every tribe. I will appoint them as leaders.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:14
You agreed my proposal was good. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:15
I appointed your wise, respected tribal leaders over you. They commanded thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens and served as tribal officers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:16
I instructed your judges, “Hear disputes between your brothers. Judge fairly between Israelites and between an Israelite and a foreign resident. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:17
Show no partiality. Hear small and great people equally. Fear no one, because judgment belongs to God. Bring difficult cases to me, and I will hear them.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:18
I commanded everything you were to do at that time. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:19
We obeyed the LORD our God and left Horeb for the Amorites’ hill country. We crossed the vast, terrifying wilderness you saw and reached Kadesh-barnea. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:20
I said, “You reached the Amorites’ hill country, which the LORD our God gives us. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:21
The LORD your God placed the land before you. Go and possess it as your fathers’ God commanded. Do not fear or be discouraged.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:22
You all approached me and said, “Send men ahead to explore the land. Let them report our route and the cities to enter.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:23
I approved and chose twelve men, one per tribe. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:24
They climbed into the hills, reached the Valley of Eshcol, and explored the land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:25
They brought us some fruit and reported, “The LORD our God gives us good land.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:26
You refused to go. You rebelled against the LORD your God’s command. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:27
You complained in your tents. “The LORD hates us. He brought us from Egypt to deliver us to the Amorites for destruction. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:28
Where can we go? Our brothers melted our hearts with fear. They described larger, taller people and huge cities walled to heaven. They even saw Anakim descendants.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:29
I told you, “Do not fear them! 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:30
The LORD your God goes ahead and will fight for you. You saw Him do this in Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:31
In the wilderness, He carried you like a father carries his son. He carried you throughout your journey to this place.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:32
Despite everything, you did not trust the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:33
He went ahead in fire at night and cloud by day. He found your campsites and showed you the road. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:34
The LORD heard your words, became angry, and swore, 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:35
“No man of this evil generation will see the good land I promised your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:36
Caleb, Jephunneh’s son, is the exception. He will see it. I will give him and his descendants the land he walked on because he followed the LORD wholeheartedly.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:37
The LORD was angry with me because of you. He said, “You will not enter either. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:38
Joshua, Nun’s son, who stands before you, will enter. Encourage him. He will enable Israel to inherit the land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:39
Your little children, whom you feared would become captives, will enter. They did not know good from evil that day. I will give them the land, and they will possess it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:40
Turn back toward the wilderness by the Red Sea route.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:41
You answered, “We sinned against the LORD. We will fight as He commanded.” You took up weapons, thinking the hill country easy to enter. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:42
The LORD told me, “Warn them against going to fight. I am not with you to protect you from defeat by your enemies.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:43
I warned you, but you refused. You rebelled against the LORD’s command and arrogantly entered the hills. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:44
The hill country’s Amorites chased you like a swarm of bees. They defeated you from Seir to Hormah. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:45
You returned and wept before the LORD. He would not listen. 📖

## Deuteronomy 1:46
So you stayed in Kadesh a very long time. 📖
