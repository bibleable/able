## Deuteronomy 17:1
Do not sacrifice an ox or sheep with any defect or serious flaw to the LORD your God. He finds this detestable. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:2
A man or woman in a town He gives you may do evil before Him by breaking His covenant. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:3
That person may worship and bow to other gods, the sun, the moon, or any heavenly host. I have forbidden this. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:4
If you hear a report of this, investigate thoroughly. Establish whether the report is true and this hateful act happened in Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:5
If so, bring the man or woman who did it to your gates. Stone that person to death. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:6
Execution requires the testimony of two or three witnesses. Do not execute anyone on one witness’s testimony. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:7
The witnesses must strike first to execute the person, followed by all the people. Thus remove evil from among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:8
A case in your town may be too difficult to judge, whether about bloodshed, lawsuits, or assaults. If so, go to the place the LORD your God chooses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:9
Consult the Levitical priests and the judge serving at that time. They will give a decision about the case. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:10
Accept their decision at the place the LORD chooses. Carefully follow all their instructions. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:11
Follow the law they teach and the decision they give. Do not turn right or left from their decision. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:12
Put to death anyone who arrogantly refuses to listen to the priest serving the LORD your God there, or to the judge. Remove evil from Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:13
All the people will hear and fear. They will stop behaving arrogantly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:14
You will enter, possess, and settle in the land He gives you. You may then say, “Let us have a king like the nations around us.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:15
If so, appoint the king the LORD your God chooses. He must be one of your brothers. Do not appoint a foreigner who is not your brother. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:16
The king must not acquire many horses or send people back to Egypt to obtain more. The LORD said, “Never go back that way again.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:17
He must not take many wives, because they could lead his heart astray. He must not accumulate great quantities of silver and gold. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:18
When he takes his royal throne, he must write himself a copy of this instruction on a scroll before the Levitical priests. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:19
He must keep it with him and read it throughout his life. Thus he learns to fear the LORD his God by carefully obeying every word of this instruction and these statutes. 📖

## Deuteronomy 17:20
Then he will not consider himself above his countrymen or turn right or left from the command. Thus he and his sons can reign many years over his kingdom in Israel. 📖
