## Deuteronomy 22:1
If you see your brother’s ox or sheep wandering away, do not ignore it. Return it to him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:2
If he lives far away or you do not know him, take the animal home. Keep it until he comes looking for it. Then return it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:3
Do the same with his donkey, cloak, or anything else he lost that you find. Do not ignore it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:4
If his donkey or ox falls on the road, do not ignore it. Help him lift it up. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:5
A woman must not wear men’s clothing, and a man must not wear women’s clothing. The LORD your God finds anyone doing this detestable. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:6
You may find a bird’s nest in a tree or on the ground along the road. If the mother sits on chicks or eggs, do not take her with the young. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:7
You may take the young, but let the mother go. Then things can go well for you, and you can live long. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:8
When you build a house, put a railing around its roof. Then you will not bring bloodguilt on your house if someone falls. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:9
Do not plant two kinds of seed in your vineyard. Otherwise, the whole harvest will be defiled, both the planted crop and the vineyard’s fruit. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:10
Do not plow with an ox and donkey under the same yoke. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:11
Do not wear clothing woven from wool and linen together. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:12
Make tassels on the four corners of your cloak. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:13
Suppose a man marries a woman, has sexual relations with her, and comes to hate her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:14
He may accuse her of shameful conduct and damage her reputation. He may say, “I married her and had sexual relations with her, but found she was not a virgin.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:15
Then her father and mother must bring proof of her virginity to the town’s elders at the gate. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:16
They must tell the elders, “I gave my daughter to this man in marriage, but he has come to hate her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:17
He accuses her of shameful conduct, saying, ‘Your daughter was not a virgin.’ But here is proof of her virginity.” They must spread the cloth before the elders. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:18
The town’s elders must take the man and punish him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:19
Fine him one hundred shekels of silver and give them to her father. He damaged an Israelite virgin’s reputation. She must remain his wife. He must never divorce her while he lives. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:20
But the accusation may be true, and no proof of her virginity may be found. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:21
If so, bring her to her father’s doorway. The town’s men must stone her to death there. She committed an outrage in Israel by being sexually promiscuous in her father’s house. Thus remove evil from among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:22
If a man is found having sexual relations with another man’s wife, both must die. Remove evil from Israel. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:23
A man may meet a virgin in town who is pledged to marry another man. He may have sexual relations with her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:24
If so, take both to the town’s gate and stone them to death. The woman did not cry out in town. The man violated his neighbor’s wife. Thus remove evil from among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:25
But if he finds a woman pledged in marriage in the open country and forces sexual relations on her, only he must die. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:26
Do nothing to the young woman. She committed no sin deserving death. This is like a man attacking and murdering his neighbor. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:27
He found her in the field. The woman pledged in marriage cried out, but no one was there to save her. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:28
A man may find a virgin who is not pledged in marriage, seize her, and have sexual relations with her. They may be discovered. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:29
If so, he must pay her father fifty shekels of silver. She must become his wife because he violated her. He must never divorce her while he lives. 📖

## Deuteronomy 22:30
A man must not marry his father’s wife. This would dishonor his father’s marriage bed. 📖
