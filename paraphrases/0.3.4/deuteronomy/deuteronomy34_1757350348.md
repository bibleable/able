## Deuteronomy 34:1
Moses climbed from Moab’s plains to Mount Nebo, to Pisgah’s summit facing Jericho. The LORD showed him all the land, from Gilead to Dan, 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:2
all Naphtali, Ephraim’s and Manasseh’s land, and all Judah as far as the Western Sea, 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:3
the Negev, and the region from Jericho’s valley to Zoar. Jericho is the City of Palms. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:4
The LORD said, “This is the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, ‘I will give it to your descendants.’ I let you see it with your own eyes. But you will not cross into it.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:5
Moses, the LORD’s servant, died there in Moab, as the LORD said. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:6
He buried Moses in a valley in Moab facing Beth-peor. No one knows his grave’s location to this day. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:7
Moses was one hundred and twenty years old when he died. His eyes were not weak, and his vitality had not decreased. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:8
Israel mourned Moses on Moab’s plains for thirty days. Then the time of weeping and mourning ended. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:9
Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses laid his hands on him. Israel obeyed him and did what the LORD commanded Moses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:10
Since then, no prophet like Moses has arisen in Israel. The LORD knew him face to face. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:11
No prophet did all the signs and wonders the LORD sent Moses to do in Egypt. He did them to Pharaoh, all his officials, and his whole land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 34:12
No prophet performed all the mighty acts and awesome deeds Moses did before all Israel. 📖
