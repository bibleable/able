## Deuteronomy 14:1
You are sons of the LORD your God. Do not cut yourselves or shave your foreheads to mourn the dead. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:2
You are a people holy to Him. From all earth’s peoples, He chose you as His prized possession. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:3
Do not eat any detestable thing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:4
You may eat these animals: oxen, sheep, and goats, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:5
also deer, gazelles, roe deer, wild goats, ibexes, antelopes, and mountain sheep. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:6
You may eat any animal with a hoof split in two that also chews the cud. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:7
Do not eat camels, rabbits, or rock badgers. They chew the cud but lack divided hooves. They are unclean for you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:8
Pigs are also unclean for you. They have divided hooves but do not chew the cud. Do not eat their meat or touch their dead bodies. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:9
You may eat water creatures that have both fins and scales. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:10
Do not eat any without fins and scales. They are unclean for you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:11
You may eat any clean bird. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:12
Do not eat eagles, bearded vultures, or black vultures, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:13
red kites, falcons, or any kind of kite, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:14
any kind of raven, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:15
ostriches, screech owls, gulls, or any kind of hawk, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:16
little owls, great owls, or white owls, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:17
desert owls, ospreys, or cormorants, 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:18
storks, any kind of heron, hoopoes, or bats. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:19
All flying insects are unclean for you. Do not eat them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:20
But you may eat any clean bird. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:21
Do not eat an animal found dead. You may give it to a foreigner living in your town to eat, or sell it to a foreigner. You are a holy people belonging to the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:22
Set aside a tenth of everything your fields produce each year. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:23
Eat a tenth of your grain, new wine, and oil, and the firstborn of your herds and flocks before Him. Do this where He chooses a home for His Name. This teaches you to fear the LORD your God always. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:24
The place He chooses for His Name may be too far away. You may be unable to carry the produce with which He has blessed you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:25
If so, exchange it for money. Carry the money to the place the LORD your God chooses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:26
Spend it on whatever you want: cattle, sheep, wine, strong drink, or anything else you desire. Feast there before the LORD your God and rejoice with your household. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:27
Do not neglect the Levite in your town. He has no share or inheritance among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:28
At the end of every three years, bring a tenth of that year’s produce. Store it in your towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 14:29
Then Levites, foreigners, fatherless people, and widows in your towns can eat and be satisfied. Levites have no share or inheritance among you. The LORD your God will bless all your work. 📖
