## Deuteronomy 12:1
Carefully follow these statutes and ordinances throughout your life in the land. The LORD, your fathers’ God, gave it to you to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:2
Completely destroy every place where the nations you drive out worshiped their gods. Destroy those on high mountains, on hills, and under every green tree. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:3
Pull down their altars and smash their sacred pillars. Burn their Asherah poles and cut down their gods’ idols. Remove their names from every place. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:4
Do not worship the LORD your God this way. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:5
Seek the place He will choose among all your tribes as a home for His Name. You must go there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:6
Bring your burnt offerings, sacrifices, tithes, and heave offerings there. Also bring your vow offerings, freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herds and flocks. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:7
You and your households must eat there before the LORD your God. Rejoice in all you do, because He has blessed you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:8
Do not follow our practice here today. Each person does what seems right to him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:9
You have not yet reached the resting place and inheritance the LORD your God gives you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:10
You will cross the Jordan and live in the land He gives you as an inheritance. He will give you rest from all surrounding enemies, and you will live safely. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:11
Then He will choose a home for His Name. Bring everything I command there: burnt offerings, sacrifices, tithes, special gifts, and all choice offerings you vow to the LORD. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:12
Rejoice before Him with your sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, and the Levite in your town. The Levite has no share or inheritance among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:13
Be careful. Do not offer burnt offerings at any place you happen to see. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:14
Offer them only where the LORD chooses within one of your tribes’ territories. Do everything I command there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:15
You may kill animals and eat meat in any town whenever you want, according to the blessing He gives you. People who are ceremonially clean or unclean may eat it, as they would gazelle or deer. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:16
But do not eat the blood. Pour it on the ground like water. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:17
Do not eat these in your towns: tithes of grain, new wine, or oil, or the firstborn of herds or flocks. Also, do not eat vow offerings, freewill offerings, or special gifts there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:18
Eat them before the LORD your God at the place He chooses. Include your sons, daughters, male servants, female servants, and the Levite in your town. Rejoice before Him in all you do. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:19
Be careful never to neglect the Levites while you live in your land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:20
He will expand your territory as promised. When you crave meat and say, “I want meat,” you may eat it whenever you want. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:21
The place He chooses for His Name may be too far away. If so, you may kill animals from the herd or flock He gave you, as I commanded. Eat the meat in your towns whenever you want. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:22
You may eat it as you would gazelle or deer. People who are ceremonially unclean or clean may eat it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:23
Be sure not to eat blood. Blood is the life, and you must not eat the life with the meat. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:24
Do not eat the blood. Pour it on the ground like water. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:25
Do not eat it, so things can go well for you and your children after you. You will be doing what the LORD sees as right. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:26
Take your holy things and vow offerings to the place the LORD chooses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:27
Present the meat and blood of burnt offerings on His altar. Pour the blood of your other sacrifices beside His altar, but you may eat their meat. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:28
Carefully obey everything I command, so things can always go well for you and your children after you. You will be doing what the LORD your God sees as good and right. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:29
He will destroy the nations before you as you enter to take their land. You will drive them out and live there. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:30
After their destruction, be careful their ways do not trap you. Do not ask about their gods, saying, “How do these nations serve their gods? I will do the same.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:31
Do not worship the LORD your God this way. They do every hateful thing He hates for their gods. They even burn their sons and daughters as sacrifices to their gods. 📖

## Deuteronomy 12:32
Do everything I command you. Do not add anything or remove anything. 📖
