## Deuteronomy 27:1
Moses and Israel’s elders commanded the people, “Keep every command I give you today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:2
On the day you cross the Jordan into the land the LORD your God gives you, set up large stones. Cover them with plaster. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:3
After crossing, write every word of this law on them. You will enter the land flowing with milk and honey that He gives you, as the LORD your fathers’ God promised. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:4
After crossing the Jordan, set up these stones on Mount Ebal, as I command today. Cover them with plaster. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:5
Build a stone altar to the LORD your God there. Do not use iron tools on its stones. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:6
Build His altar from uncut stones. Offer burnt offerings to Him on it. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:7
Sacrifice your peace offerings there. Eat them and rejoice before the LORD your God. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:8
Write every word of this law clearly on these stones.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:9
Moses and the Levitical priests told all Israel, “Be silent and listen, Israel! Today you became the LORD your God’s people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:10
Therefore, obey His voice. Follow His commands and statutes I give you today.” 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:11
That day Moses commanded the people, 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:12
“After you cross the Jordan, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin must stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:13
Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali must stand on Mount Ebal to proclaim the curse. 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:14
The Levites must proclaim loudly to every Israelite, 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:15
‘Cursed is anyone who makes a carved idol or cast image and secretly sets it up. It is a craftsman’s work, detestable to the LORD.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:16
‘Cursed is anyone who dishonors his father or mother.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:17
‘Cursed is anyone who moves his neighbor’s boundary stone.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:18
‘Cursed is anyone who lets a blind man wander in the road.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:19
‘Cursed is anyone who denies justice to foreigners, fatherless people, or widows.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:20
‘Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with his father’s wife. He violated his father’s marriage bed.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:21
‘Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with an animal.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:22
‘Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with his sister, his father’s daughter or his mother’s daughter.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:23
‘Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with his mother-in-law.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:24
‘Cursed is anyone who secretly strikes down his neighbor.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:25
‘Cursed is anyone who accepts a bribe to kill an innocent person.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’ 📖

## Deuteronomy 27:26
‘Cursed is anyone who fails to put this law’s words into practice.’ All the people must say, ‘Amen!’” 📖
