## Deuteronomy 8:1
Carefully obey every command I give today. Then you may live, multiply, and possess the land the LORD swore to your fathers. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:2
Remember His guidance throughout forty wilderness years. He humbled and tested you to know your heart and whether you would obey His commands. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:3
He humbled you and fed you manna in your hunger. Neither you nor your fathers knew this food. He taught you that people live by every word from the LORD’s mouth, rather than bread alone. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:4
For forty years, your clothes did not wear out and your feet did not swell. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:5
Know in your heart that the LORD your God disciplines you like a father disciplines his son. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:6
Obey His commands, follow His ways, and fear Him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:7
He brings you into good land with brooks, fountains, and springs flowing through valleys and hills. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:8
It has wheat, barley, vines, fig trees, pomegranates, olive oil, and honey. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:9
Food will be abundant, and you will lack nothing. The rocks contain iron, and the hills provide copper to mine. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:10
After eating your fill, bless the LORD your God for the good land He gave. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:11
Remember Him by keeping the commands, ordinances, and statutes I give today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:12
Beware when you eat your fill and build fine houses to live in. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:13
Beware when your herds, flocks, silver, gold, and everything you own increase. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:14
Otherwise, pride will fill your heart. You will forget the LORD your God who freed you from Egypt’s house of slavery. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:15
He led you through the vast, terrifying, waterless wilderness with venomous snakes and scorpions. He brought water from flint rock. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:16
He fed you manna unknown to your fathers. He humbled and tested you so you might prosper in the end. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:17
You may claim your own hands’ strength and power made your wealth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:18
Remember the LORD your God gives power to gain wealth. He confirms His sworn covenant with your fathers, as today. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:19
If you forget Him and worship and bow to other gods, I testify today that you will certainly perish. 📖

## Deuteronomy 8:20
If you disobey Him, you will perish like the nations He destroyed before you. 📖
