## Deuteronomy 24:1
A man may marry a woman and find some indecency in her that displeases him. He may write a divorce certificate, give it to her, and send her from his house. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:2
After leaving, she may marry another man. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:3
The second husband may hate her and also write a divorce certificate, give it to her, and send her away. Or he may die. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:4
Then her first husband must not remarry her after she has been defiled. The LORD finds that detestable. Do not bring sin on the land the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:5
Do not send a newly married man to war or assign him any duty. He is free to stay home for one year and bring joy to his wife. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:6
Do not take a pair of millstones, or even the upper stone, as security for a debt. That would take someone’s livelihood as security. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:7
If someone kidnaps an Israelite brother and enslaves or sells him, the kidnapper must die. Thus remove evil from among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:8
In cases of infectious skin disease, carefully follow all the Levitical priests’ instructions. Do everything I commanded them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:9
Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on your journey after leaving Egypt. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:10
When you lend anything to your neighbor, do not enter his house to collect security. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:11
Wait outside while the borrower brings it out to you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:12
If he is poor, do not go to sleep while holding his security. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:13
Return it by sunset, so he can sleep in his own cloak and bless you. The LORD your God will count this as righteousness for you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:14
Do not oppress a poor, needy hired worker, whether a brother or a foreigner living in your towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:15
Pay his wages each day before sunset. He is poor and depends on them. Otherwise, he may cry to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:16
Do not execute fathers for their children’s sins, or children for their fathers’ sins. Each must die for his own sin. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:17
Do not deny justice to foreigners or fatherless people. Do not take a widow’s cloak as security. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:18
Remember that you were slaves in Egypt, and the LORD your God freed you from there. Therefore, I command you to do this. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:19
If you forget a sheaf while harvesting your field, do not return for it. Leave it for foreigners, fatherless people, and widows. Then the LORD your God can bless all your work. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:20
When you beat olives from your trees, do not check the branches again. Leave what remains for foreigners, fatherless people, and widows. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:21
When you gather grapes, do not check the vines again. Leave what remains for foreigners, fatherless people, and widows. 📖

## Deuteronomy 24:22
Remember that you were slaves in Egypt. Therefore, I command you to do this. 📖
