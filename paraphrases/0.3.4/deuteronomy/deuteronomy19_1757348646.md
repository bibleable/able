## Deuteronomy 19:1
The LORD your God will destroy the nations whose land He gives you. You will drive them out and settle in their towns and houses. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:2
Then set apart three towns in the land He gives you to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:3
Build roads and divide the land He gives you as an inheritance into three regions. Anyone who kills another person can flee to these towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:4
This is the case of someone who flees there to save his life. He killed his neighbor accidentally, without intending harm. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:5
He may enter a forest with his neighbor to cut wood. As he swings his axe at a tree, the blade may fly off and kill his neighbor. He can flee to one of these towns to save his life. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:6
Otherwise, the avenger of blood might chase him in anger. If the distance is great, the avenger might catch and kill him. He does not deserve death because he intended no harm. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:7
Therefore, I command you to set apart three towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:8
The LORD your God may enlarge your territory as He swore to your fathers. He may give you all the land He promised them. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:9
If He does, and you carefully keep every command I give today, add three more towns. Love the LORD your God and always walk in His ways. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:10
Then no innocent blood will be shed in the land He gives you as an inheritance. You will not be guilty of bloodshed. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:11
But a man may hate his neighbor, wait in hiding, attack and kill him, then flee to one of these towns. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:12
If so, his town’s elders must send for him and bring him back. Hand him to the avenger of blood to die. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:13
Do not pity him. Remove the guilt of shedding innocent blood from Israel, so things can go well for you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:14
Do not move your neighbor’s boundary marker. Your ancestors placed it to mark your inheritance in the land the LORD your God gives you to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:15
One witness cannot establish guilt for any wrongdoing or sin, whatever the offense. Two or three witnesses’ testimony must establish the matter. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:16
A false witness may accuse someone of a crime. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:17
If so, both parties must stand before the LORD, in front of the priests and judges serving at that time. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:18
The judges must investigate thoroughly. They may establish that the witness lied and falsely accused his brother. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:19
If so, do to the witness what he intended for his brother. Thus remove evil from among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:20
The rest of the people will hear and fear. They will never again do such evil among you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 19:21
Show no pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, and foot for foot. 📖
