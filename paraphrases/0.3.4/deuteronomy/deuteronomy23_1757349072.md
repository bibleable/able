## Deuteronomy 23:1
No man with crushed or severed genitals may enter the LORD’s assembly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:2
No one born illegitimately may enter the LORD’s assembly. None of his descendants may enter, even to the tenth generation. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:3
No Ammonite, Moabite, or any of their descendants may enter the LORD’s assembly, even to the tenth generation. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:4
They did not meet you with food and water as you left Egypt. They hired Balaam son of Beor, from Pethor in Aram-naharaim, to curse you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:5
But the LORD your God refused to listen to Balaam. Because He loves you, He turned the curse into a blessing for you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:6
Never seek peace or prosperity from them throughout your life. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:7
Do not despise an Edomite, because he is your brother. Do not despise an Egyptian, because you lived as a foreigner in his land. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:8
Their children in the third generation may enter the LORD’s assembly. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:9
When you camp against your enemies, keep away from every wicked thing. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:10
If a man becomes unclean through an emission during the night, he must leave the camp and remain outside. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:11
As evening approaches, he must wash with water. After sunset, he may return to camp. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:12
Have a place outside the camp where you can relieve yourselves. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:13
Include a digging tool in your equipment. When you relieve yourself, dig a hole and cover your excrement. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:14
The LORD your God walks through your camp to protect you and give your enemies into your hands. Keep your camp holy, so He does not see anything unclean and turn away. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:15
Do not return a slave to his master if he takes refuge with you. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:16
Let him live among you wherever he chooses, in the town he likes. Do not oppress him. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:17
No daughter or son of Israel may be a shrine prostitute. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:18
Do not bring a female or male prostitute’s earnings into the LORD your God’s house to fulfill a vow. He finds both detestable. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:19
Do not charge your brother interest on loans of money, food, or anything else. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:20
You may charge foreigners interest, but never your brother. Then the LORD your God can bless everything you do in the land you enter to possess. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:21
If you vow something to Him, fulfill it promptly. He will require it from you, and failure will make you guilty of sin. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:22
If you make no vow, you will not be guilty of sin. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:23
Carefully fulfill what your lips promise. You freely made that vow to the LORD your God with your own mouth. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:24
When you enter your neighbor’s vineyard, you may eat grapes until satisfied. But put none in your basket. 📖

## Deuteronomy 23:25
When you enter your neighbor’s grainfield, you may pick grain heads by hand. But do not cut his grain with a sickle. 📖
