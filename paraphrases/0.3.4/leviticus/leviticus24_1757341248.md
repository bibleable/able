## Leviticus 24:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 24:2
“Command Israel to bring you pure pressed olive oil for the light, keeping the lamps burning continually. 📖
## Leviticus 24:3
Aaron must tend them before the LORD from evening to morning outside the Testimony’s veil in the Tent of Meeting. This is a permanent statute for every generation. 📖
## Leviticus 24:4
Tend the lamps on the pure gold lampstand continually before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 24:5
Bake twelve loaves with fine flour, two-tenths of an ephah for each. 📖
## Leviticus 24:6
Arrange them in two rows of six on the pure gold table before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 24:7
Put pure frankincense near each row as the bread’s memorial portion, a food offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 24:8
Set out the bread before the LORD every Sabbath for Israel as a permanent covenant. 📖
## Leviticus 24:9
It belongs to Aaron and his sons, who must eat it in a holy place. It is his permanent, most holy portion of the LORD’s food offerings.” 📖
## Leviticus 24:10
A man with an Israelite mother and Egyptian father went among Israel. He fought an Israelite in the camp. 📖
## Leviticus 24:11
He blasphemed the Name with a curse and was brought to Moses. His mother was Shelomith daughter of Dibri, of Dan’s tribe. 📖
## Leviticus 24:12
They held him in custody until the LORD’s will became clear. 📖
## Leviticus 24:13
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 24:14
“Take the blasphemer outside the camp. Have all who heard him lay their hands on his head. The whole assembly must stone him. 📖
## Leviticus 24:15
Tell Israel: Anyone cursing his God bears his sin’s consequences. 📖
## Leviticus 24:16
Anyone blaspheming the LORD’s name must be put to death. The whole assembly must stone him, whether native or resident foreigner. 📖
## Leviticus 24:17
Anyone taking another person’s life must be put to death. 📖
## Leviticus 24:18
Anyone killing an animal must make restitution, life for life. 📖
## Leviticus 24:19
Do to someone who injures his neighbor what he did: 📖
## Leviticus 24:20
fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. Inflict the same injury on him. 📖
## Leviticus 24:21
Anyone killing an animal must make restitution. Anyone killing a person must be put to death. 📖
## Leviticus 24:22
Apply the same law to resident foreigner and native. I am the LORD your God.” 📖
## Leviticus 24:23
Moses told Israel. They took the blasphemer outside the camp and stoned him, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖
