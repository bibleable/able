## Leviticus 18:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 18:2
“Tell Israel: I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 18:3
Do not follow Egypt’s practices, where you lived, or Canaan’s practices, where I am bringing you. Do not follow their customs. 📖
## Leviticus 18:4
Obey My judgments and statutes. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 18:5
Keep My statutes and judgments, for whoever does so will live by them. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 18:6
Do not approach a close relative for sexual relations. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 18:7
Do not expose your father’s nakedness by having sexual relations with your mother. She is your mother. Do not have sexual relations with her. 📖
## Leviticus 18:8
Do not have sexual relations with your father’s wife. It dishonors your father. 📖
## Leviticus 18:9
Do not have sexual relations with your sister, whether your father’s or mother’s daughter. This applies whether she was born in the same home or elsewhere. 📖
## Leviticus 18:10
Do not have sexual relations with your son’s daughter or daughter’s daughter. That shames your family. 📖
## Leviticus 18:11
Do not have sexual relations with your father’s wife’s daughter born to your father. She is your sister. 📖
## Leviticus 18:12
Do not have sexual relations with your father’s sister. She is his close relative. 📖
## Leviticus 18:13
Do not have sexual relations with your mother’s sister. She is her close relative. 📖
## Leviticus 18:14
Do not dishonor your father’s brother by approaching his wife for sexual relations. She is your aunt. 📖
## Leviticus 18:15
Do not have sexual relations with your daughter-in-law, your son’s wife. 📖
## Leviticus 18:16
Do not have sexual relations with your brother’s wife. That shames your brother. 📖
## Leviticus 18:17
Do not have sexual relations with both a woman and her daughter. Do not marry and have sexual relations with her son’s daughter or daughter’s daughter. They are close relatives. It is depraved. 📖
## Leviticus 18:18
Do not take your wife’s sister as a rival wife for sexual relations while your wife lives. 📖
## Leviticus 18:19
Do not approach a menstruating woman for sexual relations. 📖
## Leviticus 18:20
Do not have sexual relations with your neighbor’s wife and defile yourself with her. 📖
## Leviticus 18:21
Do not give your children to be sacrificed to Molech. Do not profane your God’s name. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 18:22
Do not lie with a man as with a woman. It is an abomination. 📖
## Leviticus 18:23
Do not have sexual relations with an animal and defile yourself. A woman must not approach an animal to mate with it. It is a perversion. 📖
## Leviticus 18:24
Do not defile yourselves with these practices. The nations I am driving out defiled themselves with them. 📖
## Leviticus 18:25
The land is defiled, so I am punishing it for its sin. It will vomit out its inhabitants. 📖
## Leviticus 18:26
Natives and resident foreigners must keep My statutes and ordinances. Commit none of these abominations. 📖
## Leviticus 18:27
The previous inhabitants committed them all, defiling the land. 📖
## Leviticus 18:28
If you defile it, it will vomit you out as it expelled the nations before you. 📖
## Leviticus 18:29
Anyone committing these abominations must be cut off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 18:30
Keep My charge against these former abominable customs so you do not defile yourselves. I am the LORD your God.” 📖
