## Leviticus 14:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 14:2
“This is the cleansing law for someone with skin disease when he is brought to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 14:3
The priest must examine him outside the camp. If his disease healed, 📖
## Leviticus 14:4
the priest must order two live clean birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop brought for his cleansing. 📖
## Leviticus 14:5
Order one bird slaughtered over fresh water in a clay pot. 📖
## Leviticus 14:6
Dip the live bird, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop into the slaughtered bird’s blood over the fresh water. 📖
## Leviticus 14:7
Sprinkle the person seven times, pronounce him clean, and release the live bird into the open field. 📖
## Leviticus 14:8
He must wash his clothes, shave all his hair, and bathe with water to become ceremonially clean. He may enter the camp but must stay outside his tent seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 14:9
On day seven, shave all hair, including his head, beard, and eyebrows. Wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be clean. 📖
## Leviticus 14:10
On day eight, bring two male lambs and a year-old ewe lamb, all without defect. Bring three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with olive oil as a grain offering and one log of olive oil. 📖
## Leviticus 14:11
The cleansing priest must present the person and these offerings before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 14:12
Present one male lamb as a guilt offering with the log of olive oil. Wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖
## Leviticus 14:13
Slaughter the lamb in the sanctuary area where sin and burnt offerings are slaughtered. Like the sin offering, it belongs to the priest and is most holy. 📖
## Leviticus 14:14
Put guilt offering blood on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe. 📖
## Leviticus 14:15
The priest must pour some olive oil from the log into his left palm. 📖
## Leviticus 14:16
He must dip his right forefinger into the oil in his left palm and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 14:17
Put oil from his palm on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe, atop the guilt offering blood. 📖
## Leviticus 14:18
Put the oil remaining in the priest’s palm on the person’s head to make atonement before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 14:19
Sacrifice the sin offering to make atonement for his uncleanness. Then slaughter the burnt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 14:20
Offer it on the altar with the grain offering to make atonement for him. He will be clean. 📖
## Leviticus 14:21
If he cannot afford these, bring one male lamb as a guilt offering to wave for atonement. Bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with olive oil as a grain offering and a log of olive oil. 📖
## Leviticus 14:22
Also bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford. One is a sin offering, the other a burnt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 14:23
On day eight, bring them for cleansing to the priest before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 14:24
The priest must wave the guilt offering lamb and log of olive oil before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖
## Leviticus 14:25
Slaughter the lamb and put its blood on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe. 📖
## Leviticus 14:26
The priest must pour some oil into his left palm. 📖
## Leviticus 14:27
He must sprinkle oil from his left palm seven times before the LORD with his right forefinger. 📖
## Leviticus 14:28
Put oil on the person’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe, on the same places as the guilt offering blood. 📖
## Leviticus 14:29
Put the oil remaining in the priest’s palm on the person’s head to make atonement for him before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 14:30
Sacrifice the turtledoves or young pigeons, whichever he can afford. 📖
## Leviticus 14:31
Offer one for sin and the other as a burnt offering, with the grain offering. The priest will make atonement before the LORD for the person being cleansed. 📖
## Leviticus 14:32
This is the law for someone with skin disease who cannot afford his cleansing.” 📖
## Leviticus 14:33
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖
## Leviticus 14:34
“I am giving you Canaan as your possession. If I put mildew in a house there after you enter, 📖
## Leviticus 14:35
its owner must tell the priest, ‘Something like mildew appeared in my house.’ 📖
## Leviticus 14:36
The priest must order the house emptied before inspecting it so nothing inside becomes unclean. Then he must enter and inspect. 📖
## Leviticus 14:37
If he finds green or red wall depressions appearing beneath the surface, 📖
## Leviticus 14:38
he must leave through the doorway and close the house seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 14:39
He must return on day seven and inspect. If mildew spread on the walls, 📖
## Leviticus 14:40
order the contaminated stones removed and thrown into an unclean place outside the city. 📖
## Leviticus 14:41
Have the entire interior scraped and its plaster dumped in an unclean place outside the city. 📖
## Leviticus 14:42
Replace the stones and replaster with additional mortar. 📖
## Leviticus 14:43
If mildew returns after the stones were replaced and the house scraped and replastered, 📖
## Leviticus 14:44
the priest must inspect it. If mildew spread, it is destructive mildew, and the house is unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 14:45
Demolish the house and carry its stones, timbers, and all plaster outside the city to an unclean place. 📖
## Leviticus 14:46
Anyone entering the closed house during those days is unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 14:47
Anyone sleeping or eating there must wash his clothes. 📖
## Leviticus 14:48
If the priest finds no spreading mildew after replastering, pronounce the house clean because the mildew is gone. 📖
## Leviticus 14:49
Take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop to purify it. 📖
## Leviticus 14:50
Slaughter one bird over fresh water in a clay pot. 📖
## Leviticus 14:51
Dip the cedar wood, hyssop, scarlet yarn, and live bird into the slaughtered bird’s blood and fresh water. Sprinkle the house seven times. 📖
## Leviticus 14:52
Cleanse the house with the bird’s blood, fresh water, live bird, cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet yarn. 📖
## Leviticus 14:53
Release the live bird into open fields outside the city. This makes atonement for the house, and it will be clean. 📖
## Leviticus 14:54
This law concerns infectious skin diseases and scaly outbreaks, 📖
## Leviticus 14:55
mildew in clothing or houses, 📖
## Leviticus 14:56
and swellings, rashes, or spots. 📖
## Leviticus 14:57
It determines when something is clean or unclean. This is the skin disease and mildew law.” 📖
