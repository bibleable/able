## Leviticus 20:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 20:2
“Tell Israel: An Israelite or resident foreigner giving a child to Molech must be put to death. The land’s people must stone him. 📖
## Leviticus 20:3
I will set My face against him and cut him off from his people. Giving his offspring to Molech defiled My sanctuary and profaned My holy name. 📖
## Leviticus 20:4
If the people look away and fail to execute the man giving his child to Molech, 📖
## Leviticus 20:5
I will set My face against him and his family. I will cut him off from his people. I will also cut off everyone following him in prostitution with Molech. 📖
## Leviticus 20:6
I will set My face against anyone consulting mediums or spiritists to prostitute himself with them. I will cut him off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 20:7
Consecrate yourselves and be holy because I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 20:8
Obey My statutes. I am the LORD who sanctifies you. 📖
## Leviticus 20:9
Anyone cursing his father or mother must be put to death. He cursed his parent, and his blood is upon him. 📖
## Leviticus 20:10
If a man commits adultery with his neighbor’s wife, both adulterer and adulteress must be put to death. 📖
## Leviticus 20:11
If a man lies with his father’s wife, he exposes his father’s nakedness. Both must be put to death. Their blood is upon them. 📖
## Leviticus 20:12
If a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both must be put to death. They acted perversely. Their blood is upon them. 📖
## Leviticus 20:13
If a man lies with a man as with a woman, both committed an abomination. Both must be put to death. Their blood is upon them. 📖
## Leviticus 20:14
If a man marries a woman and her mother, it is depraved. Burn him and both women in fire so no depravity remains among you. 📖
## Leviticus 20:15
If a man has sexual relations with an animal, put him to death and kill the animal. 📖
## Leviticus 20:16
If a woman approaches an animal to mate, kill both her and the animal. They must be put to death. Their blood is upon them. 📖
## Leviticus 20:17
If a man marries his sister, his father’s or mother’s daughter, and they have sexual relations, it is disgraceful. Cut them off before their people. He exposed his sister’s nakedness and bears his iniquity. 📖
## Leviticus 20:18
If a man has sexual relations with a menstruating woman, both exposed the source of her blood flow. Cut both off from their people. 📖
## Leviticus 20:19
Do not have sexual relations with your mother’s or father’s sister. This exposes your own kin. Both bear their iniquity. 📖
## Leviticus 20:20
Lying with your uncle’s wife exposes his nakedness. They bear their sin and will die childless. 📖
## Leviticus 20:21
Marrying your brother’s wife is impurity and exposes his nakedness. They will be childless. 📖
## Leviticus 20:22
Keep all My statutes and ordinances so the land I am bringing you to inhabit will not vomit you out. 📖
## Leviticus 20:23
Do not follow the statutes of nations I am driving out. I abhorred them because they did all these things. 📖
## Leviticus 20:24
You will inherit their land, flowing with milk and honey, because I give it to you. I am the LORD your God, who separated you from the peoples. 📖
## Leviticus 20:25
Distinguish clean animals and birds from unclean ones. Do not be contaminated by animals, birds, or crawling things I set apart as unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 20:26
Be holy to Me because I, the LORD, am holy. I separated you from the nations to be My own. 📖
## Leviticus 20:27
A male or female medium or spiritist must be put to death by stoning. Their blood is upon them.” 📖
