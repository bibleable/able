## Leviticus 4:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 4:2
“Tell Israel what to do when someone unintentionally violates a command of the LORD and does what it forbids. 📖
## Leviticus 4:3
If the anointed priest sins and brings guilt on the people, he must offer a young bull without defect to the LORD. It is a sin offering for his sin. 📖
## Leviticus 4:4
Bring it to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance before the LORD. Lay your hand on its head and slaughter it before Him. 📖
## Leviticus 4:5
The anointed priest must bring some blood into the Tent of Meeting. 📖
## Leviticus 4:6
He must dip his finger in it and sprinkle blood seven times before the LORD, in front of the sanctuary veil. 📖
## Leviticus 4:7
Put blood on the fragrant incense altar’s horns before the LORD inside the Tent of Meeting. Pour the remaining blood at the burnt offering altar’s base at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 4:8
Remove all the sin offering bull’s fat, including the fat covering the entrails and all their fat. 📖
## Leviticus 4:9
Include both kidneys and their fat near the loins. Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys. 📖
## Leviticus 4:10
Remove these as from the peace offering ox. The priest must burn them on the burnt offering altar. 📖
## Leviticus 4:11
The bull’s hide, all its flesh, head, legs, entrails, and dung must be taken away. 📖
## Leviticus 4:12
Take all these remaining parts outside the camp to the ceremonially clean ash disposal place. Burn them on a wood fire on the ash heap. 📖
## Leviticus 4:13
Suppose all Israel’s congregation unintentionally strays without realizing it. They violate one of the LORD’s commands by doing what is forbidden and become guilty. 📖
## Leviticus 4:14
When they realize their sin, the assembly must bring a young bull as a sin offering before the Tent of Meeting. 📖
## Leviticus 4:15
The elders must lay their hands on its head before the LORD. Slaughter it before Him. 📖
## Leviticus 4:16
The anointed priest must bring some blood into the Tent of Meeting. 📖
## Leviticus 4:17
He must dip his finger in it and sprinkle blood seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil. 📖
## Leviticus 4:18
Put blood on the altar’s horns before the LORD inside the Tent of Meeting. Pour the rest at the burnt offering altar’s base at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 4:19
Remove all its fat and burn it on the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 4:20
Offer this bull as the previous sin offering bull. The priest will make atonement for the people, and they will be forgiven. 📖
## Leviticus 4:21
Take the bull outside the camp and burn it like the first bull. It is the assembly’s sin offering. 📖
## Leviticus 4:22
A leader becomes guilty when he unintentionally violates a command of the LORD his God and does what it forbids. 📖
## Leviticus 4:23
When he realizes his sin, he must bring a male goat without defect. 📖
## Leviticus 4:24
Lay your hand on its head and slaughter it where burnt offerings are slaughtered before the LORD. It is a sin offering. 📖
## Leviticus 4:25
The priest must take blood with his finger and put it on the burnt offering altar’s horns. Pour the rest at the altar’s base. 📖
## Leviticus 4:26
Burn all its fat like peace offering fat. The priest will make atonement for the leader’s sin, and he will be forgiven. 📖
## Leviticus 4:27
A common person becomes guilty when he unintentionally violates one of the LORD’s commands and does what it forbids. 📖
## Leviticus 4:28
When he realizes his sin, he must offer a female goat without defect for it. 📖
## Leviticus 4:29
Lay your hand on the sin offering’s head and slaughter it at the burnt offering place. 📖
## Leviticus 4:30
The priest must take blood with his finger and put it on the burnt offering altar’s horns. Pour the rest at its base. 📖
## Leviticus 4:31
Remove all fat as from the peace offering. The priest must burn it on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. He will make atonement for the person, who will be forgiven. 📖
## Leviticus 4:32
If he brings a lamb as a sin offering, it must be a female without defect. 📖
## Leviticus 4:33
Lay your hand on its head. Slaughter it as a sin offering where burnt offerings are slaughtered. 📖
## Leviticus 4:34
The priest must take blood with his finger and put it on the burnt offering altar’s horns. Pour the rest at its base. 📖
## Leviticus 4:35
Remove all fat as from the peace offering lamb. Burn it on the altar with the LORD’s food offerings. The priest will make atonement for the person’s sin, and he will be forgiven. 📖
