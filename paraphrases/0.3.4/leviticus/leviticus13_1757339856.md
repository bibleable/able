## Leviticus 13:1
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖
## Leviticus 13:2
“Bring anyone with a swelling, rash, or bright skin spot that might be infectious to Aaron or one of his priestly sons. 📖
## Leviticus 13:3
The priest must examine it. White hair in a sore appearing deeper than the skin indicates skin disease. He must pronounce the person unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:4
If the spot is white, appears no deeper than the skin, and its hair has not turned white, isolate the person seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:5
Reexamine him on day seven. If the infection is unchanged and has not spread, isolate him seven more days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:6
Reexamine him on day seven. If it faded without spreading, pronounce him clean. It is a rash. He must wash his clothes and be clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:7
If the rash spreads after the priest examined him for cleansing, he must return to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 13:8
The priest must reexamine him. If it spread, pronounce him unclean. It is skin disease. 📖
## Leviticus 13:9
Bring anyone developing skin disease to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 13:10
The priest must examine him. If he finds a white swelling with hair turned white and raw flesh in it, 📖
## Leviticus 13:11
it is chronic skin disease. Pronounce him unclean. Isolation is unnecessary because he is unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:12
If the disease covers all his skin from head to foot, as far as the priest can see, 📖
## Leviticus 13:13
the priest must examine him. If the disease covers his whole body, pronounce him clean. He is clean because it all turned white. 📖
## Leviticus 13:14
Whenever raw flesh appears, he becomes unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:15
The priest must pronounce him unclean upon seeing raw flesh. It is unclean skin disease. 📖
## Leviticus 13:16
If the raw flesh turns white, he must go to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 13:17
The priest must reexamine him. If the infection turned white, pronounce him clean, and he is clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:18
These rules apply when a skin boil has healed. 📖
## Leviticus 13:19
If a white swelling or reddish-white spot develops there, the person must go to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 13:20
The priest must examine it. If it appears beneath the skin and its hair turned white, pronounce him unclean. Disease broke out in the boil. 📖
## Leviticus 13:21
If it has no white hair, is not beneath the skin, and has faded, isolate him seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:22
If it spreads, pronounce him unclean. It is an infection. 📖
## Leviticus 13:23
If the spot remains unchanged without spreading, it is a boil scar. Pronounce him clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:24
If a burn’s raw area becomes reddish-white or white, 📖
## Leviticus 13:25
the priest must examine it. If its hair turned white and it appears deeper than the skin, disease broke out in the burn. Pronounce him unclean because it is a diseased infection. 📖
## Leviticus 13:26
If it has no white hair, is not beneath the skin, and has faded, isolate him seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:27
Reexamine him on day seven. If it spread, pronounce him unclean. It is a diseased infection. 📖
## Leviticus 13:28
If the spot remains unchanged without spreading and has faded, it is swelling from the burn. Pronounce him clean because it is only a burn scar. 📖
## Leviticus 13:29
If a man or woman has a head or chin infection, 📖
## Leviticus 13:30
the priest must examine it. If it appears deeper than the skin with thin yellow hair, pronounce the person unclean. It is a scaly outbreak, an infectious head or chin disease. 📖
## Leviticus 13:31
If the scaly infection appears no deeper than the skin and has no black hair, isolate the person seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:32
On day seven, examine whether it has not spread, has no yellow hair, and appears no deeper than the skin. 📖
## Leviticus 13:33
If so, the person must shave except on the scaly area. Isolate him seven more days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:34
Reexamine it on day seven. If it has not spread and appears no deeper than the skin, pronounce him clean. He must wash his clothes and be clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:35
If the scaly outbreak spreads after cleansing, 📖
## Leviticus 13:36
the priest must examine him. If it spread, no yellow hair check is needed. He is unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:37
If it remains unchanged and black hair grows in it, it has healed. He is clean, and the priest must pronounce him clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:38
If a man or woman has white skin spots, 📖
## Leviticus 13:39
the priest must examine them. Dull white spots are a harmless rash. The person is clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:40
A man who loses his hair and becomes bald remains clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:41
A man whose hairline recedes, leaving his forehead bald, remains clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:42
A reddish-white sore on a bald head or forehead is an infectious disease breaking out there. 📖
## Leviticus 13:43
The priest must examine him. If the swelling on his bald head or forehead is reddish-white like skin disease, 📖
## Leviticus 13:44
he is diseased and unclean. The priest must pronounce him unclean because of his head infection. 📖
## Leviticus 13:45
A diseased person must wear torn clothes, let his hair hang loose, cover his mouth, and cry, ‘Unclean, unclean!’ 📖
## Leviticus 13:46
He remains unclean while infected and must live alone outside the camp. 📖
## Leviticus 13:47
These rules apply to mildew in a wool or linen garment. 📖
## Leviticus 13:48
They also apply to linen or wool weaves or knits and leather articles. 📖
## Leviticus 13:49
Green or red marks in fabric, leather, weave, or knit indicate mildew contamination. Show the article to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 13:50
The priest must examine it and isolate the article seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:51
Reexamine it on day seven. If mildew spread in fabric, weave, knit, or leather, regardless of its use, it is harmful mildew. The article is unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:52
Burn the contaminated wool, linen, weave, knit, or leather article. It must be burned because the mildew is harmful. 📖
## Leviticus 13:53
If the priest finds no spreading mildew in the fabric, weave, knit, or leather article, 📖
## Leviticus 13:54
he must order it washed and isolated seven more days. 📖
## Leviticus 13:55
Examine it after washing. If its appearance is unchanged, it is unclean even without spreading. Burn it whether the rot is on the front or back. 📖
## Leviticus 13:56
If the mildew faded after washing, cut the contaminated section from the fabric, leather, weave, or knit. 📖
## Leviticus 13:57
If it returns in fabric, weave, knit, or leather, it is spreading. Burn the article. 📖
## Leviticus 13:58
If mildew disappears from fabric, weave, knit, or leather after washing, wash it again. It will be clean. 📖
## Leviticus 13:59
This law concerns mildew in wool or linen fabric, weave, knit, or leather articles, for pronouncing them clean or unclean.” 📖
