## Leviticus 9:1
On day eight, Moses summoned Aaron, his sons, and Israel’s elders. 📖
## Leviticus 9:2
He told Aaron, “Present a young bull as your sin offering and a ram as your burnt offering before the LORD. Both must be without defect. 📖
## Leviticus 9:3
Tell Israel to bring a male goat as a sin offering. Bring a calf and lamb, both one year old and without defect, as a burnt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 9:4
Bring an ox and ram as a peace offering before the LORD and a grain offering mixed with oil. The LORD will appear to you today.” 📖
## Leviticus 9:5
They brought what Moses commanded before the Tent of Meeting. The whole congregation stood before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 9:6
Moses said, “Do what the LORD commanded so His glory may appear to you.” 📖
## Leviticus 9:7
He told Aaron, “Offer your sin and burnt offerings to make atonement for yourself and the people. Offer the people’s sacrifice to make atonement for them, as the LORD commanded.” 📖
## Leviticus 9:8
Aaron approached the altar and slaughtered his sin offering calf. 📖
## Leviticus 9:9
His sons brought the blood. He dipped his finger in it, applied blood to the altar horns, and poured the rest at its base. 📖
## Leviticus 9:10
He burned its fat, kidneys, and liver’s lobe on the altar, as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖
## Leviticus 9:11
He burned the flesh and hide outside the camp. 📖
## Leviticus 9:12
He slaughtered the burnt offering. His sons brought blood, and he splashed it on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 9:13
They brought the pieces, including the head, and he burned them on the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 9:14
He washed the entrails and legs and burned them atop the altar’s burnt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 9:15
He presented the people’s offering. He slaughtered their sin offering male goat and offered it for sin like the first offering. 📖
## Leviticus 9:16
He presented and offered the burnt offering according to the ordinance. 📖
## Leviticus 9:17
He presented the grain offering and burned a handful on the altar in addition to the morning burnt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 9:18
He slaughtered the ox and ram as the people’s peace offering. His sons brought blood, and he splashed it on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 9:19
They brought their fat portions: the fat tail, fat covering the entrails, kidneys, and liver’s lobe. 📖
## Leviticus 9:20
They placed these on the breasts. Aaron burned the fat on the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 9:21
He waved the breasts and right thigh before the LORD as a wave offering, as Moses commanded. 📖
## Leviticus 9:22
Aaron raised his hands toward the people and blessed them. After making the sin, burnt, and peace offerings, he stepped down. 📖
## Leviticus 9:23
Moses and Aaron entered the Tent of Meeting. They came out and blessed the people. The LORD’s glory appeared to everyone. ✨
## Leviticus 9:24
Fire from the LORD’s presence consumed the altar’s burnt offering and fat portions. Everyone saw this, shouted for joy, and fell facedown. 🙏
