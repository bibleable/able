## Leviticus 3:1
“For a peace offering from the herd, present a male or female without defect before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:2
Lay your hand on its head and slaughter it at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. Aaron’s sons, the priests, must splash its blood on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 3:3
Bring the fat covering the entrails and all the fat on them as a food offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:4
Include both kidneys and their fat near the loins. Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys. 📖
## Leviticus 3:5
Aaron’s sons must burn these on the altar, atop the burnt offering on the burning wood. It is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:6
For a peace offering from the flock to the LORD, present a male or female without defect. 📖
## Leviticus 3:7
If your offering is a lamb, present it before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:8
Lay your hand on its head and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting. Aaron’s sons must splash its blood on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 3:9
Bring its fat as a food offering to the LORD. Include the entire fat tail, cut close to the backbone, the fat covering the entrails, and all their fat. 📖
## Leviticus 3:10
Include both kidneys and their fat near the loins. Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys. 📖
## Leviticus 3:11
The priest must burn these on the altar as food, a food offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:12
If your offering is a goat, present it before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:13
Lay your hand on its head and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting. Aaron’s sons must splash its blood on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 3:14
Bring the fat covering the entrails and all the fat on them as a food offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:15
Include both kidneys and their fat near the loins. Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys. 📖
## Leviticus 3:16
The priest must burn the food on the altar as a food offering with a pleasing aroma. All fat belongs to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 3:17
This is a permanent statute for every generation, wherever you live. Eat no fat or blood.” 📖
