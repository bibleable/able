## Leviticus 5:1
“A witness must testify when he hears a public charge about something he saw or learned. If he fails, he sins and bears his iniquity. 📖
## Leviticus 5:2
Anyone touching anything unclean becomes unclean and guilty, even if unaware. This includes carcasses of unclean wild animals, livestock, or crawling creatures. 📖
## Leviticus 5:3
Anyone touching human uncleanness becomes guilty when he realizes it, even if previously unaware. This includes anything by which people become unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 5:4
If someone thoughtlessly swears to do good or evil, he becomes guilty when he realizes it, even if previously unaware. 📖
## Leviticus 5:5
Anyone guilty in these ways must confess his sin. 📖
## Leviticus 5:6
Bring a female lamb or goat from the flock as your guilt offering to the LORD, a sin offering for your sin. The priest will make atonement for you. 📖
## Leviticus 5:7
If you cannot afford a lamb, you may bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the LORD as restitution for your sin. One is a sin offering, the other a burnt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 5:8
Bring them to the priest. He must present the sin offering first, twisting its head at the front of the neck without severing it. 📖
## Leviticus 5:9
Sprinkle some blood on the altar’s side. Drain the rest at its base. It is a sin offering. 📖
## Leviticus 5:10
Prepare the second bird as a burnt offering according to the ordinance. The priest will make atonement for your sin, and you will be forgiven. 📖
## Leviticus 5:11
If you cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, you may offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour. Add neither olive oil nor frankincense because it is a sin offering. 📖
## Leviticus 5:12
Bring it to the priest. He must burn a handful as its memorial portion atop the LORD’s food offerings on the altar. It is a sin offering. 📖
## Leviticus 5:13
The priest will make atonement for any of these sins, and you will be forgiven. The remainder belongs to him, like the grain offering.” 📖

## Leviticus 5:15
“Anyone unfaithfully sinning unintentionally against the LORD’s holy things must bring a guilt offering. Bring a ram without defect from the flock, properly valued in silver shekels by the sanctuary shekel. 📖
## Leviticus 5:16
Repay the holy thing’s loss, adding a fifth of its value, and give it to the priest. He will make atonement with the guilt offering ram, and you will be forgiven. 📖
## Leviticus 5:17
Anyone violating a command of the LORD is guilty and must bear his punishment, even if unaware. 📖
## Leviticus 5:18
Bring the priest a properly valued ram without defect from the flock as a guilt offering. He will make atonement for your unintentional wrong, and you will be forgiven. 📖
## Leviticus 5:19
It is a guilt offering. The person was certainly guilty before the LORD.” 📖
