## Leviticus 19:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 19:2
“Tell all Israel’s congregation: Be holy because I, the LORD your God, am holy. 📖
## Leviticus 19:3
Respect your mother and father and keep My Sabbaths. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 19:4
Do not turn to idols or make cast metal gods. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 19:5
Present a peace offering to the LORD so you may be accepted. 📖
## Leviticus 19:6
Eat it that day or the next. Burn anything remaining on day three. 📖
## Leviticus 19:7
If eaten on day three, it is tainted and will not be accepted. 📖
## Leviticus 19:8
Its eater bears his iniquity because he profaned what is holy to the LORD. He must be cut off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 19:9
Do not harvest to your field’s very edges or collect the harvest’s gleanings. 📖
## Leviticus 19:10
Do not strip your vineyard bare or collect fallen grapes. Leave them for the poor and foreigner. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 19:11
Do not steal, lie, or deceive one another. 📖
## Leviticus 19:12
Do not swear falsely by My name and profane your God’s name. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:13
Do not defraud or rob your neighbor. Do not withhold a hired worker’s wages until morning. 📖
## Leviticus 19:14
Do not curse the deaf or put a stumbling block before the blind. Fear your God. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:15
Do not distort justice, favor the poor, or favor the rich. Judge your neighbor fairly. 📖
## Leviticus 19:16
Do not spread slander among your people or endanger your neighbor’s life. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:17
Do not hate your brother in your heart. Rebuke your neighbor directly so you do not incur guilt because of him. 📖
## Leviticus 19:18
Do not take revenge or hold grudges against your people. Love your neighbor as yourself. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:19
Keep My statutes. Do not breed different kinds of livestock together. Do not sow two kinds of seed in your field or wear clothing of two kinds of material. 📖
## Leviticus 19:20
If a man has sexual relations with a slave girl promised to another man, but not redeemed or freed, punishment is required. They must not be executed because she was not free. 📖
## Leviticus 19:21
He must bring a ram as his guilt offering to the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 19:22
The priest will make atonement before the LORD with the guilt offering ram. The man’s sin will be forgiven. 📖
## Leviticus 19:23
When you enter the land and plant food trees, regard their fruit as forbidden for three years. Do not eat it. 📖
## Leviticus 19:24
In year four, consecrate all fruit as a praise offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:25
In year five, you may eat the fruit, and your harvest will increase. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 19:26
Do not eat anything containing blood. Do not practice divination or sorcery. 📖
## Leviticus 19:27
Do not cut hair from your head’s sides or clip your beard’s edges. 📖
## Leviticus 19:28
Do not cut your bodies for the dead or put tattoo marks on yourselves. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:29
Do not defile your daughter by making her a prostitute. Otherwise the land will be prostituted and filled with depravity. 📖
## Leviticus 19:30
Keep My Sabbaths and revere My sanctuary. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:31
Do not consult mediums or spiritists and become defiled by them. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 19:32
Rise before the elderly, honor the aged, and fear your God. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 19:33
Do not oppress a foreigner living in your land. 📖
## Leviticus 19:34
Treat him like a native and love him as yourself because you were foreigners in Egypt. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 19:35
Do not use dishonest measures of length, weight, or volume. 📖
## Leviticus 19:36
Use honest scales, weights, ephahs, and hins. I am the LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt. 📖
## Leviticus 19:37
Obey all My statutes and ordinances. I am the LORD.” 📖
