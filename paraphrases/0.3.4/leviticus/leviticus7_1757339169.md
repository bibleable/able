## Leviticus 7:1
“This is the guilt offering law. It is most holy. 📖
## Leviticus 7:2
Slaughter it where burnt offerings are slaughtered. The priest must splash its blood on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 7:3
Offer all its fat, including the fat tail and fat covering the entrails. 📖
## Leviticus 7:4
Include both kidneys and their fat near the loins. Remove the liver’s lobe with the kidneys. 📖
## Leviticus 7:5
The priest must burn these on the altar as a food offering to the LORD. It is a guilt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 7:6
Every male priest may eat it in a holy place. It is most holy. 📖
## Leviticus 7:7
The same law applies to guilt and sin offerings. The offering belongs to the priest making atonement with it. 📖
## Leviticus 7:8
A priest offering someone’s burnt offering receives its hide. 📖
## Leviticus 7:9
Every grain offering baked in an oven or cooked in a pan or on a griddle belongs to its offering priest. 📖
## Leviticus 7:10
Every grain offering, dry or mixed with oil, belongs equally to all Aaron’s sons. 📖
## Leviticus 7:11
This is the law of peace offerings presented to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 7:12
For thanksgiving, add unleavened cakes mixed with olive oil and unleavened wafers coated with oil. Also add well-kneaded cakes of fine flour mixed with oil. 📖
## Leviticus 7:13
Present cakes of leavened bread with your thanksgiving peace offering. 📖
## Leviticus 7:14
Give one portion of each offering as a contribution to the LORD. It belongs to the priest sprinkling the peace offering’s blood. 📖
## Leviticus 7:15
Eat thanksgiving peace offering meat on the day it is offered. Leave none until morning. 📖
## Leviticus 7:16
Eat a vow or freewill sacrifice on the day it is offered. You may eat the remainder the next day. 📖
## Leviticus 7:17
Burn any meat remaining on day three. 📖
## Leviticus 7:18
If peace offering meat is eaten on day three, it is not accepted or credited to its presenter. It is an abomination, and its eater bears his iniquity. 📖
## Leviticus 7:19
Burn meat that touches anything unclean. Do not eat it. Any ceremonially clean person may eat other meat. 📖
## Leviticus 7:20
An unclean person eating the LORD’s peace offering meat must be cut off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 7:21
Suppose someone touches human uncleanness, an unclean animal, or another unclean, detestable thing. If he then eats the LORD’s peace offering meat, he must be cut off from his people.” 📖
## Leviticus 7:22
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 7:23
“Tell Israel, ‘Eat no ox, sheep, or goat fat. 📖
## Leviticus 7:24
You may use fat from an animal found dead or mauled by wild beasts for other purposes. Never eat it. 📖
## Leviticus 7:25
Anyone eating fat from an animal eligible as a food offering to the LORD must be cut off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 7:26
Eat no bird’s or animal’s blood wherever you live. 📖
## Leviticus 7:27
Anyone eating blood must be cut off from his people.’” 📖
## Leviticus 7:28
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 7:29
“Tell Israel, ‘Anyone presenting a peace offering to the LORD must bring it as his sacrifice to Him. 📖
## Leviticus 7:30
Bring the LORD’s food offerings with your own hands. Bring the fat and breast, waving the breast before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖
## Leviticus 7:31
The priest must burn the fat on the altar. The breast belongs to Aaron and his sons. 📖
## Leviticus 7:32
Give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your peace offering. 📖
## Leviticus 7:33
Aaron’s son who presents its blood and fat receives the right thigh. 📖
## Leviticus 7:34
I took Israel’s peace offerings’ wave offering breast and contribution thigh. I gave them to Aaron the priest and his sons as their permanent portion from Israel.’” 📖
## Leviticus 7:35
This is Aaron’s and his sons’ portion of the LORD’s food offerings from the day they were presented to serve as priests. 📖
## Leviticus 7:36
The LORD commanded Israel to give this on their anointing day. It is their permanent portion for every generation. 📖
## Leviticus 7:37
These are the laws of burnt, grain, sin, guilt, ordination, and peace offerings. 📖
## Leviticus 7:38
The LORD gave them to Moses on Mount Sinai when He commanded Israel’s offerings in the Wilderness of Sinai. 📖
