## Leviticus 16:1
The LORD spoke to Moses after two of Aaron’s sons died when approaching His presence. 📖
## Leviticus 16:2
“Tell your brother Aaron not to enter the Most Holy Place behind the veil freely, before the ark’s mercy seat. Otherwise he will die because I appear in the cloud above the mercy seat. 📖
## Leviticus 16:3
Enter the Holy Place with a young bull as a sin offering and a ram as a burnt offering. 📖
## Leviticus 16:4
Wear the sacred linen tunic and linen undergarments. Tie the linen sash and put on the linen turban. Bathe with water before wearing these holy garments. 📖
## Leviticus 16:5
Take two male goats as a sin offering and one ram as a burnt offering from Israel’s congregation. 📖
## Leviticus 16:6
Aaron must present his sin offering bull and make atonement for himself and his household. 📖
## Leviticus 16:7
Present the two goats before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 16:8
Aaron must cast lots for the two goats, one for the LORD and one for the scapegoat. 📖
## Leviticus 16:9
Present the goat chosen for the LORD and sacrifice it as a sin offering. 📖
## Leviticus 16:10
Present the scapegoat alive before the LORD to make atonement by sending it into the wilderness. 📖
## Leviticus 16:11
Present and slaughter your sin offering bull to make atonement for yourself and your household. 📖
## Leviticus 16:12
Take a censer full of burning coals from the altar before the LORD and two handfuls of finely ground fragrant incense. Bring them behind the veil. 📖
## Leviticus 16:13
Put incense on the fire before the LORD. Its cloud will cover the mercy seat above the Testimony so you do not die. 📖
## Leviticus 16:14
Sprinkle bull’s blood with your finger on the mercy seat’s east side. Sprinkle blood seven times before the mercy seat. 📖
## Leviticus 16:15
Slaughter the people’s sin offering goat and bring its blood behind the veil. As with the bull’s blood, sprinkle it against and before the mercy seat. 📖
## Leviticus 16:16
Make atonement for the Most Holy Place because of Israel’s impurities, rebellion, and all their sins. Do the same for the Tent of Meeting among their impurities. 📖
## Leviticus 16:17
No one may be in the Tent of Meeting while Aaron enters the Most Holy Place for atonement until he leaves. He leaves after making atonement for himself, his household, and all Israel’s assembly. 📖
## Leviticus 16:18
Go out to the altar before the LORD and make atonement for it. Put bull’s and goat’s blood on all its horns. 📖
## Leviticus 16:19
Sprinkle blood with your finger seven times to cleanse and consecrate it from Israel’s uncleanness. 📖
## Leviticus 16:20
After purifying the Most Holy Place, Tent of Meeting, and altar, bring the live goat. 📖
## Leviticus 16:21
Lay both hands on its head and confess all Israel’s iniquities, rebellion, and sins. Put them on its head and send it into the wilderness with an appointed man. 📖
## Leviticus 16:22
The goat will carry all their iniquities to a solitary place. The man must release it in the wilderness. 📖
## Leviticus 16:23
Enter the Tent of Meeting, remove the linen garments worn inside the Most Holy Place, and leave them there. 📖
## Leviticus 16:24
Bathe with water in a holy place and put on your own clothes. Offer your and the people’s burnt offerings to make atonement for yourself and them. 📖
## Leviticus 16:25
Burn the sin offering’s fat on the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 16:26
The man releasing the scapegoat must wash his clothes and bathe with water before reentering the camp. 📖
## Leviticus 16:27
Take the sin offering bull and goat outside the camp. Their blood entered the Most Holy Place for atonement. Burn their hides, flesh, and dung. 📖
## Leviticus 16:28
The one who burns them must wash his clothes and bathe with water before reentering the camp. 📖
## Leviticus 16:29
This is a permanent statute. On day ten of month seven, humble yourselves and do no work. This applies to natives and resident foreigners. 📖
## Leviticus 16:30
This is because atonement will be made on this day to cleanse you. You will be clean from all your sins before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 16:31
It is a Sabbath of complete rest for humbling yourselves, a permanent statute. 📖
## Leviticus 16:32
The priest anointed and ordained as his father’s successor as high priest must make atonement. He must wear the sacred linen garments. 📖
## Leviticus 16:33
Make atonement for the Most Holy Place, Tent of Meeting, altar, priests, and all the assembly’s people. 📖
## Leviticus 16:34
This permanent statute requires atonement once a year for all Israel’s sins.” Everything was done as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖
