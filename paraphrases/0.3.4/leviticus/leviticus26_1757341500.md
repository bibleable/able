## Leviticus 26:1
“Do not make idols, carved images, or sacred pillars. Do not place sculpted stones in your land to bow down to them. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 26:2
Keep My Sabbaths and revere My sanctuary. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 26:3
If you follow My statutes and carefully obey My commands, 📖
## Leviticus 26:4
I will send seasonal rain. The land will produce crops, and field trees will bear fruit. 📖
## Leviticus 26:5
Threshing will last until grape harvest, and grape harvest until sowing. You will eat your fill and live securely in your land. 📖
## Leviticus 26:6
I will give the land peace, and you will rest without fear. I will remove dangerous animals, and no sword will cross your land. 📖
## Leviticus 26:7
You will pursue enemies, who will fall by the sword before you. 📖
## Leviticus 26:8
Five of you will pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you will pursue ten thousand. Your enemies will fall by the sword before you. 📖
## Leviticus 26:9
I will turn toward you, make you fruitful, multiply you, and establish My covenant with you. 📖
## Leviticus 26:10
You will still eat old grain when you must clear it out to make room for the new. 📖
## Leviticus 26:11
I will dwell among you. My soul will not despise you. 📖
## Leviticus 26:12
I will walk among you and be your God. You will be My people. 📖
## Leviticus 26:13
I am the LORD your God, who freed you from Egyptian slavery. I broke your yoke’s bars and enabled you to walk upright. 📖
## Leviticus 26:14
But if you disobey Me and fail to carry out all these commands, 📖
## Leviticus 26:15
rejecting My statutes, despising My ordinances, and breaking My covenant by neglecting My commands, 📖
## Leviticus 26:16
I will bring sudden terror, wasting disease, and fever that destroy sight and drain life. Your sowing will be wasted because enemies will eat your crops. 📖
## Leviticus 26:17
I will set My face against you, and enemies will defeat you. Those hating you will rule you. You will flee when no one pursues. 📖
## Leviticus 26:18
If you still disobey, I will punish your sins sevenfold. 📖
## Leviticus 26:19
I will break your stubborn pride, making your sky like iron and land like bronze. 📖
## Leviticus 26:20
Your strength will be wasted because your land and trees will bear no produce. 📖
## Leviticus 26:21
If you remain hostile and disobey Me, I will multiply your plagues seven times according to your sins. 📖
## Leviticus 26:22
Wild animals I send will rob you of children, destroy livestock, and reduce your numbers until your roads are deserted. 📖
## Leviticus 26:23
If you still reject My discipline and remain hostile, 📖
## Leviticus 26:24
I will act with hostility and strike you sevenfold for your sins. 📖
## Leviticus 26:25
I will bring a sword to avenge the covenant. Even if you retreat into cities, I will send plague and hand you to enemies. 📖
## Leviticus 26:26
I will cut off your bread supply. Ten women will bake bread in one oven and distribute it by weight. You will eat but remain hungry. 📖
## Leviticus 26:27
If you still disobey and remain hostile, 📖
## Leviticus 26:28
I Myself will act in fury and punish your sins sevenfold. 📖
## Leviticus 26:29
You will eat your own sons’ and daughters’ flesh. 📖
## Leviticus 26:30
I will destroy high places and incense altars and heap your corpses on your idols’ lifeless remains. My soul will despise you. 📖
## Leviticus 26:31
I will reduce your cities to rubble, devastate your sanctuaries, and refuse to smell your sacrifices’ pleasing aroma. 📖
## Leviticus 26:32
I will devastate the land, appalling the enemies who settle there. 📖
## Leviticus 26:33
I will scatter you among nations and draw a sword after you. Your land will be desolate, and your cities ruined. 📖
## Leviticus 26:34
While you are in enemy lands, your desolate land will rest and enjoy its Sabbaths. 📖
## Leviticus 26:35
It will rest throughout its desolation, receiving the Sabbath rest denied it while you lived there. 📖
## Leviticus 26:36
I will fill survivors’ hearts with fear in enemy lands. A windblown leaf will send them fleeing as from a sword. They will fall when no one pursues. 📖
## Leviticus 26:37
They will stumble over each other as before a sword though no one pursues. You will not withstand enemies. 📖
## Leviticus 26:38
You will perish among nations. Your enemies’ land will consume you. 📖
## Leviticus 26:39
Survivors in enemy lands will waste away in their iniquity and decay in their fathers’ sins. 📖
## Leviticus 26:40
But if they confess their and their fathers’ iniquity, unfaithfulness, and hostility against Me, 📖
## Leviticus 26:41
(I acted with hostility and brought them into enemy lands.) And if their uncircumcised hearts are humbled and they make amends for their iniquity, 📖
## Leviticus 26:42
then I will remember My covenants with Jacob, Isaac, and Abraham and remember the land. 📖
## Leviticus 26:43
Their abandoned land will enjoy Sabbaths while desolate without them. They will pay for their iniquity because they rejected My ordinances and abhorred My statutes. 📖
## Leviticus 26:44
Even in enemy lands, I will not reject or despise them so as to destroy them and break My covenant. I am the LORD their God. 📖
## Leviticus 26:45
For their sake I will remember My covenant with their fathers. I brought them from Egypt before the nations to be their God. I am the LORD.” 📖
## Leviticus 26:46
The LORD established these statutes, ordinances, and laws between Himself and Israel through Moses on Mount Sinai. 📖
