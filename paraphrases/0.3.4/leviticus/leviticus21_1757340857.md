## Leviticus 21:1
The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron’s sons, the priests: Do not defile yourself for a dead person among your people, 📖
## Leviticus 21:2
except immediate family: your mother, father, son, daughter, or brother, 📖
## Leviticus 21:3
or your unmarried sister near to you, who has no husband. 📖
## Leviticus 21:4
Do not defile and profane yourself for relatives by marriage. 📖
## Leviticus 21:5
Priests must not make bald spots, shave their beard edges, or cut their bodies. 📖
## Leviticus 21:6
They must be holy and not profane their God’s name. They must be holy because they present the LORD’s food offerings, their God’s food. 📖
## Leviticus 21:7
A priest must not marry a woman defiled by prostitution or divorced by her husband. He is holy to his God. 📖
## Leviticus 21:8
Regard him as holy because he presents your God’s food. He must be holy to you because I, the LORD who sets you apart, am holy. 📖
## Leviticus 21:9
A priest’s daughter who prostitutes herself defiles herself and profanes her father. She must be burned in fire. 📖
## Leviticus 21:10
The highest priest, anointed with oil on his head and ordained to wear priestly garments, must not loosen his hair or tear his garments. 📖
## Leviticus 21:11
He must approach no dead body or defile himself even for his father or mother. 📖
## Leviticus 21:12
He must not leave or desecrate his God’s sanctuary because his God’s consecrating anointing oil is on him. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 21:13
He must marry a virgin. 📖
## Leviticus 21:14
He must not marry a widow, divorced woman, or woman defiled by prostitution. Marry a virgin from his own people. 📖
## Leviticus 21:15
This is so he does not defile his offspring among his people. I am the LORD who sanctifies him.” 📖
## Leviticus 21:16
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 21:17
“Tell Aaron: In every generation, no descendant with a physical defect may approach to offer his God’s food. 📖
## Leviticus 21:18
No man with a defect may approach, whether blind, lame, disfigured, or deformed, 📖
## Leviticus 21:19
with a broken foot or hand, 📖
## Leviticus 21:20
a hunchback, dwarf, or someone with an eye defect, festering rash, scabs, or crushed testicle. 📖
## Leviticus 21:21
No descendant of Aaron the priest with a defect may present the LORD’s food offerings. Because of his defect, he must not approach to offer his God’s food. 📖
## Leviticus 21:22
He may eat his God’s most holy food and holy food. 📖
## Leviticus 21:23
Because of his defect, he must not approach the veil or altar, so he does not desecrate My sanctuaries. I am the LORD who sanctifies them.” 📖
## Leviticus 21:24
Moses told Aaron, his sons, and all Israel. 📖
