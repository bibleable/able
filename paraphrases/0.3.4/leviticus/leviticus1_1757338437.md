## Leviticus 1:1
The LORD called Moses and spoke from the Tent of Meeting. 📖
## Leviticus 1:2
“Tell the Israelites: When you bring an offering to the LORD, you may bring an animal from the herd or flock. 📖
## Leviticus 1:3
For a burnt offering from the herd, present a male without defect. Bring it to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance for acceptance before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 1:4
Lay your hand on its head so it can be accepted for you to make atonement for you. 📖
## Leviticus 1:5
Slaughter the young bull before the LORD. Aaron’s sons, the priests, must present its blood and splash it on every side of the altar at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 1:6
Skin the burnt offering and cut it into pieces. 📖
## Leviticus 1:7
Aaron the priest’s sons must put fire on the altar and arrange wood on it. 📖
## Leviticus 1:8
They must arrange the pieces, head, and fat on the burning wood. 📖
## Leviticus 1:9
Wash the entrails and legs with water. The priest must burn everything on the altar. It is a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 1:10
For a burnt offering of sheep or goats from the flock, present a male without defect. 📖
## Leviticus 1:11
Slaughter it north of the altar before the LORD. Aaron’s sons, the priests, must splash its blood on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 1:12
Cut the animal into pieces. The priest must arrange them, including the head and fat, on the altar’s burning wood. 📖
## Leviticus 1:13
Wash the entrails and legs with water. The priest must present everything and burn it on the altar. It is a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 1:14
For a burnt offering of birds to the LORD, present a turtledove or young pigeon. 📖
## Leviticus 1:15
The priest must bring it to the altar, twist off its head, and burn it there. Drain its blood on the altar’s side. 📖
## Leviticus 1:16
Remove its crop and contents. Throw them east of the altar in the ash area. 📖
## Leviticus 1:17
Tear it open by the wings without dividing it completely. The priest must burn it on the altar’s burning wood. It is a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
