## Leviticus 6:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 6:2
“Someone sins unfaithfully against the LORD by deceiving a neighbor about entrusted deposits, security, or stolen property, or by extorting him. 📖
## Leviticus 6:3
He also sins by finding lost property, lying about it, and swearing falsely, or committing any similar sin. 📖
## Leviticus 6:4
When guilty, return the stolen or extorted goods, entrusted deposit, or lost property. 📖
## Leviticus 6:5
Return anything about which you swore falsely. Repay its full value plus a fifth to the owner on the day you acknowledge guilt. 📖
## Leviticus 6:6
Bring the priest your guilt offering to the LORD, a properly valued ram without defect from the flock. 📖
## Leviticus 6:7
He will make atonement before the LORD for you. You will be forgiven for whatever made you guilty.” 📖
## Leviticus 6:8
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 6:9
“Command Aaron and his sons about burnt offerings. Keep the burnt offering on the altar hearth all night until morning. Keep the altar fire burning. 📖
## Leviticus 6:10
The priest must wear his linen robe and linen undergarments. Remove the burnt offering’s ashes from the altar and place them beside it. 📖
## Leviticus 6:11
Change into other clothes. Carry the ashes outside the camp to a ceremonially clean place. 📖
## Leviticus 6:12
Never let the altar fire go out. Each morning add wood, arrange the burnt offering, and burn the peace offerings’ fat portions on it. 📖
## Leviticus 6:13
Keep the altar fire burning continually. Never extinguish it. 📖
## Leviticus 6:14
This is the grain offering law. Aaron’s sons must present it before the LORD in front of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 6:15
Take a handful of fine flour and olive oil with all the frankincense. Burn this memorial portion on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 6:16
Aaron and his sons must eat the remainder without leaven. Eat it in a holy place, the Tent of Meeting’s courtyard. 📖
## Leviticus 6:17
Do not bake it with leaven. I assigned them this portion of My food offerings. It is most holy, like sin and guilt offerings. 📖
## Leviticus 6:18
Any male descendant of Aaron may eat it. This is their permanent portion from the LORD’s food offerings for every generation. Anything touching these offerings becomes holy.” 📖
## Leviticus 6:19
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 6:20
“On Aaron’s anointing day, he and his sons must offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour to the LORD. Present this regular grain offering half in the morning and half in the evening. 📖
## Leviticus 6:21
Prepare it with oil on a griddle, well-kneaded and broken into pieces. Present this grain offering as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 6:22
Aaron’s son anointed as his successor must prepare it. This permanent portion for the LORD must be burned completely. 📖
## Leviticus 6:23
Burn every priest’s grain offering completely. Do not eat it.” 📖
## Leviticus 6:24
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 6:25
“Tell Aaron and his sons the sin offering law. Slaughter it before the LORD where burnt offerings are slaughtered. It is most holy. 📖
## Leviticus 6:26
The offering priest must eat it in a holy place, the Tent of Meeting’s courtyard. 📖
## Leviticus 6:27
Anything touching its flesh becomes holy. Wash any garment splashed with its blood in a holy place. 📖
## Leviticus 6:28
Break a clay pot used to boil it. Scrub and rinse a bronze pot with water if it was used instead. 📖
## Leviticus 6:29
Any male priest may eat it. It is most holy. 📖
## Leviticus 6:30
Do not eat a sin offering whose blood entered the Tent of Meeting to make atonement in the Holy Place. Burn it. 📖
