## Leviticus 23:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 23:2
“Tell Israel: Proclaim My appointed feasts, the LORD’s feasts, as sacred assemblies. 📖
## Leviticus 23:3
You may work six days. Day seven is a Sabbath of complete rest and sacred assembly. Do no work. Wherever you live, it is the LORD’s Sabbath. 📖
## Leviticus 23:4
Proclaim the LORD’s appointed feasts as sacred assemblies at their appointed times. 📖
## Leviticus 23:5
The LORD’s Passover begins at twilight on day fourteen of month one. 📖
## Leviticus 23:6
The Feast of Unleavened Bread to the LORD begins on day fifteen of that month. Eat unleavened bread seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 23:7
Hold a sacred assembly on day one. Do no regular work. 📖
## Leviticus 23:8
Present food offerings to the LORD seven days. Hold a sacred assembly on day seven and do no regular work.” 📖
## Leviticus 23:9
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 23:10
“Tell Israel: When harvesting the land I give you after entering it, bring the priest a sheaf of your harvest’s firstfruits. 📖
## Leviticus 23:11
He must wave it before the LORD for acceptance on your behalf, on the day after the Sabbath. 📖
## Leviticus 23:12
That day, offer a year-old lamb without defect as a burnt offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 23:13
Add two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as its grain offering. It is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. Its drink offering is a quarter hin of wine. 📖
## Leviticus 23:14
Eat no bread, roasted grain, or new grain until the day you bring this offering to your God. This is a permanent statute for every generation wherever you live. 📖
## Leviticus 23:15
Count seven full weeks from the day after the Sabbath when you brought the wave offering sheaf. 📖
## Leviticus 23:16
Count fifty days to the day after the seventh Sabbath. Then present a new grain offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 23:17
Bring two loaves from your homes as a wave offering of firstfruits to the LORD. Bake each with leaven from two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour. 📖
## Leviticus 23:18
With the bread, present seven year-old male lambs without defect, one young bull, and two rams. Offer them as burnt offerings with grain and drink offerings, food offerings with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 23:19
Prepare one male goat as a sin offering and two year-old male lambs as a peace offering. 📖
## Leviticus 23:20
The priest must wave the lambs with the firstfruits bread before the LORD. The bread and two lambs are holy to the LORD for the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 23:21
Proclaim a sacred assembly that day and do no regular work. This is a permanent statute for every generation wherever you live. 📖
## Leviticus 23:22
Do not harvest to your field’s edges or collect its gleanings. Leave them for the poor and resident foreigner. I am the LORD your God.” 📖
## Leviticus 23:23
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 23:24
“Tell Israel: On day one of month seven, rest and hold a sacred assembly announced by trumpet blasts. 📖
## Leviticus 23:25
Do no regular work. Present a food offering to the LORD.” 📖
## Leviticus 23:26
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 23:27
“Day ten of month seven is the Day of Atonement. Hold a sacred assembly, humble yourselves, and present a food offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 23:28
Do no work that day because atonement is made for you before the LORD your God on the Day of Atonement. 📖
## Leviticus 23:29
Anyone failing to humble himself that day must be cut off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 23:30
I will destroy anyone working that day from among his people. 📖
## Leviticus 23:31
Do no work at all. This is a permanent statute for every generation wherever you live. 📖
## Leviticus 23:32
It is a Sabbath of complete rest. Humble yourselves. Keep it from the evening of day nine until the next evening.” 📖
## Leviticus 23:33
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 23:34
“Tell Israel: The LORD’s Feast of Tabernacles begins on day fifteen of month seven and lasts seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 23:35
Hold a sacred assembly on day one and do no regular work. 📖
## Leviticus 23:36
Present food offerings to the LORD seven days. On day eight hold a sacred assembly and present a food offering to the LORD. It is a solemn assembly. Do no regular work. 📖
## Leviticus 23:37
Proclaim these appointed feasts as sacred assemblies for the LORD’s food offerings. Present burnt offerings, grain offerings, sacrifices, and drink offerings on their designated days. 📖
## Leviticus 23:38
These are additional to the LORD’s Sabbath offerings, your gifts, and all your vow and freewill offerings to Him. 📖
## Leviticus 23:39
After gathering the land’s produce, celebrate to the LORD for seven days from day fifteen of month seven. Rest completely on days one and eight. 📖
## Leviticus 23:40
On day one gather majestic trees’ fruit, palm branches, leafy boughs, and brook willow boughs. Rejoice before the LORD your God seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 23:41
Celebrate this feast to the LORD seven days every year in month seven. This is a permanent statute for every generation. 📖
## Leviticus 23:42
Live in booths seven days. All native Israelites must do this. 📖
## Leviticus 23:43
This is so your descendants may know I made Israel live in booths when I brought them from Egypt. I am the LORD your God.” 📖
## Leviticus 23:44
Moses announced the LORD’s appointed feasts to Israel. 📖
