## Leviticus 12:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 12:2
“Tell Israel: A woman giving birth to a son after pregnancy is unclean seven days, as during menstruation. 📖
## Leviticus 12:3
Circumcise the boy’s foreskin on day eight. 📖
## Leviticus 12:4
She must continue purification from bleeding for thirty-three days. Until this is complete, she must touch nothing sacred or enter the sanctuary. 📖
## Leviticus 12:5
After bearing a daughter, she is unclean two weeks, as during menstruation. Continue purification from bleeding for sixty-six days. 📖
## Leviticus 12:6
After purification for a son or daughter, bring offerings to the priest at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. Bring a year-old lamb as a burnt offering and a young pigeon or turtledove as a sin offering. 📖
## Leviticus 12:7
The priest must present them before the LORD and make atonement for her. She will be ceremonially cleansed from her blood flow. This is the law for mothers of sons or daughters. 📖
## Leviticus 12:8
If she cannot afford a lamb, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one as a burnt offering and one as a sin offering. The priest will make atonement for her, and she will be clean.” 📖
