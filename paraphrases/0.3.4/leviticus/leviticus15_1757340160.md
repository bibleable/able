## Leviticus 15:1
The LORD told Moses and Aaron, 📖
## Leviticus 15:2
“Tell Israel: A man’s bodily discharge is unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 15:3
It causes uncleanness whether his body lets it flow or blocks it. 📖
## Leviticus 15:4
Any bed he lies on or furniture he sits on becomes unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 15:5
Anyone touching his bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:6
Anyone sitting where he sat must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:7
Anyone touching his body must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:8
If he spits on a clean person, that person must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:9
Any saddle he rides becomes unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 15:10
Anyone touching something that was under him is unclean until evening. Anyone carrying it must wash his clothes, bathe with water, and remain unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:11
If he touches someone without first rinsing his hands with water, that person must wash his clothes and bathe with water. The person remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:12
Break any clay pot he touches. Rinse any wooden utensil he touches with water. 📖
## Leviticus 15:13
After his discharge clears, count seven days for cleansing. Wash his clothes and bathe with fresh water. He will be clean. 📖
## Leviticus 15:14
On day eight, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons before the LORD at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. Give them to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 15:15
The priest must offer one for sin and the other as a burnt offering. He will make atonement before the LORD for the man’s discharge. 📖
## Leviticus 15:16
After an emission of semen, a man must bathe his whole body with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:17
Wash clothing or leather with semen on it using water. It remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:18
If a man lies with a woman and emits semen, both must bathe with water. They remain unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:19
A woman’s menstrual blood discharge makes her unclean seven days. Anyone touching her is unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:20
Anything she lies or sits on during menstruation becomes unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 15:21
Anyone touching her bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:22
Anyone touching furniture she sat on must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:23
Anyone touching her bed or furniture she sat on is unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:24
If a man lies with her and her menstrual flow touches him, he is unclean seven days. Any bed he lies on becomes unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 15:25
If her blood flow lasts many days outside her period or continues beyond it, she remains unclean throughout, as during menstruation. 📖
## Leviticus 15:26
Her bed and any furniture she sits on during this discharge are unclean, as during menstruation. 📖
## Leviticus 15:27
Anyone touching these becomes unclean and must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 15:28
After her discharge clears, count seven days. Then she is ceremonially clean. 📖
## Leviticus 15:29
On day eight, bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖
## Leviticus 15:30
The priest must offer one for sin and the other as a burnt offering. He will make atonement before the LORD for her unclean discharge. 📖
## Leviticus 15:31
Keep Israel separate from uncleanness so they do not die by defiling My tabernacle among them. 📖
## Leviticus 15:32
This law concerns a man with a discharge or an emission of semen making him unclean, 📖
## Leviticus 15:33
a menstruating woman, any male or female with a discharge, and a man lying with an unclean woman.” 📖
