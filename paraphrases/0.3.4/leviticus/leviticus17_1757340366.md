## Leviticus 17:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 17:2
“Give Aaron, his sons, and all Israel these commands from the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 17:3
If an Israelite slaughters an ox, lamb, or goat inside or outside the camp, 📖
## Leviticus 17:4
without bringing it to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance as an offering before the LORD’s tabernacle, he incurs bloodguilt. He shed blood and must be cut off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 17:5
Therefore Israel must bring their open-field sacrifices to the priest at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. Offer them there as peace offerings to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 17:6
The priest must splash blood on the LORD’s altar there and burn the fat as a pleasing aroma to Him. 📖
## Leviticus 17:7
Stop sacrificing to goat demons, to which you prostituted yourselves. This is a permanent statute for every generation. 📖
## Leviticus 17:8
Tell them: If an Israelite or resident foreigner offers a burnt offering or sacrifice, 📖
## Leviticus 17:9
without bringing it to the Tent of Meeting’s entrance to sacrifice to the LORD, he must be cut off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 17:10
If an Israelite or resident foreigner eats blood, I will set My face against him and cut him off from his people. 📖
## Leviticus 17:11
The flesh’s life is in its blood. I gave it to you on the altar to make atonement for your souls. Blood makes atonement for the soul. 📖
## Leviticus 17:12
Therefore neither Israelites nor resident foreigners may eat blood. 📖
## Leviticus 17:13
An Israelite or resident foreigner hunting an edible wild animal or bird must drain its blood and cover it with dirt. 📖
## Leviticus 17:14
The life of all flesh is its blood. Therefore I told Israel not to eat any living thing’s blood. Anyone eating it must be cut off. 📖
## Leviticus 17:15
Anyone, native or foreigner, eating an animal found dead or mauled by wild beasts must wash his clothes and bathe with water. He remains unclean until evening, then becomes clean. 📖
## Leviticus 17:16
If he fails to wash his clothes and bathe, he bears his iniquity.” 📖
