## Leviticus 10:1
Aaron’s sons Nadab and Abihu put fire and incense in their censers. They offered unauthorized fire before the LORD, contrary to His command. 📖
## Leviticus 10:2
Fire came from His presence and consumed them. They died before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 10:3
Moses told Aaron, “The LORD said, ‘I will show My holiness to those approaching Me and My glory before all the people.’” Aaron remained silent. 📖
## Leviticus 10:4
Moses summoned Mishael and Elzaphan, sons of Aaron’s uncle Uzziel. “Carry your cousins’ bodies from before the sanctuary to outside the camp.” 📖
## Leviticus 10:5
They carried the bodies, still in their tunics, outside as Moses directed. 📖
## Leviticus 10:6
Moses told Aaron, Eleazar, and Ithamar, “Do not dishevel your hair or tear your garments. Otherwise you will die, and the LORD will be angry with the whole congregation. Your brothers, all Israel, may mourn the LORD’s fire. 📖
## Leviticus 10:7
Do not leave the Tent of Meeting’s entrance, or you will die, because the LORD’s anointing oil is on you.” They obeyed. 📖
## Leviticus 10:8
The LORD told Aaron, 📖
## Leviticus 10:9
“You and your sons must not drink wine or strong drink when entering the Tent of Meeting, or you will die. This is a permanent statute for every generation. 📖
## Leviticus 10:10
Distinguish the holy from the common and the clean from the unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 10:11
This is so you may teach Israel all the statutes the LORD gave through Moses.” 📖
## Leviticus 10:12
Moses told Aaron and his surviving sons Eleazar and Ithamar, “Eat the remaining grain offering from the LORD’s food offerings without leaven beside the altar. It is most holy. 📖
## Leviticus 10:13
Eat it in a holy place because it is your and your sons’ share of the LORD’s food offerings. This is what I was commanded. 📖
## Leviticus 10:14
You, your sons, and daughters may eat the wave offering breast and contribution thigh in a ceremonially clean place. These are your and your children’s portions from Israel’s peace offerings. 📖
## Leviticus 10:15
Bring the contribution thigh and wave offering breast with the food offerings’ fat portions. Wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. They permanently belong to you and your children, as He commanded.” 📖
## Leviticus 10:16
Moses searched for the sin offering goat and found it burned. Angry, he asked Aaron’s remaining sons Eleazar and Ithamar, 📖
## Leviticus 10:17
“Why did you not eat it in the holy place? It is most holy, given to remove the congregation’s guilt by making atonement before the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 10:18
Its blood did not enter the holy place. You should have eaten it in the sanctuary area as commanded.” 📖
## Leviticus 10:19
Aaron answered, “Today they offered their sin and burnt offerings before the LORD. Since these things happened to me, would eating the sin offering today have pleased the LORD?” ❓
## Leviticus 10:20
Moses heard and was satisfied. 📖
