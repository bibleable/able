## Leviticus 2:1
“A grain offering to the LORD must be fine flour. Pour olive oil on it and add frankincense. 📖
## Leviticus 2:2
Bring it to Aaron’s sons, the priests. A priest must burn a handful of flour and oil with all the frankincense on the altar. This memorial portion is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 2:3
The remainder belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the LORD’s food offerings. 📖
## Leviticus 2:4
For oven-baked grain offerings, use fine flour. Make unleavened cakes mixed with oil or unleavened wafers coated with oil. 📖
## Leviticus 2:5
A griddle-cooked grain offering must be unleavened bread of fine flour mixed with oil. 📖
## Leviticus 2:6
Crumble it and pour oil over it. It is a grain offering. 📖
## Leviticus 2:7
A pan-cooked grain offering must be fine flour with oil. 📖
## Leviticus 2:8
Present grain offerings prepared these ways to the priest for the LORD. He must take them to the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 2:9
He must remove and burn the memorial portion on the altar. It is a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 2:10
The remainder belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the LORD’s food offerings. 📖
## Leviticus 2:11
Use no leaven in grain offerings to the LORD. Burn neither leaven nor honey as a food offering to Him. 📖
## Leviticus 2:12
You may bring leaven and honey to the LORD as firstfruits. They must not be burned on the altar as a pleasing aroma. 📖
## Leviticus 2:13
Season every grain offering with salt. Never omit the salt of your God’s covenant. Add salt to every offering. 📖
## Leviticus 2:14
For a firstfruits grain offering to the LORD, offer crushed heads of new grain roasted in fire. 📖
## Leviticus 2:15
Add oil and frankincense. It is a grain offering. 📖
## Leviticus 2:16
The priest must burn the memorial portion of grain and oil with all its frankincense as a food offering to the LORD. 📖
