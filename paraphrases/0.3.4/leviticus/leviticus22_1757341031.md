## Leviticus 22:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 22:2
“Tell Aaron and his sons to respect Israel’s sacred offerings to Me so they do not profane My holy name. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 22:3
In every generation, an unclean descendant approaching Israel’s sacred offerings to the LORD must be cut off from My presence. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 22:4
Aaron’s descendant with skin disease or a discharge must not eat sacred offerings until clean. This also concerns anyone touching something defiled by a corpse or a man with an emission of semen, 📖
## Leviticus 22:5
or touching a crawling creature or person causing uncleanness, of whatever kind. 📖
## Leviticus 22:6
He remains unclean until evening. He must bathe with water before eating sacred offerings. 📖
## Leviticus 22:7
At sunset he becomes clean and may eat sacred offerings because they are his food. 📖
## Leviticus 22:8
He must not eat an animal found dead or torn by wild beasts and become unclean. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 22:9
Priests must keep My charge so they do not incur guilt and die by profaning it. I am the LORD who sanctifies them. 📖
## Leviticus 22:10
No one outside a priest’s family may eat the sacred offering. Neither his guest nor hired worker may eat it. 📖
## Leviticus 22:11
A slave bought with the priest’s own money or born in his household may eat his food. 📖
## Leviticus 22:12
A priest’s daughter married to someone other than a priest must not eat sacred contributions. 📖
## Leviticus 22:13
If a priest’s daughter has no children, becomes widowed or divorced, and returns to her father’s house, she may eat his food. This is as in her youth. No outsider may eat it. 📖
## Leviticus 22:14
If someone eats a sacred offering by mistake, add a fifth of its value and give the sacred offering to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 22:15
Priests must not profane Israel’s sacred offerings to the LORD, 📖
## Leviticus 22:16
by allowing people to eat them and incur punishment for guilt. I am the LORD who sanctifies them.” 📖
## Leviticus 22:17
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 22:18
“Tell Aaron, his sons, and all Israel: If an Israelite or resident foreigner brings a burnt offering for a vow or as a freewill offering, 📖
## Leviticus 22:19
present a male without defect from cattle, sheep, or goats for acceptance on your behalf. 📖
## Leviticus 22:20
Do not present an animal with a defect. It will not be accepted for you. 📖
## Leviticus 22:21
A peace offering from herd or flock, for a vow or as a freewill offering, must be without blemish or defect to be accepted. 📖
## Leviticus 22:22
Do not offer blind, injured, or maimed animals, or those with running sores, festering rashes, or scabs. Put none on the altar as food offerings to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 22:23
An ox or sheep with a deformed or stunted limb may be a freewill offering, but not a vow offering. 📖
## Leviticus 22:24
Do not present an animal with bruised, crushed, torn, or cut testicles to the LORD. Do not sacrifice them in your land. 📖
## Leviticus 22:25
Neither you nor a foreigner may present such animals as your God’s food. Their deformities and flaws make them unacceptable for you.” 📖
## Leviticus 22:26
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 22:27
“A newborn ox, sheep, or goat must stay with its mother seven days. From day eight it is acceptable as a food offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 22:28
Do not slaughter an ox or sheep and its young on the same day. 📖
## Leviticus 22:29
Present a thank offering to the LORD so it may be accepted for you. 📖
## Leviticus 22:30
Eat it that day. Leave none until morning. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 22:31
Obey My commands. I am the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 22:32
Do not profane My holy name. Israel must acknowledge Me as holy. I am the LORD who sanctifies you, 📖
## Leviticus 22:33
who brought you from Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD.” 📖
