## Leviticus 11:1
The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. 📖
## Leviticus 11:2
“Tell Israel which land animals they may eat. 📖
## Leviticus 11:3
You may eat animals with completely divided, split hooves that also chew the cud. 📖
## Leviticus 11:4
Do not eat animals meeting only one requirement. The camel chews the cud but lacks a divided hoof. It is unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:5
The rock badger chews the cud but lacks a divided hoof. It is unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:6
The rabbit chews the cud but lacks a divided hoof. It is unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:7
The pig has completely divided, split hooves but does not chew the cud. It is unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:8
Do not eat their meat or touch their carcasses. They are unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:9
You may eat water creatures with fins and scales, whether from seas or streams. 📖
## Leviticus 11:10
All sea or stream creatures without fins and scales are detestable to you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:11
They are an abomination to you. Do not eat their meat, and detest their carcasses. 📖
## Leviticus 11:12
All water creatures without fins and scales are detestable to you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:13
Detest these birds and do not eat them: the eagle, bearded vulture, and black vulture, 📖
## Leviticus 11:14
the kite and every kind of falcon, 📖
## Leviticus 11:15
every kind of raven, 📖
## Leviticus 11:16
the ostrich, screech owl, gull, and every kind of hawk, 📖
## Leviticus 11:17
the little owl, cormorant, and great owl, 📖
## Leviticus 11:18
the white owl, desert owl, and osprey, 📖
## Leviticus 11:19
the stork, every kind of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat. 📖
## Leviticus 11:20
All flying insects walking on all fours are detestable to you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:21
However, you may eat those with jointed legs above their feet for hopping on the ground. 📖
## Leviticus 11:22
You may eat every kind of locust, katydid, cricket, or grasshopper. 📖
## Leviticus 11:23
All other flying insects with four legs are detestable to you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:24
These creatures make you unclean. Anyone touching their carcasses is unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 11:25
Anyone carrying a carcass must wash his clothes and remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 11:26
Animals whose hooves are not completely divided or that do not chew the cud are unclean for you. Anyone touching them becomes unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 11:27
Four-footed animals walking on paws are unclean for you. Anyone touching their carcasses is unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 11:28
Anyone carrying their carcasses must wash his clothes and remains unclean until evening. They are unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:29
These ground creatures are unclean for you: the mole, mouse, and every kind of great lizard, 📖
## Leviticus 11:30
the gecko, monitor lizard, common lizard, skink, and chameleon. 📖
## Leviticus 11:31
These crawling creatures are unclean for you. Anyone touching them dead is unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 11:32
Anything a dead creature falls on becomes unclean. Rinse any wooden article, clothing, leather, sackcloth, or work implement with water. It remains unclean until evening, then becomes clean. 📖
## Leviticus 11:33
If one falls into a clay pot, everything inside becomes unclean. Break the pot. 📖
## Leviticus 11:34
Food touched by water from that pot becomes unclean. Any drink in such a container is unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 11:35
Anything their carcasses fall on becomes unclean. Smash an affected oven or cooking pot. It is unclean and remains unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:36
A spring or water cistern remains clean. Anyone touching a carcass in it becomes unclean. 📖
## Leviticus 11:37
Seed for sowing remains clean if a carcass falls on it. 📖
## Leviticus 11:38
But if water was put on the seed before the carcass fell, it is unclean for you. 📖
## Leviticus 11:39
If an animal you may eat dies, anyone touching its carcass is unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 11:40
Anyone eating from it must wash his clothes and remains unclean until evening. Anyone carrying it must also wash his clothes and remains unclean until evening. 📖
## Leviticus 11:41
Every creature moving along the ground is detestable. Do not eat it. 📖
## Leviticus 11:42
Eat no ground creature crawling on its belly or walking on four or more feet. They are detestable. 📖
## Leviticus 11:43
Do not defile yourselves with crawling creatures. Do not become unclean or defiled by them. 📖
## Leviticus 11:44
I am the LORD your God. Consecrate yourselves and be holy because I am holy. Do not defile yourselves with ground creatures. 📖
## Leviticus 11:45
I brought you from Egypt to be your God. Be holy because I, the LORD, am holy. 📖
## Leviticus 11:46
This law concerns animals, birds, water creatures, and creatures crawling on the ground. 📖
## Leviticus 11:47
Distinguish the clean from the unclean and animals you may eat from those you may not.” 📖
