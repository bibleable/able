## Leviticus 25:1
The LORD told Moses on Mount Sinai, 📖
## Leviticus 25:2
“Tell Israel: When you enter the land I give you, the land must keep a Sabbath to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 25:3
For six years, you may sow fields, prune vineyards, and gather crops. 📖
## Leviticus 25:4
Year seven is a Sabbath of complete rest for the land to the LORD. Do not sow fields or prune vineyards. 📖
## Leviticus 25:5
Do not reap aftergrowth or harvest untended vines. Give the land a year of complete rest. 📖
## Leviticus 25:6
Its Sabbath year yield is food for you, your male and female servants, hired workers, and foreigners staying with you. 📖
## Leviticus 25:7
It also feeds your livestock and wild animals. All its growth may be food. 📖
## Leviticus 25:8
Count seven Sabbaths of years, seven times seven, totaling forty-nine years. 📖
## Leviticus 25:9
On day ten of month seven, the Day of Atonement, sound the horn throughout your land. 📖
## Leviticus 25:10
Consecrate year fifty and proclaim liberty for all inhabitants. It is your Jubilee. Everyone must return to his property and clan. 📖
## Leviticus 25:11
Year fifty is a Jubilee. Do not sow, reap aftergrowth, or harvest untended vines. 📖
## Leviticus 25:12
It is a Jubilee, holy to you. Eat only crops taken directly from the field. 📖
## Leviticus 25:13
Everyone must return to his property in the Year of Jubilee. 📖
## Leviticus 25:14
Do not exploit each other when buying or selling. 📖
## Leviticus 25:15
Buy from your neighbor according to the years since the last Jubilee. Sell according to the harvest years remaining. 📖
## Leviticus 25:16
Increase the price proportionally for more years and decrease it for fewer. He is selling a number of harvests. 📖
## Leviticus 25:17
Do not exploit each other. Fear your God. I am the LORD your God. 📖
## Leviticus 25:18
Carefully obey My statutes and judgments so you may live securely in the land. 📖
## Leviticus 25:19
The land will yield fruit. You will eat your fill and live safely. 📖
## Leviticus 25:20
You may ask, ‘What will we eat in year seven without sowing or gathering crops?’ ❓
## Leviticus 25:21
I will bless year six with enough produce for three years. 📖
## Leviticus 25:22
While sowing in year eight, you will eat the old harvest until year nine’s harvest arrives. 📖
## Leviticus 25:23
Never sell the land permanently. It is Mine, and you are foreigners and residents with Me. 📖
## Leviticus 25:24
Allow redemption of every piece of land you possess. 📖
## Leviticus 25:25
If your brother becomes poor and sells property, his nearest relative may redeem it. 📖
## Leviticus 25:26
If he has no redeemer but prospers enough to redeem his land, 📖
## Leviticus 25:27
calculate the years since sale, repay the buyer the balance, and return to the property. 📖
## Leviticus 25:28
If he cannot repay, the buyer keeps it until the Year of Jubilee. Then release it so he can return to his property. 📖
## Leviticus 25:29
A house sold in a walled city may be redeemed for one full year after sale. 📖
## Leviticus 25:30
After a full year without redemption, it permanently belongs to the buyer and his descendants. It is not released at Jubilee. 📖
## Leviticus 25:31
Unwalled village houses count as open fields. They may be redeemed and must be released at Jubilee. 📖
## Leviticus 25:32
Levites may always redeem houses in the cities they possess. 📖
## Leviticus 25:33
A Levite’s sold house may be redeemed and must be released at Jubilee. These city houses are the Levites’ possession among Israel. 📖
## Leviticus 25:34
Never sell their cities’ surrounding pastureland. It is their permanent possession. 📖
## Leviticus 25:35
Help a countryman unable to support himself as you would a foreigner or stranger, so he can live among you. 📖
## Leviticus 25:36
Take no interest or profit from him. Fear your God so your countryman may live among you. 📖
## Leviticus 25:37
Do not lend him silver at interest or sell him food for profit. 📖
## Leviticus 25:38
I am the LORD your God, who brought you from Egypt to give you Canaan and be your God. 📖
## Leviticus 25:39
If a poor countryman sells himself to you, do not force him into slave labor. 📖
## Leviticus 25:40
Treat him as a hired worker or temporary resident working until the Year of Jubilee. 📖
## Leviticus 25:41
Release him and his children then. He may return to his clan and his fathers’ property. 📖
## Leviticus 25:42
Israelites are My servants whom I brought from Egypt. Do not sell them as slaves. 📖
## Leviticus 25:43
Do not rule them harshly. Fear your God. 📖
## Leviticus 25:44
Your male and female servants must come from surrounding nations. You may purchase them there. 📖
## Leviticus 25:45
You may also purchase from foreigners living among you or their clans living among you who were born in your land. They may become your property. 📖
## Leviticus 25:46
Your sons may inherit them as property, and you may enslave them for life. Do not rule your fellow Israelites harshly. 📖
## Leviticus 25:47
If a resident foreigner prospers and your nearby countryman becomes poor and sells himself to him or his clan, 📖
## Leviticus 25:48
the countryman keeps redemption rights. One of his brothers may redeem him. 📖
## Leviticus 25:49
His uncle, cousin, or another close clan relative may redeem him. If he prospers, he may redeem himself. 📖
## Leviticus 25:50
He and the buyer must count from the sale year to Jubilee. Determine the sale price by years, based on a hired worker’s daily wages. 📖
## Leviticus 25:51
If many years remain, pay redemption proportionally to the purchase price. 📖
## Leviticus 25:52
If few remain until Jubilee, calculate and pay redemption according to the remaining years. 📖
## Leviticus 25:53
Treat him like someone hired year by year. His foreign owner must not rule him harshly in your sight. 📖
## Leviticus 25:54
Even without redemption, release him and his children in the Year of Jubilee. 📖
## Leviticus 25:55
Israelites are My servants whom I brought from Egypt. I am the LORD your God. 📖
