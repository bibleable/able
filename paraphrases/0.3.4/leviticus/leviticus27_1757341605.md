## Leviticus 27:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 27:2
“Tell Israel: These are the valuations for a special vow to the LORD involving persons. 📖
## Leviticus 27:3
Value a male aged twenty to sixty at fifty silver shekels by the sanctuary shekel. 📖
## Leviticus 27:4
Value a female in that age range at thirty shekels. 📖
## Leviticus 27:5
From age five to twenty, value males at twenty shekels and females at ten. 📖
## Leviticus 27:6
From one month to five years, value males at five silver shekels and females at three silver shekels. 📖
## Leviticus 27:7
At sixty or older, value males at fifteen shekels and females at ten. 📖
## Leviticus 27:8
If the vow maker cannot afford the valuation, present the person to the priest. He must set the value according to what the vow maker can afford. 📖
## Leviticus 27:9
An animal vowed to the LORD that is eligible as an offering becomes holy. 📖
## Leviticus 27:10
Do not exchange it, good for bad or bad for good. If you substitute another animal, both become holy. 📖
## Leviticus 27:11
If the vowed animal is unclean and cannot be offered to the LORD, present it to the priest. 📖
## Leviticus 27:12
His valuation sets its price, high or low. 📖
## Leviticus 27:13
An owner redeeming it must add a fifth to its value. 📖
## Leviticus 27:14
For a house consecrated to the LORD, the priest must assess its value as good or bad. His valuation determines the price. 📖
## Leviticus 27:15
Its consecrator may redeem it by paying the assessed value plus a fifth. It will belong to him. 📖
## Leviticus 27:16
For land from your own property consecrated to the LORD, value it by the seed needed. Value it at fifty silver shekels per homer of barley seed. 📖
## Leviticus 27:17
If consecrated during the Year of Jubilee, the full valuation stands. 📖
## Leviticus 27:18
If afterward, the priest must reduce the valuation proportionally to the years remaining until Jubilee. 📖
## Leviticus 27:19
Its consecrator may redeem it by paying the assessed value plus a fifth. It will belong to him. 📖
## Leviticus 27:20
If he does not redeem it or has sold it to someone else, it cannot be redeemed. 📖
## Leviticus 27:21
At Jubilee, it becomes holy like land devoted to the LORD. It belongs to the priests. 📖
## Leviticus 27:22
If someone consecrates a purchased field that is not his own inherited property to the LORD, 📖
## Leviticus 27:23
the priest must calculate its value until Jubilee. Pay that value that day as a sacred offering to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 27:24
At Jubilee, return the field to its original landowner from whom it was bought. 📖
## Leviticus 27:25
Use the sanctuary shekel for all valuations, twenty gerahs per shekel. 📖
## Leviticus 27:26
Do not consecrate firstborn livestock. Whether ox or sheep, it already belongs to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 27:27
If it is an unclean animal, you may redeem it for its assessed value plus a fifth. If not redeemed, sell it at the assessed value. 📖
## Leviticus 27:28
Anything devoted to the LORD from your possessions, whether a person, animal, or inherited land, cannot be sold or redeemed. It is most holy to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 27:29
No person set apart for destruction may be ransomed. He must be put to death. 📖
## Leviticus 27:30
Every land tithe, from seed or tree fruit, belongs to the LORD and is holy to Him. 📖
## Leviticus 27:31
To redeem part of a tithe, add a fifth to its value. 📖
## Leviticus 27:32
Every tenth herd or flock animal passing under the shepherd’s rod is holy to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 27:33
Do not inspect good or bad or substitute another. If you substitute, both animals become holy and cannot be redeemed.” 📖
## Leviticus 27:34
The LORD gave these commands to Moses for Israel on Mount Sinai. 📖
