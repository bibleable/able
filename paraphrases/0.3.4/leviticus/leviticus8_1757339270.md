## Leviticus 8:1
The LORD told Moses, 📖
## Leviticus 8:2
“Take Aaron and his sons, their garments, anointing oil, the sin offering bull, two rams, and the unleavened bread basket. 📖
## Leviticus 8:3
Gather the whole congregation at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance.” 📖
## Leviticus 8:4
Moses obeyed, and the assembly gathered there. 📖
## Leviticus 8:5
He said, “The LORD commanded this to be done.” 📖
## Leviticus 8:6
He presented Aaron and his sons and washed them with water. 📖
## Leviticus 8:7
He put Aaron’s tunic on, tied his sash, and dressed him in the robe and ephod. He tied and fastened the ephod’s woven band around him. 📖
## Leviticus 8:8
He put the breastpiece on him and placed the Urim and Thummim inside it. 📖
## Leviticus 8:9
He put the turban on Aaron and the gold plate, the holy diadem, on its front, as the LORD commanded. 📖
## Leviticus 8:10
Moses anointed the tabernacle and everything inside with anointing oil to consecrate them. 📖
## Leviticus 8:11
He sprinkled oil on the altar seven times. He anointed the altar, all its utensils, and the basin and stand to consecrate them. 📖
## Leviticus 8:12
He poured anointing oil on Aaron’s head to consecrate him. 📖
## Leviticus 8:13
He presented Aaron’s sons, dressed them in tunics, wrapped their sashes, and tied their headbands, as the LORD commanded. 📖
## Leviticus 8:14
He brought the sin offering bull. Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 📖
## Leviticus 8:15
Moses slaughtered it and applied blood with his finger to all four altar horns, purifying the altar. He poured the rest at its base and consecrated it for atonement. 📖
## Leviticus 8:16
He burned all the entrails’ fat, the liver’s lobe, and both kidneys with their fat on the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 8:17
He burned the bull’s hide, flesh, and dung outside the camp, as the LORD commanded. 📖
## Leviticus 8:18
He presented the burnt offering ram. Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 📖
## Leviticus 8:19
He slaughtered it and splashed blood on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 8:20
He cut it into pieces and burned the head, pieces, and fat. 📖
## Leviticus 8:21
He washed the entrails and legs with water and burned the whole ram on the altar, as the LORD commanded. It was a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 8:22
He presented the second ram for ordination. Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 📖
## Leviticus 8:23
Moses slaughtered it and put blood on Aaron’s right earlobe, right thumb, and right big toe. 📖
## Leviticus 8:24
He presented Aaron’s sons and put blood on their right earlobes, right thumbs, and right big toes. He splashed blood on every side of the altar. 📖
## Leviticus 8:25
He took the fat tail, all entrails’ fat, the liver’s lobe, both kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh. 📖
## Leviticus 8:26
From the unleavened bread basket before the LORD, he took one unleavened cake, one oil bread cake, and one wafer. He placed these on the fat and right thigh. 📖
## Leviticus 8:27
He put everything in Aaron’s and his sons’ hands and waved it before the LORD as a wave offering. 📖
## Leviticus 8:28
He took it from their hands and burned it with the burnt offering on the altar. It was an ordination offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 📖
## Leviticus 8:29
He waved the breast before the LORD as a wave offering, as commanded. It was Moses’ portion of the ordination ram. 📖
## Leviticus 8:30
Moses sprinkled anointing oil and altar blood on Aaron, his sons, and their garments. He consecrated them and their garments. 📖
## Leviticus 8:31
He told Aaron and his sons, “Boil the meat at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. Eat it there with the ordination basket’s bread, as I commanded for Aaron and his sons. 📖
## Leviticus 8:32
Burn the remaining meat and bread. 📖
## Leviticus 8:33
Do not go outside the Tent of Meeting’s entrance for seven days, until your ordination is complete. Ordaining you takes seven days. 📖
## Leviticus 8:34
The LORD commanded today’s actions to make atonement for you. 📖
## Leviticus 8:35
Remain at the entrance day and night for seven days. Keep the LORD’s charge so you do not die. This is what I was commanded.” 📖
## Leviticus 8:36
Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖
