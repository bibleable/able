## 1 Corinthians 12:1
Brothers, I want you informed about spiritual gifts. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 12:2
When you were pagans, you were influenced and led astray to idols unable to speak. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 12:3
No one speaking by God’s Spirit says, “Jesus be cursed.” No one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” except by the Holy Spirit. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 12:4
Gifts differ, but the Spirit is the same. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 12:5
Ministries differ, but the Lord is the same. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 12:6
Ways of working differ, but the same God works everything in everyone. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 12:7
Each receives a manifestation of the Spirit for everyone’s good. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 12:8
The Spirit gives one a message of wisdom and another a message of knowledge. The same Spirit gives both. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 12:9
The same Spirit gives another faith and another gifts of healing. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 12:10
Another receives miracles, another prophecy, another distinguishing between spirits, another various tongues, and another interpretation of tongues. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 12:11
The same Spirit works all these and gives them to each person as He decides. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 12:12
The body has many parts, but remains one body. It is the same with Christ. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 12:13
In one Spirit, we were all baptized into one body: Jews or Greeks, slaves or free. We were all given one Spirit to drink. 💧
## 1 Corinthians 12:14
The body has many parts, rather than one. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 12:15
If the foot says, “I am not a hand, so I do not belong,” it still belongs to the body. 🦶
## 1 Corinthians 12:16
If the ear says, “I am not an eye, so I do not belong,” it still belongs to the body. 👂
## 1 Corinthians 12:17
If the whole body were an eye, where would hearing be? If it were an ear, where would smell be? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 12:18
God arranged every body part according to His design. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 12:19
If all were one part, where would the body be? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 12:20
There are many parts, but one body. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 12:21
The eye cannot tell the hand, “I do not need you.” Neither can the head say this to the feet. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 12:22
Parts that seem weaker are essential. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 12:23
We give greater honor to parts we consider less honorable. We treat unpresentable parts with special modesty, 👥
## 1 Corinthians 12:24
which presentable parts do not need. God composed the body, giving greater honor to parts lacking it, 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 12:25
so the body would have no divisions and its parts would care for each other. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 12:26
If one part suffers, all suffer with it. If one receives honor, all rejoice with it. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 12:27
You are Christ’s body, and each is a part of it. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 12:28
In the church, God appointed first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then miracle workers. He also appointed those with gifts of healing, helping, administration, and various tongues. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 12:29
Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work miracles? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 12:30
Do all have healing gifts? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 12:31
Eagerly desire the greater gifts. I will show you the most excellent way. ❤️
