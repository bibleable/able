## 1 Corinthians 10:1
Brothers, remember that all our ancestors were under the cloud and crossed the sea. 🌊
## 1 Corinthians 10:2
All were baptized into Moses in the cloud and sea. 💧
## 1 Corinthians 10:3
All ate the same spiritual food. 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 10:4
All drank the same spiritual drink from the spiritual rock accompanying them. That rock was Christ. 🪨
## 1 Corinthians 10:5
God was displeased with most of them. They were struck down in the wilderness. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 10:6
These examples warn us against craving evil as they did. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 10:7
Avoid idolatry, unlike some of them. Scripture says, “The people sat to eat and drink, then rose to celebrate wildly.” ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 10:8
Avoid sexual immorality, which some practiced. Twenty-three thousand died in one day. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 10:9
Do not test Christ, as some did and were killed by snakes. 🐍
## 1 Corinthians 10:10
Do not complain, as some did and were killed by the destroying angel. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 10:11
These events were examples and were recorded to warn us. The fulfillment of the ages has come upon us. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 10:12
Anyone thinking he stands firmly should beware of falling. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 10:13
Your temptations are common to humanity. God is faithful and will not let you be tempted beyond your ability to bear it. With temptation, He provides escape so you can endure. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 10:14
Beloved, flee idolatry. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 10:15
You are reasonable people. Judge my words yourselves. 💭
## 1 Corinthians 10:16
Is the cup of blessing we bless not a sharing in Christ’s blood? Is the bread we break not a sharing in Christ’s body? 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 10:17
We are many, but form one body because there is one loaf. We all share that loaf. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 10:18
Consider Israel: do those eating sacrifices not share in the altar? 🏛️
## 1 Corinthians 10:19
Am I suggesting idol food or an idol amounts to anything? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 10:20
No. Pagans sacrifice to demons, rather than God. I do not want you sharing with demons. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 10:21
You cannot drink both the Lord’s cup and demons’ cup. You cannot share both the Lord’s table and demons’ table. 🍷
## 1 Corinthians 10:22
Are we provoking the Lord’s jealousy? Are we stronger than Him? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 10:23
“Everything is permitted,” but not everything benefits us. “Everything is permitted,” but not everything builds us up. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 10:24
Seek others’ good, rather than your own. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 10:25
Eat anything sold in the meat market without questions of conscience. 🍖
## 1 Corinthians 10:26
“The earth and everything in it belong to the Lord.” 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 10:27
If an unbeliever invites you and you want to go, eat whatever is served without questions of conscience. 🍽️
## 1 Corinthians 10:28
If someone says the food was offered to idols, do not eat it for that person’s sake and for conscience. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 10:29
I mean the other person’s conscience, rather than yours. Why should another’s conscience determine my freedom? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 10:30
If I eat thankfully, why am I condemned for what I thank God for? 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 10:31
Eating, drinking, or doing anything else, do everything for God’s glory. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 10:32
Do not make Jews, Greeks, or God’s church stumble. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 10:33
I too try to please everyone in everything. I seek the good of many rather than myself, so they may be saved. ❤️
