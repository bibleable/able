## 1 Corinthians 13:1
If I speak human and angelic tongues without love, I am only a ringing gong or clanging cymbal. 🎵
## 1 Corinthians 13:2
Suppose I have prophecy, understand all mysteries and knowledge, and possess absolute faith to move mountains. Without love, I am nothing. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 13:3
If I give the poor everything I own and rejoice in surrendering my body, I gain nothing without love. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 13:4
Love is patient and kind. It does not envy or boast and is not proud. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 13:5
It is not rude or selfish. It is not easily angered and keeps no record of wrongs. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 13:6
Love rejoices in truth, rather than taking pleasure in evil. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 13:7
It bears everything, believes everything, hopes everything, and endures everything. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 13:8
Love never fails. Prophecies will cease. Tongues will be restrained. Knowledge will be dismissed. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 13:9
We know and prophesy in part. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 13:10
When the perfect comes, the partial passes away. ✨
## 1 Corinthians 13:11
As a child, I talked, thought, and reasoned like a child. When I became a man, I set aside childish ways. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 13:12
Now we see a dim reflection in a mirror. Then we will see face to face. Now I know in part. Then I will know fully, as I am fully known. 👁️
## 1 Corinthians 13:13
These three remain: faith, hope, and love. Love is greatest. ❤️
