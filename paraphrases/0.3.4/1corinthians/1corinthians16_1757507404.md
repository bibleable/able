## 1 Corinthians 16:1
For the saints’ collection, follow my directions to Galatia’s churches. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 16:2
Each week’s first day, save a portion of your income. Then no collection will be needed when I arrive. 🪙
## 1 Corinthians 16:3
I will send letters with your recommended people to carry the gift to Jerusalem when I arrive. 📜
## 1 Corinthians 16:4
If I should go too, they can travel with me. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 16:5
I will pass through Macedonia and then visit you. 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 16:6
Perhaps I will stay awhile or even spend the winter, so you can help my journey wherever I go. 🏠
## 1 Corinthians 16:7
I want more than a passing visit. If the Lord permits, I hope to spend time with you. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 16:8
I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost, 📅
## 1 Corinthians 16:9
because a great door opened for effective work, despite many opponents. 🚪
## 1 Corinthians 16:10
If Timothy comes, make sure he has nothing to fear among you. He does the Lord’s work, as I do. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 16:11
No one should despise him. Send him in peace to return to me. I expect him along with the brothers. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 16:12
I strongly urged our brother Apollos to visit with the brothers. He did not want to go now, but will when he has opportunity. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 16:13
Stay alert. Stand firmly in faith. Be courageous and strong. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 16:14
Do everything in love. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 16:15
Stephanas and his household were Achaia’s first converts and devoted themselves to serving saints. You know this. Brothers, I urge you 📖
## 1 Corinthians 16:16
to submit to such people and every fellow worker and laborer. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 16:17
I rejoice that Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus arrived. They supplied what was lacking from you. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 16:18
They refreshed my spirit and yours. Appreciate such men. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 16:19
The churches in the province of Asia greet you. Aquila, Prisca, and the church meeting at their house greet you warmly in the Lord. 🏠
## 1 Corinthians 16:20
All the brothers here greet you. Greet each other with a holy kiss. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 16:21
I, Paul, write this greeting in my own hand. 📜
## 1 Corinthians 16:22
Let anyone not loving the Lord be cursed. Come, Lord! 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 16:23
May the Lord Jesus’ grace be with you. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 16:24
May my love be with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen. ❤️
