## 1 Corinthians 15:1
Brothers, remember the good news I preached. You received it and stand firmly in it. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 15:2
It saves you if you hold firmly to the word I preached. Otherwise, you believed in vain. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 15:3
I passed on what I received as most important: Christ died for our sins according to Scripture. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 15:4
He was buried and raised on the third day according to Scripture. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:5
He appeared to Cephas, then the Twelve. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 15:6
He then appeared to over five hundred brothers at once. Most still live, though some have fallen asleep. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 15:7
He appeared to James, then all the apostles. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 15:8
Finally, He appeared to me, as to one of untimely birth. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 15:9
I am the least apostle, unworthy of the name because I persecuted God’s church. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 15:10
God’s grace made me what I am, and His grace was not wasted. I worked harder than all of them. Yet it was God’s grace with me, rather than I. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 15:11
Whether they or I preached, this is our message and what you believed. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 15:12
If we preach Christ rose from the dead, how can some of you deny the dead’s resurrection? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 15:13
If the dead do not rise, Christ has not risen either. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 15:14
If Christ has not risen, our preaching and your faith are worthless. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 15:15
Then we falsely witness about God because we testified He raised Christ. If the dead are not raised, He did not raise Christ. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 15:16
If the dead do not rise, Christ has not risen. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 15:17
If Christ has not risen, your faith is useless, and you remain in your sins. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 15:18
Then those who fell asleep in Christ have also perished. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 15:19
If our hope in Christ concerns only this life, we deserve more pity than everyone else. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 15:20
Christ truly rose from the dead, the firstfruits of those who fell asleep. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:21
Death came through a man. The resurrection of the dead also comes through a man. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 15:22
As all die in Adam, so all will be made alive in Christ. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:23
Each has his turn: Christ the firstfruits, then those belonging to Him at His coming. 👑
## 1 Corinthians 15:24
The end will come when He hands the kingdom to God the Father. This follows His destruction of all dominion, authority, and power. 👑
## 1 Corinthians 15:25
He must reign until He puts all His enemies under His feet. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 15:26
Death is the last enemy to be destroyed. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:27
“God put everything under His feet.” This excludes the One who put everything under Him. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 15:28
When everything is subjected to the Son, He Himself will be made subject to the One who subjected everything to Him. This is so God may be all in all. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 15:29
If these things are false, what will people baptized for the dead do? If the dead never rise, why be baptized for them? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 15:30
Why do we risk danger every hour? ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 15:31
Brothers, I face death daily, as surely as I boast about you in Christ Jesus our Lord. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 15:32
If I fought wild beasts in Ephesus for human motives, what did I gain? If the dead do not rise, “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.” ❓
## 1 Corinthians 15:33
Do not be deceived. “Bad company corrupts good character.” ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 15:34
Become sober as you should and stop sinning. Some do not know God. I say this to shame you. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 15:35
Someone will ask, “How do the dead rise? What kind of body will they have?” ❓
## 1 Corinthians 15:36
Fool! What you sow comes to life only if it dies. 🌱
## 1 Corinthians 15:37
You sow a seed, perhaps wheat or something else, rather than the body that will be. 🌾
## 1 Corinthians 15:38
God gives it a body as He designed, each kind of seed its own body. 🌱
## 1 Corinthians 15:39
Human flesh differs from animal flesh, bird flesh, and fish flesh. Each kind is different. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 15:40
Heavenly and earthly bodies exist. Their degrees of splendor differ. ✨
## 1 Corinthians 15:41
The sun, moon, and stars differ in splendor. Even stars differ from each other. 🌌
## 1 Corinthians 15:42
Likewise, at the resurrection, what is sown perishable is raised imperishable. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:43
It is sown in dishonor and raised in glory. It is sown in weakness and raised in power. ✨
## 1 Corinthians 15:44
It is sown a natural body and raised a spiritual body. If a natural body exists, a spiritual body also exists. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 15:45
Scripture says, “The first man, Adam, became a living being.” The last Adam became a spirit giving life. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:46
The natural came first, followed by the spiritual. ⏳
## 1 Corinthians 15:47
The first man came from earth’s dust. The second came from heaven. 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 15:48
Earthly people are like the earthly man. Heavenly people are like the heavenly man. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 15:49
We bore the earthly man’s likeness. We will also bear the heavenly man’s likeness. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:50
Brothers, flesh and blood cannot inherit God’s kingdom. The perishable cannot inherit the imperishable. 👑
## 1 Corinthians 15:51
I tell you a mystery: we will not all sleep, but all will be changed, ✨
## 1 Corinthians 15:52
in an instant, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. It will sound, the dead will be raised imperishable, and we will be changed. 📯
## 1 Corinthians 15:53
The perishable must be clothed with the imperishable. The mortal must be clothed with immortality. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 15:54
When the perishable puts on the imperishable and the mortal puts on immortality, Scripture’s saying will be fulfilled. “Death has been swallowed up in victory.” 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 15:55
“Death, where is your victory? Death, where is your sting?” ❓
## 1 Corinthians 15:56
Death’s sting is sin. Sin’s power is the law. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 15:57
Thank God for giving us victory through our Lord Jesus Christ! 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 15:58
Beloved brothers, stand firm and immovable. Always excel in the Lord’s work. You know your work in Him is not wasted. 🛡️
