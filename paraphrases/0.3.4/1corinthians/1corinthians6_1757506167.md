## 1 Corinthians 6:1
If you have a complaint against another, how dare you take it before unrighteous judges rather than saints? ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 6:2
Do you not know saints will judge the world? If you will judge the world, can you not judge small cases? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 6:3
Do you not know we will judge angels? How much more this life’s matters! 👼
## 1 Corinthians 6:4
For everyday disputes, do you appoint judges with no standing in the church? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 6:5
I say this to shame you. Is no one among you wise enough to settle disputes between brothers? ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 6:6
Instead, one brother takes another to court before unbelievers! ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 6:7
Having lawsuits against each other already means complete defeat. Why not accept being wronged or cheated? 💔
## 1 Corinthians 6:8
Instead, you cheat and wrong even your own brothers! ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 6:9
Do you not know the wicked will not inherit God’s kingdom? Do not be deceived: sexually immoral people, idolaters, adulterers, men who submit to or perform homosexual acts, ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 6:10
thieves, greedy people, drunkards, verbal abusers, and swindlers will not inherit God’s kingdom. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 6:11
Some of you were like this. You were washed, made holy, and declared righteous in the Lord Jesus Christ’s name and by our God’s Spirit. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 6:12
“Everything is permitted for me,” but not everything benefits me. “Everything is permitted for me,” but I will let nothing master me. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 6:13
“Food is for the stomach, and the stomach for food,” but God will destroy both. The body is for the Lord, rather than sexual immorality. The Lord is for the body. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 6:14
God’s power raised the Lord from the dead. He will raise us too. 🌅
## 1 Corinthians 6:15
Do you not know your bodies are Christ’s members? Should I join Christ’s members to a prostitute? Never! ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 6:16
Anyone joining a prostitute becomes one body with her. Scripture says, “The two will become one flesh.” 📖
## 1 Corinthians 6:17
Anyone joining the Lord becomes one spirit with Him. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 6:18
Flee sexual immorality. Every other sin is outside the body. Sexual sin is against one’s own body. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 6:19
Do you not know your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you received from God? You do not belong to yourself. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 6:20
You were bought at a price. Glorify God with your body. 🙏
