## 1 Corinthians 5:1
I hear of sexual immorality among you that even pagans cannot tolerate. A man has his father’s wife. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 5:2
Yet you are proud! Should you not grieve and remove this man from your fellowship? 💔
## 1 Corinthians 5:3
I am absent in body, but present in spirit. I already judged the man who did this as though I were there. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 5:4
When you gather in our Lord Jesus’ name, I am with you in spirit, along with the Lord Jesus’ power. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 5:5
Hand this man to Satan for the flesh’s destruction, so his spirit may be saved on the Day of the Lord. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 5:6
Your boasting is wrong. Do you not know a little leaven spreads through all the dough? 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 5:7
Remove old leaven so you may become a new unleavened batch, as you really are. Christ, our Passover lamb, was sacrificed. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 5:8
Keep the feast with unleavened bread of sincerity and truth. Avoid the old bread leavened with malice and wickedness. 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 5:9
My letter told you not to associate with sexually immoral people. 📜
## 1 Corinthians 5:10
I did not mean this world’s sexually immoral people, greedy people, swindlers, or idolaters. Avoiding them would require leaving the world. 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 5:11
Do not associate with anyone claiming brotherhood who is sexually immoral, greedy, an idolater, a verbal abuser, a drunkard, or a swindler. Do not even eat with him. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 5:12
Why should I judge people outside the church? Should you not judge those inside? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 5:13
God will judge outsiders. “Remove the wicked man from among you.” ⚖️
