## 1 Corinthians 8:1
About food sacrificed to idols: we know we all have knowledge. Knowledge makes people proud, but love builds them up. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 8:2
Anyone thinking he knows something does not yet know as he should. 💭
## 1 Corinthians 8:3
God knows the person who loves Him. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 8:4
About eating idol sacrifices: an idol is nothing in the world. There is only one God. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 8:5
Even if there are so-called gods in heaven or on earth, as there are many so-called gods and lords, 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 8:6
we have one God, the Father, from whom everything came and for whom we exist. We have one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom everything came and we exist. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 8:7
Not everyone knows this. Some remain accustomed to idols and eat food as sacrificed to an idol. Their weak consciences become unclean. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 8:8
Food does not bring us closer to God. Eating makes us no better, and abstaining makes us no worse. 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 8:9
Do not let your freedom become a stumbling block to the weak. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 8:10
If someone with a weak conscience sees you with knowledge eating in an idol’s temple, will he not be encouraged to eat idol sacrifices? 🏛️
## 1 Corinthians 8:11
Your knowledge destroys this weak brother, for whom Christ died. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 8:12
Sinning against brothers this way and wounding their weak consciences is sin against Christ. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 8:13
If my food makes my brother stumble, I will never eat meat again, so I do not make him stumble. ❤️
