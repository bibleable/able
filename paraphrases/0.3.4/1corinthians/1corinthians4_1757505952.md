## 1 Corinthians 4:1
Regard us as Christ’s servants and stewards of God’s mysteries. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 4:2
Stewards must prove faithful. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 4:3
Your judgment or any human court’s judgment matters little to me. I do not even judge myself. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 4:4
My conscience is clear, but this does not prove me innocent. The Lord judges me. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 4:5
Judge nothing before the appointed time. Wait for the Lord’s coming. He will reveal what darkness hides and expose people’s heart motives. Each will then receive praise from God. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 4:6
Brothers, I applied these things to myself and Apollos for your benefit. Learn from us to stay within what is written. Then you will not favor one person proudly over another. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 4:7
Who makes you superior? What do you have that you did not receive? If you received it, why boast as though you did not? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 4:8
You already have everything you want and have become rich! You became kings without us! I wish you really were kings, so we could reign with you! 👑
## 1 Corinthians 4:9
It seems to me God displayed us apostles last in the procession, like prisoners appointed to die. We became a spectacle to the world, to angels and people. 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 4:10
We are fools for Christ, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You are honored, but we are dishonored. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 4:11
Even now, we are hungry, thirsty, poorly clothed, brutally treated, and homeless. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 4:12
We work hard with our hands. When insulted, we bless. When persecuted, we endure. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 4:13
When slandered, we answer gently. Even now, we have become the earth’s scum and the world’s garbage. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 4:14
I write to warn you as beloved children, rather than shame you. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 4:15
Even if you have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers. In Christ Jesus, I became your father through the good news. 👨‍👦
## 1 Corinthians 4:16
I therefore urge you to imitate me. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 4:17
I sent Timothy, my beloved, faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you how I live in Christ Jesus. This agrees with my teaching everywhere, in every church. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 4:18
Some became proud, as though I would not visit. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 4:19
If the Lord wills, I will visit soon. I will discover these proud people’s power, as well as their words. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 4:20
God’s kingdom concerns power, rather than talk. 👑
## 1 Corinthians 4:21
Should I come with a rod, or in love with a gentle spirit? Which do you prefer? ❓
