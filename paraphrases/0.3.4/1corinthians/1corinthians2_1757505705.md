## 1 Corinthians 2:1
Brothers, I came to announce the testimony about God. I did not use impressive speech or wisdom. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 2:2
Among you, I decided to know only Jesus Christ and Him crucified. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 2:3
I came weak, afraid, and trembling greatly. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 2:4
My message and preaching demonstrated the Spirit’s power, rather than using persuasive words of wisdom. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 2:5
This was so your faith would rest on God’s power rather than human wisdom. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 2:6
We speak wisdom among the mature. It differs from this age’s wisdom and that of its rulers, who are coming to nothing. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 2:7
We speak God’s mysterious, hidden wisdom, which He destined for our glory before time began. ✨
## 1 Corinthians 2:8
This age’s rulers did not understand it. Otherwise, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 2:9
Scripture says, “No eye saw, ear heard, or heart imagined what God prepared for those loving Him.” ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 2:10
God revealed it through the Spirit. The Spirit searches everything, even God’s deep things. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 2:11
Only a person’s own spirit within him knows his thoughts. Likewise, only God’s Spirit knows God’s thoughts. 💭
## 1 Corinthians 2:12
We received the Spirit from God rather than the world’s spirit. This was so we could understand what God freely gave us. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 2:13
We speak these things in words taught by the Spirit, rather than human wisdom. We express spiritual truths in spiritual words. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 2:14
The natural man rejects things from God’s Spirit as foolishness. He cannot understand them because they are spiritually discerned. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 2:15
The spiritual man judges everything, but is not subject to anyone’s judgment. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 2:16
“Who knew the Lord’s mind to instruct Him?” We have Christ’s mind. 🙏
