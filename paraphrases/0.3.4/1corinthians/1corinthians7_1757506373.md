## 1 Corinthians 7:1
About your written questions: it is good to abstain from sexual relations. 📜
## 1 Corinthians 7:2
Because sexual immorality is widespread, each man should have his own wife and each woman her own husband. 💍
## 1 Corinthians 7:3
Husbands should fulfill their marital duty to their wives. Wives should likewise fulfill theirs to their husbands. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 7:4
The husband has authority over his wife’s body, rather than she herself. Likewise, the wife has authority over her husband’s body, rather than he himself. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 7:5
Do not deprive each other, except with mutual consent for a time devoted to prayer. Then come together again, so Satan will not tempt you through your lack of self-control. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 7:6
I say this as a concession, rather than a command. 💬
## 1 Corinthians 7:7
I wish everyone were as I am. Each has his own gift from God, one this gift, another that gift. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 7:8
I tell unmarried people and widows: it is good to remain unmarried, as I am. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 7:9
If they cannot control themselves, they should marry. Marriage is better than burning with passion. 💍
## 1 Corinthians 7:10
This command for married people comes from the Lord, rather than me: a wife must not separate from her husband. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 7:11
If she does, she must remain unmarried or reconcile with him. A husband must not divorce his wife. 💍
## 1 Corinthians 7:12
I tell the rest this, rather than the Lord. If a brother’s unbelieving wife wants to live with him, he must not divorce her. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 7:13
If a woman’s unbelieving husband wants to live with her, she must not divorce him. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 7:14
The unbelieving husband is made holy through his believing wife, and the unbelieving wife through her believing husband. Otherwise, your children would be unclean. Now they are holy. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 7:15
If the unbeliever leaves, let him leave. In such cases, the believing brother or sister is not bound. God called you to peace. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 7:16
Wife, how do you know whether you will save your husband? Husband, how do you know whether you will save your wife? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 7:17
Each should live the life the Lord assigned and God called him to. I prescribe this in every church. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 7:18
Was someone circumcised when called? He should not become uncircumcised. Was he uncircumcised? He should not be circumcised. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 7:19
Circumcision and uncircumcision count for nothing. Keeping God’s commands matters. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 7:20
Remain in the situation you had when called. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 7:21
Were you a slave when called? Do not worry about it. If you can gain freedom, take the opportunity. ⛓️
## 1 Corinthians 7:22
A slave called by the Lord is the Lord’s freedman. A free person called by Him is Christ’s slave. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 7:23
You were bought at a price. Do not become people’s slaves. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 7:24
Brothers, each should remain in the situation he had when God called him. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 7:25
I have no command from the Lord about virgins. I give my judgment as someone made trustworthy through His mercy. 💬
## 1 Corinthians 7:26
Because of the present crisis, I think a man should remain as he is. ⏳
## 1 Corinthians 7:27
Are you committed to a wife? Do not seek release. Are you free of commitment? Do not seek a wife. 💍
## 1 Corinthians 7:28
If you marry, you do not sin. Neither does a virgin marrying. Married people will face troubles in this life, and I want to spare you. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 7:29
Brothers, time is short. From now on, those with wives should live as though they had none. ⏳
## 1 Corinthians 7:30
Those weeping should live as though not weeping, and those rejoicing as though not rejoicing. Buyers should live as though owning nothing. 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 7:31
Those using worldly things should live without depending on them. This world’s present form is passing away. ⏳
## 1 Corinthians 7:32
I want you free from concern. An unmarried man concerns himself with the Lord’s work and pleasing Him. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 7:33
A married man concerns himself with worldly affairs and pleasing his wife. 💍
## 1 Corinthians 7:34
His interests are divided. An unmarried woman or virgin concerns herself with the Lord’s work, to be holy in body and spirit. A married woman concerns herself with worldly affairs and pleasing her husband. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 7:35
I say this for your good, to promote proper behavior and undivided devotion to the Lord, rather than restrict you. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 7:36
If someone thinks he treats his betrothed improperly, and she is beyond youth and they ought to marry, he may do as he wishes. He does not sin. They should marry. 💍
## 1 Corinthians 7:37
A man firmly resolved in his heart, under no constraint, and controlling his will does well if he decides not to marry the virgin. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 7:38
Whoever marries the virgin does well. Whoever does not marry her does even better. 💍
## 1 Corinthians 7:39
A wife is bound to her husband while he lives. If he dies, she may marry anyone she wants who belongs to the Lord. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 7:40
In my judgment, she is happier remaining as she is. I think I too have God’s Spirit. 🕊️
