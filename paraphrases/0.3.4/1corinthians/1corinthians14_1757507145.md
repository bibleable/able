## 1 Corinthians 14:1
Earnestly seek love and eagerly desire spiritual gifts, especially prophecy. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 14:2
Anyone speaking in a tongue speaks to God, rather than people. No one understands him. He speaks mysteries in the Spirit. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 14:3
Anyone prophesying speaks to people to build them up, encourage, and comfort them. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 14:4
The tongue speaker builds himself up. The prophet builds the church up. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 14:5
I wish you all spoke in tongues, but prefer prophecy. The prophet is greater than the tongue speaker, unless he interprets so the church is built up. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 14:6
Brothers, if I come speaking in tongues, how do you benefit unless I bring revelation, knowledge, prophecy, or teaching? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 14:7
Even lifeless instruments like flutes and harps must make distinct notes. Otherwise, how can anyone recognize the tune? 🎵
## 1 Corinthians 14:8
If a trumpet gives a muffled call, who will prepare for battle? 📯
## 1 Corinthians 14:9
Likewise, unless your tongue speaks understandable words, how will anyone know your meaning? You will speak into the air. 💬
## 1 Corinthians 14:10
The world has many languages. Each has meaning. 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 14:11
If I do not understand someone’s language, we are foreigners to each other. 💬
## 1 Corinthians 14:12
Since you eagerly want spiritual gifts, excel in gifts that build the church up. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 14:13
Anyone speaking in a tongue should pray that he may interpret. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 14:14
If I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful. 💭
## 1 Corinthians 14:15
What should I do? I will pray and sing with my spirit and also with my mind. 🎵
## 1 Corinthians 14:16
If you bless in spirit, how can someone uninstructed say “Amen” to your thanks? He does not understand your words. ❓
## 1 Corinthians 14:17
You may thank God well, but the other person is not built up. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 14:18
I thank God I speak in tongues more than all of you. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 14:19
In church, I prefer five understandable words instructing others to ten thousand words in a tongue. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 14:20
Brothers, think maturely, rather than like children. Be infants regarding evil. 👶
## 1 Corinthians 14:21
The Law says, “I will speak to these people through strange tongues and foreign lips. Even then, they will not listen,” says the Lord. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 14:22
Tongues are a sign for unbelievers, rather than believers. Prophecy is for believers, rather than unbelievers. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 14:23
If the whole church speaks in tongues when uninstructed people or unbelievers enter, will they not say you are out of your minds? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 14:24
If an unbeliever or uninstructed person enters while all prophesy, everyone convicts him and calls him to account, ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 14:25
and his heart’s secrets are revealed. He will fall facedown, worship God, and declare, “God is truly among you!” 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 14:26
Brothers, when you gather, each has a psalm, teaching, revelation, tongue, or interpretation. Everything must build the church up. 🎵
## 1 Corinthians 14:27
If speaking in tongues, two or at most three should speak in turn. Someone must interpret. 💬
## 1 Corinthians 14:28
Without an interpreter, the speaker should stay silent in church and speak to himself and God. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 14:29
Two or three prophets should speak. Others should carefully weigh their words. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 14:30
If someone seated receives a revelation, the first speaker should stop. 👂
## 1 Corinthians 14:31
All can prophesy in turn, so everyone may receive instruction and encouragement. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 14:32
Prophets’ spirits are subject to prophets. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 14:33
God is a God of peace, rather than disorder, as in all the saints’ churches. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 14:34
Women must remain silent in churches. They are not permitted to speak and must submit, as the law says. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 14:35
If they want to ask something, they should ask their husbands at home. A woman speaking in church is dishonorable. 🏠
## 1 Corinthians 14:36
Did God’s word come from you? Did it reach only you? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 14:37
Anyone considering himself a prophet or spiritual person must acknowledge my writing as the Lord’s command. 📜
## 1 Corinthians 14:38
Anyone ignoring this will himself be ignored. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 14:39
Brothers, eagerly desire prophecy and do not forbid speaking in tongues. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 14:40
Do everything properly and in order. 🤝
