## 1 Corinthians 1:1
I am Paul, called by God’s will to be Christ Jesus’ apostle. Our brother Sosthenes joins me. 📜
## 1 Corinthians 1:2
I write to God’s church in Corinth, made holy in Christ Jesus and called to be holy. I also write to everyone everywhere calling on our Lord Jesus Christ’s name. He is their Lord and ours. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 1:3
Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 1:4
I always thank my God for the grace He gave you in Christ Jesus. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 1:5
In Him, you were enriched in every way, in all speech and knowledge. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 1:6
Our testimony about Christ was confirmed in you. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 1:7
You therefore lack no spiritual gift while eagerly awaiting our Lord Jesus Christ’s revelation. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 1:8
He will sustain you to the end, so you will be blameless on our Lord Jesus Christ’s day. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 1:9
God is faithful. He called you into fellowship with His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 1:10
Brothers, in our Lord Jesus Christ’s name, I urge you all to agree. Let there be no divisions. Be united in mind and conviction. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 1:11
Brothers, people from Chloe’s household told me you quarrel. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 1:12
Some say, “I follow Paul,” “I follow Apollos,” “I follow Cephas,” or “I follow Christ.” 💬
## 1 Corinthians 1:13
Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Were you baptized into Paul’s name? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 1:14
I thank God I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius. 💧
## 1 Corinthians 1:15
Therefore, no one can say you were baptized into my name. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 1:16
I also baptized Stephanas’s household. Beyond that, I cannot remember baptizing anyone else. 💧
## 1 Corinthians 1:17
Christ sent me to preach the good news, rather than baptize. I do not use words of wisdom, so Christ’s cross will not lose its power. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 1:18
The cross’s message is foolishness to those perishing. To us being saved, it is God’s power. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 1:19
Scripture says, “I will destroy the wise people’s wisdom and frustrate the intelligent people’s intelligence.” 📖
## 1 Corinthians 1:20
Where is the wise man, the scribe, or this age’s philosopher? Has God not made the world’s wisdom foolish? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 1:21
In God’s wisdom, the world did not know Him through its wisdom. God was pleased to save believers through the foolishness of what was preached. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 1:22
Jews demand signs, and Greeks seek wisdom. 👁️
## 1 Corinthians 1:23
We preach Christ crucified, a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to Gentiles. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 1:24
To those called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is God’s power and wisdom. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 1:25
God’s foolishness is wiser than human wisdom. God’s weakness is stronger than human strength. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 1:26
Brothers, consider when you were called. Not many were wise by human standards. Not many were powerful. Not many were of noble birth. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 1:27
God chose the world’s foolish things to shame the wise. He chose its weak things to shame the strong. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 1:28
He chose the world’s lowly and despised things, and things that are not, to cancel things that are. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 1:29
This was so no one could boast before Him. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 1:30
Because of God, you are in Christ Jesus. Christ became wisdom from God for us: our righteousness, holiness, and redemption. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 1:31
Scripture therefore says, “Whoever boasts should boast in the Lord.” 🙏
