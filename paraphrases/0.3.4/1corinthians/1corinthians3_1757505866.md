## 1 Corinthians 3:1
Brothers, I could not speak to you as spiritual people. I addressed you as worldly, as infants in Christ. 👶
## 1 Corinthians 3:2
I gave you milk because you were not ready for solid food. You are still not ready. 🥛
## 1 Corinthians 3:3
You remain worldly. Do your jealousy and conflict not show this? Do you not walk in human ways? ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 3:4
One says, “I follow Paul.” Another says, “I follow Apollos.” Does this not show you are merely human? 💬
## 1 Corinthians 3:5
What are Apollos and Paul? Servants through whom you believed. The Lord assigned each his role. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 3:6
I planted the seed, Apollos watered it, and God made it grow. 🌱
## 1 Corinthians 3:7
Neither planter nor waterer is anything. God alone makes things grow. 🌾
## 1 Corinthians 3:8
Planter and waterer share one purpose. Each will receive a reward for his own work. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 3:9
We are God’s fellow workers. You are God’s field and building. 🏗️
## 1 Corinthians 3:10
Through God’s grace, I laid a foundation as an expert builder. Another builds on it. Each must build carefully. 🏗️
## 1 Corinthians 3:11
No one can lay any foundation except the one already laid: Jesus Christ. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 3:12
If someone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw, 🪨
## 1 Corinthians 3:13
his work will become clear because the Day will reveal it with fire. Fire will test the quality of each person’s work. 🔥
## 1 Corinthians 3:14
If his building survives, he will receive a reward. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 3:15
If it burns, he will suffer loss. He will be saved, but only as through flames. 🔥
## 1 Corinthians 3:16
Do you not know you are God’s temple and His Spirit lives in you? 🕊️
## 1 Corinthians 3:17
If someone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy him. His temple is holy, and you are that temple. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 3:18
Let no one deceive himself. If you think you are wise in this age, become a fool so you may become wise. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 3:19
The world’s wisdom is foolishness before God. Scripture says, “He catches the wise in their craftiness.” 📖
## 1 Corinthians 3:20
It also says, “The Lord knows the wise people’s thoughts are useless.” 💭
## 1 Corinthians 3:21
Stop boasting in people. Everything is yours: 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 3:22
Paul, Apollos, Cephas, the world, life, death, the present, and the future. All belong to you. 🌍
## 1 Corinthians 3:23
You belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God. ✝️
