## 1 Corinthians 9:1
Am I not free and an apostle? Have I not seen Jesus our Lord? Are you not my work in the Lord? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 9:2
Even if I am not an apostle to others, I am to you. You seal my apostleship in the Lord. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 9:3
This is my defense to those examining me: ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 9:4
Do we have no right to food and drink? 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 9:5
Do we have no right to bring a believing wife, like other apostles, the Lord’s brothers, and Cephas? 💍
## 1 Corinthians 9:6
Must only Barnabas and I work for our living among the apostles? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 9:7
What soldier pays his own expenses? What vineyard planter eats none of its fruit? What shepherd drinks none of his flock’s milk? 🍇
## 1 Corinthians 9:8
Is this only a human view? Does the Law not say the same? 📖
## 1 Corinthians 9:9
Moses’ Law says, “Do not muzzle an ox treading grain.” Does God concern Himself with oxen? 🐂
## 1 Corinthians 9:10
Does He not speak for us? This was written for us. Plowmen and threshers should expect to share the harvest. 🌾
## 1 Corinthians 9:11
If we sowed spiritual seed among you, is a material harvest from you too much? 🌱
## 1 Corinthians 9:12
If others have a right to your support, do we not have more? We did not use this right. We endure anything to avoid hindering Christ’s good news. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 9:13
Do you not know temple workers eat its food? Those serving the altar share its offerings. 🏛️
## 1 Corinthians 9:14
Likewise, the Lord directed preachers of the good news to receive their living from it. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 9:15
I used none of these rights. I do not write to request something for myself. I would rather die than let anyone cancel my boast. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 9:16
Preaching gives me no reason to boast because I must preach. Woe to me if I do not preach the good news! 📣
## 1 Corinthians 9:17
If I preach willingly, I have a reward. Otherwise, I still have an entrusted responsibility. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 9:18
My reward is offering the good news freely when preaching, without fully using my preaching rights. 🎁
## 1 Corinthians 9:19
Although free from obligation to anyone, I enslave myself to everyone to win as many as possible. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 9:20
I became like a Jew to win Jews. I became like someone under the law to win those under it, although I am not under it. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 9:21
I became like someone without the law to win those without it. I am not outside God’s law, but under Christ’s law. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 9:22
I became weak to win the weak. I became everything to everyone, so by every possible means I might save some. ❤️
## 1 Corinthians 9:23
I do this for the good news, so I may share its blessings. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 9:24
In a race, everyone runs, but only one wins the prize. Do you not know this? Run to win it. 🏃
## 1 Corinthians 9:25
Competitors train with strict discipline for a crown that perishes. We do so for a crown that never perishes. 👑
## 1 Corinthians 9:26
I do not run without a goal or fight as though punching air. 🏃
## 1 Corinthians 9:27
I discipline my body and enslave it, so I will not be disqualified after preaching to others. 🛡️
