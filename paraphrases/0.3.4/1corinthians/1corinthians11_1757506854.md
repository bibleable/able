## 1 Corinthians 11:1
Imitate me as I imitate Christ. 🚶
## 1 Corinthians 11:2
I praise you for remembering me in everything and keeping the traditions as I passed them on. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 11:3
Understand that Christ is every man’s head. Man is woman’s head, and God is Christ’s head. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 11:4
A man praying or prophesying with his head covered dishonors his head. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 11:5
A woman praying or prophesying with her head uncovered dishonors her head. It is as though her head were shaved. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 11:6
If a woman does not cover her head, she should have her hair cut off. If cutting or shaving it is shameful, she should cover her head. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 11:7
A man should not cover his head because he is God’s image and glory. Woman is man’s glory. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 11:8
Woman came from man, rather than man from woman. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 11:9
Woman was created for man, rather than man for woman. 👥
## 1 Corinthians 11:10
A woman should therefore have a sign of authority on her head because of the angels. 👼
## 1 Corinthians 11:11
In the Lord, neither woman nor man is independent of the other. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 11:12
Woman came from man, and man is born of woman. Everything comes from God. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 11:13
Judge for yourselves: should a woman pray to God with her head uncovered? ❓
## 1 Corinthians 11:14
Does nature not teach that long hair disgraces a man, ❓
## 1 Corinthians 11:15
but glorifies a woman? Her long hair was given as a covering. 📖
## 1 Corinthians 11:16
If anyone disputes this, neither we nor God’s churches have another practice. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 11:17
I cannot praise you in these instructions. Your gatherings do more harm than good. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 11:18
First, I hear divisions arise when you gather as a church. I partly believe this. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 11:19
Differences must exist among you to show who is approved. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 11:20
When you gather, you are not eating the Lord’s Supper. 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 11:21
Each eats ahead without sharing. One stays hungry while another becomes drunk. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 11:22
Do you not have homes for eating and drinking? Do you despise God’s church and shame those with nothing? What should I say? Should I praise you? No! 💔
## 1 Corinthians 11:23
I passed on what I received from the Lord. On the night of His betrayal, the Lord Jesus took bread, 🍞
## 1 Corinthians 11:24
gave thanks, broke it, and said, “This is My body for you. Do this to remember Me.” 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 11:25
After supper, He likewise took the cup and said, “This cup is the new covenant in My blood. Whenever you drink it, do this to remember Me.” 🍷
## 1 Corinthians 11:26
Whenever you eat this bread and drink this cup, you announce the Lord’s death until He comes. ✝️
## 1 Corinthians 11:27
Anyone eating the Lord’s bread or drinking His cup in an unworthy manner sins against His body and blood. ⚠️
## 1 Corinthians 11:28
Examine yourself before eating the bread and drinking the cup. 🙏
## 1 Corinthians 11:29
Eating and drinking without recognizing the body brings judgment on yourself. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 11:30
This is why many are weak and sick, and some have fallen asleep. 💔
## 1 Corinthians 11:31
If we judged ourselves properly, we would not come under judgment. ⚖️
## 1 Corinthians 11:32
When the Lord judges us, He disciplines us so we will not be condemned with the world. 🛡️
## 1 Corinthians 11:33
Brothers, wait for each other when gathering to eat. 🤝
## 1 Corinthians 11:34
Anyone hungry should eat at home, so gathering will not bring judgment. I will instruct you about the rest when I come. 🏠
