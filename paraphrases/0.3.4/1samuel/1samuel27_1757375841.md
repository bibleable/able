## 1 Samuel 27:1
David thought, “Saul will kill me someday. My best choice is to escape to Philistine land. He will stop searching Israel, and I will escape him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:2
He took six hundred men to Achish, Maoch’s son and Gath’s king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:3
They settled with Achish in Gath, each with his family. David brought his wives Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail of Carmel, Nabal’s widow. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:4
Saul heard David fled to Gath and stopped searching. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:5
David told Achish, “If you favor me, give me an outlying town to live in. Why should your servant live with you in the royal city?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:6
Achish gave him Ziklag that day. It belongs to Judah’s kings to this day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:7
David lived in Philistine territory a year and four months. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:8
David and his men raided the Geshurites, Girzites, and Amalekites. These peoples had lived since ancient times across the land extending to Shur and Egypt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:9
David left no man or woman alive in each territory he attacked. He took flocks, herds, donkeys, camels, and clothing. Then he returned to Achish. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:10
Achish asked where he raided. David answered, “Judah’s Negev,” “Jerahmeel’s Negev,” or “The Kenites’ Negev.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:11
David left no man or woman alive to bring to Gath. He said, “They would report what I did.” He followed this practice throughout his time in Philistine territory. 📖

## 1 Samuel 27:12
Achish trusted him. He thought David had become a stench to Israel and would serve him forever. 📖
