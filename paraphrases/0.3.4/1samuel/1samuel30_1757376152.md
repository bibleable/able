## 1 Samuel 30:1
David and his men reached Ziklag on the third day. The Amalekites had raided the Negev, attacked Ziklag, and burned it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:2
They captured the women and everyone else, young and old. They killed no one but carried them away. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:3
David and his men found their city burned. Their wives, sons, and daughters were captives. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:4
They wept loudly until they lacked strength to cry. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:5
David’s wives were also captured: Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, Nabal of Carmel’s widow. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:6
David was deeply distressed. Every man grieved for his children, and they threatened to stone him. David found strength in the LORD his God. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:7
He told Abiathar the priest, Ahimelech’s son, “Bring the ephod.” Abiathar brought it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:8
David asked the LORD, “Should I pursue the raiders? Will I catch them?” He answered, “Pursue them. You will catch them and rescue the captives.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:9
David and six hundred men reached Besor’s brook. Some stayed there. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:10
Two hundred were too exhausted to cross. David and four hundred continued pursuing. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:11
His men found an Egyptian in a field and brought him to David. They gave him water and food. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:12
He ate a piece of fig cake and two raisin clusters and regained strength. He had eaten and drunk nothing for three days and nights. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:13
David asked his master and origin. He answered, “I am an Egyptian slave of an Amalekite. My master abandoned me three days ago when I became sick. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:14
We raided the Cherethites’ Negev, Judah’s territory, and Caleb’s Negev. We burned Ziklag.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:15
David asked, “Will you lead me to them?” He answered, “Swear by God not to kill me or return me to my master. Then I will guide you.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:16
He led David to the Amalekites. They spread across the land, eating, drinking, and celebrating their great plunder from Philistine and Judean land. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:17
David struck them from twilight until the following evening. Only four hundred young men escaped on camels. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:18
David recovered everything the Amalekites took, including his two wives. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:19
Nothing was missing: young or old, sons or daughters, or plunder. David brought everything back. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:20
He took all flocks and herds. His men drove them before the other livestock, calling, “This is David’s plunder!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:21
David reached the two hundred exhausted men left at Besor’s brook. They came to meet him and his troops. He greeted them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:22
Wicked, worthless men among his followers said, “They did not fight with us. They get none of the plunder except their wives and children. They can take them and leave.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:23
David said, “Brothers, do not treat the LORD’s gifts this way. He protected us and delivered the raiders into our hands. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:24
Who will accept your proposal? Those who fought and those who guarded supplies will receive equal shares.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:25
David made this a statute and ordinance for Israel. It continues from that day to this day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:26
At Ziklag, David sent plunder to his friends, Judah’s elders. He said, “Here is a gift from the LORD’s enemies’ plunder.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:27
He sent gifts to Bethel, Ramoth Negev, and Jattir. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:28
He also sent gifts to Aroer, Siphmoth, and Eshtemoa. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:29
He also sent gifts to Racal and the Jerahmeelites’ and Kenites’ cities. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:30
He also sent gifts to Hormah, Bor-ashan, and Athach. 📖

## 1 Samuel 30:31
He also sent gifts to Hebron and every place he and his men had roamed. 📖
