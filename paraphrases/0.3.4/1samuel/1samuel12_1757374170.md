## 1 Samuel 12:1
Samuel told all Israel, “I listened to everything you said and appointed your king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:2
Your king now walks before you. I am old and gray, and my sons are here with you. I have walked before you from youth until today. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:3
Here I am. Testify against me before the LORD and His anointed. Whose ox or donkey did I take? Whom did I cheat or oppress? Whose bribe did I accept to close my eyes? Tell me, and I will restore it.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:4
They answered, “You neither cheated nor oppressed us. You took nothing from anyone.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:5
Samuel said, “The LORD and His anointed witness today that you found nothing in my hands.” They answered, “He is witness.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:6
Samuel said, “The LORD appointed Moses and Aaron and brought your fathers from Egypt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:7
Stand here before the LORD. I will confront you with all His righteous acts for you and your fathers. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:8
Jacob went to Egypt, and your fathers cried to the LORD. He sent Moses and Aaron. They brought your fathers from Egypt and settled them here. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:9
They forgot the LORD their God. He sold them into the power of Sisera, Hazor’s army commander, the Philistines, and Moab’s king. These enemies fought them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:10
They cried to the LORD, ‘We sinned. We abandoned the LORD and served Baals and Ashtoreths. Rescue us from our enemies so we may serve You.’ 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:11
The LORD sent Jerubbaal, Barak, Jephthah, and Samuel. He rescued you from enemies on every side. You lived securely. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:12
You saw Nahash, the Ammonites’ king, approaching. You demanded a king, although the LORD your God was your king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:13
Here is the king you chose and requested. The LORD set him over you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:14
All will be well if you fear the LORD, serve Him, obey His voice, and do not rebel against His command. Both you and your king must follow the LORD your God. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:15
If you disobey the LORD and rebel against His command, His hand will oppose you as it opposed your fathers. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:16
Stand and see the great thing the LORD will do before your eyes. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:17
Is this not wheat harvest? I will ask the LORD for thunder and rain. Then you will see how greatly you sinned before Him by requesting a king.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:18
Samuel called to the LORD. He sent thunder and rain that day. Everyone greatly feared the LORD and Samuel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:19
They pleaded, “Pray to the LORD your God so your servants do not die! We added the evil of requesting a king to our other sins.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:20
Samuel answered, “Do not fear. You committed this evil, but keep following the LORD. Serve Him wholeheartedly. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:21
Do not pursue worthless things that cannot help or rescue you. They are empty. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:22
For His great name’s sake, the LORD will not abandon His people. He was pleased to make you His own. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:23
May I never sin against the LORD by stopping prayer for you. I will keep teaching you the good and right way. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:24
Above all, fear the LORD and serve Him faithfully with your whole heart. Consider His great deeds for you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 12:25
If you continue doing evil, you and your king will be swept away.” 📖
