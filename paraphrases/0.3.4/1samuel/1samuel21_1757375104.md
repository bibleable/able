## 1 Samuel 21:1
David came to Ahimelech the priest at Nob. Ahimelech trembled and asked, “Why are you alone? Why is no one with you?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:2
David answered, “The king gave me a secret mission. He ordered no one to know about it. I told my young men to meet me at a certain place. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:3
What food do you have? Give me five bread loaves or whatever you can find.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:4
The priest answered, “I have no ordinary bread. I have consecrated bread, if your young men have kept away from women.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:5
David said, “We kept away from women as usual when I leave on missions. The men’s bodies are holy even on ordinary missions. How much more today!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:6
The priest gave him consecrated bread because only the Bread of the Presence was available. It had been removed from before the LORD and replaced with hot bread that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:7
Doeg the Edomite was there that day, detained before the LORD. He was Saul’s chief shepherd. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:8
David asked Ahimelech, “Do you have a spear or sword? The king’s urgent mission left me without my sword or weapons.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:9
The priest said, “Goliath the Philistine’s sword is wrapped in cloth behind the ephod. You killed him in the Valley of Elah. Take it if you want. No other sword is here.” David answered, “None is like it. Give it to me.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:10
David fled Saul that day and went to Achish, Gath’s king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:11
Achish’s servants said, “Is this not David, the land’s king? People danced and sang, ‘Saul killed thousands, and David tens of thousands.’” 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:12
David took their words to heart. He greatly feared Achish, Gath’s king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:13
He changed his behavior and pretended madness in their hands. He scratched the gate’s doors and let saliva run down his beard. 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:14
Achish said, “You see he is insane! Why bring him to me? 📖

## 1 Samuel 21:15
Do I lack madmen, that you bring him to rave before me? Must he enter my house?” 📖
