## 1 Samuel 13:1
Saul became king at thirty years old. He ruled Israel forty-two years. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:2
He chose three thousand Israelites. Two thousand stayed with him at Michmash and Bethel’s hill country. A thousand stayed with Jonathan at Gibeah of Benjamin. He sent the others home. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:3
Jonathan attacked the Philistine outpost at Geba. The Philistines heard. Saul blew the ram’s horn throughout the land, saying, “Let the Hebrews hear!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:4
All Israel heard, “Saul attacked a Philistine outpost. Israel has become a stench to the Philistines!” The people were summoned to Saul at Gilgal. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:5
The Philistines gathered three thousand chariots, six thousand horsemen, and troops as numerous as seaside sand. They camped at Michmash, east of Beth-aven, to fight Israel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:6
Israel’s men saw their danger and their troops under pressure. They hid in caves, thickets, rocks, cellars, and cisterns. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:7
Some Hebrews crossed the Jordan to Gad and Gilead. Saul stayed in Gilgal. All his troops trembled with fear. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:8
Saul waited seven days for Samuel’s appointed time. Samuel did not reach Gilgal, and troops began deserting Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:9
Saul said, “Bring the burnt offering and peace offerings.” He offered the burnt offering. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:10
Samuel arrived as Saul finished. Saul went to greet him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:11
Samuel asked, “What did you do?” Saul answered, “My troops deserted me. You did not come at the appointed time. The Philistines gathered at Michmash. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:12
I thought, ‘The Philistines will attack me at Gilgal. I have not sought the LORD’s favor.’ I felt compelled to offer the burnt offering.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:13
Samuel said, “You acted foolishly and disobeyed the LORD your God’s command. If you had obeyed, He would have established your kingdom over Israel forever. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:14
Your kingdom will not endure because you disobeyed the LORD’s command. The LORD sought a man after His heart and appointed him ruler of His people.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:15
Samuel left Gilgal for Gibeah of Benjamin. Saul counted about six hundred remaining troops. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:16
Saul, his son Jonathan, and their troops stayed at Geba of Benjamin. The Philistines camped at Michmash. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:17
Philistine raiders left camp in three divisions. One headed toward Ophrah in Shual’s land. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:18
Another headed toward Beth-horon. The third took the border road overlooking the Valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:19
Israel had no blacksmiths. The Philistines had said, “The Hebrews must not make swords or spears.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:20
Israelites went to the Philistines to sharpen plowshares, mattocks, axes, and sickles. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:21
Sharpening a plowshare or mattock cost a pim. Sharpening a pitchfork or axe cost a third of a shekel. Repointing an oxgoad also cost a third of a shekel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:22
On battle day, no troops with Saul and Jonathan had swords or spears. Only Saul and his son Jonathan had weapons. 📖

## 1 Samuel 13:23
A Philistine garrison went to Michmash’s pass. 📖
