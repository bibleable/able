## 1 Samuel 5:1
The Philistines captured God’s ark and took it from Ebenezer to Ashdod. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:2
They carried it into Dagon’s temple and set it beside his statue. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:3
Early the next morning, Ashdod’s people found Dagon fallen facedown before the LORD’s ark. They put him back in his place. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:4
Early the following morning, Dagon again lay facedown before the LORD’s ark. His head and hands had broken off and lay on the threshold. Only his torso remained. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:5
That is why Dagon’s priests and everyone entering his temple in Ashdod avoid stepping on the threshold to this day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:6
The LORD’s hand was heavy on Ashdod and its surrounding area. He ravaged the people and afflicted them with tumors. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:7
Ashdod’s men saw this and said, “Israel’s God’s ark must leave. His hand is heavy on us and our god Dagon.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:8
They gathered all Philistine rulers and asked, “What should we do with Israel’s God’s ark?” They answered, “Move it to Gath.” They moved the ark. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:9
After the ark reached Gath, the LORD’s hand struck that city too. He caused great confusion and afflicted young and old men with tumors. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:10
They sent God’s ark to Ekron. As it arrived, the Ekronites cried, “They brought Israel’s God’s ark to kill us and our people!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:11
The Ekronites gathered all Philistine rulers. They said, “Send Israel’s God’s ark back to its place so it does not kill us and our people!” Deadly confusion filled the city. God’s hand was very heavy on it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 5:12
Those who survived suffered tumors. The city’s cries rose to heaven. 📖
