## 1 Samuel 29:1
The Philistines gathered all their forces at Aphek. Israel camped by Jezreel’s spring. 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:2
Philistine leaders marched with units of hundreds and thousands. David and his men followed with Achish. 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:3
The commanders asked about the Hebrews. Achish answered, “This is David, Saul king of Israel’s servant. He has been with me these days, even years. Since he defected, I found no fault in him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:4
The commanders angrily told Achish, “Send him to his assigned place. He must not become our enemy in battle. How better could he regain his master’s favor than with our men’s heads? 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:5
Is he not the David from the dance song, ‘Saul killed thousands, and David tens of thousands’?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:6
Achish told David, “As the LORD lives, you have been upright. I want you to march with my army. I found no fault since you arrived. But the leaders disapprove of you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:7
Return peacefully. Do nothing to displease the Philistine leaders.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:8
David asked, “What did I do? What fault did you find since I arrived that stops me fighting my king’s enemies?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:9
Achish answered, “You please me like an angel of God. The commanders insist you must not fight with us. 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:10
Rise early with your master’s servants who came with you. Leave as soon as daylight comes.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 29:11
David and his men rose early to return to Philistine land. The Philistines marched to Jezreel. 📖
