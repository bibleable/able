## 1 Samuel 6:1
The LORD’s ark stayed in Philistine land seven months. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:2
The Philistines called priests and diviners. They asked, “What should we do with the LORD’s ark? Tell us how to return it.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:3
They answered, “Do not return Israel’s God’s ark empty. Send a guilt offering with it. Then you will be healed and understand why His hand has not lifted from you.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:4
The Philistines asked, “What guilt offering?” They answered, “Five gold tumors and five gold rats, matching the number of your rulers. The same plague struck you and your rulers. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:5
Make images of your tumors and the rats ravaging your land. Give glory to Israel’s God. Perhaps He will lift His hand from you, your gods, and your land. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:6
Why harden your hearts like the Egyptians and Pharaoh? After He afflicted them, did they not release the people to leave? 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:7
Prepare a new cart and two milk cows never used under a yoke. Hitch the cows to the cart. Pen their calves away from them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:8
Put the LORD’s ark on the cart. Place the gold guilt offering objects in a chest beside it. Send the ark away. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:9
Watch it. If it goes toward Beth-shemesh, its homeland, the LORD caused our disaster. Otherwise, we will know His hand did not punish us. It happened by chance.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:10
The men obeyed. They hitched two milk cows to the cart and penned up their calves. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:11
They put the LORD’s ark on the cart. They also put the chest containing gold rats and tumor images on it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:12
The cows followed the highway straight toward Beth-shemesh, lowing as they went. They turned neither right nor left. The Philistine rulers followed to Beth-shemesh’s border. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:13
Beth-shemesh’s people harvested wheat in the valley. They looked up, saw the ark, and rejoiced greatly. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:14
The cart stopped by a large rock in the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh. The people chopped up the cart. They offered the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:15
The Levites removed the LORD’s ark and the chest of gold objects. They placed them on the large rock. Beth-shemesh’s men offered burnt offerings and sacrifices to the LORD that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:16
The five Philistine rulers saw this. They returned to Ekron the same day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:17
The Philistines sent one gold tumor for each city as a guilt offering to the LORD. These cities were Ashdod, Gaza, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:18
The gold rats matched the number of the five rulers’ cities, both fortified cities and surrounding villages. The large rock where they placed the LORD’s ark remains in Joshua of Beth-shemesh’s field to this day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:19
God killed seventy men of Beth-shemesh because they looked inside the LORD’s ark. The people mourned because the LORD struck them with a great slaughter. 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:20
Beth-shemesh’s men asked, “Who can stand before the LORD, this holy God? To whom should the ark go from here?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 6:21
They sent messengers to Kiriath-jearim’s people. “The Philistines returned the LORD’s ark. Come and take it with you.” 📖
