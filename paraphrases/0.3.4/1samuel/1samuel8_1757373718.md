## 1 Samuel 8:1
Samuel grew old. He appointed his sons as Israel’s judges. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:2
His firstborn was Joel, and his second son was Abijah. They judged in Beersheba. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:3
They did not follow Samuel’s ways. They pursued dishonest gain, accepted bribes, and twisted justice. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:4
All Israel’s elders gathered and came to Samuel at Ramah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:5
They said, “You are old, and your sons do not follow your ways. Appoint a king to judge us like other nations.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:6
Their demand for a king to judge them displeased Samuel. He prayed to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:7
The LORD said, “Listen to everything the people say. They rejected Me as king, rather than you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:8
Since I brought them from Egypt, they have abandoned Me and served other gods. Now they treat you the same way. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:9
Listen to them. But solemnly warn them about how their king will rule.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:10
Samuel told those requesting a king everything the LORD said. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:11
“Your king will act this way. He will take your sons for his chariots and horses. Some will run before his chariots. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:12
He will appoint commanders of thousands and fifties. Others will plow his fields, reap his harvest, and make weapons and chariot equipment. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:13
He will take your daughters as perfumers, cooks, and bakers. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:14
He will take your best fields, vineyards, and olive groves and give them to his servants. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:15
He will take a tenth of your grain and grapes for his officials and servants. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:16
He will take your male and female servants and your best cattle and donkeys for his own use. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:17
He will take a tenth of your flocks. You will become his slaves. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:18
Then you will beg for relief from your chosen king. The LORD will not answer that day.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:19
The people refused Samuel’s warning. They said, “No! We must have a king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:20
Then we will be like other nations. Our king will judge us, lead us, and fight our battles.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:21
Samuel heard all their words and repeated them before the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 8:22
The LORD said, “Listen to them and appoint their king.” Samuel told Israel’s men, “Everyone must return to his city.” 📖
