## 1 Samuel 7:1
Kiriath-jearim’s men took the LORD’s ark to Abinadab’s house on the hill. They consecrated his son Eleazar to guard it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:2
The ark remained in Kiriath-jearim a long time, twenty years. All Israel sought the LORD with mourning. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:3
Samuel told all Israel, “If you return wholeheartedly to the LORD, remove your foreign gods and Ashtoreths. Prepare your hearts for Him and serve Him alone. He will rescue you from the Philistines.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:4
Israel removed the Baals and Ashtoreths. They served only the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:5
Samuel said, “Gather all Israel at Mizpah. I will pray to the LORD for you.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:6
They gathered at Mizpah, drew water, and poured it before the LORD. They fasted that day and confessed, “We sinned against the LORD.” Samuel judged Israel at Mizpah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:7
The Philistines heard Israel had gathered at Mizpah. Their rulers marched toward Israel. The Israelites heard and feared the Philistines. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:8
They told Samuel, “Keep crying to the LORD our God for us. Ask Him to rescue us from the Philistines.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:9
Samuel offered a nursing lamb as a whole burnt offering to the LORD. He cried to the LORD for Israel. The LORD answered. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:10
The Philistines approached to attack while Samuel offered the sacrifice. The LORD thundered loudly against them. He confused them so greatly that they fled from Israel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:11
Israel’s men charged from Mizpah. They pursued and struck down the Philistines as far as the area below Beth-car. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:12
Samuel set a stone between Mizpah and Shen. He named it Ebenezer, saying, “The LORD has helped us this far.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:13
The Philistines were subdued and stopped invading Israel. The LORD’s hand opposed them throughout Samuel’s days. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:14
The cities from Ekron to Gath returned to Israel from Philistine control. Israel also recovered the surrounding territory. Israel and the Amorites had peace. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:15
Samuel judged Israel throughout his life. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:16
Each year, he traveled a circuit through Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah. He judged Israel in all these places. 📖

## 1 Samuel 7:17
He returned to Ramah because he lived there. He judged Israel there and built an altar to the LORD. 📖
