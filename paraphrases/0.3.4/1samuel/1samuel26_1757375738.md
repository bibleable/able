## 1 Samuel 26:1
The Ziphites told Saul at Gibeah, “David hides on Hachilah’s hill, opposite Jeshimon.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:2
Saul took three thousand chosen Israelites to search Ziph’s wilderness for David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:3
Saul camped beside the road on Hachilah’s hill, opposite Jeshimon. David stayed in the wilderness. He realized Saul followed him there. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:4
David sent spies to confirm Saul’s arrival. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:5
He visited Saul’s camp. He saw where Saul and army commander Abner, Ner’s son, lay. Saul lay in the camp’s inner circle, surrounded by troops. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:6
David asked Ahimelech the Hittite and Abishai, Zeruiah’s son and Joab’s brother, “Who will enter Saul’s camp with me?” Abishai volunteered. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:7
They entered that night. Saul slept in the inner circle, his spear planted beside his head. Abner and the troops lay around him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:8
Abishai said, “God delivered your enemy today. Let me pin him to the ground with one spear stroke. I will not need another!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:9
David answered, “Do not kill him. Who can strike the LORD’s anointed without guilt? 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:10
As the LORD lives, He Himself will strike Saul down. His day may come to die, or he may perish in battle. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:11
May the LORD forbid me to strike His anointed! Take the spear and water jug beside his head. Let us leave.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:12
David took Saul’s spear and water jug, and they left. No one saw, knew, or woke. A deep sleep from the LORD kept everyone asleep. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:13
David crossed to the other side and stood far away on the mountaintop. A wide gulf separated them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:14
He shouted to the troops and Abner, Ner’s son, “Will you answer, Abner?” Abner replied, “Who calls to the king?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:15
David said, “Are you not a man without equal in Israel? Why fail to guard your king when someone came to kill him? 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:16
You did wrong. As the LORD lives, you all deserve death for failing to protect His anointed. Where are the spear and water jug beside the king’s head?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:17
Saul recognized David’s voice. “Is that you, my son David?” David answered, “Yes, my lord and king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:18
Why pursue your servant? What did I do? What evil is in my hand? 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:19
My king, listen. If the LORD stirred you against me, may He accept an offering. If people did this, may they be cursed before Him. Today they drove me from sharing His inheritance, saying, ‘Serve other gods.’ 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:20
Do not let my blood fall far from the LORD’s presence. Israel’s king hunts a flea, like someone hunting a partridge in the mountains.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:21
Saul answered, “I sinned. Return, my son David. I will never harm you again because you valued my life today. I acted foolishly and made a grave error!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:22
David said, “Here is your spear. Send a young man to collect it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:23
May the LORD repay each person’s righteousness and faithfulness. He delivered you into my hand today. I refused to strike His anointed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:24
As I valued your life today, may the LORD value mine and rescue me from every trouble.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 26:25
Saul said, “Blessed be you, my son David. You will do great things and prevail.” David continued on his way, and Saul returned home. 📖
