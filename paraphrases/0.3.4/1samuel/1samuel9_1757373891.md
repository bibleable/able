## 1 Samuel 9:1
Kish was a powerful Benjamite, Abiel’s son. Abiel was Zeror’s son, Zeror was Becorath’s son, and Becorath was Aphiah of Benjamin’s son. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:2
His son Saul was choice and handsome, without equal in Israel. He stood a head taller than anyone else. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:3
Kish’s donkeys wandered away. He told Saul, “Take a servant and find the donkeys.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:4
Saul and the servant searched Ephraim’s hill country and Shalishah but found no donkeys. They searched Shaalim without success. They searched Benjamin’s land and still found none. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:5
At Zuph, Saul told his servant, “Let us return. My father may stop worrying about the donkeys and start worrying about us.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:6
The servant said, “A respected man of God lives in this city. Everything he says comes true. Let us visit him. Perhaps he can tell us which way to go.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:7
Saul asked, “If we go, what can we give him? Our bread is gone. We have no gift for the man of God. What do we have?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:8
The servant answered, “I have a quarter shekel of silver. I will give it to him, and he will tell us our way.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:9
Formerly in Israel, someone seeking God’s guidance would say, “Let us visit the seer.” A person now called a prophet was formerly called a seer. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:10
Saul said, “Good. Let us go.” They set out for the man of God’s city. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:11
They climbed toward the city and met young women going to draw water. They asked, “Is the seer here?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:12
The women answered, “Yes, ahead of you. Hurry! He came today because the people have a sacrifice at the high place. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:13
You will find him as you enter, before he goes to eat at the high place. The people wait for him to bless the sacrifice. Then the guests eat. Go now, and you will find him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:14
Saul and his servant went toward the city. As they entered, Samuel approached on his way to the high place. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:15
The day before Saul arrived, the LORD revealed this to Samuel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:16
“Tomorrow at this time, I will send a man from Benjamin. Anoint him ruler of My people Israel. He will rescue them from the Philistines. I have looked upon My people because their cries reached Me.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:17
Samuel saw Saul. The LORD said, “This is the man I mentioned. He will rule My people.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:18
Saul approached Samuel in the gateway. He asked, “Please tell me where the seer lives.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:19
Samuel answered, “I am the seer. Go ahead to the high place and eat with me today. Tomorrow morning, I will send you away and tell you everything in your heart. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:20
Do not worry about the donkeys lost three days ago. They have been found. Is not all Israel’s desire directed toward you and your father’s house?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:21
Saul asked, “Am I not from Benjamin, Israel’s smallest tribe? Is my clan not Benjamin’s least? Why say this to me?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:22
Samuel brought Saul and his servant into the hall. He seated them in honor among about thirty invited guests. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:23
Samuel told the cook, “Bring the portion I gave you to set aside.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:24
The cook put the leg and what was attached to it before Saul. Samuel said, “This was kept for you. Eat, because I kept it for this occasion, from when I said, ‘I invited the people.’” Saul ate with Samuel that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:25
They came down from the high place to the city. Samuel spoke with Saul on his house’s roof. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:26
They rose early. Before dawn, Samuel called to Saul on the roof, “Get ready, and I will send you away!” Saul prepared, and he and Samuel went outside together. 📖

## 1 Samuel 9:27
Near the city’s edge, Samuel said, “Send the servant ahead. Stay here a while, and I will tell you God’s word.” The servant went ahead. 📖
