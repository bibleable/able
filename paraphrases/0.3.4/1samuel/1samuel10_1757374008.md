## 1 Samuel 10:1
Samuel poured a flask of oil on Saul’s head and kissed him. He said, “Has the LORD not anointed you ruler over His inheritance? 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:2
Today, you will meet two men by Rachel’s tomb at Zelzah on Benjamin’s border. They will say, ‘The donkeys were found. Your father now worries about you instead. He asks, “What should I do about my son?”’ 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:3
Continue to Tabor’s oak. Three men going to God at Bethel will meet you. One carries three young goats, one three bread loaves, and one a skin of wine. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:4
They will greet you and give you two bread loaves. Accept them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:5
Then you will reach Gibeah of God, where a Philistine outpost stands. Approaching the city, you will meet prophets coming from the high place. Harps, tambourines, flutes, and lyres will precede them. They will prophesy. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:6
The LORD’s Spirit will rush upon you. You will prophesy with them and become a different person. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:7
When these signs occur, do what the occasion requires. God is with you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:8
Go ahead of me to Gilgal. I will come to offer burnt offerings and peace offerings. Wait seven days until I come and show you what to do.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:9
Saul turned to leave Samuel. God changed Saul’s heart. All the signs occurred that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:10
Saul and his servant reached Gibeah and met prophets. God’s Spirit rushed upon Saul, and he prophesied with them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:11
People who knew Saul before saw him prophesying. They asked, “What happened to Kish’s son? Is Saul also among the prophets?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:12
A local man replied, “Who is their father?” This became a proverb: “Is Saul also among the prophets?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:13
Saul finished prophesying and went to the high place. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:14
His uncle asked him and his servant, “Where did you go?” Saul said, “To find the donkeys. We could not find them, so we visited Samuel.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:15
His uncle asked, “What did Samuel tell you?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:16
Saul said, “He assured us the donkeys were found.” He did not tell his uncle Samuel’s words about kingship. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:17
Samuel gathered the people before the LORD at Mizpah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:18
He told Israel, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I brought you from Egypt. I rescued you from the Egyptians and every kingdom that oppressed you.’ 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:19
Today you rejected your God, who saves you from all troubles and suffering. You said, ‘No, give us a king.’ Present yourselves before the LORD by tribe and clan.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:20
Samuel brought all Israel’s tribes forward. Benjamin’s tribe was selected. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:21
He brought Benjamin’s clans forward, and Matri’s clan was selected. Then Saul, Kish’s son, was selected. They searched but could not find him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:22
They asked the LORD again, “Has the man arrived?” He answered, “He is hiding among the baggage.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:23
They ran and brought Saul. He stood a head taller than anyone else. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:24
Samuel said, “See whom the LORD chose! No one among the people is like him.” Everyone shouted, “Long live the king!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:25
Samuel explained kingship’s rights. He wrote them on a scroll and placed it before the LORD. He sent everyone home. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:26
Saul returned home to Gibeah. Brave men whose hearts God touched went with him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 10:27
Worthless men asked, “How can he save us?” They despised Saul and brought no gifts. Saul remained silent. 📖
