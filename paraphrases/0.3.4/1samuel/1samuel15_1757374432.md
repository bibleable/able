## 1 Samuel 15:1
Samuel told Saul, “The LORD sent me to anoint you king over His people Israel. Listen to His words. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:2
The LORD of Hosts says, ‘I saw the Amalekites oppose Israel on the journey from Egypt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:3
Attack the Amalekites and devote everything they own to destruction. Spare no one. Kill men, women, children, infants, oxen, sheep, camels, and donkeys.’” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:4
Saul gathered and counted the troops at Telaim. There were 200,000 foot soldiers and 10,000 men of Judah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:5
Saul reached Amalek’s city and waited in ambush in the valley. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:6
He warned the Kenites, “You were kind to Israel when they left Egypt. Leave the Amalekites so I do not sweep you away with them.” The Kenites moved away. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:7
Saul struck down the Amalekites from Havilah to Shur, east of Egypt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:8
He captured their king Agag alive. He devoted everyone else to destruction by the sword. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:9
Saul and his troops spared Agag and the best sheep, cattle, fat calves, lambs, and everything else of value. They refused to destroy these. They devoted only despised and worthless things to destruction. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:10
The LORD’s word came to Samuel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:11
“I regret making Saul king. He stopped following Me and disobeyed My instructions.” Samuel was distressed and cried to the LORD all night. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:12
Samuel rose early to confront Saul. He heard, “Saul went to Carmel and erected a monument for himself. He then went down to Gilgal.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:13
Samuel arrived. Saul said, “May the LORD bless you! I obeyed His instructions.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:14
Samuel asked, “Why do I hear sheep bleating and cattle lowing?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:15
Saul answered, “The troops brought them from the Amalekites. They spared the best sheep and cattle to sacrifice to the LORD your God. We devoted the rest to destruction.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:16
Samuel said, “Stop! Hear what the LORD told me last night.” Saul answered, “Tell me.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:17
Samuel said, “You once thought yourself small. Have you not become head of Israel’s tribes? The LORD anointed you Israel’s king. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:18
He sent you with this mission: ‘Devote the sinful Amalekites to destruction. Fight until you wipe them out.’ 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:19
Why disobey the LORD? Why rush to the plunder and do evil before Him?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:20
Saul answered, “I obeyed the LORD and completed His mission. I brought Agag, Amalek’s king, and devoted the Amalekites to destruction. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:21
The troops took the best sheep and cattle from the things devoted to destruction. They meant to sacrifice them to the LORD your God at Gilgal.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:22
Samuel said, “Does the LORD value burnt offerings and sacrifices as much as obedience? Obeying His voice is better than sacrifice. Attentiveness is better than rams’ fat. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:23
Rebellion resembles divination’s sin. Arrogance resembles idolatry’s wickedness. You rejected the LORD’s word, so He rejected you as king.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:24
Saul said, “I sinned and broke the LORD’s command and your instructions. I feared the people and obeyed them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:25
Please forgive my sin. Return with me so I can worship the LORD.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:26
Samuel answered, “I will not return. You rejected the LORD’s word. He rejected you as Israel’s king.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:27
Samuel turned to leave. Saul grabbed his robe’s hem, and it tore. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:28
Samuel said, “Today the LORD tore Israel’s kingdom from you. He gave it to your neighbor, who is better than you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:29
Israel’s Glory neither lies nor changes His mind. He is not a man who changes his mind.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:30
Saul said, “I sinned. Please honor me before my people’s elders and Israel. Return so I may worship the LORD your God.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:31
Samuel returned with Saul. Saul worshiped the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:32
Samuel ordered, “Bring Agag, the Amalekites’ king.” Agag came cheerfully, thinking death’s bitterness had surely passed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:33
Samuel said, “Your sword made women childless. Your mother will likewise be childless among women.” He cut Agag to pieces before the LORD at Gilgal. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:34
Samuel went to Ramah. Saul returned home to Gibeah of Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 15:35
Until he died, Samuel never visited Saul again. He mourned for Saul. The LORD regretted making Saul Israel’s king. 📖
