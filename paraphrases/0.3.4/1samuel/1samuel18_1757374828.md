## 1 Samuel 18:1
David finished speaking to Saul. Jonathan’s soul became closely joined to David’s. Jonathan loved him as himself. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:2
Saul kept David from that day onward. He would not let him return to his father’s house. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:3
Jonathan made a covenant with David because he loved him as himself. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:4
Jonathan gave David his robe, tunic, sword, bow, and belt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:5
David succeeded in every mission Saul gave him. Saul appointed him over the warriors. The people and Saul’s officers approved. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:6
The troops returned after David killed the Philistine. Women from all Israel’s cities greeted King Saul with joyful singing, dancing, tambourines, and other instruments. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:7
They danced and sang, “Saul killed thousands, and David tens of thousands.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:8
Saul resented the song and became furious. “They credit David with tens of thousands but me with thousands. What remains for him except the kingdom?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:9
Saul watched David jealously from that day onward. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:10
The next day, a spirit of distress from God came upon Saul. He prophesied inside the house. David played his harp as usual. Saul held a spear. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:11
Saul threw it, intending to pin David to the wall. David escaped him twice. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:12
Saul feared David. The LORD was with David but had left Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:13
Saul sent David away to command a thousand men. David led them to battle and back. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:14
He succeeded in everything because the LORD was with him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:15
Saul saw David’s great success and feared him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:16
Israel and Judah loved David because he led them to battle and back. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:17
Saul offered David his older daughter Merab in marriage. “Be brave for me and fight the LORD’s battles.” He thought, “Let the Philistines strike him instead of me.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:18
David asked, “Who am I? What is my family or father’s clan in Israel, that I should become the king’s son-in-law?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:19
When Merab should have married David, she was given to Adriel of Meholah instead. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:20
Saul’s daughter Michal loved David. Saul heard and was pleased. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:21
Saul thought, “I will give her to David as a trap, bringing the Philistines against him.” He told David, “You have a second opportunity to become my son-in-law.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:22
Saul ordered his servants to speak privately to David. “Tell him the king is pleased and all his servants love him. Ask him to become my son-in-law.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:23
They told David. He answered, “Is becoming the king’s son-in-law a small matter? I am poor and little respected.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:24
The servants reported David’s answer to Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:25
Saul said, “Tell David the king wants only a hundred Philistine foreskins as dowry, to avenge his enemies.” Saul meant the Philistines to kill David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:26
The servants told David these terms. He was pleased to become the king’s son-in-law. The wedding day had not yet arrived. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:27
David and his men killed two hundred Philistines. He presented their foreskins as full payment to become the king’s son-in-law. Saul gave Michal to David in marriage. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:28
Saul realized the LORD was with David and Michal loved him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:29
He feared David even more. He remained David’s enemy from then onward. 📖

## 1 Samuel 18:30
Whenever Philistine commanders fought, David surpassed all Saul’s officers in success. His name became greatly respected. 📖
