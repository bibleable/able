## 1 Samuel 14:1
Jonathan, Saul’s son, told his young armor-bearer, “Let us cross to the Philistine outpost.” He did not tell his father. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:2
Saul stayed under Migron’s pomegranate tree on Gibeah’s outskirts. About six hundred men were with him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:3
They included Ahijah, wearing an ephod. He was Ahitub’s son, and Ahitub was Ichabod’s brother. Ahitub was Phinehas’ son, and Phinehas was Eli’s son. Eli was the LORD’s priest at Shiloh. The troops did not know Jonathan had left. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:4
Cliffs stood on both sides of the pass Jonathan planned to cross toward the Philistine outpost. One was Bozez, and the other Seneh. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:5
One stood northward toward Michmash, the other southward toward Geba. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:6
Jonathan said, “Let us cross to these uncircumcised men’s outpost. Perhaps the LORD will act for us. Nothing prevents Him from saving by many or few.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:7
His armor-bearer said, “Do everything in your heart. Go ahead. I am with you heart and soul.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:8
Jonathan said, “We will cross and show ourselves. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:9
If they tell us to wait for them to come down, we will stay here. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:10
If they tell us to come up, we will climb. This will show the LORD has delivered them into our hands.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:11
They showed themselves to the outpost. The Philistines said, “The Hebrews are leaving their hiding holes!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:12
The men called, “Come up! We will teach you a lesson!” Jonathan told his armor-bearer, “Follow me. The LORD delivered them into Israel’s hand.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:13
Jonathan climbed on his hands and feet, followed by his armor-bearer. The Philistines fell before Jonathan. His armor-bearer followed and finished them off. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:14
They killed about twenty men in their first attack, across about half an acre. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:15
Panic struck the Philistine camp, field, and all the people. Outpost troops and raiders also trembled. The earth shook. Panic spread from God. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:16
Saul’s watchmen at Gibeah of Benjamin saw troops scattering in every direction. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:17
Saul ordered a roll call to identify who had left. They discovered Jonathan and his armor-bearer were missing. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:18
Saul told Ahijah, “Bring God’s ark.” It was with the Israelites then. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:19
As Saul spoke to the priest, the Philistine camp’s uproar increased. Saul told the priest, “Withdraw your hand.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:20
Saul and all his troops gathered and marched into battle. The Philistines were completely confused. Each man used his sword against his neighbor. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:21
Hebrews who had joined the Philistines in their surrounding camps switched sides. They joined Israel with Saul and Jonathan. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:22
Israelites hiding in Ephraim’s hill country heard the Philistines were fleeing. They joined the pursuit closely. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:23
The LORD saved Israel that day. The battle passed beyond Beth-aven. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:24
Israel’s men were distressed because Saul bound them with an oath. “Cursed is anyone eating before evening, before I avenge myself on my enemies.” No troops ate. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:25
The troops entered a forest. Honey lay on the ground. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:26
They saw flowing honey but did not eat. They feared the oath. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:27
Jonathan had not heard his father’s oath. He dipped his staff’s end into the honeycomb and brought honey to his mouth. His eyes brightened. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:28
A soldier told him, “Your father solemnly swore, ‘Cursed is anyone eating today.’ That is why the people are faint.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:29
Jonathan said, “My father troubled the land. See how my eyes brightened after a little honey! 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:30
How much better if the troops freely ate their enemies’ plunder today! Would they not have killed many more Philistines?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:31
Israel struck down the Philistines from Michmash to Aijalon that day. The people were very faint. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:32
They greedily rushed to the plunder. They took sheep, cattle, and calves, slaughtered them on the ground, and ate the meat with blood still in it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:33
Someone told Saul, “The troops sin against the LORD by eating meat with blood.” Saul said, “You broke faith! Roll a large stone here now.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:34
He ordered, “Tell everyone to bring his ox or sheep here, slaughter it, and eat. Do not sin against the LORD by eating meat with blood still in it.” Everyone brought his ox and slaughtered it there that night. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:35
Saul built an altar to the LORD. It was his first altar to Him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:36
Saul said, “Let us pursue the Philistines tonight and plunder until dawn. Leave no one alive!” The troops agreed. The priest said, “We must consult God here.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:37
Saul asked God, “Should I pursue the Philistines? Will You give them into Israel’s hand?” God did not answer that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:38
Saul said, “Troop leaders, come. Let us investigate today’s sin. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:39
As surely as the LORD who saves Israel lives, the offender must die, even if it is Jonathan!” No troop member spoke. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:40
Saul said, “Israel, stand on one side. Jonathan and I will stand opposite.” The troops agreed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:41
Saul prayed, “LORD, Israel’s God, why no answer today? If the fault is with Jonathan or me, answer with Urim. If Israel’s men are guilty, answer with Thummim.” Saul and Jonathan were selected. The people were cleared. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 14:42
Saul ordered, “Cast the lot between Jonathan and me.” Jonathan was selected. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:43
Saul demanded, “What did you do?” Jonathan answered, “I tasted a little honey with my staff’s end. Must I die now?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:44
Saul declared, “May God punish me very severely if you do not die, Jonathan!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:45
The people said, “Must Jonathan die after this great deliverance for Israel? Never! As the LORD lives, not one hair will fall from his head. He acted with God’s help today.” They rescued Jonathan, and he did not die. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:46
Saul stopped pursuing the Philistines. They returned to their land. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:47
After becoming king, Saul fought enemies on every side. These included Moabites, Ammonites, Edomites, Zobah’s kings, and Philistines. Wherever he turned, he defeated them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:48
He fought bravely and defeated the Amalekites. He rescued Israel from plunderers. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:49
Saul’s sons were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchishua. His daughters were Merab, the firstborn, and Michal, the younger. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:50
His wife was Ahinoam, Ahimaaz’s daughter. Abner commanded his army. Abner was the son of Ner, Saul’s uncle. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:51
Kish, Saul’s father, and Ner, Abner’s father, were Abiel’s sons. 📖

## 1 Samuel 14:52
Saul’s war with the Philistines was fierce throughout his days. He enlisted every strong or brave man he noticed. 📖
