## 1 Samuel 24:1
Saul returned from pursuing the Philistines. He heard David was in En-gedi’s wilderness. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:2
He took three thousand chosen Israelites to search for David and his men near the Rocks of the Wild Goats. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:3
Saul reached roadside sheepfolds and entered a cave to relieve himself. David and his men hid deep inside. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:4
David’s men said, “This is the day the LORD promised to deliver your enemy into your hand to treat as you wish.” David crept forward and secretly cut a corner from Saul’s robe. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:5
Afterward, his conscience troubled him for cutting Saul’s robe. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:6
He told his men, “May the LORD forbid me to harm my master, His anointed! I must never raise my hand against the LORD’s anointed.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:7
David restrained his men with these words and prevented their attack. Saul left the cave and continued on his way. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:8
David left the cave and called, “My lord the king!” Saul turned. David bowed facedown in reverence. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:9
He asked, “Why believe people who say I intend to harm you? 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:10
Today the LORD delivered you into my hand in the cave. Others urged me to kill you. I spared you because you are the LORD’s anointed. I said I would not raise my hand against my lord. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:11
My father, see your robe’s corner in my hand. I cut it but did not kill you. I have no evil or rebellion in my hands. I did not sin against you, though you hunt my life. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:12
May the LORD judge between us and avenge me on you. My hand will never oppose you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:13
The old proverb says, ‘Wickedness comes from the wicked.’ My hand will never oppose you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:14
Whom does Israel’s king pursue? A dead dog? A flea? 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:15
May the LORD judge between us. May He notice, plead my case, and rescue me from your hand.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:16
Saul answered, “Is that your voice, my son David?” He wept loudly. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:17
He said, “You are more righteous than I. You returned good for my evil. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:18
Today you showed your goodness by sparing me when the LORD delivered me into your hand. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:19
Does someone find his enemy and let him leave unharmed? May the LORD reward your goodness to me today. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:20
I now know you will be king. Israel’s kingdom will be established in your hands. 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:21
Swear by the LORD not to destroy my descendants or erase my name from my father’s house.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 24:22
David swore to Saul. Saul returned home. David and his men went to the stronghold. 📖
