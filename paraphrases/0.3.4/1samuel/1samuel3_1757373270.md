## 1 Samuel 3:1
The boy Samuel served the LORD before Eli. The LORD’s word was rare then, and visions were scarce. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:2
Eli lay in his room. His eyesight had become so dim that he could not see. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:3
God’s lamp had not yet gone out. Samuel lay in the LORD’s temple, where God’s ark stood. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:4
The LORD called Samuel. He answered, “Here I am.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:5
Samuel ran to Eli and said, “Here I am. You called me.” Eli answered, “I did not call. Lie down again.” Samuel went and lay down. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:6
The LORD called again, “Samuel!” Samuel went to Eli and said, “Here I am. You called me.” Eli answered, “My son, I did not call. Lie down again.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:7
Samuel did not yet know the LORD. The LORD’s word had not yet been revealed to him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:8
The LORD called Samuel a third time. Samuel went to Eli and said, “Here I am. You called me.” Eli realized the LORD was calling the boy. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:9
Eli said, “Lie down. If He calls, say, ‘Speak, LORD. Your servant is listening.’” Samuel went and lay in his place. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:10
The LORD came and stood there. As before, He called, “Samuel! Samuel!” Samuel answered, “Speak. Your servant is listening.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:11
The LORD said, “I will do something in Israel that makes every hearer’s ears tingle. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:12
That day, I will fulfill everything I said against Eli’s house, from beginning to end. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:13
I told Eli I would judge his house forever for the sin he knows about. His sons blasphemed God, and he did not restrain them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:14
Therefore, I swore to Eli’s house, ‘No sacrifice or offering will ever atone for Eli’s house’s sin.’” 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:15
Samuel lay until morning, then opened the LORD’s house’s doors. He feared telling Eli the vision. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:16
Eli called, “Samuel, my son.” Samuel answered, “Here I am.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:17
Eli asked, “What did He tell you? Hide nothing. May God punish you very severely if you hide any of His words from me.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:18
Samuel told everything and hid nothing. Eli said, “He is the LORD. Let Him do what He sees as good.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:19
Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him. He let none of Samuel’s words fall to the ground. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:20
All Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, knew Samuel was confirmed as the LORD’s prophet. 📖

## 1 Samuel 3:21
The LORD continued appearing at Shiloh. There He revealed Himself to Samuel through His word. 📖
