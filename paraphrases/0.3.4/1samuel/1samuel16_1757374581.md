## 1 Samuel 16:1
The LORD told Samuel, “How long will you mourn Saul, whom I rejected as Israel’s king? Fill your horn with oil. Visit Jesse of Bethlehem. I chose a king from his sons.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:2
Samuel asked, “How can I go? Saul will hear and kill me!” The LORD answered, “Take a heifer. Say you came to sacrifice to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:3
Invite Jesse. I will show you what to do. Anoint the one I indicate.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:4
Samuel obeyed and went to Bethlehem. The town’s elders trembled as they met him. They asked, “Do you come peacefully?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:5
He answered, “Yes. I came to sacrifice to the LORD. Consecrate yourselves and join me.” He consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:6
They arrived. Samuel saw Eliab and thought, “Surely the LORD’s anointed stands here before Him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:7
The LORD said, “Ignore his appearance and height. I rejected him. The LORD sees differently from people. People see outward appearance, but the LORD sees the heart.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:8
Jesse presented Abinadab. Samuel said, “The LORD did not choose him either.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:9
Jesse presented Shammah. Samuel said, “The LORD did not choose him either.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:10
Jesse presented seven sons. Samuel said, “The LORD chose none of these.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:11
Samuel asked, “Are these all your sons?” Jesse answered, “The youngest remains, tending sheep.” Samuel said, “Send for him. We will not sit to eat until he arrives.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:12
Jesse brought his youngest son. He had a ruddy complexion, beautiful eyes, and a handsome appearance. The LORD said, “Rise and anoint him. He is the one.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:13
Samuel anointed David with the horn of oil before his brothers. The LORD’s Spirit rushed upon David from that day onward. Samuel returned to Ramah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:14
The LORD’s Spirit left Saul. A spirit of distress from the LORD began tormenting him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:15
Saul’s servants said, “A spirit of distress from God torments you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:16
Command us to find a skilled harp player. When the spirit from God comes upon you, he can play, and you will feel well.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:17
Saul ordered, “Find a good player and bring him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:18
A servant answered, “I saw Jesse of Bethlehem’s son. He plays the harp well and is a mighty warrior, eloquent and handsome. The LORD is with him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:19
Saul sent messengers to Jesse. “Send your son David, who tends the sheep.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:20
Jesse sent David with a donkey loaded with bread, a skin of wine, and one young goat for Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:21
David entered Saul’s service. Saul loved him greatly. David became his armor-bearer. 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:22
Saul told Jesse, “Let David stay in my service. I am pleased with him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 16:23
Whenever the spirit from God came upon Saul, David played his harp. Saul found relief and felt better. The spirit of distress left him. 📖
