## 1 Samuel 4:1
Samuel’s word came to all Israel. Israel marched to fight the Philistines and camped at Ebenezer. The Philistines camped at Aphek. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:2
The Philistines formed their battle line against Israel. As the battle spread, they defeated Israel and killed about four thousand men on the battlefield. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:3
The troops returned to camp. Israel’s elders asked, “Why did the LORD bring defeat on us before the Philistines today? Let us bring the ark of His covenant from Shiloh. It may go with us and save us from our enemies.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:4
They sent men to Shiloh for the ark of the LORD of Hosts’ covenant. He is enthroned between the cherubim. Eli’s sons Hophni and Phinehas accompanied the ark of God’s covenant. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:5
The ark of the LORD’s covenant entered camp. All Israel shouted so loudly that the ground shook. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:6
The Philistines heard and asked, “What is this loud shouting in the Hebrews’ camp?” They learned the LORD’s ark had entered the camp. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:7
They feared and said, “The gods entered their camp! Woe to us! Nothing like this happened before. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:8
Woe to us! Who can rescue us from these mighty gods? They struck the Egyptians with all kinds of plagues in the wilderness. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:9
Philistines, take courage and act like men! Otherwise, you will serve the Hebrews as they served you. Be men and fight!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:10
The Philistines fought and defeated Israel. Each Israelite fled to his tent. The slaughter was great. Thirty thousand Israelite foot soldiers died. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:11
God’s ark was captured. Eli’s sons Hophni and Phinehas died. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:12
That day, a Benjamite ran from the battle line to Shiloh. His clothes were torn, and dirt covered his head. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:13
Eli sat watching beside the road. His heart trembled for God’s ark. The man entered the city and reported what happened. The whole city cried out. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:14
Eli heard and asked, “Why this uproar?” The man hurried to report to him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:15
Eli was ninety-eight years old. His eyes were fixed because he could not see. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:16
The man said, “I came from the battle. I fled today.” Eli asked, “What happened, my son?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:17
He answered, “Israel fled from the Philistines, and many were slaughtered. Your sons Hophni and Phinehas are dead. God’s ark was captured.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:18
When God’s ark was mentioned, Eli fell backward from his chair by the city gate. He was old and heavy. His neck broke, and he died. He had judged Israel forty years. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:19
Phinehas’ wife, Eli’s daughter-in-law, was pregnant and near childbirth. She heard God’s ark was captured and her husband and father-in-law were dead. Labor pains overtook her. She collapsed and gave birth. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:20
As she died, her attendants said, “Do not fear. You have a son!” She neither answered nor paid attention. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:21
She named the boy Ichabod, saying, “Glory has left Israel.” She said this because God’s ark was captured and her husband and father-in-law had been killed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 4:22
She said, “Glory has left Israel because God’s ark was captured.” 📖
