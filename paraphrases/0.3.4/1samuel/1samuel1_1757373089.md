## 1 Samuel 1:1
Elkanah was from Ramathaim-zophim in Ephraim’s hill country. He was Jeroham’s son, Jeroham was Elihu’s son, Elihu was Tohu’s son, and Tohu was Zuph’s son. He was an Ephraimite. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:2
He had two wives, Hannah and Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:3
Every year, Elkanah left his city to worship and sacrifice to the LORD of Hosts at Shiloh. Eli’s sons Hophni and Phinehas served there as the LORD’s priests. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:4
Whenever Elkanah sacrificed, he gave portions to Peninnah and all her sons and daughters. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:5
He gave Hannah a double portion because he loved her, although the LORD had closed her womb. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:6
Her rival cruelly provoked and mocked her because the LORD had closed her womb. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:7
This continued every year. Whenever Hannah went to the LORD’s house, her rival mocked her until she cried and refused food. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:8
Elkanah asked, “Hannah, why cry? Why refuse food? Why is your heart so sad? Am I not better to you than ten sons?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:9
After they finished eating and drinking at Shiloh, Hannah stood. Eli the priest sat on a chair beside the LORD’s temple’s doorpost. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:10
Hannah was deeply distressed. She prayed to the LORD and cried many tears. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 1:11
She vowed, “LORD of Hosts, look at Your maidservant’s suffering. Remember me and do not forget me. Give me a son. Then I will dedicate him to You all his life. No razor will ever touch his head.” 🙏

## 1 Samuel 1:12
Hannah continued praying before the LORD. Eli watched her mouth. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:13
She prayed in her heart. Her lips moved, but her voice was silent. Eli thought she was drunk. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:14
He said, “How long will you stay drunk? Put away your wine!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:15
Hannah answered, “No, my lord. My spirit is troubled. I drank neither wine nor strong drink. I poured out my soul before the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:16
Do not think your servant is a wicked woman. I have prayed all this time from deep anguish and grief.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:17
Eli answered, “Go in peace. May Israel’s God grant what you asked Him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:18
Hannah said, “May your maidservant find favor with you.” She left and began eating. Her face was no longer sad. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:19
Early the next morning, they bowed in worship before the LORD. They returned home to Ramah. Elkanah slept with Hannah, and the LORD remembered her. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:20
In time, Hannah conceived and bore a son. She named him Samuel because she had asked the LORD for him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:21
Elkanah and his whole household went to offer the annual sacrifice to the LORD and fulfill his vow. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:22
Hannah stayed home. She told her husband, “After the boy is weaned, I will take him before the LORD. He will stay there permanently.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:23
Elkanah answered, “Do what you think best. Stay until you wean him. May the LORD confirm His word.” Hannah stayed and nursed her son until she weaned him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:24
After weaning him, Hannah brought the young boy to the LORD’s house at Shiloh. She also brought a bull aged three years, an ephah of flour, and a skin of wine. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:25
They slaughtered the bull and brought the boy to Eli. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:26
Hannah said, “Please, my lord, as surely as you live, I am the woman who stood beside you here praying to the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:27
I prayed for this boy. The LORD granted what I asked. 📖

## 1 Samuel 1:28
So I now dedicate him to the LORD. He is dedicated to the LORD for his whole life.” They worshiped the LORD there. 📖
