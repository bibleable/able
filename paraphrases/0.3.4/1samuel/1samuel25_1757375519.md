## 1 Samuel 25:1
Samuel died. All Israel gathered to mourn and buried him at home in Ramah. David went to Paran’s wilderness. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:2
A wealthy man lived in Maon and conducted business in Carmel. He owned a thousand goats and three thousand sheep. He was shearing sheep in Carmel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:3
His name was Nabal, and his wife was Abigail. She was intelligent and beautiful. He was a Calebite, harsh and evil in his dealings. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:4
David heard in the wilderness that Nabal was shearing sheep. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:5
He sent ten young men. “Go to Nabal at Carmel and greet him in my name. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:6
Wish him long life and peace for himself, his household, and everything he owns. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:7
Say, ‘I hear you are shearing. We never harassed your shepherds in Carmel. Nothing of theirs went missing throughout their time with us. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:8
Ask your young men, and they will confirm it. Favor my young men on this feast day. Give what you can spare to your servants and your son David.’” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:9
David’s men delivered the whole message to Nabal and waited. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:10
Nabal answered, “Who is David, Jesse’s son? Many servants escape their masters these days. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:11
Why give my bread, water, and my shearers’ meat to men whose origin I do not know?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:12
David’s men returned and told him everything. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:13
David ordered, “Strap on your swords!” He and his men did so. About four hundred followed him. Two hundred guarded the supplies. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:14
A young man told Abigail, “David sent wilderness messengers to greet our master. Nabal screamed at them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:15
These men treated us well. They never harassed us, and nothing went missing while we stayed among them in the fields. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:16
They were a protective wall around us, day and night, while we herded sheep near them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:17
Consider what to do. Disaster threatens our master and his household. He is such a scoundrel that no one can speak to him!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:18
Abigail hurried, taking two hundred bread loaves, two wine skins, five butchered sheep, and five seahs of roasted grain. She also took a hundred raisin clusters and two hundred fig cakes and loaded them on donkeys. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:19
She told her young men, “Go ahead. I will follow.” She did not tell Nabal. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:20
She rode her donkey into a mountain ravine. David and his men approached, and she met them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:21
David had just said, “I guarded his wilderness property in vain. Nothing went missing. He repaid my good with evil. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:22
May God punish David severely if one male belonging to Nabal survives until morning.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:23
Abigail saw David and quickly dismounted. She fell facedown and bowed before him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:24
She fell at his feet. “My lord, blame me alone. Let your servant speak. Please listen. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:25
Ignore this scoundrel Nabal. His name means Fool, and folly follows him. I did not see the young men you sent. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:26
As the LORD and you live, He held you back from bloodshed and personal revenge. May your enemies and everyone seeking your harm be like Nabal. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:27
Give my gift to the young men who follow you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:28
Forgive your servant’s offense. The LORD will build you a lasting dynasty because you fight His battles. May no evil be found in you throughout your life. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:29
If someone pursues your life, the LORD your God will bind it securely in the bundle of the living. He will fling your enemies’ lives away like stones from a sling. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:30
The LORD will fulfill His good promises and make you Israel’s ruler. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:31
Then you will have no remorse or guilt from needless bloodshed and revenge. When He treats you well, remember your servant.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:32
David said, “Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God, who sent you today! 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:33
Blessed are your discernment and you. You stopped my bloodshed and personal revenge today. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:34
As the LORD, Israel’s God, lives, He restrained me from harming you. Without your quick arrival, no male belonging to Nabal would survive until dawn.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:35
David accepted her gifts. He said, “Return home peacefully. I listened and granted your request.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:36
Abigail returned to Nabal. He held a feast fit for a king, cheerful and very drunk. She told him nothing until morning. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:37
He was sober in the morning. Abigail told him everything. His heart failed, and he became like stone. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:38
About ten days later, the LORD struck Nabal dead. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:39
David heard and said, “Blessed be the LORD! He defended my cause against Nabal’s insult and restrained me from evil. He returned Nabal’s wickedness upon his own head.” David sent a marriage proposal to Abigail. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:40
His servants reached Abigail in Carmel. “David sent us to take you as his wife.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:41
She rose and bowed facedown. “Your servant is ready to serve and wash my lord’s servants’ feet.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:42
Abigail hurried onto a donkey. Five maidens accompanied her. She followed David’s messengers and became his wife. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:43
David also married Ahinoam of Jezreel. Both women were his wives. 📖

## 1 Samuel 25:44
Saul gave Michal, his daughter and David’s wife, to Palti, Laish’s son from Gallim. 📖
