## 1 Samuel 23:1
David heard, “The Philistines attack Keilah and plunder its threshing floors.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:2
He asked the LORD, “Should I attack them?” The LORD answered, “Attack the Philistines and save Keilah.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:3
His men said, “We fear even here in Judah. How much more if we face Philistine armies at Keilah?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:4
David asked the LORD again. He answered, “Go to Keilah immediately. I will deliver the Philistines into your hand.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:5
David and his men fought at Keilah. They struck the Philistines powerfully and took their livestock. David saved Keilah’s people. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:6
Abiathar, Ahimelech’s son, brought the ephod when he fled to David at Keilah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:7
Saul heard David entered Keilah. He said, “God delivered him into my hand. He trapped himself in a town with gates and bars.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:8
Saul gathered all his troops to besiege David and his men at Keilah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:9
David learned Saul plotted evil. He told Abiathar the priest, “Bring the ephod.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:10
David prayed, “LORD, Israel’s God, I heard Saul plans to destroy Keilah because of me. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 23:11
Will its citizens surrender me? Will Saul come, as I heard? LORD, Israel’s God, please tell Your servant.” The LORD answered, “He will come.” 🙏

## 1 Samuel 23:12
David asked, “Will Keilah’s citizens surrender me and my men to Saul?” The LORD answered, “They will.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:13
David and about six hundred men left Keilah, moving from place to place. Saul heard they escaped and canceled his advance. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:14
David stayed in wilderness strongholds and Ziph’s wilderness hill country. Saul searched daily. God did not deliver David into his hand. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:15
At Horesh in Ziph’s wilderness, David saw Saul had come to kill him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:16
Jonathan, Saul’s son, visited David at Horesh. He strengthened David’s hand in God. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:17
He said, “Do not fear. My father Saul will never seize you. You will rule Israel, and I will be second in command. My father knows this too.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:18
They made a covenant before the LORD. David stayed in Horesh, and Jonathan went home. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:19
The Ziphites told Saul at Gibeah, “David hides among us at Horesh’s strongholds, on Hachilah’s hill south of Jeshimon. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:20
Come whenever you wish, king. We will deliver him into your hand.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:21
Saul answered, “May the LORD bless you for your compassion toward me. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:22
Prepare further. Watch where he goes and who sees him. I hear he is extremely cunning. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:23
Find every hiding place. Return with certainty, and I will join you. If he is in the land, I will search all Judah’s clans.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:24
They went to Ziph before Saul. David and his men were in Maon’s wilderness, in the Arabah south of Jeshimon. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:25
Saul and his men searched for David. David heard and went down to the rock in Maon’s wilderness. Saul learned this and pursued him there. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:26
Saul moved along one mountainside, while David and his men moved along the other. David hurried away. Saul’s men closed in to capture them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:27
A messenger told Saul, “Hurry! The Philistines raided the land!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:28
Saul stopped pursuing David and went against the Philistines. That is why the place is called Sela-hammahlekoth. 📖

## 1 Samuel 23:29
David went from there to En-gedi’s strongholds. 📖
