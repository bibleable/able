## 1 Samuel 22:1
David left Gath and sheltered in Adullam’s cave. His brothers and his father’s household heard and joined him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:2
Distressed, indebted, and discontented people gathered to him. He led about four hundred men. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:3
David went to Mizpeh of Moab. He asked Moab’s king, “Let my parents stay with you until I learn what God will do for me.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:4
He entrusted them to Moab’s king. They stayed throughout David’s time in the stronghold. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:5
Gad the prophet said, “Leave the stronghold. Go to Judah.” David went to Hereth’s forest. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:6
Saul heard David and his men were found. He sat under the tamarisk tree on Gibeah’s hill, holding his spear. His servants stood around him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:7
Saul said, “Benjamites, listen! Will Jesse’s son give you fields and vineyards and make you commanders of thousands or hundreds? 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:8
Have you all conspired against me? None told me my son made a covenant with Jesse’s son. None cares for me. None revealed my son stirred my servant to ambush me, as he does today.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:9
Doeg the Edomite stood with Saul’s servants. He said, “I saw Jesse’s son visit Ahimelech, Ahitub’s son, at Nob. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:10
Ahimelech inquired of the LORD for him and gave him supplies. He gave him Goliath the Philistine’s sword.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:11
Saul summoned Ahimelech, Ahitub’s son, and his father’s entire priestly family from Nob. They all came. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:12
Saul said, “Listen, Ahitub’s son.” Ahimelech answered, “Here I am, my lord.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:13
Saul asked, “Why conspire with Jesse’s son against me? You gave bread and a sword and inquired of God for him. You helped him rise and ambush me, as he does today.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:14
Ahimelech answered, “Who serves you more faithfully than David? He is your son-in-law, your bodyguard’s captain, and honored in your house. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:15
Was this my first inquiry of God for him? Far from it! Accuse neither me nor my father’s household. I knew nothing of this affair, in whole or part.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:16
Saul said, “Ahimelech, you and all your father’s house will die!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:17
He ordered his guards, “Kill the LORD’s priests! They sided with David. They knew he fled but did not tell me.” The servants refused to strike the LORD’s priests. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:18
Saul ordered Doeg to kill them. Doeg the Edomite killed eighty-five men wearing linen ephods that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:19
He also killed Nob’s men, women, children, infants, oxen, donkeys, and sheep with the sword. Nob was the priests’ city. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:20
Abiathar, a son of Ahimelech, Ahitub’s son, escaped to David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:21
Abiathar reported that Saul killed the LORD’s priests. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:22
David said, “I knew Doeg the Edomite would tell Saul when I saw him that day. I am responsible for the lives of everyone in your father’s house. 📖

## 1 Samuel 22:23
Stay with me without fear. The one seeking your life also seeks mine. You will be safe with me.” 📖
