## 1 Samuel 2:1
Hannah prayed, “My heart rejoices in the LORD. My horn is lifted high in the LORD. My mouth speaks boldly against my enemies because I rejoice in Your salvation. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:2
No one is holy like the LORD. There is no one besides You! No Rock is like our God. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:3
Do not boast proudly or speak arrogantly. The LORD is a God who knows. He weighs actions. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:4
The mighty people’s bows are broken. But the weak are equipped with strength. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:5
The well-fed hire themselves out for food, while the starving hunger no longer. The barren woman bears seven children. But the woman with many sons wastes away. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:6
The LORD brings death and gives life. He brings down to Sheol and raises up. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:7
The LORD sends poverty and wealth. He humbles and exalts. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:8
He raises poor people from the dust and needy people from the ash heap. He seats them with princes and gives them a throne of honor. Earth’s foundations belong to the LORD. He set the world upon them. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:9
He guards His faithful ones’ steps. The wicked perish in darkness. No one prevails by his own strength. 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:10
The LORD’s opponents will be shattered. He will thunder against them from heaven. He will judge earth’s ends and strengthen His king. He will exalt His anointed one’s horn.” 🙏

## 1 Samuel 2:11
Elkanah returned home to Ramah. The boy began serving the LORD before Eli the priest. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:12
Eli’s sons were wicked. They had no regard for the LORD. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:13
They also disregarded the priests’ custom with the people. When someone sacrificed, a priest’s servant came with a meat fork with three prongs while the meat boiled. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:14
He plunged it into the pan, kettle, cauldron, or cooking pot. The priest claimed whatever the fork brought up. They treated all Israelites coming to Shiloh this way. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:15
Even before the fat burned, the servant told the person sacrificing, “Give the priest meat to roast. He accepts only raw meat from you, not boiled meat.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:16
The person might say, “The fat must burn first. Then take whatever you want.” The servant replied, “No, give it now. Otherwise, I will take it by force!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:17
These young men’s sin was severe in the LORD’s sight. They treated His offering with contempt. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:18
Samuel served before the LORD as a boy wearing a linen ephod. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:19
His mother made him a little robe every year. She brought it when she and her husband came for the annual sacrifice. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:20
Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife. He said, “May the LORD give you children by this woman in place of the one she dedicated to Him.” They returned home. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:21
The LORD attended to Hannah. She conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. Samuel grew up in the LORD’s presence. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:22
Eli was very old. He heard everything his sons did to Israel. He also heard they slept with women serving at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:23
He asked them, “Why do these things? All these people tell me about your wicked deeds. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:24
No, my sons. The report spreading among the LORD’s people is not good. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:25
If someone sins against another person, God can intercede. But who can intercede when someone sins against the LORD?” They refused their father’s warning because the LORD intended to kill them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:26
Samuel continued growing in stature and in favor with the LORD and people. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:27
A man of God told Eli, “The LORD says, ‘Did I not clearly reveal Myself to your father’s house in Egypt, under Pharaoh’s house? 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:28
I chose your father from all Israel’s tribes as My priest. He would sacrifice on My altar, burn incense, and wear an ephod before Me. I gave your father’s house all Israel’s food offerings. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:29
Why kick at the sacrifices and offerings I prescribed for My dwelling? You honor your sons above Me. You fatten yourselves with the best of My people Israel’s offerings.’ 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:30
Israel’s God, the LORD, declares, ‘I promised your house and your father’s house would walk before Me forever. Now the LORD says, “Far be it from Me!” I honor those honoring Me. Those despising Me will be disdained. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:31
Days are coming when I will cut off your strength and your father’s house’s strength. No one in it will reach old age. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:32
You will see distress in My dwelling. Despite all Israel’s good, no one in your house will ever again reach old age. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:33
For every one of you I do not cut off from My altar, I will cause your eyes to fail and your heart to grieve. All your descendants will die by human swords. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:34
Your two sons Hophni and Phinehas will both die the same day. This will be your sign. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:35
I will raise up a faithful priest for Myself. He will do everything in My heart and mind. I will build him an enduring house. He will walk before My anointed one for all time. 📖

## 1 Samuel 2:36
Everyone left in your house will bow to him for silver or bread. They will plead, “Please appoint me to a priestly office so I can eat a piece of bread.”’” 📖
