## 1 Samuel 28:1
The Philistines gathered for war against Israel. Achish told David, “You and your men must fight with me.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:2
David answered, “You will see what your servant can do.” Achish said, “I will make you my lifelong bodyguard.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:3
Samuel had died. All Israel mourned and buried him in Ramah, his city. Saul had removed mediums and spiritists from the land. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:4
The Philistines camped at Shunem. Saul gathered all Israel and camped at Gilboa. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:5
Saul saw the Philistine army and trembled violently with fear. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:6
He inquired of the LORD. The LORD answered neither through dreams, Urim, nor prophets. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:7
Saul told his servants, “Find a woman who is a medium so I can consult her.” They answered, “A medium lives at Endor.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:8
Saul disguised himself with different clothes. He visited her at night with two men. He said, “Consult a spirit and bring up the person I name.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:9
She answered, “You know Saul killed the mediums and spiritists. Why trap me and get me killed?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:10
Saul swore by the LORD, “As the LORD lives, you will not be punished for this.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:11
She asked whom to bring up. He answered, “Samuel.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:12
She saw Samuel and cried loudly. She told Saul, “You deceived me! You are Saul!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:13
He said, “Do not fear. What do you see?” She answered, “A god coming from the earth.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:14
Saul asked his appearance. She said, “An old man wearing a robe.” Saul knew it was Samuel and bowed facedown in reverence. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:15
Samuel asked, “Why disturb me by bringing me up?” Saul answered, “I am deeply distressed. The Philistines fight me. God turned away and answers neither through prophets nor dreams. I called you for guidance.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:16
Samuel asked, “Why consult me when the LORD turned away and became your enemy? 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:17
He fulfilled His words through me. He tore the kingdom from you and gave it to your neighbor David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:18
You disobeyed the LORD and failed to carry out His burning anger against Amalek. That is why He did this today. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:19
The LORD will deliver you and Israel to the Philistines. Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. He will deliver Israel’s army to the Philistines.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:20
Saul immediately fell flat, terrified by Samuel’s words. He had no strength because he ate nothing all day and night. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:21
The woman saw his distress. She said, “Your maidservant obeyed you and risked her life. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:22
Listen to me now. Let me give you bread to strengthen you for your journey.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:23
Saul refused to eat. His servants and the woman urged him until he agreed. He rose and sat on the bed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:24
She quickly slaughtered her fattened calf. She kneaded flour and baked unleavened bread. 📖

## 1 Samuel 28:25
She served Saul and his servants. They ate and departed that night. 📖
