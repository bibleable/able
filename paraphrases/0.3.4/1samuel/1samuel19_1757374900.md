## 1 Samuel 19:1
Saul ordered Jonathan and all his servants to kill David. Jonathan greatly delighted in David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:2
He warned David, “My father Saul means to kill you. Be careful tomorrow morning. Find a secret hiding place. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:3
I will stand beside my father in the field where you are and ask about you. I will tell you anything I learn.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:4
Jonathan spoke well of David to Saul. “Do not sin against your servant David. He did not sin against you. His deeds greatly benefited you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:5
He risked his life to kill the Philistine. The LORD brought great salvation to Israel. You saw and rejoiced. Why shed innocent blood by killing David without cause?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:6
Saul listened and swore, “As surely as the LORD lives, David will not die.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:7
Jonathan called David and told him everything. He brought David to Saul. David served Saul as before. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:8
War began again. David struck the Philistines so powerfully that they fled. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:9
Saul sat at home holding his spear. A spirit of distress from the LORD came upon him while David played the harp. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:10
Saul tried to pin David to the wall. David escaped, and the spear hit the wall. He fled that night. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:11
Saul sent messengers to watch David’s house and kill him in the morning. Michal warned David, “Escape tonight, or tomorrow you will die!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:12
Michal lowered David through the window. He fled and escaped. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:13
She put a household idol in the bed. She put goat hair on its head and covered it with a garment. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:14
Saul’s messengers came to seize David. Michal said, “He is sick.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:15
Saul sent them back to see David. “Bring him in his bed so I can kill him.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:16
They entered and found the idol with goat hair on its head in the bed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:17
Saul asked Michal, “Why deceive me and let my enemy escape?” She answered, “He said, ‘Help me escape, or I will kill you!’” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:18
David escaped to Samuel in Ramah. He told him everything Saul did. They went to Naioth and stayed there. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:19
Saul heard David was at Naioth in Ramah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:20
He sent messengers to seize him. They saw prophets prophesying under Samuel’s leadership. God’s Spirit came upon the messengers. They also prophesied. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:21
Saul heard and sent more messengers. They also prophesied. He sent a third group, and they too prophesied. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:22
Saul went to the large cistern at Secu, on his way to Ramah. He asked, “Where are Samuel and David?” Someone answered, “At Naioth in Ramah.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:23
Saul headed toward Naioth in Ramah. God’s Spirit came upon him too. He prophesied as he walked until he arrived. 📖

## 1 Samuel 19:24
He removed his robes and prophesied before Samuel. He collapsed and lay naked all day and night. That is why people say, “Is Saul also among the prophets?” 📖
