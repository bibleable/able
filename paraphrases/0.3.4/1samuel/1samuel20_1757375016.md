## 1 Samuel 20:1
David fled Naioth in Ramah and came to Jonathan. He asked, “What did I do? What is my sin? How did I sin against your father, that he wants to kill me?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:2
Jonathan answered, “Never! You will not die. My father tells me everything, great or small. Why hide this from me? It cannot be true!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:3
David vowed, “Your father knows you favor me. He means to hide this so you do not grieve. As surely as the LORD and you live, I stand one step from death.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:4
Jonathan said, “I will do whatever you want.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:5
David said, “Tomorrow is the New Moon. I should eat with the king. Let me hide in the field until the third evening. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:6
If your father misses me, say I urgently asked permission to visit Bethlehem. My whole clan has its annual sacrifice in my hometown. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:7
If he approves, I am safe. If he becomes furious, you will know he intends evil. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:8
Be kind to your servant. You brought me into a covenant before the LORD. If I am guilty, kill me yourself. Why take me to your father?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:9
Jonathan said, “Never! If I knew my father intended evil, would I not tell you?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:10
David asked, “Who will tell me if your father answers harshly?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:11
Jonathan said, “Let us go to the field.” They went together. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:12
Jonathan said, “By the LORD, Israel’s God, I will question my father by this time tomorrow or the next day. If he favors you, I will send word. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:13
If he intends evil, may the LORD punish me severely if I fail to warn you and send you away safely. May the LORD be with you as He was with my father. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:14
While I live, treat me with the LORD’s loving devotion so I do not die. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:15
Never withdraw your loving devotion from my household, even when the LORD removes all David’s enemies from the earth.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:16
Jonathan made a covenant with David’s house. He said, “May the LORD hold David’s enemies accountable.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:17
Jonathan asked David to reaffirm his vow because he loved him. He loved David as himself. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:18
Jonathan said, “Tomorrow is the New Moon. Your empty seat will make people miss you. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:19
After three days, hurry to your hiding place from when this trouble began. Stay beside the stone called Ezel. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:20
I will shoot three arrows beside it, as though aiming at a target. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:21
I will send a boy to find them. If I clearly say, ‘The arrows are on this side of you, bring them,’ come. As the LORD lives, you are safe and face no danger. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:22
If I say, ‘The arrows are beyond you,’ leave. The LORD has sent you away. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:23
The LORD witnesses our agreement forever.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:24
David hid in the field. At the New Moon, the king sat to eat. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:25
He sat as usual by the wall, opposite Jonathan and beside Abner. David’s seat was empty. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:26
Saul said nothing that day. He thought something must have made David ceremonially unclean. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:27
On the second day, after the New Moon, David’s seat remained empty. Saul asked Jonathan, “Why did Jesse’s son miss yesterday’s and today’s meals?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:28
Jonathan answered, “David urgently asked my permission to visit Bethlehem. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:29
He said, ‘Our clan has a sacrifice in the city, and my brother told me to attend. If you favor me, let me see my brothers.’ That is why he missed your table.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:30
Saul became furious. “Son of a perverse, rebellious woman! You chose Jesse’s son to your shame and your mother’s shame. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:31
While Jesse’s son lives, neither you nor your kingdom will be established. Bring him here. He must die!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:32
Jonathan asked, “Why must he die? What did he do?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:33
Saul threw his spear to kill Jonathan. Jonathan knew his father intended to kill David. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:34
Jonathan left the table furiously. He ate nothing that second day of the month. His father’s shameful treatment of David grieved him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:35
In the morning, Jonathan went to meet David in the field. A small boy accompanied him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:36
He told the boy, “Run and find my arrows.” As the boy ran, Jonathan shot an arrow beyond him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:37
The boy reached the arrow’s landing place. Jonathan called, “Is the arrow not beyond you?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:38
He shouted, “Hurry! Do not delay!” The boy collected the arrow and returned. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:39
The boy knew nothing of the arrangement. Only Jonathan and David knew. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:40
Jonathan gave his equipment to the boy. “Take this to the city.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:41
The boy left. David rose south of the stone, fell facedown, and bowed three times. He and Jonathan kissed and wept. David wept more. 📖

## 1 Samuel 20:42
Jonathan said, “Go peacefully. We swore in the LORD’s name that He witnesses between us and our descendants forever.” David left, and Jonathan returned to the city. 📖
