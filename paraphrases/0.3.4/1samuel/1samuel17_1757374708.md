## 1 Samuel 17:1
The Philistines gathered for war at Socoh in Judah. They camped at Ephes-dammim, between Socoh and Azekah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:2
Saul and Israel’s men camped in the Valley of Elah. They formed their battle line against the Philistines. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:3
The armies stood on opposite hills, with the valley between them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:4
Goliath, a champion from Gath, left the Philistine camp. He was six cubits and a span tall. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:5
He wore a bronze helmet and bronze coat of mail. The coat weighed five thousand shekels. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:6
Bronze armor covered his legs. A bronze javelin hung between his shoulders. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:7
His spear shaft resembled a weaver’s beam. Its iron point weighed six hundred shekels. His shield-bearer went ahead. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:8
Goliath shouted to Israel’s ranks, “Why form battle lines? Am I not a Philistine, and are you not Saul’s servants? Choose a man to fight me. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:9
If he kills me, we will serve you. If I defeat and kill him, you will serve and work for us.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:10
He said, “I defy Israel’s ranks today! Give me a man to fight!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:11
Saul and all Israel heard and became deeply afraid and dismayed. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:12
David’s father was Jesse, an Ephrathite from Bethlehem of Judah. He had eight sons. Jesse was old during Saul’s days. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:13
Jesse’s three oldest sons followed Saul into battle. Eliab was firstborn, Abinadab second, and Shammah third. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:14
David was youngest. The three oldest followed Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:15
David traveled between Saul and Bethlehem to tend his father’s sheep. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:16
For forty days, the Philistine took his stand each morning and evening. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:17
Jesse told David, “Take this ephah of roasted grain and ten bread loaves to your brothers. Hurry to their camp. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:18
Take ten portions of cheese to their unit’s commander. Check your brothers’ welfare and bring assurance from them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:19
They are with Saul and Israel’s men in the Valley of Elah, fighting the Philistines.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:20
David rose early, left the sheep with a keeper, loaded supplies, and obeyed Jesse. He reached camp as the army marched out shouting its battle cry. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:21
Israel and the Philistines formed opposing battle lines. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:22
David left his supplies with the quartermaster and ran to the battle line. He asked his brothers how they were. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:23
While they talked, Goliath, the Philistine champion from Gath, stepped forward. He shouted his usual challenge, and David heard. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:24
Israel’s men saw Goliath and fled in great fear. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:25
They said, “See how he keeps defying Israel! The king will richly reward whoever kills him. He will give his daughter in marriage and exempt the man’s father’s household from Israel’s taxes.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:26
David asked nearby men, “What reward will the man receive who kills this Philistine and removes Israel’s disgrace? Who is this uncircumcised Philistine to defy the living God’s armies?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:27
They repeated the offer for the man who killed him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:28
Eliab, David’s oldest brother, heard and became angry. “Why come here? With whom did you leave those few wilderness sheep? I know your pride and wicked heart. You came to watch the battle!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:29
David answered, “What did I do now? Was that not just a question?” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:30
He turned to another person and asked about the offer. The answer was the same. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:31
Someone reported David’s words to Saul. Saul sent for him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:32
David said, “Let no one lose heart because of this Philistine. Your servant will fight him!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:33
Saul answered, “You cannot fight him. You are a boy. He has fought since youth.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:34
David said, “I tended my father’s sheep. A lion or bear sometimes carried off a lamb. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:35
I pursued it, struck it, and rescued the lamb from its mouth. If it rose against me, I grabbed its fur, struck it, and killed it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:36
I killed lions and bears. This uncircumcised Philistine will be like them. He defied the living God’s armies. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:37
The LORD rescued me from lion and bear claws. He will rescue me from this Philistine.” Saul said, “Go. May the LORD be with you.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:38
Saul dressed David in his own tunic, a bronze helmet, and armor. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:39
David fastened his sword over the tunic and tried walking. He was unused to them. He said, “I cannot walk in these. I am unused to them.” He removed them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:40
David took his staff and chose five smooth stones from the brook. He put them in his shepherd’s bag’s pouch. Holding his sling, he approached the Philistine. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:41
The Philistine approached David, with his shield-bearer ahead. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:42
He saw David was a ruddy, handsome boy and despised him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:43
He asked, “Am I a dog that you approach with sticks?” He cursed David by his gods. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:44
He called, “Come here! I will feed your flesh to birds and wild animals!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:45
David answered, “You come with sword, spear, and javelin. I come in the name of the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s armies’ God, whom you defied. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:46
Today the LORD will deliver you into my hand. I will strike you down and cut off your head. I will give the Philistine army’s bodies to birds and creatures of the earth. The whole world will know Israel has a God. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:47
Everyone here will know the LORD saves without sword or spear. The battle belongs to Him. He will give you all into our hands.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:48
The Philistine advanced to attack. David ran quickly toward the battle line to meet him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:49
David took a stone from his bag and slung it into the Philistine’s forehead. It sank in, and he fell facedown. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:50
David defeated and killed the Philistine with a sling and stone. He held no sword. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:51
David ran and stood over him. He drew the Philistine’s sword, killed him, and cut off his head. The Philistines saw their hero was dead and fled. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:52
Israel’s and Judah’s men shouted and pursued them to Gath’s entrance and Ekron’s gates. Philistine bodies lay along Shaaraim’s road to Gath and Ekron. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:53
Israel returned from pursuit and plundered the Philistine camps. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:54
David brought the Philistine’s head to Jerusalem. He put Goliath’s weapons in his own tent. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:55
Saul watched David approach the Philistine and asked army commander Abner, “Whose son is this young man?” Abner answered, “As surely as you live, king, I do not know.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:56
The king ordered, “Find out whose son he is!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:57
David returned holding the Philistine’s head. Abner brought him before Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 17:58
Saul asked, “Whose son are you?” David answered, “Your servant Jesse of Bethlehem’s son.” 📖
