## 1 Samuel 11:1
Nahash the Ammonite besieged Jabesh-gilead. Jabesh’s men said, “Make a treaty with us. We will serve you.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:2
Nahash answered, “Only if I may put out everyone’s right eye and disgrace all Israel.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:3
Jabesh’s elders said, “Give us seven days to send messengers throughout Israel. If no one saves us, we will surrender.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:4
The messengers reached Gibeah of Saul and told the people. Everyone wept loudly. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:5
Saul returned from the field behind his oxen. He asked, “What troubles the people? Why do they cry?” They told him Jabesh’s message. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:6
Saul heard, and God’s Spirit rushed upon him. He burned with great anger. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:7
He cut a pair of oxen into pieces and sent them throughout Israel by messengers. “This will happen to anyone’s oxen if he does not march behind Saul and Samuel.” Terror from the LORD fell on the people. They gathered as one. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:8
Saul counted them at Bezek. There were 300,000 Israelites and 30,000 men of Judah. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:9
They told the messengers, “Tell Jabesh-gilead’s men they will be rescued tomorrow, by the time the sun is hot.” Jabesh’s men heard and rejoiced. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:10
They told Nahash, “Tomorrow we will come out. Do whatever seems good to you.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:11
The next day, Saul divided his troops into three groups. They attacked the Ammonite camp during the morning watch and slaughtered them until the hottest part of the day. The survivors scattered so widely that no two remained together. 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:12
The people told Samuel, “Who said Saul should not rule us? Bring them so we can kill them!” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:13
Saul ordered, “No one dies today. Today the LORD brought salvation to Israel.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:14
Samuel said, “Let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingship.” 📖

## 1 Samuel 11:15
Everyone went to Gilgal and confirmed Saul as king before the LORD. They sacrificed peace offerings there. Saul and all Israel rejoiced greatly. 📖
