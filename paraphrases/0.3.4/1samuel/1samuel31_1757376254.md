## 1 Samuel 31:1
The Philistines fought Israel. Israel’s men fled, and many died on Mount Gilboa. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:2
The Philistines closely pursued Saul and his sons. They killed his sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:3
The battle intensified around Saul. Archers overtook and critically wounded him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:4
Saul told his armor-bearer, “Draw your sword and run me through. Otherwise, these uncircumcised men will stab and torture me!” His armor-bearer was terrified and refused. Saul took his own sword and fell on it. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:5
His armor-bearer saw Saul was dead. He fell on his own sword and died with him. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:6
Saul, his three sons, his armor-bearer, and all his men died together that day. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:7
Israelites along the valley and beyond the Jordan saw their army flee and learned Saul and his sons had died. They abandoned their cities. The Philistines occupied them. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:8
The next day, the Philistines came to strip the dead. They found Saul and his three sons on Mount Gilboa. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:9
They cut off Saul’s head and removed his armor. Messengers announced the news throughout Philistine land, among their people and in their idols’ temples. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:10
They put his armor in the Ashtoreths’ temple. They hung his body on Beth-shan’s wall. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:11
Jabesh-gilead’s people heard what the Philistines did to Saul. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:12
Their brave men traveled all night and retrieved Saul’s and his sons’ bodies from Beth-shan’s wall. They returned to Jabesh and burned the bodies. 📖

## 1 Samuel 31:13
They buried the bones under Jabesh’s tamarisk tree and fasted seven days. 📖
