## 1 Timothy 3:1
This statement is trustworthy: Anyone who wants to be a church overseer desires a noble task. 👥
## 1 Timothy 3:2
An overseer must be free from blame and the husband of one wife. He must avoid excess, control himself, be respectable, welcome guests, and be able to teach. 👣
## 1 Timothy 3:3
He must not depend on wine or act violently. He must be gentle and peaceful, and free from love of money. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 3:4
He must manage his household well and keep his children under control with full dignity. 🏠
## 1 Timothy 3:5
If someone cannot manage his own household, how can he care for God’s church? 💬
## 1 Timothy 3:6
He must not be a new believer. Otherwise, he may become proud and receive the same condemnation as the devil. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 3:7
People outside the church must also respect him. Then he will not fall into disgrace or the devil’s trap. 🛡️
## 1 Timothy 3:8
Deacons must likewise behave with dignity. They must not speak deceitfully, drink too much wine, or be greedy for money. 👥
## 1 Timothy 3:9
They must hold to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience. 📖
## 1 Timothy 3:10
Test them first. If they are free from blame, let them serve as deacons. ⚖️
## 1 Timothy 3:11
Likewise, the women must behave with dignity and must not slander others. They must avoid excess and be faithful in everything. 👣
## 1 Timothy 3:12
A deacon must be the husband of one wife. He must manage his children and household well. 🏠
## 1 Timothy 3:13
Those who serve well as deacons gain high standing and great confidence in their faith in Christ Jesus. 🌱
## 1 Timothy 3:14
I hope to visit you soon, but I write these things now. ✉️
## 1 Timothy 3:15
If I am delayed, you will know how people must behave in God’s household. It is the church of the living God, the pillar and foundation of the truth. 🏠
## 1 Timothy 3:16
We all confess that the mystery of godliness is great. He appeared in the flesh, and the Spirit proved Him right. Angels saw Him. He was announced among the nations and believed in throughout the world. He was taken up in glory. ✨
