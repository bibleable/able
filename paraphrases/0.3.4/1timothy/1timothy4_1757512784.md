## 1 Timothy 4:1
The Spirit clearly says that in later times some will abandon the faith. They will follow deceptive spirits and demons’ teachings. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 4:2
Hypocritical liars will influence them. These liars’ consciences are burned as with a hot iron. ⛔
## 1 Timothy 4:3
They will forbid marriage and require people to avoid certain foods. God created these foods for believers who know the truth to receive with thanks. 🍞
## 1 Timothy 4:4
Everything God created is good. Do not reject anything received with thanks. 🙏
## 1 Timothy 4:5
God’s word and prayer make it holy. 📖
## 1 Timothy 4:6
Explain these things to the brothers, and you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus. The words of faith and correct teaching you followed will nourish you. 🌱
## 1 Timothy 4:7
Reject silly myths that dishonor God. Train yourself to live in a way that honors Him. 👣
## 1 Timothy 4:8
Physical exercise has some value. But a life that honors God has value in every way, with promises for this life and the life to come. 💪
## 1 Timothy 4:9
This statement is trustworthy and deserves full acceptance. 📖
## 1 Timothy 4:10
We work hard and strive for this. We hope in the living God, who is the Savior of everyone, especially believers. 🙏
## 1 Timothy 4:11
Command and teach these things. 📣
## 1 Timothy 4:12
Let no one despise you because you are young. Be an example to believers in speech, behavior, love, faith, and purity. 👣
## 1 Timothy 4:13
Until I come, give your attention to reading Scripture publicly, encouraging others, and teaching. 📖
## 1 Timothy 4:14
Do not neglect your gift. It was given through prophecy when the elders placed their hands on you. 🎁
## 1 Timothy 4:15
Work carefully at these things and give yourself fully to them. Then everyone can see your progress. 🌱
## 1 Timothy 4:16
Pay close attention to your life and teaching. Continue in these things. In this way, you will save yourself and those who hear you. 👣
