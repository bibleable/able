## 1 Timothy 2:1
First, I urge you to offer requests, prayers, prayers for others, and thanks for everyone. 🙏
## 1 Timothy 2:2
Pray for kings and everyone in authority. Then we can live peaceful and quiet lives, with full respect for God and dignity. 👑
## 1 Timothy 2:3
This is good and pleases God our Savior. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 2:4
He wants everyone to be saved and know the truth. 🌍
## 1 Timothy 2:5
There is one God and one mediator between God and people: the man Christ Jesus. ✝️

*Word help: A mediator acts between parties.*
## 1 Timothy 2:6
He gave Himself as a ransom for everyone. This testimony was given at the proper time. ✝️
## 1 Timothy 2:7
For this, I was appointed as a preacher, apostle, and faithful and true teacher of the Gentiles. I tell the truth and do not lie. 📣
## 1 Timothy 2:8
I want men everywhere to pray and lift holy hands, without anger or arguments. 🙏
## 1 Timothy 2:9
I also want women to dress respectably, with modesty and control of themselves. Their beauty must not depend on braided hair, gold, pearls, or expensive clothes. 👣
## 1 Timothy 2:10
Instead, let good actions adorn them, as is proper for women who claim to worship God. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 2:11
A woman must learn quietly and with full submission. 📖
## 1 Timothy 2:12
I do not permit a woman to teach or have authority over a man. She must remain quiet. 💬
## 1 Timothy 2:13
Adam was formed first, then Eve. 🌱
## 1 Timothy 2:14
Adam was not deceived. The woman was deceived and fell into sin. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 2:15
Women will be saved through childbearing if they continue in faith, love, and holiness, with control of themselves. 🌱
