## 1 Timothy 6:1
Those under slavery’s yoke must consider their masters fully worthy of honor. Then people will not speak against God’s name and our teaching. 🛠️
## 1 Timothy 6:2
Those with believing masters must not disrespect them because they are brothers. Serve them even better, because those who benefit from this service are beloved believers. Teach and encourage these principles. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 6:3
Suppose someone teaches differently and rejects our Lord Jesus Christ’s correct words and teaching that honors God. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 6:4
He is proud and understands nothing. He has an unhealthy interest in debates and fights about words. These cause envy, conflict, abusive talk, and evil suspicions. ⛔
## 1 Timothy 6:5
They also cause constant friction among people with corrupt minds who lack the truth. These people think that a life of religion is a way to gain wealth. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 6:6
But a life that honors God, with contentment, is great gain. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 6:7
We brought nothing into the world and can take nothing out. 💭
## 1 Timothy 6:8
If we have food and clothes, we will be content with these. 🍞
## 1 Timothy 6:9
Those who want wealth fall into temptation and a trap. Many foolish and harmful desires drag them into ruin and destruction. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 6:10
Love of money is the root of all kinds of evil. Some desired it, wandered from the faith, and pierced themselves with many sorrows. ⛔
## 1 Timothy 6:11
But you, man of God, run from these things. Pursue righteousness, a life that honors God, faith, love, endurance, and gentleness. 👣
## 1 Timothy 6:12
Fight the good fight of faith. Take hold of eternal life. You were called to it when you made the good confession before many witnesses. 🛡️
## 1 Timothy 6:13
I command you before God, who gives life to everything, and Christ Jesus, who made the good confession before Pontius Pilate. 📣
## 1 Timothy 6:14
Keep this command without stain or blame until our Lord Jesus Christ appears. ✨
## 1 Timothy 6:15
The blessed and only Sovereign will bring this about at His chosen time. He is the King of kings and Lord of lords. 👑
## 1 Timothy 6:16
He alone cannot die and lives in light that no one can approach. No one has seen Him or can see Him. May He receive honor and eternal rule! Amen. ✨
## 1 Timothy 6:17
Tell those rich in this age not to be proud or trust uncertain wealth. They must hope in God, who richly gives us everything to enjoy. 🎁
## 1 Timothy 6:18
Tell them to do good, be rich in good actions, and be generous and ready to share. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 6:19
Then they store up a firm foundation for the future and take hold of true life. 🌅
## 1 Timothy 6:20
Timothy, guard what was entrusted to you. Avoid empty talk that dishonors God and opposing arguments from what people falsely call knowledge. 🛡️
## 1 Timothy 6:21
Some claimed this knowledge and turned away from the faith. May grace be with all of you. 🕊️
