## 1 Timothy 1:1
This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and Christ Jesus our hope. ✉️
## 1 Timothy 1:2
I write to Timothy, my true child in the faith. May God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord give you grace, mercy, and peace. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 1:3
When I left for Macedonia, I urged you to stay in Ephesus. Instruct certain men to stop teaching false beliefs. 📖
## 1 Timothy 1:4
Tell them to stop giving their attention to myths and endless family records. These promote speculation instead of the faithful management of God’s work through faith. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 1:5
Our instruction aims at love that comes from a pure heart, a clear conscience, and sincere faith. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 1:6
Some people wandered from these things and turned to empty talk. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 1:7
They want to teach the law. But they do not understand their own words or the things they confidently claim. 📜
## 1 Timothy 1:8
We know the law is good if someone uses it properly. ⚖️
## 1 Timothy 1:9
The law was made for lawless and rebellious people, rather than righteous people. It is for those who dishonor God, sinners, the unholy, and those who treat sacred things with contempt. It is for those who kill fathers or mothers, and for murderers. ⛔
## 1 Timothy 1:10
It is also for the sexually immoral, homosexuals, slave traders, liars, and those who lie under oath. It is for anyone else who opposes correct teaching. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 1:11
This teaching agrees with the glorious good news of the blessed God. He entrusted this good news to me. 📣
## 1 Timothy 1:12
I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who strengthened me. He considered me faithful and appointed me to serve. 🙏
## 1 Timothy 1:13
I once insulted God, persecuted others, and acted violently. But I received mercy because I acted in ignorance and unbelief. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 1:14
Our Lord’s grace overflowed to me, with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. ✨
## 1 Timothy 1:15
This statement is trustworthy and deserves full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners. I am the worst of them. ✝️
## 1 Timothy 1:16
For that very reason, I received mercy. In me, the worst sinner, Christ Jesus could show His perfect patience. I became an example for those who would believe in Him for eternal life. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 1:17
May the eternal King, the only God, who cannot die or be seen, receive honor and glory forever and ever. Amen. 👑
## 1 Timothy 1:18
Timothy, my child, I entrust this command to you according to the earlier prophecies about you. Through them, fight the good fight. 🛡️
## 1 Timothy 1:19
Hold to faith and a good conscience. Some rejected a good conscience and wrecked their faith like a ship. ⛵
## 1 Timothy 1:20
Hymenaeus and Alexander are among them. I handed them over to Satan so that they would learn not to insult God. ⚖️
