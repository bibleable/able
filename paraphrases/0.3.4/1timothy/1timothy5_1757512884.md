## 1 Timothy 5:1
Do not speak harshly to an older man. Appeal to him as a father. Treat younger men as brothers. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 5:2
Treat older women as mothers and younger women as sisters, with complete purity. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 5:3
Honor widows who are truly alone and in need. 🤝
## 1 Timothy 5:4
If a widow has children or grandchildren, they must first learn to honor God by caring for their family. They must repay their parents, because this pleases God. 🏠
## 1 Timothy 5:5
A widow truly in need and left alone puts her hope in God. She continues her requests and prayers night and day. 🙏
## 1 Timothy 5:6
But a widow who lives for pleasure is dead even while alive. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 5:7
Give believers these instructions so that they are free from blame. 📖
## 1 Timothy 5:8
Anyone who does not provide for his relatives, especially his own household, denies the faith. He is worse than an unbeliever. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 5:9
Put a widow on the list if she is at least sixty years old and was faithful to her husband. 📜
## 1 Timothy 5:10
She must be known for good actions. These include raising children, welcoming strangers, washing the feet of God’s holy people, helping those who suffer, and doing every good task. ❤️
## 1 Timothy 5:11
Do not put younger widows on the list. When their passions draw them away from Christ, they will want to marry. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 5:12
Then they bring judgment on themselves because they set aside their first faith. ⚖️
## 1 Timothy 5:13
They also learn to be idle and go from house to house. They gossip, interfere in other people’s affairs, and discuss things they should not mention. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 5:14
I advise younger widows to marry, have children, and manage their households. Then the enemy has no chance to slander them. 🏠
## 1 Timothy 5:15
Some have already turned away to follow Satan. ⛔
## 1 Timothy 5:16
If a believing woman has widows who depend on her, she must help them. Do not burden the church. Then it can help widows truly in need. 🤝
## 1 Timothy 5:17
Elders who lead well deserve double honor, especially those who work hard to preach and teach. 👥
## 1 Timothy 5:18
Scripture says, “Do not muzzle an ox as it treads out grain.” It also says, “A worker deserves his pay.” 📖
## 1 Timothy 5:19
Do not accept an accusation against an elder without testimony from two or three witnesses. ⚖️
## 1 Timothy 5:20
Correct those who continue to sin before everyone, so that the others fear sin. ⚠️
## 1 Timothy 5:21
Before God, Christ Jesus, and the chosen angels, I solemnly command you to follow these principles fairly. Do nothing from favoritism. ⚖️
## 1 Timothy 5:22
Do not place your hands on people too quickly and so share their sins. Keep yourself pure. 🛡️
## 1 Timothy 5:23
Stop using only water to drink. Use a little wine because of your stomach and frequent illnesses. 🍷
## 1 Timothy 5:24
Some people’s sins are obvious and go ahead of them to judgment. Other people’s sins appear later. ⚖️
## 1 Timothy 5:25
Good actions are also obvious. Even those that are not obvious cannot remain hidden. 👀
