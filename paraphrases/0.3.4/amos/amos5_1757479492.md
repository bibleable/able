## Amos 5:1
House of Israel, hear this song of grief that I raise against you. 😢
## Amos 5:2
“Virgin Israel has fallen and will never rise again. She lies abandoned on her land, with no one to lift her.” 💔
## Amos 5:3
The Lord GOD says, “In Israel, a city that sends out a thousand will have only a hundred left. One that sends a hundred will have only ten left.” ⚖️
## Amos 5:4
The LORD says to Israel, “Seek Me and live! 👣
## Amos 5:5
Do not seek Bethel, go to Gilgal, or travel to Beersheba. Gilgal will surely go into exile, and Bethel will come to nothing. ⚠️
## Amos 5:6
Seek the LORD and live. Otherwise, He will sweep through Joseph’s house like fire and consume everything. No one in Bethel will put it out. 🔥
## Amos 5:7
Some turn justice into wormwood and throw righteousness to the ground. ⚠️

*Word help: Wormwood is a bitter plant.*
## Amos 5:8
He made the Pleiades and Orion. He turns darkness to dawn and day to night. He calls the sea’s waters and pours them over the earth. His name is the LORD. ✨
## Amos 5:9
He flashes destruction on strong people, and fury falls on the stronghold. ⚖️
## Amos 5:10
Some hate the person who corrects wrong at the gate and despise anyone who speaks honestly. ⛔
## Amos 5:11
You trample poor people and take grain taxes from them. So you will not live in the stone houses you built. You will not drink wine from the rich vineyards you planted. ⚖️
## Amos 5:12
I know how many rebellions and sins you have committed. You oppress righteous people and take bribes. You deny poor people justice at the gate. ⛔
## Amos 5:13
Wise people keep silent at such times, because the days are evil. 🤫
## Amos 5:14
Seek good instead of evil, so that you can live. Then the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be with you, as you claimed. 👣
## Amos 5:15
Hate evil, love good, and establish justice at the gate. Perhaps the LORD, the God of Hosts, will show grace to Joseph’s remaining people.” ⚖️
## Amos 5:16
The LORD, the God of Hosts, the Lord, says, “People will wail in every public square and cry, ‘Alas! Alas!’ in every street. They will call farmers to mourn and mourners to wail. 😢
## Amos 5:17
People will wail in every vineyard, because I will pass among you,” says the LORD. 💔
## Amos 5:18
How terrible for you who long for the Day of the LORD! What will that day mean for you? It will be darkness instead of light. 🌑
## Amos 5:19
It will be like someone who escapes a lion and meets a bear. He enters his house, rests his hand on the wall, and a snake bites him. 🐍
## Amos 5:20
Will the Day of the LORD not be darkness instead of light? Will it not be gloom without brightness? 🌑
## Amos 5:21
“I hate and despise your feasts! I cannot bear the stench of your solemn assemblies. ⛔
## Amos 5:22
I will not accept your burnt offerings or grain offerings. I will pay no attention to your peace offerings of fattened cattle. ⚠️
## Amos 5:23
Take your noisy songs away from Me! I will not listen to your harps. 🎵
## Amos 5:24
Let justice flow like a river and righteousness like a stream that never stops. 🌊
## Amos 5:25
House of Israel, did you bring Me sacrifices and offerings for forty years in the wilderness? 💬
## Amos 5:26
You carried Sakkuth your king and Kaiwan your star god, idols you made yourselves. ⛔
## Amos 5:27
So I will send you into exile beyond Damascus,” says the LORD, whose name is the God of Hosts. ⛓️
