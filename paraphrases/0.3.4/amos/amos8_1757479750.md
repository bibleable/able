## Amos 8:1
The Lord GOD showed me a basket of summer fruit. 🍇
## Amos 8:2
He asked, “Amos, what do you see?” I replied, “A basket of summer fruit.” The LORD said, “The end has come for My people Israel. I will no longer spare them.” ⚖️
## Amos 8:3
The Lord GOD declares, “On that day, the temple’s songs will become wails. Many dead bodies will lie everywhere in silence!” 💔
## Amos 8:4
Hear this, you who trample needy people and destroy the land’s poor people. ⚠️
## Amos 8:5
You ask, “When will the New Moon end so we can sell grain? When will the Sabbath end so we can sell wheat? Let us reduce the ephah and increase the shekel. Let us cheat with dishonest scales. ⛔

*Word help: The ephah measures grain, and the shekel measures weight. These sellers want to give less grain and demand more payment.*
## Amos 8:6
Let us buy poor people with silver and needy people for a pair of sandals. Let us even sell chaff with the wheat!” ⛔
## Amos 8:7
The LORD swore by the Pride of Jacob, “I will never forget any of their actions. ⚖️
## Amos 8:8
Will the land not shake because of this, and all its people mourn? It will rise, surge, and sink like Egypt’s Nile. 🌊
## Amos 8:9
On that day, I will make the sun set at noon and darken the earth in daylight,” declares the Lord GOD. 🌑
## Amos 8:10
“I will turn your feasts and songs into mourning. I will make everyone wear sackcloth and shave their heads. It will be like grief for an only son, and its end will be a bitter day. 😢
## Amos 8:11
Days are coming when I will send a famine,” declares the Lord GOD. “It will be a famine of hearing the LORD’s words, rather than hunger for bread or thirst for water. 📖
## Amos 8:12
People will stagger from sea to sea and wander from north to east. They will seek the LORD’s word but not find it. 👣
## Amos 8:13
On that day, beautiful young women and young men will faint from thirst. 💧
## Amos 8:14
Some swear by Samaria’s guilt and say, ‘As surely as your god lives, Dan,’ or, ‘As surely as Beersheba’s way lives.’ They will fall and never rise again.” ⚖️
