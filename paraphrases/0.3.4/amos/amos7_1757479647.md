## Amos 7:1
The Lord GOD showed me this: He prepared swarms of locusts just after the king’s harvest. The late spring crop was beginning to grow. 🌾
## Amos 7:2
After the locusts ate every green plant, I said, “Lord GOD, please forgive! How can Jacob survive? He is so small.” 🙏
## Amos 7:3
The LORD changed His decision. He said, “It will not happen.” 🕊️
## Amos 7:4
The Lord GOD showed me this: He called for judgment by fire. The fire consumed the great deep and devoured the land. 🔥
## Amos 7:5
I said, “Lord GOD, please stop! How can Jacob survive? He is so small.” 🙏
## Amos 7:6
The Lord GOD changed His decision. He said, “This will not happen either.” 🕊️
## Amos 7:7
He showed me the Lord beside a straight wall. A plumb line was in His hand. 🧱

*Word help: A plumb line is a weighted line used to check whether something is upright.*
## Amos 7:8
The LORD asked, “Amos, what do you see?” I replied, “A plumb line.” The Lord said, “I place a plumb line among My people Israel. I will no longer spare them. ⚖️
## Amos 7:9
Isaac’s high places will be deserted, and Israel’s holy places will be ruined. I will rise against Jeroboam’s house with My sword.” ⚔️
## Amos 7:10
Amaziah, Bethel’s priest, sent this message to Jeroboam king of Israel: “Amos plots against you among Israel’s people. The land cannot bear all his words. 📣
## Amos 7:11
He says, ‘Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will surely leave its homeland for exile.’” ⛓️
## Amos 7:12
Amaziah told Amos, “Go away, seer! Flee to Judah. Earn your bread and prophesy there. 👣
## Amos 7:13
Never prophesy in Bethel again. It is the king’s holy place and the kingdom’s temple.” ⛔
## Amos 7:14
Amos replied, “I was neither a prophet nor a prophet’s son. I was a herdsman and cared for sycamore-fig trees. 🌳
## Amos 7:15
The LORD took me from the flock and said, ‘Go and prophesy to My people Israel.’ 📣
## Amos 7:16
Hear the LORD’s word. You say, ‘Do not prophesy against Israel or preach against Isaac’s house.’ 📖
## Amos 7:17
The LORD says, ‘Your wife will become a prostitute in the city, and your sons and daughters will fall by the sword. A measuring line will divide your land, and you will die on pagan soil. Israel will surely leave its homeland for exile.’” ⚖️
