## Amos 6:1
How terrible for those at ease in Zion and secure on Mount Samaria! You are distinguished leaders of the foremost nation, to whom Israel comes. ⚠️
## Amos 6:2
Go to Calneh and see. Continue to great Hamath, then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are you better than these kingdoms? Is their territory larger than yours? 💬
## Amos 6:3
You dismiss the day of disaster and bring a rule of violence near. ⛔
## Amos 6:4
You lie on beds decorated with ivory and lounge on couches. You eat lambs from the flock and calves from the stall. 🍽️
## Amos 6:5
Like David, you invent songs on the harp and make your own musical instruments. 🎵
## Amos 6:6
You drink bowls of wine and put the finest oils on yourselves. But you do not grieve over Joseph’s ruin. ⚠️
## Amos 6:7
You will be the first captives to go into exile. Your feasts and lazy rest will end. ⛓️
## Amos 6:8
The Lord GOD swore by Himself. The LORD, the God of Hosts, declares, “I hate Jacob’s pride and despise his strongholds. I will hand over the city and everything in it.” ⚖️
## Amos 6:9
If ten men remain in one house, they too will die. 💔
## Amos 6:10
A relative will collect the bodies to burn them and carry them from the house. He will ask someone inside, “Is anyone else with you?” The answer will be, “No one.” The relative will say, “Be silent! We must not call on the LORD’s name.” 🤫
## Amos 6:11
The LORD commands, “The great house will be smashed to pieces, and the small house to rubble.” 🏚️
## Amos 6:12
“Do horses run on cliffs? Does someone plow the sea with oxen? But you turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood. ⚠️
## Amos 6:13
You rejoice in Lo-debar and say, ‘Did we not take Karnaim through our own strength?’ ⛔
## Amos 6:14
House of Israel, I will raise a nation against you,” declares the LORD, the God of Hosts. “They will oppress you from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of the Arabah.” ⚔️
