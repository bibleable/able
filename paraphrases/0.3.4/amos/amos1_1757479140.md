## Amos 1:1
These are the words of Amos, a shepherd from Tekoa. He saw this about Israel two years before the earthquake. Uzziah was king of Judah, and Jeroboam son of Jehoash was king of Israel. 📣
## Amos 1:2
He said, “The LORD roars from Zion and raises His voice from Jerusalem. The shepherds’ pastures mourn, and Carmel’s summit dries up.” ⛰️
## Amos 1:3
The LORD says, “For three sins of Damascus, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. They threshed Gilead with iron sledges. ⚖️
## Amos 1:4
I will send fire on Hazael’s house to consume Ben-hadad’s strongholds. 🔥
## Amos 1:5
I will break Damascus’s gates and remove the ruler from the Valley of Aven. I will remove the one who holds the scepter in Beth-eden. Aram’s people will go into exile in Kir,” says the LORD. ⛓️
## Amos 1:6
The LORD says, “For three sins of Gaza, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. They took a whole population into exile and handed them to Edom. ⚖️
## Amos 1:7
I will send fire on Gaza’s walls to consume its strongholds. 🔥
## Amos 1:8
I will remove Ashdod’s ruler and the one who holds the scepter in Ashkelon. I will turn My hand against Ekron. The remaining Philistines will perish,” says the Lord GOD. ⚖️
## Amos 1:9
The LORD says, “For three sins of Tyre, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. They handed a whole group of exiles to Edom and broke a covenant of brotherhood. ⛓️
## Amos 1:10
I will send fire on Tyre’s walls to consume its strongholds.” 🔥
## Amos 1:11
The LORD says, “For three sins of Edom, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. He pursued his brother with the sword and suppressed all mercy. His anger raged, and his fury burned without pause. ⚖️
## Amos 1:12
I will send fire on Teman to consume Bozrah’s strongholds.” 🔥
## Amos 1:13
The LORD says, “For three sins of the Ammonites, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. They ripped open Gilead’s pregnant women to enlarge their territory. ⛔
## Amos 1:14
I will light a fire in Rabbah’s walls to consume its strongholds. There will be war cries on the battle day and violent wind on the storm day. 🔥
## Amos 1:15
Their king and princes will go into exile together,” says the LORD. ⛓️
