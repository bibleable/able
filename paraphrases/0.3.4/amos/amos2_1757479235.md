## Amos 2:1
The LORD says, “For three sins of Moab, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. He burned the bones of Edom’s king into lime. ⚖️
## Amos 2:2
I will send fire on Moab to consume Kerioth’s strongholds. Moab will die amid confusion, war cries, and the sound of the ram’s horn. 🔥
## Amos 2:3
I will remove Moab’s ruler and kill all his officials with him,” says the LORD. ⚖️
## Amos 2:4
The LORD says, “For three sins of Judah, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. They rejected My law and disobeyed My commands. The lies their ancestors followed mislead them. ⚠️
## Amos 2:5
I will send fire on Judah to consume Jerusalem’s strongholds.” 🔥
## Amos 2:6
The LORD says, “For three sins of Israel, even four, I will not cancel My judgment. They sell righteous people for silver and needy people for a pair of sandals. ⛔
## Amos 2:7
They trample the heads of poor people like dust and push needy people aside. A man and his father have sexual relations with the same girl. In this way, they dishonor My holy name. ⚠️
## Amos 2:8
Beside every altar, they lie on clothes taken as security for debts. In their God’s house, they drink wine obtained through fines. ⛔
## Amos 2:9
Yet I destroyed the Amorite before them, though he was tall as cedars and strong as oaks. I destroyed his fruit above and roots below. 🌳
## Amos 2:10
I brought you from Egypt and led you forty years in the wilderness. You could then take the Amorite’s land. 👣
## Amos 2:11
I raised prophets from your sons and Nazirites from your young men. Children of Israel, is this not true?” declares the LORD. 📣
## Amos 2:12
“But you made the Nazirites drink wine and told the prophets not to prophesy. ⚠️
## Amos 2:13
I am about to crush you where you stand, as with a cart full of grain. ⚖️
## Amos 2:14
Fast runners will not escape. Strong people cannot prevail through their strength, and mighty people cannot save their lives. ⚔️
## Amos 2:15
Archers will not stand their ground. Fast runners will not escape, and riders will not save their lives. 🐎
## Amos 2:16
Even the bravest warriors will flee naked that day,” declares the LORD. ⚖️
