## Amos 3:1
Children of Israel, hear the LORD’s message against you. It is against the whole family I brought from Egypt. 📣
## Amos 3:2
“Of all the earth’s families, I have known only you. So I will punish all your sins.” ⚖️
## Amos 3:3
Can two people walk together without agreeing where to go? 👣
## Amos 3:4
Does a lion roar in the forest without prey? Does a young lion growl in its den when it has caught nothing? 🦁
## Amos 3:5
Does a bird enter a trap without bait? Does a trap spring from the ground with nothing to catch? 🐦
## Amos 3:6
Do people not tremble when a ram’s horn sounds in their city? If disaster comes to a city, has the LORD not caused it? 📯
## Amos 3:7
The Lord GOD does nothing without revealing His plan to His servants, the prophets. 📖
## Amos 3:8
The lion roared. Who will not fear? The Lord GOD spoke. Who will not prophesy? 🦁
## Amos 3:9
Announce to Ashdod’s strongholds and Egypt’s strongholds, “Gather on Samaria’s mountains. See the great unrest and oppression in the city.” 📣
## Amos 3:10
The LORD declares, “They do not know how to do right. They store violence and destruction in their strongholds.” ⛔
## Amos 3:11
The Lord GOD says, “An enemy will surround the land, tear down your defenses, and plunder your strongholds.” ⚔️
## Amos 3:12
The LORD says, “A shepherd rescues two legs or part of an ear from a lion’s mouth. Israelites in Samaria will be rescued like this, with only a bed’s corner or a couch’s cushion. 🦁
## Amos 3:13
Hear and testify against Jacob’s house,” declares the Lord GOD, the God of Hosts. ⚖️
## Amos 3:14
“When I punish Israel’s sins, I will destroy Bethel’s altars. The altar’s horns will be cut off and fall to the ground. ⚖️
## Amos 3:15
I will destroy the winter house and summer house. The ivory houses will perish, and the great houses will end,” declares the LORD. 🏚️
