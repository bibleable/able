## Amos 4:1
Hear this, you cows of Bashan on Mount Samaria! You women oppress poor people and crush needy people. You tell your husbands, “Bring us more to drink.” ⚠️
## Amos 4:2
The Lord GOD swore by His holiness, “Days are coming when hooks will carry you away, and fishhooks will carry your descendants. ⛓️
## Amos 4:3
Each of you will go straight through broken walls. You will be thrown out toward Harmon,” declares the LORD. ⚖️
## Amos 4:4
“Go to Bethel and sin! Rebel even more at Gilgal! Bring sacrifices every morning and tithes every three days. ⚠️
## Amos 4:5
Offer bread with yeast as a thank offering, and loudly announce your voluntary offerings. Children of Israel, this is what you love,” declares the Lord GOD. 📣
## Amos 4:6
“I left your teeth clean in every city and your towns without bread. Yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD. 🍞
## Amos 4:7
“I withheld rain when the harvest was three months away. I sent rain to one city and withheld it from another. One field received rain, but another dried up without it. 🌧️
## Amos 4:8
People staggered from city to city for drinking water, but they did not have enough. Yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD. 💧
## Amos 4:9
“I struck your growing gardens and vineyards with blight and mildew. Locusts ate your fig and olive trees. Yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD. 🌾
## Amos 4:10
“I sent plagues like Egypt’s plagues among you. I killed your young men with the sword, along with your captured horses. I filled your noses with your camp’s stench. Yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD. ⚖️
## Amos 4:11
“I overthrew some of you as I overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah. You were like a burning stick pulled from a fire. Yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD. 🔥
## Amos 4:12
“Israel, this is what I will do to you. Since I will do it, prepare to meet your God! ⚖️
## Amos 4:13
He forms mountains, creates wind, and reveals His thoughts to people. He turns dawn to darkness and walks on the earth’s heights. His name is the LORD, the God of Hosts.” ⛰️
