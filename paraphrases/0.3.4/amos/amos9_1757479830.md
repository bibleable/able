## Amos 9:1
I saw the Lord beside the altar. He said, “Strike the pillars’ tops so the thresholds shake. Drop them on everyone’s heads, and I will kill the rest with the sword. No one who flees will escape. ⚖️
## Amos 9:2
If they dig down to Sheol, My hand will take them from there. If they climb to heaven, I will pull them down. ⛓️

*Word help: Sheol is the place of the dead.*
## Amos 9:3
If they hide on Carmel’s summit, I will find and seize them. If they hide at the sea’s bottom, I will command the serpent to bite them. ⛰️
## Amos 9:4
If their enemies take them captive, I will command the sword to kill them there. I will watch them for harm instead of good.” ⚖️
## Amos 9:5
The Lord GOD of Hosts touches the earth, and it melts. All its people mourn. The land rises and sinks like Egypt’s Nile. 🌊
## Amos 9:6
He builds His upper rooms in heaven and sets His vault on the earth. He calls the sea’s waters and pours them over the earth. His name is the LORD. ✨
## Amos 9:7
The LORD declares, “Children of Israel, are you not like the Cushites to Me? Did I not bring Israel from Egypt, the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Arameans from Kir? 🌍
## Amos 9:8
The Lord GOD watches the sinful kingdom. I will destroy it from the earth. But I will not completely destroy Jacob’s house,” declares the LORD. ⚖️
## Amos 9:9
“I will command Israel’s house to be shaken among all nations, like grain in a sieve. But not one pebble will fall to the ground. 🌾
## Amos 9:10
All sinners among My people will die by the sword. They say, ‘Disaster will never come near us or confront us.’” ⚖️
## Amos 9:11
“On that day, I will restore David’s fallen tent. I will repair its gaps and ruins, and rebuild it as in earlier days. 🏠
## Amos 9:12
Then they can possess Edom’s remaining people and all nations that bear My name,” declares the LORD, who will do this. 🌍
## Amos 9:13
The LORD declares, “Days are coming when the plowman will overtake the harvester and the grape treader will overtake the seed planter. The mountains will drip sweet wine, and all the hills will flow with it. 🌾
## Amos 9:14
I will bring My people Israel back from captivity. They will rebuild ruined cities and live in them. They will plant vineyards and drink the wine. They will make gardens and eat their fruit. 🌱
## Amos 9:15
I will plant them firmly in their land. They will never again be uprooted from the land I gave them,” says the LORD your God. 🏠
