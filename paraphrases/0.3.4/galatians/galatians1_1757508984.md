## Galatians 1:1
This letter is from Paul, an apostle sent by Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised Him from the dead. No person sent or appointed me. ✉️
## Galatians 1:2
All the brothers with me join in this letter to the churches of Galatia. 🤝
## Galatians 1:3
May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace. 🕊️
## Galatians 1:4
Jesus gave Himself for our sins to rescue us from this present evil age. He did this according to the will of our God and Father. ✝️
## Galatians 1:5
May God receive glory forever and ever. Amen. ✨
## Galatians 1:6
I am amazed that you so quickly abandon the One who called you through Christ’s grace. You turn to a different good news. ⚠️
## Galatians 1:7
But it is not good news at all. Some people trouble you and try to twist Christ’s good news. ⛔
## Galatians 1:8
Suppose we or an angel from heaven preach a message against the good news we preached to you. Let that preacher be under a curse! ⚖️
## Galatians 1:9
I repeat what we said before. If anyone preaches a message against the good news you received, let that preacher be under a curse! ⚖️
## Galatians 1:10
Do I now seek people’s approval or God’s approval? Do I try to please people? If I still tried to please people, I would not be Christ’s servant. 💭
## Galatians 1:11
Brothers, I want you to know that no person invented the good news I preached. 📣
## Galatians 1:12
No person gave it to me or taught it to me. Jesus Christ revealed it to me. ✨
## Galatians 1:13
You heard how I once lived in Judaism. I severely persecuted God’s church and tried to destroy it. ⚠️
## Galatians 1:14
I advanced in Judaism beyond many people my age. I was extremely eager to follow my ancestors’ traditions. 📜
## Galatians 1:15
But God set me apart from my mother’s womb and called me through His grace. ❤️
## Galatians 1:16
He was pleased to reveal His Son in me so that I could preach Him among the Gentiles. I did not immediately seek advice from other people. 📣
## Galatians 1:17
I did not go to Jerusalem to the apostles appointed before me. Instead, I went to Arabia and later returned to Damascus. 👣
## Galatians 1:18
After three years, I went to Jerusalem to speak with Cephas. I stayed with him for fifteen days. 🧳
## Galatians 1:19
I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord’s brother. 👥
## Galatians 1:20
Before God, I assure you that I do not lie in this letter. ✍️
## Galatians 1:21
Later, I went to the regions of Syria and Cilicia. 👣
## Galatians 1:22
The churches in Christ in Judea did not know me personally. 👥
## Galatians 1:23
They only heard this report: “The man who persecuted us now preaches the faith he once tried to destroy.” 📣
## Galatians 1:24
They glorified God because of me. 🙏
