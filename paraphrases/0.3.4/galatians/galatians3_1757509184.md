## Galatians 3:1
Foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you? Jesus Christ was clearly pictured before your eyes as crucified. ✝️
## Galatians 3:2
Tell me just this: Did you receive the Spirit through works of the law or through hearing with faith? 💬
## Galatians 3:3
Are you so foolish? You began in the Spirit. Do you now finish through the flesh? ⚠️
## Galatians 3:4
Did you suffer so much for nothing, if it really was for nothing? 💭
## Galatians 3:5
God freely gives you His Spirit and works miracles among you. Does He do this because you practice the law, or because you hear and believe? 🕊️
## Galatians 3:6
“Abraham believed God, and God counted this as righteousness.” 📖
## Galatians 3:7
Understand that people who have faith are Abraham’s sons. 🌱
## Galatians 3:8
Scripture foresaw that God would declare the Gentiles righteous through faith. It announced the good news to Abraham beforehand: “All nations will be blessed through you.” 🌍
## Galatians 3:9
Those who have faith are blessed with Abraham, the man of faith. ❤️
## Galatians 3:10
All who depend on works of the law are under a curse. Scripture says, “Everyone who does not continue to obey everything in the Book of the Law is under a curse.” ⚖️
## Galatians 3:11
Clearly, no one is declared righteous before God through the law. Scripture says, “The righteous will live by faith.” 🙏
## Galatians 3:12
The law does not depend on faith. Instead, “The person who does these things will live by them.” 📜
## Galatians 3:13
Christ freed us from the law’s curse by becoming a curse for us. Scripture says, “Everyone hung on a tree is under a curse.” ✝️
## Galatians 3:14
He did this so that Abraham’s promised blessing could reach the Gentiles in Christ Jesus. Through faith, we could receive the promised Spirit. 🕊️
## Galatians 3:15
Brothers, consider a human example. Once a human covenant is confirmed, no one can cancel it or add to it. 📜
## Galatians 3:16
God spoke the promises to Abraham and his seed. Scripture says “to your seed,” meaning one, rather than “to seeds,” meaning many. That One is Christ. 🌱

*Word help: Seed here means offspring.*
## Galatians 3:17
The law came 430 years later. It cannot cancel the covenant God already established or make the promise useless. 📜
## Galatians 3:18
If the inheritance depends on the law, it no longer depends on a promise. But God freely gave it to Abraham through a promise. 🎁
## Galatians 3:19
Why was the law given? It was added because of sins, until the seed named in the promise came. Angels put it into effect through a mediator. 📜

*Word help: A mediator acts between parties.*
## Galatians 3:20
A mediator is unnecessary when there is only one party. But God is one. 💭
## Galatians 3:21
Does the law oppose God’s promises? Certainly not! If a law could give life, righteousness would surely come through that law. 📜
## Galatians 3:22
But Scripture declares that sin has confined all things. So the promise can be given to those who believe through faith in Jesus Christ. 🙏
## Galatians 3:23
Before this faith came, the law held us as prisoners. We were locked up until faith was revealed. ⛓️
## Galatians 3:24
The law became our guardian to lead us to Christ. Then we could be declared righteous through faith. 👣
## Galatians 3:25
Now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian. 🌅
## Galatians 3:26
You are all God’s sons through faith in Christ Jesus. ❤️
## Galatians 3:27
All of you baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ. ✝️
## Galatians 3:28
There is no Jew or Greek, slave or free, male or female. You are all one in Christ Jesus. 🤝
## Galatians 3:29
If you belong to Christ, you are Abraham’s seed. You are heirs according to the promise. 🌱
