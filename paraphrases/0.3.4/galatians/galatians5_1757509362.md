## Galatians 5:1
Christ freed us so that we could live in freedom. Stand firm. Do not carry the yoke of slavery again. 🛡️
## Galatians 5:2
Listen! I, Paul, tell you that if you let yourselves be circumcised, Christ will be of no value to you. ⚠️
## Galatians 5:3
Again, I warn every man who gets circumcised: He must obey the whole law. 📜
## Galatians 5:4
You who try to be declared righteous through the law have been cut off from Christ. You have fallen away from grace. ⛔
## Galatians 5:5
Through the Spirit and by faith, we eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness. 🕊️
## Galatians 5:6
In Christ Jesus, neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has value. What matters is faith expressed through love. ❤️
## Galatians 5:7
You were running well. Who stopped you from obeying the truth? 🏃
## Galatians 5:8
This persuasion does not come from the One who calls you. ⚠️
## Galatians 5:9
A little yeast spreads through the whole batch of dough. 🍞
## Galatians 5:10
I trust in the Lord that you will take no other view. Whoever troubles you will bear the judgment, no matter who he is. ⚖️
## Galatians 5:11
Brothers, if I still preach circumcision, why do people still persecute me? In that case, the cross would no longer cause offense. ✝️
## Galatians 5:12
I wish those who disturb you would go further and castrate themselves! ⚠️
## Galatians 5:13
Brothers, you were called to freedom. Do not use freedom as a chance to satisfy sinful physical desires. Instead, serve each other in love. ❤️
## Galatians 5:14
One command fulfills the whole law: “Love your neighbor as yourself.” 📖
## Galatians 5:15
If you keep biting and devouring each other, beware! You may destroy each other. ⚠️
## Galatians 5:16
Live by the Spirit. Then you will not satisfy sinful physical desires. 🕊️
## Galatians 5:17
The flesh desires what opposes the Spirit, and the Spirit desires what opposes the flesh. They fight each other, so that you do not do what you want. ⚔️
## Galatians 5:18
If the Spirit leads you, you are not under the law. 🕊️
## Galatians 5:19
The acts of the flesh are clear. They include sexual immorality, impurity, and wild immoral behavior. ⛔
## Galatians 5:20
They also include idol worship, sorcery, hatred, conflict, jealousy, rage, rivalries, divisions, and factions. ⚠️
## Galatians 5:21
They include envy, drunkenness, orgies, and similar things. I warn you again, as before: Those who practice these things will not inherit God’s kingdom. ⚖️
## Galatians 5:22
But the Spirit’s fruit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, and faithfulness. 🌱
## Galatians 5:23
It also includes gentleness and control of yourself. No law stands against these things. ❤️
## Galatians 5:24
Those who belong to Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires. ✝️
## Galatians 5:25
Since we live by the Spirit, let us keep in step with the Spirit. 👣
## Galatians 5:26
Let us not become proud, provoke each other, or envy each other. 🤝
