## Galatians 4:1
While an heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, though he owns everything. 👤
## Galatians 4:2
Guardians and trustees control him until the date his father set. ⏳
## Galatians 4:3
In the same way, when we were children, the world’s basic principles held us as slaves. ⛓️
## Galatians 4:4
When the full time came, God sent His Son. He was born of a woman and born under the law. ✝️
## Galatians 4:5
He came to free those under the law, so that we could be adopted as sons. ❤️
## Galatians 4:6
Because you are sons, God sent His Son’s Spirit into our hearts. The Spirit cries, “Abba, Father!” 🕊️
## Galatians 4:7
You are no longer a slave, but a son. As a son, you are also an heir through God. 👑
## Galatians 4:8
Before you knew God, you were slaves to those who are not gods by nature. ⛓️
## Galatians 4:9
Now you know God, or rather, God knows you. Why do you turn back to those weak and worthless principles? Do you want to become their slaves again? ⚠️
## Galatians 4:10
You observe special days, months, seasons, and years! 📅
## Galatians 4:11
I fear that my work for you may have been useless. 💭
## Galatians 4:12
Brothers, I beg you to become like me, because I became like you. You did me no wrong. 🤝
## Galatians 4:13
You know that I first preached the good news to you because of an illness. 📣
## Galatians 4:14
My illness tested you, but you did not despise or reject me. You welcomed me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus Himself. ❤️
## Galatians 4:15
What happened to your blessing? I can testify that, if possible, you would have torn out your eyes and given them to me. 💭
## Galatians 4:16
Have I become your enemy because I tell you the truth? 💬
## Galatians 4:17
Those people eagerly seek you, but their purpose is bad. They want to separate you from us so that you eagerly seek them. ⚠️
## Galatians 4:18
It is good to be eager for a good purpose at any time, rather than only when I am with you. 👣
## Galatians 4:19
My children, I again feel the pains of childbirth for you until Christ is formed in you. ❤️
## Galatians 4:20
I wish I could be with you now and change my tone. I am confused about you. 💭
## Galatians 4:21
You who want to be under the law, tell me: Do you not understand what the law says? 📜
## Galatians 4:22
Scripture says Abraham had two sons. One was born to a slave woman and the other to a free woman. 👥
## Galatians 4:23
The slave woman’s son was born by human means. The free woman’s son was born through the promise. 🌱
## Galatians 4:24
These things are illustrations. The women represent two covenants. One covenant comes from Mount Sinai and gives birth to slaves. This is Hagar. 📜
## Galatians 4:25
Hagar represents Mount Sinai in Arabia. She corresponds to the present Jerusalem, because she and her children are slaves. ⛓️
## Galatians 4:26
But the Jerusalem above is free. She is our mother. 🌅
## Galatians 4:27
Scripture says, “Rejoice, woman who cannot bear children! Cry aloud, you who never felt birth pains! The deserted woman has more children than the woman with a husband.” 📖
## Galatians 4:28
Brothers, you are children of promise, like Isaac. ❤️
## Galatians 4:29
Then, the son born by human means persecuted the son born by the Spirit. The same happens now. ⚠️
## Galatians 4:30
But Scripture says, “Send away the slave woman and her son. Her son will never share the inheritance with the free woman’s son.” 📖
## Galatians 4:31
So, brothers, we are children of the free woman, rather than the slave woman. 🌅
