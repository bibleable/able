## Galatians 2:1
Fourteen years later, I went to Jerusalem again with Barnabas. I also took Titus. 👣
## Galatians 2:2
I went because of a revelation and explained the good news I preach among the Gentiles. I spoke privately to the recognized leaders. I feared that I was running, or had run, for nothing. 📣
## Galatians 2:3
But no one forced Titus, who was with me, to be circumcised, though he was Greek. 👤
## Galatians 2:4
This issue arose because false brothers secretly entered under false pretenses. They spied on our freedom in Christ Jesus to make us slaves. ⚠️
## Galatians 2:5
We did not yield to them for even a moment. We wanted the truth of the good news to remain with you. 🛡️
## Galatians 2:6
The respected leaders added nothing to my message. Their status makes no difference to me, because God does not favor some people over others. ⚖️
## Galatians 2:7
They saw that I was entrusted with the good news for the uncircumcised. Peter was entrusted with it for the circumcised. 📣
## Galatians 2:8
The One who worked through Peter as an apostle to the circumcised also worked through me as an apostle to the Gentiles. ✨
## Galatians 2:9
James, Cephas, and John were regarded as pillars. They recognized the grace given to me and offered Barnabas and me their right hands in fellowship. We would go to the Gentiles, and they would go to the circumcised. 🤝
## Galatians 2:10
They asked only that we remember the poor. I was already eager to do this. ❤️
## Galatians 2:11
When Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him directly because he stood condemned. ⚠️
## Galatians 2:12
Before some men came from James, Cephas ate with the Gentiles. When they arrived, he withdrew and separated himself. He feared the circumcision group. 👥
## Galatians 2:13
The other Jews joined in his hypocrisy. Even Barnabas was led astray by their hypocrisy. ⚠️
## Galatians 2:14
I saw that they did not live according to the truth of the good news. I said to Cephas before everyone, “You are a Jew, but you live like a Gentile. How can you force Gentiles to live like Jews?” 💬
## Galatians 2:15
We are Jews by birth, rather than Gentile “sinners.” 👥
## Galatians 2:16
We know that a person is declared righteous through faith in Jesus Christ, rather than works of the law. We too believed in Christ Jesus to be declared righteous through faith in Him. No one will be declared righteous through works of the law. 🙏
## Galatians 2:17
Suppose we seek to be declared righteous in Christ but are found to be sinners ourselves. Does that make Christ a servant of sin? Certainly not! ⛔
## Galatians 2:18
If I rebuild what I tore down, I prove that I break the law. 📜
## Galatians 2:19
Through the law, I died to the law so that I could live for God. ✝️
## Galatians 2:20
I have been crucified with Christ. I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. I live this bodily life by faith in God’s Son, who loved me and gave Himself for me. ❤️
## Galatians 2:21
I do not set God’s grace aside. If righteousness comes through the law, Christ died for nothing. ✝️
