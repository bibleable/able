## Galatians 6:1
Brothers, if someone is caught in a sin, you who are spiritual must gently restore him. Watch yourself, so that you are not tempted too. ❤️
## Galatians 6:2
Carry each other’s burdens. In this way, you will fulfill Christ’s law. 🤝
## Galatians 6:3
Anyone who thinks he is something when he is nothing deceives himself. ⚠️
## Galatians 6:4
Each person must test his own work. Then he can take pride in his own work without comparing it with someone else’s. 🛠️
## Galatians 6:5
Each person must carry his own load. 👣
## Galatians 6:6
Anyone taught the word must share all good things with his teacher. 🎁
## Galatians 6:7
Do not be deceived. God will not be mocked. A person will harvest what he plants. 🌱
## Galatians 6:8
Anyone who plants to please his flesh will harvest destruction from it. Anyone who plants to please the Spirit will harvest eternal life from the Spirit. 🌾
## Galatians 6:9
Let us not grow tired of doing good. At the proper time, we will harvest if we do not give up. 🌱
## Galatians 6:10
Whenever we have the chance, let us do good to everyone, especially the family of faith. ❤️
## Galatians 6:11
Look at the large letters I write with my own hand! ✍️
## Galatians 6:12
Those who want to look good outwardly try to force you to be circumcised. They do this only to avoid persecution for Christ’s cross. ⚠️
## Galatians 6:13
Even those who are circumcised do not obey the law themselves. They want you circumcised so that they can boast about your flesh. ⛔
## Galatians 6:14
May I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ. Through it, the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world. ✝️
## Galatians 6:15
Neither circumcision nor uncircumcision matters. What matters is a new creation. 🌱
## Galatians 6:16
May peace and mercy come to everyone who follows this rule, even to the Israel of God. 🕊️
## Galatians 6:17
From now on, let no one trouble me. I carry Jesus’ marks on my body. ✝️
## Galatians 6:18
Brothers, may the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen. 🕊️
