## Job 5:1
“Call out if you wish. But who will answer? Which of the holy ones will you turn to? 📖

## Job 5:2
Resentment kills a fool. Envy kills a person who lacks judgment. 📖

## Job 5:3
I have seen a fool take root. But suddenly his house was cursed. 📖

## Job 5:4
His sons are far from safety. They are crushed in court, with no one to defend them. 📖

## Job 5:5
Hungry people eat his harvest, even taking it from among thorns. Thirsty people long for his wealth. 📖

## Job 5:6
Distress does not rise from the dust. Trouble does not grow from the ground. 📖

## Job 5:7
Yet people are born to trouble as surely as sparks fly upward. 📖

## Job 5:8
If I were you, I would appeal to God. I would present my case to Him. 📖

## Job 5:9
He does great things beyond our understanding. His wonders cannot be counted. 📖

## Job 5:10
He gives rain to the earth. He sends water onto the fields. 📖

## Job 5:11
He raises the lowly to high places. He lifts those who mourn to safety. 📖

## Job 5:12
He defeats the plans of crafty people. Their hands cannot achieve success. 📖

## Job 5:13
He catches wise people in their own craftiness. He sweeps away the plans of cunning people. 📖

## Job 5:14
They meet darkness during the day. At noon they feel their way as if it were night. 📖

## Job 5:15
He saves needy people from the sword in their enemies' mouths. He saves them from the grip of the powerful. 📖

## Job 5:16
Then the poor have hope. Injustice closes its mouth. 📖

## Job 5:17
The person God corrects is blessed. Do not despise the Almighty's discipline. 📖

## Job 5:18
He wounds, but He also bandages the wounds. He strikes, but His hands also heal. 📖

## Job 5:19
He will rescue you from six disasters. In seven, no harm will touch you. 📖

## Job 5:20
During famine, He will save you from death. In battle, He will save you from the sword's strike. 📖

## Job 5:21
You will be hidden from the tongue's whip. You will not fear destruction when it comes. 📖

## Job 5:22
You will laugh at destruction and famine. You will not need to fear the earth's wild animals. 📖

## Job 5:23
You will have a covenant with the stones in the field. The wild animals will be at peace with you. 📖

## Job 5:24
You will know your tent is secure. When you inspect your home, you will find nothing wrong. 📖

## Job 5:25
You will know that your offspring are many. Your descendants will be like the grass of the earth. 📖

## Job 5:26
You will come to the grave with full strength. You will be like a bundle of grain gathered at the proper time. 📖

## Job 5:27
We have examined this, and it is true. Listen and learn it for yourself.” 📖
