## Job 11:1
Zophar the Naamathite replied. 📖

## Job 11:2
“Should this flood of words receive no answer? Should a man who talks so much be declared right? 📖

## Job 11:3
Should your empty talk silence others? Should you mock without anyone correcting you? 📖

## Job 11:4
You have said, ‘My teaching is sound. I am pure in Your sight.’ 📖

## Job 11:5
If only God would speak! If only He would open His mouth against you! 📖

## Job 11:6
If only He would reveal wisdom's secrets to you! True wisdom has two sides. Know that God demands less from you than your wrongdoing deserves. 📖

## Job 11:7
Can you understand God's deep things? Can you discover the Almighty's limits? 📖

## Job 11:8
They are higher than the heavens. What can you do? They are deeper than Sheol. What can you know? 📖

## Job 11:9
Their measure is longer than the earth and wider than the sea. 📖

## Job 11:10
If He comes to imprison you or calls a court together, who can stop Him? 📖

## Job 11:11
He knows human deceit. If He sees wrongdoing, does He not notice it? 📖

## Job 11:12
A witless man cannot become wise, just as a wild donkey's colt cannot be born a man! 📖

## Job 11:13
Suppose you direct your heart to Him and lift your hands to Him. 📖

## Job 11:14
Suppose you remove wrongdoing from your hand and let no injustice stay in your tents. 📖

## Job 11:15
Then you will lift your face without blemish. You will stand firm and have no fear. 📖

## Job 11:16
You will forget your misery. You will remember it only as water that has passed by. 📖

## Job 11:17
Your life will shine more brightly than noon. Its darkness will be like morning. 📖

## Job 11:18
You will be secure because there is hope. You will look around and lie down safely. 📖

## Job 11:19
You will lie down without fear. Many people will seek your favor. 📖

## Job 11:20
But wicked people's eyes will fail. They will not escape. They will hope to breathe their last breath.” 📖
