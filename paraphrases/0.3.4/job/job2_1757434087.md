## Job 2:1
On another day, the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD. Satan also came with them to present himself before the LORD. 📖

## Job 2:2
The LORD asked Satan, “Where have you come from?” Satan replied, “I have been roaming the earth, walking back and forth on it.” 📖

## Job 2:3
The LORD asked Satan, “Have you considered My servant Job? No one on earth is like him. He is blameless and upright, fears God, and avoids evil. He remains faithful to what is right. You urged Me to ruin him without cause, but he still remains faithful.” 📖

## Job 2:4
Satan replied, “Skin for skin! A man will give up everything he owns to save his life. 📖

## Job 2:5
But reach out Your hand and strike his flesh and bones. He will certainly curse You to Your face.” 📖

## Job 2:6
The LORD told Satan, “He is in your hands. But you must spare his life.” 📖

## Job 2:7
Satan left the LORD's presence. He struck Job with terrible boils from the soles of his feet to the top of his head. 📖

## Job 2:8
Job sat among the ashes. He took a piece of broken pottery to scrape himself. 📖

## Job 2:9
His wife asked, “Do you still remain faithful to what is right? Curse God and die!” 📖

## Job 2:10
Job told her, “You speak like a foolish woman. Should we accept only good from God and refuse trouble?” Through all this, Job did not sin with his words. 📖

## Job 2:11
Job's three friends heard about all his troubles. They were Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite. Each left his home. They met to go and show sympathy to Job and comfort him. 📖

## Job 2:12
They saw Job from a distance and could barely recognize him. They wept aloud. Each tore his robe and threw dust into the air over his head. 📖

## Job 2:13
They sat on the ground with him for seven days and seven nights. No one spoke to him because they saw his terrible suffering. 📖
