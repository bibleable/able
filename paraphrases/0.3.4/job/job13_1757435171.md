## Job 13:1
“My eyes have seen all this. My ears have heard and understood it. 📖

## Job 13:2
I know what you know. I am not inferior to you. 📖

## Job 13:3
But I want to speak to the Almighty. I want to argue my case before God. 📖

## Job 13:4
You smear me with lies. All of you are worthless physicians. 📖

## Job 13:5
If only you would remain silent! That would be wisdom from you. 📖

## Job 13:6
Listen to my argument now. Hear the plea from my lips. 📖

## Job 13:7
Will you speak wickedly on God's behalf? Will you speak deceitfully for Him? 📖

## Job 13:8
Will you show favoritism to Him? Will you argue in His defense? 📖

## Job 13:9
Would things go well if He examined you? Could you deceive Him as you deceive a man? 📖

## Job 13:10
He would certainly rebuke you if you secretly showed favoritism. 📖

## Job 13:11
Would His majesty not terrify you? Would fear of Him not fall on you? 📖

## Job 13:12
Your sayings are proverbs of ashes. Your defenses are made from clay. 📖

## Job 13:13
Be silent, and I will speak. Then let whatever happens come to me. 📖

## Job 13:14
Why do I put myself at risk? Why do I take my life in my own hands? 📖

## Job 13:15
Even if He kills me, I will hope in Him. I will still defend my ways to His face. 📖

## Job 13:16
This will also be my salvation. No godless man can appear before Him. 📖

## Job 13:17
Listen carefully to my words. Let my declaration ring in your ears. 📖

## Job 13:18
I have prepared my case. I know I will be declared right. 📖

## Job 13:19
Can anyone bring charges against me? If so, I will be silent and die. 📖

## Job 13:20
Grant me just two things. Then I will not need to hide from You. 📖

## Job 13:21
Take Your hand away from me. Do not let Your terror frighten me. 📖

## Job 13:22
Then call me, and I will answer. Or let me speak, and You can reply. 📖

## Job 13:23
How many wrongs and sins have I committed? Show me my rebellion and sin. 📖

## Job 13:24
Why do You hide Your face? Why do You regard me as Your enemy? 📖

## Job 13:25
Would You frighten a leaf blown by the wind? Would You chase dry chaff? 📖

## Job 13:26
You record bitter accusations against me. You make me inherit the wrongdoing of my youth. 📖

## Job 13:27
You lock my feet in stocks. You watch all my paths. You limit where the soles of my feet can go. 📖

## Job 13:28
A man wastes away like something rotten. He is like a garment eaten by moths. 📖
