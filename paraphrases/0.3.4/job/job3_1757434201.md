## Job 3:1
After this, Job spoke and cursed the day he was born. 📖

## Job 3:2
He said these words. 📖

## Job 3:3
“May the day I was born disappear. May the night when someone said, ‘A boy has been conceived,’ disappear. 📖

## Job 3:4
If only that day had become darkness! May God above ignore it. May no light shine on it. 📖

## Job 3:5
May darkness and gloom claim it again. May a cloud settle over it. May blackness overwhelm that day. 📖

## Job 3:6
If only darkness had taken that night away! May it not be counted among the days of the year. May it never appear in any month. 📖

## Job 3:7
May that night be barren. May no joyful voice be heard in it. 📖

## Job 3:8
May those who curse the day curse it. These are the people ready to stir up Leviathan. 📖

## Job 3:9
May its morning stars become dark. May it wait for daylight that never comes. May it never see dawn break. 📖

## Job 3:10
That night did not close the doors of the womb. It did not hide sorrow from my eyes. 📖

## Job 3:11
Why did I not die at birth? Why did I not die when I came from the womb? 📖

## Job 3:12
Why were there knees to receive me? Why were there breasts to nurse me? 📖

## Job 3:13
Then I would now lie down in peace. I would sleep and rest. 📖

## Job 3:14
I would rest with the earth's kings and counselors. They built cities for themselves that now lie in ruins. 📖

## Job 3:15
Or I would rest with princes who owned gold and filled their houses with silver. 📖

## Job 3:16
Why was I not hidden like a stillborn child, like an infant who never sees daylight? 📖

## Job 3:17
There the wicked stop raging. There the weary find rest. 📖

## Job 3:18
Captives rest in peace. They no longer hear the voice of their oppressor. 📖

## Job 3:19
The small and great are both there. The slave is free from his master. 📖

## Job 3:20
Why is light given to miserable people? Why is life given to those whose souls are bitter? 📖

## Job 3:21
They long for death, but it does not come. They search for it as they would search for hidden treasure. 📖

## Job 3:22
They are filled with joy when they reach the grave. 📖

## Job 3:23
Why is life given to a man whose path is hidden? God has enclosed him within a hedge. 📖

## Job 3:24
I sigh when food is set before me. My groans pour out like water. 📖

## Job 3:25
What I feared has overtaken me. What I dreaded has happened to me. 📖

## Job 3:26
I have no ease, quiet, or rest. Trouble has come.” 📖
