## Job 16:1
Job answered. 📖

## Job 16:2
“I have heard many things like these. All of you are miserable comforters. 📖

## Job 16:3
Will your long speeches never end? What makes you keep testifying? 📖

## Job 16:4
If you were in my place, I could speak like you. I could pile up words against you and shake my head at you. 📖

## Job 16:5
But I would encourage you with my words. The comfort from my lips would bring relief. 📖

## Job 16:6
Speaking does not ease my pain. If I remain silent, how will it go away? 📖

## Job 16:7
He has exhausted me. You have destroyed all my family. 📖

## Job 16:8
You have bound me, and this has become a witness. My weakness rises and testifies against me. 📖

## Job 16:9
His anger has torn me and opposed me. He grinds His teeth at me. My enemy pierces me with His eyes. 📖

## Job 16:10
They open their mouths against me. They slap my cheeks with contempt. They gather together against me. 📖

## Job 16:11
God has handed me over to unjust men. He has thrown me into wicked people's grip. 📖

## Job 16:12
I was at ease, but He shattered me. He seized my neck and crushed me. He made me His target. 📖

## Job 16:13
His archers surround me. He pierces my kidneys without mercy. He spills my gall on the ground. 📖

## Job 16:14
He breaks me with wound after wound. He rushes at me like a mighty warrior. 📖

## Job 16:15
I have sewn sackcloth over my skin. I have buried my horn in the dust. 📖

## Job 16:16
My face is red from weeping. Deep shadows surround my eyes. 📖

## Job 16:17
Yet my hands are free from violence. My prayer is pure. 📖

## Job 16:18
Earth, do not cover my blood. May my cry for help never be laid to rest. 📖

## Job 16:19
Even now, my witness is in heaven. My advocate is on high. 📖

## Job 16:20
My friends mock me. My eyes pour out tears to God. 📖

## Job 16:21
If only a man could plead with God as he pleads with his neighbor! 📖

## Job 16:22
In just a few years, I will go down the path from which no one returns. 📖
