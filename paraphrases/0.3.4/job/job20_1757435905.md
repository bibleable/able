## Job 20:1
Zophar the Naamathite replied. 📖

## Job 20:2
“My anxious thoughts force me to answer. I am disturbed within myself. 📖

## Job 20:3
I have heard a rebuke that insults me. My understanding moves me to reply. 📖

## Job 20:4
Do you not know what has been true since ancient times, since people were placed on earth? 📖

## Job 20:5
Wicked people's triumph is brief. Godless people's joy lasts only a moment. 📖

## Job 20:6
A wicked man's pride may reach the heavens. His head may touch the clouds. 📖

## Job 20:7
Yet he will die forever, like his own dung. Those who saw him will ask, ‘Where is he?’ 📖

## Job 20:8
He will fly away like a dream, never to be found. He will be chased away like a vision in the night. 📖

## Job 20:9
The eye that saw him will see him no longer. His place will no longer see him. 📖

## Job 20:10
His sons will seek the poor people's favor. His own hands must return his wealth. 📖

## Job 20:11
Youthful strength fills his bones. But it will lie down with him in the dust. 📖

## Job 20:12
Evil may taste sweet in his mouth. He may hide it under his tongue. 📖

## Job 20:13
He may refuse to let it go. He may keep it in his mouth. 📖

## Job 20:14
Yet the food turns sour in his stomach. Within him, it becomes cobra venom. 📖

## Job 20:15
He swallows wealth but vomits it out. God will force it out of his stomach. 📖

## Job 20:16
He will suck cobra poison. A viper's fangs will kill him. 📖

## Job 20:17
He will not enjoy the streams and rivers flowing with honey and cream. 📖

## Job 20:18
He must return what his labor produced without consuming it. He cannot enjoy the profits from his trading. 📖

## Job 20:19
He has oppressed and abandoned poor people. He has seized houses he did not build. 📖

## Job 20:20
His appetite is never satisfied. Therefore, he cannot escape with his treasure. 📖

## Job 20:21
Nothing remains for him to consume. Therefore, his prosperity will not last. 📖

## Job 20:22
Even with all his plenty, he will be distressed. Misery will come against him with full force. 📖

## Job 20:23
When his stomach is full, God will release His fury on him. He will rain it on him while he eats. 📖

## Job 20:24
He may flee an iron weapon. But an arrow with a bronze tip will pierce him. 📖

## Job 20:25
The arrow is pulled from his back. Its shining point comes out of his liver. Terrors seize him. 📖

## Job 20:26
Complete darkness is reserved for his treasures. A fire that no one fans will consume him. It will eat what remains in his tent. 📖

## Job 20:27
The heavens will expose his wrongdoing. The earth will rise against him. 📖

## Job 20:28
His household possessions will be removed. They will flow away on the day of God's wrath. 📖

## Job 20:29
This is a wicked man's portion from God. It is the inheritance God has assigned him.” 📖
