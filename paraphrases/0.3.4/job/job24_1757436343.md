## Job 24:1
“Why does the Almighty not set times for judgment? Why do those who know Him never see His days? 📖

## Job 24:2
Men move boundary stones. They graze stolen flocks. 📖

## Job 24:3
They drive away the fatherless child's donkey. They take the widow's ox as security for a debt. 📖

## Job 24:4
They push needy people off the road. They force all the land's poor people to hide. 📖

## Job 24:5
Poor people work to find food like wild donkeys in the desert. The wasteland provides food for their children. 📖

## Job 24:6
They gather animal food in the fields. They collect leftover grapes in wicked people's vineyards. 📖

## Job 24:7
They spend the night naked, without clothing. They have no covering against the cold. 📖

## Job 24:8
Mountain rains soak them. They press against the rocks because they have no shelter. 📖

## Job 24:9
A fatherless infant is taken from the breast. A poor person's nursing child is seized for a debt. 📖

## Job 24:10
Without clothing, they wander naked. They carry bundles of grain but remain hungry. 📖

## Job 24:11
They crush olives within their walls. They tread the winepresses but remain thirsty. 📖

## Job 24:12
Men groan in the city. Wounded people's souls cry out. Yet God accuses no one of wrongdoing. 📖

## Job 24:13
Some people rebel against the light. They do not know its ways or stay on its paths. 📖

## Job 24:14
When daylight is gone, the murderer rises to kill poor and needy people. At night, he is like a thief. 📖

## Job 24:15
The adulterer watches for twilight. He thinks, ‘No eye will see me.’ He covers his face. 📖

## Job 24:16
In darkness, they dig through houses. During the day, they shut themselves inside. They never experience the light. 📖

## Job 24:17
Deep darkness is their morning. They are friends with the terrors of darkness! 📖

## Job 24:18
They are only foam on the water's surface. Their portion of land is cursed. No one turns toward their vineyards. 📖

## Job 24:19
Drought and heat consume melting snow. In the same way, Sheol takes those who have sinned. 📖

## Job 24:20
The womb forgets them. Worms feed on them. No one remembers them anymore. Injustice is broken like a tree. 📖

## Job 24:21
They prey on barren, childless women. They show no kindness to widows. 📖

## Job 24:22
Yet God drags the mighty away by His power. Even when they rise, they have no assurance of life. 📖

## Job 24:23
He gives them a sense of security. But His eyes watch their ways. 📖

## Job 24:24
They are raised high for a moment, then they are gone. They are brought low and gathered like everyone else. They are cut off like heads of grain. 📖

## Job 24:25
If this is not true, who can prove me a liar? Who can reduce my words to nothing?” 📖
