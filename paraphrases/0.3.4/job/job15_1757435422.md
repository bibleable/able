## Job 15:1
Eliphaz the Temanite replied. 📖

## Job 15:2
“Does a wise man answer with empty advice? Does he fill his belly with the hot east wind? 📖

## Job 15:3
Should he argue with useless words? Should he give speeches that serve no purpose? 📖

## Job 15:4
You even weaken the fear of God. You hinder meditation before Him. 📖

## Job 15:5
Your wrongdoing teaches your mouth. You choose crafty people's language. 📖

## Job 15:6
Your own mouth condemns you. Mine does not. Your own lips testify against you. 📖

## Job 15:7
Were you the first man born? Were you brought into existence before the hills? 📖

## Job 15:8
Do you listen to God's council? Do you keep wisdom only for yourself? 📖

## Job 15:9
What do you know that we do not know? What do you understand that we do not understand? 📖

## Job 15:10
People with gray hair and the aged are on our side. They are much older than your father. 📖

## Job 15:11
Is God's comfort not enough for you? Are words spoken gently to you not enough? 📖

## Job 15:12
Why has your heart carried you away? Why do your eyes flash? 📖

## Job 15:13
Why do you turn your spirit against God? Why do you pour such words from your mouth? 📖

## Job 15:14
What is a man, that he should be pure? How can someone born of woman be righteous? 📖

## Job 15:15
Suppose God does not trust His holy ones. Suppose even the heavens are not pure in His eyes. 📖

## Job 15:16
How much less pure is a vile and corrupt man? He drinks injustice like water. 📖

## Job 15:17
Listen to me, and I will tell you. I will describe what I have seen. 📖

## Job 15:18
Wise men declared these things. These things were not hidden from their fathers. 📖

## Job 15:19
The land was given to those fathers alone. No foreigner passed among them. 📖

## Job 15:20
A wicked man twists in pain all his days. Only a few years are reserved for a ruthless man. 📖

## Job 15:21
Terrifying sounds fill his ears. The destroyer attacks him during his prosperity. 📖

## Job 15:22
He has no hope of returning from darkness. He is marked for the sword. 📖

## Job 15:23
He wanders as food for vultures. He knows the day of darkness is near. 📖

## Job 15:24
Distress and anguish terrify him. They overwhelm him like a king ready to attack. 📖

## Job 15:25
He has reached out his hand against God. He has proudly challenged the Almighty. 📖

## Job 15:26
He rushes directly at God with a thick shield covered with studs. 📖

## Job 15:27
His face may be covered with fat. His waist may bulge with flesh. 📖

## Job 15:28
Yet he will live in ruined cities. He will occupy abandoned houses destined to become rubble. 📖

## Job 15:29
He will no longer be rich. His wealth will not last. His possessions will not spread over the land. 📖

## Job 15:30
He will not escape darkness. Flames will dry up his shoots. The breath from God's mouth will carry him away. 📖

## Job 15:31
He must not deceive himself by trusting emptiness. Emptiness will be his reward. 📖

## Job 15:32
He will receive this reward in full before his time. His branch will not flourish. 📖

## Job 15:33
He will be like a vine stripped of its unripe grapes. He will be like an olive tree that drops its blossoms. 📖

## Job 15:34
The company of godless people will be barren. Fire will consume tents filled with bribery. 📖

## Job 15:35
They conceive trouble and give birth to evil. Their womb is pregnant with deceit.” 📖
