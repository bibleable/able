## Job 41:1
“Can you pull Leviathan in with a hook? Can you tie down his tongue with a rope? 📖

## Job 41:2
Can you put a cord through his nose? Can you pierce his jaw with a hook? 📖

## Job 41:3
Will he beg you for mercy? Will he speak softly to you? 📖

## Job 41:4
Will he make a covenant with you? Will he agree to become your slave for life? 📖

## Job 41:5
Can you pet him like a bird? Can you put him on a leash for your maidens? 📖

## Job 41:6
Will traders bargain for him? Will they divide him among merchants? 📖

## Job 41:7
Can you fill his hide with harpoons? Can you fill his head with fishing spears? 📖

## Job 41:8
If you put your hand on him, you will remember the battle. You will never repeat it! 📖

## Job 41:9
Hope of overcoming him is false. Does the sight of him not overwhelm you? 📖

## Job 41:10
No one is fierce enough to stir up Leviathan. Then who can stand against Me? 📖

## Job 41:11
Who has given Me anything that I must repay? Everything under heaven belongs to Me. 📖

## Job 41:12
I cannot remain silent about his limbs. I must speak of his power and graceful form. 📖

## Job 41:13
Who can strip off his outer coat? Who can approach him with a bridle? 📖

## Job 41:14
Who can open his jaws? Fearsome teeth surround them. 📖

## Job 41:15
His rows of scales are his pride. They are tightly sealed together. 📖

## Job 41:16
Each scale is so close to the next that no air passes between them. 📖

## Job 41:17
They are joined together. They hold tightly and cannot be separated. 📖

## Job 41:18
His snorting flashes with light. His eyes are like dawn's rays. 📖

## Job 41:19
Burning brands stream from his mouth. Fiery sparks shoot out! 📖

## Job 41:20
Smoke pours from his nostrils. It is like smoke from a boiling pot over burning reeds. 📖

## Job 41:21
His breath sets coals on fire. Flames pour from his mouth. 📖

## Job 41:22
Strength lives in his neck. Dismay leaps before him. 📖

## Job 41:23
The folds of his flesh are tightly joined. They are firm and cannot be moved. 📖

## Job 41:24
His chest is as hard as rock. It is as hard as a lower millstone! 📖

## Job 41:25
When Leviathan rises, the mighty are terrified. They retreat before his violent movements. 📖

## Job 41:26
A sword that reaches him has no effect. Neither does a spear, dart, or arrow. 📖

## Job 41:27
He treats iron like straw. He treats bronze like rotten wood. 📖

## Job 41:28
An arrow cannot make him flee. Stones from a sling are like chaff to him. 📖

## Job 41:29
He regards a club as straw. He laughs at the sound of a lance. 📖

## Job 41:30
His underside is like jagged pieces of pottery. It spreads the mud like a threshing sledge. 📖

## Job 41:31
He makes the depths boil like a cauldron. He makes the sea like a jar of ointment. 📖

## Job 41:32
He leaves a shining trail behind him. The deep looks as if it has white hair! 📖

## Job 41:33
Nothing on earth equals him. He is a creature without fear! 📖

## Job 41:34
He looks down on everyone who is haughty. He is king over all the proud.” 📖
