## Job 40:1
The LORD spoke to Job. 📖

## Job 40:2
“Will someone who finds fault argue with the Almighty? Let the person who argues with God answer.” 📖

## Job 40:3
Job answered the LORD. 📖

## Job 40:4
“I am insignificant. How can I reply to You? I put my hand over my mouth. 📖

## Job 40:5
I spoke once, but I have no answer. I spoke twice, but I have nothing to add.” 📖

## Job 40:6
The LORD answered Job from the whirlwind. He spoke these words. 📖

## Job 40:7
“Prepare yourself like a man. I will question you, and you must answer Me. 📖

## Job 40:8
Would you cancel My justice? Would you condemn Me to justify yourself? 📖

## Job 40:9
Do you have an arm like God's? Can your voice thunder like His? 📖

## Job 40:10
Then decorate yourself with majesty and splendor. Clothe yourself with honor and glory. 📖

## Job 40:11
Release the fury of your anger. Look at every proud man and bring him low. 📖

## Job 40:12
Look at every proud man and humble him. Trample wicked people where they stand. 📖

## Job 40:13
Bury them together in the dust. Imprison them in the grave. 📖

## Job 40:14
Then I will admit that your own right hand can save you. 📖

## Job 40:15
Look at Behemoth, which I made along with you. He eats grass like an ox. 📖

## Job 40:16
See the strength in his loins. See the power in his belly's muscles. 📖

## Job 40:17
His tail sways like a cedar. The sinews of his thighs are tightly joined. 📖

## Job 40:18
His bones are bronze tubes. His limbs are iron rods. 📖

## Job 40:19
He is the foremost of God's works. Only his Maker can draw a sword against him. 📖

## Job 40:20
The hills provide him with their produce. All the field's animals play nearby. 📖

## Job 40:21
He lies under lotus plants. Reeds in the marsh hide him. 📖

## Job 40:22
Lotus plants hide him in their shade. The brook's willows surround him. 📖

## Job 40:23
Behemoth is not afraid when the river rages. He stays secure even when the Jordan surges to his mouth. 📖

## Job 40:24
Can anyone capture him while he watches? Can anyone pierce his nose with a snare? 📖
