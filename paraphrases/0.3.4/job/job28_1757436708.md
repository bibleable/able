## Job 28:1
“There is a mine for silver. There is a place to refine gold. 📖

## Job 28:2
Iron is taken from the earth. Copper is extracted from ore by melting it. 📖

## Job 28:3
Man puts an end to darkness. He searches the farthest hidden places for ore in deep darkness. 📖

## Job 28:4
Far from human homes, he cuts a shaft where no one walks. Far from people, he hangs and swings. 📖

## Job 28:5
Food comes from the earth. But below, the earth is changed as if by fire. 📖

## Job 28:6
Its rocks provide sapphires. They contain small pieces of gold. 📖

## Job 28:7
No bird of prey knows that path. No falcon's eye has seen it. 📖

## Job 28:8
Proud beasts have never walked on it. No lion has ever prowled there. 📖

## Job 28:9
The miner strikes the flint. He overturns mountains at their bases. 📖

## Job 28:10
He cuts channels in the rocks. His eyes find every treasure. 📖

## Job 28:11
He blocks the sources of streams. He brings hidden things into the light. 📖

## Job 28:12
But where can wisdom be found? Where does understanding live? 📖

## Job 28:13
No one can know its value. It is not found in the land of the living. 📖

## Job 28:14
The ocean depths say, ‘It is not in me.’ The sea says, ‘It is not with me.’ 📖

## Job 28:15
Gold cannot buy it. Silver cannot be weighed out to pay for it. 📖

## Job 28:16
It cannot be valued in gold from Ophir. Precious onyx and sapphire cannot measure its worth. 📖

## Job 28:17
Gold and crystal cannot compare with it. Jewels of fine gold cannot be exchanged for it. 📖

## Job 28:18
Coral and quartz are not worth mentioning. Wisdom's price is greater than rubies. 📖

## Job 28:19
Topaz from Cush cannot compare with it. Pure gold cannot measure its value. 📖

## Job 28:20
Where does wisdom come from? Where does understanding live? 📖

## Job 28:21
It is hidden from every living thing's eyes. It is concealed from the birds of the air. 📖

## Job 28:22
Abaddon and Death say, ‘We have heard a rumor about it.’ 📖

## Job 28:23
But God understands its way. He knows its place. 📖

## Job 28:24
He looks to the ends of the earth. He sees everything under the heavens. 📖

## Job 28:25
God fixed the wind's weight. He measured the waters. 📖

## Job 28:26
He set a limit for the rain. He set a path for the thunderbolt. 📖

## Job 28:27
Then He looked at wisdom and assessed its value. He established it and searched it out. 📖

## Job 28:28
He told mankind, ‘Fear of the Lord is wisdom. Turning away from evil is understanding.’” 📖
