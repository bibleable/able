## Job 31:1
“I have made a covenant with my eyes. How could I look with desire at a virgin? 📖

## Job 31:2
What portion comes from God above? What inheritance comes from the Almighty on high? 📖

## Job 31:3
Does disaster not come to unjust people? Does calamity not come to those who do wrong? 📖

## Job 31:4
Does He not see my ways? Does He not count every step I take? 📖

## Job 31:5
If I have walked in falsehood, or if my foot has rushed toward deceit, consider this. 📖

## Job 31:6
Let God weigh me on honest scales. Then He will know my integrity. 📖

## Job 31:7
Suppose my steps have left the path. Suppose my heart has followed my eyes, or impurity has stuck to my hands. 📖

## Job 31:8
Then may someone else eat what I planted. May my crops be pulled up. 📖

## Job 31:9
Suppose my neighbor's wife has attracted my heart. Suppose I have waited secretly at his door. 📖

## Job 31:10
Then may my wife grind grain for another. May other men sleep with her. 📖

## Job 31:11
That would be a terrible crime. It would be wrongdoing that deserves judgment. 📖

## Job 31:12
It is a fire that burns down to Abaddon. It would destroy my whole harvest at its roots. 📖

## Job 31:13
Suppose I rejected my male or female servant's case when they complained against me. 📖

## Job 31:14
What would I do when God rose to judge? How would I answer when He called me to account? 📖

## Job 31:15
Did the One who made me in the womb not also make them? Did the same One not form us in the womb? 📖

## Job 31:16
Suppose I denied poor people's desires. Suppose I let a widow's eyes fail. 📖

## Job 31:17
Suppose I ate my food alone and did not share it with fatherless children. 📖

## Job 31:18
Yet from my youth I raised the fatherless child like a father. From my mother's womb, I guided the widow. 📖

## Job 31:19
Suppose I saw someone dying because he lacked clothing. Suppose I saw a needy man without a cloak. 📖

## Job 31:20
Suppose his heart did not bless me for warming him with wool from my sheep. 📖

## Job 31:21
Suppose I raised my hand against the fatherless child because I saw support for myself at the gate. 📖

## Job 31:22
Then may my arm fall from my shoulder. May it be torn from its socket. 📖

## Job 31:23
Disaster from God terrifies me. I cannot overpower His splendor. 📖

## Job 31:24
Suppose I trusted gold. Suppose I called pure gold my security. 📖

## Job 31:25
Suppose I rejoiced in my great wealth because my hand had gained so much. 📖

## Job 31:26
Suppose I looked at the shining sun or the moon moving in splendor. 📖

## Job 31:27
Suppose they secretly attracted my heart, and my hand blew them a kiss from my mouth. 📖

## Job 31:28
That would also be wrongdoing that deserves judgment. I would have denied God on high. 📖

## Job 31:29
Suppose I rejoiced at my enemy's ruin. Suppose I celebrated when evil came to him. 📖

## Job 31:30
Yet I have not let my mouth sin by calling for his death with a curse. 📖

## Job 31:31
Suppose the men in my house did not say, ‘Who has not had enough to eat?’ 📖

## Job 31:32
Yet no stranger had to sleep in the street. My door was open to travelers. 📖

## Job 31:33
Suppose I covered my rebellion like Adam. Suppose I hid my guilt in my heart. 📖

## Job 31:34
Suppose I greatly feared the crowds and was terrified by the clans' contempt. Suppose this kept me silent and stopped me from going outside. 📖

## Job 31:35
(If only someone would hear me! Here is my signature. Let the Almighty answer me. Let my accuser write the charges against me. 📖

## Job 31:36
I would carry them on my shoulder. I would wear them like a crown. 📖

## Job 31:37
I would account for every step I took. I would approach Him like a prince.) 📖

## Job 31:38
Suppose my land cries out against me. Suppose its furrows weep together. 📖

## Job 31:39
Suppose I consumed its produce without paying. Suppose I broke its tenants' spirit. 📖

## Job 31:40
Then let briers grow instead of wheat. Let stinkweed grow instead of barley.” This ends Job's words. 📖
