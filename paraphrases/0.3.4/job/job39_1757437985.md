## Job 39:1
“Do you know when mountain goats give birth? Have you watched a doe give birth to her fawn? 📖

## Job 39:2
Can you count the months of their pregnancy? Do you know when they give birth? 📖

## Job 39:3
They crouch and give birth. They deliver their young. 📖

## Job 39:4
Their young thrive and grow in the open field. They leave and do not return. 📖

## Job 39:5
Who freed the wild donkey? Who released the swift donkey from its harness? 📖

## Job 39:6
I made the wilderness his home. I made the salt flats his dwelling. 📖

## Job 39:7
He despises the city's noise. He never hears a driver's shouts. 📖

## Job 39:8
He roams the mountains to find pasture. He searches for anything green. 📖

## Job 39:9
Will the wild ox agree to serve you? Will he stay beside your manger at night? 📖

## Job 39:10
Can you hold him in a furrow with a harness? Will he plow the valleys behind you? 📖

## Job 39:11
Can you rely on his great strength? Will you leave your hard work to him? 📖

## Job 39:12
Can you trust him to bring in your grain? Will he gather it to your threshing floor? 📖

## Job 39:13
The ostrich's wings flap joyfully. But they cannot match the stork's wings and feathers. 📖

## Job 39:14
She leaves her eggs on the ground. She lets them become warm in the sand. 📖

## Job 39:15
She forgets that a foot could crush them. A wild animal could trample them. 📖

## Job 39:16
She treats her young harshly, as if they were not hers. She does not care if her labor was wasted. 📖

## Job 39:17
God has denied her wisdom. He has not given her understanding. 📖

## Job 39:18
Yet when she proudly spreads her wings, she laughs at the horse and its rider. 📖

## Job 39:19
Do you give the horse strength? Do you decorate his neck with a mane? 📖

## Job 39:20
Do you make him leap like a locust? His proud snorting causes terror. 📖

## Job 39:21
He paws the ground in the valley. He rejoices in his strength. He charges into battle. 📖

## Job 39:22
He laughs at fear. Nothing frightens him. He does not turn back from the sword. 📖

## Job 39:23
A quiver rattles at his side. A flashing spear and lance also rattle there. 📖

## Job 39:24
He trembles with excitement and devours the distance. He cannot stand still when the ram's horn sounds. 📖

## Job 39:25
He snorts eagerly when the horn sounds. He smells battle from far away. He hears captains shouting and the cry of war. 📖

## Job 39:26
Does your understanding make the hawk fly? Does it make him spread his wings southward? 📖

## Job 39:27
Does the eagle rise high at your command? Does he build his nest on high because you command him? 📖

## Job 39:28
He lives and stays on a cliff. His stronghold is on a rocky peak. 📖

## Job 39:29
He searches for food from there. His eyes see it far away. 📖

## Job 39:30
His young feast on blood. He is where those who have been killed lie.” 📖
