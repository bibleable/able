## Job 6:1
Job replied. 📖

## Job 6:2
“If only someone could weigh my grief! If only they could put it on the scales with my disaster! 📖

## Job 6:3
Together they would weigh more than the sand of the seas. No wonder I have spoken rashly. 📖

## Job 6:4
The Almighty's arrows have pierced me. My spirit drinks their poison. God's terrors are lined up against me. 📖

## Job 6:5
Does a wild donkey bray when it has fresh grass? Does an ox bellow when it has food? 📖

## Job 6:6
Do people eat tasteless food without salt? Does the white of an egg have flavor? 📖

## Job 6:7
My soul refuses to touch such food. It disgusts me. 📖

## Job 6:8
If only God would grant my request! If only He would fulfill my hope! 📖

## Job 6:9
If only God would agree to crush me! If only He would release His hand and cut me off! 📖

## Job 6:10
I have not denied the Holy One's words. This still comforts me and gives me joy through pain that never stops. 📖

## Job 6:11
What strength do I have to keep hoping? What future do I have to make me patient? 📖

## Job 6:12
Am I as strong as stone? Is my flesh made from bronze? 📖

## Job 6:13
Do I have any help within myself? Success has been driven away from me. 📖

## Job 6:14
A man in despair should receive kindness from his friend, even if he abandons fear of the Almighty. 📖

## Job 6:15
My brothers are as unreliable as wadis. They are like seasonal streams that overflow. 📖

## Job 6:16
Ice and melting snow make these streams dark. 📖

## Job 6:17
But they stop flowing in the dry season. Their channels become empty in the heat. 📖

## Job 6:18
Caravans turn away from their routes. They go into the wasteland and die. 📖

## Job 6:19
Caravans from Tema look for water. Travelers from Sheba hope to find it. 📖

## Job 6:20
Their hopes leave them confused. They arrive and find disappointment. 📖

## Job 6:21
Now you are no help to me. You see something terrible, and you are afraid. 📖

## Job 6:22
Did I ever ask you to give me something? Did I ask you to offer a bribe from your wealth? 📖

## Job 6:23
Did I ask you to save me from my enemy's hand? Did I ask you to redeem me from the grip of ruthless people? 📖

## Job 6:24
Teach me, and I will be silent. Show me where I have gone wrong. 📖

## Job 6:25
Honest words are painful! But what does your argument prove? 📖

## Job 6:26
Do you want to correct my words? Will you treat my desperate cries as wind? 📖

## Job 6:27
You would even cast lots for an orphan. You would trade away your friend. 📖

## Job 6:28
Please look at me now. Would I lie to your face? 📖

## Job 6:29
Think again, and do not be unjust. Think again, because my righteousness is at stake. 📖

## Job 6:30
Is there wrongdoing on my tongue? Can my mouth not recognize malice? 📖
