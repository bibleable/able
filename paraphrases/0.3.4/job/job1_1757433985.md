## Job 1:1
A man named Job lived in the land of Uz. He was blameless and upright. He feared God and avoided evil. 📖

## Job 1:2
He had seven sons and three daughters. 📖

## Job 1:3
He owned 7,000 sheep, 3,000 camels, 500 pairs of oxen, and 500 female donkeys. He had many servants. He was the greatest man among all the people of the East. 📖

## Job 1:4
Job's sons took turns holding feasts in their homes. They invited their three sisters to eat and drink with them. 📖

## Job 1:5
After each period of feasting, Job sent for his children to purify them. He got up early to offer burnt offerings for all of them. He thought, “Perhaps my children sinned and cursed God in their hearts.” Job did this regularly. 📖

## Job 1:6
One day, the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD. Satan came with them. 📖

## Job 1:7
The LORD asked Satan, “Where have you come from?” Satan replied, “I have been roaming the earth, walking back and forth on it.” 📖

## Job 1:8
The LORD asked Satan, “Have you considered My servant Job? No one on earth is like him. He is blameless and upright. He fears God and avoids evil.” 📖

## Job 1:9
Satan answered the LORD, “Does Job fear God without getting anything in return? 📖

## Job 1:10
You have put a protective hedge around him, his household, and everything he owns. You have blessed the work of his hands. His possessions have increased throughout the land. 📖

## Job 1:11
But reach out Your hand and strike everything he owns. He will certainly curse You to Your face.” 📖

## Job 1:12
The LORD told Satan, “Everything he owns is in your hands. But you must not touch Job himself.” Then Satan left the LORD's presence. 📖

## Job 1:13
One day, Job's sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother's house. 📖

## Job 1:14
A messenger came to Job. He said, “The oxen were plowing, and the donkeys were grazing nearby. 📖

## Job 1:15
The Sabeans attacked and took them away. They killed the servants with swords. I alone escaped to tell you!” 📖

## Job 1:16
While he was speaking, another messenger arrived. He said, “The fire of God fell from heaven. It burned up the sheep and servants. I alone escaped to tell you!” 📖

## Job 1:17
While he was speaking, another messenger arrived. He said, “The Chaldeans formed three groups and raided the camels. They took them away and killed the servants with swords. I alone escaped to tell you!” 📖

## Job 1:18
While he was speaking, another messenger arrived. He said, “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother's house. 📖

## Job 1:19
A strong wind suddenly came from the desert and struck the house at its four corners. The house collapsed on the young people and killed them. I alone escaped to tell you!” 📖

## Job 1:20
Job stood up, tore his robe, and shaved his head. He fell to the ground and worshiped. 📖

## Job 1:21
He said, “I came naked from my mother's womb, and I will return naked. The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away. May the LORD's name be blessed.” 📖

## Job 1:22
Through all this, Job did not sin or accuse God of wrongdoing. 📖
