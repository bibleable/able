## Job 30:1
“But now younger men mock me. I would not have put their fathers with my sheep dogs. 📖

## Job 30:2
What use was the strength of their hands to me? Their vigor had left them. 📖

## Job 30:3
Poverty and hunger made them thin. At night, they gnawed the dry ground in the empty wasteland. 📖

## Job 30:4
They picked mallow among the shrubs. They ate broom tree roots. 📖

## Job 30:5
They were driven away from people. People shouted at them as if they were thieves. 📖

## Job 30:6
They lived on the slopes of wadis. They lived among rocks and in holes in the ground. 📖

## Job 30:7
They cried out among the shrubs. They crowded together beneath the nettles. 📖

## Job 30:8
They were a senseless and nameless group. They were driven from the land. 📖

## Job 30:9
Now they mock me in songs. I have become a saying among them. 📖

## Job 30:10
They hate me and stay far away. They do not hesitate to spit in my face. 📖

## Job 30:11
God has removed my bowstring and made me suffer. Therefore, they act without restraint in my presence. 📖

## Job 30:12
The mob rises at my right side. They set traps for my feet. They build siege ramps against me. 📖

## Job 30:13
They tear up my path. They gain from my destruction. No one restrains them. 📖

## Job 30:14
They advance as if through a wide break in a wall. They keep rolling through the ruins. 📖

## Job 30:15
Terrors are released against me. They drive away my dignity as if by wind. My prosperity passes like a cloud. 📖

## Job 30:16
Now my soul pours out within me. Days of suffering grip me. 📖

## Job 30:17
Night pierces my bones. The pain that gnaws at me never rests. 📖

## Job 30:18
He grips my garment with great force. He seizes the collar of my tunic. 📖

## Job 30:19
He throws me into the mud. I have become like dust and ashes. 📖

## Job 30:20
I cry to You for help, but You do not answer. I stand up, but You only look at me. 📖

## Job 30:21
You have turned against me without mercy. You oppose me with Your strong hand. 📖

## Job 30:22
You lift me into the wind and drive me before it. You toss me around in the storm. 📖

## Job 30:23
I know You will bring me to death. It is the place assigned to everyone who lives. 📖

## Job 30:24
Yet no one reaches out to attack a ruined man when he cries for help in distress. 📖

## Job 30:25
Have I not wept for people in trouble? Has my soul not grieved for needy people? 📖

## Job 30:26
I hoped for good, but evil came. I looked for light, but darkness fell. 📖

## Job 30:27
I churn within and cannot rest. Days of suffering confront me. 📖

## Job 30:28
I walk around blackened, but the sun did not cause it. I stand in the assembly and cry for help. 📖

## Job 30:29
I have become a brother to jackals. I am a companion to ostriches. 📖

## Job 30:30
My skin turns black and peels. My bones burn with fever. 📖

## Job 30:31
My harp is tuned to mourning. My flute is tuned to the sound of weeping. 📖
