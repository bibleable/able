## Job 23:1
Job answered. 📖

## Job 23:2
“Even today, my complaint is bitter. His hand remains heavy despite my groans. 📖

## Job 23:3
If only I knew where to find Him! Then I could go to His seat. 📖

## Job 23:4
I would plead my case before Him. I would fill my mouth with arguments. 📖

## Job 23:5
I would learn His answer. I would consider His words. 📖

## Job 23:6
Would He oppose me with His great power? No, He would certainly take notice of me. 📖

## Job 23:7
Then an upright man could reason with Him. I would be rescued forever from my Judge. 📖

## Job 23:8
If I go east, He is not there. If I go west, I cannot find Him. 📖

## Job 23:9
When He works in the north, I cannot see Him. When He turns south, I cannot see Him. 📖

## Job 23:10
Yet He knows the way I have taken. When He has tested me, I will come out as gold. 📖

## Job 23:11
My feet have followed His tracks. I have stayed on His way without turning aside. 📖

## Job 23:12
I have not left the command from His lips. I have treasured His words more than my daily bread. 📖

## Job 23:13
But He does not change. Who can oppose Him? He does what He desires. 📖

## Job 23:14
He carries out His decree against me. He has many such plans. 📖

## Job 23:15
Therefore, I am terrified in His presence. When I consider this, I fear Him. 📖

## Job 23:16
God has made my heart faint. The Almighty has terrified me. 📖

## Job 23:17
Yet darkness does not silence me. Even the thick darkness covering my face does not silence me. 📖
