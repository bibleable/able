## Job 22:1
Eliphaz the Temanite replied. 📖

## Job 22:2
“Can a man be useful to God? Can even a wise man benefit Him? 📖

## Job 22:3
Does your righteousness delight the Almighty? Does He gain anything if your ways are blameless? 📖

## Job 22:4
Does He rebuke you because of your reverence? Is that why He brings you to judgment? 📖

## Job 22:5
Is your wickedness not great? Are your wrongdoings not endless? 📖

## Job 22:6
You demanded security from your brothers without need. You took clothing from people who were naked. 📖

## Job 22:7
You gave no water to weary people. You withheld food from starving people. 📖

## Job 22:8
Meanwhile, a mighty man owned the land. A man of honor lived on it. 📖

## Job 22:9
You sent widows away with empty hands. The strength of fatherless children was crushed. 📖

## Job 22:10
Therefore, snares surround you. Sudden danger terrifies you. 📖

## Job 22:11
It is so dark that you cannot see. A flood of water covers you. 📖

## Job 22:12
Is God not as high as the heavens? Look how high the highest stars are! 📖

## Job 22:13
Yet you say, ‘What does God know? Does He judge through thick darkness? 📖

## Job 22:14
Thick clouds cover Him so He cannot see us. He walks across the vault of heaven.’ 📖

## Job 22:15
Will you stay on the ancient path that wicked men walked? 📖

## Job 22:16
They were taken away before their time. A flood swept away their foundations. 📖

## Job 22:17
They told God, ‘Leave us. What can the Almighty do to us?’ 📖

## Job 22:18
Yet He filled their houses with good things. Therefore, I stay far from wicked people's counsel. 📖

## Job 22:19
Righteous people see this and are glad. Innocent people mock them. 📖

## Job 22:20
They say, ‘Our enemies are destroyed. Fire has consumed their surplus.’ 📖

## Job 22:21
Make peace with God now. Then good will come to you. 📖

## Job 22:22
Receive instruction from His mouth. Store His words in your heart. 📖

## Job 22:23
If you return to the Almighty, you will be restored. Suppose you remove injustice from your tents. 📖

## Job 22:24
Suppose you put your gold in the dust. Suppose you put gold from Ophir among the ravines' stones. 📖

## Job 22:25
Then the Almighty will be your gold. He will be your finest silver. 📖

## Job 22:26
Then you will delight in the Almighty. You will lift your face to God. 📖

## Job 22:27
You will pray to Him, and He will hear you. You will fulfill your vows. 📖

## Job 22:28
Your decisions will be carried out. Light will shine on your ways. 📖

## Job 22:29
When people are brought low, you will say, ‘Lift them up!’ Then He will save the lowly. 📖

## Job 22:30
He will rescue even someone who is not innocent. He will rescue him through the cleanness of your hands.” 📖
