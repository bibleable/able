## Job 21:1
Job answered. 📖

## Job 21:2
“Listen carefully to my words. Let this be the comfort you give me. 📖

## Job 21:3
Bear with me while I speak. After I finish, you can continue mocking. 📖

## Job 21:4
Is my complaint against a man? Why should I not be impatient? 📖

## Job 21:5
Look at me and be horrified. Put your hand over your mouth. 📖

## Job 21:6
When I remember, terror seizes me. My body trembles in horror. 📖

## Job 21:7
Why do wicked people live on? Why do they grow old and gain power? 📖

## Job 21:8
Their descendants are established around them. They see their offspring before their eyes. 📖

## Job 21:9
Their homes are safe from fear. God's rod of punishment is not on them. 📖

## Job 21:10
Their bulls breed without failure. Their cows give birth to calves and do not miscarry. 📖

## Job 21:11
They send their little ones out like a flock. Their children skip around. 📖

## Job 21:12
Their children sing with the tambourine and lyre. They celebrate to the sound of the flute. 📖

## Job 21:13
They spend their days in prosperity. They go down to Sheol in peace. 📖

## Job 21:14
Yet they tell God, ‘Leave us alone! We do not want to know Your ways. 📖

## Job 21:15
Who is the Almighty, that we should serve Him? What would we gain by praying to Him?’ 📖

## Job 21:16
Still, their prosperity is not in their own hands. Therefore, I stay far from wicked people's counsel. 📖

## Job 21:17
How often does a wicked person's lamp go out? Does disaster come on them? Does God assign destruction in His anger? 📖

## Job 21:18
Are they like straw before the wind? Are they like chaff that a storm sweeps away? 📖

## Job 21:19
People say God stores a man's punishment for his children. Let God repay the man himself so he will know it. 📖

## Job 21:20
Let him see his own destruction. Let him drink the Almighty's wrath himself. 📖

## Job 21:21
What does he care about his household after him? His allotted months have ended. 📖

## Job 21:22
Can anyone teach God knowledge? He judges those on high. 📖

## Job 21:23
One man dies full of strength. He is completely secure and at ease. 📖

## Job 21:24
His body is well fed. His bones are rich with marrow. 📖

## Job 21:25
Another man dies with bitterness in his soul. He has never tasted prosperity. 📖

## Job 21:26
Yet both lie down in the dust. Worms cover them both. 📖

## Job 21:27
I know your thoughts very well. I know the plans you would use to wrong me. 📖

## Job 21:28
You ask, ‘Where is the nobleman's house now? Where are the tents where wicked people live?’ 📖

## Job 21:29
Have you never asked travelers on the roads? Do you not accept their reports? 📖

## Job 21:30
An evil man is spared on the day of disaster. He is rescued from the day of wrath. 📖

## Job 21:31
Who condemns his behavior to his face? Who repays him for what he has done? 📖

## Job 21:32
He is carried to the grave. People keep watch over his tomb. 📖

## Job 21:33
The valley's clods are sweet to him. Everyone follows behind him. Countless people went before him. 📖

## Job 21:34
How can you comfort me with empty words? Your answers are still full of lies.” 📖
