## Job 25:1
Bildad the Shuhite replied. 📖

## Job 25:2
“Rule and awe belong to God. He establishes harmony in heaven's heights. 📖

## Job 25:3
Can anyone count His troops? Does His light not rise on everyone? 📖

## Job 25:4
How can a man be just before God? How can someone born of woman be pure? 📖

## Job 25:5
Suppose even the moon does not shine. Suppose the stars are not pure in His sight. 📖

## Job 25:6
How much less pure is a man, who is only a maggot? A son of man is only a worm!” 📖
