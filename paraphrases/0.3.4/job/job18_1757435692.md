## Job 18:1
Bildad the Shuhite replied. 📖

## Job 18:2
“How long before you end these speeches? Show some sense. Then we can talk. 📖

## Job 18:3
Why do you regard us as cattle? Why do we seem stupid to you? 📖

## Job 18:4
You tear yourself in anger. Should the earth be abandoned because of you? Should rocks be moved from their places? 📖

## Job 18:5
A wicked man's lamp goes out. The flame of his fire no longer glows. 📖

## Job 18:6
The light in his tent becomes dark. The lamp beside him goes out. 📖

## Job 18:7
His strong stride becomes shorter. His own plans trip him. 📖

## Job 18:8
His own feet lead him into a net. He walks into its mesh. 📖

## Job 18:9
A trap seizes his heel. A snare grips him. 📖

## Job 18:10
A noose is hidden in the ground. A trap lies in his path. 📖

## Job 18:11
Terrors frighten him on every side. They harass him at every step. 📖

## Job 18:12
His strength is used up. Disaster stands ready beside him. 📖

## Job 18:13
It eats patches of his skin. The firstborn of death eats his limbs. 📖

## Job 18:14
He is torn from his tent's shelter. He is marched to the king of terrors. 📖

## Job 18:15
Fire lives in his tent. Burning sulfur rains on his home. 📖

## Job 18:16
His roots below dry up. His branches above wither. 📖

## Job 18:17
The memory of him disappears from the earth. He has no name in the land. 📖

## Job 18:18
He is driven from light into darkness. He is chased out of the inhabited world. 📖

## Job 18:19
He has no offspring or descendants among his people. No one survives where he once lived. 📖

## Job 18:20
People in the west are horrified by his fate. People in the east tremble in horror. 📖

## Job 18:21
This is the home of wicked people. This is the place of someone who does not know God.” 📖
