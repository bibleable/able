## Job 19:1
Job answered. 📖

## Job 19:2
“How long will you torment me? How long will you crush me with words? 📖

## Job 19:3
You have reproached me ten times now. You mistreat me without shame. 📖

## Job 19:4
Even if I have gone wrong, my error concerns me alone. 📖

## Job 19:5
Suppose you raise yourselves above me and use my disgrace against me. 📖

## Job 19:6
Then understand that God has wronged me. He has drawn His net around me. 📖

## Job 19:7
I cry out, ‘Violence!’ But no one answers. I call for help, but there is no justice. 📖

## Job 19:8
He has blocked my way so I cannot pass. He has covered my paths with darkness. 📖

## Job 19:9
He has stripped away my honor. He has removed the crown from my head. 📖

## Job 19:10
He tears me down on every side until I am gone. He pulls up my hope like a tree. 📖

## Job 19:11
His anger burns against me. He counts me among His enemies. 📖

## Job 19:12
His troops advance together. They build a ramp against me. They camp around my tent. 📖

## Job 19:13
He has taken my brothers away from me. My acquaintances have abandoned me. 📖

## Job 19:14
My relatives have failed me. My friends have forgotten me. 📖

## Job 19:15
My guests and female servants regard me as a stranger. They see me as a foreigner. 📖

## Job 19:16
I call my servant, but he does not answer. I beg him with my own mouth. 📖

## Job 19:17
My breath disgusts my wife. My own family finds me repulsive. 📖

## Job 19:18
Even little boys despise me. They mock me when I appear. 📖

## Job 19:19
All my best friends despise me. Those I love have turned against me. 📖

## Job 19:20
My skin and flesh cling to my bones. I have escaped by the skin of my teeth. 📖

## Job 19:21
Have pity on me, my friends! Have pity, because God's hand has struck me. 📖

## Job 19:22
Why do you persecute me as God does? Will you never have enough of my flesh? 📖

## Job 19:23
I wish someone would record my words. I wish they would write them in a book. 📖

## Job 19:24
I wish they would inscribe them on lead with an iron stylus. Or they could chisel them into stone forever. 📖

## Job 19:25
But I know my Redeemer lives. In the end, He will stand on the earth. 📖

## Job 19:26
Even after my skin is destroyed, I will see God in my flesh. 📖

## Job 19:27
I will see Him for myself. My own eyes will see Him, and not as a stranger. My heart longs for this within me! 📖

## Job 19:28
Suppose you say, ‘Let us persecute him. The root of the problem lies in him.’ 📖

## Job 19:29
Then you should fear the sword yourselves. Wrath brings punishment by the sword. Thus you may know that there is a judgment.” 📖
