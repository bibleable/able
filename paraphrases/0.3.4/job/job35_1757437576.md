## Job 35:1
Elihu continued speaking. 📖

## Job 35:2
“Do you think this is just? You say, ‘I am more righteous than God.’ 📖

## Job 35:3
You ask, ‘What do I gain? How do I benefit by staying away from sin?’ 📖

## Job 35:4
I will answer you and your friends. 📖

## Job 35:5
Look at the heavens. See the clouds high above you. 📖

## Job 35:6
If you sin, what do you achieve against Him? If you increase your rebellion, what do you do to Him? 📖

## Job 35:7
If you are righteous, what do you give Him? What does He receive from your hand? 📖

## Job 35:8
Your wickedness affects only people like you. Your righteousness affects only other human beings. 📖

## Job 35:9
People cry out under great oppression. They plead for relief from the mighty person's arm. 📖

## Job 35:10
But no one asks, ‘Where is God my Maker? He gives us songs in the night. 📖

## Job 35:11
He teaches us more than the earth's animals. He makes us wiser than the birds of the air.’ 📖

## Job 35:12
They cry out there, but He does not answer. This is because of evil men's pride. 📖

## Job 35:13
God does not listen to empty pleas. The Almighty does not notice them. 📖

## Job 35:14
How much less will He listen when you say you cannot see Him? You say your case is before Him and you must wait for Him. 📖

## Job 35:15
You also say He has not punished in His anger. You say He has taken little notice of folly. 📖

## Job 35:16
Job opens his mouth without purpose. He adds more and more words without knowledge.” 📖
