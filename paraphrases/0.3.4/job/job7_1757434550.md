## Job 7:1
“Are people not assigned hard labor on earth? Are their days not like those of hired workers? 📖

## Job 7:2
A slave longs for shade. A hired worker waits for his wages. 📖

## Job 7:3
In the same way, I have been given months of emptiness. Nights of misery have been assigned to me. 📖

## Job 7:4
When I lie down, I ask, ‘When will I get up?’ The night continues slowly. I toss and turn until dawn. 📖

## Job 7:5
Worms cover my flesh, and dirt forms a crust on it. My skin is cracked and filled with infected sores. 📖

## Job 7:6
My days move faster than a weaver's shuttle. They end without hope. 📖

## Job 7:7
Remember that my life is only a breath. My eyes will never see happiness again. 📖

## Job 7:8
Those who see me now will no longer see me. You will look for me, but I will be gone. 📖

## Job 7:9
A cloud disappears and is gone. In the same way, a person who goes down to Sheol does not return. 📖

## Job 7:10
He never returns to his house. His place remembers him no longer. 📖

## Job 7:11
Therefore, I will not hold back my words. I will speak from my spirit's anguish. I will complain from my soul's bitterness. 📖

## Job 7:12
Am I the sea or the monster of the deep? Why must You keep me under guard? 📖

## Job 7:13
I think my bed will comfort me. I think my couch will ease my complaint. 📖

## Job 7:14
But then You frighten me with dreams. You terrify me with visions. 📖

## Job 7:15
I would prefer strangling and death to life in this body. 📖

## Job 7:16
I hate my life! I would not live forever. Leave me alone, because my days are only a breath. 📖

## Job 7:17
What are people, that You should raise them so high? Why do You set Your heart on them? 📖

## Job 7:18
Why do You attend to them every morning? Why do You test them every moment? 📖

## Job 7:19
Will You never look away from me? Will You not leave me alone long enough to swallow my saliva? 📖

## Job 7:20
If I have sinned, what have I done to You, watcher of mankind? Why have You made me Your target? Why am I a burden to You? 📖

## Job 7:21
Why do You not forgive my rebellion and remove my wrongdoing? Soon I will lie in the dust. You will look for me, but I will be gone.” 📖
