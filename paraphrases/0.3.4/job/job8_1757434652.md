## Job 8:1
Bildad the Shuhite replied. 📖

## Job 8:2
“How long will you keep saying these things? Your words are a strong, noisy wind. 📖

## Job 8:3
Does God twist justice? Does the Almighty twist what is right? 📖

## Job 8:4
When your children sinned against Him, He handed them over to their rebellion. 📖

## Job 8:5
Suppose you sincerely seek God and ask the Almighty for mercy. 📖

## Job 8:6
If you are pure and upright, He will act for you even now. He will restore your righteous estate. 📖

## Job 8:7
Your beginnings were small. But your later days will be prosperous. 📖

## Job 8:8
Please ask earlier generations. Consider what their fathers discovered. 📖

## Job 8:9
We were born yesterday and know nothing. Our days on earth are only a shadow. 📖

## Job 8:10
Will they not teach you and tell you? Will they not speak from their understanding? 📖

## Job 8:11
Does papyrus grow without a marsh? Do reeds grow well without water? 📖

## Job 8:12
Their shoots dry up faster than grass, even before anyone cuts them. 📖

## Job 8:13
This is the fate of everyone who forgets God. The hope of godless people will die. 📖

## Job 8:14
A godless person's confidence is fragile. He finds security in a spider's web. 📖

## Job 8:15
He leans on his web, but it gives way. He holds tightly to it, but it does not last. 📖

## Job 8:16
He is like a plant with plenty of water in the sunshine. Its shoots spread over the garden. 📖

## Job 8:17
Its roots wrap around a pile of rocks. It looks for a home among the stones. 📖

## Job 8:18
If it is uprooted, its place rejects it. Its place says, ‘I never saw you.’ 📖

## Job 8:19
This is the joy of its way. Yet other plants will grow from the dust. 📖

## Job 8:20
God does not reject blameless people. He does not strengthen the hands of those who do evil. 📖

## Job 8:21
He will fill your mouth with laughter again. Your lips will shout for joy. 📖

## Job 8:22
Shame will clothe your enemies. The tent of wicked people will disappear.” 📖
