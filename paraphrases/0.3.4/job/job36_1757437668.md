## Job 36:1
Elihu continued speaking. 📖

## Job 36:2
“Bear with me a little longer. I will show you there is more to say on God's behalf. 📖

## Job 36:3
I get my knowledge from far away. I will declare my Maker just. 📖

## Job 36:4
My words contain no falsehood. Someone perfect in knowledge is with you. 📖

## Job 36:5
God is mighty, but He despises no one. He has great strength of understanding. 📖

## Job 36:6
He does not keep wicked people alive. He gives justice to suffering people. 📖

## Job 36:7
He does not take His eyes off righteous people. He puts them on thrones with kings. He raises them to honor forever. 📖

## Job 36:8
Suppose people are bound with chains. Suppose cords of suffering hold them. 📖

## Job 36:9
Then He tells them what they have done. He shows them how proudly they rebelled. 📖

## Job 36:10
He opens their ears to correction. He commands them to turn from wrongdoing. 📖

## Job 36:11
If they obey and serve Him, their days will end in prosperity. Their years will end in happiness. 📖

## Job 36:12
But if they do not obey, they will die by the sword. They will die without knowledge. 📖

## Job 36:13
People with godless hearts hold resentment. Even when He binds them, they do not cry for help. 📖

## Job 36:14
They die young, among male shrine prostitutes. 📖

## Job 36:15
God rescues suffering people through their suffering. He opens their ears through oppression. 📖

## Job 36:16
He drew you from distress's jaws into a wide, open place. He brought you to a table full of rich food. 📖

## Job 36:17
But now the judgment due to wicked people weighs on you. Judgment and justice have seized you. 📖

## Job 36:18
Be careful that no one attracts you with riches. Do not let a large bribe lead you away from what is right. 📖

## Job 36:19
Can your wealth keep you from distress? Can all your mighty effort do this? 📖

## Job 36:20
Do not long for night, when people disappear from their homes. 📖

## Job 36:21
Be careful not to turn to wrongdoing. You have preferred it to suffering. 📖

## Job 36:22
God is exalted in His power. Who teaches like Him? 📖

## Job 36:23
Who has chosen His way for Him? Who has told Him, ‘You have done wrong’? 📖

## Job 36:24
Remember to praise His work highly. People have praised it in songs. 📖

## Job 36:25
All mankind has seen it. People see it from far away. 📖

## Job 36:26
God is great, beyond our knowledge. No one can discover the number of His years. 📖

## Job 36:27
He draws up drops of water. They form rain from the mist. 📖

## Job 36:28
The clouds pour it out. They shower it abundantly on mankind. 📖

## Job 36:29
Who can understand how the clouds spread? Who can understand the thunder roaring from His pavilion? 📖

## Job 36:30
See how He scatters lightning around Himself. He covers the sea's depths. 📖

## Job 36:31
He uses these things to judge nations. He provides abundant food. 📖

## Job 36:32
He fills His hands with lightning. He commands it to strike its target. 📖

## Job 36:33
Thunder announces His presence. Even the cattle take notice of the rising storm. 📖
