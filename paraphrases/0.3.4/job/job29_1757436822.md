## Job 29:1
Job continued speaking. 📖

## Job 29:2
“How I long for the months that have passed! I long for the days when God watched over me. 📖

## Job 29:3
His lamp shone above my head. His light guided me through darkness. 📖

## Job 29:4
I was in my prime. God's friendship rested on my tent. 📖

## Job 29:5
The Almighty was still with me. My children were around me. 📖

## Job 29:6
Cream bathed my steps. The rock poured out streams of oil for me. 📖

## Job 29:7
I went to the city gate. I took my seat in the public square. 📖

## Job 29:8
Young men saw me and stepped back. Old men stood up. 📖

## Job 29:9
Princes stopped speaking. They covered their mouths with their hands. 📖

## Job 29:10
Nobles' voices became quiet. Their tongues stuck to the roofs of their mouths. 📖

## Job 29:11
Those who heard me called me blessed. Those who saw me praised me. 📖

## Job 29:12
I rescued poor people who cried for help. I rescued fatherless children who had no helper. 📖

## Job 29:13
A dying man blessed me. I made the widow's heart sing for joy. 📖

## Job 29:14
I put on righteousness, and it clothed me. Justice was my robe and turban. 📖

## Job 29:15
I was eyes for blind people. I was feet for lame people. 📖

## Job 29:16
I was a father to needy people. I took up the stranger's case. 📖

## Job 29:17
I broke the unjust man's fangs. I pulled his prey from his teeth. 📖

## Job 29:18
I thought, ‘I will die in my nest. My days will multiply like sand. 📖

## Job 29:19
My roots will reach the waters. Dew will rest on my branches every night. 📖

## Job 29:20
My glory is always new within me. My bow is renewed in my hand.’ 📖

## Job 29:21
People listened to me with expectation. They waited silently for my counsel. 📖

## Job 29:22
After I spoke, they said nothing more. My words settled on them like dew. 📖

## Job 29:23
They waited for me as they waited for rain. They drank in my words like spring showers. 📖

## Job 29:24
If I smiled at them, they could not believe it. The light of my face was precious to them. 📖

## Job 29:25
I chose their course and led them as chief. I lived like a king among his troops. I was like someone who comforts mourners. 📖
