## Job 10:1
“I hate my life. I will express my complaint. I will speak from my soul's bitterness. 📖

## Job 10:2
I will tell God, ‘Do not condemn me! Tell me why You bring charges against me. 📖

## Job 10:3
Does it please You to oppress me and reject the work of Your hands? Does it please You to favor wicked people's plans? 📖

## Job 10:4
Do You have eyes of flesh? Do You see as people see? 📖

## Job 10:5
Are Your days like a mortal's days? Are Your years like a man's years? 📖

## Job 10:6
Is that why You search for my wrongdoing and investigate my sin? 📖

## Job 10:7
Yet You know I am not guilty. No one can rescue me from Your hand. 📖

## Job 10:8
Your hands shaped me and formed every part of me. Would You now turn and destroy me? 📖

## Job 10:9
Please remember that You molded me like clay. Would You now return me to dust? 📖

## Job 10:10
Did You not pour me out like milk? Did You not make me curdle like cheese? 📖

## Job 10:11
You clothed me with skin and flesh. You joined me together with bones and sinews. 📖

## Job 10:12
You gave me life and faithful love. Your care preserved my spirit. 📖

## Job 10:13
Yet You hid these things in Your heart. I know You had this in mind. 📖

## Job 10:14
If I sinned, You would notice. You would not clear me of my wrongdoing. 📖

## Job 10:15
If I am guilty, disaster awaits me! Even if I am righteous, I cannot lift my head. I am full of shame and aware of my suffering. 📖

## Job 10:16
If I held my head high, You would hunt me like a lion. You would show Your power against me again. 📖

## Job 10:17
You bring new witnesses against me. Your anger toward me increases. Hardships attack me in wave after wave. 📖

## Job 10:18
Why did You bring me from the womb? If only I had died before any eye saw me! 📖

## Job 10:19
If only I had never existed! If only I had been carried from the womb to the grave! 📖

## Job 10:20
Are my days not few? Leave me alone so I can have a little comfort. 📖

## Job 10:21
Soon I will go to the land of darkness and gloom. I will never return. 📖

## Job 10:22
It is a land of complete darkness, deep shadow, and disorder. Even its light is like darkness.’” 📖
