## Job 26:1
Job answered. 📖

## Job 26:2
“How you have helped powerless people! How you have saved the weak arm! 📖

## Job 26:3
How you have advised those without wisdom! How much sound insight you have given! 📖

## Job 26:4
To whom have you spoken these words? Whose spirit spoke through you? 📖

## Job 26:5
The dead tremble, those beneath the waters and those who dwell in them. 📖

## Job 26:6
Sheol is naked before God. Abaddon has no covering. 📖

## Job 26:7
He stretches the north over empty space. He hangs the earth on nothing. 📖

## Job 26:8
He wraps the waters in His clouds. Yet the clouds do not burst under their own weight. 📖

## Job 26:9
He covers the full moon's face. He spreads His cloud over it. 📖

## Job 26:10
He marks a horizon on the waters' surface. It is the boundary between light and darkness. 📖

## Job 26:11
Heaven's foundations shake. His rebuke astonishes them. 📖

## Job 26:12
He stirred the sea by His power. He shattered Rahab by His understanding. 📖

## Job 26:13
His breath cleared the skies. His hand pierced the fleeing serpent. 📖

## Job 26:14
These are only the edges of His ways. We hear only a faint whisper of Him. Who can understand the thunder of His power?” 📖
