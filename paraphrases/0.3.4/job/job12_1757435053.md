## Job 12:1
Job answered. 📖

## Job 12:2
“Truly, you are the people with whom wisdom itself will die! 📖

## Job 12:3
But I also have a mind. I am not inferior to you. Who does not know these things? 📖

## Job 12:4
My friends laugh at me, although I called to God and He answered. They laugh at a righteous and upright man. 📖

## Job 12:5
A person at ease despises misfortune. He sees it as the fate of people whose feet are slipping. 📖

## Job 12:6
Robbers' tents are safe. People who provoke God are secure. They carry their god in their hands. 📖

## Job 12:7
Ask the animals, and they will teach you. Ask the birds of the air, and they will tell you. 📖

## Job 12:8
Speak to the earth, and it will teach you. Let the fish of the sea inform you. 📖

## Job 12:9
Which of these does not know that the LORD's hand has done this? 📖

## Job 12:10
Every living thing's life is in His hand. So is the breath of all mankind. 📖

## Job 12:11
Does the ear not test words as the tongue tastes food? 📖

## Job 12:12
Wisdom is found among elderly people. Understanding comes with long life. 📖

## Job 12:13
God has wisdom and strength. Counsel and understanding belong to Him. 📖

## Job 12:14
What He tears down cannot be rebuilt. A man He imprisons cannot be released. 📖

## Job 12:15
If He holds back the waters, they dry up. If He releases them, they flood the land. 📖

## Job 12:16
True wisdom and power belong to Him. Both the deceiver and the deceived belong to Him. 📖

## Job 12:17
He leads counselors away barefoot. He makes judges into fools. 📖

## Job 12:18
He loosens the bonds that kings have placed. He fastens a belt around their waists. 📖

## Job 12:19
He leads priests away barefoot. He overthrows those who are established. 📖

## Job 12:20
He takes speech from trusted people. He takes discernment from elders. 📖

## Job 12:21
He pours contempt on nobles. He disarms the mighty. 📖

## Job 12:22
He reveals the deep things hidden in darkness. He brings deep shadows into the light. 📖

## Job 12:23
He makes nations great and destroys them. He enlarges nations and then scatters them. 📖

## Job 12:24
He takes reason from the earth's leaders. He makes them wander in a wasteland with no paths. 📖

## Job 12:25
They feel their way in darkness without light. He makes them stagger like drunk people.” 📖
