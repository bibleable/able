## Job 4:1
Eliphaz the Temanite replied. 📖

## Job 4:2
“Will a word from someone make you weary? But who can remain silent? 📖

## Job 4:3
You have taught many people. You have strengthened their weak hands. 📖

## Job 4:4
Your words have supported people who stumbled. You have strengthened knees that were giving way. 📖

## Job 4:5
But now trouble has come to you, and you are weary. It strikes you, and you lose courage. 📖

## Job 4:6
Does your reverence for God not give you confidence? Do your upright ways not give you hope? 📖

## Job 4:7
Please consider this: When has an innocent person died? Where have upright people been destroyed? 📖

## Job 4:8
I have seen people plow wrongdoing and sow trouble. They harvest the same things. 📖

## Job 4:9
God's breath destroys them. The blast of His anger consumes them. 📖

## Job 4:10
The lion roars, and the fierce lion growls. Yet the young lions' teeth are broken. 📖

## Job 4:11
The old lion dies because it has no prey. The lioness's cubs are scattered. 📖

## Job 4:12
A message came to me secretly. My ears heard a whisper of it. 📖

## Job 4:13
I had disturbing visions at night, when people fall into deep sleep. 📖

## Job 4:14
Fear and trembling came over me. All my bones shook. 📖

## Job 4:15
A spirit moved past my face. The hair on my body stood up. 📖

## Job 4:16
It stood still, but I could not recognize its appearance. A shape stood before my eyes. I heard a voice whisper. 📖

## Job 4:17
‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God? Can a man be purer than his Maker? 📖

## Job 4:18
Suppose God does not trust His servants and accuses His angels of error. 📖

## Job 4:19
How much more does this apply to those who live in houses of clay? Their foundations are in the dust. They can be crushed like a moth. 📖

## Job 4:20
They are broken to pieces from dawn to dusk. They die forever, and no one notices. 📖

## Job 4:21
Are their tent cords not pulled up? They die without wisdom.’ 📖
