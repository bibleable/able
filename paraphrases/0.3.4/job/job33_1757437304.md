## Job 33:1
“Now, Job, hear my speech. Listen to all my words. 📖

## Job 33:2
I will open my mouth. My speech is on the tip of my tongue. 📖

## Job 33:3
My words come from an upright heart. My lips sincerely speak what I know. 📖

## Job 33:4
God's Spirit made me. The Almighty's breath gives me life. 📖

## Job 33:5
Prove me wrong if you can. Prepare your case and confront me. 📖

## Job 33:6
I am like you before God. I was also formed from clay. 📖

## Job 33:7
Fear of me should not terrify you. My hand will not be heavy on you. 📖

## Job 33:8
You spoke where I could hear you. I heard these very words. 📖

## Job 33:9
‘I am pure and have not rebelled. I am clean and have no wrongdoing in me. 📖

## Job 33:10
Yet God finds reasons to accuse me. He counts me as His enemy. 📖

## Job 33:11
He locks my feet in stocks. He watches all my paths.’ 📖

## Job 33:12
You are not right about this. I will answer you, because God is greater than man. 📖

## Job 33:13
Why do you complain that He answers none of a man's questions? 📖

## Job 33:14
God speaks in one way and then another. Yet no one notices. 📖

## Job 33:15
He speaks in dreams and visions at night. People are in deep sleep on their beds. 📖

## Job 33:16
He opens their ears. He terrifies them with warnings. 📖

## Job 33:17
He does this to turn a man from wrongdoing. He keeps him from pride. 📖

## Job 33:18
He preserves the man's soul from the Pit. He saves his life from death by the sword. 📖

## Job 33:19
A man is also disciplined with pain on his bed. His bones suffer constant distress. 📖

## Job 33:20
Then he hates his bread. His soul hates even his favorite food. 📖

## Job 33:21
His flesh wastes away until it can hardly be seen. His bones, once hidden, stick out. 📖

## Job 33:22
His soul approaches the Pit. His life approaches the messengers of death. 📖

## Job 33:23
Suppose a messenger is on his side, one mediator in a thousand. This messenger tells the man what is right for him. 📖

## Job 33:24
Suppose the messenger is gracious to him and says, ‘Spare him from going down to the Pit. I have found his ransom.’ 📖

## Job 33:25
Then his flesh becomes fresh like a child's flesh. He returns to the days of his youth. 📖

## Job 33:26
He prays to God and receives favor. He sees God's face and shouts for joy. God restores His righteousness to that man. 📖

## Job 33:27
Then he sings before people. He says, ‘I sinned and twisted what was right. Yet I did not receive what I deserved. 📖

## Job 33:28
He redeemed my soul from going down to the Pit. I will live to see the light.’ 📖

## Job 33:29
God does all these things to a man two or even three times. 📖

## Job 33:30
He does this to bring the man's soul back from the Pit. Then the light of life can shine on him. 📖

## Job 33:31
Pay attention, Job. Listen to me. Be silent, and I will speak. 📖

## Job 33:32
If you have something to say, answer me. Speak, because I would like to prove you right. 📖

## Job 33:33
If you have nothing to say, listen to me. Be quiet, and I will teach you wisdom.” 📖
