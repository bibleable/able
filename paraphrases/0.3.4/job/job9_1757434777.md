## Job 9:1
Job answered. 📖

## Job 9:2
“Yes, I know this is true. But how can a mortal be righteous before God? 📖

## Job 9:3
If someone wanted to argue with God, he could not answer Him once in a thousand times. 📖

## Job 9:4
God's heart is wise, and His strength is mighty. Who has resisted Him and prospered? 📖

## Job 9:5
He moves mountains without their knowledge. He overturns them in His anger. 📖

## Job 9:6
He shakes the earth from its place. Its foundations tremble. 📖

## Job 9:7
He commands the sun not to shine. He seals off the stars. 📖

## Job 9:8
He alone stretches out the heavens. He walks on the waves of the sea. 📖

## Job 9:9
He made the Bear, Orion, the Pleiades, and the constellations in the south. 📖

## Job 9:10
He does great things beyond our understanding. His wonders cannot be counted. 📖

## Job 9:11
If He passed me, I would not see Him. If He moved, I would not recognize Him. 📖

## Job 9:12
If He takes something away, who can stop Him? Who dares to ask Him, ‘What are You doing?’ 📖

## Job 9:13
God does not hold back His anger. Rahab's helpers crouch in fear beneath Him. 📖

## Job 9:14
How could I answer Him? How could I choose arguments against Him? 📖

## Job 9:15
Even if I were right, I could not answer. I could only beg my Judge for mercy. 📖

## Job 9:16
If I called Him and He answered, I would not believe He would listen to me. 📖

## Job 9:17
He would crush me with a violent storm. He would increase my wounds without cause. 📖

## Job 9:18
He does not let me catch my breath. He overwhelms me with bitterness. 📖

## Job 9:19
If strength is the issue, He is mighty! If justice is the issue, who can summon Him? 📖

## Job 9:20
Even if I were righteous, my mouth would condemn me. Even if I were blameless, it would declare me guilty. 📖

## Job 9:21
I am blameless, but I have no concern for myself. I despise my life. 📖

## Job 9:22
It is all the same. Therefore, I say that He destroys both blameless and wicked people. 📖

## Job 9:23
When a scourge brings sudden death, He mocks the despair of innocent people. 📖

## Job 9:24
The earth is handed over to wicked people. He covers the judges' eyes. If He is not doing this, who is? 📖

## Job 9:25
My days move faster than a runner. They flee without seeing good. 📖

## Job 9:26
They pass like papyrus boats. They pass like an eagle diving on its prey. 📖

## Job 9:27
Suppose I say, ‘I will forget my complaint. I will change my expression and smile.’ 📖

## Job 9:28
I would still fear all my suffering. I know You will not declare me innocent. 📖

## Job 9:29
I have already been found guilty. Why should I struggle without success? 📖

## Job 9:30
Suppose I wash myself with snow and clean my hands with lye. 📖

## Job 9:31
Then You would plunge me into a pit. Even my clothes would despise me. 📖

## Job 9:32
He is not a man like me, whom I can answer. We cannot take each other to court. 📖

## Job 9:33
There is no mediator between us to put his hand on both of us. 📖

## Job 9:34
Let Him take His rod away from me. Then His terror will no longer frighten me. 📖

## Job 9:35
Then I could speak without fearing Him. But now I am on my own.” 📖
