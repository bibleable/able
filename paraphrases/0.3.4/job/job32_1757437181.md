## Job 32:1
The three men stopped answering Job because he considered himself righteous. 📖

## Job 32:2
Elihu became angry. He was the son of Barachel the Buzite, from the family of Ram. He was angry because Job justified himself rather than God. 📖

## Job 32:3
He was also angry with Job's three friends. They had not proved Job wrong, yet they had condemned him. 📖

## Job 32:4
Elihu had waited to speak to Job because the others were older than him. 📖

## Job 32:5
When Elihu saw that the three men had no more answers, he became angry. 📖

## Job 32:6
Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite said, “I am young, and you are old. Therefore, I was timid and afraid to tell you what I know. 📖

## Job 32:7
I thought older people should speak. Many years should teach wisdom. 📖

## Job 32:8
But a spirit in a man gives him understanding. It is the Almighty's breath. 📖

## Job 32:9
Old people are not the only wise ones. Elderly people are not the only ones who understand justice. 📖

## Job 32:10
Therefore, I say, ‘Listen to me. I will also tell you what I know.’ 📖

## Job 32:11
I waited while you spoke. I listened to your reasoning as you searched for words. 📖

## Job 32:12
I gave you my full attention. But no one proved Job wrong. None of you answered his arguments successfully. 📖

## Job 32:13
Do not claim, ‘We have found wisdom. Let God prove him wrong, rather than a man.’ 📖

## Job 32:14
Job did not direct his words against me. I will not answer him with your arguments. 📖

## Job 32:15
Job's friends are dismayed and have nothing more to say. Words have escaped them. 📖

## Job 32:16
Must I keep waiting? They are silent. They stand and no longer answer. 📖

## Job 32:17
I will also answer. I will tell you what I know. 📖

## Job 32:18
I am full of words. My spirit within me forces me to speak. 📖

## Job 32:19
My belly is like wine with no opening to release pressure. It is about to burst like a new wineskin. 📖

## Job 32:20
I must speak to find relief. I must open my lips and answer. 📖

## Job 32:21
I will show favoritism to no one. I will flatter no man. 📖

## Job 32:22
I do not know how to flatter. If I did, my Maker would remove me instantly. 📖
