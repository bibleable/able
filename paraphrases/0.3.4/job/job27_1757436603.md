## Job 27:1
Job continued speaking. 📖

## Job 27:2
“As surely as God lives, I make this promise. God has denied me justice. The Almighty has made my soul bitter. 📖

## Job 27:3
As long as my breath remains in me and God's breath remains in my nostrils, I make this promise. 📖

## Job 27:4
My lips will not speak wickedness. My tongue will not speak deceit. 📖

## Job 27:5
I will never say you are right. I will remain faithful to what is right until I die. 📖

## Job 27:6
I will hold tightly to my righteousness and never release it. My conscience will not accuse me as long as I live. 📖

## Job 27:7
May my enemy be like a wicked man. May my opponent be like an unjust man. 📖

## Job 27:8
What hope does a godless man have when he is cut off? What hope does he have when God takes his life? 📖

## Job 27:9
Will God hear his cry when distress comes to him? 📖

## Job 27:10
Will he delight in the Almighty? Will he call to God at all times? 📖

## Job 27:11
I will teach you about God's power. I will not hide the Almighty's ways. 📖

## Job 27:12
You have all seen this yourselves. Why do you continue this empty talk? 📖

## Job 27:13
This is a wicked man's portion from God. This is the inheritance ruthless people receive from the Almighty. 📖

## Job 27:14
His sons may be many, but they are destined for the sword. His offspring will never have enough food. 📖

## Job 27:15
The plague will bury his survivors. Their widows will not weep for them. 📖

## Job 27:16
He may heap up silver like dust. He may pile up clothes like clay. 📖

## Job 27:17
But righteous people will wear the clothes he stores. Innocent people will divide his silver. 📖

## Job 27:18
The house he built is like a moth's cocoon. It is like a watchman's hut. 📖

## Job 27:19
He lies down wealthy, but he will not do this again. When he opens his eyes, everything is gone. 📖

## Job 27:20
Terrors overtake him like a flood. A violent storm sweeps him away at night. 📖

## Job 27:21
The east wind carries him away, and he is gone. It sweeps him from his place. 📖

## Job 27:22
It throws itself against him without mercy. He flees at full speed from its power. 📖

## Job 27:23
It claps its hands at him. It hisses him out of his place. 📖
