## Job 42:1
Job answered the LORD. 📖

## Job 42:2
“I know You can do everything. No one can defeat any plan of Yours. 📖

## Job 42:3
You asked, ‘Who hides My counsel without knowledge?’ I spoke about things I did not understand. They were too wonderful for me to know. 📖

## Job 42:4
You said, ‘Listen now, and I will speak. I will question you, and you must answer Me.’ 📖

## Job 42:5
My ears had heard about You. But now my eyes have seen You. 📖

## Job 42:6
Therefore, I despise myself. I repent in dust and ashes.” 📖

## Job 42:7
After speaking to Job, the LORD spoke to Eliphaz the Temanite. He said, “I am angry with you and your two friends. You have not spoken accurately about Me as My servant Job has. 📖

## Job 42:8
Take seven bulls and seven rams to My servant Job. Sacrifice a burnt offering for yourselves. My servant Job will pray for you. I will accept his prayer and will not treat you as your folly deserves. You have not spoken accurately about Me as My servant Job has.” 📖

## Job 42:9
Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite did what the LORD told them. The LORD accepted Job's prayer. 📖

## Job 42:10
After Job prayed for his friends, the LORD restored his prosperity. He gave Job twice as many possessions as before. 📖

## Job 42:11
All Job's brothers, sisters, and former acquaintances came to eat with him in his house. They consoled and comforted him for all the trouble the LORD had brought on him. Each gave him a piece of silver and a gold ring. 📖

## Job 42:12
The LORD blessed Job's later days more than his early days. Job owned 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 pairs of oxen, and 1,000 female donkeys. 📖

## Job 42:13
He also had seven sons and three daughters. 📖

## Job 42:14
He named his first daughter Jemimah. He named his second Keziah and his third Keren-happuch. 📖

## Job 42:15
No women in the whole land were as beautiful as Job's daughters. Their father gave them an inheritance among their brothers. 📖

## Job 42:16
After this, Job lived 140 years. He saw his children and their children, to the fourth generation. 📖

## Job 42:17
Job died old and full of years. 📖
