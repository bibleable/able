## Job 34:1
Elihu continued speaking. 📖

## Job 34:2
“Wise men, hear my words. Learned men, listen to me. 📖

## Job 34:3
The ear tests words as the mouth tastes food. 📖

## Job 34:4
Let us choose what is right. Let us learn together what is good. 📖

## Job 34:5
Job has said, ‘I am righteous. Yet God has denied me justice. 📖

## Job 34:6
Would I lie about my case? My wound cannot be healed, although I have not rebelled.’ 📖

## Job 34:7
Who is like Job? He drinks mockery like water. 📖

## Job 34:8
He keeps company with people who do evil. He walks with wicked men. 📖

## Job 34:9
He has said, ‘A man gains nothing by delighting in God.’ 📖

## Job 34:10
Men of understanding, listen to me. God would never do wrong. The Almighty would never act unjustly. 📖

## Job 34:11
He repays a man according to his deeds. He brings consequences according to a man's ways. 📖

## Job 34:12
God does not act wickedly. The Almighty does not twist justice. This is true. 📖

## Job 34:13
Who gave Him charge of the earth? Who appointed Him over the whole world? 📖

## Job 34:14
Suppose He decided to withdraw His Spirit and breath. 📖

## Job 34:15
Then all flesh would die together. Mankind would return to dust. 📖

## Job 34:16
If you have understanding, hear this. Listen to my words. 📖

## Job 34:17
Could someone who hates justice govern? Will you condemn the just and mighty One? 📖

## Job 34:18
He tells kings, ‘You are worthless!’ He tells nobles, ‘You are wicked.’ 📖

## Job 34:19
He shows no favoritism to princes. He does not favor rich people over poor people. His hands made them all. 📖

## Job 34:20
They die suddenly in the middle of the night. People shake violently and die. The mighty are removed without a human hand. 📖

## Job 34:21
His eyes watch a man's ways. He sees every step the man takes. 📖

## Job 34:22
No darkness or deep shadow can hide those who do wrong. 📖

## Job 34:23
God does not need to examine a man further. He does not need the man to approach Him for judgment. 📖

## Job 34:24
He breaks the mighty without an inquiry. He puts others in their places. 📖

## Job 34:25
He knows their deeds. He overthrows them at night, and they are crushed. 📖

## Job 34:26
He strikes them for their wickedness where everyone can see. 📖

## Job 34:27
They turned away from Him. They disregarded all His ways. 📖

## Job 34:28
They caused the poor people's cries to reach Him. He heard the suffering people's cries. 📖

## Job 34:29
If He stays silent, who can condemn Him? If He hides His face, who can see Him? Yet He watches both people and nations. 📖

## Job 34:30
He does this to keep godless men from ruling or setting traps for people. 📖

## Job 34:31
Suppose someone tells God, ‘I have endured my punishment. I will do wrong no longer. 📖

## Job 34:32
Teach me what I cannot see. If I have done wrong, I will not do it again.’ 📖

## Job 34:33
Should God repay you on your terms when you have rejected Him? You must choose. I cannot choose for you. Tell me what you know. 📖

## Job 34:34
Men of understanding will tell me this. Wise men who hear me will say these words. 📖

## Job 34:35
‘Job speaks without knowledge. His words lack insight.’ 📖

## Job 34:36
If only Job were tested to the fullest for answering like a wicked man! 📖

## Job 34:37
He adds rebellion to his sin. He claps his hands among us. He adds more and more words against God.” 📖
