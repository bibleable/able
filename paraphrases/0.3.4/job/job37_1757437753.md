## Job 37:1
“At this, my heart pounds. It leaps from its place. 📖

## Job 37:2
Listen closely to the thunder of His voice. Listen to the rumbling from His mouth. 📖

## Job 37:3
He releases lightning beneath the whole sky. He sends it to the ends of the earth. 📖

## Job 37:4
Then a roaring sound comes. He thunders with His majestic voice. He does not hold back the lightning when His voice sounds. 📖

## Job 37:5
God's voice thunders in wonderful ways. He does great things we cannot understand. 📖

## Job 37:6
He tells the snow, ‘Fall on the earth.’ He tells the gentle rain, ‘Pour down heavily.’ 📖

## Job 37:7
He seals up every man's hand. Then all people can know His work. 📖

## Job 37:8
Wild animals enter their shelters. They settle in their dens. 📖

## Job 37:9
The violent storm comes from its chamber. Cold comes from the driving north winds. 📖

## Job 37:10
God's breath forms ice. Wide areas of water freeze. 📖

## Job 37:11
He fills clouds with moisture. He scatters His lightning through them. 📖

## Job 37:12
They turn and swirl at His direction. They do everything He commands over the whole earth. 📖

## Job 37:13
Whether for punishment or for His land, He does this in faithful love. 📖

## Job 37:14
Listen to this, Job. Stand still and consider God's wonders. 📖

## Job 37:15
Do you know how God sends out clouds? Do you know how He makes lightning flash? 📖

## Job 37:16
Do you understand how clouds float? These are wonders of the One whose knowledge is perfect. 📖

## Job 37:17
Your clothes become hot when the land grows quiet under the south wind. 📖

## Job 37:18
Can you spread out the skies as He does? They are as strong as a bronze mirror. 📖

## Job 37:19
Teach us what to say to Him. Our darkness keeps us from preparing our case. 📖

## Job 37:20
Should He be told that I want to speak? Would a man ask to be swallowed? 📖

## Job 37:21
No one can look at the sun when it shines brightly in the sky after the wind has cleared it. 📖

## Job 37:22
He comes from the north in golden splendor. Majestic glory that inspires awe surrounds Him. 📖

## Job 37:23
The Almighty is beyond our reach. He is exalted in power. In His justice and great righteousness, He does not oppress. 📖

## Job 37:24
Therefore, people fear Him. He shows no favoritism to those who are wise in heart.” 📖
