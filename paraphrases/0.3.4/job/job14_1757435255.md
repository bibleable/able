## Job 14:1
“A person born of woman lives few days. His life is full of trouble. 📖

## Job 14:2
He comes out like a flower and then withers. He passes like a shadow and does not last. 📖

## Job 14:3
Do You watch a person like this? Will You bring him before You for judgment? 📖

## Job 14:4
Who can bring something clean from something unclean? No one can! 📖

## Job 14:5
His days are determined. You know the number of his months. You have set limits that he cannot pass. 📖

## Job 14:6
Therefore, look away from him and let him rest. Let him enjoy his day like a hired worker. 📖

## Job 14:7
A tree has hope. If someone cuts it down, it will grow again. Its tender shoots will not fail. 📖

## Job 14:8
Its roots may grow old in the ground. Its stump may die in the soil. 📖

## Job 14:9
But at the scent of water, it will bud. It will grow twigs like a young tree. 📖

## Job 14:10
But a man dies and is laid low. He breathes his last breath. Where is he then? 📖

## Job 14:11
Water disappears from the sea. A river becomes dry. 📖

## Job 14:12
In the same way, a man lies down and does not rise. He will not wake or rise from sleep until the heavens are gone. 📖

## Job 14:13
If only You would hide me in Sheol until Your anger passes! If only You would appoint a time for me and then remember me! 📖

## Job 14:14
When a man dies, will he live again? I will wait through all the days of my hard service until my renewal comes. 📖

## Job 14:15
You will call, and I will answer. You will desire the work of Your hands. 📖

## Job 14:16
Then You would count my steps. But You would not keep track of my sin. 📖

## Job 14:17
My rebellion would be sealed in a bag. You would cover my wrongdoing. 📖

## Job 14:18
A mountain wears away and crumbles. A rock is moved from its place. 📖

## Job 14:19
Water wears away stones. Floods wash away soil. In the same way, You destroy a man's hope. 📖

## Job 14:20
You overpower him forever, and he passes away. You change his face and send him away. 📖

## Job 14:21
If his sons receive honor, he does not know. If they are brought low, he is unaware. 📖

## Job 14:22
He feels only his own body's pain. He mourns only for himself.” 📖
