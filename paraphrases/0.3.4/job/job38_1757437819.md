## Job 38:1
The LORD answered Job from the whirlwind. He spoke these words. 📖

## Job 38:2
“Who makes My counsel unclear with words that lack knowledge? 📖

## Job 38:3
Prepare yourself like a man. I will question you, and you must answer Me. 📖

## Job 38:4
Where were you when I laid the earth's foundations? Tell Me, if you understand. 📖

## Job 38:5
Who determined its measurements? Surely you know! Who stretched a measuring line across it? 📖

## Job 38:6
What supports its foundations? Who laid its cornerstone? 📖

## Job 38:7
At that time, the morning stars sang together. All the sons of God shouted for joy. 📖

## Job 38:8
Who shut the sea behind doors when it burst from the womb? 📖

## Job 38:9
I made clouds its garment. I made thick darkness its blanket. 📖

## Job 38:10
I fixed its boundaries. I put its bars and doors in place. 📖

## Job 38:11
I said, ‘You may come this far, but no farther. Your proud waves must stop here.’ 📖

## Job 38:12
Have you ever commanded the morning? Have you assigned dawn its place? 📖

## Job 38:13
Have you done this so dawn could reach the earth's ends and shake wicked people out of it? 📖

## Job 38:14
The earth takes shape like clay under a seal. Its hills stand out like folds in a garment. 📖

## Job 38:15
Light is withheld from wicked people. Their raised arm is broken. 📖

## Job 38:16
Have you traveled to the sea's vents? Have you walked in the trenches of the deep? 📖

## Job 38:17
Have death's gates been shown to you? Have you seen the gates of death's shadow? 📖

## Job 38:18
Have you surveyed the earth's full extent? Tell Me, if you know all this. 📖

## Job 38:19
Where is the path to light's home? Do you know where darkness lives? 📖

## Job 38:20
Can you lead it back to its boundary? Do you know the paths to its home? 📖

## Job 38:21
Surely you know, because you were already born! You have lived so many days! 📖

## Job 38:22
Have you entered the snow's storehouses? Have you seen the hail's storehouses? 📖

## Job 38:23
I reserve these for times of trouble. I keep them for the day of war and battle. 📖

## Job 38:24
In which direction does lightning spread? In which direction does the east wind scatter over the earth? 📖

## Job 38:25
Who cuts a channel for the flood? Who clears a path for the thunderbolt? 📖

## Job 38:26
Who does this to bring rain to barren land, to a desert where no one lives? 📖

## Job 38:27
Who satisfies the dry wasteland and makes tender grass grow there? 📖

## Job 38:28
Does rain have a father? Who fathered the drops of dew? 📖

## Job 38:29
Whose womb produces ice? Who gives birth to frost from heaven? 📖

## Job 38:30
Then the waters become as hard as stone. The surface of the deep freezes. 📖

## Job 38:31
Can you fasten the Pleiades' chains? Can you loosen Orion's belt? 📖

## Job 38:32
Can you bring out the constellations in their seasons? Can you lead out the Bear and her cubs? 📖

## Job 38:33
Do you know heaven's laws? Can you establish their rule over the earth? 📖

## Job 38:34
Can you command clouds to cover you with a flood of water? 📖

## Job 38:35
Can you send lightning bolts on their way? Do they report to you, ‘Here we are’? 📖

## Job 38:36
Who put wisdom in the heart? Who gave understanding to the mind? 📖

## Job 38:37
Who has enough wisdom to count the clouds? Who can tip heaven's water jars? 📖

## Job 38:38
Who can do this when dust becomes a hard mass and clods of earth stick together? 📖

## Job 38:39
Can you hunt prey for a lioness? Can you satisfy young lions' hunger? 📖

## Job 38:40
Can you do this when they crouch in their dens and wait in the thicket? 📖

## Job 38:41
Who feeds the raven when its young cry to God? They wander because they lack food. 📖
