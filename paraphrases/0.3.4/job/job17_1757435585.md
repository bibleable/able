## Job 17:1
“My spirit is broken. My days have gone out like a light. The grave waits for me. 📖

## Job 17:2
People who mock surround me. My eyes must watch their rebellion. 📖

## Job 17:3
Please give me the pledge You demand. Who else will guarantee it for me? 📖

## Job 17:4
You have closed their minds to understanding. Therefore, You will not raise them to honor. 📖

## Job 17:5
If a man accuses his friends for payment, his children's eyes will fail. 📖

## Job 17:6
He has made me a byword among the people. They spit in my face. 📖

## Job 17:7
Grief has made my eyes dim. My whole body is only a shadow. 📖

## Job 17:8
Upright people are horrified by this. Innocent people are stirred to act against the godless. 📖

## Job 17:9
Yet a righteous person keeps to his way. A person with clean hands grows stronger. 📖

## Job 17:10
Come back and try again, all of you. I will not find a wise man among you. 📖

## Job 17:11
My days have passed. My plans are broken off. Even my heart's desires are broken. 📖

## Job 17:12
They turn night into day. They say light is near, even in the face of darkness. 📖

## Job 17:13
Suppose I look for Sheol as my home. Suppose I spread my bed in darkness. 📖

## Job 17:14
Suppose I call decay my father. Suppose I call the worm my mother or my sister. 📖

## Job 17:15
Where is my hope then? Who can see any hope for me? 📖

## Job 17:16
Will my hope go down to Sheol's gates? Will we go down together into the dust?” 📖
