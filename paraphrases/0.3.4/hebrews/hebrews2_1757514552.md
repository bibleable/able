## Hebrews 2:1
Pay closer attention to what we heard, so we do not drift away. 🛡️
## Hebrews 2:2
The message through angels was binding, and every wrong act and disobedience received just punishment. ⚖️
## Hebrews 2:3
How will we escape if we neglect this great salvation? The Lord first announced it, and those who heard Him confirmed it to us. 📣
## Hebrews 2:4
God confirmed it through signs, wonders, miracles, and gifts of the Holy Spirit, distributed according to His will. 🕊️
## Hebrews 2:5
God did not place the future world we speak about under angels. 🌍
## Hebrews 2:6
Someone testified, “What is man that You remember him, or the son of man that You care for him? 💭
## Hebrews 2:7
You made him a little lower than angels and crowned him with glory and honor. 👑
## Hebrews 2:8
You placed everything under his feet.” God left nothing outside his control. Yet we do not now see everything under his rule. 💭
## Hebrews 2:9
We see Jesus, made a little lower than angels, now crowned with glory and honor because He suffered death. This was so He could taste death for everyone by God’s grace. ✝️
## Hebrews 2:10
Everything exists for God and through Him. In bringing many sons to glory, He fittingly made the author of their salvation perfect through suffering. 🌅
## Hebrews 2:11
The One who makes people holy and those made holy belong to one family. Jesus is not ashamed to call them brothers. ❤️
## Hebrews 2:12
He says, “I will announce Your name to My brothers and sing Your praise in the assembly.” 🎵
## Hebrews 2:13
He also says, “I will trust Him,” and, “Here I am with the children God gave Me.” ❤️
## Hebrews 2:14
The children have flesh and blood, so He shared their humanity. Through death, He could destroy the devil, who holds death’s power. ✝️
## Hebrews 2:15
He could free those whose fear of death enslaved them all their lives. 🛡️
## Hebrews 2:16
He helps Abraham’s descendants rather than angels. 🌳
## Hebrews 2:17
He had to become like His brothers in every way. He could then be a merciful, faithful high priest serving God and making atonement for the people’s sins. 🙏
## Hebrews 2:18
He suffered when tempted, so He can help those who face temptation. 🛡️
