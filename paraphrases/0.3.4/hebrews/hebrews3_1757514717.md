## Hebrews 3:1
Holy brothers who share heaven’s call, focus on Jesus. We acknowledge Him as our apostle and high priest. ✝️
## Hebrews 3:2
He was faithful to the One who appointed Him, as Moses was faithful in God’s whole house. 👣
## Hebrews 3:3
Jesus deserves more glory than Moses, as a builder deserves more honor than his house. 🏠
## Hebrews 3:4
Every house has a builder, and God builds everything. 🛠️
## Hebrews 3:5
Moses faithfully served in God’s whole house. He testified about what would be spoken later. 📖
## Hebrews 3:6
Christ faithfully rules God’s house as the Son. We are His house if we hold firmly to our confidence and the hope we boast about. 🙏
## Hebrews 3:7
The Holy Spirit says, “Today, if you hear His voice, 📣
## Hebrews 3:8
do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion, on the wilderness’s day of testing. ⚠️
## Hebrews 3:9
Your ancestors tested Me there and saw My works for forty years. 👣
## Hebrews 3:10
I was angry with that generation. I said, ‘Their hearts always wander, and they do not know My ways.’ ⚖️
## Hebrews 3:11
In anger, I swore, ‘They will never enter My rest.’” ⚠️
## Hebrews 3:12
Brothers, make sure none of you has an evil, unbelieving heart that leaves the living God. 🛡️
## Hebrews 3:13
Encourage each other daily while it is called today. Do not let sin’s deceit harden anyone. ❤️
## Hebrews 3:14
We share Christ if we hold our first assurance firmly to the end. 🙏
## Hebrews 3:15
“Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion.” 📖
## Hebrews 3:16
Who heard and rebelled? Was it not everyone Moses led from Egypt? 💭
## Hebrews 3:17
With whom was God angry for forty years? Was it not sinners whose bodies fell in the wilderness? ⚖️
## Hebrews 3:18
To whom did He swear they would never enter His rest? Was it not those who disobeyed? ⚠️
## Hebrews 3:19
Their unbelief prevented them from entering. 💭
