## Hebrews 10:1
The law is only a shadow of coming good things, rather than the realities themselves. Repeated yearly sacrifices can never perfect those approaching to worship. 📖
## Hebrews 10:2
If they could, would offerings not stop? Worshipers would be cleansed once for all, without further guilt for sins. 💭
## Hebrews 10:3
Instead, sacrifices remind them of sins each year. ⏳
## Hebrews 10:4
Bulls’ and goats’ blood cannot remove sins. ⚠️
## Hebrews 10:5
Christ entered the world and said, “You did not desire sacrifice and offering, but prepared a body for Me. ✝️
## Hebrews 10:6
You found no delight in burnt offerings or sin offerings. 🙏
## Hebrews 10:7
I said, ‘God, here I am to do Your will, as the scroll says about Me.’” 📜
## Hebrews 10:8
He said God did not desire or enjoy sacrifices, offerings, burnt offerings, and sin offerings. Yet the law required these offerings. 📖
## Hebrews 10:9
Then He said, “I came to do Your will.” He removes the first to establish the second. ✝️
## Hebrews 10:10
By that will, Jesus Christ’s bodily sacrifice made us holy once for all. ❤️
## Hebrews 10:11
Every priest serves daily and repeats sacrifices that can never remove sins. 🙏
## Hebrews 10:12
This Priest offered one sacrifice for sins for all time, then sat at God’s right hand. 👑
## Hebrews 10:13
He waits for His enemies to become a footstool for His feet. 👑
## Hebrews 10:14
One offering perfected forever those being made holy. ✝️
## Hebrews 10:15
The Holy Spirit also testifies about this. He first says: 🕊️
## Hebrews 10:16
“After those days, I will make this covenant with them,” declares the Lord. “I will place My laws in their hearts and write them on their minds.” ❤️
## Hebrews 10:17
He adds, “I will no longer remember their sins and lawless acts.” ❤️
## Hebrews 10:18
Once these are forgiven, no offering for sin is needed. 🙏
## Hebrews 10:19
Brothers, Jesus’ blood gives us confidence to enter the Most Holy Place. ✝️
## Hebrews 10:20
He opened a new, living way through the curtain of His body. 🌅
## Hebrews 10:21
We have a great priest over God’s house. 🙏
## Hebrews 10:22
Draw near with sincere hearts and full assurance of faith. Our hearts are sprinkled clean from guilty consciences, and our bodies washed with pure water. 💧
## Hebrews 10:23
Hold firmly to the hope we profess. The One who promised is faithful. 🛡️
## Hebrews 10:24
Consider how to encourage each other toward love and good actions. ❤️
## Hebrews 10:25
Do not neglect meeting together, as some do. Encourage each other more as you see the Day approach. 🤝
## Hebrews 10:26
If we deliberately continue sinning after receiving knowledge of the truth, no further sacrifice for sins remains. ⚠️
## Hebrews 10:27
Only fearful expectation of judgment and raging fire remains. That fire will consume all adversaries. 🔥
## Hebrews 10:28
Those rejecting Moses’ law died without mercy on two or three witnesses’ testimony. ⚖️
## Hebrews 10:29
What greater punishment does someone deserve who trampled God’s Son? He treated the covenant blood that made him holy as unholy and insulted the Spirit of grace. ⚠️
## Hebrews 10:30
We know the One who said, “Revenge is Mine. I will repay,” and, “The Lord will judge His people.” 📖
## Hebrews 10:31
Falling into the living God’s hands is fearful. ⚖️
## Hebrews 10:32
Remember your early days in the light. You endured great conflict and suffering. 💪
## Hebrews 10:33
Sometimes you faced public mockery and persecution. Other times, you stood with those treated that way. 🤝
## Hebrews 10:34
You cared for prisoners and gladly accepted the loss of your property. You knew you had a better, permanent possession. ❤️
## Hebrews 10:35
Do not throw away your confidence. It brings a great reward. 🌅
## Hebrews 10:36
You need endurance. After doing God’s will, you will receive His promise. 💪
## Hebrews 10:37
“In a little while, the coming One will arrive without delay. ⏳
## Hebrews 10:38
My righteous one will live by faith. If he turns back, I will take no pleasure in him.” 🙏
## Hebrews 10:39
We belong to those whose faith preserves their souls, rather than those who turn back to destruction. 🛡️
