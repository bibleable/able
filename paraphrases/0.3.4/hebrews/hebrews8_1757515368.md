## Hebrews 8:1
Here is our main point: our high priest sat at the right hand of the Majesty’s throne in heaven. 👑
## Hebrews 8:2
He serves in the holy place and true sacred tent, which the Lord set up rather than people. 🙏
## Hebrews 8:3
Every high priest offers gifts and sacrifices. This One also needed something to offer. 🎁
## Hebrews 8:4
If He were on earth, He would not be a priest. Other priests already offer gifts by the law. 💭
## Hebrews 8:5
Their place of service is a copy and shadow of heavenly things. God warned Moses before he built the sacred tent: “Make everything by the pattern shown on the mountain.” ⛰️
## Hebrews 8:6
Jesus received a greater ministry. He mediates a better covenant based on better promises. ✝️
## Hebrews 8:7
If the first covenant had no fault, no second would be needed. 📜
## Hebrews 8:8
God found fault with the people and said, “Days are coming when I will make a new covenant with Israel and Judah. 📖
## Hebrews 8:9
It differs from the covenant I made when I took their ancestors’ hands and led them from Egypt. They abandoned My covenant, and I disregarded them,” declares the Lord. 👣
## Hebrews 8:10
“After those days, I will make this covenant with Israel. I will place My laws in their minds and write them on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be My people. ❤️
## Hebrews 8:11
No one will teach his neighbor or brother, ‘Know the Lord.’ Everyone, least to greatest, will know Me. 🙏
## Hebrews 8:12
I will forgive their evil and no longer remember their sins.” ❤️
## Hebrews 8:13
Calling the covenant new makes the first obsolete. What is obsolete and aging will soon disappear. 📜
