## Hebrews 1:1
Long ago, God spoke to our ancestors through prophets on many occasions and in many ways. 📖
## Hebrews 1:2
In these last days, He spoke through His Son. He appointed the Son heir of everything and made the universe through Him. ✨
## Hebrews 1:3
The Son is the radiance of God’s glory and the exact representation of His nature. His powerful word sustains everything. After providing purification for sins, He sat at the right hand of the Majesty on high. 👑
## Hebrews 1:4
He became far greater than angels, just as His inherited name is far more excellent than theirs. ✨
## Hebrews 1:5
To which angel did God say, “You are My Son. Today I became Your Father”? Or, “I will be His Father, and He will be My Son”? ❤️
## Hebrews 1:6
When God brings His firstborn into the world, He says, “Let all God’s angels worship Him.” 🙏
## Hebrews 1:7
About angels, He says, “He makes His angels winds and His servants flames of fire.” 🔥
## Hebrews 1:8
About the Son, He says, “Your throne, God, lasts forever and ever. Justice is Your kingdom’s scepter. 👑
## Hebrews 1:9
You loved righteousness and hated evil. So God, Your God, anointed You above Your companions with the oil of joy.” ✨
## Hebrews 1:10
He also says, “Lord, You laid earth’s foundations in the beginning. You made the heavens with Your hands. 🌍
## Hebrews 1:11
They will perish, but You remain. They will wear out like clothes. ⏳
## Hebrews 1:12
You will roll them like a robe, and they will be changed like clothes. You remain the same, and Your years never end.” ✨
## Hebrews 1:13
To which angel did He say, “Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet”? 👑
## Hebrews 1:14
Are angels not serving spirits sent to help those who will inherit salvation? 🕊️
