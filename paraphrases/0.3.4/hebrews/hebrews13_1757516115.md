## Hebrews 13:1
Continue to love each other as brothers. ❤️
## Hebrews 13:2
Remember to welcome strangers. Some people have welcomed angels without knowing it. 🏠
## Hebrews 13:3
Remember prisoners as if you were bound with them. Remember mistreated people as if you suffered with them. ⛓️
## Hebrews 13:4
Everyone must honor marriage and keep the marriage bed pure. God will judge sexually immoral people and those who commit adultery. 💍
## Hebrews 13:5
Keep your lives free from love of money. Be satisfied with what you have. God said, “I will never leave you or abandon you.” ❤️
## Hebrews 13:6
We can confidently say, “The Lord helps me. I will not fear. What can people do to me?” 🛡️
## Hebrews 13:7
Remember the leaders who spoke God’s word to you. Consider the result of their way of life and imitate their faith. 🙏
## Hebrews 13:8
Jesus Christ remains the same yesterday, today, and forever. ✝️
## Hebrews 13:9
Do not let strange teachings carry you away. Grace strengthens the heart. Foods bring no benefit to those devoted to them. ❤️
## Hebrews 13:10
We have an altar from which those serving at the sacred tent have no right to eat. 🙏
## Hebrews 13:11
The high priest brings animals’ blood into the Holy Place as a sin sacrifice. Their bodies are burned outside the camp. 🔥
## Hebrews 13:12
Jesus likewise suffered outside the city gate to make the people holy through His own blood. ✝️
## Hebrews 13:13
Go to Him outside the camp and bear the disgrace He bore. 🚶
## Hebrews 13:14
We have no permanent city here. We seek the coming city. 🏙️
## Hebrews 13:15
Through Jesus, continually offer God a sacrifice of praise. This is the fruit of lips that confess His name. 🙏
## Hebrews 13:16
Remember to do good and share with others. These sacrifices please God. 🤝
## Hebrews 13:17
Obey your leaders and submit to them. They watch over your souls and must give an account. Let them lead with joy, since grief would bring you no benefit. 🙏
## Hebrews 13:18
Pray for us. We are convinced our consciences are clear, and we want to live honorably in every way. 🙏
## Hebrews 13:19
I especially urge you to pray that I may return to you soon. 🤝
## Hebrews 13:20
The God of peace raised our Lord Jesus from the dead through the eternal covenant’s blood. Jesus is the great Shepherd of the sheep. ✝️
## Hebrews 13:21
May this God give you every good thing needed to do His will. Through Jesus Christ, may He accomplish in us what pleases Him. Glory belongs to Jesus Christ forever and ever. Amen. 🙏
## Hebrews 13:22
Brothers, I urge you to accept my words of encouragement. I wrote only briefly. 📜
## Hebrews 13:23
Our brother Timothy has been released. If he arrives soon, I will visit you with him. 🤝
## Hebrews 13:24
Greet all your leaders and all the saints. Those from Italy greet you. 👋
## Hebrews 13:25
Grace be with you all. ❤️
