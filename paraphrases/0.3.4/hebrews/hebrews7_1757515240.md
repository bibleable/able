## Hebrews 7:1
Melchizedek was Salem’s king and priest of God Most High. He met Abraham returning from killing the kings and blessed him. 👑
## Hebrews 7:2
Abraham gave him a tenth of everything. His name means “king of righteousness.” King of Salem also means “king of peace.” 🕊️
## Hebrews 7:3
He has no father, mother, or genealogy, no beginning of days or end of life. Like God’s Son, he remains a priest forever. ⏳
## Hebrews 7:4
Consider Melchizedek’s greatness. Even Abraham, the patriarch, gave him a tenth of the plunder. 🎁
## Hebrews 7:5
The law commands Levi’s priestly sons to collect a tenth from their brothers. Those brothers also descend from Abraham. ⚖️
## Hebrews 7:6
Melchizedek did not descend from Levi. Yet he collected Abraham’s tenth and blessed the man who held the promises. 🙏
## Hebrews 7:7
Clearly, the greater person blesses the lesser. 💭
## Hebrews 7:8
Mortal Levites receive the tenth. But Melchizedek is declared to live on. 📖
## Hebrews 7:9
In a way, Levi, who receives the tenth, paid it through Abraham. 🎁
## Hebrews 7:10
Levi was still in his ancestor’s loins when Melchizedek met Abraham. 🌳
## Hebrews 7:11
The people received the law based on Levi’s priesthood. If that priesthood could bring perfection, why need another priest in Melchizedek’s order rather than Aaron’s? 💭
## Hebrews 7:12
A changed priesthood requires a changed law. 📜
## Hebrews 7:13
The One described belonged to another tribe. No one from it served at the altar. 💭
## Hebrews 7:14
Our Lord clearly descended from Judah. Moses said nothing about priests from that tribe. 🌳
## Hebrews 7:15
This becomes clearer if another priest like Melchizedek appears. 📖
## Hebrews 7:16
He became priest through the power of a life that cannot be destroyed, rather than a law of succession. 🌅
## Hebrews 7:17
Scripture testifies, “You are a priest forever in Melchizedek’s order.” 📖
## Hebrews 7:18
The former command is set aside because it was weak and useless. 📜
## Hebrews 7:19
The law made nothing perfect. A better hope now lets us draw near to God. 🙏
## Hebrews 7:20
This happened with an oath. Others became priests without one. 📜
## Hebrews 7:21
Jesus became priest through this oath: “The Lord swore and will not change His mind. You are a priest forever.” 📖
## Hebrews 7:22
This oath makes Jesus the guarantee of a better covenant. ✝️
## Hebrews 7:23
There were many other priests because death ended their service. ⏳
## Hebrews 7:24
Jesus lives forever, so His priesthood is permanent. 👑
## Hebrews 7:25
He can completely save those approaching God through Him. He always lives to plead for them. 🙏
## Hebrews 7:26
This high priest suits us: holy, innocent, without stain, set apart from sinners, and exalted above the heavens. ✨
## Hebrews 7:27
He does not need daily sacrifices for His sins and then the people’s, as other high priests do. He offered Himself once for all. ✝️
## Hebrews 7:28
The law appoints weak men as high priests. The later oath appointed the Son, made perfect forever. 👑
