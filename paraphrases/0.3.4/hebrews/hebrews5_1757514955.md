## Hebrews 5:1
Every high priest is chosen from people to represent them before God. He offers gifts and sacrifices for sins. 🙏
## Hebrews 5:2
He can treat ignorant and misguided people gently because he is weak too. ❤️
## Hebrews 5:3
He must therefore offer sacrifices for his own sins and the people’s sins. 🙏
## Hebrews 5:4
No one takes this honor himself. God must call him, as He called Aaron. 📣
## Hebrews 5:5
Christ did not take the glory of high priesthood Himself. God called Him, saying, “You are My Son. Today I became Your Father.” ❤️
## Hebrews 5:6
He also says, “You are a priest forever in Melchizedek’s order.” 📖
## Hebrews 5:7
On earth, Jesus prayed with loud cries and tears to the One who could save Him from death. His reverence caused His prayers to be heard. 🙏
## Hebrews 5:8
Though He was a Son, He learned obedience through suffering. ✝️
## Hebrews 5:9
Made perfect, He became the source of eternal salvation for everyone who obeys Him. 🌅
## Hebrews 5:10
God appointed Him high priest in Melchizedek’s order. 👑
## Hebrews 5:11
We have much to say, but explaining is hard because you are slow to hear. 📖
## Hebrews 5:12
You should be teachers by now, but need someone to teach God’s basic truths again. You need milk rather than solid food! 🥛
## Hebrews 5:13
Anyone living on milk is still a baby, inexperienced in the message of righteousness. 🌱
## Hebrews 5:14
Solid food is for mature people. Constant practice trains their senses to distinguish good from evil. 💭
