## Hebrews 6:1
Leave Christ’s basic teachings and move toward maturity. Do not lay the foundation again of repentance from dead works and faith in God. 🌱
## Hebrews 6:2
This foundation includes instruction about baptisms, placing hands on people, resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment. 📖
## Hebrews 6:3
We will do this if God permits. 🙏
## Hebrews 6:4
This warning concerns those once enlightened, who tasted heaven’s gift and shared the Holy Spirit. ⚠️
## Hebrews 6:5
They tasted God’s good word and the powers of the coming age. 📖
## Hebrews 6:6
If they then fall away, it is impossible to restore them to repentance. They crucify God’s Son again and publicly shame Him. ⚠️
## Hebrews 6:7
Land receives God’s blessing when it drinks frequent rain and grows crops useful to those who tend it. 🌾
## Hebrews 6:8
Land growing thorns and thistles is worthless and near a curse. It will finally be burned. 🔥
## Hebrews 6:9
Dear friends, though we speak this way, we are convinced of better things for you, things that accompany salvation. ❤️
## Hebrews 6:10
God is just and will not forget your work and love for His name. You served His holy people and still do. 🤝
## Hebrews 6:11
We want everyone to show the same diligence to the end, making your hope sure. 💪
## Hebrews 6:12
Do not be lazy. Follow those who inherit the promises through faith and patience. 👣
## Hebrews 6:13
God promised Abraham and swore by Himself because no one greater existed to swear by. 📜
## Hebrews 6:14
He said, “I will surely bless you and multiply your descendants.” 🌳
## Hebrews 6:15
Abraham waited patiently and received the promise. ⏳
## Hebrews 6:16
People swear by someone greater. Their oath confirms a matter and ends argument. ⚖️
## Hebrews 6:17
God wanted the promise’s heirs to know His purpose never changes. He guaranteed it with an oath. 📜
## Hebrews 6:18
By these two unchanging things, in which God cannot lie, we can be strongly encouraged. We fled to take hold of the hope before us. 🛡️
## Hebrews 6:19
This hope is a firm, secure anchor for the soul. It enters the inner holy place behind the curtain. ⚓
## Hebrews 6:20
Jesus entered ahead of us and for us. He became high priest forever in Melchizedek’s order. ✝️
