## Hebrews 12:1
Such a great cloud of witnesses surrounds us. Remove every burden and the sin that easily traps us. Run with endurance the race set before us. 🏃
## Hebrews 12:2
Keep your eyes on Jesus, who begins and perfects our faith. For the joy ahead, He endured the cross and disregarded its shame. He sat at the right hand of God’s throne. ✝️
## Hebrews 12:3
Consider how He endured hostility from sinners, so you will not become weary or lose courage. 💪
## Hebrews 12:4
You have struggled against sin, but have not yet resisted to the point of shedding your blood. 🛡️
## Hebrews 12:5
You forgot the encouragement that addresses you as sons: “My son, take the Lord’s discipline seriously. Do not lose courage when He corrects you. 📖
## Hebrews 12:6
The Lord disciplines those He loves and punishes every son He accepts.” ❤️
## Hebrews 12:7
Endure suffering as discipline. God treats you as sons. What son does his father not discipline? 👨‍👦
## Hebrews 12:8
Everyone receives discipline. Without it, you are illegitimate children, rather than true sons. ⚠️
## Hebrews 12:9
We respected our earthly fathers when they disciplined us. Should we not submit much more to the Father of our spirits and live? 🙏
## Hebrews 12:10
Our fathers disciplined us briefly as they thought best. God disciplines us for our good, so we may share His holiness. ❤️
## Hebrews 12:11
Discipline feels painful at the time, rather than enjoyable. Later, it produces a harvest of righteousness and peace for those it has trained. 🌾
## Hebrews 12:12
Strengthen your limp hands and weak knees. 💪
## Hebrews 12:13
Make straight paths for your feet, so the lame may be healed rather than disabled. 🚶
## Hebrews 12:14
Seek peace with everyone and seek holiness. Without holiness, no one will see the Lord. 🕊️
## Hebrews 12:15
Make sure no one falls short of God’s grace. Do not let a bitter root grow, cause trouble, and make many unclean. 🌱
## Hebrews 12:16
Make sure no one is sexually immoral or godless like Esau. He sold his rights as firstborn for one meal. ⚠️
## Hebrews 12:17
Later, he wanted to inherit the blessing, but was rejected. He found no ground for repentance, although he sought the blessing with tears. 💧
## Hebrews 12:18
You have not come to a mountain you can touch, burning with fire. You have not come to darkness, gloom, and storm, ⛰️
## Hebrews 12:19
or to a trumpet blast or voice whose hearers begged to hear no more. 📯
## Hebrews 12:20
They could not bear the command: “Even an animal that touches the mountain must be stoned.” ⚠️
## Hebrews 12:21
The sight was so frightening that Moses said, “I tremble with fear.” 😨
## Hebrews 12:22
Instead, you have come to Mount Zion, the living God’s city, the heavenly Jerusalem. You have come to countless angels 🏙️
## Hebrews 12:23
gathered with joy and to the congregation of the firstborn, registered in heaven. You have come to God, everyone’s Judge, and the spirits of righteous people made perfect. 🙏
## Hebrews 12:24
You have come to Jesus, who mediates a new covenant. You have come to sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than Abel’s blood. ✝️
## Hebrews 12:25
Do not refuse the One who speaks. Those who refused the One warning them on earth did not escape. How much less will we escape if we reject the One warning us from heaven? ⚠️
## Hebrews 12:26
His voice shook the earth then. Now He promises, “Once more, I will shake both earth and heaven.” 🌍
## Hebrews 12:27
“Once more” means that created things, which can be shaken, will be removed. Things that cannot be shaken will remain. 🛡️
## Hebrews 12:28
We are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken. Be thankful and worship God acceptably, with deep respect and awe. 👑
## Hebrews 12:29
Our God is a fire that consumes. 🔥
