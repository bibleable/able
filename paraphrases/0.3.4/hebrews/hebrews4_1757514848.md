## Hebrews 4:1
God’s promise of entering His rest remains. Be careful that none of you falls short of it. 🛡️
## Hebrews 4:2
We received good news as they did. It did not help them because they did not share the faith of those who understood it. 📖
## Hebrews 4:3
We who believe enter His rest. For the others, God said, “In anger, I swore, ‘They will never enter My rest.’” His works were finished from the world’s foundation. 🌍
## Hebrews 4:4
He said about the seventh day, “God rested from all His work on the seventh day.” 📅
## Hebrews 4:5
He also said, “They will never enter My rest.” ⚠️
## Hebrews 4:6
Some may still enter His rest. Those who first heard the good news did not enter because they disobeyed. 👣
## Hebrews 4:7
Much later, God chose another day called “Today” through David. He said, “Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts.” 📖
## Hebrews 4:8
If Joshua had given them rest, God would not later have spoken of another day. 💭
## Hebrews 4:9
A Sabbath rest remains for God’s people. 🕊️
## Hebrews 4:10
Anyone entering God’s rest rests from his own work, as God rested from His. 🙏
## Hebrews 4:11
Make every effort to enter that rest. Do not fall through the same disobedience. 🛡️
## Hebrews 4:12
God’s word is living and active, sharper than a sword with two edges. It pierces to divide soul and spirit, joints and marrow. It judges the heart’s thoughts and intentions. ⚔️
## Hebrews 4:13
Nothing created is hidden from God. Everything lies uncovered before Him, and we must answer to Him. 👀
## Hebrews 4:14
Jesus, God’s Son, is our great high priest who passed through the heavens. Hold firmly to what we profess. ✝️
## Hebrews 4:15
Our high priest understands our weaknesses. He was tempted in every way we are, yet never sinned. ❤️
## Hebrews 4:16
Approach the throne of grace confidently, so we may receive mercy and find grace to help when we need it. 🙏
