## Hebrews 11:1
Faith assures us of what we hope for. It gives certainty about what we cannot see. 🙏
## Hebrews 11:2
The people of old received approval because of their faith. 📖
## Hebrews 11:3
By faith, we understand that God formed the universe by His command. What we see did not come from visible things. 🌌
## Hebrews 11:4
By faith, Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain. God approved his gifts and declared him righteous because of his faith. Through faith, Abel still speaks, although he is dead. 🙏
## Hebrews 11:5
By faith, Enoch was taken away without experiencing death. No one could find him because God had taken him. Before this, he received approval as someone who pleased God. 🌅
## Hebrews 11:6
Without faith, it is impossible to please God. Anyone approaching Him must believe He exists and rewards those who earnestly seek Him. 🙏
## Hebrews 11:7
God warned Noah about things not yet seen. By faith, Noah feared God and built an ark to save his family. Through faith, he condemned the world and became an heir of the righteousness that comes through faith. 🚢
## Hebrews 11:8
God called Abraham to a place he would later receive as an inheritance. By faith, Abraham obeyed and left, although he did not know his destination. 🚶
## Hebrews 11:9
By faith, he lived in the promised land as a stranger in a foreign country. He lived in tents with Isaac and Jacob, who shared the same promised inheritance. ⛺
## Hebrews 11:10
He looked forward to the city with foundations, which God designed and built. 🏙️
## Hebrews 11:11
Sarah could not bear children and was too old. By faith, she received the ability to conceive because she considered the One who promised faithful. 👶
## Hebrews 11:12
Thus, one man, as good as dead, had descendants as numerous as the sky’s stars and as countless as the seashore’s sand. ✨
## Hebrews 11:13
All these people died in faith without receiving the promised things. They saw and welcomed them from far away. They admitted they were strangers and exiles on earth. 🌍
## Hebrews 11:14
People who say this show that they seek a country of their own. 🏠
## Hebrews 11:15
If they had thought of the country they left, they would have had opportunities to return. 🚶
## Hebrews 11:16
Instead, they wanted a better country, a heavenly one. God is therefore not ashamed to be called their God. He prepared a city for them. 🌅
## Hebrews 11:17
By faith, Abraham offered Isaac on the altar when tested. He had received the promises and was ready to offer his one and only son. 🙏
## Hebrews 11:18
Yet God had told him, “Your descendants will be counted through Isaac.” 📖
## Hebrews 11:19
Abraham reasoned that God could raise the dead. In a sense, he received Isaac back from death. 🌅
## Hebrews 11:20
By faith, Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau concerning their future. 🙏
## Hebrews 11:21
By faith, the dying Jacob blessed each of Joseph’s sons. He worshiped as he leaned on the top of his staff. 🙏
## Hebrews 11:22
By faith, Joseph spoke about the Israelites’ departure from Egypt as his death approached. He gave instructions about his bones. 🦴
## Hebrews 11:23
By faith, Moses’ parents hid him for three months after his birth. They saw he was beautiful and did not fear the king’s order. 👶
## Hebrews 11:24
By faith, the grown Moses refused to be called Pharaoh’s daughter’s son. 👑
## Hebrews 11:25
He chose to suffer oppression with God’s people rather than enjoy sin for a short time. 🛡️
## Hebrews 11:26
He valued disgrace for Christ more than Egypt’s treasures because he looked ahead to his reward. ✝️
## Hebrews 11:27
By faith, Moses left Egypt without fearing the king’s anger. He endured because he saw the invisible One. 🚶
## Hebrews 11:28
By faith, he kept the Passover and sprinkled blood, so the destroyer of the firstborn would not touch Israel’s firstborn. 🩸
## Hebrews 11:29
By faith, the people crossed the Red Sea as on dry land. When the Egyptians tried to follow, they drowned. 🌊
## Hebrews 11:30
By faith, Jericho’s walls fell after the people marched around them for seven days. 🧱
## Hebrews 11:31
By faith, Rahab the prostitute welcomed the spies in peace. She did not die with those who disobeyed. 🏠
## Hebrews 11:32
What more can I say? Time prevents me from telling about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets. 📖
## Hebrews 11:33
Through faith, they conquered kingdoms, carried out justice, and received promised things. They shut lions’ mouths. 🦁
## Hebrews 11:34
They put out raging fire and escaped death by the sword. Their weakness became strength. They became mighty in battle and made foreign armies flee. 🛡️
## Hebrews 11:35
Women received their dead back, raised to life. Others endured torture and refused release so they might receive a better resurrection. 🌅
## Hebrews 11:36
Others endured mockery, whipping, chains, and prison. ⛓️
## Hebrews 11:37
They were stoned, sawed in two, and killed by the sword. They wore sheepskins and goatskins. They were poor, oppressed, and mistreated. 💔
## Hebrews 11:38
The world was not worthy of them. They wandered in deserts and mountains and hid in caves and holes in the ground. 🏔️
## Hebrews 11:39
All received approval for their faith, yet they did not receive what was promised. 🙏
## Hebrews 11:40
God planned something better for us, so they would be made perfect together with us. 🤝
