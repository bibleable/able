## Hebrews 9:1
The first covenant had worship rules and an earthly holy place. 🏠
## Hebrews 9:2
A sacred tent was prepared. Its first room held the lampstand, table, and holy bread. This was the Holy Place. 🕯️
## Hebrews 9:3
Behind the second curtain was the Most Holy Place. 🙏
## Hebrews 9:4
It held the gold incense altar and gold-covered ark of the covenant. The ark held a gold manna jar, Aaron’s budded staff, and the covenant’s stone tablets. 🏺
## Hebrews 9:5
Glorious cherubim stood above the ark and overshadowed the mercy seat. We cannot discuss these details now. ✨
## Hebrews 9:6
With everything prepared, priests regularly entered the first room for sacred duties. 🙏
## Hebrews 9:7
Only the high priest entered the second room, once yearly. He always brought blood for himself and the people’s sins committed in ignorance. ⚖️
## Hebrews 9:8
The Holy Spirit showed that the way into the Most Holy Place remained undisclosed while the first sacred tent stood. 🕊️
## Hebrews 9:9
This illustrates the present time. The offered gifts and sacrifices could not cleanse a worshiper’s conscience. 💭
## Hebrews 9:10
They concern food, drink, and special washings. These outward rules lasted until reform’s time. 💧
## Hebrews 9:11
Christ came as high priest of the good things that have come. He passed through a greater, more perfect sacred tent, not made by hands or belonging to this creation. ✝️
## Hebrews 9:12
He entered the Most Holy Place once for all through His own blood, rather than goats’ and calves’ blood. He secured eternal redemption. ❤️
## Hebrews 9:13
Goats’ and bulls’ blood and a heifer’s ashes, sprinkled on unclean people, make their bodies ceremonially clean. 🙏
## Hebrews 9:14
How much more will Christ’s blood purify our consciences from dead works, so we may serve the living God! Through the eternal Spirit, He offered Himself without blemish to God. ✝️
## Hebrews 9:15
Christ mediates a new covenant so those called can receive the promised eternal inheritance. He died to redeem them from sins committed under the first covenant. 🌅
## Hebrews 9:16
A will requires proof that its maker died. 📜
## Hebrews 9:17
It takes effect after death, never while its maker lives. ⏳
## Hebrews 9:18
The first covenant also took effect with blood. 📜
## Hebrews 9:19
Moses announced every legal command to the people. He took calves’ and goats’ blood, water, scarlet wool, and hyssop, and sprinkled the scroll and everyone. 💧
## Hebrews 9:20
He said, “This is the covenant’s blood. God commanded you to keep this covenant.” 📖
## Hebrews 9:21
He also sprinkled the sacred tent and every worship vessel with blood. 🏺
## Hebrews 9:22
The law requires blood to purify nearly everything. Without blood being shed, there is no forgiveness. ⚖️
## Hebrews 9:23
These sacrifices had to purify copies of heavenly things. The heavenly things needed better sacrifices. ✨
## Hebrews 9:24
Christ entered heaven itself rather than a human-made copy of the true holy place. He now appears before God for us. 🙏
## Hebrews 9:25
He does not repeatedly offer Himself. The high priest enters the Most Holy Place yearly with another’s blood. ✝️
## Hebrews 9:26
Otherwise, Christ would suffer repeatedly from the world’s foundation. He appeared once for all at the ages’ end to remove sin through His own sacrifice. 🌅
## Hebrews 9:27
People are appointed to die once, then face judgment. ⚖️
## Hebrews 9:28
Christ was likewise offered once to bear many people’s sins. He will appear again to save those eagerly awaiting Him, rather than to bear sin. ✝️
