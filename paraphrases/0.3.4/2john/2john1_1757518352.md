## 2 John 1:1
I am the elder. I write to the chosen lady and her children. I love you in the truth. Everyone who knows the truth loves you too. ✉️❤️
## 2 John 1:2
We love you because the truth lives in us. It will stay with us forever. ❤️
## 2 John 1:3
Kindness, care, and peace from God the Father and Jesus Christ, His Son, will be with us. They will be with us in truth and love. ❤️🤝
## 2 John 1:4
I found that some of your children live by the truth. This gave me great joy. The Father commands us to live this way. 😊✅
## 2 John 1:5
Dear lady, I now ask that we love one another. This is not a new command. We received it at the beginning. ❤️
## 2 John 1:6
Love means that we live by His commands. You heard this command from the beginning: you must live in love. ❤️📖
## 2 John 1:7
Many people who deceive others go out into the world. They refuse to confess that Jesus Christ comes in a real human body. Such a person is a deceiver and the antichrist, an enemy of Christ. ❌
## 2 John 1:8
Watch yourselves. Do not lose what we worked for. Then you can receive your full reward. 👀
## 2 John 1:9
A person who goes beyond Christ’s teaching and does not stay in it does not have God. Whoever stays in His teaching has both the Father and the Son. 📖
## 2 John 1:10
If someone comes without this teaching, do not welcome that person into your house. Do not even give that person a greeting. 🏠
## 2 John 1:11
Anyone who greets such a person shares in that person’s evil acts. ❌
## 2 John 1:12
I have much to write to you. But I prefer not to use paper and ink. I hope to come and talk face to face. Then our joy can be complete. 😊
## 2 John 1:13
The children of your chosen sister send you greetings. 👋
