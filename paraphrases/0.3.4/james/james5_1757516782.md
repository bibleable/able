## James 5:1
Now, rich people, cry and wail because of the misery that will come upon you. 😢
## James 5:2
Your riches have rotted, and moths have eaten your clothes. ⚠️
## James 5:3
Your gold and silver have corroded. Their corrosion will testify against you and consume your flesh like fire. You stored up treasure in the last days. 🔥
## James 5:4
You withheld pay from the workers who harvested your fields. That unpaid money cries out against you. The workers’ cries have reached the ears of the Lord of Hosts. ⚖️
## James 5:5
You lived on earth in luxury and gave yourselves every pleasure. You fattened your hearts on the day of slaughter. ⚠️
## James 5:6
You condemned and murdered righteous people who did not resist you. ⛔
## James 5:7
Brothers, be patient until the Lord comes. A farmer waits patiently for the precious harvest and for the autumn and spring rains. 🌾
## James 5:8
You too must be patient and strengthen your hearts. The Lord’s return is near. 💪
## James 5:9
Brothers, do not complain about each other, so that you will not be judged. Look, the Judge stands at the door! ⚖️
## James 5:10
Brothers, follow the example of the prophets who spoke in the Lord’s name. They were patient through suffering. 👣
## James 5:11
We call those who endured blessed. You heard how Job endured and saw the outcome the Lord brought. The Lord is full of mercy and compassion. ❤️
## James 5:12
Above all, my brothers, do not swear oaths by heaven, earth, or anything else. Let your yes mean yes and your no mean no. Then you will not fall under judgment. 🗣️
## James 5:13
Does anyone among you suffer? He must pray. Is anyone cheerful? He must sing praises. 🙏
## James 5:14
Is anyone sick? He must call the church elders to pray over him. They must anoint him with oil in the Lord’s name. 🙏
## James 5:15
Prayer offered in faith will restore the sick person. The Lord will raise him up. If he has sinned, he will be forgiven. ❤️
## James 5:16
Confess your sins to each other and pray for each other, so that you may be healed. A righteous person’s prayer has great power to succeed. 🙏
## James 5:17
Elijah was a person like us. He prayed earnestly for no rain, and no rain fell on the land for three and a half years. 🌧️
## James 5:18
He prayed again. The sky gave rain, and the earth produced its crops. 🌾
## James 5:19
My brothers, suppose one of you wanders from the truth and someone brings him back. 👣
## James 5:20
Remember this: Anyone who turns a sinner from his wrong path will save his soul from death and cover many sins. ❤️
