## James 1:1
This letter is from James, a servant of God and the Lord Jesus Christ. Greetings to the twelve tribes scattered among the nations. ✉️
## James 1:2
My brothers, count it pure joy when you face trials of many kinds. 🌱
## James 1:3
You know that tests of your faith develop endurance. 💪
## James 1:4
Let endurance finish its work. Then you can be mature and complete, without anything missing. 🌱
## James 1:5
If you lack wisdom, ask God. He gives generously to everyone without criticism. He will give it to you. 🙏
## James 1:6
But ask with faith and without doubt. A person who doubts is like a sea wave blown and tossed by the wind. 🌊
## James 1:7
That person must not expect to receive anything from the Lord. ⚠️
## James 1:8
His mind is divided, and all his ways are unstable. 💭
## James 1:9
A brother in humble circumstances must rejoice in his high position. 🌅
## James 1:10
But a rich person must rejoice in his low position. He will pass away like a flower in a field. 🌸
## James 1:11
The sun rises with fierce heat and dries the plant. Its flower falls, and its beauty disappears. In the same way, a rich person will fade away in the middle of his activities. ☀️
## James 1:12
The person who endures a trial is blessed. When he passes the test, he will receive the crown of life God promised to those who love Him. 👑
## James 1:13
When tempted, no one must say, “God tempts me.” Evil cannot tempt God, and He does not tempt anyone. ⚠️
## James 1:14
Each person is tempted when his own evil desires lure him away. ⛔
## James 1:15
Then desire becomes pregnant and gives birth to sin. When sin is fully grown, it gives birth to death. ⚠️
## James 1:16
My dear brothers, do not be deceived. 🛡️
## James 1:17
Every good and perfect gift comes from above, from the Father of the heavenly lights. He does not change or cast a shifting shadow. ✨
## James 1:18
He chose to give us birth through the word of truth. He did this so that we would be like the firstfruits of His creation. 🌱

*Word help: Firstfruits are the first portion of a harvest.*
## James 1:19
My dear brothers, understand this: Everyone must be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to become angry. 👂
## James 1:20
Human anger does not produce the righteousness God desires. ⚠️
## James 1:21
Get rid of all moral filth and every form of evil. Humbly accept the word planted in you, which can save your souls. 🌱
## James 1:22
Do what the word says. If you only listen, you deceive yourselves. 👣
## James 1:23
Anyone who hears the word without obeying is like a person who looks at his face in a mirror. 🪞
## James 1:24
He looks at himself, walks away, and immediately forgets what he looks like. 💭
## James 1:25
But another person looks carefully into the perfect law that gives freedom and continues in it. He remembers what he hears and does it. He will be blessed in what he does. 📖
## James 1:26
Anyone who thinks he is religious but does not control his tongue deceives himself. His religion is worthless. ⚠️
## James 1:27
Pure and clean religion before our God and Father means caring for orphans and widows in their distress. It also means keeping yourself free from the world’s pollution. ❤️
