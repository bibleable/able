## James 3:1
My brothers, few of you should become teachers. You know that those of us who teach will face stricter judgment. 📖
## James 3:2
We all stumble in many ways. Anyone who never says anything wrong is perfect and can control his whole body. 🗣️
## James 3:3
We put bits in horses’ mouths to make them obey us. With these, we guide their whole bodies. 🐎
## James 3:4
Think about ships too. They are large, and strong winds drive them. But a tiny rudder guides them wherever the pilot wants. ⛵
## James 3:5
The tongue is also a small part of the body, but it boasts about great things. A tiny spark can set a great forest on fire! 🔥
## James 3:6
The tongue is a fire, a world of evil among the body’s parts. It pollutes the whole person and sets his whole life on fire. Hell itself sets the tongue on fire. 🔥
## James 3:7
People tame and have tamed all kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and sea creatures. 🐾
## James 3:8
But no person can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison. ⚠️
## James 3:9
We use the tongue to praise our Lord and Father. We also use it to curse people, who were made in God’s likeness. 🗣️
## James 3:10
Praise and curses come from the same mouth. My brothers, this must not happen! ⚠️
## James 3:11
Can fresh water and salt water flow from the same spring? 💧
## James 3:12
My brothers, can a fig tree produce olives or a grapevine produce figs? A salt spring cannot produce fresh water either. 🌳
## James 3:13
Who among you is wise and understanding? Let him show this through good behavior and humble actions that come from wisdom. 👣
## James 3:14
If your hearts contain bitter jealousy and selfish ambition, do not boast or deny the truth. ⚠️
## James 3:15
Such wisdom comes from the earth, rather than from above. It is unspiritual and comes from demons. ⛔
## James 3:16
Where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, disorder and every evil practice follow. ⚠️
## James 3:17
Wisdom from above is first pure. It loves peace, is gentle, and is willing to listen. It is full of mercy and good fruit, fair to everyone, and sincere. 🕊️
## James 3:18
Those who make peace plant in peace and harvest the fruit of righteousness. 🌱
