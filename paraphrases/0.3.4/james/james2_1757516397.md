## James 2:1
My brothers, as you hold to faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ, do not favor some people over others. ⚖️
## James 2:2
Suppose a man with a gold ring and fine clothes enters your meeting. A poor man in worn clothes also enters. 👥
## James 2:3
You give special attention to the man in fine clothes and say, “Here is an honored seat.” But you tell the poor man, “Stand there,” or, “Sit at my feet.” ⚠️
## James 2:4
Have you not made unfair differences among yourselves? Have you not become judges with evil thoughts? ⚖️
## James 2:5
Listen, my dear brothers. Did God not choose the world’s poor to be rich in faith? Did He not choose them to inherit the kingdom He promised to those who love Him? 👑
## James 2:6
But you dishonored the poor. Is it not the rich who oppress you and drag you into court? ⚠️
## James 2:7
Do they not insult the noble name by which you were called? ⛔
## James 2:8
You do well if you obey the royal law in Scripture: “Love your neighbor as yourself.” ❤️
## James 2:9
But if you favor some people over others, you sin. The law shows that you have broken it. ⚖️
## James 2:10
Anyone who keeps the whole law but breaks one part is guilty of breaking it all. 📜
## James 2:11
God said, “Do not commit adultery,” and also, “Do not murder.” If you avoid adultery but commit murder, you have broken the law. ⚖️
## James 2:12
Speak and act as people who will be judged by the law that gives freedom. 👣
## James 2:13
Those who show no mercy will receive judgment without mercy. Mercy wins over judgment. ❤️
## James 2:14
My brothers, what good is a claim of faith without actions? Can such faith save that person? 💬
## James 2:15
Suppose a brother or sister has no clothes or daily food. 🍞
## James 2:16
You say, “Go in peace. Stay warm and well fed.” But you give nothing to meet that person’s physical needs. What good is that? 💬
## James 2:17
In the same way, faith alone is dead if it produces no action. ⛔
## James 2:18
Someone may say, “You have faith, and I have actions.” Show me your faith without actions. I will show you my faith by my actions. 👣
## James 2:19
You believe there is one God. Good! Even demons believe this, and they tremble. ⚠️
## James 2:20
Foolish person, do you want proof that faith without actions is worthless? 💬
## James 2:21
Was our father Abraham not declared righteous by his actions when he offered his son Isaac on the altar? 🙏
## James 2:22
His faith worked together with his actions. What he did made his faith complete. 🌱
## James 2:23
This fulfilled the Scripture: “Abraham believed God, and God counted this as righteousness.” He was called God’s friend. ❤️
## James 2:24
You see that a person is declared righteous by actions, rather than by faith alone. ⚖️
## James 2:25
Was Rahab the prostitute not also declared righteous by her actions? She welcomed the spies and sent them away by another route. 👣
## James 2:26
A body without the spirit is dead. In the same way, faith without actions is dead. 🌱
