## James 4:1
What causes fights and arguments among you? Do they not come from the desires that fight within you? 💬
## James 4:2
You desire what you do not have. You kill and envy, but you cannot get what you want. You argue and fight. You do not have because you do not ask. ⚠️
## James 4:3
When you ask, you do not receive because your motives are wrong. You want to waste what you receive on your pleasures. ⛔
## James 4:4
You adulteresses! Do you not know that friendship with the world means hostility toward God? Anyone who chooses to be the world’s friend becomes God’s enemy. ⚠️
## James 4:5
Do you think Scripture speaks without reason when it says the Spirit God placed in us yearns with envy? 📖
## James 4:6
But God gives us more grace. Scripture says, “God opposes proud people but gives grace to humble people.” ❤️
## James 4:7
Submit yourselves to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. 🛡️
## James 4:8
Come near to God, and He will come near to you. Sinners, clean your hands. You with divided minds, purify your hearts. 🙏
## James 4:9
Grieve, mourn, and cry. Turn your laughter into mourning and your joy into sorrow. 😢
## James 4:10
Humble yourselves before the Lord, and He will lift you up. 🌅
## James 4:11
Brothers, do not slander each other. Anyone who speaks against or judges his brother speaks against and judges the law. If you judge the law, you act as its judge instead of obeying it. ⚖️
## James 4:12
Only one Lawgiver and Judge can save and destroy. Who are you to judge your neighbor? ⚖️
## James 4:13
Listen, you who say, “Today or tomorrow, we will go to a certain city. We will stay a year, do business, and make a profit.” 🧳
## James 4:14
You do not even know what tomorrow holds! What is your life? You are a mist that appears briefly and then disappears. 🌫️
## James 4:15
Instead, say, “If the Lord is willing, we will live and do this or that.” 🙏
## James 4:16
But you boast about your proud plans. All such boasting is evil. ⚠️
## James 4:17
Anyone who knows the right thing to do but fails to do it sins. 👣
