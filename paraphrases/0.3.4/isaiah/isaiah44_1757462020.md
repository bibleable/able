## Isaiah 44:1
Jacob My servant, Israel whom I chose, listen now. 📖

## Isaiah 44:2
The LORD made you, formed you from the womb, and will help you. He says, “My servant Jacob, chosen Jeshurun, do not fear. 📖

## Isaiah 44:3
“I will pour water on thirsty land and currents on dry ground. I will pour My Spirit on your descendants and My blessing on your offspring. 📖

## Isaiah 44:4
“They will sprout among grass like willows beside flowing streams. 📖

## Isaiah 44:5
“One will say, ‘I belong to the LORD.’ Another will call himself Jacob. Another will write ‘The LORD’s’ on his hand and take Israel’s name.” 📖

## Isaiah 44:6
The LORD, Israel’s King and Redeemer, the LORD of Hosts, says, “I am first and last. There is no God except Me. 📖

## Isaiah 44:7
“Who is like Me? Let him speak and present his case before Me. I established an ancient people. Let him announce what is coming and what will happen. 📖

## Isaiah 44:8
“Do not tremble or fear. Did I not tell you long ago? You are My witnesses! Is there another God? There is no other Rock. I know none.” 📖

## Isaiah 44:9
All idol makers are nothing. Their treasured things are worthless. Their witnesses fail to see or understand, so they are shamed. 📖

## Isaiah 44:10
Who makes a god or casts an idol that gives no benefit? 📖

## Isaiah 44:11
All his companions will be shamed. The craftsmen are only human. Let them gather and stand. They will all face terror and shame. 📖

## Isaiah 44:12
The blacksmith works over coals with a tool. His strong arms shape and forge an idol with hammers. But hunger weakens him. Without water, he grows faint. 📖

## Isaiah 44:13
The woodworker stretches a measuring line and marks with a stylus. He shapes with chisels and outlines with a compass. He makes a human likeness in all its glory to live in a shrine. 📖

## Isaiah 44:14
He cuts cedars or takes a cypress or oak. He lets it grow strong among forest trees. He plants a laurel, and rain grows it. 📖

## Isaiah 44:15
He uses it as fuel. Some warms him, and some makes a fire to bake bread. He also makes a god and worships it. He makes an idol and bows to it. 📖

## Isaiah 44:16
He burns half in the fire and roasts meat on it. He eats and is satisfied. He warms himself and says, “Ah, I am warm! I see the fire.” 📖

## Isaiah 44:17
He makes the rest into a god, a carved image. He bows, worships, and prays to it. “Save me! You are my god.” 📖

## Isaiah 44:18
They do not understand or discern. He shut their eyes so they cannot see. He closed their minds so they cannot understand. 📖

## Isaiah 44:19
No one considers or understands enough to say, “I burned half in the fire. I baked bread on its coals and ate roasted meat. Should I make something detestable from the rest? Should I bow to a wooden block?” 📖

## Isaiah 44:20
He feeds on ashes. His deluded heart leads him astray. He cannot save himself or ask, “Is this thing in my right hand not a lie?” 📖

## Isaiah 44:21
Jacob, Israel, remember this. You are My servant. I made you. Israel, I will never forget you. 📖

## Isaiah 44:22
I erased your transgressions like a cloud and your sins like mist. Return to Me. I redeemed you. 📖

## Isaiah 44:23
Heavens, sing joyfully, because the LORD did this! Earth’s depths, shout! Mountains, forests, and all trees, sing! The LORD redeemed Jacob and revealed His glory in Israel. 📖

## Isaiah 44:24
The LORD, your Redeemer who formed you from the womb, says, “I made everything. I alone stretched the heavens and spread the earth. 📖

## Isaiah 44:25
“I defeat false prophets’ signs and make diviners foolish. I confuse the wise and turn their knowledge to nonsense. 📖

## Isaiah 44:26
“I confirm My servant’s message and fulfill My messengers’ counsel. I say Jerusalem will be inhabited. Judah’s cities will be rebuilt. I will restore their ruins. 📖

## Isaiah 44:27
“I tell the sea’s depths, ‘Be dry. I will dry your currents.’ 📖

## Isaiah 44:28
“I say of Cyrus, ‘My shepherd will fulfill all My desires.’ I say Jerusalem will be rebuilt and the temple’s foundation laid.” 📖
