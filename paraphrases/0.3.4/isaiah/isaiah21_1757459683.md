## Isaiah 21:1
This is the burden against the Desert by the Sea. An invader comes from a desert land of terror, like whirlwinds sweeping through the Negev. 📖

## Isaiah 21:2
A dreadful vision is announced to me. “The traitor still betrays, and the destroyer still destroys. Elam, advance! Media, lay siege! I will end all her groaning.” 📖

## Isaiah 21:3
Anguish fills my body. Pain grips me like a woman giving birth. What I hear bewilders me. What I see dismays me. 📖

## Isaiah 21:4
My heart falters, and fear makes me tremble. The twilight I wanted has become horror. 📖

## Isaiah 21:5
They prepare a table and spread a carpet. They eat and drink! Princes, rise and oil the shields! 📖

## Isaiah 21:6
The Lord told me, “Post a lookout. Have him report what he sees. 📖

## Isaiah 21:7
“When he sees chariots with teams of horsemen, donkey riders, and camel riders, he must be fully alert.” 📖

## Isaiah 21:8
The lookout shouted, “My lord, I stand on the watchtower every day. I stay at my post every night. 📖

## Isaiah 21:9
“Look! Riders come, horsemen in pairs.” One answered, “Babylon has fallen, fallen! All her gods’ images lie broken on the ground!” 📖

## Isaiah 21:10
My people, crushed on the threshing floor, I tell you what I heard from the LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God. 📖

## Isaiah 21:11
This is the burden against Dumah. Someone calls from Seir, “Watchman, how much of the night remains? Watchman, how much of the night remains?” 📖

## Isaiah 21:12
The watchman answers, “Morning has come, but so has night. If you want to ask, ask. Come back again.” 📖

## Isaiah 21:13
This is the burden against Arabia. Dedanite caravans, you must stay in Arabia’s thickets. 📖

## Isaiah 21:14
People of Tema, bring water to the thirsty. Meet refugees with food. 📖

## Isaiah 21:15
They flee the drawn sword, the bent bow, and the stress of battle. 📖

## Isaiah 21:16
The Lord told me, “Within one year, counted as a hired worker counts it, all Kedar’s glory will disappear. 📖

## Isaiah 21:17
“Few archers and warriors of Kedar will remain.” The LORD, Israel’s God, has spoken. 📖
