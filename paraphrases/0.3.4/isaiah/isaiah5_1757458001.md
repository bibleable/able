## Isaiah 5:1
I will sing my beloved a song about his vineyard. My beloved had a vineyard on a very fertile hill. 📖

## Isaiah 5:2
He dug the ground, removed stones, and planted the finest vines. He built a watchtower in the middle and dug a winepress. He expected good grapes. But the vineyard produced sour fruit! 📖

## Isaiah 5:3
“People of Jerusalem and men of Judah, judge between Me and My vineyard. 📖

## Isaiah 5:4
“What more could I have done for it? I expected sweet grapes. Why did it produce sour fruit? 📖

## Isaiah 5:5
“I will tell you what I will do to My vineyard. I will remove its hedge, and it will be consumed. I will tear down its wall, and it will be trampled. 📖

## Isaiah 5:6
“I will make it a wasteland. No one will prune or cultivate it. Thorns and briers will grow there. I will command the clouds not to rain on it.” 📖

## Isaiah 5:7
The house of Israel is the LORD of Hosts’ vineyard. Judah’s men are the plant He delights in. He sought justice but saw bloodshed. He sought righteousness but heard cries of distress. 📖

## Isaiah 5:8
How terrible for you who add house to house and field to field! You leave no room and live alone in the land. 📖

## Isaiah 5:9
I heard the LORD of Hosts declare, “Many houses will certainly become desolate. Great mansions will stand empty. 📖

## Isaiah 5:10
“Ten acres of vineyard will yield only one bath of wine. A homer of seed will yield only an ephah of grain.” 📖

## Isaiah 5:11
How terrible for those who rise early to pursue strong drink! They linger until evening, inflamed by wine. 📖

## Isaiah 5:12
Their feasts have lyres, harps, tambourines, flutes, and wine. They ignore the LORD’s actions. They do not see His hands’ work. 📖

## Isaiah 5:13
My people will go into exile because they lack understanding. Their dignitaries starve, and the crowds suffer great thirst. 📖

## Isaiah 5:14
Sheol widens its throat and opens its enormous jaws. Zion’s nobles and crowds go down, with her revelers and carousers! 📖

## Isaiah 5:15
People will be brought low and humbled. The proud will lower their eyes. 📖

## Isaiah 5:16
The LORD of Hosts will be exalted by His justice. The holy God will show His holiness through righteousness. 📖

## Isaiah 5:17
Lambs will graze as in their own pastures. Strangers will feed among the wealthy people’s ruins. 📖

## Isaiah 5:18
How terrible for those who drag wrongdoing with cords of deceit! They pull sin with cart ropes. 📖

## Isaiah 5:19
They say, “Let Him hurry His work so we can see it! Let Israel’s Holy One bring His plan so we can know it!” 📖

## Isaiah 5:20
How terrible for those who call evil good and good evil! They change darkness to light and light to darkness. They replace bitter with sweet and sweet with bitter. 📖

## Isaiah 5:21
How terrible for those who think themselves wise and clever! 📖

## Isaiah 5:22
How terrible for heroes at drinking wine and champions at mixing strong drink! 📖

## Isaiah 5:23
They accept bribes to acquit the guilty. They deny justice to the innocent. 📖

## Isaiah 5:24
Fire consumes straw, and dry grass shrivels in flames. In the same way, their roots will rot, and their blossoms will blow away like dust. They rejected the LORD of Hosts’ instruction. They despised the word of Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Isaiah 5:25
The LORD’s anger burns against His people. He raises His hand to strike them. Mountains shake, and corpses lie like rubbish in the streets. Even after this, His anger has not turned away. His hand remains raised. 📖

## Isaiah 5:26
He raises a banner for distant nations. He whistles for those at earth’s ends. They come quickly! 📖

## Isaiah 5:27
None grows tired or stumbles. None slumbers or sleeps. No belt is loose, and no sandal strap is broken. 📖

## Isaiah 5:28
Their arrows are sharp, and all their bows are strung. Their horses’ hooves are like flint. Their chariot wheels are like a whirlwind. 📖

## Isaiah 5:29
They roar like lions, like young lions. They growl and seize their prey. They carry it away, and no one can rescue it. 📖

## Isaiah 5:30
On that day, they will roar over it like the sea. Anyone looking over the land will see darkness and distress. Clouds will hide even the light. 📖
