## Isaiah 10:1
How terrible for those who make unjust laws and oppressive decrees! 📖

## Isaiah 10:2
They deny fair treatment to the poor and justice to My oppressed people. They make widows their prey and orphans their plunder. 📖

## Isaiah 10:3
What will you do on judgment day when destruction comes from far away? Where will you flee for help? Where will you leave your wealth? 📖

## Isaiah 10:4
You can only crouch among captives or fall among the dead. Despite this, His anger has not turned away. His hand remains raised. 📖

## Isaiah 10:5
How terrible for Assyria, the rod of My anger! The staff in their hands is My wrath. 📖

## Isaiah 10:6
I will send him against a godless nation, a people destined for My rage. He will seize plunder and trample them like clay in the streets. 📖

## Isaiah 10:7
But he has a different intention and plan. His heart seeks to destroy and cut off many nations. 📖

## Isaiah 10:8
He says, “Are all my commanders not kings? 📖

## Isaiah 10:9
“Is Calno not like Carchemish? Is Hamath not like Arpad? Is Samaria not like Damascus? 📖

## Isaiah 10:10
“My hand seized idol-worshiping kingdoms whose images surpassed those of Jerusalem and Samaria. 📖

## Isaiah 10:11
“Will I not do to Jerusalem and her idols what I did to Samaria and its idols?” 📖

## Isaiah 10:12
After the Lord finishes all His work against Mount Zion and Jerusalem, He will speak. “I will punish Assyria’s king for his arrogant heart’s fruit and proud eyes. 📖

## Isaiah 10:13
“He says, ‘My own strength and wisdom did this, because I am clever. I removed nations’ boundaries and plundered their treasures. Like a mighty one, I subdued their rulers. 📖

## Isaiah 10:14
“‘My hand took nations’ wealth as from a nest. I gathered the whole earth like abandoned eggs. No wing fluttered. No beak opened or chirped.’” 📖

## Isaiah 10:15
Does an axe rise above the one swinging it? Does a saw boast over the one using it? That is like a rod waving its holder. It is like a staff lifting someone who is not wood! 📖

## Isaiah 10:16
The Lord GOD of Hosts will send a wasting disease among Assyria’s strong warriors. A burning flame will be lit under his splendor. 📖

## Isaiah 10:17
The Light of Israel will become a fire. Its Holy One will become a flame. In one day, it will burn and devour Assyria’s thorns and thistles. 📖

## Isaiah 10:18
It will completely destroy the splendor of his forests and orchards, both soul and body. It will be like sickness consuming a man. 📖

## Isaiah 10:19
So few forest trees will remain that a child could count them. 📖

## Isaiah 10:20
On that day, Israel’s survivors and those remaining from Jacob’s house will stop depending on the one who struck them. They will truly rely on the LORD, Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Isaiah 10:21
A remnant of Jacob will return to the Mighty God. 📖

## Isaiah 10:22
Israel, your people may be as numerous as the sea’s sand. But only a remnant will return. Destruction overflowing with righteousness has been decreed. 📖

## Isaiah 10:23
The Lord GOD of Hosts will carry out the destruction decreed over the whole land. 📖

## Isaiah 10:24
The Lord GOD of Hosts says, “My people living in Zion, do not fear Assyria. He strikes you with a rod and raises his staff, as Egypt did. 📖

## Isaiah 10:25
“Soon My fury against you will end. My anger will turn to destroying them.” 📖

## Isaiah 10:26
The LORD of Hosts will raise a whip against them as when He struck Midian at Oreb’s rock. He will raise His staff over the sea as He did in Egypt. 📖

## Isaiah 10:27
On that day, the burden will leave your shoulders and the yoke will leave your neck. The yoke will break because your neck is too large. 📖

## Isaiah 10:28
Assyria entered Aiath and passed through Migron. They stored supplies at Michmash. 📖

## Isaiah 10:29
They crossed the ford. “We will spend the night at Geba.” Ramah trembles, and Saul’s Gibeah flees. 📖

## Isaiah 10:30
Daughter of Gallim, cry loudly! Laishah, listen! How wretched you are, Anathoth! 📖

## Isaiah 10:31
Madmenah flees. Gebim’s people seek shelter. 📖

## Isaiah 10:32
Today they will stop at Nob. They shake a fist at the Daughter of Zion’s mountain, Jerusalem’s hill. 📖

## Isaiah 10:33
The Lord GOD of Hosts will cut off branches with terrifying power. Tall, lofty trees will be cut down. 📖

## Isaiah 10:34
He will clear the forest thickets with an axe. Lebanon will fall before the Mighty One. 📖
