## Isaiah 25:1
LORD, You are my God! I will exalt You and praise Your name. In perfect faithfulness, You did wonders planned long ago. 📖

## Isaiah 25:2
You made the city rubble and the fortified town a ruin. The foreigners’ fortress is no longer a city. It will never be rebuilt. 📖

## Isaiah 25:3
A strong people will honor You. Cities of ruthless nations will revere You. 📖

## Isaiah 25:4
You sheltered the poor and protected the needy in distress. You sheltered them from storms and shaded them from heat. The ruthless people’s breath is like rain against a wall. 📖

## Isaiah 25:5
It is like heat in a dry land. You silence foreigners’ uproar. As cloud shade cools heat, the ruthless people’s song is silenced. 📖

## Isaiah 25:6
On this mountain, the LORD of Hosts will prepare a rich banquet for all peoples. It will have choice meat and finely aged wine. 📖

## Isaiah 25:7
On this mountain, He will swallow the shroud covering all peoples, the sheet covering all nations. 📖

## Isaiah 25:8
He will swallow death forever. The Lord GOD will wipe tears from every face. He will remove His people’s disgrace from the whole earth. The LORD has spoken. 📖

## Isaiah 25:9
On that day, people will say, “This is certainly our God! We waited for Him, and He saved us. This is the LORD we waited for. Let us rejoice in His salvation!” 📖

## Isaiah 25:10
The LORD’s hand will rest on this mountain. But Moab will be trampled in his place like straw in a dung pile. 📖

## Isaiah 25:11
He will spread his hands within it like a swimmer spreading his arms. His pride will be humbled despite his hands’ skill. 📖

## Isaiah 25:12
The fortress with high walls will be brought down. It will be thrown to the ground, into the dust. 📖
