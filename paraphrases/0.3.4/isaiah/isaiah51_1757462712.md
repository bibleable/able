## Isaiah 51:1
“You pursuing righteousness and seeking the LORD, listen. Look at the rock you were cut from and the quarry you were hewn from. 📖

## Isaiah 51:2
“Look at your father Abraham and Sarah who bore you. He was one when I called him. I blessed him and multiplied him. 📖

## Isaiah 51:3
“The LORD will comfort Zion and show compassion to all her ruins. He will make her wilderness like Eden and her desert like His garden. Joy, gladness, thanksgiving, and melodious songs will be there. 📖

## Isaiah 51:4
“My people and nation, listen. Law will come from Me. My justice will light the nations. I will bring this quickly. 📖

## Isaiah 51:5
“My righteousness is near. My salvation comes. My arms will bring nations justice. Islands will seek Me and hope for My arm. 📖

## Isaiah 51:6
“Look at heaven and earth. Heaven will vanish like smoke. Earth will wear out like clothes, and its people will die like gnats. My salvation lasts forever. My righteousness never fails. 📖

## Isaiah 51:7
“You who know right and carry My law in your hearts, listen. Do not fear people’s scorn or let their insults break you. 📖

## Isaiah 51:8
“Moths will devour them like clothes. Worms will eat them like wool. My righteousness lasts forever. My salvation continues through all generations.” 📖

## Isaiah 51:9
Awake, awake, arm of the LORD! Put on strength as in ancient generations. Did You not cut Rahab apart and pierce the dragon? 📖

## Isaiah 51:10
Did You not dry the sea, the great deep’s waters? Did You not make a road through its depths for the redeemed to cross? 📖

## Isaiah 51:11
The LORD’s redeemed will return to Zion singing, crowned with everlasting joy. Gladness and joy will overtake them. Sorrow and sighing will flee. 📖

## Isaiah 51:12
“I Myself comfort you. Why fear mortals, people withering like grass? 📖

## Isaiah 51:13
“You forgot the LORD, your Maker, who stretched heaven and founded earth. All day you fear the oppressor’s fury as he plans destruction. But where is that fury? 📖

## Isaiah 51:14
“The captive will soon be free. He will not die in the dungeon or lack bread. 📖

## Isaiah 51:15
“I am the LORD your God. I stir the sea, and its waves roar. His name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 51:16
“I put My words in your mouth and covered you in My hand’s shadow. This is to establish heaven, found earth, and tell Zion, ‘You are My people.’” 📖

## Isaiah 51:17
Awake, awake! Rise, Jerusalem! You drank the LORD’s cup of fury from His hand. You drained the staggering cup to its dregs. 📖

## Isaiah 51:18
None of the sons she bore and raised guides her. None takes her hand. 📖

## Isaiah 51:19
These pairs struck you: devastation and destruction, famine and sword. Who will grieve for you? Who can comfort you? 📖

## Isaiah 51:20
Your sons fainted. They lie at every street’s head like an antelope in a net. They are full of the LORD’s wrath, your God’s rebuke. 📖

## Isaiah 51:21
Afflicted one, drunk without wine, hear this now. 📖

## Isaiah 51:22
Your Lord, the LORD your God, defends His people. He says, “I removed the staggering cup, My fury’s goblet, from your hand. You will never drink it again. 📖

## Isaiah 51:23
“I will give it to your tormentors. They told you, ‘Lie down so we can walk over you.’ You made your back like ground, like a street to cross.” 📖
