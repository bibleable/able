## Isaiah 54:1
The LORD says, “Barren woman who never bore a child, sing joyfully! You who never labored, cry out! The desolate woman has more children than the woman with a husband. 📖

## Isaiah 54:2
“Enlarge your tent’s site and stretch its curtains. Do not hold back. Lengthen ropes and drive stakes deep. 📖

## Isaiah 54:3
“You will spread right and left. Your descendants will dispossess nations and inhabit desolate cities. 📖

## Isaiah 54:4
“Do not fear or be intimidated. You will not be shamed or humiliated. You will forget your youth’s shame and widowhood’s reproach. 📖

## Isaiah 54:5
“Your Maker is your husband. His name is the LORD of Hosts. Israel’s Holy One is your Redeemer. He is called God of all earth. 📖

## Isaiah 54:6
“The LORD called you back like a deserted wife wounded in spirit, like a rejected wife from youth,” says your God. 📖

## Isaiah 54:7
“I abandoned you briefly. With great compassion, I will bring you back. 📖

## Isaiah 54:8
“For a moment, I hid My face in surging anger. With everlasting kindness, I will have compassion on you,” says the LORD your Redeemer. 📖

## Isaiah 54:9
“This is like Noah’s days to Me. I swore Noah’s waters would never cover earth again. So I swear not to be angry with you or rebuke you. 📖

## Isaiah 54:10
“Mountains may move and hills shake. My loving devotion will not leave you. My covenant of peace will not break,” says the LORD, who has compassion on you. 📖

## Isaiah 54:11
“Afflicted city, storm-lashed and without comfort, I will set your stones in antimony. I will lay your foundations with sapphires. 📖

## Isaiah 54:12
“Your pinnacles will be rubies. Your gates will be sparkling jewels. All your walls will be precious stones. 📖

## Isaiah 54:13
“The LORD will teach all your sons. Their prosperity will be great. 📖

## Isaiah 54:14
“You will be established in righteousness, far from oppression and fear. Terror will stay far away. It will not approach you. 📖

## Isaiah 54:15
“If anyone attacks you, it is not from Me. Your attackers will fall before you. 📖

## Isaiah 54:16
“I created the craftsman fanning coals into flame and forging a weapon for its task. I also created the destroyer to cause havoc. 📖

## Isaiah 54:17
“No weapon made against you will prosper. You will refute every accusing tongue. This is My servants’ heritage. Their vindication comes from Me,” declares the LORD. 📖
