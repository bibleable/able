## Isaiah 38:1
Hezekiah became mortally ill in those days. The prophet Isaiah, Amoz’s son, came and said, “The LORD says, ‘Put your house in order. You will die and not recover.’” 📖

## Isaiah 38:2
Hezekiah faced the wall and prayed to the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 38:3
“LORD, remember my faithful walk before You and wholehearted devotion. I did what pleased You.” Hezekiah wept bitterly. 📖

## Isaiah 38:4
The LORD’s word came to Isaiah. 📖

## Isaiah 38:5
“Tell Hezekiah what the LORD, his father David’s God, says. ‘I heard your prayer and saw your tears. I will add fifteen years to your life. 📖

## Isaiah 38:6
“‘I will rescue you and this city from Assyria’s king. I will defend this city. 📖

## Isaiah 38:7
“‘The LORD will give you this sign that He will fulfill His promise. 📖

## Isaiah 38:8
“‘I will make the sun’s shadow on Ahaz’s stairway go back ten steps.’” The sunlight went back the ten steps it had descended. 📖

## Isaiah 38:9
Judah’s king Hezekiah wrote this after his illness and recovery. 📖

## Isaiah 38:10
I said, “In my life’s prime, I must enter Sheol’s gates. I will lose my remaining years.” 📖

## Isaiah 38:11
I said, “I will never see the LORD again in the land of the living. I will no longer see people among this world’s inhabitants. 📖

## Isaiah 38:12
“My dwelling was removed like a shepherd’s tent. I rolled up my life like a weaver. He cuts me from the loom. From day to night, You end me. 📖

## Isaiah 38:13
“I composed myself until morning. Like a lion, He breaks all my bones. From day to night, You end me. 📖

## Isaiah 38:14
“I chirp like a swallow or crane and moan like a dove. My eyes weaken as I look up. Lord, I am oppressed. Be my security.” 📖

## Isaiah 38:15
What can I say? He spoke to me and did this Himself. I will walk slowly all my years because of my soul’s anguish. 📖

## Isaiah 38:16
Lord, people live through such things. In all of them, my spirit finds life. You restored my health and let me live. 📖

## Isaiah 38:17
My great anguish was for my welfare. Your love saved me from oblivion’s pit. You threw all my sins behind Your back. 📖

## Isaiah 38:18
Sheol cannot thank You. Death cannot praise You. Those going to the Pit cannot hope for Your faithfulness. 📖

## Isaiah 38:19
Only the living can thank You, as I do today. Fathers will tell their children about Your faithfulness. 📖

## Isaiah 38:20
The LORD will save me. We will play songs on stringed instruments in His house throughout our lives. 📖

## Isaiah 38:21
Isaiah had said, “Make a lump of pressed figs and put it on the boil. He will recover.” 📖

## Isaiah 38:22
Hezekiah had asked, “What sign will show that I will go to the LORD’s house?” 📖
