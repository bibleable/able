## Isaiah 11:1
A shoot will grow from Jesse’s stump. A Branch from his roots will bear fruit. 📖

## Isaiah 11:2
The LORD’s Spirit will rest on Him. This is the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, counsel and strength, knowledge and fear of the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 11:3
He will delight in the fear of the LORD. He will not judge by what His eyes see or decide by what His ears hear. 📖

## Isaiah 11:4
He will judge the poor with righteousness and decide fairly for earth’s lowly people. He will strike the earth with His mouth’s rod. His lips’ breath will kill the wicked. 📖

## Isaiah 11:5
Righteousness will be His belt around His hips. Faithfulness will be His sash around His waist. 📖

## Isaiah 11:6
The wolf will live with the lamb. The leopard will lie with the goat. The calf, young lion, and fattened animal will be together. A little child will lead them. 📖

## Isaiah 11:7
The cow and bear will graze together. Their young will lie together. The lion will eat straw like an ox. 📖

## Isaiah 11:8
An infant will play beside a cobra’s den. A toddler will reach into a viper’s nest. 📖

## Isaiah 11:9
They will neither harm nor destroy anywhere on My holy mountain. Knowledge of the LORD will fill the earth as water fills the sea. 📖

## Isaiah 11:10
On that day, the Root of Jesse will stand as a banner for peoples. Nations will seek Him. His resting place will be glorious. 📖

## Isaiah 11:11
On that day, the Lord will extend His hand a second time to recover His people’s survivors. They will come from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the sea’s islands. 📖

## Isaiah 11:12
He will raise a banner for the nations. He will gather Israel’s exiles and Judah’s scattered people from earth’s four corners. 📖

## Isaiah 11:13
Ephraim’s jealousy will end, and Judah’s enemies will be cut off. Ephraim will stop envying Judah. Judah will stop harassing Ephraim. 📖

## Isaiah 11:14
They will swoop down on the Philistines’ slopes to the west. Together they will plunder the sons of the east. They will lay hands on Edom and Moab. The Ammonites will submit to them. 📖

## Isaiah 11:15
The LORD will devote the gulf of Egypt’s Sea to destruction. With a scorching wind, He will sweep His hand over the Euphrates. He will divide it into seven streams. People will cross with dry sandals. 📖

## Isaiah 11:16
A highway will serve His people’s survivors remaining from Assyria. It will be like Israel’s highway when they came out of Egypt. 📖
