## Isaiah 43:1
The LORD created you, Jacob, and formed you, Israel. Now He says, “Do not fear. I redeemed you and called you by name. You are Mine! 📖

## Isaiah 43:2
“I will be with you through waters. Rivers will not overwhelm you. Fire will not scorch you, and flames will not burn you when you walk through them. 📖

## Isaiah 43:3
“I am the LORD your God, Israel’s Holy One, your Savior. I give Egypt as your ransom, Cush and Seba in your place. 📖

## Isaiah 43:4
“You are precious and honored in My sight. I love you. So I will give people in exchange for you and nations for your life. 📖

## Isaiah 43:5
“Do not fear, because I am with you. I will bring your offspring from the east and gather you from the west. 📖

## Isaiah 43:6
“I will tell the north, ‘Give them up!’ I will tell the south, ‘Do not hold them back!’ Bring My sons from afar and My daughters from earth’s ends. 📖

## Isaiah 43:7
“Bring everyone called by My name, whom I formed and made for My glory.” 📖

## Isaiah 43:8
Bring out people with eyes who are blind and with ears who are deaf. 📖

## Isaiah 43:9
All nations and peoples gather. Who among them can announce this or explain former things? Let them bring witnesses to prove them right. Let others hear and say, “It is true.” 📖

## Isaiah 43:10
“You are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “and My chosen servant. Consider, believe Me, and understand that I am He. No god was formed before Me. None will come after Me. 📖

## Isaiah 43:11
“I alone am the LORD. There is no Savior except Me. 📖

## Isaiah 43:12
“I alone decreed, saved, and proclaimed. No foreign god among you did this. You are My witnesses that I am God,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 43:13
“From eternity, I am He. No one can rescue from My hand. Who can reverse My actions?” 📖

## Isaiah 43:14
The LORD, your Redeemer, Israel’s Holy One, says, “For your sake, I will send to Babylon. I will bring them all as fugitives, even the Chaldeans, aboard the ships they delight in. 📖

## Isaiah 43:15
“I am the LORD, your Holy One, Israel’s Creator, your King.” 📖

## Isaiah 43:16
The LORD makes a way in the sea and a path through surging waters. He says, 📖

## Isaiah 43:17
He brings out chariots, horses, armies, and warriors together. They lie down and never rise. They are put out like a wick. 📖

## Isaiah 43:18
“Do not recall former things or dwell on the past. 📖

## Isaiah 43:19
“I am doing something new. It comes even now. Do you not see it? I will make a way in the wilderness and streams in the desert. 📖

## Isaiah 43:20
“Field animals, jackals, and ostriches will honor Me. I provide wilderness water and desert rivers to give My chosen people drink. 📖

## Isaiah 43:21
“The people I formed for Myself will proclaim My praise. 📖

## Isaiah 43:22
“But Jacob, you did not call on Me. Israel, you grew tired of Me. 📖

## Isaiah 43:23
“You brought no sheep for My burnt offerings. You did not honor Me with sacrifices. I did not burden you with offerings or tire you with frankincense. 📖

## Isaiah 43:24
“You bought Me no sweet cane with silver. You did not satisfy Me with your sacrifices’ fat. But your sins burdened Me. Your wrongdoing tired Me. 📖

## Isaiah 43:25
“I Myself erase your transgressions for My own sake. I remember your sins no more. 📖

## Isaiah 43:26
“Remind Me. Let us argue together. Present your case so you can be proved right. 📖

## Isaiah 43:27
“Your first father sinned. Your spokesmen rebelled against Me. 📖

## Isaiah 43:28
“I will disgrace your sanctuary’s princes. I will devote Jacob to destruction and Israel to reproach.” 📖
