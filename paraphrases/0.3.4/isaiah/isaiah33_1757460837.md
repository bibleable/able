## Isaiah 33:1
How terrible for you, destroyer never destroyed, traitor never betrayed! When you finish destroying, you will be destroyed. When you finish betraying, you will be betrayed. 📖

## Isaiah 33:2
LORD, show us grace! We wait for You. Be our strength every morning and our salvation in trouble. 📖

## Isaiah 33:3
Peoples flee Your thundering voice. Nations scatter when You rise. 📖

## Isaiah 33:4
Nations, your plunder is gathered as by locusts. People sweep over it like a locust swarm. 📖

## Isaiah 33:5
The LORD is exalted and lives on high. He filled Zion with justice and righteousness. 📖

## Isaiah 33:6
He will be the sure foundation for your times, a storehouse of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge. Fear of the LORD is Zion’s treasure. 📖

## Isaiah 33:7
Their brave men cry in the streets. Peace envoys weep bitterly. 📖

## Isaiah 33:8
Highways are deserted, and travel has stopped. The treaty is broken. Witnesses are despised, and human life is disregarded. 📖

## Isaiah 33:9
The land mourns and weakens. Lebanon is shamed and decayed. Sharon is like a desert. Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves. 📖

## Isaiah 33:10
The LORD says, “Now I will rise and lift Myself up. Now I will be exalted. 📖

## Isaiah 33:11
“You conceive chaff and bear stubble. Your breath is a fire that will consume you. 📖

## Isaiah 33:12
“Peoples will burn to ashes like cut thorns set on fire. 📖

## Isaiah 33:13
“You far away, hear what I did. You nearby, acknowledge My might.” 📖

## Isaiah 33:14
Zion’s sinners fear, and the ungodly tremble. “Who among us can live with consuming fire? Who can live with everlasting flames?” 📖

## Isaiah 33:15
The one who walks righteously and speaks sincerely refuses profit from extortion and never takes bribes. He closes his ears to murderous plots and shuts his eyes against evil. 📖

## Isaiah 33:16
He will live on the heights. A mountain fortress will shelter him. His food will be supplied, and his water assured. 📖

## Isaiah 33:17
Your eyes will see the King in His beauty. You will see a land stretching far away. 📖

## Isaiah 33:18
Your mind will consider the former terror. “Where is the one tallying? Where is the one weighing? Where is the one counting towers?” 📖

## Isaiah 33:19
You will no longer see the insolent people. Their speech is unintelligible. They stammer in a language you cannot understand. 📖

## Isaiah 33:20
Look at Zion, the city of our appointed feasts. You will see Jerusalem, a peaceful pasture and a tent that never moves. Its pegs will not be pulled out. None of its ropes will break. 📖

## Isaiah 33:21
There the Majestic One, our LORD, will be a place of rivers and wide canals for us. No galley with oars will row there. No majestic vessel will pass. 📖

## Isaiah 33:22
The LORD is our Judge, lawgiver, and King. He will save us. 📖

## Isaiah 33:23
Your ropes hang loose. They cannot hold the mast or spread the sail. Then abundant plunder will be divided. Even the lame will carry it away. 📖

## Isaiah 33:24
No one in Zion will say, “I am sick.” Those living there will have their wrongdoing forgiven. 📖
