## Isaiah 9:1
Those in distress will have no more gloom. In the past, He humbled Zebulun’s land and Naphtali’s land. In the future, He will honor the Way of the Sea beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations. 📖

## Isaiah 9:2
People walking in darkness have seen a great light. Light has dawned on those living in the land of death’s shadow. 📖

## Isaiah 9:3
You enlarged the nation and increased its joy. People rejoice before You as at harvest or when dividing plunder. 📖

## Isaiah 9:4
As on Midian’s day, You shattered their burden’s yoke, the bar across their shoulders, and their oppressor’s rod. 📖

## Isaiah 9:5
Every trampling battle boot and garment rolled in blood will burn as fuel. 📖

## Isaiah 9:6
A child is born to us. A son is given to us. Government will rest on His shoulders. He will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace. 📖

## Isaiah 9:7
His government and peace will grow without end. He will reign on David’s throne over David’s kingdom. He will establish and sustain it with justice and righteousness forever from that time. The LORD of Hosts’ zeal will accomplish this. 📖

## Isaiah 9:8
The Lord sent a message against Jacob. It fell on Israel. 📖

## Isaiah 9:9
Everyone will know it, including Ephraim and Samaria’s people. With proud, arrogant hearts they say, 📖

## Isaiah 9:10
“Bricks have fallen, but we will rebuild with finished stone. Sycamores are cut down, but we will replace them with cedars.” 📖

## Isaiah 9:11
The LORD raised Rezin’s foes against him. He joined his enemies together. 📖

## Isaiah 9:12
Aram from the east and Philistia from the west devoured Israel with open mouths. Despite this, His anger has not turned away. His hand remains raised. 📖

## Isaiah 9:13
The people did not return to the One who struck them. They did not seek the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 9:14
In one day, the LORD will cut off Israel’s head and tail, palm branch and reed. 📖

## Isaiah 9:15
The elder and honorable man is the head. The prophet who teaches lies is the tail. 📖

## Isaiah 9:16
The people’s guides mislead them. Those they mislead are swallowed up. 📖

## Isaiah 9:17
The Lord takes no pleasure in their young men. He has no compassion for their fatherless and widows. All are godless and wicked, and every mouth speaks foolishness. Despite this, His anger has not turned away. His hand remains raised. 📖

## Isaiah 9:18
Wickedness burns like fire, consuming thorns and briers. It sets the forest thickets on fire. They rise in clouds of smoke. 📖

## Isaiah 9:19
The LORD of Hosts’ wrath scorches the land. The people fuel the fire. No one spares even his brother. 📖

## Isaiah 9:20
They cut what is on the right but remain hungry. They eat what is on the left but remain unsatisfied. Each devours his own offspring’s flesh. 📖

## Isaiah 9:21
Manasseh devours Ephraim, and Ephraim devours Manasseh. Together they turn against Judah. Despite this, His anger has not turned away. His hand remains raised. 📖
