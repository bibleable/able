## Isaiah 4:1
On that day, seven women will hold one man. They will say, “We will provide our own bread and clothes. Only let us bear your name. Remove our disgrace!” 📖

## Isaiah 4:2
On that day, the LORD’s Branch will be beautiful and glorious. The land’s fruit will be the pride and glory of Israel’s survivors. 📖

## Isaiah 4:3
Everyone remaining in Zion and Jerusalem will be called holy. This includes all in Jerusalem recorded among the living. 📖

## Isaiah 4:4
This will happen when the Lord washes away Zion’s daughters’ filth. He will cleanse bloodstains from Jerusalem’s heart by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of fire. 📖

## Isaiah 4:5
The LORD will create a cloud of smoke by day over all Mount Zion and its assemblies. At night, He will create a glowing flame of fire. A canopy will cover all the glory. 📖

## Isaiah 4:6
It will give shade from daytime heat. It will provide shelter and a hiding place from storms and rain. 📖
