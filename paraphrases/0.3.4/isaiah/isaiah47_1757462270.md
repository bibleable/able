## Isaiah 47:1
“Virgin Daughter of Babylon, sit in the dust. Daughter of the Chaldeans, sit on the ground without a throne! No one will call you tender or delicate again. 📖

## Isaiah 47:2
“Take millstones and grind flour. Remove your veil and skirt. Bare your thigh and cross the streams. 📖

## Isaiah 47:3
“Your nakedness and shame will be exposed. I will take vengeance and spare no one.” 📖

## Isaiah 47:4
Our Redeemer is Israel’s Holy One. His name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 47:5
“Daughter of the Chaldeans, sit silently in darkness. No one will call you queen of kingdoms again. 📖

## Isaiah 47:6
“I was angry with My people. I profaned My heritage and put them under your control. You showed no mercy. You put a heavy yoke even on the elderly. 📖

## Isaiah 47:7
“You said, ‘I will be queen forever.’ You did not consider these things or their outcome. 📖

## Isaiah 47:8
“Luxury lover living securely, hear this. You tell yourself, ‘I am, and there is no one besides me. I will never be widowed or lose children.’ 📖

## Isaiah 47:9
“Both losses will come suddenly, in one day: children and husband. They will come fully despite your many sorceries and powerful spells. 📖

## Isaiah 47:10
“You felt secure in wickedness and said, ‘No one sees me.’ Your wisdom and knowledge led you astray. You told yourself, ‘I am, and there is no one besides me.’ 📖

## Isaiah 47:11
“Disaster will come that you cannot charm away. You cannot prevent the calamity. Destruction will come suddenly and unexpectedly. 📖

## Isaiah 47:12
“Stand with your spells and many sorceries. They have exhausted you since youth. Perhaps you will succeed. Perhaps you will inspire terror! 📖

## Isaiah 47:13
“Your many counselors tire you. Let your astrologers come and save you! They watch stars and predict your fate each month. 📖

## Isaiah 47:14
“They are stubble that fire consumes. They cannot save themselves from the flames. No coals will warm them. There will be no fire to sit beside. 📖

## Isaiah 47:15
“Those you worked and traded with since youth are like this. Each wanders his own way. None can save you. 📖
