## Isaiah 62:1
For Zion and Jerusalem, I will not stay silent or still. I will continue until her righteousness shines brightly and her salvation burns like a torch. 📖

## Isaiah 62:2
Nations will see your righteousness, and all kings your glory. The LORD’s mouth will give you a new name. 📖

## Isaiah 62:3
You will be a glorious crown in the LORD’s hand, a royal diadem in your God’s palm. 📖

## Isaiah 62:4
You will no longer be called Forsaken or your land Desolate. You will be Hephzibah, and your land Beulah. The LORD will delight in you. Your land will be His bride. 📖

## Isaiah 62:5
As a young man marries a young woman, your sons will marry you. As a bridegroom rejoices over his bride, your God will rejoice over you. 📖

## Isaiah 62:6
Jerusalem, I posted watchmen on your walls. They will never be silent, day or night. You calling on the LORD, take no rest. 📖

## Isaiah 62:7
Give Him no rest until He establishes Jerusalem and makes her earth’s praise. 📖

## Isaiah 62:8
The LORD swore by His right hand and mighty arm. “I will never give your grain to enemies for food again. Foreigners will not drink the new wine you worked for. 📖

## Isaiah 62:9
“Grain harvesters will eat it and praise the LORD. Grape gatherers will drink wine in My holy courts.” 📖

## Isaiah 62:10
Go out, go out through the gates! Prepare the people’s way. Build the highway, build it! Clear stones and raise a banner for nations. 📖

## Isaiah 62:11
The LORD announced to earth’s ends, “Tell Daughter Zion, ‘Your Savior comes! His reward is with Him. His repayment goes before Him.’” 📖

## Isaiah 62:12
They will be called the Holy People, the Redeemed of the LORD. You will be called Sought Out, A City Not Forsaken. 📖
