## Isaiah 14:1
The LORD will have compassion on Jacob. He will choose Israel again and settle them in their own land. Foreigners will join Jacob’s house and unite with them. 📖

## Isaiah 14:2
Nations will escort Israel to its homeland. Israel’s house will possess the nations as male and female servants in the LORD’s land. They will capture their captors and rule their oppressors. 📖

## Isaiah 14:3
The LORD will give you rest from pain, torment, and forced hard labor. 📖

## Isaiah 14:4
On that day, you will sing this song of contempt against Babylon’s king. The oppressor has stopped! His fury has ended! 📖

## Isaiah 14:5
The LORD broke the wicked people’s staff, the rulers’ scepter. 📖

## Isaiah 14:6
It struck peoples angrily with endless blows. It subdued nations in rage with relentless persecution. 📖

## Isaiah 14:7
The whole earth rests in peace. They begin to sing. 📖

## Isaiah 14:8
Even Lebanon’s cypresses and cedars rejoice over you. “Since you fell, no woodcutter comes against us.” 📖

## Isaiah 14:9
Sheol below eagerly waits to meet you. It stirs the dead spirits, all earth’s rulers, to greet you. It makes all the nations’ kings rise from their thrones. 📖

## Isaiah 14:10
They will all answer, “You too became weak like us! You have become like us!” 📖

## Isaiah 14:11
Your splendor and harp music have gone down to Sheol. Maggots are your bed, and worms are your blanket. 📖

## Isaiah 14:12
Day star, son of dawn, you have fallen from heaven! Destroyer of nations, you have been cut to the ground! 📖

## Isaiah 14:13
You said in your heart, “I will climb to heaven and raise my throne above God’s stars. I will sit on the assembly mountain in the far north. 📖

## Isaiah 14:14
“I will rise above the cloud tops. I will make myself like the Most High.” 📖

## Isaiah 14:15
But you will go down to Sheol, to the Pit’s lowest depths. 📖

## Isaiah 14:16
Those who see you will stare and consider your fate. “Is this the man who shook the earth and made kingdoms tremble? 📖

## Isaiah 14:17
“Did he turn the world into a desert and destroy its cities? Did he refuse to let captives return home?” 📖

## Isaiah 14:18
All the nations’ kings lie in state, each in his own tomb. 📖

## Isaiah 14:19
But you are thrown from your grave like a rejected branch. Those killed by the sword cover you. You are dumped into a rocky pit like a trampled corpse. 📖

## Isaiah 14:20
You will not join them in burial. You destroyed your land and slaughtered your people. No one will mention the wicked people’s offspring again. 📖

## Isaiah 14:21
Prepare a place to slaughter his sons for their forefathers’ wrongdoing. They will never rise to possess land or cover the earth with cities. 📖

## Isaiah 14:22
“I will rise against them,” declares the LORD of Hosts. “I will cut off Babylon’s name, survivors, offspring, and future generations,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 14:23
“I will make her a place for owls and swamps. I will sweep her away with destruction’s broom,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 14:24
The LORD of Hosts swore, “What I plan will certainly happen. What I purpose will stand. 📖

## Isaiah 14:25
“I will break Assyria in My land and trample him on My mountain. His yoke will leave My people, and his burden will leave their shoulders.” 📖

## Isaiah 14:26
This plan is for the whole earth. This hand stretches over all nations. 📖

## Isaiah 14:27
The LORD of Hosts has planned it. Who can stop Him? His hand is stretched out. Who can turn it back? 📖

## Isaiah 14:28
This burden was received in the year King Ahaz died. 📖

## Isaiah 14:29
Philistines, do not rejoice because the rod that struck you is broken. A viper will come from the snake’s root. A flying serpent will come from its egg. 📖

## Isaiah 14:30
The poor people’s firstborn will find pasture. The needy will rest safely. But I will kill your root with famine. Your survivors will be killed. 📖

## Isaiah 14:31
Gate, wail! City, cry! All Philistines, melt away! Smoke comes from the north, and no one lags behind in its ranks. 📖

## Isaiah 14:32
What answer will the nation’s envoys receive? “The LORD founded Zion. His afflicted people will find shelter there.” 📖
