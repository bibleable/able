## Isaiah 42:1
“Here is My Servant, whom I uphold. He is My Chosen One, My soul’s delight. I will put My Spirit on Him. He will bring justice to the nations. 📖

## Isaiah 42:2
“He will not shout, raise His voice, or make it heard in the streets. 📖

## Isaiah 42:3
“He will not break a bruised reed or put out a smoldering wick. He will bring justice faithfully. 📖

## Isaiah 42:4
“He will not weaken or lose heart before establishing justice on earth. Islands will hope in His law.” 📖

## Isaiah 42:5
God the LORD created and stretched the heavens. He spread the earth and its offspring. He gives breath and life to those living and walking on it. He says, 📖

## Isaiah 42:6
“I, the LORD, called you for a righteous purpose. I will hold your hand and keep you. I will appoint you as a covenant for the people and a light for nations. 📖

## Isaiah 42:7
“You will open blind eyes. You will release prisoners from dungeons and free those sitting in prison darkness. 📖

## Isaiah 42:8
“I am the LORD. That is My name! I will not give My glory to another or My praise to idols. 📖

## Isaiah 42:9
“Former things happened. Now I announce new things. I tell you before they happen.” 📖

## Isaiah 42:10
Sing the LORD a new song! Praise Him from earth’s ends, you going to sea and everything in it. Islands and their people, praise Him! 📖

## Isaiah 42:11
Let deserts, cities, and Kedar’s villages cry out. Let Sela’s people sing joyfully from mountaintops. 📖

## Isaiah 42:12
Let them glorify the LORD. Let them proclaim His praise in the islands. 📖

## Isaiah 42:13
The LORD advances like a mighty one. He stirs His zeal like a warrior. He shouts and roars in triumph over His enemies. 📖

## Isaiah 42:14
“I have been silent, quiet, and restrained for ages. Now I will groan like a woman giving birth. I will gasp and pant together. 📖

## Isaiah 42:15
“I will ruin mountains and hills and dry all their plants. I will turn rivers into dry land and drain marshes. 📖

## Isaiah 42:16
“I will lead the blind on unknown ways and unfamiliar paths. I will turn darkness to light and rough ground level before them. I will do these things and not abandon them. 📖

## Isaiah 42:17
“Those trusting idols and telling cast images, ‘You are our gods,’ will turn back in complete shame. 📖

## Isaiah 42:18
“Deaf ones, listen! Blind ones, look and see! 📖

## Isaiah 42:19
“Who is blind like My servant or deaf like My messenger whom I am sending? Who is blind like My covenant partner, like the LORD’s servant? 📖

## Isaiah 42:20
“You see much but do not watch. Your ears are open, but you do not hear.” 📖

## Isaiah 42:21
For His righteousness’ sake, the LORD was pleased to magnify His law and make it glorious. 📖

## Isaiah 42:22
These people are plundered and looted, all trapped in caves or imprisoned in dungeons. No one rescues them from being plunder. No one says, “Send them back!” 📖

## Isaiah 42:23
Who will pay attention? Who will listen and obey from now on? 📖

## Isaiah 42:24
Who handed Jacob to plunderers? Was it not the LORD, against whom we sinned? They refused His ways and disobeyed His law. 📖

## Isaiah 42:25
He poured furious anger and fierce battle on them. Flames surrounded them, but they did not understand. It consumed them, but they did not take it to heart. 📖
