## Isaiah 57:1
The righteous perish, and no one takes it to heart. Devout people are taken away. No one considers that the righteous are removed from evil’s presence. 📖

## Isaiah 57:2
The upright enter peace. They lie in death and rest. 📖

## Isaiah 57:3
“Come here, sons of a sorceress, offspring of adulterers and prostitutes! 📖

## Isaiah 57:4
“Whom do you mock? At whom do you sneer and stick your tongue out? Are you not children of transgression and offspring of deceit? 📖

## Isaiah 57:5
“You burn with lust among oaks, under every lush tree. You slaughter your children in valleys beneath rock crevices. 📖

## Isaiah 57:6
“Your portion and lot are among the valley’s smooth stones. You gave them drink and grain offerings. Should this make Me relent? 📖

## Isaiah 57:7
“You made your bed on a high hill. You climbed there to sacrifice. 📖

## Isaiah 57:8
“You put your memorial behind the door and doorpost. You abandoned Me and uncovered your bed. You climbed up and spread it wide. You made a pact with those whose bed you loved. You gazed at their nakedness. 📖

## Isaiah 57:9
“You went to Molech with oil and many perfumes. You sent envoys far away. You descended to Sheol itself. 📖

## Isaiah 57:10
“Your many journeys tired you, but you never said, ‘There is no hope!’ Your strength renewed, so you did not weaken. 📖

## Isaiah 57:11
“Whom did you fear so much that you lied and forgot Me? Why did you not take this to heart? Is My long silence why you do not fear Me? 📖

## Isaiah 57:12
“I will expose your righteousness and works. They will not benefit you. 📖

## Isaiah 57:13
“When you cry, let your idol collections rescue you! Wind will carry them all away. A breath will take them. But whoever shelters in Me will inherit the land and possess My holy mountain.” 📖

## Isaiah 57:14
It will be said, “Build up, build up! Prepare the way. Remove every obstacle from My people’s path.” 📖

## Isaiah 57:15
The high, exalted One inhabits eternity. His name is Holy. He says, “I live in a high, holy place and with the oppressed and humble in spirit. I restore the lowly spirit and revive the contrite heart. 📖

## Isaiah 57:16
“I will not accuse or stay angry forever. Otherwise, the human spirit, the life breath I made, would weaken before Me. 📖

## Isaiah 57:17
“His sinful greed enraged Me. I struck him and hid My face in anger. But he kept turning to his heart’s desires. 📖

## Isaiah 57:18
“I saw his ways, but I will heal him. I will guide him and restore comfort to him and his mourners. 📖

## Isaiah 57:19
“I will bring praise to their lips. Peace, peace to those far and near,” says the LORD. “I will heal them.” 📖

## Isaiah 57:20
The wicked are like a stormy sea that cannot rest. Its waves churn mud and muck. 📖

## Isaiah 57:21
My God says, “There is no peace for the wicked.” 📖
