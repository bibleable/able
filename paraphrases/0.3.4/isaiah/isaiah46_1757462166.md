## Isaiah 46:1
Bel crouches, and Nebo cowers. Their idols weigh down beasts and cattle. The images you carry burden weary animals. 📖

## Isaiah 46:2
The gods cower and crouch together. They cannot relieve the burden. They themselves go into captivity. 📖

## Isaiah 46:3
“Jacob’s house, all Israel’s survivors, listen to Me. You have been sustained from the womb and carried since birth. 📖

## Isaiah 46:4
“I will stay the same until your old age. I will support you when you turn gray. I made you and will carry you. I will sustain and rescue you. 📖

## Isaiah 46:5
“Whom will you compare to Me or call My equal? Who resembles Me? 📖

## Isaiah 46:6
“They pour gold from bags and weigh silver on scales. They hire a goldsmith to make a god. Then they bow and worship. 📖

## Isaiah 46:7
“They carry it on their shoulders and set it in place. It stands there without moving. They cry to it, but it never answers. It saves no one from trouble. 📖

## Isaiah 46:8
“Transgressors, remember this and be brave. Take it to heart! 📖

## Isaiah 46:9
“Remember long ago. I am God, and there is no other. No one is like Me. 📖

## Isaiah 46:10
“I announce the end from the beginning. From ancient times, I announce what is coming. My purpose will stand. I will accomplish all My pleasure. 📖

## Isaiah 46:11
“I call a bird of prey from the east, a man for My purpose from a distant land. I spoke and will fulfill it. I planned and will certainly do it. 📖

## Isaiah 46:12
“Stubborn people far from righteousness, listen! 📖

## Isaiah 46:13
“I bring My righteousness near. My salvation will not delay. I will give Zion salvation and adorn Israel with My splendor. 📖
