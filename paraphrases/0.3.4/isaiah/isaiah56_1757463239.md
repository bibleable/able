## Isaiah 56:1
The LORD says, “Keep justice and do right. My salvation is near. My righteousness will be revealed. 📖

## Isaiah 56:2
“Blessed is the person who holds firmly to this. He keeps the Sabbath holy and keeps his hand from all evil.” 📖

## Isaiah 56:3
No foreigner joined to the LORD should say, “The LORD will completely exclude me from His people.” No eunuch should say, “I am only a dry tree.” 📖

## Isaiah 56:4
The LORD says, “Eunuchs who keep My Sabbaths, choose what pleases Me, and hold My covenant will receive this. 📖

## Isaiah 56:5
“Within My house and walls, I will give them a memorial and name better than sons and daughters. I will give them an everlasting name that cannot be cut off. 📖

## Isaiah 56:6
“Foreigners join the LORD to serve Him, love His name, and be His servants. Those foreigners keeping the Sabbath holy and holding firmly to My covenant will receive this. 📖

## Isaiah 56:7
“I will bring them to My holy mountain and make them joyful in My house of prayer. I will accept their burnt offerings and sacrifices on My altar. My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations.” 📖

## Isaiah 56:8
The Lord GOD gathers Israel’s scattered people. He declares, “I will gather others to them beyond those already gathered.” 📖

## Isaiah 56:9
Field animals, come! Forest animals, eat greedily! 📖

## Isaiah 56:10
Israel’s watchmen are blind and unaware. They are mute dogs unable to bark. They lie dreaming and love sleep. 📖

## Isaiah 56:11
They are like hungry dogs, never satisfied. They are shepherds without discernment. Each follows his own way and seeks his own profit. 📖

## Isaiah 56:12
“Come, I will get wine! Let us drink strong drink. Tomorrow will be like today, only much better!” 📖
