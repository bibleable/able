## Isaiah 12:1
On that day, you will say, “LORD, I will praise You. You were angry with me. But Your anger turned away, and You comforted me. 📖

## Isaiah 12:2
“God is certainly my salvation. I will trust and not fear. The LORD GOD is my strength and song. He has become my salvation.” 📖

## Isaiah 12:3
You will draw water joyfully from salvation’s springs. 📖

## Isaiah 12:4
On that day, you will say, “Praise the LORD! Proclaim His name. Tell the peoples what He has done. Announce that His name is exalted. 📖

## Isaiah 12:5
“Sing to the LORD, because He has done glorious things. Tell the whole earth about this. 📖

## Isaiah 12:6
“Citizen of Zion, shout and sing! Israel’s Holy One is great among you.” 📖
