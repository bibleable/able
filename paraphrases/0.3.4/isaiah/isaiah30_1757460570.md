## Isaiah 30:1
“How terrible for rebellious children!” declares the LORD. “They carry out plans that are not Mine and make alliances against My will. They pile sin on sin. 📖

## Isaiah 30:2
“They go to Egypt without asking My advice. They seek Pharaoh’s protection and shelter in Egypt’s shade. 📖

## Isaiah 30:3
“But Pharaoh’s protection will bring shame. Egypt’s shade will bring disgrace. 📖

## Isaiah 30:4
“Their princes are at Zoan, and their envoys reached Hanes. 📖

## Isaiah 30:5
“Yet everyone will be ashamed because these people are useless to them. They bring no help or benefit, only shame and disgrace.” 📖

## Isaiah 30:6
This is the burden against the Negev’s beasts. They cross a land of hardship and distress, of lionesses, lions, vipers, and flying serpents. They carry wealth on donkeys’ backs and treasure on camels’ humps. They take it to people who cannot help them. 📖

## Isaiah 30:7
Egypt’s help is useless and empty. I have called her Rahab Who Sits Still. 📖

## Isaiah 30:8
Write it on a tablet before them and on a scroll. It will be a witness for future days, forever. 📖

## Isaiah 30:9
They are rebellious people, deceitful children. They refuse the LORD’s instruction. 📖

## Isaiah 30:10
They tell seers, “Stop seeing visions!” They tell prophets, “Do not tell us truth! Speak pleasant words. Prophesy illusions. 📖

## Isaiah 30:11
“Get out of the way and leave the road. Rid us of Israel’s Holy One!” 📖

## Isaiah 30:12
Israel’s Holy One says, “You rejected this message. You trust oppression and rely on deceit. 📖

## Isaiah 30:13
“So your wrongdoing is like a break about to fail, a bulge in a high wall. It will collapse suddenly, in an instant! 📖

## Isaiah 30:14
“It will shatter like a potter’s jar, leaving no fragment to find. No piece in the dust will be big enough to scoop coals from a hearth or skim water from a cistern.” 📖

## Isaiah 30:15
The Lord GOD, Israel’s Holy One, said, “Repentance and rest would save you. Quiet confidence would be your strength. But you refused.” 📖

## Isaiah 30:16
You say, “No, we will flee on horses.” So you will flee! You say, “We will ride swift horses.” But your pursuers will be faster. 📖

## Isaiah 30:17
A thousand will flee when one threatens. At five people’s threat, you will all flee. You will be left alone like a pole on a mountaintop, like a banner on a hill. 📖

## Isaiah 30:18
The LORD longs to show you grace. He rises to show compassion because He is a just God. Blessed are all who wait for Him. 📖

## Isaiah 30:19
People in Zion, living in Jerusalem, you will weep no more. He will certainly show grace when you cry for help. He will hear and answer. 📖

## Isaiah 30:20
The Lord will give you adversity’s bread and affliction’s water. But your Teacher will no longer hide Himself. Your own eyes will see Him. 📖

## Isaiah 30:21
Whether you turn right or left, you will hear a command behind you. “This is the way. Walk in it.” 📖

## Isaiah 30:22
You will desecrate your silver-plated idols and gold-plated images. You will throw them away like menstrual cloths. You will tell them, “Be gone!” 📖

## Isaiah 30:23
He will send rain for your planted seed. Your land’s food will be rich and abundant. On that day, your cattle will graze in open pastures. 📖

## Isaiah 30:24
Oxen and donkeys working the ground will eat salted fodder, winnowed with shovel and pitchfork. 📖

## Isaiah 30:25
Water will flow from every high mountain and hill. This will happen on the day of great slaughter, when towers fall. 📖

## Isaiah 30:26
The moon will shine as brightly as the sun. The sun will shine seven times brighter, like seven days’ light. On that day, the LORD will bind His people’s brokenness and heal the wounds He inflicted. 📖

## Isaiah 30:27
The LORD’s Name comes from afar with burning anger and thick smoke. His lips are full of fury. His tongue is like consuming fire. 📖

## Isaiah 30:28
His breath is like a rushing flood reaching the neck. He sifts nations in destruction’s sieve. He puts bridles in peoples’ jaws to lead them astray. 📖

## Isaiah 30:29
You will sing as on a holy festival’s night. Your heart will rejoice like someone walking to flute music. He goes to the LORD’s mountain, to Israel’s Rock. 📖

## Isaiah 30:30
The LORD will make His majestic voice heard and reveal His mighty arm. He will strike in angry wrath with consuming fire, cloudbursts, storms, and hailstones. 📖

## Isaiah 30:31
The LORD’s voice will shatter Assyria. He will strike them with His scepter. 📖

## Isaiah 30:32
Tambourines and lyres will sound at every stroke of the LORD’s rod of punishment. He battles against them with weapons raised. 📖

## Isaiah 30:33
Topheth was prepared long ago for the king. Its funeral pyre is deep and wide, with much fire and wood. The LORD’s breath lights it like a torrent of burning sulfur. 📖
