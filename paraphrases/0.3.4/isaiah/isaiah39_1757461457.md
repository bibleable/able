## Isaiah 39:1
Merodach-baladan, Baladan’s son, king of Babylon, heard about Hezekiah’s illness and recovery. At that time, he sent Hezekiah letters and a gift. 📖

## Isaiah 39:2
Hezekiah gladly welcomed the envoys and showed his treasure house. He showed silver, gold, spices, precious oil, his whole armory, and everything in his storehouses. He hid nothing in his palace or entire kingdom from them. 📖

## Isaiah 39:3
The prophet Isaiah asked King Hezekiah, “Where did these men come from? What did they say?” Hezekiah answered, “They came from far away, from Babylon.” 📖

## Isaiah 39:4
Isaiah asked, “What did they see in your palace?” Hezekiah answered, “Everything. I showed them all my treasures.” 📖

## Isaiah 39:5
Isaiah told Hezekiah, “Hear the LORD of Hosts’ word. 📖

## Isaiah 39:6
“A time will certainly come when everything in your palace goes to Babylon. Everything your fathers stored until today will go too. Nothing will remain,” says the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 39:7
“Some of your own descendants, your flesh and blood, will be taken as eunuchs in Babylon’s royal palace.” 📖

## Isaiah 39:8
Hezekiah told Isaiah, “The LORD’s word you spoke is good.” He thought, “At least my lifetime will have peace and security.” 📖
