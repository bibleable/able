## Isaiah 26:1
On that day, this song will be sung in Judah. We have a strong city. Salvation forms its walls and ramparts. 📖

## Isaiah 26:2
Open the gates. Let a righteous, faithful nation enter. 📖

## Isaiah 26:3
You will keep the person with a steadfast mind in perfect peace. He trusts You. 📖

## Isaiah 26:4
Trust the LORD forever. GOD the LORD is the eternal Rock. 📖

## Isaiah 26:5
He humbled those living high above. He brought the lofty city to the ground, into the dust. 📖

## Isaiah 26:6
The oppressed and poor trample it with their feet. 📖

## Isaiah 26:7
The righteous people’s path is level. You clear a straight path for the upright. 📖

## Isaiah 26:8
LORD, we wait for You and follow Your judgments’ path. Our souls desire Your name and renown. 📖

## Isaiah 26:9
My soul longs for You at night. My spirit seeks You at dawn. When Your judgments come on earth, the world’s people learn righteousness. 📖

## Isaiah 26:10
Even when the wicked receive grace, they do not learn righteousness. They act unjustly in the land of righteousness. They do not see the LORD’s majesty. 📖

## Isaiah 26:11
LORD, Your hand is raised, but they do not see it. They will see Your zeal for Your people and be shamed. The fire for Your enemies will consume them! 📖

## Isaiah 26:12
LORD, You will establish peace for us. You have done for us everything we accomplished. 📖

## Isaiah 26:13
LORD our God, other lords ruled us. But we confess only Your name. 📖

## Isaiah 26:14
The dead will not live. Departed spirits will not rise. You punished and destroyed them. You erased all memory of them. 📖

## Isaiah 26:15
LORD, You enlarged the nation. You enlarged it and gained glory for Yourself. You extended all the land’s borders. 📖

## Isaiah 26:16
LORD, they sought You in distress. When You disciplined them, they poured out a quiet prayer. 📖

## Isaiah 26:17
A woman about to give birth twists and cries in pain. LORD, we were like that before You. 📖

## Isaiah 26:18
We were pregnant and twisted in pain. But we gave birth to wind. We brought neither salvation to the earth nor life to the world. 📖

## Isaiah 26:19
Your dead will live. Their bodies will rise. You living in dust, wake and sing! Your dew is like morning dew. The earth will bring forth her dead. 📖

## Isaiah 26:20
My people, enter your rooms and close the doors behind you. Hide briefly until the wrath passes. 📖

## Isaiah 26:21
The LORD comes from His dwelling to punish earth’s people for their wrongdoing. The earth will reveal her bloodshed. She will no longer hide her slain. 📖
