## Isaiah 27:1
On that day, the LORD will take His sharp, great, mighty sword. He will judge Leviathan, the fleeing, coiling serpent. He will kill the sea’s dragon. 📖

## Isaiah 27:2
On that day, “Sing about a fruitful vineyard. 📖

## Isaiah 27:3
“I, the LORD, keep it and water it continually. I guard it day and night so no one disturbs it. 📖

## Isaiah 27:4
“I am not angry. If thorns and briers confronted Me, I would march against and trample them. I would burn them to the ground. 📖

## Isaiah 27:5
“Or let them claim My protection. Let them make peace with Me. Yes, let them make peace with Me.” 📖

## Isaiah 27:6
In the days ahead, Jacob will take root. Israel will bud and blossom. It will fill the whole world with fruit. 📖

## Isaiah 27:7
Did the LORD strike Israel as He struck her oppressors? Was she killed like those who killed her? 📖

## Isaiah 27:8
You contended with her through war and exile. You removed her with a fierce wind, like a day when the east wind blows. 📖

## Isaiah 27:9
Jacob’s guilt will be atoned for. This will be the full fruit of removing his sin. When he makes all altar stones like crushed chalk, no Asherah poles or incense altars will remain standing. 📖

## Isaiah 27:10
The fortified city lies deserted, an abandoned homestead and forsaken wilderness. Calves graze and lie there. They strip its branches bare. 📖

## Isaiah 27:11
When its branches dry, they break off. Women gather them for kindling. These people lack understanding. Their Maker has no compassion on them. Their Creator shows them no favor. 📖

## Isaiah 27:12
On that day, the LORD will thresh from the flowing Euphrates to the Wadi of Egypt. Israelites, you will be gathered one by one. 📖

## Isaiah 27:13
On that day, a great ram’s horn will sound. Those perishing in Assyria will come with Egypt’s exiles. They will worship the LORD on Jerusalem’s holy mountain. 📖
