## Isaiah 6:1
In the year King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord on a high, exalted throne. His robe’s train filled the temple. 📖

## Isaiah 6:2
Seraphim stood above Him. Each had six wings. Two covered their faces, two covered their feet, and two carried them in flight. 📖

## Isaiah 6:3
They called to one another, “Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Hosts! The whole earth is full of His glory.” 📖

## Isaiah 6:4
Their voices shook the doorposts and thresholds. Smoke filled the temple. 📖

## Isaiah 6:5
I said, “How terrible for me! I am ruined. My lips are unclean, and I live among people with unclean lips. My eyes have seen the King, the LORD of Hosts!” 📖

## Isaiah 6:6
One seraph flew to me. He held a glowing coal taken from the altar with tongs. 📖

## Isaiah 6:7
He touched my mouth with it. He said, “This touched your lips. Your wrongdoing is removed. Your sin is atoned for.” 📖

## Isaiah 6:8
I heard the Lord ask, “Whom should I send? Who will go for Us?” I answered, “Here I am. Send me!” 📖

## Isaiah 6:9
He answered, “Tell these people, ‘Keep hearing, but never understand. Keep seeing, but never perceive.’ 📖

## Isaiah 6:10
“Make these people’s hearts calloused. Deafen their ears and close their eyes. Otherwise, they might see, hear, understand in their hearts, turn, and be healed.” 📖

## Isaiah 6:11
I asked, “How long, Lord?” He answered, “Until cities lie ruined with no one living there. Until houses stand empty and the land is desolate and ravaged. 📖

## Isaiah 6:12
“Until the LORD drives people far away and the land is completely abandoned. 📖

## Isaiah 6:13
“Even the tenth that remains in the land will be burned again. Terebinth and oak trees leave stumps when cut down. In the same way, the holy seed will be a stump in the land.” 📖
