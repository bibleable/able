## Isaiah 20:1
Assyria’s king Sargon sent his chief commander to Ashdod. He attacked and captured it. Before that year, 📖

## Isaiah 20:2
the LORD had already spoken through Isaiah, Amoz’s son. “Remove the sackcloth from your waist and sandals from your feet.” Isaiah obeyed and walked naked and barefoot. 📖

## Isaiah 20:3
The LORD said, “My servant Isaiah walked naked and barefoot for three years. This was a sign and omen against Egypt and Cush. 📖

## Isaiah 20:4
“In the same way, Assyria’s king will take Egypt’s captives and Cush’s exiles, young and old. They will go naked and barefoot, with bare buttocks, to Egypt’s shame. 📖

## Isaiah 20:5
“Those who hoped in Cush and boasted in Egypt will be dismayed and shamed. 📖

## Isaiah 20:6
“On that day, this coastland’s people will say, ‘Look what happened to our hope! We fled to them for rescue from Assyria’s king. How can we escape?’” 📖
