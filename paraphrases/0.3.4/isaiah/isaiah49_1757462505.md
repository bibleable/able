## Isaiah 49:1
Islands and distant peoples, listen to Me. The LORD called Me from the womb. He named Me from My mother’s body. 📖

## Isaiah 49:2
He made My mouth like a sharp sword. He hid Me in His hand’s shadow. He made Me a polished arrow and hid Me in His quiver. 📖

## Isaiah 49:3
He told Me, “You are My Servant, Israel. I will display My glory in You.” 📖

## Isaiah 49:4
I said, “My work achieved nothing. I spent My strength uselessly. Yet the LORD holds My vindication. My God holds My reward.” 📖

## Isaiah 49:5
The LORD formed Me from the womb as His Servant to bring Jacob back and gather Israel to Him. I am honored before the LORD. My God is My strength. Now He speaks. 📖

## Isaiah 49:6
“Being My Servant to raise Jacob’s tribes and restore Israel’s protected ones is not enough. I will also make You a light for nations. You will bring My salvation to earth’s ends.” 📖

## Isaiah 49:7
The LORD, Israel’s Redeemer and Holy One, speaks to the One despised and abhorred by the nation, the Servant of rulers. “Kings will see You and rise. Princes will bow because the LORD is faithful. Israel’s Holy One chose You.” 📖

## Isaiah 49:8
The LORD says, “I will answer You in the time of favor and help You on salvation’s day. I will keep You and make You a covenant for the people. You will restore the land and distribute its desolate inheritances. 📖

## Isaiah 49:9
“Tell prisoners, ‘Come out!’ Tell those in darkness, ‘Show yourselves!’ They will feed beside paths and find pasture on every bare hill. 📖

## Isaiah 49:10
“They will neither hunger nor thirst. Scorching heat and sun will not strike them. The One with compassion will guide them beside springs. 📖

## Isaiah 49:11
“I will turn all My mountains into roads. My highways will be raised. 📖

## Isaiah 49:12
“They will come from far away, from north, west, and Aswan’s land.” 📖

## Isaiah 49:13
Heavens, shout! Earth, rejoice! Mountains, sing! The LORD comforted His people. He will show compassion to His afflicted ones. 📖

## Isaiah 49:14
Zion said, “The LORD abandoned me! The Lord forgot me!” 📖

## Isaiah 49:15
“Can a woman forget her nursing child or lack compassion for her son? Even if she forgets, I will not forget you! 📖

## Isaiah 49:16
“I inscribed you on My palms. Your walls are always before Me. 📖

## Isaiah 49:17
“Your builders hurry back. Your destroyers and wreckers leave. 📖

## Isaiah 49:18
“Look up and around. They all gather and come to you. As surely as I live,” declares the LORD, “you will wear them like jewelry, like a bride. 📖

## Isaiah 49:19
“Your ruined, desolate places and ravaged land will become too small for your people. Those who devoured you will be far away. 📖

## Isaiah 49:20
“The children of your bereavement will tell you, ‘This place is too small. Make room for us to live.’ 📖

## Isaiah 49:21
“You will ask in your heart, ‘Who bore these for me? I was bereaved, barren, exiled, and rejected. Who raised them? I was alone. Where did they come from?’” 📖

## Isaiah 49:22
The Lord GOD says, “I will raise My hand and banner to nations and peoples. They will carry your sons in their arms and daughters on their shoulders. 📖

## Isaiah 49:23
“Kings will be your foster fathers and queens your nursing mothers. They will bow facedown and lick dust at your feet. You will know I am the LORD. Those hoping in Me will never be shamed.” 📖

## Isaiah 49:24
Can plunder be taken from the mighty? Can a tyrant’s captives be rescued? 📖

## Isaiah 49:25
The LORD says, “Even the mighty’s captives will be freed. The tyrant’s plunder will be recovered. I will oppose those opposing you and save your children. 📖

## Isaiah 49:26
“I will make your oppressors eat their flesh and become drunk on their blood like wine. All humanity will know I am the LORD, your Savior and Redeemer, Jacob’s Mighty One.” 📖
