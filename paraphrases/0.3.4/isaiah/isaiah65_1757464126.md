## Isaiah 65:1
“I revealed Myself to those not asking for Me. Those not seeking Me found Me. I told a nation not calling My name, ‘Here I am! Here I am!’ 📖

## Isaiah 65:2
“All day, I held My hands out to stubborn people. They follow a wrong path and their own imaginations. 📖

## Isaiah 65:3
“They provoke Me continually to My face. They sacrifice in gardens and burn incense on brick altars. 📖

## Isaiah 65:4
“They sit among graves and spend nights in secret places. They eat pork and polluted broth from bowls. 📖

## Isaiah 65:5
“They say, ‘Stay away! I am holier than you!’ They are smoke in My nostrils, fire burning all day. 📖

## Isaiah 65:6
“It is written before Me. I will not stay silent. I will repay into their laps, 📖

## Isaiah 65:7
“for your wrongdoing and your fathers’ wrongdoing,” says the LORD. “They burned incense on mountains and scorned Me on hills. I will measure full payment for their former deeds into their laps.” 📖

## Isaiah 65:8
The LORD says, “People find new wine in a grape cluster and say, ‘Do not destroy it. It holds a blessing.’ I will act like this for My servants. I will not destroy them all. 📖

## Isaiah 65:9
“I will bring descendants from Jacob and heirs from Judah. My chosen ones will possess My mountains. My servants will live there. 📖

## Isaiah 65:10
“Sharon will be pasture for flocks. The Valley of Achor will be a resting place for herds. This is for My people seeking Me. 📖

## Isaiah 65:11
“But you abandon the LORD and forget My holy mountain. You set a table for Fortune and fill bowls of mixed wine for Destiny. 📖

## Isaiah 65:12
“I will destine you for the sword. You will all kneel for slaughter because I called without an answer and spoke without being heard. You did evil before Me. You chose what displeased Me.” 📖

## Isaiah 65:13
The Lord GOD says, “My servants will eat, but you will hunger. They will drink, but you will thirst. They will rejoice, but you will be shamed. 📖

## Isaiah 65:14
“My servants will shout from glad hearts. You will cry with heavy hearts and wail with broken spirits. 📖

## Isaiah 65:15
“You will leave your name as a curse for My chosen ones. The Lord GOD will kill you. He will give His servants another name. 📖

## Isaiah 65:16
“Whoever invokes a blessing or swears an oath in the land will do so by the God of truth. Former troubles will be forgotten and hidden from My sight. 📖

## Isaiah 65:17
“I will create new heavens and a new earth. Former things will neither be remembered nor come to mind. 📖

## Isaiah 65:18
“Rejoice forever in My creation. I will create Jerusalem as a joy and her people as a delight. 📖

## Isaiah 65:19
“I will rejoice in Jerusalem and delight in My people. Weeping and crying will no longer sound there. 📖

## Isaiah 65:20
“Nursing infants will no longer live only a few days. Old men will complete their years. A youth will die at a hundred. Anyone failing to reach a hundred will be considered accursed. 📖

## Isaiah 65:21
“They will build houses and live in them. They will plant vineyards and eat their fruit. 📖

## Isaiah 65:22
“They will not build or plant for others to enjoy. My people’s days will be like a tree’s lifetime. My chosen ones will fully enjoy their hands’ work. 📖

## Isaiah 65:23
“They will not work uselessly or bear children doomed to disaster. They and their descendants will be a people blessed by the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 65:24
“I will answer before they call. I will hear while they still speak. 📖

## Isaiah 65:25
“Wolves and lambs will feed together. Lions will eat straw like oxen. Serpents will eat dust. None will harm or destroy on My holy mountain,” says the LORD. 📖
