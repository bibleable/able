## Isaiah 50:1
The LORD says, “Where is your mother’s divorce certificate, with which I sent her away? To which creditor did I sell you? Your wrongdoing sold you. Your transgressions sent your mother away. 📖

## Isaiah 50:2
“Why did no one meet Me when I arrived or answer when I called? Is My hand too short to redeem, or am I too weak to rescue? My rebuke dries the sea and turns rivers to desert. Fish rot without water and die of thirst. 📖

## Isaiah 50:3
“I dress heaven in black and cover it with sackcloth.” 📖

## Isaiah 50:4
The Lord GOD gave Me a disciple’s tongue to sustain the weary with a word. Every morning, He wakes Me. He opens My ear to listen as a disciple. 📖

## Isaiah 50:5
The Lord GOD opened My ears. I did not rebel or turn back. 📖

## Isaiah 50:6
I offered My back to those striking Me and My cheeks to those tearing My beard. I did not hide My face from scorn or spit. 📖

## Isaiah 50:7
The Lord GOD helps Me, so I am not disgraced. I set My face like flint. I know I will not be shamed. 📖

## Isaiah 50:8
The One vindicating Me is near. Who dares oppose Me? Let us confront each other! Who has a case? Let him approach! 📖

## Isaiah 50:9
The Lord GOD certainly helps Me. Who can condemn Me? They will all wear out like clothes. Moths will devour them. 📖

## Isaiah 50:10
Who fears the LORD and obeys His Servant? Who walks in darkness without light? Let him trust the LORD’s name and rely on his God. 📖

## Isaiah 50:11
You lighting fires and surrounding yourselves with firebrands, walk in your fire’s light! Walk by the firebrands you lit. My hand will give you this: you will lie in torment. 📖
