## Isaiah 1:1
Isaiah, Amoz’s son, saw this vision about Judah and Jerusalem. It came during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, Judah’s kings. 📖

## Isaiah 1:2
Heavens and earth, listen! The LORD has spoken. “I raised children and brought them up. But they rebelled against Me. 📖

## Isaiah 1:3
“An ox knows its owner, and a donkey knows its master’s manger. But Israel does not know. My people do not understand.” 📖

## Isaiah 1:4
How terrible for this sinful nation, a people burdened with wrongdoing! They are a family of evildoers, children who act corruptly. They abandoned the LORD. They despised Israel’s Holy One and turned their backs on Him. 📖

## Isaiah 1:5
Why do you want more beatings? Why keep rebelling? Your head has a great wound. Your whole heart is afflicted. 📖

## Isaiah 1:6
From the bottom of your feet to the top of your head, nothing is healthy. You have wounds, welts, and festering sores. No one has cleaned, bandaged, or soothed them with oil. 📖

## Isaiah 1:7
Your land is desolate, and your cities burn. Foreigners devour your fields before your eyes. Strangers have destroyed your land and left it desolate. 📖

## Isaiah 1:8
The Daughter of Zion is abandoned like a vineyard shelter or a shack in a cucumber field. She is like a city under siege. 📖

## Isaiah 1:9
Unless the LORD of Hosts had left us a few survivors, we would have become like Sodom and Gomorrah. 📖

## Isaiah 1:10
Rulers of Sodom, hear the LORD’s word! People of Gomorrah, listen to our God’s instruction! 📖

## Isaiah 1:11
The LORD says, “What good are your many sacrifices to Me? I have had enough burnt offerings of rams and fat from well-fed cattle. Bulls’, lambs’, and goats’ blood gives Me no pleasure. 📖

## Isaiah 1:12
“When you appear before Me, who asked you to trample My courts? 📖

## Isaiah 1:13
“Stop bringing worthless offerings. Your incense is detestable to Me. I cannot bear wrongdoing in your solemn assemblies, New Moons, Sabbaths, and gatherings. 📖

## Isaiah 1:14
“I hate your New Moons and appointed feasts. They burden Me. I am tired of bearing them. 📖

## Isaiah 1:15
“When you spread your hands to pray, I will hide My eyes. Even if you offer many prayers, I will not listen. Blood covers your hands. 📖

## Isaiah 1:16
“Wash and cleanse yourselves. Remove your evil deeds from My sight. Stop doing evil! 📖

## Isaiah 1:17
“Learn to do right. Seek justice and correct oppressors. Defend the fatherless and plead widows’ cases.” 📖

## Isaiah 1:18
The LORD says, “Come, let us reason together. Your sins may be scarlet, but they will become white as snow. They may be crimson red, but they will become like wool. 📖

## Isaiah 1:19
“If you are willing and obedient, you will eat the land’s best produce. 📖

## Isaiah 1:20
“But if you resist and rebel, the sword will devour you.” The LORD’s mouth has spoken. 📖

## Isaiah 1:21
The faithful city has become a prostitute! She was once full of justice, and righteousness lived within her. Now murderers live there! 📖

## Isaiah 1:22
Your silver has become dross. Water dilutes your fine wine. 📖

## Isaiah 1:23
Your rulers are rebels and thieves’ friends. They all love bribes and pursue rewards. They do not defend the fatherless. Widows’ requests never reach them. 📖

## Isaiah 1:24
The Lord GOD of Hosts, Israel’s Mighty One, declares, “I will gain relief from My foes. I will take vengeance on My enemies. 📖

## Isaiah 1:25
“I will turn My hand against you. I will thoroughly remove your dross and all your impurities. 📖

## Isaiah 1:26
“I will restore your judges and counselors as they were at first. Then people will call you the City of Righteousness, the Faithful City.” 📖

## Isaiah 1:27
Zion will be redeemed with justice. Her repentant people will be redeemed with righteousness. 📖

## Isaiah 1:28
Rebels and sinners will be shattered together. Those who abandon the LORD will perish. 📖

## Isaiah 1:29
You will certainly be ashamed of the sacred oaks you enjoyed. The gardens you chose will embarrass you. 📖

## Isaiah 1:30
You will become like an oak with withered leaves or a garden without water. 📖

## Isaiah 1:31
The strong man will become tinder. His work will be a spark. Both will burn together, and no one will put out the flames. 📖
