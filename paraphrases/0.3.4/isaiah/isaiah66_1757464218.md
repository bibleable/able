## Isaiah 66:1
The LORD says, “Heaven is My throne, and earth My footstool. What house will you build Me? Where will I rest? 📖

## Isaiah 66:2
“Did My hand not make everything, bringing it into being?” declares the LORD. “I esteem the humble, contrite person who trembles at My word. 📖

## Isaiah 66:3
“Slaughtering an ox is like killing a man. Sacrificing a lamb is like breaking a dog’s neck. Giving grain is like offering pig’s blood. Offering frankincense is like blessing an idol. They chose their own ways and delighted in abominations. 📖

## Isaiah 66:4
“I will choose their punishment and bring terror. I called, but no one answered. I spoke, but no one listened. They did evil before Me and chose what displeased Me.” 📖

## Isaiah 66:5
You trembling at His word, hear the LORD. “Your brothers hate and exclude you because of My name. They say, ‘Let the LORD be glorified so we can see your joy!’ They will be shamed.” 📖

## Isaiah 66:6
Hear the city’s uproar and the voice from the temple! The LORD’s voice repays His enemies what they deserve! 📖

## Isaiah 66:7
“She gave birth before labor. She delivered a boy before pain. 📖

## Isaiah 66:8
“Who heard or saw anything like this? Can a country be born in a day or a nation delivered instantly? Yet Zion bore children as soon as labor began. 📖

## Isaiah 66:9
“Would I bring a baby to birth without delivering?” asks the LORD. “Would I, who deliver, close the womb?” asks your God. 📖

## Isaiah 66:10
Everyone loving Jerusalem, rejoice with her! Everyone mourning for her, be very glad, 📖

## Isaiah 66:11
so you can nurse and be satisfied at her comforting breasts. Drink deeply and delight in her glorious abundance. 📖

## Isaiah 66:12
The LORD says, “I will extend peace like a river and nations’ wealth like a flowing stream. You will nurse, be carried on her arm, and bounced on her knees. 📖

## Isaiah 66:13
“I will comfort you as a mother comforts her son. You will be consoled over Jerusalem.” 📖

## Isaiah 66:14
You will see, rejoice, and flourish like grass. The LORD’s hand will be revealed to His servants. His wrath will be shown to His enemies. 📖

## Isaiah 66:15
The LORD will come with fire, His chariots like a whirlwind. He will carry out His anger with fury and His rebuke with fiery flames. 📖

## Isaiah 66:16
The LORD will judge all flesh with fire and His sword. He will kill many. 📖

## Isaiah 66:17
“Some consecrate and purify themselves to enter groves, following one in the center. They eat pork, vermin, and rats. They will perish together,” declares the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 66:18
“I know their deeds and thoughts. I come to gather all nations and languages. They will see My glory. 📖

## Isaiah 66:19
“I will set a sign among them and send survivors to nations. They will go to Tarshish, Put, Lud’s archers, Tubal, Javan, and distant islands. Those have neither heard My fame nor seen My glory. They will proclaim My glory among nations. 📖

## Isaiah 66:20
“They will bring all your brothers from every nation as a gift to the LORD,” says the LORD. “They will come to My holy mountain Jerusalem on horses, chariots, wagons, mules, and camels. This is like Israelites bringing an offering in a clean vessel to the LORD’s house. 📖

## Isaiah 66:21
“I will choose some as priests and Levites,” says the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 66:22
“My new heavens and new earth will endure before Me,” declares the LORD. “Your descendants and name will endure in the same way. 📖

## Isaiah 66:23
“From New Moon to New Moon and Sabbath to Sabbath, all humanity will come to worship before Me,” says the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 66:24
“As they leave, they will see the corpses of those who rebelled against Me. Their worm will never die. Their fire will never be put out. They will be a horror to all humanity.” 📖
