## Isaiah 15:1
This is the burden against Moab. Ar in Moab is ruined, destroyed in one night! Kir in Moab is devastated, destroyed in one night! 📖

## Isaiah 15:2
Dibon goes to its temple to weep at its high places. Moab wails for Nebo and Medeba. Every head is shaved, and every beard is cut off. 📖

## Isaiah 15:3
People wear sackcloth in the streets. On rooftops and in public squares, everyone wails and falls down weeping. 📖

## Isaiah 15:4
Heshbon and Elealeh cry out. Their voices reach Jahaz. Moab’s soldiers cry out, and their souls tremble. 📖

## Isaiah 15:5
My heart cries for Moab. Her fugitives flee to Zoar and Eglath-shelishiyah. They climb Luhith’s slope weeping. On the road to Horonaim, they lament their destruction. 📖

## Isaiah 15:6
Nimrim’s waters are dry, and the grass has withered. Plants and greenery are gone. 📖

## Isaiah 15:7
They carry their wealth and belongings across the Brook of the Willows. 📖

## Isaiah 15:8
Their cries echo to Moab’s border. Their wailing reaches Eglaim and Beer-elim. 📖

## Isaiah 15:9
Dimon’s waters are full of blood. But I will bring more on Dimon. I will bring a lion against Moab’s fugitives and those remaining in the land. 📖
