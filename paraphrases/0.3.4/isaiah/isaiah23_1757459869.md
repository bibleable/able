## Isaiah 23:1
This is the burden against Tyre. Ships of Tarshish, wail! Tyre is ruined, without house or harbor. News reached them from Cyprus. 📖

## Isaiah 23:2
Coastland people, be silent! Be silent, Sidon’s merchants whose traders crossed the sea. 📖

## Isaiah 23:3
Shihor’s grain came across the great waters. The Nile’s harvest brought Tyre income. She traded with the nations. 📖

## Isaiah 23:4
Sidon, stronghold of the sea, be ashamed! The sea said, “I have not labored or given birth. I have not raised young men or women.” 📖

## Isaiah 23:5
When the news of Tyre reaches Egypt, they will twist in agony. 📖

## Isaiah 23:6
Coastland people, cross to Tarshish and wail! 📖

## Isaiah 23:7
Is this your joyful city from ancient times? Did her feet take her to settle far away? 📖

## Isaiah 23:8
Who planned this against Tyre, the giver of crowns? Her traders are princes. Her merchants are famous throughout the earth. 📖

## Isaiah 23:9
The LORD of Hosts planned it. He will defile all her glorious beauty and disgrace earth’s renowned people. 📖

## Isaiah 23:10
Daughter of Tarshish, cultivate your land like the Nile. There is no harbor anymore. 📖

## Isaiah 23:11
The LORD stretched His hand over the sea and made kingdoms tremble. He commanded the destruction of Canaan’s strongholds. 📖

## Isaiah 23:12
He said, “Oppressed Virgin Daughter of Sidon, you will rejoice no more. Get up and cross to Cyprus. Even there you will find no rest.” 📖

## Isaiah 23:13
Look at the Chaldeans’ land, a people now of no account. Assyria destined it for desert creatures. They raised siege towers, stripped its palaces, and ruined it. 📖

## Isaiah 23:14
Ships of Tarshish, wail! Your harbor is destroyed! 📖

## Isaiah 23:15
Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years, a king’s lifetime. After seventy years, Tyre will be like the prostitute in this song. 📖

## Isaiah 23:16
“Forgotten prostitute, take your harp and walk through the city. Play sweet music and sing many songs so people remember you.” 📖

## Isaiah 23:17
After seventy years, the LORD will restore Tyre. She will return to hiring herself as a prostitute. She will sell herself to all earth’s kingdoms. 📖

## Isaiah 23:18
But her profits and wages will be set apart for the LORD. They will not be stored or saved. They will provide abundant food and fine clothes for those living before Him. 📖
