## Isaiah 37:1
King Hezekiah heard the report. He tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and entered the LORD’s house. 📖

## Isaiah 37:2
He sent Eliakim the palace administrator, Shebna the scribe, and the leading priests to the prophet Isaiah, Amoz’s son. They all wore sackcloth. 📖

## Isaiah 37:3
They said, “Hezekiah says, ‘Today brings distress, rebuke, and disgrace. Children are ready for birth, but there is no strength to deliver them. 📖

## Isaiah 37:4
“‘Perhaps the LORD your God will hear the Rabshakeh’s words. His master, Assyria’s king, sent him to defy the living God. Perhaps God will rebuke him for the words He heard. Pray for those who still survive.’” 📖

## Isaiah 37:5
King Hezekiah’s servants went to Isaiah. 📖

## Isaiah 37:6
Isaiah answered, “Tell your master what the LORD says. ‘Do not fear the words you heard. The servants of Assyria’s king used them to blaspheme Me. 📖

## Isaiah 37:7
“‘I will put a spirit in him. He will hear a rumor and return home. There I will make him fall by the sword.’” 📖

## Isaiah 37:8
The Rabshakeh heard that Assyria’s king left Lachish. He withdrew and found the king fighting Libnah. 📖

## Isaiah 37:9
Sennacherib heard this warning about Tirhakah, Cush’s king: “He has come to fight you.” Sennacherib sent messengers to Hezekiah. 📖

## Isaiah 37:10
“Tell Hezekiah, Judah’s king, ‘Do not let the God you trust deceive you. He says Jerusalem will not be handed to Assyria’s king. 📖

## Isaiah 37:11
“‘You heard how Assyria’s kings devoted other countries to destruction. Will you escape? 📖

## Isaiah 37:12
“‘Did their gods save the nations my fathers destroyed? Did they save Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, or Eden’s people in Telassar? 📖

## Isaiah 37:13
“‘Where are the kings of Hamath, Arpad, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’” 📖

## Isaiah 37:14
Hezekiah received and read the messengers’ letter. He went to the LORD’s house and spread it before the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 37:15
Hezekiah prayed to the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 37:16
“LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, You are enthroned above the cherubim. You alone are God over all earth’s kingdoms. You made heaven and earth. 📖

## Isaiah 37:17
“LORD, listen and open Your eyes. Hear every word Sennacherib sent to defy the living God. 📖

## Isaiah 37:18
“LORD, Assyria’s kings truly destroyed these countries and their lands. 📖

## Isaiah 37:19
“They burned their gods and destroyed them. Those were not gods, only wood and stone made by human hands. 📖

## Isaiah 37:20
“LORD our God, save us from him. Then all earth’s kingdoms will know that You alone, LORD, are God.” 📖

## Isaiah 37:21
Isaiah, Amoz’s son, sent Hezekiah this message. “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘You prayed to Me about Sennacherib, Assyria’s king. 📖

## Isaiah 37:22
“‘Because of this, the LORD speaks against him. The Virgin Daughter of Zion despises and mocks you. Jerusalem’s daughter shakes her head behind you. 📖

## Isaiah 37:23
“‘Whom did you taunt and blaspheme? Against whom did you raise your voice and proud eyes? Against Israel’s Holy One! 📖

## Isaiah 37:24
“‘Through your servants, you taunted the Lord. You said, “With my many chariots, I climbed mountain heights and Lebanon’s remote peaks. I cut its tallest cedars and finest cypresses. I reached its farthest heights and densest forests. 📖

## Isaiah 37:25
“‘“I dug wells and drank foreign waters. My feet’s soles dried all Egypt’s streams.” 📖

## Isaiah 37:26
“‘Have you not heard? Long ago I ordained and planned this. Now I have made you crush fortified cities into piles of rubble. 📖

## Isaiah 37:27
“‘Their people are powerless, dismayed, and shamed. They are like field plants, tender shoots, and rooftop grass scorched before growing. 📖

## Isaiah 37:28
“‘I know when you sit, leave, and enter. I know your rage against Me. 📖

## Isaiah 37:29
“‘I heard your rage and arrogance against Me. I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your mouth. I will send you back the way you came.’ 📖

## Isaiah 37:30
“Hezekiah, this will be your sign. This year, you will eat what grows by itself. In the second year, you will eat what springs from it. In the third, you will sow and harvest, plant vineyards and eat their fruit. 📖

## Isaiah 37:31
“Judah’s surviving remnant will again take root below and bear fruit above. 📖

## Isaiah 37:32
“A remnant will leave Jerusalem. Survivors will come from Mount Zion. The LORD of Hosts’ zeal will accomplish this. 📖

## Isaiah 37:33
“The LORD says about Assyria’s king, ‘He will not enter this city or shoot an arrow into it. He will neither approach with a shield nor build a siege ramp against it. 📖

## Isaiah 37:34
“‘He will return the way he came and never enter this city,’ declares the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 37:35
“‘I will defend and save this city for My own sake and My servant David’s sake.’” 📖

## Isaiah 37:36
The LORD’s angel struck down 185,000 men in the Assyrian camp. The next morning, people got up and saw all the corpses! 📖

## Isaiah 37:37
Sennacherib, Assyria’s king, broke camp and withdrew. He returned to Nineveh and stayed there. 📖

## Isaiah 37:38
One day, he worshiped in his god Nisroch’s temple. His sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with the sword and fled to Ararat’s land. His son Esar-haddon became king in his place. 📖
