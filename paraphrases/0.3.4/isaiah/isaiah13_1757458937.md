## Isaiah 13:1
Isaiah, Amoz’s son, received this burden against Babylon. 📖

## Isaiah 13:2
Raise a banner on a bare hilltop. Call loudly to them. Wave your hand so they can enter the nobles’ gates. 📖

## Isaiah 13:3
I commanded My sanctified ones. I called My warriors to carry out My wrath and rejoice in My triumph. 📖

## Isaiah 13:4
Listen to the mountain noise, like a great crowd! Hear the uproar of kingdoms and gathering nations! The LORD of Hosts prepares an army for war. 📖

## Isaiah 13:5
They come from distant lands, from heaven’s ends. The LORD and His weapons of wrath come to destroy the whole country. 📖

## Isaiah 13:6
Wail, because the Day of the LORD is near! It will bring destruction from the Almighty. 📖

## Isaiah 13:7
All hands will hang limp. Every heart will melt. 📖

## Isaiah 13:8
Terror, pain, and anguish will seize them. They will twist like a woman giving birth. They will look at one another with faces flushed in fear. 📖

## Isaiah 13:9
The Day of the LORD comes, cruel and full of fury and burning anger. It will make the earth desolate and destroy its sinners. 📖

## Isaiah 13:10
Heaven’s stars and constellations will give no light. The rising sun will be darkened. The moon will give no light. 📖

## Isaiah 13:11
I will punish the world’s evil and the wicked people’s wrongdoing. I will end arrogant pride and humble the ruthless. 📖

## Isaiah 13:12
I will make people scarcer than pure gold, rarer than Ophir’s gold. 📖

## Isaiah 13:13
I will make the heavens tremble and shake the earth from its place. This will happen at the LORD of Hosts’ wrath, on His day of burning anger. 📖

## Isaiah 13:14
Like a hunted gazelle or a sheep without a shepherd, everyone will return to his people and flee to his native land. 📖

## Isaiah 13:15
Anyone caught will be stabbed. Anyone captured will die by the sword. 📖

## Isaiah 13:16
Their infants will be dashed to pieces before their eyes. Their houses will be looted, and their wives raped. 📖

## Isaiah 13:17
I will stir the Medes against them. They value no silver and desire no gold. 📖

## Isaiah 13:18
Their bows will smash young men to pieces. They will show no mercy to the womb’s fruit and no pity to children. 📖

## Isaiah 13:19
Babylon is the kingdoms’ jewel, the glory of Chaldean pride. God will overthrow it like Sodom and Gomorrah. 📖

## Isaiah 13:20
No one will ever settle or live there, from generation to generation. No nomad will pitch a tent there. No shepherd will rest a flock there. 📖

## Isaiah 13:21
Desert creatures will lie there. Howling creatures will fill the houses. Ostriches will live there, and wild goats will leap about. 📖

## Isaiah 13:22
Hyenas will howl in the fortresses and jackals in the luxurious palaces. Babylon’s time is near. Its days will not be extended. 📖
