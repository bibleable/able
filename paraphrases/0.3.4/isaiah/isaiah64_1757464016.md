## Isaiah 64:1
If only You would tear heaven open and descend! Mountains would shake before You, 📖

## Isaiah 64:2
as fire lights brushwood and boils water. Make Your name known to enemies so nations tremble before You! 📖

## Isaiah 64:3
You did awesome, unexpected things. You descended, and mountains trembled before You. 📖

## Isaiah 64:4
Since ancient times, no one heard, perceived, or saw another God besides You. You act for those waiting for You. 📖

## Isaiah 64:5
You welcome those gladly doing right and remembering Your ways. You were angry because we sinned. How can we be saved if we keep sinning? 📖

## Isaiah 64:6
We all became unclean. All our righteous acts are filthy rags. We wither like leaves. Our wrongdoing carries us away like wind. 📖

## Isaiah 64:7
No one calls Your name or strives to hold You. You hid Your face and handed us to our wrongdoing. 📖

## Isaiah 64:8
LORD, You are our Father. We are clay, and You are the potter. We are all Your hand’s work. 📖

## Isaiah 64:9
LORD, do not stay angry beyond measure or remember wrongdoing forever. Please look at us! We are all Your people. 📖

## Isaiah 64:10
Your holy cities became wilderness. Zion is a wasteland, and Jerusalem desolate. 📖

## Isaiah 64:11
Fire burned our holy, beautiful temple where our fathers praised You. Everything dear to us lies ruined. 📖

## Isaiah 64:12
LORD, will You restrain Yourself after all this? Will You stay silent and afflict us beyond measure? 📖
