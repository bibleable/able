## Isaiah 41:1
“Islands, be silent before Me. Let peoples renew their strength. Let them approach and testify. Let us come together for judgment. 📖

## Isaiah 41:2
“Who stirred someone from the east and called him to his feet in righteousness? He gives nations to him and subdues kings before him. His sword turns them to dust. His bow makes them windblown chaff. 📖

## Isaiah 41:3
“He pursues them safely, his feet barely touching the path. 📖

## Isaiah 41:4
“Who did this, calling generations from the beginning? I, the LORD, am He, the first and last.” 📖

## Isaiah 41:5
Islands see and fear. Earth’s ends tremble. They come forward. 📖

## Isaiah 41:6
Each helps the other. Each tells his brother, “Be strong!” 📖

## Isaiah 41:7
The craftsman encourages the goldsmith. The hammer worker encourages the one striking the anvil. He says the welding is good. He nails it down so it cannot fall. 📖

## Isaiah 41:8
“Israel, you are My servant. Jacob, I chose you, descendant of My friend Abraham. 📖

## Isaiah 41:9
“I brought you from earth’s ends and called you from its farthest corners. I said, ‘You are My servant.’ I chose you and did not reject you. 📖

## Isaiah 41:10
“Do not fear, because I am with you. I am your God. I will strengthen and certainly help you. I will uphold you with My righteous right hand. 📖

## Isaiah 41:11
“All who rage against you will be shamed and disgraced. Those opposing you will become nothing and perish. 📖

## Isaiah 41:12
“You will look for them but find none. Those fighting you will become nothing. 📖

## Isaiah 41:13
“I am the LORD your God. I hold your right hand and say, ‘Do not fear. I will help you.’ 📖

## Isaiah 41:14
“Jacob, you worm, and Israel’s few men, do not fear. I will help you,” declares the LORD. “Your Redeemer is Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Isaiah 41:15
“I will make you a new, sharp threshing sledge with many teeth. You will thresh and crush mountains. You will reduce hills to chaff. 📖

## Isaiah 41:16
“You will winnow them. Wind will carry them away, and a gale will scatter them. You will rejoice in the LORD and glory in Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Isaiah 41:17
“The poor and needy seek water but find none. Their tongues are dry with thirst. I, the LORD, will answer. I, Israel’s God, will not abandon them. 📖

## Isaiah 41:18
“I will open rivers on bare heights and fountains in valleys. I will turn desert into pools and dry land into flowing springs. 📖

## Isaiah 41:19
“I will plant cedars, acacias, myrtles, and olive trees in the wilderness. I will put cypresses, elms, and boxwood together in the desert. 📖

## Isaiah 41:20
“Everyone will see, know, consider, and understand. The LORD’s hand did this. Israel’s Holy One created it.” 📖

## Isaiah 41:21
The LORD says, “Present your case.” Jacob’s King says, “Submit your arguments. 📖

## Isaiah 41:22
“Let them tell us what will happen. Explain former events so we can consider them and know their outcome. Or announce what is coming. 📖

## Isaiah 41:23
“Tell the future so we know you are gods. Do good or evil, so we can look together in dismay. 📖

## Isaiah 41:24
“You are nothing. Your work is worthless. Anyone choosing you is detestable. 📖

## Isaiah 41:25
“I raised someone from the north, and he came. He comes from the east and calls on My name. He will trample rulers like mortar, like a potter treading clay. 📖

## Isaiah 41:26
“Who announced this from the beginning so we could know? Who spoke long ago so we could say, ‘He was right’? No one announced or predicted it. No one heard your words. 📖

## Isaiah 41:27
“I first told Zion, ‘Look, here they are!’ I gave Jerusalem a herald of good news. 📖

## Isaiah 41:28
“I look and find no one, no counselor among them. When I ask, they have no answer. 📖

## Isaiah 41:29
“They are all a delusion. Their works are nothing. Their images are empty as wind. 📖
