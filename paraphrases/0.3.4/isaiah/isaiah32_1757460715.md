## Isaiah 32:1
A king will reign with righteousness. Princes will rule with justice. 📖

## Isaiah 32:2
Each will be like shelter from wind and storms. Each will be like streams in dry land or a great rock’s shade in an arid land. 📖

## Isaiah 32:3
The eyes of those seeing will no longer be closed. The ears of those hearing will listen. 📖

## Isaiah 32:4
Rash minds will gain knowledge and understanding. Stammering tongues will speak clearly and fluently. 📖

## Isaiah 32:5
No one will call a fool noble or respect a scoundrel. 📖

## Isaiah 32:6
A fool speaks foolishness and plans wrongdoing. He acts godlessly and speaks falsely about the LORD. He leaves the hungry empty and denies drink to the thirsty. 📖

## Isaiah 32:7
A scoundrel’s weapons destroy. He plots to ruin the poor with lies, even when the needy have a just case. 📖

## Isaiah 32:8
A noble man makes honorable plans. He stands for worthy causes. 📖

## Isaiah 32:9
Complacent women, stand and listen to me. Overconfident daughters, hear my words. 📖

## Isaiah 32:10
Secure ones, in a little more than a year you will tremble. The grape harvest will fail. The fruit harvest will not come. 📖

## Isaiah 32:11
Women of leisure, shudder! Complacent daughters, tremble! Strip yourselves bare and put sackcloth around your waists. 📖

## Isaiah 32:12
Beat your breasts for the pleasant fields and fruitful vines. 📖

## Isaiah 32:13
Mourn for my people’s land, overgrown with thorns and briers. Mourn for every joyful house in this city of revelry. 📖

## Isaiah 32:14
The palace and busy city will be abandoned. The hill and watchtower will become caves forever. Wild donkeys will delight there, and flocks will graze, 📖

## Isaiah 32:15
until the Spirit is poured on us from above. Then the desert will become an orchard, and the orchard will seem like a forest. 📖

## Isaiah 32:16
Justice will live in the wilderness. Righteousness will live in the fertile field. 📖

## Isaiah 32:17
Righteousness will produce peace. Its service will produce quiet confidence forever. 📖

## Isaiah 32:18
My people will live in peace, safety, and secure places of rest. 📖

## Isaiah 32:19
But hail will level the forest. The city will sink to the depths. 📖

## Isaiah 32:20
Blessed are those planting beside abundant waters and letting oxen and donkeys roam freely. 📖
