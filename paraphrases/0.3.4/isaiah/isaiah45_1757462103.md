## Isaiah 45:1
The LORD speaks to His anointed, Cyrus. “I grasped your right hand to subdue nations, disarm kings, and open doors before you. Gates will not close. 📖

## Isaiah 45:2
“I will go before you and level mountains. I will break bronze gates and cut iron bars. 📖

## Isaiah 45:3
“I will give you dark places’ treasures and hidden riches. You will know that I am the LORD, Israel’s God, who calls you by name. 📖

## Isaiah 45:4
“For My servant Jacob and My chosen Israel, I call you by name. I gave you an honored title, although you did not know Me. 📖

## Isaiah 45:5
“I am the LORD. There is no other God. I will equip you for battle, although you did not know Me. 📖

## Isaiah 45:6
“Everyone from sunrise to sunset will know that there is none except Me. I am the LORD. There is no other. 📖

## Isaiah 45:7
“I form light and create darkness. I bring prosperity and create disaster. I, the LORD, do all this. 📖

## Isaiah 45:8
“Heavens, drip from above. Skies, pour righteousness. Let earth open so salvation sprouts and righteousness grows with it. I, the LORD, created it. 📖

## Isaiah 45:9
“How terrible for a clay pot quarreling with its Maker among other pots! Does clay ask its potter, ‘What are you making?’ Does your work say, ‘He has no hands’? 📖

## Isaiah 45:10
“How terrible for someone asking his father, ‘What did you beget?’ How terrible for someone asking his mother, ‘What did you bear?’” 📖

## Isaiah 45:11
The LORD, Israel’s Holy One and Maker, says, “About future things, do you question Me about My sons? Do you instruct Me in My hands’ work? 📖

## Isaiah 45:12
“I made the earth and created people on it. My hands stretched heaven. I ordained all its host. 📖

## Isaiah 45:13
“I will raise Cyrus in righteousness and make all his paths straight. He will rebuild My city and free My exiles without payment or reward,” says the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 45:14
The LORD says, “Egypt’s products, Cush’s merchandise, and the tall Sabeans will come to you and be yours. They will walk behind you in chains and bow to you. They will confess, ‘God is truly with you. There is no other God.’” 📖

## Isaiah 45:15
You truly are a God who hides Himself, Israel’s God and Savior. 📖

## Isaiah 45:16
All idol makers will be shamed and humiliated. They will leave together in disgrace. 📖

## Isaiah 45:17
The LORD will save Israel with everlasting salvation. You will never be shamed or humiliated, forever. 📖

## Isaiah 45:18
The LORD created heaven and is God. He formed, fashioned, and established earth to be inhabited, not empty. He says, “I am the LORD. There is no other. 📖

## Isaiah 45:19
“I did not speak secretly from a dark land. I did not tell Jacob’s descendants to seek Me in a wasteland. I, the LORD, speak truth and say what is right. 📖

## Isaiah 45:20
“Fugitives from nations, gather and approach. Those carrying wooden idols and praying to a god unable to save lack knowledge. 📖

## Isaiah 45:21
“Present your case and consult together. Who foretold and announced this long ago? Was it not I, the LORD? There is no God except Me, a righteous God and Savior. There is none except Me. 📖

## Isaiah 45:22
“All earth’s ends, turn to Me and be saved. I am God. There is no other. 📖

## Isaiah 45:23
“I swear by Myself. Truth left My mouth, a word I will not withdraw. Every knee will bow to Me. Every tongue will swear allegiance. 📖

## Isaiah 45:24
“They will certainly say, ‘Righteousness and strength are in the LORD alone.’” All who rage against Him will approach and be shamed. 📖

## Isaiah 45:25
In the LORD, all Israel’s descendants will be justified and rejoice. 📖
