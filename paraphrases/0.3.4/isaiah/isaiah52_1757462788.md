## Isaiah 52:1
Awake, awake, Zion! Put on strength. Holy Jerusalem, wear splendid clothes! The uncircumcised and unclean will no longer enter you. 📖

## Isaiah 52:2
Shake off dust! Jerusalem, rise and sit on your throne. Captive Daughter of Zion, remove your neck’s chains. 📖

## Isaiah 52:3
The LORD says, “You were sold for nothing. You will be redeemed without money.” 📖

## Isaiah 52:4
The Lord GOD says, “My people first went to live in Egypt. Then Assyria oppressed them without cause. 📖

## Isaiah 52:5
“What do I have here now?” declares the LORD. “My people were taken without cause. Their rulers taunt,” declares the LORD. “My name is blasphemed all day. 📖

## Isaiah 52:6
“My people will know My name. On that day, they will know I am the One speaking. Here I am!” 📖

## Isaiah 52:7
How beautiful on the mountains are the good news bearers’ feet! They announce peace, good tidings, and salvation. They tell Zion, “Your God reigns!” 📖

## Isaiah 52:8
Listen! Your watchmen raise their voices and shout joyfully together. Every eye will see when the LORD returns to Zion. 📖

## Isaiah 52:9
Jerusalem’s ruins, sing joyfully together! The LORD comforted His people and redeemed Jerusalem. 📖

## Isaiah 52:10
The LORD bared His holy arm before all nations. All earth’s ends will see our God’s salvation. 📖

## Isaiah 52:11
Leave, leave that place! Touch nothing unclean. Come out and purify yourselves, you carrying the LORD’s vessels. 📖

## Isaiah 52:12
You will not leave hurriedly or flee in haste. The LORD goes before you. Israel’s God guards your rear. 📖

## Isaiah 52:13
My Servant will prosper. He will be raised, lifted up, and highly exalted. 📖

## Isaiah 52:14
Many were horrified at Him. His appearance was disfigured beyond any man’s. His form was marred beyond human likeness. 📖

## Isaiah 52:15
In the same way, He will sprinkle many nations. Kings will close their mouths because of Him. They will see what no one told them and understand what they never heard. 📖
