## Isaiah 36:1
In Hezekiah’s fourteenth year, Sennacherib, Assyria’s king, attacked and captured all Judah’s fortified cities. 📖

## Isaiah 36:2
Assyria’s king sent the Rabshakeh with a great army from Lachish to King Hezekiah in Jerusalem. He stopped by the upper pool’s aqueduct, on the road to the Launderer’s Field. 📖

## Isaiah 36:3
Eliakim, Hilkiah’s son, the palace administrator, went to him. Shebna the scribe and Joah, Asaph’s son, the recorder, also went. 📖

## Isaiah 36:4
The Rabshakeh said, “Tell Hezekiah what the great king, Assyria’s king, says. ‘What gives you this confidence? 📖

## Isaiah 36:5
“‘You claim strategy and strength for war. But these are empty words. Whom do you trust, that you rebel against me? 📖

## Isaiah 36:6
“‘You trust Egypt, a splintered reed staff. It pierces the hand of anyone leaning on it. Egypt’s king Pharaoh is like that to everyone trusting him. 📖

## Isaiah 36:7
“‘You may say, “We trust the LORD our God.” But did Hezekiah not remove His high places and altars? Did he not tell Judah and Jerusalem to worship before this altar? 📖

## Isaiah 36:8
“‘Make a bargain with my master, Assyria’s king. I will give you two thousand horses if you can supply riders! 📖

## Isaiah 36:9
“‘How can you repel even one of my master’s least officers? You depend on Egypt for chariots and horsemen. 📖

## Isaiah 36:10
“‘Did I come to destroy this land without the LORD? The LORD Himself told me, “Attack this land and destroy it.”’” 📖

## Isaiah 36:11
Eliakim, Shebna, and Joah told the Rabshakeh, “Please speak Aramaic to your servants. We understand it. Do not speak Hebrew for those on the wall to hear.” 📖

## Isaiah 36:12
The Rabshakeh answered, “Did my master send this message only to you and your master? He also sent it to those on the wall. Like you, they are destined to eat their dung and drink their urine.” 📖

## Isaiah 36:13
The Rabshakeh stood and shouted in Hebrew. “Hear the great king, Assyria’s king! 📖

## Isaiah 36:14
“The king says, ‘Do not let Hezekiah deceive you. He cannot rescue you. 📖

## Isaiah 36:15
“‘Do not let him persuade you to trust the LORD. He says, “The LORD will certainly save us. Assyria’s king will not receive this city.” 📖

## Isaiah 36:16
“‘Do not listen to Hezekiah. Assyria’s king says, “Make peace and come out to me. Each will eat from his own vine and fig tree and drink from his own cistern, 📖

## Isaiah 36:17
“‘“until I take you to a land like yours. It has grain, new wine, bread, and vineyards.” 📖

## Isaiah 36:18
“‘Do not let Hezekiah mislead you by saying, “The LORD will save us.” Has any nation’s god rescued its land from Assyria’s king? 📖

## Isaiah 36:19
“‘Where are Hamath and Arpad’s gods? Where are Sepharvaim’s gods? Did they rescue Samaria from my hand? 📖

## Isaiah 36:20
“‘Which god of these lands rescued its land from my hand? How can the LORD rescue Jerusalem from me?’” 📖

## Isaiah 36:21
The people were silent and said nothing. Hezekiah had commanded, “Do not answer him.” 📖

## Isaiah 36:22
Eliakim, Hilkiah’s son, the palace administrator, Shebna the scribe, and Joah, Asaph’s son, the recorder, came to Hezekiah with torn clothes. They reported the Rabshakeh’s words. 📖
