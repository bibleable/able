## Isaiah 24:1
The LORD ruins the earth and leaves it desolate. He will twist its surface and scatter its people. 📖

## Isaiah 24:2
This includes people and priests, servants and masters, maids and mistresses. It includes buyers and sellers, lenders and borrowers, creditors and debtors. 📖

## Isaiah 24:3
The earth will be completely ruined and thoroughly plundered. The LORD spoke this word. 📖

## Isaiah 24:4
The earth mourns and withers. The world weakens and fades. Earth’s exalted people waste away. 📖

## Isaiah 24:5
Earth’s people defiled it. They broke laws, overstepped decrees, and broke the everlasting covenant. 📖

## Isaiah 24:6
A curse consumes the earth. Its people must bear the guilt. They have been burned, and few survive. 📖

## Isaiah 24:7
New wine dries up, and the vine withers. All the merrymakers groan. 📖

## Isaiah 24:8
Joyful tambourines have stopped. Revelers are quiet. The joyful harp is silent. 📖

## Isaiah 24:9
They no longer sing and drink wine. Strong drink tastes bitter to its drinkers. 📖

## Isaiah 24:10
The city of chaos is broken. Every house is closed to entry. 📖

## Isaiah 24:11
People cry for wine in the streets. All joy turns to gloom. Rejoicing is exiled from the land. 📖

## Isaiah 24:12
The city lies ruined. Its gate is rubble. 📖

## Isaiah 24:13
This will happen throughout the earth and among the nations. It will be like an olive tree after harvest or gleanings after a grape harvest. 📖

## Isaiah 24:14
They raise their voices and shout joyfully. From the west, they proclaim the LORD’s majesty. 📖

## Isaiah 24:15
Glorify the LORD in the east. Praise the name of the LORD, Israel’s God, in the sea’s islands. 📖

## Isaiah 24:16
We hear songs from earth’s ends: “Glory to the Righteous One!” But I said, “I am wasting away! I am wasting away! How terrible for me!” The treacherous betray and deal in treachery. 📖

## Isaiah 24:17
People of the earth, terror, pit, and snare await you. 📖

## Isaiah 24:18
Anyone fleeing panic’s sound will fall into the pit. Anyone climbing from the pit will be caught in the snare. Heaven’s windows are open, and earth’s foundations shake. 📖

## Isaiah 24:19
The earth breaks apart. It splits open and shakes violently. 📖

## Isaiah 24:20
The earth staggers like a drunkard and sways like a shack. Its rebellion weighs it down. It falls and will never rise again. 📖

## Isaiah 24:21
On that day, the LORD will punish heaven’s host above and earth’s kings below. 📖

## Isaiah 24:22
They will be gathered like prisoners in a pit. They will be confined in a dungeon and punished after many days. 📖

## Isaiah 24:23
The moon will be confounded, and the sun ashamed. The LORD of Hosts will reign on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem. He will reign in great glory before His elders. 📖
