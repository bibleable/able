## Isaiah 8:1
The LORD told me, “Take a large scroll. Write ‘Maher-shalal-hash-baz’ on it with an ordinary stylus. 📖

## Isaiah 8:2
“I will appoint trustworthy witnesses for Myself: Uriah the priest and Zechariah, Jeberekiah’s son.” 📖

## Isaiah 8:3
I had relations with the prophetess. She became pregnant and bore a son. The LORD told me, “Name him Maher-shalal-hash-baz. 📖

## Isaiah 8:4
“Before the boy can say ‘Father’ or ‘Mother,’ Assyria’s king will carry away Damascus’s wealth and Samaria’s plunder.” 📖

## Isaiah 8:5
The LORD spoke to me again. 📖

## Isaiah 8:6
“These people rejected Shiloah’s gently flowing waters. They rejoiced in Rezin and Remaliah’s son. 📖

## Isaiah 8:7
“Because of this, the Lord will bring the Euphrates’ mighty floodwaters against them, Assyria’s king and all his splendor. It will overflow its channels and banks. 📖

## Isaiah 8:8
“It will pour into Judah, swirl over it, and reach the neck. Immanuel, its spreading streams will cover your whole land! 📖

## Isaiah 8:9
“Peoples, gather together and be shattered! Distant lands, listen! Prepare for battle and be shattered! Prepare for battle and be shattered! 📖

## Isaiah 8:10
“Make a plan, but it will fail. State a proposal, but it will not happen. God is with us.” 📖

## Isaiah 8:11
The LORD spoke to me with a strong hand. He instructed me not to follow these people’s way. 📖

## Isaiah 8:12
“Do not call everything a conspiracy that these people call a conspiracy. Do not fear or dread what they fear. 📖

## Isaiah 8:13
“Regard the LORD of Hosts as holy. Fear and dread Him alone. 📖

## Isaiah 8:14
“He will be a sanctuary. But to both houses of Israel, He will be a stumbling stone and a rock of offense. To Jerusalem’s people, He will be a trap and snare. 📖

## Isaiah 8:15
“Many will stumble, fall, and be broken. They will be trapped and captured.” 📖

## Isaiah 8:16
Bind the testimony. Seal the law among my disciples. 📖

## Isaiah 8:17
I will wait for the LORD, who hides His face from Jacob’s house. I will trust Him. 📖

## Isaiah 8:18
Here I am with the children the LORD gave me. We are signs and symbols in Israel from the LORD of Hosts, who lives on Mount Zion. 📖

## Isaiah 8:19
People may tell you to consult mediums and spiritists who whisper and mutter. Should a people not consult their God? Why consult the dead for the living? 📖

## Isaiah 8:20
Go to the law and testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, they have no light of dawn. 📖

## Isaiah 8:21
They will wander the land, dejected and hungry. Hunger will make them angry. Looking upward, they will curse their king and God. 📖

## Isaiah 8:22
They will look at the earth and see only distress, darkness, and the gloom of anguish. They will be driven into complete darkness. 📖
