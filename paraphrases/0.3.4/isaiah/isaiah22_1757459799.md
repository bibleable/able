## Isaiah 22:1
This is the burden against the Valley of Vision. What troubles you now? Why have you all climbed to the rooftops? 📖

## Isaiah 22:2
You are a noisy city, a town of revelry. Your dead were not killed by swords or in battle. 📖

## Isaiah 22:3
All your rulers fled together and were captured without a bow. All your fugitives fled far away but were captured together. 📖

## Isaiah 22:4
I said, “Turn away and let me weep bitterly! Do not comfort me over the destruction of the daughter of my people.” 📖

## Isaiah 22:5
The Lord GOD of Hosts appointed a day of uproar, trampling, and confusion in the Valley of Vision. Walls will break, and cries will reach the mountains. 📖

## Isaiah 22:6
Elam takes a quiver, with chariots and horsemen. Kir uncovers the shield. 📖

## Isaiah 22:7
Chariots fill your best valleys. Horsemen stand at the gates. 📖

## Isaiah 22:8
He uncovered Judah’s defenses. On that day, you looked to the weapons in the House of the Forest. 📖

## Isaiah 22:9
You saw many breaks in the City of David’s walls. You gathered water from the lower pool. 📖

## Isaiah 22:10
You counted Jerusalem’s houses. You tore them down to strengthen the wall. 📖

## Isaiah 22:11
You built a reservoir between the walls for the ancient pool’s water. But you ignored its Maker. You did not consider the One who planned it long ago. 📖

## Isaiah 22:12
On that day, the Lord GOD of Hosts called for weeping, wailing, shaved heads, and sackcloth. 📖

## Isaiah 22:13
Instead, there is joy and gladness. People slaughter cattle and sheep, eat meat, and drink wine. “Let us eat and drink, because tomorrow we die!” 📖

## Isaiah 22:14
The LORD of Hosts revealed this for me to hear. “Until your death, this sin will never be atoned for,” says the Lord GOD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 22:15
The Lord GOD of Hosts says, “Go to Shebna, the steward in charge of the palace. Tell him, 📖

## Isaiah 22:16
“‘What are you doing here? Who authorized you to carve your tomb here? Who let you chisel it high up and cut your resting place in the rock? 📖

## Isaiah 22:17
“‘Mighty man, look! The LORD will seize you and shake you violently. 📖

## Isaiah 22:18
“‘He will roll you into a ball and throw you into a wide land. You will die there. Your glorious chariots will remain there, a disgrace to your master’s house. 📖

## Isaiah 22:19
“‘I will remove you from office. You will lose your position. 📖

## Isaiah 22:20
“‘On that day, I will call My servant Eliakim, Hilkiah’s son. 📖

## Isaiah 22:21
“‘I will dress him in your robe and tie your sash around him. I will give him your authority. He will be a father to Jerusalem’s people and Judah’s house. 📖

## Isaiah 22:22
“‘I will place the key to David’s house on his shoulder. No one can shut what he opens. No one can open what he shuts. 📖

## Isaiah 22:23
“‘I will drive him like a peg into a firm place. He will be a throne of glory for his father’s house. 📖

## Isaiah 22:24
“‘All the glory of his father’s house will hang on him. This includes descendants and offshoots, all the lesser vessels, from bowls to every kind of jar. 📖

## Isaiah 22:25
“‘On that day,’ declares the LORD of Hosts, ‘the peg in the firm place will give way. It will be cut off and fall. Its load will be cut down.’” The LORD has spoken. 📖
