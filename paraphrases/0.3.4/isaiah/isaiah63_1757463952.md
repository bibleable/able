## Isaiah 63:1
Who comes from Edom and Bozrah with crimson-stained clothes? Who wears splendor and marches in great strength? “I proclaim vindication. I am mighty to save.” 📖

## Isaiah 63:2
Why are Your clothes red like someone treading a winepress? 📖

## Isaiah 63:3
“I trod the winepress alone. No one from the nations joined Me. I trampled them in anger and fury. Their blood spattered My garments and stained all My clothes. 📖

## Isaiah 63:4
“Vengeance’s day was in My heart. My redemption’s year had come. 📖

## Isaiah 63:5
“I looked and found no helper. I was appalled no one assisted. My arm brought Me salvation. My wrath upheld Me. 📖

## Isaiah 63:6
“I trampled nations in anger. My wrath made them drunk. I poured their blood on the ground.” 📖

## Isaiah 63:7
I will proclaim the LORD’s loving devotion and praiseworthy deeds. He did many good things for us, for Israel’s house. His great compassion and loving devotion brought them. 📖

## Isaiah 63:8
He said, “They are certainly My people, sons who will not be disloyal.” He became their Savior. 📖

## Isaiah 63:9
He too was afflicted in all their distress. The Angel of His Presence saved them. In love and compassion, He redeemed them. He lifted and carried them throughout ancient days. 📖

## Isaiah 63:10
They rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit. He turned and became their enemy. He fought against them Himself. 📖

## Isaiah 63:11
His people remembered ancient days, Moses’ days. Where is the One who brought them through the sea with His flock’s shepherds? Where is the One who put His Holy Spirit among them? 📖

## Isaiah 63:12
Where is the One whose glorious arm led them by Moses’ right hand? He divided waters before them to gain everlasting renown. 📖

## Isaiah 63:13
He led them through the depths like a horse in the wilderness. They did not stumble. 📖

## Isaiah 63:14
Like cattle descending to a valley, the LORD’s Spirit gave them rest. You led Your people this way to make Yourself a glorious name. 📖

## Isaiah 63:15
Look down from Your holy, glorious home in heaven. Where are Your zeal and might? You restrain Your yearning and compassion for me. 📖

## Isaiah 63:16
You are our Father, although Abraham does not know us and Israel does not acknowledge us. LORD, You are our Father. Your name is our Redeemer from Everlasting. 📖

## Isaiah 63:17
LORD, why make us stray from Your ways and harden our hearts against fearing You? Return for Your servants’ sake, the tribes of Your heritage. 📖

## Isaiah 63:18
Your people held Your holy place briefly. Our enemies trampled Your sanctuary. 📖

## Isaiah 63:19
We became like people You never ruled, like those never called by Your name. 📖
