## Isaiah 60:1
Rise and shine! Your light has come. The LORD’s glory rises over you. 📖

## Isaiah 60:2
Darkness covers earth, and thick darkness covers peoples. But the LORD will rise over you. His glory will appear above you. 📖

## Isaiah 60:3
Nations will come to your light. Kings will come to your bright dawn. 📖

## Isaiah 60:4
Look up and around. They all gather and come to you. Your sons come from afar. Your daughters will be carried in arms. 📖

## Isaiah 60:5
You will look and shine with joy. Your heart will tremble and swell with joy. The sea’s riches and nations’ wealth will come to you. 📖

## Isaiah 60:6
Camel caravans will cover your land, young camels from Midian and Ephah. Everyone from Sheba will bring gold and frankincense. They will proclaim the LORD’s praise. 📖

## Isaiah 60:7
All Kedar’s flocks will gather to you. Nebaioth’s rams will serve you and be accepted on My altar. I will adorn My glorious house. 📖

## Isaiah 60:8
Who are these flying like clouds, like doves to their shelters? 📖

## Isaiah 60:9
Islands will wait for Me, with Tarshish’s ships leading. They will bring your children from afar with silver and gold. This will honor the LORD your God, Israel’s Holy One, because He glorified you. 📖

## Isaiah 60:10
Foreigners will rebuild your walls. Their kings will serve you. I struck you in anger, but in favor I will show mercy. 📖

## Isaiah 60:11
Your gates will stay open day and night. Nations’ wealth will enter, with kings led in procession. 📖

## Isaiah 60:12
Any nation or kingdom refusing to serve you will perish. It will be completely destroyed. 📖

## Isaiah 60:13
Lebanon’s glory will come, cypress, elm, and boxwood together, to adorn My sanctuary. I will glorify the place of My feet. 📖

## Isaiah 60:14
Your oppressors’ sons will bow to you. Everyone who reviled you will fall facedown at your feet. They will call you the City of the LORD, Zion of Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Isaiah 60:15
You were abandoned and despised, with no one passing through. I will make you an everlasting pride, a joy through every age. 📖

## Isaiah 60:16
You will drink nations’ milk and nurse at royalty’s breasts. You will know I am the LORD, your Savior and Redeemer, Jacob’s Mighty One. 📖

## Isaiah 60:17
I will bring gold for bronze, silver for iron, bronze for wood, and iron for stones. I will make peace your governor and righteousness your ruler. 📖

## Isaiah 60:18
Violence, ruin, and destruction will no longer be heard within your land’s borders. You will name your walls Salvation and gates Praise. 📖

## Isaiah 60:19
The sun and moon will no longer give you daylight or night brightness. The LORD will be your everlasting light. Your God will be your splendor. 📖

## Isaiah 60:20
Your sun will never set, and your moon will never wane. The LORD will be your everlasting light. Your sorrow’s days will end. 📖

## Isaiah 60:21
All your people will be righteous and possess the land forever. They are My planted branch, My hands’ work, for My glory. 📖

## Isaiah 60:22
Your least will become a thousand. Your smallest will become a mighty nation. I am the LORD. At its time, I will do this quickly. 📖
