## Isaiah 48:1
“Jacob’s house, listen! You bear Israel’s name and descend from Judah. You swear by the LORD’s name and invoke Israel’s God. But you do not do this truthfully or righteously. 📖

## Isaiah 48:2
“You name yourselves after the holy city and rely on Israel’s God. His name is the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 48:3
“I announced former things long ago. My mouth proclaimed them. Suddenly I acted, and they happened. 📖

## Isaiah 48:4
“I knew your stubbornness. Your neck is iron, and your forehead bronze. 📖

## Isaiah 48:5
“I announced them long before they happened. You could not say, ‘My idol did this. My carved image and cast god ordered it.’ 📖

## Isaiah 48:6
“You heard all this. Look at it! Will you not acknowledge it? Now I will tell you new things, hidden things you do not know. 📖

## Isaiah 48:7
“They are created now, not long ago. You had not heard them before today. You cannot say, ‘I knew them already!’ 📖

## Isaiah 48:8
“You never heard or understood. Your ears were closed long ago. I knew your deceit. You were called a rebel from birth. 📖

## Isaiah 48:9
“For My name’s sake, I delay My wrath. For My praise, I restrain it so you are not cut off. 📖

## Isaiah 48:10
“I refined you, but not as silver. I tested you in affliction’s furnace. 📖

## Isaiah 48:11
“For My own sake, My very own sake, I will act. How can I let Myself be defamed? I will not give My glory to another. 📖

## Isaiah 48:12
“Jacob and Israel whom I called, listen. I am He, the first and last. 📖

## Isaiah 48:13
“My hand founded earth. My right hand spread heaven. When I call, they stand together. 📖

## Isaiah 48:14
“Gather and listen. Which idol predicted this? The LORD’s chosen ally will fulfill His desire against Babylon. His arm will oppose the Chaldeans. 📖

## Isaiah 48:15
“I Myself spoke and called him. I brought him. His mission will succeed. 📖

## Isaiah 48:16
“Come near and listen. From the beginning, I did not speak secretly. I was there from the time it happened.” Now the Lord GOD sent me with His Spirit. 📖

## Isaiah 48:17
The LORD your Redeemer, Israel’s Holy One, says, “I am the LORD your God. I teach you for your benefit and guide your way. 📖

## Isaiah 48:18
“If only you had obeyed My commands! Your peace would have been like a river. Your righteousness would have been like sea waves. 📖

## Isaiah 48:19
“Your descendants would have been countless as sand, your offspring as numerous as its grains. Their name would never be cut off or removed from My presence.” 📖

## Isaiah 48:20
Leave Babylon! Flee the Chaldeans! Announce joyfully to earth’s ends, “The LORD redeemed His servant Jacob!” 📖

## Isaiah 48:21
They did not thirst when He led them through deserts. He made water flow from rock. He split rock, and water gushed out. 📖

## Isaiah 48:22
The LORD says, “There is no peace for the wicked.” 📖
