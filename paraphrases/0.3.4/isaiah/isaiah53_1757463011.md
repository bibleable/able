## Isaiah 53:1
Who believed our message? To whom was the LORD’s arm revealed? 📖

## Isaiah 53:2
He grew before Him like a tender shoot or a root from dry ground. He had no stately form or majesty to attract us, no beauty to make us desire Him. 📖

## Isaiah 53:3
People despised and rejected Him. He was a man of sorrows who knew grief. People hid their faces from Him. We despised Him and gave Him no esteem. 📖

## Isaiah 53:4
He certainly took our infirmities and carried our sorrows. But we thought God struck and afflicted Him. 📖

## Isaiah 53:5
He was pierced for our transgressions and crushed for our wrongdoing. He bore the punishment that brought us peace. His stripes heal us. 📖

## Isaiah 53:6
We all strayed like sheep. Each followed his own way. The LORD laid all our wrongdoing on Him. 📖

## Isaiah 53:7
He was oppressed and afflicted but did not open His mouth. He was led like a lamb to slaughter. Like a sheep silent before shearers, He did not speak. 📖

## Isaiah 53:8
Oppression and judgment took Him away. Who can recount His descendants? He was cut off from the land of the living. He was stricken for My people’s transgression. 📖

## Isaiah 53:9
He was assigned a grave with the wicked and with a rich man in death. Yet He committed no violence. His mouth held no deceit. 📖

## Isaiah 53:10
The LORD willed to crush Him and make Him suffer. When His soul becomes a guilt offering, He will see offspring and prolong His days. The LORD’s good pleasure will prosper in His hand. 📖

## Isaiah 53:11
After His soul’s anguish, He will see life’s light and be satisfied. By His knowledge, My righteous Servant will justify many. He will bear their wrongdoing. 📖

## Isaiah 53:12
I will give Him a portion with the great. He will divide plunder with the strong because He poured out His life to death. He was counted among transgressors. Yet He bore many people’s sin and interceded for transgressors. 📖
