## Isaiah 40:1
Your God says, “Comfort, comfort My people. 📖

## Isaiah 40:2
“Speak tenderly to Jerusalem. Announce that her forced labor has ended and her wrongdoing is pardoned. The LORD’s hand has given her double for all her sins.” 📖

## Isaiah 40:3
A voice calls, “Prepare the LORD’s way in the wilderness. Make a straight highway for our God in the desert. 📖

## Isaiah 40:4
“Every valley will rise. Every mountain and hill will be lowered. Uneven ground will become smooth, and rugged land will become a plain. 📖

## Isaiah 40:5
“The LORD’s glory will be revealed. All humanity will see it together.” The LORD’s mouth has spoken. 📖

## Isaiah 40:6
A voice says, “Cry out!” I ask, “What should I cry?” “All flesh is like grass. All its glory is like field flowers. 📖

## Isaiah 40:7
“Grass withers and flowers fall when the LORD’s breath blows on them. The people are indeed grass. 📖

## Isaiah 40:8
“Grass withers and flowers fall. But our God’s word stands forever.” 📖

## Isaiah 40:9
Zion, herald of good news, climb a high mountain! Jerusalem, herald of good news, raise your voice loudly! Do not fear. Tell Judah’s cities, “Here is your God!” 📖

## Isaiah 40:10
The Lord GOD comes with might. His arm establishes His rule. His reward and repayment are with Him. 📖

## Isaiah 40:11
He tends His flock like a shepherd. He gathers lambs in His arms and carries them close to His heart. He gently leads nursing ewes. 📖

## Isaiah 40:12
Who measured waters in his cupped hand or marked heaven with his hand’s span? Who held earth’s dust in a basket? Who weighed mountains on scales and hills in a balance? 📖

## Isaiah 40:13
Who directed the LORD’s Spirit? Who informed Him as His counselor? 📖

## Isaiah 40:14
Whom did He consult for enlightenment? Who taught Him justice’s paths? Who gave Him knowledge and understanding? 📖

## Isaiah 40:15
Nations are like a bucket’s drop or dust on scales. He lifts islands like fine dust. 📖

## Isaiah 40:16
Lebanon cannot supply enough fuel. Its animals cannot supply a sufficient burnt offering. 📖

## Isaiah 40:17
All nations are nothing before Him. He regards them as nothingness and emptiness. 📖

## Isaiah 40:18
To whom will you compare God? What image resembles Him? 📖

## Isaiah 40:19
An idol cast by a craftsman, covered with gold by a metalworker, and fitted with silver chains? 📖

## Isaiah 40:20
Someone unable to afford that offering chooses wood that will not rot. He finds a skilled craftsman to make an idol that will not fall. 📖

## Isaiah 40:21
Do you not know? Have you not heard? Were you not told from the beginning? Have you not understood since earth’s foundation? 📖

## Isaiah 40:22
He sits enthroned above earth’s circle. Its people are like grasshoppers. He stretches heaven like a curtain and spreads it like a tent to live in. 📖

## Isaiah 40:23
He reduces princes to nothing. He makes earth’s rulers meaningless. 📖

## Isaiah 40:24
They are barely planted or sown, barely rooted, when He blows on them. They wither. A whirlwind sweeps them away like stubble. 📖

## Isaiah 40:25
The Holy One asks, “To whom will you compare Me? Who equals Me?” 📖

## Isaiah 40:26
Look up! Who made these? He leads the starry host by number and calls each by name. His great power and mighty strength ensure none is missing. 📖

## Isaiah 40:27
Jacob, Israel, why do you say, “My way is hidden from the LORD. My God ignores my claim”? 📖

## Isaiah 40:28
Do you not know or hear? The LORD is the everlasting God, Creator of earth’s ends. He never tires or grows weary. No one can search out His understanding. 📖

## Isaiah 40:29
He gives power to the faint. He strengthens the weak. 📖

## Isaiah 40:30
Even youths tire and grow weary. Young men stumble and fall. 📖

## Isaiah 40:31
Those waiting for the LORD will renew their strength. They will rise with wings like eagles. They will run without tiring and walk without fainting. 📖
