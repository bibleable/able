## Isaiah 31:1
How terrible for those seeking Egypt’s help! They rely on horses, many chariots, and many horsemen. They neither look to Israel’s Holy One nor seek the LORD. 📖

## Isaiah 31:2
He too is wise and brings disaster. He does not withdraw His words. He will rise against the wicked house and evildoers’ allies. 📖

## Isaiah 31:3
Egyptians are people, not God. Their horses are flesh, not spirit. When the LORD stretches His hand, the helper will stumble, and the person helped will fall. Both will perish together. 📖

## Isaiah 31:4
The LORD told me, “A lion roars over its prey. A band of shepherds cannot terrify it with shouting or subdue it with noise. In the same way, the LORD of Hosts will come down to fight on Mount Zion and its heights. 📖

## Isaiah 31:5
“Like birds hovering overhead, the LORD of Hosts will protect Jerusalem. He will shield and rescue it. He will pass over and preserve it.” 📖

## Isaiah 31:6
Children of Israel, return to the One you rebelled against so openly. 📖

## Isaiah 31:7
On that day, each of you will reject the silver and gold idols your hands sinfully made. 📖

## Isaiah 31:8
“Assyria will fall by a sword that is not human. A sword not made by mortals will devour them. They will flee it. Their young men will be forced to work. 📖

## Isaiah 31:9
“Their rock will pass away in fear. Their princes will panic at the battle standard,” declares the LORD. His fire is in Zion, and His furnace is in Jerusalem. 📖
