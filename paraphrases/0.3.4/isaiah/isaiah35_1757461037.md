## Isaiah 35:1
The wilderness and dry land will be glad. The desert will rejoice and blossom like a rose. 📖

## Isaiah 35:2
It will bloom abundantly and rejoice with singing. It will receive Lebanon’s glory and Carmel and Sharon’s splendor. They will see the LORD’s glory, our God’s splendor. 📖

## Isaiah 35:3
Strengthen limp hands and steady weak knees! 📖

## Isaiah 35:4
Tell anxious hearts, “Be strong and do not fear! Your God will come with vengeance. He will come with divine repayment to save you.” 📖

## Isaiah 35:5
Blind eyes will open. Deaf ears will hear. 📖

## Isaiah 35:6
The lame will leap like deer. Mute tongues will shout joyfully. Water will gush in the wilderness, and streams will flow in the desert. 📖

## Isaiah 35:7
Dry ground will become a pool. Thirsty land will have springs. Grass, reeds, and papyrus will grow where jackals once lay. 📖

## Isaiah 35:8
A highway called the Way of Holiness will be there. The unclean will not travel it. Only those walking in the Way will use it. Fools will not stray onto it. 📖

## Isaiah 35:9
No lion or vicious animal will go there. None will be found there. But the redeemed will walk on it. 📖

## Isaiah 35:10
The LORD’s redeemed will return to Zion singing, crowned with everlasting joy. Gladness and joy will overtake them. Sorrow and sighing will flee. 📖
