## Isaiah 17:1
This is the burden against Damascus. “Damascus is no longer a city. It has become a pile of ruins. 📖

## Isaiah 17:2
“Aroer’s cities are abandoned and left for flocks. They will lie there without fear. 📖

## Isaiah 17:3
“Ephraim’s fortress and Damascus’s rule will disappear. Aram’s survivors will be like Israel’s splendor,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 17:4
“On that day, Jacob’s splendor will fade. His body’s fat will waste away. 📖

## Isaiah 17:5
“It will be like a reaper gathering standing grain and harvesting heads with his arm. It will be like someone gleaning grain heads in the Valley of Rephaim. 📖

## Isaiah 17:6
“But gleanings will remain, like an olive tree after beating. Two or three berries remain at the top. Four or five remain on its fruitful branches,” declares the LORD, Israel’s God. 📖

## Isaiah 17:7
On that day, people will look to their Maker. They will turn their eyes to Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Isaiah 17:8
They will not look to altars their hands made. They will not look to the Asherahs and incense altars their fingers made. 📖

## Isaiah 17:9
On that day, their strong cities will be like abandoned thickets and summits. They will be left to the Israelites and complete desolation. 📖

## Isaiah 17:10
You forgot the God of your salvation. You did not remember the Rock of your shelter. You may cultivate delightful plots and plant cuttings from exotic vines. 📖

## Isaiah 17:11
You may make them grow on planting day and make seed sprout that morning. But the harvest will disappear on the day of disease and incurable pain. 📖

## Isaiah 17:12
Hear the uproar of many peoples! They rage like roaring seas and clamoring nations. They rumble like crashing mighty waters. 📖

## Isaiah 17:13
Nations rage like rushing waters. He rebukes them, and they flee far away. Wind drives them like chaff on hills, like tumbleweeds in a gale. 📖

## Isaiah 17:14
Sudden terror comes in the evening! Before morning, they are gone! This is the share and lot of those who loot and plunder us. 📖
