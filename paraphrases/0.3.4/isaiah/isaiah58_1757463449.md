## Isaiah 58:1
“Shout without holding back! Raise your voice like a ram’s horn. Tell My people their transgression and Jacob’s house its sins. 📖

## Isaiah 58:2
“Every day, they seek Me and delight in knowing My ways. They act like a righteous nation that never abandoned its God’s justice. They ask for righteous judgments. They delight in God’s nearness.” 📖

## Isaiah 58:3
“Why did we fast without You seeing? Why did we humble ourselves without You noticing?” “On your fast day, you please yourselves and oppress all your workers. 📖

## Isaiah 58:4
“You fast with quarrels and fighting, striking viciously with fists. Fasting this way will not make your voice heard above. 📖

## Isaiah 58:5
“Did I choose a fast of self-denial, bowed heads like reeds, sackcloth, and ashes? Do you call that a fast, a day acceptable to the LORD? 📖

## Isaiah 58:6
“Is My chosen fast not breaking the chains of wickedness and untying the yoke’s cords? Is it not freeing the oppressed and tearing off every yoke? 📖

## Isaiah 58:7
“Is it not sharing bread with the hungry and sheltering the poor and homeless? Is it not clothing the naked and refusing to turn from your own flesh and blood? 📖

## Isaiah 58:8
“Your light will break like dawn, and healing will come quickly. Righteousness will go before you. The LORD’s glory will guard your rear. 📖

## Isaiah 58:9
“You will call, and the LORD will answer. You will cry, and He will say, ‘Here I am.’ If you remove the yoke, accusing fingers, and malicious talk from among you, 📖

## Isaiah 58:10
“and give yourself to the hungry and satisfy the afflicted soul, your light will rise in darkness. Your night will become like noon. 📖

## Isaiah 58:11
“The LORD will always guide you. He will satisfy you in sun-scorched land and strengthen your frame. You will be like a watered garden or a spring that never fails. 📖

## Isaiah 58:12
“Your people will rebuild ancient ruins. You will restore age-old foundations. You will be called Repairer of the Breach, Restorer of the Streets of Dwelling. 📖

## Isaiah 58:13
“Suppose you stop breaking the Sabbath and seeking your pleasure on My holy day. Suppose you call the Sabbath a delight and the LORD’s holy day honorable. Suppose you honor it by avoiding your own ways, pleasures, and idle words. 📖

## Isaiah 58:14
“Then you will delight in the LORD. I will make you ride the land’s heights and feed you with your father Jacob’s heritage.” The LORD’s mouth has spoken. 📖
