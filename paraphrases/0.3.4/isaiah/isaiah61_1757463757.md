## Isaiah 61:1
The Lord GOD’s Spirit is on Me because the LORD anointed Me to preach good news to the poor. He sent Me to bind broken hearts. I will proclaim liberty to captives and freedom to prisoners. 📖

## Isaiah 61:2
I will proclaim the LORD’s year of favor and our God’s day of vengeance. I will comfort all mourners. 📖

## Isaiah 61:3
I will console Zion’s mourners. I will give a crown of beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, and a garment of praise for despair. They will be called oaks of righteousness, the LORD’s planting, for His glory. 📖

## Isaiah 61:4
They will rebuild ancient ruins and restore long-devastated places. They will renew cities ruined for many generations. 📖

## Isaiah 61:5
Strangers will feed your flocks. Foreigners will plow your fields and tend your vines. 📖

## Isaiah 61:6
You will be called the LORD’s priests and our God’s ministers. You will feed on nations’ wealth and boast in their riches. 📖

## Isaiah 61:7
My people will receive a double portion instead of shame. They will rejoice in their share instead of humiliation. They will inherit a double portion in their land. Everlasting joy will be theirs. 📖

## Isaiah 61:8
I, the LORD, love justice and hate robbery and wrongdoing. Faithfully, I will give their repayment. I will make an everlasting covenant with them. 📖

## Isaiah 61:9
Their descendants will be known among nations and peoples. Everyone seeing them will acknowledge that the LORD blessed them. 📖

## Isaiah 61:10
I will rejoice greatly in the LORD. My soul will exult in my God. He dressed me in garments of salvation and a robe of righteousness. I am like a bridegroom in a priestly headdress or a bride with jewels. 📖

## Isaiah 61:11
Earth produces growth, and gardens make seeds sprout. In the same way, the Lord GOD will make righteousness and praise sprout before all nations. 📖
