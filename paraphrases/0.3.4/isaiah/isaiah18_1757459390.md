## Isaiah 18:1
How terrible for the land of whirring wings along Cush’s rivers! 📖

## Isaiah 18:2
It sends couriers across the sea in papyrus boats. Swift messengers, go to a tall people with smooth skin. They are widely feared, a powerful nation with strange speech. Rivers divide their land. 📖

## Isaiah 18:3
Everyone living on earth will see the banner when it rises on the mountains. You will hear the ram’s horn when it sounds. 📖

## Isaiah 18:4
The LORD told me, “I will quietly watch from My dwelling. I will be like shimmering heat in sunshine, like a cloud of dew in harvest heat.” 📖

## Isaiah 18:5
Before harvest, the blossom will be gone, and the flower will become a ripening grape. Then He will cut shoots with a pruning knife. He will remove and discard branches. 📖

## Isaiah 18:6
They will all be left for mountain birds of prey and the land’s animals. Birds will feed on them in summer. All the wild animals will feed on them in winter. 📖

## Isaiah 18:7
At that time, gifts will come to the LORD of Hosts from tall people with smooth skin. They are widely feared, a powerful nation with strange speech, whose land rivers divide. They will bring gifts to Mount Zion, the place of the LORD of Hosts’ Name. 📖
