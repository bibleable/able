## Isaiah 34:1
Nations and peoples, come near and listen! Let the earth and everything filling it hear. Let the world and everything growing from it listen. 📖

## Isaiah 34:2
The LORD is angry with all nations and furious with all their armies. He will devote them to destruction and slaughter. 📖

## Isaiah 34:3
Their dead will lie unburied. Their corpses’ stench will rise. Mountains will flow with their blood. 📖

## Isaiah 34:4
All heaven’s stars will dissolve. The skies will roll up like a scroll. All their stars will fall like withered vine leaves or fig leaves. 📖

## Isaiah 34:5
My sword will drink its fill in heaven. Then it will descend on Edom, the people I devoted to destruction. 📖

## Isaiah 34:6
The LORD’s sword is bathed in blood and drips fat. It has lambs’ and goats’ blood and fat from rams’ kidneys. The LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah, a great slaughter in Edom. 📖

## Isaiah 34:7
Wild oxen will fall with them, young bulls with strong ones. Blood will drench their land. Fat will soak their soil. 📖

## Isaiah 34:8
The LORD has a day of vengeance. He has a year of repayment for Zion’s cause. 📖

## Isaiah 34:9
Edom’s streams will become tar, and her soil sulfur. Her land will become burning pitch. 📖

## Isaiah 34:10
No one will put it out, day or night. Its smoke will rise forever. It will lie desolate from generation to generation. No one will ever pass through again. 📖

## Isaiah 34:11
Desert owls and screech owls will possess it. Great owls and ravens will live there. The LORD will stretch chaos’s measuring line and destruction’s plumb line over Edom. 📖

## Isaiah 34:12
No nobles will remain to proclaim a king. All her princes will come to nothing. 📖

## Isaiah 34:13
Thorns will cover her towers. Thistles and briers will cover her fortresses. She will become a home for jackals and ostriches. 📖

## Isaiah 34:14
Desert creatures will meet hyenas. Wild goats will call to one another. The night creature will settle and find rest there. 📖

## Isaiah 34:15
The owl will nest, lay eggs, and hatch them there. She will gather her young under her shadow. Birds of prey will gather there, each with its mate. 📖

## Isaiah 34:16
Search and read the LORD’s scroll. None will be missing, and none will lack her mate. His mouth ordered it. His Spirit will gather them. 📖

## Isaiah 34:17
He allotted their portion. His hand distributed it by measure. They will possess it forever and live there from generation to generation. 📖
