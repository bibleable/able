## Isaiah 7:1
Ahaz, Jotham’s son and Uzziah’s grandson, was Judah’s king. Rezin, Aram’s king, attacked Jerusalem with Pekah, Remaliah’s son, Israel’s king. But he could not conquer the city. 📖

## Isaiah 7:2
David’s house heard that Aram had joined Ephraim. Ahaz and his people trembled like forest trees shaken by wind. 📖

## Isaiah 7:3
The LORD told Isaiah, “Take your son Shear-jashub and meet Ahaz. Go to the end of the aqueduct feeding the upper pool, on the road to the Launderer’s Field. 📖

## Isaiah 7:4
“Tell him, ‘Calm down and be quiet. Do not fear or lose heart over these two smoldering pieces of firewood. Do not fear the fierce anger of Rezin, Aram, and Remaliah’s son. 📖

## Isaiah 7:5
“‘Aram, Ephraim, and Remaliah’s son have plotted your ruin. They say, 📖

## Isaiah 7:6
“‘“Let us invade Judah, terrify it, and divide it among ourselves. We can make Tabeal’s son king over it.” 📖

## Isaiah 7:7
“‘But the Lord GOD says, “This will not arise or happen. 📖

## Isaiah 7:8
“‘“Aram’s head is Damascus, and Damascus’s head is Rezin. Within sixty-five years, Ephraim will be shattered as a people. 📖

## Isaiah 7:9
“‘“Ephraim’s head is Samaria, and Samaria’s head is Remaliah’s son. If you do not stand firmly in faith, you will not stand at all.”’” 📖

## Isaiah 7:10
The LORD spoke to Ahaz again. 📖

## Isaiah 7:11
“Ask the LORD your God for a sign. Ask from Sheol’s depths or heaven’s heights.” 📖

## Isaiah 7:12
Ahaz answered, “I will not ask or test the LORD.” 📖

## Isaiah 7:13
Isaiah said, “House of David, listen! Is trying people’s patience not enough? Will you try my God’s patience too? 📖

## Isaiah 7:14
“The Lord Himself will give you a sign. The virgin will become pregnant and bear a son. She will name Him Immanuel. 📖

## Isaiah 7:15
“By the time He knows to reject evil and choose good, He will eat curds and honey. 📖

## Isaiah 7:16
“Before the boy knows to reject evil and choose good, the land of the two kings you fear will be laid waste. 📖

## Isaiah 7:17
“The LORD will bring Assyria’s king against you, your people, and your father’s house. It will be a time unlike any since Ephraim separated from Judah.” 📖

## Isaiah 7:18
On that day, the LORD will whistle for flies from the Nile’s farthest streams. He will whistle for bees from Assyria’s land. 📖

## Isaiah 7:19
They will all settle in steep ravines, rock crevices, all the thornbushes, and watering holes. 📖

## Isaiah 7:20
On that day, the Lord will use a razor hired from beyond the Euphrates, Assyria’s king. He will shave your head and legs and remove your beard. 📖

## Isaiah 7:21
On that day, a man will raise a young cow and two sheep. 📖

## Isaiah 7:22
They will give abundant milk, so he will eat curds. Everyone remaining in the land will eat curds and honey. 📖

## Isaiah 7:23
On that day, places with a thousand vines worth a thousand silver shekels will hold only briers and thorns. 📖

## Isaiah 7:24
Men will go there with bows and arrows. Briers and thorns will cover the land. 📖

## Isaiah 7:25
You will fear briers and thorns and stop crossing hills once worked with hoes. Oxen will graze there, and sheep will trample them. 📖
