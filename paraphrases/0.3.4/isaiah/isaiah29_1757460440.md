## Isaiah 29:1
How terrible for Ariel, the city where David camped! Let your festivals return year after year. 📖

## Isaiah 29:2
I will constrain Ariel. Mourning and lamentation will come. She will be like an altar hearth before Me. 📖

## Isaiah 29:3
I will camp around you. I will surround you with towers and build siege works against you. 📖

## Isaiah 29:4
You will be brought low and speak from the ground. Dust will muffle your words. Your voice will be like a spirit from the ground. Your speech will whisper from the dust. 📖

## Isaiah 29:5
But your many enemies will become like fine dust. The ruthless crowd will become like windblown chaff. Suddenly, in an instant, 📖

## Isaiah 29:6
the LORD of Hosts will visit you with thunder, earthquakes, and loud noise. He will come with windstorm, tempest, and consuming fire. 📖

## Isaiah 29:7
All the many nations attacking Ariel will become like a dream, a night vision. This includes all who fight, besiege, and attack her. 📖

## Isaiah 29:8
A hungry man dreams of eating but wakes hungry. A thirsty man dreams of drinking but wakes weak and thirsty. All nations attacking Mount Zion will be like this. 📖

## Isaiah 29:9
Stop and be astonished. Blind yourselves and lose sight. Be drunk, but without wine. Stagger, but without strong drink. 📖

## Isaiah 29:10
The LORD poured a spirit of deep sleep on you. Prophets, He shut your eyes. Seers, He covered your heads. 📖

## Isaiah 29:11
The whole vision will be like words in a sealed scroll. Someone asked to read it will say, “I cannot. It is sealed.” 📖

## Isaiah 29:12
If someone unable to read receives the scroll, he will say, “I cannot read.” 📖

## Isaiah 29:13
The Lord said, “These people approach Me with their mouths and honor Me with their lips. But their hearts are far from Me. Their worship is only rules taught by people. 📖

## Isaiah 29:14
“I will astonish them again with wonder after wonder. The wise people’s wisdom will disappear. The intelligent people’s intelligence will be hidden.” 📖

## Isaiah 29:15
How terrible for those hiding their plans deep from the LORD! They work in darkness and say, “Who sees or knows us?” 📖

## Isaiah 29:16
You turn things upside down, treating the potter like clay. Can something formed say of its maker, “He did not make me”? Can pottery say of the potter, “He has no understanding”? 📖

## Isaiah 29:17
Soon, will Lebanon not become an orchard? Will the orchard not seem like a forest? 📖

## Isaiah 29:18
On that day, the deaf will hear the scroll’s words. The blind will see from deep darkness. 📖

## Isaiah 29:19
The humble will gain more joy in the LORD. The poor will rejoice in Israel’s Holy One. 📖

## Isaiah 29:20
The ruthless will vanish. Mockers will disappear. All who seek evil will be cut down. 📖

## Isaiah 29:21
These accuse a man with a word and trap the mediator at the gate. They use false charges to deny the innocent justice. 📖

## Isaiah 29:22
The LORD redeemed Abraham. He says about Jacob’s house, “Jacob will no longer be ashamed. His face will no longer turn pale. 📖

## Isaiah 29:23
“He will see his children around him, the work of My hands. They will honor My name and regard Jacob’s Holy One as holy. They will stand in awe of Israel’s God. 📖

## Isaiah 29:24
“Those with wayward spirits will gain understanding. Grumblers will accept instruction.” 📖
