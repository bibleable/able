## Isaiah 19:1
This is the burden against Egypt. The LORD rides a swift cloud toward Egypt. Egypt’s idols will tremble before Him. The Egyptians’ hearts will melt within them. 📖

## Isaiah 19:2
“I will stir Egyptians against one another. Brother will fight brother, neighbor will fight neighbor, city will fight city, and kingdom will fight kingdom. 📖

## Isaiah 19:3
“Egypt’s spirit will be emptied out, and I will frustrate their plans. They will turn to idols, dead spirits, mediums, and spiritists. 📖

## Isaiah 19:4
“I will hand Egypt to a harsh master. A fierce king will rule them,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 19:5
The Nile’s waters will dry up. Its riverbed will be dry and empty. 📖

## Isaiah 19:6
Canals will stink, and Egypt’s streams will trickle and dry up. Reeds and rushes will wither. 📖

## Isaiah 19:7
Bulrushes beside the Nile and its mouth will wither. All fields planted along it will wither, blow away, and disappear. 📖

## Isaiah 19:8
Fishermen will mourn. Everyone casting hooks into the Nile will lament. Those spreading nets will waste away. 📖

## Isaiah 19:9
Flax workers will be dismayed. Fine linen weavers will turn pale. 📖

## Isaiah 19:10
Cloth workers will be dejected. All hired workers will be sick at heart. 📖

## Isaiah 19:11
Zoan’s princes are fools. Pharaoh’s wise counselors give senseless advice. How can you tell Pharaoh, “I am wise, a son of eastern kings”? 📖

## Isaiah 19:12
Where are your wise men now? Let them reveal the LORD of Hosts’ plans against Egypt. 📖

## Isaiah 19:13
Zoan’s princes became fools, and Memphis’s princes are deceived. Her tribes’ cornerstones led Egypt astray. 📖

## Isaiah 19:14
The LORD poured a spirit of confusion into Egypt. She is led astray in everything, like a drunkard staggering through his vomit. 📖

## Isaiah 19:15
Egypt can do nothing, neither head nor tail, neither palm nor reed. 📖

## Isaiah 19:16
On that day, Egyptians will be like women. They will tremble in fear when the LORD of Hosts waves His raised hand against them. 📖

## Isaiah 19:17
Judah’s land will terrify Egypt. Every mention of Judah will make Egypt tremble at the LORD of Hosts’ plans against it. 📖

## Isaiah 19:18
On that day, five Egyptian cities will speak Canaan’s language and swear allegiance to the LORD of Hosts. One will be called the City of the Sun. 📖

## Isaiah 19:19
On that day, an altar to the LORD will stand in Egypt’s center. A pillar to the LORD will stand near its border. 📖

## Isaiah 19:20
It will be a sign and witness to the LORD of Hosts in Egypt. They will cry to Him because of oppressors. He will send a savior and defender to rescue them. 📖

## Isaiah 19:21
The LORD will make Himself known to Egypt. On that day, Egypt will acknowledge Him. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings. They will make vows to the LORD and keep them. 📖

## Isaiah 19:22
The LORD will strike Egypt with plague, but He will heal them. They will turn to Him. He will hear their prayers and heal them. 📖

## Isaiah 19:23
On that day, a highway will connect Egypt and Assyria. Assyrians will go to Egypt, and Egyptians will go to Assyria. They will worship together. 📖

## Isaiah 19:24
On that day, Israel will join Egypt and Assyria in a three-party alliance. They will be a blessing on earth. 📖

## Isaiah 19:25
The LORD of Hosts will bless them. He will say, “Blessed be Egypt, My people, Assyria, My handiwork, and Israel, My inheritance.” 📖
