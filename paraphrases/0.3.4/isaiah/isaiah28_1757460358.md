## Isaiah 28:1
How terrible for the majestic crown of Ephraim’s drunkards! Their glorious splendor is a fading flower on the summit above the fertile valley. It is the pride of those overcome by wine. 📖

## Isaiah 28:2
The Lord has someone strong and mighty. Like hail, a destructive storm, or a flooding downpour, he will smash that crown to the ground. 📖

## Isaiah 28:3
The majestic crown of Ephraim’s drunkards will be trampled. 📖

## Isaiah 28:4
His beautiful splendor is a fading flower above the fertile valley. It will be like a ripe fig before summer harvest. Anyone seeing it will take and swallow it. 📖

## Isaiah 28:5
On that day, the LORD of Hosts will be a glorious crown and splendid diadem for His people’s survivors. 📖

## Isaiah 28:6
He will be a spirit of justice for the one judging. He will strengthen those who repel attacks at the gate. 📖

## Isaiah 28:7
These too stagger from wine and strong drink. Priests and prophets reel, confused by wine. They stumble from strong drink. Their visions are muddled, and their judgments falter. 📖

## Isaiah 28:8
Vomit covers all their tables. No place is clean. 📖

## Isaiah 28:9
Whom is He teaching? To whom is He explaining His message? To infants just weaned? To babies removed from the breast? 📖

## Isaiah 28:10
They hear, “Order on order, order on order. Line on line, line on line. A little here, a little there.” 📖

## Isaiah 28:11
He will speak to these people with mocking lips and foreign languages. 📖

## Isaiah 28:12
He told them, “This is the place of rest. Let the weary rest here.” But they refused to listen. 📖

## Isaiah 28:13
The LORD’s word will become, “Order on order, order on order. Line on line, line on line. A little here, a little there.” They will stumble backward, be injured, trapped, and captured. 📖

## Isaiah 28:14
Mockers ruling these people in Jerusalem, hear the LORD’s word. 📖

## Isaiah 28:15
You said, “We made a covenant with death and an agreement with Sheol. The overwhelming scourge will not touch us when it passes. Lies are our shelter. Falsehood is our hiding place.” 📖

## Isaiah 28:16
The Lord GOD says, “I lay a stone in Zion. It is tested, a precious cornerstone and sure foundation. The one who believes will never be shaken. 📖

## Isaiah 28:17
“I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the level. Hail will sweep away your shelter of lies. Water will flood your hiding place. 📖

## Isaiah 28:18
“Your covenant with death will dissolve. Your agreement with Sheol will not stand. The overwhelming scourge will trample you when it passes. 📖

## Isaiah 28:19
“It will carry you away whenever it passes. It will sweep through every morning, by day and night.” Understanding this message will bring complete terror. 📖

## Isaiah 28:20
The bed is too short to stretch on. The blanket is too small to wrap around you. 📖

## Isaiah 28:21
The LORD will rise as at Mount Perazim. He will rouse Himself as in the Valley of Gibeon. He will do His strange work and perform His disturbing task. 📖

## Isaiah 28:22
Stop mocking, or your shackles will grow heavier. I heard the Lord GOD of Hosts decree destruction against the whole land. 📖

## Isaiah 28:23
Listen to my voice. Pay attention to what I say. 📖

## Isaiah 28:24
Does a farmer plow for planting every day? Does he keep loosening and harrowing the soil? 📖

## Isaiah 28:25
After leveling it, does he not sow caraway and scatter cumin? He plants wheat in rows, barley in plots, and rye along its border. 📖

## Isaiah 28:26
His God instructs and teaches him properly. 📖

## Isaiah 28:27
Caraway is not threshed with a sledge. A cart wheel does not roll over cumin. Caraway is beaten with a stick, and cumin with a rod. 📖

## Isaiah 28:28
Bread grain must be ground, but no one threshes it forever. Cart wheels roll over it, but horses do not crush it. 📖

## Isaiah 28:29
This also comes from the LORD of Hosts. His counsel is wonderful, and His wisdom is excellent. 📖
