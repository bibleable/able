## Isaiah 3:1
The Lord GOD of Hosts will remove supply and support from Jerusalem and Judah. He will remove all their food and water. 📖

## Isaiah 3:2
He will remove mighty men, warriors, judges, prophets, soothsayers, and elders. 📖

## Isaiah 3:3
He will remove commanders of fifty, dignitaries, counselors, skilled magicians, and clever enchanters. 📖

## Isaiah 3:4
“I will make boys their leaders. Children will rule them.” 📖

## Isaiah 3:5
People will oppress one another, neighbor against neighbor. The young will rise against the old. The dishonorable will rise against the honorable. 📖

## Isaiah 3:6
A man will seize his brother in their father’s house. “You have a cloak. Be our leader! Take charge of this pile of rubble.” 📖

## Isaiah 3:7
On that day, he will shout, “I am not a healer. My house has no food or clothing. Do not make me the people’s leader!” 📖

## Isaiah 3:8
Jerusalem stumbled, and Judah fell. Their words and actions opposed the LORD and defied His glorious presence. 📖

## Isaiah 3:9
Their faces testify against them. Like Sodom, they display their sin instead of hiding it. How terrible for them! They brought disaster on themselves. 📖

## Isaiah 3:10
Tell the righteous that things will go well for them. They will enjoy the fruit of their work. 📖

## Isaiah 3:11
How terrible for the wicked! Disaster is on them. They will receive payment for what their hands did. 📖

## Isaiah 3:12
Youths oppress My people, and women rule them. My people, your guides mislead you. They turn you away from your paths. 📖

## Isaiah 3:13
The LORD rises to bring His case. He stands to judge the people. 📖

## Isaiah 3:14
The LORD accuses His people’s elders and leaders. “You devoured the vineyard. Your houses hold plunder taken from the poor. 📖

## Isaiah 3:15
“Why do you crush My people and grind the poor people’s faces?” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts. 📖

## Isaiah 3:16
The LORD says, “Zion’s daughters are proud. They walk with raised heads and seductive eyes. They prance and skip, jingling their ankle bracelets. 📖

## Isaiah 3:17
“Because of this, the Lord will bring sores on their heads. The LORD will make the foreheads of Zion’s daughters bare.” 📖

## Isaiah 3:18
On that day, the Lord will take their finery: anklets, headbands, and crescents. 📖

## Isaiah 3:19
He will take their pendants, bracelets, and veils. 📖

## Isaiah 3:20
He will take their headdresses, ankle chains, sashes, perfume bottles, and charms. 📖

## Isaiah 3:21
He will take their signet rings and nose rings. 📖

## Isaiah 3:22
He will take their festive robes, capes, cloaks, and purses. 📖

## Isaiah 3:23
He will take their mirrors, linen clothes, tiaras, and shawls. 📖

## Isaiah 3:24
Stench will replace fragrance, and rope will replace belts. Baldness will replace styled hair, and sackcloth will replace fine clothes. Shame will replace beauty. 📖

## Isaiah 3:25
Your men will fall by the sword. Your warriors will fall in battle. 📖

## Isaiah 3:26
Zion’s gates will mourn and lament. She will sit on the ground with nothing left. 📖
