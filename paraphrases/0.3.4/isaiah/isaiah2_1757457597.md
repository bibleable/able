## Isaiah 2:1
This message about Judah and Jerusalem was revealed to Isaiah, Amoz’s son. 📖

## Isaiah 2:2
In the last days, the mountain of the LORD’s house will stand as the chief mountain, raised above the hills. All nations will stream to it. 📖

## Isaiah 2:3
Many peoples will say, “Let us go to the LORD’s mountain, to the house of Jacob’s God. He will teach us His ways so that we can follow His paths.” The law will come from Zion. The LORD’s word will come from Jerusalem. 📖

## Isaiah 2:4
He will judge between nations and settle disputes for many peoples. They will beat swords into plowshares and spears into pruning hooks. Nations will no longer fight one another with swords. They will no longer train for war. 📖

## Isaiah 2:5
House of Jacob, come. Let us walk in the LORD’s light. 📖

## Isaiah 2:6
You abandoned Your people, Jacob’s house. They are full of eastern influences. They practice fortune-telling like the Philistines. They join hands with foreigners’ children. 📖

## Isaiah 2:7
Their land is full of silver and gold, with endless treasures. It is full of horses, with endless chariots. 📖

## Isaiah 2:8
Their land is full of idols. They bow to things their hands and fingers made. 📖

## Isaiah 2:9
People are brought low and humbled. Do not forgive them! 📖

## Isaiah 2:10
Go into the rocks and hide in the dust. Hide from the LORD’s terror and the splendor of His majesty. 📖

## Isaiah 2:11
People’s proud looks and lofty pride will be humbled. On that day, the LORD alone will be exalted. 📖

## Isaiah 2:12
The Day of the LORD of Hosts will come against everyone proud and lofty. Everything exalted will be humbled. 📖

## Isaiah 2:13
It will come against all Lebanon’s tall, lofty cedars and all Bashan’s oaks. 📖

## Isaiah 2:14
It will come against all tall mountains and high hills. 📖

## Isaiah 2:15
It will come against every high tower and fortified wall. 📖

## Isaiah 2:16
It will come against every ship of Tarshish and every stately vessel. 📖

## Isaiah 2:17
People’s pride and loftiness will be humbled. On that day, the LORD alone will be exalted. 📖

## Isaiah 2:18
The idols will disappear completely. 📖

## Isaiah 2:19
When the LORD rises to shake the earth, people will flee into rock caves and holes in the ground. They will flee His terror and the splendor of His majesty. 📖

## Isaiah 2:20
On that day, people will throw their silver and gold idols to moles and bats. These are the idols they made to worship. 📖

## Isaiah 2:21
When the LORD rises to shake the earth, they will flee into rock caverns and cracks in cliffs. They will flee His terror and the splendor of His majesty. 📖

## Isaiah 2:22
Stop trusting people, who have only breath in their nostrils. What do they amount to? 📖
