## Isaiah 16:1
Send tribute lambs to the land’s ruler. Send them from Sela in the desert to the Daughter of Zion’s mountain. 📖

## Isaiah 16:2
Moab’s daughters at the Arnon’s fords are like fluttering birds pushed from a nest. 📖

## Isaiah 16:3
“Give us counsel and a decision. Shelter us at noon with shade dark as night. Hide refugees. Do not betray anyone fleeing. 📖

## Isaiah 16:4
“Let my fugitives stay with you. Shelter Moab from the destroyer.” The oppressor will go, destruction will end, and oppressors will disappear from the land. 📖

## Isaiah 16:5
Then a throne will be established in David’s tent through loving devotion. A judge will sit on it faithfully. He will seek justice and hasten righteousness. 📖

## Isaiah 16:6
We have heard of Moab’s great pride, conceit, and overflowing arrogance. But his boasting is empty. 📖

## Isaiah 16:7
Let Moab wail together for Moab. You who are completely stricken, moan for Kir-hareseth’s raisin cakes. 📖

## Isaiah 16:8
Heshbon’s fields and Sibmah’s grapevines have withered. The nations’ rulers trampled its best vines. Those vines reached Jazer and spread toward the desert. Their shoots spread across the sea. 📖

## Isaiah 16:9
I weep with Jazer for Sibmah’s vines. My tears drench Heshbon and Elealeh. Triumphant shouts over your summer fruit and harvest have fallen silent. 📖

## Isaiah 16:10
Joy and gladness have left the orchard. No one sings or shouts in the vineyards. No one treads grapes in the winepresses. I ended the cheering. 📖

## Isaiah 16:11
My heart laments like a harp for Moab. My inmost being laments for Kir-heres. 📖

## Isaiah 16:12
Moab will appear on the high place and wear himself out. He will enter his sanctuary to pray. But it will do him no good. 📖

## Isaiah 16:13
The LORD spoke this message about Moab earlier. 📖

## Isaiah 16:14
Now the LORD says, “In three years, counted as a hired worker counts them, Moab’s splendor and many people will face contempt. Only a few weak survivors will remain.” 📖
