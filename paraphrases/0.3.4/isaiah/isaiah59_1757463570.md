## Isaiah 59:1
The LORD’s arm is not too short to save. His ear is not too dull to hear. 📖

## Isaiah 59:2
Your wrongdoing built barriers between you and God. Your sins hid His face so He does not hear. 📖

## Isaiah 59:3
Blood stains your hands, and wrongdoing stains your fingers. Your lips lied. Your tongue mutters injustice. 📖

## Isaiah 59:4
No one calls for justice or pleads honestly. They rely on empty pleas and lies. They conceive mischief and bear wrongdoing. 📖

## Isaiah 59:5
They hatch vipers’ eggs and weave spiderwebs. Anyone eating their eggs dies. Break one, and a viper hatches. 📖

## Isaiah 59:6
Their webs cannot make clothes. Their works cannot cover them. Their deeds are sinful. Their hands commit violence. 📖

## Isaiah 59:7
Their feet run to evil. They hurry to shed innocent blood. Their thoughts are sinful. Ruin and destruction follow them. 📖

## Isaiah 59:8
They do not know peace’s way. Their tracks have no justice. They made crooked paths. No one following them will know peace. 📖

## Isaiah 59:9
Justice stays far from us. Righteousness does not reach us. We seek light but find darkness. We hope for brightness but walk in gloom. 📖

## Isaiah 59:10
We feel along walls like the blind, like people without eyes. We stumble at noon as at twilight. Among the vigorous, we are like the dead. 📖

## Isaiah 59:11
We all growl like bears and moan like doves. We hope for justice but find none. We hope for salvation, but it is far away. 📖

## Isaiah 59:12
Our many transgressions lie before You. Our sins testify against us. Our transgressions stay with us, and we know our wrongdoing. 📖

## Isaiah 59:13
We rebel, deny the LORD, and turn from our God. We speak oppression and revolt. Our hearts conceive and utter lies. 📖

## Isaiah 59:14
Justice turns away, and righteousness stands far off. Truth stumbled in the public square. Honesty cannot enter. 📖

## Isaiah 59:15
Truth is missing. Whoever abandons evil becomes prey. The LORD saw no justice, and it displeased Him. 📖

## Isaiah 59:16
He saw no one and was amazed no one interceded. His own arm brought salvation. His righteousness sustained Him. 📖

## Isaiah 59:17
He wore righteousness like a breastplate and salvation’s helmet on His head. He wore vengeance as clothes and wrapped Himself in zeal’s cloak. 📖

## Isaiah 59:18
He will repay their deeds with fury for enemies, retribution for foes, and repayment for islands. 📖

## Isaiah 59:19
They will fear the LORD’s name in the west and His glory in the east. He will come like a raging flood driven by the LORD’s breath. 📖

## Isaiah 59:20
The LORD declares, “The Redeemer will come to Zion, to Jacob’s people who turn from transgression. 📖

## Isaiah 59:21
“This is My covenant with them,” says the LORD. “My Spirit will not leave you. My words in your mouth will never leave your mouth, your children’s mouths, or your grandchildren’s mouths. This is from now and forever,” says the LORD. 📖
