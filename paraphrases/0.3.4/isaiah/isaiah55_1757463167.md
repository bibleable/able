## Isaiah 55:1
“All thirsty people, come to the waters! You without money, come, buy, and eat! Buy wine and milk without money or cost! 📖

## Isaiah 55:2
“Why spend money on what is not bread or labor on what cannot satisfy? Listen carefully to Me. Eat good food, and your soul will delight in the richest food. 📖

## Isaiah 55:3
“Come and listen so your soul can live. I will make an everlasting covenant with you, My loving devotion promised to David. 📖

## Isaiah 55:4
“I made him a witness, leader, and commander for nations and peoples. 📖

## Isaiah 55:5
“You will call a nation you do not know. Nations not knowing you will run to you. The LORD your God, Israel’s Holy One, gave you glory.” 📖

## Isaiah 55:6
Seek the LORD while He can be found. Call Him while He is near. 📖

## Isaiah 55:7
Let the wicked abandon their ways and the unrighteous their thoughts. Let them return to the LORD for compassion, to our God for free pardon. 📖

## Isaiah 55:8
The LORD declares, “My thoughts are not yours. Your ways are not Mine. 📖

## Isaiah 55:9
“As heaven is higher than earth, My ways and thoughts are higher than yours. 📖

## Isaiah 55:10
“Rain and snow fall from heaven. Before returning, they water earth, make it bud and sprout, and provide seed to sow and food to eat. 📖

## Isaiah 55:11
“In the same way, My spoken word will not return empty. It will accomplish My pleasure and prosper where I send it. 📖

## Isaiah 55:12
“You will leave joyfully and be led in peace. Mountains and hills will sing before you. All field trees will clap their hands. 📖

## Isaiah 55:13
“Cypresses will replace thornbushes, and myrtles will replace briers. This will make a name for the LORD. It will be an everlasting sign that will never be destroyed.” 📖
