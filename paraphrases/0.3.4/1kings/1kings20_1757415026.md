## 1 Kings 20:1
Ben-hadad king of Aram gathered his whole army. Thirty-two kings joined him with horses and chariots. He marched to Samaria, surrounded it, and fought against it. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:2
He sent messengers into the city to Ahab king of Israel, 👥
## 1 Kings 20:3
saying, “Ben-hadad says, ‘Your silver and gold are mine. Your best wives and children are mine!’” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 20:4
Israel’s king answered, “As you say, my lord the king. I and everything I have are yours.” 💬
## 1 Kings 20:5
The messengers returned and said, “Ben-hadad says, ‘I sent my demand for your silver, gold, wives, and children. 💬
## 1 Kings 20:6
About this time tomorrow, I will send my servants to search your palace and your servants’ houses. They will take away everything precious to you.’” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 20:7
Israel’s king gathered all the land’s elders. He said, “See how this man seeks trouble. I did not refuse his demand for my wives, children, silver, and gold.” 👥
## 1 Kings 20:8
All the elders and people said, “Do not listen to him or accept his terms.” 💬
## 1 Kings 20:9
Ahab told Ben-hadad’s messengers, “Tell my lord the king, ‘I will do everything you first demanded of your servant. But I cannot do this.’” They left and told Ben-hadad. 💬
## 1 Kings 20:10
Ben-hadad sent Ahab another message. “May the gods punish me severely if enough dust remains in Samaria for each of my men to have a handful!” he said. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 20:11
Israel’s king answered, “Tell him, ‘A man putting on armor should not boast like a man taking it off.’” 💬
## 1 Kings 20:12
Ben-hadad heard this while drinking with the kings in their tents. He told his servants, “Take your positions.” They stationed themselves against the city. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:13
A prophet approached Ahab king of Israel. He said, “The LORD says, ‘Do you see this great army? I will deliver it into your hand today. You will know I am the LORD.’” 📖
## 1 Kings 20:14
Ahab asked, “Through whom?” The prophet answered, “The LORD says, ‘Through the district governors’ young officers.’” Ahab asked, “Who starts the battle?” The prophet answered, “You.” 💬
## 1 Kings 20:15
Ahab gathered the district governors’ young officers, 232 men. Then he gathered the other Israelite troops, 7,000 in all. 👥
## 1 Kings 20:16
They marched out at noon. Ben-hadad and his 32 allied kings were getting drunk in their tents. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:17
The district governors’ young officers marched out first. Ben-hadad had sent scouts, who reported, “Men are marching out of Samaria.” 👥
## 1 Kings 20:18
He said, “If they come in peace, take them alive. Even if they come for war, take them alive.” 💬
## 1 Kings 20:19
The district governors’ young officers marched out of the city, with the army behind them, ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:20
and each struck down his opponent. The Arameans fled, and the Israelites pursued them. Ben-hadad king of Aram escaped on horseback with the cavalry. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:21
Israel’s king marched out and attacked the horses and chariots. He inflicted a great slaughter on the Arameans. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:22
The prophet then told Israel’s king, “Strengthen your position and consider what you must do. Aram’s king will attack you in the spring.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 20:23
Aram’s king heard this advice from his servants: “Their gods are gods of the hills. That is why they defeated us. Fight them on the plains, and we will surely win. 💬
## 1 Kings 20:24
Remove the kings from their positions and replace them with other officers. 👥
## 1 Kings 20:25
Raise an army like the one you lost, horse for horse and chariot for chariot. Then we can fight Israel on the plain and surely win.” The king approved their plan and acted on it. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:26
In spring, Ben-hadad gathered the Arameans and went to Aphek to fight Israel. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:27
The Israelites gathered their troops and supplies and marched to meet them. Their camp looked like two small flocks of goats. The Arameans covered the countryside. 👥
## 1 Kings 20:28
The man of God told Israel’s king, “The LORD says, ‘The Arameans said the LORD is a god of hills, not valleys. Because of this, I will deliver this whole great army into your hand. Then you will know I am the LORD.’” 📖
## 1 Kings 20:29
The armies camped opposite each other for seven days. Battle began on the seventh day. Israel killed a hundred thousand Aramean foot soldiers in one day. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:30
The survivors fled into Aphek. The wall fell on twenty-seven thousand of the remaining men. Ben-hadad also fled there and hid in an inner room. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 20:31
Ben-hadad’s servants said, “We heard that Israel’s kings are merciful. Let us approach Israel’s king with sackcloth around our waists and ropes around our heads. Perhaps he will spare your life.” 🙏
## 1 Kings 20:32
They wore sackcloth around their waists and ropes around their heads. They told Israel’s king, “Your servant Ben-hadad says, ‘Please spare my life.’” The king answered, “Is he alive? He is my brother.” 💬
## 1 Kings 20:33
The men sought a hopeful sign and quickly seized on his words. They said, “Yes, your brother Ben-hadad.” The king said, “Bring him.” Ben-hadad came, and Ahab had him enter his chariot. 💬
## 1 Kings 20:34
Ben-hadad said, “I will return the cities my father took from yours. You may establish marketplaces in Damascus, as my father did in Samaria.” Ahab answered, “I release you by this treaty.” He made a treaty with him and sent him away. 🤝
## 1 Kings 20:35
By the LORD’s word, one of the sons of the prophets told his companion, “Please strike me!” The man refused. 📖
## 1 Kings 20:36
The prophet said, “Because you did not obey the LORD’s voice, a lion will kill you as soon as you leave me.” When he left, a lion found him and killed him. 🦁
## 1 Kings 20:37
The prophet found another man and said, “Please strike me!” The man struck and wounded him, 📖
## 1 Kings 20:38
and the prophet waited for the king beside the road. He disguised himself with a bandage over his eyes. 📖
## 1 Kings 20:39
As the king passed, he cried, “Your servant went into battle. A man brought a captive and told me, ‘Guard him! If he disappears for any reason, your life will pay for his, or you must weigh out a talent of silver.’ 💬
## 1 Kings 20:40
While your servant was busy here and there, the man disappeared.” Israel’s king answered, “That will be your judgment. You pronounced it on yourself.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 20:41
The prophet quickly removed the bandage from his eyes. Israel’s king recognized him as one of the prophets. 📖
## 1 Kings 20:42
The prophet said, “The LORD says, ‘You released the man I devoted to destruction. Because of this, your life will pay for his, and your people for his people.’” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 20:43
Israel’s king went home to Samaria, sullen and angry. 📖
