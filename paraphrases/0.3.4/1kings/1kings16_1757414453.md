## 1 Kings 16:1
The LORD’s word came to Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha: 📖
## 1 Kings 16:2
“I lifted you from the dust and made you ruler over My people Israel. But you followed Jeroboam’s way and caused My people Israel to sin. Their sins provoked Me to anger. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:3
I will consume Baasha and his house. I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:4
Dogs will eat anyone belonging to Baasha who dies in the city. Birds of the air will eat anyone who dies in the field.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:5
Are Baasha’s other acts, accomplishments, and might not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 📜
## 1 Kings 16:6
Baasha died and rested with his fathers. He was buried in Tirzah. His son Elah reigned in his place. 🪦
## 1 Kings 16:7
The LORD’s word through the prophet Jehu son of Hanani opposed Baasha and his house because of his evil in the LORD’s sight. His works provoked the LORD to anger, and he became like Jeroboam’s house. The word also came because Baasha struck down Jeroboam’s house. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:8
In Asa’s twenty-sixth year over Judah, Elah son of Baasha became king of Israel. He reigned in Tirzah for two years. 👑
## 1 Kings 16:9
Elah was in Tirzah getting drunk in the house of Arza, who managed his household there. His servant Zimri, commander of half his chariots, plotted against him. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 16:10
In Asa’s twenty-seventh year over Judah, Zimri entered and struck Elah down, killing him. Zimri reigned in his place. 👑
## 1 Kings 16:11
As soon as Zimri began to reign and sat on the throne, he killed Baasha’s entire household. He left no male alive, whether relative or friend. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 16:12
Zimri destroyed Baasha’s whole household according to the LORD’s word against Baasha through Jehu the prophet. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:13
This happened because Baasha and his son Elah sinned and caused Israel to sin. Their worthless idols provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:14
Are Elah’s other acts and accomplishments not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 📜
## 1 Kings 16:15
In Asa’s twenty-seventh year over Judah, Zimri reigned in Tirzah for seven days. The troops were camped against Gibbethon of the Philistines, ⚔️
## 1 Kings 16:16
and heard that Zimri plotted against the king and killed him. That very day in the camp, all Israel proclaimed Omri, the army commander, king over Israel. 👑
## 1 Kings 16:17
Omri and all the Israelites marched from Gibbethon and surrounded Tirzah. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 16:18
Zimri saw that the city was captured. He entered the royal palace’s citadel and burned it down on himself. He died 🔥
## 1 Kings 16:19
because of his sins. He did evil in the LORD’s sight. He followed Jeroboam’s example and the sin Jeroboam committed and caused Israel to commit. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:20
Are Zimri’s other acts and his treason not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 📜
## 1 Kings 16:21
Israel’s people were divided. Half supported Tibni son of Ginath as king, and half supported Omri. 👥
## 1 Kings 16:22
Omri’s followers were stronger than the followers of Tibni son of Ginath. Tibni died, and Omri became king. 👑
## 1 Kings 16:23
In Asa’s thirty-first year over Judah, Omri became king of Israel. He reigned twelve years, including six in Tirzah. 👑
## 1 Kings 16:24
He bought Samaria’s hill from Shemer for two talents of silver and built a city there. He named it Samaria after Shemer, the hill’s former owner. 🏙️
## 1 Kings 16:25
Omri did evil in the LORD’s sight. He acted more wickedly than all before him. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 16:26
Omri followed all the ways of Jeroboam son of Nebat and the sins Jeroboam caused Israel to commit. Their worthless idols provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:27
Are Omri’s other acts, accomplishments, and the might he exercised not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 📜
## 1 Kings 16:28
Omri died and rested with his fathers. He was buried in Samaria. His son Ahab reigned in his place. 🪦
## 1 Kings 16:29
In Asa’s thirty-eighth year over Judah, Ahab son of Omri became king of Israel. He reigned in Samaria for twenty-two years. 👑
## 1 Kings 16:30
Ahab son of Omri did more evil in the LORD’s sight than all before him. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 16:31
Following the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat was not enough for him. He married Jezebel daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians. He began serving and worshiping Baal. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 16:32
Ahab built Baal’s temple in Samaria and set up an altar for Baal there. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 16:33
He also set up an Asherah pole. Ahab did more to provoke the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger than all Israel’s kings before him. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 16:34
During Ahab’s reign, Hiel the Bethelite rebuilt Jericho. Laying its foundation cost him his firstborn Abiram. Setting up its gates cost him his youngest son Segub. This fulfilled the LORD’s word through Joshua son of Nun. ⚖️
