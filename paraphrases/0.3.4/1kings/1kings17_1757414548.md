## 1 Kings 17:1
Elijah the Tishbite was among Gilead’s settlers. He told Ahab, “I stand before the LORD, the God of Israel. As surely as He lives, no dew or rain will fall these years except at my word!” 🌧️
## 1 Kings 17:2
A revelation from the LORD came to Elijah: 📖
## 1 Kings 17:3
“Leave here and turn east. Hide by the Brook of Cherith, east of the Jordan. 📖
## 1 Kings 17:4
Drink from the brook. I commanded the ravens to feed you there.” 🐦
## 1 Kings 17:5
Elijah obeyed the LORD. He went and lived by the Brook of Cherith, east of the Jordan. 📖
## 1 Kings 17:6
The ravens brought him bread and meat each morning and evening. He drank from the brook. 🐦
## 1 Kings 17:7
Later the brook dried up because no rain had fallen on the land. ☀️
## 1 Kings 17:8
The LORD’s word came to Elijah: 📖
## 1 Kings 17:9
“Go to Zarephath of Sidon and stay there. I commanded a widow there to provide for you.” 📖
## 1 Kings 17:10
Elijah went to Zarephath. At the city gate, a widow was gathering sticks. He called, “Please bring me a little water in a cup so I can drink.” 💧
## 1 Kings 17:11
As she went to get it, he called, “Please bring me a piece of bread.” 🍞
## 1 Kings 17:12
She answered, “As surely as the LORD your God lives, I have no bread. I have only a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug. I am gathering two sticks to take home and prepare a meal for myself and my son. We will eat it and die.” 💔
## 1 Kings 17:13
Elijah said, “Do not be afraid. Do what you said, but first make me a small cake of bread from what you have. Bring it to me. Afterward make some for yourself and your son, 🍞
## 1 Kings 17:14
because the LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘The flour jar will not become empty, and the oil jug will not run dry. This will continue until the LORD sends rain on the earth.’” 🌧️
## 1 Kings 17:15
She did as Elijah said. Every day there was food for Elijah, the woman, and her household. 🍞
## 1 Kings 17:16
The flour jar did not become empty, and the oil jug did not run dry. This fulfilled the LORD’s word through Elijah. 🙏
## 1 Kings 17:17
Later the son of the woman who owned the house became ill. His illness grew worse until he stopped breathing. 💔
## 1 Kings 17:18
She told Elijah, “Man of God, what have you done to me? Did you come to remind me of my sin and cause my son’s death?” 💔
## 1 Kings 17:19
Elijah said, “Give me your son.” He took the boy from her arms and carried him to the upper room where he stayed. He laid him on his own bed. 🛏️
## 1 Kings 17:20
He cried to the LORD, “LORD my God, have You brought tragedy even on this widow who received me into her home? Have You caused her son to die?” 🙏
## 1 Kings 17:21
He stretched himself over the child three times. He cried to the LORD, “LORD my God, please let this boy’s life return to him!” 🙏
## 1 Kings 17:22
The LORD heard Elijah’s voice. The child’s life returned, and he lived. ❤️
## 1 Kings 17:23
Elijah carried the child from the upper room into the house and gave him to his mother. He said, “Look, your son is alive!” 👶
## 1 Kings 17:24
The woman told Elijah, “Now I know you are a man of God. The LORD’s word from your mouth is true.” 📖
