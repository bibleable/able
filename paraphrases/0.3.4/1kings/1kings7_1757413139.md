## 1 Kings 7:1
Solomon took thirteen years to finish building his entire palace. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 7:2
He built the House of the Forest of Lebanon a hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high. Four rows of cedar pillars supported its cedar beams. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:3
Cedar roofing covered the beams resting on the pillars. There were forty-five beams, fifteen in each row. 🌲
## 1 Kings 7:4
Three rows of high windows faced one another in three tiers. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 7:5
All the doorways had rectangular frames. The openings faced one another in three tiers. 🚪
## 1 Kings 7:6
Solomon made a hall with columns, fifty cubits long and thirty cubits wide. A portico stood in front of it. A canopy with pillars stood in front of the portico. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:7
He built the Hall of Justice for his throne, where he would judge. Cedar panels covered it from floor to ceiling. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 7:8
Solomon’s palace, where he would live, stood farther back and had similar construction. He also built a palace like this hall for Pharaoh’s daughter, whom he had married. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 7:9
All these buildings used costly stones cut to size and trimmed with saws on the inside and outside. These stones extended from foundation to eaves and from outside to the great courtyard. 🪨
## 1 Kings 7:10
The foundations used large, costly stones. Some were ten cubits long, and some were eight cubits long. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:11
Above them were costly stones cut to size and cedar beams. 🪨
## 1 Kings 7:12
Three rows of stones cut to shape and one row of trimmed cedar beams surrounded the great courtyard. The LORD’s house had the same arrangement around its inner courtyard and portico. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 7:13
King Solomon sent for Huram from Tyre. 👥
## 1 Kings 7:14
Huram was the son of a widow from Naphtali’s tribe. His father was from Tyre and worked with bronze. Huram had great skill, understanding, and knowledge for every kind of bronze work. He came to King Solomon and did all his work. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:15
He cast two bronze pillars. Each was eighteen cubits high and twelve cubits around. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:16
He cast two bronze capitals, the pieces that sat on top of the pillars. Each capital was five cubits high. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:17
He made lattice networks with wreaths of chainwork for the capitals on the pillars. Each capital had seven. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:18
He made two rows of pomegranates around each grating to cover each capital on top of the pillars. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:19
The capitals on the pillars in the portico were shaped like lilies and were four cubits high. 🌼
## 1 Kings 7:20
Two hundred pomegranates encircled each capital in rows. They were just above the rounded projection beside the network on each pillar’s capital. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:21
He set the pillars at the temple’s portico. He named the south pillar Jachin and the north pillar Boaz. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 7:22
The tops of the pillars were shaped like lilies. This completed the work on the pillars. 🌼
## 1 Kings 7:23
He made the Sea from cast metal. It was circular, ten cubits from rim to rim, five cubits high, and thirty cubits around. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:24
Two rows of ornamental buds encircled the Sea below its rim, ten buds per cubit. They were cast as part of the Sea. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:25
The Sea stood on twelve oxen. Three faced north, three west, three south, and three east. Their hindquarters pointed toward the center, and the Sea rested on them. 🐂
## 1 Kings 7:26
The Sea was a handbreadth thick. Its rim was shaped like a cup’s brim, like a lily blossom. It could hold two thousand baths. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:27
He made ten movable bronze stands. Each was four cubits long, four cubits wide, and three cubits high. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:28
The stands had this design: side panels were attached to uprights, 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:29
with lions, oxen, and cherubim on the panels between the uprights. Above the uprights was a pedestal. Below the lions and oxen were wreaths of beveled work. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:30
Each stand had four bronze wheels with bronze axles. Its basin rested on four supports, with wreaths on each side. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:31
The opening inside the crown at the stand’s top was one cubit deep. It was round like a pedestal and a cubit and a half wide. Engravings surrounded the opening. The stand’s panels were square, not round. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:32
Four wheels were under the panels, with their axles attached to the stand. Each wheel was a cubit and a half across. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:33
The wheels were like chariot wheels. Their axles, rims, spokes, and hubs were all cast metal. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:34
Four handles projected from each stand, one at each corner. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:35
A circular band half a cubit high was at each stand’s top. The supports and panels were cast together with the top as one unit. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:36
He engraved cherubim, lions, and palm trees on the supports and panels wherever there was room. Wreaths surrounded them. 🌴
## 1 Kings 7:37
He made all ten stands this way. They had the same casting, dimensions, and shape. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:38
He made ten bronze basins, one for each of the ten stands. Each basin held forty baths and measured four cubits across. 📏
## 1 Kings 7:39
He put five stands on the temple’s south side and five on its north side. He placed the Sea on the south side, at the temple’s southeast corner. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 7:40
Huram also made pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. He finished all the work he had undertaken for King Solomon in the LORD’s house: 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:41
the two pillars, their two capitals shaped like bowls, and the two lattice networks covering the bowls of the capitals on the pillars, 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:42
the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks. Each network had two rows of pomegranates covering the capitals shaped like bowls on the pillars, 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:43
the ten stands and the ten basins on them, 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:44
the Sea and the twelve oxen under it, 🐂
## 1 Kings 7:45
and the pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. All these articles Huram made for King Solomon in the LORD’s house were polished bronze. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:46
The king had them cast in clay molds on the Jordan plain between Succoth and Zarethan. 🛠️
## 1 Kings 7:47
Solomon left these articles unweighed because there were so many. The bronze’s weight could not be determined. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 7:48
Solomon made all the furnishings for the LORD’s house. These included the gold altar and gold table for the Bread of the Presence, 🏛️
## 1 Kings 7:49
the pure gold lampstands in front of the inner sanctuary, five on the right and five on the left. They also included the gold flowers, lamps, and tongs, ✨
## 1 Kings 7:50
the pure gold basins, wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, ladles, and censers. They included gold hinges for the doors of the inner temple, the Most Holy Place, and the temple’s main hall. ✨
## 1 Kings 7:51
All King Solomon’s work for the LORD’s house was finished. He brought in the silver, gold, and furnishings his father David had dedicated. He put them in the treasuries of the LORD’s house. 🏛️
