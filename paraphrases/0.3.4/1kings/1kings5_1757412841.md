## 1 Kings 5:1
Hiram king of Tyre heard that Solomon had been anointed king in his father’s place. He sent messengers to Solomon, because he had always been David’s friend. 🤝
## 1 Kings 5:2
Solomon sent Hiram this message: 📜
## 1 Kings 5:3
“You know that wars surrounded my father David. He could not build a house for the Name of the LORD his God until the LORD put his enemies under his feet. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 5:4
Now the LORD my God has given me rest on every side. There is no enemy or crisis. 🙏
## 1 Kings 5:5
I plan to build a house for the Name of the LORD my God. The LORD told my father David, ‘I will put your son on your throne in your place. He will build the house for My Name.’ 🏛️
## 1 Kings 5:6
Order your servants to cut cedars of Lebanon for me. My servants will work with yours. I will pay your servants whatever wages you set. You know that none of us are as skilled at cutting timber as the Sidonians.” 🌲
## 1 Kings 5:7
Hiram was very glad when he received Solomon’s message. He said, “Blessed be the LORD today! He has given David a wise son to rule this great people!” 🙏
## 1 Kings 5:8
Hiram replied to Solomon, “I received your message. I will provide all the cedar and cypress timber you want. 🌲
## 1 Kings 5:9
My servants will bring the logs from Lebanon to the Sea. I will float them as rafts to the place you specify. There I will separate the logs so you can take them away. In exchange, provide food for my household.” 🌊
## 1 Kings 5:10
Hiram supplied all the cedar and cypress timber Solomon wanted. 🌲
## 1 Kings 5:11
Each year Solomon gave Hiram 20,000 cors of wheat to feed his household. He also gave him 20,000 baths of pure olive oil. 🌾
## 1 Kings 5:12
The LORD gave Solomon wisdom, as He promised. Hiram and Solomon had peace, and the two made a treaty. 🤝
## 1 Kings 5:13
King Solomon required 30,000 men from all Israel to serve as laborers. 👥
## 1 Kings 5:14
He sent them to Lebanon in monthly groups of 10,000. They spent one month in Lebanon and two months at home. Adoniram supervised the forced labor. 👥
## 1 Kings 5:15
Solomon had 70,000 men to carry loads and 80,000 men to cut stone in the mountains, 🪨
## 1 Kings 5:16
besides his 3,300 foremen who supervised the workers. 👥
## 1 Kings 5:17
The king commanded them to quarry large, costly stones. They were to lay the temple’s foundation with stones cut to shape. 🪨
## 1 Kings 5:18
Solomon’s builders, Hiram’s builders, and the Gebalites quarried the stone. They prepared timber and stone to build the temple. 🏛️
