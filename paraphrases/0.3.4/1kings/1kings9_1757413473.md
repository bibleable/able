## 1 Kings 9:1
Solomon finished building the LORD’s house and the royal palace. He accomplished everything he desired to do. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 9:2
The LORD appeared to him a second time, as He had at Gibeon. 🙏
## 1 Kings 9:3
The LORD said, “I heard your prayer and plea before Me. I set this temple you built apart as holy, putting My Name there forever. My eyes and heart will be there for all time. ❤️
## 1 Kings 9:4
If you walk before Me with an honest and upright heart, as your father David did. If you do all I command and keep My statutes and ordinances, 📖
## 1 Kings 9:5
then I will establish your royal throne over Israel forever. I promised your father David, ‘You will always have a man on Israel’s throne.’ 👑
## 1 Kings 9:6
But if you or your sons turn away from Me and fail to keep the commandments and statutes I gave you. If you go to serve and worship other gods, ⚠️
## 1 Kings 9:7
then I will cut Israel off from the land I gave them. I will remove from My presence this temple I set apart for My Name. All peoples will mock and scorn Israel. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 9:8
When this temple becomes a heap of rubble, everyone passing will be horrified and hiss. They will ask, ‘Why did the LORD do this to this land and temple?’ 🪨
## 1 Kings 9:9
Others will answer, ‘They abandoned the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of Egypt. They turned to other gods, worshiping and serving them. That is why the LORD brought this disaster on them.’” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 9:10
At the end of the twenty years Solomon spent building these two houses, the LORD’s house and the royal palace, 🏛️
## 1 Kings 9:11
King Solomon gave Hiram king of Tyre twenty towns in Galilee. Hiram had supplied all the cedar and cypress logs and gold he wanted. 🤝
## 1 Kings 9:12
Hiram went from Tyre to inspect the towns Solomon gave him. He was not pleased with them. 🏘️
## 1 Kings 9:13
Hiram asked, “What are these towns you gave me, my brother?” He called them the Land of Cabul, their name to this day. 💬
## 1 Kings 9:14
Hiram had sent the king 120 talents of gold. ✨
## 1 Kings 9:15
This is the account of the forced labor King Solomon required. The workers built the LORD’s house, his palace, the supporting terraces, and Jerusalem’s wall. They also built Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 9:16
Pharaoh king of Egypt had attacked and captured Gezer. He burned it and killed the Canaanites living there. He gave the city as a dowry to his daughter, Solomon’s wife. 🔥
## 1 Kings 9:17
Solomon rebuilt Gezer, Lower Beth-horon, 🏘️
## 1 Kings 9:18
Baalath, and Tamar in the Wilderness of Judah. 🏘️
## 1 Kings 9:19
He also built all his storage cities for chariots and horses. He built whatever he desired in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout the land he ruled. 🏘️
## 1 Kings 9:20
Some Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites remained. They were not Israelites. 👥
## 1 Kings 9:21
Their descendants remained in the land because the Israelites could not devote them to destruction. Solomon required them to serve as forced laborers, as they do to this day. 👥
## 1 Kings 9:22
Solomon did not make any Israelites slaves. They were his soldiers, servants, officers, captains, and commanders of his chariots and cavalry. 👥
## 1 Kings 9:23
They were also the chief officers over Solomon’s projects. There were 550 supervisors over the workers. 👥
## 1 Kings 9:24
Pharaoh’s daughter moved from the City of David to the palace Solomon built for her. As soon as she moved, he built the supporting terraces. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 9:25
Three times each year Solomon offered burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar he built for the LORD. He burned incense with them before the LORD. So he completed the temple. 🔥
## 1 Kings 9:26
King Solomon assembled a fleet of ships at Ezion-geber. This was near Eloth in Edom, on the Red Sea’s shore. ⛵
## 1 Kings 9:27
Hiram sent his servants, sailors who knew the sea, to work in the fleet with Solomon’s servants. ⛵
## 1 Kings 9:28
They sailed to Ophir and brought back 420 talents of gold for Solomon. ✨
