## 1 Kings 10:1
The queen of Sheba heard about Solomon’s fame concerning the LORD’s name. She came to test him with difficult questions. 👑
## 1 Kings 10:2
She arrived in Jerusalem with a very large caravan. Camels carried spices, much gold, and precious stones. She came to Solomon and told him everything on her mind. 🐪
## 1 Kings 10:3
Solomon answered all her questions. Nothing was too difficult for the king to explain. 💬
## 1 Kings 10:4
The queen of Sheba saw all Solomon’s wisdom and the palace he built. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 10:5
She saw his table’s food, his servants’ seating, his attendants’ service and clothing, his cupbearers, and his burnt offerings at the LORD’s house. It all took her breath away. 👑
## 1 Kings 10:6
She told the king, “The report I heard in my country about your words and wisdom is true. 💬
## 1 Kings 10:7
I did not believe it until I came and saw with my own eyes. I was not told even half! Your wisdom and prosperity far exceed the report I heard. 👑
## 1 Kings 10:8
How blessed your men are! How blessed your servants are, always standing before you and hearing your wisdom! 👥
## 1 Kings 10:9
Blessed be the LORD your God, who delighted in you and placed you on Israel’s throne. His love for Israel lasts forever. Because of this love, He made you king to administer justice and righteousness.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 10:10
She gave the king 120 talents of gold, many spices, and precious stones. Never again were so many spices brought as those the queen of Sheba gave King Solomon. ✨
## 1 Kings 10:11
Hiram’s fleet brought gold from Ophir. It also brought a large cargo of almug wood and precious stones from there. ⛵
## 1 Kings 10:12
The king used almug wood for steps in the LORD’s house and his palace, and for the singers’ lyres and harps. Such almug wood had never been brought before, and none has been seen again to this day. 🎶
## 1 Kings 10:13
King Solomon gave the queen of Sheba everything she wanted and requested, besides his generous royal gifts. She and her servants then returned to her country. 👑
## 1 Kings 10:14
Solomon received 666 talents of gold each year, ✨
## 1 Kings 10:15
besides revenue from merchants, traders, all the Arabian kings, and the land’s governors. ✨
## 1 Kings 10:16
King Solomon made two hundred large shields of hammered gold. Each used six hundred shekels of gold. 🛡️
## 1 Kings 10:17
He made three hundred small shields of hammered gold. Each used three minas of gold. He put them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon. 🛡️
## 1 Kings 10:18
The king made a large ivory throne and covered it with pure gold. 👑
## 1 Kings 10:19
The throne had six steps and a back with a rounded top. Each side of the seat had an armrest, with a lion beside it. 👑
## 1 Kings 10:20
Twelve lions stood on the six steps, one at each end of each step. No kingdom had ever had anything like it. 🦁
## 1 Kings 10:21
All King Solomon’s drinking cups were gold. All utensils in the House of the Forest of Lebanon were pure gold. None were silver, because silver was considered worth nothing in Solomon’s time. ✨
## 1 Kings 10:22
The king’s ships of Tarshish sailed with Hiram’s fleet. Once every three years, the ships of Tarshish brought gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks. ⛵
## 1 Kings 10:23
King Solomon exceeded all the earth’s kings in riches and wisdom. 👑
## 1 Kings 10:24
The whole world sought to meet Solomon and hear the wisdom God put in his heart. 👥
## 1 Kings 10:25
Year after year, each visitor brought tribute: silver and gold articles, clothing, weapons, spices, horses, and mules. 🎁
## 1 Kings 10:26
Solomon gathered 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horses. He stationed them in the chariot cities and with him in Jerusalem. 🐎
## 1 Kings 10:27
The king made silver as common as stones in Jerusalem. He made cedar as plentiful as sycamore in the foothills. 🌳
## 1 Kings 10:28
Solomon’s horses were imported from Egypt and Kue. The royal merchants bought them from Kue. 🐎
## 1 Kings 10:29
A chariot could be imported from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty. They also exported them to all the Hittite kings and the kings of Aram. 🐎
