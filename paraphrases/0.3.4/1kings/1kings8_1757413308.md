## 1 Kings 8:1
Solomon assembled Israel’s elders before him in Jerusalem, including all the tribal heads and family leaders. They were to bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant from Zion, the City of David. 👥
## 1 Kings 8:2
All the men of Israel gathered before King Solomon at the feast in Ethanim, the seventh month. 📖
## 1 Kings 8:3
When all Israel’s elders arrived, the priests lifted the ark, 👥
## 1 Kings 8:4
and brought it up with the Tent of Meeting and all its sacred furnishings. The priests and Levites carried up the LORD’s ark and these furnishings. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 8:5
King Solomon and the whole assembly of Israel with him sacrificed sheep and oxen before the ark. There were too many sacrifices to count. 🐑
## 1 Kings 8:6
The priests brought the ark of the LORD’s covenant into the temple’s inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place. They put it beneath the cherubim’s wings. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 8:7
The cherubim spread their wings over the ark’s place, covering the ark and its poles with their shadow. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 8:8
The poles extended far enough for their ends to be seen from the Holy Place in front of the inner sanctuary. They could not be seen from outside the Holy Place. They remain there to this day. 📖
## 1 Kings 8:9
Only the two stone tablets Moses placed there at Horeb were in the ark. There the LORD made a covenant with the Israelites after they left Egypt. 📜
## 1 Kings 8:10
When the priests left the Holy Place, the cloud filled the LORD’s house. ☁️
## 1 Kings 8:11
The cloud prevented the priests from standing there to serve. The LORD’s glory filled His house. ☁️
## 1 Kings 8:12
Solomon said, “The LORD said He would live in the thick cloud. ☁️
## 1 Kings 8:13
I have built You a magnificent house, a place for You to live forever.” 🏛️
## 1 Kings 8:14
The king turned and blessed the whole assembly of Israel while they stood there. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:15
He said, “Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel. His own hand has fulfilled what His mouth told my father David. He said, 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:16
‘Since I brought My people Israel out of Egypt, I have not chosen a city in any tribe of Israel for My house. That house would be a place for My Name. But I have chosen David to rule My people Israel.’ 👑
## 1 Kings 8:17
My father David desired in his heart to build a house for the Name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 8:18
The LORD told my father David, ‘You desired in your heart to build a house for My Name. It was good to have this desire. ❤️
## 1 Kings 8:19
But you will not build it. Your son, your own offspring, will build the house for My Name.’ 🏛️
## 1 Kings 8:20
The LORD has fulfilled His word. I have succeeded my father David and sit on Israel’s throne, as the LORD promised. I built the house for the Name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 👑
## 1 Kings 8:21
I provided a place there for the ark. It contains the LORD’s covenant with our fathers, made when He brought them out of Egypt.” 📜
## 1 Kings 8:22
Solomon stood before the LORD’s altar in front of the whole assembly of Israel. He spread his hands toward heaven, 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:23
and said, “LORD, God of Israel, no God is like You in heaven above or earth below. You keep Your covenant of faithful love with Your servants who walk before You with all their hearts. ❤️
## 1 Kings 8:24
You kept Your promise to Your servant, my father David. Your hand has fulfilled today what Your mouth spoke. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:25
LORD, God of Israel, keep Your promise to Your servant, my father David. You said, ‘You will always have a man on Israel’s throne before Me if your descendants carefully walk before Me as you did.’ 👑
## 1 Kings 8:26
God of Israel, please confirm Your promise to Your servant, my father David. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:27
But will God really live on earth? Heaven, even the highest heaven, cannot contain You. How much less can this temple I built contain You! 🏛️
## 1 Kings 8:28
LORD my God, consider Your servant’s prayer and plea. Hear the cry and prayer Your servant brings before You today. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:29
May Your eyes be open toward this temple day and night, the place where You said, ‘My Name will be there.’ Then may You hear the prayer Your servant prays toward this place. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:30
Hear Your servant’s plea and Your people Israel’s plea when they pray toward this place. Hear from heaven, where You live. Hear and forgive. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:31
When a man sins against his neighbor and must take an oath, and he comes before Your altar in this temple to swear, ⚖️
## 1 Kings 8:32
then may You hear from heaven and act. Judge Your servants. Condemn the wicked man by bringing what he did back on his own head. Declare the righteous man innocent and reward him according to his righteousness. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 8:33
When enemies defeat Your people Israel because they sinned against You, and they return to You and confess Your name, consider their prayers. They pray and plead with You in this temple. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:34
Then may You hear from heaven and forgive Your people Israel’s sin. Restore them to the land You gave their fathers. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:35
When the skies close and no rain falls because Your people sinned, and they pray toward this place and confess Your name. When they also turn from their sins because You afflicted them, 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:36
May You hear from heaven and forgive the sin of Your servants, Your people Israel, so You may teach them the good way to walk. Send rain on the land You gave Your people as an inheritance. 🌧️
## 1 Kings 8:37
When famine, plague, blight, mildew, locusts, or grasshoppers come on the land, or enemies surround their cities, whatever plague or sickness comes, ⚠️
## 1 Kings 8:38
whatever prayer or plea Your people Israel bring, each knowing his own afflictions and spreading his hands toward this temple, 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:39
may You hear from heaven, where You live. Forgive and act. Repay each man according to all his ways, because You know his heart. You alone know the hearts of all people. ❤️
## 1 Kings 8:40
Then may they fear You all their lives in the land You gave our fathers. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:41
Consider the foreigner who is not from Your people Israel but comes from a distant land because of Your name. 👥
## 1 Kings 8:42
They will hear of Your great name, mighty hand, and outstretched arm. When that foreigner comes and prays toward this temple, 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:43
may You hear from heaven, where You live, and do everything the foreigner asks of You. Then all peoples on earth will know Your name and fear You as Your people Israel do. They will know that this house I built bears Your Name. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:44
When Your people go to war against their enemies wherever You send them. When they pray to the LORD toward Your chosen city and the house I built for Your Name, ⚔️
## 1 Kings 8:45
then may You hear their prayer and plea from heaven and uphold their cause. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:46
No one is without sin. When they sin against You and You angrily hand them to an enemy who carries them captive to his land, near or far, ⚠️
## 1 Kings 8:47
and when they come to their senses in their place of captivity. When they repent and plead with You in their captors’ land, saying, ‘We sinned and did wrong. We acted wickedly,’ 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:48
and when they return to You with all their heart and soul in the land of the enemies who captured them. When they pray to You toward the land You gave their fathers, Your chosen city, and the house I built for Your Name, 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:49
then may You hear their prayer and plea from heaven, where You live. Uphold their cause. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:50
Forgive Your people’s sins against You and all their offenses against You. Cause their captors to feel compassion for them and show them mercy. ❤️
## 1 Kings 8:51
They are Your people and Your inheritance. You brought them out of Egypt, out of the furnace for iron. 🔥
## 1 Kings 8:52
May Your eyes be open to Your servant’s pleas and Your people Israel’s pleas. Listen whenever they call to You. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:53
Lord GOD, You set them apart from all peoples on earth as Your inheritance. You said this through Your servant Moses when You brought our fathers out of Egypt.” 📖
## 1 Kings 8:54
Solomon finished his entire prayer and plea to the LORD. He rose from before the LORD’s altar, where he had knelt with his hands spread toward heaven. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:55
He stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel in a loud voice: 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:56
“Blessed be the LORD, who gave His people Israel rest, as He promised. Not one word of His good promises through His servant Moses has failed. 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:57
May the LORD our God be with us as He was with our fathers. May He never leave or abandon us. ❤️
## 1 Kings 8:58
May He turn our hearts toward Himself. Then may we walk in all His ways and keep the commandments, statutes, and ordinances He gave our fathers. 📖
## 1 Kings 8:59
May these words of my plea remain near the LORD our God day and night. Then may He uphold His servant’s cause and His people Israel’s cause as each day requires, 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:60
so all peoples on earth may know that the LORD is God. There is no other! 🙏
## 1 Kings 8:61
Keep your heart fully devoted to the LORD our God, as it is today. Walk in His statutes and keep His commandments.” ❤️
## 1 Kings 8:62
The king and all Israel with him offered sacrifices before the LORD. 🐑
## 1 Kings 8:63
Solomon offered the LORD 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep as peace offerings. The king and all the Israelites dedicated the LORD’s house. 🐑
## 1 Kings 8:64
That day the king set apart as holy the middle of the courtyard in front of the LORD’s house. There he offered burnt offerings, grain offerings, and the fat of peace offerings. The bronze altar before the LORD was too small to hold them all. 🔥
## 1 Kings 8:65
Solomon and all Israel kept the feast before the LORD our God. This great assembly came from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt. They celebrated for seven days and seven more days, fourteen days in all. 🎶
## 1 Kings 8:66
On the fifteenth day, Solomon sent the people away. They blessed the king and went home with joyful hearts. They rejoiced in all the good the LORD had done for His servant David and His people Israel. 🎶
