## 1 Kings 14:1
Jeroboam’s son Abijah became ill. 🤒
## 1 Kings 14:2
Jeroboam told his wife, “Disguise yourself so people will not recognize you as my wife. Go to Shiloh, where Ahijah the prophet lives. He spoke about my becoming king over these people. 💬
## 1 Kings 14:3
Take ten loaves of bread, some cakes, and a jar of honey. Go to him. He will tell you what will happen to the boy.” 🍞
## 1 Kings 14:4
Jeroboam’s wife obeyed. She went to Shiloh and arrived at Ahijah’s house. Ahijah could not see, because age had dimmed his eyes. 📖
## 1 Kings 14:5
The LORD told Ahijah, “Jeroboam’s wife is coming to ask about her sick son. Say these things to her. She will arrive in disguise.” 📖
## 1 Kings 14:6
Ahijah heard her feet at the doorway. He said, “Come in, Jeroboam’s wife! Why are you disguised? I have been sent to give you bad news. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 14:7
Tell Jeroboam, ‘The LORD, the God of Israel, says: I raised you from among the people and made you ruler over My people Israel. 👑
## 1 Kings 14:8
I tore the kingdom from David’s house and gave it to you. But you have not been like My servant David. He kept My commandments, followed Me with all his heart, and did only what was right in My sight. 📖
## 1 Kings 14:9
You did more evil than all before you. You made other gods and cast images to provoke Me. You threw Me behind your back. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 14:10
Because of this, I will bring disaster on Jeroboam’s house. I will cut off every male belonging to Jeroboam in Israel, both slave and free. I will burn up Jeroboam’s house as one burns dung until none remains! ⚖️
## 1 Kings 14:11
Dogs will eat anyone belonging to Jeroboam who dies in the city. Birds of the air will eat anyone who dies in the field.’ The LORD has spoken. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 14:12
Get up and go home. When your feet enter the city, the child will die. 💔
## 1 Kings 14:13
All Israel will mourn him and bury him. He alone from Jeroboam’s house will have a proper burial. Only in him did the LORD, the God of Israel, find any good in Jeroboam’s house. 🪦
## 1 Kings 14:14
The LORD will raise up a king over Israel for Himself who will cut off Jeroboam’s house. This is the day, yes, even today! ⚖️
## 1 Kings 14:15
The LORD will strike Israel like a reed shaken in water. He will uproot Israel from this good land He gave their fathers and scatter them beyond the Euphrates. This is because they made Asherah poles and provoked the LORD to anger. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 14:16
He will give Israel over because of the sins Jeroboam committed and caused Israel to commit.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 14:17
Jeroboam’s wife left for Tirzah. The boy died as soon as she stepped across the house’s threshold. 💔
## 1 Kings 14:18
They buried him, and all Israel mourned him. This fulfilled the LORD’s word through His servant Ahijah the prophet. 🪦
## 1 Kings 14:19
Jeroboam’s other acts, his wars, and his reign are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📜
## 1 Kings 14:20
Jeroboam reigned twenty-two years. He died and rested with his fathers. His son Nadab reigned in his place. 👑
## 1 Kings 14:21
Rehoboam son of Solomon reigned in Judah. He became king at forty-one years old and reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem. The LORD chose this city from all Israel’s tribes to put His Name there. Rehoboam’s mother was Naamah the Ammonite. 👑
## 1 Kings 14:22
Judah did evil in the LORD’s sight. Their sins provoked Him to jealous anger more than all their fathers had done. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 14:23
They built high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree. 🌳
## 1 Kings 14:24
There were even male shrine prostitutes in the land. They imitated all the detestable practices of the nations the LORD drove out before the Israelites. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 14:25
In Rehoboam’s fifth year, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 14:26
He seized the treasures of the LORD’s house and the royal palace. He took everything, including all the gold shields Solomon made. 🛡️
## 1 Kings 14:27
King Rehoboam replaced them with bronze shields. He entrusted them to the guard captains on duty at the royal palace’s entrance. 🛡️
## 1 Kings 14:28
Whenever the king entered the LORD’s house, the guards carried the shields. Afterward they returned them to the guardroom. 🛡️
## 1 Kings 14:29
Are Rehoboam’s other acts and all he did not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 📜
## 1 Kings 14:30
Rehoboam and Jeroboam were at war throughout their lives. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 14:31
Rehoboam died and rested with his fathers. He was buried with them in the City of David. His mother was Naamah the Ammonite. His son Abijam reigned in his place. 🪦
