## 1 Kings 21:1
After these events, Naboth the Jezreelite owned a vineyard in Jezreel. It was beside the palace of Ahab king of Samaria. 🍇
## 1 Kings 21:2
Ahab told Naboth, “Give me your vineyard for a vegetable garden because it is beside my palace. I will give you a better vineyard. Or, if you prefer, I will pay its value in silver.” 💬
## 1 Kings 21:3
Naboth answered, “May the LORD forbid me to give you my fathers’ inheritance!” 📖
## 1 Kings 21:4
Ahab returned to his palace, sullen and angry. Naboth the Jezreelite had said, “I will not give you my fathers’ inheritance.” Ahab lay on his bed, turned his face away, and refused food. 🛏️
## 1 Kings 21:5
His wife Jezebel came and asked, “Why are you so sullen that you will not eat?” ❓
## 1 Kings 21:6
Ahab answered, “I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite. I offered silver for his vineyard or another vineyard if he preferred. He said, ‘I will not give you my vineyard!’” 💬
## 1 Kings 21:7
Jezebel answered, “Do you not rule Israel? Get up, eat, and be cheerful. I will get you Naboth the Jezreelite’s vineyard.” 💬
## 1 Kings 21:8
Jezebel wrote letters in Ahab’s name and sealed them with his seal. She sent them to the elders and nobles living with Naboth in his city. 📜
## 1 Kings 21:9
She wrote, “Announce a fast. Give Naboth a place of honor among the people. 📜
## 1 Kings 21:10
Seat two scoundrels opposite him. Have them testify, ‘You cursed both God and the king!’ Then take him out and stone him to death.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 21:11
The elders and nobles in Naboth’s city did what Jezebel instructed in her letters. 📖
## 1 Kings 21:12
They announced a fast and gave Naboth a place of honor among the people. 👥
## 1 Kings 21:13
Two scoundrels came and sat opposite Naboth. Before the people they testified, “Naboth cursed both God and the king!” The people took him outside the city and stoned him to death. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 21:14
They told Jezebel, “Naboth has been stoned to death.” 💬
## 1 Kings 21:15
Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned to death. She told Ahab, “Take Naboth the Jezreelite’s vineyard. He refused to give it to you for silver, but now he is dead.” 🍇
## 1 Kings 21:16
Ahab heard that Naboth was dead. He went down to take possession of Naboth the Jezreelite’s vineyard. 🍇
## 1 Kings 21:17
The LORD’s word came to Elijah the Tishbite: 📖
## 1 Kings 21:18
“Go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria. He has gone to Naboth’s vineyard to take possession of it. 🍇
## 1 Kings 21:19
Tell him, ‘The LORD says: Did you not murder a man and seize his land?’ Also tell him, ‘The LORD says: Where dogs licked Naboth’s blood, they will lick your blood, yes, yours!’” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 21:20
Ahab told Elijah, “You found me out, my enemy.” Elijah answered, “I found you because you sold yourself to do evil in the LORD’s sight. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 21:21
The LORD says, ‘I will bring disaster on you and consume your descendants. I will cut off every male belonging to Ahab in Israel, both slave and free. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 21:22
I will make your house like Jeroboam son of Nebat’s house and Baasha son of Ahijah’s house. This is because you provoked My anger and caused Israel to sin.’ ⚖️
## 1 Kings 21:23
The LORD also says about Jezebel, ‘Dogs will eat Jezebel beside Jezreel’s wall.’ ⚖️
## 1 Kings 21:24
Dogs will eat anyone belonging to Ahab who dies in the city. Birds of the air will eat anyone who dies in the field.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 21:25
No one was like Ahab. Urged on by his wife Jezebel, he sold himself to do evil in the LORD’s sight. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 21:26
He did detestable things by following idols, like the Amorites the LORD drove out before the Israelites. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 21:27
Ahab heard these words, tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and fasted. He lay in sackcloth and walked meekly. 🙏
## 1 Kings 21:28
The LORD’s word came to Elijah the Tishbite: 📖
## 1 Kings 21:29
“Have you seen how Ahab humbled himself before Me? Because he did this, I will not bring disaster during his life. I will bring it on his house during his son’s life.” ⚖️
