## 1 Kings 12:1
Rehoboam went to Shechem, because all Israel had gone there to make him king. 👑
## 1 Kings 12:2
Jeroboam son of Nebat heard about this while still in Egypt. He had fled there from King Solomon and had lived there ever since. 📖
## 1 Kings 12:3
They sent for Jeroboam. He and the whole assembly of Israel came to Rehoboam and said, 👥
## 1 Kings 12:4
“Your father put a heavy yoke on us. Lighten the burden of his service and the heavy yoke he imposed, and we will serve you.” 💬
## 1 Kings 12:5
Rehoboam answered, “Leave for three days, then return to me.” The people left. 💬
## 1 Kings 12:6
King Rehoboam consulted the elders who served his father Solomon during his life. He asked, “How should I answer these people?” 👥
## 1 Kings 12:7
They answered, “If you serve these people today and answer them kindly, they will be your servants forever.” 💬
## 1 Kings 12:8
Rehoboam rejected the elders’ advice. He consulted the young men who grew up with him and served him. 👥
## 1 Kings 12:9
He asked, “How should we answer these people? They asked me to lighten the yoke my father imposed on them.” ❓
## 1 Kings 12:10
The young men who grew up with him answered, “The people said your father made their yoke heavy and asked you to lighten it. Tell them, ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist! 💬
## 1 Kings 12:11
My father burdened you with a heavy yoke, but I will add to it. He whipped you with whips, but I will whip you with scorpions.’” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 12:12
Jeroboam and all the people returned to Rehoboam after three days. The king had told them, “Return to me on the third day.” 👥
## 1 Kings 12:13
The king answered the people harshly. He rejected the elders’ advice, ⚠️
## 1 Kings 12:14
and answered as the young men advised. He said, “My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add to it. He whipped you with whips, but I will whip you with scorpions.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 12:15
The king did not listen to the people. This turn of events came from the LORD. It fulfilled His word to Jeroboam son of Nebat through Ahijah the Shilonite. 📖
## 1 Kings 12:16
All Israel saw that the king would not listen. They answered, “What share do we have in David? What inheritance do we have in Jesse’s son? To your tents, Israel! Care for your own house, David!” The Israelites went home, 👥
## 1 Kings 12:17
but Rehoboam still ruled the Israelites living in Judah’s cities. 👑
## 1 Kings 12:18
King Rehoboam sent Adoram, who supervised the forced labor. All Israel stoned him to death. Rehoboam hurried into his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 12:19
Israel has rebelled against David’s house to this day. 📖
## 1 Kings 12:20
All Israel heard that Jeroboam had returned. They called him to the assembly and made him king over all Israel. Only Judah’s tribe followed David’s house. 👑
## 1 Kings 12:21
Rehoboam reached Jerusalem and gathered all Judah’s household and Benjamin’s tribe, 180,000 chosen warriors. They would fight Israel’s household and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam son of Solomon. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 12:22
God’s word came to Shemaiah, the man of God: 📖
## 1 Kings 12:23
“Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, all the household of Judah and Benjamin, and the rest of the people, 👥
## 1 Kings 12:24
‘The LORD says: Do not fight your brothers, the Israelites. Return home, each of you, because this is My doing.’” They obeyed the LORD’s word and turned back as He commanded. 🙏
## 1 Kings 12:25
Jeroboam built Shechem in Ephraim’s hill country and lived there. He went from there and built Penuel. 🏘️
## 1 Kings 12:26
Jeroboam thought, “The kingdom might return to David’s house. 👑
## 1 Kings 12:27
If the people offer sacrifices in the LORD’s house in Jerusalem, their hearts will return to their lord, Rehoboam king of Judah. Then they will kill me and return to Rehoboam king of Judah.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 12:28
The king sought advice and made two gold calves. He told the people, “Going to Jerusalem is too much for you. Israel, here are your gods who brought you out of Egypt.” 🐂
## 1 Kings 12:29
He put one calf in Bethel and the other in Dan. 🐂
## 1 Kings 12:30
This became a sin. The people went as far as Dan to worship before one calf. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 12:31
Jeroboam built shrines at the high places. He appointed priests from every class of people, although they were not Levites. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 12:32
Jeroboam established a feast on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, like Judah’s feast. He offered sacrifices on the altar in Bethel to the calves he made. He appointed priests in Bethel for the high places he built. 🔥
## 1 Kings 12:33
On the fifteenth day of the eighth month, a month he chose himself, Jeroboam sacrificed on his altar in Bethel. He established a feast for the Israelites, offered sacrifices on the altar, and burned incense. 🔥
