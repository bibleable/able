## 1 Kings 18:1
After a long time, in the drought’s third year, the LORD’s word came to Elijah. “Present yourself to Ahab,” He said, “and I will send rain on the earth.” 🌧️
## 1 Kings 18:2
Elijah went to present himself to Ahab. The famine was severe in Samaria. 📖
## 1 Kings 18:3
Ahab called Obadiah, who managed the palace. Obadiah greatly feared the LORD. 👥
## 1 Kings 18:4
When Jezebel slaughtered the LORD’s prophets, Obadiah hid a hundred prophets in caves, fifty men in each cave. He provided food and water for them. 👥
## 1 Kings 18:5
Ahab told Obadiah, “Search the whole land, every spring and valley. Perhaps we will find grass to keep the horses and mules alive. Then we will not have to destroy any livestock.” 🌱
## 1 Kings 18:6
They divided the land to search. Ahab went one way alone, and Obadiah went the other way alone. 📖
## 1 Kings 18:7
Elijah suddenly met Obadiah on his way. Obadiah recognized him, fell facedown, and asked, “Is it you, my lord Elijah?” 🙇
## 1 Kings 18:8
He answered, “It is I. Tell your master, ‘Elijah is here!’” 💬
## 1 Kings 18:9
Obadiah answered, “How have I sinned? Why are you handing your servant over to Ahab to kill me? ⚠️
## 1 Kings 18:10
As surely as the LORD your God lives, my lord sent someone to every nation and kingdom to search for you. When they said you were not there, he made them swear they had not found you. 📖
## 1 Kings 18:11
Now you tell me to say to my master, ‘Elijah is here!’ 💬
## 1 Kings 18:12
When I leave, I do not know where the LORD’s Spirit might carry you. If I tell Ahab and he cannot find you, he will kill me. But I, your servant, have feared the LORD since my youth. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 18:13
Were you not told what I did when Jezebel slaughtered the LORD’s prophets? I hid a hundred of the LORD’s prophets, fifty per cave, and supplied them with food and water. 👥
## 1 Kings 18:14
Now you tell me to say to my lord, ‘Elijah is here!’ He will kill me!” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 18:15
Elijah answered, “As surely as the LORD of Hosts lives, before whom I stand, I will present myself to Ahab today.” 🙏
## 1 Kings 18:16
Obadiah told Ahab, and Ahab went to meet Elijah. 👥
## 1 Kings 18:17
Ahab saw Elijah and said, “Is that you, the one who troubles Israel?” 💬
## 1 Kings 18:18
Elijah answered, “I have not troubled Israel. You and your father’s house have. You abandoned the LORD’s commandments and followed the Baals. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 18:19
Gather all Israel to meet me on Mount Carmel. Bring the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of Asherah who eat at Jezebel’s table.” 👥
## 1 Kings 18:20
Ahab called all the Israelites and gathered the prophets on Mount Carmel. 👥
## 1 Kings 18:21
Elijah approached the people and asked, “How long will you hesitate between two opinions? If the LORD is God, follow Him. If Baal is God, follow him.” The people said nothing. ❓
## 1 Kings 18:22
Elijah said, “I am the only remaining prophet of the LORD. Baal has four hundred and fifty prophets. 👥
## 1 Kings 18:23
Bring two bulls. Let Baal’s prophets choose one, cut it up, and put it on the wood without lighting a fire. I will prepare the other bull and put it on the wood without lighting a fire. 📖
## 1 Kings 18:24
Call on your god’s name, and I will call on the LORD’s name. The God who answers with fire is God.” All the people answered, “Your proposal is good.” 🔥
## 1 Kings 18:25
Elijah told Baal’s prophets, “There are so many of you, so choose a bull and prepare it first. Call on your god’s name, but do not light the fire.” 📖
## 1 Kings 18:26
They prepared the bull they received. From morning to noon they called on Baal’s name, shouting, “Baal, answer us!” They leaped around the altar they made. But there was no sound or answer. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 18:27
At noon Elijah mocked them. “Shout louder, because he is a god!” he said. “Perhaps he is thinking deeply, busy, or traveling. Perhaps he is asleep and needs waking!” 💬
## 1 Kings 18:28
They shouted louder. As was their custom, they cut themselves with knives and lances until blood flowed over them. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 18:29
Noon passed, and they kept raving until the evening sacrifice. There was no response. No one answered or paid attention. 📖
## 1 Kings 18:30
Elijah told the people, “Come near me.” They approached, and he repaired the LORD’s altar that had been torn down. 🪨
## 1 Kings 18:31
Elijah took twelve stones, one for each tribe of Jacob’s sons. The LORD’s word had told Jacob, “Your name will be Israel.” 🪨
## 1 Kings 18:32
With the stones, Elijah built an altar in the LORD’s name. He dug a trench around it large enough to hold two seahs of seed. 🪨
## 1 Kings 18:33
He arranged the wood, cut up the bull, and put it on the wood. 📖
## 1 Kings 18:34
He said, “Fill four waterpots and pour the water on the offering and wood.” He said, “Do it a second time,” and they did. He said, “Do it a third time,” and they did. 💧
## 1 Kings 18:35
Water ran around the altar and filled the trench. 💧
## 1 Kings 18:36
At the evening sacrifice, Elijah the prophet approached the altar. He prayed, “LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known today that You are God in Israel. Let them know I am Your servant and did all this at Your command. 🙏
## 1 Kings 18:37
Answer me, LORD! Answer so these people know that You, the LORD, are God and that You have turned their hearts back.” 🙏
## 1 Kings 18:38
The LORD’s fire fell and consumed the sacrifice, wood, stones, and dust. It also consumed the water in the trench. 🔥
## 1 Kings 18:39
The people saw this, fell facedown, and said, “The LORD is God! The LORD is God!” 🙇
## 1 Kings 18:40
Elijah commanded, “Seize Baal’s prophets! Let none escape.” The people seized them. Elijah took them to the Kishon Valley and slaughtered them there. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 18:41
Elijah told Ahab, “Go up, eat, and drink. There is the sound of heavy rain.” 🌧️
## 1 Kings 18:42
Ahab went up to eat and drink. Elijah climbed to Carmel’s summit, bent to the ground, and put his face between his knees. 🙏
## 1 Kings 18:43
He told his servant, “Look toward the sea.” The servant looked and said, “There is nothing.” Seven times Elijah told him, “Go back.” 🌊
## 1 Kings 18:44
On the seventh time, the servant said, “A cloud as small as a man’s hand is rising from the sea.” Elijah answered, “Tell Ahab, ‘Prepare your chariot and go down before the rain stops you.’” ☁️
## 1 Kings 18:45
Clouds and wind darkened the sky, and heavy rain began. Ahab rode away to Jezreel. 🌧️
## 1 Kings 18:46
The LORD’s hand came on Elijah. He tucked his cloak into his belt and ran ahead of Ahab all the way to Jezreel. 🏃
