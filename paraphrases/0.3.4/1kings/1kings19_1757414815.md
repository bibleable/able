## 1 Kings 19:1
Ahab told Jezebel everything Elijah did, including how he killed all the prophets with the sword. 💬
## 1 Kings 19:2
Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah. She said, “May the gods punish me severely if I do not kill you by this time tomorrow, as you killed the prophets!” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 19:3
Elijah was afraid and fled for his life. He reached Beersheba in Judah and left his servant there, 📖
## 1 Kings 19:4
but went a day’s journey into the wilderness himself. He sat under a broom tree and prayed to die. “I have had enough, LORD,” he said. “Take my life. I am no better than my fathers.” 🙏
## 1 Kings 19:5
He lay under the broom tree and slept. Suddenly an angel touched him and said, “Get up and eat.” 👼
## 1 Kings 19:6
He looked and saw bread baked over hot coals and a jar of water beside his head. He ate and drank, then lay down again. 🍞
## 1 Kings 19:7
The LORD’s angel returned a second time and touched him. He said, “Get up and eat, or the journey will be too much for you.” 👼
## 1 Kings 19:8
Elijah got up and ate and drank. The food strengthened him to walk forty days and forty nights to Horeb, God’s mountain. 📖
## 1 Kings 19:9
He entered a cave and spent the night. The LORD’s word came to him: “What are you doing here, Elijah?” ❓
## 1 Kings 19:10
He answered, “I have been very zealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts. The Israelites abandoned Your covenant, tore down Your altars, and killed Your prophets with the sword. I alone remain, and they want to kill me too.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 19:11
The LORD said, “Stand outside on the mountain before the LORD. The LORD is about to pass by.” A powerful wind tore the mountains and shattered rocks before the LORD, but He was not in the wind. An earthquake followed, but the LORD was not in the earthquake. 📖
## 1 Kings 19:12
A fire followed the earthquake, but the LORD was not in the fire. After the fire came a still, small voice. 📖
## 1 Kings 19:13
Elijah heard it and covered his face with his cloak. He went out and stood at the cave’s entrance. A voice asked, “What are you doing here, Elijah?” ❓
## 1 Kings 19:14
He answered, “I have been very zealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts. The Israelites abandoned Your covenant, tore down Your altars, and killed Your prophets with the sword. I alone remain, and they want to kill me too.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 19:15
The LORD said, “Return by the way you came and go to the Desert of Damascus. When you arrive, anoint Hazael king over Aram. 👑
## 1 Kings 19:16
Also anoint Jehu son of Nimshi king over Israel. Anoint Elisha son of Shaphat from Abel-meholah to succeed you as prophet. 📖
## 1 Kings 19:17
Jehu will kill those who escape Hazael’s sword. Elisha will kill those who escape Jehu’s sword. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 19:18
But I have kept seven thousand in Israel whose knees have not bowed to Baal and whose mouths have not kissed him.” 👥
## 1 Kings 19:19
Elijah left and found Elisha son of Shaphat plowing with twelve teams of oxen. Elisha was with the twelfth team. Elijah passed and threw his cloak around him. 🐂
## 1 Kings 19:20
Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah. He said, “Let me kiss my father and mother goodbye, then I will follow you.” Elijah answered, “Go back. What have I done to you?” 💬
## 1 Kings 19:21
Elisha returned, took his pair of oxen, and slaughtered them. He used their equipment as fuel to cook the meat and gave it to the people to eat. Then he followed and served Elijah. 🐂
