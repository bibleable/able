## 1 Kings 3:1
Solomon formed an alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt by marrying his daughter. He brought her to the City of David. She stayed there until he finished his palace, the LORD’s house, and the wall around Jerusalem. 👑
## 1 Kings 3:2
The people still sacrificed at the high places. A house for the LORD’s Name had not yet been built. 🙏
## 1 Kings 3:3
Solomon loved the LORD and followed his father David’s statutes. But he sacrificed and burned incense at the high places. 📖
## 1 Kings 3:4
The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice, because it was the great high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on its altar. 🔥
## 1 Kings 3:5
One night at Gibeon, the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream. God said, “Ask, and I will give it to you!” 💭
## 1 Kings 3:6
Solomon answered, “You showed great faithful love to Your servant, my father David. You did this because he walked before You in faithfulness, righteousness, and an upright heart. You continued this faithful love by giving him a son to sit on his throne today. ❤️
## 1 Kings 3:7
LORD my God, You made Your servant king in my father David’s place. But I am only a little child. I do not know how to go out or come in. 🙏
## 1 Kings 3:8
Your servant is among Your chosen people. There are too many of them to count. 👥
## 1 Kings 3:9
Give Your servant an understanding heart to judge Your people and distinguish good from evil. Who can govern this great people of Yours?” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 3:10
The Lord was pleased with Solomon’s request. 🙏
## 1 Kings 3:11
God said, “You asked for understanding to administer justice. You did not ask for a long life, wealth, or the death of your enemies. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 3:12
Because of this, I will do what you asked. I will give you a wise heart that can distinguish right from wrong. No one before you has been like you, and no one after you will be like you. 📖
## 1 Kings 3:13
I will also give you what you did not ask for: riches and honor. Throughout your life, no man in any kingdom will equal you. 👑
## 1 Kings 3:14
If you walk in My ways and keep My statutes and commandments as your father David did, I will give you a long life.” 🙏
## 1 Kings 3:15
Solomon awoke and realized it was a dream. He returned to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the Lord’s covenant. He offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. Then he held a feast for all his servants. 🔥
## 1 Kings 3:16
Two prostitutes came and stood before the king. 👥
## 1 Kings 3:17
One woman said, “Please, my lord, this woman and I live in the same house. I gave birth while she was there. 💬
## 1 Kings 3:18
On the third day after I gave birth, this woman also had a baby. We were alone. Only the two of us were in the house. 🏠
## 1 Kings 3:19
That night her son died because she rolled over on him. 💔
## 1 Kings 3:20
She rose in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while I slept. She held him against her chest and put her dead son against mine. 📖
## 1 Kings 3:21
When I got up the next morning to nurse my son, I found that he was dead. But when I examined him, I realized he was not the son I had borne.” 💔
## 1 Kings 3:22
The other woman said, “No, the living son is mine, and the dead son is yours.” The first woman insisted, “No, the dead son is yours, and the living son is mine.” They argued before the king. 💬
## 1 Kings 3:23
The king said, “One woman says, ‘My son is alive, and yours is dead.’ The other says, ‘No, your son is dead, and mine is alive.’” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 3:24
The king said, “Bring me a sword.” They brought him a sword, ⚔️
## 1 Kings 3:25
and he commanded, “Cut the living child in two. Give half to one woman and half to the other.” ⚔️
## 1 Kings 3:26
The living son’s mother felt deep compassion for him. She said, “Please, my lord, give her the living baby. Do not kill him!” The other woman said, “He will belong to neither of us. Cut him in two!” 💔
## 1 Kings 3:27
The king ruled, “Give the living baby to the first woman. Do not kill him. She is his mother.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 3:28
All Israel heard the king’s judgment and stood in awe of him. They saw that God’s wisdom was in him to administer justice. ⚖️
