## 1 Kings 4:1
King Solomon ruled over Israel. 👑
## 1 Kings 4:2
These were his chief officials: Azariah son of Zadok was the priest. 👥
## 1 Kings 4:3
Elihoreph and Ahijah, sons of Shisha, were secretaries. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud kept the records. 📜
## 1 Kings 4:4
Benaiah son of Jehoiada commanded the army. Zadok and Abiathar were priests. 👥
## 1 Kings 4:5
Azariah son of Nathan supervised the governors. Zabud son of Nathan was a priest and the king’s adviser. 👥
## 1 Kings 4:6
Ahishar managed the palace. Adoniram son of Abda supervised the forced labor. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 4:7
Solomon had twelve governors over all Israel. They provided food for the king and his household. Each governor arranged the supplies for one month each year. 🍞
## 1 Kings 4:8
These were their names and territories: Ben-hur governed the hill country of Ephraim. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:9
Ben-deker governed Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:10
Ben-hesed governed Arubboth. Socoh and all the land of Hepher belonged to him. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:11
Ben-abinadab governed Naphath-dor. His wife was Taphath, Solomon’s daughter. 👥
## 1 Kings 4:12
Baana son of Ahilud governed Taanach, Megiddo, and all Beth-shean beside Zarethan below Jezreel. His territory extended from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah and beyond Jokmeam. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:13
Ben-geber governed Ramoth-gilead. The villages of Jair son of Manasseh in Gilead belonged to him. So did Argob in Bashan, with sixty large cities protected by walls and bronze bars. 🏙️
## 1 Kings 4:14
Ahinadab son of Iddo governed Mahanaim. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:15
Ahimaaz governed Naphtali. He had married Basemath, Solomon’s daughter. 👥
## 1 Kings 4:16
Baana son of Hushai governed Asher and Aloth. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:17
Jehoshaphat son of Paruah governed Issachar. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:18
Shimei son of Ela governed Benjamin. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:19
Geber son of Uri governed Gilead, including the territories of Sihon king of the Amorites and Og king of Bashan. There was also one governor in Judah. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:20
Judah and Israel had as many people as the sand on the seashore. They ate, drank, and rejoiced. 🎶
## 1 Kings 4:21
Solomon ruled all the kingdoms from the Euphrates to the Philistines’ land, as far as Egypt’s border. They paid tribute and served Solomon throughout his life. 👑
## 1 Kings 4:22
Solomon’s food supplies for one day included thirty cors of fine flour and sixty cors of meal, 🍞
## 1 Kings 4:23
ten fat oxen, twenty range oxen, and a hundred sheep. They also included deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened poultry. 🐑
## 1 Kings 4:24
Solomon ruled everything west of the Euphrates, including all the kingdoms from Tiphsah to Gaza. He had peace on every side. 👑
## 1 Kings 4:25
Throughout Solomon’s reign, Judah and Israel lived safely from Dan to Beersheba. Each man lived under his own vine and fig tree. 🌳
## 1 Kings 4:26
Solomon had 4,000 stalls for his chariot horses and 12,000 horses. 🐎
## 1 Kings 4:27
The governors took turns each month to supply food for King Solomon and everyone who ate at his table. They made sure nothing was missing. 🍞
## 1 Kings 4:28
Each governor also brought his required amounts of barley and straw for the chariot horses and other horses. He brought them to the required place. 🐎
## 1 Kings 4:29
God gave Solomon wisdom, very deep insight, and understanding beyond measure, like the sand on the seashore. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:30
Solomon’s wisdom exceeded the wisdom of all the men of the East and all Egypt’s wisdom. 📖
## 1 Kings 4:31
He was wiser than all men. He was wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite and Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol. His fame spread through the surrounding nations. 👑
## 1 Kings 4:32
Solomon composed three thousand proverbs and a thousand and five songs. 🎶
## 1 Kings 4:33
He spoke about trees, from Lebanon’s cedar to the hyssop growing in the wall. He taught about animals, birds, reptiles, and fish. 🌳
## 1 Kings 4:34
Men from all nations came to hear Solomon’s wisdom. All the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom sent them. 👥
