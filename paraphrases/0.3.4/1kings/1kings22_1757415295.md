## 1 Kings 22:1
Three years passed without war between Aram and Israel. 📖
## 1 Kings 22:2
In the third year, Jehoshaphat king of Judah visited Israel’s king, 👥
## 1 Kings 22:3
who told his servants, “Do you not know Ramoth-gilead belongs to us? We have failed to take it from Aram’s king.” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:4
He asked Jehoshaphat, “Will you fight Ramoth-gilead with me?” Jehoshaphat answered Israel’s king, “I am as you are. My people are your people, and my horses are your horses.” 🤝
## 1 Kings 22:5
Jehoshaphat also told Israel’s king, “First ask for the LORD’s word.” 📖
## 1 Kings 22:6
Israel’s king gathered about four hundred prophets. He asked, “Should I attack Ramoth-gilead or hold back?” They answered, “Attack. The Lord will deliver it into the king’s hand.” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:7
Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there another prophet of the LORD here whom we can ask?” ❓
## 1 Kings 22:8
Israel’s king answered, “We can ask the LORD through Micaiah son of Imlah. I hate him because he never prophesies good for me, only bad.” Jehoshaphat answered, “The king should not say that!” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:9
Israel’s king called an official and said, “Bring Micaiah son of Imlah at once.” 👥
## 1 Kings 22:10
Israel’s king and Jehoshaphat king of Judah sat on their thrones in royal clothing. They were at the threshing floor by Samaria’s gate entrance. All the prophets prophesied before them. 👑
## 1 Kings 22:11
Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns. He declared, “The LORD says, ‘With these you will gore the Arameans until they are destroyed.’” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:12
All the prophets gave the same message: “Attack Ramoth-gilead and win! The LORD will deliver it into the king’s hand.” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:13
The messenger sent for Micaiah told him, “All the prophets agree on a favorable message for the king. Please speak favorably as they do.” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:14
Micaiah answered, “As surely as the LORD lives, I will speak whatever He tells me.” 📖
## 1 Kings 22:15
Micaiah arrived, and the king asked, “Micaiah, should we attack Ramoth-gilead or hold back?” He answered, “Attack and win. The LORD will deliver it into the king’s hand.” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:16
The king said, “How often must I make you swear to speak only truth to me in the LORD’s name?” ❓
## 1 Kings 22:17
Micaiah said, “I saw all Israel scattered on the hills like sheep without a shepherd. The LORD said, ‘They have no master. Let each return home in peace.’” 🐑
## 1 Kings 22:18
Israel’s king told Jehoshaphat, “Did I not tell you he never prophesies good for me, only bad?” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:19
Micaiah continued, “Hear the LORD’s word. I saw the LORD sitting on His throne. All heaven’s host stood beside Him, on His right and left. 👑
## 1 Kings 22:20
The LORD asked, ‘Who will entice Ahab to attack Ramoth-gilead and fall there?’ One suggested one thing, and another suggested something else. 💬
## 1 Kings 22:21
A spirit came forward and stood before the LORD. He said, ‘I will entice him.’ The LORD asked, ‘How?’ 💬
## 1 Kings 22:22
The spirit answered, ‘I will be a lying spirit in all his prophets’ mouths.’ The LORD said, ‘You will entice him and succeed. Go and do it.’ 💬
## 1 Kings 22:23
The LORD has put a lying spirit in all these prophets’ mouths. He has declared disaster against you.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 22:24
Zedekiah son of Chenaanah approached, struck Micaiah’s face, and demanded, “Which way did the LORD’s Spirit go when He left me to speak to you?” 💬
## 1 Kings 22:25
Micaiah answered, “You will see on the day you hide in an inner room.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 22:26
Israel’s king ordered, “Take Micaiah back to Amon the city’s governor and Joash the king’s son. 👥
## 1 Kings 22:27
Tell them, ‘The king says: Put this man in prison. Give him only bread and water until I return safely.’” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 22:28
Micaiah answered, “If you ever return safely, the LORD has not spoken through me.” He added, “Listen carefully, all you people!” 📖
## 1 Kings 22:29
Israel’s king and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went to Ramoth-gilead. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 22:30
Israel’s king told Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself for battle, but you wear your royal robes.” Israel’s king disguised himself and entered battle. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 22:31
Aram’s king commanded his thirty-two chariot commanders, “Fight no one, small or great, except Israel’s king.” ⚔️
## 1 Kings 22:32
The chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat and said, “This must be Israel’s king!” They turned to fight him, but he cried out. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 22:33
They saw he was not Israel’s king and stopped pursuing him. 📖
## 1 Kings 22:34
A man shot his bow without special aim and struck Israel’s king between his armor’s joints. The king told his charioteer, “Turn around and take me out of battle. I am badly wounded!” 🏹
## 1 Kings 22:35
The battle raged all day. The king was held upright in his chariot facing the Arameans. Blood from his wound ran onto the chariot’s floor. He died that evening. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 22:36
At sunset, a cry went through the army: “Every man to his own city! Every man to his own land!” 👥
## 1 Kings 22:37
The king died and was brought to Samaria. They buried him there. 🪦
## 1 Kings 22:38
The chariot was washed at Samaria’s pool, where prostitutes bathed. Dogs licked Ahab’s blood, fulfilling the LORD’s word. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 22:39
Are Ahab’s other acts, all his accomplishments, his ivory palace, and all the cities he built not recorded? They are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📜
## 1 Kings 22:40
Ahab died and rested with his fathers. His son Ahaziah reigned in his place. 🪦
## 1 Kings 22:41
In Ahab’s fourth year over Israel, Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king of Judah. 👑
## 1 Kings 22:42
Jehoshaphat became king at thirty-five years old. He reigned in Jerusalem for twenty-five years. His mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi. 👑
## 1 Kings 22:43
Jehoshaphat followed all his father Asa’s ways without turning aside. He did right in the LORD’s sight. But the high places remained, and the people still sacrificed and burned incense there. 📖
## 1 Kings 22:44
Jehoshaphat made peace with Israel’s king. 🤝
## 1 Kings 22:45
Are Jehoshaphat’s other acts, the might he exercised, and his wars not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 📜
## 1 Kings 22:46
He banished the male shrine prostitutes who remained in the land from his father Asa’s time. 📖
## 1 Kings 22:47
Edom had no king. A deputy served as king. 👑
## 1 Kings 22:48
Jehoshaphat built ships of Tarshish to sail to Ophir for gold. They never sailed, because they were wrecked at Ezion-geber. ⛵
## 1 Kings 22:49
Ahaziah son of Ahab told Jehoshaphat, “Let my servants sail with yours.” Jehoshaphat refused. 💬
## 1 Kings 22:50
Jehoshaphat died and rested with his fathers. He was buried with them in his father David’s city. His son Jehoram reigned in his place. 🪦
## 1 Kings 22:51
In Jehoshaphat’s seventeenth year over Judah, Ahaziah son of Ahab became king of Israel. He reigned in Samaria for two years. 👑
## 1 Kings 22:52
He did evil in the LORD’s sight. He followed the ways of his father, his mother, and Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 22:53
Ahaziah served and worshiped Baal. Like his father, he provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger. ⚖️
