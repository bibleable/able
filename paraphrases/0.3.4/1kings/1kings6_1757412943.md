## 1 Kings 6:1
Solomon began building the LORD’s house in the four hundred and eightieth year after Israel left Egypt. It was the fourth year of his reign over Israel. Work began in Ziv, the second month. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:2
The house King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:3
The portico in front of the temple’s main hall was twenty cubits long, across the temple’s width. It projected ten cubits in front of the temple. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:4
He had narrow windows framed high in the temple. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:5
Solomon built a structure with rooms around the temple, against the walls of the temple and inner sanctuary. He built side rooms in this structure. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:6
The bottom floor was five cubits wide, the middle floor six cubits wide, and the third floor seven cubits wide. He placed offset ledges around the temple’s outside so nothing would be inserted into its walls. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:7
The temple was built with finished stones cut at the quarry. No hammer, chisel, or other iron tool was heard in the temple during construction. 🪨
## 1 Kings 6:8
The entrance to the bottom floor was on the temple’s south side. Stairs led to the middle floor and from there to the third floor. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:9
Solomon built and finished the temple. He roofed it with cedar beams and planks. 🌲
## 1 Kings 6:10
He built rooms along the whole temple, each five cubits high. Cedar beams attached them to the temple. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:11
Then the LORD’s word came to Solomon: 📖
## 1 Kings 6:12
“You are building this temple. If you follow My statutes, carry out My ordinances, and obey all My commandments, I will fulfill My promise through you. It is the promise I made to your father David. 🙏
## 1 Kings 6:13
I will live among the Israelites and will not abandon My people Israel.” ❤️
## 1 Kings 6:14
Solomon built and finished the temple. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:15
He lined its inside walls with cedar panels from floor to ceiling. He covered the floor with cypress boards. 🌲
## 1 Kings 6:16
He separated the twenty cubits at the temple’s rear with cedar boards from floor to ceiling. This formed the inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:17
The main hall in front of this room was forty cubits long. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:18
The cedar panels inside the temple had carvings of gourds and open flowers. Everything was cedar. No stone was visible. 🌼
## 1 Kings 6:19
Solomon prepared the inner sanctuary inside the temple to hold the ark of the LORD’s covenant. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:20
The inner sanctuary was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and twenty cubits high. He covered its inside with pure gold and also covered the cedar altar with gold. ✨
## 1 Kings 6:21
Solomon covered the temple’s inside with pure gold. He stretched gold chains across the front of the inner sanctuary, which was covered with gold. ✨
## 1 Kings 6:22
He covered the whole temple interior with gold until everything was finished. He also covered the whole altar belonging to the inner sanctuary with gold. ✨
## 1 Kings 6:23
He made two cherubim from olive wood in the inner sanctuary. Each was ten cubits high. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:24
Each wing of the first cherub was five cubits long. Its full wingspan was ten cubits. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:25
The second cherub also measured ten cubits. Both cherubim had the same size and shape, 📏
## 1 Kings 6:26
and each cherub was ten cubits high. 📏
## 1 Kings 6:27
He put the cherubim in the temple’s innermost room with their wings spread. One wing of the first cherub touched one wall. One wing of the second touched the other wall. Their wingtips touched in the room’s center. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:28
He also covered the cherubim with gold. ✨
## 1 Kings 6:29
He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers on the walls around the temple. These carvings were in both the inner and outer sanctuaries. 🌴
## 1 Kings 6:30
He covered the floors of both the inner and outer sanctuaries with gold. ✨
## 1 Kings 6:31
Solomon made olive wood doors with five-sided doorposts for the inner sanctuary’s entrance. 🚪
## 1 Kings 6:32
He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers on the olive wood double doors. He covered the cherubim and palm trees with hammered gold. ✨
## 1 Kings 6:33
In the same way, he made four-sided olive wood doorposts for the sanctuary entrance. 🚪
## 1 Kings 6:34
The two doors were cypress wood. Each had two folding panels. 🚪
## 1 Kings 6:35
He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers on them. He covered them with gold hammered evenly over the carvings. ✨
## 1 Kings 6:36
Solomon built the inner courtyard with three rows of stones cut to shape and one row of trimmed cedar beams. 🪨
## 1 Kings 6:37
The LORD’s house had its foundation laid in Ziv, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 6:38
The temple was finished in every detail and to every specification in the eleventh year, in Bul, the eighth month. Solomon took seven years to build it. 🏛️
