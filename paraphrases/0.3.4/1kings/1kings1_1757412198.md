## 1 Kings 1:1
King David was very old. They covered him with blankets, but he could not keep warm. 🛏️
## 1 Kings 1:2
His servants said, “Let us find a young virgin for our lord the king. She can serve and care for him. She can lie beside him to keep him warm.” 💬
## 1 Kings 1:3
They searched throughout Israel for a beautiful girl. They found Abishag the Shunammite and brought her to the king. 👥
## 1 Kings 1:4
She was very beautiful. She cared for the king and served him, but he did not have sexual relations with her. 📖
## 1 Kings 1:5
Adonijah, David’s son by Haggith, began to honor himself above others. He said, “I will be king!” He obtained chariots, horsemen, and fifty men to run ahead of him. 👑
## 1 Kings 1:6
His father had never corrected him by asking, “Why do you act this way?” Adonijah was very handsome. He was born next after Absalom. 📖
## 1 Kings 1:7
Adonijah spoke with Joab son of Zeruiah and Abiathar the priest. They supported him. 🤝
## 1 Kings 1:8
Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David’s mighty men would not join Adonijah. 👥
## 1 Kings 1:9
Adonijah sacrificed sheep, oxen, and fattened calves near the stone of Zoheleth, beside En-rogel. He invited all his royal brothers and all the men of Judah who served the king. 🐑
## 1 Kings 1:10
He did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon. 📖
## 1 Kings 1:11
Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother, “Have you heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king? Our lord David does not know it. 👑
## 1 Kings 1:12
Please come and let me advise you. Save your life and the life of your son Solomon. 💬
## 1 Kings 1:13
Go to King David at once. Say, ‘My lord the king, did you not swear this to your maidservant? “Your son Solomon will reign after me and sit on my throne.” Why has Adonijah become king?’ 👑
## 1 Kings 1:14
While you are still speaking with the king, I will come in after you and confirm your words.” 💬
## 1 Kings 1:15
Bathsheba went to the king’s bedroom. Because he was very old, Abishag the Shunammite was serving him. 🛏️
## 1 Kings 1:16
Bathsheba bowed to honor the king. He asked, “What do you want?” 🙇
## 1 Kings 1:17
She answered, “My lord, you swore to your maidservant by the LORD your God. You said, ‘Your son Solomon will reign after me and sit on my throne.’ 👑
## 1 Kings 1:18
But Adonijah has now become king, and you, my lord the king, do not know it. 📖
## 1 Kings 1:19
He sacrificed many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep. He invited all the king’s other sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the army commander. He did not invite your servant Solomon. 🐑
## 1 Kings 1:20
My lord the king, all Israel is watching you. They expect you to tell them who will sit on your throne after you. 👑
## 1 Kings 1:21
Otherwise, when you die and rest with your fathers, people will treat me and my son Solomon as criminals.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 1:22
While Bathsheba was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet arrived. 👥
## 1 Kings 1:23
The king was told, “Nathan the prophet is here.” Nathan entered and bowed facedown before the king. 🙇
## 1 Kings 1:24
Nathan said, “My lord the king, did you say that Adonijah would reign after you and sit on your throne? ❓
## 1 Kings 1:25
Today he sacrificed many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep. He invited all the king’s sons, the army commanders, and Abiathar the priest. They are eating and drinking before him, saying, ‘Long live King Adonijah!’ 👑
## 1 Kings 1:26
He did not invite me your servant, Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or your servant Solomon. 📖
## 1 Kings 1:27
Did my lord the king allow this without telling your servant who should sit on your throne after you?” ❓
## 1 Kings 1:28
King David said, “Call Bathsheba in for me.” She came and stood before him. 👥
## 1 Kings 1:29
The king swore, “As surely as the LORD lives, who has saved my life from every distress, 🙏
## 1 Kings 1:30
I will do today exactly what I swore to you by the LORD, the God of Israel. Your son Solomon will reign after me. He will sit on my throne in my place.” 👑
## 1 Kings 1:31
Bathsheba bowed facedown to honor the king. She said, “May my lord King David live forever!” 🙇
## 1 Kings 1:32
King David said, “Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada.” They came before the king. 👥
## 1 Kings 1:33
He said, “Take my servants with you. Put my son Solomon on my own mule and take him down to Gihon. 🫏
## 1 Kings 1:34
There Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet must anoint him king over Israel. Blow the ram’s horn and announce, ‘Long live King Solomon!’ 👑
## 1 Kings 1:35
Then go up with him. He must sit on my throne and reign in my place. I have appointed him ruler over Israel and Judah.” 👑
## 1 Kings 1:36
Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered, “Amen. May the LORD, the God of my lord the king, declare this. 🙏
## 1 Kings 1:37
May the LORD be with Solomon as He was with my lord the king. May He make Solomon’s throne greater than the throne of my lord King David.” 👑
## 1 Kings 1:38
Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada went down with the Cherethites and Pelethites. They put Solomon on King David’s mule and escorted him to Gihon. 🫏
## 1 Kings 1:39
Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tabernacle and anointed Solomon. They blew the ram’s horn. All the people proclaimed, “Long live King Solomon!” 👑
## 1 Kings 1:40
All the people followed him, playing flutes and rejoicing greatly. Their joyful sound was so loud that it split the earth. 🎶
## 1 Kings 1:41
Adonijah and his guests were finishing their feast when they heard the ram’s horn. Joab asked, “Why is the city making such a loud noise?” 📯
## 1 Kings 1:42
While he spoke, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. Adonijah said, “Come in. You are a brave man. You must have good news.” 💬
## 1 Kings 1:43
Jonathan answered, “No. Our lord King David has made Solomon king. 👑
## 1 Kings 1:44
The king sent Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada with Solomon, along with the Cherethites and Pelethites. They put him on the king’s mule. 🫏
## 1 Kings 1:45
Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him king at Gihon. They went up from there with joyful shouts that ring through the city. That is the noise you hear. 🎶
## 1 Kings 1:46
Solomon has also taken his seat on the royal throne. 👑
## 1 Kings 1:47
The king’s servants congratulated our lord King David. They said, ‘May your God make Solomon’s name more famous than yours and his throne greater than yours.’ The king bowed in worship on his bed, 🙏
## 1 Kings 1:48
saying, ‘Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel! Today He provided someone to sit on my throne, and I have seen it with my own eyes.’” 🙏
## 1 Kings 1:49
All Adonijah’s guests rose in terror and scattered. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 1:50
Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He got up and went to hold the horns of the altar. 🙏
## 1 Kings 1:51
Solomon was told, “Adonijah fears King Solomon and holds the horns of the altar. He says, ‘Let King Solomon first swear that he will not kill his servant with the sword.’” ⚔️
## 1 Kings 1:52
Solomon answered, “If he is a man of good character, not one hair of his will fall to the ground. But if evil is found in him, he will die.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 1:53
King Solomon called Adonijah down from the altar. Adonijah came and bowed before him. Solomon said, “Go to your home.” 🏠
