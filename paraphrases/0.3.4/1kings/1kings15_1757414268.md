## 1 Kings 15:1
In the eighteenth year of Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abijam became king of Judah. 👑
## 1 Kings 15:2
He reigned in Jerusalem for three years. His mother was Maacah daughter of Abishalom. 👑
## 1 Kings 15:3
Abijam committed all the sins of his father before him. His heart was not fully devoted to the LORD his God as his forefather David’s heart had been. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 15:4
For David’s sake, the LORD his God gave him a lamp in Jerusalem. He raised up a son to succeed him and make Jerusalem strong. 🪔
## 1 Kings 15:5
David did what was right in the LORD’s sight. Throughout his life, he did not turn from anything the LORD commanded, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite. 📖
## 1 Kings 15:6
Rehoboam’s house and Jeroboam’s house were at war throughout Abijam’s life. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 15:7
Are Abijam’s other acts and accomplishments not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? Abijam and Jeroboam were at war. 📜
## 1 Kings 15:8
Abijam died and rested with his fathers. He was buried in the City of David. His son Asa reigned in his place. 🪦
## 1 Kings 15:9
In Jeroboam’s twentieth year over Israel, Asa became king of Judah. 👑
## 1 Kings 15:10
He reigned in Jerusalem for forty-one years. His grandmother was Maacah daughter of Abishalom. 👑
## 1 Kings 15:11
Asa did right in the LORD’s sight, as his father David had done. 📖
## 1 Kings 15:12
He banished the male shrine prostitutes from the land. He removed all the idols his fathers made. 📖
## 1 Kings 15:13
He removed his grandmother Maacah as queen mother because she made a detestable Asherah pole. Asa cut down the pole and burned it in the Kidron Valley. 🔥
## 1 Kings 15:14
The high places remained. But Asa’s heart was fully devoted to the LORD throughout his life. ❤️
## 1 Kings 15:15
He brought into the LORD’s house the silver, gold, and articles he and his father dedicated. ✨
## 1 Kings 15:16
Asa and Baasha king of Israel were at war throughout their lives. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 15:17
Baasha king of Israel attacked Judah and fortified Ramah. He wanted to prevent anyone from entering or leaving Asa king of Judah’s territory. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 15:18
Asa took all the silver and gold left in the treasuries of the LORD’s house and the royal palace. He entrusted it to his servants. He sent them to Ben-hadad king of Aram, who ruled in Damascus. Ben-hadad was the son of Tabrimmon, son of Hezion. Asa sent this message: 📜
## 1 Kings 15:19
“Let us make a treaty, as our fathers did. I sent you silver and gold as a gift. Break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel so he withdraws from me.” 🤝
## 1 Kings 15:20
Ben-hadad listened to King Asa and sent his army commanders against Israel’s cities. They conquered Ijon, Dan, Abel-beth-maacah, and all Naphtali’s land, including Chinnereth’s region. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 15:21
Baasha heard this, stopped fortifying Ramah, and withdrew to Tirzah. 📖
## 1 Kings 15:22
King Asa summoned every man of Judah without exception. They carried away Ramah’s stones and the timber Baasha used to build. With these materials, Asa built up Geba of Benjamin and Mizpah. 🪨
## 1 Kings 15:23
Are Asa’s other acts, all his might and accomplishments, and the cities he built not recorded? They are in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. In his old age, his feet became diseased. 📜
## 1 Kings 15:24
Asa died and rested with his fathers. He was buried with them in his father David’s city. His son Jehoshaphat reigned in his place. 🪦
## 1 Kings 15:25
In Asa’s second year over Judah, Nadab son of Jeroboam became king of Israel. He reigned two years. 👑
## 1 Kings 15:26
He did evil in the LORD’s sight. He followed his father’s way and the sin his father caused Israel to commit. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 15:27
Baasha son of Ahijah, from Issachar’s house, plotted against Nadab. He struck Nadab down at Gibbethon of the Philistines while Nadab and all Israel surrounded the city. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 15:28
In Asa’s third year over Judah, Baasha killed Nadab and reigned in his place. 👑
## 1 Kings 15:29
As soon as Baasha became king, he killed Jeroboam’s entire household. He left no one breathing. This fulfilled the LORD’s word through His servant Ahijah the Shilonite, ⚖️
## 1 Kings 15:30
because Jeroboam sinned and caused Israel to sin, provoking the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 15:31
Are Nadab’s other acts and accomplishments not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 📜
## 1 Kings 15:32
Asa and Baasha king of Israel were at war throughout their lives. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 15:33
In Asa’s third year over Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah became king over all Israel. He reigned in Tirzah for twenty-four years. 👑
## 1 Kings 15:34
Baasha did evil in the LORD’s sight. He followed Jeroboam’s way and the sin Jeroboam caused Israel to commit. ⚠️
