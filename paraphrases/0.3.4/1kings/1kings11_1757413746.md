## 1 Kings 11:1
King Solomon loved many foreign women besides Pharaoh’s daughter. They were women from Moab, Ammon, Edom, and Sidon, and Hittite women. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:2
The LORD had warned the Israelites about these nations: “Do not marry their people. They will turn your hearts toward their gods.” But Solomon held firmly to these women in love. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 11:3
He had seven hundred wives of royal birth and three hundred concubines. His wives turned his heart away. 👥
## 1 Kings 11:4
When Solomon grew old, his wives turned his heart toward other gods. Unlike his father David, he was not fully devoted to the LORD his God. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 11:5
Solomon followed Ashtoreth, the Sidonians’ goddess, and Milcom, the Ammonites’ detestable god. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 11:6
Solomon did evil in the LORD’s sight. Unlike his father David, he did not follow the LORD completely. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 11:7
On a hill east of Jerusalem, Solomon built a high place for Chemosh, Moab’s detestable god, and Molech, the Ammonites’ detestable god. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 11:8
He did the same for all his foreign wives. They burned incense and sacrificed to their gods. 🔥
## 1 Kings 11:9
The LORD became angry because Solomon’s heart turned away from Him. The LORD, the God of Israel, had appeared to him twice. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 11:10
He had clearly warned Solomon not to follow other gods. But Solomon did not obey the LORD’s command. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 11:11
The LORD told Solomon, “You have done this and failed to keep My covenant and the statutes I commanded you. Because of this, I will tear the kingdom from you and give it to your servant. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:12
For your father David’s sake, I will not do this during your life. I will tear it from your son’s hand. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:13
I will not tear the whole kingdom from him. I will give your son one tribe for My servant David’s sake and for the sake of Jerusalem, which I chose.” 👑
## 1 Kings 11:14
The LORD raised up an enemy against Solomon: Hadad the Edomite, from Edom’s royal family. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 11:15
Earlier, when David was in Edom, Joab the army commander went to bury the dead. He struck down every male in Edom. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 11:16
Joab and all Israel stayed there six months, until he had killed every male in Edom. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 11:17
Hadad was still a young boy. He fled to Egypt with some Edomites who served his father. 👥
## 1 Kings 11:18
Hadad and his men left Midian and went to Paran. They took men from Paran and went to Pharaoh king of Egypt. Pharaoh gave Hadad a house, land, and food. 🏠
## 1 Kings 11:19
Pharaoh favored Hadad greatly. He gave Hadad the sister of his own wife, Queen Tahpenes, in marriage. 👥
## 1 Kings 11:20
Tahpenes’ sister bore Hadad a son named Genubath. Tahpenes herself weaned him in Pharaoh’s palace. Genubath lived there among Pharaoh’s sons. 👶
## 1 Kings 11:21
In Egypt, Hadad heard that David had died and rested with his fathers, and that Joab the army commander was dead. He told Pharaoh, “Let me return to my country.” 💬
## 1 Kings 11:22
Pharaoh asked, “What have you lacked here with me? Why do you suddenly want to return to your country?” Hadad answered, “Nothing, but please let me go.” 💬
## 1 Kings 11:23
God raised up another enemy against Solomon: Rezon son of Eliada. He had fled from his master, Hadadezer king of Zobah, ⚔️
## 1 Kings 11:24
and gathered men around him. When David killed the Zobaites, Rezon led a band of raiders. They went to Damascus, settled there, and gained control. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 11:25
Rezon was Israel’s enemy throughout Solomon’s reign, adding to Hadad’s trouble. Rezon ruled Aram with hostility toward Israel. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 11:26
Jeroboam son of Nebat was an Ephraimite from Zeredah. His mother was a widow named Zeruah. Jeroboam served Solomon but rebelled against him. 👥
## 1 Kings 11:27
This is the account of his rebellion against the king. Solomon built the supporting terraces and repaired the gap in the wall of his father David’s city. 🏛️
## 1 Kings 11:28
Jeroboam was a strong, brave man. Solomon saw that this young man worked hard. He placed him over the whole labor force of Joseph’s household. 👥
## 1 Kings 11:29
At that time, the prophet Ahijah the Shilonite met Jeroboam on the road as Jeroboam left Jerusalem. Ahijah wore a new cloak. The two were alone in the open field. 📖
## 1 Kings 11:30
Ahijah took the new cloak he wore and tore it into twelve pieces. 📖
## 1 Kings 11:31
He told Jeroboam, “Take ten pieces. The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘I will tear the kingdom from Solomon’s hand and give you ten tribes. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:32
One tribe will remain for My servant David’s sake and for Jerusalem’s sake. I chose that city out of all Israel’s tribes. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:33
They abandoned Me to worship Ashtoreth, the Sidonians’ goddess, Chemosh, the Moabites’ god, and Milcom, the Ammonites’ god. They have not walked in My ways, done right in My sight, or kept My statutes and judgments as Solomon’s father David did. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 11:34
I will not take the whole kingdom from Solomon’s hand. I made him ruler for his whole life for the sake of My servant David. I chose David because he kept My commandments and statutes. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:35
But I will take ten tribes of the kingdom from his son’s hand and give them to you. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:36
I will give his son one tribe, so My servant David always has a lamp before Me in Jerusalem. That is the city where I chose to put My Name. 🪔
## 1 Kings 11:37
I will take you to reign over everything your heart desires. You will be king over Israel. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:38
If you obey all My commands, walk in My ways, and do right before Me, I will be with you. This means keeping My statutes and commandments as My servant David did. I will build you a lasting dynasty as I built for David. I will give Israel to you. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:39
Because of this, I will humble David’s descendants, but not forever.’” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 11:40
Solomon tried to kill Jeroboam. Jeroboam fled to Shishak king of Egypt and stayed there until Solomon died. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 11:41
Are Solomon’s other acts, all he did, and his wisdom not written in the Book of the Acts of Solomon? 📜
## 1 Kings 11:42
Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel for forty years. 👑
## 1 Kings 11:43
Solomon died and rested with his fathers. He was buried in his father David’s city. His son Rehoboam reigned in his place. 🪦
