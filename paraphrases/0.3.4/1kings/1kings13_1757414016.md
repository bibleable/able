## 1 Kings 13:1
Jeroboam stood beside the altar to burn incense. Suddenly a man of God came from Judah to Bethel by the LORD’s word. 📖
## 1 Kings 13:2
By the LORD’s word, he cried against the altar, “Altar, altar! The LORD says, ‘A son named Josiah will be born to David’s house. On you he will sacrifice the priests of the high places who burn incense on you. Human bones will be burned on you.’” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 13:3
That day the man of God gave a sign. He said, “The LORD declares this sign: ‘The altar will split apart, and its ashes will pour out.’” 📖
## 1 Kings 13:4
King Jeroboam heard the man of God’s cry against the altar in Bethel. He stretched out his hand, saying, “Seize him!” But his outstretched hand withered, and he could not pull it back. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 13:5
The altar split apart, and the ashes poured out. This fulfilled the sign the man of God gave by the LORD’s word. 📖
## 1 Kings 13:6
The king said, “Ask the LORD your God to help me. Pray that my hand is restored.” The man of God prayed to the LORD, and the king’s hand became as it was before. 🙏
## 1 Kings 13:7
The king told the man of God, “Come home with me and refresh yourself. I will give you a reward.” 💬
## 1 Kings 13:8
The man of God answered, “Even if you gave me half your possessions, I would not go with you. I would not eat bread or drink water in this place. 💬
## 1 Kings 13:9
The LORD’s word commanded me, ‘Do not eat bread or drink water. Do not return by the way you came.’” 📖
## 1 Kings 13:10
The man of God took another way. He did not return by the way he came to Bethel. 📖
## 1 Kings 13:11
An old prophet lived in Bethel. His sons told him everything the man of God did there that day. They also told him what the man said to the king. 👥
## 1 Kings 13:12
Their father asked, “Which way did he go?” His sons showed him the way the man of God from Judah took. 💬
## 1 Kings 13:13
The prophet told his sons, “Saddle the donkey for me.” They saddled it, and he mounted it, 🫏
## 1 Kings 13:14
and followed the man of God. He found him sitting under an oak tree. He asked, “Are you the man of God who came from Judah?” The man answered, “I am.” 🌳
## 1 Kings 13:15
The prophet said, “Come home with me and eat some bread.” 🍞
## 1 Kings 13:16
The man answered, “I cannot go home with you. I will not eat bread or drink water with you here. 💬
## 1 Kings 13:17
The LORD’s word told me, ‘Do not eat bread or drink water there. Do not return by the way you came.’” 📖
## 1 Kings 13:18
The prophet answered, “I am also a prophet like you. An angel told me by the LORD’s word, ‘Bring him home with you to eat bread and drink water.’” The old prophet was lying, ⚠️
## 1 Kings 13:19
but the man of God returned with him. He ate bread in his house and drank water. 🍞
## 1 Kings 13:20
While they sat at the table, the LORD’s word came to the prophet who brought him back. 📖
## 1 Kings 13:21
The prophet cried to the man of God from Judah, “The LORD says, ‘You defied the LORD’s word. You did not keep the command of the LORD your God, ⚖️
## 1 Kings 13:22
but returned to eat bread and drink water where He told you not to. Because of this, your body will never reach your fathers’ tomb.’” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 13:23
After the man of God ate and drank, the old prophet who brought him back saddled the donkey for him. 🫏
## 1 Kings 13:24
A lion met him on the road and killed him. His body lay in the road, with the donkey and lion standing beside it. 🦁
## 1 Kings 13:25
Men passing saw the body in the road and the lion beside it. They reported this in the city where the old prophet lived. 👥
## 1 Kings 13:26
The prophet who brought him back heard this. He said, “It is the man of God who disobeyed the LORD’s command. The LORD handed him to the lion, which mauled and killed him. This fulfills the LORD’s word to him.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 13:27
The old prophet told his sons, “Saddle the donkey for me.” They saddled it, 🫏
## 1 Kings 13:28
and he went and found the body in the road, with the donkey and lion beside it. The lion had not eaten the body or mauled the donkey. 🦁
## 1 Kings 13:29
The old prophet lifted the man of God’s body onto the donkey. He brought it to his own city to mourn and bury him. 🪦
## 1 Kings 13:30
He laid the body in his own tomb. They mourned him, saying, “Oh, my brother!” 💔
## 1 Kings 13:31
After burying him, the prophet told his sons, “When I die, bury me in the man of God’s tomb. Lay my bones beside his bones. 🪦
## 1 Kings 13:32
His message by the LORD’s word against Bethel’s altar will surely come true. So will his message against all the high place shrines in Samaria’s cities.” 📖
## 1 Kings 13:33
Even afterward, Jeroboam did not turn from his evil ways. He again appointed priests for the high places from every class of people. He ordained anyone who wanted to be a priest there. ⚠️
## 1 Kings 13:34
This sin of Jeroboam’s house led to its extermination and destruction from the earth. ⚖️
