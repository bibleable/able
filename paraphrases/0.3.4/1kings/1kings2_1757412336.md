## 1 Kings 2:1
As David’s death approached, he gave these commands to his son Solomon: 💬
## 1 Kings 2:2
“I am about to die, as all people do. Be strong and prove yourself a man. 💪
## 1 Kings 2:3
Obey the LORD your God and walk in His ways. Keep His statutes, commandments, ordinances, and decrees, as written in the Law of Moses. Then you may prosper in everything you do and wherever you go, 📖
## 1 Kings 2:4
and the LORD may fulfill His promise to me. He said, ‘If your descendants walk faithfully before Me with all their heart and soul, you will always have a man on Israel’s throne.’ 👑
## 1 Kings 2:5
You know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me. He killed Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether, the two commanders of Israel’s armies. He killed them in peacetime to avenge blood shed in war. He stained the belt around his waist and the sandals on his feet with the blood of war. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 2:6
Act with wisdom. Do not let his gray head go down to Sheol in peace. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 2:7
Show faithful love to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite. Let them eat at your table. They stood by me when I fled from your brother Absalom. 🤝
## 1 Kings 2:8
Watch Shimei son of Gera, the Benjamite from Bahurim who is with you. He cursed me bitterly when I went to Mahanaim. When he met me at the Jordan, I swore by the LORD, ‘I will never kill you with the sword.’ 📖
## 1 Kings 2:9
Do not consider him innocent. You are wise and know what to do to him. Bring his gray head down to Sheol in blood.” ⚖️
## 1 Kings 2:10
David died and rested with his fathers. He was buried in the City of David. 🪦
## 1 Kings 2:11
David reigned over Israel for forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. 👑
## 1 Kings 2:12
Solomon sat on his father David’s throne. His kingdom was firmly established. 👑
## 1 Kings 2:13
Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother. She asked, “Do you come in peace?” He answered, “Yes, in peace.” 💬
## 1 Kings 2:14
He said, “I have something to tell you.” She answered, “Say it.” 💬
## 1 Kings 2:15
He said, “You know the kingdom was mine. All Israel expected me to reign. But the kingdom passed to my brother, because the LORD gave it to him. 👑
## 1 Kings 2:16
Now I have one request. Do not refuse me.” She said, “Tell me your request.” 💬
## 1 Kings 2:17
Adonijah answered, “Please speak to King Solomon, because he will not refuse you. Ask him to give me Abishag the Shunammite as my wife.” 💬
## 1 Kings 2:18
Bathsheba answered, “Very well. I will speak to the king for you.” 💬
## 1 Kings 2:19
Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak for Adonijah. The king stood to greet her, bowed to her, and sat on his throne. He had a throne brought for his mother, and she sat at his right hand. 👑
## 1 Kings 2:20
She said, “I have one small request. Do not refuse me.” The king answered, “Make your request, my mother. I will not refuse you.” 💬
## 1 Kings 2:21
Bathsheba said, “Give Abishag the Shunammite to your brother Adonijah as his wife.” 💬
## 1 Kings 2:22
King Solomon answered his mother, “Why do you ask for Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? He is my older brother. You might as well ask for the kingdom for him, Abiathar the priest, and Joab son of Zeruiah!” 👑
## 1 Kings 2:23
King Solomon swore by the LORD, “May God punish me severely if this request does not cost Adonijah his life. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 2:24
As surely as the LORD lives, Adonijah will die today! The LORD established me and placed me on my father David’s throne. He founded a dynasty for me, as He promised.” 👑
## 1 Kings 2:25
King Solomon gave orders to Benaiah son of Jehoiada. Benaiah struck Adonijah down, and he died. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 2:26
The king told Abiathar the priest, “Return to your fields in Anathoth. You deserve to die, but I will not kill you now. This is because you carried the ark of the Lord GOD before my father David and shared all his suffering.” 📖
## 1 Kings 2:27
Solomon removed Abiathar from the LORD’s priesthood. This fulfilled the LORD’s word at Shiloh against Eli’s household. 📖
## 1 Kings 2:28
Joab heard the news. He had conspired with Adonijah, but not with Absalom. He fled to the LORD’s tent and held the horns of the altar. 🙏
## 1 Kings 2:29
King Solomon was told, “Joab has fled to the LORD’s tent and is beside the altar.” Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, “Go and strike him down!” ⚔️
## 1 Kings 2:30
Benaiah entered the LORD’s tent and told Joab, “The king says, ‘Come out!’” Joab answered, “No, I will die here.” Benaiah returned to the king and reported Joab’s answer. 💬
## 1 Kings 2:31
The king answered, “Do what he says. Strike him down and bury him. Remove from me and my father’s household the guilt for the innocent blood Joab shed. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 2:32
The LORD will bring Joab’s bloodshed back on his own head. Without my father David’s knowledge, he killed two men more righteous and better than himself. He killed Abner son of Ner, commander of Israel’s army, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of Judah’s army, with the sword. ⚔️
## 1 Kings 2:33
Their blood will come back on Joab and his descendants forever. But the LORD will give peace forever to David, his descendants, his household, and his throne.” 🙏
## 1 Kings 2:34
Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up and struck Joab down, killing him. Joab was buried at his own home in the wilderness. 🪦
## 1 Kings 2:35
The king appointed Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the army in Joab’s place. He appointed Zadok the priest in Abiathar’s place. 👥
## 1 Kings 2:36
The king called Shimei and said, “Build a house in Jerusalem and live there. Do not go anywhere else. 🏠
## 1 Kings 2:37
Know for certain that you will die on the day you leave and cross the Kidron Valley. Your blood will be on your own head.” ⚠️
## 1 Kings 2:38
Shimei answered, “The sentence is fair. Your servant will do what my lord the king has said.” Shimei lived in Jerusalem for a long time. 🏠
## 1 Kings 2:39
After three years, two of Shimei’s slaves fled to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. Shimei was told, “Your slaves are in Gath.” 👥
## 1 Kings 2:40
Shimei saddled his donkey and went to Achish at Gath to find his slaves. He brought them back from Gath. 🫏
## 1 Kings 2:41
Solomon was told that Shimei had left Jerusalem for Gath and returned. 📖
## 1 Kings 2:42
The king called Shimei and said, “Did I not make you swear by the LORD? I warned you that you would certainly die on the day you left to go elsewhere. You answered, ‘The sentence is fair. I will obey.’ ⚠️
## 1 Kings 2:43
Why did you not keep your oath to the LORD and the command I gave you?” ❓
## 1 Kings 2:44
The king also said, “In your heart you know all the evil you did to my father David. The LORD will bring your evil back on your own head. ⚖️
## 1 Kings 2:45
But King Solomon will be blessed. David’s throne will remain secure before the LORD forever.” 👑
## 1 Kings 2:46
The king commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who went out and struck Shimei down, killing him. Solomon’s rule over the kingdom was firmly established. 👑
