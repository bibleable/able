## Judges 4:1
After Ehud died, Israel again did evil before the LORD. ⚠️
## Judges 4:2
The LORD sold them to Jabin king of Canaan, ruling in Hazor. His commander Sisera lived in Harosheth-hagoyim. ⛓️
## Judges 4:3
Israel cried to the LORD. Jabin had nine hundred iron chariots and harshly oppressed them twenty years. 🙏
## Judges 4:4
Deborah, a prophetess and Lappidoth’s wife, judged Israel then. 📖
## Judges 4:5
She sat under the Palm of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in Ephraim’s hills. Israelites came to her for judgment. 🌴
## Judges 4:6
She called Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali. She said, “Israel’s God, the LORD, commands you to march to Mount Tabor with ten thousand men from Naphtali and Zebulun. ⚔️
## Judges 4:7
He says, ‘I will draw Jabin’s commander Sisera, his chariots, and troops to the River Kishon. I will give you victory over him.’” 🛡️
## Judges 4:8
Barak answered, “I will go if you go with me. Otherwise, I will not.” 💬
## Judges 4:9
Deborah said, “I will go. But you will gain no honor on this road, because the LORD will sell Sisera into a woman’s hands.” She went with Barak to Kedesh. 🚶
## Judges 4:10
He called Zebulun and Naphtali. Ten thousand men followed, and Deborah went too. 👥
## Judges 4:11
Heber the Kenite separated from the Kenites, descendants of Moses’ father-in-law Hobab. He camped by Zaanannim’s great tree near Kedesh. ⛺
## Judges 4:12
Sisera heard that Barak son of Abinoam went up Mount Tabor. ⛰️
## Judges 4:13
He called all nine hundred iron chariots and his men from Harosheth-hagoyim to the River Kishon. ⚔️
## Judges 4:14
Deborah told Barak, “Rise! Today the LORD has given you victory over Sisera. Has He not gone before you?” Barak descended Mount Tabor with ten thousand men. 🛡️
## Judges 4:15
Before Barak, the LORD defeated Sisera, his charioteers, and his entire army with the sword. Sisera abandoned his chariot and fled on foot. ⚔️
## Judges 4:16
Barak chased the chariots and army to Harosheth-hagoyim. Sisera’s entire army died by the sword. None remained. ⚔️
## Judges 4:17
Sisera fled on foot to Jael’s tent. She was Heber the Kenite’s wife. Heber’s house had peace with Jabin king of Hazor. ⛺
## Judges 4:18
Jael greeted Sisera, saying, “Come in, my lord. Do not fear.” He entered, and she covered him with a blanket. ⛺
## Judges 4:19
Sisera said, “Give me water. I am thirsty.” She opened a milk container, gave him a drink, and covered him again. 🥛
## Judges 4:20
He said, “Guard the entrance. If anyone asks whether a man is here, say, ‘No.’” 🚪
## Judges 4:21
Exhausted, Sisera slept. Heber’s wife Jael quietly approached with a tent peg and hammer. She drove the peg through his temple into the ground, and he died. ⚠️
## Judges 4:22
Barak came chasing Sisera. Jael said, “Come see the man you seek.” He entered and saw Sisera dead, with the peg through his temple. 💔
## Judges 4:23
God subdued Jabin king of Canaan before Israel that day. 🛡️
## Judges 4:24
Israel grew stronger against Jabin king of Canaan until they destroyed him. ⚔️
