## Judges 10:1
After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, grandson of Dodo, from Issachar, arose to save Israel. He lived in Shamir in Ephraim’s hills. 🛡️
## Judges 10:2
Tola judged Israel twenty-three years, died, and was buried in Shamir. 🪦
## Judges 10:3
After Tola, Jair the Gileadite judged Israel twenty-two years. 📖
## Judges 10:4
His thirty sons rode thirty donkeys and owned thirty towns in Gilead. They are called Havvoth-jair to this day. 🫏
## Judges 10:5
Jair died and was buried in Kamon. 🪦
## Judges 10:6
Israel again did evil before the LORD. They served the Baals, Ashtoreths, and gods of Aram, Sidon, Moab, the Ammonites, and Philistines. They abandoned the LORD and did not serve Him. ⚠️
## Judges 10:7
The LORD’s anger burned. He sold Israel to the Philistines and Ammonites, ⚖️
## Judges 10:8
who began oppressing them that year. For eighteen years, they oppressed all Israel beyond the Jordan in Gilead, the Amorites’ land. 💔
## Judges 10:9
Ammonites crossed the Jordan against Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim’s house. Israel suffered greatly. ⚔️
## Judges 10:10
Israel cried, “LORD, we sinned against You, abandoned our God, and served the Baals.” 🙏
## Judges 10:11
The LORD answered, “When Egyptians, Amorites, Ammonites, Philistines, 📖
## Judges 10:12
Sidonians, Amalekites, and Maonites oppressed you, did I not rescue you when you cried to Me? 🛡️
## Judges 10:13
You abandoned Me for other gods. I will no longer save you. ⚠️
## Judges 10:14
Cry to your chosen gods. Let them save you in trouble.” 💬
## Judges 10:15
Israel answered the LORD, “We sinned. Do as You see fit, but please rescue us today!” 🙏
## Judges 10:16
They removed foreign gods and served the LORD. He could no longer bear Israel’s misery. ❤️
## Judges 10:17
Ammonites gathered for war in Gilead. Israel gathered and camped at Mizpah. ⛺
## Judges 10:18
Gilead’s rulers said, “Whoever leads the attack against the Ammonites will lead everyone living in Gilead.” 👑
