## Judges 16:1
At Gaza, Samson saw a prostitute and went to spend the night with her. 🌌
## Judges 16:2
The Gazites heard Samson was there, surrounded the place, and quietly waited all night at the gate. They planned to kill him at dawn. ⚠️
## Judges 16:3
At midnight, Samson pulled out the gate’s doors, both posts, and bar. He carried them on his shoulders to the mountain overlooking Hebron. ⛰️
## Judges 16:4
Samson later loved Delilah, a woman in the Valley of Sorek. ❤️
## Judges 16:5
Philistine lords asked her to entice him and discover his strength’s source and how to bind and subdue him. Each offered eleven hundred silver shekels. 🪙
## Judges 16:6
Delilah asked Samson, “What gives you strength? How can you be bound and subdued?” ❓
## Judges 16:7
Samson answered, “If they tie me with seven fresh, undried bowstrings, I will become as weak as other men.” 💬
## Judges 16:8
Philistine lords brought seven fresh, undried bowstrings. Delilah tied him with them. ⛓️
## Judges 16:9
Men hid in her room. She shouted, “Samson, Philistines!” He broke the bowstrings like yarn touched by flame. His strength’s source remained unknown. 🛡️
## Judges 16:10
Delilah told Samson, “You mocked and lied to me! Tell me how to bind you.” 💬
## Judges 16:11
He answered, “If they tie me with new, unused ropes, I will become as weak as other men.” ⛓️
## Judges 16:12
Delilah tied him with new ropes and shouted, “Samson, Philistines!” Men hid in her room, but he broke the ropes from his arms like threads. 🛡️
## Judges 16:13
Delilah demanded how to bind Samson after his lies. He said, “If you weave my seven braids into the loom’s web and tighten with a pin, I will be as weak as other men.” 💬
## Judges 16:14
As he slept, Delilah wove his seven braids and pinned them. She shouted, “Samson, Philistines!” He awoke and pulled out the pin, loom, and web. 🛡️
## Judges 16:15
She asked, “How can you claim love when your heart is not with me? This is the third time you mocked me without revealing your strength’s source!” 💔
## Judges 16:16
She pressed and pleaded daily until he could no longer bear it. 💬
## Judges 16:17
Samson told everything: “My hair was never cut. I have been God’s Nazirite from my mother’s womb. If I am shaved, my strength will leave, and I will be as weak as other men.” 💬
## Judges 16:18
Delilah realized he told everything and summoned the Philistine lords: “Come again. He told me everything.” They brought the money. 🪙
## Judges 16:19
She lulled him to sleep on her lap and called a man to shave his seven braids. She began subduing him, and his strength left. ⚠️
## Judges 16:20
She shouted, “Samson, Philistines!” He awoke, expecting to escape as before. He did not know the LORD had left him. 💔
## Judges 16:21
Philistines captured him, gouged out his eyes, and took him to Gaza. Bound in bronze shackles, he ground grain in prison. ⛓️
## Judges 16:22
His shaved hair began growing again. 📖
## Judges 16:23
Philistine lords gathered for a great sacrifice to their god Dagon. They rejoiced, “Our god gave us Samson, our enemy!” 💬
## Judges 16:24
Seeing him, the people praised their god: “He gave us the enemy who ruined our land and killed so many!” ⚠️
## Judges 16:25
They merrily called Samson from prison to entertain them and placed him between pillars. 🏛️
## Judges 16:26
Samson told the servant holding his hand, “Let me feel the temple’s supporting pillars so I can lean on them.” 💬
## Judges 16:27
The temple was full of men and women and all Philistine lords. About three thousand men and women watched Samson from the roof. 👥
## Judges 16:28
Samson prayed, “Lord GOD, remember me. Strengthen me once more, so one vengeful blow may repay the Philistines for my two eyes.” 🙏
## Judges 16:29
Samson grasped the temple’s two central supporting pillars, bracing his right hand on one and left on the other. 🏛️
## Judges 16:30
Samson said, “Let me die with Philistines.” He pushed with all his strength, collapsing the temple on its lords and everyone inside. He killed more in death than in life. 💔
## Judges 16:31
Samson’s brothers and father’s family carried him home. They buried him in his father Manoah’s tomb between Zorah and Eshtaol. He judged Israel twenty years. 🪦
