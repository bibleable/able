## Judges 12:1
Ephraim’s men crossed the Jordan to Zaphon. They asked Jephthah, “Why fight Ammonites without calling us? We will burn your house with you inside!” ⚠️
## Judges 12:2
Jephthah replied, “My people and I had a serious conflict with Ammonites. I called, but you did not rescue me. ⚔️
## Judges 12:3
I risked my life and attacked Ammonites when you would not help. The LORD gave me victory. Why fight me today?” ❓
## Judges 12:4
Jephthah gathered Gilead’s men and defeated Ephraim. Ephraimites had said, “You Gileadites are fugitives in Ephraim, living in Ephraim and Manasseh’s territories.” ⚔️
## Judges 12:5
Gileadites captured the Jordan’s crossings to Ephraim. They asked each Ephraimite fugitive seeking passage whether he was an Ephraimite. If he denied it, 🌊
## Judges 12:6
they asked him to say “Shibboleth.” If he said “Sibboleth,” unable to pronounce it correctly, they killed him at the Jordan’s crossings. They killed 42,000 Ephraimites then. 💔
## Judges 12:7
Jephthah judged Israel six years, died, and was buried in one of Gilead’s cities. 🪦
## Judges 12:8
After Jephthah, Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel. 📖
## Judges 12:9
He had thirty sons and thirty daughters. He married his daughters outside his clan and brought thirty outside wives for his sons. Ibzan judged Israel seven years. 💍
## Judges 12:10
Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem. 🪦
## Judges 12:11
After Ibzan, Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel ten years. 📖
## Judges 12:12
Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried at Aijalon in Zebulun. 🪦
## Judges 12:13
After Elon, Abdon son of Hillel, from Pirathon, became judge. 📖
## Judges 12:14
His forty sons and thirty grandsons rode seventy donkeys. He judged Israel eight years. 🫏
## Judges 12:15
Abdon son of Hillel, from Pirathon, died. He was buried at Pirathon in Ephraim, in the Amalekites’ hills. 🪦
