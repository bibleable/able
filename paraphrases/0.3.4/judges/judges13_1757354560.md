## Judges 13:1
Israel again did evil before the LORD. He handed them to the Philistines forty years. ⚠️
## Judges 13:2
Manoah, a Danite from Zorah, had a wife unable to bear children. 👥
## Judges 13:3
The LORD’s angel told her, “You cannot bear children now, but you will conceive and bear a son. 👶
## Judges 13:4
Carefully avoid wine, strong drink, and unclean food. ⚠️
## Judges 13:5
You will conceive and bear a son. No razor may touch his head. He will be God’s Nazirite from the womb and begin rescuing Israel from Philistines.” 🛡️
## Judges 13:6
She told her husband, “A man of God appeared, exceedingly awesome like God’s angel. I did not ask his origin, and he gave no name. 💬
## Judges 13:7
He said I would conceive a son. I must avoid wine, strong drink, and unclean food because he will be God’s Nazirite from the womb until death.” 👶
## Judges 13:8
Manoah prayed, “Lord, please send the man of God again to teach us how to raise the coming boy.” 🙏
## Judges 13:9
God heard Manoah. God’s angel returned to the woman in the field, while Manoah was absent. 🌾
## Judges 13:10
She ran to her husband, saying, “The man who appeared the other day returned!” 📣
## Judges 13:11
Manoah followed her and asked, “Are you the man who spoke to my wife?” He answered, “I am.” ❓
## Judges 13:12
Manoah asked, “When your words come true, what rules and mission will the boy have?” 📖
## Judges 13:13
The LORD’s angel answered Manoah, “Your wife must follow everything I told her. 📖
## Judges 13:14
She must eat nothing from the vine, drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean. She must obey all my commands.” ⚠️
## Judges 13:15
Manoah told the LORD’s angel, “Please stay while we prepare a young goat.” 🎁
## Judges 13:16
The angel answered, “Even if I stay, I will not eat your food. Offer any burnt offering to the LORD.” Manoah did not know he was the LORD’s angel. 🙏
## Judges 13:17
Manoah asked the LORD’s angel, “What is your name, so we can honor you when your word comes true?” ❓
## Judges 13:18
The LORD’s angel answered, “Why ask my name? It is beyond understanding.” ✨
## Judges 13:19
Manoah offered a young goat and a grain offering on a rock to the LORD. The LORD did something marvelous as Manoah and his wife watched. 🔥
## Judges 13:20
The LORD’s angel rose in the altar’s flame toward heaven. Manoah and his wife saw and fell facedown. 🙏
## Judges 13:21
The LORD’s angel did not reappear to Manoah and his wife. Manoah realized he was the LORD’s angel. 👁️
## Judges 13:22
He told his wife, “We will die because we saw God!” 😨
## Judges 13:23
She answered, “If the LORD intended to kill us, He would not accept our burnt and grain offerings. He would not show or tell us all this.” 🙏
## Judges 13:24
She bore a son and named him Samson. He grew, and the LORD blessed him. 👶
## Judges 13:25
The LORD’s Spirit began stirring him at Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol. 🕊️
