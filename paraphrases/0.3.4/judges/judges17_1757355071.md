## Judges 17:1
Micah lived in Ephraim’s hill country. 🏠
## Judges 17:2
He told his mother, “I took your eleven hundred silver shekels. I heard your curse, and I have the silver.” She said, “May the LORD bless my son!” 🪙
## Judges 17:3
He returned the eleven hundred shekels. She said, “I dedicate all this to the LORD for my son, to make a carved image and cast idol. I now return it to you.” 🎁
## Judges 17:4
He returned it to his mother. She gave two hundred shekels to a silversmith for a carved image and cast idol. They were placed in Micah’s house. 🏠
## Judges 17:5
Micah had a shrine, made an ephod and household idols, and appointed one son as priest. ⚠️
## Judges 17:6
Israel had no king then. Everyone did what was right in his own eyes. 📖
## Judges 17:7
A young Levite lived in Bethlehem in Judah among Judah’s clan. 👥
## Judges 17:8
He left Bethlehem in Judah to find somewhere to settle. He reached Micah’s house in Ephraim’s hills. 🚶
## Judges 17:9
Micah asked his origin. He answered, “I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, seeking a place to settle.” 💬
## Judges 17:10
Micah offered, “Be my father and priest. I will give ten silver shekels yearly, clothes, and provisions.” The Levite entered 📖
## Judges 17:11
and agreed to stay. He became like a son to Micah. 🤝
## Judges 17:12
Micah appointed the Levite as his priest, and he lived there. 🏠
## Judges 17:13
Micah said, “Now I know the LORD will favor me, because my priest is a Levite.” 🙏
