## Judges 1:1
After Joshua died, Israel asked the LORD, “Who should first fight the Canaanites for us?” ⚔️
## Judges 1:2
The LORD answered, “Judah should go. I have given them the land.” 🛡️
## Judges 1:3
Judah’s men told the Simeonites, “Help us fight the Canaanites in our allotted land. We will likewise help in yours.” The Simeonites went with them. 🤝
## Judges 1:4
Judah attacked, and the LORD gave them victory over the Canaanites and Perizzites. They killed ten thousand men at Bezek. ⚔️
## Judges 1:5
There they found and fought Adoni-bezek, defeating the Canaanites and Perizzites. ⚔️
## Judges 1:6
Adoni-bezek fled. They chased and captured him, then cut off his thumbs and big toes. ⚠️
## Judges 1:7
Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings with thumbs and big toes cut off gathered scraps under my table. God repaid me as I treated them.” They brought him to Jerusalem, where he died. ⚖️
## Judges 1:8
Judah’s men captured Jerusalem, killed people with the sword, and burned the city. 🔥
## Judges 1:9
Judah’s men went down to fight Canaanites in the hill country, Negev, and foothills. ⚔️
## Judges 1:10
Judah attacked Hebron’s Canaanites and killed Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai. Hebron was formerly Kiriath-arba. 🏙️
## Judges 1:11
They marched against Debir’s people. Debir was formerly Kiriath-sepher. ⚔️
## Judges 1:12
Caleb promised, “I will give my daughter Acsah in marriage to whoever defeats and captures Kiriath-sepher.” 💍
## Judges 1:13
Othniel captured it. He was the son of Caleb’s younger brother Kenaz. Caleb gave him Acsah in marriage. 💍
## Judges 1:14
Acsah urged Othniel to ask her father for a field. She got off her donkey, and Caleb asked, “What do you want?” 🌾
## Judges 1:15
She said, “Give me a blessing. You gave me Negev land, so give me water springs too.” Caleb gave her the upper and lower springs. 💧
## Judges 1:16
The descendants of Moses’ father-in-law, the Kenite, left the City of Palms with Judah’s men. They went to Judah’s Wilderness in the Negev near Arad and settled among the people. ⛺
## Judges 1:17
Judah and the Simeonites attacked Zephath’s Canaanites and devoted the city to destruction. It was therefore called Hormah. ⚔️
## Judges 1:18
Judah captured Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron, with each city’s territory. 🏙️
## Judges 1:19
The LORD was with Judah, who captured the hill country. They could not drive out the plains’ people because they had iron chariots. ⚔️
## Judges 1:20
As Moses promised, Judah gave Hebron to Caleb. He drove out the descendants of Anak’s three sons. 🛡️
## Judges 1:21
The Benjamites did not drive out Jerusalem’s Jebusites. The Jebusites still live there among the Benjamites to this day. 🏙️
## Judges 1:22
Joseph’s house attacked Bethel, and the LORD was with them. ⚔️
## Judges 1:23
They sent spies to Bethel, formerly Luz. 👁️
## Judges 1:24
The spies saw a man leaving and said, “Show us the city’s entrance, and we will treat you kindly.” 🤝
## Judges 1:25
He showed them. They killed the city’s people with the sword, but released him and his entire family. ⚔️
## Judges 1:26
He went to Hittite land, built a city, and named it Luz. It still has that name to this day. 🏙️
## Judges 1:27
Manasseh failed to drive out people from Beth-shean, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, Megiddo, and their villages. The Canaanites insisted on living there. 🏙️
## Judges 1:28
Stronger Israel forced Canaanites to labor, but never drove them out completely. ⛓️
## Judges 1:29
Ephraim failed to drive Gezer’s Canaanites out. They continued living among them in Gezer. 🏙️
## Judges 1:30
Zebulun failed to drive out Kitron and Nahalol’s people. Canaanites lived among them as forced laborers. ⛓️
## Judges 1:31
Asher failed to drive out people from Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob. 🏙️
## Judges 1:32
The Asherites therefore lived among the land’s Canaanites, whom they did not drive out. 🏠
## Judges 1:33
Naphtali failed to drive out Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath’s people. They lived among the Canaanites, but forced Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath’s people to labor. ⛓️
## Judges 1:34
The Amorites forced the Danites into the hills and prevented them from entering the plain. ⛰️
## Judges 1:35
Amorites insisted on living in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. Joseph’s house grew stronger and forced them to labor. ⛓️
## Judges 1:36
The Amorites’ border reached from the Ascent of Akrabbim to Sela and beyond. 🌍
