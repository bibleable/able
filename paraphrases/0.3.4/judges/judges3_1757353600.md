## Judges 3:1
The LORD left these nations to test Israelites who had not experienced Canaan’s wars. ⚔️
## Judges 3:2
He would teach later generations warfare, especially those with no previous experience: 📖
## Judges 3:3
the five Philistine rulers, all Canaanites, Sidonians, and Hivites living in Lebanon’s mountains from Mount Baal-hermon to Lebo-hamath. ⛰️
## Judges 3:4
These nations were left to test whether Israel would keep the LORD’s commands given their fathers through Moses. 📖
## Judges 3:5
Israel lived among Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 🏠
## Judges 3:6
They married these peoples’ daughters, gave their daughters to these peoples’ sons, and served their gods. 💍
## Judges 3:7
Israel did evil before the LORD. They forgot their God and served the Baals and Asherahs. ⚠️
## Judges 3:8
The LORD angrily sold Israel to Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram-naharaim. They served him eight years. ⛓️
## Judges 3:9
Israel cried to the LORD. He raised Othniel, son of Caleb’s younger brother Kenaz, to rescue them. 🙏
## Judges 3:10
The LORD’s Spirit came on Othniel, who judged Israel and went to war. The LORD gave him victory over Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram, and Othniel defeated him. 🛡️
## Judges 3:11
The land rested forty years until Othniel son of Kenaz died. 🕊️
## Judges 3:12
Israel again did evil before the LORD. Because of this, He gave Eglon king of Moab power over them. ⚖️
## Judges 3:13
Eglon joined with Ammonites and Amalekites, defeated Israel, and captured the City of Palms. ⚔️
## Judges 3:14
Israel served Eglon king of Moab eighteen years. ⛓️
## Judges 3:15
They cried to the LORD, who raised Ehud son of Gera, a left-handed Benjamite, as deliverer. They sent him with tribute to Eglon king of Moab. 🙏
## Judges 3:16
Ehud made a sword with two cutting edges, a cubit long. He strapped it under his cloak to his right thigh. ⚔️
## Judges 3:17
He brought tribute to Eglon king of Moab, an obese man. 🪙
## Judges 3:18
After presenting it, Ehud sent the tribute carriers away. 🚶
## Judges 3:19
At the idols near Gilgal, he turned back and said, “King, I have a secret message.” The king ordered silence, and all attendants left. 💬
## Judges 3:20
Ehud approached Eglon, sitting alone in his cool upper room. Ehud said, “I have a message from God for you.” The king stood. 🏠
## Judges 3:21
Ehud drew the sword from his right thigh with his left hand and thrust it into Eglon’s belly. ⚔️
## Judges 3:22
The handle sank in after the blade, and fat closed over it. Ehud left the sword in his belly. Eglon’s bowels emptied. 💔
## Judges 3:23
Ehud left through the porch, shutting and locking the upper room’s doors. 🚪
## Judges 3:24
Eglon’s servants found the doors locked after Ehud left. They said, “He must be relieving himself in the cool room.” 💬
## Judges 3:25
They waited until worried, but he never opened the doors. They used a key and found their lord dead on the floor. ⚠️
## Judges 3:26
Ehud escaped while they waited. He passed the idols and fled to Seirah. 🚶
## Judges 3:27
At Seirah, he blew the ram’s horn throughout Ephraim’s hills. Israel followed him down from the hills, and he led them. 📯
## Judges 3:28
He said, “Follow me. The LORD gave you victory over your enemies, the Moabites.” They followed, seized the Jordan’s crossings to Moab, and let no one cross. 🛡️
## Judges 3:29
They killed about ten thousand strong, brave Moabites. None escaped. ⚔️
## Judges 3:30
Moab was subdued under Israel that day. The land rested eighty years. 🕊️
## Judges 3:31
After Ehud came Shamgar son of Anath. He also saved Israel, killing six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad. 🛡️
