## Judges 11:1
Jephthah the Gileadite was mighty and brave. His mother was a prostitute, and his father was Gilead. 👥
## Judges 11:2
Gilead’s wife bore sons who grew up and expelled Jephthah. They said, “You get no inheritance here because your mother is another woman.” 💔
## Judges 11:3
Jephthah fled to Tob. Worthless men gathered and traveled with him. 🚶
## Judges 11:4
Later, Ammonites fought Israel. ⚔️
## Judges 11:5
During the war, Gilead’s elders went to Tob for Jephthah. 👥
## Judges 11:6
They said, “Be our commander so we can fight Ammonites.” ⚔️
## Judges 11:7
Jephthah asked Gilead’s elders, “Did you not hate and expel me from my father’s house? Why seek me now in trouble?” ❓
## Judges 11:8
They told Jephthah, “We turn to you to fight Ammonites with us and lead everyone in Gilead.” 👑
## Judges 11:9
Jephthah asked, “If I return to fight Ammonites and the LORD gives me victory, will I truly lead you?” ❓
## Judges 11:10
Gilead’s elders told Jephthah, “The LORD witnesses whether we keep your terms.” 🙏
## Judges 11:11
Jephthah went with Gilead’s elders. The people made him leader and commander. He repeated every term before the LORD at Mizpah. 📖
## Judges 11:12
Jephthah sent messengers to the Ammonites’ king: “Why do you come to fight my land? What do you have against me?” 💬
## Judges 11:13
The Ammonites’ king told Jephthah’s messengers, “Israel seized my land from the Arnon to the Jabbok and Jordan when they left Egypt. Return it peacefully.” 🌍
## Judges 11:14
Jephthah sent messengers back to the Ammonites’ king, 💬
## Judges 11:15
saying, “Jephthah says Israel did not seize the land of Moab or the Ammonites. 🌍
## Judges 11:16
From Egypt, Israel crossed the wilderness to the Red Sea and Kadesh. 🚶
## Judges 11:17
Israel asked Edom’s king for passage, but he refused. Moab’s king also refused their messengers. Israel stayed in Kadesh. 💬
## Judges 11:18
They traveled around Edom and Moab through the wilderness. They camped east of Moab, beyond the Arnon, without entering Moab because the Arnon was its border. 🌍
## Judges 11:19
Israel asked Sihon, the Amorite king in Heshbon, for passage through his land to their own place. 💬
## Judges 11:20
Sihon distrusted them, assembled his people at Jahaz, and fought Israel. ⚔️
## Judges 11:21
Israel’s God, the LORD, gave them victory over Sihon and all his people. Israel captured all the Amorites’ land there, 🛡️
## Judges 11:22
from the Arnon to the Jabbok, and from wilderness to Jordan. 🌍
## Judges 11:23
The LORD, Israel’s God, drove the Amorites out before His people. Should you possess their land now? ❓
## Judges 11:24
You possess what your god Chemosh grants. Likewise, we possess what the LORD our God grants. 🌍
## Judges 11:25
Are you better than Balak son of Zippor, Moab’s king? Did he fight or contend with Israel? ❓
## Judges 11:26
Israel lived three hundred years in Heshbon, Aroer, their villages, and cities along the Arnon. Why did you not reclaim them then? ⏳
## Judges 11:27
I did not wrong you. You wronged me by waging war. May the LORD, the Judge, decide between Israel and the Ammonites today.” ⚖️
## Judges 11:28
The Ammonites’ king ignored Jephthah’s message. ⚠️
## Judges 11:29
The LORD’s Spirit came on Jephthah. He passed through Gilead, Manasseh, and Mizpah of Gilead, then advanced against Ammonites. 🛡️
## Judges 11:30
Jephthah vowed to the LORD, “If You give me victory over Ammonites, 🙏
## Judges 11:31
whatever comes from my house’s door to greet my victorious return from the Ammonites will belong to the LORD. I will offer it as a burnt offering.” 🔥
## Judges 11:32
Jephthah attacked the Ammonites, and the LORD gave him victory. ⚔️
## Judges 11:33
He devastated twenty cities from Aroer to near Minnith, as far as Abel-keramim. The Ammonites were subdued before Israel. ⚔️
## Judges 11:34
Jephthah returned to Mizpah. His daughter greeted him with tambourines and dancing. She was his only child. He had no other son or daughter. 🎵
## Judges 11:35
Seeing her, Jephthah tore his clothes and cried, “No, my daughter! You brought me to my knees and into great misery. I gave the LORD my word and cannot take it back.” 💔
## Judges 11:36
She answered, “Father, do as you promised the LORD. He avenged you against your enemies, the Ammonites.” 🙏
## Judges 11:37
She asked, “Let me wander in the mountains with my friends for two months to mourn my virginity.” 💧
## Judges 11:38
He said, “Go,” and sent her away two months. She and her friends mourned her virginity in the mountains. ⛰️
## Judges 11:39
After two months, she returned. Her father did as he vowed. She had never had relations with a man. Israel adopted a custom: 💔
## Judges 11:40
each year, Israel’s young women go out four days to lament Jephthah the Gileadite’s daughter. 💧
