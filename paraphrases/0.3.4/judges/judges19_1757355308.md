## Judges 19:1
Israel had no king. A Levite in Ephraim’s remote hills took a concubine from Bethlehem in Judah. 👥
## Judges 19:2
She was unfaithful and returned to her father in Bethlehem in Judah. She stayed four months. 💔
## Judges 19:3
Her husband followed with a servant and pair of donkeys to speak kindly and bring her back. She brought him inside, and her father welcomed him gladly. 🤝
## Judges 19:4
Her father persuaded him to stay three days, eating, drinking, and lodging there. 🏠
## Judges 19:5
Early on the fourth day, they prepared to leave. Her father said, “Refresh yourself with bread, then go.” 🍞
## Judges 19:6
The two ate and drank. Her father asked, “Stay overnight and enjoy yourself.” 🍽️
## Judges 19:7
The man tried to leave, but his father-in-law persuaded him to stay that night. 🏠
## Judges 19:8
Early on the fifth day, he again prepared to leave. Her father said, “Refresh yourself.” They waited until late afternoon, and the two ate. 🍞
## Judges 19:9
He rose to leave with his concubine and servant. Her father urged, “Evening is here. Stay tonight and enjoy yourself. Leave early tomorrow for home.” 🌆
## Judges 19:10
He refused another night and reached Jebus, meaning Jerusalem, with his two saddled donkeys and concubine. 🚶
## Judges 19:11
Near Jebus at nightfall, the servant said, “Let us stay in this Jebusite city.” 🌆
## Judges 19:12
His master answered, “We will not enter a foreign city without Israelites. We will go to Gibeah.” 💬
## Judges 19:13
He said, “Let us reach Gibeah or Ramah for the night.” 🚶
## Judges 19:14
They reached Gibeah in Benjamin as the sun set. 🌆
## Judges 19:15
They entered Gibeah to stay. The Levite sat in the square, but no one offered lodging. 🏙️
## Judges 19:16
An old man from Ephraim’s hills, living among Gibeah’s Benjamites, returned from fieldwork that evening. 🌾
## Judges 19:17
Seeing the traveler, he asked, “Where do you come from? Where are you going?” ❓
## Judges 19:18
The Levite answered, “We travel from Bethlehem in Judah to my home in Ephraim’s remote hills. I visited Bethlehem in Judah and now go to the LORD’s house. No one offers lodging, 💬
## Judges 19:19
though we have straw and donkey feed, bread and wine for me, the maidservant, and the young man. Your servants lack nothing.” 🍞
## Judges 19:20
The old man said, “Peace. I will supply everything. Only avoid sleeping in the square.” 🏠
## Judges 19:21
He brought him home and fed the donkeys. They washed their feet, ate, and drank. 🍽️
## Judges 19:22
As the guests enjoyed themselves, wicked city men surrounded the house and pounded the door. They demanded, “Bring your male guest out so we can have relations with him!” ⚠️
## Judges 19:23
The old owner pleaded, “Brothers, do not do this wicked outrage! He is my guest. 💬
## Judges 19:24
I will bring my virgin daughter and his concubine. Use them as you wish, but do not do this vile thing to him.” 💔
## Judges 19:25
They refused to listen. The Levite sent his concubine out. They raped and abused her all night, releasing her at dawn. 💔
## Judges 19:26
She returned early to the house where her master stayed, collapsed at the door, and lay until daylight. 💔
## Judges 19:27
He opened the door in the morning to leave. His concubine lay collapsed with her hands on the threshold. 💔
## Judges 19:28
He said, “Rise. Let us go.” She did not answer. He put her on his donkey and headed home. 🚶
## Judges 19:29
At home, he took a knife, cut his concubine limb by limb into twelve pieces, and sent her throughout Israel. 💔
## Judges 19:30
Everyone seeing it said, “Nothing like this happened since Israel left Egypt! Consider it, seek advice, and speak up!” ⚠️
