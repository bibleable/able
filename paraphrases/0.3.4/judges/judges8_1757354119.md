## Judges 8:1
Ephraim’s men argued violently with Gideon. “Why did you fail to call us to fight Midian?” they demanded. 💬
## Judges 8:2
Gideon answered, “What did I do compared with you? Are Ephraim’s gleanings not better than Abiezer’s grape harvest? 🍇
## Judges 8:3
God handed you Midian’s two princes, Oreb and Zeeb. What did I do compared with you?” Their anger faded. 🤝
## Judges 8:4
Gideon and his three hundred crossed the Jordan, exhausted but still pursuing. 🚶
## Judges 8:5
Gideon asked Succoth’s men, “Give my tired troops bread. I still pursue Zebah and Zalmunna, Midian’s kings.” 🍞
## Judges 8:6
Succoth’s leaders asked, “Have you already captured Zebah and Zalmunna, so we should feed your army?” ❓
## Judges 8:7
Gideon replied, “When the LORD hands me Zebah and Zalmunna, I will tear your flesh with wilderness thorns and briers!” ⚠️
## Judges 8:8
He made the same request at Penuel. Its men answered like Succoth’s men. 🏙️
## Judges 8:9
Gideon told Penuel’s men, “When I return victorious, I will destroy this tower!” ⚠️
## Judges 8:10
Zebah and Zalmunna were at Karkor with about fifteen thousand men. They were all that remained of the eastern armies. A hundred and twenty thousand swordsmen had died. ⚔️
## Judges 8:11
Gideon followed the caravan route east of Nobah and Jogbehah, attacking the army by surprise. ⚔️
## Judges 8:12
Zebah and Zalmunna fled. Gideon captured the two kings of Midian and defeated their whole army. 🛡️
## Judges 8:13
Gideon son of Joash returned from battle along the Ascent of Heres. 🚶
## Judges 8:14
He captured and questioned a young man from Succoth. The young man listed Succoth’s seventy-seven leaders and elders for him. 📜
## Judges 8:15
Gideon told Succoth’s men, “Here are Zebah and Zalmunna! You taunted me, asking, ‘Have you captured them, so we should feed your tired men?’” 💬
## Judges 8:16
He took the city’s elders and disciplined Succoth’s men with wilderness thorns and briers. ⚠️
## Judges 8:17
He destroyed Penuel’s tower and killed the city’s men. ⚔️
## Judges 8:18
Gideon asked Zebah and Zalmunna, “What men did you kill at Tabor?” They answered, “Men like you, each resembling a king’s son.” ❓
## Judges 8:19
Gideon replied, “My brothers, my mother’s sons! As surely as the LORD lives, if you had spared them, I would not kill you.” ⚖️
## Judges 8:20
He told his firstborn Jether, “Kill them.” Jether was young and afraid, so he did not draw his sword. ⚔️
## Judges 8:21
Zebah and Zalmunna said, “Kill us yourself, for as the man is, so is his strength.” Gideon killed them and took the camels’ crescent neck ornaments. ⚔️
## Judges 8:22
Israel asked, “Gideon, rule us with your son and grandson, because you saved us from Midian.” 👑
## Judges 8:23
Gideon answered, “Neither I nor my son will rule you. The LORD will rule you.” 🙏
## Judges 8:24
He requested an earring from each man’s plunder. The enemies wore gold earrings because they were Ishmaelites. 🪙
## Judges 8:25
They gladly agreed, spread a garment, and each threw an earring onto it. 🎁
## Judges 8:26
The gold earrings weighed 1,700 shekels. This excluded crescent ornaments, pendants, purple garments of Midian’s kings, and chains from their camels’ necks. ⚖️
## Judges 8:27
Gideon made an ephod from all this and placed it in his hometown, Ophrah. Soon all Israel prostituted themselves by worshiping it. It trapped Gideon and his household. ⚠️
## Judges 8:28
Midian was subdued before Israel and never raised its head again. The land rested forty years during Gideon’s days. 🕊️
## Judges 8:29
Jerubbaal son of Joash, meaning Gideon, returned and settled at home. 🏠
## Judges 8:30
Gideon had seventy sons of his own because he had many wives. 👥
## Judges 8:31
His concubine in Shechem bore a son. He named him Abimelech. 👶
## Judges 8:32
Gideon son of Joash died at a ripe old age. He was buried in his father Joash’s tomb in Ophrah of the Abiezrites. 🪦
## Judges 8:33
As soon as Gideon died, Israel again prostituted themselves with the Baals. They made Baal-berith their god. ⚠️
## Judges 8:34
They forgot the LORD their God, who rescued them from all enemies around them. 💔
## Judges 8:35
They showed no kindness to Jerubbaal’s house, meaning Gideon’s, despite all he did for Israel. 💔
