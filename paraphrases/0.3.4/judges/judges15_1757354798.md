## Judges 15:1
At wheat harvest, Samson brought a young goat to visit his wife. He asked to enter her room, but her father refused. 🌾
## Judges 15:2
Her father said, “I thought you hated her, so I gave her to your companion. Take her younger, more beautiful sister instead.” 💬
## Judges 15:3
Samson said, “This time, harming the Philistines will leave me blameless.” ⚠️
## Judges 15:4
Samson caught three hundred foxes, paired them tail to tail, and fixed a torch between each pair’s tails. 🔥
## Judges 15:5
He lit them and released the foxes into Philistine grainfields. They burned standing grain, grain piles, vineyards, and olive groves. 🔥
## Judges 15:6
Philistines asked who did it. They learned, “Samson, the Timnite’s son-in-law, because his wife was given to his companion.” They burned her and her father to death. 💔
## Judges 15:7
Samson said, “Because you did this, I will not rest until I take revenge.” ⚔️
## Judges 15:8
He slaughtered them ruthlessly, then stayed in the cave at Etam’s rock. ⚔️
## Judges 15:9
Philistines camped in Judah and deployed near Lehi. ⛺
## Judges 15:10
Judah’s men asked, “Why attack us?” Philistines answered, “To arrest Samson and repay what he did.” 💬
## Judges 15:11
Three thousand men of Judah came to the cave at Etam’s rock. They asked Samson, “Philistines rule us. What did you do?” He answered, “What they did to me.” 💬
## Judges 15:12
They said, “We will arrest you and hand you to Philistines.” Samson answered, “Swear you will not kill me yourselves.” ⚠️
## Judges 15:13
They promised, “We will not kill you. We will tie you securely and hand you over.” They bound him with two new ropes and brought him from the rock. ⛓️
## Judges 15:14
Philistines shouted at Samson in Lehi. The LORD’s Spirit came powerfully on him. His arm ropes became like burnt flax, and his hands’ bonds loosened. 🛡️
## Judges 15:15
He picked up a fresh donkey jawbone and killed a thousand men. ⚔️
## Judges 15:16
Samson said, “With a donkey’s jawbone, I piled them in heaps. With a donkey’s jawbone, I killed a thousand men.” ⚔️
## Judges 15:17
Samson threw the jawbone away and named the place Ramath-lehi. 🌍
## Judges 15:18
Very thirsty, Samson cried, “LORD, You accomplished great deliverance through Your servant. Must I die thirsty and fall to the uncircumcised?” 🙏
## Judges 15:19
God opened Lehi’s hollow place, and water flowed out. Samson drank, his strength returned, and he revived. He named it En-hakkore, which remains in Lehi to this day. 💧
## Judges 15:20
Samson judged Israel twenty years during the Philistines’ days. 📖
