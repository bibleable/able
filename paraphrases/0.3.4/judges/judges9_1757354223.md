## Judges 9:1
Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to his mother’s brothers and whole clan in Shechem. He said: 👥
## Judges 9:2
“Ask Shechem’s leaders whether seventy men, Jerubbaal’s sons, should rule them, or one man. Remember, I am your flesh and blood.” 👑
## Judges 9:3
His mother’s brothers repeated this to Shechem’s leaders. They favored Abimelech, saying, “He is our brother.” 🤝
## Judges 9:4
They gave him seventy silver shekels from Baal-berith’s temple. Abimelech hired worthless, reckless followers with it. 🪙
## Judges 9:5
At his father’s house in Ophrah, he murdered his seventy brothers, Jerubbaal’s sons, on one stone. Jerubbaal’s youngest son, Jotham, hid and survived. 💔
## Judges 9:6
Shechem and Beth-millo’s leaders gathered by the oak at Shechem’s pillar and made Abimelech king. 👑
## Judges 9:7
Jotham heard, climbed Mount Gerizim, and shouted, “Shechem’s leaders, listen! May God listen to you. 📣
## Judges 9:8
The trees went to anoint a king. They asked the olive tree, ‘Rule us.’ 🌳
## Judges 9:9
It answered, ‘Should I stop giving oil that honors God and people to rule trees?’ 🫒
## Judges 9:10
They asked the fig tree, ‘Come rule us.’ 🌳
## Judges 9:11
It answered, ‘Should I stop giving sweet, good fruit to rule trees?’ 🌳
## Judges 9:12
They asked the grapevine, ‘Come rule us.’ 🍇
## Judges 9:13
It answered, ‘Should I stop giving wine that cheers God and people to rule trees?’ 🍷
## Judges 9:14
All the trees finally asked the thornbush, ‘Come rule us.’ 🌵
## Judges 9:15
It answered, ‘If you truly anoint me king, shelter in my shade. Otherwise, may fire come from the thornbush and consume Lebanon’s cedars.’ 🔥
## Judges 9:16
Consider whether you acted faithfully and honestly in making Abimelech king. Did you treat Jerubbaal and his family well, as he deserved? ⚖️
## Judges 9:17
My father fought for you, risking his life to rescue you from Midian. 🛡️
## Judges 9:18
Today you attacked his house and killed seventy sons on one stone. You made his maidservant’s son Abimelech king over Shechem’s leaders because he is your brother. 💔
## Judges 9:19
If you treated Jerubbaal and his house faithfully and honestly today, may you and Abimelech rejoice in each other. 🤝
## Judges 9:20
Otherwise, may Abimelech’s fire consume Shechem and Beth-millo’s leaders. May their fire consume Abimelech.” 🔥
## Judges 9:21
Jotham fled to Beer and lived there, fearing his brother Abimelech. 🚶
## Judges 9:22
Abimelech ruled Israel three years. 👑
## Judges 9:23
God sent a spirit of hostility between Abimelech and Shechem’s leaders, causing them to deceive him. ⚖️
## Judges 9:24
This was to bring justice for Jerubbaal’s seventy murdered sons and avenge their blood. Their brother Abimelech and Shechem’s leaders, who helped him murder them, would face this vengeance. ⚖️
## Judges 9:25
Shechem’s leaders set an ambush against Abimelech on hilltops and robbed everyone passing on the road. Abimelech heard about it. ⚠️
## Judges 9:26
Gaal son of Ebed entered Shechem with his brothers. Its leaders trusted him. 👥
## Judges 9:27
They harvested and crushed grapes, then celebrated in their god’s house. They ate, drank, and cursed Abimelech. 🍇
## Judges 9:28
Gaal son of Ebed asked, “Why serve Abimelech? Who is he, and who is Shechem? He is Jerubbaal’s son, and Zebul is his officer. Serve the men of Hamor, Shechem’s father. Why serve Abimelech? 💬
## Judges 9:29
If I controlled these people, I would remove Abimelech. I would tell him, ‘Gather your army and come out!’” ⚔️
## Judges 9:30
Zebul, the city’s governor, became furious at Gaal son of Ebed’s words. ⚠️
## Judges 9:31
He secretly told Abimelech through messengers, “Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers are turning Shechem against you. 💬
## Judges 9:32
Bring your men tonight and wait in ambush in the fields. 🌌
## Judges 9:33
At sunrise, advance against the city. When Gaal and his men come out, do whatever you can against them.” ⚔️
## Judges 9:34
Abimelech and his troops went at night and waited in ambush against Shechem in four groups. ⚔️
## Judges 9:35
Gaal son of Ebed stood at the city gate as Abimelech’s men left their hiding places. 🚪
## Judges 9:36
Gaal told Zebul, “People are coming from the mountains!” Zebul answered, “You mistake mountain shadows for men.” 👁️
## Judges 9:37
Gaal said, “People come from the land’s center. One group comes by the Diviners’ Oak.” 🌳
## Judges 9:38
Zebul asked, “Where is your boasting now? You asked why we should serve Abimelech. These are the people you mocked. Go fight them!” ⚔️
## Judges 9:39
Gaal led Shechem’s leaders out against Abimelech. ⚔️
## Judges 9:40
Abimelech chased Gaal, who fled. Many Shechemites fell wounded along the way to the gate. 💔
## Judges 9:41
Abimelech stayed in Arumah. Zebul expelled Gaal and his brothers from Shechem. 🏙️
## Judges 9:42
Next day, Shechem’s people entered the fields. Abimelech heard this. 🌾
## Judges 9:43
Abimelech divided his men into three groups and waited in ambush in the fields. He attacked when the people came out. ⚔️
## Judges 9:44
Abimelech’s group rushed to the gate’s entrance. The other two groups rushed against everyone in the fields and killed them. ⚔️
## Judges 9:45
Abimelech fought all day, captured the city, and killed its people. He demolished it and sowed salt over it. 💔
## Judges 9:46
All leaders in Shechem’s tower heard and entered the inner chamber of El-berith’s temple. 🏛️
## Judges 9:47
Abimelech learned that all Shechem’s tower leaders had gathered there. 💬
## Judges 9:48
He took all his men up Mount Zalmon. Abimelech cut a branch with his axe, shouldered it, and said, “Quickly copy me.” 🌳
## Judges 9:49
Each cut a branch and followed Abimelech. They piled branches against the chamber and burned it over those inside. Everyone in Shechem’s tower died, about a thousand men and women. 🔥
## Judges 9:50
Abimelech besieged and captured Thebez. ⚔️
## Judges 9:51
Its men, women, and leaders fled into a strong tower. They locked it and climbed to its roof. 🏠
## Judges 9:52
Abimelech attacked and approached the tower’s entrance to burn it. 🔥
## Judges 9:53
A woman dropped an upper millstone on his head, crushing Abimelech’s skull. ⚠️
## Judges 9:54
He called his armor-bearer, “Kill me with your sword, so they cannot say a woman killed me.” The armor-bearer pierced Abimelech with his sword, and he died. ⚔️
## Judges 9:55
Israel saw Abimelech dead and went home. 🏠
## Judges 9:56
God repaid Abimelech’s wickedness against his father in murdering his seventy brothers. ⚖️
## Judges 9:57
God brought Shechem’s wickedness back on its people’s heads. Jotham son of Jerubbaal’s curse came on them. ⚖️
