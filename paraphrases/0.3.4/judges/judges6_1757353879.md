## Judges 6:1
Israel again did evil before the LORD. He handed them to Midian for seven years. ⚠️
## Judges 6:2
Midian overpowered Israel. The Israelites made shelters in mountains, caves, and strongholds because of the Midianites. ⛰️
## Judges 6:3
Whenever Israel planted crops, Midianites, Amalekites, and other eastern peoples invaded, 🌾
## Judges 6:4
camping against them as far as Gaza and destroying the crops. They left no food, sheep, oxen, or donkeys for Israel. 💔
## Judges 6:5
Midianites came with livestock and tents like a great locust swarm. Their people and camels were countless. They came to ruin the land. ⚠️
## Judges 6:6
Midian made Israel desperately poor. Israel cried to the LORD. 🙏
## Judges 6:7
When Israel cried to the LORD because of Midian, 🙏
## Judges 6:8
He sent a prophet. The prophet said, “Israel’s God, the LORD, says, ‘I brought you from Egypt’s house of slavery. 📖
## Judges 6:9
I rescued you from Egypt and every oppressor, drove them out, and gave you their land. 🛡️
## Judges 6:10
I told you, “I am the LORD your God. Do not fear the Amorites’ gods in the land you inhabit.” You disobeyed Me.’” ⚠️
## Judges 6:11
The LORD’s angel sat under Joash the Abiezrite’s oak in Ophrah. Joash’s son Gideon threshed wheat in a winepress to hide it from Midianites. 🌾
## Judges 6:12
The LORD’s angel appeared to Gideon and said, “The LORD is with you, mighty, brave man.” 🛡️
## Judges 6:13
Gideon asked, “My Lord, if the LORD is with us, why did this happen? Where are the wonders our fathers described? They said, ‘Did the LORD not bring us from Egypt?’ Now He abandoned us to Midian.” ❓
## Judges 6:14
The LORD said, “Go with your strength and save Israel from Midian. Am I not sending you?” 🛡️
## Judges 6:15
Gideon answered, “My Lord, how can I save Israel? My clan is Manasseh’s weakest, and I am youngest in my father’s house.” 🙏
## Judges 6:16
The LORD replied, “I will be with you. You will strike all Midianites as one man.” 🛡️
## Judges 6:17
Gideon said, “If I found favor with You, give a sign that You are speaking to me. 🙏
## Judges 6:18
Please stay until I bring my offering and present it.” The LORD answered, “I will stay until you return.” 🎁
## Judges 6:19
Gideon prepared a young goat, unleavened bread, and an ephah of flour. He put meat in a basket and broth in a pot. He presented them under the oak. 🏺
## Judges 6:20
God’s angel said, “Put the meat and unleavened bread on this rock. Pour out the broth.” Gideon obeyed. 🪨
## Judges 6:21
The LORD’s angel touched the meat and bread with his staff’s tip. Fire rose from the rock and consumed them. The angel vanished. 🔥
## Judges 6:22
Gideon recognized the LORD’s angel and cried, “Lord GOD! I saw Your angel face to face!” 😨
## Judges 6:23
The LORD said, “Peace to you. Do not fear. You will not die.” 🕊️
## Judges 6:24
Gideon built an altar named The LORD Is Peace. It stands in Ophrah of the Abiezrites to this day. 🙏
## Judges 6:25
That night, the LORD told Gideon, “Take your father’s young bull and a second bull seven years old. Destroy your father’s Baal altar and cut down the Asherah pole beside it. ⚖️
## Judges 6:26
Build a proper altar to the LORD your God atop this stronghold. Offer the second bull as a burnt offering, using the Asherah pole’s wood.” 🔥
## Judges 6:27
Gideon obeyed with ten servants. Fearing his father’s household and the city’s men, he worked at night instead of day. 🌌
## Judges 6:28
In the morning, the city’s men saw Baal’s altar destroyed and the Asherah pole cut down. The second bull had been offered on the new altar. ⚠️
## Judges 6:29
They asked, “Who did this?” After investigating thoroughly, they learned, “Gideon son of Joash.” 💬
## Judges 6:30
They told Joash, “Bring your son out to die! He destroyed Baal’s altar and cut down its Asherah pole.” ⚠️
## Judges 6:31
Joash asked those opposing him, “Do you defend Baal or try to save him? Whoever pleads his case will be put to death by morning! If he is a god, let him contend with whoever destroyed his altar.” 💬
## Judges 6:32
Gideon was therefore called Jerubbaal, meaning “Let Baal contend with him,” because he destroyed Baal’s altar. 📖
## Judges 6:33
All Midianites, Amalekites, and other eastern peoples gathered, crossed the Jordan, and camped in the Valley of Jezreel. ⚔️
## Judges 6:34
The LORD’s Spirit came on Gideon. He blew the ram’s horn, rallying the Abiezrites behind him. 📯
## Judges 6:35
Gideon sent messengers calling Manasseh, Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali to arms. They came to meet him. 👥
## Judges 6:36
Gideon told God, “If You will save Israel through my hand as You said, 🙏
## Judges 6:37
I will put wool fleece on the threshing floor. If dew wets only the fleece while all the ground stays dry, I will know You will save Israel through me as promised.” 💧
## Judges 6:38
It happened. Next morning, Gideon squeezed a bowlful of water from the fleece. 🏺
## Judges 6:39
Gideon told God, “Do not be angry. Let me speak and test the fleece once more. Let the fleece stay dry and the ground be covered with dew.” 🙏
## Judges 6:40
God did this that night. Only the fleece stayed dry, and dew covered the ground. 💧
