## Judges 2:1
The LORD’s angel went from Gilgal to Bochim. He said, “I brought you from Egypt to the land promised to your fathers. I said, ‘I will never break My covenant with you. 📖
## Judges 2:2
Make no covenant with this land’s people, and destroy their altars.’ You disobeyed My voice. What have you done? ⚠️
## Judges 2:3
I will no longer drive these people out before you. They will be thorns in your sides, and their gods a trap for you.” ⚖️
## Judges 2:4
All Israel heard the LORD’s angel and wept loudly. 💧
## Judges 2:5
They named the place Bochim and sacrificed to the LORD there. 🙏
## Judges 2:6
Joshua dismissed the people. Each Israelite went to possess his inheritance. 🌍
## Judges 2:7
The people served the LORD throughout Joshua’s life and the lives of elders who outlived him. These elders saw all the LORD’s great works for Israel. 🙏
## Judges 2:8
The LORD’s servant Joshua son of Nun died at 110. 🪦
## Judges 2:9
They buried him in his inherited land at Timnath-heres, in Ephraim’s hills north of Mount Gaash. 🪦
## Judges 2:10
That whole generation joined their fathers in death. Another arose, knowing neither the LORD nor His works for Israel. 💔
## Judges 2:11
Israel did evil before the LORD and served the Baals. ⚠️
## Judges 2:12
They abandoned their fathers’ God, the LORD, who brought them from Egypt. They followed neighboring peoples’ gods and bowed to them, angering the LORD. 💔
## Judges 2:13
They abandoned Him for Baal and the Ashtoreths. ⚠️
## Judges 2:14
The LORD’s anger burned against Israel. He handed them to plunderers and sold them to surrounding enemies they could no longer resist. ⚖️
## Judges 2:15
Wherever Israel marched, the LORD’s hand brought disaster as He had sworn. They suffered greatly. 💔
## Judges 2:16
The LORD raised judges to save them from plunderers. 🛡️
## Judges 2:17
Israel ignored the judges and prostituted themselves with other gods, bowing to them. They quickly abandoned their fathers’ obedient ways. They did not keep the LORD’s commands as their fathers did. ⚠️
## Judges 2:18
Whenever the LORD raised a judge, He stayed with that judge and saved Israel from enemies throughout the judge’s life. Their groans under oppression and suffering moved the LORD to pity. ❤️
## Judges 2:19
After the judge died, Israel became more corrupt than their fathers. They served and bowed to other gods, refusing to abandon evil and stubbornness. 💔
## Judges 2:20
The LORD’s anger burned. He said, “This nation broke the covenant I commanded their fathers and ignored My voice. ⚖️
## Judges 2:21
I will no longer drive out nations Joshua left at his death. ⚠️
## Judges 2:22
I will test whether Israel follows the LORD’s way as their fathers did.” 📖
## Judges 2:23
The LORD therefore left those nations, instead of immediately handing them to Joshua to drive out. 🌍
