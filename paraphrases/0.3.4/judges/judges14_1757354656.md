## Judges 14:1
Samson went to Timnah and saw a young Philistine woman. 👥
## Judges 14:2
He told his parents, “I saw a Philistine woman in Timnah. Get her as my wife.” 💍
## Judges 14:3
They asked, “Why seek a wife from uncircumcised Philistines instead of relatives or our people?” Samson told his father, “Get her. She pleases my eyes.” 💬
## Judges 14:4
His parents did not know this was from the LORD, seeking an opportunity against the Philistines. They ruled Israel then. 📖
## Judges 14:5
Samson went to Timnah’s vineyards with his parents. A young lion roared at him, 🦁
## Judges 14:6
and the LORD’s Spirit came powerfully on him. He tore it apart barehanded as one tears a young goat. He did not tell his parents. 🛡️
## Judges 14:7
Samson spoke to the woman because she pleased his eyes. 💬
## Judges 14:8
Returning later to take her, Samson left the road to see the lion’s carcass. A bee swarm and honey were inside. 🍯
## Judges 14:9
He ate honey from his hands while walking. He gave his parents some, and they ate it. He did not tell them it came from the lion’s carcass. 🍯
## Judges 14:10
His father visited the woman. Samson held the customary bridegroom’s feast. 🍽️
## Judges 14:11
The Philistines saw him and chose thirty companions. 👥
## Judges 14:12
Samson said, “Solve my riddle during this seven-day feast, and I will give thirty linen garments and thirty sets of clothes. 💬
## Judges 14:13
If you fail, give me thirty linen garments and thirty sets of clothes.” They said, “Tell us the riddle.” 👕
## Judges 14:14
He said, “From the eater came food, and from the strong came sweetness.” They could not solve it for three days. ❓
## Judges 14:15
On the fourth day, they threatened Samson’s wife: “Get your husband to explain, or we will burn you and your father’s household to death. Did you invite us to rob us?” ⚠️
## Judges 14:16
Samson’s wife wept, “You hate me and do not love me! You gave my people a riddle, but never explained it to me.” He said, “I did not even tell my parents. Why tell you?” 💔
## Judges 14:17
She wept through all seven days. On the seventh, her pressure made Samson tell her. She explained it to her people. 💬
## Judges 14:18
Before sunset on the seventh day, the men asked, “What is sweeter than honey or stronger than a lion?” Samson replied, “Without plowing with my young cow, you would not have solved my riddle!” ❓
## Judges 14:19
The LORD’s Spirit came powerfully on him. At Ashkelon, he killed thirty men and gave their clothes to the riddle solvers. Samson returned angrily to his father’s house. ⚔️
## Judges 14:20
His wife was given to one of his companions. 💔
