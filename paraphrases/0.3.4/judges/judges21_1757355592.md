## Judges 21:1
Israel’s men had sworn at Mizpah, “None will give his daughter to a Benjamite in marriage.” 📖
## Judges 21:2
They went to Bethel and wept bitterly before God until evening. 💧
## Judges 21:3
They cried, “LORD, Israel’s God, why did this happen? One tribe is missing today!” 💔
## Judges 21:4
Early next day, they built an altar and gave burnt and peace offerings. 🙏
## Judges 21:5
They asked who failed to assemble before the LORD. They had solemnly sworn to kill anyone not assembling before Him at Mizpah. ⚠️
## Judges 21:6
Grieving for their Benjamite brothers, they said, “A tribe is cut off today. 💔
## Judges 21:7
How can survivors get wives when we swore by the LORD not to give our daughters?” ❓
## Judges 21:8
They asked which tribe failed to assemble at Mizpah before the LORD. No one from Jabesh-gilead came. 👥
## Judges 21:9
The count found no residents of Jabesh-gilead. 📖
## Judges 21:10
They sent 12,000 of their bravest men: “Kill Jabesh-gilead’s people with swords, including women and children. ⚔️
## Judges 21:11
Devote every male and every female who had relations with a man to destruction.” 💔
## Judges 21:12
They found four hundred young women in Jabesh-gilead who had never had relations with men. They brought them to Shiloh’s camp in Canaan. 👥
## Judges 21:13
The whole congregation sent a message of peace to Benjamites at Rimmon’s rock. 🕊️
## Judges 21:14
Benjamites returned and received Jabesh-gilead’s surviving women. There were not enough for everyone. 💔
## Judges 21:15
The people grieved for Benjamin. The LORD had made a void in Israel’s tribes. 💧
## Judges 21:16
The elders asked, “How can the remaining men get wives? Benjamin’s women were destroyed. ❓
## Judges 21:17
Benjamin’s survivors need heirs, so Israel will not lose a tribe. 👥
## Judges 21:18
We cannot give our daughters.” Israel had sworn, “Whoever gives a Benjamite a wife is cursed.” ⚠️
## Judges 21:19
They said, “The LORD has a yearly feast at Shiloh, north of Bethel. It is east of the road from Bethel to Shechem and south of Lebonah.” 📅
## Judges 21:20
They told Benjamites, “Hide in the vineyards 🍇
## Judges 21:21
and watch. When Shiloh’s daughters come out dancing, each must leave the vineyards, seize a wife, and return to Benjamin. 💔
## Judges 21:22
When their fathers or brothers complain, we will say, ‘Help them as a favor to us. We did not get wives for each man in the war. You did not give your daughters, so you have no guilt.’” 💬
## Judges 21:23
Benjamites seized the dancers they needed and carried them away. They returned to their inheritance, rebuilt their cities, and settled. 🏠
## Judges 21:24
Each Israelite returned to his tribe, clan, and inheritance. 🚶
## Judges 21:25
Israel had no king then. Everyone did what was right in his own eyes. 📖
