## Judges 18:1
Israel had no king. Dan’s tribe sought land because they had not received an inheritance among Israel’s tribes. 🌍
## Judges 18:2
Danites sent five brave men from Zorah and Eshtaol to explore the land. They reached Micah’s house in Ephraim’s hills and stayed overnight. 👁️
## Judges 18:3
They recognized the young Levite’s voice near Micah’s house. They asked, “Who brought you? What do you do? Why are you here?” ❓
## Judges 18:4
He described Micah’s help and said, “He hired me as priest.” 💬
## Judges 18:5
They asked, “Ask God whether our journey will succeed.” 🙏
## Judges 18:6
The priest answered, “Go in peace. The LORD watches your journey.” 🕊️
## Judges 18:7
The five reached Laish. Its people lived securely like Sidonians, quietly and unsuspectingly. The land lacked nothing and had no oppressive ruler. They were far from Sidonians and had no allies. 🏙️
## Judges 18:8
They returned to Zorah and Eshtaol. Their brothers asked what they found. 💬
## Judges 18:9
They answered, “Attack! The land is very good. Why delay? Go possess it! ⚔️
## Judges 18:10
You will find unsuspecting people in spacious land. God gave it to you. Nothing is lacking there.” 🌍
## Judges 18:11
Six hundred Danites left Zorah and Eshtaol armed for war. ⚔️
## Judges 18:12
They camped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. The place west of Kiriath-jearim is therefore called Mahaneh-dan to this day. ⛺
## Judges 18:13
They went to Ephraim’s hills and Micah’s house. 🚶
## Judges 18:14
The five Laish spies said, “These houses contain an ephod, household gods, a carved image, and cast idol. Consider what to do.” 💬
## Judges 18:15
They turned into Micah’s house and greeted the young Levite. 🤝
## Judges 18:16
Six hundred armed Danites stood at the gate. ⚔️
## Judges 18:17
The five spies took the carved image, ephod, household idols, and cast idol. The priest stood at the gate with six hundred armed men. ⚠️
## Judges 18:18
As they took these objects from Micah’s house, the priest asked, “What are you doing?” ❓
## Judges 18:19
They said, “Be quiet. Cover your mouth and join us as father and priest. Is serving one household better than serving a tribe and family in Israel?” 💬
## Judges 18:20
The priest gladly took the ephod, household idols, and carved image, and joined them. ⚠️
## Judges 18:21
They left with their small children, livestock, and possessions in front. 🚶
## Judges 18:22
After they traveled some distance, Micah’s neighbors assembled and overtook the Danites. 👥
## Judges 18:23
They called out. The Danites turned and asked Micah, “Why gather this crowd? What is wrong?” ❓
## Judges 18:24
He said, “You took the gods I made and my priest! What remains? How can you ask what is wrong?” 💔
## Judges 18:25
Danites warned, “Stop shouting, or angry men will attack and kill you and your family.” ⚠️
## Judges 18:26
They continued. Micah saw they were stronger and returned home. 🏠
## Judges 18:27
With Micah’s idols and priest, they reached unsuspecting, quiet Laish. They killed its people with swords and burned the city. ⚔️
## Judges 18:28
No one rescued them because they were far from Sidon and had no allies. Laish lay in a valley near Beth-rehob. Danites rebuilt it and settled. 🏙️
## Judges 18:29
They named former Laish “Dan,” after Dan, their ancestor born to Israel. 🌍
## Judges 18:30
Danites erected idols. Jonathan son of Gershom, grandson of Moses, and his sons served as priests to Dan until the land’s captivity. ⚠️
## Judges 18:31
Micah’s carved image remained there throughout the time God’s house was in Shiloh. 🏛️
