## Judges 20:1
All Israel from Dan to Beersheba and Gilead gathered as one before the LORD at Mizpah. 👥
## Judges 20:2
All leaders and tribes assembled as God’s people: 400,000 foot soldiers armed with swords. ⚔️
## Judges 20:3
Benjamites heard Israel assembled at Mizpah. Israel asked, “How did this wickedness happen?” ❓
## Judges 20:4
The murdered woman’s husband, the Levite, answered, “My concubine and I stayed at Gibeah in Benjamin. 💬
## Judges 20:5
Gibeah’s men surrounded me at night, intending to kill me. They abused my concubine, and she died. 💔
## Judges 20:6
I cut her into pieces and sent her throughout Israel’s inheritance because they committed a lewd, disgraceful act in Israel. ⚠️
## Judges 20:7
Israelites, give your advice and verdict now.” ⚖️
## Judges 20:8
Everyone rose together and said, “No one returns to his tent or house. 👥
## Judges 20:9
We will attack Gibeah as the lot directs. ⚔️
## Judges 20:10
Take ten from every hundred, a hundred from every thousand, and a thousand from every ten thousand throughout Israel. They will supply the army attacking Gibeah in Benjamin to punish Israel’s atrocity.” 🍞
## Judges 20:11
All Israel united against the city. ⚔️
## Judges 20:12
Israel’s tribes sent men throughout Benjamin, asking, “What wickedness happened among you? 💬
## Judges 20:13
Surrender Gibeah’s wicked men to be killed and purge Israel’s evil.” Benjamites refused their fellow Israelites. ⚠️
## Judges 20:14
They gathered from their cities at Gibeah to fight Israel. ⚔️
## Judges 20:15
Benjamin gathered 26,000 swordsmen from its cities, plus Gibeah’s 700 chosen men. 👥
## Judges 20:16
Among them were 700 chosen left-handers, able to sling a stone at a hair without missing. 🪨
## Judges 20:17
Israel, excluding Benjamin, gathered 400,000 experienced swordsmen. ⚔️
## Judges 20:18
Israel asked God at Bethel, “Who fights Benjamin first?” The LORD answered, “Judah.” 🙏
## Judges 20:19
Next morning, Israel camped near Gibeah. ⛺
## Judges 20:20
Israel took battle positions against Benjamin at Gibeah. ⚔️
## Judges 20:21
Benjamites came out from Gibeah and killed 22,000 Israelites that day. 💔
## Judges 20:22
Israel took courage and occupied the same battle positions as the first day. 🛡️
## Judges 20:23
They wept before the LORD until evening, asking, “Should we fight our Benjamite brothers again?” He answered, “Attack.” 🙏
## Judges 20:24
Israel advanced against Benjamin on the second day. ⚔️
## Judges 20:25
Benjamites came from Gibeah and killed another 18,000 Israelite swordsmen. 💔
## Judges 20:26
All Israel wept at Bethel before the LORD. They fasted until evening and gave Him burnt and peace offerings. 🙏
## Judges 20:27
They consulted the LORD. God’s covenant ark was there then, 📖
## Judges 20:28
with Phinehas son of Eleazar, grandson of Aaron, serving before it. They asked whether to fight their Benjamite brothers again or stop. The LORD said, “Fight. Tomorrow I will give you victory.” 🙏
## Judges 20:29
Israel set an ambush around Gibeah. ⚔️
## Judges 20:30
On the third day, Israel took positions against Gibeah as before. ⚔️
## Judges 20:31
Benjamites were drawn away from the city. They killed about thirty Israelites in fields and on roads to Bethel and Gibeah. 💔
## Judges 20:32
Benjamites said, “We defeat them as before.” Israel said, “Retreat and draw them onto the roads.” ⚔️
## Judges 20:33
All Israel took positions at Baal-tamar. The ambush charged from west of Gibeah. ⚔️
## Judges 20:34
Then 10,000 chosen Israelites attacked Gibeah from the front. Fighting was fierce, but Benjamin did not see disaster coming. ⚠️
## Judges 20:35
The LORD defeated Benjamin before Israel. Israel killed 25,100 Benjamite swordsmen that day. ⚔️
## Judges 20:36
Benjamin realized defeat. Israel had withdrawn because they relied on the ambush against Gibeah. ⚔️
## Judges 20:37
The ambush rushed into Gibeah and killed all its people with the sword. 💔
## Judges 20:38
Israel had arranged a signal with the ambush: when a great smoke cloud rose from the city, 🔥
## Judges 20:39
Israel would turn in battle. After killing about thirty Israelites, Benjamites claimed victory as in the first battle. ⚔️
## Judges 20:40
Smoke rose, and Benjamites looked back to see the whole city going up in smoke. 🔥
## Judges 20:41
Israel turned against them. Benjamin saw disaster and became terrified. ⚠️
## Judges 20:42
They fled toward wilderness, but battle overtook them. Men coming from the cities killed them there. ⚔️
## Judges 20:43
Israel surrounded, chased, and easily overtook Benjamites east of Gibeah. ⚔️
## Judges 20:44
18,000 brave Benjamites fell. 💔
## Judges 20:45
They fled toward Rimmon’s rock in the wilderness. Israel killed 5,000 on the roads and 2,000 more at Gidom. ⚔️
## Judges 20:46
That day, 25,000 brave Benjamite swordsmen died. 💔
## Judges 20:47
600 fled to Rimmon’s rock and stayed four months. ⛰️
## Judges 20:48
Israel attacked the remaining Benjamites. They killed the cities’ inhabitants and animals, destroying everything else they found. They burned all cities in their path. 🔥
