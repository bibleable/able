## Judges 7:1
Early, Jerubbaal, meaning Gideon, camped with his men by Harod’s spring. Midian camped north in the valley near Moreh’s hill. ⛺
## Judges 7:2
The LORD told Gideon, “You have too many men for Me to give them victory over Midian. This is so Israel will not boast against Me, ‘My own hand saved me.’ ⚠️
## Judges 7:3
Announce, ‘Anyone afraid and trembling may leave Mount Gilead.’” Twenty-two thousand left, and ten thousand remained. 👥
## Judges 7:4
The LORD told Gideon, “Still too many. Bring them to the water, where I will sift them. Those I say should go must go. Those I say should not go must stay.” 💧
## Judges 7:5
Gideon brought them to the water. The LORD said, “Separate those lapping like dogs with their tongues from those kneeling to drink.” 💧
## Judges 7:6
Three hundred lapped with their hands to their mouths. All others knelt to drink. 👥
## Judges 7:7
The LORD told Gideon, “I will save you through the three hundred who lapped and hand Midian to you. Send everyone else home.” 🛡️
## Judges 7:8
Gideon sent the others to their tents. The three hundred took the others’ provisions and rams’ horns. Midian camped in the valley below. ⛺
## Judges 7:9
That night, the LORD told Gideon, “Rise and attack the camp. I have given it to you. 🛡️
## Judges 7:10
If you fear going, take your servant Purah down to the camp 💬
## Judges 7:11
and listen. Your hands will be strengthened to attack.” He went with Purah to the outposts guarded by armed men. 👂
## Judges 7:12
Midianites, Amalekites, and all other eastern peoples filled the valley like locusts. Their camels were countless as seashore sand. 🐪
## Judges 7:13
Gideon heard a man describing a dream to his friend. “A barley loaf tumbled into Midian’s camp, struck a tent, and overturned and collapsed it.” 💭
## Judges 7:14
His friend answered, “This is Gideon son of Joash the Israelite’s sword! God gave him Midian and the whole camp.” ⚔️
## Judges 7:15
Gideon heard the dream and interpretation, then bowed in worship. Returning, he told Israel, “Rise! The LORD gave you Midian’s camp.” 🙏
## Judges 7:16
He divided the three hundred into three groups. Each man held a ram’s horn in one hand and a large jar containing a torch in the other. 📯
## Judges 7:17
Gideon said, “Watch me. When I reach the camp’s edge, do exactly as I do. 👁️
## Judges 7:18
When I and all with me blow our horns, blow yours around the camp. Shout, ‘For the LORD and Gideon!’” 📣
## Judges 7:19
Gideon and his hundred men reached the camp’s edge at the middle watch’s beginning, just after the guards changed. They blew horns and broke jars. 🌌
## Judges 7:20
The three groups blew horns and shattered jars. Torches in left hands and horns in right hands, they shouted, “A sword for the LORD and Gideon!” 🔥
## Judges 7:21
Each Israelite stood around the camp. Midian’s whole army fled, crying out. ⚔️
## Judges 7:22
When three hundred horns sounded, the LORD turned the camp’s men against each other with swords. They fled to Beth-shittah toward Zererah, to Abel-meholah’s border near Tabbath. ⚔️
## Judges 7:23
Israel called men from Naphtali, Asher, and all Manasseh to chase Midian. 👥
## Judges 7:24
Gideon sent messengers through Ephraim’s hills: “Attack Midian and seize the Jordan’s waters before them, as far as Beth-barah.” All Ephraim’s men gathered and captured those waters to Beth-barah. 🌊
## Judges 7:25
They captured Midian’s two princes, Oreb and Zeeb. They killed Oreb at Oreb’s rock and Zeeb at Zeeb’s winepress. They chased Midian and brought the princes’ heads to Gideon on the other side of the Jordan. ⚔️
