## Judges 5:5
Mountains shook before the LORD, the One of Sinai, before Israel’s God. ⛰️
## Judges 5:6

## Judges 5:7
Village life ended in Israel until I, Deborah, arose as a mother in Israel. ❤️
## Judges 5:8
They chose new gods, and war reached their gates. No shield or spear was found among forty thousand in Israel. ⚔️
## Judges 5:9
My heart is with Israel’s princes and the people’s volunteers. Bless the LORD! 🙏
## Judges 5:10

## Judges 5:13
Survivors came down to the nobles. The LORD’s people came down to me against the mighty. 👥

Some came from Ephraim, with roots in Amalek. Benjamin came with your people after you. Commanders came from Machir, and those carrying the marshal’s staff from Zebulun. 👥

Gilead stayed beyond the Jordan. Dan, why linger by ships? Asher stayed along the coast in his harbors. 🚢

Canaan’s kings fought at Taanach beside Megiddo’s waters. They took no silver plunder. ⚔️
## Judges 5:20
