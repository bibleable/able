## Philippians 2:1
Do you receive encouragement in Christ, comfort from His love, and fellowship with the Spirit? Do you have affection and mercy? ❤️
## Philippians 2:2
If so, make my joy complete. Think alike, share the same love, and be united in spirit and purpose. 🤝
## Philippians 2:3
Do nothing from selfish ambition or empty pride. Be humble and consider others more important than yourselves. ❤️
## Philippians 2:4
Each of you must care about the interests of others as well as your own. 🤝
## Philippians 2:5
Have the same attitude that Christ Jesus had. ✝️
## Philippians 2:6
He existed in the form of God. But He did not consider equality with God something to hold onto. ✨
## Philippians 2:7
Instead, He emptied Himself. He took the form of a servant and became like a human being. 👣
## Philippians 2:8
He appeared as a man and humbled Himself. He obeyed to the point of death, even death on a cross. ✝️
## Philippians 2:9
So God raised Him to the highest place. God gave Him the name above every other name. 👑
## Philippians 2:10
At the name of Jesus, every knee must bow, in heaven, on earth, and under the earth. 🙏
## Philippians 2:11
Every tongue must declare that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father. 📣
## Philippians 2:12
My dear friends, you have always obeyed, both when I was with you and even more now while I am away. Continue to work out your salvation with fear and trembling. 👣
## Philippians 2:13
God works in you. He gives you the desire and the ability to act for His good purpose. 🌱
## Philippians 2:14
Do everything without complaints or arguments. 🕊️
## Philippians 2:15
Then you can be pure and free from blame, God’s children without fault among crooked and corrupt people. Among them, you shine like lights in the world. ✨
## Philippians 2:16
Hold out the word of life. Then, on the day of Christ, I can rejoice that I did not run or work for nothing. 📖
## Philippians 2:17
Your faithful service is like a sacrifice. Even if my life is poured out like a drink offering upon it, I am glad and rejoice with all of you. 🙏
## Philippians 2:18
You too must be glad and rejoice with me. 😊
## Philippians 2:19
I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon. News about you will encourage me too. ✉️
## Philippians 2:20
I have no one else like him who will truly care about your needs. ❤️
## Philippians 2:21
All the others care about their own interests instead of those of Jesus Christ. ⚠️
## Philippians 2:22
You know Timothy’s proven worth. He served with me to spread the good news, like a son with his father. 🤝
## Philippians 2:23
I hope to send him as soon as I see what will happen to me. ⏳
## Philippians 2:24
I trust in the Lord that I too will come soon. 👣
## Philippians 2:25
I thought I must send Epaphroditus back to you. He is my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier. He is also your messenger, whom you sent to care for my needs. 🤝
## Philippians 2:26
He longs for all of you. He is distressed because you heard that he was ill. ❤️
## Philippians 2:27
He was so ill that he nearly died. But God had mercy on him, and on me too, to spare me sorrow upon sorrow. 🙏
## Philippians 2:28
So I am even more eager to send him. You can rejoice when you see him again, and I can have less worry. 😊
## Philippians 2:29
Welcome him in the Lord with great joy. Honor people like him. 🤝
## Philippians 2:30
He nearly died for Christ’s work. He risked his life to provide the service to me that you could not provide. ✝️
