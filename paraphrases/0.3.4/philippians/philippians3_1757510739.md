## Philippians 3:1
Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. It is no trouble for me to repeat these things in a letter. This helps keep you safe. 😊
## Philippians 3:2
Watch out for those dogs, those people who do evil, those who mutilate the flesh! ⚠️
## Philippians 3:3
We are the true circumcision. We worship by God’s Spirit and boast in Christ Jesus. We do not trust in human status or achievements. 🕊️
## Philippians 3:4
Yet I myself could have such confidence. If anyone thinks he has reasons to trust in human status or achievements, I have more. 💭
## Philippians 3:5
I was circumcised on the eighth day. I belong to Israel, to Benjamin’s tribe. I am a Hebrew of Hebrews. In regard to the law, I was a Pharisee. 📜
## Philippians 3:6
I was so eager that I persecuted the church. By the standard of righteousness in the law, I was without fault. ⚠️
## Philippians 3:7
But I now count everything that was gain for me as loss for Christ’s sake. ✝️
## Philippians 3:8
I count everything as loss compared with the far greater value of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For Him, I have lost everything. I consider these things rubbish, so that I may gain Christ. ✝️
## Philippians 3:9
I want to be found in Him. My righteousness does not come from obeying the law. It comes through faith in Christ. This righteousness comes from God on the basis of faith. 🙏
## Philippians 3:10
I want to know Christ and the power of His resurrection. I want to share His sufferings and become like Him in His death. ✝️
## Philippians 3:11
In this way, I hope somehow to reach the resurrection from the dead. 🌅
## Philippians 3:12
I have not already received all this or become perfect. But I press on to take hold of what Christ Jesus took hold of me for. 👣
## Philippians 3:13
Brothers, I do not think I have reached it yet. But I do this one thing: I forget what lies behind and strain toward what lies ahead. 🏃
## Philippians 3:14
I press toward the goal to win the prize of God’s heavenly call in Christ Jesus. 🏆
## Philippians 3:15
All of us who are mature must think this way. If you think differently about anything, God will make that clear to you too. 💭
## Philippians 3:16
But we must live up to what we have already reached. 👣
## Philippians 3:17
Brothers, follow my example together. Watch carefully those who live by the example we gave you. 👣
## Philippians 3:18
I have often told you this, and now I say it again with tears. Many people live as enemies of Christ’s cross. 😢
## Philippians 3:19
Their end is destruction. Their belly is their god, and they boast about shameful things. Their minds are fixed on earthly things. ⚠️
## Philippians 3:20
But we are citizens of heaven. We eagerly wait for a Savior from there, the Lord Jesus Christ. 🌅
## Philippians 3:21
He will change our humble bodies to be like His glorious body. He will use the power by which He brings everything under His control. ✨
