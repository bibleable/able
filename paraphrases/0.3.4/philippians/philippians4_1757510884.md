## Philippians 4:1
My dear brothers, I love you and long to see you. You are my joy and crown. Stand firm in the Lord in this way, my dear friends. ❤️
## Philippians 4:2
I urge Euodia and Syntyche to agree with each other in the Lord. 🤝
## Philippians 4:3
My true fellow worker, please help these women. They worked hard beside me for the good news, with Clement and my other fellow workers. Their names are in the Book of Life. 📖
## Philippians 4:4
Always rejoice in the Lord. I say it again: Rejoice! 😊
## Philippians 4:5
Let everyone see your gentleness. The Lord is near. 🕊️
## Philippians 4:6
Do not worry about anything. In everything, pray and ask God for what you need, with thanks. 🙏
## Philippians 4:7
God’s peace is greater than we can understand. It will guard your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus. 🛡️
## Philippians 4:8
Finally, brothers, think about what is true, honorable, right, pure, lovely, and worthy of respect. Think about anything excellent or worthy of praise. 💭
## Philippians 4:9
Practice what you learned, received, and heard from me, and what you saw me do. The God of peace will be with you. 👣
## Philippians 4:10
I rejoice greatly in the Lord because you have again shown your care for me. You did care before, but you had no chance to show it. ❤️
## Philippians 4:11
I do not say this because I need something. I have learned to be content in every situation. 🕊️
## Philippians 4:12
I know how to live with little and how to live with plenty. In every situation, I have learned the secret of contentment, whether full or hungry, with plenty or in need. 🍞
## Philippians 4:13
I can do all things through Christ, who gives me strength. 💪
## Philippians 4:14
But you did well to share in my trouble. 🤝
## Philippians 4:15
You Philippians know what happened when the good news first reached you and I left Macedonia. No church except yours shared with me in giving and receiving. ❤️
## Philippians 4:16
Even when I was in Thessalonica, you sent help for my needs again and again. 🎁
## Philippians 4:17
I do not seek a gift. I seek the good results that can be credited to your account. 🌱
## Philippians 4:18
I have all I need and more, because Epaphroditus brought your gifts. They are like a sweet-smelling offering, a sacrifice that God accepts and enjoys. 🎁
## Philippians 4:19
My God will meet all your needs from His glorious riches in Christ Jesus. 🙏
## Philippians 4:20
May our God and Father receive glory forever and ever. Amen. ✨
## Philippians 4:21
Greet all God’s holy people in Christ Jesus. The brothers with me send you greetings. 👋
## Philippians 4:22
All God’s holy people send greetings, especially those in Caesar’s household. 👋
## Philippians 4:23
May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. 🕊️
