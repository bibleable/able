## Philippians 1:1
This letter is from Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus. We write to all God’s holy people in Christ Jesus at Philippi, with their church overseers and deacons. ✉️
## Philippians 1:2
May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace. 🕊️
## Philippians 1:3
I thank my God every time I remember you. 🙏
## Philippians 1:4
Whenever I pray for all of you, I pray with joy. 😊
## Philippians 1:5
You have shared in the work of the good news from the first day until now. 🤝
## Philippians 1:6
I am sure that God, who began this good work in you, will continue it until the day of Christ Jesus. He will bring it to completion. 🌱
## Philippians 1:7
It is right for me to feel this way about all of you, because you are in my heart. You all share God’s grace with me, both in my imprisonment and as I defend and confirm the good news. ❤️
## Philippians 1:8
God knows how much I long for all of you with the love of Christ Jesus. ❤️
## Philippians 1:9
I pray that your love will grow more and more, with knowledge and deep understanding. 🙏
## Philippians 1:10
Then you can test and recognize what is best. You can be pure and free from blame for the day of Christ. ✨
## Philippians 1:11
May your lives be full of the fruit of righteousness that comes through Jesus Christ. This will bring glory and praise to God. 🌱
## Philippians 1:12
Brothers, I want you to know that what happened to me has helped the good news spread. 📣
## Philippians 1:13
The whole palace guard and everyone else now know that I am in chains because of Christ. ⛓️
## Philippians 1:14
Because of my chains, most of the brothers have greater confidence in the Lord. They now speak His word more boldly and without fear. 💪
## Philippians 1:15
Some people preach Christ because they are jealous and want to compete with me. Others preach with good intentions. 📣
## Philippians 1:16
Those with good intentions act from love. They know that I was appointed to defend the good news. ❤️
## Philippians 1:17
The others preach Christ for selfish reasons, without sincerity. They think they can add to my distress while I am in chains. ⚠️
## Philippians 1:18
What matters? In every way, people preach Christ, whether their motives are false or true. This gives me joy, and I will continue to rejoice. 😊
## Philippians 1:19
I know that your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ will turn my distress toward my deliverance. 🙏
## Philippians 1:20
I eagerly hope and expect that I will have no reason for shame. I hope to be fully bold. Now, as always, may Christ receive honor through my body, whether I live or die. ✨
## Philippians 1:21
For me, life is Christ, and death is gain. ✝️
## Philippians 1:22
If I continue to live in this body, I can do useful work. What will I choose? I do not know. 💭
## Philippians 1:23
I feel pulled in both directions. I want to leave this life and be with Christ, which is far better. ✝️
## Philippians 1:24
But you need me more to remain alive in this body. ❤️
## Philippians 1:25
I am sure of this. I know I will stay and continue with all of you, to help you grow and find joy in your faith. 🌱
## Philippians 1:26
When I come to you again, you will have even more reason to rejoice in Christ Jesus because of me. 😊
## Philippians 1:27
Live in a way worthy of Christ’s good news. Whether I visit you or hear about you from elsewhere, I want to know that you stand firm in one spirit. Strive together as one for the faith of the good news. 🤝
## Philippians 1:28
Do not be afraid of those who oppose you. This clearly shows that they face destruction, but you will be saved. This sign comes from God. 🛡️
## Philippians 1:29
God has given you the privilege to believe in Christ and also to suffer for Him. ✝️
## Philippians 1:30
You face the same struggle that you saw me face. You now hear that I still face it. 💪
