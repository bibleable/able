## Ephesians 1:1
This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God’s will. I write to God’s holy people in Ephesus, who are faithful in Christ Jesus. ✉️
## Ephesians 1:2
May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace. 🕊️
## Ephesians 1:3
Praise the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! In Christ, He blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly realms. ✨
## Ephesians 1:4
Before the world’s foundation, He chose us in Christ to be holy and without blame before Him. He acted in love. ❤️
## Ephesians 1:5
He decided beforehand to adopt us as His sons through Jesus Christ. This pleased Him and agreed with His will. ❤️
## Ephesians 1:6
This brings praise to His glorious grace, which He freely gave us in the Beloved One. ✨
## Ephesians 1:7
In Him, His blood redeems us and brings forgiveness for our sins. This comes from the riches of His grace. ✝️
## Ephesians 1:8
He poured this grace on us with all wisdom and understanding. 🎁
## Ephesians 1:9
He made the mystery of His will known to us. This pleased Him and fulfilled the purpose He set in Christ. 📖
## Ephesians 1:10
His plan for the fullness of time is to bring everything in heaven and on earth together in Christ. 🌍
## Ephesians 1:11
In Christ, we were chosen as God’s own people. God decided this beforehand according to His plan. He works everything out according to the counsel of His will. 👑
## Ephesians 1:12
We were the first to hope in Christ. God intended us to bring praise to His glory. ✨
## Ephesians 1:13
You also heard the true message, the good news of your salvation, and believed. In Christ, you were marked with the seal of the promised Holy Spirit. 🕊️
## Ephesians 1:14
The Spirit guarantees our inheritance until God redeems His own people. This brings praise to His glory. 🌅
## Ephesians 1:15
I heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all God’s holy people. ❤️
## Ephesians 1:16
Since then, I have continued to thank God for you and remember you in my prayers. 🙏
## Ephesians 1:17
I ask the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, to give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation. Then you can know Him. 📖
## Ephesians 1:18
I ask Him to bring light to the eyes of your heart. Then you can know the hope of His call and the riches of His glorious inheritance among His holy people. ✨
## Ephesians 1:19
May you also know how very great His power is toward us who believe. This power works according to His mighty strength. 💪
## Ephesians 1:20
He used this strength in Christ when He raised Him from the dead. He seated Christ at His right hand in the heavenly realms. 👑
## Ephesians 1:21
Christ is far above every rule, authority, power, and dominion, and every name anyone can name. This is true in this age and the age to come. ✨
## Ephesians 1:22
God put everything under Christ’s feet. He made Christ the head over everything for the church. 👑
## Ephesians 1:23
The church is His body, the fullness of Him who fills everything in every way. 🌱
