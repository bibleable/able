## Ephesians 5:1
Follow God’s example as His beloved children. 👣
## Ephesians 5:2
Live in love, as Christ loved us and gave Himself for us. He was a fragrant sacrifice and offering to God. ✝️
## Ephesians 5:3
There must not be even a hint of sexual immorality, any kind of impurity, or greed among you. This is proper for God’s holy people. ⛔
## Ephesians 5:4
Avoid obscene words, foolish talk, and crude jokes. These are improper. Give thanks instead. 🗣️
## Ephesians 5:5
Be sure of this: No sexually immoral, impure, or greedy person has an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God. A greedy person worships an idol. ⚖️
## Ephesians 5:6
Let no one deceive you with empty words. Because of these things, God’s anger is coming on those who disobey. ⚠️
## Ephesians 5:7
Do not join them in these things. ⛔
## Ephesians 5:8
You were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light. ✨
## Ephesians 5:9
The fruit of light is every kind of goodness, righteousness, and truth. 🌱
## Ephesians 5:10
Test and discover what pleases the Lord. 📖
## Ephesians 5:11
Do not share in the fruitless acts of darkness. Expose them instead. 🛡️
## Ephesians 5:12
It is shameful even to speak about what disobedient people do secretly. ⚠️
## Ephesians 5:13
Light makes everything it exposes visible. Everything lit up becomes a light itself. ✨
## Ephesians 5:14
So it is said, “Wake up, sleeper! Rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you.” 🌅
## Ephesians 5:15
Pay careful attention to how you live. Live wisely instead of foolishly. 👣
## Ephesians 5:16
Make the most of your time, because the days are evil. ⏳
## Ephesians 5:17
Do not be foolish. Understand the Lord’s will. 💭
## Ephesians 5:18
Do not get drunk on wine, which leads to reckless behavior. Instead, be filled with the Spirit. 🕊️
## Ephesians 5:19
Speak to each other with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs. Sing and make music in your hearts to the Lord. 🎵
## Ephesians 5:20
Always thank God the Father for everything in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. 🙏
## Ephesians 5:21
Submit to each other out of deep respect for Christ. 🤝
## Ephesians 5:22
Wives, submit to your husbands as to the Lord. ❤️
## Ephesians 5:23
The husband is the wife’s head, as Christ is the church’s head. The church is His body, and He is its Savior. ✝️
## Ephesians 5:24
As the church submits to Christ, wives must submit to their husbands in everything. 🤝
## Ephesians 5:25
Husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the church and gave Himself for her. ❤️
## Ephesians 5:26
He did this to make her holy. He cleansed her by washing with water through the word. 💧
## Ephesians 5:27
He wanted to present her to Himself as a glorious church, without stain, wrinkle, or any defect. She would be holy and without blame. ✨
## Ephesians 5:28
In the same way, husbands must love their wives as their own bodies. Anyone who loves his wife loves himself. ❤️
## Ephesians 5:29
No one ever hated his own body. He feeds and cares for it, just as Christ does for the church. ❤️
## Ephesians 5:30
We are parts of His body. 🤝
## Ephesians 5:31
“A man will leave his father and mother and join his wife for this reason. The two will become one flesh.” 💍
## Ephesians 5:32
This is a deep mystery. I speak about Christ and the church. ✝️
## Ephesians 5:33
Each of you must love his wife as himself. The wife must respect her husband. ❤️
