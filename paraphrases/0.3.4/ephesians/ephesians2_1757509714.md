## Ephesians 2:1
You were dead in your wrong acts and sins. 💔
## Ephesians 2:2
You once lived in them, following this world’s ways and the ruler of the power of the air. That spirit now works in those who disobey. ⚠️
## Ephesians 2:3
We all once lived among them and satisfied our sinful physical desires and thoughts. Like the others, we were by nature children under God’s anger. ⛔
## Ephesians 2:4
But God is rich in mercy, and He loved us greatly. ❤️
## Ephesians 2:5
He made us alive with Christ even when we were dead in our sins. You have been saved by grace! ✝️
## Ephesians 2:6
God raised us with Christ. In Christ Jesus, He seated us with Him in the heavenly realms. 🌅
## Ephesians 2:7
He did this to show the very great riches of His grace in the ages to come. His kindness to us in Christ Jesus shows this grace. ❤️
## Ephesians 2:8
You have been saved by grace through faith. This does not come from yourselves. It is God’s gift. 🎁
## Ephesians 2:9
It does not come from works, so no one can boast. 🙏
## Ephesians 2:10
We are God’s work, created in Christ Jesus for good actions. God prepared these beforehand as our way of life. 👣
## Ephesians 2:11
Remember that you were Gentiles in the flesh. Those who call themselves the circumcision called you uncircumcised. Their circumcision is performed on the body by human hands. 💭
## Ephesians 2:12
At that time, you were separated from Christ and excluded from Israel’s community. You were strangers to the covenants of promise. You had no hope and were without God in the world. 💔
## Ephesians 2:13
Now, in Christ Jesus, you who were far away have been brought near through Christ’s blood. ✝️
## Ephesians 2:14
Christ Himself is our peace. He made the two groups one and tore down the wall of hostility that divided them. 🕊️
## Ephesians 2:15
In His flesh, He abolished the law of commands and rules. He did this to create one new person from the two in Himself and make peace. 🤝
## Ephesians 2:16
Through the cross, He brought both groups back to God in one body. Through it, He killed their hostility. ✝️
## Ephesians 2:17
He came and preached peace to you who were far away and to those who were near. 🕊️
## Ephesians 2:18
Through Him, both groups can come to the Father by one Spirit. 🕊️
## Ephesians 2:19
You are no longer strangers or foreigners. You are fellow citizens with God’s holy people and members of His household. 🏠
## Ephesians 2:20
You are built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets. Christ Jesus Himself is the cornerstone. 🪨
## Ephesians 2:21
In Him, the whole building fits together and grows into a holy temple in the Lord. 🏠
## Ephesians 2:22
In Him, you too are built together into a home for God through His Spirit. 🕊️
