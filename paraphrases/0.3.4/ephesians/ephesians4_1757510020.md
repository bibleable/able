## Ephesians 4:1
As a prisoner in the Lord, I urge you to live in a way worthy of the call you received. 👣
## Ephesians 4:2
Be fully humble, gentle, and patient. Bear with each other in love. ❤️
## Ephesians 4:3
Work hard to preserve the Spirit’s unity through the bond of peace. 🤝
## Ephesians 4:4
There is one body and one Spirit. You were called to one hope. 🕊️
## Ephesians 4:5
There is one Lord, one faith, and one baptism. ✝️
## Ephesians 4:6
There is one God and Father of all. He is over all, through all, and in all. ✨
## Ephesians 4:7
Each of us received grace according to the measure of Christ’s gift. 🎁
## Ephesians 4:8
Scripture says, “When He went up on high, He led prisoners away and gave gifts to people.” 📖
## Ephesians 4:9
What does “He went up” mean? It means He also went down to the lower parts of the earth. 💭
## Ephesians 4:10
The One who went down also went up above all the heavens to fill everything. ✨
## Ephesians 4:11
He gave some as apostles, some as prophets, some as preachers of good news, and some as pastors and teachers. 👥
## Ephesians 4:12
He gave them to prepare God’s holy people for service and build up Christ’s body. 🛠️
## Ephesians 4:13
This continues until we all become united in faith and in our knowledge of God’s Son. We are to grow mature, to the full measure of Christ’s stature. 🌱
## Ephesians 4:14
Then we will no longer be babies tossed by waves and carried by every wind of teaching. Clever people will no longer carry us away through their deceptive plans. 🌊
## Ephesians 4:15
Instead, speak the truth in love. In every way, we will grow up into Christ, who is the head. ❤️
## Ephesians 4:16
From Him, the whole body fits together and is held by every supporting ligament. As each part works, the body grows and builds itself up in love. 🌱
## Ephesians 4:17
I tell you and insist in the Lord: Stop living like the Gentiles, whose thoughts are useless. ⚠️
## Ephesians 4:18
Their understanding is darkened. Their hard hearts cause ignorance, which separates them from God’s life. 🌑
## Ephesians 4:19
They lost all sense of shame and gave themselves to sensual pleasure. They practice every kind of impurity and always want more. ⛔
## Ephesians 4:20
But this is not how you learned to know Christ. 👣
## Ephesians 4:21
Surely you heard about Him and were taught in Him, according to the truth that is in Jesus. 📖
## Ephesians 4:22
You were taught to remove your old self and former way of life. Deceptive desires corrupt that old self. ⚠️
## Ephesians 4:23
Let the spirit of your minds be renewed. 🌱
## Ephesians 4:24
Put on the new self, created to be like God in true righteousness and holiness. ✨
## Ephesians 4:25
Stop speaking falsehood. Each of you must tell his neighbor the truth, because we are all parts of each other. 🗣️
## Ephesians 4:26
“Be angry, but do not sin.” Do not let the sun set while you remain angry. 🌅
## Ephesians 4:27
Do not give the devil a foothold. 🛡️
## Ephesians 4:28
Anyone who steals must stop. He must work and do good with his hands, so that he has something to share with a person in need. 🛠️
## Ephesians 4:29
Do not speak harmful words. Speak only what helps people grow according to their needs and brings grace to those who listen. 🗣️
## Ephesians 4:30
Do not grieve God’s Holy Spirit. In Him, you were sealed for the day of redemption. 🕊️
## Ephesians 4:31
Get rid of all bitterness, rage, anger, shouting, slander, and every form of malice. ⛔
## Ephesians 4:32
Be kind and tenderhearted toward each other. Forgive each other as God forgave you in Christ. ❤️
