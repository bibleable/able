## Ephesians 6:1
Children, obey your parents in the Lord, because this is right. 👣
## Ephesians 6:2
“Honor your father and mother.” This is the first command with a promise. 📜
## Ephesians 6:3
The promise is “that life may go well for you and you may live long on the earth.” 🌅
## Ephesians 6:4
Fathers, do not make your children angry. Raise them with the Lord’s discipline and teaching. ❤️
## Ephesians 6:5
Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect, fear, and sincere hearts, as you would obey Christ. 🛠️
## Ephesians 6:6
Do this as Christ’s servants who do God’s will from the heart. Do not work only to please your masters when they watch you. 👣
## Ephesians 6:7
Serve willingly, as if you serve the Lord rather than people. ❤️
## Ephesians 6:8
You know that the Lord will reward each person for the good he does, whether slave or free. 🎁
## Ephesians 6:9
Masters, treat your slaves in the same way. Stop threatening them. You know that their Master and yours is in heaven. He does not favor some people over others. ⚖️
## Ephesians 6:10
Finally, be strong in the Lord and His mighty power. 💪
## Ephesians 6:11
Put on God’s full armor so that you can stand against the devil’s plans. 🛡️
## Ephesians 6:12
Our struggle is against rulers, authorities, and powers of this world’s darkness, rather than human beings. We fight spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms. ⚔️
## Ephesians 6:13
Take up God’s full armor. Then you can stand your ground when the evil day comes. After you have done everything, you can still stand. 🛡️
## Ephesians 6:14
Stand firm with truth as the belt around your waist. Wear righteousness as armor over your chest. 🛡️
## Ephesians 6:15
Fit your feet with the readiness that comes from the good news of peace. 👣
## Ephesians 6:16
Also take up faith as your shield. With it, you can put out all the evil one’s flaming arrows. 🛡️
## Ephesians 6:17
Take salvation as your helmet and God’s word as the Spirit’s sword. ⚔️
## Ephesians 6:18
Pray in the Spirit at all times with every kind of prayer and request. Stay alert and continue to pray for all God’s holy people. 🙏
## Ephesians 6:19
Pray for me too. Ask God to give me words whenever I speak, so that I can boldly announce the mystery of the good news. 📣
## Ephesians 6:20
I am its ambassador in chains. Pray that I can announce it without fear, as I should. ⛓️
## Ephesians 6:21
Tychicus, our dear brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will tell you everything. You can learn about me and what I do. ✉️
## Ephesians 6:22
I sent him so that you could learn about us and receive encouragement in your hearts. ❤️
## Ephesians 6:23
May God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give the brothers peace, love, and faith. 🕊️
## Ephesians 6:24
May grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with a love that never dies. ❤️
