## Ephesians 3:1
For this reason, I, Paul, am Christ Jesus’ prisoner for your sake, you Gentiles. ⛓️
## Ephesians 3:2
Surely you heard about the task God gave me to manage His grace for you. 🎁
## Ephesians 3:3
He revealed the mystery to me, as I already wrote briefly. 📖
## Ephesians 3:4
When you read this, you can understand my insight into Christ’s mystery. 💭
## Ephesians 3:5
People in earlier generations did not know this mystery as they do now. The Spirit has now revealed it to God’s holy apostles and prophets. 🕊️
## Ephesians 3:6
Through the good news, the Gentiles share the inheritance, belong to the same body, and share the promise in Christ Jesus. 🤝
## Ephesians 3:7
I became a servant of this good news through the gift of God’s grace. His power worked to give me this gift. 📣
## Ephesians 3:8
I am less than the least of all God’s holy people. Yet I received grace to tell the Gentiles about Christ’s riches, which cannot be fully searched out. ✨
## Ephesians 3:9
I also received grace to explain to everyone how God manages this mystery. For ages, it was hidden in God, who created everything. 📖
## Ephesians 3:10
God intended the church to show His wisdom in its many forms to rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms. ✨
## Ephesians 3:11
This agrees with the eternal purpose He accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord. ✝️
## Ephesians 3:12
In Christ and through faith in Him, we can enter God’s presence boldly and confidently. 🙏
## Ephesians 3:13
Do not lose heart because I suffer for you. These sufferings are your glory. ❤️
## Ephesians 3:14
For this reason, I kneel before the Father. 🙏
## Ephesians 3:15
Every family in heaven and on earth receives its name from Him. 🌍
## Ephesians 3:16
I ask Him to strengthen your inner being with power through His Spirit, from the riches of His glory. 💪
## Ephesians 3:17
Then Christ can live in your hearts through faith. May you be rooted and firmly based in love. ❤️
## Ephesians 3:18
May you have power, with all God’s holy people, to understand how long, wide, high, and deep Christ’s love is. ✨
## Ephesians 3:19
May you know this love that goes beyond knowledge. May you be filled with all God’s fullness. ❤️
## Ephesians 3:20
God can do far more than we ask or imagine through His power at work in us. 💪
## Ephesians 3:21
May He receive glory in the church and in Christ Jesus through every generation, forever and ever. Amen. ✨
