## Philemon 1:1
This letter comes from Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and our brother Timothy. We write to Philemon, our dear fellow worker. ✉️
## Philemon 1:2
We also write to our sister Apphia and our fellow soldier Archippus. We write to the church that meets in your house. 🏠👥
## Philemon 1:3
Let God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace. ❤️🤝
## Philemon 1:4
I always thank my God when I remember you in my prayers. 🙏
## Philemon 1:5
I hear about your faith in the Lord Jesus. I hear about your love for all His holy people. ❤️
## Philemon 1:6
I pray that the faith you share works well as you recognize every good thing we have in Christ. 🙏
## Philemon 1:7
Brother, your love gives me great joy and courage. You give new strength to the hearts of God’s holy people. ❤️😊
## Philemon 1:8
In Christ, I have enough boldness to order you to do what is right. ✅
## Philemon 1:9
But I prefer to ask you because of love. I, Paul, am now old. I am also a prisoner of Christ Jesus. ❤️
## Philemon 1:10
I ask you for my child Onesimus. I became like his father in faith while I was in chains. 🤲
## Philemon 1:11
Before, he was not useful to you. But now he is useful to both you and me. 🤝
## Philemon 1:12
I send him back to you. He is like my own heart. ❤️
## Philemon 1:13
I wanted to keep him with me. He could serve me for you while I am in chains for the good news. ⛓️
## Philemon 1:14
But I did not want to act without your agreement. Your good act must be your free choice. I do not want to force you. 🤝
## Philemon 1:15
Perhaps he was away from you for a short time so that you can have him back forever. ⏳
## Philemon 1:16
Receive him as more than a slave, as a dear brother. He is very dear to me. He is even dearer to you, both as a person and in the Lord. ❤️🤝
## Philemon 1:17
If you see me as a partner, welcome him just as you welcome me. 🤝
## Philemon 1:18
If he did you any wrong or owes you anything, charge it to me. 💰
## Philemon 1:19
I, Paul, write this with my own hand. I will pay it back. Remember that you owe me your very self. ✍️
## Philemon 1:20
Yes, brother, let me receive this good from you in the Lord. Give my heart new strength in Christ. ❤️
## Philemon 1:21
I write because I trust you to obey. I know you will do even more than I ask. ✅
## Philemon 1:22
Also prepare a guest room for me. I hope that God will bring me back to you through your prayers. 🏠🙏
## Philemon 1:23
Epaphras sends you greetings. He is my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus. 👋
## Philemon 1:24
Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke send greetings too. They work with me. 👋
## Philemon 1:25
Let the kindness of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. ❤️
