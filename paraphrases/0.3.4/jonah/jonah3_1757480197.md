## Jonah 3:1
The LORD gave Jonah a message for the second time: 📣
## Jonah 3:2
“Get up! Go to the great city of Nineveh. Announce the message I give you.” 🏙️
## Jonah 3:3
This time Jonah got up and went to Nineveh, as the LORD commanded. Nineveh was a very great city, a journey of three days. 👣
## Jonah 3:4
On the first day, Jonah went into the city. He announced, “In forty more days, Nineveh will be overthrown!” 📣
## Jonah 3:5
The people of Nineveh believed God. They declared a fast. Everyone, from the greatest to the least, put on sackcloth. 🙏

*Word help: A fast is a period without food. Sackcloth is rough cloth worn to show grief or sorrow for sin.*
## Jonah 3:6
When the king of Nineveh heard the message, he rose from his throne. He took off his royal robe, put on sackcloth, and sat in ashes. 👑
## Jonah 3:7
He announced this order in Nineveh: “By order of the king and his nobles, no person or animal may taste anything. No herd or flock may eat or drink. 📣
## Jonah 3:8
Cover both people and animals with sackcloth. Everyone must call earnestly to God. Each person must turn from evil behavior and stop violent acts. 🙏
## Jonah 3:9
Who knows? God may change His decision and turn from His fierce anger, so that we do not perish.” 🙏
## Jonah 3:10
God saw what they did and how they turned from evil. He changed His decision and did not bring the disaster He had threatened. 🕊️
