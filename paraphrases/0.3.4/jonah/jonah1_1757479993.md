## Jonah 1:1
The LORD gave this message to Jonah son of Amittai: 📣
## Jonah 1:2
“Get up! Go to the great city of Nineveh and speak against it. Its evil has come before Me.” 🏙️
## Jonah 1:3
But Jonah got up to flee to Tarshish, away from the LORD’s presence. He went down to Joppa and found a ship bound for Tarshish. He paid the fare and went aboard to sail there, away from the LORD’s presence. ⛵
## Jonah 1:4
The LORD sent a powerful wind over the sea. A violent storm arose, and the ship was in danger of breaking apart. 🌊
## Jonah 1:5
The sailors were afraid, and each called to his own god. They threw the cargo into the sea to make the ship lighter. But Jonah had gone down to the lowest part of the ship. He lay there, deep in sleep. ⛵
## Jonah 1:6
The captain came to him and said, “How can you sleep? Get up and call to your God! Perhaps He will care about us, so we do not perish.” 🙏
## Jonah 1:7
The sailors said to each other, “Let us cast lots to learn who caused this disaster.” They cast lots, and the lot pointed to Jonah. 🎲

*Word help: People cast lots to make a selection and seek an answer.*
## Jonah 1:8
They demanded, “Tell us who is to blame for this disaster. What work do you do? Where do you come from? What is your country, and who are your people?” 💬
## Jonah 1:9
Jonah replied, “I am a Hebrew. I worship the LORD, the God of heaven, who made the sea and dry land.” 🌍
## Jonah 1:10
The men became even more afraid and asked, “What have you done?” Jonah had told them that he was fleeing from the LORD’s presence. 😨
## Jonah 1:11
The sea became rougher and rougher. They asked Jonah, “What must we do to you to make the sea calm for us?” 🌊
## Jonah 1:12
He replied, “Pick me up and throw me into the sea. Then it will become calm for you. I know that I caused this violent storm to come upon you.” 🌊
## Jonah 1:13
Instead, the men rowed hard to reach dry land. They could not, because the sea raged more and more against them. 🚣
## Jonah 1:14
They called to the LORD, “Please, LORD, do not let us die because of this man’s life! Do not hold us guilty of innocent blood! You, LORD, have done what pleased You.” 🙏
## Jonah 1:15
They picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea. The raging sea became calm. 🌊
## Jonah 1:16
The men became deeply afraid of the LORD. They offered a sacrifice to Him and made promises to Him. 🙏
## Jonah 1:17
The LORD had appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah. Jonah stayed in its belly for three days and three nights. 🐟
