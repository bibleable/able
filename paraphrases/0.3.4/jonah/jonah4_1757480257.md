## Jonah 4:1
Jonah was very displeased and became angry. 😠
## Jonah 4:2
He prayed, “LORD, is this not what I said when I was still in my own country? That is why I hurried to flee toward Tarshish. I knew You are gracious and full of mercy. You are slow to become angry and full of faithful love. You change Your decision to send disaster. 🙏
## Jonah 4:3
Now, LORD, please take my life. It is better for me to die than to live.” 💔
## Jonah 4:4
The LORD replied, “Do you have a right to be angry?” 💬
## Jonah 4:5
Jonah left the city and sat east of it. He made a shelter and sat in its shade to see what would happen to the city. 🛖
## Jonah 4:6
The LORD God appointed a vine to grow over Jonah’s head. It gave him shade and eased his discomfort. Jonah was very happy about the plant. 🌿
## Jonah 4:7
At dawn the next day, God appointed a worm to attack the plant. The plant dried up. 🐛
## Jonah 4:8
When the sun rose, God appointed a hot east wind. The sun beat down on Jonah’s head, and he felt faint. He wished to die and said, “It is better for me to die than to live.” ☀️
## Jonah 4:9
God asked, “Do you have a right to be angry about the plant?” Jonah replied, “I do. I am angry enough to die!” 😠
## Jonah 4:10
The LORD said, “You cared about the plant, though you did not tend it or make it grow. It grew in one night and died in one night. 🌿
## Jonah 4:11
Should I not care about the great city of Nineveh? More than 120,000 people there cannot tell their right hand from their left. There are also many cattle.” 🏙️
