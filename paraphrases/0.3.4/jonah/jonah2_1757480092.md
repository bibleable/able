## Jonah 2:1
Inside the fish, Jonah prayed to the LORD his God. 🙏
## Jonah 2:2
He said, “In my distress, I called to the LORD, and He answered me. From the belly of Sheol, I cried for help, and You heard me. 🙏

*Word help: Sheol is the place of the dead.*
## Jonah 2:3
You threw me into the depths, into the heart of the seas. The current swirled around me. All Your waves swept over me. 🌊
## Jonah 2:4
I said, ‘I have been driven away from Your sight. Yet I will look again toward Your holy temple.’ 🛕
## Jonah 2:5
Water surrounded me and threatened my life. The deep sea closed around me. Seaweed wrapped around my head. 🌊
## Jonah 2:6
I sank to the roots of the mountains. The earth below shut me in forever. But You brought my life up from the pit, LORD my God! 🛡️
## Jonah 2:7
As my life faded, I remembered the LORD. My prayer reached You in Your holy temple. 🙏
## Jonah 2:8
Those who hold to worthless idols abandon His faithful love. ⛔
## Jonah 2:9
But I will offer sacrifices to You with thanks. I will keep my promises. Salvation comes from the LORD!” 🙏
## Jonah 2:10
The LORD commanded the fish, and it vomited Jonah onto dry land. 🐟
