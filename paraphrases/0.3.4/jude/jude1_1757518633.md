## Jude 1:1
I am Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and James’s brother. I write to the people God calls. God the Father loves you, and Jesus Christ keeps you safe. ✉️❤️
## Jude 1:2
Let kindness, peace, and love grow more and more for you. ❤️🤝
## Jude 1:3
Dear friends, I was eager to write about the rescue we share. But I need to urge you to fight firmly for the faith. God gave this faith to His holy people once for all. 📖🛡️
## Jude 1:4
Some people slip in among you without notice. They do not honor God. Long ago, a judgment of guilt was appointed for them. They use God’s kindness as permission for sexual sin. They deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ. ❌
## Jude 1:5
You already know this, but I want to remind you. Jesus rescued His people from Egypt. Afterward, He destroyed the people who did not believe. 💭⚖️
## Jude 1:6
Some angels did not stay in their proper place. They left their home. He keeps them in eternal chains in darkness for judgment on the great day. ⛓️⚖️
## Jude 1:7
Sodom, Gomorrah, and the nearby cities acted in the same way. They committed sexual sin and pursued sexual relations with strange flesh. Their punishment by eternal fire is an example for others. 🔥
## Jude 1:8
In the same way, these people follow their dreams. They make their bodies unclean. They reject authority and insult glorious beings. ❌
## Jude 1:9
Michael, an angel with high authority, argued with the devil about Moses’s body. He did not dare accuse the devil with insulting words. He only said, “Let the Lord correct you!” ⚖️
## Jude 1:10
But these people insult things they do not understand. They are like animals without understanding. The things they do without thought will destroy them. ❌
## Jude 1:11
Great trouble waits for them! They follow Cain’s path. They rush into Balaam’s error. They perish in rebellion like Korah’s. ⚖️
## Jude 1:12
These people are like hidden rocks at your meals of love. They eat with you without shame, but care only for themselves. They are like clouds without rain that the wind carries away. They are like trees without autumn fruit, twice dead and pulled up by the roots. 🌬️🌳
## Jude 1:13
They are like wild sea waves that throw up their shame like foam. They are like stars that wander. The deepest darkness waits for them forever. 🌊🌑
## Jude 1:14
Enoch, the seventh from Adam, also spoke God’s message about them. He said, “Look! The Lord comes with thousands upon thousands of His holy ones. 📣
## Jude 1:15
He will judge everyone. He will prove all ungodly people guilty of every evil act they do. He will prove them guilty of every harsh word they speak against Him.” ⚖️
## Jude 1:16
These people complain and are never content. They follow their own sinful desires. They speak proud words. They praise others falsely to gain something for themselves. 🗣️❌
## Jude 1:17
But dear friends, remember the predictions of our Lord Jesus Christ’s apostles. 💭
## Jude 1:18
They told you, “In the last times, people will mock. They will follow their own desires that do not honor God.” 🗣️
## Jude 1:19
These people divide you. They follow the world and do not have the Spirit. ❌
## Jude 1:20
But dear friends, make yourselves strong in your most holy faith. Pray in the Holy Spirit. 🙏
## Jude 1:21
Keep yourselves in God’s love. Wait for our Lord Jesus Christ’s kindness to bring you life that lasts forever. ❤️⏳
## Jude 1:22
Be kind to people who doubt. 🤲
## Jude 1:23
Rescue others as if you pull them from fire. Show kindness to still others with fear. Hate even clothes that sinful flesh stains. 🔥🤲
## Jude 1:24
God can keep you from a fall. He can bring you without fault before His glorious presence with great joy. 🛡️😊
## Jude 1:25
Give glory, greatness, rule, and authority to the only God, our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord. This is true before all time, now, and forever. Amen. 🙌
