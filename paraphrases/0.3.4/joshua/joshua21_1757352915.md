## Joshua 21:1
The Levites’ family heads approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and Israel’s other tribal heads, 👥
## Joshua 21:2
at Shiloh in Canaan. They said, “The LORD commanded through Moses that we receive cities to live in, with pasturelands for our livestock.” 📖
## Joshua 21:3
By the LORD’s command, Israel gave the Levites these cities and pasturelands from their own inheritance: 📖
## Joshua 21:4
The first lot belonged to the Kohathite clans. The Levite descendants of Aaron the priest received thirteen cities by lot from Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin. 📖
## Joshua 21:5
Kohath’s other descendants received ten cities by lot from Ephraim, Dan, and Manasseh’s half-tribe. 📖
## Joshua 21:6
Gershon’s descendants received thirteen cities by lot from Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Manasseh’s half-tribe in Bashan. 📖
## Joshua 21:7
Merari’s descendants received twelve cities from Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun. 📖
## Joshua 21:8
Israel allotted these cities and pasturelands to the Levites as the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖
## Joshua 21:9
Judah’s and Simeon’s tribes gave these named cities, 📖
## Joshua 21:10
to Aaron’s descendants from the Levites’ Kohathite clans, because they received the first lot. 📖
## Joshua 21:11
They received Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in Judah’s hills, with the surrounding pasturelands. Arba was Anak’s father. 📖
## Joshua 21:12
But Caleb son of Jephunneh had received the fields and villages around the city as his possession. 📖
## Joshua 21:13
Aaron the priest’s descendants received Hebron, a refuge city for the manslayer, and Libnah, with their pasturelands, 📖
## Joshua 21:14
Jattir and Eshtemoa, 📖
## Joshua 21:15
Holon and Debir, 📖
## Joshua 21:16
Ain, Juttah, and Beth-shemesh. There were nine cities from these two tribes, with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:17
Benjamin’s tribe gave them Gibeon and Geba, 📖
## Joshua 21:18
Anathoth and Almon. There were four cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:19
Aaron’s descendants, the priests, received thirteen cities in all, with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:20
The Levites’ remaining Kohathite clans received these cities from Ephraim’s tribe: 📖
## Joshua 21:21
Shechem in Ephraim’s hills, a refuge city for the manslayer, Gezer, 📖
## Joshua 21:22
Kibzaim, and Beth-horon. There were four cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:23
Dan’s tribe gave them Elteke and Gibbethon, 📖
## Joshua 21:24
Aijalon and Gath-rimmon. There were four cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:25
Manasseh’s half-tribe gave them Taanach and Gath-rimmon, two cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:26
The remaining Kohathite clans received ten cities in all, with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:27
Manasseh’s half-tribe gave the Gershonites’ Levite clans Golan in Bashan, a refuge city for the manslayer, and Beeshterah. These two cities had pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:28
Issachar’s tribe gave them Kishion and Daberath, 📖
## Joshua 21:29
Jarmuth and En-gannim. There were four cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:30
Asher’s tribe gave them Mishal and Abdon, 📖
## Joshua 21:31
Helkath and Rehob. There were four cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:32
Naphtali’s tribe gave them Kedesh in Galilee, a refuge city for the manslayer, Hammoth-dor, and Kartan. There were three cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:33
The Gershonite clans received thirteen cities in all, with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:34
The Merarite clan, the remaining Levites, received Jokneam and Kartah from Zebulun’s tribe, 📖
## Joshua 21:35
Dimnah and Nahalal. There were four cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:36
Reuben’s tribe gave them Bezer and Jahaz, 📖
## Joshua 21:37
Kedemoth and Mephaath. There were four cities with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:38
Gad’s tribe gave them Ramoth in Gilead, a refuge city for the manslayer, Mahanaim, 📖
## Joshua 21:39
Heshbon, and Jazer. There were four cities in all, with their pasturelands. 📖
## Joshua 21:40
Merari’s clans, the remaining Levite clans, received twelve cities in all. 📖
## Joshua 21:41
The Levites had forty-eight cities in all, with their pasturelands, within Israel’s territory. 📖
## Joshua 21:42
Every city had pasturelands surrounding it. This was true of all the cities. 📖
## Joshua 21:43
The LORD gave Israel all the land He swore to give their fathers. They possessed it and settled there. 📖
## Joshua 21:44
He gave them rest on every side, as He swore to their fathers. No enemy could stand against them, because the LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand. 📖
## Joshua 21:45
Not one of the LORD’s good promises to Israel’s house failed. Everything was fulfilled. 📖
