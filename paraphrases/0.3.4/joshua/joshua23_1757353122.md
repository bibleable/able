## Joshua 23:1
The LORD gave Israel rest from all surrounding enemies. A long time later, Joshua was very old, 📖
## Joshua 23:2
and called all Israel, its elders, leaders, judges, and officers. He said, “I am very old. 👥
## Joshua 23:3
You saw everything the LORD your God did to these nations for you, because He fought for you. 📖
## Joshua 23:4
I allotted the remaining nations to your tribes as an inheritance, including those I already cut off. Their land stretches from the Jordan west to the Great Sea. 📖
## Joshua 23:5
The LORD your God will push them away and drive them out before you. This is so you can possess their land, as He promised. 📖
## Joshua 23:6
Be very strong so you can obey everything written in the Book of the Law of Moses. Do not turn right or left from it. 📖
## Joshua 23:7
Do not associate with these nations remaining among you. Do not call on their gods’ names or swear by them. Do not serve or bow to them. ⚠️
## Joshua 23:8
Hold firmly to the LORD your God, as you have until today. 📖
## Joshua 23:9
The LORD drove out great, powerful nations before you. No one can stand against you to this day. 📖
## Joshua 23:10
One of you can make a thousand flee, because the LORD your God fights for you as He promised. 📖
## Joshua 23:11
Carefully guard yourselves to love the LORD your God. ❤️
## Joshua 23:12
If you turn away and cling to the remaining nations among you, marrying and associating with them, ⚠️
## Joshua 23:13
know that the LORD your God will no longer drive them out before you. They will be a snare and trap, a whip at your sides, and thorns in your eyes. This will last until you perish from the good land the LORD your God gave you. ⚖️
## Joshua 23:14
Today I am about to die, as all people do. You know with all your heart and soul that none of the LORD your God’s good promises has failed. Everything was fulfilled for you. Not one promise failed. 📖
## Joshua 23:15
Every good thing the LORD your God promised has happened. He will likewise bring the disaster He threatened until He destroys you from this good land He gave you. ⚖️
## Joshua 23:16
If you break the covenant the LORD your God commanded and serve and bow to other gods, His anger will burn against you. You will quickly perish from the good land He gave you.” ⚖️
