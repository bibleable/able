## Joshua 18:1
All Israel assembled at Shiloh and set up the Tent of Meeting. The land was subdued before them, 📖
## Joshua 18:2
but seven tribes of Israel had not received their inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 18:3
Joshua asked the Israelites, “How long will you delay entering and possessing the land the LORD, your fathers’ God, gave you? ❓
## Joshua 18:4
Choose three men from each tribe. I will send them to survey and map the land according to each inheritance. Then they will return to me, 📖
## Joshua 18:5
and divide it into seven portions. Judah will remain in the south, and Joseph’s house in the north, in their own territories. 📖
## Joshua 18:6
Bring me the map of the seven portions. I will cast lots for you here before the LORD our God. 📖
## Joshua 18:7
The Levites have no portion among you because their inheritance is the LORD’s priesthood. Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh’s half-tribe already received their inheritance east of the Jordan from Moses the LORD’s servant.” 📖
## Joshua 18:8
As the men prepared to leave, Joshua commanded, “Survey the land, map it, and return. Then I will cast lots for you here in Shiloh before the LORD.” 📖
## Joshua 18:9
The men traveled through the land and mapped it city by city into seven portions. They returned with the document to Joshua’s camp at Shiloh. 📜
## Joshua 18:10
Joshua cast lots before the LORD at Shiloh. He distributed the land to Israel according to their divisions. 📖
## Joshua 18:11
The first lot belonged to Benjamin’s tribal clans. Their territory lay between Judah’s and Joseph’s tribes. 📖
## Joshua 18:12
Their northern border began at the Jordan, rose past Jericho’s northern slope, and went west through the hills. It ended at Beth-aven’s wilderness. 📖
## Joshua 18:13
It crossed Luz’s southern slope, that is, Bethel. It descended to Ataroth-addar on the hill south of Lower Beth-horon. 📖
## Joshua 18:14
On the west, it curved south from the hill facing Beth-horon on the south. It ended at Kiriath-baal, that is, Kiriath-jearim, a city of Judah’s sons. This was the western side. 📖
## Joshua 18:15
The southern border began at Kiriath-jearim’s outskirts and ran west to the spring at the Waters of Nephtoah. 📖
## Joshua 18:16
It descended to the hill’s foot facing the Valley of Ben-hinnom, at the Valley of Rephaim’s northern end. It descended the Valley of Hinnom toward the Jebusites’ southern slope and down to En-rogel. 📖
## Joshua 18:17
It curved north to En-shemesh and Geliloth, facing the Ascent of Adummim. It continued down to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben. 📖
## Joshua 18:18
It reached Beth-arabah’s northern slope and descended into the valley. 📖
## Joshua 18:19
It continued to Beth-hoglah’s northern slope and ended at the Salt Sea’s northern bay, at the Jordan’s mouth. This was the southern border. 📖
## Joshua 18:20
The Jordan was the eastern border. These boundaries surrounded Benjamin’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 18:21
Benjamin’s tribal clans’ cities were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz, 📖
## Joshua 18:22
Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel, 📖
## Joshua 18:23
Avvim, Parah, Ophrah, 📖
## Joshua 18:24
Chephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba. There were twelve cities with their villages. 📖
## Joshua 18:25
Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth, 📖
## Joshua 18:26
Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah, 📖
## Joshua 18:27
Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah, 📖
## Joshua 18:28
Zelah, Haeleph, Jebus, that is, Jerusalem, Gibeah, and Kiriath-jearim. There were fourteen cities with their villages. This was Benjamin’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
