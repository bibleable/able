## Joshua 5:1
All the Amorite kings west of the Jordan and Canaanite kings along the coast heard what the LORD did. He dried the Jordan before the Israelites until they crossed. Their hearts melted, and they lost courage because they feared the Israelites. 📖
## Joshua 5:2
The LORD told Joshua, “Make flint knives and circumcise the sons of Israel once again.” 📖
## Joshua 5:3
Joshua made flint knives and circumcised Israel’s sons at Gibeath-haaraloth. 📖
## Joshua 5:4
This is why he circumcised them. All the men of war who left Egypt had died on the wilderness journey after leaving Egypt. 📖
## Joshua 5:5
Everyone who left Egypt was circumcised. But no one born during the wilderness journey from Egypt had been circumcised. 📖
## Joshua 5:6
Israel wandered in the wilderness forty years until all the nation’s men of war from Egypt died, because they disobeyed the LORD. He swore they would never see the land He promised their fathers to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey. 📖
## Joshua 5:7
He raised up their sons in their place. Joshua circumcised these sons, who had not been circumcised during the journey. 📖
## Joshua 5:8
After the whole nation was circumcised, they remained in camp until they healed. 📖
## Joshua 5:9
The LORD told Joshua, “Today I rolled Egypt’s disgrace away from you.” That place has been called Gilgal to this day. 📖
## Joshua 5:10
The Israelites camped at Gilgal on Jericho’s plains. On the evening of the month’s fourteenth day, they kept the Passover. 📖
## Joshua 5:11
The day after Passover, on that very day, they ate the land’s produce: unleavened bread and roasted grain. 🍞
## Joshua 5:12
Manna stopped the day after they ate the land’s produce. The Israelites received no more manna. That year they ate Canaan’s crops. 🌾
## Joshua 5:13
Near Jericho, Joshua looked up and saw a man before him with a drawn sword. He approached and asked, “Are You for us or our enemies?” ⚔️
## Joshua 5:14
He answered, “Neither. I have come as Commander of the LORD’s army.” Joshua fell facedown in reverence and asked, “What does my Lord say to His servant?” 🙇
## Joshua 5:15
The Commander of the LORD’s army answered, “Remove your sandals, because the place where you stand is holy.” Joshua obeyed. 📖
