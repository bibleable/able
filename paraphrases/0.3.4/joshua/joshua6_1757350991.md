## Joshua 6:1
Jericho was tightly closed because of the Israelites. No one entered or left. 📖
## Joshua 6:2
The LORD told Joshua, “I have delivered Jericho, its king, and its strong, brave men into your hand. 📖
## Joshua 6:3
March all your men of war around the city once. Do this for six days. ⚔️
## Joshua 6:4
Seven priests must carry seven rams’ horns before the ark. On the seventh day, march around the city seven times while the priests blow the horns. 📯
## Joshua 6:5
When you hear a long blast from the ram’s horn, have all the people shout loudly. The city wall will collapse. Your people will charge straight into the city.” 📯
## Joshua 6:6
Joshua son of Nun called the priests. He said, “Carry the covenant ark. Have seven priests carry seven rams’ horns before the LORD’s ark.” 👥
## Joshua 6:7
He told the people, “Advance and march around the city. The armed troops must go ahead of the LORD’s ark.” ⚔️
## Joshua 6:8
After Joshua spoke, seven priests advanced before the LORD, carrying and blowing seven rams’ horns. The ark of the LORD’s covenant followed. 📯
## Joshua 6:9
Armed troops marched ahead of the priests blowing the horns. The rear guard followed the ark. The horns kept sounding. ⚔️
## Joshua 6:10
Joshua commanded, “Do not shout or let your voices be heard. Say nothing until the day I tell you to shout. Then shout!” 📖
## Joshua 6:11
He had the LORD’s ark carried around the city once. The people returned to camp and spent the night. 📖
## Joshua 6:12
Joshua rose early the next morning. The priests took the LORD’s ark. 👥
## Joshua 6:13
Seven priests marched ahead of the LORD’s ark, carrying and blowing seven rams’ horns. Armed troops went before them, and the rear guard followed the ark. The horns kept sounding. 📯
## Joshua 6:14
On the second day, they circled the city once and returned to camp. They did this for six days. 📖
## Joshua 6:15
On the seventh day, they rose at dawn and circled the city seven times in the same way. Only that day did they circle it seven times. 📖
## Joshua 6:16
After the seventh circuit, the priests blew the horns. Joshua commanded, “Shout! The LORD has given you the city! 📯
## Joshua 6:17
The city and everything in it must be devoted to the LORD for destruction. Only Rahab the prostitute and everyone in her house will live, because she hid our spies. 📖
## Joshua 6:18
Keep away from things devoted to destruction, so you are not also set apart for destruction. Taking them will set Israel’s camp apart for destruction and bring disaster on it. ⚠️
## Joshua 6:19
All silver, gold, and bronze and iron articles are holy to the LORD. They must go into His treasury.” 📖
## Joshua 6:20
The rams’ horns sounded, and the people gave a great shout when they heard them. The wall collapsed. They charged straight into the city and captured it. ⚔️
## Joshua 6:21
With the sword, they devoted everyone and everything to destruction: men, women, young, old, oxen, sheep, and donkeys. ⚔️
## Joshua 6:22
Joshua told the two spies, “Enter the prostitute’s house. Bring out the woman and everyone with her, as you promised.” 👥
## Joshua 6:23
The young spies brought out Rahab, her father, mother, brothers, and everyone belonging to her. They settled her whole family outside Israel’s camp. 👥
## Joshua 6:24
The Israelites burned the city and everything in it. They put the silver, gold, and bronze and iron articles into the treasury of the LORD’s house. 🔥
## Joshua 6:25
Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute, her father’s household, and everyone belonging to her because she hid the spies he sent to Jericho. She lives among the Israelites to this day. 👥
## Joshua 6:26
Joshua declared this solemn oath: “Cursed before the LORD is the man who rebuilds Jericho! Laying its foundation will cost him his firstborn. Setting up its gates will cost him his youngest.” ⚖️
## Joshua 6:27
The LORD was with Joshua. His fame spread throughout the land. 📖
