## Joshua 2:1
Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two spies from Shittim. He said, “Inspect the land, especially Jericho.” They went to the house of a prostitute named Rahab and stayed there. 👥
## Joshua 2:2
Jericho’s king was told, “Some men from Israel came tonight to spy on the land.” 📖
## Joshua 2:3
He sent Rahab this message: “Bring out the men who entered your house. They came to spy on the whole land.” 💬
## Joshua 2:4
She had hidden the two men. She answered, “Yes, they came, but I did not know where they came from. 📖
## Joshua 2:5
They left at dusk before the gate closed. I do not know which way they went. Pursue them quickly, and you may catch them!” 💬
## Joshua 2:6
But Rahab had taken them to the roof. She hid them among the flax stalks she laid there. 📖
## Joshua 2:7
The king’s men pursued the spies along the road to the Jordan’s fords. The gate shut as soon as they left. 📖
## Joshua 2:8
Before the spies lay down for the night, Rahab went onto the roof, 📖
## Joshua 2:9
and said, “I know the LORD gave you this land. We are afraid of you. Everyone in the land is melting with fear of you. 📖
## Joshua 2:10
We heard how the LORD dried the Red Sea before you when you left Egypt. We heard what you did to Sihon and Og, the two Amorite kings across the Jordan. You devoted them to destruction. 📖
## Joshua 2:11
Hearing this melted our hearts. No one had courage left because of you. The LORD your God is God in heaven above and on earth below. 🙏
## Joshua 2:12
Please swear by the LORD to show kindness to my family, because I showed kindness to you. Give me a sure sign, 🙏
## Joshua 2:13
that you will spare my father, mother, brothers, sisters, and everyone belonging to them. Save us from death.” 🙏
## Joshua 2:14
The men answered, “Our lives for yours! If you keep our mission secret, we will show you kindness and faithfulness when the LORD gives us the land.” 🤝
## Joshua 2:15
Rahab lowered them on a rope through the window because her house was built into the city wall. 📖
## Joshua 2:16
She said, “Go to the hill country so your pursuers do not find you. Hide there for three days until they return. Then go on your way.” 📖
## Joshua 2:17
The men said, “We will not be bound by the oath you made us swear, 📖
## Joshua 2:18
unless you tie this scarlet cord in the window where you lowered us, when we enter the land. You must also gather your father, mother, brothers, and all your family into your house. 📖
## Joshua 2:19
If anyone leaves your house for the street, his blood will be on his own head. We will be innocent. If anyone in the house with you is harmed, his blood will be on our heads. 📖
## Joshua 2:20
If you reveal our mission, we are released from your oath.” 📖
## Joshua 2:21
She answered, “Let it be as you say,” and sent them away. After they left, she tied the scarlet cord in the window. 📖
## Joshua 2:22
The spies hid in the hill country for three days. Their pursuers searched the whole road without finding them and returned. 📖
## Joshua 2:23
The two men came down from the hill country and crossed the river. They returned to Joshua son of Nun and reported everything that happened. 👥
## Joshua 2:24
They told Joshua, “The LORD has surely delivered the whole land into our hands. Everyone there is melting with fear of us.” 📖
