## Joshua 16:1
Joseph’s descendants received land from the Jordan at Jericho to Jericho’s waters on the east. It passed through the wilderness rising from Jericho to Bethel’s hill country. 📖
## Joshua 16:2
It continued from Bethel, that is, Luz, to the Archites’ border at Ataroth. 📖
## Joshua 16:3
It descended west to the Japhletites’ border, to Lower Beth-horon’s border and Gezer. It ended at the Sea. 📖
## Joshua 16:4
Joseph’s sons Ephraim and Manasseh received their inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 16:5
Ephraim’s clans received this territory. Its border ran from Ataroth-addar in the east to Upper Beth-horon, 📖
## Joshua 16:6
and toward the Sea. From Michmethath in the north, it turned east toward Taanath-shiloh and passed it to Janoah on the east. 📖
## Joshua 16:7
From Janoah it descended to Ataroth and Naarah, reached Jericho, and ended at the Jordan. 📖
## Joshua 16:8
From Tappuah it ran west to the Brook of Kanah and ended at the Sea. This was Ephraim’s tribal clans’ inheritance, 📖
## Joshua 16:9
with all the cities and villages set apart for Ephraim’s descendants within Manasseh’s inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 16:10
They did not drive out Gezer’s Canaanites. The Canaanites remain among the Ephraimites to this day as forced laborers. 📖
