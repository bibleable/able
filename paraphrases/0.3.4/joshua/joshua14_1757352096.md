## Joshua 14:1
These are Israel’s inherited portions in Canaan. Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and Israel’s tribal family heads distributed them. 📖
## Joshua 14:2
They assigned the inheritance by lot to the nine and a half tribes, as the LORD commanded through Moses. 📖
## Joshua 14:3
Moses gave the other two and a half tribes their inheritance east of the Jordan. He gave the Levites no inheritance among them. 📖
## Joshua 14:4
Joseph’s descendants became two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. The Levites received no land portion, except cities to live in and pasture for their flocks and herds. 📖
## Joshua 14:5
Israel divided the land as the LORD commanded Moses. 📖
## Joshua 14:6
Judah’s sons approached Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said, “You know what the LORD told Moses, the man of God, about us at Kadesh-barnea. 📖
## Joshua 14:7
I was forty years old when Moses the LORD’s servant sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy on the land. I brought him an honest report. 📖
## Joshua 14:8
My brothers who went with me melted the people’s hearts with fear. But I remained loyal to the LORD my God. 📖
## Joshua 14:9
Moses swore to me that day. He said, ‘The land where you set foot will belong to you and your children forever because you followed the LORD my God completely.’ 📖
## Joshua 14:10
The LORD kept me alive these forty-five years, as He promised. He spoke this word to Moses while Israel wandered in the wilderness. Today I am eighty-five years old, 📖
## Joshua 14:11
and as strong as the day Moses sent me. My strength for war, going out, and coming in is the same now as then. 💪
## Joshua 14:12
Give me this hill country the LORD promised that day. You heard that the Anakim lived there in great, fortified cities. Perhaps with the LORD’s help I will drive them out, as He said.” 📖
## Joshua 14:13
Joshua blessed Caleb son of Jephunneh and gave him Hebron as his inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 14:14
Hebron remains the inheritance of Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day because he fully followed the LORD, the God of Israel. 📖
## Joshua 14:15
Hebron was formerly Kiriath-arba, named after Arba, the greatest man among the Anakim. Then the land rested from war. 📖
