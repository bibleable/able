## Joshua 15:1
Judah’s tribal clans received land to Edom’s border, to the Wilderness of Zin at the far southern boundary. 📖
## Joshua 15:2
Their southern border began at the bay on the Salt Sea’s southern tip, 📖
## Joshua 15:3
passed south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, continued to Zin, and went south of Kadesh-barnea. It passed Hezron, went up to Addar, and curved toward Karka. 📖
## Joshua 15:4
It went to Azmon, joined the Brook of Egypt, and ended at the Sea. This was their southern border. 📖
## Joshua 15:5
The Salt Sea was the eastern border up to the Jordan’s mouth. The northern border began at the sea’s bay at the Jordan’s mouth, 📖
## Joshua 15:6
went up to Beth-hoglah, passed north of Beth-arabah, and reached the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben. 📖
## Joshua 15:7
It rose from the Valley of Achor to Debir and turned north to Gilgal. Gilgal faces the Ascent of Adummim south of the ravine. The border followed En-shemesh’s waters and ended at En-rogel. 📖
## Joshua 15:8
It rose through the Valley of Ben-hinnom along the Jebusites’ southern slope, that is, Jerusalem. It climbed the hill facing the Valley of Hinnom to the west, at the Valley of Rephaim’s northern end. 📖
## Joshua 15:9
From the hilltop, it curved to the spring of the Waters of Nephtoah. It continued to Mount Ephron’s cities, then curved toward Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim. 📖
## Joshua 15:10
It curved west from Baalah to Mount Seir and followed Mount Jearim’s northern slope, that is, Chesalon. It descended to Beth-shemesh and crossed to Timnah. 📖
## Joshua 15:11
It reached Ekron’s northern slope, curved toward Shikkeron, continued to Mount Baalah and Jabneel, and ended at the Sea. 📖
## Joshua 15:12
The Great Sea’s coast was the western border. These boundaries surrounded Judah’s clans. 📖
## Joshua 15:13
As the LORD commanded him, Joshua gave Caleb son of Jephunneh a portion among Judah’s sons: Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron. Arba was Anak’s forefather. 📖
## Joshua 15:14
Caleb drove out Anak’s three sons: the descendants of Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, the children of Anak. ⚔️
## Joshua 15:15
He advanced against Debir’s inhabitants. Its former name was Kiriath-sepher. ⚔️
## Joshua 15:16
Caleb said, “I will give my daughter Acsah in marriage to the man who defeats and captures Kiriath-sepher.” 💬
## Joshua 15:17
Othniel, son of Caleb’s brother Kenaz, captured it. Caleb gave him his daughter Acsah in marriage. 👥
## Joshua 15:18
Acsah came to Othniel and urged him to ask her father for a field. She dismounted her donkey, and Caleb asked, “What do you want?” 💬
## Joshua 15:19
She answered, “Give me a blessing. You gave me land in the Negev, so give me springs too.” Caleb gave her the upper and lower springs. 💧
## Joshua 15:20
This is Judah’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
