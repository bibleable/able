## Joshua 11:1
Jabin king of Hazor heard this news. He sent messages to Jobab king of Madon and the kings of Shimron and Achshaph, 👥
## Joshua 11:2
to the northern kings in the mountains, the Arabah south of Chinnereth, the foothills, and Naphoth-dor to the west, 📖
## Joshua 11:3
and to the eastern and western Canaanites. He contacted the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites in the hills, and Hivites below Hermon in Mizpah’s land. 📖
## Joshua 11:4
The kings brought all their armies, as numerous as sand on the seashore. They had many horses and chariots. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:5
They united and camped at Merom’s waters to fight Israel. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:6
The LORD told Joshua, “Do not fear them. By this time tomorrow, I will deliver them all slain before Israel. Hamstring their horses and burn their chariots.” 📖
## Joshua 11:7
Joshua and his whole army surprised and attacked them at Merom’s waters, ⚔️
## Joshua 11:8
and the LORD delivered them to Israel. Israel struck and pursued them to Greater Sidon and Misrephoth-maim, and east to the Valley of Mizpeh. They left no survivors. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:9
Joshua obeyed the LORD. He hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots. 🔥
## Joshua 11:10
Joshua returned, captured Hazor, and killed its king with the sword, because it had been the head of all these kingdoms. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:11
Israel put everyone in Hazor to the sword, devoting them to destruction. Nothing that breathed remained. Joshua burned Hazor. 🔥
## Joshua 11:12
Joshua captured all these kings and cities and put them to the sword. He devoted them to destruction as Moses the LORD’s servant commanded. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:13
Israel did not burn the cities built on their mounds, except Hazor, which Joshua burned. 🔥
## Joshua 11:14
Israel kept all the cities’ plunder and livestock. But they put all the people to the sword, sparing no one who breathed. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:15
The LORD commanded Moses, and Moses commanded Joshua. Joshua did everything the LORD commanded Moses, leaving nothing undone. 📖
## Joshua 11:16
Joshua took all the hill country, Negev, Goshen’s land, western foothills, Arabah, and Israel’s mountains and foothills, 📖
## Joshua 11:17
from Mount Halak toward Seir to Baal-gad in Lebanon’s valley below Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings and killed them. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:18
Joshua fought these kings for a long time. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:19
Only the Hivites in Gibeon made peace with Israel. Every other city was taken in battle. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:20
The LORD hardened their hearts to fight Israel, so they would be set apart for destruction without mercy. They would be annihilated as He commanded Moses. ⚖️
## Joshua 11:21
Joshua eliminated the Anakim from the hills of Hebron, Debir, and Anab, and all Judah’s and Israel’s hill country. He devoted them and their cities to destruction. ⚔️
## Joshua 11:22
No Anakim remained in Israel’s land. Some survived only in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod. 📖
## Joshua 11:23
Joshua took the whole land as the LORD told Moses. He gave Israel its inheritance according to the tribal allotments. Then the land rested from war. 📖
