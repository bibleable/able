## Joshua 9:1
The news reached all kings west of the Jordan, in the hills, foothills, and along the Great Sea’s coast toward Lebanon. They were Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 📖
## Joshua 9:2
They united to fight Joshua and Israel. ⚔️
## Joshua 9:3
The people of Gibeon heard what Joshua did to Jericho and Ai, 📖
## Joshua 9:4
and acted deceptively. They traveled as envoys with worn sacks on their donkeys and old wineskins, cracked and repaired. 📖
## Joshua 9:5
They wore worn, patched sandals and threadbare clothes. All their bread was dry and moldy. 🍞
## Joshua 9:6
They came to Joshua’s camp at Gilgal. They told him and Israel’s men, “We came from a distant land. Please make a treaty with us.” 🤝
## Joshua 9:7
Israel’s men told the Hivites, “Perhaps you live nearby. How can we make a treaty with you?” ❓
## Joshua 9:8
They told Joshua, “We are your servants.” He asked, “Who are you? Where did you come from?” ❓
## Joshua 9:9
They answered, “Your servants came from a very distant land because of the LORD your God’s fame. We heard about everything He did in Egypt, 📖
## Joshua 9:10
and to the two Amorite kings beyond the Jordan. They were Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth. 📖
## Joshua 9:11
Our elders and people told us, ‘Take supplies and go meet them. Say, We are your servants. Please make a treaty with us.’ 🤝
## Joshua 9:12
Our bread was warm when we packed it at home on the day we left. Now it is dry and moldy. 🍞
## Joshua 9:13
These wineskins were new when we filled them. Now they are cracked. Our clothes and sandals wore out on our very long journey.” 📖
## Joshua 9:14
Israel’s men sampled their supplies but did not ask the LORD for counsel. ⚠️
## Joshua 9:15
Joshua made a peace treaty to let them live. The assembly’s leaders swore an oath to them. 🤝
## Joshua 9:16
Three days after making the treaty with the Gibeonites, Israel learned they were neighbors living among them. 📖
## Joshua 9:17
The Israelites traveled and reached their cities on the third day: Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath-jearim. 📖
## Joshua 9:18
Israel did not attack because the assembly’s leaders swore an oath by the LORD, the God of Israel. The whole assembly grumbled against the leaders. 👥
## Joshua 9:19
The leaders answered, “We swore to them by the LORD, the God of Israel. We cannot touch them now. 📖
## Joshua 9:20
Let them live so no wrath falls on us because of our oath.” 📖
## Joshua 9:21
They continued, “Let them live as woodcutters and water carriers for the whole assembly.” The leaders kept their promise. 👥
## Joshua 9:22
Joshua called the Gibeonites and asked, “Why did you deceive us? You said you lived far away, but you live among us. ❓
## Joshua 9:23
You are cursed. You will always serve as woodcutters and water carriers for my God’s house.” 📖
## Joshua 9:24
They answered, “We clearly heard the LORD your God’s command to His servant Moses. The command was to give you all the land and destroy all its inhabitants before you. We feared for our lives because of you. That is why we did this. 📖
## Joshua 9:25
We are in your hands. Do whatever you consider good and right.” 💬
## Joshua 9:26
Joshua rescued them from the Israelites. They did not kill the Gibeonites. 📖
## Joshua 9:27
He made them woodcutters and water carriers for the LORD’s assembly and altar at the place He would choose. They serve this way to this day. 👥
