## Joshua 7:1
Israel acted unfaithfully concerning things devoted to destruction. Achan took some of them. He was the son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from Judah’s tribe. The LORD’s anger burned against the Israelites. ⚖️
## Joshua 7:2
Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, near Beth-aven east of Bethel. He told them, “Spy on the land.” They went and inspected Ai. 👥
## Joshua 7:3
They reported, “Do not send everyone. Two or three thousand men can attack Ai. Its people are few, so do not wear out all our people.” 💬
## Joshua 7:4
About three thousand men went, but fled from Ai’s men. ⚔️
## Joshua 7:5
Ai’s men killed about thirty-six Israelites. They chased them from the gate to the quarries and struck them down on the slopes. The people’s hearts melted and became like water. ⚔️
## Joshua 7:6
Joshua tore his clothes and fell facedown before the LORD’s ark until evening. Israel’s elders did the same. They sprinkled dust on their heads. 🙏
## Joshua 7:7
Joshua said, “Lord GOD, why did You bring these people across the Jordan to hand us to the Amorites for destruction? If only we stayed content on the Jordan’s other side! 🙏
## Joshua 7:8
Lord, what can I say now that Israel turned its back and fled from its enemies? ❓
## Joshua 7:9
The Canaanites and everyone in the land will hear, surround us, and erase our name from the earth. What will You do for Your great name?” 🙏
## Joshua 7:10
The LORD told Joshua, “Stand up! Why have you fallen on your face? 📖
## Joshua 7:11
Israel sinned and broke the covenant I commanded them. They took things devoted to destruction. They stole, lied, and put these things among their possessions. ⚖️
## Joshua 7:12
That is why Israel cannot stand against its enemies. They flee because they themselves are set apart for destruction. I will not remain with you unless you remove what is devoted to destruction. ⚖️
## Joshua 7:13
Get up and set the people apart as holy. Tell them, ‘Prepare yourselves as holy for tomorrow. The LORD, the God of Israel, says you have things devoted to destruction among you. You cannot stand against your enemies until you remove them. 📖
## Joshua 7:14
Present yourselves tribe by tribe in the morning. The LORD’s chosen tribe must come clan by clan. His chosen clan must come family by family. His chosen family must come man by man. 👥
## Joshua 7:15
The man caught with things devoted to destruction must be burned, with everything belonging to him. This is because he broke the LORD’s covenant and committed an outrage in Israel.’” ⚖️
## Joshua 7:16
Joshua rose early and brought Israel forward tribe by tribe. Judah’s tribe was selected. 👥
## Joshua 7:17
He brought Judah’s clans forward, and the Zerahites’ clan was selected. He brought the Zerahites forward, and Zabdi’s family was selected. 👥
## Joshua 7:18
He brought Zabdi’s family forward man by man. Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from Judah’s tribe, was selected. 👥
## Joshua 7:19
Joshua told Achan, “My son, glorify the LORD, the God of Israel, and confess to Him. Tell me what you did. Do not hide it.” 📖
## Joshua 7:20
Achan answered, “It is true. I sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. This is what I did: 📖
## Joshua 7:21
I saw a beautiful cloak from Shinar, two hundred shekels of silver, and a gold bar weighing fifty shekels among the plunder. I desired them and took them. They are buried in my tent, with the silver underneath.” 📖
## Joshua 7:22
Joshua’s messengers ran to the tent. Everything was hidden there, with the silver underneath. 📖
## Joshua 7:23
They brought the things to Joshua and all the Israelites and spread them before the LORD. 📖
## Joshua 7:24
Joshua and all Israel took Achan son of Zerah to the Valley of Achor. They took the silver, cloak, gold bar, his sons, daughters, oxen, donkeys, sheep, tent, and everything else he owned. 👥
## Joshua 7:25
Joshua said, “Why did you bring trouble on us? Today the LORD will bring trouble on you!” All Israel stoned him to death. They stoned the others and burned their bodies. ⚖️
## Joshua 7:26
They piled many rocks over Achan. The pile remains to this day. The LORD turned from His burning anger. That place is called the Valley of Achor to this day. 🪨
