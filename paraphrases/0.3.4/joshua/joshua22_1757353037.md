## Joshua 22:1
Joshua called the Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh, 👥
## Joshua 22:2
and said, “You did everything Moses the LORD’s servant commanded. You also obeyed everything I commanded. 📖
## Joshua 22:3
You have not deserted your brothers throughout this time, even to today. You kept the charge the LORD your God gave you. 📖
## Joshua 22:4
The LORD your God gave your brothers rest as He promised. Return home to the land Moses the LORD’s servant gave you across the Jordan. 📖
## Joshua 22:5
Carefully obey the commandment and law Moses the LORD’s servant gave you. Love the LORD your God, walk in all His ways, and keep His commandments. Hold firmly to Him and serve Him with all your heart and soul.” ❤️
## Joshua 22:6
Joshua blessed them and sent them home. 👥
## Joshua 22:7
Moses gave one half of Manasseh land in Bashan. Joshua gave the other half land west of the Jordan among their brothers. He blessed them as he sent them home, 📖
## Joshua 22:8
saying, “Return with your great wealth, immense herds, silver, gold, bronze, iron, and many clothes. Share your enemies’ plunder with your brothers.” 📖
## Joshua 22:9
The Reubenites, Gadites, and Manasseh’s half-tribe left Israel at Shiloh in Canaan. They returned to Gilead, the land they acquired by the LORD’s command through Moses. 📖
## Joshua 22:10
They reached Geliloth near the Jordan in Canaan. The Reubenites, Gadites, and Manasseh’s half-tribe built an imposing altar there beside the Jordan. 🪨
## Joshua 22:11
Israel heard, “The Reubenites, Gadites, and Manasseh’s half-tribe built an altar at Canaan’s border. It stands at Geliloth near the Jordan on the Israelite side.” 📖
## Joshua 22:12
The whole assembly of Israel gathered at Shiloh to fight them. ⚔️
## Joshua 22:13
Israel sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the Reubenites, Gadites, and Manasseh’s half-tribe in Gilead. 👥
## Joshua 22:14
Ten chiefs accompanied him, one family leader from each tribe of Israel. Each headed a family among Israel’s clans. 👥
## Joshua 22:15
They went to the Reubenites, Gadites, and Manasseh’s half-tribe in Gilead and said, 👥
## Joshua 22:16
“The LORD’s whole assembly asks, ‘Why did you break faith with Israel’s God today? You turned from the LORD and built an altar to rebel against Him. ⚠️
## Joshua 22:17
Was Peor’s sin not enough? We remain unclean from it to this day. It even brought a plague on the LORD’s assembly. ⚠️
## Joshua 22:18
Will you turn from the LORD now? If you rebel today, tomorrow He will be angry with Israel’s whole assembly. ⚠️
## Joshua 22:19
If your inherited land is unclean, cross to the LORD’s land where His tabernacle stands and take possession among us. Do not rebel against the LORD or us by building another altar besides the LORD our God’s altar. ⚠️
## Joshua 22:20
Did Achan son of Zerah not break faith concerning things devoted to destruction and bring wrath on Israel’s whole assembly? He was not the only one who died because of his sin!’” ⚖️
## Joshua 22:21
The Reubenites, Gadites, and Manasseh’s half-tribe answered Israel’s clan leaders: 👥
## Joshua 22:22
“The LORD, the Mighty One, is God! The LORD, the Mighty One, is God! He knows, and may Israel know too. If this was rebellion or unfaithfulness against the LORD, do not spare us today. 📖
## Joshua 22:23
If we built this altar to turn from Him and offer burnt offerings, grain offerings, or peace offerings, may the LORD Himself hold us accountable. 📖
## Joshua 22:24
We did this because we feared your descendants might tell ours in the future, ‘What have you to do with the LORD, Israel’s God? 📖
## Joshua 22:25
The LORD made the Jordan a border between us and you Reubenites and Gadites. You have no share in the LORD!’ Your descendants could cause ours to stop fearing the LORD. 📖
## Joshua 22:26
That is why we said, ‘Let us build an altar, but not for burnt offerings or sacrifices. 🪨
## Joshua 22:27
It will witness between us, you, and future generations that we will worship the LORD in His presence. We will bring burnt offerings, sacrifices, and peace offerings.’ Your descendants then cannot tell ours, ‘You have no share in the LORD!’ 📖
## Joshua 22:28
We said, ‘If they ever tell us or our descendants this, we will answer them. Look at the replica of the LORD’s altar that our fathers made. It is not for burnt offerings or sacrifices, but a witness between us and you.’ 🪨
## Joshua 22:29
We would never rebel against the LORD or turn from Him today. We would not build an altar for burnt offerings, grain offerings, or sacrifices other than the LORD our God’s altar before His tabernacle.” 📖
## Joshua 22:30
Phinehas the priest and the chiefs with him, Israel’s clan heads, heard Reuben’s, Gad’s, and Manasseh’s descendants. They were satisfied. 👥
## Joshua 22:31
Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest addressed Reuben’s, Gad’s, and Manasseh’s descendants. He said, “We know today that the LORD is among us because you have not broken faith with Him. This delivered Israel from the LORD’s hand.” 📖
## Joshua 22:32
Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned to Israel in Canaan. They reported about the Reubenites and Gadites in Gilead. 👥
## Joshua 22:33
Israel was satisfied and blessed God. They no longer spoke of war to destroy the land where the Reubenites and Gadites lived. 🙏
## Joshua 22:34
The Reubenites and Gadites named the altar Witness. They said, “It witnesses between us that the LORD is God.” 🪨
