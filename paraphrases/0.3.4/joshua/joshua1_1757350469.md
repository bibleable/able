## Joshua 1:1
After His servant Moses died, the LORD spoke to Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ assistant: 📖
## Joshua 1:2
“My servant Moses is dead. Get up with all these people and cross the Jordan. Enter the land I am giving the children of Israel. 📖
## Joshua 1:3
I have given you every place where your foot steps, as I promised Moses. 📖
## Joshua 1:4
Your territory will reach from the wilderness and Lebanon to the great River Euphrates. It will include all the Hittites’ land and extend west to the Great Sea. 📖
## Joshua 1:5
No one will stand against you throughout your life. I will be with you as I was with Moses. I will never leave or abandon you. ❤️
## Joshua 1:6
Be strong and courageous. You will give these people the land as their inheritance, as I swore to their fathers. 💪
## Joshua 1:7
Above all, be strong and very courageous. Carefully obey all the law My servant Moses commanded you. Do not turn right or left from it, so you may prosper wherever you go. 📖
## Joshua 1:8
Keep speaking the words of this Book of the Law. Think deeply about it day and night so you carefully do everything written in it. Then you will prosper and succeed in all you do. 📖
## Joshua 1:9
Did I not command you to be strong and courageous? Do not fear or lose courage. The LORD your God is with you wherever you go.” ❤️
## Joshua 1:10
Joshua commanded the people’s officers, 👥
## Joshua 1:11
“Go through the camp and tell the people to prepare their supplies. Within three days, you will cross the Jordan to possess the land the LORD your God is giving you.” 📖
## Joshua 1:12
Joshua spoke to the Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh: 👥
## Joshua 1:13
“Remember what Moses, the LORD’s servant, commanded you. He said, ‘The LORD your God will give you rest and this land.’ 📖
## Joshua 1:14
Your wives, young children, and livestock may stay in the land Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan. All your strong, brave men must cross ahead of your brothers, armed for battle, to help them, ⚔️
## Joshua 1:15
until the LORD gives them rest as He gave you, and they possess the land He gives them. Then return and possess your inheritance east of the Jordan, which Moses the LORD’s servant gave you.” 📖
## Joshua 1:16
They answered Joshua, “We will do everything you command and go wherever you send us. 💬
## Joshua 1:17
We will obey you in everything as we obeyed Moses. May the LORD your God be with you as He was with Moses. 🙏
## Joshua 1:18
Anyone who rebels against your order and disobeys any of your commands will be put to death. Above all, be strong and courageous!” 💪
