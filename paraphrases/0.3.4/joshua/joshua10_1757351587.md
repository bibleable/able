## Joshua 10:1
Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua captured Ai and devoted it to destruction. He treated Ai and its king as he treated Jericho and its king. Adoni-zedek also heard that Gibeon’s people made peace with Israel and lived near them. 📖
## Joshua 10:2
Adoni-zedek and his people were greatly afraid. Gibeon was a great city like a royal city, larger than Ai. All its men were mighty. 📖
## Joshua 10:3
Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem sent a message to Hoham king of Hebron and Piram king of Jarmuth. He also sent it to Japhia king of Lachish and Debir king of Eglon: 👥
## Joshua 10:4
“Help me attack Gibeon, because they made peace with Joshua and the Israelites.” ⚔️
## Joshua 10:5
The five Amorite kings united: the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon. They advanced with all their armies, camped before Gibeon, and attacked it. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:6
Gibeon’s men sent Joshua a message at Gilgal. “Do not abandon your servants,” they said. “Come quickly and save us! All the Amorite kings from the hill country united against us.” 🙏
## Joshua 10:7
Joshua came from Gilgal with his whole army, including all the strong, brave men. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:8
The LORD told Joshua, “Do not fear them. I delivered them into your hand. Not one will stand against you.” 📖
## Joshua 10:9
Joshua marched all night from Gilgal and surprised them. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:10
The LORD confused them before Israel. Israel defeated them in a great slaughter at Gibeon and pursued them up to Beth-horon. They struck them down as far as Azekah and Makkedah. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:11
They fled down from Beth-horon toward Azekah. The LORD threw large hailstones on them from the sky. More died from hailstones than from Israel’s swords. ⚖️
## Joshua 10:12
The day the LORD delivered the Amorites to Israel, Joshua spoke to Him before Israel. “Sun, stand still over Gibeon,” he said. “Moon, stand still over the Valley of Aijalon.” ☀️
## Joshua 10:13
The sun stood still and the moon stopped until the nation took vengeance on its enemies. Is this not written in the Book of Jashar? “The sun stopped in the sky’s middle and delayed setting about a full day.” ☀️
## Joshua 10:14
No day before or since was like it, when the LORD listened to a man’s voice. The LORD fought for Israel. 📖
## Joshua 10:15
Joshua and all Israel returned to camp at Gilgal. 👥
## Joshua 10:16
The five kings had fled into the cave at Makkedah and hidden there. 📖
## Joshua 10:17
Joshua was told, “The five kings are hiding in Makkedah’s cave. They have been found.” 📖
## Joshua 10:18
Joshua said, “Roll large stones against the cave’s entrance. Post men to guard them. 🪨
## Joshua 10:19
Do not stop there. Pursue your enemies and attack from behind. Do not let them reach their cities, because the LORD your God delivered them into your hand.” ⚔️
## Joshua 10:20
Joshua and Israel inflicted a terrible slaughter until they finished them off. The remaining survivors retreated to fortified cities. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:21
The whole army returned safely to Joshua at Makkedah’s camp. No one dared say a word against the Israelites. 👥
## Joshua 10:22
Joshua said, “Open the cave and bring me the five kings.” 👥
## Joshua 10:23
They brought out the five kings: the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon. 👥
## Joshua 10:24
Joshua received the kings and called all Israel’s men. He told the commanders who accompanied him, “Put your feet on these kings’ necks.” They came forward and did so. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:25
Joshua said, “Do not fear or lose courage. Be strong and courageous. The LORD will do this to all enemies you fight.” 💪
## Joshua 10:26
Joshua struck down and killed the kings. He hung their bodies on five trees until evening. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:27
At sunset, he ordered the bodies taken down and thrown into the cave where they hid. Large stones closed the entrance. The stones remain to this day. 🪨
## Joshua 10:28
That day Joshua captured Makkedah and put it and its king to the sword. He devoted everyone to destruction, leaving no survivors. He treated Makkedah’s king as he treated Jericho’s king. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:29
Joshua and all Israel went from Makkedah to Libnah and fought it. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:30
The LORD delivered Libnah and its king to Israel. Joshua put everyone to the sword, leaving no survivors. He treated its king as he treated Jericho’s king. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:31
Joshua and all Israel went from Libnah to Lachish. They surrounded and fought it. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:32
The LORD delivered Lachish to Israel. Joshua captured it on the second day. He put everyone to the sword as he did at Libnah. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:33
Horam king of Gezer came to help Lachish. Joshua struck down him and his people, leaving no survivors. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:34
Joshua and all Israel moved from Lachish to Eglon. They surrounded and fought it. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:35
They captured Eglon that day and put it to the sword. Joshua devoted everyone there to destruction as he did at Lachish. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:36
Joshua and all Israel went from Eglon to Hebron and fought it. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:37
They captured Hebron and put its king, villages, and everyone there to the sword. Joshua left no survivors, as at Eglon. He devoted Hebron and everyone in it to destruction. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:38
Joshua and all Israel turned toward Debir and fought it. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:39
They captured Debir, its king, and villages. They put them to the sword and devoted everyone to destruction, leaving no survivors. Joshua treated Debir and its king as he treated Hebron and Libnah and its king. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:40
Joshua conquered the hill country, Negev, foothills, and slopes, and all their kings, leaving no survivors. He devoted everything that breathed to destruction, as the LORD, the God of Israel, commanded. ⚔️
## Joshua 10:41
He conquered from Kadesh-barnea to Gaza and all Goshen’s region as far as Gibeon. 📖
## Joshua 10:42
Joshua captured all these kings and their land in one campaign because the LORD, the God of Israel, fought for Israel. 📖
## Joshua 10:43
Joshua and all Israel returned to camp at Gilgal. 👥
