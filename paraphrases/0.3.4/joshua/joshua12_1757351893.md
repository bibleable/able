## Joshua 12:1
These are the kings Israel defeated east of the Jordan. Israel took their land from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon, including all the eastern Arabah: 📖
## Joshua 12:2
Sihon king of the Amorites lived in Heshbon. He ruled from Aroer on the Arnon Valley’s rim, along the valley’s middle to the Jabbok River, the Ammonites’ border. This was half of Gilead, 📖
## Joshua 12:3
and the Arabah east of the Sea of Chinnereth to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea. His land extended east through Beth-jeshimoth and south below Pisgah’s slopes. 📖
## Joshua 12:4
Og king of Bashan remained from the Rephaim. He lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei. 📖
## Joshua 12:5
He ruled Mount Hermon, Salecah, and all Bashan to the Geshurites’ and Maacathites’ border. He ruled half of Gilead to the border of Sihon king of Heshbon. 📖
## Joshua 12:6
Moses the LORD’s servant and Israel defeated them. They gave the land to the Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh as an inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 12:7
These are the kings Joshua and Israel defeated west of the Jordan, from Baal-gad in Lebanon’s valley to Mount Halak toward Seir. Joshua gave the land as an inheritance to Israel’s tribes according to their allotments: 📖
## Joshua 12:8
the hill country, foothills, Arabah, slopes, wilderness, and Negev. These were the lands of the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 📖
