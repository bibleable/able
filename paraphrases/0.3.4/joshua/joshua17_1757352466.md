## Joshua 17:1
This was Manasseh’s tribal allotment as Joseph’s firstborn. Machir was Manasseh’s firstborn and father of the Gileadites. He received Gilead and Bashan because he was a man of war. 📖
## Joshua 17:2
The remaining allotment belonged to Manasseh’s clans: Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. These were the other male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph. 📖
## Joshua 17:3
Zelophehad had no sons, only daughters. He was the son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh. His daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 👥
## Joshua 17:4
They approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders. They said, “The LORD commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our brothers.” Joshua obeyed the LORD’s command and gave them an inheritance among their father’s brothers. 📖
## Joshua 17:5
Manasseh received ten shares besides Gilead and Bashan beyond the Jordan, 📖
## Joshua 17:6
because Manasseh’s daughters received an inheritance among his sons. Gilead belonged to the rest of Manasseh’s sons. 📖
## Joshua 17:7
Manasseh’s border ran from Asher to Michmethath near Shechem, then south to include En-tappuah’s inhabitants. 📖
## Joshua 17:8
Tappuah’s region belonged to Manasseh. But Tappuah itself, on Manasseh’s border, belonged to Ephraim. 📖
## Joshua 17:9
The border continued south to the Brook of Kanah. Some cities among Manasseh’s cities belonged to Ephraim. Manasseh’s border ran north of the brook and ended at the Sea. 📖
## Joshua 17:10
Ephraim’s territory lay south, and Manasseh’s north, with the Sea as its border. It adjoined Asher on the north and Issachar on the east. 📖
## Joshua 17:11
Within Issachar and Asher, Manasseh received Beth-shean, Ibleam, Dor, that is, Naphath, Endor, Taanach, and Megiddo. Each had surrounding settlements. 📖
## Joshua 17:12
Manasseh’s descendants could not occupy these cities because the Canaanites insisted on staying in the land. 📖
## Joshua 17:13
When Israel grew stronger, they forced the Canaanites to labor. But they did not drive them out completely. 📖
## Joshua 17:14
Joseph’s sons asked Joshua, “Why did you give us only one inheritance portion? We have many people because the LORD blessed us abundantly.” 💬
## Joshua 17:15
Joshua answered, “If your people are too many for Ephraim’s hill country, clear a forest area for yourselves in the Perizzites’ and Rephaim’s land.” 📖
## Joshua 17:16
They answered, “The hill country is not enough. All the valley’s Canaanites have iron chariots, both in Beth-shean and its towns and in the Valley of Jezreel.” ⚔️
## Joshua 17:17
Joshua told Joseph’s house, Ephraim and Manasseh, “You have many people and great strength. You will have more than one allotment, 📖
## Joshua 17:18
because the hill country will also be yours. Clear its forest, and its farthest limits will be yours. The Canaanites have iron chariots and are strong, but you can drive them out.” 📖
