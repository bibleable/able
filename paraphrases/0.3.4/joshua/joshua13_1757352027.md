## Joshua 13:1
Joshua was very old. The LORD told him, “You are very old, but much land remains to be possessed. 📖
## Joshua 13:2
This land remains: all the Philistines’ and Geshurites’ territory, 📖
## Joshua 13:3
from the Shihor east of Egypt to Ekron’s territory in the north, considered Canaanite land. It includes the five Philistine rulers’ lands: Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron, and the Avvites’ land. 📖
## Joshua 13:4
To the south is all the Canaanites’ land, from Mearah of the Sidonians to Aphek, as far as the Amorites’ border. 📖
## Joshua 13:5
Also the Gebalites’ land and all Lebanon to the east, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath. 📖
## Joshua 13:6
I Myself will drive out all the Sidonians in the hills from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim before Israel. Divide it by lot as Israel’s inheritance, as I commanded. 📖
## Joshua 13:7
Divide this land among the nine tribes and Manasseh’s half-tribe as their inheritance.” 📖
## Joshua 13:8
Manasseh’s other half, the Reubenites, and the Gadites received their inheritance east of the Jordan. Moses the LORD’s servant had assigned it to them: 📖
## Joshua 13:9
from Aroer on the Arnon Valley’s rim, including the city in the valley’s middle and all Medeba’s plateau to Dibon, 📖
## Joshua 13:10
and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon, as far as the Ammonites’ border. 📖
## Joshua 13:11
Also Gilead, the Geshurites’ and Maacathites’ territory, all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan to Salecah, 📖
## Joshua 13:12
the whole kingdom of Og in Bashan. He ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei and was one of the remaining Rephaim. Moses defeated and dispossessed them, 📖
## Joshua 13:13
but Israel did not drive out the Geshurites or Maacathites. Geshur and Maacath live among the Israelites to this day. 📖
## Joshua 13:14
Moses gave Levi’s tribe no land inheritance. Their inheritance is the food offerings to the LORD, the God of Israel, as He promised them. 📖
## Joshua 13:15
Moses gave Reuben’s tribal clans this territory: 📖
## Joshua 13:16
from Aroer on the Arnon Valley’s rim, including the city in the valley’s middle, to the whole plateau beyond Medeba, 📖
## Joshua 13:17
to Heshbon and all its plateau cities, including Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon, 📖
## Joshua 13:18
Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath, 📖
## Joshua 13:19
Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley, 📖
## Joshua 13:20
Beth-peor, Pisgah’s slopes, and Beth-jeshimoth. 📖
## Joshua 13:21
This included all the plateau’s cities and Sihon’s whole kingdom. Sihon king of the Amorites ruled Heshbon until Moses killed him and Midian’s chiefs. Those chiefs were Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, Sihon’s princes who lived in the land. 📖
## Joshua 13:22
Israel also killed Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, with the others they put to the sword. ⚔️
## Joshua 13:23
The Reubenites’ border was the Jordan’s bank. This was their clans’ inheritance, including the cities and villages. 📖
## Joshua 13:24
Moses gave Gad’s tribal clans this territory: 📖
## Joshua 13:25
Jazer, all Gilead’s cities, and half the Ammonites’ land to Aroer near Rabbah, 📖
## Joshua 13:26
from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to Debir’s border. 📖
## Joshua 13:27
In the valley were Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, with the rest of Sihon king of Heshbon’s kingdom. It lay east of the Jordan up to the Sea of Chinnereth’s edge. 📖
## Joshua 13:28
This was the Gadites’ clans’ inheritance, including the cities and villages. 📖
## Joshua 13:29
Moses gave this to the clans of Manasseh’s half-tribe, half of Manasseh’s descendants: 📖
## Joshua 13:30
from Mahanaim through all Bashan, all Og king of Bashan’s kingdom, including Jair’s towns in Bashan, sixty cities, 📖
## Joshua 13:31
half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, Og’s royal cities in Bashan. This was for the clans of Machir son of Manasseh’s descendants, half of Machir’s descendants. 📖
## Joshua 13:32
Moses gave these portions on Moab’s plains beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho. 📖
## Joshua 13:33
Moses gave Levi’s tribe no land inheritance. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, as He promised them. 📖
