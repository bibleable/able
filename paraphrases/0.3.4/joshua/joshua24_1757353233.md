## Joshua 24:1
Joshua gathered all Israel’s tribes at Shechem. He called Israel’s elders, leaders, judges, and officers. They presented themselves before God. 👥
## Joshua 24:2
Joshua told the people, “The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘Long ago your fathers lived beyond the Euphrates and worshiped other gods. They included Terah, the father of Abraham and Nahor. 📖
## Joshua 24:3
I took your father Abraham from beyond the Euphrates and led him through all Canaan. I increased his descendants and gave him Isaac, 📖
## Joshua 24:4
and gave Isaac Jacob and Esau. I gave Esau Mount Seir to possess, but Jacob and his sons went to Egypt. 📖
## Joshua 24:5
I sent Moses and Aaron and afflicted the Egyptians by what I did there. Then I brought you out. 📖
## Joshua 24:6
I brought your fathers out of Egypt, and you reached the Red Sea. The Egyptians pursued them with chariots and horsemen to the Red Sea. 📖
## Joshua 24:7
Your fathers cried to the LORD. He put darkness between you and the Egyptians, and brought the sea over them to engulf them. Your own eyes saw what I did to the Egyptians. Then you lived in the wilderness a long time. 📖
## Joshua 24:8
I brought you to the Amorites’ land beyond the Jordan. They fought you, but I delivered them into your hand. You could possess their land when I destroyed them before you. 📖
## Joshua 24:9
Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, set out to fight Israel. He sent for Balaam son of Beor to curse you, 📖
## Joshua 24:10
but I would not listen to Balaam. He blessed you repeatedly, and I delivered you from his hand. 📖
## Joshua 24:11
You crossed the Jordan and reached Jericho. Its people fought you, as did the Amorites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hittites, Girgashites, Hivites, and Jebusites. I delivered them into your hand. 📖
## Joshua 24:12
I sent the hornet ahead of you. It drove out the two Amorite kings before you, not by your sword or bow. 📖
## Joshua 24:13
I gave you land you did not cultivate and cities you did not build. You live there and eat from vineyards and olive groves you did not plant.’ 📖
## Joshua 24:14
Fear the LORD and serve Him sincerely and truly. Reject the gods your fathers served beyond the Euphrates and in Egypt. Serve the LORD. 🙏
## Joshua 24:15
If serving the LORD displeases you, choose today whom you will serve. Will it be the gods your fathers served beyond the Euphrates or the Amorites’ gods in whose land you live? But I and my house will serve the LORD!” 🙏
## Joshua 24:16
The people answered, “We would never abandon the LORD to serve other gods! 📖
## Joshua 24:17
The LORD our God brought us and our fathers from Egypt, from slavery’s house. We saw His great signs. He protected us on our whole journey and among all the nations we traveled through. 📖
## Joshua 24:18
He drove out all the nations before us, including the Amorites living here. We will serve the LORD too, because He is our God!” 🙏
## Joshua 24:19
Joshua answered, “You cannot serve the LORD, because He is holy and jealous. He will not forgive your rebellion or sins. ⚠️
## Joshua 24:20
If you abandon the LORD and serve foreign gods, He will turn and bring disaster on you. He will consume you even after being good to you.” ⚖️
## Joshua 24:21
The people answered, “No! We will serve the LORD!” 🙏
## Joshua 24:22
Joshua said, “You witness against yourselves that you chose to serve the LORD.” They answered, “We are witnesses!” 📖
## Joshua 24:23
He said, “Remove the foreign gods among you. Turn your hearts toward the LORD, the God of Israel.” ❤️
## Joshua 24:24
The people told Joshua, “We will serve the LORD our God and obey His voice.” 🙏
## Joshua 24:25
Joshua made a covenant for the people that day. At Shechem he established a statute and ordinance for them. 📖
## Joshua 24:26
He recorded these things in the Book of the Law of God. He set a large stone under the oak near the LORD’s sanctuary. 🪨
## Joshua 24:27
Joshua told the people, “This stone will witness against us. It heard every word the LORD spoke to us. It will witness against you if you deny your God.” 🪨
## Joshua 24:28
Joshua sent the people away, each to his inheritance. 👥
## Joshua 24:29
Later, Joshua son of Nun, the LORD’s servant, died at 110 years old. 🪦
## Joshua 24:30
They buried him on his inherited land at Timnath-serah in Ephraim’s hills, north of Mount Gaash. 🪦
## Joshua 24:31
Israel served the LORD throughout Joshua’s life and the lives of the elders who outlived him. They experienced all the LORD’s works for Israel. 📖
## Joshua 24:32
Israel brought Joseph’s bones from Egypt and buried them at Shechem. Jacob bought the plot from the sons of Hamor, Shechem’s father, for a hundred pieces of silver. It became Joseph’s descendants’ inheritance. 🪦
## Joshua 24:33
Eleazar son of Aaron also died. They buried him at Gibeah, in Ephraim’s hills, which had been given to his son Phinehas. 🪦
