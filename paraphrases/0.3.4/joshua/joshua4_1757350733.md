## Joshua 4:1
The whole nation finished crossing the Jordan. The LORD told Joshua, 📖
## Joshua 4:2
“Choose twelve men, one from each tribe, 👥
## Joshua 4:3
and tell them to take twelve stones from the Jordan’s middle, where the priests stood. Carry them to the place where you spend the night and set them down there.” 🪨
## Joshua 4:4
Joshua called the twelve Israelites he appointed, one from each tribe. 👥
## Joshua 4:5
He said, “Go before the ark of the LORD your God into the Jordan’s middle. Each man must carry a stone on his shoulder, one for each of Israel’s tribes. 🪨
## Joshua 4:6
These will be a sign among you. In the future, your children will ask, ‘What do these stones mean to you?’ ❓
## Joshua 4:7
Tell them, ‘The Jordan’s waters stopped before the ark of the LORD’s covenant. They stopped when it crossed the Jordan.’ These stones will be a memorial to the Israelites forever.” 🪨
## Joshua 4:8
The Israelites obeyed Joshua. They took twelve stones from the Jordan’s middle, one for each tribe, as the LORD told Joshua. They carried them to camp and set them down. 🪨
## Joshua 4:9
Joshua also set twelve stones in the Jordan’s middle, where the priests carrying the covenant ark stood. They remain there to this day. 🪨
## Joshua 4:10
The priests carrying the ark stayed in the Jordan’s middle until the people completed all the LORD’s commands through Joshua. This was as Moses directed Joshua. The people hurried across, 👥
## Joshua 4:11
and after everyone crossed, the priests carrying the LORD’s ark crossed in the people’s sight. 👥
## Joshua 4:12
The Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh crossed ahead of the Israelites, armed for battle as Moses commanded. ⚔️
## Joshua 4:13
About 40,000 armed troops crossed before the LORD onto Jericho’s plains. ⚔️
## Joshua 4:14
That day the LORD honored Joshua before all Israel. They revered him throughout his life as they revered Moses. 📖
## Joshua 4:15
The LORD told Joshua, 📖
## Joshua 4:16
“Command the priests carrying the ark of the Testimony to come out of the Jordan.” 📖
## Joshua 4:17
Joshua told the priests, “Come out of the Jordan.” 👥
## Joshua 4:18
The priests carrying the ark of the LORD’s covenant left the Jordan. When their feet touched dry land, the water returned to its course and overflowed the banks as before. 📖
## Joshua 4:19
On the first month’s tenth day, the people left the Jordan and camped at Gilgal on Jericho’s eastern border. 📖
## Joshua 4:20
Joshua set up the twelve stones from the Jordan at Gilgal. 🪨
## Joshua 4:21
He told the Israelites, “In the future, when your children ask their fathers what these stones mean, ❓
## Joshua 4:22
tell them, ‘Israel crossed the Jordan on dry ground.’ 📖
## Joshua 4:23
The LORD your God dried the Jordan before you until you crossed. He did the same at the Red Sea, drying it before us until we crossed. 📖
## Joshua 4:24
He did this so all earth’s peoples may know His hand is mighty. He did it so you may always fear the LORD your God.” 🙏
