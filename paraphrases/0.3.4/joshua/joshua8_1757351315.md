## Joshua 8:1
The LORD told Joshua, “Do not fear or lose courage. Take the whole army and attack Ai. I delivered its king, people, city, and land into your hand. 📖
## Joshua 8:2
Do to Ai and its king what you did to Jericho and its king. But you may keep its plunder and livestock. Set an ambush behind the city.” ⚔️
## Joshua 8:3
Joshua and the whole army set out against Ai. He chose 30,000 strong, brave men and sent them out at night, ⚔️
## Joshua 8:4
commanding, “Pay attention. Hide in ambush behind the city, not far away. All of you must be ready. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:5
I and my troops will approach the city. When they attack as before, we will flee. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:6
They will pursue us, saying, ‘Israel runs away as before.’ We will draw them away from the city. As we flee, ⚔️
## Joshua 8:7
rise from your ambush and capture the city. The LORD your God will deliver it into your hand. 📖
## Joshua 8:8
After you capture it, burn it. Obey the LORD’s command! I have given you your orders.” 🔥
## Joshua 8:9
Joshua sent them to hide between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai. He spent the night among the people. 📖
## Joshua 8:10
Early the next morning, Joshua gathered his men. He and Israel’s elders marched ahead of them to Ai. 👥
## Joshua 8:11
His troops approached Ai and camped north of it, with the valley between them and the city. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:12
Joshua had placed about five thousand men in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the city. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:13
The main camp was north of the city, and the rear guard west of it. That night Joshua entered the valley. 📖
## Joshua 8:14
Ai’s king saw the Israelites and hurried out early with the city’s men. They went to battle at an appointed place overlooking the Arabah. He did not know about the ambush behind the city. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:15
Joshua and all Israel let themselves be driven back. They fled toward the wilderness. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:16
All Ai’s men were called to pursue them. They followed Joshua and were drawn away from the city. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:17
Every man in Ai and Bethel went after Israel. They left the city wide open. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:18
The LORD told Joshua, “Hold your battle lance toward Ai. I will deliver the city into your hand.” Joshua held his battle lance toward Ai, 📖
## Joshua 8:19
and immediately the ambush rose. The men rushed into the city, captured it, and immediately burned it. 🔥
## Joshua 8:20
Ai’s men looked back and saw smoke rising from the city. They could not escape in any direction. The troops fleeing toward the wilderness turned against their pursuers. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:21
Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush captured the city and smoke rose from it. They turned and struck down Ai’s men. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:22
The men in ambush came from the city to attack them. Ai’s men were trapped between Israel’s forces on both sides. Israel killed them, leaving no survivor or fugitive. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:23
They captured Ai’s king alive and brought him to Joshua. 👥
## Joshua 8:24
Israel killed every man of Ai who pursued them into the field and wilderness. Every one fell by the sword. Then Israel returned to Ai and put it to the sword too. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:25
Twelve thousand men and women died that day, all Ai’s people. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:26
Joshua kept his battle lance extended until he devoted all Ai’s inhabitants to destruction. ⚔️
## Joshua 8:27
Israel kept only the cattle and plunder, as the LORD commanded Joshua. 📖
## Joshua 8:28
Joshua burned Ai into a permanent heap of ruins. It remains desolate to this day. 🔥
## Joshua 8:29
He hung Ai’s king on a tree until evening. At sunset, Joshua ordered the body taken down and thrown at the city gate’s entrance. They piled rocks over it, which remain to this day. 🪨
## Joshua 8:30
Joshua built an altar on Mount Ebal to the LORD, the God of Israel, 🪨
## Joshua 8:31
as Moses the LORD’s servant commanded Israel. He followed the Book of the Law of Moses, using uncut stones that no iron tool had touched. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings to the LORD there. 🔥
## Joshua 8:32
Before the Israelites, Joshua wrote a copy of Moses’ law on the stones. 📜
## Joshua 8:33
All Israel, foreigners and citizens, stood on both sides of the LORD’s covenant ark with their elders, officers, and judges. They faced the Levitical priests carrying it. Half stood before Mount Gerizim and half before Mount Ebal. Moses the LORD’s servant had commanded this earlier to bless Israel. 👥
## Joshua 8:34
Joshua read all the law aloud, the blessings and curses, as written in the Book of the Law. 📖
## Joshua 8:35
He read every word Moses commanded before the whole assembly of Israel. Women, little children, and foreigners living among them were included. 👥
