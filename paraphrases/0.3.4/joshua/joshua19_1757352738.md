## Joshua 19:1
The second lot belonged to Simeon’s tribal clans. Their inheritance lay within Judah’s territory, 📖
## Joshua 19:2
including Beersheba, or Sheba, Moladah, 📖
## Joshua 19:3
Hazar-shual, Balah, Ezem, 📖
## Joshua 19:4
Eltolad, Bethul, Hormah, 📖
## Joshua 19:5
Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susah, 📖
## Joshua 19:6
Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen. There were thirteen cities with their villages. 📖
## Joshua 19:7
Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan were four cities with their villages, 📖
## Joshua 19:8
and all villages around these cities to Baalath-beer, Ramah of the Negev. This was Simeon’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 19:9
Simeon’s inheritance came from Judah’s territory because Judah’s share was too large for them. The Simeonites inherited land within Judah’s portion. 📖
## Joshua 19:10
The third lot belonged to Zebulun’s tribal clans. Their inheritance border reached Sarid. 📖
## Joshua 19:11
It went west to Maralah and Dabbesheth and met the brook east of Jokneam. 📖
## Joshua 19:12
From Sarid it turned east along Chisloth-tabor’s border, continued to Daberath, and rose to Japhia. 📖
## Joshua 19:13
It crossed east to Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin, extended to Rimmon, and curved toward Neah. 📖
## Joshua 19:14
It circled Neah’s north side to Hannathon and ended at the Valley of Iphtah-el. 📖
## Joshua 19:15
It included Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem. There were twelve cities with their villages. 📖
## Joshua 19:16
These cities and villages were Zebulun’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 19:17
The fourth lot belonged to Issachar’s tribal clans. 📖
## Joshua 19:18
Their territory included Jezreel, Chesulloth, Shunem, 📖
## Joshua 19:19
Hapharaim, Shion, Anaharath, 📖
## Joshua 19:20
Rabbith, Kishion, Ebez, 📖
## Joshua 19:21
Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez. 📖
## Joshua 19:22
The border reached Tabor, Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh and ended at the Jordan. There were sixteen cities with their villages. 📖
## Joshua 19:23
These cities and villages were Issachar’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 19:24
The fifth lot belonged to Asher’s tribal clans. 📖
## Joshua 19:25
Their territory included Helkath, Hali, Beten, Achshaph, 📖
## Joshua 19:26
Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal. On the west, the border touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath, 📖
## Joshua 19:27
then turned east toward Beth-dagon and touched Zebulun and the Valley of Iphtah-el. It went north to Beth-emek and Neiel, passing Cabul on the left. 📖
## Joshua 19:28
It reached Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah, as far as Greater Sidon. 📖
## Joshua 19:29
It turned back toward Ramah to the fortified city of Tyre, then toward Hosah. It ended at the Sea in the region of Achzib, 📖
## Joshua 19:30
Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob. There were twenty-two cities with their villages. 📖
## Joshua 19:31
These cities and villages were Asher’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 19:32
The sixth lot belonged to Naphtali’s tribal clans. 📖
## Joshua 19:33
Their border began at Heleph and Zaanannim’s great tree. It passed Adami-nekeb and Jabneel, reached Lakkum, and ended at the Jordan. 📖
## Joshua 19:34
It turned west to Aznoth-tabor and Hukkok. It touched Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan on the east. 📖
## Joshua 19:35
The fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, Chinnereth, 📖
## Joshua 19:36
Adamah, Ramah, Hazor, 📖
## Joshua 19:37
Kedesh, Edrei, En-hazor, 📖
## Joshua 19:38
Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh. There were nineteen cities with their villages. 📖
## Joshua 19:39
These cities and villages were Naphtali’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 19:40
The seventh lot belonged to Dan’s tribal clans. 📖
## Joshua 19:41
Their inheritance included Zorah, Eshtaol, Ir-shemesh, 📖
## Joshua 19:42
Shaalabbin, Aijalon, Ithlah, 📖
## Joshua 19:43
Elon, Timnah, Ekron, 📖
## Joshua 19:44
Eltekeh, Gibbethon, Baalath, 📖
## Joshua 19:45
Jehud, Bene-berak, Gath-rimmon, 📖
## Joshua 19:46
Me-jarkon, and Rakkon, including the territory across from Joppa. 📖
## Joshua 19:47
Later, the Danites lost their territory. They attacked Leshem, captured it, and put it to the sword. They occupied it, settled there, and renamed it Dan after their father. ⚔️
## Joshua 19:48
These cities and villages were Dan’s tribal clans’ inheritance. 📖
## Joshua 19:49
After dividing the land into territories, Israel gave Joshua son of Nun an inheritance among them, 📖
## Joshua 19:50
as the LORD commanded. They gave him Timnath-serah in Ephraim’s hill country, as he requested. He rebuilt the city and settled there. 📖
## Joshua 19:51
Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the family heads distributed these inheritances by lot to Israel’s tribes. They did this at Shiloh before the LORD, at the Tent of Meeting’s entrance. They finished dividing the land. 📖
