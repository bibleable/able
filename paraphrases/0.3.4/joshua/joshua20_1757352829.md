## Joshua 20:1
The LORD told Joshua, 📖
## Joshua 20:2
“Tell Israel to designate cities of refuge, as I commanded through Moses, 📖
## Joshua 20:3
so anyone who kills another unintentionally or accidentally may flee there. These cities will shelter you from the avenger of blood. 📖
## Joshua 20:4
The person fleeing there must stand at the city gate and state his case before the elders. They must bring him into the city and give him a place to live among them. 📖
## Joshua 20:5
If the avenger of blood pursues him, they must not surrender the manslayer. He killed his neighbor accidentally without prior malice. 📖
## Joshua 20:6
He must stay until he stands trial before the assembly and the high priest serving at that time dies. Then he may return to his own home in the city he fled.” 📖
## Joshua 20:7
They set apart Kedesh in Galilee, in Naphtali’s hills, and Shechem in Ephraim’s hills. They also chose Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in Judah’s hills. 📖
## Joshua 20:8
Beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho, they chose Bezer on the wilderness plateau from Reuben’s tribe. They chose Ramoth in Gilead from Gad’s tribe and Golan in Bashan from Manasseh’s tribe. 📖
## Joshua 20:9
These cities served all Israelites and foreigners among them who killed unintentionally. They could flee there and avoid death by the avenger of blood before standing trial before the assembly. 📖
