## Joshua 3:1
Early the next morning, Joshua and all the Israelites left Shittim. They reached the Jordan and camped before crossing. 📖
## Joshua 3:2
After three days, the officers went through the camp, 👥
## Joshua 3:3
and commanded, “When you see the Levitical priests carrying the ark of the LORD your God’s covenant, leave your positions and follow it. 📖
## Joshua 3:4
Stay about two thousand cubits from the ark. Do not approach it, so you can see the way. You have never traveled this way before.” 📖
## Joshua 3:5
Joshua told the people, “Set yourselves apart as holy. Tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you.” 🙏
## Joshua 3:6
He told the priests, “Carry the ark of the covenant ahead of the people.” They took it and went ahead. 👥
## Joshua 3:7
The LORD told Joshua, “Today I will begin to honor you before all Israel. This is so they may know I am with you as I was with Moses. 📖
## Joshua 3:8
Tell the priests carrying the covenant ark, ‘When you reach the water’s edge, stand in the Jordan.’” 📖
## Joshua 3:9
Joshua told the Israelites, “Come and hear the LORD your God’s words. 📖
## Joshua 3:10
This is how you will know the living God is among you. He will drive out the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites before you. 📖
## Joshua 3:11
The covenant ark of the Lord of all the earth will go ahead of you into the Jordan. 📖
## Joshua 3:12
Choose twelve men from Israel’s tribes, one from each tribe. 👥
## Joshua 3:13
When the priests’ feet touch the Jordan’s water, its flow will stop and stand in a heap. They carry the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth.” 📖
## Joshua 3:14
The people left camp to cross the Jordan. The priests carried the covenant ark ahead of them. 👥
## Joshua 3:15
The Jordan overflows its banks throughout harvest. As soon as the priests carrying the ark reached the river and their feet touched its edge, 📖
## Joshua 3:16
and the flowing water stopped. It backed up far upstream to Adam, near Zarethan. The flow toward the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, was completely cut off. The people crossed opposite Jericho. 📖
## Joshua 3:17
The priests carrying the ark of the LORD’s covenant stood firmly on dry ground in the Jordan’s middle. All Israel crossed on dry ground until the whole nation was across. 👥
