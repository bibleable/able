## Luke 13:1
Some people told Jesus about Galileans whose blood Pilate mixed with their sacrifices. 📖
## Luke 13:2
He asked, “Were these Galileans worse sinners than all other Galileans because they suffered this way? ❓
## Luke 13:3
No! Unless you repent, you will all perish too. 📖
## Luke 13:4
Eighteen people died when Siloam’s tower fell on them. Were they more sinful than everyone else in Jerusalem? ❓
## Luke 13:5
No! Unless you repent, you will all perish too.” 📖
## Luke 13:6
Jesus told a parable: “A man had a fig tree in his vineyard. He looked for fruit but found none. 🌳
## Luke 13:7
He told the keeper, ‘For three years I have found no fruit on this tree. Cut it down! Why should it use the soil?’ 📖
## Luke 13:8
The keeper replied, ‘Sir, leave it this year. Let me dig around it and fertilize it. 📖
## Luke 13:9
If it bears fruit next year, good. Otherwise, you can cut it down.’” 🌳
## Luke 13:10
Jesus taught in a synagogue one Sabbath. 📖
## Luke 13:11
A spirit had disabled a woman there for eighteen years. She was bent over and could not stand straight. 📖
## Luke 13:12
Jesus saw her and called her over. He said, “Woman, you are freed from your disability.” ❤️
## Luke 13:13
He laid His hands on her. Immediately she stood straight and glorified God. 🙏
## Luke 13:14
The synagogue leader was angry that Jesus healed on the Sabbath. He told the crowd, “Six days are for work. Come for healing on those days, not the Sabbath.” 📖
## Luke 13:15
The Lord answered, “Hypocrites! On the Sabbath, does each of you not untie his ox or donkey and lead it from the stall to water? ❓
## Luke 13:16
Satan bound this daughter of Abraham for eighteen long years. Should she not be freed on the Sabbath?” 📖
## Luke 13:17
His words humiliated all His opponents. The whole crowd rejoiced at His glorious deeds. 🙏
## Luke 13:18
Jesus asked, “What is the kingdom of God like? What can I compare it to? ❓
## Luke 13:19
It is like a mustard seed a man tossed into his garden. It grew into a tree, and birds nested in its branches.” 🌳
## Luke 13:20
He asked again, “What can I compare the kingdom of God to? ❓
## Luke 13:21
It is like leaven a woman mixed into three measures of flour until all the flour was leavened.” 📖
## Luke 13:22
Jesus taught in towns and villages as He traveled toward Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 13:23
Someone asked, “Lord, will only a few people be saved?” He answered, ❓
## Luke 13:24
“Try hard to enter through the narrow door. Many will try but will be unable. 📖
## Luke 13:25
The house’s master will rise and shut the door. You will stand outside, knocking and saying, ‘Lord, open for us.’ He will reply, ‘I do not know where you are from.’ 📖
## Luke 13:26
You will say, ‘We ate and drank with you. You taught in our streets.’ 📖
## Luke 13:27
He will answer, ‘I do not know where you are from. Leave me, all you evildoers!’ 📖
## Luke 13:28
You will weep and grind your teeth when you see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all the prophets in God’s kingdom. You yourselves will be thrown out. 📖
## Luke 13:29
People will come from east, west, north, and south to recline at the table in the kingdom of God. 👥
## Luke 13:30
Some who are last will be first. Some who are first will be last.” 📖
## Luke 13:31
That hour some Pharisees said, “Leave here. Herod wants to kill You.” 📖
## Luke 13:32
He answered, “Tell that fox, ‘I will keep driving out demons and healing today and tomorrow. On the third day I will reach My goal.’ 📖
## Luke 13:33
I must travel today, tomorrow, and the next day. It is not admissible for a prophet to perish outside Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 13:34
Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you kill prophets and stone those sent to you! I often longed to gather your children as a hen gathers chicks under her wings. But you were unwilling! 💔
## Luke 13:35
Your house is left desolate. You will not see Me again until you say, ‘Blessed is He who comes in the Lord’s name.’” 📖
