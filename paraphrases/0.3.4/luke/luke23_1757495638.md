## Luke 23:1
The whole council rose and took Jesus to Pilate. 📖
## Luke 23:2
They accused Him: “He undermines our nation, forbids paying Caesar’s taxes, and calls Himself Christ, a King.” 📖
## Luke 23:3
Pilate asked, “Are You the King of the Jews?” Jesus answered, “You have said so.” 📖
## Luke 23:4
Pilate told the chief priests and crowds, “I find no basis to charge this man.” 📖
## Luke 23:5
They insisted, “His teaching stirs people throughout Judea. He began in Galilee and came here.” 📖
## Luke 23:6
Pilate asked whether He was a Galilean. ❓
## Luke 23:7
He learned Jesus was under Herod’s authority and sent Him to Herod, who was also in Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 23:8
Herod was delighted to see Jesus. He had long wanted this because he heard about Him and hoped to see a miracle. 📖
## Luke 23:9
Herod questioned Him at length, but Jesus gave no answer. 📖
## Luke 23:10
The chief priests and scribes stood there, fiercely accusing Him. 📖
## Luke 23:11
Herod and his soldiers ridiculed and mocked Him. They dressed Him in a fine robe and sent Him to Pilate. 📖
## Luke 23:12
Herod and Pilate became friends that day. Previously they were enemies. 📖
## Luke 23:13
Pilate gathered the chief priests, rulers, and people. 👥
## Luke 23:14
He said, “You accuse this man of inciting rebellion. I examined Him before you and found Him innocent of your charges. 📖
## Luke 23:15
Herod found no guilt either, for he sent Him back. He did nothing deserving death. 📖
## Luke 23:16
I will therefore punish and release Him.” 📖
## Luke 23:17

## Luke 23:18
Everyone shouted together, “Away with this man! Release Barabbas!” 📖
## Luke 23:19
Barabbas was imprisoned for rebellion in the city and murder. 📖
## Luke 23:20
Pilate wanted to release Jesus and addressed them again. 📖
## Luke 23:21
They kept shouting, “Crucify Him! Crucify Him!” 📖
## Luke 23:22
He asked a third time, “What evil did He do? I found no offense deserving death. I will punish and release Him.” 📖
## Luke 23:23
They loudly insisted on His crucifixion, and their shouting prevailed. 📖
## Luke 23:24
Pilate ruled that their demand be granted. 📖
## Luke 23:25
He released the man imprisoned for rebellion and murder as requested. He handed Jesus over to their will. 📖
## Luke 23:26
The soldiers led Jesus away and seized Simon of Cyrene, coming from the countryside. They placed the cross on him to carry behind Jesus. 📖
## Luke 23:27
A large crowd followed, including women mourning and wailing for Him. 💔
## Luke 23:28
Jesus turned and said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, weep for yourselves and your children rather than for Me. 📖
## Luke 23:29
Days will come when people say, ‘Blessed are barren women, wombs that never bore children, and breasts that never nursed!’ 📖
## Luke 23:30
They will tell mountains, ‘Fall on us!’ and hills, ‘Cover us!’ 📖
## Luke 23:31
If people do this while the tree is green, what will happen when it is dry?” ❓
## Luke 23:32
Two criminals were also led away to be executed with Jesus. 📖
## Luke 23:33
At the place called The Skull, they crucified Him and the criminals, one on His right and one on His left. 📖
## Luke 23:34
Jesus said, “Father, forgive them because they do not know what they do.” They cast lots to divide His clothes. 🙏
## Luke 23:35
The people watched. The rulers sneered, “He saved others. Let Him save Himself if He is God’s Christ, the Chosen One.” 📖
## Luke 23:36
Soldiers mocked Him and offered sour wine. 📖
## Luke 23:37
They said, “Save Yourself if You are the King of the Jews!” 📖
## Luke 23:38
The inscription above Him read: THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS. 📖
## Luke 23:39
One criminal hanging there abused Him. “Are You not the Christ? Save Yourself and us!” 📖
## Luke 23:40
The other rebuked him: “Do you not fear God? You are under the same judgment. 📖
## Luke 23:41
Our punishment is just because our deeds deserve it. He did nothing wrong.” 📖
## Luke 23:42
He said, “Jesus, remember me when You come into Your kingdom.” 🙏
## Luke 23:43
Jesus answered, “Truly, today you will be with Me in Paradise.” ❤️
## Luke 23:44
About the sixth hour, darkness covered all the land until the ninth hour. 📖
## Luke 23:45
The sun was darkened. The temple veil tore down the middle. 📖
## Luke 23:46
Jesus cried loudly, “Father, into Your hands I commit My Spirit.” Then He breathed His last. 💔
## Luke 23:47
The centurion saw this and glorified God. He said, “Certainly this man was righteous.” 🙏
## Luke 23:48
Everyone gathered to watch saw what happened and returned home beating their chests. 💔
## Luke 23:49
All who knew Jesus watched from a distance, including women who followed Him from Galilee. 👥
## Luke 23:50
Joseph, a Council member, was good and righteous. 📖
## Luke 23:51
He had not agreed to their decision or action. He came from Arimathea in Judea and awaited the kingdom of God. 📖
## Luke 23:52
He asked Pilate for Jesus’ body. 📖
## Luke 23:53
He took it down, wrapped it in linen, and placed it in a rock-cut tomb. No one had been laid there before. 📖
## Luke 23:54
It was Preparation Day, and the Sabbath was beginning. 📖
## Luke 23:55
The women from Galilee followed and saw the tomb and how Jesus’ body was placed. 📖
## Luke 23:56
They returned and prepared spices and perfumes. They rested on the Sabbath as commanded. 📖
