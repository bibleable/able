## Luke 2:1
Caesar Augustus ordered a census of the whole empire. 📖
## Luke 2:2
This was the first census while Quirinius governed Syria. 📖
## Luke 2:3
Everyone went to his own town to register. 👥
## Luke 2:4
Joseph went from Nazareth in Galilee to Bethlehem, David’s city in Judea. He belonged to David’s house and family line. 📖
## Luke 2:5
He went to register with Mary, his pledged bride, who was pregnant. 👥
## Luke 2:6
While there, her Child’s time to be born came. 👶
## Luke 2:7
She bore her firstborn, a Son. She wrapped Him in swaddling cloths and laid Him in a manger because the inn had no room for them. 👶
## Luke 2:8
Shepherds lived in nearby fields, watching their flocks at night. 📖
## Luke 2:9
The Lord’s angel stood before them, and His glory shone around them. They were terrified. 👼
## Luke 2:10
The angel said, “Do not fear! I bring good news of great joy for all the people. 📖
## Luke 2:11
Today a Savior was born for you in David’s city. He is Christ the Lord! 👶
## Luke 2:12
This is your sign: a baby wrapped in swaddling cloths, lying in a manger.” 👶
## Luke 2:13
A great heavenly host suddenly joined the angel, praising God and saying, 👼
## Luke 2:14
“Glory to God in the highest! Peace on earth to people on whom His favor rests!” 🙏
## Luke 2:15
The angels returned to heaven. The shepherds said, “Let us go to Bethlehem and see what happened, which the Lord told us.” 📖
## Luke 2:16
They hurried and found Mary, Joseph, and the Baby lying in the manger. 👥
## Luke 2:17
After seeing Him, they spread the message they received about the Child. 📖
## Luke 2:18
Everyone who heard was amazed at the shepherds’ words. 📖
## Luke 2:19
Mary treasured these things and considered them in her heart. ❤️
## Luke 2:20
The shepherds returned, praising and glorifying God. Everything they heard and saw was as the angel told them. 🙏
## Luke 2:21
After the eight days before His circumcision passed, He was named Jesus. The angel gave this name before He was conceived. 📖
## Luke 2:22
After the purification time required by Moses’ Law, His parents brought Him to Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord, 📖
## Luke 2:23
as the Lord’s Law says, “Every firstborn male must be set apart for the Lord,” 📖
## Luke 2:24
and to offer its required sacrifice, “A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons.” 📖
## Luke 2:25
Simeon lived in Jerusalem, righteous and devout, awaiting Israel’s consolation. The Holy Spirit was upon him. 📖
## Luke 2:26
The Holy Spirit told him he would not die before seeing the Lord’s Christ. 📖
## Luke 2:27
The Spirit led him into the temple courts. The parents brought the child Jesus to fulfill the Law’s custom, 📖
## Luke 2:28
and Simeon took Him in his arms and blessed God. He said, 🙏
## Luke 2:29
“Sovereign Lord, as You promised, You now dismiss Your servant in peace. 🙏
## Luke 2:30
My eyes have seen Your salvation, 📖
## Luke 2:31
which You prepared before all people, 📖
## Luke 2:32
a light revealing to the Gentiles and bringing glory to Your people Israel.” ✨
## Luke 2:33
The Child’s father and mother marveled at these words about Him. 👥
## Luke 2:34
Simeon blessed them and told Mary, “This Child is appointed for the rise and fall of many in Israel. He will be a sign spoken against, 📖
## Luke 2:35
so many hearts’ thoughts will be revealed. A sword will pierce your own soul too.” 📖
## Luke 2:36
Anna, a prophetess, was Phanuel’s daughter from Asher’s tribe. She was old and had been married seven years, 👥
## Luke 2:37
and was now a widow, eighty-four years old. She never left the temple, worshiping night and day with fasting and prayer. 🙏
## Luke 2:38
She came forward then, thanked God, and spoke about the Child to everyone waiting for Jerusalem’s redemption. 📖
## Luke 2:39
His parents fulfilled the Lord’s Law and returned to their town, Nazareth in Galilee. 👥
## Luke 2:40
The Child grew and became strong. He was filled with wisdom, and God’s grace was upon Him. 📖
## Luke 2:41
His parents went to Jerusalem each year for the Passover Feast. 📖
## Luke 2:42
At twelve years old, He went with them according to the Feast’s custom. 👥
## Luke 2:43
When they returned home after the Feast, Jesus stayed in Jerusalem. His parents did not know. 📖
## Luke 2:44
They thought He was with their company and traveled a day. Then they searched among relatives and friends. 📖
## Luke 2:45
They could not find Him and returned to Jerusalem to search. 📖
## Luke 2:46
After three days, they found Him in the temple courts among the teachers. He listened and asked questions. 📖
## Luke 2:47
Everyone hearing Him marveled at His understanding and answers. 📖
## Luke 2:48
His parents were astonished. His mother asked, “Child, why did You do this to us? Your father and I searched anxiously.” 💬
## Luke 2:49
He asked, “Why did you search for Me? Did you not know I must be in My Father’s house?” 📖
## Luke 2:50
They did not understand His statement. 📖
## Luke 2:51
He returned to Nazareth with them and obeyed them. His mother treasured all these things in her heart. ❤️
## Luke 2:52
Jesus grew in wisdom, stature, and favor with God and man. 📖
