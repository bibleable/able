## Luke 24:1
Very early on the week’s first day, the women brought their prepared spices to the tomb. 📖
## Luke 24:2
They found its stone rolled away. 📖
## Luke 24:3
They entered but did not find the Lord Jesus’ body. 📖
## Luke 24:4
They were puzzled. Suddenly two men in shining clothes stood beside them. ✨
## Luke 24:5
Terrified, the women bowed their faces to the ground. The men asked, “Why seek the living among the dead? ❓
## Luke 24:6
He is not here. He has risen! Remember what He told you in Galilee: 📖
## Luke 24:7
‘The Son of Man must be handed to sinful men, crucified, and rise again on the third day.’” 📖
## Luke 24:8
They remembered His words. 📖
## Luke 24:9
They returned and reported everything to the Eleven and all the others. 📖
## Luke 24:10
Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women told the apostles. 👥
## Luke 24:11
The apostles thought this was nonsense and disbelieved them. 📖
## Luke 24:12
Peter ran to the tomb, bent down, and saw only linen cloths. He left, wondering what happened. 📖
## Luke 24:13
That day two of them traveled to Emmaus, a village about seven miles from Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 24:14
They discussed everything that happened. 📖
## Luke 24:15
Jesus approached and walked with them as they talked and considered these events. 📖
## Luke 24:16
Their eyes were kept from recognizing Him. 📖
## Luke 24:17
He asked, “What are you discussing so intently?” They stopped, their faces sad. ❓
## Luke 24:18
Cleopas asked, “Are You the only visitor to Jerusalem who does not know what happened recently?” ❓
## Luke 24:19
He asked, “What happened?” They answered, “The events concerning Jesus of Nazareth, a prophet powerful in word and deed before God and everyone. 📖
## Luke 24:20
Our chief priests and rulers handed Him over for a death sentence and crucified Him. 📖
## Luke 24:21
We hoped He would redeem Israel. It is now the third day since this happened. 📖
## Luke 24:22
Some women among us also amazed us. They visited the tomb early today, 📖
## Luke 24:23
but did not find His body. They reported a vision of angels saying Jesus was alive. 📖
## Luke 24:24
Some companions went and found the tomb as the women described. They did not see Him.” 📖
## Luke 24:25
Jesus said, “Foolish ones, how slow your hearts are to believe all the prophets said! 📖
## Luke 24:26
Did the Christ not have to suffer this and enter His glory?” ❓
## Luke 24:27
Beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, He explained what all the Scriptures said about Himself. 📖
## Luke 24:28
Near their destination, He seemed to be going farther. 📖
## Luke 24:29
They pleaded, “Stay with us. Evening is near, and the day is almost over.” He entered to stay. 📖
## Luke 24:30
Reclining with them at the table, He took bread, blessed and broke it, and gave it to them. 📖
## Luke 24:31
Their eyes opened, and they recognized Jesus. He disappeared from their sight. 📖
## Luke 24:32
They asked each other, “Did our hearts not burn as He spoke on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?” ❤️
## Luke 24:33
That hour they returned to Jerusalem and found the Eleven gathered with others. 👥
## Luke 24:34
Those gathered said, “The Lord has truly risen and appeared to Simon!” 📖
## Luke 24:35
The two described the journey and how they recognized Jesus when He broke bread. 📖
## Luke 24:36
As they spoke, Jesus stood among them and said, “Peace be with you.” ❤️
## Luke 24:37
They were startled and afraid, thinking they saw a spirit. 📖
## Luke 24:38
He asked, “Why are you troubled? Why do your hearts doubt? ❓
## Luke 24:39
Look at My hands and feet. It is I Myself. Touch Me and see. A spirit lacks flesh and bones, which you see I have.” 📖
## Luke 24:40
He showed them His hands and feet. 📖
## Luke 24:41
Their joy and amazement still kept them from believing. He asked, “Do you have food here?” ❓
## Luke 24:42
They gave Him broiled fish. 📖
## Luke 24:43
He ate it before them. 📖
## Luke 24:44
He said, “I told you this while still with you. Everything about Me in Moses’ Law, the Prophets, and the Psalms must be fulfilled.” 📖
## Luke 24:45
He opened their minds to understand the Scriptures. 📖
## Luke 24:46
He said, “It is written that the Christ will suffer and rise from the dead on the third day. 📖
## Luke 24:47
In His name, repentance and forgiveness of sins will be proclaimed to all nations, beginning in Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 24:48
You are witnesses of these things. 📖
## Luke 24:49
I am sending My Father’s promise upon you. Stay in the city until you are clothed with power from on high.” 📖
## Luke 24:50
Jesus led them as far as Bethany. He raised His hands and blessed them. 📖
## Luke 24:51
While blessing them, He left and was carried up into heaven. ✨
## Luke 24:52
They worshiped Him and returned joyfully to Jerusalem, 🙏
## Luke 24:53
continually praising God in the temple. 🙏
