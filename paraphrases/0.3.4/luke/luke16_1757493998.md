## Luke 16:1
Jesus told His disciples, “A rich man’s manager was accused of wasting his possessions. 📖
## Luke 16:2
The rich man asked him, ‘What do I hear about you? Give an account of your management. You cannot remain manager.’ 📖
## Luke 16:3
The manager thought, ‘My master is removing me. What shall I do? I am too weak to dig and too ashamed to beg. 📖
## Luke 16:4
I know how to make people welcome me into their homes after I lose my position.’ 📖
## Luke 16:5
He called each of his master’s debtors. He asked the first, ‘How much do you owe my master?’ ❓
## Luke 16:6
He answered, ‘A hundred measures of olive oil.’ The manager said, ‘Take your bill, sit quickly, and write fifty.’ 📖
## Luke 16:7
He asked another, ‘How much do you owe?’ He answered, ‘A hundred measures of wheat.’ The manager said, ‘Take your bill and write eighty.’ 📖
## Luke 16:8
The master praised the dishonest manager for acting shrewdly. This age’s sons deal more shrewdly with their own kind than the sons of light do. 📖
## Luke 16:9
Use worldly wealth to make friends. When it is gone, they will welcome you into eternal dwellings. 📖
## Luke 16:10
Whoever is faithful with little is faithful with much. Whoever is dishonest with little is dishonest with much. 📖
## Luke 16:11
If you were unfaithful with worldly wealth, who will trust you with true riches? ❓
## Luke 16:12
If you were unfaithful with another’s belongings, who will give you your own? ❓
## Luke 16:13
No servant can serve two masters. He will hate one and love the other, or be devoted to one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and money.” 📖
## Luke 16:14
The Pharisees loved money. They heard this and mocked Jesus. 📖
## Luke 16:15
He said, “You justify yourselves before people, but God knows your hearts. What people prize is detestable before God. 📖
## Luke 16:16
The Law and Prophets were proclaimed until John. Since then, the good news of the kingdom of God is preached, and everyone forces his way into it. 📖
## Luke 16:17
Heaven and earth could pass away more easily than one pen stroke could disappear from the Law. 📖
## Luke 16:18
A man who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery. A man who marries a divorced woman commits adultery. 📖
## Luke 16:19
A rich man wore purple and fine linen. Each day he lived joyfully in splendor. 📖
## Luke 16:20
A beggar named Lazarus lay at his gate, covered with sores. 📖
## Luke 16:21
He longed to eat crumbs from the rich man’s table. Even dogs licked his sores. 📖
## Luke 16:22
The beggar died, and angels carried him to Abraham’s side. The rich man also died and was buried. 📖
## Luke 16:23
In torment in Hades, he looked up and saw Abraham far away with Lazarus beside him. 📖
## Luke 16:24
He cried, ‘Father Abraham, have mercy! Send Lazarus to dip his fingertip in water and cool my tongue. This fire torments me.’ 🙏
## Luke 16:25
Abraham answered, ‘Child, you received good things during your life. Lazarus received bad things. Now he is comforted here, and you are tormented. 📖
## Luke 16:26
A great chasm is fixed between us and you. No one can cross from either side to the other, even if they want to.’ 📖
## Luke 16:27
He said, ‘Then, father, please send Lazarus to my father’s house. 🙏
## Luke 16:28
I have five brothers. Let him warn them so they do not come to this place of torment.’ 📖
## Luke 16:29
Abraham replied, ‘They have Moses and the Prophets. Let them listen to them.’ 📖
## Luke 16:30
He said, ‘No, father Abraham. If someone comes to them from the dead, they will repent.’ 📖
## Luke 16:31
Abraham answered, ‘If they do not listen to Moses and the Prophets, they will not be persuaded even if someone rises from the dead.’” 📖
