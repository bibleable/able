## Luke 18:1
Jesus told a parable to show they should always pray and not lose heart. 🙏
## Luke 18:2
“A town had a judge who neither feared God nor respected people. 📖
## Luke 18:3
A widow there kept asking him, ‘Give me justice against my adversary.’ 📖
## Luke 18:4
He refused for a while. Later he thought, ‘I neither fear God nor respect people. 📖
## Luke 18:5
But this widow keeps bothering me, so I will give her justice. Otherwise, her endless requests will wear me out.’” 📖
## Luke 18:6
The Lord said, “Listen to the unjust judge’s words. 📖
## Luke 18:7
Will God not give justice to His chosen ones who cry to Him day and night? Will He delay helping them? ❓
## Luke 18:8
He will promptly give them justice. But when the Son of Man comes, will He find faith on earth?” ❓
## Luke 18:9
Some trusted their own righteousness and despised others. He told them this parable: 📖
## Luke 18:10
“Two men went up to the temple to pray. One was a Pharisee, the other a tax collector. 🙏
## Luke 18:11
The Pharisee stood alone and prayed, ‘God, I thank You. I am unlike other men: swindlers, evildoers, adulterers, or even this tax collector. 🙏
## Luke 18:12
I fast twice a week and give a tenth of everything I acquire.’ 📖
## Luke 18:13
The tax collector stood far away and would not even look up to heaven. He beat his chest and said, ‘God, have mercy on me, a sinner!’ 🙏
## Luke 18:14
This man went home justified, unlike the Pharisee. Everyone who exalts himself will be humbled. Whoever humbles himself will be exalted.” 📖
## Luke 18:15
People brought even babies for Jesus to lay His hands on them. The disciples saw this and rebuked those bringing them. 📖
## Luke 18:16
Jesus called the children to Him. He said, “Let little children come to Me. Do not prevent them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these. ❤️
## Luke 18:17
Truly, anyone who does not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.” 📖
## Luke 18:18
A ruler asked, “Good Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?” ❓
## Luke 18:19
Jesus answered, “Why call Me good? Only God is good. 📖
## Luke 18:20
You know the commandments. ‘Do not commit adultery. Do not murder, steal, or bear false witness. Honor your father and mother.’” 📖
## Luke 18:21
He answered, “I kept all these from my youth.” 📖
## Luke 18:22
Jesus said, “You still lack one thing. Sell everything you own and give to the poor. You will have treasure in heaven. Then follow Me.” 📖
## Luke 18:23
Hearing this made the ruler very sad because he was extremely wealthy. 📖
## Luke 18:24
Jesus saw his sadness and said, “How hard it is for the rich to enter the kingdom of God! 📖
## Luke 18:25
A camel can pass through a needle’s eye more easily than a rich man can enter the kingdom of God.” 📖
## Luke 18:26
Those listening asked, “Who can be saved, then?” ❓
## Luke 18:27
Jesus answered, “What is impossible with people is possible with God.” 📖
## Luke 18:28
Peter said, “We left everything we had to follow You.” 📖
## Luke 18:29
Jesus answered, “Truly, this applies to anyone who left home, wife, brothers, parents, or children for the kingdom of God. 📖
## Luke 18:30
He will receive many times more in this age and eternal life in the coming age.” 📖
## Luke 18:31
Jesus took the Twelve aside. He said, “We are going up to Jerusalem. Everything the prophets wrote about the Son of Man will be fulfilled. 📖
## Luke 18:32
He will be handed to the Gentiles. They will mock Him, insult Him, and spit on Him. 📖
## Luke 18:33
They will flog and kill Him. On the third day, He will rise again.” 📖
## Luke 18:34
The disciples understood none of this. Its meaning was hidden from them, and they did not understand His words. 📖
## Luke 18:35
Near Jericho, a blind man sat by the road begging. 📖
## Luke 18:36
He heard the crowd pass and asked what was happening. ❓
## Luke 18:37
They said, “Jesus of Nazareth is passing.” 📖
## Luke 18:38
He cried, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” 🙏
## Luke 18:39
Those leading told him to be quiet. He cried louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!” 🙏
## Luke 18:40
Jesus stopped and ordered them to bring the man. When he came near, Jesus asked, 📖
## Luke 18:41
“What do you want Me to do?” He answered, “Lord, let me see again.” 🙏
## Luke 18:42
Jesus said, “Receive your sight! Your faith healed you.” ❤️
## Luke 18:43
He immediately saw and followed Jesus, glorifying God. Everyone who saw it praised God. 🙏
