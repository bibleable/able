## Luke 8:1
Jesus traveled through towns and villages, proclaiming the good news of the kingdom of God. The Twelve accompanied Him. 👥
## Luke 8:2
Women healed of evil spirits and illnesses also accompanied Him. Among them was Mary called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out. 👥
## Luke 8:3
There were also Joanna, wife of Herod’s household manager Chuza, Susanna, and many others. These women supported them from their own resources. 👥
## Luke 8:4
People gathered from town after town. Jesus told the large crowd a parable: 📖
## Luke 8:5
“A farmer sowed seed. Some fell on the path, where it was trampled and eaten by birds. 🌱
## Luke 8:6
Some fell on rocky ground and sprouted. It withered without moisture. 🌱
## Luke 8:7
Some fell among thorns. They grew with it and choked the seedlings. 🌱
## Luke 8:8
Some fell on good soil, grew, and yielded a hundredfold crop.” He called, “Let anyone with ears to hear listen.” 🌱
## Luke 8:9
His disciples asked the parable’s meaning. ❓
## Luke 8:10
He answered, “You received knowledge of the mysteries of the kingdom of God. I speak to others in parables so that, ‘Though seeing, they may not see. Though hearing, they may not understand.’ 📖
## Luke 8:11
The seed is God’s word. 📖
## Luke 8:12
Those along the path hear, but the devil removes the word from their hearts to prevent them believing and being saved. 📖
## Luke 8:13
Those on rocky ground hear and receive the word joyfully but have no root. They believe for a time but fall away when tested. 📖
## Luke 8:14
Those among thorns hear, but life’s worries, riches, and pleasures choke them. Their fruit does not mature. 📖
## Luke 8:15
Those on good soil hear with noble, good hearts. They hold the word and produce a crop through perseverance. 🌱
## Luke 8:16
No one lights a lamp and covers it with a jar or puts it under a bed. He sets it on a stand so entrants see the light. ✨
## Luke 8:17
Everything hidden will be disclosed. Everything concealed will be made known and brought to light. 📖
## Luke 8:18
Watch how you listen. Whoever has will receive more. Whoever does not have will lose even what he thinks he has.” 📖
## Luke 8:19
His mother and brothers came but could not reach Him through the crowd. 👥
## Luke 8:20
He was told, “Your mother and brothers wait outside to see You.” 💬
## Luke 8:21
He answered, “My mother and brothers are those who hear God’s word and do it.” 📖
## Luke 8:22
Jesus told His disciples, “Let us cross the lake.” They entered a boat together and left. 📖
## Luke 8:23
He slept as they sailed. A windstorm struck the lake, swamping the boat and endangering them. 🌊
## Luke 8:24
They woke Him, crying, “Master, Master, we are perishing!” He rebuked the wind and raging water. They subsided, and all was calm. 📖
## Luke 8:25
He asked, “Where is your faith?” Frightened and amazed, they asked, “Who commands wind and water, and they obey Him?” ❓
## Luke 8:26
They sailed to the Gerasenes’ region, across the lake from Galilee. 📖
## Luke 8:27
A demon-possessed man from the town met Jesus ashore. He had not worn clothing or lived in a house for a long time. He lived in tombs. 📖
## Luke 8:28
He cried and fell before Jesus, shouting, “What do You want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? Do not torture me!” 📖
## Luke 8:29
Jesus had commanded the unclean spirit to leave. It often seized him. Though bound with chains and shackles, he broke the chains, and the demon drove him into lonely places. 📖
## Luke 8:30
Jesus asked his name. He answered, “Legion,” because many demons had entered him. 📖
## Luke 8:31
The demons begged Him not to send them to the Abyss. 📖
## Luke 8:32
A large pig herd fed on the hillside. They begged to enter it, and Jesus permitted them. 📖
## Luke 8:33
The demons left the man for the pigs. The herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned. 📖
## Luke 8:34
The pigs’ keepers saw this, fled, and reported it in the town and countryside. 📖
## Luke 8:35
The people came and found the man at Jesus’ feet, clothed and in his right mind. The demons had left him, and the people feared. 📖
## Luke 8:36
Witnesses reported how the man possessed by demons was healed. 📖
## Luke 8:37
Everyone in the Gerasenes’ region asked Jesus to leave because they were greatly afraid. He entered the boat to return. 📖
## Luke 8:38
The man freed from demons begged to accompany Him. Jesus sent him away, saying, 📖
## Luke 8:39
“Return home and tell how much God did for you.” He went throughout the town, proclaiming how much Jesus did for him. 📖
## Luke 8:40
The waiting crowd welcomed Jesus when He returned. 👥
## Luke 8:41
Jairus, a synagogue leader, fell at Jesus’ feet and begged Him to visit his house, 🙏
## Luke 8:42
because his only daughter, about twelve years old, was dying. The crowd pressed around Jesus as He went, 📖
## Luke 8:43
including a woman bleeding for twelve years. She spent all her money on physicians, but none could heal her. 📖
## Luke 8:44
She touched Jesus’ cloak fringe from behind. Her bleeding stopped immediately. 📖
## Luke 8:45
Jesus asked, “Who touched Me?” Everyone denied it. Peter said, “Master, the crowd presses against You.” ❓
## Luke 8:46
Jesus said, “Someone touched Me. I know power left Me.” 📖
## Luke 8:47
She saw she could not remain unnoticed. Trembling, she fell before Him and explained publicly why she touched Him and how she was immediately healed. 📖
## Luke 8:48
Jesus said, “Daughter, your faith healed you. Go in peace.” ❤️
## Luke 8:49
Someone from the synagogue leader’s house arrived while He spoke. He told Jairus, “Your daughter died. Do not bother the Teacher anymore.” 💔
## Luke 8:50
Jesus overheard and told Jairus, “Do not fear. Just believe, and she will be healed.” 📖
## Luke 8:51
He allowed only Peter, John, James, and the girl’s parents into the house with Him. 👥
## Luke 8:52
Everyone mourned and wept. Jesus said, “Stop weeping. She is not dead but asleep.” 📖
## Luke 8:53
They laughed, knowing she was dead. 📖
## Luke 8:54
He took her hand and called, “Child, get up!” 📖
## Luke 8:55
Her spirit returned, and she rose immediately. He told them to give her food. ❤️
## Luke 8:56
Her parents marveled. Jesus ordered them not to tell anyone what happened. 📖
