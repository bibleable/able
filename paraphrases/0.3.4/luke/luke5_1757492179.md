## Luke 5:1
Jesus stood beside the Lake of Gennesaret. The crowd pressed toward Him to hear God’s word, 📖
## Luke 5:2
and He saw two boats at the shore. The fishermen had left them to wash their nets. 📖
## Luke 5:3
He entered Simon’s boat and asked him to move a little offshore. He sat and taught the people from the boat. 📖
## Luke 5:4
After speaking, Jesus told Simon, “Go into deep water and lower your nets for a catch.” 📖
## Luke 5:5
Simon answered, “Master, we worked hard all night and caught nothing. Because You say so, I will lower the nets.” 📖
## Luke 5:6
They caught so many fish that the nets began tearing. 🐟
## Luke 5:7
They signaled their partners in the other boat for help. They came and filled both boats so much that the boats began sinking. 🐟
## Luke 5:8
Simon Peter fell at Jesus’ knees. He said, “Leave me, Lord, because I am a sinful man.” 🙏
## Luke 5:9
He and his companions marveled at their catch, 🐟
## Luke 5:10
as did his partners James and John, Zebedee’s sons. Jesus told Simon, “Do not fear. From now on you will catch men.” 📖
## Luke 5:11
They brought their boats ashore, left everything, and followed Him. 👥
## Luke 5:12
In one town, a man covered with leprosy fell facedown before Jesus. He begged, “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean.” 🙏
## Luke 5:13
Jesus touched him and said, “I am willing. Be clean!” The leprosy left immediately. 📖
## Luke 5:14
He said, “Tell no one. Show yourself to the priest and offer what Moses prescribed for your cleansing, as testimony to them.” 📖
## Luke 5:15
News about Jesus spread further. Great crowds came to hear Him and receive healing from their sicknesses. 📖
## Luke 5:16
He often withdrew to the wilderness to pray. 🙏
## Luke 5:17
One day He taught while Pharisees and law teachers sat nearby. People came from Jerusalem and every village of Galilee and Judea. The Lord’s power was present for Him to heal the sick. 📖
## Luke 5:18
Men brought a paralyzed man on a mat. They tried to place him before Jesus inside, 📖
## Luke 5:19
but could not pass the crowd. They climbed the roof and lowered him on his mat through the tiles, in front of Jesus among the crowd. 📖
## Luke 5:20
Jesus saw their faith and said, “Friend, your sins are forgiven.” ❤️
## Luke 5:21
The scribes and Pharisees thought, “Who is this speaking blasphemy? Only God can forgive sins.” ❓
## Luke 5:22
Jesus knew their thoughts. He asked, “Why think these things in your hearts? ❓
## Luke 5:23
Which is easier to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or ‘Get up and walk’? ❓
## Luke 5:24
But so you may know the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins...” He told the paralyzed man, “I tell you, get up, take your mat, and go home.” 📖
## Luke 5:25
The man immediately stood before them, took his mat, and went home glorifying God. 🙏
## Luke 5:26
Everyone marveled and glorified God. They were filled with awe and said, “We saw remarkable things today.” 🙏
## Luke 5:27
Jesus went out and saw Levi, a tax collector, at his booth. He said, “Follow Me,” 📖
## Luke 5:28
and Levi rose, left everything, and followed Him. 👥
## Luke 5:29
Levi hosted a great banquet for Jesus at his house. Many tax collectors and others ate with them. 👥
## Luke 5:30
The Pharisees and their scribes complained to His disciples, “Why eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?” ❓
## Luke 5:31
Jesus answered, “The sick need a doctor, not the healthy. 📖
## Luke 5:32
I came to call sinners to repentance, not the righteous.” 📖
## Luke 5:33
They said, “John’s disciples and the Pharisees’ disciples often fast and pray. Yours eat and drink.” 💬
## Luke 5:34
Jesus answered, “Can you make the bridegroom’s guests fast while He is with them? ❓
## Luke 5:35
The time will come when He is taken from them. Then they will fast.” 📖
## Luke 5:36
He told a parable: “No one tears cloth from a new garment to patch an old one. Doing so tears the new garment, and its patch will not match the old. 📖
## Luke 5:37
No one pours new wine into old wineskins. It would burst the skins, spill the wine, and ruin the skins. 📖
## Luke 5:38
New wine goes into new wineskins. 📖
## Luke 5:39
No one wants new wine after drinking old, because he says, ‘The old is better.’” 📖
