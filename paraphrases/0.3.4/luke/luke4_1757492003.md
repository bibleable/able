## Luke 4:1
Jesus returned from the Jordan, full of the Holy Spirit. The Spirit led Him into the wilderness, 📖
## Luke 4:2
where the devil tempted Him for forty days. He ate nothing then and was hungry afterward. 📖
## Luke 4:3
The devil said, “If You are God’s Son, command this stone to become bread.” 📖
## Luke 4:4
Jesus answered, “It is written, ‘Man shall not live on bread alone.’” 📖
## Luke 4:5
The devil led Him to a high place and instantly showed Him all the world’s kingdoms. 📖
## Luke 4:6
He said, “I will give You their authority and glory. It was given to me, and I can give it to anyone I wish. ⚠️
## Luke 4:7
If You worship me, it will all be Yours.” ⚠️
## Luke 4:8
Jesus answered, “It is written, ‘Worship the Lord your God and serve Him only.’” 📖
## Luke 4:9
The devil placed Him on Jerusalem’s temple pinnacle. He said, “If You are God’s Son, throw Yourself down. 📖
## Luke 4:10
It is written, ‘He will command His angels to guard You carefully, 📖
## Luke 4:11
and they will lift You in their hands so Your foot does not strike a stone.’” 📖
## Luke 4:12
Jesus answered, “It also says, ‘Do not test the Lord your God.’” 📖
## Luke 4:13
After every temptation, the devil left Him until a suitable time. 📖
## Luke 4:14
Jesus returned to Galilee in the Spirit’s power. News about Him spread throughout the region. 📖
## Luke 4:15
He taught in their synagogues, and everyone glorified Him. 📖
## Luke 4:16
He came to Nazareth, where He grew up. As usual, He entered the synagogue on the Sabbath and stood to read, 📖
## Luke 4:17
and received Isaiah the prophet’s scroll. He unrolled it and found these words: 📜
## Luke 4:18
“The Lord’s Spirit is on Me because He anointed Me to preach good news to the poor. He sent Me to proclaim freedom to captives and sight for the blind, and to release the oppressed, 📖
## Luke 4:19
to proclaim the year of the Lord’s favor.” 📖
## Luke 4:20
He rolled the scroll, returned it to the attendant, and sat. Everyone in the synagogue watched Him, 📜
## Luke 4:21
and He said, “Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing.” 📖
## Luke 4:22
Everyone spoke well of Him and marveled at His gracious words. They asked, “Is He not Joseph’s son?” ❓
## Luke 4:23
Jesus said, “You will quote, ‘Physician, heal yourself!’ You will ask Me to do here in My hometown what you heard I did in Capernaum.” 📖
## Luke 4:24
He added, “I tell you the truth: No prophet is accepted in his hometown. 📖
## Luke 4:25
Many widows lived in Israel in Elijah’s time. The sky was shut three and a half years, and great famine covered all the land. 📖
## Luke 4:26
Elijah was sent to none of them, only to the widow in Zarephath of Sidon. 📖
## Luke 4:27
Many lepers lived in Israel in the prophet Elisha’s time. None was cleansed except Naaman the Syrian.” 📖
## Luke 4:28
Everyone in the synagogue heard and became furious. 📖
## Luke 4:29
They drove Him outside the town to the hill’s brow where the town stood. They intended to throw Him off the cliff. ⚠️
## Luke 4:30
Jesus passed through the crowd and continued on His way. 📖
## Luke 4:31
He went to Capernaum in Galilee and taught on the Sabbath. 📖
## Luke 4:32
His teaching amazed them because His message had authority. 📖
## Luke 4:33
A man in the synagogue was possessed by an unclean demon’s spirit. He cried loudly, 📖
## Luke 4:34
“Ha! What do You want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Did You come to destroy us? I know You are God’s Holy One!” 📖
## Luke 4:35
Jesus rebuked the demon, “Be silent! Come out!” It threw the man down before everyone and left without harming him. 📖
## Luke 4:36
Everyone marveled and asked, “What is this message? He commands unclean spirits with authority and power, and they leave!” 📖
## Luke 4:37
News about Jesus spread throughout the surrounding region. 📖
## Luke 4:38
He left the synagogue for Simon’s home. Simon’s mother-in-law had a high fever. They appealed to Jesus for her, 📖
## Luke 4:39
and He stood over her and rebuked the fever. It left, and she immediately rose and served them. 📖
## Luke 4:40
At sunset, people brought everyone suffering various diseases to Jesus. He laid His hands on each and healed them. 📖
## Luke 4:41
Demons left many, crying, “You are God’s Son!” He rebuked and silenced them because they knew He was the Christ. 📖
## Luke 4:42
At dawn, Jesus went to a lonely place. The crowds searched, found Him, and tried to stop Him leaving. 📖
## Luke 4:43
He said, “I must preach the good news of the kingdom of God to other towns too. That is why I was sent.” 📖
## Luke 4:44
He continued preaching in Judea’s synagogues. 📖
