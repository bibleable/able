## Luke 3:1
It was Tiberius Caesar’s fifteenth year. Pontius Pilate governed Judea, and Herod was Galilee’s tetrarch. His brother Philip was tetrarch of Ituraea and Trachonitis. Lysanias was tetrarch of Abilene, 📖
## Luke 3:2
It was also during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas. God’s word came to John son of Zechariah in the wilderness. 📖
## Luke 3:3
He traveled throughout the Jordan’s region, preaching a baptism of repentance for forgiveness of sins, 📖
## Luke 3:4
as Isaiah the prophet’s book says, “A voice calls in the wilderness, ‘Prepare the Lord’s way. Make His paths straight. 📖
## Luke 3:5
Every valley will be filled, and every mountain and hill lowered. Crooked ways will become straight, and rough ways smooth. 📖
## Luke 3:6
All humanity will see God’s salvation.’” 📖
## Luke 3:7
John told the crowds seeking baptism, “Brood of vipers, who warned you to flee the coming wrath? ⚠️
## Luke 3:8
Produce fruit matching repentance. Do not say, ‘Abraham is our father.’ God can raise children for Abraham from these stones. 📖
## Luke 3:9
The axe is ready at the trees’ root. Every tree without good fruit will be cut down and thrown into fire.” 🔥
## Luke 3:10
The crowds asked, “What should we do?” ❓
## Luke 3:11
John answered, “Whoever has two tunics should share with someone who has none. Whoever has food should do likewise.” ❤️
## Luke 3:12
Tax collectors came for baptism too. They asked, “Teacher, what should we do?” ❓
## Luke 3:13
He answered, “Collect only the authorized amount.” 📖
## Luke 3:14
Soldiers asked the same question. He said, “Do not take money by force or false accusation. Be content with your wages.” 📖
## Luke 3:15
The people waited expectantly. In their hearts, all wondered whether John was the Christ. 📖
## Luke 3:16
John answered, “I baptize with water. One more powerful than me is coming. I am unworthy to untie His sandal straps. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire. 📖
## Luke 3:17
He holds His winnowing fork to clear His threshing floor and gather wheat into His barn. He will burn the chaff with fire that cannot be put out.” 🔥
## Luke 3:18
With these and many other appeals, John proclaimed good news to the people. 📖
## Luke 3:19
He rebuked Herod the tetrarch about his brother’s wife Herodias and all his evil deeds, 📖
## Luke 3:20
and Herod added another evil: He imprisoned John. ⚖️
## Luke 3:21
Jesus was baptized while the people were being baptized. As He prayed, heaven opened, 🙏
## Luke 3:22
and the Holy Spirit descended on Him in bodily form like a dove. A voice from heaven said, “You are My beloved Son. I am well pleased with You.” 🕊️
