## Luke 7:1
After speaking to the people, Jesus went to Capernaum. 📖
## Luke 7:2
A centurion’s highly valued servant was sick and near death. 📖
## Luke 7:3
The centurion heard about Jesus and sent Jewish elders to ask Him to come and heal his servant. 📖
## Luke 7:4
They pleaded earnestly with Jesus, “He deserves this favor, 🙏
## Luke 7:5
because he loves our nation and built our synagogue.” 📖
## Luke 7:6
Jesus went with them. Near the house, the centurion’s friends brought his message: “Lord, do not trouble Yourself. I am unworthy to receive You under my roof. 📖
## Luke 7:7
That is why I felt unworthy to come myself. Say the word, and my servant will be healed. 📖
## Luke 7:8
I am under authority and have soldiers under me. I tell one to go, and he goes, or another to come, and he comes. My servant does what I tell him.” 📖
## Luke 7:9
Jesus marveled at him. He told the following crowd, “I have not found such great faith even in Israel.” 📖
## Luke 7:10
The messengers returned and found the servant healthy. 📖
## Luke 7:11
Jesus soon went to Nain with His disciples and a large crowd. 👥
## Luke 7:12
At the town gate, a dead man was being carried out. He was his widowed mother’s only son. Many townspeople accompanied her. 💔
## Luke 7:13
The Lord saw her and felt compassion. He said, “Do not weep.” ❤️
## Luke 7:14
He touched the coffin, and its carriers stopped. He said, “Young man, I tell you, get up!” 📖
## Luke 7:15
The dead man sat up and spoke. Jesus gave him back to his mother. ❤️
## Luke 7:16
Everyone was filled with awe and glorified God. They said, “A great prophet appeared among us! God visited His people!” 🙏
## Luke 7:17
News about Jesus spread throughout Judea and the surrounding region. 📖
## Luke 7:18
John’s disciples reported all this to him. 📖
## Luke 7:19
John sent two disciples to ask the Lord, “Are You the One who was to come, or should we expect another?” ❓
## Luke 7:20
They told Jesus, “John the Baptist sent us to ask whether You are the One who was to come or we should expect another.” ❓
## Luke 7:21
That hour Jesus healed many people of diseases, afflictions, and evil spirits. He also gave sight to many blind people. 📖
## Luke 7:22
He said, “Tell John what you saw and heard. The blind see, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, and the dead rise. Good news is preached to the poor. 📖
## Luke 7:23
Blessed is the one who does not turn away because of Me.” 📖
## Luke 7:24
After the messengers left, Jesus asked the crowd about John. “What did you seek in the wilderness? A reed swaying in the wind? ❓
## Luke 7:25
Did you seek a man in fine clothes? Those wearing elegant clothes and living luxuriously are in palaces. 📖
## Luke 7:26
Did you seek a prophet? Yes, and more than a prophet. 📖
## Luke 7:27
It is written about him, ‘I will send My messenger ahead of You to prepare Your way.’ 📖
## Luke 7:28
No one born of women is greater than John. Yet the least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.” 📖
## Luke 7:29
Everyone hearing, even tax collectors, acknowledged God’s justice because they had received John’s baptism. 📖
## Luke 7:30
The Pharisees and law experts rejected God’s purpose for themselves because John had not baptized them. 📖
## Luke 7:31
“What are this generation’s people like? What can I compare them to? ❓
## Luke 7:32
They resemble children calling in the marketplace, ‘We played the flute, but you did not dance. We sang a dirge, but you did not weep.’ 📖
## Luke 7:33
John the Baptist came without eating bread or drinking wine, and you say he has a demon. 📖
## Luke 7:34
The Son of Man came eating and drinking. You call Him a glutton, drunkard, and friend of tax collectors and sinners. 📖
## Luke 7:35
Wisdom is shown right by all her children.” 📖
## Luke 7:36
A Pharisee invited Jesus to eat. He entered his house and reclined at the table. 👥
## Luke 7:37
A sinful woman from the town heard Jesus was dining there. She brought an alabaster jar of perfume. 📖
## Luke 7:38
She stood behind Him, weeping at His feet. She wet His feet with tears, wiped them with her hair, kissed them, and anointed them with perfume. 📖
## Luke 7:39
The Pharisee thought, “If He were a prophet, He would know this woman touching Him is a sinner!” 📖
## Luke 7:40
Jesus said, “Simon, I have something to tell you.” He answered, “Tell me, Teacher.” 💬
## Luke 7:41
“Two men owed a moneylender. One owed five hundred denarii, and the other fifty. 📖
## Luke 7:42
Neither could repay, so he forgave both. Who will love him more?” ❓
## Luke 7:43
Simon answered, “I suppose the one forgiven more.” Jesus said, “You judged correctly.” 📖
## Luke 7:44
He turned toward the woman and told Simon, “Do you see her? You gave no water for My feet. She wet them with tears and wiped them with her hair. 📖
## Luke 7:45
You gave Me no kiss. She has not stopped kissing My feet since I arrived. 📖
## Luke 7:46
You did not anoint My head with oil. She anointed My feet with perfume. 📖
## Luke 7:47
Her many sins have been forgiven, for she loved much. The one forgiven little loves little.” ❤️
## Luke 7:48
Jesus told her, “Your sins are forgiven.” ❤️
## Luke 7:49
Others at the table thought, “Who is this who even forgives sins?” ❓
## Luke 7:50
He told the woman, “Your faith saved you. Go in peace.” ❤️
