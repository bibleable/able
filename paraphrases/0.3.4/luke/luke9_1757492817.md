## Luke 9:1
Jesus gathered the Twelve. He gave them power and authority over all demons and power to cure diseases. 📖
## Luke 9:2
He sent them to proclaim the kingdom of God and heal the sick. 📖
## Luke 9:3
He said, “Take nothing for the journey. Take no staff, bag, bread, money, or second tunic. 📖
## Luke 9:4
Stay in the house you enter until you leave that area. 📖
## Luke 9:5
If people do not welcome you, shake the dust from your feet as you leave their town. This is a testimony against them.” 📖
## Luke 9:6
They went from village to village, preaching the gospel and healing people everywhere. 📖
## Luke 9:7
Herod the tetrarch heard all that was happening and was puzzled. Some said John had risen from the dead, 📖
## Luke 9:8
others that Elijah had appeared, and others that an ancient prophet had risen. 📖
## Luke 9:9
Herod said, “I beheaded John. Who is this man I hear such things about?” He kept trying to see Jesus. ❓
## Luke 9:10
The apostles returned and told Jesus all they had done. He took them away privately to a town called Bethsaida. 📖
## Luke 9:11
The crowds learned this and followed Him. He welcomed them, spoke about the kingdom of God, and healed those needing healing. 📖
## Luke 9:12
Near evening the Twelve said, “Send the crowd to nearby villages and countryside to find food and lodging. This place is deserted.” 📖
## Luke 9:13
Jesus said, “You give them food.” They answered, “We have only five loaves and two fish, unless we buy food for everyone.” 📖
## Luke 9:14
There were about five thousand men. He told His disciples, “Have them sit in groups of about fifty.” 👥
## Luke 9:15
They did this, and everyone sat down. 📖
## Luke 9:16
Jesus took the five loaves and two fish, looked up to heaven, spoke a blessing, and broke them. He gave them to the disciples to serve the people. 📖
## Luke 9:17
Everyone ate and was satisfied. The disciples collected twelve baskets of leftover pieces. 📖
## Luke 9:18
Jesus was praying privately, with His disciples present. He asked, “Who do the crowds say I am?” ❓
## Luke 9:19
They answered, “Some say John the Baptist, others Elijah, and others an ancient prophet who has risen.” 📖
## Luke 9:20
He asked, “What about you? Who do you say I am?” Peter answered, “The Christ of God.” 📖
## Luke 9:21
Jesus strictly warned them not to tell anyone this. 📖
## Luke 9:22
He said, “The Son of Man must suffer many things. The elders, chief priests, and scribes must reject Him. He must be killed and raised to life on the third day.” 📖
## Luke 9:23
He told them all, “Anyone who wants to follow Me must deny himself, take up his cross daily, and follow Me. 📖
## Luke 9:24
Whoever wants to save his life will lose it. Whoever loses his life for My sake will save it. 📖
## Luke 9:25
What does a man gain if he gets the whole world but loses or gives up his very self? ❓
## Luke 9:26
If someone is ashamed of Me and My words, the Son of Man will be ashamed of him. This will happen when He comes in His glory and the glory of the Father and holy angels. 📖
## Luke 9:27
I tell you truly, some standing here will not die before they see the kingdom of God.” 📖
## Luke 9:28
About eight days later, Jesus took Peter, John, and James up a mountain to pray. 🙏
## Luke 9:29
As He prayed, His face changed in appearance, and His clothes became shining white. ✨
## Luke 9:30
Suddenly Moses and Elijah, two men, began talking with Jesus. 📖
## Luke 9:31
They appeared in glory and discussed His departure, which He was about to accomplish at Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 9:32
Peter and his companions were overcome by sleep. When they awoke, they saw Jesus’ glory and the two men with Him. ✨
## Luke 9:33
As the men left, Peter said, “Master, it is good to be here. Let us build three shelters, one each for You, Moses, and Elijah.” He did not know what he was saying. 📖
## Luke 9:34
While Peter spoke, a cloud appeared and surrounded them. They feared as they entered it. 📖
## Luke 9:35
A voice from the cloud said, “This is My Son, whom I have chosen. Listen to Him!” 📖
## Luke 9:36
After the voice spoke, only Jesus was with them. They kept this to themselves and told no one in those days what they saw. 📖
## Luke 9:37
The next day they came down the mountain, and a large crowd met Jesus. 👥
## Luke 9:38
A man cried from the crowd, “Teacher, please look at my son. He is my only child. 🙏
## Luke 9:39
A spirit seizes him, making him scream suddenly. It throws him into convulsions, and he foams at the mouth. It keeps injuring him and rarely leaves him. 📖
## Luke 9:40
I begged Your disciples to drive it out, but they could not.” 📖
## Luke 9:41
Jesus answered, “Unbelieving and perverse generation! How long must I stay with you and put up with you? Bring your son here.” 📖
## Luke 9:42
As the boy approached, the demon threw him down in a convulsion. Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, healed the boy, and returned him to his father. ❤️
## Luke 9:43
Everyone marveled at God’s greatness. While they marveled at all Jesus did, He told His disciples, 📖
## Luke 9:44
“Listen carefully to these words. The Son of Man is about to be delivered into men’s hands.” 📖
## Luke 9:45
They did not understand. The statement was hidden from them so they could not understand it. They feared asking Him about it. 📖
## Luke 9:46
The disciples began arguing about which of them would be greatest. 📖
## Luke 9:47
Jesus knew their hearts’ thoughts. He had a little child stand beside Him. 📖
## Luke 9:48
He said, “Whoever welcomes this child in My name welcomes Me. Whoever welcomes Me welcomes the One who sent Me. The least among you all is the greatest.” 📖
## Luke 9:49
John said, “Master, we saw someone driving out demons in Your name. We tried to stop him because he does not accompany us.” 📖
## Luke 9:50
Jesus answered, “Do not stop him. Whoever is not against you is for you.” 📖
## Luke 9:51
As His ascension approached, Jesus firmly set out for Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 9:52
He sent messengers ahead into a Samaritan village to prepare for Him. 📖
## Luke 9:53
The villagers refused to welcome Him because He was going to Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 9:54
James and John saw this and asked, “Lord, shall we call fire from heaven to consume them?” ❓
## Luke 9:55
Jesus turned and rebuked them. 📖
## Luke 9:56
He and His disciples went to another village. 📖
## Luke 9:57
As they walked, someone said, “I will follow You wherever You go.” 📖
## Luke 9:58
Jesus replied, “Foxes have dens, and birds have nests. The Son of Man has no place to lay His head.” 📖
## Luke 9:59
He told another man, “Follow Me.” The man replied, “Lord, first let me bury my father.” 📖
## Luke 9:60
Jesus said, “Let the dead bury their own dead. You go and proclaim the kingdom of God.” 📖
## Luke 9:61
Another said, “Lord, I will follow You. First let me say goodbye to my family.” 📖
## Luke 9:62
Jesus said, “Anyone who puts his hand to the plow and looks back is unfit for the kingdom of God.” 📖
