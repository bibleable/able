## Luke 17:1
Jesus told His disciples, “Stumbling blocks will certainly come. But woe to whoever brings them! 📖
## Luke 17:2
Having a millstone hung around his neck and being thrown into the sea would be better than causing one of these little ones to stumble. 📖
## Luke 17:3
Watch yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him. If he repents, forgive him. 📖
## Luke 17:4
Even if he sins against you seven times a day and returns seven times saying, ‘I repent,’ forgive him.” 📖
## Luke 17:5
The apostles said, “Lord, increase our faith!” 🙏
## Luke 17:6
He answered, “With faith as small as a mustard seed, you could tell this mulberry tree, ‘Be uprooted and planted in the sea.’ It would obey you. 🌳
## Luke 17:7
Your servant returns from plowing or tending sheep. Would you tell him, ‘Come and eat immediately’? ❓
## Luke 17:8
Would you not say, ‘Prepare my meal and dress to serve while I eat and drink. Then you may eat and drink’? 📖
## Luke 17:9
Do you thank him for doing as ordered? ❓
## Luke 17:10
Likewise, after doing everything commanded, say, ‘We are unworthy servants. We only did our duty.’” 📖
## Luke 17:11
On His way to Jerusalem, Jesus passed between Samaria and Galilee. 📖
## Luke 17:12
He entered a village and met ten lepers. They stood at a distance, 👥
## Luke 17:13
calling loudly, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!” 🙏
## Luke 17:14
He saw them and said, “Show yourselves to the priests.” They were cleansed as they went. ❤️
## Luke 17:15
One saw he was healed and returned, praising God loudly. 🙏
## Luke 17:16
He fell facedown at Jesus’ feet and thanked Him. He was a Samaritan. 🙏
## Luke 17:17
Jesus asked, “Were all ten not cleansed? Where are the other nine? ❓
## Luke 17:18
Did only this foreigner return to glorify God?” ❓
## Luke 17:19
He said, “Get up and go. Your faith made you well.” ❤️
## Luke 17:20
The Pharisees asked when God’s kingdom would come. Jesus answered, “The kingdom of God will not come with observable signs. 📖
## Luke 17:21
People will not say, ‘Here it is!’ or ‘There it is!’ The kingdom of God is in your midst.” 📖
## Luke 17:22
He told the disciples, “You will long to see one of the Son of Man’s days but will not see it. 📖
## Luke 17:23
People will say, ‘There He is!’ or ‘Here He is!’ Do not go out or pursue them. 📖
## Luke 17:24
Lightning flashes across the whole sky. The Son of Man will be like that in His day. 📖
## Luke 17:25
First He must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation. 📖
## Luke 17:26
The Son of Man’s days will be like Noah’s days. 📖
## Luke 17:27
People ate, drank, married, and were given in marriage until Noah entered the ark. Then the flood destroyed them all. 🌊
## Luke 17:28
In Lot’s days, people also ate, drank, bought, sold, planted, and built. 📖
## Luke 17:29
When Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained from heaven and destroyed them all. 📖
## Luke 17:30
It will be like that when the Son of Man is revealed. 📖
## Luke 17:31
On that day, do not come down from your rooftop to collect possessions. Do not return from the field for anything left behind. 📖
## Luke 17:32
Remember Lot’s wife! 📖
## Luke 17:33
Whoever tries to save his life will lose it. Whoever loses his life will preserve it. 📖
## Luke 17:34
That night, two people will be in one bed. One will be taken, and the other left. 📖
## Luke 17:35
Two women will grind grain together. One will be taken, and the other left.” 📖
