## Luke 1:1
Many have set out to write accounts of the events fulfilled among us, 📖
## Luke 1:2
as the first eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed them down to us. 📖
## Luke 1:3
I carefully investigated everything from the beginning. I also decided to write an orderly account for you, most excellent Theophilus, 📖
## Luke 1:4
so you may know the certainty of what you were taught. 📖
## Luke 1:5
During Herod’s reign over Judea, a priest named Zechariah belonged to Abijah’s priestly division. His wife Elizabeth descended from Aaron. 👥
## Luke 1:6
Both were righteous before God. They followed all the Lord’s commandments and decrees blamelessly. 📖
## Luke 1:7
They had no children because Elizabeth was barren. Both were old. 📖
## Luke 1:8
While Zechariah’s division was on duty and he served as priest before God, 📖
## Luke 1:9
he was chosen by lot to enter the Lord’s temple and burn incense, according to priestly custom. 📖
## Luke 1:10
The whole assembly prayed outside during the incense offering. 🙏
## Luke 1:11
An angel of the Lord appeared to Zechariah on the incense altar’s right side. 👼
## Luke 1:12
Zechariah saw him and was startled and afraid. 📖
## Luke 1:13
The angel said, “Do not fear, Zechariah, because your prayer was heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear a son. Name him John. 👶
## Luke 1:14
He will bring you joy and delight, and many will rejoice at his birth, ❤️
## Luke 1:15
because he will be great before the Lord. He must never drink wine or strong drink. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother’s womb. 📖
## Luke 1:16
He will turn many of Israel’s sons back to the Lord their God. 📖
## Luke 1:17
He will go before the Lord in Elijah’s spirit and power. He will turn fathers’ hearts toward their children and the disobedient toward the righteous people’s wisdom. He will prepare a people for the Lord.” 📖
## Luke 1:18
Zechariah asked, “How can I be sure? I am old, and so is my wife.” ❓
## Luke 1:19
The angel answered, “I am Gabriel. I stand before God. I was sent to speak to you and bring this good news. 👼
## Luke 1:20
You will be silent and unable to speak until these things happen because you did not believe my words. They will be fulfilled at their proper time.” 📖
## Luke 1:21
The people waited for Zechariah and wondered why he stayed so long in the temple. 👥
## Luke 1:22
He came out unable to speak. They realized he saw a vision in the temple. He made signs but remained unable to speak. 📖
## Luke 1:23
After completing his service, he returned home. 📖
## Luke 1:24
Elizabeth became pregnant afterward and stayed in seclusion for five months. She said, 👶
## Luke 1:25
“The Lord did this for me. He favored me and removed my disgrace among the people.” ❤️
## Luke 1:26
In the sixth month, God sent Gabriel to Nazareth in Galilee, 👼
## Luke 1:27
to a virgin named Mary, pledged to marry Joseph. Joseph belonged to David’s house. 👥
## Luke 1:28
The angel greeted her, “You are highly favored! The Lord is with you.” 👼
## Luke 1:29
His words troubled Mary greatly. She wondered what his greeting meant. 📖
## Luke 1:30
He said, “Do not fear, Mary. You found favor with God. ❤️
## Luke 1:31
You will conceive and bear a son. Name Him Jesus. 👶
## Luke 1:32
He will be great and called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give Him His father David’s throne, 👑
## Luke 1:33
and He will rule Jacob’s house forever. His kingdom will never end!” 👑
## Luke 1:34
Mary asked, “How can this happen, since I am a virgin?” ❓
## Luke 1:35
The angel answered, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the Most High’s power will overshadow you. So the Holy One born will be called the Son of God. 📖
## Luke 1:36
Your relative Elizabeth conceived a son in her old age. She was called barren, but she is in her sixth month. 👶
## Luke 1:37
No word from God will ever fail.” 📖
## Luke 1:38
Mary answered, “I am the Lord’s servant. May it happen as you said.” The angel left. 🙏
## Luke 1:39
Mary prepared and hurried to a town in Judah’s hill country, 📖
## Luke 1:40
where she entered Zechariah’s home and greeted Elizabeth. 👥
## Luke 1:41
Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting. The baby leaped in her womb, and she was filled with the Holy Spirit. 📖
## Luke 1:42
She cried loudly, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is your womb’s fruit! ❤️
## Luke 1:43
Why am I honored with a visit from my Lord’s mother? ❓
## Luke 1:44
As soon as I heard your greeting, the baby in my womb leaped for joy. ❤️
## Luke 1:45
Blessed is she who believed the Lord’s word to her would be fulfilled.” 📖
## Luke 1:46
Mary said, “My soul magnifies the Lord, 🙏
## Luke 1:47
and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior! ❤️
## Luke 1:48
He favored His servant’s humble state. All generations from now on will call me blessed. 📖
## Luke 1:49
The Mighty One did great things for me. His name is holy. 🙏
## Luke 1:50
His mercy reaches those who fear Him from generation to generation. ❤️
## Luke 1:51
His arm did mighty deeds. He scattered those proud in their hearts’ thoughts. 📖
## Luke 1:52
He removed rulers from their thrones and exalted the humble. 👑
## Luke 1:53
He filled the hungry with good things and sent the rich away empty. 📖
## Luke 1:54
He helped His servant Israel, remembering mercy, ❤️
## Luke 1:55
as He promised our fathers, Abraham and his descendants forever.” 📖
## Luke 1:56
Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months, then returned home. 👥
## Luke 1:57
Elizabeth’s time came, and she bore a son. 👶
## Luke 1:58
Her neighbors and relatives heard the Lord showed her great mercy. They rejoiced with her. ❤️
## Luke 1:59
On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child. They planned to name him Zechariah after his father. 📖
## Luke 1:60
His mother answered, “No! His name will be John.” 💬
## Luke 1:61
They said, “None of your relatives has that name.” 💬
## Luke 1:62
They made signs to his father to ask what he wanted to name the child. 📖
## Luke 1:63
Zechariah requested a tablet and wrote, “His name is John.” Everyone was amazed. 📜
## Luke 1:64
Immediately his mouth opened and his tongue was released. Zechariah spoke, praising God. 🙏
## Luke 1:65
The neighbors were filled with awe. People throughout Judea’s hill country discussed these events. 📖
## Luke 1:66
Everyone who heard wondered, “What will this child become?” The Lord’s hand was with him. 📖
## Luke 1:67
His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, 📖
## Luke 1:68
“Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel! He visited and redeemed His people. 🙏
## Luke 1:69
He raised a horn of salvation for us in His servant David’s house, 📖
## Luke 1:70
as He said through His holy prophets long ago, 📖
## Luke 1:71
salvation from our enemies and everyone who hates us, 📖
## Luke 1:72
to show mercy to our fathers and remember His holy covenant, ❤️
## Luke 1:73
the oath He swore to our father Abraham, granting us, 📖
## Luke 1:74
rescue from hostile hands so we may serve Him without fear, 🙏
## Luke 1:75
in holiness and righteousness before Him throughout our lives. 📖
## Luke 1:76
You, child, will be called the Most High’s prophet. You will go before the Lord to prepare His way, 📖
## Luke 1:77
to give His people knowledge of salvation through forgiveness of their sins, 📖
## Luke 1:78
because of our God’s tender mercy. Through this mercy, the Dawn will visit us from above, ✨
## Luke 1:79
to shine on those in darkness and death’s shadow and guide our feet onto peace’s path.” ✨
## Luke 1:80
The child grew and became strong in spirit. He lived in the wilderness until he appeared publicly to Israel. 📖
