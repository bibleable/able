## Luke 19:1
Jesus entered Jericho and passed through it. 📖
## Luke 19:2
A very wealthy chief tax collector named Zacchaeus was there. 📖
## Luke 19:3
He wanted to see who Jesus was. He was short and could not see over the crowd. 📖
## Luke 19:4
He ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus, who would pass that way. 🌳
## Luke 19:5
Jesus looked up and said, “Zacchaeus, hurry down. I must stay at your house today.” 📖
## Luke 19:6
Zacchaeus hurried down and welcomed Him joyfully. ❤️
## Luke 19:7
Everyone who saw complained, “He is the guest of a sinful man!” 📖
## Luke 19:8
Zacchaeus stood and said, “Lord, I give half my possessions to the poor. If I cheated anyone, I will repay four times as much.” 📖
## Luke 19:9
Jesus said, “Salvation came to this house today because this man is also a son of Abraham. 📖
## Luke 19:10
The Son of Man came to seek and save the lost.” ❤️
## Luke 19:11
Jesus told the listening people a parable because He was near Jerusalem and they expected God’s kingdom to appear immediately. 📖
## Luke 19:12
“A nobleman traveled to a distant country to claim his kingship and then return. 📖
## Luke 19:13
He called ten servants and gave them ten minas. He said, ‘Do business with this until I return.’ 📖
## Luke 19:14
His subjects hated him. They sent a delegation after him, saying, ‘We do not want him to rule us.’ 📖
## Luke 19:15
He received his kingship and returned. He summoned the servants given money to learn what each earned. 📖
## Luke 19:16
The first said, ‘Master, your mina produced ten more minas.’ 📖
## Luke 19:17
He replied, ‘Well done, good servant! Because you were faithful in a very small matter, you will have authority over ten cities.’ 📖
## Luke 19:18
The second said, ‘Master, your mina made five minas.’ 📖
## Luke 19:19
He answered, ‘You will have authority over five cities.’ 📖
## Luke 19:20
Another said, ‘Master, here is your mina. I stored it in a cloth. 📖
## Luke 19:21
I feared you because you are harsh. You withdraw what you never deposited and reap what you never sowed.’ 📖
## Luke 19:22
He answered, ‘Wicked servant, I will judge you by your words! You knew I was harsh, withdrawing what I never deposited and reaping what I never sowed? 📖
## Luke 19:23
Why did you not put my money in the bank? I could have collected it with interest on my return.’ 📖
## Luke 19:24
He told those nearby, ‘Take his mina and give it to the one with ten.’ 📖
## Luke 19:25
They said, ‘Master, he already has ten!’ 📖
## Luke 19:26
He replied, ‘Everyone who has will receive more. Whoever does not have will lose even what he has. 📖
## Luke 19:27
Bring here my enemies who refused my rule. Kill them before me.’” 📖
## Luke 19:28
Jesus then went ahead, up to Jerusalem. 📖
## Luke 19:29
Near Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives, He sent two disciples. 📖
## Luke 19:30
He said, “Enter the village ahead. You will find a tied colt no one has ridden. Untie it and bring it here. 📖
## Luke 19:31
If anyone asks why, say, ‘The Lord needs it.’” 📖
## Luke 19:32
They went and found everything as He said. 📖
## Luke 19:33
As they untied the colt, its owners asked why they were untying it. ❓
## Luke 19:34
They answered, “The Lord needs it.” 📖
## Luke 19:35
They brought it to Jesus, put their cloaks on it, and seated Him on it. 📖
## Luke 19:36
People spread their cloaks on the road as He rode. 📖
## Luke 19:37
Near the descent from the Mount of Olives, all the disciples praised God joyfully and loudly for the miracles they saw. 🙏
## Luke 19:38
“Blessed is the King who comes in the Lord’s name!” “Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!” 🙏
## Luke 19:39
Some Pharisees in the crowd said, “Teacher, rebuke Your disciples!” 📖
## Luke 19:40
He answered, “If they remain silent, the stones will cry out.” 📖
## Luke 19:41
Jesus saw Jerusalem as He approached and wept over it. 💔
## Luke 19:42
He said, “If only you knew today what would bring peace! Now it is hidden from you. 📖
## Luke 19:43
Days will come when your enemies build barricades against you, surround you, and hem you in on every side. 📖
## Luke 19:44
They will level you and your children within your walls. No stone will remain on another, because you failed to recognize God’s time of visiting you.” 📖
## Luke 19:45
Jesus entered the temple courts and began driving out the sellers. 📖
## Luke 19:46
He said, “It is written, ‘My house will be a house of prayer.’ You made it ‘a den of robbers.’” 📖
## Luke 19:47
He taught at the temple daily. The chief priests, scribes, and people’s leaders intended to kill Him. 📖
## Luke 19:48
They could find no way because all the people listened intently to His words. 📖
