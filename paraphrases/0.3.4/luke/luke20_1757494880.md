## Luke 20:1
Jesus taught in the temple courts and proclaimed the gospel. The chief priests, scribes, and elders approached. 📖
## Luke 20:2
They asked, “What authority lets You do these things? Who gave You this authority?” ❓
## Luke 20:3
He answered, “I will ask you a question too. Tell Me: 📖
## Luke 20:4
Did John’s baptism come from heaven or people?” ❓
## Luke 20:5
They discussed it together. “If we say, ‘Heaven,’ He will ask why we did not believe John. 📖
## Luke 20:6
If we say, ‘People,’ everyone will stone us. They believe John was a prophet.” 📖
## Luke 20:7
They answered that they did not know its source. 📖
## Luke 20:8
Jesus said, “Then I will not tell you My authority for doing these things.” 📖
## Luke 20:9
He told the people a parable: “A man planted a vineyard, rented it to tenants, and left for a long time. 🌱
## Luke 20:10
At harvest he sent a servant to collect his share of fruit. The tenants beat him and sent him away with nothing. 📖
## Luke 20:11
He sent another. They beat and shamed him, sending him away with nothing. 📖
## Luke 20:12
He sent a third. They wounded him and threw him out. 📖
## Luke 20:13
The owner said, ‘What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. Perhaps they will respect him.’ 📖
## Luke 20:14
The tenants saw him and discussed it. They said, ‘This is the heir. Let us kill him and take the inheritance.’ 📖
## Luke 20:15
They threw him outside the vineyard and killed him. What will its owner do to them? ❓
## Luke 20:16
He will kill those tenants and give the vineyard to others.” The people said, “May this never happen!” 📖
## Luke 20:17
Jesus looked at them and asked, “What does this Scripture mean? ‘The stone the builders rejected became the cornerstone.’ ❓
## Luke 20:18
Everyone falling on this stone will be broken to pieces. Anyone it falls on will be crushed.” 📖
## Luke 20:19
The scribes and chief priests knew the parable opposed them. They wanted to arrest Him that hour but feared the people. 📖
## Luke 20:20
They watched closely and sent spies pretending sincerity. They hoped to trap Him with His words and hand Him to the governor’s rule and authority. 📖
## Luke 20:21
They said, “Teacher, You speak and teach correctly without favoritism. You teach God’s way truthfully. 📖
## Luke 20:22
Does the Law permit us to pay taxes to Caesar?” ❓
## Luke 20:23
Jesus recognized their deceit and said, 📖
## Luke 20:24
“Show Me a denarius. Whose image and inscription are there?” They answered, “Caesar’s.” 📖
## Luke 20:25
He said, “Give Caesar what is Caesar’s and God what is God’s.” 📖
## Luke 20:26
They could not trap His words before the people. Amazed at His answer, they became silent. 📖
## Luke 20:27
Some Sadducees came to question Him. They say there is no resurrection. 📖
## Luke 20:28
“Teacher, Moses wrote about a man whose brother dies, leaving a childless widow. The man must marry her and raise offspring for his brother. 📖
## Luke 20:29
There were seven brothers. The first married and died childless. 📖
## Luke 20:30
Then the second 📖
## Luke 20:31
and third married her. All seven died without children. 📖
## Luke 20:32
Finally, she died. 📖
## Luke 20:33
Whose wife will she be in the resurrection? All seven married her.” ❓
## Luke 20:34
Jesus answered, “This age’s sons marry and are given in marriage. 📖
## Luke 20:35
Those considered worthy to share the coming age and resurrection from the dead will neither marry nor be given in marriage. 📖
## Luke 20:36
They cannot die anymore because they are like angels. They are sons of God because they are sons of the resurrection. 📖
## Luke 20:37
Moses shows that the dead rise in the burning bush passage. He calls the Lord ‘the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’ 📖
## Luke 20:38
He is God of the living, not the dead, because all are alive to Him.” 📖
## Luke 20:39
Some scribes said, “Teacher, You spoke well!” 📖
## Luke 20:40
They dared ask no more questions. 📖
## Luke 20:41
Jesus asked, “How can they say the Christ is David’s Son? ❓
## Luke 20:42
David says in the book of Psalms, ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand 📖
## Luke 20:43
until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”’ 📖
## Luke 20:44
David calls Him ‘Lord.’ How is He David’s son?” ❓
## Luke 20:45
Everyone heard Jesus tell His disciples, 👥
## Luke 20:46
“Beware of scribes who like walking in long robes. They love marketplace greetings, chief synagogue seats, and places of honor at banquets. 📖
## Luke 20:47
They defraud widows of their houses and pray at length for show. They will receive greater condemnation.” 📖
