## Luke 12:1
Thousands gathered, trampling one another. Jesus spoke first to His disciples: “Beware of the Pharisees’ leaven, which is hypocrisy. 📖
## Luke 12:2
Everything concealed will be disclosed. Everything hidden will be made known. 📖
## Luke 12:3
Your words spoken in darkness will be heard in daylight. Your whispers in inner rooms will be proclaimed from rooftops. 📖
## Luke 12:4
My friends, do not fear those who kill the body and can do nothing more. 📖
## Luke 12:5
Fear the One who has authority to throw you into hell after you have been killed. Yes, fear Him! 📖
## Luke 12:6
Are five sparrows not sold for two pennies? Yet God forgets none of them. 📖
## Luke 12:7
Even your head’s hairs are all numbered. Do not fear. You are worth more than many sparrows. ❤️
## Luke 12:8
Whoever acknowledges Me before people will be acknowledged by the Son of Man before God’s angels. 📖
## Luke 12:9
Whoever denies Me before people will be denied before God’s angels. 📖
## Luke 12:10
Anyone speaking against the Son of Man will be forgiven. Anyone who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven. 📖
## Luke 12:11
When brought before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, do not worry about defending yourselves or what to say. 📖
## Luke 12:12
The Holy Spirit will teach you then what to say.” 📖
## Luke 12:13
Someone said, “Teacher, tell my brother to share the inheritance with me.” 📖
## Luke 12:14
Jesus replied, “Man, who appointed Me your judge or executor?” ❓
## Luke 12:15
He said, “Guard against every kind of greed. Life does not consist of abundant possessions.” 📖
## Luke 12:16
He told a parable: “A rich man’s land produced abundant crops. 🌱
## Luke 12:17
He thought, ‘What shall I do? I have nowhere to store my crops.’ ❓
## Luke 12:18
He said, ‘I will tear down my barns and build larger ones. I will store all my grain and goods there. 📖
## Luke 12:19
Then I will tell myself, “You have many years’ supply of good things. Rest, eat, drink, and enjoy yourself!”’ 📖
## Luke 12:20
God said, ‘Fool! Your life will be required of you tonight. Who will own what you stored?’ 📖
## Luke 12:21
This is how it will be for anyone storing treasure for himself without being rich toward God.” 📖
## Luke 12:22
Jesus told His disciples, “Therefore, do not worry about food for your life or clothing for your body. 📖
## Luke 12:23
Life is more than food, and the body is more than clothes. 📖
## Luke 12:24
Ravens do not sow, reap, or have barns or storehouses. Yet God feeds them. You are much more valuable than birds! 📖
## Luke 12:25
Can worrying add even one hour to your life? ❓
## Luke 12:26
If you cannot do this small thing, why worry about the rest? ❓
## Luke 12:27
Look how lilies grow without working or spinning thread. Even Solomon in all his glory was not dressed like one of them. 🌸
## Luke 12:28
God clothes grass that lives today and is thrown into a furnace tomorrow. How much more will He clothe you, people of little faith! 📖
## Luke 12:29
Do not worry about what you will eat or drink. 📖
## Luke 12:30
The world’s Gentiles pursue these things. Your Father knows you need them. 📖
## Luke 12:31
Seek His kingdom, and you will receive these things too. 📖
## Luke 12:32
Do not fear, little flock. Your Father is pleased to give you the kingdom. ❤️
## Luke 12:33
Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Obtain purses that never wear out, with treasure in heaven that never runs out. No thief approaches it, and no moth destroys it. 📖
## Luke 12:34
Where your treasure is, your heart will be too. ❤️
## Luke 12:35
Dress for service and keep your lamps burning. 📖
## Luke 12:36
Be like servants waiting for their master’s return from a wedding banquet. They are ready to open the door immediately when he knocks. 📖
## Luke 12:37
Blessed are servants their returning master finds watching. Truly, he will dress to serve, seat them at the table, and serve them himself. 📖
## Luke 12:38
Even if he returns in the second or third night watch, the servants he finds alert are blessed. 📖
## Luke 12:39
If the homeowner knew when the thief was coming, he would not have let his house be broken into. 📖
## Luke 12:40
Be ready too. The Son of Man will come when you do not expect Him.” 📖
## Luke 12:41
Peter asked, “Lord, is this parable for us or for everyone too?” ❓
## Luke 12:42
The Lord answered, “Who is the faithful, wise manager? His master puts him over the servants to give them their portion at the proper time. 📖
## Luke 12:43
Blessed is the servant whose returning master finds him doing this. 📖
## Luke 12:44
Truly, the master will put him over all his possessions. 📖
## Luke 12:45
Suppose he thinks, ‘My master will return much later.’ He starts beating male and female servants, eating, drinking, and getting drunk. 📖
## Luke 12:46
His master will return on an unexpected day and hour. He will cut that servant to pieces and assign him a place with unbelievers. 📖
## Luke 12:47
A servant who knows his master’s will but fails to prepare or obey will receive many blows. 📖
## Luke 12:48
One who unknowingly deserves punishment will receive few blows. Much is required from everyone given much. Even more is demanded from those entrusted with much. 📖
## Luke 12:49
I came to ignite fire on earth. How I wish it were already burning! 📖
## Luke 12:50
I have a baptism to undergo. How distressed I am until it is accomplished! 📖
## Luke 12:51
Do you think I came to bring peace on earth? No, I came to bring division. 📖
## Luke 12:52
From now on, a household of five will be divided, three against two and two against three. 📖
## Luke 12:53
Father and son will oppose each other, as will mother and daughter. Mother-in-law and daughter-in-law will also oppose each other.” 📖
## Luke 12:54
He told the crowds, “You see a cloud rising in the west and predict rain. Then it rains. 📖
## Luke 12:55
When the south wind blows, you predict heat. Then it is hot. 📖
## Luke 12:56
Hypocrites! You can interpret earth’s and sky’s appearance. Why can you not interpret the present time? ❓
## Luke 12:57
Why do you not judge what is right for yourselves? ❓
## Luke 12:58
Try hard to settle with your adversary on the way to the magistrate. Otherwise, he may drag you before the judge. The judge may hand you to the officer, who may imprison you. 📖
## Luke 12:59
You will not leave until you pay the last penny.” 📖
