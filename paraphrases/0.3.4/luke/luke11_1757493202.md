## Luke 11:1
After Jesus finished praying, a disciple said, “Lord, teach us to pray, as John taught his disciples.” 🙏
## Luke 11:2
Jesus said, “When you pray, say, ‘Father, may Your name be honored as holy. May Your kingdom come. 🙏
## Luke 11:3
Give us our daily bread each day. 🙏
## Luke 11:4
Forgive our sins, for we also forgive everyone who sins against us. Do not lead us into temptation.’” 🙏
## Luke 11:5
He said, “Suppose you visit a friend at midnight and say, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves. 📖
## Luke 11:6
A traveling friend arrived, and I have no food to offer him.’ 📖
## Luke 11:7
Suppose the friend inside says, ‘Do not bother me. The door is shut, and my children and I are in bed. I cannot get up to give you anything.’ 📖
## Luke 11:8
He will not get up to provide food because of friendship. But the man’s persistence will make him get up and give him all he needs. 📖
## Luke 11:9
So ask, and you will receive. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and the door will be opened to you. 📖
## Luke 11:10
Everyone who asks receives. The one who seeks finds. The door will be opened to the one who knocks. 📖
## Luke 11:11
Which father would give his son a snake instead of the fish he requested? ❓
## Luke 11:12
Would he give him a scorpion instead of an egg? ❓
## Luke 11:13
You are evil, yet you know how to give good gifts to your children. How much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him!” 📖
## Luke 11:14
Jesus drove out a mute demon. After it left, the man who had been mute spoke. The crowds marveled, 📖
## Luke 11:15
but some said, “He drives out demons by Beelzebul, the demons’ prince.” 📖
## Luke 11:16
Others tested Him by demanding a sign from heaven. 📖
## Luke 11:17
Jesus knew their thoughts. He said, “A kingdom divided against itself will be destroyed. A house divided against a house will fall. 📖
## Luke 11:18
If Satan is divided against himself, how can his kingdom stand? You say I drive out demons by Beelzebul. 📖
## Luke 11:19
If I drive them out by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons drive them out? So they will be your judges. 📖
## Luke 11:20
But if I drive out demons by God’s finger, His kingdom has come upon you. 📖
## Luke 11:21
A strong, fully armed man guards his house, and his possessions are safe. 📖
## Luke 11:22
Someone stronger attacks and defeats him. He takes the armor the man trusted and divides the plunder. 📖
## Luke 11:23
Whoever is not with Me is against Me. Whoever does not gather with Me scatters. 📖
## Luke 11:24
An unclean spirit leaves a man and seeks rest in dry places. Finding none, it says, ‘I will return to my former house.’ 📖
## Luke 11:25
It returns and finds the house swept clean and ordered. 📖
## Luke 11:26
It brings seven spirits more wicked than itself, and they live there. The man’s final condition is worse than his first.” 📖
## Luke 11:27
A woman called from the crowd, “Blessed is the womb that bore You and the breasts that nursed You!” 📖
## Luke 11:28
He answered, “Rather, blessed are those who hear God’s word and obey it.” 📖
## Luke 11:29
The crowds grew. Jesus said, “This wicked generation demands a sign. It will receive only the sign of Jonah. 📖
## Luke 11:30
Jonah was a sign to the Ninevites. Likewise, the Son of Man will be a sign to this generation. 📖
## Luke 11:31
The Queen of the South will rise at judgment with this generation’s men and condemn them. She came from earth’s ends to hear Solomon’s wisdom. Now One greater than Solomon is here. 📖
## Luke 11:32
Nineveh’s men will stand at judgment with this generation and condemn it. They repented at Jonah’s preaching. Now One greater than Jonah is here. 📖
## Luke 11:33
No one lights a lamp and puts it in a cellar or under a basket. He puts it on a stand so those entering see its light. ✨
## Luke 11:34
Your eye is your body’s lamp. Good eyes fill your whole body with light. Bad eyes fill it with darkness. 📖
## Luke 11:35
Be careful that the light within you is not darkness. 📖
## Luke 11:36
If your whole body is full of light, with no dark part, you will shine as though a lamp shone on you.” ✨
## Luke 11:37
A Pharisee invited Jesus to dine while He spoke. He entered and reclined at the table. 📖
## Luke 11:38
The Pharisee was surprised that Jesus did not wash before eating. 📖
## Luke 11:39
The Lord said, “You Pharisees clean the cup’s and dish’s outside. Inside you are full of greed and wickedness. 📖
## Luke 11:40
Fools! Did the One who made the outside not make the inside too? ❓
## Luke 11:41
Give what is within you as gifts to the poor. Then everything will be clean for you. 📖
## Luke 11:42
Woe to you Pharisees! You give a tenth of mint, rue, and every herb, but disregard justice and God’s love. You should practice justice and God’s love without neglecting those tithes. 📖
## Luke 11:43
Woe to you Pharisees! You love the chief synagogue seats and greetings in marketplaces. 📖
## Luke 11:44
Woe to you! You resemble unmarked graves that people unknowingly walk over.” 📖
## Luke 11:45
A law expert said, “Teacher, saying this insults us too.” 📖
## Luke 11:46
He answered, “Woe to you law experts too! You burden people with heavy loads but will not lift a finger to help. 📖
## Luke 11:47
Woe to you! You build tombs for the prophets your fathers killed. 📖
## Luke 11:48
You witness and approve your fathers’ deeds. They killed the prophets, and you build their tombs. 📖
## Luke 11:49
Therefore God’s wisdom said, ‘I will send them prophets and apostles. They will kill some and persecute others.’ 📖
## Luke 11:50
Consequently, this generation will be charged with all the prophets’ blood shed since the world’s foundation. 📖
## Luke 11:51
This bloodshed extends from Abel to Zechariah, who was killed between the altar and sanctuary. Yes, this generation will be charged with all of it. 📖
## Luke 11:52
Woe to you law experts! You took away the key to knowledge. You did not enter and prevented others from entering.” 📖
## Luke 11:53
As Jesus left, the scribes and Pharisees bitterly opposed Him and questioned Him about many things. 📖
## Luke 11:54
They waited to catch Him in something He said. 📖
