## Luke 6:1
One Sabbath, Jesus passed through grainfields. His disciples picked grain heads, rubbed them in their hands, and ate them. 📖
## Luke 6:2
Some Pharisees asked, “Why do what is unlawful on the Sabbath?” ❓
## Luke 6:3
Jesus answered, “Did you not read what David did when he and his companions were hungry? 📖
## Luke 6:4
He entered God’s house, took the consecrated bread, gave it to his companions, and ate it. Only priests may lawfully eat it.” 📖
## Luke 6:5
Jesus declared, “The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.” 📖
## Luke 6:6
On another Sabbath, He taught in a synagogue. A man there had a withered right hand. 📖
## Luke 6:7
The scribes and Pharisees watched whether Jesus would heal on the Sabbath. They sought a reason to accuse Him. 📖
## Luke 6:8
He knew their thoughts. He told the man with the withered hand, “Stand among us.” The man rose and stood there. 📖
## Luke 6:9
Jesus asked, “On the Sabbath, is it lawful to do good or evil, to save life or destroy it?” ❓
## Luke 6:10
He looked at everyone and said, “Stretch out your hand.” The man did, and his hand was restored. 📖
## Luke 6:11
The scribes and Pharisees were furious and discussed what to do to Jesus. ⚠️
## Luke 6:12
Jesus went to the mountain and prayed to God all night. 🙏
## Luke 6:13
At daylight, He called His disciples and chose twelve, naming them apostles: 👥
## Luke 6:14
Simon, whom He named Peter, and his brother Andrew, James, John, Philip, Bartholomew, 👥
## Luke 6:15
Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Simon called the Zealot, 👥
## Luke 6:16
Judas son of James, and Judas Iscariot, who became a traitor. 👥
## Luke 6:17
Jesus descended with them to a level place. Many disciples and people from Judea, Jerusalem, and the coast of Tyre and Sidon gathered there. 👥
## Luke 6:18
They came to hear Him and receive healing. Those troubled by unclean spirits were healed. 📖
## Luke 6:19
Everyone tried to touch Him because power came from Him and healed them all. 📖
## Luke 6:20
He looked at His disciples and said, “Blessed are you poor, because God’s kingdom is yours. ❤️
## Luke 6:21
Blessed are you hungry now, because you will be filled. Blessed are you weeping now, because you will laugh. ❤️
## Luke 6:22
Blessed are you when people hate, exclude, and insult you because of the Son of Man. They reject your name as evil because of Him. 📖
## Luke 6:23
Rejoice and leap for joy that day, because your reward in heaven is great. Their fathers treated the prophets the same way. ❤️
## Luke 6:24
Woe to you rich, because you received your comfort already. ⚠️
## Luke 6:25
Woe to you well fed now, because you will hunger. Woe to you laughing now, because you will mourn and weep. ⚠️
## Luke 6:26
Woe when everyone praises you, because their fathers treated false prophets the same way. ⚠️
## Luke 6:27
To those listening I say, love your enemies and do good to those who hate you, ❤️
## Luke 6:28
bless those who curse you, and pray for those mistreating you. 🙏
## Luke 6:29
If someone strikes one cheek, turn the other too. If someone takes your cloak, do not withhold your tunic. 📖
## Luke 6:30
Give to everyone asking you. If anyone takes your possessions, do not demand them back. 📖
## Luke 6:31
Treat others as you want them to treat you. ❤️
## Luke 6:32
If you love those loving you, what credit is that? Sinners love those loving them too. 📖
## Luke 6:33
If you do good to those who do good to you, what credit is that? Even sinners do the same. 📖
## Luke 6:34
If you lend to people you expect will repay you, what credit is that? Sinners lend to sinners expecting full repayment. 📖
## Luke 6:35
Love your enemies, do good to them, and lend expecting nothing back. Then your reward will be great. You will be the Most High’s sons because He is kind to the ungrateful and wicked. ❤️
## Luke 6:36
Be merciful as your Father is merciful. ❤️
## Luke 6:37
Do not judge, and you will not be judged. Do not condemn, and you will not be condemned. Forgive, and you will be forgiven. 📖
## Luke 6:38
Give, and you will receive. A good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and overflowing, will be poured into your lap. The measure you use will be measured back to you.” 📖
## Luke 6:39
Jesus told a parable: “Can a blind man lead another blind man? Will both not fall into a pit? ❓
## Luke 6:40
A disciple is not above his teacher. Everyone fully trained will be like his teacher. 📖
## Luke 6:41
Why notice the speck in your brother’s eye but not the beam in your own? ❓
## Luke 6:42
How can you offer to remove your brother’s speck when you do not see your own beam? Hypocrite! Remove your beam first. Then you will see clearly to remove his speck. 📖
## Luke 6:43
Good trees do not bear bad fruit. Bad trees do not bear good fruit. 🌳
## Luke 6:44
A tree is known by its fruit. Figs do not come from thornbushes, nor grapes from brambles. 🌳
## Luke 6:45
A good man brings good from his heart’s good treasure. An evil man brings evil from his heart’s evil treasure. The mouth speaks from the heart’s overflow. ❤️
## Luke 6:46
Why call Me ‘Lord, Lord,’ but not do what I say? ❓
## Luke 6:47
I will show what the person who comes to Me, hears My words, and acts on them is like: 📖
## Luke 6:48
He is like a man who built a house, dug deep, and laid its foundation on rock. A flood came, and the torrent struck it. It could not shake the house because it was well built. 📖
## Luke 6:49
The one who hears My words but does not act on them is like a man building on ground without a foundation. The torrent struck it, and it immediately fell. Its destruction was great!” 📖
