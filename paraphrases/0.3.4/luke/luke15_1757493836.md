## Luke 15:1
All the tax collectors and sinners gathered to listen to Jesus. 👥
## Luke 15:2
The Pharisees and scribes complained, “This man welcomes sinners and eats with them.” 📖
## Luke 15:3
Jesus told them this parable: 📖
## Luke 15:4
“Suppose you have a hundred sheep and lose one. Would you not leave the ninety-nine in the pasture and search until you find it? 🐑
## Luke 15:5
When you find it, you joyfully put it on your shoulders, 🐑
## Luke 15:6
return home, and gather your friends and neighbors. You say, ‘Rejoice with me! I found my lost sheep!’ ❤️
## Luke 15:7
Likewise, heaven will rejoice more over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous people who need no repentance. 📖
## Luke 15:8
Suppose a woman has ten silver coins and loses one. Would she not light a lamp, sweep the house, and search carefully until she finds it? 📖
## Luke 15:9
She gathers her friends and neighbors and says, ‘Rejoice with me! I found my lost coin!’ ❤️
## Luke 15:10
Likewise, there is joy before God’s angels over one sinner who repents.” 📖
## Luke 15:11
Jesus said, “A man had two sons. 👥
## Luke 15:12
The younger said, ‘Father, give me my share of the estate.’ He divided his property between them. 📖
## Luke 15:13
A few days later, the younger son gathered everything and went to a distant country. He wasted his wealth in wild living. 📖
## Luke 15:14
After he spent everything, severe famine struck that country, and he became needy. 📖
## Luke 15:15
He hired himself to a citizen who sent him into the fields to feed pigs. 📖
## Luke 15:16
He wanted to fill his stomach with the pods the pigs ate. No one gave him anything. 📖
## Luke 15:17
He came to his senses and said, ‘My father’s hired servants have plenty of food. Here I am starving to death! 📖
## Luke 15:18
I will return to my father and say, “Father, I sinned against heaven and you. 📖
## Luke 15:19
I no longer deserve to be called your son. Treat me as one of your hired servants.”’ 📖
## Luke 15:20
He returned to his father. His father saw him far away and felt compassion. He ran, embraced him, and kissed him. ❤️
## Luke 15:21
The son said, ‘Father, I sinned against heaven and you. I no longer deserve to be called your son.’ 📖
## Luke 15:22
His father told the servants, ‘Quickly bring the best robe and dress him. Put a ring on his finger and sandals on his feet. 📖
## Luke 15:23
Kill the fattened calf. Let us feast and celebrate! 📖
## Luke 15:24
My son was dead and is alive again! He was lost and is found!’ They began celebrating. ❤️
## Luke 15:25
The older son was in the field. Near the house, he heard music and dancing. 📖
## Luke 15:26
He called a servant and asked what was happening. ❓
## Luke 15:27
The servant said, ‘Your brother returned safely. Your father killed the fattened calf because he has him back safe and sound.’ 📖
## Luke 15:28
The older son became angry and refused to enter. His father came out and pleaded with him. 📖
## Luke 15:29
He replied, ‘I served you all these years and never disobeyed your commands. You never gave me even a young goat to celebrate with friends. 📖
## Luke 15:30
This son of yours wasted your wealth with prostitutes. When he returns, you kill the fattened calf for him!’ 📖
## Luke 15:31
His father said, ‘Son, you are always with me. Everything I have is yours. ❤️
## Luke 15:32
Celebrating and rejoicing was right because your brother was dead and is alive again. He was lost and is found.’” ❤️
