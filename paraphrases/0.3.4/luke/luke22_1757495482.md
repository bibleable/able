## Luke 22:1
The Feast of Unleavened Bread, called the Passover, was near. 📖
## Luke 22:2
The chief priests and scribes sought a way to kill Jesus because they feared the people. 📖
## Luke 22:3
Satan entered Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve. 📖
## Luke 22:4
Judas discussed with the chief priests and temple officers how to betray Jesus to them. 📖
## Luke 22:5
They were delighted and agreed to pay him. 📖
## Luke 22:6
He agreed and sought a chance to betray Jesus without a crowd present. 📖
## Luke 22:7
The day of Unleavened Bread came when the Passover lamb was to be sacrificed. 📖
## Luke 22:8
Jesus sent Peter and John to prepare the Passover meal for them. 📖
## Luke 22:9
They asked, “Where should we prepare it?” ❓
## Luke 22:10
He answered, “A man carrying a water jug will meet you in the city. Follow him into the house. 📖
## Luke 22:11
Tell its owner, ‘The Teacher asks where the guest room is, where He can eat the Passover with His disciples.’ 📖
## Luke 22:12
He will show you a large, furnished upper room. Prepare there.” 📖
## Luke 22:13
They found everything as Jesus said and prepared the Passover. 📖
## Luke 22:14
At the proper hour, Jesus reclined at the table with His apostles. 👥
## Luke 22:15
He said, “I eagerly desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer. 📖
## Luke 22:16
I will not eat it again until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God.” 📖
## Luke 22:17
He took the cup, gave thanks, and said, “Take this and divide it among yourselves. 📖
## Luke 22:18
I will not drink the vine’s fruit again until the kingdom of God comes.” 📖
## Luke 22:19
He took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them. He said, “This is My body, given for you. Do this in remembrance of Me.” 📖
## Luke 22:20
After supper He took the cup likewise. He said, “This cup is the new covenant in My blood, poured out for you. 📖
## Luke 22:21
My betrayer’s hand is with Mine on the table. 📖
## Luke 22:22
The Son of Man will go as determined. But woe to His betrayer!” 📖
## Luke 22:23
They questioned one another about who would do this. ❓
## Luke 22:24
They also disputed which disciple should be considered greatest. 📖
## Luke 22:25
Jesus said, “Gentile kings lord it over their people. Those in authority call themselves benefactors. 📖
## Luke 22:26
You must be different. Your greatest should be like the youngest, and your leader like one who serves. 📖
## Luke 22:27
Who is greater: the one reclining at the table or the servant? Is it not the one reclining? Yet I am among you as one who serves. 📖
## Luke 22:28
You stood by Me in My trials. 📖
## Luke 22:29
I give you a kingdom as My Father gave Me one. 📖
## Luke 22:30
This is so you may eat and drink at My table in My kingdom and sit on thrones judging Israel’s twelve tribes. 📖
## Luke 22:31
Simon, Simon, Satan asked to sift all of you like wheat. 📖
## Luke 22:32
I prayed for you, Simon, that your faith would not fail. When you turn back, strengthen your brothers.” 🙏
## Luke 22:33
Peter said, “Lord, I am ready for prison and death with You.” 📖
## Luke 22:34
Jesus replied, “Peter, before the rooster crows today, you will deny knowing Me three times.” 📖
## Luke 22:35
He asked, “When I sent you without purse, bag, or sandals, did you lack anything?” They answered, “Nothing.” ❓
## Luke 22:36
He said, “Now take a purse and bag if you have them. If you lack a sword, sell your cloak and buy one. 📖
## Luke 22:37
This Scripture must be fulfilled in Me: ‘He was counted with the transgressors.’ What is written about Me is reaching fulfillment.” 📖
## Luke 22:38
They said, “Lord, here are two swords.” He answered, “That is enough.” 📖
## Luke 22:39
Jesus went as usual to the Mount of Olives. His disciples followed. 📖
## Luke 22:40
There He said, “Pray that you will not enter temptation.” 🙏
## Luke 22:41
He went about a stone’s throw beyond them, knelt, and prayed, 🙏
## Luke 22:42
“Father, if You are willing, take this cup from Me. Let Your will be done, not Mine.” 🙏
## Luke 22:43
An angel from heaven appeared and strengthened Him. 📖
## Luke 22:44
In anguish, He prayed more earnestly. His sweat became like drops of blood falling to the ground. 🙏
## Luke 22:45
He rose from prayer and returned to the disciples. They slept, exhausted from sorrow. 📖
## Luke 22:46
He asked, “Why are you sleeping? Rise and pray so you do not enter temptation.” 🙏
## Luke 22:47
While He spoke, a crowd arrived led by Judas, one of the Twelve. Judas approached to kiss Jesus. 📖
## Luke 22:48
Jesus asked, “Judas, do you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?” ❓
## Luke 22:49
His companions saw what was coming and asked, “Lord, should we strike with our swords?” ❓
## Luke 22:50
One struck the high priest’s servant and cut off his right ear. 📖
## Luke 22:51
Jesus said, “No more of this!” He touched the ear and healed him. ❤️
## Luke 22:52
He addressed the chief priests, temple officers, and elders who came for Him. “Why bring swords and clubs as though confronting an outlaw? ❓
## Luke 22:53
I was with you daily in the temple courts, and you did not seize Me. This hour belongs to you and the power of darkness.” 📖
## Luke 22:54
They seized Jesus and took Him to the high priest’s house. Peter followed far behind. 📖
## Luke 22:55
People lit a fire in the courtyard’s middle and sat together. Peter sat among them. 📖
## Luke 22:56
A servant girl looked closely at him in the firelight. She said, “This man was with Him too.” 📖
## Luke 22:57
Peter denied it, saying, “Woman, I do not know Him.” 📖
## Luke 22:58
Soon another said, “You are one of them too.” Peter replied, “Man, I am not.” 📖
## Luke 22:59
About an hour later, another insisted, “He certainly was with Him. He is a Galilean too.” 📖
## Luke 22:60
Peter said, “Man, I do not know what you mean.” The rooster immediately crowed while he spoke. 📖
## Luke 22:61
The Lord turned and looked at Peter. Peter remembered His words: “Before the rooster crows today, you will deny Me three times.” 📖
## Luke 22:62
He went outside and wept bitterly. 💔
## Luke 22:63
Jesus’ captors mocked and beat Him. 📖
## Luke 22:64
They blindfolded Him and demanded, “Prophesy! Who hit You?” 📖
## Luke 22:65
They spoke many other blasphemies against Him. 📖
## Luke 22:66
At daybreak, the elders’ council, including chief priests and scribes, met. They led Jesus into their Sanhedrin and said, 📖
## Luke 22:67
“Tell us if You are the Christ.” Jesus answered, “If I tell you, you will not believe. 📖
## Luke 22:68
If I ask a question, you will not answer. 📖
## Luke 22:69
From now on, the Son of Man will sit at the right hand of God’s power.” 📖
## Luke 22:70
All asked, “Are You the Son of God, then?” He replied, “You say that I am.” 📖
## Luke 22:71
They said, “Why need more testimony? We heard it ourselves from His lips.” 📖
