## Luke 10:1
The Lord appointed seventy-two others. He sent them in pairs ahead of Him to every town and place He would visit. 👥
## Luke 10:2
He said, “The harvest is plentiful, but workers are few. So ask the Lord of the harvest to send workers into His harvest. 🙏
## Luke 10:3
Go! I send you like lambs among wolves. 📖
## Luke 10:4
Carry no purse, bag, or sandals. Greet no one on the road. 📖
## Luke 10:5
When you enter a house, first say, ‘Peace to this house.’ 📖
## Luke 10:6
If a man of peace is there, your peace will rest on him. Otherwise, it will return to you. 📖
## Luke 10:7
Stay in that house and eat and drink what they offer. The worker deserves his wages. Do not move from house to house. 📖
## Luke 10:8
If a town welcomes you, eat what is served. 📖
## Luke 10:9
Heal its sick people and tell them, ‘The kingdom of God is near you.’ 📖
## Luke 10:10
If a town does not welcome you, go into its streets and say, 📖
## Luke 10:11
‘We wipe even your town’s dust from our feet as a testimony against you. But know this: The kingdom of God is near.’ 📖
## Luke 10:12
On that day, Sodom will find it more bearable than that town. 📖
## Luke 10:13
Woe to you, Chorazin and Bethsaida! Suppose your miracles had occurred in Tyre and Sidon. They would have repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes. 📖
## Luke 10:14
At the judgment, Tyre and Sidon will find it more bearable than you. 📖
## Luke 10:15
Capernaum, will you be raised to heaven? No! You will be brought down to Hades. 📖
## Luke 10:16
Whoever listens to you listens to Me. Whoever rejects you rejects Me. Whoever rejects Me rejects the One who sent Me.” 📖
## Luke 10:17
The seventy-two returned joyfully. They said, “Lord, even demons submit to us in Your name.” 📖
## Luke 10:18
He answered, “I saw Satan fall from heaven like lightning. 📖
## Luke 10:19
I have given you authority to tread on snakes and scorpions and over all the enemy’s power. Nothing will harm you. 📖
## Luke 10:20
Rejoice because your names are written in heaven, rather than because spirits submit to you.” 📖
## Luke 10:21
Jesus rejoiced in the Holy Spirit. He said, “Father, Lord of heaven and earth, I praise You. You hid these things from the wise and learned and revealed them to little children. Yes, Father, this pleased You. 🙏
## Luke 10:22
My Father entrusted all things to Me. Only the Father knows who the Son is. Only the Son and those to whom He chooses to reveal the Father know who the Father is.” 📖
## Luke 10:23
He turned and told the disciples privately, “Blessed are the eyes that see what you see. 📖
## Luke 10:24
Many prophets and kings wanted to see and hear what you see and hear. They did not see or hear it.” 📖
## Luke 10:25
An expert in the law stood to test Him. He asked, “Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?” ❓
## Luke 10:26
Jesus asked, “What does the Law say? How do you read it?” ❓
## Luke 10:27
He answered, “‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, strength, and mind.’ Also, ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’” ❤️
## Luke 10:28
Jesus said, “You answered correctly. Do this and you will live.” 📖
## Luke 10:29
Wanting to justify himself, he asked, “Who is my neighbor?” ❓
## Luke 10:30
Jesus answered, “A man traveling down from Jerusalem to Jericho met robbers. They stripped and beat him, then left him half dead. 📖
## Luke 10:31
A priest happened to travel down that road. He saw the man and passed on the other side. 📖
## Luke 10:32
A Levite also came, saw him, and passed on the other side. 📖
## Luke 10:33
A traveling Samaritan came, saw him, and felt compassion. ❤️
## Luke 10:34
He poured oil and wine on the wounds and bandaged them. He put the man on his own animal, took him to an inn, and cared for him. ❤️
## Luke 10:35
The next day he gave the innkeeper two denarii. He said, ‘Care for him. I will repay any extra expense when I return.’ 📖
## Luke 10:36
Which of the three was a neighbor to the man attacked by robbers?” ❓
## Luke 10:37
The law expert answered, “The one who showed mercy.” Jesus said, “Go and do the same.” ❤️
## Luke 10:38
As they traveled, Jesus entered a village. A woman named Martha welcomed Him into her home. 📖
## Luke 10:39
Her sister Mary sat at the Lord’s feet, listening to His message. 📖
## Luke 10:40
The preparations distracted Martha. She said, “Lord, do You not care that my sister left me serving alone? Tell her to help!” 📖
## Luke 10:41
The Lord answered, “Martha, Martha, many things worry and upset you. 📖
## Luke 10:42
Only one thing is necessary. Mary chose the good portion. It will not be taken from her.” 📖
