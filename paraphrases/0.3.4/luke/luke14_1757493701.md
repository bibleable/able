## Luke 14:1
One Sabbath Jesus ate at a leading Pharisee’s home. Those present watched Him closely. 📖
## Luke 14:2
A man with dropsy was before Him. 📖
## Luke 14:3
Jesus asked the law experts and Pharisees, “Does the Law permit healing on the Sabbath?” ❓
## Luke 14:4
They remained silent. He took hold of the man, healed him, and sent him away. ❤️
## Luke 14:5
He asked, “If your son or ox fell into a pit on the Sabbath, would you not pull him out immediately?” ❓
## Luke 14:6
They could not answer His questions. 📖
## Luke 14:7
He saw guests choosing places of honor and told them a parable: 📖
## Luke 14:8
“At a wedding banquet, do not take the place of honor. A more distinguished guest may have been invited. 📖
## Luke 14:9
Your host will say, ‘Give him your seat.’ Then you must take the last place in humiliation. 📖
## Luke 14:10
Instead, take the last place. Your host will say, ‘Friend, move to a better place.’ Then everyone at the table will see you honored. 📖
## Luke 14:11
Everyone who exalts himself will be humbled. Whoever humbles himself will be exalted.” 📖
## Luke 14:12
He told His host, “Do not invite your friends, brothers, relatives, or rich neighbors to your dinners or banquets. They may invite you back and repay you. 📖
## Luke 14:13
Invite the poor, crippled, lame, and blind to your banquet. 👥
## Luke 14:14
You will be blessed because they cannot repay you. You will be repaid at the resurrection of the righteous.” 📖
## Luke 14:15
A man at the table said, “Blessed is everyone who will eat at the feast in the kingdom of God.” 📖
## Luke 14:16
Jesus replied, “A man prepared a great banquet and invited many. 📖
## Luke 14:17
At banquet time, he sent his servant to tell them, ‘Come, everything is ready.’ 📖
## Luke 14:18
They all made excuses. The first said, ‘I bought a field and must see it. Please excuse me.’ 📖
## Luke 14:19
Another said, ‘I bought five pairs of oxen and am going to test them. Please excuse me.’ 📖
## Luke 14:20
Another said, ‘I married a wife, so I cannot come.’ 📖
## Luke 14:21
The servant reported this to his master. The angry master said, ‘Quickly go into the city’s streets and alleys. Bring the poor, crippled, blind, and lame.’ 📖
## Luke 14:22
The servant said, ‘Sir, I did as you ordered. There is still room.’ 📖
## Luke 14:23
The master said, ‘Go to the highways and hedges. Compel people to come so my house will be full. 📖
## Luke 14:24
None of those men invited will taste my banquet.’” 📖
## Luke 14:25
Large crowds traveled with Jesus. He turned and said, 👥
## Luke 14:26
“Anyone who comes to Me must hate his father, mother, wife, children, brothers, sisters, and even his own life to be My disciple. 📖
## Luke 14:27
Whoever does not carry his cross and follow Me cannot be My disciple. 📖
## Luke 14:28
Before building a tower, would you not sit and calculate its cost to see whether you can finish it? ❓
## Luke 14:29
Otherwise, you may lay the foundation but fail to finish. Everyone who sees will mock you, 📖
## Luke 14:30
saying, ‘This man could not finish what he began building.’ 📖
## Luke 14:31
Before war, a king sits and considers his strength. Can his ten thousand men fight the twenty thousand coming against him? ❓
## Luke 14:32
If he cannot, he sends a delegation to request peace terms while the other king is still far away. 📖
## Luke 14:33
Likewise, you cannot be My disciple unless you give up everything you have. 📖
## Luke 14:34
Salt is good. But if it loses its flavor, how can it be seasoned? ❓
## Luke 14:35
It is useless for soil and manure piles, so it is thrown away. Let anyone with ears to hear listen.” 📖
