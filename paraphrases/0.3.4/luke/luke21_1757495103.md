## Luke 21:1
Jesus looked up and saw rich people putting gifts into the treasury. 📖
## Luke 21:2
He saw a poor widow put in two small copper coins. 📖
## Luke 21:3
He said, “Truly, this poor widow gave more than all the others. 📖
## Luke 21:4
They gave from their surplus. In her poverty, she gave everything she had to live on.” 📖
## Luke 21:5
Some disciples spoke about the temple’s beautiful stones and consecrated gifts. Jesus said, 📖
## Luke 21:6
“A time will come when no stone you see here remains on another. Every stone will be thrown down.” 📖
## Luke 21:7
They asked, “Teacher, when will this happen? What sign will show it is about to happen?” ❓
## Luke 21:8
He answered, “Do not be deceived. Many will come in My name, saying, ‘I am He,’ and, ‘The time is near.’ Do not follow them. 📖
## Luke 21:9
Do not be alarmed by wars and rebellions. These must happen first, but the end will not follow immediately.” 📖
## Luke 21:10
He said, “Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. 📖
## Luke 21:11
Great earthquakes, famines, and pestilences will occur in various places. There will be fearful sights and great signs from heaven. 📖
## Luke 21:12
Before all this, people will seize and persecute you. Because of My name, they will deliver you to synagogues and prisons and bring you before kings and governors. 📖
## Luke 21:13
This will give you an opportunity to testify. 📖
## Luke 21:14
Decide not to worry beforehand about your defense. 📖
## Luke 21:15
I will give you speech and wisdom that no opponent can resist or contradict. 📖
## Luke 21:16
Even parents, brothers, relatives, and friends will betray you. Some of you will be killed. 📖
## Luke 21:17
Everyone will hate you because of My name. 📖
## Luke 21:18
Yet not one hair of your head will perish. 📖
## Luke 21:19
By patiently enduring, you will gain your souls. 📖
## Luke 21:20
When armies surround Jerusalem, know that its desolation is near. 📖
## Luke 21:21
Those in Judea must flee to the mountains. Those in the city must leave. Those in the countryside must stay out. 📖
## Luke 21:22
These are days of vengeance to fulfill everything written. 📖
## Luke 21:23
How miserable those days will be for pregnant and nursing mothers! Great distress will strike the land, and wrath will come against this people. 📖
## Luke 21:24
They will die by the sword and be taken captive into all nations. Gentiles will trample Jerusalem until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled. 📖
## Luke 21:25
Signs will appear in the sun, moon, and stars. Earth’s nations will be distressed and bewildered by roaring seas and surging waves. 🌊
## Luke 21:26
People will faint with fear and anxiety about what is coming on earth, because heaven’s powers will be shaken. 📖
## Luke 21:27
Then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory. ✨
## Luke 21:28
When this begins, stand and raise your heads because your redemption is near.” 📖
## Luke 21:29
He told a parable: “Look at the fig tree and all trees. 🌳
## Luke 21:30
When they sprout leaves, you know summer is near. 🌳
## Luke 21:31
Likewise, when these things happen, know that the kingdom of God is near. 📖
## Luke 21:32
Truly, this generation will not pass away until all this happens. 📖
## Luke 21:33
Heaven and earth will pass away. My words will never pass away. 📖
## Luke 21:34
Watch yourselves. Wild living, drunkenness, and life’s worries may burden your hearts. That day may suddenly catch you like a trap. 📖
## Luke 21:35
It will come upon everyone living everywhere on earth. 📖
## Luke 21:36
Always keep watch. Pray for strength to escape everything about to happen and to stand before the Son of Man.” 🙏
## Luke 21:37
Jesus taught at the temple daily. Each evening He spent the night on the Mount of Olives. 📖
## Luke 21:38
All the people came early each morning to hear Him at the temple. 👥
