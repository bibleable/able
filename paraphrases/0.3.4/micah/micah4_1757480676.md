## Micah 4:1
In the last days, the mountain of the LORD’s house will stand as the chief of mountains. It will rise above the hills, and peoples will stream to it. ⛰️
## Micah 4:2
Many nations will say, “Come to the LORD’s mountain, to the house of Jacob’s God. He will teach us His ways so we can walk His paths.” The law will go from Zion, and the LORD’s word from Jerusalem. 📖
## Micah 4:3
He will judge between many peoples and settle disputes for strong nations far away. They will hammer swords into plow blades and spears into tools for pruning. Nations will no longer fight each other or train for war. 🕊️
## Micah 4:4
Everyone will sit under his own vine and fig tree, with no one to frighten him. The LORD of Hosts has spoken. 🌳
## Micah 4:5
Other nations may walk in their gods’ names. But we will walk in the name of the LORD our God forever and ever. 👣
## Micah 4:6
The LORD declares, “On that day, I will gather the lame and the outcasts, even those I afflicted. 🤝
## Micah 4:7
I will make the lame a surviving people and the outcasts a strong nation. The LORD will rule over them on Mount Zion from that day forever. 👑
## Micah 4:8
Watchtower of the flock, stronghold of the Daughter of Zion, your former rule will return. Royal power will come to the Daughter of Jerusalem.” 🏰
## Micah 4:9
Why do you cry aloud? Have you no king? Has your counselor died, so that pain grips you like a woman in labor? 💔
## Micah 4:10
Daughter of Zion, twist in pain like a woman in labor. You will leave the city, camp in open fields, and go to Babylon. There you will be rescued. The LORD will redeem you there from your enemies! 🛡️
## Micah 4:11
Many nations now gather against you. They say, “Let her be made unclean. Let us feast our eyes on Zion.” ⚠️
## Micah 4:12
They do not know the LORD’s thoughts or understand His plan. He gathered them like bundles of grain on a threshing floor. 🌾
## Micah 4:13
Daughter of Zion, rise and thresh! I will give you iron horns and bronze hooves to crush many peoples. You will give their gains to the LORD and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth. ⚖️
