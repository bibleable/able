## Micah 2:1
How terrible for those who plan sin and evil in bed! At dawn, they carry it out because they have the power. ⚠️
## Micah 2:2
They desire fields and seize them. They take houses and rob people of their homes and inheritance. ⛔
## Micah 2:3
The LORD says, “I plan a disaster against this nation that you cannot pull your necks free from. You will no longer walk proudly, because it will be a time of disaster. ⚖️
## Micah 2:4
People will mock you with a saying and this bitter lament: ‘We are completely ruined! He changed my people’s portion. He took it from me and gave our fields to traitors.’” 😢
## Micah 2:5
So you will have no one in the LORD’s assembly to divide the land by lot. 📜
## Micah 2:6
They preach, “Do not preach! Do not preach these things. Disgrace will not overtake us.” ⚠️
## Micah 2:7
House of Jacob, should you ask, “Is the LORD’s Spirit impatient? Does He do such things?” Do My words not bring good to those who live rightly? 📖
## Micah 2:8
Recently, My people rose like an enemy. You strip fine robes from unsuspecting travelers, like men returning from battle. ⛔
## Micah 2:9
You drive the women of My people out of their pleasant homes. You take My blessing from their children forever. 💔
## Micah 2:10
Get up and leave! This is not your place of rest. Its uncleanness brings terrible destruction. ⚠️
## Micah 2:11
Suppose an empty talker falsely says, “I will preach to you about wine and strong drink.” He would be just the preacher these people want! 🍷
## Micah 2:12
I will surely gather all of you, Jacob, and collect Israel’s remaining people. I will bring them together like sheep in a pen or a flock in its pasture. They will be a noisy crowd. 🐑
## Micah 2:13
One who opens the way will go ahead of them. They will break through the gate and leave through it. Their King will go before them, with the LORD as their leader. 👑
