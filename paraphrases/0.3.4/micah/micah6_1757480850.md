## Micah 6:1
Hear what the LORD says: “Get up and argue your case before the mountains. Let the hills hear your voice. ⚖️
## Micah 6:2
Mountains and lasting foundations of the earth, hear the LORD’s accusation. He has a case against His people and will argue against Israel. ⛰️
## Micah 6:3
‘My people, what have I done to you? Tell Me how I have tired you! 💬
## Micah 6:4
I brought you from Egypt and freed you from slavery’s house. I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam ahead of you. 👣
## Micah 6:5
Remember what Balak king of Moab planned and what Balaam son of Beor answered. Remember your journey from Shittim to Gilgal. Then you can acknowledge the LORD’s righteousness.’” 💭
## Micah 6:6
What should I bring when I bow before the LORD, the God on high? Should I bring burnt offerings and calves a year old? 🙏
## Micah 6:7
Would thousands of rams or ten thousand rivers of oil please the LORD? Should I give my firstborn child, the fruit of my body, for my soul’s sin? 💬
## Micah 6:8
He has shown you what is good. What does the LORD require? Act justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with your God. 👣
## Micah 6:9
The LORD calls to the city. It is wise to fear Your name. “Pay attention to the rod and the One who appointed it. 📣
## Micah 6:10
Wicked household, can I keep ignoring your wicked treasures and your cursed short ephah? ⚖️

*Word help: An ephah is a measure for grain. A short measure cheats the buyer.*
## Micah 6:11
Can I excuse dishonest scales and bags of false weights? ⚖️
## Micah 6:12
The city’s rich people are full of violence. Its people speak lies with deceitful tongues. ⛔
## Micah 6:13
So I strike you severely and ruin you because of your sins. ⚖️
## Micah 6:14
You will eat but remain hungry. You will not preserve what you acquire. What you save, I will give to the sword. 🍞
## Micah 6:15
You will plant but not harvest. You will press olives but not use the oil on yourselves. You will tread grapes but not drink the wine. 🌾
## Micah 6:16
You followed Omri’s rules, the practices of Ahab’s house, and their advice. So I will ruin you and make your people objects of contempt. You will bear the nations’ scorn.” ⚖️
