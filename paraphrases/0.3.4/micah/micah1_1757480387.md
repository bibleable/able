## Micah 1:1
The LORD gave this message to Micah from Moresheth during the reigns of Judah’s kings Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah. This is what Micah saw about Samaria and Jerusalem. 📣
## Micah 1:2
All peoples, hear! Earth and everyone in it, listen! May the Lord GOD testify against you from His holy temple. ⚖️
## Micah 1:3
Look, the LORD leaves His home. He will come down and walk on the earth’s high places. ⛰️
## Micah 1:4
The mountains will melt under Him, and the valleys will split apart. They will be like wax before fire and water rushing down a slope. 🔥
## Micah 1:5
All this happens because of Jacob’s rebellion and Israel’s sins. What is Jacob’s rebellion? Is it not Samaria? What is Judah’s high place? Is it not Jerusalem? ⚠️
## Micah 1:6
I will make Samaria a pile of rubble in a field, a place for a vineyard. I will pour her stones into the valley and expose her foundations. 🏚️
## Micah 1:7
All her carved images will be smashed, and all her earnings will burn. I will destroy all her idols. She gathered a prostitute’s wages, and they will be used on a prostitute again. ⛔
## Micah 1:8
Because of this, I will mourn and wail. I will walk barefoot and naked. I will howl like a jackal and mourn like an ostrich. 😢
## Micah 1:9
Her wound cannot be healed. It reached Judah and came to my people’s gate, to Jerusalem itself. 💔
## Micah 1:10
Do not tell this in Gath. Do not cry at all. Roll in the dust in Beth-leaphrah. 😢
## Micah 1:11
People of Shaphir, leave in shameful nakedness. The people of Zaanan will not come out. Beth-ezel mourns, and its support is taken from you. 💔
## Micah 1:12
The people of Maroth longed for good. But disaster came from the LORD, even to Jerusalem’s gate. ⚖️
## Micah 1:13
People of Lachish, harness your chariot horses. You began Zion’s sin, because Israel’s rebellions were found in you. 🐎
## Micah 1:14
Send farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath. The houses of Achzib will deceive Israel’s kings. 🎁
## Micah 1:15
People of Mareshah, I will again bring a conqueror against you. Israel’s glory will come to Adullam. ⚔️
## Micah 1:16
Shave your heads and cut off your hair in grief for your precious children. Make yourselves bald as an eagle, because your children will leave you for exile. 😢
