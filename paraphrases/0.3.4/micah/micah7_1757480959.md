## Micah 7:1
How terrible for me! I am like someone collecting summer fruit after the grape harvest. There is no bunch of grapes to eat or early fig I desire. 🌾
## Micah 7:2
Those who honor God have disappeared from the earth. No one is upright. Everyone waits to shed blood and hunts others with a net. ⛔
## Micah 7:3
Both hands are skilled at evil. Princes and judges demand bribes. Powerful people speak their evil desires, and all work together to carry them out. ⚠️
## Micah 7:4
The best of them is like a thorn bush, and the most upright is sharper than a thorn hedge. The day for your watchmen has come, the day of your visitation. Now they will be confused. ⚖️
## Micah 7:5
Do not rely on a friend or trust a companion. Guard your words even from the woman who lies in your arms. ⚠️
## Micah 7:6
A son dishonors his father, a daughter opposes her mother, and a daughter-in-law opposes her mother-in-law. A person’s enemies are his own household members. 💔
## Micah 7:7
But I will look to the LORD and wait for the God who saves me. My God will hear me. 🙏
## Micah 7:8
My enemy, do not rejoice over me! Though I fell, I will rise. Though I sit in darkness, the LORD will be my light. 🌅
## Micah 7:9
I sinned against Him, so I must bear the LORD’s anger until He argues my case and gives me justice. He will bring me into the light, and I will see His righteousness. ⚖️
## Micah 7:10
My enemy will see and be covered with shame. She asked, “Where is the LORD your God?” I will see her trampled like mud in the streets. ⚖️
## Micah 7:11
The day will come to rebuild your walls and extend your boundary. 🏘️
## Micah 7:12
People will come to you from Assyria and Egypt’s cities, from Egypt to the Euphrates. They will come from sea to sea and mountain to mountain. 🌍
## Micah 7:13
The earth will become a wasteland because of its people, as the result of their actions. ⚖️
## Micah 7:14
Shepherd Your people with Your staff, the flock of Your inheritance. They live alone in a woodland surrounded by pastures. Let them graze in Bashan and Gilead as in earlier days. 🐑
## Micah 7:15
I will show My wonders as in the days when you left Egypt. ✨
## Micah 7:16
Nations will see and be ashamed, without their might. They will cover their mouths with their hands, and their ears will become deaf. ⚖️
## Micah 7:17
They will lick dust like snakes and reptiles that crawl on the ground. They will come trembling from their strongholds before the LORD our God. They will tremble in fear of You. 🐍
## Micah 7:18
Who is a God like You? You forgive sin and pass over the rebellion of the remaining people of Your inheritance. You do not stay angry forever, because You delight in faithful love. ❤️
## Micah 7:19
He will show us mercy again and defeat our sins. You will throw all our sins into the deep sea. 🌊
## Micah 7:20
You will show faithfulness to Jacob and faithful love to Abraham. You swore this to our ancestors long ago. ❤️
