## Micah 5:1
Daughter of troops, gather your troops! An army surrounds us. They will strike Israel’s judge on the cheek with a rod. ⚔️
## Micah 5:2
Bethlehem Ephrathah, you are small among Judah’s clans. But from you, One will come for Me to rule Israel. His origins are from long ago, from the days of eternity. 👑
## Micah 5:3
Israel will be abandoned until the woman in labor gives birth. Then the rest of His brothers will return to the children of Israel. 🌅
## Micah 5:4
He will stand and shepherd His flock with the LORD’s strength and the majesty of the name of the LORD His God. They will live safely, because His greatness will reach the earth’s ends. 🐑
## Micah 5:5
He will be our peace when Assyria invades our land and tramples our strongholds. We will raise seven shepherds, even eight leaders, against it. 🛡️
## Micah 5:6
They will rule Assyria with the sword and Nimrod’s land with a drawn blade. He will rescue us when Assyria invades our land and crosses our borders. ⚔️
## Micah 5:7
Jacob’s remaining people will live among many peoples like dew from the LORD and showers on grass. These do not wait for human beings. 🌧️
## Micah 5:8
Jacob’s remaining people will live among nations like a lion among forest animals or a young lion among sheep. It tramples and tears as it passes, and no one can rescue them. 🦁
## Micah 5:9
Your hand will rise over your foes. All your enemies will be cut off. ⚖️
## Micah 5:10
The LORD declares, “On that day, I will remove your horses and destroy your chariots. 🐎
## Micah 5:11
I will remove your cities and tear down all your strongholds. 🏚️
## Micah 5:12
I will remove sorcery from your hands. You will have no fortune-tellers. ⛔
## Micah 5:13
I will remove your carved images and sacred pillars. You will no longer bow to things your own hands made. ⛔
## Micah 5:14
I will uproot your Asherah poles and destroy your cities. ⚖️
## Micah 5:15
In anger and wrath, I will take revenge on the nations that disobeyed Me.” ⚖️
