## 1 John 5:1
Everyone who believes Jesus is the Christ has been born of God. Everyone who loves the Father also loves those born of Him. ❤️
## 1 John 5:2
We know we love God’s children when we love God and obey His commands. 👣
## 1 John 5:3
Love for God means obeying His commands. His commands are not a heavy burden. ❤️
## 1 John 5:4
Everyone born of God overcomes the world. Our faith is the victory that has overcome the world. 🛡️
## 1 John 5:5
Who overcomes the world? Only the person who believes that Jesus is God’s Son. 💪
## 1 John 5:6
Jesus Christ came by water and blood. He came by both, rather than water alone. The Spirit testifies to this, because the Spirit is the truth. 🕊️
## 1 John 5:7
Three witnesses testify: 📣
## 1 John 5:8
They are the Spirit, the water, and the blood. These three agree. 🕊️
## 1 John 5:9
We accept human testimony, but God’s testimony is greater. This is the testimony God gave about His Son. 📖
## 1 John 5:10
Anyone who believes in God’s Son has this testimony within him. Anyone who disbelieves God calls Him a liar, because he rejected God’s testimony about His Son. ⚠️
## 1 John 5:11
This is the testimony: God gave us eternal life, and this life is in His Son. 🌅
## 1 John 5:12
Anyone who has the Son has life. Anyone who does not have God’s Son does not have life. ✝️
## 1 John 5:13
I wrote these things to you who believe in the name of God’s Son. I want you to know that you have eternal life. 🌅
## 1 John 5:14
We have this confidence before Him: If we ask for anything according to His will, He hears us. 🙏
## 1 John 5:15
If we know He hears whatever we ask, we know we already have what we asked Him for. 🙏
## 1 John 5:16
If you see your brother commit a sin that does not lead to death, ask God for him. God will give life to those who commit this kind of sin. There is a sin that leads to death. I do not say to ask about that sin. 🙏
## 1 John 5:17
All unrighteousness is sin. Yet some sin does not lead to death. ⚠️
## 1 John 5:18
We know that anyone born of God does not continue to sin. The One born of God protects him, and the evil one cannot touch him. 🛡️
## 1 John 5:19
We know we belong to God. The whole world is under the evil one’s power. 🌍
## 1 John 5:20
God’s Son has come and given us understanding so that we can know the One who is true. We are in the One who is true, in His Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life. 🌅
## 1 John 5:21
Little children, keep yourselves away from idols. ⛔
