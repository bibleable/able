## 1 John 2:1
My little children, I write these things so that you will not sin. But if anyone sins, Jesus Christ the Righteous One speaks for us before the Father. ❤️
## 1 John 2:2
He Himself is the atoning sacrifice for our sins. He is also the sacrifice for the sins of the whole world. ✝️
## 1 John 2:3
We can be sure that we know Him if we obey His commands. 👣
## 1 John 2:4
Anyone who says, “I know Him,” but disobeys His commands is a liar. The truth is not in that person. ⚠️
## 1 John 2:5
But God’s love is truly made complete in anyone who obeys His word. This is how we know we are in Him. ❤️
## 1 John 2:6
Anyone who claims to remain in Him must live as Jesus lived. 👣
## 1 John 2:7
Dear friends, I write an old command that you have had from the beginning. It is not a new command. It is the message you heard. 📖
## 1 John 2:8
Yet I also write a new command, which is true in Him and in you. The darkness is passing away, and the true light already shines. ✨
## 1 John 2:9
Anyone who claims to be in the light but hates his brother is still in darkness. ⚠️
## 1 John 2:10
Anyone who loves his brother remains in the light. Nothing in him causes stumbling. ❤️
## 1 John 2:11
Anyone who hates his brother is in darkness and walks in it. He does not know where he goes, because darkness has blinded his eyes. 🌑
## 1 John 2:12
Little children, I write to you because your sins have been forgiven through His name. ❤️
## 1 John 2:13
Fathers, I write because you know Him who is from the beginning. Young men, I write because you have overcome the evil one. Children, I have written because you know the Father. 💪
## 1 John 2:14
Fathers, I have written because you know Him who is from the beginning. Young men, I have written because you are strong. God’s word remains in you, and you have overcome the evil one. 🛡️
## 1 John 2:15
Do not love the world or anything in it. Anyone who loves the world does not have the Father’s love in him. ⚠️
## 1 John 2:16
The world contains sinful physical desires, desires for what the eyes see, and pride in life. These come from the world, rather than the Father. ⛔
## 1 John 2:17
The world and its desires pass away. But anyone who does God’s will remains forever. 🌅
## 1 John 2:18
Children, this is the last hour. You heard that the antichrist is coming, and many antichrists have now appeared. This tells us it is the last hour. ⚠️
## 1 John 2:19
They left us because they did not belong to us. If they had belonged, they would have stayed. Their departure showed that none of them belonged to us. 👣
## 1 John 2:20
But you have an anointing from the Holy One. All of you know the truth. 🕊️
## 1 John 2:21
I wrote because you know the truth, rather than because you do not know it. No lie comes from the truth. 📖
## 1 John 2:22
Who is the liar? It is the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ. This is the antichrist, who denies the Father and the Son. ⛔
## 1 John 2:23
Anyone who denies the Son does not have the Father. Anyone who acknowledges the Son has the Father too. ❤️
## 1 John 2:24
Let the message you heard from the beginning remain in you. If it does, you too will remain in the Son and the Father. 📖
## 1 John 2:25
He Himself promised us eternal life. 🌅
## 1 John 2:26
I wrote these things about those who try to deceive you. 🛡️
## 1 John 2:27
The anointing you received from Him remains in you. You do not need anyone to teach you. His anointing is true and genuine, and it teaches you about everything. Remain in Him as it taught you. 🕊️
## 1 John 2:28
Little children, remain in Christ. Then, when He appears, we can have confidence and stand without shame before Him at His return. 🌅
## 1 John 2:29
You know He is righteous. So you also know that everyone who practices righteousness has been born of Him. 🌱
