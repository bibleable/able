## 1 John 4:1
Dear friends, do not believe every spirit. Test the spirits to see if they come from God. Many false prophets have gone into the world. 🛡️
## 1 John 4:2
This is how you recognize God’s Spirit. Every spirit that acknowledges Jesus Christ came in the flesh is from God. 🕊️
## 1 John 4:3
Every spirit that does not acknowledge Jesus is not from God. This is the spirit of the antichrist. You heard it would come, and it is already in the world. ⚠️
## 1 John 4:4
Little children, you belong to God and have overcome them. The One in you is greater than the one in the world. 🛡️
## 1 John 4:5
They belong to the world. They speak from its point of view, and the world listens to them. 🌍
## 1 John 4:6
We belong to God. Anyone who knows God listens to us. Anyone who does not belong to God does not listen. This is how we recognize the Spirit of truth and the spirit of deception. 📖
## 1 John 4:7
Dear friends, let us love each other, because love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows Him. ❤️
## 1 John 4:8
Anyone who does not love does not know God, because God is love. ❤️
## 1 John 4:9
God showed His love among us by sending His one and only Son into the world. He did this so that we could live through Him. ✝️
## 1 John 4:10
This is love: God loved us and sent His Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins. It did not begin with our love for God. ❤️
## 1 John 4:11
Dear friends, since God loved us this way, we too must love each other. ❤️
## 1 John 4:12
No one has ever seen God. If we love each other, God remains in us, and His love is made complete in us. ❤️
## 1 John 4:13
We know that we remain in Him and He remains in us because He gave us of His Spirit. 🕊️
## 1 John 4:14
We have seen and testify that the Father sent His Son to be the world’s Savior. 📣
## 1 John 4:15
If anyone acknowledges that Jesus is God’s Son, God remains in him, and he remains in God. ❤️
## 1 John 4:16
We have come to know and trust God’s love for us. God is love. Anyone who remains in love remains in God, and God remains in him. ❤️
## 1 John 4:17
In this way, love has been made complete among us, so that we can have confidence on the day of judgment. In this world, we are like Him. 🕊️
## 1 John 4:18
There is no fear in love. Perfect love drives fear away, because fear concerns punishment. Anyone who fears has not been made complete in love. ❤️
## 1 John 4:19
We love because He loved us first. ❤️
## 1 John 4:20
Anyone who says, “I love God,” but hates his brother is a liar. Anyone who does not love the brother he has seen cannot love the God he has not seen. ⚠️
## 1 John 4:21
He gave us this command: Anyone who loves God must also love his brother. ❤️
