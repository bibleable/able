## 1 John 1:1
We announce the Word of life, who was from the beginning. We heard Him, saw Him with our own eyes, watched Him, and touched Him with our own hands. 👀
## 1 John 1:2
This life was revealed to us. We saw it and testify about it. We announce to you the eternal life that was with the Father and was revealed to us. 🌅
## 1 John 1:3
We announce what we saw and heard, so that you can share fellowship with us. Our fellowship is with the Father and His Son, Jesus Christ. 🤝
## 1 John 1:4
We write these things so that our joy can be complete. 😊
## 1 John 1:5
This is the message we heard from Him and announce to you: God is light. There is no darkness at all in Him. ✨
## 1 John 1:6
If we claim fellowship with Him but walk in darkness, we lie and do not live by the truth. ⚠️
## 1 John 1:7
If we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with each other. The blood of Jesus His Son cleanses us from every sin. ✝️
## 1 John 1:8
If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves. The truth is not in us. ⚠️
## 1 John 1:9
If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just. He will forgive our sins and cleanse us from all unrighteousness. ❤️
## 1 John 1:10
If we say we have not sinned, we call Him a liar. His word is not in us. ⛔
