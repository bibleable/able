## 1 John 3:1
Look at the love the Father gave us! We are called God’s children, and that is what we are. The world does not know us because it did not know Him. ❤️
## 1 John 3:2
Dear friends, we are God’s children now. What we will become has not yet been revealed. We know that when Christ appears, we will be like Him, because we will see Him as He is. 🌅
## 1 John 3:3
Everyone who has this hope in Him purifies himself, just as Christ is pure. ✨
## 1 John 3:4
Everyone who practices sin also practices lawlessness. Sin is lawlessness. ⛔
## 1 John 3:5
You know that Christ appeared to take away sins. There is no sin in Him. ✝️
## 1 John 3:6
No one who remains in Him continues to sin. Anyone who continues to sin has neither seen Him nor known Him. ⚠️
## 1 John 3:7
Little children, let no one deceive you. Anyone who practices righteousness is righteous, just as Christ is righteous. 👣
## 1 John 3:8
Anyone who practices sin belongs to the devil, who has sinned from the beginning. The Son of God appeared to destroy the devil’s works. 🛡️
## 1 John 3:9
Anyone born of God refuses to practice sin, because God’s seed remains in him. He cannot continue to sin, because he has been born of God. 🌱
## 1 John 3:10
This shows the difference between God’s children and the devil’s children. Anyone who does not practice righteousness does not belong to God. Neither does anyone who does not love his brother. ❤️
## 1 John 3:11
This is the message you heard from the beginning: We must love each other. ❤️
## 1 John 3:12
Do not be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one and murdered his brother. Why did he kill him? His own actions were evil, but his brother’s were righteous. ⚠️
## 1 John 3:13
Brothers, do not be surprised if the world hates you. 🛡️
## 1 John 3:14
We know we passed from death to life because we love our brothers. Anyone who does not love remains in death. ❤️
## 1 John 3:15
Anyone who hates his brother is a murderer. You know that eternal life does not remain in a murderer. ⛔
## 1 John 3:16
We know love because Jesus gave His life for us. We too must give our lives for our brothers. ✝️
## 1 John 3:17
Suppose someone has possessions and sees his brother in need but refuses to show mercy. How can God’s love remain in him? ❤️
## 1 John 3:18
Little children, love with actions and truth, rather than only words and speech. 👣
## 1 John 3:19
This is how we will know that we belong to the truth. It will reassure our hearts before Him. 🕊️
## 1 John 3:20
Even if our hearts condemn us, God is greater than our hearts. He knows everything. ❤️
## 1 John 3:21
Dear friends, if our hearts do not condemn us, we have confidence before God. 🙏
## 1 John 3:22
We receive whatever we ask from Him because we obey His commands and do what pleases Him. 🙏
## 1 John 3:23
His command is to believe in the name of His Son, Jesus Christ, and love each other, as He commanded. ❤️
## 1 John 3:24
Anyone who obeys His commands remains in God, and God remains in him. We know He remains in us through the Spirit He gave us. 🕊️
