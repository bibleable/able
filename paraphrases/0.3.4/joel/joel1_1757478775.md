## Joel 1:1
The LORD gave this message to Joel, Pethuel’s son. 📣
## Joel 1:2
Elders, hear this! Everyone in the land, listen. Did anything like this happen in your life or your ancestors’ lives? 👂
## Joel 1:3
Tell your children. Let them tell their children. Let those children tell the next generation. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦📣
## Joel 1:4
The swarming locust ate what the devouring locust left. The young locust ate what the swarming locust left. The destroying locust ate what the young locust left. 🌾
## Joel 1:5
People who drink too much wine, wake up and cry! All wine drinkers, cry loudly. You have no sweet wine to drink. 😢
## Joel 1:6
A powerful nation invades My land. Its people are too many to count. Its teeth are like a lion’s teeth. Its sharp teeth are like a lioness’s. 🦁
## Joel 1:7
It ruins My grapevine and breaks My fig tree. It strips the bark and throws it away. The branches turn white. 🌳
## Joel 1:8
Cry like a young woman who never had sex. She wears rough clothes of grief for the husband of her youth. 💔
## Joel 1:9
The LORD’s house has no grain or drink offerings. The priests who serve the LORD grieve. 🏠😢
## Joel 1:10
The fields are ruined, and the land grieves. The grain is gone. The new wine dries up, and the oil fails. 🌾
## Joel 1:11
Farmers, feel great sorrow! People who care for vines, cry over the wheat and barley. The field’s harvest is gone. 😢🌾
## Joel 1:12
The grapevine dries up, and the fig tree fades. Pomegranate, palm, and apple trees fade. All the orchard’s trees fade. People’s joy dries up too. 🌳💔
## Joel 1:13
Priests who serve at the altar, wear rough clothes and cry. Servants of my God, spend the night in those clothes. Your God’s house has no grain or drink offerings. 🙏😢
## Joel 1:14
Set apart a time without food to seek God. Call a holy meeting. Gather the elders and everyone in the land at the LORD your God’s house. Cry to the LORD. 👥🙏
## Joel 1:15
How terrible that day will be! The Day of the LORD is near. Destruction will come from the Almighty. ⚖️
## Joel 1:16
We see our food disappear before our eyes. Joy and gladness leave our God’s house. 💔
## Joel 1:17
Seeds dry up under the lumps of soil. Storehouses lie in ruins. Grain stores fall apart because the grain dries up. 🏚️
## Joel 1:18
The cattle cry in pain! Herds wander in confusion because there is no grass. Even the sheep suffer. 🐂🐑
## Joel 1:19
LORD, I call to You. Fire burns the open fields of grass. Flames burn all the field’s trees. 🔥🙏
## Joel 1:20
Even wild animals long for You. The streams dry up. Fire burns the open fields of grass. 💧🔥
