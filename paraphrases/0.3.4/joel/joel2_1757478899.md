## Joel 2:1
Blow the ram’s horn in Zion. Sound the alarm on My holy mountain! Let everyone in the land tremble. The Day of the LORD comes near. 📯
## Joel 2:2
It is a day of darkness, clouds, and gloom. A great, strong army spreads over the mountains like dawn. No army like it existed long ago. No army like it will exist in ages to come. ⛰️🌑
## Joel 2:3
Fire burns before them, and flames burn behind them. Before them, the land is like the Garden of Eden. Behind them, it is an empty desert. Nothing escapes them. 🔥🏜️
## Joel 2:4
They look like horses. They run like fast horses. 🐎
## Joel 2:5
They jump over mountain peaks with the sound of chariots. They sound like fire that burns dry stalks. They are like a powerful army ready for battle. ⛰️🔥
## Joel 2:6
Nations twist in terror before them. Every face turns pale. 😨
## Joel 2:7
They charge like powerful soldiers. They climb walls like men of war. Each keeps his place and stays on his path. 🧱
## Joel 2:8
They do not push one another. Each stays on his own path. They break through defenses without leaving their lines. 🛤️
## Joel 2:9
They rush into the city and run along the wall. They climb into houses. They enter through windows like thieves. 🏙️
## Joel 2:10
The earth and heavens shake before them. The sun and moon grow dark. The stars lose their light. 🌍🌑
## Joel 2:11
The LORD raises His voice before His army. His camp is very large. Those who obey His command are powerful. The Day of the LORD is great and very terrible. Who can survive it? ⚖️
## Joel 2:12
The LORD says, “Even now, return to Me with all your heart. Go without food. Cry and grieve.” ❤️🙏
## Joel 2:13
Tear your hearts in sorrow, not just your clothes. Return to the LORD your God. He is kind and caring. He does not become angry quickly, and His faithful love is great. He holds back disaster. 💔❤️
## Joel 2:14
Who knows? He may turn and hold back punishment. He may leave good gifts behind, grain and drink offerings for the LORD your God. 🙏
## Joel 2:15
Blow the ram’s horn in Zion. Set apart a time without food to seek God. Call a holy meeting. 📯👥
## Joel 2:16
Gather the people and set the group apart for God. Gather old people and children, even babies who drink their mothers’ milk. Let bridegrooms and brides leave their rooms. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Joel 2:17
Let the LORD’s priests cry between the entrance porch and the altar. Let them say, “LORD, spare Your people. Do not let the nations mock the people who belong to You. Why must they ask, ‘Where is their God?’” 🙏
## Joel 2:18
Then the LORD showed strong concern for His land. He spared His people. ❤️
## Joel 2:19
He answered them, “I will send grain, new wine, and oil. You will have enough. I will never again let the nations mock you. 🌾
## Joel 2:20
I will drive the northern army away into an empty, dry land. Its front will enter the Eastern Sea and its rear the Western Sea. Its terrible smell will rise.” He does great things. 🌊
## Joel 2:21
Land, do not fear! Celebrate and be glad. The LORD does great things. 😊
## Joel 2:22
Wild animals, do not fear. The fields of grass turn green. Trees give fruit. Fig trees and vines give their best crops. 🌿
## Joel 2:23
Zion’s children, be glad in the LORD your God. He gives autumn rain for your vindication. He sends autumn and spring rain as before. *Word help: Vindication shows that someone’s cause is right.* 🌧️
## Joel 2:24
The places where people separate grain will be full. The containers will overflow with new wine and oil. 🌾
## Joel 2:25
God says, “I will repay the years the locusts ate. These were the swarming, young, destroying, and devouring locusts. They were My great army that I sent against you. 🌾
## Joel 2:26
You will eat plenty and have enough. You will praise the LORD your God, who does wonderful things for you. My people will never again face shame. 🙌
## Joel 2:27
You will know that I am among Israel. I am the LORD your God, and there is no other. My people will never again face shame. ❤️
## Joel 2:28
Afterward, I will pour out My Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will speak God’s messages. Your old men will dream dreams. Your young men will see visions. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Joel 2:29
In those days, I will pour out My Spirit even on My men and women servants. ❤️
## Joel 2:30
I will show wonders in heaven and on earth. There will be blood, fire, and columns of smoke. 🔥
## Joel 2:31
The sun will turn dark, and the moon will turn to blood. This will happen before the LORD’s great day that fills people with wonder and fear.” 🌑
## Joel 2:32
The LORD will save everyone who calls on His name. There will be rescue on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, as the LORD promises. The survivors will be those the LORD calls. ⛰️🛡️
