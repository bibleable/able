## Joel 3:1
God says, “At that time, I will bring Judah and Jerusalem back from captivity. ⛓️
## Joel 3:2
Then I will gather all nations in the Valley of Jehoshaphat. I will judge them for what they did to My own people Israel. They scattered My people among nations and divided My land. 🌍⚖️
## Joel 3:3
They cast lots for My people. They traded a boy for a prostitute and sold a girl for wine to drink. *Word help: People cast lots to choose by chance. A prostitute sells sex.* 💔
## Joel 3:4
Tyre, Sidon, and all Philistia, what do you have against Me? Do you want to repay Me? If you attack Me in return, I will quickly put that punishment on your heads. ⚖️
## Joel 3:5
You took My silver and gold. You carried My finest treasures to your temples. 💰
## Joel 3:6
You sold Judah’s and Jerusalem’s people to the Greeks. You sent them far from home. 💔
## Joel 3:7
I will bring them back from the places where you sold them. I will put your punishment on your own heads. ⚖️
## Joel 3:8
I will sell your sons and daughters to Judah’s people. They will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation.” The LORD speaks this. ⚖️
## Joel 3:9
Tell the nations, “Prepare for war! Wake the powerful soldiers. Let all soldiers advance and attack! 📣
## Joel 3:10
Beat your plow blades into swords. Beat your tools for cutting branches into spears. Let weak people say, ‘I am strong!’ ⚔️
## Joel 3:11
All nearby nations, hurry and gather!” LORD, bring down Your powerful ones. 👥🙏
## Joel 3:12
God says, “Let the nations rise and enter the Valley of Jehoshaphat. I will sit there to judge all the nearby nations. ⚖️
## Joel 3:13
Swing the sickle because the crops are ripe. Come and press the grapes because the winepress is full. The containers overflow because the people’s evil is great.” *Word help: A sickle is a tool for cutting crops.* 🌾⚖️
## Joel 3:14
Crowds and crowds fill the valley of decision! The Day of the LORD is near in that valley. 👥⚖️
## Joel 3:15
The sun and moon will grow dark. The stars will no longer shine. 🌑
## Joel 3:16
The LORD will roar from Zion and raise His voice from Jerusalem. Heaven and earth will shake. But the LORD will be His people’s safe place. He will protect Israel like a strong fort. ⛰️🛡️
## Joel 3:17
God says, “Then you will know that I am the LORD your God. I live on Zion, My holy mountain. Jerusalem will be holy. Foreigners will never overrun it again. ⛰️
## Joel 3:18
On that day, mountains will drip sweet wine, and hills will flow with milk. All Judah’s streams will have water. A spring from the LORD’s house will water the Valley of Acacias. 💧
## Joel 3:19
Egypt will become empty, and Edom will become a desert. They used violence against Judah’s people. They shed innocent blood in Judah. 🏜️
## Joel 3:20
But people will live in Judah forever. They will live in Jerusalem through all generations. 🏙️
## Joel 3:21
I will punish those who shed their blood. That bloodshed still awaits punishment.” The LORD lives in Zion. ⚖️
