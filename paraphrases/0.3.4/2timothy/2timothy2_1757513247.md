## 2 Timothy 2:1
My child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus. 💪
## 2 Timothy 2:2
You heard my teaching before many witnesses. Entrust it to faithful men who can also teach others. 📖
## 2 Timothy 2:3
Share my suffering like a good soldier of Christ Jesus. 🛡️
## 2 Timothy 2:4
A soldier avoids getting caught up in civilian affairs. He wants to please the one who enlisted him. 🛡️
## 2 Timothy 2:5
An athlete receives no crown unless he competes according to the rules. 🏆
## 2 Timothy 2:6
The farmer who works hard must receive the first share of the crops. 🌾
## 2 Timothy 2:7
Think about what I say. The Lord will help you understand all these things. 💭
## 2 Timothy 2:8
Remember Jesus Christ, who rose from the dead and descended from David. This is the good news I announce. ✝️
## 2 Timothy 2:9
For this message, I suffer and wear chains like a criminal. But God’s word cannot be chained! ⛓️
## 2 Timothy 2:10
So I endure everything for God’s chosen people. I want them also to receive salvation in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory. ❤️
## 2 Timothy 2:11
This statement is trustworthy: If we died with Him, we will also live with Him. 🌅
## 2 Timothy 2:12
If we endure, we will also rule with Him. If we deny Him, He will also deny us. 👑
## 2 Timothy 2:13
If we are unfaithful, He remains faithful. He cannot deny Himself. ✨
## 2 Timothy 2:14
Remind believers of these things. Warn them before God not to fight over words. Such fights only ruin those who listen. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 2:15
Make every effort to present yourself to God as an approved worker. Have no reason for shame, and handle the word of truth correctly. 📖
## 2 Timothy 2:16
Avoid empty talk that dishonors God. It only leads people further away from a life that honors Him. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 2:17
Such people’s talk spreads like gangrene. Hymenaeus and Philetus are among them. ⛔

*Word help: Gangrene is the death of body tissue that can spread.*
## 2 Timothy 2:18
They turned away from the truth. They say that the resurrection has already happened. This weakens the faith of some people. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 2:19
But God’s firm foundation stands with this seal: “The Lord knows those who belong to Him.” It also says, “Everyone who calls on the Lord’s name must turn from sin.” 🛡️
## 2 Timothy 2:20
A large house has containers of gold and silver, and also of wood and clay. Some have honorable uses, and others have ordinary uses. 🏺
## 2 Timothy 2:21
If anyone cleanses himself from what is unfit, he will be a container for honorable use. He will be holy, useful to the Master, and ready for every good task. ✨
## 2 Timothy 2:22
Run from youthful passions. Pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those who call to the Lord from a pure heart. 👣
## 2 Timothy 2:23
Reject foolish and ignorant debates. You know that they cause fights. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 2:24
A servant of the Lord must not fight with people. He must be kind to everyone, able to teach, and patient. ❤️
## 2 Timothy 2:25
He must gently correct those who oppose him. Perhaps God will help them turn from sin and come to know the truth. 📖
## 2 Timothy 2:26
Then they can come to their senses and escape the devil’s trap. The devil captured them to do his will. 🛡️
