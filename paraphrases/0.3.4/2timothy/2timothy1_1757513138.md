## 2 Timothy 1:1
This letter is from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God’s will. I serve according to His promise of life in Christ Jesus. ✉️
## 2 Timothy 1:2
I write to Timothy, my beloved child. May God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord give you grace, mercy, and peace. ❤️
## 2 Timothy 1:3
I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience, as my ancestors did. I constantly remember you in my prayers, night and day. 🙏
## 2 Timothy 1:4
I remember your tears and long to see you. Then I can be full of joy. ❤️
## 2 Timothy 1:5
I remember your sincere faith. This faith first lived in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice. I am sure it lives in you too. 🌱
## 2 Timothy 1:6
So I remind you to stir up God’s gift like a flame. You received this gift when I placed my hands on you. 🔥
## 2 Timothy 1:7
God gave us a spirit of power, love, and control of ourselves, rather than fear. 💪
## 2 Timothy 1:8
Do not be ashamed of the message about our Lord or of me, His prisoner. Instead, share my suffering for the good news, through God’s power. ✝️
## 2 Timothy 1:9
God saved us and called us to a holy life. He did this because of His own purpose and grace, rather than our works. He gave us this grace in Christ Jesus before time began. ❤️
## 2 Timothy 1:10
Now He has revealed this grace through the appearance of our Savior, Christ Jesus. Christ destroyed death’s power. Through the good news, He brought life and immortality into the light. 🌅
## 2 Timothy 1:11
I was appointed to announce this good news as a preacher, apostle, and teacher. 📣
## 2 Timothy 1:12
That is why I suffer, but I am not ashamed. I know whom I have trusted. I am sure He can guard what I entrusted to Him until that day. 🛡️
## 2 Timothy 1:13
Hold to the pattern of correct teaching you heard from me. Do this with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. 📖
## 2 Timothy 1:14
Guard the treasure entrusted to you. The Holy Spirit, who lives in us, will help you. 🛡️
## 2 Timothy 1:15
You know that everyone in the Province of Asia deserted me. Phygelus and Hermogenes are among them. 💔
## 2 Timothy 1:16
May the Lord show mercy to Onesiphorus’s household. He often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chains. ❤️
## 2 Timothy 1:17
When he came to Rome, he searched carefully until he found me. 👣
## 2 Timothy 1:18
May the Lord show Onesiphorus mercy on that day. You know very well how much he served me in Ephesus. 🙏
