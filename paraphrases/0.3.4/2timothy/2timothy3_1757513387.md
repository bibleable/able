## 2 Timothy 3:1
Understand this: Terrible times will come in the last days. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 3:2
People will love themselves and money. They will boast, be proud, and abuse others. They will disobey their parents, be ungrateful, and be unholy. ⛔
## 2 Timothy 3:3
They will have no love or forgiveness. They will slander others, lack control of themselves, act brutally, and have no love for good. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 3:4
They will betray others, act recklessly, and be full of pride. They will love pleasure more than God. ⛔
## 2 Timothy 3:5
They will appear to honor God but deny the power of such a life. Stay away from these people. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 3:6
They sneak into households and gain control over vulnerable women. These women carry heavy burdens of sin, and various passions lead them astray. ⛔
## 2 Timothy 3:7
They always learn but never manage to know the truth. 📖
## 2 Timothy 3:8
These men oppose the truth as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses. Their minds are corrupt, and their faith fails the test. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 3:9
But they will not go much further. Everyone will see their foolishness, just as they saw that of Jannes and Jambres. ⚖️
## 2 Timothy 3:10
But you have observed my teaching, behavior, purpose, faith, patience, love, and endurance. 👣
## 2 Timothy 3:11
You also know the persecution and suffering I faced at Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. I endured terrible persecution! Yet the Lord rescued me from it all. 🛡️
## 2 Timothy 3:12
Everyone who wants to live a life that honors God in Christ Jesus will face persecution. ✝️
## 2 Timothy 3:13
But evil people and imposters will become worse and worse. They will deceive others and be deceived themselves. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 3:14
Continue in what you learned and firmly believed. You know who taught you. 📖
## 2 Timothy 3:15
You have known the Holy Scriptures since infancy. They can give you wisdom for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus. 📖
## 2 Timothy 3:16
All Scripture is breathed out by God. It is useful to teach, expose wrong, correct, and train people in righteousness. 📖
## 2 Timothy 3:17
Then the person who serves God can be complete and fully ready for every good task. 🛠️
