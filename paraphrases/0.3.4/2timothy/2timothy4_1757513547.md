## 2 Timothy 4:1
I give you this solemn command before God and Christ Jesus, who will judge the living and the dead. I give it in view of His appearance and His kingdom. 👑
## 2 Timothy 4:2
Preach the word. Be ready at suitable and unsuitable times. Expose wrong, correct, and encourage with fully patient teaching. 📣
## 2 Timothy 4:3
A time will come when people refuse correct teaching. Their ears will itch for what they want to hear. They will gather teachers who suit their own desires. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 4:4
They will turn away from the truth and turn to myths. ⛔
## 2 Timothy 4:5
But keep a clear head in everything. Endure hardship, do the work of one who announces the good news, and complete your ministry. 💪
## 2 Timothy 4:6
My life is already being poured out like a drink offering. The time for me to leave is near. ✝️
## 2 Timothy 4:7
I have fought the good fight. I have finished the race. I have kept the faith. 🏁
## 2 Timothy 4:8
The crown of righteousness now waits for me. The Lord, the righteous Judge, will give it to me on that day. He will also give it to everyone who longs for His appearance. 👑
## 2 Timothy 4:9
Make every effort to come to me quickly. 👣
## 2 Timothy 4:10
Demas loved this world and deserted me. He went to Thessalonica. Crescens went to Galatia, and Titus went to Dalmatia. 💔
## 2 Timothy 4:11
Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you. He is useful to me in the ministry. 🤝
## 2 Timothy 4:12
I sent Tychicus to Ephesus. 👣
## 2 Timothy 4:13
When you come, bring the cloak I left with Carpus at Troas. Bring my scrolls too, especially the parchments. 📜
## 2 Timothy 4:14
Alexander the copper worker harmed me greatly. The Lord will repay him for his actions. ⚖️
## 2 Timothy 4:15
You too must watch out for him. He strongly opposed our message. ⚠️
## 2 Timothy 4:16
At my first defense, no one stood beside me. Everyone deserted me. May God not hold this against them. 🙏
## 2 Timothy 4:17
But the Lord stood beside me and gave me strength. Through me, the full message was announced so that all the Gentiles could hear it. I was rescued from the lion’s mouth. 🛡️
## 2 Timothy 4:18
The Lord will rescue me from every evil act and bring me safely into His heavenly kingdom. May He receive glory forever and ever. Amen. 👑
## 2 Timothy 4:19
Greet Prisca, Aquila, and Onesiphorus’s household. 👋
## 2 Timothy 4:20
Erastus stayed in Corinth. I left Trophimus sick in Miletus. 🧳
## 2 Timothy 4:21
Make every effort to come before winter. Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers send greetings. 👋
## 2 Timothy 4:22
May the Lord be with your spirit. May grace be with all of you. 🕊️
