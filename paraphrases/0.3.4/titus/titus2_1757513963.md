## Titus 2:1
But you must say what agrees with correct teaching. 📖
## Titus 2:2
Older men must avoid excess, behave with dignity, and control themselves. They must have strong faith, love, and patience. 👴
## Titus 2:3
Older women must also behave with respect for God. They must not speak lies about others or be slaves to wine. They must teach what is good. 👵
## Titus 2:4
Then they can teach younger women to love their husbands and children. ❤️
## Titus 2:5
Teach them to control themselves, remain pure, manage their homes, and be kind. They must submit to their own husbands, so that no one speaks against God’s word. 🏠
## Titus 2:6
In the same way, urge younger men to control themselves. 💪
## Titus 2:7
In everything, be an example through your good actions. Teach with honesty and dignity. 👣
## Titus 2:8
Use good words that no one can rightly criticize. Then anyone who opposes us will feel ashamed, because they have nothing bad to say about us. 🗣️
## Titus 2:9
Slaves must submit to their own masters in everything. They must try to please their masters and must not argue with them. 🛠️
## Titus 2:10
They must not steal from their masters. Instead, they must show that they can be trusted fully. Then, in every way, their lives will show the beauty of the teaching about God our Savior. 📖
## Titus 2:11
God’s grace has appeared to bring salvation to everyone. ✨
## Titus 2:12
His grace teaches us to reject behavior that dishonors God and the desires of this world. It teaches us to live wisely, rightly, and with respect for God in this present age. 👣
## Titus 2:13
We wait for our blessed hope: the glorious appearance of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ. 🌅
## Titus 2:14
He gave Himself for us to free us from all lawlessness. He did this to make us pure, as His own people who eagerly do good. ❤️
## Titus 2:15
Teach these things. Encourage people and correct them with full authority. Do not let anyone despise you. 📣
