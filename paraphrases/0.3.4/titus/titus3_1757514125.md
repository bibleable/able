## Titus 3:1
Remind believers to submit to rulers and authorities. They must obey and be ready for every good task. 👣
## Titus 3:2
They must not speak evil of anyone. They must be peaceful and gentle, and show full care and respect for everyone. 🕊️
## Titus 3:3
We too were once foolish, disobedient, and deceived. We were slaves to all kinds of desires and pleasures. We lived with malice and envy. Others hated us, and we hated each other. 💭
## Titus 3:4
But the kindness of God our Savior and His love for all people appeared. ❤️
## Titus 3:5
He saved us because of His mercy, not because of the good things we had done. He saved us through the washing of new birth and renewal by the Holy Spirit. 🕊️
## Titus 3:6
God poured out this Spirit on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Savior. ✨
## Titus 3:7
Through His grace, God declared us right with Him. So we became heirs with the hope of eternal life. 🌅
## Titus 3:8
This message is trustworthy. I want you to stress these things, so that those who believe God take care to give themselves to good actions. These things are excellent and useful for people. 📖
## Titus 3:9
Avoid foolish debates, family records, arguments, and fights about the law. These things have no use or value. ⚠️
## Titus 3:10
Warn a person who causes divisions once, and then a second time. After that, reject him. ⛔
## Titus 3:11
You know that such a person has turned away from what is right and continues to sin. He condemns himself. ⚖️
## Titus 3:12
When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, make every effort to come to me at Nicopolis. I have decided to stay there for the winter. 🧳
## Titus 3:13
Do your best to provide for Zenas the lawyer and Apollos, so that they have everything they need. 🎒
## Titus 3:14
Our people must also learn to give themselves to good tasks that meet the urgent needs of others. Then their lives will produce good results. 🛠️
## Titus 3:15
Everyone with me sends you greetings. Greet those who love us in the faith. May grace be with all of you. 🕊️
