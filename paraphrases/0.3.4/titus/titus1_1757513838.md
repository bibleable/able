## Titus 1:1
I am Paul, God’s servant and an apostle of Jesus Christ. I serve to strengthen the faith of God’s chosen people and their knowledge of the truth. This truth leads them to live in a way that honors God. ✉️
## Titus 1:2
This service rests on the hope of eternal life. God, who cannot lie, promised this life before time began. 🌅
## Titus 1:3
At His chosen time, God made His word known through the message He trusted me to announce. God our Savior commanded me to do this. 📣
## Titus 1:4
I write to Titus, my true child in the faith we share. May God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior give you grace and peace. 🕊️
## Titus 1:5
I left you in Crete to put the unfinished matters in order. Appoint elders in every town, as I instructed you. 🏘️
## Titus 1:6
An elder must be free from blame and the husband of one wife. His children must be believers. They must not give anyone reason to accuse them of reckless behavior or disobedience. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
## Titus 1:7
A church overseer manages what belongs to God, so he must be free from blame. He must not insist on his own way or become angry quickly. He must not be a drunk, violent, or greedy for money. ⚖️
## Titus 1:8
Instead, he must welcome guests and love what is good. He must control himself and live in a right, holy, and disciplined way. ❤️
## Titus 1:9
He must hold firmly to the trustworthy message he received. Then he can encourage others with correct teaching and show why those who oppose it are wrong. 📖
## Titus 1:10
Many people rebel, speak empty words, and deceive others. This is especially true of those in the circumcision group. ⚠️
## Titus 1:11
You must stop them from teaching these things. They ruin whole households because they teach what they should not teach, to gain money dishonestly. ⛔
## Titus 1:12
One of their own prophets said, “Cretans always lie. They are evil animals and lazy people who eat too much.” ⚠️
## Titus 1:13
This statement is true. So correct them firmly, so that their faith becomes sound. 📖
## Titus 1:14
Then they will pay no attention to Jewish myths or commands from people who rejected the truth. ⚠️
## Titus 1:15
For pure people, all things are pure. But for those who are morally unclean and do not believe, nothing is pure. Both their minds and their sense of right and wrong are unclean. 💭
## Titus 1:16
They say they know God, but their actions deny Him. They are detestable, disobedient, and unfit to do anything good. ⛔
