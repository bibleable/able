## 2 Kings 20:1
Hezekiah became mortally ill. Isaiah son of Amoz said, “The LORD says, ‘Set your house in order. You will die and will not recover.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 20:2
Hezekiah faced the wall and prayed to the LORD. 🙏
## 2 Kings 20:3
“LORD, remember my faithfulness and wholehearted devotion. I did good before You.” He wept bitterly. 🙏
## 2 Kings 20:4
Before Isaiah left the middle courtyard, the LORD’s word came. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:5
“Go back to Hezekiah, My people’s leader. Tell him the LORD, his ancestor David’s God, says, ‘I heard your prayer and saw your tears. I will heal you. On day three, you will enter the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:6
I will add fifteen years to your life and rescue you and this city from Assyria’s king. I will defend it for My sake and My servant David’s sake.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 20:7
Isaiah requested a fig poultice. They applied it to the boil, and Hezekiah recovered. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:8
Hezekiah had asked for a sign that the LORD would heal him and he would enter His house on day three. ❓
## 2 Kings 20:9
Isaiah said, “This sign will show the LORD will do what He promised. Should the shadow move ten steps forward or ten back?” ❓
## 2 Kings 20:10
Hezekiah answered, “Lengthening ten steps is easy for the shadow. Going back ten is not.” 📖
## 2 Kings 20:11
Isaiah called to the LORD. He moved the shadow ten steps back on Ahaz’s stairway, where it had descended. 🙏
## 2 Kings 20:12
Babylon’s King Merodach-baladan son of Baladan heard Hezekiah was ill and sent letters and a gift. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:13
Hezekiah welcomed the envoys and showed his silver, gold, spices, precious oil, armory, and all his stores. He withheld nothing in his palace or dominion. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:14
Isaiah asked where the men came from and what they said. Hezekiah answered, “A distant land, Babylon.” 📖
## 2 Kings 20:15
Isaiah asked what they saw. Hezekiah answered, “Everything in my palace. I showed all my treasures.” 📖
## 2 Kings 20:16
Isaiah said, “Hear the LORD’s word. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:17
Everything in your palace and everything your fathers stored until now will be carried to Babylon. Nothing will remain, says the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:18
Some of your own descendants will be taken to be eunuchs in Babylon’s king’s palace.” 📖
## 2 Kings 20:19
Hezekiah said, “The LORD’s word you spoke is good.” He thought, “Will there not at least be peace and security in my lifetime?” 📖
## 2 Kings 20:20
Hezekiah’s other deeds and might are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. This includes his pool and tunnel bringing water into the city. 📖
## 2 Kings 20:21
He died, and his son Manasseh succeeded him. 📖
