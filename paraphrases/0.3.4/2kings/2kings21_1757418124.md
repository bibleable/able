## 2 Kings 21:1
Manasseh became king at twelve and ruled in Jerusalem fifty-five years. His mother was Hephzibah. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:2
He did evil before the LORD, following the abominations of nations the LORD expelled before Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:3
He rebuilt high places his father Hezekiah destroyed and erected Baal altars. Like Israel’s King Ahab, he made an Asherah pole. He worshiped and served all heaven’s host. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:4
He built altars in the LORD’s house, where the LORD said, “I will put My Name in Jerusalem.” 📖
## 2 Kings 21:5
He built altars to all heaven’s host in both temple courtyards. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:6
He sacrificed his son in fire, practiced sorcery and divination, and consulted mediums and spiritists. His great evil angered the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:7
He placed his carved Asherah pole in the temple. The LORD told David and his son Solomon, “I chose Jerusalem from all Israel’s tribes. I will establish My Name here and in this temple forever. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:8
I will never again make Israel wander from the land I gave their fathers. This requires careful obedience to all My commands, the whole Law of My servant Moses.” 📖
## 2 Kings 21:9
They refused to listen. Manasseh led them into greater evil than the nations the LORD destroyed before Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:10
The LORD spoke through His servants the prophets. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:11
“Judah’s King Manasseh committed these abominations, worse than the Amorites before him. His idols led Judah to sin. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:12
Therefore the LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I will bring disaster on Jerusalem and Judah. The news will reverberate in every hearer’s ears. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:13
I will stretch over Jerusalem the measuring line used against Samaria and the plumb line used against Ahab’s house. I will wipe Jerusalem like a bowl, wiping it and turning it upside down. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:14
I will abandon the remnant of My inheritance and hand them to their enemies. All their enemies will plunder them. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:15
This is because they did evil before Me and angered Me from their fathers’ departure from Egypt until today.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 21:16
Manasseh also filled Jerusalem end to end with innocent blood. This added to the evil he led Judah to do before the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:17
His other deeds, achievements, and sins are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:18
He died and was buried in his palace’s garden of Uzza. His son Amon succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:19
Amon became king at twenty-two and ruled in Jerusalem two years. His mother was Meshullemeth daughter of Haruz, from Jotbah. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:20
He did evil before the LORD like his father Manasseh. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:21
He followed all his father’s ways and worshiped and served his idols. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:22
He abandoned the LORD, his fathers’ God, and refused His way. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:23
His servants conspired and killed him in his palace. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:24
The people killed all the conspirators and made his son Josiah king. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:25
Amon’s other deeds and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 21:26
He was buried in his tomb in Uzza’s garden. His son Josiah succeeded him. 📖
