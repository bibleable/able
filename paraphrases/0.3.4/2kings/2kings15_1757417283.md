## 2 Kings 15:1
Azariah son of Amaziah became Judah’s king in year twenty-seven of Jeroboam’s reign over Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:2
He became king at sixteen and ruled in Jerusalem fifty-two years. His mother Jecoliah came from Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:3
He did right before the LORD, as his father Amaziah did. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:4
The high places remained. People still sacrificed and burned incense there. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:5
The LORD afflicted him with leprosy until death. He lived separately while his son Jotham managed the palace and governed the people. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:6
Azariah’s other deeds and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:7
He died and was buried near his fathers in the City of David. His son Jotham succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:8
Zechariah son of Jeroboam became Israel’s king in year thirty-eight of Azariah’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria six months. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:9
He did evil before the LORD like his fathers. He kept the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:10
Shallum son of Jabesh conspired against him, killed him before the people, and succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:11
Zechariah’s other acts are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:12
The LORD’s promise to Jehu was fulfilled: “Four generations of your sons will rule Israel.” 📖
## 2 Kings 15:13
Shallum son of Jabesh became king in year thirty-nine of Uzziah’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria one full month. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:14
Menahem son of Gadi came from Tirzah to Samaria, killed Shallum son of Jabesh, and succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:15
Shallum’s other acts and conspiracy are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:16
From Tirzah, Menahem attacked Tiphsah and its surrounding people because they refused to open their gates. He ripped open all the pregnant women there. 💔
## 2 Kings 15:17
Menahem son of Gadi became Israel’s king in year thirty-nine of Azariah’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria ten years. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:18
He did evil before the LORD. Throughout his reign he kept the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:19
Assyria’s King Pul invaded. Menahem gave him a thousand silver talents to gain support and strengthen his rule. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:20
He collected fifty silver shekels from each wealthy Israelite to pay Assyria’s king. The king withdrew and did not remain in the land. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:21
Menahem’s other acts and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:22
He died, and his son Pekahiah succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:23
Pekahiah son of Menahem became Israel’s king in year fifty of Azariah’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria two years. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:24
He did evil before the LORD and kept the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:25
His officer Pekah son of Remaliah conspired with Argob, Arieh, and fifty Gileadites. Pekah killed Pekahiah in Samaria’s palace citadel and succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:26
Pekahiah’s other acts and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:27
Pekah son of Remaliah became Israel’s king in year fifty-two of Azariah’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria twenty years. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:28
He did evil before the LORD and kept the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:29
Assyria’s King Tiglath-pileser captured Ijon, Abel-beth-maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead, and Galilee during Pekah’s reign. This included all Naphtali’s land. He took the people captive to Assyria. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:30
Hoshea son of Elah conspired against Pekah son of Remaliah. He killed and replaced him in year twenty of Jotham son of Uzziah’s reign. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:31
Pekah’s other acts and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:32
Jotham son of Uzziah became Judah’s king in year two of Pekah son of Remaliah’s reign over Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:33
He became king at twenty-five and ruled in Jerusalem sixteen years. His mother was Jerusha daughter of Zadok. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:34
He did right before the LORD, as his father Uzziah did. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:35
The high places remained, with people sacrificing and burning incense there. Jotham rebuilt the Upper Gate of the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:36
Jotham’s other deeds and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:37
The LORD began sending Aram’s King Rezin and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 15:38
Jotham died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David, his ancestor. His son Ahaz succeeded him. 📖
