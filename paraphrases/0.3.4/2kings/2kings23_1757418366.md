## 2 Kings 23:1
The king summoned all Judah’s and Jerusalem’s elders. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:2
He entered the LORD’s house with all Judah and Jerusalem, priests, prophets, and people small and great. He read them every word of the Book of the Covenant found there. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:3
Beside the pillar, he covenanted before the LORD to follow Him wholeheartedly, with all his soul. He pledged to keep His commands, decrees, statutes, and the book’s covenant. Everyone joined the covenant. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:4
He ordered high priest Hilkiah, the second-ranking priests, and doorkeepers to remove Baal’s, Asherah’s, and heaven’s host’s articles from the temple. He burned them in Kidron’s fields outside Jerusalem and took the ashes to Bethel. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:5
He removed idolatrous priests Judah’s kings appointed to burn incense at high places in Judah’s towns and around Jerusalem. They burned incense to Baal, sun, moon, constellations, and all heaven’s host. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:6
He took the temple’s Asherah pole outside Jerusalem to the Kidron Valley. He burned it, ground it to powder, and scattered its dust on common people’s graves. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:7
He demolished the male shrine prostitutes’ quarters in the LORD’s house. Women had woven Asherah tapestries there. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:8
He brought all priests from Judah’s towns and defiled their incense high places from Geba to Beersheba. He demolished the gate high places by governor Joshua’s gate, left of the city gate. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:9
The high place priests did not approach the LORD’s altar in Jerusalem. They ate unleavened bread with their fellow priests. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:10
He defiled Topheth in the Valley of Ben-hinnom to prevent sons or daughters being sacrificed in fire to Molech. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:11
He removed the horses Judah’s kings dedicated to the sun from the temple entrance court near official Nathan-melech’s chamber. He burned the sun’s chariots. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:12
He demolished Judah’s kings’ rooftop altars near Ahaz’s upper chamber and Manasseh’s altars in both temple courtyards. He pulverized them and threw their dust into Kidron Valley. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:13
He defiled King Solomon of Israel’s high places east of Jerusalem, south of the Mount of Corruption. They honored Ashtoreth, the Sidonians’ abomination, Chemosh, the Moabites’ abomination, and Milcom, the Ammonites’ abomination. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:14
He smashed sacred pillars, cut Asherah poles, and covered their sites with human bones. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:15
He demolished Bethel’s altar and high place established by Jeroboam son of Nebat, who led Israel to sin. He burned the high place, ground it to powder, and burned its Asherah pole. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:16
Josiah saw hillside tombs and had their bones burned on the altar to defile it. This fulfilled the LORD’s word foretold by the man of God. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:17
He asked about a monument. The townsmen said, “It marks the tomb of Judah’s man of God. He foretold what you did to Bethel’s altar.” 📖
## 2 Kings 23:18
Josiah forbade disturbing his bones. They left them and the bones of the prophet from Samaria undisturbed. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:19
He removed Samaria’s high place shrines as at Bethel. Israel’s kings had built them, angering the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:20
He slaughtered all the high place priests on their altars and burned human bones there. He returned to Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:21
He commanded everyone to keep the LORD their God’s Passover as written in the Book of the Covenant. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:22
No such Passover had been kept since Israel’s judges, through all Israel’s and Judah’s kings’ days. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:23
This Passover was kept to the LORD in Jerusalem in Josiah’s eighteenth year. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:24
He removed mediums, spiritists, household gods, idols, and all visible abominations in Judah and Jerusalem. He did this to carry out the Law in the book Hilkiah found in the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:25
No king before or after Josiah turned to the LORD like him, with all his heart, soul, and strength according to Moses’ whole Law. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:26
The LORD’s burning fury against Judah remained because of all Manasseh did to anger Him. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:27
He said, “I will remove Judah as I removed Israel. I will reject My chosen city Jerusalem and the temple of which I said, ‘My Name shall be there.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 23:28
Josiah’s other acts and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:29
Egypt’s Pharaoh Neco marched to help Assyria’s king at the Euphrates River. Josiah confronted him, but Neco killed him at Megiddo. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:30
His servants brought his body by chariot from Megiddo to Jerusalem and buried him in his tomb. The people anointed his son Jehoahaz king. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:31
Jehoahaz became king at twenty-three and ruled in Jerusalem three months. His mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah, from Libnah. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:32
He did evil before the LORD like his fathers. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:33
Neco imprisoned him at Riblah in Hamath to stop his reign in Jerusalem. He levied a hundred silver talents and one gold talent on Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:34
Neco made Josiah’s son Eliakim king and renamed him Jehoiakim. He took Jehoahaz to Egypt, where he died. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:35
Jehoiakim paid Neco by taxing the land. He collected silver and gold from each person according to his wealth to meet Pharaoh’s demand. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:36
Jehoiakim became king at twenty-five and ruled in Jerusalem eleven years. His mother was Zebidah daughter of Pedaiah, from Rumah. 📖
## 2 Kings 23:37
He did evil before the LORD like his fathers. 📖
