## 2 Kings 10:1
Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. Jehu sent letters there to Jezreel’s officials, the elders, and the sons’ guardians. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:2
“Your master’s sons are with you. You have chariots, horses, weapons, and a fortified city. When this letter arrives, 📖
## 2 Kings 10:3
choose your master’s best, most worthy son. Put him on his father’s throne and fight for your master’s house.” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:4
They were terrified. “Two kings could not resist him. How can we?” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:5
The palace administrator, city overseer, elders, and guardians replied, “We are your servants and will obey. We will appoint no king. Do as you please.” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:6
Jehu wrote again: “If you support and obey me, bring your master’s sons’ heads to Jezreel tomorrow at this time.” The city’s leading men were raising the seventy royal sons. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:7
They received the letter and slaughtered all seventy. They sent their heads in baskets to Jehu at Jezreel. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:8
A messenger reported the heads’ arrival. Jehu said, “Pile them in two heaps at the gate entrance until morning.” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:9
Next morning he told the people, “You are innocent. I conspired against my master and killed him. But who killed these? ❓
## 2 Kings 10:10
None of the LORD’s words against Ahab’s house will fail. He fulfilled His promise through His servant Elijah.” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:11
Jehu killed everyone of Ahab’s house remaining in Jezreel, plus his great men, close friends, and priests. He left no survivor. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:12
He traveled toward Samaria and reached Beth-eked of the Shepherds. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:13
He met Judah’s King Ahaziah’s relatives and asked who they were. They answered, “Ahaziah’s relatives. We came to greet the king’s and queen mother’s sons.” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:14
Jehu ordered them captured alive. His men captured and slaughtered them at Beth-eked’s well, forty-two men. He spared none. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:15
He met Jehonadab son of Rechab. “Is your heart true to mine as mine is to yours?” Jehonadab agreed. Jehu requested his hand and helped him into the chariot. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:16
“Come and see my zeal for the LORD!” Jehu had him ride along. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:17
In Samaria he killed everyone remaining of Ahab’s house, as the LORD said to Elijah. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:18
He gathered everyone and said, “Ahab served Baal a little. Jehu will serve him much. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:19
Summon every Baal prophet, servant, and priest for my great sacrifice. Anyone absent will die.” Jehu used this deception to destroy Baal’s servants. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:20
He ordered a solemn assembly for Baal proclaimed, and it was announced. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:21
He sent word throughout Israel. All Baal’s servants came, none missing. They filled Baal’s temple from end to end. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:22
He told the wardrobe keeper to give them garments. He did. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:23
Jehu and Jehonadab son of Rechab entered. Jehu told Baal’s servants to check that only Baal’s servants were present, with none belonging to the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:24
They entered to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Jehu stationed eighty men outside. He warned, “Whoever lets one of these men escape will forfeit his life for that man’s.” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:25
After the burnt offering, Jehu ordered the guards and officers to kill everyone and allow no escape. They killed them with swords, threw out the bodies, and entered Baal’s temple’s inner room. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:26
They removed the temple’s sacred pillar and burned it. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:27
They demolished Baal’s sacred pillar and temple. They made the site a latrine, which it remains today. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:28
Jehu eradicated Baal from Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:29
But he kept Jeroboam son of Nebat’s sins that led Israel astray: worshiping golden calves at Bethel and Dan. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:30
The LORD told Jehu, “You fulfilled My will against Ahab’s house and did right before Me. Therefore four generations of your sons will rule Israel.” 📖
## 2 Kings 10:31
Jehu did not carefully follow the instruction of the LORD, Israel’s God, with all his heart. He kept the sins Jeroboam led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:32
The LORD began reducing Israel’s territory. Hazael defeated Israel throughout its territory. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:33
This extended east of the Jordan through all Gilead, the lands of Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh. It ran from Aroer by the Arnon Valley through Gilead to Bashan. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:34
Jehu’s other deeds, achievements, and might are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:35
He died and was buried in Samaria. His son Jehoahaz succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 10:36
Jehu ruled Israel in Samaria for twenty-eight years. 📖
