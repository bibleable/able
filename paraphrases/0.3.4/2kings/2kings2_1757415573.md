## 2 Kings 2:1
Before the LORD took Elijah to heaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha traveled from Gilgal. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:2
Elijah said, “Stay here. The LORD sent me to Bethel.” Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.” They went down to Bethel. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:3
Bethel’s sons of the prophets asked Elisha, “Do you know the LORD will take your master away today?” He answered, “Yes. Do not discuss it.” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:4
Elijah said, “Stay here. The LORD sent me to Jericho.” Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.” They went to Jericho. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:5
Jericho’s sons of the prophets asked Elisha, “Do you know the LORD will take your master away today?” He answered, “Yes. Do not discuss it.” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:6
Elijah said, “Stay here. The LORD sent me to the Jordan.” Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.” They continued. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:7
Fifty sons of the prophets stood at a distance, facing Elijah and Elisha beside the Jordan. 👥
## 2 Kings 2:8
Elijah rolled his cloak and struck the water. It parted right and left, and both men crossed on dry ground. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:9
Elijah asked, “What can I do for you before I am taken away?” Elisha answered, “Let me inherit a double portion of your spirit.” 🙏
## 2 Kings 2:10
Elijah said, “You asked something difficult. If you see me being taken away, you will receive it. Otherwise, you will not.” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:11
As they walked and talked, a fiery chariot and fiery horses separated them. Elijah rose to heaven in a whirlwind. ✨
## 2 Kings 2:12
Elisha watched and cried, “My father, my father, Israel’s chariots and horsemen!” He saw Elijah no more and tore his own clothes in two. 💔
## 2 Kings 2:13
He picked up Elijah’s fallen cloak and returned to the Jordan’s bank. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:14
He struck the water with Elijah’s cloak and asked, “Where is the LORD, Elijah’s God?” The water parted right and left, and Elisha crossed. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:15
The sons of the prophets watching from Jericho said, “Elijah’s spirit rests on Elisha.” They met him and bowed to the ground before him. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:16
They said, “Your servants have fifty brave men. Let them search for your master. Perhaps the LORD’s Spirit lifted him and placed him on a mountain or in a valley.” Elisha said, “Do not send them.” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:17
They pressed him until he was embarrassed and said, “Send them.” Fifty men searched three days but found no Elijah. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:18
They returned to Elisha in Jericho. He said, “Did I not tell you to stay?” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:19
The townsmen said, “Our lord, you can see that the town’s location is good. But its water is bad, and its land is unfruitful.” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:20
He said, “Bring a new bowl with salt in it.” They brought it. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:21
Elisha threw salt into the spring. He said, “The LORD says, ‘I healed this water. It will no longer cause death or unfruitfulness.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:22
The water remains healthy to this day, as Elisha said. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:23
Elisha went up toward Bethel. Boys came from the town and mocked him: “Go up, baldhead! Go up, baldhead!” 📖
## 2 Kings 2:24
He turned, saw them, and cursed them in the LORD’s name. Two female bears came from the woods and mauled forty-two boys. 📖
## 2 Kings 2:25
He went to Mount Carmel and returned to Samaria. 📖
