## 2 Kings 8:1
Elisha had warned the woman whose son he revived, “Take your household and live abroad wherever possible. The LORD decreed seven years of famine.” 📖
## 2 Kings 8:2
She obeyed. Her household lived as foreigners in Philistine land for seven years. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:3
After seven years she returned and appealed to the king for her house and land. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:4
The king was asking Gehazi, Elisha’s servant, to describe all Elisha’s great deeds. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:5
Gehazi described Elisha restoring the dead to life. The woman arrived to request her property. Gehazi said, “My lord, this is the woman and the son Elisha revived.” 📖
## 2 Kings 8:6
She confirmed it. The king assigned an officer to restore all her property and the field’s proceeds from her departure until now. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:7
Elisha came to Damascus while Aram’s King Ben-hadad was ill. The king heard the man of God had arrived. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:8
He told Hazael to take a gift and consult the LORD through Elisha about his recovery. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:9
Hazael brought forty camel loads of Damascus’s good things. He said, “Your son Ben-hadad, Aram’s king, asks whether he will recover.” 📖
## 2 Kings 8:10
Elisha said, “Tell him, ‘You will certainly recover.’ But the LORD showed me he will actually die.” 📖
## 2 Kings 8:11
Elisha stared steadily until Hazael was uncomfortable. Then he wept. 💔
## 2 Kings 8:12
Hazael asked why. Elisha answered, “I know the evil you will do to Israel. You will burn fortresses, kill young men with swords, dash little ones to pieces, and rip open pregnant women.” 💔
## 2 Kings 8:13
Hazael said, “How could your servant, a mere dog, do such a monstrous thing?” Elisha answered, “The LORD showed me you will rule Aram.” 📖
## 2 Kings 8:14
Hazael returned to his master, who asked what Elisha said. He answered, “He said you would certainly recover.” 📖
## 2 Kings 8:15
The next day, Hazael soaked a thick cloth and spread it over the king’s face. Ben-hadad died, and Hazael succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:16
In year five of Joram son of Ahab’s reign over Israel, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat succeeded his father as Judah’s king. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:17
Jehoram became king at thirty-two and ruled in Jerusalem eight years. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:18
He followed Israel’s kings and Ahab’s house because he married Ahab’s daughter. He did evil before the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:19
The LORD would not destroy Judah for His servant David’s sake. He promised to maintain a lamp for David and his descendants forever. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:20
During Jehoram’s reign, Edom rebelled against Judah and appointed its own king. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:21
Jehoram crossed to Zair with all his chariots. The Edomites surrounded him and his chariot commanders. He attacked by night, but his troops fled home. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:22
Edom remains in rebellion against Judah to this day. Libnah also rebelled then. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:23
Jehoram’s other acts and achievements are recorded in the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:24
He died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His son Ahaziah succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:25
Ahaziah son of Jehoram became Judah’s king in year twelve of Joram son of Ahab’s reign over Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:26
Ahaziah became king at twenty-two and ruled in Jerusalem one year. His mother Athaliah was a granddaughter of Israel’s King Omri. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:27
He followed Ahab’s house and did evil before the LORD like them. He was a son-in-law of Ahab’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:28
He joined Joram son of Ahab against Aram’s King Hazael at Ramoth-gilead. The Arameans wounded Joram. 📖
## 2 Kings 8:29
Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from wounds the Arameans inflicted at Ramah. He had fought Aram’s King Hazael. Judah’s King Ahaziah son of Jehoram visited Joram son of Ahab there because he was wounded. 📖
