## 2 Kings 11:1
Athaliah, Ahaziah’s mother, saw he was dead and began destroying all the royal heirs. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:2
Jehosheba, King Joram’s daughter and Ahaziah’s sister, rescued Ahaziah’s son Joash from the royal sons being murdered. She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom from Athaliah. He survived. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:3
He and his nurse hid in the LORD’s house six years while Athaliah ruled. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:4
In year seven, Jehoiada summoned commanders of hundreds, Carites, and guards to the LORD’s house. He made a covenant with them under oath and showed them the king’s son. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:5
He ordered, “A third of those beginning Sabbath duty must guard the palace. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:6
A third must guard Sur’s gate, and a third the gate behind the guards. Take turns guarding the temple. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:7
The two divisions finishing Sabbath duty must guard the LORD’s house for the king. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:8
Surround the king with weapons in hand. Kill anyone approaching the ranks. Stay close wherever he goes.” 📖
## 2 Kings 11:9
The commanders obeyed Jehoiada the priest. Each brought his men, both those beginning and those finishing Sabbath duty, to him. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:10
He gave the commanders King David’s spears and shields from the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:11
Armed guards surrounded the king near the altar and temple. They stood from the temple’s south side to its north side. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:12
Jehoiada brought out the king’s son, crowned him, gave him the Testimony, and proclaimed him king. They anointed him. The people clapped, saying, “Long live the king!” 📖
## 2 Kings 11:13
Athaliah heard the guards and people and went to the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:14
The king stood by the pillar as customary, with officers and trumpeters beside him. The people rejoiced and blew trumpets. Athaliah tore her clothes, screaming, “Treason! Treason!” 📖
## 2 Kings 11:15
Jehoiada ordered the army commanders, “Bring her out between the ranks. Kill anyone following her.” He had forbidden killing her in the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:16
They seized and killed her at the horses’ entrance to the palace grounds. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:17
Jehoiada made a covenant between the LORD, king, and people that they would be the LORD’s people. He also made a covenant between king and people. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:18
The people demolished Baal’s temple, altars, and idols. They killed Baal’s priest Mattan before the altars. Jehoiada posted guards for the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:19
Jehoiada, the commanders, Carites, guards, and all the people brought the king from the LORD’s house. They entered the palace through the Gate of the Guards. Joash sat on the royal throne. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:20
The people rejoiced. The city was quiet because Athaliah had been killed with a sword at the palace. 📖
## 2 Kings 11:21
Joash became king at seven years old. 📖
