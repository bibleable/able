## 2 Kings 12:1
Joash became king in Jehu’s seventh year and ruled in Jerusalem forty years. His mother Zibiah came from Beersheba. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:2
Joash did right before the LORD all the days Jehoiada the priest instructed him. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:3
The high places remained. People still sacrificed and burned incense there. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:4
Joash told the priests, “Collect all sacred gift money brought to the LORD’s house. This includes census money, vow money, and voluntary gifts. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:5
Each priest must receive it from his constituency. Use it to repair all temple damage.” 📖
## 2 Kings 12:6
By Joash’s twenty-third year, the priests had not repaired the temple. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:7
He asked Jehoiada and the priests, “Why have you not repaired it? Stop collecting money from your constituencies. Hand it over for repairs.” 📖
## 2 Kings 12:8
They agreed to stop collecting money and not make the repairs themselves. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:9
Jehoiada bored a hole in a chest’s lid. He placed it beside the altar, to the right when entering the LORD’s house. The threshold priests put all incoming temple money there. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:10
When much accumulated, the royal scribe and high priest counted it and tied it in bags. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:11
They gave the counted money to the temple work supervisors. These paid the carpenters and builders, 📖
## 2 Kings 12:12
masons, and stonecutters. They bought timber and cut stone and paid other temple repair costs. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:13
This money did not buy silver basins, wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, trumpets, or any gold or silver temple articles. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:14
It paid the workers who repaired the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:15
Those paying the workers were not required to give accounts because they acted honestly. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:16
Guilt offering and sin offering money belonged to the priests and did not enter the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:17
Aram’s King Hazael captured Gath and decided to attack Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:18
Judah’s King Joash sent Hazael all sacred objects dedicated by his fathers Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, along with his own. He also sent all gold from the temple and palace treasuries. Hazael withdrew from Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:19
Joash’s other acts and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:20
His servants conspired and killed him at Beth-millo, on the road down to Silla. 📖
## 2 Kings 12:21
His servants Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer killed him. He was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His son Amaziah succeeded him. 📖
