## 2 Kings 22:1
Josiah became king at eight and ruled in Jerusalem thirty-one years. His mother was Jedidah daughter of Adaiah, from Bozkath. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:2
He did right before the LORD, following his ancestor David’s ways without turning right or left. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:3
In year eighteen, he sent his scribe Shaphan son of Azaliah, son of Meshullam, to the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:4
“Have the high priest Hilkiah count the temple money the doorkeepers collected from the people. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:5
Give it to the temple work supervisors to pay those repairing damage: 📖
## 2 Kings 22:6
the carpenters, builders, and masons, and to buy timber and cut stone for repairs. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:7
They need give no account because they work honestly.” 📖
## 2 Kings 22:8
Hilkiah told Shaphan, “I found the Book of the Law in the LORD’s house!” He gave it to him, and Shaphan read it. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:9
Shaphan reported to the king that his servants gave the temple money to its workers and supervisors. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:10
He said, “Hilkiah gave me a book.” He read it before the king. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:11
The king heard the Book of the Law and tore his clothes. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:12
He commanded the priest Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Achbor son of Micaiah, the scribe Shaphan, and his servant Asaiah. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:13
“Consult the LORD for me, the people, and all Judah about this book. His wrath burns greatly because our fathers failed to obey everything written about us.” 📖
## 2 Kings 22:14
Hilkiah, Ahikam, Achbor, Shaphan, and Asaiah spoke with the prophetess Huldah in Jerusalem’s Second District. Her husband, wardrobe keeper Shallum, was Tikvah’s son and Harhas’s grandson. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:15
Huldah said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘Tell the man who sent you: 📖
## 2 Kings 22:16
The LORD will bring disaster on this place and people, according to everything in the book Judah’s king read. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:17
They abandoned Me and burned incense to other gods, angering Me with their handiwork. My wrath against this place will burn and not be quenched.’ 📖
## 2 Kings 22:18
Tell Judah’s king, who sent you to consult the LORD, that the LORD, Israel’s God, says concerning what he heard: 📖
## 2 Kings 22:19
‘Your heart was tender, and you humbled yourself when you heard this place and its people would become a desolation and curse. You tore your clothes and wept before Me. Therefore I heard you,’ declares the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 22:20
‘I will gather you to your fathers and your grave in peace. You will not see this disaster.’” They brought her answer to the king. 📖
