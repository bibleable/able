## 2 Kings 6:1
The sons of the prophets told Elisha, “Our meeting place is too small. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:2
Let us each get a log at the Jordan and build a place to live.” He said, “Go.” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:3
One asked him to accompany his servants. He agreed. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:4
He went, and they began cutting trees at the Jordan. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:5
A man’s iron axe head fell into the water. He cried, “Master, it was borrowed!” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:6
The man of God asked where. The man showed him, and Elisha cut a stick and threw it there. He made the iron float. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:7
He said, “Lift it out.” The man reached and took it. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:8
Aram’s king was fighting Israel. After consulting his servants, he said, “My camp will be in this place.” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:9
The man of God warned Israel’s king, “Avoid that place. The Arameans are going there.” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:10
Israel’s king sent word to that place. Elisha repeatedly warned him, keeping him on guard in those places. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:11
Aram’s king was enraged and demanded, “Which of us supports Israel’s king?” ❓
## 2 Kings 6:12
A servant answered, “No one, my lord. Israel’s prophet Elisha tells its king even what you say in your bedroom.” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:13
He said, “Find him so I can capture him.” He learned Elisha was in Dothan. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:14
Aram’s king sent horses, chariots, and a large army. They surrounded the town by night. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:15
Elisha’s servant rose early and saw the surrounding army, horses, and chariots. He asked, “Master, what shall we do?” ❓
## 2 Kings 6:16
Elisha answered, “Do not fear. More are with us than with them.” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:17
He prayed, “LORD, open his eyes to see.” The LORD did, and the young man saw fiery horses and chariots filling the hills around Elisha. 🙏
## 2 Kings 6:18
As the Arameans approached, Elisha prayed, “LORD, blind these people.” The LORD struck them with blindness as Elisha requested. 🙏
## 2 Kings 6:19
Elisha said, “This is not the way or city. Follow me to the man you seek.” He led them to Samaria. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:20
There he prayed, “LORD, open their eyes.” The LORD did, and they discovered they were in Samaria. 🙏
## 2 Kings 6:21
Israel’s king asked Elisha, “My father, shall I kill them? Shall I?” ❓
## 2 Kings 6:22
He answered, “No. Would you kill prisoners captured with your sword or bow? Give them food and water, then let them return to their master.” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:23
The king gave them a great feast and sent them home after they ate and drank. Aramean raiders did not enter Israel again. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:24
Later, Aram’s King Ben-hadad gathered his whole army and besieged Samaria. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:25
The long siege caused severe famine. A donkey’s head cost eighty silver shekels. A quarter cab of dove’s dung cost five silver shekels. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:26
A woman called to Israel’s king as he walked on the wall: “Help, my lord the king!” 🙏
## 2 Kings 6:27
He said, “If the LORD does not help you, where can I find help for you? From the threshing floor or winepress?” ❓
## 2 Kings 6:28
He asked her problem. She said, “This woman said, ‘Give your son for us to eat. Tomorrow we will eat mine.’ 📖
## 2 Kings 6:29
We boiled my son and ate him. The next day I asked for hers, but she had hidden him.” 💔
## 2 Kings 6:30
The king tore his clothes. As he passed on the wall, the people saw sackcloth underneath, next to his skin. 📖
## 2 Kings 6:31
He said, “May God punish me severely if Elisha son of Shaphat still has his head by day’s end!” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:32
Elisha sat with the elders at home. The king sent a messenger. Before he arrived, Elisha said, “This murderer sent someone to behead me. Shut the door against the messenger. Do you not hear his master following?” 📖
## 2 Kings 6:33
The messenger arrived while Elisha spoke. The king said, “This disaster is from the LORD. Why wait for Him longer?” 📖
