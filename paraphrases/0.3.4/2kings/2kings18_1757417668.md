## 2 Kings 18:1
Hezekiah son of Ahaz became Judah’s king in year three of Hoshea son of Elah’s reign over Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:2
He became king at twenty-five and ruled in Jerusalem twenty-nine years. His mother was Abi daughter of Zechariah. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:3
He did right before the LORD, as his ancestor David did. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:4
He removed high places, shattered sacred pillars, and cut down Asherah poles. He demolished Moses’ bronze snake, Nehushtan, because Israel burned incense to it. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:5
He trusted the LORD, Israel’s God. No Judah king before or after him was like him. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:6
He remained faithful to the LORD, following Him and keeping His commands to Moses. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:7
The LORD was with him, and he succeeded wherever he went. He rebelled against Assyria’s king and refused to serve him. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:8
He defeated the Philistines as far as Gaza and its borders, from watchtower to fortified city. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:9
Assyria’s King Shalmaneser besieged Samaria in Hezekiah’s fourth year, Hoshea son of Elah’s seventh year over Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:10
They captured it after three years, in Hezekiah’s sixth year and Israel’s King Hoshea’s ninth year. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:11
Assyria’s king exiled Israel to Halah, Gozan by the Habor River, and the Medes’ cities in Assyria. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:12
This happened because they disobeyed the LORD their God and violated His covenant, all His servant Moses commanded. They neither listened nor obeyed. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:13
Assyria’s King Sennacherib captured all Judah’s fortified cities in Hezekiah’s fourteenth year. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:14
Hezekiah sent word to him at Lachish: “I did wrong. Withdraw, and I will pay your demand.” He demanded three hundred silver talents and thirty gold talents. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:15
Hezekiah gave all silver from the LORD’s house and palace treasuries. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:16
He stripped the gold he plated on the temple doors and doorposts and gave it to Assyria’s king. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:17
Assyria’s king still sent the Tartan, Rabsaris, and Rabshakeh with a great army from Lachish to Hezekiah in Jerusalem. They stood beside the upper pool’s aqueduct on the road to the Launderer’s Field. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:18
They called for the king. Palace administrator Eliakim son of Hilkiah, scribe Shebnah, and recorder Joah son of Asaph came out. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:19
The Rabshakeh said, “Tell Hezekiah the great king of Assyria asks, ‘What is your confidence based on? ❓
## 2 Kings 18:20
Your claimed war strategy and strength are empty words. Whom do you trust that you rebel against me? ❓
## 2 Kings 18:21
Egypt is a splintered reed that pierces anyone leaning on it. Pharaoh is like this to everyone trusting him. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:22
You say you trust the LORD your God. Did Hezekiah not remove His high places and altars, ordering Judah and Jerusalem to worship only at Jerusalem’s altar? ❓
## 2 Kings 18:23
Bargain with my master, Assyria’s king. I will give two thousand horses if you can supply riders! 📖
## 2 Kings 18:24
How can you repel even my master’s least officer while depending on Egypt’s chariots and horsemen? ❓
## 2 Kings 18:25
Did I attack without the LORD? He told me to attack and destroy this land.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 18:26
Eliakim son of Hilkiah, Shebnah, and Joah said, “Speak Aramaic. We understand it. Do not speak Hebrew for the people on the wall to hear.” 📖
## 2 Kings 18:27
The Rabshakeh answered, “Did my master send me only to you and your master? These men on the wall will also eat their dung and drink their urine with you!” 📖
## 2 Kings 18:28
He called loudly in Hebrew, “Hear Assyria’s great king! 📖
## 2 Kings 18:29
He says, ‘Do not let Hezekiah deceive you. He cannot rescue you from me. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:30
Do not trust his promise that the LORD will rescue you and keep Assyria’s king from taking this city. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:31
Ignore Hezekiah. Make peace and come out to me. Each will eat from his vine and fig tree and drink from his cistern. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:32
This will last until I take you to a land like yours, with grain, new wine, bread, vineyards, olive trees, and honey. You will live instead of dying. Hezekiah misleads you when he says the LORD will rescue you. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:33
Has any nation’s god rescued its land from Assyria’s king? ❓
## 2 Kings 18:34
Where are Hamath’s and Arpad’s gods, or Sepharvaim’s, Hena’s, and Ivvah’s gods? Did they rescue Samaria from me? ❓
## 2 Kings 18:35
Which god rescued its land from me? How can the LORD rescue Jerusalem?’” ❓
## 2 Kings 18:36
The people remained silent because Hezekiah ordered them not to answer. 📖
## 2 Kings 18:37
Palace administrator Eliakim son of Hilkiah, scribe Shebna, and recorder Joah son of Asaph returned with torn clothes. They told Hezekiah the Rabshakeh’s words. 📖
