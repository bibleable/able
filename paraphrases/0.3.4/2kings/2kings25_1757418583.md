## 2 Kings 25:1
On day ten of month ten in Zedekiah’s ninth year, Babylon’s King Nebuchadnezzar marched against Jerusalem with his whole army. They camped outside and built a surrounding siege wall. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:2
The siege lasted until Zedekiah’s eleventh year. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:3
By day nine of month four, the famine was severe and the people had no food. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:4
The city was breached. Though surrounded by Chaldeans, all its warriors escaped at night through the gate between the two walls near the king’s garden. They headed toward the Arabah. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:5
The Chaldean army caught the king on Jericho’s plains. His whole army deserted him. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:6
They seized him and brought him to Babylon’s king at Riblah, where they pronounced judgment. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:7
They killed Zedekiah’s sons before him. They put out his eyes, bound him with bronze shackles, and took him to Babylon. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:8
On day seven of month five, in Nebuchadnezzar’s nineteenth year over Babylon, his guard captain Nebuzaradan entered Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:9
He burned the LORD’s house, palace, and all Jerusalem’s houses, every significant building. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:10
The whole Chaldean army under the guard captain demolished Jerusalem’s surrounding walls. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:11
Nebuzaradan exiled those remaining in the city, the deserters who joined Babylon’s king, and the rest of the population. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:12
He left some of the poorest people to tend vineyards and fields. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:13
The Chaldeans broke the temple’s bronze pillars, stands, and Sea and took the bronze to Babylon. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:14
They took pots, shovels, wick trimmers, dishes, and every bronze temple service article. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:15
The guard captain took censers, sprinkling bowls, and everything made of pure gold or fine silver. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:16
The bronze weight of Solomon’s two pillars, Sea, and movable stands for the LORD’s house was beyond measure. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:17
Each pillar stood eighteen cubits high. Its bronze capital was three cubits high with a bronze pomegranate network around it. The second pillar and network were similar. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:18
He took chief priest Seraiah, second-ranking priest Zephaniah, and three doorkeepers. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:19
He took the court official over the warriors, five royal advisors, and the army captain’s recruiting scribe. He also took sixty men found in the city. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:20
Guard captain Nebuzaradan brought them to Babylon’s king at Riblah. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:21
The king executed them at Riblah in Hamath. Judah was exiled from its land. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:22
Nebuchadnezzar appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, over Judah’s remaining people. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:23
All the army commanders and their men heard this and came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. They included Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah son of the Maacathite. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:24
Gedaliah swore, “Do not fear the Chaldeans’ servants. Stay in the land, serve Babylon’s king, and all will go well.” 📖
## 2 Kings 25:25
In month seven, royal family member Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, came with ten men. They killed Gedaliah and the Judeans and Chaldeans with him at Mizpah. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:26
Everyone, small and great, and the army commanders fled to Egypt, fearing the Chaldeans. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:27
In year thirty-seven of Jehoiachin’s exile, on day twenty-seven of month twelve, Babylon’s King Evil-merodach released Judah’s King Jehoiachin from prison. This was the year Evil-merodach became king. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:28
He spoke kindly and set Jehoiachin’s throne above the other kings with him in Babylon. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:29
Jehoiachin changed out of his prison clothes and regularly dined at the king’s table for the rest of his life. 📖
## 2 Kings 25:30
The king provided him a daily portion for the rest of his life. 📖
