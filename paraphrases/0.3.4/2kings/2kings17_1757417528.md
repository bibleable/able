## 2 Kings 17:1
Hoshea son of Elah became Israel’s king in year twelve of Ahaz’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria nine years. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:2
He did evil before the LORD, though unlike Israel’s earlier kings. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:3
Assyria’s King Shalmaneser attacked. Hoshea became his subject and paid tribute. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:4
Hoshea sent envoys to Egypt’s King So and stopped his yearly tribute. Assyria’s king discovered this conspiracy and imprisoned him. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:5
He invaded the whole land and besieged Samaria three years. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:6
He captured Samaria in Hoshea’s ninth year and exiled the Israelites to Assyria. He settled them in Halah, Gozan by the Habor River, and the Medes’ cities. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:7
Israel sinned against the LORD their God, causing all this. He freed them from Egypt and Pharaoh’s power. They worshiped other gods. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:8
They followed the customs of nations the LORD expelled and practices Israel’s kings introduced. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:9
They secretly did wrong against the LORD their God. They built high places in every town, from watchtower to fortified city. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:10
They erected sacred pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:11
They burned incense at all high places like the expelled nations. Their wicked acts angered the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:12
They served idols despite His prohibition. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:13
Through prophets and seers, the LORD warned Israel and Judah. “Turn from evil. Keep My commands and statutes according to the entire Law I gave your fathers and sent through My servants the prophets.” 📖
## 2 Kings 17:14
They refused to listen and were stubborn like their fathers, who disbelieved the LORD their God. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:15
They rejected His statutes, covenant with their fathers, and decrees. They pursued worthless idols and became worthless. They imitated surrounding nations despite His prohibition. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:16
They abandoned all His commands, cast two calf idols, and made an Asherah pole. They bowed to all heaven’s host and served Baal. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:17
They sacrificed sons and daughters in fire and practiced divination and soothsaying. They devoted themselves to evil before the LORD, angering Him. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:18
The LORD was very angry and removed Israel from His presence. Only Judah’s tribe remained. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:19
Even Judah disobeyed the LORD their God and followed customs Israel introduced. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:20
The LORD rejected all Israel’s descendants. He afflicted them and handed them to plunderers until He banished them from His presence. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:21
He tore Israel from David’s house. They made Jeroboam son of Nebat king. Jeroboam led them away from the LORD into great sin. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:22
They persisted in all Jeroboam’s sins. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:23
The LORD finally removed Israel as all His prophets foretold. They were exiled to Assyria, where they remain to this day. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:24
Assyria’s king replaced Israel with people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim. They possessed Samaria and lived in its towns. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:25
They did not worship the LORD when they first settled. He sent lions that killed some. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:26
They told Assyria’s king, “Your settlers do not know what the God of this land requires. Therefore He sent lions that are killing them.” 📖
## 2 Kings 17:27
He ordered one exiled priest sent back to live in Samaria and teach the requirements of the land’s God. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:28
One returned to Bethel and taught them how to worship the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:29
Each nation still made its own gods. They put them in Samaria’s high place shrines in their towns. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:30
Babylon’s people made Succoth-benoth, Cuth’s people Nergal, and Hamath’s people Ashima. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:31
The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. The Sepharvites burned their children in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, Sepharvaim’s gods. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:32
They worshiped the LORD but appointed all sorts of priests for their high place shrines. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:33
They worshiped the LORD and their own gods, keeping their former nations’ customs. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:34
They keep those customs to this day. They neither worship the LORD nor obey His statutes, ordinances, laws, and commands to Jacob’s descendants. He named Jacob Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:35
He made a covenant with Israel: “Do not worship, bow to, serve, or sacrifice to other gods. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:36
Worship the LORD, who freed you from Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm. Bow and sacrifice to Him. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:37
Always carefully obey His written statutes, ordinances, laws, and commands. Do not worship other gods. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:38
Remember My covenant with you. Do not worship other gods. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:39
Worship the LORD your God. He will rescue you from all your enemies.” 📖
## 2 Kings 17:40
They refused to listen and kept their old customs. 📖
## 2 Kings 17:41
They worshiped the LORD and served idols too. Their children and grandchildren still follow their example to this day. 📖
