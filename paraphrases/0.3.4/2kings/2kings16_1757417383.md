## 2 Kings 16:1
Ahaz son of Jotham became Judah’s king in year seventeen of Pekah son of Remaliah’s reign. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:2
He became king at twenty and ruled in Jerusalem sixteen years. Unlike his ancestor David, he did not do right before the LORD his God. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:3
He followed Israel’s kings and even sacrificed his son in fire. This followed the abominations of nations the LORD expelled before Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:4
He sacrificed and burned incense at high places, on hills, and under every green tree. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:5
Aram’s King Rezin and Israel’s King Pekah son of Remaliah attacked Jerusalem. They besieged Ahaz but could not overcome him. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:6
Rezin recovered Elath for Aram and expelled Judah’s men. He sent Edomites there, where they live to this day. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:7
Ahaz sent word to Assyria’s King Tiglath-pileser: “I am your servant and son. Save me from Aram’s and Israel’s kings attacking me.” 📖
## 2 Kings 16:8
He sent Assyria’s king silver and gold from the LORD’s house and palace treasuries as a gift. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:9
Assyria’s king responded by capturing Damascus. He took its people captive to Kir and killed Rezin. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:10
Ahaz met Tiglath-pileser in Damascus. He saw an altar and sent the priest Uriah a model and complete building plans. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:11
Uriah followed all Ahaz’s instructions and finished the altar before his return from Damascus. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:12
Ahaz returned, saw the altar, and approached to offer sacrifices. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:13
He offered burnt and grain offerings, poured his drink offering, and splashed his peace offerings’ blood on the altar. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:14
He moved the bronze altar from before the LORD, between the new altar and the temple. He placed it north of the new altar. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:15
He told Uriah, “Use the great altar for the morning burnt offering and evening grain offering. Offer the king’s burnt and grain offerings there. Also offer the people’s burnt, grain, and drink offerings. Splash all burnt offering and sacrifice blood on it. I will use the bronze altar to seek guidance.” 📖
## 2 Kings 16:16
Uriah obeyed Ahaz’s instructions. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:17
Ahaz cut the frames from the movable stands and removed their bronze basins. He moved the Sea from its bronze oxen onto a stone base. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:18
Because of Assyria’s king, he removed the temple’s Sabbath canopy and closed the royal entryway outside the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:19
Ahaz’s other deeds and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 16:20
He died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His son Hezekiah succeeded him. 📖
