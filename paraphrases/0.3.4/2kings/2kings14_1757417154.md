## 2 Kings 14:1
Amaziah son of Joash became Judah’s king in year two of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz’s reign over Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:2
He became king at twenty-five and ruled in Jerusalem twenty-nine years. His mother Jehoaddan came from Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:3
He did right before the LORD, though unlike his ancestor David. He followed his father Joash’s example in everything. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:4
The high places remained. People still sacrificed and burned incense there. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:5
Once his rule was secure, Amaziah executed the servants who murdered his father. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:6
He spared their sons according to Moses’ Law. The LORD commanded, “Do not execute fathers for their children or children for their fathers. Each must die for his own sin.” 📖
## 2 Kings 14:7
He killed 10,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. He captured Sela and named it Joktheel, its name to this day. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:8
He sent word to Israel’s King Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu: “Let us meet face to face.” 📖
## 2 Kings 14:9
Jehoash replied, “A Lebanese thistle asked a Lebanese cedar to give its daughter to its son in marriage. A wild beast in Lebanon trampled the thistle. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:10
Defeating Edom made you proud. Enjoy your glory and stay home. Why cause trouble and bring yourself and Judah down?” ❓
## 2 Kings 14:11
Amaziah refused to listen. Israel’s King Jehoash advanced, and the kings faced each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:12
Israel routed Judah. Every man fled home. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:13
Jehoash captured Judah’s King Amaziah son of Joash, son of Ahaziah, at Beth-shemesh. In Jerusalem he demolished four hundred cubits of wall between the Ephraim Gate and Corner Gate. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:14
He took all the gold, silver, and articles in the LORD’s house and palace treasuries, plus hostages. He returned to Samaria. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:15
Jehoash’s other deeds, achievements, might, and war against Judah’s King Amaziah are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:16
He died and was buried with Israel’s kings in Samaria. His son Jeroboam succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:17
Judah’s King Amaziah son of Joash outlived Israel’s King Jehoash son of Jehoahaz by fifteen years. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:18
Amaziah’s other acts are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:19
Conspirators plotted against him in Jerusalem. He fled to Lachish, but men pursued and killed him there. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:20
They brought him back on horses and buried him with his fathers in Jerusalem, in the City of David. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:21
Judah’s people made sixteen-year-old Azariah king after his father Amaziah. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:22
Azariah rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah after Amaziah died. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:23
Jeroboam son of Jehoash became Israel’s king in year fifteen of Amaziah son of Joash’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria forty-one years. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:24
He did evil before the LORD and kept all the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:25
He restored Israel’s border from Lebo-hamath to the Sea of the Arabah. The LORD, Israel’s God, foretold this through Jonah son of Amittai, His prophet from Gath-hepher. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:26
The LORD saw Israel’s bitter suffering, slave and free alike. No one helped Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:27
The LORD said He would not erase Israel’s name from under heaven. He saved them through Jeroboam son of Jehoash. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:28
Jeroboam’s other deeds, achievements, might, and wars are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. This includes recovering Damascus and Hamath for Israel from Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 14:29
Jeroboam died and rested with his fathers, Israel’s kings. His son Zechariah succeeded him. 📖
