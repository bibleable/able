## 2 Kings 1:1
After Ahab died, Moab rebelled against Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 1:2
Ahaziah fell through his upper room’s lattice in Samaria and was injured. He sent messengers to ask Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god, whether he would recover. 📖
## 2 Kings 1:3
The LORD’s angel told Elijah the Tishbite to meet the king of Samaria’s messengers. “Ask, ‘Are you consulting Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god, because Israel has no God?’ 📖
## 2 Kings 1:4
Therefore the LORD says, ‘You will not rise from your bed. You will certainly die.’” Elijah went. 📖
## 2 Kings 1:5
The messengers returned. The king asked, “Why did you return?” ❓
## 2 Kings 1:6
They answered, “A man told us to return with the LORD’s message. ‘Does Israel have no God, that you consult Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god? Therefore you will not rise from your bed. You will certainly die.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 1:7
The king asked, “What was the man who told you this like?” ❓
## 2 Kings 1:8
They answered, “He was hairy, with a leather belt around his waist.” The king said, “That was Elijah the Tishbite.” 📖
## 2 Kings 1:9
Ahaziah sent a captain and fifty men to Elijah, who sat on a hilltop. The captain said, “Man of God, the king commands you to come down!” 📖
## 2 Kings 1:10
Elijah answered, “If I am a man of God, may fire from heaven consume you and your fifty men.” Fire came and consumed them. 📖
## 2 Kings 1:11
The king sent another captain with fifty men. He said, “Man of God, the king commands you to come down immediately!” 📖
## 2 Kings 1:12
Elijah answered, “If I am a man of God, may fire from heaven consume you and your fifty men.” God’s fire came and consumed them. 📖
## 2 Kings 1:13
The king sent a third captain and fifty men. The captain knelt before Elijah and begged, “Man of God, please value my life and these fifty servants’ lives. 🙏
## 2 Kings 1:14
Fire from heaven consumed the first two captains and their fifty men each. Please value my life.” 🙏
## 2 Kings 1:15
The LORD’s angel said, “Go with him. Do not fear him.” Elijah rose and went with him to the king. 📖
## 2 Kings 1:16
Elijah told Ahaziah, “The LORD says, ‘Is there no God in Israel whose word you could seek? Is that why you sent messengers to consult Baal-zebub, Ekron’s god? Therefore you will not rise from your bed. You will certainly die.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 1:17
Ahaziah died as the LORD said through Elijah. He had no son, so Jehoram succeeded him. This was in year two of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat’s reign over Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 1:18
Ahaziah’s other acts and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
