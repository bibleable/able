## 2 Kings 3:1
Jehoram son of Ahab became Israel’s king in year eighteen of Jehoshaphat’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria twelve years. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:2
He did evil before the LORD, though unlike his father and mother. He removed the sacred Baal pillar his father made. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:3
But he clung to the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. He did not abandon them. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:4
Mesha, Moab’s king, bred sheep. He paid Israel’s king a hundred thousand lambs and the wool of a hundred thousand rams. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:5
After Ahab died, Moab’s king rebelled against Israel’s king. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:6
Jehoram left Samaria and mobilized all Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:7
He asked Judah’s King Jehoshaphat, “Will you fight Moab with me? Its king rebelled.” Jehoshaphat answered, “Yes. I am as you are. My people and horses are yours.” 📖
## 2 Kings 3:8
He asked, “Which route shall we take?” Joram answered, “Through the Desert of Edom.” 📖
## 2 Kings 3:9
The kings of Israel, Judah, and Edom traveled a roundabout route for seven days. They ran out of water for their army and animals. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:10
Israel’s king said, “Alas! The LORD summoned us three kings to hand us over to Moab!” 📖
## 2 Kings 3:11
Jehoshaphat asked, “Is a prophet of the LORD here? Let us consult the LORD through him.” Israel’s king’s servant answered, “Elisha son of Shaphat is here. He poured water on Elijah’s hands.” 📖
## 2 Kings 3:12
Jehoshaphat said, “The LORD’s word is with him.” The three kings went down to Elisha. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:13
Elisha told Israel’s king, “What have we to do with each other? Consult your parents’ prophets!” The king replied, “No. The LORD summoned us three kings to hand us over to Moab.” 📖
## 2 Kings 3:14
Elisha said, “As surely as the LORD of Hosts lives, whom I serve, I would ignore you if I did not regard Judah’s King Jehoshaphat. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:15
Bring a harpist.” As the harpist played, the LORD’s hand came upon Elisha. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:16
He said, “The LORD says, ‘Fill this valley with ditches.’ 📖
## 2 Kings 3:17
The LORD says, ‘You will see neither wind nor rain. Yet water will fill the valley for you, your cattle, and other animals to drink.’ 📖
## 2 Kings 3:18
This is easy for the LORD. He will also hand the Moabites over to you. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:19
Attack every fortified and important city. Cut down every good tree, block every spring, and ruin every good field with stones.” 📖
## 2 Kings 3:20
The next morning, at sacrifice time, water came from Edom’s direction and filled the land. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:21
Moab heard the kings were coming to fight. All who could bear arms, young and old, were summoned and stationed at the border. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:22
Early in the morning, sunlight made the water look red as blood to the Moabites. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:23
They cried, “Blood! The kings killed one another. Moab, take the plunder!” 📖
## 2 Kings 3:24
The Moabites reached Israel’s camp. Israel attacked, and they fled. The Israelites invaded their land and struck down the Moabites. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:25
They destroyed the cities and covered every good field with stones. They blocked every spring and cut down every good tree. Only Kir-haraseth’s stones remained in place, but men with slings surrounded and attacked it too. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:26
Moab’s king saw the battle was too fierce. He took seven hundred swordsmen to break through to Edom’s king, but they failed. 📖
## 2 Kings 3:27
He offered his firstborn son and heir as a burnt offering on the city wall. Great fury came against Israel, so the Israelites withdrew to their land. 📖
