## 2 Kings 5:1
Naaman commanded Aram’s king’s army. His master highly honored him because the LORD gave Aram victory through him. He was a mighty warrior but had leprosy. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:2
Aramean raiders captured a young Israelite girl. She served Naaman’s wife. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:3
She said, “If my master visited Samaria’s prophet, he would cure his leprosy.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:4
Naaman told his master what the Israelite girl said. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:5
Aram’s king said, “Go. I will send a letter to Israel’s king.” Naaman took ten silver talents, six thousand gold shekels, and ten clothing sets. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:6
The letter said, “I am sending my servant Naaman for you to cure his leprosy.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:7
Israel’s king tore his clothes after reading. “Am I God, who kills and gives life? He expects me to cure leprosy! He seeks a quarrel!” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:8
Elisha heard and sent word to the king: “Why tear your clothes? Send him to me. He will know Israel has a prophet.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:9
Naaman arrived with horses and chariots and stopped at Elisha’s door. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:10
Elisha sent a messenger: “Wash seven times in the Jordan. Your flesh will be restored, and you will be clean.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:11
Naaman left angrily, saying, “I expected him to come out, stand, and call on the LORD his God. I expected him to wave his hand over the spot to cure my leprosy. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:12
Are Damascus’s rivers, Abanah and Pharpar, not better than Israel’s waters? Could I not wash there and be clean?” He left in rage. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:13
His servants said, “My father, would you not have done something great if the prophet asked? How much more should you simply wash and be cleansed?” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:14
He dipped in the Jordan seven times as the man of God said. His flesh became like a little child’s, and he was clean. ❤️
## 2 Kings 5:15
He returned with all his attendants. He said, “Now I know there is no God on earth except in Israel. Please accept your servant’s gift.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:16
Elisha said, “As surely as the LORD lives, whom I serve, I will accept nothing.” Naaman urged him, but he refused. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:17
Naaman said, “Then give your servant enough soil for two mules to carry. I will offer burnt offerings and sacrifices only to the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:18
May the LORD forgive this: My master leans on my arm while worshiping in Rimmon’s temple. When I bow there, may the LORD forgive me.” 🙏
## 2 Kings 5:19
Elisha said, “Go in peace.” Naaman traveled a short distance. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:20
Gehazi, Elisha’s servant, said, “My master spared Naaman the Aramean by refusing his gifts. As surely as the LORD lives, I will pursue him and get something.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:21
He pursued Naaman, who saw him running and left his chariot to meet him. Naaman asked, “Is everything all right?” ❓
## 2 Kings 5:22
Gehazi answered, “Yes. My master sent me. Two young sons of the prophets arrived from Ephraim’s hills. Please give them a silver talent and two clothing sets.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:23
Naaman urged him to accept two talents. He packed two silver talents in two bags and two clothing sets. Two servants carried them ahead of Gehazi. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:24
At the hill, Gehazi took the gifts and stored them in the house. He dismissed the servants, who left. 📖
## 2 Kings 5:25
Elisha asked the returning Gehazi, “Where were you?” He answered, “Your servant went nowhere.” 📖
## 2 Kings 5:26
Elisha asked, “Did my spirit not accompany you when he left his chariot to meet you? Is this a time to accept money, clothing, olive groves, vineyards, sheep, oxen, and male and female servants? 📖
## 2 Kings 5:27
Naaman’s leprosy will cling to you and your descendants forever!” Gehazi left, leprous and white as snow. 📖
