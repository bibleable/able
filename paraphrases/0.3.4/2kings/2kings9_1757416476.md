## 2 Kings 9:1
Elisha summoned a son of the prophets. He said, “Tuck your cloak into your belt. Take this oil flask to Ramoth-gilead. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:2
Find Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi. Take him from his companions to an inner room. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:3
Pour oil on his head. Say, ‘The LORD says, “I anoint you Israel’s king.”’ Open the door and run without delay!” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:4
The young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:5
The army commanders sat there. He said, “Commander, I have a message.” Jehu asked, “For whom?” He answered, “You.” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:6
Jehu entered the house. The prophet poured oil on his head. “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I anoint you king over the LORD’s people Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:7
Strike down your master Ahab’s house. I will avenge the blood of My servants, the prophets, and all the LORD’s servants Jezebel killed. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:8
Ahab’s whole house will perish. I will destroy every male belonging to Ahab in Israel, slave and free. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:9
His house will become like Jeroboam son of Nebat’s and Baasha son of Ahijah’s houses. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:10
Dogs will devour Jezebel on Jezreel’s plot of ground. No one will bury her.’” The prophet opened the door and ran. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:11
Jehu returned. His master’s servants asked, “Is everything all right? Why did this madman visit?” He answered, “You know his kind and their babble.” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:12
They said, “That is a lie! Tell us!” He answered, “He said, ‘The LORD says, “I anoint you Israel’s king.”’” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:13
They quickly spread their garments under him on the bare steps. They blew the ram’s horn, proclaiming, “Jehu is king!” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:14
Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. Joram and all Israel had been defending Ramoth-gilead against Aram’s King Hazael. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:15
Joram had returned to Jezreel to recover from Aramean wounds received fighting Hazael. Jehu said, “If you want me as king, let no one leave to tell Jezreel.” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:16
Jehu drove to Jezreel because Joram was recovering there. Judah’s King Ahaziah had come to visit. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:17
Jezreel’s tower watchman saw Jehu’s troops and reported them. Joram ordered a rider to ask whether they came in peace. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:18
The rider delivered the king’s question. Jehu answered, “What do you know about peace? Follow behind me.” The watchman said the messenger reached them but did not return. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:19
A second rider asked the king’s question. Jehu answered, “What do you know about peace? Follow behind me.” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:20
The watchman said, “He is not returning either. The driver drives madly, like Jehu son of Nimshi!” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:21
Joram ordered his chariot harnessed. Israel’s King Joram and Judah’s King Ahaziah rode in separate chariots and met Jehu on Naboth the Jezreelite’s property. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:22
Joram asked, “Do you come in peace?” Jehu answered, “How can there be peace while your mother Jezebel’s idolatry and witchcraft abound?” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:23
Joram fled, shouting to Ahaziah, “Treachery, Ahaziah!” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:24
Jehu drew his bow and shot Joram between the shoulders. The arrow pierced his heart, and he slumped in his chariot. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:25
Jehu told his officer Bidkar, “Throw him in Naboth the Jezreelite’s field. Remember riding behind his father Ahab together? The LORD pronounced this message against him: 📖
## 2 Kings 9:26
‘I saw Naboth’s and his sons’ blood yesterday. I will repay you on this ground,’ the LORD declared. Throw him there as the LORD said.” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:27
Judah’s King Ahaziah fled toward Beth-haggan. Jehu pursued, ordering him shot too. They shot him in his chariot on the Ascent of Gur near Ibleam. He fled to Megiddo and died. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:28
His servants carried him by chariot to Jerusalem. They buried him with his fathers in his tomb in the City of David. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:29
Ahaziah became Judah’s king in year eleven of Joram son of Ahab’s reign. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:30
Jezebel heard Jehu reached Jezreel. She painted her eyes, adorned her head, and looked from a window. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:31
He entered the gate. She asked, “Do you come in peace, Zimri, your master’s murderer?” ❓
## 2 Kings 9:32
He called to the window, “Who supports me? Who?” Two or three eunuchs looked down. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:33
He ordered them to throw her down. Her blood splashed on the wall and horses as the horses trampled her. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:34
Jehu ate and drank. He said, “Bury this cursed woman. She was a king’s daughter.” 📖
## 2 Kings 9:35
They found only her skull, feet, and the palms of her hands. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:36
They reported this. Jehu said, “The LORD said through Elijah the Tishbite, ‘Dogs will devour Jezebel’s flesh on Jezreel’s plot of ground. 📖
## 2 Kings 9:37
Her body will lie like dung there in the field. No one will be able to say, “This is Jezebel.”’” 📖
