## 2 Kings 24:1
Babylon’s King Nebuchadnezzar invaded during Jehoiakim’s reign. Jehoiakim served him three years, then rebelled. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:2
The LORD sent Chaldean, Aramean, Moabite, and Ammonite raiders against Jehoiakim to destroy Judah, as His prophets foretold. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:3
The LORD commanded this to remove Judah from His presence because of Manasseh’s sins and all his deeds. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:4
It was also because he filled Jerusalem with innocent blood. The LORD was unwilling to forgive. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:5
Jehoiakim’s other deeds and achievements are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:6
He died, and his son Jehoiachin succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:7
Egypt’s king stopped marching beyond his land because Babylon’s king took all his territory. This stretched from the Brook of Egypt to the Euphrates River. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:8
Jehoiachin became king at eighteen and ruled in Jerusalem three months. His mother was Nehushta daughter of Elnathan, from Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:9
He did evil before the LORD like his father. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:10
Nebuchadnezzar’s servants besieged Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:11
Babylon’s King Nebuchadnezzar arrived during their siege. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:12
Jehoiachin, his mother, servants, commanders, and officials surrendered to Babylon’s king. Babylon’s king took him captive in the eighth year of his reign. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:13
Nebuchadnezzar took all temple and palace treasures as the LORD foretold. He cut apart every gold temple article made by Israel’s King Solomon. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:14
He exiled Jerusalem’s people, ten thousand captives in all. These included all commanders, mighty warriors, craftsmen, and metalsmiths. Only the poorest people remained. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:15
He exiled Jehoiachin, his mother, wives, officials, and the land’s leading men from Jerusalem to Babylon. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:16
He exiled seven thousand warriors and a thousand craftsmen and metalsmiths to Babylon. All were strong and fit for battle. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:17
He appointed Jehoiachin’s uncle Mattaniah king and renamed him Zedekiah. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:18
Zedekiah became king at twenty-one and ruled in Jerusalem eleven years. His mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah, from Libnah. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:19
He did evil before the LORD like Jehoiakim. 📖
## 2 Kings 24:20
The LORD’s anger caused all this in Jerusalem and Judah until He banished them from His presence. Zedekiah also rebelled against Babylon’s king. 📖
