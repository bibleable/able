## 2 Kings 19:1
Hezekiah heard, tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and entered the LORD’s house. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:2
He sent palace administrator Eliakim, scribe Shebna, and leading priests in sackcloth to Isaiah son of Amoz. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:3
They said, “Hezekiah says this day brings distress, rebuke, and disgrace. Children are ready for birth, but there is no strength to deliver them. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:4
Perhaps the LORD your God will hear and rebuke the Rabshakeh, whom Assyria’s king sent to defy the living God. Pray for the surviving remnant.” 🙏
## 2 Kings 19:5
Hezekiah’s servants went to Isaiah. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:6
He replied, “Tell your master the LORD says, ‘Do not fear Assyria’s servants’ blasphemous words against Me. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:7
I will put a spirit in him. He will hear a rumor and return home, where I will cause him to die by the sword.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 19:8
The Rabshakeh heard Assyria’s king left Lachish. He withdrew and found him fighting Libnah. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:9
Sennacherib heard Cush’s King Tirhakah was marching to fight him. He again sent messengers to Hezekiah. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:10
“Tell Judah’s King Hezekiah, ‘Do not let your God deceive you by promising Jerusalem will not fall to Assyria’s king. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:11
You heard how Assyria’s kings utterly destroyed other countries. Will you escape? ❓
## 2 Kings 19:12
Did their gods rescue Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, or Eden’s people in Telassar from my fathers? ❓
## 2 Kings 19:13
Where are Hamath’s, Arpad’s, Sepharvaim’s, Hena’s, and Ivvah’s kings?’” ❓
## 2 Kings 19:14
Hezekiah received and read the letter. He spread it before the LORD in His house. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:15
He prayed, “LORD, Israel’s God enthroned between the cherubim, You alone are God over every earthly kingdom. You made heaven and earth. 🙏
## 2 Kings 19:16
LORD, listen and open Your eyes to see. Hear Sennacherib’s words defying the living God. 🙏
## 2 Kings 19:17
LORD, Assyria’s kings truly destroyed these nations and lands. 🙏
## 2 Kings 19:18
They burned their gods because they were only human-made wood and stone, not gods. 🙏
## 2 Kings 19:19
LORD our God, rescue us from him so every kingdom knows You alone are God.” 🙏
## 2 Kings 19:20
Isaiah son of Amoz sent word: “The LORD, Israel’s God, says, ‘I heard your prayer about Assyria’s King Sennacherib.’ 📖
## 2 Kings 19:21
The LORD says against him, ‘The Virgin Daughter of Zion despises and mocks you. The Daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head behind you. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:22
Whom did you taunt and blaspheme? Against whom did you raise your voice and lift your eyes proudly? Israel’s Holy One! 📖
## 2 Kings 19:23
Through your servants you taunted the Lord, saying, “With my many chariots I climbed mountain heights to Lebanon’s remotest peaks. I cut its tallest cedars and finest cypresses. I reached its farthest outposts and densest forests. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:24
I dug wells and drank foreign water. My feet’s soles dried all Egypt’s streams.” 📖
## 2 Kings 19:25
Have you not heard? I ordained and planned this long ago. I now brought it about, that you would crush fortified cities into rubble. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:26
Their powerless people are therefore dismayed and ashamed. They are like field plants, tender shoots, and rooftop grass scorched before it grows. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:27
I know when you sit, leave, or enter, and how you rage against Me. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:28
Your rage and arrogance reached My ears. I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your mouth. I will send you back as you came.’ 📖
## 2 Kings 19:29
Hezekiah, this is your sign. This year you will eat what grows by itself, and next year what springs from the same. In year three you will sow, reap, plant vineyards, and eat their fruit. 🌱
## 2 Kings 19:30
Judah’s surviving remnant will again take root below and bear fruit above. 🌱
## 2 Kings 19:31
Survivors will come from Jerusalem and Mount Zion. The LORD of Hosts’ zeal will accomplish this. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:32
The LORD says of Assyria’s king, ‘He will not enter this city or shoot an arrow here. He will not approach with a shield or build a siege ramp. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:33
He will return as he came and not enter this city,’ declares the LORD. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:34
‘I will defend and save it for My sake and My servant David’s sake.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 19:35
That night the LORD’s angel struck down 185,000 men in Assyria’s camp. People rose next morning and saw all the dead bodies. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:36
Sennacherib withdrew to Nineveh and stayed there. 📖
## 2 Kings 19:37
His sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with a sword while he worshiped in Nisroch’s temple. They fled to Ararat. His son Esar-haddon succeeded him. 📖
