## 2 Kings 13:1
Jehoahaz son of Jehu became Israel’s king in year twenty-three of Joash son of Ahaziah’s reign over Judah. He ruled in Samaria seventeen years. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:2
He did evil before the LORD and kept the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:3
The LORD’s anger burned against Israel. He repeatedly handed them to Aram’s King Hazael and his son Ben-hadad. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:4
Jehoahaz sought the LORD’s favor. The LORD listened because He saw Aram’s king oppressing Israel. 🙏
## 2 Kings 13:5
He gave Israel a deliverer. They escaped Aram’s power and lived in their homes as before. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:6
They kept the sins Jeroboam’s house led Israel to commit. The Asherah pole still stood in Samaria. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:7
Only fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers remained to Jehoahaz. Aram’s king destroyed his army, making it like dust at threshing. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:8
Jehoahaz’s other deeds, achievements, and might are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:9
He died and was buried in Samaria. His son Jehoash succeeded him. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:10
Jehoash son of Jehoahaz became Israel’s king in Samaria in year thirty-seven of Joash’s reign over Judah. He ruled sixteen years. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:11
He did evil before the LORD and kept all the sins Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel to commit. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:12
Jehoash’s other deeds, achievements, might, and war against Judah’s King Amaziah are recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:13
Jehoash died, and Jeroboam succeeded him. Jehoash was buried with Israel’s kings in Samaria. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:14
Elisha became ill with the sickness that would kill him. Israel’s King Jehoash wept over him: “My father, my father, Israel’s chariots and horsemen!” 💔
## 2 Kings 13:15
Elisha told him to take a bow and arrows. He did. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:16
Elisha told him to put his hand on the bow. The king obeyed, and Elisha placed his hands on the king’s hands. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:17
Elisha told him to open the east window and shoot. He obeyed. Elisha said, “The LORD’s arrow of victory over Aram! You will strike the Arameans at Aphek until you destroy them.” 📖
## 2 Kings 13:18
Elisha said, “Take the arrows and strike the ground.” The king struck three times and stopped. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:19
Elisha was angry. “You should have struck five or six times. Then you would have destroyed Aram. Now you will defeat it only three times.” 📖
## 2 Kings 13:20
Elisha died and was buried. Moabite raiders entered the land every spring. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:21
Israelites burying a man saw raiders and threw his body into Elisha’s tomb. It touched Elisha’s bones, and the man revived and stood. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:22
Aram’s King Hazael oppressed Israel throughout Jehoahaz’s reign. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:23
The LORD showed grace and compassion because of His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. To this day He has refused to destroy Israel or banish them from His presence. ❤️
## 2 Kings 13:24
Hazael died, and his son Ben-hadad succeeded him as Aram’s king. 📖
## 2 Kings 13:25
Jehoash son of Jehoahaz recovered from Ben-hadad son of Hazael the cities Hazael captured from his father Jehoahaz. Jehoash defeated Ben-hadad three times and recovered Israel’s cities. 📖
