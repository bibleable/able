## 2 Kings 7:1
Elisha said, “Hear the LORD’s word. Tomorrow at this time, a seah of fine flour will cost one shekel at Samaria’s gate. Two seahs of barley will also cost one shekel.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:2
The officer on whose arm the king leaned asked, “Could this happen even if the LORD opened heaven’s windows?” Elisha answered, “You will see it but eat none.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:3
Four lepers at the city gate asked, “Why sit here until we die? ❓
## 2 Kings 7:4
Famine will kill us in the city, and we will die here too. Let us go to the Arameans’ camp. If they spare us, we live. If they kill us, we die.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:5
They went at twilight and found no one at the camp’s edge. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:6
The Lord made the Arameans hear chariots, horses, and a great army. They said, “Israel’s king must have hired Hittite and Egyptian kings to attack!” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:7
They fled for their lives at twilight, leaving tents, horses, and donkeys. The camp remained intact. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:8
The lepers ate and drank in a tent, then took silver, gold, and clothing and hid them. They returned to another tent, took more, and hid it. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:9
They said, “This is wrong. Today brings good news. If we stay silent until morning, our sin will overtake us. Let us tell the king’s household.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:10
They told the gatekeepers, “No one is in the Aramean camp, not a trace. Only tied horses and donkeys remain, with tents intact.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:11
The gatekeepers shouted the news, and the king’s household heard it. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:12
The king rose at night and said, “They know we are starving. They hid in the fields, planning to capture us alive when we leave and enter our city.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:13
A servant said, “Send scouts with five remaining horses. Their fate will be no worse than the Israelites still here, all doomed. Let us find out.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:14
Scouts took two chariots with horses. The king sent them after Aram’s army to investigate. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:15
They followed as far as the Jordan. Clothing and equipment the Arameans hastily discarded littered the road. They returned and reported. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:16
The people plundered Aram’s camp. A seah of fine flour cost one shekel, and two seahs of barley cost one shekel, as the LORD said. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:17
The king put the officer on whose arm he leaned in charge of the gate. The crowd trampled him there, and he died, as Elisha foretold during the king’s visit. 📖
## 2 Kings 7:18
It happened as the man of God told the king. “Tomorrow at this time, two seahs of barley will cost one shekel at Samaria’s gate. A seah of fine flour will cost one shekel.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:19
The officer had asked, “Could this happen even if the LORD opened heaven’s windows?” Elisha answered, “You will see it but eat none.” 📖
## 2 Kings 7:20
This happened. The crowd trampled him at the gate, and he died. 📖
