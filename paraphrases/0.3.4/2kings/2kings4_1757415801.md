## 2 Kings 4:1
A widow whose husband was one of the sons of the prophets cried to Elisha, “My husband, your servant, died. You know he feared the LORD. His creditor is coming to enslave my two children!” 💔
## 2 Kings 4:2
Elisha asked, “How can I help? What is in your house?” She answered, “Your servant has only a jar of oil.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:3
He said, “Borrow empty jars from all your neighbors. Get many. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:4
Shut yourself and your sons inside. Pour oil into the jars, setting full ones aside.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:5
She shut herself and her sons inside. They brought jars, and she poured. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:6
When they were full, she requested another. Her son said, “No jars remain.” The oil stopped flowing. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:7
She told the man of God. He said, “Sell the oil and pay your debt. You and your sons can live on the remainder.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:8
A prominent woman in Shunem persuaded Elisha to eat with her. Afterward, he ate there whenever he passed. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:9
She told her husband, “I know our frequent visitor is a holy man of God. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:10
Let us make a small upper room with a bed, table, chair, and lamp. He can stay there when he visits.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:11
One day Elisha visited and lay down upstairs. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:12
He told his servant Gehazi to call the Shunammite woman. She came and stood before him. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:13
Elisha told Gehazi, “Ask how we can repay her trouble for us. Shall we speak to the king or army commander for her?” She answered, “I have a home among my people.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:14
He asked, “What can we do for her?” Gehazi answered, “She has no son, and her husband is old.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:15
Elisha said, “Call her.” Gehazi called, and she stood in the doorway. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:16
Elisha said, “Next year at this time, you will hold a son.” She answered, “No, my lord! Man of God, do not lie to your maidservant.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:17
She conceived and bore a son at that time next year, as Elisha said. ❤️
## 2 Kings 4:18
The child grew. One day he went to his father among the harvesters. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:19
He cried, “My head! My head!” His father told a servant to carry him to his mother. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:20
The servant carried him to her. He sat on her lap until noon, then died. 💔
## 2 Kings 4:21
She laid him on the man of God’s bed, shut the door, and left. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:22
She asked her husband for a servant and donkey to visit the man of God quickly and return. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:23
He asked, “Why today? It is neither New Moon nor Sabbath.” She answered, “Everything is all right.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:24
She saddled the donkey and told her servant, “Drive onward. Do not slow down for me unless I ask.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:25
She went to Mount Carmel. The man of God saw her far away and told Gehazi, “There is the Shunammite woman. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:26
Run to ask whether she, her husband, and her child are all right.” She answered, “Everything is all right.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:27
She reached him and clung to his feet. Gehazi tried to push her away. Elisha said, “Leave her. She is deeply distressed, but the LORD hid it and did not tell me.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:28
She said, “My lord, did I ask for a son? Did I not ask you not to deceive me?” ❓
## 2 Kings 4:29
Elisha told Gehazi, “Tie up your garment, take my staff, and go. Greet no one, and answer no greetings. Lay my staff on the boy’s face.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:30
The mother said, “As surely as the LORD and you live, I will not leave you.” Elisha rose and followed her. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:31
Gehazi went ahead and laid the staff on the boy’s face. There was no sound or response. He returned to Elisha, saying, “The boy has not awakened.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:32
Elisha reached the house and found the boy dead on his bed. 💔
## 2 Kings 4:33
He shut himself inside with the boy and prayed to the LORD. 🙏
## 2 Kings 4:34
He lay on the boy, mouth to mouth, eye to eye, and hand to hand. As he stretched over him, the boy’s body warmed. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:35
Elisha paced across the room, then stretched over the boy again. The boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes. ❤️
## 2 Kings 4:36
Elisha told Gehazi to call the Shunammite woman. She came, and Elisha said, “Pick up your son.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:37
She fell at his feet and bowed to the ground. She took her son and left. ❤️
## 2 Kings 4:38
Elisha returned to Gilgal during a famine. The sons of the prophets sat at his feet. He told his attendant to cook stew for them in the large pot. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:39
A man gathering herbs found a wild vine and filled his garment with gourds. He cut them into the stew, though no one recognized them. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:40
The men tasted it and cried, “Man of God, death is in the pot!” They could not eat it. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:41
Elisha asked for flour and threw it in the pot. He said, “Serve it.” Nothing harmful remained. 📖
## 2 Kings 4:42
A man from Baal-shalishah brought a sack of twenty barley loaves made from the first ripe grain. Elisha said, “Give the people food.” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:43
His servant asked, “How can twenty loaves feed a hundred men?” Elisha said, “Serve them. The LORD says, ‘They will eat and have leftovers.’” 📖
## 2 Kings 4:44
He served them. They ate and had leftovers, as the LORD said. 📖
