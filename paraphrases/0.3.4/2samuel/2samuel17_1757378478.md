## 2 Samuel 17:1
Ahithophel told Absalom, “Let me choose twelve thousand men to pursue David tonight. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 17:2
I will attack him weak and weary, cause panic, and make all his people flee. I will kill only the king, ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 17:3
and bring everyone back to you like a bride returning to her husband. You seek only one man’s life. Then all the people will have peace.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:4
Absalom and Israel’s elders liked this proposal. 👥
## 2 Samuel 17:5
Absalom said, “Call Hushai the Archite too. Let us hear him.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 17:6
Hushai came. Absalom said, “Ahithophel proposed this. Should we do it? If not, what do you advise?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 17:7
Hushai answered, “Ahithophel’s advice is not sound this time.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:8
He continued, “You know your father and his mighty men. They are fierce like a wild bear robbed of her cubs. Your father is a man of war and will not sleep among the troops. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:9
He is already hiding in a cave or elsewhere. If some of your troops fall first, anyone hearing it will report a slaughter among Absalom’s followers. ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 17:10
Even a brave soldier with a lion’s heart will melt with fear. All Israel knows your father and his men are mighty and brave. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:11
Gather all Israel from Dan to Beersheba, as numerous as seashore sand. Lead them into battle yourself. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 17:12
Then we will attack David wherever we find him and descend on him like dew on the ground. Not one man with him will remain. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 17:13
If he retreats into a city, all Israel will bring ropes. We will drag it into the valley until not even a pebble remains.” ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 17:14
Absalom and Israel’s men preferred Hushai the Archite’s advice. The LORD intended to thwart Ahithophel’s good counsel to bring disaster on Absalom. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:15
Hushai told the priests Zadok and Abiathar what Ahithophel advised Absalom and Israel’s elders. He told them his own advice too. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:16
He said, “Tell David quickly not to stay at the wilderness fords tonight. He must cross, or he and all his people will be swallowed up.” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 17:17
Jonathan and Ahimaaz stayed at En-rogel. A servant girl brought information to them, which they took to King David. They dared not enter the city where they could be seen. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:18
A young man saw them and told Absalom. The two hurried to a house in Bahurim and climbed into its courtyard well. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:19
The man’s wife covered the well’s opening and spread grain over it so no one would know. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:20
Absalom’s servants asked her, “Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?” She answered, “They crossed the brook.” They searched, found nothing, and returned to Jerusalem. 💬
## 2 Samuel 17:21
The two left the well after the men departed. They told King David, “Cross the river immediately. Ahithophel advised this against you.” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 17:22
David and all his people crossed the Jordan. By dawn, not one remained who had not crossed. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:23
Ahithophel saw his advice was not followed. He saddled his donkey and went home to his town. He put his affairs in order, hanged himself, and died. He was buried in his father’s tomb. 🪦
## 2 Samuel 17:24
David went to Mahanaim. Absalom crossed the Jordan with all Israel’s men. 👥
## 2 Samuel 17:25
Absalom appointed Amasa over the army instead of Joab. Amasa’s father was Ithra, an Ishmaelite who married Abigail daughter of Nahash. She was the sister of Zeruiah, Joab’s mother. 👥
## 2 Samuel 17:26
Absalom and the Israelites camped in Gilead. 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:27
At Mahanaim, David met Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the Ammonites. He also met Machir son of Ammiel from Lo-debar and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim. 👥
## 2 Samuel 17:28
They brought beds, basins, earthen vessels, wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, lentils, 📖
## 2 Samuel 17:29
honey, curds, sheep, and cheese from the herd for David and his people. They said, “The people are hungry, exhausted, and thirsty in the wilderness.” 📖
