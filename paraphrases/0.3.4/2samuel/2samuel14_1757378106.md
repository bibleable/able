## 2 Samuel 14:1
Joab son of Zeruiah saw that the king’s heart longed for Absalom. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:2
He brought a wise woman from Tekoa. He told her, “Pretend to mourn. Wear mourning clothes and do not anoint yourself with oil. Act as if you mourned the dead a long time. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:3
Go to the king and say these words.” Joab told her what to say. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:4
The woman from Tekoa fell facedown before the king to honor him. She said, “Help me, king!” 🙇
## 2 Samuel 14:5
He asked, “What troubles you?” She said, “I am a widow. My husband died. 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:6
Your maidservant had two sons. They fought in a field with no one to separate them. One struck and killed the other. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:7
The whole clan demands, ‘Give us the brother’s killer so we can kill him for his brother’s life. We will cut off the heir too!’ They would extinguish my last ember and leave my husband no name or descendants on earth.” 💔
## 2 Samuel 14:8
The king said, “Go home. I will give orders for you.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:9
The woman of Tekoa said, “My lord the king, let blame fall on me and my father’s house. Let the king and his throne remain innocent.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:10
He said, “If anyone speaks to you, bring him to me. He will not trouble you again.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:11
She said, “Invoke the LORD your God to stop the avenger of blood from causing more devastation. Do not let my son be destroyed!” He swore, “As surely as the LORD lives, not a hair of your son’s head will fall to the ground.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:12
She asked, “May your servant speak to my lord the king?” He answered, “Speak.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:13
She asked, “Why plan this against God’s people? Does the king not condemn himself by saying this when he has not returned his own banished son? ❓
## 2 Samuel 14:14
We will die like water poured onto the ground that cannot be recovered. Yet God does not take a life. He devises ways so a banished one may not remain cast out from Him. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:15
I brought this matter because the people frightened me. Your servant thought, ‘I will speak to the king. Perhaps he will grant my request. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:16
He will hear and rescue his maidservant from the man who would cut off me and my son from God’s inheritance.’ 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:17
Your servant says, ‘May my lord the king’s word bring me rest. Like God’s angel, he can distinguish good from evil. May the LORD your God be with you.’” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 14:18
The king said, “I will ask you something. Do not hide it.” She answered, “Let my lord the king speak.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:19
He asked, “Is Joab behind this?” She answered, “As surely as you live, my lord the king, no one can turn right or left from what you say. Your servant Joab commanded me and told me exactly what to say. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:20
Joab did this to change these affairs. My lord has wisdom like God’s angel and knows everything happening in the land.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:21
The king told Joab, “I grant the request. Bring back the young man Absalom.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:22
Joab bowed facedown and blessed the king. He said, “Today your servant knows you favor him because you granted his request, my lord the king.” 🙇
## 2 Samuel 14:23
Joab went to Geshur and brought Absalom to Jerusalem. 👥
## 2 Samuel 14:24
The king said, “He may return home but must not see my face.” Absalom returned home without seeing the king. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:25
No man in Israel was as handsome and highly praised as Absalom. He had no flaw from the soles of his feet to his head. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:26
He shaved his hair each year because it became heavy. He weighed it at two hundred shekels by the royal standard. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:27
Absalom had three sons and a daughter named Tamar, a beautiful woman. 👥
## 2 Samuel 14:28
Absalom lived in Jerusalem two years without seeing the king. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:29
He sent for Joab to send him to the king. Joab refused to come. Absalom sent a second time, but Joab still refused. 📖
## 2 Samuel 14:30
Absalom told his servants, “Joab’s barley field is beside mine. Burn it!” They burned his field. 🔥
## 2 Samuel 14:31
Joab came to Absalom’s house and asked, “Why did your servants burn my field?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:32
Absalom said, “I asked you to come so I could send you to the king. Why did I return from Geshur? I was better off there. Let me see the king. If I am guilty, let him kill me.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 14:33
Joab told the king, who summoned Absalom. He came and bowed facedown before David. The king kissed him. 🙇
