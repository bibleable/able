## 2 Samuel 1:1
After Saul died, David returned from slaughtering the Amalekites. He stayed in Ziklag for two days. 📖
## 2 Samuel 1:2
On the third day, a man came from Saul’s camp with torn clothes and dust on his head. He fell before David to honor him. 👥
## 2 Samuel 1:3
David asked, “Where did you come from?” He answered, “I escaped from the Israelite camp.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 1:4
David asked, “What happened? Please tell me.” He answered, “The troops fled the battle. Many fell and died. Saul and his son Jonathan are dead too.” 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:5
David asked the young man who brought the report, “How do you know Saul and his son Jonathan are dead?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 1:6
He answered, “I happened to be on Mount Gilboa. Saul leaned on his spear, and chariots and cavalry were closing in. 📖
## 2 Samuel 1:7
He turned, saw me, and called. I answered, ‘Here I am!’ 💬
## 2 Samuel 1:8
He asked, ‘Who are you?’ I said, ‘I am an Amalekite.’ 💬
## 2 Samuel 1:9
He begged, ‘Stand over me and kill me. Agony has seized me, but my life still lingers.’ 📖
## 2 Samuel 1:10
I stood over him and killed him because I knew he could not survive after falling. I took his crown and the band on his arm. I brought them here to my lord.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 1:11
David tore his clothes. All the men with him did the same. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:12
They mourned, wept, and fasted until evening for Saul, Jonathan his son, the LORD’s people, and Israel’s house. This was because they had fallen by the sword. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:13
David asked the young man, “Where are you from?” He answered, “I am a foreigner’s son, an Amalekite.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 1:14
David asked, “Why were you not afraid to raise your hand against the LORD’s anointed and kill him?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 1:15
David called a young man and said, “Execute him!” The young man struck him down, and he died. ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 1:16
David had told the Amalekite, “Your blood is on your own head. This is because your own mouth testified against you, saying, ‘I killed the LORD’s anointed.’” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 1:17
David lamented Saul and his son Jonathan with these words, 🎶
## 2 Samuel 1:18
and ordered Judah’s sons to learn the Song of the Bow. It is written in the Book of Jashar: 📜
## 2 Samuel 1:19
“Israel, your glory lies slain on your heights. How the mighty have fallen! 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:20
Do not tell it in Gath or proclaim it in Ashkelon’s streets. Otherwise the Philistines’ daughters will rejoice, and the daughters of the uncircumcised will celebrate. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:21
Mountains of Gilboa, may you have no dew, rain, or fields yielding grain offerings. There the mighty warrior’s shield was defiled, Saul’s shield, no longer anointed with oil. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:22
Jonathan’s bow did not retreat from the slain’s blood or the mighty’s fat. Saul’s sword did not return empty. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 1:23
Saul and Jonathan were loved and delightful in life, and not divided in death. They were faster than eagles and stronger than lions. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:24
Daughters of Israel, weep for Saul. He clothed you in scarlet and luxury and adorned your garments with gold ornaments. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:25
How the mighty have fallen in the battle’s thickest part! Jonathan lies slain on your heights. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:26
I grieve for you, Jonathan my brother. You were delightful to me. Your love for me was extraordinary, surpassing the love of women. 💔
## 2 Samuel 1:27
How the mighty have fallen! The weapons of war have perished!” 💔
