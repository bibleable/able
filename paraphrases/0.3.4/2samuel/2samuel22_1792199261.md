## 2 Samuel 22:1
David sang this song to the LORD when He delivered him from all his enemies and Saul: 🎶
## 2 Samuel 22:2
“The LORD is my rock, fortress, and deliverer. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:3
My God is my rock and refuge, my shield and the horn of my salvation. My stronghold, refuge, and Savior, You save me from violence. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:4
I will call on the LORD, who deserves praise, and so be saved from my enemies. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:5
Death’s waves engulfed me. Torrents of chaos overwhelmed me. 🌊
## 2 Samuel 22:6
Sheol’s cords entangled me. Death’s snares confronted me. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:7
In distress I called to the LORD and cried to my God. He heard my voice from His temple. My cry reached His ears. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:8
The earth shook and quaked. Heaven’s foundations trembled because He burned with anger. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:9
Smoke rose from His nostrils. Consuming fire came from His mouth, and glowing coals blazed. 🔥
## 2 Samuel 22:10
He parted heaven and descended, with dark clouds under His feet. ☁️
## 2 Samuel 22:11
He mounted a cherub and flew on the wind’s wings. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:12
He made darkness, gathered water, and thick clouds His surrounding canopy. ☁️
## 2 Samuel 22:13
Fiery coals blazed from His presence’s brightness. 🔥
## 2 Samuel 22:14
The LORD thundered from heaven. The Most High’s voice sounded. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:15
His arrows scattered the enemies. His lightning routed them. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:16
The sea’s channels appeared and the world’s foundations were exposed at the LORD’s rebuke and the blast of breath from His nostrils. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:17
He reached from above, took hold of me, and drew me from deep waters. 🌊
## 2 Samuel 22:18
He rescued me from my powerful enemy and foes too strong for me. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:19
They confronted me in my day of disaster, but the LORD supported me. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:20
He brought me into the open. He rescued me because He delighted in me. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 22:21
The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness. He repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:22
I kept the LORD’s ways and did not wickedly leave my God. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:23
All His ordinances are before me. I have not disregarded His statutes. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:24
I have been blameless before Him and kept myself from sin. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:25
The LORD repaid me according to my righteousness and cleanness in His sight. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:26
You show Yourself faithful to the faithful and blameless to the blameless. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:27
You show Yourself pure to the pure and shrewd to the crooked. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:28
You save afflicted people. You watch the proud to bring them down. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:29
LORD, You are my lamp. You light my darkness. ✨
## 2 Samuel 22:30
In You I can charge an army. With my God I can climb a wall. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:31
God’s way is perfect, and the LORD’s word is flawless. He shields everyone who takes refuge in Him. 🛡️
## 2 Samuel 22:32
Who is God except the LORD? Who is the Rock except our God? 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:33
God is my strong fortress. He makes my way clear. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:34
He makes my feet like a deer’s and places me on the heights. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:35
He trains my hands for battle. My arms can bend a bronze bow. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:36
You gave me Your shield of salvation. Your gentleness exalts me. 🛡️
## 2 Samuel 22:37
You widen the path beneath me so my ankles do not give way. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:38
I pursued and destroyed my enemies. I did not turn back until they were consumed. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:39
I devoured and crushed them so they could not rise. They fell beneath my feet. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:40
You armed me with strength for battle and subdued my enemies beneath me. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:41
You made my enemies retreat. I destroyed those who hated me. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:42
They looked for help, but no one saved them. They looked to the LORD, but He did not answer. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:43
I ground them like earth’s dust. I crushed and trampled them like mud in the streets. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 22:44
You rescued me from my people’s strife and preserved me as head of nations. A people I did not know will serve me. 👑
## 2 Samuel 22:45
Foreigners cower before me. When they hear me, they obey. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:46
Foreigners lose heart and come trembling from their strongholds. 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:47
The LORD lives! Blessed be my Rock! May God, the Rock of my salvation, be exalted, 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:48
the God who avenges me and subdues nations beneath me, 📖
## 2 Samuel 22:49
who frees me from my enemies. You exalt me over my foes and rescue me from violent men. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 22:50
I will praise You among the nations, LORD. I will sing praises to Your name. 🎶
## 2 Samuel 22:51
He brings great salvation to His king. He shows faithful love to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever.” ❤️
