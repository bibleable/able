## 2 Samuel 2:1
Later, David asked the LORD, “Should I go to a town of Judah?” The LORD answered, “Go.” David asked, “Where?” The LORD answered, “Hebron.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 2:2
David went with his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal of Carmel. 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:3
His men came too, each with his household. They settled in the towns near Hebron. 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:4
Judah’s men came to Hebron and anointed David king over Judah’s house. They told him, “The men of Jabesh-gilead buried Saul.” 👑
## 2 Samuel 2:5
David sent messengers to Jabesh-gilead’s men. They said, “May the LORD bless you because you kindly buried Saul your lord. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 2:6
May the LORD show you faithful love and faithfulness. I will show you the same favor because you did this. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 2:7
Be strong and courageous. Saul your lord is dead, but Judah’s house anointed me as their king.” 👑
## 2 Samuel 2:8
Abner son of Ner, Saul’s army commander, took Saul’s son Ish-bosheth to Mahanaim, 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:9
and made him king of Gilead, Asher, Jezreel, Ephraim, and Benjamin, all Israel. 👑
## 2 Samuel 2:10
Ish-bosheth son of Saul became Israel’s king at forty years old. He reigned two years. Judah’s house followed David. 👑
## 2 Samuel 2:11
David ruled Judah’s house from Hebron for seven years and six months. 👑
## 2 Samuel 2:12
Abner son of Ner and the servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul marched from Mahanaim to Gibeon. 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:13
Joab son of Zeruiah and David’s servants met them at Gibeon’s pool. The two groups stood on opposite sides. 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:14
Abner told Joab, “Let the young men rise and compete before us.” Joab answered, “Let them rise.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 2:15
They counted twelve men for Benjamin and Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and twelve for David. 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:16
Each grabbed his opponent’s head and drove his sword into his opponent’s side. They all fell together. That place in Gibeon is called Helkath-hazzurim. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 2:17
The battle was fierce that day. David’s servants defeated Abner and Israel’s men. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 2:18
Zeruiah’s three sons were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel ran as swiftly as a wild gazelle, 📖
## 2 Samuel 2:19
and chased Abner without turning right or left. 📖
## 2 Samuel 2:20
Abner looked back and asked, “Is that you, Asahel?” Asahel answered, “It is.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 2:21
Abner said, “Turn right or left. Seize a young man and take his equipment.” Asahel would not stop chasing him. 📖
## 2 Samuel 2:22
Abner warned again, “Stop chasing me. Why should I strike you down? How could I face your brother Joab?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 2:23
Asahel refused to turn away. Abner drove the butt of his spear into Asahel’s stomach, and it came out his back. He fell dead there. Every man stopped at the place where Asahel died. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 2:24
Joab and Abishai pursued Abner. At sunset they reached Ammah’s hill opposite Giah, on the way to Gibeon’s wilderness. 📖
## 2 Samuel 2:25
The Benjamites gathered with Abner as one group and stood on a hilltop. 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:26
Abner called to Joab, “Must the sword consume forever? Do you not know this ends in bitterness? When will you tell the troops to stop pursuing their brothers?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 2:27
Joab answered, “As surely as God lives, if you had not spoken, the troops would have pursued their brothers until morning.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 2:28
Joab blew the ram’s horn. All the troops stopped pursuing Israel and fighting. 📯
## 2 Samuel 2:29
Abner and his men marched through the Arabah all night. They crossed the Jordan, marched all morning, and reached Mahanaim. 📖
## 2 Samuel 2:30
Joab returned from pursuing Abner and gathered his troops. Nineteen of David’s servants were missing, besides Asahel, 👥
## 2 Samuel 2:31
but they killed 360 Benjamites who were with Abner. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 2:32
They buried Asahel in his father’s tomb in Bethlehem. Joab and his men marched all night and reached Hebron at dawn. 🪦
