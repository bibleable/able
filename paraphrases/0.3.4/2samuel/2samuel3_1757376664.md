## 2 Samuel 3:1
Saul’s house and David’s house fought a long war. David grew stronger, and Saul’s house grew weaker. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 3:2
David had sons in Hebron. His firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam of Jezreel. 👶
## 2 Samuel 3:3
His second was Chileab, by Abigail, Nabal of Carmel’s widow. His third was Absalom, son of Maacah daughter of King Talmai of Geshur. 👶
## 2 Samuel 3:4
His fourth was Adonijah, Haggith’s son. His fifth was Shephatiah, Abital’s son, 👶
## 2 Samuel 3:5
and his sixth was Ithream, by David’s wife Eglah. These sons were born in Hebron. 👶
## 2 Samuel 3:6
During the war between Saul’s and David’s houses, Abner strengthened his position in Saul’s house. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:7
Saul had a concubine named Rizpah daughter of Aiah. Ish-bosheth asked Abner, “Why did you sleep with my father’s concubine?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 3:8
Abner became furious at Ish-bosheth’s accusation. He asked, “Am I the head of a dog belonging to Judah? I remained loyal to your father Saul’s house, his brothers, and his friends. I did not hand you to David. Now you accuse me of wrongdoing with this woman! ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 3:9
May God punish Abner severely if I do not do for David what the LORD swore to him: 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:10
transfer the kingdom from Saul’s house and establish David’s throne over Israel and Judah, from Dan to Beersheba.” 👑
## 2 Samuel 3:11
Ish-bosheth feared Abner and dared say nothing more to him. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:12
Abner sent messengers to David with this message: “To whom does the land belong? Make a covenant with me, and I will help bring all Israel to you.” 🤝
## 2 Samuel 3:13
David answered, “Good. I will make a covenant with you. I require one thing: Do not appear before me without Saul’s daughter Michal.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 3:14
David sent messengers to Ish-bosheth son of Saul. He said, “Return my wife Michal. I betrothed her to myself for a hundred Philistine foreskins.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 3:15
Ish-bosheth sent for Michal and took her from her husband Paltiel son of Laish. 👥
## 2 Samuel 3:16
Her husband followed her, weeping all the way to Bahurim. Abner told him, “Go back.” He returned home. 💔
## 2 Samuel 3:17
Abner told Israel’s elders, “You wanted David as king in the past. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:18
Act now, because the LORD told David, ‘Through My servant David, I will save My people Israel from the Philistines and all their enemies.’” 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:19
Abner also spoke with the Benjamites. He went to David in Hebron and reported everything Israel and Benjamin’s whole house considered good. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:20
Abner and twenty men came to David in Hebron. David held a feast for them. 👥
## 2 Samuel 3:21
Abner told David, “Let me go at once and gather all Israel to my lord the king. Then they may make a covenant with you, and you may rule everything your heart desires.” David sent Abner away in peace. 🤝
## 2 Samuel 3:22
David’s soldiers and Joab returned from a raid with much plunder. Abner was not with David in Hebron because David sent him away in peace. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:23
Joab and all his troops arrived. He was told, “Abner son of Ner came to the king, who sent him away in peace.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:24
Joab told the king, “What have you done? Abner came to you. Why did you send him away? Now he escapes! ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 3:25
You know Abner son of Ner came to deceive you and track your movements and everything you do.” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 3:26
After leaving David, Joab sent messengers who brought Abner back from Sirah’s well. David knew nothing about it. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:27
Abner returned to Hebron. Joab drew him aside into the gateway as if to speak privately, then stabbed his stomach. Abner died because of the blood of Joab’s brother Asahel. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 3:28
David later heard and said, “I and my kingdom are forever innocent before the LORD concerning the blood of Abner son of Ner. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:29
May it turn on Joab’s head and his father’s whole house. May Joab’s house never lack someone with a discharge or skin disease, or one leaning on a staff, dying by the sword, or lacking food.” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 3:30
Joab and his brother Abishai murdered Abner because he killed their brother Asahel in the battle at Gibeon. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 3:31
David told Joab and everyone with him, “Tear your clothes, put on sackcloth, and mourn before Abner.” King David himself walked behind the funeral bier. 💔
## 2 Samuel 3:32
They buried Abner in Hebron. The king wept loudly at Abner’s tomb, and all the people wept. 🪦
## 2 Samuel 3:33
The king sang this lament for Abner: “Should Abner die like a fool? 🎶
## 2 Samuel 3:34
Your hands were not tied, and your feet were not bound. You fell as a man falls before the wicked.” All the people wept even more. 💔
## 2 Samuel 3:35
Before day ended, the people urged David to eat. He swore, “May God punish me severely if I taste bread or anything else before sunset!” 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:36
The people noticed and were pleased. Everything the king did pleased them. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:37
That day all the troops and all Israel were convinced the king had no part in murdering Abner son of Ner. 📖
## 2 Samuel 3:38
The king told his servants, “Do you not know a great prince fell in Israel today? 💔
## 2 Samuel 3:39
I am weak today, although anointed king. Zeruiah’s sons are too fierce for me. May the LORD repay the evildoer according to his evil!” ⚖️
