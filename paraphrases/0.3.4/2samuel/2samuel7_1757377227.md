## 2 Samuel 7:1
The king settled in his palace, and the LORD gave him rest from all surrounding enemies. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:2
He told Nathan the prophet, “I live in a cedar house, but God’s ark remains in a tent.” 🏛️
## 2 Samuel 7:3
Nathan answered, “Do everything in your heart, because the LORD is with you.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:4
That night the LORD’s word came to Nathan: 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:5
“Tell My servant David, ‘The LORD asks: Are you the one to build a house for Me to live in? 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:6
I have not lived in a house since I brought Israel from Egypt. Until today I have traveled with a tent as My dwelling. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:7
I appointed leaders to shepherd My people Israel. In all My journeys with Israel, did I ever ask one, ‘Why have you not built Me a cedar house?’ 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:8
Tell My servant David that the LORD of Hosts says: I took you from the pasture and following the flock to rule My people Israel. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:9
I have been with you everywhere and cut off all your enemies before you. I will make your name like the greatest in the land. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:10
I will provide a place for My people Israel and plant them there to live undisturbed in their own place. Wicked people will no longer oppress them as they did at the beginning, 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:11
and since I appointed judges over My people Israel. I will give you rest from all your enemies. The LORD declares that He Himself will establish a house for you. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:12
When your days end and you rest with your fathers, I will raise up your own descendant after you. He will come from your body, and I will establish his kingdom. 👑
## 2 Samuel 7:13
He will build a house for My Name. I will establish his kingdom’s throne forever. 👑
## 2 Samuel 7:14
I will be his Father, and he will be My son. When he does wrong, I will discipline him with men’s rods and human blows. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:15
I will never remove My faithful love from him as I removed it from Saul, whom I removed before you. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 7:16
Your house and kingdom will endure forever before Me. Your throne will be established forever.’” 👑
## 2 Samuel 7:17
Nathan told David every word of this whole revelation. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:18
King David went in and sat before the LORD. He said, “Lord GOD, who am I, and what is my house, that You brought me this far? 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:19
Lord GOD, as if that were a small thing, You spoke about Your servant’s house in the future. Is this Your custom with man, Lord GOD? 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:20
What more can David say? You know Your servant, Lord GOD. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:21
For Your word’s sake and according to Your heart, You accomplished this great thing and revealed it to Your servant. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:22
How great You are, Lord GOD! No one is like You, and there is no God except You, according to everything we heard ourselves. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:23
Who is like Your people Israel, the one nation God redeemed for Himself to make His name known? You did great, awesome wonders, driving out nations and their gods before Your people, whom You redeemed from Egypt for Yourself. 📖
## 2 Samuel 7:24
You established Israel as Your own people forever. You, LORD, became their God. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 7:25
LORD God, confirm forever Your word about Your servant and his house. Do as You promised, 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:26
so Your name is magnified forever when people say, ‘The LORD of Hosts is God over Israel.’ Your servant David’s house will be established before You. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:27
LORD of Hosts, God of Israel, You told Your servant, ‘I will build a house for you.’ That is why Your servant found courage to pray this prayer. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:28
Lord GOD, You are God! Your words are true. You promised this goodness to Your servant. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 7:29
May it please You to bless Your servant’s house so it continues forever before You. Lord GOD, You have spoken. With Your blessing, Your servant’s house will be blessed forever.” 🙏
