## 2 Samuel 24:1
The LORD’s anger again burned against Israel. He stirred David against them, saying, “Take a census of Israel and Judah.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:2
The king told his army commander Joab, “Go through Israel’s tribes from Dan to Beersheba. Register the troops so I know their number.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:3
Joab answered, “May the LORD your God multiply the troops a hundred times and let you see it! Why does my lord the king want this?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 24:4
The king overruled Joab and the army commanders. They left to register Israel’s troops. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:5
They crossed the Jordan and camped near Aroer, south of the town in the valley’s middle. They proceeded toward Gad and Jazer. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:6
They went to Gilead and Tahtim-hodshi’s land, then Dan-jaan and around to Sidon. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:7
They reached Tyre’s fortress and all the Hivites’ and Canaanites’ cities. Finally, they went to Judah’s Negev, to Beersheba. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:8
After surveying the whole land for nine months and twenty days, they returned to Jerusalem. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:9
Joab reported the troop numbers. Israel had 800,000 brave men who drew the sword, and Judah had 500,000. 👥
## 2 Samuel 24:10
After the census, David’s conscience troubled him. He told the LORD, “I sinned greatly. LORD, forgive Your servant’s sin. I acted very foolishly.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 24:11
When David rose in the morning, the LORD’s word had come to Gad the prophet, David’s seer: 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:12
“Tell David, ‘The LORD offers three options. Choose one, and I will carry it out against you.’” 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:13
Gad said, “Do you choose three years of famine, three months fleeing your enemies, or three days of plague on your land? Consider and tell me how to answer Him who sent me.” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 24:14
David answered, “I am deeply distressed. Let us fall into the LORD’s hand, because His mercies are great. Do not let me fall into human hands.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 24:15
The LORD sent a plague from that morning until the appointed time. Seventy thousand people died from Dan to Beersheba. ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 24:16
The angel stretched his hand to destroy Jerusalem, but the LORD relented. He told the destroying angel, “Enough! Withdraw your hand!” The angel stood by Araunah the Jebusite’s threshing floor. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:17
David saw the angel striking the people and told the LORD, “I, the shepherd, sinned and acted wickedly. What did these sheep do? Let Your hand fall on me and my father’s house.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 24:18
Gad told David that day, “Build an altar to the LORD on Araunah the Jebusite’s threshing floor.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:19
David obeyed Gad’s word as the LORD commanded. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:20
Araunah saw the king and his servants approaching. He went out and bowed facedown before the king. 🙇
## 2 Samuel 24:21
Araunah asked, “Why did my lord the king come?” David answered, “To buy your threshing floor and build the LORD an altar, so the people’s plague may stop.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 24:22
Araunah said, “Take and offer whatever you consider good. Here are oxen for a burnt offering and threshing sledges and ox yokes for wood. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:23
Araunah gives all this to the king.” He added, “May the LORD your God accept you.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 24:24
The king answered, “No. I insist on paying. I will not offer the LORD my God burnt offerings that cost me nothing.” He bought the threshing floor and oxen for fifty silver shekels. 📖
## 2 Samuel 24:25
He built the LORD an altar and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. The LORD answered prayers for the land. The plague on Israel stopped. 🙏
