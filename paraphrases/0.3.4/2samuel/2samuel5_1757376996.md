## 2 Samuel 5:1
All Israel’s tribes came to David at Hebron. They said, “We are your own flesh and blood. 👥
## 2 Samuel 5:2
Even while Saul was king, you led Israel out and brought them back. The LORD told you, ‘You will shepherd My people Israel and rule over them.’” 👑
## 2 Samuel 5:3
All Israel’s elders came to the king at Hebron. King David made a covenant with them before the LORD. They anointed him king over Israel. 👑
## 2 Samuel 5:4
David became king at thirty years old and reigned forty years. 👑
## 2 Samuel 5:5
He reigned over Judah in Hebron for seven years and six months. In Jerusalem he reigned over all Israel and Judah for thirty-three years. 👑
## 2 Samuel 5:6
David and his men marched against Jerusalem’s Jebusites. They said, “You cannot enter here. Even the blind and lame can repel you.” They thought David could not enter. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 5:7
David captured Zion’s fortress, the City of David. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 5:8
He said, “Whoever attacks the Jebusites must use the water shaft to reach the lame and blind whom David despises.” That is why people say, “The blind and lame will never enter the palace.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 5:9
David lived in the fortress and named it the City of David. He built all around it, from the supporting terraces inward. 🏛️
## 2 Samuel 5:10
David grew greater because the LORD God of Hosts was with him. 📖
## 2 Samuel 5:11
Hiram king of Tyre sent David messengers, cedar logs, carpenters, and stonemasons. They built David a palace. 🏛️
## 2 Samuel 5:12
David realized the LORD established him as Israel’s king. The LORD exalted David’s kingdom for His people Israel’s sake. 👑
## 2 Samuel 5:13
After coming from Hebron, David took more concubines and wives in Jerusalem. More sons and daughters were born to him. 👥
## 2 Samuel 5:14
His children born in Jerusalem were Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon, 👶
## 2 Samuel 5:15
Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, Japhia, 👶
## 2 Samuel 5:16
Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet. 👶
## 2 Samuel 5:17
The Philistines heard David was anointed king over Israel and went to find him. David heard this and descended to the stronghold. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 5:18
The Philistines spread out in the Valley of Rephaim. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 5:19
David asked the LORD, “Should I attack the Philistines? Will You deliver them into my hand?” The LORD answered, “Attack. I will surely deliver them into your hand.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 5:20
David defeated them at Baal-perazim. He said, “The LORD burst through my enemies before me like a bursting flood.” He named the place Baal-perazim. 📖
## 2 Samuel 5:21
The Philistines abandoned their idols there. David and his men carried them away. 📖
## 2 Samuel 5:22
The Philistines again spread out in the Valley of Rephaim. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 5:23
David asked the LORD. He answered, “Do not march directly against them. Circle behind and attack in front of the balsam trees. 📖
## 2 Samuel 5:24
When you hear marching in the balsam treetops, move quickly. This means the LORD has gone before you to strike the Philistine camp.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 5:25
David obeyed the LORD and struck the Philistines from Gibeon to Gezer. ⚔️
