## 2 Samuel 16:1
Just beyond the summit, David met Mephibosheth’s servant Ziba. He brought a pair of saddled donkeys with two hundred loaves, a hundred raisin clusters, a hundred summer fruits, and a wineskin. 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:2
The king asked, “Why bring these?” Ziba answered, “The donkeys are for the king’s household to ride. The bread and fruit feed the young men. The wine refreshes those exhausted in the wilderness.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:3
The king asked, “Where is your master’s grandson?” Ziba answered, “He stayed in Jerusalem. He said, ‘Today Israel’s house will restore my grandfather’s kingdom to me.’” 💬
## 2 Samuel 16:4
The king told Ziba, “Everything belonging to Mephibosheth is yours!” Ziba said, “I bow humbly. May my lord the king favor me!” 💬
## 2 Samuel 16:5
Near Bahurim, King David met Shimei son of Gera from Saul’s family. Shimei came toward him, shouting curses. 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:6
He threw stones at David and his servants. Troops and mighty men surrounded David on his right and left. 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:7
Shimei cursed, “Get out! Get out, you worthless man of bloodshed! ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 16:8
You reigned in Saul’s place. The LORD repaid you for all the blood of Saul’s house and gave the kingdom to your son Absalom. You came to ruin because you are a man of bloodshed!” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 16:9
Abishai son of Zeruiah said, “Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me cut off his head!” 💬
## 2 Samuel 16:10
The king answered, “What have I to do with you, Zeruiah’s sons? If he curses because the LORD told him, ‘Curse David,’ who can ask why he did it?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 16:11
David told Abishai and his servants, “My own son, my flesh and blood, wants to kill me. How much more this Benjamite! Leave him and let him curse, because the LORD told him to. 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:12
Perhaps the LORD will see my affliction and repay me with good for today’s curses.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 16:13
David and his men continued on the road. Shimei followed the opposite hillside ridge, cursing, throwing stones, and flinging dust at David. 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:14
The king and his people arrived exhausted. He refreshed himself there. 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:15
Absalom and all Israel’s men reached Jerusalem. Ahithophel was with him. 👥
## 2 Samuel 16:16
David’s friend Hushai the Archite approached Absalom. He said, “Long live the king! Long live the king!” 💬
## 2 Samuel 16:17
Absalom asked, “Is this your loyalty to your friend? Why did you not go with him?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 16:18
Hushai answered, “No. I will belong to and stay with the one the LORD, this people, and all Israel’s men have chosen. 💬
## 2 Samuel 16:19
Whom should I serve except his son? I will serve in your presence as I served in your father’s.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 16:20
Absalom told Ahithophel, “Advise me. What should we do?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 16:21
Ahithophel said, “Have sexual relations with your father’s concubines left to care for the palace. All Israel will hear you became a stench to your father. Then everyone supporting you will have their hands strengthened.” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 16:22
They pitched a tent on the roof for Absalom. He had sexual relations with his father’s concubines before all Israel. 📖
## 2 Samuel 16:23
In those days, Ahithophel’s advice was regarded like consulting God’s word. David and Absalom both regarded it this way. 📖
