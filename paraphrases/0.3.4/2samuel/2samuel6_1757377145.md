## 2 Samuel 6:1
David again gathered Israel’s chosen men, thirty thousand in all. 👥
## 2 Samuel 6:2
David and all his troops went to Baale of Judah to bring God’s ark from there. It is called by the Name, the name of the LORD of Hosts. He sits enthroned between the cherubim on it. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:3
They placed God’s ark on a new cart and brought it from Abinadab’s house on the hill. Abinadab’s sons Uzzah and Ahio guided the new cart, 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:4
bringing God’s ark with it. Ahio walked in front of the ark. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:5
David and all Israel’s house celebrated before the LORD. They played wood instruments, harps, stringed instruments, tambourines, sistrums, and cymbals. 🎶
## 2 Samuel 6:6
At Nacon’s threshing floor, Uzzah reached out and held God’s ark because the oxen stumbled. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:7
The LORD’s anger burned against Uzzah. God struck him there for his irreverence, and he died beside God’s ark. ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 6:8
David became angry because the LORD burst against Uzzah. He named the place Perez-uzzah, its name to this day. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:9
David feared the LORD that day. He asked, “How can the LORD’s ark ever come to me?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 6:10
David would not bring the LORD’s ark to the City of David. He took it instead to Obed-edom the Gittite’s house. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:11
It stayed at Obed-edom the Gittite’s house for three months. The LORD blessed him and his whole household. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 6:12
David heard, “The LORD blessed Obed-edom’s house and everything he owns because of God’s ark.” David brought the ark from Obed-edom’s house to the City of David with joy. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:13
After the ark’s carriers took six paces, David sacrificed an ox and a fattened calf. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:14
David wore a linen ephod and danced with all his strength before the LORD, 🎶
## 2 Samuel 6:15
as he and all Israel’s house brought the LORD’s ark with shouts and the ram’s horn sounding. 📯
## 2 Samuel 6:16
The LORD’s ark entered the City of David. Saul’s daughter Michal watched from a window. She saw King David leaping and dancing before the LORD and despised him in her heart. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:17
They placed the LORD’s ark in the tent David prepared for it. David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD. 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:18
After the burnt offerings and peace offerings, David blessed the people in the LORD of Hosts’ name. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 6:19
He gave every man and woman in Israel’s crowd a loaf of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake. Everyone returned home. 🍞
## 2 Samuel 6:20
David returned to bless his household. Saul’s daughter Michal met him and said, “How Israel’s king distinguished himself today! He uncovered himself before his subjects’ maidservants, like a vulgar person!” 💬
## 2 Samuel 6:21
David answered Michal, “I danced before the LORD. He chose me over your father and all his house to rule His people Israel. I will celebrate before the LORD, 🎶
## 2 Samuel 6:22
and humiliate and humble myself even more. Yet the maidservants you mentioned will honor me.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 6:23
Saul’s daughter Michal had no children until the day she died. 📖
