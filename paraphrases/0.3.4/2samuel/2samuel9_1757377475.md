## 2 Samuel 9:1
David asked, “Does anyone remain from Saul’s house to whom I can show kindness for Jonathan’s sake?” ❤️
## 2 Samuel 9:2
They called Ziba, a servant from Saul’s house, to David. The king asked, “Are you Ziba?” He answered, “I am your servant.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 9:3
The king asked, “Does anyone remain from Saul’s house to receive God’s kindness?” Ziba answered, “Jonathan’s son remains. He is lame in both feet.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 9:4
The king asked, “Where is he?” Ziba answered, “In Lo-debar, at Machir son of Ammiel’s house.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 9:5
King David had him brought from Machir son of Ammiel’s house in Lo-debar. 👥
## 2 Samuel 9:6
Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, came and fell facedown before David in reverence. David said, “Mephibosheth!” He answered, “I am your servant.” 🙇
## 2 Samuel 9:7
David said, “Do not fear. I will show you kindness for your father Jonathan’s sake. I will restore all your grandfather Saul’s land. You will always eat at my table.” ❤️
## 2 Samuel 9:8
Mephibosheth bowed and said, “What is your servant, that you should care for a dead dog like me?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 9:9
The king called Saul’s servant Ziba. He said, “I gave your master’s grandson everything belonging to Saul and his whole house. 📖
## 2 Samuel 9:10
You, your sons, and your servants must farm his land and gather the harvest so your master’s grandson has food. But Mephibosheth, your master’s grandson, will always eat at my table.” Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants. 👥
## 2 Samuel 9:11
Ziba said, “Your servant will obey everything my lord the king commanded.” Mephibosheth ate at David’s table like one of his own sons. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 9:12
Mephibosheth had a young son named Mica. Everyone in Ziba’s house served Mephibosheth. 👥
## 2 Samuel 9:13
Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem because he always ate at the king’s table. Both his feet were lame. 📖
