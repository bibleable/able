## 2 Samuel 13:1
Later, David’s son Amnon fell in love with Tamar, the beautiful sister of David’s son Absalom. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:2
Amnon became sick with frustration over his sister Tamar. She was a virgin, and doing anything to her seemed impossible to him. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:3
Amnon’s friend Jonadab was the son of David’s brother Shimeah. Jonadab was very shrewd. 👥
## 2 Samuel 13:4
He asked, “Why are you, the king’s son, so depressed every morning? Will you not tell me?” Amnon answered, “I love Tamar, my brother Absalom’s sister.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 13:5
Jonadab said, “Lie in bed and pretend to be ill. When your father visits, ask him to send Tamar to give you food. Ask to watch her prepare it and eat from her hand.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:6
Amnon lay down and pretended to be ill. When the king visited, he said, “Let my sister Tamar make two cakes where I can watch and eat from her hand.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:7
David sent Tamar a message at the palace: “Go to your brother Amnon’s house and prepare him a meal.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:8
Tamar went to Amnon’s house, where he lay down. She kneaded dough, made cakes before him, and baked them. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:9
She set the pan before him, but he refused to eat. Amnon said, “Send everyone out!” Everyone left. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:10
Amnon said, “Bring the food into the bedroom so I can eat from your hand.” Tamar took her cakes to her brother Amnon’s bedroom. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:11
When she brought the food, he seized her. He said, “Have sexual relations with me, my sister!” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 13:12
She cried, “No, my brother! Do not violate me. This should never happen in Israel. Do not do this disgraceful thing! ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 13:13
Where could I take my shame? You would be like a fool in Israel! Please speak to the king. He will not withhold me from you.” 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:14
Amnon refused to listen. He was stronger than she was and raped her. ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 13:15
Amnon then hated Tamar intensely. His hatred exceeded his previous love. He said, “Get up! Leave!” 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:16
She answered, “No! Sending me away is worse than the great wrong you already did!” He would not listen. 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:17
He called his attendant and said, “Throw this woman out and bolt the door behind her!” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 13:18
The attendant threw her out and bolted the door. Tamar wore a robe of many colors, as the king’s virgin daughters did. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:19
Tamar put ashes on her head and tore her robe. She put her hand on her head and left, crying loudly. 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:20
Her brother Absalom asked, “Has your brother Amnon been with you? Be quiet for now, my sister. He is your brother. Do not take this to heart.” Tamar lived desolate in her brother Absalom’s house. 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:21
King David heard all this and became furious. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:22
Absalom said nothing to Amnon, good or bad. He hated Amnon for violating his sister Tamar. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:23
Two years later, Absalom’s sheepshearers were at Baal-hazor near Ephraim. He invited all the king’s sons. 👥
## 2 Samuel 13:24
He told the king, “Your servant hired shearers. Will you and your servants come with me?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 13:25
The king answered, “No, my son. If we all go, we will burden you.” Absalom urged him, but he refused to go. He gave Absalom his blessing. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:26
Absalom said, “Then let my brother Amnon come.” The king asked, “Why should he go with you?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 13:27
Absalom kept urging him. The king sent Amnon and the rest of his sons. 👥
## 2 Samuel 13:28
Absalom commanded his young men, “Watch until Amnon is cheerful with wine. When I tell you to strike him, kill him. Do not fear. I commanded you. Be courageous and brave!” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 13:29
The young men did what Absalom commanded to Amnon. All the king’s other sons fled, each on his mule. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 13:30
On their way, David received this report: “Absalom killed all the king’s sons. Not one remains!” 💬
## 2 Samuel 13:31
The king rose, tore his clothes, and lay on the ground. His servants stood with torn clothes. 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:32
Jonadab, son of David’s brother Shimeah, said, “Do not think all the king’s sons died. Only Amnon died. Absalom planned this since Amnon violated his sister Tamar. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:33
My lord the king, do not believe the report that all your sons died. Only Amnon is dead.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 13:34
Absalom had fled. The watchman saw many people coming down the road west of him, along the hillside. He told the king, “I see men coming from Horonaim’s direction along the hill.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:35
Jonadab said, “The king’s sons have arrived! It is as your servant said.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 13:36
As he finished, the king’s sons arrived, wailing loudly. The king and all his servants wept bitterly. 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:37
Absalom fled to Talmai son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. David mourned his son every day. 💔
## 2 Samuel 13:38
Absalom stayed in Geshur for three years after fleeing there. 📖
## 2 Samuel 13:39
David longed to go to Absalom because he had found comfort over Amnon’s death. 📖
