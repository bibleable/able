## 2 Samuel 11:1
In spring, when kings went to war, David sent Joab, his servants, and Israel’s whole army. They destroyed the Ammonites and surrounded Rabbah. David stayed in Jerusalem. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 11:2
One evening David left his bed and walked on the palace roof. He saw a very beautiful woman bathing. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:3
David asked about her. He was told, “She is Bathsheba daughter of Eliam, the wife of Uriah the Hittite.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:4
David sent messengers to bring her. She came, and he had sexual relations with her. She had just purified herself from her uncleanness. Then she returned home. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:5
She conceived and sent David a message: “I am pregnant.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:6
David ordered Joab, “Send me Uriah the Hittite.” Joab sent him to David. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:7
David asked Uriah how Joab and the troops were doing and how the war was going. 💬
## 2 Samuel 11:8
He told Uriah, “Go home and wash your feet.” Uriah left the palace, followed by a gift from the king. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:9
Uriah slept at the palace door with his master’s servants. He did not go home. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:10
David heard Uriah did not go home. He asked, “Did you not just arrive from a journey? Why did you not go home?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 11:11
Uriah answered, “The ark, Israel, and Judah stay in tents. My master Joab and his soldiers camp in the field. How can I eat, drink, and sleep with my wife at home? As surely as you live and your soul lives, I will not do this!” 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:12
David said, “Stay one more day, and tomorrow I will send you back.” Uriah stayed in Jerusalem that day and the next. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:13
David invited Uriah to eat and drink and made him drunk. That evening Uriah lay on his cot with his master’s servants. He did not go home. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:14
The next morning David wrote Joab a letter and sent it with Uriah. 📜
## 2 Samuel 11:15
He wrote, “Put Uriah at the fiercest battle’s front. Withdraw from him so he may be struck down and killed.” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 11:16
While surrounding the city, Joab assigned Uriah to the place where he knew the strongest enemy soldiers stood. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 11:17
The city’s men attacked Joab. Some of David’s servants fell, and Uriah the Hittite died too. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 11:18
Joab sent David a full battle report, 📜
## 2 Samuel 11:19
and instructed the messenger, “When you finish giving the king a full report of the battle, 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:20
if he becomes angry, he may ask, ‘Why did you fight so near the city? Did you not know they would shoot from the wall? ❓
## 2 Samuel 11:21
Who killed Abimelech son of Jerubbesheth? Did a woman not drop an upper millstone from the wall, killing him at Thebez? Why did you approach the wall?’ If he asks, say, ‘Your servant Uriah the Hittite died too.’” 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:22
The messenger reported everything Joab sent him to tell David. 📖
## 2 Samuel 11:23
He said, “Their men overpowered us and attacked in the field. We drove them back to the gate’s entrance. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 11:24
Archers shot your servants from the wall, killing some of the king’s servants. Your servant Uriah the Hittite died too.” ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 11:25
David said, “Tell Joab, ‘Do not be upset. The sword consumes one as well as another. Strengthen your attack and demolish the city.’ Encourage him with these words.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 11:26
Uriah’s wife heard her husband died and mourned him. 💔
## 2 Samuel 11:27
After mourning ended, David brought her to his house. She became his wife and bore him a son. What David did was evil in the LORD’s sight. ⚖️
