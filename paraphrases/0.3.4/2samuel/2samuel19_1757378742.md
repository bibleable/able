## 2 Samuel 19:1
Joab heard, “The king weeps and mourns Absalom.” 💔
## 2 Samuel 19:2
That day’s victory became mourning for everyone because they heard the king was grieving his son. 💔
## 2 Samuel 19:3
They entered the city quietly, like people slipping away in shame after fleeing battle. 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:4
The king covered his face and cried loudly, “My son Absalom! Absalom, my son, my son!” 💔
## 2 Samuel 19:5
Joab entered the house and told him, “You disgraced your servants today. They saved your life and your sons, daughters, wives, and concubines. ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 19:6
You love those who hate you and hate those who love you! You showed that commanders and soldiers mean nothing to you. You would be pleased if Absalom lived and all of us died! ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 19:7
Get up and comfort your servants. I swear by the LORD, no man will stay tonight unless you go out. This will be worse than all your troubles since youth!” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 19:8
The king sat at the gate. Everyone heard he was there and came before him. Meanwhile, Israel’s men fled home. 👥
## 2 Samuel 19:9
Throughout Israel’s tribes, people argued, “The king rescued us from our enemies and the Philistines. Now he fled the land because of Absalom. 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:10
Absalom, whom we anointed over us, died in battle. Why say nothing about restoring the king?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 19:11
David sent the priests Zadok and Abiathar a message. “Ask Judah’s elders why they are last to restore the king to his palace. All Israel’s talk has reached the king at his quarters. 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:12
You are my brothers, my flesh and blood. Why are you last to restore the king? 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:13
Tell Amasa, ‘Are you not my flesh and blood? May God punish me severely if you do not replace Joab as my army commander from now on!’” 👑
## 2 Samuel 19:14
He united Judah’s men’s hearts as one. They told the king, “Return with all your servants.” 👥
## 2 Samuel 19:15
The king returned to the Jordan. Judah’s men met him at Gilgal to escort him across the Jordan. 👥
## 2 Samuel 19:16
Shimei son of Gera, a Benjamite from Bahurim, hurried with Judah’s men to meet King David, 👥
## 2 Samuel 19:17
with a thousand men of Benjamin and Ziba, steward of Saul’s house, with his fifteen sons and twenty servants. They hurried to the Jordan before the king, 👥
## 2 Samuel 19:18
and crossed the ford to carry the king’s household over and please him. Shimei son of Gera crossed and fell before the king, 🙇
## 2 Samuel 19:19
saying, “My lord, do not hold me guilty. Do not remember my wrong when you left Jerusalem or take it to heart. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 19:20
I know I sinned. Today I came first of Joseph’s whole house to meet my lord the king.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:21
Abishai son of Zeruiah asked, “Should Shimei not die for cursing the LORD’s anointed?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 19:22
David answered, “Zeruiah’s sons, why are you my adversaries today? Should anyone die in Israel today? Do I not know I am Israel’s king today?” 👑
## 2 Samuel 19:23
The king swore to Shimei, “You will not die.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:24
Mephibosheth, Saul’s grandson, came to meet the king. He had not cared for his feet, trimmed his mustache, or washed his clothes since the king left until his safe return. 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:25
He came from Jerusalem. The king asked, “Mephibosheth, why did you not come with me?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 19:26
He answered, “My lord the king, I am lame. I planned to have my donkey saddled to ride with you. My servant Ziba deceived me, 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:27
and slandered your servant to you. My lord the king is like God’s angel. Do what you consider good. 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:28
My grandfather’s whole house deserves death from my lord the king. Yet you let your servant eat at your table. What right have I to keep appealing to you?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 19:29
The king answered, “Why say more? You and Ziba must divide the land.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:30
Mephibosheth said, “Let Ziba take everything, since my lord the king returned home safely!” 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:31
Barzillai the Gileadite came from Rogelim to cross the Jordan with the king and send him onward. 👥
## 2 Samuel 19:32
Barzillai was eighty years old. Because he was very wealthy, he provided for the king during his stay in Mahanaim. 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:33
The king said, “Cross with me. I will provide for you beside me in Jerusalem.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 19:34
Barzillai answered, “How many years remain for me to go to Jerusalem with the king? ❓
## 2 Samuel 19:35
I am eighty years old. Can I distinguish good from bad? Can I taste food or drink, or hear singing men and women? Why should I burden my lord the king? 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:36
I will accompany you only a short distance past the Jordan. Why reward me like this? 💬
## 2 Samuel 19:37
Let me return to die in my city near my parents’ tomb. Here is your servant Chimham. Let him cross with you and receive what you consider good.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:38
The king answered, “Chimham will cross with me. I will treat him as you consider good and do for you whatever you desire.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 19:39
The people crossed the Jordan, then the king crossed. He kissed and blessed Barzillai, who returned home. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 19:40
The king and Chimham crossed to Gilgal. All Judah’s troops and half Israel’s troops escorted him. 👥
## 2 Samuel 19:41
Israel’s men asked the king, “Why did our brothers from Judah secretly bring you, your household, and all David’s men across the Jordan?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 19:42
Judah’s men answered, “We did this because the king is our relative. Why are you angry? Have we eaten at his expense or received anything for ourselves?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 19:43
Israel’s men answered, “We have ten shares in the king and more claim to David than you. Why despise us? We first proposed restoring him!” Judah’s men spoke more fiercely than Israel’s men. ⚠️
