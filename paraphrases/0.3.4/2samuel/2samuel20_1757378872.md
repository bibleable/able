## 2 Samuel 20:1
Sheba son of Bichri, a worthless Benjamite, was there. He blew the ram’s horn. He shouted, “We have no share in David or inheritance in Jesse’s son. Every man to his tent, Israel!” 📯
## 2 Samuel 20:2
Israel’s men deserted David for Sheba son of Bichri. Judah’s men remained with their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem. 👥
## 2 Samuel 20:3
David returned to Jerusalem’s palace. He placed the ten concubines left to care for it in a guarded house. He provided for them but no longer had sexual relations with them. They remained confined until death, living as widows. 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:4
The king told Amasa, “Gather Judah’s men to me within three days. Be here yourself.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:5
Amasa went, but took longer than his allotted time. 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:6
David told Abishai, “Sheba son of Bichri will harm us more than Absalom. Pursue him with your lord’s servants before he finds fortified cities and escapes us.” ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 20:7
Joab’s men, the Cherethites, Pelethites, and all the mighty men left Jerusalem to pursue Sheba son of Bichri. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 20:8
At Gibeon’s great stone, Amasa met them. Joab wore military clothes with a dagger strapped to his belt. As he stepped forward, he slipped it from its sheath. 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:9
Joab asked Amasa, “Are you well, my brother?” He seized Amasa’s beard with his right hand to kiss him. 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:10
Amasa was not guarding against Joab’s dagger. Joab stabbed his stomach, spilling his intestines onto the ground. No second blow was needed, because Amasa was dead. Joab and his brother Abishai pursued Sheba son of Bichri. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 20:11
A young man of Joab stood near Amasa. He said, “Whoever favors Joab, and whoever is for David, follow Joab!” 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:12
Amasa lay in his blood in the road. The man saw the troops stopping there, dragged his body into a field, and covered it with a garment. 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:13
After the body left the road, everyone followed Joab to pursue Sheba son of Bichri. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 20:14
Sheba traveled through Israel’s tribes to Abel-beth-maacah and the Berites’ whole region. They gathered and followed him. 👥
## 2 Samuel 20:15
Joab’s troops surrounded Sheba in Abel-beth-maacah. They built a siege ramp against the city’s outer rampart and battered the wall to topple it, ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 20:16
when a wise woman called from the city, “Listen! Listen! Ask Joab to come and speak with me.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 20:17
He approached, and she asked, “Are you Joab?” He answered, “I am.” She said, “Listen to your servant.” He answered, “I am listening.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 20:18
She said, “People once said, ‘Seek counsel at Abel.’ This settled disputes. 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:19
I am peaceable and faithful in Israel. You seek to destroy a city that is a mother in Israel. Why swallow the LORD’s inheritance?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 20:20
Joab answered, “Far be it! Far be it from me to swallow up or destroy! 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:21
Sheba son of Bichri from Ephraim’s hills rebelled against King David. Hand over only Sheba, and I will leave the city.” She answered, “His head will be thrown over the wall to you.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 20:22
She gave the people wise counsel. They cut off Sheba son of Bichri’s head and threw it to Joab. He blew the ram’s horn, and everyone dispersed home from the city. Joab returned to the king in Jerusalem. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 20:23
Joab commanded Israel’s whole army. Benaiah son of Jehoiada commanded the Cherethites and Pelethites. 👥
## 2 Samuel 20:24
Adoram supervised forced labor. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud kept the records. 👥
## 2 Samuel 20:25
Sheva was the scribe. Zadok and Abiathar were priests. 👥
## 2 Samuel 20:26
Ira the Jairite was David’s priest. 👥
