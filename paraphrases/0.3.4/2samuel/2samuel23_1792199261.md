## 2 Samuel 23:1
These are David’s last words: “The oracle of David son of Jesse, the man raised high. The God of Jacob anointed him. He is Israel’s sweet psalmist. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:2
The LORD’s Spirit spoke through me. His word was on my tongue. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:3
Israel’s God spoke. Israel’s Rock told me, ‘The ruler who governs people justly and fears God, 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:4
is like morning light at a cloudless sunrise. He is like the glistening after rain on earth’s sprouting grass.’ 🌱
## 2 Samuel 23:5
Is my house not right with God? He established an everlasting covenant with me, fully ordered and secured. Will He not bring my full salvation and every desire? 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:6
The worthless are like thorns raked aside. They cannot be gathered by hand. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:7
Anyone touching them must be armed with iron or a spear shaft. Fire burns them to ashes where they lie.” 🔥
## 2 Samuel 23:8
These were David’s mighty men. Josheb-basshebeth the Tahchemonite led the Three. He killed eight hundred men with his spear at one time. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:9
Next in command was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, one of the three mighty men. He went with David to taunt the Philistines gathered at Pas-dammim for battle. Israel’s men retreated, ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:10
Eleazar stood his ground and struck the Philistines until his hand grew weary and stuck to his sword. The LORD gave a great victory that day. The troops returned only to plunder the dead. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:11
Next was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines gathered near a lentil field, and Israel’s troops fled. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:12
Shammah stood in the field’s middle, defended it, and struck down the Philistines. The LORD gave a great victory. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:13
At harvest, three of the thirty chiefs went to David at Adullam’s cave. Philistines camped in the Valley of Rephaim. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:14
David was in the stronghold. A Philistine garrison occupied Bethlehem. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:15
David longed for water. He said, “If only someone brought me water from the well near Bethlehem’s gate!” 💧
## 2 Samuel 23:16
The three mighty men broke through the Philistine camp and brought David water from that well. He would not drink it, but poured it out to the LORD, 💧
## 2 Samuel 23:17
saying, “LORD, far be it from me! Is this not the blood of men who risked their lives?” He refused to drink. These were the three mighty men’s exploits. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:18
Abishai, Joab’s brother and Zeruiah’s son, led the Three. He killed three hundred men with his spear and gained a name with the Three. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:19
Was he not more honored than the Three? He commanded them but was not included among the Three. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:20
Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a brave man from Kabzeel who did many exploits. He killed two champions of Moab. On a snowy day he entered a pit and killed a lion. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:21
He attacked a huge Egyptian with a club, though the Egyptian carried a spear. Benaiah seized the spear and killed him with his own weapon. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 23:22
Benaiah son of Jehoiada gained a name with the three mighty men through these exploits. 📖
## 2 Samuel 23:23
He was most honored among the Thirty but did not join the Three. David placed him over his guard. 👥
