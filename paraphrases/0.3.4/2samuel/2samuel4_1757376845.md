Ish-bosheth son of Saul heard that Abner died in Hebron. He lost courage, and all Israel was dismayed. 📖
## 2 Samuel 4:2
Saul’s son had two raiding leaders, Baanah and Rechab. They were sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, from Benjamin’s tribe. Beeroth is considered part of Benjamin, 👥
## 2 Samuel 4:3
because the Beerothites fled to Gittaim. They have lived there as foreigners to this day. 📖
## 2 Samuel 4:4
Jonathan son of Saul had a son named Mephibosheth, whose feet were lame. He was five years old when news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. His nurse picked him up and fled. As she hurried, he fell and became lame. 📖
## 2 Samuel 4:5
Rechab and Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, reached Ish-bosheth’s house in the day’s heat. The king was taking his midday nap. 📖
## 2 Samuel 4:6
They entered the house’s interior as if to get wheat and stabbed him in the stomach. Rechab and his brother Baanah escaped. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 4:7
They entered while Ish-bosheth lay on his bed. They stabbed and killed him, cut off his head, and took it. They traveled through the Arabah all night. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 4:8
They brought Ish-bosheth’s head to David at Hebron. They said, “Here is the head of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, your enemy who tried to kill you. Today the LORD avenged my lord the king against Saul and his descendants.” 💬

## 2 Samuel 4:10
I seized and killed the man who told me, ‘Saul is dead,’ and thought he brought good news. I did this at Ziklag. That was his reward for his news! ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 4:11
How much more when wicked men kill a righteous man in his own house, on his own bed! Will I not demand his blood from your hands and remove you from the earth?” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 4:12
David commanded his young men, and they killed Rechab and Baanah. They cut off their hands and feet and hung their bodies beside Hebron’s pool. They buried Ish-bosheth’s head in Abner’s tomb at Hebron. ⚖️
