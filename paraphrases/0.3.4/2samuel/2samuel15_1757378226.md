## 2 Samuel 15:1
Absalom obtained a chariot, horses, and fifty men to run ahead of him. 👑
## 2 Samuel 15:2
He rose early and stood by the road to the city gate. He asked anyone bringing a case to the king, “What city are you from?” If the answer was, “Your servant is from an Israelite tribe,” 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:3
Absalom said, “Your claims are good and right, but the king has no deputy to hear you.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 15:4
He added, “If only I were appointed judge! Everyone with a grievance or dispute could come to me, and I would give justice.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 15:5
When anyone approached to bow to him, Absalom reached out his hand, took hold of him, and kissed him. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:6
He treated every Israelite seeking the king’s justice this way. He stole the hearts of Israel’s men. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:7
After four years, Absalom told the king, “Let me go to Hebron and fulfill my vow to the LORD. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:8
While living in Geshur of Aram, I vowed to worship the LORD in Hebron if He returned me to Jerusalem.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:9
The king said, “Go in peace.” Absalom went to Hebron. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:10
Absalom sent spies throughout Israel’s tribes. Their message was, “When you hear the horn, say, ‘Absalom reigns in Hebron!’” 📯
## 2 Samuel 15:11
Two hundred invited guests from Jerusalem accompanied him. They went innocently, knowing nothing about the matter. 👥
## 2 Samuel 15:12
While sacrificing, Absalom sent for Ahithophel the Gilonite, David’s counselor, from Giloh. The conspiracy strengthened, and his followers increased. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:13
A messenger told David, “The hearts of Israel’s men are with Absalom.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 15:14
David told his servants in Jerusalem, “Flee, or we will not escape Absalom. We must leave quickly, or he will overtake us, bring disaster on us, and put the city to the sword.” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 15:15
The servants answered, “We are your servants, whatever our lord the king decides.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 15:16
The king left, followed by his whole household, but ten concubines stayed to care for the palace. 👥
## 2 Samuel 15:17
The king left with everyone following him. He stopped at the last house, 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:18
and his servants marched past, all the Cherethites and Pelethites and six hundred Gittites who followed him from Gath. 👥
## 2 Samuel 15:19
He told Ittai the Gittite, “Why come with us? Return and stay with the new king. You are a foreigner and an exile from your homeland. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:20
You arrived yesterday. Should I make you wander with us today when I do not know where I am going? Return with your brothers. May the LORD show you faithful love and faithfulness.” ❤️
## 2 Samuel 15:21
Ittai answered, “As surely as the LORD lives and my lord the king lives, your servant will be wherever you are, in life or death!” 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:22
David told Ittai, “March on.” Ittai the Gittite passed with all his men and all the little children with him. 👥
## 2 Samuel 15:23
The countryside wept loudly as the people passed. The king crossed the Kidron Valley, and everyone headed toward the wilderness. 💔
## 2 Samuel 15:24
Zadok was there with all the Levites, who carried God’s covenant ark. They set it down. Abiathar offered sacrifices until everyone left the city. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:25
The king told Zadok, “Return God’s ark to the city. If the LORD favors me, He will bring me back to see it and His dwelling again. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:26
If He says, ‘I do not delight in you,’ here I am. Let Him do whatever He considers good.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 15:27
He also told Zadok the priest, “Are you not a seer? Return peacefully with your son Ahimaaz and Abiathar with his son Jonathan. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:28
I will wait at the wilderness fords until you send me news.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:29
Zadok and Abiathar returned God’s ark to Jerusalem and stayed there. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:30
David climbed the Mount of Olives, weeping, with his head covered and his feet bare. Everyone with him covered their heads and climbed, weeping. 💔
## 2 Samuel 15:31
David heard Ahithophel joined Absalom’s conspirators. He prayed, “LORD, turn Ahithophel’s counsel into foolishness!” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 15:32
At the summit where David used to worship God, Hushai the Archite met him with torn robes and dust on his head. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:33
David said, “If you come with me, you will burden me. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:34
Return to the city and tell Absalom, ‘I was your father’s servant. Now I will serve you, my king.’ This way you can thwart Ahithophel’s counsel for me. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:35
Zadok and Abiathar the priests will be there. Tell them everything you hear from the palace. 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:36
Their two sons, Ahimaaz son of Zadok and Jonathan son of Abiathar, are there. Send them to me with everything you hear.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 15:37
David’s friend Hushai reached Jerusalem as Absalom entered the city. 📖
