## 2 Samuel 12:1
The LORD sent Nathan to David. Nathan said, “Two men lived in a city, one rich and one poor. 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:2
The rich man had many sheep and cattle, 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:3
but the poor man owned only one small ewe lamb he bought. He raised it with his children. It ate his food, drank from his cup, and slept in his arms. It was like a daughter to him. 🐑
## 2 Samuel 12:4
A traveler came to the rich man. He refused to take his own sheep or cattle to feed the traveler. He took and prepared the poor man’s lamb instead.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:5
David became furious. He told Nathan, “As surely as the LORD lives, that man deserves to die! ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 12:6
He must repay the lamb four times because he did this and showed no pity.” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 12:7
Nathan said, “You are that man! The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘I anointed you Israel’s king and rescued you from Saul’s hand. 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:8
I gave you your master’s house and his wives into your arms. I gave you Israel’s and Judah’s house. If that were not enough, I would have given more. 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:9
Why did you despise the LORD’s command by doing evil in His sight? You killed Uriah the Hittite with the sword and took his wife as your own. You killed him with the Ammonites’ sword. ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 12:10
The sword will never leave your house because you despised Me and took Uriah the Hittite’s wife as your own.’ ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 12:11
The LORD says, ‘I will raise trouble against you from your own house. Before your eyes, I will take your wives and give them to another. He will have sexual relations with them in broad daylight. ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 12:12
You acted secretly. I will do this in broad daylight before all Israel.’” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 12:13
David told Nathan, “I sinned against the LORD.” Nathan answered, “The LORD took away your sin. You will not die. 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:14
But you showed utter contempt for the LORD’s word by this deed. Because of this, the son born to you will surely die.” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 12:15
Nathan went home. The LORD struck the child Uriah’s wife bore to David, and he became ill. 💔
## 2 Samuel 12:16
David pleaded with God for the boy and fasted. He went into his house and spent the night in sackcloth on the ground. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 12:17
His household’s elders tried to help him rise from the ground. He refused and would not eat with them. 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:18
The child died on the seventh day. David’s servants feared telling him. They said, “He would not listen while the child lived. How can we tell him the child died? He may harm himself.” 💔
## 2 Samuel 12:19
David saw his servants whispering and understood the child died. He asked, “Is the child dead?” They answered, “He is.” 💔
## 2 Samuel 12:20
David rose, washed, anointed himself, and changed clothes. He entered the LORD’s house and worshiped. He returned home and asked for food. They served him, and he ate. 🙏
## 2 Samuel 12:21
His servants asked, “What have you done? While the child lived, you fasted and wept. When he died, you rose and ate.” ❓
## 2 Samuel 12:22
David answered, “While he lived, I fasted and wept. I thought, ‘Who knows? The LORD may show me grace and let him live.’ 🙏
## 2 Samuel 12:23
Now he is dead. Why fast? Can I bring him back? I will go to him, but he will not return to me.” 💔
## 2 Samuel 12:24
David comforted his wife Bathsheba and had sexual relations with her. She bore a son, whom they named Solomon. The LORD loved the child, 👶
## 2 Samuel 12:25
and sent word through Nathan the prophet to name him Jedidiah because the LORD loved him. ❤️
## 2 Samuel 12:26
Joab fought Rabbah of the Ammonites and captured its royal fortress. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 12:27
He sent David this message: “I fought Rabbah and captured the city’s water supply. 📖
## 2 Samuel 12:28
Gather the remaining troops, surround the city, and capture it. Otherwise I will capture it, and it will bear my name.” ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 12:29
David gathered all the troops, went to Rabbah, fought it, and captured it. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 12:30
He took the crown from their king’s head. It weighed a talent of gold and had precious stones. It was placed on David’s head. He took much plunder from the city. 👑
## 2 Samuel 12:31
David put the city’s people to work with saws, iron picks, axes, and brick kilns. He did the same in all the Ammonite cities. He and all his troops returned to Jerusalem. 📖
