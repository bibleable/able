## 2 Samuel 8:1
Later, David defeated and subdued the Philistines. He took Metheg-ammah from them. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 8:2
David defeated the Moabites and made them lie on the ground. He measured them with a cord, two lengths for those to die and one for those to live. The Moabites became his subjects and brought tribute. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 8:3
David defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah. Hadadezer had marched to restore his rule along the Euphrates River. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 8:4
David captured a thousand chariots, seven thousand charioteers, and twenty thousand foot soldiers. He hamstrung all the horses except a hundred kept for the chariots. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 8:5
Damascus’s Arameans came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah. David killed twenty-two thousand of them. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 8:6
He stationed garrisons in Aram of Damascus. The Arameans became his subjects and brought tribute. The LORD made David victorious wherever he went. 📖
## 2 Samuel 8:7
David took Hadadezer’s officers’ gold shields to Jerusalem. 🛡️
## 2 Samuel 8:8
He took much bronze from Betah and Berothai, Hadadezer’s cities. 📖
## 2 Samuel 8:9
King Toi of Hamath heard David defeated Hadadezer’s whole army, 📖
## 2 Samuel 8:10
He sent his son Joram to greet and bless King David for defeating Hadadezer, who had been at war with Toi. Joram brought silver, gold, and bronze articles, 📖
## 2 Samuel 8:11
which David dedicated to the LORD. He had also dedicated silver and gold from all the nations he subdued, 📖
## 2 Samuel 8:12
Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, Philistines, and Amalekites, and plunder from Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah. 📖
## 2 Samuel 8:13
David made a name for himself after defeating eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 8:14
He stationed garrisons throughout Edom. All the Edomites became his subjects. The LORD made David victorious wherever he went. 📖
## 2 Samuel 8:15
David ruled all Israel and administered justice and righteousness for all his people. 👑
## 2 Samuel 8:16
Joab son of Zeruiah commanded the army. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud kept the records. 👥
## 2 Samuel 8:17
Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests. Seraiah was the scribe. 👥
## 2 Samuel 8:18
Benaiah son of Jehoiada commanded the Cherethites and Pelethites. David’s sons were priestly leaders. 👥
