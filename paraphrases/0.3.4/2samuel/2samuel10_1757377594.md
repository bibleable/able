## 2 Samuel 10:1
Later, the Ammonites’ king died. His son Hanun succeeded him. 👑
## 2 Samuel 10:2
David said, “I will be kind to Hanun son of Nahash, as his father was kind to me.” He sent servants to comfort Hanun about his father. They reached the Ammonites’ land, 👥
## 2 Samuel 10:3
and its princes told Hanun, “Do you think David’s comforters show respect for your father? Did he not send them to explore, spy on, and overthrow the city?” ⚠️
## 2 Samuel 10:4
Hanun shaved off half of each servant’s beard and cut their garments at the hips. He sent them away. 📖
## 2 Samuel 10:5
David heard and sent messengers to meet the humiliated men. He told them, “Stay in Jericho until your beards grow back. Then return.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 10:6
The Ammonites realized they became a stench to David. They hired twenty thousand Aramean foot soldiers from Beth-rehob and Zoba. They also hired a thousand men from Maacah’s king and twelve thousand from Tob. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:7
David heard and sent Joab with the whole army of mighty men. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:8
The Ammonites prepared for battle at the city gate’s entrance. The Arameans from Zobah and Rehob and the men of Tob and Maacah stood separately in the open country. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:9
Joab saw battle lines ahead and behind him. He chose some of Israel’s best men and positioned them against the Arameans. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:10
He placed the remaining troops under his brother Abishai, who positioned them against the Ammonites. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:11
Joab said, “If the Arameans are too strong for me, rescue me. If the Ammonites are too strong for you, I will rescue you. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:12
Be strong. Let us fight bravely for our people and our God’s cities. May the LORD do what He considers good.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 10:13
Joab and his troops advanced against the Arameans, who fled. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:14
The Ammonites saw the Arameans flee. They fled from Abishai into the city. Joab returned from fighting the Ammonites to Jerusalem. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:15
The Arameans saw Israel defeated them and regrouped. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:16
Hadadezer sent messengers for more Arameans beyond the Euphrates. They came to Helam, led by Shobach, Hadadezer’s army commander. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:17
David heard, gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan, and went to Helam. The Arameans prepared for battle and fought him. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:18
The Arameans fled from Israel. David killed seven hundred charioteers and forty thousand foot soldiers. He struck down their commander Shobach, who died there. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 10:19
Hadadezer’s subject kings saw Israel defeated them. They made peace with Israel and became Israel’s subjects. The Arameans feared helping the Ammonites again. 📖
