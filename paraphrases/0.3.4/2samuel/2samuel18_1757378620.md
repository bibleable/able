## 2 Samuel 18:1
David reviewed his troops and appointed commanders over thousands and hundreds. 👥
## 2 Samuel 18:2
He sent a third under Joab, a third under Joab’s brother Abishai son of Zeruiah, and a third under Ittai the Gittite. He said, “I will march with you too.” ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 18:3
They pleaded, “Do not go! They will not care if we flee or even if half of us die. You are worth ten thousand of us. Support us from the city instead.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:4
The king answered, “I will do what seems best to you.” He stood by the gate as the troops left by hundreds and thousands. 👥
## 2 Samuel 18:5
He commanded Joab, Abishai, and Ittai, “Treat young Absalom gently for my sake.” Everyone heard his orders to each commander. 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:6
David’s army met Israel in battle in Ephraim’s forest. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 18:7
David’s servants defeated Israel’s people there. Twenty thousand men died in the great slaughter that day. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 18:8
Battle spread across the countryside. The forest consumed more people than the sword that day. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 18:9
Absalom met David’s servants while riding his mule. Beneath a large oak’s thick branches, his head became caught in the tree. The mule kept going, leaving him hanging in midair. 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:10
A man told Joab, “I saw Absalom hanging in an oak!” 💬
## 2 Samuel 18:11
Joab said, “You saw him! Why did you not strike him down? I would have given you ten silver shekels and a warrior’s belt!” 💬
## 2 Samuel 18:12
The man answered, “Even for a thousand silver shekels, I would not attack the king’s son. We heard the king tell you, Abishai, and Ittai to protect young Absalom for his sake. 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:13
If I had risked my life, you would have abandoned me. Nothing is hidden from the king.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:14
Joab said, “I will not wait like this with you!” He thrust three spears through Absalom’s heart while he was still alive in the oak. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 18:15
Ten young men carrying Joab’s armor surrounded Absalom and killed him. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 18:16
Joab blew the ram’s horn and restrained his troops. They stopped pursuing Israel. 📯
## 2 Samuel 18:17
They threw Absalom into a large forest pit and piled many stones over him. All the Israelites fled home. 🪨
## 2 Samuel 18:18
Absalom had built a pillar in the King’s Valley during his life. He said, “I have no son to preserve my name’s memory.” He named the pillar after himself. It is called Absalom’s Monument to this day. 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:19
Ahimaaz son of Zadok said, “Let me run and tell the king the good news. The LORD avenged him against his enemies.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:20
Joab answered, “Take good news another day, not today. The king’s son is dead.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:21
He told a Cushite, “Tell the king what you saw.” The Cushite bowed to Joab and ran. 👥
## 2 Samuel 18:22
Ahimaaz son of Zadok insisted, “Let me run after the Cushite, no matter what!” Joab asked, “My son, why run without a reward?” 💬
## 2 Samuel 18:23
He answered, “I want to run, no matter what!” Joab said, “Run!” Ahimaaz ran by the plain and passed the Cushite. 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:24
David sat between the two gates. The watchman climbed the gateway roof by the wall and saw a man running alone. 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:25
He called to the king, who answered, “If he is alone, he brings good news.” The first runner approached, 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:26
and the watchman saw another runner. He told the gatekeeper, “Another man runs alone!” The king said, “He brings good news too.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:27
The watchman said, “The first runs like Ahimaaz son of Zadok.” The king answered, “He is a good man and brings good news.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:28
Ahimaaz called, “All is well!” He bowed facedown before the king. “Blessed be the LORD your God!” he said. “He delivered the men who opposed my lord the king.” 🙏
## 2 Samuel 18:29
The king asked, “Is young Absalom safe?” Ahimaaz answered, “When Joab sent the king’s servant and your servant, I saw great turmoil. I do not know what it was.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 18:30
The king said, “Stand aside here.” He stepped aside. 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:31
The Cushite arrived and said, “Hear good news, my lord the king! Today the LORD avenged you against everyone who rose against you!” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:32
The king asked the Cushite, “Is young Absalom safe?” He answered, “May your enemies and everyone seeking to harm you be like that young man.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 18:33
The king was shaken. He went to the room above the gate and wept. As he walked, he cried, “My son Absalom! My son, my son Absalom! If only I had died instead of you, Absalom, my son, my son!” 💔
