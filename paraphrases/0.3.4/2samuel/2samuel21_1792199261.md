## 2 Samuel 21:1
During David’s reign, famine lasted three successive years. He sought the LORD, who said, “Saul and his family shed blood by killing the Gibeonites. That is why this happened.” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 21:2
David called the Gibeonites and spoke to them. They were remaining Amorites, not Israelites. Israel had sworn an oath concerning them. Saul tried to kill them in his zeal for Israel and Judah. 📖
## 2 Samuel 21:3
David asked, “What can I do for you? How can I make amends so you may bless the LORD’s inheritance?” ❓
## 2 Samuel 21:4
They answered, “We need no silver or gold from Saul or his house. Nor should you kill anyone in Israel for us.” He said, “I will do whatever you ask.” 💬
## 2 Samuel 21:5
They answered, “The man consumed us and plotted to exterminate us from all Israel’s territory. 📖
## 2 Samuel 21:6
Give us seven of his male descendants to hang before the LORD at Gibeah of Saul, the LORD’s chosen.” The king answered, “I will give them.” ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 21:7
He spared Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, because of David’s and Jonathan son of Saul’s oath before the LORD. 📖
## 2 Samuel 21:8
He took Armoni and Mephibosheth, the two sons Rizpah daughter of Aiah bore to Saul. He also took the five sons Merab daughter of Saul bore to Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite. 👥
## 2 Samuel 21:9
He gave them to the Gibeonites, who hanged them on the hill before the LORD. All seven died together at the beginning of the barley harvest. ⚖️
## 2 Samuel 21:10
Rizpah daughter of Aiah spread sackcloth for herself on a rock. From harvest’s beginning until rain fell on the bodies, she kept birds away by day and wild beasts by night. 💔
## 2 Samuel 21:11
David heard what Saul’s concubine Rizpah daughter of Aiah had done, 📖
## 2 Samuel 21:12
He took Saul’s and his son Jonathan’s bones from Jabesh-gilead’s men. They had stolen them from Beth-shan’s public square, where the Philistines hung the bodies after defeating Saul at Gilboa. 🪦
## 2 Samuel 21:13
David brought their bones from there. They gathered the bones of those hanged too. 🪦
## 2 Samuel 21:14
They buried Saul’s and Jonathan’s bones at Zela in Benjamin, in Saul’s father Kish’s tomb. After they obeyed all the king’s commands, God answered their prayers for the land. 📖
## 2 Samuel 21:15
The Philistines again fought Israel. David and his servants fought them, but David became exhausted. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 21:16
Ishbi-benob, a descendant of Rapha, resolved to kill David. His bronze spear weighed three hundred shekels, and he carried a new sword. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 21:17
Abishai son of Zeruiah helped David and killed the Philistine. David’s men swore, “Never fight with us again, so Israel’s lamp is not extinguished.” 📖
## 2 Samuel 21:18
Later, at Gob, they fought the Philistines again. Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Saph, a descendant of Rapha. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 21:19
There was another battle with the Philistines at Gob. Elhanan son of Jair the Bethlehemite killed the brother of Goliath the Gittite, whose spear shaft was like a weaver’s beam. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 21:20
At Gath, a very tall descendant of Rapha had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot, twenty-four in all. 📖
## 2 Samuel 21:21
He taunted Israel, and Jonathan, son of David’s brother Shimei, killed him. ⚔️
## 2 Samuel 21:22
These four descendants of Rapha in Gath fell to David and his servants. ⚔️
