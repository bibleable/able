# 2 Chronicles 5 — The priests bring the ark into the temple

**About this chapter**

Solomon finishes building the temple in Jerusalem.

The priests carry a sacred chest into the temple.

The people praise the LORD, and a cloud fills his house.

## Solomon gathers Israel’s leaders

## 2 Chronicles 5:1

King Solomon finished the work on the LORD’s temple.

His father David had set gifts aside for God.

These included silver, gold, and things for the temple.

Solomon put them in the temple’s treasure rooms.

## 2 Chronicles 5:2

Solomon called Israel’s elders together in Jerusalem.

These men led Israel’s tribes and families.

They came to bring the ark up from Zion.

Zion was called the City of David.

The ark was a sacred chest.

It reminded Israel of their covenant with the LORD.

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

## 2 Chronicles 5:3

All the men of Israel gathered around the king.

They came for the festival in the seventh month.

## 2 Chronicles 5:4

All the elders arrived.

Then men from the tribe of Levi lifted the ark.

## 2 Chronicles 5:5

Priests from Levi’s tribe carried the ark up to the temple.

They also brought the Tent of Meeting.

This was the tent where Israel had worshiped God.

They brought all the holy things from inside the tent.

## 2 Chronicles 5:6

King Solomon and the people gathered in front of the ark.

They offered sheep and oxen as sacrifices to God.

They offered more animals than anyone could count.

## The ark goes into the Most Holy Place

## 2 Chronicles 5:7

The priests carried the ark into the temple’s inner room.

This room was called the Most Holy Place.

There were two figures with wings in this room.

They represented heavenly beings called cherubim.

The priests set the ark beneath their wings.

![The temple plan shows the main hall on the left and the Most Holy Place on the right.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 5:8

The wings stretched out over the ark.

Their shadow also covered the poles used to carry it.

## 2 Chronicles 5:9

The carrying poles were long.

From just in front of the inner room, people could see their ends.

From outside the Holy Place, no one could see them.

The poles were still there when this account was written.

## 2 Chronicles 5:10

Inside the ark were two flat stones and nothing else.

Moses had put them there at Mount Horeb.

There the LORD made his covenant with Israel.

This happened after the people left Egypt.

## Singers and trumpeters praise the LORD

## 2 Chronicles 5:11

The priests usually served in groups.

Every priest there had prepared himself for holy service.

They did this whatever group they belonged to.

Then the priests came out of the Holy Place.

## 2 Chronicles 5:12

The singers from Levi’s tribe stood east of the altar.

The altar was where the people offered sacrifices.

Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun stood with their sons and relatives.

The singers wore fine linen clothes.

They played cymbals and two kinds of string instruments.

These were harps and lyres.

Alongside them, 120 priests blew trumpets.

## 2 Chronicles 5:13

The trumpeters and singers made music together.

With one voice they praised and thanked the LORD.

Over the sound of trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments, they sang,

“The LORD is good.

His faithful love lasts forever.”

Then a cloud filled the LORD’s temple.

## 2 Chronicles 5:14

Because of the cloud, the priests could no longer stand there to serve.

The LORD’s glory filled the temple.

The cloud showed the people that the LORD was there.
