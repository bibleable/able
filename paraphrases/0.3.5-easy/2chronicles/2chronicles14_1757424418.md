# 2 Chronicles 14 — Asa asks the LORD for help

**About this chapter**

King Asa leads Judah through ten years of peace.

Then a large army comes from Cush.

Asa asks the LORD to help his people.

## Asa leads Judah back to the LORD

## 2 Chronicles 14:1

King Abijah died.

His people buried him in the City of David.

His son Asa became king.

For ten years Judah had peace.

## 2 Chronicles 14:2

Asa did what the LORD his God saw was good and right.

## 2 Chronicles 14:3

Asa removed the altars people used to worship foreign gods.

He removed their hilltop places of worship.

He smashed the stone pillars they treated as holy.

He cut down the poles they used to worship Asherah.

## 2 Chronicles 14:4

Asa told Judah’s people to seek the LORD.

Their ancestors had worshiped him.

Asa told them to follow the LORD’s law and obey his commands.

## 2 Chronicles 14:5

Asa removed the hilltop places of worship throughout Judah’s cities.

He also removed the altars where people burned incense.

Incense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

Judah had peace under Asa.

## The people build during the years of peace

## 2 Chronicles 14:6

The LORD gave Asa’s people rest from war.

No one attacked Asa during those years.

So Asa built cities in Judah with strong defenses.

## 2 Chronicles 14:7

Asa told the people, “Let us build up these cities.

Let us build walls and towers around them.

Let us put up gates with bars to hold them shut.

This land is still ours because we sought the LORD our God.

We sought him, and he gave us peace on every side.”

So the people built, and their work went well.

![A bar holds the city gate shut between two towers.](../pictures/asa-city-gate.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 14:8

Asa had 300,000 soldiers from Judah.

They carried large shields and spears.

Another 280,000 soldiers came from Benjamin.

They carried small shields and shot arrows with bows.

All these men were brave fighters.

## Asa prays when an army comes from Cush

## 2 Chronicles 14:9

Zerah came from Cush to attack Judah.

He brought a million soldiers and 300 chariots.

Chariots were carts that horses pulled into battle.

Zerah’s army reached the town of Mareshah.

## 2 Chronicles 14:10

Asa marched out to meet Zerah.

Asa’s army lined up for battle near Mareshah.

They stood in the Valley of Zephathah.

## 2 Chronicles 14:11

Asa called out to the LORD his God.

“LORD, only you can help the weak against the strong.

Help us, LORD our God.

We depend on you.

We have come in your name to face this great army.

LORD, you are our God.

Do not let a human being win against you.”

## Judah’s army returns to Jerusalem

## 2 Chronicles 14:12

The LORD struck down the soldiers from Cush before Asa and Judah.

The soldiers from Cush ran away.

## 2 Chronicles 14:13

Asa and his army chased them as far as Gerar.

The LORD and his army defeated the soldiers from Cush.

Those soldiers fell and could not recover.

Judah’s soldiers carried away many things from the defeated army.

## 2 Chronicles 14:14

The people in the cities around Gerar were terrified of the LORD.

Judah’s soldiers attacked all those cities.

They found many valuable things there and took them away.

## 2 Chronicles 14:15

Judah’s soldiers also attacked the tents of the people tending the animals.

They took many sheep and camels.

Then they went back to Jerusalem.
