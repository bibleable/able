# 2 Chronicles 19 — Jehoshaphat appoints judges

**About this chapter**

A messenger challenges King Jehoshaphat about a choice he made. Later the king chooses judges and tells them how to treat people fairly.

## Jehu speaks to the king

## 2 Chronicles 19:1

Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, came home safely to Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 19:2

Jehu, the son of Hanani, went out to meet the king.

Jehu was a seer, someone who received messages from God.

Jehu asked, “Should you help people who do evil?

Should you love those who hate the LORD?

The LORD is angry with you because you did this.

## 2 Chronicles 19:3

“Yet you have also done good things.

You removed the poles people used to worship the goddess Asherah.

You have made up your mind to seek God.”

## Judges in the cities of Judah

## 2 Chronicles 19:4

Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem.

Once again he went out to visit the people.

He traveled from Beersheba to the hills of Ephraim.

He led the people back to the LORD, the God their ancestors worshiped.

## 2 Chronicles 19:5

Jehoshaphat chose judges for each city in Judah that had strong defenses.

A judge listens to a case and decides what is right.

## 2 Chronicles 19:6

The king told the judges, “Think carefully about each decision.

You judge on behalf of the LORD.

He is with you when you decide a case.

## 2 Chronicles 19:7

“Show deep respect for the LORD.

Take care to judge as He does.

The LORD our God treats people fairly.

He has no favorites.

He never takes a bribe.”

A bribe is a gift meant to buy an unfair decision.

## Judges in Jerusalem

## 2 Chronicles 19:8

Jehoshaphat also chose judges who lived in Jerusalem.

Some were Levites, who served at the temple.

Others were priests or leaders of Israelite families.

They were to judge for the LORD and settle disagreements.

## 2 Chronicles 19:9

He told them, “Serve the LORD with deep respect.

Do this work faithfully and with your whole heart.

## 2 Chronicles 19:10

“People from other cities will bring their disagreements to you.

A case might concern a killing.

Or it might concern one of God’s laws or commands.

Warn the people so they do not become guilty before the LORD.

Then His anger will not fall on you or them.

If you do your duty, you will be free of guilt.

## 2 Chronicles 19:11

“Amariah, the chief priest, will lead you in matters concerning the LORD.

Zebadiah, the son of Ishmael, leads the people of Judah.

He will lead you in matters concerning the king.

The Levites will serve as your officers.

Be brave and act!

May the LORD be with those who do right.”
