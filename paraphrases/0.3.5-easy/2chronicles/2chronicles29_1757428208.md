# 2 Chronicles 29 — Hezekiah opens the temple doors

**About this chapter**

King Ahaz had shut the doors of the LORD’s temple. His son Hezekiah opens them. The priests and Levites get the temple ready so the people can worship there again.

## Hezekiah opens the doors

## 2 Chronicles 29:1

Hezekiah was twenty-five when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-nine years.

His mother was Abijah, daughter of Zechariah.

## 2 Chronicles 29:2

He did what pleased the LORD, as his ancestor David had done.

## 2 Chronicles 29:3

In his very first month as king, Hezekiah opened the doors of the LORD’s house and had them repaired.

## 2 Chronicles 29:4

He called the priests and Levites together in the open square on the east side.

## 2 Chronicles 29:5

“Listen, Levites,” he said.

“Make yourselves ready for holy service.

Set the temple apart again for the LORD, the God of your ancestors.

Take everything unclean out of the Holy Place.

## 2 Chronicles 29:6

Our ancestors broke faith with the LORD our God.

They did what he saw was evil.

They turned their backs on him and left his house.

## 2 Chronicles 29:7

They shut the porch doors and put out the lamps.

They stopped burning incense and bringing burnt offerings to Israel’s God in his Holy Place.

## 2 Chronicles 29:8

That is why the LORD was angry with Judah and Jerusalem.

You have seen what happened.

Some people look at us in horror.

Others mock us.

## 2 Chronicles 29:9

Our fathers died by the sword.

Enemies took our sons, daughters, and wives away as prisoners.

## 2 Chronicles 29:10

I want to make a covenant with the LORD, Israel’s God.

A covenant is a solemn promise.

I want his fierce anger to turn away from us.

## 2 Chronicles 29:11

My sons, give this work your full care.

The LORD chose you to stand before him, serve him, and burn incense.”

## The Levites gather their families

## 2 Chronicles 29:12

The Levites began their work.

From Kohath’s family came Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah.

From Merari’s family came Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel.

From Gershon’s family came Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah.

## 2 Chronicles 29:13

Shimri and Jeuel came from Elizaphan’s family.

Zechariah and Mattaniah came from Asaph’s family.

## 2 Chronicles 29:14

Jehiel and Shimei came from Heman’s family.

Shemaiah and Uzziel came from Jeduthun’s family.

## 2 Chronicles 29:15

These men gathered their fellow Levites.

They prepared themselves for holy service.

Then they went to cleanse the LORD’s house, as the king had ordered according to the LORD’s words.

## The priests and Levites cleanse the temple

## 2 Chronicles 29:16

The priests went inside the temple.

They took everything unclean into the courtyard.

From there the Levites carried those things to the Kidron Valley.

## 2 Chronicles 29:17

They began on the first day of the first month.

On day eight they reached the temple porch.

They spent eight more days making the temple ready for holy use.

They finished on day sixteen.

## 2 Chronicles 29:18

They told Hezekiah, “We have cleansed the whole temple.

The altar for burnt offerings and its tools are ready.

So are the table for the sacred bread and all its tools.

## 2 Chronicles 29:19

When King Ahaz turned away from the LORD, he threw aside things used for worship.

We have prepared them for holy use again.

They are in front of the LORD’s altar.”

## The king brings offerings for all Israel

## 2 Chronicles 29:20

Early the next morning Hezekiah gathered the city officials.

Together they went to the LORD’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 29:21

They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats.

They brought them because of sin in the kingdom, in the holy place, and among Judah’s people.

Hezekiah told the priests, Aaron’s descendants, to offer the animals on the LORD’s altar.

## 2 Chronicles 29:22

They killed the bulls.

The priests sprinkled the blood on the altar.

They did the same with the rams and the lambs.

## 2 Chronicles 29:23

They brought the goats for the sin offering to the king and the people gathered there.

The king and the people laid their hands on the goats.

## 2 Chronicles 29:24

The priests killed the goats and put their blood on the altar.

This was an offering asking God to forgive all Israel’s sins.

The king had ordered both the burnt offering and the sin offering for all Israel.

## Music fills the temple

## 2 Chronicles 29:25

Hezekiah gave the Levites places in the temple with cymbals, harps, and lyres.

They followed directions from David, Gad the king’s seer, and Nathan the prophet.

The LORD had given these directions through his prophets.

## 2 Chronicles 29:26

The Levites stood ready with David’s instruments.

The priests held trumpets.

![A harp like the stringed instruments the Levites played.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 29:27

Hezekiah gave the order for the burnt offering.

As the sacrifice began, the singers began their song to the LORD.

The trumpets sounded, and the musicians played David’s instruments.

## 2 Chronicles 29:28

Everyone gathered there worshiped.

The singers sang and the trumpeters played until the burnt offering was complete.

## 2 Chronicles 29:29

Then the king and everyone with him bowed down and worshiped.

## 2 Chronicles 29:30

Hezekiah and his officials asked the Levites to sing to the LORD.

They used words from David and Asaph the seer, who spoke messages from God.

The Levites sang with joy.

Then they bowed down and worshiped.

## The people give thanks

## 2 Chronicles 29:31

Hezekiah said, “You have set yourselves apart for the LORD.

Come to his house with sacrifices and gifts of thanks.” The people brought those gifts.

Everyone who wanted to also brought burnt offerings.

## 2 Chronicles 29:32

For burnt offerings to the LORD, the people brought seventy bulls, a hundred rams, and two hundred lambs.

## 2 Chronicles 29:33

They also set apart six hundred bulls and three thousand sheep as holy gifts.

## 2 Chronicles 29:34

Too few priests were ready to remove the skins from all the burnt offerings.

The Levites had taken more care to get ready for holy service.

They helped until the work was done and the priests were ready.

## 2 Chronicles 29:35

There were many burnt offerings.

There was also fat from the peace offerings and there were drink offerings to go with the burnt offerings.

Worship in the LORD’s house had begun again.

## 2 Chronicles 29:36

Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced at what God had prepared for them.

It had all happened so quickly.
