# 2 Chronicles 10 — The people ask Rehoboam for lighter work

**About this chapter**

Solomon’s son Rehoboam is about to become king.

The people ask him to ease the work his father required.

Rehoboam has three days to decide how to answer.

## The people bring their request

## 2 Chronicles 10:1

The people of Israel gathered at Shechem to make Rehoboam king.

Rehoboam went there to meet them.

## 2 Chronicles 10:2

Jeroboam, son of Nebat, heard the news in Egypt.

He had fled there to escape King Solomon.

Now Jeroboam returned from Egypt.

## 2 Chronicles 10:3

The people sent for Jeroboam.

Together, Jeroboam and the people went to speak to Rehoboam.

## 2 Chronicles 10:4

They said, “Your father put a heavy yoke on us.”

A yoke is a wooden bar an animal wears to pull a load.

The people meant the hard work Solomon made them do.

They said, “Make our work lighter, and we will serve you.”

## 2 Chronicles 10:5

Rehoboam said, “Come back to me in three days.”

So the people left.

## Rehoboam asks two groups for advice

## 2 Chronicles 10:6

King Rehoboam turned to the elders for advice.

These men had served his father Solomon.

Rehoboam asked, “How should I answer the people?”

## 2 Chronicles 10:7

The elders said, “Be kind to the people.

Give them an answer that pleases them.

Speak kindly to them, and they will always serve you.”

## 2 Chronicles 10:8

Rehoboam rejected the elders’ advice.

He turned to the young men who had grown up with him.

They now served him.

## 2 Chronicles 10:9

He asked, “The people want me to lighten my father’s yoke.

What answer do you think we should give them?”

## 2 Chronicles 10:10

The young men told Rehoboam, “The people want lighter work.

Tell them, ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist!’”

This boast meant Rehoboam could be even tougher than Solomon.

## 2 Chronicles 10:11

The young men told him to say,

“My father put a heavy yoke on you.

I will make it heavier.

My father used whips on you.

I will use scorpions.”

A scorpion has a painful sting.

The words were a threat of even harsher punishment.

## Rehoboam gives his answer

## 2 Chronicles 10:12

On the third day Jeroboam and the people came back.

That was when King Rehoboam had told them to return.

## 2 Chronicles 10:13

The king spoke harshly to the people.

He refused to follow the elders’ advice.

## 2 Chronicles 10:14

He gave the answer the young men had suggested.

“My father made your yoke heavy.

I will make it heavier.

My father used whips on you.

I will use scorpions.”

## 2 Chronicles 10:15

The king refused to listen to the people.

God brought about this turn of events.

Earlier the LORD had sent Ahijah from Shiloh to speak to Jeroboam.

Now the LORD was making that message come true.

## Israel leaves David’s royal family

## 2 Chronicles 10:16

The Israelites saw that the king would not listen.

They said, “What share do we have in David’s family?

What will Jesse’s son leave to us?”

David was Jesse’s son and Rehoboam’s grandfather.

The people shouted, “Israel, go home to your tents!

David’s family can look after itself!”

Then the people went home.

## 2 Chronicles 10:17

Rehoboam still ruled the Israelites who lived in Judah’s cities.

## 2 Chronicles 10:18

King Rehoboam sent Hadoram to the Israelites.

Hadoram was in charge of the people forced to work.

The Israelites threw stones at Hadoram and killed him.

Rehoboam hurried into his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 10:19

Israel broke away from David’s family.

When this account was written, Israel still refused to serve that family.
