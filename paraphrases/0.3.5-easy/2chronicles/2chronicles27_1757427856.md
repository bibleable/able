# 2 Chronicles 27 — Jotham rules in Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jotham becomes king of Judah. He follows the LORD and grows strong. He builds in Jerusalem and in other parts of Judah.

## Jotham becomes king

## 2 Chronicles 27:1

Jotham was 25 years old when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years.

His mother was Jerushah, the daughter of Zadok.

## 2 Chronicles 27:2

Jotham did what the LORD saw was right.

His father Uzziah had also done what was right.

Jotham stayed out of the LORD’s temple.

But the people kept doing wrong.

## Jotham builds and fights

## 2 Chronicles 27:3

Jotham rebuilt the Upper Gate at the LORD’s temple.

He also did much work on the wall at Ophel.

Ophel was a hill in Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 27:4

Jotham built cities in the hills of Judah.

In the forests, he built forts and towers.

Forts are strong places where people can defend themselves.

## 2 Chronicles 27:5

Jotham fought the king of the Ammonites and won.

That year the Ammonites paid Jotham 100 talents of silver.

A talent was a measure of weight.

They also gave him 10,000 cors of wheat.

They gave him 10,000 cors of barley too.

A cor was a large measure for grain.

The next year they paid the same amounts.

In the third year they paid them again.

## Why Jotham grew strong

## 2 Chronicles 27:6

Jotham grew powerful because he took care to follow the LORD his God.

## 2 Chronicles 27:7

People wrote down the rest of what Jotham did.

Their record tells about his wars and how he lived.

It is called the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah.

## 2 Chronicles 27:8

Jotham became king at 25.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years.

## 2 Chronicles 27:9

Then Jotham died, as his ancestors had.

People buried him in the City of David.

His son Ahaz became king after him.
