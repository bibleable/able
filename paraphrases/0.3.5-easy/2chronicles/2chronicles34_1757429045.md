# 2 Chronicles 34 — Josiah hears the Book of the Law

**About this chapter**

During repairs to the temple, a priest finds the book of God’s law. King Josiah listens to it. He sends messengers to ask what the LORD wants him and his people to do.

## Josiah clears away the idols

## 2 Chronicles 34:1

Josiah became king at eight years old.

He ruled in Jerusalem for thirty-one years.

## 2 Chronicles 34:2

He did what the LORD saw was right.

He followed the example of his ancestor David and stayed on that path.

## 2 Chronicles 34:3

In his eighth year as king, Josiah began seeking the God David had served.

Josiah was still young.

In his twelfth year he began clearing away places of worship on the hills of Judah and Jerusalem.

He removed poles used to worship the goddess Asherah and idols made of wood or metal.

## 2 Chronicles 34:4

Josiah watched as workers tore down the altars of the gods called Baals.

He broke up the altars above them where people burned incense.

He crushed the poles and idols to dust.

He scattered the dust on the graves of the people who had worshiped them.

## 2 Chronicles 34:5

He burned the bones of the priests of those gods on their altars.

This was how he cleared Judah and Jerusalem of those places of worship.

## 2 Chronicles 34:6

Josiah did the same in the towns of Manasseh, Ephraim, and Simeon.

He went as far as Naphtali and into the ruins around the towns.

## 2 Chronicles 34:7

Throughout Israel he tore down altars and poles.

He crushed idols to powder and broke up the incense altars.

Then he returned to Jerusalem.

## Workers repair the temple

## 2 Chronicles 34:8

In his eighteenth year as king, Josiah wanted the land and the temple to be holy again.

He sent three men to have the LORD’s house repaired.

They were Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the city governor, and Joah son of Joahaz, who kept records.

## 2 Chronicles 34:9

They gave Hilkiah the high priest the money collected at the temple doors.

Levites who guarded the doors had received it from Manasseh, Ephraim, and the rest of Israel.

People throughout Judah, Benjamin, and Jerusalem had also given money.

## 2 Chronicles 34:10

The men in charge of repairs received the money.

They paid the workers who repaired the LORD’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 34:11

Carpenters and builders bought stone already cut to shape.

They bought wood to join and support the buildings.

Earlier kings of Judah had let those buildings fall into disrepair.

## 2 Chronicles 34:12

The workers did their jobs faithfully.

Levites led the work.

Jahath and Obadiah came from Merari’s family.

Zechariah and Meshullam came from Kohath’s family.

Other Levites were skilled musicians.

## 2 Chronicles 34:13

Those musicians directed the laborers and supervised each kind of work.

Other Levites kept records, served as officers, or guarded gates.

## Hilkiah finds a book

## 2 Chronicles 34:14

While the men were bringing out the money, Hilkiah found a book in the temple.

It was the Book of the Law that the LORD had given through Moses.

## 2 Chronicles 34:15

Hilkiah told Shaphan the scribe, “I found the Book of the Law in the LORD’s house!” A scribe was someone whose work included reading and writing.

Hilkiah gave Shaphan the book.

![A scroll, the kind of book a reader unrolled.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 34:16

Shaphan took the book to King Josiah.

He said, “Your servants are doing everything you asked.

## 2 Chronicles 34:17

They have paid out the money from the LORD’s house.

The supervisors and workers have received it.”

## 2 Chronicles 34:18

Then Shaphan said, “Hilkiah the priest gave me a book.” He read it aloud to Josiah.

## 2 Chronicles 34:19

When Josiah heard the words of the law, he tore his clothes.

This showed how deeply the words upset him.

## 2 Chronicles 34:20

Josiah gave an order to Hilkiah and four others.

They were Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah, the king’s servant.

## 2 Chronicles 34:21

“Ask the LORD about the words in this book.

Ask for me and for the people still in Israel and Judah.

Our ancestors failed to obey what the LORD had written here.

That is why his anger against us is so great.”

## Huldah speaks for the LORD

## 2 Chronicles 34:22

Hilkiah and the others went to Huldah, a prophet who spoke for God.

She lived in the Second District of Jerusalem.

Her husband Shallum cared for the clothing.

He was the son of Tokhath and grandson of Hasrah.

## 2 Chronicles 34:23

Huldah said, “The LORD, Israel’s God, has a message for the man who sent you.

## 2 Chronicles 34:24

The LORD says, ‘I will bring disaster on this place and its people.

The king heard the curses read aloud.

What the book warned about will happen.

## 2 Chronicles 34:25

My people left me and burned incense to other gods.

What they made with their own hands made me angry.

My anger will burn against this place like a fire no one can put out.’

## 2 Chronicles 34:26

Now give the king of Judah this answer from the LORD, the God of Israel.

He sent you to ask about the words he heard.

## 2 Chronicles 34:27

The LORD says, ‘My words about this place and its people touched your heart.

You humbled yourself before me.

You tore your clothes and wept.

I heard you.

## 2 Chronicles 34:28

You will join your ancestors, and people will bury you in peace.

You will never see the disaster I bring on this place and its people.’” The messengers took Huldah’s answer back to Josiah.

## Josiah reads the book to everyone

## 2 Chronicles 34:29

Josiah called together all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 34:30

He went to the LORD’s house with the people of Judah and Jerusalem.

Priests and Levites came too.

People of every rank heard Josiah read the whole Book of the Covenant found in the temple.

A covenant is a solemn promise.

## 2 Chronicles 34:31

Josiah stood beside the pillar and made a promise before the LORD.

He would follow the LORD with his whole heart and soul.

He would keep all the LORD’s laws and do what the covenant said.

## 2 Chronicles 34:32

Josiah asked everyone in Jerusalem and Benjamin to join in the promise.

The people of Jerusalem kept the covenant with the God of their ancestors.

## 2 Chronicles 34:33

Josiah removed things used in forbidden worship throughout Israel’s lands.

He required everyone in Israel to serve the LORD their God.

As long as Josiah lived, they kept following the God of their ancestors.
