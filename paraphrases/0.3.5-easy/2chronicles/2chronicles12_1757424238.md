# 2 Chronicles 12 — Rehoboam listens when Egypt attacks

**About this chapter**

Rehoboam turns away from the LORD.

When Egypt attacks, the LORD sends him a message.

Rehoboam and his leaders admit that the LORD is right.

## Egypt’s army comes to Jerusalem

## 2 Chronicles 12:1

King Rehoboam had gained full power over his kingdom.

Once he was strong, he stopped following the LORD’s law.

All the Israelites with him did the same.

## 2 Chronicles 12:2

Shishak was the king of Egypt.

He attacked Jerusalem in Rehoboam’s fifth year as king.

This happened because the people had turned away from the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 12:3

Shishak brought 1,200 chariots and 60,000 men on horses.

Chariots were carts pulled by horses.

So many other soldiers came that no one could count them.

Libyans, Sukkites, and Cushites came with Shishak from Egypt.

## 2 Chronicles 12:4

Shishak captured Judah’s cities with their strong walls.

Then his army reached Jerusalem.

## Shemaiah brings the LORD’s message

## 2 Chronicles 12:5

Judah’s leaders gathered in Jerusalem because Shishak was coming.

Shemaiah came to speak to them and King Rehoboam.

Shemaiah was a prophet, someone who spoke God’s message.

He said, “The LORD says, ‘You have turned away from me.

So I have left you in Shishak’s power.’”

## 2 Chronicles 12:6

The king and Israel’s leaders put aside their pride.

They said, “The LORD is right.”

## 2 Chronicles 12:7

The LORD saw that they had put aside their pride.

He told Shemaiah, “They have humbled themselves.

I will spare them and soon give them relief.

In my anger I could let Shishak destroy Jerusalem.

But I will hold back my anger.

## 2 Chronicles 12:8

“They will still have to serve Shishak.

They know what it is like to serve me.

Now they will learn what it means to serve foreign kings.”

## Shishak takes the gold shields

## 2 Chronicles 12:9

Shishak attacked Jerusalem.

He took the treasures from the LORD’s temple and the king’s palace.

He took everything, even the gold shields Solomon had made.

## 2 Chronicles 12:10

Rehoboam made bronze shields to replace the gold ones.

Bronze is a metal.

He put the shields in the care of the officers who guarded the palace entrance.

## 2 Chronicles 12:11

Whenever Rehoboam went to the LORD’s temple, the guards went too.

They carried the shields with them.

Afterward they put the shields back in the guardroom.

## 2 Chronicles 12:12

Rehoboam had humbled himself, so the LORD turned his anger away.

He spared Rehoboam from complete ruin.

Things went well in Judah.

## The rest of Rehoboam’s reign

## 2 Chronicles 12:13

Rehoboam ruled as king in Jerusalem and grew strong there.

He was forty-one when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for seventeen years.

Of all the cities in Israel, the LORD had chosen Jerusalem.

There the people would worship him by name.

Rehoboam’s mother was Naamah, an Ammonite woman.

## 2 Chronicles 12:14

Rehoboam did evil.

He had never made up his mind to seek the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 12:15

Shemaiah the prophet and Iddo the seer kept family records.

A seer was someone God showed things to.

Their records tell what Rehoboam did from beginning to end.

Rehoboam and Jeroboam were at war throughout their lives.

## 2 Chronicles 12:16

Rehoboam died, and his people buried him in the City of David.

His son Abijah became king after him.
