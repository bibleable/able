# 2 Chronicles 30 — Hezekiah invites Israel and Judah to Passover

**About this chapter**

Passover was a feast to remember how the LORD freed Israel from Egypt. King Hezekiah invites people from the north and the south to celebrate it together in Jerusalem.

## A new date for the feast

## 2 Chronicles 30:1

Hezekiah sent an invitation throughout Israel and Judah.

He also wrote to Ephraim and Manasseh.

He invited them to the LORD’s house in Jerusalem to celebrate Passover and honor the God of Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 30:2

The king, his officials, and the people gathered in Jerusalem had chosen the second month for Passover.

## 2 Chronicles 30:3

They had missed the usual date.

Too few priests had made themselves ready for holy service.

The people had yet to gather in Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 30:4

The king and everyone at the meeting were pleased with the plan.

## 2 Chronicles 30:5

They decided to announce the feast from Beersheba in the south to Dan in the north.

People throughout Israel were to come to Jerusalem for the LORD’s Passover.

They had not been gathering in large numbers as the law called for.

## Messengers carry the invitation

## 2 Chronicles 30:6

At the king’s command, messengers carried letters throughout Israel and Judah.

The king and his officials wrote, “People of Israel, return to the LORD, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel.

Some of you escaped the kings of Assyria.

Turn back to the LORD so he may turn back to you.

![An unrolled scroll with lines of writing, like a message carried from town to town.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 30:7

Your fathers and brothers broke faith with the God their ancestors worshiped.

You have seen the terrible sight they became.

Learn from what happened to them.

## 2 Chronicles 30:8

Your ancestors stubbornly refused to listen.

Give yourselves to the LORD.

Come to his temple, the place he set apart forever.

Serve the LORD your God so that his fierce anger will turn away from you.

## 2 Chronicles 30:9

If you return to the LORD, those holding your brothers and sons captive will show them mercy.

Your families will come back to this land.

The LORD your God is kind and merciful.

He will welcome you if you come back to him.”

## 2 Chronicles 30:10

The messengers went from town to town in Ephraim and Manasseh, as far as Zebulun.

People laughed at them and made fun of them.

## 2 Chronicles 30:11

Yet some people from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun set aside their pride.

They came to Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 30:12

In Judah, God helped the people act together with one purpose.

They obeyed the king and his officials as the LORD’s word directed.

## Hezekiah prays for the guests

## 2 Chronicles 30:13

In the second month a great crowd gathered in Jerusalem for the Feast of Unleavened Bread.

This bread is made without yeast to make it rise.

## 2 Chronicles 30:14

The people removed altars from Jerusalem, including the altars where people burned incense.

They threw them into the Kidron Valley.

## 2 Chronicles 30:15

On day fourteen of the second month, they killed the Passover lambs.

The priests and Levites felt ashamed.

They made themselves ready for holy service and brought burnt offerings to the LORD’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 30:16

They took their places as the law of Moses, God’s servant, required.

The Levites handed the blood to the priests.

The priests sprinkled it.

## 2 Chronicles 30:17

Many people had yet to complete the steps that made them clean for worship.

The Levites killed their Passover lambs for them and set the lambs apart for the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 30:18

Many from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun ate the Passover meal without first completing those steps.

This went against the written law.

Hezekiah prayed for them.

“LORD, you are good.

Please forgive everyone

who truly wants to seek you, the God of their ancestors, even if they have not followed all the temple rules for becoming clean.”

## 2 Chronicles 30:20

The LORD heard Hezekiah and healed the people.

## The people stay for another week

## 2 Chronicles 30:21

For seven days the Israelites in Jerusalem joyfully celebrated the Feast of Unleavened Bread.

Each day the Levites and priests praised the LORD.

Their instruments rang out loudly.

## 2 Chronicles 30:22

Hezekiah encouraged the Levites who served the LORD skillfully.

For seven days the people ate the shares set aside for them.

They brought peace offerings and thanked the LORD, the God of their ancestors.

## 2 Chronicles 30:23

Everyone agreed to celebrate for seven more days.

They gladly kept the feast for another week.

## 2 Chronicles 30:24

Hezekiah gave the people 1,000 bulls and 7,000 sheep.

The officials gave another 1,000 bulls and 10,000 sheep.

Many priests made themselves ready for holy service.

## 2 Chronicles 30:25

Everyone from Judah rejoiced alongside the priests, Levites, and visitors from Israel.

Foreigners who had traveled from Israel and foreigners living in Judah shared their joy.

## 2 Chronicles 30:26

Jerusalem had seen nothing like this since Solomon, David’s son, ruled Israel.

The city was full of joy.

## 2 Chronicles 30:27

The priests and Levites stood and asked God to bless the people.

Their prayer reached his holy home in heaven, and God heard them.
