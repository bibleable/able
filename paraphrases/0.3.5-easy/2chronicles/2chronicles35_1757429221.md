# 2 Chronicles 35 — Josiah gathers the people for Passover

**About this chapter**

At Passover, Israel remembers how the LORD brought his people out of Egypt. King Josiah helps the people celebrate together. Later, the people of Judah mourn his death.

## Josiah gets the temple workers ready

## 2 Chronicles 35:1

Josiah celebrated Passover to honor the LORD in Jerusalem.

On day fourteen of the first month, the people killed the Passover lambs.

## 2 Chronicles 35:2

Josiah gave the priests their duties.

He encouraged them as they served in the LORD’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 35:3

The Levites taught the people of Israel.

They were set apart for the LORD.

Josiah told them, “Put the ark, the sacred chest, in the temple that King David’s son Solomon built.

You no longer need to carry it on your shoulders.

Now serve the LORD your God and his people Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 35:4

Get ready for your work in your family groups.

Follow the instructions King David and his son Solomon wrote down.

## 2 Chronicles 35:5

Take your places in the Holy Place.

Each group of Levite families has families from among the people to serve.

## 2 Chronicles 35:6

Kill the Passover lambs.

Make yourselves ready for holy service.

Prepare for your fellow Israelites so they can do what the LORD told Moses.”

## The king and the leaders give animals

## 2 Chronicles 35:7

Josiah gave animals from his own flocks and herds for everyone present.

He gave 30,000 lambs and goats and 3,000 bulls.

## 2 Chronicles 35:8

His officials gladly gave to the people, priests, and Levites too.

Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel led the work in God’s house.

They gave the priests 2,600 Passover animals and 300 bulls.

## 2 Chronicles 35:9

Conaniah and his brothers Shemaiah and Nethanel were leaders among the Levites.

So were Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad.

Together they gave the Levites 5,000 Passover animals and 500 bulls.

## The Levites make sure everyone can eat

## 2 Chronicles 35:10

Everything was ready.

The priests took their places.

The Levites stood in their groups as Josiah had ordered.

## 2 Chronicles 35:11

The Levites killed the Passover lambs.

The priests sprinkled the blood the Levites handed them.

The Levites removed the animals’ skins.

## 2 Chronicles 35:12

They set aside the burnt offerings for each group of families.

The families could then offer them to the LORD as the Book of Moses directed.

They did the same with the bulls.

## 2 Chronicles 35:13

They roasted the Passover animals over the fire as the law required.

They boiled the other holy offerings in pots, kettles, and bowls.

Then they hurried to bring the food to everyone.

## 2 Chronicles 35:14

After that, the Levites prepared food for themselves and for the priests.

The priests, who came from Aaron’s family, stayed busy until dark.

They were burning offerings and fat on the altar.

## 2 Chronicles 35:15

The singers from Asaph’s family stayed in their places.

They followed the instructions of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun.

Jeduthun was the king’s seer, someone who received messages from God.

The gatekeepers could stay at their gates because other Levites prepared food for them.

## A Passover the people remembered

## 2 Chronicles 35:16

That day they completed the whole service as Josiah had ordered.

They kept the Passover and burned offerings on the LORD’s altar.

## 2 Chronicles 35:17

The Israelites there celebrated Passover.

For seven days they also kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread.

This bread is made without yeast to make it rise.

## 2 Chronicles 35:18

Israel had seen no Passover like this since the days of the prophet Samuel.

No king of Israel had celebrated it quite like Josiah.

The priests, Levites, people of Judah and Jerusalem, and other Israelites there all took part.

## 2 Chronicles 35:19

This Passover took place in Josiah’s eighteenth year as king.

## Josiah goes to meet the king of Egypt

## 2 Chronicles 35:20

After Josiah put the temple in order, King Neco of Egypt marched toward Carchemish.

He was going to fight by the Euphrates River.

Josiah went out to meet him.

## 2 Chronicles 35:21

Neco sent messengers to Josiah.

“King of Judah, what quarrel is there between us?

I came to fight another royal family.

God told me to hurry.

God is with me.

Stop opposing him, or he will destroy you.”

## 2 Chronicles 35:22

Josiah refused to turn back.

God had spoken through Neco, but Josiah did not listen.

Josiah disguised himself and went to fight on the plain of Megiddo.

## 2 Chronicles 35:23

Archers shot Josiah with arrows.

He told his servants, “Take me away!

I am badly hurt.”

## 2 Chronicles 35:24

They moved him from his chariot to his second chariot.

They brought him to Jerusalem, where he died.

They buried him in his ancestors’ tomb.

All Judah and Jerusalem mourned for him.

## The people sing songs of grief

## 2 Chronicles 35:25

Jeremiah sang a lament, a song of grief for Josiah.

Men and women still sang laments for Josiah when this account was written.

Israel made the songs part of its custom.

The Book of Laments keeps a record of them.

## 2 Chronicles 35:26

Josiah showed faithful love by doing what the LORD’s law said.

## 2 Chronicles 35:27

The Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah tells about all Josiah did, from beginning to end.
