# 2 Chronicles 6 — Solomon asks God to hear everyone who prays

**About this chapter**

Solomon stands before Israel at the new temple. He thanks the LORD for keeping his promise to David. Then he kneels and asks God to hear people who turn toward the temple to pray.

## Solomon blesses the people

## 2 Chronicles 6:1

Solomon said, “The LORD said he would live in the thick cloud.

## 2 Chronicles 6:2

LORD, I have built you a splendid house.

May it be your home forever.”

## 2 Chronicles 6:3

All the people of Israel stood there.

The king turned toward them and blessed them.

## 2 Chronicles 6:4

Solomon said, “Praise the LORD, the God of Israel!

He has done what he promised my father David.

He said,

## 2 Chronicles 6:5

‘I brought my people out of Egypt.

From then until now, I had chosen no city in Israel for a house to bear my name.

I had chosen no one to rule my people Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 6:6

Now I have chosen Jerusalem as the city that will bear my name.

I have chosen David to lead my people Israel.’

## 2 Chronicles 6:7

My father David wanted to build a house to honor the name of the LORD, Israel’s God.

## 2 Chronicles 6:8

The LORD told David, ‘It was good of you to want to build me a house.

## 2 Chronicles 6:9

Your own son will do the building.

He will build the house that bears my name.’

## 2 Chronicles 6:10

The LORD has kept that promise.

I became king of Israel after my father David.

I have built this house for the LORD, Israel’s God.

## 2 Chronicles 6:11

I put the sacred chest, the ark, inside it.

The ark holds the record of the covenant, the solemn promise the LORD made with Israel.”

![The temple had a main hall and an inner room. Solomon placed the ark inside the temple.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

## Solomon kneels to pray

## 2 Chronicles 6:12

Solomon stood before the LORD’s altar.

He stretched out his hands where all Israel could see him.

## 2 Chronicles 6:13

In the middle of the courtyard he had placed a bronze platform.

It was five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high.

A cubit was about the length of a forearm.

Solomon stood on the platform.

Then he knelt and raised his open hands toward heaven.

## 2 Chronicles 6:14

He prayed, “LORD, God of Israel, no god in heaven or on earth is like you.

You keep your promise of love to your servants who follow you with their whole hearts.

## 2 Chronicles 6:15

You kept your word to my father David.

Today we see what you have done.

## 2 Chronicles 6:16

LORD, God of Israel, keep the promise you made to David about his family.

You said, ‘If your descendants obey my law as you have done, someone from your family will always sit on Israel’s throne.’

## 2 Chronicles 6:17

God of Israel, please make that promise to David come true.

## 2 Chronicles 6:18

Can God really live here on earth with people?

Even the highest heavens cannot hold you.

How much less could this house I built hold you!

## 2 Chronicles 6:19

Yet, LORD my God, listen to me, your servant.

Hear my cry for help as I pray before you.

## 2 Chronicles 6:20

Watch over this temple day and night.

You said your name would be here.

Listen when I pray toward this place.

## 2 Chronicles 6:21

Hear me and your people Israel when we turn this way to pray.

Hear us from your home in heaven.

Hear us and forgive us.

## When people need justice, forgiveness, or rain

## 2 Chronicles 6:22

Suppose someone wrongs a neighbor.

That person must come before your altar here and swear to tell the truth.

## 2 Chronicles 6:23

Hear from heaven and act as judge.

Hold the guilty person responsible for the harm they did.

Clear the innocent person and reward them for doing right.

## 2 Chronicles 6:24

Suppose an enemy defeats Israel because your people have sinned against you.

Then they come back to you.

They honor your name and ask for help in this temple.

## 2 Chronicles 6:25

Hear from heaven and forgive your people Israel.

Bring them back to the land you gave them and their ancestors.

## 2 Chronicles 6:26

Suppose rain stops falling because your people have sinned.

In their trouble they turn from sin, honor your name, and pray toward this house.

## 2 Chronicles 6:27

Hear from heaven and forgive them.

Teach your people the good way to live.

Send rain on the land you gave them to keep.

## 2 Chronicles 6:28

Food may run out, or disease may spread.

Mold and sickness may ruin crops, or locusts and grasshoppers may eat them.

Enemies may surround the towns.

Any kind of sickness or disaster may come.

## 2 Chronicles 6:29

Each person in Israel knows their own pain.

They may stretch out their hands toward this house and ask for help.

## 2 Chronicles 6:30

Hear them from your home in heaven.

Forgive them and deal with each person according to what they have done.

You alone know what is in every heart.

## 2 Chronicles 6:31

Then they can honor you and follow your ways all their lives in the land you gave our ancestors.

## When people pray far from home

## 2 Chronicles 6:32

Someone from a distant country may hear of your great name and your mighty deeds.

That person may come and pray toward this temple.

## 2 Chronicles 6:33

Hear that visitor from heaven and grant what they ask.

Then people everywhere will know and honor you as Israel does.

They will know that this house bears your name.

## 2 Chronicles 6:34

You may send your people away to fight an enemy.

From there they may pray toward this city you chose and the house I built to honor you.

## 2 Chronicles 6:35

Hear their cries for help from heaven.

Stand up for them.

## 2 Chronicles 6:36

Everyone sins.

If your people sin, you may become angry and let an enemy take them prisoner.

They may be taken to a land near or far.

## 2 Chronicles 6:37

In that land they may realize what they have done.

They may turn back and plead with you, saying, ‘We have sinned.

We have done wrong and acted wickedly.’

## 2 Chronicles 6:38

Even among their enemies, they may turn to you with their whole hearts.

They may face the land you gave their ancestors, your chosen city, and this temple as they pray.

## 2 Chronicles 6:39

Hear their prayer from your home in heaven.

Stand up for them.

Forgive your people for sinning against you.

## Solomon finishes his prayer

## 2 Chronicles 6:40

My God, watch over this place.

Listen closely to the prayers people offer here.

## 2 Chronicles 6:41

Rise, LORD God, and come to your resting place with the ark that shows your power.

Let your priests wear your saving help like clothing.

Let those who love you rejoice in your goodness.

## 2 Chronicles 6:42

LORD God, remember the king you chose.

Please welcome him.

Remember the faithful love you promised to your servant David.”
