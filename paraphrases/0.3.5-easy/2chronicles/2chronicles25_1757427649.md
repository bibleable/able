# 2 Chronicles 25 — Amaziah listens to one warning and refuses another

**About this chapter**

Amaziah rules Judah. When a man of God warns him about the soldiers he has hired, Amaziah listens. Later he turns to other gods and refuses to listen.

## Amaziah follows the law of Moses

## 2 Chronicles 25:1

Amaziah was 25 when he became king of Judah.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 29 years.

His mother, Jehoaddan, came from Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 25:2

Amaziah did what the LORD saw was right.

Yet he held back from serving God with his whole heart.

## 2 Chronicles 25:3

Once Amaziah was firmly in charge, he punished his father’s killers.

He put to death the servants who had murdered King Joash.

## 2 Chronicles 25:4

Amaziah let their sons live.

He followed the law the LORD had given through Moses.

A father must never die for his child’s sin.

A child must never die for a father’s sin.

Each person answers for the wrong they themselves do.

## Amaziah sends the hired soldiers home

## 2 Chronicles 25:5

Amaziah gathered men from Judah and Benjamin by family.

He chose officers to lead groups of a thousand and a hundred.

He counted 300,000 men aged twenty or older ready to serve.

They could carry spears and shields.

## 2 Chronicles 25:6

Amaziah also hired 100,000 strong soldiers from Israel, the northern kingdom.

He paid a hundred talents of silver.

A talent was a large measure of weight.

## 2 Chronicles 25:7

A man of God told him, “King Amaziah, send Israel’s army away.

The LORD is not with those troops from Ephraim in Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 25:8

If you fight with them, even great courage will not save you.

God will let your enemy defeat you.

God has power to help you win or to bring you down.”

## 2 Chronicles 25:9

Amaziah asked, “What about the silver I already paid?”

The man answered, “The LORD can give you much more than that.”

## 2 Chronicles 25:10

So Amaziah sent the troops from Ephraim home.

They went back furious with Judah.

## Victory over Edom and a warning

## 2 Chronicles 25:11

Amaziah took courage and led his soldiers to the Valley of Salt.

There his army killed 10,000 men from Seir in Edom.

## 2 Chronicles 25:12

Judah’s army took another 10,000 men prisoner.

The soldiers threw the prisoners from a cliff.

All the prisoners died.

## 2 Chronicles 25:13

Meanwhile the soldiers Amaziah had sent away attacked towns in Judah.

Their raids stretched from Samaria to Beth-horon.

They killed 3,000 people and took many belongings.

## 2 Chronicles 25:14

After defeating Edom, Amaziah brought home the gods the people of Seir worshiped.

He set them up and bowed down to them.

He burned offerings to them as his own gods.

## 2 Chronicles 25:15

The LORD was angry and sent a prophet to Amaziah.

A prophet speaks God’s message.

The prophet asked, “Those gods could not save their people from you.

Why are you asking them for help?”

## 2 Chronicles 25:16

Amaziah interrupted. “Who made you my adviser?

Stop speaking if you want to live!”

The prophet gave one last warning.

“God has decided to destroy you.

You worshiped those gods and refused to listen to me.”

## Israel’s king tells a story

## 2 Chronicles 25:17

After speaking with his advisers, Amaziah sent Israel’s king a challenge.

That king was Jehoash, son of Jehoahaz and grandson of Jehu.

Amaziah said, “Come and face me in battle.”

## 2 Chronicles 25:18

Jehoash answered with a story.

“A thistle in Lebanon sent word to a cedar tree.

‘Let your daughter marry my son,’ said the thistle.

Then a wild animal walked by and crushed the thistle.”

![A small prickly thistle beside a tall cedar tree.](../pictures/thistle-and-cedar.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 25:19

Jehoash was warning Amaziah about his pride.

He said, “You boast because you defeated Edom.

Stay home! Why bring trouble on yourself and all Judah?”

## 2 Chronicles 25:20

Amaziah refused to listen.

God let this happen because Judah had turned to Edom’s gods.

He would let Jehoash defeat them.

## 2 Chronicles 25:21

Jehoash led his army into Judah.

The two kings met in battle at Beth-shemesh.

## 2 Chronicles 25:22

Israel defeated Judah. Judah’s soldiers fled to their homes.

## Jerusalem loses part of its wall

## 2 Chronicles 25:23

At Beth-shemesh, Jehoash captured Amaziah, son of Joash and grandson of Jehoahaz.

Jehoash took Amaziah to Jerusalem.

There Jehoash tore down part of the city wall.

From the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate, he destroyed 400 cubits of wall.

That was about 180 meters.

## 2 Chronicles 25:24

Jehoash took the gold, silver, and objects kept with Obed-edom in God’s house.

He took the treasures from the king’s palace too.

He also took people away as hostages, holding them as prisoners.

Then he went back to Samaria.

## 2 Chronicles 25:25

Amaziah, king of Judah, lived another fifteen years after Jehoash of Israel died.

## 2 Chronicles 25:26

The Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel tells more about Amaziah.

It records his deeds from beginning to end.

## 2 Chronicles 25:27

After Amaziah turned away from the LORD, people in Jerusalem plotted against him.

Amaziah fled to Lachish.

Men went after him and killed him there.

## 2 Chronicles 25:28

They brought his body back on horses.

They buried him beside his ancestors in the City of Judah.
