# 2 Chronicles 31 — The people bring enough to share

**About this chapter**

King Hezekiah arranges for people to bring gifts to the LORD’s house. The gifts feed the people who serve there and their families.

## The people return home after Passover

## 2 Chronicles 31:1

After the feast, the Israelites went into Judah’s cities.

They smashed the stone pillars people used to worship other gods.

They cut down poles used to worship the goddess Asherah.

They tore down the hilltop places of worship and their altars.

They did this throughout Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh.

When they had finished, everyone went home.

## 2 Chronicles 31:2

Hezekiah put the priests and Levites back in their work groups.

The Levites were people from Levi’s tribe who served God.

Each group had its own work.

Some brought offerings to burn or offerings to celebrate peace with God.

Others served, gave thanks, or sang praise at the temple gates.

## 2 Chronicles 31:3

Hezekiah gave from his own property for the burnt offerings.

Some offerings were for each morning and evening.

Others were for weekly rest days, new moons, and feasts.

These offerings followed the LORD’s law.

## 2 Chronicles 31:4

Hezekiah told the people of Jerusalem to support the priests and Levites.

Then the priests and Levites could give their time to the LORD’s law.

## The gifts grow into heaps

## 2 Chronicles 31:5

The people heard the order and gave generously.

They brought the first grain, new wine, oil, honey, and other crops.

They also brought a tithe of everything.

A tithe is one part out of ten equal parts.

![Ten equal baskets. A ring around one basket shows one part out of ten.](../pictures/one-basket-in-ten.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 31:6

People from Israel and Judah living in Judah’s towns brought gifts too.

They gave a tenth from their herds and flocks.

They also gave a tenth of the things set apart for God.

They placed the gifts in large heaps.

## 2 Chronicles 31:7

They started piling up the gifts in the third month.

They finished in the seventh month.

## 2 Chronicles 31:8

Hezekiah and his officials came to see the heaps.

They praised the LORD and blessed his people Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 31:9

Hezekiah asked the priests and Levites about the heaps.

## 2 Chronicles 31:10

Azariah, the chief priest, came from Zadok’s family.

He said, “People began bringing gifts to the LORD’s house.

Since then we have had enough to eat.

The LORD has blessed his people.

Look how much we still have left!”

## People take care of the gifts

## 2 Chronicles 31:11

Hezekiah told the people to prepare rooms in the LORD’s house.

They got the rooms ready to store the gifts.

## 2 Chronicles 31:12

The people faithfully brought in the gifts.

These included the tithes and things set apart for the LORD.

Conaniah, a Levite, took charge of them.

His brother Shimei helped him as second in charge.

## 2 Chronicles 31:13

King Hezekiah and Azariah, the temple leader, chose ten men to help.

They worked under Conaniah and Shimei.

They were Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, and Jerimoth.

The others were Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah.

## 2 Chronicles 31:14

Kore, son of Imnah, was a Levite who guarded the East Gate.

He took charge of gifts people freely chose to give God.

He handed out the offerings and the gifts set apart for God.

## 2 Chronicles 31:15

Six men helped Kore in the towns where the priests lived.

They were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah.

They faithfully gave the priests their shares, group by group.

Both young and old received a share.

## The lists show who receives a share

## 2 Chronicles 31:16

The family lists included boys and men aged three and older.

Those on the lists received shares.

So did those who came to the LORD’s house for daily duties.

Each group had its own work to do.

## 2 Chronicles 31:17

The lists named the priests by family.

They also named the Levites aged twenty and older.

They listed those Levites by their jobs and groups.

## 2 Chronicles 31:18

The family lists included little children, wives, sons, and daughters.

The whole community had faithfully set themselves apart for God.

## 2 Chronicles 31:19

Some priests from Aaron’s family lived on farmland around their towns.

Others lived in the towns themselves.

Men chosen by name gave out shares in each place.

Every male in the priests’ families received a share.

So did every Levite on the family lists.

## 2 Chronicles 31:20

Hezekiah did these things throughout Judah.

The LORD saw that he acted with goodness, fairness, and honesty.

## 2 Chronicles 31:21

Hezekiah sought God with his whole heart.

He did this in his work for God’s house.

He did this as he followed God’s laws and commands.

And his work went well.
