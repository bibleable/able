# 2 Chronicles 26 — Uzziah becomes proud and ignores the priests

**About this chapter**

Uzziah becomes king when he is sixteen. He listens to God, and his kingdom grows strong. Later, Uzziah tries to do work God has given to the priests.

## A young king seeks God

## 2 Chronicles 26:1

The people of Judah chose sixteen-year-old Uzziah as king.

He took the place of his father, Amaziah.

## 2 Chronicles 26:2

After Amaziah died, Uzziah rebuilt the city of Eloth.

The city belonged to Judah again.

## 2 Chronicles 26:3

Uzziah was sixteen when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for fifty-two years.

His mother, Jecoliah, came from Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 26:4

Like his father Amaziah, Uzziah did what the LORD saw was right.

## 2 Chronicles 26:5

Zechariah taught Uzziah to honor God with deep respect.

While Zechariah lived, Uzziah kept turning to God for guidance.

As long as Uzziah sought the LORD, God helped him succeed.

## Uzziah builds towns and cares for the land

## 2 Chronicles 26:6

Uzziah fought the Philistines.

He tore down the walls of Gath, Jabneh, and Ashdod.

He built towns near Ashdod and elsewhere in Philistine land.

## 2 Chronicles 26:7

God helped Uzziah against the Philistines.

God also helped him against the Arabs who lived in Gur-baal and against the Meunites.

## 2 Chronicles 26:8

The Ammonites brought Uzziah payments to show they accepted his power.

News of his great power reached the border of Egypt.

## 2 Chronicles 26:9

Uzziah built strong towers in Jerusalem.

He built them at the Corner Gate and the Valley Gate.

He built another where the city wall turned a corner.

## 2 Chronicles 26:10

Uzziah owned many animals in the foothills and on the plain.

He built towers in the desert and dug pits to store water.

Uzziah loved farming.

Workers tended his fields and grapevines on the hills and in rich soil.

## Uzziah’s army grows strong

## 2 Chronicles 26:11

Uzziah’s army went out to battle in groups.

Jeiel kept records. Maaseiah, an officer, helped list the groups.

Hananiah, one of the king’s officers, directed their work.

## 2 Chronicles 26:12

The army had 2,600 family leaders in charge of its brave men.

## 2 Chronicles 26:13

These leaders commanded 307,500 men trained for war.

This strong army helped the king face his enemies.

## 2 Chronicles 26:14

Uzziah supplied the whole army with shields, helmets, and armor.

He also gave them spears, bows, and stones for slings.

## 2 Chronicles 26:15

In Jerusalem, Uzziah had skilled workers make machines for the city defenses.

The machines sent arrows and large stones from the towers and corners.

God had helped Uzziah greatly, and Uzziah became powerful.

People far away heard about him.

## The priests speak up

## 2 Chronicles 26:16

Once Uzziah was powerful, he became proud.

That pride brought him to ruin.

He was unfaithful to the LORD his God.

He entered the LORD’s temple to burn incense on its special altar.

Incense gives off sweet-smelling smoke when it burns.

## 2 Chronicles 26:17

Azariah the priest went in after the king.

Eighty brave priests of the LORD went with Azariah.

## 2 Chronicles 26:18

The priests stood up to Uzziah.

They said, “Only priests from Aaron’s family may burn incense to the LORD.

God set them apart for this work.

Leave this holy place. You have been unfaithful.

The LORD God will give you no honor for this.”

## 2 Chronicles 26:19

Uzziah held a censer, a container for burning incense.

He stood by the incense altar, furious with the priests.

As they watched, a skin disease called leprosy appeared on his forehead.

## 2 Chronicles 26:20

Azariah the chief priest and the other priests saw Uzziah’s forehead.

They quickly led him out.

Uzziah himself hurried to leave because the LORD had struck him with the disease.

## Jotham takes responsibility

## 2 Chronicles 26:21

Uzziah had the skin disease for the rest of his life.

He lived apart from others and could no longer enter the LORD’s house.

His son Jotham managed the palace and governed the people.

## 2 Chronicles 26:22

Isaiah the prophet, son of Amoz, recorded the rest of Uzziah’s deeds.

He wrote about Uzziah’s rule from beginning to end.

## 2 Chronicles 26:23

Uzziah died. The people buried him near his ancestors.

His grave was in a burial field belonging to the kings.

They chose this place because Uzziah had the skin disease.

His son Jotham became king.
