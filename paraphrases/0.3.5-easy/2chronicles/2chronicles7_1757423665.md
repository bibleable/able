# 2 Chronicles 7 — The LORD hears Solomon’s prayer

**About this chapter**

Israel gathers at the new temple in Jerusalem. The people see fire come down from heaven. Later, the LORD speaks to Solomon about the people who will pray there.

## The people bow down

## 2 Chronicles 7:1

Solomon finished praying. Fire came down from heaven.

It burned up the offerings and sacrifices on the altar.

The LORD filled the temple with his glory.

His glory showed the people how great he is.

## 2 Chronicles 7:2

The priests could not enter the LORD’s house.

His glory filled it.

## 2 Chronicles 7:3

The Israelites saw the fire come down.

They saw the LORD’s glory above the temple.

They bowed until their faces touched the stone pavement.

They worshiped the LORD and thanked him.

“He is good,” they said. “His faithful love lasts forever.”

## The people set the temple apart for God

## 2 Chronicles 7:4

The king and all the people brought sacrifices.

These were gifts they offered to the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 7:5

Solomon offered 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep.

In this way the king and people dedicated the temple.

They set it apart as God’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 7:6

The priests and Levites stood in their places.

The Levites played instruments King David had made for thanking the LORD.

David had praised God with the words, “His faithful love lasts forever.”

Across from the Levites, the priests blew trumpets.

All the Israelites stood.

## 2 Chronicles 7:7

The bronze altar could not hold all the gifts.

So Solomon set apart the middle of the courtyard for worship.

This was the open space in front of the temple.

There he burned whole offerings and the fat from peace offerings.

## 2 Chronicles 7:8

People came from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt.

A great crowd gathered with Solomon.

Together they celebrated the feast for seven days.

## 2 Chronicles 7:9

First they spent seven days dedicating the altar.

Then came the seven days of the feast.

On the eighth day of the feast they gathered for worship.

## 2 Chronicles 7:10

On day twenty-three of the seventh month, Solomon sent everyone home.

They went home happy.

They remembered the good things the LORD had done for David, Solomon, and Israel.

## The LORD answers Solomon

## 2 Chronicles 7:11

Solomon finished the LORD’s house and his own palace.

He had done everything he planned for both buildings.

![A plan shows the main hall and the inner room of Solomon’s temple.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 7:12

One night the LORD appeared to Solomon.

He said, “I have heard your prayer.

I choose this house for people to offer sacrifices to me.

## 2 Chronicles 7:13

I may keep the rain from falling.

I may send locusts, insects that eat the crops.

Or I may send a terrible sickness among my people.

## 2 Chronicles 7:14

My people bear my name.

If they humble themselves, pray, seek me, and turn from doing evil, I will hear them from heaven.

I will forgive their sins and heal their land.

## 2 Chronicles 7:15

I will watch over this place.

I will listen when people pray here.

## 2 Chronicles 7:16

I have chosen this temple and made it holy.

It will bear my name forever.

I will always watch over it and care for it.

## A promise and a warning

## 2 Chronicles 7:17

Solomon, follow me as your father David did.

Do everything I tell you. Keep my laws.

## 2 Chronicles 7:18

If you do this, I will keep your kingdom secure.

I made your father David a solemn promise.

Someone from his family would always rule over Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 7:19

But you and your people might turn away.

You might leave my commands and worship and serve other gods.

## 2 Chronicles 7:20

If you do, I will take Israel out of the land I gave them.

I will send away from my presence even this temple I set apart for myself.

People from every nation will laugh at it and insult it.

## 2 Chronicles 7:21

The temple will become a pile of broken stones.

People passing by will look at it in horror.

They will ask, ‘Why did the LORD do this to the land and the temple?’

## 2 Chronicles 7:22

Others will answer, ‘The LORD brought their ancestors out of Egypt.

But the people left him.

They chose other gods and worshiped and served them.

That is why the LORD brought this disaster on them.’”
