# 2 Chronicles 16 — Asa asks another king for help

**About this chapter**

An enemy king tries to close the way into Judah.

King Asa sends silver and gold to the king of Aram.

Later Hanani tells Asa why his decision was foolish.

## Asa makes an agreement with Aram

## 2 Chronicles 16:1

Asa had ruled Judah for thirty-six years when Baasha attacked.

Baasha was the king of Israel.

He began building defenses at Ramah.

He wanted to stop people from entering or leaving Asa’s land.

## 2 Chronicles 16:2

Asa took silver and gold from the LORD’s temple.

He also took silver and gold from his palace.

He sent it to Ben-hadad, king of Aram.

Ben-hadad ruled in Damascus.

Asa sent him this message.

## 2 Chronicles 16:3

“Let us make an agreement as our fathers did.

I have sent you silver and gold.

End your agreement with Baasha, king of Israel.

Then he will pull back from my land.”

## 2 Chronicles 16:4

Ben-hadad agreed to help Asa.

He sent his army commanders to attack Israel’s cities.

They captured Ijon, Dan, and Abel-maim.

They also took every city where Naphtali kept its supplies.

## 2 Chronicles 16:5

Baasha heard what had happened.

He stopped building defenses at Ramah and left the work unfinished.

## 2 Chronicles 16:6

King Asa brought all the men of Judah to Ramah.

They carried away the stones and wooden beams Baasha had used.

Asa used them to strengthen two towns, Geba and Mizpah.

## Hanani reminds Asa who helped him before

## 2 Chronicles 16:7

Hanani came to King Asa of Judah.

Hanani was a seer, someone God gave messages to.

He told Asa, “You depended on the king of Aram.

You did not depend on the LORD your God.

So the army of Aram has slipped away from you.

## 2 Chronicles 16:8

“Remember the huge army from Cush and Libya?

They had many war chariots and soldiers on horses.

Yet you depended on the LORD.

He gave you victory over them.

## 2 Chronicles 16:9

“The LORD looks over the whole earth.

He sees those who trust him with their whole heart.

He uses his strength to help them.

Asa, you have made a foolish choice.

From now on you will face war.”

## 2 Chronicles 16:10

Asa was furious with Hanani for saying this.

He put Hanani in prison.

At that time Asa also treated some of his people cruelly.

## Asa’s last years

## 2 Chronicles 16:11

The Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel tells Asa’s story.

It records what he did from beginning to end.

## 2 Chronicles 16:12

In Asa’s thirty-ninth year as king, his feet became diseased.

The disease grew worse.

Even then Asa asked only the doctors for help.

He did not turn to the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 16:13

Asa died in his forty-first year as king.

Like his ancestors before him, he rested in death.

## 2 Chronicles 16:14

Asa had prepared a tomb for himself in the City of David.

His people laid him on a platform for carrying the dead.

They filled it with spices and sweet-smelling perfumes.

They buried him in his tomb.

They lit a great fire to honor him.
