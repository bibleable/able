# 2 Chronicles 20 — Jehoshaphat asks God for help

**About this chapter**

A large army is coming toward Judah. King Jehoshaphat gathers the people to pray. Families stand together to hear how the LORD answers.

## Judah gathers to pray

## 2 Chronicles 20:1

People from Moab and Ammon joined some of the Meunites.

They came to fight King Jehoshaphat.

## 2 Chronicles 20:2

Men brought the king news.

“A huge army is coming from Edom, across the sea.

They have already reached Hazazon-tamar.” That place is also called En-gedi.

## 2 Chronicles 20:3

Jehoshaphat was afraid.

He turned to the LORD for help.

He called for a fast throughout Judah.

During a fast, people go without food for a time.

## 2 Chronicles 20:4

People from every town in Judah came together to ask the LORD for help.

## 2 Chronicles 20:5

Jehoshaphat stood before the new courtyard at the LORD’s house.

People from Jerusalem and the rest of Judah stood with him.

## 2 Chronicles 20:6

He prayed, “LORD, God of our ancestors, you are God in heaven.

You rule over every kingdom.

You are so powerful that no one can stand against you.

## 2 Chronicles 20:7

Our God, you drove out the people of this land before Israel.

You gave the land forever to the family of your friend Abraham.

## 2 Chronicles 20:8

They settled here and built this holy house to honor your name.

They said,

## 2 Chronicles 20:9

‘War or judgment may come.

Disease may spread, or food may run out.

Then we will stand before you at this temple that bears your name.

We will cry to you in our trouble.

You will hear us and save us.’

## 2 Chronicles 20:10

Now look at the people of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir.

When Israel came from Egypt, you kept us from attacking them.

Our people turned aside and let them live.

## 2 Chronicles 20:11

Look how they repay us!

They want to drive us out of the land you gave us.

## 2 Chronicles 20:12

Our God, will you judge them?

We have no power against this huge army.

We do not know what to do.

We are looking to you for help.”

## 2 Chronicles 20:13

All the families of Judah stood before the LORD.

The men were there with their wives and children, even the little ones.

## Jahaziel brings God’s answer

## 2 Chronicles 20:14

The LORD’s Spirit came upon Jahaziel as he stood among the people.

Jahaziel was a Levite from Asaph’s family.

His father was Zechariah and his grandfather was Benaiah.

Benaiah’s father was Jeiel, son of Mattaniah.

## 2 Chronicles 20:15

Jahaziel said, “Listen, people of Judah and Jerusalem!

Listen, King Jehoshaphat!

The LORD says, ‘Take heart when you see this great army.

This battle belongs to God.

## 2 Chronicles 20:16

Tomorrow go down to meet them.

They will be coming up the slope called Ziz.

You will find them at the end of the valley facing the wilderness of Jeruel.

## 2 Chronicles 20:17

You will have no need to fight.

Stand firm and see how the LORD saves you.

People of Judah and Jerusalem, take courage!

Go out tomorrow.

The LORD is with you.’”

## 2 Chronicles 20:18

Jehoshaphat bowed with his face to the ground.

Everyone from Judah and Jerusalem bowed to worship the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 20:19

Levites from the families of Kohath and Korah stood up.

In loud voices they praised the LORD, the God of Israel.

## Singers go ahead of the army

## 2 Chronicles 20:20

Early the next morning the people set out for the wilderness of Tekoa.

Jehoshaphat told them, “Listen, Judah and Jerusalem!

Trust the LORD your God.

He will hold you up.

Trust his prophets, and you will succeed.”

## 2 Chronicles 20:21

After speaking with the people, Jehoshaphat chose singers to go ahead of the army.

They praised the beauty of the LORD’s holiness.

They sang, “Thank the LORD!

His faithful love lasts forever.”

## 2 Chronicles 20:22

As they began to sing and praise him, the LORD took the invaders by surprise.

The armies of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir fell in defeat.

## 2 Chronicles 20:23

The men of Ammon and Moab attacked and wiped out the men from Mount Seir.

Then they turned on each other and destroyed one another.

## 2 Chronicles 20:24

The men of Judah reached a lookout over the wilderness.

They looked for the great army and saw dead bodies on the ground.

No one had escaped.

## 2 Chronicles 20:25

Jehoshaphat and his people found goods and valuables on the bodies.

There was more than they could carry.

It took three days to collect it all.

## The people return with joy

## 2 Chronicles 20:26

On the fourth day the people met in the valley to bless the LORD.

They named it the Valley of Beracah, meaning the Valley of Blessing.

It still had that name when this account was written.

## 2 Chronicles 20:27

Jehoshaphat led the men of Judah and Jerusalem home.

They were joyful because of what the LORD had done about their enemies.

## 2 Chronicles 20:28

They entered Jerusalem and went to the LORD’s house.

They played harps, lyres, and trumpets.

![A harp with strings, like one of the instruments the people played on their return.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 20:29

The surrounding kingdoms heard that the LORD had fought Israel’s enemies.

They feared God.

## 2 Chronicles 20:30

Jehoshaphat’s kingdom had peace.

God gave him rest on every side.

## Jehoshaphat’s rule and his agreement with Ahaziah

## 2 Chronicles 20:31

Jehoshaphat became king of Judah at thirty-five.

He ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-five years.

His mother was Azubah, daughter of Shilhi.

## 2 Chronicles 20:32

He followed the example of his father Asa.

He kept doing what the LORD saw was right.

## 2 Chronicles 20:33

Yet the hilltop places of worship remained.

The people still held back from giving their hearts to the God of their ancestors.

## 2 Chronicles 20:34

Jehu son of Hanani recorded the rest of Jehoshaphat’s deeds, from first to last.

His account is in the Book of the Kings of Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 20:35

Later Jehoshaphat made an agreement with Ahaziah, king of Israel.

Ahaziah did evil.

## 2 Chronicles 20:36

The kings agreed to build ships to sail to Tarshish.

Their workers built the ships at Ezion-geber.

## 2 Chronicles 20:37

Eliezer son of Dodavahu came from Mareshah.

He brought Jehoshaphat this warning.

“You joined with Ahaziah, so the LORD has destroyed your work.” The ships broke apart and could never sail to Tarshish.
