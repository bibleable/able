# 2 Chronicles 21 — Elijah sends King Jehoram a letter

**About this chapter**

Jehoram becomes king of Judah and kills his brothers. Elijah sends him a warning from the LORD.

## Jehoram takes his father’s place

## 2 Chronicles 21:1

King Jehoshaphat died.

His people buried him beside his ancestors in the City of David.

His son Jehoram became king.

## 2 Chronicles 21:2

King Jehoshaphat had six other sons.

Their names were Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah.

## 2 Chronicles 21:3

Their father gave them silver, gold, and other treasures.

He also gave them cities in Judah with strong defenses.

Jehoram was his oldest son, so he gave Jehoram the kingdom.

## 2 Chronicles 21:4

Once Jehoram was firmly in power, he killed all his brothers.

He also killed some of Israel’s princes with the sword.

## 2 Chronicles 21:5

Jehoram was 32 when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for eight years.

## 2 Chronicles 21:6

Jehoram married a daughter of Ahab, king of Israel.

Jehoram followed the example of Ahab’s family and Israel’s kings.

The LORD saw the evil Jehoram did.

## 2 Chronicles 21:7

Yet the LORD kept his covenant with David, Jehoram’s ancestor.

A covenant is a solemn promise.

The LORD had promised to keep a lamp burning for David forever.

The lamp pictured David’s royal family continuing through the generations.

So the LORD spared David’s family.

## Edom and Libnah reject Jehoram’s rule

## 2 Chronicles 21:8

During Jehoram’s reign, the people of Edom rebelled.

They refused to obey Judah and chose their own king.

## 2 Chronicles 21:9

Jehoram went into Edom with his officers and chariots.

Chariots were carts that horses pulled in battle.

Edom’s soldiers surrounded Jehoram and his chariot commanders.

During the night Jehoram got up and attacked.

## 2 Chronicles 21:10

Edom still ruled itself when this account was written.

Around that same time, the city of Libnah also rebelled.

Jehoram had turned away from the LORD, the God his ancestors worshiped.

## 2 Chronicles 21:11

Jehoram built places on Judah’s hills to worship other gods.

He led Jerusalem and all Judah away from the LORD.

The people broke faith with God like someone unfaithful in marriage.

## A letter from Elijah

## 2 Chronicles 21:12

A letter came from Elijah the prophet.

A prophet speaks God’s message to people.

Elijah wrote, “The LORD, the God of your ancestor David, says this.

‘Your father Jehoshaphat and King Asa of Judah showed you how to live.

You did not follow their example.

## 2 Chronicles 21:13

You followed Israel’s kings instead.

Like Ahab’s family, you led Jerusalem and Judah to worship other gods.

You even killed your own brothers, your father’s sons.

They were better men than you.

## 2 Chronicles 21:14

So I, the LORD, will bring great trouble on your people.

It will strike your sons, your wives, and everything you own.

## 2 Chronicles 21:15

You yourself will fall seriously ill.

The illness will hurt your bowels, the parts inside you that carry food through your body.

You will suffer day after day until your bowels come out.’”

## Jehoram loses his family and dies

## 2 Chronicles 21:16

The LORD stirred up the Philistines against Jehoram.

He also stirred up the Arabs who lived near the Cushites.

## 2 Chronicles 21:17

They invaded Judah and took everything they found in the palace.

They took Jehoram’s wives and sons away.

Only his youngest son, Jehoahaz, remained.

## 2 Chronicles 21:18

After this, the LORD struck Jehoram with an illness in his bowels.

No one could cure it.

## 2 Chronicles 21:19

Jehoram suffered for two whole years.

Then his bowels came out, and he died in great pain.

The people had lit fires to honor earlier kings at their deaths.

They lit no fire for Jehoram.

## 2 Chronicles 21:20

Jehoram became king at 32 and ruled in Jerusalem for eight years.

No one was sorry when he died.

People buried him in the City of David.

They gave him a grave outside the royal tombs.
