# 2 Chronicles 24 — Joash repairs the temple, then turns away from God

**About this chapter**

King Joash works with Jehoiada the priest to repair the temple. After Jehoiada dies, Joash listens to other leaders. When Jehoiada’s son warns him, Joash refuses to listen.

## Joash wants to repair the temple

## 2 Chronicles 24:1

Joash became king at seven years old.

He ruled in Jerusalem for forty years.

His mother was Zibiah, from Beersheba.

## 2 Chronicles 24:2

While Jehoiada the priest lived, Joash did what pleased the LORD.

## 2 Chronicles 24:3

Jehoiada arranged for Joash to marry two women.

Joash had sons and daughters.

## 2 Chronicles 24:4

Later, Joash decided to repair the LORD’s house, the temple.

## 2 Chronicles 24:5

Joash called the priests and Levites together.

The Levites helped care for the temple.

Joash said, “Go to the towns of Judah.

Collect the money all Israel owes each year.

Use it to repair your God’s house. Hurry!”

But the Levites were slow to act.

## 2 Chronicles 24:6

Joash called for Jehoiada, the high priest.

He asked, “Why haven’t you sent the Levites to collect the tax from Judah and Jerusalem?

Moses, the LORD’s servant, and the people of Israel required that payment.

It was for the sacred tent where our people worshiped.”

## 2 Chronicles 24:7

The sons of Athaliah, the wicked queen, had broken into God’s house.

They took things set apart for the LORD.

They used them to worship gods called the Baals.

## People put money in a chest

## 2 Chronicles 24:8

Joash ordered people to make a chest, a large box.

They set it outside, at the gate of the LORD’s house.

![A wooden chest with an opening in the lid for money.](../pictures/temple-money-chest.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 24:9

A message went out across Judah and Jerusalem.

“Bring the tax to the LORD.

Moses, God’s servant, required it when Israel lived in the wilderness.”

## 2 Chronicles 24:10

The leaders and the people gladly brought their money.

They dropped it into the chest until it was full.

## 2 Chronicles 24:11

The Levites took the chest to the king’s officials.

When it held plenty of money, two men emptied it.

One kept records for the king. The other served the high priest.

They put the chest back at the gate.

They did this daily and collected a great deal of money.

## 2 Chronicles 24:12

Joash and Jehoiada gave the money to the people overseeing repairs.

They hired workers to cut stone and shape wood.

Other workers repaired the iron and bronze parts.

## 2 Chronicles 24:13

The workers followed the temple plans.

They restored the building and made it strong again.

## 2 Chronicles 24:14

When the repairs were finished, money remained.

The workers brought it to Joash and Jehoiada.

With it they had tools made for worship and burnt offerings.

They also had dishes and other gold and silver objects made.

As long as Jehoiada lived, the priests regularly burned offerings to the LORD.

## Joash stops listening to the LORD

## 2 Chronicles 24:15

Jehoiada lived a long life. He died at 130.

## 2 Chronicles 24:16

The people buried Jehoiada with the kings in the City of David.

They honored him for the good he had done in Israel.

He had served God and cared for God’s house.

## 2 Chronicles 24:17

After Jehoiada died, the leaders of Judah bowed before Joash.

The king listened to them.

## 2 Chronicles 24:18

They left the house of the LORD, the God their ancestors worshiped.

They worshiped poles made for the goddess Asherah and other idols.

Idols are objects people worship as gods.

The LORD was angry with Judah and Jerusalem for doing this.

## 2 Chronicles 24:19

Even then, the LORD sent prophets, people who spoke for him.

They told the people what they had done wrong.

They urged them to come back to God.

The people refused to listen.

## 2 Chronicles 24:20

God’s Spirit came upon Zechariah, the son of Jehoiada the priest.

Zechariah stood before the people and spoke God’s message.

“Why do you break the LORD’s commands?

Things cannot go well for you this way.

You have left the LORD, and he has left you.”

## 2 Chronicles 24:21

Some people made a plan against Zechariah.

At the king’s command, they threw stones at him in the temple courtyard.

## 2 Chronicles 24:22

Joash had forgotten how kindly Jehoiada had treated him.

Now Joash had ordered the death of Jehoiada’s son.

As Zechariah died, he said, “May the LORD see this.

May he make you answer for what you have done.”

## The end of Joash’s rule

## 2 Chronicles 24:23

In spring, the army of Aram attacked Joash.

They entered Judah and Jerusalem and killed all the people’s leaders.

They sent the goods they took to their king in Damascus.

## 2 Chronicles 24:24

Aram had few soldiers. Judah had a much larger army.

Yet the LORD let Aram defeat Judah’s army.

Judah had left the LORD, the God their ancestors worshiped.

This was how judgment came on Joash.

## 2 Chronicles 24:25

When Aram’s soldiers left, Joash was badly wounded.

His servants plotted against him because he had killed Jehoiada’s son.

They killed Joash in his bed.

The people buried him in the City of David, outside the royal tombs.

## 2 Chronicles 24:26

Two servants plotted against Joash.

Zabad was the son of Shimeath, a woman from Ammon.

Jehozabad was the son of Shimrith, a woman from Moab.

## 2 Chronicles 24:27

A record explaining the Book of the Kings tells more about Joash.

It tells about his sons and the many messages about him.

It also describes the repairs to God’s house.

Amaziah, the son of Joash, became the next king.
