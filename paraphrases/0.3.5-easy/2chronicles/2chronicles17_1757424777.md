# 2 Chronicles 17 — Jehoshaphat sends teachers through Judah

**About this chapter**

Jehoshaphat follows his father Asa as king of Judah. He sends teachers to the towns so the people can hear God’s law.

## Jehoshaphat becomes king

## 2 Chronicles 17:1

When Asa’s reign ended, his son Jehoshaphat became king of Judah.

He made Judah’s defenses stronger against Israel, the northern kingdom.

## 2 Chronicles 17:2

He sent soldiers to guard every city in Judah that had strong defenses.

He also posted soldiers elsewhere in Judah.

Some guarded the towns in Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.

## 2 Chronicles 17:3

The LORD was with Jehoshaphat.

He followed the example his ancestor David had set in his early years.

Jehoshaphat did not turn to the false gods called the Baals.

## 2 Chronicles 17:4

He turned to the God his father had worshiped.

He obeyed God’s commands.

He stayed away from the worship practices of northern Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 17:5

The LORD made Jehoshaphat secure as king.

People from all over Judah brought gifts to their king.

Jehoshaphat grew very wealthy, and people honored him.

## 2 Chronicles 17:6

Jehoshaphat loved doing what pleased the LORD.

He removed the hilltop places of worship from Judah.

He also removed the poles used to worship the goddess Asherah.

## Teachers take God’s law to the towns

## 2 Chronicles 17:7

In his third year as king, Jehoshaphat sent five officials to teach in Judah’s towns.

Their names were Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah.

## 2 Chronicles 17:8

Nine Levites went with them.

Their names were Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, and Shemiramoth.

The others were Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah.

Two priests, Elishama and Jehoram, also went.

## 2 Chronicles 17:9

The teachers took the Book of the Law of the LORD with them.

They went from town to town throughout Judah.

They taught the people what the LORD’s law said.

## Judah’s neighbors keep the peace

## 2 Chronicles 17:10

All the kingdoms around Judah feared the LORD.

So they did not go to war against Jehoshaphat.

## 2 Chronicles 17:11

Some Philistines brought gifts and paid silver to Jehoshaphat.

The Arabs brought him 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats from their flocks.

## 2 Chronicles 17:12

Jehoshaphat grew more powerful.

He built forts in Judah.

He also built cities where supplies could be stored.

## 2 Chronicles 17:13

He kept large amounts of supplies in Judah’s cities.

Brave, strong soldiers served him in Jerusalem.

## The commanders and their men

## 2 Chronicles 17:14

The soldiers were counted in groups by family.

These commanders from Judah led thousands of men.

Adnah led 300,000 brave fighting men.

## 2 Chronicles 17:15

The next commander was Jehohanan.

He led 280,000 men.

## 2 Chronicles 17:16

Next came Amasiah, the son of Zichri.

He had volunteered to serve the LORD.

He led 200,000 brave fighting men.

## 2 Chronicles 17:17

Eliada was a brave commander from Benjamin.

He led 200,000 men who carried bows and shields.

## 2 Chronicles 17:18

Next came Jehozabad.

He led 180,000 men equipped for battle.

## 2 Chronicles 17:19

All these men served the king.

Other soldiers also served him in the fortified cities throughout Judah.
