# 2 Chronicles 2 — Solomon asks Hiram for workers and wood

**About this chapter**

Solomon needs workers and wood to build the temple.

Hiram tells Solomon how the logs will reach Israel.

## Solomon plans the work

## 2 Chronicles 2:1

Solomon decided to build a temple to honor the LORD.

He also planned a palace where he could live.

## 2 Chronicles 2:2

Solomon ordered 70,000 men to carry heavy loads.

He ordered 80,000 men to cut stone in the mountains.

He put 3,600 men in charge of the workers.

## Solomon sends a message to Hiram

## 2 Chronicles 2:3

Solomon sent a message to Hiram, the king of Tyre.

He said, “You sent cedar wood to my father David.

He used it to build his house.

Please do the same for me.”

## 2 Chronicles 2:4

Solomon explained, “I will set this temple apart for the LORD my God.

We will burn incense there. Incense makes sweet-smelling smoke.

We will always keep bread there as an offering to God.

We will burn offerings every morning and evening.

We will also bring offerings on our weekly days of rest, at each new moon, and at the feasts the LORD has set for us.

The LORD gave Israel this duty for all time.”

## 2 Chronicles 2:5

Solomon said the temple would be great because Israel’s God is greater than all gods.

## 2 Chronicles 2:6

Solomon said, “Even the highest heavens cannot hold God.

How could anyone build a house big enough for him?

Who am I to build him a house?

I can only build a place to burn offerings to him.”

## 2 Chronicles 2:7

Solomon asked Hiram to send someone who could carve designs.

The worker needed to know how to shape gold, silver, bronze, and iron.

He also needed to work with purple, deep red, and blue yarn.

He would join the workers David had chosen in Judah and Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 2:8

Solomon asked for logs from three kinds of trees in Lebanon.

They were cedar, cypress, and algum trees.

He said, “Your men know how to cut trees there.

My men will work with yours.”

## 2 Chronicles 2:9

Solomon said they would need many logs.

The temple would be large and beautiful.

## 2 Chronicles 2:10

Solomon promised to pay the woodcutters with food and drink.

They would get 20,000 cors of ground wheat and 20,000 cors of barley.

A cor was a measure for grain.

They would also get 20,000 baths of wine and 20,000 baths of olive oil.

A bath was a measure for liquids.

## Hiram writes back

## 2 Chronicles 2:11

King Hiram wrote back to Solomon.

He said, “The LORD loves his people.

That is why he made you their king.”

## 2 Chronicles 2:12

Hiram wrote, “Praise the LORD, the God of Israel!

He made the sky and the earth.

He gave King David a wise son who understands things well.

You will build a temple for the LORD and a palace for yourself.”

## 2 Chronicles 2:13

Hiram said he would send a man named Huram-abi.

Huram-abi was skilled at making things and thinking of new designs.

## 2 Chronicles 2:14

Huram-abi’s mother came from the tribe of Dan. His father came from Tyre.

Huram-abi could shape gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, and wood.

He could work with purple, blue, and deep red yarn and fine linen cloth.

He could carve designs and make things from any plan he was given.

He would work with Solomon’s workers and those who had served David.

## 2 Chronicles 2:15

Hiram asked Solomon to send the workers the wheat, barley, olive oil, and wine he had promised.

## 2 Chronicles 2:16

Hiram said, “We will cut as many logs as you need in Lebanon.

We will tie the logs together to make rafts.

We will float the rafts on the sea to Joppa.

From Joppa you can take the logs up to Jerusalem.”

## Solomon counts the workers

## 2 Chronicles 2:17

Solomon’s father David had once counted the men from other lands who lived in Israel.

Solomon counted them again. There were 153,600 men.

## 2 Chronicles 2:18

Solomon ordered 70,000 of these men to carry loads.

He sent 80,000 to cut stone in the mountains.

He put the other 3,600 in charge of the workers.
