# 2 Chronicles 8 — Solomon builds cities and arranges worship

**About this chapter**

Solomon finishes the temple and his palace.

He builds cities and assigns work to different people.

At the temple, each group has its own job.

## Solomon builds and strengthens cities

## 2 Chronicles 8:1

Solomon spent twenty years building the LORD’s temple and his own palace.

At the end of those years he began other work.

## 2 Chronicles 8:2

Solomon rebuilt the cities Hiram had given him.

He brought Israelites to live there.

## 2 Chronicles 8:3

Then Solomon went to Hamath-zobah and captured it.

## 2 Chronicles 8:4

He built Tadmor in the desert.

In Hamath he also built cities for storing supplies.

## 2 Chronicles 8:5

Solomon rebuilt Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon.

He protected both cities with walls and gates.

Heavy bars held the gates shut.

![A bar holds a city gate shut between two towers.](../pictures/asa-city-gate.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 8:6

Solomon also built up Baalath and all his cities for storing supplies.

He built up the cities where he kept chariots and horses.

Chariots were carts pulled by horses.

Solomon built whatever he wanted in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his kingdom.

## The people who worked for Solomon

## 2 Chronicles 8:7

Several peoples besides the Israelites still lived in the land.

They were Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

## 2 Chronicles 8:8

Their ancestors had survived when the Israelites took the land.

Solomon forced their descendants to work for him.

This forced labor continued up to the time of this account.

## 2 Chronicles 8:9

Solomon did not make the Israelites work as slaves.

Israelites served as soldiers and as leaders over army officers.

They commanded his chariots and his soldiers on horses.

## 2 Chronicles 8:10

Israelites also served as Solomon’s chief officers.

These 250 men supervised the work.

## Solomon arranges worship at the temple

## 2 Chronicles 8:11

Solomon’s wife was the daughter of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

Solomon moved her from the City of David.

He took her to the palace he had built for her.

He said, “My wife must live outside King David’s house.

The places where the LORD’s ark has been are holy.”

The ark was a sacred chest.

## 2 Chronicles 8:12

Solomon had built an altar in front of the temple porch.

There he gave burnt offerings to the LORD.

These were gifts burned on the altar.

## 2 Chronicles 8:13

Solomon followed Moses’ command about what to offer each day.

He made offerings on the weekly Sabbath, the day of rest.

He made offerings at each new moon, when a month began.

He also made offerings at three festivals each year.

One was the Feast of Unleavened Bread.

That is bread made without yeast.

The others were the Feast of Weeks and the Feast of Tabernacles.

Tabernacles were temporary shelters.

## 2 Chronicles 8:14

Solomon followed the instructions of his father David, who served God.

He gave each group of priests its turn to serve.

The Levites praised God and helped the priests each day.

Solomon also assigned groups of guards to each gate.

## 2 Chronicles 8:15

The people followed all the king’s instructions for the priests and Levites.

They even followed his instructions for the temple’s treasure rooms.

## 2 Chronicles 8:16

The work began when the builders laid the temple’s foundation.

They carried out all Solomon’s work until the LORD’s temple was complete.

## Sailors bring gold to Solomon

## 2 Chronicles 8:17

Solomon went to Ezion-geber and Eloth.

These towns stood on the coast of Edom.

## 2 Chronicles 8:18

Hiram sent Solomon ships with captains and experienced sailors.

They sailed with Solomon’s servants to Ophir.

There they obtained 450 talents of gold.

A talent was a unit for weighing heavy loads.

They brought the gold back to King Solomon.
