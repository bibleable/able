# 2 Chronicles 32 — Hezekiah and Isaiah pray for Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

The king of Assyria wants Jerusalem to surrender. He sends messages to frighten the people. King Hezekiah and the prophet Isaiah ask the LORD for help.

## Hezekiah encourages the people

## 2 Chronicles 32:1

Hezekiah had served the LORD faithfully.

Then Sennacherib, king of Assyria, invaded Judah.

He surrounded its walled towns and tried to take them.

## 2 Chronicles 32:2

Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib also meant to attack Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 32:3

He spoke to his leaders and strongest soldiers about the springs outside the city.

They agreed to help him stop the water there.

## 2 Chronicles 32:4

Many people helped close off the springs and the stream.

They said, “Why should Assyria’s kings find plenty of water when they come?”

## 2 Chronicles 32:5

Hezekiah worked hard to repair the broken city wall and put towers on it.

He built another wall outside it.

He strengthened the terraces that supported the City of David.

He also provided many weapons and shields.

## 2 Chronicles 32:6

He chose commanders for the people.

Then he gathered the people in the square by the city gate and encouraged them.

## 2 Chronicles 32:7

“Be strong and brave,” he said.

“Take heart, even when you see Assyria’s king and his great army.

The one with us is greater than anyone with him.

## 2 Chronicles 32:8

Assyria’s king depends on human strength.

We have the LORD our God to help us and fight for us.” Hezekiah’s words gave the people courage.

## Assyria’s king sends a message

## 2 Chronicles 32:9

Sennacherib and his army were surrounding the city of Lachish.

From there he sent messengers to Hezekiah and everyone in Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 32:10

The messengers spoke for Sennacherib.

“Why are you so sure you can stay in Jerusalem while an army surrounds it?

## 2 Chronicles 32:11

Hezekiah says your God will save you from me.

He is misleading you.

You will die of hunger and thirst.

## 2 Chronicles 32:12

Didn’t Hezekiah remove your God’s hilltop places of worship and altars?

He told Judah and Jerusalem to worship and burn offerings at only one altar.

## 2 Chronicles 32:13

You know what my ancestors and I did to other nations.

Did their gods ever save their lands from me?

## 2 Chronicles 32:14

My ancestors destroyed whole nations.

None of their gods saved those people from my power.

How can your God save you?

## 2 Chronicles 32:15

Stop believing Hezekiah.

No god of any kingdom saved its people from me or from my ancestors.

Why would your God be able to save you?”

## 2 Chronicles 32:16

Sennacherib’s messengers said even more against the LORD and his servant Hezekiah.

## 2 Chronicles 32:17

Sennacherib also wrote letters making fun of Israel’s God.

“The gods of other nations could not save their people from me.

Hezekiah’s God will not save his people either.”

![An open scroll with lines of writing. Sennacherib sent his challenge in letters as well as by word of mouth.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 2 Chronicles 32:18

The Assyrians shouted in Hebrew so the people on Jerusalem’s wall would understand.

They hoped to frighten the people and take the city.

## 2 Chronicles 32:19

They spoke about Jerusalem’s God as if he were like the gods people had made with their hands.

## The LORD answers the prayer

## 2 Chronicles 32:20

King Hezekiah and Isaiah the prophet, son of Amoz, cried out to God in heaven.

## 2 Chronicles 32:21

The LORD sent an angel to the Assyrian camp.

The angel struck down all its strongest soldiers, leaders, and commanders.

Sennacherib went home in shame.

When he entered his god’s temple, some of his own sons killed him with swords.

## 2 Chronicles 32:22

So the LORD saved Hezekiah and Jerusalem from Sennacherib and their other enemies.

He gave them peace on every side.

## 2 Chronicles 32:23

Many people brought offerings to the LORD in Jerusalem.

They brought valuable gifts to Hezekiah too.

From then on, nations everywhere held him in high honor.

## Hezekiah sets aside his pride

## 2 Chronicles 32:24

About that time Hezekiah became so ill that he was close to death.

He prayed to the LORD.

The LORD answered and gave him a sign.

## 2 Chronicles 32:25

Hezekiah grew proud.

He failed to respond with gratitude to the kindness God showed him.

The LORD was angry with him and with Judah and Jerusalem.

## 2 Chronicles 32:26

Then Hezekiah humbled himself.

The people of Jerusalem did the same.

The LORD held back his punishment during Hezekiah’s lifetime.

## Hezekiah’s work and the end of his reign

## 2 Chronicles 32:27

Hezekiah received great wealth and honor.

He built rooms to store gold, silver, precious stones, spices, shields, and other treasures.

## 2 Chronicles 32:28

He built storehouses for grain, new wine, and oil.

He made stalls for livestock and pens for his flocks.

## 2 Chronicles 32:29

He built towns and gained large flocks of sheep and herds of cattle.

God gave him great wealth.

## 2 Chronicles 32:30

Hezekiah closed the upper outlet of the Gihon Spring.

He directed the water down to the west side of the City of David.

His work went well.

## 2 Chronicles 32:31

The rulers of Babylon sent messengers to ask about the remarkable sign in the land.

This time God let Hezekiah act on his own.

God tested him to see what was in his heart.

## 2 Chronicles 32:32

The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz tells more about Hezekiah and his acts of faithful love.

That account is in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel.

## 2 Chronicles 32:33

Hezekiah died.

The people buried him in the upper tombs of David’s family.

All Judah and Jerusalem honored him at his death.

His son Manasseh became king.
