## Reading with a learner

Let the reader choose a section. Give them time to read it, or read it aloud together.

Ask, “What happened here?” Wait for their answer.

For a section with a picture, ask, “What does this picture show?” Wait again.

If something is unclear, ask the reader to point to it. Write down the verse, the word or picture, and what the reader understood. Try a different word or picture with the reader. Read the section together again before adopting the change.
