# Romans 7 — Paul wants to do good but struggles with sin

**About this chapter**

Paul explains how a good command helped him recognise sin.

He describes what it feels like to act against his own desire to do good.

## Paul gives an example from marriage

## Romans 7:1

Brothers and sisters, you know the law.

The law has authority over people only while they live.

## Romans 7:2

Think about a married woman.

The law binds her to her husband while he lives.

When her husband dies, that marriage bond ends.

## Romans 7:3

Suppose she joins herself to another man while her husband lives.

That is adultery.

If her husband dies, she is free to marry again.

That new marriage is not adultery.

## Romans 7:4

Brothers and sisters, Christ died in his body.

Through his death, you also died to the law.

Now you belong to Christ, whom God raised from the dead.

So our lives can bear fruit for God.

**Help with these words**

To bear fruit for God means to live in a way that produces good for him.

![Grapes growing on a vine. A plant produces fruit as it grows.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Romans 7:5

We once lived by our own sinful desires.

The law stirred those desires into action in our bodies.

What grew from that way of living was death.

## Romans 7:6

Now we have died to what once held us.

The law no longer holds us as it did.

We serve God in the new way of the Holy Spirit.

The old way depended on the written law.

**Pause and answer**

Who guides believers in their new way of serving God?

## The law helps Paul recognise sin

## Romans 7:7

Does this mean the law itself is sin?

Of course not!

The law helped me recognise sin.

It says, “Do not covet.”

Without that command I would not have recognised coveting.

**Help with these words**

To covet is to want something that belongs to someone else for yourself.

## Romans 7:8

Sin used that command as its chance.

I began wanting all sorts of things that belonged to others.

Apart from the law, sin was dead.

## Romans 7:9

I once lived apart from the law.

Then the command came.

Sin sprang to life, and I died.

## Romans 7:10

I discovered something about the command meant to bring life.

For me, it led to death.

## Romans 7:11

Sin used the command to trick me.

Through that very command, sin brought me death.

**Pause and answer**

Which command helped Paul recognise coveting?

## The command is good

## Romans 7:12

So the law is holy.

The command is holy, right and good.

## Romans 7:13

Did something good cause my death?

No.

Sin used the good command to bring me death.

The command showed me just how evil sin is.

## Romans 7:14

We know the law comes from God’s Spirit.

But on my own I am like a slave whom sin has bought.

**Help with these words**

Paul speaks of sin as a master. He means that sin has power over his actions.

**Pause and answer**

What did the good command show Paul about sin?

## Paul describes his struggle

## Romans 7:15

I do not understand what I do.

I fail to do what I want to do.

I do the very thing I hate.

## Romans 7:16

If I hate doing wrong, I agree that the law is good.

## Romans 7:17

What makes me act this way?

It is sin living in me, acting against what I want.

## Romans 7:18

I know nothing good lives in my sinful human nature.

I want to do what is good.

Yet I cannot carry it out.

## Romans 7:19

I do not do the good I want to do.

I keep doing the evil I want to avoid.

## Romans 7:20

When I act against what I want, the same thing is happening.

It is sin living in me that does it.

**Pause and answer**

How do Paul’s actions differ from what he wants to do?

## Paul asks who can rescue him

## Romans 7:21

I have found that this keeps happening.

When I want to do good, evil is right there with me.

## Romans 7:22

Deep inside, I take joy in God’s law.

## Romans 7:23

Yet I see another power at work in my body.

It fights against what my mind knows is right.

It holds me prisoner to the sin within me.

## Romans 7:24

How miserable I am!

Who will rescue me from this body that leads to death?

## Romans 7:25

I thank God, who rescues me through Jesus Christ our Lord!

With my mind I serve God’s law.

Yet in my sinful human nature I serve the law of sin.
