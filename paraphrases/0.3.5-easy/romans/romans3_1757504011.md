# Romans 3 — God makes people right with him through faith

**About this chapter**

Paul asks whether anyone can claim to be free from sin.

Then he explains what God gives people who trust Jesus.

## God keeps his word

## Romans 3:1

What good comes from being Jewish?

What value does circumcision have?

**Help with these words**

Circumcision is a sign on the body of a Jewish boy or man. It marks the agreement God made with his people.

## Romans 3:2

There are many good things.

First, God trusted the Jewish people with his own words.

## Romans 3:3

Some people did not trust God.

Does that mean God will fail to keep his word?

## Romans 3:4

Of course not!

Even if everyone else lies, God tells the truth.

The Scriptures say to God, “When you speak, people will see that you are right.

When you judge, you will win your case.”

**Pause and answer**

What did God trust the Jewish people with?

## Paul answers an objection

## Romans 3:5

Someone might ask, “When we do wrong, people see more clearly that God does right.

Does that make God unfair when he shows his anger against us?”

I am asking the question as people might ask it.

## Romans 3:6

My answer is no!

If God were unfair, how could he judge the world?

## Romans 3:7

Someone might still ask, “Suppose my lie shows how truthful God is.

Suppose people praise him more because of that.

Why does God still judge me guilty of sin?”

## Romans 3:8

Some people even claim we teach this: “Let us do evil so that good will come.”

That claim about us is a lie.

God is right to judge those who say this.

**Pause and answer**

What teaching did people falsely say Paul taught?

## The Scriptures describe the power of sin

## Romans 3:9

Are we Jews any better than others?

No.

We have already shown that sin has power over everyone, both Jews and Greeks.

## Romans 3:10

The Scriptures say, “No one does what is right.

Not even one person does.

## Romans 3:11

No one understands.

No one looks for God.

## Romans 3:12

Everyone has turned away from God.

Together they have become useless.

No one does good, not even one.”

## Romans 3:13

The Scriptures describe their throats as open graves.

They use their tongues to tell lies.

Their lips carry poison like the poison of snakes.

**Help with these words**

Graves and poison are word pictures. Paul is describing the deadly harm people can do with their words.

## Romans 3:14

They curse people and speak bitter words.

## Romans 3:15

They rush to kill.

## Romans 3:16

Where they go, they destroy things and cause suffering.

## Romans 3:17

They do not know how to live in peace.

## Romans 3:18

They have no fear of God.

**Pause and answer**

How can people hurt others with their words?

## The law shows people their sin

## Romans 3:19

We know the law speaks to people who live under it.

No one has an excuse to give.

The whole world must answer to God.

## Romans 3:20

No one can make themselves right with God by doing what the law says.

The law shows us our sin.

## Romans 3:21

Now God has shown how he makes people right with him apart from the law.

The law of Moses and the writings of the prophets both tell us about this.

**Pause and answer**

What does God’s law help people recognise?

## God gives what no one can earn

## Romans 3:22

God makes people right with him when they trust Jesus Christ.

This is true for everyone who believes.

God makes no distinction between them.

## Romans 3:23

Everyone has sinned.

We all fall short of God’s glory.

## Romans 3:24

In his kindness God freely makes us right with him.

Christ Jesus paid the price to set us free.

## Romans 3:25

God gave Jesus as a sacrifice for sin.

Jesus shed his blood for us.

We receive this gift through faith.

God had patiently passed over sins people committed long ago.

Now he showed that he does what is right.

## Romans 3:26

God did this to show his justice today.

God is fair when he judges.

He also makes the person who trusts Jesus right with him.

**Pause and answer**

How does a person receive God’s gift?

## The same God welcomes Jews and Gentiles

## Romans 3:27

Do we have anything to boast about?

No.

What matters here is faith.

We cannot boast about what we have done.

## Romans 3:28

We teach that God makes a person right with him through faith.

Keeping the law cannot earn that gift.

## Romans 3:29

Is God the God of Jewish people alone?

Is he also the God of Gentiles?

Yes, he is their God too.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Romans 3:30

There is only one God.

He makes people who have received circumcision right with him through faith.

He does the same for people without circumcision through that same faith.

## Romans 3:31

Does faith mean we throw away the law?

Of course not!

We uphold the law.

**Help with these words**

To uphold the law means to recognise and support what it says.

**Pause and answer**

How does God make both groups right with him?
