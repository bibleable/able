# Romans 14 — Welcome believers who choose differently

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to believers who disagree about food and special days.

He asks them to think about how their choices affect each other.

## Welcome each other at the table

## Romans 14:1

Welcome a believer whose faith is weak.

Do not judge that person’s opinions.

## Romans 14:2

One believer trusts that they may eat any food.

Another believer is unsure and eats only vegetables.

## Romans 14:3

If you eat every kind of food, respect the person who does not.

If you avoid some foods, do not judge the person who eats them.

God has welcomed that person.

## Romans 14:4

Who are you to judge someone else’s servant?

The servant’s own master decides how well the servant has done.

The Lord can help his servant stand, and that servant will stand.

**Help with these words**

Here the servant stands for a believer. Each believer answers to the Lord.

**Pause and answer**

How should you treat a believer who eats different food?

## Both believers give thanks to God

## Romans 14:5

One person sets aside a day as special.

Another treats every day alike.

Each person should be sure about their own decision.

## Romans 14:6

The person who keeps a special day honours the Lord.

The person who eats gives thanks to God and honours the Lord.

The person who avoids a food also honours the Lord and thanks God.

## Romans 14:7

None of us lives for ourselves alone.

None of us dies for ourselves alone.

## Romans 14:8

While we live, we live for the Lord.

When we die, we still belong to him.

In life and in death, we are the Lord’s.

## Romans 14:9

Christ died and came back to life for this reason.

He is Lord of the dead and the living.

**Pause and answer**

What can both believers do even when they choose different foods?

## Each person answers to God

## Romans 14:10

Why judge another believer?

Why look down on them?

We will all stand before God as our judge.

## Romans 14:11

The Scriptures tell us what the Lord says.

“As surely as I live, everyone will kneel before me.

Everyone will openly acknowledge God.”

## Romans 14:12

So each of us will answer to God for what we do.

**Pause and answer**

Who will each of us answer to?

## Think about the person beside you

## Romans 14:13

Let us stop judging each other.

Decide that you will put nothing in another believer’s path to make them stumble.

**Help with these words**

To make someone stumble means to lead them into doing something they believe is wrong.

## Romans 14:14

I belong to the Lord Jesus, and I am sure of this.

No food is unclean in itself.

But if someone believes a food is unclean, it is unclean for that person.

**Help with these words**

Here “unclean” means a food someone believes God forbids them to eat.

## Romans 14:15

If your food causes another believer distress, you have stopped acting in love.

Christ died for that person.

Do not let what you eat destroy them.

## Romans 14:16

Take care that people have no cause to call what you consider good evil.

## Romans 14:17

Eating and drinking do not define God’s kingdom.

What matters is doing right, living in peace and knowing joy in the Holy Spirit.

## Romans 14:18

People who serve Christ in this way please God.

Other people approve of them too.

**Pause and answer**

What matters in God’s kingdom when believers share a meal?

## Choose what helps another believer

## Romans 14:19

Let us do what brings peace.

Let us help each other grow stronger in faith.

## Romans 14:20

Do not tear down God’s work over food.

All food is clean.

Yet it is wrong to eat in a way that makes someone else stumble.

## Romans 14:21

It is better to leave the meat or wine alone if it makes another believer stumble.

The same goes for anything else that makes them stumble.

## Romans 14:22

Keep what you believe about these choices between you and God.

God blesses the person whose conscience is clear about what they approve.

## Romans 14:23

If someone doubts it is right to eat a food and eats it anyway, they stand guilty.

They did not act from faith.

Whatever does not come from faith is sin.

**Pause and answer**

What could you do if drinking wine would lead someone else to act against their faith?
