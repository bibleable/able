# Romans 1 — Paul writes to the believers in Rome

**About this chapter**

Paul writes a letter to believers in Rome.

He explains why he wants to visit them and share the good news.

## Paul introduces himself

## Romans 1:1

I am Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ.

God called me to be an apostle.

He chose me to tell his good news.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone God sends to speak for him.

## Romans 1:2

Long ago, God promised this good news.

His prophets wrote the promise in the holy Scriptures.

## Romans 1:3

This good news is about God’s Son.

Jesus was born into the family of King David.

## Romans 1:4

God raised Jesus from the dead.

Through the Holy Spirit, this showed with power that Jesus is God’s Son.

He is Jesus Christ our Lord.

## Romans 1:5

Through Jesus we received God’s kindness and our work as apostles.

Jesus sent us to speak for him among all the Gentiles.

We call people to trust him and obey him.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Romans 1:6

God has also called you to belong to Jesus Christ.

## Romans 1:7

I write to all of you in Rome whom God loves.

He has called you to be his holy people.

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace.

**Pause and answer**

What good news did God choose Paul to share?

## Paul hopes to visit Rome

## Romans 1:8

First, I thank my God for you all through Jesus Christ.

People everywhere are talking about your faith.

## Romans 1:9

I serve God with my whole heart.

I tell people the good news about his Son.

God knows how often I remember you.

## Romans 1:10

I keep praying for you.

I ask God to let me come to you at last, if he is willing.

## Romans 1:11

I long to see you.

I want to share a gift from God’s Spirit to make you strong.

## Romans 1:12

When we meet, your faith can encourage me.

My faith can encourage you too.

## Romans 1:13

My brothers and sisters, I have often planned to visit you.

But each time something has kept me from coming.

I hope for a harvest among you, as among other Gentiles.

**Help with these words**

Harvest is a word picture. A farmer sees the result of the work when grain is ready to gather. Paul hopes to see people in Rome come to trust Jesus and grow in faith.

**Pause and answer**

How could the believers in Rome encourage Paul?

## Paul explains why he shares the good news

## Romans 1:14

I have a duty to speak to Greeks and to people who are not Greek.

My duty is to both the wise and the foolish.

## Romans 1:15

That is why I am eager to tell you the good news in Rome too.

## Romans 1:16

I am not ashamed of the good news.

Through it God uses his power to save everyone who believes.

It came first to Jewish people, then to Greeks.

## Romans 1:17

The good news shows how God makes people right with him.

We trust him from beginning to end.

The Scriptures say, “The person who is right with God will live by faith.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom does God save through the good news?

## People can learn about God from what he made

## Romans 1:18

From heaven God shows his anger at the wrong people do.

They refuse to honour God.

Their evil actions hide the truth.

## Romans 1:19

What they can know about God is clear to them.

God himself has shown them.

## Romans 1:20

People cannot see God.

But ever since the world began, they could see what he made.

His work shows his power lasts for ever.

It shows what makes him God.

So people have no excuse.

## Romans 1:21

They knew God, but they gave him no honour or thanks.

Their thinking led nowhere.

Their hearts grew dark.

**Help with these words**

Here a dark heart means a person has lost the ability to recognise what is true about God.

## Romans 1:22

They claimed to be wise, but became foolish.

## Romans 1:23

God lives for ever and deserves honour.

Instead, they honoured images of people who die.

They also honoured images of birds, animals and reptiles.

**Pause and answer**

What can people learn about God by looking at what he made?

## People worship the things God made

## Romans 1:24

So God let them follow their own desires.

They treated each other’s bodies in shameful ways.

## Romans 1:25

They traded the truth about God for a lie.

They worshipped and served things God made.

They turned away from the one who made them.

He deserves praise for ever!

Amen.

## Romans 1:26

So God let them follow desires that brought shame.

Women gave up natural sexual relations for unnatural ones.

## Romans 1:27

In the same way, men gave up sexual relations with women.

They felt strong sexual desire for other men.

Men did shameful things with other men.

They suffered the consequences of what they did.

**Pause and answer**

What did people worship instead of the one who made them?

## Paul describes other ways people do wrong

## Romans 1:28

They refused to recognise God.

So God let them follow their own twisted thinking.

They did things no one should do.

## Romans 1:29

Their lives were full of evil.

They wanted more and more for themselves.

They envied others, killed, quarrelled and told lies.

They wanted to hurt people.

They spread stories behind people’s backs.

## Romans 1:30

They told lies to damage other people’s names.

They hated God and insulted others.

They acted proud and boasted.

They thought up new ways to do wrong.

They disobeyed their parents.

## Romans 1:31

They showed no understanding.

They broke their promises.

They showed neither love nor mercy.

## Romans 1:32

They know God’s judgment is right.

They know he judges such acts worthy of death.

Yet they keep doing these things.

They even approve when others do them.

**Pause and answer**

How did these people treat their promises?
