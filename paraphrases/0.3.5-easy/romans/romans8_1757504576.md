# Romans 8 — Nothing can separate us from God’s love

**About this chapter**

Paul describes how God’s Spirit helps us live and pray.

He also speaks about hope when we suffer.

## The Spirit gives life

## Romans 8:1

God no longer condemns those who belong to Christ Jesus.

**Help with these words**

To condemn someone means to judge them guilty and sentence them to punishment.

## Romans 8:2

You belong to Christ Jesus.

The Spirit who gives life has set you free.

Sin and death no longer rule you.

## Romans 8:3

Our human weakness kept the law from setting us free.

So God sent his own Son in a body like ours.

His Son came as an offering for sin.

In his Son’s human body, God passed judgment on sin.

## Romans 8:4

God did this so that what the law rightly asks could come true in us.

We follow the Spirit instead of our sinful nature.

## Romans 8:5

People who follow their sinful nature think about what it wants.

People who follow the Spirit think about what the Spirit wants.

## Romans 8:6

Following the desires of our sinful nature leads to death.

Following the Spirit leads to life and peace.

## Romans 8:7

A mind set on sinful desires fights against God.

It cannot obey God’s law.

## Romans 8:8

People who let their sinful nature rule them cannot please God.

**Pause and answer**

Where does following the Spirit lead?

## The Spirit lives in believers

## Romans 8:9

If God’s Spirit lives in you, the Spirit rules your life.

Your sinful nature is no longer your master.

Anyone without the Spirit of Christ does not belong to Christ.

## Romans 8:10

If Christ lives in you, your body still faces death because of sin.

Yet your spirit has life because God has made you right with him.

## Romans 8:11

God raised Jesus from the dead.

If God’s Spirit lives in you, God will give life to your bodies too.

He will do this through his Spirit, who lives in you.

## Romans 8:12

So, brothers and sisters, we owe our sinful nature nothing.

We do not have to do what it wants.

## Romans 8:13

If you follow your sinful nature, you will die.

With the Spirit’s help, put a stop to the wrong things you do.

Then you will live.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Jesus life again?

## God welcomes us as his children

## Romans 8:14

All the people God’s Spirit leads are God’s children.

## Romans 8:15

God did not make you slaves living in fear again.

He gave you his Spirit and adopted you as his children.

Through the Spirit we call out to God, “Abba! Father!”

**Help with these words**

Abba means Father. Adoption means taking someone into a family as your own child.

## Romans 8:16

The Spirit himself joins with our own spirit in telling us that we are God’s children.

## Romans 8:17

As God’s children, we are his heirs.

We share with Christ in what God gives him.

If we suffer with Christ, we will also share his glory.

**Help with these words**

An heir is someone who will receive what belongs to the family.

**Pause and answer**

What does the Spirit help us call God?

## Creation waits in hope

## Romans 8:18

I think about what we suffer now.

It is small beside the glory God will show in us.

## Romans 8:19

Everything God made waits eagerly for him to show who his children are.

## Romans 8:20

Creation did not choose its struggles.

God allowed its efforts to come to nothing for a time.

Yet God gave it hope.

## Romans 8:21

One day God will free creation from the power of decay.

Creation will share the glorious freedom of God’s children.

## Romans 8:22

Even now, all creation groans together.

Its pain is like the pain of a woman giving birth.

## Romans 8:23

We also groan deep inside.

We have received the Spirit like the first part of a harvest.

We eagerly wait for all that comes with being God’s adopted children.

God will set our bodies free.

![Ripe grain, like the first part of a harvest.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Help with these words**

The first grain a farmer gathers shows the harvest has begun. God has already given believers his Spirit. They still look forward to what he will do for their bodies.

## Romans 8:24

God saved us with this hope.

Who needs to hope for what they can already see?

## Romans 8:25

We hope for what we cannot see yet.

So we wait for it patiently.

**Pause and answer**

What gift have believers already received while they wait?

## The Spirit helps us pray

## Romans 8:26

The Spirit helps us when we are weak.

We do not know how we should pray.

The Spirit himself prays for us with groans deeper than words.

## Romans 8:27

God knows what is in our hearts.

He also knows what the Spirit means.

The Spirit asks for what God wants for his people.

## Romans 8:28

We know God works through all things together for good.

He does this for those who love him.

He has called them according to his plan.

## Romans 8:29

God knew these people beforehand.

He decided long ago to make them like his Son.

His Son would be the firstborn in a family of many brothers and sisters.

## Romans 8:30

God called the people he had chosen beforehand.

He made those he called right with him.

He also gave them glory.

**Pause and answer**

Who prays for us when we cannot find the words?

## God is for us

## Romans 8:31

What can we say about all this?

If God is for us, who can stand against us?

## Romans 8:32

God gave up his own Son for all of us.

Since he gave us his Son, surely he will freely give us all things with him.

## Romans 8:33

Who can bring a charge against the people God chose?

God himself has declared them right with him.

## Romans 8:34

Who can condemn us?

Christ Jesus died, and God raised him to life.

Christ is at God’s right hand and speaks to God for us.

**Help with these words**

God’s right hand means the place of honour and authority beside him.

**Pause and answer**

Who speaks to God for us at his right hand?

## Nothing can take Christ’s love away

## Romans 8:35

What could separate us from Christ’s love?

Could trouble, suffering, or people attacking us for our faith?

Could hunger, lack of clothes, danger, or the threat of a sword?

## Romans 8:36

The Scriptures say to God, “For your sake we face death all day long.

People treat us like sheep they intend to kill.”

## Romans 8:37

Even in all these things, we win a great victory through Christ, who loved us.

## Romans 8:38

I am sure of this.

Neither death nor life can separate us from God’s love.

Neither angels nor rulers can do it.

Nothing happening now or in the future can do it.

No power can do it.

## Romans 8:39

Nothing high above or deep below can do it.

Nothing else in all creation can separate us from God’s love.

That love is ours in Christ Jesus our Lord.
