# Romans 5 — Jesus brings us peace with God

**About this chapter**

Paul tells believers why they can have hope even in hard times.

He explains how Jesus gives life to people who face death.

## We have peace with God

## Romans 5:1

We trust God, and he has made us right with him.

So we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.

## Romans 5:2

Through Jesus we have come to trust and receive God’s kindness.

We still live in that kindness.

We rejoice because we hope to share in God’s glory.

## Romans 5:3

Even when we suffer, we can rejoice.

We know that suffering teaches us to keep going.

## Romans 5:4

As we keep going, we grow stronger inside.

As our character grows stronger, so does our hope.

## Romans 5:5

This hope will not let us down.

God has given us the Holy Spirit.

Through the Spirit, God has filled our hearts with his love.

**Pause and answer**

What grows as we learn to keep going through suffering?

## Christ died for us

## Romans 5:6

We could do nothing to save ourselves.

At just the right time, Christ died for people who did not honour God.

## Romans 5:7

Hardly anyone would die for someone who does what is right.

Perhaps someone would dare to die for a good person.

## Romans 5:8

God showed how much he loves us.

Christ died for us while we were still sinners.

## Romans 5:9

Jesus shed his blood for us.

Through his death, God has now made us right with him.

So we can be even more certain that Jesus will save us from God’s anger.

## Romans 5:10

Once we were God’s enemies.

Even then, God made peace with us through his Son’s death.

Now that we have peace with God, we can be even more certain he will save us through his Son’s life.

## Romans 5:11

We also rejoice in God because of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Jesus has brought us back into friendship with God.

**Pause and answer**

How did God show his love while we were still sinners?

## Adam’s sin brought death

## Romans 5:12

Sin entered the world through one man, Adam.

Death came through sin.

Death reached everyone because everyone sinned.

## Romans 5:13

People sinned even before God gave the law.

But where there is no law, sin does not count as breaking a law.

## Romans 5:14

Even so, death ruled from Adam’s time until Moses’ time.

People died even when their sin was different from Adam’s sin.

Adam points ahead to someone who would come later.

**Help with these words**

Paul is about to compare Adam with Jesus. Each man did something that affected many other people.

**Pause and answer**

Were people already dying before Moses received the law?

## God gives a gift through Jesus

## Romans 5:15

Adam disobeyed God, and many people died.

God’s gift is greater than the wrong Adam did.

God showed great kindness to many people.

His gift came through the kindness of one man, Jesus Christ.

## Romans 5:16

One sin led God to judge people guilty.

God gave his gift after people had sinned many times.

Through that gift, God declares people right with him.

## Romans 5:17

Because Adam sinned, death ruled over people.

But God gives people his great kindness.

He gives them the gift of being right with him.

Those who receive these gifts will rule in life through Jesus Christ.

**Pause and answer**

What gift does God give people through Jesus?

## Jesus obeyed God

## Romans 5:18

Adam did one wrong thing, and all people came under judgment.

Jesus did one right thing, bringing a right standing with God and life for all people.

## Romans 5:19

Adam disobeyed God, and many became sinners.

Jesus obeyed God, and through him God will make many people right with him.

## Romans 5:20

God gave the law so that people’s wrongdoing would increase.

Yet wherever sin increased, God’s kindness increased even more.

## Romans 5:21

Sin ruled over people and brought death.

Now God’s kindness rules by making us right with him.

It brings us life for ever through our Lord Jesus Christ.

**Help with these words**

Paul speaks of sin as a ruler because of its power over people. He says God’s kindness has even greater power.

**Pause and answer**

When sin increased, what increased even more?
