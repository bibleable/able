# Romans 13 — Give people what you owe and keep loving them

**About this chapter**

Paul writes about taxes, respect and love for neighbours.

He asks believers to pay attention to how they live.

## Paul writes about those who govern

## Romans 13:1

Everyone should accept the authority of those who govern.

Their authority comes from God.

God has put the present rulers in place.

## Romans 13:2

So anyone who resists that authority opposes what God has put in place.

People who do this bring judgment on themselves.

## Romans 13:3

Rulers cause fear when people do wrong.

Do you want to live without fear of a ruler?

Do what is right, and the ruler will approve.

## Romans 13:4

The ruler serves God for your good.

But if you do wrong, you have reason to fear.

The ruler carries a sword for a reason.

The ruler serves God by punishing people who do wrong.

**Help with these words**

In Paul’s time a ruler’s sword showed the power to punish.

## Romans 13:5

So accept their authority.

One reason is to avoid punishment.

Another is that your conscience tells you it is right.

**Help with these words**

Your conscience helps you recognise whether your own actions are right or wrong.

**Pause and answer**

Besides avoiding punishment, what reason does Paul give for respecting authority?

## Give each person what you owe

## Romans 13:6

This is also why you pay taxes.

Those who govern serve God as they give their time to this work.

## Romans 13:7

Give everyone what you owe them.

Pay the taxes and fees you owe.

Show respect to those you owe respect.

Give honour to those you owe honour.

**Pause and answer**

Besides money, what does Paul say you can owe someone?

## Keep loving your neighbour

## Romans 13:8

Pay your debts.

One thing you always owe each other is love.

When you love your neighbour, you do what the law asks.

## Romans 13:9

The law says, “Do not commit adultery.

Do not murder.

Do not steal.

Do not covet.”

One command brings all these and the other commands together.

“Love your neighbour as you love yourself.”

**Help with these words**

Adultery means being sexually unfaithful in marriage. To covet is to want what belongs to someone else for yourself.

## Romans 13:10

Someone who loves a neighbour does that person no wrong.

That is how love fulfils the law.

**Pause and answer**

What one command brings the other commands together?

## Live as people who belong to Jesus

## Romans 13:11

Do these things because you know what time it is.

It is time to wake up.

The day when God will save us is closer now than when we first believed.

## Romans 13:12

The night is almost over.

Daylight is near.

Let us put aside the wrong things people do in darkness.

Let us put on the armour of light.

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures a new day beginning. To wake up means to pay attention to how we live now. Putting on the armour of light means getting ready to do what is right.

## Romans 13:13

Let us live decently, as people do in daylight.

Stay away from wild drinking parties and drunkenness.

Turn away from sexual wrongdoing and uncontrolled desires.

Give up quarrelling and jealousy.

## Romans 13:14

Clothe yourselves with the Lord Jesus Christ.

Do not plan how to satisfy sinful desires.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses clothing as a picture. He asks us to let the way Jesus lived show in what we do.

**Pause and answer**

What would it mean to “clothe yourself” with Jesus today?
