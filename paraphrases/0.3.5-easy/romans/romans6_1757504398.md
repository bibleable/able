# Romans 6 — Living a new life with Jesus

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to people who trust Jesus.

He asks them to think about whom they obey each day.

## Baptism and a new life

## Romans 6:1

Should we keep sinning so that God will show us even more kindness?

## Romans 6:2

Of course not!

We died to sin.

How can we still live as though sin rules us?

**Help with these words**

Paul says “we died to sin” because sin has lost its claim on us. He is speaking to believers who are alive and can choose how to act.

## Romans 6:3

Remember what happened when we received baptism into Christ Jesus.

In baptism we shared in his death.

## Romans 6:4

Through baptism we shared in his death and burial.

God the Father raised Christ from the dead by his glorious power.

So we too can live a new life.

## Romans 6:5

We have been joined to Christ in his death.

We will also share in his rising to life.

**Pause and answer**

What new beginning does Paul describe for people who belong to Jesus?

## Death no longer rules over Jesus

## Romans 6:6

Our old self died with Christ on the cross.

This broke the power sin held over our bodies.

We no longer have to serve sin as slaves.

**Help with these words**

Our “old self” means who we were when sin ruled us. Paul pictures sin as a master who gives orders.

## Romans 6:7

When someone dies, sin loses its claim on that person.

## Romans 6:8

Since we died with Christ, we believe we will live with him too.

## Romans 6:9

God raised Christ from the dead.

Christ cannot die again.

Death no longer rules over him.

## Romans 6:10

Christ died to sin once for all.

Now he lives for God.

## Romans 6:11

Think of yourselves in the same way.

In Christ Jesus you are dead to sin and alive to God.

**Pause and answer**

Why can death no longer rule over Christ?

## Use your body to do what is right

## Romans 6:12

Your body will one day die.

Do not let sin rule it.

Do not obey its wrong desires.

## Romans 6:13

Do not use any part of your body to do wrong.

God has brought you from death to life.

Give yourself to God.

Use every part of your body to do what is right.

## Romans 6:14

Sin will no longer be your master.

You live under God’s kindness rather than under the law.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul ask believers to use their bodies?

## Whom will you obey?

## Romans 6:15

We live under God’s kindness instead of under the law.

Does that give us a reason to sin?

Of course not!

## Romans 6:16

When you give yourself to obey a master, you become that master’s slave.

Serving sin leads to death.

Obeying God leads to what is right.

## Romans 6:17

Thank God!

Once you were slaves to sin.

Then you received teaching to guide your lives.

You obeyed it with your whole heart.

## Romans 6:18

God set you free from sin.

Now you serve what is right as fully as a slave serves a master.

## Romans 6:19

I use this familiar example because these things are hard for you to grasp.

Once you used your bodies to do shameful things.

One wrong act led to more wrongdoing.

Now use your bodies to do what is right.

This leads to a life set apart for God.

**Pause and answer**

What did the believers do with the teaching they received?

## What comes from serving God?

## Romans 6:20

When you were slaves to sin, you lived as if you owed nothing to what is right.

## Romans 6:21

What good came from the things you now feel ashamed of?

Those things lead to death.

## Romans 6:22

Now God has set you free from sin.

You belong to God and serve him.

The fruit of serving God is a life set apart for him.

In the end you receive life for ever.

**Help with these words**

Fruit grows from a plant. Here “fruit” means the result that grows out of the way a person lives.

## Romans 6:23

Death is the pay that sin gives.

But God gives us a gift.

In Christ Jesus our Lord, he gives us life for ever.

![Two coins, like pay a worker earns.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

**Help with these words**

Think of wages, the pay someone earns for work. Paul calls death the wages of sin. He calls life with Jesus a gift because God gives it freely.

**Pause and answer**

What gift does God give through Jesus?
