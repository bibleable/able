# Romans 15 — Welcome one another as Christ welcomed you

**About this chapter**

Paul asks believers from different peoples to welcome one another.

Then he tells his readers in Rome about a gift and a journey.

## Help one another grow strong

## Romans 15:1

Those of us with strong faith should help believers whose faith is weak.

Be patient with their difficulties.

Think beyond what pleases you.

## Romans 15:2

Each of us should do what pleases our neighbour and helps them grow stronger.

## Romans 15:3

Even Christ did not live to please himself.

The Scriptures say, “When people insulted you, God, their insults fell on me.”

## Romans 15:4

People wrote the Scriptures long ago to teach us.

Their words encourage us.

As we keep going with that encouragement, we can have hope.

## Romans 15:5

God gives you courage and helps you keep going.

May he help you live in harmony as you follow Christ Jesus.

## Romans 15:6

Then together you can praise the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

You can praise him with one voice.

**Help with these words**

“With one voice” means people joining together in praise.

**Pause and answer**

How can a believer with strong faith help another believer?

## People from every nation can praise God

## Romans 15:7

Welcome one another as Christ welcomed you.

This brings honour to God.

## Romans 15:8

Christ came to serve the Jewish people.

He showed that God tells the truth.

He confirmed the promises God made to their ancestors.

## Romans 15:9

The Gentiles too can praise God for his mercy.

The Scriptures say, “I will praise you among the Gentiles.

I will sing songs to honour your name.”

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Romans 15:10

The Scriptures also say, “You Gentiles, rejoice with God’s people!”

## Romans 15:11

And again, “All you Gentiles, praise the Lord!

All you peoples, tell how great he is!”

## Romans 15:12

Isaiah said, “The Root of Jesse will come.

He will rule over the Gentiles.

They will put their hope in him.”

**Help with these words**

“The Root of Jesse” is a name for the promised ruler from Jesse’s family. Paul is speaking about Jesus.

## Romans 15:13

God gives hope.

As you trust him, may he fill you with joy and peace.

May the Holy Spirit give you so much hope that it overflows.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives believers the power to hope?

## Paul explains why he wrote

## Romans 15:14

My brothers and sisters, I know you are full of goodness.

You know a great deal and can teach one another.

## Romans 15:15

I have written boldly to remind you of some things.

I do this because of the kindness God has shown me.

## Romans 15:16

God chose me to serve Christ Jesus among the Gentiles.

I share God’s good news as a priest serves God.

The Gentiles themselves are like an offering God gladly receives.

The Holy Spirit sets them apart for God.

**Help with these words**

A priest brought offerings to God. Paul uses this picture to describe people coming to belong to God.

## Romans 15:17

So I rejoice in Christ Jesus as I serve God.

## Romans 15:18

I will speak only of what Christ has done through me.

Through my words and actions, Christ has led Gentiles to obey God.

## Romans 15:19

God’s Spirit worked with power through signs and wonders.

I have told the whole good news about Christ from Jerusalem all the way around to Illyricum.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Paul credit for the good his work has done?

## Paul wants to reach people who have never heard

## Romans 15:20

I aim to tell the good news where people do not know Christ.

I do not want to build on a foundation someone else has laid.

**Help with these words**

A foundation is the base of a building. Paul means he wants to begin teaching in places where nobody has yet taught about Christ.

## Romans 15:21

The Scriptures say, “People who never learned about him will see.

Those who never heard will understand.”

## Romans 15:22

This work has often kept me from visiting you in Rome.

## Romans 15:23

Now I have no more places to begin this work in these regions.

For many years I have wanted to visit you.

## Romans 15:24

I hope to see you on my way to Spain.

I want to spend time with you first.

Then you can help me with what I need for the journey.

**Pause and answer**

Why had Paul often put off visiting Rome?

## Believers send a gift to Jerusalem

## Romans 15:25

Right now I am on my way to Jerusalem.

I am going to help God’s people there.

## Romans 15:26

Believers in Macedonia and Achaia gladly collected a gift.

It is for poor believers in Jerusalem.

![Two coins, like money collected to help believers in need.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

## Romans 15:27

They were glad to give, and they owed this help.

Gentile believers had shared in the spiritual gifts of the Jewish believers.

Now they should share what they have for daily needs.

## Romans 15:28

First I will make sure the gift reaches the believers in Jerusalem.

Once that work is done, I plan to go to Spain by way of Rome.

## Romans 15:29

I know I will come to you with the full blessing of Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Who was the gift from Macedonia and Achaia meant to help?

## Paul asks his friends to pray

## Romans 15:30

Brothers and sisters, remember our Lord Jesus Christ and the love the Spirit gives.

I urge you to join me in my struggle.

You can help by praying to God for me.

## Romans 15:31

Ask God to keep me safe from those in Judea who do not believe.

Ask that the believers in Jerusalem will welcome the help I bring.

## Romans 15:32

Then, if God wills, I can come to you with joy.

We can find fresh strength together.

## Romans 15:33

May the God of peace be with all of you.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

How can the believers in Rome help Paul before he arrives?
