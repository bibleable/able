# Romans 16 — Paul greets his friends in Rome

**About this chapter**

Paul names people he loves and remembers what they have done.

Some friends who are with Paul also send greetings.

## Paul asks the believers to welcome Phoebe

## Romans 16:1

I want you to meet Phoebe, our sister in the faith.

She serves the church in Cenchrea.

## Romans 16:2

Welcome Phoebe as someone who belongs to the Lord.

That is how God’s people should welcome each other.

Give her whatever help she needs.

She has helped many people, including me.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul ask the believers to help Phoebe?

## Friends who worked with Paul

## Romans 16:3

Give my greetings to Prisca and Aquila.

They work with me for Christ Jesus.

## Romans 16:4

They risked their lives to save mine.

I am grateful to them.

So are all the churches among the Gentiles.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Romans 16:5

Greet the church that meets in their house.

Greet my dear friend Epenetus.

He was the first person in the province of Asia to follow Christ.

## Romans 16:6

Greet Mary, who has worked very hard for you.

## Romans 16:7

Greet Andronicus and Junia.

They come from my people and were in prison with me.

They stand out among the apostles.

They belonged to Christ before I did.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone sent to speak for Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the church that Paul mentions here meet?

## Paul remembers more friends by name

## Romans 16:8

Greet Ampliatus, my dear friend in the Lord.

## Romans 16:9

Greet Urbanus, who serves Christ with us.

Greet my dear friend Stachys.

## Romans 16:10

Greet Apelles, who has proved faithful to Christ.

Greet the people of Aristobulus’s household.

## Romans 16:11

Greet Herodion, who comes from my people.

Greet those in Narcissus’s household who belong to the Lord.

## Romans 16:12

Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa.

These women have worked hard for the Lord.

Greet my dear friend Persis.

She too has worked very hard for the Lord.

## Romans 16:13

Greet Rufus, whom the Lord chose.

Greet his mother too.

She has cared for me like a mother.

**Pause and answer**

How did Rufus’s mother care for Paul?

## Greetings for believers who gather together

## Romans 16:14

Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon and Hermes.

Greet Patrobas, Hermas and the believers with them.

## Romans 16:15

Greet Philologus and Julia.

Greet Nereus and his sister.

Greet Olympas and all God’s people who are with them.

## Romans 16:16

Greet each other with a holy kiss.

All the churches of Christ send you their greetings.

**Help with these words**

Believers used a kiss as a greeting to show they belonged to one family in Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Who sends greetings to the believers in Rome?

## Watch out for people who cause division

## Romans 16:17

Brothers and sisters, watch out for people who split you into groups that fight.

They put things in your way that go against the teaching you learned.

Turn away from those people.

## Romans 16:18

They serve their own desires instead of our Lord Christ.

They use pleasing words and praise to trick people who trust them.

## Romans 16:19

Everyone has heard how you obey God.

That makes me glad.

I want you to recognise what is good and stay clear of evil.

## Romans 16:20

The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet.

May our Lord Jesus Christ show you his kindness.

**Help with these words**

“Under your feet” pictures a defeated enemy. Paul tells the believers that God will defeat Satan.

**Pause and answer**

How do the people Paul warns about try to trick others?

## Friends with Paul send their greetings

## Romans 16:21

Timothy works with me and sends his greetings.

Lucius, Jason and Sosipater send greetings too.

They come from my people.

## Romans 16:22

I am Tertius, the one who wrote down this letter.

I greet you in the Lord.

## Romans 16:23

Gaius sends greetings.

He has welcomed me and the whole church into his home.

Erastus, who looks after the city’s money, sends greetings.

Our brother Quartus greets you too.

## Paul gives glory to God

## Romans 16:25

God can make you strong through the good news I share about Jesus Christ.

For ages God kept his plan a mystery.

## Romans 16:26

Now God has made his plan known.

God lives for ever.

He commanded that people everywhere should hear this message through the prophets’ writings.

He wants people in every nation to trust him and obey him.

## Romans 16:27

Through Jesus Christ, glory belongs for ever to the only wise God!

Amen.
