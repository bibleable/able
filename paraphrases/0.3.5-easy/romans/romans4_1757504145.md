# Romans 4 — Abraham trusts God to keep his promise

**About this chapter**

Paul tells how Abraham trusted God.

He uses a worker’s pay to explain what people earn and what God freely gives.

## A worker earns pay

## Romans 4:1

Abraham was our ancestor.

What did he learn about being right with God?

## Romans 4:2

Suppose Abraham had earned his place by what he did.

Then he would have had something to boast about.

But he could never boast before God.

## Romans 4:3

What do the Scriptures say?

“Abraham believed God, and God counted him as right with him.”

## Romans 4:4

Think of someone who works for pay.

When the work is done, the employer owes the worker money.

That pay is something the worker has earned.

![Two coins, like money a worker might earn.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

## Romans 4:5

A person cannot earn the gift God gives.

God makes people right with him even though they have done wrong.

When a person trusts God, God counts that person as right with him.

**Pause and answer**

Why does an employer owe a worker pay?

## David describes the gift of forgiveness

## Romans 4:6

David also told how God blesses people.

God counts them as right with him apart from what they have done.

## Romans 4:7

David wrote, “God blesses the people whose wrongs he forgives.

He covers their sins.

## Romans 4:8

God blesses the person whose sin the Lord will never count against them.”

**Help with these words**

When David says God covers sins, he means God forgives them.

**Pause and answer**

What does David mean when he says God covers sins?

## Abraham trusted God before he received a sign

## Romans 4:9

Who can receive this blessing?

Only people who have received circumcision, or people without it too?

Remember, Abraham trusted God, and God counted him as right with him.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision is a sign on the body of a Jewish boy or man. It marks the agreement God made with his people.

## Romans 4:10

When did God count Abraham as right with him?

It was before Abraham received circumcision.

## Romans 4:11

Later Abraham received circumcision as a sign.

It confirmed that God had already accepted him through faith.

So Abraham is the father of believers who have never received circumcision.

God counts them as right with him too.

## Romans 4:12

Abraham is also the father of those who have received circumcision and follow his example.

They trust God as Abraham did before he received the sign.

**Help with these words**

Here “father” includes people who follow Abraham’s example of trusting God.

**Pause and answer**

Which came first for Abraham, trusting God or receiving the sign?

## God makes a promise to Abraham’s family

## Romans 4:13

God promised Abraham and his descendants that Abraham would inherit the world.

This promise did not depend on keeping the law.

It came because Abraham trusted God and God counted him as right with him.

**Help with these words**

Descendants are a person’s children and the generations who come after them. To inherit something means to receive what someone has set aside for you.

## Romans 4:14

Suppose only people who keep the law could inherit what God promised.

Then faith would have no use.

The promise would mean nothing.

## Romans 4:15

The law brings God’s anger when people break it.

Where there is no law, there is no law to break.

## Romans 4:16

So people receive the promise by trusting God.

It is a gift of his kindness.

All Abraham’s family can count on it.

This includes people who live under the law.

It also includes everyone who trusts God as Abraham did.

Abraham is the father of us all.

## Romans 4:17

The Scriptures tell us what God said to Abraham.

“I have made you a father of many nations.”

In God’s sight Abraham is our father.

Abraham trusted God, who gives life to the dead.

God speaks, and things that did not exist come into being.

**Pause and answer**

How can people receive what God promised?

## Abraham keeps trusting God

## Romans 4:18

Even when there seemed to be no hope, Abraham still hoped.

He trusted God and became the father of many nations.

God had told him, “That is how many descendants you will have.”

## Romans 4:19

Abraham knew how old his body was.

He was about a hundred years old.

He knew Sarah could no longer have children.

Still his faith did not grow weak.

## Romans 4:20

Abraham kept trusting God’s promise.

His faith grew stronger, and he praised God.

## Romans 4:21

Abraham was sure God could do what he had promised.

## Romans 4:22

That is why the Scriptures say God counted Abraham as right with him.

**Pause and answer**

What was Abraham sure God could do?

## Paul says these words are for us too

## Romans 4:23

The Scriptures say God counted Abraham as right with him.

Those words tell us more than what happened to Abraham.

## Romans 4:24

They are also for us who trust God.

God raised our Lord Jesus from the dead.

He will count us as right with him too.

## Romans 4:25

God handed Jesus over to die for the wrong things we did.

God raised Jesus to life to make us right with him.

Who raised Jesus from the dead?
