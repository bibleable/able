# Romans 10 — People hear the good news about Jesus

**About this chapter**

Paul prays for his own people and explains how anyone can call on Jesus.

He asks how people can believe good news they have never heard.

## Paul prays for Israel

## Romans 10:1

Brothers and sisters, I long for God to save my people Israel.

That is what I pray for.

## Romans 10:2

I know how eager my people are to serve God.

Yet they do not understand how God makes people right with him.

## Romans 10:3

They tried to make themselves right with God in their own way.

They did not accept God’s way of making people right with him.

## Romans 10:4

The law reaches its goal in Christ.

Through Christ, everyone who believes can be right with God.

What does Paul ask God to do for Israel?

## The message is near you

## Romans 10:5

Moses wrote about becoming right with God by keeping the law.

He said, “The person who does what the law says will live by it.”

## Romans 10:6

But what do the Scriptures say about trusting God?

“Do not ask yourself, ‘Who will go up to heaven?’”

That would mean someone had to bring Christ down.

## Romans 10:7

“Do not ask, ‘Who will go down into the deep?’”

That would mean someone had to bring Christ back from the dead.

## Romans 10:8

What do the Scriptures say instead?

“The message is near you.

It is in your mouth and in your heart.”

This is the message about faith that we bring you.

**Help with these words**

Paul explains the picture in the next verse. You can say the message aloud and believe it deep within you.

## Romans 10:9

Say openly, “Jesus is Lord.”

Believe in your heart that God raised Jesus from the dead.

If you do these things, God will save you.

## Romans 10:10

You trust Jesus in your heart, and God makes you right with him.

You say openly that Jesus is Lord, and God saves you.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say people believe in their hearts about Jesus?

## The same Lord hears everyone who calls

## Romans 10:11

The Scriptures say, “No one who trusts in him will be put to shame.”

## Romans 10:12

This is true for Jews and Greeks alike.

The same Lord is Lord of everyone.

He gives generously to all who call on him.

## Romans 10:13

The Scriptures say, “The Lord will save everyone who calls on his name.”

**Help with these words**

Calling on the Lord means asking him for help and trusting him to save you.

**Pause and answer**

Who can call on the Lord?

## Someone must bring the message

## Romans 10:14

How can people call on Jesus unless they trust him?

How can they trust him unless they hear about him?

How can they hear unless someone tells them?

## Romans 10:15

How can anyone bring the message unless someone sends them?

The Scriptures say, “How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!”

**Help with these words**

Imagine people waiting for news. They are glad when they see the messenger coming. That is why the writer calls the messenger’s feet beautiful.

## Romans 10:16

Yet some people did not welcome the good news.

Isaiah asked, “Lord, who has believed our message?”

## Romans 10:17

So faith comes when people hear.

What they hear is the message about Christ.

## God keeps reaching out

## Romans 10:18

I ask, “Did the people of Israel hear?”

Yes, they did.

The Scriptures say, “Their voices went out across the earth.

Their words reached the ends of the world.”

## Romans 10:19

Then I ask, “Did Israel understand?”

First, Moses told Israel what God had said.

“I will make you jealous through people you do not even count as a nation.

I will make you angry through a nation that lacks understanding.”

## Romans 10:20

Isaiah spoke God’s words boldly: “People who did not look for me found me.

I showed myself to people who did not ask for me.”

## Romans 10:21

Isaiah also told Israel what God said about them.

“All day long I held out my hands to my people.

They kept disobeying me and refusing to listen.”

**Help with these words**

Holding out hands is a picture of welcome. God kept inviting his people to turn to him.
