# Romans 12 — Show love by what you do

**About this chapter**

Paul asks believers to use the gifts God has given them.

He describes how to love friends, strangers and even enemies.

## Give your whole life to God

## Romans 12:1

Brothers and sisters, remember the mercy God has shown you.

I urge you to offer your bodies to God as a living sacrifice.

Set yourselves apart for God and do what pleases him.

This is how you worship him.

**Help with these words**

A sacrifice is an offering to God. A living sacrifice means giving yourself to God in the things you do each day.

## Romans 12:2

Do not simply copy the ways of the world around you.

Let God change you by making your thinking new.

Then you can recognise what God wants and choose it.

What God wants is good, pleasing and perfect.

**Pause and answer**

How can you offer yourself to God each day?

## Many parts belong to one body

## Romans 12:3

God has been kind to me, so I say this to each of you.

Think honestly about yourself.

Do not think you are better than you are.

Remember the faith God has given you.

## Romans 12:4

Each of us has one body with many parts.

Those parts do different jobs.

## Romans 12:5

In the same way, we are many people.

Because we belong to Christ, we form one body.

Each of us belongs to the others.

**Help with these words**

Think of your hand and your foot. They do different things, but both are parts of your body. Paul says believers belong together like that.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul compare believers to parts of a body?

## Use the gift God gave you

## Romans 12:6

God is kind and gives us different gifts.

If your gift is speaking a message from God, speak according to the faith God gave you.

## Romans 12:7

If your gift is serving others, serve them.

If your gift is teaching, teach.

## Romans 12:8

If you can encourage others, encourage them.

If you give to others, give generously.

If you lead, give the work your full attention.

If you show mercy, do it gladly.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul ask a leader to use that gift?

## Love people as family

## Romans 12:9

Mean it when you show love.

Hate what is evil.

Hold on to what is good.

## Romans 12:10

Love each other as brothers and sisters.

Take the lead in showing honour to one another.

## Romans 12:11

Stay eager to serve the Lord.

Serve him with your whole heart.

## Romans 12:12

Let hope bring you joy.

Be patient when life is hard.

Keep praying.

## Romans 12:13

Share what you have with believers in need.

Welcome people into your home.

**Pause and answer**

How can you help a believer who needs something you have?

## Share in other people’s joys and sorrows

## Romans 12:14

Ask God to bless people who hurt you for your faith.

Ask for good things for them.

Do not curse them.

## Romans 12:15

Be glad with people who are glad.

Cry with people who are crying.

## Romans 12:16

Live in harmony with one another.

Set pride aside.

Spend time with people others look down on.

Do not think you always know best.

**Pause and answer**

What can you do when someone is crying?

## Answer harm with good

## Romans 12:17

When someone does wrong to you, do not hurt them in return.

Think carefully about what everyone can recognise as right.

## Romans 12:18

Do what you can to live at peace with everyone.

## Romans 12:19

Dear friends, do not take revenge.

Leave it to God to judge the wrong.

The Scriptures say, “The Lord says, ‘It is mine to repay.

I will repay.’”

## Romans 12:20

The Scriptures also say, “If your enemy is hungry, give them food.

If your enemy is thirsty, give them a drink.

Doing this is like piling burning coals on their head.”

![A drinking cup like one you could offer to someone who is thirsty.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Help with these words**

The burning coals are a word picture. Kindness can make someone feel ashamed of the harm they have done. Paul asks you to give your enemy food or a drink.

## Romans 12:21

Do not let evil defeat you.

Defeat evil by doing good.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask you to do for a thirsty enemy?
