# Ecclesiastes 8 — What even the wise cannot know

**About this chapter**

The Teacher watches a king give orders and a city praise people who did wrong. He asks how much even a wise person can understand.

## Wisdom before a king

## Ecclesiastes 8:1

Who is like a wise person?

Who can explain what something means?

Wisdom makes a person’s face light up.

Even a stern look can soften.

## Ecclesiastes 8:2

I say, obey the king’s command.

Remember the promise you made before God.

## Ecclesiastes 8:3

Take your time before leaving the king’s presence.

Do not keep pressing a cause you know is wrong.

The king can do whatever he wants.

## Ecclesiastes 8:4

The king’s word carries the highest authority.

Who can ask him, “What are you doing?”

## Knowing when to act

## Ecclesiastes 8:5

Those who obey his command will come to no harm.

A wise person knows when and how to act.

## Ecclesiastes 8:6

There is a right time and way to act in every matter.

Even so, troubles can weigh heavily on a person.

## Ecclesiastes 8:7

Nobody knows what will happen next.

Who can tell someone what the future holds?

## Ecclesiastes 8:8

Nobody can hold back the wind.

Nobody can command the day of their death.

A soldier cannot leave service during a war.

In the same way, evil holds on to those who practice it.

## When justice seems far away

## Ecclesiastes 8:9

I thought hard about all the things people do here on earth.

I saw one person use power over another.

That power could even harm the person using it.

## Ecclesiastes 8:10

I saw people bury the wicked.

Those who died had come and gone from the holy place.

In that very city, people praised them.

That too seemed empty to me.

## Ecclesiastes 8:11

A court may decide to punish someone for a crime.

When the punishment takes a long time, others become determined to do wrong.

## What happens to good and wicked people

## Ecclesiastes 8:12

Someone may do wrong a hundred times and still live long.

Yet I know it will go well for people who deeply respect God.

They remember whose presence they stand in.

## Ecclesiastes 8:13

The wicked have no such respect for God.

Things will go badly for them.

Their days will not keep growing longer like an evening shadow.

## Ecclesiastes 8:14

Here is something else on earth that seems senseless.

Good people sometimes suffer what wrongdoers deserve.

Wrongdoers sometimes receive the good that honest people deserve.

I cannot make sense of that.

## Joy within the limits of what we know

## Ecclesiastes 8:15

So I say it is good to enjoy life.

Eat, drink, and be glad.

That joy can stay with you as you work through the days God gives you.

## Ecclesiastes 8:16

I set my mind on learning wisdom.

I watched the work people do on earth.

A person can go without sleep day and night trying to understand it.

## Ecclesiastes 8:17

I looked at all that God does.

No one can fully understand his work here on earth.

However hard we search, we cannot explain it all.

Even wise people who say they know cannot grasp it all.
