# Ecclesiastes 9 — The wise man nobody remembered

**About this chapter**

The Teacher watches a race and tells of a city under attack. Strength and skill do not always decide what happens.

## What all people share

## Ecclesiastes 9:1

I thought hard about all this.

Wise people and those who do right are in God’s hands.

So is all their work.

Nobody knows whether love or hatred lies ahead.

## Ecclesiastes 9:2

Everyone dies.

This is true for good people and for those who do wrong.

It is true for people counted clean or unclean for worship.

Some bring God an offering, and some do not.

Some make solemn promises, and some refuse.

All face the same end.

## Ecclesiastes 9:3

Here is something terrible I see on earth.

The same end comes to us all.

While people live, their hearts are full of evil and wild thoughts.

Then they join the dead.

## Hope for the living

## Ecclesiastes 9:4

Yet anyone still alive has hope.

Even a living dog is better off than a dead lion.

## Ecclesiastes 9:5

Living people know they will die.

The dead know nothing.

They receive no more reward.

In time people forget them.

## Ecclesiastes 9:6

Their love, hatred, and envy are gone.

They no longer take part in anything people do on earth.

## Enjoy the days you have

## Ecclesiastes 9:7

Go and enjoy your bread.

Drink your wine with a glad heart.

God has already accepted what you do.

## Ecclesiastes 9:8

Wear your white clothes.

Put oil on your head.

Dress as you would for a celebration.

## Ecclesiastes 9:9

Enjoy life with the wife you love.

God has given you these brief days together on earth.

This is your share of joy amid all your hard work.

## Ecclesiastes 9:10

Give your full strength to the work you find to do.

You are going to Sheol, the place of the dead.

There no one works or plans or uses knowledge or wisdom.

## What no one can predict

## Ecclesiastes 9:11

I saw something else.

The fastest runner can lose the race.

The strongest army can lose the battle.

Wise people can go hungry.

Clever people can stay poor.

Skilled people can go unnoticed.

Timing and chance affect everyone.

## Ecclesiastes 9:12

Nobody knows when trouble will come.

A net closes on fish.

A trap catches birds.

Trouble can catch people just as suddenly.

**Look at the net**

## A small city and a powerful king

## Ecclesiastes 9:13

I also saw what wisdom could do here on earth.

What I saw impressed me greatly.

## Ecclesiastes 9:14

A small city had few people.

A powerful king surrounded it with his army.

The king built great ramps against the city walls.

## Ecclesiastes 9:15

A poor man lived in that city.

He was wise, and his wisdom saved the city.

Yet nobody remembered him.

## Ecclesiastes 9:16

I thought, “Wisdom is better than strength.” Yet people look down on a poor man’s wisdom.

They ignore what he says.

## Ecclesiastes 9:17

The quiet words of a wise person deserve a hearing.

They carry more weight than a ruler shouting among fools.

## Ecclesiastes 9:18

Wisdom is better than weapons of war.

Yet one person doing wrong can undo much good.

**Pause and answer**

Who saved the small city?
