# Ecclesiastes 12 — Remember the One who made you

**About this chapter**

The Teacher pictures a house growing quiet and a water jar breaking. He speaks to a young person about growing old and remembering God.

## Remember God while you are young

## Ecclesiastes 12:1

Remember your Creator while you are young.

Your Creator is the God who made you.

Hard days will come.

You may say of those years, “I find no joy in them.”

## Ecclesiastes 12:2

Remember him before the sun, moon, and stars grow dim.

Picture clouds coming back even after the rain.

## A house grows quiet

## Ecclesiastes 12:3

Picture a house whose guards tremble.

Strong men bend low.

So few workers are left that the grinding stops.

People at the windows struggle to see.

The Teacher uses the house to describe growing old.

## Ecclesiastes 12:4

The doors to the street close.

The sound of grinding grain fades.

A bird’s call wakes you.

The voices of singers grow faint.

## Ecclesiastes 12:5

High places frighten you.

The road feels dangerous.

The almond tree fills with pale flowers.

The grasshopper can hardly jump.

The caper berry dries up.

A person goes to their lasting home in death.

People walk the streets in sorrow.

## The broken jar

## Ecclesiastes 12:6

Remember God before the silver cord breaks.

Picture a gold bowl crushed and a jar broken at a spring.

The wheel for drawing water breaks at the well.

These pictures show a life coming to its end.

![A brown clay jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the jar**

Imagine carrying water home in this jar. If the jar shatters, it can no longer carry water. That is one picture the Teacher uses for the end of life.

## Ecclesiastes 12:7

The body returns to the dust it came from.

The spirit returns to God, who gave it.

## Ecclesiastes 12:8

“Everything slips away,” says the Teacher.

“What can we hold on to?

Everything slips away!”

## How the Teacher chose his words

## Ecclesiastes 12:9

The Teacher was wise and shared what he knew.

He searched for many wise sayings.

He thought about them and put them in order.

## Ecclesiastes 12:10

The Teacher looked for words people would enjoy hearing.

He took care to write down what was true.

## Ecclesiastes 12:11

Wise words are like pointed sticks that guide cattle forward.

Gathered together, wise sayings are like nails driven in firmly.

One Shepherd puts them in place.

His words give people something firm to hold on to.

## Ecclesiastes 12:12

My son, take this warning to heart.

People can go on writing books without end.

Long study wears the body out.

## The final words

## Ecclesiastes 12:13

Now you have heard it all.

Show deep respect for God and keep his commands.

This is what everyone should do.

## Ecclesiastes 12:14

God will judge everything we do, good or evil.

He will judge even the things we keep hidden.

**Pause and answer**

Who does the Teacher ask you to remember while you are young?
