# Ecclesiastes 5 — Listen and keep your promises

**About this chapter**

The Teacher asks people to listen before speaking to God. He also notices who sleeps peacefully after work and who lies awake.

## In God’s house

## Ecclesiastes 5:1

Take care when you enter God’s house.

Come close and listen.

Foolish people bring offerings without realizing they are doing wrong.

## Ecclesiastes 5:2

Think before you speak to God.

God is in heaven, and you are on earth.

Let your words be few.

## Ecclesiastes 5:3

Many worries can fill your sleep with dreams.

Too much talking can fill your mouth with foolish words.

## When you make a promise

## Ecclesiastes 5:4

A vow is a serious promise.

When you make one to God, keep it without delay.

God takes no pleasure in foolish people who put it off.

## Ecclesiastes 5:5

It is better to make no promise than to make one and break it.

## Ecclesiastes 5:6

Careless words can lead you into sin.

Do not tell the messenger, “My promise was a mistake.” Why make God angry with your words?

He could destroy what you worked to build.

## Ecclesiastes 5:7

A pile of dreams can come to nothing.

So can a flood of words.

Show deep respect for God.

## Who gains from the fields?

## Ecclesiastes 5:8

You may see officials mistreat poor people and deny them justice.

Do not be surprised.

Each official answers to someone higher up.

Others hold power over them both.

## Ecclesiastes 5:9

Everyone takes a share of what the land grows.

Even the king gains from the fields.

## When money never seems enough

## Ecclesiastes 5:10

Someone who loves money never has enough.

However much comes in, they want more.

What lasting good does that do?

## Ecclesiastes 5:11

The more goods someone owns, the more people come to use them.

What does the owner gain except the sight of them?

## Ecclesiastes 5:12

A worker sleeps well after a large meal or a small one.

A rich person’s many possessions can keep them awake.

## What can we carry away?

## Ecclesiastes 5:13

I have seen something deeply wrong here on earth.

Someone holds on to wealth until it harms the owner.

## Ecclesiastes 5:14

Someone else loses a fortune when a business fails.

Then a son is born.

The father has nothing left to give him.

## Ecclesiastes 5:15

We come naked from our mother’s womb.

We leave this world as we came.

Our hands can carry away nothing we earned.

## Ecclesiastes 5:16

This too is painful.

We leave with exactly as much as we brought.

What did we gain by working for the wind?

## Ecclesiastes 5:17

Day after day that person eats in darkness.

Sorrow, sickness, and anger fill those days.

## Joy in the days God gives

## Ecclesiastes 5:18

Here is what I have found good.

Eat, drink, and enjoy your work.

God gives you a few days here on earth.

These things are yours to enjoy in that time.

## Ecclesiastes 5:19

When God gives someone wealth and possessions, he also gives the power to enjoy them.

Accepting your share and finding joy in work are gifts from God.

## Ecclesiastes 5:20

Then you seldom stop to count the days of your life.

God keeps your heart busy with joy.
