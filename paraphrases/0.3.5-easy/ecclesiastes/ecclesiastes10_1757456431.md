# Ecclesiastes 10 — A little foolishness

**About this chapter**

The Teacher notices a jar of perfume, a worker with an ax, and a house with a leaking roof. Each helps him explain a choice people make.

## Dead flies in the perfume

## Ecclesiastes 10:1

Dead flies give sweet perfume a rotten smell.

In the same way, one foolish act can spoil the honor a wise person has earned.

![A small perfume jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

**Find the opening**

Imagine a fly dropping through that small opening. Even something tiny can spoil a whole jar of sweet oil.

## Ecclesiastes 10:2

A wise heart turns right.

A foolish heart turns left.

Picture two people choosing opposite paths.

## Ecclesiastes 10:3

Even on an ordinary walk, a fool shows poor judgment.

Everyone on the road can see it.

## When a ruler makes a mistake

## Ecclesiastes 10:4

Suppose your ruler gets angry with you.

Stay at your post.

A calm response can settle even a serious matter.

## Ecclesiastes 10:5

I have seen something go badly wrong here on earth.

The mistake began with a ruler.

## Ecclesiastes 10:6

The ruler gave foolish people high positions.

Rich people sat in low places.

## Ecclesiastes 10:7

I saw slaves riding horses.

Princes walked beside them like slaves.

## Care and skill at work

## Ecclesiastes 10:8

Someone who digs a pit may fall into it.

Someone who breaks through a wall may disturb a snake and get bitten.

## Ecclesiastes 10:9

A worker cutting stone can get hurt by the stone.

A worker splitting logs can also get hurt.

## Ecclesiastes 10:10

A blunt ax makes you work harder with every swing.

A skilled worker sharpens the blade.

Skill helps the work go well.

## Ecclesiastes 10:11

Someone may know how to calm a snake.

If the snake bites first, that skill earns the worker nothing.

## Where foolish talk leads

## Ecclesiastes 10:12

Wise people speak with kindness.

Foolish people bring ruin on themselves by what they say.

## Ecclesiastes 10:13

The fool begins by talking nonsense.

By the end, the words are wild and harmful.

## Ecclesiastes 10:14

Still the fool goes on talking.

Nobody knows what will happen next.

Who can tell a person what will happen after they are gone?

## Ecclesiastes 10:15

A fool works until he wears himself out.

He cannot even find the road into town.

## How rulers spend their mornings

## Ecclesiastes 10:16

What trouble for a land where the king is still a boy and the princes start feasting first thing in the morning!

## Ecclesiastes 10:17

How fortunate the land whose king comes from a noble family.

Its princes eat at the right time.

They eat to gain strength and stay sober.

## A leaking roof and a private word

## Ecclesiastes 10:18

If nobody bothers to repair the roof, it sags.

Leave the house untended, and rain comes through.

## Ecclesiastes 10:19

People make a feast to laugh together.

Wine brings cheer.

Money pays for it all and meets all kinds of needs.

## Ecclesiastes 10:20

Do not curse the king even in your thoughts.

Do not curse the rich even in your bedroom.

Imagine a bird carrying your words away on its wings.

What you say in private can reach someone far away.

**Pause and answer**

What does a skilled worker do when the ax is blunt?
