# Ecclesiastes 6 — Having enough and still wanting more

**About this chapter**

The Teacher pictures a man who owns everything he wants. A stranger gets to enjoy it all. The Teacher asks what that man has gained.

## When someone else enjoys the wealth

## Ecclesiastes 6:1

I have seen another sad thing happen on earth.

It weighs heavily on people.

## Ecclesiastes 6:2

God gives someone wealth, possessions, and honor.

The man has everything he wants.

Yet God does not let him enjoy it.

A stranger enjoys it instead.

This seems empty to me, and it brings great pain.

## Many years without rest

## Ecclesiastes 6:3

Imagine a man with a hundred children.

He lives many years but never enjoys his good things.

When he dies, he does not even receive a proper burial.

I think a baby born dead is better off than that man.

## Ecclesiastes 6:4

The baby comes into the world for such a brief moment.

The child passes into darkness.

People never come to know the child’s name.

## Ecclesiastes 6:5

The child never sees the sun or knows the world.

Yet the child has more rest than that man.

## Ecclesiastes 6:6

Suppose the man lives a thousand years, then a thousand more.

What good are those years if he cannot enjoy what he has?

Both he and the child go to the same place when they die.

## An appetite that returns

## Ecclesiastes 6:7

People work to put food in their mouths.

Yet their hunger keeps coming back.

## Ecclesiastes 6:8

So what advantage does a wise person have over a fool?

What does a poor person gain by knowing how to act among others?

## Ecclesiastes 6:9

Better to enjoy what you see before you than to keep wishing for more.

Always wanting more is like chasing the wind.

Your hands stay empty.

![Nine silver coins together and one coin apart.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Look at the coins**

Imagine owning the group of nine. Now imagine thinking only about the coin beside them. The Teacher asks what happens when all we want is more.

## A life as brief as a shadow

## Ecclesiastes 6:10

What exists already had a name long ago.

People know what it means to be human.

No one can win an argument with someone stronger.

## Ecclesiastes 6:11

Words pile up, but they achieve less and less.

What does anyone gain?

## Ecclesiastes 6:12

Our few days pass like a shadow.

Who knows what is good for us in that short time?

Who can tell us what will happen on earth after we are gone?
