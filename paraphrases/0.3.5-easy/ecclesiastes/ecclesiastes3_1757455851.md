# Ecclesiastes 3 — A time for everything

**About this chapter**

The Teacher sets one time beside another. He watches people plant and pull up plants, cry and laugh, work and enjoy a meal.

## The changing seasons of life

## Ecclesiastes 3:1

Everything has its season.

There is a time for everything people do on earth.

## Ecclesiastes 3:2

A time to be born and a time to die.

A time to plant and a time to pull up plants.

## Ecclesiastes 3:3

A time to kill and a time to heal.

A time to tear down and a time to build.

## Ecclesiastes 3:4

A time to cry and a time to laugh.

A time to grieve and a time to dance.

## Ecclesiastes 3:5

A time to scatter stones and a time to gather them.

A time to hold someone close and a time to step back.

## Ecclesiastes 3:6

A time to search and a time to accept a loss.

A time to keep things and a time to throw them away.

## Ecclesiastes 3:7

A time to tear cloth and a time to mend it.

A time to stay quiet and a time to speak.

## Ecclesiastes 3:8

A time to love and a time to hate.

A time for war and a time for peace.

**Picture the torn cloth**

Trace the edge of the cloth. Imagine a tear along that edge. In verse 7, mending means sewing the torn edges together again.

## The work God gives us

## Ecclesiastes 3:9

What does a worker gain from all that hard work?

## Ecclesiastes 3:10

I have seen the work God gives people.

It keeps them busy and weighs on them.

## Ecclesiastes 3:11

God makes everything beautiful in its own time.

He gives us a sense of eternity, of time without end.

Yet we cannot see all his work from beginning to end.

## Ecclesiastes 3:12

I know this much.

The best thing we can do is enjoy life and do good while we live.

## Ecclesiastes 3:13

We can eat and drink.

We can enjoy the work we do.

That joy is a gift from God.

## What lasts

## Ecclesiastes 3:14

I know that what God does lasts forever.

No one can add to it or take anything away.

God acts this way so people will deeply respect him.

## Ecclesiastes 3:15

What happens now has happened before.

What is still to come has happened before too.

God calls people to account for what has already happened.

## Where people should find justice

## Ecclesiastes 3:16

I saw something else here on earth.

Where judges should have been fair, I saw wrongdoing.

Where people should have done right, I saw evil.

## Ecclesiastes 3:17

I told myself that God will judge everyone.

He will judge people who do right and people who do wrong.

Every act has its time.

## The breath we share

## Ecclesiastes 3:18

I thought about how God tests us.

He lets us see how much we are like animals.

## Ecclesiastes 3:19

People and animals breathe the same air.

Animals die, and so do we.

In this we have no advantage over them.

What lasting gain is there?

## Ecclesiastes 3:20

We all go to the same place.

People and animals come from dust.

We all return to dust.

## Ecclesiastes 3:21

Does a person’s spirit rise upward?

Does an animal’s spirit go down into the earth?

Who knows?

## Ecclesiastes 3:22

So I saw that the best thing people can do is enjoy their work.

This is their share in life.

Who can show them what will happen after they are gone?
