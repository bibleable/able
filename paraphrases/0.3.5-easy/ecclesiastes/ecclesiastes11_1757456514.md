# Ecclesiastes 11 — Sow your seed in the morning

**About this chapter**

The Teacher pictures a farmer looking up at the sky. The farmer must decide when to plant, even without knowing what the weather will do.

## What you send out

## Ecclesiastes 11:1

Picture sending your bread out across the water.

Many days later, you find it again.

You had to let it go before it could come back.

## Ecclesiastes 11:2

Divide what you have into seven shares, or even eight.

You do not know what trouble might come upon the land.

## Wind, rain, and planting

## Ecclesiastes 11:3

When clouds fill with water, rain falls on the ground.

A tree may fall north or south.

It stays where it falls.

## Ecclesiastes 11:4

A farmer who keeps watching the wind never plants.

A farmer who keeps watching the clouds never gathers the crop.

## Ecclesiastes 11:5

You do not know where the wind will go.

You cannot see how a baby’s bones form inside its mother.

In the same way, you cannot fully understand God’s work.

God makes everything.

## Ecclesiastes 11:6

Plant your seed in the morning.

Keep working in the evening too.

You cannot know which planting will grow well.

Perhaps both will grow equally well.

**Find the grain**

Look at the seeds at the top of the stalk. Imagine the farmer holding a seed before planting it. At that moment, the farmer cannot see the harvest yet.

## Enjoy the daylight

## Ecclesiastes 11:7

How good the daylight is!

What a joy to see the sun.

## Ecclesiastes 11:8

If you live many years, enjoy every one.

Remember that many dark days will come too.

What lies ahead is as hard to hold as a breath.

## A word to the young

## Ecclesiastes 11:9

Enjoy being young.

Take delight in these days.

Follow what your heart desires and what your eyes see.

Remember that God will judge all these choices.

## Ecclesiastes 11:10

Let the worries in your heart go.

Put away the pain that troubles your body.

Youth and strength pass quickly.

**Pause and answer**

Why does the Teacher tell the farmer to plant in the morning and keep working in the evening?
