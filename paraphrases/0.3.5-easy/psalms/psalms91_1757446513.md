# Psalm 91 — I will be with him in trouble

**About this psalm**

The singer calls God a safe place to stay. He describes God’s care as a bird sheltering its young. At the end, God speaks.

## A safe place with God

## Psalm 91:1

Anyone who lives in the shelter of God Most High will rest in the shade of the Almighty.

**Names for God**

Most High and Almighty are names that speak of God’s greatness and power.

## Psalm 91:2

I will say to the LORD, “You are my safe place and my strong fort.

You are my God. I trust you.”

## A bird shelters its young

## Psalm 91:3

God will rescue you from the bird catcher’s trap.

He will save you from deadly sickness.

## Psalm 91:4

He will cover you with his feathers.

Under his wings you will find safety.

He keeps his word. That protects you like a shield and a strong wall.

**Why talk about wings?**

A bird can spread its wings over its young. The singer wants you to picture how close God is to those who trust him.

## Psalm 91:5

You will have no fear of terrors at night.

You will have no fear of arrows flying by day.

## Psalm 91:6

You need not fear the sickness that moves through the dark.

You need not fear the disaster that strikes at noon.

## God sends his angels

## Psalm 91:7

A thousand may fall at your side.

Ten thousand may fall on your right.

Yet the harm will not reach you.

## Psalm 91:8

You will see it with your own eyes.

You will see what happens to those who do evil when God judges them.

## Psalm 91:9

You have made the LORD your home.

He is God Most High, my own safe place.

## Psalm 91:10

So no evil will come upon you.

No deadly sickness will come near your tent.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Think of someone resting inside. The singer uses the place where a person sleeps to describe God’s care close to home.

## Psalm 91:11

God will tell his angels to guard you wherever you go.

## Psalm 91:12

They will lift you up in their hands.

Your foot will not strike a stone.

## Psalm 91:13

You will walk over lions and cobras.

Young lions and snakes will be under your feet.

## God promises to answer

## Psalm 91:14

God says, “He loves me, so I will rescue him.

He knows my name, so I will keep him safe.

## Psalm 91:15

When he calls, I will answer.

I will be with him in trouble.

I will rescue him and give him honor.

## Psalm 91:16

I will give him a long, full life.

I will show him how I save him.”

**Pause and answer**

What does God say he will do when the person who loves him calls?
