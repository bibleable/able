# Psalm 78 — Tell the children what God has done

**About this psalm**

Asaph tells the story his people heard from their families. He remembers help the people received and times they refused to trust God. He wants their children to hear the whole story.

## Tell the story to the children

## Psalm 78:1

A Maskil of Asaph.

My people, listen to what I teach.

Pay attention to my words.

**A word in the title**

Maskil is an old word used in several song titles. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 78:2

I will teach you through stories.

I will speak about things hidden since long ago.

## Psalm 78:3

We have heard these things and know them.

Our fathers told us about them.

## Psalm 78:4

We will tell their children too.

We will tell the next generation why the LORD deserves praise.

They will hear about his power and his wonderful acts.

## Psalm 78:5

God gave rules to Jacob’s family.

He gave Israel his law.

He told our fathers to teach it to their children.

## Psalm 78:6

Then even children yet to be born would know it.

They in turn would tell their own children.

## Psalm 78:7

Then they would trust God.

They would remember what he did and obey his commands.

## Psalm 78:8

Our fathers stubbornly fought against God.

They did not stay true to him.

Their children can choose a different way.

## Ephraim forgets what God did

## Psalm 78:9

The men of Ephraim carried bows.

But on the day of battle, they turned back.

## Psalm 78:10

They broke their covenant with God.

They refused to live by his law.

**What is a covenant?**

A covenant is a binding promise. The people had promised to obey God.

## Psalm 78:11

They forgot what God had done.

They forgot the wonders he had shown them.

## A path through the sea and water from rock

## Psalm 78:12

Their fathers saw God do wonders in Egypt, near Zoan.

## Psalm 78:13

God split the sea and led his people through.

He made the water stand up like a wall.

## Psalm 78:14

By day he led them with a cloud.

All night he led them with light from a fire.

## Psalm 78:15

He split rocks in the wilderness.

He gave them so much water it seemed like a sea.

## Psalm 78:16

Streams burst from the rock.

He made water flow down like rivers.

## The people demand food

## Psalm 78:17

Still the people sinned against him.

There in the desert they turned against God Most High.

## Psalm 78:18

They chose to test God.

They demanded the food they wanted.

## Psalm 78:19

They spoke against him and asked, “Can God set a table for us here in the wilderness?

## Psalm 78:20

He struck the rock and water poured out in rushing streams.

But can he give us bread?

Can he bring his people meat?”

## Psalm 78:21

The LORD heard them and grew angry.

His anger burned like fire against Jacob’s people, Israel.

## Psalm 78:22

They did not believe God.

They did not trust him to save them.

## God sends bread and birds

## Psalm 78:23

Even so, he gave orders to the clouds above.

He opened the doors of heaven.

## Psalm 78:24

God sent down manna like rain for them to eat.

He gave them grain from heaven.

**What is manna?**

Manna is the name the people gave the food God sent them in the wilderness.

## Psalm 78:25

People ate the bread of angels.

God sent them plenty of food.

## Psalm 78:26

He made the east wind blow across the sky.

By his power he sent the south wind.

## Psalm 78:27

He sent down meat as plentiful as dust.

Birds came in numbers like grains of sand on the shore.

## Psalm 78:28

God made the birds fall inside the camp, all around the people’s tents.

## Psalm 78:29

The people ate until they were full.

God gave them the food they wanted.

## The people still struggle to trust God

## Psalm 78:30

They still wanted more.

The food was still in their mouths.

## Psalm 78:31

Then God’s anger burned against them.

He killed their strongest men and struck down Israel’s young men.

## Psalm 78:32

Even after this, the people kept doing wrong.

They saw his wonders and still did not believe.

## Psalm 78:33

So he let their days come to nothing.

Their years ended in sudden terror.

## Psalm 78:34

When God killed some of them, the others looked for him.

They turned back and searched for God.

## Psalm 78:35

They remembered that God was their rock.

God Most High was the one who set them free.

## Psalm 78:36

But they tried to fool him with their words.

They lied to him.

## Psalm 78:37

Deep inside, they did not stay true to him.

They broke their covenant with God.

## God remembers how short their lives are

## Psalm 78:38

Yet God showed them mercy.

He forgave their sins and spared their lives.

Many times he held back his anger.

## Psalm 78:39

He remembered they were only human.

Their lives were like a breeze that passes and never returns.

## Psalm 78:40

How often they disobeyed God in the wilderness!

How often they caused him sorrow there!

## Psalm 78:41

Again and again they put God to the test.

They made the Holy One of Israel angry.

## Psalm 78:42

They forgot his power.

They forgot the day he freed them from their enemy.

## What God did in Egypt

## Psalm 78:43

In Egypt he had given signs of his power.

He did wonders in the fields near Zoan.

## Psalm 78:44

He turned Egypt’s rivers to blood.

The Egyptians could no longer drink from the streams.

## Psalm 78:45

He sent swarms of flies that ate at them.

He sent frogs that ruined things around them.

## Psalm 78:46

Grasshoppers ate their crops.

Locusts ate the food they had worked to grow.

## Psalm 78:47

Hailstones killed their vines.

Icy rain killed their fig trees.

## Psalm 78:48

He left their cattle out in the hail.

Lightning struck their farm animals.

## God frees his people

## Psalm 78:49

God let his fierce anger loose against them.

He sent a group of angels who brought destruction.

## Psalm 78:50

He made a way for his anger.

He let a deadly sickness take their lives.

## Psalm 78:51

He struck down every firstborn son in Egypt.

Those sons had been the strength of the families of Ham.

**Who are the families of Ham?**

Here the song uses this family name for the people of Egypt.

## Psalm 78:52

Then God led his own people out like sheep.

He guided them through the wilderness like a flock.

## Psalm 78:53

God led them safely, so they were not afraid.

But the sea swept over their enemies.

## The tribes settle in the land

## Psalm 78:54

He brought his people to his holy land.

He brought them to the mountain he had won by his power.

## Psalm 78:55

He drove out nations ahead of them.

He gave each tribe of Israel a share of the land.

He gave them homes where those nations had lived.

## Psalm 78:56

But the people tested God Most High and disobeyed him.

They did not keep his commands.

## Psalm 78:57

They turned away just as their fathers had done.

They were like a crooked bow that misses its mark.

## Psalm 78:58

They made God angry by worshiping at shrines on the hills.

They gave their love to idols, and God was jealous.

## Loss and grief at Shiloh

## Psalm 78:59

God heard and grew very angry.

He turned away from Israel completely.

## Psalm 78:60

He left his holy tent at Shiloh.

He had set up that tent to live among his people.

## Psalm 78:61

He let enemies carry away the sign of his strength and glory.

**What did the enemies carry away?**

They captured the ark, the sacred chest that reminded Israel of God’s presence.

## Psalm 78:62

God was angry with his own people.

He let their enemies kill them with swords.

## Psalm 78:63

Fire took the lives of the young men.

The young women heard no wedding songs.

## Psalm 78:64

Enemies killed the priests with swords.

Their widows could not even mourn for them.

## God chooses Judah and David

## Psalm 78:65

Then the Lord was like someone waking from sleep.

He rose like a mighty warrior roused from wine.

## Psalm 78:66

He drove his enemies back.

He put them to lasting shame.

## Psalm 78:67

He turned away from Joseph’s tent.

He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim.

## Psalm 78:68

He chose the tribe of Judah.

He chose Mount Zion, the mountain he loved.

## Psalm 78:69

He built his holy place as high as the heavens.

He made it firm like the earth he set in place forever.

## Psalm 78:70

God chose David as his servant.

He took David from the sheep pens.

## Psalm 78:71

David had looked after mother sheep.

God brought him to lead his own people, the family of Jacob, also called Israel.

## Psalm 78:72

David cared for them as a shepherd cares for sheep.

He led them with an honest heart and skillful hands.
