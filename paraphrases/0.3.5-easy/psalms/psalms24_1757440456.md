# Psalm 24 — Who is the King of Glory?

**About this psalm**

David sings about the LORD who made the world. Near the end of the song, a call rings out twice for the gates to open.

## The world belongs to the LORD

## Psalm 24:1

David wrote this song.

The earth and everything in it belong to the LORD.

The world is his, and so is everyone who lives here.

## Psalm 24:2

The LORD set the earth firmly above the seas.

He made it stand over the waters.

## Who may come near the LORD?

## Psalm 24:3

Who may climb the LORD’s hill?

Who may stand in his holy place?

![A path going up a mountain.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the path**

Follow the path up with your eyes. David pictures people going up the LORD’s hill to worship.

## Psalm 24:4

Someone with clean hands and a pure heart may stand there.

This person refuses to worship an idol.

When making a solemn promise, this person tells the truth.

**What do clean hands mean here?**

Hands stand for what someone does. The heart stands for what someone wants deep inside. David is describing someone who lives honestly.

## Psalm 24:5

The LORD will bless this person.

God will save them and show that they are in the right.

## Psalm 24:6

These are the people who look for the LORD.

God of Jacob, they want to be near you.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears here and at the end. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Open the gates for the King

## Psalm 24:7

Lift up your heads, you gates!

Open wide, you ancient doors!

Let the King of Glory come in!

## Psalm 24:8

Who is this King of Glory?

The LORD, strong and mighty!

The LORD, mighty in battle!

## Psalm 24:9

Lift up your heads, you gates!

Open wide, you ancient doors!

Let the King of Glory come in!

## Psalm 24:10

Who is this King of Glory?

The LORD who commands the armies of heaven!

He is the King of Glory.

Selah.
