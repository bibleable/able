# Psalm 30 — You turned my mourning into dancing

**About this psalm**

David remembers how afraid he was when he faced death. He thanks the LORD for healing him and invites others to sing with him.

## David thanks the LORD for healing him

## Psalm 30:1

David wrote this song for the dedication of the temple.

I will praise you, LORD, because you lifted me up.

You kept my enemies from celebrating my defeat.

**A word to know**

At the dedication, the people set the temple apart for worshiping God.

## Psalm 30:2

LORD my God, I cried to you for help.

You healed me.

## Psalm 30:3

LORD, you pulled me up from Sheol, the place of the dead.

You kept me from going down into the grave.

## David invites others to sing

## Psalm 30:4

All you who belong to the LORD, sing to him!

Praise his holy name!

## Psalm 30:5

His anger lasts only a moment.

His kindness lasts a lifetime.

Tears may stay through the night.

Joy comes in the morning.

**Pause and answer**

What does David say comes after the night of tears?

## David remembers his fear

## Psalm 30:6

When life was going well, I said, “Nothing will ever shake me.”

## Psalm 30:7

LORD, you showed me kindness.

You made my mountain stand firm.

Then you hid your face, and I was afraid.

**Why a mountain?**

A mountain stands firm. David felt that secure when the LORD showed him kindness. When the LORD seemed to turn away, David felt afraid.

## David recalls his cry for help

## Psalm 30:8

I called out to you, LORD.

I begged you to show me mercy.

## Psalm 30:9

What good would it do if I died?

If I went down to the grave, could my dust praise you?

Could it tell others how faithful you are?

## Psalm 30:10

Hear me, LORD. Have mercy on me.

LORD, please help me.

## David dances for joy

## Psalm 30:11

You turned my mourning into dancing.

You took off my sackcloth and dressed me in joy.

![A patch of rough, woven sackcloth.](../pictures/job-sackcloth.svg)

**Look at the cloth**

People wore rough cloth called sackcloth to show their grief. David pictures the LORD taking it off him. Now he feels so glad he can dance.

## Psalm 30:12

So I will sing your praise with all my heart.

I cannot keep silent.

LORD my God, I will thank you forever.
