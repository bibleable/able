# Psalm 141 — Guard the door of my lips

**About this psalm**

David asks God for help with his own words and choices. He also asks for protection from people who set traps for him.

## Receive my prayer

## Psalm 141:1

A song of David.

LORD, I am calling to you. Come quickly!

Listen to me when I call.

## Psalm 141:2

Receive my prayer like the incense people offer you.

I lift my hands to you like someone bringing the evening offering.

**What is incense?**

People burned incense as an offering. Its sweet-smelling smoke rose into the air. David wants his prayer to reach God.

## Help me choose my words and actions

## Psalm 141:3

LORD, put a guard over my mouth.

Keep watch at the door of my lips.

![A gate in the wall of Jerusalem.](../pictures/valley-gate-to-dung-gate.svg)

**Find an opening in the wall**

A guard watches what goes through a gate. David pictures his lips as a door and asks God to help him watch what he says.

## Psalm 141:4

Keep me from wanting to do wrong.

Keep me from joining people who do evil.

Do not let me share their rich food.

## Psalm 141:5

Let someone who does right strike me and correct me in love.

I will welcome that correction like oil on my head.

I keep praying against the harm that cruel people do.

## David looks to God for safety

## Psalm 141:6

When their rulers fall from the cliffs, the people will listen to me.

They will hear that my words are good.

## Psalm 141:7

A farmer plows the ground and breaks up the soil.

Like those scattered pieces of earth, our bones lie at the great opening to the place of the dead.

## Psalm 141:8

But I keep looking to you, Lord GOD.

I come to you for safety.

Do not leave me without protection.

## Psalm 141:9

Keep me clear of the traps they set for me.

Help me pass the bait they have laid out.

## Psalm 141:10

Let those who do evil fall into their own nets.

Let me go past safely.

**Pause and answer**

What does David want God to guard when he speaks?
