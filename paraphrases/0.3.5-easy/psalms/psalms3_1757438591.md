# Psalm 3 — David calls to the LORD for help

**About this psalm**

David sings about a night when enemies surround him. He tells the LORD what frightens him and why he can still sleep.

## David tells the LORD about his enemies

## Psalm 3:1

David wrote this song when he fled from his son Absalom.

LORD, my enemies keep growing in number!

So many people have turned against me!

## Psalm 3:2

Many people say about me, “God will never save him.”

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is a word that appears several times in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## David remembers who protects him

## Psalm 3:3

But you, LORD, are like a shield around me.

You give me honor.

You lift my head again.

## Psalm 3:4

I call out loud to the LORD.

He answers me from his holy mountain.

Selah.

![A mountain rising above the ground.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

David says the LORD answers him from his holy mountain. David also calls the LORD a shield. A shield protects someone from harm.

**Pause and answer**

Who answers when David calls out loud?

## David sleeps and wakes again

## Psalm 3:5

I lie down and sleep.

I wake up again because the LORD keeps me alive.

## Psalm 3:6

Even if thousands surround me on every side, I will not be afraid.

## David asks the LORD to save him

## Psalm 3:7

Rise up, LORD!

My God, save me!

Strike all my enemies on the jaw.

Break the teeth of those who do evil.

## Psalm 3:8

The LORD is the one who saves.

LORD, bless your people.

Selah.
