# Psalm 110 — A king and a priest forever

**About this psalm**

David tells what the LORD says to the one David calls his Lord. The LORD gives him a place of honor and promises that he will always be a priest.

## The LORD gives the king a place of honor

## Psalm 110:1

A song of David.

The LORD said to my Lord, “Sit at my right side.

Stay until I put your enemies under your feet like a footstool.”

**Why a footstool?**

A footstool is a low support for someone’s feet. Here it pictures the king’s enemies losing their power over him.

## Psalm 110:2

From Zion the LORD holds out your mighty royal staff.

He says, “Rule even among your enemies.”

![Steps leading up to a king’s throne.](../pictures/solomons-throne-steps.svg)

**Find the king’s seat**

Imagine someone taking the seat of honor beside a king. David hears the LORD invite his Lord to sit at his right side.

## Psalm 110:3

Your people will freely offer to serve on the day of battle.

They come dressed in holy splendor.

The song pictures your youth like fresh dew at dawn.

## The LORD makes a lasting promise

## Psalm 110:4

The LORD has made a solemn promise.

He will never change his mind.

He says, “You will be a priest forever, like Melchizedek.”

**Who was Melchizedek?**

Melchizedek was a king and a priest who blessed Abraham. A priest serves God on behalf of the people.

## The Lord judges rulers

## Psalm 110:5

The Lord stands at your right side.

On the day of his anger he will crush kings.

## Psalm 110:6

He will judge the nations.

The dead will lie in heaps.

He will crush rulers in lands far and wide.

## Psalm 110:7

He will stop to drink from a stream beside the road.

Then he will lift his head high.
