# Psalm 29 — The voice of the LORD

**About this psalm**

David describes a storm moving over water, mountains, and desert. Listen for the words he repeats as the storm moves.

## A call to worship

## Psalm 29:1

David wrote this song.

You who live in heaven, give honor to the LORD.

Praise the LORD for his glory and strength.

## Psalm 29:2

Give the LORD the honor his name deserves.

Worship the LORD in the beauty of his holiness.

## The voice over the water

## Psalm 29:3

The voice of the LORD sounds over the waters.

The God of glory thunders.

The LORD’s voice carries across the wide waters.

## Psalm 29:4

The voice of the LORD is strong.

The voice of the LORD is like the voice of a great king.

## The mountains shake

## Psalm 29:5

The voice of the LORD breaks the cedar trees.

The LORD splits the tall cedars of Lebanon.

## Psalm 29:6

He makes Lebanon leap like a calf.

He makes Mount Sirion jump like a young wild ox.

![A high mountain with a path up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Picture the mountain**

Think of how still a mountain usually looks. David describes the ground shaking so hard that the mountains seem to jump like young animals.

## Psalm 29:7

The voice of the LORD strikes with flashes of fire.

## The desert and the forest

## Psalm 29:8

The voice of the LORD shakes the desert.

The LORD shakes the desert of Kadesh.

## Psalm 29:9

The voice of the LORD twists the oak trees.

It strips the forests bare.

In his temple everyone shouts, “Glory!”

**What are the people shouting?**

Glory is the greatness and splendor that belong to God. The people praise him together.

## The LORD gives his people peace

## Psalm 29:10

The LORD sits on his throne above the flood.

The LORD is King forever.

## Psalm 29:11

The LORD gives strength to his people.

The LORD blesses his people with peace.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does the LORD give his people at the end of the storm?
