# Psalm 79 — Hear the prisoners and help us

**About this psalm**

Jerusalem lies in ruins. The people tell God what has happened. They ask him to forgive them and to hear the prisoners who are still alive.

## Jerusalem lies in ruins

## Psalm 79:1

A song of Asaph.

God, other nations have invaded the land that belongs to you.

They have shown contempt for your holy temple.

They have left Jerusalem in heaps of broken stone.

## Psalm 79:2

They left the bodies of your servants for the birds to eat.

Wild animals feed on the bodies of your faithful people.

## Psalm 79:3

All around Jerusalem, our enemies spilled blood like water.

No one is here to bury the dead.

## Psalm 79:4

Our neighbors insult us.

People all around us laugh at us.

## The people ask how long

## Psalm 79:5

How long, LORD?

Will you be angry forever?

Will your jealousy keep burning like fire?

## Psalm 79:6

Turn your anger against the nations that refuse to recognize you.

Turn it against the kingdoms that refuse to call on you.

## Psalm 79:7

They have torn Jacob’s people apart.

They have ruined the land where his family lived.

**Who are Jacob’s people?**

Jacob was also called Israel. Here his name stands for the people of Israel.

## A prayer for mercy

## Psalm 79:8

Please forgive the wrongs of the past.

Hurry and show us mercy.

We have fallen so low.

## Psalm 79:9

God, you are the one who saves us.

Help us so people will honor your name.

Rescue us and take away our guilt for your name’s sake.

## Psalm 79:10

Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?”

Let us see you punish those who killed your servants.

Let the nations see it too.

## The prisoners cry out

## Psalm 79:11

Listen to the prisoners groan.

Use your strength to keep alive those who face a death sentence.

## Psalm 79:12

Lord, our neighbors have insulted you.

Pay them back seven times over for those insults.

## Psalm 79:13

Then we, your people, will thank you forever.

We are the sheep you care for.

One generation after another will tell how great you are.

**Look at the sheep pen**

A shepherd gathers sheep and keeps them safe. The people ask God to care for them like that.
