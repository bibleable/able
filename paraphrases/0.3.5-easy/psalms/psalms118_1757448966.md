# Psalm 118 — His love lasts forever

**About this psalm**

A singer remembers calling to the LORD when enemies surrounded him. Now he asks for the gates to open so he can give thanks. The people join in his song.

## Everyone joins in

## Psalm 118:1

Thank the LORD because he is good.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 118:2

Let the people of Israel say, “His love lasts forever.”

## Psalm 118:3

Let Aaron’s family say, “His love lasts forever.”

## Psalm 118:4

Let all who honor the LORD say, “His love lasts forever.”

## The singer calls for help

## Psalm 118:5

When I was in trouble, I called to the LORD.

He answered me and set me free.

## Psalm 118:6

The LORD is on my side. I will not be afraid.

What can anyone do to me?

## Psalm 118:7

The LORD is on my side. He helps me.

I will see those who hate me defeated.

## Psalm 118:8

It is better to go to the LORD for safety than to put your trust in people.

## Psalm 118:9

It is better to go to the LORD for safety than to put your trust in rulers.

## Enemies close in

## Psalm 118:10

Nations surrounded me.

In the LORD’s name, I drove them back.

## Psalm 118:11

They closed in on every side.

In the LORD’s name, I drove them back.

## Psalm 118:12

They swarmed around me like bees.

They died out like a fire of dry thorns.

In the LORD’s name, I drove them back.

## Psalm 118:13

My enemies pushed me so hard I was falling.

Then the LORD helped me.

## Psalm 118:14

The LORD gives me strength.

He is the one I sing about. He has saved me.

## Joyful shouts in the tents

## Psalm 118:15

Listen to the joyful shouts in the tents of those who do right!

The LORD has saved them. They sing, “The LORD’s right hand has done mighty things!

## Psalm 118:16

The LORD lifts his right hand high!

The LORD’s right hand has done mighty things!”

**What does the right hand mean?**

The singers picture the LORD raising a strong hand. They are celebrating how he used his power to help them.

## Psalm 118:17

I will live. I will not die.

I will tell what the LORD has done.

## Psalm 118:18

The LORD corrected me severely.

Yet he let me live.

## Open the gates

## Psalm 118:19

Open the gates where those who do right go in.

I want to enter and thank the LORD.

## Psalm 118:20

This is the LORD’s gate.

People who do right will enter here.

## Psalm 118:21

LORD, I thank you because you answered me.

You have saved me.

## Psalm 118:22

The builders set a stone aside.

Now it is the cornerstone.

![A cornerstone where two stone walls meet.](../pictures/cornerstone.svg)

**Find the corner**

Follow each wall to the stone where the walls meet. The builders once set this stone aside. Now it has an important place in the building.

## Psalm 118:23

The LORD has done this.

What we see fills us with wonder.

## Psalm 118:24

The LORD has made this day.

Let us be glad and celebrate today!

## A blessing from the LORD’s house

## Psalm 118:25

Please, LORD, save us!

LORD, please help us do well!

## Psalm 118:26

May God bless the one who comes in the LORD’s name.

From the LORD’s house we ask him to bless you.

## Psalm 118:27

The LORD is God.

He has made his light shine on us.

Use ropes to tie the festival offering to the horns of the altar.

**What are the horns of the altar?**

The altar is the place where people bring offerings to God. Its raised corners are called horns.

## Psalm 118:28

You are my God, and I thank you.

You are my God, and I praise your greatness.

## Psalm 118:29

Thank the LORD because he is good.

His love lasts forever.
