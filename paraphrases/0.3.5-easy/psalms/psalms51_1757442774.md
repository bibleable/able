# Psalm 51 — God, give me a clean heart

**About this psalm**

David tells God the truth about the wrong he has done. He asks God to forgive him and to change what he wants deep inside.

## David asks for mercy

## Psalm 51:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

David had slept with Bathsheba, another man’s wife.

Then Nathan the prophet came to David.

God, show me mercy because of your faithful love.

In your great kindness, wipe away the wrongs I have done.

## Psalm 51:2

Wash away my guilt.

Make me clean from my sin.

## David admits his sin

## Psalm 51:3

I know what I have done wrong.

My sin is always on my mind.

## Psalm 51:4

I have sinned against you, against you alone.

I did what you saw was evil.

Your words about me are right.

Your judgment is fair.

## Psalm 51:5

Sin has been with me since my birth.

Even when my life began inside my mother, I was sinful.

## Psalm 51:6

You want me to be honest deep inside.

There, deep inside me, you teach me wisdom.

## David asks to be clean

## Psalm 51:7

Cleanse me with hyssop, and I will be clean.

Wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.

![A basin of water beside a folded towel.](../pictures/basin-and-towel.svg)

**What does washing mean here?**

Think of water washing dirt away. Hyssop was a plant used in ceremonies for cleansing. David asks God to take away his guilt.

## Psalm 51:8

Let me hear sounds of joy again.

Let even the bones you crushed rejoice.

## Psalm 51:9

Turn your face away from my sins.

Wipe away every wrong I have done.

## David asks for a willing heart

## Psalm 51:10

God, create a clean heart in me.

Give me a steady spirit again, ready to do right.

**What is a clean heart?**

David is talking about his thoughts and desires. He wants God to help him want what is right.

## Psalm 51:11

Please do not send me away from you.

Do not take your Holy Spirit from me.

## Psalm 51:12

Give me back the joy of knowing you save me.

Help me stay willing to follow you.

**Pause and answer**

What joy does David ask God to give back to him?

## David wants to sing again

## Psalm 51:13

Then I will teach your ways to people who do wrong.

People who have sinned will come back to you.

## Psalm 51:14

God, you are the one who saves me.

Free me from the guilt of causing someone’s death.

Then I will sing about how just you are.

## Psalm 51:15

Lord, open my lips.

I will speak your praise.

## What David can offer

## Psalm 51:16

If a sacrifice could please you now, I would bring one.

A burnt offering brings you no pleasure.

## Psalm 51:17

What I can offer you is a broken spirit.

God, you welcome a heart broken with sorrow over sin.

## David prays for Jerusalem

## Psalm 51:18

Look kindly on Zion and help it thrive.

Build up the walls of Jerusalem.

## Psalm 51:19

Then you will delight in the right sacrifices.

People will bring whole burnt offerings.

They will offer bulls on your altar.
