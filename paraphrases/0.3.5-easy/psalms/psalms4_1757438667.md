# Psalm 4 — David lies down in peace

**About this psalm**

David asks God for help. He also speaks to people who tell lies about him. By the end of the song, David is ready to sleep.

## David calls to God

## Psalm 4:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Play it on instruments with strings.

God, you are the one who puts me in the right.

Please answer when I call.

You helped me when I was in trouble.

Be kind to me now and hear my prayer.

![A harp with strings.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the harp**

Find the strings in the picture. A musician can pluck strings like these to play along with a song.

## David speaks to the people

## Psalm 4:2

People, how long will you try to shame me?

How long will you love empty things and chase lies?

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears here and again in verse 4. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 4:3

Remember this. The LORD has chosen faithful people for himself.

The LORD hears me when I call.

## Psalm 4:4

When you feel angry, take care not to sin.

As you lie in bed, think about what is in your heart.

Be still.

Selah.

## Psalm 4:5

Give the LORD the offerings that are right.

Put your trust in the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

What does David ask people to do when they lie in bed?

## David finds joy and rest

## Psalm 4:6

Many people ask, “Who will show us anything good?”

LORD, let the light of your face shine on us.

**What does the light mean?**

David is asking the LORD to look on the people with kindness.

## Psalm 4:7

You fill me with joy.

Even a harvest rich in grain and new wine brings less joy.

## Psalm 4:8

I will lie down and sleep in peace.

LORD, you alone keep me safe where I live.
