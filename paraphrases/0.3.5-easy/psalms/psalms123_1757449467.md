# Psalm 123 — We look to you for mercy

**About this psalm**

Proud people have treated the singers with contempt. The singers look to God and ask for mercy together.

## Waiting for the LORD

## Psalm 123:1

A song for going up to worship.

I look up to you, God.

You sit on your throne in heaven.

## Psalm 123:2

Servants watch their master’s hand.

A woman who serves watches her mistress’s hand.

That is how we look to the LORD our God.

We wait for him to show us mercy.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Find the eye**

Imagine watching someone’s hand for a signal. The singers are watching and waiting for God to help them.

## A cry for mercy

## Psalm 123:3

LORD, have mercy on us! Have mercy!

People have looked down on us for so long.

## Psalm 123:4

We have had enough of their proud laughter.

We have had enough of people treating us as worthless.

**Pause and answer**

What do the singers ask the LORD to show them?
