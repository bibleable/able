# Psalm 93 — The LORD rules over the roaring waters

**About this psalm**

The singer hears the crash of great waves. Above all that noise, the LORD rules as king.

## The LORD is king

## Psalm 93:1

The LORD is king!

He wears his greatness like a royal robe.

He wears strength like a belt, ready to act.

The world stands firm. Nothing can move it.

## Psalm 93:2

LORD, your throne has stood since long ago.

You have always been God.

![Steps leading up to a king’s throne.](../pictures/solomons-throne-steps.svg)

**Find the throne**

A throne is the seat of a king. The singer speaks of God’s throne to tell us that God has always ruled.

## The sound of the waters

## Psalm 93:3

The floodwaters rise, LORD.

The rivers raise their voices.

The sea lifts its waves, and they crash down.

**Why say rivers have voices?**

Fast water makes a loud rushing sound. The singer hears it like a voice.

## Psalm 93:4

The waters roar. Great waves break against the shore.

High above them, the LORD rules in all his greatness.

## God’s words can be trusted

## Psalm 93:5

LORD, we can trust every word you have spoken.

Your house is holy now and for all time.

**Pause and answer**

Who rules even when great waves crash?
