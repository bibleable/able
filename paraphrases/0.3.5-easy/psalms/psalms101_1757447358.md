# Psalm 101 — I will live honestly in my house

**About this psalm**

David sings about God’s love and justice. As king, he promises to pay attention to how he lives and whom he trusts to serve with him.

## David promises to live honestly

## Psalm 101:1

A song of David.

I will sing about your lasting love and your justice.

LORD, I will sing praise to you.

## Psalm 101:2

I will think carefully about how to do what is right.

When will you come to me?

I will live honestly in my own house.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the house**

Think about David coming home. His promise to do what is right includes what he does inside his own house.

## David refuses to take part in evil

## Psalm 101:3

I will keep worthless things out of my sight.

I hate what people do when they turn away from you.

I will not let their ways become mine.

## Psalm 101:4

I will turn away from crooked thinking.

I want no part in evil.

## Psalm 101:5

If someone secretly tells lies about a neighbor, I will stop that person from speaking against them.

I will not put up with proud people who look down on others.

## David chooses people he can trust

## Psalm 101:6

I will look for people in the land who keep their word.

They may live with me.

I will choose people who live honestly to serve me.

## Psalm 101:7

No one who tricks others will live in my house.

No one who tells lies will have a place at my side.

## Psalm 101:8

Every morning I will remove from the land those who do evil.

I will rid the LORD’s city of everyone who does wrong.

**Which city does David mean?**

The LORD’s city is Jerusalem. David speaks here as its king.

**Pause and answer**

What kind of person does David choose to serve with him?
