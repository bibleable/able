# Psalm 19 — The sky tells of God’s glory

**About this psalm**

David looks at the sky and thinks about its Maker. Then he thinks about the LORD’s teaching. He asks the LORD to guide his words and thoughts.

## The sky has a message

## Psalm 19:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

The sky tells us how great God is.

It shows us what his hands have made.

## Psalm 19:2

Day after day, the sky tells its story.

Night after night, it teaches us.

## Psalm 19:3

The sky uses no words in any language.

No one hears a sound.

## The sun crosses the sky

## Psalm 19:4

Yet its message reaches the whole earth.

It goes to the farthest places in the world.

God has set up a tent for the sun in the sky.

## Psalm 19:5

The sun comes out like a groom leaving his room.

It is like a strong runner eager to race.

## Psalm 19:6

It rises at one end of the sky.

It runs all the way across.

Its warmth reaches everything.

![The bright sun shines over the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

**Look at the sun**

Imagine a runner crossing from one side of the picture to the other. David sees the sun cross the sky like that runner.

## The LORD’s teaching brings joy

## Psalm 19:7

The LORD’s law is perfect. It gives me new strength.

We can trust what the LORD tells us.

His words help even a beginner become wise.

## Psalm 19:8

The LORD’s instructions are right. They bring us joy.

His commands shine brightly. They help us see clearly.

## Psalm 19:9

Deep respect for the LORD is pure and lasts forever.

The LORD’s decisions are true.

Every one of them is fair.

## Psalm 19:10

They are worth more than a great heap of pure gold.

They are sweeter than honey straight from the honeycomb.

**Pause and answer**

What does David treasure more than gold?

## David asks for help

## Psalm 19:11

LORD, your words warn me, your servant.

Following them brings a great reward.

## Psalm 19:12

Who can see every wrong they do?

Make me clean even from faults I cannot see.

## Psalm 19:13

Keep me from doing wrong on purpose.

Never let those sins take charge of me.

Then I will be innocent and free of great wrong.

## Psalm 19:14

LORD, may my words please you.

May my quiet thoughts please you too.

You are my Rock and my Redeemer.

**Two names David uses for the LORD**

A rock is strong and steady. David can depend on the LORD. A redeemer is someone who sets another person free.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David want the LORD to find pleasing?
