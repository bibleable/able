# Psalm 128 — A blessing around the table

**About this psalm**

The singer pictures a family eating together. He asks God to bless their home and give peace to Israel.

## The family at home

## Psalm 128:1

A song for going up to worship.

The LORD blesses all who honor him and follow his ways.

## Psalm 128:2

You will eat the food your work provides.

You will enjoy it, and things will go well for you.

## Psalm 128:3

In your home your wife will be like a vine full of fruit.

Your sons around your table will be like young olive trees.

![A vine with a cluster of grapes.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Find the grapes**

Picture a healthy vine bearing fruit, and little olive shoots beginning to grow. The singer uses both pictures to describe a growing family.

## Psalm 128:4

This is how the LORD blesses the man who honors him.

## A prayer for Jerusalem and Israel

## Psalm 128:5

May the LORD bless you from Zion.

May you see Jerusalem thrive all your life.

## Psalm 128:6

May you live to see your grandchildren.

May Israel have peace!
