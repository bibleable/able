# Psalm 34 — Taste and see that the LORD is good

**About this psalm**

David remembers a time when he was afraid and the LORD answered him. He invites others to trust the LORD and teaches them to seek peace.

## David invites others to praise the LORD

## Psalm 34:1

David wrote this song after he pretended to have lost his mind before King Abimelech.

The king sent him away.

I will praise the LORD at all times.

I will always speak his praise.

## Psalm 34:2

I gladly tell what the LORD has done.

Let people who suffer under others hear and rejoice.

## Psalm 34:3

Tell how great the LORD is with me.

Let us praise his name together.

## David remembers how the LORD answered

## Psalm 34:4

I looked to the LORD, and he answered me.

He freed me from all my fears.

## Psalm 34:5

Look to him, and your faces will shine with joy.

You will never hang your heads in shame.

## Psalm 34:6

I was poor and called for help.

The LORD heard me.

He saved me from all my troubles.

## Psalm 34:7

The angel of the LORD camps around those who honor the LORD.

The angel rescues them.

## Try trusting the LORD

## Psalm 34:8

Taste and see that the LORD is good.

How blessed is the person who goes to him for shelter!

**What does tasting mean here?**

When you taste food, you find out for yourself what it is like. David invites people to trust the LORD and experience his goodness for themselves.

## Psalm 34:9

All you who belong to the LORD, honor him.

Those who honor him have what they need.

## Psalm 34:10

Even young lions sometimes go hungry.

Those who seek the LORD have every good thing they need.

## David teaches the children

## Psalm 34:11

Come, children, and listen to me.

I will teach you to honor the LORD.

## Psalm 34:12

Do you love life?

Do you want to see good days?

## Psalm 34:13

Keep hurtful words off your tongue.

Tell the truth when you speak.

## Psalm 34:14

Turn from evil and do good.

Look for peace and work to bring it about.

**Pause and answer**

What does David tell the children to do when they speak?

## The LORD sees and hears

## Psalm 34:15

The LORD watches over people who do right.

He listens when they cry for help.

![An eye beside an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Look at the eye and the ear**

The eye can remind you that the LORD sees people in trouble. The ear can remind you that he hears them call.

## Psalm 34:16

The LORD stands against people who do evil.

He wipes away their memory from the earth.

## Psalm 34:17

People who do right cry out, and the LORD hears.

He rescues them from all their troubles.

## Psalm 34:18

The LORD stays close to people whose hearts are breaking.

He saves those who feel crushed inside.

## The LORD rescues his servants

## Psalm 34:19

Even someone who does right has many troubles.

The LORD rescues that person from every one.

## Psalm 34:20

He guards every bone in that person’s body.

Not one bone will break.

## Psalm 34:21

Evil brings death to people who do evil.

Those who hate people who do right will face judgment and punishment.

## Psalm 34:22

The LORD sets his servants free.

No one who turns to him for shelter will be found guilty.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David say about the LORD when someone feels crushed inside?
