# Psalm 95 — Today, listen to his voice

**About this psalm**

The singers invite everyone to thank God together. Then God reminds them of a time when their families refused to trust him.

## Come and sing

## Psalm 95:1

Come, let us sing to the LORD with joy!

Let us shout to the Rock who saves us!

**Why call God a rock?**

A large rock gives someone a firm place to stand. The singers know they can depend on God to save them.

## Psalm 95:2

Let us come to him and give thanks.

Let us sing so he can hear our joy.

## Psalm 95:3

For the LORD is a great God.

He is the great King above all gods.

## Sea and land belong to God

## Psalm 95:4

He holds the deepest places of the earth in his hand.

Even the highest mountain peaks belong to him.

## Psalm 95:5

The sea belongs to him because he made it.

His hands shaped the dry land too.

## Come and listen

## Psalm 95:6

Come, let us bow down and worship.

Let us kneel before the LORD who made us.

## Psalm 95:7

He is our God, and we are his people.

We are like sheep he feeds and cares for.

Listen to his voice today.

![A pen where sheep can shelter.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

**Look at the sheep pen**

Think of a shepherd gathering the sheep to keep them safe. The singers say God cares for them like that.

## God remembers the wilderness

## Psalm 95:8

God says, “Do not stubbornly refuse to listen.

Remember Meribah and that day at Massah in the wilderness.

## Psalm 95:9

There your ancestors put me to the test.

They had already seen what I had done.

**What happened at Meribah and Massah?**

The people needed water. They argued with Moses and questioned whether the LORD was with them.

## Psalm 95:10

For forty years I was angry with those people.

I said, ‘They keep turning away from me.

They do not know my ways.’

## Psalm 95:11

So in my anger I made this solemn promise.

I said, ‘They will never enter the rest I give.’”
