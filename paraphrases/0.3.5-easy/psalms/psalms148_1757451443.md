# Psalm 148 — Praise from heaven and earth

**About this psalm**

The singer calls on one part of creation after another. The invitation starts high in the sky and reaches people of every age.

## Praise from the sky

## Psalm 148:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

Praise him from the heavens, from the highest places!

## Psalm 148:2

Praise him, all his angels!

Praise him, all his heavenly armies!

## Psalm 148:3

Sun and moon, praise him!

All you shining stars, praise him!

![The sun and moon in the sky.](../pictures/gibeon-sun-moon.svg)

**Find the sun and the moon**

Imagine the song beginning high above you. As you read on, listen for the invitation to praise reaching the ground beneath your feet.

## Psalm 148:4

Praise him, highest heavens!

Praise him, waters above the skies!

## God made them all

## Psalm 148:5

Let them all praise the LORD.

He gave the command, and they came into being.

## Psalm 148:6

He set them in place forever.

The order he gave them will always stand.

## Praise from land and sea

## Psalm 148:7

Now praise the LORD from the earth!

Praise him, great sea creatures and ocean depths!

## Psalm 148:8

Praise him, lightning and hail, snow and clouds!

Praise him, strong winds that obey his word!

## Psalm 148:9

Praise him, mountains and hills, fruit trees and tall cedars!

## Psalm 148:10

Praise him, wild animals and cattle, crawling creatures and flying birds!

## People join together

## Psalm 148:11

Praise him, kings and peoples everywhere!

Praise him, princes and all other rulers!

## Psalm 148:12

Young men and women, praise him!

Old and young, praise him together!

## Psalm 148:13

Let them all praise the LORD’s name.

His name alone stands above all others.

His glory reaches above earth and sky.

## Psalm 148:14

He has raised a horn of strength for his people.

All his faithful people praise him for it.

Israel is the people close to him. Praise the LORD!

**What does a horn mean here?**

A strong animal raises its horn. The singer uses that picture to say God has given strength to his people.
