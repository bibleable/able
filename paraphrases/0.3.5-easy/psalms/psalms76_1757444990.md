# Psalm 76 — God rises to save the lowly

**About this psalm**

Asaph sings about God stopping an army. The whole earth grows quiet when God speaks up for people with little power.

## God breaks the weapons of war

## Psalm 76:1

Asaph wrote this song for the music leader.

Play it on instruments with strings.

People in Judah know God.

People in Israel honor his great name.

## Psalm 76:2

God’s tent is in Salem.

His home is in Zion.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Point to the tent. Asaph speaks of God making his home among his people. Salem and Zion are names linked to Jerusalem.

## Psalm 76:3

There God broke the flaming arrows.

He broke the shield, the sword, and the other weapons of war.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## The strongest fighters lie still

## Psalm 76:4

God, you shine with light.

You are more glorious than mountains full of wild animals.

## Psalm 76:5

The brave fighters lie dead.

Others have taken their belongings.

Even the strongest fighters could not lift a hand.

## Psalm 76:6

God of Jacob, you spoke against them.

At your words, horses and riders lay stunned.

## The earth grows quiet

## Psalm 76:7

You alone inspire such fear.

Who can stand before you when you are angry?

## Psalm 76:8

From heaven you announced your judgment.

Everyone on earth grew afraid and fell silent.

## Psalm 76:9

God, you rose to judge.

You came to save all the lowly people on earth.

Selah.

**Who are the lowly?**

Here they are people with little power. God comes to help them.

## Psalm 76:10

Even human anger will bring you praise.

You will wrap those who survive that anger around you like clothing.

## The nations bring gifts

## Psalm 76:11

Keep the promises you make to the LORD your God.

Let the nearby nations bring him gifts.

He is the one they should honor with deep respect.

## Psalm 76:12

God breaks the proud spirit of rulers.

Even the kings of the earth fear him.

**Pause and answer**

Who does God rise to save?
