# Psalm 50 — God calls his people to listen

**About this psalm**

Asaph sings about God coming as a judge. God speaks to people who bring him offerings. Then he speaks to people who repeat his words while hurting others.

## God calls heaven and earth

## Psalm 50:1

Asaph wrote this song.

The LORD, the mighty God, speaks.

He calls the whole earth to listen, from sunrise to sunset.

## Psalm 50:2

God shines out from Zion, a place of perfect beauty.

## Psalm 50:3

Our God is coming. He will speak!

Fire burns up everything ahead of him.

A fierce storm rages around him.

## Psalm 50:4

God calls to the heavens above and the earth below.

He is about to judge his people.

## Psalm 50:5

He says, “Gather my faithful people here with me.

They brought a sacrifice when they made their covenant with me.”

## Psalm 50:6

The heavens tell how fair God is.

God himself is the judge.

Selah.

**Words in the song**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. Selah is an old word in the song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## God speaks to Israel about offerings

## Psalm 50:7

God says, “Listen, my people. I will speak.

Israel, I will tell you what you have done wrong.

I am God, your God.

## Psalm 50:8

I am not blaming you for the sacrifices you bring.

Your burnt offerings are always before me.

## Psalm 50:9

I do not need a bull from your stall.

I do not need goats from your pens.

## The animals already belong to God

## Psalm 50:10

Every animal in the forest belongs to me.

So do the cattle on a thousand hills.

## Psalm 50:11

I know every bird in the mountains.

All the creatures in the fields are mine.

![A mountain with a path leading up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

Imagine a small bird high on that slope, far from the people below. God says he knows every bird there.

## Psalm 50:12

If I were hungry, I would have no need to tell you.

The world and everything in it belong to me.

## Psalm 50:13

Do I eat the meat of bulls?

Do I drink the blood of goats?

## Thank God and call for help

## Psalm 50:14

Bring God an offering to say thank you.

Keep the promises you made to God Most High.

## Psalm 50:15

Call to me when trouble comes.

I will rescue you, and you will honor me.”

**Pause and answer**

What does God ask his people to do when trouble comes?

## God speaks to people who ignore his words

## Psalm 50:16

God says to people who do evil, “What right have you to repeat my laws?

What right have you to speak about my covenant?

## Psalm 50:17

You hate it when I teach you.

You toss my words behind you.

## Psalm 50:18

When you see a thief, you make friends with him.

You side with people who break their marriage vows by sleeping with someone else.

## Psalm 50:19

You use your words to do harm.

You tell lies to trick people.

## Psalm 50:20

You sit and speak against your brother.

You tell lies about your own mother’s son.

## God breaks his silence

## Psalm 50:21

I stayed silent while you did these things.

You thought I was just like you.

Now I tell you to your face what you have done wrong.

## Psalm 50:22

Listen carefully, you who forget God.

Otherwise I will tear you to pieces.

No one will be able to rescue you.

## Psalm 50:23

The person who brings a thank offering honors me.

I will show those who live rightly how I save them.”

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how can people show that they mean the words they say to God?
