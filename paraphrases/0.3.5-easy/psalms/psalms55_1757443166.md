# Psalm 55 — When a close friend turns against me

**About this psalm**

David is afraid. He wishes he could fly somewhere safe. What hurts him most is that someone he trusted has turned against him.

## David asks God to listen

## Psalm 55:1

David wrote this song, called a Maskil, for the music leader.

Play it on instruments with strings.

God, listen to my prayer.

Please hear me when I beg for help.

![A harp with strings stretched across its frame.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the harp**

Imagine someone plucking the strings as David’s prayer is sung. Maskil is an old word for a kind of song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 55:2

Pay attention to me. Answer me.

I cannot settle down as I tell you my troubles.

I am deeply upset.

## Psalm 55:3

I hear my enemies. Cruel people press in on me.

They bring trouble down on me.

In their anger they turn against me.

## David longs to escape

## Psalm 55:4

My heart pounds in my chest.

I am terrified that I might die.

## Psalm 55:5

I shake with fear.

Terror sweeps over me.

## Psalm 55:6

I said, “If only I had wings like a dove!

I would fly away and rest.

## Psalm 55:7

I would go far away.

I would stay out in the wilderness.

Selah.

## Psalm 55:8

I would hurry to a place of shelter, far from this fierce storm.”

**Why does David want wings?**

A dove can fly away from danger. David longs to get away from the people who frighten him. Selah is another old word in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Trouble fills the city

## Psalm 55:9

Lord, mix up their words so they cannot understand each other.

I see people hurting and fighting each other in the city.

## Psalm 55:10

Day and night they go around its walls.

Inside, people plan harm and make trouble.

## Psalm 55:11

People destroy things inside the city.

In the streets they bully and cheat people every day.

## David speaks to his friend

## Psalm 55:12

If an enemy insulted me, I could bear it.

If an enemy came against me, I could hide.

## Psalm 55:13

But you were my equal.

You were my companion, my close friend.

## Psalm 55:14

We used to enjoy talking together.

We walked with the crowd into God’s house.

**Pause and answer**

Where did David and his friend used to go together?

## Psalm 55:15

Let death take my attackers by surprise.

Let them go down alive to Sheol, the place of the dead.

Evil is at home where they live.

## God hears David’s voice

## Psalm 55:16

I call to God, and the LORD saves me.

## Psalm 55:17

Morning, noon, and night I cry out in distress.

God hears my voice.

## Psalm 55:18

Many people fight against me.

Yet God brings me safely out of the battle and gives me peace.

## Psalm 55:19

God has sat on his throne for ages.

He will hear them and bring them low.

Selah.

They refuse to change. They have no fear of God.

## A broken promise

## Psalm 55:20

My friend attacks people who trusted him.

He breaks his solemn agreement with them.

## Psalm 55:21

His speech is smooth as butter. Inside, he wants war.

His words sound gentle, as soft as oil.

Yet they cut like swords drawn for a fight.

## David puts his trust in God

## Psalm 55:22

Give the LORD the load you carry. He will support you.

He will never let those who do right lose their footing.

**What load does David mean?**

A heavy load can wear someone down. David asks people to bring their troubles to the LORD and rely on his help.

## Psalm 55:23

God, you will bring the killers and liars down to the pit of destruction.

They will not live even half their days.

As for me, I will trust you.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, who does David turn to after his friend breaks his promise?
