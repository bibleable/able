# Psalm 136 — His love lasts forever

**About this psalm**

One voice can read the first line of each verse. Everyone can answer with the same words each time. The song remembers how God made the world, rescued Israel, and feeds living creatures.

## Thank the LORD

## Psalm 136:1

Thank the LORD because he is good.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:2

Thank the God who is above all gods.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:3

Thank the Lord who rules over all lords.

His love lasts forever.

## The lights in the sky

## Psalm 136:4

He alone does great and wonderful things.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:5

He made the heavens with wisdom.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:6

He spread the land out over the waters.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:7

He made the great lights in the sky.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:8

He made the sun to rule the day.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:9

He made the moon and stars to rule the night.

His love lasts forever.

![The sun and the moon in the sky.](../pictures/gibeon-sun-moon.svg)

**Find the two lights**

Point to the light that rules the day. Then find the one that shines with the stars at night.

## The way out of Egypt

## Psalm 136:10

He struck down the firstborn in Egypt.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:11

He brought the people of Israel out of Egypt.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:12

He reached out his strong arm to lead them out.

His love lasts forever.

**God’s strong arm**

The singers picture God reaching out to help. They remember how he used his power to free Israel.

## Psalm 136:13

He split the Red Sea in two.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:14

He led Israel through the middle of the sea.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:15

He swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea.

His love lasts forever.

## The journey to a new land

## Psalm 136:16

He led his people through the wilderness.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:17

He struck down great kings.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:18

He took the lives of powerful kings.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:19

One was Sihon, king of the Amorites.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:20

Another was Og, king of Bashan.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:21

God gave their land as a lasting gift.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:22

He gave that land to his servant Israel.

His love lasts forever.

## God remembers and feeds us

## Psalm 136:23

He remembered us when we were brought low.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:24

He set us free from our enemies.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:25

He gives food to every living creature.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 136:26

Give thanks to the God of heaven!

His love lasts forever.

What do the singers say about God’s love after every verse?
