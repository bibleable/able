# Psalm 36 — Your love reaches to the sky

**About this psalm**

David begins with a man who plans harm even in bed. Then David looks up at the sky and sings about how far the LORD’s love reaches.

## A man plans to do wrong

## Psalm 36:1

David, the LORD’s servant, wrote this song for the music leader.

In my heart I have a message about someone who does evil.

This man has no fear of God.

## Psalm 36:2

He thinks so highly of himself that he fails to see his own sin.

He feels no hatred for the wrong he does.

## Psalm 36:3

His words hurt people. He tells lies.

He has stopped acting wisely and doing good.

## Psalm 36:4

Even in bed he plans to do wrong.

He chooses a path that leads him into trouble.

He refuses to turn away from evil.

## David looks to the sky and the mountains

## Psalm 36:5

LORD, your faithful love reaches to the heavens.

Your faithfulness reaches as high as the clouds.

## Psalm 36:6

You do what is right. Your goodness stands like the highest mountains.

Your judgments are like the deepest sea.

LORD, you keep people and animals alive.

![A high mountain with a path leading to the top.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

Follow the path up with your eyes. David thinks of the tallest mountains and the deepest sea when he speaks of how great and fair God is.

## People find shelter with God

## Psalm 36:7

God, how precious your faithful love is!

People find shelter in the shade of your wings.

**What does the shade of wings mean?**

A bird spreads its wings over its young. David pictures people coming close to God for that kind of shelter.

## Psalm 36:8

In your house people eat until they are full.

You give them a river of good things to drink from.

## Psalm 36:9

Life flows from you like water from a spring.

By your light we see light.

**Pause and answer**

What does the flowing spring help you picture about God?

## David asks for protection

## Psalm 36:10

Keep showing your faithful love to those who know you.

Keep doing what is right for those with honest hearts.

## Psalm 36:11

Keep proud people from trampling me.

Keep those who do evil from driving me away.

## Psalm 36:12

Look! Those who do evil lie fallen.

They have been thrown down and cannot get up.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David ask God to do when proud people threaten him?
