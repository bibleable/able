# Psalm 122 — Pray for peace in Jerusalem

**About this psalm**

David remembers the invitation to go to the LORD’s house. Now he stands at the city gates and asks for peace for the people inside.

## An invitation to worship

## Psalm 122:1

David’s song for going up to worship.

I was glad when people said to me, “Let us go to the LORD’s house!”

## Psalm 122:2

Jerusalem, now our feet stand inside your gates!

![Gates in the wall of Jerusalem.](../pictures/valley-gate-to-dung-gate.svg)

**Find a gate in the wall**

Picture a group coming through that opening. David is glad to stand inside the city with the others.

## The tribes gather in the city

## Psalm 122:3

Jerusalem is a city built close together.

Its parts fit together as one.

## Psalm 122:4

The LORD’s tribes go up there.

They go there to thank the LORD, as he instructed Israel.

## Psalm 122:5

There stand the thrones where rulers give their judgments.

They belong to the kings of David’s family.

## A prayer for the people inside

## Psalm 122:6

Pray for peace in Jerusalem.

Say, “May all who love you do well.

## Psalm 122:7

May people live in peace inside your walls.

May they have plenty inside your forts.”

## Psalm 122:8

For my family and friends, I say, “May there be peace in you, Jerusalem.”

## Psalm 122:9

The house of the LORD our God is here.

For its sake, I will work for your good.
