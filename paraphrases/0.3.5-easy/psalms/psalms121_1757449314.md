# Psalm 121 — Where does my help come from?

**About this psalm**

The singer looks up at the hills and asks a question. Listen for the answer and for the promise that the LORD stays awake.

## The maker of heaven and earth

## Psalm 121:1

A song for going up to worship.

I look up at the hills.

Where does my help come from?

![A path runs up the side of a mountain.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look up from the path**

Trace the path toward the peak. Imagine a traveler looking up and asking who will help on the journey.

## Psalm 121:2

My help comes from the LORD.

He made heaven and earth.

## The one who guards Israel

## Psalm 121:3

He will keep your foot from slipping.

The one who guards you will not doze off.

## Psalm 121:4

Look! The one who guards Israel never dozes or sleeps.

## Psalm 121:5

The LORD watches over you.

He is like shade at your right side.

**Why speak of shade?**

Shade gives a traveler shelter from the hot sun. The singer pictures the LORD staying close enough to protect someone.

## Psalm 121:6

The sun will not hurt you by day.

The moon will not hurt you by night.

## When you leave and when you return

## Psalm 121:7

The LORD will guard you from every evil.

He will keep your life safe.

## Psalm 121:8

The LORD watches over you when you leave and when you return.

He does this now and forever.
