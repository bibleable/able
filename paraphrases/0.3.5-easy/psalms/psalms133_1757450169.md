# Psalm 133 — The joy of living together in peace

**About this psalm**

David compares a family living in peace to two things people valued — fragrant oil and fresh dew.

## A family at peace

## Psalm 133:1

David’s song for going up to worship.

Look how good it is when brothers live together in peace!

What a joy it is!

## Oil and dew

## Psalm 133:2

It is like fine oil poured on Aaron’s head.

The oil runs down his beard, all the way to the collar of his robe.

**Who was Aaron?**

Aaron served as a priest. People poured special oil on his head to set him apart for that work.

## Psalm 133:3

It is like the dew of Mount Hermon falling on the mountains of Zion.

There the LORD gives his blessing — life forever.

![A mountain with a path going up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Picture the mountain in the morning**

Dew is the little drops of water that form on the ground overnight. Imagine the ground fresh with dew. David pictures the blessing of peace that way.
