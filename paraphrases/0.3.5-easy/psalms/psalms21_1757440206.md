# Psalm 21 — The king rejoices because the LORD saved him

**About this psalm**

The people sing to the LORD about their king. They remember what the king asked for and how the LORD answered.

## The LORD answers the king

## Psalm 21:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

LORD, your strength makes the king glad.

How he rejoices because you saved him!

## Psalm 21:2

You gave him what his heart longed for.

You gave him what he asked for.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old word in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 21:3

You welcomed him with many good gifts.

You placed a crown of pure gold on his head.

![A crown beside an armband.](../pictures/sauls-crown-and-armband.svg)

**Look at the crown**

Find the crown in the picture. A king wears a crown on his head. In this song, the LORD himself gives the king that honor.

## The king receives life and joy

## Psalm 21:4

He asked you for life, and you gave it.

You gave him days that go on forever.

## Psalm 21:5

Because you saved him, the king has great honor.

You have made him great and clothed him in glory.

## Psalm 21:6

You bless him forever.

He is full of joy when he is with you.

## Psalm 21:7

The king puts his trust in the LORD.

The faithful love of God Most High keeps him steady.

**Pause and answer**

What keeps the king steady when trouble comes?

## The enemies’ plans fail

## Psalm 21:8

Your hand will catch all your enemies.

Your right hand will seize those who hate you.

## Psalm 21:9

When you appear, you will put them in a blazing furnace.

In his anger the LORD will overwhelm them.

The fire will burn them up.

**A word to know**

A furnace is an oven that gets very hot. The song uses its blazing fire to describe the LORD’s judgment.

## Psalm 21:10

You will wipe out their children and their children’s children.

None of their family will remain on earth.

## Psalm 21:11

Your enemies plan to harm you.

But their plans will fail.

## Psalm 21:12

When you aim your bow at them, they will turn and run.

## The people sing to the LORD

## Psalm 21:13

LORD, show how great your strength is!

We will sing and praise you for your power.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, why do the people want to sing?
