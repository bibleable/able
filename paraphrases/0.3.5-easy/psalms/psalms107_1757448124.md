# Psalm 107 — They cried out to the LORD

**About this psalm**

Travelers, prisoners, sick people, and sailors tell of danger. Each time, the same words return. The people cry out, the LORD helps them, and the singer asks them to give thanks.

## Let the people God rescued speak

## Psalm 107:1

Give thanks to the LORD because he is good.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 107:2

Let those the LORD has rescued say so.

He freed them from their enemy’s grip.

## Psalm 107:3

He gathered them from many lands.

They came from east and west, north and south.

## Travelers lost in the desert

## Psalm 107:4

Some people wandered in an empty desert.

They could find no road to a city where they could live.

## Psalm 107:5

They were hungry and thirsty.

They felt their strength slipping away.

## Psalm 107:6

In their trouble they cried out to the LORD.

He rescued them from their distress.

## Psalm 107:7

He led them along a straight road.

They reached a city where they could live.

## Psalm 107:8

Let them thank the LORD for his lasting love.

Let them thank him for the wonders he does for people.

## Psalm 107:9

He gives thirsty people enough to drink.

He fills hungry people with good food.

## Prisoners in the dark

## Psalm 107:10

Some people sat in darkness, close to death.

They suffered in prison, bound with chains.

## Psalm 107:11

They had turned against God’s words.

They had brushed aside the advice of God Most High.

## Psalm 107:12

He humbled them with hard work.

They stumbled, and no one helped.

## Psalm 107:13

In their trouble they cried out to the LORD.

He rescued them from their distress.

## Psalm 107:14

He led them out of darkness, away from death.

He broke their chains.

## Psalm 107:15

Let them thank the LORD for his lasting love.

Let them thank him for the wonders he does for people.

## Psalm 107:16

He broke down bronze gates.

He cut through iron bars.

## Sick people near death

## Psalm 107:17

Some people foolishly turned against God.

Their wrongdoing brought suffering on them.

## Psalm 107:18

They could not bear to eat any food.

They came close to death.

## Psalm 107:19

In their trouble they cried out to the LORD.

He rescued them from their distress.

## Psalm 107:20

He spoke, and they were healed.

He saved them from the pit of death.

## Psalm 107:21

Let them thank the LORD for his lasting love.

Let them thank him for the wonders he does for people.

## Psalm 107:22

Let them bring gifts to God to show their thanks.

Let them tell with joy what he has done.

## Sailors in a storm

## Psalm 107:23

Others sailed across the sea in ships.

They took goods across deep waters to trade.

![A boat.](../pictures/boat.svg)

**Look at the boat**

Imagine standing on its deck. In the next lines, follow the sailors up one wave and down the other side.

## Psalm 107:24

Out at sea they saw the LORD at work.

They saw his wonders in the deep water.

## Psalm 107:25

He spoke, and a fierce storm blew up.

The wind raised great waves.

## Psalm 107:26

The sailors rose high on the waves, then plunged down.

In their fear they lost courage.

## Psalm 107:27

They stumbled about like people who had drunk too much.

None of their sailing skills could help them.

## Psalm 107:28

In their trouble they cried out to the LORD.

He rescued them from their distress.

## A quiet sea and a safe harbor

## Psalm 107:29

He quieted the storm to a whisper.

The waves fell still.

## Psalm 107:30

The sailors were glad when the sea grew quiet.

He guided them to the harbor they wanted to reach.

**What is a harbor?**

A harbor is a sheltered place where ships can stop safely.

## Psalm 107:31

Let them thank the LORD for his lasting love.

Let them thank him for the wonders he does for people.

## Psalm 107:32

Let them praise him when the people gather.

Let them praise him when the elders meet.

## Dry ground and flowing springs

## Psalm 107:33

God turns rivers into deserts.

Where springs once flowed, the ground goes dry.

## Psalm 107:34

He turns good farmland into fields of salt because the people there do evil.

## Psalm 107:35

He also turns a desert into pools of water.

On dry land he makes springs flow.

## Psalm 107:36

There he settles people who have been hungry.

They build a city to live in.

## Psalm 107:37

They sow seeds in fields and plant grapevines.

In time they gather a good harvest.

## Psalm 107:38

He blesses them, and their families grow.

He keeps their herds from growing smaller.

## God lifts up people in need

## Psalm 107:39

Then cruelty, trouble, and sorrow can bring people low.

Their numbers grow small.

## Psalm 107:40

God brings proud rulers into disgrace.

He makes them wander in a desert without paths.

## Psalm 107:41

But he lifts people in need out of their suffering.

Their families grow like flocks of sheep.

## Psalm 107:42

Honest people see this and rejoice.

Those who do wrong have nothing left to say.

## Psalm 107:43

Anyone who is wise should pay attention to these things.

Think about the lasting love of the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

What did the sailors do when their skills could no longer help them?
