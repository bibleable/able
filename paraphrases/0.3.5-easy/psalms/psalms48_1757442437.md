# Psalm 48 — Walk around Zion

**About this psalm**

The singers praise God in Jerusalem, the city they call Zion. They ask people to look closely at the walls and remember who keeps the city safe.

## The city of the great King

## Psalm 48:1

This is a song from the sons of Korah.

The LORD is great. He deserves all our praise.

We praise him in his city, on his holy mountain.

## Psalm 48:2

Mount Zion rises high and beautiful, like the peaks of Zaphon.

It brings joy to the whole earth.

It is the city of the great King.

## Psalm 48:3

God is there in the city’s forts.

He has shown the people that he himself is their strong fort.

**Why call God a fort?**

A fort gives people a safe place when danger comes. The singers say God protects the people of Zion.

## The kings see Zion

## Psalm 48:4

Look! The kings gathered together.

They marched toward the city side by side.

## Psalm 48:5

When they saw it, they stared in amazement.

Then they ran away in fear.

## Psalm 48:6

Right there they began to shake.

Pain seized them like the pain of giving birth.

## Psalm 48:7

God, you sent a wind from the east.

It smashed the ships of Tarshish.

## The people praise God in his city

## Psalm 48:8

We had heard what God had done.

Now we have seen it in our God’s city.

It belongs to the LORD who commands the armies of heaven.

God will keep his city standing forever.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old word in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 48:9

God, here in your temple we think about your faithful love.

## Psalm 48:10

People praise your name to the ends of the earth.

Your strong right hand does what is right.

## Psalm 48:11

The people of Mount Zion are glad.

The towns of Judah rejoice because you judge fairly.

**Pause and answer**

Why are the people of Judah glad?

## Walk around the walls

## Psalm 48:12

Walk around Zion. Go all the way around.

Count its towers.

## Psalm 48:13

Look closely at its strong walls. Walk through its forts.

Then tell the next generation what you saw.

**Follow the wall**

Trace the line from one gate to the other. This is one section of wall. Imagine walking on around the city and counting the towers you pass.

## Psalm 48:14

This God is our God forever.

He will guide us for as long as we live.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what do the singers want the next generation to know about God?
