# Psalm 82 — Defend those who need help

**About this psalm**

Asaph pictures God calling a meeting to judge those who judge others. God asks why they keep taking the side of people who do wrong.

## God calls the judges to account

## Psalm 82:1

A song of Asaph.

God leads a gathering of the gods.

He gives his judgment among them.

## Psalm 82:2

God asks, “How long will you keep judging unfairly?

How long will you favor people who do wrong?”

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## God tells them whom to protect

## Psalm 82:3

God says, “Speak up for weak people and children without fathers.

Stand up for the rights of people who suffer cruelty.

## Psalm 82:4

Rescue those who are weak or in need.

Save them from the grip of those who do wrong.

## Psalm 82:5

They know nothing. They understand nothing.

They walk about in the dark.

The very foundations of the earth shake.

![A stone at the corner where two walls meet.](../pictures/cornerstone.svg)

**Find the stones at the bottom**

The stones below hold up the walls. Imagine them shaking. Asaph uses this picture to show how deeply unfair judgments disturb the world.

## God gives his judgment

## Psalm 82:6

I said that you are gods.

You are all sons of God Most High.

## Psalm 82:7

Yet you will die as people do.

You will fall like other rulers.”

## Asaph asks God to judge

## Psalm 82:8

Rise up, God! Judge the earth.

All nations belong to you.
