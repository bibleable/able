# Psalm 112 — A generous person trusts the LORD

**About this psalm**

The singer describes someone who loves God’s commands. You can see that trust in the way this person lends, gives, and faces bad news.

## Joy in God’s commands

## Psalm 112:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

The LORD blesses those who honor him.

They find great joy in his commands.

## Psalm 112:2

Their children and grandchildren will be strong in the land.

God will bless the families of those who do right.

## Psalm 112:3

Their homes are full of riches.

The good they do lasts forever.

## Kindness in action

## Psalm 112:4

Light shines in the dark for those who do right.

They are kind, show mercy, and treat people fairly.

## Psalm 112:5

Things go well for those who give and lend freely.

They are fair in all their dealings.

![Two coins.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

**Look at the coins**

Imagine someone lending these coins to a neighbor in need. The singer describes a person who lends freely and treats the neighbor fairly.

## Psalm 112:6

They will stand firm.

People will always remember those who do right.

## A steady heart

## Psalm 112:7

Bad news does not frighten them.

They trust the LORD, and their hearts are steady.

## Psalm 112:8

They are confident and have no fear.

They will live to see their enemies defeated.

## Psalm 112:9

They give freely to people who are poor.

The good they do lasts forever.

They will stand tall in honor.

## Psalm 112:10

Those who do evil see this and are upset.

They grind their teeth and waste away.

What they hoped to gain will come to nothing.

**Pause and answer**

Who do the generous people trust when bad news comes?
