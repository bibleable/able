# Psalm 143 — Teach me the way to go

**About this psalm**

David feels weak and afraid. He thinks about what God has done before. He asks God to answer him and show him how to live.

## David asks God to listen

## Psalm 143:1

A song of David.

LORD, hear my prayer.

You are faithful, so listen when I ask for help.

You do what is right, so please answer me.

## Psalm 143:2

Please do not put me, your servant, on trial.

No one alive is without fault before you.

## Psalm 143:3

My enemy has chased me and crushed me to the ground.

I live in darkness like someone long dead.

## Psalm 143:4

My strength is slipping away.

Deep inside, I feel afraid.

## David remembers and reaches out

## Psalm 143:5

I remember the days long ago.

I think carefully about everything you have done.

I remember the work of your hands.

## Psalm 143:6

I reach out my hands to you.

I long for you like dry ground longs for rain.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 143:7

Answer me quickly, LORD! My strength is almost gone.

Do not turn away from me, or I will be like those going down to the grave.

## A prayer for guidance

## Psalm 143:8

In the morning let me hear of your lasting love.

I trust you.

Show me the way I should go, for I turn to you with my whole being.

![The sun shines above the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

**Find the sun**

Imagine David waiting for daylight. When morning comes, he wants to hear again that God loves him.

## Psalm 143:9

LORD, rescue me from my enemies.

I run to you for safety.

## Psalm 143:10

You are my God. Teach me to do what you want.

May your good Spirit lead me on level ground.

**Why ask for level ground?**

It is easier to walk on level ground without falling. David asks God’s Spirit to guide his choices like someone guiding his steps.

## A prayer for rescue

## Psalm 143:11

LORD, for the honor of your name, give me life again.

You do what is right. Bring me out of trouble.

## Psalm 143:12

Because of your lasting love, put an end to my enemies.

Destroy those who cause me suffering, for I am your servant.
