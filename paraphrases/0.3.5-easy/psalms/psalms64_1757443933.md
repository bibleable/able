# Psalm 64 — God hears David’s complaint

**About this psalm**

People use cruel words to hurt others. They think nobody sees their plans. David tells God what frightens him.

## David asks God to hear him

## Psalm 64:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

God, listen as I tell you what troubles me.

I fear my enemies. Keep me safe.

## Psalm 64:2

Hide me from people who secretly plan evil.

Keep me safe from the crowd that does wrong.

## People use words to hurt others

## Psalm 64:3

They sharpen their tongues like swords.

They aim their cruel words like arrows.

**Why mention swords and arrows?**

The people speaking can hurt someone even from far away. David compares the harm their words do to a wound.

## Psalm 64:4

They hide and wait for an innocent person.

Then they shoot without warning.

They have no fear.

## Psalm 64:5

They keep to their evil plan.

They talk about hiding traps.

They ask, “Who will see them?”

![A net with ropes crossing each other.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Follow a rope across the net. Think of how a net catches a fish. David’s enemies hope their secret plans will catch someone by surprise.

## Psalm 64:6

They plan to treat people unfairly.

They say, “We have worked out our secret plan.”

Who can tell what lies deep in a person’s heart?

## Their own words bring them down

## Psalm 64:7

But God will shoot his arrows at them.

Suddenly they will feel the wounds.

## Psalm 64:8

Their own words will turn against them.

Those words will bring them down.

Everyone who sees them will shake their head.

## People think about what God has done

## Psalm 64:9

Then everyone will be filled with fear.

People will tell what God has done.

They will think carefully about it.

## Psalm 64:10

Let those who do right rejoice in the LORD.

Let them go to him for safety.

Let everyone with an honest heart shout for joy.

**Pause and answer**

Where does David tell people to go for safety?
