# Psalm 84 — One day in your house

**About this psalm**

The singer longs to worship God in Jerusalem. Even the small birds have found a home near God’s altars.

## The singer longs for God’s house

## Psalm 84:1

A song of the sons of Korah for the music leader, according to Gittith.

LORD, you lead heaven’s armies.

How beautiful is the place where you live!

**A word in the title**

Gittith is an old musical term. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 84:2

I long to be in the courtyards of the LORD.

I feel faint with longing.

My whole body cries out for the living God.

## Psalm 84:3

Even a sparrow finds a home there.

A swallow makes a nest and keeps her young near your altars.

LORD of heaven’s armies, you are my King and my God.

**What is an altar?**

It is a place where people bring offerings to God.

## Psalm 84:4

How blessed are those who live in your house!

They keep praising you.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## People travel to worship God

## Psalm 84:5

You bless those who draw strength from you.

They long to make the journey to worship you.

## Psalm 84:6

They pass through the Valley of Baca and make it a place of springs.

The autumn rains fill it with pools.

## Psalm 84:7

They grow stronger as they go.

Each one comes before God in Zion.

**Where are the travelers going?**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem. The travelers are going there to worship God.

## A prayer for the king

## Psalm 84:8

LORD God of heaven’s armies, hear my prayer.

God of Jacob, listen to me.

Selah.

## Psalm 84:9

God, look with kindness on our shield, the king you chose.

## One day near God

## Psalm 84:10

One day in your courtyards is better than a thousand days elsewhere.

I would choose to stand at the door of my God’s house before living in the tents of those who do evil.

## Psalm 84:11

The LORD God is our sun and our shield.

The LORD gives kindness and honor.

He holds back nothing good from people who live honestly.

![The sun shines above the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

**Look at the sun**

Think about how sunlight helps you see your way. The singer calls God a sun and a shield because God gives light and protection.

## Psalm 84:12

LORD of heaven’s armies, how blessed is the person who trusts you!

**Pause and answer**

Where does the singer most want to spend a day?
