# Psalm 17 — David asks the LORD to keep him safe

**About this psalm**

David asks the LORD to hear his side. His enemies have surrounded him. He asks God to keep him as safe as a bird keeps its young.

## David asks for a fair decision

## Psalm 17:1

David prayed these words.

LORD, hear my cry for a fair decision.

Listen to my prayer. I speak honestly.

## Psalm 17:2

You see what is right.

Show that I am innocent.

## David remembers how he has lived

## Psalm 17:3

You have tested what is in my heart.

You visited me at night.

You tested me and found no evil.

I have decided to do no wrong with my words.

## Psalm 17:4

Other people have acted violently.

I have listened to your words and kept away from their ways.

## Psalm 17:5

I have stayed on your paths.

My feet have never slipped.

**What does staying on a path mean?**

David means he has kept on living the way God taught him.

## David asks for protection

## Psalm 17:6

God, I call because I know you will answer.

Lean close and hear what I say.

## Psalm 17:7

Show me how wonderful your faithful love is.

People run to you to escape their enemies.

You save them with your strong right hand.

## Psalm 17:8

Guard me as closely as the center of your eye.

Hide me in the shade of your wings.

![An eye beside an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Look at the eye**

Think how quickly you blink to protect your eye. David asks God to guard him that carefully. He also pictures a bird spreading its wings over its young.

## Psalm 17:9

Keep me safe from those who attack me.

Enemies who want me dead are all around me.

## David describes his enemies

## Psalm 17:10

They feel no pity.

They speak as though they are better than everyone else.

## Psalm 17:11

They followed our tracks and found us.

Now they surround us.

They watch for a chance to throw us down.

## Psalm 17:12

They are like a lion hungry for food.

They are like a young lion hiding until it can spring.

## David asks the LORD to act

## Psalm 17:13

Get up, LORD, and face them!

Bring them to their knees.

Save my life from these evil people with your sword.

## Psalm 17:14

LORD, use your hand to save me from them.

Their share is all here in this life.

Give the people you treasure enough to eat.

Give their sons enough, with plenty left for their children.

## Psalm 17:15

As for me, I will stand before you in the right.

I will see your face.

When I wake, being with you will be enough for me.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David say will be enough for him when he wakes?
