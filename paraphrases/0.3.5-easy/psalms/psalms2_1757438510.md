# Psalm 2 — The LORD chooses his King

**About this psalm**

Earth’s rulers meet to make a plan. Listen for three voices in this song. The rulers speak first. Then the LORD speaks. Then the King tells us what the LORD promised him.

## The rulers speak

## Psalm 2:1

Why are the nations so angry?

Why do people make plans that will fail?

## Psalm 2:2

Kings and rulers meet together.

They take their stand against the LORD.

They also oppose his Anointed One.

## Psalm 2:3

The rulers say, “Let us break their chains!

Let us throw off their ropes!”

**What do these pictures mean?**

The Anointed One is the King God chose. People poured oil on a king’s head to mark him as king. The rulers speak of chains and ropes because they want to escape his rule.

## The LORD answers

## Psalm 2:4

The Lord sits on his throne in heaven.

He laughs at the rulers and mocks their plans.

## Psalm 2:5

Then the LORD speaks sharply to the rulers.

His fierce anger terrifies them.

## Psalm 2:6

The LORD says, “I have made my King ruler on Zion.

Zion is my holy mountain.”

## The King repeats the LORD’s promise

## Psalm 2:7

The King says, “I will tell you what the LORD declared.

The LORD told me, ‘You are my Son.

Today I have become your Father.

## Psalm 2:8

Ask me, and I will give you the nations.

Even the farthest lands will belong to you.

## Psalm 2:9

You will break the nations with an iron rod.

They will shatter like a clay pot.’”

![A clay pot with a narrow neck.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the pot**

An iron rod is a sign of a king’s power. Imagine how easily a clay pot would break under it.

**Pause and answer**

Who promises to give the nations to the King?

## A warning to rulers

## Psalm 2:10

So be wise, you kings.

Listen to this warning, you judges of the earth.

## Psalm 2:11

Serve the LORD with deep respect and fear.

Rejoice before him, even as you tremble.

## Psalm 2:12

Kiss the Son to show him honor.

His anger can flare up in a moment.

If you rebel, you could lose your lives.

God blesses everyone who turns to him for safety.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what are the rulers being asked to do?
