# Psalm 49 — What can money buy?

**About this psalm**

The singer asks everyone to listen, rich and poor alike. He has seen people trust their money. He explains why he puts his own life in God’s hands.

## A song for everyone

## Psalm 49:1

This song from the sons of Korah is for the music leader.

Hear this, people of every nation.

Listen, everyone on earth.

## Psalm 49:2

Listen, whether you have little power or much.

Listen, rich and poor alike.

## Psalm 49:3

I will speak words of wisdom.

I have thought deeply about what I want you to understand.

## Psalm 49:4

I will listen closely to a wise saying.

I will play my harp and sing about a puzzle.

## A price no one can pay

## Psalm 49:5

Why should I be afraid when trouble comes?

Why fear the people who surround me and take what belongs to others?

## Psalm 49:6

They trust in their money.

They boast about how rich they are.

## Psalm 49:7

No one can buy back a brother’s life.

No one can pay God the price to free him from death.

## Psalm 49:8

A life costs more than anyone can pay.

No payment will ever be enough.

![Nine silver coins in a group and one coin apart.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Look at the coins**

Imagine adding one more coin to the pile, then another. The singer says even the largest pile could never buy a life that lasts forever.

## Psalm 49:9

Money cannot make someone live forever.

It cannot keep a body from wasting away after death.

## Who keeps the wealth?

## Psalm 49:10

We all see wise people die.

Foolish people and people without sense die too.

They leave their wealth to others.

## Psalm 49:11

They once owned lands that carried their names.

Now their graves are their homes, generation after generation.

## Psalm 49:12

Even a rich person cannot live forever.

Like the animals, that person dies.

## The singer trusts God with his life

## Psalm 49:13

This is what happens to foolish people.

The same goes for those who follow them and praise their words.

Selah.

## Psalm 49:14

Like sheep, they go to Sheol, the place of the dead.

Death is their shepherd.

In the morning those who do right will rule over them.

Far from their grand homes, their bodies will waste away.

**Why call death a shepherd?**

A shepherd leads sheep along a path. The singer pictures death leading people away from the riches they trusted.

## Psalm 49:15

But God will buy back my life from the place of the dead.

He will take me to himself.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears twice in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

**Pause and answer**

Who does the singer trust to take him out of the power of death?

## What can someone take to the grave?

## Psalm 49:16

Do not be afraid when someone grows rich.

Do not fear when that person’s house fills with fine things.

## Psalm 49:17

When that person dies, they can take nothing along.

None of their riches will follow them to the grave.

## Psalm 49:18

Rich people may tell themselves how well they are doing.

People praise you when you do well.

## Psalm 49:19

Even so, they will join their ancestors.

There they will never see daylight again.

## Psalm 49:20

Someone who has riches but lacks understanding is like the animals that die.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, why does the singer say you do not need to fear a rich person?
