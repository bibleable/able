# Psalm 80 — Look after the vine you planted

**About this psalm**

Asaph calls God the shepherd of Israel. Then he pictures the people as a vine God planted. Three times the people ask God to look on them with kindness.

## The shepherd of Israel

## Psalm 80:1

Asaph wrote this song for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “The Lilies of the Covenant.”

Shepherd of Israel, hear us!

You lead Joseph’s people like a flock of sheep.

You sit as king between the cherubim. Shine out!

**What are cherubim?**

Cherubim are heavenly beings. The song pictures God sitting as king between them.

## Psalm 80:2

Let Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh see you shine.

Use your great strength. Come and save us.

## Psalm 80:3

God, bring us back.

Let your face shine on us and save us.

**What does a shining face mean?**

The people ask God to turn toward them with kindness.

## The people weep

## Psalm 80:4

LORD God, you lead heaven’s armies.

How long will you be angry when your people pray?

## Psalm 80:5

You have given us tears for bread.

You have given us a full cup of tears to drink.

**Why speak of eating tears?**

The people cry so much that even meals remind them of their sorrow.

## Psalm 80:6

You let our neighbors fight with us.

Our enemies make fun of us.

## Psalm 80:7

God who leads heaven’s armies, bring us back.

Let your face shine on us and save us.

## God plants a vine

## Psalm 80:8

You took a vine out of Egypt.

You drove out nations and planted the vine in their land.

## Psalm 80:9

You cleared the ground for it.

Its roots grew deep, and it filled the land.

![A vine with a cluster of grapes.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Find the vine**

Follow the stem to the grapes. The vine pictures Israel. God brought the people out of Egypt and gave them a place to grow.

## Psalm 80:10

Its shade spread over the mountains.

Its branches covered the great cedar trees.

## Psalm 80:11

Its branches reached to the sea.

Its new shoots reached toward the great river.

## The wall comes down

## Psalm 80:12

Why did you tear down the walls around it?

Now anyone passing by can pick its fruit.

## Psalm 80:13

A wild pig from the forest tears it up.

Animals from the fields feed on it.

## Psalm 80:14

God who leads heaven’s armies, please come back!

Look down from heaven and see.

Care for this vine.

## Psalm 80:15

Care for the root your own hand planted.

Care for the son you raised for yourself.

## Psalm 80:16

People have cut down your vine and burned it.

When you look at them in anger, they perish.

## A prayer for life

## Psalm 80:17

Rest your hand on the man at your right side.

Care for the son of man you raised for yourself.

## Psalm 80:18

Then we will stay with you.

Give us life again, and we will call on you.

## Psalm 80:19

LORD God who leads heaven’s armies, bring us back.

Let your face shine on us and save us.

**Pause and answer**

When the people ask God to care for the vine, who are they asking him to help?
