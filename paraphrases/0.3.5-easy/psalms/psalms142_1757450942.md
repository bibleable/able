# Psalm 142 — You know my way

**About this psalm**

David is in a cave. He looks around for someone to help him and sees no one. He tells the LORD what is happening.

## A cry from the cave

## Psalm 142:1

A prayer of David from the cave, called a Maskil.

I cry out loud to the LORD.

I raise my voice to ask the LORD for mercy.

**A word in the title**

Maskil is an old word for a kind of song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 142:2

I tell him everything that troubles me.

I tell him what has happened to me.

## David looks for help

## Psalm 142:3

When I feel too weak to go on, you know my way.

My enemies have hidden a trap on the path I take.

![A path going up the side of a mountain.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Trace the path**

Imagine walking along a path without knowing what lies ahead. Even when David feels weak, he believes God knows where he is going.

## Psalm 142:4

Look to my right. No one stands beside me.

I have nowhere safe to go.

No one seems to care what happens to me.

## God is David’s safe place

## Psalm 142:5

LORD, I cry to you, “You are my safe place.

Here among the living, you are my share of all that is good.”

## Psalm 142:6

Listen to my cry. I have been brought very low.

Rescue me from the people chasing me.

They are too strong for me.

## Psalm 142:7

Set me free from this prison so I can praise you.

People who do right will gather around me because you have been good to me.
