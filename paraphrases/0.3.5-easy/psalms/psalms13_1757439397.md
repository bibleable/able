# Psalm 13 — How long, LORD?

**About this psalm**

David feels forgotten. He tells the LORD how long the days feel. His prayer ends with a song of trust.

## David asks how long he must wait

## Psalm 13:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

How long, LORD?

Will you forget me forever?

How long will you hide your face from me?

## Psalm 13:2

How long must I struggle with my thoughts and feel sad every day?

How long will my enemy have power over me?

**What does hiding your face mean?**

David feels as though the LORD has turned away. He wants to know the LORD still sees him.

## David asks the LORD to answer

## Psalm 13:3

Look at me and answer, LORD my God.

Bring the light back to my eyes so I do not die.

**What does the light mean?**

David is asking God to give him strength to live.

## Psalm 13:4

Do not let my enemy say, “I have beaten him.”

Do not let my enemies celebrate when I fall.

## David remembers the LORD’s love

## Psalm 13:5

I have trusted in your faithful love.

I will rejoice because you save me.

## Psalm 13:6

I will sing to the LORD.

The LORD has been good to me.

![A harp with strings for playing music.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the harp**

Think of the sound a string makes. David ends his prayer with words he wants to sing.

**Pause and answer**
