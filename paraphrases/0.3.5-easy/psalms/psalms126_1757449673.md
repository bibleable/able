# Psalm 126 — Coming home with joy

**About this psalm**

The people remember the joy of coming home. They ask the LORD to bring back those who are still away.

## The people remember coming home

## Psalm 126:1

A song for going up to worship.

The LORD brought the captives home to Zion.

We felt as though we were dreaming.

## Psalm 126:2

We laughed and shouted for joy.

People in other nations said, “The LORD has done great things for them.”

## Psalm 126:3

The LORD has done great things for us.

We are full of joy!

## A prayer and a picture of harvest

## Psalm 126:4

LORD, bring our captives home like streams returning to the dry Negev.

**Where is the Negev?**

The Negev is dry country in the south. Rain can fill its dry streambeds with running water.

## Psalm 126:5

Those who plant seeds in tears will gather the crop with shouts of joy.

## Psalm 126:6

A farmer goes out weeping and scatters seed along the way.

That farmer will come back shouting for joy, carrying bundles of grain.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Find the grain**

Picture the farmer tying many stalks into a bundle. The song looks forward to the day when people who cried will have a harvest to bring home.
