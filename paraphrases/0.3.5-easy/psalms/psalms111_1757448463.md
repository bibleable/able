# Psalm 111 — Remember what the LORD has done

**About this psalm**

The singer thanks God in a gathering of people. Together they remember how God fed his people, set them free, and kept his promise.

## The people gather to give thanks

## Psalm 111:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

I will thank the LORD with my whole heart.

I will thank him among those who do right and in the whole gathering.

## Psalm 111:2

The LORD has done great things.

Everyone who delights in them thinks carefully about them.

## Psalm 111:3

What he does is full of greatness and beauty.

He will always do what is right.

## God remembers his promise

## Psalm 111:4

He has helped people remember his wonders.

The LORD is kind and full of mercy.

## Psalm 111:5

He gives food to those who honor him.

He remembers his covenant forever.

**What is a covenant?**

A covenant is a binding promise. The singer thanks God because he keeps his word to his people.

## Psalm 111:6

He showed his people what his power could do.

He gave them lands where other nations had lived.

## God gives commands people can trust

## Psalm 111:7

Everything he does is true and fair.

We can trust every command he gives.

![A scroll for writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Look at the scroll**

Picture someone reading God’s commands aloud from a scroll. The singer trusts those commands because God is fair in what he does.

## Psalm 111:8

His commands stand firm forever.

Truth and honesty guide how they are carried out.

## Psalm 111:9

He set his people free.

He made his covenant to last forever.

His name is holy and fills us with awe.

## Psalm 111:10

Wisdom begins with deep respect for the LORD.

Those who follow his commands grow in understanding.

Praise for him will never end!
