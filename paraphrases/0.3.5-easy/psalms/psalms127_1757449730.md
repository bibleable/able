# Psalm 127 — The LORD builds the house

**About this psalm**

Solomon sings about builders, city guards, and families. He asks people to see what the LORD gives them, including sleep after work.

## A house, a city, and a night’s rest

## Psalm 127:1

Solomon’s song for going up to worship.

Unless the LORD builds the house, the builders work for nothing.

Unless the LORD protects the city, the guards keep watch for nothing.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the house**

Think of the people who built its walls. Solomon says the builders depend on the LORD for their work to succeed.

## Psalm 127:2

You get up early and stay up late, working hard for bread.

All that effort can come to nothing.

The LORD gives sleep to those he loves.

## Children are a gift

## Psalm 127:3

Children are a gift from the LORD.

The birth of a child is his reward.

## Psalm 127:4

Children born while a parent is young are like arrows in a warrior’s hand.

## Psalm 127:5

How blessed is the man whose arrow bag is full!

When he faces enemies at the city gate, he will not stand ashamed.

**Why mention arrows?**

A warrior carried arrows in a bag called a quiver. Solomon pictures a parent with grown children who can stand beside him when someone challenges him.

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD give to those he loves after their work?
