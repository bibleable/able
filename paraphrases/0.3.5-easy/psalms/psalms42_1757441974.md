# Psalm 42 — I thirst for the living God

**About this psalm**

The singer remembers leading people to worship. Now the singer misses those joyful days and asks when it will be possible to go back.

## The singer longs for God

## Psalm 42:1

This song from the sons of Korah is for the music leader.

It is called a Maskil.

A thirsty deer longs for a stream of water.

God, that is how much I long for you.

![One streambed is full of water. The other is dry.](../pictures/job-seasonal-stream.svg)

**Look for the water**

Which side could a thirsty deer drink from? The singer longs to be close to God as much as a thirsty deer longs for water.

**A word in the title**

Maskil is an old word for a kind of song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 42:2

I thirst for God, the living God.

When can I come and stand before him?

## Psalm 42:3

Day and night my tears have been my food.

All day people ask me, “Where is your God?”

## The singer remembers the crowd

## Psalm 42:4

I remember this as I tell God all I feel.

I used to walk with a great crowd.

I led them to God’s house for the feast.

We shouted for joy and praised God together.

## Psalm 42:5

Why do I feel so low?

Why am I so troubled inside?

Put your hope in God, I tell myself.

I will praise him again because his presence saves me.

## Far from God’s house

## Psalm 42:6

My God, I feel so low.

So I remember you from the land of the Jordan, from the peaks of Hermon and from Mount Mizar.

## Psalm 42:7

Your waterfalls roar. Deep water calls to deep water.

All your great waves have rolled over me.

**What do the waves mean here?**

Picture one wave rolling over you before you can stand up from the last one. The singer feels that troubles keep coming like those waves.

## Psalm 42:8

By day the LORD sends his faithful love.

At night his song stays with me.

I sing it as a prayer to the God who gives me life.

## The singer speaks to God and to himself

## Psalm 42:9

I say to God, my Rock, “Why have you forgotten me?

Why must I go about in sorrow while my enemy mistreats me?”

## Psalm 42:10

My enemies’ taunts hurt as if my bones were breaking.

All day long they ask me, “Where is your God?”

## Psalm 42:11

Why do I feel so low?

Why am I so troubled inside?

Put your hope in God, I tell myself.

I will praise him again. He is my Savior and my God.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does the singer say to himself when he feels low?
