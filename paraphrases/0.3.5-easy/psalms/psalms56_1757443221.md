# Psalm 56 — When I am afraid, I trust you

**About this psalm**

David prays after the Philistines capture him in Gath. His enemies watch every step he takes. David asks God to remember every tear he cries.

## David tells God he is afraid

## Psalm 56:1

David wrote this song when the Philistines seized him in Gath.

It is a Miktam for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “A Dove on Distant Oaks.”

God, have mercy on me. People keep chasing me.

All day long they press their attack.

**A word in the title**

Miktam is an old word used in the titles of several psalms. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 56:2

My enemies chase me all day.

Many people are proud to attack me.

## Psalm 56:3

When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.

## Psalm 56:4

I praise God for his word.

I trust God. I will not be afraid.

What can people do to me?

## Enemies watch David’s steps

## Psalm 56:5

All day they twist what I say.

They think of ways to bring me down.

## Psalm 56:6

They make plans together and hide.

They watch my steps and wait to take my life.

## Psalm 56:7

Will they get away with such wrong?

God, in your anger bring the nations down.

## God remembers David’s tears

## Psalm 56:8

You have kept track of all my wandering.

Gather my tears in your bottle.

Haven’t you written them in your book?

![A clay jar with a narrow opening.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Picture the tears**

Imagine saving every tear in a small container. David uses a bottle and a written record to show that God remembers what he has suffered.

## Psalm 56:9

When I call for help, my enemies will turn back.

Then I will know that God is on my side.

## David repeats his words of trust

## Psalm 56:10

I praise God for his word.

I praise the LORD for his word.

## Psalm 56:11

I trust God. I will not be afraid.

What can people do to me?

**Pause and answer**

## Psalm 56:12

God, I will keep the promises I made to you.

I will bring you gifts to give you thanks.

## Psalm 56:13

You saved my life from death.

You kept my feet from slipping.

Now I can walk with you in the light of life.
