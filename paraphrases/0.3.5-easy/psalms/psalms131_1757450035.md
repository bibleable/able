# Psalm 131 — Quiet like a child

**About this psalm**

David lets go of pride. He describes the quiet he feels beside God by remembering how a child can rest beside a mother.

## David grows quiet

## Psalm 131:1

David’s song for going up to worship.

LORD, I am not proud.

I do not look down on others.

I do not chase after great things or matters beyond my understanding.

## Psalm 131:2

I have let myself grow still and quiet.

A child who no longer nurses rests close to its mother.

Inside, I am quiet like that child.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Picture a quiet place**

Find the house. Imagine a mother sitting there and her child resting beside her. David describes that feeling of being safe and still.
