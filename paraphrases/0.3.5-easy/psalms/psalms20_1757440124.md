# Psalm 20 — The people pray for their king

**About this psalm**

The people ask the LORD to help their king in trouble. They look forward to celebrating together when the LORD answers.

## The people ask God to help the king

## Psalm 20:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

May the LORD answer you when trouble comes.

May the name of Jacob’s God keep you safe.

## Psalm 20:2

May God send help from his holy place.

May God give you strength from Zion.

![A path leads up the side of a mountain.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Picture Zion**

Zion is a hill in Jerusalem. The people look to God’s holy place there as they ask for help.

## Psalm 20:3

May God remember every gift you brought him.

May he gladly accept the offerings you burned for him.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old word in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## The people hope to celebrate

## Psalm 20:4

May God give you what you long for.

May he help all your plans succeed.

## Psalm 20:5

May we shout with joy when you win!

May we lift our flag high to honor our God!

May the LORD give you everything you ask for.

## The people put their trust in the LORD

## Psalm 20:6

Now I know the LORD saves the king he chose.

From his holy place in heaven, the LORD answers him.

The LORD’s strong right hand saves him.

## Psalm 20:7

Some people trust in chariots. Others trust in horses.

We trust in the name of the LORD our God.

**What is a chariot?**

A chariot is a cart that horses pull. Armies used chariots in battle.

## Psalm 20:8

Those people fall to the ground.

We get up and stand firm.

## Psalm 20:9

LORD, save the king!

Answer us when we call to you.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, who do the people count on when the king is in trouble?
