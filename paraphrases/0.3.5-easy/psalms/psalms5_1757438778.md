# Psalm 5 — David prays in the morning

**About this psalm**

David calls out to the LORD when the day begins. He asks the LORD to lead him and protect the people who trust him.

## David calls out at daybreak

## Psalm 5:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Play flutes with the song.

LORD, listen to my words.

Hear me even when all I can do is groan.

## Psalm 5:2

My King and my God, hear my cry.

You are the one I pray to.

## Psalm 5:3

LORD, you hear my voice in the morning.

At first light I ask you for help.

Then I wait for your answer.

## David speaks about evil

## Psalm 5:4

You take no pleasure in evil.

Evil has no place in your home.

## Psalm 5:5

People who brag cannot stand before you.

You hate all who do evil.

## Psalm 5:6

You destroy those who tell lies.

LORD, you hate people who kill and deceive others.

## David asks the LORD to lead him

## Psalm 5:7

Your great love lets me come into your house.

I bow toward your holy temple with deep respect.

![A drawing showing the rooms of a temple.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

**What is a temple?**

A temple is a place where people worship. David bows to show the LORD respect.

## Psalm 5:8

LORD, my enemies are near.

Lead me to do what is right in your eyes.

Make your path straight before me.

**Pause and answer**

What does David ask the LORD to help him do?

## David asks God to judge his enemies

## Psalm 5:9

I cannot trust a word my enemies say.

Deep inside, they want to destroy.

Their throats are like open graves.

They use their tongues to trick people.

**What does an open grave mean here?**

A grave is a place to bury someone who has died. David uses this picture to show how deadly his enemies’ lies can be.

## Psalm 5:10

God, declare them guilty.

Let their own plans bring them down.

Drive them away for their many sins.

They have turned against you.

## David asks God to shelter his people

## Psalm 5:11

Let everyone who comes to you for safety rejoice.

Let them always shout for joy.

Shelter them, so all who love your name can rejoice in you.

## Psalm 5:12

LORD, you bless people who do right.

Your kindness surrounds them like a shield.
