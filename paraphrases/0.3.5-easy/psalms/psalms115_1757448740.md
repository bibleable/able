# Psalm 115 — Trust in the LORD

**About this psalm**

People from other nations ask where Israel’s God is. The singers answer together. Three times they call on people to trust the LORD.

## Who deserves the glory?

## Psalm 115:1

LORD, we claim no glory for ourselves.

All honor belongs to your name.

You love us and keep your word.

## Psalm 115:2

Why should the nations ask, “Where is their God?”

## Psalm 115:3

Our God is in heaven.

He does what he chooses.

## Statues made by human hands

## Psalm 115:4

Their idols are statues of silver and gold.

People made them with their own hands.

## Psalm 115:5

The statues have mouths, yet they cannot speak.

They have eyes, yet they cannot see.

## Psalm 115:6

They have ears, yet they cannot hear.

They have noses, yet they cannot smell.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Find the ear**

Listen for a sound nearby. A person can hear that sound. A statue with carved ears cannot hear it.

## Psalm 115:7

Their hands cannot feel. Their feet cannot walk.

They cannot even clear their throats.

## Psalm 115:8

The people who make idols become like them.

So does everyone who trusts in them.

## Three calls to trust the LORD

## Psalm 115:9

People of Israel, trust in the LORD!

He helps you and shields you.

## Psalm 115:10

Family of Aaron, trust in the LORD!

He helps you and shields you.

**Who is the family of Aaron?**

Aaron served as a priest. His descendants served as priests among the people of Israel.

## Psalm 115:11

All who honor the LORD, trust in the LORD!

He helps you and shields you.

## The LORD remembers his people

## Psalm 115:12

The LORD remembers us and will bless us.

He will bless the people of Israel.

He will bless the family of Aaron.

## Psalm 115:13

He will bless everyone who honors him, small and great alike.

## Psalm 115:14

May the LORD help your families grow.

May he bless you and your children with more children.

## Psalm 115:15

May the LORD who made heaven and earth bless you.

## The living praise the LORD

## Psalm 115:16

The highest heavens belong to the LORD.

He has given the earth to people.

## Psalm 115:17

The dead go down into silence.

They do not sing praise to the LORD.

## Psalm 115:18

We will praise the LORD now and forever.

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

**Pause and answer**

What do the singers say the LORD does for those who trust him?
