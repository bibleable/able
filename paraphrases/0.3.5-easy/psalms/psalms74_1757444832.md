# Psalm 74 — God, remember your people

**About this psalm**

Enemies have burned the place where the people worship God. Asaph asks God to come and see. He remembers God’s power and asks him to protect his people.

## Come and see the ruins

## Psalm 74:1

A Maskil of Asaph.

God, why have you turned us away forever?

We are like sheep in your care.

Why does your anger still burn against us?

**A word in the title**

Maskil is an old word used in the titles of several psalms. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 74:2

Remember the people you bought long ago.

You set us free to be your own people.

Remember Mount Zion, where you live.

## Psalm 74:3

Come and see the ruins that seem to last forever.

Look at everything the enemy destroyed in your holy place.

## The enemies burn the holy place

## Psalm 74:4

Your enemies shouted inside the place where we meet you.

They spread out their own flags there for everyone to see.

## Psalm 74:5

They swung their axes like people cutting through thick trees.

## Psalm 74:6

With small axes and sharp picks, they smashed all the carvings.

## Psalm 74:7

They burned your holy place to the ground.

They showed no respect for the place where people honor your name.

## Psalm 74:8

They said to themselves, “We will crush these people completely.”

They burned every place in the land where you met with us.

## How long will this last?

## Psalm 74:9

We see no signs from you.

No prophet is left to speak your message.

None of us knows how long this will last.

## Psalm 74:10

God, how long will the enemy mock you?

Will they insult your name forever?

## Psalm 74:11

Why do you hold back your strong right hand?

Reach out and destroy them!

## God rules the sea and the land

## Psalm 74:12

Yet you, God, have been my King since long ago.

You have saved people here on earth.

## Psalm 74:13

With your strength you split the sea.

You smashed the heads of the great sea creatures.

## Psalm 74:14

You crushed the heads of Leviathan, the sea monster.

You gave its body to desert creatures for food.

## Psalm 74:15

You broke open springs and sent water rushing out.

You dried up rivers that had always flowed.

## Psalm 74:16

Day belongs to you, and so does night.

You set the moon and sun in place.

## Psalm 74:17

You set all the boundaries of the earth.

You made summer and winter.

## Remember the people who suffer

## Psalm 74:18

LORD, remember how the enemy mocked you.

Remember how foolish people treated your name with scorn.

## Psalm 74:19

Please do not give your dove to wild animals.

Remember your people who suffer.

Do not forget them forever.

**Why call the people a dove?**

A dove is a bird that a wild animal can catch. Asaph asks God to protect people who cannot protect themselves.

## Psalm 74:20

Remember the covenant you made.

People hide in dark places across the land to hurt others.

**What is a covenant?**

A covenant is a binding promise. Asaph asks God to remember the promise he made to his people.

## Psalm 74:21

Do not let those who suffer cruelty go away ashamed.

May poor people and those in need praise your name.

## Psalm 74:22

Rise up, God, and speak in your own defense!

Remember how foolish people mock you all day long.

## Psalm 74:23

Listen to the noise your enemies make.

Their angry shouts rise without stopping.
