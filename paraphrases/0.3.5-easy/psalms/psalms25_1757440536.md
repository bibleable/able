# Psalm 25 — LORD, teach me your paths

**About this psalm**

David asks the LORD to teach him how to live. He remembers the wrong he has done and asks for forgiveness. At the end, he prays for all Israel.

## David puts his trust in the LORD

## Psalm 25:1

David wrote this song.

LORD, I turn to you with my whole heart.

## Psalm 25:2

My God, I trust you.

Please keep me from shame.

Do not let my enemies celebrate my defeat.

## Psalm 25:3

No one who waits for you will face shame.

But shame will come to those who betray others without cause.

## David asks the LORD to teach him

## Psalm 25:4

LORD, show me your ways.

Teach me your paths.

## Psalm 25:5

Teach me what is true and guide me by it.

You are the God who saves me.

I wait for you all day long.

**What does a path mean here?**

A path shows you where to walk. David asks the LORD to show him how to live.

## Psalm 25:6

LORD, remember your tender care and faithful love.

Your love has lasted through all the ages.

## Psalm 25:7

Please forget the sins of my youth.

Forget the times I turned against you.

LORD, you are good.

Remember me with faithful love.

## The LORD guides people

## Psalm 25:8

The LORD is good and does what is right.

That is why he shows sinners which way to go.

## Psalm 25:9

He guides humble people to do what is right.

He teaches them his way.

## Psalm 25:10

Some people keep the LORD’s covenant and obey his commands.

In all he does for them, the LORD shows faithful love.

**A word to know**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. Here it is the agreement between the LORD and his people.

## Psalm 25:11

LORD, my guilt is great.

For the honor of your name, please forgive me.

## The LORD shares his covenant

## Psalm 25:12

What about someone who lives in awe of the LORD?

The LORD shows that person which path to follow.

## Psalm 25:13

That person will have what they need and more.

Their children and grandchildren will inherit the land.

## Psalm 25:14

The LORD shares his thoughts with those who honor him.

He helps them understand his covenant.

## Psalm 25:15

I keep looking to the LORD.

He will free my feet from the net.

![A net made of ropes that cross each other.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Imagine trying to walk with your feet caught in those ropes. David pictures the LORD freeing him from trouble.

**Pause and answer**

Who does David trust to free him?

## David asks for help in his trouble

## Psalm 25:16

Turn to me and show me kindness.

I am lonely and in pain.

## Psalm 25:17

My worries keep growing.

Set me free from this trouble.

## Psalm 25:18

See how much trouble and pain I face.

Take away all my sins.

## Psalm 25:19

Look how many enemies I have.

See how fiercely they hate me.

## David prays for himself and Israel

## Psalm 25:20

Guard my life and rescue me.

I come to you for safety.

Please keep me from shame.

## Psalm 25:21

May honesty and right living keep me safe.

I wait for you.

## Psalm 25:22

God, set Israel free from all its troubles.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David ask God to do for Israel?
