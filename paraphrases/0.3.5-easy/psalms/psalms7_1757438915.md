# Psalm 7 — David asks God to judge fairly

**About this psalm**

David asks the LORD to hear his case. He is willing for God to judge his own actions too.

## David asks for safety

## Psalm 7:1

David sang this song, called a Shiggaion, to the LORD.

It was about the words of Cush, from the tribe of Benjamin.

LORD my God, I come to you for safety.

Save me from everyone chasing me.

## Psalm 7:2

Otherwise they will tear me apart like a lion.

No one will be there to rescue me.

## David asks God to examine his actions

## Psalm 7:3

LORD my God, suppose I did what they say.

Suppose my own hands did something wrong.

## Psalm 7:4

Suppose I hurt someone who stood by me.

Suppose I robbed my enemy for no reason.

## Psalm 7:5

If so, let my enemy chase me and catch me.

Let my enemy trample me into the ground.

Let my honor lie in the dust.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears here in the song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## The LORD sits as judge

## Psalm 7:6

Rise up in anger, LORD!

Stand against my furious enemies.

Wake up, my God, and call for judgment.

## Psalm 7:7

Let the peoples gather around you.

Take your seat high above them.

![Steps leading up to King Solomon’s throne.](../pictures/solomons-throne-steps.svg)

**Look at the high seat**

A throne is a king’s seat. The steps help us picture what David asks God to do. He wants God to sit above the gathered people and judge fairly.

## Psalm 7:8

The LORD judges the peoples.

LORD, judge me too.

See how I have acted honestly and done right.

Show that I am innocent.

## Psalm 7:9

Stop those who do evil from hurting others.

Help those who do right stand firm.

God, you judge fairly.

You know what we feel and think.

**Pause and answer**

What does David say God knows about people?

## God protects and judges

## Psalm 7:10

God protects me like a shield.

God saves those who are honest in their hearts.

## Psalm 7:11

God is a fair judge.

Every day God feels anger at evil.

## Psalm 7:12

If someone refuses to turn from evil, God will sharpen his sword.

God has already bent his bow and fitted the string.

## Psalm 7:13

God has his deadly weapons ready.

His arrows are ready to blaze with fire.

## A man falls into his own pit

## Psalm 7:14

Look at the person who plans evil.

He carries trouble inside him and gives birth to lies.

## Psalm 7:15

He digs a deep pit.

Then he falls into the hole he made.

## Psalm 7:16

The trouble he planned comes back on him.

The violence he meant for others lands on his own head.

**What does the pit mean?**

Picture someone digging a trap and falling into it himself. David says the harm someone plans can come back to hurt that same person.

## Psalm 7:17

I will thank the LORD because he does what is right.

I will sing praise to the LORD Most High.
