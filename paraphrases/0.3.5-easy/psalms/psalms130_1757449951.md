# Psalm 130 — Waiting for the morning

**About this psalm**

In deep trouble, the singer asks God for mercy. He waits for God as eagerly as a night guard waits for daylight.

## A cry for forgiveness

## Psalm 130:1

A song for going up to worship.

LORD, from deep in trouble I cry out to you!

## Psalm 130:2

Lord, hear my voice.

Listen as I ask for mercy.

## Psalm 130:3

LORD, if you kept a record of every wrong, who could stand before you?

## Psalm 130:4

But you forgive.

That is why we honor you with deep respect.

## The singer waits

## Psalm 130:5

I wait for the LORD with my whole being.

I put my hope in his word.

## Psalm 130:6

I wait for the Lord more than night guards wait for morning.

Yes, more than night guards wait for morning!

![A dark night sky above the horizon.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Look toward the horizon**

Imagine keeping watch through the night, looking for the first light. The singer longs for the Lord even more than the guard longs for morning.

## Hope for Israel

## Psalm 130:7

Israel, put your hope in the LORD.

His love lasts.

He has more than enough power to set his people free.

## Psalm 130:8

He will free Israel from all its sins.

**Pause and answer**

What does the singer put his hope in while he waits?
