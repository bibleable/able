# Psalm 139 — LORD, you know me

**About this psalm**

David thinks about the places he could go and the words he has yet to speak. God already knows him. David asks God to look inside his heart and guide him.

## You know my everyday life

## Psalm 139:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

LORD, you have looked deep inside me.

You know me.

## Psalm 139:2

You know when I sit down and when I get up.

Even from far away, you understand my thoughts.

## Psalm 139:3

You know the paths I take and where I lie down.

You know everything I do.

## Psalm 139:4

LORD, before I say a word, you already know it.

## Psalm 139:5

You are behind me and in front of me.

You rest your hand on me.

## Psalm 139:6

What you know fills me with wonder.

It is more than I can understand.

## You are there wherever I go

## Psalm 139:7

Where could I go to get away from your Spirit?

Where could I run so you would not be there?

## Psalm 139:8

If I went up to heaven, you would be there.

If I lay down in Sheol, the place of the dead, you would be there too.

## Psalm 139:9

Suppose I could fly on the wings of the dawn.

Suppose I went to live beyond the farthest sea.

**Wings of the dawn**

David imagines traveling with the first light of morning. Even if he could go that far, God would be there.

## Psalm 139:10

Even there you would lead me by the hand.

Your strong right hand would hold me firmly.

## You see even in the dark

## Psalm 139:11

I might say, “Surely the darkness will hide me.

The light around me will turn to night.”

## Psalm 139:12

But you see even in the dark.

To you the night shines like the day.

Darkness and light are alike to you.

![A dark sky above the horizon at night.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Look at the dark sky**

Imagine looking for a path after the daylight fades. David says God can see him as clearly at night as during the day.

## You saw me before I was born

## Psalm 139:13

You made every part inside me.

You knit me together as I grew inside my mother.

## Psalm 139:14

I praise you because the way you made me fills me with awe.

Your work is wonderful. I know that well.

## Psalm 139:15

You saw my body taking shape in secret.

It was like being woven together deep in the earth.

**Why speak of weaving?**

A weaver joins threads to make cloth. David pictures God carefully forming the parts of his body while he was hidden inside his mother.

## Psalm 139:16

You saw me before my body had taken shape.

You wrote all my days in your book.

You planned them before the first one began.

## More thoughts than grains of sand

## Psalm 139:17

God, your thoughts are precious to me!

There are so many of them!

## Psalm 139:18

If I tried to count them, they would outnumber the grains of sand.

When I wake up, I am still with you.

## David speaks about God’s enemies

## Psalm 139:19

God, I wish you would put those who do evil to death.

You men who want to shed blood, get away from me!

## Psalm 139:20

They tell lies about you.

Your enemies misuse your name.

## Psalm 139:21

LORD, do I not hate those who hate you?

Do I not despise those who rise against you?

## Psalm 139:22

My hatred for them is complete.

I count them as my enemies.

## Look inside my heart

## Psalm 139:23

God, look deep inside me and know my heart.

Test me and see what worries me.

## Psalm 139:24

See whether anything I do offends you.

Lead me along the path that lasts forever.

**Pause and answer**

What does David ask God to do after looking inside his heart?
