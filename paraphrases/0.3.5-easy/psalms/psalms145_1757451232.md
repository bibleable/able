# Psalm 145 — The LORD is near to those who call

**About this psalm**

David promises to praise God every day. He thinks about how one generation tells the next what God has done.

## Praise each day

## Psalm 145:1

A song of praise from David.

My God and King, I will tell how great you are.

I will praise your name forever.

## Psalm 145:2

Every day I will thank you.

I will keep praising your name forever.

## Psalm 145:3

The LORD is great and deserves great praise.

No one can measure his greatness.

## Tell the next generation

## Psalm 145:4

One generation will tell the next about your work.

They will tell of the mighty things you have done.

## Psalm 145:5

They will speak of your glory as king.

I will think about the wonders you have done.

## Psalm 145:6

They will tell how your power fills them with awe.

I too will tell how great you are.

## Psalm 145:7

They will tell everyone how good you are.

They will sing with joy because you do what is right.

## The kindness of the King

## Psalm 145:8

The LORD is kind and full of mercy.

He is slow to become angry. His love is great.

## Psalm 145:9

The LORD is good to everyone.

He cares for everything he has made.

## Psalm 145:10

LORD, everything you made will thank you.

Your faithful people will praise you.

## Psalm 145:11

They will speak of your glorious kingdom.

They will tell how strong you are.

## Psalm 145:12

Then others will know of your mighty acts.

They will hear how glorious your kingdom is.

## Psalm 145:13

Your kingdom lasts forever.

You rule over every generation.

The LORD keeps every promise and acts with kindness.

## An open hand

## Psalm 145:14

The LORD supports those who fall.

He lifts up everyone bent low.

## Psalm 145:15

All living things look to you.

You give them food when they need it.

**Find the grain**

Picture the grain ripening when it is time for harvest. David thanks God for giving creatures food when they need it.

## Psalm 145:16

You open your hand and give every living thing what it longs for.

## Psalm 145:17

The LORD does right in all his ways.

He shows kindness in everything he does.

## The LORD hears

## Psalm 145:18

The LORD is near to everyone who calls to him sincerely.

## Psalm 145:19

He gives those who honor him what they long for.

He hears their cry and saves them.

## Psalm 145:20

The LORD guards all who love him.

He will destroy all who do evil.

## Psalm 145:21

I will speak aloud to praise the LORD.

May every living creature praise his holy name forever!

**Pause and answer**

What does one generation tell the next?
