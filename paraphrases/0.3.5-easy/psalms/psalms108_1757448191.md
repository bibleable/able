# Psalm 108 — I will wake the dawn

**About this psalm**

David gets ready to sing before the sun comes up. He praises God’s lasting love. Then he asks God to help his people.

## David greets the morning with music

## Psalm 108:1

A song of David.

My heart is steady, God.

With my whole being I will sing and play music.

## Psalm 108:2

Wake up, harp and lyre!

I will wake the dawn.

![A harp with strings stretched across its frame.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the strings**

A harp and a lyre both have strings to pluck. David wants to play so early that it will seem as if his music wakes the morning.

## Psalm 108:3

LORD, I will praise you among the nations.

I will sing to you among all the peoples.

## Psalm 108:4

Your faithful love reaches beyond the heavens.

Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

## David asks God to save his people

## Psalm 108:5

God, may you be honored above the heavens.

May your glory cover the whole earth.

## Psalm 108:6

Answer us!

Use your strong right hand to save the people you love.

## God names the lands he rules

## Psalm 108:7

God spoke from his holy place.

He said, “I will win!

I will divide up the land of Shechem.

I will give out shares of the Valley of Succoth.

## Psalm 108:8

Gilead belongs to me.

Manasseh belongs to me.

Ephraim is my helmet.

Judah is the staff I carry as king.

## Psalm 108:9

Moab is the bowl where I wash.

I throw my sandal on Edom.

I shout because I have won against Philistia.”

**Why speak of a helmet and a staff?**

A helmet protects a soldier’s head. A king’s staff shows his right to rule. God uses these pictures to describe Ephraim and Judah.

## The people depend on God

## Psalm 108:10

Who will take me to the city with strong walls?

Who will lead me to Edom?

## Psalm 108:11

God, have you turned away from us?

Will you still go out with our armies?

## Psalm 108:12

Help us against our enemies.

People alone cannot give us the help we need.

## Psalm 108:13

With God we will act bravely.

God himself will crush our enemies under his feet.
