# Psalm 27 — The LORD is my light

**About this psalm**

David’s enemies are close. He tells us why he trusts the LORD. Then he speaks to the LORD and asks him to stay near.

## David trusts the LORD in danger

## Psalm 27:1

David wrote this song.

The LORD is my light. The LORD saves me.

Who can make me afraid?

The LORD keeps my life safe like a strong fort.

Who should I fear?

## Psalm 27:2

My enemies came at me as if to eat me alive.

But they stumbled and fell.

## Psalm 27:3

Even if an army camps around me, I will not fear.

Even if war breaks out, I will keep trusting the LORD.

## David asks for one thing

## Psalm 27:4

One thing I ask of the LORD.

I want to live in his house all my life.

I want to see the beauty of the LORD.

I want to seek him in his temple.

## Psalm 27:5

When trouble comes, the LORD will give me shelter.

He will hide me inside his tent.

He will lift me high onto a rock.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Look at the tent**

Picture going inside the tent for shelter. David uses the tent to show how safe he feels with the LORD.

## Psalm 27:6

Then I will hold my head high above my enemies.

At his tent I will bring offerings and shout for joy.

I will sing and make music to the LORD.

## David calls to the LORD

## Psalm 27:7

LORD, hear me when I call.

Show me mercy and answer me.

## Psalm 27:8

Deep inside, I hear the words, “Seek his face.”

LORD, I will seek your face.

**What does seeking the LORD’s face mean?**

David wants to be close to the LORD. He turns to him in prayer.

## Psalm 27:9

Please do not hide your face from me.

Do not send me away in anger. I am your servant.

You have helped me before.

God who saves me, please stay with me.

## Psalm 27:10

Even if my father and mother leave me, the LORD will take me in.

## David asks the LORD to guide him

## Psalm 27:11

LORD, teach me how to live.

Lead me on a level path because people want to hurt me.

## Psalm 27:12

Do not let my enemies do what they want with me.

People stand up and tell lies about me.

They threaten to hurt me.

## David waits with hope

## Psalm 27:13

Still, I am sure I will see the LORD’s goodness while I am alive.

## Psalm 27:14

Wait patiently for the LORD.

Be strong. Take courage.

Wait patiently for the LORD!
