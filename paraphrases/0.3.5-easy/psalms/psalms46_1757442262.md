# Psalm 46 — God is our shelter and strength

**About this psalm**

The singers picture mountains falling and nations in turmoil. Twice they sing the same words about why they feel safe.

## Even if the mountains shake

## Psalm 46:1

This song from the sons of Korah is for the music leader.

Follow the musical instruction called Alamoth.

God is our shelter and our strength.

He is always here to help when trouble comes.

**A word in the song**

Alamoth is an old musical term. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 46:2

So we will not be afraid, even if the earth changes shape.

Even if mountains fall into the deep sea, we will trust God.

## Psalm 46:3

The waters may roar and foam.

The mountains may shake as the waves rise.

Selah.

![A mountain with a path leading up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Picture the mountain**

Imagine even this tall mountain shaking. The singers say God can still help them when the ground beneath them moves.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears three times in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## God stays with his city

## Psalm 46:4

A river sends streams through the city of God.

They bring joy to the city.

This is the holy place where God Most High lives.

## Psalm 46:5

God is in the city. It will stand firm.

God will help the city when morning comes.

## Psalm 46:6

Nations rage and kingdoms fall apart.

God raises his voice, and the earth melts.

## Psalm 46:7

The LORD who commands the armies of heaven is with us.

The God of Jacob is our strong fort.

Selah.

**What is a fort?**

A fort is a strong place where people can shelter from danger. The singers use it as a picture of God’s protection.

## The LORD brings wars to an end

## Psalm 46:8

Come and see what the LORD has done.

See the places on earth he has laid waste.

## Psalm 46:9

He puts an end to wars everywhere on earth.

He breaks bows and snaps spears apart.

He burns shields in the fire.

## Psalm 46:10

God says, “Be still and know that I am God.

The nations will honor me.

People across the whole earth will honor me.”

## Psalm 46:11

The LORD who commands the armies of heaven is with us.

The God of Jacob is our strong fort.

Selah.

In your own words, why do the singers say they can trust God when they are afraid?
