# Psalm 102 — Hear my prayer when I grow weak

**About this psalm**

A suffering person lies awake and calls to God. As he thinks about Jerusalem and the people who will come after him, he remembers that God’s years never end.

## A cry for a quick answer

## Psalm 102:1

A prayer for someone who feels faint with suffering and tells the LORD his sorrow.

LORD, hear my prayer.

Let my cry for help reach you.

## Psalm 102:2

When I am in trouble, do not turn away.

Listen to me. Answer quickly when I call.

## Psalm 102:3

My days disappear like smoke.

My bones burn like hot coals.

## Psalm 102:4

My heart hurts. I feel like dried-up grass.

I even forget to eat my bread.

## Psalm 102:5

I groan out loud.

I have grown so thin that my skin hangs on my bones.

## Alone like a bird

## Psalm 102:6

I am like an owl alone in the desert.

I am like an owl among ruined buildings.

## Psalm 102:7

I lie awake.

I am like a lone bird on a rooftop.

![A dark sky above the horizon at night.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Look at the night sky**

Picture one bird on a roof while the houses are quiet. The person praying feels that alone.

## Psalm 102:8

All day long my enemies insult me.

They make fun of me and curse me.

## Psalm 102:9

Ashes have become like bread to me.

My tears fall into my drink.

## Psalm 102:10

You are fiercely angry with me.

You have picked me up and thrown me aside.

## Psalm 102:11

My days are like shadows growing long as evening comes.

I dry up like grass.

## God remembers Jerusalem

## Psalm 102:12

But you, LORD, are king forever.

One generation after another will remember your name.

## Psalm 102:13

You will rise and show kindness to Zion.

The time you chose to help the city has come.

**Where is Zion?**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem. The person praying hopes to see God rebuild this city.

## Psalm 102:14

Your servants love even the stones of the city.

The sight of its dust moves them to pity.

## Psalm 102:15

The nations will honor the LORD with awe.

Every king on earth will tremble at your greatness.

## Psalm 102:16

The LORD will rebuild Zion.

He has shown himself in all his glory.

## Psalm 102:17

He will listen when people who have nothing pray.

Their prayers matter to him.

## Write it down for the children

## Psalm 102:18

Write this down for those who come after us.

Then children yet to be born can praise the LORD.

## Psalm 102:19

The LORD looked down from his holy place high above.

From heaven he looked at the earth.

## Psalm 102:20

He listened to prisoners groan.

He came to free people who faced a death sentence.

## Psalm 102:21

Then they could speak the LORD’s name in Zion.

They could praise him in Jerusalem.

## Psalm 102:22

Peoples and kingdoms will gather there to serve the LORD.

## God’s years never end

## Psalm 102:23

He has drained my strength on life’s journey.

He has cut my days short.

## Psalm 102:24

I say, “My God, do not take my life halfway through!

Your years go on from one generation to the next.

## Psalm 102:25

Long ago you set the earth in place.

You made the heavens with your own hands.

## Psalm 102:26

The earth and sky will pass away. You will remain.

They will wear out like clothes.

You will change them like a worn coat, and they will be gone.

## Psalm 102:27

You stay the same.

Your years will never end.

## Psalm 102:28

The children of your servants will live in safety.

Their families after them will have a lasting place with you.”
