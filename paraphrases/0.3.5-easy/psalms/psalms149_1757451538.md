# Psalm 149 — Sing together with joy

**About this psalm**

The song begins with people dancing and making music for their King. Then it speaks about the judgment God has written down.

## Praise with music and dancing

## Psalm 149:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

Sing a new song to the LORD.

Praise him when his faithful people gather.

## Psalm 149:2

Israel, rejoice in the God who made you!

People of Zion, rejoice in your King!

## Psalm 149:3

Dance to praise his name.

Play tambourines and harps for him.

![A harp with strings stretched across its frame.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Find the strings**

Imagine a harp playing while people dance together. The people are glad because God is their King.

## God delights in his people

## Psalm 149:4

The LORD delights in his people.

He gives those who suffer the honor of being rescued.

## Psalm 149:5

Let his faithful people celebrate that honor.

Let them shout for joy even on their beds.

## The judgment in the song

## Psalm 149:6

May their mouths be full of praise for God.

May they hold swords with two sharp edges.

## Psalm 149:7

The song calls for punishment to fall on the nations for what they have done.

## Psalm 149:8

Their kings will be bound with chains and their leaders with iron shackles.

## Psalm 149:9

In this way God’s people carry out the judgment written against those nations.

All his faithful people share in this honor. Praise the LORD!
