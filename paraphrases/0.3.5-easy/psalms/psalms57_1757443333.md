# Psalm 57 — I will wake the dawn

**About this psalm**

David has run from Saul and taken shelter in a cave. He asks God to keep him safe. Even there, he finds words to sing.

## David asks God for shelter

## Psalm 57:1

David wrote this song when he fled from Saul into a cave.

It is a Miktam for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “Do Not Destroy.”

God, have mercy on me! Have mercy on me!

I come to you for safety.

I will shelter under your wings until the danger passes.

**What does shelter under wings mean?**

A bird spreads its wings over its young. David wants to stay close to God and trust him for protection.

**A word in the title**

Miktam is an old word for a kind of song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 57:2

I call out to God Most High.

He carries out his plan for me.

## Psalm 57:3

God reaches down from heaven and saves me.

He speaks against those who trample me.

Selah.

God sends me his faithful love and his truth.

## David describes his enemies

## Psalm 57:4

I lie down among people who are like hungry lions.

Their teeth are like spears and arrows.

Their tongues are like sharp swords.

## Psalm 57:5

God, may you be honored above the heavens.

May your glory cover the whole earth.

## Psalm 57:6

My enemies spread a net for my feet.

I lost heart.

They dug a pit in my path.

Then they fell into it themselves!

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears twice in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

**Pause and answer**

Who fell into the pit that David’s enemies dug?

## David gets ready to sing

## Psalm 57:7

My heart is steady, God. My heart is steady.

I will sing and play music.

## Psalm 57:8

Wake up, my soul!

Wake up, harp and lyre!

I will wake the dawn.

![A harp with strings stretched across its frame.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the harp**

Both a harp and a lyre make music when someone plucks their strings. David speaks to them as if they could wake up. He wants to start playing so early that his music greets the morning.

## Psalm 57:9

Lord, I will praise you among the nations.

I will sing to you among all the peoples.

## Psalm 57:10

Your faithful love reaches to the heavens.

Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

## Psalm 57:11

God, may you be honored above the heavens.

May your glory cover the whole earth.
