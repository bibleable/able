# Psalm 44 — Rise up and help us

**About this psalm**

The people remember stories their families told about God. Now their enemies have defeated them. They tell God what has happened and ask him to help.

## What our ancestors told us

## Psalm 44:1

This song from the sons of Korah is for the music leader.

It is called a Maskil.

God, we heard the stories ourselves.

Our ancestors told us what you did long ago.

**A word in the song**

Maskil is an old word used in the titles of several psalms. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 44:2

You drove out nations with your own hand.

You planted our ancestors there.

You broke the power of those peoples and sent them away.

**Why say “planted”?**

When a plant takes root, it grows in one place. The singers mean that God gave their ancestors a place to live.

## Psalm 44:3

Our ancestors could never have taken the land with their swords alone.

Their own strength could never have won it.

Your strong right hand won the victory.

Your face shone on them because you loved them.

## God is our King

## Psalm 44:4

God, you are my King.

You give the word for the people of Jacob to win.

## Psalm 44:5

With your help we push our enemies back.

In your name we trample those who attack us.

## Psalm 44:6

I do not put my trust in my bow.

My sword cannot save me.

## Psalm 44:7

You save us from our enemies.

You put those who hate us to shame.

## Psalm 44:8

All day we tell how great our God is.

We will praise your name forever.

Selah.

**Pause and answer**

Who do the singers say gave their ancestors victory?

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old word in the song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Our enemies have defeated us

## Psalm 44:9

But now you have turned us away and humbled us.

You no longer go out with our armies.

## Psalm 44:10

You made us turn back from our enemies.

Those who hate us took what belonged to us.

## Psalm 44:11

You handed us over like sheep for others to eat.

You scattered us among the nations.

## Psalm 44:12

You sell your people for almost nothing.

You gain nothing from the sale.

## Our neighbors laugh at us

## Psalm 44:13

You let our neighbors insult us.

People all around us laugh at us.

## Psalm 44:14

Other nations now use our name as an insult.

People everywhere make fun of us.

## Psalm 44:15

All day long I see my disgrace.

My face burns with shame.

## Psalm 44:16

I hear people mocking and insulting me.

My enemy wants revenge.

## We have kept our agreement

## Psalm 44:17

All this has happened to us, yet we still remember you.

We have kept our agreement with you.

## Psalm 44:18

In our hearts we have stayed true to you.

Our feet have stayed on your path.

## Psalm 44:19

Yet you crushed us where wild jackals live.

You covered us in deep darkness.

**What is a jackal?**

A jackal is a wild animal related to a dog. The singers picture themselves far from safety.

## Psalm 44:20

Suppose we had forgotten our God’s name.

Suppose we had lifted our hands to pray to another god.

## Psalm 44:21

Surely God would have known!

He knows even our secret thoughts.

## Psalm 44:22

Yet because we belong to you, we face death all day.

People see us as sheep ready for slaughter.

![A sheep pen with an open gate.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

**Look at the pen**

Imagine sheep taken from the pen with no one to protect them. The singers use sheep to show how helpless they feel before their enemies.

## The people call for help

## Psalm 44:23

Wake up, Lord! Why are you sleeping?

Get up! Please do not turn us away forever.

## Psalm 44:24

Why do you hide your face?

Why do you forget how others hurt us and hold us down?

## Psalm 44:25

We have sunk into the dust.

Our bodies lie flat against the ground.

## Psalm 44:26

Rise up and help us!

Set us free because of your faithful love.
