# Psalm 37 — Trust the LORD and do good

**About this psalm**

David has seen cruel people grow rich and powerful. Now that he is old, he tells others what he has learned about waiting for the LORD.

## Trust the LORD

## Psalm 37:1

David wrote this song.

Do not worry about people who do evil.

Do not envy people who do wrong.

## Psalm 37:2

They will soon dry up like grass.

They will wilt like young green plants.

## Psalm 37:3

Trust the LORD and do good.

Live in the land and practice being faithful.

## Psalm 37:4

Find your joy in the LORD.

He will give you what your heart longs for.

## Psalm 37:5

Put your life in the LORD’s hands.

Trust him, and he will act.

## Psalm 37:6

He will show that you did right.

It will be as clear as the morning light.

Your just cause will shine like the sun at noon.

## Wait patiently

## Psalm 37:7

Be still before the LORD. Wait patiently for him.

Do not worry when people succeed with cruel plans.

## Psalm 37:8

Let go of your anger. Put fury aside.

Do not let worry drive you to do harm.

## Psalm 37:9

Those who do evil will lose their place.

Those who hope in the LORD will inherit the land.

## Psalm 37:10

In a little while the wicked will be gone.

Look where they used to be. You will not find them.

## Psalm 37:11

Humble people will inherit the land.

They will enjoy having plenty.

![A boundary stone marks where two fields meet.](../pictures/field-boundary.svg)

**Look at the field**

Imagine receiving a field as your own. To inherit the land means to receive it as a lasting home.

## Cruel plans come to ruin

## Psalm 37:12

People who do evil plot against those who do right.

They grind their teeth at them in anger.

## Psalm 37:13

The Lord laughs at those who plan evil.

He sees the day coming when they must answer for it.

## Psalm 37:14

They draw swords and bend bows to attack the poor.

They mean to kill people who live honestly.

## Psalm 37:15

Their swords will pierce their own hearts.

Their bows will break.

## What lasts

## Psalm 37:16

An honest person may have very little.

That little is better than the riches of many wicked people.

## Psalm 37:17

Those who do evil will lose the strength of their arms.

The LORD holds up those who do right.

## Psalm 37:18

The LORD knows every day of those who live honestly.

What he gives them will be theirs forever.

## Psalm 37:19

When trouble comes, they will not face shame.

When food is scarce, they will have enough.

## Psalm 37:20

The LORD’s enemies will die.

Like the beauty of a field, they will fade.

Like smoke, they will disappear.

## People who share

## Psalm 37:21

People who do evil borrow and never pay back.

People who do right gladly share what they have.

## Psalm 37:22

Those the LORD blesses will inherit the land.

Those he curses will come to ruin.

## Psalm 37:23

The LORD guides a person’s steps.

He takes joy in the way that person lives.

## Psalm 37:24

Even if that person falls, they will not stay down.

The LORD holds their hand.

**A picture of help**

Think of someone holding your hand when you stumble. David uses this picture to show how the LORD supports people.

## David remembers a lifetime

## Psalm 37:25

I was young once. Now I am old.

I have never seen the LORD abandon those who do right.

I have never seen their children begging for bread.

## Psalm 37:26

They keep giving and are quick to lend.

Their children bring blessing to others.

**Pause and answer**

What do people who do right do with what they have?

## Psalm 37:27

Turn from evil and do good.

Then you will have a home forever.

## Psalm 37:28

The LORD loves what is fair.

He never abandons his faithful people. He keeps them safe forever.

The families of those who do evil will die out.

## Psalm 37:29

Those who do right will inherit the land.

They will live there forever.

## People who speak wisely

## Psalm 37:30

People who do right speak wisely.

They say what is fair.

## Psalm 37:31

They keep God’s teaching in their hearts.

Their feet stay steady on his path.

## Psalm 37:32

People who do evil wait in hiding.

They hope to kill those who do right.

## Psalm 37:33

The LORD will never leave his people in their enemies’ hands.

When they face a judge, he will not let them be found guilty.

## Psalm 37:34

Wait for the LORD. Follow his way.

He will lift you up to inherit the land.

You will see those who do evil lose their place.

## David remembers a powerful man

## Psalm 37:35

I once saw a cruel man doing well.

He was like a great tree rooted in its own soil.

## Psalm 37:36

Then he was gone.

I looked for him and could not find him.

## Psalm 37:37

Notice the people who live honest lives.

Those who live in peace have a future for their families.

## Psalm 37:38

Those who keep doing wrong will all come to ruin.

Their future will come to an end.

## The LORD gives shelter

## Psalm 37:39

The LORD saves those who do right.

When trouble comes, he is their strong place of safety.

## Psalm 37:40

The LORD helps them and sets them free.

He saves them from people who do evil because they seek shelter in him.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David tell people to do when they see others doing wrong?
