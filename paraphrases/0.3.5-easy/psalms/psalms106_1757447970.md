# Psalm 106 — Remember us and gather us home

**About this psalm**

The people thank the LORD for his lasting love. They tell the truth about the wrong they and their ancestors have done. They ask God to bring them together again.

## A song of thanks and a prayer

## Psalm 106:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

Thank the LORD because he is good.

His love lasts forever.

## Psalm 106:2

Who can tell all the mighty things the LORD has done?

Who can give him all the praise he deserves?

## Psalm 106:3

God blesses those who stand up for what is fair.

They keep doing what is right.

## Psalm 106:4

LORD, when you show kindness to your people, remember me.

Come and save me too.

## Psalm 106:5

Let me see good things come to the people you chose.

Let me share their joy.

Let me praise you with the people who belong to you.

## Our ancestors at the Red Sea

## Psalm 106:6

We have sinned, just as our ancestors did.

We have done what is wrong and evil.

## Psalm 106:7

In Egypt our ancestors did not understand your wonders.

They forgot how much kindness you had shown them.

At the Red Sea they turned against you.

## Psalm 106:8

Yet the LORD saved them for the honor of his name.

He wanted people to know his power.

## Psalm 106:9

He spoke sharply to the Red Sea, and it dried up.

He led them through the deep on ground as dry as a desert.

## Psalm 106:10

He rescued them from those who hated them.

He set them free from their enemies.

## Psalm 106:11

Water covered the enemies who followed.

None of those enemies survived.

## Psalm 106:12

Then our ancestors believed his promises.

They sang to praise him.

## The people forget

## Psalm 106:13

But soon they forgot what he had done.

They did not wait for him to guide them.

## Psalm 106:14

In the wilderness they longed for food they did not have.

They put God to the test there in the desert.

## Psalm 106:15

God gave them what they asked for.

But he sent a sickness that made them waste away.

## Psalm 106:16

In the camp, people grew jealous of Moses.

They also envied Aaron, whom the LORD had set apart.

## Psalm 106:17

The ground opened and swallowed Dathan.

It closed over the people gathered with Abiram.

## Psalm 106:18

Fire swept through their group.

The flames killed those who had done evil.

## A calf made of metal

## Psalm 106:19

At Mount Horeb the people shaped melted metal into a calf.

They bowed down to the statue.

**What is the calf?**

A calf is a young cow. Here the people make a statue shaped like a calf and treat it as their god.

## Psalm 106:20

They traded the glory of their God for a statue of an ox that eats grass.

## Psalm 106:21

They forgot the God who saved them.

He had done great things in Egypt.

## Psalm 106:22

He had done wonders there in the land of Ham.

His mighty deeds at the Red Sea filled people with awe.

## Psalm 106:23

God said he would destroy them.

But Moses, whom God had chosen, stood before him to plead for them.

God turned from his anger and spared them.

## The people refuse the land

## Psalm 106:24

The people rejected the good land.

They did not believe God’s promise.

## Psalm 106:25

They complained inside their tents.

They refused to listen to the LORD.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Picture families talking inside their tents. The singer remembers how they complained about the land God had promised.

## Psalm 106:26

So the LORD raised his hand and made a solemn promise.

He said they would fall in the wilderness.

## Psalm 106:27

He would scatter their children among other nations.

Their families would live far apart in different lands.

## At Peor and Meribah

## Psalm 106:28

The people bound themselves to a god called Baal of Peor.

They ate food offered to gods that had no life.

## Psalm 106:29

What they did made the LORD angry.

A deadly sickness spread among them.

## Psalm 106:30

Then Phinehas stood up and took action.

The sickness stopped spreading.

## Psalm 106:31

God counted what Phinehas did as right.

Generations to come would remember it that way.

## Psalm 106:32

At the waters of Meribah, the people angered the LORD.

Moses got into trouble because of them.

## Psalm 106:33

They turned against God’s Spirit.

Moses spoke without thinking.

## The people follow other gods

## Psalm 106:34

They did not destroy the peoples the LORD had told them to destroy.

## Psalm 106:35

Instead, they lived among those nations and learned their customs.

## Psalm 106:36

They worshiped the nations’ idols.

That worship caught them like a trap.

## Psalm 106:37

They offered their sons and daughters as sacrifices to demons.

## Psalm 106:38

They killed their innocent children as offerings to the idols of Canaan.

The blood they shed stained the land.

## Psalm 106:39

Their actions made them unclean.

They betrayed God like a spouse who breaks a marriage promise.

## God hears his people cry

## Psalm 106:40

The LORD grew fiercely angry with his people.

He could hardly bear the sight of the people who belonged to him.

## Psalm 106:41

He handed them over to other nations.

People who hated them became their rulers.

## Psalm 106:42

Their enemies treated them cruelly and forced them to obey.

## Psalm 106:43

Many times God rescued them.

Still they chose to turn against him.

Their wrongdoing brought them low.

## Psalm 106:44

Even then God heard their cries.

He saw how much they suffered.

## Psalm 106:45

He remembered his covenant with them.

Because his love was so great, he held back his punishment.

**What is a covenant?**

A covenant is a binding promise. Even when the people cried out from other lands, God remembered his promise to them.

## Psalm 106:46

He moved everyone who held them prisoner to show them pity.

## Gather us together

## Psalm 106:47

Save us, LORD our God!

Gather us from the nations so we can thank you together.

Let us rejoice as we praise your holy name.

## Psalm 106:48

Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, forever and ever!

Let everyone say, “Amen!”

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

**Pause and answer**

What do the people ask God to do when they are scattered among the nations?
