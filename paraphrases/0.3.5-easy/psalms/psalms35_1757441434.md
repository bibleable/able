# Psalm 35 — David asks the LORD to defend him

**About this psalm**

David prayed for people when they were sick. Now some of those same people laugh at his pain. David asks the LORD to speak up for him.

## David calls for help

## Psalm 35:1

David wrote this song.

LORD, stand against those who stand against me.

Fight those who fight me.

## Psalm 35:2

Pick up your shields.

Get up and come to help me.

## Psalm 35:3

Raise your spears against those who chase me.

Tell me deep inside, “I am the one who saves you.”

## David describes the trap

## Psalm 35:4

Let those who want me dead face shame and disgrace.

Turn back those who plan to hurt me.

Leave them confused.

## Psalm 35:5

Let the LORD’s angel drive them away like dry husks in the wind.

## Psalm 35:6

Let their path grow dark and slippery as the LORD’s angel chases them.

## Psalm 35:7

I gave them no reason to hide a net for me.

I gave them no reason to dig a pit for me.

## Psalm 35:8

Let trouble catch them by surprise.

Let them get caught in their own net.

Let them fall into the trap they made.

![A net made of crossing ropes.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Imagine a person caught in a net meant for someone else. David asks that the people planning his ruin get caught in their own plans.

## David promises to give thanks

## Psalm 35:9

Then I will rejoice in the LORD.

I will be glad because he saved me.

## Psalm 35:10

With my whole body I will say, “LORD, who is like you?

You rescue hurting people from those too strong for them.

You save poor people from those who rob them.”

## People David cared for accuse him

## Psalm 35:11

Witnesses who hate me step forward.

They accuse me of things I know nothing about.

## Psalm 35:12

I did good to them. They hurt me in return.

I feel as sad as someone who has lost a loved one.

## Psalm 35:13

When they were sick, I wore rough cloth to show my grief.

I went without food to humble myself.

I prayed, but my prayers went unanswered.

## Psalm 35:14

I walked about grieving as for a friend or brother.

I bent low with sorrow as if my mother had died.

**Pause and answer**

What did David do when the people who now accuse him were sick?

## Psalm 35:15

Yet when I stumbled, they gathered to celebrate.

Even strangers joined in the attack.

They kept telling lies about me.

## Psalm 35:16

Like people at a feast who mock others and ignore God, they made fun of me.

They ground their teeth at me.

## David asks the Lord to rescue him

## Psalm 35:17

Lord, how long will you watch?

Save my life from these people who tear at me like lions.

## Psalm 35:18

Then I will thank you when the people gather.

I will praise you before a great crowd.

## Psalm 35:19

Do not let my enemies celebrate my pain.

They hate me for no reason.

Do not let them wink at each other to mock me.

## Psalm 35:20

Their words stir up trouble.

They plan lies about people who live quietly in the land.

## Psalm 35:21

They open their mouths wide and shout at me,

“Aha! Aha! We saw it ourselves!”

## David asks the LORD to speak

## Psalm 35:22

LORD, you have seen what happened.

Please speak up. Stay close to me, Lord.

## Psalm 35:23

My God and my Lord, wake up!

Rise to defend me and speak for my cause.

## Psalm 35:24

LORD my God, judge fairly and clear my name.

Do not let them celebrate my defeat.

## Psalm 35:25

Do not let them think, “This is just what we wanted!”

Do not let them say, “We swallowed him up!”

**What does swallowing someone up mean?**

The enemies want to be able to say they have completely destroyed David.

## Psalm 35:26

Let those who enjoy my troubles face shame and confusion.

Let those who boast that they are better than me face disgrace.

## A shout of joy

## Psalm 35:27

Let those who want my name cleared shout for joy.

May they always say, “How great is the LORD!

He delights in seeing his servant do well.”

## Psalm 35:28

Then I will tell how you do what is right.

All day long I will sing your praise.
