# Psalm 71 — Stay with me when I grow old

**About this psalm**

The singer has trusted God since childhood. Now enemies think God has left him. He asks God to stay close so he can tell younger people what God has done.

## A safe place to go

## Psalm 71:1

LORD, I have come to you for safety.

Never let me end up in shame.

## Psalm 71:2

You do what is right.

Rescue me and set me free.

Listen to me and save me.

## Psalm 71:3

Be the rock where I can always go for safety.

Give the order to save me.

You are my rock and my fortress.

**Why call God a fortress?**

A fortress has strong walls where people can shelter. The singer trusts God to keep him safe when enemies come.

## Psalm 71:4

My God, rescue me from those who do evil.

Set me free from the grip of cruel, unfair people.

## The singer remembers being young

## Psalm 71:5

Lord GOD, you are my hope.

I have trusted you since I was young.

## Psalm 71:6

I have depended on you since birth.

You brought me safely from my mother’s womb.

I will always praise you.

## Psalm 71:7

Many people see what happens to me as a sign.

But you are my strong place of safety.

## Psalm 71:8

All day I praise you.

I tell people how great you are.

## A prayer for help in old age

## Psalm 71:9

When I grow old, do not cast me aside.

Stay with me when my strength gives out.

## Psalm 71:10

My enemies speak against me.

Those who want me dead make plans together.

## Psalm 71:11

They say, “God has left him.

Chase him and catch him!

No one will rescue him.”

## Psalm 71:12

God, stay close to me.

My God, hurry to help me!

## Psalm 71:13

Let those who accuse me face shame and come to an end.

Let those who want to hurt me face scorn and disgrace.

## The singer will keep hoping

## Psalm 71:14

I will keep hoping in you.

I will praise you more and more.

## Psalm 71:15

All day I will tell how you do right and save people.

I cannot tell the full story of all you have done.

## Psalm 71:16

I will come with the strength the Lord GOD gives me.

I will speak about your goodness, yours alone.

## Good news for the next generation

## Psalm 71:17

God, you have taught me since I was young.

I still tell people about the wonderful things you have done.

## Psalm 71:18

God, stay with me even when my hair turns gray.

Let me tell the next generation how powerful you are.

Let those still to come hear of your strength.

**Who is the next generation?**

The singer means people growing up after him. He wants them to hear what God has done.

## Psalm 71:19

God, your goodness reaches to the sky.

You have done great things.

Who else is like you, God?

## Psalm 71:20

You have let me face many terrible troubles.

You will give me life again.

Even from deep in the earth, you will lift me up.

## Psalm 71:21

You will give me greater honor.

You will comfort me again.

## The singer picks up a harp

## Psalm 71:22

My God, I will play the harp to praise you.

You keep your promises.

Holy One of Israel, I will play the lyre and sing to you.

![A harp and its strings.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Find the strings**

Follow one string with your finger. A harp and a lyre both have strings that a player plucks. The singer wants to make music to thank God.

## Psalm 71:23

When I sing to you, I will shout for joy.

My whole being rejoices because you set me free.

## Psalm 71:24

All day I will tell how you do what is right.

Those who tried to hurt me face shame and confusion.
