# Psalm 16 — The LORD shows David the path of life

**About this psalm**

David thinks about what makes him glad. Above all, he wants to be close to the LORD.

## David chooses the LORD

## Psalm 16:1

David wrote this song, called a Miktam.

God, keep me safe.

I come to you for shelter.

**A word in the song**

Miktam is an old word used in the titles of several psalms. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 16:2

I said to the LORD, “You are my Lord.

Without you, I have nothing good.”

## Psalm 16:3

The people in this land who belong to God are wonderful.

They bring me great joy.

## Psalm 16:4

People who chase after other gods face more and more sorrow.

I will never pour out blood as a gift to those gods.

I will never say their names.

## David delights in his share

## Psalm 16:5

LORD, you are the share I choose and my cup.

You keep my share safe for me.

## Psalm 16:6

The lines around my land mark out a lovely place.

What I have received brings me joy.

![A stone marks where two fields meet.](../pictures/field-boundary.svg)

**Look at the field**

The stone shows where one field ends and the next begins. David pictures receiving a good share of land and a cup to drink from. The gift David treasures is the LORD himself.

## The LORD stands beside David

## Psalm 16:7

I praise the LORD who guides me.

Even at night, my own thoughts teach me what is right.

## Psalm 16:8

I always keep the LORD in mind.

He stands at my right hand.

Nothing can shake me.

## David looks forward to life with God

## Psalm 16:9

So my heart is glad.

I speak with joy.

Even my body can rest in safety.

## Psalm 16:10

You will never leave me in Sheol, the place of the dead.

You will never let the body of your Holy One rot away.

## Psalm 16:11

You have shown me the path that leads to life.

With you I will be full of joy.

At your right hand, joy lasts forever.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David look forward to when he thinks about being with the LORD?
