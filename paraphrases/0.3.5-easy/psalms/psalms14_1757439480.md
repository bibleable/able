# Psalm 14 — The LORD shelters people in trouble

**About this psalm**

David describes what the LORD sees when he looks at people. David longs for the day the LORD will bring his people home.

## The LORD looks for people who seek him

## Psalm 14:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

A foolish person thinks, “There is no God.”

People have turned bad. They do terrible things.

No one does what is good.

## Psalm 14:2

The LORD looks down from heaven at all people.

Does anyone understand?

Is anyone looking for God?

## Psalm 14:3

Everyone has turned away.

Together they have gone bad.

No one does what is good.

Not even one.

## The LORD stands with those who suffer

## Psalm 14:4

Will the people who do evil ever learn?

They eat up my people as easily as they eat bread.

They refuse to call on the LORD.

**What does eating people like bread mean?**

Bread is something people eat every day. David means these people harm others as casually as they eat a meal.

## Psalm 14:5

Now fear overwhelms those who do evil.

God is with the people who do right.

## Psalm 14:6

You who do wrong spoil the plans of people who suffer.

Yet the LORD gives those people shelter.

![A tent and a house, two places where people can take shelter.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Look at the shelters**

Imagine going inside to escape a storm. David uses shelter as a picture of the LORD’s protection.

## David asks for help from Zion

## Psalm 14:7

If only help would come from Zion to save Israel!

When the LORD brings his captive people back, let Jacob rejoice!

Let Israel be glad!

**Two names for one people**

Zion is in Jerusalem. Here Jacob and Israel both mean the people of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, why does David hope the people will soon be glad?
