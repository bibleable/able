# Psalm 68 — God carries our load each day

**About this psalm**

David sings about God leading his people. God cares for people who are alone. Singers and musicians gather to praise him in Jerusalem.

## God rises and his enemies run

## Psalm 68:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

God rises, and his enemies scatter.

Those who hate him run away.

## Psalm 68:2

God, you drive them away like smoke in the wind.

Wax melts near a fire.

In the same way, people who do evil perish before you.

## Psalm 68:3

But people who do right are glad to see God.

They celebrate with joy.

## Psalm 68:4

Sing to God and praise his name!

Give honor to the one who rides on the clouds.

His name is the LORD.

Be glad in his presence.

## God gives lonely people a home

## Psalm 68:5

From his holy home, God cares for children without fathers.

He is a father to them.

He stands up for women whose husbands have died.

## Psalm 68:6

God gives lonely people a home with a family.

He leads prisoners out to a good life.

But those who turn against him live on sunbaked land.

## God leads his people through dry land

## Psalm 68:7

God, you went ahead of your people.

You marched through the dry, empty land.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 68:8

The earth shook before you.

The sky poured down rain.

You are Israel’s God, who appeared on Mount Sinai.

## Psalm 68:9

God, you sent plenty of rain.

It brought new life to the worn-out land that belongs to you.

## Psalm 68:10

Your people settled there like a flock of sheep.

God, you had plenty to give.

You gave poor people what they needed.

## Women announce the victory

## Psalm 68:11

The Lord gives the word.

A great crowd of women calls out the news.

## Psalm 68:12

They say, “Kings are running away with their armies!

The woman who stayed home shares out what they left behind.

## Psalm 68:13

Even while you rest among the sheep pens, look at the dove.

Silver covers its wings.

Its feathers shine like gold.”

## Psalm 68:14

God, who has all power, scattered the kings across the land.

It was like snow falling on Mount Zalmon.

## God chooses a mountain for his home

## Psalm 68:15

Mount Bashan is a great mountain of God.

It has many high peaks.

## Psalm 68:16

Mountains with many peaks, why look with envy at this mountain?

God chose this mountain for his home.

The LORD will live here forever.

![A high mountain with a path up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

Follow the path up the mountain. David imagines the high peaks of Bashan looking across at the mountain God chose in Jerusalem.

## Psalm 68:17

God has thousands upon thousands of chariots.

The Lord is in his holy place, just as he was at Sinai.

**What is a chariot?**

A chariot is a cart that horses pull. Armies used chariots in battle.

## Psalm 68:18

God, you climbed to the heights.

You led away prisoners.

Even people who fought against you gave you gifts.

You, the LORD God, came to live there.

## God carries his people’s load

## Psalm 68:19

Praise the Lord!

Each day he carries our heavy load.

He is the God who saves us.

Selah.

**What load does God carry?**

A heavy load can be hard to lift alone. David uses this picture to say that God helps his people with their troubles each day.

## Psalm 68:20

Our God sets us free.

The Lord GOD rescues us from death.

## Psalm 68:21

God will crush the heads of his enemies.

He will strike the heads, hair and all, of those who keep doing wrong.

## Psalm 68:22

The Lord said, “I will bring my enemies back from Bashan.

Even from the bottom of the sea I will bring them back.

## Psalm 68:23

Then you will dip your foot in your enemies’ blood.

Even your dogs will lick it.”

## Singers lead the march

## Psalm 68:24

God, people have watched your march.

You are my God and King.

They saw you enter your holy place.

## Psalm 68:25

Singers walk in front.

Musicians follow.

Young women among them play tambourines.

**What is a tambourine?**

A tambourine is a small instrument you can hold in your hand. You shake it or tap it to keep time with a song.

## Psalm 68:26

Praise God when the people gather!

All of you who come from Israel, praise the LORD!

## Psalm 68:27

Benjamin, the youngest tribe, leads them.

The leaders of Judah come together.

The leaders of Zebulun and Naphtali come too.

## David asks God to use his strength

## Psalm 68:28

God, show your power!

Use your strength for us as you have done before.

## Psalm 68:29

Kings will bring you gifts because your temple stands in Jerusalem.

## Psalm 68:30

Speak against the wild beast among the reeds.

Speak against the bulls among the calves of the nations.

Make the beast bow down and bring bars of silver.

Scatter the nations that love war.

**Why describe nations as animals?**

The song pictures nations that love war as powerful animals. David asks God to stop them.

## Psalm 68:31

Egypt will send messengers.

The people of Cush will reach out their hands to God.

## All kingdoms are invited to sing

## Psalm 68:32

Kingdoms everywhere on earth, sing to God!

Sing to praise the Lord.

Selah.

## Psalm 68:33

He rides through the highest heavens, there since ancient times.

Listen! His powerful voice rings out.

## Psalm 68:34

Tell everyone that power belongs to God.

He rules in greatness over Israel.

His strength fills the skies.

## Psalm 68:35

God, we stand in wonder before you in your holy place.

The God of Israel gives his people strength and power.

Praise God!
