# Psalm 62 — Tell God what is in your heart

**About this psalm**

People threaten David. He reminds himself why he can rest. Then he invites the people to trust God with their troubles.

## David finds rest in God

## Psalm 62:1

David wrote this song for the music leader, according to Jeduthun.

Only in God do I find rest.

God is the one who saves me.

## Psalm 62:2

God alone is my rock.

He saves me.

He is my fortress.

Nothing will ever shake me loose.

**What does David mean by a rock and a fortress?**

A rock gives someone a firm place to stand. A fortress is a strong place to shelter. David trusts God to hold him steady and keep him safe.

## People threaten to bring a man down

## Psalm 62:3

How long will all of you threaten one man?

Will you push him over like a leaning wall?

Will you knock him down like a shaky fence?

## Psalm 62:4

They mean to pull him down from his high position.

They enjoy telling lies.

They speak good wishes out loud.

Inside, they wish him harm.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## David reminds himself to trust God

## Psalm 62:5

I tell myself, “Rest in God alone.”

God gives me hope.

## Psalm 62:6

God alone is my rock.

He saves me.

He is my fortress.

Nothing will shake me loose.

## Psalm 62:7

I depend on God to save me.

My honor comes from him.

God is my strong rock.

I go to him for safety.

## David speaks to the people

## Psalm 62:8

People, trust God at all times.

Tell him everything that is in your hearts.

God keeps us safe.

Selah.

## Psalm 62:9

Ordinary people are like a breath that soon disappears.

The power of important people can fool us.

Put them all on one side of a scale.

That side rises. Together they weigh no more than a breath.

## Psalm 62:10

Never count on what you get by threatening people.

Never count on things you steal.

Even if your wealth grows, do not put your trust in it.

![Ten silver coins.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Look at the coins**

Imagine getting more coins tomorrow. David says to keep trusting God even when you have more money.

## David remembers what God said

## Psalm 62:11

God spoke once.

Twice I heard this message.

Power belongs to God.

## Psalm 62:12

Lord, your love lasts.

You repay each person for what they do.
