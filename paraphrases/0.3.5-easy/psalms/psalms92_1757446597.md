# Psalm 92 — Praise in the morning and at night

**About this psalm**

This is a song for the Sabbath, the weekly day of rest. The singer thanks God for what he has done and pictures people who do right as strong, growing trees.

## A song for the day of rest

## Psalm 92:1

A song for the Sabbath day.

It is good to thank the LORD.

God Most High, it is good to sing your praise.

## Psalm 92:2

In the morning we tell of your lasting love.

At night we tell how you keep your word.

## Psalm 92:3

We play the harp with ten strings.

We play a tune on the lyre.

![A harp and its strings.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Find the strings**

Imagine plucking a string while someone sings. The singers use their instruments to thank God.

## God’s work makes the singer glad

## Psalm 92:4

LORD, what you have done makes me glad.

I sing for joy when I think of your work.

## Psalm 92:5

LORD, how great are the things you do!

How deep your thoughts are!

## Psalm 92:6

Someone without sense misses this.

A foolish person fails to understand.

## Psalm 92:7

People who do evil may spring up like grass.

They may seem to do well for a time.

But they will perish forever.

## The LORD rules forever

## Psalm 92:8

You, LORD, will always reign on high!

## Psalm 92:9

LORD, your enemies will surely perish.

All who do evil will scatter.

## Psalm 92:10

You have raised my horn like the horn of a wild ox.

You have honored me with fine oil.

**Why mention a horn?**

The horn of a wild ox pictures strength. The singer thanks God for making him strong.

## Psalm 92:11

I see my enemies fall.

I hear those who did evil crying out.

## Trees that still bear fruit

## Psalm 92:12

People who do right will grow like palm trees.

They will grow tall like the cedar trees of Lebanon.

## Psalm 92:13

They are like trees planted at the LORD’s house.

They grow strong in our God’s courtyards.

## Psalm 92:14

Even in old age they will still bear fruit.

They will stay healthy and green.

**What does bearing fruit mean here?**

A healthy tree keeps giving fruit. People who trust God can keep doing good and telling others about him as they grow old.

## Psalm 92:15

They will say, “The LORD does what is right.

He is my rock.

He never does wrong.”
