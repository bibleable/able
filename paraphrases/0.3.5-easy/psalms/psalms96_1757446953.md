# Psalm 96 — Sing to the LORD a new song

**About this psalm**

The song invites people in every land to praise the LORD. Even the sea and the trees join the celebration because God is coming to judge fairly.

## A song for the whole earth

## Psalm 96:1

Sing to the LORD a new song!

Everyone on earth, sing to the LORD!

## Psalm 96:2

Sing to the LORD and praise his name.

Day after day, tell how he saves.

## Psalm 96:3

Tell every nation how great he is.

Tell all peoples about the wonders he has done.

## The LORD made the heavens

## Psalm 96:4

The LORD is great and deserves great praise.

Honor him with awe above all gods.

## Psalm 96:5

The gods the nations worship are idols.

The LORD made the heavens.

**What is an idol?**

An idol is something people make and worship as a god.

## Psalm 96:6

Greatness and beauty surround him.

Strength and beauty fill his holy place.

## Families come to worship

## Psalm 96:7

Families of every nation, give the LORD honor.

Tell of his greatness and strength.

## Psalm 96:8

Give the LORD the honor his name deserves.

Bring him a gift and come into his courtyards.

## Psalm 96:9

Worship the LORD in the beauty of his holiness.

Everyone on earth, tremble in his presence.

## The world rejoices at God’s coming

## Psalm 96:10

Tell the nations, “The LORD is king!”

The world stands firm. Nothing can move it.

He will judge all peoples fairly.

## Psalm 96:11

Let the sky and the earth be glad!

Let the sea roar with everything that lives in it!

## Psalm 96:12

Let the fields rejoice with everything growing there!

Then all the forest trees will sing for joy.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the grain**

Picture a whole field of stalks moving in the wind. The singer imagines everything in the field celebrating God’s coming.

## Psalm 96:13

They will sing before the LORD because he is coming.

He is coming to judge the earth.

He will do what is right for the whole world.

He will judge its people faithfully.

**Pause and answer**

How will the LORD judge the people of the world?
