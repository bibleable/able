# Psalm 39 — LORD, show me how short my life is

**About this psalm**

David tries to stay quiet, but his sorrow grows. When he speaks, he asks the LORD to help him understand how quickly his days pass.

## David tries to stay silent

## Psalm 39:1

David wrote this song for the music leader, for Jeduthun.

I said, “I will watch what I do.

I want to keep from sinning with my words.

While people who do evil are here, I will keep my mouth shut.”

## Psalm 39:2

I stayed still and said nothing.

I kept quiet even when I had something good to say.

My sorrow grew.

## Psalm 39:3

My heart grew hot inside me.

The more I thought, the more the fire burned.

Then I spoke.

**What does the fire mean?**

David is describing a feeling. His thoughts and sorrow grew so strong that he could no longer keep quiet.

## David asks about his days

## Psalm 39:4

LORD, show me how my life will end.

Help me see how few days I have.

Help me understand how quickly my life passes.

## Psalm 39:5

You made my days as short as the width of a hand.

To you my whole life is hardly any time at all.

Even at our best, we last only a breath.

Selah.

**Look at your hand**

Look at the short distance across your palm. David uses this small measure to picture how short his life is.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears here and again in verse 11. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 39:6

People pass through life like shadows.

They rush about, but what does it gain them?

They pile up riches without knowing who will take them away.

## David puts his hope in the Lord

## Psalm 39:7

Now, Lord, what am I waiting for?

I put my hope in you.

## Psalm 39:8

Save me from all the wrong I have done.

Do not let foolish people make fun of me.

## Psalm 39:9

I have fallen silent. I keep my mouth shut.

It is you who have acted.

## David asks for relief

## Psalm 39:10

Please stop striking me.

Your hand presses so hard on me that I am dying.

## Psalm 39:11

You correct people for the wrong they do.

Like a moth eating cloth, you wear away what they treasure.

Every person is like a mist that disappears.

Selah.

![A cloak made of cloth.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

**Picture the cloth**

Imagine finding little moth holes in a cloak you treasure. David uses the worn cloth to show how something precious can disappear.

## Psalm 39:12

LORD, hear my prayer. Listen to my cry for help.

Please hear me weeping.

I live with you as a guest from another land.

My ancestors were guests like me.

## Psalm 39:13

Look away from me so I can smile again before I leave this life and am gone.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David hope to feel again before his life ends?
