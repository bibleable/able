# Psalm 59 — David sings to God in the morning

**About this psalm**

Saul sends men to watch David’s house and kill him. David asks God for help. David looks forward to singing to God in the morning.

## David asks God to protect him

## Psalm 59:1

David wrote this song, called a Miktam, for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “Do Not Destroy.”

Saul had sent men to watch David’s house and kill him.

My God, save me from my enemies.

Keep me safe from those who attack me.

**A word in the title**

Miktam is an old word for a kind of song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 59:2

Save me from people who do evil.

Save me from men who kill.

## Psalm 59:3

Look! They hide and wait for me.

Fierce men plan to hurt me.

LORD, I have done nothing wrong to bring this on myself.

## Psalm 59:4

They rush to attack me without cause.

Come and help me!

Look at what is happening!

## Psalm 59:5

LORD, you lead heaven’s armies.

You are the God of Israel.

Rise up and punish all the nations.

Show no mercy to those who betray others and do evil.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old word used in some Bible songs. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## The men come back in the evening

## Psalm 59:6

They come back in the evening.

Like growling dogs, they roam around the city.

## Psalm 59:7

Listen to the cruel words that pour from their mouths.

Their words cut like swords.

They say, “Who can hear us?”

## Psalm 59:8

But you laugh at them, LORD.

You laugh at the nations that oppose you.

## David watches for God

## Psalm 59:9

God, you give me strength.

I will watch for you because you are my fortress.

**What is a fortress?**

A fortress is a strong place where people can go for safety. David trusts God to keep him safe.

## Psalm 59:10

My God, your love stays with me.

You will come to meet me.

You will help me face my enemies without fear.

## David asks God to scatter his enemies

## Psalm 59:11

Do not kill them, or my people will forget.

Use your power to scatter them.

Bring them down, Lord.

You protect us like a shield.

## Psalm 59:12

They do wrong when they speak.

They curse people and tell lies.

Let their proud words catch them like a trap.

## Psalm 59:13

In your anger, put an end to them.

Put an end to them until none are left.

Then people everywhere will know that God rules over Jacob’s people.

Selah.

## David looks ahead to morning

## Psalm 59:14

They come back in the evening.

Like growling dogs, they roam around the city.

## Psalm 59:15

They search for scraps of food.

They growl if they cannot find enough.

## Psalm 59:16

But I will sing about your strength.

In the morning I will sing aloud about your lasting love.

You are my fortress.

When trouble comes, I can go to you for safety.
