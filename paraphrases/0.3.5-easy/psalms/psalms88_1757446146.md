# Psalm 88 — Day and night I call to you

**About this psalm**

Heman feels close to death. His friends are far away. He tells God how lonely he feels and keeps asking God to listen.

## Heman calls out

## Psalm 88:1

A song of the sons of Korah for the music leader.

It is a Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite, according to Mahalath Leannoth.

LORD, you are the God who saves me.

Day and night I cry out to you.

**Words in the title**

Maskil and Mahalath Leannoth are old words used in song titles. Their exact meanings are uncertain.

## Psalm 88:2

Let my prayer reach you.

Listen to my cry.

## Heman feels close to death

## Psalm 88:3

Trouble fills my life.

I am close to Sheol, the place of the dead.

## Psalm 88:4

People count me among those going down to the pit of death.

I feel like a man with no strength left.

## Psalm 88:5

I feel abandoned among the dead.

I am like those who were killed and lie in graves.

You no longer remember them.

They are beyond your care.

## Psalm 88:6

You have put me in the deepest pit.

There is nothing but darkness down here.

## Psalm 88:7

Your anger presses down on me.

Your waves have swept over my head.

Selah.

**Why describe waves?**

Heman feels as helpless as someone under deep water.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## His friends have gone

## Psalm 88:8

You have taken my friends away.

You have made them turn from me in disgust.

I am trapped. I cannot get out.

## Psalm 88:9

My eyes grow dim with sorrow.

LORD, I call to you every day.

I reach out my hands to you.

## Questions about the grave

## Psalm 88:10

Do you do wonders for the dead?

Do the spirits of the dead rise to praise you?

Selah.

## Psalm 88:11

Who can tell of your lasting love in the grave?

Who can speak of your faithfulness in Abaddon, the place of destruction?

## Psalm 88:12

Who will know your wonders in the dark?

Who will know your goodness in the land where all is forgotten?

## Heman prays again in the morning

## Psalm 88:13

Yet I cry to you for help, LORD.

In the morning I bring you my prayer.

## Psalm 88:14

LORD, why do you turn me away?

Why do you hide your face from me?

## Psalm 88:15

Since I was young, I have suffered and come close to death.

I have borne the terrors you sent.

I have lost hope.

## Psalm 88:16

Your anger has swept over me.

The terrors you sent have broken me.

## Psalm 88:17

All day they close around me like water.

They surround me on every side.

## Psalm 88:18

You have taken away the ones I love and my friend.

Darkness is my closest friend.

![A dark night sky above the horizon.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Look at the dark sky**

Heman speaks of darkness as if it could sit beside him. He misses the people who used to keep him company.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Heman keep calling to when he feels alone?
