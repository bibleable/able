# Psalm 61 — Lead me to the high rock

**About this psalm**

David calls to God when he feels weak. He remembers how God has kept him safe. Then he asks God to guard the king.

## David calls out to God

## Psalm 61:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Play it on instruments with strings.

God, hear me cry out!

Listen to my prayer.

## Psalm 61:2

Even from the far ends of the earth, I call to you.

I call when I feel weak inside.

Lead me to the rock high above me.

**Why ask for a high rock?**

Someone standing on a high rock can be out of danger. David wants God to lead him to safety.

## David remembers where he is safe

## Psalm 61:3

You have been a safe place for me.

You are like a strong tower that keeps my enemy away.

## Psalm 61:4

Let me live in your tent forever.

Keep me safe under your wings.

Selah.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Point to the tent. David wants to stay close to God, like a guest who is safe in someone’s tent. He also pictures a bird spreading its wings over its young.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 61:5

God, you heard the promises I made to you.

You gave me a share in what you set aside for those who honor you.

## David asks God to guard the king

## Psalm 61:6

Give the king a long life.

May his years stretch from one generation to the next.

## Psalm 61:7

May the king rule in your presence forever.

Guard him with your lasting love.

Keep him safe because you are faithful.

## Psalm 61:8

Then I will always sing to praise you.

Day after day, I will do what I promised you.
