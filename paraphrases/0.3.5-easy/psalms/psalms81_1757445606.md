# Psalm 81 — If only my people would listen

**About this psalm**

Asaph calls Israel to sing at a feast. Then the people hear God recall the heavy loads they carried in Egypt. God tells them how he longs for them to listen.

## Music for the feast

## Psalm 81:1

Asaph wrote this song for the music leader, according to Gittith.

Sing with joy to God, who gives us strength.

Shout for joy to the God of Jacob!

**A word in the title**

Gittith is an old musical term. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 81:2

Start the song! Tap the tambourine.

Play sweet music on the harp and the lyre.

## Psalm 81:3

Blow the ram’s horn when the new moon comes.

Blow it at the full moon on our feast day.

## Psalm 81:4

The God of Jacob gave Israel this rule.

## Psalm 81:5

God gave this command to Joseph’s people as a reminder when he went out over Egypt.

There I heard words in a language I did not know.

**Who are Joseph’s people?**

Joseph was a son of Jacob. Here his name stands for the people of Israel.

## God remembers the heavy loads

## Psalm 81:6

God says, “I took the heavy load off your shoulders.

Your hands could put down the basket.

![A woven basket with a handle.](../pictures/basket.svg)

**Look at the basket**

Imagine carrying a heavy basket and finally putting it down. God reminds the people that he freed them from hard labor.

## Psalm 81:7

When you were in trouble, you called to me.

I rescued you.

I answered from the thundercloud.

I tested you at the waters of Meribah.”

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## God asks Israel to listen

## Psalm 81:8

God says, “My people, listen to my warning.

Israel, if only you would listen to me!

## Psalm 81:9

Do not keep other gods among you.

Do not bow down to a foreign god.

## Psalm 81:10

I am the LORD your God.

I brought you out of Egypt.

Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.

## Psalm 81:11

But my people would not listen to me.

Israel would not obey me.

## Psalm 81:12

So I let them follow their stubborn hearts.

They went ahead with their own plans.

## What God wants to give his people

## Psalm 81:13

If only my people would listen to me!

If only Israel would live as I teach!

## Psalm 81:14

How quickly I would defeat their enemies!

I would turn my power against those who attack them.

## Psalm 81:15

Those who hate the LORD would pretend to obey him.

Their punishment would last forever.

## Psalm 81:16

But I would feed you the finest wheat.

I would give you honey from the rock until you had enough.”
