# Psalm 120 — I want peace

**About this psalm**

The singer lives among people who tell lies and want war. He remembers calling to God and getting an answer.

## A prayer about lies

## Psalm 120:1

A song for going up to worship.

When I was in trouble, I cried to the LORD.

He answered me.

## Psalm 120:2

LORD, rescue me from people who tell lies.

Save me from those who trick me with their words.

## A warning to those who lie

## Psalm 120:3

You who tell lies, what will God do to you?

What more will he give you?

## Psalm 120:4

A warrior’s sharp arrows will come your way, along with hot coals from a broom tree!

**What is a broom tree?**

It is a desert shrub. Its wood can burn down to hot coals.

## Longing for peace

## Psalm 120:5

How sad I am to live in Meshech and stay among the tents of Kedar!

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Kedar was known for its tents. The singer names Meshech and Kedar as he describes how hard it is to live where he feels like a stranger.

## Psalm 120:6

I have lived far too long among people who hate peace.

## Psalm 120:7

I want peace.

But when I speak, they want war.

**Pause and answer**

What does the singer want when the people around him want war?
