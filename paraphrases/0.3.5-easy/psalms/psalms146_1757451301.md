# Psalm 146 — The LORD helps people in need

**About this psalm**

The singer asks who can be trusted to help. He remembers the hungry, the prisoners, and people living far from home.

## Praise for a lifetime

## Psalm 146:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

With my whole self, I praise the LORD.

## Psalm 146:2

All my life I will praise the LORD.

As long as I live, I will sing to my God.

## Where to place your hope

## Psalm 146:3

Do not put your trust in rulers.

They are human. They cannot save you.

## Psalm 146:4

When a ruler dies, his body returns to the ground.

That very day his plans come to an end.

## Psalm 146:5

How blessed is the person who looks to Jacob’s God for help!

That person hopes in the LORD.

## Psalm 146:6

The LORD made heaven and earth, the sea and everything in them.

He keeps his word forever.

## People the LORD cares for

## Psalm 146:7

He wins justice for people who suffer cruelty.

He gives food to the hungry.

The LORD sets prisoners free.

## Psalm 146:8

The LORD opens blind eyes.

He lifts people who are bent low.

He loves those who do right.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Find the eye**

The singer names several ways the LORD helps people. One is giving sight to people who are blind.

## Psalm 146:9

The LORD protects people from other lands.

He cares for children without fathers and women whose husbands have died.

He stops the plans of those who do wrong.

## Psalm 146:10

People of Zion, the LORD is your God.

He rules forever, through every generation. Praise the LORD!
