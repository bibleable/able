# Psalm 67 — Let all the peoples praise you

**About this psalm**

The singers ask God to show them kindness. They want people in every land to know how God saves.

## The singers ask God for kindness

## Psalm 67:1

This song is for the music leader.

Play it on instruments with strings.

May God show us kindness and bless us.

May his face shine on us.

Selah.

**What does a shining face mean?**

The singers ask God to look on them with kindness.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 67:2

Then people everywhere will know your ways, God.

Every nation will know how you save people.

## Every nation can sing

## Psalm 67:3

God, let the peoples praise you.

Let all the peoples praise you.

## Psalm 67:4

Let the nations be glad and sing for joy.

You judge people fairly.

You guide the nations of the earth.

Selah.

## Psalm 67:5

God, let the peoples praise you.

Let all the peoples praise you.

## The people thank God for the harvest

## Psalm 67:6

The land has given us a harvest.

God, our own God, blesses us.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the grain**

Point to the head of grain. At harvest time, people gather ripe crops for food. The singers thank God for what the land has grown.

## Psalm 67:7

God blesses us.

May people even in the farthest lands honor him with deep respect.
