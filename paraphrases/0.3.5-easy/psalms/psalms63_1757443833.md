# Psalm 63 — David longs for God

**About this psalm**

David is in the dry wilderness of Judah. He remembers meeting God in a holy place. Even at night he thinks about God’s love.

## David longs to be close to God

## Psalm 63:1

David wrote this song in the wilderness of Judah.

God, you are my God.

I look for you with all my heart.

My whole body longs for you.

I thirst for you like someone in dry land without water.

**Why does David speak of thirst?**

In dry country a thirsty person longs for water. That is how deeply David wants to be close to God.

## Psalm 63:2

I have seen you in your holy place.

There I saw your power and your greatness.

## David praises God for his love

## Psalm 63:3

Your lasting love is better than life.

That is why I will praise you.

## Psalm 63:4

I will praise you as long as I live.

I will lift my hands and call on your name.

## Psalm 63:5

You satisfy me like a feast of rich food.

I will gladly sing your praise.

## David remembers God at night

## Psalm 63:6

I remember you as I lie on my bed.

Through the hours of the night I think about you.

![Night sky above a dark horizon.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Look at the night sky**

Picture David awake while it is dark. He spends those quiet hours thinking about God.

## Psalm 63:7

You have helped me.

In the shade of your wings I sing for joy.

**What do the wings mean?**

A bird spreads its wings over its young to protect them. David feels safe with God.

## Psalm 63:8

I hold on tight to you.

Your strong right hand holds me up.

## David trusts God with his life

## Psalm 63:9

People are trying to kill me.

They will go down deep into the earth.

## Psalm 63:10

They will die by the sword.

Foxes will feed on their bodies.

## Psalm 63:11

But the king will rejoice in God.

All who make their promises in God’s name will celebrate.

Those who tell lies will have to fall silent.

**Pause and answer**

What does David do when he lies awake at night?
