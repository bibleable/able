# Psalm 28 — The LORD hears David call for help

**About this psalm**

David lifts his hands and calls to the LORD. When David knows the LORD has heard him, he sings his thanks and prays for the whole people.

## David lifts his hands in prayer

## Psalm 28:1

David wrote this song.

LORD, I call to you. You are my Rock.

Please listen to me.

If you stay silent, I will be like someone going down to the grave.

## Psalm 28:2

Hear me as I beg you for mercy.

I call to you for help.

I lift my hands toward your holy place.

**Why does David call the LORD his Rock?**

Think of a rock that stays firm beneath your feet. David relies on the LORD to hold him steady.

## David asks for justice

## Psalm 28:3

Please do not drag me away with those who do evil.

They speak of peace to their neighbors.

Inside, they want to hurt them.

## Psalm 28:4

Pay them back for the evil things they do.

Give them what their actions deserve.

## Psalm 28:5

They pay no attention to what the LORD has done.

They ignore the work of his hands.

So he will tear them down and never build them up again.

## David gives thanks

## Psalm 28:6

Praise the LORD!

He has heard my cry for mercy.

## Psalm 28:7

The LORD gives me strength and shields me from harm.

I trust him with all my heart. He helps me.

My heart is full of joy.

I sing to thank him.

## David prays for the people

## Psalm 28:8

The LORD gives his people strength.

He is a safe place for the king he chose.

He keeps the king safe like a strong fort.

## Psalm 28:9

Save your people, LORD.

Bless the people who belong to you.

Care for them like a shepherd.

Carry them forever.
