# Psalm 45 — A wedding song for the king

**About this psalm**

A singer brings a love song to a royal wedding. The singer praises the king, speaks to the bride, and pictures the wedding party entering the palace.

## The singer brings a song

## Psalm 45:1

This love song from the sons of Korah is for the music leader.

It is called a Maskil. Sing it to the tune “The Lilies.”

My heart stirs as I think of something wonderful.

I speak the verses I have made for the king.

My tongue is like the pen of a skilled writer.

**A word in the title**

Maskil is an old word used for some psalms. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## The singer praises the king

## Psalm 45:2

King, you are the most handsome of men.

You speak with such grace.

That is why God has blessed you forever.

## Psalm 45:3

Mighty warrior, wear your sword at your side.

Let everyone see your royal splendor.

## Psalm 45:4

Ride out in splendor and win the victory.

Stand for truth, humility, and justice.

May your strong right hand do wonderful and fearsome things.

## Psalm 45:5

King, your arrows pierce the hearts of your enemies.

Nations fall at your feet.

## A throne that lasts

## Psalm 45:6

Your throne, O God, will stand forever.

Justice is the royal staff with which you rule.

![A royal scepter, a staff held by a ruler.](../pictures/esther-gold-scepter.svg)

**Look at the royal staff**

A royal staff is called a scepter. It shows that a ruler has authority. The singer says this ruler uses his authority to do what is fair.

## Psalm 45:7

You love what is right and hate what is evil.

So God, your God, has honored you above your companions.

He poured the oil of joy on you.

## Psalm 45:8

Your clothes smell of myrrh, aloes, and cassia.

Harp music from palaces decorated with ivory makes you glad.

**The scents on the king’s clothes**

Myrrh, aloes, and cassia are fragrant materials from plants. They were used to perfume clothing.

## Psalm 45:9

Daughters of kings are among the women you honor.

The queen stands on your right.

She wears gold from Ophir.

**Pause and answer**

Who stands at the king’s right hand?

## The singer speaks to the bride

## Psalm 45:10

Listen, daughter. Think about what I say.

Leave thoughts of your people and your father’s home behind.

## Psalm 45:11

The king will long for you because you are beautiful.

Bow to him, for he is your lord.

## Psalm 45:12

People from Tyre will come with a gift.

Wealthy people will ask for your favor.

## The wedding party comes in

## Psalm 45:13

In her room the princess looks splendid.

Gold thread shines in the patterns on her gown.

## Psalm 45:14

They lead the bride to the king in colorful clothes.

They bring her young unmarried companions before you, O king.

## Psalm 45:15

With joy they lead the women inside.

The wedding party enters the king’s palace.

## A promise to remember the king

## Psalm 45:16

Your sons will take the place of your fathers.

You will make them princes throughout the land.

## Psalm 45:17

I will keep your name alive in this song.

Each new generation will remember you.

So the nations will praise you forever.
