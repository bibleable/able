# Psalm 104 — All creatures look to you for food

**About this psalm**

The singer watches wild donkeys drink, birds nest, and lions hunt. He sees God caring for creatures on land and in the sea.

## Light, clouds, and wind

## Psalm 104:1

With my whole self, I praise the LORD!

LORD my God, how great you are!

You wear beauty and greatness like royal clothes.

## Psalm 104:2

You wrap light around yourself like a robe.

You spread the sky out like a tent.

**Why picture the sky as a tent?**

When people put up a tent, they spread its covering overhead. The singer imagines God spreading the sky over the earth.

## Psalm 104:3

You lay the beams of your home on the waters above.

You ride the clouds like a chariot.

You walk on the wings of the wind.

## Psalm 104:4

You send winds as your messengers.

Flames of fire serve you.

## The waters find their place

## Psalm 104:5

You set the earth firmly in place.

Nothing can move it.

## Psalm 104:6

You covered the earth with deep water like a blanket.

The water stood higher than the mountains.

## Psalm 104:7

At your command, the waters fled.

At the sound of your thunder, they rushed away.

## Psalm 104:8

The mountains rose. The valleys sank.

Each took the place you had chosen.

## Psalm 104:9

You set a limit the waters cannot cross.

They will never cover the whole earth again.

## Animals come to the springs

## Psalm 104:10

You send springs into the valleys.

Their water flows between the mountains.

## Psalm 104:11

Every wild animal can drink there.

The wild donkeys go there when they are thirsty.

## Psalm 104:12

Birds build nests beside the springs.

They sing among the branches.

## Psalm 104:13

From your home above, you water the mountains.

Your work gives the earth all it needs.

## Food and homes on the land

## Psalm 104:14

You grow grass for the farm animals.

You give people crops to tend.

Food comes up from the soil.

## Psalm 104:15

Wine makes people glad.

Oil makes their faces shine.

Bread gives them strength.

## Psalm 104:16

The LORD’s trees get all the water they need.

He planted the tall cedars of Lebanon.

## Psalm 104:17

Birds build their nests there.

The stork makes her home in a cypress tree.

## Psalm 104:18

Wild goats live high in the mountains.

Small animals called rock badgers find shelter in the cliffs.

## Lions at night and people by day

## Psalm 104:19

You made the moon to mark the seasons.

The sun knows when to set.

![The sun and the moon in the sky.](../pictures/gibeon-sun-moon.svg)

**Find the sun**

Imagine the sun rising over the forest. While the lions return to their dens, people head out to work.

## Psalm 104:20

You bring darkness, and night comes.

Then animals roam the forest.

## Psalm 104:21

Young lions roar as they hunt.

They look to God for their food.

## Psalm 104:22

When the sun rises, the lions leave.

They lie down in their dens.

## Psalm 104:23

Then people go out to work.

They work until evening.

## Life in the wide sea

## Psalm 104:24

LORD, you have made so many things!

You made them all with wisdom.

The earth is full of your creatures.

## Psalm 104:25

Look at the wide sea!

Living things swim there, great and small.

No one could count them all.

## Psalm 104:26

Ships sail across the sea.

Leviathan, the great sea creature you made, plays there.

## God gives food and breath

## Psalm 104:27

All creatures look to you for food at the right time.

## Psalm 104:28

You give them food, and they gather it.

You open your hand, and they eat their fill.

## Psalm 104:29

When you turn away, they are afraid.

When you take away their breath, they die.

Their bodies return to dust.

## Psalm 104:30

You send your Spirit and create new life.

You make the earth new again.

## The singer praises God

## Psalm 104:31

May the LORD’s glory last forever!

May the LORD delight in what he has made!

## Psalm 104:32

He looks at the earth, and it shakes.

He touches the mountains, and they give off smoke.

## Psalm 104:33

I will sing to the LORD all my life.

As long as I live, I will praise my God.

## Psalm 104:34

May my thoughts please him.

The LORD fills me with joy.

## Psalm 104:35

May sinners disappear from the earth.

May those who do evil be gone.

With my whole self, I praise the LORD!

Hallelujah!

**What does Hallelujah mean?**

It means “Praise the LORD!”
