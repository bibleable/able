# Psalm 94 — The God who made the ear can hear

**About this psalm**

Cruel people hurt others and say that God pays no attention. The singer calls on God to judge them. He remembers how God helped him when he was afraid.

## A call for justice

## Psalm 94:1

LORD, you are the God who punishes wrong.

God who punishes wrong, shine out!

## Psalm 94:2

Rise up, Judge of the earth!

Give proud people what their deeds deserve.

## Psalm 94:3

LORD, how long will people who do evil celebrate?

How long will they delight in what they have done?

## Cruel people boast

## Psalm 94:4

Proud words pour from their mouths.

Those who do wrong boast about it.

## Psalm 94:5

LORD, they crush your people.

They treat the people who belong to you cruelly.

## Psalm 94:6

They kill widows and people from other lands.

They murder children who have no father.

## Psalm 94:7

They say, “The LORD does not see.

The God of Jacob pays no attention.”

## God hears and sees

## Psalm 94:8

You foolish people, pay attention!

When will you learn to be wise?

## Psalm 94:9

God made the ear. Do you think he cannot hear?

God formed the eye. Do you think he cannot see?

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Find the ear**

Think about the sounds an ear can hear. The singer asks why anyone would think the God who made ears could not hear them.

## Psalm 94:10

God corrects whole nations. Will he let wrong go unpunished?

God teaches people. How could he lack knowledge?

## Psalm 94:11

The LORD knows what people think.

He knows how little their thoughts amount to.

## The LORD stays with his people

## Psalm 94:12

LORD, you bless the person you correct.

You teach that person from your law.

## Psalm 94:13

You give that person rest in times of trouble, until the pit is ready for those who do evil.

## Psalm 94:14

The LORD will never leave his people.

He will never give up on those who belong to him.

## Psalm 94:15

Once again judges will do what is right.

Honest people will welcome those decisions and follow them.

## The singer remembers God’s help

## Psalm 94:16

Who will stand up for me against those who do evil?

Who will take my side against them?

## Psalm 94:17

The LORD helped me.

Without his help, I would soon have gone to the silence of the grave.

## Psalm 94:18

When I said, “My foot is slipping,” your lasting love held me up, LORD.

**What does a slipping foot mean?**

Think of losing your balance. The singer felt close to falling, and God’s love helped him stand.

## Psalm 94:19

When worries crowded my mind, you comforted me.

You brought me joy again.

## God judges cruel rulers

## Psalm 94:20

Can rulers who use laws to hurt people be on your side?

## Psalm 94:21

They join forces against those who do right.

They sentence innocent people to death.

## Psalm 94:22

But the LORD has been a strong fort for me.

My God is the rock where I find safety.

## Psalm 94:23

God will turn their own evil deeds back on them.

He will destroy them because of what they do.

The LORD our God will bring them to an end.

**Pause and answer**

What did God do when worries crowded the singer’s mind?
