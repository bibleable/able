# Psalm 113 — The LORD lifts up people in need

**About this psalm**

The song looks up to God on his throne. Then it looks down to the dust, where God sees people in need and raises them to a place of honor.

## Praise from east to west

## Psalm 113:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

All who serve the LORD, praise him.

Praise the name of the LORD.

## Psalm 113:2

Praise the LORD’s name now and forever.

## Psalm 113:3

From the land where the sun rises to the land where it sets, people praise the LORD.

![The sun shines over the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

**Find the sun**

Think of the east, where the sun rises, and the west, where it sets. The song calls people everywhere to praise the LORD.

## God looks down from his throne

## Psalm 113:4

The LORD rules above all nations.

His glory reaches higher than the heavens.

## Psalm 113:5

Who is like the LORD our God?

He sits on his throne high above us.

## Psalm 113:6

Yet he bends down to look at the heavens and the earth.

## God gives people a place of honor

## Psalm 113:7

He raises poor people from the dust.

He lifts people in need from the rubbish heap.

## Psalm 113:8

He gives them seats beside the leaders of his people.

## Psalm 113:9

He gives a home to the woman who could not have children.

She rejoices there as a mother with her children.

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

**Pause and answer**

Where does God seat the people he lifts from the dust?
