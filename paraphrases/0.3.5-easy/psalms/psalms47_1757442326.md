# Psalm 47 — Sing praises to our King

**About this psalm**

The singers call people from every nation to clap and sing. They celebrate God as King over the whole earth.

## Everyone joins the celebration

## Psalm 47:1

This song from the sons of Korah is for the music leader.

People everywhere, clap your hands!

Shout to God with the joy of victory!

## Psalm 47:2

The LORD Most High fills us with wonder.

He is the great King over all the earth.

## God gives his people their land

## Psalm 47:3

He brings nations under our rule.

He puts peoples under our feet.

## Psalm 47:4

He chooses the land we receive as our own.

It is the pride of Jacob, whom God loves.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old word in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## The horn sounds

## Psalm 47:5

God has gone up while the people shout for joy.

The horn sounds as the LORD goes up.

![A curved ram’s horn with a narrow end and a wide opening.](../pictures/sheba-and-joabs-horn.svg)

**Look at the horn**

Find the narrow end. A player blows into that end to make a loud sound. In this song the horn sounds along with the people’s joyful shouts.

## Psalm 47:6

Sing praises to God! Sing praises!

Sing praises to our King! Sing praises!

## Psalm 47:7

God is King over all the earth.

Sing him a song of praise.

**Pause and answer**

Why do the singers invite people everywhere to praise God?

## God rules over the nations

## Psalm 47:8

God rules over the nations.

He sits on his holy throne.

## Psalm 47:9

Leaders from the nations gather as one people.

They belong to the God of Abraham.

The shields of the earth belong to God.

How high above all others he is!

**What do the shields mean?**

A shield protects someone in danger. Here the shields bring to mind the rulers who protect their people. Even these rulers belong to God.
