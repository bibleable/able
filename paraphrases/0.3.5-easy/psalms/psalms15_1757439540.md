# Psalm 15 — Who may stay in the LORD’s tent?

**About this psalm**

David asks who may live close to the LORD. His answer includes what a person says, how they treat a friend, and what they do with money.

## David asks who may stay

## Psalm 15:1

David wrote this song.

LORD, who may stay in your tent?

Who may live on your holy mountain?

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Look at the tent**

Find the tent in the picture. David asks about staying as a guest in the LORD’s own tent.

## An honest person speaks and acts with care

## Psalm 15:2

Someone who lives honestly and does what is right.

What they say is true, and they mean it.

## Psalm 15:3

They never spread lies about another person.

They do no harm to a neighbor.

They never shame a friend with insults.

## An honest person keeps a promise

## Psalm 15:4

They look down on those who do terrible things.

They honor people who treat the LORD with deep respect.

They keep a solemn promise even when it costs them.

**Pause and answer**

What does an honest person do when a promise becomes hard to keep?

## Psalm 15:5

When they lend money, they ask for no extra money back.

They refuse money offered to hurt an innocent person.

Whoever lives this way will always stand firm.

**What does standing firm mean?**

Picture someone whose feet stay steady when the ground shakes. David uses this picture to describe a life that stays secure.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how does the person in this song treat a neighbor?
