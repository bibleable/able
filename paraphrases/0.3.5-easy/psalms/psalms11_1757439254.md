# Psalm 11 — David trusts the LORD when others tell him to run

**About this psalm**

Someone warns David to run away. David answers by telling what he knows about the LORD.

## David repeats the warning he hears

## Psalm 11:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

I turn to the LORD for safety.

How can you give me this advice?

You tell me, “Fly like a bird to your mountain!

## Psalm 11:2

Look! People who do evil are bending their bows.

In the shadows they fit arrows to the strings.

They aim at people who do what is right.

## Psalm 11:3

If the foundations fall apart, what can good people do?”

![A stone at the corner of a building.](../pictures/cornerstone.svg)

**Look at the stone**

The foundation holds up a building. The speaker pictures a building falling apart. What will happen if the things that hold people’s lives together give way?

## David says the LORD sees everyone

## Psalm 11:4

The LORD is in his holy temple.

The LORD sits on his throne in heaven.

He watches people closely.

He sees what everyone does.

## Psalm 11:5

The LORD tests people who do right and people who do evil.

He hates those who love to hurt others.

## Psalm 11:6

The LORD will send burning coals and sulfur down like rain on people who do evil.

A burning hot wind is what they will receive.

**A word to know**

Sulfur is a substance that burns. The song describes a terrible judgment.

## Psalm 11:7

The LORD does what is right.

He loves to see people treated fairly.

Those who do right will see his face.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, why does David turn to the LORD for safety?
