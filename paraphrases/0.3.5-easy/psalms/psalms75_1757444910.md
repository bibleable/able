# Psalm 75 — God chooses the time to judge

**About this psalm**

The people thank God for staying near. God warns proud people who think nobody can take away their power.

## The people give thanks

## Psalm 75:1

Asaph wrote this song for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “Do Not Destroy.”

We thank you, God. We thank you because you are near.

People tell of the wonderful things you have done.

## God warns the proud

## Psalm 75:2

God says, “I choose the time to judge.

When that time comes, I will judge fairly.

## Psalm 75:3

The earth and everyone on it may shake.

I hold its pillars steady.

Selah.

**What are pillars?**

Pillars are strong posts that hold something up. The song pictures God holding the whole earth steady.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 75:4

I tell proud people to stop boasting.

I tell those who do wrong to stop lifting their horns high.

## Psalm 75:5

Do not lift your horns against heaven.

Do not hold your heads high and speak so proudly.”

**Why mention horns?**

A strong animal can lift its horns to threaten another animal. Here a raised horn pictures someone showing off their power.

## God decides who receives honor

## Psalm 75:6

Who gives someone a place of honor?

That honor does not come from the east, the west, or the desert.

## Psalm 75:7

God is the judge.

He brings one person down and lifts another up.

## Psalm 75:8

The LORD holds a cup of foaming wine mixed with spices.

He pours it out.

Everyone on earth who does evil must drink it, even the bits at the bottom.

![A cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Look at the cup**

Point to the bottom of the cup. Drinking everything in it pictures receiving the full judgment God gives.

## Asaph sings to God

## Psalm 75:9

As for me, I will tell of God forever.

I will sing praise to the God of Jacob.

## Psalm 75:10

“I will cut off the horns of those who do evil.

Those who do right will hold their horns high.”

**Pause and answer**

Who decides when it is time to judge?
