# Psalm 40 — The LORD heard my cry

**About this psalm**

David remembers waiting for help and finding firm ground again. He tells the people what the LORD has done. When trouble surrounds him again, he calls to the LORD.

## David remembers the muddy pit

## Psalm 40:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

I waited patiently for the LORD.

He leaned toward me and heard my cry.

## Psalm 40:2

He lifted me out of a deep pit of sticky mud.

Down there I had lost hope.

He set my feet on a rock.

Now I could take steady steps.

**From mud to rock**

Feet can sink in sticky mud. On rock they can stand firm. David uses this picture to describe the relief of being rescued.

## Psalm 40:3

He gave me a new song to sing.

It is a song of praise to our God.

Many will see what happened and stand in awe.

They will put their trust in the LORD.

## David puts his trust in the LORD

## Psalm 40:4

How blessed is someone who trusts the LORD!

That person refuses to follow proud people or those who tell lies.

## Psalm 40:5

LORD my God, you have done so many wonderful things.

You have made so many plans for us.

No one compares with you.

I could tell of your deeds and plans, but they are too many to count.

## David listens and comes to God

## Psalm 40:6

You did not ask me for sacrifices and gifts.

You opened my ears to listen.

You did not require burnt offerings or offerings for sin.

## Psalm 40:7

Then I said, “Here I am. I have come.

The scroll has these words about me.

## Psalm 40:8

My God, I love to do what you want.

I keep your teaching in my heart.”

![A scroll unrolled to show lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Look at the scroll**

A scroll is a long sheet people roll up. They unroll it to read the words. David says he keeps God’s teaching close, even when he walks away from the written words.

## David speaks to the gathered people

## Psalm 40:9

When the people gather, I say what is right.

LORD, you know I speak up.

## Psalm 40:10

I tell them openly how you do what is right.

I tell them how you keep your word and save us.

All the people hear of your faithful love.

## Psalm 40:11

LORD, keep showing me mercy.

Your love and faithfulness will always guard me.

## David asks for help again

## Psalm 40:12

More troubles surround me than I can count.

My sins have caught up with me. I cannot see.

They outnumber the hairs on my head.

My courage has left me.

## Psalm 40:13

LORD, please rescue me.

Hurry to help me, LORD.

## Psalm 40:14

Let those who want me dead face shame and confusion.

Turn back those who want to hurt me.

Let them leave in shame.

## Psalm 40:15

Some people laugh at me and say, “Aha! Aha!”

Let them be shocked by their own shame.

## A prayer for those who seek the LORD

## Psalm 40:16

Let everyone who seeks you find joy in you.

Let those who love how you save them always say, “How great is the LORD!”

## Psalm 40:17

I am poor and need help.

May the Lord remember me.

You help me and set me free.

My God, please hurry.
