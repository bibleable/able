# Psalm 89 — Remember your promise to David

**About this psalm**

Ethan sings about God’s promise to David and his family. Then he looks at a defeated king. He asks how long the trouble will last.

## Ethan remembers a promise

## Psalm 89:1

A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.

I will always sing of the LORD’s lasting love.

I will tell each new generation how you keep your word.

**A word in the title**

Maskil is an old word used in several song titles. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 89:2

I say, “Your love lasts forever.

Your faithfulness stands firm in the heavens.”

## Psalm 89:3

You said, “I made a covenant with the one I chose.

I gave my servant David my solemn promise.

## Psalm 89:4

Your family will continue forever.

Your children and their children will sit on your throne.”

Selah.

**What is a covenant?**

A covenant is a binding promise. Here God promises that David’s family will continue to rule.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Who is like the LORD?

## Psalm 89:5

LORD, the heavens praise the wonders you do.

The holy ones gather there and praise your faithfulness.

## Psalm 89:6

Who in the sky can compare with the LORD?

Is anyone in heaven like him?

## Psalm 89:7

The holy ones gather around God in great awe.

He is greater than all who stand around him.

## Psalm 89:8

LORD God, you lead heaven’s armies.

Who is like you, mighty LORD?

Your faithfulness is all around you.

## God rules the sea and the land

## Psalm 89:9

You rule the stormy sea.

When its waves rise high, you calm them.

## Psalm 89:10

You crushed Rahab and left it dead.

With your strong arm you scattered your enemies.

**Who is Rahab here?**

In this song Rahab pictures a powerful enemy associated with the sea. Even this enemy is within God’s power.

## Psalm 89:11

The sky and the earth belong to you.

You made the world and everything in it.

## Psalm 89:12

You made the north and the south.

Mount Tabor and Mount Hermon shout for joy at your name.

## Psalm 89:13

Your arm is mighty. Your hand is strong.

You hold your right hand high.

## Psalm 89:14

You rule by doing what is right and fair.

Your lasting love and faithfulness go ahead of you.

## The people celebrate

## Psalm 89:15

You bless the people who know the joyful shout of praise.

LORD, they walk in the light of your presence.

## Psalm 89:16

They rejoice in your name all day.

They celebrate because you do what is right.

## Psalm 89:17

You give them strength they can be proud of.

Your kindness gives us power and honor.

## Psalm 89:18

Our shield belongs to the LORD.

Our king belongs to the Holy One of Israel.

## God recalls choosing David

## Psalm 89:19

Once you showed your faithful people a vision.

You said, “I have helped a warrior.

I have raised up a man I chose from among the people.

## Psalm 89:20

I found David, my servant.

I chose him as king and anointed him with holy oil.

## Psalm 89:21

My hand will hold him steady.

My arm will give him strength.

## Psalm 89:22

No enemy will force him to pay tribute.

No cruel man will have power over him.

**What is tribute?**

Tribute is a payment a ruler demands from someone under his power.

## Psalm 89:23

I will crush the enemies who stand against him.

I will strike down those who hate him.

## Psalm 89:24

I will be faithful to David and keep loving him.

In my name he will grow strong.

## Psalm 89:25

I will extend his rule over the sea and the rivers.

## A lasting promise to David’s family

## Psalm 89:26

David will call to me, ‘You are my Father and my God.

You are my rock, the one who saves me.’

## Psalm 89:27

I will give him the place of my firstborn son.

He will rank above all the kings of the earth.

## Psalm 89:28

My love for him will last forever.

My covenant with him will stand firm.

## Psalm 89:29

His family will continue forever.

His throne will last as long as the heavens.

## What if David’s sons disobey?

## Psalm 89:30

His sons might turn away from my law.

They might refuse to live by my decisions.

## Psalm 89:31

They might break my rules and disobey my commands.

## Psalm 89:32

If they do, I will punish their wrongdoing.

They will feel the blows of the rod for their sins.

## Psalm 89:33

Even then I will keep loving David.

I will always be faithful.

## Psalm 89:34

I will keep my covenant.

I will keep the promise I spoke.

## A promise as lasting as the moon

## Psalm 89:35

I am holy, and I have sworn this once for all.

I will never lie to David.

## Psalm 89:36

His family will continue forever.

His throne will stand before me as long as the sun shines.

## Psalm 89:37

It will last like the moon, a faithful witness in the sky.”

Selah.

![The sun and the moon in the sky.](../pictures/gibeon-sun-moon.svg)

**Find the sun and the moon**

Think of seeing these lights return to the sky. Ethan recalls God’s promise that David’s family will last like them.

## Ethan sees the king’s defeat

## Psalm 89:38

But now you have turned away from the king you chose.

You are angry with him.

## Psalm 89:39

You have set aside your covenant with your servant.

You have thrown his crown in the dirt.

## Psalm 89:40

You have broken down all his walls.

His forts lie in heaps of broken stone.

## Psalm 89:41

People passing by take whatever they want from him.

His neighbors make fun of him.

## Psalm 89:42

You have made his enemies strong.

Now they all celebrate.

## Psalm 89:43

You have bent the edge of his sword.

You have given him no help in battle.

## Psalm 89:44

You have taken away his royal glory.

You have thrown his throne to the ground.

## Psalm 89:45

You have cut short his younger days.

You have covered him with shame.

Selah.

## How long, LORD?

## Psalm 89:46

How long, LORD?

Will you hide yourself forever?

Will your anger keep burning like fire?

## Psalm 89:47

Remember how short my life is!

Did you make all of us only to live for nothing?

## Psalm 89:48

Who can live and never die?

Who can escape the power of Sheol, the place of the dead?

Selah.

## Remember your lasting love

## Psalm 89:49

Lord, where is the lasting love you showed long ago?

You gave David your word that you would keep loving him.

## Psalm 89:50

Lord, remember how people insult your servants.

I carry the insults of many nations in my heart.

## Psalm 89:51

LORD, your enemies mock the king you chose.

They make fun of every step he takes.

## Psalm 89:52

Praise the LORD forever!

Amen and amen.

**What does amen mean?**

It means “May it be so.”
