# Psalm 98 — Let the rivers clap their hands

**About this psalm**

The singer invites people everywhere to celebrate what God has done. Then the song invites the sea, the rivers, and the mountains to join in.

## The nations see how God saves

## Psalm 98:1

Sing a new song to the LORD!

He has done wonderful things.

With his strong right hand and holy arm, he has won the victory.

## Psalm 98:2

The LORD has announced how he saves.

He has shown the nations that he does what is right.

## Psalm 98:3

He has remembered his love for Israel and kept his word.

Even people in the farthest lands have seen our God save.

## Voices and instruments join in

## Psalm 98:4

Everyone on earth, shout to the LORD with joy!

Let your voices ring out! Sing his praise!

## Psalm 98:5

Play the lyre and sing praise to the LORD.

Play the harp as you sing your tune.

![A harp and its strings.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Find the strings**

Imagine plucking the strings while others sing. A lyre is another instrument with strings.

## Psalm 98:6

Sound the trumpets and blow the ram’s horn.

Shout for joy before the LORD, the King!

## Rivers and mountains celebrate

## Psalm 98:7

Let the sea roar with everything that lives in it.

Let the world and all its people join in!

## Psalm 98:8

Let the rivers clap their hands!

Let the mountains sing together for joy!

**How can a river clap?**

The singer pictures the rivers joining a crowd that claps for its king. Even the mountains seem to share the joy.

## Psalm 98:9

Let them celebrate before the LORD, because he is coming to judge the earth.

He will do what is right for the world.

He will judge all peoples fairly.

**Pause and answer**

How will the LORD judge the people of the earth?
