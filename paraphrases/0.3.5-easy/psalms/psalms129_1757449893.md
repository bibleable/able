# Psalm 129 — The LORD cut the ropes

**About this psalm**

Israel speaks as one person who has suffered for a long time. The people remember how the LORD set them free.

## Israel remembers the pain

## Psalm 129:1

A song for going up to worship.

Let Israel say, “Since I was young, enemies have hurt me many times.

## Psalm 129:2

Since I was young, enemies have hurt me many times.

Yet they have never defeated me.

## Psalm 129:3

It was as if farmers plowed my back.

They left long cuts like rows in a field.

## Psalm 129:4

The LORD does what is right.

He cut the ropes that cruel people used to bind me.”

## Grass that dries up

## Psalm 129:5

May all who hate Zion turn back in shame.

## Psalm 129:6

May they be like grass growing on a roof.

It dries up before it can grow tall.

## Psalm 129:7

There is not even a handful for the person cutting it.

There is no armful for the person tying the bundles.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the stalk**

At harvest, workers gather stalks like this one into bundles. The singer says the grass on the roof would give them nothing to gather.

## Psalm 129:8

May nobody passing by say to them, “May the LORD bless you!

We bless you in the LORD’s name.”

**Pause and answer**

Who set Israel free from the ropes of cruel people?
