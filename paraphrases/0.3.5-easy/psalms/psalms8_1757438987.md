# Psalm 8 — David looks up at the sky

**About this psalm**

David looks at the moon and the stars. He wonders why the God who made them cares so much about people.

## The whole earth praises the LORD’s name

## Psalm 8:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Use the musical instruction called Gittith.

LORD, our Lord, how great is your name everywhere on earth!

Your glory reaches higher than the sky.

**A word in the song**

Gittith is an old musical term. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 8:2

You give children and babies a part in praising you.

Their praise silences your enemies, even those who want revenge.

## David looks at the night sky

## Psalm 8:3

I look up at the sky your fingers made.

I see the moon and stars you put in place.

## Psalm 8:4

Why do you think about human beings?

Why do you care for people like us?

![The full moon partly behind a cloud.](../pictures/job-cloud-over-moon.svg)

**Look at the moon**

Find the bright moon. David thinks about the one who put it in the sky.

**Pause and answer**

Who does David say put the moon in place?

## Psalm 8:5

You made us just a little lower than the angels.

You gave us glory and honor like a crown.

## God gives people a task

## Psalm 8:6

You put us in charge of what you made.

You placed it all under our feet.

**What does “under our feet” mean?**

David means God gave people authority over the living things he made.

## Psalm 8:7

You put us in charge of sheep and oxen.

You included the wild animals in the fields.

## Psalm 8:8

You included the birds that fly overhead.

You included the fish and everything swimming through the sea.

## Psalm 8:9

LORD, our Lord, how great is your name everywhere on earth!

**Pause and answer**

Tell in your own words one way God shows that people matter to him.
