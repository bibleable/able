# Psalm 70 — Hurry to help me, LORD

**About this psalm**

People want to hurt David. He asks God to come quickly. He also prays for everyone who turns to God.

## David asks God for help

## Psalm 70:1

David wrote this song for the music leader as a reminder.

God, hurry to rescue me!

Hurry to help me, LORD!

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Listen to David’s prayer**

Point to the ear. Listen as someone reads David’s prayer aloud. Hear how David says “hurry” twice.

## Psalm 70:2

Let those who want me dead face shame and confusion.

Turn back those who want to hurt me.

Let them leave in shame.

## Psalm 70:3

Some people laugh at me and say, “Aha! Aha!”

Let them turn back in shame.

## David prays for people who seek God

## Psalm 70:4

Let everyone who seeks you find joy in you.

Let those who love how you save them always say, “How great is God!”

## Psalm 70:5

I am poor and need help.

God, hurry to me!

You help me and set me free.

LORD, please do not wait.
