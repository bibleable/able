# Psalm 1 — A tree beside streams of water

**About this psalm**

A psalm is a song or poem. This psalm asks us to picture two things. One is a tree with deep roots. The other is a dry husk in the wind.

## Someone who loves the LORD’s teaching

## Psalm 1:1

God blesses the person who turns away from evil advice.

That person refuses to follow others into sin.

They refuse to join people who laugh at others.

## Psalm 1:2

This person loves the law of the LORD.

Day and night, they think about what it teaches.

## Psalm 1:3

That person is like a tree beside streams of water.

The tree grows fruit when its season comes.

Its leaves stay green.

Like that tree, the person does well in all they do.

**Pause and answer**

What does this person enjoy thinking about?

## What the wind carries away

## Psalm 1:4

People who do evil are like chaff.

The wind blows the chaff away.

![Wind carries the light husks sideways while the grain falls.](../pictures/winnowing-barley.svg)

**Look at the picture**

Chaff is the light, dry covering around grain. A farmer tosses the grain into the air. Look for the husks that blow away. The psalm says people who do evil are like those husks.

## Psalm 1:5

So when God judges, the wicked cannot stand before him.

People who keep sinning have no place among those who do right.

## Psalm 1:6

The LORD watches over those who do right as they go.

Those who follow the path of evil come to ruin.

**What does a path mean here?**

A path is the way a person chooses to live.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does the LORD do for people who follow his path?
