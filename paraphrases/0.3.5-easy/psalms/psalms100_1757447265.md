# Psalm 100 — Come to God with thanks

**About this psalm**

This is a song of thanks. It invites everyone to come and sing to the God who made them.

## Come and sing

## Psalm 100:1

A song for giving thanks.

Everyone on earth, shout for joy to the LORD!

## Psalm 100:2

Serve the LORD gladly.

Come to him singing joyful songs.

## We belong to God

## Psalm 100:3

Know that the LORD is God.

He made us, and we belong to him.

We are his people, like sheep he feeds in his pasture.

![A pen where sheep can shelter.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

**Think about the sheep**

A shepherd brings the sheep to grass so they can eat. The singers remember that God cares for them like a shepherd.

## Walk through the gates with thanks

## Psalm 100:4

Come through his gates with thanks.

Enter his courtyards with praise.

Thank him and praise his name.

**What are courtyards?**

They are open spaces inside the gates. People gathered there to worship.

## Psalm 100:5

The LORD is good.

His love lasts forever.

He keeps his word to one generation after another.
