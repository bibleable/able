# Psalm 83 — God, do not stay silent

**About this psalm**

Asaph hears that several nations have joined forces against Israel. He asks God to act, as God did when Israel was in danger before.

## The enemies make a plan

## Psalm 83:1

A song of Asaph.

God, do not stay silent.

Please speak, God. Please act!

## Psalm 83:2

See how your enemies rage!

Those who hate you hold their heads high.

## Psalm 83:3

They make secret plans against your people.

They gather to plot against the people you love.

## Psalm 83:4

They say, “Come, let us wipe out their nation.

Then no one will remember the name of Israel.”

## Nations join forces

## Psalm 83:5

They have all agreed on one plan.

They have joined forces against you.

## Psalm 83:6

The people in the tents of Edom have joined.

So have the Ishmaelites, the people of Moab, and the Hagrites.

## Psalm 83:7

Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek are part of the group.

So are Philistia and the people of Tyre.

## Psalm 83:8

Even Assyria has joined them.

Its people have come to help the descendants of Lot.

Selah.

**Who are Lot’s descendants?**

Here Asaph means the peoples of Moab and Ammon.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Asaph remembers earlier enemies

## Psalm 83:9

God, deal with them as you dealt with Midian.

Remember how you defeated Sisera and Jabin at the Kishon River.

## Psalm 83:10

Your enemies died at Endor.

Their bodies lay like dung on the ground.

## Psalm 83:11

Let their leaders meet the same end as Oreb and Zeeb.

Let their princes meet the same end as Zebah and Zalmunna.

**Who are these four men?**

Oreb, Zeeb, Zebah, and Zalmunna were leaders of Midian. Asaph remembers that their power came to an end.

## Psalm 83:12

Those men said, “Let us take God’s pasturelands for ourselves.”

## Asaph asks God to scatter them

## Psalm 83:13

My God, scatter them like dry plants rolling in the wind.

Let them blow away like the husks of grain.

![Wind blows the light husks aside as barley grains fall.](../pictures/winnowing-barley.svg)

**Follow the husks**

Find the light pieces blowing sideways. Asaph wants the nations that joined together to scatter just as easily.

## Psalm 83:14

Think of a fire spreading through a forest.

Think of flames racing over a mountainside.

## Psalm 83:15

Chase them like that with your fierce wind.

Frighten them with your storm.

## Psalm 83:16

LORD, make them feel ashamed, so they will turn to you.

## Psalm 83:17

May they live in shame and fear.

May they die in disgrace.

## Psalm 83:18

Let them know that your name is the LORD.

You alone are God Most High over all the earth.
