# Psalm 6 — The LORD hears David weep

**About this psalm**

David is weak and in pain. He asks the LORD how long he must wait for help. By the end of the song, David is sure the LORD has heard him.

## David asks for mercy

## Psalm 6:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Play it on stringed instruments, according to Sheminith.

LORD, please do not scold me in your anger.

Do not punish me in your fury.

![A harp with strings.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the strings**

A musician plucks the strings to make music. Sheminith is a musical instruction. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 6:2

LORD, have mercy on me.

I have little strength left.

Heal me, LORD. Even my bones hurt terribly.

## Psalm 6:3

Deep inside, I am in great distress.

How long, LORD?

How long must I wait?

## David asks the LORD to save his life

## Psalm 6:4

Turn back to me, LORD. Rescue me.

Save my life because your love lasts.

## Psalm 6:5

Who speaks of you after death?

Who can praise you from Sheol, the place of the dead?

## David weeps through the night

## Psalm 6:6

I am worn out from groaning.

All night I cry until my bed is wet.

My tears soak the place where I lie.

**Picture David’s bed**

David describes his tears as a flood. This helps us understand how much he has cried.

## Psalm 6:7

Grief makes it hard for me to see.

My sight grows dim because of all my enemies.

## David knows the LORD has heard him

## Psalm 6:8

Go away, all you who do evil!

The LORD has heard me weeping.

## Psalm 6:9

The LORD has heard me beg for mercy.

The LORD accepts my prayer.

**Pause and answer**

What does David say the LORD has heard?

## Psalm 6:10

All my enemies will feel shame and fear.

They will suddenly turn back in shame.
