# Psalm 43 — Send your light and your truth

**About this psalm**

The singer asks God for a fair hearing and a way home. He longs to play music for God again.

## The singer asks God to defend him

## Psalm 43:1

God, show that I am innocent.

Speak for me against people who reject you.

Rescue me from people who lie and act unfairly.

## Psalm 43:2

You are the God I turn to for shelter.

Why have you turned me away?

Why must I go about in sorrow while my enemy mistreats me?

## The singer asks God to guide him

## Psalm 43:3

Send your light and your truth to guide me.

Let them lead me up your holy mountain.

Bring me to the place where you live.

## Psalm 43:4

Then I will go to your altar.

You, God, are my greatest joy.

My God, I will play the harp to praise you.

![Strings stretched across the frame of a harp.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the harp**

A player plucks the strings to make music. The singer hopes to do this at the altar, the place where people bring gifts to God.

## The singer speaks to himself

## Psalm 43:5

Why do I feel so low?

Why am I so troubled inside?

Put your hope in God, I tell myself.

I will praise him again. He is my Savior and my God.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does the singer hope to do when he reaches God’s holy mountain?
