# Psalm 10 — The LORD hears people who need help

**About this psalm**

The singer sees a proud man hurting people who have little power. The singer asks the LORD to act.

## The singer calls to the LORD

## Psalm 10:1

LORD, why do you stand so far away?

Why do you hide when trouble comes?

## Psalm 10:2

Proud people who do evil chase those in need.

Let their own plans trap them.

## A proud man boasts

## Psalm 10:3

The man who does evil brags about what he wants.

He praises greedy people and insults the LORD.

## Psalm 10:4

He is too proud to look for God.

He makes all his plans as though there were no God.

## Psalm 10:5

Everything he does seems to go well.

Your judgments are far above him.

He laughs at all his enemies.

## Psalm 10:6

He tells himself, “Nothing can shake me.

No trouble will ever come to me.”

## Psalm 10:7

He curses people and tells lies.

He threatens to hurt them.

Even the words he holds back are full of hate.

## He waits for people who cannot defend themselves

## Psalm 10:8

He hides near the villages and waits.

He kills people who have done nothing wrong.

He secretly watches for people who cannot defend themselves.

## Psalm 10:9

Like a lion hiding in the bushes, he waits.

He seizes people who are already suffering.

He catches them in his net.

![Ropes cross to form a net.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Picture someone caught in these ropes. The singer uses a net to show how the man traps people.

## Psalm 10:10

His victims fall under his strength.

He crushes people who have no one to help.

## Psalm 10:11

He tells himself, “God has forgotten.

God turns his face away and never sees.”

## The singer asks God to act

## Psalm 10:12

Rise up, LORD!

God, lift your hand to help!

Remember the people who cannot defend themselves.

## Psalm 10:13

Why does this man reject you, God?

He tells himself, “God will never make me answer for this.”

## Psalm 10:14

But you do see the hurt and grief.

You see it and act to set things right.

Those who suffer put themselves in your care.

You help the child who has no father.

## Psalm 10:15

Break the arm of the man who does evil.

Make him answer for every wrong until none is left.

**What does the arm mean here?**

The arm stands for the strength the man uses to hurt people. The singer wants God to stop him.

## The LORD listens

## Psalm 10:16

The LORD is King forever.

The nations perish from his land.

## Psalm 10:17

LORD, you hear what humble people long for.

You give them courage.

You bend down to listen.

## Psalm 10:18

You stand up for children without fathers and for people others mistreat.

Then no one on earth can frighten them anymore.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how does the LORD help the people who are afraid?
