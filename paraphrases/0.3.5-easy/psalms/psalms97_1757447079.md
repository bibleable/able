# Psalm 97 — Even the mountains melt before the LORD

**About this psalm**

The singer pictures the LORD coming as king. The earth shakes, and people in Judah celebrate because his decisions are fair.

## The LORD rules fairly

## Psalm 97:1

The LORD is king! Let the earth rejoice!

Let people on faraway shores be glad.

## Psalm 97:2

Clouds and deep darkness surround him.

His rule rests on doing what is right and fair.

## The earth shakes before him

## Psalm 97:3

Fire goes ahead of him.

It burns up his enemies on every side.

## Psalm 97:4

His lightning lights up the world.

The earth sees it and shakes.

## Psalm 97:5

Mountains melt like wax before the LORD.

He is the Lord of the whole earth.

![A high mountain with a path up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

Imagine heat softening a piece of wax until it melts. The singer pictures even a mountain giving way like that when God comes.

## Psalm 97:6

The heavens tell how he does what is right.

People from every nation see his greatness.

## The people of Judah rejoice

## Psalm 97:7

Those who boast about the idols they worship will feel ashamed.

All you gods, bow down to him!

## Psalm 97:8

The people of Zion hear the news and rejoice.

LORD, the towns of Judah celebrate your fair decisions.

**Where is Zion?**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem, a city in Judah.

## Psalm 97:9

LORD, you are God Most High over the whole earth.

You stand far above all gods.

## The LORD guards his people

## Psalm 97:10

You who love the LORD, hate what is evil.

He guards the lives of his faithful people.

He rescues them from the grip of people who do wrong.

## Psalm 97:11

Light shines on people who do right.

Joy comes to people with honest hearts.

## Psalm 97:12

You who do right, rejoice in the LORD!

Praise his holy name.

**Pause and answer**

Why do the people in the towns of Judah celebrate?
