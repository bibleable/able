# Psalm 31 — David puts his life in the LORD’s hands

**About this psalm**

People tell lies about David. Even his friends run away. David tells the LORD how deeply this hurts and asks him for shelter.

## David asks for shelter

## Psalm 31:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

LORD, I come to you for safety.

Never let me face shame.

You do what is right. Please save me.

## Psalm 31:2

Lean close and listen to me.

Hurry to rescue me.

Be my rock where I can take shelter.

Be the strong fort that keeps me safe.

## Psalm 31:3

You are my rock and my fort.

For the honor of your name, show me where to go.

## Psalm 31:4

My enemies set a net to catch me.

You free me from it because you are my shelter.

## David trusts the LORD with his life

## Psalm 31:5

I place my spirit in your hands.

LORD, you have set me free.

You are the God who speaks the truth.

**What does David mean by God’s hands?**

When you put something precious in someone’s hands, you trust that person to care for it. David trusts God with his whole life.

## Psalm 31:6

I hate those who hold on to worthless idols.

I put my trust in the LORD.

## Psalm 31:7

Your faithful love gives me joy.

You have seen what I suffer.

You know how deeply I hurt.

## Psalm 31:8

You kept me out of my enemy’s hands.

You gave me room to stand in the open.

## David tells the LORD what hurts

## Psalm 31:9

LORD, show me mercy. I am in trouble.

Sorrow has worn out my eyes.

My whole body aches, and I hurt deep inside.

## Psalm 31:10

Grief fills my days. I spend years groaning.

My own wrongdoing has drained my strength.

Even my bones are wasting away.

## Psalm 31:11

My enemies look down on me.

My neighbors look down on me even more.

My friends are afraid of me.

They run when they see me in the street.

## Psalm 31:12

People have forgotten me as though I were dead.

I feel like a broken jar.

![A round clay jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the jar**

Picture this jar lying broken and forgotten. David feels that people have set him aside like that jar.

## Psalm 31:13

I hear so many people telling lies about me.

Fear surrounds me.

They meet and plan how to take my life.

## David asks the LORD to rescue him

## Psalm 31:14

Yet I trust you, LORD.

I say, “You are my God.”

## Psalm 31:15

My days are in your hands.

Save me from my enemies and those who chase me.

## Psalm 31:16

Let your face shine on me, your servant.

Save me because of your faithful love.

**A face that shines**

David asks the LORD to look at him with kindness.

## Psalm 31:17

LORD, I have called to you. Keep me from shame.

Let shame fall on those who do evil.

Let them lie silent in Sheol, the place of the dead.

## Psalm 31:18

Silence those who tell lies.

In their pride they insult people who do right.

They speak as if those people are worth nothing.

## The LORD shelters his people

## Psalm 31:19

How much goodness you have stored up for those who honor you!

When people take shelter in you, you show them that goodness.

Others see what you do.

## Psalm 31:20

You keep them close, hidden from those plotting against them.

You shelter them from those who accuse them.

## Psalm 31:21

Praise the LORD!

He showed me faithful love in a city surrounded by enemies.

## Psalm 31:22

In my fear I said, “You cannot see me anymore!”

Yet when I begged you for mercy, you heard me.

**Pause and answer**

When David thought the LORD could no longer see him, what did the LORD hear?

## David calls others to take courage

## Psalm 31:23

All you who belong to the LORD, love him!

The LORD protects those who stay faithful.

He pays proud people back in full for what they do.

## Psalm 31:24

All you who put your hope in the LORD, be strong.

Take courage!

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David want people who hope in the LORD to remember?
