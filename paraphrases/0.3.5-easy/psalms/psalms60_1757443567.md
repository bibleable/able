# Psalm 60 — God, help your people again

**About this psalm**

David wrote this song to teach his people. After a defeat, they ask God to help them again.

## The people ask God for help

## Psalm 60:1

David gave this Miktam to the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “The Lily of the Covenant.”

David fought the armies of Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah.

Joab came back and killed 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

God, you have turned away from us.

You have broken us.

You have been angry with us.

Help us stand again!

**A word in the title**

Miktam is an old word for a kind of song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 60:2

You shook the land and split it open.

Close its cracks, because the ground still shakes.

## Psalm 60:3

You made your people face hard times.

You gave us wine that made us stumble.

![A cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Look at the cup**

Picture someone stumbling after drinking strong wine. David says trouble has made his people feel that unsteady.

## A flag for those who honor God

## Psalm 60:4

You raised a flag for those who honor you.

They can run to it to escape the arrows.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 60:5

Answer us!

Use your strong right hand to save the people you love.

## God names the lands he rules

## Psalm 60:6

God spoke from his holy place.

He said, “I will win!

I will divide up the land of Shechem.

I will give out shares of the Valley of Succoth.

## Psalm 60:7

Gilead belongs to me.

Manasseh belongs to me.

Ephraim is my helmet.

Judah is the staff I carry as king.

## Psalm 60:8

Moab is the bowl where I wash.

I throw my sandal on Edom.

I shout because I have won against Philistia.”

**Why name these everyday things?**

A helmet protects a soldier. A king’s staff shows that he rules. Even the bowl and sandal show God’s power over the lands he names.

## The people depend on God

## Psalm 60:9

Who will take me to the city with strong walls?

Who will lead me to Edom?

## Psalm 60:10

God, have you turned away from us?

Will you still go out with our armies?

## Psalm 60:11

Help us against our enemies.

People alone cannot give us the help we need.

## Psalm 60:12

With God we will act bravely.

God himself will crush our enemies under his feet.

**Pause and answer**

Who do the people count on when their enemies come?
