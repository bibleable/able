# Psalm 117 — Every nation, praise the LORD

**About this psalm**

This short song invites people from every nation to praise the LORD together.

## An invitation to everyone

## Psalm 117:1

Every nation, praise the LORD!

All peoples, tell how great he is!

![A harp and its strings.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Imagine the song**

Follow one string with your finger. Imagine people from many lands joining in as someone plays.

## Why we praise him

## Psalm 117:2

His love for us is great.

The LORD keeps his word forever.

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!
