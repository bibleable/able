# Psalm 26 — David asks the LORD to look at his life

**About this psalm**

David asks the LORD to examine his thoughts and actions. He tells the LORD why he loves to come and worship.

## David asks the LORD to test him

## Psalm 26:1

David wrote this song.

LORD, show that I am innocent.

I have lived honestly.

I have kept trusting you.

## Psalm 26:2

Test me, LORD.

Look closely at what I feel and think.

## Psalm 26:3

I keep your faithful love in mind.

I have lived by your truth.

## David chooses his company

## Psalm 26:4

I do not sit with people who tell lies.

I stay away from people who only pretend to do right.

## Psalm 26:5

I hate it when people gather to do evil.

I refuse to join them.

## David gives thanks at the altar

## Psalm 26:6

LORD, I wash my hands to show my innocence.

Then I walk around your altar.

![A basin and a towel for washing.](../pictures/basin-and-towel.svg)

**Look at the basin**

Imagine washing your hands in a basin of water. David uses clean hands as a sign that he has acted honestly. An altar is a place where people bring gifts to God.

## Psalm 26:7

There I thank you out loud.

I tell about all the wonderful things you have done.

## Psalm 26:8

LORD, I love the house where you live.

Your glory fills that place.

**Pause and answer**

What does David want to tell people at the altar?

## David asks for mercy

## Psalm 26:9

Spare my life when you take away those who sin.

Do not let me die with those who kill people.

## Psalm 26:10

Those people put evil plans into action.

Their hands are full of money they take as bribes.

**A word to know**

A bribe is a gift or payment meant to make someone act unfairly.

## Psalm 26:11

I will keep living honestly.

Rescue me and show me mercy.

## Psalm 26:12

My feet stand on level ground.

When people gather to worship, I will praise the LORD.

**What does level ground mean?**

On level ground, a person can stand firmly. David uses that picture to show how secure he feels as he trusts the LORD.
