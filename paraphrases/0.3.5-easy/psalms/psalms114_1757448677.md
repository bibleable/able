# Psalm 114 — The sea and the mountains move

**About this psalm**

The singer remembers Israel leaving Egypt. He speaks to the sea, the river, and the hills as if they could answer him.

## God makes his home with Israel

## Psalm 114:1

Israel left Egypt.

Jacob’s family left people who spoke another language.

## Psalm 114:2

Judah became God’s holy home.

Israel became the people he ruled.

## The sea flees and the hills skip

## Psalm 114:3

The sea saw what was happening and fled.

The Jordan River turned back.

## Psalm 114:4

The mountains leaped like rams.

The hills skipped like lambs.

![A high mountain with a path up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

Imagine this great mountain jumping like a sheep. The singer uses that surprising picture to show the earth trembling before God.

## Psalm 114:5

Sea, why did you flee?

Jordan River, why did you turn back?

## Psalm 114:6

Mountains, why did you leap like rams?

Hills, why did you skip like lambs?

## The God of Jacob is here

## Psalm 114:7

Earth, tremble before the Lord!

The God of Jacob is here.

## Psalm 114:8

He turned rock into a pool of water.

He made a spring flow from hard flint!

**What is flint?**

Flint is a very hard stone. The song remembers God bringing water even from rock.

**Pause and answer**
