# Psalm 109 — Stand beside me and help me

**About this psalm**

David prays when people tell lies about him. The prayer describes the harm one man has done and asks God to judge him. David also speaks about his own need for help.

## People tell lies about David

## Psalm 109:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

God, you are the one I praise.

Please do not stay silent.

## Psalm 109:2

People who do evil speak against me.

They tell lies about me.

## Psalm 109:3

Their hateful words surround me.

They attack me for no reason.

## Psalm 109:4

I show them love, and they accuse me.

Still I pray.

## Psalm 109:5

I do good to them, and they hurt me in return.

I love them, and they hate me.

## A plea for judgment

## Psalm 109:6

Put a cruel man in charge of him.

Let someone stand at his right side to accuse him.

**What does an accuser do?**

An accuser tells a court that someone has done wrong. Later in the song, David pictures God standing beside a person who needs help.

## Psalm 109:7

When the court hears his case, let it find him guilty.

Let even his prayer count as a sin.

## Psalm 109:8

May his life be short.

May someone else take his place.

## A prayer about the man’s family

## Psalm 109:9

May his children lose their father.

May his wife lose her husband.

## Psalm 109:10

May his children wander and beg for food far from their ruined home.

## Psalm 109:11

May the person he owes take everything he owns.

May strangers steal everything he worked for.

## Psalm 109:12

May no one show him kindness.

May no one help the children he leaves without a father.

## Psalm 109:13

May his family line come to an end.

May the next generation lose all record of their name.

## Psalm 109:14

LORD, remember the wrong his ancestors did.

Never erase the record of his mother’s sin.

## Psalm 109:15

May their sins always be before you, LORD.

May you remove every memory of them from the earth.

## The harm the man wished on others

## Psalm 109:16

That man never thought to show kindness.

He hunted down poor people and people whose hearts were broken.

He pursued them even to their deaths.

## Psalm 109:17

He loved to wish harm on others.

May that harm come back to him.

He took no joy in blessing others. May blessing stay far from him.

## Psalm 109:18

He wore his curses like a coat.

May they soak into his body like water and into his bones like oil.

## Psalm 109:19

May those curses wrap around him like a robe.

May they stay with him like a belt he always wears.

![A cloak that wraps around a person.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

**Look at the cloak**

Imagine wrapping the cloak around your shoulders. The prayer asks that the cruel words stay as close to the man as his clothing.

## Psalm 109:20

LORD, may this be what my accusers receive from you.

They are the ones who speak evil against me.

## David asks for kindness

## Psalm 109:21

But you, Lord GOD, show me kindness for the honor of your name.

Rescue me because your lasting love is good.

## Psalm 109:22

I am poor and need help.

My heart aches deep inside me.

## Psalm 109:23

I fade like a long evening shadow.

I am like a locust someone shakes off.

## Psalm 109:24

My knees are weak from going without food.

My body has grown thin.

## Psalm 109:25

My accusers make fun of me.

They shake their heads when they see me.

## The LORD stands beside the person in need

## Psalm 109:26

LORD my God, help me!

Save me because your love lasts.

## Psalm 109:27

Let my accusers see whose hand has saved me.

Let them know you did it, LORD.

## Psalm 109:28

They may curse me, but you will bless me.

When they rise against me, they will face shame.

I am your servant, and I will rejoice.

## Psalm 109:29

May disgrace cover my accusers.

May their shame wrap around them like a robe.

## Psalm 109:30

I will thank the LORD aloud again and again.

I will praise him in front of many people.

## Psalm 109:31

He stands at the right side of the person in need.

He saves that person from those who would sentence him to death.

**Pause and answer**

Who stands beside the person in need at the end of the song?
