# Psalm 124 — We escaped like a bird

**About this psalm**

David asks Israel to remember a time when enemies attacked. The people picture their escape as a bird flying free.

## Israel remembers the danger

## Psalm 124:1

David’s song for going up to worship.

Let all Israel say, “What if the LORD had not been on our side?

## Psalm 124:2

What if the LORD had not been on our side when people attacked us?

## Psalm 124:3

Their anger burned against us.

They would have swallowed us alive.

## Psalm 124:4

It would have been like a flood covering us.

A rushing stream would have swept over us.

## Psalm 124:5

The wild waters would have carried us away.”

## The people give thanks

## Psalm 124:6

Praise the LORD!

He did not leave us for our enemies to tear apart with their teeth.

## Psalm 124:7

We escaped like a bird from a bird catcher’s net.

The net tore, and we got away!

![A net with ropes crossing each other.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the ropes**

Imagine a tear in a net big enough for a bird to fly through. The people use that picture to describe their escape.

## Psalm 124:8

Our help comes from the LORD.

He made heaven and earth.

**Pause and answer**

Who helped the people escape?
