# Psalm 38 — LORD, stay close to me

**About this psalm**

David is in pain. His friends stand far away. David tells God what he has done wrong and asks God to come close.

## David asks for mercy

## Psalm 38:1

David wrote this song for remembrance.

LORD, please do not scold me in your anger.

Do not punish me in your fury.

## Psalm 38:2

Your arrows have gone deep into me.

Your hand presses down on me.

## Psalm 38:3

My whole body hurts because of your anger.

Even my bones find no rest because of my sin.

## Psalm 38:4

My wrongs have risen over my head.

They are a load too heavy for me to carry.

**A load too heavy to carry**

David pictures his guilt as a weight pressing down on him. He feels unable to carry it any longer.

## David describes his pain

## Psalm 38:5

My wounds smell bad and fill with pus.

I acted foolishly and did wrong.

## Psalm 38:6

I walk bent over, with my head down.

All day long I grieve.

## Psalm 38:7

A burning pain fills my lower back.

No part of my body feels well.

## Psalm 38:8

I feel numb and crushed.

The pain deep inside me makes me groan.

## God hears David groan

## Psalm 38:9

Lord, you know everything I long for.

You hear every groan.

## Psalm 38:10

My heart pounds. My strength is gone.

Even my eyes have lost their light.

## Psalm 38:11

Those I love and my friends avoid me because I am sick.

My own family stands far away.

## Psalm 38:12

People who want me dead set traps for me.

They talk about how to hurt me.

All day long they plan ways to trick me.

![A net made from crossing ropes.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Imagine getting your foot caught in the ropes. David says his enemies set traps. They want to catch him through their lies.

## David waits for an answer

## Psalm 38:13

I am like someone who cannot hear them.

I keep my mouth closed like someone who cannot speak.

## Psalm 38:14

It is as if I hear nothing.

I give no answer.

## Psalm 38:15

LORD, I wait for you.

Lord my God, you will answer me.

**Pause and answer**

When David gives his enemies no reply, who does he expect to answer him?

## Psalm 38:16

I asked you, “Do not let them celebrate my trouble.

They laugh at me when my foot slips.”

## David admits his wrong

## Psalm 38:17

I am about to fall.

The pain never leaves me.

## Psalm 38:18

I admit the wrong I have done.

My sin troubles me.

## Psalm 38:19

I have many enemies, though I gave them no cause.

Many people hate me for no reason.

## Psalm 38:20

I did them good, and they hurt me in return.

They attack me because I try to do what is right.

## David asks God to stay

## Psalm 38:21

LORD, please do not leave me.

My God, stay close to me.

## Psalm 38:22

Hurry and help me, Lord.

You are the one who saves me.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David ask God to do while his family stands far away?
