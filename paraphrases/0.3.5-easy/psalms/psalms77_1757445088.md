# Psalm 77 — I remember what you have done

**About this psalm**

Asaph lies awake and wonders if God still cares. He remembers how God led his people through the sea.

## Asaph calls to God at night

## Psalm 77:1

Asaph wrote this song for the music leader, according to Jeduthun.

I cried out to God.

I called aloud for God to hear me.

## Psalm 77:2

When trouble came, I turned to the Lord.

All night I kept reaching out my hands in prayer.

Nothing could comfort me.

## Psalm 77:3

God, when I remembered you, I groaned.

As I thought about you, my strength drained away.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 77:4

You kept me awake.

I was too troubled to speak.

## Asaph remembers a song and asks questions

## Psalm 77:5

I thought about the old days, many years ago.

## Psalm 77:6

At night I remembered the song I used to sing.

I thought deeply and asked myself these questions.

![A night sky above a dark horizon.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Look at the night sky**

Picture Asaph lying awake in the dark. A song comes to mind as he thinks about God.

## Psalm 77:7

“Will the Lord turn us away forever?

Will he ever show us kindness again?

## Psalm 77:8

Has his lasting love come to an end?

Has his promise failed for all time?

## Psalm 77:9

Has God forgotten to be kind?

Has his anger left no room for pity?”

Selah.

## Psalm 77:10

Then I said, “It hurts to think that God Most High no longer uses his strong hand as he once did.”

**What does God’s strong hand mean?**

Asaph remembers how God used his power to help people. Now Asaph wonders why God seems to act differently.

## Asaph remembers what God has done

## Psalm 77:11

I will remember what the LORD has done.

Yes, I will remember the wonders you did long ago.

## Psalm 77:12

I will think about all you have done.

I will take time to remember your mighty acts.

## Psalm 77:13

God, your ways are holy.

What god is as great as our God?

## Psalm 77:14

You are the God who does wonders.

People from many nations see your strength.

## Psalm 77:15

You used your power to set your people free.

They were the families of Jacob and Joseph.

Selah.

## God makes a way through the sea

## Psalm 77:16

God, the waters saw you.

They saw you and churned.

Even the deep waters shook.

## Psalm 77:17

Rain poured from the clouds.

Thunder rolled across the sky.

Your arrows flashed back and forth.

**Why speak of arrows?**

Asaph pictures flashes of lightning as arrows streaking across the sky.

## Psalm 77:18

Your thunder roared in the swirling wind.

Lightning lit up the world.

The ground shook and trembled.

## Psalm 77:19

Your path went through the sea.

You made your way through the mighty waters.

Yet no one could find your footprints.

## Psalm 77:20

You led your people like a shepherd leading sheep.

You used Moses and Aaron to guide them.

**Pause and answer**

Who did God use to guide his people through the sea?
