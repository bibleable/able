# Psalm 85 — Give your people life again

**About this psalm**

The people remember a time when God brought them home. They ask him to help them again. Then one singer listens for God’s answer.

## The people remember God’s kindness

## Psalm 85:1

A song of the sons of Korah for the music leader.

LORD, you were kind to your land.

You brought Jacob’s people back from captivity.

## Psalm 85:2

You forgave the wrong your people had done.

You covered all their sins.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 85:3

You held back all your fury.

Your fierce anger came to an end.

## They ask God to restore them

## Psalm 85:4

God, you are the one who saves us.

Restore us again.

Let go of your anger toward us.

## Psalm 85:5

Will you stay angry with us forever?

Will you stay angry with our children and their children?

## Psalm 85:6

Will you give us life again?

Then we, your people, can rejoice in you.

## Psalm 85:7

LORD, show us your lasting love.

Come and save us.

## A singer listens for peace

## Psalm 85:8

I will listen to what the LORD God says.

He will speak peace to his faithful people.

He will keep them from going back to their foolish ways.

## Psalm 85:9

He is near and ready to save those who honor him.

Then his glory will be at home in our land.

## Goodness and peace meet

## Psalm 85:10

Lasting love and faithfulness meet.

Goodness and peace greet each other with a kiss.

**Why speak of a kiss?**

The singer pictures goodness and peace as friends greeting each other. When God restores the people, they can live in peace and do what is right together.

## Psalm 85:11

Faithfulness grows up from the earth like a plant.

Goodness looks down from heaven.

## Psalm 85:12

The LORD will give us good things.

Our land will produce its crops.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Find the head of grain**

Picture this stalk growing out of the soil. The singer looks forward to the food the land will produce.

## Psalm 85:13

Goodness will go ahead of the LORD.

It will prepare the path where he will walk.

**Pause and answer**

What does the singer expect God to speak to his people?
