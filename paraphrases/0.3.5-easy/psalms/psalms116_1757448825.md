# Psalm 116 — The LORD heard my cry

**About this psalm**

The singer remembers being close to death and calling for help. Now he wants to thank God where everyone can hear.

## A cry for mercy

## Psalm 116:1

I love the LORD.

He heard my voice when I asked for mercy.

## Psalm 116:2

He listened to me.

So I will call on him as long as I live.

## Psalm 116:3

Death wrapped around me like ropes.

The terror of Sheol, the place of the dead, swept over me.

I faced trouble and sorrow.

## Psalm 116:4

Then I called to the LORD.

I said, “LORD, please save my life!”

## The singer can rest again

## Psalm 116:5

The LORD is kind and does what is right.

Our God cares deeply for us.

## Psalm 116:6

The LORD protects those who trust him like a child.

When I was helpless, he saved me.

## Psalm 116:7

I tell myself, “Rest again.

The LORD has been good to you.”

## Psalm 116:8

You saved me from death.

You kept my eyes from tears and my feet from falling.

## Psalm 116:9

I will walk with the LORD here among the living.

## What can I give the LORD?

## Psalm 116:10

I trusted God, so I spoke openly.

I said, “I am suffering terribly.”

## Psalm 116:11

In my fear I said, “Everyone lies!”

## Psalm 116:12

How can I thank the LORD for all the good he has done for me?

## Psalm 116:13

I will lift the cup of salvation.

I will call on the name of the LORD.

![A cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Look at the cup**

Picture the singer holding up this cup in front of the people. It is his way of thanking the LORD for saving him.

## Psalm 116:14

In front of all his people, I will keep my promises to the LORD.

## A promise made in public

## Psalm 116:15

When one of his faithful people dies, it matters deeply to the LORD.

## Psalm 116:16

LORD, I truly am your servant.

I serve you, just as my mother served you.

You broke the bonds that held me.

## Psalm 116:17

I will bring you an offering to say thank you.

I will call on the name of the LORD.

## Psalm 116:18

In front of all his people, I will keep my promises to the LORD.

## Psalm 116:19

I will do this in the courtyards of the LORD’s house in Jerusalem.

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!
