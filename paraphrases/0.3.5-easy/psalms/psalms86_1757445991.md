# Psalm 86 — Teach me your way

**About this psalm**

David asks God to listen because he needs help. He wants to learn God’s way and follow it with his whole heart.

## David asks God to listen

## Psalm 86:1

A prayer of David.

LORD, listen and answer me.

I am poor and need help.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Find the ear**

Imagine hearing someone call for help. David asks God to listen when he calls.

## Psalm 86:2

Keep me alive, for I am devoted to you.

You are my God.

I am your servant. I trust you to save me.

## Psalm 86:3

Lord, show me mercy.

I call to you all day.

## Psalm 86:4

Lord, bring joy to me, your servant.

I turn to you with my whole being.

## David trusts God to answer

## Psalm 86:5

Lord, you are kind and ready to forgive.

Your lasting love is great for all who call on you.

## Psalm 86:6

LORD, hear my prayer.

Listen as I ask you for mercy.

## Psalm 86:7

When trouble comes, I call to you because you answer me.

## All nations will honor God

## Psalm 86:8

Lord, none of the gods is like you.

No one does what you do.

## Psalm 86:9

Lord, you made all the nations.

They will come and bow down before you.

They will give honor to your name.

## Psalm 86:10

You are great and do wonderful things.

You alone are God.

## David wants to follow God

## Psalm 86:11

LORD, teach me your way.

Help me live by your truth.

Help me honor you with my whole heart.

**What does David mean by his whole heart?**

David wants everything he chooses and does to show his love and respect for God.

## Psalm 86:12

Lord my God, I will praise you with my whole heart.

I will honor your name forever.

## Psalm 86:13

Your lasting love for me is great.

You rescued me from deep in Sheol, the place of the dead.

## David asks for strength

## Psalm 86:14

God, proud people have come against me.

A cruel group of men wants to kill me.

They pay no attention to you.

## Psalm 86:15

But you, Lord, care deeply and show kindness.

You are slow to become angry.

Your love lasts, and you keep your word.

## Psalm 86:16

Turn to me and show me mercy.

Give me your strength, for I serve you.

Save me, the son of a woman who served you.

## Psalm 86:17

Give me a sign of your goodness.

Let my enemies see it and feel ashamed.

For you, LORD, have helped and comforted me.
