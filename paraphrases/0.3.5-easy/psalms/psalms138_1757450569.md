# Psalm 138 — When I called, you answered

**About this psalm**

David thanks God for answering him when he called. He looks ahead to the day when kings will hear God’s words and sing too.

## David gives thanks

## Psalm 138:1

A song of David.

I thank you with my whole heart.

I sing your praise in front of the gods.

## Psalm 138:2

I bow toward your holy temple.

I thank you for your lasting love and for keeping your word.

You have honored your name and your word above everything else.

![Two rooms inside Solomon’s temple.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

**Look at the place of worship**

Find the large hall. The drawing shows what a temple looks like inside. David says he bows toward God’s holy place to give thanks.

## Psalm 138:3

When I called, you answered me that very day.

You gave me courage and made me strong inside.

## The kings will sing

## Psalm 138:4

LORD, all the kings on earth will thank you when they hear what you have said.

## Psalm 138:5

They will sing about what you do, LORD, because your glory is great.

## Psalm 138:6

The LORD rules from high above, yet he notices people who have little power.

He knows the proud even from far away.

## David trusts God in trouble

## Psalm 138:7

When trouble surrounds me, you keep me alive despite my enemies’ anger.

You reach out your strong right hand and save me.

**Why speak of a strong hand?**

Picture someone reaching out to pull another person to safety. David uses that picture for the way God helps him.

## Psalm 138:8

The LORD will finish what he has planned for me.

LORD, your love lasts forever.

Please keep caring for what your own hands have made.
