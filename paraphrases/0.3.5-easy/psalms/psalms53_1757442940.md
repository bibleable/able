# Psalm 53 — God looks down from heaven

**About this psalm**

David asks whether anyone is looking for God. He tells how God defeated the people who surrounded Israel, then prays for Israel to be free.

## God looks for people who seek him

## Psalm 53:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

The song is called a Maskil. Follow the musical instruction called Mahalath.

A foolish person thinks, “There is no God.”

People have turned bad. They do terrible things.

No one does what is good.

**Words in the title**

Maskil is an old word for a kind of song. Mahalath is a musical term. Their exact meanings are uncertain.

## Psalm 53:2

God looks down from heaven at all people.

Does anyone understand?

Is anyone looking for God?

## Psalm 53:3

Everyone has turned away.

Together they have gone bad.

No one does what is good.

Not even one.

## God defeats the attackers

## Psalm 53:4

Will the people who do evil ever learn?

They eat up my people as easily as they eat bread.

They refuse to call on God.

![Loaves of bread.](../pictures/ahimelech-gives-david-bread.svg)

**Look at the bread**

Think of how often someone eats bread. David says these people hurt others as casually as they eat a meal.

## Psalm 53:5

Terror grips them, even where there is nothing to fear.

God scattered the bones of the enemies who surrounded you.

You put them to shame because God rejected them.

## David longs for Israel to be free

## Psalm 53:6

If only help would come from Zion to save Israel!

When God brings his captive people back, let Jacob rejoice!

Let Israel be glad!

**Two names for one people**

Zion is in Jerusalem. Here Jacob and Israel both mean the people of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David hope God will do for Israel?
