# Psalm 65 — God waters the earth

**About this psalm**

David sings to the God who listens to prayer. He sees signs of God’s care in the rain, the sheep, and the ripe grain.

## God hears prayer

## Psalm 65:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

God, we are ready to praise you in Zion.

We will keep the promises we made to you.

**A place named Zion**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem, where the people came to worship God.

## Psalm 65:2

You listen when people pray.

People everywhere will come to you.

## Psalm 65:3

My sins are too much for me.

You take away the guilt of the wrong we have done.

## Psalm 65:4

You choose people and bring them close.

How good it is to live in your temple courts!

Your house is holy.

The good things there fill us.

**What are temple courts?**

They are open spaces around the temple where people came to worship.

## God stills the waves

## Psalm 65:5

God, you save us.

You answer us by doing what is right.

What you do fills us with wonder.

Even people on distant shores and faraway seas hope in you.

## Psalm 65:6

You are strong.

You used your strength to form the mountains.

## Psalm 65:7

You quiet the roaring seas and their crashing waves.

You also quiet the uproar among nations.

## Psalm 65:8

Far away, people see your wonders and tremble.

You make the sunrise and sunset shout for joy.

## God sends water for the grain

## Psalm 65:9

You care for the earth and water it.

You make the soil rich.

Your streams are full of water.

You prepare grain for us by caring for the land.

## Psalm 65:10

You soak the rows that farmers have dug.

You smooth the raised strips of earth.

Your rain softens the soil.

You help the crops grow.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the grain**

Find the head of grain at the top. Think of the rain that softened the soil so this plant could grow.

## Psalm 65:11

You give the year a rich harvest.

Wherever you go, good things overflow.

## The land seems to sing

## Psalm 65:12

Grass grows thick in the wild places.

The hills seem to dress themselves in joy.

## Psalm 65:13

Flocks of sheep cover the grasslands.

Grain fills the valleys.

The whole land seems to sing and shout for joy.
