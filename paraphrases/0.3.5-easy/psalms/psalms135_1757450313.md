# Psalm 135 — Praise the LORD in his courtyards

**About this psalm**

The singer calls to people standing in God’s house. He remembers what God did for Israel and asks everyone there to join in praise.

## The people gather to praise

## Psalm 135:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

All who serve the LORD, praise his name.

## Psalm 135:2

You stand in the LORD’s house.

You gather in the courtyards of our God.

## Psalm 135:3

Praise the LORD because he is good.

Sing to him because his name is lovely.

## Psalm 135:4

The LORD chose Jacob’s family for his own.

He treasures his people Israel.

## God rules the sky, land, and sea

## Psalm 135:5

I know the LORD is great.

Our Lord is above all gods.

## Psalm 135:6

The LORD does what he chooses in heaven and on earth.

He rules even the deepest parts of the seas.

## Psalm 135:7

He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth.

He sends lightning with the rain.

He brings the wind out of his storehouses.

**Where does the wind come from?**

The singer pictures God opening a storehouse and letting out the wind. Even the weather is in God’s hands.

## God gave Israel a land

## Psalm 135:8

In Egypt he struck down the firstborn of both people and animals.

## Psalm 135:9

Egypt, you saw the signs and wonders he sent against Pharaoh and all his servants.

## Psalm 135:10

He defeated many nations and killed powerful kings.

## Psalm 135:11

Among them were Sihon, king of the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan.

He also defeated all the kings of Canaan.

## Psalm 135:12

He gave their land to his people Israel.

They could pass it on to their children.

## The LORD remembers his people

## Psalm 135:13

LORD, your name lasts forever.

One generation after another remembers what you have done.

## Psalm 135:14

The LORD will speak up for his people and show that they are in the right.

He will show mercy to his servants.

## Statues cannot hear

## Psalm 135:15

People make the idols of the nations from silver and gold.

## Psalm 135:16

These statues have mouths, yet they cannot speak.

They have eyes, yet they cannot see.

## Psalm 135:17

They have ears, yet they cannot hear.

No breath comes from their mouths.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Find the ear**

Listen for a sound near you. A statue may have an ear carved into it, but it cannot hear that sound.

## Psalm 135:18

The people who make idols become like them.

So does everyone who trusts in them.

## Everyone joins in praise

## Psalm 135:19

People of Israel, praise the LORD!

Family of Aaron, praise the LORD!

## Psalm 135:20

Family of Levi, praise the LORD!

All who honor the LORD, praise him!

## Psalm 135:21

Let praise rise from Zion to the LORD who lives in Jerusalem.

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

**Pause and answer**

How does the LORD feel about the people he chose?
