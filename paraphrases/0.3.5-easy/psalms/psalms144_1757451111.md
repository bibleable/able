# Psalm 144 — A prayer for peace in our streets

**About this psalm**

David asks God to save him from people who tell lies. Then he pictures what peace would mean for the children, the fields, and the streets of his people.

## David praises his protector

## Psalm 144:1

A song of David.

Praise the LORD, my rock!

He trains my hands and fingers for battle.

## Psalm 144:2

He loves me faithfully. He is my strong fort and my rescuer.

He is my shield. I go to him for safety.

He brings peoples under my rule.

## A life as short as a breath

## Psalm 144:3

LORD, what are human beings that you notice them?

Why do you give them a thought?

## Psalm 144:4

A human life is like a breath.

Our days pass like a moving shadow.

## David asks God to come down

## Psalm 144:5

LORD, open the heavens and come down.

Touch the mountains and make them smoke.

## Psalm 144:6

Send lightning and scatter my enemies.

Send your arrows and make them flee.

## Psalm 144:7

Reach down from above and set me free.

Pull me from deep waters, from the grip of people from other lands.

## Psalm 144:8

They tell lies.

Even when they raise their right hands to make promises, they deceive.

## A new song for God

## Psalm 144:9

God, I will sing a new song to you.

I will play for you on a harp with ten strings.

![A harp with strings stretched across its frame.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the strings**

Imagine plucking the strings while singing a new tune. David wants to thank the God who rescues him.

## Psalm 144:10

You give kings victory.

You rescue your servant David from the sword that kills.

## Psalm 144:11

Set me free from those people from other lands.

They tell lies.

Even when they raise their right hands to make promises, they deceive.

## Children grow and fields give food

## Psalm 144:12

Then our sons will grow like young plants that receive good care.

Our daughters will be like beautifully carved pillars at the corners of a palace.

**Why mention plants and pillars?**

A young plant can grow strong with care. A carved stone pillar gives a palace strength and beauty. David pictures the children of his people growing up in safety.

## Psalm 144:13

Our storehouses will hold every kind of crop.

Our sheep will have thousands of lambs in our fields, even tens of thousands.

## Psalm 144:14

Our oxen will carry heavy loads.

No one will break through our walls or carry our people away.

No one will cry out in grief in our streets.

## Psalm 144:15

How blessed are the people who live like this!

How blessed are the people whose God is the LORD!

**Pause and answer**

What does David hope will change in the streets of his city?
