# Psalm 69 — God, do not let me sink

**About this psalm**

David feels as if deep water is closing over him. People laugh at him when he asks God for help. He tells God what hurts and asks God to act.

## David feels as if he is drowning

## Psalm 69:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “Lilies.”

God, save me!

The water is up to my neck.

## Psalm 69:2

I sink deep into mud.

My feet cannot find firm ground.

I have drifted into deep water.

The flood sweeps over me.

**Why does David describe deep water?**

Picture someone trying to stand in deep mud. David feels just as helpless in the trouble his enemies cause.

## Psalm 69:3

I am worn out from calling for help.

My throat is dry.

My eyes grow tired as I look for my God.

## Psalm 69:4

People hate me for no reason.

They outnumber the hairs on my head.

Many want to destroy me, though I never hurt them.

I must pay back what I never stole.

## People mock David for honoring God

## Psalm 69:5

God, you know the foolish things I have done.

You see everything I have done wrong.

## Psalm 69:6

Lord GOD, you lead heaven’s armies.

Keep those who hope in you from feeling ashamed because of me.

God of Israel, protect the good name of those who seek you.

## Psalm 69:7

People insult me because I belong to you.

My face shows my shame.

## Psalm 69:8

My own brothers treat me like a stranger.

Even my mother’s sons act as if I came from another land.

## Psalm 69:9

I care so deeply about your house that it takes all my strength.

When people insult you, their words hurt me too.

**God’s house**

David means the place where people worship God.

## Psalm 69:10

I cried and went without food.

People insulted me for that.

## Psalm 69:11

I wore rough cloth to show my sorrow.

People made a joke of me.

## Psalm 69:12

People sitting at the city gate make fun of me.

People who get drunk sing songs mocking me.

## David asks God to pull him out

## Psalm 69:13

But I pray to you, LORD.

May this be the time you show me kindness.

Your lasting love is great.

Answer me, God. I can count on you to save me.

## Psalm 69:14

Pull me out of the mud.

Do not let me sink.

Save me from those who hate me.

Pull me out of deep water.

## Psalm 69:15

Do not let the flood sweep over me.

Do not let the deep water swallow me.

Do not let the pit of death close over me.

## Psalm 69:16

Answer me, LORD, because your lasting love is good.

Turn toward me because you care so deeply.

## Psalm 69:17

I am your servant. Do not turn away.

I am in trouble. Please answer quickly!

## Psalm 69:18

Come close to me and set me free.

Rescue me from my enemies.

## David finds no one to comfort him

## Psalm 69:19

You know how people insult me.

You see my shame and how they dishonor me.

You see every one of my enemies.

## Psalm 69:20

Their insults break my heart.

I have lost hope.

I looked for someone who cared.

I found nobody to comfort me.

## Psalm 69:21

They put bitter poison in my food.

When I was thirsty, they gave me sour vinegar.

## David asks God to judge his enemies

## Psalm 69:22

May their own dinner table become a trap.

May it catch them and bring them the punishment they deserve.

## Psalm 69:23

May their eyes grow dark until they cannot see.

May their backs stay bent forever.

## Psalm 69:24

Pour out your anger on them.

Let your burning anger catch up with them.

## Psalm 69:25

May their camp stand empty.

Let no one live in their tents.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Imagine coming to a camp and finding every tent empty. David asks God to leave his enemies’ camp like that.

## Psalm 69:26

They hunt down the one you struck.

They talk about the pain of those you wounded.

## Psalm 69:27

Count each wrong they do against them.

Do not count them among those who are right with you.

## Psalm 69:28

Erase their names from the Book of Life.

Leave their names off the list of those who do right.

**What is the Book of Life?**

David pictures God keeping a record of names. He asks God to remove his enemies from that record.

## David will sing his thanks

## Psalm 69:29

But I am hurting and in trouble.

God, save me and keep me safe.

## Psalm 69:30

I will sing to praise God’s name.

I will thank him and tell how great he is.

## Psalm 69:31

My song will please the LORD more than the gift of an ox, even a bull with horns and hooves.

## Psalm 69:32

Humble people will see this and be glad.

You who seek God, take heart again!

## Psalm 69:33

The LORD listens to people in need.

He cares for his people in prison.

## God will give his people a home

## Psalm 69:34

Let heaven and earth praise him!

Let the seas praise him, with everything that moves in them.

## Psalm 69:35

God will save Zion.

He will rebuild the cities of Judah.

His people will live there and own the land.

**A place named Zion**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem. Jerusalem is a city in Judah.

## Psalm 69:36

The families of God’s servants will inherit the land.

People who love his name will make their homes there.

**Pause and answer**

What does David say the LORD does when people in need call to him?
