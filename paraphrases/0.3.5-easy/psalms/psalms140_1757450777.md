# Psalm 140 — Protect me from violent people

**About this psalm**

David tells God about people who want to hurt him. He asks God to stop their plans and remembers how God stands up for people in need.

## Words that hurt

## Psalm 140:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

LORD, rescue me from people who do evil.

Keep me safe from violent people.

## Psalm 140:2

They plan to do harm.

All day long they stir up fights that lead to war.

## Psalm 140:3

Their tongues are sharp like snakes’ tongues.

Their words are like poison from a snake.

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## A trap along the path

## Psalm 140:4

LORD, keep me out of their hands.

Protect me from violent people who plan to make me fall.

## Psalm 140:5

Proud people have hidden a trap for me.

They have stretched a net across my path.

They have set out bait to catch me.

Selah.

![A net with ropes crossing each other.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Follow one rope across the net. David feels as if his enemies have spread a net where he needs to walk.

## David asks God to stop the harm

## Psalm 140:6

I say to the LORD, “You are my God.”

LORD, hear my cry for help.

## Psalm 140:7

Lord GOD, you are strong enough to save me.

You protect my head when I go into battle.

## Psalm 140:8

LORD, do not give cruel people what they want.

Stop their evil plans so they cannot boast.

Selah.

## Psalm 140:9

My enemies surround me.

May the trouble their words have caused fall on their own heads.

## Psalm 140:10

May hot coals fall on them.

May they fall into fire and deep mud and never rise again.

## Psalm 140:11

May those who spread lies find no lasting place in the land.

May disaster catch up with those who act violently.

## God stands up for people in need

## Psalm 140:12

I know the LORD stands up for poor people.

He sees that people in need receive justice.

## Psalm 140:13

People who do right will thank you, LORD.

Honest people will live close to you.
