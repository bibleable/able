# Psalm 22 — My God, why have you left me?

**About this psalm**

David cries out to God while people laugh at him. Later he tells the people gathered for worship that God heard his cry.

## David calls out by day and by night

## Psalm 22:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “The Doe of the Dawn.”

My God, my God, why have you left me?

Why are you so far from saving me?

Why do my groans seem so far from you?

## Psalm 22:2

My God, I cry out all day.

You do not answer.

I cry out at night and find no rest.

## Psalm 22:3

Yet you are holy.

You sit as King while Israel sings your praise.

## Psalm 22:4

Our ancestors trusted you.

They trusted you, and you rescued them.

## Psalm 22:5

They cried out, and you set them free.

They trusted you, and you never let them down.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when David’s ancestors cried out to God?

## People laugh at David

## Psalm 22:6

But I feel like a worm, hardly even human.

People look down on me.

They treat me as if I have no worth.

## Psalm 22:7

Everyone who sees me laughs at me.

They curl their lips and shake their heads.

## Psalm 22:8

They say, “He trusts the LORD.

Let the LORD set him free!

If the LORD delights in him, let the LORD rescue him!”

## David remembers his mother’s care

## Psalm 22:9

Yet you brought me out of my mother’s womb.

You kept me safe as she fed me at her breast.

## Psalm 22:10

I have depended on you since birth.

You have been my God since I was in the womb.

## Psalm 22:11

Stay close to me. Trouble is near.

There is no one to help.

## Enemies close in

## Psalm 22:12

Many bulls stand around me.

Strong bulls from Bashan close in on every side.

## Psalm 22:13

They open their mouths wide at me.

They are like lions roaring and tearing their prey.

## Psalm 22:14

I feel like water poured out on the ground.

All my bones feel out of joint.

My heart melts inside me like wax.

## Psalm 22:15

My strength has dried up like a broken clay pot.

My tongue sticks to the roof of my mouth.

You lay me down in the dust to die.

![A jar made of clay.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the clay jar**

Imagine picking up a dry piece of a broken jar. David uses that dry clay to show how weak and thirsty he feels. The bulls and lions are pictures of how frightening his enemies are.

## David’s enemies take his clothes

## Psalm 22:16

Dogs are all around me.

A group of cruel men closes in on me.

They have pierced my hands and my feet.

## Psalm 22:17

I can count every bone in my body.

My enemies stare at me and enjoy my pain.

## Psalm 22:18

They share out my clothes among themselves.

They cast lots to decide who gets my clothing.

**What does casting lots mean?**

People used small objects called lots to make a choice by chance. Here the choice is who gets the clothes.

## David asks for rescue

## Psalm 22:19

LORD, stay close to me!

You are my strength. Hurry and help me!

## Psalm 22:20

Save me from the sword.

Save my precious life from the wild dogs.

## Psalm 22:21

Save me from the lion’s mouth!

At the horns of the wild oxen, you answered me!

## David praises the LORD with others

## Psalm 22:22

I will tell my brothers about you.

When the people gather, I will praise you.

## Psalm 22:23

All who honor the LORD, sing his praise!

All you children of Jacob, give the LORD honor!

All you people of Israel, stand in awe of him!

## Psalm 22:24

The LORD saw the pain of the suffering man.

He did not look down on him or turn away.

He listened when the man cried for help.

## Psalm 22:25

In the great gathering I sing your praise.

Before those who honor you, I will keep my promises to you.

## Psalm 22:26

Poor people will eat until they are full.

Those who seek the LORD will praise him.

May your hearts live forever!

## The song reaches future generations

## Psalm 22:27

People everywhere will remember and turn to the LORD.

Families from every nation will bow before him.

## Psalm 22:28

The LORD is King.

He rules over the nations.

## Psalm 22:29

Rich people throughout the earth will feast and worship.

Everyone going down to the dust will kneel before him.

Even those who cannot stay alive will bow.

## Psalm 22:30

People yet to come will serve him.

They will tell the next generation about the Lord.

## Psalm 22:31

They will tell people still to be born what is right about God’s ways.

They will tell what he has done.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does David want people to tell the next generation?
