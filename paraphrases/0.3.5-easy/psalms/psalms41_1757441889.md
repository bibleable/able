# Psalm 41 — The LORD cares for the sick

**About this psalm**

David sings about people who care for the poor. Then he tells the LORD about his own sickness and a friend who turned against him.

## The LORD sees those who care for the poor

## Psalm 41:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

How blessed is the person who cares for the poor!

When trouble comes, the LORD will rescue that person.

## Psalm 41:2

The LORD will protect them and keep them alive.

He will bless them in the land.

He will keep them from falling into their enemies’ hands.

## Psalm 41:3

When they lie sick in bed, the LORD will support them.

He will help them get well again.

![A sleeping mat.](../pictures/mat.svg)

**Look at the mat**

Picture someone lying down, too sick to get up. David says the LORD stays with that person and gives them strength.

## David hears cruel words

## Psalm 41:4

I said, “LORD, show me kindness.

Heal me, for I have sinned against you.”

## Psalm 41:5

My enemies say cruel things about me.

They ask, “When will he die so people forget him?”

## Psalm 41:6

A visitor comes and lies to me.

He collects ugly stories about me.

When he leaves, he tells them to everyone.

## Psalm 41:7

Those who hate me whisper together about me.

They imagine the worst for me.

## Psalm 41:8

They say, “A terrible sickness has come over him.

He will never get out of that bed!”

## A trusted friend turns against David

## Psalm 41:9

Even a close friend turned against me.

I trusted him. We shared my bread.

Now he has raised his heel against me.

**What does a raised heel mean?**

Picture a foot lifting to kick. David uses that picture to say that a friend he trusted has turned on him.

## Psalm 41:10

LORD, show me kindness. Help me stand up again.

Then I can pay them back for what they did.

**Pause and answer**

What does David ask the LORD to help him do while he is sick?

## David gives praise

## Psalm 41:11

I know you are pleased with me.

My enemy has not won against me.

## Psalm 41:12

You hold me up as I live honestly.

You keep me with you forever.

## Psalm 41:13

Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, forever and ever!

Amen and amen.

**A word in the song**

Amen means “Let it be so.” David says it twice as he praises God.
