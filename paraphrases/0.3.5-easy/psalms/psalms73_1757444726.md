# Psalm 73 — You hold my right hand

**About this psalm**

Asaph sees cruel people getting rich. He wonders why he should keep doing right. His thinking changes when he goes to worship God.

## Asaph almost loses his footing

## Psalm 73:1

A song of Asaph.

God truly is good to Israel, to those whose hearts are pure.

## Psalm 73:2

But my feet almost slipped.

I nearly fell.

## Psalm 73:3

I saw proud people getting rich while doing wrong.

I wanted what they had.

**Why does Asaph say his feet slipped?**

Asaph nearly stopped trusting God when he saw how well proud people lived. He compares that moment to losing his footing.

## Proud people seem to have easy lives

## Psalm 73:4

They have plenty to eat.

Even when they die, they seem to have no struggle.

## Psalm 73:5

They do not carry the troubles other people carry.

They do not suffer the way others do.

## Psalm 73:6

So they wear their pride like a necklace.

They wear violence like a coat.

**Why a necklace and a coat?**

People can see what someone wears. Just as plainly, everyone can see how proud and violent these people are.

## Psalm 73:7

Their wealth gives them room to do wrong.

There is no end to the things they dream up.

## Psalm 73:8

They make fun of people and speak to hurt them.

They boast about how they will push others down.

## Psalm 73:9

They even speak against heaven.

They boast as if the whole earth belongs to them.

## People question what God knows

## Psalm 73:10

So their people come back to them.

They drink all the water they want.

## Psalm 73:11

Those who do wrong say, “How could God know?

Does God Most High know what we do?”

## Psalm 73:12

Look at the people who do wrong.

They live without worry and keep getting richer.

![Ten silver coins.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Look at the coins**

Imagine watching someone gain more coins while hurting others. Asaph struggles to understand why that person seems to do so well.

## Asaph wonders why he does right

## Psalm 73:13

I thought, “What good did it do to keep my heart pure?

Why did I wash my hands to show my innocence?

## Psalm 73:14

I suffer all day long.

Every morning brings another punishment.”

## Psalm 73:15

God, if I had spoken that way to others, I would have betrayed your children.

**Who are God’s children here?**

Asaph means the people who trust God. He cares about how his words might affect them.

## Psalm 73:16

I tried to make sense of all this.

It troubled me.

## Asaph sees where this leads

## Psalm 73:17

Then I went into your holy place.

There I understood what would happen to those people in the end.

## Psalm 73:18

God, you set them on slippery ground.

You bring them down to ruin.

## Psalm 73:19

Disaster comes on them in a moment!

Terror sweeps them away.

## Psalm 73:20

Lord, a dream fades when someone wakes up.

When you rise to act, you dismiss them like figures in a dream.

## God holds Asaph’s hand

## Psalm 73:21

My heart was bitter.

I felt a sharp pain deep inside.

## Psalm 73:22

I did not understand.

I acted like an animal that knew no better in your presence.

## Psalm 73:23

Yet I am always with you.

You hold my right hand.

## Psalm 73:24

You show me what to do.

Later you will welcome me into glory.

## It is good to be near God

## Psalm 73:25

Who else do I have in heaven but you?

Here on earth, you are the one I long for.

## Psalm 73:26

My body and my heart may grow weak.

But God gives my heart strength.

God is mine forever.

## Psalm 73:27

Those who stay far from you will perish.

You destroy those who turn away from you and break faith with you.

## Psalm 73:28

As for me, it is good to be near God.

I go to the Lord GOD for safety.

I will tell what you have done.

**Pause and answer**

When Asaph feels weak, who gives him strength?
