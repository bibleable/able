# Psalm 119 — Your word lights my path

**About this psalm**

The singer talks to God about learning how to live. The song has 22 parts, one for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet. Each part has eight verses. Read one part at a time.

## Aleph — A life guided by God

## Psalm 119:1

The LORD blesses those who live honestly and follow his law.

## Psalm 119:2

He blesses those who obey his teaching and seek him with their whole heart.

## Psalm 119:3

They turn from wrong and live the way he shows them.

## Psalm 119:4

You gave us instructions to follow with care.

## Psalm 119:5

How I want to keep your rules in everything I do!

## Psalm 119:6

Then I can look at all your commands without shame.

## Psalm 119:7

As I learn your fair decisions, I will praise you with an honest heart.

## Psalm 119:8

I will keep your rules. Please never leave me.

## Beth — Keeping God’s word close

## Psalm 119:9

## Psalm 119:10

Keep me from wandering away from your commands.

## Psalm 119:11

**Keeping a word in your heart**

**Why a leather bag?**

People kept wine in bags made from animal skin. Smoke could dry out the leather. The singer feels worn out like that bag.

## Nun — A lamp for my feet

## Psalm 119:105

![A lamp that gives light.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Find the lamp**

Imagine walking after dark. A lamp shows where to put your foot next. The singer says God’s word helps him choose his next step.
