# Psalm 134 — Praise the LORD at night

**About this psalm**

A call goes out to the people who serve at the LORD’s house after dark. The song ends with a blessing.

## A call to praise

## Psalm 134:1

A song for going up to worship.

Come, all you servants of the LORD!

Praise the LORD as you serve at night in his house.

![A dark night sky above the horizon.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Look at the night sky**

Imagine standing in the LORD’s house after the sun has gone down. The song calls the people on night duty to lift their hands in praise.

## Psalm 134:2

Lift your hands toward his holy place and praise the LORD!

## A blessing from Zion

## Psalm 134:3

May the LORD bless you from Zion.

He is the one who made heaven and earth.

**Pause and answer**

Who does the song call on to praise the LORD at night?
