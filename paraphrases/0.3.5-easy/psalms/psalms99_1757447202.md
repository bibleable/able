# Psalm 99 — The LORD our God is holy

**About this psalm**

The singer invites the nations to honor God as king. Moses, Aaron, and Samuel called out to this king, and he answered them.

## The LORD rules in Zion

## Psalm 99:1

The LORD is king! Let the nations tremble.

He sits on his throne above the cherubim.

Let the earth shake!

**What are cherubim?**

Cherubim are heavenly beings. The song pictures God sitting as king above them.

## Psalm 99:2

The LORD is great in Zion.

He rules high above all peoples.

## Psalm 99:3

Let them praise your great name with wonder.

The LORD is holy!

## The king loves justice

## Psalm 99:4

You are a mighty king who loves justice.

You have made fairness the rule.

You have done what is right and fair for Jacob’s people.

## Psalm 99:5

Praise the LORD our God!

Bow down at his footstool.

He is holy!

**What is a footstool?**

It is a low support for someone’s feet. The singers picture themselves bowing low before God’s throne.

## God answers those who call

## Psalm 99:6

Moses and Aaron served as his priests.

Samuel also called on the LORD.

They called to the LORD, and he answered them.

## Psalm 99:7

He spoke to them from a tall column of cloud.

They obeyed the commands and rules he gave them.

## Psalm 99:8

LORD our God, you answered them.

You forgave them.

You also punished them for the wrong they did.

## Psalm 99:9

Praise the LORD our God!

Worship him at his holy mountain.

The LORD our God is holy.

![A high mountain with a path up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Find the path up the mountain**

Think of people going up to worship. The holy mountain in this song is Zion in Jerusalem.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD do when Moses, Aaron, and Samuel called to him?
