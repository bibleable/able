# Psalm 103 — Remember the LORD’s kindness

**About this psalm**

David reminds himself of the good things the LORD has done. He looks up at the sky to describe how great God’s love is. By the end of the song, he invites everything God made to join in.

## David remembers the LORD’s gifts

## Psalm 103:1

A song of David.

With my whole self, I praise the LORD!

With everything in me, I praise his holy name.

## Psalm 103:2

With my whole self, I praise the LORD!

I must remember all the kind things he has done.

## Psalm 103:3

He forgives all my sins.

He heals all my diseases.

## Psalm 103:4

He rescues my life from the pit of death.

He crowns me with lasting love and tender care.

**What kind of crown does David mean?**

A crown shows that someone has received honor. David feels honored because God treats him with love and care.

## Psalm 103:5

He fills my life with good things.

I feel young and strong again, like an eagle.

## The LORD shows mercy

## Psalm 103:6

The LORD does what is right.

He brings justice to all who suffer under cruel people.

## Psalm 103:7

He showed Moses what his ways were like.

He showed the people of Israel what he could do.

## Psalm 103:8

The LORD cares for us and shows us kindness.

He is slow to grow angry.

His lasting love is great.

## Psalm 103:9

He will not keep accusing us.

His anger will come to an end.

## Psalm 103:10

We have done wrong, yet he has spared us the full punishment our sins deserve.

## Love as great as the sky is high

## Psalm 103:11

How high is the sky above the earth?

That is how great his love is for those who honor him.

![The sun shines high above the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

**Look up from the hills**

Follow the space from the ground to the sun with your eyes. David uses that great distance to help us picture the LORD’s love.

## Psalm 103:12

East is far from west.

That is how far he has taken our sins away from us.

## Psalm 103:13

A father cares tenderly for his children.

The LORD cares like that for those who honor him.

## Psalm 103:14

He knows how we are made.

He remembers that we came from dust.

## A flower fades, and God’s love lasts

## Psalm 103:15

Our lives are like grass.

We bloom like flowers in a field.

## Psalm 103:16

When the wind passes over a flower, it disappears.

Soon there is no sign of where it grew.

## Psalm 103:17

The LORD’s love for those who honor him lasts forever.

He does what is right for their children and grandchildren.

## Psalm 103:18

They keep their covenant with him.

They remember his commands and obey them.

**What is a covenant?**

A covenant is a binding promise. Here the people show they keep their promise to God by doing what he asks.

## Everything God made joins in praise

## Psalm 103:19

The LORD has set up his throne in heaven.

He rules over all.

## Psalm 103:20

Praise the LORD, you mighty angels!

You listen to his voice and do what he commands.

## Psalm 103:21

Praise the LORD, all you armies of heaven!

You serve him and do what he wants.

## Psalm 103:22

Praise the LORD, everything he made, everywhere he rules!

With my whole self, I praise the LORD!
