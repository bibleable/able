# Psalm 12 — The LORD promises safety

**About this psalm**

David hears people use kind-sounding words to hide lies. Then he tells us what the LORD says about people who need help.

## David asks the LORD for help

## Psalm 12:1

David wrote this song for the music leader.

Follow the musical instruction called Sheminith.

Help us, LORD!

The people who follow you are gone.

I cannot find anyone who stays faithful.

**A word in the song**

Sheminith is an old musical term. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 12:2

People tell lies to each other.

They say nice things they do not mean.

They think one thing and say another.

## People boast about their words

## Psalm 12:3

LORD, silence those who use praise to trick people.

Stop those who brag about their power.

## Psalm 12:4

They say, “Our words will help us win.

We can say whatever we like.

Who can rule over us?”

## The LORD answers

## Psalm 12:5

The LORD says, “People are suffering because others hurt them.

I hear people in need groaning.

Now I will rise and help.

I will give safety to those who long for it.”

## Psalm 12:6

The LORD’s words are pure.

They are like silver heated in a furnace.

They are like gold made pure seven times.

![Ten silver coins.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Why does David mention silver?**

Look at the metal in the coins. A furnace is a very hot oven. Workers heat silver to remove what does not belong in it. David means there is no lie in the LORD’s words.

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD promise people who long for safety?

## David trusts the LORD

## Psalm 12:7

LORD, you will keep us safe.

You will always guard us from the people of this generation.

## Psalm 12:8

Those who do evil walk about freely.

People praise things that are shameful.
