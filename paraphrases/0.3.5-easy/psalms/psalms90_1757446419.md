# Psalm 90 — Teach us to number our days

**About this psalm**

Moses prays for the people. He remembers that God was there before the mountains. He asks God to help them live wisely in the time they have.

## God has always been our home

## Psalm 90:1

A prayer of Moses, the man of God.

Lord, one generation after another has found a home in you.

## Psalm 90:2

Before the mountains rose, you were God.

Before you brought the world into being, you were God.

You have always been God. You always will be.

![A tall mountain with a path up its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

Trace its outline from the foot to the top. Moses says God was there before even this mountain existed.

## Our lives pass quickly

## Psalm 90:3

You turn people back to dust.

You say, “Return to dust, you children of earth.”

## Psalm 90:4

To you a thousand years are like a day already gone.

They are like a few hours of the night.

## Psalm 90:5

You sweep people away in their sleep.

They are like fresh grass in the morning.

## Psalm 90:6

In the morning the grass grows green.

By evening it dries up and fades.

**Why speak about grass?**

Grass can turn brown in a single hot day. Moses uses it to show how quickly a human life passes.

## God sees even our secret sins

## Psalm 90:7

Your anger wears us away.

Your fierce anger fills us with fear.

## Psalm 90:8

You see the wrong things we do.

Even our secret sins stand in your light.

## Psalm 90:9

Our days slip away under your anger.

We come to the end of our years with a sigh.

## Psalm 90:10

We may live seventy years, or eighty if we are strong.

Even the years we take pride in bring hard work and sorrow.

Soon the years pass, and our lives fly away.

## Psalm 90:11

Who understands how powerful your anger is?

Your great anger deserves our deepest respect and fear.

## Moses asks God for wisdom and joy

## Psalm 90:12

Teach us to number our days.

Help us grow wise in the way we live.

**What does it mean to number our days?**

Moses asks God to help people remember that their time is short. That can help them choose wisely today.

## Psalm 90:13

Come back to us, LORD! How long must we wait?

Show kindness to us, your servants.

## Psalm 90:14

Fill us with your lasting love in the morning.

Then we can sing for joy all our days.

## Psalm 90:15

Give us as many glad days as the days you let us suffer.

Give us joy to match the years of trouble.

## Psalm 90:16

Let us, your servants, see what you do.

Let our children see your greatness.

## Psalm 90:17

Lord our God, look on us with kindness.

Make the work of our hands last.

Yes, make the work of our hands last!
