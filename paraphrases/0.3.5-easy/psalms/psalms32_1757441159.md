# Psalm 32 — David tells the LORD what he has done

**About this psalm**

David remembers how he felt while he kept his sin secret. Then he tells what happened when he spoke honestly to the LORD.

## The joy of forgiveness

## Psalm 32:1

David wrote this song, called a Maskil.

How blessed is the person whose wrongs God forgives!

God covers that person’s sins.

**A word in the title**

Maskil is an old word used in the titles of several psalms. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 32:2

How blessed is the person who is honest deep inside!

The LORD does not count their sin against them.

## David stops hiding his sin

## Psalm 32:3

When I kept silent, I groaned all day.

Even my bones grew weak.

## Psalm 32:4

Day and night your hand felt heavy on me.

My strength dried up as it does in summer heat.

Selah.

![The sun shines over the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

**Picture the summer heat**

Think of how tired someone feels after a long day in the hot sun. David felt worn out like that while he kept his sin secret.

**A word in the song**

Selah appears three times in this song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 32:5

Then I told you about my sin.

I stopped hiding what I had done.

I said, “I will tell the LORD what I did wrong.”

And you forgave me and took away my guilt.

Selah.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD do when David told him the truth?

## David finds shelter

## Psalm 32:6

So let everyone who follows you pray while they can find you.

Even when floodwaters rise, the waters will not reach them.

## Psalm 32:7

You are my hiding place.

You keep me safe from trouble.

All around me I hear songs about how you save me.

Selah.

## Listen to guidance

## Psalm 32:8

I will teach you which way to go.

I will give you advice and watch over you.

## Psalm 32:9

Do not be like a horse or a mule without understanding.

To bring it near, someone must guide it with a bit and bridle.

**What are a bit and bridle?**

A bridle has straps that fit around an animal’s head. A bit fits in its mouth. A rider uses them to guide the animal. The song asks people to listen willingly.

## A call to rejoice

## Psalm 32:10

People who do evil have many sorrows.

The LORD surrounds those who trust him with faithful love.

## Psalm 32:11

All you who do right, rejoice in the LORD!

All who are honest in your hearts, shout for joy!
