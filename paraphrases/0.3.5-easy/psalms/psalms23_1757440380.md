# Psalm 23 — The LORD is my shepherd

**About this psalm**

David sings about the LORD as a shepherd who stays beside him. Then David pictures himself as a guest at the LORD’s table.

## The shepherd leads David

## Psalm 23:1

David wrote this song.

The LORD is my shepherd.

I have everything I need.

## Psalm 23:2

He gives me green grass to lie down in.

He leads me beside quiet water.

## Psalm 23:3

He gives me new life and strength.

He leads me along paths that are right.

He does this to honor his name.

**What does a shepherd do?**

A shepherd leads sheep to grass and water. David trusts the LORD to care for him like that.

## The LORD stays close in danger

## Psalm 23:4

Even when I walk through a valley dark with the shadow of death, I fear no evil.

You are with me, LORD.

Your rod and your staff bring me comfort.

**A rod and a staff**

A rod is a short, strong stick a shepherd carries for protection. A staff is a long stick a shepherd uses to guide the sheep.

**Pause and answer**

Why does David feel safe in the dark valley?

## The LORD welcomes David

## Psalm 23:5

You set a table for me while my enemies watch.

You pour oil on my head.

You fill my cup until it spills over.

![A cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Look at the cup**

Imagine filling this cup until the drink runs over its edge. David pictures a host who welcomes him with oil and gives him more than enough to drink.

## Psalm 23:6

Your goodness and mercy will follow me every day I live.

I will live in the LORD’s house forever.
