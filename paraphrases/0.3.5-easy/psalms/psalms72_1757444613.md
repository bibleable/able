# Psalm 72 — A king who hears people in need

**About this psalm**

This song asks God to help the king judge fairly. It describes the kind of king who listens when someone with no helper cries out.

## A prayer for a fair king

## Psalm 72:1

A psalm of Solomon.

God, help the king judge fairly, as you do.

Help the king’s son do what is right.

## Psalm 72:2

May the king judge your people fairly.

May he give people who suffer a fair hearing.

## Psalm 72:3

May the mountains bring peace to the people.

May the hills bring what is right.

## Psalm 72:4

May the king stand up for those who suffer.

May he save the children of people in need.

May he crush the power of those who hurt them.

## A king who brings life like rain

## Psalm 72:5

May people honor him as long as the sun shines.

May their children and grandchildren honor him as long as the moon remains.

## Psalm 72:6

May the king be like rain on freshly cut grass.

May he be like spring showers that water the ground.

**Why compare a king to rain?**

Grass grows again when rain soaks the ground. The singer hopes people will thrive under the king’s care.

## Psalm 72:7

May those who do right thrive while he rules.

May people have plenty as long as the moon lasts.

## Kings from distant lands bring gifts

## Psalm 72:8

May he rule from one sea to the next.

May he rule from the Euphrates River to the ends of the earth.

## Psalm 72:9

May people who travel from place to place bow before him.

May his enemies bow so low they lick the dust.

## Psalm 72:10

May kings from Tarshish and distant shores bring gifts to honor him.

May the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts too.

## Psalm 72:11

May every king bow down to him.

May every nation serve him.

## The king listens when people cry out

## Psalm 72:12

For he will rescue people who cry out in need.

He will help those who suffer when nobody else helps.

## Psalm 72:13

He will show kindness to poor and needy people.

He will save the lives of people others mistreat.

## Psalm 72:14

He will free them from cruelty and violence.

Their lives are precious to him.

## A prayer for the king and his land

## Psalm 72:15

May the king live a long life!

May people give him gold from Sheba.

May they always pray for him.

May they speak blessings over him all day long.

## Psalm 72:16

May grain grow thick across the land.

May it sway in the wind on the hilltops.

May fruit trees grow tall like Lebanon’s forests.

May the cities fill with people like a field fills with grass.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Find the head of grain**

Imagine a whole hillside covered with stalks like this one. The singer asks for enough grain to feed the people.

## Psalm 72:17

May people remember his name forever, as long as the sun shines.

May God bless every nation through him.

May all nations call him blessed.

## Praise to the God of Israel

## Psalm 72:18

Praise the LORD God, the God of Israel!

He alone does these wonderful things.

## Psalm 72:19

Praise his glorious name forever!

May his greatness fill the whole earth.

Amen and amen.

**What does amen mean?**

To say amen is to agree with a prayer. It means “May it be so.”

## Psalm 72:20

Here the prayers of David son of Jesse come to an end.

**Pause and answer**

What will the king do when someone with no helper cries out?
