# Psalm 87 — This one was born in Zion

**About this psalm**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem. In this song, people from many lands hear God count them among the people of his city.

## The city God loves

## Psalm 87:1

A song of the sons of Korah.

God built his city on the holy mountains.

## Psalm 87:2

The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the other homes of Jacob’s people.

## Psalm 87:3

City of God, people say wonderful things about you!

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## People from many lands

## Psalm 87:4

God says, “Egypt and Babylon are among those who know me.

So are Philistia, Tyre, and Cush.

I will say of them, ‘This one was born in Zion.’”

**Another name for Egypt**

The song calls Egypt “Rahab.” Here that name means a country.

## Psalm 87:5

People will say of Zion, “This person and that person were born here.

God Most High himself will make the city stand firm.”

## God writes down their names

## Psalm 87:6

The LORD will write down the names of the peoples.

Beside a name he will write, “This one was born in Zion.”

Selah.

![A scroll for writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Look at the scroll**

Imagine a list of names on it. Each time God says someone was born in Zion, that person belongs to his city.

## Psalm 87:7

Singers and people playing pipes will say, “All my springs of joy are in you.”

**What are springs of joy?**

A spring is a place where water flows from the ground. The musicians sing about where their joy comes from.
