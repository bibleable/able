# Psalm 132 — A home for the ark

**About this psalm**

The singers remember a promise David made to the LORD. Then they remember the promise the LORD made to David about the kings who would come after him.

## David makes a promise

## Psalm 132:1

A song for going up to worship.

LORD, remember David and all the hardships he went through.

## Psalm 132:2

Remember the promise he made to you, the Mighty One of Jacob.

## Psalm 132:3

David said, “I will not go into my house.

I will not get into bed.

## Psalm 132:4

I will not close my eyes to sleep or even doze off

## Psalm 132:5

until I find a place for the LORD, a home for the Mighty One of Jacob.”

## The people come to worship

## Psalm 132:6

We heard that the ark was in Ephrathah.

We found it in the fields of Jaar.

**What is the ark?**

The ark was a sacred chest. It reminded Israel that the LORD was with his people.

## Psalm 132:7

Let us go to the LORD’s home.

Let us bow down at his footstool.

## Psalm 132:8

Rise, LORD, and come to your resting place.

May the ark, the sign of your strength, come with you.

## Psalm 132:9

May your priests wear goodness like a robe.

May your faithful people shout for joy.

## Psalm 132:10

Remember your servant David.

For his sake, welcome the king you chose.

## The LORD promises David a king

## Psalm 132:11

The LORD made David a promise he will never take back.

He said, “I will put one of your descendants on your throne.

## Psalm 132:12

Your sons must keep my covenant and follow what I teach them.

If they do, their sons too will sit on your throne forever.”

## Psalm 132:13

The LORD chose Zion.

He wanted to make his home there.

## The LORD chooses his home

## Psalm 132:14

He said, “This is my resting place forever.

I will live here because this is the home I want.

## Psalm 132:15

I will give this city plenty of food.

Its poor people will have enough bread to eat.

## Psalm 132:16

I will wrap its priests in my saving care like a robe.

Its faithful people will sing for joy.

## Psalm 132:17

Here I will make a horn grow for David.

I have prepared a lamp for the king I chose.

![A lamp that gives light.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Look at the lamp**

A strong horn pictures royal power. A lamp that keeps burning pictures David’s royal line continuing.

## Psalm 132:18

I will cover his enemies with shame like a cloak.

But his crown will shine.”
