# Psalm 58 — God judges the earth

**About this psalm**

David asks rulers whether they judge fairly. He sees them using their power to hurt people. He asks God to end their power to do harm.

## David questions the rulers

## Psalm 58:1

David wrote this song, called a Miktam, for the music leader.

Sing it to the tune “Do Not Destroy.”

You rulers, do you really speak up for what is right?

Do you judge people fairly?

**A word in the title**

Miktam is an old word for a kind of song. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Psalm 58:2

No. In your hearts you plan what is unfair.

With your own hands you hurt people across the land.

## The rulers refuse to listen

## Psalm 58:3

Those who do evil have turned away from God since birth.

Those who tell lies have gone astray from the start.

## Psalm 58:4

Their poison is like a snake’s poison.

They are like a cobra that shuts its ears.

## Psalm 58:5

It refuses to listen to the snake charmer.

Even his skillful tune has no effect.

## David asks God to stop the harm

## Psalm 58:6

God, break the teeth in their mouths!

LORD, tear out the lions’ sharp teeth!

**Why mention lions’ teeth?**

A lion can injure someone with its teeth. David pictures the rulers as lions and asks God to take away their power to hurt people.

## Psalm 58:7

Let these people disappear like water that runs away.

When they draw their bows, let their arrows lose their sharp points.

![A tipped cup spills water onto the ground.](../pictures/water-spilled-on-the-ground.svg)

**Look at the water**

Follow the water as it leaves the cup and spreads out. David wants the danger from these rulers to disappear like that water.

## Psalm 58:8

Let them be like a slug that seems to melt into its wet trail.

Like a child born without life, may they never see the sun.

## Psalm 58:9

Green or dry, thorn branches burn under your pots.

Before the pots even feel the heat, God will sweep the wicked away.

## People see that God judges fairly

## Psalm 58:10

Those who do right will rejoice when God acts against those who hurt them.

They will wash their feet in the blood of the wicked.

## Psalm 58:11

Then people will say, “God really does reward those who do right!

There really is a God who judges the earth!”

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what do the people learn when God puts a stop to the rulers’ violence?
