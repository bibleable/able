# Psalm 54 — God is my helper

**About this psalm**

People from Ziph tell Saul where David is hiding. David asks God to hear his prayer and save his life.

## David calls to God

## Psalm 54:1

David wrote this song, called a Maskil, for the music leader.

Play it on instruments with strings.

People from Ziph went to Saul and said, “Isn’t David hiding among us?”

God, save me by your name.

Use your power to show that I am in the right.

![A harp with strings stretched across its frame.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the harp**

Find the strings a player can pluck. David’s prayer was a song for people to sing with stringed instruments.

## Psalm 54:2

God, hear my prayer.

Listen to what I say.

## God keeps David alive

## Psalm 54:3

Strangers have turned against me.

Cruel men want to kill me.

They give no thought to God.

Selah.

**Words in the song**

Maskil is an old word for a kind of song. Selah appears after David describes the danger. Their exact meanings are uncertain.

## Psalm 54:4

God is the one who helps me.

The Lord keeps me alive.

## Psalm 54:5

God will repay my enemies for the harm they do.

God, you are faithful. Bring them to an end.

## David gives thanks

## Psalm 54:6

I will gladly bring you an offering of my own free will.

LORD, I will praise your name because it is good.

## Psalm 54:7

God has rescued me from every trouble.

With my own eyes I have seen the defeat of my enemies.
