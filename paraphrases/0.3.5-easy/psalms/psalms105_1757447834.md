# Psalm 105 — The LORD remembers his promise

**About this psalm**

The singer asks Israel to remember what God did for their families. The story follows Joseph into Egypt and tells how God later led his people out.

## Remember what the LORD has done

## Psalm 105:1

Thank the LORD and call on him.

Tell people in every nation what he has done.

## Psalm 105:2

Sing to him! Sing his praise!

Tell of all his wonders.

## Psalm 105:3

Take joy in his holy name.

Let everyone who seeks the LORD be glad.

## Psalm 105:4

Turn to the LORD for strength.

Keep seeking his presence.

## Psalm 105:5

Remember the wonders he has done.

Remember his mighty acts and the decisions he has announced.

## Psalm 105:6

This song is for you, the family of his servant Abraham.

You are the descendants of Jacob, the people God chose.

## God promises land to Abraham’s family

## Psalm 105:7

He is the LORD our God.

His decisions reach the whole earth.

## Psalm 105:8

He remembers his covenant forever.

He gave his word for a thousand generations.

## Psalm 105:9

He made that promise to Abraham.

He gave his solemn word to Isaac.

**What is a covenant?**

A covenant is a binding promise. Abraham’s son was Isaac, and Isaac’s son was Jacob. God gave his word to all three.

## Psalm 105:10

He confirmed his promise to Jacob.

It would stand forever for Israel.

## Psalm 105:11

God said, “I will give you the land of Canaan.

It will be yours to pass on to your children.”

## God protects a small family

## Psalm 105:12

At first there were very few of them.

They lived as strangers in that land.

## Psalm 105:13

They traveled from nation to nation.

They went from one kingdom to another.

## Psalm 105:14

God let no one mistreat them.

He warned kings for their sake.

## Psalm 105:15

God said, “Keep your hands off those I have chosen!

Do not harm my prophets!”

## Joseph goes ahead to Egypt

## Psalm 105:16

Then God sent a famine over the land.

He cut off the whole supply of food.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the grain**

People grind grain to make flour for bread. Imagine the fields empty at harvest time. A famine is a time when people cannot get enough food.

## Psalm 105:17

God sent Joseph ahead of his family.

People sold Joseph as a slave.

## Psalm 105:18

Iron chains bruised Joseph’s feet.

His captors put an iron collar around his neck.

## Psalm 105:19

Then what Joseph had said would happen came true.

The LORD’s word showed that Joseph was right.

## The king sets Joseph free

## Psalm 105:20

The king of Egypt gave the order to release Joseph.

The ruler set him free.

## Psalm 105:21

The king put Joseph in charge of his household.

Joseph looked after everything the king owned.

## Psalm 105:22

Joseph could instruct the king’s princes as he saw fit.

He taught the older leaders how to make wise decisions.

## Jacob’s family grows in Egypt

## Psalm 105:23

Then Jacob, also called Israel, went to Egypt.

He settled there, in the land of Ham.

**Where is the land of Ham?**

Here the song uses this name for Egypt.

## Psalm 105:24

The LORD gave his people many children.

They grew more numerous than their enemies.

## Psalm 105:25

God turned the Egyptians’ hearts against his people.

The Egyptians hated God’s servants and made plans to harm them.

## Psalm 105:26

God sent his servant Moses.

He also sent Aaron, the man he had chosen.

## Psalm 105:27

Moses and Aaron showed the Egyptians signs of God’s power.

They did wonders in the land of Ham.

## Signs in Egypt

## Psalm 105:28

God sent darkness over Egypt.

Even then the Egyptians refused to obey his words.

## Psalm 105:29

God turned their water to blood.

Their fish died.

## Psalm 105:30

Frogs filled the land.

They even entered the royal rooms.

## Psalm 105:31

God spoke, and insects came in swarms.

Tiny gnats spread across the whole country.

## Psalm 105:32

Instead of rain, God sent hail.

Lightning flashed across their land.

## Psalm 105:33

He struck their grapevines and fig trees.

He broke the trees throughout the country.

## The crops disappear

## Psalm 105:34

God spoke, and locusts came.

No one could count all the young locusts.

## Psalm 105:35

The locusts ate every plant in the land.

They ate everything growing in the fields.

## Psalm 105:36

Then God struck down all the firstborn in Egypt.

These were the first children born to their families, a sign of their strength.

## God leads his people out

## Psalm 105:37

God led Israel out with silver and gold.

No one in all their tribes stumbled.

## Psalm 105:38

The Egyptians were glad to see them go.

They had become afraid of the Israelites.

## Psalm 105:39

God spread a cloud over his people like a covering.

He gave them fire to light up the night.

## Psalm 105:40

They asked for food, and he brought birds called quail.

He gave them bread from heaven until they had enough.

## Psalm 105:41

He split open a rock, and water rushed out.

It flowed through the desert like a river.

## God keeps his word

## Psalm 105:42

God did this because he remembered his holy promise to his servant Abraham.

## Psalm 105:43

He led his people out with joy.

The people he had chosen shouted for joy.

## Psalm 105:44

He gave them lands where other nations had lived.

His people received what others had worked to grow.

## Psalm 105:45

God did this so his people would keep his rules and obey his laws.

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!
