# Psalm 66 — Come and hear what God has done

**About this psalm**

The people remember how God brought them through trouble. Then one singer tells how God heard a prayer.

## Everyone joins in praise

## Psalm 66:1

This song is for the music leader.

Everyone on earth, shout to God for joy!

## Psalm 66:2

Sing about how great God is.

Give him the praise he deserves.

## Psalm 66:3

Say to God, “What you do fills us with wonder!

Your power is so great that your enemies tremble before you.

## Psalm 66:4

Everyone on earth bows down to you.

They sing to praise you.

They sing to honor your name.”

Selah.

**A word in the song**

Selah is an old song word. Its exact meaning is uncertain.

## God made a way through water

## Psalm 66:5

Come and see what God has done.

What he does for people fills us with wonder.

## Psalm 66:6

God turned the sea into dry land.

Our people walked through the water on foot.

There we rejoiced because of God.

## Psalm 66:7

God is powerful and will always rule.

He watches the nations.

Those who fight against him must not boast.

Selah.

## God brought his people through trouble

## Psalm 66:8

People of every nation, praise our God.

Sing so others can hear you praise him.

## Psalm 66:9

God keeps us alive.

He keeps our feet from slipping.

## Psalm 66:10

God, you tested us.

You made us pure like silver.

**Why mention silver?**

A worker heats silver to remove what does not belong in it. The singers say God tested them like that.

## Psalm 66:11

You led us into a net.

You put heavy loads on our backs.

![A net with ropes crossing each other.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Trace the ropes with your finger. Think of something caught there. The singers remember trouble they could not escape on their own.

## Psalm 66:12

You let people ride over our heads.

We went through fire and water.

Then you brought us to a place with more than enough.

## The singer keeps a promise

## Psalm 66:13

I will enter your house with gifts to burn on the altar.

I will keep my promises to you.

**What is an altar?**

An altar is a place where people offered gifts to God. Here the singer promises to burn gifts there.

## Psalm 66:14

I made those promises out loud when I was in trouble.

## Psalm 66:15

I will offer well-fed animals to you.

Sweet-smelling smoke will rise as I offer male sheep.

I will also bring bulls and goats.

Selah.

## The singer tells how God listened

## Psalm 66:16

Come and listen, all who honor God.

I will tell you what he has done for me.

## Psalm 66:17

I called out to God for help.

I also spoke words of praise to him.

## Psalm 66:18

If I had held on to thoughts of doing wrong, the Lord would not have listened.

## Psalm 66:19

But God did hear me.

He listened to my prayer.

## Psalm 66:20

Praise God!

He listened when I prayed.

He keeps showing me his lasting love.
