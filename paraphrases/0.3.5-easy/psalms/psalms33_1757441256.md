# Psalm 33 — Sing a new song to the LORD

**About this psalm**

The singers invite people to make music together. They remember how the LORD made the world. At the end, they tell the LORD why they trust him.

## Sing and play for the LORD

## Psalm 33:1

You who do right, rejoice in the LORD!

It is good for honest people to praise him.

## Psalm 33:2

Praise the LORD by playing the harp.

Make music for him on ten strings.

## Psalm 33:3

Sing him a new song.

Play it well, and shout for joy!

![A harp with strings stretched across its frame.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Look at the harp**

Find the strings. Imagine hearing a player pluck them while the people sing together.

## The LORD speaks and creates

## Psalm 33:4

What the LORD says is right.

We can trust him in everything he does.

## Psalm 33:5

The LORD loves what is right and fair.

His faithful love fills the earth.

## Psalm 33:6

The LORD spoke and made the heavens.

With the breath of his mouth he made all the stars.

## Psalm 33:7

He gathers the sea into a great heap.

He holds its deep waters as if in storerooms.

**What are the storerooms?**

People use storerooms to hold their supplies. The singer pictures the LORD gathering even the deep waters and keeping them in place.

## Psalm 33:8

Let the whole earth stand in awe of the LORD.

Let everyone honor him.

## Psalm 33:9

He spoke, and the world came to be.

At his command, it stood firm.

## The LORD’s plans last

## Psalm 33:10

The LORD brings the plans of nations to nothing.

He stops what the peoples have planned.

## Psalm 33:11

The LORD’s own plans stand firm forever.

What he means to do holds true for every generation.

## Psalm 33:12

How blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD!

He chose these people to belong to him.

## The LORD sees everyone

## Psalm 33:13

The LORD looks down from heaven.

He sees every person.

## Psalm 33:14

From his home he watches everyone on earth.

## Psalm 33:15

He shapes each person’s heart.

He considers everything they do.

**Pause and answer**

Who can the LORD see from heaven?

## Where does help come from?

## Psalm 33:16

Even a great army cannot keep a king safe.

A soldier cannot rely on strength alone for rescue.

## Psalm 33:17

A warhorse gives false hope of rescue.

Even a horse that strong cannot save you.

## Psalm 33:18

The LORD watches over those who honor him.

They put their hope in his faithful love.

## Psalm 33:19

He rescues them from death.

He keeps them alive when food is scarce.

## The singers pray together

## Psalm 33:20

We wait for the LORD.

He helps us and protects us like a shield.

## Psalm 33:21

Our hearts are glad because of him.

We trust in his holy name.

## Psalm 33:22

LORD, let your faithful love rest on us.

We put our hope in you.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, why do the singers put their hope in the LORD?
