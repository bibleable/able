# Psalm 150 — Let everything that breathes praise the LORD

**About this psalm**

The last psalm fills the holy place and the heavens with music. Its final invitation reaches every living thing that breathes.

## Where and why to praise

## Psalm 150:1

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!

Praise God in his holy place.

Praise him in his mighty heavens.

## Psalm 150:2

Praise him for the powerful things he has done.

Praise him for his greatness beyond measure.

## Instruments join the song

## Psalm 150:3

Sound the horn to praise him.

Play the harp and the lyre to praise him.

![A harp and its strings.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

**Trace a string**

Imagine the sound of one string. Then imagine the horn, flutes, and ringing cymbals joining in.

## Psalm 150:4

Praise him with tambourines and dancing.

Praise him with strings and flutes.

## Psalm 150:5

Clash the cymbals to praise him!

Let the cymbals ring out loud!

**What are cymbals?**

Cymbals are metal plates. A musician strikes them together to make a ringing sound.

## Every breath

## Psalm 150:6

Let everything that breathes praise the LORD!

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD!
