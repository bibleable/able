# Psalm 125 — Mountains surround Jerusalem

**About this psalm**

The singer looks at the mountains around Jerusalem. They remind him that the LORD stays close to his people.

## A mountain that stands firm

## Psalm 125:1

A song for going up to worship.

People who trust the LORD are like Mount Zion.

It stands firm. It stays in place forever.

## Psalm 125:2

Mountains surround Jerusalem.

In the same way, the LORD surrounds his people now and forever.

![A high mountain with a path on its side.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Look at the mountain**

Imagine other mountains around a city. Whichever way you turn, a mountain is near. That is how the singer pictures the LORD’s care.

## A prayer for those who do right

## Psalm 125:3

Cruel rulers will not keep control of the land given to those who do right.

Then those who do right will not turn to doing wrong themselves.

## Psalm 125:4

LORD, do good to those who do good.

Show kindness to those whose hearts are honest.

## Psalm 125:5

The LORD will send away those who choose crooked ways, along with others who do evil.

May Israel have peace.
