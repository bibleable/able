# Hebrews 4 — God still offers his rest

**About this chapter**

The writer explains why God still calls people to enter his rest.

He reminds them that Jesus understands them when they need help.

## God still offers his rest

## Hebrews 4:1

God’s promise still stands.

He offers us a share in his rest.

Let us take care that none of you misses it.

## Hebrews 4:2

We heard good news just as our ancestors did.

The message did them no good.

They did not join those who heard it with faith.

## Hebrews 4:3

We who trust God enter his rest.

About the others God said, “In my anger I swore they would never enter my rest.”

Yet God finished his work when he made the world.

## Hebrews 4:4

Scripture tells us what happened on the seventh day.

God rested from all his work.

![A scroll open for reading](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Hebrews 4:5

Remember what God said about the people who disobeyed.

“They will never enter my rest.”

**Pause and answer**

What did God do on the seventh day?

## God calls this day “today”

## Hebrews 4:6

Some people can still enter God’s rest.

Those who first heard the good news disobeyed and missed it.

## Hebrews 4:7

Long afterward God spoke through David and named another day.

He called it “today.”

He said, “Today, if you hear God’s voice, do not close your hearts.”

## Hebrews 4:8

If Joshua had given the people that rest, God would never have needed to speak of another day.

## Hebrews 4:9

So a Sabbath rest still awaits God’s people.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is a day set apart for rest.

The writer uses that day to help readers understand the rest God promises.

## Hebrews 4:10

Anyone who enters God’s rest rests from their own work.

God himself rested from his work.

## Hebrews 4:11

Let us make every effort to enter that rest.

Take care that no one falls by disobeying as those people did.

**Pause and answer**

Through whom did God speak long after Joshua’s time?

## God knows our thoughts

## Hebrews 4:12

God’s word is alive and at work.

It cuts deeper than a sword with two sharp edges.

It reaches even between soul and spirit, between joints and the marrow inside bones.

It judges our thoughts and the reasons we act.

**Help with these words**

The sword is a picture of how deeply God’s words reach inside a person.

## Hebrews 4:13

God sees everything he made.

We can hide nothing from him.

He is the one we must answer to.

**Pause and answer**

What does God’s word show about the reasons we act?

## Come to God for help

## Hebrews 4:14

Jesus the Son of God is our great high priest.

He has passed through the heavens.

So hold firmly to the faith we openly declare.

## Hebrews 4:15

Jesus understands how it feels to be weak.

He faced temptation in every way we do.

Yet he never sinned.

## Hebrews 4:16

So we can come confidently to God’s throne of grace.

When we need help, God will show us mercy and give us grace.

**Help with these words**

A throne is a ruler’s seat.

Coming to God’s throne means coming to God himself.

Grace is the kindness God gives freely.

**Pause and answer**

Why can Jesus understand our weakness?
