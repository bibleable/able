# Hebrews 9 — Christ offered himself once for all

**About this chapter**

The writer describes the tent where Israel worshiped God.

He explains where Jesus went and what Jesus offered for us.

## Two rooms in the tent

## Hebrews 9:1

The first covenant had rules for worship.

It also had a holy place here on earth.

**Help with these words**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Hebrews 9:2

The people set up a tent for worship.

In the first room stood a lampstand and a table.

On the table was bread set apart for God.

They called this room the Holy Place.

## Hebrews 9:3

Behind the second curtain was another room.

They called it the Most Holy Place.

## Hebrews 9:4

There was a gold altar for burning incense and a chest covered with gold.

The chest held a gold jar of manna and Aaron’s walking stick, which had grown buds.

It also held the two stone tablets of the covenant.

**Help with these words**

Manna was the food God gave his people in the wilderness.

Incense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

## Hebrews 9:5

Above the chest were figures called cherubim of glory.

Their wings spread over the cover called the mercy seat.

We cannot explain all these things in detail now.

**Pause and answer**

What was the name of the room behind the second curtain?

## Where the priests could go

## Hebrews 9:6

Once everything was ready, the priests went into the first room regularly.

There they did their work for God.

## Hebrews 9:7

Only the high priest went into the second room.

He went once a year and always took blood.

He offered it for himself and for sins the people had committed without knowing it.

| Room | Who entered | How often |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holy Place | The priests | Regularly |
| Most Holy Place | The high priest alone | Once a year |

## Hebrews 9:8

Through this the Holy Spirit showed something important.

While the first tent still stood, the way into the Most Holy Place had not yet been shown.

## Hebrews 9:9

That tent teaches us something today.

People brought gifts and sacrifices.

Those offerings could not clear the conscience of the person who came to worship.

**Help with these words**

Your conscience is your awareness of right and wrong in what you do.

## Hebrews 9:10

The rules dealt with food, drink, and special washings.

They governed outward actions until God put things in a new order.

**Pause and answer**

Who could enter the second room once a year?

## What Christ offered

## Hebrews 9:11

Christ came as high priest of the good things we now have.

He went through a greater, more perfect tent.

No human hands made it.

It is outside this created world.

## Hebrews 9:12

Christ entered the Most Holy Place once for all time.

He brought his own blood instead of blood from goats and calves.

In this way he set us free forever.

## Hebrews 9:13

Under the old rules, some people needed cleansing before worship.

Priests sprinkled them with the blood of goats and bulls or the ashes of a young cow.

This made their bodies clean for worship.

## Hebrews 9:14

How much more can Christ’s blood do!

Through the eternal Spirit, Christ offered himself to God without any fault.

He clears our consciences of deeds that lead to death.

Now we can serve the living God.

**Pause and answer**

Whose blood did Christ bring into the Most Holy Place?

## A promise and an inheritance

## Hebrews 9:15

Christ brings God and people together in a new covenant.

He died to free people from the wrong they did under the first covenant.

Now those God calls can receive the inheritance he promised forever.

## Hebrews 9:16

Think about a person who makes a will.

Someone must show that the person who made it has died.

**Help with these words**

A will says who should receive a person’s belongings after that person dies.

## Hebrews 9:17

As long as the person lives, the will cannot take effect.

It takes effect after that person dies.

## Hebrews 9:18

Even the first covenant took effect through blood.

**Pause and answer**

When does a will take effect?

## Moses and the first covenant

## Hebrews 9:19

Moses told all the people every command in the law.

Then he took blood from calves and goats, water, red wool, and a plant called hyssop.

He sprinkled the scroll and all the people.

![A scroll open for reading](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Hebrews 9:20

Moses said, “This blood marks the covenant God has commanded you to keep.”

## Hebrews 9:21

Moses also sprinkled blood on the tent and all the things used for worship.

## Hebrews 9:22

The law called for blood to cleanse nearly everything.

Without the shedding of blood, there is no forgiveness.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses say the blood marked?

## Christ stands before God for us

## Hebrews 9:23

The things in the tent were copies of things in heaven.

The copies needed cleansing with those sacrifices.

The heavenly things themselves needed better sacrifices.

## Hebrews 9:24

Christ went beyond the copy of the holy place that people made.

He entered heaven itself.

Now he stands before God for us.

## Hebrews 9:25

The high priest went into the Most Holy Place each year with blood from an animal.

Christ did not enter heaven to offer himself again and again.

## Hebrews 9:26

If that were necessary, Christ would have suffered many times since the world began.

Instead, he came once for all at the end of the ages.

He gave himself as a sacrifice to take away sin.

## Hebrews 9:27

Each person must die once.

After death comes judgment.

## Hebrews 9:28

Christ too offered himself once to carry the sins of many.

He will come a second time.

He has already dealt with sin.

He will bring salvation to those who eagerly wait for him.

**Pause and answer**

Where does Christ now stand before God for us?
