# Hebrews 3 — Listen to God today

**About this chapter**

The writer compares the work of Jesus and Moses.

Then he asks readers to remember the people Moses led out of Egypt.

## Jesus and Moses in God’s house

## Hebrews 3:1

Brothers and sisters, God has set you apart.

You share a call from heaven.

Think carefully about Jesus.

We say openly that God sent Jesus and that Jesus is our high priest.

**Help with these words**

A high priest represents people before God.

## Hebrews 3:2

Jesus faithfully served God, who appointed him.

Moses too was faithful in all God’s house.

## Hebrews 3:3

Jesus deserves greater honor than Moses.

Think of a house.

The builder deserves more honor than the house.

## Hebrews 3:4

Every house has a builder.

God is the builder of everything.

## Hebrews 3:5

Moses faithfully served in all God’s house.

His service pointed ahead to what God would say later.

## Hebrews 3:6

Christ faithfully cares for God’s house as his Son.

We are God’s house if we keep our courage and hold firmly to the hope we speak of with joy.

| Moses | Christ |
| --- | --- |
| Serves faithfully in God’s house | Cares for God’s house as the Son |

**Help with these words**

Here God’s house means the people who belong to God.

**Pause and answer**

What was Moses’ role in God’s house?

## Listen to God today

## Hebrews 3:7

The Holy Spirit says, “Today, if you hear God’s voice,

## Hebrews 3:8

do not close your hearts to him.”

Remember the time the people rebelled against God in the wilderness.

**Help with these words**

Closing your heart means refusing to listen to God or trust him.

## Hebrews 3:9

God says, “Your ancestors put me to the test there.

For forty years they saw what I did.

## Hebrews 3:10

That is why I was angry with those people.

I said that their hearts kept turning away from me.

They did not know my ways.

## Hebrews 3:11

In my anger I made this solemn promise.

They would never enter my rest.”

**Pause and answer**

For how long did the people see God’s work in the wilderness?

## Encourage each other every day

## Hebrews 3:12

Brothers and sisters, watch out for each other.

A heart that refuses to trust can turn away from the living God.

Do not let that happen to any of you.

## Hebrews 3:13

Encourage each other every day while it is still called “today.”

Sin can fool people until they refuse to listen.

## Hebrews 3:14

We share in Christ if we hold firmly to the end the trust we had at first.

## Hebrews 3:15

Remember what God said.

“Today, if you hear God’s voice, do not close your hearts as you did when you rebelled.”

**Pause and answer**

How often should believers encourage one another?

## Why the people missed God’s rest

## Hebrews 3:16

Who heard God and still rebelled?

It was the people Moses led out of Egypt.

## Hebrews 3:17

Who made God angry for forty years?

It was the people who sinned and died in the wilderness.

## Hebrews 3:18

Who heard God swear that they would never enter his rest?

It was the people who disobeyed him.

## Hebrews 3:19

Now we see why they could not enter.

They did not trust God.

**Pause and answer**

Why could the people not enter God’s rest?
