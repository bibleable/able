# Hebrews 11 — People who trusted God

**About this chapter**

People trusted God before they could see what he promised.

Look for what each person did because they trusted him.

## What faith means

## Hebrews 11:1

Faith means trusting God.

Faith makes us sure of what we hope for.

We can be sure even of things we cannot see.

## Hebrews 11:2

Long ago, people trusted God.

That is why God spoke well of them.

## Hebrews 11:3

By faith we understand how the world began.

God spoke, and the whole universe took shape.

Everything we see came from what we cannot see.

**Pause and answer**

How did God form the universe?

## Abel, Enoch and Noah trust God

## Hebrews 11:4

Abel trusted God.

He offered God a better gift than Cain did.

God accepted Abel’s gifts and called Abel a righteous man.

That means Abel was right with God.

Abel died, but his faith still speaks to us.

His example still teaches us to trust God.

## Hebrews 11:5

Enoch trusted God.

Even before God took Enoch away, God said Enoch pleased him.

Then God took Enoch away without Enoch dying.

No one could find him.

## Hebrews 11:6

Only by trusting God can anyone please him.

Anyone coming to God must believe he exists.

They must believe he rewards those who truly seek him.

## Hebrews 11:7

God warned Noah about things Noah could not yet see.

Noah trusted God and took the warning seriously.

He built an ark, a large boat, to save his family.

His trust showed that the world was in the wrong.

God counted Noah as right with him because of his faith.

**Pause and answer**

What did Noah build when he heard God’s warning?

## Abraham sets out and Sarah trusts God

## Hebrews 11:8

God called Abraham to go to another land.

God would give him that land.

Abraham trusted God and obeyed.

He set out without knowing where he was going.

## Hebrews 11:9

Abraham trusted God and lived as a stranger in that land.

Abraham lived in tents.

So did Isaac and Jacob.

God’s promise was for them too.

## Hebrews 11:10

Abraham looked forward to a city with firm foundations.

God himself planned and built that city.

## Hebrews 11:11

Sarah had never been able to have children.

Now she was too old to have a baby.

Yet Sarah trusted God to keep his promise.

God made it possible for her to have a child.

## Hebrews 11:12

Abraham, too, seemed far too old to father children.

Yet from him came a family no one could count.

Think of the stars across the sky.

Think of the grains of sand along the shore.

That is how large his family grew.

**Picture the promise**

✦　✦　✦　✦　✦

Picture these few stars in a sky full of stars.

Abraham’s family would grow too large to count.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Sarah trust to keep his promise?

## A home to look forward to

## Hebrews 11:13

These people still trusted God when they died.

They had yet to receive what God had promised.

They welcomed it like people greeting someone far away.

They said they were strangers on earth, far from home.

## Hebrews 11:14

Their words show they wanted a country of their own.

## Hebrews 11:15

They could have gone back to the country they left.

## Hebrews 11:16

But they longed for a better country in heaven.

So God gladly lets them call him their God.

He has prepared a city for them.

**Pause and answer**

What kind of home did these people long for?

## One family looks ahead

## Hebrews 11:17

God tested Abraham, and Abraham trusted him.

Abraham brought Isaac to the altar as an offering.

An altar is a place for giving sacrifices to God.

Abraham had received God’s promises.

He was ready to give up his only son of promise.

## Hebrews 11:18

Yet God had told Abraham that the family he promised would come through Isaac.

## Hebrews 11:19

Abraham believed God could bring dead people back to life.

Isaac lived.

For Abraham, it was like receiving Isaac back from death.

## Hebrews 11:20

Isaac trusted God.

He spoke a blessing over Jacob and Esau.

He spoke about what lay ahead for them.

## Hebrews 11:21

Jacob trusted God as he lay dying.

He blessed each of Joseph’s sons.

Jacob leaned on his walking stick and worshiped God.

## Hebrews 11:22

Joseph trusted God as his death drew near.

He said the people of Israel would leave Egypt.

He told them what to do with his bones then.

**Pause and answer**

What did Abraham believe God could do?

## Moses chooses to stand with his people

## Hebrews 11:23

Moses’ parents trusted God.

When Moses was born, they saw a beautiful child.

They hid him for three months.

The king’s order did not frighten them.

## Hebrews 11:24

Moses grew up and trusted God.

Pharaoh was the king of Egypt.

Moses refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter.

## Hebrews 11:25

Moses chose to suffer with God’s people.

He knew the pleasure of sin would soon end.

## Hebrews 11:26

People shamed him for Christ’s sake.

Moses valued that more than all Egypt’s riches.

He looked ahead to his reward.

## Hebrews 11:27

Moses trusted God and left Egypt.

The king’s anger did not frighten him.

No one can see God.

Yet Moses kept going as if he could see him.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Moses value serving Christ more than Egypt’s riches?

## God brings people through danger

## Hebrews 11:28

Moses trusted God and kept the Passover.

At that first Passover, he had the people sprinkle blood on their doorways.

So the destroyer passed over the homes of Israel.

Their firstborn children lived.

## Hebrews 11:29

The people trusted God and crossed the Red Sea.

They walked as if on dry ground.

The Egyptians tried to follow them and drowned.

## Hebrews 11:30

The people trusted God at the city of Jericho.

They marched around its walls for seven days.

Then the walls fell.

## Hebrews 11:31

Rahab was a woman who sold sex.

She trusted God and welcomed Israel’s spies in peace.

Spies are people sent to gather information in secret.

Rahab lived when those who disobeyed God died.

**Pause and answer**

How did Rahab treat the spies?

## People trust God in battle and in suffering

## Hebrews 11:32

What more can I say?

I have too little time to tell every story.

Think of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David and Samuel.

Think also of the prophets, who spoke for God.

## Hebrews 11:33

These people trusted God.

Some defeated kingdoms.

Some judged people fairly.

Some received what God had promised.

Some shut the mouths of lions.

## Hebrews 11:34

Some put out fierce fires.

Some escaped people who came with swords.

God gave strength to people who had been weak.

Some fought bravely and drove away foreign armies.

## Hebrews 11:35

Some women received their loved ones back from death.

Others suffered torture and refused to accept release.

They hoped for something better when God raised them from death.

## Hebrews 11:36

People made fun of others and beat them with whips.

Some wore chains and went to prison.

## Hebrews 11:37

People killed some by throwing stones at them.

They sawed some in two and killed others with swords.

Others wore sheep or goat skins and had almost nothing.

People were cruel to them and made them suffer.

## Hebrews 11:38

The world did not deserve people like these.

They wandered through deserts and mountains.

They sheltered in caves and holes in the ground.

**Pause and answer**

Where did some people find shelter?

## God’s promise brings us together

## Hebrews 11:39

God spoke well of all these people because they trusted him.

Yet they did not receive what God had promised.

## Hebrews 11:40

God had planned something better for us.

His plan was to make them and us perfect together.

**Pause and answer**

Who will share in God’s plan with those people of faith?
