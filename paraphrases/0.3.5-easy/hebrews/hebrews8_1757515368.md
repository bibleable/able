# Hebrews 8 — God promises a new covenant

**About this chapter**

The writer compares the tent where priests served on earth with the holy place in heaven.

Then he recalls God’s promise to his people.

## The true place of worship

## Hebrews 8:1

Here is the main point.

We have a high priest like this.

Jesus sat down at the right hand of God’s royal throne in heaven.

## Hebrews 8:2

Jesus serves in the holy place, the true tent of worship.

The Lord himself set up that tent.

**Help with these words**

The Bible calls the tent set apart for worship the tabernacle.

## Hebrews 8:3

Every high priest has the task of offering gifts and sacrifices.

So Jesus too needed something to offer.

## Hebrews 8:4

If Jesus were on earth, he would not be a priest.

The priests who serve here already offer gifts as the law commands.

## Hebrews 8:5

Their place of worship is a copy and shadow of the one in heaven.

Before Moses built the tent, God warned him to follow the pattern.

God said, “Make everything the way I showed you on the mountain.”

![A mountain like the place where Moses saw the pattern](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Pause and answer**

Where did God show Moses the pattern for the tent?

## God promises a new covenant

## Hebrews 8:6

Jesus has received a far greater work.

He brings God and people together in a better covenant.

That covenant rests on better promises.

## Hebrews 8:7

If the first covenant had been without fault, there would have been no need for another.

## Hebrews 8:8

But God found fault with the people.

The Lord said, “A time is coming when I will make a new covenant with the people of Israel and Judah.”

## Hebrews 8:9

The Lord said, “It will be different from the covenant I made with their ancestors.

I took their ancestors by the hand and led them out of Egypt.

They broke my covenant, so I turned away from them.”

**Pause and answer**

With whom did God promise to make the new covenant?

## God writes on people’s hearts

## Hebrews 8:10

The Lord says, “After those days I will make this covenant with Israel.

I will put my laws in their minds.

I will write them on their hearts.

I will be their God, and they will be my people.”

**Help with these words**

Writing on people’s hearts is a picture.

God will help his people know his laws deep within them.

## Hebrews 8:11

The Lord says, “Nobody will need to tell a neighbor or family member to know me.

Everyone will know me, from the least to the greatest.

## Hebrews 8:12

I will forgive the wrong they have done.

I will no longer remember their sins.”

## Hebrews 8:13

By calling this covenant new, God has made the first one out of date.

What has grown old and out of date will soon disappear.

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise to do about his people’s sins?
