# Hebrews 6 — Hope holds us steady

**About this chapter**

The writer asks the readers to keep trusting God.

He reminds them how Abraham waited for God’s promise.

## Build on what you have learned

## Hebrews 6:1

Let us build on the first lessons about Christ and grow in faith.

We need not lay the foundation again.

We learned to turn from deeds that lead to death and to trust God.

**Help with these words**

A builder lays a foundation to support the rest of a building.

Here the foundation means the first lessons the believers learned.

## Hebrews 6:2

We learned about baptisms and laying hands on people.

We learned that the dead will rise and God’s judgment will last forever.

## Hebrews 6:3

With God’s permission, we will move forward.

**Pause and answer**

What did the believers first learn to do about deeds that lead to death?

## A serious warning

## Hebrews 6:4

Think about people who have already seen the light.

They have tasted the gift from heaven and shared in the Holy Spirit.

## Hebrews 6:5

They have experienced the goodness of God’s word.

They have felt the powers of the age to come.

## Hebrews 6:6

If they then turn away, it is impossible to bring them back to repentance.

By doing this, they crucify God’s Son all over again for themselves.

They hold him up to public shame.

**Help with these words**

Repentance means turning away from sin and back to God.

## Hebrews 6:7

Think of a field that often soaks up rain.

It grows a useful crop for the people who farm it.

God blesses that land.

## Hebrews 6:8

A field that grows only thorns and thistles is useless.

It is close to being cursed.

In the end it will be burned.

**Pause and answer**

What grows in the field that receives God’s blessing?

## God remembers your love

## Hebrews 6:9

Dear friends, we have given you a warning.

Yet we expect better things from you, the things that go with salvation.

## Hebrews 6:10

God is fair.

He remembers your work and your love for his name.

You showed that love by helping his people.

You are still helping them.

## Hebrews 6:11

We want each of you to keep showing that care to the very end.

Then you can be sure of what you hope for.

## Hebrews 6:12

Keep going instead of growing lazy.

Follow the people who trust God and wait patiently.

They receive what God promised.

**Pause and answer**

How have the believers shown their love for God?

## God keeps his promise to Abraham

## Hebrews 6:13

When God made a promise to Abraham, he swore by his own name.

There was no one greater whose name he could use.

## Hebrews 6:14

God said, “I will certainly bless you.

I will give you many descendants.”

## Hebrews 6:15

Abraham waited patiently.

Then he received what God had promised.

## Hebrews 6:16

People swear by someone greater than themselves.

Their oath confirms their word and puts an end to the argument.

**Help with these words**

An oath is a solemn way of giving your word.

## Hebrews 6:17

God wanted the people who would receive his promise to be sure of his purpose.

His purpose never changes.

So God confirmed his promise with an oath.

**Pause and answer**

How did Abraham wait for God’s promise?

## Hope holds us steady

## Hebrews 6:18

God’s promise and his oath cannot change.

God cannot lie about either.

We who have run to him for safety can take courage.

We can hold on to the hope he has set before us.

## Hebrews 6:19

This hope is like an anchor that holds us steady.

It is firm and secure.

It reaches behind the curtain into the most holy place.

![A rope fastened to an anchor](../pictures/anchor.svg)

**Help with these words**

An anchor keeps a boat from drifting away.

Here hope keeps a person steady.

The curtain marked off the most holy place where the high priest went before God.

## Hebrews 6:20

Jesus has already gone there ahead of us and for us.

He is a high priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.

**Pause and answer**

Who has gone ahead of us into the most holy place?
