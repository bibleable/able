# Hebrews 12 — Keep your eyes on Jesus

**About this chapter**

The writer pictures a runner who feels tired.

Jesus shows us how to keep going.

## Keep your eyes on Jesus

## Hebrews 12:1

Think of all those people who trusted God.

Picture them like a great crowd around a runner.

Let us lay down everything that slows us down.

Sin can trip us like a cord around our feet.

Let us keep running the race God has given us.

This means keeping on with a life of faith.

**Picture the race**

Heavy load　→　Lay it down　→　Run freely

Picture the runner laying down a heavy bag. What could you lay aside to follow Jesus?

## Hebrews 12:2

Keep looking to Jesus.

Jesus begins our faith and brings it to completion.

Jesus looked ahead to the joy waiting for him.

So he endured the cross, despite its shame.

Then he sat at God’s right hand, the place of honor.

## Hebrews 12:3

Think how Jesus endured hatred from people who sinned.

Remember him when you grow tired and feel like giving up.

**Pause and answer**

Who does the writer tell tired runners to look to?

## God corrects the children he loves

## Hebrews 12:4

You have struggled against sin.

So far that struggle has not cost you your blood.

## Hebrews 12:5

You have forgotten the words that encourage you as God’s children.

“My son, take the Lord’s correction seriously.

When the Lord tells you you are wrong, take heart.”

## Hebrews 12:6

The Lord corrects those he loves.

He disciplines every child he accepts as his own.

## Hebrews 12:7

When hardship comes, keep going and learn from God’s correction.

God is treating you as his children.

A father teaches and corrects his child.

## Hebrews 12:8

All God’s children receive this training.

Without it, you would be outside his family.

**Pause and answer**

How does God treat the people he corrects?

## What God’s training produces

## Hebrews 12:9

Our fathers on earth corrected us.

We respected them for it.

How much more should we obey the Father of our spirits and live!

## Hebrews 12:10

Our fathers trained us briefly as they thought best.

God trains us for our good.

He wants us to share his holiness.

This means becoming holy as he is holy.

## Hebrews 12:11

Correction hurts while it is happening.

Later, those who learn from it do what is right.

They also come to know peace.

It is like gathering good fruit after a long wait.

**Pause and answer**

What grows in people who learn from God’s correction?

## Help each other keep going

## Hebrews 12:12

So lift your tired hands.

Steady your weak knees.

Picture a tired runner finding strength to go on.

## Hebrews 12:13

Make a straight path for your feet.

Then someone who limps can heal without further injury.

Help each other keep going.

## Hebrews 12:14

Work to live in peace with everyone.

Seek to live a holy life.

Without holiness no one will see the Lord.

## Hebrews 12:15

Help each other receive God’s grace, his freely given kindness.

Watch for bitterness taking root.

Like a harmful plant, it can spread and spoil many lives.

## Hebrews 12:16

Take care that no one does wrong in their sexual life.

Take care that no one treats God’s gifts like Esau did.

Esau sold his rights as the oldest son for one meal.

## Hebrews 12:17

Later Esau wanted his father’s blessing.

He begged for it with tears.

But his father refused, and Esau could not undo what he had done.

**Pause and answer**

What did Esau give up for a meal?

## A mountain that filled people with fear

## Hebrews 12:18

Think of the mountain where God spoke to Israel.

It was a mountain people could touch.

Fire blazed there amid darkness and a violent storm.

You have come to a different place.

## Hebrews 12:19

Israel heard a trumpet blast.

Then the people heard a voice.

They begged to hear no more.

## Hebrews 12:20

They could hardly bear the command they heard.

Even an animal touching the mountain had to die by stoning.

Stoning meant throwing stones until the animal died.

## Hebrews 12:21

Even Moses said the sight made him shake with fear.

**Pause and answer**

How did Moses feel at the mountain?

## The city of the living God

## Hebrews 12:22

You have come to Mount Zion, the heavenly Jerusalem.

This is the city of the living God.

Countless angels have gathered there.

## Hebrews 12:23

They have gathered with joy.

God’s people gather there with the rights of firstborn children.

Their names are on record in heaven.

You have come to God, who judges everyone.

You have come to the spirits of people God has made perfectly right with him.

## Hebrews 12:24

You have come to Jesus.

Through him God makes a new covenant with his people.

A covenant is a binding agreement.

Jesus’ sprinkled blood speaks a better word than Abel’s blood.

Abel’s death cried out for justice.

Jesus’ death brings forgiveness.

**Pause and answer**

Who brings God and his people together in the new covenant?

## Listen when God speaks

## Hebrews 12:25

Listen to God when he speaks.

People refused his warning on earth and faced judgment.

How could we escape if we refused his warning from heaven?

## Hebrews 12:26

God’s voice shook the earth then.

God has promised to shake earth and heaven once more.

## Hebrews 12:27

“Once more” means God will remove the created things that can shake.

What cannot shake will remain.

## Hebrews 12:28

God is giving us a kingdom that will stand forever.

So let us thank God.

Let us worship him with deep respect and wonder.

This worship pleases him.

## Hebrews 12:29

Scripture calls our God a fire that burns things up.

The picture reminds us how seriously to take God’s judgment.

**Pause and answer**

Why can we thank God for his kingdom?
