# Hebrews 13 — Show love in everyday life

**About this chapter**

The writer gives practical ways to care for people.

He closes with a prayer and greetings.

## Show love in everyday life

## Hebrews 13:1

Keep loving each other as brothers and sisters.

## Hebrews 13:2

Welcome strangers into your home.

Some people who did this welcomed angels without knowing it.

## Hebrews 13:3

Remember people in prison.

Think how you would feel in chains beside them.

Remember people who suffer cruelty, as though you suffered with them.

**Picture the welcome**

🚪 Open door　→　🪑 A place to sit

Picture making room in your home for a guest.

## Hebrews 13:4

Everyone should honor marriage.

Married people should be faithful to each other.

God will judge those who have sex with someone else’s husband or wife.

He will also judge other sexual wrongdoing.

**Pause and answer**

How does the writer ask you to remember someone in prison?

## Trust the God who stays with you

## Hebrews 13:5

Keep the love of money out of your life.

Be content with what you have.

God has promised never to leave you or abandon you.

## Hebrews 13:6

So we can say with confidence, “The Lord helps me.

I will have no fear.

What can anyone do to me?”

## Hebrews 13:7

Remember the leaders who taught you God’s word.

Think about their lives and how those lives turned out.

Follow their example of trusting God.

## Hebrews 13:8

Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today.

He will stay the same forever.

**Pause and answer**

What promise helps you be content with what you have?

## Stand with Jesus

## Hebrews 13:9

Do not let strange teachings lead you away.

God’s grace gives your heart strength.

Grace is God’s freely given kindness.

Rules about eating certain foods gave their followers no real help.

## Hebrews 13:10

We have an altar of our own.

An altar is a place for offering a sacrifice.

Those who serve in the worship tent have no right to eat from our altar.

## Hebrews 13:11

The high priest takes animal blood into the Holy Place.

He offers it to God for sin.

People burn the animals’ bodies outside the camp.

## Hebrews 13:12

Jesus also suffered outside the city gate.

He gave his own blood to make the people holy.

## Hebrews 13:13

So let us go to Jesus outside the camp.

Let us stand with him even when people shame us as they shamed him.

## Hebrews 13:14

Here on earth we have no city that lasts forever.

We look forward to the city still to come.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus suffer?

## Praise God and share what you have

## Hebrews 13:15

Through Jesus, let us keep offering praise to God.

The writer calls our words a sacrifice.

We give God praise when we openly honor his name.

## Hebrews 13:16

Remember to do good and share what you have.

God is pleased with gifts like these.

## Hebrews 13:17

Obey your leaders and accept their guidance.

They watch over you and must answer to God for their care.

Help them do this with joy.

Making their work painful would do you no good.

**Pause and answer**

What could you share with someone else?

## A request and a prayer

## Hebrews 13:18

Pray for us.

We believe our consciences are clear.

We want to do what is right in every way.

## Hebrews 13:19

Please pray especially that I can return to you soon.

## Hebrews 13:20

God gives peace.

He brought our Lord Jesus back from death through the blood of the covenant that lasts forever.

Jesus is the great shepherd of the sheep.

A shepherd cares for sheep, and Jesus cares for us.

## Hebrews 13:21

May God give you everything good you need to obey him.

Through Jesus Christ, may God do in us what pleases him.

May Jesus receive honor forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

What does the writer ask God to give the readers?

## Greetings to the whole community

## Hebrews 13:22

Brothers and sisters, please listen patiently to my words of encouragement.

I have written you a short letter.

## Hebrews 13:23

Our brother Timothy is free now.

If Timothy comes soon, we will visit you together.

## Hebrews 13:24

Greet all your leaders and all God’s people.

Those from Italy send their greetings.

## Hebrews 13:25

May God show his grace to every one of you.

**Pause and answer**

Who might visit the readers with the writer?
