# Hebrews 2 — Jesus shares our human life

**About this chapter**

The writer asks us to pay attention to the message about Jesus.

Then he explains why Jesus shared our life and death.

## Pay attention to the message

## Hebrews 2:1

So we must listen closely to what we have heard.

Otherwise we may drift away from it.

![A boat on the water](../pictures/boat.svg)

**Help with these words**

Think of a boat slowly moving away from the shore.

The writer pictures what can happen when people stop listening.

## Hebrews 2:2

The message the angels brought had to be obeyed.

Everyone who broke that command received a fair punishment.

## Hebrews 2:3

How could we escape punishment if we ignored so great a rescue?

The Lord first told people how God would save them.

Those who heard the Lord confirmed his message to us.

## Hebrews 2:4

God also showed that the message was true.

He gave signs, wonders, and many kinds of miracles.

He gave gifts of the Holy Spirit as he chose.

**Pause and answer**

Who first announced this rescue?

## God cares for human beings

## Hebrews 2:5

We are speaking about the world to come.

God did not give angels authority over that world.

## Hebrews 2:6

Someone asked God in Scripture, “What is a human being that you remember him?

Why do you care for this child of man?

## Hebrews 2:7

You made him a little lower than the angels.

You crowned him with glory and honor.

## Hebrews 2:8

You put everything under his feet.”

God left nothing outside his authority.

Yet we do not see everything under his authority now.

**Help with these words**

Here, having everything under someone’s feet means having authority over it.

## Hebrews 2:9

But we do see Jesus.

For a time he took a place lower than the angels.

By God’s kindness, Jesus died for everyone.

Because he suffered death, he now wears a crown of glory and honor.

**Pause and answer**

For whom did Jesus die?

## Jesus calls us his family

## Hebrews 2:10

Everything exists through God and for God.

God wanted to bring many children into his glory.

So it was fitting for Jesus, who leads them to salvation, to be made perfect for that work through suffering.

## Hebrews 2:11

Jesus makes people holy.

He and the people he makes holy are one family.

Jesus gladly calls them his brothers and sisters.

## Hebrews 2:12

Jesus says to God, “I will tell my brothers and sisters about you.

When your people gather, I will sing your praise.”

## Hebrews 2:13

He also says, “I will trust God.”

And he says, “Here I am with the children God has given me.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus call the people he makes holy?

## Jesus shares our human life

## Hebrews 2:14

The children have bodies of flesh and blood.

So Jesus shared their human life.

By dying, Jesus could break the power of the devil, who held the power of death.

## Hebrews 2:15

Fear of death had held people like slaves all their lives.

Jesus came to set them free.

## Hebrews 2:16

The ones Jesus helps here are Abraham’s descendants, rather than angels.

## Hebrews 2:17

That is why Jesus had to become like his brothers and sisters in every way.

He could then serve God as their merciful and faithful high priest.

He could make atonement for the people’s sins.

**Help with these words**

A high priest represents the people before God.

Making atonement means dealing with the sins that keep people apart from God.

## Hebrews 2:18

Jesus himself suffered when he faced temptation.

So he can help people who face temptation now.

**Pause and answer**

Why can Jesus help someone who faces temptation?
