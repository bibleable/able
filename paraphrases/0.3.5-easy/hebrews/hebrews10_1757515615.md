# Hebrews 10 — Keep trusting God

**About this chapter**

Jesus gave his life so we can come close to God.

The writer asks us to keep trusting God together.

## What the sacrifices could do

## Hebrews 10:1

God’s law was like a shadow.

A shadow shows the shape of something coming.

The law pointed ahead to good things.

Each year people brought the same sacrifices to worship God.

Those sacrifices could never make the worshipers perfect.

## Hebrews 10:2

Suppose those sacrifices had washed away sin forever.

People would have stopped bringing them.

They would no longer have felt guilty about their sins.

## Hebrews 10:3

Instead, each year the sacrifices reminded people of their sins.

## Hebrews 10:4

The blood of bulls and goats cannot take away sins.

**Pause and answer**

What did the sacrifices remind people of each year?

## Jesus came to do what God wanted

## Hebrews 10:5

So when Christ came into the world, he told God, “You did not ask for sacrifices or gifts from me.

You prepared a body for me.

## Hebrews 10:6

“Animals burned on the altar did not please you.

Neither did offerings for sin.

## Hebrews 10:7

“Then I said, ‘Here I am, God.

The scroll tells about me.

I have come to do what you want.’”

**Help with this word**

A scroll is a long piece of writing rolled up for storage.

## Hebrews 10:8

First Christ said that sacrifices and gifts did not please God.

That included burnt offerings and offerings for sin.

People brought all these gifts because the law required them.

## Hebrews 10:9

Then Christ said, “I have come to do what you want.” Christ set aside the first way of offering sacrifices.

In its place came his own act of obedience.

## Hebrews 10:10

God wanted Jesus Christ to give his own body for us.

Jesus did this once for all time.

In this way God made us holy.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus offer to God?

## One sacrifice for all time

## Hebrews 10:11

Each priest stands and serves God every day.

Again and again the priest brings the same sacrifices.

These can never take away sins.

## Hebrews 10:12

Jesus offered one sacrifice for sins for all time.

Then Jesus sat down at God’s right hand.

This is the place of honor beside God.

## Hebrews 10:13

Now Jesus waits until God puts his enemies under his feet.

The picture is of a king resting his feet on a stool.

It means his enemies will come under his rule.

## Hebrews 10:14

By one offering Jesus has made people perfect forever.

These are the people God is making holy.

| Who? | What did they do? | How often? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The priests | Stood to offer sacrifices | Day after day |
| Jesus | Offered himself and sat down | Once for all time |

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus sit after offering his sacrifice?

## God promises to forgive

## Hebrews 10:15

The Holy Spirit also tells us this is true.

First the Spirit gives us these words from God.

## Hebrews 10:16

The Lord says, “After those days I will make a covenant with them.

I will put my laws in their hearts.

I will write them on their minds.” A covenant is a binding agreement.

God means his people will know his laws deep inside.

## Hebrews 10:17

Then God says, “I will no longer remember their sins or the ways they broke my law.”

## Hebrews 10:18

When God forgives sins, people need no more offerings for them.

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise about his people’s sins?

## Come close to God together

## Hebrews 10:19

Brothers and sisters, we can enter the Most Holy Place with confidence.

We can come before God because Jesus shed his blood.

## Hebrews 10:20

Jesus opened a new and living way for us.

The writer pictures Jesus’ body as a curtain.

Jesus gave his body so we could come through to God.

## Hebrews 10:21

Jesus is also the great priest over God’s household.

## Hebrews 10:22

So let us come close to God.

Let us mean what we say and trust him fully.

God has sprinkled our hearts clean from guilt.

Pure water has washed our bodies.

## Hebrews 10:23

Let us hold firmly to the hope we speak about.

God keeps his promises.

## Hebrews 10:24

Think about how to help each other show love.

How can we help each other do good?

![Encouragement can help someone show love and do good.](../pictures/encourage-love-and-good-work.svg)

Think of someone you can encourage today.

## Hebrews 10:25

Keep meeting together.

Some people have given up this habit.

Encourage each other, especially as you see the Lord’s day coming closer.

**Pause and answer**

What can you do for someone when you meet together?

## A warning about turning away

## Hebrews 10:26

Suppose we learn the truth and still choose to keep on sinning.

No other sacrifice for sins remains.

## Hebrews 10:27

All that remains is the fear of God’s judgment.

A fierce fire will burn up those who oppose God.

## Hebrews 10:28

Under Moses’ law, two or three witnesses had to give evidence.

Anyone they proved had rejected that law died without mercy.

## Hebrews 10:29

Think how much greater a punishment someone deserves for treating God’s Son like dirt underfoot.

That person treats the blood that made them holy as something common.

This is the blood that sealed the covenant.

That person insults the Spirit who shows us God’s kindness.

## Hebrews 10:30

We know who said, “I will repay people for the wrong they do.” God also said, “The Lord will judge his people.”

## Hebrews 10:31

It is frightening to face the living God when he judges.

**Pause and answer**

Who will judge his people?

## Remember how you helped

## Hebrews 10:32

Remember when you first saw the light.

That means when you first understood the truth.

You suffered greatly then, yet you stood firm.

## Hebrews 10:33

Sometimes people mocked you and hurt you in public.

At other times you stood beside people who suffered like that.

## Hebrews 10:34

You cared about people in prison.

People took away what you owned, yet you still had joy.

You knew you had something better that would last.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the readers care about in prison?

## Keep trusting God

## Hebrews 10:35

So keep your confidence in God.

A great reward lies ahead.

## Hebrews 10:36

You need to keep going.

Do what God wants.

Then you will receive what God promised.

## Hebrews 10:37

Scripture says, “In a little while, the one who is coming will arrive.

He will come on time.

## Hebrews 10:38

“The person who is right with me will live by trusting me.

If that person turns back, I will take no pleasure in them.”

## Hebrews 10:39

We belong to the people who keep trusting God.

Those who turn back face ruin.

Those who trust God save their lives.

**Pause and answer**

What does the writer urge the readers to keep doing?
