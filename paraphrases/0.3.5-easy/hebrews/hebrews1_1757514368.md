# Hebrews 1 — God speaks through his Son

**About this chapter**

The writer of Hebrews asks us to listen to God’s Son.

Words from Scripture show how the Son differs from angels.

## God speaks through his Son

## Hebrews 1:1

Long ago, God spoke to our ancestors through the prophets.

He spoke many times and in many ways.

**Help with these words**

Prophets spoke messages from God.

## Hebrews 1:2

Now, in these last days, God has spoken through his Son.

God chose his Son to inherit everything.

Through his Son, God made the whole universe.

## Hebrews 1:3

God’s glory shines from his Son.

The Son shows exactly what God is like.

His powerful word keeps everything in existence.

After cleansing people from sin, the Son sat at God’s right hand in heaven.

**Help with these words**

Sitting at a king’s right hand was a sign of honor and authority.

## Hebrews 1:4

The Son holds a place far above the angels.

The name he received is greater than theirs.

**Pause and answer**

Through whom has God spoken in these last days?

## Listen to what God calls his Son

## Hebrews 1:5

Did God ever say to an angel, “You are my Son.

Today I have become your Father”?

Did he ever say about an angel, “I will be his Father, and he will be my Son”?

## Hebrews 1:6

When God brings his firstborn Son into the world, he says, “Let all God’s angels worship him.”

## Hebrews 1:7

Scripture describes the angels this way.

“God makes his angels winds and his servants flames of fire.”

## Hebrews 1:8

But God says to the Son, “Your throne, O God, lasts forever.

You rule your kingdom with justice.”

## Hebrews 1:9

God says, “You love what is right and hate what is evil.

That is why God, your God, has poured the oil of joy on you.

He has honored you above your companions.”

**Help with these words**

People poured oil on someone to set him apart as king.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does God tell the angels to worship?

## The Son remains the same

## Hebrews 1:10

Scripture also says to the Son, “Lord, you laid the foundations of the earth in the beginning.

You made the heavens with your hands.

## Hebrews 1:11

The earth and heavens will pass away, but you will remain.

They will wear out like clothes.

## Hebrews 1:12

You will roll them up like a robe.

You will change them like clothes.

You remain the same, and your life never ends.”

## Hebrews 1:13

Did God ever say to an angel, “Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies a stool for your feet”?

**Help with these words**

The footstool pictures a king’s complete victory over his enemies.

## Hebrews 1:14

Angels are spirits who serve God.

God sends them to help the people he will save.

**Pause and answer**

Who remains when the earth and heavens wear out?
