# Hebrews 7 — Jesus remains a priest forever

**About this chapter**

The writer remembers a meeting between Abraham and a priest named Melchizedek.

He explains why the work of Jesus as a priest will never end.

## Abraham meets a king who is also a priest

## Hebrews 7:1

Melchizedek was king of Salem and priest of God Most High.

Abraham was returning from battle after killing the kings.

Melchizedek met Abraham and blessed him.

## Hebrews 7:2

Abraham gave Melchizedek a tenth of everything he had taken.

Melchizedek’s name means “king of righteousness.”

His title, “king of Salem,” means “king of peace.”

| Abraham | Melchizedek |
| --- | --- |
| Gave a tenth | Blessed Abraham |

**Help with these words**

A tenth is one part out of ten equal parts.

Righteousness means doing what is right in God’s sight.

## Hebrews 7:3

Scripture names no father, mother, or ancestors for Melchizedek.

It tells of neither his birth nor his death.

Like the Son of God, he remains a priest for all time.

## Hebrews 7:4

Think how great Melchizedek was.

Even Abraham, the father of our people, gave him a tenth of what he brought back.

**Pause and answer**

Who blessed Abraham after the battle?

## Who received the tenth?

## Hebrews 7:5

The law tells priests from Levi’s family to collect a tenth from their people.

These priests and the people who give are all descendants of Abraham.

## Hebrews 7:6

Melchizedek did not come from Levi’s family.

Yet he received a tenth from Abraham.

He blessed Abraham, the man who had received God’s promises.

## Hebrews 7:7

Everyone agrees that the greater person blesses the lesser one.

## Hebrews 7:8

Priests in Levi’s family collect a tenth, but they die.

Scripture speaks of Melchizedek as someone who lives on.

## Hebrews 7:9

In a sense Levi himself gave a tenth through Abraham, even though Levi’s family later collected it.

## Hebrews 7:10

Levi was not yet born when Abraham met Melchizedek.

Abraham was his ancestor.

**Pause and answer**

How was Levi related to Abraham?

## A priest from another tribe

## Hebrews 7:11

The people received the law with priests from Levi’s family.

If those priests could make everything perfect, why would another priest be needed?

The promised priest belongs to Melchizedek’s order instead of Aaron’s.

**Help with these words**

An order of priests is a kind of priesthood.

Aaron belonged to Levi’s family.

## Hebrews 7:12

A change in the priesthood requires a change in the law too.

## Hebrews 7:13

These words speak about Jesus.

He came from a different tribe.

No one from his tribe had served at the altar.

## Hebrews 7:14

Our Lord clearly came from Judah’s tribe.

Moses said nothing about priests coming from Judah.

## Hebrews 7:15

We can see the change more clearly when a priest like Melchizedek appears.

## Hebrews 7:16

Jesus became a priest through the power of a life that nothing can destroy.

No law about family descent gave him this place.

## Hebrews 7:17

Scripture says to him, “You are a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.”

**Pause and answer**

What gives Jesus the power to remain a priest?

## God gives his word

## Hebrews 7:18

The earlier rule about priests could not do what was needed.

So God set it aside.

## Hebrews 7:19

The law made nothing perfect.

Now we have a better hope.

Through that hope we can come near to God.

## Hebrews 7:20

God gave his solemn word about Jesus.

The other priests took up their work without such an oath.

## Hebrews 7:21

When Jesus became a priest, God made him this promise.

“The Lord has sworn and will never change his mind.

You are a priest forever.”

## Hebrews 7:22

Because God gave his word, Jesus guarantees a better covenant.

**Help with these words**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

A guarantee gives people a reason to trust that a promise will be kept.

**Pause and answer**

What did God promise Jesus about his work as a priest?

## Jesus always lives to speak for us

## Hebrews 7:23

There were many priests because each one died.

No one could keep serving after death.

## Hebrews 7:24

Jesus lives forever.

His work as priest never passes to someone else.

## Hebrews 7:25

Jesus can fully save everyone who comes to God through him.

He always lives to speak to God for them.

## Hebrews 7:26

Jesus is the high priest we need.

He is holy, innocent, and free from sin.

God set him apart from sinners and raised him above the heavens.

## Hebrews 7:27

Other high priests must offer sacrifices day after day.

They offer them first for their own sins and then for the people’s sins.

Jesus offered himself once for all time.

## Hebrews 7:28

Under the law, men with weaknesses become high priests.

Later God appointed his Son by a solemn promise.

The Son is perfect for this work forever.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus offer once for all time?
