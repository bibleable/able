# John 1 — The Word becomes human and people begin to follow Jesus

**About this chapter**

Jesus is called the Word.

John the Baptist tells people who Jesus is.

Andrew and Philip bring others to meet Jesus.

## The Word was with God

## John 1:1

In the beginning, the Word was already there.

The Word was with God.

The Word was God.

**Help with these words**

“The Word” is a name for Jesus.

Through Jesus, God makes himself known.

## John 1:2

The Word was with God in the beginning.

## John 1:3

God made everything through the Word.

Nothing came into being without him.

## John 1:4

Life was in him.

That life was light for all people.

## John 1:5

The light shines in the darkness.

The darkness has never put it out.

**Help with these words**

Light helps people see where to go.

Jesus is called light because he shows people God’s way.

**Pause and answer**

Who was with God in the beginning?

## John tells people about the light

## John 1:6

God sent a man named John.

**Help with these words**

This man is John the Baptist.

## John 1:7

John came to tell people about the light.

He wanted everyone who heard him to believe.

## John 1:8

John himself was not the light.

He came to tell people who the light was.

## John 1:9

The true light was coming into the world.

He gives light to everyone.

**Pause and answer**

What did God send John to tell people about?

## God gives people new life

## John 1:10

He was here in the world.

God had made the world through him.

Yet the people of the world did not recognise him.

## John 1:11

He came to his own people.

His own people did not welcome him.

## John 1:12

But some people welcomed him and trusted him.

He gave them the right to become God’s children.

## John 1:13

Their new life came from God.

It did not come from their family line or from anyone’s decision to have a child.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives people this new life?

## The Word lives among us

## John 1:14

The Word became human and lived among us.

We saw his glory.

It was the glory of the Father’s one and only Son.

He was full of grace and truth.

**Help with these words**

Glory means God’s greatness on display.

Grace is kindness God gives freely.

## John 1:15

John spoke out about him.

“This is the one I told you about!

He comes after me, yet he is greater than I am.

He was already there before me.”

## John 1:16

He is so full of grace that we have all received one gift of grace after another.

## John 1:17

God gave the law through Moses.

Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.

## John 1:18

No one has ever seen God.

The one and only Son is himself God.

He is at the Father’s side.

The Son has shown us what the Father is like.

**Pause and answer**

Who shows us what the Father is like?

## People ask John who he is

## John 1:19

Jewish leaders in Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to John.

They asked, “Who are you?”

Here is what John told them.

**Help with these words**

Levites helped with the work of worship in the temple.

## John 1:20

John answered openly, “I am not the Christ.”

## John 1:21

They asked, “Then who are you?

Are you Elijah?”

John said, “I am not.”

They asked, “Are you the Prophet?”

John answered, “No.”

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

Elijah was a prophet who spoke for God long ago.

The people were also waiting for a prophet God had promised.

## John 1:22

They said, “We need an answer for the people who sent us.

Who do you say you are?”

## John 1:23

John answered with words from the prophet Isaiah.

“I am a voice calling in the wilderness.

Make a straight path for the Lord!”

**Help with these words**

Preparing a path is a picture of getting ready to welcome the Lord.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did John tell the people to get ready for?

## John baptizes with water

## John 1:24

Some of the people who came were Pharisees.

## John 1:25

They asked, “You say you are not the Christ, Elijah, or the Prophet.

Why, then, do you baptize people?”

## John 1:26

John answered, “I baptize with water.

But someone you do not know is standing among you.

**Help with these words**

To baptize someone is to use water as a sign that they are turning to God.

## John 1:27

“He comes after me.

I am not worthy even to untie his sandal straps.”

## John 1:28

This happened at Bethany, across the Jordan River.

John was baptizing people there.

**Pause and answer**

What did John use to baptize people?

## John calls Jesus the Lamb of God

## John 1:29

The next day John saw Jesus coming towards him.

John said, “Look! The Lamb of God!

He takes away the sin of the world.

**Help with these words**

A lamb is a young sheep.

People offered lambs to God.

John calls Jesus the Lamb of God because Jesus gives his life to take away sin.

## John 1:30

“This is the man I told you about.

He comes after me, yet he is greater than I am.

He was already there before me.

## John 1:31

“I did not know who he was.

I came to baptize with water so the people of Israel could learn who he is.”

**Pause and answer**

What does John say the Lamb of God takes away?

## John sees the Spirit come down

## John 1:32

John told what he had seen.

“I saw the Spirit come down from heaven like a dove.

The Spirit stayed on Jesus.

## John 1:33

“I did not know who he was.

But God sent me to baptize with water.

God told me, ‘Watch for the man on whom the Spirit comes down and stays.

He is the one who will baptize with the Holy Spirit.’

## John 1:34

“I have seen this for myself.

I tell you that Jesus is the Son of God.”

**Pause and answer**

What did John see the Spirit do?

## Two followers go with Jesus

## John 1:35

The next day John stood there again with two of his followers.

## John 1:36

Jesus walked past.

John said, “Look! The Lamb of God!”

## John 1:37

The two followers heard John and followed Jesus.

## John 1:38

Jesus turned and saw them following him.

He asked, “What do you want?”

They said, “Rabbi, where are you staying?”

Rabbi means teacher.

## John 1:39

Jesus answered, “Come and see.”

They went and saw where he was staying.

It was about four in the afternoon.

They spent the rest of the day with him.

**Pause and answer**

What invitation did Jesus give the two followers?

## Andrew brings Simon to Jesus

## John 1:40

Andrew was one of those two followers.

He was Simon Peter’s brother.

## John 1:41

Andrew first found his brother Simon.

He said, “We have found the Messiah!”

Messiah means Christ.

## John 1:42

Andrew brought Simon to Jesus.

Jesus looked at him and said, “You are Simon, son of John.

People will call you Cephas.”

Cephas is another way to say Peter.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Andrew bring to Jesus?

## Philip invites Nathanael to come

## John 1:43

The next day Jesus decided to go to Galilee.

He found Philip and said, “Follow me.”

## John 1:44

Philip came from Bethsaida.

Andrew and Peter came from that town too.

## John 1:45

Philip found Nathanael.

He said, “We have found the one Moses wrote about in the law.

The prophets wrote about him too.

He is Jesus from Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”

## John 1:46

Nathanael asked, “Can anything good come from Nazareth?”

Philip answered, “Come and see.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Philip invite Nathanael to do?

## Jesus speaks with Nathanael

## John 1:47

Jesus saw Nathanael coming.

He said, “Here is an honest man from Israel.

He does not try to fool people.”

## John 1:48

Nathanael asked, “How do you know me?”

Jesus answered, “Before Philip called you, I saw you under the fig tree.”

## John 1:49

Nathanael answered, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God!

You are the King of Israel!”

## John 1:50

Jesus asked, “Do you believe because I said I saw you under the fig tree?

You will see greater things than that.”

## John 1:51

Then Jesus said, “I tell you the truth.

All of you will see heaven open.

You will see God’s angels going up and coming down on the Son of Man.”

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus see Nathanael before Philip called him?
