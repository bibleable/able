# John 4 — Jesus meets a woman at a well and helps a sick boy

**About this chapter**

A woman invites her neighbours to meet Jesus.

Later, a father comes to Jesus for his son.

## Jesus stops at a well

## John 4:1

The Pharisees had heard that Jesus was gaining and baptizing more followers than John the Baptist.

Jesus learned that they had heard this.

## John 4:2

Actually, Jesus’ followers did the baptizing.

## John 4:3

Jesus left Judea to go back to Galilee.

## John 4:4

His journey took him through Samaria.

## John 4:5

Jesus reached Sychar, a town in Samaria.

Nearby was the land Jacob had given his son Joseph.

## John 4:6

Jacob’s well was there.

Jesus was tired from walking and sat beside the well.

It was about noon.

**Help with these words**

A well is a deep hole where people can reach water underground.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus sit down to rest?

## Jesus asks for a drink

## John 4:7

A Samaritan woman came to draw water.

Jesus said to her, “Give me a drink.”

## John 4:8

His followers had gone into town to buy food.

## John 4:9

The woman said, “You are Jewish.

I am a Samaritan woman.

How can you ask me for a drink?”

Jewish people and Samaritans did not usually mix.

## John 4:10

Jesus answered, “If you knew what God gives and who is asking for a drink, you would ask me.

I would give you living water.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the woman to give him?

## The woman asks about living water

## John 4:11

The woman answered, “Sir, the well is deep.

You have nothing to draw water with.

Where will you get this living water?

## John 4:12

“Our ancestor Jacob gave us this well.

He drank from it.

So did his sons and his animals.

Are you greater than Jacob?”

## John 4:13

Jesus said, “People who drink this water will get thirsty again.

## John 4:14

“But anyone who drinks the water I give will never be thirsty again.

The water I give will become a spring inside them.

It will flow up into life that lasts forever.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls his gift “living water.”

A spring keeps giving water.

Jesus gives life with God that lasts forever.

## John 4:15

The woman said, “Sir, give me this water.

Then I will not get thirsty or have to keep coming here for water.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus offer when he speaks of living water?

## Jesus speaks about the woman’s family

## John 4:16

Jesus told her, “Go and call your husband.

Then come back.”

## John 4:17

The woman answered, “I have no husband.”

Jesus said, “You are right to say you have no husband.

## John 4:18

“You have had five husbands.

The man you are with now is not your husband.

You have told the truth.”

## John 4:19

The woman said, “Sir, I see you are a prophet.

**Help with these words**

A prophet speaks for God.

## John 4:20

“Our ancestors worshipped God on this mountain.

You Jewish people say we must worship in Jerusalem.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the woman say her ancestors worshipped?

## Jesus speaks about worship

## John 4:21

Jesus answered, “Believe me, a time is coming when you will worship the Father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem.

## John 4:22

“You Samaritans worship what you do not know.

We Jews know whom we worship.

God’s saving help comes through the Jewish people.

## John 4:23

“The time is coming, and it is already here.

True worshippers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth.

The Father looks for people who worship him this way.

## John 4:24

“God is Spirit.

Those who worship him must worship in spirit and in truth.”

## John 4:25

The woman said, “I know the Messiah is coming.

When he comes, he will explain everything to us.”

Messiah is another word for Christ.

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

## John 4:26

Jesus answered, “I am the Messiah.

I am the one speaking to you.”

**Pause and answer**

Who did Jesus say was speaking to the woman?

## The woman invites the town to meet Jesus

## John 4:27

Just then his followers returned.

They were surprised to find Jesus talking with a woman.

No one asked what he wanted from her.

No one asked why he was talking with her.

## John 4:28

The woman left her water jar and went back to town.

She spoke to the people there.

## John 4:29

“Come and see a man who told me everything I ever did.

Could he be the Christ?”

## John 4:30

The people left town and went to meet Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people do after the woman invited them?

## The followers bring food

## John 4:31

Meanwhile, the followers said to Jesus, “Rabbi, please eat something.”

**Help with these words**

Rabbi means teacher.

## John 4:32

Jesus told them, “I have food you know nothing about.”

## John 4:33

His followers asked each other, “Did someone bring him food?”

## John 4:34

Jesus explained, “My food is to do what God wants.

He sent me, and I am here to finish his work.

**Pause and answer**

What work did Jesus want to finish?

## One worker plants and another gathers

## John 4:35

“You say, ‘Four more months until harvest.’

But look up! Look at the fields.

The crops are ready to gather now.

**About the picture**

![A stalk with ripe grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Point to the grain the worker will gather.

## John 4:36

“The worker gathering the crop is already earning wages.

This worker gathers a crop for life that lasts forever.

The one who plants and the one who gathers can be glad together.

## John 4:37

“The saying is true here.

One person plants and another gathers.

## John 4:38

“I sent you to gather a crop you did not grow.

Other people did the hard work.

Now you share in their work.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus uses farm work as a picture.

People share the work of helping others come to trust him.

**Pause and answer**

Who can be glad together when the crop is ready?

## The Samaritans hear for themselves

## John 4:39

Many Samaritans in the town trusted Jesus because of what the woman said.

She had told them, “He told me everything I ever did.”

## John 4:40

When the Samaritans met Jesus, they asked him to stay.

Jesus stayed with them two days.

## John 4:41

Many more trusted Jesus after hearing his words.

## John 4:42

They told the woman, “At first we believed because of what you said.

Now we have heard Jesus for ourselves.

We know he really is the Saviour of the world.”

**Help with these words**

A saviour is someone who rescues people.

**Pause and answer**

What convinced more Samaritans to trust Jesus?

## Jesus returns to Galilee

## John 4:43

After two days Jesus left for Galilee.

## John 4:44

Jesus himself had said, “People do not honour a prophet in his own town.”

## John 4:45

When Jesus arrived in Galilee, people welcomed him.

They too had gone to the feast in Jerusalem.

They had seen what Jesus did there.

## John 4:46

Jesus went back to Cana in Galilee.

There he had turned water into wine.

A man who served the king had a sick son in Capernaum.

**Pause and answer**

In which town did the sick boy lie?

## A father asks Jesus for help

## John 4:47

The man heard that Jesus had come from Judea to Galilee.

His son was close to death.

He went to Jesus and begged him to come and heal his son.

## John 4:48

Jesus told him, “You people will not believe unless you see signs and wonders.”

## John 4:49

The man said, “Sir, come down before my child dies.”

## John 4:50

Jesus said, “Go. Your son will live.”

The man trusted what Jesus said and set off.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell the father about his son?

## The father hears good news

## John 4:51

On his way home, his servants came to meet him.

They told him, “Your son is alive!”

## John 4:52

The father asked, “When did he start to get better?”

They said, “His fever left him yesterday at about one in the afternoon.”

## John 4:53

The father remembered the time Jesus had said, “Your son will live.”

It was the same hour his son’s fever left.

The father and everyone in his household believed in Jesus.

## John 4:54

This was the second sign Jesus did after coming from Judea to Galilee.

**Pause and answer**

What happened at the same hour Jesus said the boy would live?
