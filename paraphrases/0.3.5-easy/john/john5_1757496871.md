# John 5 — Jesus heals a man and speaks about his Father

**About this chapter**

A man who has been ill for many years meets Jesus.

Later, Jesus tells the leaders how his work shows who sent him.

## People beside the pool

## John 5:1

Some time later, Jesus went up to Jerusalem for a Jewish feast.

## John 5:2

Near the Sheep Gate in Jerusalem there is a pool.

Its Hebrew name is Bethesda.

Five covered paths run beside the pool.

## John 5:3

Many people lay along those paths.

Some were sick or blind.

Some had trouble walking or could not move.

## John 5:4

**About this verse**

The BSB source keeps this verse number but has no verse text here.

**Pause and answer**

Where did people lie near the pool?

## Jesus tells a man to get up

## John 5:5

One man there had been ill for thirty-eight years.

## John 5:6

Jesus saw him lying there.

Jesus knew the man had been ill a long time.

He asked, “Do you want to get well?”

## John 5:7

The man answered, “Sir, when the water moves, I have no one to help me into the pool.

While I am trying to get there, someone else goes in first.”

## John 5:8

Jesus told him, “Get up.

Pick up your mat and walk.”

**About the picture**

Point to the rolled end.

## John 5:9

The man was well at once.

He picked up his mat and began to walk.

That day was the Sabbath.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

**Pause and answer**

What did the man do when Jesus made him well?

## The leaders ask about the mat

## John 5:10

Some Jewish leaders told the man, “Today is the Sabbath.

Our law does not allow you to carry your mat today.”

## John 5:11

The man answered, “The man who made me well told me to pick up my mat and walk.”

## John 5:12

They asked, “Who told you to pick it up and walk?”

## John 5:13

The man did not know who had healed him.

Jesus had slipped away in the crowd.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the man not know who had healed him?

## Jesus speaks about his Father’s work

## John 5:14

Later Jesus found the man at the temple.

He said, “Look, you are well now.

Stop sinning, or something worse may happen to you.”

## John 5:15

The man went back to the leaders.

He told them Jesus had made him well.

## John 5:16

The leaders began to mistreat Jesus because he did these things on the Sabbath.

## John 5:17

Jesus answered them, “My Father is still working today.

I am working too.”

## John 5:18

The leaders tried even harder to kill Jesus.

They said he was breaking the Sabbath.

He also called God his own Father.

This made him equal with God.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus call his Father?

## The Son does what the Father does

## John 5:19

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

The Son can do nothing on his own.

He does what he sees his Father doing.

Whatever the Father does, the Son does too.

**Help with these words**

When Jesus says “the Son,” he is speaking about himself.

## John 5:20

“The Father loves the Son.

He shows his Son everything he does.

He will show him even greater things.

You will be amazed.

## John 5:21

“The Father raises the dead and gives them life.

The Son also gives life to anyone he chooses.

## John 5:22

“The Father does not judge anyone himself.

He has given the Son the work of judging everyone.

## John 5:23

“So everyone should honour the Son just as they honour the Father.

Anyone who refuses to honour the Son also refuses to honour the Father who sent him.

**Pause and answer**

What does the Father show the Son?

## The dead will hear the Son’s voice

## John 5:24

“I tell you the truth.

Anyone who hears my words and trusts the Father who sent me has life that lasts forever.

That person will not come under judgment.

They have already passed from death to life.

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls this change passing from death to life.

The person who trusts God already has new life.

## John 5:25

“I tell you the truth.

The time is coming, and it is already here.

The dead will hear the voice of the Son of God.

Those who hear will live.

## John 5:26

“The Father has life in himself.

He has also given the Son life in himself.

## John 5:27

“The Father gave the Son the right to judge because he is the Son of Man.

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

**Pause and answer**

Whose voice will the dead hear?

## Jesus explains how he judges

## John 5:28

“Do not be surprised.

The time is coming when everyone in the graves will hear his voice.

## John 5:29

“They will come out of their graves.

Those who did good will rise to live.

Those who did evil will rise to face judgment.

## John 5:30

“I can do nothing on my own.

I judge by what I hear from the Father.

I judge fairly because I want what the Father who sent me wants.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Jesus say he judges fairly?

## John spoke about Jesus

## John 5:31

“If I alone speak for myself, my words do not establish my claim.

## John 5:32

“There is someone else who speaks for me.

I know that what he says about me is true.

## John 5:33

“You sent people to John the Baptist.

John told them the truth.

## John 5:34

“I do not depend on what people say about me.

I tell you these things so you can be saved.

## John 5:35

“John was like a burning lamp that gave light.

For a while you were glad to enjoy his light.

**Help with these words**

A lamp helps people see.

John helped people recognise Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus compare John to?

## Jesus points to the work his Father gave him

## John 5:36

“But what I do says more about me than John’s words.

My Father gave me work to finish.

The work you see me doing shows that my Father sent me.

## John 5:37

“The Father who sent me has himself spoken about me.

You have never heard his voice or seen him.

## John 5:38

“His word has no lasting place in you.

You do not trust the one he sent.

## John 5:39

“You study the Scriptures carefully.

You think they give you life that lasts forever.

Those very writings tell you about me.

**Help with these words**

The Scriptures are the holy writings.

## John 5:40

“Yet you refuse to come to me for life.

**Pause and answer**

Whom do the Scriptures tell the leaders about?

## Whose praise do the leaders want?

## John 5:41

“I do not accept praise from people.

## John 5:42

“I know you.

You do not have the love of God in you.

## John 5:43

“I came in my Father’s name.

You did not welcome me.

If someone comes in his own name, you will welcome him.

## John 5:44

“You accept praise from one another.

You do not look for praise from the only God.

How then can you believe?

**Pause and answer**

Whose praise did the leaders accept?

## Moses wrote about Jesus

## John 5:45

“Do not think I will accuse you before the Father.

Moses is the one who accuses you.

Yet Moses is the one you have put your hope in.

## John 5:46

“If you believed Moses, you would believe me.

Moses wrote about me.

## John 5:47

“You do not believe what Moses wrote.

How will you believe what I say?”
