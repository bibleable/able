# John 20 — Mary sees Jesus alive

**About this chapter**

Mary Magdalene finds the tomb open.

Later, Jesus visits his followers and speaks with Thomas.

## Mary finds the stone moved

## John 20:1

Early on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene went to the tomb.

It was still dark.

The stone no longer covered the entrance.

**About the picture**

![The tomb is open and the stone is to one side.](../pictures/tomb.svg)

Point to the stone Mary saw beside the entrance.

## John 20:2

Mary ran to Simon Peter and the follower Jesus loved.

She said, “They have taken the Lord out of the tomb!

We do not know where they put him.”

## John 20:3

Peter and the other follower set out for the tomb.

## John 20:4

They ran together, but the other follower ran faster.

He reached the tomb first.

## John 20:5

He bent down and saw the linen cloths inside.

He stayed outside.

**Pause and answer**

Who reached the tomb first?

## Peter goes inside

## John 20:6

Simon Peter came up behind him.

Peter went inside and saw the linen cloths.

## John 20:7

The cloth from Jesus’ head lay rolled up by itself.

It was separate from the other cloths.

## John 20:8

Then the follower who had arrived first went in too.

He saw and believed.

## John 20:9

They still did not understand what the Scriptures said.

Jesus had to rise from the dead.

## John 20:10

Then both followers went home.

**Pause and answer**

What lay separate from the other cloths?

## Mary looks inside the tomb

## John 20:11

Mary stood outside the tomb, crying.

As she cried, she bent down to look inside.

## John 20:12

Two angels in white sat where Jesus’ body had lain.

One sat where his head had been.

The other sat where his feet had been.

## John 20:13

They asked, “Woman, why are you crying?”

Mary answered, “They have taken my Lord away.

I do not know where they put him.”

**Pause and answer**

Why was Mary crying?

## Jesus calls Mary by name

## John 20:14

Mary turned and saw Jesus standing there.

She did not recognise him.

## John 20:15

Jesus asked, “Woman, why are you crying?

Who are you looking for?”

Mary thought he was the gardener.

She said, “Sir, if you moved him, tell me where.

I will go and get him.”

## John 20:16

Jesus said, “Mary.”

She turned to him and said in Hebrew, “Rabboni!”

Rabboni means teacher.

## John 20:17

Jesus said, “Do not hold on to me.

I have not yet gone up to the Father.

Go and tell my brothers that I am going up to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls his followers his brothers.

## John 20:18

Mary Magdalene went to the followers.

She said, “I have seen the Lord!”

She told them what Jesus had said.

**Pause and answer**

What did Mary tell the followers she had seen?

## Jesus visits the followers

## John 20:19

That evening the followers gathered behind locked doors.

They were afraid of the Jewish leaders.

Jesus came and stood among them.

He said, “Peace be with you.”

## John 20:20

Jesus showed them his hands and his side.

The followers were glad to see the Lord.

## John 20:21

Jesus said again, “Peace be with you.

My Father sent me, and now I am sending you.”

## John 20:22

Jesus breathed on them.

He said, “Receive the Holy Spirit.

## John 20:23

“If you forgive people their sins, their sins are forgiven.

If you withhold forgiveness, their sins remain unforgiven.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus tell the followers to receive?

## Thomas hears what happened

## John 20:24

Thomas, one of the twelve, was also called the Twin.

He was away when Jesus came.

## John 20:25

The other followers told him, “We have seen the Lord!”

Thomas said, “I must see the nail marks in his hands.

I must put my finger in those marks and my hand in his side.

Unless I do, I will never believe.”

**Pause and answer**

What marks did Thomas want to see?

## Jesus speaks to Thomas

## John 20:26

Eight days later the followers were inside again.

This time Thomas was there.

The doors were locked, but Jesus came and stood among them.

He said, “Peace be with you.”

## John 20:27

Jesus told Thomas, “Put your finger here.

Look at my hands.

Reach out and put your hand in my side.

Stop doubting and believe.”

## John 20:28

Thomas answered Jesus, “My Lord and my God!”

## John 20:29

Jesus said, “You believe because you have seen me.

God blesses those who trust me without seeing me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Thomas call Jesus?

## Why this book tells about the signs

## John 20:30

Jesus did many other signs while his followers watched.

This book tells of only some of them.

## John 20:31

These signs are written here so you may trust that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God.

By trusting him, you can have life in his name.

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.
