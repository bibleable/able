# John 15 — The vine, the branches, and Jesus’ friends

**About this chapter**

Jesus asks his friends to stay joined to him.

He tells them to love one another as he has loved them.

## The gardener cares for the vine

## John 15:1

Jesus told his followers, “I am the true vine.

My Father is the gardener who cares for the vines.

## John 15:2

“He cuts off every branch in me that grows no fruit.

He trims each branch that grows fruit.

Then it can grow even more fruit.

## John 15:3

“You are already clean because of the words I have spoken to you.

**Pause and answer**

What does the gardener do to help a branch grow more fruit?

## Stay joined to the vine

## John 15:4

“Stay joined to me, and I will stay joined to you.

A branch can grow fruit only if it stays joined to the vine.

You can grow fruit only if you stay joined to me.

**Help with these words**

Jesus uses a vine to describe life with him.

His followers depend on him as branches depend on the vine.

## John 15:5

“I am the vine.

You are the branches.

Stay joined to me, and I will stay joined to you.

Then you will grow much fruit.

Apart from me you can do nothing.

**About the picture**

![A branch grows from a thick vine and carries a bunch of grapes.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

Start at the thick vine on the left.

Follow its branch to the grapes.

## John 15:6

“Anyone who leaves me is like a branch thrown away.

It dries up.

People gather those branches and burn them in the fire.

## John 15:7

“Stay joined to me and keep my words within you.

Then ask for whatever you want, and you will receive it.

## John 15:8

“When you grow much fruit, you bring honour to my Father.

You show that you are my followers.

**Help with these words**

Fruit is a picture of what grows out of following Jesus.

Loving one another is one example Jesus gives.

**Pause and answer**

What must a branch stay joined to if it is to grow fruit?

## Stay in Jesus’ love

## John 15:9

“I have loved you as my Father has loved me.

Stay in my love.

## John 15:10

“If you obey my commands, you will stay in my love.

I obey my Father’s commands and stay in his love.

## John 15:11

“I have told you this so my joy will be in you.

Then your joy will be complete.

**Pause and answer**

Whose joy does Jesus want to share with his followers?

## Jesus calls his followers friends

## John 15:12

“Here is my command.

Love one another as I have loved you.

## John 15:13

“The greatest love anyone can show is to give up their life for their friends.

## John 15:14

“You are my friends if you do what I command.

## John 15:15

“I no longer call you servants.

A servant does not know what the master is doing.

I call you friends.

I have told you everything I learned from my Father.

## John 15:16

“You did not choose me.

I chose you to go and grow fruit that lasts.

Then my Father will give you whatever you ask in my name.

## John 15:17

“This is what I command you to do.

Love one another.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Jesus call his followers friends?

## Jesus prepares his friends for hatred

## John 15:18

“If the world hates you, remember that it hated me first.

**Help with these words**

Here “the world” means people who turn against Jesus.

## John 15:19

“If you belonged to the world, it would love you as its own.

But I chose you out of the world.

You no longer belong to it.

That is why the world hates you.

## John 15:20

“Remember what I told you.

‘No servant is greater than the master.’

If people mistreated me, they will mistreat you too.

If they obeyed my words, they will obey yours too.

## John 15:21

“They will treat you this way because you belong to me.

They do not know the one who sent me.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus ask his friends to remember when people hate them?

## People saw what Jesus did

## John 15:22

“If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not be guilty of this sin.

Now they have no excuse for their sin.

## John 15:23

“Anyone who hates me hates my Father too.

## John 15:24

“I did things among them that no one else had done.

Without those works, they would not be guilty of this sin.

But they saw what I did.

Still they hated both me and my Father.

## John 15:25

“So these words in their law came true.

‘They hated me for no reason.’

**Pause and answer**

What reason did the law give for the people’s hatred?

## The Helper and the followers will speak

## John 15:26

“I will send you the Helper from my Father.

He is the Spirit of truth who comes from the Father.

He will tell people about me.

**Help with these words**

The Helper is the Holy Spirit.

## John 15:27

“You too must tell people about me.

You have been with me from the beginning.”

**Pause and answer**

Why can the followers tell people about Jesus?
