# John 21 — Jesus shares breakfast and speaks with Peter

**About this chapter**

Jesus meets his followers beside the lake.

After breakfast, he asks Peter the same question three times.

## A night without fish

## John 21:1

Later Jesus showed himself again to his followers by the Sea of Tiberias.

This is how it happened.

**Help with these words**

The Sea of Tiberias is another name for the Sea of Galilee.

## John 21:2

Simon Peter was with Thomas the Twin and Nathanael from Cana in Galilee.

The sons of Zebedee and two other followers were there too.

## John 21:3

Peter said, “I am going fishing.”

The others said, “We will come with you.”

They got into the boat.

That night they caught nothing.

**Pause and answer**

What did the followers catch that night?

## Jesus calls from the shore

## John 21:4

Early in the morning Jesus stood on the shore.

The followers did not recognise him.

## John 21:5

Jesus called out, “Children, do you have any fish?”

They answered, “No.”

## John 21:6

Jesus said, “Throw your net on the right side of the boat.

You will find fish there.”

They did what he said.

There were too many fish to pull the net in.

## John 21:7

The follower Jesus loved told Peter, “It is the Lord!”

Peter had taken off his outer clothing.

When he heard it was the Lord, he put it back on and jumped into the lake.

## John 21:8

The other followers brought the boat in, dragging the net full of fish.

They were only about ninety metres from shore.

**Pause and answer**

What did the follower tell Peter when the net filled with fish?

## Breakfast with Jesus

## John 21:9

On shore they saw fish cooking on a charcoal fire.

There was bread too.

## John 21:10

Jesus told them, “Bring some of the fish you just caught.”

## John 21:11

Peter climbed into the boat and pulled the net ashore.

It held 153 large fish.

Even with so many fish, the net stayed whole.

**About the picture**

![A fishing net.](../pictures/net.svg)

Point to the net.

Peter used a net to bring the fish ashore.

## John 21:12

Jesus said, “Come and have breakfast.”

None of the followers dared to ask who he was.

They knew it was the Lord.

## John 21:13

Jesus took the bread and gave it to them.

Then he gave them fish.

## John 21:14

This was the third time Jesus appeared to his followers after he rose from the dead.

**Pause and answer**

What food did Jesus share with his followers?

## Jesus asks Peter three times

## John 21:15

After breakfast Jesus asked Peter, “Simon, son of John, do you love me more than these?”

Peter answered, “Yes, Lord. You know I love you.”

Jesus said, “Feed my lambs.”

## John 21:16

Jesus asked again, “Simon, son of John, do you love me?”

Peter answered, “Yes, Lord. You know I love you.”

Jesus said, “Care for my sheep.”

## John 21:17

For the third time Jesus asked, “Simon, son of John, do you love me?”

It hurt Peter that Jesus asked him three times.

Peter answered, “Lord, you know everything.

You know I love you.”

Jesus said, “Feed my sheep.

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls his followers his sheep.

He asks Peter to look after them as a shepherd looks after a flock.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus ask Peter to care for?

## Jesus tells Peter to follow him

## John 21:18

“I tell you the truth.

When you were young, you dressed yourself.

You walked wherever you wanted.

When you are old, you will stretch out your hands.

Someone else will dress you and lead you where you do not want to go.”

## John 21:19

Jesus was telling Peter about the death by which Peter would honour God.

Then Jesus told him, “Follow me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell Peter to do?

## Peter asks about another follower

## John 21:20

Peter turned and saw the follower Jesus loved walking behind them.

At supper this follower had leaned close to Jesus and asked, “Lord, who will hand you over?”

## John 21:21

Peter asked Jesus, “Lord, what about him?”

## John 21:22

Jesus answered, “Suppose I want him to stay until I return.

What does that matter to you?

You follow me.”

## John 21:23

Word spread among the followers that this man would never die.

But Jesus had only said, “Suppose I want him to stay until I return.

What does that matter to you?”

**Pause and answer**

What words did Jesus repeat to Peter when Peter asked about the other follower?

## The follower who wrote these things

## John 21:24

This is the follower who tells about these things.

He wrote them down.

We know that what he says is true.

## John 21:25

Jesus did many other things too.

Suppose someone wrote down every one of them.

I think the whole world would have no room for all the books!

**Pause and answer**

What did the follower do so others could hear what happened?
