# John 6 — Jesus feeds a crowd and speaks about the bread of life

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives a hungry crowd enough to eat.

Later, the crowd crosses the lake to find him.

## A crowd follows Jesus

## John 6:1

Jesus crossed the Sea of Galilee.

People also called this lake the Sea of Tiberias.

## John 6:2

A large crowd followed Jesus.

They had seen the signs he did when he healed sick people.

## John 6:3

Jesus went up the mountain.

He sat down with his followers.

## John 6:4

The Jewish feast of Passover was near.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the crowd follow Jesus?

## Philip and Andrew look for bread

## John 6:5

Jesus looked up and saw the crowd coming.

He asked Philip, “Where can we buy bread for all these people?”

## John 6:6

Jesus asked this to test Philip.

Jesus already knew what he would do.

## John 6:7

Philip answered, “Even two hundred silver coins would not buy enough bread for everyone to have a little.”

## John 6:8

Another follower spoke up.

It was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother.

## John 6:9

Andrew said, “Here is a boy with five barley loaves and two small fish.

How can this feed so many people?”

**Help with these words**

Barley is a grain people use to make bread.

**About the picture**

![Five loaves above two small fish.](../pictures/loaves-and-fish.svg)

Point to the five loaves.

Now find the two fish.

**Pause and answer**

What food did the boy have?

## Everyone has enough to eat

## John 6:10

Jesus said, “Tell the people to sit down.”

There was plenty of grass there.

About five thousand men sat down.

## John 6:11

Jesus took the bread and fish and thanked God.

He gave the people as much as they wanted.

## John 6:12

Everyone had enough to eat.

Jesus told his followers, “Gather the pieces left over.

Do not waste any.”

## John 6:13

They gathered what was left of the five barley loaves.

The pieces filled twelve baskets.

**Pause and answer**

How many baskets did the leftover bread fill?

## Jesus leaves the crowd

## John 6:14

The people saw the sign Jesus had done.

They said, “This really is the Prophet who was to come into the world!”

## John 6:15

Jesus knew they wanted to make him king by force.

So he went back up the mountain alone.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jesus go up the mountain alone?

## Jesus walks on the lake

## John 6:16

That evening his followers went down to the lake.

## John 6:17

They got into a boat and set off for Capernaum.

It was already dark.

Jesus had not yet joined them.

## John 6:18

A strong wind blew.

The water grew rough.

## John 6:19

They rowed about three or four miles.

Then they saw Jesus walking towards the boat on the water.

They were terrified.

## John 6:20

Jesus told them, “It is me.

Do not be afraid.”

## John 6:21

Then they were glad to take him into the boat.

At once the boat reached the shore they were heading for.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say when the people in the boat were afraid?

## The crowd looks for Jesus

## John 6:22

Next day the crowd was still across the lake.

They knew only one boat had been there.

The followers had left in it without Jesus.

## John 6:23

Some boats came from Tiberias.

They landed near the place where the Lord Jesus had thanked God and given the people bread.

## John 6:24

The crowd saw that Jesus and his followers had gone.

The people took those boats to Capernaum to look for Jesus.

## John 6:25

They found him across the lake.

They asked, “Rabbi, when did you get here?”

**Help with these words**

Rabbi means teacher.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the crowd go to look for Jesus?

## The people ask what God wants

## John 6:26

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

You came looking for me because you ate your fill of bread.

You did not come because you saw the signs.

## John 6:27

“Food can spoil.

Work for the food that lasts and gives life forever.

The Son of Man will give it to you.

God the Father has shown that he approves of him.”

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

## John 6:28

The people asked, “What work does God want us to do?”

## John 6:29

Jesus answered, “God wants you to trust the one he sent.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus ask the people to trust?

## The crowd asks for a sign

## John 6:30

They asked, “What sign will you do?

Let us see it so we can believe you.

## John 6:31

“Our ancestors ate manna in the wilderness.

The Scriptures say, ‘He gave them bread from heaven to eat.’”

**Help with these words**

Manna was the food God gave the people of Israel in the wilderness.

## John 6:32

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

Moses did not give you the bread from heaven.

My Father gives you the true bread from heaven.

## John 6:33

“God’s bread is the one who comes down from heaven and gives life to the world.”

## John 6:34

They said, “Sir, give us this bread always.”

**Pause and answer**

Who gives the true bread from heaven?

## Jesus calls himself the bread of life

## John 6:35

Jesus answered, “I am the bread of life.

Anyone who comes to me will never be hungry.

Anyone who trusts me will never be thirsty.

**Help with these words**

Bread keeps us alive.

Jesus calls himself the bread of life because he gives people life with God.

## John 6:36

“As I said, you have seen me.

Yet you still do not trust me.

## John 6:37

“Everyone the Father gives me will come to me.

I will never turn away anyone who comes to me.

## John 6:38

“I came down from heaven to do what the Father wants.

He sent me.

I did not come to do just what I want.

## John 6:39

“The Father wants me to keep everyone he has given me.

I must lose no one.

I will raise them from the dead on the last day.

## John 6:40

“My Father wants everyone who looks to the Son and trusts him to have life that lasts forever.

I will raise them on the last day.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus promise anyone who comes to him?

## People question where Jesus came from

## John 6:41

Some Jewish people there began to complain about Jesus.

He had said, “I am the bread that came down from heaven.”

## John 6:42

They said, “Isn’t this Jesus, Joseph’s son?

We know his father and mother.

How can he say he came down from heaven?”

## John 6:43

Jesus answered, “Stop complaining to one another.

## John 6:44

“The Father sent me.

No one can come to me unless the Father draws them to me.

I will raise them on the last day.

## John 6:45

“The prophets wrote, ‘God will teach them all.’

Everyone who listens to the Father and learns from him comes to me.

## John 6:46

“Only the one who comes from God has seen the Father.

No one else has seen him.

**Pause and answer**

Who teaches the people who come to Jesus?

## Jesus speaks about living bread

## John 6:47

“I tell you the truth.

Anyone who trusts me has life that lasts forever.

## John 6:48

“I am the bread of life.

## John 6:49

“Your ancestors ate manna in the wilderness.

Yet they died.

## John 6:50

“But here is the bread that comes down from heaven.

Anyone who eats it will not die.

## John 6:51

“I am the living bread that came down from heaven.

Anyone who eats this bread will live forever.

This bread is my flesh.

I will give it so the world may live.”

**Help with these words**

Here “my flesh” means Jesus’ own body.

Jesus says he will give himself so others can live.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say he will give so the world may live?

## The people ask what Jesus means

## John 6:52

The people there argued with each other.

They asked, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?”

## John 6:53

Jesus told them, “I tell you the truth.

Unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you have no life in you.

## John 6:54

“Anyone who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has life that lasts forever.

I will raise them on the last day.

## John 6:55

“My flesh is real food.

My blood is real drink.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus promise to do on the last day?

## Jesus speaks about sharing his life

## John 6:56

“Anyone who eats my flesh and drinks my blood stays in me.

I stay in them too.

**Help with these words**

Jesus describes a life shared with him.

He stays with those who receive him, and they depend on him for life.

## John 6:57

“The living Father sent me.

I live because of the Father.

In the same way, anyone who feeds on me will live because of me.

## John 6:58

“This is the bread that came down from heaven.

Your ancestors ate manna and died.

Anyone who eats this bread will live forever.”

## John 6:59

Jesus taught these things in the Jewish meeting house in Capernaum.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives Jesus life?

## Some followers find this hard to accept

## John 6:60

Many followers heard Jesus and said, “This teaching is hard.

Who can accept it?”

## John 6:61

Jesus knew his followers were complaining.

He asked, “Does this upset you?

## John 6:62

“What if you see the Son of Man go back up to where he was before?

## John 6:63

“The Spirit gives life.

Human flesh alone cannot give you this life.

The words I have spoken to you are spirit and life.

## John 6:64

“Yet some of you do not trust me.”

From the beginning Jesus knew who did not trust him.

He also knew who would hand him over.

## John 6:65

Jesus said, “That is why I told you that no one can come to me unless the Father makes it possible.”

**Pause and answer**

Who does Jesus say gives life?

## Peter answers Jesus

## John 6:66

After this, many followers turned back.

They no longer went with Jesus.

## John 6:67

Jesus asked his twelve closest followers, “Do you want to leave too?”

## John 6:68

Simon Peter answered, “Lord, who else could we go to?

Your words give life that lasts forever.

## John 6:69

“We trust you.

We know you are the Holy One of God.”

## John 6:70

Jesus answered, “I chose all twelve of you, didn’t I?

Yet one of you is a devil!”

## John 6:71

Jesus meant Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot.

Judas was one of the twelve.

Later he would hand Jesus over to his enemies.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Peter want to stay with Jesus?
