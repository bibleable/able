# John 12 — Mary honours Jesus and a crowd welcomes him as king

**About this chapter**

Mary pours perfume on Jesus’ feet in Bethany.

Later, Jesus teaches in Jerusalem about his coming death.

## Mary pours perfume on Jesus’ feet

## John 12:1

Six days before Passover, Jesus came to Bethany.

Lazarus lived there.

Jesus had raised Lazarus from the dead.

## John 12:2

People gave a dinner for Jesus.

Martha served the meal.

Lazarus was at the table with Jesus.

## John 12:3

Mary took about half a litre of expensive perfume made from pure nard.

She poured it on Jesus’ feet.

Then she wiped his feet with her hair.

The smell of the perfume filled the house.

**Help with these words**

Nard is a plant used to make perfume.

**About the picture**

![A perfume jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

Point to the opening where the perfume pours out.

**Pause and answer**

What did Mary use to wipe Jesus’ feet?

## Judas questions Mary’s gift

## John 12:4

Judas Iscariot was one of Jesus’ followers.

He would later hand Jesus over to his enemies.

Judas asked a question.

## John 12:5

“Why didn’t we sell this perfume for three hundred silver coins?

We could have given the money to poor people.”

## John 12:6

Judas said this because he was a thief.

He looked after the group’s money bag.

He used to steal from it.

He did not care about poor people.

## John 12:7

Jesus answered, “Leave Mary alone.

She kept this perfume to prepare for my burial.

## John 12:8

“You will always have poor people with you.

You will not always have me with you.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say Mary had kept the perfume for?

## People come to see Lazarus

## John 12:9

Many Jewish people heard Jesus was in Bethany.

They came to see both Jesus and Lazarus.

Jesus had brought Lazarus back to life.

## John 12:10

The chief priests planned to kill Lazarus too.

## John 12:11

Because of Lazarus, many people were leaving the chief priests and putting their trust in Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Why did people want to see Lazarus?

## A crowd welcomes Jesus as king

## John 12:12

The next day, people at the feast heard some news.

Jesus was coming to Jerusalem.

## John 12:13

They took palm branches and went out to meet him.

They shouted, “Hosanna!

God bless the one who comes in the Lord’s name!

God bless the King of Israel!”

**Help with these words**

Hosanna means “Save us, please!”

Here the people shout it to welcome their king.

## John 12:14

Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it.

The Scriptures had spoken about this.

## John 12:15

“People of Zion, do not be afraid.

Look! Your king is coming, riding a young donkey.”

**Help with these words**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people carry when they went to meet Jesus?

## People remember what Jesus did

## John 12:16

At first his followers did not understand.

Later, when Jesus received glory, they remembered what people had done for him.

They realised the Scriptures had described these things about Jesus.

## John 12:17

People kept telling others what they had seen.

They had seen Jesus call Lazarus out of the tomb and bring him back to life.

## John 12:18

The crowd had heard about this sign.

That was another reason they went to meet Jesus.

## John 12:19

The Pharisees said to each other, “Look! Our efforts have failed.

The whole world has gone after him!”

**Pause and answer**

What had the witnesses seen Jesus do for Lazarus?

## Visitors ask to see Jesus

## John 12:20

Some Greeks had come to worship God at the feast.

## John 12:21

They went to Philip, who came from Bethsaida in Galilee.

They said, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.”

## John 12:22

Philip told Andrew.

Together they took the request to Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Who took the visitors’ request to Jesus?

## One grain of wheat produces many grains

## John 12:23

Jesus answered, “The time has come for the Son of Man to receive glory.

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

## John 12:24

“I tell you the truth.

A grain of wheat stays a single seed unless it falls to the ground and dies.

But if it dies, it produces many grains.

**Help with these words**

Jesus uses the grain to explain his coming death.

One grain produces many more.

Through Jesus’ death, many people will receive life.

**About the picture**

![A stalk with many grains at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Find the grains that grew from one seed.

## John 12:25

“Anyone who loves their life above all else will lose it.

Anyone willing to give up their life in this world will keep it forever.

## John 12:26

“Anyone who serves me must follow me.

My servant will be where I am.

My Father will honour anyone who serves me.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to the grain after it falls into the ground and dies?

## A voice answers Jesus

## John 12:27

“Now I am deeply troubled.

Should I ask my Father to save me from this hour?

No. This is the very reason I came.

## John 12:28

“Father, bring honour to your name!”

A voice from heaven answered, “I have brought honour to my name.

I will do so again.”

## John 12:29

The crowd heard the sound.

Some said it was thunder.

Others said an angel had spoken to Jesus.

## John 12:30

Jesus answered, “You are the ones who needed to hear that voice.

**Pause and answer**

Who needed to hear the voice from heaven?

## Jesus speaks about being lifted up

## John 12:31

“Now is the time for God to judge this world.

Now the ruler of this world will be thrown out.

## John 12:32

“When people lift me up from the earth, I will draw everyone to myself.”

## John 12:33

Jesus was showing them how he would die.

**Help with these words**

People would lift Jesus up on a cross.

## John 12:34

The crowd answered, “The law says the Christ will live forever.

How can you say the Son of Man must be lifted up?

Who is this Son of Man?”

**Pause and answer**

What was Jesus describing when he spoke of being lifted up?

## Walk while you have the light

## John 12:35

Jesus said, “The light will be with you a little longer.

Walk while you have the light, before darkness overtakes you.

Someone walking in darkness cannot see where they are going.

## John 12:36

“Trust the light while you have it.

Then you can become children of light.”

After speaking, Jesus went away, out of their sight.

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls himself the light because he shows people God’s way.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask people to do while they had the light?

## Isaiah’s words come true

## John 12:37

Jesus had done many signs before their eyes.

Yet they still did not trust him.

## John 12:38

So the words of the prophet Isaiah came true.

“Lord, who has believed our message?

Who has seen the power of the Lord’s arm?”

## John 12:39

Isaiah also explained why they could not believe.

## John 12:40

“He has blinded their eyes and made their hearts hard.

So they cannot see or understand.

If they turned back to me, I would heal them.”

**Help with these words**

The Lord’s arm is a picture of God’s power.

A hard heart describes someone who will not listen to God.

## John 12:41

Isaiah spoke about Jesus because he had seen his glory.

**Pause and answer**

Whose glory did Isaiah see?

## Some leaders are afraid to speak

## John 12:42

Even so, many leaders trusted Jesus.

They kept quiet because they feared the Pharisees would put them out of the synagogue.

## John 12:43

They wanted praise from people more than praise from God.

## John 12:44

Jesus called out, “Anyone who trusts me also trusts the one who sent me.

## John 12:45

“Anyone who sees me sees the one who sent me.

## John 12:46

“I came into the world as light.

Anyone who trusts me can leave the darkness.

**Pause and answer**

Why did some leaders keep quiet about trusting Jesus?

## Jesus speaks the Father’s words

## John 12:47

“If people hear my words and do not obey, I do not judge them.

I came to save the world.

## John 12:48

“But anyone who rejects me and my words has a judge.

The words I have spoken will judge that person on the last day.

## John 12:49

“I did not speak on my own.

The Father who sent me told me what to say and how to say it.

## John 12:50

“I know his command leads to life that lasts forever.

So I say exactly what my Father told me to say.”

**Pause and answer**

Who told Jesus what to say?
