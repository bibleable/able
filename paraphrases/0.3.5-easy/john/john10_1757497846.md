# John 10 — Jesus calls himself the good shepherd

**About this chapter**

A shepherd calls his sheep by name.

Jesus uses this picture to explain how he cares for his followers.

## The sheep know their shepherd’s voice

## John 10:1

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth.

A thief climbs into the sheep pen some other way.

He does not use the gate.

## John 10:2

“The shepherd comes in through the gate.

**Help with these words**

A shepherd looks after sheep.

A sheep pen is an enclosed place where sheep can stay.

## John 10:3

“The gatekeeper opens it for the shepherd.

The sheep listen to the shepherd’s voice.

He calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.

**About the picture**

![A fenced sheep pen with its gate open.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

Find the open gate where the shepherd leads the sheep out.

## John 10:4

“Once all his sheep are out, he walks ahead.

They follow because they know his voice.

## John 10:5

“They will run away from a stranger.

They will not follow someone whose voice they do not know.”

## John 10:6

Jesus used this picture as he spoke to the people.

They did not understand what he meant.

**Pause and answer**

Why do the sheep follow their shepherd?

## Jesus calls himself the gate

## John 10:7

So Jesus spoke again.

“I tell you the truth.

I am the gate for the sheep.

## John 10:8

“All who came before me were thieves and robbers.

But the sheep did not listen to them.

## John 10:9

“I am the gate.

Anyone who enters through me will be saved.

They will come in and go out.

They will find grass to eat.

**Help with these words**

A gate is the way into the pen.

Jesus calls himself the gate because people come to him to be saved.

## John 10:10

“A thief comes only to steal, kill, and destroy.

I came to give the sheep life in all its fullness.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say he came to give the sheep?

## The good shepherd stays with his sheep

## John 10:11

“I am the good shepherd.

The good shepherd gives his life for the sheep.

## John 10:12

“A hired worker sees a wolf coming.

The sheep do not belong to him.

He leaves the sheep and runs away.

The wolf attacks, and the sheep scatter.

## John 10:13

“The worker runs because he only works for pay.

He does not care about the sheep.

## John 10:14

“I am the good shepherd.

I know my sheep, and my sheep know me.

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls his followers his sheep.

He knows and cares for them as a shepherd cares for his sheep.

## John 10:15

“It is like the way my Father knows me and I know my Father.

I give my life for the sheep.

**Pause and answer**

What does the hired worker do when a wolf comes?

## One flock and one shepherd

## John 10:16

“I have other sheep outside this pen.

I must bring them too.

They will listen to my voice.

Then there will be one flock with one shepherd.

## John 10:17

“My Father loves me because I give up my life so I can take it up again.

## John 10:18

“No one takes my life from me.

I choose to give it up.

I have the right to give up my life.

I have the right to take it up again.

This is what my Father commanded me to do.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus is speaking about dying and coming back to life.

**Pause and answer**

Who chooses to give up his life?

## The listeners disagree

## John 10:19

The Jewish people listening to Jesus disagreed again about his words.

## John 10:20

Many said, “He has a demon and is out of his mind.

Why listen to him?”

## John 10:21

Others answered, “Someone with a demon would not speak like this.

Can a demon give sight to a blind person?”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people who defended Jesus say about giving sight?

## People question Jesus at the temple

## John 10:22

It was winter in Jerusalem.

The people were celebrating the Feast of Dedication.

**Help with these words**

At this feast, people remembered when the temple was set apart again for worship.

## John 10:23

Jesus walked through the temple courts.

He was on the covered walkway called Solomon’s Colonnade.

## John 10:24

Some Jewish people gathered around Jesus.

They asked, “How long will you keep us wondering?

If you are the Christ, tell us plainly.”

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

## John 10:25

Jesus answered, “I told you, but you did not believe me.

The things I do in my Father’s name show who I am.

## John 10:26

“You refuse to believe because you are not my sheep.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say shows who he is?

## No one can take the sheep away

## John 10:27

“My sheep listen to my voice.

I know them, and they follow me.

## John 10:28

“I give them life that lasts forever.

They will never perish.

No one can pull them out of my hand.

## John 10:29

“My Father gave them to me.

He is greater than all.

No one can pull them out of my Father’s hand.

**Help with these words**

Being in someone’s hand is a picture of being in their care.

## John 10:30

“My Father and I are one.”

**Pause and answer**

Who gave the sheep to Jesus?

## People accuse Jesus

## John 10:31

The people arguing with Jesus again picked up stones to throw at him.

## John 10:32

Jesus asked, “I have shown you many good works from my Father.

For which of them are you throwing stones at me?”

## John 10:33

They answered, “It is not for any good work.

It is because you speak against God.

You are a man, yet you claim to be God.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people say they wanted to stone Jesus?

## Jesus asks them to consider his work

## John 10:34

Jesus answered, “Your law records these words of God.

‘I have said you are gods.’

## John 10:35

“God called the people who received his words ‘gods.’

What the Scriptures say always stands.

## John 10:36

“Now think about the one the Father set apart and sent into the world.

I said, ‘I am the Son of God.’

Why do you say those words are an insult to God?

## John 10:37

“Do not believe me if I do not do my Father’s work.

## John 10:38

“But suppose I do his work and you still do not believe me.

Trust what the work itself shows you.

Then you can know and understand that the Father is in me and I am in the Father.”

## John 10:39

Again they tried to seize Jesus.

But he got away from them.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the people to look at if they doubted his words?

## Many trust Jesus across the Jordan

## John 10:40

Jesus went back across the Jordan River.

He stayed where John the Baptist had first baptized people.

## John 10:41

Many people came to Jesus there.

They said, “John never did a miraculous sign.

But everything John said about Jesus was true.”

## John 10:42

Many people there put their trust in Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people remember about John’s words?
