# John 13 — Jesus washes his followers’ feet

**About this chapter**

Jesus gets up from supper to wash his followers’ feet.

Later he tells them to love one another as he has loved them.

## Jesus takes a towel and a basin

## John 13:1

It was just before the Passover feast.

Jesus knew it was time to leave this world and return to his Father.

He loved his followers in this world to the very end.

## John 13:2

The evening meal had begun.

The devil had already given Judas the idea of handing Jesus over to his enemies.

Judas was the son of Simon Iscariot.

## John 13:3

Jesus knew his Father had put everything in his care.

He knew he had come from God.

Now he was returning to God.

## John 13:4

Jesus got up from the meal.

He took off his outer clothes.

He wrapped a towel around his waist.

## John 13:5

He poured water into a basin.

He began washing his followers’ feet.

He dried them with the towel around his waist.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus use to dry his followers’ feet?

## Peter asks Jesus about washing

## John 13:6

When Jesus came to Simon Peter, Peter asked, “Lord, are you going to wash my feet?”

## John 13:7

Jesus answered, “You do not understand what I am doing now.

Later you will understand.”

## John 13:8

Peter said, “You will never wash my feet!”

Jesus answered, “Unless I wash you, you have no part with me.”

## John 13:9

Simon Peter said, “Lord, then wash my hands and my head too!”

## John 13:10

Jesus told Peter, “Someone who has had a bath needs only to wash their feet.

Then their whole body is clean.

You are clean, but not all of you.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus speaks about washing feet and about being clean inside.

His next words point to the person who will turn against him.

## John 13:11

Jesus knew who would hand him over to his enemies.

That is why he said they were not all clean.

**Pause and answer**

What else did Peter ask Jesus to wash?

## Jesus shows his followers how to serve

## John 13:12

Jesus finished washing their feet.

He put on his outer clothes and returned to the table.

He asked, “Do you understand what I have done for you?

## John 13:13

“You call me Teacher and Lord.

You are right, because that is who I am.

## John 13:14

“I am your Lord and Teacher, yet I washed your feet.

You should wash one another’s feet too.

## John 13:15

“I have shown you what to do.

Do for one another what I have done for you.

## John 13:16

“I tell you the truth.

No servant is greater than the master.

No messenger is greater than the person who sent them.

## John 13:17

“Now you know these things.

God will bless you when you do them.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell his followers to do for one another?

## Someone at the meal will turn against Jesus

## John 13:18

“I do not mean all of you.

I know the people I chose.

But these words in the Scriptures must come true.

‘The one who shares my bread has turned against me.’

**Help with these words**

Sharing bread means eating a meal together.

The person Jesus speaks of is at the table with him.

## John 13:19

“I am telling you before it happens.

When it happens, you will believe that I am he.

## John 13:20

“I tell you the truth.

Whoever welcomes someone I send welcomes me.

Whoever welcomes me welcomes the one who sent me.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom do people welcome when they welcome someone Jesus sends?

## Peter signals to the follower beside Jesus

## John 13:21

Jesus was deeply troubled.

He said, “I tell you the truth.

One of you will hand me over to my enemies.”

## John 13:22

The followers looked at each other.

They did not know whom Jesus meant.

## John 13:23

The follower Jesus loved was beside him at the table.

## John 13:24

Simon Peter made a sign to that follower.

Peter wanted him to ask whom Jesus meant.

## John 13:25

The follower leaned back against Jesus.

He asked, “Lord, who is it?”

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter want the follower beside Jesus to ask?

## Jesus gives Judas a piece of bread

## John 13:26

Jesus answered, “Watch whom I give this piece of bread after I dip it.”

Jesus dipped the bread.

He gave it to Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot.

## John 13:27

When Judas took the bread, Satan entered him.

Jesus told Judas, “Do quickly what you are about to do.”

## John 13:28

No one at the table understood why Jesus said this to Judas.

## John 13:29

Judas kept the money bag.

Some thought Jesus wanted him to buy things for the feast.

Or perhaps Jesus wanted him to give something to poor people.

## John 13:30

As soon as Judas took the bread, he went out.

It was night.

**Pause and answer**

Who went out into the night?

## Jesus tells his followers to love one another

## John 13:31

After Judas left, Jesus said, “Now the glory of the Son of Man is on display.

Through him, people can see God’s glory too.

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

Glory means the greatness and honour that belong to God.

## John 13:32

“Since the Son shows God’s glory, God himself will show the Son’s glory.

He will do it at once.

## John 13:33

“My children, I will be with you only a little longer.

You will look for me.

I told the other Jewish people what I now tell you.

You cannot come where I am going.

## John 13:34

“I give you a new command.

Love one another.

Love one another as I have loved you.

## John 13:35

“When you love one another, everyone will know you are my followers.”

**Pause and answer**

How will people recognise Jesus’ followers?

## Peter wants to follow Jesus now

## John 13:36

Simon Peter asked, “Lord, where are you going?”

Jesus answered, “You cannot follow me there now.

You will follow later.”

## John 13:37

Peter asked, “Lord, why can’t I follow you now?

I am ready to die for you.”

## John 13:38

Jesus answered, “Are you ready to die for me?

I tell you the truth.

Before the rooster crows, you will say three times that you do not know me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say Peter would do before the rooster crowed?
