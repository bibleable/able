# John 11 — Jesus calls Lazarus out of the tomb

**About this chapter**

Martha and Mary are grieving for their brother.

Jesus goes to meet them in Bethany.

## The sisters send a message

## John 11:1

A man named Lazarus was sick.

He lived in Bethany with his sisters, Mary and Martha.

## John 11:2

This was the Mary who would later put perfume on the Lord Jesus and wipe his feet with her hair.

Lazarus was her brother.

## John 11:3

The sisters sent Jesus a message.

“Lord, the friend you love is sick.”

## John 11:4

Jesus heard the message and said, “Death will not be the end of this sickness.

What happens will show God’s glory.

It will bring glory to the Son of God.”

**Help with these words**

God’s glory is his greatness made known.

## John 11:5

Jesus loved Martha, Mary, and Lazarus.

## John 11:6

When Jesus heard that Lazarus was sick, he stayed where he was for two more days.

**Pause and answer**

What message did the sisters send Jesus?

## Jesus decides to return to Judea

## John 11:7

Then Jesus told his followers, “Let us go back to Judea.”

## John 11:8

His followers asked, “Rabbi, people there just tried to stone you.

Are you going back?”

**Help with these words**

Rabbi means teacher.

## John 11:9

Jesus answered, “Aren’t there twelve hours of daylight?

A person walking by day can see the way.

The daylight keeps that person from stumbling.

## John 11:10

“At night a person stumbles because there is no light.”

**Pause and answer**

Why were the followers afraid to go back to Judea?

## Jesus explains what he means by sleep

## John 11:11

Then Jesus said, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep.

I am going to wake him.”

## John 11:12

His followers said, “Lord, if he is sleeping, he will get better.”

## John 11:13

The followers thought Jesus meant ordinary sleep.

Jesus meant that Lazarus had died.

## John 11:14

So Jesus told them plainly, “Lazarus is dead.

## John 11:15

“For your sake, I am glad I was not there.

Now you will have a reason to believe.

Let us go to him.”

## John 11:16

Thomas, also called the Twin, spoke to the other followers.

“Let us go too, so we may die with Jesus.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus mean when he said Lazarus had fallen asleep?

## Martha goes to meet Jesus

## John 11:17

When Jesus arrived, Lazarus had been in the tomb four days.

## John 11:18

Bethany was less than two miles from Jerusalem.

## John 11:19

Many Jewish people had come to comfort Martha and Mary because their brother had died.

## John 11:20

Martha heard that Jesus was coming.

She went out to meet him.

Mary stayed home.

## John 11:21

Martha told Jesus, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.

## John 11:22

“Even now, I know God will give you whatever you ask.”

**Pause and answer**

Who went out first to meet Jesus?

## Jesus speaks about resurrection

## John 11:23

Jesus told Martha, “Your brother will rise again.”

## John 11:24

Martha said, “I know he will rise on the last day, when God raises the dead.”

**Help with these words**

Resurrection means rising from the dead to live again.

## John 11:25

Jesus told Martha, “I am the resurrection and the life.

Anyone who trusts me will live, even if they die.

## John 11:26

“Everyone who lives and trusts me will never die.

Do you believe this?”

## John 11:27

Martha answered, “Yes, Lord.

I believe you are the Christ, the Son of God.

You are the one who was to come into the world.”

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Martha believe Jesus was?

## Mary goes to meet Jesus

## John 11:28

Martha went back and spoke to Mary in private.

“The Teacher is here.

He is asking for you.”

## John 11:29

Mary got up quickly and went to Jesus.

## John 11:30

Jesus was still outside the village, where Martha had met him.

## John 11:31

The visitors comforting Mary saw her hurry out.

They thought she was going to cry at the tomb.

So they followed her.

## John 11:32

Mary saw Jesus and fell at his feet.

She said, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Mary find Jesus?

## Jesus weeps

## John 11:33

Mary was crying.

The people who had come with her were crying too.

Seeing them, Jesus felt deeply troubled.

## John 11:34

Jesus asked, “Where have you laid him?”

They answered, “Lord, come and see.”

## John 11:35

Jesus wept.

## John 11:36

Some of the people said, “Look how much he loved Lazarus!”

## John 11:37

Others asked, “He gave a blind man sight.

Couldn’t he have kept Lazarus from dying?”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people see Jesus do when he shared their grief?

## The stone at the entrance

## John 11:38

Deeply moved again, Jesus came to the tomb.

It was a cave with a stone across the entrance.

## John 11:39

Jesus said, “Take the stone away.”

Martha said, “Lord, there will be a bad smell by now.

My brother has been dead four days.”

## John 11:40

Jesus answered, “Didn’t I tell you that if you believed, you would see God’s glory?”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Martha hesitate to have the stone taken away?

## Lazarus comes out

## John 11:41

They took the stone away.

Jesus looked up and said, “Father, thank you for hearing me.

**About the picture**

![An open tomb with its stone moved aside.](../pictures/tomb.svg)

Point to the entrance where Lazarus would hear Jesus call.

## John 11:42

“I know you always hear me.

I say this for the people standing here.

I want them to believe you sent me.”

## John 11:43

Then Jesus called out loudly, “Lazarus, come out!”

## John 11:44

Lazarus came out alive.

Strips of linen were still wrapped around his hands and feet.

A cloth covered his face.

Jesus told the people, “Unwrap him and let him go.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the people to do after Lazarus came out?

## The leaders call a meeting

## John 11:45

Many of the Jewish visitors who had come to Mary saw what Jesus did.

They put their trust in Jesus.

## John 11:46

Others went and told the Pharisees what Jesus had done.

## John 11:47

The chief priests and Pharisees called their council together.

They asked, “What shall we do?

This man is doing many signs.

## John 11:48

“If he goes on, everyone will believe in him.

Then the Romans will come.

They will take away our holy place and our nation.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders fear the Romans would do?

## Caiaphas speaks to the council

## John 11:49

Caiaphas was the high priest that year.

He told the council, “You do not understand!

## John 11:50

“It is better for you that one man should die for the people than that the whole nation should perish.”

## John 11:51

These words were more than Caiaphas’s own idea.

As high priest that year, he spoke a prophecy.

Jesus would die for the nation.

**Help with these words**

A prophecy is a message from God.

Caiaphas wanted the council to act.

John explains what his words meant about Jesus’ death.

## John 11:52

Jesus would also die to gather God’s scattered children into one family.

## John 11:53

From that day on, the leaders planned to kill Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Whom would Jesus gather into one family?

## People look for Jesus before Passover

## John 11:54

Jesus stopped going about openly among the people.

He went to Ephraim, a town near the wilderness.

He stayed there with his followers.

## John 11:55

The Jewish feast of Passover was near.

Many people went from the countryside to Jerusalem before the feast.

They went to make themselves clean for worship.

## John 11:56

They looked for Jesus in the temple courts.

They asked each other, “What do you think?

Will he come to the feast?”

## John 11:57

The chief priests and Pharisees had given an order.

Anyone who knew where Jesus was had to tell them.

They wanted to arrest him.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the leaders want people to report where Jesus was?
