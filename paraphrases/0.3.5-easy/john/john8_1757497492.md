# John 8 — Jesus speaks about light, truth, and freedom

**About this chapter**

People bring a woman to Jesus and accuse her.

Later, Jesus speaks about the freedom he gives his followers.

## A woman stands before Jesus

## John 8:1

Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.

## John 8:2

Early the next morning, Jesus returned to the temple courts.

The people gathered around him.

He sat down to teach them.

## John 8:3

Some teachers of God’s law and some Pharisees brought a woman to Jesus.

They had caught her committing adultery.

They made her stand in front of everyone.

**Help with these words**

Adultery means having sex with someone when one of you is married to someone else.

## John 8:4

They said, “Teacher, we caught this woman committing adultery.

## John 8:5

“The law of Moses tells us to stone a woman who does this.

What do you say?”

**Pause and answer**

What did the teachers ask Jesus to decide?

## The people accusing her leave

## John 8:6

They wanted to use Jesus’ answer to accuse him.

Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger.

## John 8:7

They kept asking him the same question.

Jesus stood up and said, “Who among you has never sinned?

Let that person throw the first stone at her.”

## John 8:8

Then Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground again.

## John 8:9

When they heard him, the people accusing her left one by one.

The oldest went first.

Only Jesus was left with the woman still standing there.

## John 8:10

Jesus stood up and asked her, “Where are the people who accused you?

Has anyone condemned you?”

## John 8:11

She answered, “No one, Lord.”

Jesus said, “I do not condemn you either.

Go now and leave your sin behind.”

**Help with these words**

To condemn someone means to judge them guilty and say they must face punishment.

**Pause and answer**

Who left first when Jesus answered?

## Jesus calls himself the light of the world

## John 8:12

Jesus spoke to the people again.

“I am the light of the world.

Anyone who follows me will never walk in darkness.

They will have the light that gives life.”

**Help with these words**

Light helps people find their way.

Jesus says he guides the people who follow him and gives them life.

## John 8:13

The Pharisees said, “You are speaking as your own witness.

Your words alone cannot prove your claim.”

## John 8:14

Jesus answered, “Even when I speak for myself, my words are true.

I know where I came from and where I am going.

You know neither.

## John 8:15

“You judge by human standards.

I judge no one.

## John 8:16

“Even if I do judge, my judgment is true.

I am not alone.

The Father who sent me is with me.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus call himself when he speaks about light?

## Two witnesses

## John 8:17

“Your own law accepts the words of two witnesses.

**Help with these words**

A witness tells others what they know to be true.

**About the picture**

![A scroll opened to show lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Find the writing on the scroll.

People read the law of Moses from scrolls like this.

## John 8:18

“I speak as one witness about myself.

The Father who sent me also speaks about me.”

## John 8:19

They asked, “Where is your Father?”

Jesus answered, “You know neither me nor my Father.

If you knew me, you would know my Father too.”

## John 8:20

Jesus taught near the place where people gave money at the temple.

No one arrested him.

His time had not yet come.

**Pause and answer**

Who else speaks as a witness about Jesus?

## People ask where Jesus is going

## John 8:21

Jesus spoke to them again.

“I am going away.

You will look for me, but you will die in your sin.

You cannot come where I am going.”

## John 8:22

Some of the people asked each other, “Does he mean he will kill himself?

Is that why he says we cannot come?”

## John 8:23

Jesus answered, “You come from below.

I come from above.

You belong to this world.

I do not belong to this world.

## John 8:24

“That is why I said you would die in your sins.

Unless you believe that I am he, you will die in your sins.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus say he came from?

## Jesus speaks about the Father

## John 8:25

They asked, “Who are you?”

Jesus answered, “I am who I have told you I am from the beginning.

## John 8:26

“I have much to say about you and much to judge.

The one who sent me tells the truth.

I tell the world what I have heard from him.”

## John 8:27

They did not understand that Jesus meant the Father.

## John 8:28

Jesus said, “When you lift up the Son of Man, you will know that I am he.

You will know I do nothing on my own.

I say just what my Father taught me.

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

He speaks about the time when people will lift him up on a cross.

## John 8:29

“The one who sent me is with me.

He has not left me alone.

I always do what pleases him.”

## John 8:30

Many people put their trust in Jesus as he spoke.

**Pause and answer**

Who taught Jesus what to say?

## The truth will set you free

## John 8:31

Jesus told the Jewish people who had believed him, “Keep living by my words.

Then you are truly my followers.

## John 8:32

“You will know the truth.

The truth will set you free.”

## John 8:33

They answered, “Abraham is our ancestor.

We have never been anyone’s slaves.

Why do you say we will be set free?”

## John 8:34

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

Everyone who sins is a slave to sin.

**Help with these words**

Jesus speaks of sin as a master that gives orders.

He says the Son can free people from its rule.

## John 8:35

“A slave does not have a place in the household forever.

A son belongs to the family forever.

## John 8:36

“So if the Son sets you free, you really will be free.

**Pause and answer**

Who can set people free from sin?

## What did Abraham do?

## John 8:37

“I know you come from Abraham’s family.

Yet you are trying to kill me.

You have no room for my words.

## John 8:38

“I tell you what I have seen with my Father.

You do what you have heard from your father.”

## John 8:39

They answered, “Abraham is our father.”

Jesus said, “If you were Abraham’s children, you would do what Abraham did.

## John 8:40

“I have told you the truth I heard from God.

Now you are trying to kill me.

Abraham never did that.

## John 8:41

“You do what your father does.”

They answered, “We were not born outside marriage.

God himself is our only Father.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say Abraham never did?

## Jesus asks why his listeners refuse the truth

## John 8:42

Jesus answered, “If God were your Father, you would love me.

I came here from God.

I did not come on my own.

God sent me.

## John 8:43

“Why do you fail to understand me?

Because you cannot accept my words.

## John 8:44

“Your father is the devil.

You want to do what he wants.

He was a murderer from the beginning.

There is no truth in him.

Lying comes naturally to him.

He is a liar and the father of lies.

**Help with these words**

Jesus is speaking to people who want to kill him.

Calling the devil their father describes whose ways they are following.

## John 8:45

“I tell you the truth, and that is why you do not believe me!

## John 8:46

“Can any of you prove I have sinned?

If I tell the truth, why do you not believe me?

## John 8:47

“People who belong to God listen to God’s words.

You do not listen because you do not belong to God.”

**Pause and answer**

What do people who belong to God listen to?

## Jesus speaks about keeping his word

## John 8:48

Those arguing with Jesus answered, “Aren’t we right to say you are a Samaritan and have a demon?”

## John 8:49

Jesus replied, “I do not have a demon.

I honour my Father, but you dishonour me.

## John 8:50

“I do not seek honour for myself.

There is one who seeks honour for me.

He is the judge.

## John 8:51

“I tell you the truth.

Anyone who keeps my word will never see death.”

## John 8:52

They answered, “Now we know you have a demon!

Abraham died, and the prophets died too.

Yet you say that whoever keeps your word will never taste death.

**Help with these words**

To “taste death” means to experience death.

## John 8:53

“Are you greater than our father Abraham?

He died, and so did the prophets.

Who do you claim to be?”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the people ask Jesus to compare himself with?

## Before Abraham was born

## John 8:54

Jesus answered, “If I give myself honour, it means nothing.

My Father gives me honour.

He is the one you call your God.

## John 8:55

“You do not know him, but I know him.

If I said otherwise, I would be a liar like you.

I know him and keep his word.

## John 8:56

“Your father Abraham was full of joy at the thought of seeing my day.

He saw it and was glad.”

## John 8:57

They said, “You are not yet fifty years old.

How could you have seen Abraham?”

## John 8:58

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

Before Abraham was born, I am!”

## John 8:59

They picked up stones to throw at Jesus.

But Jesus was hidden from their sight.

He left the temple courts.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives Jesus honour?
