# John 19 — Jesus dies and his friends lay him in a tomb

**About this chapter**

Pilate hands Jesus over to the soldiers.

Jesus speaks to his mother before he dies.

Joseph and Nicodemus care for his body.

## The soldiers mock Jesus

## John 19:1

Pilate ordered the soldiers to whip Jesus.

## John 19:2

The soldiers made a crown from thorny branches.

They put it on Jesus’ head.

They dressed him in a purple robe.

## John 19:3

Again and again they came up to Jesus.

They mocked him, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!”

They slapped his face.

**Pause and answer**

What did the soldiers put on Jesus’ head?

## Pilate brings Jesus outside

## John 19:4

Pilate went outside again.

He said, “I am bringing Jesus out to you.

I find no reason to charge him with a crime.”

## John 19:5

Jesus came out wearing the thorny crown and purple robe.

Pilate said, “Here is the man!”

## John 19:6

The chief priests and the officers saw Jesus.

They shouted, “Crucify him! Crucify him!”

Pilate answered, “Take him and crucify him yourselves.

I find no reason to charge him with a crime.”

**Help with these words**

To crucify someone means to put them to death on a cross.

## John 19:7

The people accusing Jesus answered, “We have a law.

Under that law he must die because he called himself the Son of God.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Pilate say about the charges against Jesus?

## Pilate questions Jesus again

## John 19:8

These words frightened Pilate even more.

## John 19:9

Pilate went back inside his headquarters.

He asked Jesus, “Where do you come from?”

Jesus gave no answer.

## John 19:10

Pilate said, “Will you not speak to me?

I have the power to set you free.

I also have the power to have you crucified.”

## John 19:11

Jesus answered, “You have power over me only because God gave it to you.

So the one who handed me over to you is guilty of a greater sin.”

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Pilate his power over Jesus?

## The chief priests demand a decision

## John 19:12

Pilate tried to set Jesus free.

But the people kept shouting, “If you let him go, you are no friend of Caesar!

Anyone who calls himself a king stands against Caesar.”

**Help with these words**

Caesar was the title of the Roman emperor.

## John 19:13

Pilate heard them and brought Jesus outside.

He sat in the judge’s seat at the Stone Pavement.

In Hebrew that place was called Gabbatha.

## John 19:14

It was about noon on the day people prepared for Passover.

Pilate said, “Here is your king!”

## John 19:15

They shouted, “Take him away! Take him away! Crucify him!”

Pilate asked, “Shall I crucify your king?”

The chief priests answered, “Caesar is our only king.”

## John 19:16

Then Pilate handed Jesus over to die on a cross.

The soldiers took Jesus away.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the chief priests call their only king?

## A sign names Jesus as king

## John 19:17

Jesus carried his own cross.

He went to the Place of the Skull.

In Hebrew its name is Golgotha.

## John 19:18

There the soldiers crucified Jesus and two other men.

Jesus was in the middle, with one man on each side.

## John 19:19

Pilate had a sign put on Jesus’ cross.

It said, “Jesus of Nazareth, the King of the Jews.”

## John 19:20

Many Jewish people read it because the cross was near the city.

The sign was in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek.

## John 19:21

The chief priests told Pilate, “Change the sign.

It should say that he claimed to be the King of the Jews.”

## John 19:22

Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.”

**Pause and answer**

What words did Pilate put on the sign?

## The soldiers divide the clothes

## John 19:23

After putting Jesus on the cross, the soldiers divided his clothes into four shares.

Each soldier took one share.

His tunic was left.

Someone had woven it in one piece, without a seam.

## John 19:24

The soldiers said, “Let us keep it whole.

We can cast lots to choose who gets it.”

So the words of Scripture came true.

“They shared out my clothes and cast lots for them.”

That is what the soldiers did.

**Help with these words**

A tunic is a piece of clothing worn next to the body.

To cast lots means to choose by chance.

**Pause and answer**

How did the soldiers choose who would get the tunic?

## Jesus speaks to his mother and his follower

## John 19:25

Jesus’ mother stood near his cross with her sister.

Mary the wife of Clopas and Mary Magdalene stood there too.

## John 19:26

Jesus saw his mother and the follower he loved standing nearby.

He told his mother, “Woman, here is your son.”

## John 19:27

Then Jesus told his follower, “Here is your mother.”

From then on, the follower took her into his home.

**Pause and answer**

What did the follower do for Jesus’ mother?

## Jesus dies

## John 19:28

Jesus knew he had now finished everything.

So that the words of Scripture would come true, he said, “I am thirsty.”

## John 19:29

A jar of sour wine stood nearby.

People dipped a sponge into the wine.

They put it on a hyssop stalk and lifted it to Jesus’ mouth.

**Help with these words**

Hyssop is a plant. A stalk is a stem.

## John 19:30

Jesus received the sour wine and said, “It is finished.”

He bowed his head, gave up his spirit, and died.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say after he received the sour wine?

## The soldiers find Jesus already dead

## John 19:31

It was the day of Preparation.

The next day was a special Sabbath.

The Jewish leaders wanted the bodies taken down before then.

They asked Pilate to have the men’s legs broken and their bodies taken down.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## John 19:32

The soldiers broke the legs of both men on either side of Jesus.

## John 19:33

When the soldiers came to Jesus, they saw he was already dead.

They left his legs unbroken.

## John 19:34

One soldier pierced Jesus’ side with a spear.

At once blood and water flowed out.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the soldiers leave Jesus’ legs unbroken?

## A witness tells what he saw

## John 19:35

The person who saw this has told you what happened.

He knows his words are true.

He tells you so that you too may believe.

## John 19:36

These things happened just as the Scriptures said.

“No one will break any of his bones.”

## John 19:37

Another Scripture says, “They will look at the one they pierced.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the witness tell people what he saw?

## Joseph and Nicodemus bury Jesus

## John 19:38

Joseph from Arimathea followed Jesus in secret because he feared the Jewish leaders.

He asked Pilate for Jesus’ body.

Pilate agreed, and Joseph took the body down.

## John 19:39

Nicodemus came too.

He was the man who had visited Jesus at night.

He brought about thirty-four kilograms of myrrh and aloes.

**Help with these words**

Myrrh and aloes were sweet-smelling materials used in burial.

## John 19:40

Joseph and Nicodemus wrapped Jesus’ body in linen cloths with the spices.

This was the Jewish way of preparing a body for burial.

## John 19:41

There was a garden where Jesus had been crucified.

In the garden was a new tomb.

No one had ever been buried in it.

## John 19:42

Joseph and Nicodemus laid Jesus there.

The tomb was close by, and it was the day of Preparation.

Where did Joseph and Nicodemus lay Jesus?
