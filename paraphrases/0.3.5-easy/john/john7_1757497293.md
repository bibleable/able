# John 7 — Jesus teaches at the feast

**About this chapter**

People gather in Jerusalem for a feast.

They hear Jesus teach and disagree about who he is.

## Jesus’ brothers ask him to go to Judea

## John 7:1

Jesus travelled through Galilee.

He stayed away from Judea because people there were trying to kill him.

## John 7:2

The Jewish Feast of Tabernacles was near.

**Help with these words**

Tabernacles means shelters.

At this feast, families stayed in shelters to remember how their ancestors lived in the wilderness.

**About the picture**

![A simple shelter with a roof of branches.](../pictures/feast-shelter.svg)

Find the branches that form the roof of the shelter.

## John 7:3

Jesus’ brothers told him, “Leave here and go to Judea.

Let your followers there see what you are doing.

## John 7:4

“People who want everyone to know them do not work in secret.

If you do these things, show the whole world.”

## John 7:5

Even Jesus’ own brothers did not trust him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus’ brothers want the followers in Judea to see?

## Jesus speaks about his time

## John 7:6

Jesus answered, “You can go at any time.

My time has not come yet.

## John 7:7

“The world cannot hate you.

But it hates me because I tell people that what they do is evil.

## John 7:8

“You go to the feast yourselves.

I am not going up to this feast.

My time has not yet come.”

## John 7:9

After saying this, Jesus stayed in Galilee.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus stay after his brothers spoke to him?

## People whisper at the feast

## John 7:10

After his brothers left, Jesus also went to the feast.

He went in secret.

## John 7:11

At the feast, some Jewish leaders looked for Jesus.

They asked, “Where is he?”

## John 7:12

People in the crowd whispered about Jesus.

Some said, “He is a good man.”

Others said, “No, he tricks people.”

## John 7:13

No one spoke openly about Jesus.

They were afraid of the leaders.

**Pause and answer**

Why did people whisper about Jesus?

## Jesus explains where his teaching comes from

## John 7:14

About halfway through the feast, Jesus went into the temple courts.

He began to teach.

## John 7:15

The people listening were amazed.

They asked, “How does he know so much without having studied?”

## John 7:16

Jesus answered, “What I teach comes from the one who sent me.

I did not make it up.

## John 7:17

“Anyone who wants to do what God wants will know whether my teaching comes from God or from me alone.

## John 7:18

“Someone who speaks for himself wants praise for himself.

Someone who wants honour for the one who sent him tells the truth.

There is no lie in him.

**Pause and answer**

Who sent Jesus to teach?

## Jesus asks the crowd to judge fairly

## John 7:19

“Moses gave you the law, didn’t he?

Yet none of you keeps it.

Why are you trying to kill me?”

## John 7:20

The crowd answered, “You have a demon!

Who is trying to kill you?”

## John 7:21

Jesus answered, “I did one miracle, and you are all amazed.

## John 7:22

“Moses gave you the practice of circumcision.

It began even earlier with your ancestors.

You circumcise a boy even on the Sabbath.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision is a mark on a boy’s body that recalls God’s agreement with Abraham’s family.

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## John 7:23

“You do this on the Sabbath to keep the law of Moses.

Then why are you angry that I made a man’s whole body well on the Sabbath?

## John 7:24

“Look beyond how things appear.

Judge fairly.”

**Pause and answer**

What had Jesus done for a man on the Sabbath?

## People ask where Jesus comes from

## John 7:25

Some people from Jerusalem asked, “Isn’t this the man they want to kill?

## John 7:26

“He speaks where everyone can hear him.

Yet the leaders say nothing to him.

Could our rulers have decided that he is the Christ?

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

## John 7:27

“We know where this man comes from.

When the Christ comes, no one will know where he comes from.”

## John 7:28

Jesus called out as he taught in the temple courts.

“You know me, and you know where I come from.

I did not come on my own.

The one who sent me is true.

You do not know him.

## John 7:29

“I know him because I come from him.

He sent me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say about the one who sent him?

## Officers are sent to arrest Jesus

## John 7:30

Then people tried to seize Jesus.

But no one laid a hand on him.

His time had not yet come.

## John 7:31

Many in the crowd trusted Jesus.

They asked, “When the Christ comes, will he do more signs than this man has done?”

**Help with these words**

Here a sign is something Jesus does that shows who he is.

## John 7:32

The Pharisees heard the crowd whispering about Jesus.

The Pharisees and chief priests sent temple officers to arrest him.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the leaders send to arrest Jesus?

## People wonder where Jesus will go

## John 7:33

Jesus said, “I will be with you a little longer.

Then I will go to the one who sent me.

## John 7:34

“You will look for me and will not find me.

You cannot come where I am.”

## John 7:35

Some Jewish people asked each other, “Where could he go that we would not find him?

Will he go to our people who live among the Greeks?

Will he teach the Greeks?”

## John 7:36

They asked, “What did he mean when he said we would look for him and not find him?

Why did he say we cannot come where he is?”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus say he was going back to?

## Jesus speaks about living water

## John 7:37

The last day was the greatest day of the feast.

Jesus stood and called out, “If anyone is thirsty, come to me and drink.

## John 7:38

“The Scriptures speak about the person who trusts me.

Streams of living water will flow from within that person.”

## John 7:39

Jesus meant the Spirit.

People who trusted Jesus would later receive the Spirit.

God had not yet given them the Spirit in this way.

Jesus had not yet received his glory.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus mean by the streams of living water?

## The crowd disagrees about Jesus

## John 7:40

Some people heard this and said, “He really is the Prophet!”

**Help with these words**

The people were waiting for a prophet God had promised.

## John 7:41

Others said, “He is the Christ.”

Still others asked, “How can the Christ come from Galilee?

## John 7:42

“The Scriptures say he will come from David’s family.

They say he will come from Bethlehem, where David lived.”

## John 7:43

So the crowd disagreed about who Jesus was.

## John 7:44

Some wanted to arrest Jesus.

But no one laid a hand on him.

**Pause and answer**

Which village did people name as the home of David?

## The temple officers return

## John 7:45

The temple officers went back to the chief priests and Pharisees.

The leaders asked, “Why didn’t you bring Jesus here?”

## John 7:46

The officers answered, “No one has ever spoken like him!”

## John 7:47

The Pharisees replied, “Has he tricked you too?

## John 7:48

“Do you see any of the rulers or Pharisees trusting him?

## John 7:49

“This crowd knows nothing about the law.

They are under a curse!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the officers say about the way Jesus spoke?

## Nicodemus asks for a fair hearing

## John 7:50

Nicodemus was one of the Pharisees.

He had visited Jesus before.

Now he asked the other leaders a question.

## John 7:51

“Does our law let us judge a man guilty before we hear him and find out what he has done?”

## John 7:52

They answered, “Are you from Galilee too?

Look in the Scriptures.

You will see that no prophet comes from Galilee.”

## John 7:53

Then everyone went home.

**Pause and answer**

What did Nicodemus say the leaders should do before judging a man?
