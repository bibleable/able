# Joel 3 — The LORD judges the nations

**About this chapter**

Other nations took people from Judah and sold them far from home. The LORD tells Joel that he remembers those people. He will bring them back and judge the nations that harmed them.

## The LORD remembers his people

## Joel 3:1

The LORD says, “The time will come when I bring back the captives from Judah and Jerusalem.

## Joel 3:2

Then I will gather all the nations in the Valley of Jehoshaphat.

There I will judge them for what they did to Israel.

Israel is my people. They belong to me.

The nations scattered my people and divided up my land.

## Joel 3:3

They cast lots to decide who would get each captive.

They traded a boy to pay for sex.

They sold a girl to buy wine to drink.

## Joel 3:4

Tyre, Sidon, and all you people of Philistia, answer me.

What have I done to you?

Are you trying to pay me back?

If so, I will quickly bring your deeds back on you.

## Joel 3:5

You took my silver and gold.

You carried my best treasures into your temples.

## Joel 3:6

You sold people from Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks.

You wanted them far from home.

## Joel 3:7

I will bring them back from the places you sold them.

What you did to them will happen to you.

## Joel 3:8

I will sell your sons and daughters to Judah’s people.

They will sell them to the Sabeans, a people far away.”

The LORD has spoken.

## The nations come to the valley

## Joel 3:9

Give this message to the nations.

“Get ready for war. Wake your strongest soldiers.

Call every soldier forward to fight.

## Joel 3:10

Turn the tools you use to plow into swords.

Turn the hooks you use to trim vines into spears.

Even those who feel weak must say, ‘I am strong.’

## Joel 3:11

Hurry, all you nations nearby. Gather together!”

Joel calls out, “LORD, bring down your mighty ones.”

## Joel 3:12

The LORD says, “Let the nations come to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.

I will sit there as judge over all the nations around them.

## Joel 3:13

The crop is ripe. Cut it with the harvest blade.

The place for crushing grapes is full. Tread on the grapes.

The juice runs over the sides of the tubs.”

The harvest is a picture of judgment.

The nations have done so much evil that the time has come to judge them.

## Joel 3:14

Crowds upon crowds fill the valley!

Here the LORD will give his decision.

The day of the LORD is near.

## Joel 3:15

The sun and the moon will go dark.

The stars will stop shining.

## Joel 3:16

The LORD’s voice will roar from Zion in Jerusalem.

The sky and earth will shake.

But Israel’s people can run to him for safety.

The LORD will keep his people safe.

## Water and food for Judah

## Joel 3:17

The LORD says, “Then you will know I am the LORD your God.

I live on Zion, my holy mountain.

Jerusalem will be holy. The city belongs to me.

Foreign armies will never overrun it again.

## Joel 3:18

Sweet wine will drip from the mountains.

Milk will flow from the hills.

That is a picture of how much people will have.

Water will run in every stream in Judah.

A spring will flow from the LORD’s house.

It will water the Valley of Acacias.

## Joel 3:19

Egypt will lie empty. Edom will become a desert.

They attacked the people of Judah.

They killed innocent people in Judah’s own land.

## Joel 3:20

But people will always live in Judah.

Families will live in Jerusalem, generation after generation.

## Joel 3:21

Those innocent deaths have gone unpunished.

I will now punish those who killed them.”

The LORD lives in Zion.
