# Joel 1 — Locusts eat the crops

**About this chapter**

Locusts are insects that eat plants. So many came that people lost their food. Joel calls everyone together to ask the LORD for help.

## Tell your children what happened

## Joel 1:1

The LORD gave this message to Joel.

Joel was the son of Pethuel.

## Joel 1:2

Joel said, “Listen, you elders who lead the people.

Everyone who lives here, listen too.

Have you ever seen anything like this?

Did your parents ever see anything like it?”

## Joel 1:3

Tell your children what happened.

Ask them to tell their children.

Then those children can tell their own children.

## Joel 1:4

First, hungry locusts ate the plants.

A swarm of locusts ate what was left.

Then young locusts ate what the swarm left.

More locusts came and ate the rest.

## The vines and trees are bare

## Joel 1:5

Wake up and cry, you who get drunk!

All you who drink wine, cry out!

There is no more sweet wine for you.

## Joel 1:6

The LORD describes the locusts as a mighty nation.

They have come into his land.

There are too many to count.

Their teeth tear plants like a lion’s sharp teeth tear food.

## Joel 1:7

They ruined the LORD’s grapevines and broke his fig trees.

They tore off the bark and threw it away.

Now the bare branches look white.

## Joel 1:8

Cry like a young bride whose husband has died.

She wears rough cloth called sackcloth to show her grief.

## Joel 1:9

The priests serve the LORD in his house.

They grieve because the offerings have stopped.

People have no grain or wine to bring as gifts.

## Joel 1:10

The fields lie ruined. The land itself seems sad.

The grain is gone. The new wine has dried up.

There is no oil from the olives.

## Joel 1:11

Farmers, look at what you have lost!

You who care for vines, cry out!

The wheat and barley you hoped to gather are gone.

## Joel 1:12

Grapevines and fig trees have dried up.

So have the pomegranate, palm, and apple trees.

Every fruit tree has dried up.

Even the people’s joy is gone.

## Joel calls the people together

## Joel 1:13

Priests who serve my God at the altar, cry out!

The altar is where you bring gifts to God.

Wear rough cloth and spend the night in it.

No one brings grain or wine to God’s house now.

## Joel 1:14

Set aside a day for a fast.

A fast means going without food for a time.

Call everyone together at the house of the LORD your God.

Bring the elders and everyone who lives here.

Call out to the LORD together.

## Joel 1:15

What a terrible day is coming!

The day of the LORD is near.

God, the Almighty, will bring destruction.

## Joel 1:16

We have seen our food disappear.

We used to celebrate in our God’s house.

Now that joy is gone.

## Joel 1:17

Under lumps of earth, the seeds have dried up.

The buildings where we store grain are falling apart.

The grain has dried up in the fields.

## Joel 1:18

Listen to the cattle groan.

They wander around looking for grass to eat.

Even the sheep are suffering.

## Joel calls out to the LORD

## Joel 1:19

LORD, I call to you for help.

Fire has burned the open grasslands.

Flames have burned all the trees in the fields.

## Joel 1:20

Even the wild animals pant for you.

The streams where they drank have dried up.

Fire has burned the grass where they fed.
