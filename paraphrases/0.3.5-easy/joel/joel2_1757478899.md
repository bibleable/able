# Joel 2 — Come back to the LORD

**About this chapter**

Joel sounds an alarm for the people of Zion, another name for Jerusalem. The LORD asks his people to come back to him. He promises food for their land and his Spirit for people everywhere.

## An alarm sounds in Zion

## Joel 2:1

Blow the ram’s horn in Zion!

People blow this horn to warn others of danger.

Sound the alarm on God’s holy mountain.

Everyone in the land has reason to tremble.

The day of the LORD is near.

## Joel 2:2

Clouds will make that day dark and gloomy.

A great army spreads across the mountains like the morning light.

No one has ever seen an army like this.

No one will see its like again.

## Joel 2:3

Fire burns in front of them and behind them.

Ahead lies a land full of plants, like the garden of Eden.

Behind them lies an empty desert. Nothing escapes.

## Joel 2:4

They look like horses. They run like fast horses.

## Joel 2:5

They leap across the mountaintops.

They sound like chariots, the carts soldiers ride in.

They crackle like fire burning dry stalks.

They look like a great army ready for battle.

## Joel 2:6

People in many nations shake with fear.

Their faces turn pale.

## Joel 2:7

The army rushes forward like strong soldiers.

They climb the walls. Each stays in line.

## Joel 2:8

They move without pushing one another aside.

They break through the defenses and keep their lines.

## Joel 2:9

They rush into the city and run along its wall.

They climb into houses through windows like thieves.

## Joel 2:10

The ground shakes before them. The sky trembles.

The sun and moon go dark. The stars stop shining.

## Joel 2:11

The LORD calls out before his great army.

Those who obey his orders are powerful.

The day of the LORD brings such terror.

Who can live through it?

## Everyone gathers to pray

## Joel 2:12

The LORD says, “Even now, come back to me.

Come with your whole heart.

Go without food for a time. Weep and grieve.”

## Joel 2:13

People sometimes tore their clothes to show their sorrow.

Joel says, “Let that sorrow reach deep into your hearts.

Come back to the LORD your God.

He shows kindness and cares when people suffer.

He is slow to anger. His love holds firm.

He can choose to hold back disaster.”

## Joel 2:14

Perhaps the LORD will hold back the disaster.

Perhaps he will bless you with another harvest.

Then you can bring him gifts of grain and wine.

## Joel 2:15

Blow the ram’s horn in Zion.

Set aside time for a fast, a time without food.

Call the people together to worship.

## Joel 2:16

Prepare everyone to come before God.

Bring the elders, the children, and even nursing babies.

Even the bride and groom must leave their rooms and come.

## Joel 2:17

Let the priests weep between the temple porch and the altar.

Let them pray, “LORD, spare your people.

We belong to you. Keep other nations from mocking us.

Keep them from asking, ‘Where is their God?’”

**Pause and answer**

**Who should come when the horn calls people to worship?**

## The fields will grow green again

## Joel 2:18

Then the LORD acted to protect his land.

He had pity on his people and spared them.

## Joel 2:19

The LORD answered, “I will give you grain, new wine, and oil.

You will have enough.

I will never again let the nations shame you.

## Joel 2:20

I will drive the northern army into an empty, dry land.

Its front ranks will go into the sea to the east.

Its back ranks will go into the sea to the west.

A terrible smell will rise from them.”

The LORD has done great things.

## Joel 2:21

Land, you can rejoice and put fear aside!

The LORD has done great things.

## Joel 2:22

Wild animals, you can put fear aside too.

The grasslands have turned green.

Fruit grows on the trees again.

Fig trees and grapevines give rich crops.

## Joel 2:23

People of Zion, rejoice in the LORD your God.

The autumn rain shows that he has acted for you.

He sends rain in autumn and spring as before.

## Joel 2:24

Grain will cover the floors where people separate it from the stalks.

The tubs that hold new wine and oil will overflow.

![A head of ripe grain growing on its stalk.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Joel 2:25

The LORD says, “Remember the years the locusts ate your crops.

The swarms, the young locusts, and those that ate everything were my great army.

I sent them. I will give back what you lost.

## Joel 2:26

You will eat until you are full.

You will praise me, the LORD your God.

I have done wonderful things for you.

My people will never face shame again.

## Joel 2:27

Then you will know I am here in Israel.

I am the LORD your God. There is no other.

My people will never face shame again.

## The LORD promises his Spirit

## Joel 2:28

Afterward, I will give my Spirit freely to all people.

Your sons and daughters will speak messages from me.

Your old men will have dreams.

Your young men will see visions, pictures I show them.

## Joel 2:29

In those days I will give my Spirit to servants too.

Both men and women will receive my Spirit.

## Joel 2:30

I will show wonders in the sky and on earth.

People will see blood, fire, and tall columns of smoke.

## Joel 2:31

The sun will go dark. The moon will turn blood-red.

Then the great and fearsome day of the LORD will come.

## Joel 2:32

The LORD will save everyone who calls to him for help.”

The LORD has promised a way to safety on Mount Zion in Jerusalem.

Those who survive will include the people the LORD calls.
