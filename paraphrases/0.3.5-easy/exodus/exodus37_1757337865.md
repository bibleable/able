# Exodus 37 — Bezalel makes the furnishings for the holy tent

**About this chapter**

Bezalel makes the ark, the table, the lampstand and the incense altar. The chapter describes how he shapes wood and gold for each one.

## The ark and its carrying poles

## Exodus 37:1

Bezalel built the ark, a chest of acacia wood.

It was two and a half cubits long.

It was one and a half cubits wide and just as high.

**Reading help**

A cubit was about the length from your elbow to your fingertip.

## Exodus 37:2

He covered the ark with pure gold, inside and out.

He added gold trim around it.

## Exodus 37:3

He made four gold rings for the ark’s four feet.

Two rings went on each side.

## Exodus 37:4

Bezalel made poles from acacia wood.

He covered the poles with gold.

## Exodus 37:5

He slid the poles through the rings on the sides.

People could lift the poles to carry the ark.

**Pause and answer**

How could people carry the ark?

## Two figures on the mercy seat

## Exodus 37:6

Bezalel made the ark’s cover from pure gold.

The cover was called the mercy seat.

It was two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.

## Exodus 37:7

He hammered the gold into two figures called cherubim.

He shaped them at the ends of the mercy seat.

**Reading help**

Cherubim are heavenly beings with wings. Bezalel made gold figures in their shape.

## Exodus 37:8

One figure stood at each end.

Both figures and the cover came from one piece of gold.

## Exodus 37:9

The figures faced each other and looked toward the cover.

Their wings spread upward over the mercy seat.

**Pause and answer**

Which way did the figures face?

## A table covered with gold

## Exodus 37:10

Bezalel made a table from acacia wood.

It was two cubits long and one cubit wide.

It stood one and a half cubits high.

## Exodus 37:11

He covered the table with pure gold.

He added gold trim around it.

## Exodus 37:12

He made a rim around the table.

It was as wide as a hand.

He put gold trim on the rim.

## Exodus 37:13

He made four gold rings.

He fastened one to each corner by a table leg.

## Exodus 37:14

The rings sat close to the rim.

They held the poles for carrying the table.

## Exodus 37:15

Bezalel made the table’s carrying poles from acacia wood.

He covered them with gold.

## Exodus 37:16

He made the plates and dishes from pure gold.

He also made gold bowls and pitchers for pouring out drink offerings.

**Pause and answer**

What did Bezalel make for pouring out drink offerings?

## A lampstand with six branches

## Exodus 37:17

Bezalel hammered one piece of pure gold into a lampstand.

He shaped its base, upright stem, cups, buds and petals from that piece.

## Exodus 37:18

Six branches reached out from the stem.

Three were on each side.

![A central stem with three branches on the left and three on the right.](../pictures/six-branches-of-lampstand.svg)

Bezalel made the branches and stem as one piece.

## Exodus 37:19

Each branch had three cups shaped like almond flowers.

Each flower had buds and petals.

All six branches had the same pattern.

## Exodus 37:20

The upright stem had four cups shaped like almond flowers.

These flowers had buds and petals too.

## Exodus 37:21

One bud sat below the first pair of branches.

Another sat below the second pair.

A third sat below the third pair.

| Where to look | What to picture |
| --- | --- |
| On each side of the stem | Three branches |
| On each branch | Three flower-shaped cups |
| On the upright stem | Four flower-shaped cups |
| Just below each pair of branches | One bud |

## Exodus 37:22

The buds and branches were part of the lampstand itself.

Bezalel hammered them all from one piece of pure gold.

**Pause and answer**

What shape were the cups on each branch?

## Seven lamps and their tools

## Exodus 37:23

He made seven lamps from pure gold.

He also made gold wick trimmers and trays.

## Exodus 37:24

The lampstand and all its tools together took one talent of pure gold.

**Reading help**

A wick is the part of an oil lamp that burns. The trimmers cut the wick. A talent was a large measure of weight.

**Pause and answer**

How many lamps did Bezalel make?

## The incense altar

## Exodus 37:25

Bezalel made the incense altar from acacia wood.

Its square top was one cubit long and one cubit wide.

The altar stood two cubits high.

Its raised corners, called horns, were part of the altar itself.

## Exodus 37:26

He covered the top, sides and horns with pure gold.

He added gold trim around the altar.

## Exodus 37:27

He made two gold rings below the trim on opposite sides.

The rings held the poles for carrying the altar.

## Exodus 37:28

Bezalel made the poles from acacia wood.

He covered them with gold.

## Exodus 37:29

He also made the holy anointing oil and the pure, sweet-smelling incense.

He blended them with the skill of a perfume maker.

**Reading help**

Anointing oil was used to set people or things apart for God. Incense gives off a scent when it burns.

Where did Bezalel put the carrying rings on the incense altar?
