# Exodus 8 — Frogs, gnats, and flies come to Egypt

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Pharaoh to let the Israelites go and worship.

Pharaoh asks Moses to pray when his people need relief.

## Frogs come out of the water

## Exodus 8:1

The LORD told Moses, “Go to Pharaoh with this message from me.

‘Let my people go so they can worship me.

## Exodus 8:2

If you refuse, I will send frogs throughout your country.

## Exodus 8:3

The Nile River will fill with frogs. They will enter your palace.

They will climb into your bedroom and onto your bed.

They will enter the homes of your officials and your people.

They will get into your ovens and your bread bowls.

## Exodus 8:4

Frogs will climb on you, your people, and all your officials.’”

## Exodus 8:5

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to hold his staff out over the water.

Hold it over the rivers, canals, and ponds.

Bring the frogs up onto the land of Egypt.”

## Exodus 8:6

Aaron held his hand out over the water.

Frogs came up and covered the land.

## Exodus 8:7

Egypt’s magicians used their magic.

They too brought frogs up onto the land.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the frogs come from?

## Pharaoh asks for the frogs to go

## Exodus 8:8

Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron.

“Pray to the LORD to take the frogs away from us.

Then I will let your people go and offer sacrifices to the LORD.”

## Exodus 8:9

Moses told Pharaoh, “You may choose the time.

When shall I pray for you, your officials, and your people?

I will ask God to clear the frogs from your homes.

Only the frogs in the Nile will remain.”

## Exodus 8:10

“Tomorrow,” Pharaoh answered. Moses said, “It will happen as you say.

Then you will know there is no one like the LORD our God.

## Exodus 8:11

The frogs will leave you, your homes, your officials, and your people.

Only the frogs in the Nile will remain.”

## Exodus 8:12

Moses and Aaron left Pharaoh.

Moses cried out to the LORD about the frogs he had sent against Pharaoh.

## Exodus 8:13

The LORD did what Moses asked.

The frogs in the homes, yards, and fields died.

## Exodus 8:14

People gathered the dead frogs into heaps too many to count.

The whole land smelled terrible.

## Exodus 8:15

Pharaoh saw that the trouble had passed.

He grew stubborn and refused to listen to Moses and Aaron.

The LORD had said this would happen.

**Pause and answer**

How did Pharaoh respond when the trouble passed?

## The dust turns into gnats

## Exodus 8:16

The LORD told Moses, “Tell Aaron to strike the dust with his staff.

The dust will turn into swarms of gnats throughout Egypt.”

## Exodus 8:17

Aaron did as God said. He struck the dust with his staff.

All over Egypt the dust turned into gnats.

These tiny insects covered people and animals.

## Exodus 8:18

The magicians tried to make gnats with their magic. They could not.

The gnats stayed on the people and animals.

## Exodus 8:19

The magicians told Pharaoh, “This is the finger of God.”

Pharaoh stayed stubborn. He would not listen, just as the LORD had said.

**Reading help**

“The finger of God” means the magicians saw God’s power at work.

**Pause and answer**

What did the magicians tell Pharaoh about the gnats?

## The flies stay away from Goshen

## Exodus 8:20

The LORD said to Moses, “Get up early tomorrow.

Meet Pharaoh when he goes to the water.

Give him this message from me. ‘Let my people go to worship me.

## Exodus 8:21

If you refuse, I will send swarms of flies on you, your officials, and your people.

The flies will fill the houses and cover the ground.

## Exodus 8:22

I will protect Goshen, where my people live.

No swarms of flies will go there.

Then you will know that I, the LORD, am here in this land.

## Exodus 8:23

You will see the difference between my people and yours.

This sign will happen tomorrow.’”

## Exodus 8:24

The LORD did what he had said.

Thick swarms of flies filled Pharaoh’s palace and his officials’ homes.

Across Egypt, the flies ruined the land.

**Pause and answer**

Which part of the land did the LORD protect from the flies?

## Moses asks to travel into the wilderness

## Exodus 8:25

Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron. “Go and offer sacrifices to your God,” he said.

“But stay here in Egypt.”

## Exodus 8:26

Moses answered, “That would be wrong.

The Egyptians would be disgusted by the sacrifices we offer the LORD.

If they saw us, would they not throw stones at us?

## Exodus 8:27

We must travel three days into the wilderness.

There we must offer sacrifices as the LORD our God commands.”

## Exodus 8:28

Pharaoh said, “I will let you go and sacrifice to the LORD in the wilderness.

Stay close by. Now pray for me.”

## Exodus 8:29

Moses answered, “I will pray to the LORD as soon as I leave.

Tomorrow the flies will leave you, your officials, and your people.

Please keep your word this time.

Let the people go and offer sacrifices to the LORD.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Moses say the people must go to worship?

## Moses prays and the flies leave

## Exodus 8:30

Moses left Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD.

## Exodus 8:31

The LORD did what Moses asked.

He took the flies away from Pharaoh, his officials, and his people.

Not one fly remained.

## Exodus 8:32

Again Pharaoh hardened his heart. He refused to let the people go.

| What came to Egypt? | What happened next? |
| --- | --- |
| Frogs | Moses prayed. The frogs on the land died. |
| Gnats | The magicians tried to make gnats and failed. |
| Flies | Moses prayed. The LORD took every fly away. |

**Pause and answer**

Did Pharaoh keep his promise when the flies were gone?
