# Exodus 24 — Israel agrees to the covenant

**About this chapter**

Moses reads the LORD’s agreement to the people. They promise to obey. Later, the LORD calls Moses up Mount Sinai.

## The people promise to obey

## Exodus 24:1

The LORD told Moses, “Come up the mountain.

Bring Aaron, Nadab, Abihu and 70 of Israel’s leaders.

Worship me from a distance.

## Exodus 24:2

Only you, Moses, may come near me.

The other leaders must stay back.

The rest of the people must stay below.”

## Exodus 24:3

Moses told the people everything the LORD had said.

He told them all the LORD’s rules.

Everyone answered together, “We will do everything the LORD says.”

**Pause and answer**

How did the people answer when they heard the LORD’s rules?

## Moses reads the covenant

## Exodus 24:4

Moses wrote down all the LORD’s words.

Early the next morning, Moses built an altar at the foot of the mountain.

He also set up 12 pillars.

Each pillar stood for one of Israel’s 12 tribes.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place to offer gifts to God. A tribe is a large group of related families.

## Exodus 24:5

Moses sent some young men of Israel to make offerings.

They gave burnt offerings to the LORD.

They also sacrificed young bulls as peace offerings.

## Exodus 24:6

Moses put half the blood in bowls.

He splashed the other half on the altar.

## Exodus 24:7

Then Moses read the Book of the Covenant aloud.

The people said, “We will do everything the LORD says.

We will obey him.”

![An open scroll with lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Moses read the written agreement aloud so the people could hear it.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. Moses read its words so everyone could hear what they were agreeing to.

## Exodus 24:8

Moses splashed the blood from the bowls on the people.

He said, “This is the blood of the covenant.

The LORD has made this covenant with you.

You have heard all its words.”

| Where did the blood go? | When did Moses put it there? |
| --- | --- |
| On the altar | Before he read the covenant aloud |
| On the people | After they promised to obey |

**Pause and answer**

What did the people promise after Moses read to them?

## The leaders see God

## Exodus 24:9

Moses went up with Aaron, Nadab and Abihu.

Seventy of Israel’s leaders went with them.

## Exodus 24:10

They saw the God of Israel.

Beneath his feet they saw something like a floor of blue sapphire stone.

It was as clear as the sky.

## Exodus 24:11

God did not harm Israel’s leaders.

They saw God, and they ate and drank.

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders see beneath God’s feet?

## The LORD calls Moses higher

## Exodus 24:12

The LORD told Moses, “Come up the mountain to me.

Stay here.

I will give you flat pieces of stone called tablets.

I have written my law and commands on them to teach the people.”

## Exodus 24:13

Moses set out with Joshua, his helper.

Moses went up the mountain of God.

## Exodus 24:14

Moses told the leaders, “Wait here until we return.

Aaron and Hur are here with you.

Anyone who has an argument to settle can go to them.”

**Pause and answer**

Who could help settle arguments while Moses was away?

## The cloud covers Mount Sinai

## Exodus 24:15

Moses went up the mountain.

The cloud covered it.

## Exodus 24:16

The LORD’s glory settled on Mount Sinai.

The cloud covered the mountain for six days.

On day seven, the LORD called to Moses from inside the cloud.

## Exodus 24:17

The Israelites could see the LORD’s glory on the mountaintop.

To them it looked like a fire burning everything in its path.

**Reading help**

God’s glory is his greatness. The people describe what they could see by comparing it to fire.

## Exodus 24:18

Moses went into the cloud as he climbed the mountain.

He stayed on the mountain for 40 days and 40 nights.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what happened on the seventh day?
