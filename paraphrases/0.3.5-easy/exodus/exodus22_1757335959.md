# Exodus 22 — Paying back losses and caring for neighbors

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Israel more rules. Some explain how to repay a loss. Others tell the people how to treat neighbors who need help.

## When someone steals an animal

## Exodus 22:1

Suppose a thief steals an ox or a sheep.

He kills it or sells it.

For one ox, he must give the owner five oxen.

For one sheep, he must give the owner four sheep.

## Exodus 22:2

Suppose someone catches a thief breaking in at night.

If the thief dies from a beating, no one bears guilt for his death.

## Exodus 22:3

If this happens after sunrise, the person who kills the thief bears guilt.

A thief must pay back the whole loss.

If he has nothing, he himself must be sold to pay for the theft.

## Exodus 22:4

Suppose someone finds the stolen animal alive with the thief.

The thief must pay back double.

This rule covers oxen, donkeys and sheep.

| If a thief stole this animal | What did the thief owe? |
| --- | --- |
| One ox, then killed or sold it | Five oxen |
| One sheep, then killed or sold it | Four sheep |
| One ox, donkey or sheep, still alive with the thief | Two animals for one |

**Pause and answer**

What did the thief owe for a sheep that the thief had sold?

## Paying for damaged crops

## Exodus 22:5

Suppose a farmer lets animals feed in a field or vineyard.

The animals wander into someone else’s field and eat there.

The farmer must pay with the best crops from the farmer’s own land.

**Reading help**

A vineyard is a field where grapes grow.

## Exodus 22:6

Suppose someone starts a fire that spreads through thorn bushes.

It burns grain in piles or grain still growing.

It might even burn the whole field.

The person who started the fire must pay for the whole loss.

**Pause and answer**

Who had to pay if a fire burned someone’s grain?

## When property goes missing

## Exodus 22:7

Suppose you leave money or goods with a neighbor for safekeeping.

A thief steals them from your neighbor’s house.

If someone catches the thief, the thief must pay back double.

## Exodus 22:8

If no one finds the thief, the neighbor must face the judges.

The judges must decide whether the neighbor took your things.

## Exodus 22:9

Two people may claim the same animal, piece of clothing or other lost item.

Both must tell the judges what happened.

The person the judges find guilty must pay the neighbor double.

**Pause and answer**

Who decided which person owed double?

## Looking after a neighbor’s animal

## Exodus 22:10

Suppose you leave an animal with a neighbor.

It could be a donkey, an ox, a sheep or another animal.

The animal dies, gets hurt or disappears in a theft.

No one saw what happened.

## Exodus 22:11

Your neighbor must swear before the LORD that your neighbor did not take the animal.

You must accept that promise.

You must not ask your neighbor to pay.

## Exodus 22:12

But if a thief did steal it from your neighbor, your neighbor must pay you for it.

## Exodus 22:13

If another animal tore it apart, your neighbor must show you the remains.

The remains show what happened.

Your neighbor owes you nothing for that animal.

**Pause and answer**

What could the neighbor show the owner if another animal killed the missing animal?

## Borrowing or renting an animal

## Exodus 22:14

Suppose you borrow a neighbor’s animal.

It gets hurt or dies while the owner is away.

You must pay for the whole loss.

## Exodus 22:15

If the owner was there, you owe nothing.

If you rented the animal, the rental fee covers the loss.

**Pause and answer**

What covered the loss if someone rented the animal?

## Marriage payments and other laws

## Exodus 22:16

Suppose a man persuades a woman to have sex with him.

She has never had sex before.

She has made no promise to marry anyone.

The man must pay the full marriage payment to marry her.

## Exodus 22:17

Her father may refuse to let her marry him.

The man still owes the usual marriage payment for a woman who has never had sex.

## Exodus 22:18

Under this law, a woman who practiced sorcery faced death.

**Reading help**

Sorcery means using magic spells.

## Exodus 22:19

Anyone who had sex with an animal faced death.

## Exodus 22:20

Anyone who offered a sacrifice to another god faced destruction.

The people were to offer sacrifices to the LORD alone.

**Pause and answer**

To whom were the Israelites to offer sacrifices?

## The LORD hears people in trouble

## Exodus 22:21

The LORD said, “Do not take advantage of foreigners living among you.

Do not treat them cruelly.

Remember, you once lived as foreigners in Egypt.

## Exodus 22:22

Do not mistreat a widow or an orphan.

**Reading help**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died. An orphan is a child who has lost a parent.

## Exodus 22:23

If you mistreat them, they may cry to me for help.

I will hear them.

## Exodus 22:24

I will be angry.

I will kill you with the sword.

Then your wives will be widows.

Your children will lose their fathers.

**Pause and answer**

Why were the Israelites to remember their time in Egypt?

## A loan and a cloak

## Exodus 22:25

Suppose you lend money to one of my people who is poor.

Do not demand extra money for the loan.

## Exodus 22:26

You may take your neighbor’s cloak as a promise of repayment.

Give it back by sunset.

![A cloak hangs down from the shoulders.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

At sunset the neighbor needs the cloak back to keep warm.

**Reading help**

A cloak is a loose outer garment. A person could also wrap it around their body at night.

## Exodus 22:27

That cloak is your neighbor’s only covering.

What else can your neighbor sleep in?

If your neighbor calls to me, I will listen.

I care about people who suffer.

**Pause and answer**

Explain why the lender had to return the cloak by sunset.

## Honoring God

## Exodus 22:28

Do not insult God.

Do not call down harm on your people’s ruler.

## Exodus 22:29

Do not hold back gifts from your grain stores or your vats.

Give me your firstborn sons.

**Reading help**

Vats are large containers that hold liquids such as wine.

## Exodus 22:30

Give me the firstborn of your cattle and sheep too.

Leave each newborn with its mother for seven days.

Give it to me on the eighth day.

## Exodus 22:31

You are to be my holy people.

If you find an animal torn apart in a field, do not eat its meat.

Throw the meat to the dogs.”
