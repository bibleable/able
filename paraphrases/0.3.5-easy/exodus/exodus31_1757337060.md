# Exodus 31 — Skilled workers and a day of rest

**About this chapter**

The LORD names the workers who will make the holy tent. He tells Moses why Israel must keep the Sabbath, the weekly day of rest.

## The LORD chooses Bezalel

## Exodus 31:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 31:2

“I have chosen Bezalel by name.

His father is Uri.

His grandfather is Hur.

Bezalel belongs to the tribe of Judah.

## Exodus 31:3

I have filled Bezalel with the Spirit of God.

I have given him skill and understanding.

He knows how to make many kinds of things.

## Exodus 31:4

Bezalel can design beautiful things in gold, silver and bronze.

## Exodus 31:5

He can cut precious stones to fit their settings.

He can carve wood.

He is skilled in every kind of craft.

**Reading help**

A setting is a holder for a precious stone.

**Pause and answer**

What could Bezalel do with precious stones?

## Oholiab and the other workers

## Exodus 31:6

I have chosen Oholiab to help Bezalel.

Oholiab is the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan.

I have given skill to the other workers too.

Together they can make everything I told you to make.

| Worker | Family group | Work the LORD gave him |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bezalel | Judah | Design and make things for the holy tent |
| Oholiab | Dan | Help Bezalel with the work |

## Exodus 31:7

They must make the Tent of Meeting and its furnishings.

They must make the ark that holds the Testimony.

They must make its cover, the mercy seat.

## Exodus 31:8

They must make the table and its tools.

They must make the pure gold lampstand and its tools.

They must make the altar for burning incense.

## Exodus 31:9

They must make the altar for burnt offerings and its tools.

They must make the washing bowl and its stand.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the LORD choose to help Bezalel?

## Clothes, oil and incense

## Exodus 31:10

They must weave holy clothes for Aaron the priest.

They must also make clothes for Aaron’s sons to wear as priests.

## Exodus 31:11

They must make the anointing oil and sweet-smelling incense for the Holy Place.

The workers must follow all the instructions I gave you.”

**Reading help**

To anoint means to put oil on someone or something to set it apart for God. Incense gives off a scent when it burns.

**Pause and answer**

Whose instructions were the workers to follow?

## A holy day of rest

## Exodus 31:12

Then the LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Exodus 31:13

“Tell the Israelites, ‘Keep my Sabbaths.

The Sabbath will be a sign between us for generations to come.

It will help you know that I am the LORD who sets you apart for myself.

## Exodus 31:14

Keep the Sabbath because it is holy to you.

Anyone who treats it as an ordinary day faces death.

Anyone who works that day must be cut off from the people.

## Exodus 31:15

You may work for six days.

The seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest.

That day is holy to the LORD.

The penalty for working on the Sabbath is death.

**Pause and answer**

Which day did the LORD set apart for rest?

## A sign for every generation

## Exodus 31:16

The Israelites must keep the Sabbath.

Each generation must celebrate it as a lasting covenant.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. By resting every seventh day, the Israelites showed that they belonged to the LORD.

## Exodus 31:17

The Sabbath is a sign between me and Israel forever.

In six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth.

On the seventh day he rested and was refreshed.’”

## Exodus 31:18

On Mount Sinai the LORD finished speaking to Moses.

Then he gave Moses two stone tablets called the Testimony.

God had written on the stones with his own finger.

**Reading help**

The tablets were flat pieces of stone. The words about God’s finger tell us that God himself wrote the words on them.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what did the Sabbath remind Israel about the LORD?
