# Exodus 6 — God promises to free his people

**About this chapter**

God tells Moses what he will do for the Israelites.

The family records show where Moses and Aaron come from.

## God remembers his promise

## Exodus 6:1

The LORD told Moses, “Now you will see what I do to Pharaoh.

My power will make him let the people go.

He will even drive them out of his land.”

## Exodus 6:2

God also told Moses, “I am the LORD.

## Exodus 6:3

I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty.

I did not make myself known to them by my name, the LORD.

## Exodus 6:4

I made a covenant with them.

I promised to give them the land of Canaan.

They lived there as strangers.

## Exodus 6:5

Now I hear the Israelites groaning.

The Egyptians are forcing them to work as slaves.

I remember my covenant.”

**Reading help**

A covenant is a firm agreement. God remembers what he promised to do.

**Pause and answer**

What land did God promise to give Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob?

## God gives Moses a message for the Israelites

## Exodus 6:6

God said, “Tell the Israelites, ‘I am the LORD.

I will free you from the heavy work the Egyptians force you to do.

I will rescue you from slavery.

I will reach out my arm and free you.

You will see my power when I judge the Egyptians.

## Exodus 6:7

I will take you as my people. I will be your God.

You will know that I am the LORD your God.

I am the one who frees you from Egypt’s heavy work.

## Exodus 6:8

I will bring you to the land I promised Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

I will give it to you as your own land.

I am the LORD.’”

## Exodus 6:9

Moses told the Israelites what God had said.

Cruel slavery had worn them down. They could not listen to Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the Israelites find it hard to listen?

## God sends Moses and Aaron

## Exodus 6:10

Then the LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Exodus 6:11

“Go to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt,” God said.

“Tell him to let the Israelites leave his land.”

## Exodus 6:12

Moses answered the LORD, “Even the Israelites will not listen to me.

Speaking is hard for me. Why would Pharaoh listen?”

## Exodus 6:13

The LORD gave Moses and Aaron orders about the Israelites and Pharaoh.

Their task was to bring the Israelites out of Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

What task did God give Moses and Aaron?

## The families of Reuben, Simeon, and Levi

## Exodus 6:14

Here are the leaders of the family groups.

Reuben was Israel’s oldest son.

His sons were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

Their families formed the clans of Reuben.

## Exodus 6:15

Simeon’s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul.

Shaul’s mother was a Canaanite woman.

These sons had families called the clans of Simeon.

## Exodus 6:16

The family records name Levi’s sons as Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

Levi lived for 137 years.

## Exodus 6:17

Gershon’s sons were Libni and Shimei. Each had a family group.

## Exodus 6:18

Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

Kohath lived for 133 years.

## Exodus 6:19

Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi.

These families appear in the records of Levi’s people, the Levites.

**Pause and answer**

Which of Levi’s sons was Amram’s father?

## Aaron and Moses belong to Levi’s family

## Exodus 6:20

Amram married Jochebed, his father’s sister.

Jochebed gave birth to Aaron and Moses.

Amram lived for 137 years.

## Exodus 6:21

Izhar’s sons were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.

## Exodus 6:22

Uzziel’s sons were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.

## Exodus 6:23

Aaron married Elisheba. Her father was Amminadab.

Her brother was Nahshon.

Aaron and Elisheba had four sons named Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

## Exodus 6:24

Korah’s sons were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph.

Their families were called the Korahites.

## Exodus 6:25

Eleazar, Aaron’s son, married a daughter of Putiel.

She gave birth to Phinehas.

These records name the leaders of the Levite families.

| Parent | Children in the line leading to Moses and Aaron |
| --- | --- |
| Levi | Kohath |
| Kohath | Amram |
| Amram and Jochebed | Aaron and Moses |

## God tells the brothers to lead the people out

## Exodus 6:26

It was to these brothers, Aaron and Moses, that the LORD said,

“Bring the Israelites out of Egypt in their groups.”

## Exodus 6:27

These were the men who spoke to Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

Moses and Aaron spoke so the Israelites could leave Egypt.

## Exodus 6:28

One day in Egypt the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 6:29

He said, “I am the LORD. Tell Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, everything I tell you.”

## Exodus 6:30

Moses answered the LORD, “Speaking is hard for me.

Why would Pharaoh listen to me?”
