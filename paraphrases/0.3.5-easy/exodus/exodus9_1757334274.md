# Exodus 9 — The LORD warns Pharaoh before the hail falls

**About this chapter**

Pharaoh still refuses to let the Israelites go.

Before a great storm, God gives people time to find shelter.

## A disease strikes the animals

## Exodus 9:1

The LORD sent Moses to Pharaoh with this message.

“The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says, ‘Let my people go to worship me.

## Exodus 9:2

If you keep holding them here and refuse to let them go,

## Exodus 9:3

I will send a terrible disease among your animals in the fields.

It will strike your horses, donkeys, camels, herds, and flocks.

## Exodus 9:4

I will protect the animals that belong to the Israelites.

None of their animals will die.’”

## Exodus 9:5

The LORD set the time. He said, “I will do this tomorrow.”

## Exodus 9:6

The next day the LORD did what he had said.

All the Egyptians’ livestock died. Every animal belonging to the Israelites lived.

## Exodus 9:7

Pharaoh sent officials to find out what had happened.

None of the Israelites’ animals had died.

Even so, Pharaoh stayed stubborn. He would not let the people go.

## Sores break out on people and animals

## Exodus 9:8

The LORD told Moses and Aaron, “Take handfuls of soot from the furnace.

Moses, throw it into the air while Pharaoh watches.

## Exodus 9:9

It will become fine dust over all Egypt.

Painful sores called boils will break out on people and animals.”

## Exodus 9:10

Moses and Aaron took soot and stood before Pharaoh.

Moses threw it into the air.

Boils broke out on people and animals.

## Exodus 9:11

The magicians had boils like the other Egyptians.

They could not stand before Moses.

## Exodus 9:12

The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart. Pharaoh refused to listen to Moses and Aaron.

The LORD had told Moses this would happen.

**Pause and answer**

Why could the magicians no longer stand before Moses?

## The LORD warns Pharaoh about hail

## Exodus 9:13

The LORD told Moses, “Get up early. Go and stand before Pharaoh.

Give him this message from the LORD, the God of the Hebrews.

‘Let my people go and worship me.

## Exodus 9:14

If you refuse, I will send all my plagues against you, your officials, and your people.

Then you will know that nobody on earth is like me.

## Exodus 9:15

I could already have struck you and your people with a plague.

It could have wiped you off the earth.

## Exodus 9:16

I raised you up to show you my power.

I want people everywhere to speak of my name.

## Exodus 9:17

Still you use your power to hold my people here.

## Exodus 9:18

At this time tomorrow I will send hail.

Egypt has never seen hail this bad in all its history.

## Exodus 9:19

Give the order now. Bring your animals and everything in the fields into shelter.

Any person or animal still outside in the fields will die when the hail falls.’”

**Reading help**

Hail is ice that falls from the sky during a storm.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD tell Pharaoh to do before the storm?

## Some officials take the warning seriously

## Exodus 9:20

Some of Pharaoh’s officials feared what the LORD had said.

They hurried their servants and animals into shelter.

## Exodus 9:21

Other officials ignored the LORD’s warning.

They left their servants and animals in the fields.

## Exodus 9:22

The LORD told Moses, “Reach your hand toward the sky.

Hail will fall across Egypt on people, animals, and growing plants.”

## Exodus 9:23

Moses raised his staff toward the sky.

The LORD sent thunder and hail. Lightning struck the ground.

## Exodus 9:24

Lightning kept flashing as the hail fell.

Since Egypt became a nation, it had never seen such a storm.

## Exodus 9:25

Across Egypt the hail struck down people and animals in the fields.

It flattened the plants and stripped the trees bare.

## Exodus 9:26

Only Goshen, where the Israelites lived, had no hail.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the Israelites live during the storm?

## Pharaoh asks Moses to pray

## Exodus 9:27

Pharaoh sent for Moses and Aaron.

He said, “This time I have sinned. The LORD has done what is right.

My people and I have done wrong.

## Exodus 9:28

Pray to the LORD. We have had enough of God’s thunder and hail.

I will let you go. You need not stay any longer.”

## Exodus 9:29

Moses said, “When I leave the city, I will lift my hands to the LORD.

The thunder and hail will stop.

Then you will know the earth belongs to the LORD.

## Exodus 9:30

But I know you and your officials still do not fear the LORD our God.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Pharaoh promise Moses and Aaron?

## The storm ends

## Exodus 9:31

The hail destroyed the barley and flax.

The barley was ripe, and the flax plants had flowers.

## Exodus 9:32

The wheat and spelt survived. These grain crops grow later.

## Exodus 9:33

Moses left Pharaoh and went outside the city.

He spread out his hands to the LORD.

The thunder, hail, and pouring rain stopped.

## Exodus 9:34

Pharaoh saw that the storm was over. He sinned again.

He and his officials chose to stay stubborn.

## Exodus 9:35

Pharaoh refused to let the Israelites go.

It happened just as the LORD had said through Moses.
