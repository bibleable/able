# Exodus 32 — The gold calf and Moses’ prayer

**About this chapter**

While Moses is on the mountain, the people gather around a gold calf. Moses asks the LORD to spare them. Then he goes down to see what has happened.

## The people ask Aaron for gods

## Exodus 32:1

Moses stayed on the mountain a long time.

The people gathered around Aaron.

They said, “Make us gods to lead us.

Moses brought us out of Egypt.

We do not know what has happened to him.”

## Exodus 32:2

Aaron said, “Take the gold earrings from your wives, sons and daughters.

Bring the earrings to me.”

## Exodus 32:3

All the people took off their gold earrings.

They brought them to Aaron.

## Exodus 32:4

Aaron took the gold and melted it.

He shaped it into a calf with a tool.

The people said, “Israel, these are your gods!

They brought you out of Egypt!”

**Reading help**

A calf is a young cow or bull. Here the people bowed to a figure made of gold.

## Exodus 32:5

Aaron saw this and built an altar in front of the calf.

He announced, “Tomorrow we will hold a feast for the LORD.”

## Exodus 32:6

The next day the people got up and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.

They sat down to eat and drink.

Then they got up for a wild celebration.

**Pause and answer**

What did Aaron make from the gold earrings?

## The LORD speaks to Moses

## Exodus 32:7

The LORD told Moses, “Go down at once.

Your people, whom you led out of Egypt, have done great wrong.

## Exodus 32:8

They have already turned away from my commands.

They made a calf out of melted gold.

They bowed down and offered sacrifices to it.

They said, ‘Israel, these are your gods who brought you out of Egypt!’”

## Exodus 32:9

The LORD said to Moses, “I have seen how stubborn these people are.

**Reading help**

The Bible calls the people “stiff-necked.” This describes people who refuse to change their ways.

## Exodus 32:10

Now leave me alone.

I am so angry that I will destroy them.

Then I will make a great nation from your family.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the LORD tell Moses to go down?

## Moses asks the LORD to spare Israel

## Exodus 32:11

Moses pleaded with the LORD his God.

“LORD, why are you so angry with your people?

You used your great power to bring them out of Egypt.

## Exodus 32:12

Why should the Egyptians say you meant to harm them?

They will say you took them into the mountains to kill them all.

Please turn from your anger.

Spare your people.

## Exodus 32:13

Remember your servants Abraham, Isaac and Israel.

You gave them your word and swore by your own name.

You said, ‘Your descendants will be as many as the stars.

I will give them all the land I promised.

It will be theirs forever.’”

**Reading help**

Israel was another name for Jacob. Here Moses names Jacob alongside his father Isaac and his grandfather Abraham.

## Exodus 32:14

Then the LORD decided to spare his people the disaster he had spoken of.

**Pause and answer**

What promise did Moses ask the LORD to remember?

## Moses comes down the mountain

## Exodus 32:15

Moses turned and went down the mountain.

He carried the two stone tablets called the Testimony.

There was writing on the front and back of each stone.

## Exodus 32:16

God himself had made the tablets.

God himself had carved the words into the stone.

## Exodus 32:17

Joshua heard the people shouting.

He told Moses, “It sounds like a battle in the camp.”

## Exodus 32:18

Moses said, “I hear no shouts of victory.

I hear no cries of defeat.

I hear singing.”

## Exodus 32:19

Near the camp, Moses saw the calf and the people dancing.

Moses became very angry and threw down the tablets.

They broke at the foot of the mountain.

## Exodus 32:20

Moses burned the calf and ground it into powder.

He scattered the powder on the water.

Then he made the Israelites drink it.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses do with the stone tablets when he saw the calf?

## Aaron tells his story

## Exodus 32:21

Moses asked Aaron, “What did these people do to you?

Why did you lead them into such a great sin?”

## Exodus 32:22

Aaron replied, “Please do not be angry, my lord.

You know how determined these people are to do wrong.

## Exodus 32:23

They told me, ‘Make us gods to lead us.

Moses brought us out of Egypt.

We do not know what has happened to him.’

## Exodus 32:24

I told everyone who had gold to take it off.

They gave me the gold.

I threw it into the fire, and out came this calf!”

| Who tells about the calf? | What does the reader hear? |
| --- | --- |
| The narrator in verse 4 | Aaron shaped the gold with a tool. |
| Aaron in verse 24 | Aaron says the calf came out of the fire. |

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how did Aaron’s account differ from the narrator’s account?

## The Levites gather around Moses

## Exodus 32:25

Moses saw that Aaron had let the people run wild.

Their enemies could laugh at them.

## Exodus 32:26

Moses stood at the entrance to the camp.

He called, “Come to me if you are on the LORD’s side.”

All the Levites gathered around Moses.

## Exodus 32:27

Moses told them what the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded.

The Levites were to take their swords through the camp from gate to gate.

They were to kill even their own brothers, friends and neighbors.

## Exodus 32:28

The Levites did what Moses told them.

About 3,000 people died that day.

## Exodus 32:29

Moses told the Levites, “Today you stood against your own sons and brothers.

Today you have been set apart to serve the LORD.

The LORD has given you his blessing.”

**Pause and answer**

Who gathered around Moses at the camp entrance?

## Moses asks for forgiveness

## Exodus 32:30

The next day Moses told the people, “You have done a great wrong.

I will go back up to the LORD.

Perhaps I can make atonement for your sin.”

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with the sin that has come between people and God. Moses goes to ask God to forgive the people.

## Exodus 32:31

Moses returned to the LORD.

He said, “These people have sinned greatly.

They made gods of gold for themselves.

## Exodus 32:32

Please forgive their sin.

If you will not, erase my name from the book you have written.”

## Exodus 32:33

The LORD told Moses, “I will erase from my book the names of those who have sinned against me.

## Exodus 32:34

Now lead the people to the place I spoke of.

My angel will go ahead of you.

When the time comes, I will punish them for their sin.”

## Exodus 32:35

The LORD sent a plague among the people because of what they had done with the calf Aaron made.

What did Moses ask the LORD to do for the people?
