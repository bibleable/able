# Exodus 10 — Locusts and darkness cover Egypt

**About this chapter**

Moses asks Pharaoh to let the Israelites go and worship.

Pharaoh wants to choose who can go and what they can take.

## Moses warns Pharaoh about locusts

## Exodus 10:1

The LORD told Moses, “Go to Pharaoh.

I have hardened his heart and the hearts of his officials.

I will show them signs of my power.

## Exodus 10:2

Tell your children and grandchildren how severely I dealt with the Egyptians.

Tell them what signs I showed in Egypt.

Then all of you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Exodus 10:3

Moses and Aaron gave Pharaoh a message from the LORD, the God of the Hebrews.

“How long will you refuse to humble yourself before me?

Let my people go so they can worship me.

## Exodus 10:4

If you refuse, I will bring locusts into your land tomorrow.

## Exodus 10:5

They will cover the ground and hide it from sight.

They will eat what the hail left behind.

They will eat the trees growing in your fields.

## Exodus 10:6

They will fill your houses and your officials’ houses.

They will fill the homes of all the Egyptians.

Your fathers and grandfathers never saw anything like this in Egypt.”

Then Moses turned and left Pharaoh.

**Reading help**

Locusts are insects that can travel together in huge groups.

They feed on plants.

## Pharaoh asks who will go

## Exodus 10:7

Pharaoh’s officials asked, “How much longer will we be caught in trouble because of this man?

Let the people go and worship the LORD their God.

Can’t you see that Egypt is already in ruins?”

## Exodus 10:8

Pharaoh had Moses and Aaron brought back.

“Go and worship the LORD your God,” he said.

“Who exactly will go?”

## Exodus 10:9

Moses answered, “All of us, young and old, will go.

We will take our sons and daughters, our flocks and herds.

We must hold a feast for the LORD.”

## Exodus 10:10

Pharaoh said, “The LORD had better be with you if I ever let your children go too!

It is clear you are planning something evil.

## Exodus 10:11

Only the men may go and worship the LORD.

That is what you have been asking for.”

Pharaoh had Moses and Aaron driven away.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Pharaoh say could go this time?

## The wind brings locusts

## Exodus 10:12

The LORD told Moses, “Stretch out your hand over Egypt.

Locusts will come and eat every plant the hail has left.”

## Exodus 10:13

Moses held his staff out over Egypt.

The LORD sent a wind from the east all day and all night.

By morning it had brought the locusts.

## Exodus 10:14

Locusts settled everywhere in Egypt.

Never before had there been so many locusts.

Never again would there be so many.

## Exodus 10:15

There were so many that the ground looked black.

They ate all the plants and fruit the hail had left.

Nothing green remained on any tree or plant in Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

What brought the locusts to Egypt?

## Pharaoh asks Moses to pray

## Exodus 10:16

Pharaoh hurried to call Moses and Aaron back.

“I have sinned against the LORD your God and against you,” he said.

## Exodus 10:17

“Please forgive me one more time. Pray to the LORD your God.

Ask him to take this deadly trouble away from me.”

## Exodus 10:18

Moses left Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD.

## Exodus 10:19

The LORD changed the wind.

A strong wind from the west blew the locusts into the Red Sea.

Not one locust remained in Egypt.

## Exodus 10:20

But the LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart.

Pharaoh still refused to let the Israelites go.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the west wind carry the locusts?

## Three days of darkness

## Exodus 10:21

The LORD told Moses, “Reach your hand toward the sky.

Darkness will cover Egypt, so thick people can feel it.”

## Exodus 10:22

Moses reached his hand toward the sky.

For three days Egypt was completely dark.

## Exodus 10:23

People could not see each other. For three days nobody moved from their place.

But the Israelites had light in their homes.

The Israelites could see inside their homes during the three days of darkness.

## Moses says all the animals must go

## Exodus 10:24

Pharaoh called Moses. “Go and worship the LORD,” he said.

“Take your children too. Leave your flocks and herds here.”

## Exodus 10:25

Moses answered, “You must also give us animals to offer to the LORD our God.

We need animals for sacrifices and burnt offerings.

## Exodus 10:26

All our animals must go with us.

We must take every last one because we need some for worship.

Only when we arrive will we know how to worship the LORD.”

## Exodus 10:27

The LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart. Pharaoh refused to let the people go.

## Exodus 10:28

“Get out!” Pharaoh told Moses. “Never come before me again.

If I see you here again, you will die.”

## Exodus 10:29

Moses answered, “It will be as you say.

I will never come before you again.”
