# Exodus 3 — God calls Moses from a burning bush

**About this chapter**

Moses stops to look at a burning bush.

God calls him by name and sends him to Egypt.

## Moses stops to look

## Exodus 3:1

Moses cared for a flock that belonged to Jethro.

Jethro was his wife’s father and the priest of Midian.

Moses led the flock to the far side of the wilderness.

There he came to Horeb, the mountain of God.

## Exodus 3:2

The angel of the LORD appeared to Moses there.

Flames rose from inside a bush.

Moses watched. The bush was on fire, yet it did not burn up.

![Flames rise among the branches of a living bush.](../pictures/moses-sees-burning-bush.svg)

Moses stopped because the bush kept burning without turning to ashes.

## Exodus 3:3

Moses thought, “I must go closer and see this strange sight.

Why does the bush keep burning?”

## Exodus 3:4

The LORD saw Moses turn aside to look.

God called from the bush, “Moses! Moses!”

“Here I am,” Moses answered.

## Exodus 3:5

God said, “Stay where you are. Take off your sandals.

The ground where you stand is holy.”

**Reading help**

Holy ground is a place set apart for God. Here God was meeting with Moses.

## Exodus 3:6

God said, “I am the God of your father.

I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”

Moses hid his face. He was afraid to look at God.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Moses stop to look at the bush?

## God hears his people

## Exodus 3:7

The LORD said, “I have seen my people suffer in Egypt.

I have heard them cry because of those who hurt them.

I know their pain.

## Exodus 3:8

I have come down to rescue them from the Egyptians.

I will take them to a good land with plenty of room.

It is a land flowing with milk and honey.

The Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites live there.

**Reading help**

“Flowing with milk and honey” pictures a land with plenty of good food.

## Exodus 3:9

I have heard the Israelites cry out.

I have seen how cruelly the Egyptians treat them.

## Exodus 3:10

Now go. I am sending you to Pharaoh, the king.

Bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt.”

**Pause and answer**

What did God send Moses to do?

## Moses asks who sent him

## Exodus 3:11

Moses asked God, “Who am I to go to Pharaoh?

How can I bring the Israelites out of Egypt?”

## Exodus 3:12

God answered, “I will be with you.

Here is how you will know I sent you.

After you bring the people out of Egypt, you will all worship God on this mountain.”

## Exodus 3:13

Moses asked, “Suppose I tell the Israelites, ‘The God of your fathers sent me.’

What if they ask me his name? What should I tell them?”

## Exodus 3:14

God said, “I AM WHO I AM.

Tell the Israelites, ‘I AM has sent me to you.’”

## Exodus 3:15

God also said, “Tell them, ‘The LORD sent me to you.

He is the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’

This is my name forever.

Each generation is to remember me by this name.”

**Pause and answer**

What name did God tell Moses to use in verse 14?

## God gives Moses a message for the leaders

## Exodus 3:16

God said, “Gather the elders, the leaders of Israel.

Tell them that the LORD appeared to you.

I am the God of their fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

Tell them I have watched over them.

I have seen what the Egyptians have done to them.

## Exodus 3:17

I promise to bring them out of their suffering in Egypt.

I will take them to the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites.

The Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites also live there.

It is a land flowing with milk and honey.

## Exodus 3:18

The elders will listen to you.

Go with them to the king of Egypt.

Say, ‘The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met with us.

Please let us travel three days into the wilderness.

There we will give sacrifices to the LORD our God.’”

**Reading help**

A sacrifice is an offering people give to God.

| Who hears the message? | What Moses will tell them |
| --- | --- |
| The elders of Israel | God has seen their suffering and promises to rescue them. |
| Pharaoh | Let the people go into the wilderness to worship. |

**Pause and answer**

Who will go with Moses to speak to the king?

## God tells Moses what will happen in Egypt

## Exodus 3:19

God continued, “I know the king will refuse to let you go.

Only great power will make him let you leave.

## Exodus 3:20

I will reach out my hand against the Egyptians.

They will see the mighty things I do among them.

Then the king will let you go.

## Exodus 3:21

I will make the Egyptians willing to give my people gifts.

When you leave, you will have things to take with you.

## Exodus 3:22

Each woman will ask her neighbor for silver and gold jewelry and clothes.

She will also ask any woman staying in her home.

Put these things on your sons and daughters.

In this way you will carry away the Egyptians’ wealth.”

**Pause and answer**

What will the Israelites carry away from Egypt?
