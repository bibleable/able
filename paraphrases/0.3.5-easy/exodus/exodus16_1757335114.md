# Exodus 16 — Bread in the desert

**About this chapter**

The Israelites are hungry. The LORD gives them food and teaches them when to gather it.

## The people ask for food

## Exodus 16:1

All the Israelites left Elim.

They reached the Desert of Sin, between Elim and Sinai.

It was day 15 of the second month since they left Egypt.

## Exodus 16:2

There in the desert, everyone complained to Moses and Aaron.

## Exodus 16:3

The people said, “If only the LORD had let us die in Egypt!

There we sat beside pots of meat.

We ate all the bread we wanted.

You brought us into this desert.

Now all of us will starve!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people miss about Egypt?

## The LORD promises bread

## Exodus 16:4

The LORD told Moses, “I will send bread from heaven like rain.

Each day the people must gather enough for that day.

This will show whether they follow my instructions.

## Exodus 16:5

On day six, they will prepare what they gather.

They will have twice as much as usual.”

## Exodus 16:6

Moses and Aaron told the Israelites, “This evening you will know who brought you out of Egypt.

It was the LORD.

## Exodus 16:7

In the morning you will see the LORD’s glory.

The LORD has heard your complaints against him.

Why complain to us?

Who are we?”

**Reading help**

The LORD’s glory is his greatness. Here the people will see a sign of it.

## Exodus 16:8

Moses added, “The LORD will give you meat this evening.

In the morning he will give you enough bread to fill you.

The LORD has heard your complaints.

Who are we?

When you complain to us, you are complaining against the LORD.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses say the people would eat that evening?

## A cloud appears

## Exodus 16:9

Moses told Aaron, “Call all the Israelites together.

Tell them to come before the LORD.

He has heard their complaints.”

## Exodus 16:10

While Aaron spoke, the people looked toward the desert.

They saw the LORD’s glory appear in a cloud.

## Exodus 16:11

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 16:12

“I have heard the Israelites complain.

Tell them, ‘As daylight fades, you will eat meat.

In the morning you will have plenty of bread.

Then you will know that I am the LORD your God.’”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people see the LORD’s glory?

## Food around the camp

## Exodus 16:13

That evening, birds called quail covered the camp.

In the morning, tiny drops of water covered the ground around the camp.

These drops are called dew.

## Exodus 16:14

When the dew dried up, thin flakes lay on the ground.

The flakes were as fine as frost.

## Exodus 16:15

The Israelites asked each other, “What is it?”

They had never seen it before.

Moses said, “This is the bread the LORD has given you to eat.

## Exodus 16:16

The LORD says to gather as much as you need.

Take one measure for each person in your tent.”

That measure was called an omer.

**Pause and answer**

How much bread could they gather for each person?

## Enough bread for each person

## Exodus 16:17

The Israelites gathered the bread.

Some gathered more.

Others gathered less.

## Exodus 16:18

Then they measured the bread in omers.

Those who gathered more had none left over.

Those who gathered less still had enough.

Each person had enough to eat.

## Exodus 16:19

Moses told them, “Keep none of it until morning.”

## Exodus 16:20

Some people ignored Moses.

They kept some bread until morning.

Maggots grew in it, and it smelled bad.

Moses was angry with those people.

**Reading help**

Maggots are the tiny, worm-like young of flies.

## Exodus 16:21

Each morning the people gathered as much as they needed.

When the sun grew hot, the flakes melted away.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people gather bread in the morning?

## Bread for two days

## Exodus 16:22

On day six, they gathered twice as much bread.

They had two omers for each person.

All the leaders came and told Moses.

## Exodus 16:23

Moses said, “The LORD has set tomorrow apart.

It is the Sabbath, a day of complete rest for the LORD.

Bake what you want to bake today.

Boil what you want to boil.

Keep the rest until morning.”

## Exodus 16:24

They kept the bread until morning, as Moses had said.

This time it still smelled good.

They found no maggots in it.

## Exodus 16:25

Moses said, “Eat this bread today.

Today is a Sabbath for the LORD.

You will find no bread on the ground today.

## Exodus 16:26

You can gather bread for six days.

On day seven, the Sabbath, there will be none.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people eat on the Sabbath?

## The people rest

## Exodus 16:27

On day seven, some people went to gather bread.

They found nothing.

## Exodus 16:28

The LORD asked Moses, “How long will you people refuse to follow my commands and instructions?

## Exodus 16:29

The LORD has given you the Sabbath.

That is why he gives you two days’ bread on day six.

On day seven, everyone must stay where they are.”

## Exodus 16:30

So the people rested on day seven.

| Which days? | How much bread? | What did the LORD tell the people to do? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1 to 5 | One omer each day per person | Gather enough for today. |
| Day 6 | Two omers per person | Save enough for tomorrow. |
| Day 7 | No bread to gather | Eat the saved bread and rest. |

**Pause and answer**

Using the table, explain why the people gathered extra bread on day six.

## A jar for the children to see

## Exodus 16:31

The Israelites called the bread manna.

It was white and looked like coriander seed.

It tasted like thin cakes made with honey.

## Exodus 16:32

Moses said, “The LORD commands us to keep one omer of manna.

He wants our children and their children to see it.

This is the bread he gave us in the desert when he brought us out of Egypt.”

## Exodus 16:33

Moses told Aaron, “Put one omer of manna in a jar.

Place it before the LORD.

Keep it for the children who come after us.”

## Exodus 16:34

Aaron placed the jar in front of the Testimony.

He kept it there as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**Reading help**

The Testimony was the name for the stone tablets with God’s commands.

## Exodus 16:35

The Israelites ate manna for 40 years.

They ate it until they reached the border of Canaan.

There they came to land where they could settle.

## Exodus 16:36

Ten omers make one ephah.

Both were measures the Israelites used.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Aaron keep a jar of manna?
