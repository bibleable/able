# Exodus 28 — Clothes for Aaron and his sons

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses what the priests will wear. Aaron will carry the names of Israel’s tribes on his shoulders and over his heart.

## God calls Aaron and his sons

## Exodus 28:1

The LORD told Moses, “Call your brother Aaron from among the Israelites.

Call his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar too.

They will serve me as priests.

## Exodus 28:2

Make holy clothes for Aaron.

These beautiful clothes will show his place of honor.

## Exodus 28:3

I have filled skilled workers with wisdom.

Tell them to make Aaron’s clothes.

These clothes will help set Aaron apart to serve me as a priest.

## Exodus 28:4

Have the workers make a breastpiece, an ephod and a robe.

They must also weave a tunic and make a turban and a sash.

Make holy clothes for Aaron and his sons to serve me as priests.

**Reading help**

The ephod was a garment worn over the robe. The breastpiece covered the chest. A tunic was a long shirt. A turban was a cloth head covering. A sash tied around the waist.

## Exodus 28:5

The workers must use gold and fine linen.

They must use blue, purple and bright red yarn.

## The ephod and its shoulder pieces

## Exodus 28:6

Make the ephod from fine linen thread.

Stitch designs into it with gold and with blue, purple and bright red yarn.

## Exodus 28:7

Attach two shoulder pieces at two corners.

Use them to fasten the ephod.

## Exodus 28:8

Weave the waistband and the ephod as one piece.

Use the same fine work and the same materials.

Use gold, colored yarn and fine linen thread.

**Pause and answer**

What held the ephod together at the shoulders?

## Two stones with twelve names

## Exodus 28:9

Take two onyx stones.

Carve the names of Israel’s sons into them.

## Exodus 28:10

Put six names on each stone.

Write the names in birth order.

## Exodus 28:11

Carve the names the way a worker carves a seal.

Fit both stones into holders made of fine gold wire.

**Reading help**

A seal had a carved name or design. Pressing it into soft material left a mark.

## Exodus 28:12

Fasten one stone to each shoulder piece of the ephod.

Aaron will carry the names on his shoulders.

The stones will be a reminder of Israel’s sons before the LORD.

## Exodus 28:13

Make holders from fine gold wire.

## Exodus 28:14

Braid two chains of pure gold like cords.

Attach the chains to the gold holders.

**Pause and answer**

Whose names did Aaron carry on his shoulders?

## Four rows of stones

## Exodus 28:15

Make a breastpiece for seeking God’s decisions.

Make it with the same skill as the ephod.

Use gold, fine linen and blue, purple and bright red yarn.

## Exodus 28:16

Fold the cloth double to make a square.

Make each side one handspan long.

**Reading help**

A handspan is the distance from thumb to little finger when the hand is stretched wide.

## Exodus 28:17

Set precious stones on it in four rows.

In row one, place a ruby, a topaz and an emerald.

## Exodus 28:18

In row two, place a turquoise, a sapphire and a diamond.

## Exodus 28:19

In row three, place a jacinth, an agate and an amethyst.

## Exodus 28:20

In row four, place a beryl, an onyx and a jasper.

Fit the stones into holders made of fine gold wire.

## Exodus 28:21

Use one stone for each of Israel’s twelve sons.

On each stone carve the name of one tribe.

Carve it the way a worker carves a seal.

| Row | First stone | Second stone | Third stone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ruby | Topaz | Emerald |
| 2 | Turquoise | Sapphire | Diamond |
| 3 | Jacinth | Agate | Amethyst |
| 4 | Beryl | Onyx | Jasper |

## Fasten the breastpiece

## Exodus 28:22

For the breastpiece, braid pure gold into chains like cords.

## Exodus 28:23

Make two gold rings.

Attach one to each of two corners of the breastpiece.

## Exodus 28:24

Fasten the two gold chains to those rings.

## Exodus 28:25

Fasten the other ends to the two gold holders.

Attach these to the front of the ephod’s shoulder pieces.

## Exodus 28:26

Make two more gold rings for the other two corners of the breastpiece.

Put them on the inside edge, next to the ephod.

## Exodus 28:27

Make another pair of gold rings.

Attach them low on the front of the shoulder pieces.

Put them near the seam, just above the woven waistband.

## Exodus 28:28

Tie the breastpiece rings to the ephod rings with blue cord.

This holds the breastpiece above the waistband.

It keeps the breastpiece from swinging away from the ephod.

**Pause and answer**

What kept the breastpiece from swinging?

## What Aaron carries over his heart

## Exodus 28:29

Aaron will wear the breastpiece whenever he enters the Holy Place.

Over his heart, he will carry the names of Israel’s sons.

Those names will always be a reminder before the LORD.

## Exodus 28:30

Put the Urim and Thummim inside the breastpiece.

Aaron will carry them over his heart before the LORD.

In this way he will always bring Israel’s decisions before the LORD.

**Reading help**

The Urim and Thummim were objects the priest used to seek God’s decision. “Over his heart” tells us where Aaron wore them, on his chest.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Aaron carry the Urim and Thummim?

## The blue robe and its bells

## Exodus 28:31

Make the robe for the ephod entirely of blue cloth.

## Exodus 28:32

Leave an opening for the head at the top center.

Weave a collar around the opening to keep it from tearing.

## Exodus 28:33

Make fruit shapes called pomegranates from blue, purple and bright red yarn.

Put them around the robe’s bottom edge.

Hang gold bells between them.

## Exodus 28:34

Follow the same pattern all around the bottom edge.

Put one gold bell, then one yarn pomegranate.

## Exodus 28:35

Aaron must wear the robe when he serves.

The bells will sound when he enters or leaves the holy place before the LORD.

In this way Aaron will stay alive.

**Pause and answer**

What made a sound when Aaron entered the holy place?

## The words on Aaron’s forehead

## Exodus 28:36

Make a plate of pure gold.

Carve these words into it as on a seal.

HOLY TO THE LORD.

## Exodus 28:37

Fasten the plate to a blue cord.

Put it on the front of the turban.

## Exodus 28:38

Aaron must always wear the plate on his forehead.

He will bear the guilt for anything wrong with the holy gifts the Israelites offer.

In this way the LORD can accept their gifts.

## Exodus 28:39

Weave the tunic from fine linen.

Make the turban from fine linen too.

Stitch a design into the sash.

**Pause and answer**

What words appeared on Aaron’s forehead?

## Clothes for the priests

## Exodus 28:40

Make tunics, sashes and headbands for Aaron’s sons.

These beautiful clothes will show their place of honor.

## Exodus 28:41

Dress your brother Aaron and his sons in these clothes.

Anoint them with oil.

Give them their duties and set them apart for me.

They will serve me as priests.

## Exodus 28:42

Make linen underclothes for them.

These must cover their bodies from waist to thigh.

## Exodus 28:43

Aaron and his sons must wear the underclothes when they enter the Tent of Meeting.

They must also wear them when they approach the altar to serve in the Holy Place.

Then they will avoid guilt and stay alive.

Aaron and his descendants must always follow this rule.”
