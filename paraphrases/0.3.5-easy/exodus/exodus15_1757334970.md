# Exodus 15 — A song by the sea

**About this chapter**

Moses and the Israelites sing after crossing the sea. Miriam takes up the song. Then Moses leads the people into the desert.

## Moses and the people sing

## Exodus 15:1

Moses and the Israelites sang to the LORD.

“I will sing to the LORD.

He deserves the highest praise.

He threw horse and rider into the sea.

## Exodus 15:2

The LORD makes me strong.

He gives me a song to sing.

He has saved me.

He is my God, and I will praise him.

He is my father’s God, and I will honor him.

## Exodus 15:3

The LORD fights as a warrior.

His name is the LORD.

## Exodus 15:4

He threw Pharaoh’s chariots and army into the sea.

Pharaoh’s best officers drowned in the Red Sea.

## Exodus 15:5

Deep water covered them.

They sank like a stone.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the people sing to?

## The song tells how the water moved

## Exodus 15:6

LORD, how powerful your right hand is!

LORD, your right hand crushed the enemy.

## Exodus 15:7

In your greatness you brought down those who fought you.

Your anger burned like fire.

It swept them away like dry stalks in a field.

## Exodus 15:8

You blew through your nose, and the waters piled up.

The rushing water stood like a wall.

Even the deep water stood still in the middle of the sea.

**Reading help**

The song calls the wind the LORD’s breath. The wind pushed the water back.

## Exodus 15:9

The enemy said, ‘I will chase them.

I will catch them.

I will share out what I take from them.

I will take all I want.

I will pull out my sword and destroy them.’

## Exodus 15:10

Then you blew, and the sea covered them.

They sank like heavy metal in the great waters.

**Pause and answer**

What covered the riders?

## The song praises the LORD

## Exodus 15:11

LORD, which of the gods is like you?

Who is holy and great like you?

Who else fills us with wonder as we sing praise?

Who else does such amazing things?

## Exodus 15:12

You reached out your right hand.

The earth swallowed them up.

**Reading help**

“The earth swallowed them up” is a picture in the song. The soldiers sank and disappeared.

## Exodus 15:13

You set your people free.

Your love for them holds firm.

You will lead them with that love.

With your strength, you will guide them to your holy home.

**Pause and answer**

Who will guide the people to their new home?

## The song looks ahead

## Exodus 15:14

Other nations will hear and shake with fear.

The people of Philistia will be afraid and in pain.

## Exodus 15:15

The chiefs of Edom will lose courage.

The leaders of Moab will shake with fear.

The people of Canaan will lose all their courage.

## Exodus 15:16

Fear will come over them.

Your arm is powerful.

They will stand as still as a stone until your people pass by, LORD.

They will stand still until the people you bought pass by.

## Exodus 15:17

You will bring your people to your own mountain.

You will plant them there.

LORD, you prepared that place as your home.

Lord, your own hands made that holy place.

**Reading help**

To “plant” people means to give them a lasting home. Think of a tree growing roots in one place.

## Exodus 15:18

The LORD will rule forever!”

**Pause and answer**

How long will the LORD rule?

## Miriam leads the women in song

## Exodus 15:19

Pharaoh’s horses, chariots and riders had gone into the sea.

The LORD brought the water back over them.

The Israelites had walked through on dry ground.

## Exodus 15:20

Aaron’s sister Miriam was a prophet.

She took a tambourine in her hand.

All the women followed her, playing tambourines and dancing.

**Reading help**

A prophet speaks to people for God. A tambourine is a small instrument you hold and tap or shake.

## Exodus 15:21

Miriam sang back to them, “Sing to the LORD.

He deserves the highest praise.

He threw horse and rider into the sea.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Miriam hold as she led the women?

## The people look for water

## Exodus 15:22

Moses led the Israelites away from the Red Sea.

They entered the Desert of Shur.

They walked for three days without finding water.

## Exodus 15:23

At Marah they found water.

It tasted so bitter that they could not drink it.

That is why the place was called Marah.

Marah means bitter.

## Exodus 15:24

The people complained to Moses.

They asked, “What can we drink?”

**Pause and answer**

Why could the people at Marah not drink the water?

## The LORD answers Moses

## Exodus 15:25

Moses called to the LORD for help.

The LORD showed Moses a log.

Moses threw the log into the water.

Then the water tasted good.

There the LORD gave the people rules to follow.

There he also tested them.

## Exodus 15:26

The LORD said, “Listen carefully to me, the LORD your God.

Do what I see is right.

Pay attention to my commands.

Keep all my rules.

If you do this, I will spare you the diseases I brought on the Egyptians.

I am the LORD who heals you.”

## Exodus 15:27

Then the Israelites came to Elim.

There they found 12 springs of water and 70 palm trees.

They camped there beside the water.

**Follow the journey**

| Place | What the people found |
| --- | --- |
| Desert of Shur | No water for three days |
| Marah | Bitter water that became good to drink |
| Elim | Springs and palm trees |

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how did the people at Marah get water they could drink?
