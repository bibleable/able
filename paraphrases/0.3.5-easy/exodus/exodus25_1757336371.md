# Exodus 25 — Gifts for God’s tent

**About this chapter**

The LORD shows Moses how to make a holy tent. The people can bring gifts to build it. Inside will be a chest, a table and a lampstand.

## Gifts the people can bring

## Exodus 25:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 25:2

“Ask the Israelites to bring me gifts.

Accept a gift from everyone who wants to give.

## Exodus 25:3

Accept these metals from them.

Take gold, silver and bronze.

## Exodus 25:4

Take blue, purple and bright red yarn.

Take fine linen cloth and goat hair.

## Exodus 25:5

Take ram skins dyed red.

Take fine leather and wood from acacia trees.

## Exodus 25:6

Take olive oil for the lamps.

Take spices for the special anointing oil and the sweet-smelling incense.

## Exodus 25:7

Take onyx and other precious stones.

Attach the stones to the priest’s ephod and breastpiece.

**Reading help**

People poured anointing oil on someone or something to set it apart for God. Incense gives off a scent when burned. The ephod was clothing a priest wore. The breastpiece lay over his chest.

**Pause and answer**

Who could bring a gift?

## A tent and a chest

## Exodus 25:8

Have the people make me a holy place.

Then I will live among them.

## Exodus 25:9

Make the holy tent, called the tabernacle.

Make the things that go inside it too.

Follow the pattern I show you for everything.

## Exodus 25:10

Have them make a chest from acacia wood.

This chest is called the ark.

Make it two and a half cubits long.

Make it one and a half cubits wide and just as high.

**Reading help**

A cubit was a measure about as long as a forearm, from elbow to fingertip.

## Exodus 25:11

Cover the ark with pure gold, inside and out.

Add a strip of gold trim around it.

## Exodus 25:12

Cast four gold rings and attach them to its four feet.

Put two rings on each side.

**Pause and answer**

What covered the inside of the ark?

## Poles for carrying the ark

## Exodus 25:13

Make poles from acacia wood.

Cover the poles with gold.

## Exodus 25:14

Slide the poles through the rings on the sides.

Use the poles to carry the ark.

## Exodus 25:15

Leave the poles in the rings.

Never take them out.

## Exodus 25:16

I will give you the Testimony.

Place it inside the ark.

**Reading help**

The Testimony was the name for the stone tablets with God’s commands.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the carrying poles stay?

## A gold cover for the ark

## Exodus 25:17

Make a cover of pure gold called the mercy seat.

Make it two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.

## Exodus 25:18

Hammer the gold into two figures called cherubim.

Put them at the ends of the mercy seat.

**Reading help**

Cherubim are heavenly beings with wings. Here the figures show their shape.

## Exodus 25:19

Make one figure at each end.

Shape both figures and the cover from one piece of gold.

## Exodus 25:20

Spread their wings upward over the mercy seat.

Have the two figures face each other.

Their faces should look down toward the cover.

## Exodus 25:21

Put the Testimony I give you inside the ark.

Place the mercy seat on top.

## Exodus 25:22

I will meet you above the mercy seat.

I will speak between the two cherubim above the ark.

There I will give you my commands for Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the LORD say he would meet Moses?

## A table with carrying poles

## Exodus 25:23

Make a table from acacia wood.

Make it two cubits long and one cubit wide.

Make it one and a half cubits high.

## Exodus 25:24

Cover the table with pure gold.

Add gold trim around it.

## Exodus 25:25

Make a rim around the table.

Make it as wide as a hand.

Add gold trim to the rim.

## Exodus 25:26

Make four gold rings for the table.

Attach one at each corner, by each leg.

## Exodus 25:27

Place the rings close to the rim.

They will hold the poles for carrying the table.

## Exodus 25:28

Make the carrying poles from acacia wood.

Cover them with gold.

Use them to carry the table.

**Pause and answer**

What held the carrying poles on the table?

## Bread on the table

## Exodus 25:29

Make plates and dishes.

Make pitchers and bowls for pouring out drink offerings.

Use pure gold for them all.

## Exodus 25:30

Set the Bread of the Presence on the table.

Keep this bread there before me at all times.

**Pause and answer**

What stayed on the table?

## A lampstand shaped like a flowering plant

## Exodus 25:31

Hammer pure gold into a lampstand.

Use one piece for the base and upright stem.

Shape its cups, buds and petals from that same piece.

## Exodus 25:32

Make six branches reaching out from the lampstand.

Put three on each side.

## Exodus 25:33

Shape three cups like almond flowers on each branch.

Give each flower buds and petals.

Do this on all six branches.

## Exodus 25:34

On the upright stem, make four cups like almond flowers.

Give them buds and petals too.

## Exodus 25:35

Shape a bud below the first pair of branches.

Put another below the second pair.

Put another below the third pair.

## Exodus 25:36

Hammer the buds, branches and lampstand from one piece of pure gold.

| Part of the lampstand | What to picture |
| --- | --- |
| Upright stem | Four flower-shaped cups |
| Branches | Three branches on each side of the stem |
| Each branch | Three flower-shaped cups |
| Below each pair of branches | One bud |

**Pause and answer**

Using the table, describe one branch of the lampstand.

## Seven lamps

## Exodus 25:37

Make seven lamps.

Place them on the stand so they light the space in front.

## Exodus 25:38

Make tools for trimming the lamp wicks.

Make the tools and their trays from pure gold.

## Exodus 25:39

Use one talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all its tools.

**Reading help**

A wick is the part of an oil lamp that burns. A talent was a large measure of weight.

## Exodus 25:40

Make everything just like the pattern I showed you on the mountain.”

**Pause and answer**

Which way were the seven lamps to shine?
