# Exodus 40 — The cloud covers the holy tent

**About this chapter**

Moses sets up the holy tent. When he finishes, a cloud covers it. The Israelites watch the cloud to know when to travel.

## Where each item belongs

## Exodus 40:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 40:2

“On the first day of the first month, set up the holy tent.

This is the Tent of Meeting.

## Exodus 40:3

Put the ark of the Testimony inside.

Hang the inner curtain, called the veil, to hide the ark.

**Reading help**

The ark was a chest. The Testimony was the name for the stone tablets with God’s commands.

## Exodus 40:4

Bring in the table.

Arrange what belongs on it.

Bring in the lampstand and put its lamps in place.

## Exodus 40:5

Put the gold incense altar in front of the ark.

Hang the entrance curtain for the holy tent.

## Exodus 40:6

Put the altar for burnt offerings outside the tent entrance.

## Exodus 40:7

Put the washing bowl between the tent and that altar.

Fill the bowl with water.

## Exodus 40:8

Set up the courtyard around them.

Hang the curtain at the entrance to the courtyard.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the LORD tell Moses to put the washing bowl?

## Set the tent apart

## Exodus 40:9

Take the special oil and anoint the tent and everything inside.

Set the tent and its furnishings apart for me.

They will be holy.

**Reading help**

To anoint means to put oil on someone or something for a special purpose.

## Exodus 40:10

Anoint the altar for burnt offerings and all its tools.

Set the altar apart for me.

It will be most holy.

## Exodus 40:11

Anoint the washing bowl and its stand.

Set them apart for me too.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses use to anoint the tent?

## Prepare the priests

## Exodus 40:12

Bring Aaron and his sons to the tent entrance.

Wash them with water.

## Exodus 40:13

Dress Aaron in the holy clothes.

Anoint him and set him apart to serve me as a priest.

## Exodus 40:14

Bring his sons forward.

Dress them in tunics.

**Reading help**

A tunic was a long shirt.

## Exodus 40:15

Anoint them just as you anointed their father.

Then they too can serve me as priests.

This anointing sets their family apart to serve as priests for generations to come.”

## Exodus 40:16

Moses did everything the LORD told him to do.

**Pause and answer**

What work did the LORD give Aaron and his sons?

## Moses sets up the tent

## Exodus 40:17

Moses set up the holy tent on the first day of the first month of the second year.

## Exodus 40:18

He laid down the bases and stood the frames in place.

He fitted the crossbars and set up the posts.

## Exodus 40:19

Moses spread the outer tent over the frames.

Then he put its covering on top.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

## Exodus 40:20

Moses put the Testimony inside the ark.

He fitted the carrying poles to the ark.

On top he placed the gold cover, called the mercy seat.

## Exodus 40:21

He brought the ark into the holy tent.

He hung the veil to hide the ark from view.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses put inside the ark?

## The table, lamps and gold altar

## Exodus 40:22

Moses put the table on the north side inside the tent.

It stood outside the veil.

## Exodus 40:23

He set the bread on the table before the LORD.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

## Exodus 40:24

Moses set the lampstand on the south side inside the tent.

It stood opposite the table.

## Exodus 40:25

He put the lamps in place before the LORD.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

## Exodus 40:26

Moses placed the gold altar inside the tent, in front of the veil.

## Exodus 40:27

He burned sweet-smelling incense on the gold altar.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

**Pause and answer**

What stood opposite the table?

## The altar and washing bowl outside

## Exodus 40:28

Moses hung the curtain at the tent entrance.

## Exodus 40:29

Moses put the altar for burnt offerings near the tent entrance.

There he offered the burnt offering and the grain offering.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

## Exodus 40:30

He set the washing bowl between the tent and the altar.

He put water in it for washing.

## Exodus 40:31

Moses, Aaron and Aaron’s sons used that water to wash their hands and feet.

## Exodus 40:32

They washed whenever they entered the tent or approached the altar.

They did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Exodus 40:33

Moses set up the courtyard around the tent and altar.

He hung the curtain at the courtyard entrance.

Now Moses had finished the work.

**Pause and answer**

When did Moses, Aaron and Aaron’s sons wash?

## The cloud guides Israel

## Exodus 40:34

Then the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting.

The LORD’s glory filled the holy tent.

## Exodus 40:35

Moses could not enter the tent.

The cloud had settled on it.

The LORD’s glory filled it.

**Reading help**

The LORD’s glory is his greatness. Here the cloud shows the people that the LORD is with them.

## Exodus 40:36

Whenever the cloud lifted from the tent, the Israelites set out.

This happened throughout their journey.

## Exodus 40:37

When the cloud stayed in place, the people stayed in camp.

They waited until the cloud lifted.

## Exodus 40:38

By day the LORD’s cloud rested over the tent.

At night fire glowed within the cloud.

All the Israelites could see it throughout their travels.

| What happened to the cloud? | What did the people do? |
| --- | --- |
| It stayed over the tent. | They stayed in camp. |
| It lifted from the tent. | They set out. |
