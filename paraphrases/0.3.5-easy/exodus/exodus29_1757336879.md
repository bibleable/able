# Exodus 29 — Setting Aaron and his sons apart

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses how to prepare Aaron and his sons to serve as priests. Then he describes the offerings Israel will bring each day.

## Bring the animals and the bread

## Exodus 29:1

The LORD told Moses, “Set Aaron and his sons apart to serve me as priests.

Choose a young bull and two rams without defects.

## Exodus 29:2

Use fine wheat flour to make three kinds of bread.

Make plain loaves, cakes with oil mixed in, and thin wafers brushed with oil.

Make them all without yeast.

## Exodus 29:3

Put the bread in a basket.

Bring the basket, the bull and both rams.

The basket holds the bread that Moses must bring.

## Wash and dress the priests

## Exodus 29:4

Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

Wash them with water.

## Exodus 29:5

Dress Aaron in the tunic and the robe.

Put the ephod over the robe.

Add the breastpiece and fasten the ephod with its woven waistband.

**Reading help**

The ephod was a garment Aaron wore over his robe. The breastpiece covered his chest.

## Exodus 29:6

Put the turban on Aaron’s head.

Fasten the holy gold plate to the turban.

## Exodus 29:7

Pour the special anointing oil on Aaron’s head.

## Exodus 29:8

Bring Aaron’s sons forward.

Dress them in tunics.

## Exodus 29:9

Tie sashes around Aaron and his sons.

Tie on their headbands.

The work of the priests will be theirs by a lasting rule.

This is how you are to give them that work.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses pour on Aaron’s head?

## The bull for the sin offering

## Exodus 29:10

Bring the bull to the front of the Tent of Meeting.

Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head.

## Exodus 29:11

Kill the bull as an offering before the LORD at the tent entrance.

## Exodus 29:12

With your finger, put some blood on the altar’s horns.

Pour the rest at the foot of the altar.

**Reading help**

The altar’s horns were the raised parts at its four corners.

## Exodus 29:13

Take the fat covering the inner parts.

Take the lobe of the liver and both kidneys with their fat.

Burn these on the altar.

## Exodus 29:14

Burn the bull’s meat, skin and dung outside the camp.

The bull is a sin offering.

**Reading help**

A sin offering was a gift brought to God to deal with sin.

**Pause and answer**

Which parts of the bull did Moses burn outside the camp?

## The first ram

## Exodus 29:15

Bring the first ram forward.

Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head.

## Exodus 29:16

Kill the ram.

Splash its blood on every side of the altar.

## Exodus 29:17

Cut the ram into pieces.

Wash its inner parts and legs.

Put them with the head and the other pieces.

## Exodus 29:18

Burn the whole ram on the altar.

This is a burnt offering to the LORD.

The smell of this food offering pleases the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

How much of the first ram went on the altar?

## The second ram

## Exodus 29:19

Bring the second ram forward.

Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head.

## Exodus 29:20

Kill the second ram.

Put some blood on each priest’s right earlobe.

Put some on each right thumb and right big toe.

Splash the rest on every side of the altar.

## Exodus 29:21

Take some blood from the altar and some anointing oil.

Sprinkle them on Aaron, his sons and their clothes.

This sets the priests and their clothes apart for me.

## Exodus 29:22

This ram is for the ceremony that makes them priests.

Take its fat, including its fat tail.

Take the fat covering the inner parts.

Take the lobe of the liver and both kidneys with their fat.

Take the right thigh too.

## Exodus 29:23

From the basket before the LORD, take one loaf, one oil cake and one wafer.

This is the bread made without yeast.

**Pause and answer**

Whose clothes did Moses sprinkle with blood and oil?

## Gifts lifted before the LORD

## Exodus 29:24

Put everything you have just taken into the hands of Aaron and his sons.

Wave these gifts before the LORD.

This is a wave offering.

## Exodus 29:25

Take the gifts from their hands.

Burn them on top of the burnt offering on the altar.

The smell of this food offering pleases the LORD.

## Exodus 29:26

Take the breast of the ram for Aaron’s ceremony.

Wave it before the LORD.

That piece is your share, Moses.

## Exodus 29:27

Set apart the breast and thigh from the ram for Aaron and his sons.

The breast is the part waved before the LORD.

The thigh is the part lifted up before him.

## Exodus 29:28

These pieces will be the priests’ regular share.

The Israelites will lift them up to the LORD whenever they bring peace offerings.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Israelites lift up from their peace offerings for the priests?

## The holy clothes and the meal

## Exodus 29:29

When Aaron’s sons take his place, his holy clothes will be theirs.

They will wear them when they receive the oil and begin their work as priests.

## Exodus 29:30

The son who takes Aaron’s place must wear those clothes for seven days.

He will enter the Tent of Meeting to serve in the Holy Place.

## Exodus 29:31

Boil the meat of the ram for the ceremony in a holy place.

## Exodus 29:32

Aaron and his sons must eat the ram’s meat and the bread from the basket.

They must eat at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

## Exodus 29:33

They must eat the food used to make atonement as they become my priests.

No one else may eat it.

This food is holy.

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with the wrong that stands between people and God. Here the offerings prepared the priests to serve him.

## Exodus 29:34

If meat or bread remains in the morning, burn it.

No one may eat the leftovers because the food is holy.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Aaron and his sons eat the meal?

## Seven days to set the priests apart

## Exodus 29:35

Follow all my commands for Aaron and his sons.

The ceremony to make them priests lasts seven days.

## Exodus 29:36

Each day offer a bull as a sin offering to make atonement.

Make atonement for the altar to make it clean.

Put special oil on the altar to make it holy.

## Exodus 29:37

Make atonement for the altar throughout the seven days.

Set it apart for me.

Then the altar will be most holy.

Anything that touches the altar will be holy.

**Pause and answer**

How long did the ceremony last?

## Offerings every morning and evening

## Exodus 29:38

Every day offer two lambs on the altar.

Each lamb must be one year old.

## Exodus 29:39

Offer one lamb in the morning.

Offer the other as daylight fades.

## Exodus 29:40

With the morning lamb, bring one tenth of an ephah of fine flour.

Mix it with a quarter of a hin of pressed olive oil.

Offer a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering.

**Reading help**

An ephah measured dry food such as flour. A hin measured liquids such as oil and wine.

## Exodus 29:41

At dusk, offer the second lamb.

Bring the same flour, oil and wine as in the morning.

The smell of this food offering pleases the LORD.

| When | Which lamb? | What went with it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | The first lamb | Flour mixed with oil, and wine |
| As daylight faded | The second lamb | The same amounts of flour, oil and wine |

## The LORD will live among his people

## Exodus 29:42

Keep bringing this burnt offering for generations to come.

Bring it before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

There I will meet you and speak to you.

## Exodus 29:43

I will meet the Israelites there too.

My glory will make that place holy.

## Exodus 29:44

I will set apart the Tent of Meeting and the altar.

I will set Aaron and his sons apart to serve me as priests.

## Exodus 29:45

I will live among the Israelites.

I will be their God.

## Exodus 29:46

They will know that I am the LORD their God.

I brought them out of Egypt to live among them.

I am the LORD their God.”
