# Exodus 34 — New stone tablets and Moses’ shining face

**About this chapter**

Moses takes two new tablets up Mount Sinai. The LORD speaks to him there. When Moses returns, the people see a change in his face.

## Moses brings two new tablets

## Exodus 34:1

The LORD told Moses, “Cut two stone tablets like the ones you broke.

I will write the same words on the new tablets.

![Two whole stone tablets stand side by side.](../pictures/two-new-tablets.svg)

Moses must cut two new tablets to replace the broken ones.

## Exodus 34:2

Be ready in the morning.

Climb Mount Sinai and meet me at the top.

## Exodus 34:3

Come alone.

No one else may be anywhere on the mountain.

Keep the flocks and herds from feeding near it.”

## Exodus 34:4

Moses cut two stone tablets like the first ones.

Early in the morning, he carried them up Mount Sinai.

He did what the LORD had told him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses carry up the mountain?

## The LORD tells Moses what he is like

## Exodus 34:5

The LORD came down in a cloud.

He stood there with Moses and announced his name, the LORD.

## Exodus 34:6

The LORD passed in front of Moses and called out, “The LORD!

The LORD God cares for people and shows them kindness.

He is slow to anger.

His love is great, and he keeps his word.

## Exodus 34:7

He keeps loving his people for a thousand generations.

He forgives wrongs, rebellion and sin.

Yet he holds guilty people responsible.

When parents do wrong, their punishment reaches their children and grandchildren, even the third and fourth generations.”

## Exodus 34:8

At once Moses bowed to the ground and worshiped.

## Exodus 34:9

Moses said, “My Lord, if you are pleased with me, please go with us.

We are stubborn.

Please forgive our wrongs and sins.

Take us as your own people.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses ask the LORD to forgive?

## A covenant with Israel

## Exodus 34:10

The LORD said, “I am making a covenant.

Your people will see me do wonders no nation has ever seen.

Everyone around you will see my work.

What I do with you will fill them with awe.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. Here the LORD tells Israel what he will do and how the people must live.

## Exodus 34:11

Obey what I command you today.

I will drive out the Amorites, Canaanites and Hittites ahead of you.

I will also drive out the Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites.

## Exodus 34:12

Be careful when you enter their land.

An agreement with the people there could trap you.

## Exodus 34:13

Tear down their altars and break their sacred stones.

Cut down the poles they use to worship Asherah.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD promise the people would see?

## Worship the LORD alone

## Exodus 34:14

You must worship no other god.

The LORD’s name is Jealous.

He wants your worship for himself alone.

## Exodus 34:15

Do not make a covenant with those people.

They give themselves to other gods and offer sacrifices to them.

They will invite you to eat those sacrifices.

## Exodus 34:16

You might choose their daughters to marry your sons.

Those women will give themselves to their gods.

They will lead your sons to worship those gods too.

**Reading help**

The LORD describes worshiping other gods as being unfaithful. Israel had promised to worship him alone.

## Exodus 34:17

Do not make gods for yourselves out of melted metal.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the LORD warn Israel about sharing in sacrifices to other gods?

## The firstborn and the feast

## Exodus 34:18

Keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread.

At the set time in the month of Abib, eat bread made without yeast for seven days.

That was the month you left Egypt.

## Exodus 34:19

The first to be born to each mother belongs to me.

This includes the firstborn males among your cattle and sheep.

## Exodus 34:20

Give a lamb in place of a firstborn donkey.

If you do not, you must break the donkey’s neck.

You must redeem every firstborn son.

Everyone must bring a gift when they come before me.

**Reading help**

To redeem someone means to give something in their place.

**Pause and answer**

What did the bread without yeast help the people remember?

## Rest and worship through the year

## Exodus 34:21

Work for six days and rest on the seventh.

Rest even when it is time to plow or harvest.

## Exodus 34:22

Celebrate the Feast of Weeks with the first wheat you gather.

Celebrate the Feast of Ingathering at the turn of the year.

## Exodus 34:23

Three times each year, all the males must come before the Lord GOD, the God of Israel.

## Exodus 34:24

I will drive out nations ahead of you and extend your borders.

Three times a year you will go to meet the LORD your God.

While you are away, no one will want your land for themselves.

## Exodus 34:25

When you offer blood from a sacrifice, bring nothing made with yeast.

Leave none of the Passover sacrifice until morning.

## Exodus 34:26

Bring the best of your first crops to the house of the LORD your God.

Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.”

**Pause and answer**

When did the people rest, even during harvest?

## The words on the stone tablets

## Exodus 34:27

The LORD told Moses, “Write down these words.

These are the terms of my covenant with you and Israel.”

## Exodus 34:28

Moses stayed with the LORD for 40 days and 40 nights.

Moses ate no bread and drank no water.

The two tablets now bore the words of the covenant, the Ten Commandments.

## Exodus 34:29

Moses came down Mount Sinai carrying the two tablets of the Testimony.

His face was shining because he had spoken with the LORD.

Moses did not know it.

**Pause and answer**

Why was Moses’ face shining?

## The people see Moses’ face

## Exodus 34:30

Aaron and all the Israelites saw Moses’ shining face.

They were afraid to come near him.

## Exodus 34:31

Moses called to them.

Aaron and all the leaders came back to Moses.

Moses spoke to them.

## Exodus 34:32

Then all the Israelites came near.

Moses told them to obey everything the LORD had said on Mount Sinai.

## Exodus 34:33

When Moses finished speaking, he covered his face with a veil.

## Exodus 34:34

Whenever Moses went in to speak with the LORD, he took off the veil.

He left it off when he came out.

He told the Israelites what the LORD had commanded.

## Exodus 34:35

The Israelites could see his face shining.

Then Moses covered his face again.

The veil stayed on until he went back to speak with the LORD.

**Follow the veil**

| When | What Moses did |
| --- | --- |
| When he went in to speak with the LORD | Took off the veil |
| When he gave the LORD’s message to Israel | Kept his face uncovered |
| After he finished speaking | Covered his face again |
