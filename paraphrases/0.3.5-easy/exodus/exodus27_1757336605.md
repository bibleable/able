# Exodus 27 — The altar, the courtyard and the lamps

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses how to build an altar. He describes the courtyard around the holy tent. He gives Aaron and his sons work to do each night.

## A square altar

## Exodus 27:1

The LORD told Moses, “Build a square altar from acacia wood.

Make it five cubits long and five cubits wide.

Make it three cubits high.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place to offer gifts to God. A cubit was about the length from your elbow to your fingertip.

## Exodus 27:2

Shape a horn at each of the four corners.

Make the horns and altar all one piece.

Cover the altar with bronze.

**Reading help**

Here a horn is a part that sticks up from the altar’s corner.

## Exodus 27:3

Use bronze for all the altar tools.

Make pots to carry away ashes.

Make shovels, bowls for sprinkling, meat forks and pans for hot coals.

**Pause and answer**

What metal covered the altar?

## A grate and carrying poles

## Exodus 27:4

Make a grate for the altar.

Use bronze mesh, like a net made of metal.

Put a bronze ring at each of its four corners.

## Exodus 27:5

Place the grate below the ledge on the altar.

The mesh should reach halfway up the altar.

## Exodus 27:6

Make poles from acacia wood to carry the altar.

Cover the poles with bronze.

## Exodus 27:7

Slide the poles through the rings.

When people carry the altar, one pole runs along each of two opposite sides.

## Exodus 27:8

Build the altar from boards.

Leave it hollow inside.

Follow the pattern I showed you on the mountain.

**Pause and answer**

How did people carry the altar?

## Curtains around the courtyard

## Exodus 27:9

Make a courtyard around the holy tent.

Hang curtains made from fine linen thread along the south side.

Together the curtains must reach 100 cubits.

## Exodus 27:10

Use 20 posts and 20 bronze bases.

Fit the posts with silver hooks and silver bands.

## Exodus 27:11

Hang curtains along the north side for 100 cubits too.

Use 20 posts and 20 bronze bases.

Fit these posts with silver hooks and bands too.

## Exodus 27:12

Hang curtains across the west end for 50 cubits.

Use ten posts and ten bases.

**Pause and answer**

Which was longer, the south side or the west end?

## The entrance on the east side

## Exodus 27:13

The east end faces the sunrise.

Make that end 50 cubits wide.

## Exodus 27:14

Hang 15 cubits of curtains on one side of the entrance.

Use three posts and three bases.

## Exodus 27:15

Hang 15 cubits of curtains on the other side too.

Use three more posts and three more bases.

## Exodus 27:16

Make the entrance curtain 20 cubits wide.

Stitch blue, purple and bright red yarn into fine linen.

Hang this curtain on four posts with four bases.

**Picture the east end**

Imagine standing outside and facing the entrance. The three sections together measure 50 cubits.

| Curtain on one side | Entrance curtain | Curtain on the other side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 cubits | 20 cubits | 15 cubits |
| 3 posts | 4 posts | 3 posts |

**Pause and answer**

Which part of the east end had the colored curtain?

## The whole courtyard

## Exodus 27:17

All the posts around the courtyard need silver bands and silver hooks.

Stand the posts on bronze bases.

## Exodus 27:18

Make the whole courtyard 100 cubits long and 50 cubits wide.

Make the fine linen curtains five cubits high.

Use bronze bases.

## Exodus 27:19

Make all the tools for work at the holy tent from bronze.

Use bronze for the pegs that hold the tent in place.

Use bronze for the courtyard pegs too.

**Pause and answer**

How high were the courtyard curtains?

## Oil for the lamps

## Exodus 27:20

Tell the Israelites to bring you pure oil from pressed olives.

Use this oil to keep the lamps burning regularly.

## Exodus 27:21

Aaron and his sons must tend the lamps before the LORD from evening until morning.

The lamps belong inside the Tent of Meeting.

They stand outside the veil that hangs in front of the Testimony.

The Israelites must keep this rule for every generation.”

**Reading help**

The Tent of Meeting was the holy tent. The veil was the curtain between its two rooms. The Testimony was the name for the stone tablets with God’s commands.
