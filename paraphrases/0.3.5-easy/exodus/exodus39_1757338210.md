# Exodus 39 — The workers bring their finished work to Moses

**About this chapter**

The workers make the priests’ clothes. Then they bring the clothes, the tent and its furnishings to Moses for him to examine.

## Gold threads and shoulder stones

## Exodus 39:1

The workers wove blue, purple and bright red yarn into clothes for serving in the holy place.

They also made Aaron’s holy clothes.

They followed the instructions the LORD had given Moses.

## Exodus 39:2

Bezalel made the ephod from fine linen.

He stitched designs into it with gold and with blue, purple and bright red yarn.

**Reading help**

The ephod was a garment Aaron wore over his robe.

## Exodus 39:3

The workers hammered gold into thin sheets.

They cut the sheets into threads.

With great skill, they wove the gold into the colored yarn and fine linen.

## Exodus 39:4

They made shoulder pieces to fasten the ephod.

They attached them at two corners.

## Exodus 39:5

They wove the waistband and the ephod as one piece.

They used the same gold, colored yarn and fine linen.

They did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Exodus 39:6

They carved the names of Israel’s sons on onyx stones, as on a seal.

They fitted the stones into holders made of fine gold wire.

## Exodus 39:7

They fastened the stones to the ephod’s shoulder pieces.

The stones served as reminders of Israel’s sons.

The LORD had told Moses to do this.

**Pause and answer**

Whose names did the workers carve on the shoulder stones?

## Twelve stones on the breastpiece

## Exodus 39:8

Bezalel made the breastpiece with the same skill as the ephod.

He used gold, fine linen and blue, purple and bright red yarn.

## Exodus 39:9

He folded the cloth double to make a square.

Each side measured one handspan.

**Reading help**

A breastpiece covered the chest. A handspan is the distance from thumb to little finger on a spread hand.

## Exodus 39:10

The workers set precious stones in four rows.

The first row held a ruby, a topaz and an emerald.

## Exodus 39:11

The second row held a turquoise, a sapphire and a diamond.

## Exodus 39:12

The third row held a jacinth, an agate and an amethyst.

## Exodus 39:13

The fourth row held a beryl, an onyx and a jasper.

Fine gold wire held the stones in place.

## Exodus 39:14

The twelve stones stood for Israel’s twelve sons.

The workers carved one tribe’s name on each stone, as on a seal.

| Row | First stone | Second stone | Third stone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ruby | Topaz | Emerald |
| 2 | Turquoise | Sapphire | Diamond |
| 3 | Jacinth | Agate | Amethyst |
| 4 | Beryl | Onyx | Jasper |

## How the breastpiece stayed in place

## Exodus 39:15

For the breastpiece, the workers braided pure gold into chains that looked like cords.

## Exodus 39:16

They made two fine gold wire holders and two gold rings.

They attached the rings to two corners of the breastpiece.

## Exodus 39:17

They fastened one gold chain to each ring.

## Exodus 39:18

They fixed the other ends to the two holders.

These joined the chains to the front of the shoulder pieces.

## Exodus 39:19

They made two more gold rings for the other corners of the breastpiece.

These went on the inside edge, next to the ephod.

## Exodus 39:20

They made another pair of gold rings for the ephod.

These went low on the front of its shoulder pieces, near the seam above the waistband.

## Exodus 39:21

They tied the breastpiece rings to the ephod rings with blue cord.

The breastpiece stayed above the waistband without swinging loose.

They did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**Pause and answer**

What kept the breastpiece from swinging loose?

## Bells on the blue robe

## Exodus 39:22

The workers wove the robe for the ephod entirely from blue cloth.

## Exodus 39:23

They left an opening for the head in the center.

A collar kept the opening from tearing.

## Exodus 39:24

They made fruit shapes called pomegranates from fine linen and blue, purple and bright red yarn.

They put these around the robe’s bottom edge.

## Exodus 39:25

They made bells of pure gold.

They put the bells between the pomegranates.

## Exodus 39:26

A bell followed by a pomegranate formed a pattern around the whole edge.

This was the robe for serving in the holy place.

They did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**Pause and answer**

What hung between the pomegranates?

## Linen clothes and a gold plate

## Exodus 39:27

The workers wove fine linen tunics for Aaron and his sons.

## Exodus 39:28

They made the linen turban, decorated headbands and fine linen underclothes.

## Exodus 39:29

They made a sash from fine linen.

They stitched designs into it with blue, purple and bright red yarn.

They did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**Reading help**

A tunic was a long shirt. A turban covered the head. A sash tied around the waist.

## Exodus 39:30

They made a plate of pure gold for the holy crown.

They carved words into it as on a seal.

The words were “HOLY TO THE LORD.”

## Exodus 39:31

They used a blue cord to fasten the plate to the turban.

They did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**Pause and answer**

What words did the workers carve on the gold plate?

## The workers bring the tent to Moses

## Exodus 39:32

The Israelites finished the holy tent, called the Tent of Meeting.

They did everything the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Exodus 39:33

They brought the tent and all its furnishings to Moses.

They brought the clasps, frames and crossbars, the posts and their bases.

## Exodus 39:34

They brought two coverings, one of ram skins dyed red and one of fine leather.

They also brought the inner curtain, called the veil.

## Exodus 39:35

They brought the ark that would hold the Testimony.

With it came the carrying poles and the gold cover called the mercy seat.

**Reading help**

The ark was a chest. The Testimony was the name for the stone tablets with God’s commands.

## Exodus 39:36

They brought the table, its dishes and the Bread of the Presence.

**Pause and answer**

What did the workers bring with the ark?

## Moses examines the finished work

## Exodus 39:37

They brought the pure gold lampstand with its row of lamps.

They brought its tools and the oil for light.

## Exodus 39:38

They brought the gold altar, the anointing oil and the sweet-smelling incense.

They also brought the curtain for the tent entrance.

## Exodus 39:39

They brought the bronze altar with its bronze grate, poles and tools.

They brought the washing bowl and its stand.

## Exodus 39:40

They brought the courtyard curtains, posts and bases.

They brought the gate curtain, ropes and tent pegs.

They brought all the equipment for serving in the Tent of Meeting.

## Exodus 39:41

They brought the woven clothes for serving in the holy place.

These included Aaron’s holy clothes and the clothes for his sons to serve as priests.

## Exodus 39:42

The Israelites had done all the work just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Exodus 39:43

Moses examined all their work.

He saw that they had followed the LORD’s instructions.

So Moses blessed the people.
