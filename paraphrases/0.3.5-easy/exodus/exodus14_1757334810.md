# Exodus 14 — The Israelites walk through the sea

**About this chapter**

The Israelites camp beside the sea. Pharaoh comes after them with his army. The LORD makes a path through the water.

## The people camp beside the sea

## Exodus 14:1

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 14:2

“Tell the Israelites to turn back.

Tell them to camp by the sea near Pi-hahiroth.

That place is between Migdol and the sea.

Set up camp across from Baal-zephon.

## Exodus 14:3

Pharaoh will think, ‘The Israelites have lost their way.

The wilderness has trapped them.’

## Exodus 14:4

I will make Pharaoh stubborn, so he will chase them.

What I do to Pharaoh and his army will bring me honor.

Then the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD.”

The Israelites camped where the LORD told them.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the LORD tell the people to camp?

## Pharaoh chases the people

## Exodus 14:5

Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, heard that the Israelites had fled.

Pharaoh and his officials changed their minds.

They said, “What have we done?

We let the Israelites go!

Now they no longer work for us.”

## Exodus 14:6

Pharaoh got his chariot ready.

He took his army with him.

**Reading help**

A chariot was a cart that horses pulled. Soldiers rode in it.

## Exodus 14:7

Pharaoh took 600 of his best chariots.

He took all the other chariots of Egypt too.

Officers led them all.

## Exodus 14:8

The LORD made Pharaoh stubborn.

Pharaoh chased the Israelites as they marched boldly away.

## Exodus 14:9

Pharaoh’s horses, chariots, riders and soldiers caught up with them.

The Israelites were camping by the sea near Pi-hahiroth.

Their camp stood across from Baal-zephon.

**Pause and answer**

Who chased the Israelites?

## The people ask Moses for help

## Exodus 14:10

The Israelites looked up as Pharaoh came closer.

They saw the Egyptians marching after them.

In great fear, the Israelites cried to the LORD for help.

## Exodus 14:11

The people said to Moses, “Were there no graves in Egypt?

Is that why you brought us here to die?

Look what you have done by bringing us out of Egypt!

**Reading help**

The people were angry and afraid. Their question about graves meant they expected to die there.

## Exodus 14:12

In Egypt we told you, ‘Leave us alone.

Let us work for the Egyptians.’

We would rather serve the Egyptians than die here in the wilderness.”

## Exodus 14:13

Moses told the people, “Do not be afraid.

Stand where you are.

Today you will see the LORD save you.

You will never see these Egyptians again.

## Exodus 14:14

The LORD will fight for you.

You only need to be still.”

**Pause and answer**

Who did Moses say would fight for the people?

## The LORD tells the people to move

## Exodus 14:15

The LORD asked Moses, “Why are you crying to me for help?

Tell the Israelites to move forward.

## Exodus 14:16

Lift your walking stick.

Reach your hand out over the sea.

Divide the water so the Israelites can walk through on dry ground.

## Exodus 14:17

I will make the Egyptians stubborn.

They will follow the Israelites into the sea.

I will gain honor through Pharaoh and his whole army, including his chariots and riders.

## Exodus 14:18

When I gain honor through Pharaoh, his chariots and his riders, the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD tell Moses to lift?

## A dry path opens

## Exodus 14:19

God’s angel had been leading the Israelites.

Now the angel went behind them.

The tall column of cloud also moved behind them.

## Exodus 14:20

The cloud stood between the two camps.

In the darkness, the cloud lit up the night.

All night the two camps stayed apart.

**Where was the cloud?**

| Behind the Israelites | Between the camps | Beside the sea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Egyptian army | Tall column of cloud | Israelites |

## Exodus 14:21

Moses reached his hand out over the sea.

All night the LORD sent a strong east wind.

The wind pushed the sea back.

The water divided, leaving dry ground.

## Exodus 14:22

The Israelites walked through the sea on dry ground.

Water stood like a wall on their right.

Water stood like a wall on their left.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Israelites walk on?

## The Egyptians follow

## Exodus 14:23

The Egyptians chased the Israelites into the sea.

All Pharaoh’s horses, chariots and riders went in.

## Exodus 14:24

In the early morning, the LORD looked down from the column of fire and cloud.

He made the Egyptian army confused.

## Exodus 14:25

The LORD made their chariot wheels wobble.

The Egyptians could hardly steer.

They said, “Let’s get away from the Israelites!

The LORD is fighting against us for them!”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the Egyptians want to get away?

## The sea flows back

## Exodus 14:26

The LORD told Moses, “Reach your hand out over the sea.

Then the water will flow back over the Egyptians, their chariots and their riders.”

## Exodus 14:27

Moses reached his hand out over the sea.

At dawn, the sea went back to its place.

The Egyptians tried to escape.

The LORD swept them into the sea.

## Exodus 14:28

The water covered the chariots and riders.

It covered Pharaoh’s whole army that had followed Israel into the sea.

Everyone in that army died.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the water at dawn?

## The people trust the LORD

## Exodus 14:29

The Israelites had walked through on dry ground.

Water had stood like walls on both sides.

## Exodus 14:30

That day the LORD saved the Israelites from the Egyptians.

The Israelites saw the dead Egyptians on the shore.

## Exodus 14:31

The Israelites saw the LORD’s great power over the Egyptians.

They stood in fear of the LORD.

They trusted the LORD and his servant Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the Israelites trust?
