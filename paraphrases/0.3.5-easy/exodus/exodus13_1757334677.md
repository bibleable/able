# Exodus 13 — The LORD leads the people out of Egypt

**About this chapter**

As the Israelites leave Egypt, Moses tells them how to remember that day. Then the LORD shows them which way to go.

## The firstborn belong to the LORD

## Exodus 13:1

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 13:2

“Set apart for me every firstborn male in Israel.

If a mother’s first child is a boy, he belongs to me.

If an animal’s first young is male, it belongs to me too.”

**Pause and answer**

To whom did the firstborn belong?

## Remember the day you left

## Exodus 13:3

Moses told the people, “Remember this day.

You were slaves in Egypt.

The LORD used his great power to bring you out.

Eat nothing made with anything that makes dough rise.

## Exodus 13:4

Today you are leaving Egypt.

This month is called Abib.

## Exodus 13:5

The LORD promised your ancestors a land rich in food.

The Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Hivites and Jebusites live there.

When the LORD brings you there, keep this celebration in this same month.

**Reading help**

The Bible calls this a land “flowing with milk and honey.” These words picture a land with plenty to eat.

## Exodus 13:6

For seven days, eat bread made without yeast.

On the seventh day, hold a feast for the LORD.

## Exodus 13:7

Eat bread made without yeast all seven days.

Keep your whole land free of anything that makes dough rise.

Keep it free of food made with it too.

**Pause and answer**

What kind of bread did the people eat for seven days?

## Tell your children

## Exodus 13:8

That day, tell your son, ‘I do this because of what the LORD did for me when I left Egypt.’

## Exodus 13:9

This celebration will be a sign on your hand.

It will be a reminder on your forehead.

It will remind you to speak about the LORD’s teaching.

The LORD used his great power to bring you out of Egypt.

**Reading help**

A “mighty hand” means great power. The words point to what the LORD did for his people.

## Exodus 13:10

So keep this rule at the same time each year.

**Pause and answer**

What did the celebration remind the people to speak about?

## Give what belongs to the LORD

## Exodus 13:11

The LORD promised you and your ancestors the land of the Canaanites.

He will bring you there and give you the land.

## Exodus 13:12

Then set apart every firstborn male for the LORD.

Every firstborn male among your farm animals belongs to the LORD.

## Exodus 13:13

Give a lamb in place of each firstborn male donkey.

This is called redeeming the donkey.

If you do not redeem the donkey, break its neck.

You must redeem every firstborn son.

## Exodus 13:14

One day your son will ask, ‘What does this mean?’

Tell him, ‘We were slaves in Egypt.

The LORD used his great power to bring us out.

## Exodus 13:15

Pharaoh kept refusing to let us go.

So the LORD killed the firstborn in Egypt, both people and animals.

That is why I offer firstborn male animals as sacrifices to the LORD.

I redeem every firstborn son.’

## Exodus 13:16

This will be a sign on your hand and a reminder on your forehead.

Remember how the LORD used his great power to bring us out of Egypt.”

**Pause and answer**

What animal could a family give in place of a donkey?

## God chooses the road

## Exodus 13:17

Pharaoh let the people go.

The shortest road went through the land of the Philistines.

God chose another road.

God said, “If the people face war, they might change their minds.

They might go back to Egypt.”

## Exodus 13:18

So God led the people through the wilderness toward the Red Sea.

The Israelites left Egypt in groups ready for battle.

**Reading help**

A wilderness is open land where few people live.

## Exodus 13:19

Moses took Joseph’s bones with him.

Long ago, Joseph had made the sons of Israel promise to carry his bones with them.

Joseph had said, “God will surely come to help you.

Then you must take my bones away from here.”

**Pause and answer**

Whose bones did Moses take with him?

## A cloud and a fire show the way

## Exodus 13:20

The people left Succoth and camped at Etham.

Their camp stood at the edge of the wilderness.

## Exodus 13:21

By day the LORD went ahead in a tall column of cloud.

The cloud showed the people the way.

By night the LORD went ahead in a tall column of fire.

The fire gave them light.

So they could travel by day or by night.

## Exodus 13:22

The cloud stayed ahead of the people by day.

The fire stayed ahead of them by night.

| When | What the people saw ahead | How it helped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day | A tall cloud | It showed the way. |
| Night | A tall fire | It gave light. |

**Pause and answer**

What gave the people light at night?
