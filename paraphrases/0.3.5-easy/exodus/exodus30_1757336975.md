# Exodus 30 — The incense altar and the washing basin

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses where Aaron will burn incense and where the priests will wash. He also tells Moses which oil and incense to set apart for worship.

## Build the incense altar

## Exodus 30:1

The LORD told Moses, “Make an altar from acacia wood.

Use it for burning incense.

## Exodus 30:2

Make it square, one cubit long and one cubit wide.

Make it two cubits high.

Shape its horns as part of the altar itself.

**Reading help**

Incense gives off a scent when it burns. A cubit was about the length from elbow to fingertip. The horns were raised parts at the altar’s corners.

## Exodus 30:3

Cover the top, sides and horns with pure gold.

Add gold trim around the altar.

## Exodus 30:4

Make two gold rings below the trim, on opposite sides.

The rings will hold the poles for carrying the altar.

## Exodus 30:5

Make the poles from acacia wood.

Cover them with gold.

## Exodus 30:6

Place the altar in front of the curtain.

Behind the curtain stands the ark that holds the Testimony.

The mercy seat covers the ark.

That is where I will meet you.

**Reading help**

The Testimony was the name for the stone tablets with God’s commands. The ark was the chest that held them.

## Morning, evening and once a year

## Exodus 30:7

Every morning Aaron must tend the lamps.

At that time he must burn sweet-smelling incense on the altar.

## Exodus 30:8

At dusk Aaron must get the lamps ready.

He must burn incense then too.

Keep burning incense before the LORD this way for generations to come.

## Exodus 30:9

Use only the incense I have approved for this altar.

Do not bring burnt offerings or grain offerings here.

Do not pour drink offerings on it.

## Exodus 30:10

Once a year Aaron must make atonement on the altar’s horns.

He must use blood from the sin offering for atonement.

Keep doing this each year for generations to come.

This altar is most holy to the LORD.”

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with sin before God. Here the priest uses the sin offering to make the altar clean for worship.

## A gift when the people are counted

## Exodus 30:11

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 30:12

“When you count the Israelites, each man you count must make a payment to the LORD for his life.

Then no deadly sickness will strike the people when you count them.

## Exodus 30:13

Each man who crosses over to join the counted group must give half a shekel.

Use the weight called the sanctuary shekel.

One whole shekel weighs 20 gerahs.

The half shekel is a gift to the LORD.

## Exodus 30:14

Every man aged 20 or older who crosses over must give this gift to the LORD.

## Exodus 30:15

The rich must give half a shekel.

The poor must give the same amount.

This gift to the LORD makes atonement for your lives.

## Exodus 30:16

Collect the money and use it for work at the Tent of Meeting.

It will be a reminder of the Israelites before the LORD and make atonement for your lives.”

## Water for the priests

## Exodus 30:17

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Exodus 30:18

“Make a bronze washing bowl and a bronze stand.

Put the bowl between the Tent of Meeting and the altar.

Fill it with water.

## Exodus 30:19

Aaron and his sons must use this water to wash their hands and feet.

## Exodus 30:20

They must wash before entering the Tent of Meeting.

They must also wash before going to the altar to offer food to the LORD.

Then they will stay alive.

## Exodus 30:21

They must wash their hands and feet so they will not die.

Aaron and his descendants must always follow this rule.”

## Make the holy oil

## Exodus 30:22

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Exodus 30:23

“Choose the finest spices.

Weigh out 500 shekels of liquid myrrh.

Take half that weight, 250 shekels, of sweet-smelling cinnamon.

Take 250 shekels of sweet-smelling cane.

## Exodus 30:24

Take 500 shekels of cassia.

Weigh all the spices with the sanctuary shekel.

Measure out one hin of olive oil.

## Exodus 30:25

Blend them as a perfume maker would.

This sweet-smelling oil is holy anointing oil.

**Reading help**

A shekel measured weight. A hin measured liquid. To anoint someone means to put oil on them for a special purpose.

**Pause and answer**

## Set apart the tent and the priests

## Exodus 30:26

Put this oil on the Tent of Meeting and on the ark that holds the Testimony.

## Exodus 30:27

Put it on the table and all its tools.

Put it on the lampstand and its tools.

Put it on the incense altar.

## Exodus 30:28

Put it on the altar for burnt offerings and all its tools.

Put it on the washing bowl and its stand.

## Exodus 30:29

Set these things apart for me.

They will be most holy.

Whatever touches them will be holy.

## Exodus 30:30

Put the oil on Aaron and his sons.

Set them apart to serve me as priests.

## Keep the oil holy

## Exodus 30:31

Tell the Israelites, ‘This is my holy anointing oil.

It will be holy for generations to come.

## Exodus 30:32

Do not put this oil on anyone for ordinary use.

Do not copy the mixture.

The oil is holy.

Treat it as holy.

## Exodus 30:33

Anyone who copies its scent or puts it on an outsider must be cut off from the people.’”

## Incense for the LORD

## Exodus 30:34

The LORD also told Moses, “Take four sweet-smelling ingredients.

Use gum resin, onycha, galbanum and pure frankincense.

Take the same amount of each.

## Exodus 30:35

Blend them as a perfume maker would to make incense.

Add salt.

Keep the incense pure and holy.

## Exodus 30:36

Grind some into fine powder.

Put it in front of the Testimony in the Tent of Meeting.

I will meet you there.

Treat the incense as most holy.

## Exodus 30:37

Do not use this mixture to make incense for yourselves.

It belongs to the LORD.

Treat it as holy.

## Exodus 30:38

Anyone who copies it to enjoy its smell must be cut off from the people.”
