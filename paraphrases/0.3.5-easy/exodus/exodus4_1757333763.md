# Exodus 4 — Moses and Aaron bring God’s message

**About this chapter**

Moses worries that the Israelites will not believe him.

The LORD gives him signs and sends his brother Aaron to help.

## The staff becomes a snake

## Exodus 4:1

Moses asked, “What if the Israelites will not believe me or listen?

They might say, ‘The LORD never appeared to you.’”

## Exodus 4:2

The LORD asked, “What are you holding?”

Moses answered, “A staff.” It was his long walking stick.

## Exodus 4:3

The LORD said, “Throw it on the ground.”

Moses threw it down. It became a snake, and Moses ran away.

## Exodus 4:4

The LORD told Moses, “Reach out and take it by the tail.”

Moses caught the snake. It became a staff in his hand again.

## Exodus 4:5

The LORD said, “This sign will help the people believe I appeared to you.

I am the LORD, the God of their fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”

**Pause and answer**

What happened when Moses caught the snake?

## God gives Moses two more signs

## Exodus 4:6

The LORD said, “Put your hand inside your cloak.”

Moses put his hand inside his outer clothes.

When he took it out, a skin disease had turned it white as snow.

## Exodus 4:7

The LORD said, “Put your hand back inside your cloak.”

Moses did so. When he took it out, his skin was healthy again.

## Exodus 4:8

The LORD said, “The people may refuse to believe the first sign.

Then the second sign may help them believe.

## Exodus 4:9

If they still refuse to listen, take water from the Nile.

Pour it on dry ground. The water will become blood there.”

| Sign | What Moses saw or heard |
| --- | --- |
| Staff | It became a snake, then a staff again. |
| Hand | The skin turned white, then became healthy again. |
| Water | God said Nile water would become blood on the ground. |

**Pause and answer**

What changed when Moses put his hand inside his cloak again?

## Moses asks God to send someone else

## Exodus 4:10

Moses said, “Please, Lord, speaking has always been hard for me.

It is still hard now that you have spoken to me.

My words come slowly.”

## Exodus 4:11

The LORD asked, “Who gave people their mouths?

Who makes some people able to speak and others unable to speak?

Who makes people deaf, able to see, or blind?

I am the LORD. I do these things.

## Exodus 4:12

Now go. I will help you speak.

I will teach you what to say.”

## Exodus 4:13

Moses answered, “Please, Lord, send someone else.”

## Exodus 4:14

The LORD grew angry with Moses.

“Your brother Aaron is a Levite. I know he speaks well.

He is coming to meet you. He will be glad to see you.

## Exodus 4:15

Tell Aaron what to say. I will help you both speak.

I will teach you both what to do.

## Exodus 4:16

Aaron will speak to the people for you.

You will be like God to Aaron, giving him the words to say.

## Exodus 4:17

Take this staff with you. Use it to show the signs.”

**Pause and answer**

Who will speak to the people for Moses?

## Moses sets out for Egypt

## Exodus 4:18

Moses went back to Jethro, his wife’s father.

“Please let me go back to my people in Egypt,” Moses said.

“I want to see if they are still alive.”

Jethro answered, “Go in peace.”

## Exodus 4:19

In Midian, the LORD had told Moses, “Go back to Egypt.

The men who wanted to kill you have all died.”

## Exodus 4:20

Moses put his wife and sons on a donkey.

They set out for Egypt. Moses carried the staff of God.

## Exodus 4:21

The LORD told Moses, “When you reach Egypt, go to Pharaoh.

Show him all the wonders I have given you power to do.

I will harden his heart. He will refuse to let the people go.

## Exodus 4:22

Tell Pharaoh, ‘The LORD says that Israel is his firstborn son.

## Exodus 4:23

He told you to let his son go and worship him.

You refused. Now the LORD will take the life of your firstborn son.’”

**Reading help**

Here “Israel” means the Israelite people. God calls them his firstborn son.

**Pause and answer**

Why did God tell Pharaoh to let the Israelites go?

## Zipporah acts on the journey

## Exodus 4:24

Moses and his family stopped to stay for the night.

There the LORD met Moses and was about to kill him.

## Exodus 4:25

Zipporah took a stone knife and circumcised her son.

She touched the foreskin to Moses’ feet.

She told Moses, “You are a bridegroom of blood to me.”

## Exodus 4:26

The LORD let Moses alone.

Zipporah used the words “bridegroom of blood” because of the circumcision.

**Reading help**

Circumcision means removing the foreskin, the skin covering the tip of the penis.

Zipporah was speaking to Moses, her husband.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to Moses after Zipporah circumcised her son?

## The people hear God’s message

## Exodus 4:27

The LORD had told Aaron, “Meet Moses in the wilderness.”

Aaron met Moses at the mountain of God and kissed him.

## Exodus 4:28

Moses told Aaron everything the LORD had sent him to say.

He also described all the signs the LORD had told him to show.

## Exodus 4:29

Together they gathered the elders, the leaders of the Israelites.

## Exodus 4:30

Aaron told them everything the LORD had said to Moses.

Moses showed the signs while the people watched.

## Exodus 4:31

The people believed. They heard that the LORD cared about them.

He had seen how they suffered. They bowed down and worshiped.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people do when they heard that the LORD had seen their suffering?
