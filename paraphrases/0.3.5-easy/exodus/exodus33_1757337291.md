# Exodus 33 — Moses asks the LORD to go with Israel

**About this chapter**

The Israelites hear that the LORD will send an angel ahead. Moses wants the LORD himself to go with them.

## The LORD tells Moses to leave

## Exodus 33:1

The LORD told Moses, “Leave this place.

Take the people you led out of Egypt.

Go to the land I promised Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.

I told them, ‘I will give it to your descendants.’

## Exodus 33:2

I will send an angel ahead of you.

I will drive out the Canaanites, Amorites and Hittites.

I will also drive out the Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites.

## Exodus 33:3

Go to the land flowing with milk and honey.

But I will not go with you.

You are stubborn.

I might destroy you on the way.”

**Reading help**

“Flowing with milk and honey” pictures a land with plenty of food. The LORD calls the people “stiff-necked.” This means they are stubborn and refuse to listen.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the LORD say he would send ahead?

## The people take off their jewelry

## Exodus 33:4

The people were deeply sad when they heard this news.

No one put on any jewelry.

## Exodus 33:5

The LORD had told Moses, “Tell the Israelites, ‘You are stubborn.

If I went with you even for a moment, I would destroy you.

Take off your jewelry.

I will decide what to do with you.’”

## Exodus 33:6

So the Israelites took off their jewelry at Mount Horeb.

From then on, they left it off.

What did the people take off?

## A tent outside the camp

## Exodus 33:7

Moses used to set up a tent well outside the camp.

He called it the Tent of Meeting.

Anyone who wanted to ask the LORD something went there.

## Exodus 33:8

Whenever Moses walked out to that tent, the people stood at their own tent entrances.

They watched until Moses went inside.

## Exodus 33:9

As Moses went in, a tall column of cloud came down.

It stayed at the tent entrance.

The LORD spoke with Moses there.

![A cloud rests at the entrance of a tent.](../pictures/cloud-at-tent-entrance.svg)

The LORD spoke to Moses here.

## Exodus 33:10

The people saw the cloud at the Tent of Meeting.

Each person stood at their own tent entrance and worshiped.

## Exodus 33:11

The LORD spoke with Moses face to face, as friends speak to each other.

Then Moses returned to camp.

His young helper Joshua, the son of Nun, stayed at the tent.

**Picture the two places**

| At the tents in the camp | At the Tent of Meeting outside the camp |
| --- | --- |
| The people watched and worshiped. | The cloud came down and the LORD spoke to Moses. |

**Reading help**

Here “face to face” describes how directly the LORD spoke to Moses, as one friend speaks to another.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Joshua stay when Moses went back to camp?

## Moses asks who will go with him

## Exodus 33:12

Moses said to the LORD, “You told me to lead these people.

Who will you send with me?

You have not told me.

Yet you said, ‘I know you by name.

I am pleased with you.’

## Exodus 33:13

If you are pleased with me, please teach me your ways.

I want to know you and keep pleasing you.

Remember that these people are yours.”

## Exodus 33:14

The LORD answered, “I myself will go with you.

I will give you rest.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD promise Moses?

## Moses asks for all the people

## Exodus 33:15

Moses replied, “If you do not go with us, please leave us here.

## Exodus 33:16

How else will anyone know you are pleased with me and your people?

When you go with us, people can see what sets us apart from every other people on earth.”

## Exodus 33:17

The LORD told Moses, “I will do what you ask.

I am pleased with you.

I know you by name.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Moses want the LORD to go with the people?

## Moses asks to see God’s glory

## Exodus 33:18

Moses said, “Please show me your glory.”

**Reading help**

God’s glory is his greatness. Moses wants to see that greatness for himself.

## Exodus 33:19

The LORD answered, “I will let all my goodness pass before you.

I will speak my name, the LORD, where you can hear me.

I choose whom I will show mercy to.

I choose whom I will treat with compassion.”

## Exodus 33:20

The LORD added, “You cannot see my face.

No one can see me and live.”

## Exodus 33:21

The LORD said, “There is a place near me.

Stand there on the rock.

## Exodus 33:22

When my glory passes by, I will put you in an opening in the rock.

I will cover you with my hand until I have passed.

## Exodus 33:23

Then I will take my hand away.

You will see my back.

You must not see my face.”

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how would the LORD protect Moses as he passed by?
