# Exodus 2 — A baby by the Nile

**About this chapter**

Pharaoh has ordered the Egyptians to throw newborn Hebrew boys into the Nile.

One mother hides her son. This is the story of that child, Moses.

## A mother hides her baby

## Exodus 2:1

A man and a woman from Levi’s family got married.

## Exodus 2:2

The woman gave birth to a son.

She saw what a beautiful baby he was.

She hid him for three months.

## Exodus 2:3

At last she could hide him no longer.

She took a basket made from a plant called papyrus.

She covered it with tar and pitch to keep water out.

She put her baby in the basket.

Then she set it among the reeds at the edge of the Nile River.

## Exodus 2:4

The baby’s sister stood some distance away.

She watched to see what would happen to him.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the mother put the basket?

## Pharaoh’s daughter finds the baby

## Exodus 2:5

Pharaoh’s daughter went down to bathe in the Nile.

Her servants walked along the riverbank.

She saw the basket among the reeds.

She sent a servant to fetch it.

## Exodus 2:6

Pharaoh’s daughter opened the basket. The baby boy was crying.

She felt sorry for him. “This is a Hebrew child,” she said.

## Exodus 2:7

The baby’s sister asked, “Shall I find a Hebrew woman to feed him her milk?”

## Exodus 2:8

“Go ahead,” said Pharaoh’s daughter.

The girl went and called the baby’s own mother.

## Exodus 2:9

Pharaoh’s daughter told the mother, “Take this baby and nurse him for me.

I will pay you.” The mother took him and fed him her milk.

## Exodus 2:10

When the boy was older, his mother brought him to Pharaoh’s daughter.

He became her son. Pharaoh’s daughter named him Moses.

She said, “I pulled him out of the water.”

| Person | How she cared for the baby |
| --- | --- |
| His mother | Hid him and later nursed him |
| His sister | Watched him and called his mother |
| Pharaoh’s daughter | Had him taken from the water and made him her son |

**Pause and answer**

Who chose the name Moses?

## Moses leaves Egypt

## Exodus 2:11

When Moses was grown, he went to see his own people, the Hebrews.

He saw how hard they had to work.

An Egyptian was beating one of them.

## Exodus 2:12

Moses looked around and saw nobody else.

He killed the Egyptian and hid his body in the sand.

## Exodus 2:13

The next day Moses saw two Hebrews fighting.

He asked the one at fault, “Why are you hitting the other man?”

## Exodus 2:14

The man said, “Who made you our ruler and judge?

Will you kill me as you killed the Egyptian?”

Moses was afraid. He thought, “People know what I did.”

## Exodus 2:15

When Pharaoh heard about the killing, he tried to kill Moses.

Moses ran away to the land of Midian.

There he sat down beside a well.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Moses leave Egypt?

## Moses helps seven sisters

## Exodus 2:16

The priest of Midian had seven daughters.

They came to draw water from the well.

They filled long containers so their father’s flock could drink.

## Exodus 2:17

Some shepherds came and drove the women away.

Moses stood up to help the women.

He gave their flock water.

## Exodus 2:18

The women went home to their father, Reuel.

He asked, “How did you get home so early today?”

## Exodus 2:19

They answered, “An Egyptian saved us from the shepherds.

He even drew water for us and gave our flock a drink.”

## Exodus 2:20

Reuel asked, “Where is he? Why did you leave him there?

Invite him to eat with us.”

## Exodus 2:21

Moses agreed to stay with Reuel.

Reuel gave his daughter Zipporah to Moses in marriage.

## Exodus 2:22

Zipporah had a son. Moses named him Gershom.

Moses explained, “I am a stranger living far from my own land.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Reuel ask his daughters to invite Moses to do?

## God hears his people

## Exodus 2:23

A long time passed. The king of Egypt died.

The Israelites groaned because they were slaves.

They cried out for help, and their cry reached God.

## Exodus 2:24

God heard them groaning.

He remembered his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a firm agreement. God had promised to be their God and the God of their families.

## Exodus 2:25

God saw the Israelites and took notice of them.
