# 2 John 1 — Keep loving each other

**About this chapter**

The writer calls himself the elder.

An elder is a leader who cares for believers.

## A greeting from the elder

## 2 John 1:1

I am the elder.

I write to the lady God chose and to her children.

I love you because we share the truth.

Everyone who knows the truth loves you too.

## 2 John 1:2

That truth stays in us.

It will be with us forever.

## 2 John 1:3

God the Father and his Son Jesus Christ give us grace, mercy and peace.

Grace is kindness freely given.

Mercy is kindness shown to someone in need.

These gifts will be with us as we live in truth and love.

**Pause and answer**

How long will the truth stay with us?

## How to walk in love

## 2 John 1:4

I was very glad to find some of your children living by the truth.

That is what the Father commanded us to do.

## 2 John 1:5

Dear lady, I ask that we love one another.

We have known this command from the beginning.

## 2 John 1:6

We show love by doing what God commands.

From the beginning you heard his command to walk in love.

Walking in love means showing love in how you live each day.

**Pause and answer**

What does it mean to walk in love?

## Pay attention to what a teacher says

## 2 John 1:7

Many people have gone out to deceive others.

They refuse to say that Jesus Christ came as a real human being.

Anyone who teaches this lie is a deceiver and an antichrist.

An antichrist is someone who opposes Christ.

## 2 John 1:8

Watch yourselves.

You could lose what we have worked for.

I want you to receive the full reward.

## 2 John 1:9

Someone may claim to go further and leave Christ’s teaching behind.

That person does not have God.

Whoever stays with Christ’s teaching has both the Father and the Son.

## 2 John 1:10

Suppose someone comes to teach you.

If they do not bring this teaching about Christ, keep your home closed to them.

Do not even greet them.

## 2 John 1:11

Anyone who welcomes such a teacher shares in that person’s wrongdoing.

**Pause and answer**

What do the false teachers refuse to say about Jesus?

## A hope to visit

## 2 John 1:12

There is much more I want to tell you.

For that, I would rather put down my paper and ink.

I hope to visit and talk with you face to face.

Then our joy will be complete.

## 2 John 1:13

Your sister is also someone God chose.

Her children send you their greetings.
