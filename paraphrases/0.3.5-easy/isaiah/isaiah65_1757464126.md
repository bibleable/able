# Isaiah 65 — The LORD promises a new earth

**About this chapter**

The LORD remembers how His people answered when He called. He promises a day when families will live safely in the homes they build.

## The LORD reaches out

## Isaiah 65:1

The LORD says, “I showed Myself to people who never asked for Me.

People found Me without looking for Me.

A nation never called My name.

Yet I told them, ‘Here I am! Here I am!’

## Isaiah 65:2

All day I held out My hands to welcome stubborn people.

They kept doing wrong.

They followed their own ideas.

## Isaiah 65:3

They kept making Me angry right in front of Me.

They offered sacrifices in gardens.

They burned incense on brick altars.

Incense gives off sweet-smelling smoke.

## Isaiah 65:4

They sat among graves.

They spent nights in secret places.

They ate pork and drank broth My law called unclean.

## Isaiah 65:5

They told others, ‘Stay away! I am holier than you.’

They bother Me like smoke blowing into My nose all day.

## The LORD sees what people do

## Isaiah 65:6

Look! I have their deeds in writing before Me.

I will speak.

I will pay them back for what they did.

## Isaiah 65:7

I will punish you for your sins and your ancestors’ sins.

They burned incense on mountains.

They showed contempt for Me on the hills.

I will give them the full punishment their deeds deserve.”

## Good grapes in a bunch

## Isaiah 65:8

The LORD says, “Think of a bunch of grapes.

Someone sees that the grapes can make new wine.

That person says, ‘Save them! There is good in them.’

In the same way, I will spare My servants.

I will not destroy everyone.

![A bunch of grapes on a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Isaiah 65:9

Jacob’s family will have children after them.

People from Judah will inherit My mountains.

My chosen people will take possession of them.

My servants will live there.

## Isaiah 65:10

My people who seek Me will have grass for their sheep in Sharon.

Their cattle will rest in the Valley of Achor.

## Two different responses to God

## Isaiah 65:11

But some of you have left Me, the LORD.

You forget My holy mountain.

You set out food for a god called Fortune.

You fill bowls with wine for a god called Destiny.

## Isaiah 65:12

I have decided what will happen to you.

You will kneel, and the sword will take your lives.

I called, and you gave no answer.

I spoke, and you refused to listen.

You chose to do what I saw was evil.

You chose what displeased Me.”

## Isaiah 65:13

The Lord GOD says, “My servants will eat.

You who left Me will go hungry.

My servants will drink, and you will go thirsty.

My servants will rejoice, and you will feel shame.

## Isaiah 65:14

My servants will shout because their hearts are glad.

You will cry out because your hearts ache.

You will weep because you have lost hope.

## Isaiah 65:15

My chosen people will use your name in a curse.

They will speak of your fate when wishing harm on someone.

The Lord GOD will put you to death.

He will give His servants a different name.

## Isaiah 65:16

People here will ask the God of truth to bless them.

When they make a solemn promise, they will call on Him as their witness.

They will forget the troubles they once had.

I will no longer look on those troubles.

## Joy in Jerusalem

## Isaiah 65:17

Look! I will make new heavens and a new earth.

People will no longer think about what happened before.

## Isaiah 65:18

Be glad forever because of what I am making.

I will make Jerusalem a place of joy.

Its people will bring delight.

## Isaiah 65:19

Jerusalem will bring Me joy.

My people will make Me glad.

No one there will hear weeping or cries of distress.

## Isaiah 65:20

Babies there will live beyond those first few days.

Old people will live out their years.

Someone who dies at a hundred will still seem young.

People will think anyone dying younger was under a curse.

## Homes, gardens, and peace

## Isaiah 65:21

People will build houses and live in them.

They will plant grapevines and eat their grapes.

## Isaiah 65:22

No one else will take the homes they build.

No one else will take the crops they plant.

My people will live long lives, like trees.

My chosen people will enjoy everything they work for.

## Isaiah 65:23

Their hard work will bring good results.

Their children will have a future free from disaster.

The LORD will bless them and the families that come after them.

## Isaiah 65:24

Before they call, I will answer.

While they are still speaking, I will listen.

## Isaiah 65:25

The wolf and the lamb will eat together.

The lion will eat straw like an ox.

The snake will eat dust.

On My whole holy mountain, no creature will hurt or destroy another.”

The LORD has said this.

**Pause and answer**

Who will live in the houses the people build?
