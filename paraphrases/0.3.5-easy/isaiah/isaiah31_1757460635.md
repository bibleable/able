# Isaiah 31 — The LORD protects Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

People in Judah hope Egypt’s army will keep them safe. Isaiah asks them to turn back to the LORD.

## What can Egypt’s horses do?

## Isaiah 31:1

Trouble is coming to those who go to Egypt for help.

They count on Egypt’s horses.

They trust its many war chariots and riders.

They do not ask the LORD for help.

He is the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 31:2

The LORD is wise too.

He can bring disaster.

He stands by what He has said.

He will act against those who do evil.

He will also act against those who help them.

## Isaiah 31:3

The Egyptians are only human. They have no power like God’s.

Their horses have bodies of flesh. They are not spirits.

When the LORD reaches out His hand, Egypt will stumble.

The people who count on Egypt will fall.

Both will perish together.

## A lion stands firm and birds hover overhead

## Isaiah 31:4

The LORD told Isaiah to picture a young lion.

It stands over the animal it has caught and growls.

A group of shepherds comes out against it.

They shout, but the noise cannot frighten the lion away.

In the same way, the LORD who commands heaven’s armies will stand firm.

He will come down to fight on Mount Zion and its heights.

## Isaiah 31:5

Now picture birds hovering overhead.

Like those birds, the LORD will watch over Jerusalem.

He will shield the city and rescue it.

He will pass over it and keep it safe.

## Israel turns back and Assyria falls

## Isaiah 31:6

People of Israel, you have turned against the LORD again and again.

Come back to Him.

## Isaiah 31:7

You made silver and gold idols with your own hands.

You did wrong when you made them to worship.

On that day each of you will throw those idols away.

## Isaiah 31:8

The LORD says, “Assyria will fall by a sword.

No human hand will make or wield that sword.

The Assyrians will run from it.

Their young men will have to work against their will.

## Isaiah 31:9

“Even the one they count on like a solid rock will flee in fear.

Their leaders will panic when they see the battle flag.”

The LORD has spoken. His fire burns in Zion.

Isaiah pictures a blazing furnace in Jerusalem.
