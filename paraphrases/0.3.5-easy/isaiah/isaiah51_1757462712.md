# Isaiah 51 — The LORD comforts His people

**About this chapter**

The people ask the LORD to use His strength as He did long ago. He answers people who are afraid and gives Jerusalem a reason to stand up again.

## Remember Abraham and Sarah

## Isaiah 51:1

The LORD says, “Listen, you who want to do right and seek the LORD.

Think of a stone cut from a great rock.

Look at the place it came from.

Remember where you came from too.

## Isaiah 51:2

“Think of your ancestors Abraham and Sarah.

When I called Abraham, he was just one man.

I blessed him, and his family grew into a great people.”

## Isaiah 51:3

The LORD will comfort Zion, the city of Jerusalem.

He sees its ruined places and cares about them.

He will make the desert bloom like Eden, His garden.

Joy will fill the city.

People will sing songs of thanks.

## A light for the nations

## Isaiah 51:4

The LORD says, “My people, listen carefully.

You are my nation.

My teaching will go out to others.

The justice I bring will guide the nations like a light.

I will act soon.

## Isaiah 51:5

“I am coming to put things right.

I am on my way to save you.

I will use my strength to judge the nations fairly.

People on distant islands will wait for me.

They will count on my strength.

## Isaiah 51:6

“Look up at the sky.

Look down at the earth.

The sky will disappear like smoke.

The earth will wear out like clothing.

People will die like tiny flies.

Yet the rescue I bring will last forever.

I will never stop doing what is right.

## Do not fear cruel words

## Isaiah 51:7

“Listen, you who know what is right.

You keep my teaching in your hearts.

When people mock you, do not be afraid.

Do not let their insults crush you.

## Isaiah 51:8

“Those people will wear out like clothes eaten by moths.

They will be like wool eaten by worms.

I will always do what is right.

My saving help will last through every generation.”

## A prayer for the LORD to act

## Isaiah 51:9

The people call out, “Wake up, wake up, strong arm of the LORD!

Show your strength as you did for our ancestors.

You cut Rahab to pieces.

You pierced that sea monster.

## Isaiah 51:10

“You dried up the deep sea.

You made a road on the seabed.

The people you set free walked across it.”

## Isaiah 51:11

The people the LORD has set free will return.

They will enter Zion singing.

Joy will rest on their heads like a crown that lasts forever.

Gladness will fill their lives.

Their grief and sighing will end.

## The LORD answers

## Isaiah 51:12

The LORD answers, “I myself am the one who comforts you.

Why fear people who must die?

They dry up like grass.

## Isaiah 51:13

“You have forgotten the LORD who made you.

I stretched out the sky and laid the earth on firm foundations.

All day you fear the anger of someone determined to destroy you.

Where is that anger now?

## Isaiah 51:14

“The prisoner will soon go free.

He will live to leave his cell.

He will have bread to eat.

## Isaiah 51:15

“I am the LORD your God.

I stir the sea until its waves roar.

My name is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

## Isaiah 51:16

“I have given you my words to speak.

I have sheltered you in the shade of my hand.

I set the heavens in place and laid the foundations of the earth.

I say to Zion, ‘You are my people.’”

## Jerusalem struggles to stand

## Isaiah 51:17

Wake up, wake up!

Stand up, Jerusalem!

Isaiah pictures the city as a woman holding a cup.

The LORD handed her the cup of His anger.

She drank the last drop and could hardly stand.

The cup pictures the punishment the city suffered.

## Isaiah 51:18

Jerusalem raised many sons.

Now none of them can guide her.

No one takes her hand.

## Isaiah 51:19

Your land lies ruined and broken.

You have suffered hunger and war.

Who will weep with you?

Who can comfort you?

## Isaiah 51:20

Your sons have fallen at every street corner.

They lie helpless like an antelope caught in a net.

They have felt the full force of their God’s anger and His sharp words.

## God takes away the cup

## Isaiah 51:21

Listen now, you who suffer.

You stumble like someone drunk, though you drank no wine.

## Isaiah 51:22

The LORD is your God.

He speaks up for His people.

He says, “Look!

I have taken the cup out of your hand.

My anger made you stagger.

You will never drink from that cup again.

**Picture the cup changing hands**

Imagine holding the cup. Then imagine someone lifting it from your hand. For Jerusalem, that means the punishment is coming to an end.

## Isaiah 51:23

“I will give the cup to those who hurt you.

They said, ‘Lie down so we can walk over you.’ You lay with your back to them.

They walked over your back as if it were a street.”
