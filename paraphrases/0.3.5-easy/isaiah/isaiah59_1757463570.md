# Isaiah 59 — The LORD sees and comes to save

**About this chapter**

People wonder why help seems so far away. They name the wrongs they have done. The LORD sees what is happening and steps in when no one else will.

## What stands between God and His people?

## Isaiah 59:1

The LORD can reach you and save you.

He can hear when you call.

## Isaiah 59:2

Your wrongs stand like a wall between you and your God.

Because of your sins, He has turned His face away.

He does not listen to you.

## Isaiah 59:3

Your hands are stained with the blood of people you have hurt.

Your fingers have done wrong.

You tell lies and whisper things that hurt others unfairly.

## Lies and crooked paths

## Isaiah 59:4

No one asks for a fair judgment.

No one speaks honestly in court.

People build their cases on empty claims and lies.

They plan trouble and bring evil into the world.

## Isaiah 59:5

Isaiah pictures those plans as snake eggs and spiderwebs.

Whoever eats the eggs dies.

Break one open, and out comes a dangerous snake.

## Isaiah 59:6

No one can weave those webs into clothes.

In the same way, their deeds give them no covering.

What their hands have done is wrong and violent.

## Isaiah 59:7

They hurry to do harm.

They are quick to kill innocent people.

They keep thinking up evil things.

Wherever they go, they leave ruin behind.

## Isaiah 59:8

They do not know how to live in peace.

They treat people unfairly wherever they go.

Isaiah pictures the way they live as a crooked path.

No one who follows it finds peace.

## The people speak

## Isaiah 59:9

Now the people speak.

“Justice is far from us.

We wait for someone to put things right.

We hope to see light, yet we walk in darkness.

## Isaiah 59:10

“We feel our way along a wall like people who cannot see.

Even at noon we stumble as if it were getting dark.

Other people are full of life.

We feel as helpless as the dead.

## Isaiah 59:11

“We growl like bears.

We moan like doves.

We hope for justice and find none.

The rescue we long for seems far away.

## We know what we have done

## Isaiah 59:12

“God, You have seen how often we have done wrong.

Our sins speak against us like witnesses in court.

We carry those wrongs with us.

We know what we have done.

## Isaiah 59:13

“We rebelled against the LORD.

We denied Him and turned away from our God.

We spoke in favor of cruelty and rebellion.

We made up lies in our hearts and spoke them aloud.

## Isaiah 59:14

“So justice has had to turn back.

What is right stands far away.

Imagine truth stumbling in the town square.

Honesty cannot even get in.”

## The LORD sees the injustice

## Isaiah 59:15

Truth is missing.

Anyone who turns from evil becomes a target.

The LORD saw all this.

It displeased Him that no one acted fairly.

## Isaiah 59:16

He looked for someone to step in.

He was astonished that no one spoke up to help.

So He used His own strength to bring rescue.

His own goodness kept Him going.

## Isaiah 59:17

Isaiah pictures the LORD dressing for battle.

Doing right is the armor that covers His chest.

His power to save is the helmet on His head.

He dresses to pay back those who do harm.

He wraps His strong determination around Him like a cloak.

## Isaiah 59:18

The LORD will repay people for what they have done.

His enemies will face His anger.

Even on faraway islands, He will repay those who oppose Him.

## Isaiah 59:19

People in the west, where the sun sets, will honor the LORD’s name with awe.

People in the east, where it rises, will see His glory and stand in awe.

He will come like a rushing flood driven by His breath.

## A promise for the generations

## Isaiah 59:20

The LORD says, “The Redeemer will come to Zion.

He will come to those in Jacob’s family who turn away from sin.” A redeemer is someone who sets people free.

## Isaiah 59:21

The LORD says, “This is my covenant, my lasting promise to them.

My Spirit will stay with you.

You will keep speaking the words I have given you.

Your children and grandchildren will speak them too, now and forever.”
