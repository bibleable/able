# Isaiah 34 — Owls will nest in Edom

**About this chapter**

Isaiah asks all nations to listen. He describes what will happen to Edom and the creatures that will make their homes there.

## A call to every nation

## Isaiah 34:1

People of every nation, come close and listen.

Everyone on earth, pay attention.

Let the whole world hear, with every living thing in it.

## Isaiah 34:2

The LORD is angry with all the nations.

He is furious with their armies.

He has decided to give them over to destruction.

Their soldiers will die.

## Isaiah 34:3

No one will bury the dead.

A foul smell will rise from their bodies.

Their blood will run down the mountains.

## Isaiah 34:4

The stars will come apart.

Isaiah pictures the sky rolling up like a scroll.

A scroll is a long sheet that rolls up at the ends.

The stars will drop like dry leaves falling from a vine or a fig tree.

![An open scroll with writing between its rolled ends.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Find the ends of the scroll**

Look at the rolled ends. Imagine rolling them together until the writing disappears. Isaiah uses that picture to describe what happens to the sky.

## Judgment comes to Edom

## Isaiah 34:5

The LORD says, “When my sword has finished its work in the heavens, it will come down on Edom.

I have chosen to bring judgment on that people.”

## Isaiah 34:6

Isaiah pictures the LORD’s sword covered in blood and fat.

He speaks of the blood of lambs and goats and the fat around rams’ kidneys.

The LORD’s judgment is like a great sacrifice in Bozrah, a city in Edom.

Many will die there.

## Isaiah 34:7

Wild oxen will fall too.

Young bulls and powerful bulls will fall together.

Blood will soak into the ground.

Fat will cover the soil.

## Isaiah 34:8

The LORD has set a day to punish those who wronged Zion.

Zion is Jerusalem.

The year has come for Him to act on its behalf.

## The land lies empty

## Isaiah 34:9

Edom’s streams will turn to tar.

Its soil will turn to sulfur, a substance that burns.

The land itself will blaze like tar on fire.

## Isaiah 34:10

Day and night the fire will burn.

Its smoke will rise forever.

Generation after generation the land will lie empty.

No travelers will pass through it again.

## Isaiah 34:11

Desert owls and screech owls will take over the land.

Great owls and ravens will live there.

Isaiah pictures the LORD measuring Edom with a builder’s lines.

What He marks out for Edom is ruin and disorder.

## Isaiah 34:12

No nobles will remain to name a king.

All the princes will lose their power.

## Wild animals find homes

## Isaiah 34:13

Thorn bushes will climb over the towers.

Prickly plants will cover the forts.

Jackals will wander there.

Ostriches will make their homes there.

## Isaiah 34:14

Desert animals will meet hyenas.

Wild goats will call to one another.

A creature of the night will find a quiet place to rest.

## Isaiah 34:15

An owl will make a nest and lay her eggs.

When the eggs hatch, she will gather her young under her wings.

Birds that hunt other animals will gather there, each with its mate.

## Read what the LORD has said

## Isaiah 34:16

Find the LORD’s scroll and read it.

Every one of these creatures will be there.

Each will have a mate.

The LORD has given the order.

His Spirit will gather them.

## Isaiah 34:17

The LORD has measured out the land for these creatures.

He has given each one its share.

It will be their home forever.

Their young will live there, generation after generation.
