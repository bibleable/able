# Isaiah 47 — Babylon loses its throne

**About this chapter**

Babylon once ruled other nations. Isaiah pictures the city as a queen who believes she will always be safe. The LORD tells her why her rule will end.

## From a throne to the dust

## Isaiah 47:1

The LORD speaks to Babylon as if the city were a young queen.

“Daughter of Babylon, come down and sit in the dust.

Daughter of the Chaldeans, sit on the ground.

Your throne is gone.

No one will call you delicate and gentle anymore.”

## Isaiah 47:2

The LORD tells her, “Take the heavy grinding stones.

Grind grain into flour.

Take off your veil.

Lift away your skirt and uncover your legs to cross the streams.

## Isaiah 47:3

“People will see you naked and see your shame.

I will punish the wrong you have done.

I will spare no one.”

## Babylon showed no mercy

## Isaiah 47:4

The Holy One of Israel is our Redeemer.

He is the one who sets us free.

His name is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

## Isaiah 47:5

The LORD says, “Sit quietly in the dark, daughter of the Chaldeans.

No one will call you queen over the kingdoms anymore.

## Isaiah 47:6

“I was angry with my own people.

I let the people who belong to me suffer disgrace.

I put them under your rule.

You showed them no mercy.

Even the oldest people had to carry a heavy load.

![A wooden yoke rests across the necks of two oxen.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

**Find the wooden bar**

The bar helps the oxen pull a load. Isaiah uses a heavy yoke to picture the hard work Babylon made even older people do.

## Isaiah 47:7

“You said, ‘I will be queen forever.’ You gave no thought to what you were doing.

You never thought about how it would end.

## A queen who thought she was safe

## Isaiah 47:8

“Listen now, Babylon.

You love your comforts and think you are safe.

You tell yourself, ‘No one can equal me.

I will never lose my husband.

I will never lose my children.’

## Isaiah 47:9

“Yet both losses will come in a single day.

You will lose your children and become a widow.

All your magic and powerful spells will fail to keep those losses away.

## Isaiah 47:10

“You felt safe doing wrong.

You said, ‘No one sees me.’ The very things you thought you knew led you astray.

You told yourself again, ‘No one can equal me.’

## Isaiah 47:11

“Trouble will come.

You will have no spell to send it away.

You will have no way to stop the disaster.

It will come suddenly, when you least expect it.

## Who can save Babylon?

## Isaiah 47:12

“Go ahead, then!

Try the spells and magic you have worked at since you were young.

Perhaps you can succeed!

Perhaps you can frighten someone!” The LORD challenges Babylon to see what its magic can do.

## Isaiah 47:13

“You have worn yourself out listening to advisers.

Some watch the stars and claim to tell your future each month.

Let them step forward and save you now!

## Isaiah 47:14

“They are like dry stalks in a fire.

The flames will burn them up.

They cannot even save themselves.

No glowing coals will be left to warm them.

No one will sit beside that fire.

## Isaiah 47:15

“Think of the people you have worked and traded with since you were young.

Each goes a different way.

None of them can save you.”
