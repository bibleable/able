# Isaiah 9 — A child who will rule in peace

**About this chapter**

Isaiah tells people in trouble about a child who will bring peace. Then Isaiah warns the people of Israel about the harm they are doing.

## Light for people in trouble

## Isaiah 9:1

The darkness will end for people in trouble.

Long ago God brought low the lands of Zebulun and Naphtali.

One day God will give honor to the Way of the Sea, the land beyond the Jordan River, and Galilee, where many nations live.

## Isaiah 9:2

The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light.

They lived in the shadow of death.

Now light shines on them.

Isaiah pictures hope coming like the morning sun.

## Isaiah 9:3

God, you have made the nation grow.

You have given the people more joy.

They celebrate before you like farmers bringing home a harvest.

They rejoice like soldiers sharing what they took after a victory.

## God breaks the heavy yoke

## Isaiah 9:4

You broke the yoke that weighed on their shoulders.

You broke the stick their cruel ruler used against them.

You freed them as you did when you defeated Midian.

![Two oxen joined by a wooden yoke.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

**Find the yoke**

Point to the wooden bar across the animals. It makes them pull together. Isaiah pictures a cruel ruler making people work for him. Breaking the yoke means setting the people free.

## Isaiah 9:5

Soldiers once marched in heavy boots.

Their clothes were stained with blood.

Now those boots and clothes will feed a fire.

## A child will be King

## Isaiah 9:6

A child has been born for us.

God has given us a son.

He will carry the duty of ruling us.

People will call Him Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.

A counselor helps people know what to do.

Everlasting means lasting forever.

## Isaiah 9:7

His rule will keep growing.

The peace He brings will never end.

He will sit on King David’s throne and rule David’s kingdom.

He will do what is right and treat people fairly.

This will hold His kingdom together forever.

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies is determined to do this.

## The people boast

## Isaiah 9:8

The Lord sent a message against Jacob’s people.

That message reached Israel.

Jacob’s people are the people of Israel.

## Isaiah 9:9

Everyone in Ephraim and in its capital, Samaria, will know the message.

Yet the people speak with pride.

## Isaiah 9:10

They say, “Our brick walls fell down.

We will build better walls with cut stone.

Someone cut down our sycamore trees.

We will plant tall cedars in their place.”

## Isaiah 9:11

The LORD has brought King Rezin’s enemies together.

He has stirred them up to attack.

## Isaiah 9:12

Aram attacks Israel from the east.

The Philistines attack from the west.

They are like hungry animals swallowing Israel whole.

Even after this, the LORD is still angry.

His hand is still raised to strike.

## Leaders guide the people the wrong way

## Isaiah 9:13

The people did not turn back to the God who struck them.

They did not seek the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

## Isaiah 9:14

So in one day the LORD will cut off Israel’s head and tail.

Isaiah also pictures a tall palm branch and a low reed.

Both will fall in one day.

## Isaiah 9:15

The head stands for the elders people honor.

The tail stands for the prophet who teaches lies.

A prophet is someone who claims to speak for God.

## Isaiah 9:16

The leaders guide the people the wrong way.

The people who follow them come to ruin.

## Isaiah 9:17

So the Lord takes no joy in their young men.

He shows no pity to children without fathers or to widows.

Everyone has turned from God and does evil.

Everyone says foolish things.

Even after this, the LORD is still angry.

His hand is still raised to strike.

## People turn against their own families

## Isaiah 9:18

Evil spreads like fire.

First the thorn bushes burn.

Then flames run through the thick forest.

Great clouds of smoke roll up.

## Isaiah 9:19

The anger of the LORD who commands heaven’s armies burns the land.

The people are like fuel for the fire.

No one spares even his own brother.

## Isaiah 9:20

People grab food on their right and stay hungry.

They eat on their left and still want more.

They even eat the flesh of their own children.

## Isaiah 9:21

The people of Manasseh and Ephraim tear each other apart.

Then both turn against Judah.

Even after this, the LORD is still angry.

His hand is still raised to strike.

**Pause and answer**

How will the promised King treat the people He rules?
