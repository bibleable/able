# Isaiah 41 — I will hold your hand

**About this chapter**

God calls the nations to speak. Then He speaks to Israel, a people who feel small and afraid. He promises to help them.

## The nations come forward

## Isaiah 41:1

The LORD says, “People on the islands, be quiet before me.

Let the nations gather their strength.

Come forward and speak.

Let us meet as people do in court, so the case can be decided.

## Isaiah 41:2

“Who called a man from the east to serve a just purpose?

Who gives him nations and brings kings under his power?

His sword turns them to dust.

His bow scatters them like dry husks in the wind.

## Isaiah 41:3

“He chases them and goes on safely.

He moves so swiftly his feet hardly touch the path.

## Isaiah 41:4

“Who has done this?

Who has called each generation into being from the beginning?

I have.

I am the LORD, the first and the last.”

## Workers fasten an idol in place

## Isaiah 41:5

People on the islands see this and are afraid.

Even people in the farthest lands tremble.

They come forward.

## Isaiah 41:6

They help each other.

One neighbor says to another, “Be strong!”

## Isaiah 41:7

The workers who make an idol encourage each other.

One shapes it.

Another works with gold.

One swings a hammer while another works at a block of metal called an anvil.

“The pieces are joined well,” a worker says.

Then he nails the idol down to keep it standing.

## God speaks to Israel

## Isaiah 41:8

Now the LORD speaks to Israel.

“You are my servant.

You belong to the family of Jacob, whom I chose.

Abraham, my friend, was your ancestor.

## Isaiah 41:9

“I brought you from the ends of the earth.

I called you from its farthest corners.

I said, ‘You are my servant.’ I chose you.

I have never turned you away.

## Isaiah 41:10

“Do not fear.

I am with you.

I am your God.

I will give you strength.

I will help you.

With my right hand I will hold you up.

I always do what is right.

## Isaiah 41:11

“Everyone who rages against you will face shame.

Those who fight you will lose their power and perish.

## Isaiah 41:12

“Even if you look for them, you will not find them.

The people who make war on you will come to nothing.

## The LORD holds Israel’s hand

## Isaiah 41:13

“I am the LORD your God.

I take hold of your right hand.

I tell you, ‘Do not fear.

I will help you.’

## Isaiah 41:14

“Jacob’s family, you are as small as a worm.

People of Israel, there are so few of you.

Do not be afraid.

I will help you.” The Holy One of Israel is their Redeemer.

That means He comes to rescue them.

## Isaiah 41:15

The LORD says, “I will make you like a new threshing sledge.” This is a farm tool with sharp teeth underneath.

“You will crush mountains with it.

The hills will break up like dry husks.

## Isaiah 41:16

“You will toss them into the air.

A strong wind will blow them away.

You will be glad because of the LORD.

You will praise the Holy One of Israel.

## Water for thirsty people

## Isaiah 41:17

“Poor people look for water and find none.

Their tongues are dry with thirst.

I, the LORD, will answer them.

I am Israel’s God.

I will never leave them.

## Isaiah 41:18

“I will make rivers flow on bare hills.

Springs will bubble up in the valleys.

There will be pools in the desert.

Water will flow from the dry ground.

![Water flows from springs on a hillside.](../pictures/acsah-springs.svg)

**Follow the water**

Find a spring and follow the water downhill. Imagine coming to this place thirsty. The LORD promises water where His people could find none.

## Isaiah 41:19

“I will plant cedar, acacia, myrtle and olive trees in the wilderness.

Cypress, elm and boxwood trees will grow together in the desert.

## Isaiah 41:20

“Then everyone can see what has happened.

They can think about it and understand.

I, the LORD, have done this.

The Holy One of Israel has made it.”

## Can the idols answer?

## Isaiah 41:21

The LORD, the King of Jacob’s people, challenges the idols.

“Bring your case before me.

Give your reasons.

## Isaiah 41:22

“Tell us what is going to happen.

Explain the things that happened long ago.

Let us think about them and see how they turned out.

Or tell us what lies ahead.

## Isaiah 41:23

“Tell us the future so we can know you are gods.

Do something, good or bad!

Give us something that will make us all tremble.

## Isaiah 41:24

“You are nothing.

What you do has no value.

I find it hateful when people choose you.

## Who told Jerusalem the news?

## Isaiah 41:25

“I called a man to come from the north, and he came.

He comes from the sunrise and calls on my name.

He will walk over rulers like a potter pressing soft clay underfoot.

## Isaiah 41:26

“Which of you told us about him long ago?

Did anyone hear you and say, ‘You were right’?

None of you spoke beforehand.

No one heard a word from you.

## Isaiah 41:27

“I was the first to tell Zion, ‘Look!

Here they are!’ I sent Jerusalem a messenger with good news.

## Isaiah 41:28

“I look among the idols.

No one there can give advice.

When I ask a question, none of them can answer.

## Isaiah 41:29

“They fool those who trust them.

All their work comes to nothing.

Their statues are as empty as the wind.”
