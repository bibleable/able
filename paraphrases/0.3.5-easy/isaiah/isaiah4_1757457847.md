# Isaiah 4 — A shelter over Zion

**About this chapter**

After describing the losses in Jerusalem, Isaiah looks ahead. He pictures the LORD making the city clean and giving the people who remain a place of safety.

## Seven women ask for a name

## Isaiah 4:1

In those days seven women will take hold of one man.

They will say, “We will bring our own bread and clothes.

Just let us carry your name as your wives.

Take away our shame.”

## New growth and a clean city

## Isaiah 4:2

On that day the Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious.

Isaiah pictures new growth.

The people of Israel who survive will look at the fruit of their land with pride and joy.

## Isaiah 4:3

Those who remain in Zion will be called holy.

Zion is Jerusalem.

Everyone whose name is on the list of the living there will belong to God.

## Isaiah 4:4

The Lord will wash away the dirt from the women of Zion.

He will clean the bloodstains from the heart of Jerusalem.

Isaiah pictures the guilt of the city as a stain.

The Lord will cleanse it by a spirit of judgment and fire.

## Shade and shelter

## Isaiah 4:5

Then the LORD will make a cloud of smoke over Mount Zion by day.

At night a bright flame will shine there.

This will cover the whole mountain and everyone gathered there.

Over all that glory there will be a canopy, a covering spread overhead.

## Isaiah 4:6

It will give shade when the day is hot.

When wind and rain beat down, people will have a safe place to hide.

**Find the shade**

Look beneath the roof. Imagine stepping out of the midday sun and standing there. Isaiah pictures shelter like this spread over everyone gathered on the mountain.
