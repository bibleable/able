# Isaiah 39 — Hezekiah shows his treasures

**About this chapter**

Visitors from Babylon come to see King Hezekiah after his illness. Later Isaiah asks what the visitors saw.

## Visitors bring letters and a gift

## Isaiah 39:1

Merodach-baladan was the king of Babylon.

His father was Baladan.

The king heard that Hezekiah had been sick and had recovered.

So he sent Hezekiah letters and a gift.

## Isaiah 39:2

Hezekiah was glad to welcome the messengers.

He showed them his silver and gold.

He showed them his spices and costly oil.

He took them to see all his weapons.

They saw everything he kept in his storerooms.

He showed them everything in his palace and throughout his kingdom.

## Isaiah asks about the visitors

## Isaiah 39:3

Isaiah the prophet went to see King Hezekiah.

Isaiah asked, “Where did those men come from?

What did they say to you?”

Hezekiah answered, “They came from Babylon, a long way from here.”

## Isaiah 39:4

Isaiah asked, “What did they see in your palace?”

Hezekiah said, “They saw everything here.

I showed them every treasure I own.”

## Isaiah speaks about the years ahead

## Isaiah 39:5

Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Listen to the message from the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

## Isaiah 39:6

“One day people will carry everything in your palace to Babylon.

They will take all that your ancestors have stored up.

The LORD says nothing will be left.

## Isaiah 39:7

“They will take away some of your own family born in years to come.

These men will become eunuchs in the palace of Babylon’s king.”

A eunuch was a man whose body had been changed so he could not father children.

## Isaiah 39:8

Hezekiah told Isaiah, “The message you brought from the LORD is good.”

He thought, “At least there will be peace and safety while I live.”
