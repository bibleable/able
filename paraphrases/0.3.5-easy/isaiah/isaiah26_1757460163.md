# Isaiah 26 — A song of trust in the LORD

**About this chapter**

Isaiah gives the people of Judah a song to sing. They remember what God has done and wait for Him to act again.

## Open the city gates

## Isaiah 26:1

On that day people in Judah will sing this song.

“Our city is strong.

God saves us.

His care surrounds us like the walls that protect a city.”

## Isaiah 26:2

Open the gates!

Let the nation that does right come in.

These people stay faithful to God.

![Two city gates joined by a stretch of wall.](../pictures/valley-gate-to-dung-gate.svg)

**Find a way into the city**

Trace the wall until you reach a gate. Imagine the gate opening so people can enter. The song welcomes people who stay faithful to God.

## Isaiah 26:3

LORD, You give lasting, complete peace to those who keep their minds fixed on You.

They trust You.

## Isaiah 26:4

Always trust the LORD.

The LORD God is our Rock forever.

Like a great rock, He stands firm.

## The high city falls

## Isaiah 26:5

He brings down those who live high above others.

Their city once stood tall.

He brings it down to the ground, right into the dust.

## Isaiah 26:6

Now poor people walk over it.

Those who suffered under others trample it underfoot.

## A level path

## Isaiah 26:7

LORD, You make a level path for people who do right.

You clear the way ahead of them.

## Isaiah 26:8

LORD, we wait for You.

We follow the way You say is right.

More than anything, we want people to know and honor Your name.

## Isaiah 26:9

At night I long for You.

When morning comes, I look for You.

When You judge what happens on earth, people learn what is right.

## Some people refuse to learn

## Isaiah 26:10

Even when You show kindness to people who do evil, they do not learn.

They live among people who do right.

Yet they treat others unfairly.

They do not see how great the LORD is.

## Isaiah 26:11

LORD, You have raised Your hand to act.

They do not see it.

They will see how deeply You care for Your people.

Then they will feel ashamed.

The fire meant for Your enemies will burn them up.

## The LORD gives peace

## Isaiah 26:12

LORD, You will give us peace.

Everything we have achieved, You have done for us.

## Isaiah 26:13

LORD our God, other masters have ruled us.

Yet we honor Your name alone.

## Isaiah 26:14

Those rulers are dead.

They will never rise to rule us again.

You judged and destroyed them.

You wiped away even the memory of them.

## Isaiah 26:15

LORD, You have made our nation grow.

Yes, You have made it grow!

You have extended its borders in every direction.

This brings honor to You.

## A quiet prayer

## Isaiah 26:16

LORD, Your people came looking for You in their trouble.

When You corrected them, they whispered their prayer to You.

## Isaiah 26:17

LORD, we cried out before You.

We were like a woman in pain just before her baby is born.

## Isaiah 26:18

We felt all that pain, yet we gave birth only to wind.

We could not save the earth.

We could not bring new life into the world.

## The dead will rise

## Isaiah 26:19

But Your dead will live!

Their bodies will rise.

You who lie in the dust, wake up and sing!

Fresh dew covers the ground in the morning.

Like that dew, new life will come.

The earth will give up its dead.

## Isaiah 26:20

“My people, go into your rooms.

Shut the doors behind you.

Hide for a little while until the anger passes.”

## Isaiah 26:21

Look!

The LORD is coming from His home.

He will punish the people of the earth for doing wrong.

The earth will uncover the blood people have spilled.

Everyone will see those whose deaths were hidden.

**Pause and answer**

Who do the singers trust to give them peace?
