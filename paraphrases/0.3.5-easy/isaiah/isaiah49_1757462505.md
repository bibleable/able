# Isaiah 49 — I will never forget you

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives His servant work that reaches far beyond Israel. Then He answers the people of Jerusalem, who feel alone and forgotten.

## The servant speaks

## Isaiah 49:1

The LORD’s servant speaks.

“People on the islands, listen to me.

People far away, pay attention.

Before I was born, the LORD called me.

While I was still inside my mother, He gave me my name.

## Isaiah 49:2

“He made my words like a sharp sword.

He kept me hidden in the shade of His hand.

I was like a polished arrow kept ready in His arrow case.

## Isaiah 49:3

“He told me, ‘Israel, you are my servant.

Through you I will show my glory.’

## Isaiah 49:4

“I thought, ‘I have worked so hard for nothing.

All my strength is gone, and what have I achieved?’ Yet the LORD will judge my work fairly.

I trust my God to reward me.”

## A light for the nations

## Isaiah 49:5

The servant says, “Before I was born, the LORD formed me to serve Him.

My work is to bring Jacob’s people back to Him and gather Israel together.

The LORD honors me.

My God gives me strength.

## Isaiah 49:6

“He says, ‘You will help the tribes of Jacob stand again.

You will bring back the people of Israel who have survived.

I have more for you to do.

You will be a light for other nations.

Through you I will bring rescue to the ends of the earth.’”

## Isaiah 49:7

The LORD is Israel’s Holy One, the one who rescues His people.

He speaks to the servant whom rulers order about and whom the nation hates.

“Kings will stand up to honor you.

Princes will bow down.

The LORD keeps His word.

The Holy One of Israel has chosen you.”

## Prisoners come home

## Isaiah 49:8

The LORD tells His servant, “When the time comes to show favor, I will answer you.

When it is time to save, I will help you.

I will protect you.

Through you I will keep my covenant, my lasting promise to the people.

You will restore their land.

You will give families back the places that now lie empty.

## Isaiah 49:9

“Tell prisoners, ‘Come out!’

Tell people in darkness, ‘Come where you can be seen!’

Like sheep finding grass, they will find food along the roads.

Even the bare hills will feed them.

## Isaiah 49:10

“They will have enough to eat and drink.

The burning sun will not harm them.

The One who cares for them will guide them to springs of water.

## Isaiah 49:11

“I will make roads across my mountains.

I will raise up highways for the travelers.

## Isaiah 49:12

“Look!

People are coming from far away.

Some come from the north and the west.

Others come from the land of Aswan.”

## Isaiah 49:13

Heavens, shout for joy!

Earth, be glad!

Mountains, sing!

The LORD has comforted His people.

He will show kindness to those who suffer.

## Zion feels forgotten

## Isaiah 49:14

Zion says, “The LORD has left me.

My Lord has forgotten me.” Zion is Jerusalem.

Isaiah speaks of the city as a mother missing her children.

## Isaiah 49:15

The LORD answers, “Could a mother forget the baby she feeds at her breast?

Could she stop caring for the child she bore?

Even if she forgot, I would never forget you.

## Isaiah 49:16

“Look, I have written you on the palms of my hands.

I always see your walls before me.” The LORD pictures a mark on His hands to show that He remembers Zion.

**Follow the wall**

Trace the wall from one gate to the other. The people see a damaged city. The LORD tells them that He still sees their walls and remembers them.

## Isaiah 49:17

“Your builders hurry back.

The people who tore you down are leaving.

## A city fills with children

## Isaiah 49:18

“Look up, Zion!

See them gathering and coming to you.

As surely as I live, they will be like jewels you put on for your wedding day.

## Isaiah 49:19

“Your land now lies ruined and empty.

Soon there will hardly be room for all your people.

Those who destroyed your land will be far away.

## Isaiah 49:20

“You thought you had lost your children.

Yet you will hear children saying, ‘It is too crowded here.

Make room for us to live!’

## Isaiah 49:21

“You will wonder, ‘Where did all these children come from?

I lost my children and could have no more.

People drove me from home and turned me away.

Who raised these children?

I thought I was all alone.’”

## The nations help

## Isaiah 49:22

The Lord GOD says, “Watch me lift my hand to the nations.

I will raise a flag for the peoples to see.

They will carry your sons in their arms.

They will bring your daughters on their shoulders.

## Isaiah 49:23

“Kings will care for you like foster fathers.

Queens will nurse you like mothers.

They will bow facedown and lick the dust at your feet.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

People who place their hope in me will never be ashamed.”

## God answers a hard question

## Isaiah 49:24

Can anyone take back what a strong warrior has seized?

Can anyone free people held by a cruel ruler?

## Isaiah 49:25

The LORD answers, “Yes.

Even a mighty warrior will lose his captives.

Even a cruel ruler will lose what he seized.

I will fight those who fight you.

I will save your children.

## Isaiah 49:26

“I will make those who hurt you eat their own flesh.

They will be drunk on their own blood as if it were wine.

Then everyone will know that I, the LORD, save you and set you free.

I am the Mighty One of Jacob.”

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD promise Zion when she says He has forgotten her?
