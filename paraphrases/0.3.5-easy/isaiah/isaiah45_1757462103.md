# Isaiah 45 — God calls Cyrus by name

**About this chapter**

God gives Cyrus a task. He is to rebuild Jerusalem and set its people free. Then God calls people everywhere to turn to Him.

## The gates open for Cyrus

## Isaiah 45:1

The LORD speaks to Cyrus, the ruler He has chosen.

“I have taken hold of your right hand.

Nations will fall before you.

I will take weapons from their kings.

City gates will stand open for you.

## Isaiah 45:2

“I will go ahead of you and level the mountains.

I will break down bronze gates.

I will cut through the iron bars that hold them shut.

## Isaiah 45:3

“I will give you treasures kept in the dark.

You will receive riches from secret hiding places.

Then you will know who called you by name.

I am the LORD, the God of Israel.

## Why God chose Cyrus

## Isaiah 45:4

“I call you by name for the sake of my servant Israel, the family of Jacob.

I chose these people.

I gave you a name of honor even though you did not know me.

## Isaiah 45:5

“I am the LORD.

I alone am God.

I will get you ready for battle, even though you did not know me.

## Isaiah 45:6

“Then people everywhere will know that I alone am the LORD.

They will know it from the lands of sunrise to the lands of sunset.

## Isaiah 45:7

“I make the light and the darkness.

I bring good times, and I bring disaster.

I, the LORD, do all these things.

## Rain on the earth and clay in a potter’s hands

## Isaiah 45:8

“Let what is right pour from the sky like rain.

Let the earth open up.

Let rescue and goodness grow together like new plants.

I, the LORD, make this happen.

## Isaiah 45:9

“Trouble comes to anyone who quarrels with their Maker.

Think of one clay pot among many.

Does the clay ask the potter, ‘What are you making?’ Does the pot say its maker has no hands?

![A clay jar with a wide middle and a narrow neck.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the jar**

Trace its shape from the base to the rim. Picture the potter’s hands shaping the clay. Isaiah asks whether the clay can tell its maker what to do.

## Isaiah 45:10

“Trouble comes to anyone who challenges a father about the child he has fathered, or a mother about the child she has brought into the world.”

## Jerusalem will be built again

## Isaiah 45:11

The LORD made Israel.

He is Israel’s Holy One.

He says, “Will you question me about what will happen to my children?

Will you tell me what to do with the work of my hands?

## Isaiah 45:12

“I made the earth and the people on it.

My hands stretched out the heavens.

All the stars are under my command.

## Isaiah 45:13

“I will raise up Cyrus to do what is right.

I will clear the way for him.

He will rebuild my city, Jerusalem.

He will free my people who had to leave their homes.

He will do it without payment or reward.” The LORD who commands heaven’s armies has spoken.

## People recognize Israel’s God

## Isaiah 45:14

The LORD says, “The goods of Egypt and Cush will be yours.

The tall people of Seba will come to you.

They will walk behind you in chains and bow before you.

They will say, ‘God really is with you.

There is no other God.’”

## Isaiah 45:15

God of Israel, You truly are a God who hides Himself.

Yet You are the one who saves.

## Isaiah 45:16

People who make statues to worship will face shame.

Together they will go away in disgrace.

## Isaiah 45:17

The LORD will save Israel.

What He does to save you will last forever.

You will never have to hang your heads in shame.

## The LORD speaks openly

## Isaiah 45:18

The LORD made the heavens.

He shaped the earth and set it firmly in place.

He made it to be a home where people could live.

He says, “I am the LORD.

There is no other.

## Isaiah 45:19

“I have spoken openly.

I did not speak from some secret place in the dark.

I never told Jacob’s family to search for me in an empty wasteland.

I, the LORD, tell the truth.

I say what is right.

## Isaiah 45:20

“Come close together, all who have escaped from the nations.

Some people carry wooden idols.

They do not understand that the god they pray to cannot save them.

## Isaiah 45:21

“Talk it over together, then give your answer.

Who told you long ago that these things would happen?

I did.

I am the LORD.

I alone am God.

I do what is right, and I save.

## An invitation to the whole earth

## Isaiah 45:22

“People everywhere, even at the ends of the earth, turn to me.

Let me save you.

I alone am God.

## Isaiah 45:23

“I have made a solemn promise in my own name.

I have spoken the truth.

I will never take back my words.

Everyone will kneel before me.

Everyone will promise to be faithful to me.

## Isaiah 45:24

“They will say, ‘The LORD alone gives us strength and makes us right.’” Those who were angry with Him will come before Him in shame.

## Isaiah 45:25

The LORD will declare the people of Israel to be in the right.

They will rejoice in Him.

**Pause and answer**

What will Cyrus do for the people who had to leave their homes?
