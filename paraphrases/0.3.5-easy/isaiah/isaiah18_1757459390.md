# Isaiah 18 — Gifts from a land of rivers

**About this chapter**

Isaiah speaks about a distant land and its messengers. He asks everyone to watch and listen. The LORD is watching too.

## Messengers on the water

## Isaiah 18:1

Isaiah warns a land along the rivers of Cush.

The air there hums with the sound of wings.

## Isaiah 18:2

That land sends messengers across the water.

Their boats are made from papyrus, a tall water plant.

“Go quickly, messengers!

Go to the people who are tall and smooth-skinned.

People far away fear this strong nation.

Their speech sounds unfamiliar to others.

Rivers run through their land and divide it.”

## Isaiah 18:3

Everyone on earth, watch the mountains.

When someone raises a flag there, you will see it.

When someone blows a ram’s horn, you will hear it.

A ram is a male sheep.

People used its horn to sound a signal.

## The LORD watches quietly

## Isaiah 18:4

The LORD told Isaiah, “From my home I will watch quietly.”

Think of heat shimmering in the sunshine.

Think of mist resting over the fields at harvest time.

That is how quietly the LORD watches.

## Isaiah 18:5

Before the grape harvest, the flowers fall away.

The little grapes begin to ripen.

Then the LORD cuts off the new shoots with a pruning knife.

He cuts away the branches and throws them aside.

Isaiah pictures the LORD acting before the fruit is ready to pick.

**Find the branch**

Trace the branch that holds the grapes. Isaiah pictures someone cutting that branch before the grapes are ripe.

## Isaiah 18:6

Those who fall will lie out in the open.

Birds from the mountains will feed on them in summer.

Wild animals will feed on them in winter.

## Gifts come to Mount Zion

## Isaiah 18:7

Then the tall, smooth-skinned people will bring gifts.

They come from the strong nation that many people fear.

They speak a language others find unfamiliar.

Rivers divide their land.

They will bring their gifts to Mount Zion.

There people honor the name of the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

**Pause and answer**

Where will the people from the land of rivers bring their gifts?
