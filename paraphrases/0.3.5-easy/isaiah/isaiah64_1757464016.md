# Isaiah 64 — You are the potter

**About this chapter**

The people of Israel pray together. Their temple lies in ruins. They tell the LORD what they have done wrong and ask Him to come close again.

## LORD, come down

## Isaiah 64:1

LORD, if only You would tear the sky open!

If only You would come down!

Even the mountains would shake when You came.

## Isaiah 64:2

Fire makes dry twigs burst into flame.

Fire makes water boil.

Come with power like that!

Then Your enemies will know who You are.

The nations will shake with fear when You come.

## Isaiah 64:3

Long ago You did things that amazed us.

We never expected them.

You came down, and the mountains shook.

## Isaiah 64:4

From long ago, who has seen a God like You?

Who has ever heard of one?

You act for people who wait for You.

## We have sinned

## Isaiah 64:5

You welcome people who gladly do what is right.

They remember how You want them to live.

But we sinned, and You were angry.

How can You save us if we keep sinning?

## Isaiah 64:6

Our sin has made us all unclean before You.

Even our good deeds are like dirty rags.

We dry up like leaves.

The wind blows dry leaves away.

That is how our sins carry us away.

## Isaiah 64:7

No one calls on You for help.

No one makes the effort to reach out to You.

You have turned Your face away from us.

You have left us in the grip of our sins.

## Remember that we are Your people

## Isaiah 64:8

Yet You, LORD, are our Father.

A potter shapes clay with both hands.

You are like the potter.

We are like the clay.

Your hands shaped every one of us.

![A jar shaped from clay.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

## Isaiah 64:9

LORD, please do not stay so angry with us.

Please do not hold our sins against us forever.

Look at us, we pray.

All of us are Your people.

## Isaiah 64:10

Your holy cities are like empty stretches of land.

Zion lies in ruins.

Zion is another name for Jerusalem.

The city stands empty.

## Isaiah 64:11

Our ancestors praised You in Your holy, beautiful temple.

Now fire has burned it down.

Everything we loved lies in ruins.

## Isaiah 64:12

LORD, after all this, will You still hold back?

Will You stay silent?

Will You keep making us suffer so much?

**Pause and answer**

In this prayer, who is like the potter?
