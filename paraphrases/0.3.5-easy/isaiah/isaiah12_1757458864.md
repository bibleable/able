# Isaiah 12 — A song of thanks

**About this chapter**

Isaiah gives God’s people words to sing when God brings them home. The song begins with one voice. Then everyone joins in.

## I will trust the LORD

## Isaiah 12:1

On that day you will say, “LORD, I praise You.

You were angry with me.

Now Your anger has passed.

You have comforted me.

## Isaiah 12:2

“God is the one who saves me.

I will trust Him.

I will not be afraid.

The LORD God gives me strength.

He is my song because He has saved me.”

## Share the good news

## Isaiah 12:3

With joy you will draw water from the springs of salvation.

A spring is a place where water flows from the ground.

Salvation means that God saves you.

Isaiah pictures the joy of God’s help as the joy of finding water.

![Water flowing from springs.](../pictures/acsah-springs.svg)

**Find the water**

Trace the water with your finger. Imagine how glad you would be to find a spring when you were thirsty.

## Isaiah 12:4

On that day you will say, “Give thanks to the LORD!

Call out His name.

Tell people everywhere what He has done.

Tell them how great His name is.

## Isaiah 12:5

“Sing to the LORD!

He has done wonderful things.

Let people everywhere on earth hear about them.

## Isaiah 12:6

“People of Zion, shout for joy and sing!

The Holy One of Israel is great, and He is here among you.”

Zion is another name for Jerusalem.

**Pause and answer**

Why do the people of Zion sing for joy?
