# Isaiah 21 — A lookout reports that Babylon has fallen

**About this chapter**

Isaiah listens for news of Babylon. He also gives messages about Dumah and Arabia.

## Isaiah sees a frightening vision

## Isaiah 21:1

Isaiah gives a warning about the Desert by the Sea.

An enemy comes from a frightening desert land.

The enemy sweeps in like a twisting wind in the Negev, the dry land in the south.

## Isaiah 21:2

God shows Isaiah something terrible.

People still betray those who trust them.

Others keep destroying what they find.

The order comes, “Advance, Elam!

Surround the city, Media!

I will put an end to all the groaning.”

## Isaiah 21:3

Isaiah says, “My whole body aches.

The pain grips me like the pain of giving birth.

What I hear leaves me confused.

What I see terrifies me.

## Isaiah 21:4

“My heart feels weak.

I shake with fear.

I had wanted evening to come.

Now the evening fills me with dread.”

## The lookout takes his place

## Isaiah 21:5

People set the table and spread out a rug.

They eat and drink.

Then comes the call, “Get up, leaders!

Put oil on your shields!” They must get ready for battle.

## Isaiah 21:6

The Lord told Isaiah, “Put someone on watch.

Tell him to report what he sees.

## Isaiah 21:7

“He must watch for war chariots and teams of horsemen.

He must watch for riders on donkeys and camels.

When they come, he must pay close attention.”

## Isaiah 21:8

The lookout shouted, “My lord, day after day I stand on the tower.

Night after night I stay at my post.

## News about Babylon

## Isaiah 21:9

“Look!

Here come men on horses.

They ride in pairs.” Then a voice called out, “Babylon has fallen!

Babylon has fallen!

The statues of its gods lie broken on the ground.”

## Isaiah 21:10

Isaiah says, “My people, you have suffered like grain beaten on a threshing floor.

I am telling you what I heard from Israel’s God.

He is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.” A threshing floor is a place where people beat grain loose from the stalks.

![A stalk with ripe grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Find the grain**

Point to the grain at the top of the stalk. Think about the blows that knock the grain loose. Isaiah uses that picture to speak to people who have suffered.

## A question in the night

## Isaiah 21:11

Isaiah also gives a warning about Dumah.

Someone calls to him from Seir, “Watchman, how much of the night is left?

Watchman, how much of the night is left?”

## Isaiah 21:12

The watchman answers, “Morning is coming.

Night is coming too.

If you want to ask, come back and ask again.”

## Food and water for people fleeing war

## Isaiah 21:13

Isaiah gives a warning about Arabia.

“Travelers from Dedan, you travel in groups.

You must spend the night among the bushes of Arabia.

## Isaiah 21:14

“People of Tema, bring water to the thirsty.

Go out with food to meet the people who have fled their homes.

## Isaiah 21:15

“They are running from drawn swords.

They are running from bows ready to shoot.

They are running from the horrors of battle.”

## Isaiah 21:16

The Lord told Isaiah, “Count a year as carefully as workers count their time on a job.

Within that year Kedar will lose everything that makes it proud.

## Isaiah 21:17

“Only a few of Kedar’s fighting men who shoot arrows will remain.” The LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken.
