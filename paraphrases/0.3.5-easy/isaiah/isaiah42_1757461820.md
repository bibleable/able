# Isaiah 42 — A gentle servant brings justice

**About this chapter**

God describes the servant He has chosen to bring justice. Later He asks His own people to listen to a warning they have ignored.

## The servant God has chosen

## Isaiah 42:1

God says, “Here is my servant.

I hold Him up.

I chose Him, and He brings me joy.

I will put my Spirit on Him.

He will bring justice to the nations.” Justice means treating people fairly and putting wrongs right.

## Isaiah 42:2

The servant will not shout.

He will not raise His voice to make people hear Him in the streets.

## Isaiah 42:3

He will not snap a reed whose stem is already bent.

He will not put out a lamp whose wick barely glows.

These pictures show how gently He acts.

He will be faithful as He brings justice.

![A tall reed bends to one side.](../pictures/bending-reed.svg)

**Find the bend**

Follow the stem to the place where it bends. Imagine how easily it could snap. The servant leaves even a damaged stem unbroken.

## Isaiah 42:4

He will keep His strength and courage until He has established justice on earth.

People on distant islands will put their hope in His teaching.

## A light for the nations

## Isaiah 42:5

The LORD God stretched out the sky.

He spread out the earth and made everything that grows on it.

He gives people breath.

Everyone who walks the earth has life from Him.

This God speaks to His servant.

## Isaiah 42:6

“I, the LORD, have called You to do what is right.

I will hold Your hand and keep You safe.

Through You I will give my people a covenant, a lasting promise.

You will be a light to guide the nations.

## Isaiah 42:7

“You will open blind eyes.

You will bring prisoners out of their cells.

You will free people who sit in dark prisons.

## Isaiah 42:8

“I am the LORD.

That is my name.

My glory belongs to me alone.

The praise due to me does not belong to statues people worship.

## Isaiah 42:9

“What I announced before has happened.

Now I am telling you new things.

You will hear about them from me before they happen.”

## A new song from land and sea

## Isaiah 42:10

Sing a new song to the LORD!

Praise Him from the farthest parts of the earth.

You who sail the sea, join in.

Let everything in the sea praise Him.

Let the islands and their people sing.

## Isaiah 42:11

Let the desert towns lift their voices.

People in the villages of Kedar, call out!

People of Sela, sing for joy.

Shout from the mountaintops!

## Isaiah 42:12

Give the LORD honor.

Tell the people on the islands how great He is.

## Isaiah 42:13

The LORD goes out like a mighty warrior, ready to fight.

He gives a loud shout of victory over His enemies.

## God leads people along a new path

## Isaiah 42:14

The LORD says, “For a long time I have kept quiet.

I have held myself back.

Now I will cry out like a woman giving birth.

I will gasp and breathe hard.

## Isaiah 42:15

“I will lay waste the hills and mountains.

The plants on them will dry up.

Riverbeds will become dry land.

I will drain the marshes.

## Isaiah 42:16

“I will take blind people along roads they have never known.

I will guide them.

Ahead of them I will turn darkness into light.

I will make the rough ground level.

I will do this for them.

I will never leave them.

## Isaiah 42:17

“Some people trust statues made of metal.

They tell those statues, ‘You are our gods.’ Those people will turn back full of shame.

## A messenger who does not listen

## Isaiah 42:18

“Listen, you who are deaf!

Look, you who are blind, and see!”

## Isaiah 42:19

God asks, “Who is as blind as my servant?

Who is as deaf as the messenger I send?

I made a covenant with my servant.

Yet who is as blind as the one who serves the LORD?”

## Isaiah 42:20

God tells His people, “You have seen a great deal, yet you pay no attention.

Your ears are open, yet you do not listen.”

## Isaiah 42:21

The LORD loves what is right.

It pleased Him to show how great and wonderful His teaching is.

## Who will pay attention?

## Isaiah 42:22

Yet enemies have robbed these people.

They lie trapped in caves or locked in dark cells.

Their enemies have carried them off.

No one comes to rescue them.

No one says, “Send them back!”

## Isaiah 42:23

Who among you will pay attention?

Who will listen and obey from now on?

## Isaiah 42:24

Who let robbers take what belonged to Jacob’s family, the people of Israel?

The LORD did.

We sinned against Him.

His people refused to follow His ways.

They would not obey His teaching.

## Isaiah 42:25

So the LORD poured out His anger on them.

War swept over them like a fierce fire.

Flames surrounded them, yet they did not understand.

Even as the fire burned them, they did not take the warning to heart.

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise to do for people on a road they have never known?
