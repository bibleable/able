# Isaiah 24 — Songs from the ends of the earth

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes a city where the music has stopped. Then he hears people singing far away. They are praising the LORD.

## The earth lies in ruins

## Isaiah 24:1

Look!

The LORD will lay the earth in ruins.

He will twist the ground out of shape.

He will scatter the people who live on it.

## Isaiah 24:2

The same thing will happen to everyone.

It will happen to priests and the people they serve.

It will happen to servants and the men and women they work for.

It will happen to buyers and sellers.

It will happen to people who lend and people who borrow.

It will happen to those who owe money and those waiting for repayment.

## Isaiah 24:3

The whole earth will lie in ruins.

People will take everything of value.

The LORD has said this will happen.

## The people have broken their agreement

## Isaiah 24:4

The earth dries up as if it were grieving.

The whole world grows weak.

Even its most important people lose their strength.

## Isaiah 24:5

The people have made the earth unclean.

They have disobeyed God’s laws and ignored His commands.

They have broken the lasting agreement called a covenant.

## Isaiah 24:6

So a curse spreads over the earth.

The people must answer for the wrong they have done.

Fire burns the people of the earth.

Only a few survive.

## The music stops

## Isaiah 24:7

The fresh wine dries up.

The grapevines wither.

People who once celebrated now groan.

## Isaiah 24:8

The tambourines stop beating.

The partygoers fall silent.

No one plays a joyful tune on the harp.

## Isaiah 24:9

People no longer sing as they drink wine.

Strong drink tastes bitter to them.

## Isaiah 24:10

The city is in confusion.

Its buildings lie broken.

Every house is shut so no one can enter.

## Isaiah 24:11

People in the streets cry out for wine.

Their joy turns to sadness.

Across the land the celebrations stop.

## Isaiah 24:12

Only ruins remain in the city.

Its gate is a pile of broken stones.

## Voices rise in praise

## Isaiah 24:13

Think of an olive tree after people pick the olives.

Think of the few grapes left after the harvest.

That is how few people will remain among the nations.

**Picture the vine after harvest**

Find the bunch of grapes. Imagine picking almost every grape. The few grapes left help us picture the people who survive.

## Isaiah 24:14

Those who remain lift their voices and shout for joy.

In the west they tell how great the LORD is.

## Isaiah 24:15

People in the east, give the LORD honor!

People on the islands, praise the name of the LORD.

He is the God of Israel.

## Isaiah 24:16

From the ends of the earth we hear people singing.

“Give honor to the One who does what is right!” Yet Isaiah cries, “I am wasting away!

I am wasting away!

What sorrow is mine!

People keep betraying those who trust them.”

## The earth shakes

## Isaiah 24:17

People of the earth, terror lies ahead of you.

A deep pit and a trap lie ahead too.

## Isaiah 24:18

Someone hears a frightening noise and runs.

They fall into the pit.

They climb out, only to get caught in the trap.

The windows of heaven open wide.

The very ground beneath the earth shakes.

## Isaiah 24:19

The earth breaks apart.

Great cracks split it open.

The whole earth shakes violently.

## Isaiah 24:20

The earth stumbles like someone who has drunk too much.

It sways like a little hut.

The wrong its people have done weighs it down.

It falls and never rises again.

## The LORD rules in Jerusalem

## Isaiah 24:21

On that day the LORD will punish the powers in heaven above.

He will also punish the kings on earth below.

## Isaiah 24:22

They will be like prisoners gathered in a deep pit.

The LORD will shut them in prison.

After many days He will punish them.

## Isaiah 24:23

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies will rule on Mount Zion in Jerusalem.

His elders will see His great glory.

Isaiah pictures even the moon and the sun feeling ashamed beside Him.
