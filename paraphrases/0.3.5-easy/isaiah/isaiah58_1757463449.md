# Isaiah 58 — Share your bread with the hungry

**About this chapter**

The people go without food and ask why God seems silent. The LORD points to what happens to the workers, the hungry, and the people without homes around them.

## Tell the people what is wrong

## Isaiah 58:1

The LORD says, “Call out!

Let your voice ring like a ram’s horn.

Tell my people, the family of Jacob, how they have sinned.

## Isaiah 58:2

“Every day they come looking for me.

They enjoy learning my ways.

They act like people who do right and follow my rules about justice.

They ask me for fair decisions.

They say they love having God near.”

## The people ask about fasting

## Isaiah 58:3

The people ask, “Why do we fast if You never notice?

Why humble ourselves if You do not see?” To fast means to go without food for a time.

The LORD answers, “On the very day you fast, you do as you please.

You treat your workers harshly.

## Isaiah 58:4

“You go without food, then quarrel and fight.

You even hit people with your fists.

You cannot fast like this and expect me to hear your prayers in heaven.

## Isaiah 58:5

“Is this the kind of fast I want?

You bow your head like a bending reed.

You sit on rough cloth and ashes to show sorrow.

Do you think that alone makes the day pleasing to me?

## The fast the LORD chooses

## Isaiah 58:6

“Here is the fast I choose.

Break the chains that hold people unjustly.

Untie the ropes and take off every yoke.

Set free the people you treat cruelly.” A yoke is a bar used to make animals pull a load.

Here it pictures the heavy demands people force on others.

![Two oxen joined by a wooden yoke.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

**Find the wooden bar**

Trace the bar that holds the animals together. Imagine lifting it off. The LORD asks His people to lift the burdens they have put on others.

## Isaiah 58:7

“Share your bread with someone who is hungry.

Welcome poor people who have no home into yours.

When you see someone without clothes, give them clothes.

When your own family needs help, stay and help.

## Light like the dawn

## Isaiah 58:8

“Then your light will shine like the first light of day.

You will heal quickly.

What you do right will go ahead of you.

The glory of the LORD will guard you from behind.

## Isaiah 58:9

“You will call, and the LORD will answer.

You will cry for help, and He will say, ‘Here I am.’ Take away the heavy burdens you put on others.

Stop pointing fingers to blame people.

Stop saying things to hurt them.

## Isaiah 58:10

“Give your time and food to hungry people.

Care for people who are suffering until their needs are met.

Then your light will shine in the dark.

Your night will be as bright as midday.

## Water for a garden and homes to live in

## Isaiah 58:11

“The LORD will always guide you.

Even in land baked dry by the sun, He will meet your needs.

He will give your body strength.

You will be like a garden with plenty of water.

You will be like a spring that never runs dry.

## Isaiah 58:12

“Your people will rebuild places that have long been in ruins.

You will build again on the old foundations.

People will call you the ones who mend broken walls.

They will say, ‘You make our streets places to live again.’

## Enjoy the Sabbath

## Isaiah 58:13

“Keep the Sabbath holy.

This is my day of rest.

Welcome it with joy and treat it with honor.

Set aside your own plans and pleasures that day.

Put aside careless talk.

## Isaiah 58:14

“Then the LORD Himself will be your joy.

I will let you ride over the high places of the land.

I will feed you from the land I gave your ancestor Jacob.” The LORD Himself has spoken.

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD ask you to share when someone is hungry?
