# Isaiah 27 — The LORD tends His vineyard

**About this chapter**

Isaiah gives Israel a song about God’s care. It pictures the LORD at work in a field of grapevines.

## The sea monster

## Isaiah 27:1

On that day the LORD will take His great sword.

It is sharp and powerful.

He will punish Leviathan, the great snake that twists and flees.

The LORD will kill that monster of the sea.

## A song about the vineyard

## Isaiah 27:2

On that day the LORD will say, “Sing about the vineyard full of fruit!” A vineyard is a field where grapes grow.

## Isaiah 27:3

“I am the LORD.

I look after this vineyard.

I keep it watered.

I guard it night and day so no one can harm it.

## Isaiah 27:4

“I am no longer angry.

If thorn bushes rose up against me, I would march over them.

I would crush them and burn them to the ground.

## Isaiah 27:5

“Or they can come to me for safety.

Let them make peace with me.

Yes, let them make peace with me.”

## Isaiah 27:6

The song pictures the people of Israel, the family of Jacob.

In days to come they will take root like a vine.

Buds will open into flowers.

Israel will fill the whole world with fruit.

![A bunch of grapes hanging from a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Find the fruit**

Follow the branch to the grapes. In the song, the LORD waters and guards the vine. This pictures how He cares for His people.

## Israel turns from its false gods

## Isaiah 27:7

Did the LORD strike Israel as hard as He struck Israel’s attackers?

Did He kill Israel’s people as He killed those who attacked them?

## Isaiah 27:8

LORD, You dealt with Israel through war.

You sent the people far from home.

It was as if a fierce wind from the east had swept them away.

## Isaiah 27:9

This is how God will take away the guilt of Jacob’s family.

The people will show the change by crushing their altar stones like pieces of chalk.

They will pull down the poles used to worship the goddess Asherah.

No altar for burning incense to false gods will stand.

## An empty city

## Isaiah 27:10

The city with strong walls stands empty.

Its homes lie abandoned like places in the desert.

Calves eat grass there and lie down to rest.

They strip the leaves from the branches.

## Isaiah 27:11

The dry branches snap off.

Women gather them to start their fires.

The people have no understanding.

So the God who made them shows them no pity or favor.

## The LORD gathers His people

## Isaiah 27:12

On that day the LORD will work like a farmer separating grain from the stalks.

His work will reach from the Euphrates River to the stream at Egypt’s border.

People of Israel, He will gather you one by one.

## Isaiah 27:13

On that day a great ram’s horn will sound.

The people near death in Assyria will come.

So will the people who had to live far from home in Egypt.

Together they will worship the LORD on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.
