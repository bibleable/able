# Isaiah 33 — A tent that stays in place

**About this chapter**

The people ask the LORD for strength while the roads around them stand empty. Isaiah looks ahead to a day when they can live safely in Jerusalem.

## A warning and a prayer

## Isaiah 33:1

Trouble is coming to you who destroy others!

So far no one has destroyed you.

You betray people, and no one has betrayed you.

When you finish destroying, someone will destroy you.

When you finish betraying, someone will betray you.

## Isaiah 33:2

LORD, show us kindness.

We are waiting for You.

Give us strength each morning.

Save us when trouble comes.

## Isaiah 33:3

People run when Your voice sounds like thunder.

When You rise, the nations scatter.

## Isaiah 33:4

People rush to gather the goods those nations leave behind.

They sweep over everything like a swarm of locusts eating a field bare.

## What Zion treasures

## Isaiah 33:5

The LORD is great.

His home is high above us.

He fills Zion with justice.

He teaches its people to do what is right.

Zion is Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 33:6

The LORD will keep you steady in the days ahead.

He has all the help, wisdom and knowledge you need.

The people of Zion have a treasure.

It is their deep respect for the LORD.

## The roads are empty

## Isaiah 33:7

Look!

Brave people cry out in the streets.

The messengers who went to make peace weep bitterly.

## Isaiah 33:8

The roads are empty.

Travelers have stopped using them.

People have broken their peace agreement.

They scorn those who witnessed it.

They have no regard for human life.

## Isaiah 33:9

Even the land seems to mourn.

Lebanon withers in shame.

The plain of Sharon looks like a desert.

The trees of Bashan and Carmel lose their leaves.

## The LORD rises to act

## Isaiah 33:10

The LORD says, “Now I will rise.

Now I will show how great I am.

People will honor me.

## Isaiah 33:11

“Your plans produce nothing but dry husks and straw.

Your own breath is like a fire that burns you up.

## Isaiah 33:12

“The peoples will burn to ashes like thorn bushes cut down and thrown into a fire.

## Isaiah 33:13

“You who live far away, hear what I have done.

You who live nearby, recognize my power.”

## Who can live here?

## Isaiah 33:14

People in Zion who do wrong shake with fear.

They ask, “Who among us can live beside a fire that burns everything up?

Who can live beside flames that never go out?”

## Isaiah 33:15

Here is the kind of person who can live there.

This person does right and speaks honestly.

They refuse money gained by threats.

They turn down money offered to make them act unfairly.

They refuse to listen to plans for murder.

They turn their eyes away from evil.

## Isaiah 33:16

Such a person will live safely up high.

A strong place in the mountains will shelter them.

They will have food to eat and water to drink.

## The King and His city

## Isaiah 33:17

You will see the King in His beauty.

You will look out over land stretching far into the distance.

## Isaiah 33:18

You will remember the people who once frightened you.

You will ask, “Where is the one who kept the count?

Where is the one who weighed things?

Where is the one who counted the towers?”

## Isaiah 33:19

You will no longer see those arrogant people.

Their strange speech sounded broken to you.

You could not understand their language.

## Isaiah 33:20

Look at Zion, where we gather for our feasts.

You will see Jerusalem, peaceful as a field where sheep rest.

Think of a tent that stays in place.

No one will pull up its pegs.

None of its ropes will snap.

![A tent stands beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Imagine staying in that tent without ever having to pack it up. Isaiah uses the tent to picture a safe home for the people of Jerusalem.

## The LORD will save us

## Isaiah 33:21

There the LORD in all His greatness will protect us.

He will be like wide rivers and waterways around us.

No ship with rows of oars will sail there.

No great ship will pass through.

## Isaiah 33:22

The LORD is our Judge.

The LORD gives us our laws.

The LORD is our King.

He Himself will save us.

## Isaiah 33:23

Isaiah pictures a ship with loose ropes.

They cannot hold its mast upright or spread its sail.

There will be plenty of goods to share from the battle.

Even people who have trouble walking will carry some away.

## Isaiah 33:24

No one living in Zion will say, “I am sick.” God will forgive the people there for the wrong they have done.
