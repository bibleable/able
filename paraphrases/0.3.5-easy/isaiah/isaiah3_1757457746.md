# Isaiah 3 — The LORD calls the leaders to account

**About this chapter**

Isaiah sees a city running out of food and leaders whose houses are full. The LORD asks those leaders where their wealth came from.

## The city loses what it depends on

## Isaiah 3:1

The Lord GOD commands heaven’s armies.

He is about to take away everything Jerusalem and Judah depend on.

Their whole supply of food and water will go.

## Isaiah 3:2

Their strong men and soldiers will go.

So will their judges, prophets, fortune-tellers, and elders.

The elders are the leaders of their communities.

## Isaiah 3:3

They will lose captains who lead fifty soldiers.

They will lose honored officials and advisers.

Even their skilled magicians and people who cast spells will go.

## Who will lead the people?

## Isaiah 3:4

The LORD says, “I will give them boys as leaders.

Children will rule them.”

## Isaiah 3:5

People will push each other around.

Neighbors will turn on neighbors.

Young people will rise against the old.

People who do shameful things will challenge those who deserve honor.

## Isaiah 3:6

A man will grab his brother in their father’s house.

He will say, “You have a cloak.

Be our leader!

Take charge of this pile of broken stones.”

## Isaiah 3:7

His brother will shout, “I cannot heal this place!

My house has no food or clothes to give.

Do not put me in charge of the people!”

![A cloak with a long lower edge.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

**Look at the cloak**

Imagine this cloak around the brother’s shoulders. His family sees it and asks him to lead. He knows how little he has to offer the hungry city.

## The results of their choices

## Isaiah 3:8

Jerusalem has stumbled, and Judah has fallen.

The people spoke and acted against the LORD.

They defied him in his glorious presence.

## Isaiah 3:9

Their faces give them away.

Like the people of Sodom, they show off the wrong they do.

What sorrow awaits them!

They have brought disaster on themselves.

## Isaiah 3:10

Tell those who do right that things will go well for them.

They will enjoy what their work produces.

## Isaiah 3:11

What sorrow awaits the wicked!

Disaster is coming.

They will suffer the kind of harm they have done to others.

## Isaiah 3:12

The LORD says, “Young people mistreat my people.

Women rule over them.

My people, those who guide you lead you astray.

They turn you off the path.”

## What the leaders took

## Isaiah 3:13

The LORD stands up to present his case.

He rises to judge the people.

## Isaiah 3:14

The LORD accuses the elders and leaders.

“You have eaten up the vineyard.

Your houses are full of things you took from poor people.”

## Isaiah 3:15

The Lord GOD who commands heaven’s armies asks, “Why are you crushing my people?

Why do you grind the faces of the poor into the ground?”

## The finery of Jerusalem

## Isaiah 3:16

The LORD says, “Look at the proud women of Zion.” Zion is Jerusalem.

They hold their heads high and cast inviting glances.

They take little dancing steps, making their ankle bracelets ring.

## Isaiah 3:17

The Lord will bring sores on their heads.

The LORD will leave their foreheads bare.

## Isaiah 3:18

On that day the Lord will take away their fine things.

Their ankle bracelets and headbands will go.

So will their jewels shaped like little moons.

## Isaiah 3:19

Their hanging jewels, bracelets, and veils will go.

## Isaiah 3:20

They will lose their fancy head coverings, ankle chains, and belts of cloth.

Their perfume bottles and charms will go too.

## Isaiah 3:21

They will lose their nose rings and their signet rings.

A signet ring leaves its owner’s mark when pressed into something soft.

## Isaiah 3:22

Their clothes for celebrations will go.

So will their capes, cloaks, and purses.

## Isaiah 3:23

They will lose their mirrors and fine linen clothes.

Their little crowns and shawls will go too.

## The city mourns

## Isaiah 3:24

Where perfume smelled sweet, there will be a bad smell.

A rope will take the place of a fine belt.

Carefully arranged hair will be gone, leaving bare heads.

People will wear rough cloth in place of fine clothes.

Where there was beauty, there will be shame.

## Isaiah 3:25

Zion, your men will die by the sword.

Your soldiers will fall in battle.

## Isaiah 3:26

At Zion’s gates there will be weeping and mourning.

Isaiah pictures Jerusalem as a woman who has lost everything.

She sits on the ground.

**Pause and answer**

Whose belongings does the LORD find in the leaders’ houses?
