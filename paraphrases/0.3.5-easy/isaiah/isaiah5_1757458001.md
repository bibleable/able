# Isaiah 5 — The song about the vineyard

**About this chapter**

Isaiah sings about a man who cared for his grapevines. Then Isaiah explains what the song tells us about God and His people.

## A friend plants grapevines

## Isaiah 5:1

Isaiah says, “I will sing about someone I love.

My friend had a vineyard on a hill with rich soil.” A vineyard is a field where grapes grow.

## Isaiah 5:2

The owner dug the soil and cleared away stones.

He planted the best grapevines.

In the middle he built a tower to watch over them.

He dug a place to press juice from grapes.

He waited for good grapes.

The grapes grew sour.

![A bunch of grapes hanging from a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Look at the grapes**

Point to the bunch of grapes. The owner waited for grapes like these to grow. Imagine tasting one and finding it sour.

## The owner asks a question

## Isaiah 5:3

Now the owner speaks.

“People of Jerusalem and Judah, listen.

Decide who is at fault, my vineyard or me.

## Isaiah 5:4

“What else could I have done for my vineyard?

I waited for sweet grapes.

Why did it grow sour ones?

## Isaiah 5:5

“Here is what I will do.

I will remove the hedge around my vineyard.

Animals will eat what grows there.

I will pull down the wall.

Feet will crush the plants.

## Isaiah 5:6

“I will let the vineyard go to waste.

No one will trim the vines or work the soil.

Thorn bushes will grow there.

I will tell the clouds to hold back their rain.”

## What the vineyard means

## Isaiah 5:7

The LORD commands the armies of heaven.

Israel is the vineyard in His song.

The people of Judah are the plants He loved.

The LORD looked for people who treated others fairly.

Instead, people killed each other.

He listened for signs that people did right.

He heard people crying for help.

## Houses and fields

## Isaiah 5:8

Isaiah warns, “Trouble is coming to you who keep taking more houses and fields.

Soon you alone will live there.

Where will anyone else live?”

## Isaiah 5:9

Isaiah heard the LORD say, “Many houses will stand empty.

Even the big, beautiful houses will have nobody living in them.

## Isaiah 5:10

“Ten acres of grapevines will give only one large jar of wine.

Plant ten baskets of seed.

You will harvest just one basket of grain.”

## People who pay no attention to God

## Isaiah 5:11

Trouble is coming to those who get up early to drink.

They stay up late getting drunk on wine.

## Isaiah 5:12

At their feasts people play harps, tambourines and flutes.

The guests drink wine.

They pay no attention to what the LORD does.

## Isaiah 5:13

The LORD says, “My people do not understand.

So others will take them far from home.

Their leaders will go hungry.

The crowds will suffer from thirst.”

## Isaiah 5:14

Isaiah pictures the place of the dead as a huge mouth.

It opens wider to swallow the people of Jerusalem.

Down go the leaders, the crowds and the noisy party guests.

## Isaiah 5:15

Everyone will have to bow low.

Even proud people will look down at the ground.

## Isaiah 5:16

People will see how great the LORD is when He judges fairly.

God shows that He is holy by doing what is right.

## Isaiah 5:17

Lambs will eat grass there as in their own fields.

Strangers will find food where rich people once lived.

## People who twist the truth

## Isaiah 5:18

Trouble is coming to people who keep doing wrong.

Isaiah pictures them pulling their sins behind them like a heavy cart.

Each lie is like a rope holding them to that cart.

## Isaiah 5:19

These people laugh at God's warning.

They say, “Hurry up!

Let us see God do His work!

Let the Holy One of Israel show us His plan!”

## Isaiah 5:20

Trouble is coming to people who call evil good.

They call good things evil too.

That is like calling darkness light and light darkness.

It is like calling bitter food sweet and sweet food bitter.

## Isaiah 5:21

Trouble is coming to those who think they are so wise.

They admire how clever they are.

## Isaiah 5:22

Trouble is coming to those famous for drinking wine.

They take pride in mixing strong drinks.

## Isaiah 5:23

They take money to let guilty people go free.

They take away the rights of innocent people.

## The LORD judges His people

## Isaiah 5:24

Fire eats up straw.

Dry grass shrinks in a flame.

These people will be like plants with rotten roots.

Their flowers will blow away like dust.

They refused to follow what the LORD taught.

They treated the Holy One of Israel's words as worthless.

## Isaiah 5:25

So the LORD grew angry with His people.

He raised His hand and struck them.

The mountains shook.

Dead bodies lay like trash in the streets.

The LORD is still angry.

His hand is still raised.

## An army comes from far away

## Isaiah 5:26

The LORD lifts a flag for nations far away.

He whistles to call them from the ends of the earth.

See how quickly their soldiers come!

## Isaiah 5:27

No soldier grows tired or trips.

None of them sleeps.

Every belt is tight.

Every sandal strap holds.

## Isaiah 5:28

Their arrows are sharp.

Their bows are ready.

Their horses have hooves as hard as stone.

The wheels of their war carts spin like a windstorm.

## Isaiah 5:29

The soldiers roar like young lions.

Like lions catching food, they seize people and carry them away.

No one can rescue the people they take.

## Isaiah 5:30

On that day the army will roar like the sea.

Look across the land.

People are in great trouble.

Clouds cover the light, and the land grows dark.

**Pause and answer**

When people cried for help, what did the LORD want the people of Judah to do for them?
