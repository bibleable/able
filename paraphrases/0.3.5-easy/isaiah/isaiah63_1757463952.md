# Isaiah 63 — The people remember how God rescued them

**About this chapter**

A watchful speaker sees someone coming from Edom. It is the LORD, returning after a battle. Later the people remember Moses and ask God for help.

## The LORD comes from Edom

## Isaiah 63:1

“Who is coming from Bozrah, a city in Edom?

His fine clothes have deep red stains.

Who is this, marching with such strength?”

The LORD answers, “It is I.

I speak up for what is right.

I am strong enough to save.”

## Isaiah 63:2

“Why are Your clothes red?

You look like someone who has been crushing grapes.”

People stepped on grapes in a place called a winepress.

The juice could stain their clothes.

![Bunches of grapes growing on a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Isaiah 63:3

The LORD answers, “I worked the winepress alone.

No one from any nation helped Me.”

The winepress is a picture of the battle.

“In My anger I crushed My enemies.

Their blood splashed on My clothes.

It stained everything I wore.

## Isaiah 63:4

“I had decided on a day to punish them.

The time had come to set My people free.

## Isaiah 63:5

“I looked for someone to help.

I was shocked that no one came.

So I won the victory by My own strength.

My anger kept Me going.

## Isaiah 63:6

“In My anger I crushed the nations.

I made them stagger like people drunk with wine.

I spilled their blood on the ground.”

## The LORD cared for Israel

## Isaiah 63:7

I will tell how faithfully the LORD loves us.

I will praise Him for all He has done.

He has done many good things for Israel.

He sees our suffering and cares for us.

## Isaiah 63:8

The LORD said, “Surely these are My people.

They are My children. They will stay true to Me.”

So the LORD became their Savior.

## Isaiah 63:9

When the people suffered, the LORD suffered too.

The angel who stood in His presence saved them.

The LORD loved them and felt their pain.

He set them free.

Long ago He lifted them up and carried them.

## Isaiah 63:10

But the people turned against the LORD.

They made His Holy Spirit sad.

Then the LORD became their enemy.

He fought against them Himself.

## The people remember Moses

## Isaiah 63:11

Then the people remembered the days of Moses.

They asked, “Where is the God who brought us through the sea?

Our leaders cared for us like shepherds tending sheep.

Where is the God who put His Holy Spirit among us?

## Isaiah 63:12

“God used His great power to guide Moses’ right hand.

God split the waters in front of us.

People would remember His name forever.

## Isaiah 63:13

“Where is the God who led us through the deep sea?

We walked as surely as a horse on open land.

Our feet did not slip.”

## Isaiah 63:14

Cattle go down into a valley to rest.

In the same way, the LORD’s Spirit gave Israel rest.

LORD, You led Your people like this.

You showed everyone how great You are.

## A prayer for the LORD to return

## Isaiah 63:15

LORD, look down from Your holy, glorious home in heaven.

Where is Your strong desire to help?

Where is Your power?

I long for You to show me Your tender care.

## Isaiah 63:16

You are our Father.

Abraham does not know us.

Our ancestor Israel does not claim us as his own.

Yet You, LORD, are our Father.

From long ago we have called You our Redeemer.

A redeemer is someone who sets people free.

## Isaiah 63:17

LORD, why do You make us wander from Your ways?

Why do You make our hearts so stubborn?

We have lost our deep respect for You.

Come back for the sake of Your servants.

We are the tribes You chose to be Yours.

## Isaiah 63:18

Your people held Your holy place only a short time.

Then our enemies trampled the place where we worshiped You.

## Isaiah 63:19

Now we are like people You never ruled.

We are like people who never bore Your name.
