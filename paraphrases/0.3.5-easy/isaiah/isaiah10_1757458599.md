# Isaiah 10 — Assyria’s power will end

**About this chapter**

The king of Assyria thinks he can take any city he wants. Isaiah tells the people of Jerusalem why they can still trust God.

## The rulers write unfair laws

## Isaiah 10:1

Trouble is coming to rulers who write unfair laws.

Their orders make life hard for people.

## Isaiah 10:2

The LORD says these rulers cheat poor people.

People suffer, and the rulers refuse to help them.

The rulers rob women whose husbands have died.

They take what belongs to children without parents.

## Isaiah 10:3

Rulers, one day you must answer for what you have done.

Enemies will come from far away and ruin your land.

Who will help you then?

Where will you put your riches?

## Isaiah 10:4

Some of you will crouch among the prisoners.

Others will fall among the dead.

Even after this, the LORD is still angry.

His hand is still raised to strike.

## God sends Assyria

## Isaiah 10:5

The LORD says, “Trouble is coming to Assyria too.

In my anger I use Assyria like a stick.

When its army strikes, people feel my anger.

## Isaiah 10:6

“I am sending its king against people who turned away from me.

I am angry with them.

He will take what belongs to them.

He will crush them like mud underfoot.

## Isaiah 10:7

“But the king has his own plan.

He wants to destroy one nation after another.”

## The king of Assyria boasts

## Isaiah 10:8

The king of Assyria boasts, “Even my army commanders are kings!

## Isaiah 10:9

“Look at the cities I took!

Calno fell like Carchemish.

Hamath fell like Arpad.

Samaria fell like Damascus.

## Isaiah 10:10

“I took kingdoms that worshiped idols.

An idol is something people make and worship as a god.

Their idols were more impressive than those in Jerusalem or Samaria.

## Isaiah 10:11

“I took Samaria with all its idols.

I can do the same to Jerusalem!”

## Who holds the axe?

## Isaiah 10:12

The Lord will finish His work on Mount Zion in Jerusalem.

Then He will punish the king of Assyria for his pride.

Even the look in the king’s eyes shows his pride.

## Isaiah 10:13

The king boasts, “I did this with my own strength.

I was clever enough to do it.

I moved the borders between countries.

I took their treasures.

I forced their rulers to bow to my power.

## Isaiah 10:14

“Taking their riches was like reaching into a bird’s nest.

I took the whole earth like someone gathering eggs.

No bird flapped a wing or made a sound.” The king means that nobody stopped him.

## Isaiah 10:15

Can an axe boast that it is greater than the person swinging it?

Can a saw claim it did the work alone?

Imagine a wooden stick picking up the person who holds it!

The king is like a tool in God’s hand.

## Assyria will lose its strength

## Isaiah 10:16

So the Lord GOD, who commands heaven’s armies, will send sickness.

Assyria’s strong soldiers will grow weak and thin.

Fire will burn away the things that make Assyria proud.

## Isaiah 10:17

God is Israel’s Light.

He is the Holy One.

Isaiah pictures Him as a flame.

In one day the flame burns up Assyria’s thorn bushes.

## Isaiah 10:18

The fire destroys Assyria’s fine forests and fruit trees.

It leaves nothing of their beauty.

Assyria wastes away like a person growing weaker from sickness.

## Isaiah 10:19

So few trees will remain that a child could count them.

## Some of Israel’s people return

## Isaiah 10:20

Some of Israel’s people will live through this.

They are the family of Jacob.

They will stop depending on Assyria, the nation that struck them.

They will truly trust the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 10:21

Those who are left from Jacob’s family will return to the Mighty God.

## Isaiah 10:22

Israel, your people are as many as grains of sand by the sea.

Yet only a small part of your people will return.

God has decided to bring destruction.

His judgment is right, and it will sweep through like a flood.

## Isaiah 10:23

The Lord GOD commands heaven’s armies.

He will bring the destruction He has announced across the whole land.

## God speaks to the people of Zion

## Isaiah 10:24

The Lord GOD says, “My people in Zion, do not fear Assyria.

Assyria beats you with a stick, as Egypt once did.

## Isaiah 10:25

“Soon my anger against you will end.

Then my anger will turn against Assyria and bring it down.”

## Isaiah 10:26

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies will raise a whip against Assyria.

Remember how He struck Midian at the rock of Oreb.

Remember how He raised His stick over the sea in Egypt.

He will act again.

## Isaiah 10:27

On that day the load will come off your shoulders.

Imagine a wooden yoke around your neck.

Your neck grows too big for it, and it breaks.

This means Assyria will lose its power over you.

![Two oxen with a wooden yoke across their necks.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

**Find the yoke**

Point to the wooden bar that holds the animals together. Imagine the bar breaking. Isaiah uses this picture to show the end of Assyria’s control.

## An army comes near Jerusalem

## Isaiah 10:28

Now Isaiah names the places on the army’s march.

Assyria’s soldiers enter Aiath and pass through Migron.

They leave their supplies at Michmash.

## Isaiah 10:29

The soldiers cross at a shallow place in the water.

They say, “We will spend the night at Geba.” The people of Ramah shake with fear.

People run from Gibeah, Saul’s town.

## Isaiah 10:30

People of Gallim, cry out!

People of Laishah, listen!

What trouble has come to Anathoth!

## Isaiah 10:31

The people of Madmenah run away.

The people of Gebim look for shelter.

## Isaiah 10:32

Today the soldiers will stop at Nob.

They will shake their fists at the hill of Jerusalem.

This hill is Mount Zion.

## The tall trees fall

## Isaiah 10:33

Look!

The Lord GOD who commands heaven’s armies cuts off branches with terrifying power.

He cuts down even the tallest trees.

Isaiah pictures the fall of the proud.

## Isaiah 10:34

God swings an axe and clears the thick forest.

Before the Mighty One, even Lebanon will fall.

**Pause and answer**

Who will the people of Israel trust when they stop depending on Assyria?
