# Isaiah 43 — I have called you by name

**About this chapter**

God speaks to Israel as a people He knows by name. He promises to bring His scattered children home and asks them to tell what He has done.

## God speaks to His people

## Isaiah 43:1

The LORD made Israel, the people of Jacob.

Now He says, “Do not be afraid.

I have set you free.

I have called you by name.

You belong to me.

## Isaiah 43:2

“When you pass through deep water, I will be with you.

The rivers will not sweep you away.

When you walk through fire, the flames will not burn you.

## Isaiah 43:3

“I am the LORD your God.

I am the Holy One of Israel who saves you.

I give Egypt as the price for your freedom.

I give Cush and Seba in your place.

## Isaiah 43:4

“You are precious to me.

I honor you, and I love you.

I will give other people in exchange for you.

I will give nations in exchange for your life.

## Bring my children home

## Isaiah 43:5

“Do not be afraid.

I am with you.

I will bring your children from the east.

I will gather you from the west.

## Isaiah 43:6

“To the north I will say, ‘Let them go!’ To the south I will say, ‘Do not hold them back!’ Bring my sons and daughters from the farthest places on earth.

## Isaiah 43:7

“Bring everyone who bears my name.

I shaped them and gave them life to show my glory.”

## Witnesses speak

## Isaiah 43:8

Bring out the people who have eyes but cannot see.

Bring out those who have ears but cannot hear.

## Isaiah 43:9

The nations gather as if for a court hearing.

Which of them can tell what will happen?

Who can show what they announced long ago?

Let them bring witnesses to speak for them.

Then others can listen and say, “It is true.”

## Isaiah 43:10

The LORD tells Israel, “You are my witnesses.

I chose you to serve me, to know me and to believe me.

I want you to understand who I am.

There was no god before me.

None will come after me.

## Isaiah 43:11

“I myself am the LORD.

I alone can save you.

## Isaiah 43:12

“I told you what would happen.

I saved you and made it known.

No foreign god among you did this.

You are my witnesses that I am God.

## Isaiah 43:13

“I have always been God.

No one can take anyone out of my hand.

Who can undo what I have done?”

## A way through the sea

## Isaiah 43:14

The Holy One of Israel is their Redeemer, the one who sets them free.

He says, “For your sake I will send someone to Babylon.

The people of Babylon, the Chaldeans, will have to flee.

They will leave in the very ships they took such joy in.

## Isaiah 43:15

“I am the LORD, your Holy One.

I made you, Israel.

I am your King.”

## Isaiah 43:16

The LORD made a road through the sea.

His people had a path through the rushing water.

## Isaiah 43:17

He led out the enemy’s war chariots, horses and soldiers.

Together they fell, never to rise again.

They were like the flame of a lamp that goes out.

## Streams in the desert

## Isaiah 43:18

Now the LORD says, “Do not keep looking back at what happened long ago.

## Isaiah 43:19

“Look!

I am doing something new.

It is beginning already.

Can you see it?

I will make a road through the wilderness.

I will make streams flow in the desert.

## Isaiah 43:20

“Wild animals will honor me, even jackals and ostriches.

I will put water in the wilderness and rivers in the desert.

My chosen people will have water to drink.

## Isaiah 43:21

“I formed this people for myself.

They will tell others how great I am.

## The people grow tired of God

## Isaiah 43:22

“Yet you have not called to me, people of Jacob.

Israel, you have grown tired of me.

## Isaiah 43:23

“You have brought me no sheep to burn as offerings.

You have not honored me with sacrifices.

I have not weighed you down with demands for gifts.

I have not worn you out asking for frankincense, a sweet-smelling incense.

## Isaiah 43:24

“You have spent no silver on sweet-smelling cane for me.

You have not satisfied me with the fat from your sacrifices.

Yet you have loaded me down with your sins.

The wrong you do has worn me out.

## God wipes away sins

## Isaiah 43:25

“I myself wipe away your wrongs for my own sake.

I will no longer remember your sins.

## Isaiah 43:26

“Remind me what you think I have forgotten.

Let us discuss the matter together.

Tell your side.

Show that you are in the right.

## Isaiah 43:27

“Your first ancestor sinned.

Those who spoke for you turned against me.

## Isaiah 43:28

“So I will bring shame on the leaders in my holy place.

I will give Jacob’s people over to destruction.

Others will speak of Israel with contempt.”
