# Isaiah 55 — Come to the water

**About this chapter**

The LORD invites hungry and thirsty people to come and listen. Later He describes rain soaking into the ground to show what happens when He speaks.

## An invitation without a price

## Isaiah 55:1

The LORD says, “Are you thirsty?

Come to the water!

Have you no money?

Come and eat!

Come for wine and milk.

You can have them without paying.

## Isaiah 55:2

“Why spend your money on things that cannot feed you?

Why work hard for things that leave you hungry?

Listen closely to me.

Eat what is good.

Enjoy the finest food.”

## A promise that lasts

## Isaiah 55:3

The LORD says, “Come near and listen to me so you may live.

I will make a covenant with you, a promise that lasts forever.

I will give you the faithful love I promised David.

## Isaiah 55:4

“I made David a witness to the nations.

I made him a leader who gave commands to peoples.

## Isaiah 55:5

“You will call to a nation you have never known.

Nations that have never known you will run to you.

The LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, has given you honor.”

## Turn back to the LORD

## Isaiah 55:6

Look for the LORD while you can find Him.

Call to Him while He is near.

## Isaiah 55:7

People who do evil must leave that way of life.

They must give up their evil plans.

Let them turn back to the LORD.

He will show them mercy.

Our God is ready to forgive them freely.

## Rain waters the ground

## Isaiah 55:8

The LORD says, “My thoughts are different from yours.

My ways are different from yours.

## Isaiah 55:9

“Look how high the sky is above the ground.

That is how much higher my ways are than yours.

My thoughts are that much higher than yours too.

## Isaiah 55:10

“Rain and snow fall from the sky.

Before the water returns, it soaks the earth.

Plants sprout and grow.

Then people have seed to plant and food to eat.

## Isaiah 55:11

“My word is like that rain.

When I speak, my word does the work I send it to do.

It never comes back to me empty.

**Find the grain**

Point to the grain at the top of the stalk. Rain helped this plant grow. Some grain can become food, and some can be planted to grow another crop.

## A joyful journey

## Isaiah 55:12

“You will go out with joy.

I will lead you in peace.

The mountains and hills ahead of you will sing.

The trees in the fields will clap their hands.” Isaiah pictures the whole land celebrating with the people.

## Isaiah 55:13

“Cypress trees will grow where thorn bushes grew.

Myrtle trees will spring up where prickly vines grew.

This will bring honor to the LORD.

It will be a sign that lasts forever.”

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise to do when people leave their evil ways and turn back to Him?
