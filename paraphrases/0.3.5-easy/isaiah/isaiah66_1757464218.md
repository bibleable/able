# Isaiah 66 — The LORD comforts His people

**About this chapter**

The LORD promises comfort to the people of Jerusalem. He also speaks about those who turn against Him. People from distant lands will hear about His glory.

## The LORD looks for humble people

## Isaiah 66:1

The LORD says, “Heaven is My throne.

Earth is the stool where I rest My feet.

What house could you build for Me?

Where could you make a place for Me to rest?”

## Isaiah 66:2

The LORD says, “My own hands made all these things.

That is how they came to be.

I look with favor on people who are humble.

They are sorry when they do wrong.

They tremble when I speak.

They take My words seriously.

## Isaiah 66:3

Some people bring Me gifts but choose their own ways.

They delight in things I hate.

When they offer an ox, it is like killing a person.

Offering a lamb is like breaking a dog’s neck.

Giving grain is like offering a pig’s blood.

Burning sweet incense is like praising an idol.

An idol is an object people worship.

## Isaiah 66:4

So I will choose their punishment.

I will bring them the things they fear.

I called, and no one answered.

I spoke, and no one listened.

I saw them choose the evil things I hate.”

## A voice from the temple

## Isaiah 66:5

You who tremble at His words, listen to the LORD.

“Some of your own people hate you.

They shut you out because you belong to Me.

They mock you and say, ‘Let the LORD show His glory!

Then we can see how happy you are!’

Those who mock you will face shame.”

## Isaiah 66:6

Listen to the noise in the city!

A voice comes from the temple.

It is the LORD giving His enemies what they deserve.

## Jerusalem is like a mother

## Isaiah 66:7

The LORD speaks about Zion as if the city were a mother.

“Before her labor began, she gave birth.

Before she felt pain, her son was born.

## Isaiah 66:8

Has anyone ever heard of such a thing?

Has anyone ever seen it?

Can a whole country be born in one day?

Can a nation be born in a moment?

Yet Zion gave birth as soon as her labor began.”

Zion means Jerusalem.

Her children are her people.

## Isaiah 66:9

The LORD says, “Would I bring a baby to the moment of birth and stop there?

I am the One who brings the baby out.

Would I keep the baby from being born?”

## Isaiah 66:10

All you who love Jerusalem, be glad for her!

All you who have wept for her, share her joy!

## Isaiah 66:11

Think of a baby drinking its mother’s milk.

The baby has enough and rests in her arms.

In the same way, Jerusalem will comfort you.

You will enjoy all the good things she has.

## Isaiah 66:12

The LORD says, “I will send Jerusalem peace like a river.

The wealth of nations will flow to her like a stream.

Like a baby, you will drink milk.

Jerusalem will carry you and bounce you on her knees.

## Isaiah 66:13

As a mother comforts her child, I will comfort you.

In Jerusalem you will find comfort.”

## The LORD judges

## Isaiah 66:14

When you see this, your hearts will be glad.

You will grow strong like fresh green grass.

The LORD’s servants will see His power at work.

His enemies will see His anger.

## Isaiah 66:15

Look! The LORD is coming with fire.

His chariots sweep in like a twisting windstorm.

He comes in anger.

With flames He shows how fiercely He condemns evil.

## Isaiah 66:16

The LORD will judge all people with fire and His sword.

Many will die by His hand.

## Isaiah 66:17

The LORD says, “Some people wash and prepare themselves for worship among the trees.

They follow a leader into the middle of the group.

They eat pigs, rats, and other unclean creatures.

Those people will die together.

## People come from distant lands

## Isaiah 66:18

I know what they do.

I know what they think.

I am coming to gather people from every nation.

People who speak every language will see My glory.

## Isaiah 66:19

I will give them a sign.

I will send some who survive to distant nations.

They will go to Tarshish and Put.

They will go to Lud, where people use bows.

They will go to Tubal, Javan, and faraway islands.

The people there have never heard of My greatness.

They have never seen My glory.

The survivors will tell them how glorious I am.

## Isaiah 66:20

They will bring your people home from every nation.

Some will come on horses, mules, and camels.

Others will ride in chariots and wagons.

They will come to My holy mountain in Jerusalem.

Your people will be a gift to Me.

Think of Israelites bringing a gift to My house in a clean container.

## Isaiah 66:21

I will choose some of them as priests and Levites.”

Priests and Levites serve at the LORD’s house.

## People everywhere worship the LORD

## Isaiah 66:22

The LORD says, “The new heavens and earth I make will last.

Your family will last too.

People will remember your name.

## Isaiah 66:23

At each new moon, people will gather.

They will gather each Sabbath, the weekly day of rest.

People everywhere will come to worship Me.

## Isaiah 66:24

When they go out, they will see dead bodies.

These were the people who turned against Me.

The worms that feed on them will never die.

The fire will never go out.

Everyone who sees them will be horrified.”
