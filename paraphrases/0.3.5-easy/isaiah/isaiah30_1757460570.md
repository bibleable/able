# Isaiah 30 — This is the way

**About this chapter**

The people of Judah send gifts to Egypt because they want protection. Isaiah tells them what the LORD is ready to do when they turn to Him.

## The people ask Egypt for help

## Isaiah 30:1

The LORD says, “Trouble is coming to my children who refuse to listen.

They carry out plans I never gave them.

They make a pact against my will.

They keep adding one wrong to another.

## Isaiah 30:2

“They go down to Egypt without asking me what to do.

They want Pharaoh, Egypt’s king, to protect them.

They hope Egypt will shelter them like shade on a hot day.

## Isaiah 30:3

“You will be ashamed that you trusted Pharaoh.

The shelter you hoped to find in Egypt will bring you disgrace.

## Isaiah 30:4

“Your leaders have reached the Egyptian city of Zoan.

Your messengers have arrived at Hanes.

## Isaiah 30:5

“Yet every one of you will feel ashamed.

The Egyptians have nothing to offer that will help you.”

## A costly journey

## Isaiah 30:6

Isaiah gives a message about the pack animals crossing the Negev, the dry land in the south.

Lions, lionesses, venomous snakes and flying serpents live there.

Donkeys carry riches on their backs.

Camels carry treasures on their humps.

They make this hard journey to people who cannot help.

## Isaiah 30:7

Egypt offers help that comes to nothing.

So the LORD calls Egypt “Rahab Who Sits Still.” The name pictures a great power that does nothing.

## Write down the warning

## Isaiah 30:8

Now the LORD tells Isaiah, “Write this message on a tablet while the people watch.

Write it on a scroll too.

Keep it as a record for all the years to come.”

## Isaiah 30:9

These people turn against the LORD.

They are like children who tell lies.

They refuse to follow what the LORD teaches.

## Isaiah 30:10

They tell those who see visions, “Stop seeing them!” They tell the prophets, “Stop telling us the truth!

Tell us things we like to hear.

Tell us things that only seem true.

## Isaiah 30:11

“Step aside!

Get off our road!

We want to hear no more about the Holy One of Israel.”

## A wall falls and a jar breaks

## Isaiah 30:12

The Holy One of Israel answers, “You have rejected this message.

You think you can keep yourselves safe by mistreating people and telling lies.

## Isaiah 30:13

“This sin is like a high wall bulging outward.

Suddenly the whole wall falls.

## Isaiah 30:14

“It will break like a clay jar smashed to bits.

Among the pieces in the dust, none will be big enough to lift a hot coal.

None will be big enough to scoop water from a tank.”

![A brown clay jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the jar**

Imagine this jar holding water. Now imagine it breaking into tiny chips. Isaiah says the plan the people trust will break just as completely.

## The people trust their horses

## Isaiah 30:15

The Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel, says, “Turn back to me and rest, and I will save you.

Trust me quietly, and you will have strength.

But you refused.”

## Isaiah 30:16

You said, “No!

We will escape on horses.” So you will have to run away.

You said, “Our horses are fast.” Those who chase you will be faster.

## Isaiah 30:17

A threat from one person will send a thousand running.

A threat from five will send you all running.

Those left will stand alone like a flagpole on a mountaintop, like a flag on a hill.

## The LORD will answer

## Isaiah 30:18

Even so, the LORD longs to be kind to you.

He rises to show you mercy.

The LORD does what is fair.

He blesses everyone who waits for Him.

## Isaiah 30:19

People of Zion, you who live in Jerusalem, your weeping will end.

When you call for help, the LORD will show you kindness.

He will hear and answer.

## Isaiah 30:20

Hardship will be as familiar as the bread you eat and the water you drink.

The Lord will let you live through trouble.

Yet your Teacher will hide no longer.

You will see Him with your own eyes.

## Isaiah 30:21

Whether you turn right or left, you will hear a voice behind you.

It will say, “This is the way.

Walk in it.”

## Isaiah 30:22

You will strip your idols of their honor.

Even the images covered with silver and gold will seem unclean to you.

You will throw them away like used menstrual cloths.

“Get out!” you will say.

## Rain for the fields

## Isaiah 30:23

The LORD will send rain on the seeds you plant.

Your land will give you plenty of good food.

Your cattle will eat grass in wide fields.

## Isaiah 30:24

The oxen and donkeys that work your soil will eat feed mixed with salt.

Workers will toss the feed into the air with shovels and forks to clear away the husks.

## Isaiah 30:25

On the day the towers fall and many people die, streams will flow from every high hill and mountain.

## Isaiah 30:26

The moon will shine as brightly as the sun.

The sun will shine with the light of seven days at once.

That day the LORD will bandage His people’s injuries.

He will heal the wounds He gave them.

## The LORD comes to judge

## Isaiah 30:27

Look!

The LORD comes from far away in burning anger and thick smoke.

His angry words are like a fire that burns up everything in its path.

## Isaiah 30:28

His breath is like floodwater reaching up to the neck.

Isaiah pictures the LORD shaking the nations in a sieve that brings destruction.

He leads them off course like animals that must follow the pull on their mouths.

## Isaiah 30:29

You will sing as you do on the night of a holy feast.

You will feel the joy of walking to flute music on the way up the LORD’s mountain.

You are going to the Rock of Israel, the God who keeps Israel safe.

## Isaiah 30:30

The LORD will make people hear His powerful voice.

They will see Him strike in anger with His mighty arm.

Fire will blaze.

Rain will pour down, and hailstones will fall.

## Assyria falls

## Isaiah 30:31

When Assyria hears the LORD’s voice, its strength will break.

The LORD will strike Assyria with His royal staff.

## Isaiah 30:32

Each time the LORD’s rod strikes Assyria, people will beat tambourines and play stringed instruments.

The LORD Himself will fight.

## Isaiah 30:33

Long ago a place called Topheth was made ready for the king.

There is a deep, wide place for a funeral fire, with plenty of wood.

The LORD’s breath is like a stream of burning sulfur.

It sets the wood on fire.

**Pause and answer**

What will the voice behind the people tell them to do?
