# Isaiah 8 — A name on a scroll

**About this chapter**

God tells Isaiah to write a name down. Later that name helps explain what will happen to two cities.

## Isaiah writes a name

## Isaiah 8:1

The LORD told me, “Take a large scroll.

Write this name on it with an ordinary writing tool.

Write Maher-shalal-hash-baz.” A scroll is a long sheet that rolls up.

The name means someone will hurry to take what belongs to others after a battle.

## Isaiah 8:2

The LORD said, “I will choose two people I trust as witnesses.” One was Uriah the priest.

The other was Zechariah, the son of Jeberekiah.

A witness can tell others what happened.

![An open scroll.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Look at the scroll**

Point to the place where Isaiah could write the name. Later he would give his son that same name.

## Isaiah 8:3

My wife was a prophet.

A prophet speaks God's messages to people.

We slept together, and she became pregnant.

When our son was born, the LORD told me, “Name him Maher-shalal-hash-baz.

## Isaiah 8:4

“Soon the king of Assyria will take away the riches of Damascus.

He will also take the goods from Samaria.

This will happen before the boy can call out ‘Father’ or ‘Mother.’”

## An army like a flood

## Isaiah 8:5

Then the LORD spoke to me again.

## Isaiah 8:6

He said, “These people have turned away from the gentle waters of Shiloah.

They are glad about King Rezin and Pekah, the son of Remaliah.”

## Isaiah 8:7

So the Lord will bring the king of Assyria against them with all his power.

Think of the great Euphrates River rising in a flood.

Water spills over both banks.

In the same way, the king's army will spread across the land.

## Isaiah 8:8

The flood will sweep into Judah.

Imagine water rising right up to your neck.

Immanuel, the water will spread across your whole land!

## God is with us

## Isaiah 8:9

Listen, people of faraway lands!

Gather together, and you will break apart.

Get ready to fight, and you will break apart.

Get ready again, and you will still break apart.

## Isaiah 8:10

Make your plans.

They will fail.

Say what you want to happen.

It will never happen, because God is with us.

## Whom should the people fear?

## Isaiah 8:11

Isaiah says, “I felt the LORD's strong hand on me.

He warned me to live differently from these people.”

## Isaiah 8:12

The LORD said, “People keep saying that others are making secret plans against them.

Do not call everything a secret plan just because they do.

Do not live in fear of what frightens them.

## Isaiah 8:13

“Honor the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

He alone is holy.

He is the one you should fear.”

## Isaiah 8:14

The LORD will be a holy place of safety.

Yet both kingdoms, Israel and Judah, will trip over Him like a stone in their path.

For the people of Jerusalem, He will be like a trap.

## Isaiah 8:15

Many people will trip and fall.

They will lie broken.

Like animals caught in a trap, they will lose their freedom.

## Isaiah waits for the LORD

## Isaiah 8:16

Tie up the written message.

Seal the teaching so it stays safe among my followers.

## Isaiah 8:17

Isaiah says, “I will wait for the LORD.

He is hiding His face from Jacob's family.

Even now I will trust Him.” Jacob's family means the people of Israel.

## Isaiah 8:18

“Look at me and the children the LORD gave me.

We are signs for the people of Israel.

We point to what the LORD says will happen.

These signs come from the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

His home is on Mount Zion.”

## Where should the people turn?

## Isaiah 8:19

Some people whisper and mutter.

They claim to speak with the dead.

Others may tell you to ask them for help.

Shouldn't people ask their God for help?

Why ask the dead what living people should do?

## Isaiah 8:20

Turn to God's teaching and His message!

People who speak against His word have no light of dawn.

Isaiah pictures them in darkness, unable to see their way.

## Isaiah 8:21

They will wander through the land hungry and discouraged.

Their hunger will make them angry.

They will look up and curse their king and their God.

## Isaiah 8:22

Then they will look down at the earth.

Everywhere they look, they will see darkness and suffering.

They will sink into complete darkness.
