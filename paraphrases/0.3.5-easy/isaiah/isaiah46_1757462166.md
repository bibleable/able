# Isaiah 46 — I will carry you

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes animals struggling to carry heavy statues. Then God reminds Israel who has carried them all their lives.

## Animals carry the idols

## Isaiah 46:1

Bel and Nebo are gods the people of Babylon worship.

Look how low their statues bow!

The people load these heavy statues onto animals.

The tired animals strain under the weight.

## Isaiah 46:2

The two gods bow down together.

They cannot even lift the load.

Their enemies carry the statues away as captives.

## God carries His people

## Isaiah 46:3

Now the LORD speaks to the people of Jacob.

“Listen to me, all who are left of Israel.

I cared for you before you were born.

I have carried you since birth.

## Isaiah 46:4

“When you are old, I will still be the same.

When your hair turns gray, I will still hold you up.

I made you.

I will carry you.

I will take care of you and rescue you.

## A statue that needs lifting

## Isaiah 46:5

“Who is like me?

Is there anyone you could call my equal?

## Isaiah 46:6

“People pour gold out of their bags.

They weigh their silver on scales.

They pay a worker to shape the metal into a god.

Then they bow down and worship it.

![Silver coins gathered together.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Look at the silver**

Imagine holding the silver in your hand. In Isaiah’s account, people give silver to a worker to make a statue. Even after the worker shapes it, someone has to carry it.

## Isaiah 46:7

“They lift the statue onto a shoulder and carry it.

They set it down.

There it stays.

It cannot move from that spot.

People call to it for help.

It gives no answer.

It cannot rescue anyone from trouble.

## God does what He plans

## Isaiah 46:8

“Remember this and take courage.

You who have done wrong, think carefully about it.

## Isaiah 46:9

“Remember what happened long ago.

I am God.

There is no other God.

No one is like me.

## Isaiah 46:10

“From the beginning I tell you how things will end.

Long ago I announced things that have yet to happen.

My plan will stand.

I will do everything I have decided to do.

## Isaiah 46:11

“I call a man from a distant land in the east.

He comes like a bird swooping down to catch its prey.

He will carry out my purpose.

What I have said will happen.

What I have planned, I will do.

## Help is near

## Isaiah 46:12

“Listen to me, you stubborn people.

You are far from doing what is right.

## Isaiah 46:13

“I am coming near to put things right.

I will rescue you without delay.

I will save Zion.

I will share my glory with Israel.” Zion is another name for Jerusalem.
