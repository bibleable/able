# Isaiah 48 — Leave Babylon with a shout of joy

**About this chapter**

God reminds Israel of things He told them before they happened. Now His people hear good news they can share with the whole earth.

## Listen, people of Israel

## Isaiah 48:1

The LORD says, “Listen, family of Jacob.

People call you Israel.

You come from the family of Judah.

You make promises in my name and call on the God of Israel.

Yet your words lack truth, and your actions are wrong.

## Isaiah 48:2

“You call yourselves people of the holy city, Jerusalem.

You say you depend on Israel’s God.

His name is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

## God spoke before it happened

## Isaiah 48:3

“Long ago I told you what would happen.

I said it aloud.

Then I acted suddenly.

What I had announced came true.

## Isaiah 48:4

“I knew how stubborn you were.

It was as if your neck were iron and your forehead bronze.” These hard metals picture people who refuse to change their minds.

## Isaiah 48:5

“So I told you about these things before they happened.

Then you could never say, ‘My idol did it.

The statue I carved or made from metal gave the order.’

## Something the people have never heard

## Isaiah 48:6

“You heard what I said.

Now look at what happened.

Will you admit it is true?

From now on I will tell you new things.

They have been hidden from you.

## Isaiah 48:7

“I am bringing them about now.

Before today you had never heard of them.

You cannot say, ‘I knew it all along!’

## Isaiah 48:8

“You did not hear or understand.

For a long time you refused to listen.

I knew you would betray my trust.

People have called you rebels since you were born.

## God holds back His anger

## Isaiah 48:9

“For the honor of my name, I hold back my anger.

So that people will praise me, I spare you.

I will not wipe you out.

## Isaiah 48:10

“I have tested you.

Your suffering has been like a furnace.

The testing you went through was different from heating silver to make it pure.

## Isaiah 48:11

“For my own sake, yes, for my own sake, I will act.

How could I let people drag my name down?

I will give my glory to no one else.

## The One who made earth and sky

## Isaiah 48:12

“Listen to me, family of Jacob.

Israel, I called you.

I am the first and the last.

## Isaiah 48:13

“My hand laid the foundation of the earth.

My right hand spread out the sky.

When I call, earth and sky stand ready together.

## Isaiah 48:14

“Come together and listen.

Which of your idols told you this would happen?

The man the LORD has chosen will do what the LORD wants in Babylon.

He will use his strength against its people, the Chaldeans.

## Isaiah 48:15

“I myself have spoken.

I called that man and brought him here.

He will finish the work I gave him.

## Isaiah 48:16

“Come close and listen.

From the beginning I have spoken openly.

When these things happened, I was there.” Now the messenger says, “The Lord GOD has sent me with His Spirit.”

## Peace like a river

## Isaiah 48:17

The LORD is the Holy One of Israel, the one who sets His people free.

He says, “I am the LORD your God.

I teach you what is good for you.

I guide you along the way you should go.

## Isaiah 48:18

“If only you had listened to my commands!

Your peace would have kept flowing like a river.

Your right actions would have followed one another like waves in the sea.

## Isaiah 48:19

“Your children and their children would have been like grains of sand, too many to count.

Their family name would never have disappeared from my presence.”

## Tell the world

## Isaiah 48:20

Leave Babylon!

Get away from the Chaldeans!

Shout the good news for joy.

Send it to the ends of the earth.

Say, “The LORD has set His servant Jacob free!”

## Isaiah 48:21

When the LORD led His people through the deserts, they had water to drink.

He split a rock for them.

Water rushed out.

**Follow the water**

Trace the water as it flows downhill. Imagine coming to drink after a long walk through dry land. Isaiah remembers water rushing out when God split the rock.

## Isaiah 48:22

The LORD says, “People who keep doing evil have no peace.”

**Pause and answer**

What good news should the people shout when they leave Babylon?
