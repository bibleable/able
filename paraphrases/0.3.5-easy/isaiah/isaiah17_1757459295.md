# Isaiah 17 — Only a few olives remain

**About this chapter**

Isaiah warns Damascus and Israel that they will lose their power. He uses a nearly bare olive tree to show what will happen to Israel’s people.

## A warning about Damascus

## Isaiah 17:1

Isaiah gives this warning about Damascus.

“Look at Damascus!

Where the city stood, only heaps of broken stones remain.

## Isaiah 17:2

“People will leave the towns around Aroer.

Flocks of sheep will lie down there.

No one will frighten them.

## Isaiah 17:3

“Ephraim will lose its strong place of defense.

The rulers in Damascus will lose their power.

The people left in Aram will lose their glory, just as Israel will.” This is the message of the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

## The field and the olive tree

## Isaiah 17:4

Jacob’s people will lose their glory that day.

Isaiah pictures Israel as a person growing thin and weak.

## Isaiah 17:5

Think of a worker gathering stalks in one arm to cut the grain.

Then someone goes through the Valley of Rephaim, picking up the last heads of grain.

Soon there is little left in the field.

## Isaiah 17:6

The LORD, the God of Israel, says a few will remain.

Think of people beating an olive tree to shake down its fruit.

Two or three olives still hang at the top.

Four or five still cling to the branches that bore so much fruit.

In the same way, some people will survive.

## People turn to their Maker

## Isaiah 17:7

On that day people will look to the One who made them.

They will turn to the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 17:8

They will stop looking for help from altars they built themselves.

They made poles to worship the goddess Asherah.

They also made altars for burning sweet-smelling incense.

They will turn away from these things their own hands made.

## A harvest disappears

## Isaiah 17:9

On that day their strong cities will stand empty.

Long ago people left wooded places and hilltops when the Israelites came.

Now Israel’s own cities will be just as empty.

## Isaiah 17:10

You have forgotten the God who saves you.

He was like a great rock where you could find shelter.

Yet you forgot Him.

You plant lovely gardens.

You bring branches from foreign grapevines and plant them.

## Isaiah 17:11

You make your plants grow the day you plant them.

Your seeds sprout that very morning.

Yet when sickness and pain come, the harvest will disappear.

No one will be able to heal that pain.

## The noisy nations flee

## Isaiah 17:12

Listen to the noise of all those peoples!

The nations roar like the sea.

Their voices sound like great waves crashing.

## Isaiah 17:13

The nations roar like rushing water.

Then God speaks sharply to them, and they run far away.

They are like the light husks of grain blowing across the hills.

They are like dry weeds rolling away in a storm.

**Follow the husks**

Find the light husks blowing to one side. Isaiah says the nations will scatter as easily as those husks.

## Isaiah 17:14

In the evening those nations terrify us.

Before morning they are gone.

That is what happens to the people who attack us and take our goods.
