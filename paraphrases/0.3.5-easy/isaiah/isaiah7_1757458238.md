# Isaiah 7 — A sign for King Ahaz

**About this chapter**

Two kings want to take Jerusalem. God sends Isaiah to tell King Ahaz what will happen and whom he can trust.

## Two kings threaten Jerusalem

## Isaiah 7:1

Ahaz ruled Judah.

His father was Jotham.

His grandfather was Uzziah.

Two kings attacked Jerusalem while Ahaz was king.

One was Rezin, king of Aram.

The other was Pekah, king of Israel.

Pekah was the son of Remaliah.

The two kings could not take the city.

## Isaiah 7:2

King Ahaz belonged to the family of King David.

The family heard that Aram had joined forces with Ephraim.

Ephraim is another name for the northern kingdom of Israel.

Ahaz and his people shook with fear.

They shook like trees in a strong wind.

## God sends Isaiah to meet Ahaz

## Isaiah 7:3

The LORD spoke to Isaiah.

“Take your son Shear-jashub with you.

Meet Ahaz where the water channel reaches the upper pool.

It is on the road to the field where people wash clothes.”

## Isaiah 7:4

The LORD gave Isaiah a message for Ahaz.

“Stay calm.

Do not lose heart.

Rezin and his people are angry.

Pekah is angry too.

Think of two burnt sticks that still give off smoke.

That is how little you need to fear these kings.

## Isaiah 7:5

“Aram and Ephraim have made a plan with Pekah.

They want to destroy you.

## Isaiah 7:6

“They want to attack Judah and frighten the people.

They plan to divide the land between them.

They want the son of Tabeal to rule Judah.”

## The plan will fail

## Isaiah 7:7

The Lord GOD says,

“Their plan will fail.

What they want will never happen.

## Isaiah 7:8

“Damascus is the capital of Aram.

Rezin rules in Damascus.

Within sixty-five years Ephraim will break apart.

Its people will no longer be a nation.

## Isaiah 7:9

“Samaria is the capital of Ephraim.

Pekah rules in Samaria.

You must keep trusting me.

Without that trust you will fall.”

## The Lord promises a sign

## Isaiah 7:10

The LORD spoke to Ahaz again.

## Isaiah 7:11

“Ask the LORD your God for a sign.

Ask for something deep in the place of the dead.

Or ask for something high in heaven.” A sign would help Ahaz know God would keep His word.

## Isaiah 7:12

Ahaz answered, “I will ask for nothing.

I will not put the LORD to the test.”

## Isaiah 7:13

Isaiah said, “Listen, family of David!

You have already worn out people’s patience.

Must you wear out my God’s patience too?

## Isaiah 7:14

“The Lord Himself will give you a sign.

A virgin will have a baby.” A virgin is someone who has never had sex.

Isaiah said, “She will give birth to a son.

She will name Him Immanuel.” Immanuel means “God is with us.”

## Isaiah 7:15

The boy will learn to refuse evil and choose good.

By then He will eat curds and honey.

Curds are the soft lumps that form when milk thickens.

## Isaiah 7:16

Think of the two kings you fear.

Before the boy can choose between good and evil, the land of those kings will lie in ruins.

## The king of Assyria will come

## Isaiah 7:17

Isaiah told Ahaz, “The LORD will bring the king of Assyria against you.

Trouble will come to you, your family and your people.

You have seen nothing like it since Ephraim broke away from Judah.”

## Isaiah 7:18

Isaiah pictured the LORD whistling to call flies and bees.

The flies come from the far streams of Egypt’s Nile River.

The bees come from Assyria.

The swarms picture the crowds coming from those lands.

## Isaiah 7:19

They will settle in the deep valleys.

They will fill the cracks in the rocks.

They will come to every thornbush and water hole.

## Isaiah 7:20

Isaiah pictured the Lord hiring a razor from beyond the Euphrates River.

The razor stands for the king of Assyria.

It will shave your head, your legs and your beard.

This shows how the king will bring shame on you.

## What will people eat?

## Isaiah 7:21

In those days someone will keep a young cow and two sheep.

## Isaiah 7:22

The animals will give plenty of milk.

Their owner will make curds to eat.

Everyone left in the land will eat curds and honey.

## Isaiah 7:23

A field might hold a thousand grapevines.

Those vines might be worth a thousand pieces of silver.

In those days only thorn bushes will grow there.

## Isaiah 7:24

People will carry bows and arrows when they go there.

Thorn bushes will cover the land.

## Isaiah 7:25

People once dug the soil on the hills with hoes.

Now people will stay away for fear of the thorns.

Cattle will eat grass there.

Sheep will walk all over the ground.
