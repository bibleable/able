# Isaiah 60 — Jerusalem’s gates stay open

**About this chapter**

Isaiah speaks to Jerusalem as if the city could look up and see its children coming home. The LORD promises safety and an end to its grief.

## Light rises over Jerusalem

## Isaiah 60:1

Jerusalem, stand up and shine!

Your light has come.

The LORD’s glory rises over you like the morning sun.

## Isaiah 60:2

Darkness covers the earth.

Thick darkness hangs over its people.

Yet the LORD rises over you.

People will see His glory shining there.

## Isaiah 60:3

Nations will come toward your light.

Kings will come toward the brightness of your dawn.

## Children come home

## Isaiah 60:4

Look up!

Look around you.

People are gathering and coming to you.

Your sons are coming home from far away.

People are carrying your daughters in their arms.

## Isaiah 60:5

When you see them, your face will shine.

Your heart will beat fast with joy.

Ships will bring you riches from across the sea.

People of many nations will bring you their wealth.

## Isaiah 60:6

Long lines of camels will fill your land.

Young camels will come from Midian and Ephah.

The people of Sheba will bring gold and sweet-smelling frankincense.

They will tell everyone how great the LORD is.

## Isaiah 60:7

The LORD says, “People will bring you the flocks of Kedar.

They will bring rams from Nebaioth for you.

I will accept the animals offered on my altar.

I will make my house of worship beautiful.”

## Ships arrive from far away

## Isaiah 60:8

Who is that coming so quickly?

They move like clouds across the sky, like doves flying home.

## Isaiah 60:9

The people on the islands wait for the LORD.

Ships from Tarshish lead the way.

They bring your children home from far away, with their silver and gold.

They come to honor the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel.

He has given you glory.

## Open gates and new walls

## Isaiah 60:10

The LORD says, “People from other lands will rebuild your walls.

Their kings will serve you.

I struck you when I was angry.

Now I will show you mercy.

## Isaiah 60:11

“Your gates will stay open day and night.

People will bring the riches of their nations through them.

Their kings will enter in a long procession.

**Find the gates**

Follow the wall from one gate to the other. Imagine the gates staying open even after sunset. People can keep coming into the city.

## Isaiah 60:12

“Any nation or kingdom that refuses to serve you will fall.

It will come to a complete end.

## Isaiah 60:13

“The finest trees of Lebanon will come to you.

Cypress, elm and boxwood will make my holy place beautiful.

I will give glory to the place where my feet rest.

## A city once left empty

## Isaiah 60:14

“The children of those who mistreated you will bow before you.

Those who insulted you will bow with their faces to the ground.

They will call you the City of the LORD.

They will call you Zion, the city of the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 60:15

“People once left you empty and looked down on you.

No one even passed through.

I will make you a city people are proud of forever.

One generation after another will find joy in you.

## Isaiah 60:16

“Think of a mother feeding her child with milk.

Nations and their rulers will provide for you like that.

You will know that I, the LORD, save you and set you free.

I am the Mighty One of Jacob.

## Peace will govern the city

## Isaiah 60:17

“I will bring you gold in place of bronze and silver in place of iron.

Wood will give way to bronze, and stone to iron.

Peace will govern you.

Doing what is right will rule your life together.

## Isaiah 60:18

“No one will hear the sounds of violence in your land.

No one will bring ruin or destruction within your borders.

You will name your walls Salvation because you are safe.

You will name your gates Praise.

## A light that lasts

## Isaiah 60:19

“You will no longer depend on the sun for daylight or the moon for light at night.

I, the LORD, will be your light forever.

Your God will be your glory.

## Isaiah 60:20

“Your sun will never set.

Your moon will never grow dim.

I, the LORD, will be your light forever.

Your days of grief will end.

## Isaiah 60:21

“All your people will do what is right.

The land will be theirs forever.

They are like a branch I planted.

They are the work of my hands.

Through them people will see my glory.

## Isaiah 60:22

“The smallest family among you will grow to a thousand.

The least will become a strong nation.

I am the LORD.

When the time comes, I will do this quickly.”

**Pause and answer**

Who will be Jerusalem’s light forever?
