# Isaiah 28 — A stone people can build on

**About this chapter**

Isaiah warns leaders who trust their own lies. A builder’s stone and a farmer’s field help explain what the LORD says.

## A city like a fading crown

## Isaiah 28:1

Trouble is coming to Ephraim’s proud city.

It stands like a crown above a valley of rich farmland.

People drunk with wine take pride in its beauty.

But the flowers in their crown are fading.

## Isaiah 28:2

The Lord has someone strong enough to bring the city down.

He will come like a storm of hail, like rain that sends a river over its banks.

He will dash that crown to the ground.

## Isaiah 28:3

Feet will trample the crown that Ephraim’s heavy drinkers prized.

## Isaiah 28:4

Look at that fading flower above the rich valley.

The city is like the first ripe fig before summer harvest.

Someone sees the fig, picks it and eats it at once.

The city will fall just as quickly.

## The LORD helps His people

## Isaiah 28:5

On that day the LORD who commands heaven’s armies will be like a beautiful crown.

The people who remain will find their honor in Him.

## Isaiah 28:6

He will help judges decide what is fair.

He will give strength to those who defend the city gate.

## Leaders who cannot see clearly

## Isaiah 28:7

Priests and prophets also stumble because they drink too much.

Wine clouds their thinking.

The prophets cannot understand what they see in visions.

The priests make mistakes when they judge.

## Isaiah 28:8

Vomit covers every table.

There is no clean spot left.

## Isaiah 28:9

The people ask, “Who is He trying to teach?

Does He think we are babies who have just stopped drinking their mothers’ milk?”

## Isaiah 28:10

This is how the message sounds to them.

“One order, then another.

One line, then another.

A little here.

A little there.”

## A message in another language

## Isaiah 28:11

So the LORD will speak to them through people who use a foreign language.

Their words will sound like mockery.

## Isaiah 28:12

The LORD had said, “Here is a place to rest.

Let tired people rest here.” His people refused to listen.

## Isaiah 28:13

Now the LORD’s words will sound like this to them.

“One order, then another.

One line, then another.

A little here.

A little there.” They will stumble backward and hurt themselves.

They will fall into a trap and become prisoners.

## A hiding place made of lies

## Isaiah 28:14

You rulers in Jerusalem who make fun of this message, listen to the LORD.

## Isaiah 28:15

You say, “We made a deal with death.

We have an agreement with the place of the dead.

When disaster sweeps through, we will be safe.

We hide behind lies and trust them to protect us.”

## A firm stone in Zion

## Isaiah 28:16

The Lord GOD says, “Look!

I am laying a stone in Zion.” Zion is Jerusalem.

“This precious cornerstone has proved strong.

People can safely build on it.

Whoever trusts will stand firm.

![A cornerstone where two stone walls meet.](../pictures/cornerstone.svg)

**Find the cornerstone**

Follow each wall to the corner where the walls meet. The builder needs a strong stone there. The LORD uses this picture to show why people can trust Him.

## Isaiah 28:17

“I will use justice like a builder’s measuring line.

I will use what is right like a tool for checking that stones lie level.

Hail will sweep away the lies you hide behind.

Water will flood your hiding place.

## Isaiah 28:18

“Your deal with death will come apart.

Your agreement with the place of the dead will fail.

When disaster sweeps through, it will knock you down and crush you.

## Isaiah 28:19

“Each time it comes, it will carry you away.

It will sweep through morning after morning, by day and by night.” Understanding this warning will fill people with fear.

## The rulers cannot get comfortable

## Isaiah 28:20

Imagine a bed too short to stretch out on.

Imagine a blanket too small to cover you.

That is how little safety your plans can give you.

## Isaiah 28:21

The LORD will rise to act as He did at Mount Perazim.

He will act as He did in the Valley of Gibeon.

What He is about to do will seem strange and deeply troubling.

## Isaiah 28:22

Stop mocking, or the chains that hold you will grow heavier.

I heard the Lord GOD who commands heaven’s armies announce His decision.

Destruction is coming on the whole land.

## What God teaches a farmer

## Isaiah 28:23

Listen closely to me.

Pay attention to what I say.

## Isaiah 28:24

Does a farmer do nothing but plow every day?

Does he keep breaking up the soil forever?

## Isaiah 28:25

Once the ground is level, he scatters caraway and cumin seeds.

These plants give food flavor.

He plants wheat in rows and barley in its own patch.

Around the edge he plants rye, another kind of grain.

## Isaiah 28:26

God teaches the farmer how to do this work.

## Isaiah 28:27

The farmer uses a stick to knock caraway seeds loose.

He uses a rod for cumin.

He keeps the heavy threshing tool off the caraway.

He keeps the cart wheel off the cumin.

## Isaiah 28:28

Grain must be ground to make bread.

But the farmer knows when to stop knocking the grain loose.

He rolls cart wheels over it without letting the horses crush it.

## Isaiah 28:29

This wisdom also comes from the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

His advice is wonderful.

His understanding is great.
