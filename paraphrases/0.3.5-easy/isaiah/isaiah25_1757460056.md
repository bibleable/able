# Isaiah 25 — The LORD will wipe away every tear

**About this chapter**

Isaiah praises God for protecting people who need help. Then he describes a feast where people from every nation will have a place.

## Isaiah praises God

## Isaiah 25:1

LORD, You are my God.

I will tell how great You are.

I will praise Your name.

Long ago You planned wonderful things.

You have done everything You said You would do.

## Isaiah 25:2

You turned a strong city into a pile of stones.

Its walls could not save it.

The city where foreigners had their fort is gone.

No one will ever build it again.

## Isaiah 25:3

So even strong nations will honor You.

In cities ruled by cruel people, people will stand in awe of You.

## A shelter for people in trouble

## Isaiah 25:4

You give poor people a safe place to go.

You protect people who need help when trouble comes.

Cruel people threaten them like driving rain beating against a wall.

You are like a shelter in that storm.

You are like shade on a hot day.

![A shelter roofed with leafy branches.](../pictures/feast-shelter.svg)

**Find the shade**

Point to the space under the branches. Imagine resting there when the sun is hot. Isaiah says God gives people safety like that.

## Isaiah 25:5

The shouting of strangers is like fierce heat on dry ground.

You quiet their shouting.

A cloud passes over and cools the ground with its shade.

Just like that, You silence the song of cruel people.

## A feast for all peoples

## Isaiah 25:6

On this mountain the LORD who commands heaven’s armies will prepare a feast.

People from every nation will eat there.

He will serve the best meat.

He will pour fine wine that has aged for years.

## Isaiah 25:7

On this mountain God will destroy the covering that lies over every nation.

Isaiah pictures death as a cloth spread over all peoples.

## Isaiah 25:8

God will swallow up death.

Death will be gone forever.

The Lord GOD will wipe the tears from every face.

Everywhere on earth, He will take away His people’s shame.

The LORD has said it.

## The people welcome the LORD

## Isaiah 25:9

On that day people will say, “This is our God!

We waited for Him, and He saved us.

This is the LORD we waited for.

Let us celebrate because He has saved us.”

## Moab’s high walls fall

## Isaiah 25:10

The LORD will rest His hand on this mountain.

In their own land the people of Moab will be trampled down.

Isaiah pictures straw trampled into a pile of manure.

## Isaiah 25:11

Moab will stretch out its hands in the mess, like someone trying to swim.

However skillful those hands may be, they cannot save Moab.

The LORD will bring down Moab’s pride.

## Isaiah 25:12

The LORD will tear down the high walls of Moab’s fort.

They will fall to the ground and lie in the dust.
