# Isaiah 6 — Isaiah says, “Send me!”

**About this chapter**

Isaiah tells what he saw and heard in the temple. The temple was the place where people worshiped God.

## Isaiah sees the King

## Isaiah 6:1

The year King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord.

The Lord sat high above me on a throne.

A throne is a king’s seat.

The long end of His robe filled the temple.

## Isaiah 6:2

Above the Lord were heavenly beings called seraphim.

Each one had six wings.

Each covered its face with two wings.

Each covered its feet with two more.

Each used the last two wings to fly.

## Isaiah 6:3

They called out to one another.

“Holy, holy, holy is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies!

His glory fills the whole earth.”

They praised God because He alone is holy like this.

His glory shows how great He is.

## Isaiah 6:4

At the sound of their voices, the door frames shook.

Even the stones under the doors shook.

Smoke filled the temple.

## Isaiah receives forgiveness

## Isaiah 6:5

I cried, “What will happen to me? I am ruined!

My lips are unclean.

The people around me have unclean lips too.

Yet I have seen the King with my own eyes!

He is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.”

Isaiah spoke about unclean lips because he and his people had said wrong things.

## Isaiah 6:6

One of the seraphim took a glowing coal from the altar.

The altar was the place for offerings to God.

He used tongs to pick up the hot coal.

Then he flew to me with it.

## Isaiah 6:7

He touched my mouth with the coal.

He said, “See, this coal has touched your lips.

God has taken away your guilt.

He has forgiven your sin.”

## The Lord sends Isaiah

## Isaiah 6:8

Then I heard the Lord ask, “Whom shall I send?

Who will go for us?”

I answered, “Here I am. Send me!”

## Isaiah 6:9

The Lord said, “Go and tell these people my message.

Tell them, ‘Keep listening. You will never understand.

Keep looking. You will never see what it means.’

## Isaiah 6:10

“Make their hearts hard.

Make their ears deaf. Close their eyes.

Otherwise they might see and hear.

They might understand and turn back to me.

Then I would heal them.”

A hard heart pictures someone who will no longer listen to God.

## How long will this last?

## Isaiah 6:11

I asked, “How long, Lord?”

He answered, “Until the cities fall into ruins.

No one will live there.

The houses will stand empty.

The land itself will lie in ruins.

## Isaiah 6:12

“The LORD will drive the people far away.

The land will stand empty and abandoned.

## Isaiah 6:13

“Even if one person in ten stays, fire will sweep through the land again.

Think of a great oak or a terebinth tree.

When someone cuts it down, a stump remains.

The holy seed will be like that stump.”

The stump pictures the people who will remain and belong to God.
