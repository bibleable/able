# Isaiah 19 — Three nations share God’s blessing

**About this chapter**

Egypt depends on the Nile for food and work. Isaiah describes what happens when it dries up. Later he tells how the LORD will answer Egypt’s prayers.

## Trouble comes to Egypt

## Isaiah 19:1

Isaiah gives this warning about Egypt.

Look!

The LORD rides a fast-moving cloud toward Egypt.

Even the idols Egypt worships shake before Him.

The people lose their courage.

## Isaiah 19:2

The LORD says, “I will turn Egyptians against one another.

Brothers will fight brothers.

Neighbors will fight neighbors.

Cities and kingdoms will fight each other.

## Isaiah 19:3

“The Egyptians will lose heart.

I will bring their plans to nothing.

They will turn to the images they worship.

They will ask the dead for help.

They will visit people who claim to speak with spirits.

## Isaiah 19:4

“I will hand the Egyptians over to a cruel master.

A harsh king will rule them.” The Lord GOD who commands heaven’s armies has spoken.

## The Nile dries up

## Isaiah 19:5

The water of the Nile River will dry up.

Only the dry riverbed will remain.

## Isaiah 19:6

Egypt’s canals will smell bad.

Water in the streams will slow to a trickle and then stop.

The tall plants along the water will dry up.

## Isaiah 19:7

Plants will wither where the Nile reaches the sea.

The crops people planted beside the river will dry up too.

The wind will carry them away.

## Isaiah 19:8

The people who fish in the Nile will grieve.

Some use hooks.

Others spread nets on the water.

All of them will suffer when the river dries up.

## Isaiah 19:9

People who work with flax will lose hope.

Flax is a plant used to make linen thread.

Those who weave fine cloth from that thread will turn pale.

## Isaiah 19:10

The cloth workers will be discouraged.

Everyone who works for wages will feel weighed down with sorrow.

## Egypt’s leaders lose their way

## Isaiah 19:11

The leaders of Zoan act like fools.

The advisers of Pharaoh, Egypt’s king, give him foolish advice.

How can an adviser tell Pharaoh, “I am wise.

Kings from the east were my ancestors”?

## Isaiah 19:12

Pharaoh, where are your wise advisers now?

Let them tell you what the LORD who commands heaven’s armies plans to do to Egypt.

## Isaiah 19:13

The leaders in Zoan act foolishly.

The leaders in Memphis believe lies.

The people Egypt depends on have led the whole country the wrong way.

## Isaiah 19:14

The LORD has filled Egypt with confusion.

Whatever the Egyptians try, they lose their way.

Isaiah compares Egypt to a drunk person stumbling through his own vomit.

## Isaiah 19:15

No one in Egypt can help.

Isaiah speaks of the head and the tail, the tall palm tree and the low reed.

He means everyone, from the most powerful to the least.

## Egypt hears of God’s plan

## Isaiah 19:16

In that day the LORD who commands heaven’s armies will raise His hand against Egypt.

The Egyptians will shake with fear.

## Isaiah 19:17

Even the name of Judah will frighten Egypt.

When people mention Judah, the Egyptians will remember the LORD’s plan against them.

They will tremble.

## Isaiah 19:18

In that day people in five Egyptian cities will speak the language of Canaan.

They will promise to be faithful to the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

One city will have the name City of the Sun.

## The LORD listens to Egypt

## Isaiah 19:19

People will build an altar to the LORD in the middle of Egypt.

An altar is a place to bring offerings.

At the border they will set up a pillar to honor the LORD.

## Isaiah 19:20

These will be signs of the LORD’s presence in Egypt.

When cruel rulers hurt the people, the Egyptians will call to the LORD.

He will send someone to defend and rescue them.

## Isaiah 19:21

The LORD will show the Egyptians who He is.

They will recognize Him as the LORD.

They will worship Him with sacrifices and other gifts.

They will make promises to Him and keep them.

## Isaiah 19:22

The LORD will strike Egypt with sickness.

The Egyptians will turn to Him.

He will listen to their prayers and heal them.

## A road between two nations

## Isaiah 19:23

In that day a road will link Egypt and Assyria.

People from each country will travel to the other.

Egyptians and Assyrians will worship together.

## Isaiah 19:24

Israel will join Egypt and Assyria.

Together the three nations will bring blessing to the earth.

## Isaiah 19:25

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies will bless all three.

He will say, “I bless Egypt, my people.

I bless Assyria, the work of my hands.

I bless Israel, my own treasured people.”
