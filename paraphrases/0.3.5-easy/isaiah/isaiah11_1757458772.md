# Isaiah 11 — A ruler from Jesse’s family

**About this chapter**

Isaiah pictures a tree that someone has cut down. A new branch grows from its stump. This is how Isaiah describes the coming of a king.

## A new branch grows

## Isaiah 11:1

A new shoot will grow from Jesse’s stump.

A branch from his roots will bear fruit.

Jesse was King David’s father.

The new growth pictures a ruler from Jesse’s family.

![A new shoot growing from a cut tree stump.](../pictures/job-stump-and-shoot.svg)

**Find the new shoot**

Point to the stump. Now find the new growth. Isaiah uses the new shoot to picture the ruler who will come from Jesse’s family.

## Isaiah 11:2

The Spirit of the LORD will stay with this ruler.

The Spirit will give Him wisdom and understanding.

He will know how to guide people.

He will have strength to act.

He will know the LORD and honor Him deeply.

## A king who judges fairly

## Isaiah 11:3

Honoring the LORD will bring Him joy.

When He judges, He will look beyond appearances.

He will also look beyond what people tell Him.

## Isaiah 11:4

He will give poor people a fair hearing.

He will do what is right for people with little power.

His words will strike the earth like a stick.

At His word, those who do evil will die.

## Isaiah 11:5

Isaiah pictures the ruler wearing a belt around His waist.

Doing right and keeping His word are like that belt.

They are always with Him.

## A little child leads the animals

## Isaiah 11:6

A wolf will live with a lamb.

A leopard will lie beside a goat.

A calf, a young lion and a well-fed animal will stay together.

A little child will lead them.

## Isaiah 11:7

A cow and a bear will eat grass together.

Their young will lie down side by side.

A lion will eat straw like an ox.

## Isaiah 11:8

A baby will play near a cobra’s hole.

A small child will reach into a viper’s nest.

These snakes can harm people with their bites.

Isaiah pictures a time when even these children will be safe.

## Isaiah 11:9

No one will hurt or destroy anything on the LORD’s holy mountain.

People everywhere will know the LORD.

That knowledge will fill the earth as water fills the sea.

## People come from far away

## Isaiah 11:10

On that day the ruler from Jesse’s family will stand like a flag.

People from many nations will come looking for Him.

His resting place will be full of glory.

## Isaiah 11:11

The Lord will reach out a second time to bring His people back.

Some have survived in Assyria, Egypt, Pathros and Cush.

Others are in Elam, Shinar, Hamath and the islands of the sea.

## Isaiah 11:12

The Lord will raise a flag for the nations to see.

He will gather the people of Israel who had to leave home.

He will bring Judah’s scattered people back from every direction.

## A road home

## Isaiah 11:13

Ephraim, the northern kingdom, will stop envying Judah.

Judah’s enemies will be gone.

Judah will stop causing trouble for Ephraim.

## Isaiah 11:14

Together they will rush down on the Philistine hills to the west.

They will take goods from the peoples to the east.

They will take control of Edom and Moab.

The people of Ammon will obey them.

## Isaiah 11:15

The LORD will dry up the gulf of the Sea of Egypt.

He will sweep His hand over the Euphrates River with a hot wind.

He will divide the river into seven streams.

People will walk across with their sandals still dry.

## Isaiah 11:16

A road will open for His people who survive in Assyria.

They will have a way home.

Long ago Israel had a way out of Egypt.
