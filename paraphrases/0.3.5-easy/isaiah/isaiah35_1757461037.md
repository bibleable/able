# Isaiah 35 — A road home to Zion

**About this chapter**

Isaiah has a message for people who are afraid. He pictures flowers growing in dry ground and people singing on their way home.

## Flowers in the desert

## Isaiah 35:1

The dry land and the wilderness will be glad.

The desert will bloom like a rose.

Isaiah pictures the land itself rejoicing.

## Isaiah 35:2

Flowers will open everywhere.

It will be as if the desert were singing for joy.

It will be as beautiful as Lebanon, Carmel and Sharon.

People will see the glory of the LORD.

They will see how great and beautiful our God is.

## Words for people who are afraid

## Isaiah 35:3

Give strength to those whose hands hang weakly.

Help those whose knees shake to stand firm.

## Isaiah 35:4

Tell those who are afraid, “Be strong. Do not fear.

Your God is coming to put wrongs right.

He will punish those who hurt you.

He is coming to save you.”

## Water flows in dry places

## Isaiah 35:5

Then blind people will see.

Deaf people will hear.

## Isaiah 35:6

People who could not walk will leap like deer.

People who could not speak will shout for joy.

Water will burst from the ground in the wilderness.

Streams will flow through the desert.

## Isaiah 35:7

A pool will form where the ground was baked dry.

Springs will bubble up from the thirsty land.

Jackals, wild animals like dogs, once rested there.

Now grass and tall water plants called reeds and papyrus will grow there.

**Follow the water**

Find where the water comes out of the ground. Imagine seeing a pool there after a long walk across dry land.

## A safe road home

## Isaiah 35:8

A road will run through that land.

Its name will be the Way of Holiness.

Holy means set apart for God.

The road is for people who follow God’s way.

Those who are unclean before God will not travel there.

Fools will not wander onto it.

## Isaiah 35:9

No lion will be on that road.

No dangerous animal will come onto it.

The people God has set free will walk there.

## Isaiah 35:10

Those the LORD has set free will come home.

They will enter Zion, also called Jerusalem, singing.

Isaiah pictures them wearing joy like a crown.

Their joy will last forever.

Their sadness and sighing will come to an end.
