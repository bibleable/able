# Isaiah 61 — Good news for people who are hurting

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends a messenger to bring good news. The messenger speaks to people who have lost their freedom, their homes, or someone they love.

## The LORD sends a messenger

## Isaiah 61:1

The messenger says, “The Spirit of the Lord GOD is on me.

The LORD anointed me.

That means He chose me for this work.

He sent me to bring good news to poor people.

He sent me to care for people whose hearts ache.

I will tell captives they can go free.

I will announce freedom to people in prison.

## Isaiah 61:2

“I will announce the year when the LORD shows kindness.

I will announce the day when God punishes wrongdoing.

He sent me to comfort everyone who grieves.

## Isaiah 61:3

“I will comfort those who grieve in Zion, another name for Jerusalem.

People put ashes on their heads to show grief.

Now they will wear beautiful crowns.

They will use sweet oil to celebrate.

They will put on clothes for a celebration and praise God.

People will call them strong oak trees the LORD planted.

This means the LORD helps them stand firm and do right.

People will see how great the LORD is.”

## The people rebuild their cities

## Isaiah 61:4

The people will build again where old buildings fell.

Whole cities have lain in ruins since their grandparents’ time.

The people will repair those cities.

## Isaiah 61:5

People from other lands will feed your flocks.

They will plow your fields.

They will care for your grapevines.

![A grapevine with grapes growing on it.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Isaiah 61:6

People will call you priests of the LORD.

Priests serve God and help people worship Him.

People will say, “You serve our God.”

The wealth of other nations will feed you.

You will speak proudly of the riches you receive.

## Isaiah 61:7

The LORD promises His people twice their share after their shame.

People once treated them with disrespect.

Now they will rejoice over their share.

They will receive twice as much in their land.

Their joy will last forever.

## The LORD makes a lasting promise

## Isaiah 61:8

The LORD says, “I love justice.

Justice means treating people fairly.

I hate robbery and wrongdoing.

I will faithfully give my people what they are due.

I will make a covenant with them.

That is a promise that will last forever.

## Isaiah 61:9

“People in many nations will know their children and grandchildren.

Everyone who sees them will say, ‘The LORD has blessed these people.’”

## A song of joy

## Isaiah 61:10

“I am full of joy because of the LORD.

I praise my God with my whole heart.

God has saved me and covered me with His goodness.

It is like wearing beautiful clothes on my wedding day.

A groom wears a special head covering like a priest’s.

A bride puts on her jewels.

## Isaiah 61:11

“New shoots grow from the ground.

Seeds come up in a garden.

In the same way, the Lord GOD will help people do right.

People will praise Him, and every nation will see it.”
