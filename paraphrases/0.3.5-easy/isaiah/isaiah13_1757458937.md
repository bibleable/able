# Isaiah 13 — A warning for Babylon

**About this chapter**

Isaiah warns that an army will come against Babylon. He describes the harm people will suffer and the city they will leave behind.

## An army gathers

## Isaiah 13:1

Isaiah was the son of Amoz.

God gave Isaiah this warning about Babylon.

## Isaiah 13:2

Raise a flag on a bare hilltop.

Call out to the soldiers.

Wave them through the gates where the rulers live.

## Isaiah 13:3

The LORD says, “I have chosen people for this task.

I have called my soldiers to carry out my judgment.

They will celebrate my victory.”

## Isaiah 13:4

Listen to the noise on the mountains!

It sounds like a great crowd.

People from many kingdoms are gathering.

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies is gathering an army for war.

## Isaiah 13:5

The soldiers come from faraway lands, where the sky meets the earth.

The LORD comes with them.

In His anger He will use them to destroy the whole country.

## A day of fear

## Isaiah 13:6

Cry out in grief!

The day of the LORD is near.

This means the time when God will act as judge.

God Almighty will bring destruction.

## Isaiah 13:7

People will let their hands drop.

Isaiah says their hearts will melt.

He means they will lose all their courage.

## Isaiah 13:8

Terrible fear and pain will grip them.

They will twist in pain like a woman giving birth.

They will stare at each other.

Their faces will grow hot with fear.

## Isaiah 13:9

Look!

The day of the LORD is coming.

It will be a cruel day.

In His fierce anger the LORD will lay waste the earth.

He will destroy the people who do evil there.

## The sky grows dark

## Isaiah 13:10

Look up at the groups of stars.

None of them will shine.

The sun will be dark even as it rises.

The moon will give no light.

## Isaiah 13:11

The LORD says, “I will punish the world for its evil.

I will punish people for the wrong they do.

I will put an end to their boasting.

I will bring down those who hurt others without pity.

## Isaiah 13:12

“So few people will remain that finding one will be harder than finding pure gold.

People will be rarer than the fine gold of Ophir.

## Isaiah 13:13

“I will make the heavens tremble.

I will shake the earth from its place.” This will happen on the day of the LORD’s fierce anger.

He is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

## People flee

## Isaiah 13:14

People will run like a gazelle fleeing a hunter.

They will scatter like sheep without a shepherd.

Each person will hurry back to their own people.

Each will flee to the land where they were born.

## Isaiah 13:15

The attackers will stab the people they catch.

They will kill their prisoners with swords.

## Isaiah 13:16

Parents will see the attackers strike their babies and kill them.

The attackers will rob their homes and rape their wives.

## Isaiah 13:17

The LORD says, “Look!

I will send the people of Media against Babylon.

They do not care about silver.

They have no desire for gold.”

## Isaiah 13:18

The Medes will kill young men with their bows.

They will show no mercy to babies.

They will have no pity for children.

## Animals live in the empty city

## Isaiah 13:19

The people of Babylon are called Chaldeans.

They take pride in their city.

Among kingdoms Babylon is as beautiful as a jewel.

Yet God will bring Babylon down as He brought down Sodom and Gomorrah.

## Isaiah 13:20

No one will make a home there again.

Even travelers who live in tents will camp elsewhere.

No shepherd will bring sheep there to rest.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Point to the tent. Usually a traveler can set one up for the night. Isaiah says even travelers will stay away from Babylon.

## Isaiah 13:21

Desert animals will lie down there.

Their howls will fill the empty houses.

Ostriches will live there.

Wild goats will leap about.

## Isaiah 13:22

Hyenas will howl in Babylon’s forts.

Jackals will call to each other in its fine palaces.

The time for Babylon to fall is near.

Its days are nearly over.
