# Isaiah 23 — Tyre loses its harbor

**About this chapter**

Tyre was a city by the sea. Its people earned money by trading with other lands. Isaiah tells what will happen when its harbor is gone.

## Sailors hear the news

## Isaiah 23:1

Isaiah gives this warning about Tyre.

Sailors on the ships of Tarshish, cry out!

News has reached you from Cyprus.

Tyre lies in ruins.

No house stands there.

No harbor remains where your ships can stop.

## Isaiah 23:2

Be silent, you people along the coast.

Be silent, you traders from Sidon.

Your people sailed across the sea to trade.

## Isaiah 23:3

Ships crossed the open sea carrying grain from Shihor in Egypt.

Tyre earned money by selling crops from the Nile River.

People from many countries traded there.

## Grief spreads along the coast

## Isaiah 23:4

People of Sidon, your strong city stands beside the sea.

Now you have reason to feel ashamed.

Isaiah pictures the sea speaking.

It says, “I have never given birth.

I have raised no sons or daughters.”

## Isaiah 23:5

The news about Tyre will reach Egypt.

The people there will double over in grief.

## Isaiah 23:6

People along the coast, cross the sea to Tarshish!

Cry out in grief!

## Isaiah 23:7

Is this the city where you once celebrated?

Tyre has stood since long ago.

Its people traveled far away to make new homes.

## Who planned this?

## Isaiah 23:8

Tyre once gave crowns to kings.

Its traders lived like princes.

People everywhere honored them.

Who planned to bring such a city down?

## Isaiah 23:9

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies planned it.

He would strip away the beauty that made Tyre proud.

He would humble the people the whole earth honored.

## Isaiah 23:10

People of Tarshish, work your fields as farmers do along the Nile.

The harbor is gone.

## The LORD speaks to Sidon

## Isaiah 23:11

The LORD stretched out His hand over the sea.

Whole kingdoms shook with fear.

He gave the order to tear down the forts of Canaan.

## Isaiah 23:12

The LORD spoke to Sidon as to a young woman who had suffered harm.

“Your days of celebration are over.

Get up and cross the sea to Cyprus.

Even there you will find no rest.”

## Isaiah 23:13

Look at the land of the Chaldeans.

Their people have lost their power.

The Assyrians built tall towers to attack their cities.

They emptied the palaces and left them in ruins.

Now desert animals can live there.

## Isaiah 23:14

Sailors on the ships of Tarshish, cry out!

Your harbor lies in ruins.

## After seventy years

## Isaiah 23:15

For seventy years people will forget Tyre.

That is as long as a king might live.

Then Tyre will be like the woman in a song.

She is a prostitute, someone who earns money by having sex.

## Isaiah 23:16

The song says, “People have forgotten you.

Pick up your harp.

Walk through the city.

Play a sweet tune and sing song after song.

Then people will remember you.”

## Isaiah 23:17

After seventy years the LORD will restore Tyre.

Like the woman in the song, the city will earn money again.

Its traders will do business with kingdoms all over the earth.

## Isaiah 23:18

This time Tyre’s earnings will belong to the LORD.

The money will go to the people who live in His presence.

It will buy them plenty of food and fine clothes.

Tyre will not store that money away.
