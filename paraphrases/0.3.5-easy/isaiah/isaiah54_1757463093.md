# Isaiah 54 — Make room in your tent

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to Jerusalem as a mother making room for her family. Later He pictures the city itself, once battered and now ready to be rebuilt.

## Room for a growing family

## Isaiah 54:1

The LORD speaks to His people as to a woman who has never given birth.

“Sing for joy!

You who have never felt the pains of childbirth, sing out loud!

The woman who was left alone will have more children than the woman who has a husband.”

## Isaiah 54:2

“Make more room for your tent.

Spread the tent cloth wide.

Use all the space you need.

Make the ropes longer.

Drive the pegs deep into the ground.

## Isaiah 54:3

“Your family will spread out on both sides.

Your children and their children will take over lands from other nations.

They will live in cities that now stand empty.

![A tent standing beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Trace the sides of the tent. Imagine spreading the cloth wider. A larger tent has room for the children coming home.

## The LORD calls His people back

## Isaiah 54:4

“Do not be afraid.

You will no longer hang your head in shame.

You will forget the shame you felt when you were young.

You will no longer remember the disgrace you felt as a widow.

## Isaiah 54:5

“The One who made you is your husband.

His name is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

The Holy One of Israel is your Redeemer, the one who sets you free.

He is the God of the whole earth.

## Isaiah 54:6

“You were like a young wife whose husband left her.

You were deeply hurt when he turned you away.

Now the LORD has called you back,” says your God.

## Isaiah 54:7

“I left you for a little while.

Now I will bring you back because I care so deeply for you.

## Isaiah 54:8

“For a moment I was angry and hid my face from you.

Now I will show you kindness that lasts forever.” The LORD who sets you free has said this.

## A promise of peace

## Isaiah 54:9

“This reminds me of Noah’s time.

I promised that floodwaters would never again cover the whole earth.

Now I promise that I will no longer be angry with you or speak against you.

## Isaiah 54:10

“Mountains may move out of place.

Hills may shake.

Still my love for you will hold firm.

I will keep my covenant of peace, my lasting promise to you.” This is the word of the LORD who cares for you.

## A city rebuilt with jewels

## Isaiah 54:11

The LORD says, “Jerusalem, you are like a city beaten by storms.

You have suffered with no one to comfort you.

I will set your stones in a dark mineral called antimony.

I will lay your foundations with blue sapphires.

## Isaiah 54:12

“I will put red rubies on top of your towers.

Your gates will sparkle with jewels.

All your walls will be made of precious stones.

## Isaiah 54:13

“I, the LORD, will teach all your children.

They will thrive.

## Isaiah 54:14

“What is right will give your city a firm foundation.

Cruel rulers will be far away.

You will have no reason to fear.

Terror will not come near you.

## The LORD defends His servants

## Isaiah 54:15

“If someone attacks you, I did not send them.

Whoever attacks you will fall before you.

## Isaiah 54:16

“I made the metalworker who blows on the coals.

He works at the fire to shape a weapon for its task.

I also made the one who brings destruction.

## Isaiah 54:17

“No weapon made to hurt you will succeed.

When people accuse you, you will show that their charges are false.

This is what my servants receive from me.

I am the one who declares them to be in the right,” says the LORD.
