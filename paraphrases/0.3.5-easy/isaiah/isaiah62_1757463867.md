# Isaiah 62 — A new name for Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Zion is another name for Jerusalem. The speaker talks to the city as if it were a person. The new names tell the people how much they matter to God.

## God delights in Jerusalem

## Isaiah 62:1

For Zion’s sake, I will keep speaking.

For Jerusalem’s sake, I will keep working.

One day everyone will see that Jerusalem does what is right.

It will shine like the light of dawn.

Everyone will see that God has saved Jerusalem.

It will be as clear as a burning torch.

## Isaiah 62:2

Jerusalem, nations will see how you do what is right.

Every king will see your glory.

The LORD Himself will give you a new name.

## Isaiah 62:3

You will be like a beautiful royal crown.

The LORD will hold you in His hand.

You are precious to Him.

## Isaiah 62:4

People used to call you “Left Alone.”

They called your land “Empty.”

Now they will call you Hephzibah.

That means “My delight is in her.”

They will call your land Beulah.

That means “Married.”

The LORD takes delight in you.

Your land will be like a bride to Him.

## Isaiah 62:5

Think of a young man marrying a young woman.

That is how closely your sons will belong to you, Jerusalem.

Think of a groom’s joy when he sees his bride.

That is how much joy you bring your God.

## Keep praying for Jerusalem

## Isaiah 62:6

Jerusalem, I have set guards on your walls.

Day and night, they will keep calling out.

You who pray to the LORD, keep praying.

## Isaiah 62:7

Keep asking the LORD to make Jerusalem stand firm.

Keep asking until people everywhere praise Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 62:8

The LORD made a promise by His strong right arm.

This means He has the power to keep His word.

He said, “I will never again give your grain to your enemies.

People from other lands will never again drink the wine you worked for.

## Isaiah 62:9

“The people who gather the grain will eat it.

They will praise the LORD.

Those who gather the grapes will drink the wine.

They will drink it in My holy courtyards.”

These are open spaces at the LORD’s house.

## Clear the road for the people

## Isaiah 62:10

Go out through the gates!

Get the road ready for the people.

Build up the road.

Clear away the stones.

Lift a flag high so the nations can see it.

## Isaiah 62:11

The LORD sent this message across the whole earth.

“Tell the people of Zion, ‘Look! Your Savior is coming!

He is coming to save you.

He brings His reward with Him.

He brings what is due to each person.’”

## Isaiah 62:12

People will call them “The Holy People.”

That means they belong to God.

People will also call them “The People the LORD Rescued.”

Jerusalem, people will call you “The City Someone Looked For.”

They will call you “The City God Has Not Abandoned.”
