# Isaiah 2 — The nations learn peace

**About this chapter**

Isaiah sees people from many nations walking up a mountain to learn from the LORD. Then he looks at his own people and sees them bowing to things they made.

## An invitation to the mountain

## Isaiah 2:1

God showed Isaiah, the son of Amoz, this message about Judah and Jerusalem.

## Isaiah 2:2

In the last days, the mountain where the LORD’s house stands will rise above the hills.

It will be first among the mountains.

People from every nation will flow toward it like a river.

## Isaiah 2:3

Many peoples will say, “Come with us to the LORD’s mountain.

Let us go to the house of Jacob’s God.

He will teach us his ways.

Then we can live as he teaches.” The LORD’s teaching will go out from Zion.

Zion is Jerusalem, the city where people will hear his word.

## Tools for tending the land

## Isaiah 2:4

The LORD will settle disputes between nations.

People will hammer their swords into blades for plowing fields.

They will turn their spears into tools for trimming vines.

Nations will stop fighting each other.

They will stop training for war.

## Isaiah 2:5

Come, family of Jacob.

Let us walk in the LORD’s light.

Let him show us how to live.

## What fills the land

## Isaiah 2:6

LORD, you have left your people, the family of Jacob.

They have filled their lives with practices from the east.

Like the Philistines, they try to tell the future.

They shake hands to make agreements with foreigners.

## Isaiah 2:7

Their land is full of silver and gold.

Their treasures seem endless.

Their land is full of horses and countless chariots.

## Isaiah 2:8

Their land is full of idols.

These are things people make and worship.

They bow down to what their own fingers shaped.

## Isaiah 2:9

People have brought themselves low.

Their pride has turned to shame.

LORD, do not forgive them!

## The day pride falls

## Isaiah 2:10

Hide among the rocks!

Hide in the dust!

The LORD’s greatness shines out, and people tremble before him.

## Isaiah 2:11

People who once looked down on others will lower their eyes.

Their pride will fall.

On that day the LORD alone will stand high above all.

## Isaiah 2:12

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies has set a day.

He will bring down everyone who is proud and everything that stands tall.

## Isaiah 2:13

That day will come against the tall cedars of Lebanon and all the great oak trees of Bashan.

## Isaiah 2:14

It will come against every towering mountain and every high hill.

## Isaiah 2:15

It will come against every tall tower and every strong city wall.

## Isaiah 2:16

It will come against the ships of Tarshish and every splendid ship.

## The idols disappear

## Isaiah 2:17

People will lose the pride that made them feel so high.

On that day the LORD alone will stand high above all.

## Isaiah 2:18

The idols will disappear completely.

## Isaiah 2:19

People will run into caves and holes in the ground.

The LORD will rise to shake the earth.

His greatness will shine out, and they will hide in fear.

## Isaiah 2:20

On that day people will throw away their silver and gold idols.

They made them to worship.

Now they will leave them to the moles and bats.

## Isaiah 2:21

People will run into caves and cracks in the cliffs.

They will try to hide from the LORD’s terrible splendor when he rises to shake the earth.

## Isaiah 2:22

Stop putting your trust in human power.

A person’s life depends on each breath.

How much can you count on that power?

**Pause and answer**

What will the nations do with their swords when the LORD settles their disputes?
