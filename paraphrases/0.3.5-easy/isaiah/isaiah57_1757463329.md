# Isaiah 57 — Peace for those far and near

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes people who turn to other gods for help. The LORD tells hurting people where they can find shelter and peace.

## Rest for those who do right

## Isaiah 57:1

People who do right die, and no one stops to think.

Faithful people pass away.

No one sees that death takes them out of the reach of evil.

## Isaiah 57:2

Those who have lived honestly enter peace.

They lie down in death and find rest.

## The LORD questions His people

## Isaiah 57:3

The LORD calls others to account.

“Come here, children of a woman who casts spells!

You are children of people who break their marriage promises and sell sex.

## Isaiah 57:4

“Who are you making fun of?

Who are you sticking your tongues out at?

You are a family of people who do wrong and tell lies.

## Isaiah 57:5

“Under the oak trees and other leafy trees, you burn with sexual desire.

Down in the valleys, under the overhanging rocks, you kill your children.

## Isaiah 57:6

“You have chosen smooth stones in the valley as your share.

You even pour out drinks and offer grain to them.

Do you think those gifts should make me turn from my anger?

## People turn to other gods

## Isaiah 57:7

“You have spread your bed high on a hill.

You climb up there to offer sacrifices.

## Isaiah 57:8

“Behind your doors you set up signs of the gods you worship.

You have left me.

You uncovered your bed and made room for others.

You made agreements with those whose beds you loved.

You looked at their naked bodies.” The LORD pictures His people as partners who have been unfaithful to Him.

## Isaiah 57:9

“You took oil to Molech and brought more and more perfume.

You sent messengers far away.

You went down even to Sheol, the place of the dead.

![A small perfume jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

**Look at the jar**

Imagine carrying a jar of perfume as a gift. In this passage, the people bring their gifts to Molech, a god they worshiped.

## Isaiah 57:10

“Those long journeys wore you out.

Yet you never said, ‘There is no hope in this.’ You found fresh strength and kept going.

## Where will you find shelter?

## Isaiah 57:11

“Who frightened you so much that you lied and forgot me?

Why did you give me no thought?

I was silent for a long time.

Is that why you no longer fear me?

## Isaiah 57:12

“You claim to do what is right.

I will show what you have actually done.

Your deeds will do you no good.

## Isaiah 57:13

“When you cry for help, let your collection of idols rescue you!

A breath of wind will blow them all away.

Whoever comes to me for safety will receive the land.

My holy mountain will be their home.”

## Make a way for my people

## Isaiah 57:14

A call will go out.

“Build up the road!

Make the way ready!

Move everything that blocks the path of my people.”

## Isaiah 57:15

God is high above us.

He lives forever, and His name is Holy.

He says, “I live in a high and holy place.

I also live with humble people and those whose spirits are crushed.

I give new strength to the lowly.

I give new life to those who are sorry for their wrongs.

## Isaiah 57:16

“I will not keep accusing people forever.

My anger will come to an end.

Otherwise the people I gave life to would grow faint before me.

## The LORD promises healing

## Isaiah 57:17

“My people greedily wanted more for themselves.

I was angry and struck them.

I turned my face away.

Still they went back to doing whatever they wanted.

## Isaiah 57:18

“I have seen how they live.

Yet I will heal them and guide them.

I will comfort them again, along with those who mourn.

## Isaiah 57:19

“I will give them words of praise.

Peace, peace to those far away and those nearby.

I will heal them,” says the LORD.

## Isaiah 57:20

People who keep doing evil are like a stormy sea.

It cannot settle down.

Its waves keep stirring up mud and dirt.

## Isaiah 57:21

My God says, “Those who do evil have no peace.”
