# Isaiah 32 — A place to live in peace

**About this chapter**

Isaiah describes rulers who protect people. He also warns that the harvest will fail. Then he looks ahead to what will change when God sends His Spirit.

## A king who does right

## Isaiah 32:1

Look!

A king will come who does what is right.

The rulers under him will treat people fairly.

## Isaiah 32:2

Each ruler will protect people like a shelter from wind and rain.

Think of a stream running through dry land.

Think of a great rock giving shade in the desert.

Good rulers will bring people relief like that.

![A shelter with a roof of branches.](../pictures/feast-shelter.svg)

**Find the shelter**

Point to the place someone could rest under the roof. Imagine stepping there out of the wind. Isaiah says a fair ruler will help people feel safe like that.

## Isaiah 32:3

Then people will look with their eyes wide open.

They will listen closely to what they hear.

## Isaiah 32:4

People who rush into things will take time to understand.

People who struggle to get their words out will speak clearly and easily.

## What makes someone worthy of honor?

## Isaiah 32:5

People will stop calling a foolish person honorable.

They will stop giving respect to someone who cheats others.

## Isaiah 32:6

Isaiah describes the kind of foolishness he means.

This person talks nonsense and plans harm.

He lives as though God does not matter.

He tells lies about the LORD.

He leaves hungry people without food.

He takes water away from thirsty people.

## Isaiah 32:7

A dishonest person finds ways to hurt others.

He plans to ruin poor people by telling lies about them.

He does this even when they are asking for what is fair.

## Isaiah 32:8

A person worthy of honor plans to do good.

When something is right, that person stands up for it.

## A warning about the harvest

## Isaiah 32:9

Isaiah says, “You women who sit at ease, stand up.

Listen to me.

You think nothing can go wrong.

Pay attention to my words.

## Isaiah 32:10

“In a little more than a year, you will shake with fear.

There will be no grapes to pick.

The fruit harvest you expect will never come.

## Isaiah 32:11

“You women who feel so safe, tremble!

Take off your clothes.

Wrap rough sackcloth around your waists to show your sorrow.

## The busy city grows empty

## Isaiah 32:12

“Beat your chests in grief for the lovely fields.

Mourn for the vines where grapes once grew.

## Isaiah 32:13

“Grieve for my people’s land.

Thorn bushes will cover it.

Grieve for all the houses where people once held parties in this cheerful city.

## Isaiah 32:14

“People will leave the palace.

The busy city will stand empty.

The hill and its watchtower will be like caves for animals.

Wild donkeys will delight in them.

Flocks will feed there.”

## God sends His Spirit

## Isaiah 32:15

This will last until God pours out His Spirit on us from above.

Then fruit trees will grow in the desert.

The orchard will grow so thick it will look like a forest.

## Isaiah 32:16

People in the wilderness will treat each other fairly.

People in the rich farmland will do what is right.

## Isaiah 32:17

Doing what is right will bring peace.

People will be able to live quietly, free from fear.

That confidence will last forever.

## Safe places to rest

## Isaiah 32:18

“My people will live in peaceful homes.

They will have safe places where they can rest.”

## Isaiah 32:19

Yet hail will flatten the forest.

The city will sink low.

## Isaiah 32:20

Blessed are the people who plant seeds beside plenty of water.

They can let their oxen and donkeys wander freely.

What does Isaiah say will come when people do what is right?
