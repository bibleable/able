# Isaiah 56 — A house of prayer for all nations

**About this chapter**

Some people fear they have no place among God’s people. The LORD tells them what they can count on. Isaiah also warns the leaders whose job is to watch over Israel.

## Do what is right

## Isaiah 56:1

The LORD says, “Treat people fairly.

Do what is right.

I will soon come to save.

People will see me put things right.

## Isaiah 56:2

“I bless the person who keeps doing these things.

Keep the Sabbath holy.

This is the day set apart for rest.

Keep your hands from doing evil.”

## A name that will last

## Isaiah 56:3

A person from another land who has joined the LORD’s people should never say, “The LORD will shut me out.” A eunuch should never say, “I am just a dry tree.” A eunuch was a man whose body had been changed so he could not father children.

A dry tree bears no fruit.

This pictures his fear that no one will carry on his name.

## Isaiah 56:4

The LORD says, “Some eunuchs keep my Sabbath days holy.

They choose what pleases me.

They stay faithful to my covenant, the lasting agreement I made with my people.

## Isaiah 56:5

“Inside the walls of my house I will give them a place of honor.

Their names will be remembered there.

That is better than having sons and daughters to carry on their names.

I will give them a name that lasts forever.

## People from other lands

## Isaiah 56:6

“People from other lands also come to me.

They love my name and choose to serve me.

They keep the Sabbath holy and stay faithful to my covenant.

## Isaiah 56:7

“I will bring them to my holy mountain.

My house will be a place of joy for them as they pray.

I will accept the sacrifices they bring to my altar, including the offerings they burn there.

People will call my house a house of prayer for all nations.”

## Isaiah 56:8

The Lord GOD gathers the people of Israel who were scattered far from home.

He says, “I have already gathered people together.

I will bring still others to join them.”

## Leaders who fail to keep watch

## Isaiah 56:9

Now Isaiah calls out to the wild animals.

“Come, animals of the fields and forests!

Come and eat your fill!”

## Isaiah 56:10

Israel’s leaders should be like guards who watch for danger.

Yet they do not see what is happening.

They are like dogs that cannot bark a warning.

They lie down and dream.

They love to sleep.

## Isaiah 56:11

They are also like hungry dogs that never get enough to eat.

These leaders should care for people as shepherds care for sheep.

Yet they have no good judgment.

Each goes his own way and looks for what he can get for himself.

## Isaiah 56:12

They say, “Come!

I will get the wine.

Let us drink strong drink together.

Tomorrow will be like today, only much better!”
