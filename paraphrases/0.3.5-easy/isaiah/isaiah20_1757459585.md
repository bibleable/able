# Isaiah 20 — Isaiah walks barefoot

**About this chapter**

People along the coast hoped Egypt and Cush would protect them. The LORD used something they could see to warn them about that hope.

## Before Ashdod fell

## Isaiah 20:1

Sargon was the king of Assyria.

He sent his chief army commander to Ashdod.

The commander attacked the city and took it.

Isaiah’s part in this story began before that year.

## Isaiah 20:2

The LORD had already spoken through Isaiah, the son of Amoz.

The LORD told Isaiah, “Take the sackcloth off your waist.

Take the sandals off your feet.”

Sackcloth is rough cloth.

Isaiah did what the LORD said.

He walked around naked and barefoot.

## What Isaiah’s actions meant

## Isaiah 20:3

Then the LORD explained what the people had seen.

“My servant Isaiah has walked naked and barefoot for three years.

His actions are a sign.

They warn Egypt and Cush about what is coming.

## Isaiah 20:4

“The king of Assyria will take prisoners from Egypt.

He will force people from Cush to leave home too.

Young and old will walk away naked and barefoot.

Everyone will see their bare backsides.

This will bring shame on Egypt.”

## The people on the coast lose hope

## Isaiah 20:5

Some people had counted on Cush to help them.

They had boasted about Egypt.

Now those people will feel afraid and ashamed.

## Isaiah 20:6

The people on the coast will say, “Look what happened to Egypt and Cush!

We ran to them for help.

We hoped they would save us from the king of Assyria.

How can we escape now?”
