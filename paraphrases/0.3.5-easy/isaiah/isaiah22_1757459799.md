# Isaiah 22 — A key for Eliakim

**About this chapter**

The people of Jerusalem prepare for danger. Isaiah also brings a message to Shebna, the man who runs the palace. Another man will take his place.

## Isaiah weeps for Jerusalem

## Isaiah 22:1

Isaiah gives a warning about the Valley of Vision.

He asks the people of Jerusalem, “What is happening?

Why have you all climbed onto your roofs?

## Isaiah 22:2

“Your city is noisy.

Your town is full of people celebrating.

Yet people from your city have died.

They did not die by the sword or in battle.

## Isaiah 22:3

“Your leaders all ran away together.

The enemy captured them without using a bow.

Others fled far away.

The enemy caught them together too.”

## Isaiah 22:4

Isaiah says, “Look away from me.

Let me cry.

My people face ruin.

Please do not try to comfort me.”

## Soldiers reach the gates

## Isaiah 22:5

The Lord GOD commands heaven’s armies.

He has set a day of shouting and confusion in the Valley of Vision.

People will trample one another.

Walls will break apart.

Cries will rise to the mountains.

## Isaiah 22:6

Soldiers from Elam carry arrows in bags.

They come with war chariots and riders on horses.

Soldiers from Kir uncover their shields.

## Isaiah 22:7

War chariots fill your best valleys.

Riders on horses take their places at your city gates.

## The people inspect the walls

## Isaiah 22:8

Judah has lost its protection.

That day you looked for weapons in the building called the House of the Forest.

## Isaiah 22:9

You inspected the walls of the City of David in Jerusalem.

You found many gaps.

You collected water from the lower pool.

## Isaiah 22:10

You counted the houses in Jerusalem.

You pulled houses down to get material to strengthen the wall.

## Isaiah 22:11

You built a place between the walls to store water from the old pool.

Yet you did not turn to God who made it.

You gave no thought to the One who planned it long ago.

## A feast when God calls for tears

## Isaiah 22:12

That day the Lord GOD called you to weep out loud.

He called you to shave your heads and wear sackcloth.

Sackcloth is rough cloth people wore to show grief.

## Isaiah 22:13

Instead, you held a joyful feast.

You killed cattle and sheep.

You ate meat and drank wine.

You said, “Let’s eat and drink!

Tomorrow we die!”

## Isaiah 22:14

Isaiah says, “I heard the LORD who commands heaven’s armies speak.” The Lord GOD told the people, “This sin will stay with you.

You will carry its guilt until you die.”

## Shebna will lose his place

## Isaiah 22:15

The Lord GOD sent Isaiah to Shebna.

Shebna managed the king’s palace.

Isaiah was to give Shebna this message.

## Isaiah 22:16

“What are you doing here?

Who gave you the right to carve out a tomb here for yourself?

You are cutting your own burial place high in the rock.

## Isaiah 22:17

“You think you are powerful.

Look!

The LORD will seize you and shake you hard.

## Isaiah 22:18

“He will roll you up like a ball.

He will throw you far away into a wide land.

You will die there.

Your fine chariots will stay there too.

You bring shame on the family of the king you serve.

## Isaiah 22:19

“I will take away your job.

You will lose your place in the palace.

## Eliakim receives the key

## Isaiah 22:20

“On that day I will call my servant Eliakim.

He is the son of Hilkiah.

## Isaiah 22:21

“I will dress Eliakim in your robe.

I will tie your sash around his waist.

I will give him the right to make decisions in your place.

He will care like a father for Jerusalem and Judah.

## Isaiah 22:22

“I will put the key to David’s royal house on his shoulder.

When he opens a door, no one can shut it.

When he shuts a door, no one can open it.”

## A peg holds a heavy load

## Isaiah 22:23

The LORD says, “I will set Eliakim firmly in place like a peg driven into a wall.

He will bring honor to his father’s family like a royal throne.”

## Isaiah 22:24

Isaiah pictures bowls and jars of every size hanging from one peg.

Eliakim’s whole family will depend on him.

Even the youngest branches of the family will depend on his position for their honor.

## Isaiah 22:25

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies says the firmly fixed peg will give way.

It will break off and fall.

Everything hanging on it will fall with it.

The LORD has spoken.
