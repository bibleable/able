# Isaiah 40 — Strength for tired people

**About this chapter**

Isaiah brings good news to people who wonder whether God sees their trouble. He asks them to look up at the stars and remember who made them.

## Speak kindly to Jerusalem

## Isaiah 40:1

Your God says, “Comfort my people.

Yes, comfort them.

## Isaiah 40:2

“Speak kindly to the people of Jerusalem.

Tell them their time of forced work is over.

I have forgiven their wrongs.

They have received double punishment from me for all their sins.”

## A road through the desert

## Isaiah 40:3

A voice calls out, “Prepare a road through the wilderness for the LORD.

Make a straight road through the desert for our God.

## Isaiah 40:4

“Fill in every valley.

Bring every hill and mountain low.

Make the uneven ground smooth.

Make the rough land level.

## Isaiah 40:5

“Then everyone together will see the glory of the LORD.” The LORD Himself has said this.

## Grass dries up, but God’s word lasts

## Isaiah 40:6

A voice says, “Call out!” I ask, “What shall I say?” The answer comes, “People are like grass.

Their beauty is like flowers growing in a field.

## Isaiah 40:7

“When the LORD’s breath blows on them, grass dries up and flowers fall.

People are like that grass.

## Isaiah 40:8

“Grass dries up.

Flowers fall.

The word of our God lasts forever.”

## Good news for Judah

## Isaiah 40:9

Zion, climb a high mountain with your good news!

Jerusalem, call out so everyone can hear.

Do not be afraid.

Tell the towns of Judah, “Here is your God!”

## Isaiah 40:10

Look!

The Lord GOD comes with power.

He has the strength to rule.

He brings His reward with Him.

He comes ready to give people what is due to them.

## Isaiah 40:11

God cares for His people as a shepherd cares for sheep.

He gathers the lambs in His arms.

He carries them close to His chest.

He leads the mother sheep gently as they feed their young.

## Who could measure the mountains?

## Isaiah 40:12

Who could measure all the waters in one cupped hand?

Who could measure the sky with outstretched fingers?

Who could gather the earth’s dust in a basket?

Who could weigh hills and mountains on a pair of scales?

## Isaiah 40:13

Who has guided the Spirit of the LORD?

Who has ever been His adviser?

## Isaiah 40:14

Who had to explain anything to Him?

Who taught Him to judge fairly?

Who gave Him knowledge and showed Him how to understand?

## Isaiah 40:15

To God, whole nations are like one drop in a bucket.

They are like a speck of dust on a scale.

He can lift islands as easily as fine dust.

## Isaiah 40:16

Even the forests of Lebanon would provide too little wood for His altar.

All its animals would be too small a gift to burn there in His honor.

## Isaiah 40:17

Before God, all the nations amount to nothing.

Their power seems empty beside His.

## Can a statue be like God?

## Isaiah 40:18

Who can you compare God to?

What picture could show what He is like?

## Isaiah 40:19

Think about how people make an idol, a statue to worship.

One worker pours metal into a mold.

Another covers the statue with gold and fits it with silver chains.

## Isaiah 40:20

Someone too poor for such a gift chooses wood that will last.

Then a skilled worker must make the idol stand without falling over.

## Look at the sky

## Isaiah 40:21

Do you know?

Have you heard?

People have told you this from the beginning.

Have you understood what the earth itself has shown since God made it?

## Isaiah 40:22

God sits as King above the circle of the earth.

The people below look as small as grasshoppers.

He draws out the sky like a curtain.

He spreads it wide like a tent people can live in.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Trace the top of the tent from one side to the other. Imagine spreading its cover wide. Isaiah uses that action to picture God spreading out the sky.

## Isaiah 40:23

He brings princes down.

He takes away the power of rulers everywhere.

## Isaiah 40:24

Those rulers are like seeds just planted.

Their roots have hardly had time to grow.

God blows on them, and they dry up.

A strong wind carries them away like bits of dry stalk.

## Isaiah 40:25

The Holy One asks, “Who can you compare me to?

Who is my equal?”

## Isaiah 40:26

Look up at the stars.

Who made them all?

God brings them out and counts them.

He calls each one by name.

He is so strong that not one goes missing.

## God sees His people

## Isaiah 40:27

People of Israel, you belong to Jacob’s family.

Why do you say, “The LORD cannot see what is happening to me.

My God pays no attention when I ask for justice”?

## Isaiah 40:28

Do you know?

Have you heard?

The LORD is God forever.

He made the earth to its farthest ends.

He never grows tired.

No one can measure all He understands.

## Isaiah 40:29

When people feel faint, God gives them strength.

When they are weak, He makes them stronger.

## Isaiah 40:30

Even young people get tired.

Even young men stumble and fall.

## Isaiah 40:31

Those who wait for the LORD will receive fresh strength.

They will rise like eagles spreading their wings.

They will run without growing tired.

They will walk without fainting.

**Pause and answer**

What does God give to people who feel weak?
