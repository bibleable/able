# Isaiah 29 — A message like a sealed scroll

**About this chapter**

Isaiah calls Jerusalem by the name Ariel. He describes a time when its people cannot understand God’s message and a day when they will hear it.

## Jerusalem surrounded

## Isaiah 29:1

Trouble is coming to Ariel!

Isaiah calls Jerusalem by this name.

This is the city where David camped.

Year after year the people celebrate their festivals.

## Isaiah 29:2

The LORD says, “I will press in on Ariel.

The people will weep and cry aloud.

The city will be like the place on an altar where the fire burns.

## Isaiah 29:3

“I will camp all around you.

I will surround you with towers.

I will set up everything needed to attack your walls.

## Isaiah 29:4

“You will lie low on the ground.

Your words will barely carry through the dust.

Your voice will sound like a spirit whispering from the earth.”

## The attackers wake from a dream

## Isaiah 29:5

Yet your many enemies will become like fine dust.

Those cruel crowds will be like dry husks blowing away.

The change will come in an instant.

## Isaiah 29:6

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies will come.

Thunder will crash and the earth will shake.

There will be a roaring noise, a fierce wind and a fire that burns everything in its path.

## Isaiah 29:7

Many nations will surround Ariel and attack the city.

But what those armies hope to gain will be like something seen in a dream.

## Isaiah 29:8

A hungry person dreams of eating and wakes up hungry.

A thirsty person dreams of drinking and wakes up weak and thirsty.

So it will be for the armies attacking Mount Zion.

## Words on a sealed scroll

## Isaiah 29:9

Stop and stare in wonder!

You close your eyes and cannot see.

You stumble about as though you were drunk.

Yet you have had no wine or strong drink.

## Isaiah 29:10

The LORD has sent a deep sleep over you.

He has closed the eyes of the prophets.

He has covered the heads of those who see visions.

They cannot tell what God is showing them.

## Isaiah 29:11

This whole vision is like a message on a sealed scroll.

A scroll is a rolled sheet of writing.

Hand it to someone who can read.

That person says, “I cannot read it.

The seal keeps it shut.”

## Isaiah 29:12

Hand it to someone who has never learned to read.

That person says, “I cannot read.”

## Words of praise and secret plans

## Isaiah 29:13

The Lord says, “These people speak as if they were close to me.

They praise me with their lips.

Yet their hearts are far from me.

They worship by following rules that people taught them.

## Isaiah 29:14

“So once again I will do things that fill them with wonder.

Even their wisest people will lose their wisdom.

No one will find answers from those known for their understanding.”

## Isaiah 29:15

Trouble is coming to those who hide their plans from the LORD.

They work in the dark.

They say, “Who can see us?

Who will ever know?”

## Isaiah 29:16

You have things upside down!

A potter shapes clay into a pot.

Can the pot say, “The potter did not make me”?

Can it say, “The potter knows nothing”?

God knows the people He made.

**Look at the jar**

Trace the curve of the jar with your finger. Imagine the potter shaping that curve in soft clay. Isaiah asks whether the jar could know more than its maker.

## People hear and see

## Isaiah 29:17

Soon Lebanon will become an orchard, a place where fruit trees grow.

The trees in the orchard will grow so thick it will look like a forest.

## Isaiah 29:18

On that day deaf people will hear the words of the scroll.

Blind people will see out of the deep darkness.

## Isaiah 29:19

Humble people will find still more joy in the LORD.

Poor people will rejoice in the Holy One of Israel.

## Isaiah 29:20

Cruel people will vanish.

Those who mock others will disappear.

Everyone who looks for a chance to do evil will come to an end.

## Isaiah 29:21

These people use a word to accuse someone of a crime.

At the city gate they set traps for anyone settling disputes.

They tell lies in court so innocent people cannot get justice.

## Jacob’s family will honor God

## Isaiah 29:22

The LORD rescued Abraham.

Now He speaks about the family of Jacob.

“Jacob’s people will no longer hang their heads in shame.

Their faces will no longer grow pale.

## Isaiah 29:23

“They will see their children around them, the children I made.

Then they will honor my name.

They will treat the Holy One of Jacob as holy.

They will stand in awe of the God of Israel.

## Isaiah 29:24

“People who went the wrong way will come to understand.

Those who complained will be willing to learn.”

**Pause and answer**

Why can’t the person who knows how to read open the scroll?
