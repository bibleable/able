# Isaiah 50 — The servant listens each morning

**About this chapter**

The LORD asks His people why they did not answer His call. Then His servant tells how listening each morning helps him speak to someone who is tired.

## The LORD asks why no one answered

## Isaiah 50:1

The LORD speaks to His people as children whose mother has been sent away.

“Where is the paper saying I divorced your mother?

If I owed someone money, to whom did I sell you?

Your own wrongdoing is why you were sold.

Your sins are why your mother was sent away.

## Isaiah 50:2

“When I came, why was no one there?

When I called, why did no one answer?

Do you think my arm cannot reach you?

Do you think I lack strength to rescue you?

At my command the sea dries up.

I can turn rivers into dry land.

With no water, the fish die and rot.

## Isaiah 50:3

“I can dress the sky in black.

I can cover it with sackcloth.” Sackcloth is rough cloth people wore to show their grief.

![Thick threads woven together into rough sackcloth.](../pictures/job-sackcloth.svg)

**Look at the sackcloth**

Follow a thread across the cloth. Now imagine a dark covering spread across the whole sky. That is how the LORD pictures His power over the heavens.

## The servant listens and speaks

## Isaiah 50:4

Now the servant speaks.

“The Lord GOD teaches me how to speak.

I know how to help someone who is tired with a word.

Every morning He wakes me.

I listen like a student ready to learn.

## Isaiah 50:5

“The Lord GOD opened my ears.

I listened and obeyed.

I did not turn back.

## Isaiah 50:6

“I offered my back to those who beat me.

I let them pull the hair from my cheeks.

When they insulted me and spat at me, I did not hide my face.

## God stands beside His servant

## Isaiah 50:7

“The Lord GOD helps me.

Their insults cannot bring me disgrace.

I set my face like flint.” Flint is a hard stone.

The servant means he is determined to keep going.

“I know I will not be put to shame.

## Isaiah 50:8

“The One who will show that I am in the right is near.

Who wants to accuse me?

Let us stand face to face.

If anyone has a case against me, let him come forward.

## Isaiah 50:9

“The Lord GOD helps me.

Who can declare me guilty?

My accusers will wear out like old clothes.

Moths will eat them up.”

## Trust God in the dark

## Isaiah 50:10

Do you honor the LORD and obey His servant?

Even if you walk in darkness with no light to see by, trust the LORD.

Lean on your God for help.

## Isaiah 50:11

The LORD warns those who light their own fires and carry burning sticks.

“Go on, walk by the light of the fire you lit!

Here is what you will receive from me.

You will lie down in a place of suffering.”

**Pause and answer**

Who does the servant learn to help with his words?
