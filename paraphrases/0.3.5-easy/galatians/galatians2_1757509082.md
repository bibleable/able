# Galatians 2 — Paul speaks up when Peter stops sharing meals

**About this chapter**

Paul remembers meeting the church leaders in Jerusalem.

Later he and Peter disagree about sharing meals in Antioch.

## Paul and Titus visit Jerusalem

## Galatians 2:1

Fourteen years later, I went to Jerusalem again with Barnabas.

I took Titus along too.

## Galatians 2:2

I went because God showed me I should go.

I met privately with the people everyone recognized as leaders.

I told them the good news I teach the Gentiles.

I wanted to be sure my work, past and present, would count.

## Galatians 2:3

Titus was Greek.

Yet no one forced even him to receive circumcision.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision means removing the fold of skin at the tip of the penis.

For Jewish men it was a sign of God’s covenant, his agreement with their people.

## Galatians 2:4

Some people had slipped in pretending to be fellow believers.

They came to spy on the freedom Christ Jesus gives us.

They wanted to make us slaves.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses slavery as a picture of losing their freedom in Christ.

## Galatians 2:5

We never gave in to them, even for a moment.

We wanted you to keep the truth of the good news.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul want the Galatians to keep?

## The leaders welcome Paul and Barnabas

## Galatians 2:6

People greatly respected those leaders.

Their high standing made no difference to me.

God shows no favoritism.

The leaders added nothing to my message.

## Galatians 2:7

They saw that God trusted me with the good news for Gentiles.

God trusted Peter with the good news for Jewish people.

## Galatians 2:8

God worked through Peter when he sent him to Jewish people.

The same God worked through me among the Gentiles.

## Galatians 2:9

James, Peter, and John were leaders the church depended on.

They saw the grace God had given me.

They shook hands with Barnabas and me as partners.

We would go to the Gentiles.

They would go to Jewish people.

## Galatians 2:10

They asked just one thing of us.

We should remember to help poor people.

I was already eager to do that.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the leaders ask Paul and Barnabas to remember?

## Peter stops sharing meals

## Galatians 2:11

Later Peter came to Antioch.

I told Peter to his face that he was wrong.

## Galatians 2:12

Peter used to eat with Gentile believers.

Then some men came from James.

Peter feared the group that insisted on circumcision.

So Peter drew away and stopped eating with the Gentiles.

| Before the men came | After the men came |
| --- | --- |
| Peter ate with Gentile believers. | Peter stopped eating with them. |

The meal changed when Peter became afraid of what others would say.

## Galatians 2:13

The other Jewish believers followed Peter’s example.

They too acted against what they believed.

Even Barnabas let their example lead him astray.

## Galatians 2:14

Their actions went against the truth of the good news.

So I spoke to Peter in front of everyone.

“Peter, you are Jewish, yet you live like a Gentile.

How can you make Gentiles live like Jews?”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Peter stop eating with the Gentile believers?

## Paul explains why he trusts Christ

## Galatians 2:15

We were born Jewish.

We do not belong to the people some call Gentile “sinners.”

## Galatians 2:16

Yet we know that keeping the law makes no one right with God.

God puts people right with him through faith in Jesus Christ.

So we too have put our trust in Christ.

## Galatians 2:17

Suppose we seek to be right with God through Christ.

Suppose that shows that we too are sinners.

Does that mean Christ helps people sin?

Certainly not!

## Galatians 2:18

If I build up again what I tore down, I show that I myself broke the law.

## Galatians 2:19

Through the law, I died to the law.

Now I can live for God.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses death as a picture here.

The law no longer rules his life.

## Galatians 2:20

When Christ died on the cross, my old life died with him.

Christ now lives in me.

I live each day by trusting the Son of God.

He loved me and gave himself for me.

## Galatians 2:21

I hold on to God’s grace.

If keeping the law could put us right with God, Christ would have died for nothing.

**Pause and answer**

Who loved Paul and gave himself for him?
