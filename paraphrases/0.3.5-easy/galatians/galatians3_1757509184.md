# Galatians 3 — God keeps his promise to Abraham

**About this chapter**

Paul asks the Galatians how they received God’s Spirit.

Then he takes them back to a promise God made to Abraham.

## Remember how you began

## Galatians 3:1

People of Galatia, how foolish you have been!

Who has put you under a spell?

We described Jesus Christ dying on the cross so clearly that you could almost see him.

## Galatians 3:2

Tell me just one thing.

Did you receive the Spirit by keeping the law?

Or did you hear the message and trust it?

## Galatians 3:3

Why act so foolishly?

You began with the Spirit’s help.

Are you now trying to finish by your own strength?

## Galatians 3:4

Was all that you suffered for nothing?

I hope it was not.

## Galatians 3:5

God gives you his Spirit freely and does miracles among you.

Does God do this because you keep the law?

Or because you hear his message and believe it?

**Pause and answer**

How did the Galatians receive the Spirit?

## Abraham trusted God

## Galatians 3:6

Abraham believed God.

God counted Abraham’s trust as righteousness.

![Stars shine in a dark sky.](../pictures/abram-looks-at-stars.svg)

When God promised Abraham a great family, Abraham trusted him.

**Help with these words**

Here, righteousness means being right in God’s sight.

## Galatians 3:7

So the people who trust God are Abraham’s children.

## Galatians 3:8

Scripture showed long ago that God would put Gentiles right with him through faith.

Abraham heard this good news in advance.

God told him, “Through you I will bless all nations.”

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Galatians 3:9

Abraham trusted God and received his blessing.

Those who trust God share that blessing with Abraham.

**Pause and answer**

Which nations did God promise to bless through Abraham?

## Christ took the curse for us

## Galatians 3:10

Everyone who relies on keeping the law is under a curse.

The Book of the Law explains why.

It says a curse falls on anyone who fails to keep doing everything it commands.

## Galatians 3:11

Clearly, keeping the law makes no one right with God.

Scripture says that those who are right with God will live by faith.

## Galatians 3:12

The law works on a different basis.

It says that the person who does what it commands will live by doing it.

## Galatians 3:13

Christ set us free from the law’s curse.

He took the curse on himself for us.

Scripture says a curse falls on anyone hung on a tree.

**Help with these words**

Paul connects the tree in Scripture with the wooden cross where Jesus died.

## Galatians 3:14

Through Christ Jesus, Gentiles can now share Abraham’s blessing.

Through faith we can receive the Spirit God promised.

**Pause and answer**

Who took the curse on himself for us?

## The law came after the promise

## Galatians 3:15

Brothers and sisters, think of an agreement between people.

Once they make it binding, no one can cancel it.

No one can add new terms.

## Galatians 3:16

God made promises to Abraham and to his “seed.”

Scripture uses the word “seed,” not the word “seeds.”

That word points to one person, Christ.

**Help with these words**

Here “seed” means someone born into Abraham’s family in a later generation.

## Galatians 3:17

God first made an agreement with Abraham.

The law came 430 years later.

That law cannot cancel the promise God already made.

| First | 430 years later |
| --- | --- |
| God made the promise. | God gave the law. |

## Galatians 3:18

If the inheritance comes by keeping the law, it no longer comes by promise.

But God freely gave it to Abraham by making a promise.

**Pause and answer**

Which came first, God’s promise to Abraham or the law?

## What the law could do

## Galatians 3:19

Then why did God give the law?

He added it because people did wrong.

It would serve until the promised descendant came.

Angels passed on the law through someone who stood between God and the people.

## Galatians 3:20

Someone who stands between others deals with more than one side.

But God is one.

## Galatians 3:21

Does the law work against God’s promises?

Certainly not!

If a law could give life, then the law could put us right with God.

## Galatians 3:22

But Scripture describes everything as shut in under sin’s power.

So those who trust Jesus Christ receive what God promised.

## Galatians 3:23

Before this faith came, the law kept watch over us.

It was like being locked up until God made the way of faith known.

## Galatians 3:24

The law guarded us and led us to Christ.

Then God could put us right with him through faith.

## Galatians 3:25

Now that faith has come, that guardian no longer has charge of us.

**Pause and answer**

To whom did the law lead people?

## One family in Christ

## Galatians 3:26

Through faith in Christ Jesus, you are all God’s children.

## Galatians 3:27

All of you who received baptism into Christ have put on Christ like clothing.

**Help with these words**

Clothes cover a person wherever they go.

Paul uses clothing as a picture of belonging fully to Christ.

## Galatians 3:28

Some of you are Jews and some are Greeks.

Some are slaves and some are free.

Some are men and some are women.

You are all one in Christ Jesus.

## Galatians 3:29

If you belong to Christ, you are Abraham’s descendants.

You will receive what God promised his family.

**Pause and answer**

What do all these different people share?
