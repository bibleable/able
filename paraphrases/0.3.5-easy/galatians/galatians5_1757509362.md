# Galatians 5 — Use your freedom to serve each other

**About this chapter**

Paul writes about the freedom Christ gives.

He explains how people can recognize the Spirit’s work in daily life.

## Hold on to your freedom

## Galatians 5:1

Christ set us free so we could live in freedom.

Stand firm.

Do not let anyone put a slave’s yoke on you again.

**Help with these words**

A yoke is a bar used to make animals pull a load.

Paul uses it as a picture of being forced back into slavery.

## Galatians 5:2

Listen to me, Paul.

If you accept circumcision to be right with God, Christ will be of no help to you.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision is a mark made by removing the foreskin of the penis.

Some teachers said Gentile believers needed this mark to belong to God.

## Galatians 5:3

I say it again.

Every man who accepts circumcision on those terms must keep the whole law.

## Galatians 5:4

Some of you are relying on the law to put you right with God.

You have cut yourselves off from Christ.

You have turned away from God’s grace.

## Galatians 5:5

Through the Spirit, we trust God and wait with hope.

We look forward to God declaring us right with him.

## Galatians 5:6

When you belong to Christ Jesus, circumcision makes no difference.

What matters is trusting Christ and showing that trust through love.

**Pause and answer**

How can you show your trust in Christ?

## Who got in your way?

## Galatians 5:7

You were running a good race.

Who got in your way and stopped you from obeying the truth?

## Galatians 5:8

God called you.

He did not persuade you to turn aside.

## Galatians 5:9

A little yeast spreads through a whole batch of dough.

![Yeast and dough](../pictures/yeast-and-dough.svg)

**Help with these words**

Yeast makes dough rise.

Paul compares a teaching that spreads among people to yeast spreading through dough.

## Galatians 5:10

I trust the Lord that you will come to agree with me.

Whoever is upsetting you will answer to God.

## Galatians 5:11

Brothers and sisters, some say I still preach circumcision.

Then why do people still attack me?

If I taught that, my message about the cross would no longer offend them.

## Galatians 5:12

Those people are upsetting you about circumcision.

I wish they would go the whole way and have themselves castrated!

**Help with these words**

Paul uses angry words about the teachers.

Castration means removing the testicles.

**Pause and answer**

In Paul’s picture, what happens to a little yeast in dough?

## Use your freedom to serve

## Galatians 5:13

Brothers and sisters, God called you to be free.

Do not use that freedom to follow selfish desires.

Use it to serve each other with love.

## Galatians 5:14

One command brings the whole law to its goal.

Love your neighbor as you love yourself.

## Galatians 5:15

Watch out if you keep biting and devouring each other.

You could end up destroying each other.

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures animals hurting each other with their teeth.

He warns about the harm people do by fighting.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask believers to do with their freedom?

## Let the Spirit guide you

## Galatians 5:16

Let God’s Spirit guide the way you live.

Then you will not give in to your selfish desires.

## Galatians 5:17

Those desires pull you away from what the Spirit wants.

The Spirit wants what goes against those desires.

This struggle keeps you from simply doing whatever you want.

## Galatians 5:18

When the Spirit leads you, the law no longer rules you.

## Galatians 5:19

You can see what happens when people follow those desires.

They misuse sex, do impure things, and give harmful desires free rein.

## Galatians 5:20

They worship idols and practice magic.

They hate, quarrel, grow jealous, and burst out in anger.

They compete for power, divide people, and form rival groups.

## Galatians 5:21

They envy others, get drunk, and hold wild sexual parties.

I warn you again about these things and others like them.

People who keep living this way will not inherit God’s kingdom.

**Pause and answer**

Who can guide the way believers live?

## The fruit the Spirit grows

## Galatians 5:22

The Spirit grows fruit in our lives.

That fruit includes love, joy, and peace.

The Spirit helps us be patient, kind, good, and faithful.

**Help with these words**

Fruit grows on a plant.

Here, fruit means the good changes the Spirit brings about in people.

## Galatians 5:23

The Spirit also helps us be gentle and practice self-control.

No law forbids any of these things.

## Galatians 5:24

People who belong to Christ Jesus have nailed their old selfish ways to the cross.

That includes the strong desires that once ruled them.

## Galatians 5:25

The Spirit gives us life.

So let us follow where the Spirit leads.

## Galatians 5:26

Let us guard against pride.

Let us stop provoking and envying each other.

**Pause and answer**

Name one kind of fruit Paul says the Spirit grows in a person’s life.
