# Galatians 1 — Paul tells how he received the good news

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to several churches in Galatia.

He tells them why they can trust the message he brought.

## Paul greets the churches

## Galatians 1:1

I am Paul, an apostle.

No person chose me or sent me.

Jesus Christ and God the Father sent me.

God the Father raised Jesus from the dead.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## Galatians 1:2

All the believers here with me join in sending this letter.

We write to the churches in Galatia.

## Galatians 1:3

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**Help with these words**

Grace is kindness God gives freely.

## Galatians 1:4

Jesus gave himself for our sins.

He did this to rescue us from the evil of this present age.

This was what our God and Father wanted.

## Galatians 1:5

May God receive glory forever and ever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who sent Paul?

## One good news about Christ

## Galatians 1:6

God called you through the grace of Christ.

I am amazed that you are leaving God so quickly.

You are turning to what sounds like different good news.

## Galatians 1:7

There is no other good news.

Some people are upsetting you.

They are trying to twist the good news about Christ.

## Galatians 1:8

Suppose we ourselves taught something against the good news we brought you.

Suppose even an angel from heaven taught it.

Let that messenger be under God’s curse!

## Galatians 1:9

We said it before, and I say it again.

You received the good news.

If anyone teaches a message against it, let that person be under God’s curse!

## Galatians 1:10

Whose approval do I want, God’s or people’s?

If pleasing people were still my aim, I would no longer be Christ’s servant.

**Pause and answer**

What are some people trying to do to the good news?

## How Paul received the message

## Galatians 1:11

Brothers and sisters, I want you to know something.

No one made up the good news I told you.

## Galatians 1:12

No human teacher gave me that message.

Jesus Christ himself made it known to me.

## Galatians 1:13

You have heard how I once practiced my Jewish faith.

I treated God’s church cruelly.

I tried to destroy it.

## Galatians 1:14

I advanced beyond many Jewish people my own age.

I cared deeply about keeping the customs my ancestors passed down.

## Galatians 1:15

God had already chosen me before my birth.

In his kindness, God called me.

## Galatians 1:16

God chose to reveal his Son in me.

He wanted me to tell the Gentiles about Jesus.

I did not rush to ask anyone for advice.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Galatians 1:17

I did not go to Jerusalem to see the apostles who came before me.

I went to Arabia.

Later I returned to Damascus.

![Paul travels to Arabia and later returns to Damascus.](../pictures/paul-arabia-damascus.svg)

These are the places Paul names when he explains his early travels.

**Pause and answer**

Who made the good news known to Paul?

## The churches hear about Paul

## Galatians 1:18

Three years later I went to Jerusalem to visit Peter, also called Cephas.

I stayed with Peter for fifteen days.

## Galatians 1:19

The only other apostle I saw was James, the Lord’s brother.

## Galatians 1:20

God knows I am telling you the truth in this letter.

## Galatians 1:21

Later I went to Syria and Cilicia.

## Galatians 1:22

The churches in Judea that belonged to Christ had never met me.

## Galatians 1:23

They had only heard this report about me.

“The man who once attacked us now teaches the very faith he tried to destroy.”

## Galatians 1:24

So they praised God because of me.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the churches in Judea praise God?
