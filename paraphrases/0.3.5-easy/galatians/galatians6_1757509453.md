# Galatians 6 — Help each other and keep doing good

**About this chapter**

Paul explains how to help someone who has done wrong.

Then he takes up the pen himself to finish his letter.

## Help each other gently

## Galatians 6:1

Brothers and sisters, someone among you may do wrong.

Those who follow the Spirit should gently help that person turn back.

Watch yourselves too.

You could be tempted as well.

## Galatians 6:2

Help each other carry heavy burdens.

This is how you obey Christ’s law.

## Galatians 6:3

A person can think they are important when they are not.

Then they fool themselves.

## Galatians 6:4

Look closely at your own work.

Then you can take pride in doing it well without comparing yourself with anyone else.

## Galatians 6:5

Each person has their own load to carry.

**Help with these words**

In verse 2, a heavy burden pictures trouble someone needs help with.

Here, carrying your own load means taking responsibility for what you do.

**Pause and answer**

How should believers help someone who has done wrong?

## Planting and gathering a crop

## Galatians 6:6

If someone teaches you God’s word, share the good things you have with your teacher.

## Galatians 6:7

Do not fool yourselves.

No one makes a fool of God.

People gather the crop that grows from the seeds they plant.

![Ripe grain on a stalk](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Galatians 6:8

Live to please your selfish desires, and those desires will bring destruction.

Live to please the Spirit, and the Spirit will give you life forever.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses planting as a picture of the choices people make.

The harvest pictures what comes from those choices.

## Galatians 6:9

Let us keep doing good even when we feel tired.

If we keep going, the harvest will come at the right time.

## Galatians 6:10

Whenever we have the chance, let us do good to everyone.

Give special care to those who share our faith.

**Pause and answer**

Who should receive our help when we have the chance?

## Paul writes in his own hand

## Galatians 6:11

Look at the large letters I am making!

I am writing this to you with my own hand.

## Galatians 6:12

Some people want to impress others.

They try to force you to accept circumcision.

They want to escape the trouble that comes from teaching about Christ’s cross.

## Galatians 6:13

Those men accept circumcision but fail to keep the law themselves.

They want the mark of circumcision on your bodies so they can boast about you.

## Galatians 6:14

May I boast only about the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Because of that cross, the world is dead to me, and I am dead to the world.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses death as a picture here.

Because Jesus died, winning the world’s approval no longer rules Paul’s life.

## Galatians 6:15

Whether someone has received circumcision makes no difference.

What matters is that God makes a person new.

**Pause and answer**

What alone does Paul want to boast about?

## Paul’s closing prayer

## Galatians 6:16

May peace and mercy come to all who live this way, to the Israel of God.

## Galatians 6:17

Please cause me no more trouble.

My body bears marks that show I belong to Jesus.

## Galatians 6:18

Brothers and sisters, may the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.

Amen.
