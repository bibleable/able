# Nahum 3 — No one comes to Nineveh’s aid

**About this chapter**

Nahum speaks to Nineveh, the capital of Assyria. He reminds the city how it treated others and asks who will comfort it when it falls.

## Violence in Nineveh

## Nahum 3:1

Trouble is coming to Nineveh, a city full of bloodshed.

Its people lie and take what belongs to others.

They always find someone else to rob.

## Nahum 3:2

Listen! Whips crack and wheels rumble.

Horses gallop and chariots bounce along.

## Nahum 3:3

Riders charge past.

Swords flash and spears shine.

So many people lie dead that others stumble over their bodies.

## Nahum 3:4

Nahum compares Nineveh to a woman who sells sex and uses spells.

The city attracts nations and families, then betrays them.

## The LORD speaks to the city

## Nahum 3:5

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies says, “Nineveh, I am against you.

I will pull your skirts up over your face.

Every kingdom will see you naked and ashamed.”

## Nahum 3:6

The LORD is describing the city’s public disgrace.

“I will throw filth at you.

I will treat you with scorn while everyone watches.

## Nahum 3:7

“People will shrink back when they see you.

They will say, ‘Nineveh lies in ruins. Who will weep for her?’

Where can I find anyone to comfort you?”

## Remember what happened to Thebes

## Nahum 3:8

Nineveh, are you any better than Thebes?

Thebes was a city beside the Nile River.

The water around it protected it like a wall.

## Nahum 3:9

Cush and Egypt gave Thebes strength that seemed to have no end.

Put and Libya were its friends.

## Nahum 3:10

Yet enemies took the people of Thebes away as prisoners.

They killed its babies at the street corners.

They drew lots to decide who would get each captured leader.

They put all its nobles in chains.

## Nahum 3:11

Nineveh, you too will stagger like someone drunk.

You will hide and look for shelter from your enemies.

## The city’s defenses give way

## Nahum 3:12

Your forts are like fig trees bearing their first ripe figs.

Shake the tree and the fruit drops into your mouth.

Your forts will fall just as easily.

## Nahum 3:13

Nahum mocks Nineveh’s soldiers by comparing them to women.

The gates of your land stand wide open to your enemies.

Fire burns the bars that held them shut.

## Nahum 3:14

Store water before the enemy surrounds you.

Make your forts strong.

Mix the clay and tread the mortar underfoot.

Repair the oven used for baking bricks.

## Nahum 3:15

Even there, fire will burn you up.

The sword will cut you down.

You will be eaten up as crops are eaten by locusts.

Go ahead, gather as many people as a swarm of locusts!

## The leaders disappear

## Nahum 3:16

You have more traders than there are stars in the sky.

But a locust eats everything and then flies away.

## Nahum 3:17

Your guards and officials are like swarms of locusts.

Those insects settle on walls on a cold day.

When the sun comes up, they fly away.

No one knows where they have gone.

## Nahum 3:18

King of Assyria, your leaders are like sleeping shepherds.

Your officers are asleep.

Your people scatter across the mountains.

No one gathers them together.

## Nahum 3:19

Your wound is too deep to heal.

Everyone who hears that you have fallen claps their hands.

Who has escaped your unending cruelty?

**Pause and answer**

Why do people clap when they hear that Nineveh has fallen?
