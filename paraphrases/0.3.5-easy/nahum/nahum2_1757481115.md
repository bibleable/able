# Nahum 2 — The people flee from Nineveh

**About this chapter**

Nahum describes the fall of Nineveh. The city once took treasure from other people. Now an army comes to take Nineveh’s treasure.

## An army comes to Nineveh

## Nahum 2:1

Nineveh, an enemy is coming to scatter your people.

Guard your fort!

Watch the road!

Get ready. You will need all your strength.

## Nahum 2:2

Enemies laid waste to the land of Jacob’s people, Israel.

They stripped the branches from Israel’s vines.

But the LORD will make His people glorious again.

## Nahum 2:3

The approaching soldiers carry red shields.

Their clothes are bright red.

As they get ready, the metal on their chariots flashes like fire.

The soldiers wave spears made of cypress wood.

## Nahum 2:4

Chariots are carts that horses pull in battle.

They race through the streets and across the city squares.

They shine like burning torches.

They move as fast as lightning.

## People run from the city

## Nahum 2:5

The commander calls his officers.

They stumble as they hurry forward.

They race to the city wall.

A large shield goes up for cover.

## Nahum 2:6

The gates by the river swing open.

The palace falls.

## Nahum 2:7

The people of Nineveh must leave.

Their enemies will take them away.

The servant women make low cries like doves.

They beat their chests in grief.

## Nahum 2:8

Nineveh used to be like a full pool.

Now its people rush away like water draining out.

People shout, “Stop! Stop!”

No one turns back.

## Nahum 2:9

“Take the silver! Take the gold!”

The city has more treasure than anyone can count.

There are precious things of every kind.

![Silver coins, one kind of treasure.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

## Nahum 2:10

Now Nineveh lies empty and ruined.

The people lose heart.

Their knees knock together.

They shake with fear, and their faces turn pale.

## Where is the lions’ den?

## Nahum 2:11

Nineveh was once like a lions’ den.

Where is that den now?

Where did the young lions feed?

The lions and their cubs once roamed there without fear.

## Nahum 2:12

The lion caught and killed animals for its cubs.

It brought food to the lioness.

Its den was full of the animals it had killed.

Like that lion, Nineveh took what it wanted from others.

## Nahum 2:13

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies says, “Nineveh, I am against you.

I will burn your chariots to ashes.

Your young lions will die by the sword.

I will leave you nothing more to hunt.

No one will hear your messengers again.”
