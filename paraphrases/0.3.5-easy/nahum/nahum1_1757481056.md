# Nahum 1 — A message of peace for Judah

**About this chapter**

Nahum speaks about Nineveh and Judah. The LORD will put an end to Nineveh’s power. The people of Judah will hear news of peace.

## Nahum’s vision about Nineveh

## Nahum 1:1

Nahum came from Elkosh.

This book tells what he saw in a vision.

It is a message against the city of Nineveh.

## Nahum 1:2

The LORD wants His people to be faithful to Him alone.

He is fiercely angry with His enemies.

He will pay them back for the harm they have done.

## Nahum 1:3

The LORD is slow to get angry.

His power is great.

He will punish those who are guilty.

He walks through storms and twisting winds.

The clouds are like dust beneath His feet.

## The earth shakes before the LORD

## Nahum 1:4

He commands the sea, and it dries up.

He makes all the rivers run dry.

Plants in Bashan and Carmel shrivel.

Even the flowers of Lebanon droop.

## Nahum 1:5

The mountains shake before Him.

The hills melt away.

When He comes, the whole earth trembles.

So does everyone who lives on it.

## Nahum 1:6

Who can stand against His anger?

Who can bear it when His anger burns?

It pours out like fire.

Even rocks break apart before Him.

## Safety for those who trust the LORD

## Nahum 1:7

The LORD is good.

When trouble comes, He is like a strong fort.

He looks after the people who trust Him.

## Nahum 1:8

But a great flood will sweep Nineveh away.

The LORD will chase His enemies into the darkness.

## Nahum 1:9

What are you planning against the LORD?

He will put an end to it.

This trouble will not come a second time.

## Nahum 1:10

His enemies will be like thorn bushes tangled together.

They will disappear like wine gulped down by a drunk person.

They will burn up like dry stalks left after harvest.

## Nahum 1:11

Nineveh, someone has come from your city who plots against the LORD.

He urges others to do evil.

## The LORD promises to free Judah

## Nahum 1:12

The LORD says, “Your enemies are many and have joined forces.

Even so, they will fall and pass away.

Judah, I have brought you trouble.

I will not bring you trouble any longer.

## Nahum 1:13

“Your enemies have ruled over you.

It is as if they put a heavy yoke on your neck.

Now I will break that yoke.

I will tear off your chains.”

## Nahum 1:14

The LORD has given this order about Nineveh.

“No children will be left to carry on your name.

I will remove the idols from the temple of your gods.

Some are carved, and some are made of poured metal.

I will prepare your grave because your ways are shameful.”

## Nahum 1:15

Look! A messenger is coming over the mountains.

He brings good news of peace.

People of Judah, celebrate your feasts.

Keep the promises you made to God.

The wicked enemy will never march through your land again.

That enemy will be gone for good.
