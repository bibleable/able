# Acts 28 — Paul reaches Rome and welcomes visitors

**About this chapter**

The people of Malta care for the travellers after the shipwreck.

When Paul reaches Rome, people come to hear him speak.

## The people of Malta welcome everyone

## Acts 28:1

We had all reached land safely.

Then we learned the island was called Malta.

**Help with these words**

The writer says “we” because he was travelling with Paul.

## Acts 28:2

The people of Malta were especially kind to us.

Rain was falling, and it was cold.

They lit a fire and welcomed us all.

## Acts 28:3

Paul gathered sticks and put them on the fire.

The heat drove a snake out of the sticks.

It bit Paul and hung on to his hand.

## Acts 28:4

The islanders saw the snake hanging from Paul’s hand.

They told each other, “This man must have killed someone.

He escaped the sea, but Justice will not let him live.”

**Help with these words**

The islanders thought the snake had come to punish Paul.

## Acts 28:5

Paul shook the snake off into the fire.

The bite did him no harm.

## Acts 28:6

The people expected Paul to swell up or fall down dead.

They waited a long time.

When nothing happened, they changed their minds.

They said Paul was a god.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people see after the snake bit Paul?

## Paul visits a sick man

## Acts 28:7

Publius was the chief official of Malta.

He owned land near the place where we had come ashore.

He welcomed us and cared for us for three days.

## Acts 28:8

His father lay sick in bed.

He had a fever and a serious illness in his bowels.

Paul went in to see him.

Paul prayed and placed his hands on him.

The man got well.

## Acts 28:9

Then the other sick people on Malta came.

They got well too.

## Acts 28:10

The islanders showed us respect in many ways.

When we were ready to sail, they gave us what we needed.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul do when he visited the father of Publius?

## Paul travels on to Rome

## Acts 28:11

After three months we sailed on a ship from Alexandria.

It had stayed at Malta for the winter.

On the front of the ship was a carving of the Twin Brothers.

## Acts 28:12

We landed at Syracuse and stayed there three days.

## Acts 28:13

We lifted the anchor and sailed to Rhegium.

A day later a wind began blowing from the south.

On the second day we reached Puteoli.

## Acts 28:14

We found believers there who invited us to stay a week.

Then we went on towards Rome.

## Acts 28:15

The believers in Rome heard we were coming.

Some travelled as far as the Forum of Appius and the Three Taverns to meet us.

Seeing them gave Paul courage.

He thanked God.

## Acts 28:16

When we reached Rome, Paul could stay in a place of his own.

A soldier stayed there to guard him.

**Pause and answer**

How did Paul feel when the believers came to meet him?

## Paul explains why he is a prisoner

## Acts 28:17

Three days later Paul invited the Jewish leaders to meet him.

He said, “Brothers, I have done nothing against our people or our ancestors’ customs.

Yet people arrested me in Jerusalem and handed me over to the Romans.

## Acts 28:18

The Romans questioned me and wanted to set me free.

They found no reason to put me to death.

## Acts 28:19

But the Jewish leaders objected.

So I had to ask Caesar to hear my case.

I have no charge to bring against my own people.

## Acts 28:20

That is why I asked to see you and speak with you.

I wear this chain because I share the hope of Israel.”

**Help with these words**

Caesar was the Roman emperor. Earlier Paul explained his hope that God would raise the dead.

## Acts 28:21

The leaders answered, “No one has sent us letters about you from Judea.

No one coming from there has said anything bad about you.

## Acts 28:22

We want to hear what you think.

We know people everywhere speak against the group you belong to.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the Romans want to set Paul free?

## Paul speaks about Jesus from the Scriptures

## Acts 28:23

They chose a day to meet again.

Many people came to the place where Paul was staying.

From morning until evening Paul told them about God’s kingdom.

He read from the law of Moses and the writings of the prophets to show them why they could trust Jesus.

![A scroll opened to show lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God’s rule over his people. The law of Moses and the writings of the prophets are parts of the Jewish Scriptures.

## Acts 28:24

Some people believed what Paul said.

Others refused to believe him.

## Acts 28:25

They disagreed with each other.

They began leaving after Paul said this last thing.

“The Holy Spirit spoke truly to your ancestors through the prophet Isaiah.

## Acts 28:26

He told Isaiah, ‘Go and speak to these people.

Tell them they will keep hearing but never understand.

They will keep looking but never see what it means.

## Acts 28:27

Their hearts have grown hard.

They hardly listen, and they have closed their eyes.

Otherwise they might see, hear and understand.

They might turn to me, and I would heal them.’

**Help with these words**

A hard heart is a word picture. It means a person refuses to listen to God or change.

## Acts 28:28

So I want you to know this.

God has sent the Gentiles the message about how he saves people.

They will listen!”

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

**Pause and answer**

How did people respond when Paul spoke about Jesus?

## Acts 28:29

**About this verse number**

In the Berean Standard Bible, the reading continues at verse 30.

## Paul keeps teaching in Rome

## Acts 28:30

Paul lived in a rented house for two full years.

He welcomed everyone who came to see him.

## Acts 28:31

Paul spoke boldly about God’s kingdom.

He taught people about the Lord Jesus Christ.

No one stopped him.

Who could come to Paul’s house to hear him teach?
