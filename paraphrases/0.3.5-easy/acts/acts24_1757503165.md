# Acts 24 — Paul answers the charges before Governor Felix

**About this chapter**

A lawyer accuses Paul of starting trouble.

Governor Felix hears the lawyer and then listens to Paul.

## A lawyer speaks against Paul

## Acts 24:1

Five days later, Ananias the high priest came to see Governor Felix.

Some elders and a lawyer named Tertullus came too.

Tertullus spoke for them against Paul.

## Acts 24:2

Felix called Paul in.

Tertullus began, “Governor, because of you we have had peace for a long time.

You have planned ahead and made life better for our people.

## Acts 24:3

Wherever we go, Governor Felix, we thank you for all this.

## Acts 24:4

I will take little of your time.

Please be kind enough to hear us.

## Acts 24:5

This man Paul causes trouble wherever he goes.

He turns Jewish people against each other all over the world.

He leads a group called the Nazarenes.

**Help with these words**

Tertullus used the name Nazarenes for people who followed Jesus of Nazareth.

## Acts 24:6

He even tried to make the temple unholy.

So we took hold of him.

## Acts 24:7

**About this verse number**

In the Berean Standard Bible, the reading continues at verse 8.

## Acts 24:8

Question Paul yourself.

Then you can find out whether our charges are true.”

## Acts 24:9

The Jewish leaders who came with Tertullus agreed.

They said his charges were true.

**Pause and answer**

What did Tertullus ask Felix to do?

## Paul tells Felix why he came to Jerusalem

## Acts 24:10

Felix signalled to Paul to speak.

Paul said, “You have judged this nation for many years.

So I am glad to answer these charges before you.

## Acts 24:11

I went to Jerusalem to worship no more than twelve days ago.

You can check this for yourself.

## Acts 24:12

My accusers never saw me arguing with anyone in the temple.

They never saw me stirring up a crowd in the synagogues or anywhere in the city.

**Help with these words**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet to worship.

## Acts 24:13

They cannot prove any of these charges against me.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul say he had gone to Jerusalem?

## Paul explains his hope in God

## Acts 24:14

Paul continued, “I do worship the God of our ancestors.

I follow the Way, which my accusers call a separate group.

I believe everything in the Law and the Prophets.

**Help with these words**

The Way was a name for following Jesus. The Law and the Prophets are parts of the Jewish Scriptures.

## Acts 24:15

I have the same hope in God as my accusers.

God will raise the dead to life.

He will raise both those who do right and those who do evil.

## Acts 24:16

Because of this hope, I always try to keep a clear conscience before God and people.”

**Help with these words**

A clear conscience means knowing of no wrong you have done that you still need to put right.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Paul say God would raise to life?

## Paul asks what the witnesses saw

## Acts 24:17

Paul said, “I had been away from Jerusalem for several years.

I returned with gifts to help my people in need.

I also came to give offerings to God.

## Acts 24:18

When they found me in the temple, I had followed the ceremony to be clean for worship.

I was not gathering a crowd or starting trouble.

Some Jewish people from the province of Asia were there.

## Acts 24:19

If they have a charge against me, they should come and tell you themselves.

## Acts 24:20

Or let the men here tell you what crime they found when I stood before the Jewish council.

## Acts 24:21

Perhaps they mean the one thing I called out there.

I said, ‘I am on trial today because I believe the dead will rise.’”

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul bring for people in need?

## Felix puts off his decision

## Acts 24:22

Felix already knew a great deal about the Way.

He stopped the hearing and said, “I will decide your case when Commander Lysias comes.”

## Acts 24:23

Felix ordered a Roman officer to keep Paul under guard.

Paul could have some freedom.

His friends could visit and bring what he needed.

**Pause and answer**

Who could come and help Paul while he was under guard?

## Felix listens to Paul again

## Acts 24:24

Several days later Felix came back with his wife Drusilla.

Drusilla was Jewish.

Felix sent for Paul and listened to him talk about trusting Jesus Christ.

## Acts 24:25

Paul spoke about doing right and controlling your actions.

He spoke about the day when God will judge people.

Felix grew afraid.

He said, “You may go now.

When I have time, I will send for you.”

## Acts 24:26

Felix also hoped Paul would offer him a bribe.

So he often sent for Paul and talked with him.

**Help with these words**

A bribe is money someone offers to get an official to do them a favour.

## Acts 24:27

Two years passed.

Porcius Festus became governor in place of Felix.

Felix wanted to please the Jewish leaders.

So he left Paul in prison.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Felix leave Paul in prison when Festus became governor?
