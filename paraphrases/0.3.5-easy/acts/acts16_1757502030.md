# Acts 16 — Lydia and a prison guard hear about Jesus

**About this chapter**

Paul and his companions travel to Philippi.

There they share the good news beside a river and in a prison.

## Timothy travels with Paul

## Acts 16:1

Paul went to Derbe and then to Lystra.

In Lystra he met Timothy, a follower of Jesus.

Timothy’s mother was Jewish and believed in Jesus.

His father was Greek.

## Acts 16:2

The believers in Lystra and Iconium spoke well of Timothy.

## Acts 16:3

Paul wanted Timothy to travel with him.

The Jewish people there all knew Timothy’s father was Greek.

Because of this, Paul circumcised Timothy.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision means removing a small piece of skin from a boy’s or man’s penis. For Jewish men, it was a sign of their people’s agreement with God.

## Acts 16:4

Paul and his companions went from town to town.

They told the believers what the Jerusalem apostles and elders had decided.

They asked the believers to follow those decisions.

**Help with these words**

The apostles were men Jesus sent to speak for him. The elders were leaders who cared for the believers.

## Acts 16:5

So the believers grew stronger in their faith.

More people joined the churches every day.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Paul invite to travel with him?

## A man asks Paul for help

## Acts 16:6

The Holy Spirit kept them from teaching God’s message in the province of Asia.

So they travelled through Phrygia and Galatia.

## Acts 16:7

At the border of Mysia they tried to enter Bithynia.

But the Spirit of Jesus did not let them go there.

## Acts 16:8

So they passed by Mysia and went down to Troas.

## Acts 16:9

During the night Paul had a vision.

He saw a man from Macedonia standing there.

The man begged him, “Come over to Macedonia and help us!”

## Acts 16:10

As soon as Paul saw the vision, we got ready to leave.

We understood that God wanted us to go to Macedonia.

We would tell the people there the good news.

**Help with these words**

The writer now says “we.” He includes himself among the people travelling with Paul.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the man in Paul’s vision ask him to go?

## Lydia listens beside the river

## Acts 16:11

We sailed from Troas straight to Samothrace.

The next day we sailed to Neapolis.

## Acts 16:12

From there we went to Philippi, a Roman colony.

It was the leading city in that part of Macedonia.

We stayed for several days.

**Help with these words**

A Roman colony was a place governed by Rome under Roman laws.

## Acts 16:13

On the Sabbath we went outside the city gate.

We went to the river, where people usually gathered to pray.

We sat down and spoke to the women there.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## Acts 16:14

Lydia was one of the women listening.

She came from Thyatira and sold purple cloth.

She worshipped God.

The Lord opened her heart to Paul’s message.

**Help with these words**

Opening Lydia’s heart means helping her listen and accept the message.

![A folded length of purple cloth like the cloth Lydia sold.](../pictures/purple-cloth.svg)

## Acts 16:15

Lydia and everyone in her home received baptism.

Then Lydia said, “If you believe I trust the Lord, come and stay at my house.”

She persuaded us to stay.

**Help with these words**

In baptism, believers use water to show that someone belongs to Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Who invited Paul and his companions to stay at her house?

## Paul speaks to a spirit

## Acts 16:16

One day we were going to the place of prayer.

An enslaved girl met us.

A spirit gave her power to tell people their future.

Her owners made a lot of money from this.

## Acts 16:17

The girl followed Paul and the rest of us.

She shouted, “These men serve the Most High God!

They tell you how God can save you!”

## Acts 16:18

She did this for many days.

At last Paul became very upset.

He turned and spoke to the spirit.

“In the name of Jesus Christ, I order you to leave her!”

At that moment the spirit left the girl.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Paul order to leave the girl?

## Paul and Silas go to prison

## Acts 16:19

The girl’s owners saw they could no longer earn money this way.

They grabbed Paul and Silas.

They dragged them to the officials in the marketplace.

## Acts 16:20

They told the city leaders, “These men are Jews.

They are causing trouble in our city.

## Acts 16:21

They teach customs that our Roman laws forbid us to follow.”

## Acts 16:22

The crowd joined the attack on Paul and Silas.

The city leaders ordered men to strip them and beat them with rods.

## Acts 16:23

The men beat Paul and Silas many times.

Then they threw them into prison.

They ordered the prison guard to keep them safe from escape.

## Acts 16:24

The guard put them in a cell deep inside the prison.

He locked their feet between heavy pieces of wood.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the guard put Paul and Silas?

## The prison doors open

## Acts 16:25

About midnight Paul and Silas prayed and sang songs to God.

The other prisoners listened.

## Acts 16:26

Suddenly a strong earthquake shook the whole prison, down to its base.

All the doors flew open.

Everyone’s chains came loose.

## Acts 16:27

The guard woke up and saw the open doors.

He thought the prisoners had escaped.

He drew his sword and was about to kill himself.

## Acts 16:28

Paul shouted, “Do not hurt yourself!

We are all here!”

## Acts 16:29

The guard called for lights and rushed inside.

He fell down trembling in front of Paul and Silas.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul tell the guard about the prisoners?

## The guard welcomes Paul and Silas

## Acts 16:30

The guard brought Paul and Silas out.

He asked, “Sirs, what must I do for God to save me?”

## Acts 16:31

They answered, “Believe in the Lord Jesus and he will save you.

This promise is for everyone in your home too.”

## Acts 16:32

They told the guard the Lord’s message.

They told everyone in his home too.

## Acts 16:33

At that same hour of the night, the guard washed their wounds.

Right away the guard and everyone in his home received baptism.

## Acts 16:34

Then he brought Paul and Silas into his house.

He gave them a meal.

The guard and his whole household were full of joy because they had come to trust God.

**Pause and answer**

How did the guard care for the men’s wounds?

## The city leaders come to the prison

## Acts 16:35

At daybreak the city leaders sent officers to the guard.

Their order was, “Set those men free.”

## Acts 16:36

The guard told Paul, “The city leaders have ordered your release.

You may go in peace.”

## Acts 16:37

Paul told the officers, “We are Roman citizens.

They beat us in public without a trial.

They threw us into prison.

Now they want to send us away in secret?

No!

Let the leaders come and lead us out themselves.”

**Help with these words**

In a trial, someone in authority hears the case before deciding whether a person is guilty.

## Acts 16:38

The officers took Paul’s message to the leaders.

The leaders were afraid when they heard both men were Roman citizens.

## Acts 16:39

The leaders came and tried to make peace with them.

They led Paul and Silas out of prison.

They asked the two men to leave the city.

## Acts 16:40

Paul and Silas went to Lydia’s house.

They met with the believers there and encouraged them.

Then Paul and Silas left Philippi.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul and Silas visit Lydia’s house before they left?
