# Acts 12 — The Lord brings Peter out of prison

**About this chapter**

King Herod locks Peter in prison.

While the believers pray, an angel comes to Peter.

## Herod puts Peter in prison

## Acts 12:1

About that time, King Herod began to hurt some believers.

## Acts 12:2

Herod ordered men to kill James with a sword.

James was John’s brother.

## Acts 12:3

Herod saw that this pleased some Jewish people.

So he arrested Peter too.

This happened during the Feast of Unleavened Bread.

**Help with these words**

At this feast, Jewish families ate bread made without yeast. They remembered how God brought their people out of Egypt.

## Acts 12:4

Herod put Peter in prison.

Four groups of soldiers guarded him.

Each group had four soldiers.

After Passover, Herod planned to bring Peter before the people.

**Help with these words**

Passover is the special meal at the start of this feast.

## Acts 12:5

The soldiers kept Peter in prison.

The believers kept praying to God for Peter with all their hearts.

**Pause and answer**

What did the believers do while Peter was in prison?

## An angel wakes Peter

## Acts 12:6

Herod planned to put Peter on trial the next day.

That night Peter slept between two soldiers.

Two chains held him.

Other soldiers guarded the prison entrance.

## Acts 12:7

Suddenly an angel from the Lord stood there.

A light shone in the prison cell.

The angel tapped Peter’s side and woke him.

“Get up quickly,” the angel said.

The chains fell off Peter’s wrists.

## Acts 12:8

The angel said, “Get dressed and put on your sandals.”

Peter did so.

Then the angel said, “Wrap your cloak around you.

Follow me.”

**Help with these words**

A cloak is a loose piece of clothing. People wrap it around their shoulders.

![A cloak like the one Peter wrapped around himself.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

## Acts 12:9

Peter followed the angel out.

Peter thought he was seeing a vision.

He did not yet know this was really happening.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the chains on Peter’s wrists?

## Peter reaches the street

## Acts 12:10

Peter and the angel passed the first guard.

Then they passed the second guard.

They reached the iron gate leading into the city.

It opened by itself.

They went out and walked the length of one street block.

Suddenly the angel left Peter.

## Acts 12:11

Then Peter understood.

He said, “Now I know the Lord sent his angel.

The Lord rescued me from Herod.

He saved me from everything the Jewish people expected would happen to me.”

**Pause and answer**

How did the iron gate open?

## Rhoda hears Peter at the gate

## Acts 12:12

Peter went to Mary’s house.

Mary was the mother of John, also called Mark.

Many people had gathered there to pray.

## Acts 12:13

Peter knocked at the outer gate.

A young servant named Rhoda came to answer.

## Acts 12:14

Rhoda knew Peter’s voice.

She was so happy she forgot to open the gate.

She ran inside and said, “Peter is at the gate!”

## Acts 12:15

The people told Rhoda, “You have lost your mind!”

Rhoda kept saying that Peter was there.

They said, “It must be his angel.”

## Acts 12:16

Peter kept knocking.

At last they opened the gate and saw him.

They were amazed.

## Acts 12:17

Peter raised his hand to ask them to be quiet.

He told them how the Lord brought him out of prison.

He said, “Tell James and the other believers.”

Then Peter left for another place.

**Help with these words**

This James was a different man from John’s brother James.

**Pause and answer**

Whose voice did Rhoda hear at the gate?

## Herod searches for Peter

## Acts 12:18

At dawn the soldiers were upset and confused.

They could not explain what had happened to Peter.

## Acts 12:19

Herod searched for Peter and could not find him.

He questioned the guards.

Then he ordered men to kill the guards.

Herod left Judea and stayed in Caesarea for a while.

**Pause and answer**

What did Herod do before he gave the order to kill the guards?

## The people ask Herod for peace

## Acts 12:20

Herod was very angry with the people of Tyre and Sidon.

Their towns depended on Herod’s country for food.

Together they went to see him.

Blastus, who looked after the king’s rooms, agreed to help them.

Then they asked Herod for peace.

## Acts 12:21

On the chosen day Herod put on his royal robes.

He sat on his throne and spoke to the people.

## Acts 12:22

The people shouted, “A god is speaking!

No human being has a voice like that!”

## Acts 12:23

Herod did not give God the praise.

So an angel of the Lord struck Herod down at once.

Worms ate him, and he died.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people of Tyre and Sidon want peace?

## God’s message reaches more people

## Acts 12:24

People kept sharing God’s message.

More and more people heard it.

## Acts 12:25

Barnabas and Saul finished their work in Jerusalem.

On their return, they took John with them.

People also called him Mark.

**Pause and answer**

Who went with Barnabas and Saul?
