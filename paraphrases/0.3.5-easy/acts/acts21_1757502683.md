# Acts 21 — Paul goes to Jerusalem and asks to speak to the crowd

**About this chapter**

Paul’s friends warn him about going to Jerusalem.

In Jerusalem, a Roman commander finds Paul in the middle of an angry crowd.

## Friends in Tyre pray on the beach

## Acts 21:1

It was hard to leave our friends.

We sailed straight to Cos.

The next day we reached Rhodes, then went on to Patara.

**Help with these words**

The writer says “we” because he was travelling with Paul.

## Acts 21:2

At Patara we found a ship going to Phoenicia.

We went aboard and set sail.

## Acts 21:3

We saw Cyprus and sailed south of the island.

We continued to Syria and landed at Tyre.

The sailors needed to unload the goods there.

## Acts 21:4

We found the followers of Jesus in Tyre.

We stayed with them for seven days.

Through the Holy Spirit they kept telling Paul to stay away from Jerusalem.

## Acts 21:5

When it was time to leave, everyone walked with us out of the city.

The believers brought their wives and children too.

Together we knelt on the beach and prayed.

## Acts 21:6

We said goodbye and went back aboard the ship.

Our friends went home.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the families pray with Paul and his friends?

## Agabus gives Paul a warning

## Acts 21:7

We sailed from Tyre to Ptolemais.

We greeted the believers and stayed with them one day.

## Acts 21:8

The next day we went to Caesarea.

We stayed with Philip, who told people the good news.

He was one of the seven men chosen to help the church.

## Acts 21:9

Philip had four unmarried daughters who spoke messages from God.

## Acts 21:10

We stayed there several days.

Then a prophet named Agabus came from Judea.

## Acts 21:11

Agabus took Paul’s belt.

He tied his own feet and hands with it.

Agabus said, “The Holy Spirit says this will happen in Jerusalem.

Jewish people there will tie up the man who owns this belt.

They will hand him over to the Gentiles.”

![A belt like the one Agabus used when he gave his warning.](../pictures/pauls-belt.svg)

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish. Agabus used Paul’s belt to show what his warning meant.

## Acts 21:12

We heard the warning and begged Paul to stay away from Jerusalem.

The local believers begged him too.

## Acts 21:13

Paul asked, “Why are you crying and breaking my heart?

I am ready even if people tie me up.

I am ready to die in Jerusalem for the Lord Jesus.”

**Help with these words**

When Paul spoke of his heart breaking, he meant he felt deep sorrow.

## Acts 21:14

We could not change Paul’s mind.

So we stopped urging him to stay.

We said, “May the Lord do what he wants.”

**Pause and answer**

Whose belt did Agabus use to show what would happen?

## The believers welcome Paul to Jerusalem

## Acts 21:15

After that we packed our things and set out for Jerusalem.

## Acts 21:16

Some believers from Caesarea came with us.

They took us to stay with Mnason, a man from Cyprus.

Mnason had followed Jesus since the early days.

## Acts 21:17

When we reached Jerusalem, the believers welcomed us with joy.

## Acts 21:18

The next day we went with Paul to see James.

All the elders were there.

**Help with these words**

The elders were leaders who cared for the believers.

## Acts 21:19

Paul greeted them.

One by one he told them what God had done among the Gentiles through his work.

## Acts 21:20

They praised God when they heard this.

Then they told Paul, “Brother, thousands of Jewish people have believed in Jesus.

They all care deeply about keeping the law of Moses.

## Acts 21:21

They have heard a story about you.

They think you tell Jewish people living among Gentiles to turn away from Moses.

They think you tell them to stop circumcising their sons and following our customs.

## Acts 21:22

They are sure to hear you have come.

What should we do?”

**Help with these words**

Circumcision means removing a small piece of skin from a boy’s or man’s penis. For Jewish men, it was a sign of their people’s agreement with God.

**Pause and answer**

How did the leaders respond when Paul told them what God had done?

## Four men have made a promise

## Acts 21:23

The leaders said, “Here is what we ask you to do.

Four men here have made a special promise to God.

## Acts 21:24

Go through the ceremony of purification with these men.

Pay their expenses so they can have their heads shaved.

Then people will see those stories about you are false.

They will see that you follow the law yourself.

**Help with these words**

Purification was a ceremony to make a person ready to worship according to Jewish law.

## Acts 21:25

We have already written our decision to Gentile believers.

They must avoid food offered to idols.

They must avoid eating blood and meat from animals that people have strangled.

They must also avoid wrong sexual behaviour.”

## Acts 21:26

The next day Paul went through the ceremony with the men.

Then Paul went to the temple to announce when the ceremony would end.

At that time each man would have an offering made for him.

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders ask Paul to pay for?

## A crowd grabs Paul at the temple

## Acts 21:27

The seven days were nearly over.

Some Jewish people from the province of Asia saw Paul at the temple.

They stirred up the crowd and grabbed Paul.

## Acts 21:28

They shouted, “People of Israel, help us!

This man teaches people everywhere to oppose our people, our law and our temple.

He even brought Greeks into the temple.

He has made this holy place unclean!”

## Acts 21:29

Earlier, these people had seen Paul in the city with Trophimus from Ephesus.

They assumed Paul had taken Trophimus into the temple.

## Acts 21:30

The whole city was in an uproar.

People rushed together and dragged Paul out of the temple.

At once the gates closed.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the people actually seen Paul with Trophimus?

## Roman soldiers take Paul away

## Acts 21:31

The crowd was trying to kill Paul.

The Roman commander heard that all Jerusalem was in an uproar.

## Acts 21:32

At once he took soldiers and officers and ran down to the crowd.

When the people saw them, they stopped beating Paul.

## Acts 21:33

The commander arrested Paul.

He ordered his soldiers to bind Paul with two chains.

Then he asked who Paul was and what he had done.

## Acts 21:34

People shouted different answers.

The commander could not find out the truth in all the noise.

He ordered the soldiers to take Paul to their barracks.

**Help with these words**

Barracks are buildings where soldiers stay.

## Acts 21:35

The crowd was so violent that the soldiers had to carry Paul up the steps.

## Acts 21:36

People followed them, shouting, “Away with him!”

**Pause and answer**

Why could the commander get no clear answer from the crowd?

## Paul asks to speak to the people

## Acts 21:37

The soldiers were about to take Paul inside.

Paul asked the commander, “May I speak to you?”

The commander answered, “Do you speak Greek?

## Acts 21:38

Are you the Egyptian who started a rebellion some time ago?

He led four thousand men called the Assassins into the wilderness.”

**Help with these words**

A rebellion is a fight against the people who rule.

## Acts 21:39

Paul answered, “I am Jewish.

I come from Tarsus, an important city in Cilicia.

I am a citizen of that city.

Please let me speak to the people.”

## Acts 21:40

The commander gave him permission.

Paul stood on the steps and raised his hand to the crowd.

The people became very quiet.

Paul began speaking to them in Hebrew.

**Pause and answer**

What language did Paul use to speak to the crowd?
