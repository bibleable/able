# Acts 7 — Stephen speaks to the council and sees Jesus

**About this chapter**

Stephen tells the council how God led their ancestors.

Later, as people hurt him, Stephen prays for them.

## God calls Abraham

## Acts 7:1

The high priest asked Stephen, “Are these things people say about you true?”

## Acts 7:2

Stephen answered, “Brothers and fathers, listen to me.

Our glorious God appeared to our ancestor Abraham in Mesopotamia.

Abraham had not yet moved to Haran.

## Acts 7:3

“God said, ‘Leave your country and your relatives.

Go to the land I will show you.’

## Acts 7:4

“Abraham left the land of the Chaldeans and settled in Haran.

After Abraham’s father died, God brought Abraham here, to the land where you live.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Abraham live before he moved to Haran?

## God promises a home to Abraham’s family

## Acts 7:5

“God gave Abraham no land of his own here, not even a place to stand.

Yet God promised this land to Abraham and his family after him.

Abraham did not yet have a child.

## Acts 7:6

“God told Abraham his family would live as foreigners in another land.

The people there would enslave and mistreat them for four hundred years.

## Acts 7:7

“God said, ‘I will punish the nation that makes them slaves.

Then they will come out and worship me here.’

## Acts 7:8

“God made an agreement with Abraham, with circumcision as its sign.

When Abraham’s son Isaac was eight days old, Abraham circumcised him.

Isaac became Jacob’s father.

Jacob became the father of the twelve heads of our tribes.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision is a mark on a boy’s body.

It reminded Abraham’s family of God’s agreement with them.

Follow the arrows from Abraham to his son and grandson.

**Pause and answer**

What did God promise before Abraham had a child?

## Joseph helps his family in Egypt

## Acts 7:9

“Joseph’s brothers were jealous of him.

They sold him as a slave, and he went to Egypt.

But God was with Joseph.

## Acts 7:10

“God rescued Joseph from all his troubles.

God gave him wisdom and helped him win the favour of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

Pharaoh put Joseph in charge of Egypt and his own household.

## Acts 7:11

“Then a great shortage of food brought suffering to Egypt and Canaan.

Our ancestors could find nothing to eat.

## Acts 7:12

“Jacob heard there was grain in Egypt.

So he sent his sons there for the first time.

## Acts 7:13

“On their second visit, Joseph told his brothers who he was.

Pharaoh learned who Joseph’s family were.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jacob send his sons to Egypt?

## Jacob moves to Egypt

## Acts 7:14

“Joseph sent for his father Jacob and all his relatives.

There were seventy-five people in all.

## Acts 7:15

“Jacob went to Egypt.

Both Jacob and our ancestors died there.

## Acts 7:16

“People carried their bones back to Shechem.

They buried them in the tomb Abraham had bought there.

He had paid the sons of Hamor in silver.

**Pause and answer**

Who asked Jacob and his relatives to come to Egypt?

## Moses grows up in Egypt

## Acts 7:17

“The time came near for God to keep his promise to Abraham.

Our people in Egypt grew in number.

## Acts 7:18

“A new king began to rule Egypt.

He knew nothing about Joseph.

## Acts 7:19

“The king tricked our people and treated them cruelly.

He made parents leave their babies outside to die.

## Acts 7:20

“Moses was born at that time.

He was beautiful in God’s sight.

His family cared for him at home for three months.

## Acts 7:21

“When they put Moses outside, Pharaoh’s daughter took him in.

She raised him as her own son.

## Acts 7:22

“Moses learned all the wisdom of the Egyptians.

He was powerful in what he said and did.

**Pause and answer**

Who raised Moses as her son?

## Moses visits his people

## Acts 7:23

“When Moses was forty, he went to visit his own people, the Israelites.

## Acts 7:24

“Moses saw an Egyptian mistreating an Israelite.

Moses defended the man and killed the Egyptian who had hurt him.

## Acts 7:25

“Moses thought his people would understand that God was rescuing them through him.

They did not understand.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses think his people would understand?

## Moses flees to Midian

## Acts 7:26

“The next day Moses saw two Israelites fighting.

He tried to make peace.

He said, ‘You are brothers.

Why are you hurting each other?’

## Acts 7:27

“The man who was hurting the other pushed Moses aside.

He said, ‘Who made you our ruler and judge?

## Acts 7:28

“‘Do you want to kill me as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’

## Acts 7:29

“When Moses heard that, he fled to Midian.

He lived there as a foreigner and had two sons.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Moses go after leaving Egypt?

## God speaks from a burning bush

## Acts 7:30

“Forty years later an angel appeared to Moses near Mount Sinai.

Moses saw the angel in a burning bush in the desert.

## Acts 7:31

“Moses was amazed.

He came closer and heard the Lord’s voice.

## Acts 7:32

“The Lord said, ‘I am the God of your ancestors, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’

Moses shook with fear and did not dare to look.

## Acts 7:33

“The Lord said, ‘Take off your sandals.

You are standing on holy ground.

## Acts 7:34

“‘I have seen how my people suffer in Egypt.

I have heard their cries.

I have come down to set them free.

Now I am sending you back to Egypt.’

**Pause and answer**

Why did God send Moses back to Egypt?

## God sends Moses to lead his people

## Acts 7:35

“The people had rejected Moses and asked who made him their ruler and judge.

Yet God sent that same Moses to lead them and set them free.

God sent him through the angel in the bush.

## Acts 7:36

“Moses led the people out.

He did wonders and signs in Egypt and at the Red Sea.

He also did them during forty years in the wilderness.

## Acts 7:37

“This same Moses told the Israelites, ‘God will raise up a prophet like me from among your own people.’

## Acts 7:38

“Moses was with our ancestors when they gathered in the wilderness.

The angel spoke to him on Mount Sinai.

Moses received God’s living words to pass on to us.

**Pause and answer**

Who received God’s words on Mount Sinai?

## The people worship what they made

## Acts 7:39

“Our ancestors refused to obey Moses.

They turned away from him and longed for Egypt again.

## Acts 7:40

“They told Aaron, ‘Make gods to lead us.

Moses brought us out of Egypt.

We do not know what has happened to him.’

## Acts 7:41

“They made a statue in the shape of a calf.

They offered a sacrifice to it.

They celebrated what their own hands had made.

**Help with these words**

An idol is something people make and worship as a god.

## Acts 7:42

“God turned away and let them worship the stars of heaven.

In the book of the prophets, God asks, ‘People of Israel, did you bring me sacrifices and gifts during those forty years in the wilderness?

## Acts 7:43

“‘You carried the tent of Molech and the star of your god Rephan.

You made these idols to worship them.

So I will send you away to live beyond Babylon.’

**Pause and answer**

What shape did the people give their statue?

## A tent and a house for worship

## Acts 7:44

“Our ancestors had the tent of the Testimony in the wilderness.

God showed Moses a pattern for it.

They built the tent exactly as God told Moses.

**Help with these words**

The tent of the Testimony was a special tent for worship.

The people could take it with them when they moved.

## Acts 7:45

“Our ancestors received the tent and brought it into the land with Joshua.

God drove out the nations before them, and they took the land.

The tent stayed there until David’s time.

## Acts 7:46

“God showed David favour.

David asked to provide a home for the God of Jacob.

## Acts 7:47

“But Solomon was the one who built the house for God.

**Pause and answer**

Who built the house David wanted for God?

## God speaks about heaven and earth

## Acts 7:48

“God Most High does not live in houses people make.

Listen to what the prophet says.

## Acts 7:49

“The Lord says, ‘Heaven is my throne.

The earth is where I rest my feet.

What house can you build for me?

Where could I rest?

## Acts 7:50

“‘I made all these things myself.’”

**Pause and answer**

Who made heaven and earth?

## Stephen speaks directly to the council

## Acts 7:51

Stephen told the council, “You are stubborn!

You close your hearts and ears to God.

Like your ancestors, you keep resisting the Holy Spirit.

## Acts 7:52

“Your ancestors mistreated every prophet.

They killed those who said the Righteous One would come.

Now you have handed him over and killed him.

**Help with these words**

The Righteous One is Jesus.

He is the one who does what is right.

## Acts 7:53

“You received God’s law through angels.

Yet you have not obeyed it.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Stephen say the council kept resisting?

## Stephen sees Jesus

## Acts 7:54

The council members were furious with Stephen.

They ground their teeth at him.

## Acts 7:55

The Holy Spirit filled Stephen.

He looked up into heaven and saw God’s glory.

Jesus was standing at God’s right hand.

## Acts 7:56

Stephen said, “Look! I see heaven open.

The Son of Man is standing at God’s right hand.”

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus used for himself.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Stephen see at God’s right hand?

## Stephen prays for the people hurting him

## Acts 7:57

The council members covered their ears.

They shouted and rushed at Stephen together.

## Acts 7:58

They dragged Stephen outside the city and threw stones at him.

The witnesses left their outer clothes at the feet of a young man named Saul.

## Acts 7:59

As the stones struck him, Stephen prayed, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.”

## Acts 7:60

Stephen fell to his knees and called out, “Lord, do not hold this sin against them.”

After he said this, Stephen died.

**Pause and answer**

What did Stephen ask the Lord to do for the people hurting him?
