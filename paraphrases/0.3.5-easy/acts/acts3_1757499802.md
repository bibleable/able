# Acts 3 — A man walks and praises God

**About this chapter**

A man asks for money at a temple gate.

When he walks into the temple courts, the crowd wants to know what happened.

## A man asks Peter and John for money

## Acts 3:1

One afternoon Peter and John went to the temple to pray.

It was about three o’clock, the hour for prayer.

## Acts 3:2

People were carrying a man who had never been able to walk.

Every day they set him beside a temple gate called Beautiful.

There he asked people going into the temple courts for money.

## Acts 3:3

He saw Peter and John about to go in.

He asked them for money.

## Acts 3:4

Peter and John looked straight at the man.

Peter said, “Look at us.”

## Acts 3:5

The man looked at them.

He expected them to give him something.

**Pause and answer**

What did the man ask Peter and John to give him?

## The man stands and walks

## Acts 3:6

Peter said, “I have no silver or gold.

But I will give you what I have.

In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, get up and walk!”

**Help with these words**

Peter speaks in Jesus’ name.

That means he acts with Jesus’ authority.

## Acts 3:7

Peter took the man’s right hand and helped him up.

At once the man’s feet and ankles became strong.

## Acts 3:8

He sprang to his feet and began to walk.

He went into the temple courts with Peter and John.

He walked and jumped and praised God.

## Acts 3:9

Everyone saw the man walking and praising God.

## Acts 3:10

They recognised him.

He was the man who used to ask for money at the Beautiful Gate.

They were amazed at what had happened to him.

**Pause and answer**

What did the man do as he went into the temple courts?

## Peter speaks to the crowd

## Acts 3:11

The man held on to Peter and John.

The astonished people ran to them at a covered walkway called Solomon’s Colonnade.

## Acts 3:12

Peter saw the crowd and spoke.

“People of Israel, why does this surprise you?

Why stare at us?

Do you think our own power made this man walk?

Do you think it happened because we are so close to God?

## Acts 3:13

“The God of our ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob has honoured his servant Jesus.

You handed Jesus over and rejected him in front of Pilate.

Pilate had decided to set Jesus free.

**About the picture**

![Abraham, his son Isaac, and Isaac’s son Jacob.](../pictures/abraham-family.svg)

Follow the names from Abraham to Jacob.

Peter names all three when he speaks about the God their families worshipped.

## Acts 3:14

“You rejected the one who is holy and does what is right.

You asked Pilate to free a man who had murdered someone.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Peter say God had honoured?

## Peter explains how the man became strong

## Acts 3:15

“You killed the one who gives life.

But God raised Jesus from the dead.

We are witnesses to this.

**Help with these words**

A witness tells others what they know from what they have seen or heard.

## Acts 3:16

“You see this man and you know him.

Trust in the name of Jesus has made him strong.

Jesus’ name and the faith that comes through him have made this man completely well.

You can see it for yourselves.

## Acts 3:17

“Brothers, I know you did not understand what you were doing.

Your leaders did not understand either.

## Acts 3:18

“Long ago God spoke through all his prophets.

He said his Christ would suffer.

Now God has done what he said would happen.

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

**Pause and answer**

In whose name did the healing happen?

## Peter asks the people to turn back to God

## Acts 3:19

“So turn away from sin and come back to God.

Then God will wipe away your sins.

**Help with these words**

To repent means to turn away from wrongdoing and turn back to God.

Wiping away sins is a picture of God forgiving people.

## Acts 3:20

“The Lord will give you times of rest and new strength.

He will send Jesus, the Christ he chose for you.

## Acts 3:21

“Jesus must stay in heaven until God makes everything right again.

God promised this long ago through his holy prophets.

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter ask the people to turn away from?

## Peter recalls what Moses said

## Acts 3:22

“Moses said, ‘The Lord your God will raise up a prophet like me from among your own people.

Listen to everything he tells you.

## Acts 3:23

“‘Anyone who refuses to listen to him will be cut off completely from God’s people.’

## Acts 3:24

“Samuel and all the prophets who spoke after him told people about these days.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses tell the people to do when the prophet spoke?

## God’s promise to Abraham

## Acts 3:25

“You are heirs to what the prophets said and to the covenant God made with your ancestors.

God told Abraham, ‘Through your offspring I will bless every family on earth.’

**Help with these words**

An heir receives what someone has promised.

A covenant is a lasting agreement.

Offspring means children or those born later in the same family.

## Acts 3:26

“God raised up his servant Jesus and sent him to you first.

He came to bless each of you by turning you away from doing wrong.”

**Pause and answer**

Which families did God promise to bless?
