# Acts 8 — Philip shares the good news in Samaria and on the road

**About this chapter**

Philip tells people in Samaria about Jesus.

Later he sits beside a traveller and helps him understand what he is reading.

## The believers leave Jerusalem

## Acts 8:1

Saul approved of putting Stephen to death.

That day people began attacking the believers in Jerusalem.

All except the apostles scattered across Judea and Samaria.

## Acts 8:2

Men who honoured God buried Stephen.

They grieved deeply for him.

## Acts 8:3

Saul tried to destroy the church.

He went from house to house.

He dragged men and women away and put them in prison.

## Acts 8:4

The scattered believers told people God’s message wherever they went.

**Pause and answer**

What did the believers do wherever they went?

## Philip tells people in Samaria about Jesus

## Acts 8:5

Philip went to a city in Samaria.

He told the people there about the Christ.

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose. Philip was speaking about Jesus.

## Acts 8:6

The crowds listened closely to Philip.

They saw the signs he did.

## Acts 8:7

Evil spirits came out of many people with loud cries.

Many people who could not move or had trouble walking became well.

## Acts 8:8

The people in that city were full of joy.

**Pause and answer**

How did the people in the city feel after the healings?

## Simon listens to Philip

## Acts 8:9

A man named Simon had been practising magic there.

He amazed the people of Samaria.

He said he was a great man.

## Acts 8:10

People of every rank listened to him.

They said, “This man is the power of God called the Great Power.”

## Acts 8:11

For a long time Simon had amazed them with his magic.

That was why they paid attention to him.

## Acts 8:12

Then Philip told them the good news about God’s kingdom and about Jesus Christ.

Men and women believed the message and were baptized.

## Acts 8:13

Simon also believed and was baptized.

He stayed close to Philip.

The signs and miracles he saw amazed him.

**Pause and answer**

What surprised Simon when he followed Philip?

## Peter and John pray for the believers

## Acts 8:14

The apostles in Jerusalem heard that people in Samaria had accepted God’s message.

They sent Peter and John to them.

## Acts 8:15

Peter and John prayed for the believers there to receive the Holy Spirit.

## Acts 8:16

The believers had been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.

But the Holy Spirit had not yet come upon them.

## Acts 8:17

Peter and John laid their hands on the believers.

Then the believers received the Holy Spirit.

**Help with these words**

Peter and John placed their hands on the believers as they prayed for them.

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter and John ask God to give the believers?

## Simon offers money

## Acts 8:18

Simon saw what happened when the apostles laid their hands on people.

He offered the apostles money.

## Acts 8:19

Simon said, “Give me this power too.

Then people will receive the Holy Spirit when I lay my hands on them.”

## Acts 8:20

Peter answered, “May you and your money perish together!

You thought you could buy God’s gift!

## Acts 8:21

“You have no share in this work.

What you want is wrong in God’s sight.

**Pause and answer**

What did Simon think his money could buy?

## Peter asks Simon to change

## Acts 8:22

“Turn away from this wrong and pray to the Lord.

Perhaps he will forgive you for what you planned.

## Acts 8:23

“I can see that bitterness is poisoning you.

Your wrongdoing holds you like a prisoner.”

**Help with these words**

Peter uses the picture of a prisoner to describe how wrongdoing controls Simon.

## Acts 8:24

Simon answered, “Pray to the Lord for me.

Ask him to keep these things from happening to me.”

## Acts 8:25

Peter and John told the people what they knew and taught the Lord’s message.

Then they returned to Jerusalem.

On the way, they shared the good news in many Samaritan villages.

**Pause and answer**

What did Simon ask Peter to do for him?

## Philip meets a traveller

## Acts 8:26

An angel of the Lord told Philip, “Go south to the desert road from Jerusalem to Gaza.”

## Acts 8:27

Philip set out and met a man from Ethiopia.

The man was a eunuch and an official of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians.

He looked after all her treasure.

He had gone to Jerusalem to worship God.

**Help with these words**

A eunuch was a man who had been castrated. Some eunuchs served in royal courts.

## Acts 8:28

Now he was on his way home.

He sat in his chariot, reading the words of the prophet Isaiah.

**Help with these words**

A chariot is a cart that horses pull.

## Acts 8:29

The Holy Spirit told Philip, “Go over to that chariot.

Stay beside it.”

**Pause and answer**

What was the official reading on his way home?

## The traveller asks about Isaiah’s words

## Acts 8:30

Philip ran up and heard the man reading Isaiah aloud.

Philip asked, “Do you understand what you are reading?”

## Acts 8:31

The man answered, “How can I understand unless someone helps me?”

He invited Philip to sit beside him.

## Acts 8:32

These were the words the man was reading.

“People led him away like a sheep going to be killed.

A lamb is quiet when someone cuts its wool.

In the same way, he did not open his mouth.

The official read Isaiah’s words aloud so Philip could hear them.

## Acts 8:33

“They shamed him and refused to treat him fairly.

Who can tell of the family that came after him?

They took his life on earth away.”

## Acts 8:34

The official asked Philip, “Who does the prophet mean?

Is he speaking about himself or someone else?”

## Acts 8:35

Philip began with those very words.

He told the official the good news about Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Philip tell the official about?

## Philip baptizes the traveller

## Acts 8:36

They came to some water beside the road.

The official said, “Look, here is water!

What could stop me from being baptized?”

## Acts 8:37

**About this verse number**

In the Berean Standard Bible, the reading continues at verse 38.

## Acts 8:38

The official ordered the chariot to stop.

Philip and the official both went down into the water.

Philip baptized him.

## Acts 8:39

When they came out of the water, the Lord’s Spirit took Philip away.

The official did not see Philip again.

He went on his way full of joy.

## Acts 8:40

Philip appeared in Azotus.

He travelled through the area, telling the good news in every town until he reached Caesarea.

**Pause and answer**

How did the official feel as he continued on his way?
