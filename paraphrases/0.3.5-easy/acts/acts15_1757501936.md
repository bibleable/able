# Acts 15 — The believers in Jerusalem send a letter

**About this chapter**

A question divides the believers in Antioch.

The church in Jerusalem listens and sends an answer.

## A disagreement in Antioch

## Acts 15:1

Some men came from Judea to Antioch.

They told the believers, “You must follow the custom Moses gave us.

Unless you receive circumcision, God will not save you.”

**Help with these words**

Circumcision means removing a small piece of skin from a boy’s or man’s penis. For Jewish men, it was a sign of their people’s agreement with God.

## Acts 15:2

Paul and Barnabas argued strongly against this teaching.

The church chose them and some other believers to visit Jerusalem.

They would ask the apostles and elders about this question.

**Help with these words**

The apostles were men Jesus sent to speak for him. The elders were leaders who cared for the believers.

## Acts 15:3

The church sent the group on their way.

They passed through Phoenicia and Samaria.

They told how Gentiles had turned to God.

All the believers who heard this were glad.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 15:4

The church in Jerusalem welcomed the visitors.

The apostles and elders welcomed them too.

The visitors told them everything God had done through them.

## Acts 15:5

Some believers who belonged to the Pharisees stood up.

They said, “Gentile men must receive circumcision.

We must tell Gentile believers to obey the law of Moses.”

**Help with these words**

The Pharisees were a Jewish group who studied and followed the law of Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the church send Paul and Barnabas to discuss this question?

## Peter reminds them what God did

## Acts 15:6

The apostles and elders met to discuss the question.

## Acts 15:7

They talked for a long time.

Then Peter stood up and said, “Brothers, you remember God’s choice some time ago.

God chose me to tell Gentiles the good news so they could believe.

## Acts 15:8

God knows what is inside each person.

He showed that he accepted the Gentiles.

He gave them the Holy Spirit just as he gave the Spirit to us.

## Acts 15:9

God treated us and them alike.

When they believed, God made their hearts clean.

**Help with these words**

Making their hearts clean means taking away their sin.

## Acts 15:10

Why are you putting God to the test?

Why put this heavy yoke on these followers of Jesus?

Even our ancestors could not carry it.

Neither can we.

**Help with these words**

A yoke is a wooden bar that animals wear to pull a load. Peter compared the demands of the law to a heavy load.

## Acts 15:11

We believe the Lord Jesus saves us by his kindness.

He saves Gentiles in the same way.”

## Acts 15:12

Everyone grew quiet and listened to Barnabas and Paul.

The two men told them about the amazing signs God had done through them among the Gentiles.

**Pause and answer**

What gift had God given both groups of believers?

## James explains what the prophets wrote

## Acts 15:13

When Barnabas and Paul finished, James spoke.

“Brothers, listen to me.

## Acts 15:14

Simon Peter has told us how God first came to the Gentiles.

God chose people from among them to belong to him.

## Acts 15:15

The prophets wrote words that agree with this.

They wrote that the Lord said these words.

## Acts 15:16

‘After this I will return.

David’s tent has fallen down.

I will rebuild what lies in ruins.

I will set it up again.

**Help with these words**

The fallen tent is a word picture. The Lord promises to restore the kingdom of David’s family.

## Acts 15:17

Then the rest of the people can seek me.

All the Gentiles I call my own can seek me too.

I am the Lord who does these things.

## Acts 15:18

People have known about these things for ages.’”

**Pause and answer**

Who does the Lord say can seek him?

## James says what to put in a letter

## Acts 15:19

James continued, “Here is what I think we should do.

We should let the Gentiles turn to God without making it hard for them.

## Acts 15:20

Let us write and ask them to avoid four things.

Avoid food offered to idols.

Avoid wrong sexual behaviour.

Avoid meat from animals that people have strangled.

Avoid eating blood.

**Help with these words**

An idol is an object people worship as a god. Strangling an animal means killing it by stopping its breathing.

## Acts 15:21

For a long time, people in every city have taught the law of Moses.

People read it aloud in the synagogues every Sabbath.”

**Help with these words**

A synagogue is a Jewish place of worship. The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

**Pause and answer**

How did James suggest telling Gentile believers about the decision?

## The church chooses people to carry the letter

## Acts 15:22

The apostles, the elders and the whole church agreed.

They chose two leaders to go to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas.

One was Judas, also called Barsabbas.

The other was Silas.

## Acts 15:23

They sent this letter with them.

“We are the apostles and elders, your brothers.

We greet the Gentile believers in Antioch, Syria and Cilicia.

## Acts 15:24

We heard that some people from here upset you.

Their words troubled you.

We gave them no orders to say those things.

## Acts 15:25

So we all agreed to choose men to visit you.

They are coming with our dear friends Barnabas and Paul.

## Acts 15:26

Barnabas and Paul have risked their lives for our Lord Jesus Christ.

## Acts 15:27

We are sending Judas and Silas.

They will tell you in person what we have written.”

**Pause and answer**

Who would explain the letter in person?

## The letter gives the decision

## Acts 15:28

The letter continued, “The Holy Spirit and we agree.

We ask you to do only what is necessary.

## Acts 15:29

Avoid food offered to idols.

Avoid eating blood.

Avoid meat from animals that people have strangled.

Avoid wrong sexual behaviour.

You will do well to avoid these things.

Goodbye.”

## Acts 15:30

The men went to Antioch.

They gathered the believers together and gave them the letter.

## Acts 15:31

The believers read the letter.

Its message encouraged them and made them glad.

## Acts 15:32

Judas and Silas were prophets who spoke messages from God.

They said much to encourage the believers and strengthen them.

## Acts 15:33

Judas and Silas stayed for a while.

Then the believers sent them back in peace to those who had sent them.

## Acts 15:34

## Acts 15:35

Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch.

They and many others taught and shared the Lord’s message.

**Pause and answer**

How did the believers feel when they read the letter?

## Paul and Barnabas take different journeys

## Acts 15:36

Later Paul said to Barnabas, “Let us visit the believers again.

We can go to every town where we shared the Lord’s message.

Let us see how they are doing.”

## Acts 15:37

Barnabas wanted to take John, also called Mark.

## Acts 15:38

Paul thought they should leave Mark behind.

Mark had left them in Pamphylia.

He had not stayed to help with the work.

## Acts 15:39

Paul and Barnabas disagreed so strongly that they went different ways.

Barnabas took Mark and sailed to Cyprus.

## Acts 15:40

Paul chose Silas to go with him.

The believers asked the Lord to care for Paul in his kindness.

Then Paul left.

## Acts 15:41

Paul travelled through Syria and Cilicia.

He helped the churches there grow stronger.
