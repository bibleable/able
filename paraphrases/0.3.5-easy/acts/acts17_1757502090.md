# Acts 17 — Paul tells the people of Athens about God

**About this chapter**

Paul speaks in synagogues and in a marketplace.

In Athens, he tells people about the God who gives them life.

## Paul speaks in Thessalonica

## Acts 17:1

Paul and Silas passed through Amphipolis and Apollonia.

They came to Thessalonica.

The Jewish people there had a synagogue, a place to worship.

## Acts 17:2

As usual, Paul went into the synagogue.

For three Sabbaths he discussed the Scriptures with the people.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest. The Scriptures are the holy writings.

## Acts 17:3

Paul showed them why the Christ had to suffer.

He showed them why the Christ had to rise from the dead.

Paul said, “Jesus is the Christ.

He is the one I am telling you about.”

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

## Acts 17:4

Some Jewish people believed and joined Paul and Silas.

Many Greeks who honoured God joined them too.

So did many women who were leaders.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul use to explain his message?

## A crowd comes to Jason’s house

## Acts 17:5

Some Jewish people grew jealous.

They gathered troublemakers from the marketplace into a crowd.

Soon the whole city was in an uproar.

The crowd burst into Jason’s house looking for Paul and Silas.

They wanted to bring them out before the people.

## Acts 17:6

The crowd could not find Paul and Silas.

They dragged Jason and some other believers to the city officials.

They shouted, “These men cause trouble everywhere!

Now they have come here.

## Acts 17:7

Jason has welcomed them into his house.

They all disobey the Roman emperor’s orders.

They say there is another king named Jesus!”

## Acts 17:8

These words upset the crowd and the city officials.

## Acts 17:9

The officials made Jason and the others give them money as a guarantee.

Then the officials let them go.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the crowd hope to find at Jason’s house?

## People in Berea check what Paul says

## Acts 17:10

As soon as night came, the believers sent Paul and Silas to Berea.

There Paul and Silas went into the synagogue.

## Acts 17:11

The people there listened more openly than the people in Thessalonica.

They were eager to hear the message.

Each day they searched the Scriptures to check what Paul taught.

![An open scroll with lines of writing, like the Scriptures the people read.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Acts 17:12

Many of them believed.

Many well-known Greek women and men believed too.

## Acts 17:13

Some Jewish people in Thessalonica heard about Paul’s work in Berea.

They came to Berea and stirred up the crowds against him.

## Acts 17:14

The believers sent Paul to the coast at once.

Silas and Timothy stayed in Berea.

## Acts 17:15

Some men took Paul to Athens.

Then they went back with a message for Silas and Timothy.

Paul wanted them to join him as soon as possible.

**Pause and answer**

How did people in Berea check whether Paul’s words were true?

## People in Athens ask to hear more

## Acts 17:16

Paul waited for Silas and Timothy in Athens.

It upset Paul deeply to see idols everywhere.

**Help with these words**

An idol is an object people worship as a god.

## Acts 17:17

In the synagogue Paul spoke with Jews and Gentiles who honoured God.

Each day he also talked with people in the marketplace.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 17:18

Two groups of thinkers also debated with Paul.

They were called Epicureans and Stoics.

Some asked, “What is this man talking on and on about?”

Others said, “He seems to be teaching about foreign gods.”

They said this because Paul spoke about Jesus and the resurrection.

**Help with these words**

Resurrection means rising from the dead.

## Acts 17:19

They took Paul to a meeting of the council called the Areopagus.

They asked, “Can you explain this new teaching to us?

## Acts 17:20

These ideas sound strange to us.

We want to know what they mean.”

## Acts 17:21

Both the Athenians and the foreigners living there spent their time discussing new ideas.

They wanted to hear each new idea and talk about it.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the council want to hear Paul speak?

## Paul tells the council about the God who gives life

## Acts 17:22

Paul stood up at the council meeting.

He said, “People of Athens, I can see how much religion matters to you.

## Acts 17:23

I walked around and looked at the things you worship.

I even found an altar with these words on it: ‘To an unknown god.’

You worship someone you do not know.

I have come to tell you about him.

**Help with these words**

An altar is a place where people offer gifts in worship.

## Acts 17:24

God made the world and everything in it.

He is Lord of heaven and earth.

He does not live in temples that people build.

## Acts 17:25

God does not need people to supply him with anything.

He gives everyone life and breath and everything else.

## Acts 17:26

From one man God made all the peoples of the earth.

He gave them the whole earth to live on.

He chose when and where each nation would live.

## Acts 17:27

God wanted people to look for him.

Perhaps they would reach out for him and find him.

Yet God is near to each of us.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say God gives everyone?

## Paul asks the people to turn to God

## Acts 17:28

Paul continued, “We depend on God to live and move and exist.

Some of your own poets have said, ‘We are his children.’

## Acts 17:29

Since we are God’s children, how can God be like a statue?

People think up statues and make them from gold, silver or stone.

## Acts 17:30

In the past God overlooked what people did without knowing him.

Now he commands everyone everywhere to turn away from sin and turn to him.

## Acts 17:31

God has chosen a day to judge the world fairly.

He has chosen a man to do this.

God raised this man from the dead as proof for everyone.”

**Pause and answer**

How did God show everyone whom he had chosen?

## People respond to Paul’s message

## Acts 17:32

When people heard about the dead rising, some made fun of Paul.

Others said, “We want to hear more about this another time.”

## Acts 17:33

Then Paul left the council meeting.

## Acts 17:34

Some people joined Paul and believed.

One was Dionysius, a member of the council.

A woman named Damaris and others also believed.

**Pause and answer**

Who was the council member who believed?
