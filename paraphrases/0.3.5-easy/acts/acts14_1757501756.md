# Acts 14 — Paul and Barnabas tell people about the living God

**About this chapter**

In Lystra a man walks for the first time.

Later, Paul and Barnabas revisit the people who believe in Jesus.

## People in Iconium hear about Jesus

## Acts 14:1

In Iconium, Paul and Barnabas went to the synagogue as usual.

They spoke so well that many Jews and Greeks believed.

**Help with these words**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet to worship.

## Acts 14:2

Some Jewish people did not believe the message.

They turned other people against the believers.

These other people were Gentiles.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 14:3

Paul and Barnabas stayed there a long time.

They spoke boldly about the Lord and his kindness.

The Lord gave them power to do amazing things.

These signs showed that their message was true.

## Acts 14:4

The people of Iconium took different sides.

Some sided with the Jewish people who opposed the message.

Others sided with the apostles, Paul and Barnabas.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone sent to speak for the Lord.

## Acts 14:5

Some Gentiles and Jews joined their leaders in a plan.

They wanted to hurt Paul and Barnabas by throwing stones at them.

## Acts 14:6

Paul and Barnabas found out about the plan.

They fled to Lystra and Derbe, cities in Lycaonia.

They also went to places near those cities.

## Acts 14:7

There they kept telling people the good news about Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul and Barnabas do after they left Iconium?

## A man in Lystra walks for the first time

## Acts 14:8

A man sat in Lystra listening.

Since birth his feet had been unable to support him.

He had never walked.

## Acts 14:9

The man listened to Paul.

Paul looked closely at him.

Paul saw that the man trusted God to heal him.

## Acts 14:10

Paul called out loudly, “Stand up on your feet!”

The man jumped up and began to walk.

**Pause and answer**

What did the man do when Paul called to him?

## The crowd wants to worship Paul and Barnabas

## Acts 14:11

The crowd saw what Paul had done.

They shouted in their own language, the language of Lycaonia.

“The gods have come down to us as human beings!”

## Acts 14:12

The people called Barnabas “Zeus.”

They called Paul “Hermes” because Paul did most of the speaking.

**Help with these words**

Zeus and Hermes were names of gods these people worshipped.

## Acts 14:13

The temple of Zeus stood just outside the city.

The priest brought bulls and rings of flowers to the city gates.

He and the crowd wanted to offer the bulls as a sacrifice.

**Help with these words**

In a sacrifice like this, people killed an animal as a gift to a god.

## Acts 14:14

Barnabas and Paul heard what the people planned.

They tore their own clothes in distress.

They rushed into the crowd and shouted.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Barnabas and Paul rush into the crowd?

## Paul and Barnabas point to the living God

## Acts 14:15

They shouted, “Why are you doing this?

We are human beings just like you.

We bring you good news.

Turn away from these worthless things.

Turn to the living God.

He made heaven, earth and sea, and everything in them.

## Acts 14:16

In times past God let every nation go its own way.

## Acts 14:17

Yet God has always shown you his goodness.

He sends you rain.

He gives you seasons when your crops grow well.

He gives you plenty to eat and fills you with joy.”

## Acts 14:18

Even then, Paul and Barnabas could hardly stop the people from offering them a sacrifice.

**Pause and answer**

What does God give people to help their crops grow?

## Paul survives an attack

## Acts 14:19

Some Jewish people came from Antioch in Pisidia and from Iconium.

They won the crowd over to their side.

They threw stones at Paul and dragged him outside the city.

They thought he was dead.

## Acts 14:20

The followers of Jesus gathered around Paul.

Paul got up and went back into Lystra.

The next day he and Barnabas left for Derbe.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Paul go as soon as he got up?

## Paul and Barnabas visit the believers again

## Acts 14:21

In Derbe they told people the good news.

Many people began to follow Jesus.

Then Paul and Barnabas went back to Lystra.

They also returned to Iconium and Antioch in Pisidia.

## Acts 14:22

They helped the believers grow strong in their faith.

They urged them to keep trusting Jesus.

They said, “We must go through many hard things to enter God’s kingdom.”

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God’s rule over his people.

## Acts 14:23

Paul and Barnabas chose leaders for each church.

These leaders were called elders.

Paul and Barnabas prayed and went without food.

They asked the Lord to care for these believers.

The believers had put their trust in him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul and Barnabas urge the believers to keep doing?

## The church that sent them hears the news

## Acts 14:24

Paul and Barnabas travelled through Pisidia and reached Pamphylia.

## Acts 14:25

They shared God’s message in Perga.

Then they went down to Attalia.

## Acts 14:26

From Attalia they sailed back to Antioch in Syria.

The believers there had asked God to care for them on their journey.

Now Paul and Barnabas had finished that work.

## Acts 14:27

They gathered the church together.

They told everyone what God had done through them.

God had opened a door for the Gentiles to believe.

**Help with these words**

An open door is a word picture. God had made a way for Gentiles to hear about Jesus and trust him.

## Acts 14:28

Paul and Barnabas stayed a long time with the believers there.

**Pause and answer**

What good news about the Gentiles did the church hear?
