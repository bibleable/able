# Acts 18 — Paul teaches in Corinth, and Apollos learns in Ephesus

**About this chapter**

Paul makes tents and teaches people in Corinth.

Later, his friends Priscilla and Aquila help another teacher.

## Paul works with Aquila and Priscilla

## Acts 18:1

Paul left Athens and went to Corinth.

## Acts 18:2

There Paul met a Jewish man named Aquila, from Pontus.

Aquila and his wife Priscilla had recently come from Italy.

Emperor Claudius had ordered all Jewish people to leave Rome.

Paul went to visit the couple.

## Acts 18:3

Like Paul, Aquila and Priscilla made tents for a living.

So Paul stayed with them and worked with them.

## Acts 18:4

Every Sabbath Paul spoke in the synagogue.

He tried to persuade both Jews and Greeks to believe his message.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest. A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet to worship.

What work did Paul do with Aquila and Priscilla?

## People in Corinth believe in Jesus

## Acts 18:5

Silas and Timothy arrived from Macedonia.

Then Paul spent all his time sharing God’s message.

He told the Jewish people that Jesus is the Christ.

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

## Acts 18:6

Some people argued against Paul and insulted him.

Paul shook out his clothes in protest.

He told them, “You are responsible for what happens to you.

I have done my part.

From now on I will go to the Gentiles.”

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Acts 18:7

Paul left the synagogue and went to the house next door.

Titus Justus lived there and worshipped God.

## Acts 18:8

Crispus, the synagogue leader, believed in the Lord Jesus.

Everyone in his home believed too.

Many other people in Corinth heard the message and believed.

They received baptism.

**Help with these words**

In baptism, believers use water to show that someone belongs to Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Who lived next door to the synagogue?

## The Lord tells Paul to keep speaking

## Acts 18:9

One night the Lord spoke to Paul in a vision.

“Do not be afraid.

Keep speaking.

## Acts 18:10

I am with you.

No one will attack you and hurt you.

Many people in this city belong to me.”

**Help with these words**

In a vision, God shows someone something or speaks to them.

## Acts 18:11

So Paul stayed in Corinth for a year and a half.

He taught the people God’s message.

**Pause and answer**

Why could Paul keep speaking without fear?

## Gallio hears a complaint about Paul

## Acts 18:12

Gallio was the Roman governor of Achaia.

Some Jewish people joined together against Paul.

They brought him before Gallio in court.

## Acts 18:13

They said, “Paul is persuading people to worship God in ways our law forbids.”

## Acts 18:14

Paul was about to speak, but Gallio spoke first.

He told the people accusing Paul, “If this were about wrongdoing or a serious crime, I would listen.

## Acts 18:15

But you disagree about words, names and your own law.

Settle this yourselves.

I will not judge such things.”

## Acts 18:16

Gallio drove them away from the court.

## Acts 18:17

The crowd grabbed Sosthenes, a synagogue leader.

They beat him there in front of the court.

Gallio showed no concern about it.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Gallio tell the people to settle the matter themselves?

## Paul leaves Corinth and visits other believers

## Acts 18:18

Paul stayed in Corinth quite a while longer.

Then he said goodbye to the believers.

In Cenchrea he had someone shave his head to keep a promise he had made.

Priscilla and Aquila sailed with Paul towards Syria.

## Acts 18:19

When they reached Ephesus, Paul left the couple there.

He went into the synagogue and discussed his message with the Jewish people.

## Acts 18:20

They asked Paul to stay longer.

Paul said he could not stay.

## Acts 18:21

As he left, he said, “I will come back if God wants me to.”

Then Paul sailed from Ephesus.

## Acts 18:22

Paul landed at Caesarea.

He went up to Jerusalem and greeted the church there.

Then he went to Antioch.

## Acts 18:23

After a while Paul left Antioch.

He travelled from place to place through Galatia and Phrygia.

He helped all the believers grow stronger in their faith.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul say about coming back to Ephesus?

## Priscilla and Aquila help Apollos

## Acts 18:24

A Jewish man named Apollos came to Ephesus.

He came from Alexandria.

Apollos spoke well and knew the Scriptures very well.

**Help with these words**

The Scriptures are the holy writings that people read to learn about God.

## Acts 18:25

Apollos had learned about the way of the Lord.

He spoke with great feeling and taught accurately about Jesus.

Yet the only baptism he knew was the one John taught.

## Acts 18:26

Apollos began speaking boldly in the synagogue.

Priscilla and Aquila heard him and took him aside.

They helped him understand God’s way more fully.

**Pause and answer**

Who took Apollos aside to help him learn?

## Apollos helps believers in Achaia

## Acts 18:27

Apollos decided to cross over to Achaia.

The believers in Ephesus encouraged him to go.

They wrote to the believers there and asked them to welcome him.

Through God’s kindness those people had come to believe.

When Apollos arrived, he helped them greatly.

## Acts 18:28

Apollos answered the arguments of his Jewish opponents in public.

He showed from the Scriptures that Jesus is the Christ.
